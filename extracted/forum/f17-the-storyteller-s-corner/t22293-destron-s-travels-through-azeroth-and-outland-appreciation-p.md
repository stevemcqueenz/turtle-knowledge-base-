# Destron's Travels through Azeroth and Outland Appreciation Post

## Post 154732 by Zorb878 — 2025-10-07T14:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154732#p154732 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

It is an extremely rare thing to see fan fiction surpass the works of the original writer or franchise but alas Destron's Travels through Azeroth and Outland is that rare exception. Although a work of fan fiction it is a compelling tale of a Forsaken mage traversing the realms of Azeroth fighting both the demons of the Burning Legion as well as the demons of his past life. For those interested it is a genuinely compelling piece of fiction, and although it does cover some of the post Eredar retcon content, you can ignore the stuff in Outland, Northrend, and Cataclysm as the stuff on Azeroth proper is mostly safe lore wise as he based most of his writing on quests in WoW, WC3 and the RPG.

Enjoy!
