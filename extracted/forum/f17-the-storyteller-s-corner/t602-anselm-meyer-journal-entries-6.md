**Journal Entry 6: Wetlands to Dustwallow Marsh; to... Astranaar.**
Apparently before retiring to my room, this High Elf woman gestures me over. I will admit, I had one to many the night after writing this. Her name was Vyrin, or something along those lines. Apparently she said for me to travel to a place called "Ashenvale" in a town called Astranaar to service our allies to the west.

Well. Why not? Ships are free to take to and fro. So Randis and I picked up our things from the tavern after picking up my payment for my chores around the city and the region and took the ship to Kalimdor. Landing in Theramore Isle, we did a bit of sight-seeing. This is my first time in Kalimdor, actually. Apparently I'll be seeing more than just High Elves, Dwarves and such here in the west. Things I've only heard tales of in books and drunk ravings. Cow-men who walk on two legs, the Orcs, Night Elves which are native to this land and not refugees in the Park in Stormwind. Floaty... spirits. Odd. Not to mention roving hordes of nomads which have donkey bodies with chests, arms, and legs of men.
Not to mention the way Night Elf society is turned topside compared to Humans. Apparently women run everything. I have nothing against that - after all, there were plenty of women serving the wars and the effort they did at home... I need to quit talking about those times and live in the now.

I have made my way to Astranaar and reported to this Sentinel which gave me a tour of the village, and I soon asked about town in ways I could help. Afterwards, I retired to the tavern to get a nice cold ale. Speaking of Elves again, what the Hell is an "Elune"? Do these peaceful individuals not know of the Light and it's teachings?

...Apparently they don't serve alcohol, let alone brew or distill anything with a kick. What kind of place am I even living in? Already, I hate the High Elf who thought it was funny to send me here. Whatever. Their juices and fruits are actually not that bad, now that I think of it. They even make this thing called a "kimchi". Odd word.
