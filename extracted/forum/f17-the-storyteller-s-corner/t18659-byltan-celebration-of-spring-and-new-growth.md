Byltan, the ancient Druidic festival enjoyed by the Kaldorei, the Children of Cenarius, the Shu'halo, and all children of nature is nearly here! As spring reaches its zenith and summer approaches, the Druidic races gather in Hyjal to celebrate the rite of spring and coming of summer as life returns to Azeroth.

The sacred creatures of the Kaldorei and Shu’Halo - the Hippogryphs and the Wyverns - hatched in February are to take their first flights at this festival. Many riders will bond with their new Mount this day, and new beginnings are upon us as we take to the soon to be summer skies soaring!
