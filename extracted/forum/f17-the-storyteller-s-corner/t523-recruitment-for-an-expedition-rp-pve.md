OOC: Hey guys! I'm Teshmer on this server and in the Discord. I've been around since around November of last year. Basically what I'm looking for is a handful of guys interested in a lengthy RP-PvE campaign involving multiple dungeons and world elite quests! This would be a zone-spanning adventure involving ICly clearing several dungeons and questlines that would enrich your character mechanically and in roleplay. If you play an adventurer of any sort I certainly think yours would be interested!

I plan on taking this group from Ironforge to Gnomeregan, Stromgarde Keep, Alterac City, then up to Scarlet Monastery with the intent of possibly then going to Razorfen Kraul. The campaign would be focused heavily on realism bolstered by the in-game RPG mechanics of Classic WoW and our expansive knowledge of the Warcraft Universe and its inner workings. We'd be travelling to our locations, fighting, camping on the road and facing the travails along it offered to us by PvE and PvP mechanics.

I've done this kind of content before and am confident you will thoroughly enjoy yourself and the experiences it gives all of us who will be involved. So hit me up here, on the Discord, or in-game and then your character can meet mine as he gathers a party for these endeavors. I'll let everyone know once we've filled our party. I understand if scheduling is a problem; I'm a dad of twins and married. I've been playing for a long time but life has changed as much as WoW has. We'll all work together to find the best times to get this done.

Personally I'm at that awkward levelling stage where there's not a lot to do without a group and I know most of us on this small tight-knit server are, too. So let's form some lasting in-character bonds and push on to 60 with lots of memorable RP along the way!
