# To Lift a Curse

## Post 3363 by Lothradis — 2019-07-06T05:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3363#p3363 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Lothradis comes from a long line of warlocks, but the generations before him have had to deal with a curse. The curse that afflicts him now. Many generations ago, Lothradis' family was cursed with never being able to summon. Imps, Voidwalkers, Succubi, nothing would answer the summons.

The answer to this curse lies somewhere across Azeroth. Are there deeds to be done? A ritual in a particular place? A creature that needs to be slain? And it has fallen to Lothradis to end family curse, and let future generations wield the power once again.

Lothradis carries the family journal that is full of notes, maps, information of the lands of Azeroth that his father, grandfather and all the warlocks in his family line have been trying to solve. Even the origin of the curse has become a mystery to time.

Reading over the last few pages of notes. Lothradis decides that Elwynn Forest is the place to start. Maybe there are more clues there.

((I have many ideas of things that need to be done to lift the curse. I'm hoping someone with some good knowledge of lore can help me out. I'm thinking there would be several things in the world that need to be done, and I'd like to tie them in to some of the dungeons and zones. Thank you for your time))
