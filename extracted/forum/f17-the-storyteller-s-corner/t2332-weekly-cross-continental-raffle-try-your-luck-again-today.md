Cross-Continental Raffle. Try your luck again today!

 **Dancing Goblin Inn** [H]
Place: **The Crossroads, The Barrens**
Time: **December 19th 5 pm ST (Today!)**
Setting: Kugg is tending to guests and serving drinks. He sometimes whispers something about a raffle..
Word has also spread through The Barrens and Durotar about the upcoming raffle.

Want to be rich in an instant? Well this evening in The Crossroads Inn, Kugg will work for you! Register at sundown for a ticket at a value in silver of your own choosing. The bigger the tickets, the bigger the chance of winning! Winner is announced when the moon is one fist above all of Barren's horizon.

Goblin communication technology has made it possible that friends amongst enemies over the sea and to the north will also contribute to your winnings!

 5% is for The Hand.

 **Stormwind - Bank** [A]
Time: December 19th 5 pm ST (Today!)
Setting: *Robbert Oakheart will be available near the bank to register your raffle tickets! Since a couple of days posters with the following text could be noticed throughout Stormwind:*

 *"Sunday evening another cross-continental Raffle is organized! You can buy your tickets for as much silver as you want. More silver means more chance to win of course. Find Robbert Oakheart near the Stormwind Bank this Sunday to participate."*

 **How does it work? (OOC)**

 - You register for one or more tickets of a certain value. Your name and the value of your ticket will be registered in The Raffle Book. Available for everyone to check. (<https://bit.ly/x-raffle>)
 - At 8 PM ST a function generates a random # between 1 and <max s.>. Is your ticket # closest to the outcome? Congratulations! All money of sold tickets is yours.
 - Pref. IC By exception you may mail Kugg the amount you wish to try your chances with. He will register you in The Raffle Book.

For any remaining questions, please first see the sheet: <https://bit.ly/x-raffle>. It explains a lot.
