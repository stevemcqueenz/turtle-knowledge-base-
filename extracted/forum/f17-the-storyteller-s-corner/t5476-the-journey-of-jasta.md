Jasta is a Troll Headhunter who resides in the barracks in Razor Hill, he roams most of the time in search of adventure and putting in work for the Horde.

(The Journey of Jasta)#1
 **In a Valley somewhere in Durotar**
 -A fleet of Kul'Tiran humans arrived on Durotar after some time of quiet, and they began to threaten the Horde members near their encampment, A hunter from the Darkspear Tribe named Jasta decides to fight for his homeland and people, he begins to undergo the trials of becoming a horde warrior. he is one of his tribe's bravest hunters.
 -Level 3, still facing challenges in the Valley of Trials.

(The Journey of Jasta)#2
 **Durotar & Sen'Jin Village**
 -Jasta completed the Trials and is now on his way to Sen'jin Village to do some quests, He found that fighting demons and other unusual creatures was not as easy as he thought it would be, but nevertheless he persisted through it all. This was Jasta's first time entering the Valley of Trials, so by completing these challenges he learned a lot and improved his skills in combat and he is now prepared to go to his next journey that awaits him in Durotar.
 -Level 6
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/VqtOt3D.png)

(The Journey of Jasta)#3
 **Durotar & Echo Isles**
 -After completing his work in Sen'jin Village, Jasta was informed by Master Gadrin that he was prepared and ready to join the horde troops stationed at Razor Hill for the protection of Durotar. Upon arriving there, Jasta met with high-ranking members of the Razor Hill forces who assigned him a mission to Thunder Ridge, Jasta fought the gigantic thunder lizards, and other dangerous animals through the deserts of Durotar, while going back to Razor Hill he encountered two Kul Tiran Marines near a destroyed keep, he ended up getting attacked but he survived and defeated the humans using his insane aim, despite being unfamiliar with the area, Jasta successfully completed the task without any hard struggles. He then returned to Razor Hill carrying two severed human heads which are the Kul Tiran Marines' heads, the orcs were impressed by his skills and didn't hesitate to put him into their ranks as a ranged soldier.

Since that time when Jasta got accepted in the Razor Hill Horde Troops, he now stayed in the Barracks of Razor Hill, now equipped better, ready for war, and earned the title "Headhunter", which is indicative of his identity and skill as a soldier.
The troops from Razor Hill were then deployed in Echo Isles for an attack on the Kul Tirans.

Jasta and the other troops marched to Echo Isles, where they would've immediately started the attack on some small human camps.
 -Level 10.

They succeeded in the attack as their enemies retreated to their main hold in Anchor's Edge, Some of the troops are injured including Headhunter Jasta, he had slices from blades, and he is now recovering from his wounds at the Barracks in Razor Hill.

(The Journey of Jasta)#4
 **Sent to Barrens and tamed a beast**
 -Headhunter Jasta was sent to Barrens to do some work there for the Horde, but before he left the Barracks in Razor Hill he told the orc commander to never let the humans salvage his people in Sen'Jin Village.

When Jasta arrived at the Barrens, he saw an injured purple raptor and was drawn to it. He approached it cautiously and discovered that it would be valuable to him. he saved the raptor and eventually tamed it.

After saving the raptor, Jasta went out to the wilderness to hunt for food together with the raptor, as they spent time together they formed a bond, after the raptor slaughtered many other beasts, Jasta named the raptor "Killa". The creature now serves as his loyal companion, Jasta had fought with the kolkar centaurs and even killed their leaders, he also took down a tough harpy and climbed almost every mountain in the barrens, and fought again with demonic creatures to accomplish his quests for the Horde.

Jasta contributed a lot of work to the people of crossroads,  which earned him respect from some guards and civilians in crossroads.
 -Level 17

(The Journey of Jasta)#5
 **To Thousand Needles**
 -After being long enough in the Barrens fighting with Quillboars, Thunder Lizards, and Thunder Hawks, Headhunter Jasta feels it is time to move out of the Barrens and explore more lands of Kalimdor.
He aims to gain more experience for future battles against threats that the Darkspear and the Horde face, but before that, he has been through dangerous missions in Stonetalon Mountains and Ashenvale where he bravely fought with the mysterious night elves that live there.

He travels to Thousand Needles, and while Jasta is on his way there, he encountered a Tauren who is seeking help. The Tauren wanted Jasta to kill the dwarves who were destroying their home. Jasta agreed to do so and went to a dig site of dwarves where there were many destructive machines and dwarves who keeps mining and destroying the place. Jasta took them down one by one until he met the commander. Both had a one on one fight and Jasta won! He took the journal and brought it back to the Tauren.

He left the crossroads as an honored one, for everything that he has done for the people of the Horde, and he continues to venture to the Thousand Needles, where he is guided by a spirit's voice.

(Journey of Jasta)#6
 **Back in the Jungle**
 -The spirits whispered to Headhunter Jasta again and gave him a vision, stating that he had been chosen. They said they would reveal themselves to him in due time. He also finished all of his tasks and missions in Thousand Needles, he earned honor from the Taurens for all of his efforts.

Headhunter Jasta returns to his homeland, where he was born,  Stranglethorn Vale. He goes there because he trusts the spirits who whisper to him. He doesn't know who it is but still believes that it is what he feels and thinks is right, so he continues his journey through the vast jungle with many dangerous creatures but he didn't took Killa with him. He meets a group of hunters in the jungle and joins their party on an expedition into a dangerous part of the jungle, they traveled through this vast wilderness filled with dangerous creatures, he proved himself to be an excellent hunter as well as an excellent member of this group of hunters that is led by a dwarf named "Hemet Nesingwary". Eventually, he succeeds by proving himself and succeeding at what they did not expect from him, he was being able to defeat even the most dangerous creatures in the jungle.

Jasta eventually arrived at a Horde base camp, the "Grom'Gol Base Camp". There he found a Troll from his tribe and they began searching for their chieftain's son, Yenniku. Jasta scouted almost every rival tribe's camp but didn't find him yet. The other Trolls in Grom'Gol saw how Jasta infiltrated into his rival's camp and were impressed by his scouting skills. They continued the hunt for Yenniku while Jasta was also looking forward to what would come next in his journey.
 -Level 31

(Journey of Jasta)#7
 **Hunting in the Jungle**
 -Headhunter Jasta spent his time tracking beasts and hunting with a group of hunters in the jungle of Stranglethorn Vale, aiding the horde forces in the Grom'Gol base camp and also tracking the son of the chieftain of his tribe.

After he had found their chieftain's son, Jasta reported his findings back to Grom'Gol. He knew that he needed to train more to help defeating Zan'Zil and his voodoo slaves, so he decided to train more and ended up in Kargath at Blasted lands, where he was sent on another dangerous hunting mission. He earned honor from the warlord in Kargath for all his efforts for them.

At long last, Headhunter Jasta had given his mission in Kargath, he was sent back to Eastern Kingdoms to deliver Neeka Bloodscar's report to the Horde forces in Stonard, Swamp of Sorrows.
He also did many works for the Horde forces in Stonard, he was a great help to them and managed to eliminate any threats against the Horde.

(Journey of Jasta)#8
 **Tracking a lost one from the Sanv Tribe**
 -Now, as the powerful magic charm called the Sanv Charm is retrieved and given back to the one who owned it who's from the Sanv Tribe, the owner of the Sanv Charm who is called Sanv K'la, asked Headhunter Jasta to report to him if he will ever find the lost Sanv Riftseeker named Akh Z'ador.

Akh Z'ador was the one who led the Sanv Draenei tribe to Azeroth. He was one of the few of their kind that knew the magic of their ancient ancestors. He was skilled enough to bring their entire tribe in Azeroth, and that of the Fallow Sanctuary, with the help of the other lesser Riftseekers.
Akh Z'ador attempted to return to their old world in search of more exiles to bring to the Harborage. Sanv K'la suspects something went terribly wrong, he could not detect Akh Z'ador's presence upon their old world with the Sanv Charm. the magic he is using is old, and somewhat unstable. They barely understand its usage, and only the bright minds like Akh Z'ador can manipulate it enough to travel.

Now while Headhunter Jasta is questing around Azeroth, he will do his best to track down Akh Z'ador.
