Astrahe is upon us all as yet another Xallavar passes into the echelons of history. The new year rises along with Elune’s first full zenith, and the Kaldorei celebrate their victory millennia past against the Legion in the War of the Ancients.

While the fallen protector Omen is laid to rest once more outside of Malfurion Stormrage’s Barrow den, his love Tyrande and the Sisterhood of Elune have prepared festivities in Darnassus. However - that isn’t to say Staghelm does not have something in store too! The Sentinel forces of Nightsong have been delegated the task to oversee the event assuring both safety and splendor for all the guests. Join us for…
