Rules of the Game!

The tournament is going to be in two sections: One is going to be your normal duel using the mechanics of the game and the second one using rolls. For the first tournament to make things fair as much as we can is going to be like this:

The first tournament is going to be hosted by brackets of 4 levels, looking like this:

 **10-14 / 15-19 / 20-24 / 25-29 / 30-34 / 35-39/ 40-44 / 45-49 / 50-54 / 55-60**

The tournament is going to be divide between the melee classes and the range/caster classes so is going to be like this:

Mages, Warlocks, Priests And Hunters (Druids/Shamans)
Warriors, Rogues And Paladins (Druids/Shamans)

The hybrid classes like the druid and the shaman can join only one section of the tournament, for example a druid that decides to participate in the range/caster section can't participate in the melee one.

 **PvP Rules:**

 1.- No consumables (Potions, runes, foods and etc)
 2.- No engineer stuff (Trinkets, grenades, etc)
 **3.- The use of bandages is allowed**
 4.- Can't use buffs that are not from your class (Paladins can only have one blessing, the using of potions for buffing is not allowed)
 **5.- Weapon procs are allowed, for example if you weapon have a chance on hit of casting a frostbolt is valid.**
 6.- Each elimination phase is going to consist of only one round. The final duel is going to be the best of 3 rounds.
 7.- In case that only 2 people show for one bracket, the winner is going to be decide by the best of 5 rounds.

The second tournament is much more simple and is going to be like this:

Each duelist is going to /roll and the one that gets a higher number is going to make one hit, the first one to make 3 hits against their rival is going to win. There is no level require to participate in this tournament. It would be recommended that people use /emote each time they try to take a strike for more immersion.

 **Example of how this will play out.**

 *Ashlynn takes a ready stance, holding her sword with both hands. It looks way too heavy for her. With a grunt, and then a yell, she threw herself at Blackmoore, using all her strength to swing her blade down onto Blackmoore's shoulder.*

Ashlynn rolls 12
Blackmoore rolls 15

 **Blackmore 1 / Ash 0**

 *Blackmoore laughed, knowing that Ashlynn hardly knew how to use a sword at all. He casually side stepped, letting Ashlynn's momentum carry her forward and would turn around to face her. Like the cheeky bugger he is, he attempt's to swat Ashlynn's bottom with the flat side of his sword, a cocky grin on his face.*

Blackmoore rolls 17
Ashlynn 3

 **Blackmoore 2 / Ash 0**

 *Ashlynn let out a gasp as she felt the heavy metal hit her, and she turned around, eyes narrowing and face reddening. With another cry, she held the too-heavy sword above her head and charged at Mister Blackmoore, pulling the sword around in an arc to attempt to cut into the man's side.*

In order to save time, regarding /roll dueling, it will be one round per combatant - pitting winners against each other until we finally have a winner!
