Beneath the Catacombs

In a land befouled by winds of evil magics, it is rumored to possess a legendary artifact of great power. Guarded by terrifying monsters and haunting shades, the bodies of those who've sought it litter the canyon pass. Despite the dangers, there comes every now and then adventurers so bold that they do not heed the warnings, but band together to brave the horrors that guard the way to the forgotten tomb. If you are willing to risk life in pursuit of fabled treasures, then seek out Skorgrim in Goldshire to begin this foolhardy quest for power.

## Post 5603 by Kazgrim (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-04-03T21:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5603#p5603 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Skorgrim looked over the rag-tag group of conscripts, letting out a sigh of frustration. "I suppose this will do." He looked each man in the eye, testing to see if their nerve would be enough for the horrors that led ahead. "Alright, move out!"

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/556152826502447129/695738781189275679/WoWScrnShot_040320_152314.jpg)

Skorgrim and his caravan moved through the forests of Elwynn and Duskwood, putting to death bandit and beast that obstructed their way. Just as they prepared to enter the dreaded Deadwind Pass, a foul wind blew past Skorgrim and his warriors. "Brace yourselves, lads. Dark magic flows freely, corrupting this wretched land." He threw his cloak over his head, walking up the shadowy path towards the ominous ivory tower in the distance. As he and his group approached a large ravine, a cloaked figure stood guard with several others He greeted them, and they made their way together down towards the lowlands. "These orcs will guide us to our destination, try not to cause any trouble."

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/556152826502447129/695738781948706846/WoWScrnShot_040320_153545.jpg)

The group made their way to the forgotten tomb. They parked themselves outside the entrance as Skorgrim gathered his explosives. "STAND BACK!" He yelled, as a large bang went off. The iron gate that sealed the tomb blew off its handles, clearing the pathway inside. "Orcs first."

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/556152826502447129/695738784611958836/WoWScrnShot_040320_155043.jpg)

Skorgrim turned to Kazgrim. "A deal's a deal." Said the annoyed Dwarf, out-stretching his hand for payment. Skorgrim stood at the edge of a tunnel that led into a dark abyss with no end in sight. Kazgrim snorted before pushing Skorgrim backwards, his body disappearing into the darkness. "If the rest of you wish to not meet a similar fate, then follow me." He motioned for the others to follow him deeper into the catacombs.

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/556152826502447129/695738786566504478/WoWScrnShot_040320_160538.jpg)

It became apparent that there was no great treasure to be found in this wretched place, only death. As Kazgrim led the group deeper into the crypt, they arrived in a room littered with skulls and bones. At the center of the room was an altar, which Kazgrim stood behind. He motioned for the others to come closer. The group approached the altar, save for the paladin that now held his sword in hand, ready to slay Kazgrim. "Place your hand on this altar, and I shall reward you immensely."

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/556152826502447129/695738789011914812/WoWScrnShot_040320_161125.jpg)

One by one the members of the group placed their hand on the altar, their soul now bound to Kazgrim's dark ritual. A mirror into a dark abyss appeared before them all, and a demon exited from it. It stood higher than them all, its head scraping the ceiling of the catacombs. With one fell swipe of its blade, it severed the body of one of its summoners in half. Kazgrim broke into a terrible laughter. "Kill them all!"

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/556152826502447129/695738790651756636/WoWScrnShot_040320_161150.jpg)

The paladin that stood behind now rushed forward, gripping his sword with a zealous fervor. He and the others fought to slay the terrible beast, sacrificing many wounds in order to do so. When the beast finally fell, they laid exhausted from the long fight, barely able to hold themselves up. Kazgrim, dismayed that his plan did not come to fruition, snarled in anger. He began to cast a spell to destroy them all when Aurrius, the paladin that had stepped forward to save them all, charged Kazgrim, attempting to disrupt his spell. What followed was a fierce duel between the two. Aurrius' strength, however, had waned considerably, and Kazgrim was able to stun him long enough to summon a portal for escape. In the end, Kazgrim fled unscathed, although his plans were foiled. He would need to look elsewhere if he were to summon a great power to wield for his own.
