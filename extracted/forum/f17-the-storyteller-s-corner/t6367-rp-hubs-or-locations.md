In my experience, it seems like this server doesn't have "RP hubs" anymore.  In the month that I've been playing (again), I've seen one roleplay event in Loch Modan which involved some NPCs.  I think there was a meeting in the Dwarven District not too long ago, too, because *Turtle RP* displayed a cluster of dots over there for a good long while.

As for "in the wild" RP?  I've seen people walking about in major cities and the like.  Buuut, I've also seen people who were supposedly in-character (again, according to *Turtle RP*) running around, hopping about, and generally acting like "normal" players.  I also see a lot of people using *Turtle RP* without actually filling it out.  Very depressing.

I'm not saying wild roleplay doesn't happen.  I've seen it happen in the past.  But, for the most part, though, it seems like people just play the game normally despite their "RP status", then line up for specific events at specific times.  Again, very depressing.  ):
Not as depressing as the absolutely dead RP servers on *Classic WoW* after Season of Mastery was over, though.  Hee hee.

Unfortunately, and also quite nonsensically, 80% to 90% of event announcements are made on a dedicated RP discord, and sometimes on Twow's main discord, with little or no in-game announcement. There's really no point in reading chat channels like /RP because hardly anyone says anything or announces anything anywhere (whereas a simple copy and paste wouldn't kill anyone).

You'll find quality RP, and some really nice and friendly guilds. And in the almost 10 months I've been playing, I've definitely never come across anyone nasty. And yet: zero cooperation between the guilds has resulted in just a bit of RP from time to time in public at Stormwind: Mission impossible to get as many people as possible to discover RP or just to create a nice hub open to all. It's just incomprehensible from my point of view.
