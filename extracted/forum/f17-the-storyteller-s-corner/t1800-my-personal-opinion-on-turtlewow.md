It’s always exciting to see a server grow and improve so much over time! Your enthusiasm is contagious, and it’s clear this server has addressed many of the concerns from its launch. Here’s a breakdown of what stands out:

Major Improvements: In just a year, the server has undergone significant changes, refining the experience and making it much more enjoyable for players.
Slower Leveling Pace: The deliberate slow leveling rate seems to be a highlight for you, capturing the grindy, nostalgic feel of classic WoW.
Custom Quests: The addition of custom quests is a huge plus. Encountering the first one so early (around level 4 or 5) adds a refreshing twist while still staying true to the authentic World of Warcraft experience.
Addressing Community Feedback: The server seems to be listening to players, tackling complaints like "not enough quests to hit 60" by adding meaningful content without straying from the game’s roots.
It sounds like this server has really hit its stride, blending custom content with WoW’s original spirit to create a unique and rewarding experience. Here’s to more great adventures ahead!
