There is nothing additive to the download as the Turtles, in their wisdom, wanted people to be able to get their own download, rather than have to host it themselves and open themselves up for legal liability.

That being said, there are several recommended add-ons and tweaks linked to in these forums. If you are an RPer, I would suggest your first step is to get MyRP, which flags you as an RPer to other RPers. There is a working link in the addons section of the forums.

While it is true that open RP isn't super common, it does happen, you just have to be in the right place. You are correct, though, in that mostof it goes on inside of guilds. Guilds sometimes have Guild Houses, structures built inside of the game to represent the guild hall of a guild. I know of two off-hand. One in Darkshire and one in Deadwind Pass, but I know there is more. The Admins do not create these willy-nilly, but only for guilds who stick around a while and have RP ideas on why they should warrant a bunch of work on the GM's part.

Being a player from the BC era, I do not find the tons of RP in the Stormwind Park I found pre-cataclysm (or Blue Recluse Post-cata), but the population here is nowhere near that of a live server. IMO, it is what you make of it.

## Post 6825 by Illyane (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-07-12T08:42:00Z

It is less about things I would like to see happening than means left at the disposition of roleplayers. The main problem with an RP community is that it literally drains time from any game master, staff member or server administrator who would want administrate it or organise events. It's fine to organise things, but you can't be everywhere, you can't watch everything, and you can't organise everything. So you need to put on place tools to facilitate the RP without obliterate your free time, or destroying the balance of the server (like how god mod or spawning artefacts can without control). That's where you need to consider the infrastructure that can offer the server. Literally, if you put a road and the signalization, everybody will use it without much question.

What I mean by that is that the server offer many possibilities to facilitate the process of formation of an active RP community. Actually, the community is already active for what I can observe (it seems that there are dozens of RP players here, that already a large community by my standards, well for a BFA standard that's it), but for newcomers and newbies, the integration can only be slow and perhaps annoying. (I need to install Add-on, I need to find by luck a canal, try again my luck to find an active roleplayer, I don't know who is a roleplayer or not, I don't know in game if there are any event currently in place, I need to make long researches, I need to...).

For an RP community to be more dynamic, we need in my opinion mainly five points : (1) First is to offer a clear choice to anybody to become a roleplayer, (2) Second is to know who is an ACTIVE roleplayer, (3) Third is to rend easy the contact between roleplayers, and between creators and roleplayers, (4) Fourth is to facilitate the advertising of community events and deeds, (5) Fifth is to increase the global "immersion". This server offer game mechanics that can have their use in the dynamization of an RP community.

 **I. Example : The magnificent Glyph system.**
When we create a character here, we receive a gentle turtle that propose us in-game shop as well as a hardcore levelling glyph. This amazing glyph system can propose RP modifiers too:

To answer (1), (2) and partially (3) and (4) : Communication, finding and meeting.
Instead of forcing people to install add-on and to literally "work" to be able to just create an only RP profile, how about a glyph that activate pre-installed (visible, but non-active) Add-on (my rp and stuff) ? The player who choose to take the glyph from Turtle will be recognize as an RP player for a register (It is possible to see on the website how many players are actually connected, you can show with that how many roleplayers are currently ingame), and will join two canals, one OORP to meet the community, another one RP where announcements and advertisings can be done by creators who want to make aware other of rumours, recruitment campaigns, RP quest, Tavern and other casual events (there is nothing like that for now).  This glyph can actually help to reference an "annular" page on the website (like : active roleplayers).

To answer (3) : Meeting, guilds and events :
[1] A "RP Guild master Glyph", where an RP player can form a guild, and automatically be referenced as an active guild master for an RP project. Any person who would like to search for an RP guild would just have to see a webpage of Turtle to see all of the projects referenced (like he should be able to see how many roleplayer there is on the server). When a guy choose this glyph, he is invited to create a presentation topic of his guild on the forum.
[2] A "RP meeting Glyph", who permits any roleplayer to join with a special hearthstone an RP tavern (like a dimensional one open by a strange magus). Anybody who just want to RP without putting to much on it will just have to use it to meet the others. Et voilà, you don't have to organize yourself everyday events, just have a horde and alliance vendors of drinks and foods (and even the possibility for anybody to obtain a "glyph of staff" to work here as a server or a bartender).

To answer (5) : Immersion :
Our chance is that Vanilla is already pretty immersive. The mobs are violent, the quests simple, in fact, the PVE/PVP system is pretty brutal. The need resides again in the "graphic" immersion, and the possibility to make you hero "more human".
[1] A "Glyph of RP immersion", that activate another pre-installed Add-on (but non-activated), Darker nights, and if possible a thing that make look the weather events more "huge". Like when there is a tempest in Stranglethorn, the rain must be strong as f*ck (for the last one, about weather, I think it will be more difficult).
[2] A "Glyph of effort", that limit by example running in city spaces (the ideal should be everywhere, but that's asking to much). Running could be a competences for roleplayers scaled on stamina (the more you have stamina, the longer you can run).

Etc. It's possible to make even a "Total RP" glyph, a hardcore RP levelling mod that change the way the player is supposed to play. (He can't run all he wants, He need to sleep on a bed in the tavern/tent to be rested, he needs to eats every 4 hours -hence malus-, to drink every hours, he needs to tend his wounds if his hp are less than 10%) and all that was show before. A way of making the game and pexxing even slower, but that give "recompenses" like the hardcore way of levelling do.

With the glyph system that's already in place and so less complicated I guess to modify, you can increase any the dynamic around an RP community, letting the choice without forcing the hand (like the hardcore levelling system doesn't make the hardcore leveller "better" or anything in comparison with another, the "hardcore RP player" will just be a type of roleplayer that will find a gameplay up to his tastes). You can like that generate a type of RP that is similar to the one we can see on any other blizzard server, to be safely away from the trap that are private RP-GM servers, but still offering more tools that can complete the offer turtle is trying to put in place. This is just an example, one that can increase the attractiveness of this server as an RP hub, without to much disturbing the PVE and asking to much time monitoring it. I didn't approach the skin shop and stuff because that's more about tastes and visions than community dynamic.

 **II. Other examples :**

Other things are about communication : In my opinion, we miss a thing between the main website and the forum. There should be more specific pages, one who presents the server and his features, one about the quality of the PVE system, one about the RP (how he is played, what are the tricks to know… etc.) and not just links to forum threads. But that’s purely marketing and communication grumbles on my part. I’ve found a little bit long to search everything on the forum, discord and in-game just to be able to understand how is supposed to work the RP here.

Last point is about Charters : Everybody seems nice here, so it didn’t seem immediately a necessity, but the more this cool server will be known, the more we will need a charter from turtle to protect the immersion, the ambiance. Like, If you aren’t a Roleplayer, breaking the immersion of them is punishable (for an example).

That’s what I mean by “infrastructure”, basic in-game structures that need work to be put in place, but that reduce  the workload after. If you have this type of things in place, you will have less worries about a need to “offer” events to the community because if tools like the ones aforementioned before were implemented, if the community die, it will be only her own fault. After that, everything will be a bonus, a kind a happy gift for the community by the staff. The apparition of a giant nerubian in the eastern plagueland ? Just a narrative text presenting the threat on the website *(five lines : "[...] The evil hidden in Naxxramas has unleashed a new threat against the mortal [...]")*, an immersive announcement in-game (a town crier?), et voilà, it’s in the lore that there is a giant nerubian.  :)
