Well leveling talents and DPS talents aren't gonna be the same. For levelling I'd grab improved conc shot. If you're always at range, I'd grab 5/5 predatory aspects for both leveling and DPS. Improved arcane shot and improved hunter's mark are kinda shit, and endless quiver is really really good. Serpent sting falls off with gear since it only scales with spell damage, not ranged attack power, so once you have good gear you should remove those points to put em into something else, imo.

I also dont find efficiency very useful for leveling. Just drink more, 5 points are too valuable.
