Thread.

For a damage class, I think we slack a bit too much behind. I don't know if it's normal / because of bugs but we're lacking the numbers for a class that offers nothing but damage and in one spec, a party buff.

The shooting animation makes us too static, multi shot requiring stationary position while it's an "instant" is dumb, the out of combat limitation for traps usage out of the survival spec is dumber, aimed shot is useless, talents offer too little bang for the bucks, our scaling with gear is overall wrong.

This needs to be fixed.
