# Hunter's redesign and changes after 1.18

## Post 149974 by Skurridizo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-03T00:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149974#p149974 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I made these talents with some changes

[https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c ... points=F--](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator?t0=HaEkvLEBZ-&t1=zFMhDOS9rv&t2=ppvJCaxG93&class=4&points=F--)

I'm going to explain the main issues I've seen the hunter has, and how I solved them.

Beast Mastery:

> Pet with low survivability: Buffed and combined Improved Mend Pet + Improved Eyes of the Beast + Improved Revive Pet. Also, there is a new talent providing 2% Vampirism to the pet.
>
>  You have to play Marksman as a second tree and rely on the Steady Shot rotation: Now it’s possible to play BM melee due to some changes in the early Survival talent tree.
>
>  Not much versatility, as there are only 3 useful options for pets: scorpids, wind serpents, or cobras. This isn’t included in the talent tree, but I will detail them later.

Marksmanship:

> Low AoE damage: Spell power buffs for Volley and an extra effect for Scorpid Sting that allows AoE damage.
>
>  Arcane Shot and Aimed Shot are useless: I added a nice combination effect so you can use one of them each time.
>
>  Not many possibilities to combine your talent tree: going deep MM, splashing into BM, or even SV if you want to focus on PvP.
>
>  Not much burst and lacking a real sniper feeling: The new ability Sniper Focus makes your timing and decision-making crucial to when you want to burst.
>
>  Not top DPS nor a supportive spec: Added a new support ability to make it more useful, along with higher-damage talents.

Survival:

> No mobility: Added an extra 5% speed to Lightning Reflexes.
>
>  Bad tree to splash talents into as a secondary tree: Now it’s possible to get +100% extra melee attack power from agility earlier, and another talent reduces minimum range to 0 yards. Feel free to play full melee BM or a hybrid melee-ranged hunter.
>
>  Traps are very clunky in certain situations. In many boss fights, you have to hit at max melee range and lose a large percentage of your total damage: Added a radius expansion for traps if they are not triggered instantly, to increase damage, and also a new mechanic if you want to min/max your traps.

Pet Changes:

> Claw hits 3 targets (like the Druid’s Swipe).
>
>  Claw attack power ratio increased from 1 damage per 42 Attack Power to 1 damage per 35 Attack Power.
>
>  Gorilla’s Thunderstomp cooldown reduced to 10 seconds.

I’m suggesting minor buffs to different identities. Altogether, these changes should allow hunters to match top-tier DPS classes and bring more versatility to both PvE and PvP.

Probably all tiers including 2.5 and above need some changes to scale properly with the 3 specs

Hello, mon. I received your letter with a request to write my comments. I looked at the talent calculator and must say that you did a good job.
Let me share my experience of playing on non-standard hunter builds, as you asked.
 1. I tried to build a viable melee BM. In the current version, his damage was quite average for the following reasons:
 - Due to the fact that 100% agility in MAP is too deep in the survival tree, I had to neglect this talent and collect items not with agility but with AP. Due to the fact that almost all items that enhance your pet, with built-in agility, I greatly lost damage in close combat.
You solved this problem by raising the talent on MAP higher, and also allows you to save talent points on the mandatory Frenzy. And in this build you need a lot of talent points because you need to strengthen not only the pet but also your close combat.

Another big problem with the build is that you don't have good and stable AoE. You tried to solve this problem by adding a 3 targets to the claws and improving the scorpion sting. These are very good changes. However, it is not clear what to do if your pet has a bite, or if you have a talent that applies a serpent sting with attacks, which interrupts the scorpion sting.

 2. I made not bad(I hope) SPD hunter who fights in close combat. Of course, it is weaker than top tier pumping builds, but still not bad. As I understand it, thanks to this build you noticed me.

 - The main problem with SPD hunter (close or ranged) is that there are very few hybrid items that would contain both SP and agility. For some of them you literally have to fight with shamans, because originally, these are their items. And thus you are faced with the problem of lack of damage. You solved this problem by adding a talent to convert part of the RAP into SP.

Overall, you've done a great job. In fact, everything looks so good that I'm starting to think that some of your talents are too strong (for example, the talent that removes the minimum shooting distance)

I hope my experience and my review will be useful to you, and thank you for paying attention to an ordinary player from the server.
