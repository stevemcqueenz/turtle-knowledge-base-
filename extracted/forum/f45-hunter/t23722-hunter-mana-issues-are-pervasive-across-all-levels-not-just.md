Hunter is a martial class. While levelling they have really good dps output from just autoshots and their pet doing the heavy lifting. This is intentional.

Your spells comparatively are not adding that much damage for a somewhat pricy bit for your manapool.

But you aren't a pure caster class. You can simply wear a bit of spirit gear and shoot the occasional baddy without the use of spells to just regain the mana. Playing hunter is a lesson in understanding the 5 second rule. Every time you pay mana, for the next seconds you will not regain any mana (except for mp5)

So bundle your spells into bursts, and then just use your potent autoattacks for a bit when you need to regen.
