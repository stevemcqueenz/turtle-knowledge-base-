# Remove Hunter Carve Ability

## Post 108139 by Neechy (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-16T15:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108139#p108139 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Why is no one talking about the abomination that is the new Carve ability?

Is it fun to use? Sure.
Is it authentic to hunter class fantasy? Absolutely not.
Why is a hunter (the only RANGED physical class) hitting more targets with one move than say a warrior, the quintessential close combat juggernaut tank class.

Does someone have a sound argument to justify why Carve was added and why it should stay in the game? It was silly when added in SoD and it is silly here. (And please don't say "because Rexxar had Carve!!" - you can't design a whole class over one outlier who is a hero character in a RTS game...)

I have reviewed pretty much all the player sentiments on class changes so far. While I agree with some players who would have preferred bare minimum class changes, I can also agree with TWoW's decision to make melee hunters more viable. I think it's not a bad idea for Survival to be melee focused (though ideally should still preserve some range functionality so as to clearly differentiate it from other melee classes like warriors).

That said, you can have a strong and fun-to-play melee hunter without completely jumping the shark by adding an ability like Carve. The ridiculous thing about Carve is not just the amount of targets it hits and the short CD, but also that it is only 3 rows deep in the Survival tree, meaning literally all hunter specs, including BM or MM can now dip into Survival to become ridiculous AoE machines.

From a class in Vanilla wow and even all throughout Wotlk+ that was loved for its unique physical ranged class identity using its pets and traps to stay a distance to now just becoming some AoE tank abomination, this change just feels like a massive overstep.

What even is the logistics being Carve supposed to be? We are supposed to be believe some ranged hunter who also happens to be skilled in close combat just whips out his big weapon and with a "sweeping attack" hits 5 targets, which is more than any other class can hit with a weapon strike? Why are big bad warriors hitting less targets with their cleaves? The fantasy behind this just makes no sense.

My suggestion?
If melee hunter is here to stay, then give it a cleave ability that is MUCH DEEPER in the Survival tree, to demonstrate that its mastery of melee combat has allowed it to now hit multiple targets at once. But DON'T make it 5, that is just ridiculous. Just make it a regular cleave in the vein of hunters and rogues. Survival tree shouldn't be AoE, it should just be standard melee with a heavy focus on trap utility to make it interesting and unique.

Apologies for the rant, but as we all know this update was pretty extreme, and seeing abilities like Carve added just worry me about the direction the server is going.

I applaud the developers for their hard work and their passion, I just hope that they can make flavour changes without vastly overhauling class identities. Interested to hear others thoughts on this as well. Cheers.   hiding_smth_turtle_head

While I truly appreciate the middle ground we’ve been able to find; after thinking on it more, I’ll be frank in admitting I don’t think what you describe is “hassle”, it sounds more like the spec playing like how it was always meant to be played. Whether or not that strategy would over-perform should come down to number tuning, but I feel like a Survival Hunter who chooses to ignore over half their kit to go full-melee *should* sacrifice some of that extra damage in exchange for the convenience of not running around switching between Shots and Strikes.

I’ll say again that the situation you’ve laid out doesn’t sound like a hassle, it genuinely sounds more fun and engaging than being forced to stay in either melee or range. I think we’d really be losing the plot once we start treating the specs’ originally-designed playstyles as a problem to be fixed, Hunter was always supposed to be a ranged/melee hybrid and Survival is supposed to encapsulate that hybrid aspect of the class. I think melee-only Hunter has a place in the game as a niche side-build, but I don’t think you should ever have to pick between going all-in for one or the other as Survival. I honestly believe having the option to use both would free the spec more than limit it.
