Not a direct answer, but if you feed your pet, you will see the buff in your combat log (so not general chat) increasing the pet's happiness.

Depending on the level of the food (the level of the zone you can find the food in as an enemy drop) and the level of the pet, it will increase either by 35, by 17, or by 8, every 2 seconds for 20 seconds, totaling 350, 170, or 80 happiness. At a given moment, your pet will be full and eating food won't increase happiness anymore. You can still feed a pet though. So, look closely at your combat log!

The buff disappears after 20 seconds, or when your pet enters combat, whichever happens first.

There are 6 ranks of Loyalty. Higher Loyalty means your pet can stays happy longer without topping up food. Loyalty has an xp component and a time component. To gain a new rank of Loyalty, your pet needs to gain combat xp worth about 5% of your xp bar; and some time needs to pass. 0 minutes to reach Loyalty 2, 30 minutes in Loyalty 2 to reach Loyalty 3, and so on...

Happiness will drop over time.

 - Standing around doing nothing (like in cities) makes it drop, reported to be 50 happiness over 6 minutes.
 - Dismissing makes it lose 50 happiness, but then you can stand around for as long as you want without a further penalty.
 - Walking out of range of your pet makes it lose 10 happiness. This includes using the Stay command while you walk away, and is often used in place of the Dismiss Pet button because of the reduced happiness loss.
 - Mounting does disappear your pet, but it does not reduce happiness.
 - When your pet dies, reports say it loses 350 happiness.

You can also look for WoW Classic for information. Lastly, [petopia](<https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic/>) is your friend, even if it doesn't include foxes and serpents (TurtleWoW-only families). (
