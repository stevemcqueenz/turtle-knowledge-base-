Currently, the enchant from Sapphiron is undoubtedly best-in-slot.  Armor penetration does not yield good returns generally, so is largely ignored, outside of warrior sunders on bosses and the AQ40 trinket.

Haste, however, is tricky.  It has a multiplicative effect on current stats, so can be very effective when stacked in coordination with end-game gear.  From my own spreadsheet calculations, 2% Haste edges out 30AP in full T3, but can be worse with lesser gear.
