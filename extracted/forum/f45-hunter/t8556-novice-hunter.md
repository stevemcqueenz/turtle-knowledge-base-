## Post 55429 by Borefficz (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-01T06:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55429#p55429 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Owl/eagle/bat is the best pet for leveling simply due to Screech generating a disgusting amount of single target threat so you can go all out whenever you need to and not get aggro on mobs. On top of that Screech generates AoE threat for all mobs in pet's melee range which allows you to cleave with Multishot and Mend Pet if needed without pulling aggro.
Many people recommend a boar, but while it's a good endgame PvP pet due to how Charge works, its threat generation is poor - boars have a damage penalty, and while Charge makes your pet's first attack hit a little harder, the lower damage multiplier (90% for boars while cats have 110% and owls/bats have 107%) catches up quickly.
To be fair early on your pet choice doesn't really matter as pets have stupidly OP damage at low levels so you won't have issues with threat, also owls don't get Screech until level 16. However, once you're in your 50s, you're going to wish you got an owl. Source: leveled two hunters, first one with a cat, second one with an eagle, the difference was huge.
