# Aspect of the Wolf - thrown weapons

## Post 55571 by Azuredrak0 — 2023-08-02T15:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55571#p55571 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Not here to argue the viability of Melee Hunters in current meta.
Wanted to float a suggestion that might sound appealing to community members that choose to play a melee oriented hunter.

Wouldn't it be great is Aspect of the Wolf provided an exception to "melee only" rule in allowing the use of Thrown Weapons?

A few interesting facts about thrown weapons and Aspect of the Wolf:
- Aspect of the Wolf provides 155 AP to compensate for prohibition of using ranged attacks

- Thrown weapons for the most part have no stats, and they do have ammunition
Ranged abilities that can be used when thrown weapon is equipped
- Auto-Shot

- Multi-shot(Rank 1)

- Rapid Fire

- Scorpid Sting

- Viper Sting

- and interestingly Wyvern Sting
Would be nice to see the list of allowed abilities with thrown weapons to be expanded to include the Distracting Shot, (and possibly even Serpent Sting and Arcane Shot).  Also as stated above, for these abilities to be allowed in Aspect of the Wolf.

For lovers of Rexxar - whozaaa!
