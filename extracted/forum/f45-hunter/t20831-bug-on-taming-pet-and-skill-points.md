## Post 144716 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-12T06:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144716#p144716 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Your pet gets 1 training point per loyality level per level.
So at loyality 6 (best friend) and level 60 you have 300.

At low level and low loyality you have none or almost none of these.
But your pet innately knows either claw or bite. Thats costs points to know. Which is taken away from your points. Since you have less training points than what your pet has in abilities you are negative.

All working as intended. Just feed your pet well and loyality (and points) will rise

## Post 144776 by Hctwowfan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-08-12T10:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144776#p144776 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Formula to calculate pet's TP:
Pet level x (Loyalty - 1) - cost of pet skills = remaining TP

Max pet:
 60 x (6 - 1) = 300 tp

Your pet:
 10 x (1 - 1) - 4 = -4 tp

once it increases loyalty (assuming same level pet):
 10 x (2 - 1) - 4 = 6 tp

Pet loyalty only increases if you keep your pet above unhappy AND only increases if the pet gains xp. So standing around in town and keeping your pet happy won't increase its loyalty, because the pet doesn't gain any xp.
