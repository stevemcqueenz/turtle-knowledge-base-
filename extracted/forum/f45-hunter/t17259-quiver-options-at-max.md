## Post 117859 by FrankFankledank (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-01-25T02:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117859#p117859 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The state of hunter quiver choice at max level is pretty grim.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/LCdwtzD.png)

There are some really neat models there for them, but stuck behind a wall of limited inventory slots and straight-up losing a decently vital stat in ranged attack speed, at least for non-Survivals.

If adding quivers to the transmogrification UI is too much a hassle, then I would suggest at least adding a vendor who can be made available after finishing the Sinew-Wrapped Lamina quest who sells copies of the quiver that use the other models in the database.
