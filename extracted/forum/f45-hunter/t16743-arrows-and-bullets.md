Let me recontextualize this for you:

The biggest quiver in the game offers less than half as much space as the largest regular bag. You are not only using 16 slots for ammo, but also the loss of regular bag slots from the incredibly prevalent 18-22 slotters now littered in raiding content, if not the massive 20 slot loss from not using a turtle shop bag.

Vanilla philosophy generally has specialized bags either matching the highest available regular bag size but with much easier acquirement, or a larger size. A Warlock's soul bag can go as high as 28 slots in core Vanilla, where 18 slots were the absolute pinnacle. Warlocks (who had multiple buffs to their Soul Shard stacking) can also restock their "ammo" in any similarly-levelled zone, whereas a Hunter needs to go to one of two very specific boss fights to get their top-tier ammo, which their best quiver can only hold about 3 runs' worth at a time. The cash shop ammo vendor doesn't change this, it doesn't even offer the second (AV quartermaster ammo) or third (Engineer-crafted Thorium ammo) best types, only basic white vendor stuff.
