I will put this as simply as i can and will not tire you with technicals but keep it generic.

The recent insignia changes have drastically impacted my experience as a Hunter in PvP. These changes introduced a mechanic borrowed from a later expansion that allows players to break out of crowd control more frequently. While this may sound like a quality-of-life improvement, it fundamentally alters the dynamics of Vanilla PvP in ways that undermine class roles and balance.

I understand that your first thought might be that this is an overreaction but in my opinion this affects and impacts the hunter class more than any other class.

In Vanilla PvP, crowd control was a key aspect of strategy, with each class leveraging its unique toolkit to contribute to a team’s success. Hunters, in particular, relied on their ability to kite, control enemies with traps, and deal sustained damage from range. The original insignia design had a cooldown that required strategic use, creating windows of opportunity where Hunters could capitalize on their strengths. However, with the new insignia changes, crowd control effects are frequently negated, drastically reducing the effectiveness of Hunter traps and kiting strategies. This not only diminishes the Hunter’s role as a disruptor and ranged threat in group PvP but also makes the class feel less impactful overall.

In a PvP group setting, Hunters often filled a versatile role, providing utility and damage while controlling the battlefield with traps and zoning tools. With crowd control so easily countered, Hunters are now pushed toward a more generic damage-dealing role, which not only feels uninspired but also overlaps with other ranged classes like Mages or Warlocks, who bring comparable damage along with additional utility. This loss of identity has made playing a Hunter in PvP far less enjoyable and rewarding.

Hunter is the class that i wanted to play and PvP the aspect i want to experience, those are simply not enjoyable anymore, so i prefer to quit TWOW overall.

Goodbye, have fun.
