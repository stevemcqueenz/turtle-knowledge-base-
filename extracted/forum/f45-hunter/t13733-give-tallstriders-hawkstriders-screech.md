# Give Tallstriders/Hawkstriders Screech

## Post 93436 by Mistermadigan — 2024-04-24T20:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93436#p93436 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Let it be known that I have always loved the Tallstrider pet family, they are plucky, looked down upon in the meta, and hated for the sounds they make in combat.

It is that last point I wish to highlight.  Because I have been in many parties where members have said they had to turn down their audio because my pet was too loud.  This makes me wonder - why don't Tallstriders have the Screech ability?

Compare to Carrion Birds/Eagles/Owls which make their cute little hooting noises.  Like in real life Eagles chirp - the stereotypical sound of eagles that you hear in movies/tv is actually a recording of Hawks.

So, while yes - giving Tallstriders/Hawkstriders Screech would be a change to the mechanics - I think it makes a lot of sense for biological reasons as well as in-game reasons.
