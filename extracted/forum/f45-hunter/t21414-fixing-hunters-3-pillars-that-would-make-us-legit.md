# Fixing Hunters: 3 Pillars That Would Make Us Legit

## Post 148457 by Sikarii — 2025-08-26T19:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148457#p148457 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Look, I love Hunters, been playin em since forever, but let’s be real: on Turtle WoW right now we’re looking like second-class citizens. Pets flop over in raids, Marksman is just sitting there for Trueshot Aura, and Survival? Half the time it dont even know what it wants to be.

 **Beast Mastery**

The Problem: Pets do scale, but it feels weak and underwhelming. BM lost the punch of Bestial Wrath and ended up with a filler ability that does not sell the fantasy or DPS identity.

The Fix:

Stronger Scaling: Pet inherits more of your AP, stamina, and crit. Gearing yourself directly gears your beast in a way that feels impactful.

Pack Leader (new CD): 2 min cooldown. While you and your pet are attacking the same target, both gain +25% damage and +20% attack speed for 12s. Pet heals for 3% of its damage done. This restores the old Bestial Wrath feeling while keeping it synergy-based.

Keeper of Beasts: Unlocks Pet Gear Slots. 3 bonded slots (Collar, Hide, Charm) that act like relics or enchants for pets. Example: Collar = +crit, Hide = +resists, Charm = reduced focus costs. Optional but rewarding for min-maxers.

Capstone Choice:

The Beast Master (burst fantasy): 5 min cooldown. Summons spectral versions of every pet family (cat, wolf, bear, boar, raptor, owl, etc.) for 10s to unleash a flurry of unique attacks. A true “call the pack” moment that delivers BM’s signature burst.

Master of the Pack (raid utility): Unlocks the ability to have two active pets. You must tame and train a second pet from scratch, level it up, and manage both. The second pet deals reduced damage (say 60–70% of a main pet) but brings full utility (Growl, Screech, Howl, etc.). This makes BM more valuable in group content with extra buffs, debuffs, and tanking tools.

End result: BM becomes the definitive pet spec. Stronger scaling makes pets feel rewarding, Pack Leader gives a synergy-based burst window, Keeper of Beasts unlocks deeper customization, and players get to choose between two distinct capstones — flashy burst through ghostly beasts or steady utility with dual companions.

 **Marksman**

The Problem: Marksman feels like a Trueshot Aura bot with clunky burst and weak personal damage. Piercing Shots is especially bad because it forgoes on-the-fly CC — a ticking DoT prevents you from landing Scatter Shot or Freezing Trap setups, leaving you with less control in PvP. The spec also lacks the Ranger identity of using specialized ammunition.

The Fix:

Elemental Quiver (new core mechanic): Buff your arrows like a Rogue uses poisons. Each buff applies to arrow stacks individually, so you must re-apply when swapping types.

Fire Arrows: Add fire damage on hit, with a small AoE splash.

Poison Arrows: Apply a stacking nature DoT.

Frost Arrows: Slow target movement speed, but with a drawback: while Frost Arrows are active, your Aimed Shot and Multi-Shot deal 15% less damage. This makes the snare strong for PvP kiting but balances it against raw DPS.

Replace Piercing Shots: Instead of an uncontrollable DoT, give MM a reactive proc mechanic tied to its signature shots:

Aimed Shot or Multi-Shot crits can reset Concussive Shot’s cooldown or make Serpent Sting instant and free.

Alternatively, procs could make your next Aimed Shot instant but reduced in damage.

This preserves control while rewarding crit-based play.

Double Tap (rework): Passive effect. Auto Shots have a small chance to reset the cooldown of your next ranged ability. This smooths out rotations and avoids clunky on-use stacking.

Capstone Choice:

The Ranger (selfish DPS): Permanently dismiss your pet. Gain +20% ranged attack power, +10% crit chance, and +15% damage on Aimed and Multi-Shot. Aimed Shot crits deal +20% bonus damage.

End result: MM becomes a true Ranger. Elemental Quiver gives ammo depth, Piercing Shots is replaced with a controllable proc system, Frost Arrows have a built-in tradeoff for balance, and you can choose to become a selfish sniper with no pet. PvE gets a competitive DPS option, PvP gets control and versatility without breaking CC balance.

 **Survival**

The Problem: Survival is supposed to be the scrappy, dirty-fighting spec, but right now it has no clear identity. Lacerate is clunky and unrealistic since fights aren’t designed around “sides,” and Hunters Mark is wasted when you’re mostly swinging in melee. The tree needs a clearer melee focus with a capstone that feels worth it.

The Fix:

Aspect of the Predator (new stance): Replaces Hawk/Monkey. Reduces ranged weapon range to 16–20 yards and increases melee attack power by +25%. Unlocks Survival-only melee abilities. Cannot be swapped while in combat, forcing commitment.

Weakened Predator (debuff): When you leave Predator, you gain a 15s debuff: -15% melee AP, -15% ranged AP, -10% movement speed. Prevents stance-dancing mid-fight, even with Feign Death.

Hunted Debuff: Replaces Hunters Mark for Survival. Hunted increases all melee damage you deal to the target by 10%. Reinforces your melee playstyle and adds group value.

Raptors Frenzy (new passive): Each time you crit with a melee ability, gain a stack of Raptors Frenzy for 10s (max 3). Each stack increases the damage of your Survival-only melee abilities by 5%. At 3 stacks, your next Survival melee ability also refreshes Hunted. Creates a ramping damage loop that rewards uptime and crit builds.

Hunters Net: 20s cooldown, roots a target in melee for 3s.

Lacerate Rework: Instead of awkward positional damage, becomes a straightforward melee bleed that stacks up to 2 times. Keeps the flavor but makes it functional in real fights.

Capstone — Ambush Predator:

 2 min cooldown. Enter stealth for up to 6s (movement not broken by stealth). Your first melee ability out of stealth stuns the target for 1.5s and applies Hunted, making them take +10% melee damage from you for 6s.

Gives Survival its own assassin-style opener for PvP and PvE, rewarding timing and positioning without being a teleport or leap.

End result: Survival is no longer half-baked ranged filler. It becomes a committed melee-control Hunter with bleeds, roots, and burst windows through Ambush Predator. PvE gets a bruiser who can contribute utility, and PvP gets a sticky predator who trades range for control and disruption.
