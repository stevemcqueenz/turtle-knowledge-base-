# What if we remake Survival as a spell power hunter meta?

## Post 58709 by Keds (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-23T16:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58709#p58709 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Forget about Moongose strike, forget about Raptor Strike.

Here we are talking about powerfull traps, powerfull Arcane shots, powerfull stings!!

Move Improved Serpent Sting, Scorpid Sting to the Survival tree, merge then into one singue talent, move Improved Arcane Shot, make it stings scale more with spell power, increase trap damage scale too, Wyvern Sting now actually does damage.

Remove Aspect of the Wolf and create a new one:
> **Aspect of the Raven**
>  The hunter takes on the aspect of the Raven, reducing it's Attack Power by 60% but increases it's Spell power by 50% of the reduced attack power. In addition the hunter gains 4% maximum mana every 3 sec, and traps can be used in combat

New Talent way down Survival tree:

> **Arcane Arrows**
>  Your Multi Shot now fires Arcane Shots instead

The gameplay style would be something like melee weaving, but more like trap weaving, you'll get close, put a trap down, go on ranged attacks to apply serpent sting, arcane shots and the now new "Multi Arcane Shots"
