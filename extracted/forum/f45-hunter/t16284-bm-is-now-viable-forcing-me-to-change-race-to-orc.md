Like, you can swap

Cannibalize from Undead.
Command from Orc.
Regeneration from Troll.
+5% Health from Tauren.

and for Alliance:
Human Spirit from Human
Dodge from Night Elves
Treasure Finding from Dwarves
+%5 Intellect from Gnomes

I see turtle took a right step in this direction with the Ancient Warfare Text quests. I hope we can do something similar for these minor racials.
