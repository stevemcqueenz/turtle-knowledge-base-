AA>Steady>Multi>AA>... with crossbows, AA>Multi/Steady>AA>... with faster weapons. Steady Shot(it was Trueshot pre CC2) damage is normalized to 2.8 weapon speed. So Auto Shots dealt more damage with slower weapons. Steady Shot's damage got buffed in CC2 while Auto Shot remained the same. That's why we had to pay attention to not clip Auto Shots. It is still the case but not that much than it was before. To keep MM performance on the same level where it was, Auto Shot's damage should have been buffed alongside Steady Shot's damage.
