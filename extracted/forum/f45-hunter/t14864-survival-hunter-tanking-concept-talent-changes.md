# Survival Hunter tanking concept: talent changes

## Post 101266 by Sinslayer (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-09-14T18:26:00Z

Hello, I read on discord that Survival Hunter Tanking has been rejected but I am here to argue that it makes more sense for Survival to be a tank spec than it to be a melee dps spec.
On the other hand, I want to argue that Beast Mastery should be the melee dps focused spec but that post is in heavy making.

Why Hunter and Survival? Hunter because we need a pet class tank in the game and Survival because it has talents that just suit a tank well. It is called Survival after all.
Let's get over the talents that can be considered tank-like in the Survival tree:

 1) *Deflection*. It gives 5% more parry.
 2) *Survivalist*. Gives 10% more hp.
 3) *Deterrence*: Increases parry and dodge by 25% for 10 seconds.
 4) *Counterattack*: A strike that becomes active after you parry.
 5) *Lighting Reflexes*: Gives 15% agility.
 6) *Wyvern Sting*: A stinging shot that puts the target to sleep for 12 sec.

On the surface level these talents may seem mediocre but the tanking synergy is there; get into Aspect of the Monkey, put traps down before you pull. Use Multi-Shot or Wyvern Sting depending if you want more threat or to play it safe. When the mobs come to attack you, increase your avoidances with Deterrence to enable the use of Counterattack and Mongoose Bite. Strike them down with your 2h using Raptor Strike making both your defenses and offenses shine during this tank dance. And if you lose threat on a mob, then taunt with your pet or use distracting shot if you're far away. Feign Death if a dps pulls the mobs first.

But I did not include the dps enhancing talents even tho they contribute a lot to tanks. Even parry by itself would greatly reduce the speed of your next auto attack, making your Raptor Strike that much smoother to use. Therefore I suspect a 2h would be the best use for this tanking style, even without any talent support. **I am not saying Survival tanking doesn't have its problems, far from it; all I am saying is that it makes sense from the right perspective.**

Either way you see at Survival Tanking, I want to show you my take on how to rework the Survival talent tree to make it more tank-like, with melee ideas in mind, but also not crazy anti-blizz-like.

I've actually been leveling a Tank Hunter (face, not pet) and for dungeoning it already works ok and the playstyle is very fun. Big proponent of some improvements to the style, and I think talents are one area that can be done. These are some cool ideas, and I appreciate that you kept an eye to most of the talents being a bit agnostic -- the trap talents are just as good for any utility focused Hunter as a Tank Hunter, for instance. It takes a bit more community approval for such changes I think, but likely has the best results in the end.

In my experience, a big part of what makes Tank Hunter fun to play is the high amount of juggling involved. Imo it's one of the most demanding playstyles for leveling, because to play it well you have to micro two characters and for one of them the positioning is very tight.

Tank Hunter as it stands involves a triple-single target style of:
- Shooting ranged enemies, or melees held by your pet, with autos, Distracting, and Multishot

- Meleeing melee enemies with autos Raptors, Mongoose, Counterattack, and sometimes Wing Clip

- Controlling the position of ranged/melee enemies so Multishot can hit all 3 targets

- Microing your pet to ensure good Screech coverage and to hold certain enemies for you

- Pre-fight trapping for control/aggro

- Moving *away* from aggro pullers on your team to be able to re-establish aggro

- Occasionally popping a defensive CD
Tl;dr you're doing melee, ranged, and pet control all at the same time.

With that in mind, I think furthering the distinction in the style is a good thing and it's power can go up some without anyone's normal conception of the gaming changing much. I would, however, keep an eye on the PVP crowd here as I doubt they'd want Hunter suddenly becoming super tanky.

To that end, I like your changes to Counterattack and Deterrence. Dodge/parry area big part of Tank Hunter, so encouraging it feels right and the melee threat is needed. I would also allow Counterattack/Mongoose some scaling as well since their damage is incredibly low.

Bonus threat on the traps is good, tho I might be happier with "Multi-shot now applies Distracting Shot." This reinforces the triple-single style we currently have and still doesn't sound like something any DPS Hunter would want. That idea is a little more wacky tho.

From a tanking perspective, I also like the need for pets to have some aggro in order to enable mid-combat traps. It's nice and reinforces the pet as a part of your tanking. Non-tank Hunters can still use Feign Death as always.

I'd say keep an eye on the Improve Ice/Fire Traps, as they seem very strong and more generically useful. I'm not against a more utility focus tho, so I'd want to see how that shook out.

The part I'm most iffy about is the Beast Mastery changes, as its quite a bit of tankiness available to any spec. In PvE I imagine this wouldn't make too big a difference, but it is a pretty general Hunter buff. In PvP I bet people would be annoyed. I'd like to see a touch more tankiness, but maybe just not in this form.

Overall I like it and I'd be happy to see it go in as is, tho I'd want the team to keep an eye on the BM and Improves Traps ones.

Lastly, I'd also like to test another idea in Hunter tanking as well:
- Improved Cower: Your pet's cower now transfers their aggro on the target to you instead of removing it.
The hope here is that, combined with the incentive to have mobs attack your pet, you can sometimes have your pet holding aggro and sometimes have them helping you hold it. Lots of good micro potential there and a versatile tool for control in fights. The worry is that it may be too much aggro gen when combined with other changes. Still, I'd like to see it tried. The other worry is that it may make pet pulling even better in raids, dunno if people would be upset about that or not. Add a distance-to-Hunter limit if that is an issue.

There's lots of additional ways you could refine the vision, like having Mongoose Bite cause aggro for your pet, encouraging aggro juggling between the two of you. Or giving one of your melee abilities a chance to refresh Distracting Shot. Or having your pet gain some focus when aggro swaps away from them. I think the room is definitely there for this to be a unique tanking style while feeling properly Vanilla+.

Anyway I'd love to see a bit more support for tank Hunter, absolutely on board, and personally I also don't mind the buffs not being super powerful. Enough to make it work well for those that want to, not enough to define the meta.
