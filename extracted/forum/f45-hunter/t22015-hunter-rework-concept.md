# Hunter Rework Concept

## Post 152597 by Stian — 2025-09-21T20:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152597#p152597 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all,
Hunter has been my first choice of class for about 20 years now. However, playing one on twow I find a less appealing idea. Sure they have a melee spec, but meleeing as a hunter always feels a bit like dating someone that is too young for you - sure they are cute and it can be nice, but there is always that feeling of weirdness to it. Having played tons of vanilla and then SoD, it is hard going back to a vanillaish hunter style again, so now I play as a hunter priest - namely the nelf kind, and I'm loving it!

A lot of people seem less then pleased with the BM rework (saucy sausage) and the generic nature of pets. So.. I made the following talent trees for the hunter specs (please keep in mind that this is poorly balanced and it is more conceptual at this stage):

[https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c ... ?points=--](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator/l3KCnHvWvf?points=--)

My aim is to have three quite distinct types of hunter being available to the player.

The BM spec is heavily focused on your pet and becoming one with the beast, most specs would want to spec into the aspect line in the BM tree.

Marksmanship is your ranger/archer and even spell ranger type.

Survival is the melee tree for your brawny Rexxar type, troll/orc axe thrower and/or your trappy forethought sneaky maniac. They offer added utility, can take a hit and hit hard themselves while having some added CC.

These three trees could support a wide variety of hunter playstyles. You can go full BM and get the melee talents in Surs, go BM/MM hybrid (focusing on either), Surv/BM, Full MM (picking up aspect talents from BM and tier 1 talents from Surv - including Lone Wolf), have an AoE farming off-spec by taking trap talents in Surv and filling out MM (picking Barrage for increased AoE dmg), Picking the survivability and CC talents in Surv and buffing your pet and defensive aspects in BM for a dungeon tank hunter and so on..

I wanted to frontload most talents so that you can start playing your spec as early as possible. As the trees are now, they would surely make hunters OP, so yes please thank you (just kidding, but not really).

 **Some things to clear up,**
 **-** A lot of changes to pets that I will detail below this section.

 **-** No more dead zone. The minimum range for ranged attack is equal to the max melee range. Switching between the two modes of attack does not happen automatically.

 **-** Change Arcane Shot into Quick Shot as the whole spell dmg shot thing is enabled via the MM tree, and Arcane Shot doesn't make sense for BM or Surv. You can turn your Quick Shot into an Arcane Shot with MM talents anyway. Could consider having it not share a CD with Aimed Shot, but the idea of making a choice between taking your time to line up an aimed shot vs taking a quick pop is quite nice.

 **-** Aimed shot chance is to make ranged wpn speed less relevant (maybe most shots should also be reworked to rely more on ranged attack power instead of wpn dmg) and to make it more interactive (might be affected by the point below). Flurry Fire would feel amazing I think, and makes sense for both (machine) guns and bows (repeating xbow anyone?).

 **-** Some of my ideas might not be possible to implement at all due to how the game works.

 **-** There would be class quests to unlock any type of aspect, akin to shaman's class quests for totems. One thing that feels most meaningful in wow are class quests. There would also be an aspect for every kind of beast.

 **-** Hunters would have class professions, like rogues do: Fletching, Poisons and Trapping.
Fletching could also be Munitions and Hunters here craft their own ammo, I prefer keeping gun ammo for the engi professions as it does fit there and belongs alongside mining. Arrows would be crafted using drops like feathers, bones, etc. maybe add a rope crafting thing in the survivalist profession for arrow construction. Poisons would be unlocking stings and crafting them.

Wanna use Scorpid Sting? Go and farm some poison from scorpions. Best I feel if this profession is low resource intensity, 1 poison drop makes a gazillion poison shots. Trapping would be trap crafting, from wood, steel, oil, rope and the rune/magic traps some magic juju items.

 **-** Hunters would get their pets much earlier. The 1-10 brackets for hunters is a very strange sort of pre-life. The transition from kite firing auto shot to having your pet become the main character and you a turret is also a strange one, though. Maybe growl is just less of a thing, so now you have to kite and use your CC toolkit with the new Snare Trap (smells like short cute class quest!) being available at lvl 1?

At level one, a series of quests will be available to you from your class trainer. You pick one (like in pokemon) and this is will lead you to your first pet and pet type and aspect. Boar and Aspect of the Boar for example (which would be an increase in strength thing? and the spirit aspect from stag or wind serpent? Owl being intellect?).

 **-** Spell dmg hunter would mean ranged weapons with stats that benefit this archetype, meaning bows with spell damage on them for my nelf priest (yes this entire thing is just a ploy to make this one thing happen). Which would fix what I'd say is kind of an issue. Also, please make Searing Shot do arcane dmg instead of fire dmg? Would of course prefer it doing holy, but might make nelf the only right choice for smite priest?

 **-** Distracting Shot is supplemented or replaced by Harass (increases your threat generation for 8 seconds).

 **-** Traps can be placed in combat, but all traps have a channeled cast time that interrupts on damage taken. Placing a trap from Feign Death makes the cast instant and breaks Feign Death.

 **-** A weak point for melee surv is the lack of reliable gap closers for pvp, this could be remedied by choosing a pet that can gap close to then apply effective CC for you to then close the gap yourself. (Hi Misha!)

 **-** Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite no longer share CD, and their CDs are now 5 and 3 sec.

 **-** The Throwing weapon talent might be better as a Troll racial. Also, adding throwing spears/javelins.

 **-** Shadowmeld (twow version) might have to be reworked as the 2second continued stealth might be too good for a sniper trained Night Elf, allowing them to open on someone from stealth up to 45 yards away (lol). Maybe reduce it to 1 second (allowing for some counterplay and also asking the hunter the question if when they want to release their Aimed Shot more pressingly, maybe reduce the 1min CD by at least 10 seconds).

 **-** Seeing as how the talent trees are less point costly then the norm, other class trees would need to be reworked too, so as not to make hunters be able to out-talent everyone else.

 **Pets**
love to hate them, hate to love them

Pet stances
Defensive stance is now assist. Assist is a targetable ability. Targeting yourself, assist functions as in retail, and then activating aggressive makes the pet keep attacking any target you have it attack should it get aggro, activating passive makes it try to escape its attacker by running towards you. Casting assist on an ally has the pet assist them. For the current versions of aggressive and passive use assist and target the pet itself.

There will be pet talent trees, replacing pet training, where most talents that just affect your pet, like ferocity and frenzy would be available (for pets that makes sense for). I am happy with the three archetypes from retail Ferocity, Tenacity and Cunning, but maybe add one or two more for healing/utility and spell based pets.

More pet uniqueness. Minor things like Lupos having increased shadow resistance and his bite being instead, shadow bite, dealing shadow damage. This would be represented in Lupos' talent tree and possible to unspecc, as it would take up a talent point, and it would be the same for other rare pets.

Foxes and Owls (cunning) would be able to get you out of movement impairing effects (Master's Call, which would be renamed), it working like Intimidation, where the pet actually has to get in melee range of you for the effect to occur. Intimidation would be available for your Tenacity pets like Gorillas and Bears etc. Turtles and Plainstriders would fit into the healing/utility type and wind serpents into this same catch all type or its own spell category. A firelands type beast (a phoenix?) would be able to resurrect itself, a turtle (for example) to take damage directed at you instead of you taking it and a stag (!) would be able to heal and possibly resurrect the hunter (which would make possible some crazy battle reincarnation situations paired with the final talents in the BM tree). This is a hugely underexplored area of possibilities.

Call Pet is still an instant cast, but it will take your 5 seconds (equal to Dismiss Pet cast time) before your pet appears.

Would appreciate feedback and thoughts and taking my ideas further or refining them, I might add stuff to this original post.
Peace and love
