First off, Petopia classic <https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic/abilities.php> helps you find your information out of the game to plan to get the abilities you might need. At level 24, you also received (well .. bought) "Beast lore" that will give you information about the stats of the beast you find in the wild by casting it on them and it shows the skills they currently can teach you.

The first one is great but won't show you the special pets this version of Turtle Wow has to offer, the second one can confirm for every beast you encounter in the wild.
