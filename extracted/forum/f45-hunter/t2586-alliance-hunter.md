Don't limit yourself to bows. Guns can pack a punch as well! Unfortunately, Alliance especially is bit gimped with regard to a good ranged weapon.

In times like this, I usually try the following approach. Why don't you go here:

[https://classic.wowhead.com/items/weapo ... ems;0-2+19](https://classic.wowhead.com/items/weapons/bows#items;0-2+19)
[https://classic.wowhead.com/items/weapo ... ems;0-2+19](https://classic.wowhead.com/items/weapons/crossbows#items;0-2+19)
[https://classic.wowhead.com/items/weapo ... ems;0-2+19](https://classic.wowhead.com/items/weapons/guns#items;0-2+19)

and sort items on their level. Then you can clearly see which are available for you as a quest reward. For example - I think you could get this one pretty easy: [https://classic.wowhead.com/item=2903/d ... unting-bow](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=2903/daryls-hunting-bow) (or a corresponding gun: [https://classic.wowhead.com/item=2904/d ... ting-rifle](https://classic.wowhead.com/item=2904/daryls-hunting-rifle))

As a final note - buying armor/weapon from a vendor is almost never a good idea.
