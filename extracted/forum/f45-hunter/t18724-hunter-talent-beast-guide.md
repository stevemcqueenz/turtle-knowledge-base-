BM is pretty easy to spec. Pets scale incredibly well at lower levels so dump all your talents in things that make them better, or that assist them (like mend pet and revive pet) and you'll be golden.

Don't forget Bestial Swiftness, it helps both your pet and you, as you'll be running around in Cheetah from lvl 20 til probably a few levels after 40.

## Post 131377 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-05-16T11:23:00Z

While leveling, you pretty much go hard Beastmaster, as you want your pet to soak up all the damage, and also do enough damage as to keep aggro. You'll be dealing with Elite enemies with relative ease
So while leveling, you'll probably rock smth like this:
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?p ... AAAAAAAAA=](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?points=BoAaRoCoRCQgBAAoAoQBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA=)
Or this:
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?p ... AAAAAAAAA=](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?points=AoAaRoCoRCQgBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAYQCYCJAAAAAAAAA=)

When you reach level cap, or you're close to reaching level cap, you wanna respec to smth like this:
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?p ... AAAAAAAAA=](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?points=FQAYBoAoQCQQAAAoCoQBAoACAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA=)

Your last 2 points are up to you. Either:
 - 2x into Improved Steady Shot (more dps overall)
 - 2x into Frenzy (so it's at 4 points) (more dps for your pet if you're using a tanking pet)
 - Intimidation into Bestial Wrath (questing and pvp utility)

You can learn more about Hunter pets here: <https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic/>

For leveling, Boars and Carrion Birds are generally your best choice. But if you go full Beastmaster, any pet will do honestly
