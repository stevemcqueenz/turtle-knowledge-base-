# Beastmaster talent updates for better pet dungeon tanking

## Post 171839 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2026-03-22T22:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171839#p171839 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

**[Introduction - can skip]** Hello again! Since my last hunter suggestion post around December, I took a long break from the game. I got back into the game recently and reviewed all of the comments, and was more than pleasantly surprised with the feedback. A lot of positive, negative, neutral but overall, very useful feedback, so thank you to everyone who shared their thoughts. My opinions on those proposed talents has shifted quite a bit, and instead of a thread facilitating all 3 talent trees, I've decided to do only 1 at a time.

 1.18.1 presented some pretty big changes to the hunter, namely BM, and it's in a far, far better spot now. Still, there are some adjustments that I wish are made, and would love to create a discussion around them.**[Intro end]**
---
 **The core of the changes are as follows:**
 - Allow for pet tanking without adding bloat to the talent tree.
 - Provide all necessary and optional pet tanking talents as early as possible, so players can utilize them for dungeon content.
 - Adjust some talents so they benefit melee hunters as well.
 - And some general buffs/improvements.

Here are the revamped talents: <https://www.talent-builder.dev/tree/QjJFAjNRXX>
 ****Note**:** *In case the talent builder doesn't open for you, try opening it through a private window.*
![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/85w8DdXw/Beast-Mastery-edit-by-Sven-S2-v2-2-0.png)

Below are the changes and the explanation behind them:

 ****Swift Aspects -> Boosts Pet Attack Speed as well****  **Description:**While active: Aspect of the Hawk provides all ranged attacks, and Aspect of the Wolf all melee attacks a 10% chance of increasing ranged or melee attack speed respectively by 3/6/9/12/15% for 12 sec.  Your pet also becomes inspired, and gains 2/4/6/8/10% increased attack speed for 12 sec.

 **Explanation:**
 - With the addition of Scent of Blood and reworked Kill Command, the increased attack speed from the Swift Aspects talent became a lot more valuable to BM. Still, it's not until the 3rd row that the pet gains any form of offensive boost. This addition is to help pets scale a bit better, while also benefiting the hunter directly.

 ****Improved Revive Pet**** and ****Improved Mend Pet  -> Merged****  **Renamed to:** "**Soothe Beast**"
 **Description:** Revive Pet's casting time is reduced by 3/6 sec, mana cost is reduced by 20/40%, and increases the health your pet returns with by an additional 15/30%. Also, increases the amount you heal with Mend Pet by 20/40%.

 **Explanation:**
 - Improved Revive Pet is often skipped while leveling, and rightfully so. It is a talent for the worst possible edge case, which is death. There are niche situations where it is viable, such as pet tanking in a group environment. But that doesn't come into play often enough to make it a viable early talent.

 - Improved Mend Pet on the other hand is a very good talent for leveling, allowing you to easily tank elite mobs with your pet. However its position on the 4th row makes it rather awkward, as it's directly competing with Ferocity and Bestial Discipline. As such, people often outright ignore it.

 - Which is the reason why I think combining the 2 talents into 1 makes sense. Mend Pet is not a crazy broken ability, long from it, so having it boosted a bit will make it more lucrative, especially as a 2nd row talent. Players will also benefit directly from the improved revive pet bonuses, encouraging riskier pet plays.

 - With their merges we're also freeing up 2 talent points

 **[IMPORTANT]** I have intentionally left out the Cure/Disease/Magic/Poison cleanse aspect of the talent. I don't frequent PvP anymore so it's definitely not my strong suit. My experience comes from memories back in the day. So feedback if I should add that as well would be appreciated. **I was considering putting that effect somewhere else, for example on Scent of Blood proc, but I'd like to hear some feedback first.**

 ****Improved Primal Aspects -> Changed bonuses****  **Description**: Increases the Dodge bonus of your Aspect of the Monkey by 2/4/6%, the speed bonus of your Aspect of the Cheetah and Aspect of the Pack by 3/4/6%, and the mana regen bonus from Aspect of the viper by 0.5/1/1.5%

 - The change here is we remove the Aspect of the Wolf leech bonus and instead add pathfinding's Aspect of the Cheetah/Pack speed increase, and also a small bonus to Aspect of the Viper.

 **Explanation:**
 - The life leech bonus for Aspect of the Wolf assumes you are going to be the one tanking, which shouldn't be the case. Instead melee hunters should be given better threat mitigation tools than ways to deal with the aggro they'll inevitable generate.

 - The earlier access to improved Aspect of the Cheetah/Pack will make the talent more lucrative. It can be useful for PvP builds without needing to dig deep into the BM tree. But it will also be more desired for leveling builds. Your 3rd row has both Coordinated Assault and Unleashed Fury, both highly desired, making the current pathfinding talent not as frequently picked up, especially if they have a pet with Dive/Dash or a boar with Charge.

 - Viper is a good addition, but some builds still struggle with mana. Picking up this talent will help alleviate that. 1% may not sound like a big addition, but it adds up over the course of a fight.

 ****Improved Eyes of the Beast**** replaced with -> ****Offhand Command****  **Description:** Every 2 sec while your pet is under a Charm, Fear, Sleep or Disorient effect, it has a 10/20% chance to break free from them early. Also increases your character's chance to hit with offhand weapons by 2/5%.

 **Reason for replacing Improved Eyes of the Beast:**
 - Eyes of the Beast is a niche ability and its upgrade is even more niche. For PvP, you can use a stealthed cat and guard a certain position, but idk how viable that is anymore. What Improved Eyes of the Beast helps out the most with is with improved generation for pet tanking. So it may seem odd that I'm removing it, given that the goal of this thread is to improve exactly that. The thing is that we've naturally buffed threat generation through other means, so the need to go into eyes of the beast isn't as big. Now there are also some solo content utility for Eyes of the Beast, where you use a stealthed pet to eliminate a specific far off target which can draw aggro. In that case IEOTB can be of use, but it's too big of an edge case, as you can already solo all non-elite enemies with your pet, and IEOTB won't really help you take down elites.

 **Benefits of Offhand Command:**
 - This talent aims to provide some more reliable utility to your pet in both PvP and PvE. The effect may seem redundant given that BM has access to Bestial Wrath, however that's a pretty deep talent. Offhand Command gives similar utility but at lower reliability for other specs to experiment with. It also gives you the option of holding off on Bestial Wrath in certain cases, if your pet breaks free early from the CC effect.

 - As for the off-hand hit chance improvement. With the changes to talents benefiting from player hits and crits, hitting the target as quickly and with as many attacks as possible is key to keeping your pet's up time on effects, and duel-wielding helps greatly with that.

 - Lore friendly to popular BMs like Rexxar. Beastlords, another name for beastmasters in other games are often depicted as duel-wielding.

 - This talent gives melee hunters the option of starting off early with the BM tree. Additionally, it separates the first 2 rows of BM talents into Player damage or utility and pet durability or utility, allowing for more interesting mix and matching.

 - Ranged hunters have other benefits in the BM tree over melee, such as Coordinated Assault being more frequently procked as ranged, so having a talent benefit melee more shouldn't be a problem.

 ****Pathfinding changed to Elusiveness -> Crit resist component****  **Description:** Increases your pet's movement speed by 15/30% and grants it 3/6% critical strike resistance.

 - Essentially we remove the Aspect of the Cheetah/Pack component and replace it with a survivability one.

 **Explanation:**

 - This talent is in a bit of an awkward position right now. It absolutely helps with leveling, but part of its value is lost as a 3rd row talent. By that time, the pet's big damaging talents start showing their face, which are a lot more lucrative. That's why the improved Aspect of the Cheetah/Pack component got moved to Primal Aspects.

 - As mentioned in my explanation for Improved Primal Aspects, the movespeed bonus of Pathfinding isn't as relevant if your pet has a good gap closer. So players that have decided to level with those pets have fewer reasons to pick up the talent. Now it also grants them a decent survivability boost, making lower hp/armor pets better and it makes tanky pets even tankier, providing value to both sides.

 ****Unleashed Fury -> Threat component****  **Description**: Increases the damage done by your pets by 4/8/12/16/20%. Your pets also generate 6/12/18/24/30% more threat while in Aggressive or Defensive mode.

 - The addition is the increased threat generation while in aggressive  mode.

 **Explanation:**

 - I've tinkered away with how to improve pet aggro, either via an ability, dedicated talent or heck, even with using eyes of the beast, but they all have their own set of cons. Which is why placing it directly into one of the core BM damage talents seems the most logical. Majority of players during dungeon runs have their pets in Petpassive mode, and use PetAttack to get it to act. So there won't be any negative impact on those players when it comes to accidentally pulling aggro with the pet.

 - I personally like to macro in pet defensive mode on attack and pet passive mode on return, so the pet doesn't awkwardly linger or return to me on mob kill. Other players like to just keep their pet in defensive mode at all times. **Which is why I'm contemplating if the effect should only trigger on Pet Aggressive Mode or also on a Defensive mode.**Please leave your feedback on that.

 - If this were to be implemented, then I hope Cower becomes an ability your pet can learn directly from the pet trainer and for it to cost 0 training points, so it's the counterpart to growl. This way the increased threat during Defensive mode can be alleviated in a more passive way.

 - On the comments that say pets need AoE taunt in order to tank effectively. That's still an option on the drawing board, and I haven't dismissed it yet. But I'd like to avoid the risk of making pet tanks better than actual tanks, so they need some kind of downside. So, the core downside of tanking with a pet would limitation to smaller packs to pull.

 ****Bestial Discipline -> CDR Buff to Commands****  **Description**: Increases the Focus regeneration of your pets by 2/5 each tick and reduces the cooldown of your pets' special ability and hunter commands by 10/20%.

 - The addition here is the reduction of cooldowns for hunter commands, those being Intimidate, Bestial Wrath and Kill Command.

 **Explanation:**

 - My core reason is the long CD for Kill Command and Intimidate right now. 8 seconds feels like forever for Kill Command, which should be a staple in your rotation. Intimidate for both PvP and PvE has great uses, but the 1min CD is definitely harsh. Considering it's competing with Ferocity, and then some very strong dps talents, it needs a bit of a buff.

 - With the buff, it won't provide an immediate benefit, or at least not until you spend a few more points to unlock Intimidate. 48 sec CD down from 60 is a significant boost, when jumping from encounter to encounter. Either that being a group of mobs or another player.

 ****Savagery -> New dps/survivability talent****  **Description**: Improves your pet's Claw and Bite abilities. Claw now also hits the target's nearest ally. Bite heals your pet for 200% of the damage done.

 - This talent allows your pet to either dps more effectively, maintain aggro on multiple targets more easily, or provide it with more sustain. It fits thematically, with Unleashed Fury being its prerequisite, and is useful for both pet dps and pet tanking.

 - Claw can be a good go to for trash clearing, as it gives some additional AoE damage. Especially useful vs many low HP targets. Can also be used to more easily maintain threat on multiple targets, useful on pets without such means, like Bears, but is even better on pets with some form of AoE threat generator like Screech.

 - Bite gives another form of sustain to your pet. Once Spirit Bond is unlocked it really starts to shine.

 ****Intimidation -> Improved targeting and earlier access****  **Description**: Command your pet to intimidate your current target with a terrifying growl, stunning it for 3 sec, and increasing the pet's threat generation by 50% for 8 sec. Casting range 10yd (from your pet).

 - The change here is how the stun is applied. It's no longer on your pet's next successful melee attack, but instead an instant cast stun on YOUR current target.

 - The talent has also been moved from a 5th row talent to a 4th row one.

 **Explanation:**

 - Having earlier access to this talent will make it both more favorable for pet tanking and for PvP. Due to its current position, it doesn't allow for many diverse builds.

 - Intimidate as a stun is great if the target you want to stun is also the 1 your pet is currently attacking. This means that if you want to stun an adjacent target, you first have to issue your pet to attack that target and then use Intimidate. Can you macro it already? Yes, but it also has the added downside of requiring a successful attack, and depending on your pet's attack speed or dodge/parry of your target, that may prove difficult. So all in all, it either saves you a button press, a macro or RNGesus not working in your favor.

 - You can use the ability from whatever distance you want, just like now. However the range at which your pet applies the ability will be 10yd. **Please let me know if this is too good, especially with the buffs to Bestial Discipline.**

 ****Frenzy -> only 3 points now****  **Description**: Gives your pet a 30/60/100% chance to gain a 30% attack speed increase for 8 sec after dealing a critical strike.

 **Explanation:**
 - Every build I've ever seen, be it new or old only ever puts 2 to 3 points into this talent. So why not just make it a 3 point talent altogether. Compared to the old scaling of 20/40/60/80/100 rank 1 provides 10% extra proc chance, rank 2 provides 20% extra proc chance and rank 3 provides 40% extra proc chance, making the full 3 point investment quite lucrative.

 - This change is also necessary imo due to the dps talent bloat towards the end.

 ****Spirit Bond -> Melee or Ranged Attack power for calculation****  **Description**: Your pet gains melee attack power equal to 12/25% and spell power equal to 7/15% of your ranged attack or melee attack power, whichever is highest. While your pet is active, you and your pet will regenerate 1/2% of total health every 5 sec.

 - The change here is that the pets gain attack and spell power based on the highest attack power value for your character (Melee or Ranged).

 **Explanation:**
 - The core reason is to facilitate melee hunter playstyle in the BM Tree. With the new changes, duel wielding hunters have become quite good at triggering Scent of Blood and Kill Command. I really wish to dive deeper into tweaking the Survival tree, so it makes melee more viable during the leveling stage of the game, but that will wait for another time.

 ****Kill Command -> Flavor buff, not really necessary****  **Description**: Commands your pet to instantly attack for 50% of its Attack Power. Can only be used after the Hunter lands a critical strike on the target. Can be used during Eyes of the Beast without breaking it, and doesn't require a crititical strike to use.

 - The addition here is that the ability can be freely used during Eyes of the Beast, without requiring a crit from your player or interrupting the channeling.

 **Explanation:**
 - I've so far omitted using Eyes of the Beast for tanking purposes, because the playstyle may not appeal to a lot of players, and I don't want to force it. Which is why it think EotB should stay as a fun flavourful ability, with this change giving it a slight boost.

 - With EotB having a 2sec cast time, it's completely illogical to use it as a dps CD in order to always proc Kill Command when it's off-cd. You'll be simply losing too much damage that way from your own character, plus it will be wonky.

---

 ****In addition** to the above talent changes, here are some other Pet related changes I hope to see.**

1. Make all ranks of Cower teachable by the pet trainer.
2. Have Cower cost 0 training points, just like Growl. That way it can be used as its dps counterpart.
3. Have Cower reduce threat from all enemies around it.
4. Increase the number of abilities pets can learn.
5. Rework Prowl to the following:
 - On use does not interrupt your pet's last action(attack command for example)
 - Can be used during combat
 - Self movement slow reduced from 50/45/40% to 40/30/20%
 - CD increased from 8 to 30 sec.
6. Increase the casting range of Mend Pet to 35yd baseline.
7. And last but not least, Increase stable slots, I beg of you.

 **Now to answer some general questions.**
[Q1] Why Pet tanking?
 - It's a niche playstyle that already works, but many players are afraid to experiment with it. Finding tanks for leveling dungeons is arguably harder than finding a healer, so the improvements here should hopefully give players an excuse to give it a try. The changes won't make your pet tank raids, but it will help it tank dungeons until you reach that stage.

[Q2] Won't this make hunter too good at solo-ing content?
 - It will be a boost, but it won't be more than what the hunter is already able to achieve with the tools he has currently. The main significance is during the leveling stages, as improved Mend Pet is unlocked too late currently, and is overshadowed by more lucrative talents. With the arrangement changes, a hunter can go for Endurance Training, Thick Hide, Soothe Beast and then continue as usual with their build. If they feel their pet needs more sustain, they can pickup Elusiveness and Savagery, but those are options, and not the rule.

[Q3] Why no AoE taunt for the pet?
 - My current argument is that I don't want pet tanking to be too easy, and overshadow tank specs for other classes. Unlike them, the pet is not really gear dependent. Improved Claw can help maintain threat on 2 targets, if the pet has no AoE ability. But in general you'll be playing it like a Warrior spamming Sunder. So long as your team plays smart and focuses 1 mob at a time, maintaining threat shouldn't be a problem.

[Q4] Won't improved Claw screw up your tank by stealing threat from them?
 - As much as any other class with AoE abilities. For threat reduction, pick up Cower, which I also suggested to be a baseline ability trained by Pet Trainers, cost 0 points to train and reduce threat from all targets around the pet.
