# Simple weapon skill grind

## Post 133122 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-05-26T21:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133122#p133122 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hunters imo have one of the easiest ways to grind up weapon skill for their ranged weapons, while out in the world

Simply spam lv1 Serpent Sting on your targets while your pet tanks. It costs virtually nothing, it has no CD, it deals minuscule damage, so the target won't die too fast. In no time you'll have your weapon skill maxed out
