# A "send to stable" QoL ability

## Post 98229 by Steakhouse (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-06-30T18:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98229#p98229 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The ability would need a prohibitively long cooldown (e.g. 1 hour) and work only out of combat.

To be clear, I'm not suggesting a remote "open the stable" as this would affect Hunter gameplay greatly, allowing them to swap pets between bosses, packs, battlegrounds, and even in the middle of fights via Feign Death (or just a Freezing trap in PvP), which isn't desirable.

I'm suggesting to add a send to stable ability for specifically two use cases :

 - You happen upon a beast you want to tame in the wild: it's rare, or it looks nice, or it's Brokentooth, or it has the latest rank of a pet spell you need. Currently you need to walk back to town, hope it has a stable master (or google it on wowhead, which breaks immersion), walk back, hope the mob is still there ... Or questing with a stabled pet and taming stuff on the go, which leads to hauling massive amounts of various foods. OR, already know everything you're gonna tame in advance, to know exactly when to stable your pet. IMO none of these scenarios are particularly desirable, giving a great niche to "send to stable" without altering balance in any way.

 - You want to tame 2 beasts in the same location, far from a Stable Master. Stable your main pet before you leave, tame the first pet, send to stable via ability, tame the second, dismiss or feed it.
