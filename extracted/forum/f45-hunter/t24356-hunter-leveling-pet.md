## Post 171445 by Elfje — 2026-03-20T14:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171445#p171445 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

- Bat, hawk, carrion birds and owls have screech, which is great for AoE aggro.

- Bears have a 2 min cooldown skill for reduced damage taken on the bear, and improved damage for all party members.

- Boar has a charge, for a small root and a bit of burst damage.

- Cats have stealth. Booooring ...

- Crabs have a bubble barrier: absorb damage and then bursts with AoE damage. 30 second cooldown.

- Crocs have a deathroll: big chunk of damage and an 80% speed reduction for 3 seconds. 30 second cooldown.

- Gorilla: the AoE thunderstomp, but a 40 sec cooldown.

- Hyena: summon a second hunter pet for 20 seconds. 90 second cooldown. Have you always wanted multiple pets, here is your chance!

- Raptor: a bleed, ignores armor.

- Scorpid: poison damage. If specced in BM, this is the highest damage ability a pet can have. This is because the damage scales with spirit bond spellpower. Quite underrated.

- Serpent: ranged poison attack.

- Spider: web ability. Great for pvp. 60 second cooldown. Personally I think deathroll is better: longer duration and shorter cooldown.

- Tallstrider: nice for raiding, due to mana and hp regen party-wide.

- Turtle: a mini-shieldwall. Lasts 12 seconds, 60 sec cooldown.

- Wind serpent: ranged nature attack.

- Wolf: improved melee damage. Great for survival hunters.
In short: pick whatever ability you think is the most fun!

Boars are a good go to. Boar Charge helps them quickly pile up some threat, so you won't pull as easily. Boars also have a wide diet which is a plus. Cats with low attack speed (Feral Mountain Lion in Hillsbrad) are also great for leveling. They have Dash, so they quicky reach the target. They can learn both bite and claw, so they can focus dump easily. And even though fast attacking pets deal less damage per attack, it still helps them quickly pile up threat. ([https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic/loo ... rskinbrown](https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic/look.php?id=tigerskinbrown))

BM is more ranged focused while leveling, so pretty much any choice would work honestly, as you also benefit from a higher threat threshold opposed to melee(Survival hunters).
