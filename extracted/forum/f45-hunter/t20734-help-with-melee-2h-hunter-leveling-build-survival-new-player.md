Hello, you are actually asking right before patch 1.18 hits the server (August 15th) so it is now harder to tell. At the moment, levelling is boring before level 40. At level 40 you get the "untamed trapper" talent - the last talent in the survival tree - which allows you put traps in combat. It drastically improves gameplay when fire traps start to deal some nice looking DoT. Torgether with lightning relfexes talent (a must-have for melee survival) you start to deal some respectable overal damage as well. After lvl 40 survival melee becomes very fun (at least for me) and levelling becomes way easier. In the end, you must go at least 34 points into the survival tree to deal good damage.

Now for patch 1.18 - you will get a new talent called "Lacerate" which is a bleed DoT and you will get it sooner, at level 30 I think. So levelling become somewhat more engaging and easier sooner compared to the current state. Also BM tree is getting a significant rework and will synergize with raptor strike, so pet will contribute more to your damage output.

Beast Master is generally the better option for PvE, since your pet does all the tanking. If you plan to play melee, keep in mind you'll be generating quite a bit of aggro, so make sure to always stay behind the mob you're fighting

Your first two respecs are cheap, so don't worry about min-maxing. You can play around with the talent calculator to get the ideal build you want. <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter>

Your end game spec will look something like this: [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?p ... YDJTAbIQoB](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?points=FoAABo--YQCYDJTAbIQoB)
Start off with the Beast Mastery talents, so your pet is tanky and can dish out some damage. After that start building the Survival tree.
