Scorpions poison deals bonus threat. Kill command on top of that hits very hard and puts the threat onto your pet.

If you are combining this with a weak tank, im not suprised you end up ripping. The same thing happens to warlocks who insist their felguard is a dps pet ignoring its threat mechanics.

The two best options for your problem are:
 1. Ask your resident paladin to buff blessing of salvation on hunter pets
 2. Switch to a less threat hugging pet such as a windserpent until your tanks get a bit better
 3. If you cant get savation and your raid has an excess of shamans, put your bm hunters with a tranquil air grp instead of a wf grp
