## Post 127619 by Worgwalker — 2025-04-20T05:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127619#p127619 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, I know this thread is over 2 weeks old but I just tested this. Furious Howl rank 4 (45-57 extra damage) and Wing Clip rank 3 (does 35% of weapon damage) I did 100 Wing Clips without Furious Howl, then 100 Wing Clips with Furious Howl, excluding crits.

Wing Clip without Furious Howl: 97 was the lowest damage, 111 was the highest.
Wing Clip with Furious Howl: 108 was the lowest damage, 124 was the highest.

So yes it does seem that Furious Howl applies to yellow damage as well as white.
