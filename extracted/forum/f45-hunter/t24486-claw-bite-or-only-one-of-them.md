## Post 172305 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2026-03-25T07:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172305#p172305 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

The difference is bite is stronger per focus spent, but has a cooldown.
Meanwhile claw is rather weak but spamable.

What you teach depends on your preferences. I like to teach claw on most of my pets as they often regenerate more focus than the other abilities use.

But if you have a pet family that has their own spamable skill (such as screech) the benefits are not that great and the pet train points can be moreuseful teaching stamina or armor

## Post 172391 by Daoc2001 — 2026-03-25T15:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172391#p172391 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

If you are leveling with a pet tanking for you with growl active, then claw will focus-starve pet even with the BM focus talent & result in delayed growls here & there - so less threat generated by pet overall as fight goes on.  Most open world mob fights are short, so probably not a problem soloing.  Add Bite or something else into that Growl+Claw mix & it gets even worse with regards to Growl firing at every cooldown.

If you are simply DPSing with something like claw & scorpion poison running, claw will focus starve pet & result in possible missed/delayed poison stack applications & it falling off mob & resetting stack back to 1.  It's even worse if you are tying to run Growl into that mix as well.

So, you have to pick & choose what you run with claw more carefully in some instances.
