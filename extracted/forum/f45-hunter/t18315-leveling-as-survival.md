Sure, it’s definitely viable. The Survival talent tree has been significantly enhanced on Turtle server. With the buffs to Aspect of the Wolf and the new talents, it’s a great choice for leveling. In my opinion, while Survival hunters still might not level as easily as BM hunters, they have their own unique advantages:
 1. They have the highest Agility and Stamina among all three specs.
 2. They can use traps in combat to escape, and the traps also have immobilization effects (with talent support), as well as Deterrence and Improved Feign Death.
 3. They have some AOE capabilities.
 4. Unlike traditional specs that rarely get attacked by mobs and may end up with insufficient defense skill experience, Survival hunters are like melee fighters. Their defense skills can always be kept at the maximum level, making them less likely to be stunned by mobs.
