Please check out <https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter>, specifically the talent Spirit Bond near the bottom of the Beastmastery tree.  Additionally, Endurance Training/Thick Hide/Elusiveness near the top now share a percent of your mitigation stats with your pets to allow them to stay alive.  Finally, with the upcoming patch 1.18, multiple new hunter pets and pet abilities for every type of creature will be added.  As far as people can tell, the general idea seems to be no points in beastmastery = weak/throwaway/unused pet, halfway down = beefy pet that stays alive to provide utility but does no damage, full beastmastery = your pet is your main damage source.

Note that in Vanilla/Classic (aka blizz 1.12), hunter pets had No Scaling Whatsoever.  If you saw pets scaling on other private servers, it was because they used code from TBC and didn't disable the scaling that TBC added.
