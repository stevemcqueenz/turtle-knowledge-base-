# Hunter Hit Table w/ 315 Weapon Skill

## Post 46292 by Condor22 — 2023-05-17T16:05:00Z

Hi Turtles,

I'm having difficulty finding reliable and recent information on Hunter hit tables when fighting lvl 63 boss mobs with 315 defense. I am probably overthinking it, but wanted to kick around some ideas and ask some questions.

So I am a high elf, and receive +5 weapon skill on bows. In addition to that, I've stumbled upon these interesting gear pieces [Trueaim Gauntlets](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13255) and [Sash of the Grand Hunt](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=22207) which add +8 weapon skill and +2 weapon skill (for Bows/Guns/Crossbows) respectively.

Based on the information I've found online so far, when I game out my options, here are the conclusions I reach:

WHEN USING BOWS
If I am using a bow, due to my racial (+5 bow weapon skill), my soft hit cap is immediately reduced from 9% to 6%.
If I equip the Gauntlets, then my weapon skill increases from 305 to 313 and my hit cap is reduced to ~5.5%.
If I equip the Sash & Gauntlet, my bow weapon skill increases to 315 and my hit cap is reduced to 5%.

This is excluding the +1% hit I am getting from the Gauntlet, when you factor that in addition to weapon skill, my soft hit cap is 4% (5% based on weapon skill alone and +1% on the gauntlets).

My question here is, are my assumptions correct regarding the math?

It seems like in this scenario, I can equip the Gauntlet/Sash for 315 bow weapon skill and +1% hit, then I only need 4% hit from the rest of my gear/talents before I reach the 9% required cap.

WHEN USING CROSSBOWS
If I am using a crossbow, my weapon skill is back down to 300 like everyone else.
If I equip the Gauntlets, then my weapon skill for crossbows increases from 300 to 308 and my hit cap is reduced from 9% to at least 6% (between 5% and 6%)

It seems like equiping these Gauntlets to get to 308 skill is a no-brainer as it will drop my 9% hit cap to 6%. In addition to that, the item has +1% hit, so I would only need 5% additional hit from other items.

However, equiping the sash to get to that 310 skill, will only get me to 5.5% and a belt with +1% hit may be flat out better (like the [Marksman's Girdle](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=22232)).

TLDR
You want to reach a weapon skill of 305 to reduce hit requirement from 9% to 6%.
This difference is based on the boss defense skill (315) minus the player weapon skill (305) and achieving a difference between the two of <=10 (315-305=10).
Any additional increase from 305 to 315 is nominal until the difference between the two is 0 (315 boss def - 315 player wep), in which case 5% hit is needed at this point.

Does anyone have a link to a hit table or resource specifically for Hunters in this patch?

Is my math and are my assumptions checking out here?

Anyone have any additional nuances or insights regarding Hunters and their hit cap table?
(I've read the weapon skill is less important for Hunters due to not having to factor in glancing blows)

Thanks for taking the time to read this, and I hope you've found this topic interesting or useful.
