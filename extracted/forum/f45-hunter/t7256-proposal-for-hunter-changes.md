I'm not sure I agree with the hard push for melee Hunters, but I do have to make a point that people sometimes confuse classes with specific names with similar archetypes. Hunters have become ubiquitously synonymous with Archers and sometimes Rangers. In fact, Hunters are more general purpose and are not simply Archers.

The Hunter archetype is associated with trapping, taming beasts, using ranged weapons, using crude melee weapons, fighting and struggling against and eventually making expert use of their skills to survive in and tame nature. They can be Archers, but Archer is a one-trick pony and is simply a facet of the archetype, not one in and of itself. Hunter is multi-faceted.

Probably unhelpful to the discussion at hand, but let's keep in mind that while most games lazily make an Archer and call it a Hunter, WoW seems to at least inherit more facets of the Hunter archetype.
