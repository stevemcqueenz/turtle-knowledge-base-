# MM hunter fixes devs should focus on

## Post 164294 by Vrag — 2026-01-02T19:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164294#p164294 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I think you devs should really focus on ranged hunter. In one of the previous patches you guys said MM is in good spot right now but I disagree and from the ton of posts I see, that doesn't seem to be the case. I can top the leaderboards in battlegrounds and can kill people 1v1 but it requires of me to really try really hard and use every trick I can think of. I also play a warlock and I do not have the same feeling and issues with that class that I have with hunter.

Aimed shot in pvp is 90% useless in pvp even in pve. You can use it to start the fight but beyond that its useless and has long wind up time, has no guaranteed crit and in majority of cases you are better off doing auto shot and steady shot and it will deal more damage. Many here have good ideas for aimed shot, to make it proccable, to make it a guaranteed critical hit etc. Anything will be better than what we have now.

Serpent sting with maxed out talent deals such a low damage that its crazy. Not to mention that talent needs 5 points to still deal that low damage. This damage should be increased and that talent slot should cost 2 talent points not 5.

Arcane shot deals max 183 damage, no attack power just pure arcane damage that low. It needs attack power boost or something

Volley same thing as arcane shot. AoE that does low damage with huge cooldown.

Winged clip if you are playing MM hunter has a 3sec cooldown which is terrible in PVP. I understand they did that because they overhauled survival tree but they screwed over MM hunters in pvp hard with that because getting melee opponents out of your deadzone is a gamble. They also removed counterattack, a proccable melee stun that procs on parry. This ability was good if you were MM playing battlegrounds to get away from attackers, but it was removed.

Trueshot aura being the final talent ability in the tree is also insane to me. Meanwhile warlock gets dark harvest, most damaging death ray ability and we get a weak aura? Replace trueshot aura with some ultimate shot ability.

These are all issues the majority of ranged hunters on turtlewow agree should be looked at.
I play MM as a sort of ninja ranged acrobat but I miss the abilities to add to rotations. Its genuinely sad that this class is reduced to autoattack, steady shot and multishot combos. I still use these abilities when I find it appropriate but that its underwhelming is an understatement.

Also, rogues now can kill you really quick. With the addition of Sweet n Crunchy Fruit Bread they pop that and you can't freeze them to get away. Even when you find opening to get away with scatter shot, you still have slow poison on you. You can deploy frost trap fd combo but rogue bypasses that with sprint. Your only option then is to use anti venom but rogue can range attack you with slow and again apply slow poison. In that case you can't use anti venom again because it has a cooldown but then again you are relying on first aid to do that. Wing clip nerf and removal of counterattack in this case is a further salt on the wound. I do not want rogues nerfed but what I am saying is that ranged hunters now have significantly reduced means of escaping. We already have big deadzone which in TBC was severely reduced and all these reductions in melee CC makes it impossible to get away. Warriors also, pretty similar situation in fights with them.
