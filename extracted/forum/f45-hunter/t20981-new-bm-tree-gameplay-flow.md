Here are the changes I’d like to see:

Ferocity Talent: Change it from a 5-point talent to a 3-point talent.
Make it give +5% crit per talent point.

New Talent (Beast Mastery tree): Add a talent that makes guns more effective for BM hunters.
 2 talents.

Baited Shot Rework:
Instead of doing 125% weapon damage, have it scale with ranged attack power or deal a % of the pet’s attack damage.
Instead of only triggering when the pet attacks, make it so both the hunter’s attacks and the pet’s attacks can restore focus.
Keep the same trigger cooldown, but give the hunter and pet separate cooldowns.
Casting Baited Shot should put Steady Shot on cooldown.

Of course, the numbers would need fine-tuning to avoid damage being too high or too low. The real benefit of these changes is that they create two distinct playstyles: faster weapons becoming the optimal choice for Beast Mastery and slower weapons remaining stronger for Marksmanship. This separation would make each spec feel more unique and rewarding to play.
