I'd rather simply make arrows/bullets a little cheaper. I enjoy having to refill my quiver, as it adds just a little bit of immersion. However, Hunters are the only class that have to pay gold to be able to use 90% of their spells, on top of paying for drinks like other mana-users, and consumables like everyone else.

Not to mention Hunters are denied a bag. Similar to Warlocks, sure, but Warlocks don't have to pay gold to use their spells, just mana. And they can refill their mana and health extremely effectively without drinking or eating.

In short, it adds an expense that's unique to Hunters and I don't think it would cause any ripples if ammo was cheaper - not free - just cheaper.

If a non-engineering Hunter wants to maximize their DPS with Thorium ammo, they have to buy Thorium Shells from the AH, at which point they're completely at the mercy of other players. That's a little weird, isn't it?
