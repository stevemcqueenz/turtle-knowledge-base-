# BM hunter and spec

## Post 145015 by Dezhan (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-13T09:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145015#p145015 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I like that you’re working on making BM a complete spec.

However, for PvE, I feel like you’re forced into building it like this, which doesn’t give the spec much flavor:
![Image](https://iili.io/FtJ9yn2.jpg)

This isn’t unique to BM—many classes/specs are basically locked into one talent setup where shifting points means losing DPS.

The problem is that BM lacks a clear identity. For example:

MM: Buffs + ranged with strong single-target damage.

Survival: Melee with strong single-target, great AoE, and in-combat traps.

BM: Ranged with maybe strong single-target damage… but nothing that really stands out when grouped with BM hunter.

You also can’t make your pet tankier than an MM hunter’s without losing threat.
And the talent that makes your pet move faster costs too much DPS to justify picking it.

Overall, BM just feels a bit flat.
Do you agree, or do you see it differently?
