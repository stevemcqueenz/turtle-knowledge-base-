## Post 171464 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2026-03-20T16:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171464#p171464 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Whatever the changes, pure BM for leveling is imo always the go to, with around lv50 being your respect threshold if you want to focus more into another spec. I'd honestly remove those Lethal Shots points and put them into Pathfinding and Bestial Discipline. The more damage your pet does the harder it'll lose aggro, and that just makes taking on 3 mobs at the same time easier. Pathfinding is good if you don't have a pet with dash or charge, and switching to aspect of the cheetah is more optimal for going from mob to mob than mounting, if your target goal is mob farming. Slap on some spirit gear, and you'll have 0 down time eating/drinking

My 2 cents at least

edit: P.S. 2-3 points into Frenzy is generally enough to keep the effect active at all times, so long as you have a fast attacking pet.

edit 2: Ignore my spirit comment too. With Aspect of the Viper you have pretty much no down time
