# A Melee Weaving aspect

## Post 98223 by Steakhouse (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-06-30T17:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98223#p98223 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Melee weaving is an interesting, engaging style of play that takes advantage of the fact that your melee swing and autoshot timers are separate, meaning that you can keep both on cooldown to increase your damage. The easiest way to do this is to keep one target at range thanks to your pet, whilst you facetank another, switching targets between each attack, but it also has single target applications by weaving in and out of melee range to shoot, melee, shoot, melee, etc. This requires to be in aspect of the cheetah however, as otherwise your movement speed is too slow to get out of your deadzone before your autoshot cooldown is over.

My suggestion is to either slightly rework Aspect of the Wolf to be the Melee Weaving aspect, or to add a new Aspect entirely, with the following effect :

> Increases melee power by X and ranged attack power by Y. Increases run speed by 20% (value not final). Lowers the maximum range of your shots to 10 yards.

The ranged power increase would likely need to be lesser than that of Hawk, otherwise Hunters that prefer playing ranged will be incentivized to play in Wolf, right outside of their deadzone, making Hawk mostly obsolete. Alternatively, the ranged power increase could be dropped entirely ; the meat and potatoes of this change is the run speed, without stepping over Cheetah's usecases (kiting, traveling, escaping).

Alternatively, as currently melee weaving is the best way of maximizing damage without spending mana (e.g. when waiting for your Spirit-based regen), an alternate description would be the following :

> Your right-hand and two-handed melee swings restore mana proportionally to your attack speed: slower attacks regenerate more mana. Increases run speed by 15%. Lowers the maximum range of your shots to 10 yards.

Limiting the effect to right hand and two handed is to prevent complex normalization math such that dual wield doesnt regenerate twice as fast as 2H, and would give hunters a much-needed mana battery as an alternative to chugging mana pots on cooldown. It would also be an interesting way to play whilst waiting for our mana to regenerate.

In addition to all this, a deep Survival talent could reduce the deadzone range to further incentivize this alternate way of playing the Hunter.

As far as I know, Wolf is in need of a rework anyways as it is only useful for autoattacking and raptor striking (melee offtank builds usualy play in Monkey for the %damage reduction from talents and Mongoose Bite procs), so this could be something to do with it.

## Post 119536 by Steakhouse (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-02-10T15:33:00Z

Bump. I'm currently leveling a BM hunter and, with the (welcome !) absence of Steady Shot, I find myself weaving in Cheetah in single target. I've also heard that Surv and BM are struggling to keep up the DPS rankings. This sounds like a good opportunity to introduce an aspect that allows Surv and BM hunters to weave.

All aspects are suboptimal for weaving: Wolf and Hawk only buff half of a weaving hunter's attacks and make them too slow: their shot and auto-attack timers will finish before they're in range to use either, even with slow weaps. Cheetah's movespeed bonus still makes it the best weaving aspect as it stands, but the risk of getting Dazed, especially while in the deadzone makes it an extremely risky strategy compared to simply going Marks and spamming Steady Shot in Hawk at a safe range, or full-melee Surv and sticking to Wolf. Furthermore, melee weaving at max DPS is very, very mana intensive as you're spending mana on Raptor, Mongoose, Traps, Multi-Shot / Carve, and possibly Sting if not Surv spec'd.

To be clear, I'm not saying that Surv and BM needs to *match* Marks' DPS, as Surv also brings excellent utility to the table with its combat trap and ability to still deal decent damage when it can't go into melee, such as during the Heigan dance, which isn't the case of Marks, whose damaged is greatly reduced if it gets stuck in melee range. Likewise BM being the best solo spec gives it a niche that doesn't require changing. Lastly, CC2 Surv is still in its infancy and hasn't been experimented on enough, so it's likely that better optimization will bring its numbers up. But throwing the playstyle a bone could be fun ! And again, all current aspects have drawbacks when weaving.

My suggestion, then, is the following :

Keep Wolf and Hawk as they are, and either:

 - Add a new dedicated weaving aspect. e.g. : Aspect of the Cobra (which can bite, ie melee, or spray venom, ie ranged) : Your melee attacks cause your next ranged attack to grant Invigoration, which increases your movement speed by 30% and restore X% of your maximum mana every second, for 3 seconds. Your ranged attacks also cause your next melee attack to trigger Invigoration.
 - Add a third-row BM or Surv talent that does the following : Aspect of the Cheetah's movement speed bonus is reduced to 15%, but can no longer cause Dazing. When succesfully striking an opponent with either a melee or ranged attack whilst in Aspect of the Cheetah, its movement speed bonus is restored to 30%.

TLDR: Hunter weaving needs high movement speed to perform, and is very mana hungry. Cheetah somewhat solves the movement speed problem, but the chance to get Dazed makes it too risky in dungeons and raids. Allowing the playstyle to have bonus movespeed without risking a daze would increase its viability, and some kind of mana battery would be icing on the cake.

Speaking without the actual knowledge of how it would play right now, but looking at the talents:
I'd see melee weaving as a hybrid build between ranged and melee talent builds.
First, looking at what BM offers, which are pet buffs and barely anything else we can remark, we can leave this talent tree unmentioned here.
So, melee weaving  could be a hybrid build between MM and Surv. The problem here is that the talents that we get from both trees are focused on specializing, and drive us away from weaving. MM benefits ranged only, and would get almost nothing from melee attacks. Same goes for surv and melee.
This idea is backed too by the lvl 19 (11 talent points) spells from each tree. Mostly for the Steady Shoot, which by having a cast time completely keeps players that want to be efficient on its use from going into melee.
About Carve, I can't really tell but I don't see how going in for Carve would be good melee weaving (other than the spell being strong right now and worth it just by itself, but not because there is an added value to the rotation when weaving).
Another talent worth mentioning is Stinging Nettle in the Surv tree, which allows hunters to apply Serpent Sting in melee. Serpent Sting would be the perfect reason to go from melee into ranged: we apply the DoT and then back to melee. Not a reason to do int once we get that talent.

I'd suggest to have some spell (either melee or ranged) that boost the next spell or attack (now opposite, ranged or melee) so that we get something from melee weaving. IE: we have some melee spells that share GCD which will give our next ranged attack/Arcane Shot/whatever some extra effect, like a bleed, an Attack Speed buff (bonus if it's just for melee/ranged) or even we get mana regen over time or just mana on each ranged attack. That would be some very interesting gameplay.
