# Allow Pets to keep their original name

## Post 147544 by Mekk (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-21T18:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147544#p147544 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One of the few things that I liked that was eventually added to WoW Retail at some point was that your pet kept its original name, with spaces and special characters.  This made hunting down Rares and Elites and other named beasts much more fun and rewarding.  Instead of getting "Raptor" you got "Takk the Leaper," or instead of "Spider" you got "Sri'skulk" or "Krethis Shadowspinner" or "Lady Sathrah".  I'm honestly surprised that TWoW has not implemented this change already.
