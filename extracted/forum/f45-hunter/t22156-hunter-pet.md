## Post 153667 by Aronymous — 2025-09-29T12:58:00Z

Some tips on how it works: You can dismiss a pet while you're not using them, as you probably know.
This comes with a bit of unhappiness if you dismiss them usually, but it's useful to know that you can dismiss them in rest areas without any happiness loss.

Another thing I took a while to realise is that when you feed a pet, they get the "pet fed" status. So when feeding them, you waste food if you feed them many pieces at once. So you ought to wait until the "pet fed" status is gone before feeding another piece.

Maybe you knew those things already, but it took me some time to realise it myself, and once I learned those things, I felt the pet system was more comfortable, as I was not using so much food to keep them happy as I was before.

Another thing that's useful to know is that you can keep 2 pets at the same time even if you only have 1 slot in the stables. You just leave 1 in the stable, train a second one, and if you ever wanted to use the first one again, you can swap their places in the stable. Maybe that was obvious, but it took me a while before I realised I could do it.

So I have two pets who eat different food types. 1 eats meat, another eats fish, fruit, bread and fungus, so that means I can switch out my pet depending on what types of food I have more of at the time.
Meats are always cheap in butchers anyway.

I know this isn't the solution you were asking for, and maybe you knew all of that already, but in case this helps the pet system feel any bit more comfortable, I offer this advice.

## Post 153669 by Azcron (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-29T13:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153669#p153669 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

To gain loyalty requires both xp gained and time spent. Basically, to gain a loyalty level, your pet needs to, no matter what, gain at least 5% of the XP needed for your next level. But there was also a time component on every loyalty level past Unruly. For example, to get to level 4 from level 5, you not only needed the experience gain, but an hour had to pass. This is why sometimes your pet would ding loyalty while you're standing around in town, you had gained the experience, but the time had to pass as well. Always feed your pet and keep them happy. Time requirements are: Rank2 - 0 minutes, Rank3 - 30 minutes, Rank4 - 45 minutes, Rank5 - 60 minutes, and Rank6 - 90 minutes

> **Aronymous wrote: Mon Sep 29, 2025 12:58 pm**
> Another thing I took a while to realise is that when you feed a pet, they get the "pet fed" status. So when feeding them, you waste food if you feed them many pieces at once. So you ought to wait until the "pet fed" status is gone before feeding another piece.

Additionally, if your pet enters combat while the Pet Fed buff is active, it will immediately cancel the effect.  So when you feed your pet, let the buff finish before going into combat.  Also, make sure you are feeding your pet food that is closer to your own level; lower level food does not benefit the pet as much.

Getting a pet from Loyalty 1 (Rebellious) to Loyalty 2 (Unruly) does feel like it takes a long time, and during that initial rank, the Pet can seem like it requires a lot of food, but if you keep the above rules in mind, its really not that bad.

As soon as you tame your pet, when you have a few spare moments, get them up to a Happy state by feeding them one food at a time and letting the buff run its entire duration. If, when you feed them, they reach Happy at the very end of the buff, go ahead and feed them one more time (this should put them a the cap, which will take longer before your next feed).  From that point on, whenever they get Content, take a moment to feed them and let the buff run its full duration.  Once they reach Unruly, their food requirements drop quite a bit; each rank lowers the frequency in which your pet must be fed.  And as Azcron stated, you want to be using them in combat during this time, as you want them gaining XP.

When feeding your pet, check your combat log to see how much happiness the pet gains per tick. Appropriate level food provides 35 happiness per tick, while lower food provides 17 or 8 per tick. If your pet doesn't gain 35 per tick, upgrade your food.
