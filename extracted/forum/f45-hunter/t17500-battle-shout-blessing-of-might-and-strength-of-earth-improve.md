For most mechanics that means I'm getting hit unless I drop all damage and get out.  Melee on the other hand get targeted as a group more, because it is a target rich environment.  But they can keep dps rolling while moving out of danger for most mechanics.  That's the trade off, A playstyle where you are more likely to get targeted and hence need to be more on the go to keep damage up vs one where prep and encounter knowledge can benefit you as you find better spots to be.  Knowing that if you are the target of an attack your DPS is going to drop to survive.

There is very little benefit to having self sufficiency in a group/raid environment so I would definitely trade of better buff scaling for that, especially in the damage department.  It's not like MM hunters are kitting mobs around to kill them, we tag them, run away and the FD when the raid is ready to kill that target.  So I would gladly do a little less personal unbuffed damage to be able to benefit for a wide array of raid buffs.

I understand the value of ranged DPS, I think where we differ in opinion is the downside that offsets that value.  Personally I don't believe the classes scalability to buffs should enter in the equation as part of that downside.

Melee is in a target rich environment for raid mechanics, so they are continually keep up DPS while on the move to dodge said mechanics.  A melee's position is always behind  and slightly to the side  of the boss, for every encounter.
Their focus is not the larger picture of the boss arena for the most part, it is their smaller melee arena which is usually in a constant state of flux because of the constant disruption of boss mechanics.

Ranged on the other hand are rarely required to clump together, quite the opposite we usually have a wide spread across the boss arena.  So larger movements in our position cost us DPS.  Sure we might have an instant or 2 that we can fire off to keep DPS up slightly but part of our design is to know how an encounter is going to play out in terms of the use of area in the arena and position accordingly to maximize DPS.

Our scalability in terms of how buffs operate for our class really have no part in this balance between melee and ranged DPS.

None of that has anything to do with how MM as a spec currently scales with the attack power buffs.  MM hunters have always been a crit focused damage dealer, that is what the whole spec centers around.  Always has, and always will.  But the downside to that is crit is a negative sloping parabolic scaling attribute.  The more you have the less valuable it becomes.  The diminishing point really starts to hit hard when crit drops below a .9% damage increase per 1% crit.  That point is already being reached in Naxx gear with raid consumes.  We will need the scalability of attack power buffs to stay competitive in the damage scene.  The Trueshot aura change is a step in the right direction for sure.  But without a way via gear to get a substantial amount of haste or armor pen MM damage is going to fall off.  Unrestricting these AP buffs from melee only is an easy solution to that problem.

If your raid is having issues, you swapping from survival to MM isn't going to fix them.  That is just a cope.  If you as a melee aren't getting hit by mechanics then you swapping to a ranged spec isn't going to make you get hit less by mechanics.  Now if your raid is way to melee heavy then sure swapping to ranged helps but that brings in a whole other aspect of the survival thing.

Why did we create another melee class in an already melee heavy era of the game?  But that is a whole other topic and quite frankly that cat is out of the bag isn never going back so no point in having the discussion.
