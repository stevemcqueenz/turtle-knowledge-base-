## Post 112507 by Jan125 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-13T05:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112507#p112507 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Note: With CC2, foxes now belong to their own family.
They can learn Grace (Dodge buff on themselves). I haven't checked Dash.

Lupos is a regular wolf.

In terms of raw damage, Wind Serpent (Lightning Breath spam), Serpent (Highest damage mod, no spammable ability), and Cat (Spammable and high damage mod) still reign.
For tanking, Owl, Bat, or Carrion Bird with Screech, due to AoE threat.

Sometimes, your pet may die, even though we now have AoE damage reduction with Avoidance.
Keep in mind that Furious Howl only affects melee hits.
