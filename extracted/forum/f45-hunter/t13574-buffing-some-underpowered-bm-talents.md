# Buffing some underpowered BM Talents.

## Post 91946 by Jammyzz — 2024-04-02T20:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91946#p91946 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Not sure what the class changes have in store but I think for BM hunter there are a few lacking talents.

I think these could do with a bufff:

Current
Endurance Training:
Increases your pets total HP by 15%

New
Endurance Training:
Increases your pets total HP by 15% and chance to resist stun and fear effects by 15%, Will help pet with getting feared or stunned and maybe losing aggro, mostly for leveling.

Current
Spirit Bond: While your pet is active, you and your pet will regenerate 2% HP every 10 sec.

New
Spirit Bond: While your pet is active, you and your pet will regenerate 2% HP every 5 sec, and your pet will gain 10% of your attributes to his own.

This will help BM Hunters pets scale somewhat with their gear.

Current
Bestial Disipline
Increases your pets focus regeneration by 10/20%

New
Bestial Disipline
Increases your pets focus regeneration by 25%/50%, and your pets chance to hit by 2%/4%

I dont know anyone who takes this talent as its so lacking and points spent elsewhere, doubling it and adding hit will make it worthwhile.
