# With the upcoming patch, Hunters need TBC style deadzone

## Post 170896 by Vrag — 2026-03-15T17:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170896#p170896 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Thanks for devs for listening to hunter complaints and suggestions, i think you guys addressed a lot of hunter stuff in the future patch. How that will turn out, we will see, I don't want to say anything ahead of time but I see you guys took into consideration what community was talking about.

Due to the upcoming patch changes, I do have to say this regarding the deadzone. In the next patch warriors are going to get a no cooldown for hamstring. I don't mind that nor want to prevent warrior from having that but it does remind me of the deadzone problem hunters have in PVP and in PVE and the issue that is going to get worse in PVP.

In the upcoming patch when warrior gets a no cooldown for hamstring, the second the warrior gets close to a hunter its going to be gg. Not in every situation but in 90% of situations its going to be over. If hunters had a tbc style deadzone they would be able to put a few shots in from close distance since wing clip on warrior is 60% movement speed reduction and hamstring on a hunter is 50%. In this case its not possible to get out of the deadzone because even if you do a warrior is going to charge at you and hamstring's slow effect gives him plenty of time for charge/intercept to reset cooldown. With tbc style deadzone you could get a few shots in from close distance, otherwise you can't do anything. It can get even worse if he disarms you, then you really can't do anything about it. Inb4 "If you get charged by a warrior you failed as a hunter".

As a hunter in PVP you will find yourself in many different situations that are not just huge open fields. And even on a huge open field, on this server atleast, you can get charged from a longer distance in certain cases because of latency and lag, the charge can be really buggy. You won't always be able to kite warriors, certain situations simply won't allow that. As a ranged hunter you can use traps with feign death, but the macro isn't always reliable and doesn't always work. You can freeze a warrior in this situation but he can trinket it and you won't survive till the next trap. If we could have tbc deadzone that would allow us to use concussive shot from closer distance also.

I don't want this to be interpreted as "nerf the warrior", just allow us a chance to get out of such situations. We used to have Counterattack ability, a 5 second stun which no longer exists, our wing clip which had no cooldown is nerfed to 3 sec cooldown. All those abilities were easily accessible to MM hunters in the survival tree but by removing these our CC is significantly reduced. This isn't just an issue when playing against a warrior, but because of the new no cooldown on hamstring change I am using it as a main example because it is definitely going to be number 1 problem.

I would also like to address that dueling area range is really small for MM hunters and more often then not you end up leaving the dueling area because of avoiding the deadzone. You then try to avoid "fleeing the duel" and it messes you up because you end up not kiting properly and you lose. Also, arenas like sunstrider court are already an LOS hell for ranged hunters, its a small arena and hard to gain distance and camera is being buggy with central circle of pillars, half of the time I don't know what is going on.

For PVE, in raids, its really annoying when tanks move and they move towards the raid and you get deadzoned, while others are doing damage you are finding a spot where you are not deadzoned to get back into combat. This is also an issue with big bosses, mobs like Maexxna for instance. You would think you could as a hunter, stand at a certain distance and shoot but because of the deadzone such big enemy gives, you have to back off really far and then shoot. It messes with your perception and again you are finding a spot where you can shoot while others began DPSing.
