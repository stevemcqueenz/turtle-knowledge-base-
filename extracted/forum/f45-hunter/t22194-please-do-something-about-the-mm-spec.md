# Please do something about the MM spec

## Post 154013 by Vrag — 2025-10-02T10:06:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154013#p154013 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Right now I feel like MM is doing less damage than it should and that it is an alpha version of an unfinished class.

My suggestion is to either replace or change ability Aimed shot, because there is literally no reason to use it as an MM Hunter if you can cast 2 steady shots in that casting time of aimed shot that will cause higher damage and you are not risking getting interrupted. I would rather replace it with Kill shot or something else.

Arcane shot is not completely useless but its used as a filler shot. In the early levels it is introduced as a core shot for hunter, but at 60 you are barely using it. Arcane shot does only really weak arcane damage and no physical damage. Please do something with it.

Volley has 1 min cooldown and does a stunning 92 arcane damage, again, no physical damage. Please do something with this.

Trueshot aura is a weird one. You constantly have to put it on in BGs along with the aspect. Why is this toggled? Just make it so its always activated since you put the point in the talent tree.

Serpent Sting - might as well never use it even with the 5/5 talent. The talent is only useful because it increases mana drain of Viper Sting, but barely. The damage it does is terrible.

Disengage - you can rework it to do a leap backwards

The hunter's mark talent is not good

The 2 new talent slots are cool though. The endless quiver makes you bursty at times but the second talent that causes bleeding, I do not notice any change in damage. I would rather that it activates expose weakness or something along those lines, or simply buff the bleeding. But overall this tree is not really interesting and compared to other 2 now it feels unfinished and more often than not, it feels like I am playing an alpha version of the class and some of these abilities are underwhelming and underpowered.

In bgs it feels like I am shooting pebbles at people despite being really good geared with maxed out enchantments and proper rotation. Despite  this, in raids people who have tier 1-2 from other classes can 100% outdps me. How can you then say "MM is in a good spot right now"?. MM Hunters have never been in a worse spot damage wise. People I know who played hunters stopped playing them because of this. I remember hunters doing good damage pre nax and seeing hunters being played by people in general, now I rarely see them.

I main the MM hunter, I love its playstyle but I think these things are an issue and not just for MM hunters, but hunters in general. In PVE its hard to keep up with the damage of other classes, even when you are ultra geared. In PVP I am shooting pebbles at people. Overall, MM hunter is insanely nerfed to oblivion to the point people stopped playing a hunter because of it. Some people don't want to play survival, me included. I will probably be switching to my rogue because why should I torture myself? I think devs shouldn't be afraid to take ideas from TBC or WOTLK in order to make things more interesting. I would love some new abilities or rework of the existing ones.
