# Mongoose Bite and what it means to be a "Melee Hunter"

## Post 104152 by therealrexxar — 2024-10-21T17:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104152#p104152 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ahhh, "melee hunter", our favorite meme spec. The moment we have been so patiently waiting for has finally come...

But first let's make sure it's done properly. This new topic won't raise the issues that the other topic has raised as that would be unnecessary, even though I agree with most points, especially giving us back Counterattack and the Wing Clip CD being reduced.

I think more changes should be made to make everyone happy. I have some suggestions:
 1.1) Change Mongoose Bite to make it require dodge again.
 1.2) Add/change a talent to allow MB to be usable without having to dodge. Perhaps by using an already existing 1-pointer talent from the Survival Tree (Rapid Strikes seems to be the best option imo).
Justification:
Changing MB to require dodge again might not be the most popular suggestion as it's not very convenient but I think it's necessary not only for the Hunter class as a whole but also for the melee hunter archetype. This way you can still use MB if you want to go deep Survival (which you will want to do anyway as you need the new Vicious Bite to make it worth using). This change would mean many things:
a) From a PvP standpoint, it will allow to separate the specs more, helping a bit with the nightmare of balancing ranged vs melee hunter in the future. MM and BM hunter can stay the same while the Vicious Bite talent (*) allows it to be used for Survival, with adjustments to the talent if necessary allowing MB to be balanced much more easily. Everyone is happy.
 * (great talent btw, 10/10. And it is very similar to the beta talent "Improved Mongoose Bite" as a bonus. Sometimes simple is better)
b) It will allow for more freedom of choice between specs, including for hybrids (more on that in my 2nd suggestion). Choosing to be a melee hunter vs ranged hunter should not be as linear as going Survival vs MM (or BM if you rely more heavily on pets). BM should also be an option for melee hunters. We all know Rexxar is a Beastmaster, not a Survival Hunter.
c) From a PvE standpoint, it doesn't really make much difference except BM melee hunters won't use it. However, as I said before, it won't be worth using without deep Survival talents anyway. Also, you won't be able to use it while leveling until you pick 21 talents tree in the Survival tree, which might not make leveling as melee as interactive, but in return, you will be able to focus on other talents in the meantime.
d) Conceptually (last but not least), it doesn't make sense for MB to be usable without dodging. The theme around that ability is to counterattack (speaking of which, please give us Counterattack back   scared_turtle_head  ) swiftly after dodging, just as mongooses do in real life. Allowing MB to be usable all the time by picking the Rapid Strikes talent seems to be a good option to me and keeps the flavor of the ability.

 2) Change the Killer Instinct and Lightning Reflexes talents. Both talents are deep in the Survival Tree and they should feel less mandatory for playing melee hunter.
Justification:
I'm sure most players think of Rexxar as the prime example of what a melee hunter is. It should come as no surprise to many that Rexxar, the archetype of melee hunter, is a Beastmaster in WC3. I believe that these changes are forcing us choose between one and another, which personally I think it's unfair. One should be able to be a beastmaster and still play as a melee hunter, they should not be mutually exclusive.
However, I feel that currently devs seem to be aiming for BM to be the "ranged but stronger pet" tree since, the way things are right now, playing as ranged will always feel much more powerful than melee if you go the Bestial Wrath route, even if you pick talents that buff your melee abilities in the Survival Tree as the remaining talents. Choosing Bestial Wrath means not being able to choose both Killer Instinct and Lightning Reflexes (both deep in the Survival Tree), which feel mandatory for melee with the way things are right now as they provide the necessary scaling for melee to be viable.
I believe that the new Survival tree needs a better arrangement. Deep Survival shouldn't be so tied to melee damage and the whole tree should have its damage better distributed among the rows. Otherwise, melee will always suck as BM. This tree makes it impossible for BMs to melee as well.
And before you think that this new tree is how it was intended to be based on the beta Survival Tree (pre-1.12), I'll just say it's not. The beta Survival tree did not have such mandatory talents like the two mentioned above. So it's not even close. Some of them were good but none of them were required. And that is what this discussion is all about. I am all for more talents for support the melee playstyle, in fact I advertise often in the discord channel for it and even used the beta Survival Tree before as an argument for more melee support.

Thank you devs for listening to us.   satisfied_turtle
