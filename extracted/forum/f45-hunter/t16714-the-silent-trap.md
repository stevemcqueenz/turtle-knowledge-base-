# The Silent Trap

## Post 114170 by Mac (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-12-25T02:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114170#p114170 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hunter traps are affected by silence, meaning that you cannot put down a trap while silenced. This doesn't make sense as putting down a trap wouldn't have a verbal component to it like a spell. In fact you would want to place your trap as silently and stealthily as possible. This oversight negatively affects Survival Hunters as they can place traps in combat and it's a good portion of their DPS. Therefore please make it so that Hunters can put down their traps while silenced. Thank you and have a merry Christmas.
