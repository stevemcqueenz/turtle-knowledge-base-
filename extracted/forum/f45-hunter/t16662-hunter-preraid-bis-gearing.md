I'ts a pretty cool tool that ive been using while I try to make my own turtle wow specific hunter pre-raid BiS list. Im starting with the standard vanilla items but also stuff from Hateforge Quarry, Stormwind Vault, karazhan Crypt, Black Morass, turtle wow specific quests, silvermoon remnant reputation rewards etc. Chance to hit requirement is also different with the weapon skill book and changes to hit chance calculations on Turtle. I'm currently using 7% Chance to hit as the required minimum and not considering chance to hit beyond that. There is a lot of variability surrounding the conversion between agility, crit chance and attack power that depends upon gear level, current spec and buffs but to compare by "AP equivalent" I've sort of landed on:

 1 Agi = 3 APeq
 1 crit chance = 30 APeq
 1 AP = 1 APeq

This only really works for PVE and marksmanship builds as it doesn't make any considerations for stamina, strength or armor as would be necessary for evaluating items for PvP or for a Survival builds. Ive been working on it for a week or so but it just takes a while to build out.

I'll post up the google sheet when I'm finished.  It will probably be ridiculed by the community for being flawed or incomplete but that is probably the best way to tweak it and make it more accurate.

I'll send a PM but the long and short of it is this; until the exact impact that haste/increased attack speed has on DPS can be predicted and replicated, I cant give an answer with complete certainty. That being said, trying to figure out the definitive pre-raid BIS list is still a fun academic exercise. I love that the items, talents and weapon skill calculations are all different enough from vanilla that we can't just copy paste a list from 20 years ago and have to figure it out. The testing I have done with attack speed has not given the expected results, so I'm still not sure where an item like the [Cloverlink Belt](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61758) really falls in the pecking order. Additionally, there is the issue of what parameters to include and what is the endpoint. I've been working under the assumption that the goal/endpoint is most DPS possible in PvE setting using a marksmanship build. As for parameters, i was just talking with my brother about this, should reputation rewards be included? Should PvP items? If so do you put an arbitrary cap on PvP rank like rank 8 or rank 10? Some of that boils down to principle vs practicality, what is mathematically perfect vs what is reasonable.
