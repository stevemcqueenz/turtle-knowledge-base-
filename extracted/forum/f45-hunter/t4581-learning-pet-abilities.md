Old mistake.

People argued once that bite and claw shouldnt be used to together, because
claw will never allow bite to go off because it will use up the focus before bite.

That is wrong and you can test it with your DPS meters on a target dummy, I used 1 minute as
time limit (I used Shagus DPS meter).

Without bite, my cat deals roughly 124 DPS over 1 minute to target dummy.
With bite and claw on my pets DPS goes up to 139.
Level 60 orc + sabertooth cat.

There are 2 reasons for that. First of all your pet isnt constantly energy starved in a real scenario.
In between targets, your pet has enough time to regenerate enough energy to use both, Bite and Claw,
at least once, wich is enough since most target die under 10 seconds, and thats bite's cooldown.

The second reason is, that your pets focus doesnt go down to 0 all the time.
Sometimes your pet gets to keep 10 focus, and when the next focus comes in, your pet will have enough
focus to use bite.

Wich is why I have 2 cats, 1 for pvp, and 1 for pve, because you need both, bite and claw,
but in PVP you dont need growl, you want dash and prowl. In solo PVE/farming you need growl.
And pet can only learn 4 abilities. And normaly you would need 3. 1x PVP, 1x solo PVE with growl, 1x raid PVE.

Alternativly you can visit pet trainer all the time. But there are more stable masters then pet trainers around the world.

EDIT: Oh and by the way, this is all assuming that your having your pets abilities on auto cast! Thats fine and 99% of hunters do it, but real pros will cast even their focus dumps manually, and they will always wait to get enough
focus for bite since bite has a better damage/focus ratio (more efficient).
