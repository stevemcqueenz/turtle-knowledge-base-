Starting in patch 3.0.2 (pre-Wrath of the Lich King), Blizzard gave hunter pets a passive called Avoidance.

- Avoidance (Rank 1): Reduces AOE damage taken by 75% from non-player sources.
- Avoidance (Rank 2): Increased that reduction to 90%, introduced in patch 3.1.0 (Ulduar).
The whole point was to negate boss mechanics meant for raid members to dodge without unduly punishing hunters.

Honestly, this seems like a better (and more balanced) fix then removing the pet altogether.

Another potential avenue might be to apply avoidance only while mend pet is active. This way it turns the passive into an active and makes mend pet more beneficial, the avoidance more reactive, and less of a "I don't even have to consider the consequences of ignoring my pet."
