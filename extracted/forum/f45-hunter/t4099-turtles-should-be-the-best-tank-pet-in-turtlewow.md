It just seems fitting to me, that turtles should be desirable pets for tanking, at least in this server. I suggest either increasing their attack speed so they build more hate, give them a dash so they are just as desirable as boars (wouldnt be very logical tho) or, my favorite idea, an aoe threat ability that lowers enemy accuracy like those damn snapjaws in the barrens like to do to me. Call it mud spash, give it a reasonabel cooldown so they can use it at least twice per pull, and BAM, you got a pocket tank for the first few dungeons in the game (with a group that isn't too gung-ho with dps and lets your pet build aggro a bit)

Turtles are the tankiest pets in vanilla with an armor rating of 1.13, wich is higher then any other pet.
On top of that, they have Shell Shield, an abilitie that makes your turtle even tankier.
So I dont quite know what else you want.

And if you want to tank dungeons with ur pet you have 2 options:
 - Either take a druid with you that can give thorns to your pet if your lazy
 - Or manually micro-manage your pet and auto attack each mobs 1 time and then switch back to main target

Dont get me wrong, Hunter pets really need some love, but what you want from turtles is already there.

You demand just too much, you want a turtle that is super fast, is super tanky to the point where it can replace
a player, and you also want it to hold AOE aggro on its own, consistently, on its own, without you having to
micro-manage anything.

Thats just too much asked imho.
