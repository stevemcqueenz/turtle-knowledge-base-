# @Devs 1.17.2 : Impact on Hunter class / why haste is good

## Post 104588 by Juzam — 2024-10-23T18:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104588#p104588 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi devs, first of all, thanks for the amazing work you did with Twow.

Before reviewing the changes, a "quick" presentation : I'm Focus, a hunter in the best PVE guild on Tel'abim (Mbappe Speedhack), we managed to kill ES HM weeks before BWL was released and we were less than 30 to do that, we also got first C'thun server kill as well as KT recently (both times the day after it was released).

I'm not the best at pressing keys or the one with the best reflexes, far from it, i start to get old  turtle_tongue_head   but one thing i'm good at is theorycrafting.

I played hunter back in the day from vanilla to Wotlk, then stopped for years, when i saw wow classic hardmode, nostalgia arrived, but after watching at people dying from server lags, i finally chosed to start on Tel'abim who was about to open.

The main reason i came on Twow was because i discovered you added steady shot as hunter skill and haste / armor penetration as new stats. I knew then after looking at your database than hunter would be very (very) good at dpsing unlike back in the day during vanilla where hunter was the worst dps.   hiding_smth_turtle_head

The reason for that is haste (from the stuff ofc but even more important from improved aspect proc : 5% chance per ranged attack to increase ranged attack speed by 30% for 12s), for other physical damage dealers, 1% haste only increase auto-attack damage, because auto attack is 50 to 60% of their total damage, it's a 0.5 to 0.6% damage increase. However for hunter it's nearly as good as for casters.

Because our rotation is basicaly : autoshot, steady shot, autoshot, steady shot, auto, steady, etc. (with one steady being replaced by a multishot once every 10s). So if we shot 10% faster, not only we will shot 10% more autoshot, but also 10% more steady shot.

Because autoshot + steadyshot represent 90% of our total damage against single target and 85%+ overall (the last 10% / 15% come from multishot who is not affected by haste), that means 1% haste = +0.85 to +0.9% damage and the proc from improved aspect was +25/27% damage for 12s (before the current big nerf) which was Huge.

Crit have some kind of diminishing returns as you can see here : ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/bzAG9N8.jpeg)
The more crit rate you have, the less gaining 1% crit increase your damage.

No diminishing returns for haste, let say your base ranged attack speed including quiver haste is 2.5 and you gain 1%, 1%, 1%, 2%, 2%, 2% and 3% haste from different sources. You could say it's 12% haste total so your new attack speed would be 2.5/1.12=2.232 but in game it's different, it's actually 2.5/1.01/1.01/1.01/1.02/1.02/1.02/1.03=2.219. the difference is thin but it's still better. In fact it means than no matter how much haste you got or haste effect or proc you are under,
gaining 1 extra % of haste will always be give the same damage increase (0.85 to 0.9%).

That means that once you're in full naxx gear, because of your very high crit rate, 1% haste will become as good as 1% crit.

I wanted to explain why haste is good because it will help when reviewing the new patch. I will talk first about PVE then later about pvp.

 **Beast Master spec**

The 2 bigs problems of BM were no scaling for the pet as well as a lack of Pet's survavibility. You adressed both, you did a pretty good job.
Few things to note however :
 -You nerfed pretty hard "swift aspects" : as i explained before haste is very good for hunter, this talent was (with mortal shots) the best in term of average ranged damage increase. You halved the damage bonus (from +26% damage to +13% under proc), but even if you doubled the proc chance to compensate, sure the uptime will increase but it will not be doubled, far from it. Giving +20% haste instead of +15 would be better, it would still be a nerf to the current version but not as big.
 -Intimidation : While it was indeed useless against mobs immune to stun, it's was still doing the job well 95 times out of 100, the job beeing for the Pet, to immediatly take back aggro.
With the new change, while it will be an improvement against mobs immune to stuns, against regular mobs, the pet will fail to take back aggro much more often than currently because a pet spend most of his time OOF (out of focus) and just use an ability every few second when he regenerate enough focus to use it before being OOF again.
The change I propose to resolve the problem is this one : reduce the threat buff duration from 8 to 4s but increase the threat by 100% instead of 50 and make Intimidation also restore 50 to 60 focus to the pet. This way when you'll use Intimidation, because the pet will also gain some focus, he will be able to do either growl + bite, or growl + claw, or lightning breath, or 2 claw or growl + screech, etc. This way it should be able to take back aggro most of the time.

Ferocity / Frenzy : it would be nice to reduce them to 3 pt talents because BM hunter still need to put 20 talent point outside of BM spec to be competitive in raid.

 **Survival new melee spec**

i was not a fan of a melee spec for hunter, not because it could not be good or fun to play, and to be honest, the way you did it will be good and fun to play, but because if you want to play a melee, there is already plenty of classes and specs to use with a large variety of gameplay, if you want to play a magical ranged damage dealer (caster), it's the same, but if you want to play a physical ranged damage healer (archer), there is only hunter and now, only 2 specs out of 3. The thing is, now the melee spec is here, it's not gonna change and we have to make the best of it.

First of all, it was pretty well implemented.
 -It emphasizes on slow 2H weapons, by having mongoose bite, wing clip and Carve all based on weapon damage, and it's a good thing as those weapons were underused. It will also make the spec much less vulnerable to Glancing Blows than it would be for dual wielding, because one auto attack out of two will be instead a raptor's Strike and will not be subject to Glancing for that reason (yellow hit).
 -Damaging traps were pretty bad, with the new scaling they will become pretty good.
 -Lightning reflexes make the current Hunter gear also very good for melee hunter.
 -Single Target damage from the melee weapon will be lower than ranged hunter but the fact mobs will be under fire trap effect / serpent sting nearly all the time, will make up for it and the total single target damage from melee hunter will be pretty close to a ranged one.
 -the real issue with this spec who will require big adjustments from the 2 other specs are the AOE potential.

 **About AOE potential :**

 -With lightning reflexes, a melee hunter will get significantly more MAP than an equally geared ranged hunter will get RAP, agility will increase both the same way, those 10% more agi will compensate the loss of trueshot aura. Both aspects of wolf and hawk gives the same increase of AP. But as a Melee Hunter you'll benefit from some strong Melee Attack Power buffs who don't apply to range such as Battle Shoot or Blessing of Might, and i don't talk about melee only consumables like wintefall firewater, strength pots, etc.
A naxx geared hunter have 2K+ RAP buffed, a new Naxx geared melee hunter will get 2K4+.

We know the damage scaling values :
Immolation Trap DoT: 1 extra tick damage for every 10 Attack Power
Explosive Trap Initial Damage: 1 extra damage for every 6.5 Attack Power
Explosive Trap DoT: 1 extra tick damage for every 30 Attack Power
and Trap mastery talent increase fire damage from traps by 30%

Explosive trap damage is 208 to 265 initial explosion (average 236.5) and then 330 over 20s, 33 per tick each 2s.
With Stinged Nettle Lacing new talent, explosive (and immolation) trap will also apply 2 tick of serpent sting.

with the scaling for our naxx geared hunter it will be :
Explosion : (236.5+(2400/6.5))*1.3=787
Dot Tick : (33+(2400/30))*1.3=147 every 2s
Serpent sting tick will deal (555/5)*1.3 = 144 every 3s

We can now see the total AOE damage per target during 6s (because volley last 6s and when we AOE mobs, they rarely live more than 8 to 10s anyway).
 787+(147*3)+(144*2) = 1516 damage per target (252 dps per target).

Those numbers by themselves are totally fine, a Naxx geared mage or warlock for example will have no problem dealing better AOE damage than a Melee hunter, but still, this spec change hunter from the worst AOE damage dealing class into a solid one with survival spec.

While it's not a problem because it will not make this spec OP versus other classes (it will not be OP but it will be pretty good). It will make it the best spec for Hunter in raid, specially for Speeds Runs where clearing trashs fast is very important, I do speed runs with my guild, I like it as many other guilds do, so i will use this melee spec because i don't want to penalise my teamates but it's where i have a problem with the hunter class change regarding PVE.

When the vast majority of people who created a Hunter did it to be a ranged physical damage dealer like i did myself and the clear best spec for PVE become a melee spec, it's a big concern.

 **Luckily there is solutions to this AOE issue :**

For MM spec :
Volley is by far the worst AOE spell in the game, 1 min cd, 80 damage per sec. for 6s, it's a channeling spell and with no scaling (technicaly there is some scaling on spell damage, but it's not a problem as if you increase SP on your stuff, you'll lose RAP at the same time). The problem with this spell is that for a Naxx geared hunter, if there is less than 8 target, it's actually a loss of dps over a standard ranged dps rotation. For Naxx, it's only decent for the packs of small spiders who patrol, few trashs groups before patchwerk and before Faerlina. and even there, it will only increase your damage by like 20% over standard damage rotation, and because of the long CD, only on 1 pack out of 2 or 3.

Solution : change base damage from 80 to 120 (this increase will also help a bit BM AOE potential without making volley good enough to be used by survival melee specs), reduce the base cooldown by 30s to reduce the problem i mentionned earlier about 1 pack out of 2 or 3, change Barrage talent from +15% volley damage to increase the periodic damage of volley by 3/6/9% of your ranged attack power.

For our naxx geared hunter with 2K RAP, it would be 120+(0.09*2000)=300 damage per tick (each second for 6 seconds).
You might say, hey 300 dps is better than the 252dps from explosive trap. But it's only a 48 dps increase for each target affected.
Don't forget Volley is a channeled spell so you'll do only that during those 6s, a melee hunter, one the other hand, will still do full melee damage (hundreds of dps from autoattack, raptor strike, mongoose bite, carve, wingclip) on top of those 252 dps of AOE damage from explosive trap, because traps are instant and only took one gcd. Keep in mind also than explosive trap deal even more damage because it continue to tick for up to 20s, not only 6s, for the MM hunter to beat a surv hunter on an AOE during those 6s, you'll need enough targets for those 48 more dps multiplicated by the number of targets to be higher than the full melee dps of the surv. hunter (hundreds of dps) which realisticaly can only happen when there will be 8 to 10+ targets while on most trashs mobs packs (2 to 7 mobs per pack), surv hunter will be much better.

For BM spec :

Increasing AOE damage from BM spec is not the easiest thing to do, the small increase from the volley base damage would help a bit but will be far from enough.
Sure you could lower again the cooldown of gorilla AOE but we don't want all raid pets to become gorillas.

my solution is :
Bestial Wrath : add to the current effect : when the pet is under bestial wrath, all his attacks will now also sweep any enemy who is close to his main target.
Just be aware than if a warrior who cleave can easily move 1 yard left or right to make sure his cleave hit the most enemies, a pet cannot be controled like that, so it might be good thing to increase pet melee range only during BW like you did with warriors using polearms. there will be no consequences on the pvp side as people will learn quickly to not stay near the main target of the pet to not be cleaved.

You can also reduce a bit the cooldown and duration (from 120 to 90s and last 15s instead of 18) as you nerfed the damage already.

 **MM spec**

Improved steady shot : pretty bad talent because it increase only bonus damage which doesn't scale, on top of that this bonus damage is only 50 so it's a 12.5 damage increase, it's bad.
Solution : Make it increase steady shot total damage by 3/6% or 4%/8%, at least it will scale with hunter gear and become significant.

Piercing shots : While the damage increase is good, it's a very bad design to upgrade hunter dps, it's another talent who will be ok with average hunters and penalise the good ones. When i pve, either on 5 man dungeons, 10 and 20 mans raid or 40 mans raid, i use scatter shot, or even FD/trap directly a lot in a broad range of situations because it's a reliable CC. And now because of this talent that you have to take because it's a significant dps upgrade and your main role as a hunter is to dps, your scattershot will often fail just because the mob will get a random bleed.
Solution : Of course you could change scatter to not break on bleeds, same for frost trap (frost stop the bleeding) but you can also change it for something else, with the name piercing shot, an easy change would be to make it give some armor penetration on your ranged attacks like serrated blade from rogue. You can also increase ranged haste by a few %, change the name in this case.
Also Hunter is already the class (with war) who scale the best with crits, it doesn't make sense to push even more on them, by making crits so much more powerful than normal hit and with the addition of Endless Quiver procs, when stars will align (crits and procs), we will kill naxx geared people in few globals then they will complain that hunter is OP.

Ranged weapon specialisation : it would be nice to turn it into a 3 pt talents.

 **PVP**

The way melee hunter works will make it really challenging to balance it for pvp, mainly because currently, it can do the same damage from range than a pre-patch survival was able to do. and it's the first time we have a spec who is at the same time very good at range and melee damage. And being able to trap every 15s while in combat is Huge, it might require some adjustements, fire traps in combat is fine, frost trap every 15s is already very powerful, but freezing every 15s might be too much.

However, while it can explain some nerfs like the fact they removed counterattack because it can really be abused as a melee hunter, the suppression of Counterattack hurt so bad the traditionnal ranged hunter, who now desesparately need a new tool to regain range.

Also the part of the talent "trap mastery" who reduce the chance for traps to be resisted need to return lower in survival tree for BM / MM to be able to pick it up. Losing Counterattack is already such a huge blow, we need trap to be reliable for all specs, a frost trap can be resisted when triggered but another check is made every 2s (or 1s if you're under DR on trap). so without the reduce trap resist talent, trap become unreliable.

But unlike for PVE where we have the numbers and can already know how things will go, for PVP, it's better to wait and test it. I will edit this post when we will be able to play and test those new specs.

 **Equipment :**

Haste is a very good stat for hunter, please add some haste on sets, on T2.5, you could remove spell power and add 1% haste on boots, spaulders and legs (who will finaly become better than those from exalted warsong)

On tier 3, it's the same, remove this useless mp5 (sure hunter is a mana class but the item point cost of mp5 is so expensive, it's not at all adapted for a class like hunter), you can, for example add, instead of mp5, 1% haste on ring, gloves and legs, because curently, there is 4 rings better than the T3 one, ES gloves are much better then T3 ones and there is 2 pairs of legs better than T3 ones, ES craft are bis (Dreamhide Leggings) then Leggings of Apocalypse.

Adding haste on T3.5 will also be great to stop with the crit inflation, our crit rates are already so high in naxx gear, adding armor pen can also work for that.

I hope my post will be helpful.

First of all, about the Chart in my post, don't take into account those blue and red area, there is no such things as "soft crit cap" or "low profit area". Even if gaining 1% crit would only increase your damage by 0.85% (which happen around 40% crit), it would still be a nice upgrade.

Then i'm gonna talk about MM spec, things will be different for BM and Surv because AP increase Pet and trap damage respectively and will be significantly better for those 2 specs, unfortunately i cannot test it, i don't have a good 2H weapon for Surv. and scorpid (who should be the best performing pet on bosses, when he have enough time to stack poison) is currently bugged for BM (scorpid poison doesn't scale with Spirit Bond talent : <https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=9369>)

Then if you want to compare agi / haste / crit / AP / hit, it depend on your hunter and his gear :

 1% hit is always a (slightly more than) 1% damage increase until you reach hit cap.
 1% haste is around 0.9% damage increase on bosses (single target) and 0.85% damage increase overall.
 1% crit, for that, look the chart in my first post, but for a naxx geared hunter, it would be around 0.9% when raid buffed.
agility can be decomposed in crit and AP. (53 agi increase crit chance by 1%, 1 agi = 2 RAP)

The big question is how much AP you need to increase your damage by 1%, to be able to compare AP / agi with the other stats.

For that the easiest way, once fully buffed and equipped for raid with your passive trinkets, is to :
 1. open your character panel in game and mouseover ranged damage to check the DPS (309 in my case).
 2. unequip and equip back an attack power item and check the DPS difference, to know how much AP you need to increase your dps by 1 (10 in my case, it's 14 without any haste, but quiver and haste on your equipment, buffs will reduce it).
 3. add regular permanent AP buffs who are not taken into account in the character panel, for example, 110 attack power from hunter mark, 200 AP against undead from the 2 consecrated sharpened stones, etc. (for me, as i don't have KT trinket for another 150 AP against undead, it's 310 AP to add which worth 31 dps)

Then you can compare, in my case my total DPS is 309+31 = 340 DPS. 1% of that would be 3.4 dps, and i need 34 attack power to increase my DPS by 3.4 which is 1% extra damage.

I can easily make the comparaison now.
