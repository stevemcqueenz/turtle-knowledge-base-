# 1.17.2 - PvP Hunter > Please Save the BM/MM Spec 🙏

## Post 103825 by Ragetto (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-10-19T11:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103825#p103825 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,

 ----------------- edit -----------------
Great news: according to the talent simulator, Hawk Eye has been moved back to Tier 3 in the MM tree (swapped with Improved Hunter's Mark), which means it no longer competes with Mortal Shots.
 -------------------------------------------

 *A comprehensive post has already been created regarding the general impact on PvP hunters (Viper Sting, Wing Clip), and more specifically on the "classic" PvP hunter (so far MM/Survival 0/21/30 - which, in 1.17.2, will become a stationary DPS turret in deep MM).*
 *edit: [viewtopic.php?f=45&t=15234](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=45&t=15234)*

As for me, I want to advocate for a spec that's a bit less common, but one of the most fun in the game across all classes, reasonably viable until now (especially useful for under-geared hunters, but it works for everyone), yet it’s being completely destroyed by the 1.17.2 update (and the MM tree restructuring), making it no longer viable. This reduces the diversity of the hunter class and the game itself.

I’m talking about the hybrid Beast Mastery/Marksmanship spec (30/21/0), which allows you to have both the pet stun and Scatter Shot. Not viable for PvE raiding, of course, but incredibly fun in PvP, with plenty of little tricks (resulting in a high skill cap) and decent dps...
[https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c ... 0000000000](https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator/custom?t1=R21loaF9Ag&t0=MPGOMWitwz&t2=nC2KvPaEKT&t=0150003221500050010000500000-0500005011030050100000000000-0000000000000000000000000000)

More broadly, what I’m going to describe applies to all specs that invest 21 points into MM to obtain Scatter Shot. You’ll see how the restructuring of the MM tree results in a significant DPS (or range) nerf compared to before.

 *Note: I’m speaking as an experienced PvP hunter who has played this class at a high level in Vanilla, TBC, WotLK, and achieved (almost) everything possible in PvP. Now on Twow, I’ve reached Rank 13, though I'm aware that rank itself only reflects time spent, not actual skill. Anyway, I just wanted to clarify that I’m not an RP backpedaler advocating for a meme spec... nothing more.*

Here is the new BM/MM build as it would be in 1.17.2:
[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?p ... AAAAAAA%3D](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?points=AoAaBoAoRBAoAAFAEoABQYIAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA%3D)

There aren’t major differences in the BM tree (more armor and HP for the pet, which can be useful in very specific situations but overall... who cares), so I won’t go into it here.

 --------------------------------
Looking at the 1.17.2 Marksmanship tree, you quickly realize that something is off..

 **Tier 4** ![⚠️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/26a0.svg)![🔥](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f525.svg)![🔥](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f525.svg)![🔥](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f525.svg)![⚠️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/26a0.svg)
Oh, here’s Hawk Eye! But what’s it doing here?
It’s now competing with Mortal Shots... I only have 5 points, but I need 7 to get back to my previous level of effectiveness.
What should I choose? Range or damage?
This "choice" didn’t exist before, it’s a massive nerf, plain and simple.

 --------------------------------

So, to summarize, as a BM/MM:

 **What’s worse:**

- Wing Clip cooldown (applies to all hunters)

- Viper Sting cooldown and nerf (applies to all hunters)

- My damage and/or range (in short, a huge sacrifice)

 **What’s better:**

- 2 more stable slots (applies to all hunters)

- Uh… that’s it   sad_turtle

Basically, as a BM/MM hunter, I just become weaker with NO compensation. At all (not even in the BM tree since I don’t go deep enough).

It’s both unexpected and depressing.

 --------------------------------

The strength of the original design was that you could play multiple specs with different playstyles, but that will no longer be possible (I don’t count the melee spec, which doesn’t really exist in my mind). Gameplay is one thing, but fun only exists when there’s a reasonable level of effectiveness, and with so many nerfs, the BM/MM spec is no longer viable for serious PvP play.

So, dear Turtle WoW team, I beg you: don’t kill such a fun spec.

Please modify your future MM tree so that it’s still possible, with 21 points in MM, to have Hawk Eye AND 5/5 Mortal Shots. I’m not asking for any improvements or buffs, just to be able to keep playing this spec.

Please don’t make your game less fun and diverse than the original.

However, we sometimes drain multiple targets to maximize the effect and impact in large battles, hoping there aren’t any skilled druids/paladins on the other side, because otherwise, the poison is dispelled in 1-2 seconds, and we end up losing far more mana than we drained.

Similarly, hunters can also oom each other (and much faster than a priest or mage, as we have 40-50% less mana).

But if you increase the amount of mana drained, then you make this ability much more annoying for classes that don’t have a dispel (priests, mages, and even hunters included). Currently, it’s fine.
