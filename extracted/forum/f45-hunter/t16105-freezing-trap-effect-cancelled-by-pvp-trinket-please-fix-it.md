# Freezing Trap Effect Cancelled by PvP Trinket: Please "fix" it

## Post 109648 by Ragetto (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-24T11:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109648#p109648 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello!

 ------------------------------------------------------
 ------------------------------------------------------
 *Edit: Looking back, I’m fully in favor of reverting to the pre-CC2 trinket (which I believe was a design mistake). Blind, sap, and Freezing Trap shouldn’t be treated like spammable CCs such as sheep or fear.*
 ------------------------------------------------------
 ------------------------------------------------------

The Scatter-Trap combo was nerfed shortly before CC2, which was already a tough hit. Then CC2 arrived, destroying the classic PvP hunter build from Vanilla and replacing it with a melee tree (wtf) that no one uses in PvP.

But this change feels like the final nail in the coffin: the new PvP trinket cancels all control effects, including Freezing Trap.

Here’s why this is a massive issue (for hunters, at least...):

- 1) FD + Freezing Trap was often a last resort to avoid getting destroyed in melee.

- 2) Freezing Trap is far harder to land than a fear or sheep in most situations (if you want to trap a healer, you have to do something counterintuitive for a ranged DPS - especially for hunters, who have a dead zone: get close to them).

- 3) It’s not spammable > 15-sec cooldown (or effectively 30 seconds since it can’t be used in combat and requires Feign Death).
For these reasons, this control ability must be EXTREMELY reliable and predictable (especially since it can resist…) or it loses all its value.

Before CC2, traps could be dispelled, but it was predictable. If you saw a priest or paladin on the enemy team, you knew your trap could be easily countered. So you had to adapt your tactics and avoid wasting a 30-sec cooldown. Now, with this new trinket, there’s no way to plan ahead. It’s just like RNG *(unless you have cyborg eyes and a computer in your brain… and even then...)*

This completely goes against the PvP hunter’s identity built around control and anticipation.

So, my request is...
 **Please remove Freezing Trap from the list of control effects canceled by the PvP trinket.**
(or just revert the trinket to how it was before CC2)

Thank you for considering this!
