# Feedback: Kill Command range and other issues

## Post 162795 by Lane13 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-12-14T18:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162795#p162795 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Kill Command has a range of 45 yards. While it may look like it's enough, it's not. On bosses with big arenas where you and your pet will be in very different spots, you won't reach your pet with it. There's a reason why both Intimidation and Bestial Wrath have a range of 100 yards so Kill Command should also be in this position.

Of course, fix the BM bugs that have been reported since day one related to Kill Command and Baited Shot.

Kill Command should also not require LoS and remove silences from pets, as a silence is essentially a CC ability for families such as wind serpents and scorpids, who rely on their spells for damage.

Finally, another request I have is to remove the LoS requirement for the Breastplate of Beast Mastery and the Unseen Path bonuses, as they can clog the screen with LoS errors and make no sense in the first place. The bonuses should always be active as long as you're wearing the gear and your pet is alive, like it happens with the Beastmaster's Boots. Additionally, clarify if all the pet % bonuses from different items should stack or not, since right now there are bug reports explaining how this is not the case and how some of them cancel each other out constantly.
