# Please make Aimed shot worth using

## Post 83017 by Gaap6969 — 2023-12-28T17:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83017#p83017 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It is really cool to get new abilities like Trueshot etc. The problem is that Trueshot is so good that Aimed shot isnt worth using. What ends up happening is that instead of adding something to the rotation, you just spam Trueshot and cast multi-shot in cd. The rotation ends up being as boring as in og vanilla and thats really too bad considering that the rotation could be much better if only Aimed shot was worth using.

Anyone else feel the same? I'd love it if Turtle added even more abilities as it really makes the game more fun imo.

Love the server btw and just want it to be the best it can be.

Getting rid of Aimed shot in PVE rotation is the best thing who could have happened.

AS force you to use super slow weapons to get the best dps even if faster weapons with better dps are available.

In Twow, it would prevent the use of haste items or fast weapons to get more Improved aspects of the Hawk procs.

Aimed shot is now an opener just like Ambush which is fine.

The other direction Twow could have taken to revamp hunter would have been, instead of adding trueshot, to make stings and traps scale on AP (or RAP) and arcane shot based on weapon damage normalised at 2.8 (which also would have helped hunter in pvp because hunter is the worst ranged class regarding to the ability of doing damage while moving, others have much better instants or dots to apply while moving).

But they choosed Trueshot, and it work great for PVE.

## Post 83232 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-31T09:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83232#p83232 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I think the hunters rotation at the moment leaves a lot to be desired. Adding trueshot was a good move, but its so good it pushed aimed shot out of the rotation. Aimed shot is an ability that demands to be used skillfully (not cast it when you are about to get hit by aoe, watch the swingtimer etc) and is a  huge dopamine spike. It being not worth using is really sad.

It also doesn't function as an opener, or rather hunter has no use for openers in such a reagard. Since your target in PvP will get you as their target if you start casting on them from an unexpected angle. While in PvE the threatspike is undesirable to the point of just sending in the pet and starting to shoot is preferable 99% of the time.

Ranged attackspeed haste also improves the cast time of Aimed shot, The haste argument is mute.

But Trueshot does make fast weapons a bit more enticing to play with (even tho i still don't think they outperform slow ones). Which is overall a welcome change.
Its a tough one to fix. Nerfing Trueshot to give aimed room is just nerfing hunter so one ability gets to shine again. Which i don't think is necessarily a good choice either.
