## Post 143055 by Mekk (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-08-04T10:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143055#p143055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The main thing is getting the quest Training the Beast that sends you to the Pet Trainer, because that will teach you Beast Training, Feed Pet, and Revive Pet and then you can use Beast Training to train your pet new/upgraded abilities.  For an Alliance character, it looks like you'll need to talk to one of these four NPCs:

Clover Spinpistol (Gnome) in Kharanos, Dun Morogh will send you to Bella Thundergranite in Ironforge.
Grif Wildheart (Dwarf) in Kharanos, Dun Morogh will send you to Bella Thundergranite in Ironforge.
Dazalar (Night Elf) in Dolanaar, Teldrassil will send you to Jocaste in the Cenarion Enclave, in Darnassus.
Daisy Windhelm (Human) in Goldshire, Elwynn Forest will send you to Marven the Tamer in the Park in Stormwind City.

Note: Even though I added the race, any Alliance race should be able to talk to these NPCs to get the quests.
Second Note: It looks like they added a quest for Goblins, but not for High Elves?  Again, I've not rolled up a High Elf, so I'm not familiar with their starting area(s).

## Post 151904 by Hctwowfan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-09-16T07:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151904#p151904 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

> **Mercer92 wrote: Tue Aug 12, 2025 5:41 am**
> im a level 12 human hunter i got the pet tamer quest done, everytime I tame a pet it has -4 skill points and I cant use the basic abilities no growl nothing and it keeps staying in rebellious level 1 and nothing I do works it doesnt matter if I reset the pet talent tree or level him up nothing changes

Your pet comes with negative training points (tp) because it has an ability already (most likely claw 1).

Your pet has a max of 300 tp at lvl 60. The math is:
pet lvl x (loyalty - 1) = tp

at 60:
 60 x (6 - 1) = 300

Your pet right after taming has (independent of pet level):
pet lvl x (1 - 1) = 0 - cost of known abilities = -4

If you understand the math you'll see that leveling your pet will still provide zero tp if the loyalty is at 1. Increasing the loyalty will provide an amount of tp equal to your pet lvl.

If you want to increase your tp, you need to increase the loyalty. Loyalty only increases if your pets happiness is green or yellow (feed the pet) AND if your pet gains experience from killing mobs. Standing in town with a green happiness pet for 10 days won't do anything for the loyalty.
