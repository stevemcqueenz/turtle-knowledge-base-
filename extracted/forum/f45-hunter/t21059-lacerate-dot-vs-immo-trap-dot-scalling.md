# Lacerate dot vs. Immo trap dot scalling

## Post 146445 by Vladis — 2025-08-17T10:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146445#p146445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all, I like the new Lacerate talent and its interaction (available after a player critically hits). What I do not like is the disparity between Immo trap fire damage and lacerate. When you go deep into the survival tree (at lease 31 points) your immo trap fire dot starts to do some rally nice damage because it scales from MAP. On the other hand, lacerate dot damage is so low that it does not make any sense to even have it.

I am talking about 300+ fire immo trap damage against 12+ bleed lacerate damage. I do not know how to fix this but one possible solution is make the bleed scale better from MAP or just remove the bleed and perhaps introduce some pet buff damage interaction mechanic like baited shot has.

## Post 149725 by Hctwowfan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-09-01T11:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149725#p149725 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sorry for the semi necro but Lacerate has to be the most awful ability ever.

You need to invest 2 talent points to get the ability. You can only use it after a crit. It costs a substantial amount of mana. Lacerate is underpowered af for its cost.

Let's say you had 1000 melee ap (which is already asking a lot tbh). You deal 35% = 350 damage before damage mitigation, best case if you're positioned correctly you gain another +10% damage = 385 and the bleed then ticks for a total of 77 damage. Did you lacerate the fingertip of your opponent? And on top of all that it can simply be dodged or parried.

Compare that to Overpower. Needs no talent points to get access to the ability. Needs 2 tp to increase crit to +50%. Costs very little rage compared to average rage cost. Can only be used after the opponent dodges, cannot be blocked, parried or dodged. Deals substantial damage.

Lacerate, for an ability this deep in the survival tree, with mana cost, requiring to crit the opponent first to use it, can be dodged and parried, deals way too little damage.

Either buff the damage, buff the bleed damage or make it baseline and not requiring talent points to get access to. Or give it a fancy additional effect, like mongoose bite and raptor strike refresh the bleed or deal additional damage to a bleeding target. In its current form, Lacerate is just useless.
