# Hunter Pet Suggestions

## Post 98607 by Moongirl — 2024-07-05T17:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98607#p98607 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've played hunter class a bit, and what I like the most is that you can pick whatever pet you want! I'm already amazed on how much the Turtle Wow Team has added. I wanted to give some suggestions for other pets, mostly different models, that can be tamable. As much as I would want a tamable Sprite Darter, I have to be realistic!   turtle_tongue_head

Most of the models I suggest are found in this website: <https://www.wow-petopia.com/classic/> - It's not up to date for Turtle Wow, but some pet models and their locations can be found here.

 **Crabs, Bats, and Turtles:** These pets have very few models, and it would be nice to have more variety. Bringing in the Pandaria Turtle Model may be a bit too much to ask, but other colors would be great.

 **Saber Cats:** I realize there is already a ton of cat models in the game. I'll be honest though: Many people like cats, many people like cats as pets more! I want to put notice on the Saber cat models, however. There is (according to Petopia) many unused Saber cat models. I feel as though you can make the case that those cats are "leaders" and make them rare mobs where appropriate. This model can be fit in Darkshore, for instance. ![Image](https://www.wow-petopia.com/images/skin_pix/ridingtigerskindarknosaddle.jpg)

 **Snakes as Pets?:** One pet I do like that is not tamable in Turtle Wow are snakes. I know it may be a stretch, but you can classify them as scorpions. If you change the "Scorpid Poison" to just "Poison" or something similar, it wouldn't seem that far off. In addition, I do not know what Snakes would eat other than meat, which is the only thing Scorpions can eat.

Those are my suggestions. Once again, I am very amazed at what Turtle Wow has added so far. I quite like the Hawkstrider Model and the Foxes as well. For all I know, some of these were added already. Thank you for reading, and good luck on your journey.   smiling_turtle_head
