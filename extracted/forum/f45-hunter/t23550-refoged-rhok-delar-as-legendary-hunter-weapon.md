# Refoged Rhok'Delar - as legendary hunter weapon

## Post 164882 by Kullez — 2026-01-11T11:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164882#p164882 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi guys,
yesterday at night I was thinking about a possible hunter legendary weapon and came up with this idea.
(yes, you can say “this is Legion chiiiild, wth”, and I’m not a Turtle WoW lore specialist, but hear me out):

 ***Questline concept:***
 **Corrupted Rhok’Delar** → **Sealed Rhok’Delar** → **Reforged Rhok’Delar, Bow of the Dragon Aspirant**
Yeah, I want to make a legendary weapon from Rhok’Delar, because I like it.

 ***Questline overview***
A corrupted boss (Old Gods/Legion/Nightmare-related) is added to the Timbermaw Hold raid.
After killing it, Hunters can obtain a legendary quest item (tentacle / heart with a 20% drop chance). ***1st step.***
Taking the quest requires equipping Rhok’Delar. Upon completion, Rhok’Delar transforms into **Corrupted Rhok’Delar**.

 **Corrupted Rhok’Delar**:
 1. damage somewhere around Naxxramas bows (≈60 DPS)
 2. each shot drains 25–50 HP from the Hunter
 3. each shot has a 2–3% chance to stun the Hunter for 3 seconds, causing total loss of control while standing in place. This stun cannot be dispelled or resisted
 4. the corruption effect remains on the weapon until it is sealed later in the questline
Quest text describes the bow as literally rotting and decaying, with the Hunter fearing it may eventually crumble in their hands (can be removed).

 ***2nd step: C’Thun and the Emerald Dream***
The next step requires defeating C’Thun, only if the Hunter is Exalted with the Brood of Nozdormu and has **Corrupted Rhok’Delar** equipped (during the entire C’Thun fight).

Details:
 1. upon entering Ahn’Qiraj, C’Thun whispers to the Hunter, and the bow starts whispering too.
 2. after defeating C’Thun, the Hunter can speak with an Avatar of C’Thun, who reveals that the corrupted boss was merely a pawn (Old Gods/Legion/Nightmare — pick one).
 3. their goal was to reach the power of the Emerald Dream using an ancient weapon infused with corruption
After the conversation, the Hunter is suddenly pulled into the Emerald Dream, where they meet an Avatar of Ysera.
Ysera initially threatens to destroy both the Hunter and the weapon. However, seeing the Hunter’s role in defeating C’Thun (reputation requirement), she instead decides to help.

She transforms the weapon into **Sealed Rhok’Delar**:
 1. power level slightly above a standard “T2” weapon (≈45 DPS).
 2. grants an Emerald Blessing bonus (see final weapon effects).
 3. maybe some pet bonuses, idk.
 4. all negative corruption effects are removed, but there is a 1% chance to break the weapon in combat.

 ***Reforging process (3rd step)***
To fully reforge the bow, the Hunter must gather dragon-related components:
 1. **Azuregos sinew** (or an instanced / summoned version without non-quest loot, because of world boss issues on Nordanaar) — provides the bowstring
 2. **Emerald Dragon Scale** for one of the four green dragon world bosses (or instanced equivalents)/Solnius from the Emerald Sanctum — provides purified scales for the bow’s frame.
 3. **Heart of Nefarian** (for “negative” balance and power source)
 4. **Blessing of Anachronos** (requires Exalted with the Brood of Nozdormu, to balance all power and stabilize the bow) - from dialog with Anachronos.

 ***4th step (final)***] after bringing all components to Ysera and receiving her blessing, the Hunter receives:
 **The title “Defender of the Emerald Dream” or “Dragon Aspirant”**
 **Reforged Rhok’Delar, Bow of the Dragon Aspirant**

 ***WEAPON:***
 **Reforged Rhok’Delar, Bow of the Dragon Aspirant**
Weapon type: Bow (Ranged)
Quality: Legendary
(Cannot be sold, deleted, or traded. Can be stored in the bank.)
Ammo: Arrows
Class: Hunter only (MM-oriented, but still good for other specs)

 **Stats**
+15 Agility
+25 Stamina
+15 Strength

Speed: 3.10
Damage: 228,3 – 268,5
DPS: ~80.1

Durability: 250 / 250
(or infinite, explained as the Green Dragonflight continuously restoring the bow)

 **Effects**
Equip: +2% Hit Chance
Equip: +2% Critical Strike Chance

Equip: **Blessing of Anachronos**
After five successful Stready Shots, your next Aimed Shot becomes instant.
Internal cooldown: 15 seconds
Mana cost is consumed, ammo is not consumed.

Equip: Chance on any attack (3%) to trigger one of the following effects
Internal cooldown: 24 seconds (starts when an effect triggers); the same effect cannot occur twice in a row; can trigger from any combat ability (including Mongoose Bite, Kill Command, Arcane Shot)

 **Emerald Blessing**: for 8 seconds, abilities cost no mana and heal the Hunter for 5% of damage dealt
 **Black Dragonflight’s Fury**: increases melee and ranged attack power by 150 for 6 seconds

Active ability:
 **Magic of the Blue Dragonflight**
Creates 200 “Blue Dragonflight Arrows”

 **Blue Dragonflight Arrows**: arrow damage 24; soulbound; cannot be sold, traded, or banked
Cooldown: 6 hours

I really want to see something like this. Right now I’m only an AQ40 raider, but believe me, I can earn this bow.
Numbers and mechanics are obviously adjustable — the main goal is to provide Hunters with a true legendary progression, because I want it aaaand an instant Aimed Shot (if a talent for it is added, the effect can trigger a new talent proc, but with a 30 sec cooldown).
