## Post 20219 by Appinger — 2022-07-22T21:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20219#p20219 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, i was searching for a solution to silence the gunshot a bit, since most hunter weapons while leveling will be noisy guns.
Here is what i figured out and i like to share with you:
 1. There is no addon for that, you need to replace the soundfiles. (add them)
 2. When you search the web to replace the gunshot files (dont worry they are out there),
dont use .ogg files, they dont work. they have to be .wav files.
 3. The folder path, create in your turtle_client_116\Sound\Item\Weapons\Gun\
in the Gun folder you paste your new soundfiles
GunFire01.wav
GunFire02.wav
GunFire03.wav
GunLoad01.wav
GunLoad02.wav
GunLoad03.wav

just to be clear, here an example:
C:\turtle_client_116\Sound\Item\Weapons\Gun\GunFire01.wav

I hope i could help you a little bit.
