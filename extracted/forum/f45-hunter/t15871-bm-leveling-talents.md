## Post 108662 by Bob022 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-19T10:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108662#p108662 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Bestial Swiftness is now a combination talent, and a no-brainer at 1 point.  You get 3% pet haste, pet movement speed, and improved aspects.  That's great value for one point.

Bestial Discipline is traditionally considered weak for what it does, seldom recommended outside a few niche strategies (if you're doing these, you don't need to ask about it).  A leveling character will usually get better quality of life out of a point in Mend Pet for the cure effect.  That being said, neither is going to make or break a build.

Note that Growl is traditionally not an actual taunt, but rather an ability that adds a flat amount of additional threat.  As such it tends to work better on autocast rather than trying to save it like a taunt.  This assumes Growl wasn't turned into a genuine taunt with 1.17.2 without my noticing, although I've heard no such claims.

Freeing up points:  Elusiveness is optional and should be thought of as discretionary.  If you need to free up an extra point someplace, you can usually drop a point out of Frenzy (to 4/5) and it's alright.  That last point has somewhat diminished returns, especially for people using pets with high attack speeds.  For solo PvE, you don't necessarily need 5/5 Endurance Training.  Pet health regenerates very rapidly between pulls and Mend Pet will heal the same amount whether your pet has 4000 HP or 3500.  That can free up a couple points.  Even if these talents are added later, freeing up these points early on can allow the player to obtain other, more critical talents sooner.

For the most part, the talents are pretty self-explanatory and the big choice for a leveling Beast Mastery hunter will amount to when he wants to go 11 into Marksman for Steady Shot--you'll get it, just a question of when, and particularly when you want it versus Intimidation or Bestial Wrath.
