# Hunter learning abilities from it's pet

## Post 29480 by Ivanturtle — 2022-11-18T21:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29480#p29480 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hunters are in no need of a buff, but it's just a potential flavorful touch I think would be cool. And nothing besides Dash and Prowl, as far as I can tell, would be a massive balance hassle in pvp. Basically, when you're with a pet for long enough, or maybe if you learn all levels of an ability, you can use it's abilities yourself, as the Hunter, regardless of which pet you have. Only one or two at a time though. Not all of them of course, like Lightning Breath is probably not a transferable ability. A list of examples might be:

Charge
Cower
Growl
Dash
Furious Howl
Screech

Prowl would probably be way too good, but very cool

Maybe a talent in the Beast Mastery tree? Again, it's mostly threat and small buffs, so I could be wrong but it seems like there's nothing game breaking here, and it could cover some of Hunter's weaker spots- make them more useful in raids with buffs and threat control, and give melee hunters a gap closing tool.

Thoughts?

Your suggestion made me wonder if it would be better if feral druids started learning the feral abilities before shapeshifting fully into an animal, maybe the shapeshift could happen later, or it would have an increasing duration, as if they were gradually learning to become like that animal until they could transform completely, kind of how a shadow priest learns first various shadow-based attacks until becomes so invested in it that learns to assume shadowform.
In Warcraft if a druid spent too much time shapeshifted, he couldn't shapeshift back by his own will because the feral state overtook him, so spending too much time shapeshifted could have similar consequences.

The Hunter has abilities conceptually inspired by animals in the wild.
Directly learning animals abilities wouldn't make sense because of the physiological differences between the Hunter and the animal. Because the Hunter isn't shapeshifting like the Druid is.

The Hunter though, instead of all races learning from the trainer attacks like 'raptor strike', could learn abilities pertaining to animals the Hunters of the chosen race interact with in those zones. Night Elf Hunters don't have raptors nearby unlike other races, for example.
Or there could be abilities inspired by the tameable animals that make sense for a humanoid with tools to use that the Hunter could learn by having this or that pet.

Yeah the point isn't to massively shake up the Hunter meta or anything, it's just intended as one of those minor Vanilla flavor touches that gives each class a different adventure. That's what made Vanilla special, not a fixation on endgame min-maxing.

I just think the idea of a wild, nature dwelling hunter learning abilities from animals is a cool idea. A big part of the identity of the class is already imitation of animals with the Aspect system. Some they physically couldn't do, like bite yes, that's why I picked the ones in my post which are basically just warrior shouts, threat control, and movement ablilities. Even then though, I'd be okay with them being able to learn like, thunder breath. We have literal druids and shamans, what's to stop the hunter from picking up a few magical tricks of their own?
