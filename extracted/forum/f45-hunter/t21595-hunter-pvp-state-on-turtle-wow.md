# Hunter PvP state on Turtle WoW

## Post 149628 by Dimastasc — 2025-08-31T20:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149628#p149628 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone,

I’ve been playing a hunter in PvP on Turtle WoW (patch 1.18), and I wanted to share some thoughts. Right now it feels like hunters are really struggling compared to other classes.

Against Warlocks or Shadow Priests it’s nearly impossible. You can’t escape DoTs, Warlock keeps you under constant control, and hunter’s damage doesn’t pressure them enough before you’re dead.

Paladins and Mages also feel overwhelming: paladins outlast everything, while mages can chain control and burst far harder than hunters can respond.

Hunters lack survival tools — there’s nothing to keep yourself alive or recover once you’re being focused.

At the moment, hunters seem to be at the very bottom in PvP. Maybe instead of buffs, some changes or adjustments could be considered — something to help hunters feel more competitive without breaking balance.

Do other players feel the same, or is it just my experience?
