# Hunter QoL Improvements

## Post 102910 by Silhouette (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-10-11T17:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102910#p102910 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello fellows hunters and Turtle devs,

Throughout my journey on Turtle WoW, I've been thinking about what could make things better for hunters while keeping a Vanilla logic, so after having done some research (also on the Forum so I don't repeat the idea of someone else), a few ideas came to my mind:

 **Ammos QoL**

 - Adding Green Arrows crafts within the Survivalist profession, so Engineering would not be the only way to craft ammos and being able to make these with a secondary job would make things more accessible.
 - Implementing a counterpart NPC in order to trade Survivalist Arrows for Bullets equivalent, just like the Thorium Shells to trade for Thorium Headed Arrows in OG/IF.
 - Adding Blue ammos to craft, in the form of a rare schematic (or survivalist plans) for example inside the loot table of a new raid or already existing one like the Schematic: Biznicks 247x128 Accurascope obtainable in Molten Core.

 **Quiver and Bandolier QoL**

 - Adding an epic Bandolier equivalent to the Ancient Sinew Wrapped Lamina (from the Rhok'delar questline), maybe obtainable through a new Hunter questline or simply as a new drop.
 - Making Quiver and Bandolier as an independent slot so it doesn't sacrifice one inventory slot. I've heard, an extra stable slot is going to be available within the Turtle Shop in an upcoming update, so maybe could this extra Quiver/Bandolier slot be sold within the Shop too ?
 - Since Quivers have different skins visible on the back of our character, sometimes it just doesn't fit with the Xmog of the character so adding an Illusion or a way to Xmog the Quiver would be great for fashionistas!

Thank you for your time, hope that some of you guys like these ideas!  smiling_turtle
