Hm. Why 250g? Why not 2500g if the point is to part players with their gold?
The answer is because if you price something too high, few if anyone will buy it. The lower the price, the more who will buy it.
Now at 250g, not every hunter will buy that extra stable slot. But if you lower the price, it would lead to more players purchasing it, which could potentially mean more money removed from the economy overall (the point of a gold sink).
The trick is finding the right price really.
