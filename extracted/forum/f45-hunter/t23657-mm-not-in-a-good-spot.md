# MM not in a good spot...

## Post 165600 by Soulforged23 — 2026-01-19T11:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165600#p165600 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I ve been playing my MM hunter for a while now, and despite the devs previously saying the spec is in a good spot, the reality is a completely different story. I also play a couple other classes, and the difference is night and day. While on my other chars I feel powerful, on my hunter, I have to sweat even with players wish same gear and even below to use every single trick in the book just to survive a 1v1, and even then it feels like an uphill battle.

The core of the problem is that our abilities simply havent kept up with the power creep of other classes. Take aimed Shot, for example. In any other class, a long cast time like pyroblast rewards you with massive damage. For us? aimed Shot is basically a trap. You can use it as an opener, but once the fight starts, you re better off just using autoshots and Steady Shot because the dps is better and it doesn’t have a ridiculous wind up for a non guaranteed crit. Its insane that we have a signature big hit that hits like a wet noodle. Whether it needs to be a guaranteed crit or a proc based instant cast something has to change because right now it’s a dead button on the bar.

Our scaling is also nonexistent. Arcane Shot and volley are jokes because they don't scale with attack power. Seeing Arcane Shot hit for 183 damage at high levels is embarrassing. Serpent sting is even worse...imagine spending 5 talent points just to have a dot that deals negligible damage. Meanwhile, trueshot Aura our ultimate 31 is just a weak aura compared to the massive damage abilities other classes get, like the warlocks dark harvest. We are reduced to a steady shot bot rotation that feels static and unrewarding.

The situation in PvP is even more dire. The 3 sec cooldown on wing clip combined with the removal of counterattack has completely crippled our ability to manage the deadzone. If a rogue or a warrior gets on you, you re basically done. Between the new custom foods like sweet n crunchy fruit bread that let Rogues ignore freezes, and Sprint bypassing traps, we have almost no way to escape anymore. It feels like the devs nerfed our CC because of the survival rework but completely forgot that MM hunters still need those tools to function at a baseline level.

The numbers dont lie either. Seeing hunters at the bottom of the charts in turtle logs, is a slap in the face for a pure DPS class. Every other class has 2k damage skills now while we are stuck with vanilla values and broken animations. Our shooting mechanics make us too static, mutlishot requiring us to stand still for an instant is clunky, traps share DR with Scatter, we los our autoshot rotation in corners while channeling spells go through walls.

We need more than just minuscule talent tweaks. Ranged hunter needs a serious look at its scaling, its utility, and its ulti abilities. We shouldnt have to lose all our utility and identity just to do baseline damage. Hopefully, someone on the dev team is actually looking at hunter feedback, because right now it feels like the class is being left in the dust while everyone else gets to play a modern version of the game.

## Post 165839 by Somersby — 2026-01-21T13:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165839#p165839 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Currently, Hunters in PvP (I'm considering BM and MM; I haven't played the Survival specialization, as I believe Hunter = ranged combat) are slightly stronger than Dummys in capital cities.

Hunters' entire gameplay revolved around controlling targets at a distance, aided by the "Hawk Eye" talent. Among other things, they had the "Counterattack" ability, which was removed in favor of the Survival specialization's AoE abilities. They increased the cooldown on "Wing Clip" but increased its damage ("Survival" is all about you). Simply put, Hunters were stripped of all their survival abilities...for the sake of "Survival." It was pretty funny.

Hunter damage is a whole other pain in the ass. No one will argue that it's negligible compared to other classes. But mindlessly increasing it by n% risks skewing the PvE aspect of the game. I can't boast of any significant raiding experience. I've currently completed all available raids on the Ambershire server in the BM specialization in Pugo Raids. So, I wouldn't dare say from personal experience that Hunters are the last in the DPS class in PVE. Experienced players who have completed Naxxramas on Nordanaar feel free to correct me.

Nevertheless, changes are urgently needed. As the saying goes, "When criticizing, suggest":

 **Marksmanship Specialization**

- Combining the two talents "Improved Stings" and "Improved Scorpid Sting" into a single talent seems logical;

- Replace the "Improved Scorpid Sting" talent with a new talent: "Enchanted Arrows" - allows the Hunter to enchant arrows with one of three possible effects:
Frost Arrows: Auto-attacks have a chance to fire a Frost Arrow (a magical attack that ignores armor), slowing the target by 30-40%;

Poison Arrow: Auto-attacks have a chance to extend the duration of an existing "Serpent Sting" by 3 or 6 seconds;

Fire Arrow: Auto-attacks have a chance to ignite the target and nearby enemies, dealing 50-150 Fire damage (slightly increased AoE);

- Add to the "Improved Steady Shot" talent: Critical strikes grant the Hunter a stacking buff that reduces the cast time of "Aimed Shot" by 20%. Stacks up to 5 times.

- Adjust "Endless Quiver": increase the proc chance to 5/10%, and the shot will now deal MAGIC damage.
 **Beast Mastery Specialization**

- Add "Improved Primal Aspects" talent: while Aspect of the Hawk is active, your attacks ignore 100/200/300 armor;

- Add "Ferocity" talent: pet critical strikes restore 2/4/6/8/10% of the damage dealt;

- "Beastly Discipline" talent: the cooldown reduction effect should apply to both "KIll Command" and "Intimidate."

- "Baited Shot" talent: change weapon-based scaling to ranged attack power-based scaling.
 **General Changes:**

- Reintroduce the "Counterattack" ability for all specializations, with the same effect but with fixed damage.

- New ability "Master's Call": Your pet rushes to the aid of a target (you or an ally) and removes all slows. Cooldown: 1 minute.  Warriors now have the "Improved Berserker Rage" talent in the "Fury" talent tree, and Ferals now have a full-fledged reshift. Why can't Hunters get a version of "Master's Call" from WOTLK?

- Arcane shot: Make damage scale with ranged attack power
These changes won't significantly impact PVE, although they will be useful there as well. However, in PVP, I think Hunters will be stronger.

I've tried to summarize my thoughts very briefly, although I have much more to say about many aspects of Hunter play. In any case, I really want to see the Turtle team's opinion on hunters, since "We believe Marksmanship is in a good spot. We are considering taking another look at the design of Trueshot Aura and possibly at Volley in a future update, but for now we don't believe the specialization needs any change." seems like a joke.

P.S. English is not my native language. I apologize if Google Translate gave me some nonsense.
