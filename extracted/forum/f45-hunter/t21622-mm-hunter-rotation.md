Hi! Could you please tell me what rotation MM Hunter has? I found on Reddit that it is:
Single Target: Pet-Attack -> Steady Shot -> Autoshot -> SteadyShot -> Autoshot
Multi Target: Pet-Attack -> Autoshot -> Steady Shot -> Autoshot -> Multishot
But why isn't Arcane Shot, Serpent Sting or Aimed Shot used?
Maybe this is a stupid question, but I've never played a Hunter before.

## Post 149801 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-09-01T21:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149801#p149801 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Mana efficiency. Steady Shot deals good damage and costs far less than Arcane Shot or Aimed Shot. Aimed Shot also suffers from very long cast time, which is fine during the early stages of the game if you have a slow firing ranged weapon, but even it falls in favor of just using multi-shot for single targets
