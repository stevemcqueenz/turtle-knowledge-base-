I will still stand by my suggestion for Baited Shot, if you really want to nail down this kind of fantasy you adjust it as such:

 -Make it a small to moderate-radius grenade cast for variable melee/range usage, applying the baited debuff to all it hits
 -Instead of the shot dealing upfront damage, it causes your pet to charge the baited area if in range and do an AoE swipe (think Poison Charge in Naxx but without the dot, this allows your capstone to benefit from +pet damage effects which... really should be a givein)
 -Remove the focus regen aspect to compensate for the AoE component, it's needlessly clunky and pigeonholes us into fast pets for PvE instead of experimenting with Windfury on 1.5 and 2.8 speed ones.

This gives BM cleave damage and on-the-spot pet repositioning, both things they could direly use.
