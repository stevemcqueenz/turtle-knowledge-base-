# Melee Hunter leveling thoughts

## Post 41891 by Therrius4121 — 2023-04-17T15:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41891#p41891 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi all, 2 month ago i joined Turtle WoW. currently i'm leveling as Survival Melee Hunter (atm lvl58).
To be honest this is my first time leveling a character in a classic version, i'm having fun althought things are way slower than tbc or wotlk, the world feels alive with people running around and i take my time to explore new things added.

Why leveling a melee hunter? mostly because i like to try new things rather to choose the mainstream specs.
I'm not gonna lie leveling a melee hunter is harsh but doable, if you wanna enjoy the leveling process taking your time with no hurries then give a try.

I'm not a hardcore player, i think is possible to do this challenge with this spec. I always used 2handed weapons althought i saw many melee hunters usign DW weapons.

 **Races**

I'm leveling as HighElf Hunter, the Queldorei meditation gives back 20% of your mana pool, Bow specialization bonus falls down since we are going melee.

Gnome hunter, yes GNOMES!!! well having a mana pool increased and engineering bonus isnt so bad plus you have Escape artist ability.

Nightelf hunter, have more agility base points than the other races, Shadowmelt ability is interesting, you can use it to lose aggro in favor of your pet.

Orc hunter, Command passive gives you increased pet damage, Axe specialization relevant for melee, Blood fury ability and sturdiness passive.

Troll hunter, passive health regeneration increased and Beserk ability.

 **Professions**

I recomend to choose **Engineering and Minning** as professions mostly because we don't have an interrupt cast ability, i rely on iron grenades to interrupts and for aoe damage in dungeons. Iron grenades are cheap and even at 58 i still use them, in dungeons runs i always use them as part of my rotation with large mob packs, at the end of a dungeon iron grenade are 7%-10% of my total damage.

 **Alchemy and Herbalism** wanna try those profession with a hc melee hunter, we dont have a healing ability so having a healing pot will always save your life.

 **First aid** mandatory secondary profession, bandages will make your mob grinding way faster and if you let your pet tank, you can use bandages in combat.

 **Talent Builds**

 **Deep Beast Mastery**

This build focuses on pet damage, your pet will tank most of the time.

 **Deep Survival**
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/NgAgEoh.png)

In this build we get some improved stats talents:  Killer Instinct +3% crit, Lightning Reflexes agility increased by 15% (it will scale better with more agility gear)

 **Aspect of the Monkey**
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/pP56CRJ.png)

You forget about Aspect of the Wolf, you will lose Swift strikes proc in favor of range abilities.

 **Defensive talents**

Deflection: improves your parry chance
Improved Wing Clip: chance to immobilize the target
Deterrence: Defensive ability

 **Rotation**

Aspect of the Wolf
- Immolation Trap or Explosive Trap

- Pull mob with pet and land him on trap

- Raptor Strike on cd

- Wing Clip if you have mana in order to proc Swift Strikes

- Refresh trap => pet passive => feing death => immolation or explosive trap
Aspect of the Monkey
- Immolation Trap or Explosive Trap

- Serpent Sting and Autoattack until mob lands in trap

- Raptor Strike on cd

- Refresh trap => pet passive => feing death => immolation or explosive trap
AoE
Start always by putting your explosive trap, you can always advice your tank to get the pack closer to your trap and then refresh the trap for more aoe damage. I haven't said enough trap? lol
- be melee range

- In combat Traps => pet passive => feing death => immolation or explosive trap

- Iron grenade on cd

- Fiery Blaze on weapon (Badlands quest reward)

- farm Ravager for whirlwind procs
That's it, i don't think the melee hunter is viable in raids. they need more changes than adding only aspect of the wolf but that is an argument for another post. I'm open to suggestions and feedbacks, i hope my post is understandable because english isn't my first language. Good luck in your adventures.

Most important thing: you can level without talents at all. Just send the pet and go watch tv.

If you want to have fun, then following is for you. I leveled hunter in melee and I used to do it "warrior" way.

Alchemy for HC is important because of Swiftness potion, which can't be bought from vendors. It is valued more than healing ones.

Most important points:
 - When you are alone(with pet), you should tank to allow additional hits by Mongoose Bite
 - To keep attention of tank, turn off Growl of your pet and start with Distracting Shot
 - Pick Wolf for damage buff
 - Pick two-handed slow weapon. Use melee kite with Wing Clip like warriors do. Staves with spirit and intellect are good two-handed slow weapon. Keep enemy away when your autoattack is not ready. Move close when it is ready. You'll get 30% less damage compared to "stand still" behavior.
 - You don't need ammo bag/quiver. Most of the time you hit enemies with your stick
 - If you are tanking, then you are interested in reducing incoming damage. This way you use Aspect of the Monkey. It will also allow you to use Mongoose Bite more often
 - You don't tank in dungeons. Do things which are usually expected from hunter. Bosses can't be slowed in Turtle and it is hard to kite them.
