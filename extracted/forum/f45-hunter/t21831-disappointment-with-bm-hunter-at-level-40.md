# Disappointment with BM Hunter at level 40

## Post 151174 by Dimastasc — 2025-09-10T18:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151174#p151174 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I leveled my Hunter to 40 in the Beast Mastery tree and unlocked the ability “Sausage Shot.” I thought: “Finally, something interesting, a new skill!” But when I tried to use it, the button simply didn’t work. At first, I thought it was a bug, even relogged into the game — but nothing changed.
Then I found out that you can only use it when your pet lands a critical strike. Which means that 99% of the time, the ability is just dead. Basically, it’s an extra button that almost never works.
Honestly, this is the disappointment of the year. An absolutely useless skill that depends on luck instead of player control. With this design, the developers pretty much killed BM Hunter. Thanks for the wasted time… now I have no desire to keep playing.
