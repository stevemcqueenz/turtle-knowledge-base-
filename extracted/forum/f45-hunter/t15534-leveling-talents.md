## Post 106303 by Steakhouse (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-04T12:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106303#p106303 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Definitely BM. Imp revive and Intimidation are incredible in particular.

With the 8 first defensive talents you nearly double your pet's survivability. It's safer in HC, and also faster in terms of xp/hour.

Imp revive means you can res your pet in combat with as little as a conc shot if you're far away, or with scare beast / freeze trap for good measure. It also generally means you can take a lot more risks for the sake of efficiency.

I haven't tried the new Intimidation, the old one functioned as a pseudo taunt while leveling ; I reckon this one will scale better at high level. At any rate threat on your pet means it can hold two mobs that you can AoE with multi shot/carve, boom trap, and spreading serpent stings. Send pet with intimidation on a baddie, and after one growl, send it to another. The first mob will likely die before it can melee you. I reckon this will be particularly strong with Screech.

In general 1.17.2 added scaling in BM but one thing remains the same: your pet's base stats are very high, especially when leveling, and BM makes them better.
