# Changes I'd like to see for the Hunter Class [BM Version - Part 2]

## Post 70562 by Steelx — 2023-10-13T09:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70562#p70562 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As previously mentioned in my last post [viewtopic.php?t=10837](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=10837). My intention with this post is not to pretend that every spec in every class should be a viable raiding spec or a "class within a class" of it's own.

I understand that the vanilla feel of classes is something very unique and that it must be preserved at every cost. And that any changes made to any given class must fall under this line of development, first respecting the Lore elements of the Class and The World around it as well as respecting it's own vanilla feel.

I preface this again because it is important to know that this has been thought through and that I wholeheartedly believe that the changes I propose at this time are not simply a request to buff a class I play, I have played and raided with every single class in vanilla and I love each one of them, I want them all to be fun and to unlock their potential to the fullest without damaging the game or breaking the vanilla experience.

Having said that, the second talent I wanted to bring to light is "Spirit Bond"

At rank 2, what this talent does is
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/Hlfk7l4.png)
(reads: Spirit Bond Rank 2: "While your pet is active, you and your pet will regenerate 2% of total health every 10 sec.")

I will skip the leveling analysis, since "spirit-based passive health regeneration" while leveling is much greater than it is at max level, I want to compare this talent fairly in its strongest form; that is to say at max level when health values start to become much much larger proportionally than at any other point in the leveling process and "spirit-based passive health regeneration" is by far at its weakest.

Assuming a raid geared lvl 60 Hunter has 4k health and knowing that pets at lvl 60 have around 3k to 3.5k health, this talent would regenerate 80 health and 60 health respectively after 10 seconds.

- In a dungeon or raid environment where a range class such as Hunter is more likely to get hit by collateral damage, 80 to 100 healing will not suffice to recover from any type of mechanic and it cannot compete with any healer's healing; it would share the same problem that Renew, Rejuvenation and Regrowth have, which is that it will largely will go to waste as overheal.

- It is not significant during really long kites, this is due to the fact that enemies that need to be kited for a long time are likely too hard to tank by your pet or yourself, meaning that one is avoiding getting hit and losing health altogether.

- And if one were to be taking damage constantly during long kites, the healing from Spirit Bond would not suffice to recover enough health to survive against this type of enemy.

- Even as a failsafe this talent is not very significant, as the healing from it is so low that in order to regenerate enough health one would have to spend too much time, and it that same time the enemy that we are trying to kill would either die or kill us before we can regen enough health to survive it again.

- This talent requires the pet to be summoned and alive, so not only is it bad at keeping the Hunter and the Pet alive, if the Pet dies the regeneration is over.

This talent is positioned at the 5th row of talents in the beast mastery talent tree. In that position there are another 40 options above and around where to put these talent points.

Here is a list of all the possible alternatives we have in the Beast Mastery tree where 2 points could be better allocated.

- Ferocity, Increases pet crit chance by 3% per rank. (5) Damage is high value.

- Improved mend pet, Gives mend pet 25% chance to cleanse the pet of any curse, disease, magic or poison effect per rank. (2) Dispels are very high value both in solo play and group play.

- Unleashed Fury, Increases the pet damage by 6% per rank. (5) Damage is high value.

- Bestial Swiftness, Increases pet movement speed outdoors by 30%. (1) Movement is very high value in both solo play and group outdoors, as well as PvP.

- Pathfinding, Increases move speed from aspect of the Cheetah by 3% per rank. (2) Movement, very high value overall.

- Improved revive pet, decreases the casting time of revive pet by 2s per rank, and the mana cost by 20% per rank, pet revives with 15% more health per rank. (2) Very high value, allows the use of revive pet in combat with losing most of your mana and wasting too much time, also revives the pet with more health which makes it ideal for use in combat.

- Thick hide, Increases pet armor by 10% per rank. (3) Very high value in solo play for pet tanking.

- Improved Aspect of the Monkey, Increases the Dodge bonus of AotM by 1% per rank. (5) Low value, can still save your life better than Spirit Bond since you can get the defensive bonus on demand.

- Improved eyes of the Beast,  Increases the duration of eyes of the beast by 30s per rank and pet damage by 5% per rank and focus regeneration by 10% per rank during eyes of the beast. (2) Very situational, now has higher value due to the new restrictions where pets can't attack enemies out of line of sight.

- Endurance Training, Increases pet health by 3% per rank. (5) High value in solo play and raids as it can help your pet survive high damage mechanics and the pet benefits from extra health instantly without needing to wait 10s.

- Improved Aspect of the Hawk. Mandatory talent for a dps Hunter.
The only talent that is worse in value than this one is Bestial Discipline and we already made a post for that one.

In conclusion, every single talent in the BM tree except for the Bestial Discipline talent has a higher value than this. Talents points that could be put into this talent would be better invested into another talent inside  the same Beast Mastery Tree or in another Talent Tree, as even talents in the first column of the Marksmanship and Survival tree provide more value than this talent.   angry_turtle

What do I propose? A solution that would solve 2 problems at once.

Pets become largely obsolete as the game progresses, this is because pets don't have any type of scaling outside of Tier  Sets and a piece of gear here and there that throws a bone to pets in some way. One of the main reasons why investing talent points into improving one's pet is a bad idea outside leveling, is that pets can't reliably deal damage to the enemies we encounter later in the game.

Pets are Hunter's personal melee dps unit, one of the most if not the most important requirements of melee dps classes is to reach the "hit cap" so that their attacks vs high level enemies don't go to waste, but pets have no way of doing this.

We also all know how pet scaling got out of hand in TBC to the point where it was largely considered too broken, what I propose is a middle path. To allow pet to increase their hit chance and (maybe, possibly) crit chance throught this talent.

It would look something like this:
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/mxX2D6i.png)
(reads: Spirit Bond Rank 2: "While your pet is active, you and your pet will regenerate 2% of total health every 5 sec. Additionally, your pets' attacks and abilities have an increased hit and critical strike chance equal to 100% of your bonus hit and critical strike chance.")

It would double the healing basically by making it twice as constant, so that it has less of a chance to be pushed over the edge by any other healer in the raid or group, and it would give Hunter Pets a very soft scaling that would allow them to do 8-20% more dps in a raid environment thanks to the extra hit (and possibly crit).

The hit alone would be a very safe step as it would not affect PvP nearly at all and would give hunters the satisfaction that pets are not necessarily getting weaker and weaker as the game progresses. The crit would take it a step further allowing for more experimentation with the BM Talent Tree in Raid Environments and most importantly, this change would make this talent truly feel like a tier 5 talent and make it worth the heavy investment into the BM Tree.

Thank you for reading my post and see you around in the World (of warcraft).   crying_turtle
