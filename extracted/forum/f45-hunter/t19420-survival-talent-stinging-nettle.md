## Post 132017 by SvenS2 (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2025-05-19T22:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132017#p132017 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It sounds odd on paper, but in practice it makes sense.

First case is, it's applied for free when using Mongoose bite/Fire Traps, so there's no cost downside to the overlapping application.

Fire Traps also apply the DoT, and the target(s) may not be your main target, so the additional duration comes into play.

It's also a way to reserve some mana, as you're not required to re-apply the dot immediately when Mongoose bite comes out of CD.

The increased duration make it more effective for PvP, where the opponent can get some distance away from you, allowing you to still apply some damage to them.
---
I understand your concern with it however. In a boss fight, where you spam Mongoose bite, you effectively gain only 1 extra second of Serpent Sting damage from this Talent. Given its other utilities, I think the talent is fine right now

While this is squarely in the "not an actual problem" category, its squarely in the needs tweaking category.

In classic, if you reapply a dot, you reset its ticks. Casting mongoose bite every 4 seconds means you never get the value from the second talent point. And despite mongoose bite being up 100% of the time, it only ticks every 4 seconds. And you likely further eat ticks by using immo trap.

Removing stinging nettle from mongoose bite and putting it at full duration on the firetraps would be a solution, but that has the downside of omegabuffing survivals already good aoe.

Alternatively an application like deadly poison could help where the ticktimer of seepent sting doesnt restart. Either by both abilities adding x time to the duration instead of reapplying it. That might especially be an option if the AoE is deemed to be a bit over the top currently
