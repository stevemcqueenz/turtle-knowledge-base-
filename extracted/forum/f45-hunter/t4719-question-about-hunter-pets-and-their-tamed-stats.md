<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28235#p28235 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Afaik, the only differences within a pets family is the attack speed.
So petopia shows you everything you need to see.

An elite boar will have the same stats as a normal boar.

There used to be differences in abilities, most famous is probably Lupos, but that was patched out as you know.

The only thing you need to worry about is Attack Speed within a pets family.
HIgh/Fast Attackspeed = lower crits, good against casters, good at picking up Aggro on multiple targets
Low/Slow Attackspeed = higher crits, worse against casters, good burst, good Aggro on single targets, bad on multiple targets
