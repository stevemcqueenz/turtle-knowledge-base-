# PETS - Problems and why Beast Mastery is not optimal

## Post 170469 by Skurridizo (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2026-03-09T23:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170469#p170469 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

> ****If you don’t know me, read this introduction. If you already know me, skip to point 1.****

At the very end I will propose some realistic solutions related to the problems explained below.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

 ****Hello, I’m Skurridizo.****

I’m one of the players who has explored hunter pets in Turtle WoW the most — if not the one who has done it the most.

I’m a huge fan of the class and currently have **FOUR level 60 hunters** across different Turtle WoW servers.

I also run **two YouTube channels** dedicated to sharing hunter knowledge specifically for Turtle WoW.

Right now I’m playing **Survival** more than any other spec in **Karazhan 40** and **Naxxramas**, but until recently I always played:

 ****Beast Mastery****

This specialization rewards player skill through small details and makes it the most complex of the three specs — and in my opinion the most fun.

Unfortunately this talent tree is overshadowed by Survival, and many Karazhan 40 mechanics punish it heavily, almost as if the dungeon was designed specifically to discourage playing this spec.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

> ****POINT 1 — Stable Slots****

Many people think having **4 + 1 stable slots** is more than enough.

 **The answer is obviously NO.**

Just like tanks swap gear pieces for different situations, there is no single pet that is the best in every scenario.

> Pet swapping is mandatory if you want to get real value from Beast Mastery.

Here are some examples of pets I tamed, leveled to 60, and eventually abandoned.

All of them are **BiS within their family** because they have the fastest attack speed.

> • Broken Tooth (2 times) – Level 37 rare with ~7h respawn
>  • Spirit Fox (1 time) – Level 53 rare with +48h respawn
>  • Ravage (1 time) – Level 51 rare with 15h respawn
>  • Ravasaur Matriarch (2 times) – Level 53 rare with 15h respawn
>  • Gretheer (2 times) – Level 57 rare with 50h respawn
>  • Lavender Mana Wyrm – Level 7 Wind Serpent from the High Elf starting zone
>  BiS in PvP because its tiny size makes it almost invisible

This is only a small portion of the countless pets I had to find in their BiS version, spend hours leveling to 60, and later discover they were not actually worth it.

 **Ideally required pets:**

> • Bear — Support playstyle
>  • Wolf — AoE farming + offensive support
>  • Slow Raptor — BiS DPS for Marksmanship
>  • Fast Raptor — BiS DPS for Survival
>  • Fast Scorpion — BiS Beast Mastery DPS in most situations
>  • Slow Scorpion — BiS BM/MM DPS in many situations
>  • Wind Serpent — Best option when Scorpion doesn't work / BiS PvP BM
>  • Spider — BiS PvP outside Beast Mastery
>  • Cat — Required for some raid skips
>  • Cobra / Bat / Tallstrider — Solo content and niche situations

Everything else becomes forgotten or sub-optimal.

 **My current stable:**

> Bear
>  Fast Raptor
>  Wolf
>  Fast Scorpion
>  Slow Raptor

 ****I am still missing several important ones.****

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

> ****POINT 2 — Training Difficulty****

In later expansions hunters could tame a low level pet and it would automatically scale to:

 **Player Level -5**

Meaning you only needed to level the last five levels.

Now try leveling the BiS bear:

 **Ursol'Lok**

> Level 32 rare
>  15 hour respawn timer

What happens?

> • You spend an entire day camping the spawn
>  • Then at least 10 hours leveling it to 60
>  • And it consumes a massive amount of food (gold)

I did this a few months ago.

Almost nobody else will do it because the effort does not justify the result compared to a regular Winterspring level 60 bear.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

> ****POINT 3 — Talents That Don’t Fit****

There are some items that increase pet damage such as:

<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60784>
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=22061>

These were useful when you were a **fresh level 60** raiding:

> Molten Core
>  Onyxia
>  Blackwing Lair

But today the situation is simple:

> **+9 Agility already gives more value than increasing your pet damage.**

 **Why does this happen?**

In WoW Classic pets had almost no real scaling.

Turtle WoW introduced new talents that try to solve this.

The most important one is:

 ****SPIRIT BOND****

> • 25% of your Ranged Attack Power → Pet Attack Power
>  • 15% of your Ranged Attack Power → Pet Spell Power
>  • Small health regeneration in combat

Beast Mastery revolves entirely around maximizing this talent.

Because of this, the pets that deal the most damage today are the ones that scale with **Spell Power**:

> Cobra
>  Scorpion
>  Wind Serpent
>  Gorilla

The Gorilla can mostly be ignored because:

> • Poor scaling
>  • 40 second cooldown ability

 **Current pet damage talents:**

> Improved Eyes of the Beast → +30% damage (but you stop attacking)
>  Unleashed Fury → +20% damage
>  Coordinated Assault → scales from hunter melee AP instead of pet
>  Ferocity → +15% crit
>  Frenzy → +30% attack speed
>  Spirit Bond
>  Baited Shot → +15% damage
>  Kill Command → GCD + huge mana cost

 **Only Spirit Bond actually scales with gear.**

And that scaling is simply not enough for late-game content.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

> ****POINT 4 — Late Game Gear Problems****

Beast Mastery benefits most from **Ranged Attack Power**.

However many items in:

> Naxxramas
>  Karazhan 40

trade attack power for **critical strike**.

This improves the hunter’s personal DPS but does almost nothing for the pet.

Additionally:

> Tier 0.5
>  Tier 1
>  Tier 2

had useful defensive bonuses for pets.

These bonuses disappear completely in:

> Tier 3
>  Tier 3.5

There is only one bonus:

> +50 Attack Power for hunter and pet

But the current meta favors **Spell Power scaling pets**.

 **A survivability bonus for pets would be far more valuable.**

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

> ****POINT 5 — Pet Survivability****

In raids almost no healers keep pet frames visible.

So hunters are expected to heal their own pets.

The problem is the spell:

 ****Mend Pet****

> 5 second channel
>  480 mana
>  1225 total healing
>  20 yard range

 **This spell is extremely inefficient.**

Even with the improved talent version it remains terrible.

A healer can outperform it with one instant GCD.

Because of this the most valuable skill becomes:

 **perfect pet positioning.**

But many mechanics still kill pets regardless:

> Void zones
>  Thaddius Tesla charges
>  Kruul cleave
>  Chess knight cleaves
>  Heigan explosions

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

> ****POINT 6 — Raid Mechanics****

The only AoE pet ability is:

 **Gorilla Thunderstomp**

> ~200–320 damage
>  40 second cooldown

It is not viable.

So pets should focus on **single-target damage**.

That role is perfectly filled by the **Scorpion**.

Except:

> Some bosses reset poison stacks
>  Others are completely immune

In Karazhan 40 the scorpion becomes unusable in:

 **6 out of 9 bosses.**

The alternative is the **Wind Serpent**.

But it will be indirectly nerfed because the following combo disappears:

> Kill Command
>  Lightning Breath
>  Baited Shot

Without this burst the pet becomes extremely weak.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

> ****POINT 7 — Consumables and Buffs****

Pets can only use:

> Scrolls
>  Jujus

None of them increase:

 **Pet Spell Power**

Which is exactly what Scorpions and Wind Serpents need.

Meanwhile many buffs increase:

> Strength
>  Attack Power

Which again benefits the wrong pets.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

> **REALISTIC SOLUTIONS**

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

 ****Solution 1 — Chimera Pet Family****

Quest:

 **"Gotta Tame'em All!"**

Requirement:

Learn every pet ability rank from every family.

Reward:

Ability to tame **Chimeras**.

> Attack modifier: +15%
>  Health modifier: +5%
>  Armor modifier: +5%

(Current best base pet: Cobra → +13.5 / +3 / +3)

Chimeras could learn abilities from every pet family.

This would solve:

> Stable slot problems
>  Training problems
>  Testing difficulty
>  Combat versatility

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

 ****Solution 2 — Improve Pet Healing Talents****

Current talents lack synergy:

> Improved Mend Pet
>  Improved Revive Pet
>  Improved Eyes of the Beast

Proposed redesign:

> Improved Mend Pet
>  → 20→25 / 40→50% healing
>  → dispel chance each tick
>
>  NEW:
>  Pet deals 15→30% more damage while channeling
>  and regenerates 15→30% more focus

> Improved Revive Pet
>
>  Cast time reduced by 3→6 sec
>  Mana cost reduced by 20→40%
>
>  NEW:
>  Mend Pet range increased by 15→30 yards

> Improved Eyes of the Beast
>
>  Duration increased by 30→60 sec
>
>  NEW:
>  Pet melee and ability range +2→3 yards

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

> ****Solution 3 — Improve Scaling****

Increase **Spirit Bond**:

> 15→30% Ranged AP → Pet Attack Power
>  10→20% Ranged AP → Pet Spell Power

New talents:

 **Bloody Attacks**

> 2% of pet damage returned as healing

 **Cunning Skills**

> • Pet gains 25% of hunter crit chance
>  • Pet gains AP and Spell Power equal to 25% of hunter Intelligence
>  • Pet copies hunter consumable buffs

This roughly translates to:

> +5–10% pet crit
>  +60 AP / Spell Power

══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

> ****FINAL CONCLUSION****

These are relatively small changes that would dramatically improve the quality of life for Beast Mastery.

Ironically the next patch introduces:

 **Aspect of the Viper**

because some hunters struggle with mana.

But Tallstriders already solved that problem.

> The real solution was never new crazy abilities.
>
>  The real solution is letting hunters craft their own strategies through pets that scale properly.

Pet base stats and abilities could also be reviewed to make more families viable.

I agree that the chimera solution proposed is not the way to go. Pet families need a rebalance and while Skurridizo is right (it won't be easy), it must be the way going forward. Many interesting abilities like Pack Ally were introduced but it didn't change the fact that hyenas, for instance, have no niche.

That said, yes, we should tame chimeras and many more beasts ([viewtopic.php?p=150953#p150953](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150953#p150953))!

The main issue with pets is their survival. As the OP lays out, they're often ignored by healers for both healing and dispelling. Solutions to the problem are:

 - Avoidance affects cleaves and chain lightnings and is buffed to 90%. Any DoT applied through AoE is always reduced, as well

 - Mend Pet becomes a HoT like its TBC version

 - A talent deep in the BM tree giving vampirism to the pet through either the pet's own damage, the hunter's or both

 - Pets should melee the target from max melee distance when sent to attack when they are outside melee range. More often than not, a pet will go dead center of a target's hitbox and this can cause trouble with cleaves or attacks like shadowflame. This behavior is observed in bosses such as Nefarian, the BWL drakes, Ragnaros, Omen or Solnius but funnily not on Onyxia: pets do attack from max distance there. Any hunter doing Nefarian will quickly realize how disruptive this problem is, especially when combined with the continuous fears that displace the pet.

On the other hand, the BM tree is bloated with 5-point talent nodes: after starting the 3rd row, there's not a single point to spare on *any* utility talent, not even Intimidation! This is relevant because if Mend Pet became a HoT, for instance, then the talent that provides a dispel would become quite attractive but PvE BM hunters would not be able to take it.

Talents such as Ferocity should have a total of 3/3 while keeping the overall same amount of damage increase. Frenzy should just be 1 or at most 2 points. In the history of Vanilla, TBC, WotLK, SoD and any fanmade variations, Frenzy has **never** been completely filled and at most had a total of 3 out of 5 points.

The incoming new talent tree worsens this situation by having a second-to-last row with a total of 9 talent points... on a class that *needs* 20 points for another tree if going ranged! No other class or spec in the game has these restrictions. For instance, rogues require less points to reach the desired crit damage talent in the Assassination tree, whereas warriors only need 2 points to fill Impale in the Arms tree.

I have provided more feedback on the upcoming changes here [viewtopic.php?p=166396#p166396](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166396#p166396), as well as the original post I quote there but I won't go into detail in this thread because it's not the focus.

However, I will say that the incoming Kill Command could be another tuning knob when it comes to pet families. What if KC had damage and effects that varied based on family? For instance, what if a hyena using KC would reduce the CD of Pack Ally by a certain amount of seconds? If KC is released as is, it's going to be one of the most, if not *the* most, underwhelming capstones of all specs in the game (it's weaker than Coordinated Assault, an 11-point talent!). But if it had a change like this one, then it'd be quite thematic and useful, and it would help balance pet families in other ways.

I also agree with Skurridizo when it comes to pet scaling. Having Spirit Bond (or any other talent) transform our intellect stat into pet damage is an interesting way of influencing gearing a bit besides only desiring raw agility. In my opinion, 30% of the hunter's total crit should benefit the pet as well as 100% of the haste from gear (so no quiver, quick shots/strikes or rapid fire). This would make scaling for BM more in line with other non-caster DPS specs.

Regardless, it's clear that something has to be done. We need more stable slots, more tameables, a better pet leveling system, and a proper look at pet survival. Hopefully, the developers will act sooner than later. TWoW is the only Classic type server that is going in the right direction when it comes to pets but there's still a long way to go.
