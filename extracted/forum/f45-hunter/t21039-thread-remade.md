The only thing that comes to mind is in WotLK, they added a Pet Talent that lowered the chance your pet could be critically hit by 3/6%, making it uncritable by bosses.  I do not, however, recall if this is required for Classic.

Other than that, I always enjoyed OTing with my Hunter.  Of course, having a guild that supported my crazy nonsense was also quite helpful. LOL  It wasn't as bad as it sounds, and even came in clutch OTing some adds in a raid more than once (happened in Gruul's Lair on more than one occasion).  Many 5-mans were doable up through LK (I stopped playing early Cata), and I did some OTing in a few raids in BC/LK, which was hilarious and fun; a little chaotic at times, but never caused any wipes or anything.  I recall having a few macros set up for some better pet control.  The biggest issue I recall having with Pet Tank is lack of solid AoE aggro, so if a fight had a bunch of adds I had to assume the role of not-quite-MT while the real MT dealt with the adds.  I also recall there being a few bosses in which there were some issues, but I think those were mostly due to a combination of boss movement (esp fliers), Pet AI, and room geometry; but that was also a long time ago. :D  (*EDIT: I recall someone made a post about them MTing Icecrown as a Hunter. Of course, I believe this was after most of the guild was already geared out.  Still, I think that would have made for a fun challenge*).

Personally, I've always wanted to be able to fight in melee alongside my pet, like Rexxar, trading aggro back and forth using a melee Distracting Strike and Intimidation, while also swapping between a Melee DPS Aspect and Monkey Aspect for Evasion; but I'm not really sure how you'd Talent that w/o making BM the melee spec. LOL
