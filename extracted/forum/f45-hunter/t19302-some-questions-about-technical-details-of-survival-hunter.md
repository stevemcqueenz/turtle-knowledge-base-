>  4、I find that survival hunters make so much aggro that my pets can't hold mobs most of the time. Should I give up tank pets and switch to my Broken Tooth?

 1. No. The ability specifies *ranged attack power*
 2. For now, Two-handed is by far the better choice, dual wield was buffed recently, but probably needs a little more to be truly comparable.

 3. You are going to need a standard ranged weapon such as bow/crossbow or gun for the end game. One of the cool things about Melee Hunt is that unlike a Rogue or a Warrior that mostly carry their ranged weapons for passive stats and the situational utility. A Hunter is still a Hunter, even specialized for close combat, your ranged playstyle remains fully available whenever you need it. Albeit significantly lower damage. There are several encounters, however, where you'd rather be alive with half the DPS.

 4. Yes, forget about your pet holding aggro as Survival. Screechers are a popular choice for this build.
 5. Used to have *significant* mana issues before they reduced the costs. Now it's just *mana issues.*
