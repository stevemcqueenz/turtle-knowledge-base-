# Ideas to buff Improved Hunter's Mark

## Post 127586 by Steakhouse (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-19T22:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127586#p127586 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know Marks is performing well, and that there are cases for speccing Imp Hunter's Mark as is, but it's kindof a boring talent that doesn't feel very good to grab, spending multiple points in giving a few extra RAP points is meh. Here are some ideas on how to improve it.

 1) Make it interact with Hit chance

This works well with the aiming scope visual and would make it a wonderful debuff to apply to bosses: "Increases chance to hit the target by 1%" at max rank. If this is too strong, simply improving the Hunter's chance to hit is great (and perhaps their pet's !)

 2) Make a % of the ranged AP bonus also apply to melee AP

This is what TBC did. I think it's pretty self explanatory. Again if this is too strong, could be limited to the Hunter and their pet.
