# Suggestions for Hunter Skill Changes

## Post 82941 by Gxmaki1 — 2023-12-28T01:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82941#p82941 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hunter's current arcane shooting and aiming shooting are very awkward, with low damage and long cooldown, and they also use the same cooldown time.

My personal suggestion is to change the arcane shooting to an arcane damage that causes weapon damage, or to a damage that maintains its current state but can refresh the duration of the Poison Serpent Sting.

Aiming shooting have been changed to an instant skill when the target enters the kill phase.

The above suggestions are based on maintaining a six second shared cooling.

Finally, there are suggestions for hunter pets.

In the current version, many bosses have individual skills, even if their pets have 50% damage reduction, their survival rate is still low, and pets do not inherit hunter attributes, resulting in very low damage.

My personal suggestion is to increase the "Lone Wolf" skill and increase the hunter's damage by 20% when dissolving pets.It can appear at the deepest part of the talent tree.
