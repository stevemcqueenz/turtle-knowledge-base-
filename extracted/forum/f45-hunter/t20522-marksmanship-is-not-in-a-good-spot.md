# Marksmanship is not in a good spot

## Post 141781 by Cabum (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-27T22:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141781#p141781 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

MM falls behind as a pure dps class. Here are some points I experience raiding MM for 2 years, and from others hunters.

 1. Mediocre single target dps, and very poor aoe solutions.

 2. Shots scale with spellpower, while survival tree has the option to change trap scaling to attackpower.

 2. New tier 3.5 set does not fit MM spec. Increased haste on quickshots by 5% and the heavy amount of %hit on gear are more suited for melee survival spec.

 3. Lack of synergy with other melees. Windfury, strength totem, battle shout, and blessing of might. Having a % from attack power to affect range attack power could make those buffs more relevant.

 4. Consume buffs that do not affect range attack power. Exp.: Elixir of Demonslaying, ele sharp stones, engi chicken.

 5. Dull talents added to MM from cc2, extra shot and bleeds. Having a % from range ap on bleeds, like the new Lacerate spell for surv, would feel more consistent. Removing the extrashot talent and let windfury work on range hits sounds fun, but maybe to strong.

I wouldn't say that MM is in a particularly terrible spot. It's not great, around middle of the pack dps. In good conditions and good hands it gets slightly better than that. It doesn't necessarily need a major overhaul to be good, but it's fair to expect more than nothing.
What gets frustrating for some MM players is that one can perceive a bias in spec design within the class. One would expect that the designers would have seen the clear performance gap that has existed since the mongoose bite rework a while back. MM loses out in every type of encounter. Sustained, bursty, aoe, single target.
It certainly doesn't help that the new raid has more aoe and add phases than any previous raid, where the gap is largest.
And yet time and again we see more survival buffs and QoL, while MM appears to be forgotten.
Having 2 dead points in the second/third tiers, haste/gcd cap issues just with its own class buffs, certain consumables and buffs not affecting ranged shots, just to name a few.
Strangely, juju flurry is about to affect spell casting speed in 1.18, but for unclear reasons a change to demonslaying elixir is inappropriate.
The implication isn't that this is maliciously done to force someone's favorite spec to the top or put down another. But personal bias happens and can be distracting from balanced design.
To end on a positive note, the upcoming BM rework and new pet abilities are very exciting! I hope MM can receive another look with fresh eyes as well.
