## Post 124846 by Wolf of Rage (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-01T06:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124846#p124846 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Track Hidden's Stealth Detection value is 30 -- considering every 5 equates to +1 Levels, that is tantamount to being six Levels above potential victims (on top of Minimap display when seen). F.ex. L50 VS L50 with Track Hidden, it's like L56 VS L50.

It must be noted that it is for Stealth only, not Invisibility.

Any +Stealth modifiers like Talents, Equipment and Enchantments are to also be kept in mind (Feral, Sub, Cloak Enchantment, etc.).

Easy to test would be by checking with two Characters that have a six-Level difference and absolutely nothing else of the above in effect; if Track Hidden then does not give the same results as though you were six Levels in difference while on, then it clearly has some issue here.
