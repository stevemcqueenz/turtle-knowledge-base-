Greetings!

First of all, thank you turtle devs for all the changes to BM, I’m very happy with most of it.

I would like to suggest switching Intimidation’s place in the talent tree with Spirit Bond’s place on row 6 though.

Right now row 5 only has 4 spots to spend one’s talent points, which I feel is a little low and forces players to spend points on earlier/less desirable talents.

With Spirit Bond on row 5 there would be 5 spots to spend talent points on. It also makes it possible to pick up more desirable talents further down the tree.

Thnx for your consideration! <3
