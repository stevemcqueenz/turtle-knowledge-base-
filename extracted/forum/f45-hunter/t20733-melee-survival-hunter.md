Call me crazy, but, melee hunter was already viable before CC2 made it a real supported thing.

As in: Your raid/group wouldn't wipe to some hidden mechanic preventing a hunter from entering melee range.

Brief fit of rambling aside- yes. Melee hunter performs good (from what I'm told) and is extremely fun. You'll have issues with levelling early on as baseline ability support (only having raptor strike until 20 something) is pretty lacking, but past the 20s it'll start to shape up and become a decent way to level. You're tankier/more slippery with talents, so your pet not being able to keep threat isn't an issue very often, and damage can get pretty high. If you're going for efficiency BM is still king, but, I had good fun with survival. The best part is you don't need to commit too much to one build since survival scales really well with agility which is a bread and butter hunter stat.

So, to summarize:

Not very good early levelling. Likely better off playing BM until you get a significant amount of melee throughput stuff. Mongoose bite and wolf aspect are important.

Gearing is simple, and straightforward, not clashing with other hunter specs making for easy switching whenever you desire.

Damage performance is great at endgame, with especially strong cleave rivalling if not eclipsing warriors, but there's a significant lack of utility/special support.

Traps count as spells, so you might lack heavily in the damage department in early raids, where fire resist is just leaking out the ass of every trash mob and boss. Hopefully this is fixed.

Very rare occurrence, but sometimes traps bug out and just won't drop. This tends to happen back to back when it does, but like I said, very rare. It's possible this is getting fixed, but I'm just guessing.

This talent build likely isn't optimal, but, it's what I've got. You could also go down MM for improved stings since stinging nettle applies serpent sting, but I feel like your pet living longer and doing more damage will see more use than ranged abilities (as a MELEE hunter lawl) costing a bit less mana. Also, serpent sting's damage isn't very high to begin with. Pets have multiple forms of throughput with autoattacks, and abilities.

[https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?p ... TAbIQoBAA=](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?points=FoAAAoAIAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAFQCYBJTAbIQoBAA=)

Also, warning. Your pet will NOT hold threat. I personally haven't tried using a bat, but my cat couldn't tank for shit the entire time I was playing survival. The trade-off for this is you can soak up damage better, and slowing down on damage so your buddy CAN tank for you is helpful when you want to minimize taking damage. Especially at levels where you're not wearing mail.
