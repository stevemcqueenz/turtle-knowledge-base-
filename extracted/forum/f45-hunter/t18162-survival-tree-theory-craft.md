# Survival tree theory-craft

## Post 123634 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-24T19:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123634#p123634 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

Wanted to create a theory-craft for the survival tree, giving it the 'enhancement shaman treatment', meaning that its goal is both to serve a melee DPS and a (dungeon) tank build now.
It is not a suggestion but I found this idea very interesting so I gave it a shot.

It's not on the tree but I imagine that in this world Aspect of the Monkey also gives a threat modifier next to the plus dodge. Melee uses Wolf anyway, Monkey is mostly used sometimes in PVP and in some open world fights, usually if the pet is down. Threat shouldn't really affect things there.
I also 'imagined' that hunters have some sort of taunt here, didn't elaborate on it on the tree itself.
Changing between aspects eats up a GCD.

Highlighted any talent I changed or added.
Removed Survivalist. While it's cool for tanks, didn't want to overload the tree with talents and it gave me 5TPs to work with.
My core idea was to make some talents 'modular ones', doing slightly different things with Aspect of the Wolf and Aspect of the Monkey. Wolf becoming 'the' melee DPS aspect, Monkey is used for tanks.

<https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/tree/bwFOSKAtNj>

 **Some explanation behind the changes:**

 * Swift Reflexes: Made it a 3TP talent. DPS hunter gets +1% haste, tank also has +1% parry. Small change. Wanted to give this +1% parry since hunters have no block but didn't want to go over 3% with the haste buff.
 * Removed Survivalist, replaced it with primal Mitigation (3TP). Wanted to give a soul link look-alike for tank's mitigation. Of course made it worse since we are far from being at the bottom of the tree. At the same time, gave DPS some threat management here. In my experience, melee hunter can go a bit over the top with threat, unless they cancel their rotation regularly to use threat-reduction strikes.
 * Carve out, Strike Command in. I felt that most hunter players complained about carve, feeling weird. Trap takes care of the AoE damage anyway, wanted to replace it with something else, which also has a pet-theme. I really felt like pet-combo melee attacks were lacking from this tree, wanted to make one. Using Wolf aspect it gives a nice buff to the pet, too. With Monkey, it has a threat modifier to facilitate tanking.
 * Rapid Strikes also reducing Strike Command's CS by 1 sec. I imagined that talent to have around 10-12 Sec CD by default.
 * Killer Instinct -> Survival Instinct: Gave the +crit for everyone but closed the + damage behind the Wolf aspect. In case of Monkey, you get a buff for damage mitigation. As it stands, hunter mitigation is still really bad. Didn't want to make them too tanky, would go against the theme. Instead, reduced the damage taken by crits. F.ex. with this 20% crit damage reduction a crit with 150% damage would deal 120% damage to them. The goal was to prevent too big spikes in the HP.
 * Lightning Reflexes: Made it modular, too. Agility increase is for everyone. Extra attack power is locked behind Wolf, Monkey gets 10% of agility as defense instead.
 * Lastly, added a new capstone talent: Aspect Mastery. Wolf grants some of your attack power to your pet. 20% was a made up number, I happily change it if needed. Monkey gives a big armor increase based on your agility. 300% seems excessive but I can accept it as a capstone talent. Hunter agi usually goes around 500 at raids if done well. It means 1500 armor which is a mediocre late-game shield at best so I think 300% is all right.
