# Surv Hunter after 1.18

## Post 147658 by Sarthrethal — 2025-08-22T07:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147658#p147658 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!
I want to raise the topic of nerfing hunters in the survival spec.
At first, if we remember, the main nerf of damage was through reducing the bonus to 2 main melee abilities from 20 percent and 20 flat to 10 percent and flat, respectively, which was already a significant nerf of overall damage for the survival spec.

 **1st version:** Vicious Strikes: Now reduces the cooldown of Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite by 0.5/1 second but the damage increase is reduced from 10/20% weapon damage and 10/20 flat damage to 5/10% weapon damage and 5/10 flat damage.

 **2nd version:** Vicious Strikes: No longer increases Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite damage.

In contrast, the survival spec was given a new skill, **Lacerate**.
Which, according to the description, looked very weak, and it no longer covered the loss of promised dps.
However, before the release, the bonus damage to mongoose and raptor strike was completely removed and two key abilities lost their identity in this spec, since the only buff left was a 1-second cooldown reduction.
The damage of these abilities became basic.
It turns out that MM and BM deal the same amount of damage with these abilities.
Which hits the identity of the survival spec hunter.
Which now only has "improved traps", and let's be honest, fire traps, without the talents of the survival spec - absolutely unused and useless.

The new skill lacerate is very weak.
 35% of AP is a very weak primary damage. With a raid gear with 2k AP - we have ~600-700 damage from the ability, which is still cut by armor. Where there are no critical value multipliers, we have ~900-1000 (taking into account the modifier of a blow to the back or side) damage from a crit.

The DOT from this ability has just a meme damage, 20% of the damage inflicted, if we take conditionally 1000, then the DOT will be 200 in 8 seconds, in a good raid composition and full dps chemistry - was 35-37 damage per tick.
We will also keep quiet about a ton of mobs and bosses with immunity to bleeding.
Damage from Dot - you can simply not take into account.

Benchmark was taken in BWL, in a good raid composition, with a shaman in the party, and full dps chemistry and full T2 gear.
If before I could compete with mages in full T2 and retpals, DC priests, ENH shamans as a main survival hunter, now the final outgoing damage has fallen by more than 25%, with all the raid multipliers and bonuses. 600-650k damage when before this amount was 850-900k.
Now I don't even have the opportunity to somehow get close to my mates. Since I did everything the same as before, and it is difficult to do better, maybe 5-7% with lucky crits and slack of my guys, but the gap of 20-25% remains, and it is no longer possible to overcome it.

I repeat, the comparison is with players who have the same level of equipment.

Lacerate is at the very bottom of the dps meter, it is lower than a **PET** that is not strengthened by any talent from the BM tree.
With 30+% crit in melee. It procs on CD and it was used on CD.
Remember, BWL is one of the worst raids for pets.
This ability is also very expensive in mana.
Surv Hunter already had problems with mana, now he becomes OOM in just 35-45 seconds, even with a paladin and a sham in the group. If we take content for 10 - 5 people, and there is no sham paladin, you drink more often than any class.
If you wanted to tie this class with the use of mana potions and teas, congratulations, you did it.

As a result, the survival spec has become extremely ineffective, yes, you can run with it, but you will not be able to give similar efficiency to other classes. To give out damage, you need to do more, but you give out significantly less. THIS IS ABSOLUTELY NOT FUN.
Satisfaction and reward for the game for this spec has disappeared.
It would not have been very common spec, but now even being a fan of the spec, I am seriously thinking about reroll to another character.

I am not against the first version of reducing damage from 20 to 10 percent and flat. Since you will no longer be competent with other specs.
Lacerate, for such a mana cost, needs to be changed, in its current form this skill is not worth it.
Perhaps increasing the primary damage from 35 to 65% and the damage dot from 20 to 60% will make this skill at least somewhat useful.
Or the ability to stack bleeding up to three times.

Thank you!
