<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110856#p110856 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**1.Consolidate Entrapment and Improve Wing Clip together.**

 **2.Replace the old Improve wing clip with Dual Weild specialization talent (3/3)**
Increase the damage done by your off hand weapon by 10%/20%/30%. When ecquiped with a 1 handed weapon, your raptor strike becomes instant and has a 10%/20%/30% chance to not incur its cooldown.

 **3.Remove Carve and replace with new Gap closer talent (1/1)**
Explosive trap+sting AOE should be enough without needing a 5 man cleave button on top of that.
Perhaps a charge when both the pet and hunter leap at the ennemy dealing a chunk of damage.

 **4.Make improve feign Death baseline and replace it with Raptors Fury (2/2)**
Raptor Strike increases damage done by Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite by 7%/15% for 30 sec, stacking up to 5 times. Subsequent doses do not extend the duration of this effect.

 **5.Swap position of improved concussive shot and Improved stings talent in marksman tree.**
