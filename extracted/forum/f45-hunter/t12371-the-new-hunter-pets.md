So far, I have only played through the High Elf starter zone, so I can't speak to what new pets came with the goblins, but I love the ability to tame Hawkstriders and Mana Wyrm. Has anyone figured out or written out a list, what pet family they each belong to and what they can and cannot learn?

## Post 82430 by Bgnome — 2023-12-23T11:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82430#p82430 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The hawkstriders are tallstriders.  The mana wyrms are wind serpents.  You can find their respective abilities on Petopia.

FYI, eagles are owls, foxes and demon hounds are wolves, lynx are cats, and Tel'abim monkeys are gorillas.
