## Post 72989 by Aldis — 2023-10-23T08:49:00Z

Pets have a happiness level and in order to maintain your pets happiness, you must feed it. Feeding your pet is very important for overall output. The happier your pet is, the more damage it deals:

Happy: 125% damage
Content: 100% damage
Unhappy: 75% damage
The pet receives loyalty points as well as experience only when in a happy mood. Therefore, keep it happy and kill mobs (without gaining experience, loyalty does not increase). If you stand in a city, then loyalty will not increase.

## Post 72991 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-10-23T09:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=72991#p72991 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Your pet has a "Happiness" icon near its portrait in the UI.  When it's green, your pet gains does bonus damage.  If it's red, your pet does less damage.  If your pet is Level 1 Loyalty and your pet's Happiness is in the red for a while, it'll run away.  In order to prevent this, you need to feed your pet with food that is equal or greater than it's current level.  It will get hungry again, after a while, and especially at lower Loyalty levels, so keep an eye on that icon.  Even just standing around idle will make your pet hungry!

In order to raise your pet to Loyalty Level 2, you need to keep its Happiness in either the green (Happy) or yellow (Content) states, then both of you need to gain one Experience Level.  Once your pet reaches Loyalty Level 2, it won't run away, anymore, and you can theoretically stop feeding it… but it won't gain any more Loyalty, thus will never gain bonus Skill Points for training.

Hope this helps.

While everything above is mostly true, I assume your problem stems from not understanding that your pet gains happiness over the course of 30 seconds when you feed it. It gains 35 happiness per tick for level appropriate food, 17 and 8 per tick respectively if the food is of lower level. You need to feed your pet and wait for the 10 ticks to run out for your pet to gain the total amount of happiness from eating. It does not help to shove food down your pets throat. Feed it, wait 30 seconds, don't send your pet to combat in that time because this will end the eating process, feed it again, wait another 30 seconds. Check your combat log to see if your food is of appropriate level, does your pet gain 35, 17 or 8 per tick. A red pet will need 2x feeding at 35 per tick to go to green.
