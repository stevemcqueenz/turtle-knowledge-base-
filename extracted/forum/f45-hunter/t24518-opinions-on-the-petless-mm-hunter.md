> **Czaro92 wrote: Thu Mar 26, 2026 2:26 pm**
> Pets have virtually no aggro for hunter MM, so whether you have a pet or not, you still have to deal with the dead zone.

Exactly. Fighting mobs as MM is as follows:
 - Turn off Aspect of the Hawk(to avoid dealing too much damage).
 - Have your pet attack the target preemptively (hopefully it has charge or dash to save you time).
 - Just auto-attack.
 - Weave in an Arcane Shot or Steady Shot when the mob is close to dying, or runs away on low hp. That's it.

For comparison, a BM Hunter has 0 downtime fighting most elite mobs, even in lv50 green gear, and has low risk of taking aggro from their pet.

The argument that it will take away the "skill" from playing MM hunters if they don't have pets is rather contradictive, as you have to actually be mobile and use your abilities in order to take down the mob fast enough, and maintain proper distance.

I also think talents like Primal Aspects shouldn't give melee hunters life-leech, as that only encourages them to tank the mob, instead of their pet. I'd much rather see improvements to their threat reduction instead.

As for how I think the Petless talent can be incorporated into the MM tree. Have it as a capstone talent and have a 5 talent prerequisite. And as for the effect, something of the sort:
 - Increase ranged damage while not having a pet by 8%.
 - Reduce CD of Concussive Shot by 3 seconds.
 - Remove dead zone (if not feasible, add some additional mana reduction to shots)
