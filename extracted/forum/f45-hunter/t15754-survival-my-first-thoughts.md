I played survival spec for a few days and after my skeptical attitude, I must say that it is a very nice spec for world pve and instance games focused on aoe dps. However, my first two main observations are:

 -It would be useful to do something with improved stings, the problem is that you have to spend 5 points in tier 1 of the mm tree on talents that are completely unnecessary for melee , and improved stings, in turn, is a very good talent for aoe dps needed for survival, but to get to it you have to spend 5 unnecessary talent points (efficency is not needed cuz stings are applied for free with moongose bite or traps) I see problem here.

 -The second issue is that some talent for dual wield boost would be useful because each skill is scaled with the weapon and here 2h obviously scales much better and we hit much bigger hits with carve/moongose ​​bite etc...
