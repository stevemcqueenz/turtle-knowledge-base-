Hey there! No matter what you go for early on, you can always rely on your pet to tank and help you out. Your first talent respec is super cheap, so if you're not happy by your mid 20s, 30s, you can switch things up. In the forum you may see a lot of people not liking the recent changes to Beast Mastery hunter. Even so, it's still a very leveling friendly build path, so that should not deter you

You can try this build for leveling: [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?p ... gB-AoAoQB-](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?points=AoAaRoSoJCQgB-AoAoQB-)

And then respec to this if you want to use Beast Mastery as your late game spec: [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?p ... -AoCoQBAo-](https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/hunter?points=FYAQBoQoBCQQB-AoCoQBAo-)

By the time you reach level 50 or so, you'll have a good feel on the class, and may want to focus entirely on Marksmanship or Survival (melee hunter). But for now, I don't think you should worry about that.

Good luck on your journey/
