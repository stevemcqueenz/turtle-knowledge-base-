# Death of a Hunter

## Post 140216 by Zogtog — 2025-07-18T04:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140216#p140216 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The young huntress crept silently in the silence of the night to the cave and carefully, slightly touching the skin at the entrance, crept inside. In the depths, an old orc sat hunched over by the fire. His once powerful hands shook slightly when he added another log to the fire, but even now it was clear that he noticed her presence. Slowly lowering the unfinished fire trap to the floor, he stuck one hand under his bed.

 - You bypassed all my traps, so it really is that I am already quite bad. Why did you come?

The elf came closer, but did not sit down by the fire, remaining in the shadows.

 - I fulfilled my part of the prophecy, whoever tracks down the Great Pathfinder will be able to become part of the family. I need your knowledge, hunter, I want to be like you, to hit a squirrel in the eye from a hundred yards away, to talk to animals and run faster than the wind, luring the most terrible monsters of this world into traps.

 - Go away, I don't take any more students!

 - Am I right? Are you the one they told legends about? Show me your Rhok'delar, Longbow of the Ancient Keepers, and I will swear an oath of allegiance to our cause!

 - No more deeds! No more oaths! No more hunters and family! The gods have cursed us and deprived us of our eyes! - the old orc screamed, tears streaming down his wrinkled cheeks - We don't hunt anymore, we only survive now...

The orc threw off the blanket. In his hand, instead of the legendary bow, was an old, jagged two-handed axe.
