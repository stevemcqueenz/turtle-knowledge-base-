A lvl 1 character uses enchants in order to obtain most of their states, but they also get a few from items they can grind for like the Furbolg Medicine Pouch. The only profession a lvl 1 can use is First Aid, but it's very good for us.

The challenge is about using knowledge to overcome the limitations lvl 1 characters face doing content outside of their area. There is a portion of the challenge that requires you to do 100 quests.
