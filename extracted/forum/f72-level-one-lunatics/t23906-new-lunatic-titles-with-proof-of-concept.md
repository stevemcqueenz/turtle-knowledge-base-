## Post 167768 by Cysthen (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-08T18:49:00Z

**Lunatic Title Recommendations**

Below are recommendations for new Lunatic Titles from the community. They would be extremely well earned and are more difficult than some of the already existing titles.

 **Tinyfin Angler**

The Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza is an appropriate title considering winning by obtaining the first 40 Speckled Tastyfish requires the use of Lures and puts us at a disadvantage against Level 60 Players. This is much harder than the current Arena's Smallest Smasher Title.

---
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/fCLNN0s.jpeg)
---
We recommend the ****Tinyfin Angler**** title be tied to the completion of the Master Angler quest.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=8193>

 **Witness to Kings' End**

Title was a little hard to come to an agreement on, however with recent events we had Lululunatic attend a Raid for killing Faction Leaders. After the success, people said there should be a title for Lunatics to kill all of the Faction Leaders and the majority seemed to agree.

---
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/kOMT7S7.jpeg)
---
This would require all appropriate Faction Leaders per Faction to obtain the title. This is a social challenge to be able to either join an existing group or form one themselves.

 **Mini Menagerie**

We have a pet collection leaderboard in our Lunatic Community and have finally hit a wall with a player that has obtained just about every available pet. Below we will contain pictures and a list of available pets currently obtainable.

Huge shoutout to Luciejayne for getting to this point and supplying us with the images.
