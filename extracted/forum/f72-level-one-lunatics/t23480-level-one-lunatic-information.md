# Level One Lunatic Information

## Post 164279 by Cysthen (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-02T17:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164279#p164279 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

**What is the Level One Lunatic challenge?**

During this challenge, you'll push the limits of a level one character and earn exclusive titles to prove just how far a noob can go:

- ****The Wee Warlord**** — earn **10,000 honorable kills** to show you're more than just fresh meat.

- ****Pint-Sized Politician**** — reach **Exalted** with at least one faction to prove that even a tiny hero can have powerful friends.

- ****Arena’s Smallest Smasher**** — obtain the **Arena Master Trinket** to demonstrate that, even at level one, you’re an unstoppable force of nature.

- ****Hero of Goldshire**** (Alliance) / ****Lazy Peon's Foreman**** (Horde) — complete **100 quests** available to you, earning the respect of tutorial NPCs everywhere.

- ****Azeroth's Most Unlucky Tourist**** — **explore every zone**, show death won't stop you and earn recognition amongst explorers. This also grants the ****Cartographer**** title.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/ciWl8Ms.png)

 **Note: YOU CAN NOT LEVEL UP.**

Think of this as a seperate endgame or game mode.
This tooltip has not been updated to exclude that or include the Exploration Title.

---
 **How to begin the challenge?**

Simply select the Lunatic Challenge from the Character Creator!

---
 **How do Lunatics communicate?**

By default, new characters on Turtle WoW cannot speak until they reach level 10 to prevent spam bots. However, Lunatics who complete **31 quests** at level 1 and spend **at least 10 hours in-game** will unlock chat privileges!

---
 **Are battlegrounds & arenas available at level 1?**

Yes! The ****1-9 Bracket for Warsong Gulch, Skirmish and Rated Arenas**** is available from the Minimap.

 ****Arathi Basin**** is now also available from the Minimap thanks to the ****PVPWhen**** Addon below:
(Originally only available at the Battlemaster).
<https://github.com/JardineWebSolutions/PVPWhen>

We are advocating for the return of Battleground Announcements.

---
 **Are dungeons or raids available at level 1?**

Yes! ****Ragefire Chasm****, ****The Deadmines****, and ****Wailing Caverns**** are all available as ****10 Man Raids**** with exclusive items that only drop if the Dungeon ID has only Lunatics inside.
We are advocating for ****Shadowfang Keep**** and ****40 Man Gnomeregan****.

Since Turtle WoW was the first realm to introduce this challenge, they're celebrating with a special **wallpaper** made by the talented artist **Lionell Schramm!**

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/3N6MJJM.jpeg)

Full Size: <https://turtlecraft.gg/images/level_one_lunatic.jpg>

Stay tuned, keep fighting, and remember size doesn't matter when you have determination and possibly a really sharp frying pan.

---
 **Frequently asked Questions.**

 **How do I fund my Lunatic if I am new to the server and interested in this challenge?**

Turtle WoW granted us ****Fishing**** from level 1 which enables us to farm items such as Stonescale Eels for Gold as well as earn ****Lucky Fishing Hat****, ****Nat Pagle's Extreme Anglin' Boots****, or ****Hook of the Master Angler**** if you're feeling up to the challenge! ****Tinyfin Angler**** title when.

---
 **Is there a guild and community for this challenge?**

Yes! ****<Level One Lunatic>**** on Nordanaar is the most active currently with 500+ Members and a Booty Bay Guild Port led by Stabsmckenzy and Cysthetic.

---
 **Is multiboxing allowed for this challenge?**

Yes! The Level One Lunatic Challenge is ****not bound by the multibox challenge rules****. This enables Lunatics to farm Timbermaw Reputation like any other Twink as well as buff outside of Raids with certain buffs like Mark of the Wild Rank 2, Thorns Rank 1, Scrolls Rank IV, Winterspring Jujus, Ungoro Crystals, and Weapon Stones/Oils.

Found here: [viewtopic.php?t=21645](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=21645)
 ****"Level One Lunatics are no longer bound by the no-multiboxing rule in challenges."****

---
 **Can you use World Buffs?**

Yes! Lunatics have access to ****Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer****, ****Spirit of Zandalar****, ****Darkmoon Faire****, ****Warchief's Blessing****, and ****Songflower Serenade****. They can even obtain ****Chronoboon Displacer**** from ****Chromie**** in ****Western Plaguelands**** to suspend them.

---
 **Where do I get shoulders?**

Cloth shoulders drop off ****Leper Gnomes**** outside ****Gnomeregan (Lunatic Exclusive)****.
Leather is from ****Gregor Agamand**** in ****Tirisfal Glades (Agamand Mills)****.
Mail is a ****Tradable World Drop****, check the Auction House, or ask the Guild.

---
 **How do I know how many quests I've completed?**

Typing ****/played**** will send you a server message with how many quests you've completed.

---
 **Where do I check how many zones I have left to discover?**

Typing ****.cart**** in /say will send you a server message telling you how many zones you have left to discover and hint at a zone you've missed.

---
 **Most Current Pressing Issues.**

- ****Lack of World PVP**** has removed our gameplay loop with nothing to do from a Solo player perspective. I recommend more incentives for World PVP, the current iteration of Warmode is commonly toggled on for Battlegrounds and then off for World Exploration in most cases. It's good that it was removed from the character creator and put on Battlemasters as it was very oppressive in starter zones but now very little use it at all. Fixing this alone would fix players being more active and in que for Battlegrounds.

- ****Lack of Gear in Raids****. We have a handful of best in slot items but ****The Deadmines**** and ****Wailing Caverns**** in particular there's no reason whatsoever to kill Bosses past Sneed and Anacondra aside from practice with the hopes more will be added soon. Rewards drive attendance, people wouldn't be farming Naxxramas in best in slot to help others for years if it wasn't for the gear. Doing so wouldn't be helping them. ****Ragefire Chasm**** gear is questionable as well as one is replaced already in ****Wailing Caverns****.

- As a community we decided to take some ****initiative**** and ****code items and progression ourselves****. Its a work in progress but that can be found here: [viewtopic.php?f=72&t=19523](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=72&t=19523)

- ****Fashion Coins**** are not obtainable in any reasonable fashion. The only way we can get them is a 1% drop rate off end bosses in our dungeons. It can be farmed with mains but is an absurd method when the rest of the server has a repeatable quest available. I recommend reducing the level requirement on the following quest or creating one with more expensive materials to help the server economy. <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=60030>

- We also have a suggestion thread for ****New Lunatic Titles**** to be added here: [viewtopic.php?p=167768#p167768](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167768#p167768)
