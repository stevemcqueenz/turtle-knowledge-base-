Welcome to my

 *Where you learn that Rogue may perhaps not be the only good class in this Bracket.*

Lets get things started.

 **Before you read anything else you need the following Addon.**

 **ItemRack**
 *<https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/ItemRack>*

 **Quickheal**
 *<https://github.com/Zebouski/QuickHeal>*

Note: In regards to Quickheal I am aware many people dislike automation addons. The reason for its inclusion is our Heal is tied to a Weapon Swap Macro. I typically use an Alt Selfcast Addon but it doesn't work with macro'd Heals. This is the best way I have found for now, I'll update it with a change in the future if I can find out other methods.

 **Spells**

 **Devotion Aura**
Gives 55 additional armor to party members. 30 Yard Range.
 **Note: Armor Scaling is very good at Level 1.**

 **Seal of Righteousness**
 20 Mana
Hits for 1-2 with a 1H Equipped and 3-4 with a 2H Equipped.
This has a chance to trigger Weapon Enchants.
In PVE this can also trigger on attacks that Miss.
For full DPS Builds and PVE I recommend using a 2h for higher Proc Rates.
Recommend putting Fiery on a Mutton Chop and making an ItemRack load out called (FIRE) to equip it.

These are the following Macros I use:
/script EquipSet("FIRE")
/cast Seal of Righteousness(Rank 1)

/script EquipSet("FIRE")
(Sometimes it's not worth the Mana to Cast Seal).

 **Holy Light**
 35 Mana
This Heals for 39-48 with a 2.5 Second Cast. 40 Yard Range.
This has 33% Scaling with +Healing.
.25 Threat Modifier in PVE.
Recommend putting +55 Healing on a Mutton Chop and making an ItemRack load out called (HEALS) to equip it.

These are the following Macros I use:
The Importance of these Macros is to Swap from Fiery to Healing without the Global in Combat and Heal Instantly.

 **With Target:**
/script EquipSet("HEALS")
/cast Holy Light(Rank 1)

 **Targeting Self:**
/target character name
/script EquipSet("HEALS")
/cast Holy Light(Rank 1)

 **Target Lowest Health Target:**
/script EquipSet("HEALS")
/quickheal

 *As well as additional Macros for DPS Sets, Intellect Weapon, Spirit Weapon/Spirit Shield.*
