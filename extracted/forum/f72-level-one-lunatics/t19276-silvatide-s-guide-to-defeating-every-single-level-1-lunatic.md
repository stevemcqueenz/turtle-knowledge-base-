# silvatide’s guide to defeating every single level 1 lunatic 100% success rate (with images)

## Post 130878 by silvatide — 2025-05-13T18:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130878#p130878 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/Ght8jq1j/banner.png)

hello,

are you a poor level 1 fresh faced character in elwynn forest or durotar who just wants to play your favourite game world of warcraft without being CORPSE CAMPED by level 1 twinks eating your face?

well, have i got the guide for you: a sure-fire 100% tried and tested method of defeating every single level 1 lunatic you encounter. you don’t even need a level 60! this works from level 1!

hard to believe right? sound too good to be true? keep reading to find out how…

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/7L85w4JB/race.png)

this secret technique works for any race but it works best as a human in elwynn forest because there are more level 1 lunatics in elwynn, which means you can get more victories over them.

durotar is a close second.

other starting zones work but you won’t be able to gloat as much because the population of lunatics is smaller in those zones which means less opportunity to beat them.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/CMmnD991/class.png)

this technique has been developed for all classes.

absolutely any class will work. whatever your favourite class is, this technique works for that class.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/zGRysvvb/macros.png)

this macro is optional but i highly recommend it. keep this on your hotbar for whenever you defeat a level 1 lunatic.
Code: Select all

```
/dance
```

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/C5LZX4nS/basics.png)

okay here it is.

the technique begins before even level 1. when you are on the character selection screen there is a secret + button that opens a hidden menu of options.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/mkXcsF7M/challenges.png)

click on this secret button to reveal the secret menu of options.

one of these options is called “war mode”.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/xdvcdZK9/warmode.png)

 **DO NOT CLICK THIS BUTTON!**

by not clicking this button you are now invincible against all level 1 lunatics. they can’t hurt you. they can’t even hit you once or do 1 single hp health point of damage. you are free to play your favourite game world of warcraft and never be CORPSE CAMPED by dingbat chairhumpers again.

every time you see a level 1 lunatic running around you can stand on their head and spam your dance macro. they are powerless to do anything about it. they are defeated and humiliated. you have won.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/RFT6gYFg/advanced.png)

 **but what if you accidentally do click the button to turn on warmode?** you saw that it gave you 20% free experience and didn’t read that it would turn on pvp. what then?

this is a little more advanced technique but should still be achievable by any level 1 character. here is the secret: you can actually turn off warmode at the battlemaster in any major city!

just run on over to whatever your closest city is and speak to the battlemaster and say “i have fought well, but now i seek reprieve from this endless conflict. [disable warmode]”.

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/tCc1vWqG/disable.png)

this makes you invincible against all level 1 lunatics! you can dance and play your favourite game world of warcraft forever unscathed by fetid dingalings.

but what if you can’t get to a major city because a level 1 twink is CORPSE CAMPING you forever? don’t worry, there’s an advanced technique you can do to defeat the level 1 twink. just follow these steps:

 - log out your level 1 character with 5 minutes played
 - delete it
 - remake the character without warmode enabled

sometimes this is actually quicker than running to a city to speak to a battlemaster! go back over to the twink who corpse camped you and dance on their head. you win. now you can play your favourite game world of warcraft unobstructed by sweatbag goobers.

you win.

 **endtroduction**

this technique currently has a 100% success rate but i’ll keep it updated if any optimisations to the technique are discovered. thanks to all the thousands of players who have playtested this technique.
