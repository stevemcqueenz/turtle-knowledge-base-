**Arathi Basin Enable Mark Turn In Suggestion**

So we have Arathi Basin enabled at the Battlemaster and we have developed 2 separate Addons to enable it anywhere.

Thanks to Chronicle for working briefly on this one and figuring it out in 5 Minutes. This addon just forks and fixes the existing PVP Menu Button and Adds Arathi Basin to 1-19 and Thorn Gorge to 30+.
<https://github.com/Emyrk/TurtleWoWBGQueue>

Thanks to Nel for working on PVPWhen this addon has similar fixes however it automates the queuing process and saves your state so you're automatically queued upon log in.
<https://github.com/JardineWebSolutions/PVPWhen>

That said I suspect Turtle will probably roll out a fix for these soon probably utilizing Chronicle's fork.

Now lets get to the real issue, I suggest fixing the Arathi Basin mark turn in Quests and enable them for Level 1+. We have access to all Warsong Gulch mark turn in Quests and they reward the same 10-19 Crate with items that are of no use to us. I suggest the same for Arathi Basin for the simple fact that its Reputation and we need a way to get rid of these Marks.

The only difference is Arathi Basin Crates have one extremely low droprate item we can actually utilize which I see as a good thing. Moist Towelette is a 5% droprate from these Crates and enables us to dispel a negative debuff.
