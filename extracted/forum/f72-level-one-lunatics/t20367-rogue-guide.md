 **Choosing Your Race**

Picking the right race as a Level 1 Rogue Lunatic boils down to how much you want to min-max. Otherwise, just pick whatever you like.

Base Agility is the most important stat to consider, as it directly enhances both your survivability and damage output.

 *Agility grants the following bonuses:*
• Dodge Chance: ~0.91% per Agility
• Critical Strike Chance: ~0.455% per Agility
• Attack Power: +1 per Agility

![:sparkles:](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2728.svg) Final Tips

• Eviscerate is bait — Missing hits wastes energy, Sinister strike only costs 4 Energy if it doesn't hit.

• Fiery vs +15 Agility in PvP — If you cant consistently hit someone higher level, you're better off using +15 Agility

• Patience is key — Timbermaw rep and Arena Grindmaster suck.

• Positioning is Important — You're only melee immune from the front. You cant dodge while "casting" so no *"bubble hearths"* and be wary using range.

• Fiery in PvE — 100% Not worth it, Only procs off hits and isn't guaranteed you're better off with higher crit chance.

• Fiery Crits — Fiery can crit if you have 10% base spell crit from Rallying Cry of the Dragonslayer. DM Int cape, gives .06% Spell crit (meme).

I like all the cute little emotes. To answer your questions though:

Goblin Rocket Boots: What does "explode" mean? Does it just mean loss of all durability (repairable) or do you mean they actually disappear and you'd have to acquire new ones?

They actually disappear and leave behind so scrap parts and a pair of black mageweave boots. However, if you have extra pairs of goblin rocket boots in your inventory those will break first before your equip ones. so if your equip ones have movement speed enchanted on them and none of the boots in your inventory do then you've got a way to safeguard the enchanted boots, keep in mind that as long as you have the speed buff from the boots the extra pairs can still break. Basically as long as the buff speed buff is active every second you have a chance of your boots exploding, so in the worst possible case you could use 20 pairs of boots over the course of the 20 second speed boost.

Silver Piffeny Ring: The goblin in mention sells a variety of rings which are wearable by level 1s in the price range of 1-250 gold. Is it correct to assume that any of these rings are viable (even the ones costing 1 gold)?

Nope, only the ones that cost 10g or more are enchantable.

Simple Dagger: You said it youself that missed swings means reducred Fiery proc. Could it be that the Rusted Pirate Dagger (2-4 dmg, 1.6 atk spd) or Skinning Knife (2-5 dmg, 1.6 atk spd) is the better option for the fiery enchantment due to the increased attack speed? Has anyone compared the dps?

Simple Dagger and Keen Machete are essentially equal in dps, not accounting for weapon skill racials. The only better weapon then those 2 is the Blazing Pan from RFC. Rusted Pirae Dagger and Skinning Knife are both worse.

Hope this helps, if you ever have questions feel free to reach out via the lunatic discord.
