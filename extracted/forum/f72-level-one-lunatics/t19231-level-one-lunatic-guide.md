LEVEL ONE LUNATIC GUIDE (not updated for RFC or DM)

The Level One Lunatic Challenge
Simply put, this server challenge requires you to perform challenges at level 1 that would normally not be done until a much higher level. There are 4 sub-challenges, each reward a title that you can keep even if you level up. These are the Titles, and the requirements:
Wee Warlord - requires the player to get 10,000 honorable kills
Pint-Sized Politician - requires the player to reach exalted with any faction
Arena's Smallest Smasher - requires the player to obtain Arena Grand Master
Hero of Goldshire/Lazy Peon's Foreman - requires the player complete 100 quests

The Official Forum post for the challenge: [Level One Lunatic Info & Rankings](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=17653)

Starting The Challenge
In each of the starting zones there is a "Glyph Master", at the glyph master you'll purchase "Glyph of the Wee Warlord", you must be level 1 in order to take the challenge and doing so will turn off your ability to gain xp. I would recommend you not take any of the other challenges while doing this one.

Removing Chat Restrictions
Level 1 characters are unable to use /say, /yell, /world, and /whisper,  you can still invite people to party and speak with them there. This restriction can be removed by completing 30 quests and spending 10 hours in game. Doing the quests in all of your faction's starting zones will get you more than the 30 quests required. Joining a guild will also allow you to speak in guild chat.

Lunatic Guilds
There's a handful of guilds that will assist Lunatics in their goals:
<Level One Lunatic> is specifically designed for helping Lunatics. Their guild teleport is in booty Bay for easy access to Gurubashi Arena.
<Macrochelys> is helpful towards Lunatics and offers a high quality raiding scene should you choose to level up after finishing the challenge. Their guild teleport is to Azshara and comes with a gryphon to ride to Durotar.

There's also a few guilds with guild bases that don't tolerate Lunatics, so you'll have to get creative to get into them:
<The Scourge> - Tirisfal Glades
<The Scarlett Crusade> - Tirisfal Glades
<The Blackwing Guard> - Elwynn Forest (via Burning Steppes)
<The Lion's Pride Legion> - Elwynn Forest (via Duskwood)

The Setup
Mods used:
 1. SuperWow
This mod is required to use ShaguScan, you'll also use it to launch wow.
 2. VanillaTweaks
You'll use this mod to set your name plate view distance to 99.
 3. ShaguScan
This mod allows you to make a list of available pvp targets near you. The scan parameters I use are(it should be copy-pastable for the mod filter line):
player,alliance,pvp,alive,attack
This makes it so that all living flagged alliance show up on a list, you'll change alliance to horde if you're playing an alliance. This should make hunting players less visually intensive.
 4. ShaguPlates
Use this to customize nameplates so that health bars only show up for the unit you're targetting, and make the targetted hp bar easier to see. the options you're looking for are "Always Show On Target Units" and "Hide Healthbar On enemynpc/enemyplayer/neutralnpc" This way when you have nameplates on and you target a player on your ShaguScan list you're able to scan visually for them MUCH easier thanks to the VanillaTweaks nameplate distance increase.

Character:
Class:

Rogue - This is the class to pick for this challenge, they fit the bill in every way. Sinister Strike and Eviscerate provide ample damage on their own, plus they proc fiery. They scale VERY well on agility, even reaching above 100% dodge. The cherry on top is they have access to stealth at level 1.

Paladin – Can heal and tank well. Gear should be a mix of healing power, stamina, and armor. Weapon should be a Half-eaten Mutton Chop with fiery and +55 healing.
[Cysthen's Paladin Guide](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=17957)

Warrior – Does good damage and can tank well. Gear should be enchanted with a lot of stamina and some strength. Weapons should have fiery, crusader, and lifesteal. Troll warriors can be scaled heavily with spirit to produce very high in combat regen.

Hunter – Does good damage, expensive to use because of twink ammo. Gear should be primarily agility, with some stamina. Weapons should have +25agi and fiery.

Shaman – Does good damage and some light tanking. Gear should be mostly stamina with some strength. Weapon should be a Half-eaten Mutton Chop with fiery.

Mage, Priest, Warlock, Druid – Able to do the challenge but are working entirely off stats instead of kit. Gear is entirely stamina. Weapon should be a Half-eaten Mutton Chop with fiery.

The tier order I believe I'd put the classes in is as follows: Rogue > Paladin > Warrior = Hunter > Shaman > Mage > Priest = Warlock = Druid

Race:
Goblin - Gives you +3 to dagger skill, and a 40% for 5s sprint on a 3min cd.
Night Elf - Gives you 1% increase to atk spd, casting spd, move spd, and dodge chance. You move 50% faster while dead, which is often. And you get access to shadowmeld which is an unmoving stealth on a 1min cd then doesn't break for 2 seconds after moving, or better stealth level if rogue. This race makes playing Warrior, Hunter, Priest, or Druid more viable then their non-NE counterparts.
Gnome - Gets 10 arcane resist which helps vs polymorph, and Escape Artist which removes and immobilization or movement speed reducing effect, on a 1.5min cd

Enchant - +4 All Stats (Enchanting 300)

Enchant - +7 Agi (Enchanting 300)

[Dirty Leather Gloves](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=714) (Buy from starting zone leather vendor) - 21 armor
Enchants - +15 Agi (Enchanting 300)

 8. Waist
[Squealer's Belt](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4951) (Drops from "Squealer" Thornmantle) - 23 armor
Enchant - +6 Agi (190 Blacksmithing)
"Squealer" Thornmantle can be found in a cave located near the quillboard chieftan's hut in Brambleblade Ravine located in southern Mulgore, within the starting zone

 9. Legs
[Dirty Leather Pants](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=209) (Buy from starting zone leather vendor) - 29 armor
Enchant - +8 Agi (Libram of Voracity, requires a lvl 50 to finish quest and apply the enchant)

 10. Boots
[Goblin Rocket Boots](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=7189) (made by engineer) - 41 armor, Use Effect: 70% increased move speed for 20 seconds, chance to break on use.
Enchant - Minor Speed (Enchanting 225)

 11. Rings
[Silver Piffeny Ring](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=7342) (buy from Qixdi Goodstitch for 10g in Booty Bay)
Enchant - +6 Agi (Gemology Jewelcrafting 225)
You're going to want two of these rings

 12. Trinkets
[Insignia of the Horde](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18849)/Alliance (Purchase from pvp vendor in capital after reaching Honor rank 2) - Use Effect: Dispels all types of crowd control effects
[Arena Grand Master](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19024) (Obtained from Gurubashi Arena chest quest) - 1% dodge, Use Effect: Absorbs 750 to 1250 damage and lasts 20 seconds

 13. Weapons
[Simple Dagger](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4565) (Drops from any starter zone mobs) - 3-6 dmg, 1.8 atk spd
Enchant - +15 Agi (Enchanting 300) And/Or Fiery (Enchanting 265)

[Furbolg Medicine Pouch](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=16768) (Buy from Timbermaw Hold after reaching Honored) - +10 Stam, Use Effect: Restores 100 health every second for 10 seconds on a 20minute CD

[Cadet's Bow](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=8179) (Drops from any starter zone mobs) - 3-6 dmg, 2.0 atk spd
Scope - +7 dmg (Engineering 240)
[Feathered Arrow](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3464) (Quest reward from "Stolen Supplies" a Gelkis quest in Desolace) - 9.5 dmg

 14. Consumables
[Heavy Runecloth Bandage](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=14530) (Requires 290 First Aid to make)  - Use Effect: Heals 2000 damage over 8 seconds (Requires 225 First Aid to use)
[Minor Healing Potions](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=118) - Use Effect: Restores 70 to 90 health
[Discombobulator Ray](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=4388) (Engineering 160 to make) - Use Effect: Transforms the target into a leper gnome, reducing its melee and spell damage by 40 and it's movement rate by 20% for 12 seconds on a 1minute cd
[Rumsey Rum Light/Dark/Black Label](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=Rumsey+Rum) - Use Effect: Increases staming by 5/10/15 for 15minutes
