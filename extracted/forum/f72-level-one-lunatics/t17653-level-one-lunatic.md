****What is the Level One Lunatic challenge?****

During this challenge, you'll push the limits of a level **one** character and earn exclusive titles to prove just how far a noob can go:

- ****The Wee Warlord**** — earn **10,000 honorable kills** to show you're more than just fresh meat.

- ****Pint-Sized Politician**** — reach **Exalted** with at least one faction to prove that even a tiny hero can have powerful friends.

- ****Arena’s Smallest Smasher**** — obtain the **Arena Master Trinket** to demonstrate that, even at level one, you’re an unstoppable force of nature.

- ****Hero of Goldshire**** (Alliance) / ****Lazy Peon's Foreman**** (Horde) — complete **100 quests** available to you, earning the respect of tutorial NPCs everywhere.

![level_one_lunatic-CXjIpv52.png](./download/file.php?id=125) <dd>level_one_lunatic-CXjIpv52.png (761.2 KiB) Viewed 96746 times</dd>

 ****How to begin the challenge?****

Simply speak to any Glyph Scribe at any starting location to sign up!

---
 ****How do Lunatics communicate?****

By default, new characters on Turtle WoW cannot speak until they reach level 5 to prevent spam bots. However, Lunatics who complete **30 quests** at level 1 and spend **at least 10 hours in-game** will unlock chat privileges! You can also join the ****/lunatic**** channel in-game. This is a community chat.

---
 ****Are battlegrounds & arenas available at level 1?****

Yes! The ****Warsong Gulch 1-9 bracket**** and ****Blood Ring**** are available to all levels.

Since **Turtle WoW** was the first realm to introduce this challenge, we're celebrating with a special **wallpaper** made by our talented artist **Lionell Schramm**!

If you think about the actuality of wpvp on Nord, the lunatic challenge is absolutely a net positive.

Would you rather lvl 60s ganking sub lvl 20s or lvl 1 twinks ganking sub lvl 20s? The answer is easy and obvious, the lvl 1s. The lvl 1 gankers provide something lvl 60 gankers don't , a sense of hope and possibility TM. The amount of options players have for dealing with a lvl 1 twink is drastically higher than for a lvl 60.

It would take an army of lvl 1s to kill a lvl 1 twink without coordination but if 3 lvl 1 mages knew how to pvp they could absolutely lock a lvl 1 down to death with just frost bolts and kiting. You can really mix that combo up however you like and even just have a few decently coordinated lvl 5 randos and they too can kill the lvl 1. What this challenge does is highlight the power of teamwork in pvp, and it does it in a way that provides clear boundries and options. If a lvl 1 can't beat the twink, then they ask someone for help, be it another lvl 1 or even a lvl 60. If that lvl 60 shows up, the twink is toast (or has to play cat and mouse, either way that twink ain't killing the 60), if a second lvl 1 shows up, those two stand a much higher chance of winning if they work together. If those two lvl 1s fail then they can call in another, that process repeats until they win. When that group of lvl 1s finally kills that twink they feel like they've taken down a raid boss and they haven't even left the starting zone, or the group is thankful to a lvl 60 for the protection, either way social interactions have been had and nothing unites players like having a shared enemy. It's also worth noting that if at any point they want to give up and not pvp they can, pvp is entirely opt in.
