Welcome to <Level One Lunatic> on Nordanaar
A Turtle WoW PvP Guild unlike any other!

We embrace the chaos of playing at Level 1 and the thrill of ganking players while we push ourselves to achieve some of the most challenging and unique goals in the game.

Whether it’s reaching Exalted with reputations, completing 100 quests, obtaining the Arena Grand Master, or pushing to an impressive 10,000 kills, we take pride in pushing the boundaries of what’s possible for Level 1 characters.

We are fully dedicated to growing this community. We provide a Minor Speed Enchant for Boots to new members and hope to revive this bracket, encouraging more active participation in Battlegrounds.

Our guild is built on the spirit of fun and while we dive into these challenging feats, we are committed to fostering a friendly, respectful environment where every guild member feels supported. Harassment and toxicity have no place here.

As we tackle these insane challenges, we do it with a sense of humor and an understanding that the journey is just as important as the destination.

Being a Level One Lunatic is all about teamwork and helping each other rise to the occasion. Whether you're working toward any of these challenges or you need support in a tough fight, you’ll find a community here that’s always ready to lend a hand.

The beauty of our guild is that, despite our unusual starting point, we are all in this together. Your success is our success, and we’re always eager to help each other along the way.

If you're looking for a guild where you can share in the thrill of ganking and grinding, taking on ambitious goals, and making lasting memories with a group of like-minded lunatics, then you've come to the right place.

Take your experience on Turtle WoW to a whole new level ~ literally.

Reach out to Stabsmckenzy or Cysthen for a guild invite!
