This patch replaces your Bear form and Dire bear to a furbolg form. Brown for taurens black for Night elves.

This patch replaces your cat form to a Tigon form (tiger boss in ZG). Both taurens and Night elves have the same appearance.

Why furbolg and Tigon? Abilities like Maul, Claw and Shred have unique animations (kinda like thunderclap animation). The only races i found with and animation for Maul are bears and furbolgs, while claw and shred only cats and TIgons have animations. Otherwise they stand still when using an ability and it's super akward.
As a bonus, you will be able to see the weapons while in feral forms, so you can quickly tell if you're equipping MCP for example

Since i don't have access to the emerald reskins they may not show properly with those patches. If they don't please report them so i can look into it.
