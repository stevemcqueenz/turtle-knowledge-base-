## Post 71853 by Vidooo — 2023-10-18T12:21:00Z

**Fog Pushback**
 18 Oct 2023

Pushes back Vanilla's view distance fog, drastically increasing view distance. Use /console farclip at your own risk, vanilla does not render extremely high farclip distances with this mod too well.

Example using farclip 1000:
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/718958512126034031/1164172654689919067/image.png?ex=65423f46&is=652fca46&hm=75901c2255921b9d6041e45c7f8dbac6515b30e7e79936c35dfb8254130dbc6e&)

 **Known bugs/errors**
 - Holes appearing in the ground and terrain popping into view at extremely high farclip distances. Client limitation, unfixable

 **How to install?**
Drag Patch-Y to the Data folder in your Turtle WoW installation folder. Rename if necessary
