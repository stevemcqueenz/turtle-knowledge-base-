Well... what if I told you I know how? :)

Working over the past few days to improve the basic Vanilla water, this mod aims to be a texture (not shader) replacement that brings the flat texture a little closer to how retail is. Without reflections/refractions it doesn't look exact but I hope some people will still find it to their taste.

There are 2 versions available as an option to use, one includes only the textures while the other includes the textures and dbc files for cata water color + water visibility. Both are compatible with HD mod and Darker Nights and even just as standalone.
