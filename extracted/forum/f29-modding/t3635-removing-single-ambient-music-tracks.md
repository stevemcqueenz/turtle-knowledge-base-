## Post 21977 by Citizenkane — 2022-08-11T14:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=21977#p21977 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I know of 1 way to disable the music. If you wish to replace the music, I'm sure you can adapt the steps to suit your needs. First you will need an MPQ editor. Next, navigate to the folder below.

Your WoW folder > Data > Patch-U.mpq > sound > music > cataclysm

You will find 10 mp3's within. All of them are the goblin theme, so feel free to delete all of them.

As a side note, I tried another method which involves creating a dummy folder path with silent mp3's within. This is a common solution employed by those who want to disable mechanostreider sounds, as it does not involve downloading an MPQ editor. Unfortunately, this method does not work with the goblin theme.
