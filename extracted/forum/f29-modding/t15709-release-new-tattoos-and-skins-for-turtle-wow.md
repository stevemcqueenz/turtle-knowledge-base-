Hi, I've been playing with a mod for a while now that I created little by little to improve things that I thought were a bit simple for Turtle WoW, now I decided to share it for whoever wants to have these little visual improvements in the game.

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1wTLxgGdo-OIrCRV7fwLEa0BeZuejux0U/preview]

 -Texture of the Thalassian Unicorn from MTL Media, credits to him.
 -Improves the race selection buttons for the High Elves (The ones that were there looked horrible to me compared to the other races).
 -Uses the Turtle WoW HD Character Creation and Selection backgrounds (I omitted the Undead one because of the visual bug it has).
 -Adds some skin modifications for High Elves and Orcs, giving them some tattoos.
