 **CLASSIIC HD Patch: Updated M2 (Items) and WMO (Buildings)**

First of all, a Merry Christmas to all of you and a good start to the New Year!

This year I was able to learn a lot, how to create custom armor with Blender, how to implement it in the game and, most recently, how to create buildings. If anyone is interested in learning the same, feel free to write to me! (Discord: appletrey1337)

With great anticipation for Turtle WoW 2.0, I would now like to use my collected knowledge to fulfill the dream of a completely overhauled Classic World for the HD community.

With this patch, every single item and every building will be reworked over the next few months and years!

For this purpose, I will develop my own custom models, as well as publish models from friends from the community. Remakes from retail will also find their place. Here you can find all the models I'm working on right now, all finished models will be listed in the upcoming posts and will always be kept up to date.

 **IMPORTANT! This patch only contains the armor and buildings, if you want to play with HD characters and mounts, you have to download the Turtle HD patch as well! The New Patch Bundle by **Stormhand81** includes my files so thats the easiest way   satisfied_turtle_head  [viewtopic.php?t=21355](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=21355)**
