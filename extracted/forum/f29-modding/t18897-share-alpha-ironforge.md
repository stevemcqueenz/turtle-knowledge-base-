## Post 128594 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-04-26T14:09:00Z

I recently came across a curious project that had backported the Ironforge Alpha and introduced it to their world. I decided to simply throw it into a MPQ and turn it into a fun mod for this place. Enjoy exploring.

COPYRIGHT:

The backport was made by Wallcraft.

WARNING
I am not responsible for any bugs, glitches, wall-crashing, calls to action, or if your character falls into the Twisting Nether and never regains their soul.

Notes:
 1. Only the model changes; NPCs, items, and other server-dependent items will remain as if it were the normal Ironforge. You'll encounter a lot of visual bugs.
 2. The flight path will also be buggy, since the holes it passes through don't exist for this Ironforge.
 3. I don't know if it's bannable. You won't be doing anything wrong, but some players will see you flying or going through walls because YOU are playing on a different Ironforge model.

RECOMMENDATION:

 -Disable PvP to use this mod, just in case. That way, at least you won't be cheating.

I intercede for those who use this mod: GMs, Devs, if you see people going through walls, they are probably using this mod, it's not a hack or anything.
