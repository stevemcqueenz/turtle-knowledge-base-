## Post 150490 by Mosttrustworthy — 2025-09-06T11:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150490#p150490 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Is it possible? Yes.
What sound would you need to replace with another sound/silence? Sound/Creature/Duck/DuckLoop.wav. It's in Patch-8.MPQ in the Data folder of your Turtle WoW directory.
If the sound you use has the wrong bitrate, no sound will play at all, which effectively mutes the duck.

How does modding work? If you head over to [the modding subforum](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewforum.php?f=29) I'm sure you'll find what you need.
