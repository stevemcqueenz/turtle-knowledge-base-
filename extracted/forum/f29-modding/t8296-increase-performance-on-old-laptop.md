## Post 56652 by Bowser (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-08-10T08:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56652#p56652 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First and foremost, it's important to avoid using both Vanillatweaks and Vanillafixes together if you encounter crashes. Additionally, ensure that your graphics drivers are up-to-date and avoid using any boosting software such as "Driver Booster."

If you have an older graphics card like the Intel HD3000, it's advisable to avoid using the DXVK version of Vanillafixes. Doing so might result in worse performance or even prevent the game from starting. If the game does start with DXVK, there is a high chance of experiencing crashes on graphics cards manufactured before 2016.

The HD3000 iGPU in your i7-2640M was built in 2011 and is considered a relatively slow integrated GPU. Even a GeForce 730 from 2014 (with a market value of around $20) is significantly faster and can run Vanilla WoW at much higher FPS.

It's perfectly normal to achieve a frame rate of 30-90 FPS with an HD3000 integrated GPU.
