Sadly this is something I've been looking into a lot because I agree with you, dynamic skyboxes i.e ones that change with time of day are not possible. That wasn't added til TBC. We have limited skybox space too... and when I say limited, I mean we have only one-two slots. However, regular skyboxes do work still, it's just real immersion breaking when it's night time with a day time skybox. The best thing I imagine someone could do is create a patch freezes the game's lighting, for example making it so it is always daytime, and then adding a skybox for one type of zone and then an extra skybox for another type.
