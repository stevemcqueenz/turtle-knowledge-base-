From *Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne game manual*:
> "Believed to be the ill omen of a coming age, white tauren are held in near reverence by their people, often becoming reclusive priests who wander the land in search of kindred spirits."

From *World of Warcraft The Roleplaying Game: Magic & Mayhem*:
> "Some tribes consider a tauren born with a white pelt to be destined to become a spirit walker."

I made a patch to make available a Tauren skin based on the W3 unit "Spirit Walker". It replaces the default white skin by a whiter skin imported from Wotlk.

The skin comes with 3 different faces only (wotlk special dk skin restriction...). In a near future, I will add face tattoos to stick more to the W3 skin, but for now I'm kinda lazy.

The patch is merged with the one made by Mr. Kazgrim: [viewtopic.php?f=29&t=994](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=29&t=994)
