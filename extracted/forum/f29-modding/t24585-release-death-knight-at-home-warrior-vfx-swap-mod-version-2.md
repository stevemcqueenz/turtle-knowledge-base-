Known Issues:

> Mobs that use the same warrior skills may or may not be affected

> Often times your hands get stuck with the casting effect of a frost spell and i have no clue why this happens, can be fixed by casting something frost related like defensive stance, pummel or hamstring.

 -Rundown of changes-

> Heroic Strike -> Execute [This is your Blood Strike]

> Rend -> Ghoul Rot [Shadow Cast that adds the common plagued visual effect on the target, i wanted this one to be Plague Strike but couldn't find a suitable spell, so Plague... Touch?]

> Hamstring -> Chains of Ice's on-hit effect

> Slam -> Shadow Shock [Shadow Spell cast animation, just a simple black, purple, green shadowy blast]

> Sunder Armor -> Insect Swarm [I struggled to find something cool for this, i initially wanted to use a frost spell because very cold temperatures can make metal brittle, and also to fit in Icy Touch, but i just couldn't find something fitting, so i choose Insect Swarm and just think the bugs are spitting corrosive acid on the target]

> Mortal Strike -> Wing Clip [This is your Scourge Strike]

> Bloodthirst -> Ghostly Strike [This is your Obliterate]

> Execute -> Lacerate [This is your roided-up Death Strike that doesn't heal you]

> Bloodrage -> Shadow of Death + Shadowmend [This is your Empower Rune Weapon]

> Berserker Rage -> Power Overwhelming [Idk if i like this one or not, though somewhat inevitable due to shortage of cool looking and fitting skills, if possible, i prefer not using that many spells from other classes, subject to change]

> Death Wish -> Violent Shield Effect [This is casted by melee mobs around Dalaran, this is basicaly your Unholy Frenzy]

> Thunderclap -> Blood Boil [Yes! it exists]

> Whirlwind -> Frost Breath [While i would've preferred something more fitting of whirlwind's AoE, i was running out of ideas]

> Pummel -> Frost Shock [This is your Mind Freeze]

> Shoot Bow/Gun/Crossbow/Throw -> Death Coil [It's Shadowbolt, there is no real Death Coil, they all just look like Shadowbolt]

> Overpower -> Death Coil [Doesn't shoot a projectile of course, but i felt its proc mechanic fit the Death Coil's occasional proc playstile]

> Revenge -> Sunder Armor [Hey it was purple, i had to put it somewhere else]

> Retaliation -> Bone Shield [I'm waiting for the database to go back up so i can check what the strikes caused by ret are called, while developing this i had changed them to Death Coil because i liked the idea that all the bones whirling around you would explode on a mob with shadowy magic when they hit you]

> Shield Wall -> Anti-Magic Shield [Anti-Magic Shield]

> Recklessness -> Dark Harvest [This is your Unholy Frenzy but cooler]

> Enrage -> Shadow Trance [From the Nightfall talent proc]

> Deep Wounds -> Sapphiron's Life Drain [Makes Deep Wounds all the more satisfying]

> Battle Stance -> Marked For Death  [Blood Presence]

> Defensive Stance -> Frost Armor [Frost Presence]

> Berserker Stance -> Dark Sludge [Unholy Presence]

 -   FAQ   -

> Can you change [Skill] into [Skill you want instead]?
 - [viewtopic.php?t=18924](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=18924)

> Can you change the spell icons and names as well so my bar is more immersive?
 - No, nope, don't. Please don't do that, it's not just a visual change, it'll change the icon's ID and ofc  the spell name, both are used by many, many macros and addons, they'll break.

Just use macros with fitting icons of your choosing.

> Does this affect gameplay?
 - Nope, it's purely cosmetic.

> Will other people be able to see the mod's changes?
 - Nope, completely client side.

> Will it only affect me?
 - Nope, it'll change every warrior, OOPS, all Death Knights!

> Is there any way to make it only affect me?
 - Idk, out of my field.

> Can you port the DK's spells from Wrath and use those instead?
 - I bloody wish i knew how m8.

> Will you abandon this mod if you stop playing like you did with your Void Paladin mod?
 - Probably, it's why i made the guide.
