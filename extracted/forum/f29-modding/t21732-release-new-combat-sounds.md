# [Release] New Combat Sounds

## Post 150552 by Dragenfell — 2025-09-06T17:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150552#p150552 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone.   smiling_turtle_head
I put together a small patch that replaces the sounds for melee weapons, as well as shots. The sounds of spells and casting were changed. There was also a replacement for the sounds of crits, damage to the player in some cases, as well as death. All game races were replaced, NPCs will also make these sounds.
The sounds of weapons were taken from some HD patch, since there are quite a few on this forum.
The sounds of crits and screams/groans are taken from an old mod for TES IV Oblivion, called Deadly Reflex 6.  dead_turtle_head
