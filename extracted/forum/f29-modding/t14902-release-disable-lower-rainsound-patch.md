# [Release] Disable / Lower Rainsound Patch

## Post 101453 by Sheed90 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-09-18T12:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101453#p101453 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings Turtle Community!   smiling_turtle

I like the ambience and the weather on Turtle WoW. But it rains very often in my opinion. The fact that it's raining doesn't bother me directly but the sound of the rain is incredibly loud and is extremely annoying when I want to listen to music or a external video.

I have therefore developed a small patch that I would like to share with you today.

Version 1:
Rain without sound

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1Qovmur5tN7xDTveGJR4bi1eqm5ZZI245/preview]

Version 2:
Rain with sound, but the volume of the rain is reduced by 20db.

[embed: //drive.google.com/file/d/1oYIW_bCDc5A6Dxcp3TA0X93NMyfukYgL/preview]

Installation:
Just drop the patch-r.mpq into your data folder.

 **Please note!**
The loadpriority of the patches depends on the letter. In other words: the higher the letter, the higher the priority of the patch. This means that all patches above R have a higher priority and therefore the patch-r could not take effect if another patch changes the rain sounds.
