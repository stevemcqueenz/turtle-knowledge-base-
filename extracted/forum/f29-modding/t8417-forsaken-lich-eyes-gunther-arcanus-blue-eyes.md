# Forsaken Lich Eyes (Gunther Arcanus blue eyes)

## Post 54252 by Witchblade — 2023-07-21T17:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54252#p54252 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello guys so this patch is going to allow you to replace the original yellow forsaken eyes with a light blue version, many of you will be familiar with this blue eyes variant if you have quested through tirisfal glades multiple times as an undead, for people that dont know turtle wow has remodelled Gunther Arcanus since he is considered a lich and a "big deal". Downloading and extracting this patch into your Data folder will allow you to replace your eye colour with his.
