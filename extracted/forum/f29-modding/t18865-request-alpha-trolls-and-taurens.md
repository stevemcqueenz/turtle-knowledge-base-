## Post 128375 by Pitokao — 2025-04-24T22:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128375#p128375 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Good evening to all members of the Turtle WoW community.

First of all, I’d like to say that I love what the Turtle WoW team is doing regarding the development of the server and how the lore stays true to Warcraft 3 and the original World of Warcraft.

I’d like to bring a suggestion to the community. I know we already have mods that update the graphics with the Turtle HD Patch, but I personally enjoy the game’s original graphics. However, there’s one thing that bothers me: the female troll and tauren models. I know that in the Alpha version of the game there were models with animations that, in my opinion, were much more faithful representations of the women of those races.

As for the males, I think the trolls and taurens had a much more interesting idle posture back then. I would love to be able to bring just these small aspects into my game through mods.

If it's possible to replace classic models with the ones from the Legion expansion and change animations, I believe it's also possible to replace the idle animation of male trolls and taurens, and fully swap out the models and animations of female trolls and taurens as well.

I hope others feel the same way, and I’m open to any tips if I decide to try creating this mod myself. Thank you in advance!
