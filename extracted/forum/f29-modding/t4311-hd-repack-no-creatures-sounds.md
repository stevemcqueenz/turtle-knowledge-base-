## Post 25663 by Leathertank — 2022-09-21T11:53:00Z

I was using redmagejoe's HD Pack [viewtopic.php?f=29&t=3691](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=29&t=3691)

and I loved some parts of it,  butI am not a fan of the new creature models. I made this for myself but thought others might enjoy it. The world, textures, and spells are much better looking, I think it accentuates the older models and makes them look better.  This is almost entirely redmagejoe's work, I just removed the sounds and creature models. I have also applied a chinese UD skin, because I main forsaken, and don't like bones.  To use drop the patch-3.mpq into your twow data folder and start the game. To uninstall, just delete the mpq file.
