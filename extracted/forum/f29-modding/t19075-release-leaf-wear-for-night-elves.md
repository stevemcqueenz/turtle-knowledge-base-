# [Release] Leaf Wear for Night Elves

## Post 129675 by Cellion — 2025-05-04T23:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129675#p129675 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

A small mod for female night elves. This replaces the normal underwear with new leafy versions, based on the vanilla dryad appearance. If you've ever wanted your druid to be even more in touch with nature, here is your opportunity. This made came out of a personal project to learn more about modding the 1.12 client, as well as a desire to have something more thematic for my gear-less hardcore druid to be clad in. The end result turned out pretty OK, so I decided to make some variants for other skin colors and make it available to the community.

 **See the spoiler for a preview picture:** ![Image](https://i.imgur.com/lHJ5mzy.png)

 ****What's In Here****
 - Replacements for chest and legs empty slot textures for female night elves on all non-store skins.
 - Appearance is based on the vanilla dryad, with a top piece that is based on their torso texture (with edits for better compatibility with gear and better-ish mapping across the HD night elf model) and a fully custom bottom piece based on dryad assets.
 - Color variation for darker skin colors.
 - *Note*: The Mark of Nature store skins have a similar aesthetic to this mod, especially the top, because both this and the store skin are based on the dryad appearance. No assets from the store skins were used to create this (just vanilla assets plus painting the texture manually). However, if the devs have any concerns with too much overlap with their existing skins, I'm happy to take this down.

 ****Things You Should Know****
 - These replacements were designed for the HD night elf model (Patch A), so that's what I recommend to use. That said, you can still use this with the vanilla model, it just won't look as good.
 - Load this mod after Patch A and any other mod that adjusts female night elf skin textures. I have it defaulted to patch-z, but anything after A will do.

You can find more pictures in the following [Imgur Album](https://imgur.com/a/u4Mqcis).

I extracted the BLP files from Cellion's MPQ and removed the skin from the background using a BLP to PNG converter and microsoft paint. Then in GIMP, I colored the background to transparent and pasted the resulting images onto the upper and lower torso for female high elves and humans. I converted these PNGs back into BLP files and put them into Cellion's MPQ. Based on a brief glance it appears that these work seamlessly and the process is very easy to replicate for any race (or gender) assuming that you have the source texture for the race without default underwear.

The invisible background with upper and lower leaf texture are included as PNG files. I also uploaded the MPQ with the modified version of Cellion's MPQ and the BLP/PNG converter for anyone who might want to generate additional textures for the other races. If I get bored or have the time I might add additional races or anyone is welcome to take the files and make their own.
