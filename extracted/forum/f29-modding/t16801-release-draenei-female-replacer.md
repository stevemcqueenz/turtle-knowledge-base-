# [Release] Draenei Female Replacer

## Post 114811 by Razar52 — 2024-12-30T02:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114811#p114811 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Draenei Female Replacer**

A Patch to turn **Human Female** player characters into **Draenei Female** characters.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/yUPXuxz.png)

 **Download**
[patch-p.mpq](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xe8IUfSu_7v-31iPivD5A0kIsuy1UVGZ/view?usp=sharing)

 **Install:** Drag the patch-p.mpq to your Data folder

I found an old patch that turned undead into draenei female and I converted it to be Human F -> Draenei F instead. I then ported it to TWoW and fixed it so that changing the Piercings changes the horns.

I then went and downloaded all of the Female Draenei sounds I could find from Wowhead, converted them from ogg to wav, and replaced the Human Female sounds.

 **Known Issues:**
- Human males won't show armor on feet (since I turned them off so that the Female hooves are not covered)
- NPC Human Hair/Helmets could be odd looking in some cases
- Any custom headgear models added by TWoW may not fit properly, but should be visible.
- Some Hairstyles might have a small seam at the back of the head (no idea why)
- Some headgear causes the horns to become hidden (if anyone knows a setting to fix this please let me know)
Fixlog:
- 29.Dec.24 -> Fixed one of the Piercings having blank horns
- 23.Jan.25 -> Refactored the patch. Human Mail headgear should no longer be affected. Custom TWoW headgear should now be visible, though they may not fit quite right.

 *Note: I am a complete noob when it comes to WoW modding, so I would not be surprised if something is goofed up somewhere.
