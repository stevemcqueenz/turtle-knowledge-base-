**ModernSpellBook - Retail-inspired Spellbook for Turtle WoW**

Tired of the vanilla spellbook? This addon replaces it with a modern, two-page layout inspired by retail WoW - while keeping the classic parchment feel.

 **Download:** [GitHub](https://github.com/vegeta1k95/ModernSpellBook)

 **Features**

- **Two-page book layout** - beautiful parchment pages with a bookmark divider
- **Tabbed navigation** - Class, General, Pet, Companions, Mounts, Toys - all in one place
- **Professions tab** - automatically separated from General spells
- **Search bar** - quickly find any spell by name or keyword
- **Round passive icons** - passives are visually distinct with round portraits and a blue ring
- **Unlearned spells** - see what spells you haven't learned yet (greyed out with level requirements)
- **Talent-granted spells** - shows which spells come from talents vs trainers
- **"Train" badge** - green badge highlights spells you can learn right now at your level
- **"New" badge** - yellow badge marks freshly learned spells
- **Cooldowns** - cooldown sweep displayed on spell icons
- **Active aura tracking** - active stances/auras glow in real-time
- **Spell tooltips** - full descriptions even for unlearned spells (captured from trainer)
- **Settings menu** - font size, text color (light/dark), highlight toggles, icon frame options

 **How Unlearned Spells Work**

The spellbook can show spells you haven't learned yet - greyed out with their level requirement. To populate this list:

1. Visit a class trainer in a major city (starting zone trainers have limited spells)
2. The addon automatically captures all available spells on your first visit
3. Open your spellbook - unlearned spells appear at the end of each spec section
4. A green **"Train"** badge appears on spells you can learn at your current level
5. Hovering over a badge dismisses it
Talent-granted spells are also detected automatically - no trainer visit needed for those.

 **Settings**

Click the gear icon next to the search bar to access:

- **Show unlearned** - toggle unlearned spell visibility
- **Highlights** - control glow and badge for learned/available spells independently
- **Font size** - choose from 9 to 13
- **Spell text color** - Light (gold text with shadows) or Dark (black text)
- **Spell icon frame** - toggle fancy borders per spell category
 **Installation**

1. Download from [GitHub](https://github.com/vegeta1k95/ModernSpellBook)
2. Extract into *Interface/AddOns/ModernSpellBook*
3. Restart the game or */reload*
 **Known Info**

- Ported from the Classic Season of Discovery addon of the same name
- Should also work on other 1.12.1 vanilla private servers
- No dependencies required
Feedback and bug reports welcome!
