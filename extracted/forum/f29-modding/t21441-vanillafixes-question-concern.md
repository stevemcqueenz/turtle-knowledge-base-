As far as I am aware, it is only the custom launcher that is included with vanillafixes which gets flagged by antivirus; which you don't even need to use - in fact, the twow launcher is much better anyway.

I definitely had this download flagged back in the day downloading it pre-twow launcher even though I always just discarded the launcher and never used it.  Never had similar issues since the option to install through the launcher was added.  I just double-checked and I don't have anything allowed in Windows Defender making it work.

I don't know what FACEIT is but if it is kernel-level anti-cheat then I completely understand the caution.  I had a pretty bad experience in the past with invasive anti-cheat in a different game.

From a system-wide anti-cheat perspective, I don't think it would care about this, since it is just an extra DLL that WoW is loading.  A lot of different programs load a ton of different DLLs, [usually from a system folder or the program's directory](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/dlls/dynamic-link-library-search-order#standard-search-order-for-unpackaged-apps), to achieve whatever functionality they're trying to accomplish.

So, in my opinion, it is probably fine.  But, as always, use your discretion.
