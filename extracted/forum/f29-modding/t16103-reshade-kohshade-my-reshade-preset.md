# [ReShade] KohShade - My ReShade Preset

## Post 109606 by Thekohtas — 2024-11-24T06:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109606#p109606 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**KohShade ReShade for Turtle WoW**

Previews: <https://imgur.com/gallery/reshade-kohshade-5PDREaa>

ReShade OFF is on the left and ReShade ON is on the right.
If you don't see a difference then look for a heavier ReShade.

How To Use
- Download ReShade from <https://reshade.me>

- Run **ReShade.exe** and direct it to your **WoW.exe** if you can't find it in the list then **Browse** for it

- Select **DirectX 9**

- While installing select **ALL SHADERS** by deselecting **ALL** and reselecting **ALL**

- Follow any prompts left to finish installing ReShade.

- Download **KohShade.ini** here [https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QvDl3b ... drive_link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QvDl3b3XL2W9975yuGE40WBnLIxxa9Q2/view?usp=drive_link)

- Place **KohShade.ini** into your **WoW folder**

- Launch the game and **ALLOW IT TO LOAD SHADERS**

- Once loaded press **Home** on your keyboard

- You will be prompted to take a **tutorial for ReShade**, this is **optional** and not needed but you may follow it if you've never used ReShade.

- With **ReShade** open use the **dropdown menu** at the top then find and select **KohShade.ini** you may **double click it** to load it or press **Select**.

- **Disable** both **Before** (top) and **After** (bottom) Shaders by **unticking their check boxes**, they are left on so you may see the difference in on (right) and off (left).

- ???

- Profit.

 **This is the final version of my ReShade, however if I make any updates I will make edits as needed.**

Notes
- My ReShade will work with or without HD Patch, Darker Nights, etc. assuming they are up to date. I personally don't use HD Path however I do use Darker Nights.

- Do not expect crazy visuals, depth of field or anything like that.

- This is a light ReShade aiming to bring out existing details as much as possible while tuning lighting and colors to make it more "realistic" and less fantasy.

- Shaders may or may not be too dark or too light for some depending on your monitor settings, etc. if you have an issue with brightness, change your monitor settings or edit the "PD80_04_Contrast_Brightness_Saturation.fx" shader.

- My ReShade may cause low FPS drops for some, if this occurs try enabling or disabling Shaders to see if it helps, if it doesn't help then your PC is not fast enough to use my ReShade.

- Feel free to try other shaders or edit existing ones as you want, ReShade is an amazing and fun tool to play with and everything is adjustable and reversable so play around with it.

Any questions or comments are welcome, I will try to help when I can but I will not be checking this post religiously and offering more support than I have time to provide.

Hope you like it, have fun and thanks for trying it out if you do!
