How do you know exactly which files don't play? Are you getting some kind of an error? This Wolf Death Sound feels like one of the game's default sounds, not something Turtle WoW has added in. To my understanding different assets, including sounds are packaged in .MPQ files so it's really hard to say what's going on. Is your antivirus preventing some WoW files from loading? Is your Turtle WoW client intact or have you tried to make some modifications to it by installing custom mods or .MPQ files and something got messed up?

I've seen people having almost game-breaking problems by just trying to run too many addons. I'd advise you to delete your client and download a fresh one and use as little addons as possible. I just use Cartographer for the windowed world map, pfQuest for quest objectives and that's it. Relying on too many addons is not a good thing either.
