**Blood Enhanced**
Release 1 - 16 Oct 2023

 **This mod is a port of the blood mod from WotLK.**

It works in the same way, when you hit a target/they hit you the blood will be enhanced and will visually display on the ground too. In order to get this port to work in vanilla (and looking good!) the blood has to be a lot more subtle and there are still some potentially unfixable bugs such as flickering when the camera zooms in too much or the blood behaving weirdly with water - aside from that this is that mod lots of people have been waiting for. Preview below and download at the bottom of the page.

Does not work with one shots!

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/1Gh5gBDrJ_Y]

Mods used in video:
Turtle HD, Alt HD, Blood Enhanced

 **Known bugs/errors**
 - Flickers upon zooming in too close
 - Depending on graphics card may render incorrectly in water

 **How to install?**
Drag Patch-Y to the **Data** folder in your Turtle WoW installation folder
