Hello everyone  smiling_turtle_head
I have a fairly old patch from Wrath, which was ported to vanilla. I played for many years on 3.3.5 with this patch. I don't even remember where I got it from. The patch is well made, a little crooked in proportions, but it works. All animations play, no crashes or bugs were noticed. The main feature of this patch for me personally is that it adds heels to the model, literally changing the leg model. The models also have larger breasts.
The female models of orcs, humans, night elves, and high elves have undergone changes. Also, the nipples of the trolls are slightly visible (but of course they are already without heels)
The patch is based on Wrath m2, then ported to vanilla bones. I have already added naked BLP textures from some ancient Zilem patch. They fit the model well.
The model also includes hairstyles that are not on Turtle. I do not guarantee full compatibility with hairstyles, etc., but everything works for me. Some hairstyles are there, some are not. Somewhere there may be no model or textures. If there are craftsmen who can fully adapt the patch to this server, I think it will be very cool.
The patch is independent, nothing else is needed.
Rename the patch at your discretion, if necessary.
NOT COMPATIBLE with the HD patch because it replaces the same files as this patch.
