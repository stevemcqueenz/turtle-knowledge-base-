# [mods] GLOW DOWN - Turn off eye glows for High Elves and Night Elves!

## Post 146115 by Hoxton — 2025-08-16T10:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146115#p146115 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Hello everyone!**

With the new patch, High Elves received a new eye glow. By the common request, I have developed a mod that turns off the said eye glows for High Elves and Night Elves, by making the initial glow texture transparent. This changed the look for High Elves, to the look the had before patch 1.18 and offer a unique look for Night Elves!

All the pictures presented, were taken on the recent patch 1.18!
