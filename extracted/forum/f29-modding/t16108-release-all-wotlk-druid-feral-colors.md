# [Release] All Wotlk Druid Feral Colors

## Post 109674 by Ace1226 — 2024-11-24T15:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109674#p109674 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Big thanks to Seacrabsam for porting the Wotlk Feral forms to Turtle. OG Wotlk Models have 5 different skincolors based the hair/fur color of your NE/Tauren Druid. Since classic doesn't support that the modder went with a preselection of colors, which didn't really matched the colors I liked for my druid.

So I ported over all Wotlk Feral Skins into 5 different Set of Feral form skins for everyone to enjoy. The mod directly replaces the Patch-F.mpq from the original mod.

If you got a special combination of the colors in mind for you personally, feel free to ask

Click on the images for a bigger version of the pictures (they are displayed so small in the forum...)

> **Jyfex wrote: Mon May 26, 2025 12:47 pm**
> How do I install this texture? Just paste the patch into data folder? Cus it does not seem to work

Yes, the patch-F.mpq has to be stored in the /data folder and activated under mods in the launcher. Do you have any other hd mod that might overwrite it? Maybe try changing patch-F to patch-Y or something to change up the loading sequence.

> **Localdruid wrote: Wed May 28, 2025 8:22 am**
> Super late to this party but I was wondering if there was a way to use the white nelf cat form for glyph of the frostsaber?

Sadly it's not that easy. The frostsaber uses a different cat model, the same as the travel form. If you change this model all cats in the whole world that are based on this model (and there are tons of it) would suddenly be druidcats. I looked into changing stuff like moonkins or travel form, but I don't think replacing all regular models with a druidform is really not worth it.
Maybe UE5 client will allow better customization for that
