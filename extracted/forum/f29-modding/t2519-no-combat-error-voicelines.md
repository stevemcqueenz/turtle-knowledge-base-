Removes combat lines like "not enough rage!" and "Not enough Energy" by replacing the files with empty sound files.
I made this because I like having my character comment on things in the game, but I get tired of being told that their too tired or too happy to attack or that they need to wait a bit before doing some attack, so I made this.

Unzip in the WoW folder or put the "sound" folder in the data folder

I will share my solution to the annoying caster's messages on 3.3.5 client some years ago. While melee was either easy to suffer or disable the constant whopping noise when you spam spell between cooldowns was unbearable to me.
 1. mpqeditor: find and extract sound files responsible for annoying errors,
 2. audacity: create empty 0b sized files with same names,
 3. mpqeditor: put the modified files back into same location and enjoy the silence.

As it was 8 years ago I don't quite remember if I had to unpack/repack MPQs or just extract and put back individual files, sorry.
