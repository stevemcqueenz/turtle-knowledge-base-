Here's the Release of my first custom WIIIUI-UI with a steampunky Gnome Theme!

How to install:
WarcraftIIIUI required!
drop 'Custom1' Folder under Interface/AddOns/WIIIUI/art
rename Custom1 with a different number (1-8), if you have other custom UI
click ingame on the bottom right on the cogwheel, then load custom
enjoy!

Minor remaining issues will be fixed with a Addon Update.

Download Link: <https://github.com/JirryJ/GnomereganUI>
