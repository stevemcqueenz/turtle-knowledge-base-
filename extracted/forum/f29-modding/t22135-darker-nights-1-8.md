## Post 154072 by Mejiro — 2025-10-02T15:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154072#p154072 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The game’s night brightness lives in the LightIntBand data: Turtle WoW ships a baseline table, and mods like Darker Nights simply push the night RGB slots downward. To go brighter instead, copy the stock file, raise the night-time RGB values (or blend the timeline toward the daytime samples), then repackage it into an MPQ so the client loads your tweaked table. It’s the same workflow in reverse, so the real decision is how high you want those “night” anchors before you rebuild the archive.

Each LightIntBand row describes one sky/weather overlay (ID). Num tells the client how many keyframes are live, Time_1…Time_16 list the minutes past midnight (0‑1440) when those keys fire, and the matching Data_1…Data_16 fields hold packed RGB colors (0xRRGGBB). During the day–night cycle the game interpolates between those keyframes, so changing the times reshapes dawn/dusk, while editing the RGB values brightens or darkens the sky.
