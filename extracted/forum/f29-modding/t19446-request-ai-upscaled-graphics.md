I remember looking into it, as I was trying to do this for some character textures, and at least the character textures are limited in the resolution they can go up to (I think the highest was like 256x256, if there even was anything 512x512).

The HD mod textures use the same resolutions IIRC. The art itself might just look "cleaner" - the textures were probably downscaled from the original expansion if that one supported higher res.

So you can't really "upscale" to higher res - the best you can do is use the AI to clean it up / make it more detailed within the limits of the existing max possible resolution.
