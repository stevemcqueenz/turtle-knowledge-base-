## Post 97794 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-06-25T07:13:00Z

Concerning why GDKP is banned—it has been heavily directly linked to an increase in RMT/gold buying wherever permitted.

In order to maintain the integrity of our server’s economies, and ensure fair play, we opted to ban GDKP outright rather than fight the losing battle of preventing RMT in a setting where the idea of earned progression is replaced by “affording” progression.

Players are free to use whatever loot system they wish, so long as progression isn’t being sold.
