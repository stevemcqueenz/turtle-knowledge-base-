# New Raid for the outlands

## Post 13739 by Diademuertos — 2021-11-10T18:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13739#p13739 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've come up with a new Raid for the outlands that was never included in Retail called the Dungeons of the Black Citadel that I would like to submit for consideration

Raid summery

located underneath the Black citadel these shadowy tunnels were constructed to house lord Illidan's most dangerous enemies such as the Warden Maiev Shadowsong and the Brutal pit lord Magtheridon

the prison blocks are operated and heavily guarded by a detachment of Illidan's Naga who were Sent by lady Vashj to Safeguard the Tunnels and prisoners held within.

Rajah Haghazed serves as the warden of the dungeons

Bosses

Lady S'theno
Maiev Shadowsong
Magtheridon
Rajah Haghazed

Because of 3 reasons

 1. Her Defeat, Capture and imprisonment by Illidan has caused her to go insane
 2. She doesn't Trust the Alliance due to the fact that the Alliance allowed Tryande wisperwind to join it's ranks who she blames for illidan's escape
 3. She also distrust the Horde because of cooperation with the Alliance and Tyrande wisperwind

 4. All of these factors make her openly hostile to the players when they free her from her cell
