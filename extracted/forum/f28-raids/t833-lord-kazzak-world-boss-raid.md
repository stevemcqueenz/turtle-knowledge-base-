# Lord Kazzak World Boss Raid

## Post 4426 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-11-14T15:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4426#p4426 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been teasing the idea of a world boss attempt on [Lord Kazzak](https://vanillawowdb.com/?npc=12397) during the week of the Thanksgiving holiday here in the states (Thursday, Nov 28th). If we stick with the traditional raid time of 18:00-21:00 server time I don't think we could do it on Thanksgiving because it would cut too close to dinner time and I don't want raiders to postpone family obligations.

I wanted to suggest we try scheduling something either the day before or after Thanksgiving in the hopes that enough players will have time off from school/work we might get a full raid. So if you're interested in attempting a world boss that week please post a reply here or talk to me in-game to give me a sense if either day will work for enough people to make an attempt.

So let me know what works for you:

- Wednesday, November 27th
- Friday, November 29th
This is a 40-man raid boss and since he's out in the game world and not inside an instance the raid scaling will not take effect. I would estimate we need at least 36 well-geared raiders to even have a shot at it. This isn't a 3-hour raid, it would only take about an hour or so to figure out if we can do it or not.

Here's a [YouTube video on the Lord Kazzak fight](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjwq9wWRWb4) that explains it pretty well. If you look at [his loot table](https://vanillawowdb.com/?npc=12397) it contains some pretty unique gear and profession patterns including the [Stronghold Gauntlets](https://vanillawowdb.com/?search=stronghold+gauntlets) and the [Lionheart Helm](https://vanillawowdb.com/?search=lionheart+helm), both good for raid progression!
