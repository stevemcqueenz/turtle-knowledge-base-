Is anyone able to explain the mechanics of the ads on the Golemagg fight?

Half the fight they don't do anything, everything's fine.
Then one second they suddenly have 10k threat on a mage and they drop all other threat.
Another second they completely threat reset to 0.
Then right away they get 10 threat on a warlock.

I am trying to figure out what they're actually doing because it sure as hell doesn't look like one tank is able to hold one ad with their abilities.
Taunt has cooldown, I can't spam that, and if I prepare my threat abilities to hit after the reset, it's like I didn't use them cause the ad gets 10k threat on a dps the next second.

it's like somebody vibecoded threat reset.

Problem is, if the raid mechanics are so random and not in line with the players' abilities, then there's no game to be played.

Also the slow debuff - unable to be countered by Hand of Freedom - another shitty mechanic, because if I can't use my abilities to react to the abilities of the enemies, then why do I even have abilities in the first place...
