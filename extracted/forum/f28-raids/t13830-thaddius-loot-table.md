This is silly I know (RNG is weird sometimes, I get it), but has anyone else noticed the Thaddius loot table for T3 head tokens being unbalanced? According to the database, each token has a 33% chance to drop twice per kill. My guild has been killing Thaddius every week since late January (15 kills according to our raid log history) and we haven't seen the Pally, Hunter, Shaman, Druid token drop yet. Every week it's 2 of the other ones.

I get RNG is weird sometimes, but a 33% chance for something to drop 30 times (15 kills x 2 drops a week) has such a strong possibility to happen that it's basically statistically impossible for it not to. If you do the actual math, the odds of it dropping in 30 chances is 0.9999999999999997465%.
