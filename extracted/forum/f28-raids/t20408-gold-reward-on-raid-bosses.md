# Gold Reward on Raid Bosses

## Post 140798 by Nikaboyoluffy — 2025-07-21T23:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140798#p140798 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I believe that it would make the raiding environment more rewarding to players who get loot and who do not a static amount of gold per boss (**10-15 gold on each boss killed**).

 **PROS:**

 1 - Encourages pick-up-group (PUG) raids to not disband.
 2 - If players' soft-reserved (SR) loot does not drop they will still remain for the gold rewards that they can use to build their wealth for gear or consume requirements (i.e. fire resist gear, elixirs, and flasks).
 3 - If the PUG is stuck on a boss mechanic the gold reward from the boss kill cover their raid / repair costs.
 4 - Encourages players who do not raid an incentive to join the community of raiders and learn the fights.
 5 - Creates a more positive learning and completion raiding environment as the entire raid will be working towards the same goal (GOLD!) if their targeted loot does not drop.
 6 - Allows guilds to build their coffers VIA boss kills too, versus just hard-reserving (HR) materials for further content progression.
 7 - Would make the players who do obtain loot feel more rewarded as they both get their gear and gold reward from raid, and a reason to stay in the raid if their loot does drop.
 8 - Longer play times from players who raid as there is an incentive to complete the entire raid for extra gold, versus just doing specific bosses for boss completion (for example Zul'Gurub).
 9 - Stabilizes player retention for players who consistently "get nothing" from raids then quit. A small, guaranteed reward helps retain raiders across dry loot streaks.
 10 - Easy to implement and scale.

 **CONS:**
 1 - Gold rewards may devalue the feeling of getting rare or powerful gear, and at the end of the day us gamers like feeling better than the other.
 2 - Classic WoW thrives on the thrill of loot drops—adding static gold makes the loot feel less impactful or special.
 3 - Encourages GDKP and RMT behavior and will put extra work on the devs to make sure that does not happen on this server.
 **4 - Classic WoW is built around scarcity, difficulty, and social organization. Adding guaranteed gold shifts the feel toward modern retail WoW design, which will alienate most of the core Classic player base.**
 5 - More gold in the server = easier access to top-tier consumables and gear = higher expectations = more pressure on raiders (new and elite) to perform at even higher levels.

I would love to hear the community / devs thoughts on a change like this, also I'd like to do a vote, but I'm not sure how. If any advice please let me know!

As an aside, it's not in interest of any WoW private server to fully cover costs of raiding by earning gold from killing bosses in the raids. If that was a thing, people wouldn't have to farm gold to buy their consumables - they would only raid-log. The effect would be dead world and as a result dead server.

It's in interest of any private server to keep players playing their game. It's another point of view that explains why this won't ever come to Turtle. Also looking at the past, Turtle devs have been doing anti-inflation policy so far and this idea stands against it.
