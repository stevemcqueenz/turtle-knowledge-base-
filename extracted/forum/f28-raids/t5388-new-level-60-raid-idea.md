I've come up with a new Scourge Raid for the plaguelands that would Serve as a way to give Kel'thuzad a proper farewell unilke the treatment he got in Retail

Raid info

Raid Name: Stratholme Catacombs

Raid difficulty: moderate

Raid entrance: Sewer Door Next to the Slaughterhouse within Slaughter Square in Stratholme

Raid requirements:  level 60, exalted with argent dawn, completion of Naxxramas

Raid ending boss: Father Inigo Montoy

Backstory

The treacherous Priest Father Inigo Montoy having fled Light's Hope Chapel with Kel'thuzad's Phylactery has taken up residence within the ancient and Long abandoned tunnels that lie beneath the Burning city of stratholme

Surrounded by Vile Scourge Montoy now oversees a ritual that if successful would restore Kel'thuzad to corporal form

Raid mobs

Zombie
Ghouls
Necromancer
Skeleton warrior
Skeleton mage
Acolyte
Banshee
Abomination
Crypt fiend

Please share your thoughts on this and if anyone has anything to add or  ways to improve the raid overall please don't hesitate to Share
