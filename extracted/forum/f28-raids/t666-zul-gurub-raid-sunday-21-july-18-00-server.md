Hi all!

So, we had a fun little ZG trash/practice run today with a group of 11 of us, and a few of us agreed to set an official day for a raid in the hopes that with some advance notice we'd be able to get a full group of 20 decently geared folks to start exploring Zul'Gurub.  This time is semi-tentative, as I only got a few affirmative responses, so if a large group of people suggest a slightly different time, I'm sure most of us could be flexible.

Date: Sunday, July 21st
Time: 18.00 (6pm) server time
Location: Gates of ZG in Stranglethorn
What to bring: Appropriate consumables (if you can) and a good attitude.  We're here to learn and have a good time while getting some rep and hopefully down a few bosses!

This run is, of course, cross-faction and open to all who are level 60 and have some solid gear from their dungeon runs.
Over the next week or so, as we iron out the roster and who the tanks and raid leaders are, we'll confirm the day and time again.  I don't intend on being Raid Leader, but I do want to help get this group together in advance so we're nice and organized, so please contact your friend Avarice in-game to get a spot.
