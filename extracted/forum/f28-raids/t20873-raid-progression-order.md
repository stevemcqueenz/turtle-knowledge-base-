UBRS < K10/ZG < MC/Ony < AQ20/ES NM < BWL < ES HM < AQ40 < Naxx < K40

If you want "tiers", from the perspective of Nordanaar (the main server):
Full world-chat PUG tier: UBRS, K10, ZG, MC, Ony, AQ20, ES NM
Needs core but can easily take randos with them: BWL, ES HM
Actual raids for stable cores: AQ40, Naxx
A challenge not everyone will beat: K40

Nordanaar is a very old, very progressed server. The last round of Class Changes in November 2024 have also put raid damage into overdrive and in two days it'll probably go up again. So, "progression" is mostly an empty husk right now. People jump into ES and BWL on fresh 60 characters, hitching a ride with a progressed guild. Even on a new guild it doesn't take months, it takes weeks. But MC is set to receive an overhaul in 2 days, that might shake things up, who knows.
