IMPORTANT SHEET UPDATE
New formula has been introduced to exact an average which is more accurate than before.
 - Weekly Top Classes will be split in a larger sheet based on Class + Spec to introduce better representation.
 - Weekly Sheet with Graph now has Boss Timers, Player Pools with big disclaimers around it to avoid miscommunication.

Hello everyone,

Since Class Changes 2.0 it has become really hard to keep up with what's good and what's not for damage in raiding scene. For that purpose I have created a Sheet which will provide results as an info graph on Patchwerk style single target dps, as a showcase of "what's possible".

The purpose of this sheet is not to give a definitive result, nor is it there to exact an average result this early into the Class Changes 2.0. It is here to give you the top DPS performance on Patchwerk with the sole purpose of showcase.

The sheet will be updated weekly with:
 - Top Class, Spec & Style. (for the entire quarter)
 - Top Players For Class Damage-Wise. (weekly)
 - Weekly Top Damage numbers & guilds.
 - All results include log links.

You can find the sheet here:
[https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/ ... 2018728657](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1GaKCpUEVT7s6JrCM8Ecap7B2ziMxe9dUSh_WYFzTTD4/edit?gid=2018728657#gid=2018728657)

![Image](https://imgur.com/a/KL0gQOq)

Disclaimers:
 - The following results are being collected as best overall since the official release of CC2 on a weekly basis - 1st of Nov 2024
 - The sheet & its future weekly updates do not account for unexpected Bugs/Buffs/Nerfs or any inadequacies that may have occurred that week.
 - Data is being collected exclusively from Turtlogs.com as it supports our custom content & will support future raids.
 - She sheet is updated daily & results will not be final until the weekly reset day.

Enjoy.
