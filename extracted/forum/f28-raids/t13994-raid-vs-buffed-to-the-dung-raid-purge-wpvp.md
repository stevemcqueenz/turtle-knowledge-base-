You suggest that re-adding world buffs to Tel'Abim would incentivize world PVP but do you really want to enjoy the world 'PVP' of the world buff meta? I can probably sum up the average 'PVP' encounter in the world buff meta that will occur 99% of the time
 - Shaman/Priest resses at a world buff location e.g. Songflower, ZG island and starts purging/dispelling people's buffs.
 - Gets killed by a raid/party
 - Waits whilst dead until the next group

I don't really think that's an enjoyable PVP experience and one that Tel'Abim should aim towards cultivating.

Outside of that, you have a host of other issues such as further unbalance in the raiding scene (as some classes benefit more from world buffs than others) and trivialising PvE content by making boss fights much shorter and mechanics less important.
