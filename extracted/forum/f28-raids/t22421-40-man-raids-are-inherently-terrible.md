I often see that "pvp was a sidenote" thesis. I totally disagree with it.
PvP was supposed to be the pinnacle of character progression, not the thing that you do when bored. It was like exam to your gear/skill check.

Reasons:
 1. Literally half of abilities that exist in the game are used only for pvp. Disarm, silence, spell interrupts, manaburn, slow and anti-slow abilities.
 2. Blizzard was a company which made Warcraft 3 - highly competitive game with tournaments and prizes. They also made Diablo 2 and get good experience in making pvp there. They know how to make pvp in general. They just have to solve "how to make pvp in group setting".
 3. This game was made with 2 faction from the start. It is naturally lead to fighting against eachother.

If you compare wow to any other cooperative game, you notice that wow is quite good even now. It relies too heavily on tank+healer+dps model, but as long as it is fun, why not?

One more thing about raids being bad. They bend economy of the server to consumable farming. Half of the problem with economics comes from the fact that people have to drink all possible elixirs each raid. Instead of gimmik that boosted people a bit, it become mandatory tax for every man involved.
If you do pvp all the time, you won't get enough time to gather consumables.
