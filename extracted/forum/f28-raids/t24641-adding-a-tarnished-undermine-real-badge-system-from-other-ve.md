# Adding a Tarnished Undermine Real/Badge System from other versions of WoW.

## Post 173499 by Zarahv — 2026-03-31T20:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173499#p173499 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

After this latest Naxxramas run last week where I ended up yet again not receiving any loot due to poor rolls across the entire raid, I've come to the conclusion that vanilla WoW's, and Turtle's loot system by extension, is still archaic and extremely dated.

Blizzard had systems in Season of Discovery and Wrath of the Lich King Classic where you could purchase raid loot for clearing dungeons/raids to reward participation and clearing aside from simply getting lucky when a boss dies, and then even luckier by winning a dice roll against upwards of 20+ people.

Ive done over 10-11 lockouts of Naxxramas and only ended up with 2 pieces of tier 3, no other items whatsoever. I feel like earning badges/tokens for killing bosses in each wing of Naxx, or any raid for instance, should reward you with tokens for said raid where you can then spend said tokens outside of the raid for gear from corresponding bosses.

Here's a hypothetical system I've thought up, completely subject to interpretation or change by the Turtle WoW Dev Team.

Naxxramas has 15 bosses.

All loot from the 4 wings before Sapphiron/KT costs between 10 (Non-weapon)/15 (Weapon) tokens/badges to buy at a vendor at Light's Hope Chapel.

Final Boss Loot of each wing, Sapphiron and Kel'thuzad costs between 20 (Non-weapon)/30 (Weapon) tokens/badges plus a special token from each final boss so you MUST kill 20-30 bosses (2 Lockouts) +  have the respective token from each boss to purchase loot that drops from them.

That means in a minimum of a week, for your participation, you receive almost any item you desire should you put in the work and fully clear the raid. It would make going in and clearing without dreading the thought of receiving nothing completely moot, as you are guaranteed at least 1-2 items per run should you fully clear. Legendaries should not be purchasable however and still stay rare drops.

I'm willing to hear feedback if this is too easy or not, but the current slog of waiting week after week, spending thousands of gold on consumables, going into these raids and getting NOTHING heavily wanes on the psyche. I'd love to see a system like this implemented so no matter what, luck or not, gear will come to you if you put forth the effort and time to attain it.
