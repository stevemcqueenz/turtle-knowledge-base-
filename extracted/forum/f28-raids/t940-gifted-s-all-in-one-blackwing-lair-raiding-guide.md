# Gifted's All-in-One Blackwing Lair Raiding Guide

## Post 5323 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-03-09T00:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5323#p5323 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I never made a guide for Molten Core and I think we could have progressed a little better/faster if I had. So I'm writing this guide to help us with our progression in Blackwing Lair. Feel free to add to it in the responses!

 **Blackwing Lair Attunement**

To begin the Blackwing Lair attunement quest line kill the [Scarshield Quartermaster](https://vanillawowdb.com/?npc=9046) located just outside of the entrance to LBRS/UBRS. He drops [Blackhand's Command](https://vanillawowdb.com/?item=18987) to start the Blackwing Lair attunement quest line. To finish, just go into UBRS, kill [General Drakkisath](https://vanillawowdb.com/?npc=10363), and click the orb behind where the general was standing.

You're now attuned to Blackwing Lair!

 **Getting in to Blackwing Lair**

The entrance to Blackwing Lair is really close to where you got the attunement quest. If you're standing in front of the entrance to LBRS/UBRS and turn right just head all the way down that hallway and click the orb there.

You're now inside Blackwing Lair!

 **Raid-Specific Addons**

Blackwing Lair is no joke. There are several addons you'll need to survive the fights. You can download addons from the [GitHub List of Vanilla World of Warcraft AddOns 1.12.1 post in the Turtle WoW addons forum](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=18&t=874). It's important all raiders get their addons from the same place so we know everyone is using the same version!

- KTM Threat Meter
- Decursive
- Big Wigs or Deadly Boss Mods
This is the BARE MINIMUM addons you need for Blackwing Lair!

 **NOTE:** The website has a small triangle on the right side of every page to collapse the advertisements. It's not easy to find, but once you do it makes reading the guides a lot easier!
