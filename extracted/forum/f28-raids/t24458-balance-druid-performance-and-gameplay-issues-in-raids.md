# Balance Druid performance and gameplay issues in raids

## Post 172024 by Auralys — 2026-03-23T21:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172024#p172024 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
After playing Balance in K40-level raids, I wanted to share some feedback on how the spec currently performs in PvE.
Right now:

DPS feels inconsistent due to heavy RNG (Eclipse procs)
Optimal play requires frequent stopcasting, which hurts fluidity and real output
No real burst or control window compared to other casters
DoTs and Hurricane not proccing trinkets significantly reduces scaling

In practice, this results in:

Lower reliability in damage output
More effort required for equal or worse results than other specs

The spec doesn’t feel competitive or rewarding in a raid setting despite proper play.
