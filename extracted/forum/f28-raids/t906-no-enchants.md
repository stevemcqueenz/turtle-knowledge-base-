# No enchants

## Post 4982 by Chlothar (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-01-31T20:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4982#p4982 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone.

during the last raids i randomly inspected members of our raids. And i recognized that like half the players dont have a single enchant on their gear. While there are people in blue gear having every piece enchanted i see others in top gear with no enchants on it.
Enchants make a difference, we clear the raids faster with enchants, you live longer and are of higher value for the whole raid.

If i was in charge of our raids i would preferably give loot to people who show dedication. Speaking as a raidleader i don`t expect you to enchant every piece. But you should atleast enchant boots/gloves/chest/cloak/bracers which is not very expensive.

The same goes for raid consumeables. Get atleast a food buff that boosts your main stats /Jujus from Winterspring and elixirs.

Think about it this way: imagine I'm not everybody's 4th favourite feral druid tank, who has enchantments on everything and has actively been working on his character(s) for a long time. I'm just a fresh 60 with a few greens remaining and I'm working on getting my hit gear etc etc.

Does enchanting my own gear and bringing food buffs get me more loot? No.
Does my unenchanted gear and level 43 green items mean I'm overlooked when it's time to roll? No.
Does a food buff or a mongoose elixir do anything for me other than cost me time/money? No.

Why would I, as that fresh 60, bother? There's no deterrent or punishment for not doing so, and people won't up their game unless there is.

Don't get me wrong, I agree with you completely that people should be getting their enchantments and bringing their consumables but there are literally zero negative consequences for not doing so, so less diligent members of the raid than you and I just won't. Unless that issue is addressed, I don't think people will change their ways.

> Are we at the point where we can be picky of who comes to raids? If so, competition for spots will naturally filter the best.

This is the real issue. Yesterday we had 36/40 raiders. We're still a casual raiding group because in order to make hardcore rules (like everyone must have all their gear enchanted) there needs to be a meaningful consequence for those who don't. In hardcore raid guilds that consequence is losing your raid position and having to re-earn your spot.

The best we can do at the moment is to put pressure on players who aren't giving 110% at raids. I think Chlothar posting here in the raid forums is a positive way to call attention to the problem without shaming any specific individuals.

When we start getting 40+ raiders showing-up for raids on a regular basis we can start using things like attendance, gear quality, class/spec builds, and raid readiness (enchants, potions, elixirs, etc...) to see who gets a raid slot.

Please be aware that veteran raiders and the raid leaders are looking to see who comes prepared for raiding. This will be a factor in the future when we get to the point of too many raiders and not enough raid slots.

 **~Gifted**
