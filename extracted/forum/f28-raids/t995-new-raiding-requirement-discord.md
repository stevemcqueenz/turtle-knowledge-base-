# New Raiding Requirement - Discord

## Post 5628 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-04-06T11:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5628#p5628 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

With our struggles against [Vaelastrasz the Corrupt](https://vanillawowdb.com/?npc=13020) (2nd boss in BWL) we've been discussing 'tightening up' the raid group with the goal of further progression. Last week I posted [class guides](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=28&t=982) and we requested all raiders research their class/spec/gear/etc.

This week we will be requiring raiders to log into the [Discord App](https://discordapp.com/) in order to raid with us. You **DO NOT** need a microphone for Discord to work. We just need you to hear boss strategies so we can better coordinate our efforts.

 **If you cannot be identified on Discord you will be dropped from raid groups from now on!**

I know this might seem like a heavy-handed approach to some of you new to raiding. We've actually been requesting raiders voluntarily get on Discord for the last 3-4 weeks. Unfortunately, there are still a few holdouts so we now are requiring all raiders have Discord in order to raid.

 **~Gifted**
