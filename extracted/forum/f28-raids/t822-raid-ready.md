# Raid Ready

## Post 4345 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-10-31T15:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4345#p4345 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Last week we talked about how some of the more ‘hardcore’ raiders are getting upset that other raiders aren’t coming ‘raid ready’. We are approaching a full raid roster and when 40+ people show-up to raid some decisions will have to be made as to who gets to raid and who doesn’t. One of the factors that will be considered is whether players are ‘raid ready’ or not.

 **What Does It Mean To Be Raid Ready?**

There is no single answer to this question. It’s more a list of things players can do to come ready to raid.

Here’s some examples:

- Your armor is fully repaired
- Your armor is fully enchanted
- You have enough flasks for the raid
- You have enough potions for the raid
- You have enough reagents for the raid
- You have enough food buffs for the raid
- You have enough soul stones for the raid
- You have enough consumables for the raid
- You have enough ammo/arrows for the raid
- You get the idea...
Basically, you’ve done everything you can to bring your best game to raiding!

 **What About Gear?**

Gear isn’t so much an issue of being ‘raid ready’ as it is a sign of how much time a player has invested in their character. Players who regularly run instances during the week tend to have better gear (blue) than players with questing gear (green) who jump into raiding without running instances.

Obviously, we’d like to progress as far as we can every week as a raid and that means taking players who will give us the best chances for success. It stands to reason that a raid full of players in blue gear will most likely advance further than a raid full of players in green gear.

So these are all things the raid leaders are looking at when it comes to being ‘raid ready’.

One argument I've heard from some players regarding gear is that it's hard to get groups for instances. While this is a valid point, Turtle WoW has always been a low-pop server averaging around 100 ppl online at any given time. So yes it's hard to get groups, but it has always been hard to get groups! If you want to get groups for instances you'll have to make a real effort like most other players have.

Another point with gear is that there's a lot of craft-able gear that's really, really nice! So if you're truly not able to get any instances going you can always log-in and farm mats for some of your BiS crafted pieces until you get a chance to run some instances!
