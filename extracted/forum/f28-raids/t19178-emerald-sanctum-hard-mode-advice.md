## Post 130281 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-05-09T09:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130281#p130281 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Here is how our guild does it.

Positioning:
> B= boss
>  T= Tank
>  H= Heal
>  M = Melee
>  R = Ranged
>
>  B T< - 40 yards- > 3H <- 10 yards -> H&R <- 20 yards-> M B T

We put the addtank & healers into a separate grp. That makes it easy for them to heal the tank and one another (preferably always a druid, priest and shaman healer + a warrior tank for dealing with erennius)
Line of sight used to matter but i think it got patched, we stopped losing since it didnt seem to help us consistently and people kept getting slept eitherway. We found everyone but the addtank being in the main raids healers range helped more in peventing sleep deaths than trying to be cute with los.

There are some debuffs that are conditionally worth removing. The manadrain curses can be removed from tree druids. And the sleep the adds cast can be removed without issue. But due to the risk generally associated with dispelling. My advice as raidleader is, drop tremor totem and dont dispell anything. If the wrong stuff gets dispelled its much more devastating.

Make sure the melees stand at max melee range fanned out in the back half of the boss. He has a nasty aoe that he casts on random players in melee. It deals way too much damage if your melees clump.

Keep detect magic on the boss and ask a offensive shaman or piest to purge the buff that looks like the goblin shrink ray debuff (a blue bolt facing bottom left)

In the addphase let a pally pick up welps and ignore them. Burn down the elite adds. Focus spellcasters and leave scalebane for last.
Only bomb adds once all 3 elites are down.

Keep 2 tanks on the main boss that race for theeat until he transforms. The tank that gets booted off tried to climb up back into second place, but thats only needed i the MT eats too many breaths in p3 and shouldnt be an issue unless your grps dps is atroceous

With that we have little problems consistently killing him
