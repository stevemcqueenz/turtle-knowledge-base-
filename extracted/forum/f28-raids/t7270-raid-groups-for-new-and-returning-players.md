# Raid Groups for New and Returning Players

## Post 46048 by Bardicdm — 2023-05-16T14:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46048#p46048 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello everyone!

I'm curious if there are any dedicated raid groups designed to help new and returning vanilla players get comfortable with the raiding experience. For completely new players, WoW itself is completely alien, let alone raiding. For some returning players from Vanilla, we come from a time where your guild progressed slowly through Ony, ZG, and MC, and there was a lot of time to learn what to do.

For most of the established guilds here on TWoW, these raids are on farm, and are cleared at a very fast pace, understandably. And also understandably, these guilds don't want to take the time to create systems for teaching things like boss mechanics, pathing, trash skips, and loot systems, since the established guild mates already know all of this and just want to clear the early raids as fast as possible.

But for some players, it is very stressful and hard to keep up and know what is going on with this system. And I thought that, with the large influx of players lately, it would be good if some experienced raid leaders created systems to give new and returning players the knowledge and experience to catch up in way that is a bit slower, relaxed, and easy.

I'm speaking on behalf of people with anxiety, ADHD, or simply learn by doing. Being carried through early raids isn't a good way to learn for some of us, who want to contribute and really get into the meat of the early raid content.

Are there any groups like this? Any interest?

Thanks!
