# Raid Slot Rules/Priorities

## Post 4371 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-11-03T15:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4371#p4371 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In last week's raid I brought-up the fact that we're quickly approaching a full 40-man raid in Molten Core. This means we need to figure-out a system for deciding who gets to raid and who doesn't if we get 40+ players asking for invites!

So in keeping with raid transparency I thought I'd start to list how we would go about making that decision if/when the time comes.

 **Rules / Priority for Raid Slots**

1. Raiders that are dual-boxing will choose a single toon to raid with.
2. Level 60 raiders take priority over lower level raiders.
3. Roll/Class balance takes priority.
4. Raiders who [sign-up for raids ahead of time](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=28&t=1008) take priority over raiders who do not.
5. First come, first served.
 **Raid Rotation**

Given the rules / priority for raid slots it stands to reason that players trying to join their first raid might struggle for a raid slot. I think we should consider working on some kind of rotation for trash/bosses so new players will get an opportunity to participate in some of the raid and learn at least 1 or 2 boss fights in the process. This would keep new people invested in raiding with us that might eventually take the place of veteran players who pause or stop raiding for one reason or another.
