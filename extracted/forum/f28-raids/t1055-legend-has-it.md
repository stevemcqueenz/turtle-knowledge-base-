# Legend Has It...

## Post 6055 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-05-16T10:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6055#p6055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Back when I played the retail version of World of Warcraft there was a guild on my server called the **<Booty Bay Yacht Club>**. It's always stuck with me as one of the more clever guild names that I've seen over the years. I like how they integrated something from the virtual world into their guild name. It's like it made them a part of the world too!

A couple of years ago, on another low population private server (before Turtle WoW) I met a kind dwarf who wanted to start a guild with me. I don't remember why we wanted to start a guild other than we felt like we needed to have one. Talking to the dwarf, and being a home brewer in real life, I thought, 'Why not start a brewery?' So I did a quick search of all the alcoholic beverages available in vanilla WoW and discovered [Thunder Ale](https://vanillawowdb.com/?item=2686)! Why had I not heard of this stuff before?!? What's that? Try playing a dwarf you say? Ah, yes, right, right...

And that my friends, is the story of how the **<Thunder Ale Brewing Co>** got it's name!

I'm going to be taking a break from World of Warcraft for a while. Relax, it's not a break from Turtle WoW. I do hope to listen to you guys wipe to Nefarion over Discord a couple of times. But I think I need to take a break from playing the game itself for a while and try some new things.

Be kind to one another while I'm gone =)
