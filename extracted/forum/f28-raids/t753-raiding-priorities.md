# Raiding Priorities

## Post 3924 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-09-13T18:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3924#p3924 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been talking with the raid leaders and several key raiders about our raid progression and wanted to start a dialogue here to get a better feel for everyone's raiding priorities. We've been raiding Molten Core every Sunday for about 5 or 6 weeks now and while we regularly down the first 3 bosses (Lucifron, Magmadar, & Gehennas) we haven't progressed much further than that. Last week we had our most successful attempts at Garr getting him down to 48%.

Now I don't want to give the impression that this is a problem. Sometimes this is what progression looks like. We have our own unique set of challenges on Turtle WoW with the population that's going to impact our raid progression. But I do want to check-in with the raiding community and see what your preferences are.

To be clear, this is intended to be a discussion and not a vote. Ultimately the raid leaders will decide how we will proceed, but the comments posted here will help guide us with making those decisions.

So the basic raiding options that have been presented to me are as follows:

1. Continue to raid Molten Core and try to be more efficient with time so we can attempt more than 3 bosses.
2. See if Torta/Pompa would consider having raids reset every 2 weeks so we can do 3-4 bosses the first week and pick-up where we left off the second week, allowing us to attempt different bosses each week.
3. Move on to Zul'Gurub.
4. Try Onyxia's Lair.
Again, this isn't a vote, just a list of options. Feel free to post any additional options you can think of.

We'll be weighing the pros & cons of all our options based on your feedback here. We'll also be discussing it in Discord voice chat during this Sunday's raid to get more input from everyone.

I'll post my own thoughts in a reply to this post.

 **~Gifted**

## Post 3925 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-09-13T18:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3925#p3925 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**1) Continue to raid Molten Core**

This is probably my main preference. I like the traditional approach to the game and I'd rather focus on MC before moving on to new content. My biggest concern is that raiders will get bored if raiding feels too repetitious and it's the same old bosses/loot every week. Time is a limiting factor, so getting more than 3-4 bosses down in a 3 hour window would be tough. I personally can't commit any more time to raiding every week, but that doesn't mean you guys need to wait for me!

 **2) Extend raid resets to every 2 weeks**

Assuming this is an option, I think it's a good one. It allows us to maintain our current raid schedule without asking people to commit any additional time each week while also allowing us to potentially progress further into Molten Core.

 **3) Move on to Zul'Gurub**

I'm not a big fan of this option just because it feels like we're leapfrogging over Molten Core. Yes we would get slightly better loot from bosses and that would help with Molten Core progression, but it feels like cheating to me. The other issue is that Zul'Gurub is a 20-man raid and we had 26 raiders show-up last week. Now it's true that 3 of us were 2-boxing to bump up our numbers, but even if we all played a single character that means a couple of people are going to have to sit-out of raids and we haven't really had a discussion on how to determine who gets to raid and who doesn't each week.

 **4) Try Onyxia's Lair**

Personally I think this would be fun to try. We don't know what impact raid scaling will have on this encounter. It might be fun to schedule this for the first or last week of every month to help avoid burnout if we continue to grind Molten Core.
