That system is also know as 'spend all' dkp, which we have used before. The only thing we had to deal with was people not wanting to spend all their dkp on one minor upgrade when there were huge upgrades possible.

This was for a guild raid though, with not as many people as an MC raid so it might be more difficult to keep track of for your raid. Regardless, best of luck going forward ![😊](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f60a.svg)
