# Raid Event Calendar / Sign-Ups

## Post 5687 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-04-13T13:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5687#p5687 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As we continue to progress with raiding and new raiders continue to join us we're regularly turning both new and veteran players away from raids once they fill. Up until now we've been using a 'first come, first served' approach, but the time has come for that to change.

We're now asking raiders to sign-up for raids ahead of time. Raiders who register prior to raids will be given priority for raid slots. You can even sign-up with a 'maybe' option if you're not 100% sure you can make it.

 **To sign-up for raids you'll need to do the following:**

1. [Register with Shivtr](https://thunderale.shivtr.com/users/sign_up)
2. Join the [Thunder Ale Brewing Co](https://thunderale.shivtr.com/) web site
3. Sign-up for raids on the [event calendar](https://thunderale.shivtr.com/events)
We're taking this step as an effort to better plan future raids so we know who's coming and what the raid composition looks like (do we have enough tanks, healers, etc...) long before the raid starts.

If you don't want to register with Shivtr and sign-up for raids ahead of time that's fine. But know that raiders who do sign-up ahead of time will be given a raid slot priority over raiders who do not sign-up ahead of time. After that we'll continue with our 'first come, first served' approach to fill the remaining raid slots.

Please post any questions you might have in the replies...

 **~Gifted**

So I've been asked by more than one person how to add all of their characters to the Thunder Ale site so when they sign-up for raids they can choose which character they intend to bring. Here's how to do that...

 **How do I add characters to my Shivtr / Thunder Ale account?**

1. After following the above steps to register with the Thunder Ale site...
2. Make sure you're signed-in and go to the [Roster](https://thunderale.shivtr.com/members) page on the Thunder Ale site
3. Find your name in the roster list (names are listed alphabetically in the left column)
4. Once you find your name in the left column look at the info in the middle column
5. Next to 'characters' you'll see a big **PLUS** sign to add characters to your account
6. Next time you sign-up for an event it'll give you a list of your characters to choose from
Look forward to seeing everyone at our next raid!

**When are new raids posted?**

I try to post new raid events roughly one week (5-7 days) in advance.

If raiders want me to post the events as re-occurring weekly events I can do that, but I'd rather not. Having players who are active during the current week signing-up for raids reduces the number of no-shows significantly.
