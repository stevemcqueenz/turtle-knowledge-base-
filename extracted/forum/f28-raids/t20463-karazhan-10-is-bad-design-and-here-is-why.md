# Karazhan 10 is bad design and here is why

## Post 141323 by Afraido23 — 2025-07-25T13:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141323#p141323 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Lower Karazhan Halls: The Worst Raid Design in the Game**

Let’s be honest — Lower Karazhan Halls (Kara10) is arguably the worst-designed raid in the game. Here is why:

 **1. The Difficulty — or Lack Thereof**

Kara10 is the textbook definition of a "tank and spank" raid. There’s virtually no risk of wiping due to complex mechanics or lacking DPS. While some players consider it a chill, laid-back raid, the reality is that it's mind-numbingly dull. You can switch off your brain, spam your basic rotation, and still clear it with ease. The only variable that affects clear time is your group’s gear level. Honestly, UBRS presents more of a challenge than Kara10.

 **2. Uninspired Mechanics and Environment**

The raid’s mechanics are as bland as they come:
- The first boss just spawns adds.

- The worgen boss requires a simple tank taunt rotation or a Paladin bubble.

- The imp boss does some generic fire AoE.

- The spider boss spits poison and spawns eggs no one cares about.

- Moroes? Just another OT requirement.
That’s it. Nothing creative, nothing engaging. The environment is no better — a linear tower filled with trash mobs. There's no optional content, no profession interactions, and nothing unique you can do as a particular class. You can’t Mind Control, Enslave, or Tame anything interesting. There are no summonable secret bosses, class-specific interactions, or meaningful choices. About the only thing worth mentioning is that you can drink some booze off the floor. That’s the highlight.

 **3. The Loot — Hilariously Imbalanced**

Now for the real kicker: the loot. For the most part, Kara10’s loot is utterly forgettable — except for one item. The Scythe of Elune.

This isn’t just a good item. It’s the best item in the entire game — and it drops from the easiest raid, with a 2% drop rate, purely based on RNG. Who thought this was a good idea?

This design is baffling. The most powerful, build-defining legendary in the game is locked behind a mindless raid and random chance. It goes against the entire spirit of earning your rewards through difficult content. It’s demoralizing to see someone decked out in greens and blues suddenly obtain the best item in the game because the dice rolled in their favor.

And it gets worse — only certain classes (casters) benefit from the Scythe, so they’re the only ones interested in running Kara10. That creates a funnel effect where the raid becomes nothing more than a farming ground for a handful of players, while everyone else has no reason to care.

Imagine if Turtle randomly added a 1% drop chance BiS legendary bow for Hunters in AQ20 — Hunters would flood the raid endlessly while everyone else would avoid it. The frustration would be universal and justified. That kind of loot design just shouldn’t exist.

 **In summary:** Lower Karazhan Halls isn’t just a badly designed raid — it’s a masterclass in how not to create content. It's boring to play, forgettable in design, and completely broken in reward structure. If Turtle wanted to create the perfect storm of wasted potential, Kara10 is it. A trash raid with a jackpot prize — and that’s not clever, it’s just lazy.

 **My only criticism about Kara 10** is that there are *too many imps to kill*. It gets repetitive and tedious quickly, making the dungeon feel more like a chore than an adventure.

Also, the dungeon lacks a truly *iconic mechanic*. Right now, it’s mostly just narrow corridors packed with mobs. There’s nothing that really makes the place stand out or feel unique.

I think this dungeon has the perfect atmosphere to introduce something more creative — like *parkour elements*, such as floating platforms or environmental challenges. It would make players engage with the dungeon itself, not just the NPCs inside.

 **But beyond that, here are a couple more ideas:**

- **Nemesis Mechanic**: After defeating a certain boss, a powerful ghostly entity (a "Nemesis") is unleashed. It’s immortal, moves very slowly, and has a short-range aura that deals lethal damage if you stay too close for too long. Players would have to keep moving and stay aware of its presence while clearing the rest of the dungeon. It would add tension and a sense of being *hunted* in a haunted place.

- **Magical Instability**: At random intervals, the dungeon experiences surges or suppressions of magical energy. Different schools of magic (Fire, Arcane, Shadow, etc.) could become temporarily stronger or weaker, affecting both players and enemy spellcasters. It would force players to adapt their strategies on the fly and reinforce the arcane chaos within Karazhan.
Of course, some players might not love things like parkour or unpredictable mechanics, but even one of these ideas could give Kara 10 a stronger identity and more replay value. Right now, it just needs **something** to elevate it beyond mob-clearing.
