# New Barrow Deeps Dungeons/Raid

## Post 109560 by Neechy (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-11-23T20:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109560#p109560 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

One piece of vanilla content I always wanted to see developed was the Timbermaw Hold zone/dungeon/raid that was locked behind the gate in Azshara and never developed. And by extension the whole area under Hyjal known as Barrow Deeps, which could serve as a massive underground area connecting from Felwood all the way to Azshara.

Here is a post with some great ideas on what it could include (can click 'Read More' if you want to stay on this page):

[embed: https://s9e.github.io/iframe/2/reddit.min.html#classicwowplus/comments/d59qfy#theme=]

In short, if TWoW could develop a Barrow Deeps dungeon complex similar to Blackrock Mountain with some open world access and multiple dungeons and a raid, it could be one of the most ambitious and impressive achievements on this server yet. After all Blackrock Mountain continues to be one of the most acclaimed design achievements in WoW history. There are already lots of great existing and unused assets that could aid with this, and also with some of the original assets the design team has made so far, I have full faith they could pull this off.

 **Some ideas:**
 **- Timbermaw City dungeon** (Lvl 60 10-Man? UBRS+ difficulty) accessible via the Azshara Timbermaw Hold gate
 **- Felwood Ext. Dungeon** (Lvl ???) accessible via a long tunnel entrance in the (originally) closed off 4th tunnel of Felwood's Timbermaw Hold
 **- Barrow Deeps Raid** (40-Man, Tier 4 alt / Tier 4.5?) accessible mainly through Hyjal tunnel entrance (i.e. Barkskin furbolg area?). It would be a raid directly under Hyjal inspired by WC3 and potentially feature Illidan as an end boss. I can understand concerns over big characters like Illidan/Lich King as killable in vanilla, but it feels reasonable given TWoW's general WC3 design influence thus far. Perhaps Illidan returned to the Barrow Deeps after his exile in Outlands in order to recover some secret relic to destroy the Lich King. However, we also have to kill the Lich King and we don't want Illidan stealing our loot so we must kill him first. Thus Illidan is Tier 4.5 end-boss and Lich King would be Tier 5 final end-boss of the game. (There have been rumours already that dev team plans to develop Northrend)

 **Example:** Map could look something like this:
 -Red area lines show Barrow Deeps coverage connects 4 different zones
 -Yellow area lines are the rough locations of three unique dungeons/raids

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/0VnTxE5.jpeg)

Anyways, haven't heard much from the devs regarding Barrow Deeps & Timbermaw Hold and would love to hear more, and any thoughts/ideas from the community. Cheers.

As per the first post in this thread, I'd still like to see Timbermaw as a dungeon/mini-raid as part of the larger underground complex that could connect Azshara, Winterspring, Felwood and Mt. Hyjal. The main raid within such underground complex would be Barrow Deeps and as you say it would have heavy demon influence.

From a comment in the Reddit post I linked:

"My dream was to have a truly massive underground complex under there, on the scale of Blackrock Mountain. Uninstanced content, multiple dungeons, multiple raids, there's a lot of room to fill.

It's not just Furbolgs in that mountain. There's Night Elven Barrow Dens, the prison where Illidan was kept, giant spiders, and the Largest Panda Ever. I could even see the tunnels having a third exit up on Hyjal."

As to whether WC3 big names should be added and killable in twow - I have no issue either way. I had just posited Illidan for Barrow Deeps given the historical context, and there have been far crazier retcons. But I can understand he is canonically supposed to be in Outlands and I don't think there are dev plans for players to leave Azeroth, aside from maybe a raid via Dark Portal.

But there have been rumours of Northrend being added in the future. LK is canonically in Northrend during vanilla timeline. So it would be strange for players to be able to be right at LK's doorstep but for some reason we can't access him? It would need to be explained in a satisfactory way, otherwise all the questing and story progression up to that point feels anticlimactic.
