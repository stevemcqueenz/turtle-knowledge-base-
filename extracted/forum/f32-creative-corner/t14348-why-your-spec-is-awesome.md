# Why your spec is awesome

## Post 97899 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-26T12:32:00Z

Hey!

Kinda the continuation of the why your class is annoying article ([viewtopic.php?f=32&t=14329](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=14329)) but this one will simp you (mostly).

 **Prot warrior**
You're totally okay with having a mage alt just to cover all of those consumes and repair costs. You are also totally okay with dealing abysmal damage - you always wear your shield, you still don't go fury-prot and... you just... won't die. Come on! With all of those armor points you could survive the apocalyspe. A meteros is falling on you from the space? Shield block gg. You kinda underestimate how important threat is but people like you because finding a tank is hard. And you usually don't roll on DPS items.

 **Fury-prot**
You are the prot warrior who read an article about how threat generation works in the game. A wise programmer friend (probably a gnome mage) wrote you a macro for shield swap and since then, you rock. You are not THAT unkillable but at least mobs stay more or less around you. Rogue loves you until you take a DPS bis 'for threat'. You good, you meta, you bis tank.

 **Fury**
You are Rambo with extra mescaline in the balls. Shout, charging in, whirlwind, you RP a 0 anger-management guy on cocaine. But it's fine, you kill stuff very fast and in theory you can OT at any moment (we all know you won't). At least you kick casters. Teammates love you because you make the dungeon twice as fast. Frost mages from the opposing faction love you since you look beautiful being frozen, being pinged to death by forstbolts. You might or might not have ninjad a healing item earlier to make flask trinket more effective.

 **Arms warrior**
You don't care about PVE, people usually find you in BGs (unless you just got whirlwind axe and feel like the manliest man in Azeroth). You need your pocket healer and/or a guy with blessing of freedom on to be a fully functional adult though. Mortal strike is cool, big melee damage is cool. Under the right setup and supporting cast you can kinda become an angel of death and destruction. Under the right setup. Chances are that frost mages and rogues caused you so much PTSD that you are either a gnome or an orc. Schwarzenegger or Danny DeVito. We understand, immersion < racials.

 **Prot pala**
At one point in your life you were a prot warrior tanking Stratholme and said 'fck it' after the 20th big undead pack in a row where your thunderclap/shout combo worked like a water move on Pikachu and all of those undeads were happily beating up everyone, including the healer. Since that day you hate undeads and big packs. Let's face it, 90% of the dungeons is about big packs. So you rerolled prot pala, righteous fury on, consec on CD, seal is up and you finally don't feel like a retard every time you have to pull a pack with 3+ mobs. You also give people blessings. Most people like you except casters because sometimes you roll for their items.

 **Ret pala**
You're either an oldschool pala gamer who is happy that non-healer palas are finally viable or you follow the PVP meta. But honestly, people like having you in their party. Cool blessings, decent PVE damage, offheal options (you won't do it), even rez (you won't do it). Chances are you were thinking about rolling a warrior but you also didn't want to súck at PVP.

 **Spellret**
Same as above, except you have a shaman BFF and flametongue goes brr every time you two play.

 **Holy pala**
You're a utility powerhouse just like the other palas. You are the guy to BoP that pesky warlock who pulled threat again (but usually you won't do it). You basically use 1 heal but it's so good that you can keep up a tank for one week without being oom. You probably hate drinking (and complexity in healing) and you're effective. Damn effective.

 **Feral bear**
You wanna try out all roles but mostly wanna tank. That's already a big plus, people love tanks. You're also a giant meatball, killing you takes at least 1000 hits with a spoon. The best part about you is your unique utility: much less drinking time because you keep innervating the healer (sometimes), a nice buff on everyone, leader of the pack for melee and you even HoT yourself before pulls which is really cool. At PVP though you are big enemy number 2 because people... just can't... kill you.

 **Feral cat**
At one point in your life you said 'fck the meta' and since then you live by that. You can provide the usual druid utility and you go after the weirdest items - people usually love you for that, except for maybe the rogues. People know that you're chill - no tryhard plays cat, and girls playing TWOW will simp you because you're cute.

 **No shapeshift bonk with a staff druid**
I swear there has to be a guy like this. Probably specced as balance. Not very effective but teammates will roll on the floor laugning, which is good.

 **Moonkin**
At one point in your life you were thinking what's your favourite animal. Owl? Bear? A ram? After some thinking you were like why pick one and went moonkin. Casters love you for your cool aura, your animations are hilarious and you're usually fun to be around. Let's face it, there are two kinds of moonkin players. There are the hypersensitive, moody ones (people will ignore them after a while) and there are the hug-bears with a dad body. If you're the latter type, your guild loves you.

 **Resto druid**
Tree or not a tree, you give great utility - specially if you're a tree (to be honest). HoT here, HoT there, making healers heal bigger and innervating someone who can actually hea... khm... and innervating a priest. People like you because you're usually a walking tree with HP regeneration characteristics. Healers love you because you make them heal bigger. Dungeon party loves you considerably less after each time they have to corpserun because plant healer can't properly rezz.

 **Elemental shaman**

Oh boy. After the guild lead trying to persuade you for 5 hours straight to respec to resto and failed, the guild will still take you to raids. Totem utility is still there. You have a kinda decent threat IF you use tranquil air totem (it won't happen, you'll be in a melee group with WF). Let's face it, your threat will be a disaster and you'll be usually dead after 3 seconds after the pull. At least you can offheal (you're actually kinda OK with that) and if tank somehow has infinite threat, you have infinite mana and target has zero nat rez, you can actually output a disgusting amount of damage. Also, you are PVP public enemy number three. Spammable purge with double buff removal is nasty. As long as allies can keep the targets far from you, you are a huge menace.

 **Shaman tank**
People... love you... if they waited at LFT for two hours and at least you pop up. Like a Chinese bootleg version of a tank. At least you give some good utility and party members can contemplate on their life between pulls because you will drink to full every time a mob died. You can be kinda decent actually if the DPS only attacks skull (won't happen) and you know how to tank with them (probably won't happen). Seriously your only loveable part is that the dungeon can finally start, tank is here.

 **BL resto**
People like you for the huge melee utility you bring, including bloodlust. Your heal is decent, too, you are kinda similar to disc priests at high raids. Melee goes brr, chain heal goes brr, mightiest pénis DPS gets BL every 5 min. Life is good.

 **Enhancement shaman**
You are the bootleg version of BL resto. People still love you for the same utility you bring and tolerate that you wanna auto-attack instead of chain heal (unless you have a Nightfall, then things are all good). There can be some conflicts when you wanna BL yourself all the time but after the 2nd lootban it gets sorted out. Even without the added healing, you make a DPS party go full Rambo, so people are okay with you.

 **Resto shaman**
You are the BL resto guy if we take out some melee utility for a bit of healing and we take out BL for a bit better mana management. People still love you because, if geared, you can make an insane amount of heal, usually topping charts. Custom items and tea made your biggest weakness, mana a non-issue.

 **Tank rogue**
Just kidding, since the avoidance nerf noone plays tank rogue.

 **Subtlety**
People love you... If you are at the same faction... ehm... And they really love seeing lvl 10 guys from the other faction die. Honestly, you are full PVP and are extremely effective (in the most digusting way possible) so for your teammates, it's all good. Except if your target is an orc. Fck orcs, stun resist is BS, ganking playstyle is oppressed, pls give fan of knives.

 **Combat**
At least you can sap, kick, open boxes, make BWL better and have poison for your offhand (mainhand bis poison is WF totem). In PVE you are a budget fury warrior but even a budget fury warrior is a good DPS. Seriously TWOW, make rogues more interesting, I'm trying to be positive here but all they have is PVP stunlock, lockpicking and below-fury melee DPS for raids. The problem with PVE rogues is that they are good but there is one class doing the same niche just better at lategame. And rogue utility (?) doesn't make up for it, maybe except for expose armor. Still loving you guys, I guess.

 **Assassination**
Just like combat but you have an inexplicable brain damage.

 **BM hunter**
You like leveling fast and easy. People also tend to underestimate you during dungeoning. You actually make a huge DPS at that part of the game while offering some utility, too. Leveling players having a Shagu DPS installed love you at least. You're also a beast (pun intended) in PVP in a good team. People just underestimate how useful good hunters are because there are way too many mediocre hunters.

 **Marksmanship hunter**
You probably swapped to MM at level 60. You have decent damage (even good damage if played by an experienced player) and some must-have raid utility. Traps here, kiting there, tranquilizing shot, nat rez, trueshot aura, even mana drain. In my eyes hunter is the 'overlooked' class, few people realize how much they bring to the table.

 **Survival hunter**
Hmm... Let's stay positive... You liked Jurassic Park. Raptors are awesome. Raptor strike has the word raptor in it. Honestly, everything you do can be done just as well by the others while actually dealing damage. At least you are not a meta gamer and everyone sees that you play the game to enjoy the damn game. Also, your tree has some really cool utility talent for other builds.

 **Arcane mage**
You used to be a joke and now you are kinda cool. Your party mana regen doesn't seem to be much until people realize that innervate and evocate is a combo. From that moment you are a core member of healer and caster parties at any raids where mana management can become an issue. You are so damn mana effective that you tend to forget that drinks exist. Not the biggest DPS mage but possibly the coolest one.

 **Frost mage**
An other cool mage spec (hehe). The nightmare of arms warriors, the king of leveling and aoe farming and a good DPS option until AQ40. You are most probably a pesky, unkillable little gnome blinking away from those enemies and everyone moves like an 80 years old geriatric. You can, without irony, solo SM Cathedral with one giga pull at level 60. You just watch all of those crusaders crawling towards you like it's a zombie apocalypse before blinking away. Just don't be angry at me pls, I don't wanna corpserun.

 **Fire mage**
You are probably at least AQ40 geared and like those big numbers. Honestly, you are the most physically fragile weapon of mass destruction I have ever seen. People like you because... I mean people like if the enemy dies quick and raid is fast and smooth. You have the usual mage utility just like the others but you might be the least willing to use them instead of DPS-ing just because your numbers are that big.

 **Disc priest**
People always tell you how cool it would be if you joined BG. But honestly, priests joining BG are shadow priest. You are a disc because your guild is farming Naxx already and PI makes runs faster. Your mana pool is big, your healing is... okay but you can make a fire mage really happy every 3 min. You just wish PW:Shield scaled better with +healing. I do, too, my friend, I do, too.

 **Holy priest**
You like healing. I mean, you selected the build with the best healing toolkit in the game. HoT, big heal, fast heal, party heal, shield, even a fcking fountain of useless light... They name it, you have it. People like you for this healing output, specially since healers are kinda hard to find. You're probably a dwarf because the game somehow introduced a core class ability as a racial. Also, desperate prayer is a lifesaver. If you join a dungeon, people probably won't die while your DPS is also cool. Pain and wands go a long way. You decided to play exactly one role in the game but you wanna play that role to the fullest. Also, some proclaim champion abilities are so bad they feel like the creators combod whiskey and cocaine when creating them.

 **Smite priest**
You... Definitely exist. People still don't know, why, though. I guess, this is the way you enjoy the game. You wanted to DPS as a priest but as a good guy, not as a schizo-edgelord which glows like an action toy figure from the 90s. Don't get me wrong, you output an okay damage and I hope class changes make your build more viable. People kinda like you in a dungeon because you are so rare, it's a nice experience to see you once in a while.

 **Shadow priest**
You listen to Finnish death metal, your hair is painted blue, you love Death Note and would explain anyone who's willing to listen why misantropy is the only justified base feeling towards life and society. But still, warlocks will simp you for that juicy shadow weaving. If you're geared, even a tank party would simp you for the passive heals. You also like PVP and you probably spend half of your free time on the forums writing posts about why mindflay should target multiple people. Chances are that you are the best PVP player on the server (if not, then a pala, a druid, a lock, an ele shaman or a freeze mage). Also, you can just jump out of shadowform and offheal pretty well when needed (you're kinda okay with that). TWOW did a pretty decent job with your class and you know it. Honestly, it's good to see finally 1+ shadow priest in a raid.

 **PVE warlock**
I won't mention all of the variants people make because the gameplay is very similar. DS/Ruin is almost SM/Ruin, just more shadow damage instead of the imp buff. SM/DS is an old concept and people leave it once they see a DPS chart. Full affli is fun and saves drinking time at leveling. But still, the core is the same and people love you for that.
You bring everyone's áss to the raid entrance then offer (kinda useless) cookies. You SS a healer (healer is happy). You might or might not commit homicide on a demon for buffs. Enemy comes, enemy gets a curse, shadowbolt goes brr, shadowburn for the finish, rinse and repeat. You banish, too, which is very cool in MC.
Once you wanted to give a firestone to a fire mage but priest had to rezz him because the guy died of laughter. You do some very decent damage and give some great utility, too. Just watch your threat, please.

 **PVP warlock**
Now, to the PVP landscape. You either create a big bulky demonology warlock who just refuses to die or you prefer a glass cannon with disgusting damage (conflag warlock). Seriously, a good conflag warlock melts everyone's face in 3 sec max. Pick your poison, you wanna be a nearly unkillable cloth user (?) or a big daddy big boom kinda guy? Teammates will love you. Chances are you are horde because PVP-ing as a warlock against undeads with will of the forsaken is roughly as enjoyable as pleasuring yourself with a razor blade.
