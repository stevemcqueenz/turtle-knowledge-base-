So read the ideas, give me your opinion, add to it and hopefully we can see this at some point in the game. I hope you like it.

Caverns under fireblooom ridge - A dungeonconcept for level 55 - 60

The complete dungeon uses the map of wailling caverns. Water gets exchanged with lava and the map is done. To get to winding chasm, you ever have to lavarun or we need some stones for jumps.

Three caverns:
In the west (Pit of fangs) there are bloodspetals and pterrordax
The center with the lavastream (dreamers rock and screaming gully) is filled with fireelementals and incendosaurs
The east (winding chasm) is nest of devilsaurs.

Bosses of the caverns
In the west (Pit of fangs) there is a giant-bloodpatel and a pterrordax boss (probably a fear mechanic). Nothing special here.
In the center, there is an Incendosaur is swimming in the lava. Main mechanic: Apply a really strong bleed mechanic (Reason see quests). Second ability is a fireball volley.
At dreamers rock is a fire elemental boss „Core oft he vulcano“. Designconcept: It starts at lvl+3 and every x seconds, the boss splits into 2 smaller bosses with lvl-1. Either wait for it to be weak and aoe or kill it fast.
In the west you can face the spirit of lor’kowi and instead of mutanus the everliving, you can kill queen mosh. For queen mosh a call of king mosh would be a nice mechanic. Maybe we need a 3. boss here. Maybe a dino from terror run?

Quests:
All the quests are followups for existing quests. Followup to…
… bait of larkorwi: The dino ist not dead! The spirit still roams under the vulcano. Banish it once and forever!
… Lost!: Ringo found caverns under the ridge! Explore them! (Get 3 items at the spots at the end of east, west and centercavern)
… Incendosaur? More like whateversaur: Get a perfect incendosaur scale (drop off the boss). Everyone did this quest before, so everyone has a luffa. The very strong bleed effect of the boss can be countered by everyone... if you play this as intended.
… bloodpetal zapper: Use the boodpatel zapper on the plant-boss. Maybe the boss gets stronger if you use the zapper?

Loot ideas:
Patterns: Everliving set (Upgrade of living set to t1 level)
Pattern Ironhide set (Upgrade devilsaur set to t1 level)
Additional pieces to Devilsaur and Everliving set to make it a 5 piece set.
