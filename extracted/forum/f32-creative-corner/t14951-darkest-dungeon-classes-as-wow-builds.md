# Darkest Dungeon classes as WOW builds

## Post 101758 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-23T13:36:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101758#p101758 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

Wanted to write an article about this for a while now.
Darkest Dungeon is a game I really like and also has different classes. The game itself works very differently but I wanna draw a comparison between these platforms arguing which DD class would play as which WOW build.
Target audience is those who know and love Darkest Dungeon.

Disclaimer: besides health, the main resource a character has in DD is stress level; since WOW has no equivalent to stress and DD lacks resources like mana, rage and energy, I handled stress-healing abilities as general utility abilities.

 **Crusader**
Crusader has to be a paladin, a prot pala to be precise. It wears plate, can do some offheals and has really good general utility. Flavorwise both represent the 'rightful crusader' and crusaders also have gimmicks associated with the light (as they can increase torchlight). Bulwark of faith can be considered a taunt as the crusader is marking himself.
Tldr: DD crusader is a prot paladin.

 **Highwayman**
Combat rogue, can't be anything else. A high damage-low utility class which heavily relies on riposte to also deal damage at the opponent's turn. Flavor-wise it screams combat rogue. Has some gun gimmicks which combat rogues rarely use but at least rogues can wear guns, too.
Tldr: DD highwayman is a combat rogue.

 **Grave Robber**
I had to highlight 'combat' at HWM because here we have the subtlety rogue with grave robber. Extreme avoidance combined with stealth and some DoTs here and there. They match perfectly.
Tldr: DD grave robber is 100% subtlety rogue.

 **Hellion**
A bit weird one. Definitely feels like a fury warrior, does damage like a fury warrior, is a fury warrior but can always be seen with a two-hander. Still, fury >> arms for her. Extreme melee damage and a huge damaging toolkit. Reasonably bulky but not too bulky. Her whole character screams barbaric rage. Basically a barbarian with a two-hander.
Tldr: DD hellion is a fury warrior.

 **Man-at-Arms**
Prot warrior. Has to be a prot warrior. Guarding talents, increasing protection into oblivion. Extremely hard to kill and can redirect damage towards himself and can proc some riposte damage as if it were 'revenge'. His pure damage is not his strong side. Has some stuns, some shouts. If geared properly and has some support, he is almost impossible to kill and he makes sure that none of your characters are killed.
Tldr: DD man-at-arms is a prot warrior.

 **Vestal**
Holy priest. A very good healbot, also has many ways to heal (basically greater heal and prayer of healing). Her damage contribution is minimal, best she can usually do is some stuns. If you bring one, people will never be at low HP but bringing her usually hurts your overall damage output.
Tldr: holy priest; way too focused on the healing aspect to be disc and she is the only character in the game with aoe heal, can't be holy pala.

 **Jester**
This was a bit hard since jester is mostly a stress healer which is an unknown concept in WOW. At the end of the day I went with ele shaman because of two reasons.
Flavor: jester is a weirdo, heavily relying on utility.
Mechanics: if I consider stress healing general utility than jester is a utility powerhouse with some limited damage and the option to very rarely do some real bs at the frontlines before running to the back. Battle ballad is giving up your turn to give big party buffs, almost feels like WF. Jester has no actual healing, resto is excluded from this. Finale feels like the LB+nature swiftness CL+shock ele sometimes pulls on CD and just like that, it can't be spammed. Frequently wanted by parties but mostly for the utility.
Tldr: DD jester might be ele shaman. Flavor doesn't really match, gameplay somewhat does. Small resemblance though.

 -> If we go more modern and ignore that jesters are rarely dragons, an augmentation evoker would work even better for him. Not sure, I don't know evokers well though.

 **Houndmaster**
BM hunter. 120% BM hunter. Relying greatly on the pet. Honestly, the only pet class. I don't know what to say, 120% BM hunter.
tldr: Totally BM hunter.

 **Arbalest/Musketeer**
MM hunter. These 2 (1?) are the classes based around ranged-damage dealing from the backlines using projectile weapons. They are also very placement-dependent, they are mostly useless at the frontlines.
tldr: DD arbalest and musketeer are MM hunters.

 **Abomination**
The shapeshifter of the crew. Abo is that feral druid guy who causes heart attack in the crew when going bear (or something like that). Great melee damage when transformed, okay utility in normal form which can be a parallel to the generic druid utility.
tldr: DD abomination is a really spooky version of a feral druid, probably a roleplayer.

 **Occultist**
The flavor screems warlock, the gameplay not so much. He has some great debuffs which we can imagine as curses/DoTs and has a strong connection to demons but his offhealing is weird for a warlock. Imagine him giving away heartstones which sometimes just bleed you to death. I have to go with affliction warlock because of the flavor though and I pretend that I've never seen the guy healing others.
tldr: DD occultist is an affliction warlock with a lot of bandages at hand.

 **Bounty Hunter**
Was thinking a lot for this one but my best bet is an assassination rogue. A high-crit damage dealer with some added utility (just like improved expose armor or improved kidney shot). Has many ways to stun and also has DoT (albeit, in the form of bleed). Flavorwise assassination rogue matches: he is a guy being paid to make someone disappear.
tldr: DD bounty hunter is an assassination rogue.

 **Flagellant**
Sorry for breaking the Vanilla+ experience. In my defence, the guy came at a later DLC. 120% blood death knight.
tldr: 120% blood death knight.

 **Plague Doctor**
Sorry for breaking the Vanilla+ immersion again but here we have an unholy death knight right here. It matches her perfectly, not sure what else I can say here.
tldr: We have an unholy death knight right here.

 **Leper**
A guy hitting like a truck but having real range problems, capable of self-buffing and self-healing. At first I wanted to go arms with him but if we're honest, leper is an overgeared retri paladin who somehow forgot to collect hit chance at his items. His self-buffs and self-heals push him to be a retri, not an arms. Also, the most independent character, perfectly happy farming alone many mobs at once.
tldr: DD leper is the ret pala.

 **Shield Breaker**
It's very hard. Imagine a traumatized DPS monk with a heavy emphasis on poisioning/blighting the targets. Probably a windwalker monk. Kinda squishy but can dodge decently. Very high speed and a stupid amount of damage which is sometimes followed by DoTs. Flavorwise also a character inspired by the exotic Orient.
tldr: DD shield breaker is a windwalker monk (maybe).
