# Shadowmeld for all races!

## Post 114759 by Gladeshadow (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-29T13:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114759#p114759 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Thank you for clicking the bait subject title! But this truly is a post about what would in effect be a (lesser) version of shadowmeld available to everyone.

It would be remiss of me not to mention that night elf shadowmeld should be restored to its 10 second cooldown. Heck, they can keep the change of 2 seconds of shadowmelded walking. Now, on to the main topic at hand!

The survival profession still has potential. This is just one craftable that could be added to it:
Camouflage: Grants the user stealth until they move or perform an action.
Much like a tent, this camouflage may require reagents in order to be built, similar to how camouflage is often created in by real life hunters using cloth to create blinds, gillie suits, and the like. The camouflage might also be a trinket. While this is less in flavor of being a uniquely created camo setup in a given area, it is in some ways reasonable as a portable blind. As a trinket, using it would also mean other valuable trinket space would be unavailable, creating a tradeoff choice for the user to only use camouflage situationally.

As for reasons to add this mechanic to survival and to the game as a whole, the skill logically makes sense in the survival tree; camouflage is a survival skill. As for stepping on anyone's toes, camouflage requiring reagents, a trinket slot, and having a cooldown longer than that of shadowmeld address many potentially foreseen issues - here I suggest the CD be one minute while night elf shadowmeld CD gets restored to 10 seconds. Night elves would never have a reason to use camouflage, freeing their bag and equip space for other goods, so they also gain some advantage over those who are required to take up their inventory space if they wish to use camouflage.
Addressing uniqueness, I think the caveats I've specified for camouflage make it in general less desirable than what would be the superior night elf racial. Considering other racials, most activated racials have versions of themselves, and often superior versions, that are activated by some kind of item in game. For example:
Will of the Forsaken - pvp trinkets replicate the effect. Some other items and class abilities are also similar to the effect
Warstomp - many bombs and grenades have a similar effect
Blood fury - many trinkets in game have a stronger effect than blood fury without the drawbacks
Escape artist - again, pvp trinkets. Also, free action potions
Perception - weather the old or new version, engineer items and cats eye elixir improved stealth detection. A lot of trinkets grant AP on activation, which is functionally similar to the new crit version of perception
Etc., etc.

The addition of camouflage in game would also mean a player is not forced to pick a night elf for this perhaps unduly unique function. Personally, I'd love its addition. I don't always want to play a night elf just so I have the ability to hide that all other races lack and currently have no way of emulating.
