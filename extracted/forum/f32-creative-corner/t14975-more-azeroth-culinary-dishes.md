# More Azeroth culinary dishes!

## Post 101891 by Reploidrocsa (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-09-25T14:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101891#p101891 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Coming from the post of my desire for more food/drink facilities in the world

[viewtopic.php?p=71713](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71713)

I went a step further and observed Azeroth world: so many different places, biomes and cultures, yet so few food/drink variety. So after some study of race lore, zones and some ideas taken from real world recipes, i've came up with some culinary dishes that ideally would be sold in specific zones or looted by humanoids only. Feels very weird to me that Scarlet monastery zealots drop Stormwind Brie instead of supplying on closer food sources.

That being said, after some race research i've came up with some general conclusions:

 -Humans like to mix strong with mild flavours.
 -Dwarves love strong and hearty flavours.
 -Gnomes usually copy dwarven recipes and artificially enrich them with additives and tend to add some form of improvements to the packaging or conservation to make it more convinient to use and transport.
 -Night elves like to distill and juice fresh fruits and vegetables.
 -High elves prefer mild flavours.

 -Orcs like acid and meaty flavours.
 -Trolls don't care too much what they eat, but they lean towards seafood for convinience.
 -Undead prefer to either preserve their food or go for very strong and foul flavours. Spicy is also very common among fantasy undead in general (probably because it's a flavour you "taste" it with your pain receptors rather than taste buds)
 -Tauren like mild solid foods and strong flavoured drinks.
 -Goblins promote foods that are "tourist traps" or "scammy", keeping the good food for themselves.
