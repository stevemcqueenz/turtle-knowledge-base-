# Fictive builds: The warlock tank

## Post 97055 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-15T12:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97055#p97055 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

Below article is about theorycrafting with the purpose of fun, it is not a proposal.
I wanna imagine how a warlock tank in TWOW would look like. Will create a custom talent tree for it which will use many entries from the current demonology tree (usually reworked, to skew everything towards tanking).

With my lock I did some tanking at lower levels (biggest challenge was earlier BRD). Based on that I could see some of its strengths, weaknesses and some misconceptions people tend to have.

 **+**
 * Despite popular belief and despite wearing only cloth, warlocks can be quite bulky. Even bulky enough to tank certain encounters. Talents like Demonic Embrace, Demonic Aegis, Master Demonologist (voidwalker) and Soul Link help a lot.
 * Warlocks can range pull.
 * Warlocks have a surprisingly good AoE-threat with hellfire. It also means that you damage yourself, too though.
 * Searing pain is a good single-target threat spell as long as you can ensure that casting time increase because of being hit doesn't happen too often.
 * Your voidie has a 'weak' taunt
 * Your voidie has sacrifice which is a perfect defensive CD, also helps with spell interruption problems.

 **-**
 * Barely any itemization options (need cloth big with stam, defense and spell damage).
 * AOE threat also damages you.
 * Single-target threat can be interrupted / cast time can increase.
 * Pet taunt is weak.

 **What the custom talent tree would focus on:**
 * Creating a double-tanking playstyle where both you and your pet build up threat and you can taunt via voidie.
 * Making voidie bulkier.
 * Threat-increase for destruction spells.
 * Reducing hellfire damage in exchange of bigger threat.
 * Making searing pain reliably castable.
 * Making voidie taunt more reliable.
 * Warlock tanking would only focus on voidie, talent buffs would only focus on this pet. Other pets are ignored here.

 **How the talent tree would look like**

 **1st row:**
 * Demonic Embrace (5): 15% stamina for 5% spirit. Also gives you defense based on 10% of your total stamina.
 * Improved Voidwalker (3): Increasing voidie spell effectiveness by 30% and the threat it generates by 30%.
 * Improved torment (2): Reducing the chance of your target to resist torment by n% (has to be tested).

 **2nd row:**
 * Dark Caster (5): You can't be interrupted when casting searing pain, and hellfire deals 100% less damage to you.
 * Improved Health Funnel (3): Changes health funnel into a HoT spell which can only be casted on your pets. Pet recharges n% of his health every 5 sec (has to be tested) while health funnel is on him.
 * Threat Pact (3): Increases the threat generated by your destruction spells by 45% but reduces the damage they deal by 30%.

 **3rd row:**
 * Fel Domination (1): The next Voidie you summon will become an instant cast and will not cost mana (15 min CD).
 * Demonic Aegis (5): Makes Demonic Skin and Armor 50% more effective.
 * Fel stamina (5): Increases voidie HP and armor by 50%.

 **4th row:**
 * Soul Collector (1): Killing a target with any destruction spell gives you a soul shard.
 * Touching Flames (5): Reduces the chance that your target resists your destruction spells by n% (has to be tested).

 **5th row:**
Master Demonologist (5): If your voidie is summoned, reduces the physical damage you take by 10% and n% of your HP is getting refilled every 5 sec.

 **6th row:**
 * Hellish Resistance (1): Gives you a 75% chance to resist silence effects and prevents you from being locked out from casting a school of magic for a certain amount of time.

 **7th row:**
 * Soul Link (1): When active, 30% of the damage you take is taken by the voidie. In addition, you both take 10% less damage from any source. Medium CD-spell.

 **What would the end result look like:**
A very bulky but relatively low damage caster, accompanied by a very bulky but relatively low damage pet.
The warlock sets up target prio via symbols, and either range pulls with rain of fire/searing pain or runs in and starts hellfiring.
Any mob with threat problems receives a searing pain.
Any mob which needs direct taunt is tormented by voidie.
Voidie always has health funnel on.
If HP gets low, soul link is activated.
If HP gets really low, voidie can be sacrificed and master demonologist can immdiately resummon it.
Silence is a problem, but happens rarely thanks to hellish resistence. Silencing mobs can be OT-d far away by someone else, if needed. Voidwalker can also be used to tank a silencing mob outside of the silence's radius.
If the target has high fire resistence, touching flames talent + CoE can be used.

Strengths of this tank:
 * Range-pull with the option of range AoE-pull.
 * Very good aoe-threat.
 * Good single-target-threat.
 * Passive health regen.
 * Two very good defensive CDs.

Weaknesses of this tank:
 * Still wears cloth.
 * No shield, no parry.
 * High fire rez mobs.
