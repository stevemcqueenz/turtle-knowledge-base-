# A Tide of Red - A Quest Chain to put the Defias Brotherhood in a new light

## Post 155299 by Shambakriger — 2025-10-12T19:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155299#p155299 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi!
I've pretty much retired from playing Turtle WoW, but seeing the promo material for 1.18 made me want to atleast try the new levelling zone of Northwind as I've always especially enjoyed the writing of Turtle WoW when it comes to showcasing the political tensions of the various kingdoms in Azeroth.
Playing through Northwind reignited a thought in me that I had quite a long time ago surrounding the Defias Brotherhood: Why do people so desperately want to root for the Defias (ie, "Vancleef did nothing wrong!" etc) when all ingame lore portrays them as absolute murderous maniacs?
People like villains who are relatable, unfortunately WoW is not a game that allows for a lot of nuance due to its quite simple, linear structure (compared to an RPG like Baldur's Gate). This means it's not really feasible for players to take a decidedly "evil" path, as in taking the sides of the villains (sorry to everyone who's still praying for the option to defect to the Scarlet Crusade).
What I propose instead is a somewhat simpler way to add nuance, by instead casting doubt on the intentions of the supposedly "good" side, in this case the Kingdom of Stormwind.
Here is a TL;DR of the (purpose) of the story:
Highlighting a diversity of thought in the early days of the Defias Brotherhood, giving creedence to their original motives of simply having their debt paid, and showing their descent into madness and villainy, led by Vancleef.

And here is a (fairly long) TL;DR of the story:
While clearing up the riot in the Stormwind Stockades you come across the badly mutilated body of an old man. It appears to have been the work of the prisoners themselves. Speaking to Warden Thelwater outside the dungeon, he lets you know that the slain man is Warwick Poole, practically Defias nobility, being one of the brotherhoods founders. This piques your interest, and you decide to investigate further by visiting the City Registry. Speaking to the Royal Factor, you learn that Warwick was transferred from the Stormwind Vaults to the Stockades just recently, but that the transfer order has gone missing. Left without a lead you head downstairs but get stopped by Baros Alexston. Having overheard your conversation about Warwick, Baros, a former stonemason himself, hands you a letter that he'd like you to deliver to an old friend of his, "Archie". Through a few twist and turns you end up finding Archie in Northwind, where he has taken up work as a lumberjack under the name of Arthur Stonefist. Archie, real name Archibald Tunnover, reveals to you that, he too, is a former stonemason, and one of the founders of the Defias Brotherhood. He tells you the story of the Brotherhoods early days, about bitter ideological rivalries, and how it went from being a movement of justice to a movement fueled only by greed, hatred and revenge. Puzzled by the timing of Warwicks transfer and subsequent murder relevant to the riot, you and Archie decide to investigate further. This leads you to speaking with a corrupt prison warden, who insinuates that the Defias themselves might have been behind the transfer of Warwick, which leads to spying on a noble and uncovering that it was in fact not the Defias Brotherhood, but the SI:7 who ordered the transfer, merely framing the Defias.
Searching for more information, you kill a top-ranking Defias member stationed in Northwind, carrying a letter from Vancleef himself. The letter states that their "friend in Stormwind" has ordered the withdrawal of forces from Northwind, giving the Defias free reign to hunt for Archibald, under the cover of their standard affair of looting and pillaging, and that she has made arrangements for the "demise" of Warwick. Once Archibald reads the letter, he can finally see the full picture, the picture that got Warwick murdered. He tells you to make haste, you must retrieve from a chest buried in Elwynn his old stash of Defias pamphlets. It is time to let the people of Stormwind know the truth!
Digging up the chest you find that most of its contents have been lost to the elements, except for an old ring and a hammer, both bearing the symbol of the Stonemasons Guild. Returning to Archibald you find that he is gone, and in his place is a single letter bearing the seal of Stormwind. It is addressed to you, summoning you to the SI:7 headquarters. Mathias Shaw greets you and informs you that you needn't worry about Archibald, as you won't be seeing him again. He thanks you for inadvertantly leading the SI:7 to the last of the Defias Brotherhoods old leadership, but warns you of ever trying to collude against Stormwind again.

The quest ends on a very open note in a very morally grey area. The "friend" in Stormwind is obviously Lady Katrana Prestor, and this quest chain is a way of hinting further towards her nefarious plans before the big reveal with Marshal Windsor later in the game. It also serves to cast doubt on the kingdom of Stormwind as a whole, seeing just how deep the corruption runs. If the SI:7 is unknowingly doing the bidding of Onyxia, then how can any of our motives be safe?
The Defias Brotherhood is unsalvageable, but once they had a shred of relatibility due to their opposition to unjust, corrupt nobles. And that relatibility is reignited by showcasing that corruption thrives just as well now as it did back then.

In the replies I'm going to post my attempt at fully mapping/fleshing out the quest. I have zero experience with either writing or implementation, and all of this was written in one evening, so view it more as an overall guideline:))) All names and all objectives are pretty much up for changing, the only core thing is the message of the story, I hope you'll enjoy it!
