# Fan Soundtrack – Quel’Thalas Complete Score & Suite

## Post 152562 by Celgirin — 2025-09-21T13:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152562#p152562 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey everyone! I’m Celgirin, a part-time composer from Sweden.

I'm a long-time fan of Turtle WoW and I’ve always admired the work of József Kiss. His music sets the tone for the server in such a unique way. That said, when I spent more time in Thalassian Highlands, I felt the zone deserved something deeper, something that carried more of its history. This region has always inspired me, so I poured a lot of time, love, and creative energy into writing a full set of themes for it.

I’ve put together two versions:

Quel’Thalas – Complete Score
All themes and motifs with descriptions

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/P69-FHsNpUQ]

Quel’Thalas – Suite
A 13-minute overture that gathers everything into one piece

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/Ba1r9HOEi6A]

 *I’d love for you to listen and share your thoughts. Do the motifs fit the history? Do they catch the feeling of the High Elves rebuilding after Warcraft III? Feedback means a lot, and I’d be happy to discuss ideas with anyone interested in music and lore.*

Below is how I imagined each theme and motif with each respective timestamp from the Complete Score:

[00:00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ) - Glory to the Quel'Dorei - Gates of Alah Thalas
A grand, solemn rise out of loss with massive choir, like the sun lifting over the land. A statement of pride and resilience, a High Elf wonder. Sunrise follows as the gentler part, warm and ceremonial.

[00:02:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=148s) - Kin in Blood - Main
The rift between High Elves and Blood Elves: once one people, now divided. Pride, memory, and mistrust share the same roots and the same sun, yet walk apart.

[00:05:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=325s) - Kin in Blood - Under One Sun
Hints of Eversong through cello and harp, holding beauty and loss. A shared need for magic, divided paths: arcane against fel. Echoes of the Sunwell in the cello’s tone.

[00:08:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=511s) - Kin in Blood - Memory of Silvermoon
A mournful reflection on former wonders, glimpsed only in rebuilding. The supernatural lingers in soaring spires and living stairways, commanding respect and recalling their greatness.

[00:14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=863s) - Kin in Blood - Among the Shaded Boughs
Quiet steps in enchanted woods where beauty meets unease. Once kin, now wary, they share paths but not trust, with shadows watching from the canopy.

[00:18:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=1128s) - Citadel of the Sun
Resilience and longing in bright upward lines, hope balanced with memory of loss, echoing a reach for former glory.

[00:21:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=1275s) - Eternal Autumn - Light in the Now
Calm in a golden forest, where even in exile there is room to breathe. Peaceful but not idle, a reminder that healing takes work while life goes on. It captures the present, honoring nature’s glow and the tasks ahead.

[00:27:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=1642s) - Eternal Autumn - Main
Life in a season that never ends: golden woods holding both calm and decay. Beauty of the present, weight of change, and new bonds as the world fades.

[00:30:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=1838s) - Eternal Autumn - Sun and Moon
Elves learning to work together. A gentle circling, careful but willing. Sun and moon, different yet connected, seeking balance.

[00:34:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=2090s) - Three Sisters Young - Main
A theme for the Windrunner sisters because they’re iconic and still central. From youth and shared duty to separation and tragedy, holding the memory of their bond and the weight of loss.

[00:36:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=2194s)- Three Sisters Young - Draw and Release
Training and the hunt: silent steps, whispered signals. The craft of the bow and the thrill of shared skill, a time when they were together.

[00:38:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=2304s) - Three Sisters Young - Bows Before Banners
Their youth together, bound by duty and by each other. Loyalty and shared purpose, with a foreshadowed split. Unity rises, then fades.

[00:44:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=2663s) - Three Sisters Young - Last Breath of the Ranger-General
The moment Sylvanas falls: time slows, cold seeps in, memories fade. Then the shift, the world rises as she sinks, and the transformation begins.

[00:47:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=2824s) - Three Sisters Young - Traces in the Scar
Through the Dead Scar carved by Arthas. Quiet, heavy, a broken promise. Sylvanas and Vereesa carry the loss in the land itself, while Alleria is far away, the news crossing worlds. Her absence deepens the separation.

[00:51:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=3071s) - Dawn of a New High Kingdom
Like sunrise, with hope of taking root and finding peace in a new land. Beneath the light lies the memory of Silvermoon, and a shared resolve to flourish in what remains.

[00:55:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=3301s) - Thalassian Glades
Stillness in the forest where light falls open and free in the clearings. Harps reflect nature as it is: unaware.

[00:59:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P69-FHsNpUQ&t=3584s)- Glory to the Quel’dorei - Sunrise
The gentler close to Glory: warmth and ceremony without the towering heights.
