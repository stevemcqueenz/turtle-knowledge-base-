How it would work
It builds upon three things:
 * You have a pet
 * You have high dodge and parry
 * You have many tools to narrow down the active targets (wyvern sting, trap, pet OT)

This build has all the defensives a hunter can get. Deterrence is your emergency defensive CD.
Your main advantage as a tank (?) is to be able to narrow down the size of the pack you need to tank at a time. Let's say there is a pack of 5 mobs which you want to pull. Freezing trap down, it will take care of one mob. Wyvern sting pull (makes sure another one will sleep for 12 sec). If needed, sending out your pet for a 3rd target. This way you only need to tank 2 mobs at a time even if the pull had 5 mobs. If there is added CC in the party, like poly, you can take the mobs 1 by 1. This means a slow but a very unique dungeon progression.

Besides your survivability the other focus is the survivability of your pet. Left out damaging talents, the pet would play a role to OT a target which is not damaged by anyone else, its main goal is just to survive.

With your very high dodge+parry chance, if you only get hit by 1-2 elites at most, the fact that you can't wear a shield is less important.

One more thing to note at the talents: aspect swapping. If you need threat, it's wolf, if you need survival, it's monkey. At rare cases when you just want to kite a target around while DPS kills 1-2 non-elites, it can even be cheetah. Also, I'm not sure if distracting shot is in the game. If yes, switching out from wolf and using it can be a part of threat generating rotation.

So key things: narrowing down active targets, dodging/parrying.
In case of emergency you can always call back pet and growl on the target where you've lost threat.
In case of huge non-elite packs you can help with aoe damage trap or slowing trap and let aoe casters do they thing.

Weaknesses:
 * No shield
 * Wearing mail is kinda okay, but not ideal (shamans can meme-tank in mail but they have a shield, too)
 * No spammable +threat ability (distracting shot?) - DPS has to watch meters
 * Slow progression - the key is to CC half of the packs on pull instead of running in and just killing them

Things I'd consider for this build:
 * Getting the +2% dodge Kara enchants both for head and leg and the +1% dodge cloak enchant -> +5% dodge on a hunter is huge
 * Getting the threat glove enchant
 * Prioritizing agi, stam, dodge, parry, defense and armor (maybe some strength to have some melee threat)
 * Getting the tank Hakkar trinket and swapping it in almost on CD. That 2000 armor feels like giving yourself a so-so shield before pulls.
 * Getting the most defensive pet you can get. It doesn't need much damage, its role is to OT a target noone is DPSing.
 * Kara blue trinket with armor, stamina and dodge is huge for this build.

I can't promise that the build is working as intended, only theorycrafted it. There is no way I boost up my hunter from level 16 to 60 for one experiment.
If you find this idea fun and give it a try, please give me a feedback and let me know what worked and what did not. If you guys tell me it's indeed working and fun, I might consider leveling up that hunter (just don't forget to wyvern sting on pull).

An ideal comp (if you wanna test it out with friends):
 * Cat druid DPS - your threat will be limited, bringing a toon with limited damage output would make it feel less bad if he needs to hold back. Also, druids can provide huge utility for you: druid buff, occassional hybernate, leader of the pack and switching into bear if things got loose. Cat picks very similar talents in feral tree, they can help a lot as emergency OTs.
 * Enh shaman DPS - enh shamans actually don't wanna use WF totem. They wanna use agi totem and WF weapon. This plays perfectly with you and with the druid. Agi + strength totems makes your tanking much better. Also, enh shaman's DPS output is limited, they slot well into this since you're threat output will be limited, too. With some WF procs they'll sometimes overaggro you but since they are melee, the mob stays in melee range, enabling you to get the mob back easier. Also, enh shamans have magma and fire nova totems which have threat independent of the shaman. They can help in case of aoe trash without causing added threat.
 * Smite priest - no, I'm not joking. Your threat will be so-so, bringing in lower DPS builds will enable them holding back less. Also, very big utility. Smite priest can give a crucial stamina buff to you, keep up with the single-target DPS and in case of big trash pulls, he can jump in and spam holy nova (if specced) without causing threat. That added aoe utility is huge. Shackle undead can be really handy in case of certain encounters. Also, he can shield you all the time. Also, perfect offheals and HoTs whenever needed.
 * Holy pala as healer - The best healer for keeping one target alive, like forever. Also can use salvation on everyone but you while you can enjoy kings (if specced) or might. And of course devotion aura. Pala alone solves half of your threat problems and makes you survive much better. They can also help in aoe trash packs in case of emergency via consec and can help you get out of roots if needed. Can also BoP a caster if you lost threat and a caster is dying.

This group will not be limited by your lower threat output and since you plan to run a slower version of dungeon crawling, CCing whatever possible and taking mobs almost 1-by-1, it is very important. This comp gives you all the essential buffs you need while can also reduce own threat a lot. Also, noone will complain because the shaman doesn't use WF totem. Offheals are also plenty and you can have every resistance except for arcane.

+1 Very good option:
Arcane mage: Arcane mages usually have lower damage than frost mages and are more inclined to do single-target DPS. They are capable of great aoe though if it is really necessary and they jump in to spam arcane explosions (hopefully with a power word:shield). They can bring excellent CC in the form of polymorph, some int buff, which is okay and some mana regen which is also okay. Sheep is the really big thing here but I think the sample comp has better utility for you.

Honorable mentions:
Rogue: Can OT for a while, can wipe threat but limited utility and will demand WF totem.
Freeze mage: Unparalelled movement utility, nice aoe, great CC via sheep, too. But higher damage output and a tendency to use aoe in cases you don't want them to use it -> leads to mobs running around and kiting.
Warrior: Can OT really well but limited utility and will demand WF totem.
Retri: Same utility as holy, even better at aoes but can easily overaggro and might demand WF totem (you really want that agi totem).
Warlock: Fear CC, stones but threat problems and overusing aoe.
Moonkin: Almost as good as cat but cats can switch to better bears if needed and moonkin aura < leader of the pack for you.
Shadow priest: Vampiric embrace has a huge threat. You're better off with a smite priest.
Elemental shaman: Very big threat.
