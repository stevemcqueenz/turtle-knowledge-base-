# 📜 Enchanter Specialization: Scribe 📜

## Post 163759 by basednoob (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-12-25T23:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163759#p163759 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The Scribe is a master of knowledge, specializing in creating magical books that teach new skills and scrolls that enhance attributes, or grant one-time spell effects.

 **Books & Skill Unlocks**
- Instead of the usual [skill books](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=book#items:0+1), [tomes](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=tome), [guides](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=guide), [codex's](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=codex), [grimoire's](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=Grimoire) dropping from raids or dungeons, Scribes now craft them instead from rare recipes.

- These new recipes drop as rare loot from specific dungeons or raids (e.g., AQ20, BWL, UBRS, LBRS, or through special [questlines](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=5061)).

- These recipes require enchanting [materials](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?items=7) and paper crafted with wood from the new Woodcutting profession.
 **Scroll Crafting**
- Scrolls same as the in-game drops are now crafted instead from recipes.

- Standard [scrolls](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=scroll) (e.g., Strength, Agility, Intellect) but also new one-time use spell scrolls, such as:[Scroll of Empowered Protection](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=65017), [Scroll of Magic Warding](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=65018) or [Scroll of Thorns](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=65016).

## Post 164626 by basednoob (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-01-07T20:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164626#p164626 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

- Once level 30 and 200 in enchanting skills, Alliance players can get a quest from [Kitta Firewind](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=11072) in Tower of Azora, and Horde players from [Hgarth](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=11074) in Stonetalon Mountains, who sends them to dungeons like Stormwrought or Scarlet Monastery to collect several missing pages or chapters to complete books. These books are important in a final quest when they must create some special crafts to prove their worth and then finally get their scribe specialization.

- A normal quill is used for crafting magic scrolls, but the [ink](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=10647) may be infused with enchanting reagents for certain scrolls. The magic itself is bound into the parchment during enchantment, while the ink helps conduct, anchor, or shape that magic.

- Parchments can be crafted with leatherworking. Using higher quality rare types of hide for the stronger, higher level magical scrolls ([Cured Rugged Hide](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=15407), [Dreamhide](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61228) [Pristine Hide of the Beast](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=12731)).

- Cured [Owl](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61336) / [Gryphon](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=2658) / [Hippogryph](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=6375) feathers crafted by leatherworkers are used to make the quills. but you can also find [Gryphon Feather Quill](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3766). They are not magical, but rarer types of feathers are preferred due to the prestige of making powerful magic scrolls.

- Alchemists can make the different types of infused ink needed by scribes by using [Engineer's Ink](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=10647) mixed with different types of [dust](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?search=dust) gathered by enchanters disenchanting gear.
