# The true story of Garithos

## Post 156913 by RPGnerd (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-10-24T18:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156913#p156913 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I'm not a real artist, but I'm good at writing and I believe I have good ideas. So, I decided to tell my version of Othmar Garithos's story in the form of Power Metal.

Garithos did nothing wrong!

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/H_ggvQF5-H8]

Lyrics:
Gloria! For Lordaeron!
Gloria! For the King!
Grand Marshal rise — to wipe all sin!

Born in lands where silver shone,
Blackwood’s pride, now ash and bone,
From noble blood, the call of war,
To fight for light forevermore!
Steel and honor, oath and flame,
No crown, no gold, no praise, no fame,
Through Quel’Thalas his banner flies,
While shadows burn beneath the skies!

And when the fire claimed his kin,
He swore revenge on every sin,
The light was bleeding — yet he stood,
A lion bound by brotherhood!

Hail! Hail! Grand Marshal of the fallen throne!
Steel and faith against the bone!
Alone he fights — his name we know,
Garithos! For Lordaeron!

Die! Die! Undead scum, face his roar!
Elf and dwarf betrayed his war!
No soul shall rest, no mercy sworn,
Garithos fights forevermore!

When hope was torn by cursed decay,
He led the last who dared to stay,
A thousand dead, yet still he cried,
“For Lordaeron, we shall not die!”
The elves turned cold, the dwarves withdrew,
But honor burned, his heart stayed true,
With silver steel and holy might,
He faced the darkness, lost to light!

Sylvanas! Witch of Blight!
He faced you through the endless night!
Two dreadlords — no honor in the blade,
A hero born, a martyr made!

Hail! Hail! Grand Marshal of the mourning crown!
Betrayed but never falling down!
The last true light in shadow’s scorn
Garithos lives! For Lordaeron!

Humanity in peril — there is no delay,
Our hero is dead, we mourn this day.
But by truth and blood — they will pay,
These inhuman creatures shall decay!
