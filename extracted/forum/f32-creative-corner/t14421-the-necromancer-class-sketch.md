# The necromancer class - sketch

## Post 98139 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-06-29T12:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98139#p98139 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I've seen in chat that the most popular 'what if' class is necromancer which I also think is an awesome idea. **In this article I do not propose anything, just sharing with you how I'd imagine such a class in TWOW.**
Will be long.

 **The necromancer**
Who are they?: Necromancers are a hybrid caster-pet user class, relying on mana. They use shadow and nature as schools of magic (flavorwise I think this fits them the most). They use cloth.

 **Differences between them and warlocks:**
 * Warlocks use shadow and fire magic, necromancers use shadow and nature magic.
 * Warlocks enslave demons and use them as pets. Necromancers revive corpses and use them as pets.
 * Warlocks can only have one active pet but that one has meaningful abilities. Necromancers can have many pets at once but - if not talented - those corpses have no abilities.

 **Builds for a necromancer:**
 * Spellslinger: a caster build focusing more on nature and shadow damage instead of the corpses.
 * Gravedigger: a caster build focusing more on the corpses.
 * Guardian: a 'puppet-tank' build, focusing mostly on tanking with one strong corpse.

 **Base mechanics of a necromancer:**
 * Raising corpses and getting benefits from sacrificing them while playing as a caster. Otherwise they keep them alive for melee pet damage or for tanking.

 **Sample abilities of a necromancer:**
 * Raise dead - Summoning two corpses (skeletons or ghouls, whichever feels cooler). Can only be done if the necromancer doesn't control any corpse at the moment and has a base CD of 30 sec. Corpses have by default no ability. They can attack via the pet action bar and that's it.
 * Atrophy - A DoT using nature damage.
 * Flay of decay: A channeled shadow spell, similar to Mindflay. Instead of movement speed reduction, it increases the physical damage the target takes from pets (including demons and hunter pets) while channeling is active.
 * Ceaseless Hunger - You sacrifice your corpses. In exchange you get an instant heal.
 * Jinx of nature - A shadow debuff which decreases the target's resistance against nature spells.
 * Decay bolt - A castable nature spell for damage - similar to lightning bolt.
 * Withering ground -  A shadow AoE affecting the ground. You sacrifice all of your corpses. In exchange a withering ground appears around all of your corpses for 5 seconds which deals shadow damage to anyone entering that area every second.
 * Bone armor - The usual cloth-wearer armor spell. This one gives you some mana every time a pet you control is dealt damage (can only activate once every 2 seconds).
 * Skeletal roots - Their CC, similar to the druid one (we have bones instead of roots here). Outdoors only.
 * Rallying army - Their buff-utility, an aura which gives a non-stackable haste buff to each nearby pets (including demons and hunter pets). Let's say it's 30 yards.
 * Forging bones - A channeled spell, healing one of your corpses.
 * Blood Soup - Can create a drink which recharges mana but damages you while drinking it (if no mage is around).
 * Portal: Cemetery -  Can open portals for team members to some of the cemeteries of Azeroth. Cannot create portals for city centers, only cemeteries. Usually to places where no mage portal goes, like EPL cemetery.
 * Melt undead - A shock-like shadow spell which by default has low damage but has a big damage multiplier against undead targets on a 10 sec CD.
 * Rise! - An out of combat rez. This class has to have one. Was considering combat rez but I don't wanna push them too much.

 **Strengths of the baseline:**
 * Added pet damage
 * Having a root effect
 * Unique utility (for pets)
 * Having AoE (on CD)
 * Emergency self-heal via ceaseless hunger
 * DoT

 **Weaknesses of the baseline:**
 * Lack of stun
 * Lack of fear
 * Lack of interrupt
 * Wearing cloth
 * No burst spikes

The talent trees

Spellslinger (Caster)

 **Flavor:**
Dark casters who use their corpses as a resource, to place AOEs or for emergency healing. Focus is on being a caster.

 **1st row**
 5tp - Reducing the mana cost of flay of decay, decay bolt and melt undead by 10%.
 5tp - Increasing the damage done by flay of decay, decay bolt and melt undead by 5%.

 **2nd row**
 3tp - Increases the range of your spells by 15%.
 2tp - Increases the area of eccect of withering ground by 10%.
 1tp - Allows skeletal roots to be used indoors.
 5tp - Increasing intellect by 10%.

 **3rd row**
 1tp - Critting via any of your spells causes clearcasting which makes your next spell free.
 2tp - Reduces the threat of your nature and shadow spells by 15%.
 5tp - 5% extra crit chance to damaging, non-channeled spells.

 **4th row**
 3tp - Increases the damage done by atrophy by 6%.
 5tp - Decay bolt crits have a 100% chance to apply atrophy on the target if the target doesn't have atrophy at the moment. Decay bolts also have a +5% crit chance on targets which have atrophy on them.

 **5th row**
 5tp - Reduces the cast time of your decay bolt by 0,5 sec.

 **6th row**
 1tp - Withering ground no longer requires corpses to be sacrificed. It becomes a 10 sec CD AoE which applies withering ground around all of your skeletons.
 1tp - Your damaging spells have lifesteal - they recharge your HP equal to 2% of the damage you deal (including corpses).

 **7th row**
 1tp - Teaches you 'Pain share'. It is a buff which can be only applied on one person. The target with pain share also receives the lifesteal effect after the damage you deal.
 1tp - When your mana goes below 30%, your spells get 20% cheaper for 10 sec. Can only happen once every minute.

 **Strengths:**
 * Tank heal utility
 * Better AoE
 * Decent caster toolkit
 * Quite bursty

 **Weaknesses:**
 * Caster toolkit lacks spell hit chance
 * Leech effect, while can be targeted outside the party is less effective than the leech effect of the shadow priest

 **Gravedigger (caster with focus on pet damage)**

 **Flavor:**
True necromancers, summoning a horde of corpses. Gameplay revolves around overwhelming the opponent while doing some caster damage from range.

 **1st row**
 2tp - Increases the health of your corpses by 10%.
 5tp - Reduces the casting time of raise dead by 100%.
 3tp - Increases the hit chance and crit chance of your corpses by 3%.

 **2nd row**
 3tp - Raise dead has a 100% chance to summon a third corpse.
 5tp - Increases the melee attack power of your corpses by n% of your intellect.
 2tp - Increases the armor of given by bone armor by 5% and increases the mana you receive when a corpse is dealt damage by n%.

 **3th row**
 3tp - Reduces the CD of raise dead by 15 sec.
 1tp - Forging bones healing can jump to a second target (50% less effective on that target).
 5tp - Increases the pet haste buff of rallying army by n%.

 **4th row**
 3tp - Critical hits dealt by one of your corpses give the target a debuff which increases the damage of decay bolt on the target for n seconds.
 5tp - There is a 50% chance that when using ceaseless hunger, the sacrificed corpses get immediately resummoned (50% on each corpse individually).

 **5th row**
 3tp - Increases the pet attack bonus granted by flay of decay by n%.
 3tp - If an opponent kills one of your corpses, there is a 30% chance that a new one gets instantly summoned on his place.

 **6th row**
 1tp - Your corpse's critical hits give the target a bleed effect.

 **7th row**
 1tp - Whenever someone in your party kills an enemy, there is a 50% chance that you get an additional corpse. This corpse decays in 10 sec and cannot leave behind a new corpse. You can't have more than 2 additional corpses at a time.
 1tp - Teaches you 'Blood sacrifice'. It is a 5 min CD ability which resets all of your current CDs but deals you damage equal to 20% of your total health.

 **Strengths:**
 * If corpses are up, this build can overwhelm non-aoe users
 * Increased pet utility
 * Decay bolt utility helps the caster necromancer build a lot
 * Has an emergency ability to clear CD on crucial abilities
 * Excellent against casters without an instant AoE since all those small corpse attacks are interfering with casting

 **Weaknesses:**
 * Emergency ability to clear CDs can kill you
 * Classes with good AoE hardcounter you
 * When you have no corpse and raise dead is on CD, there is a small window when you are really weak. Specially if the target escapes the emergency root

 **Guardian**

 **Flavor:**
You resurrect an ogre and as you get better at being a necromancer you find ways to give him back his old tools. You basically gear him up with talents and give him the abilities it once had. The ogre becomes a 'puppet-tank' which you control and tank with while you are healing it, contantly repositioning and doing damage whenever possible.

 **1st row**
 1tp - Your raise dead will only summon one corpse - the corpse of an ogre. This corpse has a higher stamina and armor, lower damage than the normal two corpses but also has an increased threat modifier. This talent is a prerequisite for any other talents in the tree.

 **2nd row**
 3tp - Gives your corpse a shield which has an armor equal to n times the armor you have (without buffs). Block amount is n% of is armor.
 5tp - Increases the stamina and the armor of your corpse by n% of your stamina and n% of your armor.
 1tp - Teaches the corpse taunt (10 sec CD, costs you mana).
 5tp - Reduces the corpse's chance of receiving a crit by n% as long as you channel forging bones on it.

 **3rd row**
 3tp - Increases the threat done by withering ground by n% and the corpse doesn't have to be sacrificed for the effect.
 1tp - Gives the corpse a spectral mace which increases the damage it does by n% (scaling with your spell stamina).
 1tp - Teaches 'Callback'. It is a 10 sec ability which forces the corpse to charge next to you (costs you mana). If you want to use this ability so that the corpse can rush towards people in danger, you have to position yourself during fight all the time.
 5tp - Increases the block chance of the corpse (if it has the shield talent) by 5%. Each block recharges 2% of your mana. Can only happen once in every sec.

 **4th row**
 5tp - Gives the corpse 5% parry chance. Every time the corpse parries or blocks, it receives 5% less damage for 5 seconds.
 1tp - Teaches the corpse 'Cleaving Smash' (only if it has the mace talent). It is a 5 sec CD ability which hits up to 3 targets and costs you mana.
 3tp - Reduces the mana cost of forging bones by 15%.
 1tp - You give the corpse the gauntlets of mastery which gives him 5% skill in maces.

 **5th row**
 3tp - If the corpse dies, it gets automatically resummoned (keeping the original threat) with 15% health. Can happen once every 10 minutes.
 1tp - You give the corpse the boots of swiftness which increase it's movement speed by 5%.
 1tp - You give the corpse the legs of evasion which gives the corpse 3% dodge chance.
 1tp - You give the corpse the chestplate of protection which gives him resistance equal to n% of your resistances. It also give sgim defense equal to n% of your intellect.

 **6th row**
 1tp - You give the corpse the cloak of brutality which gives the corpse 5% added threat.
 1tp - You give the corpse the helmet of precision which further provides 3% hit chance.

 **7th row**
 1tp - Teaches you 'Anti-magic shell'. It creates a 5 yard radius shell around the corpse, reducing the damage of spells. Costs you mana.
 1tp - You upgrade the corpse's helmet, adding 3% additional hit chance.

 **Strengths:**
 * The only tank with anti-magic utility in TWOW
 * You are a tank but also a tank-healer
 * The resummon with kept threat can be a lifesaver
 * Has a 20 sec AoE threat-spell, cleave and taunt

 **Weaknesses:**
 * Has no Aoe taunt
 * Has no way to reduce the target's armor or attack power
 * Itemization is weird (you need int, resistances, armor and stamina
 * Extremely hard build, containing elements of tank, healer and caster DPS

 **Races:**
Ally ->
Gnome, Human
Horde ->
Undead, Troll
