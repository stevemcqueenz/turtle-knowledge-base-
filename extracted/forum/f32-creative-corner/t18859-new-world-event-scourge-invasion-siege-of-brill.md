# New World Event: Scourge Invasion: Siege of Brill

## Post 128337 by Tyrusan — 2025-04-24T16:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128337#p128337 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Tirisfal:

 -Scourge Invasion: Siege of Brill

New part for low level players (lvl.20) who want to play their part in the Scourge Invasion.

At the command of Kel'thuzad, the Scourge sends troops from Agamand's Mill to Brill to take the settlement in the name of the Lich King.

The event is divided into the following parts:
 1. waves of undead attacking Brill and you have to defend the settlement against them.
 2. fight through to Agamand's mills.
 3. clearing the mills of any scourge.
 4. confront the unknown mastermind in the Agamand family crypt.

Waves:
 1. some Shuffling Corpses and Lean Ghouls attack Brill.
 2. a large horde of shuffling corpses and lean ghouls led by Thurman Agamand and Gregor Agamand attack Brill. (Announced by: Crier of Brill shouts: (” There is a great horde of Scourge coming from the direction of the mills!!!)
 3. after a short pause, a large squad of Rattling Skeleton Soldiers attacks Brill.
 4. the first real boss attacks: Gustaph the Deranged shouts,” Ulag, ride down and tear Brill down in the name of the Lich King!!!” Ulag the Cleaver (former warrior quest mob) comes riding in on a skeletal warhorse, stops briefly at the entrance to Brill and shouts: “Do you really think I'm going to be defeated by such puny maggots?”, gets off his horse and attacks as soon as he is pulled.

After the battle, Brill's crier calls out: “The Death Knight is no more, now it's up to you adventurers to take the fight to the enemy!!!! On to Agamand's mills!!!”

You fight your way through various undead and meet Devlin Agamand at the entrance to the mills, accompanied by some Darkeye Bonecasters.

After Devlin has been defeated, you fight your way to the main square of the mills where Nissa Agamand is waiting for your group. But she is not alone!!! She is accompanied by a Tormented Spirit and a Lost Soul who will help her to prevent you from reaching the family tomb.

After this fight you continue towards the family crypt only to be met by the second and penultimate boss: Captain Dargol accompanied by a bodyguard of cracked skull soldiers. As he approaches, he shouts: “I will not let anyone go any further, I swear by the Lich King!!!”

After Dargol and his bodyguards have fallen: Gustaph the Deranged shouts: “So, you have defeated the Agamands and my other servants? Now then, I await you in the Agamand family tomb!” Rotting Ancestors and Lamenting Ancestors then spawn on the way to the tomb.

After you have fought your way out, the last boss awaits you down in the family crypt: Gustaph the Deranged, an undead death knight, has gathered the Scourge troops in Agamands Mills on Kel'thuzad's orders and pulled the strings from the depths of the family crypt. When he is defeated, Gustaph calls out: “I see only darkness.”

The event restarts at regular intervals. Comparable to the event in Ashenvale. (in SOD)

Mobs, NPCs, items and rewards:

Reputation:
You receive reputation for certain actions for Undercity.

Normal mobs: No reputation.
Elite mobs: 5-10 reputation
Notable elites: 20 reputation
Bosses: 25 reputation
Final boss: 100 reputation

(Reputation ends at Revered. Only the final boss and reputation/quest items give reputation up to Exalted).
