# Moriena's Custom Quest Collective

## Post 38097 by Moriena (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2023-03-17T15:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38097#p38097 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Hello, fellow turtles!**

Just like many of you, I am a connaisseur of lore and questing in WoW - and with the added custom content TurtleWoW published, World of Warcraft got an invigorating breath of new life brought into it.

I myself want to add to this new breath of life and help the Turtle team by providing interesting and enjoyable quest ideas and concepts. Thats what this thread is for! A collection of all my quest concepts I did so far and ofcourse those I plan on doing! Since I am unexperienced with vMangos and programming in general, I've written them into concise Google .doc documents, so everyone can unterstand my thought process behind them.   smiling_turtle_head

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 **A Sister Lost [8-PART QUESTLINE / ALLIANCE LVL 47-56]**
 *Rumors have it that Kaldorei are disappearing in the shades of Ashenvale, leaving little to no trails. One night elf seems particularly taken aback by this - Sentinel Dalia Sunblade, who is praying in Darnassus' Temple of the Moon for her sister's return.*

The adventurer joins the night elf investigation of the abduction cases, leading them to Azshara, where they uncover a tragic love story and a sinister plot that can threaten the life of everyone on Kalimdor.

[A Sister Lost - Custom Questline in Azshara](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PtCbYa85IOhMSl9dGbBbg6nfgjsRbCewtYc9DGhEhog/edit?usp=sharing)

 **Hunters Of The Wild [5-PART QUESTLINE / ALLIANCE LVL 24-29]**
 *The wildlife of Ashenvale is merciless and cruel, but offers a certain beauty for those looking deep enough. Jayla, huntress of the Silverwind Refuge teaches young adventurer's the ways of the Kaldorei hunt, hoping someone brave and promising enough appears, who can help the hunters with dealing with an exceptionally dangerous predator.*

The young adventurer undergoes three lessons provided by Jayla, huntress of Silverwind. They learn how to prove their strength, cunning and adaptivity in the face of the ruthless wilderness, to let them earn their place in this world becoming more dangerous with each passing day.

[Hunters Of The Wild - Custom Questline in Ashenvale](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Xhru3jMfRFzFQajoSfcbMvWSkICkDXmEkYTRB60vNTg/edit?usp=sharing)

 **A Question Of Belief [3-PART QUESTLINE / NEUTRAL LVL 32-34]**
 *Willow is looking for promising adventurers that are willing to open their eyes to the true masters of this world. Are you brave enough to prove your worth in front of this shady and dubious character? Who knows what you will get out of this...*

Willow, Twilight's Hammer cultist in Desolace, wants to show her value to her powerful masters and needs the help of an impressionable young prodigy, that is willing to crush the adversaries of her ideology, the Burning Legion, and to allow not only her, but maybe also them, to be in the Great One's favor.

[A Question Of Belief - Custom Questline in Desolace](https://docs.google.com/document/d/17hoebEtoTqC1SgOZGVdzPSEEJa8zADRKQ6SaUpzACMw/edit?usp=sharing)

 **The Great Gogo Girls Reunion! [2-PART QUESTLINE / NEUTRAL LVL 52]**
 *Metropolitan star and media mogul, the Magnificent Gogo from Undermine arrived in Everlook to work on her newest business venture, a hot spring dance bar, hoping to get filthy rich and gain even more fans and admirers on Kalimdor!*

Gogo needs a little help with getting the necessary people together, and it is the adventurer's job to gather her old friends from Kezan. With all five Gogo Girls joined, this grandiose idea of Gogo's will surely not fail... right?

[The Great Gogo Girls Reunion! - Custom Questline in Winterspring](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Ie4qBFsYd4hDIiviBfSqrhbAwTyouKKLuokFOmE_Gxg/edit?usp=sharing)

 **Rite Of Passage [5-PART QUESTLINE / ALLIANCE LVL 44]**
 *Young Wildhammer dwarves have to follow a tradition, if they want to become a full-fledged member of the clan. By hunting for leather and feathers in the plains of the Hinterlands and crafting a feathered headdress with them, they're proving their abilities and worth as adolescents of Aerie Peak.*

Doran Steelwing shows the adventurer the ways of creating a feathered headdress, the universal rite of passage of fledgling Wildhammer dwarves. Although apparently doing it out of educational purposes, something tells the player, there is more to Doran's story...

[Rite of Passage - Custom Questline in the Hinterlands](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1P_ngAhEJH-8ofsJpKlM8MA_UOHInZI12LlOhUIgslZU/edit?usp=sharing)

 **It's A Small World After All [4-PART QUESTLINE / ALLIANCE LVL 48-51]**
 *Sometimes the world appears larger than it actually is  - and things thought independent from one another turn out to be connected. And sometimes all it takes to see these connections - is a simple name.*

Drakk Stonehand of Aerie Peak has found a peculiar clue - a clue that might warm his old friend Doran's heart. But to prove his suspicion, he requires the help from another friend.

[It's A Small World After All - Custom Questline in the Hinterlands](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WuuhIznEAGp6YeSdO21PdgKv-PuX4H9FdYmZIuvCTiE/edit?usp=sharing)

 **The Four Dons [15-PART QUESTLINE / NEUTRAL LVL 58-60]**
 *Previously famous for singing in the streets of Stormwind, the Four Dons were a barbershop quartet advertising for the Trade District's barbershop. One day, they mysteriously vanished without a trace, leaving only their legacy behind.*

Tilo Wheatleigh, Freelance Investigator, is researching on his own about what really happened the night the Four Dons disappeared. The adventurer meets him in Booty Bay's tavern - and together, they work towards uncovering the truth and the potential fortune they amassed.

[The Four Dons - Custom Questline all over the Eastern Kingdoms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1G6Efj6Be5puCswMGESZVow1XSTnJFK7M8Zmde2xKDX0/edit?usp=sharing)

 **Terror On Purgation Isle! [8-PART QUESTLINE / NEUTRAL LVL 58+]**
 *Rumors spread - the dead are walking among the Hillsbrad Foothills once more! No one knows where they're coming from, whether they're Scourge or an outside force. Both Horde and Alliance seek the aid of young adventurers to solve this mistery.*

Marshal Redpath of Southshore and High Executor Darthalia of Tarren Mill require the help of willing heroes to quell this undead rampage that ravages both Humans and Forsaken alike. Who might be behind the roaming dead that seemingly originate from an old, forsaken island...?

[Terror On Purgation Isle [Alliance] - Custom Questline in the Hillsbrad Foothills](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1XyyAlFOlb20ZE7MqxhUdCCqbv1gB_eLO72dTWHVzFPg/edit?usp=drivesdk)
[Terror on Purgation Isle [Horde] - Custom Questline in the Hillsbrad Foothills](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1syTbPnhvi81zkUOGs3EK0EbI5IuizC3ffKucvyv1Ou8/edit?usp=drivesdk)

 **Lonely Ogre, Gloomy Ogre [6-PART QUESTLINE / NEUTRAL 32-34]**
 *Fitting in isn't always as easy as others would like it to be. Sometimes you just are too different from your own kind, that not only they don't understand it, but you yourself too. A lesson Nug had to learn for himself as well.*

The tiny ogre Nug lives alone from his ogre brethren in a camp outside the Boulderfist Hall. Driven out by his own people, Nug spends his days and nights in depricating isolation - until, one day, he meets an adventurer.

[Lonely Ogre, Gloomy Ogre - Custom Questline in the Arathi Highlands](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1B0QZFQ11OWUd9pVeMjRJBW9BXWMGd3saDxdsXtwog_4/edit?usp=sharing)

 **A Debt To Pay [8-PART QUESTLINE / HORDE LVL 48-56]**
 *Groha from Ogrimmar is on a mission. A personal mission. Leaving her ailing mother Gromar in friends' care, she sets out to find her last living friend: a warrior legend of the Third War.*

Wanting to prove herself and the love she has for her mother, Groha goes out into Kalimdor once more to get the legendary warrior Shute're of the Warsong Clan back home. A chance encounter with the adventurer in the Crossroads is the key to overcoming this ordeal.

[A Debt To Pay - Custom Questline in Crossroad/Felwood](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mY54xdvAXzjpAvQhbwGkVViCgte5B5BfVs7OawvoOAo/edit?usp=sharing)

I hope you enjoy reading these - please leave feedback! There are more to come! I will be updating the OP to add new designs for a clear overview!

Regarding the questline, I often like to imagine what certain personalities are or could've been in the world of Azeroth. The Four Dons for instance - they're belongings are scattered throughout the dungeons and raids and they're only mentioned by one NPC in the game by name. So what could these characters be like?
