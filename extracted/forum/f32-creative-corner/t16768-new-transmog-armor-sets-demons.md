# New Transmog/Armor Sets-Demons

## Post 114606 by Kiercy — 2024-12-28T11:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114606#p114606 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is an idea for the fashion/gear team in regards to sets of armor. As with the next level of karazhan coming out and a lot of demon like figures being shown in previews-I figure this is just as good a time as ever to share this armor idea. On some of the demon models-mainly felguards and succubus but also the voidwalkers, each demon has an armor set attached to them and it would be cool to have them on normal player models-especially since some of these demons are illusions that could further an RP element.

Felguards obviously have the big boots and a spiked chest cage that blends into their gauntlets.

Succubi have a metal chain corset and the slightly swim wear like attire for their bottom half, along with small matching bracers. It would be extremely cool, in theme, and I'd assume relatively easily to make these items as you would just cut the items off the models assuming their not embedded.

Voidwalkers-really only have their bracers but in tbc and further, some voidwalkers had the whole cloth robe with lanterns

Imps-quite simply could just add plain black horns-could honestly just recolor the warlock tier set horns.

And felhunter.....well.....not sure there as that may be furry territory.

As for infernals and doomguards: Doomguards could easily be a set on their own while infernals give little to no real armor ideas that could easily be made or cut off from the initial model.

I'd love to hear peoples' thoughts and ideas on this as I feel this would be a relatively easy idea to pull off with a very small development time. The hardest part would be just fitting the items to each of the 10 races for both genders-so 20 individual fittings.
