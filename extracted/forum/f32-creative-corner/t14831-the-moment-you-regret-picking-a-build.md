# The moment you regret picking a build

## Post 101007 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-09-10T10:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101007#p101007 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I wanna write this for fun, hoping to cause some giggles. No offense intended.

I wanna find the moment or the thought process when specific builds question why they did this to themselves and consider either rerolling or uninstalling the game.

 **Holy Priest/Disc Priest**

You go to an MC pug and somehow you are the only priest. You start burning your sacred candles, buffing up every group - and drinking after every second party buff. Let's face it, you'll spend minutes in this loop. Finally you are ready.
Random fury warrior is asking you why you're not a dwarf. 'Magmadar mate /spit'. You ignore the guy, finally yall can start and you have a chance to get cauterizing band.
Ásspull at the beginning. Yall manage but half the pug dies. Rezzing, rebuffing, burning candles. Main tank of course pulls when you are at 20% mana after all those buffs so you can't save some guys, the rezzing/buffing/drinking loop continues. After 30 minutes (you never were above 20% mana in any fight) raid lead asks why you're healing numbers are so low. You get kicked.

 **Shadow Priest**

You always wanted to pick a caster and you heard that shadowpriest is more than a one-of warlock slave in TWOW raiding, so you gave it a shot.
You dinged 60 and got curious about all the items you can get to upgrade your gear at lower raids.
And it's world boss, world boss, world boss and world boss.
You said okay, let's get some Kara caster gear, the AQ20 staff and it's all good.
So with plain determination you went to your first AQ. You've spent half of the raid in almost melee range because your mindflay súcks, died to some cleaves because of that, too. But it's all good, Ossirian is close.
Enduring that your DPS was kinda equal to the deep prot warrior offtank all raid long was kinda harsh but Ossirian is finally down. And you've lost the staff's SR roll to the weird deep prot warrior who was saying something like 'my diamond flask gear set needs more +healing'.

 **Resto druid**

You wanted to look like a best friend of a Disney Princess, so you went tree. Out of combat rez? Why? Dungeons are easy, if noone is really stupid, rez is not needed, you thought.
Then came that LBRS run with 3 fury warriors (none of them willing to tank) and a tank shaman (battle shout wasn't up 80% of the time). You watched that foolish shaman running among the ogres and die. Then die. Then die. Corpse runs happened, a lot. You spent that night corpserunning. After around the third wipe fury warriors started the 'wtf you doing, shít heal' stuff. Shaman offered the tanking role to any of the warriors. 'I need to DPS bro, my numbers are big'. 'Stupid healer why you don't cleanse magic?' - it came later. You almost pointed out that as a dru you can't demagic but gave up. Alt+F4.

 **Moonkin**

You chose the hipster way. Moonkin aura is good, people took you to raids. You accepted your role as an almost bottom DPS, you were here for the fun. Up until BWL. 'Bro, your DPS súcks anyway, we prio tear to real caster DPS'. 'Bro, you have to understand, you are the moonkin aura guy. This leather casting item is perfect for Bobby, the resto shaman who sometimes does ele for us if we have too many heals'.
It hurt your owl? bear? whatever heart. You didn't receive any upgrade for 2 months. People think that you are a joke. You look like a joke. But you give moonkin aura and unlike mages, you are OK with decursing so they tolerate you. "Just wait for class changes, bástards!'  - you think to yourself as a last hope.

 **Cat**

One day you realized that you have two gameplay-options. You either download a one-button addon which you press for the rest of your life or you do it the hard way for half of the DPS. Tesco budget rogue, your friends told to you but you were stubborn. Tigers are strong, you saw in Animal Planet. Then you went to a dung with a rogue 3 levels below you and you watched the DPS-meter in pure desperation. Realized that your tiger is closer to Garfield.

 **Bear**

I mean... After a while you gave up caring about new items. Even fashion coins are nerfed for you. Whatever you do, whatever you farm, you will always look like that big brown bear. You want some personalisation options besides the p2w glyph. Whatever: a scarf, a hat on the bear, sunglasses, other fur patterns. That stoopid bear looks the same way since level 10 and now you're tanking Naxx. You... Want... Customization options.
You also wanna parry and block but you kinda understand why the bear doesn't do those things.
Seriously, you want to look unique in the game and whenever you play your toon, the game takes it away from you.

 **Holy Pala**

'Mate, buff isn't up'. 'Gimme kings (asked from a holy pala at Deadmines)'. 'Why do you need a drink break, mate, you're a pala' 'How could you let Bobbie's boar die, your whole rotation is 1 button' (Bobbie's boar pulled half the mine). 'Do something, not just afk between heals, use your wand' (nani?).
At around Gilneas you got really bored: of buffing all the time, od the people around you and od your almost total lack of healing options. When you were drunk you keybinded the same flash heal to 5 buttons to give you a sense of rotation. It gave you some chill days. Life was good until enhancement shaman in Gilneas demanded kings, might and salvation at the same time and wanted to kick you because you only wanted to give one.

 **Retri Pala**

People's narrow understanding of a topic lead to half-truths and rumors. You chose retri pala to be a God at raiding. Don't get me wrong, you're all cool. But DPS doesn't add up. Guild lead always talks to you about how awesome holy palas are and how cool it would be if most of the guild's palas were holy ('not implying anything bro, it was pure phylosophical'). One day an officer offered you free +healing weapon enchants and wrote you a poem about how awesome hide of the wild is.
That day you understood. The pala God saga is mostly for PVP, leveling and farming. These guys want you to push the flash heal button. To.... rez people (you haven't used that button yet). To not hit stuff with a huge hammer. (Sad, existential music.)

 **Prot pala**

Dungeons gave you a fake expectation about raid tanking. Consec = pulling 5 packs and killing everyone at one place by being top DPS and not losing any threat. People loved you, you were the knight in shining armor for those dungeon runs. So you went to tank some raids. Why is this 20 meter tall dragon hits 50x bigger than my usual prey, the Hateforge dwarves? Where are my defensives? Was ardent defender supposed to be a defensive? You look at the warrior, your heart is filled with jealousy seeing the guy using abilities like shield wall and last stand. Even bear is doing bear things to survive. You can still collect the 21321321 trash mobs but it feels like you're a knight in shining armor no more. People started to simp the warriors and the bears who have actual means to survive the Saitama punches yall receive as tanks at raids.

 **Resto shaman**

'Bro, where WF, you ruin my parsing' - when WF was not down for a sec. People told you how versatile and useful you are, how much guilds want you in their core team. What people didn't tell you is that you're not a character, you're a walking world buff which can spit-out stupid-friendly heals. You spam your mini world buffs, make warrior dóng big and hard, you make sure people don't get feared. Even the cleansing, totems do your job for you. Besides WF the one thing you have to focus on is your dumbed-down heal. Wherever you cast it, it finds a way to actually jump into injured teammates. You're the guy who people befriend because he has a Ferrari and you know it. Noone cares about you, melees.. khm... people care about your Ferrari.

 **Enhancement shaman**

Lol.

 **Elemental shaman**

You have threat problems. There is a totem which reduces threat. You make the genius connection and prepare to summon tranquil air totem. The melee, these beasts sense it! You see several warriors staring at you with killing intent. 'Wind... Fury...' - someone whispers. OK, you shrug, keep WF and die to overaggro within 10 sec as usual. You won't twist, you're a DPS. Everyone knows that DPS doesn't do utility (or rezzes).
Also, you being a relevant DPS at some raids totally depends on the tank having Thunderfury.

 **Rogues (sword + dagger)**

You didn't know that PVP is dead on this server. So instead of an OP ninja, feared by the opposite faction you became the Tesco budget fury warrior with some trap-disarm tendencies.
To soothe your soul you do some ganking sometimes so you kill actual players. But yeah, have you ever seen a Naxx token being given to a rogue instead of a warrior? Mr. Second Class Citizen.

 **Hunter (BM)**

You wanted to play Pokémon with extra steps and people started to tell you from Kara onwards that your Gorilla, the mighty Sickducker is useless. 'Don't even call pet bro, it just pulls the room. 0 DPS that monke is'. You forgot to bring arrows, you usually watch that Mewtwo movie while the gorilla is farming. How do hunters attack anyway? Want me to kite? Lol no. Tranquilizing? No arrows, bro. I JUST WANNA PLAY WITH MY GORILLA, THIS IS MY REASON TO BE HERE, BRO.

 **Hunter (MM)**

Oh Legolas! Or some kind of wordplay. Chadolas? Zugolas? Gankolas? Something like this, for sure. You like auto-attacking, you really do. What do you even wanna accomplish here? You play as an archer but you're called a hunter. You haven't even seen your pet for a while. Your auto-attack costs you money, man. Your most unique ability is acting like a pússy and feigning death (with a fake 80s pórn órgasmic moan). You're surrounded by Schwarzeneggers with burning axes, magic knights, demon-summoning sickos. You shoot a bow. That's it. And you pay for it. Then you fake your own death as the part of your rotation. Dignity where?

 **Hunter (melee)**

Even bigger lol.

 **Warlock (SM/Ruin, DS/Ruin)**

Mr. Inferiority Complex. Why did you decide to become an almost mage who has to show up early before every raid? 'Help me bro, I need to farm these imps to get shards to summ the mage'. Not surprising that you have this inferiority complex. People expect you to be everywhere in time and to ask for help to farm mobs just so that the real DPS can be ported here (who spent this time Netflixing while you were travelling to the place). Your best curse is designed for even bigger mage DPS. Even you're AoE súcks compared to a mage. Yours kills you, man.
What do you get in exchange? A demon which you immediately sack just to be slightly less bad compared to mages on the charts? One day this all clicks together and you leave a raid mid-boss.

 **Freeze mage**

You watch yourself from being OP to being overshadowed and irrelevant. It hurts. Still, you can farm all those mobs so that your warrior main has money for consumes and repair. You are good for something I guess.

 **Fire mage**

The trap behind the fire mages is quitting early. During leveling and early raiding it can hurt. Specially if you are special enough to join BWL as a fire mage, that can traumatize you. But once you reach AQ40 as a fire mage, you won't really regret it anymore. It's like an endurance test.

 **Arcane mage**

So you wanna give up half of your damage so that party members can spare 1-2 mana pots a run? You really like taking care of others' wallets. Honestly, 'rotation' and DPS numbers will probably break your heart well before raiding.

 **Arms warrior**

??? No PVP here.

 **Fury warrior**

Repairo costu OMG desu. Sooner or later you realize that you only worth anything as a fury prot if you do the big numbers all the time. You farm 3x as the other DPS. You enjoy farming instead of going out with friends? You will farm a lot. During a Strat holy water run (you skipped grandma's birthday for it) you'll probably have an existential crisis and you uninstall the game.

 **Deep prot**

No.

 **Fury-Prot**

You're like the fury warrior but you spend even more on consumes and you also have a bunch of added responsibility. Bad for the nerves. Will kill your nerves. One day someone will ásspull and the ruins of your nervous system will produce an autistic screeching which is so ugly that people will leave the guild. Really, you play a game and decided to play a build which destroys your nervous system and forces you to farm instead of anything else IRL. You'll have the same breakdown as the fury warrior but it will happen in the middle of a raid, after an error, resulting your life's most awkward screeching. 'Stop it. Get some help.' (MJ voices)
