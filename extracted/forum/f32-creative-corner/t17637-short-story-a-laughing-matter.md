# Short Story: A Laughing Matter

## Post 120379 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-02-19T07:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120379#p120379 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

"The chieftain stirs!"
"Mogor awakens!"
"Call for Obris!"

Mogor, chieftain of the Laughing Skull clan, was awakened by the light of day blinding both his heads as he opened his three eyes. In the midst of his grogginess, all around him he could hear the scurrying of feet and the cries of orcs and ogres. Easing himself up, he found that he had been laid in a resting position. The surrounding environment of the chamber was familiar, but he knew it was not his sanctum. As he took in the sight of the orcs and ogres assembled before him, Obris, his closest and most loyal advisor, was ushered into the room with haste and quickly knelt before him.

 *"Obris,"* Mogor's left head said, his deep voice echoing through the room as he addressed the orc, *"remain here. The rest of you..."*

 *"...leave us!"* his right head finished as he dismissed them with a wave of his hand.

Mogor shifted into a seated position as he waited for the assembled clansmen to leave, finally addressing Obris. *"You may rise."* He waited for Obris to straighten himself up. *"Now tell me, where are we?"* One head said. *"What happened?"* The other inquired.

 *"We are in the village, chieftain."* Obris visibly hesitated before continuing. *"You... you were felled by the Slayer of the Shadowmoon. Ner'zhul sought the death knights that you had brought to the fold. We brought you back, but we had to keep you hidden while we tended to you. You... you have been indisposed for some time now."*

Mogor pondered on this, still groggy from his awakening. He remembered now, being beset upon by an orcish warrior in Shadowmoon colors. Fury surged within him at the thought. *"How long has it been?"*

 *"Long enough. The Elder Shaman's followers have placed me in charge of the clan and forced the death knights to reopen the Great Portal. The Bleeding Hollow have returned to us, but the humans of that world - Azeroth, they called it - have followed them back here. After you fell, our clan was made to serve Ner'zhul's schemes."* Obris sneered as he spoke the last part. *"But thinking us to have submitted to his rule, he foolishly brought me into his inner circle. I know now what he plans, and how he wishes to do so. And the Laughing Skull may yet have something to gain from this."*

 *"Is that so?"* Both heads leaned forward, an inquisitive gleam in all three eyes. *"Then do tell me, my dear Obris... And spare me not a single detail."*

.............................................................................................................................................

 *"So,"* Mogor's left head said, the ogre-mage having shifted in a more comfortable lounging position, *"he wishes to use these artifacts to conquer more worlds?"* Obris' description of the artifacts that Ner'zhul sought had fascinated Mogor. Though his pride had been wounded by his defeat and the subjugation of his death knights, the knowledge that they held was trivial compared to the power that even a single one of these artifacts possessed. But above all, the Book of Medivh intrigued him the most. Yet it was not as though he could march into Fortress Shadowmoon and take it himself, believed to be dead by Ner'zhul and his followers.

His right head glanced inquisitively at Obris at the thought. *"Does Ner'zhul know of my survival?"*

 *"No, chieftain. Ner'zhul declared your life to be forfeit, and believes that his Slayer has defeated you. You were on the brink of death when we retrieved you."*

 *"Good."* He nodded with both heads. *Then we know more of him than he knows of us. For now, we shall continue to act as loyal members of the Shadow Clans until an opportunity arises for us to break away, and Ner'zhul be made to pay for his attack on us."* The ogre-mage found some measure of satisfaction in this, even though his clan yet remained subservient to the Elder Shaman for the time being. *"What of this 'Alliance'? What is it that they seek here?"*

 *"The natives of Azeroth seek the very same artifacts that Ner'zhul has claimed. From what we could uncover, their purpose is to close the Great Portal themselves and stop any further of our incursions in their lands."*

 *"Hmm. Then they do not come here as conquerors."* Mogor scratched one of his chins, deep in thought as the other head spoke. *"Perhaps they could be of use to us. If we had-"*

 *"Obris!"* An orc barged into the room, interrupting Mogor. *"Chieftain! Forgive my interruption, but our scouts report that the Alliance forces are planning to set sail for the Skeletal Coast! They're headed for Fortress Shadowmoon!"*

At the news, Mogor could not help himself from bellowing out a hearty laugh from both heads. How fortuitous for him! *"It would seem that opportunity has come to us, my dear Obris. Go! Rally the rest of the clan! You shall make for Fortress Shadowmoon at once and warn them of the incoming attack. However, when battle begins, you must seize the Book of Medivh before the Alliance can claim it, and return it here."*

 *"Yes, chieftain!"* Obris said, saluting Mogor before leaving to gather the clan's warriors.

.............................................................................................................................................

 *"Chieftain! We have returned!"* Obris entered Mogor's chambers with his prize in hand, victorious.

 *"The Book of Medivh. At last."* Sitting upon his throne, Mogor held the artifact with a look of wonder in all of his eyes as Obris handed it to him. Obris' forces had successfully retrieved it in the chaos of the Alliance attack, with the Elder Shaman apparently none the wiser - though several of the clan were felled by the Alliance along the Skeletal Coast.

It was the way of things. Only the strong could survive within the Horde. Mogor dwelled upon it no longer. What concerned him more was the conspicuous absence of Ner'zhul from Fortress Shadowmoon. Already he heard reports that Ner'zhul was gathering his faithful. Did he no longer need the Book? And where did that leave the Laughing Skull?

 *"You have done well, Obris. You have served me very well, as you always have. With the power contained within this book, we could perhaps even challenge Ner'zhul's might and claim all of Draenor for ourselves... and beyond."* But to Obris' surprise, Mogor didn't even open the book. He instead lowered it, both heads grinning at his advisor. *"But those are arrogant and lofty goals. And after all, why needlessly endanger our own clan? We would still have to contend with the combined might of the Horde."* Mogor had no intention to follow in the mad footsteps of Ner'zhul, as he found himself quite content with his clan ruling over the Blade's Edge. Yet this did not mean that he would remain passive, even as the Elder Shaman's forces remained in his territory. *"No, there are others to whom this holds value."*

Mogor rose from his throne and approached Obris. *"We shall make an offer to the Alliance. One that they will not be able to refuse. They shall need this book if they are to succeed in their efforts... and if they do, we must be the dominant Horde power in the north. Offer it to them. In exchange, they must crush Ner'zhul's followers in Blade's Edge... those sycophants from Fenris and Tagar's clans, the Thunderlords and Bonechewers."*

 *"At once, chieftain!"*

Obris gave him a salute and left. Left alone in his chambers, the ogre-mage's twin heads laughed in satisfaction with the knowledge that Ner'zhul's duplicity could not outmatch his own, and that vengeance would soon be his.

.............................................................................................................................................

 *"Chieftain!"* Mogor was roused from his thoughts at the sound of Obris' voice. *"The Alliance envoys have arrived!"*

Emerging from his chambers, the ogre-mage and his cohorts gathered at the entrance to the village to greet the Alliance envoys. As Mogor approached, three of the Alliance members broke away from their party to meet with him: a young knight, an elderly wizard and an elven ranger. Their leaders, no doubt.

 *"I am Turalyon,"* the knight said, *"and these are my companions, Khadgar and Alleria. You are Mogor, chieftain of this clan?"*

 *"I am. Have you come to accept my bargain? Shall we march as one upon the northern clans in exchange for the Book of Medivh?"*

 *"That is to be seen,"* the wizard Khadgar interjected. *"How do we know you truly have the Book?"*

 *"Is it proof that you seek? Then let me cast away all doubt, wizard. Behold! The Book of Medivh, claimed by my loyal rogues and thieves from Fortress Shadowmoon before your forces destroyed it!"* Mogor announced as he displayed the book. *"Yours, if you and your warriors aid my clan in removing the strongholds of the Thunderlords and Bonechewers in these passes."* Turalyon looked to Khadgar, who nodded at him. *"A moment, if you will."* The three then stepped back a moment to confer with one another. Though Mogor could not make out what was being said, the context was enough to determine what the urgency in the wizard's tone to the others meant: time was of the essence. Finally, the three returned.

 *"Very well,"* Turalyon said, *"we have an accord then, ogre. We shall aid you in destroying these strongholds of Ner'zhul's loyalists in exchange for the Book."*

 *"Excellent. Then let us march at once - victory or death!"*

With a rallying warcry from the clan, the combined might of the Alliance Expedition and the Laughing Skull left the village and descended upon the nearest stronghold, that of the Bonechewers. Mogor took in the air of Blade's Edge as they marched - he'd been secluded in his chambers for far too long now. The time had come now for him to unleash his fury.

 *"Mogor? But Ner'zhul said you were dead!"* One of the Bonechewers had stopped dead in his tracks before the ogre-mage as the attack begun.

 *"Believe not all that you hear, fool!"* Mogor bellowed out at the Bonechewer warrior before blasting him with a bolt of lightning. The ogre-mage let out a laugh as he fought, delighted at the thought of his return striking such discord amongst the other clans.

No matter what, the Laughing Skull will always emerge victorious.
