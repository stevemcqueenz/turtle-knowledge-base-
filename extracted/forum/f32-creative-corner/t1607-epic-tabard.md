So my awesome wife my made a lovely gift the other day and it's based on my Original Character and his Guild back in  2004.
I rocked a paladin as my main back then, and as I do now. The hoodie is so comfortable and I'm so happy with the outcome, it's the best gaming loot I've got and I've a got few around. I'm a big geek at heart and this is great to have.

My Original Character has levelled up from the world of Azeroth and made it into the world of Dungeon and Dragons, and now I can equip my old Guild tabard when playing D&D with my Original Guild member's who are my brothers and old school class mate.

It makes its easier getting into character and its amazing to see this character come from computer games to table top and you never know maybe larping could be next. Its looks like I'm slowing getting an outfit together.
Got the Tabard and Ring now maybe next I will take ago at making a Mace and Shield.
