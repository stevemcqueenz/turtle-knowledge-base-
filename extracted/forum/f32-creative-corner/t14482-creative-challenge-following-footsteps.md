# Creative Challenge: Following Footsteps

## Post 98737 by Moongirl — 2024-07-07T00:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98737#p98737 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Are you feeling up for a thought experiment? Of course, there is nothing stopping you from doing this in the game! Credit to this post: [viewtopic.php?t=2825](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=2825) - I would of never thought of doing this if I did not read this!

Lets say you are a hunter, and want to be just like Alleria Windrunner... or you are a shaman who finds Thrall a role model? Perhaps you want to take the dark path and be like the Lich King...

 **Here is the challenge:** Make a character who wants to follow the footsteps of a major character in the lore! What class would it be, and what talents will you master? Just because you want to follow Thrall doesn't mean you have to be an orc or a shaman, though it may make more sense to.

 **Extra challenge:** How about following a character that isn't exactly one class or the other? I would be interested to see what you can come up with. Perhaps if you make this an actual character, you can dress them up too?

This is just to encourage more creativity in the game, or just in yourself! I hope you find this challenge fun, and good luck on your journey!   smiling_turtle_head
