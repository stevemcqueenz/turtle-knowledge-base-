# Armor Patches / Durability Repair through Professions.

## Post 107224 by ius29159kisoq — 2024-11-09T13:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107224#p107224 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Currently, to fix your gear while far from a town/city, you need to own repair bots, be lucky to find a repair vendor, or go back to one of the towns/cities.

I suggest a 4th option: Armor Patches; craftable, stackable patches, made with simple materials, that can be applied to a piece of gear to increase it's durability. These would be available to Leatherworking, Blacksmithing and Tailoring, but perhaps could be added to some other professions.

Something like these:

Runecloth patch - requires 4 runecloth, increases an item's durability by 20.

Rugged Leather patch - requires 2 rugged leather, increases an item's durability by 30.

Thorium chainlink patch - requires 1 thorium bar, increases a mail item's durability by 35.

Thorium plate - requires 2 thorium bar, increases a plate item's durability by 40.

There would also be different levels of each of the patches, e.g. Linen patch, Truesilver plate, Heavy hide patch, etc.

These would stack to a multiple of 12 preferably as there are 12 slots that can have durability on them (all except trinkets, neck, rings, tabard and shirt).

Please do tell me what you think, how this could be improved, etc, etc.

Not a bad idea, and it fits RP well to be able to repair your own items if you have mastered your respective profession. Only thing is the 'patches' sounds like adding a layer over top of a hole in armor - maybe that sounds logical for cloth or leather, but for mail and plate that doesn't sound practical.

So I would propose just allowing a repair ability in the profession book that maybe requires a small reagant(s) (e.g. extra thread for cloth/leather and extra metal + forge for mail/plate). The repair ability would have a crafting time bar same as when crafting a new item. So it allows players that RP flavor with added utility and less expensive, but still at the cost of spending the time using your repair ability vs. using a repair vendor which is instant.

I like the idea from a flavor perspective, practically however you would never go for increased durability if there are other beneficial enchants. Situations in which you actually run out of durability are exceedingly rare. You usually run out of bag space, quests or play time before your gear breaks. Even in progression raiding the repair bot saves the problem.

Still increased durability could be a nice flavorful side effect for leather kits, crafted items and so forth. Could also see enchants that reduce durability loss in order to save money in the long run.
