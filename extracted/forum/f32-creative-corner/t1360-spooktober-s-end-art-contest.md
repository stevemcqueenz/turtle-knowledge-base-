# Spooktober's End Art Contest.

## Post 8299 by Gheor (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2020-10-29T22:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8299#p8299 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hail Turtle Community!

With Spooktober coming to an end the Turtle WoW Staff Team is hosting yet another art contest.

While Azeroth is a fun world we’ve been part of for years, it has its dark moments.
Show us your scariest experience.
What made you shiver as you leveled through a zone?

As last time there will no entry requirements, both drawn and digital art are welcomed to the table.
Will it be Stitches, a demon, or the terrible pentagram children?
Go wild.
You have two weeks.
At the end, the community will pick the best three, and the winners will be awarded 200 turtle tokens.

Upload your art on <https://imgur.com/> or <https://imgbb.com/> and share via the forums.

I am ready to shake in my boots.

I wish you good luck and hope you will have fun while you’re at it.

And never forget: the night is dark and full of turtles!
