# Shaman rework idea - not proposal

## Post 140607 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-07-20T13:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140607#p140607 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

In this post I wanna do some shaman talent tree (and ability) theorycrafting.
Didn't put it under suggestions since none of this is tested, I just wanted to ensure the logic itself is sound.
So, would it work? I think, on paper, yes, but I have no way to ensure it.
Will avoid using exact numbers wherever possible for the same reason: I cannot be sure what the correct number range is.

 **Why do I wanna write this?**
I like shamans, they are my fav class. And while I want all builds to shine, game balance comes first.
I'd cut down shaman PVP might as well as many Alliance PVP-ers because balance comes before personal preferences.
The goal is to check how the different builds can be built (imo) in an interesting, strong but not OP way.

 **Disclaimer**
Shaman's hybrid nature poses a challenge from the beginning. We gotta enable builds without creating PVP monsters by cherry-picking from trees.
I'll also nerf the baseline a bit and allow the trees, specially the late talents to carry the specs a bit more.

 **Ability changes**
I wanna meddle with some base abilities for later balance.
First is *Windfury Totem.* Would keep the extra hit effect and the percentuals but would make the free hits impossible to crit or to trigger any abilities or effects.
It would basically be a damage buff defined in a very roundabout way. Wouldn't proc flurry for example.
Windfury would still be good but not absurdly good.
Consequently, Windfury Weapon hits would work the same way. Less damage, less procs but somewhat more consistent threat.

Second is *Earth Shock.* It would still interrupt and do big damage but wouldn't lock you out from a spell school for two seconds. You got interrupted during a frostbolt? You can recast it right away.

 *Totem off-CD.* Would stay for some, but the below ones would receive a GCD:
Poison cleansing totem, disease cleansing totem, magma totem, fire nova totem, tremor totem.
These are either high-impact utility totems or high-damage totems which could 'benefit' from not being off-CD.
I think magma already has it. Or fire nova.

I'd also remove *windwall totem* from the game. Shamans have many unique utilities. The totem is never used anyway and would help us focus down the class' utility reach a bit on paper.
Would also remove *healing stream* totem. No need for quasy HoT because it raises weird design questions.

Would add a scaling damage mitigation to *stoneskin totem* (probably based on character stamina).
Would add better sp scaling to *damaging fire totems.*
Would remove *flametongue totem* (super boring as it is) and would readd it later, via ele talent with a different effect.

Would weaken lightning shield. Would add an enhancement talent which makes shields more effective.
With that talent, *lightning shield* would be as it is, water shield would be much better, earth shield would be somewhat better.
With base form, lightning shield is weaker, others are as they are now.
Would make *ghost wolf* cast time 1,5 sec for everyone.

With this, we made some drastic changes to the shaman. Got overall weaker but it allows us to be bolder via the talents.

Talent changes

Elemental
 *Row1:* Convection and Concussion stays
 *Row2:* Elemental Warding stays.

Elemental Devastation will go to Enhancement tree.
Earth's Grasp is close to being irrelevant. Would switch it for a damage spell penetration talent.
Instead of doing it the boring way, I imagine it as something like Improved Resistance Totems.
Fire ressistance totem for example would give the normal fire res and on top of it would give a party-wide spell penetration for fire spells.
The most important one for ele is nature resistance.
It is an air totem, challenging windfury. Windfury got nerfed. Still, many ele will use windfury totem. But now, they have two good air totem tools to use in need: tranquil air and nature res.
At the second row it is easily reachable for enhancement, too, buffing their damaging fire totems.

Third talent in the row could be a single-target fire spell, which is spammable, in case nature res is too high in the target. Not necessarily a theft from later releases. Can be just a boring, single target spell.

 **Row3:** Elemental Focus and Reverberation are good. Call of Thunder and Lightning Mastery should be swapped with a caveat. Lightning mastery giving a percentual damage nerf to your faster LB, CHL (and the fire spell) which is removed by fully learning Call of Thunder later. This allows ele to be much more fun pre late30 and be a valid leveling spec.

 **Row4:**
Improved fire totems also allow you to set up magma or fire nova totems in 10/20 yard distance (maybe with added CD to magma). Gives elemental shaman a pseudo-aoe.
Call of Flame is potentially good but I might consider a spell coefficient buff instead of a flat damage buff. Specially since elemental goes high sp.
Eye of the Storm is interesting. Okay for PVP but in PVE you are not supposed to be targeted. Would change it to a flat x% effect if you have earth shield on or after having dealt a spell crit.

 **Row5:**
Would add the new fire spell and flame shock to Storm Reach.
Electri... Khm... Elemental Fury.
Flame Guidance out.

 **Row6** was discussed earlier.
 **Row7:**
Elemental Mastery instead giving you a 10 second window where your spells deal n% less threat.
New talent (Flame Guidance's spot moved here): Elemental Totems:  Flametongue + Mana Tide. Flametongue is spellpower totem, fire element. Manatide is the old resto talent but has good passive mana regen now.

On paper, ele would have the below totem toolkit for a caster group:
Air: Tranquil AIr, Nat rez (in case of high nat rez enemies)
Water: Fire rez, Manatide
Fire: Flametongue, Frost rez (+ highly improved fire totem)

Enhancement

 **Row1**
Ancestral Knowledge is really strong now, maybe too strong.
Would make it only grant strength and agility (prime for enh). Making the other option more considerable for tanks and the talents 5 wasted points for eles or resto visiting enh tree.

Shield Spec: No reason for the added block amount if shaman has no realistic way to force out blows.
Instead: added block chance and added defense as long as the shield is held.

 **Row2:**
Removing improved ghost wolf. Made it baseline 1,5 sec for everyone. In its place, Elemental Devastation arrives.
Stable Shields looks cool but imo has a design problem. Ideally an enh shaman is supposed to quickly swap between shields: water if mana regen is needed, lightning for DPS, earth for defense. The talent's solution to have more orbs helps the class but doesn't reward clever swapping. Would keep orb number at three. Instead, the talent would reduce the mana cost of the shields and would make them more potent.

 **Row3:**
Calming Winds, Lightning Strike are good. Would make the armor buff from ancestral guardian agility-based to avoid the really bulky caster problem for PVP.

 **Row4:** Easiest solution is turning Flurry to 30% just like for the warriors. Alternatively we can make a shaman-tasting Flurry.
For example reducing the haste buff to 15% from 20% but adding a secondary effect (f.ex. SS and LS CD reduction).

Spirit Armor is good but should scale from agility, not giving a flat amount. Again, to avoid superbulky casters in PVP.

 **Row 5:** SS and Weapon Mastery talents getting swapped, making SS harder to reach. Also, WF weapon has a new secondary talent: the WF's extra hits have reduced threat.
Enhancing Totems: stoneskin getting a better mitigation scaling instead of the flat buff and the + blocked amount.

 **Row6:** SS then.
 **Row7:** Bloodlust is okay.

 **Resto**

Much less changes, just listing them here.
Healing Way: Includes Lesser Healing Wave but doesn't include Chain Heal. Chain Heal is overused already.

Improved water shield: some buff to the meditation percentages because in this variant shamans would have 3 orbs, not 9. Would keep it under the current mana regen though, it is absurd.

Restorative Totems: healing stream is out. Would buff the mana spring talent part though to makie it on par with max rank blessing of wisdom while making the totem cheaper since it kinda has to be spammed as we move.

Improved chain heal: small number tweak but a default 2 sec chain heal feels oppressively good. Maybe a 2,25 sec one would be better. Would also make shamans think what to use for healing.

_______

 **tldr:**
Base had some buffs but overall weakened.
Ele got the most changes. More caster utility, stronger fire totems, some spell penetration, better threat management, mana tide.
Enh: more shield-swap oriented gameplay, more reliable threat scaling, better fire totems. Tanks getting defense via talent and a better use of stoneskin for large packs.
Resto: slightly worse passive mana regen but mana spring made good. Slightly worse chain heal.
