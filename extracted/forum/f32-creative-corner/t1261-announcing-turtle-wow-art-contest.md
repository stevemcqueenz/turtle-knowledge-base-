# Announcing Turtle WoW Art Contest

## Post 7445 by Alisaie — 2020-09-02T14:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7445#p7445 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello turtles! I am Alisaie and I've come to announce a sweet new art contest!

Have you ever had a fun, embarrassing or otherwise amazing moment in a raid or dungeon? If so you're the right fit for this dungeon! Your task is to recreate that moment in an artpiece.

There are no entry requirements, it is only required that you do your best and have fun! Once the art pieces have been posted, the community will pick the top three drawings, who will receive 300 Turtle Tokens!

Upload your submissions on this website: <https://imgur.com/> or <https://imgbb.com> and share via forum.

We're giving you a week to make submissions: the contest will end on September 9!

I look forward to seeing many beautiful art pieces! Thank you!

We're postponing the art contest deadline by 2 days.   happy_turtle
