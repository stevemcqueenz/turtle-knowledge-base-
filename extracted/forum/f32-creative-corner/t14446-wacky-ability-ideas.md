# Wacky ability ideas

## Post 98372 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-07-02T12:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98372#p98372 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!
I don't consider the below ones suggestions because they are a bit wacky and very far away from the game we know.
I collected these in one place because I either think that an idea is awesome or hilarious. Compatible with this game? Debatable.

Some ideas are mine, some are not.

 **1. Hook pull aka. Death grip for warriors from a deep prot talent**
I really liked that deathknight ability and I believe TWOW will have no deathknights in the foreseeable future.
A death grip given only to prot warriors would be interesting though. They súck at PVP so a nice good utility doesn't break anything. Besides, deep prot would get something to show up against fury prot - repositioning.
Since warriors rarely use unholy powers I'd redesign the ability to be similar to Roadhog's hook pull from Overwatch.
What it'd do is an on CD range pull which generates a high amount of threat on the target.

 **2. Elemental pets**
Would imagine them as summonable pets for mages and for elemental shaman. Around the power level of an imp, without any buffs. Elemental pets would spam lower level frostbolt, fireball, arcane missiles or lightning bolt respectively.

 **3. Hunter taunt + threat reset trap - for kiting**
A hunter taunt would enable hunters to start kiting even after a fight has started with the help of the threat reset trap. There is a pesky mob which needs to be taken away from the tank - hunter goes there, taunts it off and makes it run thorugh the threat reset trap which he placed before pull. This way mob won't run back to the top threat in 2-3 seconds.

 **4. Rogue lockpicking -> Guaranteed trash loot in raids**
I'd place some chests in raids which can only be opened by 1-3 rogues with max lockpicking (1 in Kara10, 2 in 20 men raids, 3 in 40 men raids). The chest contains 1 trash loot from the respective raid. Would give the rogues a unique utility, specially during progression.

 **5. Druid water form and fishing**
Would allow druid's water form to fish (and to level fishing) while swimming in the water through an ability. Would give them an upgraded form of this ability later which also provides +fishing to offset the disadvantage of the lack of lures.

 **6. Rockbiter change**
Would give rockbiter a universal threat buff - while you use it, each of your damaging spells, melee hits and damages done through totems get an increased threat modifier (totem damage threat linked to you).

 **7. Buff-tweaking talent for hybrids**
What I mean under this is a talent for retri, enhancement shaman and feral druid which amplify their class' buffs on themselves. For example a retri, for 5 talents, getting a 25% bonus on any blessing it is on him, an enhancement shaman would get +25% from any totem stat or mana buff and a feral could have bigger benefits from mark of the wild (for less talent points, it is only one buff). I like the concept of 'selfishness within selflessness' and this mechanic would mirror that.

 **8. Hunter + pet damage synergy**
Would create a feedback system where pet crits increase ranged weapon damage and ranged crits increase pet damage. Should really be checked from PVP balance side but a way to meaningfully involve pets in raiding would be awesome.

 **9. Spammable fire spell for warlocks**
Sooner or later we'll get a raid based around shadow rez and warlocks can't respec to a build which doesn't spam shadowbolt in raids. A way to spam single target fire damage (without the threat issues of searing pain) would be cool.

 **10. Beast sacrifice for hunters**
'Come DigBick, my beloved boar, let me pigstick you for +20% ranged damage'. Jokes aside, no. The idea of a hunter saccing his beast for buff like a lock is hilarious but under no circumstance would I propose it.

 **Real 10. Enrage for cats**
Beasts can enrage. An on CD ability which makes them glow in red, makes them almost twice as big and would be a big damage spike would both feel awesome and would help with cat balance issues.

+1 Rogues providing stealth
But the target, while stealthed, cannot attack, and get's a serious damage/spell damage/heal debuff for 10 seconds after the stealth is over. Mostly a QoL for speedrunners: a rogue can give stealth to a lock and with another rogue they could advance without invisibility potion to summon the others elsewhere. Or to help your low level friend get to places he shouldn't go yet.
