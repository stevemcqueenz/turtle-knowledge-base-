# New times.

## Post 140217 by Zogtog — 2025-07-18T04:58:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140217#p140217 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Crossroads, the end of the line! - the goblin driver shouted and the kodo-drawn cart stopped.

The large orc jumped off the cart and grabbed his battered leather traveler's bag more comfortably. And although his muscles were still full of strength, gray hair had already noticeably colored his temples.

Everything here reminded him of his youth and those long years that he spent under the hot sun of these steppes, as if he had not left for many decades. Even the smell in the Barrens was the same. After long wanderings and a thousand battles, was he finally home?

Here are the viviers, here are the stalls, and here is the Burand tavern in place, nothing has changed at home.

 - Zog! Old warrior, is it really you? - the owner of the tavern, also not a young tauren, boomed and laughed.
 - We'll fight some more, I'm glad to see you, my old friend - the orc replied and they hugged.
 - Me too, really, how long have you been gone? Ten years? Twenty? More? - the owner of the establishment said and pushed a mug of fresh beer - on the house, help yourself. Young adventurers in the Wailing Caves still bring in a good income for me.
 - Yeah. In any gold rush, only shovel sellers make money - Zog smiled and took a few big gulps. Even the beer hadn't changed a bit over the years and was just as sovereign as in his youth. This whole idyll was broken by a nasal voice.
 - A mug of "Gadgetzanzy", unfiltered, with orange zest, please - the coin clattered and rolled along the bar counter towards the tauren.
 - A dwarf! Crossroads is under attack! - Zog roared and tried to grab his axe, but the tauren stopped him.
All the visitors stopped making noise and looked at the orc in bewilderment. Only now did he notice in the semi-darkness that there were not only trolls and orcs here, but also peoples, elves and even dwarves!
 - A lot has changed, brother, a lot. Everything is a little different here now - said the owner of the bar. In the ensuing silence, all that could be heard was the coin the dwarf had thrown onto the counter, slowly spinning. From the tails, the Turtle looked at the stunned orc, grinning.
