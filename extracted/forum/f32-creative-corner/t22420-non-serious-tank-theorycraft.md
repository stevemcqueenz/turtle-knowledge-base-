# Non-serious tank theorycraft

## Post 155674 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-10-15T11:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155674#p155674 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hey!

I don't wanna turn every class into a dungeon tank.
But I think it's a cool thing to theorycraft.
Will exclude warriors, palas, shammies and druids as they can be proper dungeon tanks even now.

For the sake of the argument I'd provide each class with a 10 sec taunt.
I think ideally taunt is only needed to tank if there are frequent threat reset mechanics but realistically DPS won't be hyperfocused to stay below threat curve so let's make it easier for everyone.

What do you need to tank a dungeon?
 * High-enough threat to prevent the dungeon becoming a slog. Optimally achieved through a threat modifier.
 * Enough mitigation to survive bosses without turning the healer a nervous wreck. At packs, either enough mitigation to similarly survive or enough crowd control to greatly reduce the incoming damage.

How would these tanks look like?

 **Hunter:**
 1. All they need is a threat modifier to aspect of the monkey and an own soul link which works as a shield proxy.
Traps and pet can reduce incoming damage (handle pet as an offtank which has to be kept alive) while fire trap and generic melee damage can ensure threat.
If we are fancy, hunter taunt can be distracting shot, making the hutners unique. A taunt with a huge range to help casters but one which doesn't work when melee takes over threat because they are too close.
Damage mitigation can come through pet screech.

 2. There is the BM way. Pet needs a talent for better threat scaling.

 **Rogue:**
They need a surprisingly small push for dungeons if we can assume, they can CC most mobs via stun. Imagine an opening like stealth into sap into gouge for example.
They'd need some armor or HP regain effect for bosses and spell-heavy encounters and agitating poison has to come way earlier.

 **Warlock:**
They are almost there.
They have a stone as a threat modifier, have mitigation (voidie passive, soul link, aegis) and CC.
They'd need better pushback prevention, better voidie damage prevention ultimate and maybe some push to voidie's master demonologist effect if it doesn't break PVP.

 **Priests:**
I'd imagine them as 20/0/31 shadow priests fishing for improved inner fire and improved power world shield.
They have some pretty respectable self-defense with inner fire, pw:s, shadowform and constant hot on themselves.
They'd definitely need a baseline shadow nova to pull this off and a threat modifier.
They would sneak in mindblasts between melee hits or go nova for a bigger crew.

 **Mages:**
Definitely freeze mage.
They would be unique in a sense that they are 100% kite tanks.
Give an optional threat modifier to frost spells and let them blizzard and blink for ever. Would probably be a cancer.
