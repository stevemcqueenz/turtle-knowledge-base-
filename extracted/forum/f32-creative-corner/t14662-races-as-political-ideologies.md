# Races as political ideologies

## Post 100023 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-07-21T09:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100023#p100023 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

This is a sketch made for entertainment, based on the existing race stereotypes.
Please don't ban.

So. The different playable races of Azeroth as political ideologies.

 **Goblins - Libertarians**
 * Minimal government - some goblins wanna get rid of the government altogether.
 * Minimal taxation allows growing entrepreneurs. Leading to mega-corporations.
 * Creating a wealth-based caste system after a while. Azeroth is led by a few megacorporations.
 * Destroys Azeroth in a speedrun for profit and products.

 **Gnomes - Technocrats**
 * Leaving all political decisions to scientists.
 * Scientists create an overcomplicated system and forget the human (gnomish) element.
 * Creating a technological utopia then blowing themselves up because an invention had a malfunction.
 * Migrating to Dwarfistan.

 **Dwarves - Classic Industrialism with some social welfare**
 * Main focus is mining and creating products from the mined goods.
 * Most inhabitants are either miners or artisans.
 * Killing Azeroth in a noob speed because they lack the innovations that goblins have.
 * Taking care of the individuals by safety nets, no matter how old, evil or gnomish he/she is.

 **Trolls - Primitivism**
 * Society is bad, parliament is a big cave.
 * Solving food shortages by making minorities livestocks.
 * Replacing science with weird rituals - if harvest golem brakes down, they make human sacrifice to repair it.
 * Led by big voo-doo shaman who'd totally pour Brawndo electrolytes on the wheat fields.

 **Night Elves - The Greens**
 * Mother Nature >>> Society
 * Once a tree grew in the middle of their main road. They demolished the road.
 * Showers are not invented yet.
 * Tried to cure AIDS by yeeting fadeleaf on the ill.
 * They don't understand how harvest golems work and they battle food shortages by peacefully starving to death.

 **Taurens - Hippies**
 * Protecting nature by being too lazy to industrialize.
 * They legalized smoking fadeleaf at Thunder Bluff a long time ago.
 * They recruit the biggest, baddest soldiers so the others don't attack them. The other don't know that these soldiers would refuse to fight. It's all for show.
 * Extremely developped milk industry.
 * Make moo, not war.

 **High Elves - Racial Supremacists**
 * There is only one correct race.
 * Capital has a 200m long mirror so every inhabitant can have an orgasm looking at their reflection at any time.
 * They don't know what's the difference between domestic animals and non-high elf allies.
 * If they were able to repopulate Azeroth as High Elf-only, they totally would.
 * Unironically believing in social darwinism.

 **Undeads - Posadists**
 * They believe that they need to destroy half of the planet to create a better society.
 * Noone really understands them.
 * They listen to their leader without any questions.
 * Nuclear radiation or plague is okay, they are dead anyway.
 * Might or might not be the allies of dolphins.

 **Humans - Neocon**
 * Standing on the top of a big political alliance.
 * Wanting to interfere anywhere in Azeroth to support human interests.
 * Might or might not attack the Thousand Needles if someone found oil there.
 * They are rich af and they want to keep it that way.

 **Orcs - MAGA (Make Azeroth Great Again)**
 * Building a wall in Azshara and making the night elves pay for it.
 * Since the appointment of Garrosh, minority work rights dwindled.
 * They brag about being the N1 country of Azeroth while living in the middle of a desert.
 * Fetishing big bulky orcs willing to die for the Horde.
 * Ruining their economy every now and then.
