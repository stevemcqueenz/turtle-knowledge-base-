# Half-serious proposal for hunter tanking

## Post 112882 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-12-16T12:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112882#p112882 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I'd propose some semi-serious changes to make hunter tanking (not pet tanking) a thing, without breaking any existing builds.

 **Why?:**
 * Further helps LFT problems, tanks are still scarce.
 * Gives the game a new archetype, a tank where you have to coordinate two units to do your job. Tank with traps is also a cool concept.

 **What hunter needs to tank:**
 * A taunt.
 * A threat modifier.
 * Survivability.
 * Pet's threat transfer.

For taunt, I'd go a unique way. Would give distracting shot a taunt, meaning that a tanking hunter has easy time to get back mobs from casters but struggles taking it back from nearby melee without moving. A unique flavor.

For threat modifier, I'd slap a flat 30% to the aspect of the monkey. It is mostly used in PVP or solo situations anyway where threat has no meaning. Putting the threat to the aspect of the wolf would grief melee hunters while creating a new aspect feels unnecessary. Your dodge aspect is also your tanking aspect.
Would also give a threat modifier to wing clip (maybe with a talent). It can be your 'revenge' or 'earth shock'.

For survivability, I'd change the Survivalist talent. Now it gives 10% HP for 5TP. I'd change it so it gives 10% HP and transfers 4...20% of the damage you take to your pet if your pet is alive. Hunters wear mail and can't use shields but this talent can work as a proper damage reduction if:
 * Hunter tanks choose bulky, defensive pets.
 * Defensive pets are used to OT less dangerous mobs, so they can survive a bit of extra hit.
 * Healers realize that in case of a hunter tank, they need to heal both the hunter and the pet all the time.

I don't think this would break other builds. In case of BM and MM, you usually have to fight your way through the pet first anyway. Melee hunter on the other hand is not too OP and could live with this random buff I think.
Compared to the warlock's soul link, it's 5% less damage transfer and it lacks the attack power buff. I justify this talent not being a capstone with these 'relative nerfs'.

This alone wouldn't be enough though.

 * Would also give survival a new talent  'below 21' in the tree, which for 5TP increases your armor gain from agility by 500%. By default 1 agility means 2 armor points. So a hunter with 300 agility gets 600 armor from this stat. With this talent 1 agi would give 10 armor, meaning 3000 armor gain in case of 300 agility.

 * For pet's threat, I'd introduce a new early talent, which for 2TP transfers 30% of your pet's threat to you. It allows the pet to OT a target without it running to you, and you also get an extra threat boost. As a general rule, mob tanked by the pet shouldn't be bothered by the DPS, though, pet has reduced threat generation.

 **How this would work out?**

 * Hunter having a really big single-target threat (aspect of the monkey, wingclip, threat from pet etc).
 * Hunter having some multitarget threat generation but in a more limited way (trap, carve, pet hitting the secondary target). Until fire aoe trap is spammable, aoe handling can be a weak point for them.
 * But hunter also has a toolkit to reduce the number of incoming mobs: freezing trap and a pet can take out 2 of them temporatily, always ensuring a manageable pack size.
 * Can very easily taunt away mobs from casters with distracting shot but to get a mob back from a melee, they have to out-DPS them or move away and do a distracting shot.
 * Pet always has to be used to tank a mob (to help the hunter's weakness of bad aoe tanking) while it also has to stay alive for the damage transfer.
 * Very good defensive CD under deterrence.
 * Just like shamans, hunter tanks can turn every fight into a kite anytime. Unlike shamans, they are even better at it with the aspect of the cheetah in the ready.
 * Managing movement for both units is extremely important for this build. For ex. pet's staying out of DPS's aoe with their target to avoid aggroing, hunters moving to shooting range to taunt from melee without pulling an additional mob, later setting up traps at specific places etc.

 **Do I seriously propose this?**
 * To a degree. I understand how ridiculous this sounds and I made 0 testing here. But if these things indeed don't break anything and TWOW decides to add them, then LFT gets in a better shape + people would totally enjoy a new tanking style which is unlike anything they have experienced (2 units tanking as one character).

Also, those big 2H swords with a bunch of stamina and some parry chance would finally find a place in PVE.
