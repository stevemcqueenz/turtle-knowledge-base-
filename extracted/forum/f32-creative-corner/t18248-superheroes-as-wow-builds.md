# Superheroes as WOW builds

## Post 124426 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-03-30T08:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124426#p124426 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I've recently watched some superhero movies and wanted to write a post about superheroes and their closest WOW build.

 **Marvel Universe**

 **Hawkeye** - An easy MM hunter.
 **Black Widow** - Def a rogue. I see no reliance on poisons and she is really good at prolonged combat, I'd give her a combat rogue.
 **Thor** - Enh shaman. Lightning magic, reliance on a big hammer, he just screams enh.
 **Captain America** - Prot paladon. A build with a shield is a must here and I think that Cap's values can be easily translated into paladin values.
 **Hulk** - Fury warrior. Def a rage user. As an alternative I kind of see a feral druid where Hulk is the bear mode using rage but warrior fits him more than druid in my opinion, despite the 'kind of' shapeshifting.
 **Scarlet Witch** - Was thinking a lot about her. She seemed like a mage but not really. I felt some warlock vibes but no demon in sight. At the end decided on shadow priest. She has a light-darkness dichotomy with Vision and losing him plus she seems to possess a magic amplified by her madness. She also has some healing which indicates priest over mage or warlock.
 **Dr. Strange** - Arcane mage. No explanation needed.
 **Ancient One** - Let's allow later classes, too so I can say monk. Windwalker in my opinion.
 **Shang Chi** - Windwalker Monk, again.
 **Iron Man, Warmachine, MCU Spiderman** - I'd give them all the enhancer class if it existed in WOW. All I can do is to max out their eng skill level for now.
 **Kraven** - Survival hunter.
 **Thanos** - I think an arms warrior fits him the best, using his big sword.
 **Loki** - Again, no demonic buddy but he really gives me warlock vibes. Not high on burst. Will go affliction lock with demonic sacrifice so there's a reason we don't see the demon.
 **Mysterio** - The weird subtlety rogue who RPs as a mage with engineering items.
 **Green Goblin** - Survival hunter (they rely heavily on bombs in retail) with an epic flying mount.
 **Mystique** - Assassination rogue having all of the illusions in her bag. Same goes to **Chameleon**.
 **Ben Grimm** - Fury warrior.
 **Human Torch** - Gotta be a fire mage.
 **She-Hulk** - Same as Hulk.

 **DC Universe**

 **Batman** - Was thinking a lot about this one but probably subtlety rogue. Attacking from the shadows and going back to stealth, high reliance on surprise and silent takedowns.
 **Scarecrow** - Shadow priest. Thrives on the concept of fear but has no demonic allies.
 **Bane** - Another fury warrior.
 **Poison Ivy** - High on nature magic. Even without shapeshifting I think she is closer to a druid than to a shaman. Maybe balance, without the moonkin form.
 **Mr. Feeze** - I mean... Freeze Mage.
 **Killer Croc** - Here I can go Feral Druid.
 **Green Arrow** - Another MM hunter.
 **Flash** - My best guess is some kind of hunter spamming aspect of the cheetah.
 **Wonder Woman** - Gives me ret paladin vibes.
 **Ra's al Ghul** - Another windwalker monk.
 **Deadshot** - MM hunter.
