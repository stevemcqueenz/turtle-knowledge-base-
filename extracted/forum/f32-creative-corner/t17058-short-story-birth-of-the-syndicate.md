# Short Story: Birth of the Syndicate

## Post 116325 by Aeliren (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-01-12T11:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116325#p116325 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Aiden Perenolde had been accustomed to living in lavish conditions and being attended by servants and nobility for his whole life. The significantly more humble abode of Dandred's Fold was decidedly not his residence of choice, though in these times, the old vacation estate of the Perenolde family was one of the few locations that remained overlooked by Alliance authorities.

 *"You were such a fool, father."* His son and heir Aliden sat across from him at the table, glaring at him accusingly. A regular occurrence these days. Beve, his daughter and eldest child, was quietly sitting by the fireplace, staring into the crackling flames.

Before their exile, the devout Aliden had been lending his divine abilities as a mercenary in New Stormwind on the behalf of his mentor and benefactor, Aedelas Blackmoore, and Beve had been studying magic in Dalaran. All this took end when Aiden bargained with the Horde of Draenor, granting them the Book of Medivh that one of his agents stole from Stormwind in exchange for the destruction of Lordaeron and Stromgarde's garrisons on his borders. This was the last straw for the Alliance, resulting in Aiden and all known allies of his to be cast out into the wilds - forever to be seen as pariahs of human society.

While Beve had willingly left school to join her family in exile out of devotion to her kin and questioned not his actions, the same could not be said of Aliden, who made his loathing of him and the loss of his birthright very evident. He still wore the colors of Stormwind's mercenaries - a rebellious streak that irked Aiden to no end, and in retrospect perhaps why Aliden persisted in doing so.

 *"Are we to repeat this tiresome argument once again? I had no choice! You could see just as well as I did what those orcs had done to the southern kingdom, and they were doing the same to the dwarves and Stromgarde! Had I not cut a deal with them, Alterac..."* Aiden's voice faltered, and he slumped over dejectedly in his chair remembering the recent decline of Alterac in the aftermath of the Second War. Ruined, and the once shining capital city sacked by the Alliance forces and left to be occupied by ogres. Already, Lordaeron has annexed Strahnbrad, which had now been made into an integral portion of the King's Road that lead through Andorhal. *"Well... I suppose it wouldn't be worse than what it is now."*

 *"Two years, father. Two years of my efforts to redeem the legacy of our family name!"* Aliden rose from his chair as he ranted. *"And just as I was beginning to make progress, what did you do?!"*

 *"I ensured that Lordaeron and Stromgarde would no longer hound our people and threaten our sovereignty!"* Aiden interjected defensively.

 *"You helped the Horde, again! I had Trollbane's ear, yet in your great single-minded selfishness you ruined everything I had been working towards! And now here we stand, having lost everything and being labeled traitors! Did you even think about what they would believe, when it was discovered that an Alteraci was the one who stole the Book of Medivh from them?"* Bitterly, Aliden softly added, *"What would mother say if she could see you now?"*

Aiden winced at his son's remark, and even Aliden seemed to regret his rash choice of words - though if only because he noticed Beve's pained reaction. Isolde's passing just shortly before the orcish incursions had spared her from seeing the destruction of Alterac and the downfall of their family, but even now, they missed her terribly so. Every so often, Aiden couldn't help but wonder perhaps if she'd been there... *"Your mother would understand,"* he muttered half-heartedly, *"She would know that I did what a king must do in difficult times."* But he shook his head - he'd come this far, he could not allow himself to muse on it any longer.

Aliden snidely snorted as he sat back down. *"If you insist so, father."*

 *"The two of you disgust me,"* Beve finally spoke up. *"Every day, you're always complaining about the past, yet neither of you are trying to change anything even as the other nations take from ours piece by piece. I've stayed this long because we are family, but I tire of this."* She got up from her chair and made to leave the estate. *"I admired you once, father, but this sorrowful echo of you... If this is how you choose to spend your remaining days, then so be it."*

Something in what Beve had said spurred Aiden to action. *"...Wait."*

Beve paused, turning back in the doorway at her father's voice, and after a moment of silence, Aiden now was the one who stood up from his chair. *"...Perhaps you are right, my daughter. This bickering... it is pointless, and it is precisely what Terenas and the rest of the Alliance wants."* Aiden ran a hand through his graying-brown hair, a gleam of determination replacing the tired look of sorrow in his eyes as his daughter's words awakened something within him. A rage had laid dormant from the demoralizing aspect of his constant circular arguments with his son, a fury at the other monarchs that parted his lands between themselves as he rotted away in the wilds, all while they had forced him in this position by leaving Alterac to fend for itself against the encroaching Horde.

 *"Our people have been left without homes and leadership, and in our absence, Alterac is being dismantled and given away. We must take action and reclaim our home."* He could see that Beve's interest had been piqued at the idea of doing something more than festering and bickering every day in their old estate, though Aliden remained unconvinced.

 *"Oh? And which orcs shall you call upon for your schemes this time? Oh, but yes, how could I forget - they have denied us ever since our exile."*

Aiden glared at his son at the impertinent remark, though he held his tongue before continuing. *"Not orcs, no. We shall call upon the very people of Alterac. The deprived - those who like us have been robbed, are lost, forgotten, and hiding. We are not alone to have been cast out. Even if by force... we must retake what is rightfully ours."*

Beve was beaming at her father's newfound resolve and seeing the change in his disposition, shutting the door behind her as she returned indoors. *"At last!"* She rushed over and gave him a hug, which took him by surprise and almost toppled the older man over. *"My father has returned to us."*

Aiden returned the hug, and held a hand towards Aliden. *"My son, I ask you, will you look beyond our past disagreements and join me in this endeavor?"*

Aliden, after a moment, sighed and reached out, taking his father's hand. *"Very well. For Alterac, I will."*

.............................................................................................................................................

A few days later, the Perenoldes had managed to call upon many of the members of Alteraci nobility and supporters who had been most loyal to him in secret. In the field of Dandred's Fold, Aiden had prepared a modest podium from the farming equipment, and he had prepared a speech for them to convince them to rally under him once more for the purpose of reclaiming Alterac. The 'Syndicate', he had decided to call it. Already he had managed to capture the crowd, playing to their feelings of abandonment, betrayal and resentment towards the Alliance.

 *"...stripped of our lands, our wealth, our stations - and for what? Attempting to ensure the preservation of our nation, our people, our culture? They say that our honor has been blemished. I say that they were the ones who betrayed us, leaving us alone to fend off and be destroyed by the Horde so that Lordaeron could be the one to prosper. We did what we had to do to ensure our survival... and so here we are today, still alive. Lordaeron could not take that from us, despite its best efforts."*

A few of the nobles had been nodding their heads along. Most of them had even openly agreed with him back when he'd proposed to ally with the Horde. Behind the crowd, Aiden could see his son speaking with some of the more notable aristocrats - Falconcrest and Vardus in particular. He smiled - Aliden perhaps was not as devoted to him and this new cause as his sister was, with Beve being captivated by seeing the spark that had been reignited in her father once again, but he must had evidently inherited Aiden's charisma from the way the discussion appeared to be going. Falconcrest glanced over at him and nodded before turning back to Aliden.

 *"Today, I say, is the end of Lordaeron's tyrannical occupation of sovereign Alteraci territory,"* he continued his speech, *"Their mismanagement has turned our proud nation into a waste - ogres now occupy our city, and our lands are being annexed and fought over by people not of Alterac, our people forcefully displaced from their homes! This is their 'mercy', they call it! Are we to allow this to continue? No more, I say! You have followed me before, when our alliance with the orcs has ensured that we've survived the war. Will you follow me once more?"*

The assembled crowd cheered in response. Aiden smirked, pleased with himself. As the crowd dispersed and they spoke amongst each other, Aiden walked over to the back of the field where Aliden was still speaking with Falconcrest and Vardus.

 *"Lord Falconcrest, Baron Vardus. I am pleased to see that you've made it. Have you already given thought to my proposal?"*

 *"Yes, indeed."* Falconcrest's eyes shifted between Aliden and Aiden. *" We've spoken at lengths with your son as you discussed your... vision. It is well enough time that Alterac reclaims its place in the world. For far too long have we had to fend for ourselves like... peasants."* Falconcrest had practically spat out the last word, and Aiden could've sworn he saw the spittle fly through the air.

Aiden was aware that the young lord, who had been but a boy when his family was deposed, resented his status and believed that his family would have been able to get lesser sentences from the Alliance rather than being exiled. The elder Lord Falconcrest had long seen Aiden as a rival, and it appeared that he had imparted that on his son. Aiden made a mental note to keep an eye on the young exiled lord.

 *"Indeed,"* Baron Vardus added, shaking Aiden from his thoughts, *"I too am in agreement. If I may, I would like to suggest an outline for the sustainment for our new... 'Syndicate'. We are currently small in number should all those present remain loyal to you, but I believe we could easily rustle up some of the local banditry to bolster our numbers. However, we will require further materials and equipment."*

 *"Very well."* Aiden nodded at the man. *"Baron Vardus, I hereby name you chief quartermaster of the Syndicate. All manner of material sustainment shall henceforth fall under your jurisdiction."* He turned to Lord Falconcrest. *"We must rally as many as we can to our cause. The responsibility of recruitment and training falls upon you, Lord Falconcrest."*

 *"As you command, Your Majesty."* Both nobles nodded.

 *"Alterac shall rise again."*

.............................................................................................................................................

After dismissing Falconcrest and Vardus so they could begin to make preparations for their new duties, Aiden and Aliden had retired back inside the lakeside manor, Aiden having settled down back at the table as Aliden prepared drinks taken from the estate's wine cellar, serving a cup for himself and another for his father. Beve had remained behind at Aliden's suggestion to round up all those amongst the ousted nobles and their remaining retainers with magical potential for their new Syndicate.

 *"It would seem that my ability to turn a phrase has not diminished over the years."*

 *"Indeed, father."* Aliden handed his father a glass. *"A toast, to Alterac's reclamation."*

Aliden drank from his cup before addressing his father. *"Of course, there remains the matter of what should be done with the green-skinned monsters that we once foolishly called allies that still roam our lands. Perhaps you have convinced the nobility to ally with them before, but they are no equals to humanity. And do not think that they forgot that no orc has come to aid us in our exile, and that no orc has helped Alterac in its time of need. I think perhaps that we should look to the Alliance's internment camps for inspiration."*

Aiden furrowed his brows as he sipped from his drink. *"Blackmoore has parted his ideals onto you all too well, I see."*

 *"Not merely Lord Blackmoore, father. Do not feign innocence - you know well that I learned where your true beliefs lie after the war. Had the tables been turned, you'd see them be made slaves as well."*

 *"...A gross exaggeration of what I said, I'm certain."* Aiden replied more meekly than he'd intended. The orcs had been surprisingly cordial allies to his sailors and servants during the Second War - at least until they had been captured by the Alliance. Yet, a part of him had been left wondering when he'd heard of the internment camps that the Alliance had created to hold them, as well as the construction of the statue of Sir Lothar before Blackrock Spire through orcish labor - not helped by a mix of bitterness and alcohol, which had resulted in a drunken rant one night that he was later relieved that no one had witnessed other than his son.

But before he could craft a defense for his then-drunken statements, Aiden wrinkled his nose and his legs wobbled, feeling weak all of a sudden. *"I... What vintage is this? It has... quite an odd taste."*

 *"That taste, father, would be the poison I slipped in your drink taking effect."* Aliden stated matter-of-factly, though there was a slight shiver in his voice as he revealed his subterfuge.

 *"P-poison?"* Aiden sputtered out in shock, *"Aliden, what is the meaning of this?!"*

 *"The other nobles and I spoke during your rally. They agree with what you said, yes... but they have no desire to follow a man who has betrayed his own race to inhuman monsters twice over. They have accepted to follow me, on the condition that you are ensured to no longer be a liability to us or humanity."* He paused, before throwing back into his father's face the words he had uttered so often to defend his own betrayal. *"I had no choice."*

As the poison raced through him, Aiden reached across and grasped his son's lapel, staring into his eyes as he started to slump over the table. *"Everything I've done... I did... for Alterac..."* Aiden said as his eyes bulged out, the life slipping from him as his grasp was quickly weakening.

For just a brief moment, Aliden's cold composure broke as he held his dying father in his arms. *"...I know, father."*

.............................................................................................................................................

The poison provided by Lord Falconcrest claimed the life of Lord Aiden Perenolde just shortly afterwards. Aliden closed shut his father's eyes as he laid his body on the floor.

The act of patricide had left Aliden conflicted. On one hand, it secured his own ascendency, as he would now have ensured the support of all the nobility. Yet, for as much as Aiden's repeatedly treasonous actions had infuriated him, he was still his father, and to assassinate the man who had raised him from childhood was quite a step further - a reprehensible act of familial betrayal in of itself, the hypocrisy of it was not lost upon him. He could only pray that he could find forgiveness in the Light, and that Beve would understand that he did what was necessary.

Pushing his guilt away, Aliden left the estate, upon which he found himself accosted by the group of nobles he'd been discussing with during his father's rally headed by Lord Falconcrest.

 *"Is the deed done?"*

 *"Yes."* Aliden stated. *"Today marks the beginning of a new era. No longer will we hide in the shadow of the so-called Alliance, nor will we allow ourselves to be shackled to the fallen Horde. Today, we take fate into our own hands - we are the Syndicate! Alterac is our birthright, and we shall reclaim it!* Spoken much like his father, Aliden mused, but he had to present a strong front before these men. Any sign of weakness on his part would be seen as an opportunity. *And once our work is done, the orcs shall be put in their rightful place - beneath our rule."*

Aliden raised his fist in the air to mark the end of his speech, to which the assembled nobles cheered. A new power had risen in Alterac, and Aliden would see that he claimed what is rightfully his by birth that the other northern kingdoms would have seen him denied for his father's sins. Though caught up in his own fervor, the prince had missed the duplicitous gleam in Falconcrest's eyes...
