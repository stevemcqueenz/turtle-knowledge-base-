# Alternative threat model

## Post 158779 by Xudo (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-11-07T16:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158779#p158779 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Thought was started from question: why pvp is so different from pve in terms of group dynamic?
Main difference is that PvP is about CCing/killing healer first and only then - dealing with others.
In PvE heals are considered as safe role - they get guarded by dedicated tanks.

Whole tank+healer+dps model is a staple for wow, so I don't think it should be changed. But as mental gymnastics it is interesting to imagine different model.

So, main points:
 1. All mobs should be slowable
 2. Threat counts for whole group of mobs. Any additional mobs dragged into fight, share full threat data.
 3. Healing threat is multiplied twice (instead of halved) It also should not be divided equally among all mobs. Healers should get top threat by default.
 4. If mob receives any slow effect, then threat of all players outside of melee range is halved down in comparison.
 5. Melee mobs should fight with melee players and flee if they can't.
 6. Ranged mobs should fight with ranged players and flee if they can't.

What I expect to get:
a. If melee mob is not slowed, he attacks top threat (healer>dps).
b. If melee mob slowed, he attacks top threat in melee range.
c. If melee mob slowed and noone in melee range, mob run away for help.
d. If ranged mob has enemy in melee range, mob run away towards help until noone will be in his melee range.
e. If ranged mob is slowed and noone in melee range, mob run away for help. (same as melee)
f. If ranged mob is not slowed and noone in melee range, mob attacks top threat.
g. "Run for help" means choosing whatever is closer: getting to adjacent obstacle and breaking LOS or to other pack of mobs.

What are consequences.
AoE farm will be super annoying because mobs wont't run towards the mage. They will run away from him. AoE farm will be super annoying and I think it is good.
There will be no dedicated tank. Mob will attack highest dps. So all melee dps should also care about their own defence. Swap to shield or run away for a moment.
It will be easier for people to manage "threat" - just do highest DPS and thats all. Mobs will automatically focus best melee DPS and try to kill him. AoE threat problem won't be a thing.
Slow will become mandatory option. Without it, mobs will nuke the healer or snowball to super pack and eventually nuke everyone.
All melees now have tools to slow enemies. Mages could help them with Blizzard or chain-frostbolt against enemies.
It will be possible to "tank" as mage if you have any melee in party.

For warriors. Just charge and apply hamstring to other melee enemies. Don't slow ranged ones. It doesn't matter which spec you are - you effectively will be tank if you deal good damage. Just keep your shield ready.
If you intentionally want to tank - you still should be good DPS.
For paladin "tanks" - you can just tank in Holy spec - heal everyone or yourself. Mobs will be agro to healer naturally. You won't need mix of different stats to be good tank. Just stack healing power and survivability. Strength is not needed. Though you will need Spiritual focus to tank efficiently.
For paladin DPS - don't heal. It will draw all agro to you.
For ranged hunters - your pet will effectively be tank against melee enemies if you slow them with Concussive and/or Wing Clip. Though you won't be to kite while your pet deal damage with Growl off. If mob get any slow, he instantly switch to pet. Though your job should be killing ranged mobs and don't use any slows.
For melee hunters - same as for warriors, but with option not to chase ranged mobs to kill them.
For rogues DPS - just deal damage, but be ready to turn Evasion on. Just as usual I think.
For rogues "tanks" - apply damaging poison and ask teammates to slow enemies. You still need to be best DPS, but dealing that DPS to single target is enough to keep threat of whole pack.
For priests ranged DPS - deal with ranged mobs first then switch to melee mobs.
For priests healing - pray that your team know how to slow melee enemies and that ranged DPS deal good damage against ranged ones. Otherwise you will be focused and dead (just like it is usually on battlegrounds).
For shaman healers - put Earthbind totem. It will switch melee mob attention from you to melees in your team. It also will entertain ranged mobs so they get away from you. You effectively can tank ranged mobs because of mail gear and shield.
For shaman tanks - Drop stoneskin and go healing. Simple as that. You will be automatically focused by all mobs.
For shaman melee DPS - same as for warriors - drop Earthbind to save healer.
For shaman ranged DPS - focus ranged mobs.
For mage - focus other ranged mobs first. Use Fire or Arcane for that. Kill melee mobs with Frost, but keep in mind that you need melee buddy around to prevent fleeing.
For warlock - use Coex to make your voidwalker tank melee mobs. Focus ranged mobs first.
For druid healers - apply Rejuvination and regrowth and switch to bear in case of emergency.
For druid tanks - be best melee DPS or the only melee against melee enemies. If you are not best DPS, switch to cat, become one and switch to bear back.
For druid ranged DPS - focus ranged mobs first so they won't kill your healer and don't bring reinforcements.
For druid melee DPS - just deal damage and switch to bear if you did too good.

So in conclusion, tanking become easy: just apply slow to every melee mob and deal damage. If you don't do it yourself, then your teammates can do it.
DPSing will mean tanking from time to time, so you have to balance between survivability and damage. Or between good damage and group utility. For example applying sunder armor instead of dealing damage.
Mages and warlocks with CoEx can control battlefield and targets of mobs.
