Hello all,

first of all, i know this is a farfetched story and probably won't come to fruition, but as we enter 2.0 and the possibilities are expanding, i really hope that the devs will see that it's time to right the wrong. this is my suggestion for necromancer class.

Specializations: dark wizard/sorcerer; necrolyte; lich? [ pure range caster ]
necrolord; summoner; embalmer [ semi-caster, reliying on minions ]
death / unholy / dread knight; grim reaper [ melee, less minion more benefit ]

Resource(s)	Primary: Mana
Secondary: Scourgestone [ warlocklike, but nah, i like simple things ]

Primary attribute: Intellect

Weapon skills: Staves, Daggers, Polearms, off-hand shields and frills [One-handed Swords, Wands?, Relics(Scourgestones)? ]

Armor type: Cloth

Available to: Alliance [ Humans, Gnomes, Dwarfs ] & Horde [ Ocrs, Trolls, Goblins, Ogres! ]

Magic Proficiency: Arcane [ they are wizards at first], Nature [ poisons, diseases ],  Dark/Shadow

Dark Wizard / Sorcerer; Necrolyte; [Lich?]:
Pure caster, similar to lock destro/ele shaman playstyle. without relying on minions or at least not benefiting from them so much.

Necrolord; Summoner; Embalmer:
Not complete caster nor a capable fighter, but master of puppets. Improving his servants thus getting himself stronger. Similar to demo warlock playstyle but with difference that minions do not have special abilities, just their sheer numbers benefit necromancer.

Death Knight; Unholy Knight; Grim Reaper, Dread Knight:
A capable fighter, even stronger when not having minions. Id make this spec play like wotlk unholy DK/assa rogue/enha shaman. Focusing on mele combat, spreading diseases and boosting armor. While first two specs are mostly straightforward specs, this mele one is more interesting to build. and it can be complicated, and as i sed i like simple things. Im thinking how to implement wotlk uDK diseases in a simple way. It would be cool to use poison coating like rogue or weapon imbues like shaman. Only difference would be id give only 2 wpn imbues a poison and shadow one. and only for cosmetic purposes and ofc for different types of magic. U apply coating/imbue > make dmg poison/shadow > and with that u can choose to spread dot for aoe or to make single dmg. Simple. With this spec to work u have to have bladed weapons - Daggers or Polearms.

Abilities:

Curses:   - Decrepify - A crippling curse that deals shadow damage [ *Corruption ] *shadow dmg
( here id make a twist.. its based on max hp, so more hp target has more dmg it takes,
or that could be ultimate talent )
 - Confuse - Confuses monsters, causing them to switch alliance [ pvp-pve ]
 - Attract - Causes other monsters to target selected target [ *rogues Trics of trade ]
 - Curse of Thorns - Curses an enemy  to take damage on attack.
 - Dim Vision - Decreases radius of vision. [ *shaman Calm elements ]
 - Amplify Damage - Increases the amount of damage received. [*?]

Casts:  - Bone Armor - Creates a protective shield of bone that absorbs physical damage 3 charges.
 - Bone Spear - Summons a bone missile that can damage enemies.
[ *Lightning bolt ] *arcane dmg
 - Bone Prison - Summons a ring of bone to surround a target. [ *Entangling Roots ]
 - Bone Spikes - Summon bone spikes from the ground dealing damage.
[ AoE, *Flamestrike, *physical damage ]
 - Poison Nova - A ring of poison explodes from the Necromancer.
 - Bone Spirit - A ghostly haunting turns loose to seek the prey and drain it of Life.
[ *Haunt *talented]
 - Death Gate - [ *mage teleport but not to the capital cities, to the cemetery mbe ]

Minions: - Raise Skeletal Warrior - Raise an Skeletal warrior from a corpse to fight for you.
 - Raise Skeletal Archer - Raises an Skeletal Archer to fight for you.
 - Raise Skeletal Mage - Raises an Skeletal Mage to fight for you.
 - Animate Dead - Resurrects a monster to fight for you [*talented]
 - Revive - Resurrects a fallen party member to fight for you for short period of time.
[*talented]
 - Army of the Dead - Raise a massive skeletal army to pummel the targeted location
 - Command Skeletons - Commands minions to attack the target

Melee: here are 2 builds with coatings/imbues or simple warrior style
 - Weapon imbues [ *shaman imbues ] / Poison coating [ *rogue poisons ] or *Rend
[ this would serve as basic DoT which would be used later for AoE or Single dmg
Spread Disease - Spreads existing infections from your target to all other enemies
Poison Explosion - All infected targets explode for shadow/nature dmg.
Dark Slash - Deals +weapon damage + consume diseases on that target for shad/nat dmg.
 * this does look complicated so mbe standard melee abilities should be used

Talents:
 - Animate Dead - Resurrects a monster to fight for you [*talented]
 - Revive - Resurrects a fallen party member to fight for you for short period of time.
[*talented]
 - Army of the Dead - Raise a massive skeletal army to pummel the targeted location
 - Summon Resist - Increases the Resists of your Summoned Minions
 - Skeleton Mastery - Increases number of your raised Skeletons and Revived
[ not talented u can have only 2 minions at any time ]
 - Extended Servitude - Increases the duration of Skeletal Mages and Revived
 - Spreading Malediction - Increases the Necromancer's damage done by 1%
 - Eternal Torment - Curses cost less mana/last longer.
 - Fueled by Death - [ *priest spirit tap / dk butchery ]
 - Final Service - Upon taking lethal damage, instead of dying, renders the Necromancer
immune to all damage for # seconds.
 - Stand Alone - Increases Armor by 200%, reduced by 10% for each active minion.
 - Unholy Aura - Increases the movement speed and life regeneration rate of nearby
friendly units.
 - Bone Shield - Reinforces shield with bones increases armor and absorbes dmg
 - Anti Magic - Bone Armor also increases magic resistances and reduces magic dmg recived

those are just few colorful talent names. but all in all just standard and few unique things. All standart talents from other classes for reduced mana costs, increase dmg magic/melee, decrease casting time, increase spell/mele hit chance, etc.. I would also add some talents for execute phase like warr Execute or warlock Decimation.
