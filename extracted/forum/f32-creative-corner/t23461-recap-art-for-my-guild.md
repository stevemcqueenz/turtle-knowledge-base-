Greetings!

I am still fairly new to Turtle WoW, having started just half a year ago with the launch of the new Ambershire realm. I am a Roleplayer at heart and love the game as much for its mechanics as I like it for the RP. Shortly after the creation of my account, I found the guild of my dreams: The Valiance Pact.

The pact is the perfect RP guild for me: I have found so many new friends there, the RP was marvelous and the time was just great. I really fell in love with that community and hereby want to really express my thanks for them to include me in that group.

I have created these artworks over my time there and now, just before new years eve, I posted this collection as a thank you for them. I also want to thank the Turtle WoW team: I know this was not the best year for you too. At some point, who knows, people or even the team will decide to move on. But thats just life. These artworks exist to remind you of the good times, what you made possible and what you achieved. Even when people move on, when you decide for a different hobby, a new life, different work and so on: These will serve you, so you can cherish the memories you made along the way.

Of course, its not the best artwork. I really wanted to push this out before new year, so I apologise for some bad coloring, perspective etc ^^'

Thank you! And happy new year!
