# Artisan Challenge – Profession Rank Quests for Progression & Immersion

## Post 129186 by Quetzy — 2025-04-30T20:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129186#p129186 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone!

I’d like to propose a small but meaningful addition to the Artisan Craftsman challenge mode that could help make the journey feel more dynamic, immersive, and rewarding — without giving any unfair advantage or disrupting game balance.

![🎯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3af.svg) The Core Idea
Introduce profession-based quests that unlock as a player reaches key skill milestones in their two chosen professions (e.g., 75, 150, 225, 300).

These quests would be:

Limited only to the two professions the character has committed to (locked early on to prevent respec abuse).

Optional, but rewarding for players engaging seriously with their chosen crafts.

Available only to Artisan-mode characters.

![✨](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2728.svg) Rewards
Each profession quest could grant:

A modest amount of experience, helping Artisan players stay on pace through their self-reliant gameplay.

A small bundle of profession-related materials (basic but helpful).

Possibly a cosmetic item, emote, or unique flavor text to reinforce immersion.

![🧠](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9e0.svg) Why This Makes Sense
The Artisan challenge is already a wonderfully immersive and enjoyable experience — it really reinforces the role of professions and self-sufficiency in a way that feels organic and rewarding.
However, there's currently no narrative or structural support along the journey. You simply appear in the world with your restrictions, and then… nothing happens until you hit level 60. That’s a huge gap in experience design.

This proposal is not about giving players “free gifts.” It’s about creating a sense of continuity — a way to live through the Artisan identity instead of it being a silent background condition. These profession quests would act as gentle, immersive reminders of the path the player chose, offering small but meaningful milestones that reinforce the spirit of the challenge.

![🧪](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f9ea.svg) How It Could Work
Quests appear via profession trainers or special NPCs once the character hits certain crafting thresholds (75/150/225/300).

They are tied to profession skill, not player level.

Each quest could include a bit of story or task related to the profession, ending with a small XP boost and some basic mats.

![✅](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2705.svg) Benefits
Adds structure and progression to the Artisan experience.

Keeps players engaged with their professions throughout the journey.

Helps smooth out XP gaps without feeling like power creep.

Deepens the narrative and immersion of a challenge meant to celebrate crafting.

Would love to hear the community’s thoughts — and of course, this is open to balancing and refinement. I think this could be a natural evolution of the Artisan experience that stays true to Turtle WoW’s design philosophy.
