 **The Desolation of Gilneas: Excerpts from the Diary of King Genn Greymane**

The Betrayal

I stand atop Greymane Tower, surveying the kingdom that should have been my legacy but has become my prison. My people, the very ones I bled for, turned their backs on me. How easily they were seduced by whispers of rebellion, by the false promises of change. Ingrates! I sacrificed everything for Gilneas—my strength, my wisdom, and even my son. Liam, my beloved son, who was meant to lead them in my stead, was torn from me by their insidious lies. They poisoned his mind against me. They took everything from me.

The Curse of the Worgen

The Curse of the Worgen—a fitting plague for a people who have become no better than beasts. Their betrayal manifested in a curse that twisted their bodies as much as their souls. The nobles, those treacherous vultures, saw this as an opportunity to further their own ends, manipulating the curse and turning it against me. Their machinations only drove me deeper into despair. My kingdom crumbled around me, and I, in my desperation, turned to the only power left—death magic. I have become what they fear, what they deserve.

The Rise of My Tyranny

Necromancy was not a choice but a necessity. I sought out forbidden tomes, dark rituals, and sacrificed my very soul to gain mastery over life and death. My loyalists, the few who still believe in my vision, became my enforcers. They patrol the streets, quelling any signs of rebellion. Gilneas, once vibrant, is now a nightmarish wasteland, prowled by Worgen and illuminated by the eerie glow of my necromantic energy. My experiments on the living and the dead continue. Through this dark power, I will reclaim my kingdom, punish those who betrayed me, and perhaps even bring my son back to me. They whisper of madness, but they do not understand. They cannot see the righteousness of my actions.

The Last Stand

After decades of suffering, the rabble broke through the Great Wall of Greymane. They found a kingdom ravaged by death and decay, a testament to their betrayal. United against me, they march towards Greymane Tower, determined to end what they perceive as my reign of terror. The air thickens with dread as they approach. The purple flashes of my dark magic grow more intense, casting twisted shadows. I stand at the top, surrounded by the bodies of those who opposed me, now my undead sentinels. I watch them with a mixture of rage and sorrow. They brought this upon themselves.

The Reckoning

The battle for Greymane Tower is a nightmare. The rebels fight through my loyalists and undead minions, their faces pale with fear and determination. They reach the summit, and there I stand, shrouded in dark magic, my eyes burning with unholy light. I unleash my death magic upon them, the air crackling with malevolent energy. In the chaos, I see a familiar figure—Liam. No it cannot be, he wouldn't do this to me. Perhaps a hallucination borne of my own desperation. Whether real or imagined, the sight of my son breaks through my madness. I hesitate, and in that moment, the rebels strike. Their blows are swift and final. My body crumples, my dark magic dissipating into the night.

A King's Regret

The Great Wall of Greymane stands as a grim reminder of my kingdom’s suffering and resilience. My people, the people of Gilneas, begin the long, arduous process of rebuilding, their spirits scarred but unbroken. I write these final words with a heavy heart, filled with regret and sorrow. I see now, too late, the madness that consumed me. I loved my people, even as I was blinded by rage and despair. I am sorry for the suffering I caused, for the horrors I unleashed. I only wanted to protect you, to keep you safe from the world's treacheries.

Forgive me, Gilneas. My pride, my arrogance, led us to ruin. In my heart, there remains a flicker of the love I once bore for you all. If only I could have been the king you needed, the king you deserved.

Another short story is from Lord Darius Ravenwood point of view through his diary.

 **The Reclamation of Gilneas: Excerpts from the Diary of Lord Darius Ravenwood**

A Dark Legacy

As I stand in the shadows of Greymane Tower, the scars of Gilneas' past weigh heavily upon me. Our kingdom, once a beacon of strength and independence, now lies in ruins. The walls that once protected us have become symbols of our suffering. My name is Lord Darius Ravenwood, and I have sworn to aid young Liam Greymane in reclaiming our homeland from the darkness that has consumed it.

The Harlow siblings, Celia and Mortimer, are the architects of our misery. They seized power after the tragic death of Queen Mia, manipulating the curse of the Worgen and driving King Genn into madness. Their reign of tyranny has brought Gilneas to the brink of destruction. But there is hope yet. Liam, the son of Genn Greymane, has risen to lead our people, and I have taken it upon myself to guide him.

Training a Leader

Liam Greymane is a young man of great potential. He possesses his mother’s compassion and his father's courage, but he lacks the training and experience needed to lead a rebellion. I see in him the hope of our kingdom, and it is my duty to mold him into a proper knight and leader.

Each day, I train him in the art of combat. I teach him how to wield a sword, how to read the battlefield, and how to inspire his men. He is a quick learner, his determination unwavering. He reminds me of a younger version of myself, before the weight of our kingdom's troubles settled upon my shoulders. Tess, his sister, is always close by, providing support and counsel. Together, they represent the future of Gilneas.

The Shadow of the Harlows

The Harlows are cunning and ruthless. Regent-Lady Celia Harlow, with her sharp intellect and political acumen, controls the nobles with an iron fist. Regent-Lord Mortimer Harlow, a master manipulator, spreads fear and distrust among the common folk. They are the true enemies of Gilneas, the puppeteers behind the destruction of our nation.

Liam sees them for what they are—powerful politicians who orchestrated the chaos that has plagued his lifetime. He blames them for his mother’s death, his father’s madness, and the suffering of his people. I share his hatred, for they have wronged us all. But I remind Liam that we must be strategic in our approach. Direct confrontation is not always the answer. We must outthink them, outmaneuver them.

The Battle of Ravenshire

Ravenshire was a pivotal moment in our struggle. Under Liam's leadership, we managed to reclaim the town from the clutches of the Harlow loyalists. It was a hard-fought battle, with losses on both sides, but it was a victory that filled our hearts with hope. Liam fought bravely, his newfound skills on full display. I watched with pride as he rallied our troops, his voice carrying the promise of a brighter future.

In the aftermath, we fortified our positions and prepared for the inevitable counterattack. The people of Ravenshire, once oppressed and broken, began to see a glimmer of hope. They looked to Liam as their leader, their savior. The Harlows will not take this loss lightly. They will come for us, but we will be ready.

The Final Confrontation

The time has come for our final confrontation with the Harlows. Gilneas City gate has been breached, and our forces march towards Greymane Tower. Liam stands at the forefront, his resolve stronger than ever. He knows that this battle will determine the fate of Gilneas.

The Harlows have fortified the tower, surrounding themselves with their most loyal soldiers and dark magic. But we have the support of the Alliance and the determination of our people. I stay beside Liam, ready to face whatever comes. He is not just a leader; he is the embodiment of our hopes and dreams.

A New Dawn

As I write these final words, I look upon Liam with a mixture of pride and hope. He has become the leader Gilneas needs, a beacon of light in our darkest hour. The battle ahead will be fierce, but I have faith in him. Together, along with aid of the alliance heroes we will reclaim our kingdom from the clutches of tyranny.

The Harlows' reign of terror will end, and a new dawn will rise over Gilneas. Our people will rebuild, stronger and united. I am Lord Darius Ravenwood, and I stand with the Prince Liam Greymane. For the kingdom of Gilneas, for our future, and for the memory of those we have lost. We fight not just for ourselves, but for the generations to come.
