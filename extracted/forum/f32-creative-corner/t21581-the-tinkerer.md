# The Tinkerer

## Post 149537 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-08-31T10:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149537#p149537 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I wanted to visit the idea of a **tinkerer** class.
This is not a suggestion, I'm neutral toward TWOW's addition of custom classes or a lack of it.

 **Why I find tinkerers awesome is because:**
- they are based on existing lore

- the two perfect race candidates are both underplayed

- they could provide a mechanical alternative to the druid's concept
 *So, what is a tinkerer?*
In one word: Gazlowe.

![Image](https://nexuscompendium.com/images/skins/gazlowe/base.jpg)

Tinkerer is a class which uses **mechs** in combat.
They can be **goblins** and **gnomes**, wear **leather** from start to finish (so druid items become relevant for them and we avoid the need of flooding bosses with new loots).
They can be **tank** (Ward), **melee DPS** (Brawler) and **ranged DPS** (Pilot).
They do not use any kind of magic.
Their resource is **energy**.
Mechs function as druid shapeshifts, each using energy as a resource and the mechs' effectiveness is based on the talents the player takes.

 **How would a tinkerer look like?**

In its base form, it is a leather-wearing goblin or gnome using mostly ranged attacks against foes.
Outside of a mech, they are vulnerable.
They would have some unique abilities only usable in this form though.
For example a longer cast-time ability, summoning a smaller robot to attack an enemy.
Imagine the goblin engineers at Deadmines.

Another set of skills is a rudimentary set for ranged weapons (preferably guns).
For example an on CD weapon shot which slows if hits or a 'precision shot' which adds some ranged attack power to the auto-attacks.
Another baseline ability would be setting up a mine which is akin to a hunter's immolation trap.
But all in all, their base toolkit is limited.

 **Gearing**

As stated earlier, they would be leather users.
At the relic/gun/wand slot they could insert a gun.
Weapon-wise, they could use one-handed maces, axes, swords and daggers and off-hand items.
They dual-wield but have no baseline talent for dual wield damage or accuracy.
They cannot wield two-handers and shields.

 **The trees**

 **Brawler**

This would be the earliest form they learn at around level 10.
Requires a quest-line of course.
Once learned, the tinkerer can build a combat frame used for melee.

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/LDVTVW6.jpeg)

It is basically cat form but has its own differences:
- a tinkerer can be polymorphed, the character's body was not shapeshifted

- unlike the agility-related scaling, the brawled scales off strength
The brawler form opens up some openers (combo point creators) and finishers (combo point consumers).
However, their scope is much more limited than a rogue's. A brawler cannot stun, doesn't use poisons or bleeds.

 *Its primary toolkit consists of:*
- armor shredding (cannot coexits with sunder armor or expose armor)

- short CD cleave for multitarget damage

- combo point spender increasing AP
The brawler has no crowd control abilities. No stun, fear, charm etc.
What it has in addition of the above is a bit uf utility:
- a combo point spender marking a target, receiving extra crit for melee attacks

- a tiny robot besides the brawler which can either give melee haste party bonus or crit amount party bonus
 **Ward**

This form could come at around level 15 and would be the tank form.

![Image](https://cdn-cf.ginx.tv/imgcdn/h8YEhaCNW_IK1zVEh8Gp9x7wYm5t9_uwKZbka0vXf2E/rs:fill:1350:760:1/g:ce/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ2lueC50di91cGxvYWRzMi9XYXJjcmFmdFJ1bWJsZS9MZWFkZXJzL1NuZWVkL1dhcmNyYWZ0UnVtYmxlU25lZWQuanBn)

 *Imagine a bear with the below differences:*
- can be polymorphed

- receives the same armor buff but instead of an HP buff the ward receives a passive regeneration as the small robots on its surface keep repairing the device

- a ward can parry but cannot block

- using energy and combo points for tanking, not rage

- a ward has some built-in poison, disease and bleed resistance since the operator has to be affected, not the device
Overall, the ward's HP would be relatively small but its armor would be extremely high.
The HP regeneration passive would feel like a decent but not too strong HoT effect.

 *Unlike the brawler, the ward has two crowd control effects:*
- it can stun on a decent CD (imagine hammer of justice)

- it can AoE fear on a big CD (just like the warrior shout)
 *Unique ward abilities:*
- a jump which is basically a charge; can be used in combat, costs energy

- a combo point spender increasing parry chance

- a repair emergency on a 5 min CD increasing HP regeneration but greatly decreasing melee haste

- armor shredding, same as the brawler

- a passive, greatly increasing hit threat which follows up a parry

- a swipe-like cleave

- a tiny robot which either emits some AoE threat (below stoneclaw level) or one increasing party parry chance
 **Pilot**

The range DPS class. Up until level 20, the DPS tinkerers are either brawlers or perform range DPS without any mech.
The range DPS version uses the shoot auto-attack by default and many of its abilities consume bullets just like the hunters.
Their damage is based on ranged attack power and has a designated utility for RAP, making them ideal next to MM hunters.

![Image](https://wow.zamimg.com/uploads/screenshots/normal/1214013-salvaged-goblin-gazillionaires-flying-machine.jpg)

 *The pilot form would add them the below benefits:*
- increased range

- increased haste when performing ranged skills

- some extra armor
 *A pilot would have unique abilities like:*
- slowing ray (not bullet-related); basically a mind flay

- zone marking (not bullet-related); an on CD, low damage AoE attack which in turn raises the damage of other ranged physical attacks on any target within the area

- their own steady shot as a combo point creator

- an ability increasing auto-attack RAP

- a ranged cleave as a combo point spender, the more points you spend the stronger it is

- an on CD jump allowing them to distance themselves once in a while

- an own tiny robot either increasing ranged attack damage or giving a cheetah-like party speed bonus
CC-wise, a pilot can slow and also has an on CD, ranged sleep (a special grenade which he yanks from the device).
