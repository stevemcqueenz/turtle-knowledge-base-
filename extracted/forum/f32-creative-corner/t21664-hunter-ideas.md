# Hunter ideas

## Post 150090 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-09-03T18:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150090#p150090 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!

I want to share a set of ideas which could enable additional hunter playstyles.
Since some of the ideas are radical (f.ex. hunter dungeon tanking) and most of them would require serious testing which I cannot do, this is mostly a creative endeavour, **not** a suggestion.

 **What I'd tweak concept-wise?**

I'd enable the below builds to exist and provide the necessary toolkit for that:
 * BM leveling/PVP (exists)
 * BM raiding utility DPS
 * MM with pet (exists)
 * Lone wolf MM
 * Survival DPS (exists)
 * Survival dungeon tank

 **To do this, would add some baseline changes:**
- Aspect of the Monkey gives a threat bonus.
 *It is not really used in PVE instances, can easily be the tanking stance.*
- Distracting shot taunts if aspect of the monkey is active
 *Would give a unique taunt for hunters. Because of dead zone etc, a hunter could taunt but only mobs which already run to casters; unable to taunt from melee range*
- Would give more pets a utility buff akin to the one the striders now have
 *Hunter pet utility could really count, and groups of pets would benefit different kinds of comps*

More exact changes, point by point.

 **BM raiding utility DPS**
- Baited shot pet buff becomes raid-wide
 *Several BM hunters could keep up the bonus more consistently for each other, the pets benefiting from the effect way more often*
- Strider-like utility buffs for pets AND the addition of a new BM talent, making the pet buffs more effective
 *Imagine for example the turtle giving a 5% armor party buff which becomes 10% if talented; a utility BM hunter could be a jolly joker fill to any raid party comp based on the pet they bring*

Ideas for pet buffs:
- Turtle giving party armor buff

- Wind serpent giving spell haste

- Gorilla giving a slight threat buff to party members

- Scorpion making venoms applied by party members more effective

- Bears giving HP buff to the party
And the list goes on.
The concept is giving a special party utility for pets (maybe pet families, so someone wanting to use a specific utility but hating a specific pet could have alternatives), and giving BM a talent which turns that buff potent.
A BM hunter should have a full stable.
Each pet is a beloved companion, but also a utility niche.
If raid lead wants the this kind of hunter in a tank group for progression, he could come with a turtle for example.
If the comp requires him to play alongside casters, he could bring the wind serpent.
A build designed to be a perfect filler to any raid party missing one guy.
Of course the pet has to be kept alive for the buff to apply.

 **Lone wolf MM**

Not a complex idea.
Giving you some RAP if pet is not summoned. For MM, pets are mostly an annoyance in instances anyway.
Imagine demonic sacrifice but flavorwise the hunter of course won't sacrifice his beloved pet.
Also, make volley scale from RAP. It already has a CD, it wouldn't cause a braindead playstyle.

If we wanna go fancy, we can buff trueshot aura a bit, too.
Increases party AP but unlike now, the increase is 50% more effective for RAP.
More unique, not a party-wide blessing of might clone.

If we wanna be really fancy, we could give arcane shot a 1 min CD but it could create an arcane rupture on the target, increasing the damage it receives from arcane magic for some seconds.
Would give another utility to the spec. MM hunters for example could maintain this debuff when the beatdown phase starts at a boss.
My goal here is to make arcane shot somewhat interesting.

 **Survival Dungeon Tank**

Not a raid tank.
Dungeon tank.

- Improved Primal Aspects now giving a melee damage reduction AND dodge for aspect of the monkey and health AND mana regeneration for wolf
 *I see too many leveling melee hunters going oom too fast. This could help them a bit while this change would give a base mitigation for the hunter.*
- Survivalist also giving you extra armor based on your agility
 *Let's face it, 10% HP for 5TP is kinda lame*
- Lacerate talent addition: a target affected by lacerate's bleeding has a reduced melee damage
 *Pseudo defensive*
- Intimidation is moved much higher in BM tree to be easily accessible
 *Love your offtank, hunter mon*

These changes plus the distracting shot taunt and the aspect of the monkey threat increase should do it.
A hunter dungeon tank would still be squishy, specially now that dungeons are harder.
But they still have CC trap, have a pet for offtanking and can kite really well.
