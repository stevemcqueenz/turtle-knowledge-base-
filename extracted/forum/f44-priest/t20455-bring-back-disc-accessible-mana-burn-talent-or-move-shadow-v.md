# Bring Back Disc-Accessible Mana Burn Talent (or Move Shadow Version Down)

## Post 141217 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-25T07:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141217#p141217 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Right now, Improved Mana Burn sits in the Shadow tree, and this has had a big impact on priest PvP builds. If you want to go Disc for support, use Chastise, and still have a viable Mana Burn, you end up sacrificing Holy Nova and other key Holy talents. That’s a big tradeoff and it’s one that basically killed Mana Burn as a practical spell since you’re stuck with the slow 3-second cast.

Suggestions:

 * Move Improved Mana Burn back into the Discipline tree, or
 * Keep it in the Shadow tree but shift it to the 2nd row so it’s more accessible to support builds.

This would make priests better at supporting their teams and give them a needed tool to counter overtuned casters without being forced into a pure Shadow build.

I get that Mana Burn is not fun to play against, but if that’s the concern, maybe we look at other mana drain mechanics like Hunter Viper Sting, which is instant and has no real drawback. If priests need to stand still for 3 seconds to drain mana, maybe hunters should too.

Bring it back to disc!
