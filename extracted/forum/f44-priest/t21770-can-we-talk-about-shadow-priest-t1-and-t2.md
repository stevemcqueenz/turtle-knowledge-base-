# Can we talk about Shadow Priest T1 and T2?

## Post 150785 by Keds (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-08T12:43:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150785#p150785 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello guys, I've been playing on the SA servers since launch, I'm full T1 AND missing one piece of T2.

I'm theorycrafting these two sets for some time now, and TBH, I'm not feeling good about either set bonuses.

T1 set looks amazing on paper, you get 15% threat reduction, 1 more sec of mindflay (MF), and chance to proc +45SP. But the problem here is this +1 sec of MF, to benefit from this and actually increase your DPS, you have to cancel your MF midcast to cast Mind Blast (MB) and put points into Improved Mind Blast, which will turn your rotation into a cluncky mess, and if you don't put points into Imp MB, you can use 2 MF + 1 MB, but it's a DPS LOSS since MB is the most damaging ability, and going this route, there isn't a good choice to put your talents into either, besides using 3 points to get 6 yards on your MF, also, threat wound't be too much of a problem since you would be casting MB every 8 seconds instead of the usual 6 seconds.

Now let's talk about T2, switching from T1 to T2 you get:
> +4 Stamina
>  +21 Intellect
>  +17 Spirit
>  +19 Spell Power
>  +2% Crit
>
>  -15% threat reduction
>  -1s of Mind Flray
>  -45 Spell Power from the proc
>
>  +30% damage to Mana Burn
>  +20s of Vampiric Embrace
>  +8% chance of increase your MB crit chance by 10%

As you can see, theres barely any benefit from getting T2, crit is also weak for us since our only crittable ability is Mind Blast and has a CD, and T1 leave you with a clunky rotation if you actually want a DPS increase.

My suggestion is to change both set bonuses:

> Tier 1:
>  (3) 15% threat reduction
>  (5) Increases the duration of your Shadow Word: Pain ability by 3 sec.
>  (8) 45 Spell Power from the proc
>
>  Tier 2:
>  (3) Each time you cast an offensive spell, there is a chance your next spell will cost 100 less mana.
>  (5) Increases the damage from your Mind Blast ability by 10%.
>  (8) Your Shadow Word: Pain has a 8% chance to increase the critical strike chance of your Mind Blast by 10%. This effect is expended when your Mind Blast critically strikes an enemy.

Ok guys, yesterday I did some testing, after many respecs later, I can confirm that:

- Full T1 with (Improv MB (0/5)) shadow priest does more damage than full T2 (Improv MB (4/5)) or at least about the same.

- If you change bracer/waist for Bracers of Arcane Accuracy and Sash of Whispered Secrets, and the rest with t2, you'll do somewhere between 1 to 20 more DPS than full T1. (Improv MB (4/5))

- Going Holy to get Spiritual Guidance in both sets, nets you between 17 to 25 MORE DPS than going DISC with ~250~ Spirit, but it's a lot more mana/threat intensive, not really sure if it's really worth it.
So in conclusion, just go for pieces that gives you more SP and forget about set bonuses because both, T1 and T2 sucks for shadow priests, your BIS item list, at least where we are at South Seas phase, it's about the same as Classic, besides the neck/waist I think.

This test was made to have exacly 6% hit chance in gear, on boss dummy, only self buff.
