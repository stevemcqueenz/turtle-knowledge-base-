Gear and talent changes didn't changing anything regarding how to gear a shadow priest, even relatively to Vanilla WoW.
You just get 5% hit, stack spell power and more or less ignore everything else.

I know of this spreadsheet [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/ ... id=0#gid=0](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1F1IVqSt1MEJ9yjZ9rn1aKGIWNkJiA_CNmn6fLUpakb4/edit?gid=0#gid=0)
It's quite old but nothing substantial has changed since then. Given the buff of mind flay scaling, crit is probably even less important than before (compared to spell power). Note that the spreadsheet does not attribute any importance to hit%, it's only comparing the rest. Hit is obviously very important, but reaching the cap should be easy on TWoW.
Don't take it as gospel either, totally ignoring intell and mp5 (e.g. flarecore set) can lead to mana problems on long fights. But it's a good start.
