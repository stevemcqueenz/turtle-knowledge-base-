# Priests should be able to wield 2H Maces

## Post 89294 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-03-03T17:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89294#p89294 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yes, I know it sounds a bit out there, but hear me out.
There are a lot of 2H maces that are geared towards paladins that could just as easily be worn by priests - holy damage, int, spirit, all the good stuff, but with a bit of DPS ontop.

Why would priests care about DPS on a melee weapon you say? Melee/wand weaving. Leveling priests utilize this mechanic quite a lot and it provides some really good mana-free DPS to go with your DoTs and shields. But these tactics are basically useless when it comes to the endgame - staves and one-handers just don't have the OOMPH to make it worthwhile, and priests don't really have anything to buff their melee other than Inner Fire.

With the class changes patch coming, I want to build upon this idea and propose that Discipline priests be given more of a Monk or Battle Priest focus - they already have buffs to wands and Inner Fire, so they're best suited for the job. There are a lot of things that could be done to make it work, all the way from paladin-esque melee holy spells to outright pseudo-tank talents.
I'm obviously dreaming big here, but what I want to say is that there is a lot of potential in the Discipline tree, to make it something more than just "that spec with the Power Infusion". I hope the class changes council can utilize some of these ideas.
