# Holy Priest Guide

## Post 152325 by Langorn — 2025-09-19T00:03:00Z

Hello everyone, Picodon here, Holy priest. I wanted to share a quick priest guide i made, mostly for new players, hope it will be useful.
---
 **DISCLAIMER** I am not a professional priest neither am i a long time player on turtle wow. Hovewer i’ve been playing private wow servers for a while.
While searching on the forum, I didn’t find a beginner oriented guide for priests that would have helped me on my journey. I hope this one will help new priests players.
I do not say that i have the absolute truth backed by complex calculations, experienced players might find my guide not accurate enough and i would be more than happy to share about it in the comments as long as people remain normal and constructive human beings.

1. **Introduction**

This guide will focus on the Healing (i.e. Holy Priest). I advise to go this way after you hit 60 and intend to raid. Before this, i recommand to level as shadow or discipline (my favourite) spec since it will make solo farming WAY easier and still allow you to heal any dungeon in the game.

The priest is a great tool in raiding environment for all the utility it brings. It is a very versatile healer who can apply Heal over Time, strong and slow direct heals, fast direct heals and some strong AoE healing (only for your group sadly) as well as an instant shield. While it’s not the best at anything the Holy priest is a great and reliable healer that fits any raid composition.

 ---
2. **Leveling**

As said, leveling as a priest is way easier as shadow or discipline. Leveling as holy is possible, however it will be slower and most likely less fun, i would not advise to go for it unless you are leveling in group.

Here is a quick example of a Discipline leveling build. It allows good mana regeneration, better healing efficiency than shadow and a cool gameplay revolving around different spells and big crits giving you instant smites.

 10 – 11 : Wand Specialization : As a leveling priest you will wand, A LOT, this talent is the strongest you can pick.
 12 – 16 : Spirit tap 5/5 : It seems boring to take this early because it doesn’t give you more damage. However this talent alone will grant you a huge amount of mana regen and will make the difference between drinking every 3 pulls and almost not have to drink at all which is a CONSIDERABLE gain of time.
 17 – 21 : Divinity 5/5 : 5% crit on every holy spell is great since discipline heavily scale on crits
 22 – 26 : Divine fury 5/5 : -0.5 casting time on basically every single spell you will use, mandatory
 27 – 29 : Piercing light 3/3 : 6% Hit so you don’t miss your spells so often
 30 – 31 : Improved power word 2/2 : fortitude : Not mandatory, but i like this talent since PWF if a great buff that you will always have.
 32 – 34 : Mental agility 3/5 : Less mana used on offensive spells, renew, shield. That’s a nice talent that opens the new tiers.
 35 : Inner Focus : Every 3 min you have a free spell with more critical chance. Use it for a greater heal if you’re in a bad situation or for holy fire when you open on a monster.
 36 – 37 : Improved inner fire 2/2 : More spell power, you’re happy
 38 – 39 : Meditation 2/3 : Give you some mana regen while fighting. This is not mandatory but still good and opens the door to the real deal.
 40 – 41 : Purifying flames 2/2 : You ALWAYS open a fight with holy fire which means this talent is basically a 12% damage increase, what else ?
 42 – 44 : Searing light 3/3 : While this talent is not very reliable in early game since you don’t have a lot of critical chance. This talent gives the disco priest one of its biggest strength, instant big smites in the head of your ennemies.
 45 : Enlighten : A random proc 15% damage increase, that’s good to take
 46 – 48 : Mental strength 3/3 : 3% Casting speed and 10% intellect, take this talent, it’s very good.
 49 : Meditation 3/3 : Once again, quite filler here but i like this talent for the extra mana regen when fighting
 50 – 54 : Force of will 5/5 : This talent is NUTS, 5% Damage, 5% Crit and 20% upgrade on your shield. This is absolutely enormous, you want to take this.
 55 : Chastise : The boost component of this spell is not really useful, however this is a nice instant spell that gives you a bit more control, very cool.
 56 : Holy Nova : Finally your first and only AoE damaging spell. You could very much choose to take this WAY earlier (After Divine fury). You don’t always face AoE situations but this spell can be very strong once in a while.
 57 – 58 : Holy Focus 2/2 : You can now cast spells more easily when face tanking mobs
 59 – 60 : Improved shadow word : pain 2/2 : More damage on one of your damaging spell

The final spec would look like this :

![Image](https://image.noelshack.com/fichiers/2025/38/4/1758225907-leveling.jpg)

This is only a version of what you can do, you’re free to experiment anything you want, that’s part of the fun. The final spec (31/13/7) is very reliable for solo content farming and i still use it after dinging 60.

The usual rotation of a discipline priest during leveling would be the following :
Holy Fire -> Smite -> Shadow Word Pain -> Wand till death of the monster

You can use Mind blast for the quick cast and extra burst but the mana cost is usually not worth it.

If you find yourself in some troubles, with a multiple pull for example, don't panic, the priest is a very strong solo class and has weapons to deal with this.
Fear your enemies and start focusing one to kill it as fast as possible, don't wand here, use your mana. When the mobs are in melee range don't hesitate to shield yourself. Heal yourself if you're getting low, rince and repeat.
With this strategy you can actually solo elites very easily as soon as you're getting some levels. Drop a lightwell, shield yourself before the pull and each time your shield CD is up. Use your mana to dps with spells and fear on CD to refresh shadow word pain and holy fire. Heal yourself with renew and the lightwell.

3. **Professions**

Turtle Wow made an incredible job making every profession interesting along the game. I cannot really advise you to go for a specific set of professions in order to min/max your character. It really depends on what you want to do. However here are some pointers that can help during and after the leveling :

Gathering professions (Skinning/Herbalism/Mining) are a GREAT way to make money reliably during the whole game. That's what i went for and it allowed me to make a confortable amount of money so i don't have to worry about buying my mount as lvl 40 or buying gear/enchants/consummables at lvl 60.
If you're aiming for this i would advise to sell absolutely everything you gather to vendors or the auction house and not to worry about keeping anything.

Crafting professions : While those won't help you make as much money in early game, it will help you quite a lot and might be very profitable when reaching high levels. As a priest, the most notable and useful for you would be :
 - Tailoring : You will be able to craft some gear during your leveling which is always enjoyable, if you reach 300/300 in tailoring you will also be able to craft <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=14154> which is a great chest that you will keep for a long time.
 - Alchemy : In combination with Herbalism. You can make potions that will ease your journey, this will be quite profitable in late game with the correct recipes since every raid brings some potions.
 - Jewelcrafting : In combination with Mining. Nice but very expensive profession, you will be able to craft rings, trinkets and necks which are usually quite hard to find in early game. It is also profitable in end game due to some nice epic crafts and rare enchants.
 - Enchanting : Quite hard to level, but combined with Tailoring it will be a nice way to make a lot of money when you unlock late game enchants.

4. **What race should i pick ?**

Honestly, i think the best answer to this question is : just pick the race you like the most because you will end up playing a lot on this character so you don't want to be disgusted by it.
There is still some min/maxing to do depending on your race so i list here a few arguments :

Horde :
As a proud horde player you have two choices for your priest. The undead will feel smoother during the leveling because of its great racial spell Devouring Plague. It is also superior in PvP thanks to Will of the Forsaken that will clean Sleeps/Charms every once in a while. The undead also has a little bit more of intelligence which is nice for your mana pool (but really, not significant). Your other choice is troll. While the troll suffers from really terrible priest racial spells, it has the strongest PVE racial spell in the game in my opinion : Berserking. This spell will make you cast faster for 10seconds which can be a life saver in critical situations.

Alliance :
If you're crazy enough to pick alliance after seeing how sexy Trolls and Undeads are, you are a strange person. But i'm no one to judge. In my opinion the best choice for alliance players in PvE are Humans because of their increased mana regeneration rate and their racial that gives a strong instant self heal.

 ---
Let's now tackle the important part of this guide, which is raiding and playing your Holy priest.

As a healer your goal is to help your raid survive. While this role can give some pressure it is incredibly rewarding and in my opinion the funniest role in WoW.
The only thing you have to hope for is for your group to kill the boss so you don't face the old phrase every single non-healer player might say
> That's always the healer's fault

.

My advise to achieve this goal as much as possible is, first, to always have in mind a "priority" target list which would be :

 - You : If you're dead, who will heal the group ? Not the other heals because they were so bad they let you die. So if you're dying, save yourself first.
 - The tank : If the tank dies, the fight is most likely a wipe. Your job is to not allow the tank to die so focus him and always have an eye on his health bar.
 - The other heals : As we just said, the other heals are so bad they might let you die, however that's not a reason to seek vengeance. Save your healer friends since they will help you hold the rest of the raid alive.
 - DPS : If your raid doesn't have anymore damage, your mana bar will run low and the whole group will die. While it's less of a problem if one or two dps die and you can't do anything about it, don't let them all die or they will complain for ages !

5. **Healing priest statistics**

Here are the different important stats you want on your Priest :

 - Healing Power : Your primary stat, every single of your spell will scale on that, the more you have the better. And the more you have the stronger will be your down ranked spells which gives a better mana gestion.
 - Spell Crit : This stat is strong on healing priest since most of our spells (except shield and renew) can crit for 150% the amount of heal. In addition, a crit with any direct healing spell will buff our target for 25% more armor, which is huge on a tank.
 - Intellect : Intellect will give you a bigger mana bar and more spell crit. You want as much as possible
 - Spirit : Priests use spirit very well as they get Healing Power and mana regen from it. You want to have a lot of it. Since you're gonna have a lot of spirit you will have to care about the 5 seconds rule. Basically when you stop casting for 5 seconds your mana regenaration kicks in. For this reason it is interesting to try to heal completely your team then wait for several seconds to regen a bit of mana before healing again (if it's possible of course).
 - Mp5 : Helps you regen mana when you're casting, you'll find it on a lot of healing items, valuable stat.
 - Stamina : Gives you HP, more HP = you're less likely to die, which is good. But that's not a primary stat you have to hunt for.

6. **Healing priest spells**

To be a successful healer, a priest has a large arsenal of healing and utility spells that you have to use and i will give an overview here.
Something very important as a healer is to be very careful with his mana, since a healer that is Out Of Mana (OOM) is basically useless. To achieve this goal you have several tools (Potions, Mana regen) but one very important thing is down-ranking spells.
Down-ranking means using spells of a rank that is lower that the highest you learnt this results as a less powerful spell but also considerably less mana expensive !
I won’t go too much into details because i'm no master, and very experienced priests might use differents ranks than me.

Here are the healing spells i use in order to heal in raids :

 - Heal (Max rank) : This is your bread and butter heal, it is very mana efficient, scale a lot of your healing power and has a slow 2.5 second cast. If you are not in an emergency situation, use this spell.
 - Greater Heal (Max rank) : This is your strong and expensive heal. If your facing high sustained damages (for example each boss hit takes 50% of your tank life), use this spell. However be careful, this is very mana expensive and you will get OOM very fast if you spam this spell.
 - Flash heal (Max Rank) : This is your "Oh shit" heal. This is a fast casting, mana expensive spell that you want to use when you need to quickly heal several of your teammates, or if you see someone with critical health and you don't think you have 2.5s to cast another spell in order to save them
 - Renew (Max Rank) : Renew is a powerful and mana efficient Heal over Time (HoT). I usually like to have it 100% of the time on my tanks so it smoothes damage to make the healing easier.
 - Renew (Rank 5) : Renew scale very well on your healing power, so even if rank 5 renew is less strong than max rank, it remains a very good heal over time for almost no mana. You usually don't want to use this on your tanks but it's cool to cast it on other group members that took some damage if you don't want to bother casting another spell on them. It's also a nice way to heal people when you have to move.
 - Prayer of Healing (Max Rank) : This spell is extremely costly and long to cast and sadly you cannot cast it on someone that is not in your party. I would not recommand to use this spell without Inner focus. It is a great spell if your entire group just took a large chunk of damage and you want to heal everyone before saving the rest of the raid.
 - Prayer of healing (Rank 1) : Same idea but way less costly and powerful, if you don't have much to do and want to heal your entire party for a small chunk of damage, go for it.
 - Power word : Shield (Max rank) : This spell can sometimes be tricky to use, indeed, Warrior and Druid tanks might hate you for this because the damage tanked by the shield won't let them generate rage, so use it carefully. However this spell can be extremely powerful in different situations. It can help you mitigate incoming damage on a target if you're ready for it. It's also your BEST reaction when someone is on the verge of dying. If you see someone in your raid taking a lot of damage and reaching critical health, don't bother trying to hard cast a healing spell. Start with a shield to prevent him from dying and then you can heal safely. Having an instant spell to react to critical situations is one of the priests strengths.
 - Holy Nova (Max rank) : This one is not mandatory to take. It can be useful in some situations, giving a small burst of instant heal (with pre raid BIS gear you'll crit around 800). However this is mana expensive and very situational since it has a small range.
Then you have "utility spells"  :
 - Dispell Magic (Rank 2) : Strong instant spell that debuff 2 Magic effects, either negative on allies or positive on enemies. This spell can be extremely important depending on what you have to debuff, i recommand using the Add-On Decursive for easy use.
 - Abolish Disease : Instant spell, puts a buff on an ally that debuffs a disease effect every 5s for 20s, very useful and cheap. Use it when enemies cast annoying diseases.
 - Fade (Max Rank) : Instant spell, reduces your threat towards every enemy, very useful in fights when you pull aggro because of your healing. Please do not die stupidly when pulling agro from tanks by not using this spell.
 - Mana Burn (Max Rank) : Burns the mana of an enemy to deal some damages, very niche use but can be useful against bosses that you need to mana burn.
 - Psychic Screan (Max Rank) : Instant AoE fear, you usually DONT want to use this in raid.
 - Shackle Undead (Max Rank) : Nice control spell against undeads, can be useful to ease hard pulls or controls adds.
 - Inner Focus : 3 minute cooldown, your next spell doesn't cost mana and has +25% crit chance. Use it as a panic button to hope getting a big crit heal or when your mana is running very low (casting a 0 mana spell doesn't count during the 5s rule for mana regen). I prefer using it with greater heal or prayer of healing depending on the situation
And finally your buffs :
 - Power Word : Fortitude (Max Rank) : Stamina Buff, has to be up 100% of the time on the whole raid
 - Divine Spirit (Max Rank) : Your Spirit buff, has to be up 100% of the time on the whole raid (not really if they are melee but trolls do have some use for spirit so just do it).
 - Shadow Protection (Max Rank) : Shadow resistance buff, has to be up on the whole raid as long as the mobs have shadow spells/damage.
 - Proclaim Champion : This spell is the last talent from the Holy tree and is incredible. It makes your tank tankier, reducing its damages taken, increases its magic resistance and also gives you mana depending on the tank damages ! In addition it gives you cool spells that allows you to give your champion either 5% damage or +10% healing recieved as well as a battle res every hour. Take this spell and ALWAYS have it on your tank. If your raid has multiple priests or the tank doesn't need the tanking buff and you struggle with mana you can think of putting this on your highest DPS. But i think there is no case where this is actually beneficial to the raid.

7. **Talents**
Holy priests have different talent trees possible depending on your playstyle, your gear, what raid you're doing. However you can find 2 different types of build with some variants that i'm gonna explore a bit.

The first variant we will call the "Deep Holy Build". The goal here is to get at least 31 points in the Holy tree in order to get extremely strong healing talents as well as proclaim champion.

The base of this build would be (14/31/0) with several points free to place where you want, depending on your playstyle and your needs as you can see below :

With 6 free points that you can put in any talents highlighted in yellow. Some examples of builds (but not all of them) can be deeper in discipline (17/34/0):

![Image](https://image.noelshack.com/fichiers/2025/38/5/1758236942-classic-holy.png)

Where you're getting the 15% shield buff in discipline for the extra chance to save someone in danger, as well as holy Nova for some instant AoE healing and Holy Reach for 6 increased yards on your holy spells which gives you a bit more flexibility in your positionning. ![Image](https://image.noelshack.com/fichiers/2025/38/5/1758236946-exemple-deep-holy.png)

But those builds are not the only ones, other talents, like spell warding can be strong in particular fights. It's your job to find the best one ! Usually i go for the (17/34/0) Build.

The second variant we will call the "Enlighten Build" (21/30/0). This build focuses on grabbing the enlighten talent deeper in the discipline tree by sacrificing Proclaim champion and some other holy talents. Enlighten is a strong spell than can buff your healing and a caster damage by 10% for 12s on a random proc when you cast a spell. It could be interesting to run this spec when you have too many deep holy priests in your raid or when your raid is overgeared and you wanna go for a speedrun. Hi ! I would say this depends on two things :

 - First, and i didn't do the calculations, it would depend on the actual mp5 gain with those 2 items of the lost. If we consider you're swapping Rosewine circle (+29HP, +5mp5) and Hollowbone choker (10Int, 9Spirit, 26HP and 3Mp5) then your two items need to give you at least +8mp5 to be even comparable to this setup.
 - Second it also depends on your playstyle, you're deciding to lose a bit of raw healing power (+45HP) for mana regen. I would say this solution is definitely viable, but i think it's more interesting in big raids (lots of healers, you usually spam heals and have quite a bit of overheal) than in smaller raids where you have only 2 or 3 healers and your healing output is very important.

So yeah i think this solution is very viable considering the priest makes great use of spirit, but maybe not as "optimal" as the other configuration. However being optimal in classic is not really needed, especially in ealier steps of raiding, so if you like your setup go for it !
