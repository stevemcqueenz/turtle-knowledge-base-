# Elune's Grace Redesign Suggestion

## Post 51581 by Galliformophile — 2023-06-28T03:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51581#p51581 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I really like what the TWoW devs did with the Nelf racial Elune's Grace. It is way more useful than the original (though not at all overpowered) and keeps to the original spirit of the night elf priest avoiding attacks through their inherent physical grace. That being said, low levels of the spell are very underwhelming for a 5m CD, and even though I very much like the agility - mana restore combination, by the time you're able to get enough dodge where you really will be regenerating mana the amount you'll get (unless you've somehow accumulated a LOT of agility) is trivial, but that's fine.

Still, I feel the spell could be better.

My proposal is that Elune's Grace rather should work to help the priest reduce aggro range - think of it like an effect that lasts 15s where all hostile mobs are effectively impacted by Mind Soothe. Alternatively, you can think of it as a minor invisibility or stealth, which if it had this effect could see use in PvP, too. Obviously this should be a very weak stealth so that hostile players can see the priest under the spell unless they're quite far away (so it would have some PvP use), but really this would be a PvE use for the priest to gracefully glide through hostile NPC's. I don't think Mind Soothe is at all OP and this is a unique mechanic that would really add to the flavor of Nelf priests and totally revamp the pre-existing spell which, while situationally useful, I still find underwhelming and would absolutely prefer the idea I've proposed above.

Thoughts? Is this OP? I originally had the idea when I was wishing that Mind Soothe could be used on non-humanoids. The stealth component came later when envisioning how it could also have PvP use (again, it would be a *very* weak "stealth" that's only effective really far away, so that for example if you see a hostile player in the distance and you want to avoid them, this spell could give you a few seconds to perhaps escape before finding a place to mount up safely).
