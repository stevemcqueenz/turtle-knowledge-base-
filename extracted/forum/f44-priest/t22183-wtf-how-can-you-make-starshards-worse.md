Just started a second NE Priest HC character to do the Traveling Craftsman challenge and at level 10 i see the biggest disappointment so far. Starshards on a cool down.

Starshard was the one way to make a Priest mana efficient while leveling without using the wand too much.
Our Inner Focus -> Starshards mini evocation, gone.
 5s rule shenanigqans to get 2 to 3 mana ticks while constantly casting with improved Holy Fire, gone.

I really jinxed it. I hoped for a change, but i did not expect them to make it worse ...

## Post 153895 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-10-01T09:21:00Z

Starshards is INCREDIBLE when leveling since you nuke down a mob every half minute. At max level it does 2000 dmg and it scales incredibly well. Since it's Arcane school, it's great to use when Focused Casting occurs / when shielded since if it gets kicked, it allows you to cast shadow / holy spells. And if it's not interrupted, you destroy the enemy due to the sheer amount of damage.

In addition, it's a channeled spell, which is INSANE in PVP when targets want to LOS you.

Starshards is not a bad spell, in fact it's much better.
