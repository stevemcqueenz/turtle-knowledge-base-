# My Two Cents about Disco Priest (and more)

## Post 157662 by Tscosomaz — 2025-10-29T10:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157662#p157662 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hi gamers and devs, let's start saying you guys did a MAGNIFICENT job with class changes! For someone like me who still plays this version of the game since 20+ years, after one-year-long break from TWOW (missed 1.17.2, back with 1.18), what more than a brand new original spec, always considered a meme?

At Ambershire launch, motivated by theorycrafting goat, i said to myself "let's start with this new adventure, let's try Disco Priest and aim to the Seeker title!".

You guys, both devs and community, did a MAJESTIC job, having personally tested my three 60s on Nordanaar (Rogue, Pala and Mage), tested Disco Priest on Ambershire since launch, after two months of gameplay and first raids on a fresh server, i can say imo in pve all classes are, after all, quite balanced.

Very Very GZ, guys! In the past 20+ Years, "others" never were even close to what you have done!

As the title of the thread says, i'd like to express my personal thoughts about Discipline Priest, after having leveled as Disco, run 5men as Disco dps/heal and now running first raids as Disco dps.

Leveling was SOLID, being able to solo lot of quests many other classes could not.

In 5men, as dps, numbers are close to top magic-dps, spec has its own identity, rotation is not too complicate, but not too dumb.

I am playing Disco Priest as a support dps, buffing allies with Enlighten, Chastise and Grace of the Sunwell racial on cd, dispelling nasty debuffs and supporting with PW:S or Inner Focus big heals in critical situations.

As I always say: "if I wanted pure caster dps output, i would have rolled a Mage or a Lock, not a support-dps hybrid".

The spec has even an AOE-cleave option with Hoy Nova, but with actual mana pool, even with High Elf racial up, it's nearly a manatrap in cost/damage ratio (due to the "splitting" of the sp contribution between damage and heal components), more useful in solo-farming than spell-cleave, but maybe with better or dedicated gear the situation would improve, cant say. Anyway, Priest was not supposed to be an AOE-cleaver and just the option to support on spell-cleaving is something to thank for.

In low tier raids, for now, damage numbers still stay not too far from top magic h-dps classes...

On the other hand, with higher tier raids, if the support this spec would add to the raid is less than the pure numbers given by an h-dps dedicated class (read this like: why i should get in a Raid a Disco dps Priest if another Mage/Lock give to the raid more benefits?), in my vision, if this occurs, it would mean the existence of some kind of "unbalance".

Sadly, i truly fear that for Disco dps the awful item-scaling from sets, with the lack o any useful item in t1-t2 bracket, will led to "unbalance", unless niche fights. For sure, Priests from Nordanaar can be more accurate about.

Talents:
IMHO, the only Discipline talent that should need a little rework is Resurgent Shield <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=45560>

 *When your Power Word: Shield is broken, 25% of its base mana cost is refunded and increases damage and healing done by Holy spells and effects by up to 10% of the shield's total absorption amount for 8 sec.*

With actual settings:
 - it is a "pvp talent", for a spec that in pvp would be "just-ok" if properly mastered;
+ in pve, it might be useful in some niche ranged tanking fights, but nothing more;
 - in pve, the buff nearly never procs in leveling, very rarely procs in 5men and always with awful timing (due to its design). In some raid fights with aoe damage suffered, the procs might be a biiiit more good-timed (just to stay optimistic and polite :P).

I've read someone saying on other posts that this is a talent designed for pvp (and this is true), but why not reworking a bit this great idea that replaced Reflective Shield, keeping all its features and just adding a more controlled usage and (above all) a support effect on allies, in line with the identity of the spec?

My suggestion:

 *When you cast Power Word: Shield on yourself, increases damage and healing done by your Holy spells and effects by up to 10% of the shield's total absorption amount for 8 sec. When your Power Word: Shield expires or is broken, the shielded target is energized with 3% total mana, 20 rage or 30 energy.*

+ the personal buff stays the same, what would change is WHEN you gain it;
+ added an average support effect with great versatility, giving something "back" also when the Shield is not broken by damage (atm a total mana waste);
+ pvp impact of the talent would not be affected, in addition it would be more versatile and useful also in leveling, dungeon and raid;
+ PW:S would become a third buff to watch, like Inner Fire and Purifying Flames, adding more "spice" to pve rotation;
+ due to Weakened Soul debuff, the energizing boost would occour just once every 15secs per target, making it all but overpowered.
+ adding the "When your PW:S expires" part is mandatory imo. The point is: what is the reason to spend 4 talent picks for something that has a chance to not even occour?

Improved Renew, Improved Power Word: Shield, Force of Will
Checking other posts on the forum, many people dont like these talents only affecting base amounts. IMHO they would be too overpowered otherwise:

 - Improved Renew is a Row1 talent, designed for leveling, when your gear gives nearly no +heal. In raiding, huge contribution from +heal is what makes Renew mana-efficient. Letting Improved Renew scaling with gear would make it too powerful in endgame. There is another way to make this talent attractive...
 - PW:S would be a too overpowered istant cast if talents would scale with equip, unbalancing expecially pvp.
 - Improved Renew is considered a "trap" by many, but "forcing" the picks, improving a bit Swift Recovery (read below) might change the consideration about it and maybe make more build options available.

A "New" Vanilla Archetype: Disco Healer?
A lot of ppl miss Disco Priest... I think with couple marginal changes to some talents, a new Raid archetype would raise, very close to the wotlk Disco: an healer focused mainly on Raid, using PW:S, Renew and Flash Heal. I think about some raid fights with massive aoe damage and how they could be a lot easier with even only one Disco shielding raiders. Later in this post, during my thougts about itemization, the hybrid nature of the spec will show how this archetype can play as healer or as dps even in the same fight (more dps-oriented or more heal-oriented, depending on talents setup).

What would let this archetype work?
+ mainly, the scaling to PW:S from gear in TWOW: the contribution is huge, even with talents only affecting base amount, considering that originally it was 0.

What does not let atm this archetype work fine?
 - The 4 secs cd on PW:S. This would be solved reducing the PW:S cd to 3secs via Improved Power Word: Shield third pick (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=14769>). Why to 3 secs? This is the mandatory line: you can cast a PW:S, followed by a Renew or Flash Heal, then a new PW:S on another target. With a 4secs cd you have 2 gcds before you can cast another PW:S. Reworking the bonus-set from some Priest sets (they all are terrible for Disco), the cd could be even lowered or maybe removed (i would not like that last option, tho, imo a PW:S spam is not suited for vanilla).
 - No "benefit" from the Weakened Soul debuff. Weakened Soul is what prevents the PW:S spam on the same target; imo its ok as it is, no need to lower the duration, 15secs is enough, even more duration could be a benefit IF the debuff would give some other side-effect via items or talents. In example, i would recommend to add to Swift Recovery (<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=51485>) in Holy tree something like "and by 5/10% if the target is affected by Weakened Soul". This little change by itself would create couple new talent build options, "forcing" the pick of Improved Renew and making it attractive.
 - Absence of Penance and Circle of Healing. No need imo, vanilla is not suited for Penance and Circle of Healing: Penance would unbalance pvp and Circle of Healing would unbalance raids. Moreover, i like a lot Chastise as it is, not a game-changing capstone, even if I would have added a direct support effect (mana/rage/energy gain on target) in place of the huge haste buff. Again, reworking the Priest bonus sets is the way to give solidity to this archetype and whole class.
 - Shielded Warrior/Bears not gaining rage. As i said at the start of this post, i missed last year of twow... Couple game friends are sure that after 1.17.2 devs changed the vanilla mechanic and atm shielded ragers can keep to gain rage. I have checked all the changelogs on forum and no info confirmed any change to the original mechanic. For me and for many on forum it works like it originally worked in 1.12 (no rage gain for shielded targets). Anyway, not an issue, expecially if you add a rage gain when PW:S expires or breaks on targets (via Resurgent Shield), as I suggested above; just dont shield ragers on pull unless they ask for it and keep PW:S on ragers only as a panic support button.

Itemization
This is the sore point. Liking versatility above all, I really hate dedicated +source items (+heal above all), but I understand this is an ancient setup too hard to let it go away. In the last years, however, a lot of hybrid +sp+heal items have been added or reworked, like priest t0.5/1/2, meaning there is an open-minded approach about the topic. This is the right approach imo, but it appears to have been stopped half-way, making sometimes the distribution of numbers and affects on many items totally "weird". This is due just to an "uncorrect" prospective in itemization affects-weightning during new items creation/reworking.

Let's make an example:
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=22267> considered the pre-raid bis (couple super expensive options from Jewelcrafting). What next as Disco?
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=22080> t0.5 (without considering the "struggle" to complete the quest chain): less sp, +crit (weight7) in place of +spellhit (weight19 if undercap, spec needs 10% from items. This big "unbalance" between spellcrit and spellhit, compared to other classes, is due to the lack of any source of +critdamage in talents. This leads to a loss of impact of Searing Light, the core mechanic of the spec itself).
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=16813> t1: the whole set is just +heal addition to T0.5, but lacks of 3% spellhit of T0,5 and any Shadow set, invalidating the whole set for Disco. In general, more int (just mana, crit/int conversion for Priest is an awful 1:64) and more Spirit (the +sp from talents is amazing, but too low ratio if compared with other classes pure +sp...) dont anything if there is no +sp increase... Moreover, SPI to mp5 Priest great conversion rate cannot be direcly translated into damage output). For Disco, +heal only affects healing side of the spec, unless the nearly null contribution via Resurgent Shield actual design.
<https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=16921> t2: like t1, a mere addition of some stats and a biiiit more +sp+heal, still no +spellhit, invalidating also this tier for Disco.
If you check TWOW db, the first item to improve Drakki helm is Mish'undare from Nefarian (last tier2 fight)...
In higher tiers, there is the unbalanced t2.5 set (for that one, a whole separated consideration must be done), only offset items in endgame content.

What showed for the Head slot happens also for mainly all the other slots: after T0,5 there is nothing, unless offsets or T2.5.

Mages and Locks can rely on their t1 and t2 sets, Disco Priest cant.

How Disco designed items might be?
+ as i said before, hybrid +sp+heal is the right approach for Disco, not +Holy.
+ Priest items must have Spirit stat, it is class core identity (unless Shadow).
+ just adding +heal to +sp affects, as done for T1 and T2, always led to a loss of power compared to other dedicated classes; the latter could be ok if "partially" compensated by the Spirit contribution to +sp and mp5 (less damage, more mana in longer fights), but we know that raid fights long enough to make this benefit something real and not only something on paper maybe 2-3.

Checking TWOW db, for some item slots, there are offsets not specificly designed for Disco that can be recycled, but many of them are not available yet on a fresh server, making the archetype struggling proper item scaling in almost every slot.

Above all, ignoring a-priori all the class sets for bad design is simply sad :(

My suggestion about Priest tiers:
+ a rework of Priest Tier1 and Tier2 sets, not creating a third +Holy set, the hybrid approach is the right one...
 - some bonus-set should be removed or reworked;
 - the core issue: Priest Tier1 and Tier2 do not give a single %spellhit bonus: t0.5 gives +3%, any Shadow set gives +3%, so any Disco Priest set MUST give at least 3%... The spec needs 10% from gear, Shadow only needs 6%.
+ take what follows into consideration: +spellhit has no effect on healing and +heal has no effect on dps. Given the hybrid nature of the spec, reworked items with both
+spellhit and +heal would not be overweighted in any way, just versatile.

The solution is so simple: just add +3% spellhit to t1 and t2, in item slots where there is no +crit already. And rework set bonuses...

Set bonuses thoughts
T0.5: Priest, Mage and Lock have a senseless 4set bonus pvp-oriented. After 20+ years, i still really struggle to understand the reason of a pvp-oriented bonus in a dungeon set. I strongly suggest to rework 4item-set bonus for all three previous classes, changing to something like the Paladin/Shaman chance on cast +sp proc or Druid chance on cast mana regain proc. You guys have done another AMAZING job reworking t0,5, anyway!
T1: only 5item-set bonus has a minimal impact on Disco and it's originally intended for heals.
T2: no impact on Disco.
ZG: +heal set. Imo it needs to be converted to hybrid +sp+heal. Bonus set are both acceptable and could be recycled on hybrid sets.
Aq20: +heal set. Imo it needs to be completely reworked, converted to hybrid +sp+heal (just to stay in line with the tier attitude). Bonus sets are senseless (one for Shadow and one pvp-oriented). Need to be completely changed.
Aq40: +Holy dps dedicated set. Not a fan. Lets be honest, this set is unbalanced and appears to be a compensation given in an already progressed server to long time players... 2item-set bonus is fine, 5item-set bonus is maybe the most senseless proc i have ever seen, really curious to question who originally had the idea, just to know what was in mind XD A better 5item-set bonus would be something like "Smite has a chance to increase the remaining duration of Chastise Haste buff on yourself" or "Smite has a chance to refresh the duration of Chastise Haste buff on yourself to its original duration". I suggest to rework to hybrid +heal+sp, +Holy is not the right approach for this archetype, lets +Holy items to stay on offset items…
T3: +heal set. It's ok, considering the previous set was +Holy dedicated. Lot of offset items in this tier help in counterbalancing the absence of an hybrid set, but having one also in this tier would not be bad.
Kara40: considering this is the endgame, I would create 3 different sets for this tier and further (+sp+heal, +heal, +shadow) and not couple +sp items in +Shadow set...

Finally, i can suggest couple bonus set ideas for Disco:
 - reduced threat from Holy and Discipline offensive spells.
 - chance (moderate) on PW:S cast to reduce damage taken by the target.
 - increased chance to crit with priest heals on target affected by PW:S or Weakened Soul.
 - increased PW:S absorb amount at the cost of increased Weakened Soul duration.
 - chance (low) on PW:S cast to make your next Flash Heal an istant cast.
 - increase the duration of Purifying Flame buff and Holy Fire dot.
 - reduce the mana cost of Holy and Discipline offensive spells when Inner Fire is active.
 - increase the critical stike multiplier of Smite when Inner Fire is active.
 - Inner Fire charges and duration increased.
