# Unwise complication in shadow/holy-specified DPS sets in 1.18.1 update

## Post 168767 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-02-18T14:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168767#p168767 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

First of all, I pressume that 1 univeral "spell damage and healing effect" should be evaluated as more than 1 "shadow spell damage", 1 "holy spell damage" or 1 "healing effect".

In the incoming 1.18.1 update, some universal spell effect from priest sets is transform directly into shadow damage without coefficient conversion. For example, the universal "44 spell power" on Coronet of Faith is converted into "44 shadow damage", meaning that the gear and corresponding set are no longer as efficient as before for healers while its shadow damage remains (if the damage conversion rate is the same). I take that a nerf to priest sets.

If the damage converting system is so simple, new parts with only holy damage may be unnecessary, because the attributes holy or shadow priests require that need to be converted are resistance reduction, critical rate, hit rate and casting speed. At least they should make some difference in amount of universal and single school damages, or new gears would only eat up inventory space.
