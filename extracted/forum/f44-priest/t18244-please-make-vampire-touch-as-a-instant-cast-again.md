Painspike can be coppled with mindblast for relatively big burst. Shadowpriest never was a burst class before painspike, but unfortunately thanks to the huge + shadow amplification shadows had and access to inner focus, they could have a lesser version of the feared elemental shaman nuke combo if they were afforded a brief timewindow to cast. Which wasnt all that rare considering power word shield, psychic scream and blackout could open up windows for it.

So we had a class that is designed to win by outlasting and that has 2-3k burstdmg (MB +Spike) at the ready (depending if you crit or not).
I have the feeling painspike was designed to help shadowpriest in pve fish for killing blows in order to use spirit tap, but it seems to be largely inconsequential in that role. Mana isnt an issue anymore anyway.

Further daming the spell is the fact that while it sounds like it doesnt generate progress unless you kill the target before the 5 seconds of healing window expires, this is wrong. The healing is equal to the base damage the spell deals. Thanks to crits, shadowform and other shadow amplifications you will always deal more damage than the target heals. (Or at least that used to be the case, havent played priest in half a year)

Considering painspike was added shadowpriest didnt need to outlast 100% of an enemies hp, but rather outlast ~80% and then finish you off with an instant. Thats akin to a 20% dmg amplification for pvp purposes.

I would have prefered seeing PS go than doctoring with VE, but under the constraints that PS stays, i think VE nerf was not the dumbest move for pvp balance. What however was is not applying the same caution that was made with Shadowpriest & Ememental for PvP in 1.17.2 to literally any Spec. Creating extremely bursty encounters all over the place
