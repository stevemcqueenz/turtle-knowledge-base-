# Disc priests are insane in pvp and are stronger than shadow

## Post 128453 by Forumdweller (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-04-25T12:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128453#p128453 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Every priest is playing disc spec in pvp over shadow because the damage and survivability and team utility is unparalleled. You can literally spam rank 1 flash of light to get the proc before even engaging in the fight and then
cast holy fire into insta cast smite into another insta cast smite if you crit which is enough to kill anyone.

It's absurd when you compare it to nightfall which has a 4% chance to proc every 3 second tick on a dispellable dot..

I recommend changing the searing light talent from proccing on holy/disc crits to instead functioning more like warlocks nightfall. This will keep the theme you guys are very clearly aiming for with priest being an offensive healer without making them a literal immortal dps class in pvp. And adding some form of counter in the form of dispelling the priests renews.

So in raids or BG's you just renew everyone and then every time renew ticks you have a small chance to get a searing light insta proc. More heals = more procs.
