This is written harshly, but also truthfully. CC2 has dumbed down healing a lot. Due to the general dps increase fights are now so short that mana economy (previously the core gameplay mechanic of healers) barely matters anymore.
You cannot balance healers like damage dealers. More damage onto the boss is always better. But damage-taken is a limited quantity. So the goal of healer gameplay is not to maximize your personal output, but to find and coordinate synergies with other healers to make best use of their respective strengths and weaknesses.
And that means traditionally healers would prioritize efficiency over through-put. That's the whole idea behind downranking and why every healer learns to intuitively do it.

Spamming Flash Heal is indeed something that should be looked down upon because it disregards mana efficiency in favor of through-put. It ignores the vast majority of your class' toolkit. It maximizes personal healing on the meters but usually that means you just push other healers into doing more overhealing, so the raid doesn't actually benefit. You spend more resources to do the same.
So Flash Heal is very deliberately expensive. You pay more for speed in case of emergencies. If there is no immediate emergency, you should use a slower more efficient heal. And that not only rewards you with a better mana economy, lower consumable usage, and better cooperation with other healers, it also rewards skillful predictive healing. Anyone can mash a one-button healbot and watch it snipe people to full, but at that point why even show up to play?

The same issue happened with Druids. Developers saw that Regrowth was rarely used due to its high mana cost. So they lowered the mana cost by about 30% across the board. But Regrowth has always been an awesome and very strong heal spell with a short cast time that was expensive as a trade-off. Now they removed that trade-off and suddenly a whole new generation of Druids plays the class like Paladin - just spam a 1.5s heal spell to top off people. That change killed Healing Touch as a major style of druid healing. Why be slow and mana efficient when you can be fast and not run out of mana either? The first rewards skill, the second is brain dead and tops meters. Easy choice what people are doing now. But over-all a very bad development for the class.

tl;dr: Every healing class has its strengths and weaknesses, and a diverse toolkit available. The fun and skillful way of healing is using all that potential to its fullest. And that usually means very fast spells should be prohibitively expensive as a trade-off. Don't treat healing like damage dealing and make everyone maximize output in the dumbest most brain-dead and most easily-automated way.
