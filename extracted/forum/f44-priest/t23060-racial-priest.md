For PvP, either dwarf or troll

Once troll gets t2.5/t3+ with 800+ shadow dmg, that shadowguard starts hitting..ALOT. And it has no cooldown so you can refresh it again, plus it can also stun ranged attackers unlike undead touch of the grave, so if a warlock decides to dot you or mage wants to pyroblast you, shadowguard can sometimes stun them throwing them off their rotation

Dwarf gets ward, plus desp prayer(basically minor lay on hands), and avatar which can be pretty good in team pvp buffing other players and helps in pve as well.
