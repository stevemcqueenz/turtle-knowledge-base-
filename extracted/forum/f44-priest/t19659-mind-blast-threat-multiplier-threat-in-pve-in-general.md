# Mind Blast Threat Multiplier - Threat in PvE in general

## Post 133782 by Zitronenhaze — 2025-05-31T15:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133782#p133782 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello!

Can we talk about shadow priest threat generation in PvE?

 ----------
Threat Calculation:

Mind Blast has a 200% Threat Multiplier.

Vampiric Embrace heals for 25% of the dmg dealt.
Improved VE heals for 30% of the dmg dealt per person in grp.
So if your group drops in hp, its 150% of the dmg dealt in heal threat.

Then in addition to that you generate threat for the mana restored, which is another 2% per player in grp.
So in total its 10% of the dmg dealt.

So in Total Threat:

 100% of the dmg dealt + 150% of the dmg dealt in healing + 10% of dmg in mana reg = 260% per dmg
In additon you deal 200% more dmg threat every 6 seconds with Mind Blast.
Which results in more than 300% threat.

 -----------
Reasoning:

I dont see a reason for shadow priests to do so much threat, while only being middle of the pack in dmg.
Even compared to all other casters, which have lots of tools to combat threat.
Such as Set Bonuses that reduce threat, etc.

Meanwhile the Shadow Priest Set in Kara even reduces your Mind Blast Cooldown, so you generate even more with your 200% threat multiplier on it.

In Vanilla the Mind Blast Threat Multiplier wasnt that big of a problem, since it had way lower spellpower coefficent.
In Twow several spells got adjustments, but the threat multiplier didnt.

Please dont bring up Priest Tank, thats really just a fraction of all priest players.

 ---------------
Possible Solutions that are in the game now (Spoiler, they arent close to enough):

Now you could say, just take all the anti threat talents there are.
But in reality you dont want to go into the disc tree second anymore, for the extra spellpower from spirit in the holy tree.

And in the shadow tree, its so akwardly placed, that you need to take points out of Shadow Weaving, Mind Blast(your best dmg/gcd ability, which also has a 6 sec Cooldown) or Spellhit.

Other classes can help, like Paladin's Salvation that is in every raid.
Second a Shaman in the Shadow Priest group, which is much much more rare.

 -----------
Possible Solutions that could be implemented, in my opinion:

Remove the Mind Blast Threat Multiplier
Reduce the healing done by improved VE
Give improved VE anti threat build in
Reduce points needed in a shadow talent, that allows to spec into anti threat,
without losing identity giving or damage talents.

 -----------

Sorry for the wall of text, but i really think its a problem when the best thing to do is not pressing your buttons.
I hope some Twow Dev takes the time to read this and reply :)
