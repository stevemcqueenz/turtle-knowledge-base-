# Horde race for pvp.

## Post 145377 by Podgnil — 2025-08-14T11:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145377#p145377 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I had this debate even back in the older patch — Undead or Troll for Priest — but Undead always won for me thanks to the visuals. However, in this patch, with the increased TTK coming, I think Troll is actually the better option for a Smite Priest.

Every priest has Fear Ward now, which devalues Will of the Forsaken a bit.

The Undead racial DoT costs way too much mana, which is critical in longer fights.

Over the course of a fight, Shadowguard ends up dealing more damage and has way better mana efficiency (damage per mana).

Trolls’ Hex of Weakness reduces healing by 20%, which is a must-have in comps without a warrior — and even in comps with one, it's still a nice bonus.
(Anyone know if it stacks with Mortal Strike and rogue poisons?)

You can also spam rank 1 Hex for 30 mana as dispel bait, which gives you a solid advantage at range or lets you burst more efficiently.

One more thing: I’m thinking it might be worth picking Blackout instead of Spiritual Guidance — it gives you a decent chance to escape from melees when it procs from Shadowguard or SW:Pain.

What do you guys think?

I've recently played Troll Warlock and the haste racial is better than I expected. In critical situations like duels, every millisecond matters - the meta is still to kill enemy faster than he kills you. So thanks to this racial you are able to cast spell faster than enemy - against casters you might push back their casting. And against melees... you just kill them faster. Haste racial is underrated.

I play mostly Undead Smite Priest. Undead has devouring plague which isn't much useful for us. Costs a lot of mana and with burst meta it's not even worth using because you would kill enemy faster without wasting this global CD. For Shadow Priests might be more useful though.
Regarding WotF, it's still helpful. But most of the time I use it is when I am feared. Which doesn't happen often because I usually have Fear Ward active. Sleep and Mind Control effects hardly ever happen to me so this racial - at least for Priests - isn't as good as on other classes.
