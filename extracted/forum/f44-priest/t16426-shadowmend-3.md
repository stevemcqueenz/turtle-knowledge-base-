# Shadowmend <3

## Post 111976 by Glaive (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-10T01:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111976#p111976 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I feel like shadow priest needs a little something...

In my opinion, the Shadowmend spell could be that thing.  It's not really viable right now but it could be.  Right now it costs a lot, takes a long time to cast AND has a long cooldown.  I think if you removed the cooldown that would be a step in the right direction but also you could make it a little faster as well.  Given that it is very mana inefficient, I don't think we'd be in danger of seeing any "shadow healers."  It would simply be usable for what I imagine it was intended; a spot heal that when played well, could add depth to the spec.

This is just my 2 cents.  However, I do hear other people talking wistfully about Shadowmend, so I know I'm not alone.

Thanks all,

Glaive
