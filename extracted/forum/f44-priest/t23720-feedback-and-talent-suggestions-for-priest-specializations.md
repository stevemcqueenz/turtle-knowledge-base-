## Post 166198 by Silveredmoon — 2026-01-24T02:48:00Z

Dear Turtle WoW Team,

First of all, I would like to express my sincere gratitude for your dedication to the game environment. Playing here truly recaptures that "first-time" magic of World of Warcraft—as we say, "we have one more world than everyone else."

Drawing from my extensive experience playing the Priest class, I have compiled some feedback and suggestions regarding the current talent directions. I hope these insights are helpful for future development.

 1. Holy Priest: Defining the "Heal-to-Full" Niche
In a raid environment, Holy Priests are versatile supports. While we may not match the specialized HOT output of Druids, the single-target efficiency of Paladins, or the group-healing utility of Shamans, the current meta often makes Holy Priests feel redundant. High mana costs and long cast times struggle to keep up with the modern raid tempo.

I am very pleased with the direction of the 1.18.1 updates. It seems the team is finally solidifying the Holy Priest's identity. To further balance us against other classes (who have received significant buffs to their traditional weaknesses), I suggest the following:

Introduction of Prayer of Mending: This skill would bridge the gap in our "proactive" healing. Providing a buff that heals upon taking damage and jumps to nearby allies would give Priests the necessary tools to maintain raid stability without being outpaced by instant-cast healers.

T3.5 Set Bonus Adjustment: To better align with the new vision, I suggest changing the 5-piece set bonus (mana cost reduction) to apply to all healing or Holy spells, rather than a specific subset. This would provide the sustained throughput needed for upcoming challenges.

 2. Shadow Priest: Leveling and Scaling
The current early-tier Shadow talents feel somewhat lackluster for a level 60 character.

Spirit Tap Improvement: Currently, this is only useful for solo leveling. I suggest modifying it so that Mind Blast critical strikes can trigger a minor version of the Spirit Tap buff. This would give the talent value in raid encounters where "killing blows" are rare.

AOE Capabilities: While our "Utility DPS" role is acceptable, the lack of any meaningful AOE makes certain encounters frustrating. If the goal is pure DPS, an AOE tool is essential; if the goal is support, our current kit is close, but still needs a slight edge in scaling.

 3. Discipline Priest: Making Spirit Meaningful
Historically, Discipline has been a "filler" tree for PvP or hybrid builds. In the Turtle WoW environment, its identity remains a bit blurry.

Spirit to Spell Power Conversion: I suggest fine-tuning the talents that convert Spirit into Spell Power/Healing. Spirit is a core Priest stat, and making this conversion more efficient would allow both Holy and Shadow Priests to better utilize their gear, creating a unique "stat-scaling" identity for the class.

Thank you for your time and for everything you do for the community. I look forward to the future of Turtle WoW!

Best regards
