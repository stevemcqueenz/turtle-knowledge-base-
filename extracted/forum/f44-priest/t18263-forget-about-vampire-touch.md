If you look at classes powerlevel in 1.12.1 shadowpriest is one of the strongest small skirmish pvp spec (at least among casual, noone cares about flasked up 4 resistence pots running shadow reflector and fearbreak equipped duels)

A large part of this is vampiric embrace. Since he could outsustain the damage that made it past his shadow form, inner fire and pw: shield relatively easily.

This made shadowpriest hugely unfun to fight. especially on classes with no way of getting rid of VE. The idea behind the nerf was afaik to open up possibility to deny the shadow passive healing by making VE specifically be interruptable.

In my opinion a quite sensible balance adjustment (at least in theory). In practice however, the burst got dialed up to 19/10 for all classes so outlasting oponents as a gameplan got outright removed as a viable gameplan, and so the in theory more counterplayable shadowpriest now exists in a meta where his strengths dont apply anymore. So the nerf feels unjustified
