Smite has no mana problems in raids. As long as you use tea with mana potions and your guild kills bosses quickly, you will never run out of mana. At least this is my experience in Kara40/Naxx. I don't know how it is in lower raids when your gear is not that good.

But if you somehow run out of mana, Holy Fire Rank 1 is your best spell to use because it costs about 80 mana and scales very well, 1:1 with spell damage if I remember correctly.
