# Priest quest idea for different racial spell

## Post 106370 by Silverleafeon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-04T17:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106370#p106370 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What if priests had the option to replace/learn a racial spell from a different race? Like a Night Elf learning Desperate Prayer, a Human learning Devouring Plague, etc.

I know the idea is each race is supposed to be unique, but I've always found this particular aspect to be a bit too restrictive. Arguably, it would be more realistic and lore friendly to be able to learn the teachings of other priests from different races. As a priest, adventuring out in the world, it makes sense that you'd come across different ideas and beliefs, and perhaps you would like to learn more about them.

I think a class quest should become available to priests at level 30 that allows the player to seek out a trainer of another race who sends you off on a quest chain to learn a new racial spell. For opposite faction spells, there might be a neutral npc that gives you the quest.
