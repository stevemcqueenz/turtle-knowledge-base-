Yes, you can find raids spots.  I think the main change is just spriests have had their mana issues addressed which allows them to be competitive in DPS.  But be warned that mages and warlocks do out damage spriests in later content.

Obviously, there's no guarantee that you won't be asked to heal some as a hybrid class.  Also, we have no idea how the class distribution will be on the new pvp server.  It's possible that spriests are very popular, and mages or warlocks are more in demand.  But all three are popular in PVP, so seems like a wash.  But if you really want to not heal in raids, then it's probably safer to roll a pure dps class.
