Here are some proposed changes/updates to shadow priest to make it more competitive and enticing as a spec.

Talent changes:
Revert: Blackout to 3 second stun duration OR change it so each tick of shadow damage from the caster has a chance to stun for 2 seconds with an internal CD.
Move: Shadowform moved to level 30 talent.
Add: Hex (lvl 40 talent) (traditionally, always a shadow spell since the dawn of classic wow). Cast by Vile Priestess Hexx, Smolderthorn Shadow Priest, Sandfury Shadowhunter, Atal'ai Witchdoctor (the only one not slightly related to shadow priest).
Move Vampiric Embrace to level 20 talent row.
Vampiric Touch remains on lvl 30 talent row, requiring Vampiric Embrace.
Mind Flay: Now a baseline spell learned at level 20 (or sooner, maybe even 18 since it sucks worse than wanding without spell damage gear)

Baseline Change:
Shadow Word: Pain - now does 1 tick of damage on initial cast. Duration decreased by 3 seconds to compensate for 1 free tick. Now scales with haste (decreasing time between ticks of damage, and simultaneously decreasing duration to compensate for decreased tick windows)

Mind Flay: now scales with haste, similar to Arcane mages.

Add Shadow Word: Death - only usable while target is below 35% health. Does execute worthy damage, with a cooldown of 12 seconds. Costs % of maximum mana (not base mana), deals damage to caster = 20% of damage.

These are some random ideas I've had. I hope many people, especially shadow priest enthusiasts agree with me on the Hex talent change.
