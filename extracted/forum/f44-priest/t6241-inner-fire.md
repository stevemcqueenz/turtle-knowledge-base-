"Hell to level a priest", lmao. Priest is like one of the easiest classes to level. Tanky as hell due to inner fire, has access to shields and self-heals and his wands do 25% more damage + his melee attacks with staves get AP increase now which is big help if u can find a good staff.

Here's how you do it.

Holy fire > Dot > Wand hit 1 > Wand hit 2 (they're in melee now) > Weave in Staveattack, while you do this your spells are off-cd because staveattack does not trigger GCD, you use that moment to refresh shield > Wand Attack 3, Wand attack 4 (staff ready to hit again) use it.

Seriously, research how to weave in melee attacks and you'll notice a big dps increase.
