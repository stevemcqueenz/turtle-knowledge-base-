# Spirit of Redemption - be better with 5% spirit only

## Post 114234 by Matches (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-12-25T14:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114234#p114234 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As a healer Priest, I stack spirit for mana regen.

Talent Spirit of Redemption is for me a pain in the neck - I play AV mainly, and that for PvE, and when I'm dead, I want to come back to life ASAP, not after 10 seconds.

In any event I find the talent pretty much a non-starter because most of your time is spend trying to find someone to heal and then your time is up.

However, the talent now gives 5% spirit so I've kinda gotta take it, which kinda sucks.  I'd be *much* happier if you gave me the 5% spirit and did *NOT* give me the 10 seconds being useless.

As an aside, I note I had 401 spirit, and adding the talent took me to 417 spirit, which is not 5%.

(Divine Spirit is worth 40 spirit, in and of itself, and I now as expected get 42 spirit from it, so that looks to be working correctly.)
