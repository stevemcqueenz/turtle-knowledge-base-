Let's talk Shadow Mend...

Shadow Mend is a 3 second cast heal, so the scaling should be: 3/3.5s - 85.71%. From testing, the spell coefficient is around 45%.

Can this be corrected to 85.71%?

Or, if the coefficient is going to stay ~45%, then can we get the cast speed to reflect this, 1.5/3.5s - 42.85%?

Additionally, let's take a look at its effectiveness...

The mana cost is 635 mana. Greater Heal has a similar cost in both mana 710 and cast speed 3s but is 4x as effective with no 30s cool down.

Can we get the mana cost reduced or the base heal amount increased?
