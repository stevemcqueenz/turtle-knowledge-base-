# Why do you hate Shadow Priests?

## Post 151182 by Skattberg — 2025-09-10T19:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151182#p151182 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In recent patches, all the other classes have gotten cool new stuff: Mages have two broken channeled spells, benefits from haste along with other insane burst damage combos they can mix in the talent tree, Warlocks are just broken atm, affliction with their Dark Harvest, they benefit from haste and a Felguard that dominates in arena, Rogues got insane utility they did not need and Warriors got theirs in recent patch, not to speak of paladins and shamans.

What have Shadow Priests gotten? No longer cast time on VE. What?! Blackout is useless, its unreliable and its too weak to be taken advantage of. When the global cd is down its barely 1 sec left to use to cast spells. Psychic Scream is the only big defensive spell we got, but with recent buffs to pvp trinket and all other tools other classes got to counter it, its in most cases countered easily. VE is what it is; weak, due to nerf but crucial nontheless. Mind Flay, our bread and butter, is 20 yd cast range and gets interrupted by druids shifts, paladins hand of freedom and other anti-movement impairing spells/items AND pushback removes dps. It feels VERY stale in comparison to mages/warlocks 30yd pump cast extra dmg benefiting from haste channeled spells. Can we please get some love instead of two steps back and one forward?

And by the way, Chaste on 2 sec cc? Thats so weak
