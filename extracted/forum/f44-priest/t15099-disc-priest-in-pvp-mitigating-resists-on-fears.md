Spell hit and resistance are two different things basically. You want 3% spell hit in PvP to make your spells able to hit on same-lvl targets. Increasing spell hit doesn't do anything about resistances however, so putting more than 2 points in shadow focus as priest is pointless in PvP.

The confusion is probably that both a 'miss' from a spell due to low spell hit (e.g. attacking a raid boss with no spell hit gear) and a resist due to enemy resistances show as 'resist' when the spell lands.

To counter the resists on spells which you are probably experiencing against warlocks and priests the most, you need spell penetration (that's the only thing that works there) or a way to lower targets resistances (lock curse e.g.)

Hello,

Yes, to my knowledge spell penetration works 1:1 on resistances. So 20 spell penetration lowers the resist of enemy by 20.

Spell hit cap is at level 60:
 3% for lvl 60 mob and PvP
 4% for lvl 61 mob
 5% for lvl 62 mob
 16% for lvl 63 mob (raid bosses)

As you can see, you only need the really high spell hit for raid bosses, so you can get away with 1 or 2 points (or even none) in shadow focus depending on gear for pvp
