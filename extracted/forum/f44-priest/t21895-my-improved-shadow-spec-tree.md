Hey!
I'll go talent by talent.

Blackout: I see no reason for buffing the stun duration of a randomly procking talent. This would feel a bigger RNG punishment at PVP while barely affecting PVE instances.

Shadow Affinity: It is in a weird state now. You ask two talent points for it but it reduces threat by 8/15/25%. 25% threat reduction for 2 points might be too much.

Shadow Focus: I can get behind it. The wildcard for shadow priest pushback is PW:S which is indirectly an interruption preventer. But this is something I'd accept.

Shadow Reach: I can get behind the new placement and that change.

Shadow Weaving: By default a bit too much but with recent TWOW talent changes I can accept it.

Improved Mind Flay: I agree that at PVE it needs a bigger range and to enable it from PVP pov you need to weaken the slow. But a mind flay with 100% reduced slow, is it a mind flay at all? The damage buff... Not sure, would need to see damage meters.

Vampiric Touch: You just made its mana regen effect 2,5x better for the same TP. That is too much.

Shadow Arts: This doesn't really feel Vanilla. A talent reducing GCD I mean.

Overall, if you do a rework, please highlight the talents you changed and always tell why you made a specific change.
