# Petition to change the Shameful "Elune's Grace" Priest Racial

## Post 29831 by Nightowl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-25T18:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29831#p29831 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

"Elune's Grace" - Level 20: Reduces damage taken by 95 and increases dodge by 10% for 15 seconds.

On a WHOOPING 5min cd at that btw. Blizzard feared the elusive Night Elf Priest tank so much they gave it a whooping 5min cd! This ability is so shit that you could literally reduce it to 30 second cd and would change absolutely nothing. So considering that Dwarf with addition of High Elf that gives priests access to that powerful 20% mana regeneration, I feel that Night elf priests are made obsolete. With this in mind, why not give it a buff or replace it altogether with something more Night-elf priestly? Here are a few ideas I've had in mind;

A) Buff it to 50-80% dodge. That way in something goes wrong and tank loses aggro because he got feared, the Priest can pop his own Evasion called "Elune's Grace" and try weather the storm by dodging like a graceful, elusive being which Night Elves are supposed to be. It won't be overpowered because in order to take advantage of it you'd literally would have to stand there and or use instant cast abilities.

B) Replace it with a 5min CD buff that Empowers your Starshard into becoming a "DoT", so that you can spam it on several mobs creating a mini Starfall as per WC3 Tyrande's ability:

"Elune's tears" - level 20: For the next 15-30 seconds your Starshards will act as DoTs that do not need to be channeled, your Starshard will also cost 25% less mana - 3-5 min cd.

Alternative to that would be

"Elune's blessing" - level 20: Your next starfall will hit all enemies within 30 yards - 1min cd.

With that change, the Night Elf priest would be capable of fulfilling the WC3 Priest Fantasy of channeling AOE Starfall - an ability that was later stolen from them and given to Boomkins - as well as be given their own respective niche in PVE.
