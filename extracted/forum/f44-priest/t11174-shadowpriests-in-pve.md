## Post 73757 by Quidoba (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-10-25T19:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73757#p73757 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When it comes to mana issues, shadow priest on TWOW plays a lot more like a mage in classic than a shadow priest in classic.  You don't have infinite mana, but it is pretty manageable, and gets better and better as you accumulate more gear.

The difference is attributable to: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=45553>

Just like in classic, you don't need to get as much hit. Also crit is of little value. So you can itemize for some mp5 and even spirit (you can get 30% regen while casting due to improved shadow form + talents).

In low end gear, I see shadow priests consistently out-damage mages and warlocks (re-itemization of mage and warlock t1 might level this difference).  At high end gear, shadow priests begin to fall behind.  At high end gear, smite spec actually out damages shadow spec, but you probably lose more utility for the overall raid than is worth the small dps bump.  Smite is fun to do on raids you have on farm.

Priests are pretty well PVE balanced on TWOW imo. Spriests slap in pvp.
