Can anyone share experience playing disc priest on TWOW? How viable this spec in pvp? Since gear progression went too far and no resilience... like shadow priest has damage reduction from shadowform, what i can't say about disc. Probably i have to stack up a lot stamina to increase survivability, otherwise will get oneshoted by most classes.

## Post 98805 by Springboards (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2024-07-07T13:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98805#p98805 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

precisely, you hit the nail on the head. stack lots of stamina(e.g ragnaros str cloak as stupid as it sounds, str tanking warrior ring from aq40,naxx 24 stam ring,nefarian tanking neck, etc), and you probably still want t2.5 or t3 since you need some intellect so you are not going oom. Stamina is your only defence and its better to die with 0 mana than keep dying with full mana and not being able to use it. R12+gear is also good, about as good as t2.5/t3 but as a priest you shouldnt have trouble getting into high tier raiding guilds..
