# How would you fix Shadow Priest in order for it to not be dogshit?

## Post 36112 by Nightowl (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-25T22:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36112#p36112 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In this thread I'd like to discuss and throw shit together with other Shadow Priest enthusiasts on the important topic of Shadow's viability in raids beyond Shadow Weaving talent - which can be picked by other specs btw.

 1)
The first thing I'd like to propose is to switch places between Shadow Weaving with Darkness (10% flat shadow damage at 5points at 6th tier of shadow tree will now be in 4th tier) talents.

This change will limit Discipline's and Holy's potential to effectively replace the utility of Shadow Priest by going 20 points into shadow. Now you have to put 30 points into shadow in order to get that benefit, which might be a bit too steep of a price for healing specs. In other words, if you want to buff Warlock's damage, you're gonna have to bite the bullet and bring the actual Shadow Priest into raid which is how in my eyes it should have been originally since the ability of Holy/Disc to spend 20 points to get SW effectively invalidated Shadow's existance.

 2)
Turtle Devs need to figure out the identity of Shadow Priest. Do they want it to be an utility/dps that buffs warlocks and brings mana regeneration to casters that need it, or do they want a proper dps class that can do actual competitive dps?

If it's the former, then I believe that the mana regeneration needs to be buffed since 2% is a bit too low to justify a presence of Shadow Priest in an optimal group. I think that 10% would be a nice test to see how it goes. Perhaps higher, perhaps lower, and that will of course depend on philosophy of Devs. I think that Shadow Priest can fill a nice role being a Mana Regenerator for classes with mana issues. IF you can hit a sweet spot where Shadow Priest effectively gives Moonkins, Elemental Shamans and other such classes more staying power in prolonged fight, then this class will not only be viable but also bring viability to specs with limited mana potential which is a win/win scenario in my eyes.

If it's the latter, then I believe that Shadow Priest needs some spammable single target ability that doesn't break PvP. There have been a very good thread about discussion of Shadow Priest's aoe potential, but I disagree with that philosophy since what ultimately matters in this game is single target damage potential - with some rare exceptions that can be filled by other classes that lack other types of utility priest brings to the table. "Element of choise" Bolt spamming IS vanilla gameplay unfortunately, perhaps that is what Shadow Priest also needs?

There are of course other options, such as the ones we saw in later xpacs but I don't hold much faith in Turtle-Devs team being able to implement something similar to Shadow Orb and Shadow Abberation systems we saw in more recent expansions since those required a different type of coding. Here's my proposition for such ability:

"Void bolt" - level 60 ability or some talent you can grab in tier 7 (similar fashion to Arcane tree having 2 tier 7 talents). Does x amount of damage but can only be used if target has 5 stacks of shadow weaving on it. This removes the front-burst potential that might ruin pvp but adds some inevitability for Priest in prolonged fights, as well as something spammable on Single Target to keep up with Mages, Locks and other "bolt spammers".

 3)
Back to AOE-ish discussion, and this is more of a question I have towards community, but what if one could add "cleaving potential" to Shadow Priest? I was thinking a cooldown that makes your next 1-5 mind blasts hit all targets who are currently dotted with Shadow Word: Pain. Max rank only to prevent abuse of spamming cheap low ranks since that might be a bit too strong, but I could be wrong here.

 4)
I'd love to expand this discussion to Holy and Discipline as well potentially. For instance, it never made sense to me that a supposed healing spec has access to some great damage increasing talents and even an AOE nova. And discipline should have always been a "Twilight" spec, essentially a middle ground between Holy and Shadow. That way you could have had "Twilight Nova" (does damage AND heals, essentially a Twilight spell right?) that could have been usable by Shadow Priests IN DISCIPLINE tree.

In general I really dislike Discipline tree in it's current form and I believe that it should be complitely revamped with something more interesting. The final talent is atrocious as well. Abilities and Talents like Power Infusion and Bloodlust should give effects to both caster and the target you want to buff otherwise just scrap them complitely or make them selfish talents since it really freaking sucks giving your cool shit to someone else because that someone else is more "optimal". It's like having a cool toy you never get to play with and on top of that most always give to someone else - freaking sucks man.

Topic 1
Not sure if i like the idea of moving shadowweaving further down. In my oppinion its a creative spec on a healpriest. Trying to get the best of both worlds and it comes with its own downsides. A priest that needs to keep this debuff up might not be healing in a crucial moment.
And frankly shadow priest, especially with the new debuff cap id not a bad spec to bring. However i see that priests in the stricter guilds might get "forced into it" by their raid.

I guess in the end it would be a slight positive switching the two talents up. But there are certainly arguments in both directions.

Topic 2
The talent tree website say you get 4% damage as mana. Since i dont have a priest i cant test this myself, so im sticking with your 2%
The mana & health regeneration shadows currently provide is nothing to sneeze at. Assuming 400 dps which should be within reach without consumables, you restore 8 mana and 120 health to every target within your group.
That adds up to a potential 40mana a second and 600healing. This is a lot of utility from a spellcaster as is.
Mana is a resource that is balanced around eventually depleting. It is possible to make it last extremely long or in some easier fights indefinitely. But we shouldn't make it too easy.

Also, the total value provided in health dmg & mana of shadow is already good if you can take adventage of all aspects provided. Either health or dmg would need to be massively nerfed to compensate.

I also think the flavor of Shadow as a utilitycaster is excellent and i would not want it replaced by "yet another flavor of warlock"

Topic 3
Shadow word pain can be multidoted. This is shadows cleave. I do not want a wotlk light where the default mode of operation for every class is to use aoe. You can skill holy nova for a secondary aoe (its actually qiite good on turtle) but you have to choose either nova or additional threat reduction.
