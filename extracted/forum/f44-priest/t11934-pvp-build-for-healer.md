Thats a nice spec!

I experimented a bit as a priest in bgs and I think blackout is pretty good.

Half a year ago I played alot this spec here:
<https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/priest/U2K9IC6-C-52KD3V8>

Works very well. My focus is not that much about healing. Despelling, silence, dotting, vampiric embrace, mindblast, mindflay and shield theprimary spells. If you see an ally dropping in health you pump some flashheals/shield into him.

Gear thats needed for this spec must be +spelldmg gear because it also boosts our healing abilities.

People will be surprised how good the dmg potential actually is.

The whole idea behind it was that especially if fights start I didnt had much to do, besides weak dots. The healing often even wasnt that necessary.

So you loose some healingpower (less healing, no greater heal, no heal, in fights just flashheal), but you gain quite a few useful spells like: silence, vampiric embrace, mindflay (which is pretty good) and reduced fear cd (which is pretty good too).

When I pvp these days I go full shadow, but I am able help allies quickly with shields or drop out of shadowform and pump flashheals into them.

So the better the gear the more I would lean into shadow.

Priest is definately a great class to play for all hybridlovers. Awesome supporter!

I also tried smitepriest as a hybrid/healer but went back to the old spec because the skills you gain through shadow are just too good compared to smite/holy fire.
