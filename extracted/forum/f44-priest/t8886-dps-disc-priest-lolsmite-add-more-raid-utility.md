# DPS Disc priest (LOLSmite) - Add more raid utility

## Post 57322 by Quidoba (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-14T19:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57322#p57322 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I personally haven't taken disc (lol smite) beyond the beginning raids (ZG, AQ20, MC), but it seems to have very competitive damage potential when paired with a ret pally.  Mana seems to be an issue, but that seems manageable with consumes and itemizing into some spirit/mp5.

At this point it seems the main thing that would hold a disc priest back from late game raid viability would be its comparatively low utility compared to other comparable DPS options, including shadow.  Holy nova AOE is one utility benefit, but given its mana cost, it is of limited utility in any meaningful encounter, so I don't give it much weight.

One utility disc priests sort of bring is the potential to be a decent backup healer.  Similar to boomkin, ele, shadow, disc priests have enough +healing, mana and perhaps regen to step in mid-fight when a main healer goes down, or during an enrage, etc.

I'm a big fan of not reinventing anything or shaking the status quo builds up much, so I don't want to give disc priests anything that makes them mimic or trade places with any other build out there.  Therefore, I thought it would be cool if they could be boosted in a way that is unique and somewhat already true of them currently.  Why not make disc priests the ultimate back-up healer?

Here's one idea:

New talent - **Holy Focus** (1/1) (next to reflective sheilds in disc tree)
After landing a critical strike with smite or holy fire you enter a Holy Focus state that lasts for 30 seconds.  The Holy Focus state reduces the mana cost of all direct heals, holy nova, and prayer of healing spells by 50%.

 - this would be deep enough disc to be inaccessible to holy priests taking Holy Champion.
 - the smite and holy fire crit component would make this talent less useful to deep disc healing priests, but something they might consider if they want to do some risky weaving with low rank smites in some encounters
 - the 30 second duration and 50% mana reduction would mean disc DPS priests would only really benefit from the talent for a short window after switching from DPS to backup healing. For an extended period they would also go OOM
 - the crit component would make crit slightly more valuable to smite priests without affecting their current DPS scaling
 - including Holy Nova in the list would allow for some interesting play styles in open world farming and in raids that I can't even anticipate fully.
 - the depth of the talent and the fact that it is in a different talent tree than spiritual guidance and spirit tap would keep it from having much affect on the already pretty decent smite priest leveling experience, or would give levelers a meaningful choice to make.
 - sweaty naxx raids would potentially see this as not useful, because why would you plan on healers dying in a well tuned raiding machine right?  Therefore this change doesn't disrupt current end-game raid meta much, but could still boost disc priest DPS considerations.
 - some raids might get fancy and let disc priests under holy focus take over a healing roll intermittently to allow main healers to enter full regen for a period to improve the overall mana sustain of a long fight.
 - With spirit tap and spiritual guidance fully accessible while getting this talent, the potential for intermittent cheap heals would further boost an already uniquely sustainable open-world farming strength disc priests have.  However, not adding renew or shield to the list of benefitting spells would keep this from being too perfect.

So an alternative approach would be:

New talent - Holy Focus (1/1) (next to reflective sheilds in disc tree)
Landing a critical strike with smite or holy fire applies a Holy Focus buff to all party members.  Holy Focus lasts 10 seconds, provides 1% haste, and stacks up to 3 times.

The name works even better thematically, since "Focus" seems like something that would potentially give you haste.

A small party haste buff would be useful in healer, dps, or even tank groups.  This would increase the chances that a smite priest could get into a sanc aura group which is pretty essential for keeping up with the pack in dps.

This also allows crit to stay more relevant for a smite priest.
