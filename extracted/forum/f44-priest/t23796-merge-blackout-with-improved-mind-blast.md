What do you reckon?

This would free up the 5 talent points for the devs to use elsewhere in the shadow tree, which I think it needs.

With this we could have Improved Shadowform as a talent to grab right away rather than as a level 60 ability, freeing up that level 60 ability to used by something else.

An AOE other than Holy Nova perhaps? Such as Mind Sear or Shadow Crash, it would also greatly benefit those leveling as shadow due to the the passive mana regeneration the improved shadowform would give them.

The extra talent space provided could also provide a means to give shadow talented pushback resistance, which is something it really needs.

Something to make better use of pain spike would be nice as well, something to keep it from healing the target after landing being the most obvious. This way it could be used for tagging mobs quickly in busy areas.

P.S, Apologies if this has been brought up before. I was playing my Moonkin yesterday when I randomly thought, why doesn't imp mind blast work the same way imp starfury does?

Feels like a waste of 5 points which could be used for better things.
