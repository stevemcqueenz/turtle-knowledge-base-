Firstly, if you just start out and are in greens, do not go to black morass. That dungeon is difficaultywise somewhere around zg. Get some other gearpieces from easier places before.

With that warning out of the way, the number one item to be aware of is [Nordrannar herbal tea](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=61675) it helps you sustain yourself in pve much better and if you are so inclined is the cheapest consumable for pvping.
Among the easiest way to get it is doing the blooming hyjalroot & stockpiling hyjalroot quests and turning then breaking the shards down with the shard de-harmonisation quest.

Gearwise more is always better, so i wont bore you with any longwinded explanations on what to get. But the best gear available is & most likely always will be from endgame raids.

Shadownmend is quite lackluster as a spell. Its generally not worth using it. If you really need to heal, its better to switch out of shadowform and use proper heals instead. You wont miss it if you dont have it. But its tradeable and sometimes goes for a low price on the AH, but dont waste much gold on it.

If you have the gear, shadowpriest is among the absolutely strongest classes for 1v1. But foe the beginning i can only recommend you try to pick unfair fights :)
