I haven't had the time to play for a while, but I've been leveling an Arcane Priest for a while now.

My outdoors rotation is:

Against lower-leveled enemies:
 1. Power Word: Shield on self
 2. Holy Fire
 3. Starshards
 4. Wand

Against higher-leveled enemies:
 1. Power Word: Shield on self
 2. Holy Fire
 3. Starshards
 4. Holy Fire
 5. Starshards
 6. Wand

I think you get the gist.

When dps must increase: Add in Shadow Word: Pain
When the target must die now, because they run away to get backup: Mind Blast, now!

The disadvantage compared to Shadow Priest is, dps is roughly half of a Shadow Priest.
The advantage is, this build has almost no downtime between enemies. No need to rest and drink, just go from enemy to enemy.

Between a Starshards cast and a Holy Fire cast is about 9.5 seconds. Starshards consumes the mana at the start of the cast, Holy Fire at the end of the cast.

This gives one full tick of MP5, or two if you're willing to wait/wand another 0.5 seconds. And this is what makes this build so downtime-less.

The only spells that increase your downtime are Shadow Word: Pain and Mind Blast. So as long as you're patient, you will be rewarded out of combat.

Invest in Spirit on your gear, since it increases your MP5.

MVP talent: Spirit Tap, from the Shadow tree. That's the only Shadow talent I have. Otherwise, it's all Discipline and Holy. Almost no other talents synergize, which allows me to take standard healer build talents after Spirit Tap. Because of this, I run about 5 talent points behind the build of a standard healer. You can try other things.

This build works with only 5 talent points, so you're free to take talents however you wish with your 45 remaining ones.

One note is to end combat with Wand or Starshards, not Holy Fire. Like in, the last 10% of the enemy should not be targeted with Holy Fire. Using Starshards or Wand means the moment combat ends and Spirit Tap triggers, you're already halfway there going for your first MP5 tick.

At level 50+, this build is so efficient you're likely to have full mana when your Spirit Tap is only halfway done. Go to the next enemy, and enjoy the damage bonus from Spirit Tap. If you activate Sharshards while Spirit Tap is active, the damage bonus applies to the entire 6 second duration of your Starshards, even if Spirit Tap ends 1 second later.

 -----

My dungeon rotation is:

 - Be a standard healer with healer spells
 - Wand if nobody is dying
 - Use Starshards for that extra bit of damage when your mana is maxed out and nobody is dying

 ------

Reading the patch notes, I should rethink my build. I haven't done that part yet.

The sun is shining today after being cloudy all week, so I thought I make some calculations for outdoor use.

I made a little Excel to get some calculations going before I change my talents in the game.

The major advantage of current Starshards is, its 6-second channel time. It guarantees mana regen after 5 seconds equal to (1/4 of your Spirit). And if you keep wanding/shooting for four more seconds instead of casting spells, you get an extra tick of that delicious mana. Which means less downtime;
to a minimum of 24 seconds: the cooldown of Starshards minus its Channel time.
to a minimum of 20 seconds: the cooldown of Starshards minus its Channel time and 4 seconds of wanding/shooting.

From my theorycraft, I come to the following options at level 58 (my current level) and a nice round 400 Spirit (100 mana regen after Starshards).

 **My Target:** Kill a Winterspring Bear (3680 hp)

 **Starshards Shadow Build**

 1. Mind Blast
 2. Shadow Word: Pain (24 sec)
 3. Starshards

 4004.25 damage over 9 seconds spellcasting + remaining DoT
 1420 mana used

 **Starshards Improved Shadowform Build**
 *(At level 60, with 15% mana reduction.)*

 1. Mind Blast
 2. Shadow Word: Pain (24 sec)
 3. Starshards

 4004.25 damage over 9 seconds spellcasting + remaining DoT
 1297 mana used

 **Starshards Disc Build**

 1. Holy Fire
 2. Shadow Word: Pain (24 sec)
 3. Starshards

 3776.325 damage over 10.5 seconds spellcasting + remaining DoT
 1300 mana used

 4. Holy Fire (resets mp5)

 4324.95 damage over 13.5 seconds spellcasting + remaining DoT
 1530 mana used

Disc has a hybrid flavor win as you can have a Holy DoT, a Shadow DoT, and an Arcane DoT running all at the same time. But the values are otherwise all kinda similar.

Next, I'm not a guide writer. I just post my own findings. They can be wrong, take it with a grain of salt.

This build has lots of spare talents. Use them as you see fit.

 **Shadow Build Talents**

 **Shadow**

 - Tier 1: **Improved Spirit Tap** (5/5) to reduce downtime, regaining more mana out of combat
 - Tier 2: **Improved Shadow Word: Pain** (2/2) increases damage dealt per mana spent by a lot, best talent for leveling I'd say
 - Tier 3: **Improved Mind Blast** (1/5) if you want to cast another Mind Blast after using Starshards.
 - Tier 3: **Mind Flay** (0/1) I didn't take this as it's not in my build. You can take it for enemies resisting your Starshards and you want some backup to fill in the damage lost.
 - Tier 4: **Shadow Weaving** I don't think it does much. I haven't made my calculations with the talent in mind. Though at most, it increases your Shadow DoT by 6% as you might not be casting any other spells after it. Unless you Mind Blast after Starshards, at the cost of further mana regen. Your call really.
 - Tier 5: **Vampiric Embrace** (1/1) as a tax to reach Shadowform. I don't use it outdoors, as I can't move to increase the distance between enemies and me with its 1.5 sec casting time, it costs a bit mana, it triggers threat, and with the Vampiric Touch talent the return is like 20 mana saved after the bear is dead.
 - Tier 6: **Darkness** (5/5) +10% damage to Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Pain.
 - Tier 7: **Shadowform** (1/1) +15% damage to Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Pain. At level 60, 15% mana reduction.

 **Discipline Talents**

 - Tier 1: **Wand Specialization** and **Piercing Light**. Piercing Light I believe also reduces Starshards' resist, since it's a Discipline spell. Wand Spec, well, wand. **Mental Agility** only affects Power Word: Shield, not Starshards.
 - Tier 2: No talents are required.
 - Tier 3: **Meditation** and **Inner Focus** can be fun.
 - Tier 4: Regarding **Purifying Flames**. The maximized use of the 12% bonus to Holy spells in my calculations (Holy Fire, Smite, Smite, Smite, Smite, Holy Fire) cost exactly the same amount of mana as (Holy Fire, Shadow Word: Pain, Starshards) for roughly 750 less damage. Swap out a Smite of Mind Blast if you wish, but now the 12% bonus has even less of a total effect. Either way, with Starshards you save 2 talent points that you can put somewhere else.

 *(written as damage per mana. 2.00 equals 2 damage dealt for every 1 mana spent.)*

 - Shadow Word: Pain (+talent: 24 seconds) (3.02)
 - Starshards (2.77)
 - Mind Flay (+Darkness, +Shadow Form) (2.50)
 - Shadow Word: Pain (not talented, 18 seconds) (2.27)
 - Mind Blast (+Darkness, +Shadow Form) (1.85)
