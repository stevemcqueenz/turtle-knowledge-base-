# Rework Mind Spike Into a Finisher With Controlled Self-Damage

## Post 141227 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-25T08:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141227#p141227 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Priests currently lack a true finisher in PvP, and Mind Spike could fill that gap. I suggest changing Mind Spike into a finisher with delayed self-damage, inspired by Shadow Word: Death.

Key features of the change:

(1) Keep the existing mechanic where the target heals if the spell does not kill them
(2) Add a delayed self-damage effect to the caster when used, allowing priests to time it to break Polymorph, Blind, and similar crowd control
(3) Reduce its cooldown slightly so it can be used more often as a reactive tool
(4) Keep it aligned with Discipline’s flavor of risk-reward abilities (Chastise damaging allies, Enlightenment costing HP, etc.)

This would increase the skill ceiling for priests and give them a unique way to outplay CC while still carrying a drawback. It would not create free burst but would reward precise timing and risk-taking.

Also, abilities like Chastise are fantastic because they can be used on sapped or blinded allies (CCs we normally cannot dispel), which has been a very refreshing and creative use of the spell. A reworked Mind Spike could bring that same level of interesting gameplay to the priest toolkit.
