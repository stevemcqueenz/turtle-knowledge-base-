# Discipline Priest KZ40 Edition

## Post 131166 by Tess322 — 2025-05-15T08:00:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131166#p131166 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I would like to raise some findings and glaring issues I found by progressing KZ40 as a Discipline priest (Smite) and provide possible solutions.
Discipline is not a popular spec in PVE and for a good reason. I believe it can be remedied with the right changes.

KZ40 overview
Discipline is an extremely underplayed spec in higher tiers and for good reasons. Currently one of the main issues is mana and this is even more evident during progress. For Disc to be a viable pick in your raid roster you need to use Major Mana Pots, Teas in combination with items such as Robe of Sacrifice or Darkmoon Card: Blue Dragon. Without these additional items the priest will run out of mana just by doing its normal rotation.
This brings me to another issue, utility. As its stands Discipline brings the following utility: Enlightenment, Chastise, Shielding, Dispelling and Off healing. At the moment due to mana issues Shielding and Off healing is out of the picture as if this situation occurs where these are needed priest will run out of mana and be useless for the rest of the fight. Dispelling is not as much an issue but still affects and is affected by your mana.
Compared to Shadow which brings: Vampiric Embrace, Shadow Weaving, Shielding, Dispelling and Offhealing, you can see why it is an obvious choice over Discipline.

Another issue is that at the moment Discipline deals less damage than Shadow (with full debuffs on target) whilst not bringing the passive healing and mana regen. Discipline needs to sacrifice DPS in order to heal or shield their targets. This issue will only fester as more raid tiers are added as the problem is more a scaling issue than a flat damage one.

Suggested changes
 **Searing Light**
Mana cost discount increased from 20% to 50%
 *This would greatly help with the mana situation as this talent was very overnerfed. I would not be afraid to put it to as much as 70% but that would require testing on lower gear tiers.*

 **Resurgent Shield**
Change the 10% bonus damage and healing from damage absorbed by Shield to flat 10% spell damage and healing bonus.
 *Currently Resurgent Shield is never picked as the bonus provides around 140 spell power with uptime of 8 seconds requiring 1.5s GCD as well making it not a competitive choice versus Spiritual Guidance which provides around 80 spell power on average with 100% uptime.*

Change the 25% mana cost refund to - When your Power Word: Shield is broken, your (holy) damaging spells restore 15% of its base mana cost.
 *This would ensure the usage of shields by Discipline priests as it would help with the massive 450 mana cost in an interactive way. Yes it would make the talent unusable by healers - another way preserving the talent for healers is to make healing spells to proc this effect as well but with a lower chance.*

 **Missing Tier Set**
Gearing is another issue Discipline has in endgame, the last tier was T2.5 which is not competitive in KZ40 due to lack of worthwhile effects and stats.
As with other specs such as Feral (Cat), Discipline can folllow the same pattern - meaning having tier sets in X.5 tiers -> implementing T3.5 for Discipline priest.
 *Note: Set should have general spell power instead of holy spell power as Discipline uses Shadow Word: Pain frequently.*
