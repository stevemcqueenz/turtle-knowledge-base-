# T3 for Discipline Priest

## Post 141435 by Kobiq (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-07-25T18:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141435#p141435 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Unless there is a particular reason for not having a Discipline T3 set, here is my proposition:

 2/8 - Chastise crit chance increased by 5% (helps in both usages)
 4/8 - Your Power Word Shield reduces all damage taken by the target by 3% when it's up.
 6/8 - Critical strikes against you instantly heal you for 250 health.
 8/8 - Your successfull Smite casts have a chance to expose the target to the Light, increasing Holy damage taken by 5% for 10 seconds (I like the idea of Priest-Paladin synergy).

It should have some usual damage & healing, haste and crit.

Holy Power enchant on gloves could be added on top of that.
