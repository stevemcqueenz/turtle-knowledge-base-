Mostly it doesn't matter. You can be fully specced into the Shadow tree and still heal fine in dungeons.

Early talents to help with leveling is Spirit Tap 5/5 and Wand Specialization 2/2.
Disc/Holy trees both offer healing benefits.

Disc:
Mental Agility - reduced mana cost
Improved Power Word: Fortitude - bigger buffs
Meditation - mana regen
Improved Inner Fire - bigger self buff
Inner Focus - mana free spell
Enlighten, Mental Strength, Chastise - all good talents with utility

Holy:
Improved Renew - more healing
Holy Focus - avoid interruption (good for solo too, I pick this over Divinity as you won't be stacking crit while leveling)
Divine Fury - faster heals
Improved Healing - reduced mana cost
Spiritual Healing - bigger heals
Proclaim Champion - may be useful on a squishy tank but it tries to do everything where it may have been more useful to do 1 thing really well (the fact they are nerfing enlighten to try and get people to spec into Proclaim Champion should tell you everything you need to know)

I leveled a healer priest before cc2 so be a bit skeptycal with what I say.
In dungeons, renew was a huge help for me but for unique reasons:
You can renew and heal a tank in case of extreme pressure but in a dungeon this barely happens.
What I used DOWNRANKED renew for:
 * Putting it on DPS above half health so they heal up by the end of the fight.
 * Some heals while moving, making sure everyone enters the next pull with full hp. Specially relevant for faster runs.
 * A toy for warlocks. You see a lock in your party life tap -> Downranked renew immediately.

Raids used to be different. There was always a full T2 priest who spammed renew with the set bonus. No idea how it works now.

tldr: Renew is really useful for dungeons, mostly downranked. Non-targeted DPS, moving and healing, warlocks.

+1: As others said, you can pre-hot a tank before pull. But have a threatmeter then, it can generate a surprising amount of threat.

Many dungeons you can even heal mostly using renew. Big renew to tank, some smaller to DPS if needed and wand. With a bit of experience you can abuse 5 sec spirit regen this way and never go oom.
