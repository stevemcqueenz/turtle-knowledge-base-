# Why Nerf Shadow with Martyrdom?

## Post 110413 by Caixiao99999 (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-28T22:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110413#p110413 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Did they move Martyrdom to the Holy tree just to nerf SPriests in PvP?   dead_turtle_head

It’s so obvious that Martyrdom is a PvP talent. When it was in Discipline, both Discipline and Shadow Priests could take advantage of it. But now the devs deliberately pushed it into the Holy tree, making it nearly impossible for Shadow Priests to pick up. Feels like a targeted decision to weaken Shadow in PvP.

## Post 110418 by Bigsmerf (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-11-28T23:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110418#p110418 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Weakening strong specs is a good idea. I support this.

The issue arises when you buff weaker specs (or even already strong specs) to levels that make the previously mentioned nerf unnecessary and harmful to balance. CC2 did this to some extent with ele sham. Their burst was nerfed hard, and their niche of being a glass cannon has been replaced by specs like smite priest or boomkin or mages who alternatively have great defensive/escape tools. I'm sure there's other examples but I'm just scratching the surface with a basic idea of what the PvP situation seems to be.
