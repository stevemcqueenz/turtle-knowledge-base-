# A small suggestion concerning Night Elves and their bows

## Post 132540 by Vainamoinen77 — 2025-05-22T14:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132540#p132540 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Priest is an unique class by virtue of having access to two extra racial spells meant to represent their race's religious culture, and Night Elf priests might be the most peculiar of them all: they can use bows!

 **THE ISSUE**
While I do greatly appreciate the nod towards Tyrande and WCIII in general, I feel that the implementation leaves much to be desired. Simply put, there aren't many reasons to make use of that special proficiency. Being able to cast Searing Shot while on the move is cute, but not at all worth the opportunity cost of missing out on a wand's useful stats while also having to deal with a bow's minimum range, ammo requirments and much lower dps. Perhaps the spell could be made more worthwile by tweaking the numbers, but honestly overcharging it seems like a clunky solution. I'm aware that this ability is mostly here for roleplay purposes, but given that the other racial spell available to Night Elves is also highly situational and rarely used, they could use a little push, if only to make bow-wielding priestess of Elune a more common sight.

 **A POSSIBLE SOLUTION**
My humble suggestion would be to reduce the opportunity cost rather than increasing the pay-off, or in other words, to implement a small selection of bows that a priest might want to use. Instead of making Searing Shot stronger, which might lead a Night Elf priest to begrudgingly wield a bow to gain access to it, make them want the bow and let Searing Shot be a novel, situational spell they get to enjoy along with it. I'm aware that this is trickier than it sounds: those bows can't be added to dungeon or raid loot, as they would be worthless to the overwhelming majority of the playerbase, and they can't be too good either; having devouring plague being a racial spell is polarizing enough as it is, no need to make the pendulum swing in the opposite direction. Luckily, I think that there is an easy way to ensure that Night Elves priests get to actually enjoy their added proficiency without disrupting the game's balance or the loot tables.

 **IMPLEMENTATION**
For leveling priests, a few blue bows with a level-appropriate quantity of int/spirit, maybe some spell damage and healing after level 40, could be sold by the darnassus quartermaster. At higher levels, the same quartermaster could offer two or three little quests that would require the player to turn in a wand that they would want to get anyway (for example, Touch of Chaos) and gain a roughly equivalent bow in exchange. This would also leave some room for experimentation if so desired; you could have for example a bow with an on use effect that changes the damage type of your next starshards, or one that does not consume arrows. They really do not need to be especially good, but simply being able to wield a bow without making your character weaker in the process would go a long way to make that special ability feel useful.

Anyway, just my two cents.

As a fellow Night Elf priest, I actually love Searing Shot and the bow mechanic:

•	No GCD lockout: Shooting bows doesn’t trigger the global cooldown, so I can shoot and immediately cast spells – very smooth in both PvP and PvE.

•	Instant Smite synergy: One of my favorite tricks is shooting sitting players because any hit on a sitting target is a guaranteed crit → which can proc an Instant Smite. It’s such a satisfying combo and feels like a unique “priest thing.”

•	Great vs clothies: Even without attack power gear, I’ve crit up to ~600 with Searing Shot, which hits surprisingly hard on low armor targets.

I agree it could scale better (maybe use spell damage instead of AP and convert the damage type to Holy), but in PvP it already adds fun utility and flavor. I run stamina-heavy bows and use Searing Shot on cooldown.

Even in PvE it’s nice for mana regen with improved wand/bow talents + paladin Seal of Wisdom, letting me regen mana and swap back to healing instantly since there’s no wand GCD lockout.
