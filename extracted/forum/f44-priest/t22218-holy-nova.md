# Holy Nova

## Post 154203 by Menelluin — 2025-10-03T12:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154203#p154203 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I had a quick question about the Holy Nova. As much as I appreciate the developers' idea of ​​creating mechanics to slightly lower the Nova's mana cost, I don't understand another point. The Holy Nova is already a fairly specific skill that can't be used in all circumstances, even on these servers, so why remove what, in my opinion, was its main strength on a standard server? Namely, the fact that it generates absolutely no threat in healing or dps? Was this change motivated by other particularities of the Turtle WoW servers? This spell is weaker and more expensive than its counterpart for the mage. On groups of small enemies it had only one advantage: being able to be spammed immediately with the only constraint being mana. This stabilized the tank's life and melee while momentarily producing very decent damage. Now, I find it almost unusable in instances because even with a "low threat level," I often find myself reverting to tanks' secondary targets as soon as I use the spell as originally intended.
So my question is: "Is there something at high level that I don't yet perceive that motivates this choice? Is there a hidden power that I don't yet perceive in this spell on this server that counteracts this nerf?"
Thanks in advance to anyone who takes the time to shed some light on this. =)

Adding threat to the ability was a bad move. No justification was given, and I'm pretty sure that will stay locked away in the head of the developer that dreamed it up.

For those of us fortunate enough to play smite priest before the change to holy nova, It was fun while it lasted.  Chugging mana pots and mana runes, sporting Blade of eternal darkness, downranking, and smashing big numbers on large bug packs, or other AOE situations without a concern for threat was what made twow smite priest a unique and cool experience.

Smite priest needs to look else-where for identity now. A crit chastise on pull to give your tank some rage and a haste buff to get ahead of the DPS is the biggest thing smite priest has going for it now.
