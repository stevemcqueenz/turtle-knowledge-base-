# My suggestions for possible changes in 1.8.1 for Shadow Priest, if there is still time.

## Post 167122 by Murfo — 2026-02-01T10:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167122#p167122 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

The changes you are proposing for patch 1.8.1 seem very interesting and well thought out to me. However, I would like to contribute a few additional ideas.

In my opinion, I believe that Shadow Priest is still not enough fun to play in PvE gameplay. I am not referring to its DPS numbers, but rather to its damage mechanics. With that in mind, I would like to propose a few ideas to make it more enjoyable:

 **- Vampiric Embrace as a 30-minute self-buff, or at least 10 minutes.**
We already have a very clear role: healing while dealing damage. However, it is very frustrating to lose a lot of DPS time constantly applying this effect to enemies that die within a few seconds. Having to include this ability repeatedly in the rotation becomes tedious.

 **- Shadow Word: Pain and Mind Flay should start with an initial damage tick.**
I am not asking for a DPS increase through this change, as total damage could be adjusted accordingly. However, the feeling of dealing immediate damage instead of waiting 1 to 3 seconds makes a huge difference in gameplay satisfaction.

 **- An AoE ability.**
Once again, it feels frustrating to be almost useless during pulls with multiple small mobs. In my case, all I can really do is shield other casters. But applying a shield every four seconds does not help as much as being able to contribute to DPS.
Being able to cast Holy Nova in Shadowform is an interesting idea, but requiring 10 talent points in otherwise useless talents makes it non-viable.

 **- Finally, increasing the range of Mind Flay to match Mind Blast.**
Mind Flay is our main source of damage, and it is very frustrating during boss fights to constantly reposition just to cast a channeled spell with shorter range.

Thank you very much for reading my suggestions. I hope they can be taken into consideration for future changes.

Kind regards.
