## Post 145223 by Thaladar (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-08-13T21:44:00Z

Night Elf Priests have:

 * Searing Arrow: Critting up to 1k, always crits on sitting targets --> leads to instant smite. 10 sec CD.
 * Bow profficiency: Capable of using bows, that usually have good PVP stats like stamina + can be used when chasing targets
 * Starshards: 2k channel DMG over 6 seconds, great for LOSing targets and they usually underestimate the massive dmg it does nowadays
 * Shadowmeld: sure being able to stoneform Viper is good, but being able to pull off clutch drink + SMELD during a big fight is even better I'd say.

I def think Nelf Priest is the PVP race to go
