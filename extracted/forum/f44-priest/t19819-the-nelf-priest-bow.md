First and for all anyone complaining about the bow on night elf priest has a lower IQ score than the average canine.

Bow advantage:
 **UPTIME** you will be able to shoot your bow WAY more often than your wand
Smoothness from going to casting to shooting as it does not trigger the GCD. I must insist and say how much life changer it is from going to shooting to healing!
Movement, between shoot without having to "recast" shooting is incredible in group play
Damage and Crit and Hit rate can be increased with scopes, world buffs and any buffs, arrows ...
Speed can be increased!

I assure you going back to wanding just feel wrong, clunky and slow.
I believe strongly that just for the uptime of shooting over wanding the damage is superior most people out there will talk about the dps on a target dummy and forget about reality which is in a dungeon you won't be standing still fo X hours playing with your little thing.

Yes it is not a play style for solo like wand can be. But as a priest dungeoneer it is your bread and butter from now on.
