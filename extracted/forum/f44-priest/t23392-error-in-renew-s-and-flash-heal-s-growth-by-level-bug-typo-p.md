# Error in Renew's and flash heal's growth by level? (Bug/typo, please buff/fix)

## Post 163754 by Lunattic13 — 2025-12-25T21:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163754#p163754 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

TLDR: The rank 5 of renew, gained at level 32, is very weak for the level you get it at and disproportionally weak compared to the gain of previous ranks over the last one. The same is true for rank 3 flash heal over rank 2. I am fairly sure both are supposed to be significantly stronger and dread that priest healing is lower than intended because they essentially miss a step of power increase in their spell rank progression.

In-depth: Rank 4 renew heals for 245 at 140 mana cost, while rank 5 heals for 270 at 170 mana cost. This is not a typo; for 30 more mana six or eigth levels after the previous rank, it heals for only 25 more. The spell essentially becomes much more mana inefficient for barely any effect on the numbers. I am fairly sure the healing value is supposed to be around 350-370 as each rank of renew before this one gains at least 50% base power in healing over the last one. Is this a typo, where the value was supposed to be 370 but someone put 270 by mistake?

For the sake of comparison:

Rank 1 renew heals for 45 for 30 mana.
Rank 2 heals for 100 for 65 mana
Rank 3 heals for 175 for 105 mana.
Rank 4 heals for 245 for 140 mana.
Rank 5 heals for 270 for 175 mana.

The same is true for Flash heal:

Rank 1 flash heal heals 202 to 247 for 125 mana.
Rank 2 flash heal heals 269 to 325 for 155 mana.
Rank 3 flash heal heals 278 to 334 for 185 mana.

Looking at this progression, it is very clear that something is wrong with both rank 5 renew and rank 3 flash heal. As stated above, unlike all the other ones, they barely gains in base power over its predecessor. Please fix this and future ranks to bring in in line with intended progression?

Addendum:
To add to this, let's add the other priest instant cast 'heal' as well, power word: shield:

Rank 1: shields for 48 for 45 mana.
Rank 2: shields for 94 for 80 mana.
Rank 3: shields for 166 for 130 mana.
Rank 4: shields for 244 for 175 mana.
Rank 5: Shields for 305 for 210 mana.

Again, unlike rank 5 renew or rank 3 flash heal, this other instant cast spell seems to continue scaling somewhat linearly from rank 4 to 5. I don't see why the priest hot's increase by level is so weak compared to that of power word: shield and assume this is more proof of a typo of sorts being made when programming the healing amount of rank 5 renew or rank 3 flash heal.

This is an odd progression indeed, but it's a deliberate change on Turtle made with CC2 from the [1.17.2 patch](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102970#p102970).
> **Dragunovi wrote: Sat Oct 12, 2024 6:28 pm**
> The base healing values for ranks above level 30 and Spell Power scaling for all ranks have been reduced by 15% for the following spells: Lesser Heal, Renew, Heal, Flash Heal, Prayer of Healing, Greater Heal, Desperate Prayer and Lightwell.
>
>  *Developer Comment: This change has been done alongside a buff to Spiritual Healing, the introduction of Swift Recovery and updates to Lightwell making it an useful part of a healer’s toolkit. Most players have felt that the majority of the healing talents haven’t felt important enough for most healers which caused them to go deeper into Discipline instead of Holy. The healing output should be about the same as before if not higher when Holy is talented deeper.*
>
>  **Spiritual Healing**: Healing increase has been increased from 2/4/6/8/10% to 6/12/18/24/30%
>
>  *Developer Comment: This change has been done to compensate for the baseline healing nerfs.*
