Any lore that specifies that Zuluhed was a shaman or returned to Draenor after the Second War came after vanilla released - main sources are the Tides of Darkness novel and TBC itself.

Turtle is sticking only to what was canon up to vanilla, meaning they are free to change anything that came later, including this.

Incidentally, this is also the reason why Kilrogg and presumably Kargath are still on Azeroth - original lore never had the return to Draenor.

No, Kilrogg definitely went back to Draenor. That is Warcraft II lore which should be canon for any version of WoW (including TWoW). From the WC2 manual:
> When the darkness lifted, the portal was visible once again. It illuminated the ruins as it always had, but the eerie light seemed to play tricks with the shadows. It was only upon closer inspection that the trampled mud that marked the passing of the Bleeding Hollow clan from Azeroth was found to be more than mere illusion.
>  The Bleeding Hollow crossed over into Draenor and rejoined the "old clans" that had never seen the fields of Azeroth.

Warcraft III's backstory mentions them returning back to Azeroth just as Draenor blows up. From the WC3 manual:
> As Turalyon's forces fought desperately to return home to Azeroth, the world of Draenor began to buckle in upon itself. Grom Hellscream and Kilrogg Deadeye, realizing that Ner'zhul's mad plans would doom their entire race, rallied the remaining orcs and escaped back to the relative safety of Azeroth.

As far as I understand it, this return to Azeroth is the part that Blizzard eventually retconned away to have Kilrogg die in Outland.
