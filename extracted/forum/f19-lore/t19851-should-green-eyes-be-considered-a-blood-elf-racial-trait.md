# Should green eyes be considered a blood elf racial trait?

## Post 135560 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-13T07:14:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135560#p135560 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We can confirm that in original Warcraft III and WoW games (before TBC got on media), all elven characters, including Malfurion, Kael'thas and blood elves in Sun Rock Retreat and Azshara have glowing white/yellowish eyes and in games prior to WoW, elves' eyes are monochrome without visible pupils. It was only since the release of TBC, blood elves have been depicted with green eyes.

If we agree that Turtle WoW sticks on old lore and games, "blood elves with evil green eyes" shouldn't be taken as a stereotype again. In other words, blood elf green eyes can exist but only on a proportion of blood elves as a random feature.
