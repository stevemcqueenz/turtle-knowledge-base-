# First, Second, Third War (Human Lore)

## Post 141022 by Kyern — 2025-07-23T18:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141022#p141022 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is a shortened version of the lore, but meant to summarize the conflict.

 **The First War**

Long before the First War, humans flourished in the kingdom of Arathor and later split into multiple kingdoms, the strongest of which was Stormwind. This peaceful realm in the south of the Eastern Kingdoms was known for its fertile lands, noble lords, and brave knights. Stormwind’s people were devout, many following the teachings of the Church of the Holy Light, living under the just rule of King Llane Wrynn and protected by champions like Sir Anduin Lothar.

The First War began when the Dark Portal opened between Azeroth and the shattered world of Draenor. The orcs, manipulated by the warlock Gul’dan and corrupted by demonic blood, poured through the portal in search of conquest. At first, the humans of Stormwind underestimated the threat. Orcs were brutal, savage, and unfamiliar. The king believed they could be repelled like any other barbarian force.

But this enemy was different. Orcs waged war with overwhelming ferocity, burning villages and slaying indiscriminately. Anduin Lothar, the last descendant of the ancient Arathi bloodline, rallied Stormwind’s knights and mages to resist. The war dragged on for years, and although human forces fought valiantly, they were overwhelmed. The orcs formed the Horde, unified and ruthless. Eventually, Stormwind City fell. King Llane was assassinated, betrayed by the corrupted Guardian Medivh and his pawn, Garona Halforcen.

With Stormwind in ruins, Lothar led a mass exodus north to Lordaeron, bearing news of the Horde’s invasion and the need for unity among the human kingdoms. The First War ended in tragedy for the people of Stormwind, but their spirit remained unbroken. Their burning homeland became the rallying cry for what came next.

 -------------

 **The Second War**

Lordaeron’s King Terenas Menethil II answered Lothar’s plea and helped form the Alliance of Lordaeron, a coalition of human nations including Dalaran, Kul Tiras, Stromgarde, and Gilneas, joined by the dwarves of Ironforge and the high elves of Quel’Thalas. Under Lothar’s command, the Alliance prepared for war.

The Horde, now reinforced with ogres and death knights, swept northward. They clashed with the Alliance in brutal battles across Hillsbrad, Alterac, and the Hinterlands. The war was vast, with siege engines, naval warfare, and dark magic all in play. The city of Dalaran stood as a beacon of arcane power, while Gilneas and Alterac played more selfish games, protecting their own borders or, in the case of Alterac, outright betraying the Alliance.

Stormwind’s survivors fought with unmatched resolve. Lothar, leading from the front, embodied the might and nobility of humanity. Together with heroes like Turalyon, Khadgar, and Uther, the Alliance slowly pushed the Horde back. Lothar fell in the climactic Battle of Blackrock Spire, slain by the orc Warchief Orgrim Doomhammer, but his death inspired a final surge. Turalyon took up Lothar’s blade and led the charge that shattered the Horde’s backbone.

The Second War ended with the defeat of the Horde and the capture of many orcs, who were placed in internment camps. Khadgar and others sealed the Dark Portal, believing the threat to be over. Stormwind was later rebuilt with aid from Lordaeron, and its people returned home, stronger and wiser than before.

 -------------------

 **The Third War**

Years of peace followed, but a darker storm was brewing. The orcs, now free from demonic influence, sought redemption. Meanwhile, a more insidious threat arose in the frozen north. The Lich King, a being of undeath created by the demon lord Kil’jaeden, spread a plague across Lordaeron.

The Scourge, a mindless army of the dead, began sweeping through the kingdoms. In Lordaeron, Prince Arthas Menethil took up the fight, but his desire to stop the plague led him down a dark path. He ordered the culling of Stratholme, slaughtering infected innocents. When that wasn’t enough, he journeyed to Northrend, took up the cursed blade Frostmourne, and fell into damnation.

Arthas returned not as a hero, but as a death knight of the Scourge. He murdered his own father, King Terenas, and brought ruin to Lordaeron. Cities burned, towns fell, and the dead rose to serve the Lich King. The kingdom of Dalaran was obliterated by Archimonde, a demon summoned through the betrayal of the Burning Legion’s agents.

In the chaos, Stormwind stood as one of the last bastions of human civilization. King Varian Wrynn ruled with strength, though political instability and betrayal still plagued the court. Refugees from Lordaeron poured into Stormwind. The Scarlet Crusade, once noble paladins of the Light, turned into zealous inquisitors, hunting the undead with merciless fury and often killing the innocent.
