I agree, I think that Quel'Lithien lodge in Eastern Plaguelands would be a better choice. The Farstrider Lodge in Loch Modan already a questing hub so you have levels 15-18 running around mixing with new High Elves and it's so far away from Silvermoon, it also often bothered me.

Quel'Lithien is right next to the portal to Ghostlands-to-be, makes sense it would be a first stop running away from Arthas. It's also very close to Alah'thalas, so if you imagine High Elves in the Quel'Lithien Lodge sending out scouts to seek a good location to establish a safe haven, they'd find Alah'thalas quite fast and easily.

The only drawback is, of course, EPL is a 55+ zone. However, it feels quite appropriate that, as a new HE refugee, you can see Scourge and it being far FAR stronger than you - establishes a sense of dread and despair that's simply non-existent in Loch Modan.

Quel'Lithien is also used for a single (?) Horde quest, but I don't think it would be a huge problem to reroute it, so that questing Hordies don't run into new High Elves.
The EPL lodge is also very easily accessible to Horde, sure, but with a few -highly- powerful ranger guards, any ganking ought to be easily prevented. Or some sort of a magical barrier that prevents entry of Horde characters.

I'm certain that Turtle Devs considered every single Lodge as a starting zone for High Elves and their decision won't change, it's just fun coming up with alternatives...

As far as I'm concerned, staying in Quel'Lithien only makes sense if their caravans absolutely couldn't leave. The very ground there is poisoned, plagued entities- both undead and otherwise -roam everywhere, and as the cherry ontop anyone who dies is at serious risk of reanimating and turning against the living, which is the last place you want a bunch of civilians in, as they are PRESUMABLY subpar fighters even in a pinch. With all those civilians, they had to get out as quickly as they could while the remaining rangers and other military personnel were the ones staying behind, presumably to survey the situation in their homeland and as a stop and sanctuary for those moving to or from Quel'thalas.

As for Andorhal, the refugees presumably didn't go through it, but rather through Plaguewind Ravine leading into the Hinterlands. The better question is why they didn't stay in Quel'Danil Lodge or Aerie Peak, which I can only assume is because they wanted even greater distance from The Scourge, which aren't that far away from The Hinterlands all things considered.

How they got past Dun Modr is also a good question, and my speculation there is that the place wasn't annexed by the Dark Iron Dwarves yet; but at this point I'm making wild guesses. Plomping them down in The Farstrider Lodge works in my view, but with the raised eyebrow at Hawk's Rest and Menethil Harbor which seems more fortified, closer in distance and more equipped to handle a refugee influx compared to some distant and understaffed lodge.

The only reason I see to go for the lodge is that it's manned by High Elves already, giving them more autonomy of themselves and maybe more time to handle the diplomatic situation with the Alliance after what happened with Kael'thas and Garithos. Again, I'm making wild guesses here.
