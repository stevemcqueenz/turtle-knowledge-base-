## Post 79406 by Galendor (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-11-30T15:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79406#p79406 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Indeed, before the end of the Third War, both Cenarion Circle and Sisterhood of Elune had strict gender division. Women couldn't become druids and men couldn't become priests - or, at least, be official members of these organizations. Such division was mentioned both in WC3 manuals and Warcraft Encyclopedia.
But warriors/hunters... It's a bit more tricky. I believe nobody would stop a night elf man from learning martial arts or hunting. Of course, there should be combatants among elven men, but they just weren't a part of Sentinel army. Remember that at first Sentinels, like Watchers, came from the Sisterhood so no wonder they were all females.
But anyway, "all druids were men" doesn't mean "all men were druids". There were combatants, smithes, fishers and tailors of any gender.
