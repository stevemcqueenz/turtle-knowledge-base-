In General - most races formed through thousands of years of transformation after the Curse of the Flesh, infected by the Old Gods, which, I hope you've heard about, a lot of races were actually very hard changed into a much lesser beings of what they originally were.

Mechagnomes lost all their immunities and abilities to instantle repair themselves since the Curse taken their bodies from metal to Flesh and bones.

Dwarves, as you've already could know, suffered a similar misfortune.

Humans are very distant descendants of Vry'kul race. Curse of Flesh made'em much more less resistant to magical effects but also granted them some sort of abilitie to amplify it's use.

Regarding the Night Elves is not everything that so simple - sorta troll-kin, yes, more evolution and mutations (perhaps Elune herself had her hands dirty with it, not known for sure) involved apparently. Surely the magical energies of the Well of Eternity has it's toll on these. High elves and lesser ones further down the line (Blood Elves later on) are the living proof of that.

Aliens - orcs, ogres, demons and undead. These are all outlandish. Undead, however, were there much more longer before the first Dark Portal opened.
