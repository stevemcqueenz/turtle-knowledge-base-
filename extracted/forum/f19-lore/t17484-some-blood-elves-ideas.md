# Some Blood Elves ideas

## Post 119325 by Dracarusggotham (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2025-02-08T15:18:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119325#p119325 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ok, I know what you're going to say and no, I don't want playable Blood Elves in the Horde.

Here's my idea:
Remember what Blizzard did starting with Lich King by adding certain unplayable High Elf factions in the Alliance, as well as the Silver Covenant and such?

Well, maybe something similar could be done here by adding certain smaller unplayable Blood Elf NPC's in the Horde.

It occurred to me when I saw Braelyn Firehand in Sunrock Retreat and thought, why not add some Horde-affiliated Blood Elves with some lore that aligns with them being there just for convenience and for their own purposes?

For example, I'm going to use some Blood Elves that where added in TBC and they are clearly upset by working with the Horde:

Lorrin Foxfire: Affiliated with the Horde in Stonard, he's looking to seize the sorcery items of the Atal'ai Trolls in the Sunken Temple to study the type of magic they use and harness it to the Blood Elves' advantage.

Cersei Dusksinger: Studies the Fel Magic of the area to use it to her advantage as a Warlock, testing its effects on the Lost in the area.

Pretty bland, but if it were to be developed we would have some Blood Elf lore with the Horde, showing their true intentions without becoming fully affiliated with them.

Taking advantage of the fact that the Alliance would not accept their studies as such and that the Horde would simply accept them for convenience without becoming playable.
Something like those who are scattered around the world and are neutral. (They could also be in other areas and simply be neutral NPCs)

Edit for those who don't understand: NO, I DON'T MEAN BLOOD ELVES ON THE HORDE, I MEAN BLOOD ELVES ACCEPTING HELP FROM THE HORDE.
