# Campain of Sunnyglade / First War

## Post 1743 by Paw (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2019-01-01T20:35:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1743#p1743 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://gamepedia.cursecdn.com/wowpedia/thumb/b/b1/WarCraft-Orcs%26Humans-Humans-Scenario7-Sunnyglade.png/275px-WarCraft-Orcs%26Humans-Humans-Scenario7-Sunnyglade.png?version=6226f0c457239e53ae4d65a24ae2efd5)

Orcish warlocks used to have a smaller class of casters to support their deed in the First War against the Humans and their Wizards and Clerics. Orcs knew all too well that killing their enemies isn't the only goal.

Blackhand gave the command to learn of what magic and which spells the enemy is using and what does it take to wield such power to make their soldiers to sustain so well against the might of the Horde. Therefore the order was issued. Find and claim the magical Tower on the outskirts of Sunnyglade.

Easier said than done. Region was well protected by the knights of the nearby fortress in Grand Hamlet and before you get any further to that magical tower you will need to decapitate every last knight on that patch of the land.

Order from the Warchief came: The Humans of Sunnyglade have become fat and lazy with their prosperity. The town is like a ripe plum waiting to be plucked. You will march upon their weak Human armies and smash them to pieces. Somewhere in the town is a tower that you must keep intact, so that we may study how their magiks are created. Fail me, and I will have your head on a pike at the gates of Black Rock Spire.
