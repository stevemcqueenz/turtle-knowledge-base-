We have ended up in one possible scenario: azeroth is in fact a giant turtle, probably an ancestor of the wandering isle and others. So if this scenario was true then... where are all the turtles?

Getting answers for that question is a little bit problematic...

[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/nMj4bdxv/e.png)](https://ibb.co/yFqKXvH1)

Is this a pandaren sea turtle? It looks exactly as the wanderer turtles looks like. I recently found it in the swamp of sorrows.

All over the coastlines of azeroth (eastern kingdoms and kalimdor) there are turtle shells and bones of them. They are simply too many to be counted. This is a feature that persist all over the following expansions with certain stability.

I'm afraid wow is telling us a story in which the old gods and the murloc "civilization" is involved in the mass genocide of the turtle species. Edit: thinking some more on the matter... now that we think about it, it's curious just curious the location of the old gods imprisonments of the titans, because if this theory results to be -even- possible their places means a lot. We know yogg'saron dungeon is located in the north, it is also a being involved with the power of the mind, to control and corrupt the mind (get a look to our thread where we talk about the psychic energy and the runecarvers here: [Small talk: a new class in classic?](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138295#p138295)), and the specifical region wouldn't be far from the turtle's head in such scenario. Now silithus is located to the far-southwest of the map, but back in the age of the kal'dorei when the world was not split into pieces, it should've been located not so far from the center as we can see in the images bellow.

All we can say about the original form of the land mass of azeroth is that every community has its own interpretation and sizes, it can be located and relocated in so many ways. However we can conclude two things: in fact yogg'saron lies in a place where its form of power defines so much more than just a plot against mortals over the earth, also the crucial location of y'shaarj at the very center of the map that takes us to the pandaren civilization in which we learn the remnants of y'shaarj blood ressembles corrupt aspects of core emotions, a corruption so deep into the world that its removal broke azeroth apart. The previous idea leads me to our original thread about the kal'dorei true allegiance, here: [viewtopic.php?p=137015#p137015](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137015#p137015), there we build this theory saying "kal'dorei are corrupted and serves the old gods", this is a corruption not only seen in the pink tone of their skin but also in the land but more specifically in the trees, the trees turning their wood into pink/purple color under the influence of shadow energy following the roots to the surface. What if y'shaarj wasn't in a real sha form as we've seen it, but it was possessing a giant world tree at the very center of azeroth? We also know that the true form or shell of the old gods is not real, it's just a form of containment that takes them rooted to their physical titan shackles, far from the emerald dream and other elemental also non elemental plains of azeroth.

The lovecraft myths aren't so bad to be reminded here because they have a heavy relation with the main events for the great spectrum of warcraft lore. One thing that results very useful to put here is the fix "*oth*", this is used to describe the names of the universal horrors of lovecraft myths. So is azeroth, the revamped or retcon name that was later introduced to call the world, however, we know that at the very beginning of this story the world was simply and just called as "kalimdor", telling that the world's land mass separated was kalimdor's, not azeroth's. In retail although this isn't correct anymore.

> **Cosmico wrote: Thu Jul 17, 2025 4:03 am**

on the topic of turtles with carriages, they are likely of Naga origin - <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=4725>
> This particular set has an abandoned carriage attached to the turtle's shell. Perhaps this creature was driven to the shoreline by unknown beings rather than beaching itself as some of the other remains suggested.
>  [...]
>  I have heard of the kinds of carriages found on the back of the turtle you found. Those I do believe are naga carriages, used both in battle and in transportation of their supplies on to land. The markings on the box you found are of the naga; this would explain their invasive presence in far-northern Darkshore.

> **Cosmico wrote: Thu Jul 17, 2025 4:03 am**
> I'm afraid wow is telling us a story in which the old gods and the murloc "civilization" is involved in the mass genocide of the turtle species.

on the topic of giant sea turtles dying - it appears they are native to the waters around Teldrassil and that they likely beached themselves, possibly fleeing Teldrassil's growing corruption. The Murlocs are just scavanging beached corpses, not killing them.

origins as of <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=4727>
> The bones you have are from gentle sea mammals that are known to inhabit the waters surrounding the base of Teldrassil

cause of death as of <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=4681>
> Perhaps then we will be closer to discovering the reason why these unfortunate creatures choose to end their lives beached on the coast of Darkshore.
>  [...]
>  The idea that they might be fleeing the area around Teldrassil is disturbing.
