# Holy magic is only a magic used by religious casters in lore...

## Post 151651 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-09-14T04:21:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151651#p151651 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Induction of my replies in a related thread.

Holy light could be an exclusive entity of faith/worshiping practiced by human. In original Warcraft games, elves and dwarves are more like exotic cultured races with conventional fantasy settings and skills like magic arrows, healing magic and thunder clap, but none of these are refered to as a form of holy light use in those early games.

To headconon it up, holy light can be a literally embodied but also abstract faith, just like "freedom" or "light" in real world. Alternatively, the magic of healing can be considered or defined as using magic to cure wound or diseases, which is unnecessarily categorised as a specific school of magic.

Holy magic in WoW itself is weird. Other than that, the theory of the so-called arcane energy as the original form of all cotegorised magic powers is all too weird to me. I prefer the magic/effect categorisation form in Diablo - lightning, poison, frost and fire. Whether it is evil or holy depends on who wields the power and what the magic is casted with, hence holy and chaotic powers legitimated.

To sum up, my notion/suggestion/speculation about holy light and healing magics is that they are two different types of knowledge. The holy light is only a religious form while priest and paladin's shiny healing/anti-undead spells are practical methods using mana. Or, in other words, there shouldn't be a scientifically holy magic.
