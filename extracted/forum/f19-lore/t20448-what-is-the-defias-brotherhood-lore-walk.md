# What is The Defias Brotherhood? (Lore Walk)

## Post 141123 by Kyern — 2025-07-24T19:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141123#p141123 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Alright, gather around the campfire or the soggy wooden stool behind Goldshire, wherever you like to sit and hear tales that definitely did not come from a bottle of Dwarven Stout. This one is about the Defias Brotherhood. You have probably heard the name. Maybe you’ve been stabbed in Westfall. Maybe your cousin disappeared near the Deadmines. Or maybe, like myself, you’ve killed Edwin VanCleef a few times and he just keeps coming back like a bad rash in red cloth armor.   turtle_tongue

Let me take you on a little story walk.

 **The Birth of a Brotherhood, or How to Accidentally Start a Revolution While Wearing a Mask**

Once upon a time, in the glorious and very bankrupt kingdom of Stormwind, there was a bunch of hardworking stonemasons who rebuilt the city after the First War. These were the people laying bricks, carving lion statues, fixing the plumbing, and building fancy towers for nobles who never said thank you. They worked their butts off. The kingdom looked shiny again. There were balconies. There were fountains. There were secret passageways that no one talks about.

So these stonemasons, led by a handsome genius named Edwin VanCleef, said “Cool. We rebuilt your city. Can we please be paid now”

Stormwind’s nobility responded with a hearty “No”

Just like that. No gold. No thanks. Just silence, a few guard patrols, and a polite “You can leave the city now or get arrested for trespassing”

Naturally, Edwin and his friends were not thrilled

 **From Bricks to Blades**

Edwin VanCleef was not just a stonemason. He was also a former engineer and a rogue, which means he had a particular skillset involving knives and disappearing. So when the nobles told him to shove off, he went out to Westfall and said, “Alright boys, new plan. We take the city back”

And that is how the Defias Brotherhood was born.  hiding_smth_turtle

They took off their tool belts and picked up swords. They put on red masks because nothing says “I am starting a rebellion” like a dramatic fashion statement. They started robbing travelers, burning farms, raiding caravans, and generally ruining the vibe of Westfall.

 **Was it petty? Maybe. Was it justified? Also yes.**

Let’s be honest, if you built a cathedral with your bare hands and the guy who owns it said “You may leave now, peasant,” you would be setting barns on fire too.

 **The Deadmines: One Hell of a Hideout**

Edwin and his merry band of extremely upset masons moved into the Deadmines, a massive network of tunnels and old machinery beneath Westfall. No one really remembers what the mines were originally for. Gnomes probably. But the Defias turned it into an underground fortress with forges, ships, pirates, goblins, and a seemingly endless supply of torches that never burn out.

 **Yes, they are pirates now. No, it does not make sense. Yes, it is still awesome.**

They even built a full-size ship down there. Not a rowboat. Not a canoe. A whole damn ship. Inside a mine. I do not know how they got the lumber or what their long-term plan was, but it looks fantastic and they get points for effort.

 **Who Leads These People**

Still Edwin VanCleef. Big mask. Big attitude. Big sword. He used to build cities. Now he builds chaos. He has a voice that says “I would like to lecture you while you bleed” and he monologues before dying every single time.

He is charismatic. He is clever. He is kind of a jerk. But people follow him because he gave them purpose when the nobles gave them nothing. The Defias are not just bandits. They are wronged bandits with architectural degrees.

And honestly that is more terrifying than orcs.

 **Why They Exist**

The Defias Brotherhood exists because Stormwind sucks at paying its bills. That is it. That is the whole reason. Every noble in the castle is sitting on a golden chair made by someone who now has to rob farmers to buy bread.

The Brotherhood wants revenge. They want justice. They want Stormwind to fall apart, preferably while they are laughing from a pirate ship inside a mine. Also, they just enjoy stabbing people. It is therapeutic. So are they in the wrong? Probably not. Long live the Defias! *Wears a mask slowly.*   smiling_turtle_head

Let's dig deeper then, shall we?

You are absolutely right. The Defias Brotherhood was never just a band of angry unpaid contractors. That is the surface story. Dig a little deeper and you find a slow-burning political tragedy engineered by one of the most cunning and malevolent forces in Stormwind's history.

The Stonemason's Guild was hired to rebuild Stormwind after it was destroyed in the First War. Under the leadership of Edwin VanCleef, these were not just masons swinging hammers. They were artisans, engineers, architects, and loyal veterans who helped piece the kingdom back together. They poured years into restoring Stormwind's glory. Towers, walls, fountains, even the majestic throne room. They built it all.

And then they were betrayed.
The nobles refused to pay. Whether that was out of financial desperation or deliberate sabotage depends on how deep you want to go. Either way, when payment came due, they turned their backs. Some records suggest they feared the power the Guild had amassed. Others simply claim they were broke. The truth likely lies in both.

King Varian Wrynn did try to intervene. He saw the injustice. But Stormwind’s coffers were empty from decades of war. Resources were drained. Tensions were high. And all the while, lurking in the shadows, was a creature with an agenda.

 **Lady Katrana Prestor.** *DUN DUN DUNNNNNNN*  wary_turtle_head   crying_turtle

To the court, she was a noblewoman. A trusted advisor. In truth, she was **Onyxia**, the black dragon in human disguise. Daughter of Deathwing. Mistress of manipulation. And she played both sides like a fiddle. She fueled the conflict between the nobles and the Stonemasons. She whispered in ears. She stoked the fire of resentment. And when chaos finally erupted, she stepped back and let the city burn emotionally, not physically.

The riot that broke out between the Stonemasons and the royal guard resulted in the death of Queen Tiffin Wrynn. This was not just a personal tragedy. It was a masterstroke. King Varian, consumed with grief, became politically unstable. He distanced himself from court. Power vacuum. **Enter Onyxia.**

With Varian withdrawing from leadership, Onyxia tightened her grip on Stormwind. She controlled the nobles. She influenced Bolvar. She orchestrated discontent in the city while keeping herself above suspicion. All while manipulating the rise of the Defias Brotherhood, letting them grow unchecked in Westfall as a convenient scapegoat and destabilizing force.

Edwin VanCleef, now an outlaw, took the betrayal personally. The man who built Stormwind was now leading a rebellion against it. He rallied other victims of noble corruption. Outcast mages, disenfranchised craftsmen, rogue elements from the army. He built a network. He hid it under the very land he once helped build.

The Deadmines became the heart of his movement. There, he and his followers constructed a full ship underground, ready to sail when the time came to strike. The Brotherhood was not just a gang. It was an organized insurgency. A revolutionary force that believed they were fighting a just cause. And in many ways, they were.

But Onyxia had no intention of letting VanCleef win. She kept him close enough to scare the nobles, but not strong enough to overthrow them. She let the Brotherhood fester just long enough to ensure no one trusted anyone. Her goal was not conquest through armies. It was rot through chaos. That is how the Black Dragonflight operates. Not with fire, but with division.

Onyxia was the puppet master. The Brotherhood was the blade she let twist. And the people of Stormwind paid the price. Until a group of random adventurers came along and said, enough. (*Cough Kyern Cough Cough.*) VanCleef was not a villain. He was the symptom of a kingdom poisoned from within. And Onyxia? She was the poison. Hidden in plain sight. Until the mask came off.

That's the story of that, until of course the Cataclysm rumor has that Vancleef had a daughter-but that's a topic for another forum.   hiding_smth_turtle
