Song of the Horde -

The first song is the Song of Earth, the slow heartbeat beneath your feet. It tells us that no warrior stands alone, that the soil remembers every step and every fall.

The second is the Song of Fire, the spark of courage in the chest of every fighter who dares to rise again. I was born of that fire, and so were you.

The third is the Song of Wind, the voice of freedom. It howls through the Barrens, it roars through our forges, it carries the names of our heroes to the next generation.

And the fourth… the Song of Water. That one reminds us to endure. Rivers carve canyons not with strength, but with patience. It whispers that the Horde will outlast any storm, because it knows how to bend and flow.”
