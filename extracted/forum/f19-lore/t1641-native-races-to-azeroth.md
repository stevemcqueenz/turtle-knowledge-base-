Yes, youre correct. Among the original eight playable races in World of Warcraft, the Tauren and Trolls are the only ones that are considered to be native to Azeroth. The Tauren are a race of nomadic, shamanistic tribes that have lived on the grassy plains of central Kalimdor for thousands of years, while the Trolls have a long and storied history on the continent, with powerful empires and kingdoms stretching back for millennia.

As you mentioned, the other playable races have origins that are either tied to other worlds (such as the Orcs and Draenor), or are the result of magical or scientific experimentation (such as the Dwarves, Gnomes, and Humans). The Night Elves, while technically mutated from the Trolls by exposure to the Well of Eternity, have still been a part of Azeroth's history for a very long time.

It's worth noting that there are other races on Azeroth that are native to the world, but are not playable as characters. For example, the *Furbolgs*, *Centaurs*, and *Quilboar* are all native to the continent of Kalimdor, and have their own unique cultures and histories.
