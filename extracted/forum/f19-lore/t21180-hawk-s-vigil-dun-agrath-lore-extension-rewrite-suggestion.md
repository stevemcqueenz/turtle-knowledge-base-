# Hawk's Vigil & Dun Agrath - Lore Extension/Rewrite Suggestion

## Post 147080 by Armordillo — 2025-08-19T20:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147080#p147080 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I believe more can be done with the Turtle subzones of Dun Agrath and Hawk's Vigil in fleshing out the lore of Azeroth in a more meaningful and significant way. While they are not bad, they don't really stand as well as other additions like the "Harvest Golem Mystery" questline  and the "Shady Rest" continuation which in my opinion helps build the world's history and narrative.

It is with these examples in mind I propose that the lore and quests of subzones Dun Agrath and Hawk's Vigil to be redesigned into
- Creating an origin story of Twilight's Hammer spread beyond the clan in the Eastern Kingdoms

- Giving Krasus a presence in the game world instead of only being in novels

- Fleshing out some events in the Second War regarding some of the Horde's leaders

- Giving more weigh to the impact of Twilight influence in politics

- Establishing a potential avenue of the Undercity Chapter of the cult having designs for the Shadow Well
The summaries below are my own thoughts and rough ideas on how the existing stories can be reworked into this new narrative.

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 **Hawk's Vigil**
A settlement of Lordaeronian refugees that fled during the Third War, they were originally led by Archmage Krasus of Dalaran. Quickly returning from Quel'Thalas after assessing the Sunwell's remains, Krasus found himself too late to protect his second home of Dalaran from the Legion's destruction. As the chaos of the Third War raged on, the secretive dragon-mage soon found himself shepherding many refugees to safety across the land.

While the Scourge and the Legion are the most formidable threats of their time now, Krasus knows that other forces would seek to use this distraction of Azeroth's guardians to make plays of their own. To this end, one group of refugees in particular was led across into Khaz Modan after managing to secure promises of protection and settlement rights from the Senate of Ironforge.

Brought deep into the mountains overlooking the Wetlands, the dispirited and despairing refugees were instructed by the Archmage to make new homes here and with the aid of his mysterious magic, breathed new Life into the marshy grove. To the amazement of his followers, the leaves of the trees turned into warm shades of red as all manner of herbs and berries sprouted from the invigorated foliage.

"This land is now capable of supporting new lives, a new future. Take care of your new home and forevermore will you be fed and sheltered by it."

After ensuring the safety and stability of the budding village the Archmage returned to his duties in Dalaran, leaving in his stead several trusted agents versed in the arcane. Tasked not only to watch over the settlers and assist the new leadership when needed, they are to maintain a quiet vigil over the distant Grim Batol as well as be his eyes and ears in the region as the village deepens its relations with their new dwarven neighbors, chief amongst them being Dun Agrath.

 *"Redwood and Firecherries are some of the exports of our little hamlet. If it weren't for the gifts of Master Krasus, Light knows how we would survive out here - let alone live." - A grateful survivor.*

 *"My mommy said that I should wear more thicker clothes now that we're here. But I don't feel cold at all when I'm near the trees though..." - A curious child*

 *"We must never forget those who lost their lives to make this miracle happen. If only she could see us now..." - Denalah Silverpoint*

 **Dun Agrath**
A sleepy dwarven town that serves as a breadbasket of Khaz Modan, the cozy exterior of this mountain village masks  festering scars. During the Second War the Horde were unable to breach the subterranean strongholds of Ironforge and Gnomeregan, leading Orgrim Doomhammer to station the Bleeding Hollow and appointing Kilrogg Deadeye as leader of operations in Khaz Modan. Mistrusting the Twilight's Hammer clan's loyalties and sanity, Doomhamer have also ordered the mad clan to mostly remain in Khaz Modan too, where they will be under the watchful eye of Kilrogg and largely separated from their Stormreaver allies who will be following to Lordaeron.

This course of action was fine by Cho'gall who had his own agenda. Under the pretext of studying for ways to breach the enemies' homes, Cho'gall plundered the many abandoned dwarven archives across the land, studying their histories, legends and myths in the search for forbidden knowledge regarding the End Times. During his inspection tours around Khaz Modan, Cho'gall's attuned senses felt a familiar presence beckoning him from the marshy Wetlands.

Taking this to be a sign of the presence of the Old Ones or their servants, Cho'gall made the captured town of Dun Agrath his base of operations as Dun Modr was under control of the Black Tooth Grin and their remote location within the mountains make for good protection against Kilrogg's spies. Under their control, Dun Argath became host to untold numbers of blood rituals and torture as Cho'gall continued his research into the Old Ones. Many dwarves unfortunate enough to be caught by the cult were subjected to unimaginable horror and cruelty that slowly began to strip their minds of sanity until the whispers of dark beings could be heard.

When Dun Agrath was liberated during the Alliance's counterattack, mass graves of sacrificial victims were found dug in the crop fields as surviving prisoners were found chanting strange languages within their cages. Though they were treated and eventually declared well again, some of these dwarves, having their minds peeled open to cosmic terrors, eventually became the founding members of the Khaz Modan chapter of the now-international Twilight's Hammer, whose membership now goes beyond racial and national lines.

While Dun Agrath maintains its facade of normalcy, deathly rituals and whispered prayers chant quietly behind cellar doors and hidden caves, twisting both shadows and minds eastward towards a god whose name was long forgotten. While much of the town is now under the influence of the cult, they have unexpectedly found themselves in check by the recently established settlement of Hawk's Vigil, whose people are armed and their leaders increasingly suspicious of their intentions. Worse still, the presence of a red dragon, a hated foe whose name they detest, can be felt emanating from the very center of their home.

But there is nothing to fear. Help has arrived from beyond the sea and all that would remain of this threat would shadow and ash...

 *"It's a peaceful enough post but the folks here are colder than the peaks of Coldridge. Every night I rather take the long walk to the Vigil for a drink where the beds are warm and the ladies warmer! -  Captain Snowbeard"*

 *"Do not like the look of some of the locals, especially when they seem to be noting our defenses. I lost my home to a cult once before and I'll be damned if I fail again!" - Commander Ryke*

 *"Master Krasus, you were right. This area is crawling with cultists. Ryke has been informed of the situation. I will inform you if I hear anything more about this Ashbuckle character." - Marge Blackwood*

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 **Questline Antagonist - Harlus Ashbuckle**
Born Martin Corinth to a couple of farmers in the Tirisfal Uplands, his first experience with the Old Ones was the day when he decided to explore an ancient ruin nestled deep in the southern edge. Losing his footing, he fell deep into the waters of the Shadow Well where his mind was assailed by maddening visions of fire and ruin, of a day where all would be made into ash. As he emerged from the black waters baptized with visions of destruction, he eventually joined the newly-established chapter of the Twilight's Hammer in Lordaeron City where he committed many acts of evil and depravity in the name of his new gods.

As he killed, his powers grew. But eventually Justice got hold of him as he was found guilty for the arson of the Abbey of Saint Mara where dozens of clergy, nuns and even children were burned alive as both an offering to the Lords of Fire and for their constant interference with Dark Iron plans. Initially sentenced to death in the Stockades, he was eventually released due to his connections to nobles such as Duke Ramon and Lord Lescovar who themselves are members of the Twilight's Hammer.

Ashbuckle was released as the cult had need of his zeal and shadowflame powers once again. Their stronghold of Dun Agrath is being threatened not by a hawk, but a Dragonhawk rather. It is his mission to burn that village to the ground, to bring Ruin to Life, a task he awaits with great anticipation.

This is a rough general layout of Hawk's Vigil I quickly put together in regards to the above story.

The current Vigil I feel is too strange, like a piece of Elwynn was grafted into the Wetlands. I however do like the idea of the Vigil feeling 'different' from the rest of Khaz Modan but still believable to exist within the Dwarven continent. To that end I took inspiration from the Ruby Dragonshrine and Vermillion Redoubt.

Dirt, Road and Rock tileset is Wetlands as I figured no amount of magic Krasus breathes out will change that. Grass is from Loch Modan.

Trees from Azshara were chosen as they were the color of flames and had pine trees as Dun Morogh and Loch Modan have many pines too.

Northrend-Human structures were chosen as they emphasize on more wood than stone used. Hawk's Vigil in both the original and this revised idea emphasizes on their lumber industry.

Current progress on trying to flesh out this idea. Also noticed that the area in Turtle's current subzone still have unused space surrounding their Vigil. So I tore down part of the top-left mountain to create a pass connecting Dun Algaz to the rest of this subzone I currently term as the "Deep Wells". Will eventually add two new passageways at the bottom connecting it to the slopes near Angerfang-Whelgar and Black Channel Marsh.

 ***Hawk's Vigil:***
![Image](https://images4.imagebam.com/68/02/2a/ME1AD3PY_o.png)

 ***Expanded Map:***
![Image](https://images4.imagebam.com/70/1a/ba/ME1AD4D0_o.png)

Hawk's Vigil is moved further eastward, allowing the mage tower to have good view of Grim Batol at the same time. If Dun Agrath is to be made a hostile area, a friendly dwarven waystation is made at the bottom-right to make up for it. The marshes in the center are hostile with the lower level populated by undead and the top level populated with bog beasts and oozes.

The Algaz pass would be hostile and Dragonmaw-themed. Dun Agrath is also hostile and Twilight cult-themed. The passageway connecting the Vigil with the lower marsh levels is friendly.

Also came up with several new lore ideas to build up the area and themes, such as the Dragonmaw being forced into an alliance of convenience with the Twilight cult for a project centered on reanimating dragon bones brought from Grim Batol's outskirts deep within the Dragonmaw-Twilight-controlled caverns of Dun Algaz. Their goal is to rebuild a new fleet of dragonriders since it'll be easier to control an undead with necromancy than to subjugate the current red flight.

It would climax in having to enter the caverns (probably a dungeon), eventually ascending to the open-air platform at the top of Algaz mountain where the party battles the Dragonmaw/Twilight necromancer leader and his newly-risen shadowflame wyrm.
