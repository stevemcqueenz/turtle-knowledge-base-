# The race of elf is physically more like human and dwarf

## Post 171374 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-03-20T09:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171374#p171374 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

First of all, I'm not completely against the hypothesis of night elves being descendants of trolls. But one of the most irrational "proofs" for supporting that theory is "elves and trolls have many similarities, such as the pointy long ears". I list some of disproofs as below, which I have posted twice as a reply.

> Dark trolls in WCIII look alike with other trolls rather with than any elf kind we know in game or lore. Around night elven terrotories there are also harpies who to some extent look like night elves. Humans and all known elven races look more the same than trolls and elves (five-finger hands and human-like faces), don't they? No double standard. They put dark trolls beside the World Tree for a reason, which is merely that they made troll races scattered in the world for making them good farmed targets, just like those furbolgs around same locations. Undermining the lore of the base set in early games has been what Blizzard did and do through 20 years. Blizzard didn't just add contents; they modified too much.

Comparisons with night elf as a standard, including no races distorted by unnatural powers like naga:
Night elf - five-finger hands and feet, human-like face, pointy ears, naturally glowing eyes with obscure pupils, human-like shape, grey/blue/violet/purple complexion.

High elf - five-finger hands and feet, human-like face, pointy ears, naturally glowing eyes with obscure pupils, human-like build/shape, human-like complexion.
Heavy resemblance to night elf.

Harpy - body with wings and talons, human-like face, pointy ears, naturally glowing eyes with obscure pupils.
Evident resemblance to night elf.

Human - five-finger hands and feet, human-like face, human-like build/shape, round ears, human-like complexion.
Evident resemblance to night elf.

Dwarf - five-finger hands and feet, human-like face, round ears, different build/shape, human-like complexion.
Weak resemblance to night elf.

Gnome - four-finger hands and feet, human-like face, round ears, different build/shape, human-like complexion.
Weak resmblance to night elf.

Troll - three-finger hands, two-toe feets, pointy ears different build/shape (trolls in Warcraft II and WoW), likewise night elven complexion.
Weak resemblance to night elf.

Orc - five-finger hands and feet, pointy ears, non-human-like face, different build/shape, non-human complexion (pre-TBC content)
Puny resemblance to night elf.

Goblin - four-finger hands and feet, non-human-like face, different build/shape, green complexion, pointy ears.
Puny resemblance to night elf.

To conclude, I compare night elf with several other races in order to tell the fact that at least in physical form, night elves are more like human than like troll. If any troll races/tribes are considered to be related to the evolvement of night elf due to their physical resemblance, let us take this same standard - human and dwarf are two non-elven races that bear biggest physical similarities to night elf. Even goblins and orcs have pointed ears. Steoreotypically, night elves are slender and tall and trolls also have long limbs, but in fact night elf body shape is more like the human shape. Saying that night elf is another human-like race would be safer than to assume they descend from ancient trolls.
