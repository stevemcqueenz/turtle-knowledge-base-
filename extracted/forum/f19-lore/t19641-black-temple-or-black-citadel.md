No, actually. [Hellfire Citadel existed as far back as Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal.](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/b/be/Draenor_map.jpg?6e8b3e) The game just never actually *takes you there* for any of the campaign missions on either the Alliance or Horde sides.

The Black Citadel could have been intended in Warcraft III to be Hellfire Citadel, now inhabited by Magtheridon (which would be consistent with him being trapped there in TBC; however, it then becomes inconsistent again with the Black Temple being retconned into being all the way in Shadowmoon Valley yet Magtheridon inexplicably being in Hellfire Peninsula), but truly we have very little information on what the Black Citadel's relation to Hellfire Citadel was, if any, and [the map really isn't much help at all](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/5/50/Outlandtft.jpg?5fdfa5&format=original). They could've been the same structure under two different names, or two separate citadels in Hellfire Peninsula.

We do have [this art of the Black Citadel](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/b/b8/Black_Citadel_art.jpg?9901ee&format=original) from the Art of World of Warcraft book (published 2004) since the Black Citadel [was intended to be a raid early in WoW's development](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/images/b/b2/Eastern_Kingdoms_early_layout.jpg?f9ee69). The art itself is pretty cool.
