## Post 110961 by Azcron (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-12-02T18:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110961#p110961 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Why is the mallet of Zul'Farrak in the Hinterlands with the Vilebranch tribe? The Sandfury, who were the Razorbranch tribe before the sundering, were a part of the Gurubashi Empire. The Vilebranch on the other hand were a part of the Amani Empire. Everything that I could find on these Empires indicates that they did not care for one another so why would the mallet, that summons their diety Gahz'rilla, be in the possession of another tribe? Hoping someone may have a answer or could point me in the right direction.

In fact, both tribes have something common, although it's not that obvious. It's Hakkar.
Vilebranch trolls are worshipping Hakkar and drink his blood (probably in an attempt to gain power and dominate over Hinterlands).
Sandfury trolls pledge their allegiance to Hakkar, according to Hakkari Zealot - though they didn't do to their Loa the same thing Gurubashi did.
I suppose that the Mallet was transferred to Vilebranch as an act of good will between two nations loyal to one blood god. Perhaps we could find a relic of forest trolls somewhere in treasury of Zul'Farrak, too (man, now I NEED such quest in TWoW!).
