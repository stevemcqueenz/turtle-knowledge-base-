## Post 8146 by Axoc (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2020-10-15T08:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8146#p8146 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Year 617: Fall of Quel'thalas & the Scourge of Lordaeron
 618: The Blood Elves leave Quel'Thalas, the colonization of Kalimdor (the ground-laying of both Orgrimmar & Theramore) begins, & Illidan goes to the Frozen Throne to confront Arthas, who nearly kills Illidan. Illidan narrowly escapes to the Outlands, where he currently is along with the Blood Elves and Naga.
 620: Orgrimmar & Theramore are both completed.
Early 622: (the month isn't clear, but it's towards the beginning of the year): WoW begins
Late 622: Naxxramas appears over Stratholme.
Final days of 622: Adventurers enter Naxxramas on behalf of the Argent Dawn.
Staff has said that the first patch of the expansion takes place in-lore before adventurers enter Naxx but patches 2 and 3 take place after Naxx has been conquered by the adventurers, so presumably patches 2 & 3 will take place in 623.

 624 (in retail lore): TBC begins
 628 (in retail lore): Arthas is killed. He's still alive in TWoW lore.

Those are the alliance years. Subtract 592 from alliance years to use the horde's calendar.
