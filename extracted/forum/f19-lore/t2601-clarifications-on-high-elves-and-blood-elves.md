# Clarifications on High Elves and Blood Elves

## Post 16229 by Widogast — 2022-01-23T19:13:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16229#p16229 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Having fallen in love with Turtle WoW so far and enjoying the unique perspective that we are all taking (ignoring anything that was published for the lore after Classic and then going our own way with the story) that I wanted to take some time to look into High Elves.

There's a lot of problems that pop up with Quel'thalas' story in retail, a lot of details that get overlooked and just are completely irrelevant - even to people's RP characters. One of the most notable things that sticks out to me, at least, was how Blizzard decided to turn Kael'thas "*Good Boi, Cucked by a Human Prince*" Sunstrider, a desperate prince that was respect in Dalaran and agreed even to a suicide mission under Garithos' orders, into a cartoon villain that betrayed the Betrayer... for no conceivable reason.

The retcon is later explained in the *Illidan* novel by William King where he recounts the meeting of Kil'jaeden atop Black Temple. Illidan Stormrage's inner monologue is confident of his defenses against a mental probe of his memories and motivations, as every sorcerer worth their salt has mental fortifications. Kael'thas, seemingly, does not. From this moment on, Kael'thas Sunstrider - in Blizzard's current canon - has essentially deviated from being that character.
We do not need to go down that retconned path.

After Kil'jaeden gives the order to Illidan and his lieutenants to slay the Lich King, they depart for Northrend. Kael'thas has a small encounter with Arthas that doesn't go anywhere...

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/XMBY8kn.png) Note: "Other insults" is referring to Arthas winning Jaina's heart. Our boi got scarred by that one pretty hard. Pour one out for him.

...and then it leads into the cinematic of Illidan and Arthas' famous duel. Illidan is said to have been recovered from his defeat by Kael'thas and Vashj to be taken back to Outland. Blizzard used this, originally, as an excuse for making Illidan no longer a morally grey character that skirted the line between hero and villain but ultimately serving the side of good and turn him into a crazed lunatic hellbent on building up an army big enough to defend himself from Kil'jaeden, abandoning all of his ambition. This, too, was later retconned in the William King novel (for the better, as it is a return to his original character but it shouldn't have needed to be retconned in the first place).

The High Elves of Turtle WoW offer us this lore:
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/ettPWYZ.png)

Presumably, the high elves were awaiting the return of Kael'thas Sunstrider and the Blood Elves that joined him. Curiously, I wonder why our high elves also do not refer to themselves as Blood Elves. They do not make the distinction based on the consumption of fel or their allegiance to Illidan. Kael'thas and the gathered survivors of Quel'thalas declare themselves to be Blood Elves in honor of their fallen kinsmen (about 90% of the population) and then Kael'thas takes the most able-bodied men and women to go and help the Grand Alliance of Lordaeron to the south, but I suppose the distinction is made as a personal claim from the high elves to say, "*We are not them*," and not as a means of not honoring their fallen.

Our high elves are those that were not taken with Kael'thas. They were either left behind to defend their people from the Scourge while others recovered, were incapable of fighting due to the sickness of losing the Sunwell, were injured, too young, or whatever other reason. After awaiting the return of Kael'thas for so long AND RECEIVING TROUBLING RUMORS that come from the Grand Alliance of Lordaeron's account of events (not elven accounts), the high elves decide to head south and abandon the ruins of Quel'thalas and the Scourge that have overrun it. They gather up in carts and make for abandoned lodges, camps, and other places to recover as many high elves as possible before settling in Loch Modan and later settling in the rediscovered outpost of Alah'thalas.

Presently speaking, Kael'thas Sunstrider and the Blood Elves have retreated to Outland with Illidan Stormrage while some few of them scour the world for relics of power to bring back to Outland. Even the RPG lore reinforces this idea (as people are often fond of drawing on it to supplement holes in Classic's lore / what hasn't been written yet for Turtle WoW):
> Blood elves have no permanent settlements on Azeroth; the only blood elf communities are located in Outland, the extradimensional wasteland of Illidan's exile. Despite this, a number of them can be found at Quel'Danas under the leadership of Aquel'Luer'Thala.

The character Aquel'Luer'Thala is described as:
> Aquel'Luer'Thala is a female blood elf windrider, warrior and mage. She is the commander of the dragonhawk riders of Quel'Danas. She commands the elite group of windriders from the island of Quel'Danas riding dragonhawks and giant eagles into battle to defend the blood elves' homeland. She is a beloved commander and hero. She is known for her leadership and military tactics. She is fairly charismatic and diplomatic, but can be intimidating when she has to be.

So we have the situation in Quel'thalas described as essentially left in a ruined state, overrun with the Scourge forces (likely under the command of Dar'khan Drathir), with Aquel'Luer'Thala and her force as the lone attempt at making any headway to reclaiming the destroyed Sunwell and presumably without any reinforcement or aid from Kael'thas as she is described extremely different to the typical attitude of a Blood Elf under the normal consumption of fel.

Kael'thas and the bulk of the Blood Elves remain in Outland in service to Illidan Stormrage, doing whatever it is that they're doing. Whether Illidan survived his encounter with Arthas after being recovered by Kael'thas and Vashj is sort of up to all of us as we take the story into the future. Right now, he's the least of the concerns of the world.

One final lore tidbit. After doing all of this recap of Warcraft 3 and such, it dawned on me that there's never anything in World of Warcraft that expands on King Anasterian Sunstrider, Kael'thas' father. Anasterian Sunstrider gets a bit of love, expanding a blurb about him into a footnote, in the *Arthas: Rise of the Lich King* novel by Christie Golden where he fights against Arthas at the foot of the Sunwell and Felo'melorn is shattered by Frostmourne but nothing further is ever noted about him. There's not even a footnote about Anasterian as being among the souls that were plucked from Frostmourne after the defeat of the Lich King. **BIG SAD**.

Getting into the lore and trying to re-learn from a different POV has been a lot of fun. Looking forward to doing the same with the majority of Eastern Kingdoms.

Edit: To add on some idea as to how bad the situation is in Quel'thalas, we can look to Zul'jin's words from Turtle WoW's homebrew lore:
> After da combined forces of da Amani and da Horde failed da attack on de elven lands, we didn't back down and me people and I paid the da price. Dey cornered me close to dat lake and been captured by a bashful elf named Brightwing. I healed and organized me army once again, not long before da Scourge claimed Silvermoon. It was time to act, but even with the loas' blessing we couldn't hold against the dead and failed.

I can clarify some things for you.

First of all:
The reason why the current High elves do not call themselves Blood elves is because of the confusing information that has reached the Alliance and because of the fragmented state of the High elves following the fall of Quel'thalas. Lordaeron and Quel'thalas are both in ruins and disconnected.

Kael'thas gathered as many High elves as he could but he didn't gather all of them. Furthermore the rest of the Alliance received rumors of Kael'thas's group consorting with the Naga and committing treason against the Alliance. However, that part of the Alliance has been virtually wiped out thus only rumors and some records from Dalaran exists of that affair.

Thus the High elf group that ended up joining the Alliance elected to retain their original name to avoid being associated with suspected traitors of the Alliance and incase the rumors about their Prince are true. Do keep in mind that very VERY few of the High elves who went south with the caravans lived in Quel'thalas, the majority either lived in the border regions, or in Lordaeron. It was very difficult to escape Arthas's purge and the subsequent occupation of the Scourge. This is another reason why the current High elves are so friendly towards the Alliance, because they were the High elves who mostly elected to live among the humans and the rest of the Alliance.

Finally: Any lore post-Vanilla is essentially non-canon. There are things we may choose to incorporate from future material but you should essentially take any TBC and later lore with a grain of salt.
Forexample: Kael'thas is absolutely going to be handled differently than he was in TBC. His personality, motives and characterization from TFT has not been forgotten.
