# Druidism - A Lore-loyal Solution for Curing High Elves' Magic Addiction

## Post 173698 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2026-04-02T02:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173698#p173698 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

Is druidism in earliest Warcraft works only a night elven culture? No. It's an elven culture, as the earlist Warcraftian concept or lore inclination of the druid, which is still available for search, refers to the elves in Lordaeron. The original script telling the backstory of the Runestone in Caer Darrow is "The runestone was an ancient monolith erected by the elven druids and inscribed with powerful runes of protection and warding."

Although this statement was later retconed, by replacing the word "druid" with "arcanist" or "Elven magi", it is not a secret that the druidism in Warcraft universe is a culture of elves before night elf was introduced/conceived as they are in WCIII. The design of night elf was actually based on early high elf's concept. In other words, high elf in early works bear obvious similarities with night elf, not only in physical traits but also in culture. In other associated early scripts, the elves in Lordaeron is more of a "ragged" appearance and in tune with the wild nature than they were later designed as spellcasters in ornate robes. Besides, the name of elven capital city Silvermoon can be taken a remaining clue implying that high elf's early concept is different.

In sources like the Warcraft RPG (discarded by Blizzard), there are other races, like centaur, furbolg, human and troll, who also practice druidism, but these should not be taken as mainstream examples.

Back to the discussion of high elven druidism and Turtle universe, the game has already introduced a number of quests about curing the magic addiction of the Witherd with night elven techniques. My suggestion for lore development is siple and conservative - We should confirm that high elf society is a diversified structure, just like human kindoms having differing features and cultures, conceiving a community with unified identity and a divided form of life for high elves, formally and decisively. In my concept, those in rough clothes who live in the forest are less influenced by magic addiction. The druids that live a recluse or solitary life in the deep forest of Quel'thalas for thousands of years would now seek help to cure their people through a historical link with their night elf brethrens.

The druid class available for high elf is therefore never a retcon but a tradition revival.
