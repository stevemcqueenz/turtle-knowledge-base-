# Introduction to the Scarlet Crusade (Funny)

## Post 141120 by Kyern — 2025-07-24T18:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141120#p141120 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**What the hell is the Scarlet Crusade?**

So let’s say the world ends, zombies crawl out of every crypt, and instead of uniting like normal people, a group of traumatized paladins says “What if we just kill literally everyone who doesn’t smell like holy incense.” **BOOM!**  Scarlet Crusade.

This is a fanatical religious order that rose from the ashes of Lordaeron. They worship the Holy Light, sure, but in the same way a wild-eyed street preacher screams about fire while setting things on fire. They hate the undead. They hate people who are near the undead. And eventually, they start hating each other. It’s beautiful and stupid. Like a holy soap opera with swords.

How did it start?

During the Third War, Prince Arthas went full maniac, murdered his dad, and unleashed the plague across Lordaeron. The Knights of the Silver Hand got wiped out hard. Some survivors regrouped and founded the Argent Dawn, which tried to fight evil with actual logic. The others? They said logic is for heretics and turned Lordaeron into a red-robed inquisition zone. (Which makes it fun to RP them).

High General Abbendis and Grand Inquisitor Isillien formed the Scarlet Crusade from what was left. They recruited priests, paladins, and traumatized foot soldiers. Their plan? Purge everything. If it breathes and doesn’t say three Light prayers by breakfast, it’s probably a ghoul in disguise.

 **What about the Ashbringer?**

Let’s talk about Alexandros Mograine, one of the founding legends. He helped forge the Ashbringer, a holy sword that could one-shot undead like a cheat code. This guy was a hero. So naturally, the Scarlet Crusade killed him.

His own son, Renault Mograine, got brainwashed by Isillien and stabbed Alexandros in the back during a mission. True family bonding moment. They dumped his body and ran off, and Kel’Thuzad raised Alexandros as a death knight just to add insult to murder.

Renault became one of the key commanders in the Monastery. He strutted around like a paladin while conveniently leaving out the part where he knifed his dad for Light points.

The Scarlet Monastery is a cult theme park
The Monastery in Tirisfal Glades is the Crusade’s main stronghold. If you’re new to this place, picture a gothic temple filled with hymn-chanting lunatics, propaganda everywhere, and training that involves smashing your own friends with hammers just to prove your faith. You'll probably venture there at lvl 32.

Inside you’ve got:
Whitemane
A holy priestess who thinks resurrection should be used in the middle of battle like a divine panic button. She’s terrifying, dramatic, and probably hears voices. She yells “Arise my champion” like she’s on stage and someone clapped.

Herod
The Champion of the Armory. He screams “Blades of Light” before spinning at you like an angry Beyblade on holy steroids. Not much of a thinker, but his armor looks great.

Renault Mograine
The prodigal son of the Ashbringer, now running the Cathedral wing and pretending like he did nothing wrong. You can walk in, call him out, and he still acts smug about it.

 **They spread like wildfire**

The Scarlet Crusade didn’t just sit in the Monastery. They moved into Hearthglen in Western Plaguelands and set up shop there, complete with patrols and inquisitions. They harassed civilians, murdered suspected necromancers, and basically terrorized the whole area while insisting it was for the greater good.

They even had a huge presence in the Eastern Plaguelands at Tyr’s Hand, one of the most fortified and paranoid outposts ever built. Everyone there treats outsiders like potential liches. You can’t sneeze near a Scarlet patrol without them pulling a sword and quoting scripture.

Their whole system became a snake eating its own tail. Anyone who questioned a command? Heretic. Anyone who hesitated before killing a villager? Probably a cultist. They started executing their own men just for having doubts. Real productive stuff.

But wait, it gets worse...   turtle_in_love_head

Behind the scenes, the whole operation is being manipulated. Remember the guy in charge of the Crusade? Grand Crusader Dathrohan? He isn’t even Dathrohan anymore. He got body-snatched by a dreadlord named Balnazzar. This demon literally wore a human meat suit and ran the entire Crusade like a Light-worshipping puppet show.

And no one noticed. For years.   unhappy_turtle_head

Balnazzar used the Crusade to destabilize everything. More paranoia. More purging. More chaos. He knew if they just kept killing everyone, eventually the Alliance and Horde would both be too busy to stop the Scourge. It worked, for a while.

They think they are the good guys...
That’s the wild part. Most of the rank-and-file Scarlets think they are doing the right thing. They think they’re saving the world. They quote scripture, chant blessings, and genuinely believe they are holy warriors. Even when they are bashing in someone’s head for owning a suspicious book.

Some are zealots. Others are just scared soldiers with nowhere else to go. They lost their homes. Their families. Their sanity. The Light became the only thing they could cling to. So they held on with both hands and never looked back.

Even as they burned villages.

Even as they killed children.

Even as their own leaders got corrupted and started frothing at the mouth mid-sermon.

In Vanilla, they are still strong

During Vanilla WoW, the Scarlet Crusade is still a major force. They control chunks of Tirisfal, Western Plaguelands, and Eastern Plaguelands. They’re at war with the Scourge, the Forsaken, the Argent Dawn, and sometimes each other. They are not villains in their own eyes. They are survivors. Fanatics. Holy warriors with a kill count and a choir.

Their lore is rich, tragic, stupid, and terrifying. They are what happens when faith is used as a weapon without limits. When trauma turns into dogma. When a prayer becomes an excuse to put someone in the ground. They are not just some dungeon bosses. They are the broken remains of Lordaeron’s heart, twisted by grief and burning with zeal.

And yes, they would 100 percent try to execute you for wearing a blue tabard or sneezing in a chapel. (Not really, but definitely if you're Undead).

This is the Scarlet Crusade, if you liked what I wrote you can read more into them online. They're definitely a fun thing to RP, and I would recommend getting involved if you like it.
