# Are Highborne elves and Quel'dorei "different" in Turtle WoW?

## Post 136699 by Alexmmusic — 2025-06-23T08:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136699#p136699 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In a certain quest given by Ordenal Owlmane in Alah’Thalas, the highborne are mentioned as if they were something completely foreign to the high elves. While I know that at a certain point in history, when they arrive in the Eastern Kingdoms, they gradually become “another race,” shouldn’t they still be aware that they descend from these night elven highborne?

## Post 136703 by Galendor (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-23T08:42:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136703#p136703 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Although the knowledge of the High Elves' descent from the Highborne is not a secret, not every character can possess this knowledge. For some, it is history too ancient to pay attention to, for others - something like a myth. Remember, most of the population of Quel'Thalas perished in the Third War, and it is far from certain that the refugees of Alah'Thalas are the most educated people of their race.
In addition, in the quest, Ordenal talks not only about the history of the Highborne, but also about their current status, and this, I suppose, is little-known information.

## Post 136747 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-06-23T16:06:00Z

The word “the Highborne" in both elvish languages, or Thalassian and Darnassusian, is indeed "Quel'dorei", which is firstly "translated" high elves. That means the highborne and high elf are exactly the same word, just like Scots and Scottish people. Weird, but we should beware that they're sharing the same name as different races or factions, including the Highborne or "high elves" under Azshara's reign in night elves' empire on land, the high elves that developed into a unique race in modern-day continent of Lordaeron and the Blood Elves following Kael, who have the theme music "Lament of the Highborne" in TBC.

By the way, making Blood Elves join the Horde would never be wise for developing a game inclined on defending the original lore but merely a balancing compromise or mistake Blizzard made under Activision.
