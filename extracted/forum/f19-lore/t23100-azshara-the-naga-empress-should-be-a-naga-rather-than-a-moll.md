# Azshara the Naga Empress should be a naga rather than a mollusc.

## Post 161227 by Sleeplust (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-12-01T14:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161227#p161227 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Azshara, after being transformed into a naga, should look more like a naga sea witch, which bears significant features on snakes, fish and humanoids, such as the slithering bottom body, scaled skin, the human-like face and a hissing voice. The point is that all these traits are of the vertebrate rather than an octopus. A more trustful proof is from the line of Maiev Shadowsong's, commenting about Azshara's statue erected by nagas - "I don't recall her of having a tail", which confirms that the naga empress looks more like a common female naga.
