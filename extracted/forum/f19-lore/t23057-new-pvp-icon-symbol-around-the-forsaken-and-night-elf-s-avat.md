The Forsaken should not be considered a member of the Horde in the standard of Warcraft lore. The warchief needs to be an orc. Why? In Warcraft III, the two classic faction names are Orcish Horde and Human Alliance. A leader in Night Elf faction has to be a night elf, rather than a green dragon, a dryad or a mountain giant. A centaur tribe friendly to Stormwind forces is not a member of the Alliance.

The Forsaken as a member of the Horde was only a result of playerbase balancing. Even if undead human and elves can be allied with any orcish horde, Sylvanas or Nathanos, is never eligible to become the Warchief of the Horde. Meanwhile, Cairne and Vol'jin are only playing roles like Highlord Omokk and War Master Voone, who serve under Warchief Blackhand.

Back to the topic, the Forsaken and the Night Elf, as two more aloof races/forces, should be isolated from the two classic factions. PVP tags should be diversified into at least four for current races.
