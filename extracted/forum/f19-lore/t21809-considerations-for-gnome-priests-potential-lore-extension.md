# Considerations for Gnome 'Priests' (Potential Lore Extension)

## Post 151017 by Armordillo — 2025-09-09T18:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151017#p151017 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Browsing the forums I stumbled upon a topic regarding the introduction of the Priest class as an option to the Gnomes.

While I'm not completely against the idea I do wish to highlight a consideration that must be addressed before they are introduced.

I would like to clarify I am **NOT** advocating or rejecting the idea of a gnomish priest. What I'm only doing is giving a potential lore idea for others to use so they would fit into the world more naturally, rather than feeling rather forced and inadequetly explained.

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ***Consideration***

With that said the thing to address is the potential dilution of the Gnomes' racial identity. Each race in WoW is suppose to feel unique from each other by exemplifying certain traits within their cultural depiction. Having the intellectual gnomes adopt a religious priesthood as a mainstream norm doesn't help in that regard.

I noticed two prevailing ideas to justify this. The first is the Blizzard explanation that the gnomes merely adopt the priesthood for their powers to heal the sickness of radiation. The second is that the gnomish refugees sought comfort in religion after the events in Gnomeregan.

The main issue I have with those ideas is that they are a reaction towards relatively recent events rather than basing its legitimacy on the traditions and established culture of the Gnomes. I find it strange for them to suddenly adopt a religion - an institution that they likely regard as promoting fairytales and superstitions - because why would the intellectual gnomes put their trust in something like that for actual power or take comfort in things they never believed in before. I could see this as fringe cases but to be accepted as part of the cultural mainstream so quickly is odd, never mind the fact that if those were the case then why didn't the gnomes adopt the healing magics of the faiths earlier before the wars after knowing of its uses?

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ***Proposal***

It is in my belief that for the idea of gnome priests to become acceptable, its legitimacy in the lore should be based not as a reaction to current times but instead on the idea that they had such 'priests' **BEFORE** the events of Gnomeregan and that there are cultural forces in their society supporting its existence.

I understand this may seem radical but as noted by another user before, gnomes as a whole don't really have much established lore or questlines centered on their racial story. The only plotline they currently have in vanilla is the reclamation of Gnomeregan and much of their society is left undeveloped and unexplored. As far as I'm concerned this part of their lore can be added upon so long as we maintain the cultural traits we associate with the gnomes.

As many have noted before the use of light-based powers doesn't require a divine being's intervention as it's simply the manifestation of willpower instead. Borrowing ideas from the RPG's Forsaken's Cult of Forgotten Shadows
([https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cult_o ... en_Shadows](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cult_of_Forgotten_Shadows)) whose focus is on the Self I believe instead of medics and priests, Gnomes would have **'Philosophers'** in their society under an old established institution.

Through this we can maintain the intellectual and scientific aspect of gnomish society unlike a priest while keeping the spiritual element we expect from priests which is lacking in a medic. Below is a rough idea of how the lore regarding such a thing would exist.

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ***Lore Draft***

Deep underground in western Dun Morogh is the technocratic city-state of Gnomeregan. Although led by a High-Tinker and a court of the gnomes' most brilliant minds in their respective fields, one can argue that Science is the true ruler of this wonderous marvel.

Since the beginning the ever-curious and questioning gnomes would concern themselves only with mathematical equations and chemical formulae but their inquisitive nature would inevitably have them asking questions about the natural world and eventually about themselves.

With these existential questions begging for answers, the College of Philosophy was established and from its halls echoing with endless debate and refutations came a new discipline of thinkers. To their people, these enlightened philosophers would serve as judges and as role models on how to approach life in general. Case by case they would lay justice with sound reasoning and logical conclusions in the court of law, guided by a moral compass refined by years of having one's views challenging and being challenged by others. The aura of wisdom and air of certainty they exude made them into community pillars that could be rallied around in times of trouble.

In a world of towering giants, gnomes can feel rather insignificant. When compared to the vastness of the world and even the cosmic realm of space, it would be natural to despair over such a pathetic existence in the face of the wider scheme of things. The philosophers however reject such assertions and declared that no challenge is beyond them big or small. They then made a promise to their people: to have faith in the mind's ability to lay bare all the mysteries of the world, to uncover the universe's grand design and that somewhere out there lies the answers to life's questions.

But of course ever since the disastrous fall of Gnomeregan, there is a notable rise amongst the surviving philosophers coming to much darker and bleaker conclusions to the questions of Life...

 *"A tinker builds a gizmo. A philosopher builds a society."*

 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 ***Additional Thoughts***

With the above story draft the Priest class would have more reason to be a mainstream norm amongst the gnomes instead of just being fringe cases.

Also, through this the gnomes can be more fleshed out instead of simply being genius inventors. Taking it further they can eventually be developed as a technocratic society run by various ministries coordinated by the High Tinker and known for establishing many academic universities of various scientific disciplines which Philosophy is a part of.

Furthermore by having the philosophers come to various different outlooks after the city's fall, we can create more gnomish quests out of them by having them lead various refugee groups to different parts of the world to establish new societies or undergo missions for various groups.
- For example - One group is heavily traumatized to the radiation effects on the body. They despise the weakness of flesh, how it rotted and fell apart from the bone as organs fail and their mind goes insane. Hearing stories of their potential titan-linked heritage made them wander to excavated ruins for salvation. They now head northwards, after discovering a mysterious metal head that some passing witnesses swore still spoke to them.
Lastly, when it comes to attire I envision something like the ancient Greek philosophers. I'm not very familiar with Priest sets in WoW so I put together something that goes along with the Greek-inspired Kyrian armor in Wowhead.
