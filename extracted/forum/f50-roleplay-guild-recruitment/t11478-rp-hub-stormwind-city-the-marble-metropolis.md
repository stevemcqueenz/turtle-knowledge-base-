A blooming hub of RP
[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/sFvK8fB/Screenshot-1.png)](https://ibb.co/MRDcFvX)

 *The bustling marble metropolis, Stormwind City, is considered by many to be the beating heart of the Alliance; populated by Humans, Elves, Dwarves, this thriving melting pot of cultures is home to quest-filled taverns, seedy back alleys and a myriad markets and shops catering to a traveller's every need. Home to all stocks of people, from the most gallant of knights to the most depraved of blackguards, Stormwind truly has something for everyone!*

[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/XpnYGW6/Screenshot-1-1.png)](https://ibb.co/d6NmHfX)

This is a Discord server for people to generate and discover roleplay within Stormwind: if you find you want to establish your character there or if your guild simply roleplays around the city, do join us! Do you want to open a tavern, run a business or even start your own criminal syndicate? This server is the place for you!

> <https://discord.gg/f3pCZgMEDa>

Lastly, don't forget to drop by our bi-weekly events such as the Cathedral bazaars or the gruesome trials!
