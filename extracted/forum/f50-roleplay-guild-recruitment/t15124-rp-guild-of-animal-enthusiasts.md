Howdy everyone! So, I am here to spread the word and inform everyone that a brand new guild has just been formed! The guild is named "Purrseverance". Purrseverance is a new HC leveling/RP guild seeking to provide aid and assistance to anyone brave enough to attempt the Hardcore challenge! Whether its your first attempt or your 100th, the goal of Purrserverance is to help all of its members survive and overcome any threat Azeroth has to throw at us on our HC journeys and to achieve immortality. Once you join Purrseverance, even if you find yourself lost, confused, or stuck in a cave, you will have the piece of mind knowing that Purrseverance will have your back!
