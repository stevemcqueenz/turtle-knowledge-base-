 **<The Greysteel Company>**

Hello everyone! I am pleased to announce officially on the forums that, <The Greysteel Company> is recruiting and actively seeking out new members!

Do you Role Play? Are you searching for a community of like-minded individuals to RP with? Is your character in need of work? Or, on the other side, does your character have work to give? The Greysteel Company is open to any type of character. One can ICly join the company, or they can be a contract/ally of the guild.

You do NOT have to be a "adventurer" kind of character. The premise of the guild is that we will take any and all jobs that come our way. Even it it's as simple as - collecting ingredients for some kind of *ahem* stew. Or taking a book all the way to Menethil Harbor! Of course there will always be your, rescue the damsel, kill the enemy kind of jobs.

That's where you come in! We have dedicated officers who are more than willing to build story content for the guild and progress - but the most fun we've had is when others bring forth their own ideas and events to the guild.

The best part about it is, because we're a work for contract premise - we have just about good reason to be anywhere in Azeroth! We even turned a suicide run through Orgrimmar just to run Ragefire Chasm into a IC mission to rescue an elf from deep within the bowels of the Horde City. That person then decided to join the guild to keep the RP going!

We encourage new and old RPers alike. We're a very friendly and welcoming guild. Conflicts are few and far between, and members are encouraged to bring up any concerns or questions to the officers/leadership of the guild.

Our most active time is during the weekends, usually around 10am PST. We have members in the guild from all over the world, a healthy European population as well as American. We try to have an official RP event at least once a week. The goal is to get RP flowing pretty consistently and having members RP during down time between guild events!

Ashlynn and Blackmoore co-lead the guild. With Talvik being second in command. You can message them in game for any questions/comments/concerns.
