![📜](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4dc.svg) The Second Gurubashi Empire – Rise Again in the Name of the Loa! ![🐍](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f40d.svg)

“The blood of the old empire still flows through our veins. Its temples may lie in ruin, its banners may be dust… but the will of the Loa endures.”

The Second Gurubashi Empire is a troll-led RP-PvP guild on Turtle WoW, calling all who wish to see the glory of the empire restored — warriors, shadow hunters, witch doctors, and allies from every corner of Azeroth who revere the Loa.

We are not servants of Hakkar — his dark hunger shattered the old empire. Instead, we walk the path of the true Loa, honoring their power and restoring balance to the jungles, the sands, and beyond.

![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) What We Offer:

Immersive Roleplay: Forge stories of conquest, diplomacy, and divine favor.

RP-PvP Campaigns: Join warbands in imperial crusades and border skirmishes across Azeroth.

Events & Rituals: Loa ceremonies, arena games, imperial councils, and more.

Diverse Opportunities: From loyal warriors and priests to imperial diplomats and explorers.

![🌿](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f33f.svg) Who We Seek:
Trolls, Zandalari envoys, jungle-born allies, and outsiders willing to swear loyalty to the Loa and the reborn empire. Whether you are a veteran of the old wars or a fresh blade eager to carve your place in history, the empire welcomes you.

Join us — and let the Second Gurubashi Empire rise from the ashes.
