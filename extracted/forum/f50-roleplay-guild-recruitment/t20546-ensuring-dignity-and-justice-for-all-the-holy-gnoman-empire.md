## Post 142009 by Piepai69 — 2025-07-29T06:47:00Z

My fellow Gnomes and allies to our movement, I am once again here to remind you that it is time to take a stand against oppression and gnomophobia. I invite all fair minded individuals who recognize the natural position of Gnomes to join The Holy Gnoman Empire in the 'Rally for Dignity' and the 'March for Justice.'

The rally will take place on Wednesday at 20:00 Server Time in front of the Stormwind Cathedral. We are taking aim at the inherent Gnomophobia in the Church, which still, to this day, does NOT allow Gnomes to be ordained as priests! This is despite the Gnomish superior ability to grasp spiritual concepts which the late provisional bishop Gnesmond Tutu called "The unappreciated blessing of the Gnomish race, the reflection of Divine perfection among mortal Azeroth"

Following the rally there will be refreshments.

Following the refreshments there will be a civil rights march, which all Gnomes are invited to attend. We will march from the Cathedral of Shame down to our ancestral homeland in Gnamibia. Along the way we will spread good cheer to Gnomes and combat any signs of racism we see on the road. Every fair minded Gnome is invited to attend. We will be collecting money for the charity Homes for Gnomes. Gnome Power.
