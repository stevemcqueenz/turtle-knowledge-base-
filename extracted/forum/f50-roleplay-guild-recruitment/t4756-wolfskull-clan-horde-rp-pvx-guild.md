## Post 28475 by Pistol (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-11-04T21:02:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28475#p28475 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**In the forests of Ashenvale I saw one of the Ghostpaw wolves looking up into the sky. The sky opened before me, a pack of wolves as white as snow surrounded by an enemy unseen. They battled long until the night waned and they vanished from my sight.  A wolf’s skull was there at my feet. I knew then we are to be these wolves, to stand together as one pack, one clan.** - Vision of the Wolfskull

 **Who we are:** The <Wolfskull Clan> are  dedicated to fighting for the Horde and seeking adventure in what ways we can. We have a special emphasis on hunting, on Shamanism, and other forms of Kalimdor Horde spiritualism.

 **Who we want:** We call out to all Orcs, Trolls and Tauren to join our clan. Undead and Goblins are permitted though their path is not exactly the same.

 **Out of Character Information:**
 *Guild Type:* Heavy-RP/Light PvP/Light PvE

 *Races:* Orcs, Trolls, Tauren for all ranks. Limited Undead and Goblin recruitment.
 *Classes:* Any but Warlock with possible exceptions.
We are a Horde RP guild based in TWoW that recruits from all races of the Horde. We have RP events on both Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms , both one off events and longer storylines. We have both casual and more serious events, of course.
 **How to Join:** Joining is simple, contact Nodoba in game or on Discord @Vagrul#5479 and I will get you in the guild.

The Wolfskull Clan is looking to recruit officers interested in running RP Events and recruiting new members to the guild. Please reply here or message me on discord if interested.

**Heart of the Giant**
Final event of 2023.

 *Eight months ago, the Wolfskull Clan under the former Packleader Nodoba entered Ashenvale on a new campaign. Their first stop was the memorial stone for Grom Hellscream. His sacrifice broke the deep roots the blood curse had on orcish kind, and with it a new age of freedom from the strings of demon puppet masters was born. A fitting start for the Wolfskull, who had set about in the shaded forest to bring death to the demonkind that haunt it.*

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1035952364508676138/1190629327927255060/Monument.png?ex=65a27ef8&is=659009f8&hm=6fb3c835c56e0ce73ceeac1ea2afaea535557d0a4b36bb03cd008c15049983c0&)

Now, eight months later... Untold patches of soil have tasted blood of both demons, of the Horde, and of Alliance foolish enough to step in the way. Leadership passed from one to another. Numbers have swelled to more than three times that of the old days. The largest gathering of the Wolfskull yet set out from their home of Splintertree and struck at Demon Fall Canyon to secure it for a coming caravan of orcs who wished to pay respect to Hellscream’s memorial as a sign of the ending of the year.

The demons were no match. Having grown from a small band of honorable warriors and mystics, the Wolfskull struck the demonic forces like a true strike force. Trolls, tauren, orcs, even a furbolg that had seen the might and honor of the Horde, all were united in purpose, working as one hand to swing a vicious blade.

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1035952364508676138/1190629444331765760/image.png?ex=65a27f14&is=65900a14&hm=fa6f957715d848425cb1254493d0d401e8362b3bfcb1434c28c1deb17f33fe99&)

 *There at the foot of the memorial did the Wolfskull gather around their Packleader Zorizar. There in front of the stone, the clan’s purpose was restated, and with so many gathered it was an opportunity to grow yet stronger. Two joined the Wolfskull properly as Pups. Four trials were given out to other young Pups of the clan, so that they may soon be called wolves. And one member who had been with the Wolfskull well over a year was given the honored title of Greypelt.*

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1035952364508676138/1190629524568817694/image.png?ex=65a27f27&is=65900a27&hm=0d9bb2a2648eb712e9ad76f60f9886a7e02280ae3580e4c4e899636f6dc5b548&)

 *A spy was found to be watching the clan. A Blackrock insurgent, a rat from a bygone era, surrounded and trapped. Justice came swiftly in the form of a whistling axe, and the spies head was liberated from his body. He was buried out of the fel tainted land, a small yet necessary consideration that was perhaps more than they deserved.*

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1035952364508676138/1190629598472437860/image.png?ex=65a27f39&is=65900a39&hm=e4cf04d4c03b6a611e4f7667c3952c5f9ee11b0faa84096acc27cb1ea897a695&)

Know that this banner waves outside the cave of Splintertree Post, the grinning skull laughing in the face of all who would oppose the Wolfskull Clan. Whether they be demons, Alliance, or even those of the Horde.
