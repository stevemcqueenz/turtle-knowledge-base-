So I'm new to the server and thinking of starting the above.

When I played back in Vanilla I was part of a guild on the Defias Brotherhood RP-PvP server when it first opened. The guild was called the Starseeker Sentinels.

For those familiar with Nordanaars Nightsong Sentinels the premise of the Starseeker Sentinels was to actively defend Night Elf lands, particularly Ashenvale and Darkshore.

The thought behind this guild will be one borne from the aftermath of Cenarius' demise to the Orcs with an outright hatred towards the horde and their defilement of Ashenvales forests and those living in it or buried in it.

Initially I'm thinking of this behind a BG (particularly WSG) and WorldPvP guild but if the numbers are high enough there could be raiding down the line in conjunction with other guilds for class balance.
