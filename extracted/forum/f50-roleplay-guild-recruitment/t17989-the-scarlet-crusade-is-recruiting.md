# <THE SCARLET CRUSADE> is recruiting!

## Post 122511 by Macaskill — 2025-03-13T23:40:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122511#p122511 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

***The Scarlet Crusade: A Legacy Resurrected***

 *Our brotherhood is an ancient project that, over the years, has forged its own history and heritage within the Warcraft universe. Known for its unwavering fervor, the Scarlet Crusade has cleansed its ranks of the demonic yoke following the death of Balnazzar. Now, under the banner of the Church of the Crimson Flame, we return to truly serve the Light, while remaining faithful to our dogmas and forceful methods.*

 **Our New Vision**

•More diplomatic, but without forgetting our fanatical fervor: Although we have opened the doors to dialogue with other factions, we remain a force to be feared for our unwavering determination.

•Strong hierarchical structure:We have various ranks and sections that contemplate different roles (clerics, paladins, knights, explorers, artificers, etc.). Each character can progress in a natural and coherent way, forging their own legend.

•Long-lasting campaigns: We host long-running events and storylines, allowing members to evolve and deepen their stories, earning their place in the chronicles of the Crusade.

 **What do we offer?**

•Quality and fidelity:Our mission is to represent the Scarlet Crusade in all its classic glory, without sacrificing the narrative coherence and rigor that characterizes this legendary faction.
•Serious and professional atmosphere:We take care of every detail of our roleplay—atmosphere, clothing, formations, dialogue—and design events to fully immerse you in the Scarlet experience.
•Realistic progression:Each character advances through the ranks through participation, merit, and loyalty, offering logical and satisfying growth.
•Fraternal community:Despite our harsh methods on the battlefield, within the guild we foster respect, camaraderie, and mutual support.

 **What do we seek?**

•Dedicated roleplayers: Passionate about the Scarlet faction, willing to commit to the narrative and honor the intensity of the order.
•Seriousness and involvement:We want members who take care of the details, actively participate in events, and contribute to the community, both in the story and in the brotherhood.
•Respect and commitment:We need brothers and sisters in arms who strengthen our core with their presence, ideas, and willingness to work as a team.

 **Do you dare to bear the Scarlet Crest of Lordaeron?**

If you feel the call of the Light and the fervor of the Crusade burns in your chest, join us. Here, your steel will forge history and your name will resonate in the chronicles of the order. We do not promise an easy path, but one filled with glory, honor, and unwavering passion.

Contact us for more information and prove yourself worthy of carrying the scarlet banner!

 **The Light guides us and the Crimson Flame purifies us!**
