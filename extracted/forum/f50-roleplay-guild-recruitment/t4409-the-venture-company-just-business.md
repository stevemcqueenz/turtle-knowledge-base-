![:gear:](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2699.svg) **THE VENTURE COMPANY** ![:gear:](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2699.svg)

 *"Givin' nature a run for its money!"*

 - The Trade Coalition is huge, far-reaching and massively complicated. In addition, numerous other, smaller goblin companies, created and run by entrepreneurs called moguls (the hands of the trade princes) operate throughout Azeroth. Perhaps the most famous of these smaller businesses is the **Venture Company**, founded and ruled by Mogul Razdunk.

 - The Venture Company is known for spearheading the efforts to reestablish trade routes across eastern Azeroth, to connect isolated goblin trading posts. A growing number of shops in Azeroth have signs declaring that they are owned and operated by the "Venture Company," which the proprietors claim is headquartered in a faraway city ruled by goblins.

![:pick:](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/26cf.svg) The illustrious megatherium business colossus is now looking for enterprising individuals to fill positions of:

- **Contracted mercenaries & guards**

- **Tinkerers & researchers**

- **Labourers, craftsmen & strip miners**

- **Magi, warlocks & alchemists**

- **Merchants & pencil pushers**

If you are a man, creature or unaffiliated entity with any set of skills, send an application to
 *404 Shredder St, THE VENTURE COMPANY, Undermine, Kezan.*

Alternatively, contact Taskmaster Powderkeg, Taskmaster Boabby or Executive Skizzle for more.

> OOC:
>
>  The Venture Company is a casual/heavy company-themed RP guild based on the in-game faction of the same name. We provide weekly events, casual roleplay and a welcoming environment for a vast number of character concepts and roleplayers, new and experienced.

- Venture on over to our Discord for more: <https://discord.gg/U2YbRnkChv>

Looks like a lot of folks are trying to streamline donation / payment flows for their private server. I’ve been building a small payment module for a similar WoW server — what helped me a lot was setting up a unified [payment orchestration platform](https://corefy.com/) so I didn’t have to juggle different providers manually. With Corefy I managed to combine card payments, crypto and local bank transfers in one dashboard — made life way easier when players came from different countries. If you expect international users, having one platform handling all methods can save plenty of headache
