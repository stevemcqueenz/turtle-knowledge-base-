# [A-RP] [Nord] The Gilneas Brigade

## Post 154096 by Gilneasforever — 2025-10-02T18:29:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154096#p154096 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sons and daughters of Gilneas! The world calls us arrogant. They call us pig-headed. They call us headstrong. And they are correct! But make no mistake, we are neither fools nor ungrateful. The Old King is slain and our home is free, thanks to the Alliance. We owe them a debt we could never repay in a thousand years, but we will try. The House of Nobles stands by as enemies overrun Stormwind, the scum of the Blackrock Mountain bear down on Dun Morogh, and the Elves of the north have nothing but their tears and the blood of their kin. I do not intend to stand idly by.

Rise now, my brothers and sisters, and rise with pride! The House of Greymane calls for your sword once more, to restore our name to one we should be proud to bear! For Gilneas!

 **Who we are:**

The Gilneas Brigade is a new Alliance-centric RP Guild, based on the unit from WC3, aiming to repay the debt Gilneas owes to the Alliance, whether that be through military aid or other means. We are actively hostile to the Worgen and Scourge, and are not afraid to cross blades with the Horde. Our guild base in Southshore reflects this, we are only a stone’s throw away from those that would threaten to undo the Alliance. Dwarves and humans are welcome, reflecting the lore established in WC3, and special cases may be made for other Alliance races.

What we offer:
- Roleplay opportunities, both casual and story-driven.

- Levelling, Gilneas needs the best soldiers it can get.

- A welcoming community. The spirit of our kingdom inhabits us all.
If you are interested, please reach out to Salten in game or check out our thread on the TurtleWoW discord!
