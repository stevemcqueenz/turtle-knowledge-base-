# The MacBrew Clan

## Post 138791 by Brunancio — 2025-07-12T05:22:00Z

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/B5KTb02m/foro.png)

> *Unlike their Wildhammer kin who embraced shamanism, the MacBrew Clan learned arcane magic from gnomish exiles - though they insist it was just 'liquid lightning in a bottle.' After being cast out of Aerie Peak for turning gryphon roosts into distilleries, these ale-scholars wandered Azeroth, perfecting two crafts: brewing liquid thunder and bending magic to their drunken will. Now based at Thunderbrew Distillery, they uphold three truths: 1) Any spell improves with ale, 2) All creatures can be sheared or saddled, and 3) The best lightning comes from mages, not clouds. New recruits must swear fealty to barley, bad decisions, and at least one unexplainable explosion per week.*

---
Casual RP/PvE guild for dwarves who believe ale improves everything! We explore iconic dwarf lands (Loch Modan, Dun Morogh, Wetlands, Hinterlands), level together, and turn professions into chaos—especially brewing. Whether questing, crafting, or RPing in Thunderbrew Distillery, we prioritize fun over min-maxing. Weekly activities include drunken dungeon runs, "sheep diplomacy," and tavern tales. All levels welcome—just bring your beard and a thirst for adventure! (Gnomes tolerated if they bring explosives.)
