# <House Perenolde> Casual RP guild

## Post 37328 by Houseperenolde — 2023-03-10T19:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37328#p37328 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

*"Whatever the Kingdom of Alterac may have done it did not deserve to be reduced to ruins by the Alliance. It did not deserve to have its people branded fugitives and driven out by the 'knights' of the Silver Hand. Alterac, one of the seven kingdoms of man. did not deserve any of this. My countrymen, we will retake what is rightful ours. This I swear to you."*
~ Lady Kalimas Perenolde, Address to the Exiles

 **<House Perenolde>** is a casual RP guild focusing on characters connected to the former Kingdom of Alterac.

 **Races:**
All welcome (former Alterac citizens and mercenary forces)

 **Classes:**
All, but Paladins with backstories tying them closely to the Knights of the Silver Hand will only be admitted on a case by case basis (due to their role in the occupation of Alterac)

 **Notable RP Policies:**
 - We have both roleplayers, non-roleplayers. Parallel guild ranks have been added for both groups.
 - RP guild ranks have access to the Officer Chat which has been set aside as an rp channel (through the wonders of magic/engineering techniques long-range communication between all members is possible).
 - New members wishing to have access to rp guild ranks must submit a character profile for the guild website (not required to take part in rp events).

 **OOC History:**
We formed in August 2022 as a friends and family guild. We participated in several RP events put on by the **Northern Kingdoms** alliance of guilds(<https://discord.gg/ygep5PEQSb>) and hosted our own events in January and February 2023. Through much of 2023 the guild was on hiatus as members dealt with real life matters or just became over all too busy. The guild restarted activities in July 2024. In August we held an IC raid on Ravenholdt Manor as well as an IC exploration of the Gilnean zone which culminated in an IC attack on the City of Gilneas. We are a small guild currently recruiting people interested in the lore of Alterac.

 **Contacts:**
