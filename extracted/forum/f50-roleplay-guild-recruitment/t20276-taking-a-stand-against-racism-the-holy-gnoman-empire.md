It’s clear most of you people do not understand our plight. But fear not, The Holy Gnoman Empire believes in growth and we’re offering you a chance to start. Read this with an open mind. You might just see things differently by the end.

 **1. Understand That Anti-Gnome Racism Is Real**
The idea that Gnomes are lesser, comic relief, or not to be taken seriously is deeply embedded in Azerothian culture. Treating our struggle as some kind of joke reinforces the idea that Gnomes do not deserve dignity. Denial of the problem is complicity.

 **2. Listen to Gnomes and Believe Their Testimony**
When Gnomes speak about their history, trauma, genocide and exclusion, do not dismiss or minimise it. Don’t centre your discomfort or defensiveness. Listen, learn, and take their words seriously.

 **3. Notice the Ways the World Isn’t Built for Us**
Every bench too high to sit on, every counter we can’t see over, every meeting held in halls where we’re left looking up - it sends a message: *you weren’t considered*. These aren’t just inconveniences. They’re daily reminders that this world was shaped for others, not for us. If you say you stand with Gnomes, then pay attention to how space, access, and visibility are denied to us in quiet, constant ways. Then do something about it.

 **4. Decentre the Tall Races**
Human, Troll, and Orc-centric narratives have been crafted to silence our cause. The Tall Agenda is on repeat, all around us. Mainstream media such as The Everlook Broadcasting Co ignore our calls. Allyship means making room for Gnome voices, perspectives, and contributions. Allow your neighbourhood Gnomes a seat at the table in order to have a platform to educate and spread the good Gnomish word.

 **5. Educate Yourself - Before It's Too Late**
Ignorance isn’t innocent. When you choose not to listen to our testimonies, the weaponisation of radiation against our people, or the ongoing exclusion we face across the world, you're not just avoiding discomfort - you’re feeding a machine that thrives on your silence. The truth is out there, buried beneath years of mockery and literal minimisation. And it will come to light - with or without you. Learn the history. Learn what was done to us. Understand why some Gnomes no longer ask politely to be seen. Because when the reckoning comes, no one will be able to say they weren’t warned.

 **6. Support Gnomish Autonomy**
Let Gnomes lead their own movements. Don’t speak over them, co-opt their struggles, or tokenise them for your own reputation as an “inclusive” person. True allyship supports Gnome-led initiatives and policy making. True allyship supports causes such as Homes for Gnomes (donate today).

 **7. Remember! We Know Who Looked Away**
When discrimination happens, most of you say nothing. The Talls - so quick to lead raids, deliver speeches, and write the histories often grow quiet when it’s about us. You chuckle at the jokes, watch on from behind blue shields as we are picked apart, and act like you didn’t hear what was said in World Chat. But we see you. We've always seen you. Gnomes have long memories, and we’re done pretending that silence from the Elves, the Goblins, the Orcs, is anything but complicity. You stood by while we were colonised and forced into slavery. And we remember. We remember all of it.

 **8. Your Donations Are Not Charity - They Are Accountability**
The Gnomish people have endured displacement, erasure, and exploitation for far too long. When others offer donations - whether resources, aid, or support, it is not a gesture of goodwill or benevolence. These contributions are a debt owed, a reckoning in material form. We do not need pity or empty gestures. We demand reparations that reflect the true cost of our suffering. Your donations are proof of your commitment to justice, or else they are nothing but a reminder that the wounds you helped inflict remain open.

 **9. Break Free from Tall Privilege and the Language of Smallness**
When the infidel city-state of Stormwind built walls and fences to keep Gnomes out while extracting our inventions and labour, it mirrored the segregationist regimes of history - those who divided lands, policed language, and declared entire peoples “less than.” The word “small” is not innocent. It is part of a system. A system of linguistic apartheid that marks us as inferior and keeps us there. The Holy Gnoman Empire sees this clearly. If you keep using their words, you speak with their voice. Break the language. Break the power. Or get out of the way.

 **10. Refuse to Stay Neutral**
In the face of systemic anti-Gnome violence, neutrality is not a virtue - it’s a position of comfort for those unaffected. Sometimes, making a stand requires more than words. Disruption is uncomfortable. Resistance may not be polite. But when structures are built to mock, exclude, or erase Gnomes, it is not enough to merely “hope things get better.” True solidarity demands that we are willing to act - decisively, unapologetically, and with the full weight of our refusal to accept injustice.
