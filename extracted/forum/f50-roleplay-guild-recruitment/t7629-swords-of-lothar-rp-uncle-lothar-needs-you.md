**The Swords of Lothar(RP) | Uncle Lothar Needs You!**
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/1114613646924984481/1114684564984578119/Polish_20230603_153142510.png) **A Letter from the High Marshal**
Good day to you, proud sons and daughters of the Alliance! If you are reading this, then you have considered enlisting in the Swords of Lothar division, and I am more than glad to welcome you in our ranks. I believe that It will suit you best if I'll provide you a short overview of our mission and different goals we are striving to accomplish.
The Swords of Lothar are a division of the Seventh Legion of the Alliance Army, most commonly known as "The Swords". Our main objective is to protect the people of the Alliance where the Army at large can't. To succeed in completing this task, we devote our time to constant training, skills development and gaining new knowledge for us being ready to take up arms when the time comes.
If you are planning to be a capable and contributing member of our order you should always be prepared for you will face many hardships as a loyal servant of the Alliance. I salute your bravery and resolve if you are ready for the trials ahead and wish you best of luck. May the odds be in your favor and may the Light guide your path.
 **-Koldin Gaidlick, High Marshal of the Swords of Lothar** **Azeroth, Our Home**
 *For thousands of years Azeroth has been the birthplace and home to many different races of the Alliance. We've flourished and gotten stronger in this world, facing various hardships and losses that appeared through the past centuries. Today our duty, our obligation and our oath as children of Azeroth is to protect its lands from whomever might try to harm them in any way. And we will do just that, standing proudly together, ready as one to sacrifice our lives for this nouble cause, for the well being of our land.* **Threats To The Alliance**
As a division of the Army, we are sworn to confront all the possible threats to the Alliance that may arise. It doesn't matter if our adversaries are petty crooks or Horde Warlords, all of them are our enemies and we must put an end to their atrocities.
Here I would like to give a brief overview of the main directions of conducting our military operations, depending on the region of deployment of enemy forces.
Our main enemies in the South are the so-called "Defias Brotherhood", a group of merciless bandits and ruffians that had overrun some of the important provinces of the Kingdom of Stormwind. These criminals and traitors should be brought to justice, or taken to the Stockade in the city of Stormwind for further interrogation, if they are suspected to hold a valuable information which can be crucial for our efforts against the enemy.
Another threat in this domain is the Horde, having many bases of operation in the southern regions of the Eastern Kingdoms. These murderous beasts possess a significant menace for our lands and the overall well-being of the citizens of the Allaince. In this case they must be intercepted at first sight and dealt with, for the end must be put to the their harmful deed and their wicked ways.
Our main adversary in the North are the Scourge and Forsaken that have wiped out the Kingdom of Lordaeron in the past years. These disgusting enemies of all living things pose a constant threat to the northern territories, poisoning the once magnificent forests, fields and hills of this region with their vile presence. Being abominations of the dark, they are extremely dangerous and should not be underestimated. A soldier is required to have a clear mind and enough training and experience to withstand this enemy.
Some of the other lesser threats in the North to be considered include the Scarlet Crusade, religious zealots that are inclined to kill anything and anyone they deem being corrupted and unpure. **What you can expect from us**
We are a pure roleplaying guild, with the goal of having RP as our first and only priority to live up to the name of an RP-PVE server. Our server has suffered many changes and our experience has shown us that it is now time to passionately stand up for our idea of good roleplay if we want to revive this way of experiencing the game. This idea is based on lore-oriented game and character concepts, consistent roleplaying and a system of rules for fights, which guarantees fun for all participants. Playing easy-D&D in a World of Warcraft setting without having to leave your home?! If that sounds good, the Swords of Lothar might be the perfect fit for you
We host multiple RP events every week, be it the most dangerous fights this side of the Dark Portal, or in front of the cosy fireplaces of our base or while visiting allies. We offer a community that sees roleplaying not just as an accessory or duty, but as something that many of us have come to appreciate and love over the years: Writing history together.
