**Andu Falah Dor - Let Balance be Restored**

Brother and Sister Kaldorei, let not the darkness of night quench your souls but instead invigorate you with the passion to do what must be done, we are the Children of the Stars! May our light shine forth in the darkest moment to take back our lands and let balance be restored! Anu'dorini Talah!

Let us come together Kaldorei, to restore our people and our lands.

OOC--
A Night Elf Guild- RP -PVE and PVP

We will operate in Night Elf territory with PVE and PVP, even if you don't want to participate in the PVP you can help by making potions, gear, food or just keeping us company. I will organize social and PVE events, if anyone would like to help me run these please volunteer!
I'd like to organize some PVP events with the Wolfskull Clan who also operate in Ashenvale. This is an emerging guild and quite casual (unless lots of people want to join and I need to make it more structured).  My goal is to facilitate casual, balanced and friendly PVP.

You are welcome if you are new or a veteran to RP.  -- Strictly NO ERP--

<https://discord.gg/4atjTQ2Mwh>
