*Next to the tavern entrance, a flier on the wall reads*
The **Khaz Auxiliary Guard** calls on all able dwarves and gnomes to sign up and help restore the homeland to it’s former glory! Apply today!

Duties include (but not limited to): maintaining the King’s peace, aiding the good folk of Khaz Modan, securing trade routes and supplies, aiding in exploration and rebuilding the Khaz!

Stable pay and lodging offered. No previous experience necessary.

Apply today!
 *At the bottom of the flier all sorts of contact information can be found. The name **Brockwell** Samson appears*

 ------
OOC:

The Khaz Auxiliary Guard (KAG) is an RP guild with a light military theme and heavy emphasis on the administration of the Khaz Modan region, handling everything from defense, to rebuilding, supplying outposts and villages and ensuring tax customs are being respected and no illegal goods are passing through. Events run by the guild will as such cover a vast area of activities and not be constrained to military activity only.

Our primary areas of RP will be Dun Morogh, Loch Modan, Wetlands, Badlands, Searing Gorge and Blackrock Mountain, although we hope to organize and partake in events in further regions as well, wherever the interests of Ironforge might take us.

Feel free to contact us in-game (whisper Brockwell), on the turtle discord or on our own at: <https://discord.gg/cD5QUzXzEd>
