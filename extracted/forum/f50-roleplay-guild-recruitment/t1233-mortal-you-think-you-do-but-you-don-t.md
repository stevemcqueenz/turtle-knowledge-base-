![Image](https://i.imgur.com/hdHTwSX.jpg)

Rakzul, the hunter; decided to go out of the valley of the trials, then fought against a scorpid lvl 6.

He died. Even before reaching the level 3. Rakzul don't care, he's in fact immortal in the game.

But imagine one second if he had only one life in Wow. Could you do better than him?!

RIP in peace Rakzul, First Mortal of Turtle WoW.

 **<Mortal>** is a straight Roleplay Hardcore project.

The spirit is, you have one life. If you die, you delete the character. He is mortal.
The challenge is real, i guess also, people can be attached to their character, even after their death. They'll just be off the guild project, but not rewarded to be mentionned in the wall of the dead.

RULES:

 -You have one life. Every release spirit is definitive.

 -Release spirit cannot be effective for this challenge.

 -Ankhs & Bubble-hearth are allowed. But it will not everytime save you from the death. All errors in mortal mod can be an end of the journey.

 -You cannot get any gold from donators, except from other mortal players.

 -You cannot mail yourself anything. You can only send and receive mail from other mortal players, or even Roleplayers only for RP purpose.

 -No alt-banks are accepted.

 -You are able to use the auction house.

 -You can solo dungeons, or even try with other mortal players.

 -All professions are allowed.

 -All talents are allowed

 -All gear you can craft, buy, or provide by youself is allowed.

 -Grouping out in the open world is forbidden, except for a dual leveling challenge or an elite quest.

 **LEVELING IN DUO SPECIAL RULES:**

 -If someone die, the other must fall on the sword and the run is over.

 -You must not leave the same zone as each other.

The challenge end at level 60, If you reach it you'll be able to say:

"i never died in World of Warcraft!"

Kargoz on YouTube (and a ton of other people) are doing something VERY similar on Classic.

 1) If you die, you must delete your character.
 1a) If you see the "Release Spirit" button, it counts as a death, so no Reincarnation, Soulstone, etc.
 2) The only gear you can use is what you have made and/or found yourself, so no trading or auction house.
 3) Grouping in the open world is not allowed, whether or not you're actually in a party.
 4) Dungeons are allowed, but only one run of each dungeon per character, and only with other Hardcore characters. Ex: You can only run RFC once, and everyone else in the party must also be a Hardcore character.
 4a) To party up for a dungeon, you must do it inside the instance, and then disband inside the instance.
 5) PvP deaths do count as deaths. Don't do anything stupid to get yourself flagged, unless you're that much of a masochist.

I vote on having these rules, in particular. No sense fragmenting an already niche community within a niche community.

As for rewards, I'd say make it another glyph, or maybe a tabard others can see. If you die, the tabard is shredded and turned into a junk item you can no longer wear. If you get to 60 and still have the tabard...eh, I dunno. Custom title would be nice.
