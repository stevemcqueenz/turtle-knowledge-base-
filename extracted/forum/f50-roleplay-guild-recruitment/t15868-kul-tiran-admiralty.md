# Kul Tiran Admiralty

## Post 108103 by Murci (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-16T09:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108103#p108103 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Were you thinking to roleplay a character from Kul Tiras?

If this is the case you already found a guild with people who are trying to do the same!

 ***Join the Kul Tiran Admiralty, where the sea is your home and honor is your compass. As a recruit, you'll learn the art of seamanship from seasoned sailors in Davenburg, mastering the rigging and weaponry of our mighty warships. From defending our shores to exploring distant horizons, your journey will be steeped in adventure and camaraderie. Embrace the call of the sea, and become part of a legacy that spans generations. Are you ready to sail with courage and uphold the proud traditions of Kul Tiras? Join us, and together, we will face the challenges of the Great Sea with unwavering resolve.***

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/RVLg58Xj/image.png)

 -We are representing the merchant state and one of the biggest naval power of humankind.
 -We are not in the Alliance of Stormwind,but we are in contact.
 -We are antagonists of Theramore, the Orcs and the Horde.
 -We maintain interesting diplomatic solutions to other roleplay guilds

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/KjnJYsF3/31.png)

If you wish to join, please hop in to our discord server:
<https://discord.gg/99N2XBBAWp>

Anybody from the Kul Tiran society is welcome to roleplay, or anyone who would like to work for the Navy.
For example: Sailors- Cooks- Diplomats- Bartenders- Merchants- Guards- Marines- Hydromancers- Priests of the Holy light- Treasure hunters- Lumberjacks- Brewers- Hunters- Cannoners

 **What races we accept in the guild?**

 -Mostly Human
 -Dwarfs
 -Gnomes
 -Undeads - *when you want to RP a fallen crewmember`s journey, its a path where you not really RP with us for obvious reasons.*
 -High Elf -  *with explanation, why to work for the Navy*
 -Night Elf - *with explanation, why to work for the Navy*
 -Horde characters as captives
 -Undeads for a dead Navy as separate antagonist group with Forsaken ties
 - Goblins -  as deackworkers merchants

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/nzm1dwXS/image.png)

 **Where do you find Kul Tiras related roleplay spots in Turtle Wow'?**

 **-Davenburg** - Embassy in Stormwind *(Exchanging with other factions)*
 **-Caelan's Rest** - An isolated Kul Tiran community waiting for help. *(Town RP, Scavenger hunt)*
 **-Anchor's Edge/Tiragarde Keep** - The offensive base in Kalimdor. *(Scavenger Hunt, PVP)*

Ps: our discord server is also focusing on low lvl PVP in a separate section. We try to collect and host Warsong Gulch and Arathi Basin for the lower brackets.
