# Salvagers, Inc - Horde/Neutral Laidback Lair

## Post 108929 by ZararsisRedgrin — 2024-11-20T11:19:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108929#p108929 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi everyone!

New to TurtleWow and to Nordanaar, I am very pleased to announce the creation of a casual RP guild on the Horde side of things, fostered on good mood and laidback plays.

 ****Who we are****
 - Salvagers Inc. is a casual RP guild anchored around a business-oriented sense of adventure.
 - Levelling, PvE (dungeons and raids), PvP and wPvP, RP activities, every aspect of the game we shall treasure, love and value.
 - Relatively new with low members, looking forward to becoming an active part of our community.

 ****The Business****

> "Everyone needs something these days: they've lost a family heirloom, a magic item, a loved one. You never can tell."

Specalized in the acquisiton of lost goods and knowledge, we Salvagers are the Horde's counterpart to the dwarven Reclaimers, except more proactive. Where they comfortably wait for a contract to go after some lost family heirloom, we don't wait for no one to go after Potential Treasure - P.T © - under any of its forms (magic items, rare artefacts, unique reagents or forgotten scrolls) and find ourselves some interested buyers through our network.

> "With the Salvagers around, there's no reason, for the right price, everyone can't find what they need!"

The Salvagers aren't in short supply of work as long as there are perilous sites to salvage. For any looking for a good week's (almost completely) honest work, the Salvagers will have a contract ready, punctuated by myriad of innocent fine prints. Just sign at the bottom of the parchment.

In addition to part-time contractors and full-time adventuring employees, Salvagers Inc. is always on the lookout for business partners all around Azeroth, wherever they might be. Black market liaisons, trading companies and cartel representatives should feel free to leave their cards.

What it means is we're interested in all types of candidacy, all races from both factions! While we would mainly be centred around Horde shenanigans, we maintain contact with people all around Azeroth in the hope of one day benefitting from it.
Additionally, for all RP-groups out there, if you ever need to purchase the services of the Salvagers, let us know - I'm sure we can find a decent price!

 ****Basic Rules****
 - Be a decent being and respectful to one another. No bigotry in the guild will be permitted.
 - Do not take yourself too seriously.
 - This remains a game, and IRL stuff matters! Go easy.
