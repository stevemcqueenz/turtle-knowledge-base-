**THE NIGHTBLADE**

Night Elf Military RP Guild.
(former Shadowblade, changed for lore reasons)

The Nightblade fought alongside Cenarius with the Shadowleaves, Moonhunters and Starseekers against Grommash Hellscream and the Warsong Clan. Now the Nightblade is a faction within the Sentinel Army, led by the Sentinel Commander Faelir Shadowsong, under the orders of Shandris Feathermoon, the General of the Sentinel Army.

The Nightblades operates in the regions of Darkshore and Ashenvale, however, they may also do missions in other regions.

Faelir Shadowsong and the Nightblades are now looking for capable night elves to join their ranks and to serve and protect the Kaldorei kingdom.
