*The <Hand of Wrath> is a new military unit led by Executor Rotheart. It is the iron hand of the Horde, crushing its enemies!  The Hand serves the Warchief and the leaders of the races of the Horde.  Founded out of the Forsaken Vanguard by the order of the Dark Lady as a way to forge brotherhood between all races of the Horde in the fires of war!*

 **What we do:** The <Hand of Wrath> protects the people of the Horde by destroying the enemies of the Horde wherever they are found from Kalimdor to the Eastern Kingdoms.

 **Who we want:** The <Hand of Wrath> recruits all those that are part of  the Horde whether they be Undead, Orc, Tauren, Troll or Goblin. Proud soldiers, scouts, workers, healers and any others who may be of service to the Horde

 **Where we're based:** The Hand of Wrath's headquarters is located within Splintertree Post's former mine.

 **Out of Character Information:**
We are a military RP guild based in TWoW that recruits from all races of the Horde based. We have RP events on both Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms , both one off events and longer storylines. We have both casual and serious events, of course. We work with other RP guilds as well allying with other Horde guilds and battling those of the Alliance.
 **How to Join:**
Joining is simple, contact Rotheart in game or on Discord @Vagrul5479 and I will get you in the guild. To get placed within the guilds ranks, you simply have to swear the Oath before an officer of appropriate rank. This can happen at events but its preferably going to be before them for time saving.
