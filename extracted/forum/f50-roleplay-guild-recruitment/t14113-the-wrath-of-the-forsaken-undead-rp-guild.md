>  We have shouldered the ineptitude of a king.
>  We suffered the betrayal of a prince. And at what cost?
>
>  Our homes were taken from us.
>  Our families, friends and allies were taken from us.
>  Our lives were taken from us.
>  Even the peace of our death was taken from us.
>  But no more...
>
>  We are the **Wrath of the Forsaken.**
>  We cannot take back what was lost, but we can take something: Revenge.
>  We pledge to fight the Scourge and destroy the Lich King.
>  We shall use every weapon at our disposal and create new ones if we must.
>  We shall release those brothers and sisters in undeath still trapped in the Scourge's clutches.
>  We will bring devastation to those who maimed, tortured and abused us as their slaves.
>
>  For our rage cannot be contained, and our suffering cannot end, as long as the Lich King reigns.

OOC:
![💀](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f480.svg) The Wrath of the Forsaken is an Undead only RP-guild taking in any undead human that identifies as Forsaken, i.e. those who accept Sylvanas Windrunner as their ruler, as well as any undead human who identifies with their goal of fighting the Scourge.

![📜](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4dc.svg) We offer an overarching guild plot that provides chances for all kinds of rp, from research and experiments to mercantile and craftsmen and military or combat focused RP. Our goal is to boost and support Horde RP, but Forsaken RP is our main focus.

![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) We welcome PvE, PvP and HC players alike as long as they are Undead.

If you are interested in checking us out or even joining, you can find us here: <https://discord.gg/vSkAeUgUrg>
