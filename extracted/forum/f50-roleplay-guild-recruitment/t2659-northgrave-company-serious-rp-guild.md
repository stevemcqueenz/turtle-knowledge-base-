~~~~~*The Northgrave Company of Gilneas~~~~~*
![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/938573624737144874/938574075012472902/gilneas.png)
While King Greymane has walled off the world to Gilneas, some within the country still keep up with the goings on with the world outside. Lord Galician Graveholme has noticed how weak the world outside has become. The Old Alliance is dead and the lion-led successor toils away in the far south, too distracted by their own turmoils to think of the lost lands of Lordaeron. The Scourge has fractured and whatever faction of the soulless undead that have laid claim to the fallen Kingdom of Lordaeron are disorganized and not yet established. The Orcish Horde has long departed these lands save for those miserable hermits in the Alterac Mountains. Lordaeron is ripe for conquest and the Northgrave Company is poised to make a *killing*.

Operating out of Gilneas, but with chapter houses across the Eastern Kingdoms the Northgrave Company seeks to profit off the squabbles of the Horde and Alliance. Primarily focused on activities on the continent of Lordaeron, the company dabbles in many schemes across the Eastern Kingdoms.

What the Company looks for in recruits: All but the dead. While Gilneas proper may dislike those who follow the red banner, the Northgrave company sees that some of them can be of use. Money talks and the riches that have been left rotting in the north are many and there is plenty to share.

"The Undead are lost, they may bear the resemblance of family or the shattered memories of those we once loved but they are gone. What remains outside these walls is nothing but the dead and and those that refuse to depart. It is mercy to relieve them of their Light-cursed existence and benevolence to reclaim the lands lost to the Scourge by any means necessary."
 -A Gilnean Priest
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
OOC: The Northgrave Company is a mercenary RP guild on TWoW that allows all but the undead based out of the Walled Nation of Gilneas. They mostly are active in the Eastern Kingdoms and rarely venture to the West. Events will be a mix of mostly serious with some casual events tied in. Once we've established ourselves, we look forward to being friend or enemy of other RP guilds on the server.

How to Join: Respond to an advertisement on the /world channel or message Galician online or find him in the world. We are currently searching for signatures. We are a cross-faction guild and accept all races/classes except for the Undead.

![Image](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/938842383146176643/938901507435290625/Bounty_Board.png)
In every chapterhouse town a Bounty Board can be found. Clients may choose to leave bounties open to anyone or request the aid of a specific chapterhouse. Company Bounties may show up here from time to time as well with targets that may require a team to go forth and defeat it or even just clever exploration. Chapterhouses may compete for bounties if no specific Chapter has been requested.

 **Information**: A living, functional bounty board! The Company Officers and Clients may post bounties here and offer a reward for completing it. Rewards may be supplies, equipment, potions or gold. The targets must be an achievable bounty such as a named monster's defeat such as a rare spawn, quest target, uniquely named character, enemy players, enemy faction NPCs (major lore characters will not be targetable by bounties except in exceedingly rare circumstances), or in the form of items that can be unique to the target such as Stitches' Femur or a rare spawn's unique drop or a set number of items like Large Trophy Paws. Up to 10 Bounties will be offered on the Bounty Board at a time, with the board getting refreshed as story demands or at a two week period. Occasionally a Special Bounty may be posted with a great reward and will be separate from the normal bounties.

 **Bounty Example**:
Bounty Target's Name: Unlucky Deadguy
Client: Vengeful Moneybags
Reason & General Location: Deadguy stole fifteen barrels of bananas and I want his head! I last saw him in Booty Bay skulking around the streets!
Reward: 50 Silver

 **Proof**: In the cases where a target's death is desired, the burden of proof relies on the bounty seeker who must either provide an item unique to the target in the trade window, (usually in the do not trade section) or provide a screenshot with them standing over the corpse. The proof is only accepted if shown to the Client or Company Officer in person, sending a screenshot after the kill will not count. If someone else happens to know of the bounty and takes their own screenshots and shows it to the Client, they too will be eligible for the reward if the actual slayer cannot show proof of their own. (For example, if someone kills Nimar the Slayer he will usually drop Nimar's Tribal Headdress, if someone claims to have killed him, they may be asked to prove it by showing this item.)
