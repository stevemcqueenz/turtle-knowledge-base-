# ------{The Knights Templar}------ Leveling/Casual/TightKnitCommunity/RP Guild.

## Post 4595 by Naito — 2019-12-08T19:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4595#p4595 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

At the heart of Deadwind Pass, beside the terrible tower remnant of Karazhan lays a small, somewhat untidy, but very homey church. From this church, liberally infused with the holy light and the strong final hopes and wishes and desires of the many who fell to the darkness of Karazhan. The order of The Knights Templar is housed.

The church is small, but well stocked with provisions to do battle with the corruptions of our world. It may cost a bit to fully equip yourself with the arms and armor that identify you as a proper Knight in this unfeeling world, but despair not, there are those much higher within the order that will assist you in financing your preparations.

The order grows day by day, some come seeking to purify the lands of the darkness and we plot with them how best such activities can be accomplished.

Others seek power, wishing to quickly grow strong enough to stand stably within this dangerous world. There is strength in numbers, and often those who come in search of power band together with multiple fellow Knights whom are of similar strength and venture forth into the darkness of dungeons to gain power by rooting out the corruptions within.

Some seek community, wishing to bear fellowship with their fellow order members. As they become part of the order, they will never be alone. Without regard to time of day or night, there are almost always several or even many order members to enjoy talking with.

We have great aspirations, our eye lays upon the deepest and darkest regions in Azeroth. Where sleeping dragons plot our destruction, where fire, sand, ice and the filth of undeath corrupts our world. We aspire to one day have the strength and numbers to crush these places, even without relying on outside forces to assist. But we will never obsess over them.

Our first priority is to our fellow members, for we may only grow strong enough if we stand together.

Over 300 aspirants have joined the order, you will find us adventuring all over the lands of Azeroth, any race, any class is welcome without regard to faction so long as you have a friendly attitude are willing to learn and wish to be a part of something greater. Our mission to bring hope once again to Azeroth is more important that rivalries between Kings and Warchiefs.

If this has sounded like the companionship you have searched for on your journey in Azeroth, then strike now, the time is ripe. We are almost always easily found within the world, just get in touch with a member today to join us. The world communication channel might suffice, but if not you can directly speak to any member you see who can begin the process of bringing you into the fold.

Be welcome fellow Knight aspirants!

By the Light!
 -Knight Soryen.
