# This leads to the question of why "Queen of the Eternal Winter" Malorne says to "Elune": "How dare you return, my sister

## Post 109269 by Visionmoon (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-11-21T20:22:00Z

Arthas' King Scenario (Diablo Plot):
Knowing the costs incurred by the Tirisgarde Council in defending against the Void, Arthas communicates with his subordinates and willingly descends into becoming the maintainer of a Soul Extraction Machine.

Arthas' Holy Light Scenario (Naaru Plot - the premise is that the group responsible for the Great Cataclysm could not possibly be the Twilight Cult and the Tirisgarde Council):
Arthas' ultimate ability is a mass resurrection. After his troops and subordinates are revived multiple times, the damage to their souls gradually makes them resemble the Undead. Therefore, the situation regarding Arthas' base in Northrend requires further archaeological investigation.

This leads to the question of why "Queen of the Eternal Winter" Malorne says to "Elune": "How dare you return, my sister!" "After so long, you ignored our calls for help and remained indifferent to us."
