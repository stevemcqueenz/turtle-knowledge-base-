 **Keepers of the Void**
A light to medium roleplay guild, who are dedicated to community involvement in roleplay and want to provide a method in which players can be directly involved in a guild’s lore and we intend to incorporate player submissions within the guild lore and create events that both member and non-members can participate in and giving players agency within the guild.

So far, we are small and brand new, but we are ambitious and dedicated. We hope that you will find your place here and share in many exciting adventures.

---
 **Q&A**
- 1.What factions are allowed?
 *Primarily Alliance, though we are not opposed to Horde members. There are no race restrictions.*
- 2. What do you mean by involved?
 *We want you to submit your ideas for lore, story, and roleplay ideas. It could be a quest you're having trouble with or an item you're grinding for. We want to turn that into guild lore.*
- 3. How do you handle roleplay?
 *We are lovers of freeform roleplay, however for structure we will be using a pretty standard d20 system if needed.*

The busy streets of Stormwind are awash with sounds even after dusk has settled. Amongst the chaos you hear a whisper, no, a whistle, faint but alluring. Checking your surroundings, vision blurred with pedestrians. But there your ears catch the distinct sound, your eyes see a hooded girl standing in the shadow of an alleyway.

You catch her gaze, and she beckons you closer. The curiosity pulls you closer to the hooded girl. No words are exchanged, just a single white card. It was handed to you blank face up, reversing it, you are greeted by plain black ink: "The Oracle". You look up to ask questions but the hooded girl is gone.

You go about your day. Your weeks. The memory of the hooded girl fades. Until one day you check your mail to find a letter simply addressed "An Invitation from The Oracle". The rest of the invitation is a cryptic mess of jibberish, but you look closer, and see between the lines, you can see the pieces in place now, you understand.

... KNOCK! KNOCK! KNOCK!

You open the door and standing before you is the hooded girl. Before doffing her hood, she gestures to a portal swirling with energy behind her. “The choice is yours to make; stay here and wonder who the girl is, or take her hand and see how deep the water is?”

We aren’t just treasure hunters, seekers of wealth or fame. No, we are looking for the powerful and unique, and not just dangerous. We want the obscure, the out of place, the un-belonging, the gaps between where reality just doesn’t seem right.We are the curious, the inquisitive, the adventurous. We will seek out, collect, collate, investigate, analyze, all anomalous items, areas, and objects.

We want the curious, the ambitious, the driven, the talented. We want those who are willing to look beyond the veil and ask the question most wouldn't dare. We want you.
---
Keepers of the Void is an anomaly investigation group (in the same vein as SCP or MIB). They use a public facing Treasure hunting company as a front to cover up their other "work". The idea is to use bugs in the game as anomalies and investigate them, along with some deeper lore around the guild for members to uncover.

For Recruitment, you can DM me here, or contact me in-game under the same username.
