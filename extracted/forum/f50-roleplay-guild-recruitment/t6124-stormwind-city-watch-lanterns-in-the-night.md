**![:shield:](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f6e1.svg) STORMWIND CITY WATCH ![:shield:](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f6e1.svg)**

 *The Stormwind City Watch was ostensibly created to protect the people of Stormwind from miscreants, ruffians and villains, and to uphold a semblance of law and order within the fair city. While its founder was eventually found to be corrupt, these ideals are still upheld today - ‘ideal’ being the operative word. Watchmen are now part of the furniture of Stormwind City, and are easily recognised by their distinctive blue uniforms and abundant cynicism.*

[![Image](https://i.ibb.co/Jm9dyYR/AA-ad-size1-2.jpg)](https://imgbb.com/)

> All Watchmen (to a greater or lesser degree) abide by the Book of Laws: Eighth Edition, Watchman’s Manual and, in times of great strife, ‘The Watchman’s Elbow’ - complete with handy visual aids, by the masterful Triss de Lancrét.
>
>  The Three D's are an essential part of a Watchman's everyday life, enforced since the City Watch was founded many years ago. In no order of preference, they are:
>
>  **Discipline**
>
>  Follow orders, and show respect to those above you in rank. If the Drill-Sarge says, "Jump!", you say, "How high?". If the Lieutenant says, "Run!", you say, "Where to?". And if your Captain says, "Pandaren takeaway!", you say, "Sweet chilli or soy?"
>
>  **Dedication**
>
>  Dedicate yourself to your job as a Watchman, to the law and to the City itself. A Watchman may not enjoy being vomited on by a staggering drunk in the Pig and Whistle, but he must certainly dedicate himself to being vomited on effectively.
>
>  **Doliteness**
>
>  Be friendly and polite to the citizens of Stormwind. Any new recruits who question the spelling of 'Doliteness' are bundled into a sack and never heard from again.

 *“Poor saps. They don’t even know what they’re letting ‘emselves in for.”*
