# [A-RP] <The Kingdom of Ironforge> — Dwarf & Gnome & Alliance RP Guild

## Post 153699 by Ahzdagum — 2025-09-29T17:15:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153699#p153699 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

“By the might of the mountain and the wisdom of the forge, Khaz Modan stands united!”
_______________________________________________________
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/utEQqNF.jpeg)
Greetings, travelers of Azeroth!

Are you looking for a guild that embraces the rich lore of Dwarves and Gnomes? Tired of lifeless guilds that exist in name only? Do you want to experience meaningful roleplay, community-driven storylines, and a genuine sense of belonging?

Allow me to proudly introduce…

The Kingdom of Ironforge!
A new guild forged in the heart of Khaz Modan, is here to unite the Dwarven and Gnomish RP communities! We have a bold, immersive, and in game inspired guild and community focused on reviving and evolving what Dwarven and Gnomish RP can truly be.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/UUrfgmc.png)
Who We Are:
The Kingdom of Ironforge is a quality-first guild designed for those who want to:
- Dive into authentic Dwarf/Gnome RP inspired by questlines, lore, and in-game events.
Take part in weekly RP scenes within Ironforge itself from political intrigue in the Senate to pints shared in the Forlorn Cavern.
Join military RP in the Ironforge Guard or the King’s Army.
Embark on Explorer’s League expeditions across Khaz Modan and beyond.
Participate in public events, joint adventures, and storylines with plot twists worthy of a Game of Thrones episode (Dwarven edition, of course!).
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/UUrfgmc.png)
What Makes Us Different:
Unlike many guilds that never deliver on their promises, the **Kingdom of Ironforge** is committed to:
- Canon-friendliness – Lore-abiding stories based in the world Blizzard built.
Inclusive immersion – We welcome any character that makes sense in the setting.
Multi-faceted RP – Political, military, religious, tavern, and exploration RP… all in one.
Community over cliques – No elitism. No gatekeeping. Just genuine camaraderie.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/UUrfgmc.png)
We are not a socialite RP guild that never does anything. We’re not a spam-recruiting mess that never RPs. We are a real, functioning RP guild with actual content, character growth, and cross-guild collaborations where all are welcomed! ![:slight_smile:](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f642.svg)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/UUrfgmc.png)
Our Long-Term Vision:
We aim to build a Khaz Modanian cultural revival — including:

Political RP through the Senate of Ironforge.
Military campaigns through the King’s Army and Mountaineers.
Holy RP with the Church of Ironforge and the Hall of Mysteries.
Gnomish brilliance in Tinker Town and Mechagon innovation.
Explorer’s League expeditions to distant lands (with dungeon crawls and discovery).

 **Massive server events to unite guilds against common foes!**

Whether you’re a noble Thane, a snow-bitten mountaineer, a hopeful archaeologist, or a pint-swinging tavernkeep — there’s a place for you here.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/UUrfgmc.png)
Races & Themes Welcomed:
- Dwarves – Bronzebeard, Dark Iron, Wildhammer, **Earthen** (preferred but **ALL ALLIANCE RACES** WELCOMED!)
Gnomes – Standard and Mecha/Leper gnome variants
Explorer’s League Affiliates!
ALL Members of the Grand Alliance welcomed (just with preference towards dwarves and earthen or (mecha)gnomes!
Characters who fit naturally into Dwarven-Gnomish RP themes
If your character has a logical reason to be in Ironforge, we’re excited to have you!
 **Final Words:**
- We’re here to **Make Azeroth Great Again** (with more walk-up RP and less elitism).
We’re here to **revive world RP** and **city RP within Ironforge!**
We’re here to **unite the clans** and birth a **new era of Dwarven storytelling.**
 **Join us at the Great Forge.** **The ale is hot, the anvils ring true, and your story is waiting to be forged!**

/w or DM Ahzgadum in-game to learn more or get an invite.
Discord available upon request for deeper coordination.
