The <Wayfarers> Guild!

Wayfarers, formed on a crisp summer evening in Stormwind, is a band of like-minded adventurers and oddities who unified to explore and aid the far corners of Azeroth. It is a home on the road, a place for any and all who seek the thrill of perilous wanderings. A community cooperating to live a free and full life on the path.

We are a D&D-esque adventuring group looking to diversify and expand our ranks. The primary goal is quality roleplay, with events and storylines. At the same time we all want to enjoy the content and help one another.

If you are interested, please reach out to Theodore, Lornna, or Rodrick ingame. Or bump the thread!
Stay tuned for more to come!
