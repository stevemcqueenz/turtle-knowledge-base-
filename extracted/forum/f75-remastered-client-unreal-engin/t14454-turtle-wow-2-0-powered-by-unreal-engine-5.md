# Turtle WoW 2.0 — Powered by Unreal Engine 5

## Post 98451 by Torta (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-07-03T13:27:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98451#p98451 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

![Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/images/misc_img/forumheader.png)

 **Greetings!**

Many players have expressed that Mysteries of Azeroth and Vanilla WoW are becoming difficult to play as the old 1.12 client can struggle on newer hardware and operating systems. We love the original game and want to ensure that players are able to experience it for years to come. With many years of plans and new ideas in our sights, we have chosen to take action to ensure we can offer entertaining, expanded gameplay for everyone long into the future.

We needed a solution to extend the life of our favorite game, and almost a year and a half ago, we started our collaboration with the second development team known as **Council of Tirisfal**. Today, we're excited to present the first look at our special development: **Turtle WoW 2.0** — the Vanilla WoW client fully replicated in **Unreal Engine 5**.

[embed: https://turtlecraft.gg/watch/embed/06497d28]

> Unfortunately, with the good comes the bad. After seeing explosive growth (over 200k views, doubling our subscribers), our new trailer was given a takedown. While we'll be appealing this decision as our content is highly transformative, we have uploaded it to our website at <https://twow.link/uetrailer>
>
>  We love Azeroth, and will continue to mod our favorite game, even if sometimes it doesn't love us back. We are saddened by the loss of hundreds of supportive comments and countless pieces of valuable feedback, and as we have in the past when facing similar strikes, we encourage everyone to leave their feelings and feedback on our forums :)

 **Highlights**

- Developed specifically for the Turtle WoW Core, supporting all custom content out of the box.

- Built to include custom tools, allowing our team to expand on the Mysteries of Azeroth easier.

- Developed by a dedicated team, meaning our main content development and design teams will not face delays.

- Full original WoW 1.12.* API preservation (and extended), supporting all existing 1.12 and Turtle WoW exclusive addons.

- Able to run smoothly on any PC, from potatoes to powerful systems.

- Packaged with a proprietary anti-cheat which renders all existing botting and cheating software worthless.

- Cross-platform Play Supported: Compatibility with a wide range of devices, including Android smartphones and tablets.
 **Two Graphic Modes**

The new client will support two visual modes with different system requirements:

- **Legacy Mode**: This will look and feel like your usual game.

- **Modern Mode**: This will offer all the best Unreal Engine has to offer, including dynamic shadows, superior lighting, and more.
We plan to incorporate newer HD textures and models into the Modern Mode to ensure the game looks organic, and low-poly graphics don't clash with the modern lighting. However, you will be able switch to legacy character models.

Legacy Mode System Requirements: Code: Select all

```
Operating System: Windows 7 64bit or newer
CPU: Intel Core i3-2100 or AMD A6-3600
RAM: 1 GB
GPU: Integrated graphics (e. g. Intel HD Graphics 4000)
```

Modern Mode System Requirements: Code: Select all

```
Operating System: Windows 7 64bit or newer
CPU: Intel Core i5-6600k or AMD Ryzen 5 1600 (4 core CPU required)
RAM: 4 GB
GPU: Dedicated graphics card (e. g. Geforce GTX 1060 or AMD RX 580)
```

 **Impossible Becomes Possible**

With the new custom client, we can finally change things that were previously limited by the old client:

- Extended quest logs beyond the 20-quest limit.

- Proper widescreen support.

- Add a field of view (FOV) slider.

- Support for DLSS and AMD FidelityFX for enhanced performance and visuals.

- Better performance and AddOn support.

- Action Queueing, so your DPS is no longer dependent on latency.

- Organic multi-language support.
Starting now, the development team will provide regular updates on the new client, with a planned release in 2025. Please be aware that there will be a transition period during which it will be possible to play from both clients until we are sure that the new client works flawlessly.

The client will be distributed for free among Turtle WoW players. The goal of this project is to extend the lifespan of our expansion, supporting the existing emulation software our project runs on.

 **Modding & Streamlined Development Process**

Our approach with this grand client swap is aimed at preserving the speed of our current content development without pausing it. While players will be testing and connecting from both clients, our tools will allow the modding team to continue developing their assets using the original 1.12 tools, converting them to UE formats later. Eventually, we'll all transition to the newer toolset.

 **Funding**

We've poured an ungodly amount of resources into this project. If you'd like to support the Unreal Client development and help us recoup the small fortune we've spent, please check out our donations page. Mysteries of Azeroth is free to play and enjoy, and none of our progress would be possible without your support.

## Post 98501 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2024-07-04T04:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98501#p98501 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Frequently Asked Questions**

 ****Can my PC run it?****
Yes! We're optimizing UE5 for Legacy Mode (we're even modifying the engine render code) so 99.9% of current players will be able to run at least Legacy Mode.

 ****Does this mean Turtle is going past vanilla+ into newer expansions?****
No, we'll be staying on Mysteries of Azeroth!

 ****When will this be playable?****2025.

 ****Will we still be able to play on the old client?****There will be a transitional period where you can play with both the old and new clients. Eventually, the new client will become necessary. The old client limits us, but we don't want to force a rapid transition. We aim to ensure the new client works well for everyone before ending support for the old client.

 ****What accessibility options will the client have?****Note: Accessibility options will come after the release.
- 1. Text-to-Speech support for important parts of the game (quest details, quest log, inventory, chat, character information, etc.), plus a system to help blind players understand their surroundings.

- 2. Adjustable font size for all important UI elements.

- 3. Text indicators for item quality and enemy aggressiveness instead of color.

- 4. Speech support for chat input (this is tentative).
 - 4.1. Speech control for character actions (challenging but beneficial for players with disabilities).

 ****Will it be on consoles?****Microsoft, Sony, or Nintendo are unlikely to approve a project like this. The Nintendo Switch might be an option someday.

 ****Will there be crossplay between old and new clients?****Yes, during the transition period. Eventually, we will only support the TW2 client.

 ****Can I use this to play on other servers?****No. This client will only be able to connect to the Turtle WoW network.

 ****Does it run on Mac?****Eventually, if there is a demand, we will try to provide an official port. Currently, only via emulation software.

 ****Does it run on Linux?****Eventually, if there is a demand, we will try to provide an official port. Currently, only via emulation software.

 ****Is it on iOS or just Android?****Just Android. iOS has a tightly controlled ecosystem which is not suitable for our project. If Apple allowed anonymous app creation, we could try.

 ****Will I be able to toggle different graphics between Modern Mode and Legacy Mode?****Yes, you can switch in the options. Graphics options will differ for Legacy and Modern Mode. Some features will be exclusive to each mode (e.g., Lumen will only be in Modern Mode).

 ****Will my graphical mod still work?****Mod support will come after release. On release, there will be no custom mod support, however it is planned. Previous mods will be incompatible with the new client, and MPQ files won't be recognized.

 ****How will modding work?****Instead of MPQ, there will be PAK files. You can modify files similarly to MPQ, but not all files are replaceable. Some files, like FrameXML content, will be protected. We'll provide instructions for creating 3D assets or textures for the new client later.

 ****Will there be other graphics modes beyond Legacy/Modern?****No. While there will be numerous options to tweak the modes to your personal taste, it will only be either Legacy or Modern.

 ****Will it come with a launcher?****We'll provide details later.

 ****Will there be updates after launch?****Of course! We will have ongoing updates for the TW2 client, extending long past beta and release.

 ****Will there be new addon/mod types?****Addons will remain the same, even the folder structures. Mods, however, will change (see above).

 ****What APIs will there be?****1.12.* Addon API. We have ideas for more API layers, but that will come after release.

 ****Will I be able to move my settings and ability layout from the old client to the new client?****Yes! On the first launch, the new client can import all your addons, keybinds, and settings from the old client. You'll get a dialog box that guides you through the process.

 ****Is there any possibility that this new client can support addons from other versions of WoW, such as Classic?****We have ideas about API Layers, but this will come after the release. At launch, it will support the 1.12.1 API.
