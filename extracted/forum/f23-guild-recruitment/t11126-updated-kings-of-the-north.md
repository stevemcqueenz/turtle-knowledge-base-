## Post 73209 by Angryelf0101 — 2023-10-24T00:53:00Z

**Registration for the Kings of the North guild is now open! We need more than 10 signatures to start things up.**

We are looking to be the main guild for the lands of the north and to spread the word about the greatness of this game. (Plus, to have something to do in the winter season, it can get very very cold...)
Quite simply put, we're focused on being the best Turtle WoW guild in a part of the world where the aurora borealis shines beautifully and the sun shines all night in the summer months, where happy pop music and the soothing relaxation of death metal collide (with a dash of electronic music), and where 40 centimeters of snow can fall in one day (because who doesn't like that?).
The primary language of conversation is English for our guild as this is the common language most of us speak, but each country's individual language(s) are also allowed; we abide by the main rules and basic guidelines of a Turtle WoW guild ***and we will ban anyone who violates the basic rules of Turtle WoW and chat discussion in general***. Discussions about in-game matters are allowed, as are off-topic stuff such as weather, sports, languages, music, and so on.
(We don't have Discord. In-game chat is enough as it is, you know? If someone wants to create their own Discord for their group, this is fine, but we don't require people to have Discord accounts to sign up; it can be a barrier and we'd be willing to remove that barrier, simple as that.)

 **Well, what classifies as north?**

This is quite a question, but simply any place north of the equator that consistently dips below 0 degrees Celsius in terms of temperature in winter! This allows for a 24-hour operating guild that spreads from as far east as Japan and South Korea, through Europe (the Nordics, Baltics, and south towards the Alps), and into Canada and the United States on the western side of the world map. The main guild times will be monitored through a survey, so that we can update this forum and give you the best possible time to join in. Of course, the main time of the year would be winter because most of us are in our homes during the evenings and we can band together, regularly, to reach level 60 in the months ahead.

Simply PM me if you want to join :) Good luck on your adventure in Turtle WoW!
