Greetings!

We are <Dungeons and Drunkards>, a laid back guild that is made for those who want to enjoy all that Turtle WoW has to offer, but also understand that real life takes priority. We are more about the journey than the destination. We are just starting to dip our toes into the raiding world and looking for more like minded folks to join us.

We don't care about your race/class combo, we don't care if you min/max or just play whatever. If you want to get better, we can help! New to TWoW or WoW in general? That is awesome! Welcome! We welcome all levels and skill.

You want to just hang out with some chill folks, drink the night away in various dungeons we are here for you! There is always room at the Bar, and a flagon of mead chilled.

If what you read interests you then reach on out! Look for any of the following folks: Tiladon, Kaviir, Taves, Tinytorch, Rockzo, Tellon, or just scream in world chat, we usually have someone sober enough to get you in.
