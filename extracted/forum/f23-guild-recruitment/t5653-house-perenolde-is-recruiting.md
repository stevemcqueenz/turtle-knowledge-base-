 **<House Perenolde>** is a casual RP and leveling guild focusing on characters connected to the former Kingdom of Alterac.

 **Races:** All welcome (former Alterac citizens and mercenary forces)

 **Classes:** All, but Paladins with backstories tying them closely to the Knights of the Silver Hand will only be admitted on a case by case basis (due to their role in the occupation of Alterac).

 **About us:** The guild's core group joined in August of last year. We have a mix of long-time and newbie players of WoW. We're interested in seeing everything Turtlewow has to offer but aren't interested in hurrying our way through content.

 **Contacts:** Kalimas (GM), Gerak (Mercenary Captain), Skylandra
