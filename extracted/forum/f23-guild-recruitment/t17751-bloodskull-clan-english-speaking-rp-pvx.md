Join the Bloodskull Clan!

"No skull uncleaved, no foe uncrushed!"

The Bloodskull Clan is a brutal and battle-hardened warband of Chaos Orcs that is a fel horde themed and demon worshipping guild. We are bound by fel, strength, and the relentless pursuit of victory. We are warriors, conquerors, and champions forging our path in blood and steel. Whether you are a veteran of countless wars or a fresh recruit seeking glory, there is a place for you among us.

Who We Are:
![🔥](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f525.svg) An Orcish Clan themed guild focused on immersive roleplay and the celebration of orcs in fantasy.
![⚒️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2692.svg) Rooted in strength, discipline, and loyalty, we honor our bloodthirst and pride while carving our legacy in the flesh of our enemies.
![💀](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f480.svg) We embrace the true warrior’s way in welcoming those orcs who seek the highest of strength by drinking a demon's blood- among those brave enough are grizzled veterans, ruthless raiders, fierce hunters, and dark shamans.

Who We Seek:
![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) Power hungry Orcs, mercenaries, and battle-hardened champions eager to fight under the banner of the Bloodskull Clan. Non-orcs enthusiastic about the Fel will be considered on a case-by-case basis.
![🔥](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f525.svg) New blood looking to prove themselves in the fires of war.
![🩸](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1fa78.svg) Loyalists who value fel power, carnage, and the thrill of combat.

What We Offer:
![⚔️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2694.svg) Epic Roleplay Events – From brutal duels to war councils and grand campaigns, and more to come!
![💪](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4aa.svg) Clan Rankings & Trials – Rise through the ranks by proving your strength and by hard trials that proves your worth.
![🍻](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f37b.svg) Strong Kinship– We fight, feast, and create our legacies together.
