Shadowblade is Recruiting!

Shadowblade is a mixed faction, casual but progression-focused guild on Turtle WoW. We clear ZG, Kara10, AQ20, and Ony weekly and are preparing to start Molten Core in March. We’re looking for more players to join us as we push further into endgame content.

We raid Thursday, Friday, and Saturday at 6 PM server time, and may add Sunday as we progress further. Our loot system is soft reserve but with a relaxed, fair approach—our goal is to gear up together and keep things fun.

We welcome all classes and roles! If you’re looking for a guild that progresses at a steady pace, runs dungeons together, and helps each other out—all while keeping the vibes light—Shadowblade might be a great fit for you. We enjoy a good laugh, but we also get things done—no drama, just solid teamwork. If you enjoy some good banter while slaying bosses, you'll fit right in.

Raid Schedule:

Thursday / Friday / Saturday – 6 PM Server Time
Sunday – May be added as we progress further

If you’re interested, message us in-game and ask for an officer or reply here!
