**<Lost in Auberdine>** is currently recruting **all classes and roles** to fill our core raid roster for fresh progression raiding.

 **About Us:**
Guild was formed with an intention to create a social and casual community consisted of beginners and more advanced players with a goal of progressively clearing raids. We decided to create a team from leveling and bring the same players into raids in order to experience the true Vanilla style gameplay!

 **What We Offer:**
 - A friendly and social community: For sure we can offer players a good laugh and fun while progressively expanding their journey through the game.
 - Supportive environment: More advanced people will be looking to constantly help other guild members in questing, gearing or simply helping out with game knowledge.
 - Casual fresh raiding experience: We will be looking to raid twice a week; Saturday 21:00 ST (Server Time), Thursday  20:30 ST (Server Time).
 - We will be using SR>MS>OS loot rules for item distribution.

 **For a fresh raid progression roster we are looking to fill the following positions:**

 **TANK**
 *none at the moment*

 **HEAL**
 - Holy Paladin
 - Resto Shaman
 - Resto Druid

 **DPS**
 - Fury Warrior
 - Feral  / Balance Druid
 - Hunter
 - Warlock
 - Elemental / Enhancement Shaman

Please **join our discord for more answers**, details or guild invite.
