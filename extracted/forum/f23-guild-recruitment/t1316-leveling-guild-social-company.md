Dear fellow Travellers

I am new on this wonderful server and I did not get a whole lot of response when I asked about social leveling guilds. I also know that many people are who do not want to be in a guild. So I decided to make a guild that might lure in both types.

<Traveller> was the most neutral name I could come up with. There is no ranking system, everyone is rank Traveller. Everyone can invite and nothing is expected of you. It is more of a sorts of second worldchat, for leveling people in need of quest or dungeon help.

Are you levelling an alt or new to the server, feel free to whisper anyone in the guild, and ask for an invite.

Have a nice journey to 60!

Kind regards

Human Mage Fraus
