About Us

 **<<I Lion’s Pride I>>** is a Social + PvE guild on Ambershire. We focus on creating a reliable and active community where players can enjoy the game together: *leveling, running dungeons, supporting each other with crafting and resources, and later stepping into raid content*.

**Our guild is currently looking for an experienced Raid Leader** (RL).

We are an active Turtle WoW guild and we want to start raiding actively. To make this possible, we are opening a position for a dedicated and reliable RL who can organize and lead our guild raids.

What we expect from an RL:
 - Proven experience leading raids in Vanilla / Turtle WoW.
 - Solid knowledge of raid tactics and class mechanics.
 - Ability to manage and coordinate up to 40 (?) players.
 - Responsibility, patience, and fairness when it comes to loot and discipline.
 - Good communication skills (voice or text).

What we offer as a guild:
 - Full support from the guild leadership.
 - Dedicated officer roles for all needs.
 - Suggested loot distribution systems (final choice will be agreed together).
 - A friendly and stable community that values teamwork over drama.

If you are an experienced Raid Leader looking for a new home where your efforts will be respected and supported - we would love to hear from you.

ALSO!!!!!!
Our guild is looking for active Officers.

Main condition to become an Officer:
 - You should already be someone who is noticed in guild chat - active, friendly, and recognized by other members.

What an Officer does:
 - Help guildmates with questions and basic information.
 - Keep the chat clean and friendly: mute or warn players who say offensive or rude things.

Requirements:
 - Desire to maintain a positive atmosphere in guild chat.
 - Willingness to help new or less experienced players.
 - Respectful and patient communication.
 - Frequent online presence is not required - attitude and willingness are more important.
