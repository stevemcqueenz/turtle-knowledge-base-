**W A N D E R L U S T**
A great desire to travel

If you're seeking a warm and friendly guild to embark on epic journeys with, look no further. Wanderlust is a diverse group of gamers who share a passion for exploration, raiding, and all things World of Warcraft. Whether you're a seasoned veteran or a fresh-faced newcomer, our doors are open to all who seek a welcoming, relaxed and enjoyable gaming experience.

 **About Us:**
Wanderlust was formed with one goal in mind - to recapture that precious vanilla experience of a cozy, inclusive community exploring the world together. We value friendship and mutual respect above all else, ensuring that every member feels valued and supported, regardless of their station in life, experience, or skill level.

 **What We Offer:**
 - A Friendly Community: Join a group of diverse and approachable players from all over the world who share a passion for the game and love making new friends
 - Raiding Made Fun: Enjoy raiding in a stress-free atmosphere, where learning and improvement go hand in hand. We raid Thursdays at 8pm EST and Saturdays at 2pm EST and have a 2 SR MS > OS loot system
 - Supportive Environment: We're here to help you achieve your goals, whether it's conquering that dungeon boss or mastering a new class with a multitude of resources specific to Turtle WoW
 - Guild Discord: Stay connected with the community even when you're not in the game with a screenshots channel, class help guides, roleplay sections, voice chat and memes

 **Joining Requirements:**
We believe that the right attitude matters more than experience or gear. If you have a passion for exploration, a desire to learn, and a commitment to an inclusive environment, our guild might be a fit for you. We require that our members are adults who read and follow the Turtle WoW Rules with an expectation of kindness and LGBT+ inclusivity. If you're ready to embark on an exciting journey with Wanderlust, reach out to any of our officers in-game. Code: Select all

```
Eristhel, Kadi, Morrigen, Ankleshooter, Gravepain, Siha, Seselis
```

May the winds of Azeroth carry you to adventure!
