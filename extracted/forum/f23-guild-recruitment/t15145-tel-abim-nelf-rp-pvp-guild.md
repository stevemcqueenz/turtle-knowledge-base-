# Tel'Abim NElf RP-PvP Guild

## Post 102895 by Maaliss — 2024-10-11T10:16:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102895#p102895 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So I'm new to the server and thinking of starting the above.

When I played back in Vanilla I was part of a guild on the Defias Brotherhood RP-PvP server when it first opened. The guild was called the Starseeker Sentinels.

For those familiar with Nordanaars Nightsong Sentinels the premise of the Starseeker Sentinels was to actively defend Night Elf lands, particularly Ashenvale and Darkshore.

The thought behind this guild will be one borne from the aftermath of Cenarius' demise to the Orcs with an outright hatred towards the horde and their defilement of Ashenvales forests and those living in it or buried in it.

Initially I'm thinking of this being a BG (particularly WSG) and WorldPvP guild but if the numbers are high enough there could be raiding down the line in conjunction with other guilds for class balance.

Anyway, this post is simply a notice of interest.

Let me know here, TurtleWow Discord or ingame if you are interested.

The thing is Arcanex, the server may be mainly Alliance but that isn't reflected in terms of pvp presence and I think that should change.

I also feel that if we had more Thematic Guilds on either side it would encourage the other side to respond.

I remember back in Vanilla 2005 the only RP servers where PvE and certainly on the EU servers where I was people were complaining that Blueshielding was taking away from the RP-immersion because there was no "danger" in questing in contested lands or consequences for attacking an Orc/Nelf mob right in front of a player from the opposite faction.

When the RP-PvP servers opened the RP was awesome. Lots of thematic guilds on both sides levelling, engaging in spontaneous and organised world PvP. Even BGs where RPd to a degree.

I know I'm only new on TelAbim but I would love to see that "RP environment" return.

Heck, for anyone that was playing on Defias Brotherhood server back on 2006/07 I was the one that proposed the Anniversary War where for a week each year all raiding etc was paused and all guilds took part in full-scale World PvP. It was awesome.
