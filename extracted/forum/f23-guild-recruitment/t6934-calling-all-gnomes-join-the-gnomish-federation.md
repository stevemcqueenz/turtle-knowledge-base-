 *Calling all Gnomes! Following the defeat of our people in Gnomeregan and the loss of our capital city, the **<Gnomish Federation>** has been established as a centralized government for the Gnomish people. We will strive to bring all Gnomes together in our fight to reclaim our capital city. Join today!*

 **Guild info**
The **<Gnomish Federation>** is a casual RP/PvE guild who's directive is to secure and advance the interests of the Gnomes. Members are not required to RP, but it is highly encouraged. We seek to be compatible with RPers, hardcore raiders, and even PvPers. Our main focus is to be a tight-knit group of dedicated players that make the server a more enjoyable place.

Given our lore, all members must be Gnome, with some exceptions for certain members down the road.

A huge Thank You  to all supporters of the Federation who have so far reached out to offer support in any way

 **Our mission**
We are focused on two goals: the reclamation of our capital city Gnomeregan, and the peace and security of Azeroth. Our standing with the Alliance is of the utmost importance to the Federation, and we are as equally determined to further its cause as we are our own.

 **Structure**
- **King of Gnomes** - Geblin Mekkatorque
Following the invasion of Gnomeregan by the Troggs, **High Tinker** Geblin Mekkatorque was persuaded to release toxic radiation throughout the capital city in order to eradicate the Troggs. This backfired and caused the loss of Gnome life in great numbers and forced Gnomes to abandon Gnomeregan.

The King of Gnomes now resides in Tinker Town, in Ironforge, dictating the Gnomes as they seek to take back their homeland.

- **Governor** - Oddzag Wirefizz
An engineer by trade, Oddzag Wirefizz was appointed to the governorship of the Federation by **High Tinker** Mekkatorque at its founding shortly after the Gnomes established Tinker Town. Oddzag has long been a member of reconnaissance organizations within Gnomish society.

- **Founder**
A largely sentimental title, Founders are original signers of the Federation's charter, who signed with authority on behalf of their race to lend legitimacy to the Federation's cause. They serve as representatives to the Federation from their race's governments and are forever praised for their belief in the cause of Gnomish security.

- Executive:
All matters of Federation business, from citizen affairs, interdepartmental matters, leadership tasks, etc are carried out by Federation Executives. Hand chosen by the Governor and the Department Heads, they serve in both the back and front of house, conducting high-level assignments that involve all levels of the Federation and Azeroth's affairs.

 *For more guild information, check out the wiki page <https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Gnomish_Federation>*

 **Federation Departments**

All Gnomes within the Federation are sorted into their respective department based on his or her specialty. To view a member's title within that department, check their public note. Department titles are chosen by each member, as they know the best role they're suited for.

- **Department of State**:
Responsible for the foreign policy and relations of the Gnomish people, the Dept of State handles all interactions with leaders of the Alliance, as well as with neutral factions and even the Horde.

- **Department of Defense**:
The Federation Department of Defense is tasked with the security of the Gnomish people. Entrusted with maintaining the Gnomish military, they also work hand-in-hand with the Dept of Intel.

- **Department of Intel**:
All Gnomish intelligence and covert operations are executed by the Department of Intel. While strictly confidential, the Dept of Intel has established itself amongst the Alliance as competent and highly valuable in the war against the Horde.

- **Department of Engineering**:
Most technical aspects of Gnomish society are maintained by the Department of Engineering; transport, architecture, civil engineering, etc. Engineering is a revered aspect of Gnomish society and as such, the department is well regarded.
