Hello fellow turtles,

We are a party of three (warrior, shaman and priest) at low level 50s. We are looking for two more people to consistently start farming dungeons on Fridays and weekends. Our goal is to make sure that our 5-man party will be fully pre-bis slotted and raid ready once we are done with our dungeon farming.

Our warrior is tanking and our priest is healing but if a tank or healer want to join us they are willing to dps as well. Our shaman also always has some tents ready for that juicy xp bonus.

Due to the presence of windfury totem melee cleaves can be facilitated but we are looking more for cool people than specific classes! Also, all experience levels are welcome we are no hardcore experts ourselves and we will not flame anyone for making mistakes (mostly because we might make a ''couple'' ourselves)

Our (loosely set) plan for entering dungeons is as follows:

Levels 50-53: Maraudon (including scepter run for all members obviously), Sunken temple (we want to organize a full run mostly for the unique class quests).
Levels 53-57:  BRD prison and arena runs. Supplemented with occasional Mara/ST/ZF so we won't die of repetition boredom.
Level 57-58: Two full BRD runs for quest lines to be completed (that princess won't save herself).
Level 58-60: Scholomence, Stratholme and Dire Maul (quest for keys as well).
Level 60: Runs for all attunements (that do not need entering a raid). Also, runs for specific dungeons that are needed for specific pre-bis items.

So, if you are interested in joining us, just leave a comment or send me a private message in my forum account!

P.S. If more than 2 people are interested, time availability will be prioritized, but in general whoever does not make it to the main party will always be dmed to join in case someone is missing! Also, if enough people are interested, we can always arrange for a second party to be made.
