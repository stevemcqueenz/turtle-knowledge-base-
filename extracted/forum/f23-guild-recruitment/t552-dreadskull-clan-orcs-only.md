~ Ghrakaz the Hollowed

![Image](https://i.imgur.com/gpwFrXO.png)
The Dreadskull clan was formed shortly after the end of the Third War, when the Orcs escaped the Eastern Kingdoms to Kalimdor. There, a group of orcs under the leadership of Grom journeyed into Ashenvale and found themselves under attack by Night Elven forces. With no alternatives, Grom and his orcs drank from the corrupted pool of Mannoroth's blood, gaining the strength necessary to overcome the Night Elves but at the cost of their own sanity. These orcs succumbed to the blood lust which had once fueled their wars across Azeroth. However, when Grom slayed Mannoroth the orcs were released from the blood curse, and swore vengeance on the demons that had enslaved them. Faced with their demonic master who had subjugated them, the doomguard Xhak'ariz, these noble orcs defeated the demon and claimed its head as a trophy. That day the orcs formed a clan to commemorate their victory and seal the bonds they shared on the battlefield. This clan would come to be known as the Dreadskull Clan, named after the skull of the demon they slayed, Xhak'ariz. They would carry the dreadskull into each battle, a warning to those who stood against them.

Open to orcs only. Open to all classes. Roleplayers are welcome, but not required. All levels and activity are welcome as well. Alts and mains are welcomed equally,

Whisper any member ingame to join!
