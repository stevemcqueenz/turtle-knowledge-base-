Greetings!  satisfied_turtle

We are going to rename the old Guild ''La Vieja Escuela'' because we are few Hispanics in the server and it is not worth keeping it that way. In the time that the old Guild has lasted we have lost many comrades in the battlefield, we became a group of 8 facing everything that was put ahead. Brave warriors like Asavar and Shirafune lost their lives against the union hosted in the Alterac Mountains. Also the picara Stephany who disappeared into the shadows in a demonic mission. We could not dissolve the guild after these heroic deaths, that's why we decided to keep this old brotherhood. We have simply translated the old name by the same name in English. Thus, the great stories about our most precious warriors will remain alive in the blood of all our brothers.

We do not want to limit our community so this guild will be international for people who love ''player vs environment''. That is, we will look for adventurers who want to enjoy all the Azeroth content. We focus on the PvE (dungeons, search for elite rares, elite missions, etc). We will keep the peace treaties with the Horde unless they fail to comply with established norms. If this were to happen we would be forced to attack camps of the horde and not leave anyone alive.

I have forgotten to present to those responsible for this release, the Guild Master Justice and the Perroviejo commander, whose stories will be added in the future ..
