Who are <Nephilim>

Nephilim (PVE/PVP) is started up by Morhax/Hornata and Nivea (brothers).
We have established our place on Horde side (Horde only guild).
We are a group of players that learnt to know each other somewhat here on TWoW that are active and have close to jokes when opportunity gives.
We focus alot on the fun aspect, not necessarily the speed of things.We don't expect anyone to be the best, actually, if this first time playing.You will probably have most fun, since able to see all for first time.

Be it social or want to be part of the "core" all are welcome no matter lvl.
No lvl or skill required for ranks, be a nice dude/dudette.

We all have different backgrounds, some only played Vanilla, some only retail.
Some with history as casuals and some as hardcore.

For some of us.The difference from then to now, is that we are all older and have families, but the passion remains.

Our aim

Our aim is to do what we love, raid, kill alliance and get loot - our focus will be high & "hardcore" while our schedule will be low and casual, this assists us all to have our family time, work and live outside of TWoW.
For info and/or invite, contact anyone in Nephilim/Cheers

Most members live in EU.
But all are welcome.

Guild chat and discord are friendly for all ages.
