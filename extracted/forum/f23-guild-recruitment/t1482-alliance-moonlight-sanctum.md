# [Alliance] <Moonlight Sanctum>

## Post 8822 by Shamballah — 2020-12-30T20:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8822#p8822 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

> Shades of the moon will last till it's warriors dare to fight United under the blessing of the great Goddess of Night.
>  Rest your path, and cure your illnesses through rivers of unaltered brotherhood.
>  Join the << Moonlight Sanctum >>

Moonlight Sanctum is a pure Cult of the Elune Goddess.

We will be making rituals to keep the balance of universe,
And entry rituals to purify the Soul and Body of those who join us.

Once the soul is pure, we can claim our unity,
Unite us all under the Moon shades,
And declare that we are prepared for the Fight.

 - The Fisrt Ceremonies of Elune will happen in the Sanctuary of Aessina in Ashenvale.
Wich will be the first and Main basement of <Moonlight Sanctum>

 ***

< Moonlight Sanctum >

Chart

 - Be free to live your real life first , you will not be condamned or excluded.
 - Make this place a safe Haven, be loyal to the Clan, as we will travel together.
 - RP and Well Spoken poetry is encouraged, not mandatory.
 - No rush

At the moment The < Moonlight Sanctum > will recruit around lvl 15 and under.
So all the members in that range goes together through the game and xp part.

Everyone is welcome, so we enjoy the ride.

 ***

Organisation of the Guild

Hierarchy

the Head will be a Triangle between :

 - The RP Lead and Storytelling of our Adventure ( Shamballah )
 - The PVE Lead , someone experienced with Raids
 - The PVP Lead could be me Also, but I will put an officer in charge of PVP

Then comes the Highest Circle of the Guild, The Circle of Elune.
With various Engaged & Loyal members into our conquest & story.

The High Circle of Elune will form a Council,
The Council of Wisdom that will be consulted for any inquiries, and charged of the Moon Cult.

 ***

 - The Theme is based on the Elune Goddess Sacrements, but all races (other than Night Elves) will be welcome,
Only after they accepted to vow allegiance to Elune Goddess.
( Because I the great Priestess Decided that we shall open to any soul in seek of meaning )

For the RP of our guild, it will be based on two points :
 - Your Own storytelling , Poetry , and Character.
 - The Moon Cult, which we will Celebrate every New Moons and Full Moons.
We Will gather in different places of the game at these times, that we consider sacred.

 - I will be the High Priestess in Charge of RP Missions, I will assign you and some members to a RP mission.
To serve our cause, and create a Kingdom or Secret Society. So we will convert people who just farm on a normal day, and ask them what are their beliefs, if they know the mysteries of the Great Goddess, and if they want to vow allegiance to her.

 - I Will assign RP missions to group of explorers between 3 to 5 people, to explore the map of Azeroth & Kalimdor, to search for Sacred places for further rituals, or Secret Bases we could invest.

 - We can too, offer Rituals of Elune to other guilds in search of sacraments, it's one of our missions.
( So other RP guilds will be free to contact us for merged events )

 ***

In the end, the guild is open to :

 - Dreamers of all kinds
 - Mature and Heartful peoples
 - Role-Play Poets & Noble warriors of all kinds
 - People actualy leveling Around lvl 15 and Under
 - Casual players having a life beside the game
 - Hardcore Gamers who want to help to structure the PVE advancements of the guild
( If they are willing to, So they open the gates to other players of the guild who play less )
 - PVP freaks of all kinds , thirsty of ferocious organized battles.

 - And a good healthy distance reminding us that we are here to enjoy our time, no rush.

For Joining /w ( Shamballah ) in game.

Blessing of Elune be with you.
