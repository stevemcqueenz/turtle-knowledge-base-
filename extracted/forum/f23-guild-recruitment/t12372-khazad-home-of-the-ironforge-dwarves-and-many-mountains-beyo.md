![🪓](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1fa93.svg) **<Khazad>** ![🪓](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1fa93.svg)
We are a band of mountain kin over many mountains yonder, gathered under the hearth of Ironforge. A calm, relaxed PvE progression guild comprised of dwarves, we wholeheartedly accept dwarves of any region, any level, and of any height, axe sharpness, or beard length!

What our guild seeks to offer for our kin:
 - A sociable, friendly, and helpful guild to dungeon, quest, or raid with in the future!
 - A comfortable environment for new dwarf players in TWoW to inquire and learn about from our experienced members!
 - Gatherings around the Dun Morogh inns to glug and chug ale and dance to our dwarven heart's content!
 - Just relax, banter, and ponder around on many dwarven genres like Lord of the Rings!

As we grow, we are open to any feedback that can improve the guild in terms of management, events, and etc!
