> *S&S— a family built on loyalty, profit, and the thrill of shared business and adventure. These principles guide how we treat each other, how we hustle, and how we represent our family across Azeroth and beyond.*

---
 **About Us**
The S&S Cartel ain’t just a guild — it’s a family. Founded by goblins, run like a business, open to anyone expected undeads ready to hustle.
At the top sits **Arcanzap**, the Big Boss. Around him stand his Right Hand, the Master of Coin **ShadyTom**, and the Schemers who spin the plans. Beneath them? Fixers, Smugglers, Bruisers, Cutpurses, and Mercenary — every role with its purpose, every fella with their cut. Even the Unpaid Interns start somewhere.
We will raid, we already  craft, we trade, we hustle. Gold flows, respect is earned, and no fella stands alone. Join us, and you don’t just wear a tag — **you wear the ring of family.**

---
 **Our rules**
 **1.![👨‍👩‍👧‍👦](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f468-200d-1f469-200d-1f467-200d-1f466.svg)  Family Before Fortune**
We are only as strong as our weakest fella. No one’s left behind. You help, you teach, you back up your brothers and sisters. Dungeon, deal, or duel — we rise together, or we don’t rise at all.
 **2.![🫡](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1fae1.svg)  Keep the Streets Clean**
Drama? That’s bad business. Leave your ego at the door, fellas. Inside the Cartel, we solve problems with respect, not noise. We keep the peace so the gold keeps flowing.
 **3.![🤜](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f91c.svg) ![🤛](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f91b.svg)  Respect Is Non-Negotiable**
Every member, every ally — treated with dignity. Doesn’t matter race, class, or background — if you wear the ring, you’re family. Betrayal? That’s a different story. But until then, you show respect, you get respect.
 **4.![🤫](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f92b.svg) Don’t Tarnish the Ring**
Your moves outside reflect on all of us. When you’re in the streets of Azeroth, you carry our name. Be sharp, be solid, and let outsiders see we’re a crew worth trusting — or fearing.
 **5.![📰](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4f0.svg)  Azeroth Is the Stage, Not the News**
We don’t drag real-world squabbles into our streets. Politics, religion, nonsense? Nah. In Cartel chat, it’s business, pleasure, and adventure — nothing else.
 **5½. ![💵](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4b5.svg) Gold and Craft Keep the Engine Running**
Professions are sacred. The Cartel thrives on the grind of miners, alchemists, engineers, and merchants. You pull your weight, you share the gains, you get your cut. Remember: loyalty and gold grease the gears.
