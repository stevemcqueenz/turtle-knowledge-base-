## Post 80991 by Vimgreen — 2023-12-13T08:40:00Z

![🐗](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f417.svg) **<Dancing Boars>** are looking for more raiders! ![🐗](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f417.svg)
![Image](https://i.ibb.co/2kBgJSW/Yn-Ft-Jjj-1.png)
We are mainly a raiding guild but more casual players are also welcome as many of our members still participate in PvP, other PvE content and even RP. We enthusiastically welcome those eager to learn. We currently clear MC, ES HM, BWL and AQ40 and will in a month or two go into naxx when people have amassed their gear from this tier.

We do like to have fun so we banter during raids, and even embrace dark humour without unnecessary sensitivity. Intention matters more than words alone, so naturally, we still have a strict stance against genuine racism, sexism, and homophobia etc. We are quite loud during raids as we know when focus is needed, and we can pull our own weight. Loot system is based on tokens, whereby raiders get an equal amount of tokens each phase.  And a diminishing return based rolling system for BiS items that have not been tokened, only for raiders, based on attendance. This allows non-raiders to roll on most loot too. Promotions to raiders are evaluated every Wednesday, and with good attendance and attitude, takes a maximum of 2 weeks, often 1 week.

Most of our members are European, thus the raiding hours, but we welcome all nationalities. Pref 20+ in age.

 **Raiding schedule is as follows:**
Tue, thur and  Sun – 19.00-21.30 CET.
We are currently looking for DPS specifically warriors, ret paladin, feral druid and healers. But all classes are welcome!

We don’t do any silly application forms or interviews (this is a game, not a job), they don’t show us the real you, so pop us a DM and let’s see you in action!

Contact either: **Arkintos**, **Vithas**, **Stopfeed** **Ttinkk** or **Markikus** in-game or **adam_arkintos** on discord.

Our discord: <https://discord.gg/JNHCnhCGpY>

Hope to hear from you!
