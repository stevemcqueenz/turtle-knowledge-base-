The Whiteclaw clan was an orc clan that dwelled in eastern Frostfire Ridge. The Whiteclaws were once among the oldest and proudest of the orcish kin and such like their Frostwolf neighbors, the Whiteclaws had learned to hunt alongside the ceaseless winter’s apex predators.

Likewise they too refused to drink the blood of Mannoroth and paid the price with their own.

Decades later, Chieftain Vladbu the Restorer wrenches his clan from extinction and onto the path glory. Revering the elements and preserving the old ways of Draenor, the Whiteclaws call upon the disillusioned, the scattered, and all clanless kin across this new world. As the banner once lofted high above the ridges of Frostfire rises once more, the Chieftan’s ascending ambition cleaves destiny like an axe.
