defending the weak

engaged

● Level 10-29 PvP bracket eligible.
● Active participation in world PvP and dungeon groups
● TurtleRP
● Warmode active for world PvP

disciplined, and ready for war.

Climb the ranks. Stand toe‑to‑toe in a fight, test wild builds, be
ruthless and creative, and push each other to get better. We’re at our endgame: earn your place,
chase objectives, and believe you can be the best.

Join us in Booty Bay, the heart of our guild operations, and represent your faction in the
Accord! Whether you seek gold, glory, or just a good fight, **The *Eastern Trade Accord*** is the
place to prove yourself.
