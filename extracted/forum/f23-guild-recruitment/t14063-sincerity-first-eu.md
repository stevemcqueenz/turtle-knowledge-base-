# <Sincerity First> [EU]

## Post 95833 by Syndraela — 2024-05-27T19:16:00Z

**<Sincerity First>** is a casual raid-oriented guild with a focus on the journey, not the destination. Whether you're a veteran who has grown tired of the pump-race or someone relatively new who wants to enjoy the content as you progress through it, you may find a home here with us. All classes, specs and factions are welcome.

Region: Europe (CET)
Current Weekly Raid Schedule and projected maximum TTC:
 - BWL: Sat 19:30 CET up to 3.5h (main raid)
 - MC: Wed 19:30 CET up to 3h

 **How We Operate**
 - Honest and clear communication
 - Member-focused guild decisions through polls
 - Occasional community events such as screenshot contests
 - Additional small raids, dungeons, and other group activities organized by our members

 **Low-Pressure Raid Environment**
 - Play the class and spec you want to play!
 - Bringing yourself and a good attitude is all we ask
 - Consumables are neither a requirement nor forbidden.
 - Logs and meters are not used to judge our members or dictate value.
 - We take time to explain and discuss tactics to ensure everyone is on the same page.
 - Speed naturally improves with practice and experience.

 **The Present**
<Sincerity First> is still in the early stages of its slow & steady raiding-experience. Blackwing Lair is our current weekly spotlight, with Molten Core as our secondary and the other raids and dungeons run as needed for fun, gear and profit.

We are looking to fill our our sturdy core of adventurers in order to tackle larger challenges. Currently we are seeking more [Class], but anyone with the right attitude - even if not quite 60 yet - is welcome. If interested, you're welcome to peek at our guild discord or contact one of our council members for more information.

We look forward seeing you in-game soon!
