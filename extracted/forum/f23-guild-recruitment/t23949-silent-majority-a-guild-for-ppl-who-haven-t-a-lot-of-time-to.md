TL;DR is - I don't have a lot of time to play WoW and the social part of the game is usually dominated by those who can spend every possible moment to quickly level up to 60 then grind as many endgame raids as possible.

Ambershire is large and still fresh but over time every server faces this problem.

This has always been a problem with WoW, you get a fresh realm and a vast majority races with each other in a rushe to grind out to max-level then to grind out endgame.

So if you don't/can't/won't sacrifice every possible waking moment or if you start on a server N months too late you're facing a singleplayer campaign in a game world that wasn't designed for singleplayer.

 -

So a lot of people who are time constrained basically just give up on playing, despite being a silent majority, and the purpose of this guild is to sync up instead.

One idea is to set our own notes on what we plan to do next whenever we will have the time to login. Be it a quest/quest-chain/group-quest/dungeon/whatever.

That way others can sync up, for example you set your note to "looking to do Deadmines" and next time you login there'll be other guild members ready to do Deadmines with you.

If this doesn't work we will try other approaches.
