# [Concept] Elite Guild in TWoW.

## Post 132706 by zer0g3 — 2025-05-23T17:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132706#p132706 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings, Turtlers!

Let me present an idea I randomly conjured up just now just to see if it generates any interest.

 **Introduction** I've been playing on TWoW for a few months now and during my time here I've been continuously scouting the existing guilds on the server and I've noticed that there is no "True Elite" guild around. As far as I am aware at least.
Therefore I've decided to find out if there's any interest in establishing such.

 **Definition of Elite** First of all, let's define what Elite means. This term has become a bit of a taboo in the realms of World of Warcraft mainly due to the rampant "Elitism" going around in retail servers. Now, this here is not THAT.
Elite does not equal elitist. If you are picturing a bunch of assholes who think they are better than everyone else, measuring their d**ks in guild chat that's absolutely not the idea behind this concept. What is the idea then you ask?
The idea is to establish a guild that that is like a status symbol. A goal for players to strive towards. No alts or new players. Only T3+ geared cream of the crop characters/players who have put the time, sweat and grind into the game. Basically if you are in - you have walked the walk and earned that spot!

 **Core Values** **Aside from flexing status, the other core idea behind such guild is to have a relatively small, reliable and badass community where people want to play the game together. Where if you need help with anything (within reason) you can count that people will show up and you won't have to spam World despite being in a guild with 800 people. Respect, good vibes and general positive mental attitude are the other core values. I am not even going to go into 'skill', 'performance', etc. Because the gear requirements in itself will ensure people are experienced and know what they are doing. Anything further than that, such as competitiveness, peformance, etc could be discussed further down the line.**

With all that laid out. Let me know if you align with the above concept and want to be a founding member of such a guild.
