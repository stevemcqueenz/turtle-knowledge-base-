# [Ambershire] [EU] <Grimoire> social • leveling • light RP

## Post 172635 by Ctunder — 2026-03-26T22:33:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172635#p172635 | page 1 | era: post-1.18.1 -->

**<Grimoire>** is a community-first, social, and leveling guild with a focus on light RP and shared discovery. We aren't looking to fill a roster with hundreds of strangers; we are looking for a couple dozen souls to form a permanent, family-like core.

In a world obsessed with "the meta" and "efficiency," we choose a different path. We believe the best way to experience Azeroth is together—without the pressure to "just google it."

We're looking for:
- social players, who actually want to talk, bond and create new memories.
- anyone (new or veteran) who wants to learn the game by playing it, not by reading a guide.

We aren't promising raiding yet. Our goal is to build a foundation of trust and friendship first.
We’d rather have a solid team and no clear path yet than a full calendar and no one to talk to.

If this approach resonates with you, reply to this thread or seek us out in-game!
