# <Smokey Tokers> Chill & Friendly EU Raid Guild

## Post 103066 by Ellifta (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2024-10-14T17:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103066#p103066 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The ¤ Smokey Tokers ¤ Crew is a joyful bunch of peaceful sea dawgs, pub brawlers, brave adventurers, eager explorers, frameless visioners and freethinkers, brought together from all corners of Azeroth by their fascination of the flora created by the Great Mother Nature, especially the smokable herbs!

- ZG/AQ20/MC/Ony/KARA/BWL/ES/AQ40/NAXX on plunder.
World Bosses farming with Dragonwatch.
Kara40 9/9
Guild Vault.
Friendly & chill crew.
Guild Events.
Growing community.
Coolest rank system.
Full Weekly Content Clearing with Experienced Plunder Masters.
Plunder Distribution Scheme: Soft Reserve > Main Spec +1 > Off Spec, cumulative SR bonuses.
Raids take place around 19:00 Server Time (London Time)

Raid Schedule:

⌘ Sunday: Guild Event or Open
⌘ Monday: Open
⌘ Tuesday: Naxxramas
⌘ Wednesday: Kara40
⌘ Thursday: AQ40 + ES
⌘ Friday: Weekly Quest Runs
⌘ Saturday: BWL + MC

AQ20, Kara10 or MC extra on Open days at the request of members!

We are currently accepting everyone, but especially looking healers of any class, size and shape! Resto druids and shamans, Holy priests and paladins, tag along! Shamans of other spec are also welcome! We do not require any gearchecks for anything below AQ40, so if you would like to dip your toes and see the raiding atmosphere yourself but don’t have much experience or gear yet, don’t worry!

Our aim is to create a friendly and chill community that will welcome people with open arms while still clearing content at a respectable pace.

If this guild sparks your interest or you have any questions, feel free to reply to this post or whisper Ellinna or any other officer!
