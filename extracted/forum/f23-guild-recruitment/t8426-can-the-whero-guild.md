How to Form Your Own Guild in *World of Warcraft*
1. Create a new character **or** log into an existing one.
2. Go straight to **your capitol city**. (Ironforge, Thunder Bluff, et cetera.)
3. Once in a capitol city, **ask a guard** where you can find the **Guild Master & Vendor**
4. The guard will put a **waypoint** on your map and mini-map.  Go to it.  Once you arrive at your destination, the waypoint will disappear.
5. Talk to the local **Guild Master** at that location.  The Guild Master will give you two options.  Choose "**How do I form a guild?**" followed by "**Purchase a Guild Charter**", then **name your guild** anything you want.
6. You should now have a **Guild Charter** item in your inventory.  If you right-click it, you'll be presented with some options, but the one to focus on is "**Request Signature**".
7. You need **ten player signatures** to form your own guild.  To obtain a signature, **open the charter, left-click on a player, then click "Request Signature"**.  You **cannot** do this with NPCs or mobs, and of course, this **will not work** if the player you've selected is **already in a guild**.
8. At this point, you have multiple options for obtaining signatures:
 - You can politely ask players, either via Whisper (if you're Level 5 or higher) or public chat channels, to sign your charter and see if there's any interest.
 - You can walk up to random people and try to "**force**" them to sign your charter by clicking them, then clicking "Request Signature".
 - You can attempt to *bribe* people into signing your charter.  This usually works.
9. Once you obtain 10 signatures, **put the Guild Charter into your default backpack**, return to the Guild Master, go through the menus, and select "**Register a Guild Charter**".  You should now be part of the guild you just registered.

 
If you need help with operating and maintaining your new guild, don't hesitate to ask the forums.
