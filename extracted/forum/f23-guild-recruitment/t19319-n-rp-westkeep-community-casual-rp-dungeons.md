![⛰️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/26f0.svg) Who We Are:

In the wake of a tide-turned battle, a fractured handful of survivors—soldiers, wanderers, magi, and rogues alike—stood shoulder to shoulder. With loyalty born not from banners, but from shared survival, they set their sights northward. The bitter winds of Northrend call once again.

Westguard Keep, long battered and neglected, now stirs with life. We are the ones called to its walls—those with nothing left to lose, and everything yet to fight for. Builders, protectors, seekers of purpose.

We are assisting in reclaiming what was lost, defending it from the lurking cold and all that hides in its shadows, and carving our future…together.

![🛠️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f6e0.svg) What We Do:

Our mission is twofold:

Rebuild – Fortify and breathe life back into Westguard Keep, making it a home for the lost, the weary, and the bold.
Defend – Shield those within from undead remnants, hostile factions, and stranger things that whisper beyond the veil.
Expand - Taking in those who search for a home, a place of belonging, or a renewed purpose. Forging bonds with those around and spending idle time within the community bantering, exploring, engaging in tried-and-true tavern talks over drinks, and more.
![✨](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2728.svg) Who We Seek:

We are open to all walks of life, whether you’re:

A fresh character ready to find their place in the world…
Or a seasoned adventurer looking for a new cause or a fresh chapter.
We welcome the battle-hardened and the bright-eyed alike:

![🗡️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f5e1.svg) Warriors, Soldiers, and Guards – From grizzled veterans to green recruits, your blade has a place here.
![🔮](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f52e.svg) Magic Users & Scholars – Whether you wield the Light, Void, Arcane, Nature or something stranger, your talents are needed.
![🏹](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3f9.svg) Trackers, Pathfinders & Rangers – The frozen wilds of Northrend are no friend to the unprepared. As this is new territory for many of us, we need your skills to guide and guard!
![:male_detective:](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f575.svg) Rogues, Assassins, Mercenaries & Shadows – There’s work in the dark corners of the Keep and outside it’s walls for those with the stomach for it and aren’t afraid of infiltrating lines and doing the dirty work.
![🛠️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f6e0.svg) Tradesfolk, Artisans, Alchemists & Tinkerers and More! – A stronghold and community stands only with those who build and maintain it. Slice-of-life RP is valued and embraced.
