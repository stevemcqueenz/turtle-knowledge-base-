MOLTEN CORE SALVAGE OPS FAQ

 1) What Is The Guild And What Is It All About?
Molten Core Salvage Ops is a sociable guild who help each other with quests, elites and dungeons
 - We are Molten

 2) What Is Expected Of Guildies?
o Be excellent to each other
No really, be excellent to each other ;o)

o Roll /roll on all chests in groups (unless you say skip) and as a group, ensure the highest roller loots the chest before moving on

o Download the GroupCalendar Addon for the guild calendar and use/check it for events
[viewtopic.php?f=18&t=464](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=18&t=464)

o Treat each other with respect and courtesy including treating non-guildies exactly the same way

o Help each other with quests, elites and dungeons when you can

 3) Is There A Guildbank?
No, not really. But we do have Moltvault...

Moltvault is the guild's answer to a lack of guild banks in vanilla WoW
While it doesn't function exactly as a guild bank, it does offer some similar benefits
Currently Moltvault is a place guildies can mail items/gold to, for the benefit of all guildies
It is NOT storage, anything donated WILL be given out to guildies

Anything you send to Moltvault will be listed on the AH by Moltvault for 333 gold so guildies can browse the inventory at any time eg 24/7

To find the collection more easily, you can click a Category on the AH, then just sort the items by Most Recent Bid column and highest bid order
The Moltvault collection should show up as items priced 333 gold

If a guildie needs anything for their main char, all they have to do is whisper Vantrem, Sweetviolet or even Moltvault
Another way is to mail the request for the item

The item will then be pulled down from the Moltvault AH and will be traded near the Stormwind mailbox or can be mailed to the guildie if not needed urgently

Currently Moltvault is only giving to main chars so that it is fair to all guildies and so we don't run out of inventory too quickly

The inventory will consist of many varied types of items including greens, blues, purples, mats, crafted items, gear, weapons, potions, trade goods, reagents, crafting mats, food, drink, pretty much everything that might be needed by
guildies

Donations of items sent will be logged so that whoever gets the item will be told who donated it.

Some donations have even come from outside the guild!

 4) What Events Happen And When?

Currently the GroupCalendar addon is used to make and view events within the guild

Anyone can amke an event and currently some nights have been assigned to certain events

Tuesday nights are Alt Night which is a chance for guildies to dust off their alts and get them out levelling etc

Wednesday nights are Elite Quests Night and are for those Elite quests we all have stuck in our quest logs

Regarding dungeons this is something still in progress however it is likely that dungeon boosts will return and happen on Mondays

Normal dungeons will happen in future but need more guildies hitting levels 30-40 currently

As soon as we have full groups at the right levels, we can start to tackle the better dungeons and also start taking lower levels through them for decent drops

Dungeons possible for boosts currently are Deadmines, Wailing Caverns, Blackfathom Deeps and Shadowfang Keep

satisfied_turtle

This FAQ will grow and be amended as time goes on

Now get out there and remember to help people with Hogger ;o)

Howdy all,

Regarding the vault, including donations or needs . . .  Here are the different 'Molts' and what they typically collect, although, you can send anything to any Molt, and we will route it where it needs to go, but the more that goes to the right molt, the more we save on postage.

Moltvault - gear (greens, blues, and even some whites/greys like low level shoulders)
Moltbags - bags and cloth (she makes gear for the guild also)
Moltfood - food and also any food-ish items used for quests, like goretusk livers, murloc fins, etc
Moltreagents - herbs, reagents (like essences, motes, elements, etc)
Moltquest - quest items, like rethban/incendicite ores, darkshore groupers (fishing quest), etc
Moltmaterial - crafting materials (ores, leathers, cloth, scales, etc)
Moltstore - recipes, plans, patterns, books (Strong Anti-venom, Cooking training books, etc)
Moltbuffs - Potions, Scrolls, Armor kits
Moltenchant - Enchanting mats (not sure we need this toon)
Greenhills - STV Green Hills pages - we have tonnes, if you need, please let us know

Feel free to donate ANYTHING you don't need, want, or can't auction.  We take anything from anyone.  We regularly send gear out to lower level guildies, and if they don't need, they can just hit the return button (no cost to return).  If no one in the guild needs or wants particular gear, we just disenchant.

The guild makes use of everything.  When we get stuff no one needs or wants, we can sell it to help the cost of postage and also sometime buy special mats we need to make more stuff for the guild.  And stuff like cloth armor with strength or agility bonuses which no on needs, we turn them in to enchanting mats, and then enchant other gear that people do need.

If you need anything, you can send an in game mail to Moltvault, or ask when the officers are online, and we can check.  We have so much stuff, and its all from the guild members, for the guild members.
