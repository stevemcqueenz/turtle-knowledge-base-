Trom-Ka!

I joined Turtle WoW this year, and my last encounter with World of Warcraft was about 15 years ago. After such a long time, the game feels entirely new to me and remains incredibly appealing. I didn't rush to reach the maximum level; as a result, I now have over 1500 quests completed, which allowed me to achieve level 60 as an Orc Warrior specializing in tanking. I'm also a skilled blacksmith and miner, both at their highest levels.

I tried to fully enjoy the game, embracing challenges like Slow & Steady and Exhaustion. I approach the game as a way to relax after work, so I have no intention of dedicating a second shift to it just to gain something forcibly. If you're new, choose the Horde, and share my mindset, I warmly invite you to join the fun. I'm not interested in rushing anyone in the game, and I'm always ready to help.

I also encourage a bit of role-playing with your character. You can reach out to me in-game (whisper) or through the mailbox to Ugern, and I'm looking forward to getting to know you!
