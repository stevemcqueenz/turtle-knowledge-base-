# Casual, zone-based Guild?

## Post 4722 by Filthyjack — 2019-12-28T04:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4722#p4722 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hi, I would like to know if anybody here would be interested in creating a casual, zone based, progression focused, low-population guild of 4-10 members at max.

Zone based means that all members are bound to a certain zone and cannot progress farther until all
members agreed or a set timelimit is reached.
Everytime we enter a new zone, we talk about our goals and what we want to achieve, then we set a timelimit,
hours, days, whatever, and we play.

That has multiple benefits especially for low-pop servers;

 - It gives people alot of freedom and flexibility with their gametime since they cannot fall behind much anymore
 - **The world always feels alive since your guild members are all in the same zone**
 - People can help each other out instantly
 - Very relaxed enviroment and no pressure
 - **High guild bond**
 - You can play other alts when you have time or nobody is online

Anybody interested in starting one? 2-3 people would be nice and able to do dungeons.
We would choose a starting zone and could start right away.
No guild master. All equal members. And no memers or twitch-talk please, mostly mature (im 29 yrs).

Leave a post if u want, otherwise Ill find people in game.
