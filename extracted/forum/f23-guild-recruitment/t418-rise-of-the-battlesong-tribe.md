# Rise of the <Battlesong Tribe>

## Post 2137 by Pumpkinbot — 2019-02-20T16:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2137#p2137 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The **Battlesong Tribe** is a small tauren tribe that's become worried by the recent Alliance aggression, as well as the threats from beyond the two major faction. They've recently opened their doors to more than just tauren, accepting that we'll need all kinds to help beat back the threats presented to us.

Tahue Battlesong, the daughter of the current chieftain, acts as her father's voice, seeking out those interested in joining while he focuses on rallying others in tauren territory.

The **Path of Honor** is for those who wish to protect Horde lands from the encroaching Alliance. Help keep the kaldorei at bay in the Warsong Gulch, reclaim Alterac Valley from the dwarves, or simply roam Azeroth in defense of the weak.

The **Path of Glory** is for those who look beyond the squabbling of the two major powers to see what is a threat to all of Azeroth. The Dark Iron Dwarves of Blackrock Mountain have been restless, whispers of black dragons in the area have been spread around, and the threat of the Burning Legion and the Scourge is ever present.

The **Path of Wisdom** is for craftsmen, storytellers, and anyone else who wishes to aid the tribe without directly aiding in the conflicts. Not everyone is born to be a fighter, after all.

Fliers have been posted all over Orgrimmar and Thunder Bluff (and one or two around Brill), asking those interested to send mail to Tahue expressing their interest.
