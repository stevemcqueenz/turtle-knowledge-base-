**Hard Chores - Hardcore**
Hard Chores is a hardcore only guild recruiting exceptional players seeking a semi-hardcore raiding environment. We are currently farming high level dungeons and Ubrs in preparation for hardcore 60 release. We are looking for level 40+ players in both eu and na timezones to join our ranks. Be ready to down some bosses and get some loot.

 **Our Mission:**
Together we will strive to complete all challenges available in-game quickly and efficiently, while also maintaining an active and engaging environment. Constant improvement is the key to reaching these goals. Everyone should feel empowered to contribute to solving problems - be proactive about looking for solutions as you find issues. Every small improvement we can add to the team and will amount to a larger improvement.

 **The standards we hold our characters to:**
Bigwigs, Decursive, Raid farmes, twthreat must be installed and utilized to enhance performance.
Optimized itemization, Enchants, Dual spec, Discord voice. Always be prepared with the best possible consumables for your spec.
