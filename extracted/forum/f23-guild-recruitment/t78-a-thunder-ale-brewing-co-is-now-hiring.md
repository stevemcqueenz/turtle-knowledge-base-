**FAQ** - Some questions we've been asked in-game:

 **1) Where are you located?**

We are a North American guild. We accept all players but endgame raiding will be scheduled around North American time zones. We know some guilds don't disclose their intended raiding schedule to boost their numbers. We don't want EU players to invest time and energy in our guild only to find out they can't raid with us when they hit the level cap due to scheduling conflicts.

 **2) Is roleplay required?**

No. We encourage our members to roleplay as it adds an interesting (and often times comical) aspect to the same old routine gaemplay we've all experienced on other private servers. Our guild's theme as one of Azeroth's oldest breweries creates a lot of opportunities for players to develop their own backstories.

 **3) How experienced is the guild's leadership?**

We've run several guilds on multiple private servers. Some small guilds with less than 100 members and some with over 1000 active members (yes, on a private server). If you visit our guild's web site you'll see our guild has a well defined set of rules that we actively enforce along with an event calendar we use to schedule instances, raids, and server-wide events hosted by our guild.
