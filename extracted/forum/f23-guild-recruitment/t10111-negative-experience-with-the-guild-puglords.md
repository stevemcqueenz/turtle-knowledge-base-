I make this post to inform everyone about the happenings of yesterdays raid with the guild Puglords.

The raid started as expected and the raidleader JDO(Jaydeo) came leading the mc pugraid with his level 55 warlock to gear him up (as he mentioned during the raid multiple times).
After the first boss the situation was that noone was despelling and he seemed quite frustrated.
One player made a wrong statement and JDO rebuked him to shut his mouth in an unnecessary harsh manner.
The whole raid seemed paralized and quiet after this and people stopped joking arround and having a good time.
This negative athmosphere seemed to stretch through the whole 1,5 hours.

Later in the raid the rogue t1 bracers dropped and two rogues needed them. JDO played lootcouncil and gave them to the rogue with the lower roll.
Few mobs later the bracers dropped again and this time the rogue with the higher roll rolled again, but JDO refused to hand the item out, because "his old ones are better".
The rogue /w him and JDO said, he wont hand them out he will either sell it or use them on his own rogue.
Just to point out, the t1 are obviously better than the zg, especially when counting in the setbonus + the pure agi is way better than having str/agi mixed together. This is something quite easy to spot, even for an unexperienced player.
JDO knows that for sure.

I write this from a third perspective, I have not lost anything, but my goal is to just point things out that seemed pretty off to me.
My trust is definately lost and I will not spent my time to enrich someone in an unfair manner. Especially if that person behaves like this. This post is especially dedicated to you as a possibly new wow player. You will most likely run into a Puglords raid. Keep that in mind and make your own picture, or just look for another open raid. There are plenty outside.

Edit: The nightslayer bracers are now for sale in the AH, sold by Cining, JDOs 55 warlock.
