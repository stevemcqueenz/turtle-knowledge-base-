<Toa> Horde PVP/PVE

About Us: ![ℹ️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/2139.svg)
Toa is a new Horde guild focused on both PvP and PvE. Our core members are Vanilla veterans, having conquered all baseline Vanilla content and been active on Twow for a few months.

Our Goals: ![🎯](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f3af.svg)
 - Our aim is to engage in various PvP activities, from ranked premades to small skirmishes, world bosses, and large-scale PvP, alongside PvE content.
 - Quality over quantity is our motto for recruitment, emphasizing camaraderie and teamwork. We encourage active participation on Discord to build a strong, group-oriented community.

Our Mindset: ![💡](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4a1.svg)
 - We believe in mutual support, fostering an environment where helping one another enhances everyone's experience. This includes sharing tips, UI reviews, keybinds, and addons.
 - We employ a DKP loot system for fair distribution and conduct a transparent loot council for specific items, valuing open discussions and member feedback.

Community Structure: ![🤝](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f91d.svg)
 - Toa values a close-knit community over expansion, ensuring each member plays an active role in shaping our community.
 - Surveys and polls will be used to organize raid schedules, aiming for multiple raid groups to accommodate an international community.

Joining Us:![🌍](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f30d.svg)
 - English speakers from all over the world are welcome to join Toa.

Join our discord: ![📝](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4dd.svg) <https://discord.gg/nDknupxm>
