 **Welcome aboard sailor!**

We are former sailors, marines and officers of the Kul Tiran Navy - once bound by duty, now sworn to the sea by choice.

When the tides of war scattered the fleets and peace made soldiers idle, the Lord Admiral himself offered us a path: Sail as privateers, unfettered by law, but loyal in cause. We were given freedom - under one condition:

 **"Fly your own flag - but raise ours in war!"**

And so we do.

We are the Kul Tiras Pirates - sanctioned marauders, rogue mariners, crafters, mercenaries, and adventurers. We fly no official flag, but we answer to the Alliance when it matters.

Between wars, we chart our own course - treasure hunts, mercenary contracts, brewing rum, or just raising hell wherever we go!

 **Our main focus will be on the following:**

Great social experience for anyone sharing our values
As much content able to be done guild internally
Smooth and organised raids and events on as many days as possible

Through the last 20 years of playing WoW we have experienced many great moments and achievements, but most importantly, we enjoyed a lot of good company. We are looking for people which are passionate about WoW, to share a beautiful and relaxing time with us. Our goal is to clean the whole content of course, while maintaining a good atmosphere and having fun in organised raids and other activities, such as PvP, achievement farming, Late Night Discord Games, ingame and irl guild events, as well as various other nonsense we come up with.

 **Our expectations:**

Respectful behaviour
Speaking decent English
Discord (*with working Headset/Mic for raiders)

 **Raid times:**

Captain Santori´s crew: Sunday 6pm ST
Off Raids: All days and times during the week

We intend to have events and raids every day of the week, where each organising captain can choose the time himself, so we expect to have an offer for every time zone

 **Recruitment:**

We have made some of our Discord channels public, so you can see how the guild is organised and functions. If you wish to join us, reach out to Santori or any Captain ranked player. We welcome everyone who shares our ⁠values-n-rules, no matter how experienced, active, or which time zone. The goal is to have a good time, and with the amount of raids, events and activities we do, there is something for everyone!

While we do serve the Alliance when called upon, we are a pirate crew and therefore any race from both Alliance and Horde are welcome!

If you have any questions, please contact us via Discord PM @ <https://discord.gg/aUpy3bt4h6>
