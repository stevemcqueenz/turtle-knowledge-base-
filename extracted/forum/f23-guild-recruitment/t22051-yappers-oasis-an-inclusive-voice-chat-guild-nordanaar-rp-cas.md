**Yappers Oasis**
YO is a Social/RP focused guild who mostly quests. We don't have a raid schedule, most of us are not concerned with hitting 60, but rather, exploring the game and hanging out with friends.

Most of my own chars have the SnS challenge and I started playing classic hardcore first, softcore second.

If you find me in-game, I'm p handing out little items, emoting and making small talk.
Yappers Oasis was a discord server before it was a turtle wow guild, so a there's a lot of crossover from there, but *you do not have to join the discord to be involved* with the guild.
The guild is mostly chatty role players. We're casual af, welcoming to gays and such, and willing to teach new players the ropes. No hate if that's not your thing, min/max can be fun at times too.

Shoot me a piece of ingame mail (From the mailbox) to Altruse if you'd like to be included, and I'll friend/invite you from there.
