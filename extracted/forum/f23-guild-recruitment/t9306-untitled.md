 Aside from our well-performing PvE content, we also consider ourselves a **Heavy RP** guild.
 For this reason, we offer all role-playing oriented people to take part in building **Scourge RP**!

 In general, we intend to roleplay the Scourge faction as a whole. This means that we have ideas in our heads, for example, for
 events related to Scourge Invasion at some future point.
 However, we offer our own guild storyline, mainly centered around Tirisfal Glades region and it's relation with Plaguelands.

  ![📜](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f4dc.svg) | **Lore Background**

> "Bound to the iron will of the Lich King, the vast Undead armies of the Scourge seek to eradicate all life on Azeroth.
>  As a result of the treachery of the Banshee Queen and a group of renegades, known by some as the Forsaken, the Scourge lost it's control over Tirisfal Glades, including the dark fortress beneath the ruins of the former capital of Lordaeron.
>  Though the very land is cursed, the zealous Humans of the Scarlet Crusade still cling to their scattered holdings obsessed with the eradication of the Undead and retaking their homeland.
>  At the behest of the lich, Araj the Summoner, the smarter part of the Scourge's forces from the Western Plaguelands have been sent to attack the once-lost region protected behind Bulwark.
>
>  **The Scourge of Tirisfal Glades** fights a constant battle not only to hold it's control over the land and their own kin, but also to slaughter those who might oppose the will of the Lich King."

  **Features of our background**

- **Who are we? Why are we more "free willed" than the rest of the Scourge?** – We are a local part of the whole faction, more precisely, the **Scourge of the Tirisfal Glades**. We are a type of Undead with a level of autonomy higher than the average ghoul or skeleton, but with far less power than Death Knights. We exist with this heightened level of consciousness and intelligence to help suppress the treacherous Sylvanas and her filthy Forsaken. Observing the ineffectiveness of the Scourge minions in Agamand Mills and Deathkneel, our masters needed soldiers more capable than others, but numerous enough to survive great losses.

- **Who is our regional commander?** – *"**Araj the Summoner** was a lich in the service of Ner'zhul, the Lich King, who became regional commander of Scourge forces in the Western Plaguelands. Headquartered in the ruined city of Andorhal, Araj had two primary responsibilities: spread the Plague of Undeath via massive Plague Cauldrons situated at various points throughout the area, and continue to pound at the Forsaken defenses at the strategic pass known as the Bulwark."*

- **Who are our enemies?** – Being a villianous faction means that we are surrounded by enemies on all sides. However, our most substantial opponents are:
 - **Forsaken** – these are our kinsmen who have betrayed us. However, they will return to our ranks or be suppressed along with their rebellion.

 - **Humans** – especially from the Argent and Scarlet Crusade are the prey for our ghouls. They may try to hunt us with the help of the Light, but on this unholy land we are the hunters in the shadows.

- **Who can take part in our RP?** – To ensure the best immersion, in our RP content we are mostly **exclusive to Undead characters** (including certain 'undead' skins and illusions). We require good justification for characters other than Undead to be part of our RP, for e.g. by being part of the Cult of the Damned™.

This is a new path for the Scourge guild, in which we put our first steps.
 **We warmly encourage all those interested in organizing and running events to join us on this adventure!**
