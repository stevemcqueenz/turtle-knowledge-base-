**<TurtleHeads>**
Progression guild based in the Oceanic region (Asia, Australia, New Zealand) on the Nordanaar RPPvE server.
Times: Main raid days Sunday/Monday 7:45pm AEST. We also organise raids on Wednesday, Thursday,  Friday and Saturday.

Hi there, we're a guild looking for all types of players join our ranks; raiders to bolster our progression, casual players who wanna just smash some dungeons out, levelers or pvp'rs who wanna FIGHT and have a good time! LGBTQ+ friendly <3
We have BWL/ZG/AQ20/ONY/LKH/MC/ESHM/AQ40 on farm. Naxx is 15/15.
Loot is based on an SR+ system. MS rolls are also on a +1 system. We have a special system for Sapph/KT loot, plus most weapons in Naxx/AQ40/ESHM.
We accept players from anywhere in the world! Hope to see you join us :)
The people you can contact in game are Shimanim, Priestspec, Sachakan, Dotsforu,  Bonesnarrows and Abuelo; our friendly leadership is happy to help! Feel free to DM me also.
