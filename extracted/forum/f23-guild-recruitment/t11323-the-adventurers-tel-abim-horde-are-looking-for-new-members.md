# <The Adventurers> (Tel'abim) (Horde) are looking for new members!

## Post 74917 by Slapper — 2023-10-31T05:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74917#p74917 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Adventurers are a diverse group of unique WoW players made up of all ages, races, nationalities, criminal backgrounds, and other defining things. We are a PVP/PVE Progression guild, looking to do all forms of content there is to be had.

We like to adventure, obviously, but we also like to help each other out. This sense of comradery and assistance has led many adventurers to form bonds, and enjoy their time playing.

Formed and led by <Adventurer> and <Drac>, The Adventurers have been growing since the dawn of the new server with no plans to stop. At 300 members and counting, we are sure to be a formidable association of adventurers for many years to come.

Our forms of communication consist of Discord, Guild Chat, and Adventuring to one another to talk to each other in person.

If you're interested in joining us in our adventures, Whisper or send a mail to <Adventurer> or <Drac> for a guild invite.
