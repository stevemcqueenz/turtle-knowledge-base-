 **Join the Kul Tiras Third Fleet - Conquer PvE Content with Us!**

> **Are you looking for a dedicated PvE guild to experience epic adventures in World of Warcraft? Look no further! The Kul Tiras Third Fleet is recruiting skilled and enthusiastic players like you!**

 **About Us:**
- Guild Name: **Kul Tiras Third Fleet**
- Faction: **Cross**
- Focus: RP-PvE Content
- Raiding Schedule: Tuesdays/Thursdays , 18:00 Server Time
- Loot Distribution: Fair and transparent HR>SR>MS>OS system
 **Loot Rules**
- We utilize HR>SR>MS>OS for all raids.
- At the beginning of each raid, the raid leader will post and lock all SR (Soft Reserve) links.
- Every member has an equal opportunity to reserve their loot.
 **About Hard Reserve**
- Hard reserve means an item, upon dropping, will only be given to a specific player.
- Only the Fleet Admiral and Rear Admiral can decide and utilize this system.
- To hard reserve an item, the player must meet the following criteria:
 - Significant improvement for the player’s main role in the best interests of the guild.
 - Significant need for the guild’s progression.
 - Trust beyond reasonable doubts that the player has significantly contributed to the raid/guild and will continue doing so upon receiving the hard reserved item.
 **About Non-Guild Participants**
- All items, unless hard-reserved, are soft-reservable for all guild and non-guild participants.
- Under no circumstances will one participant, regardless of rank or any other factors, be considered to have loot priority over another participant.
 **About Bind-on-Equips and Materials**
- All Bind-on-Equip (BoE) items are distributed with SR>MS>OS priority.
- Participants not registered for Off Spec (OS) are not allowed to roll for off-spec items.
- Unrolled BoE items will be submitted to the guild bank and withdrawn upon request to gear up guild members.
- Participants found selling OS rolled items will be immediately removed from the guild/raid.
- All materials will be submitted to the guild bank and released upon request for guild logistical needs, including enchants, crafts, potions, and other materials.

- An active and friendly community of like-minded players
- Experienced leadership with a focus on guild progression
- Organized raiding schedule for regular PvE and Roleplay content
- Fair Looting System
- Engage in thrilling raid encounters with a reliable group of skilled players
