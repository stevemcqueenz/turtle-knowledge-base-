Hail the Wolf, Hail the Hunt!

The Huntsmen are a group of fit and able bodied hunters who fight in honor of the Great Wolf Spirit.   Known to the Tauren as Lo'Gosh, and to others as Goldrinn, the Great Wolf appears in the myths and lore of virtually all races of Azeroth.

In honor of the mighty spirit, the Huntsmen band together in effort to hunt and take down mighty prey and enemies of the free world!

Currently accepting signatures for our charter, but are open to all races!
