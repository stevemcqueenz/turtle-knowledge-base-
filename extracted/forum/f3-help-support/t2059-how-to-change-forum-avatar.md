# How to change forum avatar?

## Post 12343 by Paw (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2021-09-19T11:04:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12343#p12343 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

We have two options to change your avatar:
- Choosing from the given pool that Gravatar has provided for us.

- Uploading a picture of your own choosing through Gravatar's website.

> Gravatar is a service that allows you to maintain the same avatar across multiple websites.

Step-by-step guide:
1. Left-**Click on your account log-in** at the right-top corner.
2. Hit **User control interface**!
3. Click on **Profile tab**!
4. Choose **Edit avatar** from the options on the left side!
5. You are given the two options to change **Avatar type**.
 ***If you wish to upload a unique profile picture*** then leave the Avatar type as is and follow the next steps:
6. Open this link on a new tab: **<https://gravatar.com/>** and register a Word-Press account with your e-mail address which will be used for your Gravatar account's log-in information as well. Then sign in and upload an image of your choosing! (Multiple pictures can be uploaded for the purpose of convenient changing.)
7. Once its done, just paste the e-mail address that you used into the **"Gravatar e-mail"** box and hit **Submit**!
