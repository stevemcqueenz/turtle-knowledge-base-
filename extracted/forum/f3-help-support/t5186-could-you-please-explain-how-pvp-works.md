## Post 31529 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2022-12-25T15:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31529#p31529 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you take part in pvp actions such as attacking players or npcs associated with the opposing faction you are flagged for pvp and can be freely attacked by them.

If you dont do any pvp related actions you will ve unflagged from pvp after 5 minutes

Notably buffing & healing players flagged for pvp (indicated by green name & the faction flag next to their portrait will also flag you for pvp.

My suggestion is dont buff other players if they have pvp active if you want go prevent accidentally flagging yourself
