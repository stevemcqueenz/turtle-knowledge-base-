Hey there!

For your character’s name being changed to numbers, you can create an in-game ticket using the /gm command, and one of us will assist you.

Regarding your character being on Nordanaar (the PvE realm), Nordanaar has always been a PvE realm. Tel’Abim, the PvP realm, opened back in October 2024. You’re free to create a character there and enjoy it!
