Is it though?

I've heard rumors that there's some telemetry-like program running in the background in similar fashion to all the games that have a launcher post 2016 - or was it even earlier than that? It's definitely not cryptominer since I run it through with 5 state of the art programs designed to sniff those out.

The interesting thing is that while those programs aren't considered virus's, some anti-viruses do sniff them out from time to time. Not that they're a big deal if you play on a computer that has nothing to hide - say a gaming pc that only has games, while you have another pc to plot conquest of this world for teh lulz - but if you do have some juicy info I'd research a bit more throughly on this topic.

For those not aware, telemetry is how they track your location, pc specs, gameplay, and in some cases even online activity together with information of files you have on your pc, and it can be active even if program doesn't run. It's big deal because you can sell that info for pretty penny, which is why all games post certain era came with a launcher. In essence they continue to make money from you even after you buy a game, money that you could have earned yourself mind you.

If you don't like these kinds of things there are some work arounds that are quite simple.
