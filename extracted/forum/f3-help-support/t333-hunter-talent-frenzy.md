So, I've tamed Sharptooth, the only vanilla pet with a 1.0 attack speed (with reduced attack power of course) and even though the Frenzy talent says it should increase the pet's attack speed by 30%, it only lowers it to 0.77 instead of 0.7.

Some suggested that it may be rounded but even if it were that way - take for an example something much lower (25% buff), the attack speed should still be 0.75.

Perhaps it works with normal pets just well but I am not sure since I just recently rerolled to the Beastmastery spec. With that said, I would like to hear some further confirmation from other hunters of higher levels as to how the talent works for them.
