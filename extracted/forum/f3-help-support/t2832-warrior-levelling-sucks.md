Perhaps warrior class isn't for you. If you love solo play then perhaps you'd see much more fun and more potential in other classes, like a rogue or a hunter…

Warriors excel in parties, no matter how small they are, warrior's skills and abilities bring much more into a group play than solo, so if you find yourself lacking in some department then it's time you pick some useful profession to compensate and do some research. Besides, warriors are the most gear dependant folks out there, so you might want to either craft your own gear or discard solo play at least for those dungeon runs where you'll feel all the power bestowed on warriors.

Taking a friend as a partner for levelling is an option worth to mention too.
