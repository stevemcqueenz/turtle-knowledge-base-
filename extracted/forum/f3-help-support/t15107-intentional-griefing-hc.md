# Intentional Griefing (HC)

## Post 102672 by Ebondusk — 2024-10-07T02:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102672#p102672 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings, first of all I'd like to thank Turtle WoW GM Sourcandy, for his words of encouragement and honesty, aswell as the guidance he provided me.

Today I was farming Moonstalkers at Darkshore with my HC Hunter, and I encountered a Lv45 Neutral NPC Named "Owl", which at first glance I thought it was a custom twow NPC, but it had "Spirit Bond" buff on, so that leads to the obvious conclusion that in fact it's an intentional (but poorly) attempt of a Hunter to flag a low level HC player in order to kill him afterwards.I'll show you the only image I was able to took:
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/mxKzYmv.png)

Now i'd like to share my thoughts on this matter. My very first argument was the follow, not having a HC Exclusive server, and co-existing with non HC players leads to this kind of problem, which puts HC players on a complete disadvantage. I was lucky because i'm aware of this type of players, but mostly because I noticed the spirit bond buff, other players who encounter this guy may not have the same knowledge as I do, and they won't pay attention to the Spirit Bond buff, thereby they'll fall to the trap and die afterwards, completly ruining his game experience.

Another thing which I thought about, it's that this type of guys only do this because they're non HC players, my point is: I could easily spread the word on my guild, saying that there is a dude poorly trying to flag low level people, get a few hunters here for flares, some warriors for aoe demoshouts, mages for nova, you get the point. Then reveal this dude out of invisibility and kill him making it so he lose his character. But right, he doesnt lose it because he's a Softcore player and can Rez and go unpunished by the simple act of dying.

I'm not suggesting the release of a HC server, but I think this matter could be prevented better if the staff emphasizes more specificly against this type of behaviours and griefing attempts.

I was about to not make this post or request, until I asked Sourcandy what's his opinion on this matter, his answer really did the job to convince me that TWoW team is deserving of our trust.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/dnNgMhk.png)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/PJjiS7E.png)

Nothing but respect from my part, now being finished I think that we as a community have the responsibility to combat this behaviour and find a common ground for us to enjoy our experience inside the game.
