Hello,

I'm back again with more questions about the junk vendor, this time specifically Akalix told me to come here and get a literal definitive answer to the question so we can all know for certain.

The junker vendor only keeps items that are [DELETED} when destroyed, not ordinarily destroyed or vendored. If you don't literally have to type DELETE when destroying an item, it will not be in his inventory to buy back.

So, with that out of the way, here are my two questions. Please answer them fully and completely so we have only certainly regarding our actions with items in the future.

How many items can this vendor store?
How long does he store the items?

The reason I need these answered is the same reason many of us do. Bank space. I have veterans in my guild telling me the man keeps 200 items indefinately, but others say different things, so once again it falls to the people who made the game to give us the genuine response once and for all.

I love collecting items as much as the next guy, but there's only so much space, an issue that will likely never be remedied, unfortunately.

Anyway, thanks for your time.

**SOLVED**

THE FOLLOWING INFORMATION HAS BEEN PERSONALLY CONFIRMED BY THE GM SAELAS:

 - The Ratchet Junk Vendor is capable of holding a whopping 128 items!
 - He holds these items indefinitely!
 - Should you ever sell/destroy a unique item (quest rewards/etc) A GM can make a new one for you!

Enjoy your new found bank slots and thank you to Saelas for all the invaluable GM work he has done for us all!

Please disregard this post.  It's not worth risking your hard earned gear.  GMs are busy.  Item swaps and restorations are not guaranteed.  Even Torta herself said this is a horrible idea.  If you're new and reading this, please do not follow this advice.  You run the risk of losing your gear.

Here are some better tips.  Buy and maximize all your bank bag slots.  Run Ony/ZG and SR the 18 slot bags.  Make sure you have a bank or storage alt to send you're boe items to.  Don't store quest items in your bank.  Finish them asap.  Similarly, don't hang onto BG tokens.  Turn them in as you get them.
