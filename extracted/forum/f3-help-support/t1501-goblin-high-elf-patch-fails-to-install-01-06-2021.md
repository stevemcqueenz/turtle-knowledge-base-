# Goblin High-Elf patch fails to install (01-06-2021)

## Post 8936 by Daddym0e — 2021-01-07T01:08:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8936#p8936 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Initially it was my anti-virus blocking it. I solved that by telling my anti-V to allow it by placing it in the "exceptions". But it kept downloading and after getting to 100% and asking to reboot to complete. It would tell me that the update had failed. After a bit of looking at the main EXE properties of a couple of files I noticed that BackgroundDownloader.exe was *NEVER* going to work because it had been flagged as "from another computer and might be insecure" ..... or something like that. I told it to trust it. Set BackgroundDownloader.exe as Admin rights as well and tried again.  Wah-Lah!  The darned thing updated and I'm in the game now.

I'm really just placing this here to hopefully help others before pulling their hair out and having several sleepless nights trying to figure this out.
