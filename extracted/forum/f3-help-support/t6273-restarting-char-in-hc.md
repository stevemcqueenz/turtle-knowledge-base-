> **Artificialowl wrote: Wed Mar 29, 2023 2:18 pm**
> how do I open an in-game ticket ?

On your action bar you have a little section for Character Info, Spellbook, Talents, Quest Log, etc. You're looking for a tab called "Help Request" with the icon of a red question mark. Click "Issues that a GM can assist with" and choose "Item".

This opens a ticket form where you can describe your problem and ask for assistance. GMs will get back to you once you subtim the ticket.

If you have any UI replacement addons, you might have certain sections of your action bar hidden. In that case you have to navigate the settings of your addon and make them visible again.
