**Earth Shock**
 **Expected behavior:**
> Instantly shocks the target with concussive force, causing 17 to 19 Nature damage.  It also interrupts spellcasting and prevents any spell in that school from being cast for 2 sec.  Causes a high amount of threat.

 **Behavior on Turtle WoW:** It interrupts spellcasting but does not prevent any spell in that school being cast for 2 sec. the mob stays where it stands and recast immediately the same spell instead of coming into melee.

 **Stoneclaw Totem**
 **Expected behavior:**
> Summons a Stoneclaw Totem with 480 health at the feet of the caster for 15 sec that taunts creatures within 8 yards to attack it.

 **Behavior on Turtle WoW:** Stoneclaw does not work on Voidwalkers and Imps in Skull Rock cave and the other one near Orgrimmar. Normally you pop that totem for the pet to get aggroed by it while you deal with the Burning Blade Apprentice (warlock). Didn't test yet with npc hunter pets.
