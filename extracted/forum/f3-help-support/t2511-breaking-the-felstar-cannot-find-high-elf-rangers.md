The description for that quest is kind of misleading. You need to head to the edge of the Felwood and click on the "corrupted felstar gem", the glowy red one there. You'll fight 2 waves of 2 regular Fel mobs and then the elite pops out. The rangers spawn in a short while later - the time between Dralox spawning in and the Rangers helping however seems rather long, and could be rough on lower level players.
