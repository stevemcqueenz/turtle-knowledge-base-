It isn't that it's hard to do, rather if the helmet type allows for all hair-types to show through or not. The setting you are refering to is just that: "Show hair on/off".

That means that if you for example have a pony tail on nightelves, the pony-tail is going to poke out of the hat. Same goes for puffy hair that isn't contained within the hat's model.

I would rather have bald women than a bunch of wierd interactions.

There are some very rare instances where it would work, for example Beards for certain helmets.

There is a mod detailing this here: [viewtopic.php?p=13632&hilit=beard#p13632](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13632&hilit=beard#p13632)
