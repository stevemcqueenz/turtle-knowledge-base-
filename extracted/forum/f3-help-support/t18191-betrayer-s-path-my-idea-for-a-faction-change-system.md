# “Betrayer’s Path” My Idea for a faction-change system

## Post 123945 by Bumerkiri1993 — 2025-03-26T12:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123945#p123945 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've been thinking about how faction changes in World of Warcraft could be more immersive and meaningful, and I came up with an idea that might offer a fresh approach.

Instead of a simple click-to-switch, imagine if changing factions was part of a questline—a journey of betrayal, transformation, and sacrifice.

Humans who wish to join the Horde could begin by slowly turning against the Alliance. They would farm reputation with the Horde while simultaneously losing standing with their own faction—perhaps by killing guards, assisting enemy factions, or engaging in espionage missions.

After enough betrayal, they would enter a 3-day transformation phase, where they go into hiding. Upon returning, they would awaken as an Undead, symbolizing their rebirth and commitment to the Horde.

Dwarves seeking to become Dark Iron Dwarves could be forced to labor in the depths of a mine for three days, enduring trials and visions from the past, until they return, changed—hardened by fire and shadow.

As an Undead you seek atonement, working with Calia Menethil and the Church of the Holy Light. After a 3-day rite of purification, you become a redeemed undead—touched by Light, able to walk among the living once more.

..... ans so on

you could ...
Gain unique titles (e.g., The Betrayer, Forsaken Redeemer, Twilight Engineer)
Receive cosmetic changes (eye color, scars, posture, tattoos) showing your transformation
or stuff like this..

I feel like this is a bit much, past the concept of Vanilla+.

However, to humor it, back in WC3, Humans turned to Undead following Arthas on his journey to Ner'zhul. That could work with Humans, Dwarves, and High Elves, already (kill all Gnomes instead of allowing them this).

Mercenary Camps allowed any Faction to recruit Units the likes of Orcs, Trolls, Goblins, and others... I think Rogues and Assassins from them can be considered Humans? You could for example be playing Human or Night Elf and get to a MC and just get Orc/Troll/Goblin Mercenary Camp Units; same as Orc/Undead getting Rogues/Assassins (implying Humans). Basically, this could work for Trolls and whatever other currently available Race of WC3 Unit is playable on Turtle.

I would give examples of Orcs and Night Elves having betrayers as well, but those tend to side with the Burning Legion and others, which would align with... none in WoW -- Warlocks already are typically in hiding within Cities or are exiled and avoided regardless of Faction (not to 100% RP faith else you'd see Warlock players with their Minions out getting swarmed by City Guards, being unfair to them), Satyrs are hated by all.

I don't recall anything regarding Taurens.

You could try finding a place for Gnomes in all of this after making it make sense (other than the bottom of a mountain cliff drop).

The High Elves have Kael'thas' Bood Elves (already a thing here on Turtle, just not playable).

Still, a bit much for here, I'd say, and you can't quite implement it equally whilst trying to follow any source material and keep it roleplay-friendly within a strict WarCraft/early WoW universe (hello TBC+ retconning).
