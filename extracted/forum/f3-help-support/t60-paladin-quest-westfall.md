While I am not staff this is most certainly bugged.  Had a very similar bug on rebirth for many years which meant this would happen if the player did not get the initial agro, however the quest would not be completed.  The quest is called The Tome of Valor (quest id 1649)

I am guessing the current scripting has had no attention from the dev team and this is the default state of the quest and the auto complete is a hack-fix (not from the staff here) to stop  players generating GM tickets.
