If you died due to a disconnect? Same as anything else. You reroll and try again.

I know it can hurt to lose your character to something stupid, but Turtle has a strict "dead is dead, no exceptions" policy. I'm pretty sure it's that they don't have the manpower to investigate a whole bunch of "not my fault" claims from people, and so have to avoid the issue entirely. Sucks, but...sometimes death in WoW can be unfair, technical issues or no. So you accept the risks when you go Hardcore, knowing that succeeding is as much luck as skill, and accept that your death may be to something out of your control.

I don't mean it in a mean-spirited way, but hardcore is optional, and if you're not willing to accept that you might get an undeserved death, you shouldn't do it. Or do it, but "unofficially", without making a hardcore character and just using the honor system. You won't get the title or the cool mount, but nothing's stopping you from setting a challenge for yourself and deciding for yourself what's fair. Play to have fun, not just for bragging rights or to win a prize.
