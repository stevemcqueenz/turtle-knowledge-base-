Depends on wich race you start, most races have their own starting areas.

Alliance:
Humans: Elwyn Forest
Dwarfs & Gnomes: Dun Morogh
Night Elves: Teldrassil
High Elves: Thalassian Highlands

Horde:
Orcs & Trolls: Durotar
Undead: Tirisfal Glades
Tauren: Mulgore
Goblins: Blackstone Island
