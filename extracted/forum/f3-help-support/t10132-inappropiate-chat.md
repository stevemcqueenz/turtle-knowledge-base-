>  Who can we lodge a complaint when we see inappropriate chat conversations. I am a free speech advocate, but even I have my limits.  Thank you.

You need to place whoever's chat offends you on your in-game ignore list.  Simply type /ignore PLAYERNAME.

If they are harassing you and refuse to leave you alone then you can type /gm and lodge a complaint in the form of ticket for the GM's to review and act on.  You should also screenshot any chat relevant to your complaint and post in an image web service so you can post it on the forums for proof if necessary. The later form is ideally the most preferred way of doing things as proof is what matters and not hearsay.

However, you being offended by being in the proximity of a chat is most definitely not going to result in any meaningful action if you can just simply ignore the offensive player.

If you can't get use to the deviants that prowl the web you may have to reconsider your place in this community and whether it is something for you to be a part of.  That said, if its a personal and direct attack you have options.
