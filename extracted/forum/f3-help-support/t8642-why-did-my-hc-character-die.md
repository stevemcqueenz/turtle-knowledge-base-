If I understand correctly, you pressed the "Exit game" in the menu, then during the 20 seconds countdown to exit when not in a resting zone you clicked "Exit now" when there was a few seconds left. Is that right?
If it is, then you were killed by a mob that respawned. If you click "Exit now" then your character stays in the same place for 3 full minutes and not the 20 seconds!
If you are playing HC, never ever use the "Exit now" button, and also in general do not log out in places where enemies can reach you.
