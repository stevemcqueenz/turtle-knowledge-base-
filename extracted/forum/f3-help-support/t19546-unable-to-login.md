Greetings!

If you are seeing this message, it could be due to several reasons:

- You are entering incorrect credentials. Either the username or password is wrong. Please note that the email is not your username. Also, remember that your forum login is not the same as your game account login.

- You are trying to log in to the wrong region. You may have selected an incorrect region on the website or in the launcher while attempting to use credentials from a different region. Keep in mind that each region requires a separate account.

- You previously deleted your account. In the past, deleting an account did not remove the associated forum account.

To regain access to your account or to check whether it still exists, please send us an email at [[email protected]](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#94fdfaf2fbd4e0e1e6e0f8f1f7e6f5f2e0baf3f3)
