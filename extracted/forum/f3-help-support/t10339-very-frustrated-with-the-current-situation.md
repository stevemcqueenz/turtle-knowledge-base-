# Very frustrated with the current situation

## Post 67549 by Rememory — 2023-09-29T08:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67549#p67549 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello dear Turtle team, please take my thoughts seriously.
Recently, you have made a lot of contributions by opening up Asian servers and adding many more. Thank you very much for everything you have done. Since the opening of the Asian server on September 8th, you have been committed to solving the queuing problem, and the opening of the transfer server function has not been fulfilled. I am an ordinary 33 year old person who wants to spend 2 to 3 hours relaxing and playing games after work every day. However, every time I enter the game, although the queue is only a few hundred or even over 1000,But I needed to waste more than 2 hours waiting in line, which gradually made me lose my passion for the game.
The European server refused us, and the Asian server was unable to transfer accounts that had been in operation for a long time.
This made me unsure what to do. Since July and August, I have many friends who also want to play together because they don't have time to try the game after waiting in line for a few hours each time.
For several months, you have been saying that you will solve the queuing problem, but it has not been solved yet. I need to spend several hours in line every day, which makes me very frustrated.Originally, a group of happy friends were supposed to experience new game content together, but now even during holidays, they can only face a few hours of queuing and cannot play games together.If I want to play the game at 5pm, I need to start queuing around 2pm, and there are only over 500 people in the queue 。During this period, if I drop the line, I will have to queue up again and lose my qualification to play the game today. This is very unfair!!!
The European server refused us, and the Asian server refused to let us transfer in. What should we do? You always say to be patient and wait. We have been waiting for a month and are still queuing up. I am very frustrated about this。
