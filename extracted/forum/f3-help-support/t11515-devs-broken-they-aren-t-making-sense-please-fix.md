Here's an analogy to help explain Kord24's point.

Imagine a subway system management company (twow developers) designed their subways system while adhering to the industry-standard and long held belief that lice outbrakes were a major concern for any subway system (not proven, but valid in theory) .  During their subway system design they decided not to apply some of the harsh (but industry-standard) lice prevention measures such as requiring all riders to have annual lice vaccination, but instead opted to add a mandatory $1 lice-check fee to the cost of every ticket.  However, they never actually added the lice-check process itself.  Furthermore, a bug in their ticketing system never actually applied the $1 fee to the tickets.

A year later, some of the staff of this subway company "realized" that the $1 fee was not being applied. They fixed this bug in the ticketing system, such that all tickets cost $1 more, but they still didn't add the lice-check process for riders entering the train.

The $1 stealth fee bump caused an outrage among riders. They were actually liking this subway system for its lack of mandatory lice vaccination, and slightly cheaper ticket cost.
