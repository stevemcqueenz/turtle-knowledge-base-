## Post 51582 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-06-28T04:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51582#p51582 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yes.

However, according to the [server rules](https://turtlecraft.gg/#/rules#multi-boxing):
> - It is strictly forbidden to multibox if either of your characters is undergoing a Hardcore challenge.
> - It is strictly forbidden to multibox if either of your characters is flagged for PvP or is assisting others in PvP. This includes Warmode.

So, if you're just doing normal, everyday PvE by yourself *with* yourself, or if you're just juggling items between characters instead of using the mail system, then go nuts.  ![👍](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f44d.svg)
