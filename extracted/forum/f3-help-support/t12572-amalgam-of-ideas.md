# Amalgam of ideas

## Post 83745 by Akos1896 (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-01-06T01:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83745#p83745 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey!
Please read it as a kinda scatter-brained note. I'm in a weird position - played TWOW for half a year, leveled several characters to 60 but never played other forms of WOW. My ideas are unique in the way that they are naive - I have no idea how Blizzard changed the game. I write down the things which in my experience could make the game even more enjoyable, from a strictly pragmatic point of view. Not taking into consideration the answers Blizzard made (if made any) because I don't know them.
List is a wall of text, writing it for fun. Loving to theorycraft. Being as broad as humanly possible because I'm not knowledgeable enough about the solutions, just wanting to write down what I think are pain points.

Travel
 1. I think zeppelins and ships should come more frequently to enable faster traveling. I waste a lot of minutes waiting for a zep (like waiting for a bus irl).
 2. Horde's Zep routes are very well created. I feel that Alliance has a harder way accessing the globe from one HS location. I'd add 1-2 ally ship routes for better travelling from SW. Would also add a neutral ship near Gadgetzan.
 3. Flying is a bit slow imo. Would make the flying mounts fly faster. I understand that it helps us experience the vastness of the world but if you are raiding with a character, that char knows this world well enough, it just wants to get to the destination.
 4. BM port item almost made the gnomish engineering teleport to Gadgetzan redundant. I'd reconsider changing the location of the gnomish engineering port.
 5. Would either activate summoning stones for dungeons or would make it usable only by warlocks (to give them some love plus added utility).
 6. Would add rentable mounts. They would serve as a lvl 40 mount for example for a certain amount of gold, for a certain period of time. Leveling alts would be much more fun with the added travel speed.

Drinks
 1. Even though there is an Alterac smoothie, I'd add a way to buy oranges (or some lvl 60 drink which is widely available). After a certain level, if you don't find a mage, you are kinda screwed if you wanna use mana during a dungeon for example.

Fishing
 1. Would add some extremely rare treasure to fishing, including epics and mounts at the highest level zones to make fishing more enjoyable. These rewards should be very rare though.

PVP
 1. Would change the honor system so afking in BGs are not rewarding. Would make it performance-based to reward players playing the game.
 2. Would consider griefing a HC character and corpse-camping a warmode character a bannable offense. World PVP is okay but it has to have some rules for enjoyment (leveling without warmode, I still think so).

Dungeons
 1. Would add quests to certain dungs which allow players to access directly some of its parts (like Maraudon) or would divide some long dungeons into smaller ones (in a way like Scarlet Monastery). Examples: Wailing Caverns, Uldaman.
 2. Would add an early ally-dungeon to keep up symmetry with Horde who has RFC.
 3. Would add some rare/epic loot to lower dungeons to make dungeon spamming more enjoyable. Like a custom lvl 20 epic ring at Wailing Caverns with 1% drop chance at the final boss.
 4. Would add a whisper option to LFT to immediately contact those who are LFT-ing dungeons you are also interested in.
