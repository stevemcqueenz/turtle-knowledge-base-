## Post 802 by Kausaali — 2018-10-15T15:12:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=802#p802 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Based on my own observations:
The bug happens if you do not overpower a mob while you could. Not sure if this is blizzlike, but here Overpower does not expire; you may cast Overpower on a mob minutes after it dodged an attack. However, OP has to be cast on the specific mob that dodged the attack, and hence the proc is lost after the mob dies. Furthermore, OP is shown as castable even if there is a dead mob that you could cast OP on, which results in the reported bug. An user-side fix is to have another mob dodge an attack and cast OP on that, after which OP is no longer shown as castable.
