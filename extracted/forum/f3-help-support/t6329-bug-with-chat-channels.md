## Post 39920 by Lien — 2023-04-02T01:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39920#p39920 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If you have Lazypig, I would second Botto1's suggestion! I had a similar issue with the add-on, though I later found out it was due to a spam filter it contains. It was preventing me from seeing my messages in /say. It can be toggled off in LazyPig's settings, which *should* fix things if the issue is related to LazyPig, but if that doesn't work, you can try removing the add-on and see if that fixes the problem.

If you don't have LazyPig, or already have it disabled, it may be part of an ongoing issue that's currently being investigated. Some players have reported not being able to send messages in /guild, /general, or /world. It seems to largely affect characters Level 20 and under; as soon as you reach Level 21, the issue will often resolve itself. I don't know of any definite causes at the moment, but some add-ons have been shown to exacerbate the issue (notably those that handle a lot of information, such as TurtleRP).

It's only started to affect players recently, so the most we can do is patiently wait for an update.

If you want to test whether it might be something on your end, other things you can try are disabling your current add-ons one-at-a-time to see whether any of them are adversely affecting your ability to send messages.
