# about warrior class quests

## Post 143910 by Cutcutcut — 2025-08-09T00:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143910#p143910 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, I’ve noticed some seemingly unreasonable designs in the Warrior class quests.
I accepted the Defensive Stance quest line around level 15. Before taking on this quest, I visited the main cities of three races to find Warrior trainers and check how to obtain this quest. At the time, I asked the city guards, and among the trainer locations they provided, only the trainer in Stormwind had this quest. Moreover, its name—"A Warrior's Training"—was very prominent. So, I thought back then that perhaps the Defensive Stance quest could only be obtained in Stormwind, and I followed through to complete the quest line.

Today, while reviewing the prerequisites of the quests I’ve completed, I discovered that this series of quests actually has three different routes. Take Darnassus, for example: the starting point of its Defensive Stance quest is a female Night Elf named Elanaria. She is not a Warrior trainer, so she cannot be found by asking the guards. Additionally, the Darnassus route of the Defensive Stance quest line starts with a quest named "Elanaria"—the same as the NPC’s name. Personally, I had no way of finding the starting point of this quest line through the quest name or by asking for directions effectively.

Among these three quest lines, only the one in Stormwind seems easy to discover. The worst part is that once you complete one of the three lines, the other two become unavailable, meaning you miss out on the rewards in the subsequent quests (these items also carry characteristics that reflect the distinct cultures of the three races). If I had chosen a Human character, I might have accepted this, but unfortunately, my character is a Night Elf.

I hope to be given a chance to rechoose. If that’s not possible, I hope this series of quests can be optimized in a future patch, allowing us the opportunity to claim those subsequent quest rewards. I have paused progress on the follow-up quests in this line (those after obtaining Defensive Stance) and hope to receive a response.
