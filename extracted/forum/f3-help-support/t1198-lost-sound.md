One thing to check is if any of your sound devices have “Exclusive Control” is enabled for them, since if that is enabled, and something else is using the device, the game won’t be able to access it

You can find it in Control Panel > Hardware and Sound > Sound, then double-click on your device, and go to the Advanced tab. If the “Allow applications to take exclusive control of this device” box is ticked, untick it, and hit Apply, then OK.
