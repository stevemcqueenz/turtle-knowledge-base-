# Hunter pet skills cannot be learned!

## Post 57647 by Diabetis56 — 2023-08-16T16:54:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57647#p57647 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have a boar as my main pet. I want to learn bite. I stabled my boar and tamed a wolf. Wolf has bite rank 1. I used bite a few times in order to learn it. It should come up as a learned skill in pet training. But I do not see it. And I cannot train my boar with bite.

## Post 57648 by Ingameacc12345 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-08-16T17:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57648#p57648 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Did you see a "You have learned Bite (Rank 1)" in the chat log? If not, then you have not in fact learned it.

## Post 57649 by Hctwowfan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-08-16T17:10:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57649#p57649 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

When you fight alongside your wolf that has the bite rank 1 ability and use it often enough, you will learn it from your wolf and can then teach it to other pets of families that can learn bite (boars can).

If bite rank 1 does not show up in your beast training window, you have not learned from your wolf yet. You should receive a yellow line of text in your chat window once that happens.
