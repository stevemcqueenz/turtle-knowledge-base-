## Post 9072 by Steyr — 2021-01-11T16:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9072#p9072 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sounds like connection issue.
All NPCs and players are loaded according to server-side information. So if your connection to the server is laggy, your client might not be able to recieve information about what npcs it should load.

Are you able to see other players at the same time, while not seeing npcs? If not, you should check what other programs are using your bandwith. A torrent client might be causing connection issues, since it tries to fill up your entire bandwith.
