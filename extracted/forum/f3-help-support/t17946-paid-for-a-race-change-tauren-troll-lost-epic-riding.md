# Paid for a race change tauren-troll - lost epic riding

## Post 122237 by Eivindur — 2025-03-11T07:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122237#p122237 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So a few weeks ago I race changed from tauren to troll, and as I played tauren, I had plainsrunning and epic riding skill. The epic riding required for the fastest running speed on the tauren I aquired maybe 2 years ago, I cant remember exactly.
When I logged into my now new troll race character I had to use my turtle mount to try it out as I only had plainsrunning on the tauren before and didnt need "a mount". So when running around I did feel it was a bit slow, but didnt think too much about it as I thought perhaps it is the animations that are weird. Then I thought perhaps it is so slow because that is just how the turtle mount is, "slow and steady" and all that, perhaps it is not above 60% running speed?
Then I got the feeling that perhaps I actually didnt get the epic riding skill I had before and went to Zenjin village to buy a raptor, and I see that I need to buy the epic riding skill AGAIN for 900 gold?!

I contacted a GM in game and was very disappointed when I was told to report "the bug" and delete cache in WDB folder. Obviously this didnt work.
Now I know that when you race change on a private server like this, I didnt expect blizzard like service when it came to the technical side, I lost some flight paths and had to run around a little extra, which I can live with! But 900 gold taken from me because i payed you guys money for a race change? Not acceptable.

I have used probably hundreds of dollars in here and love this server but 900 gold is too much for me to be stolen away from me. I would rather have my old tauren race and plainsrunning with epic riding than this troll and 60% turtle speed.

Now, who can fix this problem?
