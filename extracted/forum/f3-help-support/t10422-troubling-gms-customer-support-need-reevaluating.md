Need some support from the T-WoW staff, please. Something is going very wrong and has been going wrong for a while when it comes to items in game. I'm not sure when and how the change in procedure and protocols was made, but a reevaluation is definitely in order.

Over the past couple of weeks, I've had close contact with some very serious issues when it comes to the support provided in game. The following is three such issues.

It started with a master looting issue from trash. The master looter misclicked and sent the item to a character who couldn't even use it. A ticket was sent in. The response was fairly quick. Nothing could be done. 100% don't believe that for one instant that that nothing "could" be done... but you know what, more care needs to be taken. I can chalk that one up to human error. Not a great CS result. Easily fixable. But not bothering with it. Okay, I can suck it up.

The next was a server crash just after a downed raid boss. Half the loot was handed out then server went down without warning. We had loot logs. We had screenshots. "Sorry, nothing can be done because we don't have logs". Now we have gravitated away from human error from the user and into the realm of error of product. And even an attempt to show proof is dismissed? Now we have some serious issues.

The latest was a guildmate who had his bank alt hacked. On this one I cannot confirm the loss of items or the GM response as screenshots were not taken. I can say this member is in good character standing. Allegedly all gold, gone. All items, gone. They even got rid of the gray clothes he was wearing. The response? "We do not refund gold or items lost by players". So now, it wasn't human error, it isn't because they don't have logs, it is because.... what exactly? No attempt to track down an obvious RMT mule? No attempt to make things right for a player? No attempt... well just no attempt to do anything. Is this seriously the road we are traveling down for T-WoW? What is going on?

I've spent a good deal of cash here to support T-WoW. I believed what they were doing was good. Their support used to be top notch. I understand the need to be vigilant against abuse, but this is going a bit too far. WTF happened to the great GM's that used to be helpful and actually willing/able to do something? Are they still around? Have they left? Has training not been going well? What is happening and why? I can tell you now that if that last issue happened, I'd pack up and leave. No reason to stay when it has become evident that all hard work can be taken away in a snap and the T-WoW team doesn't give two craps about it.

Greetings! First, I want to express my gratitude for bringing this issue to our attention. I will do my best to address your questions and explain how everything works.

Firstly, I would like to discuss loot distribution. According to our server rules (which you can read on our site <https://turtlecraft.gg/#/rules#loot-distribution-issues>), players are responsible for distributing loot. When a GM receives a ticket requesting item transfers , they respond to it in accordance with the server rules. It is important to understand that these rules form the foundation of our project.

Secondly, it is important to note that server crashes are unpredictable events. If there are no remaining logs of the loot you received from killing a boss during a crash, the GM will not have information about what loot you lost. In such a situation, you can provide evidence. However, a screenshot alone is not sufficient, as it contains limited objective information. A video should be provided, showing which boss you killed, what loot dropped, and specifically what you lost. The video should also include the time and date. These conditions are necessary to prevent any attempts at cheating.

Thirdly, the case you have described requires special attention. Please message me privately on Discord, so i could investigating this case. We always prioritize cases involving hacked accounts and, in 99% of cases, we return everything to the rightful owners.

I would also appreciate it if you could provide evidence of the actions you mentioned regarding the GM, so that I can look into it as well.

Thank you for your attention!
