## Post 89014 by Atreidon (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2024-02-29T11:05:00Z

You might wanna read up on how the pvp ranking system in general works if you are after high pvp ranks.

The amount of honor you gain each weak places you in a ranking where higher ranked people gain more rank points. And you need a certain amount of rank points to get to the next rank.

Even if you earn the most amount of honor out of everyone, the points earned in a week are only enough to propell you forward about 3 ranks at best. And especially below rank 8 it makes not much sense to go for high standings in the pvp roster, around 100k honor is plenty, and playing more while fun is something you wanna mainly do once you are at rank 8 or higher. because at that point you absolutely need high standings to advance further.

Sure its boring math, but you set yourself up for failure and frustration if you wanna go for a high pvp rank and not know how the system works
