# Druid's Omen of Clarity was mistakenly consumed

## Post 103597 by Abolaboboom — 2024-10-18T11:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103597#p103597 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Wrath flying in the air will mistakenly consume Clearcasting triggered during the flight when hitting the target. Clearcasting should have been consumed when the next spell is completed instead of when the next hit occurs.Moonkin Druid's Omen of Clarity is a core skill and I hope it can be corrected  crying_turtle

## Post 103768 by Abolaboboom — 2024-10-19T04:24:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103768#p103768 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

From my observation, the phenomenon of Wrath consuming Clearcasting is generally correct, but it operates under a strange logic that after Clearcasting is triggered, if Wrath is cast, the Clearcasting icon will continue until the end of the Wrath trajectory or the completion of the next spell after this Wrath. Although there is a Clearcasting icon during this Wrath trajectory, the second spell you release does not enjoy the Clearcasting effect. This makes it meaningless to monitor the triggering of Clearcasting, stop casting, and insert large amounts of magic consuming spells.
