## Post 69195 by Whtmstttv — 2023-10-06T15:45:00Z

Hello. I'm new to this server, and today I encountered what I believe to be a blatant violation by a GM. In the Hardcore in-game chat, I posted the following message, quoting: "Hey all, is LFT addon work on hc char?" After waiting for about 30-40 seconds without receiving a response, I humorously posted a second message, quoting: "HELLLLOOOOOO, is LFT addon work on hc char?" I immediately received a response from one player, to which I replied with a thank you, and I also thanked the second player who responded. Later, about 5 minutes later, I posted in the chat that I'm a mage looking for the Deadmines dungeon and then went to complete a quest. After some time, specifically around 3-4 minutes later, I wanted to write in the general chat and ask a nearby player about the quest, but I received a notification that I couldn't chat for 20 minutes. Initially, I didn't understand what was happening and created a ticket.

A GM with the username Cringeniero responded to me tersely and not entirely clearly, claiming that I was spamming and thus violating chat rules. Naturally, I wanted to ask him where I was spamming, but since comrade Cringeniero blocked my chat, and as I understand, there is a limitation in place, or perhaps it's a server feature, I couldn't respond to the GM either. I had to create another ticket mentioning Cringeniero, asking if everything was okay with him and where I had spammed. In response, GM Cringeniero once again replied curtly, asking me not to insult the staff and providing a link to the rules. I visited the page and found only one mention of spam in the CHAT ABUSE section, which reads: "Spam: do not post more than 3 consecutive lines of the same text per minute."

I was surprised because I didn't post the same phrase/sentence three times in one minute; I only posted once: "Hey all, is LFT addon work on hc char?" and then after 30-40 seconds, "HELLLLOOOOOO, is LFT addon work on hc char?"
So, I created another ticket addressed to GM Cringeniero, asking him where I had posted three identical messages in one minute and whether he was exceeding his authority. In response, I received a message stating that abusing the ticket system is punishable. However, we want to resolve the issue and understand why I received a mute seemingly without reason. I created another ticket with the following text:

I'm waiting for your response. Where did I send three identical messages?
I wouldn't have used the ticket system if your mute hadn't blocked my communication with you.
Please, if you accidentally confused my username with someone else's in the chat or if there's another issue, let me know instead of threatening me with your authority.

I received a notification on my screen, which can be seen in the screenshot. So, I would like to know if everything is okay with GM or whoever is above. I understand that each place has its own rules, but if you have clear rules, let's follow them.
