## Post 146956 by Frantic213210 — 2025-08-19T08:38:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146956#p146956 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Today I decided to go back out of desperation to do something in the launcher in the hope that the game would start.
In general, I first started checking the integrity of the files, it was suggested to download patch 9, at first an error popped up that there was not enough space on the disk, I started downloading again and seemed to have downloaded it, but the game still did not start directly and through the launcher.
After that, in the settings, I changed "Change configuration preset" from performance to "skip this step", then in the mods tab I removed everything, after which, to my surprise, the game started!
