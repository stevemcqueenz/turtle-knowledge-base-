Welcome to Turtle!
The graphical problems you encounter are coming from graphical option problems, namely you have to enable vertex shaders and smooth shading, otherwise the custom models will bug out and/or crash your game.
See:
[viewtopic.php?p=81778&hilit=vertex+shaders#p81778](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81778&hilit=vertex+shaders#p81778)

"Vertex Animation Shaders & Smooth Shading options are MANDATORY for custom models like the new Goblins."
