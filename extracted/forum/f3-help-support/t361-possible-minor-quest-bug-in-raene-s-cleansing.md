# Possible minor quest bug in Raene's Cleansing

## Post 1950 by Habsburgchin — 2019-01-25T09:03:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1950#p1950 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In the questline Raene's Cleansing in Ashennvale there are two quests at the end. The first is to kill Ran Bloodtooth (and 4 of his guards), the other is to bring Dartol's rod and Ran's skull to an NPC in Astranaar. The problem is, Ran only drops his skull if you have the second quest, but not the first. This means that he has to be killed twice, which is a bit unimmersive, especially when he could just drop the skull on the first kill. This is not a big deal, but I just thought I'd report it.
