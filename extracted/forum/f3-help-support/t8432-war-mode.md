## Post 54348 by Elesion (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-07-23T11:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54348#p54348 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You are not limited in your grouping. The only downside for you personally is being perma-flagged and getting ganked as a result. But your group members may not be happy with your warmode choice since the pvp flag will spread to them like a plague through heals and buffs (even just Battle Shout), effectively forcing perma-pvp onto them as long as they are grouped with you.

I've just mentioned it since it's not a traditional buff where you click on a character and choose to buff them. A lot of warriors think of it as a self-buff, but since it's aoe it can very easily flag them if someone else in the party is already flagged.

For clarification:
You as a warrior battle shouting next to a flagged group member would flag you for buffing a flagged player.

A flagged warrior battle shouting close to you will never flag you. You'll only ever flag yourself by your own actions, someone else cannot flag you for pvp in that regard (yes I know, mcing satyrs in felwood yada yada)
