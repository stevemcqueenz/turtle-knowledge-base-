On the screen where your characters are displayed, there is a little checkbox at the bottom right of the currently displayed character that says something like "*auto login this character*". When I was leveling my Druid, I thought that sounded like a good idea, so I checked it. Now I am leveling a different character, but the system still autologs my Druid every time I sign on. On the login screen, select your account, press the button that says "Clear Character", and done.  No more auto-login.  Once you do that, *then* you can choose a different character to automatically log in as, if you want.
