You can talk to the Glyph Master again and buy the War Mode Glyph again. War Mode will toggle PvP and you will be at risk of being attacked by other players, however you will gain experience and honor at a higher rate. Be mindful that once you turn it on a second time, you can't turn it off again until Level 60!

For the Mysterious Stranger, that is how you enable Hardcore mode. His quest to turn on Hardcore, one life, is only available at Level 1. The Turtle WoW team will not revive your Hardcore for any reason should you fall.

Best of luck in your journey!   smiling_turtle_head
