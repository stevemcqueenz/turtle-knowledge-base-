## Post 32539 by Kairion (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-01-13T17:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32539#p32539 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Pretty sure turtle does not restore died HC characters, no matter the reason.

As to how it can happen, if you close the game you get the option to wait 20 second or extit game now. The latter option does close the game instantly, but your character spends his normal 20 seconds sitting there before being logged out. If something attacks you in that time, you can die while under the impression you are already offline
