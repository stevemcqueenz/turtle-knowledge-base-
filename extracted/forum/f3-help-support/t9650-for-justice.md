我是今年7月3号建立的账号，刚开始玩了的是一个HC的盗贼，结果50级的时候死了，然后重新建立了现在的这个角色 Imhermes, 仍然是HC ，在still alive 这个公会正常的游戏，终于到了60级。 后来加入了 TAI HAO WAN LA 这个公会。我学习的是元素制皮，做装备在AH卖，靠这个赚了很多金币。 然后给自己买了AH中的所有最好的装备。
我承认我有点炫富了，在公会聊天中说过自己有很多金币。公会里有人说，可以给小号送一些包。我当时问了，会不会被封号。他们说不会，我就从AH买了很多14格的包包，然后分别邮寄给了公会的很多小号。也确实没有因为这个事情被封号。公会里很多需要制作元素制皮四件套的，我也都是免费帮他们代做。就在前天，我从AH买下了 胸甲附魔4属性和 十字军的图纸，然后还在公会频道说，会里有需要的可以找我，带材料免费附魔。
会里有人问大家有多少金币，我当时说了我金币过万。然后会里有人说这么多金币，举报了吧，肯定是金币交易的。我当时没在意。结果第二天登录的时候，真的就显示我的账号被冻结了。我联系了客服，询问为什么我的账号被冻结。客服提示：不要参与任何形式的金币交易。
我知道我在公会聊天里的语言炫富确实可能会让有些人不舒服或者说是心里不平衡嫉妒。但是作为乌龟服的客服，如果仅仅是因为有人举报，就可以直接冻结玩家的账户，这个是不是太草率了。如果我真的购买了金币，或者向他人出售了金币，我无话可说。甚至只要有人提供出我有意向购买金币或者出售金币的聊天截图，不管是游戏内还是其他聊天渠道，我都无话可说。 我希望客服能给我提供冻结我账号的证据，任何形式的都可以。如果什么都没有，仅仅是因为有人举报就要冻结的话，你们应该给我道歉。
而作为恶意举报的人，他应该得到同等的处罚，冻结他的账号。
说实话，当我得到客服的回复是金币交易的时候，我觉得可笑。我自己的金币多到用不完，需要跟别人买金币吗？ 至于卖金币，更是个笑话，我玩乌龟魔兽就是因为这里没有金币交易，没有G团，想玩一个休闲打发时间的纯粹的魔兽。我在现实中有十几个电器卖场，员工都超过100个。我需要跑到你们服务器来打金币卖出去补贴家用吗？
最后，我就算要离开乌龟魔兽，也应该是客服解除对我账号的冻结之后，我自己删除账号离开，而不是这样被卑鄙小人恶意举报封号离开。我会持续的申述，直到账号被解封。并且我会一直跟踪，客服必须对恶意举报的账号给与处罚。

It is true that they have not been banned due to this matter. There are many people in the guild who need to make elemental leather four piece sets, and I also help them make them for free. Just the day before yesterday, I bought the Chest Armor Enchantment 4 attribute and Crusader's blueprint from AH, and then told the guild channel that if you need it, you can come to me and bring the materials for free enchantment.

Someone in the guild asked everyone how many gold coins you have , and I said at the time that my gold coins exceeded ten thousand. Then someone in the guild said there were so many gold coins, reported it, it must be gold coin trading. I didn't care at the time. The next day when I logged in, it really showed that my account was frozen. I contacted customer service and asked why my account has been banned. Customer service reminder: Do not participate in any form of gold coin trading.

I know that  I   show off my wealth may indeed make some people uncomfortable or even feel imbalanced and jealous. But as a customer service representative of Turtle server, it would be too hasty to directly freeze a player's account simply because someone reported it. If I really buy gold coins or sell them to others, I have nothing to say. Even as long as someone provides me with a chat screenshot of my intention to purchase or sell coins, whether in the game or through other chat channels, I have nothing to say. I hope customer service can provide me with evidence of freezing my account, in any form. If there's nothing, just because someone reported it and wants to freeze it, you should apologize to me.

As a malicious whistleblower, he should receive the same punishment and freeze his account.

To be honest, when I received a response from customer service that it was a gold coin transaction, I found it ridiculous. Do I need to buy gold coins from someone else? As for selling gold coins, it's even more of a joke. I play Turtle Warcraft because there are no gold coin transactions or G groups here, and I want to play a pure Warcraft to pass the time leisurely. I have over a dozen electrical stores in reality, with over 100 employees. Do I need to run to your server to cash in coins and sell them to subsidize my household expenses?

Finally, even if I want to leave Turtle Warcraft, it should have been after customer service lifted the freeze on my account and I deleted it myself, rather than being maliciously reported and banned by despicable people. I will continue to appeal until the account is unblocked. And I will always follow, customer service must be honest with evil
