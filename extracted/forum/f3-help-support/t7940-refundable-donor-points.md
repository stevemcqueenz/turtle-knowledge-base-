Hello!
Hello! If you're playing as an HC character, simply die and your points will be restored.We do not restor points to HC characters. To ensure a smooth process, you must have at least one additional character on your account. If your character isn't HC, please create a ticket to request a point refund (this is possible within 7 days of purchase). Once the refund is processed, create a new character and delete the old one.
