Hey there!

Unfortunately, the GM you contacted was correct, there’s no way for us to restore a profession once it has been deleted by the player.

There are a few warnings we’d love to add for players, like when you're about to be locked to a specific raid ID or when switching professions. Unfortunately, the vanilla client doesn't give us the ability to display popups or warnings like that.

Hopefully, this will change once the new UE5 client is released. Apologies for the inconvenience in the meantime.
