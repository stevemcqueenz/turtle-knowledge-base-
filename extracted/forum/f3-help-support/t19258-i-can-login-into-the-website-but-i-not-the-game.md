Hello my friend, my name is Lucas and I will try to assist you today.

 **First**: Have you tried checking your email to see if you can activate your account? It could be in spam or trash, be aware of that.
 **Second**: Remember that you have to use your USERNAME and not your EMAIL. Also verify your password. If you saved it on your browser or a password manager, try copying and pasting for 100% guarantee of not screwing it up (not necessary at all but gives you confidence that the password is not the problem).
 **Third**: Check your internet connection and see if it properly works, even though it surely does.
 **Fourth**: Make sure that you allow the app through firewall.
 **Fifth**: Check your antivirus and see the history to ensure no file was removed. Also, exclude the folder from your antivirus and reinstall the game if the problem persists. By excluding the folder you are guaranteeing no file gets removed when extracting.
 **Sixth**: Consider redownloading the files, in case it got corrupted.

I'd like to clarify that reinstalling and redownloading must be done exclusively when there seems to be no other option, it shouldn't be necessary at all, yet it can work.
