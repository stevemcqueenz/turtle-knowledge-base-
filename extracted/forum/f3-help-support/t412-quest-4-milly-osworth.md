The Quest, [[4] Milly Osworth](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=3903) should be available at level 4, but on TurtleWow it requires us to complete [[4] Brotherhood of Thieves](http://classicdb.ch/?quest=18). The Requires Quests for this is [[2] Wolves Across the Border](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=33).
Brotherhood of Thieves should only open [[5] Bounty on Garrick Padfoot](http://classicdb.ch/?quest=6).

I should be able to collect 12 [Red Burlap Bandana](http://classicdb.ch/?item=752) (Brotherhood of Thieves) and 8 [Milly's Harvest](https://classicdb.ch/?item=11119) ([[4] Milly's Harvest](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=3904)) together when questing.
