When receiving mail from someone, the "Delete" button is replaced by the "Return" button. Only if you send items to someone and they return it (either by waiting 30 days for automatic return or by pressing the "Return" button) will you be able to see and press a "Delete" button.

Make sure the other player does not have the items after all.
