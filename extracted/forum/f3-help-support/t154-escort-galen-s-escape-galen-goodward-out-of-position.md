Galen Goodward, the escort NPC for Galen's Escape ( <https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1393> ) in Swamp of Sorrows likes to run down the road in very high speed instead if sitting tight in his cage.

If you're lucky enough to catch him mid-run and activate his escort, he will slowly walk back to his cage first, then start the escort route properly.
