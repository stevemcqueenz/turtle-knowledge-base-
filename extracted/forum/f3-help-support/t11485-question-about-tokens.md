It's not that simple here and there are limitations.
You can only transfer tokens between accounts on the same server cluster. That is, you cannot transfer tokens between EU and Sea servers.
The minimum transfer amount is 50 tokens. To transfer tokens open a ticket in game and gm will perform the transfer operation.
