Hello!
It is best to ask such questions in the "General" channel rather than the support channel.

The support channel is primarily used to address technical questions and game-related issues.

I recommend checking out our [Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/924770583747645460) channel for assistance as well.

Additionally, you can submit a ticket in the game and a GM will help you with your question.
> **How to contact a GM and get help in-game?**
>
>  Near your key ring there is a red question mark, press it to send an in-game ticket.Also you can enter the command /gm in the game chat.
>
>  You don't need to be online for tickets to be solved. Support will respond via in-game mail.

In regards to your inquiry, completing this quest will cause Booty Bay to become hostile towards you as you align yourself with other pirates.
