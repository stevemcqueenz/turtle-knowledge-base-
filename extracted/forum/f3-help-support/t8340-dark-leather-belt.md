The pattern for Dark Leather Belt is learnable only through [Artisan Leatherworkers](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=3766).  That's just how it was with professions in Vanilla.

Anyway, you can find one in [Darnassus](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=4212/telonis) or [Thunder Bluff](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=3007/una), depending on your faction, alongside the other Leatherworking Trainers of the area.
(I linked to *Wowhead* because the *Turtle WoW* database doesn't acknowledge Darnassus or Thunder Bluff as their own zones.)
