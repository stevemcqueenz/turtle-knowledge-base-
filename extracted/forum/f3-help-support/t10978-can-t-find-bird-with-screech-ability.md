> Beeing lvl 34 I used my beast lore on dozens of those "Salt Flats Vultures" in Thousand needles:
>  [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=415 ... ts-vulture](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=4158/salt-flats-vulture)

There are a couple different kinds of vultures in the salt flats.  The more common variety does not have screech2.  The less common version has it.  If you found "dozens" of them, then you're checking the common type, because usually at any given time only about 2-4 of the correct version will be up.  Once you find the right bird, they do indeed come with screech2.
