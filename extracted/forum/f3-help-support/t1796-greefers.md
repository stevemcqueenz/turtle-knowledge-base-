I can confirm the fact. It's a lvl 43 orc hunter that has been repeatedly killing quest givers in Redridge ever since the merge.

Any player can flag himself for pvp either by attacking opposite faction, or using /pvp command. In your case he flagged himself deliberately so you would attack him and cause yourself get automatically flagged for pvp.

Additionally, the hunter was breaking rules about safespotting, from the server rules listed on forums :

> Safespotting is the act of PvPing from an advantageous position that cannot be reached through regular jumping or walking. If you got to the position by using a spell, potion, wall climbing, or other additional means, it is likely safespotting.
