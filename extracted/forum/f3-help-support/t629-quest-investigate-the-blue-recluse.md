## Post 3213 by Aidam — 2019-06-26T19:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3213#p3213 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The quest to Investigate the Blue Recluse seems broken.
Wowhead classic link: [https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=1920/ ... ue-recluse](https://classic.wowhead.com/quest=1920/investigate-the-blue-recluse)
There is a comment from Thottbot from 2005 saying:

"Ok here is what I find... Putting in the time to figure this out.  No, it's not a bug but requires patience, talk to the Jennea Cannon for the quest - Investigate the Blue Recluse quest.

 1. Get the Cantation of Manifestation &  Chest of Containment Coffers (10 charges) from the table behind her.

 2. So soon you get out of the mage tower, jump down and you will see 'Blue Recluse' inn, go in then go to the basement.

 3. Use your Cantation of Manifestation (Its a 5-minute timer between the spawn each time you use it.) - It does not matter how you hurt it, just beat it down until you see the spiral on its head.

 4. Use the Chest of Containment Coffers once the riff is stunned (spiral on its head), then it will say - 'Rift Spawn is sucked into the coffers', then as soon it's gone, click on the coffers and it will then be in your inventory.

 5. Repeat/Rinse #3 5 minutes later until you get 3 necessary coffers, then you're done...

Good luck.."

Finding riffs works, but when you damage them, they don't get stunned, just eventually die and leave some loot. I looked for some videos of the quest on youtube but couldn't find anything from vanilla or classic beta. The quest might have been done by some twitch streamer during the beta or stress test, but that's a lot of footage to dig through.

For now, all I know is, that those Riffs should get stunned at some amount of hp and then become targetable by Containment Coffer, which doesn't happen.

You can use the containment coffers on the spawn after you have killed one. I believe the normal way the quest should work is that you fight them, they become stunned at some point, and then you use a coffer. For Turtle WoW, start by killing a rift spawn, use the coffer on the rift's corpse, and then pick up the filled containment coffer. This should allow you to complete the quest.

This quest is still bugged. At no point do the rift spawns become stunned when you damage them. I was able to complete the quest by using the Cantation of Manifestation and then immediately use the Chest of Containment Coffers. Doing so spawns the quest item for you to loot. Repeat the process twice and quest complete.

You don't need to fight them at all like your normally are supposed to. They are also not hostile unless you attack them, whereas in original vanilla they would attack upon spawn, which is what makes this quest supposed to be quite hard solo.
