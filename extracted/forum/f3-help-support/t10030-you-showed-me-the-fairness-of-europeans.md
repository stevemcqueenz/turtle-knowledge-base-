Greetings! We are currently working to balance the population across all our realms.

We have been under immense pressure in recent weeks, as we have faced criticism from both communities that we attempted to bring together. The current queue was designed to prioritize veteran players from both worlds, but unfortunately, it is not functioning as intended, resulting in many players being unable to log in.

We are planning to introduce character transfers soon, as soon as we have increased the capacity of our Ravenshier PvE server and opened a new PvP realm specifically for the Asian region.

Additionally, we are exploring the possibility of expanding our European network to provide a stress-free and enjoyable experience for all players that are comfortable playing in thr English-speaking environment.

Please stay tuned for further updates. We ask everyone to stay patient, there's no instant perfect solution but we're trying to find one.
