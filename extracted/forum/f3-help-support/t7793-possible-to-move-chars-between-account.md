My understanding is that it used to be possible to transfer characters between accounts (see [here](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8471) and [here](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28135)), but that this services is either no longer available or suspended for a while until they can automate it for paid tokens (see [here](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34475)).

If it's important to you, you should probably contact a GM to get the official word.

However, I can confirm that the names of deleted characters are released immediately. I've messed up some appearances before and had to immediately recreate characters which gave me no trouble. Hopefully it's the same between different accounts.
