I actually had the exact same issue a while back — random disconnects and getting stuck at "Logging in to game server" out of nowhere. At first I assumed it was something wrong with the server, but it turned out the problem was on my side.

I had been experimenting with some custom modifications to the client — nothing crazy, just trying to implement a few mechanics inspired by gambling reward systems I came across while browsing [somagyarkaszino.com](https://somagyarkaszino.com/). Thought it would be fun to mess around with progression ideas and drop logic.

But apparently one of those tweaks broke something internally. After I reinstalled a clean version of the client and removed all the custom files, everything started working normally again.

Not saying that’s definitely what’s happening in your case, but if you’ve ever tinkered with the client or added anything manually, it’s worth checking. Even small mods can lead to unexpected issues like disconnects or login loops.
