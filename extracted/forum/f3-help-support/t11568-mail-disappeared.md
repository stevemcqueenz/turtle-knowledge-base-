Greetings!
The first thing you need to realize is that sending mail is the full responsibility of the player. Since when sending mail the player himself enters the name of the recipient and presses the send button.
Second. If the mail did not reach the recipient, it is returned to the sender within 30 days.
If the mail is not returned to you and did not reach your character, then most likely you sent mail to someone else.
Please note that we do not return mail or restore mail. Please be careful when sending mail.
