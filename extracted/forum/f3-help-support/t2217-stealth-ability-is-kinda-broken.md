I am posting a few vids i made while trying to stealth on my druid. The issue from what i gather is on mobs the same level  or 5-6 levels lower than spot you if you are not 100% behind them from an extended range , also it's troublesome when you're approaching from behind and they turn in any direction and instantly aggro.

For the people saying that it's a matter of skill and it's supposed to be harder than classic , there's no skill involved when the mob aggro's from any angle except 90° straight behind them. I think the issue is that the blind spot is way to small and any movement basically can trigger aggro thus making stealth in cat form at least basically worthless when mobs spot you from a mile a way with the corner of their eye.

I am also inviting other people with stealth issue to post here because i think that watching the videos would help the devs more to figure out what is wrong than our explanations.
