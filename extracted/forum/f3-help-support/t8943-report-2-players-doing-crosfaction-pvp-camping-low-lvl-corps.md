Make a ticket in game to report rule violations. According to server rules, such reports are to be made in private: <https://turtlecraft.gg/#/rules>.

To make a ticket, just click the red quetion mark next to your key ring. Stealing this explanagion from discord, ty Finytin:

[How to contact a GM and get help in-game]
Near your key ring there is a red question mark, press it to send an in-game ticket.

If you have pfUI and you don't have the micro-bar enabled, you can type `/gm` to open the interface.

You don't need to be online for tickets to be solved. Support will you answers via in-game mail.
