Account: bottlemao was suddenly banned by GM on November 4th. It was only after consulting Kook on the 7th that I learned about the ban due to excessive development of server resources. As a supporter of the Turtle server sub server and an independent individual leisure and entertainment player, I have been persisting in missions to fight monsters and upgrade to level 51 warrior characters for over two months. I really cannot understand why I was banned for this reason, If GM believes that players are overexploiting game resources by continuously using the same dungeon with different teammates to obtain equipment and perform tasks (normal monster fighting), then why can't the game managers of GM and Turtle server sub servers clearly state that such behavior is prohibited or have a clear judgment standard, or provide prompts and warnings to players for such behavior? Rather than simply permanently banning it, this is an irresponsible behavior for both the player and myself personally
