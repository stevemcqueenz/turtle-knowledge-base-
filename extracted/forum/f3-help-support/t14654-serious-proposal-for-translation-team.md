Hello everyone,

I just discovered this community, and after 14 years on the "official" paid server, and a few more years on other "unofficial" servers, I have arrived at this community. I am Spanish, and I currently play on a server with patch 10.1, with 5 players simultaneously, but my memories of my beginnings in WoW make me look for other alternatives, and I would like to go back to the beginning, level more slowly, with a group of friends...

My offer is to create a team to translate the game, both the interface and mainly the content (quests, texts, etc.).

Due to my job, I manage different work teams, from 5 to 30 people, so I could manage a group of volunteers for these tasks, of course under the guidance of the official team of turtlecraft.gg

I think that in the short term we can start with Spanish, French and German, and once everything is up and running, maybe add Russian and Chinese. I insist, all this under the support of the turtlecraft.gg team.

My proposal to dedicate my time and work, and personally get a team of players for the translation is firm. I await a response.

All of the above, of course, can be applied to version 2.0 of 2025. If that version is multilingual and it is necessary to give "more speed" to this part of content translation, I offer to help.

P.S. While I write, I think that the development team can use automatic translation APIs to translate all the content to other languages, and this post does not make sense... even so, I put it, I prefer to offer my help and be wrong, than just assume and remain silent.

Thanks for getting to the end :D :D
