Hello! Here is steps for reporting :
 1. You must be 100% sure that you saw the violation. Check yourself here: #rules
 2. Make a screenshot.
 3. Upload the screenshot to hosting.
 4. Make in game ticket and describe as fully as possible what did you saw ?! What nick bad boy have. + url of screen.

How to submit a ticket :
Near your key ring there is a red question mark, press it to send an in-game ticket.
If you have pfUI and you don't have the micro-bar enabled, you can type /gm to open the interface.
You don't need to be online for tickets to be solved. Support will you answers via in-game mail.
