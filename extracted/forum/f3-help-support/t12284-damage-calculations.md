> **Vdog wrote: Wed Dec 20, 2023 4:34 am**
> so in other words dont bother getting extra spell damage period?

Spell power, spell hit, and spell crit are the main stats of every caster dps (except shadow priests who basically only care about spell power).

But that is only relevant at level 60.

While leveling, int is generally going to be your most useful stat. Bigger mana pool = less downtime from drinking. You're also probably not going to be able to get any meaningful amount of spell power until you're doing endgame dungeons.
