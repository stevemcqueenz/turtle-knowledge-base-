# Regarding to the player vision problem when the Hogger server is under high load

## Post 67063 by Jiangshun — 2023-09-27T02:50:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67063#p67063 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings!
I am a Chinese Hogger server player. When my server has more than 9,000 people online at the same time, the player's field of vision will become abnormal. We can only see things 20-25 yards around. Even players on the same team will disappear from the screen as long as they exceed 25 yards. It's like there is a transparent fog 25 yards away from the player, and any object beyond this distance will disappear.
The Hogger server is basically currently in a high load state with more than 9,000 people online. In other words, basically all of the players' active game time can only be spent in this abnormal vision environment.
You can imagine, I am a warrior HC player. When I charge a gnoll monster that seems to me to be alone, 3-4 gnolls appear around me in the next moment.
In addition, I studied mining, and until I reached level 17 that I collected the first copper mine. I previously thought it was due to the lack of resources due to the large number of mining players on the new server, but the real situation is that I can only see 25 yards around me. Mineral veins, the mini-map in the upper right corner can also only display mineral vein tips within this range.
And I have reason to believe that this vision is caused by some kind of adjustment on the server, because once the number of players drops below 9,000, the normal vision will be restored.
I'm very grateful to the Turtle team for bringing more servers to the East, but I think improving the gaming experience of the new servers is the ultimate goal.
So please pay attention to this issue. It affects the game experience of 10,000 players on the entire server, regardless of Alliance or Horde.  crying_turtle   crying_turtle   crying_turtle
