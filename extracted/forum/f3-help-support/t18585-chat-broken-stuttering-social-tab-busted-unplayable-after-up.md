# chat broken, stuttering, social tab busted, unplayable after updating launcher!

## Post 126590 by Glitterpeen — 2025-04-13T07:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126590#p126590 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Everything ran great on my client until the Saturday server restart.

I updated the launcher and the client when servers became available again.

I can log in on my characters.

But now the following problems plague my client:

My game stutters for 1-2 seconds at 2 second intervals. (unplayable, I turned all the performance options on, and reduced video quality to minimum as well as multisampling to 1. stuttering remains unchanged.)

My social tab does not allow me to select other tabs like "guild" or "raid" it just locks up on "who."

I cannot chat in game. I can open up the chat dialogue and type into the bar, but pressing enter does nothing unless I delete everything that I have typed, then pressing enter again makes the chat dialogue disappear.

Attempting to click on another character's name in the chat window sometimes causes the client to crash; when it does not crash, I still cannot send a message.

I enabled superwow,nampower,VF and disabled them separately to try all combinations, nothing improved the situation.

I verified all the files using the launcher.

I deleted the WDB folder.

Nothing has worked so far.

I need help please.

Also, since the discord is on lockdown and I cannot verify my identity using a phone, I cannot chat in discord. The support staff would have to send me DMs in discord.

==================================================================================================
I logged in on a few different characters and noticed addon overlow errors.

It turns out that somehow two different versions of roid-macros addon were conflicting with each other.

I disabled one of them, now everything appears normal.

If you are having similar issues, double check your addons after the launcher update everyone, Just in case!
