[Too many unsuccessful authentication attempts...]

If you enter your login credentials wrong too often, you will get locked out for 15 minutes. It's a security measurement against the hacking attempts.

To reset your password: [viewtopic.php?p=12891#p12891](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12891#p12891)

If the issue persists, please contact us via account management panel on our website.
