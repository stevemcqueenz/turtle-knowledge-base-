Hi. If you sent mail to the wrong character then unfortunately we can't help you here. When sending mail, the game client asks you if you want to send mail, which you confirm by clicking “yes”.
If the character you sent mail to is inactive, the mail will be returned to you after 30 days.
We kindly ask you to take your time when sending mail so that you don't face a similar problem in the future.
Have a nice day !

If you’ve sent the bags to a character that doesn’t exist (due to a typo), the mail should bounce back to you after 30 days. However, if it’s gone to an actual character by mistake, you might want to open a ticket with customer support. They can sometimes help recover mistakenly sent items. Good luck!
