There's only been one specific known instance of a hardcore restoration, and that was because a GM force-disconnected them to deal with a ticket issue they had without checking to see if they were safe first, leading to their death.

The reason why Turtle WoW doesn't do restorations is because they are a veritable Pandora's box that would take up a massive chunk of effort to verify and people will try any way they can to game the system or start drama over perceived or actual favoritism cases. The only winning move is not to play.
