# Misplaced creature in Duskwood

## Post 4212 by Connelly — 2019-10-13T15:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4212#p4212 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There's a variety of ghost in Duskwood, more exactly in Raven Hill. It's called Unseen, and it's invisible except to a few spells. It only roams the abandoned buildings there, so while it's neutral, the chance anybody will hit them and be attacked by an invisible, untargeteable mob, are pretty low.

They are also level 49, in a zone of level 27-30 mobs. So if someone questing around does hit them accidentally, they're dead.

There's currently one roaming around the Manor Mistmantle area, which I discovered after using Demoralizing Shout while fighting the ghouls around there. As far as I can tell, there isn't any reason for it to be there.
