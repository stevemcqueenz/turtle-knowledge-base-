# There are two problems that affect the game economy

## Post 82090 by Skyang0409 — 2023-12-21T08:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82090#p82090 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello GM, there are two problems in the game that affect the game balance and player experience. The first is that the Rouge profession can mine with a single copy of ZG to obtain the  Crystal, and the amount of gold coins obtained per unit time is huge. This will seriously affect the preservation rate of gold coins and the price will soar. In addition, a large number of players poured into Mount Hyjal to get bright, and some players opened more than one account to get fragments of hatched dreams. This part of the players no longer get gold coins by other means, which also affects the soaring price. The appearance of these two types of players seriously affects the game experience of other normal online players. This devalues the gold coins they get in a unit time. Please pay attention to this problem, thank you!
