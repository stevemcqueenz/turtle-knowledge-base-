Worst offender I've seen was Once Upon A Sheep in Elwynn Forest where I'd wind up with 3 copies of the quest showing in the quest tracker.  Not the only offender, but it was the most reliable one.

Most likely cause is a hack of a sanity check to find out if the quest is already showing in the quest tracker that simply isn't doing the job right, so you wind up with duplicates of the quest being shown in the quest tracker.

My Dude, go to interface options and disable Auto quest tracking.

If the Auto quest traking is active then everytime you progress through the quest it will show the progress. And if you had previously manually traked the quest progression (by clicking shift+left click in the quest log) it will show the same quest 2 times. So either always manually select  quests to show in the tracker, or always use Automatic quest tracking.

Or just use the Extended quest log with it's own tracker, that has alot of options and looks exactly like the dafult one.

Cheers!
