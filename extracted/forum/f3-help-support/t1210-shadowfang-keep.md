Ran Shadowfang Keep today (30.07.20)

Encountered a known and crucial bug: Upon exiting from dungeon into the courtyard (the door that is opened by NPC)
![Image](https://i.ibb.co/56ptHKt/shadowfang-keep-courtyard-1-1024x597.png)

enemies from up on the wall to the left would target the player and charge him through all of the rooms of the keep, pulling everyone with them.

Suggestion: Some sort of line of sight blocker for the left wall or reduced aggro range for the guards on it.
