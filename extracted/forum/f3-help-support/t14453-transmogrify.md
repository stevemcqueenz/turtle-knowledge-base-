## Post 98430 by Daedalus007 (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2024-07-03T02:59:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98430#p98430 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

You are limited to items your class can use.
So you can only transmog cloth items as a clothie.  Leather users can do leather or cloth while Mail can do mail or leather or cloth and etc.
For weapons it is again limited to only what you personally can use.
