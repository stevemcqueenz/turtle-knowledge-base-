> **Fin wrote: Fri Jan 27, 2023 12:03 am**
> We classify the actions that you can undertake under 3 categories:
>
> - Strictly forbidden
>
> - Gives you a direct gameplay advantage
>
> - Does not give you a direct gameplay advantage
>  **Examples of actions that are strictly forbidden under any circumstances while controlling multiple clients**:
>
> - Using any third party software to control your characters while multi-boxing.
>
> - Multi-box in any PvP situation.
>  **Examples of actions that give a direct gameplay advantage**:
>
> - Actively helping your other character(s) in combat situations like healing them, tanking for them or damaging others for them.
>
> - Actively following your other character(s) around to keep them buffed or to serve as additional bag space etc.
>   Non-Hardcore characters are allowed a maximum of two clients at the same time while performing these actions.
>  Hardcore characters may not multi-box for these types of actions. You stand alone.

Non-hc characters can multibox in PvE only and up to a maximum of two characters. PvP multiboxing is always forbidden. Report both
