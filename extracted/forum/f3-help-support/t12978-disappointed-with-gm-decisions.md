# Disappointed with GM decisions

## Post 86914 by Corsawow — 2024-02-04T13:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86914#p86914 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

First of all, I am not here trying to overturn any decisions GM have made, but I just want to express my disappointment on the whole action they have taken in the process. I hope a GM will take time to read this.

I played in WoW for 20 years now, started in US Beta, went through all the expansions and also played in Classic, but I got fed up with all this RMT and decided to give turtle a try. I started when the queue time was few hours, but that didn't stop me and leveled my warrior to lv 60, as usual I made many friends along the way.

As you probably all experienced, sometimes the biggest obstacle between you and WoW is wife/girlfriend. During Xmas, I finally convinced my girlfriend to play WoW. First thing I did was brought 2 gaming laptops, exactly the same brand/models and they got delivered at the same time. So we started our journey and started leveling, she started a paladin and I made a warlock. BTW, we don't live together but see each other a few times a week.

I have read the rules on multi boxing many times, even asked guildies and friends on what is allowed, so I sometimes open 2 accounts on my machine and use my main to help with quests. She doesn't like do quests and prefer to stay in one place and kill mobs to level, she did this from level 36-45 just by killing in STV, I also asked her why not to create another character and get some experience, so she made a Mage on a separate account. I have a warlock, so I can always summon her Mage to the same area.

Then this happened one morning, I was on my 60 main and level 44 warlock, she is on her level 45 paladin and level 17 mage, we went to badland and looking for a spot to summon her Mage, as we were running, all the sudden both my character got a sleep spell, same as her paladin, then a level 67 hunter appeared next to us, he is a GM, and messaged us to give a warning of multi-boxing. We were not together at the time and we communicate on discord, she told me same thing happened to her. I told her probably a mistake by the GM, don't worry about it, so I raised a ticket straight away ask for a explanation, the GM got back to me saying I was running 3 account at the same time, but this clearly not the case, after explained to him, he asked me to contact Pepesmite or Akalix on discord.

At the time I thought this is clearly a mistake and didn't pay too much attention, and I left my girlfriend to level her paladin and mage, and went to somewhere else with my warrior and warlock to do a elite quest in Badland.

After maybe an hour, my girlfriend called me saying both of her account got suspended for 30 days. Then I think something serious must happened， so I went to the appeal section to appeal, and got back a message saying

“***Multi-boxing (controlling multiple characters simultaneously) is strictly prohibited if either character is flagged for PvP (Player vs. Player) or participating in the Hardcore challenge. This rule is in place to ensure fair gameplay and maintain the integrity of the challenge.***”

I went into discord and contacted Pepesmite, he kindly replied to me and shared a screenshot saying her Paladin was PvP enabled at the time of the ban. I was thinking how? and called her straight away to ask if she attacked anyone, as I have never told her about the command /pvp. She said no!!  Then Pepesmite then told me even if you healed or buffed a pvp player you are pvp enabled....  unhappy_turtle_head  . She recall she saw a level 36 player about to die and healed him and give a bless of wisdom to him.... I have always played on PvP server before, I have a warrior main on Turtle, I never know that if you heal or buff a PvP player you become pvp yourself..

Pepesmite told me that because she received a warning not long before that, so that is why she got suspended for 30 days.. But that warning should not be given in the first place, so I wrote a long message to explain to Pepesmite and he kindly forwarded to management team, I also explained in an appeal for account suspension.

This is what I got back from a response:

"***Greetings! We checked your account and found that all game clients were running from the same computer. After the warning you reduced the number of multiboxes at the same time and continued to use other two characters with the same parameters as the previous time. Do not try to mislead us, it may lead to permanent blocking of all your accounts. The appeals against your accounts have been rejected. The ban will not be reduced or revoked and will last for the allotted time***"

The sentence "Do not try to mislead us". This is a very serious accusation, we live 30 miles apart and using 2 machines.
I checked our Mac Address:
XX:XX:XX:XX:XX:E3
XX:XX:XX:XX:XX:EB
Everything is the same apart from the last digit. I asked for evidence that we were running all the clients on the same machine, but never got an answer. I am disappointed that you are not really looked into this properly. On the PvP issue, she is a protection paladin, and 1 month into this game, she was just trying to help some one and what harm to the community and game did she do.

I really enjoy playing the game in the last month, more than I ever had in the last 20 years, but this has been ruined. If we done something wrong, I would accept it, but I really feel the management team have made a mistake and need some justice. This is not about I am trying to get the account ban lifted, end of the day it is only a lv 45 paladin and lv 17 mage. We can always create new account. It is about the false accusation of misleading and the ban is not justified.

I told all my friends in game of what happened, I even got offered level 60 paladin accounts, I am grateful of all the kindness, but now my girlfriend does not want to play the game anymore because how she been treated by the management team.

I hope a GM can come out and try to resolve this situation. I still think GMs do the right things, but mistake can be made.

Thanks
