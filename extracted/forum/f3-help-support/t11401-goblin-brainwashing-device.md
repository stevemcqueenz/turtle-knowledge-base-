> Who do i speak to for a coin refund? I donated money to get coins to purchase donation items. One of the items i bought is bugged. Am i going to be ripped off or can i get my coin back to make a different purchase?

Greetings!
Returns of purchased items are subject to the following rules:
 **HC - Characters.**
 - *Returns are not processed.*
 **Regular characters**
 - *From the moment of purchase has not passed 48h.*
 - *The character has not been deleted*
If you fit these rules you can make a ticket in the game to return tokens.
 **How to contact a GM and get help in-game**
Near your key ring there is a red question mark, press it to send an in-game ticket.
If you have pfUI and you don't have the micro-bar enabled, you can type /gm to open the interface. You don't need to be online for tickets to be solved. Support will respond via in-game mail.
