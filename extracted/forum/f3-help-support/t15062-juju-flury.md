We would like to mention that the Juju Flurry buff is currently matching the behavior exhibited in the 1.12 reference client. Juju Flurry behaves similarly to Seal of the Crusader in that it increases your attack speed but also lowers your DPS by the same amount.

We also did some digging into our bug database from the Original WoW era and found that we had in fact filed a bug for this behaviour while the original game was current, but the issue was not actually fixed until the Burning Crusade pre-patch (patch 2.0.3). As this bug was present throughout Original WoW, we do not currently consider it to be a bug and is merely an aspect of the original game that was carried over into Classic.

The Juju Flurry buff in World of Warcraft Classic reduces your DPS by an amount equal to the attack speed increase. This behavior is intentional and mirrors how the buff functioned in the original WoW 1.12 client. While it might feel like a bug, this issue was present in the original game and wasn't addressed until The Burning Crusade pre-patch (2.0.3). Therefore, Blizzard does not consider it a bug in Classic WoW[drift boss game](https://driftbossgame.co/)

As for WoW Turtle, since it is a private server and not officially maintained by Blizzard, there is no confirmation whether they have altered the behavior of Juju Flurry to fix this issue. You would need to consult WoW Turtle’s patch notes or community forums for any changes they've made specific to this buff.   happy_turtle_head
