# To report a potential RMT (Real Money Trading) player

## Post 71079 by Publius — 2023-10-16T01:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71079#p71079 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Game Administrator,

Hello. I am a TurtleWoW player from China, and I have been playing on the European server, consistently following the rules of the game.
I want to express a concern that arose on October 15th. A player named Wenddy, who was a Warrior in our guild HY, left the guild and took a substantial amount of crafting materials with them.
This action has resulted in a loss of thousands of gold coins for us. This player is still active in the game, and they seem to be accumulating gold through blacksmithing and enchanting.
We are all aware that TurtleWoW prohibits Real Money Trading (RMT), and gold has very limited utility in the game. So, why is this player so fixated on accumulating gold?

We have strong suspicions that this player may be engaged in RMT activities, although we do not currently have concrete evidence. However, we hope that the game administrators can pay attention to the flow of gold and items related to Wenddy. If any evidence of RMT is found, we request that immediate action be taken. We will continue to assist the game administrators in maintaining a healthy gaming environment.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.

Sincerely,yours

Greetings!
Thank you for the feedback. We're glad you care about the server. Please send such requests through the ticket system in the future.
Near your key ring there is a red question mark, press it to send an in-game ticket.
If you have pfUI and you don't have the micro-bar enabled, you can type /gm to open the interface.
You don't need to be online for tickets to be solved. Support will respond via in-game mail.
