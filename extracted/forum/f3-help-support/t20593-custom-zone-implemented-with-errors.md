# Custom zone implemented with errors

## Post 142415 by Nathraicheann — 2025-07-31T22:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142415#p142415 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello.

The zone is "Bael Hardul".

TL;DR - This zone is not mentioned on the website, it is not marked on the map, it is an alliance zone in the midst of horde zones, it is invisible to adventurers until they literally step into the camp and when they step into the camp, they are met with two capital-city level guards that two-shot anyone under level 45. An annoyance to softcore players and the bane of the existence of hardcore players.

 **Error 1 :** This zone is not mentioned on the website under "Features -> Zone edits"  - a complete afterthought of an area.

 **Error 2 :** The zone is in no way marked on the world map (as anything more than a random camp)

 **Error 3 :** The zone is an invisible alliance camp with capital city level guards in the middle of horde low levelling zones.

 **Error 4 :** The area does not have alliance indicators around it like flag props, any alliance props, patrols, any sign of civilization leading up to the area, torches, carts, buildings or whatever - like literally any other *notable* camp.

 **Error 5 :** The entry to the area is "ontop of a cliff" and the guards are right behind the "cliff's edge". You **cannot** see the guards when going up the road. [If you go to this video](https://youtu.be/jSLKPx9IcmU) you can see my reenactment of my death. I died in the exact same manner as a lvl 31 druid in a level 15 zone with no time to react.

 **Error 6 :** The camp is located in a lvl 15-20 zone. The camp's guards are lvl 55 and do 270 dmg per hit. The average lvl 15 horde has 400 health.

 **Error 7 :** The guards are very slightly stronger than BOTH Stormwind's City Guard and Ironforge's Guards. Slightly weaker than Orgrimmar's guards. How are the guards of this literal god forgotten area (the gods of TWoW's universe are its devs and they forgot about this area - see Error 1) stronger than the strongest militia in the entire game, lore-wise?

 **Error 7.1 :** If we take guards from small camps in contested areas (where Horde and Alliance players quest
simultaneously like it is the case with this custom zone), like the guards in Ashenvale, we can see that they are lvl 40 and do about 40 damage per hit. Players in Ashenvale start at about 1000hp. These guards slap for 260 dmg in a lvl 15 zone.

 **Error 8 :** The world map paths are a clusterf*ck. The paths don't correlate to how it is in-world AT ALL. I was only there because there was another path shown on the world map that I actually just could not find.

 ---------------------------------------------------------------

I am writing with huge pain after playing hardcore and dying to this custom zone. Avoiding death was **impossible**  (due to the above points) and I lost roughly 5 days worth of gameplay time (about 120 hours gameplay) due to **extremely** poor implementation decisions. This single-handedly repulses me from playing on this server again since it is not the first time I die on HC to "custom" content implemented in an irresponsible way. I even think this is revival-worthy as I actually reacted immediately after the guards pulled me and I have overlevelled the entire zone by a huge margin and I was still not able to escape with travel form.

I keep levelling over and over in HC just to die to this bullshit after 120 hours invested. A joke in disguise.
