Hello! The process of dropping an item in WoW is based purely on probability theory. In client 1.12, there are no coefficients that can increase the likelihood of a specific item dropping if it hasn't dropped for you before. Therefore, item drops are simply a matter of luck, whether good or bad. Interestingly, according to Wowhead statistics, out of 52,275 boss kills, only 2129 handcuffs were dropped, which equates to a drop rate of 4.07%.
