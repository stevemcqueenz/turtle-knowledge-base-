## Post 40253 by Imonobor (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-04-04T11:21:00Z

Yep, I believe they got them from other private servers and just tried the same the login/password combinations to see if anyone has the same combination on this server. This is why it's never a good idea to reuse passwords for anything.
Note that this doesn't mean the owners of other servers and scamming people on this one, hackers could have breached their server and stolen the login credentials of users (not particularly hard, seeing as some wow private servers have no idea how to secure their servers against attacks and don't even encrypt user login details). It happens all the time to different services and it's up to the owners to signalize it to its users. It seems they failed to do so.
