## Post 7423 by Roxanneflowers (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-08-31T03:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7423#p7423 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Climb to a higher part of the zepplin before zoning.
Either that or jump to board, rather than just walking on.

Had something similar happen to me when boarding the boat from Auberdine to Menethil Harbor.  The boat started to leave the pier just as I was approaching, but I had the angle right to board before it left the dock.  Anyway, I forgot to jump onto the deck when boarding (which I usually do) and somehow hit an invisible wall that stopped my forward progress ... but I still had the pier under my feet as the boat started moving forwards.  I stopped running and SOMEHOW the boat "scooped me up" and carried me with it on the OUTSIDE of the ship (I wound up aft of the deck next to a side window of the stern section) so long as I wasn't trying to move.

I imagined myself clinging for dear life to the side of the ship as it sailed away from Kalimdor to the Eastern Kingdoms (don't let go!).

I was worried that on zone in I'd be dropped in the DEEP OCEAN when I spawned into the world before the ship did ... but somehow I was still be carried along on the OUTSIDE of the ship as it sailed towards the dock.  Just before the ship made the final turn into the dock, I moved and immediately fell off the side of the ship into the water, swam to shore and was perfectly fine in Menethil (albeit, kind of amazed that I'd made it).

So yeah, spooky weirdness can occur when boarding (air)ships.  Typically I'll jump to board just to ensure that there isn't any kind of state change/transfer weirdness that might encourage a "drop" upon zoning.  I've encountered some weird edge cases of timing when boarding that seem to be mitigated by jumping on board when boarding.
