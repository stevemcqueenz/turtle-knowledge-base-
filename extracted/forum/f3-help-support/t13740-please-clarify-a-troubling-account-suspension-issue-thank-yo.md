# Please clarify a troubling account suspension issue. Thank you

## Post 93486 by Yzzzzzz — 2024-04-25T12:47:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93486#p93486 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

My friend's account was banned for one month yesterday, possibly because he sent potions to some members of the guild through the auction and mailbox system. We have appealed but have not received clear feedback. We are puzzled by the reason for this account suspension, so we apologize for using forum resources to provide feedback again.
Please allow me to describe the situation before the account ban. We are a team passionate about World of Warcraft, with about 30 members, who have been on the Turtle server for almost a year. While following the rules of the Turtle server, we organize RAID activities through wishing and DKP. One team member, after nearly half a year of MC RAID activities, finally assembled the Bindings of the Windseeker on April 22rd and went to Silithus with team members to complete the mission and obtain the legendary weapon. Because of the need to complete the mission that day, team members persisted until late at night (East Asian time, early morning), and our team, due to its small size, did not smoothly kill the four Death Knight bosses in Naxxramas. This team member who obtained the legendary weapon, out of sincere gratitude for other members who persisted until late at night and to help kill the four Death Knight bosses, spontaneously purchased some potions through the auction house and sent them to members. There is no involvement in any RMT transactions here, only sincere friendship and dedication.
We sincerely hope that the Turtle server team can consider the difference between the genuine gesture of gratitude and RMT mentioned. We are a small team, and the ban of one account has a significant impact on us. Thank you  turtle_in_love_head
