Security risk detected: Heur.AdvML.C

Security risk detected: Trojan.Gen.MBT

These are likely false positives because they are generic hits. You can add it to the exclusions list in your antivirus program if you must have napower.  The question is, how important is nampower to you.  If your raid is wiping because of your latency that's one thing.  If it's just that your heals are getting sniped by other healers so you're overhealing on the logs... probably not worth the risk, however low it may be.
