They also immediately go on the defense and blame it on bugs... Which, yes, even if it WAS a bug, which it isn't, the HC policy clearly states that your character will not be revived, even if it was 100% provably the server's fault and not the player's. If you do not like that policy, do not play HC. It is a contract you implicitly agree to, and if you don't like the terms of the contract, don't participate.

In other words, this thread has no purpose other than as a place for you to vent. If you want to vent, go ahead. The outcome is the same: Your HC character died, and it will stay dead. End of story. Whether it was a bug or not doesn't matter. If it was a bug (which I highly doubt given just how much more difficult they've made it to accidentally flag multiple times in the past with code changes), it will be fixed eventually. Check the bug-tracker, and you'll see the team has a lot on its plate.

Either way, better luck on your next HC character if you decide the game mode is worth it.
