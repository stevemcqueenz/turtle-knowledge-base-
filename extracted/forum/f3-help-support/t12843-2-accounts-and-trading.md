Greetings, I recommend you to read the rules of our server: <https://turtlecraft.gg/#/rules#multi-boxing-tab-1>.
It have clearly state what you can and cannot do.
Please note that the rules for PvE and PvP servers about multiboxing are different.
To answer your question, on the PvE server you can use multiboxing provided that your characters do not pass HC Challenge and do not activate PVP mode or use Warmode.
On a PVP server, you can only exchange between characters when you are in town.
