With a good few thousands of quests, you shouldn't have an issue getting to 60 on reward exp alone. But there's no way to turn off mob exp completely, nor exploration exp. The only thing you can do is to manually turn of all your exp gains at all times, except when you're turning in a quest. You can do this by right clicking on your portrait and selecting Experience: Enabled/Disabled.
I often use this feature to not over-level a dungeon I haven't run yet or a zone I have yet to visit.

It would be a nice glyph to introduce, *Slower & Steadier*, but for now all you can do is manual exp control.

Manually.  You can disable and enable experience by right clicking your character portrait.  You would then disable to while killing mobs, and enable it only when turning in quests.  I know of at least one player who has done this.
