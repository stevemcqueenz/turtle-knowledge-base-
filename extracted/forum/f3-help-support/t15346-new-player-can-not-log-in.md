Greetings!

When you create an account, you are sent an email to confirm your account creation. In this email there is a link that you need to follow to activate your account.

The link is valid for 24 hours. If you don't do this, the account will not be activated.

You can also resend the link, for this on our website [[email protected]](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#056c6b636a4571707771696066776463712b6262) login with your username and password and click on the button “resend email”.

If these options are no longer available to you, unfortunately the only option is to create a new account with a new email address.
