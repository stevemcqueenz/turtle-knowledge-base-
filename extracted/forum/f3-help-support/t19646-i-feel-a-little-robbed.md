So I'm doing this personal achievement of getting Seeker of Knowledge on horde. I knew Alliance has like 250 extra quests, but I tried to do it on Horde.

Even finding out that I lose some quests permanently because they are level capped (like PvP quests) and GMs didn't want to activate them for me I kept going and looking for ways to finish it.

So I figured out if I do profession quests, then even though it will be expensive, it will give me a lot of quests. I unlearned my Herbalism to do Blacksmith, paid between 350-450g to level it to 300 and complete all quests (except The Way of the Weaponsmith), unlearned it and world behold, all my quest complete track got reset and I lost all those quests again.

I mean just ticking at my account those quests back would take them like 2 minutes and this would have not been for absolutely nothing and my loss of hundreds of gold would make sense.

In case GMs change their mind here is the list of quests I've completed: <https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quests=5.-121#0+4+1+2> (Without the alliance ones and without The Art of Weaponsmith)
