## Post 37676 by Mcnair (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-14T11:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37676#p37676 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Assuming you use the default UI, there is a big red question mark ("?") button on the bottom of your screen, near your bags, next to the keychain.
In case you have a modified UI, either look for this icon or check the description of the modification you use (e.g. with Shagu's pfUI there is a command "/gm").
After choosing the appropriate ticket type, use the text field to give a description of the problem. This way the GM reading your ticket can understand the problem and help you better.
