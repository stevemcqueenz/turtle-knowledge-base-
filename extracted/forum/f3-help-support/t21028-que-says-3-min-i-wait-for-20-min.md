A couple of years ago there was a massive influx of chinese players, and the player count spiked from 4000 - 5000 to well above 16000 at peak hours. More than the server could handle. I believe that a system was then implemented that would give older accounts priority when entering a queue. Basically the older your account, the more of the queue you get to skip, if there is one. My account is from when there only were a few hundred players, and I was placed in position 1 in the queue when trying to log in just now. Based on this, I would guess this system still exists.
