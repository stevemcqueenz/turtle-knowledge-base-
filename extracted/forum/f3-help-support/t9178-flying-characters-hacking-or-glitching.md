## Post 59643 by Aydea (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-08-28T23:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59643#p59643 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It is perhaps noteworthy here that flying is actually possible but in a very limited fashion. On the alliance side for instance, there are gryphons where you can fly from Goldshire to stormwind and maybe red ridge if you are fast. This only lasts for a few seconds or so though. So it is a very short lived thing and in order to be eligible for it you need to complete a quest before hand. And even then one ride costs you a couple [Dwarven Mild] so, at  lowlevels where every silver counts that is actually a somewhat costly affair.

It would not be far fetched to wonder if those quests are also available on the Horde side and if you saw flying players who moved very fast ("flying" like this -is- very fast, however short lived) there is a very good chance that they were just messing with you a little bit.
