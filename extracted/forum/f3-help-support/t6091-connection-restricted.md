You may have been IP banned.

Given you can still post on the forums, it may be a case where someone else was actually IP banned but you also copped it due to a shared IP via Network Address Translation, which ISPs are increasingly doing.

In any case, you should make an email to [[email protected]](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#553d3039257b212027213930223a22153238343c397b363a38) and they should be able to resolve it.
