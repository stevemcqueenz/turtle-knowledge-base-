> **Attention wrote: Tue Oct 31, 2023 2:06 am**
> the page you linked just makes the process even MORE complex...it says to check out the github link but doesn't actually provide one. Trying to get just the most basic addons working on this server is mind numbingly frustrating, it's honestly almost enough to keep me from even trying this server.

You need to read the instructions.  Each addon has a GitHub.  For instance, press Ctrl+f, then type "Mail" from that link listed above.    It will take you to shirsig's github.  You then click the green drop-down button that says "code" and click "download zip.  From there, you unzip it, remove the "-master" from the folder name.  Finally, place the unzipped "Mail" folder in the addons folder, restart your wow client.

I use shirsig's git as an example for two reasons.  1. Their addons are really good IMHO.  2. They have both classic and vanilla 1.12 base client support.

Lastly, in your wow character select screen, there should be a red button on your lower left that says add-ons.  Click it and you should see the "Mail" addon.  If it's red, there will be "load out of date addons check box.  Check that.

I know it's a lot of information but you need to read it.  If something you've read doesn't make sense, copy it and paste it here and say what step you're getting lost on.
