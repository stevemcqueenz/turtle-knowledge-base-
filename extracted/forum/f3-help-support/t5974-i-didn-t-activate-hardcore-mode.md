## Post 37503 by Redmagejoe (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-03-13T03:11:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37503#p37503 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

It's very likely you talked to the Mysterious Stranger, accepted his quest without reading it, then turned it back into him also without reading it. Hardcore is not activated with a glyph. It is activated by accepting and turning in this NPC's quest.

Be mindful of the quests you're accepting and be sure you're actually reading them. Better luck next time.
