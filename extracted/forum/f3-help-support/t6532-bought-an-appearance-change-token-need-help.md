Good day.
The use of that item should be like this:
You create a new character with the looks you want your current character to be like (or find another player whose looks you want to copy), then use the following command on the character that has the token in their inventory:
.copy [name of the other character you want to look like]
e.g. You have your tauren with the token who would like to look like the other, nice-looking tauren called Texaslonghorn, then long into your character, and use the command
.copy Texaslonghorn

Easy. To change the appearance of your Tauren character, you need to visit a barber shop in one of the major cities. You can find barber shops in cities like Orgrimmar, Thunder Bluff, and Mulgore. Once you're inside the barber shop, you can customize your Tauren's appearance by changing their hairstyle, hair color, and facial hair.
