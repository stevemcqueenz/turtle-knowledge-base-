## Post 7025 by Roxanneflowers (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-07-23T12:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7025#p7025 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

That's because the game engine treats all of those categories as different classes of weapons.

Main Hand weapons can ONLY go in the character's Right Hand.
Off-hand weapons can ONLY go in the character's Left Hand.
 1h weapons can go in EITHER hand.

You're focusing on the fact that all of those weapons occupy only a single hand, while completely discounting WHICH HAND they occupy (left/right/either).

The game engine does not share your ... lack of concern ... for handedness of 1h weapon types, hence why it does not allow you to do what you are wanting to do in this case.  The differences between a Main Hand vs Off-hand are in the same "category" of differences as you would have between a 1h and a 2h weapon.  They're simply different categories or classifications of weapons as far as the game engine is concerned, even if that's a difference you don't care to recognize.
