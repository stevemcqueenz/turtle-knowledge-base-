I got an answer to the question, wanted to share in case someone else needed the information and I couldn't find it searching the forum.

Race change are available by in game tickets.
The  price is 200 tokens,
To purchase:

Please open an in game ticket from the character you want to race change and say to what race.
Please create another character, that the gm will copy their looks, say the name of the character in the ticket.
