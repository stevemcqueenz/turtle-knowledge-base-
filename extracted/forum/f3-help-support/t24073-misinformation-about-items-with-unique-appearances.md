# Misinformation about items with unique appearances

## Post 169229 by Cutcutcut — 2026-02-23T01:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169229#p169229 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

Hello, I apologize for posting to ask for help.

I am a player with choice anxiety and I am very afraid of missing out on permanent content in the game, so I have tried to avoid quests with selectable rewards. However, I had no choice but to face such choices for some raid entrance quests. I used a transmog add-on to scan my local database to check if quest rewards have unique appearances, and I also asked GMs for confirmation. It turned out that some items shown as unique in the database actually share models with other items, such as Skilled Fighting Blade. I was shocked by this, as it meant the other quest I completed was at the same risk — and these are the only two quests with selectable rewards I have ever done.

After checking again, without the GM’s help, I was not so lucky this time. I ended up choosing an item with a shared appearance: Shieldplate Sabatons, which was also marked as unique in the add-on.

I first went to Ratchet to try exchanging my quest reward with an NPC, but he does not handle low-quality quest rewards. I then contacted a GM again and was told that the reward exchange service has been discontinued. I feel really
frustrated, because I have unknowingly created an irreparable gap in my transmog collection.

I chose Shieldplate Sabatons because it fit my class’s stat requirements, and the other two rewards were also shown as unique in the database. Since Shieldplate Sabatons actually has a shared appearance, I wondered if the other two were the same. After checking my transmog collection one by one, I found that Windshear Leggings also shares its appearance, while only Conservator Helm has a truly unique look.

I also checked the transmog add-on and found the issue does not seem to be with the add-on itself. Shieldplate Sabatons and Windshear Leggings are indeed marked as unique in the system database, and the items that share their appearances are also displayed as unique in the game’s default transmog collection.

![Image](https://i.mji.rip/2026/02/23/c290ea3a2cd08a8fb24a7133d1f140df.png)
![Image](https://i.mji.rip/2026/02/23/4b0ffffa09b070a415f6ffbc68f20f82.png)

I would like to ask once again if you can provide assistance with exchanging quest rewards. I also hope to offer a suggestion:When a quest offers a choice of rewards, it would be ideal if all the selectable items either have unique appearances, or all share common appearances.

This way, players would either pick one of three unique appearances (and understand they cannot collect the other two), or choose freely among three shared appearances without worrying about missing something for their transmog collection.It would avoid the current situation where only one out of three items has a unique appearance, leaving players who picked the other two feeling they made a permanent, regrettable choice.

Perhaps in future updates, you could apply the appearance of items like Conservator Helm to new gear, so that quests of this type fall into the second category I mentioned:all three rewards share the same appearance.

Thank you for reading my long message.
