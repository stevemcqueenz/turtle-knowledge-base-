Did not appear in the Enchanted Defias Mask item collection, what could be the reasons?

## Post 20072 by Jolikmc (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2022-07-17T12:51:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20072#p20072 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The [Enchanted Defias Mask](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=81007), like most other equipment, should appear in your transmog collection after you've equipped it one time.  However,  it can only be equipped by **Rogues** or **Druids**.  Additionally, it can only be used as a transmog for other **leather headgear**.  It will not work as a transmog for cloth, chain, or plate headgear.

If the item is completely missing from your collection, however, please open an in-game GM ticket with more details or ask about it on the official *Discord* server.
