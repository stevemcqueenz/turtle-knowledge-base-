Well, as mentioned before, Android play is not really worth it unless this is your only option to play. It is very clunky as for movement and iffy if you have a lot of buttons to press, but I managed to do some light questing and farming, as well as soloing some bosses in dungeons (I am playing rogue). Here is how I did it:

I recently found a windows emulator that it trustworthy enough if you download it from Github. The alternatives are worse or not trustworthy enough. It is called Winlator. Reffer to the github page for instructions to install.

I downloaded and unarchived the Turtle Wow client from the official source and placed it in the downloads folder of my phone (the d drive of the emulator by default)

I created a container and chose the settings that would favor performance the best as I do not have a particularly powerful android device, and run the .exe

After a few attempts in which the client got stuck I managed to log in and hyped that it worked I did not realize that it was a mater of luck, as the next time I got in was after about 40 minutes worth of attempts.

If you decide to give it a go, you are looking for a reactivity from the log in page. If there is any error, like an actual error or a frozen page or stuck in "connecting", just close the emulator and restart.

If you get a "good boot" and you get disconnected message, here we separate the men from the boys as only persistence can get you through. Even if it tells you that you have been disconnected, just try again long enough and you will eventually log in.

As for controls, you can connect mouse and keyboard, but winlator has built in touch controls and you can create your own mapping.

Also thank you for your concern about security, but my password does not contain keywords and it will take decades to brute-force so I am not too concerned the account name is the weak point.

Have a good one!
