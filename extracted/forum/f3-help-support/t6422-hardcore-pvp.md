## Post 40481 by Nilay (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-04-06T08:08:00Z

PVP don't enable by himself randomly. There are only four possibilities here:

 - You attacked a PVP flagged Horde player and then been flagged yourself (impossible I believe for HC by game mechanics to stop HC from dying by mistake in neutral cities)
 - You manually activated PVP (unlikely)
 - You had warmod Glyphe (unlikely)
 - You randomly buffed an alliance player who was PVP, flagging yourself in the processus.

Just know that after being flagged pvp without toggling it or having warmod, you must find a safe place where to wait 5mn before the PVP tag going away.

This server is not PVP by design, basically you can do the HC run without PVP issues if you are careful enough.
