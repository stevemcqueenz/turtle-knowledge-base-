Hi, i've opened an ingame ticket but I want to open a forum post as well to further highlight the issue.

So I was just invited to a BWL pug. I get to the raid, enter, go up the stairs, the raid leader pulls, wipes then proceeds to say 'Someones trolling' and everybody left.

I then hear one of them say it had taken 3 hours for them to get to the third boss. From my perspective it looked like I was taking the slot of a leaver so I have no awareness of this until I was told after the fact.

So now I'm raidlocked despite being in the raid for less than 10 minutes, most of which was waiting for a couple of people to catch up.

This just seemed unbelievably unfair and that the system has a flaw. If I enter, there should at least be a grace period or until a boss has been downed as i'm now locked out of BWL for a week due to being invited to an already dead raid without any knowledge that it was essentially already over.

Is there anything that can be done about this?
Is there no way to mitigate this as a problem as I doubt i'm the only person who has raised this previously.

Greetings!

In the classic version of WoW if you are in a raid group that has killed at least 1 boss and you enter through the raid portal you immediately and automatically get a Raid ID.

This game mechanic is hardcoded into the game client and we cannot influence it.

In order not to get into this situation, before you join a raid group make sure that the group has not started raiding yet.
