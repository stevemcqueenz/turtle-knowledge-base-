# Account hacked & banned

## Post 106838 by Leordian — 2024-11-07T06:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106838#p106838 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hey TurtleWoW-Team,

i’m reaching out regarding an urgent issue with my account, which appears to have been compromised. When I tried to log in yesterday, I was met with a message indicating that my account has been permanently banned due to spamming in the chat. After checking the account management page, I saw that the last login IP address is from Belgium, even though I am based in Germany. This suggests my account was accessed without my authorization.

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/7n4DFRR/Screenshot-2024-11-07-at-07-36-47.png)

Here are some details that may help with your investigation:

- Compromised IP Address: The last IP used on my account appears to belong to a server in Belgium known for hacking and DDoS activity. Here’s a link to the AbuseIPDB entry for reference: <https://www.abuseipdb.com/check/194.187.251.163>.
- Account History: I have been playing on TurtleWoW since 2020 and have always adhered to the community guidelines. To my knowledge, no suspicious activity was associated with my account until now. The hack seems to have also resulted in all my characters being deleted, which is quite distressing after years of progress.
![Image](https://i.ibb.co/hML4NQz/Screenshot-2024-11-07-at-07-37-22.png)

Unfortunately, I haven’t been able to reach anyone on Discord, and there’s no option to appeal through the account page, only the message with the chat logs that led to the ban:

![Image](https://i.ibb.co/kGtPrbK/Screenshot-2024-11-07-at-07-36-55-neu.png)

Could you please investigate this matter, review the login history, and, if possible, restore my account and any deleted characters? I already changed my password to prevent new abusive logins.

I would greatly appreciate any assistance you can provide.

Thank you!
