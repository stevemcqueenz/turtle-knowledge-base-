Hello

When you create a account you have to confirm by mail to be able to log in.

When you reset your password you have to confirm the password reset.

You can update your client with the wrong information.

Forum and in game have different usernames, you choose them when you create your account.

Forum and in game reset password in different places. Here is where you reset for in game [viewtopic.php?p=12891#p12891](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12891#p12891)

There is a known issue with not receiving mails if you are using hotmail and yahoo.

If you get your information wrong too many times you get ip locked for 15 minutes.
