# transmogrifies bug Report

## Post 166701 by Nimo999 — 2026-01-28T03:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166701#p166701 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

When a player transmogrifies a weapon, the weapon’s appearance changes, but the hit sound and its carry/sheathing state are still tied to the original model.
For example, when transmogrifying into Phantom Blade, the original Phantom Blade is carried on the character’s back, but the transmogrified version is not necessarily carried on the back.
Additionally, if the original weapon is a sword and it is transmogrified into a hammer, the attack sound remains the sword’s hit sound instead of a hammer’s.
