# Lore Bug? - High Elf Priest

## Post 33838 by Andromath (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-02-02T16:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33838#p33838 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Good afternoon.

Recently, I rolled a High Elf Priest, and I received the quest that gives you the item, 'Priest's Letter.' I noticed a small discrepancy there.

> "Light be with you, NAME.
>
>  I am Maleah Sunsworn.
>
>  I was once ordained in the temples of Quel'Thalas where I learned to preach the Light of the Sun. I walked the same halls as the High Priest, Belo'vir.
>
>  It is with much sadness that those days are gone as our temples lay in ruin, and the warmth of the sun is Forsaken.
>
>  I have seen the spark of faith and devotion within you.
>
>  Come speak with me at the lodge. I will gladly guide your path so that, one day, our people's faith may be restored not only in the light, but in the future."

Belo'vir was the previous Grand Magister of Quel'Thalas, not High Priest. The previous High Priest was Vandellor. Considering the two have rarely been mentioned outside of the novella, 'Blood of the Highborne,' it's not a difficult mistake to slip through the cracks.

I don't know if I'd go that far.

Rather, I'd say a lot of what I've seen feels like it does its best with otherwise simple concepts? The starting quests at the Farstrider Lodge are dealing with a refugee crisis, so naturally a lot of the quests are going to be revolving around food, water, shelter, the basics of survival really. I can see how it'd be tricky to really put a strong High Elven flair on the text.

If I were to offer suggestions, maybe discuss how the provisions being acquired are more than just for nutritional value. Perhaps a Leyline runs through Loch Modan, adjacent enough to the lodge that forage and local game absorbs a higher concentration of mana. While this mana is inert within said foodstuffs, once consumed by High Elves it metabolizes in such a way as to release that abundant mana and sustain the addiction to magic, as well as their nutritional needs?

It's a lot to put into very limited quest text boxes, and considering its unlikely anyone but RP'ers will pay a lot of attention, it may not have been a significant priority at the time of development. Better to have the quests there and able to be completed, than to polish and fine-tune the dialogue.
