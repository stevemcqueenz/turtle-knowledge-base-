# Pelturas Whitemoon

## Post 2325 by Sinrek (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2019-03-16T14:37:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2325#p2325 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

**Quest: Orendil's Cure.**

Ends at Pelturas Whitemoon.
Once you hit "complete" on the quest the following dialogue takes place:

 *Pelturas Whitemoon takes Orendil's Cure brought by <name>.* (emote) He then turns to his daughter.

Pelturas Whitemoon says: Here you are, Relara. Take this...

 *Relara Whitemoon drinks the cure brought by <name>.* (emote)

Relara Whitemoon says: Uh... Father...? Are you there? I'm hungry... can I have some stew?

Pelturas Whitemoon says: What?
Pelturas Whitemoon says: You're awake? Your cure must have worked! Thank you, <name>.
 *Pelturas Whitemoon turns to a player and /bow at him.* (emote)

Pelturas Whitemoon offers next quest available for players.

 **Quest: Elune's Tear**

Ends at Pelturas Whitemoon.
Once you hit "complete" on the quest the following dialogue takes place:

 *Pelturas Whitemoon crushes the tear into a bowl of stew.* (emote after he turned to face toward his daughter and animation working as if making bandage within a 2 second period)

Pelturas Whitemoon says: Eat this, my daughter. It will help you feel better.
Pelturas Whitemoon says: There you go...

 *Relara Whitemoon stirs from her delirium.* (emote and she slowly sits for animation eat in 2 seconds further)

Relara Whitemoon says: Mmmmh... (lying down animation)

Pelturas Whitemoon turns back to face the player.

Pelturas Whitemoon offers next quest available for players.

 **Quest: The Ruins of Stardust.**

Ends at Pelturas Whitemoon.
Once you hit "complete on the quest the following dialogue takes place:

Pelturas Whitemoon turns toward his daughter facing her.

 *Pelturas Whitemoon places a poultice on Relara's brow.*
 *Pelturas Whitemoon breathes a sigh of relief.* (emote after animation /point)

Pelturas Whitemoon turns back to the player and offers the last quest available for players.

 **Quest: Fallen Sky Lake**

Upon completion of this quest following dialogue takes place:

 *Pelturas Whitemoon crushes the Moonstone into a cup of tea.* (emote after he turned to face toward his daughter and animation working as if making bandage within a 2 second period)

Pelturas Whitemoon says: Here you are, Relara. Drink this...
Relara Whitemoon says: Mmh... my eyes are not so heavy now, father. Can we go out and play? (emote and she slowly sits for animation drink in 2 seconds further)
Pelturas Whitemoon says: Relara... you're awake?
Pelturas Whitemoon says: Rest now, my daughter.
Relara Whitemoon says: Mmmmh... (lying down animation)

 *Pelturas Whitemoon looks at <name>, and then weeps with joy.*

Pelturas Whitemoon turns back to the player.
Pelturas Whitemoon: Relara is saved, <name>. Thank you!
