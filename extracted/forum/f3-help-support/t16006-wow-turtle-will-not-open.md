# Wow Turtle Will Not Open

## Post 109033 by CptSadDad — 2024-11-20T19:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109033#p109033 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Downloaded the new launcher and now I cant get in the game.

Initially, updated and when I clicked Play it gave me the "another instance of WoW is running" error. Then I started giving me the "wow executable" cannot be found. When I close and update the game again, it redownloads the exe every single time and I can see it in the directory, but when I click play and get the error message, the exe is suddenly missing. I've tried reinstalling and made sure to run as administrator. I had zero issues prior to this launcher.

UPDATE: It was a firewall issue. I had to go into security settings and allow turtle to do its thing. Hope this helps anyone else who runs into this. Rookie mistake.
