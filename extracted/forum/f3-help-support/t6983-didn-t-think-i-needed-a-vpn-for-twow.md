Your game talks specifically to the login and game servers.  There are no peer-to-peer connections involved in a game like this.  The only part that's peer-to-peer is when a new patch rolls out – but even that's just the game server talking to your computer.  However, from my five minutes of research, I've found that there *are* a couple of ways someone could reasonably get your IP address "through" a game like this:

They are a Game Master or otherwise moderate the game or forums.
Game Masters should definitely have access to that information.  It's possible that the user was a GM playing a "normal character".  If a GM is found abusing their powers like this, I have to imagine they'd be found out and punished. (Unless the offender was a higher-up.  *Oo-er*~)

They have your account's e-mail address and used a paid service to back-trace it to your ISP.
If you use the same e-mail address for your bank and this server, that may have also back-traced it with a paid service, linking the two.  This is why it's always recommended that you use unique e-mail addresses (and passwords) for each individual thing.  Realistically, no one's *going to*, but it's *always* recommended.

You have a keylogger installed and they have access to the information it transmits.
According to [a *Reddit* post](https://www.reddit.com/r/classicwow/comments/ck3w5d/comment/evj8wu5), there was at least one Vanilla *World of Warcraft* AddOn which contained a keylogger.  Apparently, the *Curse Client* once did, as well.  I can imagine that someone could also slip a keylogger into fan-made patches available from this or any other places.  If you're sure your e-mail address is secure, errant AddOns or extra patches may be the most likely culprit.

There's also a couple more, less feasible theories: They know you outside the game and have enough information about you to go snooping.
Some people are just jerks that like messing with their "friends".  Or… some people have personal vendettas they can't do anything about in "real life", so they act out online.  Petty, but to them, satisfying.

They have a game client that can somehow talk to the Master Server and find any user's IP address.
It's very remotely possible that there's a game client that has some sort of "trace-routing" functionality that can find your info, but in all likelihood, the culprit is your e-mail address.

 
That's my opinion, anyway.
