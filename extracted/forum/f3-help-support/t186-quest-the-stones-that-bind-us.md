# Quest: The Stones That Bind Us

## Post 966 by Hatberg — 2018-10-28T19:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=966#p966 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Swamp of Sorrows / Blasted Lands quest "The Stones That Bind Us" is uncompletable.

Whenever you reduce a Servant to 1% health and they become immune, you're supposed to channel the nearby obelisk. You see an animation that a spirit was freed, but you get no quest progression.
