# Goblin Brainwashing Device issue

## Post 75057 by Jamarshall — 2023-11-01T03:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75057#p75057 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I just bought the goblin brainwashing device from the item shop under the impression that this item would allow you to forego the gold cost associated with respeccing. In fact, it was a big reason I wanted to play on this server (being able to swap from pve spec to pvp spec without having to farm 50 gold each time).

Obviously, I found out this wasn't the case, but only after wasting 5 gold messing around with my primary and secondary spec.

Am I blameless? No, ignorance of what you are purchasing is not an excuse, but even with a class trainer when you go to respec, each time you do, it tells you exactly how much gold you are spending while doing so. Using the Brainwashing device just allows you to respec without notifying you how much gold you are about to spend.

I'm now out 5 gold on the fresh Tel'Abim server, which currently is a lot of money as the economy hasn't had a chance to develop given the server has just released.

I would like to request a gold refund from this for obvious reasons, but more importantly, I'd like to make the suggestion that the functionality of this item is much more defined in the item shop, as well as informing you how much gold you are spending while respeccing, as the cost increases each time you do.
