# Druid Ability - Ferocious Bite

## Post 1704 by Gifted (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2018-12-30T11:55:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1704#p1704 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I've recently noticed that Ferocious Bite doesn't seem to work properly. I noticed this on mobs that are immune to bleed effects (usually I spend my combo points on Rip for the bleed over time).

I've been stacking 5 combo points on mobs and then using Ferocious Bite and it does about 300-320 points of damage, but the tool tip says with 5 combo points I should be doing 490-530 points of damage increased by attack power. All of my other abilities do damage that matches the tool tips. Just checking if this is a bug or if I'm just a failure as a druid =(

<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1722#p1722 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Ferocious bite damage seem to be affected by the mob's armor rating. Hence you never do the full damage . I expect that's normal. Hence the choice for bleed, which actually does the full amount.

Personally I always do rip if mob is still half health and fb if it's lower :-)
