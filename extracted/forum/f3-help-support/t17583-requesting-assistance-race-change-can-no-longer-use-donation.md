Hi.

All information about the rules of use and return of items in the internal store of our server is described on the first page of the store.

Regarding your question I would like to inform you as follows:

Return of items that you purchased in the in-game store and did not use (Race Change, Faction Change, Name Change) can be returned within 48 hours after purchase. If you have used the item, it cannot be returned.

When changing race or faction unique items tied to the previous race or faction can also be returned within 48 hours after purchase. If from the moment of purchase more than 48 hours have passed and you decide to change race or faction, be prepared that the unique items will remain with you, but you will not be able to use them.

A clause is needed that explains that if you donate for a skin of one race, and race change, you lose the tokens for that skin from the previous race.

Had I known that I would have lost 160 tokens, I may not have race changed. I was under the impression after my race change it would be easy to petition a GM and get the tokens back for a skin for my new race.

Forcing a player to keep a skin for a race they are no longer playing is a really weird donation rule.
