Good afternoon!

All items are tied to a character. You cannot transfer them to other characters. Make your purchases consciously. You can issue a refund through the ticket system if 2 conditions are met:
 1. 48 hours have not passed since the purchase.
 2. Your character is not HC
If at least one of the conditions is violated, you will not be able to issue a refund.
You can read the rules here: <https://turtlecraft.gg/#/rules#token-refunds>.
