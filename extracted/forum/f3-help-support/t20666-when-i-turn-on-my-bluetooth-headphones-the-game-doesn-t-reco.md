Resurrecting this in case anyone has found a solution.

If I start game with speaker output, game sounds are sent appropriately to speakers.  If, during game, I swap to BT headphones, all is still well.  Game sounds sent to BT headphones.

But, that's it.  Any attempt to swap back to speakers means no sound coming from TWoW.  Turning BT headphones back on again, still no sound.  All other Windows 11 aps send sound to the correct output.  Win11 control panel sees the output devices, shows which one is active.  Volume mixer sees WoW as an output with a volume slider but moving it results in no sound.  Sound settings in TWoW show sound ON, CTRL-S to toggle sound does not solve problem.

So, it seems to be unique to TWoW but maybe not entirely?
