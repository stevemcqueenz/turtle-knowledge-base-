# Thalassian Highlands

## Post 83849 by Murdog — 2024-01-06T21:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83849#p83849 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Granted this isn't an immediate cause of concern, but for myself (and other players doing the Vagrant Challenge) there seems to be a lack of a weapons vendor for the High Elf starting zone, and likely for the Goblins too. As mentioned previously, these vendors serve a vital purpose especially for those doing the Vagrant's Endeavor challenge. Similar to how Northshire has the weapons vendor next to the mail armor vendor, I am hoping one could be implemented the same way in Thalassian Highlands. An additional vendor for the level 5 weapons such as the one in Goldshire inside of the blacksmithing area (sells the Gladius) would also be great to have in Thalassian Highlands and the Goblin starter zone.

Arcane Pounders suddenly casting arcane explosion for over ten times their melee damage seems a little strong and unexpected as well.
