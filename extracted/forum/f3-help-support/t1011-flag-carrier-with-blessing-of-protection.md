# Flag Carrier with Blessing of Protection

## Post 5699 by Pawpaw — 2020-04-16T10:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5699#p5699 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Greetings,

It has come to my attention that some paladins here are abusing a bug that allows them to re-pick the warsong flag after casting blessing of protection on themselves, making them immune to physical damage and slows for 10 seconds (and cancelling the ones that was already affected them) while carrying the flag.

I tried to find evidence to support that this was never a thing, but it turns out its quite difficult to prove a negative. Instead I will argue that if this had ever been possible outside private servers, 10 seconds immunity to physical damage and slows should be mentioned as a severely strong tactic in the warsong gulch strategy guides, right?

[https://classic.wowhead.com/guides/wars ... lassic-wow](https://classic.wowhead.com/guides/warsong-gulch-battleground-classic-wow)
[https://classic.wowhead.com/guides/clas ... sg#paladin](https://classic.wowhead.com/guides/classic-wow-warsong-gulch-advanced-phases-classes-wsg#paladin)

It is not.

Casting blessing of protection on a flag carrier is ripe for abuse, although this WAS a thing in vanilla, some players used mind-control and blessing of protection to make the enemy flag carrier drop the flag. It was fixed at some point, but I could not find evidence of when. Probably burning crusade or later.

While the absence of evidence is no proof. It is the best I can provide. I could not find specifically a video that shows a paladin using BoP and trying to repick the flag without success. However I am certain that most people will agree that this was never a thing in vanilla.
