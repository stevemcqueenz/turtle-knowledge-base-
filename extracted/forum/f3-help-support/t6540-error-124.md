Hello Error #124 (0x8510007c) in World of Warcraft is generally related to memory issues. Here are some steps you can take to fix this issue:

 1. Restart your computer: Sometimes memory problems can be caused by overworked system. Restarting your computer can help free up memory and fix the problem.

 2. Update your drivers: Make sure you have the latest drivers for your graphics card and other important components of your computer. Out-of-date drivers can cause memory issues and other performance issues.

 3. Close Other Programs: Make sure no other programs are open while playing WoW. Programs running in the background can consume memory and cause problems.

 4. Adjust game settings: If you are experiencing memory issues, you may need to lower your game's graphics settings. Try lowering the resolution, shadows, or detail of the models.

If the steps above do not help fix the problem, you can also try completely uninstalling and reinstalling the game. I hope this helps you to fix error #124 in World of Warcraft.
