You can only cancel warmode once before reaching 60. What likely happened is that you were still flagged for pvp after removing warmode, as it takes 5 minutes of no pvp activity to unflag. Warmpde (permanent flag) was rempved then, but you were still flagged normally. So now you might have to stay flagged until 60. I would try writing a ticket ingame and explain the situation. Mayve you get lucky!
