Hi there!

Try equipping your pickaxe in your character's hand and talking to the goblin again. Also, make sure your Mining skill level is high enough for the goblin to enchant your pickaxe.

Important! Remember that the pickaxe enchantment only works when it's equipped on your character. If the pickaxe is in your bag, the enchantment won't be active.
