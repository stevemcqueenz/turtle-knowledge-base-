I've been noticing this since my first playthrough to 60 - a lot of custom Turtle WoW quests have very vague and confusing descriptions and lots of times you don't know where you have to go or what you have to do.

I think one example was a Pandaren in Ratchet who would ask you to get him some ingredients for a brew and at one point you're left dumbfounded what is this item the NPC asks for and where you're supposed to get it from. After asking on the Discord until someone FINALLY responded, turns out it was some tea that is from a rare recipe and only Cooks can make it for you.

The stupid thing is there is really no way to find this out with just the game, you have to use the internet and all kinds of information and there is still no guarantee you will.

This is very bad quest design, it reminds me of Star Wars Galaxies, where I had to use the Wikia to just be able to follow the objectives on 60% of my quests throughout the entire game - imagine playing an MMORPG and having a browser with a Wikia at the ready just to be able to do the most basic thing in the game - leveling and questing.
