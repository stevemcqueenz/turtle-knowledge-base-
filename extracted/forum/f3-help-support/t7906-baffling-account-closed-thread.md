Dear Turtle WoW,

I have been playing Turtle wow for a few months now and I really enjoy it. I like the atmosphere and really like the custom content. I donated to Turtle WoW as I was very impressed with the quality of the custom content and understand that this could not have been easy for you guys to accomplish. I respect the work that you guys have done and have been telling all of my fellow gaming friends about Turtle WoW. A few of my real life friends are now playing Turtle WoW.

Recently my account was closed and I inquired as to why. The response I got was:

" Greetings! Hacking, botting and cheating is strictly prohibited on Turtle WoW."

Now I totally understand that hacking, botting and cheating is strictly prohibited on turtle wow, but I am completely caught off guard as to what I did that could in any way be construed as hacking botting or cheating.

I replied to the message above stating that I had not hacked, botted or cheated in any way and asked what it was that I did that Turtle considered hacking, botting or cheating. I then requested that my account be reinstated. I received a follow up that was nearly as baffling to me as the first message. It read:

"Greetings! As you have refused to read and acknowledge our terms of use, we have concluded that your ban will not be revoked."

The baffling part of this message is that after I got the first message I immediately read the rules and terms of use for Turtle WoW and was very careful to specifically read the section on cheating, botting, modifications and third party programs, bug and exploit abuse. I did not see any button to acknowledge reading the rules. I have now read the rules multiple times and could probably recite them to you from memory at this point. I also feel like this response was just cut and pasted as it wasn't responsive in any way to what I had wrote.

I don't believe I have violate any of these rules, but if I did, I assure you that it was either unintentionally done or I did not known that what I was doing was considered a violation. To be honest the only thing that comes to mind is that the last time I was logged in my internet went out and I was in the middle of an Arathi Basin game. I don't know what happened to my character as I did not have internet for three days due to the weather conditions damaging spectrum internets servers. I can only assume that my character just sat there in AB doing nothing and got flagged AFK.  This was not intentional and it seems a bit harsh to be banned for being AFK once in a battleground.

I don't use any cheats or modifications to client files, I don't use or promote any third party programs to control my characters, and I don't use or promote any bugs or exploits and I promise not to ever do those things while playing Turtle WoW.

I want to believe that the staff of Turtle WoW are strict but fair. I feel like a ban from the game is a bit harsh and I would ask that you reconsider reactivating my account. I promise to follow all of the rules.

Sincerely,

Romy
