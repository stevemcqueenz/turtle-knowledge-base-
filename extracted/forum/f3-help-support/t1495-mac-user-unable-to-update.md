That's the whole point of using the Mac Native client. Not everyone wants to use Wine (which isn't as plug and play for many), Play on Mac is somewhat easy but some people may have issues and/or Bootcamp, and may still be on an older Macbook. The Mac Vanilla Client worked fine before this and it's unfair to say "Ok now do this completely different thing". If the developers don't want to find a way to support it, that's their choice and while unfortunate - I understand, they've poured a lot of effort into the expansion to be success. Many people have very fond memories of playing WoW on their Black Macbook from like 2006 (me), and like using older hardware to player older games for nostalgia. At the very least, they should look into it and find a viable solution. More players is more players.
