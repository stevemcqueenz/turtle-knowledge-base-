Hi, extract the client, move the folder to your C: drive root (or any other drive).
Mine look like this (I renamed my folder, default name is turtle_client_116)
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/TVshXDg.png)
Then open the folder and right click on WoW.exe > send a copy to your desktop.
![Image](https://i.imgur.com/qNFerU3.png)
Double click WoW.exe shortcut on your desktop to launch the game.
