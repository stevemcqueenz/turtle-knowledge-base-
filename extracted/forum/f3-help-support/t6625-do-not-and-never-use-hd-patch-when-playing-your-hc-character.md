## Post 41746 by Shener — 2023-04-16T18:39:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41746#p41746 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

6 hours ago (About 11:55 Am GMT). My alliance level 30 mage, HC character was turning in the quest “The Missing Diplomat” in Stormwind Keep, then I am about to get out and my game crashed with an error message (I was using Turtle HD patch modding, link: [viewtopic.php?t=2827](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=2827), my game has also crashed sometimes in Goldshire where everyone waited for tents, I was not sure the patch is the reason before). After mere secs I relog on my account, but IMMEDIATELY, my character died, meaning the screen grayed out. I did NOT see any attack (and who can attack a NO-PVP player in Stormwind?)

After talking to support via Discord, I feel the HD patch is the key. When Onyxia reveals herself and her minions in Stormkeep, the patch will not able to load so many models together, and that makes the game crash just like those in Goldshire. Meanwhile, The game crash will not make the character disappear immediately, who still remained an easy standing-still target for Ony and that's why I can only see a corpse when I relog in.

Sigh. In this server, one can never appeal for an HC death no matter what. So for your own sake, leave this, or any modding methods away when you are leveling your HC characters. You do not want this extremely frustrating moment to happen to you.

## Post 41751 by Johnedward — 2023-04-16T19:34:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41751#p41751 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

While the HD patch may or may not increase the odds of having Error #132 crash on Turtle, it isn't anywhere near the only reason for it (in fact it's one of the most common crash reasons for any wow version, [including current retail](https://esports.gg/guides/world-of-warcraft/how-to-fix-world-of-warcraft-error-132-0x85100084-fatal-exception/))

I've personally had it happen multiple times with and without the HD patch, but keeping all addons and modifications at a minimum with HC characters is a really good idea as they will increase the chances of something going wrong.
