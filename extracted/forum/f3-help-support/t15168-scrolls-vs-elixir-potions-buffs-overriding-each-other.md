# Scrolls vs elixir/potions buffs overriding each other

## Post 103000 by Zigfryd81 — 2024-10-13T07:09:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103000#p103000 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello,
Was this intended to have buffs non-stack when they target the same stat but are having a different origin/source?
Vanilla WoW and other p. server like Vanilla+ have those in a separate category where they are allowed to stack. Which is the preferred and intuitive way. However on Turtle this behaves differently, here couple of examples:
 1) a Scroll of Strength (+5) and a Elixir of Strength (+4) where applying both "works", but only the stronger version is applied in reality and the lower is canceled (although both buffs are still visible in GUI at the top!). Meaning the end result is +5 only (and not +5 +4 = +9 sadly).
 2) a Scroll of Protection(+60) and a Elixir of Minor Defense (+50) - works a bit differently, than the example above, in a way that the stronger buff cancels the lesser one and has GUI only display the stronger buff as applied (in contrast to the previous example where both buffs are retained in GUI).
 3) a Scroll of Intellect (+5) and a mage spell "Arcane Intellect" - cancel each other out which is stronger and leave only a single buff in GUI as well.

So all these behave a bit inconsistently as far as GUI is concerned, and I would like to know the official stand whether scroll vs consumables vs class spells buffs are to be stackable or not?   unhappy_turtle
That would make my decision easier, as that's something that is a trivial issue to some, but a make-or-brake issue to me.

P.s. I don't care about raiding and "high end" activities, so early low levelling is where this sort of things come into play for me where having +110 armor on a clothie is a big deal   happy_turtle_head

Simply put many private servers get these effects wrong. Tue way it works on turtle (aka them not stacking is "correct" for 1.12.1 reference client)

Which leaves the question if its better if they stack or not?

And the answer to this is a resounding - better if not.
For most classes, a scroll you find is a temporary powergain or at worst some free cash since you can save on a elixir instead.

But if they do stack, you create an environment where raiders will aggressively buy them - to the point where people rather hoard & sell them than just use them if they find them.

The version we have now is just way more engaging as a whole
