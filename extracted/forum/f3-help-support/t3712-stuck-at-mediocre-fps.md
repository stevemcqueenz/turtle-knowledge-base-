## Post 22359 by Akalix (Turtle WoW Team) [STAFF] — 2022-08-14T22:17:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22359#p22359 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

For performance and low fps issues please try to:
• Disable AddOns
• Disabling hardware-cursor in the graphics options.
• Fiddling with v-sync on and off.

For  v-sync off, turn it off, and switch to windowed mode full screen.

Try to limit in FPS in your GPU control panel.
For 60/75 Hz monitors, limit FPS to: 64
For 120/144/165/240 Hz monitors, limit FPS to: 120

Unfortunately the game is not made to run in the 100's of FPS, as it was developed for much weaker systems.
