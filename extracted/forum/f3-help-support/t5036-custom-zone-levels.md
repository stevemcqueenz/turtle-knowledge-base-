Well not so much to list.
There is 2 actual new zones with mobs in them: Gillijim and Lapidis isles next to STV. Mobs there are 48-54. Tons of new quests on these isles for both factions.
There are 2 subzones with new mobs in them - almost exclusively for horde quests:
 1. Tirisfal uplands (15-20) - and a small high elf questline for lvl 30+ here.
 2. Zul Razas (in northern arathi) (mobs around 40-43).

There are 2 lvling instances:
Crescent Grove (34-39) in southern ashenvale
Hateforge quarry (48-56) in eastern burning steppes.

There are 2 max lvl instances:
Stormwind vaults and Karazan Crypts.

There are a lot of new quest hubs around the world, but no actual new mobs - they send u to already existing vanilla areas to do the killing.
