Please, consider the following:
> **In the Topic "Hardcore Rules", Torta wrote: Sat Mar 06, 2021 3:20 pm**
> If you choose to create and play a hardcore character, you do so at your own risk […]

Much like with Retail *World of Warcraft* – or really, *any* online game – there will always be those players who decide that it's infinitely amusing to pick on the weak, unaware, or seemingly vulnerable.  *World of Warcraft* has an "opt-in" system for player-vs-player interaction.  This means that if the player dies to another player while questing, it is entirely their fault because they managed to flag themselves for combat.

This server, in particular, automatically flags players for PvP if they accept the "War Mode" challenge, which grants extra Experience Points at the risk of being stuck in PvP mode.  However, almost *all* known servers flag the player if they:
 - Attack a hostile player (marked with a yellow or red name)
 - Heal or buff a *friendly* player (which have a green name)
 - Wander into the major cities of the opposite faction (Stormwind, Orgrimmar, et cetera)

Players with blue names are *not* flagged for PvP and safe to cast spells on.  My advice is to play a bit more cautiously, next time, and be more aware of your surroundings.
