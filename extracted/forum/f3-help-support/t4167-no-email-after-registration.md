Since I see people are checking this post and there have been several other posts like this with no answer, I found a work around to my no registration email problem.

After checking several pages of posts and even trying a youtube video, I found a post that states there is a known issue with hotmail and yahoo emails.

So try making a new turtle wow account using an outlook or gmail email account. That worked for me.
