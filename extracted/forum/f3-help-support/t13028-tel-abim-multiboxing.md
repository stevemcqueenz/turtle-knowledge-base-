Greetings!
The rules for multiboxing on a PvP server are different from those on a PvE server, so there is no need for comparison. On a PvP server, multiboxing is generally prohibited, with the exception of using three accounts for Summoning Rituals. This decision was made to ensure fair play and prevent any unfair advantages gained through multiboxing. Now, let me address your questions:

 1. *Who am I bothering when I help my low-level character on a server with 200 people playing?* - By helping your low-level character through multiboxing, you are actually breaking a server rule that forbids this practice.

 2. *When I'm farming DM and I need to register /camp, I usually launch a second window to invite an alt, so as not to bother the guild. Is this also prohibited? Will I be banned? Seriously?* - We do allow multiboxing on PvP servers for certain purposes such as summoning, auction, and enchanting items for your alt. However, it is important to note that your characters should not move together in the game world, do quests, or participate in dungeons, among other activities.

 3. *Multiboxing has always referred to managing multiple accounts simultaneously using software, but I manually control my characters by switching between two windows. Who am I bothering, and why can't I do this?* - The use of software for multiboxing is prohibited on all our servers. As for PvP servers, I have already addressed this in the first paragraph.

I hope this clarifies the multiboxing rules on our PvP server.
