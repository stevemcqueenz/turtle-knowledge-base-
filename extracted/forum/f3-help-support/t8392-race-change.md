I kind of wonder how hard it would be to add something like that into Vanilla…  Probably a lot of trouble.  If my understanding of Vanilla is at all accurate, I believe a complete race change would entail the following:

- Allow **only** Races applicable to the character's Class.
(No Gnome Druids or Goblin Shamans.)
- Change the player's Race value.
- Remove Race-specific items from the player's equipment, where applicable.
- Adjust the player's current stats for Race-specific stat modifiers.
(For example, Humans has a base Strength of 15 while Orcs have a base Strength of 23.)
- Add and remove Race-specific… uh… Racial skills.
(These aren't "locked" to specific races, in the database.  If checks weren't made, you could have, say, a Human with Water Breathing (Undead) or an Orc with Stoneskin (Dwarf).)
- Add Race-specific quest reward spells, where applicable.
- Adjust the player's current Faction standings.
(Negligible if you're changing to a race already in your Faction, but **undeniable** important if you're changing from, say, an Alliance Gnome to a Horde Goblin.)
I'm sure there's other things I missed, but you get the gist.  There's *a lot* more to consider that simply "changing a number".  (:
