> **Aluludar wrote: Fri Feb 03, 2023 10:43 pm**
> I went through that whole Demon Fall canyon area and couldn't find any kind of cave.  Is the quest reward even worth it?

Yes, the quest is worth doing.  The [rewards](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=70033) are your choice of a nice ring or a nice wand.

Here's a guide: enter Demon Fall Canyon from Felfire Hill. Follow the path south.  **Do not take the first left going up**, that's the wrong way.  Keep heading straight to the Monument to Grom Hellscream.  When you enter the Monument area, to the immediate right there is a [path heading up](https://i.imgur.com/Uw4Hg5W.png), which may be hard to see as it will be hidden behind the root of a tree as you enter.  Take this path up and you will enter Demon Fall Ridge.  You will find yourself in an area that a small camp with a few friendly NPCs (one is name Olom) and a mailbox.  This is the guild house for the guild <Ered Ruin>.  Behind it is the [barrow den](https://i.imgur.com/7M8408R.png) you are looking for.  Enter it, and I believe you need to go down down down fighting your way through mobs until you discover what you're looking for.
