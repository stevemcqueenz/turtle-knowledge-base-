yea, no tooltips show spell coefficients. The reason spellpower gear says "up to x damage" is because that number is what you would have added to your spells if they were 100% scaling. holy strike I believe is 40% scaling after level 20, so you get 40% of whatever spellpower added to your holy strike. Before that the coefficient will be drastically less so any spellpower will change the damage very little
