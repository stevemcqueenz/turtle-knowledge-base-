Hello, I appeal against some players' unfair practices in the game.

My character is Lucildena and is a warlock.

At around 12:00 London time on September 16th, I joined a 14 person team led by Hunter ERMOR in Zulgrab. The allocation rules of the team indicate that there is one wishing equipment+one requirement equipment, and other equipment belongs to the greedy range.

After winning the first boss, I also have a record of Zulgrab and can no longer enter other copies of Zulgrab.

During the allocation, a mage with a name beginning with S (I'm sorry I couldn't remember his long name) made a clear request for a piece of blue equipment, which others understood as using the sole right of demand. After defeating the first boss, priest chongmingjue claimed that his equipment was poor and wanted this blue piece of equipment. Everyone else understood that he had used the only right of demand, so after these two individuals' ROLL points, no one else followed the ROLL points, that is, no one else was willing to use their own right of demand for those two blue equipment. Team leader Ermore also assigned the purple set components to them.

After defeating the boss, the mage and priest also requested equipment, which caused me great dissatisfaction. And the explanation from Captain ERMOR is that both demand and greed must be based on profession and talent, that is, only priests have the right to demand and greed for  clothing equipment with healing attributes. That is to say, as long as  clothing healing equipment is produced, priests can definitely obtain it. It should be added that there is only one priest in the team.

So I made it clear that when it comes to death mines, do you choose greed based on profession and talent? When there are 10 BRDs, do you choose greed based on profession and talent? So why did you choose greed here to become based on talent and profession?

And while discussing this, I finally realized that this is a team formed based on several friends led by Hunter ERMOR. This Zulgrab dungeon is largely aimed at improving equipment for some of them. But this type of team with a clear allocation bias from the beginning did not explain the rules in advance and used the ambiguity of the rules to fight for their own interests.

After a minute or so of arguing, I was removed from the team, and I thought they could scam another person to continue improving their equipment.

As far as I know, these people include:

Hunter Ermor

Druid Sosmart

Priest Chongmingjue

Mage S.....

Paladin F......

I didn't remember the names of the mage's and paladin's, but they were in the same dungeon progress. There are also some people who are their companions but have not joined the argument.

I have participated in both the US and UK public beta tests, and I really hope that the WOW world can maintain its initial friendly attitude and not let this messy situation contaminate WOW. In Turtle-wow, there is no money-workroom or G group here, it's just a clean WOW. I really like it here and hope it can continue for a long time. Therefore, I cannot accept such things happening. I hope to make WOW better through my own efforts, and there are fewer bad people here.
