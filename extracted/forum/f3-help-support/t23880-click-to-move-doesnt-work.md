I lowered the game's frame rate to 59 (via the NVIDIA control panel), and both bugs disappeared. Now follow and click-to-move work perfectly. One strange thing: these bugs have been known about for years, and there's no information anywhere that they're may caused by the frame rate. Only one guy casually posted about it in an old bug report. I'd suggest pinning this in the FAQ.
A lot of people are having problems with the most basic character control functions, a lot of bug reports about it, and there is no official information or recommendations for fixing it anywhere.
