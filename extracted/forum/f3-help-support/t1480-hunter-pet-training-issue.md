## Post 8812 by Roxanneflowers (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2020-12-29T20:05:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8812#p8812 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This is one of those problems that REALLY depends on the specifics and context to provide you a correct answer.

When you Untrain Pet you just wipe out all of their Pet Skills (Growl, Claw, Bite, etc.).
In order to retrain your Pet with new skills, you need to open up the Beast Training spell from your spellbook.  The contents of your Beast Training options depend on what your Hunter can train to your Pets ... not on what the Pet originally had.  The Untrain Pet is a "bulk erase to clean slate" for your Pet, not a "revert to what they had when you tamed them" option.

If your Hunter has never learned the skills from Taming then those skills will not be available to train your Pet with after use of Untrain Pet via the Beast Training spell from your spellbook.  If you don't HAVE a Beast Training spell in your spellbook, then you haven't yet completed the Level 10 Hunter class quest that grants the Beast Training spell to you and you would need to go finish that quest to be able to do any Beast Training at all.
