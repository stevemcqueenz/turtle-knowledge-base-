Currently, same way as in OG vanilla. Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring, get to a gate that usually has a mystical barrier preventing access, then go back out and do some mountain/wall scaling to drop into the zone. In other words, it's not officially implemented on Turtle yet, but you can go there and check the place out. It's slightly different than it was in vanilla, I believe, but not too much.
