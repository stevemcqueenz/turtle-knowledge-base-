# Addons "suddenly" not working after a server update??

## Post 9959 by Daddym0e — 2021-04-02T02:49:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9959#p9959 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

As odd as it is, and I'm still not entirely certain WHY it happened, but the 4 addons that I have incorporated into the "C:\TurtleWoW\Turtle WoW\Interface\AddOns"  folder created their *OWN* folders inside "C:\TurtleWoW".
This caused them most of them to load, but not be entirely functional. Once I deleted the directories of those addons from C:\TurtleWoW and relaunched the game. All came back online and functional. I do not know *WHY* the other directories were created. Only that removing them allowed the ones in C:\TurtleWoW\Turtle WoW\Interface\AddOns to become functional again.
