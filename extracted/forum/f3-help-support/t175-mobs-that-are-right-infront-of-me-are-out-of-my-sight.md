# Mobs that are right infront of me are "out of my sight"

## Post 908 by Feramos — 2018-10-24T12:22:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=908#p908 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

In the Lost Rigger Cove in Tanaris are pirate mobs on the docks.
First of all, they respawn insanely fast.
Second, for some reason you sometimes cant cast at mobs that are standing on the docks even if they're right infront of you. They proceed to walk around you until they stand right inside of you.
Tested on a Priest.
