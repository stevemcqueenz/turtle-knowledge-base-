# Few things

## Post 268 by Blight — 2018-09-25T14:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=268#p268 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Friendly gnomes spawn among leper ones near Gnomeregan.

Dying near Gnomeregan spawns you in Coldridge GY.

Mobs (at least at Coldridge starting area) take their time responding to certain class skills. They'll finish walking to their destination before attacking if being attacked by rogue while moving.

Mob density feels bit high, at least in starting zones. And respawn timers bit fast for solo clump clearing to some objectives.

Way too many battered chests around Goldshire compared to other low lvl zones.

Some travelling vendor paths are only half set. Like one from Eastvale logging camp, after getting to Goldshire just sprints back across woods.
