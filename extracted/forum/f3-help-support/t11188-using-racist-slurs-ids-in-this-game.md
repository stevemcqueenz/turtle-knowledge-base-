I've been playing around with chat 'bots powered by GPT, recently.  In one engine, in particular, my chats have been interrupted by "friendly reminders" that some of the language I'm using "may be considered offensive to certain people or groups".  Here's some of the language I was notified about:

 - "Punk rocker"
 - "Dumb"
 - "Nii-san"

That particular word mentioned in the opening post means absolutely nothing to me.  I'm not saying it's fair, but I *am* saying that what's offensive to "someone" may not be offensive to "everyone".

Further, because the EU realms are tailored mostly for English-speaking languages, it's relatively easy to find and censor English-based words and phrases which are universally accepted as such, but it would be *intensely difficult*  to pinpoint each and every word in each and every language.

Your best bet is to file a GM Ticket in game and explain your rationale.  Worst scenario?  You get ignored.  Best scenario?  Someone gets an unexplained name-change flag then comes on the forum to complain about it.  Either way, someone's gonna be unhappy.
