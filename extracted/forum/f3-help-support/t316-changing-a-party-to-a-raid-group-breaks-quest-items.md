# Changing a party to a raid group breaks quest items

## Post 1672 by Agentfluffles — 2018-12-26T01:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1672#p1672 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

FYI. Was questing with some fellows, and we changed the party into a raid.  Somehow this ended up breaking quest item drops and to fix we all had to leave the raid party.

## Post 1673 by Sinrek (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2018-12-26T02:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1673#p1673 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

FYI you don't quest while in raid, since game consider this as a cheating and all quests were designed for party wide size on purpose so you do not get disbalanced advantage.

TLDR: working as intended.

## Post 1679 by Tokka — 2018-12-26T20:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1679#p1679 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

What Sinrek says is true, regular quests are not achievable while in a Raid. The only quests that will work while in a Raid party are ones that are designed for Raids specifically.
