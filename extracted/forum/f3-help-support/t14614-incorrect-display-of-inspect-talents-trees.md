The inspection stuff has known problems.  Inspection of talents also doesn't work at all with some addons for inspecting.  Even without addons, certain talents are either out of bounds or have the ? icon and potentially-inaccurate text.  Even when clearing cache/WDB and not using ANY addons, it doesn't fix this bug at all.

The 1h weapon thing is because it is likely 'sheathed' and you need to unsheath it using a keybind or getting into combat.

Also based on your screenshot you appear to be using some MPQ patch files for enhanced textures.
