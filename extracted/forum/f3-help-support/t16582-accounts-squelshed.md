Mute issued for violations of the in-game chat rules cannot be canceled.
This restriction will last for the allotted time specified in the system message that you see when you log in to the game or when you try to write something in the chat.
You can familiarize yourself with the rules of the in-game chat here: <https://turtlecraft.gg/rules#chat-abuse-tab-1>.
