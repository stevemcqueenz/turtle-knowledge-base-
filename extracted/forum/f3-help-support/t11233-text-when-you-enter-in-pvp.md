The texts when you enter in PvP
This is something that has been bothering me for a while and hasn't been fixed.
When you enter PvP, your faction logo appears next to your portrait with the following text:
Alliance: "A proud member of the Alliance, enemy of the members of the Horde: Orcs, Trolls, Tauren and Undead"
Horde: "A Proud member of the Horde, enemy of the members of the Alliance: "Humans, Dwarves, Gnomes and Night Elves"
The thing is, the names of the new races have never been added to these texts, and since it feels incomplete, it would be good if with the new PvP server they fixed this on both servers.
Alliance: "A proud member of the Alliance, enemy of the members of the Horde: Orcs, Trolls, Tauren, Undead and Goblins."
Horde: "A Proud member of the Horde, enemy of the members of the Alliance: "Humans, Dwarves, Gnomes, Night Elves and High Elves."
