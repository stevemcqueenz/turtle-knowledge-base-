## Post 39921 by Lien — 2023-04-02T02:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39921#p39921 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Do you have any characters above level 20? If you do, try speaking in /guild, /general, or /world and see whether the problem persists. Since you mentioned the affected character being level 12, it may be part of an ongoing issue that's currently being investigated. Some players have reported being unable to send messages in /guild, /general, or /world.

It seems to largely affect characters Level 20 and under; as soon as you reach Level 21, the issue will often resolve itself. I don't know of any definite causes at the moment, but some add-ons have been shown to exacerbate the issue (notably those that handle a lot of information, such as TurtleRP).

Though it can understandably be a bit frustrating, given the nature of HC, the most we can do is patiently wait for an update at the moment.

If you want to test whether it might be something on your end, other things you can try are disabling your current add-ons one-at-a-time to see whether any of them are adversely affecting your ability to send messages.
