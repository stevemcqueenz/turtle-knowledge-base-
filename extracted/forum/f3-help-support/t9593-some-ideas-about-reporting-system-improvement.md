# Some ideas about reporting system improvement

## Post 62788 by Reaperzhang — 2023-09-11T09:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=62788#p62788 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello , Game administrators,

I'm a WOW superfan for more than 10 years. Appreciate this platform to enter the WOW again, your work is amazing and it's really great. :)

I ofter reported and GM's response made me impressive, comfortable and satisfy. Please forgive my pool English when I submitted the ticket.
I have wrote below ideas to improve the report ticket system.

First, could you add a shortcut to quick report the guys whom is halting in the game?
My option is to add a simple button when right click the player and it will notice this player in a short time, the character will be highlight with an shining aura, if no action(no mob killing, saying words, click nearby objects etc.) in a warning timer, the character will be marked automatically and will be unable to log in for a longer period as punishment.

Second, A proper AFK timer could be adjusted under different server pressure condition(the higher pressure, the shorter AFK timer).

Third, I think the report system form should be upgraded with some standard field required/optional such as character name, behavior, location/place, time, the required detail could help GMs to do their work better and save their time. A guidance may be good to help the new player to do that.

At last, reporting the hanging up guys are benefit for all online players so I think they should be rewarded, some special water/food/fashion coin/a thank letter etc. It's easy to build up the positive cycle and correct log in/log out pattern.

I can guess the guys next action to against this in the future.
They may hide their characters to an instance/below water/cave to avoid other players to see them.
May go back to the log in screen and log in different characters to prevent hanging out.
More advanced technical detection method/tool will be need.

Thanks for your patience and reading this.
