# Vagrant's Endeavor at 60 - can't Enchant equipped Gear

## Post 72104 by Kr00zer — 2023-10-19T13:44:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=72104#p72104 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Already posted this on the Bug Tracker but figured posting it here again might save someone a few Coins until it's fixed.

 **Please, provide a clear description what the bug is:**

After reaching Level 60 you still cannot Enchant equipped Gear and you'll lose your Materials/Item.
(Only exception I found so far was the Big Iron Fishing Pole now allowing me to apply a Lure at 60)
 **However, this can be avoided by Un-equipping the Item and applying the Enchant in your Inventory. You'll be able to Equip it once enchanted without losing anything.**
(Haven't tried removing the Vagrant's Endeavor Glyph yet, not sure if that'll also Remove the Wanderer Title.)

 **Steps to reproduce the behavior with as much detail as possible:**

 1. Apply any Enchant to an Equipped Item, like a Sniper Scope to a Ranged Weapon or a Stamina Enchant on your Bracers.
 2. An Error Message will appear letting you know the Character is undergoing the Vagrant's Endeavor Challenge and your Materials/Item used for the Enchant will disappear.

 **IDs of affected creatures, items, quests or spells with a link to the relevant page:**

Item: [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=22 ... s-bindings](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=22011/beastmasters-bindings)
Spell: [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=1 ... er-stamina](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=13648/enchant-bracer-stamina)
Item: [https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=20 ... lugthrower](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=20722/crystal-slugthrower)
Spell: <https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=10548/sniper-scope>

 **Expected behavior. Describe how it should work:**

Since I reached Level 60 and the Challenge is over, allowing me to Wear any Gear like a Normal Player, the Enchants should be applicable equipped or not.
