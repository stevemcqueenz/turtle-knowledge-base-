# Careful what you ask for

## Post 70069 by Breezy — 2023-10-10T22:23:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70069#p70069 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I logged in yesterday and seen my voidwalker's name had been changed randomly after a server restart. I submitted a ticket and asked if it was possible to get the old name back. The GM was super helpful and got it corrected for me quickly. I suppose I should have asked about the fine print because it also reset all of my learned grimoires. I submitted another ticket asking if it would be possible to get the skills restored since I didn't have the gold to relearn them. Being on my first character, on my first playthrough of wow, has been a very gold-less experience so far. I got a quick reply back saying I was on my own. 10 gold is hardly a big deal, but in hindsight I would have waited until I could afford them again before resetting him. Not complaining so much as warning other people who may find themselves in a similar situation.

The hours I was playing trying to figure out why he was all of a sudden much worse than usual was pretty funny in hindsight. It wasn't until my sacrifice ability didn't go off in a dire pvp situation I realized something was definitely off. I went to see if I had unmapped the hotkey by mistake when I finally realized his action bar was mostly empty. Still happy to get the old name back though, no regrets. Thank you GM who restored it.
