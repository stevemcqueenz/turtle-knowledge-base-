## Post 50290 by Niralthas — 2023-06-16T16:18:00Z

There is indeed a scheduled maintenance. See #announcements in Discord.

 **We unfortunately must announce that Part 1 of our Hardcore Update: Inferno Mode, has been delayed to release on Friday, June 16, 2023 6:00 PM.**

This decision was not easy, and was made to ensure ample time is available to bugtest and ensure the quality of this new challenge. We look forward to venturing into the Inferno Mode and facing new challenges with you all 17 minutes ago… and remember, there is no turning back, and there are no revivals under any circumstances.

In lighter news, we are looking to address some of the biggest complaints in the game currently.

Class Changes: We’ve been reviewing the results of our previous class changes, as well as the removal of World Buffs in raids. Soon, we will be opening applications for experienced players from all classes to contribute to the future of class balance on Turtle. More information soon.

Endgame Itemization is also definitely in an unbalanced state where some earlier tier items overtake equipment from later tiers, and we have changes planned to begin to address this and make the overall experience better by buffing, nerfing, and reworking items as needed.

PvP has a handful of flaws currently, some of which will be addressed with the previously mentioned changes, however, we are also looking into deeper changes to improve the overall experience of playing PvP on Turtle.

Stay tuned for more & thank you for playing on Turtle!
