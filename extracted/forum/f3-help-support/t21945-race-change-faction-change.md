What exactly do you want to know?
Basically you only need to check if the desiring race can be your actuall class and thats it.
Make sure you do the change in some neutral place out of towns.
All equipment even from quest dedicated just for one side (Horde/Alliance) will remain.
Even racial mounts. Reputation is exchanged 1:1 according to race distribution.
That means Stormwind (Human) - Orgrimmar (Orc), Ironforge (Dwarf) - Undercity (Undead) etc..

Race change also include one token for change your appearence.

## Post 152225 by Thule (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2025-09-18T10:50:00Z

Ok Imma try to describe it step by step as I remember it.

STEP 1:
Make sure you are in some place out of faction towns. The ideal place for that is one of the neutral goblin towns like Ratchet, Booty Bay, Everlook etc.

STEP 2:
Purchase the Race Change token from shop. In your bags should appear 1 race change token and also 1 appearence token. Both items has description tooltips when you hover over them.

STEP 3:
Click on race change and It should immediately change your toon to desired Race with some random look.

STEP 4:
Logg out and create a new lvl1 character of the race youve just changed into with your main. Create this character the way you would like your main character to look. Remember the name of this new character. We named him for example "Newchar"

STEP 5:
Logg in back with your main. Press Enter and type .copy Newchar.
Your toon now should look like Newchar. Maybe you need just relog once again to display everything correctly.

So yeah its pretty much it.
