# I hope GM can seriously review my situation

## Post 87193 by Skyang0409 — 2024-02-07T14:25:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87193#p87193 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I have been playing here for more than half a year since July 2023 when I arrived at the Turtle Suit. The reason that attracted me here in the first place was because of the serious and responsible work management of Turtle Service GM. Eliminate the vast majority of bad game players and game plug-in users. I love this server. I'm a PVE player, so 99.9% of my time is between Copy and AH. Because of the love of servers, I spent a lot of time on the illusion of equipment and various prestige. My alchemy skills are also very complete, so I would like to say that I love my account and I cherish it very much.
And three days ago, it was blocked. By the GM I have always admired the reply to my station is that I double-opened into PVP, a violation. And a second violation. The first violation sent me a double-open account that I did not recognize. So I filed an internal complaint, but I never got any reply. As for my second violation, I think it was completely unconscious, I just accidentally hit the yellow character account while killing monsters, thus entering PVP mode. And then being attacked by players who like PVP, I didn't even react in the first place. So I used my own number to add blood to myself (I thought it was the BOSS in the game beating me). Even if this behavior really violated the rules of the server, I also accept. But this is really my first offense, so isn't the 30-day ban too strict? Know how deadly this can be for DPK's PVE players. It would mean the loss of more than half a year's worth of work. And all this is because I didn't intentionally violate the server rules.
I came here to post because my account management has not responded to the letter sent by the game administrator for more than 24 hours, maybe he thinks I am sophifying. But I do wish. I've always been a law-abiding player, and I've never been a PVP player, and I've turned off war mode. It's all just a coincidence.
I hope the administrator can seriously verify my situation and impose appropriate penalties on my account. thank you.   neutral_turtle_head
