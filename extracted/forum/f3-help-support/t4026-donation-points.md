## Post 24336 by Thol (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2022-09-04T07:37:00Z

Yes appearance change copy another character of the same race appearance. If you want to change race/faction you have to make a ticket. Explain in the ticket that you wasted your appearance token and it should get refunded.

> The price for the purchase of a race change is 200 tokens.
>
>  To purchase:
>
>  Please open an ingame ticket from the character you want to change and say to what race.
>
>  Please make a new character that the GM can copy his looks. The character can be a different gender. The gender will be copied. Say the name of the character in a ticket.
>
>  Races are class locked. You can only change to a race that has your class.
>
>  Race changes do not come with free name changes,those are separate purchases.
