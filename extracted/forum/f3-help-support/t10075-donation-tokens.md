Did you spend your Tokens on in-game items, such as Portable Black Holes or character skins / illusions?

- **If no**, then you should still have a Tokens on your account.
Turtle Tokens are bind-on-account, not bind-on-character.

- **If yes**, then the Tokens are gone because you spent them, already.
Items are non-refundable on Softcore characters, I'm sorry to say.
As an aside, by playing on this or any other server, it's implied that you both understand and agree to all of the written rules.  You can be angry at the server for their policies, but it doesn't change the fact that you are the one who made the error.  But, for what it's worth, I think there *should* be a "leeway" period on certain purchases, especially in the case of a character being deleted, where Turtle Tokens *are* automatically refunded.  30 minutes, maybe.  It's not much, but that's more than enough time to compensate for a general "I wasn't paying attention" mistake.
