Vanilla release requirements:
> Windows 98/ME/2000/XP
>  800 MHz or higher CPU
>  32 MB 3D graphics card with hardware transform and lighting, such as GeForce 2 or better
>  4 GB or more of available hard drive space
>  256 MB or more of RAM
>
>  MacOS X 10.3.5 or newer
>  933 MHz or higher G4 or G5 processor
>  ATI or NVIDIA video hardware with 32 MB VRAM or more
>  56k or higher modem with an Internet connection

You are good to go in 2018 on Windows 7/8/10 if you have at least a 1,6 GHz Core2Duo CPU, 2GB RAM and a 128MB Geforce 6600 GPU. Modern integrated graphics like Intel HD 3000 or better work aswell. 1GB RAM is sufficient on WIndows XP.

You need much better hardware on large servers, no worries on Turtle WoW  smiling_turtle
