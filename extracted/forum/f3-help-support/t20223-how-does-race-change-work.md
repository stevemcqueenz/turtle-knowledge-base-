## Post 138518 by Gladeshadow (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2025-07-09T04:45:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138518#p138518 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Yes, you determine the appearance when you make a race change. How it worked when I did it is you make another character with the appearance you want on the same account as the character you want to race change. Then you enter a command in game and your character's race and appearance will change to that of the other character you made.
I know the system has gone through some changes, so the technical details may have changed.
