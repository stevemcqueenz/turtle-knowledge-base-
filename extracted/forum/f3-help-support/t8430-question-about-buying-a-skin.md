if i buy a skin do i have to make a new character or will it be used on my character i buy it with?

## Post 54432 by Monq2q — 2023-07-24T09:01:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54432#p54432 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Buying a skin or illusion will give you an item that when interacted with applies the skin/illusion. Skins have to be used on a character of the correct race. Illusions can be applied across all races but are not perfect and may have missing/broken animations.

Dont know what happens if you attempt to purchase a skin that isn't for your race but I imagine you get an error or something.
