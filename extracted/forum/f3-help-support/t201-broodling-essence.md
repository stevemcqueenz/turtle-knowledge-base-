# Broodling Essence

## Post 1076 by Hatberg — 2018-11-01T10:52:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1076#p1076 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

The Searing Gorge quest Broodling Essence
<https://classicdb.ch/?quest=4726>
Grants a zapper (Draco-Incarcinatrix 900) to use on the Whelps, so a crystal will spawn after you kill it. You need to click the crystal to progress the quest.

You can use the zapper multiple times on the same whelp, and multiple crystals will spawn.
