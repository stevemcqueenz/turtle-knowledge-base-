While soloing as a rogue and even in groups sometimes, I use the technique where I judge my energy and gouge my enemy so I can spin around and get an extra backstab in the mix. Sometimes I can do it twice.

My problem is that when I gouge an enemy it seems to turn in a random direction and it's facing is not what my screen shows. When I spin around to backstab, sometimes I can't find where the back is actually facing since it is not correct with the visual facing of the enemy.
