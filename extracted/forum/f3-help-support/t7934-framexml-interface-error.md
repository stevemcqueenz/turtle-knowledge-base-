Well, you have a couple of options, here.  You can either…
- Try downloading the client from the [official Torrent](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=7709) instead of a file-hosting service.
I've found that the version on the Torrent seems far more reliable in terms of, you know, actually working correctly.  For some reason, the hosted versions seem prone to corruption.  I don't really know why.

Or, you can…
- Install *World of Warcraft* v1.12.1, then [set it up](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=7709) to use *Turtle WoW*'s servers.
I'm not going to tell you where to get the official Blizzard v1.12 client, but I will say that a quick *Google* search will generally turn up something relevant.  Just make sure it's the **official client** and not a third-party client, like Elysium or Chronos.  Those *might* work, but they've been pre-modified with server-specific things which may conflict with *Turtle WoW*.

I'm sorry I couldn't be of any further help.
