I have noticed something strange with battleground queues and instances. Some of these may be related/the same issue but I thought I'd list them out:

 **1a. It seems like a new instance of a battleground is created before the first one is full.**

It's been a while since I've played Vanilla Retail/Classic but this seems different to what I recall. It looks as if at times there are multiple AV instances but the first one isn't full? Or is this because of AFK people in a finished instance? I know for a fact that there is an active AV in the game right now which I am not being let into and there is no way that there are 40 horde players in there right now.

 **1b. Battleground instances listed that aren't actually there.**

This could perhaps be the main problem instead of 1a above. On the most recent AB day there were 4 instances of Arathi Basin but I couldn't join any for over an hour (and then I gave up). I checked later that day and there were still 4, which lead me to believe it was a bug and those instances aren't actually in play at all.

 **2. In AV, I've noticed that Alliance always has 3/4+ people than Horde.**

There is presumably a % tolerance for this but with the numbers being so slow, an extra 4 people is really quite a huge advantage in an already imbalanced battleground.

Sorry I wasn't really sure where to post this. Just thought it would be good to get some information for peace of mind.
