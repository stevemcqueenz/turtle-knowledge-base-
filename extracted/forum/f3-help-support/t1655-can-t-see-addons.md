## Post 9855 by Exct — 2021-03-22T23:53:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9855#p9855 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Sometimes an addon folder will have -master in its name, you'll want to remove that (IE: !Questie-Master > remove the -master so it's just !Questie)

Or if they don't have -master in their name, it could be that you dragged/placed the wrong folder into your addons folder.

With Questie, The folders you want to put into your Addons folder are nested inside the Questie-1.12 folder.
You want to go into the Questie-1.12 folder and take the !Questie, JavaRefractorProject, and SQLData folders and place them instead into your Addons folder.

That might work. Hopefully helps.

If you find that you're still having issues, Turtle-Wow has an Official Discord. I suggest joining it and ask for help in the addons section and they'll probably be able to help get things sorted out!
