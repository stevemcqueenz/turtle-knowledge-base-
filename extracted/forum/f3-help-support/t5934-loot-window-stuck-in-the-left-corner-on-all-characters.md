## Post 37303 by Mcnair (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-03-10T13:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37303#p37303 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello and welcome! What you describe is the Vanilla way of the loot window. This behaviour was changed in later expansions to have the window at your mouse location, but that is sadly not an option by default in this version. There are UI remakes (e.g. pfUI) and other addons (e.g. MoveAnything) that can move it to a better position for you, but I'm not sure if there is one that adds the mouse location option.
