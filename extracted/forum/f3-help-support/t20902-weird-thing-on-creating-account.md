Hi there!

It looks like you might have already created an account with us in the past—or perhaps deleted one. Here’s what that means:

 **If your account still exists:**
You’ll need the original confirmation email from when you signed up. It has your account name and a unique recovery key to restore access.

 **If you deleted your account:**
Unfortunately, deleted accounts can’t be recovered, and the same email can’t be reused for a new one.

For any account-related questions, just shoot us an email at [[email protected]](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d3babdb5bc93a7a6a1a7bfb6b0a1b2b5a7fdb4b4)—we’re happy to help!

Thanks for your patience, and sorry for the hassle!
