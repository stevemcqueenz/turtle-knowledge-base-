Please, answer to this topic translating the following text into your mother language so i can post the translation and we can all understand its content. Also suggestions for macros/sentences can and will be included.

 **WORK IN PROGRESS**

 *English*
Greetings friend!
Turtle WoW host a comunity of people all over the world, so we use english as the vehicular language to comunicate. But what if you don't speak english? While you won't be able to hold deep conversations, you should be able to at least be able to comunicate during group play. That's why i offer you a few english sentences that will let you comunicate basic instructions to your group members.
You can either memorize those sentences or make macros if you use a different keyboard layout (Russian, chinese, japanese...).
So, shall we begin?

 *Español*
Saludos amigo!
Turtle WoW consta de una comunidad de personas de todo el mundo, asi que usamos el ingles como lengua vehicular para comunicarnos. Pero que pasa si no hablas ingles? Si bien no podras mantener conversaciones profundas, deberias ser capaz de al menos comunicarte mientras estas en grupo. Es por eso que os ofrezco algunas frases en ingles que os ayudaran a comunicar instrucciones basicas para vuestros compañeros de grupo.
Puedes tanto memorizar estas frases or hacer macros si usas una disposicion de teclado distinta (ruso, chino, japones...).
Bien, empezamos pues?
