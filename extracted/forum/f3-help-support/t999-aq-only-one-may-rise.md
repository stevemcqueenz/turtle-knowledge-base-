working as intended, the quest to pick up lashlayer's head should be in this state of the game
and yes the biggest chain in the game starts with this quest
after this quest you have to farm some bugs in silithus to get your brood of nozdormu rep up to neutral so you can talk to anachronos and he will ask you to get the 3 scepters that form the scepter of the shifting sands
red(bwl) : a quest that starts at the friendly vaelastrasz, asking you to kill nef in 5 hours
green(open world raid) : the quest starts in ST from Malfurion and ends in a standoff in moonglade where you have to defend nighthaven
blue(longest, 3 other chains):
 - get a letter from azuregos, find some goggles in mc, from trash
 - get about 10 people and kill a named elite on the isle of dread in feralas, and farm 20 chimaerok tenderloin from the elites there for the very best tanking food buff (25stam)
 - longest of the secondary chain : 5 man boss in winterspring, then you're gonna need for find the 8 chapters of Draconic for Dummies ( alcaz island boss (15-20man boss that mc's people), elite demons near kazzak, elite demons in winterspring, undercity library object, stormwind library object, onyxia drop, bwl object close to ebonroc, rag drop)
after these 3 secondary chains you get to fight a huge shark in azshara (15-20man)

complete all 3 color scepters and you can bang the gong that starts the opening event
after one person bangs that gong, others who may have completed the chain aswel can complete their part within 10 hours of the first person

all these quests reward some nice epic items / weapons and the mount
the title was added in tbc
