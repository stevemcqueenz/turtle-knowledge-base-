# Undetected+aggroed

## Post 2882 by Skullmiser (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-05-16T12:57:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2882#p2882 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Presently being undetected does not keep you off the aggro list of NPC enemies.  Be it prowl, or shadowmeld, or even invisibility. If enemies can't detect you, but you wind up on their list otherwise, then they suddenly know where you are.

[embed: https://www.youtube.com/embed/BXBZINEglxk?start=128]

This doesn't only happen with healing threat, though. If you're an instance, and you know that one of your team mates is about to pull the entire instance... and you prowl/shadowmeld to avoid a wipe. It'll work unless your team mates also run past you, to bring you into combat.  Which SHOULD kill you if they die next to you, but not if they keep running and die a mile away, and you're the last person alive.   They still target you somehow even if they can't detect you at all.
