I recently lost access to both my accounts out of the blue because turtlewow was not accepting my 2fa code. I was able to get one of my accounts reset and then was able to access my account. However, my main account, was not reset because I apparently have the wrong keyword. Although it's the only one I was given for my main account.

I have donated substantially to my main account and spent a lot of effort raiding for the past year.

My account was not hacked as my guild has informed me that my only toon on this account which is my main has not logged in since my month long departure and is just sitting there, frustrating.

So my account is lost for good because one day the server decides to freeze my accounts for no apparent reason and the information given to me almost a year ago is no longer good.

Not cool. If I knew the 2fa authentication was gonna kill my access I would never have activated that "security" feature.
