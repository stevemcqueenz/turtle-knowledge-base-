## Post 31886 by Ravenstone (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-01-01T10:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31886#p31886 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

There is no Hardcore at 60, your character losses the hardcore status so it's level 60 as normal.

You can turn off xp gain at 59 by right clicking your portrait and selecfing the xp off option, or there is a .xp command that can be used, allowing you to stay at 59.
