Hello,

I recommend disabling your addons and mods. Deleting or disabling custom patches (like Patch-A, Patch-S, Patch-O, Patch-Z, etc) in your Data folder will likely resolve the issue. Most of the time, FPS drops and crashes are caused by outdated patches or addons.
