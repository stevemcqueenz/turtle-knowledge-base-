## Post 59061 by Hctwowfan (Bug Report Enthusiast) — 2023-08-25T20:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59061#p59061 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If your level is higher than the mobs level you will gain less XP until the difference gets too high at which point you will no longer gain any XP for killing a mob of that level.

They're either red (very hard), orange (hard), yellow (medium) green (easy) or gray (too easy) in comparison to your level (the mob level indicator that is) and you'll gain XP accordingly.

It's generally 5 levels difference before they turn gray, scales with your level and ends at an 11 level difference at 59. So you'd still get XP for killing a lvl 48 mob at 59, but you won't get any XP for killing a lvl 19 mob at 30. Generally your level /5 is the max difference to still gain XP +-
