## Post 32438 by Maesus (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2023-01-11T10:20:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32438#p32438 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Good morning,

I don't know if this is a known bug, but I just came across this.

In Brill, a hunter trainer named Liott Maneskin had a quest for me, a level 10 rogue. To my surprise, it was the quest to tame 3 beasts, a darkhound, a plagued bat, and a plagued bear.
I - a rogue - was able to tame all three and return them to the hunter trainer each time. Afterwards, he sent me to Undercity for a Taming Lesson with Diane Willowfield.
All the way up to here, I was able to complete the hunter-only quest line for aquiring a pet.
Now I have Call Pet, Dismiss Pet and Tame Beast in the General tab of my spell book.
Naturally I went outside to see if I can actually tame a pet... The whole animation plays out to completion but any beast I tried it on remains hostile. (whew!)

I can also pick up the quest called "A Demonstration of Skill" - again, a hunter-only quest which awards a green crossbow or a green bow.

I'm attaching screenshots of the process if anyone's curious... I haven't seen this mentioned anywhere else on the forum, which leads me to think it's a new bug.
