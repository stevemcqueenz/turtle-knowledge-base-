# Quest Bug: "Bitter Rivals"

## Post 3652 by Nettlesprout — 2019-08-05T03:48:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3652#p3652 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

This involves  the quest in Dun Murough (Kharanos), where you are supposed to switch a keg of Thunder Ale with a barrel of Barleybrew. The Dwarf NPC guarding the keg is supposed to be given a Thunder Ale from the bartender in order to persuade him to walk away from his post, so that you can exchange the kegs.

The bug is that Jarven Thunderbrew won't talk to players as intended so that you are never able to use the purchased Thunder Ale to bribe him away from his spot.  I have the quest but it is not possible to complete it.

The quest works (should work) fine, but you need to first click the keg,  and get something like "you try to switch kegs, but it's guarded", and THEN talk to the guy / give him ale. At least this has been working for me.
