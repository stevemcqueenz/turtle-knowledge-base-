# Please help! Accidentally activated a challenge that disables resting — it ruined the game for me!

## Post 143805 by Dimastasc — 2025-08-08T10:26:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143805#p143805 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Hello, dear admins and fellow players,

I'm writing this message with genuine pain in my heart. I never thought that one accidental click could completely ruin the experience of a game I truly enjoy and put my time and soul into...
I play as a Hunter, character name Zirgon, level 33, on server Nordanaar. Everything was going fine I was doing the “Jungle Pages” quest, looting items, and organizing the quest pages in my bag to free up some space. All smooth and normal.
And then it happened: I accidentally clicked on an icon that looked exactly like one of the jungle quest pages. I didn’t think much of it assumed it was another regular quest item. But later, to my horror, I realized I had somehow activated a challenge without knowing it!
A little while later, I killed a mob and noticed something strange: my blue XP bar suddenly turned purple. I was shocked. I often rest in a tent to gain bonus experience, and I’d been saving up that rested XP for a long time. I thought it was a bug at first… but no. I started digging into it and discovered the awful truth: I had unknowingly activated a challenge that disables all rested XP.
I did not choose this challenge knowingly. There was no confirmation, no warning, nothing to make me realize what I was doing. Just one misclick — and now my entire progression feels broken. My motivation to play is completely gone.
I reached out to support, and they told me:
“It can’t be deactivated. It’s permanent.”
But why!? Why is there no way to undo a mistake that was clearly unintentional? Is this really fair? This is a game — it should be about fun, not about punishing players for honest accidents.
![💔](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f494.svg) Honestly, this feels like a punch to the gut. It’s as if I’ve unknowingly signed a sentence to quit the game I love. I’m begging you — please remove this challenge from my character. Or at least give us the option to disable it. We are all human, we all make mistakes. We are not machines...
I never meant to ruin my gameplay. I love this game. Otherwise, I wouldn’t even be here asking for help.

![🙏](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/1f64f.svg) Please, I’m asking with all sincerity — do something.
Sincerely and with hope,
Zirgon
Server: Nordanaar

If a single accidental click can permanently change gameplay — removing important mechanics (in my case, rested XP gain) — then there absolutely needs to be a proper warning.

Something like this:

![⚠️](//cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/twitter/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/26a0.svg) WARNING! You are about to activate a challenge. Once activated:
– You will NO LONGER gain rested XP.
– You will NOT be able to deactivate this.
– This change is PERMANENT.
Are you sure you want to continue? [YES] [NO]

This kind of big, bold, clear message — not just a small icon that looks like a quest item. Right now, it all happens silently — with no dialogue, no warning. That’s the real problem.

Mistakes happen. We're human. But the UI should protect players from irreversible accidents, not trap them with a single misclick.

Honestly, I’d say it’s better to not have this feature at all than to have it without even a basic confirmation prompt.

Greetings!

First, I'd like to emphasize that the GM's response was completely correct and in full accordance with our rules. Currently, it's impossible to cancel an active challenge.

From what we can see, you had previously purchased a glyph for this challenge but never used it. While we removed the glyph system itself, any purchased glyphs that players already owned were left intact and remain functional. We deliberately chose not to take away acquired glyphs from players, leaving them as legacy items.

By clicking the glyph, you activated the challenge. Now your character must complete it by reaching level 60, after which the challenge will be fulfilled and all restrictions will be lifted.
