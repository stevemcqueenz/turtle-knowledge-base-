Use case 2: A scroll of Stamina when applied to a character increases his stamina by +4 as expected, when applied to a pet - by +1 only!

Use case 3: A scroll of Intellect when applied to a character increases his Intellect by +4 as expected and when applied to a pet, also, increases by +4 its Intellect as expected!

What is going? Why such inconsistency between different scroll types and how they affect character and pet?!   angry_turtle_hea
