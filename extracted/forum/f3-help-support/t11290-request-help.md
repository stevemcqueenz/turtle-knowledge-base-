Greetings!
You are in the European Forum. Here you can get support for European servers only.
Regarding your request. All questions about game moments please send through the ticket system in game.
 **How to contact a GM and get help in-game**
Near your key ring there is a red question mark, press it to send an in-game ticket.
If you have pfUI and you don't have the micro-bar enabled, you can type /gm to open the interface.
You don't need to be online for tickets to be solved. Support will respond via in-game mail.
Furthermore if you play on SEA servers, all support questions should be directed here <https://kook.top/jzgWqY> .
