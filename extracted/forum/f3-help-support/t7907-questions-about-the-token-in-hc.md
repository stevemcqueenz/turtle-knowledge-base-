>  1. i bought a skin and use it, feel not good. how to transform back to what i look like original.

Skins are a permanent change. The only way to remove it is to request a refund within the first week of buying one. Or to buy an appearance change token. (This is what I ended up having to do)

>  2. i played HC and use token bought things, If i died, those things will disappered?

When you die as a HC, all of your tokens get refunded. This is a actually a great way to test out the look of things before buying them on your normal mode toon.
