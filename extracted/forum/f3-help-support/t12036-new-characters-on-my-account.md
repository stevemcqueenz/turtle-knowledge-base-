## Post 79735 by Zvyrhol (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-12-03T20:28:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79735#p79735 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Characters created on absent accounts have been automatically renamed. If we look at the server, it's been living for more than 4 years. During that time, many character names were reserved and for example naming your character using only 3 letters was impossible because all combinations of latin chars were in use. So devs decided to "delete" names of absent characters. So we have more naming options now without worry that someone has already taken the name we want.
