# Xabraxxis

## Post 1748 by Sinrek (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2019-01-03T23:07:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1748#p1748 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Quest **"The Blackwood Corrupted"** in Darkshore lack some events once the player is on the last step of the quest.

 *What should be happening:*
 1. Player obtians the Empty Cleansing Bowl from Thundris Windweaver.
 2. Player should fill (use it) it with water from the Moonwell, receiving Filled Cleansing Bowl.
 3. Player must obtain samples of fruit, grain and nuts from the northern Blackwood furbolg camp.
 4. Player must mix the bowl with obtained items from the camp and place it near the closest to the Cliffspring River bonfire.
 5. Once the bowl is on the ground, around the camp spawns 6 furbolgs Blackwood Warriors and while hostile to the player they charge at the bowl.
 6. Seconds later these furbolgs become friendly to player and wander off and around the bonfire.
 7. Xabraxxis spawns at the northern border of the camp near the river with yell: "What the… nobody cleanses a furbolg in MY camp! Face the wrath of Xabraxxis!"
 8. Xabraxxis engages on a player in sight. (Not aggroing if player is stealthed).
 9. Player must kill Xarbaxxis to spawn Xarbaxxis' Demon Bag.
 10. Player must loot the bag to pick up Talisman of Corruption.
 11. Player must bring the Talisman of Corruption to Thundris Windweaver.

 *How it works on Turtle WoW now:*
 1. Player obtians the Empty Cleansing Bowl from Thundris Windweaver.
 2. Player should fill (use it) it with water from the Moonwell, receiving Filled Cleansing Bowl.
 3. Player must obtain samples of fruit, grain and nuts from the northern Blackwood furbolg camp.
 4. Player must mix the bowl with obtained items from the camp and place it near the closest to the Cliffspring River bonfire.
 5. Once the bowl is on the ground, Xabraxxis spawns at the bonfire and engages on a player.
 6. Player must kill Xarbaxxis to spawn Xarbaxxis' Demon Bag.
 7. Player must loot the bag to pick up Talisman of Corruption.
 8. Player must bring the Talisman of Corruption to Thundris Windweaver.

Will we have a fix, please?   satisfied_turtle
