# Melee abilities transferring from hunter pet to hunter

## Post 9503 by Casualnerdity — 2021-02-22T21:32:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9503#p9503 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Certain npc abilities are able to be applied to both the hunter and their pet despite being tens of yards apart. From what I have experienced so far, the following npcs can use the listed abilities on a hunter pet and end up affecting the hunter themselves:
- Ferocious Rage Scar's Rend Flesh
- Blackhand Assassin's Gouge
- Blackhand Elite's Head Crack
- Skullsplitter Warrior's Head Crack
Searching the forum, I can see only one other ability mentioned, Theramore Marine's Disarm, from almost 2 years ago.

Procedure:
1. Stand outside of normal attack range (30-35 yds)
2. Send pet in to attack
3. Pet engages npc at range
4. Npc uses melee-ranged ability and affects hunter
