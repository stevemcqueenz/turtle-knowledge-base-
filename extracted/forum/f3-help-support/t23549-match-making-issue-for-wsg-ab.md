# Match making issue for WSG & AB

## Post 164881 by Alexandera114 — 2026-01-11T11:41:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164881#p164881 | page 1 | era: 1.18.1-announced-pre-release -->

I am unsure if it has been reported but for one month plus now, WSG and AB matches have tended to be imbalanced as new joiners are prioritized towards Alliance leading to most games being Alliance +1-2 numerically.

For example, a game starts with 4v4 and one person queues for the relative BG, that new joiner always joins as Alliance leading to games being a numerical disadvantage. I think it's an unintended bug as obviously the Developers would want teams to be equal.

Can a fix be implemented to ensure new queuers can only join games only if two persons are queuing.

With Cross-faction it doesn't require 1 Horde, 1 Alliance to queue so we only need two individuals queuing and also ensure one goes to either team as there are games with Alliance having 6 to 4 Horde.
