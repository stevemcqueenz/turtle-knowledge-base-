I make this thread for summary of found and fixed NPC scripts, inspired by *Sinrek*'s thread **"Deeprun Rat Roundup"**. We can start reporting those fix requests ITT, as I will keep track of their status. For reports list the affiliated quest and issue. These reports require extensive research and knowledge of the original game. The reports ITT should be only for mob scripting and interractions, not bugged quests, wrong quest text or other types of problems.
