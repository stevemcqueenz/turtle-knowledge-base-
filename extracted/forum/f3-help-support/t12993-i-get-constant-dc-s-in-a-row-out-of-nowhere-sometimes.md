Hello, my internet is above average and is consistently up for days at a time.  I noticed that when I play the game though things will run great for a few hours and then out of nowhere I'll get DC'd, log in for 10 seconds, DC'd again, repeat times 10+ times.

The odd thing is that I can see other players playing their characters, talking, fighting enemies, etc but my character personally freezes up.  My latency is sub-100 and I can go hours without issue but now and then this happens multiple times in succession. I have a level 9 Dwarf I've said basically nothing on so I don't see why this would happen.  I just want to hunt boars, skin them, and level up my skills.

It's just a strange situation altogether and I've never experienced this before today, I upgraded my internet a week ago by double to deal with input lag I was experiencing and now that is gone but this happens.  I checked my internet status and it's been up for the entire time so I know I've not lost connection even once so I'm just at a loss.

If anyone has any help I am all ears and appreciate any help suggested, thanks.
