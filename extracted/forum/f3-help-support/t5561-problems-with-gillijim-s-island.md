# Problems with Gillijim's Island

## Post 34289 by Kefke (Patch Note Conspiracy Theorist) — 2023-02-08T00:56:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34289#p34289 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

So, I noticed on my Horde alt that when I got to Booty Bay, I had an available flight path to Gillijim's Island. I decided to check it out, and ran into several issues.

1. That I have the FP at all. This is a higher level area, and for my under-20 character, there's no way I should have been able to go here in the first place.
2. A lot of the terrain here is really steep. Not a direct issue, but fall down in the wrong spot and you have to swim all the way around the island to find a way back up.
3. There doesn't seem to be a graveyard, you just ghost in place if you die. This *is* an issue, and especially if you happen to be too low a level. I ended up losing a big chunk of experience (S&S), and racking up a big repair bill because I got stuck in a spot where I couldn't respawn without instantly dying, and had to just eat the deaths and timer extensions until I could get to a place where I'd be undetected long enough to hearth. Having a spirit healer to rez at would have saved me several bars of experience, about ten minutes of ghost time, and a fair chunk of coin.
Not asking for my money or exp to be refunded, because I was the one who decided to explore without noticing that I'd get cornered by skull-level enemies, but I think that it goes a bit beyond a suggestion to ask that you fix whatever is giving lowbies the flight path, make the terrain a little less inconvenient by adding another way up from the water, and put in a graveyard for players to respawn at so they don't get death looped.

You unlock the Gillijim's Island (H) and Lapidis Isle (A) flight points automatically as soon as you learn the Booty Bay flight point. This is because there's currently no other feasible way to reach those islands; they're located fairly far off the shores of Stranglethorn, past what would normally be fatigue water. While I don't know if the water there is still fatigue water, making it necessary to swim that far across to reach a zone would likely make it so that those islands are deserted.
