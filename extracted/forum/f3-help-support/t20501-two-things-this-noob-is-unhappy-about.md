So for number one you can probably get that item refunded, try paging a GM in-game.

As for number two, you can safely reach Goldshire two ways as a Horde: swim a good ways out into the ocean from Grom'gol, then north up to Westfall (pretty simple from there besides the occasional Alliance guard patrol), or do the questline from the blacksmith in the Crossroads to construct an axe, you'll get to a point where they send you to the Forsaken in Thunder Bluff and they will teleport you to Westfall for the next step which you can then just keep using if you never finish the questline.
