# Pet Defensive Problem

## Post 59220 by Safyre — 2023-08-26T22:46:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59220#p59220 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

I tried searching on the bug list and didn't see this. I don't have an account at the other site to put this there.

I've noticed that occasionally while playing the hunter and with my pet set to Defensive, it will almost randomly attack enemies. Most of the time this has happened while traveling on a road and passing by enemies but has happened at other seemingly random times as well. My pet will be beside me as I'm running along and then suddenly run off to the side and attack something. This has happened multiple times and I have paid attention so this is before the enemy has attacked me or my pet. In most cases it looks like the enemy is just minding its own business and my pet just decides on its own to attack it. This has actually happened in dungeons a few times causing the group to have to fight extra enemies that we try to bypass.

I can only assume the reason. Because most of these times is while I am much higher level than the enemies, I can only guess that the enemy notices me, sees that I'm higher level, decides not to attack because of this, my pet notices the instinct of the enemy to attack even though it does not attack, and my pet attacks it anyway. This is just a guess.

I've made quite a few hunters so I can say that this has happened on a Troll and High Elf. I've noticed this happen using the cat (moonsabers and runts) and wolf (from Elwynn and ghost wolves) pets but mainly because those two are the main pets I have used. This has happened from as low as the teen levels up through the low 40's (my highest level hunter).

As it is I now typically only play with my pets on Passive since it's gotten me in trouble trying to play HCs with this bug. But it would be great to be able to have my pet attack an enemy when I get attacked or it gets attacked without me telling it to attack. Otherwise, on passive my pet just stands there getting attacked or while I'm getting attacked...because of Passive. It makes me waste a GCD or two while I fumble around with rotations with an extra command to have my pet attack.

Thanks much.

On another note, I've also noticed that sometimes when put on aggressive, my pet ignores enemies even when they come on over to attack me.

But I want to say that of the few priv servers I've played on and tried playing the hunter class, this has the best AI set up for the hunter pets. Other always have too many problems that make playing hunters completely useless from pets that just never attack at all even when commanded to, to pets that attack everything, even allies when not commanded to. So great work even with my little bug problem!
