# Pets at Gurubashi

## Post 2957 by Skullmiser (Barrens Chat Casualty) — 2019-05-24T14:30:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2957#p2957 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

If a hunter sends their pet at you in the arena, and then the hunter leaves the arena, their pet will still attack you, but you will not be to retaliate. You will not be able to get the chest.  The pet will STAY on you even if you leave the arena. It will attack you until the hunter tells it to stop, or until you leave the hunter's draw distance.

I'm almost certain this would work with warlock pets as well, I just haven't seen it yet.

I doubt that it would work with other pets... such as the dog whistle from scarlet monastery, but I'll test it when I have access to those. It seems something that should be checked.
