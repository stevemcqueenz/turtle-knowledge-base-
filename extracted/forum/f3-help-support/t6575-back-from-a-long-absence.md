The turtle store can now be accessed in-game by clicking the small crate icon near the mini-map.

The portable wormhole generator was changed to be an engineering item with five charges.  The old version was removed from the game, replaced with the broken version.

Other various changes: the turtle mount is now a level 18 quest.  If you already got it previously, you don't have to do the quest, you should still have it in your inventory.  Mounts are learned abilities, as are pets, so they don't take up bag space.  Survival has gone through changes, and you may have to relearn how to make tents and such but should still have your Survival skill.  Tents no longer stack.  Lot of the teleports were removed, like the arena teleport and the teleport to the Mirage Raceway.  If you roleplay, get [TurtleRP](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35002).

Welcome back.
