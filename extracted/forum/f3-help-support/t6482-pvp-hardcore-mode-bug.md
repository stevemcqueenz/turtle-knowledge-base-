The more likely scenario though, as you were ganked by some salty indiviuum, they must have exploited some feature, made you "gankable" and just killed you off.

If you start HC again, I'd suggest avoiding SW and other popular areas where assholes tend to congregate. That would include pretty much all of Human starting zones.
Ironforge is quite difficult for Horde to access and gank in, Darnassus probably even more so.

> **Mexxes786 wrote: Sat Apr 08, 2023 8:44 pm**
> ok I see. How can I avoid to be gankable? how does the exploit work?

Well, depends. Let's say you're playing an Alliance character, and another Ally (with PvP on) asks you for a buff. You oblige, since you're a good samaritan of course, and then boom their Horde friend or themselves on a Horde character jumps out and ganks you.

You have to pay attention to the colour of player names. Blue names are non-PvP, Green have PvP enabled.
To be extra safe, avoid buffing and grouping with Green named players, unless you can be positively certain that no gankers are around. As I said: SW, Elwynn, Westfall and Redrige are a huge magnet for ganking - especially in peak hours, during the weekend.

Turtle WoW generally has a very friendly and helpful community, I often group up with the opposite faction to do quests, but as the server is growing, there are more and more bad apples in the mix.
