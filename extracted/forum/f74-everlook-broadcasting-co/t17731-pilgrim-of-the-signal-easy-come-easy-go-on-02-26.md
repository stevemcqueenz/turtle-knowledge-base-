 **Hail and Signal’s Blessings, Dear Listener,**

I speak to you from beyond the Signal—a realm where the Twisting Nether twists and mingles with our own.

Horrible news, my friends: I met my end in a **HARDCORE** encounter while trying to save poor refugees from Gadgetzan. But fear not—the Light *(yes, Alanis Morissette herself)* whispered that my time had not yet come. With a gust of spectral meep, I returned—a whole Dwarf once more!

This resurrection means more show for you on Everlook Broadcasting this **Wednesday at 6:00 PM EST** for **Pilgrim of the Signal**. Join me for an evening where every track tells a tale and every beat pulses with our community’s heart.

 **TUNE IN in-game** or <https://turtlecraft.gg/radio>

Now, for the practical matters…

I’m not just a DJ—I’m your resident salvaged goods radio operator, a broadcaster for the people. What does that mean exactly? It means we’re in this together. I travel far and wide to bring the beats to you, wherever you may be:

 **• Got a celebration in mind?** Whether it’s celebrating the slaying of a particularly ornery boar in Elwynn Forest, hosting a headbangers’ psycho beach party ball in Darkshore, celebrating your guild leader’s 125th birthday, or even marking the end of a long-awaited divorce—tell me when, where, and what tunes you’d like to hear. While not every event will make the cut, every request is highly considered.

 **• A call for support:** This ol’ Dwarf owes a few debts to the Tinkers’ Union. To keep the magic alive—fuel contests, guild parties, and special events for newcomers—I invite you to pitch in. Consider donating a silver coin to help run our community contests and celebrations.

 *(In all seriousness, every donation goes directly toward hosting contests and giveaways. Some of my best moments in my MMORPG DJ life have come from both giving and receiving cool bags, mounts, in-game money, etc. There’s no fee for events—donations are appreciated, not required.)*

 **• For those with a ROLE to PLAY:** Consult with your comrades and ask yourselves, *“How can our upcoming red wedding slash poetry jam slam in the park be improved by a Dwarvish DJ?”* Then reach out to me. You are my people, and I want to help you immerse your lives in Azeroth as much as I can.

Got a request that captures your spirit? Send it along with your coin, and I’ll thoughtfully consider it for live play.

So, tune those receivers, clean those goggles, and wash those walkin’ socks (gross!)—and get ready to chase the Signal with the Pilgrim of the Signal, DJ (not-so-HARDCORE) Nopheros!

For any questions, concerns, feedback, related to the Signal of the Pilgrim, please reach out to me in-game "Nopheros" or on Discord by the same name.
