Hail, adventurers! Are you in dire need of a break from the grind? Do you crave **Techno and Electronic Dance Music** blasting through Azeroth while tales of **guild leaders, hardcore survivors, and everyday heroes** unfold before your ears? Then gather 'round, because it's time for **Tavern Talks with Sheal**!

 **Every other Friday at 19:00 ST**, I’ll be live from a different **inn somewhere in Azeroth** for a three-hour show for and about the Turtle WoW community. We’ll party so hard, the Devs will get nervous about us breaking anything! Expect **epic interviews, hilarious world chat highlights, and your favorite tunes** to fuel the fun.

Set up your **recording equipment** and capture Azeroth’s greatest moments. The best clips will be featured in the **monthly community compilation**. Send your recordings to my [Discord Channel](https://discord.gg/TPtBczA2pT), where you can also apply for an interview!

So sharpen your ears, dust off your dancing boots, and pour yourself a fresh mug. Because during this show, the community rules Azeroth!
