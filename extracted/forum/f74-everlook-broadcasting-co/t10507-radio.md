## Post 75264 by Drubarrymooer (Grandmaster of Forum PvP) — 2023-11-01T16:31:00Z
<!-- url: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75264#p75264 | page 1 | era: pre-1.18.1 -->

Uncheck the radio channel box so neither box is checked.  Then go into your sound settings and disable music, to back into the game, open sound again, then reenable music.

As for it being optional, it is.  If turning it off isn't working, that's a bug.  Unfortunately, most users haven't run into it.  There's also probably an option in the config file.  In fact, if my above solution doesn't work, close your game, delete your config file, then open the game and try again.
