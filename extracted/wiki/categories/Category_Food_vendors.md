# Category:Food vendors

This category lists articles on vendors who sell food. This includes vendors that fulfill one of two criteria:
1.Vendors that have the title of <Food>, <Food Vendor>, or <Rations> (or variations thereof).
1.Miscellaneous food vendors who sell food items that do not fit into the traditional six categories of food (meat, fish, cheese, fungus, bread, and fruit). For example, <Pie Vendor>.
