# Category:Ruins

A ruin or ruins is a [region](subzone) within a [zone](zone) characterized by ancient, abandoned or destroyed churches, settlements, and temples. Ruins frequently become re-inhabited by creatures or races, most notably [troll](troll)s, [bandit](bandit)s, or [undead](undead).
