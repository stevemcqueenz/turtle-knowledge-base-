# Category:Mobs

This category entails articles describing certain monsters ([mob](mob)s) that share a common name with several others rather than having a unique name or title. They are different from [NPC](NPC)s because they are non-interactive, and exist solely for the purpose of being killed. These are the meat and bones of [PvE](PvE), and permeate the WoW universe.

NOTE:
- Bosses can be considered mobs, but should be put into their own category, **[:Category:Bosses](:Category:Bosses)**.
- Interactive NPCs should go into the appropriate subcategory (or subcategories) of **[:Category:NPCs](:Category:NPCs)** rather than this category.
