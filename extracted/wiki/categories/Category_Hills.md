# Category:Hills

A **hill** is an elevated area that is smaller than a mountain. Some notable examples are [Thorn Hill](Thorn Hill) (in the [Barrens](Barrens)) and [Sentinel Hill](Sentinel Hill) (in [Westfall](Westfall), which is known more for its tower).
