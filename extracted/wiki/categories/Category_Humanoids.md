# Category:Humanoids

This category contains articles about humanoids. 

Specific named humanoids should not be placed in the humanoid category, but rather in the appropriate racial subcategory(-ies) if the race(-s) is/are known.

If the race is unknown, or if the character is humanoid despite the fact that most of their race is not (notably some satyr and dragons in humanoid form), they can be put here.
