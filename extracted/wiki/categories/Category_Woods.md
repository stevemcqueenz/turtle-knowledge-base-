# Category:Woods

This category contains articles on wooded areas such as woods, jungles, glades, thickets, wealds, and timberlands.

This category should list [subzone](subzone)s that are primarily wooded and [zone](zone)s not-primarily wooded, but with small some wooded areas. Zones that are primarily wooded should go in the [Forests category](:Category:Forests) (which this category is a subcategory of).
