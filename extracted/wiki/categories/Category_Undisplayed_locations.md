# Category:Undisplayed locations

This category contains locations present in Turtle WoW but not as a proper subzones. This also includes instanced locations like battlegrounds or dungeons which are lore-wise in the zone, but separated in-game. Subzones labeled as "undisplayed locations" in the overworld but otherwise displayed in their instances/zones are not in this category.
