# Devilsaur

A huge, two-legged dinosaur found in small numbers only in [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater), and one of the most recognizable and unique creatures found in the World of Warcraft.

They are all [elite](elite), and range from lvl 54-60, the largest being King Mosh.  All members of the devilsaur family are a deadly threat to the unlucky person they come across.  They can be skinned for [Devilsaur Leather](Devilsaur Leather), used in the popular Devilsaur Set Tribal Leather Armor. 

See also: [Devilsaur Set Tribal Leather Armor](Leatherworking#Tribal Leatherworking).
