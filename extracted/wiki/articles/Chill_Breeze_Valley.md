# Chill Breeze Valley

[Chill Breeze Valley](Chill Breeze Valley) is a T-shaped region of [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), situated between [Iceflow Lake](Iceflow Lake) to the west and [Kharanos](Kharanos) to the east. Although the east-west branch of the valley is a hub that sees frequent use by young dwarves and gnomes, the north-south section sees a far smaller amount of passers-through, as ominous signs of dangerous monsters can be seen staining the snow there: large bloodstains and ripped-up animal carcasses. More experienced adventurers seek out these ferocious beasts in order to end their threat to the dwarven lands, but many have died in the attempt.
