# Stone Cairn Lake

.]]

**Stone Cairn Lake** is situated in northeastern [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), and holds one large island that gives the lake a characteristic ring shape. The lake is named after [Heroes' Vigil](Heroes' Vigil), the [stone cairn monument](Stone of Remembrance) erected upon the island to commemorate the huge loss of life during the [First War](First War) against the orcs.

While the lake has previously been a safe place to make pilgrimages to, it has now fallen into the hands of [Defias Rogue Wizard](Defias Rogue Wizard)s who have settled on the island. The water also holds a tribe of [murloc](murloc)s who have raised crude structures on the southern banks of the lake. This area has possibly the highest concentration of [copper](copper) in Elwynn.

## Trivia
- A cairn is an artificial pile of stones, often in a conical form.
