# Fungal Rock

**Fungal Rock** is a cave in the northeastern wall of [Un'goro Crater](Un'goro Crater). It is inhabited by [gorilla](gorilla)s, and is also where one can find [A-Me 01](A-Me 01), [Karna Remtravel](Karna Remtravel)'s mechanical ape.
