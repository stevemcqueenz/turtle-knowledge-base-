# Demon

Hellish, twisted creatures found on the worlds conquered by the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion).  There are many types (see below) and races, most of whom typically serve either their own agenda or that of the [Legion](Burning Legion) (they are often the same). For a description of the most important/famous demons visit the race pages or the [Legion page](Burning Legion).

It is not known if there is a difference between the Demons of the Burning Legion and the Daemons of the first two WarCaft games. If we had to pick, these monsters appeared to most closely resemble Doomguard [the Daemon design was essentially abandonded for WarCraft III]. The first named demon encountered by players was Kil'Jaeden in the WarCraft II manual.  [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden) resembled the Daemons, but was switched to an Eredar when the command structure and races were finalized.

- **See also:** [Burning Legion](Burning Legion).

# Major Races
- [Eredar](Eredar) - Highest Rank of the legion. They dont usually engage in combat.
- [Pit Lords](Pit Lord) - Generals usually lead armies of the legion.
- [Nathrezim](Nathrezim) (aka: Dread Lords) - Usually command a small amount of felgaurds - dont always engage in combat and are more like master minds.
- [Doomguard](Doomguard) -  Lead dozens of felgaurds into battle. Extrememly powerful and take a dozens of heroes to take down.
- [Felguard](Felguard) - The main infantry of the Legion. Thousands go into battle. They are lethal but inexperienced and are treated poorly.
