**Darkshire** (formerly known as **Grand Hamlet**) (ref: [quest: Errand for Quel'Danil]) is a once-quaint little woodland village located in [Duskwood](Duskwood), but it has fallen on literally dark times. The surrounding forests have been corrupted after the death of [Medivh](Medivh), cloaking the land in constant darkness for decades. The walking dead choke the graveyards, and foul creatures known as [worgen](worgen) have overrun many of the outlying [farm](farm)steads. Even worse, the political instability in [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) caused the abandonment of the town and its surrounding areas by the authorities and the [Stormwind Army](Stormwind Army). This has left the stubborn people of Duskwood little choice but to defend themselves. Besides its town hall, the village also has a blacksmith and an inn.

The [Council of Darkshire](Council of Darkshire) is responsible for enforcing justice in the town, notably by ordering death sentences for those who sold the bodies from cemeteries in Duskwood. (ref: [quest: Crime and Punishment]) In the town hall, [Clerk Daltry](Clerk Daltry) keeps records on the people that live and pass through the town, although some of his records are a little outdated. (ref: [quest: The Caravan Road])
