# Well of Eternity

Ten thousand years before the [orc](orc)s and [human](human)s clashed in their First War, the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth) cradled only one massive continent, surrounded by the sea. That landmass, known as [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), was home to a number of disparate races and creatures, all vying for survival amongst the savage elements of the waking world. At the dark continent's center was a mysterious lake of incandescent energies. The lake, which would later be called the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity), was the true heart of the world's magic and natural power. Drawing its energies from the infinite [Great Dark](Great Dark) beyond the world, the Well acted as a mystical fount, sending its potent energies out across the world to nourish life in all its wondrous forms. 

(The WarCraft III manual implies that the Titans carefully placed the Well, but the War of the Ancient Trilogy implies that the Old Gods have some relationship to it [Don't worry, all of the World Tree's power came from the Aspects], so we can't be sure.) 

Later, [Sargeras](Sargeras) convinced [Queen Azshara](Queen Azshara) and the [Highborne](Highborne) to create a portal in the well and leading to the first invasion of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion).

Following the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients), the Well of Eternity was destroyed, and most of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) with it, leaving only [the Maelstrom](the Maelstrom). However, [Illidan](Illidan) had filled several magical vials with water from the Well of Eternity, and he poured some of the magical water into the lake at the top of [Mount Hyjal](Mount Hyjal), creating a new Well. For this insane act, his brother [Malfurion](Malfurion) and the demigod [Cenarius](Cenarius) imprisoned him deep beneath Mount Hyjal.

The new Well was completly absorbed by the growth of [Nordrassil](Nordrassil), its malevolent magicks lost with the enchantments of the Aspects.

However, Illidan had not emptied all of his vials in creating the second Well of Eternity (which is why the Aspects were able to enchant the World Tree with no interference). Somehow, the [Highborne](Highborne) under the leadership of [Dath'Remar](Dath'Remar) had acquired one of the remaining vials and, after founding their new homeland of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas), used it to create the [Sunwell](Sunwell).
