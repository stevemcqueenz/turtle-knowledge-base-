The **Black Tooth Grin clan** is an orcish clan that was formed as an offshoot of the [Blackrock clan](Blackrock clan), having splintered off before the Horde's passing into Azeroth. After their father was deposed from his position as Warchief by [Orgrim Doomhammer](Orgrim Doomhammer), [Rend](Rend Blackhand) and [Maim](Maim Blackhand) sought to establish their own power base within the [Horde](Orcish Horde). The clan receives its name from the tradition that every recruit must knock out one of their own teeth to show their loyalty. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Clans of the Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#The_Black_Tooth_Grin_Clan)*, The Black Tooth Grin Clan)

## History
During the Second War, the Black Tooth Grin was responsible for guarding the Dark Portal (ref:  and fought on the front lines during the campaign for Khaz Modan.<ref>[Tol Barad (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Tol_Barad_(WC2_Human))) (ref: [Dun Algaz (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Dun_Algaz_(WC2_Human)))

Many of their clan were later captured after the siege of Blackrock Spire and the end of the war and were subsequently placed in the internment camps, (ref: *Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal* manual, [The Aftermath of the Second War](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Beyond_the_Dark_Portal_manual#The_Aftermath_of_the_Second_War)) though Rend and Maim evaded the Alliance and later returned to Blackrock Mountain, where Rend declared himself the chieftain of the weakened Blackrock clan and formed the [Dark Horde](Dark Horde). Maim would later perish during their conflict with the Dark Iron dwarves, though the Dark Horde drove the dwarves into the depths of the mountain and secured the upper spire for themselves, now allied with the Black dragonflight and [Nefarian](Nefarian), son of [Deathwing](Deathwing). (ref:  [60] [Eitrigg's Wisdom](Eitrigg's Wisdom))

The identity of the Black Tooth Grin appears to have since become synonymous with that of the Blackrock clan and the Dark Horde, as none of their clan have been sighted since Rend and Maim's takeover of the Blackrock clan.

## Trivia
