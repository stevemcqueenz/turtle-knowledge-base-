# Mudsnout tribe

, who may be the Mudsnout leader.|right]]

The **Mudsnout tribe** is a [tribe](tribe) of [gnolls](gnolls) found in the [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills). They have overrun [Nethander Stead](Nethander Stead) and are apparently growing large white mushrooms called [Mudsnout Blossoms](Mudsnout Blossoms). They are the only tribe of gnolls, besides the [Rot Hide](Rot Hide), found in [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) in *World of Warcraft*.

## Classes
There seems to be two [class](class) of Mudsnouts in *World of Warcraft*.

- Mudsnout Gnoll
- [Mudsnout Shaman](Mudsnout Shaman)

## Famous/Named Mobs
[Ro'Bark](Ro'Bark) is a level 28 rare spawn, and may be their leader.
