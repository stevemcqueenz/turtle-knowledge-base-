# Lower Blackrock Spire

# General
- **Level:** 55+
- 10 Player Cap
- **Location:** Raid instance located inside [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire) under [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain).
- **Abbreviation:** BRS, LBRS

The area of the Blackrock Spire instance to the South of [Hall of Blackhand](Hall of Blackhand) is commonly known as Lower Blackrock Spire. Though it is often treated as an independent instanced dungeon from [Upper Blackrock Spire](Upper Blackrock Spire) it is, in fact, part of the same, huge, instance. The Lower Blackrock Spire distinguishes itself from Upper Blackrock Spire because it is designed for a 5-man party so none of the quests involving Lower Blackrock Spire can be completed in a [raid group](raid group).

## History
See: [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire).

The orcs of the Bloodaxe and [Blackrock Clan](Blackrock Clan)s dominate most of the ruins of Lower Blackrock Spire, but a contingent of [Spirestone](Spirestone) Ogres and [Smolderthorn](Smolderthorn) Trolls have each claimed a share of the area. The ruins are also home to a host of [Spire Spider](Spire Spider)s. The Lower Blackrock Spire is ruled by [Overlord Wyrmthalak](Overlord Wyrmthalak), a subordinate of [General Drakkisath](General Drakkisath).
