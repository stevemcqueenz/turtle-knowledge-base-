The **Dark Horde** (ref: [quest: Letter from Korlag Doomsong]) is a renegade group of orcs, trolls, ogres, and goblins who dwell in [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire), which consists largely of the remnants of the [Blackrock](Blackrock clan) and [Dragonmaw](Dragonmaw clan) clans in the aftermath of the [Second War](Second War). They are marshaled by [Nefarian](Nefarian) of the black dragonflight and ruled by the self-styled Warchief [Rend Blackhand](Rend Blackhand).
