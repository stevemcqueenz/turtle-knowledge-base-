-Barbaros** was the captain of *[The Rat's Nest](The Rat's Nest)*, a ship crewed by the [Bilgerats](Bilgerats). Having stolen age-old secrets from [Falrevere](Falrevere), he sailed to the accursed island of [Balor](Balor) with his crew in search of treasure, anchoring on a small isle north of the mainland where they built [a small compound](Bilgerat Compound). Along with [Second Mate O'Donovan](Second Mate O'Donovan), [Moralis](Moralis), [Framwin](Framwin) and [Jade](Jade), he ventured onto the island in order to unearth the treasure. However, they were ambushed by orcs of the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan), and Barbaros and his entire party save for O'Donovan and Jade were dragged to [Stormwrought Castle](Stormwrought Castle). (ref: [Logbook of The Rat's Nest](Logbook of The Rat's Nest)) Barbaros was killed by the orcs, and his body was later used to house the soul of [Deathlord Tidebane](Deathlord Tidebane).
