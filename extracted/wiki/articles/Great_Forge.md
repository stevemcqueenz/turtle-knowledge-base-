# Great Forge

The **Great Forge** is the heart and soul of [dwarven](dwarven) culture, where all of their ingenious creations and weapons are made.  At its center is the famed Great Anvil, where most of the best dwarven [blacksmithing](blacksmithing) trainers can be found. Ringed around the Great Forge are a great deal of shops and guild offices, including trainers for [cooking](cooking), [mining](mining), [first aid](first aid), [tailoring](tailoring) and [leatherworking](leatherworking), as well as the [High Seat](High Seat), the court of the High King [Magni Bronzebeard](Magni Bronzebeard). Ironforge's gryphon master is also located here.

To get to the Great Forge, you can head northeast from the [Commons](Commons), southeast from the [Mystic Ward](Mystic Ward), northwest from the [Military Ward](Military Ward), or southwest from the [Hall of Explorers](Hall of Explorers).
