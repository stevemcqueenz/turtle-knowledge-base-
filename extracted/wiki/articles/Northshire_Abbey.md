# Northshire Abbey

.]]
.]]
.]]

  -Northshire Abbey** is the home of the [human](human) religious order of [cleric](cleric)s known as the [Brotherhood of Northshire](Brotherhood of Northshire). It is located in [Northshire Valley](Northshire Valley) in the foothills of [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest) of the [Kingdom of Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)). The closest town to Northshire Abbey is [Goldshire](Goldshire) to the southwest.

Northshire Abbey is comprised of three sections: the [Main Hall](Main Hall (Northshire)), [Hall of Arms](Hall of Arms (Northshire)), and [Library Wing](Library Wing (Northshire)). It holds trainers for [mage](mage)s, [priest](priest)s, [warrior](warrior)s and and [paladin](paladin)s, as well as a couple of quest givers. The trainers for young human [warlock](warlock)s haunt the small graveyard to the east of the main doors. In the shadows of the [stable](stable)s one can find the [rogue](rogue) and [hunter](hunter) trainers.

## History
Prior to the [First War](First War), the abbey served as the home of the [Holy Order of Northshire Clerics](Holy Order of Northshire Clerics). It was the location where [Medivh](Medivh) was cared for during the years he was in a coma.  During the course of the First War, the Clerics of Northshire, led by Archbishop [Alonsus Faol](Alonsus Faol), aided the armies of the then nation of [Azeroth](Stormwind (kingdom)) in their fight against the [Horde](Old Horde). Shortly after [Garona](Garona) was captured and brought to Northshire to be held as a prisoner, the Abbey was destroyed in an [orc](orc)ish onslaught.

During the [Second War](Second War), the site of the abbey was the headquarters of [Cho'gall](Cho'gall)'s [Twilight's Hammer clan](Twilight's Hammer clan), allied to [Gul'dan](Gul'dan). After the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron) reclaimed [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), Northshire Abbey was rebuilt, though the area has been suffering a [kobold](kobold) infestation and the growing presence of bandits known as the [Defias](Defias).
