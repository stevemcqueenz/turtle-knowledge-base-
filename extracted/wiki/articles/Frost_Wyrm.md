# Frost Wyrm

In ages past, the ancient [Dragon](Dragon)s who were near death flew to the land of [Northrend](Northrend) to die. To this day there are entire Dragon graveyards that are littered with massive petrified bones and skulls. When [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul) the [Lich King](Lich King) took control of Northrend, he used his powerful magics to raise the ancient Dragon skeletons from the dead. Now the skeletal Dragons radiate cold power and think of nothing other than serving their dark master. Frost Wyrms have a cold-based breath weapon that can do shattering damage to enemies both on the ground and in the air.
