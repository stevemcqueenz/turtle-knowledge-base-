# Book of Medivh

The source of much myth and speculation both within the game world and among fans, the **Book of Medivh** refers to a spellbook (or possibly two spellbooks) written by [Medivh](Medivh), the Last Guardian.

## History
Although the Book of Medivh has not been seen in [World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft), there have been references to The Book of Medivh in both *[Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal](Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal)* and *[Warcraft III](Warcraft III)*.

### Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal
Medivh's spellbook was kept in the library of [New Stormwind](New Stormwind) following his death. [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul) stole this book during the battles that took place following the [Second War](Second War) and used it to open many portals on [Draenor](Draenor). [Khadgar](Khadgar) later stole it back and used it to seal the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal). The game ends with the book in Khadgar's possession, trapped on the other side of the Dark Portal.

### Warcraft III
Archmage [Antonidas](Antonidas) of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) apparently came into the posession of an artifact known as the Book of Medivh sometime before the [Third War](Third War).  It was stolen from [Dalaran](Dalaran) by [Arthas](Arthas) and [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) during that war and used to summon [Archimonde](Archimonde), thus beginning the [Reign of Chaos](Reign of Chaos). The book was last seen in Kel'Thuzad's possession.
