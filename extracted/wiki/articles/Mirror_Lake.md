# Mirror Lake

.]]

**Mirror Lake** is situated in western [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), near an [orchard](Mirror Lake Orchard). It is rather small and features a beautiful waterfall. Although Mirror Lake would be an ideal spot for swimming or [fishing](fishing), the [Defias](Defias) bandits that infest the area make such sports hazardous.

After [Azeroth](Azeroth) had been victim to massive mystic struggles, which tore magic currents from once stable channels, the flow of magic in [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) and [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest) was somehow altered. Consequently, the [mage](mage)s of the [Wizard's Sanctum](Wizard's Sanctum) sent a mage [adventurer](adventurer) to Mirror Lake, in order to test its water for magical properties before those who drank from it would be adversely affected. (ref: [quest: Mirror Lake|class=mage])

A side entrance to the [Stormwind Vault](Stormwind Vault) dungeon can be found at the base of [Thunder Falls](Thunder Falls) for Horde characters.
