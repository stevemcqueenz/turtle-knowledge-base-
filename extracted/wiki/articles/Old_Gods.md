# Old Gods

The **Old Gods** were malevolent deities who ruled [Azeroth](Azeroth) in the ancient past before they and their [Elemental Lieutenants](Elemental Lieutenants) were defeated by the [Titan](Titan)s.

# History
Long ago, Azeroth was a primordial world filled with the hostile elemental servants of the Old Gods.  When the Titans visited the world in their quest to bring order the universe, they fought first against the Old Gods' lieutenants, [Ragnaros the Firelord](Ragnaros), [Therazane the Stonemother](Therazane), [Al'Akir the Windlord](Al'Akir), and [Neptulon the Tidehunter](Neptulon), then against the Old Gods themselves.  Soon, the Titans defeated the Old Gods and set about the long process of forming the lands and seas of Azeroth.

- **Note:** It is suspected that The Old Gods are partially based on the legends of the [Great Old Ones](Wikipedia:Great Old Ones) and the [Outer Gods](Wikipedia:Outer Gods), creations from the legend of [Cthulhu](Wikipedia:Cthulhu mythos) written by [H.P. Lovecraft](wikipedia:H.P. Lovecraft).

# The Five
Of the five initial Old Gods, one was slain; its remains can be seen at the [Master's Glaive](Master's Glaive) in [Darkshore](Darkshore).  Three of the Gods were imprisoned beneath the earth.  The fifth God, [C'Thun](C'Thun), was defeated in [Silithus](Silithus) in a battle which also resulted in the defeat of a Titan.  C'Thun was believed to have been permanently defeated, but reemerged as the driving force behind the [Qiraji](Qiraji).

## The Three
The three Gods imprisoned beneath the earth continued to exert their will, though in limited ways.

- They created the Burning Legion to have Sargeras unintentionally let them loose when he entered Azeroth. They would then make short work of him, and subsequently destroy the world. Their escape was hindered though, by the night elven defenders led by Malfurion Stormrage.
- They corrupted the great benevolent [Dragon Aspect](Dragon Aspect) [Neltharion](Neltharion), to create for them the extremely powerful [Demon Soul](Demon Soul), which they were to have Sargeras use to unintentionally set them free. The Demon Soul was taken from Neltharion by Malfurion, then taken from him by Illidan and put in the Sargeras-summoning matrix, then taken back from ther by Neltharion, who shortly thereafter was struck away by the Old Gods. Malfurion picked up the Demon Soul and used it (in conjunction with Illidan) to prevent Sargeras' entry. It was then given to the dragons, stolen by Nekros ten-thousand years later, taken back by the dragons, and then destroyed.
- They corrupted some of the [Highborne](Highborne) of the [Night Elves](Night Elves), and used them to lure the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) to Azeroth. Many of the Highborne were transformed into the [Naga](Naga), who serve the Old Gods still.
- They invaded Nozdormu's realm and managed to open a rift in time, that, as they had planned, tossed some beings back through time, beings that would change the way the war of the ancients took place, and give Sargeras a new chance to enter the world, and therefore them a new chance to set themselves free. Their plans were although again crushed by the very same Malfurion Stormrage. 
- The imprisoned Old Gods had spent a titanic amount of power to create the time-rift (power they had been storing for ten-thousand years), and were (presumably) utterly devastated and weakened (from sacrificing the titanic amount of power)  when their plan failed. One of them, the so-called forgotten one, who imprisoned beneath Azjol-Nerub, in his weakened state, suddenly had his prison broken by the tremors caused by Illidan's channeled spell to melt away the polar ice-caps and crush the Lich King. The old gods minions, the faceless ones, spilled out of the prison and battled the undead scourge and Muradin's dwarves. Before the old god himself could do anything though, the Lich King's chosen death knight, Arthas Menethil, and the dreaded crypt-lord Anub'Arak, along with a small army of undead crypt-fiends, crashed into his prison chamber, and a mighty battle ensued. Finally though, after the weakened Old God had decimated most of the undead, the power of the two scourge champions overcame the weakened old god. Swarms of undead beetles dug into the flesh of the old god. Anub'Arak slashed at the old gods sides, causing deep wounds. And Arthas, using necromantic spells and armed with the runeblade, Frostmourne, finally managed to deliver the killing blow to the old god, who collapsed on the floor of his prison with a wrathful roar of defiance. 
Since, there are only two imprisoned old gods left. The forgotten one was the second Old God to die "for real", and the first to be killed by anything other than a Titan (even if the Titans had killed only one before). Explaining how even Arthas could accomplish such a feat is that the old god was heavily weakened, after having spent most of his energy opening the time rift.

## C'Thun
[C'Thun](C'Thun) is the only one of the Old Gods to be named so far, having receieved that name from his servants, the [Qiraji](Qiraji). He is trapped deep beneath the ruined temples of [Ahn'Qiraj](Ahn'Qiraj), where he has exerted his will for thousands of years over the Qiraji, who in turn command the [Silithid](Silithid).

C'Thun is the final boss in the [Temple of Ahn'Qiraj](Temple of Ahn'Qiraj). Players will get to kill him, and doing so was the most difficult thing to do in the game until the [Naxxramas](Naxxramas) instance was made available.

## The Dead One
One of the Old Gods was killed by the Titans. Its remains can be found in [Darkshore](Darkshore).

# The Number of Old Gods Imprisoned
There are various sources as to the number of Old Gods imprisoned:
- The [Warcraft III](Warcraft III) manual states that five Old Gods were chained beneath the world.  
- According to the [War of the Ancients Trilogy](War of the Ancients Trilogy), there are three Old Gods still living, imprisoned and chained by the Titans deep beneath the surface of Azeroth.  
- According to [The Old Gods and the Ordering of Azeroth](The Old Gods and the Ordering of Azeroth), four Old Gods were imprisoned beneath the world.  This could perhaps be reconciled with the War of the Ancients Trilogy if it were taken to include the fallen C'Thun in the count of the imprisoned Gods.
