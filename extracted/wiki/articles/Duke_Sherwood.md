-Duke Johnathan Sherwood** is a [human](human) quest giver located at the [Jousting Grounds](Jousting Grounds) in [Northwind](Northwind). Ruler of Birkhaven, his family owns the [Sherwood Quarry](Sherwood Quarry) in northeast Northwind. He is said to not tolerate failure and is quick to punish any injustices he sees, which instills fear and anxiety in his subjects. However, his methods have accumulated a multitude of wealth that cannot be denied. (ref: [The Nobles of Northwind](The Nobles of Northwind))

Duke Sherwood presides over the knightly joust, heralding the competitors and naming the victor of the bouts.

### Jousting tournament
Approximately every one and a half hours, Duke Sherwood will announce the contestants for the latest bout of the jousting tournament.

  -Sir [Rudolf of Strom](Rudolf of Strom) against [Sir Reginald of Stormwind](Sir Reginald of Stormwind)**
  On to our next joust! To my left, a stout knight of our kingdom, Sir Reginald! To my right, from the far kingdom of Strom, Sir Rudolf!
  Now, let us determine which knight will bring glory to its homeland!
  Onwards, to victory!
  A clean strike! Sir Rudolf lies on the ground, defeated by the proud Sir Reginald!
  Glory to Sir Reginald, victor of this knightly joust!

  -[Sir Barnabas of Strom](Sir Barnabas of Strom) against [Lumiere of Dalaran](Lumiere of Dalaran)**
  Another joust awaits! A valiant duel between Sir Barnabas of Strom, a gilded veteran of old, and the equally skilled Lumiere of Dalaran!
  Now, let us determine which knight will bring glory to its homeland!
  Onwards, to victory!
  Such elegance! Lumiere effortlessly demonstrated that Dalaran should not be underestimated! A lesson Sir Barnabas felt hands-on!
  A toast to Lumiere of Dalaran, victor of this knightly joust!

  -[The Black Knight](The Black Knight) against Sir [Halfar of Kul Tiras](Halfar of Kul Tiras)**
  Silence, fair citizens, for a special bout is about to happen! Hailing under no flag and bearing no color, the Black Knight steps into the ring! His opponent: the courageous Halfar of Kul Tiras, breaker of tides and storms!
  Now, let us determine which knight will bring glory to its homeland!
  Onwards, to victory!
  What a merciless display! Call for the doctor, I'm sure Sir Halfar sorely needs it! A gruesome reminder of how brutal these tournaments can become!
  The honor goes to the nameless Black Knight, victor of this knightly joust!

  -[Fritz of Kul Tiras](Fritz of Kul Tiras) against [Sir Lorenz of Theramore](Sir Lorenz of Theramore)**
  Now, fair spectators, a duel of monumental weight awaits us. Sir Lorenz of Theramore against the young and upcoming talent of Fritz of Kul Tiras!
  Now, let us determine which knight will bring glory to its homeland!
  Onwards, to victory!
  Incredible! Anyone present here at this tournament can vouch for the emotional investment brought into this duel. In the end, it is Sir Lorenz who shall be praised for this victory!
  Glory to Sir Lorenz of Theramore, victor to this knightly joust!

  -Sir [Ferdinand of Theramore](Ferdinand of Theramore) against Sir [Estanor of Dalaran](Estanor of Dalaran)**
  The next joust begins! To my left, a brave contestant from the sea-faring nation of Theramore, Sir Ferdinand! To my right, a chivalrous mage and his steadfast steed, Estanor of Dalaran!
  Now, let us determine which knight will bring glory to its homeland!
  Onwards, to victory!
  Devastating! The might of Theramore was demonstrated once more on this day! Sir Estanor could not withstand Sir Ferdinand's mighty blow!
  Glory to Sir Ferdinand, victor of this knightly joust!
