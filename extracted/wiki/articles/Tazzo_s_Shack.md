# Tazzo's Shack

**Tazzo's Shack** is a small hut with a banana plantation located in southwestern [Tel'Abim](Tel'Abim). [Tazzo Gearfire](Tazzo Gearfire) is present in the building, working on creating a gargantuan banana.
