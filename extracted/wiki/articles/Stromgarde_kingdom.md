The **kingdom of Stromgarde**, also referred to as the **kingdom of Strom**, (ref: [Duke Sherwood#Quotes](Duke Sherwood#Quotes)) is one of the [seven human nations](Seven Kingdoms) in the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), and is located on the site of the original capital of [Arathor](Empire of Arathor), [Strom](Strom), in the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). Stromgarde is all that remains of the mighty human Empire of Arathor. The national color of Stromgarde is red, and its symbols are a closed fist and a bird with its wings spread.

During the [Second War](Second War), King [Thoras Trollbane](Thoras Trollbane) pledged his kingdom to the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron) against the [Orcish Horde](Orcish Horde). However, the nation rapidly declined following the [Third War](Third War), losing control of much of its territories and capital city after Thoras was assassinated under mysterious circumstances. In the aftermath, Prince [Galen](Galen Trollbane) was unable to repel an invasion of the combined [Syndicate](Syndicate), [Boulderfist](Boulderfist), and [Witherbark](Witherbark) forces. The city fell into ruins, with only a single district still held by the end of it; leading to the establishment of [Refuge Pointe](Refuge Pointe) and the [League of Arathor](League of Arathor), locked in battle with the [Defilers](Defilers) over control of [Arathi Basin](Arathi Basin).

## History
### Early history
The city of Stromgarde was founded by the [Arathi tribe](Arathi tribe) of humans in the Arathi Highlands during the [Troll Wars](Troll Wars). Originally named [Strom](Strom), the city became the capital of the great empire of Arathor. Following the peak of that empire's power many of its citizens left to found other cities, including Dalaran, Lordaeron, Alterac and Stormwind. Many of these newer cities began to attract settlers at an astonishing rate, and it was only a matter of years before the once-powerful Empire of Arathor was splintered into seven distinct human nations. Strom was no longer the center of the human lands, and it was during this time that its leaders changed its name to Stromgarde. (ref: [Arathor and the Troll Wars](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Arathor_and_the_Troll_Wars)) (ref: [The Seven Kingdoms](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Seven_Kingdoms))

### Second and Third Wars
By the time of the Second War, Stromgarde was a harbor city (ref: [The Fall of Stromgarde (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fall_of_Stromgarde_(WC2_Orc))) that controlled the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands) and the island-fortress of [Tol Barad](Tol Barad). Though not the largest, it was one of humanity's most powerful kingdoms. Led by [Thoras Trollbane](Thoras Trollbane), it was also a close and loyal ally of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)), and their mutual friendship would allow it to quickly join the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron) to stop the [Horde](Orcish Horde). By the end of the war, [Danath Trollbane](Danath Trollbane) was one of Stromgarde's most celebrated soldiers after acting as a great leader during the final battle for the liberation of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan). A prominent mercenary veteran, he served as the military commander of Stromgarde and tactical advisor to General [Turalyon](Turalyon), and was thus elevated to the position of Overseer of the orcish internment camp system based at [New Stormwind](Stormwind City). (ref: *Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal* manual, *[Legends of the Land](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Beyond_the_Dark_Portal_manual#Danath)*, Danath)

After the end of the war, Thoras Trollbane petitioned King [Terenas](Terenas Menethil II) of Lordaeron to annex the eastern portion of Alterac's territory to Stromgarde in recognition of the latter's sacrifices and valor during the Second War. This created a great deal of political tension between the two powers, which was all the more tragic considering the friendship between Trollbane and Terenas. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*) The Alterac debate was a contributing factor when Stromgarde left the Alliance. Additionally, Trollbane had grown displeased at King Terenas' decision to not execute the captured orcs as the expense at maintaining the internment camps grew. He finally pulled out of the Alliance around the same time as Gilneas and Quel'Thalas. (ref: *[Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos manual](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual)*, p.67)

During the [Third War](Third War), Stromgarde managed to prevent the undead [Scourge](Scourge) from ravaging through its lands, and sent a detachment of troops, the [Stromgarde Brigade](Stromgarde Brigade), to accompany [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore) and the [Human Expedition](Human Expedition) across the sea to [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). Despite this, the darkest hour of Stromgarde was yet to come. King Thoras was assassinated under mysterious circumstances, and while his son Prince [Galen](Galen Trollbane) became the leader of the remains of Stromgarde, the crown prince was unable to hold the kingdom together as the nation came under siege by both the [Syndicate](Syndicate) and the [Boulderfist](Boulderfist) ogres. The two forces won several victories against the now weakened kingdom, and managed to occupy much of its terrain, including most of the capital city.

### World of Warcraft
Though weaker than before, its population and territory greatly diminished, Stromgarde was still remembered as the starting point of humanity. Though much of the capital was lost, the Stromgarde militia maintained a base of strategic operations at the crevasse known as [Refuge Pointe](Refuge Pointe) in the Highlands, as well as portions of the devastated capital city itself, which is occupied by ogres and the criminals of the Syndicate. The Argus Wake, which secretly controlled the Syndicate, also planned to take over the capital to prepare the way for the Burning Legion's next invasion of Azeroth. (ref: [Decrypted Letter](Decrypted Letter))

The forces of Stromgarde formed the [League of Arathor](League of Arathor), which fought to free the rest of the Arathi Highlands, the Arathi Basin, and its many resources from the hands of the Forsaken Defilers as well as the Horde forces from [Hammerfall](Hammerfall). With the Stromgarde Militia, Prince Galen Trollbane ruled from the Trollbane family crypt, which safeguarded several ancient artifacts. (ref: [quest: Sigil of Trollbane]) During the invasion of the capital, [Archmage Trelane](Archmage Trelane) attempted to defend the [Tower of Arathor](Tower of Arathor), but was unsuccessful and exiled himself to [Stormwind City](Stormwind City). (ref: [quest: Malin's Request])

After Thandol Span and its dwarven troops suffered heavy damage due to a Dark Iron attack, (ref: [quest: The Dark Iron War]) [Rhag Garmason](Rhag Garmason) pleaded [Captain Nials](Captain Nials) to spare some of Stromgarde's soldiers to help them reclaim Dun Modr and the Thandol Span. However, the captain had no choice but to refuse, since honor and duty required them to not leave the Highlands until they reclaimed their fallen kingdom. (ref: [quest: Plea To The Alliance])

## Military
The Stromgarde Militia is the force protecting Stromgarde. The crimson-clad defenders are known for the martial discipline in battle and has proven themselves in the Second War which they were able to hold off the entire Horde for quite a while on their own, allowing the rest of the Alliance to mobilize.
