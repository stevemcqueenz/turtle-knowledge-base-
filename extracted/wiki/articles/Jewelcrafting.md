# Jewelcrafting

This fully-fledged profession introduces over 200 new items for you to craft, with recipes obtained from trainers, found in the world, and through exclusive quest-lines for Jewelcrafters that reward a unique recipe upon completion! Begin your jewelcrafting journey at the trainers located in Stormwind, Ironforge, and Alah'Thalas for the Alliance, or Orgrimmar, Undercity, and Sparkwater Port for the Horde.

What can jewelcrafters expect? Rings, necklaces, staves, trinkets, off-hand frills, helmets, and notably, gemstones—details to follow. Not all items will be available immediately and will unlock as you progress in the profession. Expect new trade goods such as gritted papers, polishing oils, and new jewels like Amber Topaz and Pure Moonstone, to name a few. Jewelcrafting demands collaboration! Some materials will require assistance from other professions, such as Enchanted Gemstone Oils provided by Enchanters.

The profession also introduces two new sub-specializations: Goldsmith and Gemology. You can choose one of these specializations upon reaching a skill level of 225 and character level 40, after completing a related quest-line, similar to other specializations.

All Jewelcrafters will be able to craft Gemstone Clusters, enabling the recycling of materials to acquire more jewels than typically possible through mining. Additionally, rare Gemstone Deposits will now appear in the world. These deposits can replace thorium veins and require a Mining skill of 310. Mining these deposits can yield various gems from different tiers, but most notably, they guarantee an Imperial Topaz, a key material for both Gemology and Goldsmith specializations, akin to Black Lotus and vital for high-level crafts.

## Master Jewelcrafter
Fanzy Sparkspring the Master Jewelcrafter. Found in Steamwheedle Port, Tanaris.

## Vendor Recipes
Where to find the Precision Jewelers Kit?

Ranik the trade supplier in Ratchet. 175 Engineering is required to craft the Precision Jewelers Kit.

The Jewelry Scope requires 225 Engineering to craft and the schematics are sold by Gonzo Snaphands the Jewelcrafting supplier in Steamwheedle Port Tanaris.

## Specialization Quest
Gemologist and Goldsmith specialization quests can be found in Ironforge and Undercity at the Jewelcrafting trainer. These quests become available to accept at level 34. Only one specialization is available to pursue. 

Goldsmiths focus on crafting equipment such as necklaces, rings, bracers, and staves, with powerful items, such as Epics, exclusive to this specialization. Gemologists specialize in unique gemstones, empowering necklaces and rings with various effects. Almost all gemstones are exclusive to this specialization.

### Gemologist Specialization - Mastering Gemology
Accept the specialization quest [Mastering Gemology](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41277) starting at level 34.  Bring [Mayva's Crystal](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=41353) to [Thegren](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=73102) the Artisan Gemologist, found in the Ruins of Corthan, northwest Badlands. He is located at the top of the hill.

[Thegren](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=73102) gives the quest [The Lifeblood](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41279). Kill the level 42 giant [Boreghast](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=61928), found in Dustwind Gulch, northeast Badlands, and retrieve the [Lifeblood Gem](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=41355).

Completing [The Lifeblood](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41279) will award gemstone recipes which are needed for the next stage of the specialization: [Plans: Sharpened Citrine Gemstone](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=70156), [Plans: Radiant Ember Gemstone](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=70157), and [Plans: Glowing Ruby Gemstone](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=70158).  These require Jewelcrafting 200 to learn, and create: [Sharpened Citrine Gemstone](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56002) (4 agility), [Radiant Ember Gemstone](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56004) (6 fire damage), and [Glowing Ruby Gemstone](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56006) (Vampirism 1%). 

The next quest from [Thegren](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=73102) is [Demonstration](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41280). This requires creating 5 copies of each of the gems learned from the previous quest. In order to complete this immediately, the required materials are: 5 [Star Ruby](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=7910), 5 [Citrine](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=3864), 5 [Emberstone](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=55250), 45 [Heavy Gritted Paper](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=55152), 5 [Lesser Mystic Essence](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=11134), and 15 [Gemstone Oil](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=55247). 

Once  [Thegren](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=73102) is satisfied with the Demonstration, he gives [Preparation](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41281). This is a dungeon quest, requiring a [Marbled Stone Slab](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=41356), which can be looted from [Landslide](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=12203) in Maraudon. The recommended level of this Dungeon is 48-55.

Finally, [Thegren](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?npc=73102) will give the last Gemology Specialization quest, [The Final Cut](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41282).  Right click on the [Thegren's Box](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=41358) given to you. This will put 3 items in your inventory: [Marbled Stone Slab](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56078), [Silken Cloth](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56080), and [Unrefined Lifeblood Gem](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56079).  Right click on the Silken Cloth and let it cast, creating [Thegren's Gritted Paper](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56081).  Then right click on the [Thegren's Gritted Paper](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56081) and let it cast, creating [Perfect Lifeblood Gem](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=56082). Turn in the quest, and receive [Artisan Gemology Specialization](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?spell=57551).
