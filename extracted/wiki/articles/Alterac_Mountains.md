The **Alterac Mountains** (or **Mountains of Alterac**) (ref: [Dorgar Stoenbrow#Plaque](Dorgar Stoenbrow#Plaque): "Last seen in the Mountains of Alterac") are a range of towering, (ref: *[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*, chapter 1) snow-capped (ref: *[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*, chapter 4) mountains in central [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). The mountains were home to the former human nation of [Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)) and included the settlements of [Alterac City](Alterac City) and [Strahnbrad](Strahnbrad), whose ruins are now occupied by either [Crushridge](Crushridge) [ogre](ogre)s or the [Syndicate](Syndicate).

## Geography
The Alterac Mountains are high peaks rising up north of the [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills), and spreading to the border of the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands) in the north and the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands) in the east. Covered with snow at their highest point, these mountains were once the location of three major human settlements. Now these settlements lie in ruins and are controlled by hostile forces. What remains of the third settlement, [the city of Dalaran](Dalaran), is still under restoration beneath a magical dome. The mountains themselves are home to several kinds of high-altitude flora and fauna, such as yeti, mountain lions and the rare herb [Wintersbite](Wintersbite).

There are no flight paths, dungeons or raid dungeons in the Alterac Mountains. However, the [Alterac Valley](Alterac Valley), a high level PvP battleground, lies within these mountains. In addition, the [Ruins of Alterac](Ruins of Alterac) are home to many elite ogres.
