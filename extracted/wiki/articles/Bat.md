# Bat

Bats are nocturnal, flying mammals that usually feed on fruit and insects.  Most are no bigger than the average bird.  There are a number of bat species in [WoW](WoW) that grow to enormous size and pose a threat to [player](player)s.
Some of them are useful mounts for the [Jungle Trolls](Jungle Trolls) and [Undead](Undead).  Bats are found in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), [Blackrock Depths](Blackrock Depths), [Shadowfang Keep](Shadowfang Keep), [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub), [Razorfen Kraul](Razorfen Kraul), the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), and [Uldaman](Uldaman).

Additional notes:
- Bats may be [neutral](neutral) or [hostile](hostile), depending on their species.

### Examples
- Vampire Bat
- Duskbat
- Plaguebat
- Bloodseeker Bat

### As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Have high DPS, medium HP, and low armor.
- Eats only Fruit and Fungus.
- Can learn [Cower](Cower), [Growl](Growl), [Dive](Dive), [Bite](Bite), and [Screech](Screech).
- The [Bloodseeker Bat](Bloodseeker Bat) of [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub) is currently immensely popular because of its 1.0 attack speed. However, this is pending to change in an upcoming normalisation patch.
