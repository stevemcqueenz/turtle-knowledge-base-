The **Lord Admiral** is the current ruler of the human kingdom and nation of [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)). The successor to Lord Admiral [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore), who fell in Theramore at the hands of the Mok'nathal [Rexxar](Rexxar) and his allies, it was under the Lord Admiral's rule that Kul Tiras isolated itself from the new [Alliance](Alliance) that rose in the place of the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron), estranged from their sister kingdoms that attempted to make peace with the [Horde](Horde).

Several years after the battle of Mount Hyjal, the Lord Admiral opened diplomatic relations with Stormwind once again, sending [Vice Admiral Renhoff](Vice Admiral Renhoff) and [Emissary Godwin Valorcall](Emissary Godwin Valorcall) to establish the embassy of [Davenburg](Davenburg) and represent Kul Tiras before Stormwind, and determine if the southern kingdom's interests still align with those of the old Alliance.
