# Sherwood Quarry

**Sherwood Quarry** is a mine located in northeastern [Northwind](Northwind). Once ruled by [Duke Johnathan Sherwood](Duke Johnathan Sherwood) and his family, it has now fallen to the [Dark Iron dwarves](Dark Iron dwarf) led by [Overseer Bragordi](Overseer Bragordi) that have expanded into the region. In addition to the upper quarry, the area also includes a mine within which the Dark Irons have put some of the local kobolds to work.
