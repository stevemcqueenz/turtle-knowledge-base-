# Thunder lizard

A heavyset, large lizard that bears a close resemblance to a [Kodo](Kodo).  They are aggressive beasts that prefer canyons and plains.  When in combat, they shoot thunder from their horns at distance and dole out harsh headbutts at close range.  Thunder lizards come in a variety of colors, from blue to pink to grey, and live in [Durotar](Durotar), [The Barrens](The Barrens), the [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains), and [Desolace](Desolace).

#### Fighting Thunder Lizards
Attacks which interrupt the spellcasting of NPCs such as the rogue's [Kick](Kick), the shaman's [Earth Shock](Earth Shock), and the warrior's [Shield Bash](Shield Bash) are also effective against these lizards. When you see the lightning building up in its mouth, hit it with the counter to casting used by your class and you won't have to worry about getting hit by any lightning bolts...you'll only have to worry about being trampled, gored, or crushed by a huge and irate reptile.
