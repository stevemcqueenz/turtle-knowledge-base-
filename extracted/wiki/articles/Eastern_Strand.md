# Eastern Strand

The **Eastern Strand** is a stretch of coastline in the southeast of [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills), complementary to the [Western Strand](Western Strand) further to the west. The Eastern Strand is home to the [Daggerspine](Daggerspine) [naga](naga) who are found on the beaches and in the water.
