# Venture Co. Mine

**The Venture Co. Mine**61 47 Mulgore (g/s/c) (also spelled **Venture Company Mine**) is an enormous [mine](mine) run by the [Venture Company](Venture Company) in the eastern cliffs of [Mulgore](Mulgore). The mine has three entrance points. The first is the main entrance where [Supervisor Fizsprocket](Supervisor Fizsprocket) resides—it is the only entrance accessible on foot. Fizsprocket is the goblin in charge of Venture Co. operations in the tauren homeland. The second entrance is a cliff, and the third entrance is a small lumber camp.
