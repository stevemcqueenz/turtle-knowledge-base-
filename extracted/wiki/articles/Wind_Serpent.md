# Wind Serpent

A brightly-colored, winged serpent that sports a feathered head.  Wind serpents are aggressive and use their whiplike tails, thunder breath, and needle fangs to attack their foes. They're found in [The Barrens](The Barrens), the [Wailing Caverns](Wailing Caverns), [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles),[ Dustwallow Marsh]( Dustwallow Marsh), [Feralas](Feralas), [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub), and the [Sunken Temple](Sunken Temple).  Wind Serpents seem to have a connection with [Hakkar the Soulflayer](Hakkar the Soulflayer).  

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Has high damage, medium armor, and low HP.  
- Eats Cheese, Fish, and Bread.
- Can learn [Growl](Growl), [Dash](Dash), [Cower](Cower), [Bite](Bite), and their special skill, [Lightning Breath](Lightning Breath).  Lightning Breath is capable of dealing a decent amount of ranged nature damage.
- Some Wind Serpents are cursed with abnormal stats.  These snakes have higher Int and Spi and lower HP, armor, and damage.  It's best to avoid these "caster" pets.  The wind serpents that suffer this are the Thunderhawks of the Barrens, Azzere the Skyblade of the Barrens, and the white Cloud Serpents of Thousand Needles.
- Despite gaining a unique skill and having an easy diet, Wind Serpents are not very common.
