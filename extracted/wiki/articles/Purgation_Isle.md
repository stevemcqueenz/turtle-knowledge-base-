# Purgation Isle

**Purgation Isle** is an island south of the shores of [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills), in a shape of a towering spiral. It contains a cemetery and a ruined scout tower.

The island has a mysterious, unknown history. It is haunted by the ghosts of renegade [paladin](paladin)s of the [Silver Hand](Knights of the Silver Hand), [priest](priest)s of the [Church of Light](Church of Light), but also [monk](monk)s and [mage](mage)s, that for some reason were condemned to be banished on the island and since cannot find peace in death. It seems that [Kormok](Kormok) is the cause of the isles ghosts as it is said that he delighted in summoning forth the souls of the banished dead.
