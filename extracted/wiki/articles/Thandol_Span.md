# Thandol Span

The [Thandol Span](Thandol Span) was one of greatest engineering projects ever completed by the [Dwarves](Dwarves).  It was orginally composed of three gigantic stone bridges, that spanned the cliffs between the Southern continent containing [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) and the Kingdom of Azeroth, and the northern Continent of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) containing the human nation of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) and the elven nation of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas).  It was located in an area once known as the [Thandol Valley](Thandol Valley). The bridges were capable of supporting the weight of entire armies, and served as the main route of trade between the Dwarves and [Humans](Humans).  The mighty bridges survived the Second War against the [Orcs](Orcs), but recently suffered heavy damage due to a Dark Iron attack.  Two of the three bridges have now been all but destroyed, and Dwarven forces are fighting fiercely to defend the last remaining bridge and retake the nearby town of [Dun Modr](Dun Modr).
