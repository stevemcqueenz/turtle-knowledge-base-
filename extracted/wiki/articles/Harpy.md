# Harpy

Harpies resemble a cross between a [Night Elf](Night Elf) female and a hunting bird, with long talons for feet, and large graceful wings for arms.  Harpies originaly hail from the [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains), but have since spread out across [Kalimdor](Kalimdor).  Harpy legends claim that they are descend from a group of female Night Elves who were cursed by [Queen Azshara](Queen Azshara). No male harpies exist, so harpies will often capture a male of another species to fill the role for a time, after which they become food for the nest. In lean times, harpies can lay unfertilized eggs that become female copies of the mother.

Vicious and predatory by nature, harpies are the bane of all other life in the areas were they take root.  They have been raiding [Tauren](Tauren) settlements since time out of mind, and it can only be assumed that they are as much a plague on the [Quillboar](Quillboar)s and others.  The only race rumored to have any peaceful relationship with the harpies are [Kobolds](Kobolds), who trade them scavanged tools and weapons for information and protection.

If not driven out, they quickly range out attacking anything and everything they can get.  Harpies are clearly very intelligent, since they are able to communicate with other races when they wish to.  The common echelons of Harpy society attack with crude weapons or simply with their razor sharp talons. They are well used to the ways of divine magic and treachery. The more elite Harpies tend to be master of elemental storm magic and are much more dangerous.

These vile, unscrupulous winged women live only to cause mischief and sorrow.
Hailing from the windy Stonetalon Mountains, the harpies are masterful
at ambushing hapless caravans and foolish travelers alike.

Harpies are found in [Teldrassil](Teldrassil), [The Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains), [Feralas](Feralas), [Mulgore](Mulgore), [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles), [Alterac Valley](Alterac Valley), [Tthe Barrens](Barrens), and [Durotar](Durotar).

# Matriarchies
### Witchwing
Residing in the northern Barrens in an area called [The Dry Hills](The Dry Hills) this group of harpies have been ravaging the [Horde](Horde)'s caravan supplies. The lower sections of their group are led by so called lieutenants, called Slayers. They stand out from the rest by wearing rings. They, in turn, answer to the leader of the matriarchy [Serena Bloodfeather](Serena Bloodfeather). She is the sister of a harpy named [Bloodfeather](Bloodfeather) who was slain by [Rexxar](Rexxar) and her attacks on the Horde are inspired by a sense of revenge. It seems she is assisted in her leadership by [Sister Rathtalon](Sister Rathtalon). Their plumage is black.

### Bloodfury
In the [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains), the [Tauren](Tauren) are having their own problems with the harpies. The Bloodfuries are the source of the increasing number of harpies in [Kalimdor](Kalimdor); they encroach upon all likes of life preventing the innocent from leaving their boundaries. Their leader, [Bloodfury Ripper](Bloodfury Ripper), is all but a queen to the Bloodfuries. She makes her home in [The Charred Vale](The Charred Vale), close to [Desolace](Desolace) where she is busy keeping out the [Tauren](Tauren) and [Night Elves](Night Elves) who are trying to regrow the forests. Their plumage is red and white.

### Bloodfeather
In [Teldrassil](Teldrassil), the harpies have sought to nest on the night elves' own homeland. Recently grown, the great tree is a perfect location for the Bloodfeather matriarchy, who have already started laying eggs to increase the size of their tribe. The night elves are struggling to keep the number of harpies down, and give out bounties for Bloodfeather belts collected from dead harpies.

### Dustwind
Dustwind harpies have settled down in the dry gulches and ravines of [Durotar](Durotar), much to the annoyance of the recently settled [orc](orc)s. The harpies assault caravans on the road south of [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar), and have laid claim to a relatively large pocket of passages in the jagged rises just north of [Razor Hill](Razor Hill). They have blue and red plumage.

### Windfury
The harpies of the Windfury matriarcy inhabit the new home of their ancestral enemies, the [tauren](tauren). They have nests both in the southern and the northern parts of the great grasslands, and constantly harass travelers journeying to and from [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff), situated on the nearby mesa. Their plumage is blue and white.

### Northspring
More harpies nest in the lush tropical forest of [Feralas](Feralas). The Northspring harpies are among the most dangerous of the matriarchies, and can quickly slaughter the unwary traveler lost in the wilds. As with most of their habitats, the harpies in Feralas clash with the local tauren tribe, base in [Camp Mojache](Camp Mojache). They have greenish skin and dark pink plumage.
