# Bay of Storms

The **Bay of Storms** is a portion of the [Great Sea](Great Sea) off the eastern coast of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), mostly noted by travelers on the eastern coast of the [Azshara](Azshara). It is the location of [Scalebeard's Cave](Scalebeard's Cave), [Hetaera's Clutch](Hetaera's Clutch), and the [Temple of Arkkoran](Temple of Arkkoran). The bay is located around the area of 53 55 Azshara (g/s/c).
