The **Gnomeregan Exiles** are the faction of gnomes who were exiled from their home, [Gnomeregan](Gnomeregan), in [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh). Though High Tinker [Gelbin Mekkatorque](Gelbin Mekkatorque), leader of the gnomes, had temporarily had to settle down in [Tinker Town](Tinker Town) after the fall of Gnomeregan, the gnomes have since reclaimed an old factory that has been refurbished into the [Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility](Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility) to serve as new quarters for the gnomish people.

## Reputation
[Bubulo Acerbus](Bubulo Acerbus) has the repeatable cloth quests to achieve a higher reputation with Gnomeregan Exiles. In return for exalted reputation, non-gnome players are able to ride [mechanostrider](mechanostrider)s.

The zones of [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains) and [Azshara](Azshara) offer many quests for gaining reputation with the Gnomeregan Exiles.

## Rewards
At exalted, [Axis Spinpistol](Axis Spinpistol) offers a tabard and a set of unique [engineer](Engineering)-exclusive epic scrapforged mechaspider mounts, and non-gnomish Alliance races are able to purchase and ride mechanostriders mounts (gnomes can ride Gnomeregan mechanostriders regardless of faction reputation).

Gnomeregan mechanostriders are bought from [Milli Featherwhistle](Milli Featherwhistle) at [Steelgril's Depot](Steelgril's Depot), northeast of [Kharanos](Kharanos) in [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh). Regular mechanostriders are available starting at level 40, with turbo mechanostriders available at level 60.
