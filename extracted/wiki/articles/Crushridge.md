# Crushridge

The Crushridge are a clan of [ogres](ogres) that have taken up residence in the ruins of [Alterac](Alterac). Their leader, [Mug'thol](Mug'thol), was able to free himself of [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner)'s control using the [Crown of Will](Crown of Will) and he broke away from the [Forsaken](Forsaken), renamed his clan the Crushridge and established a new home in Crushridge Hold.

The Crushridge Clan currently controls the fallen human city of [Alterac](Alterac). The [Syndicate](Syndicate), being the remnants of [Alterac](Alterac)'s citizenry, are in a constant battle with the Crushridge ogres for supremacy of the region.
