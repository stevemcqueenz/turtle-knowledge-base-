# Spirit Den

The **Spirit Den** is a hidden cave located on the north side of [Mistvale Valley](Mistvale Valley). There, you can find [Witch Doctor Unbagwa](Witch Doctor Unbagwa) for the quest [quest: Stranglethorn Fever].
