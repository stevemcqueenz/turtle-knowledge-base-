# Maggot

Maggots are emplyed as the young stage of various insectoid creatures, notably the [Silithid](Silithid)s and [Nerubian](Nerubian)s.

Maggots inhabit only a few places in the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth), often among more adult stages of Silithid such as in [the Barrens](the Barrens), or in huge variants in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands). They usually come in shades of white, yellow or green, and may or may not be aggressive.

Maggots vary quite much in size, from the cat-sized larvae of the Silithid to the enormous carrion grubs of the Plaguelands, the largest of which can be the size of elephants.
