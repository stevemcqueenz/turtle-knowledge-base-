# Eye of Dalaran

Source of [magic](magic) for the [nation](nation) of [Dalaran](Dalaran) which guards their borders and allows a secure environment for the magocratic nation. The Eye was spirited away when the [Horde](Horde) invaded Dalaran, but brought back when they rebuilt the city. [Ner'Zhul](Ner'Zhul) tried to steal the Eye, but was repulsed (we assume the Eye could have stabilized [Draenor](Draenor) when the [Dimensional Gateway](Dimensional Gateway)s opened). The Eye was kept in Dalaran, but its powers were not enough to stop [Archimonde](Archimonde) from destroying the city. 

We know is wasn't in the city ruins, because [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage) or [Kael'thas Sunstrider](Kael'thas Sunstrider) would surely have tried to obtain it. Some have speculated that [Jaina](Jaina) or [Medivh](Medivh) took it to [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), meaning that it resides either in [Stonetalon Peak](Stonetalon Peak) or [Theramore](Theramore Isle) [the latter being more likely], if the artifact even exists anymore. We think the Eye could be used in methods similar to both the [Sunwell](Sunwell) and the [Eye of Sargeras](Eye of Sargeras).
