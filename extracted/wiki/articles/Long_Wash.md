# Long Wash

**The Long Wash** is ironically enough the shortest stretch of coast in [Darkshore](Darkshore). It is the desolate stretch of sand that runs south from the town of [Auberdine](Auberdine) in [Darkshore](Darkshore) until due west of [Ameth'Aran](Ameth'Aran). The shore is gloomy and dark under the perpetual overcast skies of Darkshore, and the beach is littered with debris and the carcasses of dead sea animals. [Murloc](Murloc)s have recently been showing up with greater frequency along the coastline.
