# Anvil

An **anvil** is a large, heavy, [metal](metal) environmental [object](object) with a flat top surface and a point on one end most frequently found on a sturdy block of wood or pedestal. An anvil is required for most [blacksmithing](blacksmithing) and [engineering](engineering) [recipe](recipe)s. You do not have to interact with the anvil, you just have to be in the vicinity of the anvil.

An anvil graphic is not necessarily a functional anvil in the game. Generally, a usable anvil will be selectable (but does not have to be selected).

Anvils are usually found in [cities](cities) and towns, often paired with a [forge](forge) (which has similar use characteristics).
