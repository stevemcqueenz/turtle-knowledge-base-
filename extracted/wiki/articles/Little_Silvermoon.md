# Little Silvermoon

**Little Silvermoon**, formerly known as the **[Cut-Throat Alley](Cut-Throat Alley)**, is a back alley in the [Dwarven District](Dwarven District) of [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) that currently houses [high elven](High elf) refugees that have fled the destruction of Quel'Thalas. The street was granted to the high elven refugees on behalf of the House of Nobles. (ref: [quest: A New Place in Stormwind] *([Deprecated](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=80220))*) (ref: [quest: Thalassian Diplomacy])
