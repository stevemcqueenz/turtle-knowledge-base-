-Zmodlor** is a minor [demon](demon) who specializes in infiltration. During the events in *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, he recruits citizens of [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar) and [Theramore](Theramore) into the [Burning Blade](Burning Blade) and uses them to raise tensions between the [orc](orc)s and [human](human)s. He was banished thanks to the combined efforts of [Aegwynn](Aegwynn) and [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore).

## History
### Confrontation with Aegwynn
On the [world](world) of [Azeroth](Azeroth), at the very start of [Aegwynn](Aegwynn)'s tenure as [Guardian of Tirisfal](Guardian of Tirisfal), Zmodlor appeared and started a [cult](cult) called the [Burning Blade](Burning Blade clan). Zmodlor's cultists took over the [school](school)house of the village [Jortas](Jortas), planning to sacrifice its children using a sword covered in oil and set alight as a ritual tool. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 12) (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 16) Aegwynn appeared just in time to save the children and neutralize Zmodlor, with the event being her first ever demon banishment. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 14)

### Zmodlor's return
During the [Third War](Third War), Zmodlor was among the demons summoned to [Azeroth](Azeroth). One of many demons who survived the war, (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 20, pg. 208: "But when the Burning Legion attacked, they recruited many demons, including ones that had been banished by the Tirisfalen. When the war ended, several stragglers managed to stay in this world even when the legion was driven back.") Zmodlor sought to destroy the fragile peace between [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar) and [Theramore](Theramore).

Zmodlor recruited both orcs and humans who were prejudiced against one another into a new incarnation of the Burning Blade cult, promising each that he was only interested in helping them destroy the other. None of his minions were aware that he was also recruiting among their enemies. Among the members of Zmodlor's Burning Blade were [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore)'s chamberlain, [Kristoff](Kristoff (human)), and [Burx](Burx), one of [Thrall](Thrall)'s chief warriors. Another was the human [Margoz](Margoz (human)), who recruited new members in the [Demonsbane Inn](Demonsbane Inn) in Theramore. When the soldier [Strov](Strov) followed Margoz from the inn to investigate, Zmodlor slew him. Zmodlor later cast protective wards to trap Jaina and Aegwynn in [Bladescar](Bladescar) to prevent them from interfering.

Zmodlor's plan nearly succeeded, and a battle had already broken out at [Northwatch Hold](Northwatch Hold) before Jaina was able to discover Zmodlor's hideout atop [Dreadmist Peak](Dreadmist Peak). Jaina and Aegwynn confronted Zmodlor and several of his [warlock](warlock)s, and they were able to banish him to the Twisting Nether yet again.

Aegwynn did not believe that Zmodlor was acting on his own in using the Burning Blade to inflame violence between Orgrimmar and Theramore, telling Jaina that "a plan of this complexity is far beyond him". The identity of Zmodlor's master remained a mystery. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, Epilogue, pg. 237)

While speaking with Thrall following these events, Jaina stated that the Burning Legion would have a hard time rescuing Zmodlor a second time and that because he is a minor demon it would not be worth the effort. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, pg. 234)\
