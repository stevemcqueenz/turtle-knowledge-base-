Scarlet Commander **Renault Mograine** was the young commander of the military forces of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) at the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery).

## Biography
Renault Mograine was the son of Highlord [Alexandros Mograine](Alexandros Mograine) — also known as the Ashbringer, because of the [sword he wielded against the Undead](Ashbringer). He was a member of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade), the order that his father had helped found. However, during the [Third War](Third War), Grand Crusader [Saidan Dathrohan](Saidan Dathrohan) (the mortal form of the dreadlord [Balnazzar](Balnazzar)) had struck a deal with [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad), the lich who served as the Scourge overlord in the Plaguelands, to kill Alexandros; Dathrohan/Balnazzar would be in a position to take over the Crusade, and Kel'Thuzad would be rid of a great threat to his hold in Lordaeron. To that end, Dathrohan recruited young Renault, promising him great power and prestige in his Crusade if he could kill his father. (ref: [Corrupted Ashbringer/Scarlet Monastery Event](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Corrupted_Ashbringer/Scarlet_Monastery_Event))

Renault led Alexandros and his advisor [Fairbanks](High Inquisitor Fairbanks) into the ruined city of [Stratholme](Stratholme), the primary fortress of the Scourge in Lordaeron, where they were to be ambushed. Renault remained off in the distance, watching as the legion of undead descended upon. Fairbanks fell relatively early on, but Mograine was able to destroy wave after wave of the Scourge attackers. The Highlord weakened during the long battle, however, and dropped his sword to the ground; this was the opportunity Renault had waited for. Coming from behind his father, the boy picked up Ashbringer and ran it through Alexandros' heart, killing the Highlord and fouling the blade... thus dooming both to the service of Kel'Thuzad.

As promised, Renault was promoted to Scarlet Commander through his dealings with the Grand Crusader and sent to the Monastery in Tirisfal, as commander of the operations there. Much to Renault's dismay, Fairbanks had survived the ambush and returned to the Scarlet Monastery. Shouting and screaming, he told the tale of the lesser Mograine's betrayal to any and all who would hear him. While Fairbanks was murdered in cold blood for his actions and dragged to a hidden chamber of the Monastery Cathedral, some did listen to his words, and thus they separated from the Crusade to form the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn).

But Renault's treachery would not be forgotten, even by the death knight that was Highlord Mograine. An army of soldiers from the Argent Dawn entered into the Necropolis [Naxxramas](Naxxramas) and destroyed the corrupted Highlord. One of them took his [fouled blade](Corrupted Ashbringer) and travelled to the Scarlet Monastery, where Renault cheered the coming of a new Ashbringer to lead the Scarlet Crusade to victory against "the filth of this world" - in essence, those who were not of the Crusade. Upon entering the Crusader's Chapel, however, Highlord Mograine was to have his revenge.

The Highlord's spirit, chained to the corrupted blade, appeared in the chapel and confronted his horrified son with his treachery. He then lashed out, killing the Scarlet Commander instantly. Thus, as had happened in the past, Renault Mograine was killed by his own father, an act of revenge that would not be forgotten by either the Scarlet Crusade, or any who witnessed it that day.
