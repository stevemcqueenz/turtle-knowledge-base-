# Ghost Walker Post

**Ghost Walker Post** is a small [tauren](tauren) outpost established in central [Desolace](Desolace) directly adjacent to the [Kodo Graveyard](Kodo Graveyard). It is one of two [Horde](Horde) outposts in the region, though Ghost Walker Post is much more exposed to danger compared to [Shadowprey Village](Shadowprey Village) and its relatively secure location. It is from Ghost Walker Post that the Horde wages its war on the [Burning Blade](Burning Blade) and their servants in Desolace as well as the various [centaur](centaur) tribes. Ghost Walker Post is home to only the bare necessities of adventuring in Desolace, lacking so much as an inn or a wind rider master.
