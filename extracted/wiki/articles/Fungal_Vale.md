# Fungal Vale

**The Fungal Vale** is one of the most corrupted areas of the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), where the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath) has taken a strong hold over the land. The ground is a dark purple and the Vale is filled with a murky haze. The Fungal Vale is infested with members of the [Scourge](Scourge) and the [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned), at levels 40-41. The Vale is found north of the [Marris Stead](Marris Stead) and [Crown Guard Tower](Crown Guard Tower).
