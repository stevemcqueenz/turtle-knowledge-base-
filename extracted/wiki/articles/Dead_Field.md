# Dead Field

**The Dead Field** is the name of a decrepit and abandoned farm in northern [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest). The nature and number of the farm's former inhabitants are unknown. Recently the Field has become a base for the [Rot Hide](Rot Hide) [gnolls](gnolls), servants of the [Scourge](Scourge). It is unknown what the Scourge plans at the farm, but the increasing numbers of the gnolls suggests imminent military action. 

A reconnaissance mission undertaken by the Deathstalkers passed through the field on their mission through northern Silverpine, noting the presence of the Scourge at the farm. Repeated small assaults by the [Forsaken](Forsaken) on the Dead Field have been unsuccessful because of the arrival of a [banshee](banshee) known as [Nightlash](Nightlash). The banshee appears when the Rot Hides under her command are attacked.
