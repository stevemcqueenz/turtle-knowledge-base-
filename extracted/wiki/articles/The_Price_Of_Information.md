## Description
You've extracted all the information you could get from Vladeus. Deciding you're done with him you let him rest and think about your next actions.

It seems news of Whitemane's death and the demonic influence over Stratholme reached the ears of Lady Abbendis, the last true leader of the Scarlet Crusade.

With one of her henchmen tricking young men and women to join their cause and force them into staying she's trying to regain some numbers.

You decide to report this to Stoutfist and ask for his opinion.
