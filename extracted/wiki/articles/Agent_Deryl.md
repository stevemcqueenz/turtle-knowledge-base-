-Agent Deryl** is an [SI:7](SI:7) agent assigned to investigate the island of [Balor](Balor). During the course of this mission, he and nine fellow agents were discovered while surveying the perimeter around the [Croaking Plateau](Croaking Plateau) and were taken captive. While he was held in an abandoned inn by [Sorrowmore Lake](Sorrowmore Lake), three of his companions - [Agents Cherys](Agent Cherys), [Flynn](Agent Flynn) and [Elroy](Elroy) - were instead dragged to a cave beneath [Stormreaver Spire](Stormreaver Spire) to be experimented on by the Stormreavers.
