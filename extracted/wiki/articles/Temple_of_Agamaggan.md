# Temple of Agamaggan

The **Temple of Agamaggan** is located inside [Razorfen Downs](Razorfen Downs). Here, the Death's Head cult led by [Death Prophet Rakameg](Death Prophet Rakameg) works to raise their fallen god. It lies past [Glutton](Glutton)'s patrol area in the [Spiral of Thorns](Spiral of Thorns), before the spiral rises to [Amnennar the Coldbringer](Amnennar the Coldbringer).
