# Farewell Faire

The **Farewell Faire** (ref: [quest: The Fantastical Farewell Faire]) is located just north of [Moonhoof Village](Moonhoof Village) in [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast). It is a special occasion where all citizens of Azeroth gather round and reminisce about the memories they have made with their loved ones, friends, and even strangers. The feast is celebrated to grow gratitude towards the past and to look forward into the future with steadfast resolve.

The Faire can be reached by speaking with a [Turtle Faire Barker](Turtle Faire Barker) in any of the capital cities.

## Trivia
- The Fairwell Faire was created to offer the space and opportunity to talk, drink, eat, and celebrate together, to share stories of the past, or plan adventures yet to come with the community in light of Turtle WoW officially shutting down on May 15th, 2026.
