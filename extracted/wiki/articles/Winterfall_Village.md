# Winterfall Village

-Winterfall Village** is a large [village](village) of [corrupted furbolg](corrupted furbolg)s in the far eastern area of [Winterspring](Winterspring) 65 34 Winterspring (g/s/c) near [Everlook](Everlook). The corrupted furbolgs of Winterfall Village are some of the highest level [furbolg](furbolg)s in the game, so approach the village with caution. The village is ruled by [High Chief Winterfall](High Chief Winterfall), who resides in a small cave at the very top of the settlement, though he does wander out of his holding from time to time with a guard escort at his side.
