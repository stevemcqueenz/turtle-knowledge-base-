The **Swamp of Sorrows** (also once known as the **Swamps of Sorrow** (ref: [The Swamps of Sorrow (WC1 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Swamps_of_Sorrow_(WC1_Orc)))) is a [contested territory](contested territory) in the southern part of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms). Among its murk-ridden marshes the green dragonspawn patrol at the behest of their dreaming mistress, [Ysera](Ysera). The great [Dragon Aspect](Dragon Aspect) has warned of a lurking terror within the [Temple of Atal'Hakkar](Temple of Atal'Hakkar), and her loyal servants are determined to protect the land from the emerging doom.

## Geography
The Swamp of Sorrows contains no raid dungeons or battlegrounds. The Temple of Atal'Hakkar (aka the Sunken Temple), an instanced dungeon for adventurers levels 50-60, can be found in the eastern part of the zone. The Horde's outpost of Stonard can also be found in this zone, as well as the Alliance's recently restored Sorrowguard Keep. The Harborage is a small village of draenei who deal with both the Horde and the Alliance.
