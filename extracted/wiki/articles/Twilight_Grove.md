# Twilight Grove

The **Twilight Grove** sits in the middle of [Duskwood](Duskwood) and contains a [Great Tree](Great Tree). It is home to one of the four portals to the [Emerald Dream](Emerald Dream) and is guarded by the lieutenants of [Ysera](Ysera)'s [Green dragonflight](Green dragonflight).

The Twilight Grove also contains a moonwell and a night elf gazebo. The other three dream portals can be found in [Feralas](Feralas), the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands), and [Ashenvale](Ashenvale).
