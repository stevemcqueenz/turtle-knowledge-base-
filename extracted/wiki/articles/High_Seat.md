# High Seat

The **High Seat** is the throne room of [King Magni Bronzebeard](King Magni Bronzebeard) in the [dwarf](dwarf) city of Ironforge. It also houses a few other quest NPCs, including [Deliana](Deliana) and [Royal Historian Archesonus](Royal Historian Archesonus).
