King **Barathen Wrynn**, also known as **King Wrynn III**, (ref: *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans* manual, [Chronicles of the War in Azeroth](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft:_Orcs_%26_Humans_manual#Chronicles_of_the_War_in_Azeroth), 559) was the son of King [Landan Wrynn](Landan Wrynn) and the ruler of the [Kingdom of Azeroth](Stormwind (kingdom)) prior to the [First War](First War) during the opening of the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal). His wife was the Lady [Varia](Varia Wrynn), and their only child was King [Llane Wrynn I](Llane Wrynn I). The rule of King Barathen Wrynn was prosperous and peaceful for many years prior to the coming of the orcs.

Under his reign, the island of [Balor](Balor) was discovered in the [Great Sea](Great Sea) to the west of the kingdom by [Duke Balor](Duke Balor), who settled upon them with like-minded followers in pursuit of greater wealth. It was soon discovered that the island was rich in resources, allowing it to prosper as a self-sufficient autonomous realm that soon became a valuable merchant port. The Grahan family, renowned in the kingdom for their mercantile prestige, was sent by King Barathen to join Duke Balor in order for Stormwind to maintain a measure of influence over the bustling maritime port, (ref: [The Founding of Balor](The Founding of Balor)) and Balor would later be brought under the sovereignty of Stormwind proper after Barathen dedicated the armies of Azeroth to relieve a siege by the pirates of [Plunder Island](Plunder Island) in exchange for Balor's loyalty. (ref: [The Siege of Balor](The Siege of Balor))

Barathen died the year after the initial orcish assault on Stormwind Keep, and was succeeded by his son King Llane. After the first orcish invasion, Barathen pledged to rid the land of the curse of the orcs, but died before that pledge could ever be fulfilled. (ref: *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans* manual, [Chronicles of the War in Azeroth](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft:_Orcs_%26_Humans_manual#Chronicles_of_the_War_in_Azeroth), 584)
