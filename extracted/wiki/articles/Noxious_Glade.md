# Noxious Glade

**The Noxious Glade** is a secluded glade found in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands) just north of [Light's Hope Chapel](Light's Hope Chapel) up a hill.  The glade suffers from the blight of the [Scourge](Scourge) and is even more tainted then the surrounding [Plaguelands](Plaguelands).  The air of the glade is a thick, sickly green and the ground is a dark purple.  A plague cauldron and a few [ziggurat](ziggurat)s are found within the glade, as well as several Scourge mobs.  The mobs inhabiting the glade are levels 57-60.
