# Thorium Brotherhood

## Summary
The Thorium Brotherhood are an elite group of craftsmen who can reveal a number of high-end recipes if you gain enough faction with them.

### Lokhtos Darkbargainer
Lokhtos is found in the [Blackrock Depths](Blackrock Depths).  You can give him different items to receive faction with the Brotherhood.  Ten [Dark Iron Ores](http://www.thottbot.com/index.cgi?i=8848), four [Core Leathers](http://www.thottbot.com/index.cgi?i=26938), four [Fiery Cores](http://www.thottbot.com/index.cgi?i=26592), four [Lava Cores](http://www.thottbot.com/index.cgi?i=26657), or one [Blood of the Mountain](http://www.thottbot.com/index.cgi?i=18634).  It seems that all five gifts result in the same faction and *only* faction.

Of the gifts, Dark Iron Ore can be mined in the [Blackrock Depths](Blackrock Depths). The Blood of the Mountain is a rare gem from the same veins.  Fiery and Lava Cores drop in the [Molten Core](Molten Core).  Core Leather can be skinned from certain beasts in the Molten Core, but requires [Skinning](Skinning) of over 300.  A dagger, [Finkle's Skinner](http://www.thottbot.com/?i=13374), drops in the [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire) that can assist by providing +10 Skinning.

To reach friendly faction with the Brotherhood requires thirty turn-ins, honored requires sixty more, and revered requires one hundred and twenty more.  In all, that would be 3,100 Dark Iron Ores.

At each new level of faction, Lokhtos offers a new set of recipes.  These recipes require some combination of the items you can offer him to gain faction.

### Malyfous Darkhammer
Malyfous is another member of the Brotherhood, found in Winterspring.  He gives the quests for [Fiery Plate Gauntlets](http://www.thottbot.com/index.cgi?q=4779) and [the Cap of the Scarlet Savant](http://www.thottbot.com/index.cgi?q=5135).  It isn't known whether more quests with him are unlocked with higher faction, but neither of these quests give Thorium Brotherhood faction.
