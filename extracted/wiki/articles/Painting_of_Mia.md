# Painting of Mia

The **Painting of Mia** is a depiction of [Mia Greymane](Mia Greymane), the late wife of King [Genn Greymane](Genn Greymane) of [Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)) located inside the [Gilneas City library](Gilneas City library). It is a portrait of her as a young child. [Luke Agamand](Luke Agamand) asks Horde adventurers to steal it in order to fence it for a profit. (ref: [quest: A Royal Heist])

## Objective of
Contains [Portrait of Mia Greymane](Portrait of Mia Greymane) for the quest [quest: A Royal Heist].
