# Silverwing Outpost

**Silverwing Outpost** is located in south-central [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), north of the border with [the Barrens](the Barrens) and southwest of [Splintertree Post](Splintertree Post). It is the eastern [Alliance](Alliance) bastion of defense against the [Horde](Horde), and it is located on the Horde side of the [Falfarren River](Falfarren River). Because of this, the sentinels posted there have to fight back constant attacks from the nearby [orc](orc)s, who are unrelenting in their goal of ridding the woods of the Silverwings once and for all.
