## Dungeon denizens
The Armory, the center-right entrance, consists of five sections: the [Training Grounds](Training Grounds); the [Footman's Armory](Footman's Armory); the [Crusader's Armory](Crusader's Armory); the [Warehouse](Warehouse) and the boss [Armory Quartermaster Daghelm](Armory Quartermaster Daghelm); and the [Hall of Champions](Hall of Champions) with the boss [Herod](Herod).
