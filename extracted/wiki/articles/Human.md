# Human

*This article is about the human lore. For the playable race, see [Human (playable)](Human (playable)).*

  *"Esarus thar no'Darador' - By Blood and Honor We Serve."*
   — Archaic human saying, later adopted as the motto of the [Knights of the Silver Hand](Knights of the Silver Hand).

**Humans** (collectively referred to as **mankind** or **humanity**) are a resilient species native to the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth).

Early humans were primarily a scattered and tribal people for several millennia, until the rising strength of the [troll](troll) empire forced their strategic unification. Thus the nation of [Arathor](Arathor) was formed, along with its capital, the city-state of [Strom](Strom). After several centuries of peace, however, the increasingly prosperous and independent city-states of Arathor split into separate [kingdoms](Seven Kingdoms): [Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)) to the west, [Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)), [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)), and [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)) to the northwest, [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)) to the southwest, and [Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)) to the far south. Strom itself was renamed [Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)) and remained significantly powerful.

But disaster struck when the [orcish Horde](orcish Horde) appeared on Azeroth, reducing Stormwind to ruins during the [First War](First War). The city's survivors, including the young prince, [Varian Wrynn](Varian Wrynn), fled to Lordaeron, where the leaders of the [seven kingdoms](Seven Kingdoms) resolved to unify once again in the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron). Joined together, they succeeded in defeating the Horde during the [Second War](Second War). Tensions grew among the kingdoms, however, as the costs for maintaining the Alliance rose much higher than originally anticipated. Unwilling to pay the taxes, the leaders of Gilneas and Stromgarde chose to withdraw their kingdoms following the internment of the orcs.

Further disaster came when the kingdom of Lordaeron was decimated by a [mysterious plague](Plague of Undeath) that killed thousands of humans and converted them into undead servants of the [Lich King](Lich King). Even Lordaeron's prince, [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil), was manipulated by the Lich King, leading Arthas to kill his own [father](Terenas Menethil II) and journey to Northrend, where he merged with his master. For the next five years, the Lich King remained in Northrend, plotting and building up his [armies](Scourge). But unleashing the plague of undeath was merely preparation for an invasion by the demonic [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), which had long sought to destroy all life on Azeroth. When the Legion struck, humans banded together with other races and secured a costly victory during the [Third War](Third War).

Since the fall of Lordaeron, the kingdom of Stormwind has become the strongest bastion of humanity and the most powerful force in the now multiracial Alliance.

Though humans are among the younger races on Azeroth, they have faced many challenges with fortitude and resilience. Their continued ability to adapt and rebuild has made them a vital force in an ever-changing world.

## History

### The rise and fall of the Arathor Empire
Humans joined the forefront of Azerothian history during the [Troll Wars](Troll Wars). Concerned that the [forest trolls](Forest troll) were becoming too great a threat, the [Arathi tribe](Arathi tribe) of humans embarked on a campaign to conquer its rivals through combat and politics. By offering equality and peace to the people they conquered, the Arathi were able to form a powerful nation, [Arathor](Arathor). The capital of [Strom](Strom) was built, and the humans in the area that would later become [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), traveled to its protection. (ref: [Arathor and the Troll Wars](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Arathor_and_the_Troll_Wars))

The [high elves](High elf) of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas), meanwhile, were in open war with the trolls. On the verge of defeat, they sent ambassadors to Strom to plead for assistance from the human king, [Thoradin](Thoradin). In exchange for support, the elves agreed to instruct [One Hundred](One Hundred) humans in the use of [magic](magic). With the help of the high elves, humans learned magic and waged a bitter war against the trolls that once spanned the entire length of the Eastern Kingdoms. Eventually, humanity won against the trolls and they lived in peace for several centuries. However, the increasingly prosperous and independent city-states of Arathor later declared their independence; splitting into separate [kingdoms](Seven Kingdoms): [Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)) to the west, [Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)), [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)), and [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)) to the northwest, [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)) to the southwest, and [Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)) to the far south. Strom itself was renamed [Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)) and remained a significantly powerful kingdom. (ref: [The Seven Kingdoms](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Seven_Kingdoms))

Among the history books in [Medivh](Medivh)'s [library](Guardian's Library), some mentioned references of genocides, assassinations, and massacres between humans of the seven kingdoms, using claims of nobility, heritage, and honor to justify their conflicts. Among these battles, there were military conflicts between the nations of Alterac and Stromgarde, as the proximity of the two and the apparent ambiguity of their mountain borders had led to several wars and skirmishes. (ref: *The Last Guardian*)

### The Alliance of Lordaeron
Millennia later, disaster struck when the orcish [Horde](Horde) appeared on Azeroth, reducing Stormwind to ruins during the [First War](First War) between orcs and humans. (ref: [The Dark Portal and the Fall of Stormwind](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dark_Portal_and_the_Fall_of_Stormwind)) The city's survivors, including the young prince, [Varian Wrynn](Varian Wrynn), fled to Lordaeron, where the leaders of the seven kingdoms resolved to unify once again in the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron). Joined together, they succeeded in defeating the Horde during the [Second War](Second War). (ref: [The Alliance of Lordaeron](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Alliance_of_Lordaeron))

Tensions grew among the kingdoms, however, as the costs for maintaining the Alliance rose much higher than originally anticipated. Unwilling to pay the taxes, the leaders of Gilneas and Stromgarde chose to withdraw their kingdoms from the Alliance after the Horde's defeat, refusing to spend their nation's resources on keeping the orcs alive in internment camps or in rebuilding other nations devastated by the war. At the time of the [Third War](Third War), most contemporary human and [high elven](High elf) mages bowed to the sovereignty of Dalaran and the Kirin Tor. Further disaster came when the kingdom of Lordaeron was decimated by a [mysterious plague](Plague of Undeath) that killed thousands of humans and converted them into [undead](undead) servants of the [Lich King](Lich King). Even Lordaeron's prince, [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil), was manipulated by the Lich King, leading Arthas to kill his own [father](Terenas Menethil II) and direct the [Scourge](Scourge) to eradicate humanity in his kingdom. Eventually, Arthas journeyed to [Northrend](Northrend), where he merged with his master. For the next five years, the Lich King remained in Northrend, plotting and building up his [armies](Scourge). (ref: [Legacy of the Damned](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Legacy_of_the_Damned))

The remnants of the Alliance in Lordaeron banded together to form the [Alliance Remnants](Alliance Remnants) in order to retake their lost lands from the [undead](undead). Under the command of Grand Marshal [Othmar Garithos](Othmar Garithos), the Alliance resistance managed to retake [Dalaran](Dalaran). (ref: [Misconceptions (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Misconceptions_(WC3_BloodElf))) However, Garithos' harsh bigoted leadership forced the [blood elves](Blood elf) to desert his army (ref: [The Dungeons of Dalaran (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dungeons_of_Dalaran_(WC3_BloodElf))) (ref: [The Crossing (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Crossing_(WC3_BloodElf))) and sometime later, the Alliance remnants were mind controlled by the [Dreadlord Insurgents](Dreadlord Insurgents). The Alliance remnants were freed from the dreadlords' influence by [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner)'s [forces](Sylvanas' Forces) (later known as the [Forsaken](Forsaken)) (ref: [Dreadlord's Fall (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Dreadlord%27s_Fall_(WC3_Undead))) and together, they worked to take down the last dreadlord warlord, [Balnazzar](Balnazzar), and retake the [Ruins of Lordaeron](Ruins of Lordaeron). Instead of leaving Lordaeron as Sylvanas promised however, Sylvanas betrayed Garithos and had him and his men killed; thereby taking the [Ruins of Lordaeron](Ruins of Lordaeron) for herself. (ref: [A New Power in Lordaeron (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_New_Power_in_Lordaeron_(WC3_Undead)))

### Post Third War
With the recent invasion of the Burning Legion, which left the northern [Kingdom of Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)) in ruins, [Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)) now stands as the strongest bastion of human civilization. The [Alliance](Alliance) since then has been reorganized and gained new allies in the following years, with the humans of Stormwind standing resolute in their charge to maintain the honor and might of humanity. Following the heroic examples of the legendary [Sir Lothar](Anduin Lothar) and [King Llane](Llane Wrynn I), the knights and warriors of Stormwind are considered to be among the fiercest and most courageous warriors in the land. (ref: [Game Guide/Races#Humans](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Guide/Races#Humans))

The humans' primary threat now other than the Scourge is the [Forsaken](Forsaken), who are continuing their attempts to expand south from [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades) into [Arathi](Arathi). However, the human presence in the region remained strong with the remnants of Lordaeron and Stromgarde, causing the [Hillsbrad](Hillsbrad Foothills) area to be one of Azeroth's major battlefields. Despite all of this, humans continue to be vigilant, and have banded closer together and with the Alliance as a whole.

In Lordaeron, the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) formed to fight the Scourge as a noble order led by Highlord [Alexandros Mograine](Alexandros Mograine), who wielded the [Ashbringer](Ashbringer), along with [Grand Inquisitor Isillien](Grand Inquisitor Isillien) and [High General Abbendis](High General Abbendis). The arrival of [Saidan Dathrohan](Saidan Dathrohan), former Lord Commander of the [Knights of the Silver Hand](Knights of the Silver Hand), saw the order slowly become corrupted with Dathrohan's ascension as Grand Crusader. Datheohan's identity had been usurped by the dreadlord [Balnazzar](Balnazzar), who had survived his previous encounter with Sylvanas Windrunner and began to use the Crusade to further his schemes - however, he made a bargain with [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) to rid himself of Mograine, leading to Alexandros' death at the hands of his own son [Renault](Renault Mograine) in Stratholme. When the truth of Renault's betrayal was revealed by [High Inquisitor Fairbanks](High Inquisitor Fairbanks), many of the Crusade splintered and formed the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn) in disgust at the corruption that had fostered within the Scarlet leadership. (ref: [Corrupted Ashbringer/Scarlet Monastery Event](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Corrupted_Ashbringer/Scarlet_Monastery_Event))

While en route to a secret diplomatic meeting, King [Varian Wrynn](Varian Wrynn) of Stormwind disappeared, having been kidnapped by members of the [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood) in [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor). Due to King Varian's absence, his son [Anduin](Anduin Wrynn) was crowned king, though the actual power remained in Regent Lord [Bolvar Fordragon](Bolvar Fordragon)'s hands until either Varian was returned or Anduin reached the age of ascension. The Kingdom of Stormwind was kept in a state of chaos and political unrest due to corruption created by Lady [Katrana Prestor](Katrana Prestor) and the [House of Nobles](House of Nobles). The towns of [Darkshire](Darkshire), [Lakeshire](Lakeshire), [Goldshire](Goldshire), [Ambershire](Ambershire), [Southshore](Southshore), and (to a lesser extent) [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor)- who all fall under the protection and leadership of Stormwind- had to rely more heavily on their own local militia to fend off local threats while Stormwind's leadership was in disarray.

Under the watchful leadership of the young mage [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore), formerly of [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)), the [Human Expedition](Human Expedition) that fled the destruction of Lordaeron joined forces with the [night elves](Night elf) and the [Horde](Horde) in order to defeat the demon [Archimonde](Archimonde) and banish the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) during the [Third War](Third War). Afterwards, the expedition established a new home in [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) called [Theramore Isle](Theramore Isle), which became a prominent member of the Alliance and kept close ties with Stormwind. Lady Jaina Proudmoore hopes to lead [Theramore](Theramore (nation)) to a prosperous future and aid in reuniting the distant human kingdoms once more. (ref: *World of Warcraft: Game Manual*) With the fall of Lordaeron to the Scourge and the dissolution of what remained of the old Alliance, Kul Tiras isolated itself from the new Alliance that rose in its place, estranged from their sister kingdoms that attempted to make peace with the Horde, as the remnants of Lord Admiral [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore)'s forces continued to wage war against Durotar. The city-state of [Dalaran](Dalaran), with its powerful mages, retained some control over Hillsbrad and Silverpine Forest but mostly isolated themselves behind a vast, magical dome around the heart of their city. (ref: [quest: Dalaran Patrols]) In addition, there is the human enclave in the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), the remnants of the Kingdom of [Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)). After the assassination of [Thoras Trollbane](Thoras Trollbane), Stromgarde was invaded by the [Syndicate](Syndicate), [forest trolls](Forest troll), and [ogres](Ogre). The [Horde](Horde) has also established themselves in the contested land and are fighting for the resources of [Arathi Basin](Arathi Basin). The remnants of Stromgarde rejoined the Alliance in order to rid its kingdom of interlopers and restore the kingdom to its former glory. It was rumored the once-allied kingdom of [Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)) was still flourishing and populated with humans, but for years no word had been heard of them. They too had isolated themselves from the world after they barricaded themselves with the [Greymane Wall](Greymane Wall).

### Mysteries of Azeroth
Kul Tiras, despite having isolated itself from the new Alliance, still attempted to maintain diplomatic relations with the faction. On the orders of [Daelin's successor](Lord Admiral of Kul Tiras), [Vice Admiral Renhoff](Vice Admiral Renhoff) and [Emissary Godwin Valorcall](Emissary Godwin Valorcall) established the embassy of [Davenburg](Davenburg) within the sister kingdom of Stormwind. The exploratory fleet led by [Lieutenant Alverold](Lieutenant Alverold) also returned from its mission and established the outpost of [Anchor's Edge](Anchor's Edge) on an island just south of the [Echo Isles](Echo Isles), beginning to encroach on [Durotar](Durotar) to support [Lieutenant Benedict](Lieutenant Benedict)'s forces in [Tiragarde Keep](Tiragarde Keep).

Gilneas was opened up to the world at last through the efforts of the [Ravenwood Rebellion](Ravenwood Rebellion), which cracked open the [Greymane Wall](Greymane Wall) in order to seek aid from the Alliance. The Regent-Lords Harlow, originally envoys of Alterac sent to King Greymane's court by Lord Daval Prestor during the Alterac crisis, had wrested control of the kingdom and ushered in a a reign of tyranny over Gilneas. Led by Lord [Lord Darius Ravenwood](Lord Darius Ravenwood), the people of Gilneas rose up against the oppressive regime, forming the Ravenwood Rebellion and beginning the [Gilnean civil war](Gilnean civil war). (ref: [quest: The Rebellion]) (ref: [Celia's Journal](Celia's Journal)) [Lord Ivar Pyrewood](Lord Ivar Pyrewood) travelled to [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) to petition the nobles of Azeroth to their cause and to seek aid in locating and freeing Isiden Perenolde. (ref: [quest: Legacy of Perenolde]) The Gilneas Brigade, once stationed as part of the budding city-state of Theramore, redeployed back home upon hearing of the troubles that haunted their homeland. (ref: [quest: The Brigade is Coming Home]) On orders of [Highlord Bolvar Fordragon](Highlord Bolvar Fordragon), [Marshal Redpath](Marshal Redpath) of [Southshore](Southshore) sent all able-bodied soldiers and adventurers to lend assistance to the Ravenwood Rebellion in their struggle for the liberation of Gilneas. (ref: [quest: The Wall Comes Crashing Down])
