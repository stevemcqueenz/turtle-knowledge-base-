**Grizzlowe** is a [goblin](goblin) shipmaster located in [Booty Bay](Booty Bay) in the [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). He gives information about the boat going to [Ratchet](Ratchet). He thinks Booty Bay has the best [Clam Chowder](Clam Chowder) to be found anywhere in the [South Seas](South Seas).

## Quotes
**Gossip**
I must say, Booty Bay has the best Clam Chowder to be had anywhere in the South Seas. Oh, not interested in cuisine eh? Well then let me be the first to welcome you to the berth of the Maiden's Fancy, the finest passenger ship to travel between the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor. She's due back for another trip to Ratchet soon, so make yourself comfortable till she arrives.
