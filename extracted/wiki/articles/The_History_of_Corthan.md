# The History of Corthan

"**The History of Corthan**" is a book required for [A King's Plea](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=40716). It can be looted from a chest found in the hidden crypt of [Marauder King Corthan](Marauder-King Corthan), near [Hammertoe's Digsite](Hammertoe's Digsite).
