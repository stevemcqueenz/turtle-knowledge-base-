# Ooze

Living blobs of slime that consume all that they touch.

Oozes inhabit many places in the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth), from the tomb of the king in the [Wetlands](Wetlands) to [Maraudon](Maraudon) in [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). Green and Black oozes don't contain much material useful to the average traveller: mostly broken arrows, decomposed boots and the like, though as of 1.9, they are dropping more valuable items.

One of the biggest theories how they developed on Azeroth is the idea that oozes and slimes are tied to the creation of this planet, almost like they're a secretion of it. 

## Traits
Oozes (also known as Slimes or Sludges) generally consume anything they move over.  As such their bodies are usually peppered with items that they can not digest such as bones, arrow heads or the odd piece of armor. Though its somewhat counter-intuitive, [Rogues](Rogues) can [Pick Pocket](Pick Pocket) Oozes. Because Oozes so readily absorb everything around them, they tend to closely reflect their environment.  For instance, Oozes and Slimes that live in areas tainted by the [Blight](Blight), often have the ability to pass on terrible diseases to those they attack.
