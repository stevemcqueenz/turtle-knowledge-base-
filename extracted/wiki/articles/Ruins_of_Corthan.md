# Ruins of Corthan

The **Ruins of Corthan** are the ruins of the ancient capital of [Corthan](Corthan), an old civilization named after its once warrior king. This place is inhabited by Earthen, who have recently begun to uncover these ruins and catalogue them for an unknown purpose.
