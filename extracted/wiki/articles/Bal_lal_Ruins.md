# Bal'lal Ruins

Ancient [troll](troll) ruins that are located near the Northwestern coast of Stranglethorn Vale, north of [Grom'gol Base Camp](Grom'gol Base Camp) and located on the coast of The Vile Reef.  A number of [Bloodscalp](Bloodscalp) trolls reside in what's left of the ruin.
