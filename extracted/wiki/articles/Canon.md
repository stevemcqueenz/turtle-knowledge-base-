# Canon

**Canon** is a term oft-used to refer to official lore, specifically the fictional accounts in the *Warcraft* universe as portrayed in Turtle WoW. It is used to define which lore should be considered a genuine part of the universe's history.

Turtle WoW's 'canon' is derived from the official *Warcraft* 'canon', and thus comprises a specific selection of works and sources of information considered from the *Warcraft* 'canon' to be authoritative and representative of the 'true' or official history of the *Warcraft* universe as it is portrayed in Turtle WoW. Therefore, 'canon' or 'canonical' lore is that which is part of this body of work, and is thus considered part of the true history in Turtle WoW.

In contrast, 'non-canon' information, while often containing characters and depicting events from canon sources, is not necessarily a reliable source of official information regarding the *Warcraft* universe as it is portrayed in Turtle WoW. Most works and installments of the official *Warcraft* franchise published or released during or following *[World of Warcraft: The Burning Crusade](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_The_Burning_Crusade)* (2007) are considered non-canon in Turtle WoW. (ref: [Gheor from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1340418568487043132) (dead link) - "what came before TBC is canon.") The *[Warcraft RPG](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_RPG)* and [The Sunwell Trilogy](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Sunwell_Trilogy) are not considered canon, (ref: [Gheor from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1340419086403895356) (dead link) - "that's what the canon from the rpg, if not mention--not canon.") though elements are sometimes taken from them and added in-game. (ref: [Gheor from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1340419034272890972) (dead link) - "ask in, we pick stuff from it and we present it in game.") (ref: [Moriena from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1490071852754665603) (dead link) - "I think giving it the RPG treatment is fair")
