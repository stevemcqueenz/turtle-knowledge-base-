The ogre mage **Dentarg** was one of the most trusted servants of [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul) prior to the destruction of [Draenor](Draenor). A valued servant of Ner'zhul and the [Shadowmoon clan](Shadowmoon clan), Dentarg was often called upon to rally support from wayward clans and put them back in line should they oppose Ner'zhul's absolute rule.

Some of Dentarg's most famous exploits include retrieving the [Skull of Gul'dan](Skull of Gul'dan) from a [Bonechewer clan](Bonechewer clan) captain, employing both the [Thunderlord clan](Thunderlord clan) and a group of [Shattered Hand clan](Shattered Hand clan) warriors. (ref: [The Skull of Gul'dan (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Skull_of_Gul%27dan_(WC2_Orc))) He also personally oversaw the defeat of the Bonechewers and Thunderlords during an uprising before the second crossing into Azeroth, as the two clans refused to follow Ner'zhul's orders. (ref: [Thunderlord and Bonechewer (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Thunderlord_and_Bonechewer_(WC2_Orc)))

Dentarg's fate following the destruction of Draenor is unknown, but he apparently perished and [one of his heads](Spine of Dentarg) was mounted on a staff that fell into the possession of the demon [Mergothid the All-Seeing](Mergothid).
