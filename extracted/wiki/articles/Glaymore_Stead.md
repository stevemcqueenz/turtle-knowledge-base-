# Glaymore Stead

The **Glaymore Stead** is an abandoned farmland located on the hills overlooking [Stillward Church](Stillward Church) in [Gilneas](Gilneas). The Glaymore family was once a prominent family of farmers, but during the [Gilnean civil war](Gilnean civil war) the entire family was tried for conspiracy and treason to the kingdom, found guilty to the last by [Judge Sutherland](Judge Sutherland). Now, all that remains of them is the [Angered Phantom](Angered Phantom) of Karl Glaymore, who continues to haunt his old home.
