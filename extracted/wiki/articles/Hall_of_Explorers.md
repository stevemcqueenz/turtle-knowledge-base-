# Hall of Explorers

The **Hall of Explorers** is home to the famed Ironforge [Explorers' Guild](Explorers' Guild) (aka Explorer's League). Every archaeological expedition the [Alliance](Alliance) sends out is coordinated from here. It also houses the Ironforge museum and [Library](Library).

To get to the Hall of Explorers, you can head northeast from the [Great Forge](Great Forge), north from [Tinker Town](Tinker Town), or east from the [Forlorn Cavern](Forlorn Cavern).
