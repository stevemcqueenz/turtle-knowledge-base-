# Pantheon

The group of [Titan](Titan)s who shaped worlds and watched over the universe.

  - [Aman'Thul](Aman'Thul), Highfather of the Pantheon**
  Bestowed a portion of his cosmic power upon the massive bronze dragon [Nozdormu](Nozdormu), to guard time itself and police the ever-spinning pathways of fate and destiny

  - [Eonar](Eonar), Patron of All Life**
  Gave a portion of her power to the red dragon [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza), to work to safeguard all living creatures within the world.
  Also blessed Alexstrasza's younger sister, the lithe green dragon [Ysera](Ysera), with a portion of nature's influence, to watch over the growing wilds of the world.

  - [Norgannon](Norgannon), Lore-Keeper and Master-Magician**
  Granted the blue dragon [Malygos](Malygos) a portion of his vast power, to be the guardian of magic and hidden arcanum. 

  - [Khaz'goroth](Khaz'goroth), Shaper and Forger of the World**
  Bestowed some of his vast power upon the mighty black dragon [Neltharion](Neltharion), and was given dominion over the earth and the deep places of the world.

  - [Sargeras](Sargeras)**
  The Titans' greatest warrior, defender of their worlds. Also, one who left their ranks and became their greatest traitor.

  - [Aggramar](Aggramar)**
  Successor to Sargeras in the Pantheon after his fall and involvement with the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion).
