**Trolls** (collectively referred to as **trollkind**) are a diverse sapient race that can be found in every part of [Azeroth](Azeroth). They are one of the world's oldest known native races. Trolls are divided into many subgroups adapted to different environments, namely [dark trolls](Dark troll), [forest trolls](Forest troll), [ice trolls](Ice troll), [jungle trolls](Jungle troll), [sand trolls](Sand troll), and [Zandalari trolls](Zandalari troll).

Many trolls have no loyalty except amongst their own tribes, but some important groups have joined the [Horde](Horde): the [Darkspear tribe](Darkspear tribe), [Revantusk tribe](Revantusk tribe), and others remain independent like [Zandalari Empire](Zandalari Empire). Some forest trolls cling to their allegiance to the [Old Horde](Old Horde) and its [villainous offshoots](Dark Horde), many of them remains inside the [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain) with the [Dark Horde](Dark Horde).

### History
As tribal rivalries erupted throughout the former [Gurubashi Empire](Gurubashi Empire), the Darkspear tribe found themselves driven from their homeland in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). Having settled on the [Darkspear islands](Darkspear islands), which are believed today to have been a part of the [Broken Isles](Broken Isles), the tribe soon found themselves entangled in a conflict with a group of [murlocs](Murloc). Eventually they also found new enemies in [humans](Human) when a [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras)-fleet landed on the island in search for some orcs who had stolen some ships. The trolls' fate seemed sealed until the orcish [Warchief](Warchief) [Thrall](Thrall) and his band of newly freed orcs, which were the orcs the humans were looking for, took shelter on the island after a heavy storm. The orcs managed to defeat the humans, but their victory was short-lived. Controlled by a [Sea Witch](Zar'jira), the [Underworld Minions](Underworld Minions) captured the Darkspears' leader [Sen'jin](Sen'jin) along with Thrall and several orcs, trolls and humans. They were all brought to a complex prison made by caves below the island. Thrall managed to free himself and his companions, but was ultimately unable to save the trolls' leader. Although [Sen'jin](Sen'jin) was sacrificed to the Sea Witch, he was able to reveal a vision he had in which Thrall would lead the Darkspear trolls from the island.

After returning to the island's surface, Thrall and his followers managed to fend off further attacks by the Sea Witch and her murloc minions, and set sail for Kalimdor once again. Under the new leadership of [Vol'jin](Vol'jin), the Darkspear swore allegiance to Thrall's [Horde](Horde) and followed him to Kalimdor. Now considered enemies by all other troll organizations except the [Revantusk](Revantusk), [Shatterspear](Shatterspear), and the [Zandalari](Zandalari tribe), the Darkspear are held in contempt to this day. Yet, the Darkspear have not forgotten being driven from their ancestral homes, and this animosity is eagerly returned - especially towards the other jungle trolls. Having reached and founded the orc's new homeland, [Durotar](Durotar), the trolls carved out another home for themselves — this time among the [Echo Isles](Echo Isles) on the eastern shores of the new orc kingdom.

However, with another approach by Kul Tiras and its navy, the Darkspear were forced to retreat inland under the onslaught of the misguided commander [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore). The trolls, fighting alongside their Horde brethren led by the [beastmaster](beastmaster) [Rexxar](Rexxar), defeated the enemy and reclaimed their new home. Shortly thereafter, a witch doctor by the name of [Zalazane](Zalazane) began using dark magic to take the minds of his fellow Darkspear. As his army of mindless followers grew, Vol'jin ordered the free trolls to evacuate, and Zalazane took control of the Echo Isles. The Darkspear have since settled on the nearby shore, naming their new village after their old leader, Sen'jin. From [Sen'jin Village](Sen'jin Village) they, along with their allies, send forces to battle Zalazane and his enslaved army.

### Appearance
The appearance of Trolls varies depending on their type and tribe to which they belong:

**Jungle Trolls:** The most common currently, they usually live on islands, they are organized into tribes like any other type of trolls, of which the Blackspear Tribe formally belongs to the Horde, with bluish skin and elongated arms and legs with muscles for the males.

Troll females have a build more similar to the other races, however, the bluish skin with some warts on the skin remains.

Both genders have two protruding tusks, with those of the males being more prominent. Some use war paint and certain wings on their fangs or any other area of the face.

**Forest Trolls:** The second most common type of Troll to see, they tend to reside in forested areas, they have a long history of war with the High Elves and with the human race over the lands of Quel'thalas and Lordaeron that are now under the domain of the plague, with a somewhat different constitution and with an easily recognizable pigmentation on the skin, being green, but very similar to the Jungle trolls, previously the Amani Trolls belonged to the Old Horde, currently only the Revantusk tribe has joined her.

**Ice Trolls:** They are a type of trolls that reside in cold areas similar to their other congeners, they only differ in the light blue pigmentation of their skin, probably due to the icy climates in which they live. They can be easily seen in the most distant areas of Dun Morogh.

**Sand Trolls:** A type of Troll difficult to see in the Eastern Kingdoms, residing in the distant lands of Kalimdor, in Tanaris, they are an aggressive type of Troll, they will rarely be seen being friendly with others other than their own. tribe, pigmented with a color similar to the yellowish sand due to the hot climate of the desert and with customs even more primitive than the other types of Troll, often perform dark rituals in honor of their ancient gods in the depths of the ruins of Zul 'farrak.

**Zandalari Trolls:** A special tribe of Trolls and the most civilized of all, the Zandalari Trolls come from the Far Island of Zandalar, the last bastion of the once great Zandalari empire, they are currently in Stranglethorn Valley trying to stop the invocation to the current plane of the Blood God Hakkar by the Hakkari and their loyalists within Zul'gurub and the Temple of Atal'Hakkar.

**Dark Trolls:** The most difficult type of Troll to see in all of Azeroth, of currently unknown origin and scattered in small tribes throughout the world, their pigmentation is dark purple, they tend to practice arcane magic and witchcraft, there are those who say that they have some kind of relationship with the Night Elves.
