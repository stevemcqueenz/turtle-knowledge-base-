# Bluegill Marsh

[Bluegill Marsh](Bluegill Marsh) is located in the western [Wetlands](Wetlands), and is the site of several [Bluegill](Bluegill) [murloc](murloc) camps. Many murlocs make their home here led by the ferocious [Gobbler](Gobbler). Young travelers should tread carefully through the marsh because all the murlocs come in groups of two or three, and pulling one at a time is very tough. A few quests take you into Bluegill Marsh in hunt for murlocs or stolen goods.
