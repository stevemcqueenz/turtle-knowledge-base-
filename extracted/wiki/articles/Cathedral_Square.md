# Cathedral Square

-Cathedral Square** in [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) is so named for the mighty [Cathedral of Light](Cathedral of Light) which stands as the greatest monument to the [Holy Light](Holy Light) in [Azeroth](Azeroth (world)) and dominates this district. The cathedral, headed by [Archbishop Benedictus](Archbishop Benedictus), is the center of the religion of Light and is thus home to [paladin](paladin) and [priest](priest) trainers, as well as a [first aid](first aid) trainer.

It is without doubt one of the most upclass sections of the city and includes the residence of most of the city's finest merchants and nobility. It includes selected shops as well as the headquarters to powerful organizations such as the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn). It is also an important civic center, housing [City Hall](City Hall) and the [Orphanage](Orphanage). 

To get to Cathedral Square, you can head northeast from the [Park](Park), northwest from the [Trade District](Trade District), or southeast from the [Dwarven District](Dwarven District).
