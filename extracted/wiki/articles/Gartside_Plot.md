# Gartside Plot

The **Gartside Plot** is one of the many farms in [Westfall](Westfall), and the only one other than [Saldean's Farm](Saldean's Farm) that hasn't been taken over by the [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood). Though harvest golems roam the fields, [Maltimor Gartside](Maltimor Gartside) continues to eke out his existence in the solitary cottage and seeks to find an end to the killing machines that plague Westfall's fields.
