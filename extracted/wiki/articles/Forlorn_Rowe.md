# Forlorn Rowe

The **Forlorn Rowe** is located in northwestern [Duskwood](Duskwood), on the northern edge of [Raven Hill Cemetery](Raven Hill Cemetery). It is a hill cloaked in an ominous darkness, upon which the home of [Morbent Fel](Morbent Fel) rests, the powerful necromancer who rules the forest's undead creatures through evil arcane magic. [Alliance](Alliance)-aligned adventurers will need to do a lot of running and fighting in order to obtain the only [special weapon](Morbent's Bane) that could stop him.
