# Scalebane Ridge

| field | value |
|---|---|
| 1 | Kegdesh Foulmantle |
| icon | DarkIron Male |

-Scalebane Ridge** is a Dark Iron hunting camp located on the cliffs of the eastern [Badlands](Badlands) overlooking [Lethlor Ravine](Lethlor Ravine). Led by [Kegdesh Foulmantle](Kegdesh Foulmantle), these greedy whelp hunters and dragon skinners have carved a treacherous ridge, hunting drake trophies and boldly seeking profit for Shadowforge amid scorching cliffs.
