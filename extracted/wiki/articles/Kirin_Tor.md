# Kirin Tor

The **Kirin Tor**, also known as the **Wizard Council of Dalaran**, (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, [Human History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#A_New_Generation), A New Generation) or simply the **Council of Dalaran**, (ref: [quest: Minor Magical Fortifications]) (ref: [quest: Medium Magical Fortifications]) (ref: [quest: Major Magical Fortifications]) is the ruling conclave of [magi](Mage) (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*) (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, [Undead History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Kel'Thuzad_and_the_Cult_of_the_Damned), Kel'Thuzad and the Cult of the Damned) that leads the magical nation of [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)), one of the [seven human kingdoms](Seven Kingdoms). More specifically, they are governed by the [Council of Six](Council of Six). (ref:  The Kirin Tor were members of the original [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron), and most contemporary human and high elven mages bow before it.<ref>*Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, [Creatures of Lordaeron & Northrend](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Wizards), Wizards) The organization was also described as the most learned and puissant of the magical academies, guilds, and societies, advisors to the kings, teachers of the learned, and revealers of secrets. (ref: *[The Last Guardian](The Last Guardian)*)

Following the destruction of Dalaran in the Third War, the Council has authorized the accepting of donations of magical reagents from outside sources for usage in the reconstruction and other tasks.
