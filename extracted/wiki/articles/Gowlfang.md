-Gowlfang** is a [gnoll](gnoll) and is the first boss of [Dragonmaw Retreat](Dragonmaw Retreat). He was formerly a [quest giver](quest giver) located at the Mosshide's refuge in the [Wetlands](Wetlands). Once the pack leader of the Mosshide pack, he was exiled by [Sagepaw](Sagepaw) and left to rot in [Sparkwater Port](Sparkwater Port) in [Durotar](Durotar). (ref: [quest: Cursed Sagepaw!]) He later managed to travel back to the Wetlands, and was joined by fellow Mosshide that sought to help him become pack leader again. However, after Sagepaw was killed, Gowlfang betrayed those that had aided him and aligned himself with [Zuluhed the Whacked](Zuluhed the Whacked) and the [Dragonmaw clan](Dragonmaw clan), traveling with [Bogpaw Truthsay](Bogpaw Truthsay) to the [Dragonmaw Retreat](Dragonmaw Retreat) with his pack. (ref: [quest: Gowlfang's Defeat])
