King **Landan Wrynn** (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 14) was the father of [Barathen Wrynn](Barathen Wrynn) and king of the nation of [Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)) prior to the opening of the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal).

## Notes
- According to *Cycle of Hatred*, during his reign, his court conjurer [Nielas Aran](Nielas Aran) was seduced by [Aegwynn](Aegwynn), who nine months later returned to Landan's court with the future Guardian Medivh. However, this is in conflict with accounts from the *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans* and *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* game manuals in which the events take place in the court of his son. (ref: *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans* game manual, [Chronicles of the War in Azeroth](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft:_Orcs_%26_Humans_manual#559), 559) (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness*, [A Brief History of the Fall of Azeroth](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#A_Brief_History_of_the_Fall_of_Azeroth))
