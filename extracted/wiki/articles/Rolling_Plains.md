# Rolling Plains

-The Rolling Plains** cover much of southern [Mulgore](Mulgore),61 68 Mulgore (g/s/c) stretching from [Bloodhoof Village](Bloodhoof Village) to the [Barrens](Barrens) border. It is inhabited by many species of animal; [wolves](wolf), [kodo](kodo), [tallstrider](tallstrider)s and [cougar](cougar)s can all be found here. This is also the only place in all Mulgore where [centaur](centaur)s have been able to penetrate - their outriders have been known to wander the grassy fields. A nest of [Windfury harpies](Windfury harpies) can be found in the eastern part of the region.65 69 Mulgore (g/s/c)
