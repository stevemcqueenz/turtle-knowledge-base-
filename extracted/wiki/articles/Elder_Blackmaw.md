## Objective of
- Drops [Paw of Elder Blackmaw](Paw of Elder Blackmaw) for  [34D] [The Unwise Elders](The Unwise Elders).
- Drops [Groveweald Badge](Groveweald Badge) for  [33D] [The Rampant Groveweald](The Rampant Groveweald).
