# Whispering Gardens

The **Whispering Gardens** are located in northeastern [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), on the hill south of the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery). A great battle was fought here between the what would become the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) and a host of [Scourge](Scourge). The spirits of those fallen in battle can still be heard here as faint whispers in the twilight.
