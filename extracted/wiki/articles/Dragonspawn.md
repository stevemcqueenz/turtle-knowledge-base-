# Dragonspawn

The dragonflights of [Azeroth](Azeroth) are not without retainers and assistants. Noble humanoids serve the dragons with absolute loyalty, acting as footmen, soldiers, assistants, and companions. They are endowed with many of the dragon's mystic abilities and virtuous qualities.

The Dragonspawn are semi-humanoid dragon-like creature. They're somewhat similar in concept to a [Centaur](Centaur) - from the waist up, they're humanoid lizards. From the waist down, they have the body of a dragon. They walk on their four dragon legs, and use their arms to carry their weapons. They usually wear armor. The largest, most powerful Dragonspawn (ie: [General Drakkisath](General Drakkisath) in [UBRS](UBRS)) have wings as well, though most don't. They don't seem to be able to actually fly though.

Exactly how they came into being is a mystery that dragons do not choose to solve. Though they have dragon-like qualities, these creatures are not descended drom dragons. Instead, they are distant kinfolk, descended from humanoids who spent their lives so close to the dragons that they took on the qualities of their masters after generations of service. They are gifted with unique advantages of their dragon blood and heritage, including immunity to their dragonflight's breath weapon. They have the same alignment and goals as their patron dragonflight. (In short, five species, one for each flight, colors match- blue serve [Malygos](Malygos), Green serve Ysera, ect.)
