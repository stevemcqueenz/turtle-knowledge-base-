# Valormok

-Valormok** 22 55 Azshara (g/s/c) (aka **Camp Valormok**) is an [orc](orc)-controlled [Horde](Horde) outpost in [Azshara](Azshara). It is fairly unprotected and bare, as the Horde has little interest in this hostile territory. The warriors there experience difficult times, but are still hopeful. It was populated with many more orcs, but most were slain by the treacherous [blood elf](blood elf) [Magus Rimtori](Magus Rimtori).

It is interesting to note that any [Alliance](Alliance) player can go into Valormok without being attacked because there are no guards -- useful when going AFK.
