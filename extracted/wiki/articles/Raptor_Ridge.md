# Raptor Ridge

[Raptor Ridge](Raptor Ridge) is located in the eastern [Wetlands](Wetlands), just northwest of the dangerous [Dragonmaw Gates](Dragonmaw Gates). The raptors lair here in large numbers, and the ridge also features a cavern where the fierce [Razormaw Matriarch](Razormaw Matriarch) watches over her brood.
