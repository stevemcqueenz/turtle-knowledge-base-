# Grimesilt Dig Site

The **Grimesilt Dig Site** is a digsite once used by the [Ironforge dwarves](Ironforge dwarf) but was taken over and is currently still in the possession of the [Dark Iron dwarves](Dark Iron dwarf). One original worker at the site, [Dorius Stonetender](Dorius Stonetender), lies injured at the bottom of the pit, and another [crew member](Locheed) is trapped in a nearby outhouse.

The Grimesilt Dig Site is a dwarf [archaeology](archaeology) [dig site](dig site).
