**Shandris Feathermoon** is a [night elf](night elf) quest giver located in [Feathermoon Stronghold](Feathermoon Stronghold) in [Feralas](Feralas). She is the General of the Sentinel Army.

## Biography
### War of the Ancients
As an adolescent, Shandris lived in the village of [Ara-Hinam](Ara-Hinam). When the invading forces of the Burning Legion arrived and destroyed the settlement, her family was killed and Shandris fled south with the other survivors, chased by the ravening demons. Fortunately for the refugees, the pursuing Legion was intercepted by [Kur'talos Ravencrest](Kur'talos Ravencrest) and his combined night elf forces, who slaughtered the demonic army. The survivors of Ara-Hinam joined with the defenders. Shortly thereafter, at the defender's camp, a lonely and bitter Shandris was found by the priestess of the moon [Tyrande Whisperwind](Tyrande Whisperwind), who became fond of the young girl and couldn't bear to simply let her remain among the survivors alone. (ref: *[The Demon Soul](The Demon Soul)*, chapter 9)

Shandris soon started to idolize her savior and followed her everywhere she went, learning the arts of divine magic and healing from the goddess-touched Tyrande. She wanted more than anything to take part in the battles, but Tyrande forbade it, so she secretly followed the Sisters of Elune into battle, looking on from the shadows. Her secret tours were eventually discovered by Tyrande, who told Shandris to never again put herself in such danger. Though Shandris reluctantly agreed, (ref: *[The Demon Soul](The Demon Soul)*, chapter 17) before one of the most important battles in the war, she still grabbed her bow and ran after Tyrande after watching her ride away. (ref: *[The Demon Soul](The Demon Soul)*, chapter 18)

After Neltharion revealed his betrayal and slaughtered both defenders and demons, until he was forced to retreat by Korialstrasz, the scattered mortal defenders were put under heavy pressure as the Legion renewed its charge. In the chaotic battles near the forest's edge, Malfurion Stormrage found his beloved Tyrande at the mercy of one he had thought dead, Xavius. When the first of the satyrs was about to strike the death blow to the young druid, his body was rocked by a feathered arrow plunging into his shoulder. Hidden in the forest, Shandris had proved her unnatural talent with the bow. When the satyrs attempted to open up a portal to Zin-Azshari, as the fighting between night elves and demons had reached their location, several were impaled by Shandris' arrows, allowing Malfurion to confront Xavius unhindered. Focusing on the wood of Shandris' arrow impaled into Xavius' shoulder, Malfurion caused it to grow into a massive oak tree, consuming Xavius's flesh in the process. Still, the night elf druid failed to rescue his beloved Tyrande from the escaping demons, and Shandris was rescued by Korialstrasz from the massive downpour created by Malfurion's resulting grief, and hounded him to rescue the priestess. (ref: *[The Demon Soul](The Demon Soul)*, chapter 23)

With nothing stopping her from taking part in battles, Shandris clad in armor a bit too large for her, and joined with the Sisterhood of Elune. She followed the command of [Jarod Shadowsong](Jarod Shadowsong), though the sisters themselves were now led by Jarod's arrogant sister, [Maiev](Maiev Shadowsong). (ref: *[The Sundering](The Sundering)*, chapter 3) During the final battle, when the Kaldorei resistance met the vast army of the demon lord Archimonde, Shandris was in the middle of the fray. Along with the sisters, she dropped dozens of demons with lethally aimed arrows. (ref: *[The Sundering](The Sundering)*, chapter 20)

During the Great Sundering in 10,000 BDP, Shandris fled with the other night elves to the slopes of Mount Hyjal. As the chaos subsided, Shandris stood next to Jarod, ever close to Tyrande. (ref: *[The Sundering](The Sundering)*, chapter 21)

### The Long Vigil
For millennia, Shandris remained at the right hand of Tyrande during the Long Vigil, and eventually came to lead an elite group of sentinels called the [Shadowleaves](Shadowleaves).

### Third War
During the Third War, Shandris and her forces were stirred from their long vigil by [Grommash Hellscream](Grommash Hellscream) and the [Warsong](Warsong clan) orcs, who began to assault the forests of Ashenvale. During these battles, a vast army of fel orcs infused with the blood of Mannoroth managed to kill the demigod Cenarius after a long and arduous combat. Horrified, Shandris and the Shadowleaves retreated deeper into Ashenvale to warn the High Priestess Tyrande Whisperwind, who quickly surmised that the invaders must leave. Upon sensing a looming darkness, Shandris believed it to be the demonic orcs who killed Cenarius, but Tyrande was still unsure. (ref: [Enemies at the Gate (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Enemies_at_the_Gate_(WC3_NightElf))) While Tyrande took care of the human and orc problem, Shandris remained deeper in the foothills of Mount Hyjal, until her encampment came under heavy attack by the Scourge undead, which they barely repelled. When the high priestess arrived with the survivors of the demonic attack, she knew that the time had come to awaken the druids once more. (ref: [Daughters of the Moon (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Daughters_of_the_Moon_(WC3_NightElf)))

During the Battle of Mount Hyjal, Shandris bravely fought back the demons and undead until she saw a massive demonic force amassing at the base of the mountain. Just then, Tyrande and Malfurion Stormrage arrived with human and orcish allies at their back, and united as the Great Alliance, they formed a plan to dupe Archimonde into destroying himself. Shandris remained at the side of Tyrande on the front lines, leading her forces to defend Jaina Proudmoore and Thrall's encampments. When the fortifications fell, Shandris and Tyrande, along with the rest of the night elves, retreated to defend the peak of the mountain. When Archimonde himself came, Malfurion quickly cast a spell that teleported all night elves and ancients to the safety of the shadowy forest, while thousands of wisps swarmed up from the forest and destroyed the demon lord. (ref: [Twilight of the Gods (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_of_the_Gods_(WC3_NightElf)))

### World of Warcraft
In the aftermath of the Third War, Shandris had been promoted to the rank of general of the Sentinels, and now resides in the [Feathermoon Stronghold](Feathermoon Stronghold) located on [Sardor Isle](Sardor Isle) in [Feralas](Feralas), which is named after her. When Alliance adventurers met Shandris, she tasked them to investigate the [Hatecrest](Hatecrest) naga's increased presence in the [Ruins of Solarsal](Ruins of Solarsal), (ref: [quest: The Ruins of Solarsal]) and later listened to their report of a strange gazebo in the heart of Solarsal. (ref: [quest: Return to Feathermoon Stronghold]) While the heroes worked with her second, [Latronicus Moonspear](Latronicus Moonspear), to deal with the Hatecrest threat, the general remained behind to assess their findings. (ref: [quest: Against the Hatecrest]) Upon discovering evidence of a resurgent Silithid threat in the form of a Zukk'ash hive in Feralas, Shandris thanked the adventurers for their heroics on behalf of the stronghold, (ref: [quest: A Hero's Welcome]) then tasked them to deliver their findings to an associate of the Protectorate, [Gracina Spiritmight](Gracina Spiritmight). (ref: [quest: Rise of the Silithid])

### Mysteries of Azeroth
Due to insubordination in the Sentinels' ranks, Shandris banished [Commander Anashya Starfall](Commander Anashya Starfall) and her squad to [Thalanaar](Thalanaar). (ref: [Commander Anashya Starfall#Quotes](Commander Anashya Starfall#Quotes)) Following reports of strange events along the forgotten shores of [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast), Shandris sent an expedition under the command of [Sentinel Commander Silverstreak](Sentinel Commander Silverstreak) to investigate. (ref: [quest: Endless Vigil]) She was approached by Alliance adventurers on the behalf of [Sentinel Briariel](Sentinel Briariel), who was suspicious of the druids of [Talonbranch Glade](Talonbranch Glade) and discovered that they were researching how to obtain an extraordinary amount of [Morrowgrain](Morrowgrain) without raising any suspicion. However, as the Horde has been encroaching on kaldorei lands and with mutiny within the Sentinels, Shandris could not afford to be distracted by more internal disputes between the Sentinels and the Cenarion Circle, leaving the druids in Talonbranch free to conduct their research in peace. (ref: [quest: A Sentinel's Rightful Suspicion])
