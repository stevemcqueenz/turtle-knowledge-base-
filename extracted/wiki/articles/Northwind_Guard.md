The **Northwind Guard** (ref: [Old Hamilton#Quotes](Old Hamilton#Quotes)) is the military that guards [Northwind](Northwind). The [Northwind Guards](Northwind Guard (NPC)) are led by [Sir Nathaniel Amberwood](Sir Amberwood), elder brother to [Lord Percival Amberwood](Lord Amberwood), ruler of Northwind.

Due to machinations from Lady [Katrana Prestor](Katrana Prestor) and Sir Amberwood, what little forces the Northwind Guard had left were allocated to the western parts of Northwind, leaving the east vulnerable to invasion by the [Dark Horde](Dark Horde) and the [Dark Iron clan](Dark Iron clan). Locations such as [Lion's Watch](Lion's Watch), which watched over the [Merchant's Highroad](Merchant's Highroad), were left maintained only by local militia, (ref: [Guard Ellington#Quotes](Guard Ellington#Quotes)) whereas [Northridge Point](Northridge Point) and [Birkhaven](Ruins of Birkhaven) fell to the [Blackrock clan](Blackrock clan). The weakened military presence allowed for members of the [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood) to infiltrate Northwind and for members of the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) to kidnap the inhabitants of the region.
