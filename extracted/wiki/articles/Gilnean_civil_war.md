# Gilnean civil war

The **Gilnean civil war** (ref:  [45] [The Serpentroot](The Serpentroot)) was a civil war which occurred in the [Kingdom of Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)), opposing the forces loyal to the Gilnean monarchy against the [Ravenwood Rebellion](Ravenwood Rebellion) who supported Lord [Darius Ravenwood](Darius Ravenwood).
