# Imp

The first [pet](Pet) available to a [Warlock](Warlock). The next pet available is the [Voidwalker](Voidwalker).

A quest can be obtained from your [trainer](trainer) at level 1 to gain this pet.

A ranged [nuker](Nuker) pet, suitable for use in a [party](Party).  Has good damage, but no threat or damage mitigation (initially).

The Imp is the Warlock pet with the most [mana](mana), and the lowest [health](health).

The Imp is also the only Warlock pet that doesn't require a [Soul Shard](Soul Shard) to summon.

## Acquiring
The Warlock trainer in each race's starting area has a quest for you -- supposedly it's available at level 1, but you may well be level 2 or higher by the time you find your class trainer.  The quest itself requires level 3 or 4 to accomplish; you will have to fetch some item or items, probably killing some [mob](mob)s in the process, and return it to the trainer.  You'll receive the [Summon Imp](Summon Imp) spell.

## Imp Tips
A common mistake some warlocks make is to tell their Imp to attack when Firebolt isn't set to [auto-cast](auto-cast).  Since Firebolt is the Imp's only attack form, this gives it nothing to do; right-click on the Firebolt button to fix it.  An Imp will also not attack if it is out of mana; it will also refuse to attack [mob](mob)s that are [immune](immune) to [fire](fire).

When Phase Shifted, the Imp is not capable of attracting [threat](threat) or even attention from mobs.  It is not supposed to be capable of setting off traps, but this is debated (one point of contention is whether it can trigger the dragon eggs in the Rookery in Upper [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire)).

The [Improved Imp talent](Warlock Talents#Improved Imp) increases the damage done by Firebolt, the Stamina increase provided by Blood Pact, and the damage returned to attackers by Fire Shield.

A Warlock with [Dark Pact](Warlock Talents#Dark Pact) can set the Imp to [Passive](Passive) and use it as a mana "battery," since the Imp has so much mana and regenerates it quickly.  This allows the Warlock to move on quickly after battles, tapping Dark Pact a time or two while running.  This is not as efficient when using the Imp to attack, however, because Firebolt uses its mana quickly.  The Stamina buff from Blood Pact makes it worthwhile to have the Imp out in a party even if you don't have it attack.

If a Warlock has [Demonic Sacrifice](Warlock Talents#Demonic Sacrifice) or [Master Demonologist](Warlock Talents#Master Demonologist), the Imp's benefits under those talents (increased fire damage from spells and decreased threat from mobs) can be very useful in some situations.

The Imp's only method of attacking is through the use of its Firebolt technique. The Imp uses up lots of mana on this attack and won't use Firebolt again until it has the mana to. It takes the Imp 2 seconds to cast the Firebolt ability. It can be improved to 1.45 seconds through the [Improved Firebolt talent](Warlock Talents#Improved Firebolt) (rank 2 is supposed to bring it to 1 second but is currently bugged). Firebolt also can be [resisted](Resistance), which definitely hurts the Imp. However, the Imp can crit with its Firebolts for extra damage.

Increases the stamina of the Warlock, the Imp and any group members in range. The range is 20 yards, so be sure that the Imp is close by at all times. It does not cost any mana at all from the Imp to use Blood Pact, and there is no duration on the buff (although it disappears fairly quickly from anyone who moves out of range).

Blood Pact can be set to auto-cast; when it is, the Imp will cast Blood Pact automatically on all party members in range who don't already have it, including new people who join the party, party members who move into range from farther away, and party members who resurrect.

Makes the Imp invulnerable to all hostile effects, although beneficial abilities can be used on it.  The Imp cannot attack while in this state; if it attacks, it will automatically cancel Phase Shift.  It has a 10 second cooldown and does not cost the Imp any mana to use.

Phase Shift can be set to [auto-cast](auto-cast); when this is turned on, the Imp will automatically go into Phase Shift at the first opportunity when combat is over.

Places a damage shield on the warlock and any party members within range (30 yards); it does fire damage to any enemy who hits a protected person.  One thing to note is that, as with any fire damage, mobs can resist it, and it will not work on fire-immune mobs.  The duration is 3 minutes.

Fire Shield can be set to auto-cast; when this is set, the Imp will cast Fire Shield whenever the Warlock is in combat, if it isn't already up.

## Imp Trivia
The Imp is the only pet in the game that talks.  When asked to attack, it will sometimes complain about it in [Common](Common).  At other times, it says something indecipherable in [Demonic](Demonic).
