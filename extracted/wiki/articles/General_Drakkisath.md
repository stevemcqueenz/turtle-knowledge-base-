# General Drakkisath

General Drakkisath, ruler of the dragonspawn armies of [Nefarian](Nefarian), is a cruel, mighty warrior and is responsible for the death of thousands. He is a a clever fighter, well-trained and organized. He knowns how best to use his unit, and he knows the capacity of each individual in his army. Drakkisath is always guarded by two of his best warriors.

Like all dragonspawn, he uses his skills, powers, and equipment toward the same end: to protect the dragonflight of his patron and its fellow dragonspawn.

Drakkisath can be found in his throne room behind the Halls of Ascension in [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire).
