# Shrine of the Fallen Warrior

The **Shrine of the Fallen Warrior** is located on a small hill in the [Barrens](Barrens)[47, 29] west of the [Crossroads](Crossroads). To reach it, player must follow a ridge to the southwest and half-way up the hill will find the base of a mound. The short trail will lead directly to the shrine.

## Trivia
The Shrine of the Fallen Warrior is a memorial to [Michel Koiter](Michel Koiter), a [Blizzard](Blizzard Entertainment) employee who died during the development of *[World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft)*. The base of the stone obelisk is engraved with the initials "MK" and the orc is an exact copy of Koiter's beta character: "twincruiser".

If a player visits the memorial, they will find a [spirit healer](spirit healer) named  in front of the Shrine.
