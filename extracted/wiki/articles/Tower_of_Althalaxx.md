# Tower of Althalaxx

In the time of the [Great Sundering](Great Sundering), [Queen Azshara](Queen Azshara) brought her favored servitors, those of the [Highborne](Highborne), to the twin towns of [Ameth'Aran](Ameth'Aran) and [Bashal'Aran](Bashal'Aran). To govern these towns, Azshara placed [Athrikus Narassin](Athrikus Narassin), an elf of considerable magical power. Athrikus was at first loyal to his queen, but eventually he grew bored of his post, and would spend most of his days in the **Tower of Althalaxx**, some ways removed of the towns.

10,000 years later, a dark theory about the Tower of Althalaxx has proven true: A cult has gathered at the tower with many [warlock](warlock)s amongst their number, even including a foul [satyr](satyr). They call themselves the [Cult of the Dark Strand](Cult of the Dark Strand), and it may be possible that Athrikus is indeed their leader, although why they are assembling now is a dark mystery.
