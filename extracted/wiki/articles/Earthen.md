# Earthen

Earthen are stony proto-beings similar to [dwarves](dwarves) created by the [Titan](Titan)s to help shape the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth). They hibernated after the destruction of the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity) and transformed into dwarves upon awakening.  The history of the Earthen and their transformation into the race known as Dwarves can be found recorded in the [Lorekeeper of Norgannon](Lorekeeper of Norgannon) in [Uldaman](Uldaman).

During the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients (Alternate History)), they were convinced to join the Alliance against the Legion by the dragon-mage Krasus, even while being looked down upon by the Night Elves. It was a reluctant truce, as the Earthen were called in by Krasus, Rhonin and Malfurion in defiance of the xenophobic commander, [Desdel Stareye](Desdel Stareye). The Earthen were initally put to the outskirts of the host, but after Stareye's "tragic" demise, their warriors were put to their best use under the new commander, [Jarod Shadowsong](Jarod Shadowsong). 

When the world was sundered by the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity)'s implosion, the earthen were deeply affected. Reeling with the pain of the earth itself, the earthen lost much of their identity and sealed themselves within the stone chambers where they were first created, [Uldaman](Uldaman), [Uldum](Uldum) and [Ulduar](Ulduar), the Titan cities where the earthen first took shape and form. Buried deep beneath the world, the earthen rested in peace for nearly eight thousand years. 

Though it is unclear what awakened them, the earthen sealed within Uldaman eventually arose from their self-imposed slumber. These earthen found that they had changed significantly during their hibernation. Their rocky hides had softened and become smooth skin, and their powers over stone and earth had waned. They had become mortal creatures. 

Calling themselves dwarves (a name they adopted from the humans), the last of the earthen left the halls of Uldaman and ventured out into the waking world. 

Although the present earthen of Ironforge, etc. call themselves the dwarves, a small snippet in one of the War of the Ancients books implies that during the time of War of the Ancients the term dwarf is actually a derogatory term used by the Night Elves of that time to belittle what they considered an inferior race. However it's unclear why the Earthen eventually decided to adopt this rude terminology to name themselves.
[Brann Bronzebeard](Brann Bronzebeard) suggests in one of his books that when the Humans first met the Dwarves they just simply called them Dwarves. Since then the Dwarves have kept this name, although some Dwarves insist being named Earthen.
