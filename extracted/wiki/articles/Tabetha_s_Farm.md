# Tabetha's Farm

-Tabetha's Farm**45 57 Dustwallow Marsh (g/s/c) is a small shack with a garden located in the murkiest part of [the Quagmire](the Quagmire) in [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh). [Tabetha](Tabetha) herself is present in the building, along with her two apprentices, [Garion](Apprentice Garion) and [Morlann](Apprentice Morlann). She also has a dock to host those who navigate the waters of the swamp by riverboat. [Andello Porter](Andello Porter) of [SI:7](SI:7) can be found here.
