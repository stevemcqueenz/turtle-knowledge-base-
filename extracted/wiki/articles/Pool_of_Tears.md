# Pool of Tears

The **Pool of Tears** is a large lake surrounded by marshland on the eastern side of the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows). After the construction of the [Temple of Atal'Hakkar](Temple of Atal'Hakkar), [Ysera](Ysera) sunk the temple under the Pool's waters, where it still sits. Members of the [green dragonflight](green dragonflight) cover the area nearby, watching over the corrupted temple. 

Blood-thirsty [frenzies](Frenzy) inhabit the water. Remains of [Atal'ai](Atal'ai) ruins are scattered at the shore and underwater, where [Atal'ai Artifact](Atal'ai Artifact)s can be found.
