# Moonkin

A beastly humanoid native to [Darkshore](Darkshore).  Sometimes called an "owlbear", these large bipedal creatures have the size of an upright walking bear, but with bird-like features such as a beak, feathers, and flightless wings. They also drop [egg](egg)s of various sorts.  Related to the [Wildkin](Wildkin).
[Night elf](Night elf) [Druid](Druid)s must fight a moonkin named [Lunaclaw](Lunaclaw) to earn their [Bear Form](Bear Form), and if they choose the highest tier talent in the [Balance](Druid Talents#Balance) tree, druids can assume [Moonkin Form](Moonkin Form) themselves.
