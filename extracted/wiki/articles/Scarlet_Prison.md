# Scarlet Prison

The **Scarlet Prison** is the second area of the [Scarlet Monastery Graveyard](Scarlet Monastery Graveyard). The entry is found in the [Chamber of Atonement](Chamber of Atonement), and in the prison complex below the torture chambers lies [Duke Dreadmoore](Duke Dreadmoore), a harbinger of the Dread Citadel [Naxxramas](Naxxramas) waiting for the right moment to break free.
