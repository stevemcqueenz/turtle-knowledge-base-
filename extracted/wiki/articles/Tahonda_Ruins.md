# Tahonda Ruins

The **Tahonda Ruins** are a [tauren](tauren) village that long ago came to ruin and slipped beneath the sands of the [Shimmering Flats](Shimmering Flats).

Little to no information was known about this village. The ruins were inhabited by Saltstone [basilisk](basilisk)s, which might have been the reason why it was abandoned. Other evidence points to an attack by hostile forces: two sets of three parallel tears in the leather of one of the buildings.
