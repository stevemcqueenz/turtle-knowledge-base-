# Circle of Inner Binding

One of the [Circles of Binding](Circles of Binding) in [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). It lies in the southern part of the region, near [Stromgarde](Stromgarde), and is guarded by [earth elemental](earth elemental)s.

It is through this Circle's shard that [Myzrael](Myzrael) was able to contact mortal travelers.
