# Ashbringer

-Ashbringer**, Blade of the Scarlet Highlord, was an immensely powerful weapon created for [Alexandros Mograine](Alexandros Mograine), the Highlord of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade). Mograine also became known as the Ashbringer, for legend had it that the power of the blade incinerated his enemies before he took a swing. The blade was believed lost, but has been confirmed to still be in Mograine's possession even after his enslavement by the evil lich [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad), the master of [Naxxramas](Naxxramas). However, it has been [corrupted](Corrupted Ashbringer) due to the circumstances surrounding his death along with the fact that he and his sword behaved as one entity.

## Background
Ashbringer, the Blade of the Scarlet Highlord, is an object of great mystery and power. 

Several characters have mentioned the Ashbringer on occasion, but the only beings believed to know the current location of the Ashbringer are [Nat Pagle](Nat Pagle), Master Fisherman, and [Prince Tortheldrin](Prince Tortheldrin), the leader of the [Shen'dralar](Shen'dralar) within [Dire Maul](Dire Maul). Getting the full story from them has proven difficult, but Tortheldrin's followers claim that he may be willing to tell what he knows if a player were to defeat the dragon Nefarian and share the tale with him. Some have brought Nefarian's head to Tortheldrin, though he said nothing that lead to the Ashbringer.

The corrupted Ashbringer is currently held by [Highlord Mograine](Highlord Mograine) and is a drop of [The Four Horsemen](The Four Horsemen) in [Naxxramas](Naxxramas). The corrupted Ashbringer starts a quest in the Cathedral of the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery). Once the wearer of the Ashbringer enters the Cathedral, an event starts with the following words:

"Scarlet Commander Mograine yells: Bow down! Kneel before the Ashbringer! A new dawn approaches, brothers and sisters! Our message will be delivered to the filth of the new world through the chosen one." 

[High Inquisitor Fairbanks](High Inquisitor Fairbanks) offers the continuation of the quest of the Ashbringer by telling its full story: How Mograine rose to become Highlord, founder of the Scarlet Crusade and known under the title Ashbringer, because of his might in slaying the undead. The sword was crafted for him and became attuned to him. He died through the betrayal of his son, [Scarlet Commander Mograine](Scarlet Commander Mograine) to Kel'Thuzad. The scourge sent an army to defeat the Highlord, but the Highlord slew the scourge legion. After more legions were sent, Fairbanks fell in battle and woke only to see that "the lesser Mograine" had killed his father with the sword.

Because the sword is tied to his wielder and the tale, it has become corrupted when Mograine became a death knight. High Inquisitor Fairbanks states that it can only be restored by Mograine's second son, who stays in Outland.

It is believed that the restoration of the Ashbringer can only be done once the Expansion "The Burning Crusade" is out.

This item is not currently in game as posted on the World of Warcraft General forums, by the community manager Eyonix: "It's true that currently Ashbringer is not in the game, although one day in the future it will be, but not until after there is an awesome legendary item for casters. ;)"
