## Description
I am sure you are eager to take the battle to the castle at any cost as soon as possible. But for you to do so we must first find the key to open it. Inside the same cave I have sent you to hunt the demons summoned by the Stormreaver Clan you will also find one that has claimed leadership over the rest. She calls herself Lady Harelyss.

This demon holds a great importance, simply because–if my own minions speak the truth, she holds an item that will secure us the key to the castle. While I cannot have you return her here, since you would have to drag her through a battlefield, I will give you a soulshard–the minute you deal the final blow, you will capture her soul in it, simply by having it on you.

Make sure to retrieve the item we are indeed looking for, her demise is inevitable and capturing her soul is simply our insurance in case we need to gather more information.
