Viceroy **Eldin Sunstrider** is an elite blood elf located in [Felstrider Retreat](Felstrider Retreat) in the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands). A distant cousin to Prince [Kael'thas Sunstrider](Kael'thas Sunstrider), Eldin leads a group of blood elves that did not follow their prince to Northrend, and instead fortified the mountain stronghold of Felstrider Retreat. (ref: [quest: Felstrider Retreat]) When the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant) began to resettle the Highlands, Eldin tasked [Veloren Brightstar](Veloren Brightstar) with spying on the refugees in [Brinthilien](Brinthilien). (ref: [quest: A Letter from the Royalty])

[Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn) wants Eldin assassinated in order to remove a potential rival to the throne of Quel'Thalas.
