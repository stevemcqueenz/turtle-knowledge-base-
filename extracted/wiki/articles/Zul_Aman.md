# Zul'Aman

## History
The nation of the [Forest Trolls](Forest Trolls), once the core of the great [Amani Empire](Amani Empire). Zul'Aman is a great primordial forest; the main inhabitants are the [Forest Trolls](Forest Trolls), however several bands of [High Elves](High Elves) also fled into the forests after the fall of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas). Bands of Scourge are also present, pursuing the [High Elves](High Elves).

## Landmarks
Major landmarks in Zul'Aman include the capital of the [Forest Trolls](Forest Trolls) itself, a large pyramid called the Shrine of Ula-tek. There are also the [Maisara Hills](Maisara Hills), home of the [Witch Doctor](Witch Doctor)s of the Zul'Aman, and [Lake Abassi](Lake Abassi) on whose shores the [Shrine of Ula-tek](Shrine of Ula-tek) is built.
