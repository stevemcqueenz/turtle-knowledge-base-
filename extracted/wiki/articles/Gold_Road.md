# Gold Road

The **Gold Road** runs north-south from [Mor'shan Rampart](Mor'shan Rampart) to  a bridge connecting the northern and southern half of the [Barrens](Barrens). South of the bridge, the road was called the [Southern Gold Road](Southern Gold Road).

## World of Warcraft
Starting from north, you'll be at the [Mor'shan Rampart](Mor'shan Rampart) and the [Mor'shan Base Camp](Mor'shan Base Camp). Further south will be a road heading east towards [Durotar](Durotar). Then carry on going south along the road and arrive at [Crossroads](Crossroads) where you cross the East-West road ([Ratchet](Ratchet) to [Honor's Stand](Honor's Stand)). Carry on south and you'll pass two oasis' and [Wailing Caverns](Wailing Caverns). A little while later you'll come across a dried-up river which creates a "border" between North and South Barrens. Here also will be [Mankrik's Wife](Beaten Corpse), which is a quest target.

After going further down the road, you will find a turn-off which leads into [Mulgore](Mulgore) and before that [Camp Taurajo](Camp Taurajo). Further south will be a path to [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh). Keep going south and there'll be a dwarven hold named [Bael Modan](Bael Modan). After that is a pass and then the [Great Lift](Great Lift) and two other instances, [Razorfen Downs](Razorfen Downs) and [Razorfen Kraul](Razorfen Kraul).
