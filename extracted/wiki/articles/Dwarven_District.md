# Dwarven District

The **Dwarven District** lies in the northern corner [Stormwind City](Stormwind City). Here, the dwarves of Ironforge have established an enclave. The forges in the district produce a constant haze, supplemented by the constant strokes of smiths' hammers. [Hunter](Hunter)s will find a place to stay within the Hunter's Guild, located in this area. [Profession](Profession) trainers for [mining](mining), [blacksmithing](blacksmithing), and [engineering](engineering), and corresponding [forge](forge)s and [anvil](anvil)s can also be found here. The Stormwind City end of the [Deeprun Tram](Deeprun Tram) line is accessible through a tunnel on the east side of the district.

This district is robust and heavily populated, usually with large houses heavily modified for dwarf use with additional floors and rooms dividing the original, overly large and wasteful, human designs. It is a center of commerce and industry, and the local factories work all night to supply Stormwind with all of it's goods. Almost 30 thousand dwarves reside in this district, more than in their capital city of Ironforge.

## Getting there
To get to the Dwarven District, you can head northeast from [Cathedral Square](Cathedral Square), northwest from [Old Town](Old Town), west from [Stormwind Keep](Stormwind Keep), or get off the [Deeprun Tram](Deeprun Tram) at the Stormwind City station.
