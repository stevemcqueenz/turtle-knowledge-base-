**Mathias and the Defias** is a [rogue](rogue) [quest](quest) available at level 20.  The quest is began by talking to [Mathias Shaw](Mathias Shaw) in [Stormwind](Stormwind).

## Objective
Travel to Westfall and find [Agent Kearnen](Amber Kearnen) for your debriefing.

Agent Kearnen is well hidden just to the north of the defias tower and under a small tree.

## Description
Not a moment too soon, <name>. [SI:7](SI:7) reconnaissance is reporting unusually heavy Defias activity in [Westfall](Westfall).

It would seem that the Defias have taken over an abandoned [Alliance watch tower](Mortwake's Tower) southeast of [Sentinel Hill](Sentinel Hill) and are using it as a base of operations to smuggle in goods from the [Redridge](Redridge) supply route.

Get to Westfall and contact Agent Kearnen for your debriefing. You will find her hidden near the tower.

...and <name>, should you be discovered, SI:7 will disavow all knowledge of your existence.
