# Second War

Following the [First War](First War), six years pass. In this span:

King [Terenas](Terenas) of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) offers the refugees sanctuary and convenes an emergency council to discuss the invasion. Together, [Lothar](Lothar) and Terenas convince the others to form an alliance between the seven human nations and the kingdoms of the dwarves and high elves.

The [Horde](Horde) brings ogre allies through the portal from [Draenor](Draenor) and replenishes its depleted ranks. Afraid for the survival of his people, the [troll](troll) [Zul’jin](Zul’jin) coordinates an alliance between the forest trolls and the orcish Horde.

Sir [Uther](Uther) Lightbringer and Alonsus Faol founds an order of holy defenders known as the [Knights of the Silver Hand](Knights of the Silver Hand). Their purpose is to help the populace deal with the trials of war and to be a shining banner of the Holy Light in battle.

The Second War begins.

The Horde pushes north, rolling through the continent of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) and up into Lordaeron. The conflict is widespread and bloody, with large portions of [Quel’Thalas](Quel’Thalas) destroyed by the Horde. The Alliance makes a desperate stand and stalls the Horde’s advance. The combined forces of [human](human)s, [high elves](high elves) and [dwarves](dwarves) manage to rally and push the Horde back to the ruins of [Stormwind Keep](Stormwind Keep).

[Gul'dan](Gul'dan), consumed by his thirst for personal power, secretly forges an agreement with a group of [demon](demon)s loyal to [Sargeras](Sargeras). They agree to help Gul’dan locate their master’s tomb.

Gul’dan searches for the [Tomb of Sargeras](Tomb of Sargeras) and raises it from deep beneath the ocean. When Gul’dan enters the burial chamber, however, he accidentally releases a swarm of demons that kill him. The demons surge out of the Tomb of Sargeras and inflict great damage on the forces of the Horde, allowing the Alliance to make a push against the Blackrock Spire. 

[Deathwing](Deathwing), an ancient and powerful black [dragon](dragon), assumes the form of [Lord Prestor](Lord Prestor) — a young human diplomat — and infiltrates the high council of the [Alliance](Alliance). Using subtle magic, the dragon ensnares the minds of the human leaders and forces them to grant him the title of king of Gilneas, though he is defeated and thought killed during a battle on Draenor.

The [Alliance](Alliance) pushes the weakened and disorganized Horde all the way back to the [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire), the capitol of the Horde in Azeroth. Lord [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar) is slain during the Alliance’s final victory.

The Dark Portal is shattered after the Blackrock Spire is destroyed and Orcish rule of Azeroth has ended. The Second War ends, resulting in a close victory for the Alliance.
