**Cho'gall**, Initiate of the Fifth Circle of the [Shadow Council](Shadow Council), is a two-headed ogre and the first of the Ogre-Magi, and chieftain of the [Twilight's Hammer clan](Twilight's Hammer clan). (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, [Legends of the Land](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Cho'gall), Cho'gall) He was one of the most feared and respected members of the Horde during the [First](First War) and [Second War](Second War)s, having studied magic under [Gul'dan](Gul'dan) himself. Though Cho'gall was extremely powerful, he was also dangerously insane. Less of a tribe and more of a religious cult, the Twilight's Hammer was devoted to bringing about the apocalypse. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Clans of the Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Twilight's_Hammer_Clan)*, Twilight's Hammer Clan)

After entering into Azeroth alongside [Kilrogg Deadeye](Kilrogg Deadeye) of the [Bleeding Hollow](Bleeding Hollow clan), Cho'gall led the Twilight's Hammer in the failed first siege of [Stormwind Keep](Stormwind Keep). However, the catastrophic failure of the attack threatened to tear the Horde apart, as Cho'gall and Kilrogg blamed each other's incompetence for the failure and the orcs quickly polarized into factions that supported either chieftain. Gul'dan quickly stepped in to quell the problem, installing [Blackhand](Blackhand) of the [Blackrock clan](Blackrock clan) as Warchief of the Horde to restore order to the Horde. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[The History of Orcish Ascension](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#The_Mastery_of_Forces_-_Medivh_and_Blackhand)*, The Mastery of Forces - Medivh and Blackhand)

At some point during the Second War, Cho'gall personally went to inspect the refineries at [Grim Batol](Grim Batol). Cho'gall and his convoy were traveling through the [Badlands](Badlands) of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan), and an ambush by [Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)) warriors was expected. [Doomhammer](Orgrim Doomhammer) sent additional warriors to protect the ogre mage. (ref: [The Badlands (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Badlands_(WC2_Orc))) The mission was a success and Cho'gall reported that the Khaz Modan refineries were well maintained and fulfilling their quotas. (ref: [The Fall of Stromgarde (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fall_of_Stromgarde_(WC2_Orc)))

Cho'gall and the Twilight's Hammer followed Gul'dan and the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) when the warlock went to discover the [Tomb of Sargeras](Tomb of Sargeras). Cho'gall did not enter the tomb himself, instead defending Gul'dan at the Tomb from Orgrim Doomhammer and his forces, who had been sent to destroy the traitor clans that had abandoned the war on the Alliance. (ref: [The Tomb of Sargeras (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Tomb_of_Sargeras_(WC2_Orc))) (ref: [Gul'dan and the Betrayal](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Gul'dan_and_the_Betrayal)) Though Gul'dan perished within the tomb, Cho'gall survived, leading his clan west to [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) into the sands of [Silithus](Silithus). (ref: [quest: Storm, Twilight and Hammer]) He later sent his most treasured pupil, [Ighal'for](Ighal'for), to [Balor](Balor) to oversee the summoning of the abhorrent beholder [Mergothid](Mergothid) into [Azeroth](Azeroth). (ref: [quest: Artifact of the Dark Lady])
