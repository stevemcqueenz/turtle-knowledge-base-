# Temple of Shadows

The **Temple of Shadows** is an area located deep within [Stormwrought Descent](Stormwrought Descent) in [Stormwrought Ruins](Stormwrought Ruins). It is here that [Ighal'for](Ighal'for) seeks to summon the beholder [Mergothid](Mergothid) to [Azeroth](Azeroth) and usher in a new uprising.
