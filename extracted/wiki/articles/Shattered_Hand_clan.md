The **Shattered Hand clan** is an orcish clan named as such for the practice of self-mutilation that every grunt in the clan performs.

## History
The Shattered Hand had stayed in Draenor when the orcs first crossed the Dark Portal and were spared the defeat that ended the Second War. Under Kargath, the Shattered Hand and the [Thunderlord clan](Thunderlord clan) lent their forces to [Dentarg](Dentarg) to reclaim the [Skull of Gul'dan](Skull of Gul'dan) from a captain of the [Bonechewer clan](Bonechewer clan). (ref: [The Skull of Gul'dan (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Skull_of_Gul%27dan_(WC2_Orc))) Kargath later led a force of Shattered Hand orcs into Azeroth. (ref: [Beyond the Dark Portal (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Beyond_the_Dark_Portal_(WC2_Human))) Kargath's Shattered Hand army defended the Azerothian side of the Dark Portal during the last days of the Invasion of Draenor, and they were left stranded with their chieftain after Ner'zhul abandoned them and the Portal was destroyed by the Alliance. (ref: [The Dark Portal (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dark_Portal_(WC2_Orc)))

The Shattered Hand's status during the interim period between the destruction of the Dark Portal and the fall of the internment camps is unknown; of Kargath's contemporaries, [Kilrogg](Kilrogg Deadeye) of the [Bleeding Hollow](Bleeding Hollow clan) was captured along with his clan, while [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream) of the [Warsong clan](Warsong clan) continued to evade the Alliance. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* Game Manual, *[Orcish History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Day_of_the_Dragon)*, Day of the Dragon) Supported by many of the veteran chieftains, [Thrall](Thrall) ultimately succeeded in revitalizing the Horde and giving his people a new spiritual identity. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* Game Manual, *[Orcish History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Thrall's_Tale)*, Thrall's Tale)

The Shattered Hand still exists, making their home in the [Cleft of Shadow](Cleft of Shadow), located in the orcish capital of [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar). The Shattered Hand makes a living as the Rogues Guild of Orgrimmar, now accepting various other races into their ranks, including jungle trolls and goblins. Based out of the [Shadowswift Brotherhood](Shadowswift Brotherhood) in Orgrimmar's Cleft of Shadow, they are responsible for training aspiring rogues from various races in the ways of stealth, deception, and the brewing of deadly poisons. (ref:   [16] [To Orgrimmar!](To Orgrimmar!)) (ref:   [16] [Find the Shattered Hand](Find the Shattered Hand)) Thrall acknowledged that the Hand was necessary to ensure orc and troll safety. (ref: [Jes'rimon#Quotes](Jes'rimon#Quotes)) "The great Shenthul" appeared to be considered a high-ranking member of the organization. The Forsaken Deathstalkers have allies within the Shattered Hand.

The Shattered Hand was able to place spies throughout the upper citadel of [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain). Though the information gathering process was slow, they managed to discover that the influence of the [black dragonflight](black dragonflight) had extended beyond the [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes) and into the [kingdom of Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)) itself after [Onyxia](Onyxia) infiltrated the Stormwind court.

## Notes
- Both the Warcraft III Game Manual and the in-game [The New Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_New_Horde) book mention that "many of the veteran chieftains" supported [Thrall](Thrall). The only chieftains who fit this description are [Kilrogg](Kilrogg Deadeye) of the [Bleeding Hollow clan](Bleeding Hollow clan) and [Kargath](Kargath Bladefist) of the Shattered Hand clan. The other clan chieftains such as [Zuluhed the Whacked](Zuluhed the Whacked) and [Mogor](Mogor) were either in clans that opposed the Horde at the time or were on Draenor.
