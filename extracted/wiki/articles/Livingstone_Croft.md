# Livingstone Croft

-Livingstone Croft** is a farm located in the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), to the west of [Stromgarde Keep](Stromgarde Keep). It is the home of the [Livingstone](Livingstone) family, which is looking for adventurers to keep the farm safe and operational.
