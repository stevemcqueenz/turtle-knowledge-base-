# Kargath Expeditionary Force

The **Kargath Expeditionary Force** (**K.E.F.** (ref: [quest: Operative Bijou]) (ref: [quest: Bijou's Belongings (Horde)]) (ref: [quest: Bijou's Reconnaissance Report])) is a military exploration group based out of the outpost of [Kargath](Kargath) in the [Badlands](Badlands) of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan). Sent by order of [Thrall](Thrall) himself — both to reinforce Kargath and to explore the arid region it was located in — the K.E.F. has made excursions through the region, including into the depths of [Uldaman](Uldaman), to discover why it was of more than a passing interest to the [dwarves](dwarves). They also dug a pass through to the [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge) to reach [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain) (the intent being to confront the false Warchief [Rend Blackhand](Rend Blackhand)) but inadvertently set loose the [Dark Iron dwarves](Dark Iron dwarf). 

[Warlord Goretooth](Warlord Goretooth) sends adventurers to annihilate [General Angerforge](General Angerforge)'s men because they are ruthless killers, responsible for the mass murder of the 109th division of the Kargath Expeditionary Force. (ref: [Quest:KILL ON SIGHT: Dark Iron Dwarves](Quest:KILL ON SIGHT: Dark Iron Dwarves))
