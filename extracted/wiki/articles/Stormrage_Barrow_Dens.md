# Stormrage Barrow Dens

The **Stormrage Barrow Dens** are the sacred [Barrow Den](Barrow Den)s of [Moonglade](Moonglade) where many of the [Night Elf](Night Elf) [Druid](Druid)s slumber in the [Emerald Dream](Emerald Dream).  The great druid [Malfurion Stormrage](Malfurion Stormrage), for whom the Barrow Dens are named, slumbers deep within the dens.  Tragically, sometime after the [Third War](Third War), Malfurion became lost somewhere within the Emerald Dream. 

The north-eastern corner of Moonglade holds the Barrow Dens. This area is sacred to the druids and was also formerly the home of the late demigod Cenarius. The Barrow Dens are an ancient underground fortress, with passages and caves containing the living and working chambers for the [Druids of the Wild](Druids of the Wild). The dome in the center of the hill holds [Cenarius' Lair](Cenarius' Lair), now a shrine to the valiant demigod.

During the Third War, Malfurion was awakened by [Tyrande](Tyrande) to help fight [Archimonde](Archimonde)'s forces.  He was sleeping in his Barrow Den.  He was almost destroyed by the [Dreadlord](Nathrezim) [Tichondrius](Tichondrius)' forces.

Directly north of the Barrow Dens are [Minions of Omen](Minion of Omen), high level wolf-like creatures.  They only come out at night during the Lunar Festival.

[Clintar Dreamwalker](Clintar Dreamwalker) now sleeps here.
