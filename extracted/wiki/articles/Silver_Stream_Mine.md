# Silver Stream Mine

[Silver Stream Mine](Silver Stream Mine) is located in the northern part of [Loch Modan](Loch Modan), east of [Algaz Station](Algaz Station) and southwest of the [Stonewrought Dam](Stonewrought Dam). Although it traditionally has been a bountiful source of [silver](silver) for the [dwarves](dwarves) of [Ironforge](Ironforge), it has recently been completely taken over by aggressive [kobold](kobold)s who seek the mine's resources for themselves.
