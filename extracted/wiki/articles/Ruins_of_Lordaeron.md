# Ruins of Lordaeron

The [Ruins of Lordaeron](Ruins of Lordaeron) is all that remains of the [City of Lordaeron](City of Lordaeron), the great Capital City of the [Alliance](Alliance).  Now it serves as nothing more than a sad reminder of power of the [Scourge](Scourge).  The area is literally haunted by the final tragic events that led to the death of [King Terenas](King Terenas) and the city's destruction. 

To this day you can still hear the moments before [King Terenas](King Terenas)' death; hushed whispers in the courtyard, the clanging of bells in the entryway, the faint cheer echoing the welcome of Prince [Arthas](Arthas), and the murmur of voices in the throne room - [Medivh](Medivh) warning of the coming of the Scourge, Arthas' return to Lordaeron, and then his slaying of his father, King Terenas. If you can Detect Invisibility, you will see the ghosts of the citizens of Lordaeron still haunting the castle's courtyard. Beneath the ruins now lies the [Forsaken](Forsaken) capital [city](city) - the [Undercity](Undercity).

The lands outside the ruins are no longer full of cheerful people enjoying the sun, but with demons such as [Darkhounds](Darkhounds) and [Duskbats](Duskbats) and the Scourge lurking nearby at the borders of the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands).
