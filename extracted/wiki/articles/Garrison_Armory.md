# Garrison Armory

The **Garrison Armory** is a vital [Alliance](Alliance) mining outpost near the [Nethergarde Keep](Nethergarde Keep) in the [Blasted Lands](Blasted Lands). Here, Alliance miners toiled to gather precious resources and minerals for the Nethergarde Keep. These resources are frequently shipped to Stormwind in exchange for the other vital resources Nethergarde Keep depends upon to survive. However, the mine has recently been overrun with an infestation of troggs in an incident that has claimed the lives of at least twelve workers, (ref:  [48] [The Garrison Armory Disaster](The Garrison Armory Disaster)) forcing [Foreman Tanoth](Foreman Tanoth) and his miners to evacuate the tunnels.
