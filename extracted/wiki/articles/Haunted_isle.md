# Haunted isle

The **haunted isle** (ref: [quest: The Haunted Isle]) is a medium-sized island near the coast of northern [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). Located a short distance south of [Yojamba Isle](Yojamba Isle) and northwest of the [Vile Reef](Vile Reef). The haunted isle is not a proper subzone and is only tentatively called such by [Baron Revilgaz](Baron Revilgaz).

The isle is infested with [Lesser Water Elemental](Lesser Water Elemental)s that were summoned by a [goblin](goblin) wizard named [Gazban](Gazban), who currently wanders the island. He constantly fades and appears from the physical world, using [the Stone of the Tides](the Stone of the Tides) to manipulate the elementals. The isle has little places that are safe from the water elementals. They are scattered on the flat, sandy part of the island and on the hill on the eastern side of it.
