# Fargodeep Mine

The **Fargodeep Mine** is an abandoned [gold mine](gold mine) located in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), which has been overrun by a [clan of kobolds](Elwynn kobolds).

## Inhabitants
- - (rare)
- - ## Notes
- It is a great place to work on professions and collect some much sought after raw materials. Players will probably be around level five or six when first entering the mine, but after completing all the associated quests, they should be around level nine. It usually takes one to one and a half hours to complete all of them. A handy tip is to make certain to carry as many bags as possible because they will fill quickly with loot. Not only will players be tasked with exploring the mine, but they will also gather large candles and gold dust from the kobolds.
- In addition to the mine quests, the ["Lost Necklace"](Stonefield Necklace quest chain) quest chain should be worked on at the same time, as it covers the same area. This is begun by talking with ["Auntie" Bernice Stonefield]("Auntie" Bernice Stonefield) located at the [Stonefield Farm](Stonefield Farm).The chain will culminate in a fight with [Goldtooth](Goldtooth), a rather large kobold located deep within the Fargodeep Mine.
- While running through the mine, players should watch for the kobold, [Narg the Taskmaster](Narg the Taskmaster). He is a level 10 rare mob who may spawn in the back of the mine.
