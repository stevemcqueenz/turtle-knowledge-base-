The **Remains of the Innocent** are a boss in [Stormwrought Ruins](Stormwrought Ruins). It is an amalgamation of the liquified remains of the people of [Northwind](Northwind) abducted by the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan).

## Abilities
- Diseased Slime
- Noxious Cloud

## Objective of
- [quest: Innocence Lost]

## Loot
- [Tiny Bracelet](Tiny Bracelet)
- [Bloodied Almanac](Bloodied Almanac)
- [Northwind Guard Breeches](Northwind Guard Breeches)
- [Dissolved Hookblade](Dissolved Hookblade)

## Notes
In order to open the gate leading to the Remains of the Innocent, four Chamber Levers must be pulled. Their locations are:
# To the left immediately after entering the Sanctum of Blood, in the dead end with two [Blooddrained Carcass](Blooddrained Carcass)es.
# In the last alcove on the upper floor, up the right ramp.
# Behind a pillar in the room preceding [Lady Drazare](Lady Drazare)'s boss room.
# In the back of the last left-most alcove in Lady Drazare's boss room.
