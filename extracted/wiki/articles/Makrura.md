# Makrura

The **Makrura** are a race of [humanoid](humanoid) lobsters that live primarily in the sea, but are capable of surviving on land for short periods of time. They are most commonly found on the coastlines of [Durotar](Durotar), [The Barrens](The Barrens), [Desolace](Desolace), and [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh). Some can also be found in the waters surrounding Sardor Isle in [Feralas](Feralas).

The term "Makrura" may just be a tribe name, but is generally used to denote the entire race.

Makrura do not appear especially intelligent.  They have no apparent form of speech, nor do they use any form of magic.  They are also incredibly hostile and will attempt to kill anybody that comes near them.  They are often found underwater guarding shipwrecks.

A texture model exists within the World of Warcraft file structure that depicts a Lobstrok, a creature similar to a Makrura. This creature does not exist in-game and may be the original name that was changed in favor of "Makrura," or it may also be a boss or tribal leader that may appear in the future.
