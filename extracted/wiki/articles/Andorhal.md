# Andorhal

-Andorhal** is formerly one of the largest towns in northern [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). It was the distribution center for all agricultural supplies in the northern provinces of Lordaeron east of [Lordamere Lake](Lordamere Lake), before the [Third War](Third War). It survived the initial outbreak of the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath). [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) was initially defeated by [Arthas](Arthas) here in Andorhal. However, Andorhal fell at the hands of Arthas when he return here to claim the remains of [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) for reanimation. 

Later, following the defeat of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) and the consolidation of the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands) by the Scourge, Andorhal became a regional bastion effectively separating the lands of the [Lich King](Lich King) from those still under mortal control. Later Andorhal became the headquarters of the lich [Araj the Summoner](Araj the Summoner) who used the ruined city as the base of operations from which he oversaw the use of the mighty plague cauldrons situated throughout the Western Plaguelands.

The roads leading north out of Andorhal are occasionally patrolled by forces of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) - either survivors of Andorhal or troops sent from nearby [Hearthglen](Hearthglen).
