Highlord **Taelan Fordring** was a [human](human) [paladin](paladin) found in [Hearthglen](Hearthglen) in the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands). He was the son of [Karandra](Karandra Fordring) and [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring), who he succeeded as lord of [Mardenholde Keep](Mardenholde Keep) after his [excommunication](excommunication). A former member of the [Order of the Silver Hand](Order of the Silver Hand), Taelan became a high-ranking member of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) after the [Third War](Third War), though he remained unaware of the true scale of the atrocities committed by the organization. Years later, his father eventually helped him see the truth, and though Taelan was killed by [Grand Inquisitor Isillien](Grand Inquisitor Isillien) for his "betrayal", his death motivated Tirion to reorganize the Silver Hand in [Lordaeron](Lordaeron).

## History
### Of Blood and Honor
On his 7th birthday,<ref name="OFM">[quest: Of Forgotten Memories]) Taelan received a toy hammer from his father. When he was young, he and his parents would often visit [Caer Darrow](Caer Darrow). Once, [Artist Renfray](Artist Renfray) painted a family portrait. (ref: [quest: Of Love and Family])

He was overjoyed when his father woke from his slumber after his fight with the orc [Eitrigg](Eitrigg). After [Tirion](Tirion Fordring) sought out the [orc](orc) hermit Eitrigg and conversed with him, he was convinced the orc knew honor, but his wife [Karandra](Karandra Fordring) disagreed. (ref: *[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*, chapter 2) The two then got into a fight when Tirion told their son Taelan that not all orcs were bad, and that the good ones were just harder to find. Karandra, however, was very stuck in the idea that orcs were evil and should all be killed, a very traditional and common view. (ref: *[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*, chapter 3) When Tirion was eventually [excommunicated](Excommunication) and exiled for attacking [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron) soldiers and refusing to break the promise he made to the orc, Karandra didn't go with him for Taelan's sake. (ref: *[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*, chapter 6)

Karandra told their son that Tirion had died, and took him to a false grave at the [Undercroft](Undercroft), where Taelan buried the toy warhammer his father had given him, and the memory of him, forever. (ref: 

Fifteen years later, Taelan became a paladin and, during this time frame, was noted to have become good friends with [Arden](Arden).<ref>*[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*, chapter 8)

### World of Warcraft
At some point, he fought the [Scourge](Scourge) in [Northdale](Northdale), and it was there that he renounced the [Order of the Silver Hand](Order of the Silver Hand). (ref: [quest: Of Lost Honor]) Taelan later became Highlord of the Scarlet Crusade after the death of [Highlord Mograine](Highlord Mograine).

Eventually, with the help of adventurers sent by his father, Taelan recognized the corruption that had befallen the Scarlet Crusade and defected. While escaping from Hearthglen, however, he was killed by [Grand Inquisitor Isillien](Grand Inquisitor Isillien) for his betrayal. [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring) was waiting nearby for his son and saw him cut down and, overcome with loss and outrage, avenged Taelan's death by killing Isillien. Grieving over his son's body, Tirion resolved to reform the Silver Hand to once more be a force of good in the world.

## Notes and trivia
- Aside from the quests, Highlord Taelan Fordring can also be killed for loot, although he had no unique drops.
- Taelan's age seems to have undergone a retcon, given that he is only five at the time of *[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*, yet an epilogue shows him at the age of 20 being inducted into the Knights of the Silver Hand. However, given that less than a decade passes between *Of Blood and Honor* and *World of Warcraft*, exactly how Taelan could have aged 15 years in this time is a mystery. This is further compounded by Tirion stating in a quest line that he gave Taelan the toy hammer when he was seven, the circumstances of which indicate that Taelan was aware that his father was still alive.
