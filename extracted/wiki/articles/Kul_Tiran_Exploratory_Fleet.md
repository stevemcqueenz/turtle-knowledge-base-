The **Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet** is a group of Kul Tiran sailors and marines stranded on [Lapidis Isle](Lapidis Isle). Remnants of Admiral Daelin Proudmoore's exploratory fleet, these few survivors that have not been killed by the Horde eke out a dangerous existence.
