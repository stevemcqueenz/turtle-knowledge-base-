# Moro'gai tribe

The **Moro'gai tribe** is a tribe of [Draenei](Draenei) from across the Dark Portal that has made a new home in the [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast).

## Notes and trivia
- The Moro'gai were originally an unused faction of draenei that existed in the *World of Warcraft* beta but was never implemented into the game.
- Their original description stated that they were a "faction of Draenei from across the Dark Portal is trying to make a new home in the [Swamps of Sorrow](Swamps of Sorrow) while beset by strife and the madness of their kin". Presumably, this depiction was made defunct, as the two draenei tribes that live in the Swamp of Sorrows are the [Tribe of Sanv](Tribe of Sanv) and the [Lost Ones](Lost Ones).
- Although the faction had originally been announced as the **Moro'gai** in the notes for [patch 1.16.0](patch 1.16.0), no content was ever developed for them and no NPCs were ever attributed to the faction. The tribe itself would later be added in [patch 1.18.1](patch 1.18.1) in the [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast) as part of the [Draenei Exiles](Draenei Exiles) faction.
