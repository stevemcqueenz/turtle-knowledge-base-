# Slag Pit

-The Slag Pit** lies deep within the [Cauldron](Cauldron) excavation site in [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge). It is run by cruel [Dark Iron dwarves](Dark Iron clan) and worked by their slave laborers. Within the depths of the Slag Pit there is another danger, ravenous [Incendosaur](Incendosaur)s. The Dark Irons are extremely hostile to any who would interfere with their [mining](mine) operations.

Note: although the [Slave Worker](Slave Worker)s initially show up as yellow mobs, they will turn hostile if you attack a [Dark Iron Slaver](Dark Iron Slaver) or [Dark Iron Taskmaster](Dark Iron Taskmaster) within their aggro radius.
