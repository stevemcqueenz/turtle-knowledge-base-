**Nert Blastentom** is the Grand Taskmaster of the [Durotar Labor Union](Durotar Labor Union).

## Biography
Nert once worked as part of the Venture Company's brute squads, later becoming a foreman as he grew older.

### Mysteries of Azeroth
As foreman of the Venture Company, he was tasked by his Boss with finding a valuable treasure in the Stonetalon Mountains that was presumed to be worth more than half the jewels in Undermine after a previous crew went missing, presumed to have run off with the treasure. (ref: [quest: race=goblin] *([deprecated](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=80100))*) However, the operation was compromised by the damaged gizmos from when they flew in, night elven environmentalists interfering with the operation, and the site itself was haunted by dark spirits.

Not willing to continue delving through the ruins, but afraid of reprisal on the behalf of the Venture Company, Nert chose to defect to the Horde in the belief that the Company would not dare cross them. (ref:  [quest: race=goblin] *([deprecated](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=80108))*) Taking flight in his flying machine along with the rest of the Venture Co. refugees, Nert flew to Durotar. There, he reached out to his old friend [Cook Torka](Cook Torka), (ref: [quest: race=goblin] *([deprecated](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=80109))*) hoping to find an opening in joining the Horde through him and ultimately managing to arrange a meeting with [Gar'Thok](Gar'Thok) to facilitate the founding of the [Durotar Labor Union](Durotar Labor Union). (ref: [quest: race=goblin] *([deprecated](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=80110))*)

The Venture Company later sent a third crew led by to check on Nert and his crew's mission, to find no trace of their (now-ex) employees, having dug open a way to the valley to find a clue to their whereabouts and to finish the mission that was tasked to them all that time ago: Drain the land of all of its resources.
