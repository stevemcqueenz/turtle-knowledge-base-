# Ghost

Most ghosts are harmless reflections of what they were when they still lived, and are invisible to normal eyes.  But this is not always the case.  Ghosts who have a strong desire for revenge are often tied to the physical plane by that desire.  Some ghosts are even forced to remain in the physical plane by an outside force.

Types of Ghosts:

- [Wraith](Wraith)s
- [Shade](Shade)s
- [Banshee](Banshee)s
