# Hammerfall

**Hammerfall** was formerly one of the largest of the [Alliance](Alliance) internment camps.  It was attacked and destroyed by [Thrall](Thrall) and the [Horde](Horde) in order to free the [Orcs](Orcs) held captive there.  Shortly after the end of the [Third War](Third War), the Horde re-occupied the ruins of Hammerfall to use as a forward base in the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). The city is so named in honor to [Orgrim Doomhammer](Orgrim Doomhammer), who was killed by a lance strike to the back during the camp's liberation.
