## Description
War is upon our lands, <class>.

The arrogant Kul Tirans think us weak and it is up to the brave sons and daughters of the Horde to teach them otherwise!

A second wave of their old fleet, the same we vanquished years ago, has landed just off our shores, posing a threat to our people.

On the southern edge of the Echo Isles, their scouts and commandos can be found, probing and plotting for their next move. Slay them in the name of the Horde, and return to me!

## Progress
