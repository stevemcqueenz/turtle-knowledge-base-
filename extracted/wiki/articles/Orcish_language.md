# Orcish (language)

The primary [language](Language) of [Orcs](Orcs) used throughout the [Horde](Horde) [faction](Faction). All Horde characters should understand  **Orcish** speech.

### Some words
Note: The language algorithm used by the in-game "translator" merely makes the words LOOK like Orcish. It does not actually use a specific dictionary. Therefore, translated in-game speech isn't true Orcish.

- "Grommash" = "Giant's Heart"
- "Lok'amon" = Traditional orcish song sung about starting a family.
- "Lok'tra" = Traditional orcish song sung about a battle.
- "Lok'vadnod" = Traditional orcish song sung about the life of a hero.
- "Throm'ka" = "Hail"

### Common phrases
Although no official translation has been released, these are some Orcish phrases and their rough meanings based on the actions of the units in the RTS games when they say them.

- Swobu, Dabu, Zug Zug - Acknowledgement (When you order a unit to move)
- Lok-Tar (Ogar) - A greeting/battlecry, could mean something such as "Come with me(, my warriors)".
- Lok-Regar Ogull, On-Dabu - Ready for orders.
- Lok-Narosh - Attacking enemy (Attack order).
