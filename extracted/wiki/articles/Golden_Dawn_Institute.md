# Golden Dawn Institute

The **Golden Dawn Institute** is a floating building located by the entrance to the high elven capital of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas) that serves host to refugees and travellers from afar. The grounds below the institute leading to the gates of the city are host to a ranger training ground, an auction house and bank, Tiriel Brightwater's inn that additionally serves as a home to refugees, and the sanctum of the Grand Magistrix of the Silvermoon Remnant, [Merisa Dawnbreaker](Grand Magistrix Merisa Dawnbreaker).

- - - - - ## Notes
- The Golden Dawn Institute was formerly the site of a moonwell built and consecrated by [Moon Priestess Alunasha](Moon Priestess Alunasha), an envoy of High Priestess [Tyrande Whisperwind](Tyrande Whisperwind), as a gift to the high elven people. The moonwell has been removed when the region was expanded into the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands). It was also the location of the teleport to Stormwind City, done through [Tanilaeh Sunkiss](Tanilaeh Sunkiss), which was also removed with the expansion of the Highlands.
