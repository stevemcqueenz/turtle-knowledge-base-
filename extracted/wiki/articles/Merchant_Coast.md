# Merchant Coast

The **Merchant Coast** is the name given to the small portion of the [Barrens](Barrens) that forms the coastline of the [Great Sea](Great Sea). Along the Merchant Coast is the [goblin](goblin) port town of [Ratchet](Ratchet), and the [Theramore](Theramore) outpost, [Northwatch Hold](Northwatch Hold). Off the coast is the [warrior's](warrior) haven, [Fray Island](Fray Island). The Merchant Coast receives its name from the lucrative trade that takes place through the thrifty hands of the Ratchet goblins.
