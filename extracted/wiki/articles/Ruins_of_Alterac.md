# Ruins of Alterac

The **Ruins of Alterac** lie in the snowy mountains at the center of the [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains).  After the Kingdom of [Alterac](Alterac) was destroyed by the [Alliance](Alliance) for their betrayal during the [Second War](Second War), most of the population abandoned the ruins of their former homes.  Now, [Ogre](Ogre)s have taken up the deserted ruins.  Of the former capital city, only the mighty keep remains relatively intact.  Elite Ogre lords now occupy what remains of the keep, and their soldiers patrol the snowy ruins.
