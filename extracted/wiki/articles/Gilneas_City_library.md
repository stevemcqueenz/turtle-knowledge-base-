# Gilneas City library

The **Gilneas City library** is a shop located in [Gilneas City](Gilneas City). A [painting](Painting of Mia) of the late [Mia Greymane](Mia Greymane) is hung here, (ref: [quest: A Royal Heist]) and the library holds both [Manuscript on Hydromancy II](Manuscript on Hydromancy II) (ref: [quest: Manuscript on Hydromancy II|class=mage]) and copies of the [Book of Ur: Volume Two](Book of Ur: Volume Two) (ref: [quest: Ravencroft's Ambition]) and [Gilnean Jewelry: A Compendium](Gilnean Jewelry: A Compendium). (ref: [quest: profession=Jewelcrafting])
