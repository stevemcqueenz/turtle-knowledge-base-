# Longshore

-Longshore** is the vast stretch of coastline along [Westfall](Westfall). Traveling along the coast can be hazardous as swarms of [murloc](murloc)s (including [Old Murk-Eye](Old Murk-Eye)) roam the beaches unchecked. Many shipwrecks, including elven destroyers, lie off the coast.

On the far southwestern coast is the farm of [Daphne Stilwell](Daphne Stilwell), who was attacked by members of the [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood) until aspiring [Knights of the Silver Hand](Knights of the Silver Hand) sent by [Duthorian Rall](Duthorian Rall) came to protect her. (ref: [quest: The Tome of Valor|class=paladin]) (ref: [quest: The Tome of Valor|class=paladin]) The Westfall Lighthouse is located off the far southern edge of Longshore.

The [Half Pendant of Aquatic Endurance](Half Pendant of Aquatic Endurance), which [night elf](night elf) [druid](druid)s need to collect to learn [Aquatic Form](Aquatic Form), is stored in a [Strange Lockbox](Strange Lockbox) near a shipwreck off northwestern Longshore. (ref: [quest: class=druid])
