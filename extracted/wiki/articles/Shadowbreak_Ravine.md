# Shadowbreak Ravine

**Shadowbreak Ravine** is a well-hidden area found in the mountains southeast of [Magram Village](Magram Village) in [Desolace](Desolace). This area is a haven for [Burning Blade](Burning Blade) cultists. It holds several structures including giant mushrooms and a summoning circle with giant chains. Although there is no apparent connection between them, Shadowbreak Ravine does with its enormous mushrooms and purplish atmosphere remind much of [Zangarmarsh](Zangarmarsh).

There are only two quests that make use of this area, Reagents for Reclaimers Inc., the [Ley Hunters](Ley Hunter) drop [Felhound Brain](Felhound Brain), as do other felhounds in Desolace, and The Infernal Orb for mages, as [Burning Blade Summoners](Burning Blade Summoner) drop the orbs needed. Some speculate this subzone was intended to be used in the [warlock](warlock) level 40 [mount](mount) quest (before patch 2.4) as you can find some neutral level 40 [felsteed](felsteed)s there.
