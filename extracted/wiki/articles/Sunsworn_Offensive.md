The **Sunsworn Offensive** is a faction of [high elves](High elf) of the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant) that is loyal to Lord [Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn) that formed following the removal of Lady [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner) as Speaker of the Remnant and the establishment of the [Regency Council](Regency Council). They are primarily in opposition to the [Silver Covenant](Silver Covenant), a faction of the Remnant loyal to Lady Windrunner, with whom they clash in [Sunstrider Court](Sunstrider Court) in what they call "friendly competition." (ref: [Patch 1.18.0#NEW ARENA — SUNSTRIDER COURT](Patch 1.18.0#NEW ARENA — SUNSTRIDER COURT))
