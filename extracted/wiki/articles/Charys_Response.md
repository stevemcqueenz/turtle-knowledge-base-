## Description
Who would suspect a Kul Tiran sailor to show so much affection? Those that many call crass, blunt, and overly loud at that.

He has surprised me, in a rather nice way; to be remembered after such a long time really is a compliment.

Here, I prepared a letter for him, and - just - for him. Do not think to pry it open, my magic will know if you do.

<She gives a wicked stare, and lets out a laugh to ease her feigned threat.>

Take it to the sailor, and let him know his actions are appreciated.
