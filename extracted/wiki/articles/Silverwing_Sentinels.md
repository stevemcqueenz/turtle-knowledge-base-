The [Night Elves](Night Elves), who have begun a massive push to retake the forests of [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), are now focusing their attention on ridding their land of the [Warsong Outriders](Warsong Outriders) once and for all. And so, the Silverwing Sentinels have answered the call and sworn that they will not rest until every last [Orc](Orc) is defeated and cast out of [Warsong Gulch](Warsong Gulch).

## Reputation
[Reputation](Reputation) is gained through participation in the [Warsong Gulch](Warsong Gulch) [Battleground](Battleground). You gain 35 reputation each time your side captures a flag. This reputation gain is increased to 45 on [holiday weekends](Battlegrounds holiday).

## Rewards
- Players that have exalted with [League of Arathor](League of Arathor), [Silverwing Sentinels](Silverwing Sentinels) and [Stormpike](Stormpike) receive the [title](title) of Justicar.
