# Winterhoof Water Well

The **Winterhoof Water Well** is located at the edge of the [Rolling Plains](Rolling Plains) in southern [Mulgore](Mulgore).53 66 Mulgore (g/s/c)

Years after the [Third War](Third War), the well was taken and poisoned over by the [Venture Company](Venture Company) in order to control the water supply, but they were eventually run off by the [tauren](tauren) [adventurer](adventurer)s, while [Mull Thunderhorn](Mull Thunderhorn) created a totem to cleanse the waters.
