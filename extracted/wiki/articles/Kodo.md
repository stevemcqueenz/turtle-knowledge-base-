# Kodo

Kodos are massive beasts with thick leathery skin that ranges in color from sandy brown to black.  Most kodo have a large horn that sprouts from their nose, and some are even covered in thick wooly hair.  Kodos range across central Kalimdor, but are particularly prevalent in [Mulgore](Mulgore), where the rolling grassy plains are particularly suited to their omnivorous nature.  The [Tauren](Tauren) hold them in high esteem, and breed them as [mounts](mounts).  They are also used extensively as pack animals by both the [Goblins](Goblins) and the [Horde](Horde). During [The Third War](The Third War), the [Orcs](Orcs) recieved a pack of tamed kodos as a gift from [Cairne](Cairne) and the [Tauren](Tauren) in return for Thrall's generosity.  The [Orcs](Orcs) used the kodos to carry giant wardrums which were played furiously to boost morale during combat.  These kodo were also taught the amusing trick of swallowing its enemies.  Wild Kodos are neutral and will not attack unless threatened. 

The giant, slow-moving kodo beasts of the Barrens are one of the
most intriguing species of Kalimdor. Though gentle and ponderous
by nature, these mighty beasts become fierce enemies
when threatened or attacked. Many kodos hold a special bond
with the benevolent Tauren race.
It is said that some rare kodos are bound to the spirits of the sky and
storm. Thus, the Tauren have legends about those they call Lightning
Lizards, [Salamander](Salamander)s (not the ["Fire Naga"]("Fire Naga") creatures), [Thunder Lizards](Thunder Lizard) and Storm Walkers. Whether or not these behemoths
actually have supernatural powers remains to be seen. If one <i>is</i> discovered, it should be approached with extreme caution. It is said that these Thunder Lizards are much more hostile than their more common cousins and will attack without provocation.

Most kodo travel in herds of three to five.  When one kodo of the herd is attacked, the others will join it in combat.

A legend exists that when a kodo senses the end of its life, it will journey far to a great [Kodo Graveyard](Kodo Graveyard) as a final resting place.
