# Tinker Town

**Tinker Town** is the gnomish quarter of Ironforge. It is home to a number of alchemy and engineering trainers, as well as the entrance to the famed [Deeprun Tram](Deeprun Tram).

When the gnomes lost Gnomeregan to the troggs, the dwarves of Ironforge took in the refugees. Prior to the establishment of the [Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility](Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility), Tinker Town was the center of the gnomish court in exile and [High Tinker Mekkatorque](High Tinker Mekkatorque) could be found here, along with his closest advisers. Now, [Gelwin Brasspipe](Gelwin Brasspipe) stands in his place, directing adventurers to the Reclamation Facility.
