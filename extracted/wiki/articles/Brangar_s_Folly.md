# Brangar's Folly

-Brangar's Folly**, formerly known as **Brangar's Farmstead**, is an abandoned farm located in the northeastern [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches). The farm was owned by [Brangar](Brangar), who was once the richest farmer in all the Grim Reaches. However, when the [Dragonmaw clan](Dragonmaw clan) came, he refused to leave and said that he would die before giving up his land - which he did. After his death, no one claimed the grain that had remained in his granaries. (ref: [quest: Brangar's Folly])

However, there were whispers that Brangar went mad in the last days he was hiding something darker, (ref:  and in his personal journal he wrote that he was hearing voices that compelled him to ignore the magistrates' call to evacuate.<ref>[quest: Brangar's Journal]) The corruption that seeps through the Grim Reaches has taken root within the farmstead, which has manifested as [Repugnant Ooze](Repugnant Ooze)s that plague the land and are concentrated upon the farmstead in particular. (ref: [quest: Signs of Corruption])
