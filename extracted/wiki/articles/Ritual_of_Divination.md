## Objectives
Assist Magus Halister during the ritual and report the findings to Garran Vimes at Theramore Isle in Dustwallow Marsh.
- Information from Paval Reethe

## Description
I shall need your help during the ritual. Perhaps we may learn more from the spirit realm.

Let's begin.

## Rewards
- 2850 XP

## Progress
I heard terrible noises coming from the Mage Tower. What happened?

## Completion
So it was the deserters. Very well, I suspected it, to be honest. These people have no honor. We shall send our best men to deal with them. But before this, I think the Horde should also get an explanation and... an apology.

And when it comes to this Mariner, let me consult with some of my advisors. I think I may have heard of this man before.

## Notes
  Magus Halister begins to call upon the arcane, his channeling draws forth the energy of the spiritual realm.
  **Paval Reethe says:** Who disturbs my rest? Do I owe my service to the Alliance even in death? Let me pass to the Spirit Realm in peace.
  **Magus Halister says:** Paval Reethe. In life, you failed to uphold honor. You deserted your friends, your family. Now you have a chance to redeem yourself.
  **Paval Reethe says:** Isn't it obvious? The deserters did it. Not me, though. In my last days, I did what was right. I tried to stop them. That's why they left me in the swamp.
  **Magus Halister says:** Why? Why did they burn it?
  **Paval Reethe says:** Shady Rest Inn was open to everyone. Even us. We have spent many evenings there, laughing and drinking. But one time, some of our people got too drunk. They started meddling with the innkeeper. Until they said too much. Something about the [Vengeful Mariner](Vengeful Mariner). They couldn't allow this to reach Theramore, so they burned the inn. No witnesses.
  **Magus Halister says:** Vengeful Mariner?
  Paval Reethe laughs openly.
  **Paval Reethe says:** You would not understand.
  The Spirit of Paval Reethe vanishes.
  **Magus Halister yells:** Paval! I curse you, come back! Reethe!
  Magus Halister sighs.
  **Magus Halister says:** Well, looks like he left us for good. At least we got some answers out of him. Now, go back to Captain Garram Vimes. He will know what to do next.
