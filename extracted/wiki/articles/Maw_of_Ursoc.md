The **Maw of Ursoc** is a cave within [Ursolan](Ursolan) that serves as the seat of the [Timbermaw](Timbermaw Hold (faction)) [furbolg](furbolg)s. It provides an inn and vendors to those with sufficient standing with the Timbermaw Hold faction.
