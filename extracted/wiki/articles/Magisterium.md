# Magisterium

The **Magisterium** (ref: [quest: Magister's Letter|class=mage|race=HighElf] *([Deprecated](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=80214))*) (ref: [Magister's Letter](Magister's Letter)) are a prestigious group of [high elf](high elf) mages. Though the organization is a shadow of what it once was, the remaining magisters of Quel'Thalas have continued to uphold their duties towards the Magisterium and rebuild the order.
