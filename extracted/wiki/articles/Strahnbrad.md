# Strahnbrad

**Strahnbrad** was a town located east of [Alterac](Alterac) and south of [Andorhal](Andorhal) in the human kingdom of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). The town was situated in the strategic position of the northern foothills of the Alterac Mountains, but even this was not enough of an advantage to protect the town from ravaging [Blackrock](Blackrock Clan) [Orcs](Orcs) who raided the town shortly prior to the [Third War](Third War). The [Order of the Silver Hand](Order of the Silver Hand) moved quickly to counter the Orcs' aggression, dispatching the legendary [paladin](paladin) Uther the Lightbringer and Prince [Arthas](Arthas) to the scene. A brief battle took place at Strahnbrad which saw the Orcs routed easily by the human forces, but much of the town's population was devastated by the Blackrock raid. Arthas and Uther later discovered that the Orcs had raided the town for slaves to sacrifice in the name of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion).

Strahnbrad was one of the few towns of Lordaeron to survive the coming of the Plague of Undeath, but fell years later to the [Syndicate](Syndicate) without the protection of Lordaeron's army. Now inhabited by Syndicate personnel, the town is a base of operations for that organization as it conducts efforts throughout the [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills) and the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands) aimed at weakening the [Alliance](Alliance).
