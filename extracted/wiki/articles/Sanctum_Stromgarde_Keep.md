# Sanctum (Stromgarde Keep)

[The Sanctum](The Sanctum) is a church found inside [Stromgarde Keep](Stromgarde Keep). It is currently under control of the [Alliance](Alliance), and is the residence of [Galen Trollbane](Galen Trollbane).
