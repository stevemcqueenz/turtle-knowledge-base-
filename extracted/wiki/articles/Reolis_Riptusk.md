**Reolis Riptusk** is a [forest troll](forest troll) quest giver located in [Amani'Alor](Amani'Alor) in [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains).

## Quests
He started the following quests, all of which gave [Revantusk Trolls](Revantusk Trolls) reputation:
- [60] [A Donation of Wool: Revantusk Tribe](A Donation of Wool: Revantusk Tribe)
- [60] [A Donation of Silk: Revantusk Tribe](A Donation of Silk: Revantusk Tribe)
- [60] [A Donation of Mageweave: Revantusk Tribe](A Donation of Mageweave: Revantusk Tribe)
- [60] [A Donation of Runecloth: Revantusk Tribe](A Donation of Runecloth: Revantusk Tribe)
- [60] [Additional Runecloth: Revantusk Tribe](Additional Runecloth: Revantusk Tribe) (Repeatable)
