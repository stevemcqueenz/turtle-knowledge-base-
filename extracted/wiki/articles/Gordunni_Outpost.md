# Gordunni Outpost

The **Gordunni Outpost**75 35 Feralas (g/s/c) is an area in the mountains north of [Lariss Pavilion](Lariss Pavilion) currently held by the [Gordunni](Gordunni) [ogre](ogre)s.  Their presence has wilted the normally verdant plant life of [Feralas](Feralas) into a withered brown.

The hills have two caves, a small one towards the entrance and an [ogre mound](ogre mound) at the back of the area.

[Gordunni Ogre](Gordunni Ogre)s, [Gordunni Ogre Mage](Gordunni Ogre Mage)s and [Gordunni Brute](Gordunni Brute)s roam the area.
