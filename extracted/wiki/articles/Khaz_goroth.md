Male [Vanir](Vanir) [Titan](Titan), shaper of Mountains and canyons, a craftsman. [Eonar](Eonar) and [Khaz'Goroth](Khaz'Goroth) created the [Earthen](Earthen) ([Dwarves](Dwarves) and [Trogg](Trogg)s) and [Mountain Giant](Mountain Giant)s. His Bronze-skinned body sparks flames. His weapon is a giant smith-hammer. Among Khaz'Goroth's combat strategies are the summoning of Elemental swarms, Wish(teleport) and dispell magic. imprisonment, crushing fist, and Avatar before engaging melee. He is wise in building architecture and engineering. He created and shaped the mountains with help of his creation, the Earthen.
