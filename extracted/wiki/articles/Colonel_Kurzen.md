# Colonel Kurzen

Colonel Kurzen
Level 40 Elite

Colonel Kurzen is a Level 40 Elite that resides in Kurzen's Compound.  He and his men are currently at war with the soldiers at the Rebel Compound. 
His name is a fictitious reference to Colonel Walter E. Kurtz, from Apocalypse Now, who has retreated to a camp in the Jungle.
