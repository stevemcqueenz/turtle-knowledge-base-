## Objectives
Venture into the Hateforge Quarry, and remove the Twilight Hammer presence within for Karfang in Karfang Hold.
- [Har'gesh Doomcaller](Har'gesh Doomcaller) slain

## Description
I know what it is the Warchief speaks, the Twilight Hammer has recently moved into the Hateforge Quarry, located just south of here.

It is an annoyance that was eventually going to draw in more attention from our former masters, and was something we would need to deal with eventually, now I implore you to help us with it.

We may have the strength to fight deep, but to stop the Twilight Hammer lurking deep within the Dark Iron clutches, requires covert strength, not brute strength.

As Warleader, I ask you to remove the Twilight presence on my behalf.
