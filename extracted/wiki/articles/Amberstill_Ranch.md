# Amberstill Ranch

**Amberstill Ranch** is a ranch found off the road that leads to [Loch Modan](Loch Modan), north of [Gol'Bolar Quarry](Gol'Bolar Quarry) in [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh). Players can buy a [Rabbit Crate (Snowshoe)](Rabbit Crate (Snowshoe)) here from [Yarlyn Amberstill](Yarlyn Amberstill), as well as accept a [quest](quest), [quest: Protecting the Herd], to kill a [Wendigo](Wendigo) named [Vagash](Vagash). This ranch also has a [Riding trainer](Riding trainer) and [mount](mount) [vendor](vendor), from whom [dwarves](Dwarf) (or players of any other [Alliance](Alliance) race who have obtained exalted Ironforge [reputation](reputation)) can buy [ram](ram)s to ride.

Only dwarves and people with Exalted reputation with [Ironforge](Ironforge) can learn riding skill here.
