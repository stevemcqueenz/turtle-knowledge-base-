# Chillwind Camp

Chillwind Camp is the [Alliance](Alliance) [town](town) in the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands).  It's located in the south, at the entrance to the Plaguelands from [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains).  The small camp, maintained by a small contingent of the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn), is the last bastion of safety for Alliance travelers approaching the Western Plaguelands.  Only recently established, the Dawn has begun their first assaults against the Scourge in the area.  Only having a few troops and specialists, they rely mainly on mercenaries to carry out attacks and strategic guerilla warfare against the scourge base of [Andorhal](Andorhal) and the diabolical [Plague Cauldrons](Plague Cauldrons).

As the first Argent Dawn establishment most will see, the [Horde](Horde) counterpart is [the Bulwark](the Bulwark), on the border with [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades).
