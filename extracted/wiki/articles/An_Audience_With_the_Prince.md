## Notes
  **Grand Magister Rommath says:** Aseph-ah-balla nah!
  *[Image of Kael'thas](Image of Kael'thas) appears.*
  **Image of Kael'thas says:** Rommath, my faithful servant. It has been too long since we last spoke.
  **Grand Magister Rommath says:** My Prince. I am relieved to see you alive and well. I have much to report...
  **Image of Kael'thas says:** Yet, I have little time. Are my people well?
  **Grand Magister Rommath says:** Yes, my Prince. Yet, many wither, as we are unable to sate our addiction. Also, the [Regency Council](Regency Council) is torn...
  **Image of Kael'thas says:** Do not bore me with politics, Rommath. I care little about it. Soon, our people will hunger no more. I will see to it.
  **Grand Magister Rommath says:** When, my Prince? Many believe that you have forsaken us. They call you a traitor, I can no longer bear the insults...
  **Image of Kael'thas says:** Soon, Rommath. Maintain silence for now. Until my return, it is better that they forget about me. They cannot afford to be dependent on a mere idea; they require tangible solutions. Do not sow false hope among our people. I will only return when I have discovered the path to our salvation.
  **Grand Magister Rommath says:** I understand. We await your return.
  **Image of Kael'thas says:** And I cannot wait to be with me people once more. It won't be long, I promise.
  **Grand Magister Rommath says:** May you always remember the Light of the Sunwell, my Prince.
  *Image of Kael'thas disappears.*
