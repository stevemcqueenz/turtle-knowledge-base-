## Description
Your dedication to the Horde and your courage in battle are both beyond question.

You have earned one of the highest honors Orgrimmar can bestow: A Lok'tra shall be composed of your victory at Anchor's Edge, forevermore to be sung by our people in memory of what transpired there.

But that cannot be your only reward.

The Warchief wishes to see you again — he is known to be generous to his loyal warriors.
