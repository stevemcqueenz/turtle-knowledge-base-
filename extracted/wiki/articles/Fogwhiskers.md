# Fogwhiskers

The **Fogwhiskers** were a [kobold](kobold) group based out of the [old Breezehaven mine](Ruins of Breezehaven) in [Balor](Balor). When the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) and the [Shadow Council](Shadow Council) returned to Balor, they claimed the mine from the kobolds and the Fogwhiskers were wiped out save for one, [Snugg](Snugg).
