# Valley of Spears

The **Valley of Spears** [33, 57] is home of the [Maraudine](Maraudine) [Centaur](Centaur) clan (lvl 37-42). It is found in western [Desolace](Desolace), and is the location of the underground [dungeon](dungeon) of [Maraudon](Maraudon).

The protectors of Maraudon, the Maraudine, live in this centaur village. Only the bravest and mightiest of the tribe’s warriors are chosen to guard the tomb of [Zaetar](Zaetar) at Maraudon.

Besides the countless [centaur](centaur)s, one can also find [Melizza Brimbuzzle](Melizza Brimbuzzle) here, a caged [goblin](goblin) who wants nothing more than to be let out of the valley.
