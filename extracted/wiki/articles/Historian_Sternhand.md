## Quotes
  -Gossip**
A fellow historian perhaps? Are you here to learn of the great [Baggoth Ironmantle](Baggoth Ironmantle)? Renowned general and architect of the Grim Reaches? Did you know it was Baggoth himself that helped orchestrate the defense of the first Dragonmaw incursion?

...Maybe you're more interested in the fall of [Stolgaz Keep](Stolgaz Keep) some years ago?

  > Gossip: *Tell me more about the fall of Stolgaz Keep.*

  Stolgaz Keep's destruction lives in the hearts of many dwarves within the Grim Reaches. It is a tale of woe, and one that we tell often to not forget.

  Stolgaz Keep was created some eighty years ago, and used as a fortress to defend the region from any threats that the Grim Reaches could face. It was a place that many visited to taste its more famous 'Stolgaz Ale', a traditional beverage with a sweet and textured flavor!

  A few years ago, the Dragonmaw orcs returned, and with them came conflict. Their efforts focused heavily on Stolgaz Keep, eventually bringing it, and its brave defenders into ruin. Many attempts were made to retake it through the years. Though some of them had made progress, all of them ended in failure. The land is littered in destruction, and now occupied permanently by the Dragonmaw... I would advise avoiding the roads that lead north.

  > Gossip: *Tell me more about Baggoth.*

  Baggoth Ironmantle was the Marshal of Dun Kithas many years ago. He was a renowned architect who built this very hall that we dwell in now!

  When conflict came to Dun Kithas during the first Dragonmaw incursion in the [Second War](Second War), Baggoth himself rose to the task of defending our lands. Baggoth defeated the orcs in many battles, until an orcish arrow felled him during their retreat from Grim Reaches itself.

  While Baggoth may be known for his contributions during the Dragonmaw Incursion. It was the great feats of engineering and construction that he is most known for today. [Baggoth's Rampart](Baggoth's Rampart) and [Baggoth's Wall](Baggoth's Wall) still stand as defenses that we use to this today, just to the northwest of Dun Kithas, a true testament to his ingenuity and skill.
