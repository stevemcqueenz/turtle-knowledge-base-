## Description
The entirety of Gilneas stands at our side! The tyranny of the royalists can no longer be allowed to stand! If this nation is to ever recover from the darkness, then we must hold fast, and strike even faster still!

The Greymane Crown must be recovered if our dream is ever to be realized. Enter Gilneas City. Fight your way through his most loyal soldiers. End the Harlows, and reclaim the Greymane birthright. A new king and a new beginning awaits us, <name>.
