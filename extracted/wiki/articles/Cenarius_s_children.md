# Cenarius's children

-Cenarius' Children** are literally the offspring of the demigod Cenarius. Centaur are his bastard children, while dryads are his daughters and keepers of the grove are his sons. They share the culture of the night elves, although most live apart form the Kaldorei as wardens of nature.  Cenarius' bastard children have upper bodies closer to humans and lower bodies more like a horse, while Cenarius' favored children are similar except they have an upper torso more similar to [night elves](night elves) and a lower body more like a [deer](deer) or [stag](stag).

Children of Cenarius may also include the [night elves](night elves) as well.

- Females: [Dryad](Dryad)
- Males: [Keeper of the Grove](Keeper of the Grove)
- Females and Males: [Centaur](Centaur)s
