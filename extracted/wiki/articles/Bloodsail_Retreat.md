# Bloodsail Retreat

The **Bloodsail Retreat** is a camp of the [Bloodsail Buccaneers](Bloodsail Buccaneers) found on the eastern coast of [Jaguero Isle](Jaguero Isle) in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). Admiral [Garfield Sparkblast](Garfield Sparkblast) and his crew have moored their ship here, looking for more crewmates to join them.
