**High General Brigitte Abbendis**<ref> [60] [Scarlet Aid](Scarlet Aid)) is a rare elite [human](human) found in [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand) in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands). She can be frequently found visiting the city's [abbey](Tyr's Hand Abbey) or planning the Crusade's military operations in the garrison. Along with Highlord [Alexandros Mograine](Alexandros Mograine) and [Grand Inquisitor Isillien](Grand Inquisitor Isillien), she was one of the original founders of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade). (ref: [Corrupted Ashbringer/Scarlet Monastery Event](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Corrupted_Ashbringer/Scarlet_Monastery_Event))

Following the events that transpired in the Scarlet Monastery, Hearthglen and Stratholme, Abbendis is the last true leader of the Scarlet Crusade. In the days after learning of the demonic influence she has tasked her followers with recruiting young men and women such as [Vladeus Springriver](Vladeus Springriver) to replenish the Crusade's numbers by tricking them into joining their cause.

## Abilities
- **Battle Shout** — Increases the melee attack power of nearby party members by 4 for 2 min.
- **Demoralizing Shout** — Reduces the melee attack power of nearby enemies by 10 for 30 sec.
- **Holy Strike** — Consecrates the caster's weapon, inflicting 2 additional damage on its next attack. All damage inflicted is based on Holy magic.
