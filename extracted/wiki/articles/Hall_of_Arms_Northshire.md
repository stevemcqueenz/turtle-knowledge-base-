# Hall of Arms (Northshire)

*For other uses, see [Hall of Arms (disambiguation)](Hall of Arms (disambiguation)).*

The **Hall of Arms** wing of [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey), located in [Northshire Valley](Northshire Valley) in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), is home to both the [paladin](paladin) and [warrior](warrior) trainers for the least experienced of the Stormwind Army recruits. It is situated south of the [Main Hall](Main Hall (Northshire)), and the [Library Wing](Library Wing (Northshire)) lies on the opposite side of the Abbey.
