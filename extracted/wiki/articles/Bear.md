# Bear

A very common, bulky, furry, four-legged [Beast](beast) that lopes along on all fours, but may occasionally rise up on hind legs. Most **bears** are [hostile](Hostile) when approached, despite their sometimes cuddly appearance. They tend to be larger than human sized at their smallest.  Bears are found in [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), [Loch Modan](Loch Modan), the [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills), [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), [Feralas](Feralas), the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands), [Darkshore](Darkshore), [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest), and [Winterspring](Winterspring).

## Traits
- Bear pets sometimes have the [claw](claw) ability when [tamed](Beast_Taming).
- There are varieties of "plagued" bears that roam [Felwood](Felwood) and the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands).

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Have high HP, medium armor, and low damage.
- Eats Meat, Fish, Fungus, Fruit, Cheese, and Bread, making them extraordinarily easy to feed.
- Can learn [Bite](Bite), [Cower](Cower), [Claw](Claw), and [Growl](Growl).  They have yet to recieve their species skill.
- Very popular as Hunter pets due to their easy diet and tanking stats.
- Ursius and the Young Forest Bear are two of the only known hunter pets with a swing speed higher than 2.0.  This means they hit less often, but for more damage.
