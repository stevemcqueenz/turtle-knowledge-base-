# Demon Soul

''See also [The Demon Soul](The Demon Soul), a novel in the [War of the Ancients Trilogy](War of the Ancients Trilogy).

The **Dragon Soul** - known later as the **Demon Soul** - was an artifact of immense power created by the [Aspect](Dragon Aspects) of the [Black Dragonflight](Black Dragonflight), Neltharion the Earth Warder...later known in history as the Black Scourge, [Deathwing](Deathwing).
