# Bilgerat Compound

The **Bilgerat Compound** is a pirate holdout located on one of the northern islands of [Balor](Balor). It is occupied by the [Bilgerat](Bilgerat) pirates of [Plunder Isle](Plunder Isle), who sought to enrich themselves from the ruins of [Stormwrought Castle](Stormwrought Castle). However, Captain [Barbaros](Barbaros) and several of his crew ran afoul of the [orc](orc)s of the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) that had returned to Balor, being taken captive within the castle.

The [Logbook of The Rat's Nest](Logbook of The Rat's Nest) can be found in the captain's quarters aboard the ship.
