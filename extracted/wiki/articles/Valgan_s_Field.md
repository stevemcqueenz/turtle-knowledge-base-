# Valgan's Field

**Valgan's Field** is yet another among the desolate farms of northern [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest). This farm is located just west of the [Shining Strand](Shining Strand), east of the main north-south road, and north of a small river. It is named after its former owner, who was either killed or forced to flee from his farm when the [Scourge](Scourge) arrived in Silverpine. At the present, however, Valgan's Field is occupied by the [Moonrage](Moonrage) [worgen](worgen). At its heyday, the farm would have been a lovely place, offering a magnificent view of [Lordamere Lake](Lordamere Lake).
