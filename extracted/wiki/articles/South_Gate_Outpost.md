# South Gate Outpost

[South Gate Outpost](South Gate Outpost) is the southeasternmost settlement of [dwarven](dwarven) build in [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh). It lies in the middle of [South Gate Pass](South Gate Pass), which links [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh) in the west with [Loch Modan](Loch Modan) in the east. 

The mountaineers stationed at the outpost have cleared out most of the encroaching wildlife, and thus do not face the same amount of problems as do the dwarves of [North Gate Outpost](North Gate Outpost). Many travelers pass through the outpost on their way to or from [Thelsamar](Thelsamar).
