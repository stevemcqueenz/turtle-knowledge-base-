**Edwin VanCleef** was the leader of the [Stonemasons Guild](Stonemasons Guild) that rebuilt [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) after it was sacked by the [Horde](Horde) in the [First War](First War). A disagreement with the [House of Nobles](House of Nobles) led VanCleef to found the criminal organization known as the [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood).

## Biography
### Rise of the Defias
[Mathias Shaw](Mathias Shaw) was a childhood friend of Edwin, and personally trained him as a rogue, hoping that one day he might consider a career alongside him. (ref:  Edwin also knew [Baros Alexston](Baros Alexston), who saw him as a peerless fighter, an idealist, and a romantic.<ref>[quest: The Unsent Letter])

Edwin VanCleef was the elected Guildmaster of the [Stonemasons](Stonemasons), (ref: [Report on the Defias Brotherhood](Report on the Defias Brotherhood)) and was originally one of the architects that worked on the rebuilding of [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) after its destruction at the end of the [Second War](Second War). After the city's reconstruction was finished, the [House of Nobles](House of Nobles) refused to adequately pay and offered only meager compensation, and the Stonemasons were left broke. [SI:7](SI:7) notes that the nobles of Stormwind ran up a huge debt by expanding the kingdom's military presence through [Elwynn](Elwynn Forest) and into [Stranglethorn](Stranglethorn Vale). The massive debt crippled the kingdom's economy and stripped Edwin VanCleef and his Stonemasons of their promised rewards. (ref:  Edwin spoke out, demanding restitution for their works. In response, the Stormwind House of Nobles ordered the Stonemasons' Guild disbanded, which angered VanCleef.

VanCleef and the Stonemasons set to rioting in the streets of Stormwind, and VanCleef led the Stonemasons out of the city in the rural areas of [Westfall](Westfall). There, VanCleef united them, and used his training in the arts of stealth and subterfuge to form the [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood).

During the riots, VanCleef's lieutenant and most trusted assistant, [Bazil Thredd](Bazil Thredd), was captured and placed in the [Stormwind Stockade](Stormwind Stockade).

### World of Warcraft
After he led the remnants of the Stonemasons out of Stormwind, he took advantage of the relatively unprotected state of [Westfall](Westfall), and used his considerable manpower to run many of the farmers off, and take over the handful of [gold mine](gold mine)s. Taking advantage of the resources at his disposal, VanCleef hatched a plan of retribution against the government of Stormwind.
From aboard the ship located deep within the [Deadmines](Deadmines), VanCleef awaited the moment at which he would strike at his hated foe. Before this could take place, however, [adventurer](adventurer)s of the Alliance, having previously learned of the headquarters of the Defias Brotherhood,<ref>[quest: The Defias Brotherhood]) entered the Deadmines and assaulted VanCleef's ship. After a battle, they managed to defeat VanCleef. The adventurers took an [unsent letter](An Unsent Letter) addressed to Baros Alexston on his body and severed his head for [Gryan Stoutmantle](Gryan Stoutmantle), who said that this event marked the end of the Defias Brotherhood. (ref: [quest: The Defias Brotherhood]) Yet they were left with the disconcerting worry that not all matters with the Brotherhood were yet resolved, subsequent events would prove that despite VanCleef's death, the plans of the Defias Brotherhood were underway. Conspirators with in the [House of Nobles](House of Nobles) were plotting against the king and were disposed of by [SI:7](SI:7) agents, (ref: [quest: The Attack!]) and as the Defias' operations were reaching their peak, VanCleef had sent [Edward Paddley](Edward Paddley) and other members of the Defias to plunder the nobles of [Northwind](Northwind). (ref: [VanCleef's Orders](VanCleef's Orders)) VanCleef's death and his machinations were later informed to [Lady Katrana Prestor](Lady Katrana Prestor) who thanked the adventurers on King [Varian Wrynn](Varian Wrynn)'s behalf in their part in foiling the kingpin's schemes. (ref: [quest: An Audience with the King])

## Notes

- The [Bloodsail Buccaneers](Bloodsail Buccaneers) had a deal with Edwin. (ref: [Bloodsail Orders](Bloodsail Orders))
- VanCleef's name probably comes from [Lee Van Cleef](wikipedia:Lee Van Cleef), a western film actor, who usually played villains that were similar to VanCleef.
