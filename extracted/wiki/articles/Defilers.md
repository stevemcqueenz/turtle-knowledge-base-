# Defilers

The [Forsaken](Forsaken) Defilers are the Horde faction in the [Battleground](Battleground) "[Arathi Basin](Arathi Basin)".

Like all [Battleground](Battleground) [faction](faction)s, rewards can be earned by gaining reputation with the Defilers.
