-Skuerto** is a [dwarf](dwarf) [quest giver](quest giver) located in [Refuge Pointe](Refuge Pointe) in the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). He and [Apprentice Kryten](Apprentice Kryten) were sent to the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands) by [Archmage Malin](Archmage Malin) to gather some reagents as well as to recover some artifacts that once belonged to Malin's master [Archmage Trelane](Archmage Trelane). He is very sensitive about criticisms to his skills at scouting. (ref: [quest: Worth Its Weight in Gold])
