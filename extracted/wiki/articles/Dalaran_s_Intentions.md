## Objectives
Speak with [Shadow Priest Allister](Shadow Priest Allister) at the [Sepulcher](Sepulcher). 

## Description
Of course! How could I have forgotten?

[Ambermill](Ambermill) was one of the sites earmarked by the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor), noted because it houses a dormant [ley energy node](Ley lines). The [wizard](wizard)s must be planning to reactivate the node and use its energies for some greater purpose.

We cannot allow this to happen...

It will take a great amount of power to activate the area's ley energies, we can stall them by taking the pendants away from the wizards.

Relay this information to Allister, he will decide a course of action.
