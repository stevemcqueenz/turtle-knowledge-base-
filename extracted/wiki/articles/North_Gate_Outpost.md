# North Gate Outpost

**North Gate Outpost** is the northeasternmost settlement of the [dwarves](dwarf) built in [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh). It lies in the middle of [North Gate Pass](North Gate Pass), which links Dun Morogh in the west with [Loch Modan](Loch Modan) in the east. 

The mountaineers stationed at the outpost are busily defending it from encroaching wildlife, and some dwarves have gone missing in the mountains in search of the more ferocious beasts. Young dwarves and [gnome](gnome)s are tasked with exploring the area and looking for any signs of the lost mountaineers and the animals which killed them.
