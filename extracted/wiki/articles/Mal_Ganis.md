-Mal'Ganis ** was one of the [nathrezim](nathrezim), or dreadlords, tasked by [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden) with serving as a jailor of the [Lich King](Lich King) and police him to ensure that he accomplished his dread task. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, *[Undead History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Kil'jaeden_and_the_New_Deal)*, Kil'jaeden and the New Deal)

Mal'Ganis was chosen to head up the plot to turn [Prince Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil) to darkness, and make him the Lich King's greatest champion. As Prince Arthas and Lady [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore) were investigating rumors of [plague](Plague of Undeath), they were attacked again and again by the forces of the [Scourge](Scourge) posing as the personal army of the dreadlord Mal'Ganis. [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) helped play the part when he faced the prince in battle and revealed that he was a servant of Mal'Ganis, who lurked in the city of [Stratholme](Stratholme). (ref: [The Cult of the Damned (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Cult_of_the_Damned_(WC3_Human))) As he continued battling the Scourge, Arthas grew increasingly unhinged and obsessed with killing the dreadlord. (ref: [March of the Scourge (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/March_of_the_Scourge_(WC3_Human))) On arrival to Stratholme, he discovered that it had already been infected by the plague and ordered his forces to purge the city before its inhabitants could turn into undead slaves to Mal'Ganis in death. Mal'Ganis appeared to Arthas in person amid the burning streets to taunt him during the subsequent massacre. The dreadlord challenged Arthas to face him in [Northrend](Northrend) for a true battle before teleporting away from the city. (ref: [The Culling (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Culling_(WC3_Human)))

As expected, Arthas sailed north with the royal fleet. (ref: [Divergent Courses (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Divergent_Courses_(WC3_Human))) Upon arriving in Northrend, Arthas joined forces with his old friend and teacher [Muradin Bronzebeard](Muradin Bronzebeard) and his [Dwarven Expedition](Dwarven Expedition) against the undead, (ref: [The Shores of Northrend (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Shores_of_Northrend_(WC3_Human))) but the prince's obsession with the dreadlord led him further down the path of evil, leading him to burn his own ships, lie to his men, and betray his mercenaries. (ref: [Dissension (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Dissension_(WC3_Human))) The next day, Mal'Ganis attacked Arthas' camp with an overwhelming force of Scourge. The prince and Muradin sought out Frostmourne laid, and Arthas took up the runeblade, becoming a servant of the Lich King. He rejoined his forces and easily cut through the Scourge to reach Mal'Ganis. Confidently, Mal'Ganis approached Arthas and informed him of his fate as a servant of the Dark Lord of the Dead. However, the manipulative and traitorous dreadlord was himself betrayed then, as the Lich King's first whispers to Arthas were that the time for his vengeance had come. This act of betrayal was the first step towards [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul)'s independence. Arthas killed the dreadlord and ran off into the wastes. (ref: [Frostmourne (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Frostmourne_(WC3_Human)))
