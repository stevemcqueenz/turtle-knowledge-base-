# Arathor

-Arathor** was the first great [human nation](human nation), founded by a unification of the various tribes of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms) under the [Arathi](Arathi).  Its capital was [Strom](Strom), now known as the semi-abandoned fortress of [Stromgarde](Stromgarde).

## Creation and the Troll Wars
Concerned that the [troll](Troll War)s were becoming too great a threat, the Arathi tribe embarked on a campaign to conquer its rivals through combat and politics.  By offering equality and peace to the people they conquered, the Arathi were able to form a powerful nation, Arathor.  The capital of Strom was built, and the humans in the area that would become [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) travelled to its protection.

The [high elves](high elf) of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas), meanwhile, were also under threat of the trolls.  On the verge of defeat, they sent ambassadors to Strom to plead for assistance from the human king, [Thoradin](Thoradin).  In exchange for support, the elves agreed to instruct one hundred humans in the use of magic.  Through the military might of Stromgarde, and the magic wielded by the elves and new human [mages](Mage (lore)), the trolls were soundly defeated.

## Fracturing of the Kingdom
Following the defeat of the trolls, the human mages that had been instructed by the elves eventually chose to leave Strom and found their own kingdom, [Dalaran](Dalaran), dedicated to the study and use of magic.  In time, other city-states were founded: [Gilneas](Gilneas), [Alterac](Alterac), and [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras).  Much of the nobility moved to the fertile lands of the north, founding [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), while the heirs of King Thoradin travelled far south to form the kingdom of [Azeroth](Azeroth)(later known as [Stormwind](Stormwind)).  The few remaining loyal defenders of Strom renamed it [Stromgarde](Stromgarde), all that was left of a once-great empire.

## Modern Ramifications
The human nations that splintered off from Arathor would not be united again until the [Second War](Second War), in which the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron) was formed to defeat the [Horde](Old Horde).  Lord [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar), as the last descendant of the Arathi, was able to call upon the [high elves](high elf) to join the Alliance, in payment of the help provided by the humans of Arathor during the troll wars.
