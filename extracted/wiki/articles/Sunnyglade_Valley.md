**Sunnyglade Valley** (**SGV** for short) is a [battleground](battleground) located within the depths of the [Caverns of Time](Caverns of Time), representing one of the largest clashes between the forces of the old [Kingdom of Azeroth](Stormwind (kingdom)) and the [Horde](Orcish Horde) during the [First War](First War). Here players will be acting at the behest of the guardian of the timeways, the bronze dragon [Vairozdormu](Vairozdormu), as an agent for the [Wardens of Time](Wardens of Time) and the Bronze dragonflight in their attempts to uncover the ongoing crisis threatening to alter the very fabric of history and the entirety of the modern world.

Sunnyglade Valley is a 20v20 Domination Battleground with a focus on uncovering the mystery of the shifting of time through the exploration of the nearby woodlands, as well as the local mines to gather clues.

## Battleground overview
### Basics
To win Sunnyglade Valley, your team must defeat the enemy boss ( [Marshal Greywall](Marshal Greywall) or  [Warlord Blackskull](Warlord Blackskull)) hidden deep in their base next to their standard graveyard. However, the bosses' max HP is dynamically changing - it's based on number of [Flickering Time Spark](Flickering Time Spark)s difference between both teams. If your team collects many more Sparks than enemy team, the enemy boss will have his HP massively reduced. So to achieve victory your team should collect as many Sparks as possible.

### Sparks
[Flickering Time Spark](Flickering Time Spark)s are an object that can be looted from enemy players, as well as be found and picked up across the battleground from [Small](Small Time Spark) and [Large Time Spark](Large Time Spark)s. They must be given to [Alurzion](Alurzion), who resides in the middle of the map - in [Fort Vallance](Fort Vallance). You can only turn in Sparks to him if your faction has control of the fort. Dying while in possession of Sparks results in the player who killed you inheriting all of your Sparks.

### Boss
Once one team acquires 100 Flickering Time Sparks, faction bosses will spawn. Their health is based on number of Sparks difference between both teams. The team with the leading score will have their boss with increased health, and the enemy boss will have a reduced amount of health. A greater difference in the number of Sparks equals to a significantly weaker enemy boss. The bosses spawn near their faction's primary graveyard. When enemy team starts attacking your boss, a battleground alert will sound out.

### General tactics
The most important place in Sunnyglade Valley is Fort Vallance in the center of the map. It is crucial because the faction that controls it has monopoly on turning in Flickering Time Sparks. This should always be primary focus of your team. You stay in the fort until the difference in Sparks between the teams is high enough and the bosses are spawned.

### Towers
You can capture towers to increase bonus Honor gained by your team.

### Trinkets
Sunnyglade Valley gives you opportunity to acquire powerful trinkets. These can be found in: Alliance Village, Horde Village, Castle, Western Mine, Eastern Mine. Trinkets can be looted from your corpse by enemy players. They vanish from your inventory when you leave Sunnyglade Valley.
