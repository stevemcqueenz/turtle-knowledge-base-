-Vairozdormu** is a [dragon](dragon) of the [bronze dragonflight](bronze dragonflight). The leader of the [Wardens of Time](Wardens of Time), it was at his behest that adventurers were conscripted as agents for the flight in their attempts to uncover the ongoing crisis threatening to alter the very fabric of history and the entirety of the modern world in the [Sunnyglade Valley](Sunnyglade Valley). (ref: [Patch 1.16.0 - PvP Content & Changes](https://web.archive.org/web/20260429093730/https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=2253)) (ref: [Tel'Abim (PvP) — New EU Realm!](https://web.archive.org/web/20260420123516/https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=10966)) (ref: [Patch 1.16.0 — New Battleground: Sunnyglade Valley](https://web.archive.org/web/20230524031415/https://forum.turtle-wow.org/viewtopic.php?t=4154))

## Trivia
- Although Vairozdormu is mentioned in the 1.16.0 patch notes and teaser notes for Tel'Abim and the Sunnyglade Valley battleground, he does not appear in-game.
