# Crypt lord

These ancient, evil behemoths were once the mighty kings of the subterranean spider kingdom of [Azjol-Nerub](Azjol-Nerub). In the largest communities, they still serve as advisors to a local queen. They are the holders of law and dominate the nerubian society, though the eldest members of other castes hold considerable sway.

Though the [Nerubian](Nerubian)s fell before the wrath of the [Lich King](Lich King) during the fabled [War of the Spider](War of the Spider), the insidious **Crypt Lords** were swayed over to the Lich King's ranks and granted considerable power and the immortality of undeath, in exchange for betraying thier subjects. Now the giant, thundering beasts serve as the Lich King's mightiest warriors and the guardians of the [Scourge](Scourge)'s holdings in [Northrend](Northrend). 

Crypt Lords are a different variety of the standard undead [Nerubian](Nerubian) (aka: [Crypt Fiend](Crypt Fiend)) in that they resemble beetles rather than spiders. This implies that they are a separate subpecies.  This theory is supported by the many different subspecies of the [Qiraji](Qiraji), cousins of the Nerubians.
