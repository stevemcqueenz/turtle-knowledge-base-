# Writhing Haunt

The **Writhing Haunt** is an area located in the western part of the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands).  Originally the area was a life-sustaining farm, but it has since been overrun by the vile [Scourge](Scourge). In their quest to destroy the living, they have placed a large cauldron in the middle of the field which constantly spews disease-filled vapors into the air. The cauldron itself is protected by [Cauldron Lord Razarch](Cauldron Lord Razarch).

You can find [Mulgris Deepriver](Mulgris Deepriver), a quest giving NPC, hidden inside a house at the Writhing Haunt.
