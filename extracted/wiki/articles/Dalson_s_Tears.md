# Dalson's Tears

-Dalson's Tears** is an abandoned [farm](farm) located in the middle of the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands). The cauldron is guarded by [Cauldron Lord Malvinious](Cauldron Lord Malvinious), Blighted Zombies, Rotting Cadavers, and Skeletal Terrors.

The unfortunate inhabitants of the farm still wander it in undeath. Mrs. Dalson now roams the property as a [Wandering Skeleton](Wandering Skeleton), and once you read her diary, she can drop the key to the outhouse, where Farmer Dalson, now a terrible ghoul, is trapped. If one kills [Farmer Dalson](Farmer Dalson), he drops a key to a cabinet. The cabinet left in the farmhouse can be opened for a reward.
