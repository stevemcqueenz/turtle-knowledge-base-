# Zuldazar

The capital of the [Zandalar Tribe](Zandalar Tribe) and all [Troll](Troll) tribes of Azeroth.  It is located on [Zandalar](Zandalar) in the middle of the [Great Sea](Great Sea).  Most of the tribes of Trolls outside this island share no love, but every sixth year, they meet at Zuldazar in a peace vigil to discuss things that may affect the entire Troll race.  It is lead by [King Rastakhan](King Rastakhan).
