# Altar of Storms (Burning Steppes)

.]]
An **Altar of Storms** can be found in the [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes), in the shadow of [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire). [Warlock](Warlock) members of the [Blackrock clan](Blackrock clan) have taken over the area and perform strange rituals at the altar. On a hill to the west lies the home of [Gorzeeki Wildeyes](Gorzeeki Wildeyes), studying the altar under his warlock master, [Mor'zul Bloodbringer](Mor'zul Bloodbringer).
