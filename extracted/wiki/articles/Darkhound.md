# Darkhound

A species of demonic dog.  Found in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), and [Blackrock Depths](Blackrock Depths).  The dwarves of Blackrock Depths use a variety of **Darkhound** called a [Bloodhound](Bloodhound) that easily sniffs out intruders hiding in stealth.

Darkhounds often drop wolf meat when killed.

Types of **darkhounds**:
- Darkhounds ([Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades))
- Plaguehounds ([Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands))
- Blighthounds ([Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands))
- Bloodhounds ([Blackrock Depths](Blackrock Depths))
