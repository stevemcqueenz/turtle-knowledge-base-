# Red Rocks

The **Red Rocks** are located in the northeastern area of [Mulgore](Mulgore). They are ancient ancestral holy grounds for the [tauren](tauren) [tribe](tribe)s because they hold a great spiritual energy potential. Recently, a group of [quilboar](quilboar)s had invaded the Red Rocks and defiled the holy place with their filth, which has greatly enraged the tauren of [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff).
