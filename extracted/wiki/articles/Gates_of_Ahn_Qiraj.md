# Gates of Ahn'Qiraj

A insectoid menace awaits in [Silithus](Silithus) in the ancient, brooding city of [Ahn'Qiraj](Ahn'Qiraj) that represents a danger to both [Horde](Horde) and [Alliance](Alliance) and their place in [Azeroth](Azeroth). However, no forces can preemptively deal with the threat unless they can breach the enchanted [Scarab Wall](Scarab Wall) and open the **Gates of Ahn'Qiraj** closed during [the War of the Shifting Sands](the War of the Shifting Sands).
