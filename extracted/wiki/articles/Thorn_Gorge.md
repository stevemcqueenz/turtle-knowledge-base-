-Thorn Gorge** (**TG** for short) is a [battleground](battleground) nestled in between [Mulgore](Mulgore) and the [Barrens](Barrens). The dwarven garrison at [Bael Modan](Bael Modan) has broken into the sacred grounds of the [Tauren](Tauren) in their mission to gather ground in the western frontier of Kalimdor, and the tauren enlist aid to rid the dwarves out of the lands of the Earthmother. 

Thorn Gorge is a resource race battleground with elements of Capture the Flag, featuring 4 capturable bases and a flag in the center. The overall goal is to be the first team to score 1600 points, which can be gained by holding bases or capturing flags.
