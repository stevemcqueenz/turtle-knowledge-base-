# Yeti

A large, furry [humanoid](Humanoid) animal that usually lives in or near a cave.  Some of the varieties in colder regions breathe ice breath. **Yeti** are [skinnable](Skinnable), unlike most humanoids.  Yeti can be found in [Feralas](Feralas), The [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills), [Winterspring](Winterspring), [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), and The [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains).

Also called a [Wendigo](Wendigo).
