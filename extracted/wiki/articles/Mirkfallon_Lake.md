# Mirkfallon Lake

-Mirkfallon Lake**[47, 40], high in the Stonetalon range, was once home to the tallest waterfall in Kalimdor. Aside from the breathtaking falls, one could also find the legendary hippogryphs, which migrate here from the cooler north. It is host to a wide variety of creatures, including wyverns, slimes, and thunder lizards.  The spiders of Stonetalon also lay their egg clusters here.

## Fishing
Generation of fish nodes is quite frequent, example school of sagefish and pile of junk.  Possible catch includes watertight trunk, raw sagefish and the usual common fish.  Watertight trunk may contain large healing potion, medium leather and [Weather-Beaten Journal](Weather-Beaten Journal).
