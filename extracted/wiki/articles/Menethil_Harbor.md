# Menethil Harbor

[Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor) is a port [town](town) on the western shore of the [Wetlands](Wetlands). It has a [boat](boat) that sails to [Auberdine](Auberdine) in [Darkshore](Darkshore) and another that sails to [Theramore Isle](Theramore Isle) in [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh). Both locations are on the continent of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). Within the city are many [vendor](vendor)s, a [First Aid Trainer](First Aid Trainer), the [Deepwater Tavern](Deepwater Tavern), and a [Gryphon](Gryphon) [Flight Master](Flight Master).

## History
Menethil Harbor, named after the ruling dynasty of Lordaeron, was originally established at the end of the [Second War](Second War) as the main sea link between [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) and the kingdoms of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), [Dalaran](Dalaran), [Stromgarde](Stromgarde), [Gilneas](Gilneas), and [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras). It is the only stationary Alliance presence in the Wetlands, a small well-fortified city that numbers a little over ten thousand souls. Murlock and pirate incursions are a constant threat.

With the fall of Lordaeron, and the end of the [Third War](Third War), Menethil Harbor still serves a critical purpose as it serves as the main hub of Alliance naval routes between the old kingdoms of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) and [Stormwind](Stormwind), and their new allies at [Theramore Isle](Theramore Isle) and [Teldrassil](Teldrassil).
