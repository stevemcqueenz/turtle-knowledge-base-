# Mizjah Ruins

The **Mizjah Ruins** are an area southeast of [Grom'gol Base Camp](Grom'gol Base Camp) inhabited by [Mosh'Ogg Brute](Mosh'Ogg Brute)s and [Mosh'Ogg Witch Doctor](Mosh'Ogg Witch Doctor)s. These [ogre](ogre)s are responsible for ambushing supply [caravan](caravan)s going to [Stonard](Stonard).
