# Main Hall (Tyr's Hand)

The **Main Hall** of [Tyr's Hand Abbey](Tyr's Hand Abbey), located in [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand) in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), is the part of the abbey through which all access to the tower, [Hall of Arms](Hall of Arms (Tyr's Hand)), and [Library Wing](Library Wing (Tyr's Hand)) is done. It was heavily patrolled by the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade).
