# Nethander Stead

The **Nethander Stead** is a farm just east of [Southshore](Southshore). It has been overrun by [Mudsnout](Mudsnout) [gnoll](gnoll)s, and is the only location that gnolls of this tribe can be found. Giant white mushrooms called [Mudsnout Blossoms](Mudsnout Blossoms) grow in its abandoned fields. These mushrooms are of particular interest to [Apothecary Lydon](Apothecary Lydon) in order to make a deadly brew, [Elixir of Agony](Elixir of Agony).
