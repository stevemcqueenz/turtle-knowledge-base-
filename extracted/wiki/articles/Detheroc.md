# Detheroc

The [Dreadlord](Dreadlord) of the [Nathrezim](Nathrezim), **Detheroc** and his two brothers, [Balnazzar](Balnazzar) and [Varimathras](Varimathras), were left to watch over the Legion's holdings in [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) when [Archimonde](Archimonde) and the main force of the Legion left for [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) to deal with the fleeing [orcs](orcs) and [humans](humans), as well as the [night elves](night elves) who had defeated them in ages past.

Detheroc began to notice, however, that much time had passed, and still, there was no word from Archimonde.

[Arthas](Arthas) stormed into the palace and informed them that their Legion was defeated, and that their deaths would seal that fate. But they fled before he could strike. Hiding in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), they coerced [Sylvanas](Sylvanas) into assisting them, and planned a coup to take back Lordaeron and rule the newly coined [Plaguelands](Plaguelands).  Detheroc and his brothers assumed control of the Capital City and ambushed Arthas, but the resilient [Death Knight](Death Knight) battled his way through their forces and fled across the sea, to his master in [Northrend](Northrend).

With Arthas gone, Detheroc and the Dreadlord could concentrate on their power in Lordaeron.

But when Varimathras asked Sylvanas to join their new order, she refused, butchered his forces, and turned him to her own side. Detheroc was not so easily defeated. He used his powers of domination to control the human general, [Garithos](Garithos), to his side, and when Sylvanas launched her next attack upon him, he turned Garithos and his forces loose on her. But with Varimathras at her side, Sylvanas managed to keep Garithos at bay as she stormed into Detheroc's base and tore him to shreds.
