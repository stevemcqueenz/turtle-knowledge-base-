# Misty Shore

-Misty Shore** is a stretch of waterline running from [Dandred's Fold](Dandred's Fold) to [Dalaran](Dalaran) in the [contested](contested) territory of [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains). Aside from its rather large population of [turtle](turtle)s, the shore also houses a pair of [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade)-affiliated humans, who zealously attack those who pass too close.

It is possible to view [the Dawning Isles](the Dawning Isles) from the Misty Shore, with the eerie [Fenris Keep](Fenris Keep) in the distance.
