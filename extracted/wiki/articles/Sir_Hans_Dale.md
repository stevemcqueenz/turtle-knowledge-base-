**Sir Hans Dale** is a [human](human) located at [Amberwood Keep](Amberwood Keep) in [Northwind](Northwind). He was present at a council meeting with [Lord Amberwood](Lord Amberwood), [Rutherford Justino](Rutherford Justino) and [Custodian Lambert](Custodian Lambert), where at Lambert's behest Lord Amberwood sent word out to any adventurers who could aid Northwind in the dark hours of the [Blackrock](Blackrock clan) and [Dark Iron](Dark Iron dwarf) incursions. (ref: [THE HIDDEN AREA BETWEEN STORMWIND AND IRONFORGE: Northwind Zone Trailer — Turtle WoW Patch 1.18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vIuS0DXGY6Y))

## Quotes
**Gossip**
<The young noble exhales a frustrated sigh.>

Well hello, fair peasant. Curious that the guards let you inside Amberwood Keep, given Northwind's current... let's say 'precarious' situation. Did you slide them some coin or are you just that charismatic? Alas, here you are, so you might as well entertain me. Auntie Josephine doesn't even let me out of the castle walls and I am getting bored to death reading the same old tomes again and again.

  > Gossip: *You're not like the other nobles around here.*

  Eh, thank you? You probably shouldn't say that to any other Northwind noble, adventurer. Atleast if you value your freedom. To be frank with you, I actually reside in Stormwind most of my time, it just occurred that I was visiting my aunt when all of the chaos around here happened, so I cannot leave until the other nobles get a firm grip on their lands again. I don't know if you met Duke Sherwood or Sir Amberwood at the Jousting Grounds yet, but they're probably more of what you expect Stormwind nobility to be. Eccentric personality, all the aloofness that comes with their rank, a harsh and strict disposition. If you want to rule over the common folk, it's hard not to develop these traits. Luckily my aunt is more of the benevolent type, unless you anger her, I pray for your safety should you ever accomplish that.

  > Gossip: *What's your opinion on the jousting tournament?*

  I do appreciate a good joust between honorable knights, especially with so many esteemed guests present, or so I heard. Why continue with it when your lands are under attack on all fronts eludes me, however. Lord Amberwood is a wise man, I know that much; even more puzzles me his decision to allocate what little troops he has left to the western parts of Northwind. Whatever his reasoning may be, I hope he doesn't jeopardize the safety of his subjects any longer. It is a noble's duty to ensure the safety of the common man!

  > Gossip: *Why is it forbidden for you to leave the keep?*

  A simple reason, really. Or rather, two reasons. First and foremost, I'm a noble. On top of that, my aunt is incredibly paranoid about my safety, even though I can very well fend for myself. With all the brigands and whatnot running around the hills, she ordered the guards to make sure I don't slip out of the keep. I hate being trapped here. I'd rather ride around Northwind on my trusty steed, but instead I have to twiddle my thumbs around here.
