# Shalzaru's Lair

A large, twisting [naga](naga) lair south of [Feathermoon Stronghold](Feathermoon Stronghold)'s island at the northern end of the [Isle of Dread](Isle of Dread).  The master of the [Hatecrest](Hatecrest), [Shalzaru](Shalzaru), dwells deep inside.

The cave consists of a main cave and a smaller cave. They are connected by a waterway. The cave is filled with [Hatecrest Sorceress](Hatecrest Sorceress)es and [Hatecrest Serpent Guard](Hatecrest Serpent Guard)s.
