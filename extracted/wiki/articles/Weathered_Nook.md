# Weathered Nook

The **Weathered Nook** was a small, well-hidden cave and home to [Dorn Plainstalker](Dorn Plainstalker) and [Prate Cloudseer](Prate Cloudseer), found on the northern wall of [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles). It was flooded during the cataclysm, it's unknown if Dorn and Prate survived.

## How to locate
Head north from [Freewind Post](Freewind Post) until you reach the cliffs overlooking the needles. Follow the cliffs east until you come to a ramp leading up. This area is known as the Weathered Nook. [53, 41]
