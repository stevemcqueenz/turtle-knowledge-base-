# Master's Cellar

The **Master's Cellar** is an area riddled with [ghost](ghost)s in the southernmost portion of the [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass), just south of [Karazhan](Karazhan). It is below the ruins of a small village, probably the former home of servants that worked in the nearby tower of [Karazhan](Karazhan). 

There are two separate entrances to the Master's Cellar. One leads into a vast wine cellar, filled to the brim with [Unliving Resident](Unliving Resident)s, [Unliving Caretaker](Unliving Caretaker)s, [Damned Soul](Damned Soul)s, [Restless Shade](Restless Shade)s, and [Wailing Spectre](Wailing Spectre)s. [Groundskeeper Jacoby](Groundskeeper Jacoby), who yet still lives and continues to tend to the grounds, hides within the wine cellar and emerges only when not a living soul is looking. (ref:  [60+] [The Mystery of Karazhan V](The Mystery of Karazhan V)) (ref:  [60+] [The Depths of Karazhan V](The Depths of Karazhan V)) The other leads into a similar cellar, but here the floor has collapsed into a cave system leading deep underground. The second cellar and cave system are also filled with the same type of [undead](undead), as well as being the haunting grounds of [Captain Rothynn](Captain Rothynn), once a member of the tower guard.
