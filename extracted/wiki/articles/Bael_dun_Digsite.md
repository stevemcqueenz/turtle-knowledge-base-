# Bael'dun Digsite

The **Bael'dun Digsite** is an excavation site of the [dwarven](Dwarf) [expedition](Bael'dun) based at [Bael Modan](Bael Modan). It is a large quarry dug into the mountainside in the western edge of [Mulgore](Mulgore).33 47 Mulgore (g/s/c) This incursion was against the warnings of [Baine Bloodhoof](Baine Bloodhoof), who saw the excavations as an affront to the [Earth Mother](Earth Mother), and as diplomatic solutions failed, the [Bloodhoof tribe](Bloodhoof tribe) resolved to drive the invaders out by taking and breaking their pickaxes. The dwarves refused to heed the tauren's warnings and their reckless digging infuriated the spirits of the land and killed them.
