**Light's Hope Chapel** is the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn)'s base of operations in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands). Located in the far east of the region, it serves as quest hub for both [Horde](Horde) and [Alliance](Alliance) heroes. [Scourgestones](Scourgestone) may be turned in here for reputation with the Dawn.
