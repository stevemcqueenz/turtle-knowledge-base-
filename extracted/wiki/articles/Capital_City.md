# Capital City

Capital of the Kingdom of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). The city was built by the [Arathor](Arathor) in the height of their power, and was made the capital of a kingdom based in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades) that emerged after the fall of the [Arathor](Arathor) Empire.

Its orginal founders were the aristocratic of the Arathor nation, who had grown weary of worldly attachments such as possessions and power.  These noblemen and lords founded a city in which they could pursue a spiritual life hoping to find a higher calling.  Thus, Lordaeron was founded, and soon after the Order of the [Light](Light) was established.  Uther, a powerful knight who had joined in the pilgrimage to found Lorderon, combined the new Light teachings and his knight training to become the first [Paladin](Paladin).  He then created the [Order of the Silver Hand](Order of the Silver Hand), an organization for the recruitment and training of paladins, which quickly became one of the strongest and most influential groups in the Eastern Kingdoms.

The city was first an industrial centre for the nation, but later, most of the industries were moved east to the new cities of [Stratholme](Stratholme) and [Andorhal](Andorhal), so Lordaeron became solely an administrative centre and was largely remodeled as a wonder of human architecture.

The city was the center of the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron) during the second war, and so prospered as the effective capital of [humanity](humanity). The city watched as the industrial regions of Lordaeron were overrun by plague, and when [Arthas](Arthas) killed his father [King Terenas II](King Terenas II), last true king of Lordaeron, the city fell into turmoil.

The city endured until the [undead](undead) [Scourge](Scourge) swarmed the city, killing the residents. Ever after, the city would be known as the [Ruins of Lordaeron](Ruins of Lordaeron).

After the brief Plagueland civil war, [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner) won back the city as well as the Tirisfal Glades from the scourge.  It currently stands as the capital of the [Undead](Forsaken).
