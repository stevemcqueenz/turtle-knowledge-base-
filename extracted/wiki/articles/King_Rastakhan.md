King **Rastakhan** is the leader of the [Zandalarian](Zandalar tribe) people. When the word of [Hakkar the Soulflayer](Hakkar the Soulflayer)'s presence in [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub) reached the Zandalar tribe in the [South Seas](South Seas), King Rastakhan sent emissaries to [Yojamba Isle](Yojamba Isle) in an effort to recruit help against the resurrected Blood God and his Atal'ai Priests. King Rastakhan remained well-informed of the actions within Zul'Gurub by his envoys, and the greatest heroes of Zul'Gurub were gifted rewards on his behalf for their honor in battle against the Soulflayer and his minions.
