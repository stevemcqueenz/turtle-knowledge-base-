# Naxxramas

----
*Floating above the Plaguelands, the necropolis known as Naxxramas serves as the seat of one of the Lich King's most powerful officers, the dreaded lich Kel'Thuzad. Horrors of the past and new terrors yet to be unleashed are gathering inside the necropolis as the Lich King's servants prepare their assault. Soon the Scourge will march again...*

*NOTE: Naxxramas is opened as part of [The Scourge Invasion](The Scourge Invasion) world event.*
----

- **Level:** 60+

### History
An ancient [Nerubian](Nerubian) ziggurat, Naxxramas was torn free from the ground by agents of the [Lich King](Lich King) to serve as [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad)'s base of operations as he spread the plague throughout [Lordaeron](Lordaeron).

Due to Kel'Thuzad fighting a war against:
- [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden) and the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), who still seek revenge for the [Lich King](Lich King)'s treachery,
- the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) and their demon puppet-masters, 
- the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn), 
- the [Forsaken](Forsaken), who are stealing ghouls to transform into independent, sentient soldiers, and
- the humans of the [Alliance](Alliance) intent on avenging the dead of Lordaeron.

as well as constant incursions of adventurers from every race and nation into the Scourge-controlled Plaguelands on a daily basis, his forces have been severely taxed to maintain the security of his necropolis. But soon the gates of Naxxramas will open, and Kel'Thuzad's new forces will be poised to sweep away all opposition to the Scourge.

# Gaining Entry
To enter Naxxramas, you must become attuned (which you do so through the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn)) and then enter a teleporting spire in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands). Although there is a green raid entrance deep inside [Stratholme](Stratholme), as of 1.11 it is still blocked off by a gate.

The requirements for attuning to Naxxramas will depend on your reputation with the Argent Dawn:

At **Honored**, the following is required:
- 60 Gold 
- 1 Righteous Orb 
- 2 Nexus Crystals 
- 5 Arcane Crystals 

At **Revered**:
- 30 Gold 
- 2 Arcane Crystals 
- 1 Nexus Crystal

Attunement at **Exalted** is free of charge.

# Resistance: Frost, Shadow, and Nature
According to the information from the PTR (public test realm), the beginning parts of 3 of the 4 wings (plague, spider, and abomination) are in fact rich in Nature damage.  This is good news for players who built up their Nature Resist gear in [Ahn'Qiraj](Ahn'Qiraj).  

Players will very likely need a lot of Frost resistance to fight the final bosses in Naxxramas. [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) is a lich, and all liches use a lot of powerful Frost spells. One boss in particular, a frostwyrm called [Sapphiron](Sapphiron), is confirmed to be Frost based.  Frost Resist gear has proven to be rather scarce thus far, so it will be difficult to gear up for these fights. It is rumoured that new armor recipes for frost resistance gear will become available for purchase from the Argent Dawn and the Hydraxxian Waterlords.

Shadow should be the third major resistance required.  Scourge [Death Knight](Death Knight)s and [Necromancer](Necromancer)s use powerful Shadow magic, and it is likely that Kel'Thuzad will have Shadow spells as well.  Shadow Resist gear can be found scattered throughout various instances, so this shouldn't be a problem.

# Dungeon Information
### Structure
Naxxramas is structure like a giant wheel.  Players enter at the centre of the Necropolis, and then choose 1 of 4 wings to progress through:

- Plague Wing - This wing is likely Nature Resist intensive, as players will have to deal with various Diseases.  Creatures known to populate this wing include [bat](bat)s, [gargoyle](gargoyle)s, [bog beast](Bog Beast)s, [slime](slime)s, and [ghoul](ghoul)s.  The final boss is [Loatheb](Loatheb), some sort of fungal monster.

- Spider Wing - This wing is also likely Nature Resist intensive, filled with nasty spider poisons.  Players will, of course, deal with loads of [spider](spider)s, as well as [crypt fiend](Crypt Fiend)s.  The final boss is [Maexxna](Maexxna), the giant spider.

- Abomination Wing - This wing is filled with [abomination](abomination)s, of course.  It is likely to be the most physically intensive wings, as abominations are known to hit rather hard. The slimes there deal heavy nature damage too.  The final boss is the Frankenstein-esque [Thaddius](Thaddius).

- Death Knight Wing - This wing is loaded with [Death Knight](Death Knight)s, known for their combination of powerful Shadow magic and raw melee skills.  The final bosses are the [Four Horsemen](The Four Horsemen).

Once the final bosses of the 4 wings are defeated, players will gain access to the final "wing" of Naxxramas, in which they will encounter the [frost wyrm](Frost Wyrm) [Sapphiron](Sapphiron), and the [lich](lich) [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad).

### Progress
From the recent information discovered, each boss will drop different quest items for [Tier 3](Tier 3) sets (you can see it in the boss section below). Based on the importance of each piece of the armors, seems from easiest to hardest should be: Spider Wing << Abnomination Wing = Plague Wing < DeathKnight Wing.

From some information available at them moment it seems you have to thaw runes, much like you douse flames in [Molten Core](Molten Core), to be able to gain access to the Final Chamber. You have to beat the last boss in each wing to progress up the tower. Once you beat all four wings, you get a chance to fight Sapphiron. After you beat Sapphiron you unlock the top where you can fight Kel'Thuzhad.
