# Jagged Reef

The **Jagged Reef** [68, 11] is the name for the northern coast of [Azshara](Azshara). It consists of stretches of sandy beach, broken up by jets of stone and small inlets of the ocean. Traveling along the shore here is hazardous due to the treacherous rocks and shallows that lie beneath the ocean surface. [Murloc](Murloc)s, [hydra](hydra)s, and [makrura](makrura) inhabit the region, swimming in the shallows or plodding across the beach looking for food.
