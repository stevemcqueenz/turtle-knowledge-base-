## Description
The SI:7 have means of communication that reach us quite fast and believe it or not they are very reliable.

Sparing you any further details you should know that a dwarven caravan that was meant to reach Aerie Peak never made it out of the Wetlands.

The caravan lays in ruins and you can only find the mutilated husks of what used to be dwarves.

Our agents already poked around the zone but failed to find anything, they are currently trying to localize these newly recruited Scarlet zealots.

I'm not very sure how much of a detective you believe yourself to be, but I ask you to take a second look, maybe they missed something.

If you find the recruits, keep in mind that we aren't sure what manner of persuasion the Scarlets used to brainwash them, spare as many as you can and take them to Menethil.

  -First clue**
Before you lies the body of a Dwarf Mountaineer.

  > Gossip: *Examine the body.*

  It appears that this dwarf wasn't only killed but also mutilated, the jaw was missing.

  -Second clue**
The canister was empty, its contents most likely were poured over what was still seemingly aflame.

  > Gossip: *Examine the canister.*

  -Third clue**
You are now certain that the new recruits were the ones to slaughter these dwarves.

The missing jaw however is something that raises your suspicions, your next thought would be to ask the Goblin that camps south of where you are, at the Crossroads if he was the one to sell them the oil.

  > Gossip: *Take Scarlet Recruit's Insignia Ring.*
