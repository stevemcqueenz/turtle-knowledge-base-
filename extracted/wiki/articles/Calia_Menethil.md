Princess **Calia Menethil** was the daughter of King [Terenas Menethil II](Terenas Menethil II) and the older sister of [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil). She was betrothed to Lord [Daval Prestor](Deathwing), the mysterious noble who was to replace the treacherous Lord [Aiden Perenolde](Aiden Perenolde) as the ruler of [Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)), but Prestor disappeared before any final arrangements were made. The fate of Calia following the novel *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)* has never been explained.
