# Shadow Council

The **Shadow Council** is a group of mortal [warlock](warlock (lore))s that serve the [demon](demon)s of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion).

## The First War
When the Demonlord [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden) was first in contact with the [orc](orc)ish warlock [Gul'dan](Gul'dan), he instructed the orc to form the Council in order to secretly rule the clans and spread the use of warlock magic.  In order to cement the Legion's control of the orcs, the Shadow Council summoned [Mannoroth the Destructor](Mannoroth), so that the clan chieftains could drink his blood, damning their race to decades of bloodlust.  Only [Durotan](Durotan) of the [Frostwolf](Frostwolf) clan refused.

[Blackhand the Destroyer](Blackhand) became [warchief](warchief) by the will of the Council, acting as their puppet in the conquests of Draenor and [Stormwind](Stormwind).  It was the warlocks of Shadow Council that created the Draenor side of the dimensional gateway known as the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal).  

King [Llane Wrynn](Llane Wrynn) was assassinated by one of the Shadow Council's finest killers: the half-orc, [Garona](Garona).  Following the death of [Medivh](Medivh), [Gul'dan](Gul'dan) was incapacitated for several weeks.  During this time, [Orgrim Doomhammer](Orgrim Doomhammer) killed Blackhand the Destroyer and seized the mantle of warchief.  He captured and tortured Garona to learn of the existence of the Shadow Council, and sensing it threatened his position, launched an attack on Gul'dan's citadel near the ruins of Stormwind, annihilating the warlocks of the Council.  Their spirits were subsequently imbued into the [death knight](death knight (Second War))s.

## Beyond the Third War
Even in the aftermath of the second invasion of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), servants of the Shadow Council still remain. They now reside at [Jaedenar](Jaedenar) in [Felwood](Felwood).  The order now includes members of all races, provided they possess the ideology of acceptance of the tenets of the Burning Legion.  The hold of the Shadow Council is extensive; they control cell-based organizations throughout [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) including the [Burning Blade](Burning Blade) and [Searing Blade](Searing Blade Clan) cults. Agents of the Council have even infiltrated areas of the orcish capital city of [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar).

At Jaedenar, deep within the ancient [kaldorei](kaldorei) structure that has been converted into the corrupt [Shadow Hold](Shadow Hold), Shadow Lord [Fel'dan](Fel'dan) commanded the Council's demonic activities alongside his [dreadlord](dreadlord) advisor and liaison with the remnants of the Legion, the malevolent [Lord Banehollow](Lord Banehollow). However, agents of the organization known as the [Emerald Circle](Emerald Circle) were recently dispatched to Shadow Hold. Therein, they slew Fel'dan, along with his demonic slaves, and devastated much of the Shadow Council as well. It is strongly suspected that the power vacuum created by the Shadow Lord's death will cause infighting and turmoil within the ranks of the Shadow Council that may ultimately spell the doom of the organization.
