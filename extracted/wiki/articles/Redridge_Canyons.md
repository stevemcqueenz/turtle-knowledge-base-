# Redridge Canyons

The **Redridge Canyons** are a subzone in the northwestern part of the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains). The area is home to the [den](Yowler's Den) of [Yowler](Yowler), leader of the [local gnolls](Redridge pack).
