# Raid Logging (ChronicleClassic)

# Chronicle Classic: Combat Log Analysis for Turtle WoW
## What is Chronicle Classic?
[Chronicle Classic](https://chronicleclassic.com) is a combat log analysis tool built specifically for Classic World of Warcraft on the Turtle WoW server. It parses combat logs uploaded by players and transforms them into detailed performance insights — tracking damage, healing, deaths, and other raid metrics. Whether you're a raid leader reviewing your team's performance or a player looking to improve, Chronicle Classic provides the tools to do so.

## How does it work?
# **Install the companion addon** — Download and install [ChronicleCompanion](https://github.com/Emyrk/ChronicleCompanion). The addon handles combat log collection and ensures accurate data capture during your raids.

# **Create an account** — Sign up at [https://chronicleclassic.com/](https://chronicleclassic.com) using Discord OAuth. No email confirmation is needed.

# **Upload your log** — Navigate to the upload page and submit your combat log file, selecting the appropriate realm. Chronicle will parse the log and split it into individual encounters automatically.

# **View results** — Within moments, your raid appears in the overview. Browse per-encounter breakdowns and explore your raid's performance.

## Features
- **Common Metrics** — Damage, healing, dispels, and more — all the standard raid performance metrics you'd expect.

- **Live Playback** — Watch logs replay in "realtime", stepping through the fight as it happened.

- **Video Sync** — Synchronize a YouTube video with log playback for side-by-side review.

- **Advanced Filtering** — Custom panels, custom views, and powerful filtering to slice your data however you need.

- **Armory & Guild Pages** — Player armory profiles and customizable public guild pages.
