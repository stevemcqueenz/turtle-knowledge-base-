# Isle of Dread

The **Isle of Dread** 30 80 Feralas (g/s/c) is located south of [Feathermoon Stronghold](Feathermoon Stronghold). It is home to many level 60-62 elite [chimaera](chimaera). One notable chimaera, [Lord Lakmaeran](Lord Lakmaeran), is part of the quest line for the [Scepter of the Shifting Sands](Scepter of the Shifting Sands).

The isle is also home to a group of [naga](naga) who dwell in a cave at the northern end. They are led by [Lord Shalzaru](Lord Shalzaru) whom players are asked to kill as a quest for [Feathermoon Stronghold](Feathermoon Stronghold). The cave itself seems to be completely unique in design and goes quite deep — there is a secret underwater passageway that leads into an inner chamber, but it has no apparent special uses besides being the location in which [Lady Szallah](Lady Szallah) resides. The only entrance to the central walley is in the southeastern part of the island - from all other sides the central walley is inacessible due to unyielding cliffs.

## Lore
[Centaur](Centaur)s once allied with a trio of [bronze dragon](bronze dragon)s and were the uncontested rulers of this island. The tileset is called "Barrens".
