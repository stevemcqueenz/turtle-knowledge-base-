# Chronic of the Kul Tiran Admiralty

Dear reader, here you find the unfolding story of the Kul Tiran Navy. All roleplaying activity and member list is recorded here for the future. Our main fandom page you find here: [Kul Tiran Navy](Kul Tiran Navy)

## Chronic
### **Evening at Pig and Whistle**
The Kul Tiran Navy held its first recruitment evening in the Pig and the Whistle bar. A few came to visit and they enjoyed together the perfect Thunder Ale. However in the end of the evening nobody new signed up, but for sure the city is more and more aware of the Kul Tiran presence. However not everyone is happy about this presence. Some elfs were questioning the Kul Tiran loyalty to the Alliance. However they forget that they were the ones pulling out of the Alliance during the Second War. Kul Tiras gave minor help during the Third War, and still maintains the conflict with the Orcs in Kalimdor. The details of the discussion faded in the loud tavern, where deckhands and sailmakers were discussing everyday matters... *and this was the story where the Thunder Ale dealer became the happiest man in one night.*

 

### **A treasure hunt turns to something completely else**
The Kul Tirans sacked a Bloodsail ship where an old map was found. The leadership sent a small search party to Westfall  to find clues to the hidden pirate treasure. Surprisingly they found an unconscious Kul Tiran sailor who they rescued. He was out to fish and got into a big storm, and washed ashore at Westfall. The searching party learned, that the lost fleet of Admiral Caelan wrecked at the Lapidis Island, southwest from Westfall.

### **A courageous task**
After finding Jeff Gordon from Caelan's fleet, the taskmaster decided that they should head through the open sea with their small ship. The surprise was inevitable. The ships from the Fleet they found on the shores, and also a small settlement, what was called Caelan's Rest. After the nice reunion with their kin, they showed respect to the fallen admiral, and spent an evening in the tavern. Stories were shared and they enjoyed the Junglevine wine until it turned dark. Still the newcomers know less from the Lapidis Island.

### Getting know to Lapidis
After a couple weeks of staying the guests get to know the overall picture of the Island. Trolls, Ogres, pirates, and an evil Hydromancer is around. One needs to give answers to the following questions: 

- What are the chances to keep the Kul Tiran presence on the Island? 

- If a rescue fleet can not arrive then, what alternative lay before the small colony? 

- How will they face the dangers in this exotic jungle? 

- Will the light be enough to get them out from isolated position?*

### **Kul Tiran light ceremony**
Every year when the dark and the nights are equally present in a day, the Kul Tirans are holding a light ceremony. Paladins and priest are blessing the fires in the settlements, and the citizens are bringing back the fire back to their homes for the darker part of the year. In this way everyone has the guarding holy light in their home. The navy hosted a small ceremony in Stormwind. Northlanders joined from Lordaeron and some elves. Representatives form the Scarlet Legion and from the Gilnean rebellion took a visit by the holy light. They came not just for the blessing, but also for cooperation. Meetings were held next to the holy flame. Where will these meetings point to? The stars maybe know...

### The Stormwind connections
Two members of the Navy hopped into a meeting with the Silver Covenant, and enjoyed the hospitality of the elves in Little Silvermoon. Some informal exchange with some pointy ear owners who finished up a meeting in the Cutthroat Alley. Another evening in the taverns of Stormwind. The Kul Tirans met with a fine old cloth merchant. Thes merchant call himslef Sternvul, and invited the members of the Navy for some rum. However for one of them the last portion was too much and he ended up under the table, when we woke up left a bill to pay for the others...

## Characters
**Selewkos the Fox:**  A former Highwayman and ex-member of the Stonemasons guild. Moved long away from Alterac, and recruited by the Kul Tiran Navy. His past is unclear apart that he ran a bar in the Old town. His good skills made him a Petty officer in the navy, and he is busy to recruit deckhands from the mob of Stormwind. Responsible of the finances of the Navy.

**Orvil Dawnhaven:** A marine time soldier through and through, knows his way around a ship and has seen his fair share of travels. He gives of a stern exterior and has a uncensored character about him, but gives everyone a fair chance. Weathered over the many voyages he's partaken in across Azeroth with the Kul'Tiran Navy, now stationed in Stormwind Hearty drinker as many sailors before him and can often be found in many of the Inn's around Western Kingdoms in new companies.

**Grankho the Wretched:** Born and bred in Stormwind City, Grankho spent most of his life helping out with all sorts of menial labor in addition to mugging stray passers-by to earn a living, such as it was. A thief with a strange sense of honor, he rarely picks pockets, prefering direct confrotation when in need of stolen coin. Having spent a lot of his life wandering the docks and shores, the increasingly stronger call of the sea has compelled him to try and join the Kul Tiran Navy in the hopes of escaping the life of a miserable street rat.

**Reijo Fletcher:** Holy knight of the Navy from Kul Tiras. Fletcher raised in Bolarus and share the holy light with the fellow deckhands in the time of need. Open heart and loyal to his nation. Part of the Marine corps.

**Béla Kovács:** Carpenter of the Kul Tiran Navy based in Stormwind. Looking for the best wood possible for the Navy.

**Fahren the Stricken:** Fahren is 25 years old and living a quiet life as a dockworker, he kept his head down, and earned his wages through a fair trade. He barely even thought about adventure and freedom anymore, just  the day to day of trying to get by. But that was all to be shaken up. He was tasked with unloading and supervising the resupply of a ship flying the green sails of Kul Tiras. Where, the most divine being he ever laid eyes on, was aboard. A military officer of some sort, a woman, with amber hair, wearing the emerald green tabard of Kul Tiras. Fahren was entranced, utterly fixated. Day in and day out he could think of noone or nothing else. It reignited the spark within him that was lost long ago, to dream of something better, a better life, a hope for the future.

**Fritz Teufel:** A reckless gnome mercenary who is defending Tiragarde Keep. His Gnomish firetraps and different tricks keeping the greenskin attackers away.

**Jeff Gordon:** Navy priest of the Kul Tiran Navy. Stucked on Lapidis Island with the rest of Caelan's fleet. He fulfills ceremonial duties next to the necessary everyday jobs and adventures in the Island.

**Ardghal Ó Cléirgh:** Kul Tiras is not just famous of its raw naval power but for its merchants. The merchants were making this island state big. Ardghal is coming from a merchant dynasty.
