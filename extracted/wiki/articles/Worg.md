# Worg

A **worg** is a variant of a [wolf](wolf).  There are also normal wolves, and the differences between worgs and wolves are very slight. Worgs tend to be larger than wolves and more powerfully built, but they look generally the same.  

[Orc](Orc)s use a larger variety of worgs as their [mount](mount).  The [Scarshield Legion](Scarshield Legion) utilizes worgs as powerful attack dogs, and also possess a pair of two enormous worgs, [Gizrul](Gizrul_the_Slavener) and [Halycon](Halycon).  Worgs are found in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest), the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands), [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire), [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), the[ Burning Steppes]( Burning Steppes), [Shadowfang Keep](Shadowfang Keep), [Felwood](Felwood), and [Duskwood](Duskwood).

This is one of the many names taken from the works of [Tolkien](Tolkien).

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Has medium damage, medium armor, and medium HP.
- Eats only Meat.
- Can learn [Growl](Growl), [Cower](Cower), [Dash](Dash), [Bite](Bite), and their own skill, [Furious Howl](Furious Howl).
- Deathmaw, Slavering Worg, and Bloodaxe Worg are relatively popular for their faster swing speeds.  
- A popular pet, due to a variety of colors, some wolves with fast swing speed, Furious Howl, and Dash.
- Worgs, when tamed as a hunter pet, are in the same category as wolves and have the same skills.
