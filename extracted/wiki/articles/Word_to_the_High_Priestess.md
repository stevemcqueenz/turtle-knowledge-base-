## Objectives
Bring news of the victory at Nendis to Tyrande Whisperwind.

## Description
Narvalis Point stands secure, thanks in no small part to your actions.

The naga presence in Nendis has been broken, and their commanders lie dead. For the first time since Illidan’s invasion, the ruins may finally be free of their grasp. Still, this victory belongs not only to my sentinels.

The High Priestess must be informed.

Tyrande Whisperwind watches over all our people, and she should know what has been accomplished here. The fall of Illidan’s lieutenants is no small matter.Travel to her and deliver this news.

Tell her the Tideblade are broken, and that Narvalis Point still stands vigilant.

## Completion
Lady Serpentia and Ta'jax are dead?

Those names were spoken often during the war against Illidan. Their warbands left scars along this coast that have not yet faded. If they have truly fallen, then one of the last shadows from that time has finally been lifted.

Nendis has been silent for far too long. With the naga driven out, the ruins may finally be reclaimed. Not quickly. Wounds like these take time to heal. But in time… our people will return. Homes will be rebuilt. Temples restored. The forests will hear our voices again.

Moonwhisper Coast was once a place where the kaldorei lived in peace beneath Elune's light. Perhaps, slowly, it may become so again.

You have helped make that future possible, <class>. Know that the sentinels of Narvalis Point have my gratitude… and so do you.
