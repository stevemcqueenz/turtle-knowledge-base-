Lady **Varia Wrynn** was the wife of King [Barathen Wrynn](Barathen Wrynn) of the [kingdom of Azeroth](Stormwind (kingdom)), and the mother of King [Llane Wrynn](Llane Wrynn). She and her son were sent to [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey) following the first orcish assault on [Stormwind Keep](Stormwind Keep). (ref: *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans* manual, [Chronicles of the War in Azeroth](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft:_Orcs_%26_Humans_manual#Chronicles_of_the_War_in_Azeroth)) Her subsequent fate is unknown.
