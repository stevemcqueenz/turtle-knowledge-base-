# Highborne

The upper classes of the [Night Elves](Night Elf) civilization were the favoured servitors of [Queen Azshara](Queen Azshara) whom she called the Quel'dorei ("High Elves" or "**Highborne**"). They were ordered by their Queen to plumb the depths of the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity) for knowledge and discovered primitive magic. The reckless use of magic by the **Highborne** led to their slow corruption and addiction to the power it granted. The ripples sent into the [Twisting Nether](Twisting Nether) attracted the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) to [Azeroth](Azeroth). 

In the titanic war that followed many **Highborne** were seduced by the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) who promised them as much magic as they desired; many gave themselves wholly over to the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) and were transformed into the first [Satyr](Satyr)s. The **Highborne** tried to open a portal for [Sargeras](Sargeras) to  to enter the world, however they were foiled by the efforts of [Malfurion Stormrage](Malfurion Stormrage) and many **Highborne** were killed and smashed to the sea floor with [Queen Azshara](Queen Azshara) and transformed into the [Naga](Naga). 

A few **Highborne** survived and made their way with the rest of the [Night Elves](Night Elves) to [Mount Hyjal](Mount Hyjal) where they helped rebuild the [Night Elven](Night Elf) civilization. However, many felt the pangs of their magical addiction and were tempted to use the power of the new [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity). After recklessly attempting to convince the part of [Night Elf](Night Elf) society to use magic by unleashing a terrible storm upon [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), the Highborne were exiled. The exiles landed in [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) and became the [High Elves](High Elves); they remembered the mistakes of the past and used [Runestones](Runestones) to shield the use of their magic from the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) and practiced magic with more care than their ancestors. 

After the destruction of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas), a group of [High Elves](High Elf) embraced their **Highborne** ancestry and resumed the reckless use of magic, including demonic magic. They named themselves the [Blood Elves](Blood Elves) and fled to [Outland](Outland) with [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan).
