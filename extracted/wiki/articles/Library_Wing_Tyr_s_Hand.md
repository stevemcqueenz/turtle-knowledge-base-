# Library Wing (Tyr's Hand)

The **Library Wing** of [Tyr's Hand Abbey](Tyr's Hand Abbey), located in [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand) in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), is home to numerous members of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) who seek knowledge and enlightenment in the tomes of religious propaganda and insanity that can be found here. It is situated south of the [Main Hall](Main Hall (Tyr's Hand)), and the [Hall of Arms](Hall of Arms (Tyr's Hand)) lies on the opposite side of the abbey.
