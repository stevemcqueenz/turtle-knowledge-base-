**SI:7**, also known as **Stormwind Intelligence** (ref: [quest: class=rogue]) or **Stormwind Intelligence Agency**, (ref: [https://web.archive.org/web/20051031092226/http://www.worldofwarcraft.com/info/underdev/]) is a covert Alliance organization. It includes [rogue](rogue)s specializing in special operations, assassination and stealth tactics. They are currently led by [Master Mathias Shaw](Master Mathias Shaw) and his second in command, [Renzik "The Shiv"](Renzik "The Shiv"). SI:7 is located in the [Old Town](Old Town) region of Stormwind. They have operatives all throughout the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms) who carry out secret missions for Stormwind, such as [Flint Shadowmore](Flint Shadowmore).

## History
From their base of operations in the [barracks](SI:7 (Stormwind City)) of [Old Town](Old Town), the SI:7 underwent reconnaissance efforts for many agents regarding matters as far apart as the [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood) to the [Undead](Undead) [Scourge](Scourge). In order to help Alliance heroes hunt down the Defias who sought to destroy [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) from within, [Master Mathias Shaw](Master Mathias Shaw) directed SI:7 reconnaissance operations on Defias activities, and later sent [Agent Kearnen](Agent Kearnen) to [Westfall](Westfall) to spy on [Klaven's Tower](Klaven's Tower). (ref: [quest: class=rogue])

With SI:7 having its hands full with the Defias in Westfall, Mathias could afford only to send a single agent, [Operative Baltimore Hawthorne](Operative Baltimore Hawthorne), to [Northwind](Northwind) to investigate a case of missing people. (ref: [quest: Empty Houses]) However, upon learning that the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) was involved in the abductions, Mathias sent a task force led by Operative [Verona Gillian](Verona Gillian) to the island of [Balor](Balor) (ref: [quest: Into The Tortured Past]) to scout the island and uncover the Stormreaver's plans. (ref: [quest: Assessing The Situation])

At some point, Mathias received some disturbing reports of unusual [Forsaken](Forsaken) activity in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands) and sent five agents to investigate. Only one of those agents returned, babbling incoherently about "Nathanos" and the word "Blightcaller." He died in his sleep just days after his return, and Shaw sent Alliance adventurers to investigate their disappearance. (ref: [quest: Honor the Dead]) The only thing SI:7 had to go on was that [Nathanos Marris](Nathanos Marris) may have been slain by this "Blightcaller," but the truth was far less pleasant. After investigating the grounds around the Marris Stead, adventurers reported to the Spymaster the ugly reality - Nathanos was the Blightcaller, now a champion of the Banshee Queen. (ref: [quest: The Eastern Plagues]) (ref: [quest: The Blightcaller Cometh])
