# South Tide's Run

**South Tide's Run** is complementary to [North Tide's Run](North Tide's Run), and is the name for the southwestern coast of [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest) bordering [the Great Sea](the Great Sea). Although not as famous as the northern coast, which is the site of a sunken ship and of great significance to [tauren](tauren) [druid](druid)s, **South Tide's Run** used to be a popular bathing spot for the humans of Gilneas and Pyrewood.
