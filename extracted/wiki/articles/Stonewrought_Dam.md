# Stonewrought Dam

The massive [Stonewrought Dam](Stonewrought Dam) was built by the [dwarves](dwarves) of [Ironforge](Ironforge) and is one of the most impressive wonders of the world. The construction dams up the loch, more than doubling its original size. The dam also creates the flooded plains of the [Wetlands](Wetlands), which previously were mostly underwater. Recently the dam has come under attack by the dwarves of the [Dark Iron Clan](Dark Iron Clan) and the dwarves of Ironforge have been forced to increase security at the dam.
