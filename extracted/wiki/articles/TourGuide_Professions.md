# TourGuide Professions

-TourGuide Professions** is a profession leveling guide. It is designed to help players efficiently level their in-game professions—such as Tailoring, Leatherworking, Engineering, Enchanting, Cooking, and Blacksmithing—by providing step-by-step instructions.

## Installation
  -GitAddonsManager**

The easiest way to keep **TourGuide Professions** up to date is by using [GitAddonsManager](GitAddonsManager).

# **Add the Repository:** Add the following URL to GitAddonsManager: https://github.com/cralor/TourGuide_Professions.git
# **Select the Branch:** Ensure that the master branch is selected.
# **Update:** Using GitAddonsManager, you can check for and install updates for all your AddOns with a single click.

  -Manual Installation**

If you prefer manual installation, follow these steps:

# Go to the main page of the repository.
# Click the **<> Code** dropdown and download the repository as a <code>.zip</code>.
# Unpack the <code>.zip</code> and rename the folder to <code>TourGuide_Professions</code>, removing the <code>-master</code> suffix.
# Move the folder into your <code>Interface/AddOns</code> directory and restart the game.

  -Important:** This addon requires [TourGuide](TourGuide Turtle) to work.

## Usage
- The guide list window can be toggled by right-clicking on the TourGuide then click the Guides button.
- Follow the instructions provided in each step for efficient leveling.
- The guide includes quest turn-ins, vendor purchases, and crafting recipes.

## Tailoring 1-300
  -Materials Needed (approximate):**

160x Linen Cloth, 200x Wool Cloth, 760x Silk Cloth, 520x Mageweave Cloth, 900x Runecloth, 120x Rugged Leather, Coarse Thread, Fine Thread, Dyes, Silken Thread, Heavy Silken Thread, Rune Thread

  -Key Steps:**

- 1-50: Linen Cloth, Linen Bags, Reinforced Linen Cape
- 75-105: Woolen Cloth, Woolen Shirts, Shoulders
- 125-160: Silk Cloth, Silk Hood, Headband, White Shirt
- 185-200: Mageweave Cloth, Silk Vest, Pantaloons
- 215-250: Mageweave, Black Mageweave Leggings, Gloves, Headband/Shoulders
- 260-300: Runecloth, Runecloth Belt, Runecloth Gloves

## Leatherworking 1-300
  -Materials Needed (approximate):**

275x Light Leather, Medium Heavy Leather, Heavy Leather, Rugged Leather, Thick Leather, Turtle Scale, Wildvine, Leather Belts, Boots, Bracers

  -Key Steps:**

- 1-150: Light Leather, Cured Leather, Embossed Leather, Dark Leather, Heavy Leather, Leather Belts, Shoulders, Legs
- 150-250: Thick Leather, Rugged Leather, Nightscape Headband/Tunic, Pants
- 250-300: Wicked Leather, Leather Headband, Bracers, Boots

## Engineering 1-300
  -Materials Needed (approximate):**

90x Rough Stone, Copper, Silver, Bronze, Iron, Steel, Mithril, Thorium, Dense Stone, Worn Dragonscale, Scorpid Scale

  -Key Items & Recipes:**

- Basic: Blasting Powder, Copper Tubes, Bronze Tubes, Small/Bombs, Mithril Tubes, Triggers, Casings, Gyro-Shots, Thorium Shells
- Advanced: Core of Earth, Heart of Fire, Breath of Wind, Thorium Bars, Thorium Plans (visit Gadgetzan for plans)

## Enchanting 1-300
  -Materials Needed (approximate):**

150x Strange Dust, 90x Soul Dust, 170x Vision Dust, 300x Dream Dust, Illusion Dust, Various Rods, Greater Eternal, Pearls, Gems

  -Key Steps:**

- 1-75: Runed Copper Rod, Minor Enchantments
- 75-150: Enchant Bracers, Shields, Circlets
- 150-250: Greater Enchantments, Cloaks, Gloves, Chests
- 250-300: Higher-level enchants, buy Lesser Mana Oil from vendors in Silithus, etc.

## Cooking 1-300
  -Materials Needed (approximate):**

Various meats, spices, herbs, and special ingredients from different zones.

  -For Horde:**

- 1-40: Spice Bread, Bear Meat, Smoked Bear Meat
- 90-125: Dig Rats, Dig Rat Stew, Raptor Flesh, Roast Raptor
- 200-250: Spider Meat, Spider Sausage, Zesty Clam Meat, Tender Wolf Steaks, Monster Omelet
- 285-300: Sandworm Meat, Smoked Desert Dumplings (kill Dredge Crushers/Strikers in Silithus)

  -For Alliance:**

- 1-40: Spice Bread, Bear Meat, Smoked Bear Meat
- 75-85: Crawler Meat, Crab Cake
- 85-130: Seasoned Wolf Kabobs, Stormwind Herbs
- 130-175: Raptor Eggs, Curiously Tasty Omelet, Roast Raptor
- 200-250: Spider Meat, Spider Sausage, Zesty Clam Meat, Tender Wolf Steaks
- 285-300: Sandworm Meat, Smoked Desert Dumplings

## Blacksmithing 1-300
  -Materials Needed (approximate):**

90x Rough Stone, Copper, Bronze, Iron, Steel, Mithril, Thorium, Dense Stone, Cores of Earth, Mageweave, Rugged Leather

  -Key Items & Recipes:**

- Basic: Sharpening Stones, Grinding Stones, Copper & Bronze Gear
- Advanced: Iron & Steel Plate, Mithril Coif, Earthforged & Emberforged gear, Thorium plans (visit Gadgetzan for Imperial Plate plans)

## Usage Tips:
- Follow each step for efficient farming and crafting.
- Purchase recipes from vendors when available.
- Kill specific mobs to gather materials.
- Use the guide as a checklist for materials and steps.
- Transition between zones using the "F" lines to optimize leveling.
