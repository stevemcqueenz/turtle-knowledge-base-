# Thalassian

The ancient language of [High Elves](High Elves), a derivative of the [Darnassian](Darnassian) language of the Night Elves. [Sindassi](Sindassi) is derived from it, and was altered in order to prevent Alliance Thalassian linguists from learning their secrets.
