# Grosh'gok Compound

The **Grosh'gok Compound** is an [ogre mound](ogre mound) located in the [Vice](Vice) inhabited by the [Deadwind ogres](Deadwind ogres). It is the southernmost and largest of the multiple caves in the Vice.
