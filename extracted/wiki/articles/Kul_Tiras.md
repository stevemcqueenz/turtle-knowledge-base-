# Kul Tiras

*This article is about the island itself. For the human kingdom, see [Kul Tiras (kingdom)](Kul Tiras (kingdom)). For the in-game reputation faction, see [Kul Tiras (faction)](Kul Tiras (faction)).*
**Kul Tiras** (also spelled **Kul'Tiras** (ref: [quest: Lightforge Iron]), pronounced KOOL-teer-AHS) (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* and *Beyond the Dark Portal* mission briefings. See http://www.youtube.com/watch?&v=hTjGagw64KI) is a large island off [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan)'s west coast, within the [Baradin Bay](Baradin Bay). It sits between [Azeroth](Azeroth (continent)) and [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). The island is home to [the human nation](Kul Tiras (kingdom)) of the same name.
