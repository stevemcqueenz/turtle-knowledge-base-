# Quagmire

**The Quagmire** is the name given to a large section of marshland located in central [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh). It stretches from [Lost Point](Lost Point) in the west to [Dustwallow Bay](Dustwallow Bay) in the east, and from [Brackenwall Village](Brackenwall Village) in the north to the [Dragonmurk](Dragonmurk) in the south. [Beezil's Wreck](Beezil's Wreck), the [Deserter's Hideout](Deserter's Hideout) and [Tabetha's Farm](Tabetha's Farm) are also located within the Quagmire. It is inhabited by dangerous [crocolisk](crocolisk)s, Theramore deserters, [spider](spider)s and [ooze](ooze)s that live throughout the region.
