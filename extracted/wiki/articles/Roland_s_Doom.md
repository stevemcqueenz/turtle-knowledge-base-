# Roland's Doom

-Roland's Doom** is a mine located in southeastern [Duskwood](Duskwood), due east of the [Rotting Orchard](Rotting Orchard). It is here that the [night elven](Night elf) [priestess](Priestess of the Moon) [Velinde Starsong](Velinde Starsong), the former wielder of the [Scythe of Elune](Scythe of Elune), disappeared. Interacting with the [mound of dirt](Mound of Dirt (Duskwood)) there can invoke an echo of her. (ref:  [30] [The Scythe of Elune (quest)](The Scythe of Elune (quest))) (ref:  [30] [Answered Questions](Answered Questions)) One day, [Jitters](Jitters) found the Scythe, summoning more [worgen](worgen) and in time the [Dark Riders](Dark Riders) upon [Duskwood](Duskwood) and [Darkshire](Darkshire), condemning them to permanent darkness. The mine is now the location of [Gutspill](Gutspill) and [Nefaru](Nefaru), who lead the worgen at the [Rotting Orchard](Rotting Orchard).
