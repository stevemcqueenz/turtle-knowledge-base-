# Xoroth

One of the firey, chaotic worlds belonging to the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), or more specifically, the [Nathrezim](Nathrezim) (Dreadlords). This is home to the infernal [Dreadsteed](Dreadsteed)s, mounts to the Dreadlords. They are owned by [Lord Hel'nurath](Lord Hel'nurath), the "stable keeper". You will have to summon a Dreadsteed in a quest for a [warlock](warlock)'s [epic](epic) [mount](mount) by opening a portal to this world in [Dire Maul](Dire Maul).
