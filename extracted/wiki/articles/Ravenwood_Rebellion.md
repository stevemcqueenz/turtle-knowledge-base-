The **Ravenwood Rebellion** (ref: [quest: The Brigade is Coming Home]) is a group of Gilnean rebels formed around the time of the end of the Third War. Led by [Lord Darius Ravenwood](Lord Darius Ravenwood), they fought the Greymane loyalists during the [Gilnean civil war](Gilnean civil war), which occurred as a result of King [Genn Greymane](Genn Greymane)'s decisions to leave the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron), to isolate his kingdom from the world behind the Greymane Wall, and further instigated to divide and weaken the people of the human nation as part of a plot schemed by [Celia](Regent-Lady Celia Harlow) and [Mortimer Harlow](Regent-Lord Mortimer Harlow).
