-Master Mathias Shaw** is the leader of [SI:7](SI:7) and master of all assassins, (ref: [quest: The Head of the Beast]) and is also a member or presumably the leader of the [Stormwind Assassin's Guild](Stormwind Assassin's Guild). (ref:  His second in command is the [goblin](goblin) [Renzik](Renzik "The Shiv").<ref>[quest: SI:7|class=rogue])

## Biography
Mathias Shaw was a childhood friend of [Edwin VanCleef](Edwin VanCleef), and personally trained him as a rogue, hoping that one day he might consider a career alongside him. (ref: 

From his base of operations in the [barracks](SI:7 (Stormwind City)) of [Old Town](Old Town), as the head of the [SI:7](SI:7), he coordinated reconnaissance efforts for many agents regarding matters as far apart as the [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood) to the [Undead](Undead) [Scourge](Scourge). In order to help Alliance heroes hunt down the Defias who sought to destroy [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) from within, he directed SI:7 reconnaissance operations on Defias activities, and later sent [Agent Kearnen](Agent Kearnen) to [Westfall](Westfall) to spy on [Klaven's Tower](Klaven's Tower).<ref>[quest: class=rogue])

With SI:7 having its hands full with the Defias in Westfall, he could afford only to send a single agent, [Operative Baltimore Hawthorne](Operative Baltimore Hawthorne), to [Northwind](Northwind) to investigate a case of missing people. (ref: [quest: Empty Houses]) However, upon learning that the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) was involved in the abductions, he sent a task force led by Operative [Verona Gillian](Verona Gillian) to the island of [Balor](Balor) (ref: [quest: Into The Tortured Past]) to scout the island and uncover the Stormreaver's plans. (ref: [quest: Assessing The Situation])

At some point, Mathias received some disturbing reports of unusual [Forsaken](Forsaken) activity in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands) and sent five agents to investigate. Only one of those agents returned, babbling incoherently about "Nathanos" and the word "Blightcaller." He died in his sleep just days after his return, and Shaw sent Alliance adventurers to investigate their disappearance. (ref: [quest: Honor the Dead]) The only thing SI:7 had to go on was that [Nathanos Marris](Nathanos Marris) may have been slain by this "Blightcaller," but the truth was far less pleasant. After investigating the grounds around the Marris Stead, adventurers reported to the Spymaster the ugly reality - Nathanos was the Blightcaller, now a champion of the Banshee Queen. (ref: [quest: The Eastern Plagues]) (ref: [quest: The Blightcaller Cometh])

When Mathias' agents reported a [Scarlet](Scarlet Crusade) caravan passing through various towns, he assumed that the remaining Scarlet leadership following the events that transpired in the Scarlet Monastery, Hearthglen and Stratholme has realized that they've been running short on men, and covertly entrusted [a letter](Sealed Letter) and [his ring](Mathias Shaw's Ring) to [Crier Goodman](Crier Goodman) to pass on to trusted Alliance adventurers in order to recruit them in investigating the situation. (ref: [quest: A Particular Letter]) (ref: [quest: The Elusive SI:7]) (ref: [quest: Young and Foolish])

Mathias owed [Baros Alexston](Baros Alexston) a favor, but it is unknown what for. (ref: [quest: Shadow of the Past])
