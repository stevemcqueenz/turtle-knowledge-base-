The green, verdant plains of **Mulgore** are home to the noble [tauren](tauren). Mulgore is nestled in the foothills of the [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains) to the north, and protected by a natural wall of mountains on all sides. The only passes through these mountains leads into the [Barrens](Barrens) to the east.

A land of windswept mesas and grassy plains, Mulgore provides bounty for the hunt, as a diversity of wild beasts roam the rolling plains and climb the foothills of the surrounding range. In the northern section of Mulgore, the mesas of [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff) tower above the plains, casting long shadows. [Bloodhoof Village](Bloodhoof Village) is centrally located surrounded by the clear waters of [Stonebull Lake](Stonebull Lake), and further south on the protected cliffs of [Red Cloud Mesa](Red Cloud Mesa), [Camp Narache](Camp Narache) stands as the principal training camp for all young tauren.

Despite the serene landscape and the pine-scented breeze, Mulgore is fraught with trouble. [Brambleblade Ravine](Brambleblade Ravine) and the sacred [Red Rocks](Red Rocks) are overrun by [quilboar](quilboar)s. The [goblin](goblin)-run [Venture Company](Venture Company) infests the three sacred water wells of Mulgore as well as their [Venture Co. Mine](Venture Co. Mine) in the eastern mountain face. The [Windfury](Windfury harpies) harpy tribe lay claim to the extreme northern reaches of Mulgore and the southeast mountain face. The [Alliance](Alliance) makes its presence known at the [Bael'dun Digsite](Bael'dun Digsite), where [dwarves](Dwarf) scour the mountains for traces of their shrouded ancestry, and a tribe of [gnolls](gnolls) known as the [Palemane](Palemane Gnolls) make their home at the cave called [Palemane Rock](Palemane Rock) as well as scattered camps along the southern mountains.

## Geography
Mulgore is a sheltered and pastoral valley surrounded by mountains on all sides, and accessible only through a pass to the southeast. Resembling a huge pasture, the area is covered with verdant green grass and few trees. An oddity of the landscape, the tall cliffs of [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff) tower over the fields in the center of the zone.

It is possible for non-[tauren](tauren) to explore [Red Cloud Mesa](Red Cloud Mesa) without actually running all the way up. By hugging the cliff below, the area will appear on the map.

Mulgore contains no dungeons of any type and no battlegrounds. [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff), the Horde aligned capital city for [tauren](tauren), is the only travel hub.
