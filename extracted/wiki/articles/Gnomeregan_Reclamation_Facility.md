The **Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility** is the result of [gnomekind](Gnome)'s collective efforts to reclaim their city. Located in [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), it is the fruit of gnomekind's collective efforts as it serves as the new home for the [Gnomeregan Exiles](Gnomeregan Exiles) and a new primary home and a step forward for [Gnomeregan](Gnomeregan)'s reclamation. (ref: [New Zones & World Edits](https://web.archive.org/web/20260514170802/https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps). Archived from [the original](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps) on 2026-05-14. Retrieved on 2026-29-08.)

Former residents of [Tinker Town](Tinker Town) in [Ironforge](Ironforge) have relocated here, along with new gnomes joining the cause. The facility provides quests for reclaiming Gnomeregan, including a section overrun by toxic components.
