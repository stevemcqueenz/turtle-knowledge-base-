# Faerie Dragon

Small flying lizards. They look much like a chameleon with butterfly wings. Though not actual Dragons, these mystical looking creatures police the [Emerald Dream](Emerald Dream) from magic. 

They have long been used by the [Night Elves](Night Elves) in their endeavours.

  -Also known as:**
- Sprite
- Blink dragon

Alliance players can get a [Sprite Darter Hatchling](Sprite Darter Hatchling) as a novelty pet.
