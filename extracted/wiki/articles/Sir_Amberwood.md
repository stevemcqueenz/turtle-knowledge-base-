**Sir Nathaniel Amberwood** is a [human](human) located at the [Jousting Grounds](Jousting Grounds) in [Northwind](Northwind). He commands the [Northwind Guard](Northwind Guard), the region's military force. Though he is the older brother of the two, he did not inherit rulership of Northwind, (ref: [The Nobles of Northwind](The Nobles of Northwind)) as his father chose Percival to lead instead. This has led to Nathaniel growing cold and brazen, and he plotted against his forgiving brother to gain the support of the House of Nobles in order to overthrow Percival, (ref:  [28] [In Amber Disgust](In Amber Disgust)) having ordered Northwind's military forces to oversee the jousting tournament outside [Amberwood Keep](Amberwood Keep) at the behest of [Lady Katrana Prestor](Lady Katrana Prestor) so that the other nobles of Northwind would see him as incompetent for being unable to bring the situation under control. (ref: [Sir Amberwood's Report](Sir Amberwood's Report))
