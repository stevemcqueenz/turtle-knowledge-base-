The **Mosh'Ogg Ogre Mound** is an [ogre mound](ogre mound) inhabited by the [Mosh'Ogg](Mosh'Ogg) tribe of [ogre](ogre)s located in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), just south of [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub). Deep inside the mound, their leader, the [ogre mage](ogre mage) [Mai'Zoth](Mai'Zoth) holds a powerful relic called the [Mind's Eye](Mind's Eye).
