**Liam Greymane** is the son and heir of King [Genn Greymane](Genn Greymane) of [Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)).

## Biography
Prince Liam, along with Lords [Darius Ravenwood](Darius Ravenwood) and [Gerold](Gerold) of [Gelnor](Gelnor), formed the [Gilneas Brigade](Gilneas Brigade) to ready a force for Lady [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore)'s [Human Expedition](Human Expedition) to [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) in defiance of his father's wishes. Named the Gilneas Brigade by Prince Liam with full honors on the Gilnean shores, the plot to send troops to the Human Expedition was only discovered by King Genn long after the ships, soldiers and supplies were far past the horizon. Harsh punishment fell upon all the plotters, the Crown Prince included, but the warriors found their way into the service of Jaina Proudmoore regardless. (ref: 

At the height of the corruption of the Harlow regime, King Genn was convinced to send his son and daughter away to Kul Tiras along with Alteraci claimant Isiden Perenolde. In truth, the three were held captive by the Harlow regime at the [Dryrock Pit](Dryrock Pit), with Genn having been led to believe that the three had died at sea. Liam helped his sister escape, but was thrown in the [Dryrock Mine](Dryrock Mine) as a consequence.<ref>[quest: One Chain At a Time]) [Baron Caliban Silverlaine](Baron Caliban Silverlaine) tasked adventurers with finding and freeing Liam.

While Liam was on his way to [Ravenshire](Ravenshire), he stopped by the [Hollow Web Cemetery](Hollow Web Cemetery) to pay his respects at his mother's grave. There, he was offered shelter by [Moranna Rosenberg](Moranna Rosenberg) and the [Lupine Coven](Lupine Coven) before continuing on his way to Ravenshire, and he sought to recruit them to the rebellion. (ref: [quest: Dim Light in the Darkness])

With his father's death in the Gilnean civil war and the retrieval of the Greymane crown by the Ravenwood Rebellion, Liam was crowned as the new king of Gilneas.
