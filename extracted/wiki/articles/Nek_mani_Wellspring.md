# Nek'mani Wellspring

*"In the land to the south where vines twist and creep / Lies a hidden well where the water runs deep / Pure as the Light's sacred Daughter / Bring to me now some Holy Spring Water."*
   — [Grimbooze Thunderbrew](Grimbooze Thunderbrew) (ref: [quest: Sweet Amber])

The **Nek'mani Wellspring** is a sacred spring located high in the cliffs overlooking the [Southern Savage Coast](Southern Savage Coast) in southwestern [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). The spring is the subject of great conflict among religious scholars, since every race in [Azeroth](Azeroth (continent)) claims that the waters were made divine for their people. An expedition of naga explorers have recently advanced on the spring. (ref: [quest: Mok'thardin's Enchantment (4)])
