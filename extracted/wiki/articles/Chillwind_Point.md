# Chillwind Point

-Chillwind Point** is a small rise on the far eastern edge of the [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains) region.  It is dominated by a small, ancient [troll](troll) ruin and named for the chill winds that sweep through the nearby ravine from the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands) to the north. Chillwind Point is often mistaken for [Chillwind Camp](Chillwind Camp) located just to the north, an [Alliance](Alliance) outpost just inside the plaguelands. This pass was previously part of the kingdom of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron).

[Bath'rah the Windwatcher](Bath'rah the Windwatcher), a quest giver, resides here.
