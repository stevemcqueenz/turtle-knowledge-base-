The **Warpwood Quarter** (ref: [quest: Wrapping Warpwood]) is the eastern instance in [Dire Maul](Dire Maul). It has been taken over by the nefarious [Alzzin the Wildshaper](Alzzin the Wildshaper), a satyr who has corrupted the [Fruit of Fertility](Fruit of Fertility) so that it produces vile [Felvine Shard](Felvine Shard)s. Alzzin has attracted all manner of corrupted plant life, satyrs, [mage](mage)s and [demon](demon)s to his cause. The entire district is overgrown with crazed plant life.

The main instance portal can be found in the [Broken Commons](Broken Commons) of Dire Maul, but a hidden back entrance is located beneath the [Lariss Pavilion](Lariss Pavilion) in eastern [Feralas](Feralas). This back door can only be accessed with the [Crescent Key](Crescent Key).

The area is so named for its numerous Warpwood [ancient](ancient)s, but it also contains many [lasher](lasher)s, as well as the annoying [imp](imp) [Pusillin](Pusillin), who will set off on a wild chase through the entire area, and eventually into [the Hidden Reach](the Hidden Reach). [The Conservatory](The Conservatory) lies in the middle of the quarter, and dropping down from the edge is safe as long as there are no aggressive mobs at the bottom, and the jumper is at full health.
