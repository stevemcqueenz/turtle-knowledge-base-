The **Eastern Kingdoms** (occasionally called **Azeroth** or the **Old World**) are the eastern continents on the [world of Azeroth](Azeroth). The Eastern Kingdoms is made up from a group of smaller continents ([Azeroth](Azeroth (continent)), [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) and [Lordaeron](Lordaeron)) formed from the original continent of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor (landmass)) following the [Great Sundering](Great Sundering). The Eastern Kingdoms lie to the east of the [Great Sea](Great Sea) and to the west of the [Forbidding Sea](Forbidding Sea). The central part of the Eastern Kingdoms is dominated by the mountains of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) and the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains). Both cover several zones. The Eastern Kingdom's western counterpart is the continent of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor).

The Eastern Kingdoms include the three continents of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan), and [Azeroth](Azeroth (continent)). The Eastern Kingdoms are the homes of the [undead](undead), [dwarves](Dwarf), [gnomes](Gnome), [humans](Human), and [high elves](High elf).
