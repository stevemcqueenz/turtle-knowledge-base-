# Kurzen's Compound

**Kurzen's Compound** is an unusual human settlement located east of the [Rebel Camp](Rebel Camp) in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). The humans here are led by [Colonel Kurzen](Colonel Kurzen), and attack both [Alliance](Alliance) and [Horde](Horde). 

The inhabitants of the Rebel Camp were once members of the compound before rebelling after Kurzen descended into madness. They are embroiled in a deadly conflict with the jungle fighters. It has a [lumber mill](lumber mill) and at the back of the Compound is a non-instanced dungeon, the [Stockpile](Stockpile), where the fighters keep their equipment. The men also keep [Panther](Panther)s and [Stranglethorn Tiger](Stranglethorn Tiger)s in cages near the [stables](stables) so they can be trained to become [Kurzen War Panther](Kurzen War Panther)s and [Kurzen War Tiger](Kurzen War Tiger)s.
