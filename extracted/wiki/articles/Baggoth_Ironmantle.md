-Baggoth Ironmantle** was the former marshal of [Dun Kithas](Dun Kithas) and a renowned architect who built the town's [Council Hall](Dun Kithas Magistrate), as well as the fortifications of [Baggoth's Wall](Baggoth's Wall) and [Baggoth's Rampart](Baggoth's Rampart). During the [Second War](Second War), Baggoth took charge of the defense of the Grim Reaches against the [Dragonmaw clan](Dragonmaw clan), defeating the orcs in many battles until he was felled by an orcish arrow during the Dragonmaw's retreat from the Grim Reaches. His personal banner was adopted by the [Wildhammer clan](Wildhammer clan) as their standard.
