# Azeroth (disambiguation)

Throughout the *Warcraft* universe sources, the term **Azeroth** is used to describe several things:

- **[Azeroth, the world](Azeroth)** - The name of the planet where most of the action of *World of Warcraft* takes place.
- **[Azeroth, the kingdom](Stormwind (kingdom))** - One of the [Seven Kingdoms](Seven Kingdoms), also called the Kingdom of Stormwind.
- **[Azeroth, the continent](Azeroth (continent))** - One of the three continents that comprise the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms). (ref: [Archived page](http://web.archive.org/web/20030210065238/http://www.battle.net:80/war3/orc/))
- **[Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms)** - In some sources the term "Azeroth" is used interchangeable with the Eastern Kingdoms. (ref:  [40] [Dark Council](Dark Council)) (ref: *[World of Warcraft: Game Manual#Trolls](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Game_Manual#Trolls)*)
