# Northgate Tower

-Northgate Tower** is an abandoned guard tower located near the crossroads between the [Greymane Wall](Greymane Wall), [Greyshire](Ruins of Greyshire) and [Ravenshire](Ravenshire) in [Gilneas](Gilneas). It is currently overrun by worgen of the Nighthowl pack.
