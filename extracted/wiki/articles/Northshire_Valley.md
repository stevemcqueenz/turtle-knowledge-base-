# Northshire Valley

**Northshire Valley** is a pristine valley in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest) in which both [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey) and the [Northshire Vineyards](Northshire Vineyards) are located. This is where the [human](human) starting area is located, though the serene valley is visited by adventurers from all over the world. Northshire Valley is surrounded by impenetrable mountains, with the exception of a pass to the south which is protected by thick stone walls and a guarded entryway. A [river](Northshire River) runs from [Crystal Falls](Crystal Falls) through the valley, which eventually exits Northshire and empties into [Crystal Lake](Crystal Lake).

The guards of Stormwind have trouble maintaining the peace in Northshire Valley, due to an increase in activity from various threats. [Deputy Willem](Deputy Willem) notes an increase in the number of [wolves](wolf) inhabiting the woods (having fled here from Duskwood), (ref: [Marshal McBride's Documents](Marshal McBride's Documents)) [Marshal McBride](Marshal McBride) reports a faction of [kobold](kobold)s infesting the areas in and around [Echo Ridge Mine](Echo Ridge Mine), and [Milly Osworth](Milly Osworth) fears for her vineyard at the hands of [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood) thugs.
