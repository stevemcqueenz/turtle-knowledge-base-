# Shadowthread Cave

[Shadowthread Cave](Shadowthread Cave) 56.7 32.0 Teldrassil (g/s/c) is in the northwest corner of the [Shadowglen](Shadowglen) in [Teldrassil](Teldrassil).  While it has always been home to the great [spider](spider)s of Teldrassil, recently the spiders have become increasingly hostile and the cave much more dangerous. The largest spider in the Shadowthread Cave is named [Githyiss the Vile](Githyiss the Vile), a particularly nasty specimen that has been the bane of more than one inexperienced Kaldorei.
