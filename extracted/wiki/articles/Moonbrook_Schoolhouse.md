# Moonbrook Schoolhouse

The **Moonbrook Schoolhouse** is a building located in [Moonbrook](Moonbrook) around southern [Westfall](Westfall) and looks somewhat like a town hall. There is a cemetery behind it. It is the building in north-westernmost Moonbrook.

Adventurers are sent to the schoolhouse to find a letter in a footlocker that appeared in the main room of the ground floor, just short of the elevated part. (ref: [quest: The Legend of Stalvan]) It was revealed that the schoolhouse was built specifically for [Stalvan Mistmantle](Stalvan Mistmantle), (ref: [A Dusty Unsent Letter](A Dusty Unsent Letter)) who eventually became its headmaster. (ref: [An Undelivered Letter](An Undelivered Letter))
