# Marshlands

The Marshlands is a subzone in the Southeastern portion of [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater) inhabited by very large [raptors](raptors). The entrance to Un'goro from [Tanaris](Tanaris) is on the cliff wall here.
