# Tidemaster

The ***Tidemaster*** is a sunken frigate of the [Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet](Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet) located in [Shank's Reef](Shank's Reef) on [Lapidis Isle](Lapidis Isle). Along with her sister ship, the *[Waterbreaker](Waterbreaker)*, they were shipwrecked on the island while under attack from [Southsea Pirates](Southsea Pirates). The *Tidemaster* ran aground in Shank's Reef, where the rocks ripped the ship apart. Murlocs then attacked the crew, killing everyone aboard save for [Wally Burnside](Wally Burnside). The [Tidemaster's Logbook](Tidemaster's Logbook) can be found aboard the wreck.
