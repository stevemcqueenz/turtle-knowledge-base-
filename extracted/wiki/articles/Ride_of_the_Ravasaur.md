# Ride of the Ravasaur

This is a [Horde](Horde) only quest in [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater) added in [1.18.0](1.18.0) 

This let you grind for a [Raptor](Raptor) mount as [Alliance](Alliance) can for the Wintersaber.

You need to talk to [Jama'nali](Jama'nali) near the entrance from [Tanaris](Tanaris).

After a startup quest you get the main quest https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41770

Then you have to collect 420 teeth through three quests:

https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41772 kill 8 Stegodon for 1 teeth

https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41773 kill 5 Devilsaur for 1 teeth

https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=41771 kill 20 Pterrordax or Diemetradon for 1 teeth

You need to kill 2100 Devilsaurs or 3360 Stegodons or 8400 non-elites for the mount.
