# Stormpike Guard

----
-The Stormpike [Dwarves](Dwarves) have set up residence in [Alterac Valley](Alterac Valley), where they're locked in battle with the [Frostwolf Orcs](Frostwolf).*
----

The [Alliance](Alliance) [faction](faction) for the [Alterac Valley](Alterac Valley) [Battleground](Battleground).
They are group of Dwarven explorers who are searching for artifacts in the Frostwolf territory, trying to kill off the [Frostwolf](Frostwolf) clan and attacking the villiage where frostwolves raise their children,  even though the clan which wished to remain neutral in the conflict between alliance and horde. They are seeking help from the Alliance to eliminate all the native Frostwolf Orcs and their leader, the elderly blind shaman [Drek'thar](Drek'thar) who taught [Thrall](Thrall).
