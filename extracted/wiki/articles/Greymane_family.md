# Greymane family

The **Greymane** family (also known as the **Greymane dynasty** (ref: [The First Greymane](The First Greymane)) or **House Greymane**) (ref: [Gilnean Raven](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=69000)) is the ruling line of the [kingdom of Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)). They rule the kingdom from [Greymane Tower](Greymane Tower), and are helped in their duties by the nobles of Gilneas.

## Background
The Greymane dynasty was first established by [Garalon Greymane](Garalon Greymane), a minor noble of the [Empire of Arathor](Empire of Arathor) who established the kingdom of Gilneas after the city-state won its independence from Arathor following a war that saw the death of King [Thoradin III](Thoradin III). (ref: [The First Greymane](The First Greymane)) It is closely tied with the [Ravenwood family](Ravenwood family), which has been sworn to the Greymanes ever since Aldrin Ravenwood and Garalon Greymane's friendship. (ref: [Aldrin, Protector of the Realm](Aldrin, Protector of the Realm)) During the war of Gilnean independence, Garalon made use of dark blood magics in the battle of Stillward, though this element of Garalon's history has been forgotten, his powerless scepter left with House Ravenwood (ref: [quest: The Ravenwood Scepter]) and the crystal lost to the ravages of time. (ref: [quest: The Greymane Stone])

During the reign of King [Veran](Veran Greymane), [Lordaeronian](Lordaeron (kingdom)) forces led by Prince [Arterian Lordanol](Arterian Lordanol) invaded the lands of Silverpine to claim it for Lordaeron. King Veran personally led his army, claiming victory over Lordaeron and taking the prince captive when the harsh Gilnean winters, with the aid of the usage of dark blood magics originating from Garalon's time. (ref: [The Silverpine Wars](The Silverpine Wars))

Queen [Elaena the Good](Elaena Greymane)'s reign was marked by the arrival of Wildhammer refugees of the War of Three Hammers, who had flown west from [Dun Kithas](Dun Kithas) in search of a new land to call home. Sky King Redoran's gifts and generosity charmed the queen, who gifted them a place in [Gelnor](Gelnor) and to Redoran a seat at her court. (ref: [The Dwarves of Gelnor](The Dwarves of Gelnor))

King [Genn](Genn Greymane)'s reign saw the orcish incursions in Azeroth and the establishment of the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron), though Gilneas was a token part of the Alliance and withdrew after the Second War. The [Greymane Wall](Greymane Wall) was built on Genn's command, isolating Gilneas from the rest of the world. At some point, Genn met his wife, [Mia](Mia Greymane). They had two children: [Liam](Liam Greymane), his son and heir, and [Tess](Tess Greymane).
