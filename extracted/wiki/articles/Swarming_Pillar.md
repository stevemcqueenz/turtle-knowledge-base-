# Swarming Pillar

The **Swarming Pillar** is a large [silithid](silithid)-infested peak in the middle of [Silithus](Silithus) containing one of their [hives](hives), just south of [Cenarion Hold](Cenarion Hold). It gets its name from a massive swarm of small silithids that buzz around the hive.
