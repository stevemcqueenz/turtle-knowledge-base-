# Witherbark tribe

Clan of [Forest Trolls](Forest Trolls) located in the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), and [Hinterlands](Hinterlands). The clan is at war with the [Wildhammer Clan](Wildhammer Clan) of [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak) The tribe is part of the Zul-Amani Empire.
