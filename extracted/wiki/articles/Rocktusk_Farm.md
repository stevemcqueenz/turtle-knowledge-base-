# Rocktusk Farm

Situated in [Durotar](Durotar) just outside the gates of [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar), **Rocktusk Farm**'s owners feel fairly safe as they [raise](pig farm) their swine to provide sustenance for the [Horde](Horde).
