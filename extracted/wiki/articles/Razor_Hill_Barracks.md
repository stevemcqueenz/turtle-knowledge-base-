# Razor Hill Barracks

The **Razor Hill Barracks** features trainers for [warrior](warrior)s, [priest](priest)s, [shaman](shaman)s and [first aid](first aid). It also has a general goods vendor. Along with the [zeppelin](zeppelin) tower and [Tiragarde Keep](Tiragarde Keep), the barracks is one of the largest structures in [Durotar](Durotar) (excluding [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar)).
