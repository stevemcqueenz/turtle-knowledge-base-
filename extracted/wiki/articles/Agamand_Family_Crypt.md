# Agamand Family Crypt

The **Agamand Family Crypt** is located just north of [Agamand Mills](Agamand Mills) in northern [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades). [Captain Dargol](Captain Dargol), a minion of the [Scourge](Scourge), has settled here and awakened all the corpses and spirits of the Agamands' dead ancestors. It is a dark and dusty place, and the vengeful undead attack anyone who enter unbidden.
