# Nathrezim

, [Mephistroth](Mephistroth), [Mal'Ganis](Mal'Ganis), [Tichondrius](Tichondrius), [Dalvengyr](Dalvengyr), [Balnazzar](Balnazzar), [Anetheron](Anetheron), [Detheroc](Detheroc).]]
The **nathrezim**, also called **dreadlords** (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, Undead Hero Units, [Dreadlord](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Dreadlord)) or **dread lords**, (ref: [Warcraft III - Undead -> Units -> Dread Lord](http://classic.battle.net/war3/undead/units/dreadlord.shtml)) (ref: [Beginnings of the Undead Threat](Beginnings of the Undead Threat): "Because Kil'jaeden did not trust the Lich King, he sent his dread lords to watch over the spirit and ensure that the Lich King followed his orders.") are incredibly powerful demons who wield the powers of darkness and mental domination. These cunning, malefic beings once served as the demon [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden)'s most trusted lieutenants. Yet, at Kil'jaeden's request, the vampiric dreadlords were sent to watch over the [Lich King](Lich King), [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul), and ensure that he carried out his orders to sow chaos in the mortal world. Though dreadlords have been known to revel in the gore of single combat, they generally prefer to manipulate and beguile their enemies from the shadows. (ref: 

## History
### The Burning Legion
The nathrezim were a dark race of vampiric demons that were first encountered by [Sargeras](Sargeras), then the greatest warrior of the [Pantheon](Pantheon), who carried out his duties for countless millennia, seeking out and destroying the demons wherever he could find them. Having been greatly troubled by the corruption and all-consuming evil he encountered in the [eredar](eredar), being unable to fathom such depravity and spite, the great Titan slipped into a brooding depression.

As Sargeras' confusion and depression deepened, he was forced to contend with the nathrezim, who had set out to conquer a populated world by possessing its inhabitants and turning them to the shadow. The nefarious, scheming dreadlords had turned whole nations against one another by manipulating them through unthinking hatred and mistrust. Though Sargeras defeated the nathrezim easily, their corruption affected him deeply as well.

The noble Sargeras, unable to process the raging doubt and despair that overwhelmed his senses, lost all faith in not only his mission, but the Titans' vision of an ordered universe, as well. Sargeras began to believe that the concept of order itself was folly – and that chaos and depravity were the only absolutes within the dark, lonely universe. Though his fellow Titans tried to persuade him of his error and console his raging emotions, he disregarded their theories as delusional. Storming from their ranks forever, Sargeras set out to find his own place in the universe. Though the Pantheon was sorrowful for his departure, they would never believe just how far their lost brother would go.

As Sargeras' madness consumed the last vestiges of his noble spirit, he began to rationalize that the Titans were truly responsible for creation's failure. Deciding, at last, to undo their works throughout the universe, he set out to form an unstoppable army that would set the physical universe to the flame. In his madness, Sargeras shattered the prisons of the eredar and the nathrezim and set the vile demons free. The cunning demons, bowing before the dark Titan's vast rage and power, offered themselves to him and swore to serve him in whatever malicious way they could.

From the ranks of the powerful Eredar, Sargeras chose two champions to lead his demonic army of destruction: [Kil'jaeden the Deceiver](Kil'jaeden the Deceiver) was chosen to seek out the darkest races in the universe and lure them to Sargeras' shadow. The second champion, [Archimonde the Defiler](Archimonde the Defiler), was chosen to lead Sargeras' vast armies into battle against any who would stand against the dark Titan's will. Kil'jaeden's first move was to enslave the vampiric dreadlords under his terrible power. The dreadlords, serving as his elite guard and agents throughout the universe, took pleasure in their work by locating primitive races for their master to corrupt and bring into the fold. First amongst the dreadlords was [Tichondrius the Darkener](Tichondrius the Darkener). Tichondrius served Kil'jaeden as the perfect soldier and agreed to promote Sargeras' burning will to all the dark corners of the universe.<ref>*Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, [Origin of the Burning Legion](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Sargeras_and_the_Betrayal), Sargeras and the Betrayal)

### The Third War
Although Ner'zhul agreed to serve Kil'jaeden after being captured by him and tortured in the [Twisting Nether](Twisting Nether), Kil'jaeden remained skeptical of his pawn's loyalties even though Ner'zhul was agreeable and seemingly anxious to play his part. Keeping the Lich King bodiless and trapped within the crystal cask assured his good conduct for the short term, but the demon knew that he would need to keep a watchful eye on the Lich King. To this end, Kil'jaeden called upon the vampiric dreadlords to police Ner'zhul and ensure that he accomplished his dread task. [Tichondrius](Tichondrius), the most powerful and cunning of the Dreadlords, warmed to the challenge, fascinated by the plague's severity and the Lich King's unbridled potential for genocide. (ref: <ref>*Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, Undead History, [Kil'jaeden and the New Deal](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Kil'jaeden_and_the_New_Deal)) Ner'zhul's icy cask was cast back into the world of Azeroth, where the hardened crystal streaked across the night sky and smashed into the desolate, arctic continent of Northrend, burying itself in the deep, shadowed hallows of the Icecrown glacier. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, Undead History, [Icecrown and the Frozen Throne](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Icecrown_and_the_Frozen_Throne)) When the Lich King's dominion was threatened by the nerubians in the War of the Spider, the dreadlords and the Lich King's undead warriors invaded Azjol-Nerub and crushed the spider lords, bringing its subterranean temples crashing down upon their heads. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, Undead History, [War of the Spider](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#War_of_the_Spider))

From the confines of the Frozen Throne, Ner'zhul began to reach out his vast consciousness and touch the minds of Northrend's native inhabitants. With surprising ease, he enslaved the minds of many indigenous creatures, such as ice trolls and the fierce wendigo - and drew their evil brethren into his growing shadow. He found that his psychic powers were almost limitless, and used them to create a small army that he housed within Icecrown's twisting labyrinths. As the Lich King mastered his growing powers under the Dreadlords' persistent vigil, he discovered a remote human settlement on the fringe of the vast Dragonblight. Ner'zhul decided to test his powers and his dread plague on the unsuspecting humans. (ref: 

Though the Dreadlords were pleased when Ner'zhul's true mission had finally begun, the Lich King himself brooded within the tight, shadowy confines of the Frozen Throne. Despite his vast psychic powers and his complete dominion over the undead, he longed to be free of his icy prison. He knew that Kil'jaeden would never release him from his curse. And, due to his great power, he knew that the demons would destroy him as soon as his mission was completed. As such, he sought out a suitable host – some hapless dupe who was torn between darkness and light – he could possess that body and escape the confines of the Frozen Throne forever,<ref>*Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, Undead History, [An Heir Apparent...](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#An_Heir_Apparent...)) setting his eyes on Prince [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil). [Mal'Ganis](Mal'Ganis) was chosen to head up the plot to turn Prince Arthas to darkness under the premise of making him the Lich King's greatest champion. However, while Arthas' obsession with the hated dreadlord led him further down the path of evil, first lying to his men, betraying his mercenaries, and finally forsaking his own soul to take up the runeblade [Frostmourne](Frostmourne), killing [Muradin Bronzebeard](Muradin Bronzebeard) in the process, Arthas instead killed Mal'Ganis at the Lich King's command, much to the dreadlord's surprise. (ref: [Frostmourne (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Frostmourne_(WC3_Human)))

Following the corruption of Prince [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil), Tichondrius introduced himself to the [death knight](death knight), providing him with orders from Ner'zhul, especially concerning the resurrection of [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad). (ref: [Trudging through the Ashes (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Trudging_through_the_Ashes_(WC3_Undead))) Because he didn't entirely trust Arthas, Tichondrius pretended to be another one of the Lich King's followers. The ghost of Kel'Thuzad, however, informed Arthas that Tichondrius and the other dreadlords were actually the Lich King's jailers. (ref: [Digging up the Dead (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Digging_up_the_Dead_(WC3_Undead)))

Tichondrius regularly discussed the Scourge's progress with his colleagues, [Anetheron](Anetheron) and [Mephistroth](Mephistroth), who remained on a [Legion](Burning Legion) planet in the [Nether](Twisting Nether). The Darkener expressed (justified) concern that Ner'zhul had alternative plans for his new "champion", but Mephistroth pointed out that he "wouldn't dare undermine our efforts now." (ref: [The Dreadlords Convene (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dreadlords_Convene_(WC3_Undead)))

The [dreadlord](dreadlord) continued his work, eventually overseeing the death of [Uther Lightbringer](Uther Lightbringer), the invasion and destruction of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas (kingdom)), and the siege of [Dalaran](Dalaran). Once [Archimonde](Archimonde) was summoned, he decreed that the Lich King was no longer needed, and gave Tichondrius control of the Scourge. (ref: [Under the Burning Sky (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Under_the_Burning_Sky_(WC3_Undead)))

#### Invasion of Kalimdor
While the nathrezim aided the Legion in the destruction of Lordaeron, [Tichondrius](Tichondrius) and the pit lord [Mannoroth](Mannoroth) successfully engineered the death of [Cenarius](Cenarius) by taking advantage of the demi-god's quarrel with [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream) and the [Warsong clan](Warsong clan) by corrupting the orcs into the service of the Burning Legion. (ref: [The Blood of Mannoroth (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Blood_of_Mannoroth_(WC3_Orc))) (ref: [The Hunter of Shadows (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Hunter_of_Shadows_(WC3_Orc))) However soon after this the Warsong were defeated by the Horde and Human Expedition, who then purged Grom of the corruption, who at the cost of his own life killed Mannoroth. (ref: [By Demons Be Driven (WC3_Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/By_Demons_Be_Driven_(WC3_Orc))) (ref: [The Death of Hellscream (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Death_of_Hellscream_(WC3_Orc)))

In spite of Mannoroth's death at Hellscream's hands, the Burning Legion and thus the nathrezim proceeded with the invasion. The Scourge, more dangerous than ever thanks to the demonic support of [felhound](felhound)s, [doomguard](doomguard) and [infernal](infernal)s, was more than successful in penetrating [Ashenvale](Ashenvale) without Cenarius's intervention. (ref: [Enemies at the Gate (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Enemies_at_the_Gate_(WC3_NightElf))) (ref: [Daughters of the Moon (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Daughters_of_the_Moon_(WC3_NightElf))) The combined forces of the orcs and the humans, coupled with the resistance of the night elves, eventually managed to halt their progress. (ref: [The Druids Arise (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Druids_Arise_(WC3_NightElf)))

As the invasion of Kalimdor progressed, Tichondrius began corrupting the forests of [Felwood](Felwood) using a powerful warlock artifact, the [Skull of Gul'dan](Skull of Gul'dan), which also augmented his own powers. However, the skull was consumed by [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage), who had been tipped off by none other than [Arthas](Arthas). Transformed by the skull into a [demon](demon)-hybrid, Illidan used his newfound power to vanquish Tichondrius. (ref: [A Destiny of Flame and Sorrow (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_Destiny_of_Flame_and_Sorrow_(WC3_NightElf)))

Meanwhile, [Anetheron](Anetheron) had gone with [Archimonde](Archimonde), and served as a personal guardsman. Anetheron showed his tenacity when he led the [Scourge](Scourge) against the [Sentinels](Sentinels), [Alliance](Alliance), and [Horde](Horde) in the final, climactic [Battle of Mount Hyjal](Battle of Mount Hyjal). He invaded the [Great Alliance](Great Alliance)'s base several times during the battle, calling [infernal](infernal)s to serve him, with the objective of defeating the resistance so that Archimonde could ascend to crush the World Tree. However, Anetheron was killed during the battle. (ref: [Twilight of the Gods (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_of_the_Gods_(WC3_NightElf)))

### Plaguelands civil war
Following the Legion's defeat at [Hyjal](Hyjal), the [Lich King](Lich King) openly rebelled against Kil'jaeden. In response, the demon lord recruited [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage) to destroy the Frozen Throne. Illidan's attempt failed, (ref: [Illidan's Task (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Illidan%27s_Task_(WC3_BloodElf))) but did damage the Frozen Throne to the point where the Lich King's powers began weakening, in turn causing some of the Scourge to regain their free will. (ref: [A Kingdom Divided (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_Kingdom_Divided_(WC3_Undead)))

Unaware of the Legion's defeat at Hyjal, the nathrezim brothers [Detheroc](Detheroc), [Balnazzar](Balnazzar), and [Varimathras](Varimathras) continued governing the Scourge in Lordaeron and leading them against the [Alliance Remnants](Alliance Remnants) in the area. Arthas eventually returned from Kalimdor to inform the dreadlords of the Legion's defeat and chased them off to take control of the Scourge. (ref: [King Arthas (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/King_Arthas_(WC3_Undead))) The three [Dreadlord Insurgents](Dreadlord Insurgents) refused to be defeated and plotted to take advantage of Ner'zhul and Arthas' weakening powers to regain control of the undead, and they reached out to the now free-willed Sylvanas to ask for her help in the coup. (ref: 

The nathrezim took control over the majority of the undead and set a trap for Arthas in the [Ruins of Lordaeron](Ruins of Lordaeron),<ref>[The Flight from Lordaeron (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Flight_from_Lordaeron_(WC3_Undead))) but the death knight managed to escape to his master's side in Northrend. (ref: [Sylvanas' Farewell (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Sylvanas'_Farewell_(WC3_Undead))) The Dreadlord Insurgents declared themselves the future of Lordaeron's [Plaguelands](Plaguelands) and invited [Sylvanas](Sylvanas) to join their "new order", but she refused and instead made war on them, defeating each brother in turn. She forced Varimathras to swear loyalty to her, (ref: [ The Dark Lady (WC3 Undead)]) killed Detheroc, (ref: [Dreadlord's Fall (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Dreadlord's_Fall_(WC3_Undead))) and conquered the Ruins of Lordaeron from Balnazzar, and then forced Varimathras to kill Balnazzar, (ref: [A New Power in Lordaeron (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_New_Power_in_Lordaeron_(WC3_Undead))) but the latter secretly survived through means unknown.

### After the civil war
Despite the Legion's defeat a number of dreadlords still remained on Azeroth, particularly in the [Blasted Lands](Blasted Lands), with the most notable of them being [Razelikh the Defiler](Razelikh the Defiler), who sought to secure the land for the Legion on the orders of [Lord Kazzak](Lord Kazzak). (ref:  [57] [Loramus (quest)](Loramus (quest))) Razelikh was ultimately thwarted in this endeavor by adventurers on the behalf of a [Fallen Hero of the Horde](Fallen Hero of the Horde). (ref:  [60+] [You Are Rakh'likh, Demon](You Are Rakh'likh, Demon)) At the same time in the northern [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), [Balnazzar](Balnazzar) resurfaced after having survived his encounter with Sylvanas, having taken on the guise of [Saidan Dathrohan](Saidan Dathrohan). As Dathrohan, he gained control of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade), who believed that they alone were not infected by the plague and attacked and killed all non-Crusaders on sight.

Within the forests of [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), the dreadlord [Diathorus the Seeker](Diathorus the Seeker) was uncovered by the night elves deep within a barrow den above Demon Fall Canyon. (ref:  [28] [What We Know](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=70029)) At the behest of [Farseer Grimeye](Farseer Grimeye), Horde agents were tasked with ending the nathrezim. (ref:  [30] [The Seeker's Demise](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=70033))

Ruling over the [Rock of Desolation](Rock of Desolation) on the shattered remains of [Outland](Outland), [Mephistroth](Mephistroth) continued to scheme. Through the power of the leyline upon which the tower of Karazhan is built, he attempted to use Karazhan as an entry point to invade Azeroth yet again. His plans were foiled when an expedition into the tower revealed the link between the two worlds, and he was expelled by adventurers into the Great Dark Beyond forever, with his heart being delivered to [Dolvan Bracewind](Dolvan Bracewind). (ref:  [60R] [Sovereign of Desolation](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=41577))
