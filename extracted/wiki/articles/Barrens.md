The **Barrens** is a large [zone](zone) in [central Kalimdor](central Kalimdor) controlled mostly by the [Horde](Horde). It is a massive savanna, with a few oases in the north-central region around the [Crossroads](Crossroads). Before the upheaval, several large [kaldorei](kaldorei) cities stood here. Now, those brave few willing to scrape out a life on the arid plains inevitably run afoul of [centaur](centaur) warbands or [quilboar](quilboar) raiding parties. Despite its often uninviting terrain and hostile inhabitants, the Barrens receive a good deal of interest from [Alliance](Alliance) and [Horde](Horde) alike, as well as races native to Kalimdor. Trade routes crisscross the landscape leading to and from more inviting regions, and the most notable route is the [Gold Road](Gold Road) that runs north-to-south through the Barrens and beyond. Rumours suggest that precious minerals and treasure-filled ruins await those with the courage to investigate.

## Geography
The Barrens are huge, one of the largest zones in the game. The land is mostly flat, with a few hills scattered throughout. The climate is harsh and hot, but survivable due to several lush oasis. With a stretch of [coastline](The Merchant Coast) to the east, [mountains](Stonetalon Mountains) to the northwest, [plains](Mulgore) to the west, [forest](Ashenvale) to the north, [marsh](Dustwallow Marsh) to the southeast, and the desert conditions to the [south](Thousand Needles) and [northeast](Durotar), the Barrens is surrounded by extremely varied landscapes.

The Barrens contain three instanced dungeons, the [Wailing Caverns](Wailing Caverns), [Razorfen Kraul](Razorfen Kraul), and [Razorfen Downs](Razorfen Downs). The Horde entrance to [Warsong Gulch](Warsong Gulch) is located to the north. There are two Horde aligned travel hubs in this zone: [the Crossroads](the Crossroads) and [Camp Taurajo](Camp Taurajo), in addition to the flight path and boat in [Ratchet](Ratchet) available to both factions. There are no raid dungeons in the Barrens.

## Notes and trivia
- The Barrens is one of the largest zones in *World of Warcraft*, and running from Mor'shan Rampart near Ashenvale to the Great Lift near Thousand Needles can be daunting even for players with mounts.
- Currently, the Barrens has the widest level range of any single zone, spanning 15 levels.
- The Barrens is infamous as a very busy secondary zone shared by tauren, orcs and trolls. Undead players also often come here between levels 10 to 25. With three instances and multiple travel hubs, it is a busy "in-between" area traversed by higher-level characters as well. This is a major contributing factor to the high volume of the chat in this region. The chat in the region has had the infamous tag of [Barrens Chat](Barrens Chat) due to its sometimes frequent trend of players going completely off-topic in discussions and delving into silliness, which annoys some players while amusing others. This, however, has quelled in recent time due to new zones being introduced and the desire to move such chat into more accessible channels, such as the Trade chat which is shared amongst all faction capital cities.
- There is a band of mounted [Alliance Outrunners](Alliance Outrunners) to the south near [Camp Taurajo](Camp Taurajo). There is a level 24 human mage and three level 25 night elves. In addition to attacking Horde players on sight, they drop a scroll that starts the Horde quest [quest: The Runed Scroll].
- The Crossroads is also a popular destination for Alliance raids as it is a good meeting point to raid Orgrimmar.
- Although said to be home to Black dragonkin NO dragons appear in this zone in-game.
