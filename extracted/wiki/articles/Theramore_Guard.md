The **Theramore Guard** (ref: Faction name in [A Blaze of Glory](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_Blaze_of_Glory_(WC3_Orc))) (ref: [Theramore Guard Badge](Theramore Guard Badge)) (ref: [Colonel Breen#Quotes](Colonel Breen#Quotes)) is the military that guards [Theramore Isle](Theramore Isle). They are among the finest groups of [Alliance](Alliance) soldiers based in [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) and it is their duty to uphold the laws of the Alliance. (ref: [Captain Garran Vimes#Quotes](Captain Garran Vimes#Quotes))

## History
### Third War
They were the remnants of the [Human Expedition](Human Expedition) led by [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore): former [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron) units from various expeditionary forces such as the [Stromgarde Brigade](Stromgarde Brigade) and [Lordaeron Corps](Lordaeron Corps).

During the occupation of Theramore by the [Kul Tiran](Kul Tiras (kingdom)) forces of [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore), the Theramore Guard was forced into service and fought against the [Horde](Horde) attackers. (ref:

### Cycle of Hatred
The Theramore Guard was on high alert when the actions of the [Burning Blade cult](Burning Blade clan) pit the nations of [Theramore](Theramore) and [Durotar](Orgrimmar (faction)) against each other. Theramore's military investigated the activities of the cult, and [Chamberlain Kristoff](Kristoff) sent more than twenty boats and than four hundred soldiers to reinforce [Northwatch Hold](Northwatch Hold),<ref name="CoH17">*[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 17) including the city's [Elite Guard](Elite Guard). (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 13) They all clashed against [Burx](Burx)'s own [orc](orc)ish army at Northwatch.

### World of Warcraft
The Theramore Guard is known for the exceptional training of its [warrior](warrior)s, (ref: [Captain Evencane#Quotes](Captain Evencane#Quotes)) and counts many [marine](marine)s among its ranks. [Adjutant Tesoran](Adjutant Tesoran) keeps track of all the records for the Guard, from personnel to their equipment. (ref:  [38] [Daelin's Men](Daelin's Men)) Though it is a struggle to maintain these outposts in the swamps, the Guard also occupies several defensive watchtowers through Dustwallow Marsh, even if they had to abandon [Lost Point](Lost Point). (ref:  [38] [The Deserters (1)](The Deserters (1)))

At the beginning of every month, the marines from [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)) all get paid and usually leave Theramore for somewhere else. A lot of them go to [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor) to womanize, carouse, and just have a good time. (ref:  [33] [The Missing Diplomat (13)](The Missing Diplomat (13)))

The Theramore Guard is still seeking the exact location of the [ogre](ogre) settlement of [Brackenwall](Brackenwall Village), its [infiltrators](Theramore Infiltrator) doing their best to find it. (ref:  [36] [The Missing Diplomat (15)](The Missing Diplomat (15))) Unhappy with the way their city-state is being led, several guards also [deserted](Theramore deserters) the organization.
