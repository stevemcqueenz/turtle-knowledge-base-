**Sanv Tas'dal** is a [draenei](draenei) [riftwalker](riftwalker) and the sixth boss of [Tower of Karazhan](Tower of Karazhan). The chieftain of the [Tribe of Sanv](Tribe of Sanv), he ordered his people to flee from the dying world of [Draenor](Draenor) after the destruction wrought by the [orcish Horde](orcish Horde).

However, while part of his tribe escaped to the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth) led by [Akh Z'ador](Akh Z'ador) and [Sanv K'la](Sanv K'la), Sanv Tas'dal did not follow. He and the remainder of his tribe became servants of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), plying their riftwalking abilities to the demons' cause. When Akh Z'ador attempted to communicate with his remaining tribe, he discovered their change in allegiance; though he in turn was located within the fabric of the Twisting Nether by Sanv Tas'dal, who interrupted his focus, throwing him through the Nether back to Azeroth, in [Azshara](Azshara).

After discovering the location of his tribesmen, Sanv Tas'dal sent a projection of himself to the [Harborage](Harborage) to reunite his people and bring them into the fold under the Legion by force if necessary.
