# Maras'ethil

**Maras'ethil** are [night elven](Night elf) ruins located in [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast). Once a Highborne center of learning, it is now a broken monument to their arrogance. The Highborne ghosts that still wander Maras'ethil still believe themselves to be alive, patrolling streets that no longer exist and guarding cities that fell centuries ago. (ref: [quest: Ghosts of Maras'ethil]) According to the [Moro'gai tribe](Moro'gai tribe), the ruins remain filled with relics and other valuables. (ref: [quest: Phasmophobia])
