# Crystalline Oasis

| field | value |
|---|---|
| 1 | Azurescale Whelp |
| icon | WhelpBlue |

The **Crystalline Oasis** is an oasis located in the southeastern regions of the [Badlands](Badlands). The only water to be found in the Badlands, it is guarded by a shimmering magical crystal, and with it, the [Blue dragonflight](Blue dragonflight) that is surveying its properties.
