**Tichondrius the Darkener** was the first amongst the the [nathrezim](nathrezim), or dreadlords, and served [Kil'jaeden the Deceiver](Kil'jaeden) as the perfect soldier, having agreed to promote [Sargeras](Sargeras)' burning will to all the dark corners of the universe. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, *[Origin of the Burning Legion](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Sargeras_and_the_Betrayal)*, Sargeras and the Betrayal)

## Biography
Prior to the [Third War](Third War), Tichondrius was chosen by Kil'jaeden with serving as a jailor of the [Lich King](Lich King) and police him to ensure that he accomplished his dread task (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, *[Undead History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Kil'jaeden_and_the_New_Deal)*, Kil'jaeden and the New Deal) - though he resented this duty as he believed that the Lich King would ultimately betray the Legion and attempt to free himself from its power. (ref: [Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos manual#Tichondrius](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Tichondrius)) Regardless, he warmed to the challenge, fascinated by the [plague](Plague of Undeath)'s severity and the Lich King's unbridled potential for genocide. ### The Scourging of Lordaeron
As befitting his status as the greatest of the Nathrezim, Tichondrius was placed in charge of a number of [Scourge](Scourge) operations. Following the corruption of Prince [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil), Tichondrius introduced himself to the [Death Knight](Death Knight), providing him with orders from Ner'zhul, especially concerning the resurrection of [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad).<ref>[Trudging through the Ashes (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Trudging_through_the_Ashes_(WC3_Undead))) Because he didn't entirely trust Arthas, Tichondrius pretended to be another one of the Lich King's followers. The ghost of Kel'Thuzad, however, informed Arthas that Tichondrius and the other dreadlords were actually the Lich King's jailers. (ref: [Digging up the Dead (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Digging_up_the_Dead_(WC3_Undead)))

Tichondrius regularly discussed the Scourge's progress with his colleagues, [Anetheron](Anetheron) and [Mephistroth](Mephistroth), who remained on a [Legion](Burning Legion) planet in the [Nether](Twisting Nether). The Darkener expressed (justified) concern that Ner'zhul had alternative plans for his new "champion", but Mephistroth pointed out that he "wouldn't dare undermine our efforts now." (ref: [The Dreadlords Convene (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dreadlords_Convene_(WC3_Undead)))

The [dreadlord](dreadlord) continued his work, eventually overseeing the death of [Uther Lightbringer](Uther Lightbringer), the invasion and destruction of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas (kingdom)), and the siege of [Dalaran](Dalaran). Once [Archimonde](Archimonde) was summoned, he decreed that the Lich King was no longer needed, and gave Tichondrius control of the Scourge. (ref: [Under the Burning Sky (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Under_the_Burning_Sky_(WC3_Undead)))

### Invasion of Kalimdor
The [human](human)s, having lost their most powerful leaders and heroes, quickly fell before the might of the Legion, now joined by [Mannoroth](Mannoroth). Mannoroth was still sore over the [orc](orc)s' failure to conquer [Azeroth](Azeroth) years before, and was angrier still when Tichondrius smugly observed that the Scourge had done the job the orcs couldn't. Mannoroth became even more enraged when Tichondrius informed him that the orcs had left the continent. The pit lord was eager to take his frustrations out on the orcs, so at the first opportunity he and Tichondrius picked up the trail and followed the orcs to the ancient land of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). (ref: [The Wreckage of Lordaeron (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Wreckage_of_Lordaeron_(WC3_Orc)))

The arrival at Kalimdor reminded the pair of demon lords only too well that the threat of the [night elves](Night elf) still remained, as well as the demigod, [Cenarius](Cenarius). Mannoroth suspected that unless weakened, they would still pose a considerable threat. Tichondrius, however, came up with a solution. He knew that the orcs had earned the wrath of Cenarius, and he believed that corrupting the orcs once again would give them the power to defeat Cenarius, leaving him defeated when the Legion began their invasion of Kalimdor in full. Encouraged by the Darkener's plot, Mannoroth spilled his burning blood into a sacred pool that was eventually sensed by the [Warsong clan](Warsong clan), who would eventually drink the blood in order to kill Cenarius. Following the death of Cenarius Mannoroth would personally recruit the Warsong back into the Legion's fold, while at an unknown point either during or after these events Tichondrius would return to Archimonde's side. (ref: [The Blood of Mannoroth (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Blood_of_Mannoroth_(WC3_Orc)))

In spite of Mannoroth's death at the hands of [Grommash Hellscream](Grommash Hellscream), Tichondrius proceeded with the invasion. The Scourge, more dangerous than ever thanks to the demonic support of [felhound](felhound)s, [doomguard](doomguard) and [infernal](infernal)s, was more than successful in penetrating [Ashenvale](Ashenvale) without Cenarius's intervention. (ref: [Enemies at the Gate (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Enemies_at_the_Gate_(WC3_NightElf))) (ref: [Daughters of the Moon (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Daughters_of_the_Moon_(WC3_NightElf))) The combined forces of the orcs and the humans, coupled with the resistance of the night elves, eventually managed to halt their progress. (ref: [The Druids Arise (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Druids_Arise_(WC3_NightElf)))

As the invasion of Kalimdor progressed, Tichondrius led the Scourge to [Moonglade](Moonglade) in an attempt to destroy the [barrow den](barrow den) holding the body of [Malfurion Stormrage](Malfurion Stormrage). While the ghouls were cutting down the trees near the building, Tyrande awakened Malfurion. (ref: [The Awakening of Stormrage (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Awakening_of_Stormrage_(WC3_NightElf))) Tichondrius began corrupting the forests of [Felwood](Felwood) using a powerful warlock artifact, the [Skull of Gul'dan](Skull of Gul'dan), which also augmented his own powers. However, the skull was consumed by [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage), who had been tipped off by none other than [Arthas](Arthas). Transformed by the skull into a [demon](demon)-hybrid, Illidan used his newfound power to vanquish Tichondrius. (ref: [A Destiny of Flame and Sorrow (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_Destiny_of_Flame_and_Sorrow_(WC3_NightElf)))
