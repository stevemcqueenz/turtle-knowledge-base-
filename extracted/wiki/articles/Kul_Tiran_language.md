# Kul Tiran (language)

**Kul Tiran** (**Tirasgo** in this language) is a dialect of the [common language of Azeroth](Common (language)). It is primarily spoken throughout the maritime nation of [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras), where the rhythms of the sea have shaped its flowing cadence and unique vocabulary. Sailors, tidesages, and coastal folk often weave Tirasgo into shanties, prayers to the Tidemother, and private conversations.

## Features
### Grammar
#### Word Order
Kul Tiran typically follows a **subject-object-verb (SOV)** structure, much like the flowing speech of the tides that build slowly before crashing. For example, a sentence equivalent to "The sailor catches fish" would structure as "Sailor (subject) fish (object) catches (verb)."

#### Particle System
The language employs a rich system of particles to mark grammatical roles, similar to the subtle currents that guide a ship. Particles indicate subjects, objects, locations, and emphasis. Common examples include particles that mark the topic of a sentence, the direct object, or adverbial phrases (such as direction toward the sea or the mountains).

#### Honorific System
Kul Tiran features a complex hierarchical honorific system reflecting the strict naval ranks and social strata of Kul Tiras society. Speech varies by formality and the speaker’s relationship to the listener:
- **Honorific forms** show respect to nobles, high admirals, or tidesages (often using polite verb endings or prefixes).
- **Self-deprecating forms** express humility when addressing superiors.
- Special structures are used in formal occasions, rituals to the Tidemother, or when speaking to captains and lords.

#### Honorific Vocabulary
Different suffixes and prefixes are used depending on the listener’s status. Ordinary citizens might be addressed with a neutral honorific, while high-ranking admirals or clergy receive elevated forms. These honorifics often carry nautical flavor, evoking respect for the sea’s authority.

#### Quantifiers and Classifiers
Kul Tiran has a rich set of quantifiers and classifiers tailored to maritime life. Special counters exist for ships, schools of fish, waves, crew members, or storm clouds — reflecting the people’s deep connection to the ocean.

#### Loanwords and Integration
Like any living tongue shaped by trade and exploration, Kul Tiran readily borrows terms from other cultures of Azeroth (Lordaeronian Common, dwarven, elven, or even ancient Drust influences) and localizes them with its own rhythmic, wave-like pronunciation.

### Pronunciation
#### Vowel Harmony and Flow
The vowel system is relatively simple and harmonious, producing a smooth, melodic quality reminiscent of ocean waves. Vowels flow gently into one another, giving spoken Tirasgo a lyrical, almost song-like feel — ideal for sea shanties and tidesage chants.

#### Syllable Structure
Most syllables are open (ending in a vowel), resulting in a fluent and concise pronunciation. Words tend to have a light, rolling rhythm that carries well across decks or stormy shores.
