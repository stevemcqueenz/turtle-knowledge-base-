# Dun Algaz

**Dun Algaz** (or **Algaz Gate**) (ref: [Khaz Modan map](:File:WoWKhazModan.jpg)) (ref: [quest: A Dark Threat Looms]) (ref: [quest: Search for Incendicite]) is the tunneled mountain pass area between [Loch Modan](Loch Modan) and the [Wetlands](Wetlands), as well as the name of a stronghold located in the pass. It is bordered to the south by [Algaz Station](Algaz Station) in Loch Modan.

In recent years, while they normally hid in the hills, the post has been overrun by orcs from the [Dragonmaw clan](Dragonmaw clan), forcing the dwarven mountaineers to abandon it and fall back to the upper tunnel of the pass. Travel between the dwarven homelands and Menethil Harbor is now far more dangerous as a result, and the orcs threaten to cut off Loch Modan from [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor). (ref: [quest: The Algaz Gauntlet])
