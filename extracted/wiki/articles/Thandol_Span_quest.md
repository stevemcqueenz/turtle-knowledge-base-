## Notes
The [Scarlet Recruit](Scarlet Recruit) ambush at the [Thandol Span](Thandol Span) is notorious for not triggering properly. In order to properly trigger the ambush, you must delete the [Scarlet Recruit's Insignia Ring](Scarlet Recruit's Insignia Ring) that is still in your inventory from  [60] [Caravan Meets Caravan](Caravan Meets Caravan), then abandon the quest and retake it from [Kixxle](Kixxle). Upon approaching the Thandol Span, two [Scarlet Recruit](Scarlet Recruit)s will appear from the shadows, soon followed by two more, and then finally [Vladeus Springriver](Vladeus Springriver) will appear. Beware, as the recruits can quickly swarm you and keep you stunned with Hammer of Justice.

  -Vladeus Springriver spawns**
  **Vladeus Springriver says:** For the Scarlet Crusade!

  -Vladeus Springriver is defeated**
  **Vladeus Springriver says:** Stop, I give up! Spare me, I will submit to imprisonment.

  -Speaking with Vladeus Springriver**
I am taking responsibility for what I have done, don't worry, I will follow you and behave.

  > Gossip: *I'm taking you to the local law enforcement.*

  **Vladeus Springriver says:** I understand.

  -Speaking with Captain Stoutfist**
I am taking responsibility for what I have done, don't worry, I will follow you and behave.

  > Gossip: *This man is the only survivor of the group that attacked the caravan that was meant to reach Aerie Peak. He has yielded, and I have spared his life.*

  **Captain Stoutfist says:** Great find. My boys and I will put him up in chains. Go get yourself an ale while we handle this.
