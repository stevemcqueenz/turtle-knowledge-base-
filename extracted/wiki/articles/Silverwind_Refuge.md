# Silverwind Refuge

**Silverwind Refuge**50 66 Ashenvale (g/s/c) is located in south-central [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), just north of [Mystral Lake](Mystral Lake) and southwest of [Greenpaw Village](Greenpaw Village). It is a [night elven](night elven) settlement that contains [quest giver](quest giver)s, [vendor](vendor)s, a [hunter](hunter) trainer, a [pet trainer](pet trainer), a [skinning](skinning) trainer, an [herbalism trainer](herbalism trainer), and an [alchemy trainer](alchemy trainer). It is also a popular spot for fishing, since the Silverwind sentinels are ready at hand to aid should anything attack and the view on the lake which borders the Refuge is beautiful.
