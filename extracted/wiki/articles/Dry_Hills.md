# Dry Hills

The **Dry Hills** are located in the far northwestern corner of the [Barrens](Barrens). Despite their name, the Dry Hills are actually one of the greener areas of the Barrens because the seeds from nearby [Ashenvale Forest](Ashenvale Forest) have been carried on the wind over the crags.

These foothills of the [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains) to the west are primarily populated by [Witchwing](Witchwing) [harpies](harpies) who have recently been raiding caravans traveling the northern [Gold Road](Gold Road). However, the large number of [zhevra](zhevra)s, [plainstrider](plainstrider)s, [raptor](raptor)s, and other game in this [region](region) has drawn many hunters to this area.
