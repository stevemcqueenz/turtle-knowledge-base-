Lord **Lor'themar Theron** is the last known living member of [House Theron](House Theron) and a current member of the [Regency Council](Regency Council) of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas).

## Biography
Lor'themar was raised by his uncle [Vanthalir'il](Vanthalir'il Theron), who acted as both guardian and mentor. (ref: 

### Mysteries of Azeroth
Lor'themar later joined the greater [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant) as the high elves began to resettle the region of the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands). Lor'themar, as a prominent surviving noble, was granted a place as part of the new [Regency Council](Regency Council) when [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner) was deposed by the remaining nobility as Speaker for the Remnant. However, Lor'themar remained a supporter of Vereesa.

Lor'themar's uncle and guardian, Vanthalir'il, was found to have perished upon the Highlands and returned from beyond the grave. His uncle's signet was returned to him to signify that he had been returned to his rest.

Upon hearing that the [Wildhammer dwarves](Wildhammer dwarves) of the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches) were facing a perilous plight, Lor'themar sent a detachment of rangers led by [Faellina Swiftstride](Faellina Swiftstride) to aid the people of [Dun Kithas](Dun Kithas) and honor the high elves' old alliances with the Wildhammers.<ref>[quest: Honoring Old Alliances]) In addition, he tasked the rangers with locating a means to cure the [withered](withered) of Quel'Thalas that he believed may be hidden within the depths of [Grim Batol](Grim Batol). (ref: [quest: To Cure the Withered])

Despite ostensibly being a supporter to Vereesa, Lor'themar was the one who had told Lord [Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn) of the [Silver Covenant](Silver Covenant) diverting supplies to fund their second attack on [Shalandis Isle](Shalandis Isle), and voted for Lord Sunsworn when he made a motion before the Council to be named Lord-Regent of Quel'Thalas in the aftermath of the Silver Covenant's campaign. (ref: [A GOOD DAY — Everlook Broadcasting Co. Radio Play Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u90BCZfygz0))
