# Lushwater Oasis

**Lushwater Oasis**, just south of the [Crossroads](Crossroads), is probably the most important of the oases in the [Barrens](Barrens) in that it is the entrance to the accursed [Wailing Caverns](Wailing Caverns). [Kolkar](Kolkar) [centaur](centaur) stalk between the trees, eager to hunt the wild beasts coming to drink. Due to its closeness to the Wailing Caverns and Crossroads, this is a popular spot to fish for [Deviate Fish](Deviate Fish), the integral ingredient in the popular (if idiosyncratic) [Savory Deviate Delight](Savory Deviate Delight).
