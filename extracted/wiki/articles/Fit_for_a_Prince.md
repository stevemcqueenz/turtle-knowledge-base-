## Objectives

## Description
With everything gathered, all you need to do is travel to Stormwind Keep and deliver this bag to Prince Anduin—but only to him, do you understand? Otherwise, those pesky noble flies will surround him and inspect it. Be as stubborn as a mule if you must. You need to present this bag to Anduin yourself, got it?

Here is a bit of coin for your trouble, along with the bag you must deliver and this stuffed toy. Keep it close; it might cost you your life. I am jesting, of course, but it belonged to my Tiffin. I know the child will be pleased to have something of hers. Go now, and tell him I love him as long as the leaves fall.

## Completion
Grandmother sent you, and she said that? Oh, I miss her dearly. I can't wait to try everything she sent me. I hear the donuts are very famous in Northwind. And I will treasure this toy from Mother forever. Thank you, adventurer. You have done me a great favor, and as Prince of Stormwind, I would reward you with something from our armory—if I could. Instead, I will give you this book I have from the Archbishop.

Do come and tell me of your adventures sometimes; this palace is dull and boring. I could use a friend.
