-Watch Commander Zalaphil** is a [rare mob](rare mob) [human](human) found in the receiving room of [Tiragarde Keep](Tiragarde Keep) in [Durotar](Durotar). He is friendly towards Alliance players. 

He is a higher ranking [Kul Tiran](Kul Tiras (kingdom)) soldier and was dispatched with [Lieutenant Benedict](Lieutenant Benedict) to continue [Admiral Proudmoore's](Daelin Proudmoore) goal of taking Durotar and killing the orcs.

-Shield Bash
