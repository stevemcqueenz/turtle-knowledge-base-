# Shindigger's Camp

**Shindigger's Camp** is a small camp in the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands) where the drunken dwarf [Rhapsody Shindigger](Rhapsody Shindigger) and the high elf [Gilveradin Sunchaser](Gilveradin Sunchaser) are located.
