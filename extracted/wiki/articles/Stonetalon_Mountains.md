The **Stonetalon Mountains** are a range of peaks that lie west of the [Barrens](Barrens), south of [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), and north of [Desolace](Desolace). The mountains are traditionally the ancestral home of the [harpies](Harpy), (ref: [The World of Warcraft Townhall/Beastiary#Harpy](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_World_of_Warcraft_Townhall/Beastiary#Harpy)) but their numbers have dwindled as the bird-women spread east and south. [Stonetalon Peak](Stonetalon Peak), the largest mountain in the range, has long been a sacred place for both the [night elves](Night elf) and [tauren](tauren). The night elves maintain a barrow and a glade at the top, where they commune regularly with the spirits of nature. The mountains further south are less forested, although some sparse evergreens grow in certain spots, particularly the [Greatwood Vale](Greatwood Vale). The [Venture Company](Venture Company) have put a heavy toll on the land, cutting down almost all the trees in [Windshear Crag](Windshear Crag) and filling the air with soot, spoiling the area's water with oil and waste. The tauren have a holding in the centrally located [Sun Rock Retreat](Sun Rock Retreat) and the [Darkspear](Darkspear) trolls have a settlement further south at [Malaka'jin](Malaka'jin). The mountains are also home to kobolds, spiders, and wyverns, while the [Charred Vale](Charred Vale) holds several especially dangerous kinds of beasts and elementals.

## Geography
The Stonetalon Mountains lie between the forests of [Ashenvale](Ashenvale) to the north and the bleak [Desolace](Desolace) to the south west. The highest peaks of the mountains are thickly forested with evergreens, though the forest thins with lower altitude. The logging of the [Venture Company](Venture Company) has also further thinned the forest, with clear cutting in and around [Windshear Crag](Windshear Crag). The mountains boast a lake known as [Mirkfallon Lake](Mirkfallon Lake), around which [wyvern](wyvern)s make their nests. [Charred Vale](Charred Vale), once a lush and beautiful area, is now a desolate ruin full of fire elementals.

There are no instanced dungeons nor PvP battlegrounds in the Stonetalon Mountains. The micro dungeon [Talon Den](Talon Den) can be found here. Travel hubs include [Sun Rock Retreat](Sun Rock Retreat) for the Horde and [Stonetalon Peak](Stonetalon Peak) and [Bael Hardul](Bael Hardul) for the Alliance.
