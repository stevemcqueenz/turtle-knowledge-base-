# Zuuldaia Ruins

The **Zuuldaia Ruins** are ancient [troll](troll) ruins on the northern part of the western coast of [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). A large number of [Bloodscalp](Bloodscalp) trolls inhabit the area.
