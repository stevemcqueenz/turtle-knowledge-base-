# Nightbane pack

The Nightbane are a clan of [worgen](worgen) that roam [Duskwood](Duskwood). Their most prominent presence is in [Brightwood Grove](Brightwood Grove), west of [Darkshire](Darkshire), but they are said to have come from [Roland's Doom](Roland's Doom), a cave to the south. It is there that a group of miners found a mysterious scythe jutting from the cave wall, and when it was removed, the worgen spontaneously began to appear. The scythe is suspected to be the [Scythe of Elune](Scythe of Elune).
