The **red dragonflight** is a draconic organization led by the eponymous [red dragon](red dragon)s to uphold their creed to the world. After the ordering of the planet, the titan [Eonar](Eonar) blessed [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza) and her flight, charging her with safeguarding all living creatures within the world. (ref: [Charge of the Dragonflights](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Charge_of_the_Dragonflights))

## Culture
### Nomenclature
Members of the red dragonflight most often have names ending in "strasz" for males, or "strasza" for females.

### Membership
The red dragonflight is primarily made up of, as the name implies, red dragonkin. These include the red dragons themselves, but also red-scaled variants of drakonid and dragonspawn.

## History
### The Charge of the Dragonflights
Once the [Titan](Titan)s felt satisfied with their creation upon [Azeroth](Azeroth), they left to roam the cosmos in search of new worlds on which they would shape order from chaos, creating new life throughout a myriad of worlds. Before their departure from Azeroth however, the Titans empowered five dragons to become stewards of that world. Their purpose: to guard Azeroth as the Dragon Aspects, protecting the Titans' creation.

[Eonar](Eonar), the titan patron of all life, gave a portion of her power to the Red leviathan Alexstrasza. Ever after, Alexstrasza would be known as the Life-Binder, working to safeguard all living creatures of Azeroth. Due to her supreme wisdom and limitless compassion for all living things, Alexstrasza was crowned the Dragonqueen and given dominion over her kind.
