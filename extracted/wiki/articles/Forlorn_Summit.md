# Forlorn Summit

**Forlorn Summit** is a ruined outpost located near [Stratholme](Stratholme). Forgotten and decayed, this cliff holds Prince [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil)'s base from which he issued the [Culling of Stratholme](Culling of Stratholme) during the [Scourge](Scourge)'s infestation by the [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned). In his fervent pursuit of [Mal'Ganis](Mal'Ganis), he abandoned this outpost - unbeknownst to the [Silver Hand](Silver Hand), Arthas' suspicions were about to be verified: among the refugees of Stratholme were not only bearers of the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath), but also remaining agents of the Cult of the Damned, [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad)'s necromantic acolytes. To this day, the Forlorn Summit is a breeding ground of vile sorcery and crimes against all life. The outpost's mine has been repurposed into the [Widow's Nest](Widow's Nest), the lair of [High Widow Arania](High Widow Arania) and her brood.

After [Uther the Lightbringer](Uther the Lightbringer)'s departure to battle the Scourge elsewhere, one paladin, [Leonid Barthalomew](Leonid Barthalomew the Revered), stayed behind to protect the innocent. Though he valiantly defended the people that still remained in the outpost, his unwavering devotion to the Light was not enough against the undead hordes. (ref: [quest: Vigilant Last Defender])
