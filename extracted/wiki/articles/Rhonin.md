**Rhonin** is a powerful human mage, known to be skilled with the sword. (ref: *[The Well of Eternity](The Well of Eternity)*, pg. 49) (ref: *[The Sundering](The Sundering)*, pg. 33) Rhonin was instrumental in breaking the power of the [orc](orc)s at the end of the [Second War](Second War) by freeing the Dragonqueen [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza) from her imprisonment under [Nekros Skullcrusher](Nekros Skullcrusher), an [orc](orc) [warlock](warlock) and leader of the [Dragonmaw clan](Dragonmaw clan) who used the [Demon Soul](Demon Soul) to keep her prisoner. He later traveled back to the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients) in order to heal a rift in time.

## Biography
Rhonin didn't grow up in a mage family. A rift appeared between him and his kin when he decided to enter the ranks of wizardry, not ever seeing any of them again after acting on his chosen calling. (ref: *[The Well of Eternity](The Well of Eternity)*, chapter 1)

As a student of magic, Rhonin's nose was once broken by a fellow apprentice. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, chapter 1) Rhonin decided by his own choice not to get it properly fixed by magic. (ref: 

### Second War
Rhonin was once a promising mage of the Kirin Tor until a disastrous mission involving the reckless use of magic during the Second War — whilst Rhonin was casting a spell, others in his group got in the path of his attack. Rhonin's action succeeded in preventing a band of orc warlocks from raising a demon from the dead, but it also cost the lives of all his comrades. Rhonin was cast into probation until the opportunity for penance arose with a dangerous mission into [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan). He was assisted in this mission by the [high elven](High elf) ranger [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner), the warrior [dwarf](dwarf) [Falstad Dragonreaver](Falstad Dragonreaver), and the dragon-mage [Krasus](Korialstrasz).<ref>*[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*)

He was also assisted (at first) by the evil [black dragon](black dragon) [Deathwing](Deathwing), but Deathwing's true purpose was to steal some of Alexstrasza's eggs to replenish his own decimated dragonflight. With a gemstone created from Deathwing's scale, Rhonin was able to destroy the Demon Soul and release Alexstrasza. During this mission, Rhonin and Vereesa fell in love.

### Third War
During the [Third War](Third War), Rhonin fought against the [Scourge](Scourge) and [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) as they invaded [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (continent)). He and his wife were among the few survivors of [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)). Most of Rhonin's family however, including his father, were killed during the fall of the town of [Andorhal](Andorhal). (ref: *[The Well of Eternity](The Well of Eternity)*, chapter 2)

### War of the Ancients
While he and Vereesa were preparing for the birth of their twin offspring, Rhonin was once again summoned by Krasus for help. Apparently, the dragon-mage had discovered a rift in time, which swallowed both of them, along with the orc veteran [Broxigar](Broxigar). The three were sent 10,000 years back in time to the [first invasion](War of the Ancients) of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion).

While in the past, Rhonin encountered legendary beings such as [Cenarius](Cenarius), [Malorne](Malorne), and [Aviana](Aviana). He became friends with the heroes [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage), [Malfurion Stormrage](Malfurion Stormrage), [Tyrande Whisperwind](Tyrande Whisperwind), [Jarod Shadowsong](Jarod Shadowsong), [Maiev Shadowsong](Maiev Shadowsong), and [Shandris Feathermoon](Shandris Feathermoon). Rhonin actually became Illidan's mentor, not in the least to try and harness his reckless power, but despite Illidan's admiration much of Rhonin's work involved cleaning up Illidan's mistakes. When Illidan defected, Rhonin assumed command of the [Moon Guard](Moon Guard). As a reward for Rhonin's courage, [Nozdormu](Nozdormu) allowed Rhonin to return to Vereesa at the moment she went into labor to ensure he would not miss a moment of the twins' lives. Nozdormu also offered the children the friendship of the [bronze dragonflight](bronze dragonflight). (ref: *[War of the Ancients Trilogy](War of the Ancients Trilogy)*)

### Mysteries of Azeroth
Vereesa would later leave Rhonin behind so that she could serve her people in their time of need, serving as Speaker of the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant) for a time and later as a member of the [Regency Council](Regency Council) in [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas).
