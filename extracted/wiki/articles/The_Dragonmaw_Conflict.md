## Objectives
Bring 20 Dragonmaw Heads from Dragonmaw Orcs in the Wetlands to Lieutenant Alverold at Anchor's Edge in the Barrens.
- [Dragonmaw Head](Dragonmaw Head) (20)

## Description
During the Second War, Kul Tiras proudly put an end to Orgrim Doomhammer's ramshackled fleet. Others, however, eluded our grasp: The craven orcs of the Dragonmaw Clan, who brought us untold ruin astride their winged slave-beasts, took flight into the dark corners of the world.

All Kul Tiras craves vengeance against them, as it was by their fire that so many of our noble comrades faced agonizing deaths at sea.

The same fate was served to my own father, and I have not forgotten...

In the Wetlands, these Dragonmaw animals have had the gall to finally scurry out of their hiding holes. For this error, I will have their heads.

Do this, and you will prove yourself a friend of Kul Tiras.

## Progress
