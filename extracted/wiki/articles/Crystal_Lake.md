# Crystal Lake

**Crystal Lake** is a lake located directly east of [Goldshire](Goldshire) in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest). It is crawling with [murloc](murloc)s and may be quite dangerous for the inexperienced adventurer. [William Pestle](William Pestle) sends adventurers here to gather kelp from the murlocs in order to make an invisibility potion.
