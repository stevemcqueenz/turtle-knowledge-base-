# Scarlet Watch Post

The **Scarlet Watch Post** is a guard tower located in northeastern [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), just north of the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery). Its maintenance despite its remote location is solely to prevent enemies of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) from assaulting the monastery from its less defensible north side.
