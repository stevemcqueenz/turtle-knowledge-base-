# Scorpid

The Scorpid are gigantic versions of the scorpion.  They tend to be lone hunters, attacking anything they perceive as a threat with their claws and venomous sting.  Hunting and killing scorpid is considered a rite of passage by the Orcs.  Scorpids are found in [Durotar](Durotar), [The Barrens](The Barrens), the [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes), [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles), [Uldaman](Uldaman), the [Blasted Lands](Blasted Lands), [Blackrock Depths](Blackrock Depths), [Silithus](Silithus), and [Tanaris](Tanaris).

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Have high armor, medium HP, and low damage.
- Eats only Meat.
- Can learn [Cower](Cower), [Growl](Growl), [Claw](Claw), and their special skill, [Scorpid Poison](Scorpid Poison).
- Occasionally used as a pet.  The rare spawn Death Flayer is popular, with a stunning black-and-red coloration and an 1.6 attack speed.
