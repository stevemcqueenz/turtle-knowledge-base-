## Description
All of the materials have been gathered, We have come to the culmination of our efforts. If we want to truly harness such energy we must first find a suitable conduit for which to contain it.

Long ago a great gemstone of immense power was brought to this world by Gul'dan and his minions. The Pendant of Uth'okk was left to Oronok Torn-Heart, one of Gul'dan's lieutenants. It is this pendant that I seek. You can find him lurking within the depths of Stormwrought Ruins on the island of Balor. This will not be an easy task, but I desire you to kill him, and bring me the power of the Pendant of Uth'okk.

Do this, and you shall be rewarded.
