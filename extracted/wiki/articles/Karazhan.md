*This article is about the history and lore of Karazhan. For the 5-man player dungeon, see [Karazhan Crypt](Karazhan Crypt). For the 10-player raid instance, see [Lower Karazhan Halls](Lower Karazhan Halls). For the 40-player raid instance, see [Tower of Karazhan](Tower of Karazhan).*

**Karazhan**, also known as **Medivh's Tower**, (ref: *[The Last Guardian](The Last Guardian)*) is an abandoned citadel (or castle) (ref:  located in southern [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass) perched atop powerful and chaotic leylines that weave through the tower.<ref name="1.17.1">[FACE DESOLATION: Tower of Karazhan Raid Trailer | Turtle WoW Vanilla+](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b7z-yAdPchQ)) The tower is best known for its last known occupant — Medivh, the last Guardian of Tirisfal, who was killed by [Khadgar](Khadgar), [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar), and [Garona](Garona) at the end of the [First War](First War).

Karazhan, once the towering stronghold of the former Guardian of Tirisfal, now hums with magical energy as it perches atop a powerful leyline. Its long-forgotten corridors, covered in dust, have become a haven for various creatures, though it appears that not all of its inhabitants have willingly departed. The spire holds immense, uncontrolled power, with numerous factions vying to claim it as their own. (ref: https://turtle-wow.org/raids-and-dungeons)

## History
### The Last Guardian
Karazhan is located in [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass) on the sub-continent of [Azeroth](Azeroth (continent)) in the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms). Existing long before [Medivh](Medivh) inhabited it, it is unknown who originally built Karazhan or who now resides within its walls. The land it sits on "was similar in shape to a human skull. Many had noted it over the years, though only a few had been sufficiently brave, or powerful, or tactless to mention it to the property's owner". (ref: 

Karazhan's history begins with an explosion that carved out Deadwind Pass and weakened the fabric of reality in the region. Someone then built the tower, probably to take advantage of this weakened reality and heightened magic. Medivh eventually took up residence there, but mused that the explosion and the construction only happened because he would eventually arrive.

During the [First War](First War), the tower was inhabited by Medivh, his steward [Moroes](Moroes), his cook named Cook, and his then-apprentice [Khadgar](Khadgar). [Garona](Garona) also resided here as an emissary in the same time of Khadgar's apprenticeship. [Sargeras](Sargeras) allowed Medivh to freely explore Karazhan. As a result of the region's weakened reality, many strange and disturbing visions lurked within the tower, and Moroes was known for wearing blinders to avoid seeing them. The visions were ultimately ended when most of Karazhan's ambient magical power was absorbed by the resurrected Medivh, reducing it to "a pile of stone in the remote reaches", its power taken to serve the Prophet.

### World of Warcraft
Despite Medivh's absence and the draining of magic, a new power seems to have made its home in Karazhan. The [Dark Riders](Dark Riders) of [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass), though rarely seen, have a connection to Karazhan - the [Dark Reaver of Karazhan](Dark Reaver of Karazhan) standing vigil at the entrance of Karazhan, preventing any intrusion into his master's tower. However, no one knows who their master is. [One recent sighting](Jitters' Completed Journal) is cause for concern &mdash; the Dark Riders seek the [Scythe of Elune](Scythe of Elune), a powerful artifact with the ability to summon the vile [worgen](worgen) into this world.

In the depths of the lower halls, Medivh's loyal castellan, [Moroes](Moroes), remains a vigilant guardian.

The [worgen](worgen), led by [Clawlord Howlfang](Clawlord Howlfang) and [Keeper Gnarlmoon](Keeper Gnarlmoon), were summoned to the tower by the [Scythe of Elune](Scythe of Elune), which has found itself through unknown means into the hands of [Lord Blackwald II](Lord Blackwald II), one of the [Dark Riders](Dark Riders) cursed by Medivh himself to be artifact hunters.<ref> [60R] [Scythe of the Goddess](Scythe of the Goddess)) (ref:  [60] [Scythe of the Goddess (2)](Scythe of the Goddess (2))) The worgen are further drawn to the tower seeking the Scythe up unknown means. (ref: 

Without the Last Guardian, the wild leylines that run beneath Karazhan stir with power, and have coalesced into an avatar of pure magic, taking form as [a living force driven by vengeance](Anomalus) that threatens to damage leylines across Azeroth and cause Karazhan to crumble to dust.<ref>[Turtle WoW Team on Twitter](https://x.com/turtlewowteam/status/1819688372861907427) (2024-08-03)) The [Blue dragonflight](Blue dragonflight), seeking to untangle the chaotic leylines, have also made themselves known in the tower led by [Ley-Watcher Incantigos](Ley-Watcher Incantigos). (ref: 

Uncovering the mysteries of Karazhan's haunted upper halls unveils a terrifying truth - the evil of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) still lingers in the haunted halls of Karazhan. Though [a breach in reality](Netherspace), the [nathrezim](nathrezim) [Mephistroth](Mephistroth) seeks to open a link from the [Rock of Desolation](Rock of Desolation) through Karazhan and use it as an entry point to invade Azeroth yet again.<ref> [60R] [Sovereign of Desolation](Sovereign of Desolation))
