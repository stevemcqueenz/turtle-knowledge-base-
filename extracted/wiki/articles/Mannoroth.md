**Mannoroth the Destructor** was a [Pit Lord](Pit Lord) whose ruthlessness and vile cunning made him quickly become one of the favoured lieutenants of his dark masters, [Archimonde](Archimonde) and [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden), the [Eredar](Eredar) champions of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion). 

## Background
Mannoroth was among the demons who made the excursion into [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) many centuries ago during the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients). Though he battled with brutal ferocity and millions fell at his four feet, he was defeated by [Cenarius](Cenarius) and his followers and sent back into the [Twisting Nether](Twisting Nether) as the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity) was destroyed.

Mannoroth made his mark upon the orcs, however, far back in [Draenor](Draenor). Many years before the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal) was first formed, Mannoroth, under instruction by [Archimonde](Archimonde), formed a plan to put the orcs under the thumb of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion). They would serve as harbingers of chaos, wiping out any potential resistance when the Legion arrived. With the help of [Gul'dan](Gul'dan), the [Blood of Mannoroth](Blood of Mannoroth) was drunk by the chieftains of the clans. They were engulfed by a demonic [bloodlust](bloodlust) that wiped out the [Draenei](Draenei) and damned the orcs forever.

However, after the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal) was opened and the [orc](orc)s engaged the humans over the course of two wars, they were unexpectedly defeated and routed by the humans. The orcs, and in turn, Mannoroth, had failed. Mannoroth, painfully aware of this fact, made plans to have his vengeance.

Finally, [Archimonde](Archimonde) was summoned by the [lich](lich), [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad), to the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth). Mannoroth, eager to finally have his revenge on the night elves and the orcs, followed his master, searching for the orcs.

The [Nathrezim](Nathrezim) [Dreadlord](Dreadlord), [Tichondrius](Tichondrius), gloated openly that the [Scourge](Scourge) had succeeded where the orcs had failed. This only enraged Mannoroth further, and he was horrified to discover that the orcs had left. Mannoroth and [Tichondrius](Tichondrius), in pursuit, followed [Thrall](Thrall) and the [Horde](Horde) across the sea to [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). [Archimonde](Archimonde) reminded Mannoroth, though, that the [orc](orc)s could still be a useful tool to them.

Upon arriving in [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), Mannoroth sensed the foul presence of Cenarius, and he worried that the demigod could prove to be a chink in their plans. [Tichondrius](Tichondrius) suggested that perhaps the orcs could be used to destroy [Cenarius](Cenarius), for the [orc](orc) who Mannoroth once corrupted years ago on [Draenor](Draenor), [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream), was here, and had incurred the wrath of the [demigod](demigod). 

Mannoroth spilled the [Blood of Mannoroth](Blood of Mannoroth) into a fountain of life, defiling it. [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream)'s [shaman](shaman)s, sensing the power, led him to the fountain, and [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream) unwittingly corrupted his clan by drinking its waters. Infused with the powers of the Legion, [Cenarius](Cenarius) was no match for the bloodlusted [Horde](Horde).
With the demigod dead, Mannoroth appeared and took full control of [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream) as the true invasion of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) began. Finding that the [Orc](Orc)s were still a formidable force under the leadership of [Thrall](Thrall), Mannoroth formed a new plan to take him out.

Thrall found his former friend [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream) had fallen under the sway of the legion and vowed to save him. With the help of [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore) the Orc and Human alliance successfully destroyed [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream)'s corrupted clan, captured Hellscream, and purged him of his madness. The taint however, was still within him.
After [Thrall](Thrall) reclaimed [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream), Mannoroth lured them into the craters formed by the falling [infernal](infernal)s, where he hoped to battle them to the death. [Thrall](Thrall) confronted the demon lord in battle pouring all his strength and hatred for the demon into one powerful blast with his [Doomhammer](Doomhammer). Sadly, it was not enough. The powerful Mannoroth shrugged off the blow and tossed the young war chief across the crater leaving only [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream). Mannoroth reminded Grom that he owned him body and soul and that the blood curse could never be broken. 

[Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream) then faced the choice between giving into the demon corruption once more or resisting and saving [Thrall](Thrall). He chose his kin. In a heroic last stand Grom slipped past Mannoroth’s defenses and sliced open his chest. The demonic fires erupted outward slaying the surprised Mannoroth and mortally wounding [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream).

With the death of Mannoroth the blood curse on the Orcs was finally lifted. After his defeat, Mannoroth's remains and armor were spirited to [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar) and were attached to a large, irregularly shaped tree. The remains have become a memorial to warn against consorting with demons and celebrating the feat of [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream) in finally lifing the blood curse.
