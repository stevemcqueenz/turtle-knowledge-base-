# Hushed Bank

-The Hushed Bank** is the stretch of [Duskwood](Duskwood) along the western river. It runs from where the river forks and the three corners of [Westfall](Westfall), [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), and Duskwood meet south to [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). The covered bridge connecting Duskwood and Westfall is also located on the Hushed Bank. [Lars](Lars) and [Sven Yorgen](Sven Yorgen) could be found on the northern corner of the Hushed Bank. It was also inhabited by spiders and wolves.
