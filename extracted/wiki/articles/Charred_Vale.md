# Charred Vale

The **Charred Vale** lies in the southwest section of the [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains). Once a tranquil vale, nothing is left of its beauty, and an evil glow lies over the area. The very earth is blackened by the flames that consumed its trees. Today this place is infested by [Bloodfury](Bloodfury) [harpies](Harpy) who used to call this region their home, and wandering [Scorched Basilisks](basilisk), [Burning Destroyers](fire elemental), [Furious Stone Spirits](earth elemental), [Charred Ancients](treant), and [Chimaera Matriarchs](chimaera).

Interestingly enough, there are quite a few [Mithril](Mithril) nodes scattered around the region. Most [miner](miner)s will not have a high enough skill to mine Mithril when they first reach this area, however.

At its southern end one can enter the [zone](zone) of [Desolace](Desolace).
