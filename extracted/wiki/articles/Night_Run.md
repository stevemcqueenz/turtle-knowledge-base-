# Night Run

**Night Run**[66.6, 57.0] is located in east-central [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), east of [Raynewood Retreat](Raynewood Retreat) and northwest of [Splintertree Post](Splintertree Post).

It is one of the three Ashenvale [satyr](satyr) settlements, the other two being [Satyrnaar](Satyrnaar) and [Xavian](Xavian). Of the three, Night Run is by far the smallest, boasting only a few broken pillars and pieces of ruined architecture as shelter for the satyr who live there. It is also the hardest of the satyr settlements to get to, as it is located on a naze that juts out into the land with unclimbable cliffs on the side. To get to Night Run, one has to run along the [Falfarren River](Falfarren River) almost to the waters of Xavian before turning west[72.5, 50.0], or approach it from a narrow ledge north of Raynewood.
