# Stromgarde Keep

Stromgarde Keep is the ruined capital city of Stromgarde, located on the southwest corner of the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands) ([location map](Stromgarde location map)). It is divided into three sections - one under the rule of the [Syndicate](Syndicate), another largely under the control of the [Boulderfist](Boulderfist) ogres (though the [Witherbark](Witherbark) trolls control small parts), while the last section remains under the control of the people of Stromgarde and serves as the headquarters of [Prince Galen Trollbane](Prince Galen Trollbane).

[Lord Falconcrest](Lord Falconcrest) commands Syndicate efforts in the Arathi Highlands from the ruined city. He is one of the most senior members of the organization. The Stromgarde militia based at [Refuge Point](Refuge Point) has placed a bounty on his head, and have seeked the help of Alliance heroes. It is likely that his death would disrupt Syndicate efforts in the region to a great degree.
