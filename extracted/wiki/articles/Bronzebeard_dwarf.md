# Bronzebeard dwarf

*This article is about the Bronzebeard dwarves' lore. For the dwarven playable race, see [Dwarf (playable)](Dwarf (playable)). For dwarves in general, see [Dwarf](Dwarf).*

The **Bronzebeard dwarves** (ref:  [33] [The Slavemakers](The Slavemakers)), also known as **hill dwarves**, (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, pg. 35) (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, chapter 15 - [Falstad Wildhammer](Falstad Wildhammer) - *"It would take a handsworth of these hill dwarves to bring one from the Aerie down!"*) are a type of [dwarf](dwarf) that hold the majority of the mountainous [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) region, in the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms). They are led by the [Bronzebeard clan](Bronzebeard clan).

They are the standard playable dwarves.
