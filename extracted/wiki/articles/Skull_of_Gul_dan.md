# Skull of Gul'dan

After [Gul'dan](Gul'dan) died, his skull was turned into a channeling totem for demonic energy.  The skull was used by [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul) to open portals to other worlds on [Draenor](Draenor).  Years later it would appear again on [Azeroth](Azeroth), this time used by the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) to corrupt the forests of [Felwood](Felwood).  The skull was eventually destroyed by [Illidan](Illidan) Stormrage, who absorbed its power, becoming half [Night Elf](Night Elf) and half [demon](demon).
