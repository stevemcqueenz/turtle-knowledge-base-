# Celestial Watch

-The Celestial Watch** is an observatory in [Karazhan](Karazhan). This is the room where [Khadgar](Khadgar) was brought to [Medivh](Medivh) for the first time. Back then the walls and ceilings were pierced by crystalline windows, clear and unfogged, with a brazier for heating. All manner of devices such as astrolabes and ancient texts were spread around the area. A map of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms) was stretched on a frame. (ref: *[The Last Guardian](The Last Guardian)*)

It is where the [Echo of Medivh](Echo of Medivh) is fought in the [Tower of Karazhan](Tower of Karazhan) raid.
