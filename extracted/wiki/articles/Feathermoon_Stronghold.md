**Feathermoon Stronghold** was the name of the [night elf](night elf) island fortress located off the western coast of [Feralas](Feralas) on [Sardor Isle](Sardor Isle) on the continent of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). It had a [hippogryph](hippogryph) point and could be reached from [Auberdine](Auberdine), [Nijel's Point](Nijel's Point) and [Thalanaar](Thalanaar). The stronghold was established by General [Shandris Feathermoon](Shandris Feathermoon), who for the past ten thousand years has been [Tyrande Whisperwind](Tyrande Whisperwind)'s right hand in the [Sentinels](Sentinels).

Feathermoon was most easily accessible by a [boat](boat) taken from Feralas in the [Forgotten Coast](Forgotten Coast) to an island [dock](dock), though many Alliance players who didn't like to wait for the boat preferred to swim.
