# Shining Strand

**The Shining Strand** is the name of the section of shoreline of [Lordamere Lake](Lordamere Lake) in [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest). It stretches from the Lordaeron capital city in the north and ends at the range of rough hills that divides Silverpine from the [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains). 

The [Vile Fin](Vile Fin) tribe of [murlocs](murlocs) has several settlements along the Strand making it very dangerous for unseasoned travellers. In his journal, the late [Apothecary Berard](Apothecary Berard) spoke of a rare item that could be obtained from the Vile Fins, a Hardened Tumor. Berard speculated that the tumor could be used in the concoction of a new plague.
