# Uplands

-The Uplands** are a region in the northern [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains). The area was the bread basket of the Kingdom of [Alterac](Alterac), but after the destruction of the core of that nation, the Uplands fell under the control of the [Syndicate](Syndicate). They remain under their control to this day.

This is a good place for Herbalists. There are nodes of [Fadeleaf](Fadeleaf), [Goldthorn](Goldthorn), [Khadgar's Whisker](Khadgar's Whisker) and [Wintersbite](Wintersbite), which is only found in the center region of the [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains).
