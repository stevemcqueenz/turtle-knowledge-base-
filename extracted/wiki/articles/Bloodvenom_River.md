# Bloodvenom River

The **Bloodvenom River** is a corrupted river in central [Felwood](Felwood).  The river spreads its corruption beyond Felwood, eventually becoming the [Wildbend River](Wildbend River) in [Darkshore](Darkshore). The banks of the Bloodvenom are crawling with noxious oozes and other corrupted wildlife.  Swimming is not recommended and do not drink the water if you have a compromised immune system.
