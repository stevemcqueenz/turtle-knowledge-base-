Archmage **Ansirem Runeweaver** is a [human](human) [quest giver](quest giver) located in [Dalaran](Dalaran) in the [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains). He is a current leading member of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) and is leading the process of the reconstruction of Dalaran. He has a daughter, [Catelyn the Blade](Catelyn the Blade), who decided to become a pirate and joined the [Blackwater Raiders](Blackwater Raiders).

At some point, he worked with Baron Revilgaz. He once visited Stranglethorn and found a troll relic, a blade, and later gave it to Catelyn who ran with it off.
