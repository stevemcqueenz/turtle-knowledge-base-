The **Crescent Grove** is an instance dungeon located in [Ashenvale](Ashenvale). A hidden grove in southern Ashenvale overlooking Mystral Lake that was once a retreat for druids for several years, an evil presence has taken root in the region.

## History
Originally a hidden grove that served as a calm retreat for druids, in recent times the Groveweald tribe has moved in while fleeing the madness of the Foulweald tribe, expelling several of the original inhabitants in the process. However, in spite of their attempts to escape the madness, they succumbed to it in time.

Kalanar Brightshine once lived here, before he was expelled from the Grove by the Groveweald furbolgs and his home was burned down.

Demonic forces of the Burning Legion led by the doomguard Master Raxxieth have established themselves within the grove, beginning to corrupt the glade. Already, the Legion has left their mark in the form of the Vilethorn Scar, upsetting the balance and disturbing spirits.
