# Fel Beasts

A large, dog-like [demon](Demon), often used as guardians and basically the untamed version of the [Warlock](Warlock)'s [Felhunter](Felhunter) [pet](Pet).

Felhounds are demon hounds used by the Pit Lords to sniff out
sources of magic wherever they be found. Felhounds, who feed
upon magic itself, are fond of draining the energies of hapless wizards
and then ripping their bodies to shreds.
