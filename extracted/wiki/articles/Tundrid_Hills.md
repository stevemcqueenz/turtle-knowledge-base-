# Tundrid Hills

-The Tundrid Hills** are located in the southcentral area of [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh) and west of [Gol'Bolar Quarry](Gol'Bolar Quarry). The hills are home to several species of hostile wildlife, and few travel here without a weapon handy. Sometimes, adventurers use the Tundrid Hills as a shortcut to travel between [Coldridge Valley](Coldridge Valley) and eastern Dun Morogh, although this is a significantly more dangerous route than following the road through [Kharanos](Kharanos).
