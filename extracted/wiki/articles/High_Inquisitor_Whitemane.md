# High Inquisitor Whitemane

-High Inquisitor Whitemane**, a priestess of the Light, leads the activities in the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery). The Monastery was once a proud bastion of Lordaeron's priesthood - a center for learning and enlightenment but with the rise of the undead Scourge during the Third War, the peaceful Monastery was converted into a stronghold of the fanatical Scarlet Crusade. Under Whitemane's command, the members of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) present have become ever more extreme in their battle against the [Scourge](Scourge). 

Their grasp on reality was slipping. As time moved on, Whitemane and her followers no longer trusted anyone that was not part of their own [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade), considering them plagued. Large numbers of innocent men and women were tortured and eventually killed because of this. 

### Lore Status
Whitemane is one of the four leaders of the Scarlet Monastery. It is known that both [Raleigh the Devout](Raleigh the Devout) in the [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills) and [Varimathras](Varimathras) in the [Undercity](Undercity) are determined to eliminate the leadership of the Scarlet Monastery, albeit for different reasons.

-"What, Mograine has fallen?  You shall pay for this treachery!"
After you have slain Mograine, Whitemane will join the battle *immediately*. Once you get her to about 30%, she'll shield herself, sleep the entire party, and begin to raise her fallen Champion. If you're in a group of less than 5, remained focused on removing Whitemane from the face of Azeroth. Once she's down, then focus on the raised Mograine and killing him (again).
