# Coldridge Valley

[Coldridge Valley](Coldridge Valley) is the starting area for young [dwarven](dwarven) and [gnomish](gnomish) recruits, and contains the base camp of [Anvilmar](Anvilmar). It is located in the southwestern corner of [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), and is linked to the greater area by [Coldridge Pass](Coldridge Pass) to the northeast. The road through the pass leads through [a tunnel](Coldridge Pass) which is infested with [trogg](trogg) raiders. It continues east and then north through [Kharanos](Kharanos), and further winds up the mountains, terminating in the dwarven capital of [Ironforge](Ironforge).

Coldridge Valley contains three small camps where dwarves and gnomes receive missions to defend the region from the encroaching troggs, trolls, and wildlife. Other tasks are also to be found, from collecting gear parts to delivering hot stew. When the young recruits are deemed ready to move on, they are sent out through the pass.
