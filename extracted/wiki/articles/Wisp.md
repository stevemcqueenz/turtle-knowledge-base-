# Wisp

A special [ghost](Ghost) form available to [Night Elves](Night Elf) based on a racial ability that allows faster travel when [dead](Death) to get to their [corpse](Corpse). A **wisp** looks like a misty, glowing ball of light that floats some distance above the ground.

The term also applies to friendly nature spirits who build Night Elf structures and gather resources for them. These wisps also have the power to detonate, causing a backlash of mana, and can become [Ancients](Ancients). Several thousand of them, known as the [Ancestral Spirit](Ancestral Spirit)s gathered at the call of the [Horn of Cenarius](Horn of Cenarius) to [Nordrassil](Nordrassil) to destroy [Archimonde](Archimonde).
