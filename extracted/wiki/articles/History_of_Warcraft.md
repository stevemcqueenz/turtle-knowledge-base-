# History of Warcraft

The **History of Warcraft** is a part of the [*Warcraft* universe](Warcraft universe). It is a comprehensive account of Warcraft's history created by [Blizzard Entertainment](Blizzard Entertainment) as background information to the *[World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft)* universe. (ref: ) The majority of the text was not newly written but instead copied from the earlier *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos Game Manual* and *World of Warcraft: Game Manual*. The full text was available on the official [*World of Warcraft* site](https://web.archive.org/web/20100207204945/https://www.worldofwarcraft.com/info/story/). Additionally, all chapters can be read below or within the game itself as [in-game books](:Category:Readable objects).

The compendium was first released on July 16, 2004. (ref: [Archived page](http://web.archive.org/web/20040722085445/http://www.worldofwarcraft.com))
