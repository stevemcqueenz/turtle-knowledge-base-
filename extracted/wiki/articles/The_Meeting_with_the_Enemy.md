## Notes
**Talking to Falgran Hastil**
What is it you need?

  > Gossip: I am ready for the meeting.

  **Falgran Hastil says:** I am Falgran Hastil, scout of Theramore. Why did you call for this meeting?
  **Kagoro says:** We saw you investigate the inn. We wanted to assure you it is not the Horde's doing.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** We found this shield strapped to the wall of the inn. Do you have any recollection of this object?
  **Kagoro says:** Let me see.
  Kagoro investigates the shield thoroughly.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** Do you know how it could've ended up in the inn? We found his grave. It was recently disturbed.
  **Kagoro says:** To disturb the grave of a fallen warrior I assure you, I know nothing of this.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** It seems like someone wanted to make us blame you for the burning of the inn.
  **Kagoro says:** Indeed. Have you found anything else in the investigation?
  **Falgran Hastil says:** Yes, quillboar footsteps...
  **Kagoro says:** False lead.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** I see you did some work too.
  Kagoro nods.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** And the badge of one of the deserters. Paval Reethe. We couldn't find Reethe, though. Have you seen him, by any chance?
  **Kagoro says:** This is... a delicate matter, and the actual reason I called for the meeting. Paval Reethe is dead.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** He was a deserter. While I still would've preferred to arrest him and allow for a just trial, I understand that you killed him. We do not blame you.
  **Kagoro says:** If it only was that simple. We knew he was connected to the burning of the inn, and we wanted to investigate him. Before he could talk... we were attacked. By Theramore soldiers. One of them bore the name Caldwell.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** Attacked? Unprovoked, by Theramore soldiers? I swear to the Light, orc. If you are lying...
  **Kagoro says:** I swear upon my ancestors and my Warchief. They killed him in cold blood. Quick arrow pierced his heart. We also barely made it out alive.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** Theramore soldiers would never... We shall investigate this. But if we find out that your words aren't true...
  **Kagoro says:** I am sorry for the loss of your comrades. While you investigate this, we shall follow another lead we have. Black Dragonflight.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** And we will investigate in Theramore. But be warned, orc. Your story doesn't add up.
  **Kagoro says:** Let's meet here once we know more. I hope you will find truth in your city of stones.
