# Maestra's Post

-Maestra's Post** is located in western [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), south of [the Ruins of Ordil'Aran](the Ruins of Ordil'Aran) and northwest of [Astranaar](Astranaar). It is a small camp consisting of a tower and a large [night elven](night elven) building. It is also home to several quest givers, including [Ashenvale](Ashenvale)'s only population of [human](human)s - [Feero Ironhand](Feero Ironhand) and [Delgren the Purifier](Delgren the Purifier).

Due to the low level of the NPCs here, Horde players often come to spawncamp [Feero Ironhand](Feero Ironhand) and the other two with him.
