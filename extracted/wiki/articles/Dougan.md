**Dougan** was a [human](human) [paladin](paladin) who fought with [Kardan](Kardan) and [Voldana](Voldana) during the [Second War](Second War) in battling the [Horde](Old Horde). In the aftermath of the war, he settled near [Grand Hamlet](Grand Hamlet) with his wife [Adena](Adena) and his twin baby girls: [Loania](Loania) and [Lieren](Lieren). Dougan set out with Kardan and Voldana to rescue several villagers who had been reported missing from investigating the cursed tower of [Karazhan](Karazhan). However, he never made it out alive.
