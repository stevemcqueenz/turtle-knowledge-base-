# Sanctum of the Fist

The **Sanctum of the Fist** is the third area of the [Scarlet Monastery Library](Scarlet Monastery Library). It lies within the [Gallery of Treasures](Gallery of Treasures), and is the hidden chambers of the Library where [Brother Wystan](Brother Wystan) prepares initiates with martial prowess.
