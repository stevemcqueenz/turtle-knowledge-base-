# Waterspring Field

The **Waterspring Field** of the [Tanaris Desert](Tanaris Desert) is so named because of the many water towers which pump the life-giving liquid from reserves deep underground. The towers were built by the [goblin](goblin)s of [Gadgetzan](Gadgetzan) in an attempt to provide water for their desert operations. However, these intentions were botched when a large group of nomadic bandits calling themselves the [Wastewander](Wastewander)s strolled in and claimed the field for themselves, selfishly hijacking the water towers in the process. The bandits now squat on the land hoping against hope that this home will be better than that one [ogre](ogre) camp. They still get the shivers thinking about it.
