# List of Turtle WoW clones

*"What is dead may never die."
-Common saying on the Iron Islands*
Although "official" Turtle WoW servers shut down on May 15, 2026, there are a number of independent **private servers running a version of the Turtle WoW client**. This page aims at providing an overview along with some basic information on each server (ordered by launch date).

## Sandworlds
- **Website:** https://sandworlds.com/
- **Discord:** https://discord.gg/KqQKgWBY73
- **Launched:** August 2025
- **Realms:** 1 (Normal-PvE)
- **Predominant languages:** Brazilian Portuguese; growing English-speaking community
- **Approximate population as of August 2026:** <100
- **Average ping:** ~100 ms from NA; ~200 ms from EU
- **Turtle WoW client version and language:** [1.17.1](Patch 1.17.1) (English)
- **Main deviations from Turtle WoW client:** More logistical convenience (more bank NPCs, stacks of up to 100) but fewer bag drops. Tougher enemies and questing, but bonus group-XP. Enchants for necklaces/rings. Special crafted items/consumables for Goblins and High Elves. Additional fixes of bugs and exploits.
- **Comments:** Only 2 (part-time) developers. No plans to recreate any patches beyond 1.17.1 or for other content releases. Some elements of [Patch 1.17.2](Patch 1.17.2) (e.g., dungeon extensions, but no jewelcrafting) currently tested on PTR; [Roadmap](https://sandworlds.com/#/roadmap) lists only minor mechanical and balance adjustments.
- **Anti-takedown measures:** None known.

## OctoWoW
- **Website:** https://octowow.st/
- **Unofficial Discords:** [OctoWoW Hub](https://discord.gg/yspPTHn8Aa) and [OctoWow Fan Club](https://discord.gg/sBtsZKgHwe)
- **Launched:** April 2026
- **Realms:** 3: "N'Zoth" (Normal-PvE), "C'Thun" (Hardcore-PvE), "Y'Shaarj" (Normal-PvP)
- **Predominant languages:** English
- **Approximate population as of September 2026:** ~3,800 (N'Zoth), ~600 (C'Thun), ~400 (Y'Shaarj)
- **Average ping:** ~120 ms from NA; ~100 ms from EU
- **Turtle WoW client version and language:** [1.18.1](Patch 1.18.1) (English; rebuilt from 1.17.1)
- **Main deviations from Turtle WoW client:** New leveling challenges and associated rewards; quest log increased to 25 quests; crafted tents grant up to 33% of level as rested bonus (up from 25%); bonus XP on PVP server reduced to +15% (down from +100%).
- **Comments:** 1.18.1 was restored from data mined from the original Turtle WoW before it was shut down (restoration completed on July 11, 2026). Regular patches with bugfixes. [Roadmap](https://octowow.st/roadmap) lays out phased raid content release throughout 2026 and 2027; developers said that future development of completely new content is also planned. 
- **Anti-takedown measures:** Anonymous segregation - Developers do not know each other; Full redundancy - data stored in different countries by independent, anonymous developers and players can download their character data for potential re-implementation; Obscured infrastructure - no traceable third-party services (e.g., webshop, Youtube channels) that are directly attached to the project and could be accessible for western jurisdiction (Discord is run by fans not directly attached to the project; donation shop is crypto-only).

## Capybara Paradise
- **Website:** https://capycraft.io (only accessible with a non-US IP)
- **Discord:** https://discord.gg/7Acyh6pVcE (for English-speakers); official channel is on [KOOK](https://www.kookapp.cn/) (China-only Discord clone)
- **Launched:** May 2026
- **Realms:** 2: "Eternal Wilderness" (Normal-PvE, multi-lingual), "Gehennas" (Normal-PvE, Chinese only)
- **Predominant languages:** Chinese; growing English-speaking community
- **Approximate population as of June 2026:** ~10,000
- **Average ping:** ~250 ms from EU/NA (~150 ms using a VPN)
- **Turtle WoW client version and language:** [1.18.1](Patch 1.18.1) (Chinese)
- **Main deviations from Turtle WoW client:** None.
- **Comments:** Launched and run by former Turtle WoW SEA admins. Server Gehennas includes restored characters from the original Turtle WoW SEA servers. Can be played using the English client ([Guide](https://www.reddit.com/r/wowservers/comments/1thmnvy/if_you_are_playing_capybara_paradise_make_sure_to/)). Weekly patches with bugfixes. Phased raid content release throughout 2026 and 2027. [Roadmap](Media:Capy roadmap.png) lays out future content beyond 1.18.1.
- **Anti-takedown measures:** None known (location in China offers some protection against takedown).
## LunaticPTR
- **Website:** https://lunaticptr.com/
- **Discord:** https://discord.gg/XRHCpYxGWt
- **Launched:** 27 July 2026
- **Realms:** 1 "Redwall Keep" (Normal-PvE)
- **Predominant languages:** English
- **Approximate population as of August 2026:** <100
- **Average ping:** ~100 ms from NA; ~200 ms from EU
- **Turtle WoW client version and language:** [1.17.1](Patch 1.17.1) (English)
- **Main deviations from Turtle WoW client:** Level One Lunatic Server. Custom updates to Redwall Keep in Redridge Mountains. Added New Area The Grove in respects to Turtle WoW and a means to get World Buffs. All 4681+ Quests Available at Level 1. No Donations: over 200+ Shop Cosmetics, Mounts, Vanity Pets are placed on Quests throughout the World. Added full item progression for both PvE and PvP for Level One Lunatics up to Gnomeregan 40. Added custom boss to Ragefire Chasm and Shadowfang Keep. Added a Brawler's Guild series of fights to Gadgetzan Arena and a number of quality of life fixes for Level One Lunatics.
- **Comments:** 4+ developers. Actively working on future updates past Gnomeregan 40 and weekly updates have been promising. No Donations, transparent with costs of the project. Release Repack publicly available for other Turtle Projects to adopt Lunatic Progression more easily. The same people who designed Lunatic progression on the Turtle WoW Forum many of which were actually used in Ragefire Chasm, Deadmines, and Frostmane Hollow on Turtle WoW are leading this project.
- **Anti-takedown measures:** None known.

## RavenCraft
- **Website:** https://ravencraft.io/
- **Discord:** https://discord.gg/PYeP4475Sn
- **Launched:** 22 August 2026
- **Realms:** 1: "Medivh" (Normal-PvE)
- **Predominant languages:** English
- **Approximate population as of September 2026:** ~600
- **Average ping:** ~120 ms from NA; ~30 ms from EU
- **Turtle WoW client version and language:** [1.18.1](Patch 1.18.1) (English)
- **Main deviations from Turtle WoW client:** None.
- **Comments:** Claims to be [run by former contributors](https://archive.md/TYu9V) to Turtle WoW 1.18.1 and has the final Turtle WoW 1.18.1 core. The server follows a phased content progression, aiming to expand with long-term development and brand-new content [(roadmap)](Media:RavenCraft Phases Roadmap.png).
- **Anti-takedown measures:** None known.
