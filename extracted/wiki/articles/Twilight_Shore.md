# Twilight Shore

The **Twilight Shore** is a stretch of coast in southern [Darkshore](Darkshore). It lies south of [the Long Wash](the Long Wash), which ends due west of [Ameth'Aran](Ameth'Aran), and runs all the way south to [Ashenvale](Ashenvale). The Twilight Shore is the most rugged part of Darkshore's coast, and the gray beaches get broken up by jutting cliffs and small islands in the south. [Murloc](Murloc)s are abundant here, and the waters bulge with aggressive [threshadon](threshadon)s who do not hesitate to attack adventurers swimming for shore.
