# Garren's Haunt

**Garren's Haunt** is a large farmstead in northern [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), comprised of a farmhouse, a barn, a couple of silos, and a widestretched field. Although this was a bountiful place before the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath), it now lies gray and lifeless. Garren and his family were brutally murdered by the [Scourge](Scourge) during the [Third War](Third War), and his home is now residence to a host of undead [gnoll](gnoll)s who hungrily attack anyone who comes too close.
