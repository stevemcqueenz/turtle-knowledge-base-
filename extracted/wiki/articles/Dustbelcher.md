# Dustbelcher

The **Dustbelcher** [Ogre](Ogre)s are primitive ogres living in the [Badlands](Badlands) where they try to maintain a living.

[Camp Cagg](Camp Cagg), [Camp Kosh](Camp Kosh), [Camp Wurg](Camp Wurg) and [Camp Boff](Camp Boff) is the home of some of the Dustbelcher [Ogre](Ogre)s. The Dustbelcher tribe is led by Boss Tho'grun, who wanders in between the camps.
