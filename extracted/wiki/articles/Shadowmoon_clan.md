The **Shadowmoon clan**, also spelled **Shadow Moon clan**, (ref: *Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal* manual, *[Clans of Draenor](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Beyond_the_Dark_Portal_manual#Shadow_Moon_Clan)*, Shadow Moon Clan) is an orcish clan that was led by the orc shaman [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul), and was the most powerful clan on [Draenor](Draenor) prior to the world's destruction.

## History
Much like all orcish clans at that time, the clan was very heavily influenced by shamanistic traditions. Although the Shadowmoon clan practiced traditional orc shamanism, their leader, Ner'zhul practiced a much darker version of shamanism, that was heavily influenced by Warlock magic, a result of Ner'zhul's trafficking with [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden), a demon of immense power. Kil'jaeden had the objective of turning the orcs into a Horde of blood-thirsty creatures to satisfy the Burning Legion, a powerful force bent on the destruction of various worlds in the Great Dark Beyond. Ner'zhul later realized what Kil'jaeden was doing, and attempted to halt the orcs' corruption. His efforts were in vain, as his own student, Gul'dan, continued Kil'jaeden's schemes to horrifying success. (ref: [Kil'jaeden and the Shadow Pact](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Kil%27jaeden_and_the_Shadow_Pact))

Following the Horde's defeat on Azeroth at the end of the Second War, Ner'zhul rallied the clans still left on Draenor under his new banner. Aided by the Shadowmoon clan, he planned to open portals to new worlds for the orcs to conquer using four artifacts: the Skull of Gul'dan, the Jeweled Scepter of Sargeras, the Eye of Dalaran and the Book of Medivh. (ref: [The Invasion of Draenor](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Invasion_of_Draenor)) During this time, the Shadowmoon ruled over the scattered orcish clans, and Ner'zhul kept the other clans subjugated through fear and brute force. (ref: 

After many battles with the [Alliance Expedition](Alliance Expedition), Ner'zhul gathered some of the death knights and a number of Shadowmoon orcs to begin the Spell of Conjuration. Ner'zhul finally opened his portals to other worlds, but he did not foresee the terrible price he would pay. The portals' tremendous energies began to tear the very fabric of Draenor apart. Ner'zhul and his loyal Shadowmoon clan passed through the largest of the newly created portals, and shortly thereafter the world ripped apart.

On the other side of the portal, Ner'zhul and his companions entered the Twisting Nether, where they were captured by Kil'jaeden. Kil'jaeden gruesomely tortured Ner'zhul for some time before remaking him into the spectral Lich King. His loyal death knights and Shadowmoon followers were also transformed by the demon's chaotic energies, being ripped apart and remade into skeletal liches to ensure that even in death, they would serve Ner'zhul unquestioningly.<ref>[The Birth of the Lich King](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Birth_of_the_Lich_King)) (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* manual, *[Undead Hero Units](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Lich)*, Lich)
