# Fenris Keep

The gloomy **Fenris Keep** used to be the home of [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest)'s lordship, and housed numerous servants and several armed soldiers. The keep had strategic significance, and was home to a sizable portion of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron)'s military.

When the [undead](undead) [Scourge](Scourge) came to the land, however, the inhabitants were not expecting a betrayal from within. [Thule Ravenclaw](Thule Ravenclaw), an ambitious young mage, allied with the [Scourge](Scourge), and provided them with backing that proved catastrophic for the inhabitants of the keep. All were murdered or forced to flee, and Thule now commands a large force of [Rot Hide gnolls](Rot Hide Gnoll) to do his bidding. The undead gnolls hungrily attack anyone who nears the keep and their master. The [gnoll](gnoll) [Snarlmane](Snarlmane) is the general of the rot hides and the right hand of Thule Ravenclaw.

The keeps main purpose was to keep [Lordamere Lake](Lordamere Lake) safe, and also to let no threat come against [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) City from Lordamere Lake.
