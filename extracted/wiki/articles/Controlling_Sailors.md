## Objectives
Find, and speak with the Kul Tiran Sailors sight-seeing Stormwind City to make sure they are not up to any shenanigans. Once spoken with, return to Petty Officer Milldough at the Kul Tiran Embassy in Stormwind Harbor.
- Check on [Sailor Hylreth](Sailor Hylreth)
- Check on [Sailor Brewen](Sailor Brewen)
- Check on [Sailor Pardol](Sailor Pardol)

## Description
The life of a Petty Officer is much akin to herding sheep. I am in charge of keeping my men in control, and while that is easy on a ship, when docked in Stormwind it can be -very- difficult to have my attention on all of them at once.

They are spread around the city, sight-seeing, and no doubt getting quite drunk.

I could use a favor, if you don't mind. There are three sailors that I know of in the city, Hylreth, Brewen, and Pardol. Find them, and find out what exactly it is they are up to, just make sure they are not in any trouble is all, for my sake.

## Rewards

- +200 [reputation](reputation) with [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (faction))

## Notes
Head to the [Slaughtered Lamb](Slaughtered Lamb) in the [Park](Park) to find [Sailor Pardol](Sailor Pardol):
  You better back off before we have a problem, understood?
  > Gossip: Petty Officer Milldough has asked me to check in on you.
  **Sailor Pardol says:** Check in on me? What does he think he can control me here, and who are you, some worthless constable?
Pardol will turn hostile. Defeat him:
  **Sailor Pardol says:** I yield, fine! Tell him I'll keep out of trouble then, I got the lesson.

Find [Sailor Brewen](Sailor Brewen) wandering the Canals between the Mage Quarter and Stormwind Harbor:
  All this money I've saved up; no better time to spend it than right now, aye?
  <Sailor Brewen laughs.>
  > Gossip: Petty Officer Milldough has asked me to check in on you.
  **Sailor Brewen says:** Check in on me? Haha! I ain't causing no trouble, that goody-two-shoes Milldough should learn to have some fun. All I am doing is exploring, this place is much different than Boralus after all.

Head next to the [Pig and Whistle Tavern](Pig and Whistle Tavern) in [Old Town](Old Town) to find [Sailor Hylreth](Sailor Hylreth):
  The south isn't so bad after all. Good ale, and a chance to stretch my weary sea legs from the long journey.
  What can I do for you, mainlander?
  > Gossip: Petty Officer Milldough has asked me to check in on you.
  **Sailor Hylreth says:** All is well, enjoying some ale, the views of southern lasses, and enjoying the weather on dry land. He should know me not to cause trouble in foreign lands.
