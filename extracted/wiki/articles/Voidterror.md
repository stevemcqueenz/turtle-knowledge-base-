# Voidterror

The Void terrors are huge and extremly powerful two-headed [demon](demon)s. These beasts of demonic power possess a nearly unlimited source of demonic energy.
The only known Void terror in Azeroth (Kalimdor) is [Immol'thar](Immol'thar) in [Dire Maul](Dire Maul).
