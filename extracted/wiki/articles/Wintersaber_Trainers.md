# Wintersaber Trainers

### Introduction
[Alliance](Alliance) only [faction](Faction).

This faction has two NPC (Updated or corrected by [Parasol](User:Cutekangaroo), depending if at one time if [Chal Fairwind](Chal Fairwind) [(TB)](http://www.thottbot.com/?n=443521) existed or not). Currently, only [Rivern Frostwind](Rivern Frostwind) [(Allakhazam)](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/mob.html?wmob=10618) gives quests. He is located in [Winterspring](Winterspring) on the top of [Frostsaber Rock](Frostsaber Rock).
If you are [Exalted](Exalted) with this [faction](faction), you can buy a special [mount](mount) from him, the [Winterspring Frostsaber](Winterspring Frostsaber) [(TB)](http://www.thottbot.com/index.cgi?i=25287).
For any of the other races besides [Night Elf](Night Elf), you will also need to be [Exalted](Exalted) with [Darnassus](Darnassus) in order to learn [Tiger Riding](Tiger Riding) [(TB)](http://www.thottbot.com/index.cgi?sp=6745).

### Getting Faction
[Rivern Frostwind](Rivern Frostwind) offers three quests. You gain fifty Reputations Points for each turn-in so you must do the quests a total of 840 times to get the [mount](mount).

The first quest, [Frostsaber Provisions](Frostsaber Provisions) [(Allakhazam)](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/quest.html?wquest=4970), is avalaible at level 58. It requires five [Shardtooth Meat](Shardtooth Meat) from the [Elder Shardtooths](Elder Shardtooths) [(Allakhazam)](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/mob.html?wmob=7445) and five [Chillwind Meat](Chillwind Meat) from the [Chillwind Ravagers](Chillwind Ravagers) [(Allakhazam)](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/mob.html?wmob=7449).

The second quest, [Winterfall Intrusion](Winterfall Intrusion) [(Allakhazam)](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/quest.html?wquest=5201), is avaliable after gaining 1500 Points (halfway to friendly). You need to kill five [Winterfall Shamans](Winterfall Shamans) [(Allakhazam)](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/mob.html?wmob=7439) and five [Winterfall Ursas](Winterfall Ursas) [(Allakhazam)](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/mob.html?wmob=7438) located in [Winterfall Village](Winterfall Village), east of [Everlook](Everlook). This will also give reputation points for [Timbermaw Furbolgs](Timbermaw Furbolgs) faction.

The third quest, [Rampaging Giants](Rampaging Giants) [(Allakhazam)](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/quest.html?wquest=5981), is an elite quest and is avaliable when you get [Honored](Honored). You need to kill four [Frostmaul Giants](Frostmaul Giants) [(Allakhazam)](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/mob.html?wmob=7428) and four [Frostmaul Preservers](Frostmaul Preservers) [(Allakhazam)](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/mob.html?wmob=7429) located in [Dark Whispergorge](Dark Whispergorge) in southern [Winterspring](Winterspring).
