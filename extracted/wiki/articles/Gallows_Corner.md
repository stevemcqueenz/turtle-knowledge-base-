# Gallows' Corner

-Gallows' Corner** is a crossroads in the [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains), close to the [Ruins of Alterac](Ruins of Alterac). This area is held by [ogre](ogre)s who patrol the area around the ruined tower.
