Archmage **Antonidas** was the leader of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) of [Dalaran](Dalaran) and teacher to the Archmage [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore) of [Theramore Isle](Theramore Isle). He was killed by the [Scourge](Scourge) during the invasion of Dalaran.

## Biography
### Aftermath of the Second War
After the Second War, Antonidas was a member of the [Council of Six](Council of Six), the ruling council of mages of the [Magocracy of Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)). At the Kirin Tor's behest, an assembly of mages was dispatched to search Karazhan and seize all items of import for the purposes of research. (ref: [Archmage Angela Dosantos#Gossip about Atiesh](Archmage Angela Dosantos#Gossip about Atiesh)) Antonidas approached Archmages [Khadgar](Khadgar) and [Vandol](Vandol) to task them with assisting in retrieving and safekeeping artifacts left behind in [Karazhan](Karazhan) during the chaos of Anduin Lothar's assault and the death of [Medivh](Medivh). (ref: [Khadgar's Journal](Khadgar's Journal))

In the aftermath of the Second War, many orcs were rounded up and placed into internment camps. Some of the Alliance leaders speculated that it would be best to simply kill all of them off while they were weak. Others concluded the orcs could be kept as slaves. But Antonidas posed a different hypothesis. Researching what little he could find of orcish history, Antonidas learned that the orcs had been under the crippling influence of demonic power for generations. He speculated that the orcs had been corrupted by demonic powers (or warlock magics) even before their first invasion of Azeroth. Clearly, demons had spiked the orcs' blood, which in turn granted the brutes unnaturally heightened strength, endurance and aggression. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, [Orcish History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Lethargy_and_Internment), Lethargy and Internment)

Antonidas theorized that the orcs' communal lethargy was not actually a disease, but a long-term racial withdrawal from the volatile Warlock magics that had made them fearsome, bloodlusted warriors. Though the symptoms were clear, Antonidas was unable to find a cure for the orcs' present condition. Many of his fellow mages, as well as a few notable Alliance leaders, argued that finding a cure for the orcs would be an imprudent venture. Left to ponder the orcs' mysterious condition, Antonidas' conclusion was that the orcs' only cure would have to be a spiritual one. (ref: 

### Before the Third War
It came to Antonidas' attention that [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad), another member of the Council of Six, had begun to experiment with the arcane art of [necromancy](necromancy). As this threatened the safety and reputation of Dalaran, Antonidas decided to put a stop to it. He had Kel'Thuzad's properties searched, and all items tainted by dark magic were confiscated and destroyed. Antonidas warned Kel'Thuzad that he would be stripped of his rank and holdings, and exiled from both Dalaran and Lordaeron, if he did not abandon that path. Kel'Thuzad chose to leave Dalaran and swore to serve the [Lich King](Lich King).<ref>*[Road to Damnation](Road to Damnation)*)

Antonidas held the position as one of the four jurors during the court of [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring) in [Stratholme](Stratholme). Like the other judges, he was shocked when Tirion admitted his guilt, which led the paladin to his [excommunication](excommunication) and his exile. (ref: *[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*)

### Destruction of Dalaran
Though his failing health prohibited him from spending too much time away from his beloved city, his apprentice, [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore), served as his eyes and ears in the world. Antonidas drummed into her not to be arrogant and told her the stories about Medivh and Aegwynn. Antonidas looked forward to the day when he would hand his power and mantle of leadership over to Jaina, who he felt would make a most impressive archmage. (ref: [Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos manual#Antonidas](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Antonidas))

Antonidas, like many prominent figures in the [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron), was met by the mysterious [Prophet](Medivh) before the invasion began. The Prophet warned him that he must journey west, but Antonidas was heedless, believing the Prophet to be nothing more than a magical madman. Antonidas instead chose to focus attention on the plague sweeping across the north. (ref: [Jaina's Meeting (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Jaina%27s_Meeting_(WC3_Human))) He, like many of the Kirin Tor, knew that it was a great threat, and that it should be taken very seriously. However, Dalaran's plea for quarantine went unheard by the ears of Alliance officials. (ref: [The Warning (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Warning_(WC3_Human)))

Instead, he sent his finest pupil Jaina Proudmoore to investigate, who accompanied the young paladin [Prince Arthas](Prince Arthas) as he chased after the source of the plague. (ref:  The ensuing tragedy would ravage the mind of Arthas, and result in the destruction of the city of [Stratholme](Stratholme). Antonidas continued to study the plague from afar until finally it came to his doorstep. The Scourge, led by Arthas (now a [death knight](death knight)) and [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) (now a [lich](lich)), had come to claim the [Book of Medivh](Book of Medivh), as it was needed to summon [Archimonde](Archimonde).

Greeting Arthas and the undead outside the city, Antonidas warned the fallen prince that he and his fellow archmages had conjured a powerful aura that would kill any undead that stepped inside and warned him to pull his troops back or they would unleash their full power. Despite this, Arthas and Kel'Thuzad fought their way through the mages' defenses, slowly gaining ground. Antonidas and his remaining men made a final stand against the undead, but the Archmage eventually fell to them. In his dying breath, he told Arthas that it pained him to see the prince fall astray before Arthas slew him. Kel'Thuzad then claimed Medivh's spellbook.

Although the forces of Dalaran fought on even after Antonidas' death, Arthas, the undead, and demonic reinforcements were able to hold them long enough for Kel'Thuzad to summon Archimonde.<ref>[Under the Burning Sky (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Under_the_Burning_Sky_(WC3_Undead))) Moments later, Archimonde crushed the city of Dalaran into rubble. (ref: [The Destruction of Dalaran (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Destruction_of_Dalaran_(WC3_Undead)))

Antonidas's spirit lingered for some time after the city's destruction, continuing to fight what he believed to be the invading undead forces. When the blood elf Prince Kael'thas was fleeing the dungeons, he found the ghosts of Antonidas and several of his kindred, and finally released them, allowing them peace after so many months. (ref: [The Dungeons of Dalaran (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dungeons_of_Dalaran_(WC3_BloodElf)))
