# Threshadon

A large, long-necked aquatic dinosaur that can only [swim](Swim).  Found in [Darkshore](Darkshore), the [Wailing Caverns](Wailing Caverns), [Loch Modan](Loch Modan), and more.  There is apparently a threshadon named Nessy hiding in the underwater section of the [Deeprun Tram](Deeprun Tram).
