# Beezil's Wreck

-Beezil's Wreck** is a [goblin](goblin) [zeppelin](zeppelin) that has crashed in the deepest parts of [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh), east of [Tabetha's Farm](Tabetha's Farm). The zeppelin was carrying a shipment of supplies south, and is frequently sought out by adventurers looking for [Seaforium Booster](Seaforium Booster)s.

The pilot, only known as [Beezil](Beezil), is no where to be found.
