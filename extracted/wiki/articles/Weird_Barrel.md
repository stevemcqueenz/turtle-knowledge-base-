# Weird Barrel

A **Weird Barrel** can be found by a hammock near the pier of [Stillheart Port](Stillheart Port) in [Northwind](Northwind). Interacting with it while having [Perfumed Letter #3](Perfumed Letter (3)) allows for the acquisition of [Perfumed Letter #4](Perfumed Letter (4)) and a [Violet Key](Violet Key).
