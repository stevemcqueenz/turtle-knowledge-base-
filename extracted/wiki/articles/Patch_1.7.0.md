# Patch 1.7.0

-Patch 1.7.0: Rise of the Blood God** is a [content patch](content patch) for *[World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft)* that released on 13 September 2005.

## [Trailer](World of Warcraft 2005–2006 Patch trailers)
### [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub (instance))
  Hidden within the jungles of [Stranglethorn](Stranglethorn), an ancient Troll city full of peril has been uncovered. Do you have what it takes to delve into its mysteries with a band of hardy explorers? There's only one way to find out! Zul'Gurub is a high-level, 20-man raid instance with 120 new rare and epic items to uncover. Adventure awaits! 

### [Arathi Basin](Arathi Basin)
  Join the [League of Arathor](League of Arathor) or the [Forsaken Defilers](Forsaken Defilers) as they battle for the precious resources stockpiled within the latest [Battleground](Battleground), Arathi Basin! Pitting 15 members of each faction against each other, the race is on to be the first to 2000 resources, capturing strategic landmarks around the Basin to increase your team's gain and cripple the enemy. With an all-new set of reputation-based rewards, there's never been a better time to join the war! 

### [Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza](Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza)
  The Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza is a grand new event set along the coasts of Stranglethorn Vale. Early on the appointed day, friendly neighborhood goblins will visit [Ironforge](Ironforge) and [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar) to inform aspiring anglers of the grand tournament and give instructions. At the appropriate time, the shout will ring out across Stranglethorn to bait your hooks and cast your lines!

### World Environment
-The [Ranazjar Isle](Ranazjar Isle) sub-area has been added off the northwest coast of [Desolace](Desolace). The Ranazjar Isle puzzle piece now properly pops when you venture there.
-Entering the zeppelin towers at [Durotar](Durotar) and [Grom'gol Base Camp](Grom'gol Base Camp) no longer incorrectly flags you as being in the [Valley of Strength](Valley of Strength).
-[Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn), [Timbermaw Hold](Timbermaw Hold (faction)), [Zandalar Tribe](Zandalar Tribe) and [Arathi Basin](Arathi Basin) vendors now show you their entire inventory regardless of current reputation, allowing players to peruse their full range of wares. The items in question now require the appropriate reputation level to make use of them.
-Some of the ship and zeppelin paths have been modified.
-[Gor'tesh](Gor'tesh) and [Grol the Destroyer](Grol the Destroyer) should no longer stay in evade mode indefinitely.
-A new inn has been added to the Grom'gol Base Camp in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale) at the base of the zeppelin tower.
-The [Champion's Hall](Champion's Hall) and the [Hall of Legends](Hall of Legends) have new load screens.
-Some Horde quest NPCs were moved to new locations to promote more intuitive quest flow: [Storm Shadowhoof](Storm Shadowhoof) who was previously at [Bloodvenom Falls](Bloodvenom Falls) in [Felwood](Felwood) has been moved to [Everlook](Everlook). [Witch Doctor Mau'ari](Witch Doctor Mau'ari) who was previously in [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar) has been moved to Everlook.
-Scorpids above level 10 now drop more valuable parts than they did previously.
-Rare Felwood creatures should now have loot.
-[Twilight's Hammer](Twilight's Hammer) creatures in [Silithus](Silithus) can now be pickpocketed.
-Zone bound items, such as the [Gordok Courtyard Key](Gordok Courtyard Key), will no longer leave your inventory if you are a ghost outside their intended zone. Reviving in a zone outside the item's listed zone binding will still cause the item to disappear, however.
-[Pelturas Whitemoon](Pelturas Whitemoon)'s faction has been fixed; the NPC is now properly associated with [Darnassus](Darnassus).
-[Narm Faulk](Narm Faulk) is now a part of the [Ironforge](Ironforge) Faction.
-[Hadoken Swiftstrider](Hadoken Swiftstrider) now has gossip text.
-[Ferra](Ferra) can now be skinned.
-Fixed a typo in [Ogunaro Wolfrunner](Ogunaro Wolfrunner)'s greeting to those who are not yet friendly with Orgrimmar.
-Fixed a typo on a signpost in [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands).
-[Sayge](Sayge)'s buffs at the [Darkmoon Faire](Darkmoon Faire) are now exclusive to one another.
-Onyxia no longer teleports from point to point while flying.
