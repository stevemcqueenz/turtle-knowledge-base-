# Roguefeather Den

**Roguefeather Den** is a small cavern in south central [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles),[28, 53] filled with aggressive [screeching harpies](Screeching). The harpies zealously guard their gathered [foodstuffs](Harpy Foodstuffs) in the back of the den.
