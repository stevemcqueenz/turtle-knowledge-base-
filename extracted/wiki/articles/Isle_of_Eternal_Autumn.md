# Isle of Eternal Autumn

The **Isle of Eternal Autumn** is an island located just off the coast of the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands). Millenia ago, there were those amongst the elves that sought to plant a new World Tree. However, the endeavor failed, and the cursed tree wrought a transformative spell upon the island, condemning it to an eternal autumn. (ref: [quest: Whispers of Autumn])
