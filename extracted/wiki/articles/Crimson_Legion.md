The **Crimson Legion** (ref: [Tabard of the Crimson Legion](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=50092)) is a contingent of [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade)rs led by [Grand Crusader Saidan Dathrohan](Saidan Dathrohan) in the [Crusaders' Square](Crusaders' Square) of [Stratholme](Stratholme). Their forces are locked in constant, violent combat with the [Scourge](Scourge), (ref: [Game Guide/World Dungeons#Stratholme](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Guide/World_Dungeons#Stratholme)) but can also be found in strongholds and areas across the [Eastern](Eastern Plaguelands) and [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands). They are recognizable by their unique tabards, which differs from the Scarlet Crusade's [usual tabard](Tabard of the Scarlet Crusade) by an alternating pattern of white and red, with a white version of their Scarlet flame iconography in the middle.
