Archbishop **Alonsus Faol** was the head of the [Church of Light](Church of Light) and abbot of the [Holy Order of Northshire Clerics](Brotherhood of Northshire) prior to the [First War](First War) between humans and orcs. 

## Biography
Prior to the [First War](First War), on his fourteenth birthday [Medivh](Medivh) fell into coma and the Abbot and the [King](Barathen Wrynn) agreed that the future Guardian be taken into [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey). (ref: *Warcraft: Orcs & Humans* manual, *[Chronicles of the War in Azeroth](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft:_Orcs_%26_Humans_manual#571)*, 571) During the course of the war, the Abbot received a vision about Sir [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar) being trapped in the [Deadmines](Deadmines) so [King Llane](Llane Wrynn I) sent the [Defender of the Crown](Defender of the Crown) to rescue the missing knight. (ref: [The Dead Mines (WC1 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dead_Mines_(WC1_Human)))

Following the terrible attrition suffered by his clerics in that conflict and the destruction of [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey), the aged Faol fled with the surviving citizens of [Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)) to [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)). Aided by his apprentice, [Uther Lightbringer](Uther the Lightbringer), Faol founded the [Order of the Knights of the Silver Hand](Order of the Knights of the Silver Hand), this time equipping the followers of the Holy Light in the armaments of war. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Alliance Ground Units](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Alliance_Ground_Units)*, Paladin) [Alonsus Chapel](Alonsus Chapel), in Stratholme, was the site where he anointed Uther as the first of the paladins. (ref: [Aurius#Quotes](Aurius#Quotes))

Following the [Second War](Second War), Archbishop Alonsus Faol worked ceaselessly to rebuild the [Kingdom of Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)) and bring aid to the remaining nations of the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron). (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos Game Manual*) He helped to reform the [Brotherhood of Northshire](Brotherhood of Northshire) and raised vast funds to rebuild [Stormwind City](Stormwind City). (ref: [Alonsus Faol#Death and remembrance](Alonsus Faol#Death and remembrance)) He also took under his wing the kindly [Benedictus](Archbishop Benedictus) and spent many years teaching him the ways of the [Light](Light), and together, they built the [Cathedral of Light](Cathedral of Light) in [Stormwind City](Stormwind City). (ref: *World of Warcraft: Game Manual*, pg. 170)

During the trial of [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring) in [Stratholme](Stratholme), he held the position of one of the four jurors. Like the other judges, he was shocked when Tirion admitted his guilt, for taking a vow of honor to protect the orc [Eitrigg](Eitrigg) and refusing to break it, which led the paladin to his excommunication and his exile. (ref: *[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*, chapter 5)

## Death and remembrance
Alonsus Faol died at some point prior to the [Third War](Third War) and was succeeded as Archbishop by his apprentice [Benedictus](Archbishop Benedictus), who swore to continue the good work his mentor had begun so many years ago. He is presumed to have been buried at [Faol's Rest](Faol's Rest), a graveyard in the now-plagued Tirisfal Glades, located near the Scarlet Monastery.

A statue and fountain stands in front of the [Cathedral of Light](Cathedral of Light) in [Stormwind City](Stormwind City), honoring all the hard work done by Faol during the First and Second Wars, notably his raising funds to help rebuild the city and to reestablish the Brotherhood of Northshire.
