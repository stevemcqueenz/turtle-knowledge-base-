# Emerald Circle

The **Emerald Circle** are a largely autonomous branch of the [Cenarion Circle](Cenarion Circle) working specifically to undo the corruption in [Felwood](Felwood). They are stationed at the [Emerald Sanctuary](Emerald Sanctuary) in southern Felwood.
