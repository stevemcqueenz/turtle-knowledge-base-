**Isiden Perenolde** was the nephew of Lord [Aiden Perenolde](Aiden Perenolde), King of [Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)). When Alterac was placed under martial law by order of King [Terenas](Terenas Menethil II), Isiden fled to [Gilneas](Gilneas), where he hoped to rally support. He was eventually backed by [Genn Greymane](Genn Greymane), the king of [Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)), as the matter of the succession began to heat up. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, pg. 7)

Following the ascension of [Mortimer](Mortimer Harlow) and [Celia Harlow](Celia Harlow) as Regent-Lords of Gilneas in the absence of King Greymane, Isiden was imprisoned and held captive in the [Dryrock Pit](Dryrock Pit). (ref: [quest: Legacy of Perenolde]) However, he has since escaped. [High Officer Osmark Dryrock](High Officer Osmark) speculates that his breakout was orchestrated by loyalists hailing from Alterac, and that he may have sought refuge in the secluded and remote island of [Tol Barad](Tol Barad). Isiden's escape from the Dryrock Pit was kept secret from even the Regent-Lords in order to avoid destabilizing the broader political landscape of Gilneas. (ref: [Dossier: Isiden Perenolde](Dossier: Isiden Perenolde))
