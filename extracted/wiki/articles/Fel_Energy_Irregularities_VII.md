## Objectives
Return the news to Watcher Mahar Ba at Nethergarde Keep.

## Description
So, he desires to know about the Dark Portal, and the influx of fel energy? The demon's have been gathering for some time around the Tainted Scar, and have been using it as a safe haven close to The Dark Portal.

With the increase of Fel Energy, there is no doubt that they have plans for the future. This future though, may not come for some time given what I have heard.

Go to Mahar Ba, and tell him to gather the best of his kin, and to prepare himself, time ticks slowly.

Now, begone, I have repaid his favor.
