The **Ruins of Zul'Kunda**  are very large [jungle troll](jungle troll) ruins in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). This is the territory of the [Bloodscalp tribe](Bloodscalp tribe), and their leaders, [Nezzliok the Dire](Nezzliok the Dire) and [Gan'zulah](Gan'zulah), are stationed here. It is accessed via a path heading east from [Zuuldaia Ruins](Zuuldaia Ruins).
