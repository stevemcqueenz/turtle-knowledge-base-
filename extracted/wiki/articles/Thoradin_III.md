-Thoradin III** was the king of the [Empire of Arathor](Empire of Arathor) at the time of the Gilnean war of independence. When [Garalon Greymane](Garalon Greymane) gathered like-minded people of the city-state of Gilneas to rebel, Thoradin III led his army to the peninsula to pacify the revolution. Despite the Arathorian forces being over four times larger and better equipped than the nascent Gilnean army, the final battle in the hills of [Stillward](Stillward Church) is where Thoradin met his end (ref: [The First Greymane](The First Greymane)) when Garalon made use of dark blood magics. (ref:  [46] [The Ravenwood Scepter](The Ravenwood Scepter)) He was succeeded by his son, the young prince [Lenarol I](Lenarol I), who sued for peace with the Gilneans and bestowing upon them the lands of the Gilnean peninsula into Silverpine Forest. (ref: [The Silverpine Wars](The Silverpine Wars))
