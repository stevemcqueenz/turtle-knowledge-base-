# Ruins of Aboraz

The **Ruins of Aboraz** is a collection of ancient [troll](troll) ruins on the southeastern coast of [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), now the home of the witch doctor [Zanzil the Outcast](Zanzil the Outcast).  Zanzil's ghoul, naga, and troll minions wander the ruins, and the captured Darkspear troll [Yenniku](Yenniku) is also here as a thrall of Zanzil. The bewitched debtor to ["Sea Wolf" MacKinley]("Sea Wolf" MacKinley), [Chucky "Ten Thumbs"](Chucky "Ten Thumbs"), also roams the ruins as a ghoul.

It is located southeast of [Ruins of Jubuwal](Ruins of Jubuwal), east of [Spirit Den](Spirit Den), and south of [Crystalvein Mine](Crystalvein Mine).
