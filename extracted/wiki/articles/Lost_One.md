# Lost One

[Lost One](Lost One)s are [Draenei](Draenei) who have been driven mad from homesickness after being cut off from their homeworld of [Draenor](Draenor) when they were stranded on [Azeroth](Azeroth) by the closing of the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal).  Lost Ones have been completely consumed by madness and retain none of their noble heritage.  Lost Ones have become extremely hostile and will attack all on sight.  Sizeable numbers of Lost Ones can be found in encampments in the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows) while a few can be found wandering the [Blasted Lands](Blasted Lands) desperately seeking a means to return home. They have forsaken Shamanistic practices which they once had and have instead started to use [Warlock](Warlock) magic. They call themselves "The Lost Ones" for their lost homeworld.
