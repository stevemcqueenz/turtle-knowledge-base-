# Deepwater Tavern

[Deepwater Tavern](Deepwater Tavern) is situated in the town [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor) in the [Alliance](Alliance)-aligned territory of the [Wetlands](Wetlands). It is the hub of a few quests in the area, including [Quest:The Absent Minded Prospector (5)](Quest:The Absent Minded Prospector (5)), and holds a [first aid](first aid) trainer as well as a few vendors. Deepwater Tavern is frequently the site of bar brawls, most of which are initiated by [Tapoke "Slim" Jahn](Tapoke "Slim" Jahn).
