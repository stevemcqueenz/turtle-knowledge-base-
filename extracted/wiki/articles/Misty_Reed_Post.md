# Misty Reed Post

**Misty Reed Post** is a small [Horde](Horde) outpost of [Stonard](Stonard) in the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows). It lies between the [Stagalbog](Stagalbog) and the southern [Misty Reed Strand](Misty Reed Strand). It is guarded by [Tok'Kar](Tok'Kar), along with [Katar](Katar), some [Stonard Hunter](Stonard Hunter)s and a [Stonard Shaman](Stonard Shaman). Tok'Kar dreams that one day the post will get a [burrow](burrow) or at least a [tower](Watch tower). There is no flight path from here.
