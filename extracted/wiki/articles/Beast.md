# Beast

A type of creature or [mob](mob) in the game as distinguished from [humanoids](Humanoid). This includes most animals and some fantastical creatures.

Examples of beasts:
- [Cat](Cat)s
- [Bears](Bear)
- [Goretusks](Goretusk), also known as [boars](Boar)
- Most birds
- [Wolves](Wolf)
