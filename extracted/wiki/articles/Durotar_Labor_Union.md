The **Durotar Labor Union** is a group of Goblins that joined the Horde after defecting from the Venture Co. They're eager to prove themselves as capable members of the Horde.

## Reputation
[Vanessa Clang](Vanessa Clang) has the repeatable engineering material quests to achieve a higher reputation with the Durotar Labor Union. In return for exalted reputation, non-goblin players are able to ride [rocket car](rocket car)s.

Most quests associated with the Durotar Labor Union come from the areas of Blackstone Island, Durotar, [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains), and [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh).

## Trivia
- The Durotar Labor Union is the first original faction that was added to Turtle WoW.
