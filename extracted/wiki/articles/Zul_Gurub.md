**Zul'Gurub** is a 20-man raid instance located in northeastern [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), released in patch 1.7.0. Like [Zul'Farrak](Zul'Farrak), it counts as an outdoors zone, and riding on [mount](mount)s is possible. Zul'Gurub is the capital of the [jungle troll](jungle troll) tribes, led by the [Gurubashi](Gurubashi tribe), worshippers of the terrible god, [Hakkar the Soulflayer](Hakkar the Soulflayer), who personally inhabits a temple in the deepest parts of the city.

## Guide
The most efficient route through Zul'Gurub is as follows:

1. Enter the instance and continue straight across the first bridge, turn right and head up a hill and kill [High Priestess Jeklik](High Priestess Jeklik).
1. Go back down and across the second bridge to find the room on the left containing [High Priest Venoxis](High Priest Venoxis).
1. Behind Venoxis's Room take the right path, and continue going up. Here you can take a slight left up a hill and kill [Bloodlord Mandokir](Bloodlord Mandokir) or take a right through the path with spider webs to [High Priestess Mar'li](High Priestess Mar'li).
1. Returning back down the hill past the large staircase on the right those who plan on doing the [Edge of Madness](Edge of Madness) should do it now.
1. Down the path to your right you'll find [High Priest Thekal](High Priest Thekal).
1. After [High Priest Thekal](High Priest Thekal) off the path to the left you'll find the camp and shoreline used to spawn [Gahz'ranka](Gahz'ranka) who is a water boss, kill him if desired.
1. Back up the hill and down the road you will find a pyramid, inside is [High Priestess Arlokk](High Priestess Arlokk).
1. Go back outside and continue up the path and kill [Jin'do the Hexxer](Jin'do the Hexxer) if you want to, and have time.
1. Reverse back down the ramp, and over the bridge on your right to the temple of [Hakkar](Hakkar the Soulflayer), and you've cleared all of Zul'Gurub!

## Dungeon denizens
### Bosses
**High Priests**
The Priest Aspects all assume an animal form at some point when fighting them. Moreover, if they are left alive they grant [Hakkar](Hakkar the Soulflayer) powerful abilities that make him much harder to kill.

1. [High Priestess Jeklik](High Priestess Jeklik) - Aspect of [Hir'eek](Hir'eek) (Bat)
1. [High Priest Venoxis](High Priest Venoxis) - Aspect of [Hethiss](Hethiss) (Snake)
1. [High Priestess Mar'li](High Priestess Mar'li) - Aspect of [Shadra](Shadra) (Spider)
1. [High Priest Thekal](High Priest Thekal) - Aspect of [Shirvallah](Shirvallah) (Tiger)
1. [High Priestess Arlokk](High Priestess Arlokk) - Aspect of [Bethekk](Bethekk) (Panther)
1. [Hakkar the Soulflayer](Hakkar (tactics))

**Optional**
These bosses are considered "optional" because they do not give Hakkar any abilities, so skipping them won't change your encounter with the Blood God. However, most of them are involved in gaining powerful trinkets unique to each class, making it useful to fight each boss (except Gahz'ranka) at least once.

## Patch changes
- **Patch 1.9.3 (2006-02-07):**
  - [Zandalar Tribe](Zandalar Tribe) reputation rewards for killing mobs in Zul'Gurub have been increased.
  - A small number of spawns have been removed to allow for a slightly faster clearing time.
  - Tuning changes have been made to various bosses.
- **[Hotfix](http://blue.cardplace.com/cache/wow-dungeons/189347.htm) (2005-10-12):** A hotfix was implemented to revert the raid timer in Zul'Gurub to the intended 2 days and 12 hours. A bug caused the time to decrease to 24 hours.
- **Patch 1.8.0 (2005-10-10):** Bug fixes and tuning changes have been made to various Zul'Gurub mobs and bosses. Most notably, [Hakkar](Hakkar (tactics))'s [Corrupted Blood (debuff)](Corrupted Blood (debuff)) can no longer target pets.
- **Patch 1.7.0 (2005-09-13):** Added.
