# Peacebloom

-Peacebloom** is a type of [herb](herb) that can be gathered with [Herbalism](Herbalism). It is used as a [crafting reagent](crafting reagent) in [alchemy](alchemy).

## Source
-Peacebloom is commonly found and harvested by [herbalist](herbalist)s in open fields in low-level zones.
-Peacebloom is also sold in limited supply at certain herbalism [vendor](vendor)s.
-Peacebloom sometimes drops from some mobs in low-level zones, especially plant mobs like the [timberling](timberling)s in [Teldrassil](Teldrassil).
-Peacebloom can be looted from chests in low-level zones.
