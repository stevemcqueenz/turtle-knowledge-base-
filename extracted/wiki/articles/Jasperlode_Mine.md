# Jasperlode Mine

-Jasperlode Mine**, like [Fargodeep Mine](Fargodeep Mine), was one of the key mining sites in the [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), providing [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) with raw materials much needed in the wars. Lately, however, the mine has been overrun by [kobold](kobold)s, and with all the soldiers tied up on the front lines against the [Horde](Horde), [Stormwind](Stormwind) does not have the manpower to drive the vermin out.

Unfortunately for the kobolds, the deepest recesses of this mine have been overrun by a colony of [Mine Spider](Mine Spider)s who apparently see the hapless kobolds as a tasty snack. Although these [spider](spider)s do not venture far from their nest, adventurers should beware as their queen, [Mother Fang](Mother Fang), does not approve of uninvited guests.
