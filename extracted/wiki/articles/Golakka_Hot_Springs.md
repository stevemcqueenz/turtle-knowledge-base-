# Golakka Hot Springs

-Golakka Hot Springs** is a region in western [Un'goro Crater](Un'goro Crater), north of [Terror Run](Terror Run). The area features geysers and pools heated by the nearby volcano. One lone [goblin](goblin) can also be found here - [Krakle](Krakle) is investigating the connection between the hot springs and the nearby [Fire Plume Ridge](Fire Plume Ridge). An interesting note is that the tall white geyser behind Krakle is a lot deeper than it looks...
