# Owl Wing Thicket

-Owl Wing Thicket** is a series of caves in southern [Winterspring](Winterspring).65 60 Winterspring (g/s/c) They host a large number of berserk, moontouched, and crazed [owlbeast](owlbeast)s. It is here that the ancient Guardians of [Elune](Elune) tell how the [moonkin](moonkin) came into the world (in a quest chain exclusive to the Alliance), guided by [Ranshalla](Ranshalla).

Places to safely go [AFK](AFK):
- On top of cave entrances.
- South of the southernmost cave on a cliff overlooking [Frostwhisper Gorge](Frostwhisper Gorge).
