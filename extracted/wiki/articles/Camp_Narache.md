# Camp Narache

**Camp Narache** is the starting town for the [Tauren](Tauren), which is located in southern [Mulgore](Mulgore) at the northern edge of the [Red Cloud Mesa](Red Cloud Mesa).  [Tauren](Tauren) [Shamans](Shamans) will find their first [totem](totem) quest here for the [Earth Totem](Earth Totem). Camp Narache is the main village of the Hawkwind Tribe, and its [Chief](Chief Hawkwind) is a member of that Tribe. It has 5 buildings: the main tent (where Chief Hawkwind resides), a spirit lounge (with druid and shamans trainers), a bakery tent, an armory tent and a simple tent where the a trader stands selling normal things.
