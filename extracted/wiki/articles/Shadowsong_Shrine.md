# Shadowsong Shrine

**Shadowsong Shrine**[15, 73] was a ruin-covered hill in western [Azshara](Azshara). It was haunted by the [ghost](ghost)s of the [highborne](highborne), who also roamed the surrounding landscape looking to quench all intruding life. The shrine shared its name with both [Jarod Shadowsong](Jarod Shadowsong), commander of the resistance during the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients) and [Maiev Shadowsong](Maiev Shadowsong), older sister of Jarod and the famous [warden](warden) who was held responsible for the captivity of [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage) after his betrayal at [Mt. Hyjal](Mt. Hyjal). Any connection they have with the shrine is unknown.
