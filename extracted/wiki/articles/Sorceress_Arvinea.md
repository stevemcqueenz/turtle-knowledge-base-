# Sorceress Arvinea

## Description
Arvinea is a sorceress hailing from the kingdom of Quel'Thalas. She formerly served as an operative within the Sunstrider Royal Intelligence and is a seasoned veteran of both the Second War and the campaigns in Outland. In the present day, she maintains the identity of a freelance arcanist; however, lingering suspicions among her former associates suggest that she may still be a multifaceted spy, clandestinely serving an enigmatic master whose true identity remains shrouded in shadow.

### Appearance
Arvinea possesses an allure that is exceptional even by the exacting standards of the High Elves. Her facial contours are notably softer than those of her kin, characterized by delicate and refined features. Her most striking trait is her signature long red hair, which radiates a vibrant luster akin to that of a ripe apple.

Despite her tenure within the Illidari ranks during the campaigns in Outland, she has managed to preserve the brilliant sky-blue of her eyes—a rare feat achieved through undisclosed means that allowed her to resist the pervasive temptation of Fel corruption. Furthermore, she adopts a practical habit seen among certain Rangers, trimming her long Elven eyebrows into a shorter, human-like fashion to facilitate ease of movement during her various activities.

Though she has led a nomadic and unsettled life for many years, Arvinea adeptly employs arcane magic to shield herself from the rigors of travel. As a result, her complexion remains remarkably fair and supple, and her physique maintains a lithe, elegant grace.

An avid collector of fine attire, Arvinea’s wardrobe is as versatile as her identity. She shows a marked preference for robes that are light in texture yet exquisitely adorned. Her color choices are often deliberate: deep red silks to harmonize with her hair, pure white to accentuate its fiery vibrancy, or verdant green to imbue her presence with a sense of flourishing vitality.

### Personality
In stark contrast to the temperament suggested by her fiery tresses, Arvinea is characterized by profound composure and patience. She rarely displays agitation or panic, habitually concealing her true intentions and political stances. The refined upbringing of her high-society origins serves as a sophisticated veneer, beneath which lies an exceptionally sharp sense of observation and intuition. In general, she is perceived as stoic, pragmatic, and quiet, moving through the world with an aura of detached mystery. However, as a seasoned magus, she is not immune to the common traits of her order: she can be selfish, cunning, and possesses a characteristic indifference toward conventional morality.

In her private life, Arvinea adheres to a philosophy where love and beauty are held in equal regard to life itself. She is a proponent of secular pleasures, dedicated to cultivating comfortable living conditions and maintaining uncompromisingly high aesthetic standards. Consequently, she does not engage in traditional mourning for the fall of Quel'Thalas; instead, she seeks to preserve the essence of her homeland's spirit by embodying its elegance and refinement within herself.

Since her escape from the Violet Hold, Arvinea has been diligently working to repair her compromised linguistic faculties. As a result, she avoids initiating conversation, preferring to communicate through nuanced glances, facial expressions, and subtle micro-movements. When speech becomes unavoidable, she employs a minimalist style of language that is both simple and unfailingly polite.

### Abilities and Skills
Arvinea was classically trained in the high arts of Elven sorcery from a young age, receiving a comprehensive and elite education. Among the eight traditional schools of magic, she specializes in Conjuration and Transmutation. Furthermore, as a battle-mage, she possesses a refined and formidable mastery over elemental offensive spells.

In addition to her arcane prowess, Arvinea underwent advanced training as an intelligence operative. She excels in the cultivation of clandestine networks and the complex analysis of gathered information. And, the intricate art of weaving magic into espionage—a discipline once perfected by her people—is a craft that has become exceedingly rare following the collapse of the old kingdom.

Like many of her kin, Arvinea suffers from a profound addiction to magic, a torment that intensified significantly during her time in Outland, far from the Ley Lines of Azeroth. However, she managed to satiate this hunger through means distinct from Fel energy. This suggests she has acquired obscure, extraterrestrial knowledge that fundamentally altered her methodology of mana acquisition. Though she remains tight-lipped about her secrets, lingering evidence hints that she may have evolved into a rare variant of the Blood Mage.

## Background Story
Arvinea was born into House Melloren, a venerable family of vintners in Quel'Thalas. Her original true name has since become a mystery. Centuries ago, House Melloren suffered significant losses following a failed political struggle and was forced out of Silvermoon’s inner circles. From then on, the family discouraged its members from involving themselves in politics. Arvinea, however, ignored this tradition. A graduate of Falthrien Academy and a formally trained Sorceress, she sought to ascend within the magi hierarchy of Silvermoon.

When the Second War erupted, Arvinea was called to service. During the defense of Eversong Woods, her unit was shattered by the Dragonmaw Clan, leaving her stranded behind Horde lines. She successfully rallied scattered soldiers to conduct guerrilla warfare, striking Horde supply lines, ambushing raiding parties, and assassinating Amani officers until the Horde withdraw from Quel'Thalas. Subsequently, she joined the Alliance Expedition and fought as far as the Blackrock Mountain.

After the war, Arvinea was decorated for her performance. Her intellect and appearance caught the attention of the Crown, leading to her recruitment by the Royal Secret Intelligence. Trained as a magical spy, she was dispatched to former Allied nations to gather intelligence, filling the diplomatic vacuum left after Quel'Thalas withdrew from the Alliance. Assigned to Dalaran, she went undercover under the guise of a merchant for the Melloren Wine Association and served as its "poster girl." Her beauty and the reputation of "Melloren Amber" wine allowed her frequent access to high society to steal intelligence.

Furthermore, she held a secret mission directly from the King: to monitor Prince Kael'thas Sunstrider. Her task was to ensure that his duties to Dalaran and his affection for a certain human girl did not supersede the interests of his homeland.

Arvinea’s espionage in Dalaran continued for several years. However, Kael'thas, a master of Abjuration, eventually uncovered her surveillance. Before she realized her exposure, she was secretly arrested and imprisoned in the Violet Hold. She refused to divulge any information. To prevent her from using magic tricks, the jailers used spells to damage her incantation abilities, alongside other forms of torture. As the authorities in Quel'Thalas remained silent regarding the scandal, no rescue was attempted. However, this imprisonment proved a blessing in disguise; while in the dungeon, she was shielded from the Scourge invasion, as the destruction of both Quel'Thalas and Dalaran failed to reach her.

One day, Arvinea discovered that "King" Kael'thas had also been cast into the Violet Hold, making her perhaps the only soul there capable of laughter. Shortly after, Kael'thas staged a jailbreak with Naga assistance. Arvinea regained her freedom during the riot and escaped to Outland with Kael'thas’s Blood Elf forces.

In Outland, Arvinea was forced to pledge loyalty to the new King. She participated as a Sorceress in all the Blood Elf campaigns. Despite her damaged casting abilities, she maintained her position through non-incantatory spells and veteran combat experience. From the liberation of Illidan to the fall of the Black Temple, she witnessed the defeat of Magtheridon and the arrival of Kil'jaeden, experiencing a hellish war unimaginable on Azeroth. She refused to consume Fel energy to sate her magic addiction, which caused her immense suffering. However, from ancient alien scrolls seized at the Black Temple, she learned secret methods: she created a series of unique mana gems and implanted a ruby into her own body. These gems allowed her to refine and absorb "anima" from sources she encountered—including, but not limited to, potent blood and Mojo. This provided her with a new source of mana and gradually repaired the magical trauma from her imprisonment.

Twenty-five years after the opening of the Dark Portal, Arvinea returned to Azeroth as a follower of Grand Magister Rommath to re-establish contact with her homeland. However, she vanished immediately after crossing the portal. Rommath declared her a deserter. Since then, she has occasionally appeared under her current name, traveling with various adventurers before disappearing again. Some have seen her cooperating with the Silver Covenant, others found her under the protection of SI:7, and some suspect she gathers intelligence for a mysterious master. Yet, no single individual has connected these clues, and her true face remains unknown.
