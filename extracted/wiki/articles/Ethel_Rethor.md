# Ethel Rethor

**Ethel Rethor** [41, 31] is a [night elven](night elven) ruin in the far northwest of [Desolace](Desolace).  Most of the ruins are located under-water with the exception of a lone tower with a small bridge leading to an un-openable entrance.  In the shadow of the tower, to the immediate north, there is a representative of the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn) named [Azore Aldamort](Azore Aldamort).

As with many other coastal elven ruins this one is also inhabited by [naga](naga), though they seem to avoid the tower entirely.
