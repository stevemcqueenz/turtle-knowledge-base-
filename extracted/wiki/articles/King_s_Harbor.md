# King's Harbor

**King's Harbor** is located to the southeast of [New Avalon](New Avalon), below the bluffs. It may be on Tyr's Bay. The *Embertide*, a ship of the [Scarlet Fleet](Scarlet Fleet), is docked in the harbor.
