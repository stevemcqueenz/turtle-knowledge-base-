# Wyrmbog

The **Wyrmbog** is the area in the south of [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh), south of [the Dragonmurk](the Dragonmurk). Many dragons of the [black dragonflight](black dragonflight), including [Onyxia](Onyxia) and [Emberstrife](Emberstrife), reside here. The entrances to [Emberstrife's Den](Emberstrife's Den) and [Onyxia's Lair](Onyxia's Lair) are located within this bog. 

The southern reaches of Dustwallow Marsh are called “Wyrmbog” for all of the black drakes and dragon whelps that lurk among the shadowed rocks and slither across the waters to attack anything foolish enough to come near. The name "Wyrmbog" is probably derived from a similar land of dragons in Terry Pratchett's Discworld series that was called the "Wyrmburg." Alliance and Horde often come into conflict with the dragonkind as they skirt the edges of Wyrmbog on the way to other parts of Kalimdor. (ref: )
