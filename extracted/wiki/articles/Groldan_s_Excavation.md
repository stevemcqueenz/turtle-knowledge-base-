# Groldan's Excavation

**Groldan's Excavation** is an extensive dwarven digsite located to the northeast of [Lake Kithas](Lake Kithas) in the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches), led by [Groldan Blackforge](Groldan Blackforge) of the [Explorers' League](Explorers' League). However, the dig site has been attacked by [Dark Iron dwarves](Dark Iron dwarves) under the cover of night, slaughtering most of the League's prospectors and miners. The survivors have gathered in [Dun Kithas](Dun Kithas).
