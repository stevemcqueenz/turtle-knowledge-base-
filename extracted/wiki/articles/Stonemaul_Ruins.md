# Stonemaul Ruins

The **Stonemaul Ruins** are the former home of the [Stonemaul](Stonemaul) [Ogre](Ogre)s. The original village was destroyed by members of [Onyxia](Onyxia)'s brood. The ogre leader, [Overlord Mok'Morokk](Overlord Mok'Morokk) led his people away from the ruin and established a new home to the north called [Brackenwall Village](Brackenwall Village).

The ruins are located within [the Dragonmurk](the Dragonmurk) and are currently infested with [Firemane](Firemane) [Dragonspawn](Dragonspawn).
