# Tuskarr

Humanoid walrus people who live in [Northrend](Northrend).  Although seen throughout Northrend, they make their homes primarily in the [Borean Tundra](Borean Tundra).
- **Note:** Has only appeared in [Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne](Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne) so far.
