The **Lupine Coven** (ref: [Ethan Ravencroft#Quotes](Ethan Ravencroft#Quotes)) is an order that seeks power and healthiness beyond what the human body is capable of, and to that end have accepted the [worgen curse](worgen curse), though they have yet to be able to fully control their abilities. (ref: [quest: Vilest of Men]) Basing themselves off the research done by Arugal on the worgen, they claim to be able to achieve immortality, never again to fear sickness or frailty, and become empowered with strength beyond human comprehension. (ref: [Moranna Rosenberg#Quotes](Moranna Rosenberg#Quotes))
