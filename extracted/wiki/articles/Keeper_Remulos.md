**Keeper Remulos** is a keeper of the grove and one of the sons of [Cenarius](Cenarius). As the ruler of [Moonglade](Moonglade), Remulos, along with [Fandral Staghelm](Fandral Staghelm) and [Hamuul Runetotem](Hamuul Runetotem), leads the [Cenarion Circle](Cenarion Circle) in the absence of [Malfurion Stormrage](Malfurion Stormrage).

Like Cenarius, he has produced many children, including [Celebras](Celebras) and several [dryads](dryads). He resembles his father, having the lower body of a stag, the upper body of a night elf sporting antlers, and a long wooden talon for one hand.  Remulos maintains the Moonglade, the sacred center of all [druidic](druid) studies.  He is also an important member of the [Cenarion Circle](Cenarion Circle), but has found himself increasingly in conflict with the [night elf](night elf) Archdruid [Fandral Staghelm](Fandral Staghelm).  Against Staghelm's wishes, Remulos welcomed the [tauren](tauren) into the Cenarion Circle and taught them many closely guarded secrets.  Remulos also vehemently opposed the creation of a new [World Tree](World Tree), seeing it as nothing more than a selfish gesture by the night elves eager to reclaim their immortality.
