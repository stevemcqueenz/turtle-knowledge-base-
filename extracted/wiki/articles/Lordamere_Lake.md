# Lordamere Lake

-Lordamere Lake** acts as a natural barrier between the [Forsaken](Forsaken) lands of [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest) and the contested lands of the [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains).  At its center is [Fenris Isle](Fenris Isle), which has since been taken over by agents of the [Scourge](Scourge), and threatens the nearby Forsaken outposts in Silverpine Forest. East of Fenris Isle is a small group of islands called [the Dawning Isles](the Dawning Isles). It is reportedly the second largest lake in the known world after [Darrowmere Lake](Darrowmere Lake). 

Although the lake was once a beautiful and safe place to swim, [Vile Fin](Vile Fin) [murlocs](murlocs) have now built encampments along the shores and among the islands of the lake, and on the Dawning Isles live monstrous creatures of animated flora.
