The **Fallow Sanctuary** is the village of a [tribe](tribe) of [Draenei](Draenei) simply called the ["Lost Ones"](Lost Ones (organization)), who live in the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows). All the Lost Ones here have been driven mad with homesickness and turned completely feral.
