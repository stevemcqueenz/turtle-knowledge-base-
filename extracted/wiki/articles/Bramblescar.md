# Bramblescar

-Bramblescar**53 53 Northern Barrens (g/s/c) is the location of a tribe of [Razormane](Razormane tribe) [quilboar](quilboar). They are at war with the [Bristleback tribe](Bristleback tribe) which has a holding at [Agama'gor](Agama'gor) on the opposite side of the road. The quilboar also readily attack anyone who wanders close to their lair.
