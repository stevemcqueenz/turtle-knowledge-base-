# Shadowmoon Fortress

A fortress of [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul)'s located on Shadowmoon Clan lands on the southern Peninsula of Draenor in Warcraft II. It was destroyed by Alliance forces. After its destruction [Khadgar](Khadgar) and [Turalyon](Turalyon) searched the ruins for evidence of the [Book of Medivh](Book of Medivh), but could not find it.
