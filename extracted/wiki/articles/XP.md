# XP

[Acronym](Acronym) for "E**X**perience **P**oints". Unit of measurment for character [level](Level) advancement. To advance a level usually requires some amount of experience points.

In World of Warcraft, you generally only get XP from killing [mobs](mob) and completing [quests](Quest), but you can also get some from [exploring](exploring).

This is a descendant of the old D&D concept which almost all fantasy games use.

Some players might use **EXP** to describe the same thing.

## Mob XP
(*This information does not appear to be correct and is undergoing review. For the correct information see [Formulas:Mob XP](Formulas:Mob XP) -- [Greenman](User:Greenman) and [Gemminie](User:Gemminie)*)

When you kill a mob of a certain minimum level, you get XP.  That minimum level depends on your current level.  There is a formula to calculate that minimum level: take your level, add 1, divide by 5, and round up.  If you get less than 5, erase that number and replace it with 5.  The result is how many levels below you your minimum experience-yielding mob level is.  In short,

 MML = CL - min(ceil((CL + 1)/5), 5)
