# Library (Ironforge)

[The Library](The Library) is arguably the largest singular building in [Ironforge](Ironforge), and location to a large amount of quest givers. There is also an exhibit in the front part of the Library, where various sundry items, artifacts and fossils are on display. The Library can be found in the east part of the [Hall of Explorers](Hall of Explorers).
