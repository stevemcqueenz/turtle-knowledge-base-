# Ironband's Excavation Site

[Ironband's Excavation Site](Ironband's Excavation Site) is located in southeastern [Loch Modan](Loch Modan), south of [the Loch](the Loch) and southwest of [the Farstrider Lodge](the Farstrider Lodge). It is an extensive [dwarven](dwarven) digsite, but exactly like the others in the [Wetlands](Wetlands), [Darkshore](Darkshore), the [Badlands](Badlands), and [Uldaman](Uldaman), this dig site has been  invaded by hostile creatures, displacing the prospectors and miners. In the case of [Prospector Ironband](Prospector Ironband)'s site, the culprits are [trogg](trogg)s of a particularly large and volatile breed. 

It was once known as  Stormpike's Excavation Site, and run by [Prospector Stormpike](Prospector Stormpike).Before it was handed over to [Prospector Ironband](Prospector Ironband).
