## Description
A few months ago, a mysterious blind man moved into an abandoned shack south of Darkshire. A goblin accompanied him, helping him with everyday tasks in exchange for mentorship. He was a renowned engineer, of some kind.

Strange couple, that's for sure, though they professed to only be seeking shelter.

With all our concerns across Duskwood, I was only able to spare a single Watcher to surveil their dealings, and what little I've heard has done nothing to quell my suspicions.

If you venture there and find out what you can as you go about your errand, you would render a fine service to the Night Watch.
