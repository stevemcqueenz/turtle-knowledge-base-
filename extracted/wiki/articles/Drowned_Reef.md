# Drowned Reef

A dangerous and mysterious reef on the southern shores of [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). 

The dangers of the reef are many, and many a ship found its last harbour there, including *Maiden's Folly* and *Spirit of Silverpine*. Under the waters of the bay lies a ruined, sunken, ancient Night Elven temple, now inhabited by the [Nagas](Naga) of the [Daggerspine](Daggerspine) tribe, led by [Prince Nazjak](Prince Nazjak).

The Daggerspines seem to be able to control the tides of the bay, dooming many hapless crews trying to find refuge in [Faldir's Cove](Faldir's Cove).
