# Dark Iron dwarf

The **Dark Iron dwarves** (ref:  [48] [STOLEN: Smithing Tuyere and Lookout's Spyglass](STOLEN: Smithing Tuyere and Lookout's Spyglass)) (ref:  [31+] [A Grim Task](A Grim Task)) are a type of [dwarves](Dwarf). Though the majority of them are members of the [Dark Iron clan](Dark Iron clan), many are also members of independent organizations such as the [Twilight's Hammer](Twilight's Hammer), [Thorium Brotherhood](Thorium Brotherhood) and the [Venture Company](Venture Company).

Some dwarves from the Dark Iron clan became the cursed [skardyn](skardyn) that roam [Grim Batol](Grim Batol) after the [War of the Three Hammers](War of the Three Hammers). (ref:  [39] [The Skardyn](The Skardyn))
