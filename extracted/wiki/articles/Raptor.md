# Raptor

A large, aggressive lizard that stands upright on two large hind legs similar to a kangaroo.  [Troll](Troll)s use a variety of raptors as a mount.  Raptors are found in the [Barrens](Barrens), [Wailing Caverns](Wailing Caverns), [Wetlands](Wetlands), [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater), [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), and [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh).
Raptors seem to be very intelligent for a beast.  They wear feathers and beads, and in the [Barrens](Barrens) (where the smartest raptors are said to dwell) they have a village of mud huts decorated with bone ornaments.

## Examples
- [Ravasaur](Ravasaur) - Large, social raptors dwelling in the [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater).
- Sunscale Screecher - These raptors can screech for assistance, causing other raptors in the vicinity to [aggro](aggro) their opponents.  They are common in the [Barrens](Barrens). These raptors appear to have an interest in silver.

## Biology
- Wild raptors look considerably smaller than riding raptors.
- Wild raptors come in various colours ranging from pink to turquoise.
- Riding raptors seem more muscular than the ordinary wild raptors, their heads are less sleek. While the wild raptors resemble the [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velociraptor]Velociraptor on [Earth](Earth), the riding raptors resemble a [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deinonychus]Deinonychus.
- Level 40 riding raptors are larger than wild raptors.
- Level 40 riding raptors have a large curved horn protuding horizontally from their forehead.
- Level 40 riding raptors have small fins growing from the tops of their head and hips.
- Level 40 riding raptors come in Violet with Blue stripes, Turquoise with Yellow striped and Emerald with Yellow stripes.
- [Epic](Epic) riding raptors are slightly larger than level 40 riding raptors.
- [Epic](Epic) riding raptors have a small horn protruding from the tip of their nose similar to the wild raptor's horn.
- [Epic](Epic) riding raptors are usually decorated with colourful armor and feathers.
- [Epic](Epic) riding raptors come in Blue, Olive, Orange, Black([PVP](PVP) reward at [Honor Rank](Honor Rank) 11) and Green([Razzashi Raptor](Razzashi Raptor)).

## Traits
- Raptors are [hostile](hostile) predators.
- Raptor corpses are [skinnable](skinnable).

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Have high damage, medium armor, and low HP.
- Eats only Meat.
- Can learn [Cower](Cower), [Growl](Growl), [Bite](Bite), [Cobra Reflexes](Cobra Reflexes), and [Claw](Pet:Claw).  
- A popular raptor is Takk the Leaper, who had higher chase speed than most raptors before the pet run speed standardization. 
- Raptors are moderately popular as pets, though most players looking for a high-damage pet typically choose a [Cat](Cat).

# Epic Mount
The epic raptor is buyable in [Sen'jin Village](Sen'jin Village).  The PVP raptor is the Black War Raptor.  The Swift [Razzashi Raptor](Razzashi Raptor), a prized epic mount, is an extremely rare drop from Bloodlord Mandokir of [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub).
