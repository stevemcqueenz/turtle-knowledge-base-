Lady **Vereesa Windrunner** is the former Speaker of the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant) and a current member of the [Regency Council](Regency Council) of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas). An elven ranger during the aftermath of the [Second War](Second War), she is the younger sister of [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner), who led the defense of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas) and would later become the Dark Lady of the Forsaken, and [Alleria Windrunner](Alleria Windrunner), hero of the Second War.

Previously the Speaker of the Silvermoon Remnant, Vereesa was a key individual in gaining the re-acceptance of the High Elves into the [Alliance](Alliance). However, following the expansion of Alah'Thalas and the resettlement of the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands), Vereesa was deposed by the surviving Quel'dorei nobility, though she was included in the current [Regency Council](Regency Council) alongside [Lor'themar Theron](Lor'themar Theron), [Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn) and [Grand Magister Rommath](Grand Magister Rommath).

## Biography
### Day of the Dragon
To Vereesa's disgust, her first major assignment as a ranger did not involve hunting down the [orc](orc)s that had been trapped on the planet [Azeroth](Azeroth) in the wake of the Dark Portal's destruction. Instead, she was asked to escort a human wizard named [Rhonin](Rhonin) to [Hasic](Hasic), one of the eastern ports of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). She accomplished her mission only to discover that there were no ships available to take Rhonin to his ultimate destination: [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan).

Rhonin claimed that his mission was minor and only required him to observe the orc presence in Khaz Modan. Yet, he remained unusually determined to fulfill it. He even appealed to a group of [Wildhammer dwarves](Wildhammer dwarf), asking them to fly him to Khaz Modan on one of their [gryphon](gryphon)s. At first, the dwarves refused, for they trusted humans little and wizards even less. Yet Vereesa had grown fond of Rhonin, and she suspected that his mission was more important than he wanted to admit. Appealing to the dwarves, she persuaded them to fly both her and Rhonin to Khaz Modan. Although Rhonin was separated from her along the flight, she tracked him all the way to [Grim Batol](Grim Batol), which was a Horde stronghold under the leadership of [Nekros Skullcrusher](Nekros Skullcrusher).

Using a magical artifact known as the [Demon Soul](Demon Soul), Nekros had managed to enslave [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza) and, through her, most of the [red dragonflight](red dragonflight). Thus, [Korialstrasz](Korialstrasz) had sent Rhonin to Grim Batol with instructions to free Alexstrasza. Disguised as a mage named Krasus, Korialstrasz did not truly hope that Rhonin would be able to liberate the Dragonqueen. Rather, Korialstrasz hoped to trick the Horde into believing that a full-scale invasion was about to occur. While the Horde evacuated Grim Batol, Korialstrasz planned to strike and free his queen. His plan backfired when the massive [black dragon](black dragon) [Deathwing](Deathwing) unexpectedly showed up and began stealing Alexstrasza's eggs. In fact, Deathwing had helped Rhonin reach Grim Batol precisely for that reason: the insane [Dragon Aspect](Dragon Aspect) wanted to bolster his own failing flight by kidnapping Alexstrasza's children and raising them as his own. However, the other Dragon Aspects arrived to stop Deathwing. With the help of Vereesa and a group of dwarves, Rhonin took the Demon Soul from Nekros and managed to destroy it. Their powers fully restored, Alexstrasza and the other Aspects attacked Deathwing and forced him to flee. After the battle, Vereesa and Rhonin toured the various kingdoms in the Eastern Kingdoms and grew closer in the process. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*)

### War of the Ancients Trilogy
Following the success of the mission, she and Rhonin were tasked with touring the Alliance and reminding everyone that there were other threats in the world besides orcs that would have to be faced sooner or later. (ref: *[The Well of Eternity](The Well of Eternity)*, pg. 14) Along with Rhonin and Krasus, Vereesa was among the few well-known survivors of the Scourge that destroyed Lordaeron, Dalaran, and Quel'Thalas after the corruption of Prince Arthas. The rest of her family was not so lucky. They were one of the hardest hit by the Scourge invasion into Quel'Thalas. Sylvanas was killed by Arthas Menethil and raised as a banshee in his service, later becoming Banshee Queen of the Forsaken, and Vereesa learned from her grandfather that he had to slay the ghoulish corpse of his own son, her uncle. From him she had also heard how her younger brother had been ripped apart by a hungering mob of undead led by their own elder brother who was then himself burned with the rest of the Scourge by elven resistance fighters. Her parents' fate was unknown, but they, too, were presumed dead. (ref: *[The Well of Eternity](The Well of Eternity)*, pg. 16 - 17)

While she and her husband, Rhonin, were preparing for the birth of their twin offspring, Rhonin was once again summoned by Krasus for help. As a reward for Rhonin's courage, Nozdormu allowed Rhonin to return to Vereesa at the moment she went into labor to ensure he would not miss a moment of the twins' lives. ### Mysteries of Azeroth
Following the fall of Silvermoon, Vereesa took it upon herself to gather those she could find, and set out to reclaim the outpost of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas) in the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands). Though it was not an easy task, it gave the displaced high elves a place to call home.<ref>[quest: The Kaldorei Problem] *([Deprecated](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=40376))*) From there, as the Speaker of the Silvermoon Remnant, she sought to bring her people closer to the Alliance. However, as the resettlement of the Highlands progressed, surviving members of the nobility deposed her as leader of the Remnant, forming the [Regency Council](Regency Council) in place of the Speaker. Vereesa, as a member of the prestigious Windrunner family, was granted a place amongst the Council.

In the aftermath of the formation of the Council, Vereesa formed the [Silver Covenant](Silver Covenant), a political faction loyal to her vision for the future of the high elves. (ref:  [5] [The Silver Covenant](The Silver Covenant))

Under her leadership, the Silver Covenant was successful in its second attempt to reclaim [Shalandis Isle](Shalandis Isle), and Vereesa stepped foot in Eversong for the first time in eight years. Vereesa sought to use the prestige of her victory to reclaim her title as Speaker. However, this victory was bought through the diverting of funds intended for the Regency Council and the people of the Silvermoon Remnant. As such, it undercut the Covenant's victory and sabotaged Vereesa's attempt to reclaim her position when the matter was brought to light by Lord [Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn). To her horror, her victory was shattered before her as Lord Sunsworn then took the opportunity to motion the Council to be elevated to Lord-Regent of Quel'Thalas, having acquired the five votes necessary from his fellow council members - with even Lor'themar casting his vote for Lord Sunsworn. (ref: [A GOOD DAY — Everlook Broadcasting Co. Radio Play Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u90BCZfygz0))
