# Ravenholdt

An assassin [guild](Guild) opposed to the [Syndicate](Syndicate). Seems like it might be a [rogue](Rogue) only [faction](Faction). Their headquarters are located in [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), but to get there you have to come from the northeast corner of [Hilsbrad](Hilsbrad). The only way found (so far) to raise reputation is to turn in [Syndicate Emblems](Syndicate Emblem) (usually 25 – 35). So far there is no way to get above [Friendly](Friendly), and no [reward](Reward) (found) for obtaining Friendly.
