# Hearthglen

**Hearthglen** is a town in the northern region of the former Kingdom of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), located north of [Andorhal](Andorhal) in an area now known as the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands). The town (and formerly the region) was controlled from [Mardenholde Keep](Mardenholde Keep). It is now controlled by the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade).

Hearthglen was left relatively untouched by the [Scourge](Scourge) in the [Third War](Third War), now appropriated by the Scarlet Crusade who have turned it into a major base of operation. Prior to the transformation of [Arthas](Arthas) into a death knight of [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul), the Prince tracked a shipment of grain infected with the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath) to the town of Hearthglen. He, along with soldiers of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron)'s army, were able to slay those infected by the shipment before they could spread the disease to the entire town. However, shortly thereafter a large force of [Undead](Undead) laid siege to the town, but Arthas was able to repel the invaders until [Uther](Uther Lightbringer) arrived with reinforcements. Ironically, it was the efforts of the future [Lich King](Lich King) that saved Hearthglen and ultimately allowed it to survive to the present as a bastion of the Scarlet Crusade.

Previously, Hearthglen was ruled by [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring), Lord of Mardenholde, until he was exiled for treason.
