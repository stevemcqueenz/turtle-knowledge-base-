**Elling Trias** is the owner of the shop [Trias' Cheese](Trias' Cheese), which is located in the [Trade District](Trade District) within [Stormwind City](Stormwind City). He runs the shop with [Elaine](Elaine Trias) and [Ben Trias](Ben Trias), his wife and son. Giving the impression of being a mere cheesemaker, Elling Trias is a man with more to him than it appears at first glance. He has an extensive web of contacts, stretching from [SI:7](SI:7) to [agents](Kirsta Deepshadow) as far off as [Hearthglen](Hearthglen). (ref: [quest: Unfinished Business])

[Tyrion](Tyrion) is his old friend. (ref:  [31] [Infiltrating the Castle](Infiltrating the Castle)) He also has some kind of a history with [Jorgen](Jorgen), (ref: [quest: The Missing Diplomat]) is one of [Bishop DeLavey](Bishop DeLavey)'s contacts, (ref: [quest: The Missing Diplomat]) (ref: [quest: The Missing Diplomat]) had history in Stormwind with [Mikhail](Mikhail), (ref: [quest: The Missing Diplomat]) knew [Watcher Backus](Watcher Backus) of [Darkshire](Darkshire) (ref:  and [Commander Samaul](Commander Samaul) of the [Theramore Guard](Theramore Guard),<ref>[quest: The Missing Diplomat]) and works with adventurers to uncover [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood) activity in kidnapping a "[diplomat](Varian Wrynn)" at [Theramore Isle](Theramore Isle).
