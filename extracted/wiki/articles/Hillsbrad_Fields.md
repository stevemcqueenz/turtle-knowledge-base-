The **Hillsbrad Fields** refers to the area which includes the town of **Hillsbrad** and a large and fertile cultivated area that provided food for the people of [Southshore](Southshore) and the whole of [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills).

## History
### Second War
Early during the [Second War](Second War), the Horde began construction of facilities to support the deployment of its armada within the [Southshore](Southshore) region. Zul'jin's axethrowers and destroyers were eager for revenge on the [human](human)s who imprisoned them and assisted in preparation for the assault on Hillsbrad. (ref: [Southshore (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Southshore_(WC2_Orc)))

Meanwhile, [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron) scouts had discovered an orcish secret base in the [Zul'Dare](Zul'Dare) region of the [Channel Islands](Channel Islands), south-east of Hillsbrad. Admiral [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore) ordered for transports to land troops against the outpost. (ref: [Attack on Zul'dare (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_on_Zul%27dare_(WC2_Human))) At the same time, Doomhammer ordered the assault on Hillsbrad, which was destroyed in a gruesome display in order to make an example. (ref: [Assault on Hillsbrad (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_on_Hillsbrad_(WC2_Orc))) Hillsbrad was decimated, and the news spread throughout the human kingdoms. (ref: [Tol Barad (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Tol_Barad_(WC2_Orc)))

The Alliance had succeeded in pushing the Horde out of southern Lordaeron and destroying the Horde base at Zul'Dare, but at the cost of both the townships of Hillsbrad and Southshore which were left in ruins. (ref: [Tol Barad (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Tol_Barad_(WC2_Human))) Hillsbrad and Southshore were both later rebuilt after the war. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, pg. 107)

### World of Warcraft
Hillsbrad and Southshore are the only regions of former [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)) that were not left completely devastated by the [Third War](Third War) or joined the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade). As such, guards from both communities are still wearing the tabard of their kingdom.

[Magistrate Burnside](Magistrate Burnside) leads the town of Hillsbrad from the local [town hall](Hillsbrad (subzone)), aided by the Council of Hillsbrad, as well as [Clerk Horrace Whitesteed](Clerk Horrace Whitesteed). The Hillsbrad Fields lie in the western part of the Hillsbrad Foothills, east of [Darrow Hill](Darrow Hill), north of [Azurelode Mine](Azurelode Mine), and south of [Dalaran](Dalaran).

The farms' economy appears to be based around apple production, however the southernmost orchard produces something else. There are also pumpkins and cows to be found scattered around the fields. Azurelode Mine is also still being exploited by the people of the town.

The people of Hillsbrad are constantly threatened by the forces of [High Executor Darthalia](High Executor Darthalia) at [Tarren Mill](Tarren Mill), being repeatedly attacked by the forces of the [Forsaken](Forsaken). (ref: Battle of Hillsbrad quest chain) Peasants, farmers, and other members of the human supply line and infrastructure are especially threatened. Many of the farmers are very capable of fighting back, and have staged ambushes on the undead forces. (ref:  [28] [Dangerous! (quest)](Dangerous! (quest))) [Blacksmith Verringtan](Blacksmith Verringtan) and his apprentices supply weapons to the local militia. (ref:  [28] [Battle of Hillsbrad (3)](Battle of Hillsbrad (3)))

## Inhabitants
## Services
Three [Apprentice Blacksmiths](Hillsbrad Apprentice Blacksmith) sell assorted weaponry. They are directed by a man named [Verringtan](Blacksmith Verringtan). Two [Tailors](Hillsbrad Tailor), vending assorted clothing, inhabit a small house on the far side of the southernmost orchard.

## PvP Significance
In the spirit of Hillsbrad Foothill's infamous PvP battles, Horde players need to be careful while completing the  [24] [Battle of Hillsbrad (quest)](Battle of Hillsbrad (quest)) quest. It is a known spot for high level Alliance [gank](gank)ing.

## Trivia
- In the map from the *World of Warcraft: Game Manual*, the "Hillsbrad Fields" was simply known as "Hillsbrad", just like in *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness*.

## Speculation
### The farms: a family holding?
Many of the Hillsbrad farmers, footmen and councilors were redheads. This gave rise to the possibility that the Hillsbrad Fields were privately owned and operated perhaps by a large farming family. It could also have been that the Hillsbrad humans had a dominant redhead gene, considering most if not all of the [Southshore Guard](Southshore Guard)s were auburns.

According to  [24] [Battle of Hillsbrad (quest)](Battle of Hillsbrad (quest)), the landowners were [Farmer Ray](Farmer Ray) and [Farmer Getz](Farmer Getz). Whether these two farmers were related (brothers, father/son...) is undetermined.

A sharp eye could notice that the Magistrate and his clerk were not redheads or auburns like the rest of the Fields. So if the farms were owned by a family, it was likely that they were subject to some degree of outside authority.
