-Jousting Spectators** are humans and dwarves located at the [Jousting Grounds](Jousting Grounds) in [Northwind](Northwind), watching over the tournament.

## Quotes
During the joust,  the spectators will comment on the knights' performances.
-Hopefully I don't get too much dust tossed my way.
-Here it comes!
-Ridiculous.
-What a masterful display. A bliss to behold time and time again.
-Hm. Not too shabby. Regrettably.
-NO! How could this happen? All my gold! Gone!
-By the Light! Incredible! I cannot wait for the next round.
