# Thelgen Rock

[Thelgen Rock](Thelgen Rock) is an area in the southern [Wetlands](Wetlands) that is crawling with poisonous spiders. The name refers to an extensive cavern that stretches deep into the [Khaz Mountains](Khaz Mountains). The caves hold deposits of a rare ore that miners sometimes come to dig up. Frustrated cartographers can also be seen roaming the area trying in vain to explore the last piece of their maps.
