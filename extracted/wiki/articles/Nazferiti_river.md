# Nazferiti river

This unnamed river is one of the longest in the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms). It rises in the southern peaks of the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains), and can be seen tumbling down a waterfall in southwestern Redridge. It then flows west, bordering [Duskwood](Duskwood) and [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), making it the natural border between the two regions. A tributary flowing from the mountains southwest of [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) tumbles into the river and there it sharply turns and heads south, marking the borderline of [Westfall](Westfall) with Elwynn Forest, and further south, the main river continues, making the border with Duskwood. The river then flows into the jungles of [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), tumbles into [Lake Nazferiti](Lake Nazferiti) and finally pours into the [Great Sea](Great Sea) south of [Grom'Gol Base Camp](Grom'Gol Base Camp).
