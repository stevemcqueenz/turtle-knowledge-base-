The **Tkashi Ruins** are ancient [troll](troll) ruins found in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), south-west of [Nesingwary's Expedition](Nesingwary's Expedition). A large group of [Bloodscalp](Bloodscalp tribe) trolls live here.

## Notes
[Page 21](Green Hills of Stranglethorn - Page 21) of [Hemet Nesingwary's](Hemet Nesingwary) autobiography Green Hills of Stranglethorn mentioned that the famed hunter and his followers passed through the ruins without incident.
