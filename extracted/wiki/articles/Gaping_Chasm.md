# Gaping Chasm

-The Gaping Chasm** is one of the minor [silithid](silithid) [hives](Silithid#Hives) found in southeastern [Tanaris](Tanaris). The area is swarming with Hazzali silithid and has several large hive complexes within.

## History
[Senior Surveyor Fizzledowser](Senior Surveyor Fizzledowser) sent [Junior Surveyor Scrimshank](Junior Surveyor Scrimshank) to Gaping Chasm, but he went missing. He died in the area, and his gear was recovered by [adventurer](adventurer)s and returned to Fizzledowser.
