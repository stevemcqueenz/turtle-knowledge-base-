King **Varian Wrynn** is the former and currently missing ruler of the human kingdom of [Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)), son of King [Llane Wrynn](Llane Wrynn). Following his disappearance, his 10-year-old son [Anduin](Anduin Wrynn) has been named ruler of Stormwind.

Born to King Llane, Varian was a young child when his father was assassinated during the [First War](First War). [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar) served as Regent Lord of Stormwind during the flight to Lordaeron and the retaking of Stormwind during the [Second War](Second War). Varian was instated as king when he reached the Age of Ascension, and served as the ruler of [Stormwind](Stormwind) during the rebuilding of the kingdom. He was one of the few rulers to provide continued support to the failing [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron) following the end of the Second War.

Varian Wrynn was abducted while traveling to [Theramore](Theramore), in a plot involving the [Defias](Defias).

## In World of Warcraft
Up until [Patch 1.10.0](Patch 1.10.0), he could be found on [Alcaz Island](Alcaz Island) near [Theramore Isle](Theramore Isle), where he was being held captive in a dungeon.

In Mysteries of Azeroth, a time-displaced version of Varian appears in the [Caverns of Time](Caverns of Time) and is guided by [Neto the Time Weaver](Neto the Time Weaver) to his proper timeline.
