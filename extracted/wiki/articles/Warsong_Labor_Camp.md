# Warsong Labor Camp

The **Warsong Labor Camp** is located in southeastern [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), east of [Fallen Sky Lake](Fallen Sky Lake) and southwest of [Dor'Danil Barrow Den](Dor'Danil Barrow Den). It is a small area containing loggers, harvesters, and [orc](orc)ish guards. It is occasionally attacked by [Alliance](Alliance) adventurers since they can strike the guards without fear of retribution, due to the camp's relative isolation. It is this camp that [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream) established after he was sent to [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), to cut lumber, by Warchief [Thrall](Thrall).
