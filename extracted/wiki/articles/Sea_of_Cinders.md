# Sea of Cinders

The **Sea of Cinders** is a relatively flat stretch of land in the southern area of the [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge), just north of [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain).  The flat lands have been ravaged by fire and wind and are now an uninhabitable wasteland.  Cinders and ashes from the still smoldering ground are constantly flying through the air, making it an extremely unpleasant area to travel through.
