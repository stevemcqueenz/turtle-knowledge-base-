# Mist's Edge

-Mist's Edge** is by far the longest stretch of coast in [Darkshore](Darkshore), reaching all the way from [Auberdine](Auberdine) in the south to the region's northern tip. It is dominated by gloomy bays and gray beaches, often littered with huge carcasses of sea animals — which are part of some quests —  and dotted with the wrecks of sunken ships. [Murloc](Murloc)s are an ever-present threat, but even more common are the [crab](crab)s which scuttle along the entire stretch of shore.
