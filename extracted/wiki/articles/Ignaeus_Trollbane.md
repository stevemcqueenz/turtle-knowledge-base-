In ancient times the [Arathorian Empire](Arathor) fought against the trolls. They were led by [Strom](Strom)'s most capable warlord, **Lord Ignaeus** of [Strom](Strom), who came to be known as **Trollbane**. After the departure of Thoradin's ruling descendants, the Trollbane family ascended to the throne from the barracks itself. The Trollbanes, children of the legendary and feared Ignaeus Trollbane, were wielders of the dread sword [Trol'Kalar](Trol'Kalar), and the greatest champions of the realm.
