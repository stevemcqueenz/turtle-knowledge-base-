# All Things Flora

**All Things Flora** is a [herbalist](herbalist) shop located in [Booty Bay](Booty Bay) in the [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). It's named after and run by [Flora Silverwind](Flora Silverwind).
