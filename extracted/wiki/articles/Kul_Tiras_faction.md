*This article is about the in-game faction. For the island chain, see [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras). For the human kingdom, see [Kul Tiras (kingdom)](Kul Tiras (kingdom)).*

**Kul Tiras** is an island chain located in the [Great Sea](Great Sea), west of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms). The islands are home to the human nation of the same name. Kul Tiras is a naval nation that has been aligned with the [Alliance](Alliance) since its founding, but they have yet to renew these ties after the fall of Lordaeron.
