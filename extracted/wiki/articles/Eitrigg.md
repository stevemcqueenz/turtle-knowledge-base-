# Eitrigg

[Eitrigg](Eitrigg) is a level 20 [quest giver](quest giver) located in [Grommash Hold](Grommash Hold) in the [Valley of Wisdom](Valley of Wisdom), in the [orcish](orcish) city of [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar).

He starts the quest  [1] [The New Horde](Quest:The New Horde).

See [List of Orgrimmar NPCs](List of Orgrimmar NPCs).

## Lore
Eitrigg is an aging [Orc](Orc) warrior who served with valor and distinction during the First and Second War.  After he learned the truth of [Gul'dan](Gul'dan)'s betrayal and his bargain with [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden), Eitrigg fled into the wilderness of Lordaeron.  He was discovered years later living in the ruins of an abandoned watch tower by the [paladin](paladin) [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring).  At first regarding each other as nothing but enemies, Eitrigg ended up saving Tirion's live by pulling him out of the tower as it collapsed.  From that point forward, Tirion felt he owed a debt of honor to the Orc.  This was not enough to prevent Eitrigg from being captured however.  It is certain that the Orc would have been put to death in Stratholme but thanks to the timely intervention of Tirion and the [Horde](Horde), he was saved.

After meeting [Warchief](Warchief) [Thrall](Thrall), Eitrigg discovered that his people had rediscovered their [shamanistic](Shaman) roots, and returned to life among his own kind.  He currently serves with the Horde as an advisor, and can be found in Thrall's throne room in [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar).
