# Splinterspear Junction

-Splinterspear Junction** is where the main road into the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows) splits. The north branch will take you past the [Harborage](Harborage) and to the [Fallow Sanctuary](Fallow Sanctuary), while the southern branch will take you to [Stonard](Stonard) and the path to the [Blasted Lands](Blasted Lands). An old [orc](orc)ish [watch tower](watch tower) sits there and the area is regularly patrolled by [Horde](Horde) guards, so [Alliance](Alliance) adventurers are advised to keep their eyes open when traveling the roads. The guards are PvP tagged.
