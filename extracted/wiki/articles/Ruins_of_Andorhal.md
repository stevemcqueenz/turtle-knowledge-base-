# Ruins of Andorhal

The [Ruins of Andorhal](Ruins of Andorhal) is a large city ruin in the central [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands). The ruins's leader is [Araj the Summoner](Araj the Summoner), a lvl 61 elite, surrounded by scourge agents. The inhabitants is for the most part [ghoul](ghoul)s, [skeletal mage](skeletal mage)s, [death knight](death knight)s and [abomination](abomination)s.

For an article about the city itself, see [Andorhal](Andorhal).
