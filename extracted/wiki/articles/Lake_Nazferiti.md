# Lake Nazferiti

**Lake Nazferiti** is a lake in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). Bordering it is a [large base](Venture Co. Base Camp) of the [goblin](goblin) [Venture Company](Venture Company). It features many [frenzies](Frenzy) and [crocolisk](crocolisk)s in its waters. [Nazferiti river](Nazferiti river), which flows through the lake, may be the longest on the southern [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms) with a confirmed source at a waterfall in [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains) and unconfirmed headwaters within [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass).
