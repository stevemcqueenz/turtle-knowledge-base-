# Waygate

The **Waygate** is an area located at the [Shaper's Terrace](Shaper's Terrace) in the [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater). It is devoid of NPCs.

## Trivia
- The Waygate and the Shaper's Terrace were formerly home to the [Keepers of Outerend](Keepers of Outerend), who were removed in an unknown update.
