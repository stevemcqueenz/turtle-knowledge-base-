# Mosshide Fen

[Mosshide Fen](Mosshide Fen) is a region of the eastern [Wetlands](Wetlands) which has fallen under control of the Mosshide [gnolls](gnolls). Several of their camps dot the landscape, and their vicious leader [Gnawbone](Gnawbone) command his tribe to capture and eat any adventurers they spot stalking through the marshlands looking for tinder.
