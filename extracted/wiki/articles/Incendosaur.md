# Incendosaur

The [Incendosaur](Incendosaur)s are a species of large crimson reptiles that reside deep underground in [the Slag Pit](Slag Pit) in [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge). Their [scales](Incendosaur Scales) are highly sought after by the [Dark Iron Dwarves](Dark Iron dwarf) as a constituent in creating '[Fiery Flux](Fiery Flux)'.
