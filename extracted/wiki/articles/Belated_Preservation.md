## Description
The city above us had been laying silent for millennia, an unspoken oath by all Kaldorei and druids to not enter the forlorn ruins of the Sisterhood. Thus, we had not secured the numerous relics inside for safekeeping, but let them rest in the embrace of time instead. However, now that the deranged defectors have begun trangressing and pillaging the temple, we cannot look idly by any longer. Gather allies and fight your way through their defenses. Retrieve as many artifacts as you can and bring them here to the grove.
