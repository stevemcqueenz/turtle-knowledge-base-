The **Regency Council** is the current governing body of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas) and the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant), the surviving High Elves of Quel'Thalas. The council was established after the expansion of the city, the resettlement of the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands) and after [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner) was removed from her position as Speaker of the Silvermoon Remnant due to the influence of surviving nobles, primarily those of [House Sunsworn](House Sunsworn), whose patriarch [Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn) has ambitions of being crowned king.

It is based out of the Regency Spire in Alah'Thalas, and is comprised of Vereesa Windrunner, Lor'themar Theron, Grand Magister Rommath, Astalor Sunsworn, Farenia Roseheart and Miralin Wavesinger. Following the [Silver Covenant](Silver Covenant)'s campaign to retake [Shalandis Isle](Shalandis Isle), a motion was passed to name Lord Sunsworn as Lord-Regent of Quel'Thalas. (ref: [A GOOD DAY — Everlook Broadcasting Co. Radio Play Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u90BCZfygz0)) Though the Council endures, Lord Sunsworn's position as Lord-Regent has given him the power and the right to veto it.
