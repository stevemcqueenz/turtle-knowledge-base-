# Dun Modr

**Dun Modr** is a fortified middle-sized dwarven town that sits along the road just south of [Thandol Span](Thandol Span).  As such, it has traditionally been of major strategic importance for controlling the region. During the [Second War](Second War), a major battle was fought to retake Dun Modr from the [Horde](Horde). Recently, with the [Alliance](Alliance) armies called away, both the Thandol Span and Dun Modr have fallen to the [Dark Iron Dwarves](Dark Iron Dwarves).

There are some quests that can be taken from the outskirts of Dun Modr that involve killing the Dark Irons. Other quests involve the guardpost at the center of the Span (the entrances on either side of the span), and just over the Span into [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands) to the east of the Span. It is believed the Dark Irons are led by powerful warlock, [Balgaras the Foul](Balgaras the Foul).
