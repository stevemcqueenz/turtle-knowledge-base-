# Southfury River

The **Southfury River** is likely [Kalimdor](Kalimdor)'s most prominent river. Beginning as a small stream in the highlands surrounding [Mount Hyjal](Mount Hyjal), it grows into a wide, swift-flowing river that forms the boundary between [Ashenvale](Ashenvale) Forest and [Azshara](Azshara), and further south, the border between the [Barrens](Barrens) and [Durotar](Durotar) where nasty creatures known as [crocolisks](crocolisk) patrol the waters. The river empties into the [Great Sea](Great Sea) in a cascading waterfall just northeast of the [goblin](goblin) port town of [Ratchet](Ratchet).
