# Gargoyle

Although they are not actually [undead](undead) creatures, the **Gargoyles** are staunch allies of the [Lich King](Lich King)'s forces and can often be seen circling the battlefields like hungry crows. These ice-skinned predators enjoy pain and torment, and their laughter sounds like shattering ice. They thrill to the excitement of the chase and the glory of a kill.

The dreaded gargoyles of [Northrend](Northrend) are voracious flying creatures that revel in slaughter and mayhem. Brought from the frozen north by the armies of the [Lich King](Lich King), these strange, wiry flyers have rough, crystalline hides that protect them from all manners of attacks. In time of great peril, gargoyles can land and condense their hide into a stone-like surface.
Gargoyles were brought to [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) by [Arthas](Arthas) the siege of [Silvermoon](Silvermoon). Now Gargoyles live in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands).
Gargoyles are not particualarly intelligent and are not interested in strategies or tactics. These vicious creatures only enjoy chaos and slaughter and revel in sprays of blood and torn flesh.
