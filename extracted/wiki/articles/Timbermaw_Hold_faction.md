# Timbermaw Hold (faction)

*This article is about the Timbermaw capital. For the in-game capital city of the same name, see [Timbermaw Hold](Timbermaw Hold). For the instance, see [Timbermaw Hold (raid)](Timbermaw Hold (raid)).*
As the last uncorrupted [furbolg](furbolg) tribe (at least in their view), the [Timbermaw tribe](Timbermaw tribe) seeks to preserve their spiritual ways and end the suffering of their brethren.

The Timbermaw Furbolgs inhabit four areas: [Azshara](Azshara), [Winterspring](Winterspring), [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast) and [Felwood](Felwood). They are presumed to be the only furbolg tribe to escape demonic corruption, though this may not be true due to the existence of [Krolg](Krolg), an uncorrupted furbolg of unknown tribe. However, many other races kill furbolg blindly now, without bothering to see if they are friend or foe. For this reason, the Timbermaw furbolg trust very few.

Some Timbermaw Furbolgs reside within [Timbermaw Hold](Timbermaw Hold) and will give any traveler safe passage through it as long as their standing with them is not hostile. Most of the Timbermaw tribe can be found in [Ursolan](Ursolan) in Azshara, where the [Maw of Ursoc](Maw of Ursoc) houses their leader, [Narkogg the Dark](Narkogg the Dark).
