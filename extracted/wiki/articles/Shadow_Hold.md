# Shadow Hold

-Shadow Hold**, located in western [Felwood](Felwood), is the main dwelling of the [Shadow Council](Shadow Council). It is a gigantic subterranean labyrinth roamed by [Jaedenar](Jaedenar) warlocks and their demonic pets.

[Night elf](Night elf) [Arko'narin](Arko'narin) is being held captive inside as well as her close friend, a [human](human) by the name of [Trey Lightforge](Trey Lightforge), who later turns out to have been tortured to death.

The [satyr](satyr) [Prince Xavalis](Prince Xavalis) and [dreadlord](dreadlord) [Lord Banehollow](Lord Banehollow) also reside here.

## Mobs
The area is heavily populated by [humanoid](humanoid)s and [demon](demon)s ranging between levels 52 to 57.

### Quests to get before entering
- [quest: Forces of Jaedenar] from [Greta Mosshoof](Greta Mosshoof) in the [Emerald Sanctuary](Emerald Sanctuary)

## First trip
Start [quest: Rescue From Jaedenar] by picking up a randomly dropped blood key from the Shadow Hold mobs.

Return to the Emerald Sanctuary to turn in quests and start [quest: Retribution of the Light].

## Second trip
After killing the main succubus, Rakaiah, return to the Emerald Sanctuary and claim your fabulous prizes.
