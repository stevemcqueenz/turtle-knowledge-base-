# Crystalvein Mine

-Crystalvein Mine** is located near [Zul'Mamwe](Ruins of Zul'Mamwe), and the native [basilisk](basilisk)s eat the strange crystals which can be found within this mine. This gives the reptiles their hardened skin. According to [Crank Fizzlebub](Crank Fizzlebub), these [Singing Crystals](Singing Blue Crystal) are unique to Stranglethorn and quite valuable. The [Venture Co.](Venture Company) have moved into the area hoping to harvest the mine, but the basilisk infestation has made this impossible thus far. Outside crystallized trolls and humans can be found.
