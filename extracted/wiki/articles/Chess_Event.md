The **Chess Event** is the fifth boss of [Tower of Karazhan](Tower of Karazhan). The pieces are:

- The [King](King) and [Queen](Queen);
- The [Rook](Rook) and [Broken Rook](Broken Rook);
- The [Knight](Knight) and [Malfunctioning Knight](Malfunctioning Knight);
- The [Bishop](Bishop) and [Decaying Bishop](Decaying Bishop);
- The [Withering Pawn](Withering Pawn)s.
