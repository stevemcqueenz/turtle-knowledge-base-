# Gullwing Wreckage

The **Gullwing Wreckage** is a site of a crashed zeppelin from [Booty Bay](Booty Bay) that was on a routine cargo delivery when their helmsman, [Verix](Helmsman Verix), changed course to take a shortcut over [Balor](Balor) ostensibly to save fuel, but in reality to plunder the treasures from its ruins. The zeppelin was struck down by the thunderstorms that plague the island, leaving the crew stranded.
