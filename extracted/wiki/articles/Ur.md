Archmage **Ur** was a mage of [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)) who lived during the events of the [Third War](Third War). He is the eponymous author of the [Book of Ur](Book of Ur) and the [Book of Ur: Volume Two](Book of Ur: Volume Two), as well as [Vorgendor: Myths from the Blood Dimension](Vorgendor: Myths from the Blood Dimension) and ['On the Powers of Blood']('On the Powers of Blood'), which delve into the nature of blood magic and the origin of the [worgen](worgen). Ur perished during the Scourge's assault on Dalaran.<ref name=":0">[quest: The Book of Ur])

The first volume of Ur would end up in the possession of [Archmage Arugal](Archmage Arugal) of [Shadowfang Keep](Shadowfang Keep) until Horde adventurers retrieved the book for [Keeper Bel'dugur](Keeper Bel'dugur). (ref: 

The second volume was taken from the library of [Gilneas City](Gilneas City) on behalf of [Ethan Ravencroft](Ethan Ravencroft), a member of the [Lupine Coven](Lupine Coven).<ref>[quest: Ravencroft's Ambition])

The related book, Vorgendor: Myths from the Blood Dimension, was lost in the depths of [Upper Karazhan](Upper Karazhan) until adventurers retrieved and brought it to [Arch Druid Dreamwind](Arch Druid Dreamwind), who was researching the [Scythe of Elune](Scythe of Elune). (ref: [quest: Scythe of the Goddess])

'On the Powers of Blood' was held by the [Regent-Lords of Gilneas](Regent-Lords of Gilneas), [Celia](Regent-Lady Celia Harlow) and [Mortimer Harlow](Regent-Lord Mortimer Harlow), and was retrieved from [Greymane Tower](Greymane Tower) by a Horde adventurer for [Orvan Darkeye](Orvan Darkeye). (ref: 

Ur also wrote the esoteric tome [Ur's Treatise on Shadow Magic](Ur's Treatise on Shadow Magic), which was safeguarded by the [Wizard's Sanctum](Wizard's Sanctum) in [Stormwind](Stormwind City) until it was absconded with by the warlock [Morganth](Morganth). [High Sorcerer Andromath](High Sorcerer Andromath), fearing Morganth would find a way to open the spell-sealed tome, instructed Alliance mages to return the book.<ref>[quest: class=Mage])
