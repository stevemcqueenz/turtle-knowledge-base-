**Marlos Catos** is a [human](human) located in [Booty Bay](Booty Bay) in the [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). Along with [K'ron Tusk](K'ron Tusk), he is the co-founder of the [Shell Co. Trading Company](Shell Co.) and creator of the [Shellcoin](Shellcoin) alternative currency. Originally located in [Stormwind City](Stormwind City), Catos' offices were forced to relocate from the [Dwarven District](Dwarven District) to a house along the [Canals](Canals (Stormwind City)), before being forced to close by order of [Highlord Bolvar Fordragon](Highlord Bolvar Fordragon) and the [House of Nobles](House of Nobles) and moving out of the city to [Booty Bay](Booty Bay).
