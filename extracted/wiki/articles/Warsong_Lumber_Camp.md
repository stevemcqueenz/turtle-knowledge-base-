# Warsong Lumber Camp

The Warsong Clans lumber-cutting camp was established sometime after the Third War. It is NOT the camp Grom Hellscream built to cut lumber for his warchief, because this camp is too far away from the entrance to the Barrens, and too near the entrance to Azshara. It is likely that the Warsongs built the lumber-cutting camp away from their former lumber-cutting camp for unknown reasons after their leader's death  (see [Warsong Labor Camp](Warsong Labor Camp)).
