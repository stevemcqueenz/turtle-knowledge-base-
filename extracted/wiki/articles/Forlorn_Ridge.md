# Forlorn Ridge

The **Forlorn Ridge** is a huge long-dead volcano located in southwestern [Azshara](Azshara). It completely dominates the nearby landscape, and both the [Alliance](Alliance) and the [Horde](Horde) have built fortifications on the side of the mountain. Many believe it to be the site of the "lost" battleground, [Azshara Crater](Azshara Crater).

You can discover this at 25.7 76.5 Azshara (g/s/c).
