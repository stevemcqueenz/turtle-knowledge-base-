# Gnome

*This article is about the gnomes' lore. For the playable race, see [Gnome (playable)](Gnome (playable)).*

The **gnomes** (collectively known as **gnomekind**) (ref: [Patch 1.17.0#New zones and quests](Patch 1.17.0#New zones and quests)) are a race of diminutive and highly intelligent [humanoids](Humanoid), known for their mastery of [technology](technology).  For years the gnomes were based in [Gnomeregan](Gnomeregan), providing technological support to the [Alliance](Alliance) during the [Second War](Second War), until a [trogg](trogg) invasion and [treachery](Sicco Thermaplugg) from within irradiated Gnomeregan and killed as much as eighty percent of the gnomish population. The gnomes were forced into exile in [Tinker Town](Tinker Town) in the dwarven capital of [Ironforge](Ironforge).

## History
### Early history
Little is known concerning gnomish history previous to the [Second War](Second War) — even among gnomes themselves. Much of gnomish philosophy is focused on forward-thinking, inventive concepts, and little thought has ever been given to history or non-scientific records.

The gnomes have not had a proper king or queen for over four hundred years. Instead, they prefer to elect their highest officials for set terms of service; although [Gelbin Mekkatorque](Gelbin Mekkatorque) has the title "King of Gnomes", he was elected and his position is officially called High Tinker. (ref: *World of Warcraft: Game Manual*)

Thriving within the wondrous techno-city of [Gnomeregan](Gnomeregan), the gnomes shared the resources of the forested [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh) peaks with their dwarven cousins for generations. Though dwarves also have a propensity for [technology](technology) and engineering, it is gnomes who provide the critical, visionary designs for most of dwarven weapons and steam vehicles. (ref: [The World of Warcraft Townhall/Races of Azeroth#Gnomes](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_World_of_Warcraft_Townhall/Races_of_Azeroth#Gnomes))

### Second and Third Wars
During the Second War, the gnomes of Khaz Modan displayed their talents by inventing and piloting the unbelievable Flying Machine. Although having no armaments, these awkward contraptions could be used to survey vast areas of terrain, and detecting the otherwise hidden movements of underwater units, making them invaluable for discovering the movements of the Horde. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* game manual, *[Alliance Air Units](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Alliance_Air_Units)*, Gnomish Flying Machine) Gnomish inventors were responsible for creating outlandishly clever contraptions for military use, as well as being responsible perfecting the technique of extracting various chemical compounds out of oil to make gunpowder and explosives. Although the Gnomes are a bit odd and eccentric, none could deny their value to the Alliance. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* game manual, *[Alliance Buildings](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Alliance_Buildings)*, Gnomish Inventor)

Strangely, the gnomes refused to send any personnel to aid their allies during the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion)'s invasion during the [Third War](Third War). Though their designs also helped turning the tide against the Legion, both the [humans](Human) and the Ironforge - and Wildhammer - dwarves were shocked by Gnomeregan's decision to withhold their courageous troops and pilots.

When the Third War ended, the Alliance discovered the reason for the gnomes' sudden withdrawal. Apparently, a barbaric menace — [troggs](Trogg) — had risen from the bowels of the earth and invaded Gnomeregan. Knowing that their allies' priority was defeating the Burning Legion, the gnomes decided to make their stand alone against the troggs.

Though they fought valiantly to save their beloved city, Gnomeregan was lost. At the command of the High Tinker Mekkatorque, the gnomes opened the pressure valves of their giant, grinding machines and released toxic radiation throughout the city. Though the radiation killed the troggs, the gnomes soon discovered that it killed their own people just as quickly. Nearly eighty percent of the gnomish race died within days. Those that survived evacuated the great city and fled to the protection of their dwarven cousins in Ironforge. (ref:  There, they made a new home in [Tinker Town](Tinker Town).

### Mysteries of Azeroth
Following the fall of Gnomeregan at the hands of the troggs and leper gnomes, the gnomes reclaimed an old factory and refurbished it into new quarters for the gnomish people to use as a staging ground to cleanse and reclaim their city.<ref>[Gelbin Mekkatorque#Quotes](Gelbin Mekkatorque#Quotes))
