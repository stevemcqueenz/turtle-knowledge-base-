# Menethil Keep

[Menethil Keep](Menethil Keep) is the fort that dominates the port town of [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor). It is currently led by [Captain Stoutfist](Captain Stoutfist), the commander of the Menethil militia. Besides several guards, it also holds a few additional [quest giver](quest giver)s and a [vendor](vendor).
