# Sand troll

### General
Sometimes known as Desert Trolls, the **Sand Trolls** inhabit the [Tanaris](Tanaris) desert of southern [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), near the ancient Qiraji Empire. Their capital city is located at [Zul'Farrak](Zul'Farrak), and they do not seem to have any further settlements outside this stronghold. Their proximity to [Gadgetzan](Gadgetzan) brings them into freqent conflict with the Goblin [Trade Princes](Trade Princes), the [Horde](Horde) and the [Alliance](Alliance). Sand Trolls seem to follow a shamanistic path of [Voodoo](Voodoo) similar to their [Jungle Troll](Jungle Troll) brothers, and hold reverance for a mysterious summoned creature called [Gahz'rilla](Gahz'rilla).

### History
Not a lot is known about the history of the Sand Trolls, but they seem to be just as ancient as the other Troll races. In recent times they have been in conflict with the [Goblin](Goblin)s and others who intrude on their territory. Unlike the other Troll races who have been known since at least the last great war, the Sand Trolls have remained largely unknown to all non-Trolls until the settlement and exploration of Kalimdor.

It is possible that the Sand Trolls had a small nation during the time of the [Gurubashi](Gurubashi) and [Amani](Amani) Empires.  This is supported by the vast number of troll ruins throughout [Tanaris](Tanaris).  It is likely that the reason for that nation's demise is that they were the first victims of the [Aqir](Aqir) when they began their campaign, due to their proximity to [Silithus](Silithus).

### Society
Sand Trolls seem to have one of the most diverse number of social units in their society, ranging from Axe Throwers, Hideskinners, Blood Drinkers, Guardians and Executioners who act as the warriors and hunters of Zul'Farrak, to the Shadow Hunters, Soul Eaters, Firecallers and Witch Doctors who are potent Voodoo casters. It is rumorered that slaves are also employed by the Sandfury Tribe as warriors, and extra labour. A number of priest-type Trolls such as Zealots and Acolytes seem to be at the core of the Sand Troll civilisation, acting as religious leaders. Drudges and Cretins seem to perform the menial labour, similar to the role of peons.
