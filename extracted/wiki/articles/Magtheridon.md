## Background
A brutal [Pit Lord](Pit Lord) and servant of [Mannoroth](Mannoroth) the Demon King, Magtheridon found his way to [Draenor](Draenor) after its cataclysm. With the clans in disarray, and most killed in the disaster, Magtheridon was quick to show his power, and rallied the surviving orcs under his pennant. He declared the ruined world [Outland](Outland).

Over the years, Magtheridon brought legions of demons to Outland through the portals which [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul) shattered the world with, and his forces became very expansive.

It was almost twenty years after the Cataclysm that [Illidan](Illidan) and his servants came to Outland with a plan to rid the land of all demonic entities there, so that [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden), Illidan's vengeful master, could not follow him.

Magtheridon found himself under attack by Illidan, [Kael'thas](Kael'thas) and his [Blood Elves](Blood Elves), [Lady Vashj](Lady Vashj)'s army of [naga](naga), and even the elusive [draenei](draenei), led by the Elder Sage, [Akama](Akama).

The combined disrupted the dimensional gateways which supplied Magtheridon with reinforcements, and then laid siege to Magtheridon's Black Citadel itself.

The Pit Lord rallied his forces to defend him, but the combined skills of the heroes crushed his resistances. Eventually, Magtheridon was defeated by the four generals, and asked if Illidan had been sent to test him. Illidan cackled in reply, saying that he had come to replace him.
