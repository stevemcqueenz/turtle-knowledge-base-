The **draenei** are a race native to the world of [Draenor](Draenor). For nearly five thousand years, the draenei toiled their fields on their red world until they came into conflict with the [Orcish Horde](Orcish Horde). The resulting conflict left their people destroyed as the orcs moved on to invade the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth).

## History
### Rise of the Horde
The draenei once co-existed peacefully with the orcs on the lush world of Draenor for thousands of years. While the noble orc clans roamed the open prairies and hunted for sport, the inquisitive draenei built crude cities within the world's towering cliffs and peaks. The peace was shattered when the world was discovered by the demon [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden), who saw that the warrior orcs were more susceptible to the Legion's corruption and enthralled the elder orc shaman, [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul). Using the cunning shaman as his conduit, the demon spread battle lust and savagery throughout the orc clans. Before long, the spiritual race was transformed into a bloodthirsty people. (ref: [Kil'jaeden and the Shadow Pact](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Kil%27jaeden_and_the_Shadow_Pact))

While Ner'zhul was unwilling to take the last step and give themselves over entirely to the pursuit of death and war, Kil'jaeden found the willing disciple he sought in Ner'zhul's ambitious apprentice, [Gul'dan](Gul'dan). Promising Gul'dan untold power in exchange for his utter obedience, he taught him demonic magics and helped him found the [Shadow Council](Shadow Council), a secretive sect that manipulated the clans and spread the use of warlock magics throughout Draenor. As more and more orcs began to wield warlock magics, the gentle fields and streams of Draenor began to blacken and fade. Over time, the vast prairies the orcs and draenei had called home for generations withered away, leaving only red barren soil as the demon energies were slowly killing the world. (ref: 

The orcs became increasingly aggressive under the secret control of Gul'dan and his Shadow Council, and over the course of a few months, the Horde eradicated nearly every draenei living on Draenor. Only a scattered handful of survivors managed to evade the orcs' awesome wrath.<ref>[Rise of the Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Rise_of_the_Horde_(History_of_Warcraft)))

### Third War
  -This section concerns content related to *[Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos](Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos)* or its expansion *[The Frozen Throne](Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne)*.**

Several groups of draenei managed to survive the destruction of Draenor. One of these tribes, led by Elder Sage [Akama](Akama), were hunted down to the brink of extinction by [the armies of Magtheridon](Magtheridon's Forces), the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) overlord in Outland. [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage) promised to rid the draenei of their ancient enemies in exchange for their loyalty.

The [Tribe of Sanv](Tribe of Sanv), at the time led by [Sanv Tas'dal](Sanv Tas'dal), fled from Outland to Azeroth after their world was ravaged by the orcs. However, Sanv Tas'dal had remained behind on Draenor, ordering [Sanv K'la](Sanv K'la) and [Akh Z'ador](Akh Z'ador) to escape the dying world with their brethren. Along with [another tribe](Lost Ones), they fled to Azeroth and built settlements in the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows). (ref:  [52] [Learn Of My Past](Learn Of My Past))

### World of Warcraft
  -This section concerns content related to the original *[World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft)*.**

The draenei exiles of the Tribe of Sanv who had founded the [Harborage](Harborage) within the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows) were now in open conflict with the other tribe of the [Fallow Sanctuary](Fallow Sanctuary), which had now become known as the [Lost Ones](Lost Ones). Driven mad by the rigors of their journey to Azeroth, [Noboru](Noboru) and his gang had become a terror to the draenei of the Harborage, wandering the swamps and killing any who crossed their path.

Other draenei like [Kum'isha the Collector](Kum'isha the Collector) and his [refugees](Draenei Refugee) had decided to stay in the [Blasted Lands](Blasted Lands) instead. Many of the draenei were homesick and were [in search](Portal Seeker) of [a rift](Lost One Riftseeker) that could [bring them](Lost One Rift Traveler) back on what remained of Draenor. The precious [draenethyst](draenethyst) shards were rumored to be able to open a rift, a feat that Kum'isha eventually [managed](To Serve Kum'isha) with the help of [adventurers](Adventurer) when brought a [Flawless Draenethyst Sphere](Flawless Draenethyst Sphere).

Sanv Tas'dal was later discovered to have become a servant of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) along with the remainder of his tribe when Akh Z'ador attempted to riftwalk back to Draenor in search of his tribe. Upon the [Rock of Desolation](Rock of Desolation), Sanv Tas'dal established the [Sanv Tribe Village](Sanv Tribe Village) where he attempted to bring his tribesmen of the Harborage when he discovered that they had fled to Azeroth. (ref:  [60R] Fate Of The Harborage)

## Culture
The draenei are practitioners of an ancient magical rite known as [riftwalking](riftwalking), which allows its adherents to tear open gateways in the [Twisting Nether](Twisting Nether). (ref: 

The tribe of Lost Ones in the Swamp of Sorrows are known to use [shamanism](Shaman) and dark magic.

[Draenethyst](Draenethyst), a gem native to Draenor, is seen as sacred by the draenei and holds great value to them for sentimental and magical properties. Some draenei are known to take their reverence for the draenethyst too far and are driven to steal it from their brethren, such as the Lost Ones of the Swamp of Sorrows did to their brethren of the Harborage.<ref>[THE MIREBEAST — Everlook Broadcasting Co. Radio Play Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2fhEAUpBBLU)) In addition to being a key reagent for less experienced riftwalkers, it is used as decorations on some of their larger leather huts.

Draenei typically combine zesty and spiciness in their cuisine. (ref:  [52] [Rift Fatigue: Body](Rift Fatigue: Body))
