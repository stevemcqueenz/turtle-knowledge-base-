*The subject of this article has been removed from Turtle WoW and is considered non-canon.*

The **Keepers of Outerend** are stalwart servants that have kept the relic Outerend safe for a dozen millenia. With the coming darkness, they now seek to guide mortals to fight for Azeroth.

## Trivia
- The faction is deprecated content and was removed from the game at an unknown point.
- It is possible to jump up to the Shaper's Terrace using wallclimbing and seeing the NPCs that belonged to this faction.
- Although the faction was added with [Patch 1.15.0](Patch 1.15.0), no content was ever implemented with them prior to their removal.
- There was a quartermaster for the faction, [Earthshaper Thoron](Earthshaper Thoron), but he did not sell anything.
