# Moonwell

Moonwell locations for crafting [Mooncloth](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=14342).

## **Kalimdor**
- **Teldrassil:** Various settlements, including Dolanaar
- **Darnassus:** Temple of the Moon
- **Darkshore:** Various locations, including Auberdine
- **Ashenvale:**
  - Astranaar
  - Raynewood Retreat (59, 59)
  - Shaeldryn's Moonwell, east of Iris Lake (53, 46)
  - Southern Moonwell, east of Mystral Lake (60, 72)
- **Stonetalon Mountains:** Stonetalon Peak
- **Moonglade:** Nighthaven
- **Thousand Needles:** Thalanaar
- **Desolace:** Nijel's Point
- **Feralas:**
  - Feathermoon Stronghold
  - Thalanaar
  - Dire Maul
- **Felwood:** Various locations (both corrupted, but still usable to purify Felcloth into Mooncloth):
  - Jaedenar
  - Ruins of Constellas
- **Silithus:** Cenarion Hold (48, 37)

## **Eastern Kingdoms**
- **Stormwind:** In The Park district
- **Duskwood:** In the Twilight Grove
