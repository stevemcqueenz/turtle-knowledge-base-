# Darkened Bank

**The Darkened Bank** stretches along the northern shore of [Duskwood](Duskwood). It runs from the far western edge of Duskwood east to the bridge that connects Duskwood to the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains). At the western edge of the Darkened Bank is a small camp with two refugees, [Sven Yorgen](Sven Yorgen) and [Lars](Lars), where you can receive a handful of quests that involve killing the weaker creatures in Duskwood. This is a good place to begin questing in Duskwood and is easily reached by swimming straight south from the far southwest corner of [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest).
