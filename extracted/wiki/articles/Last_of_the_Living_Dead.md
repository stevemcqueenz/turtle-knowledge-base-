## Objectives
Slay 8 Shambling Dead and 8 Lingering Skeletons for Orvan Darkeye at the Ruins of Greyshire in Gilneas.
- [Lingering Skeleton](Lingering Skeleton) slain (8)
- [Shambling Dead](Shambling Dead) slain (8)

## Description
Greymane may have thought himself safe behind these walls, but he was foolish to believe he could hide from Death. Now, his incompetence comes back to haunt him, and by extension, me. The Scourge still lurk in these lands, constantly interrupting my research and slowing it down considerably. It is time to take matters into our own hands.

I command you to eliminate the remaining Scourge forces in Greyshire.

Do not fail me, for failure is not an option.

<The Forsaken's eyes narrow with a cold, calculating intensity.>
