# Grell

A small type of [demon](demon), grells are similar in appearance to [imps](Imp), but are generally larger in size and lack the characteristic horns.  Grells come in both melee and spellcaster variants. They are common to [Shadowglen](Shadowglen) and [Fel Rock](Fel Rock) in [Teldrassil](Teldrassil), and [Bashal'Aran](Bashal'Aran) in [Darkshore](Darkshore).

Grells are also known as sprites, although this term is shared with the more benign [faerie dragons](Faerie_Dragon).
