-Auberdine** is the main center of civilization in [Darkshore](Darkshore). Most of Auberdine is populated by [night Elves](night Elves), but the occasional [dwarf](dwarf) or [gnome](gnome) can also be found there. While quite appealing, Auberdine offers a brutal contrast from the serenity that can be felt in [Darnassus](Darnassus). Even the sun seems to be too shy to cast its rays upon the land, which seems to be kept cloaked in a dark and mysterious mist.

The buildings in Auberdine are quite different from those found in [Darnassus](Darnassus). Here, the stone is mostly replaced by wood, and every house has a gloomy look, as if a spell was cast upon the outpost. 
Auberdine is a small town with little to defend except a safe way of life.

# Travel
You can get to the following places from Darkshore:

-[Teldrassil](Teldrassil) is the [night elven](night elven) world tree and the starting location for young night elves. It can be reached by [hippogryph](hippogryph) or by [boat](boat), both of which will land in [Rut'theran Village](Rut'theran Village). From there, it is only a short walk up the hill and through a portal to get to the capital city of [Darnassus](Darnassus), which offers banking, training, and auctioning opportunities not found in Auberdine. 

-[Ashenvale](Ashenvale) is south of Darkshore and can be reached either by walking or by taking a hippogryph to [Astranaar](Astranaar).

-[Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains) are south of Ashenvale, but the hippogryph will take you there if you have the flight path to [Stonetalon Peak](Stonetalon Peak).

-[Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh) is on the opposite side of Kalimdor, but even so, the hippogryph will take you to [Theramore Isle](Theramore Isle) if you have the flight path. Alternatively, you can take the boat to [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor), where you need to switch boats and then go on to Theramore Isle. Remember to get the [gryphon](gryphon) route once you are there, and you'll be able to fly the next time.

-[Wetlands](Wetlands) is located in the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms). To get there, take the boat from the southern pier in Auberdine. You can then journey on into the rest of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), or take a different boat back to Kalimdor stopping at Theramore Isle.
