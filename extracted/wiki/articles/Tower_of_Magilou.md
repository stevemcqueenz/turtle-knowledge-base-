# Tower of Magilou

The **Tower of Magilou** is a mage tower in [Northwind](Northwind). It was formerly inhabited by [Enchantress Magilou](Enchantress Magilou), but after an incident with her apprentice [Thronda](Thronda) her own assembly of arcane elementals to help with her chores, they've grown out of control and now terrorize the tower, forcing Magilou and Thronda to find refuge in a nearby home. (ref: [quest: Magilou's Magical Mishap])

[A monument to Stormwind's Court Magicians](Legacy of Stormwind's Court Magicians) is erected across from the tower.
