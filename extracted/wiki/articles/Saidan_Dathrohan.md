Grand Crusader **Saidan Dathrohan** was a paladin of the [Knights of the Silver Hand](Knights of the Silver Hand) and the leader of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade). Though once a noble knight in service to the [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron) and Lord Commander of the Silver Hand, his identity was used by the demon [Balnazzar](Balnazzar) to corrupt the Scarlet Crusade into a fanatical, corrupt and extremist sect dedicated to the destruction of the undead and anyone who opposes their doctrines.

## Background
During [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring)'s ceremony of induction into the Order of the Silver Hand, Saidan, who was one of his closest friends, was in charge of placing the silver plates upon Tirion's shoulders.

### Of Blood and Honor
A few years before the [Third War](Third War), Saidan, then Lord Commander of the Silver Hand, established himself in [Stratholme](Stratholme). He was the direct superior of his friend Tirion Fordring, who lived in [Mardenholde Keep](Mardenholde Keep) and ruled over the province of [Hearthglen](Hearthglen). Contacted by the paladin [Barthilas](Barthilas), who was suspicious of Tirion's intentions towards an [orc](Eitrigg) that had been found in the area, Saidan traveled with an escort to Hearthglen to meet his old friend. After a warm reunion, a meeting took place in Mardenholde Keep where Saidan questioned Tirion about the presence of orcs on his lands. Despite Tirion's attempts to assure him that the situation was under control, Saidan explained that a [new Warchief](Thrall) had just manifested, and that he had started the liberation of the internment camps in order to create a [new Horde](Horde).

Facing this terrible threat, he ordered Tirion Fordring to escort them to the ruined tower where he fought the old orc, in order to ensure the death of the latter or to hunt him down if not. Hours later, they reached the ruined tower in Hearthglen Woods, and after discovering Eitrigg, Saidan ordered the orc's capture. However, Tirion tried to stop his men, and Saidan was forced to capture Tirion. He tried to bring Tirion back to his senses and asked him to stop opposing them, but faced with the latter's refusal, he had no other choice than to accuse him of treason and to send him to Stratholme to stand trial. (ref: *Of Blood and Honor*, chapter 4)

During the trial, Saidan gave his testimony to the jury, consisting of Lord Admiral [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore), Arch-Mage [Antonidas](Antonidas), Archbishop [Alonsus Faol](Alonsus Faol), and Prince [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil). After being dismissed by [Uther the Lightbringer](Uther the Lightbringer), he was disgruntled by Barthilas’ lack of respect for his former master, as he tried to impress him by treating Tirion with cruelty as a traitor. When Tirion was asked to reject his vow of honor for Eitrigg, in order to put it all behind him, Saidan implored his friend to accept this pardon from Uther and the jury. But when he refused, Dathrohan sat down heavily on his chair, shaking his head in dismay. (ref: *Of Blood and Honor*, chapter 5)

Following Tirion's trial and banishment, Saidan was present at Stratholme when the execution of the orc Eitrigg was scheduled. He talked for a few moments with the crowd and Barthilas, before withdrawing, as he had no interest in watching the vulgar spectacle so soon after his friend's trial. When Tirion intervened to save the orc, Saidan reappeared and stared at his former friend with grief and revulsion, but could not intervene due to the Horde's attack on the city. (ref: *Of Blood and Honor*, chapter 7)

### World of Warcraft
At some point, Saidan's identity was adopted by the dreadlord [Balnazzar](Balnazzar), who had escaped death at the hands of [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner) and [Varimathras](Varimathras) during the civil war in the Plaguelands. The real Saidan had presumably been either possessed or murdered and replaced by the dreadlord. Under the guise of Saidan, he joined the nascent [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) founded by [Highlord Mograine](Alexandros Mograine), [High General Abbendis](High General Abbendis) and [Grand Inquisitor Isillien](Grand Inquisitor Isillien), and began planting the seeds of corruption within the organization. Becoming the Grand Crusader of the Scarlet Crusade, he made a bargain with [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad), manipulating [Renault Mograine](Renault Mograine) into killing his own father when the Highlord had been exhausted from an ambush in Stratholme. (ref: [Corrupted Ashbringer/Scarlet Monastery Event](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Corrupted_Ashbringer/Scarlet_Monastery_Event))

Five years later, from the [Scarlet Bastion](Scarlet Bastion) in the ruins of Stratholme, Balnazzar-as-Saidan led a group of elite Crusade warriors known as the [Crimson Legion](Crimson Legion), and continued to manipulate the leaders of the Scarlet Crusade, Grand Inquisitor Isillien, [Taelan Fordring](Taelan Fordring), and High General Abbendis to accomplish his goals.

When Taelan was convinced by his father Tirion to leave the Scarlet Crusade, the Grand Crusader heard the news of his betrayal and sent Isillien with assassins to kill the Fordrings. (ref:  [60+] [In Dreams](In Dreams))

Upon the disbanding of the [Veiled Blade](Veiled Blade), [Jarien](Jarien) and [Sothos](Sothos) decided to join the Scarlet Crusade, but they were slain by Dathrohan for failing the knighthood initiation rites inside his chamber. (ref: [Brazier of Invocation: User's Manual](Brazier of Invocation: User's Manual))

At some point, adventurers attacked the Scarlet Bastion and faced the Grand Crusader. During the fight, the dreadlord Balnazzar revealed his true form, shedding Saidan's form. They then brought the head of the demon to [Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff](Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff) at [Light's Hope Chapel](Light's Hope Chapel), revealing that the Scarlet Crusade was used by the demon as the pawns to a greater evil. (ref:  [60D] [The Truth Comes Crashing Down](The Truth Comes Crashing Down))
