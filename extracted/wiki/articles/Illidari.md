The **Illidari**, (ref: [Shattered Illidari Crest](https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=40079)) (ref: [quest: They Call Themselves Illidari]) also known as **Illidan's Forces**, (ref: [A Symphony of Frost and Flame (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_Symphony_of_Frost_and_Flame_(WC3_Undead))) (ref: [The Return to Northrend (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Return_to_Northrend_(WC3_Undead))) are the servitors of [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage), lord of [Outland](Outland). It is composed mainly by [blood elves](Blood elf) and [naga](naga).

## History
Following the [Third War](Third War), [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden) confronted Illidan and made him an offer he could not refuse. Kil'jaeden was angered by [Archimonde](Archimonde)'s defeat at [Mount Hyjal](Mount Hyjal), but he had bigger concerns than vengeance. Sensing that his creation, the [Lich King](Lich King), was growing too powerful to control, Kil'jaeden ordered Illidan to destroy [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul) and put an end to the [undead](undead) [Scourge](Scourge) once and for all. In exchange, Illidan would receive untold power and a true place amongst the remaining lords of the Burning Legion. Illidan agreed and immediately set out to destroy the [Frozen Throne](Frozen Throne), the icy crystal cask in which the Lich King's [spirit](Ghost) resided. Illidan knew he needed allies to accomplish his tremendous task so he cast a powerful spell and called the [naga](naga) to the surface, calling in some old [Highborne](Highborne) debts. A group of naga led by [Lady Vashj](Lady Vashj) emerged from the depths and pledged themselves to him. (ref: *[World of Warcraft: Game Manual](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Game_Manual#The_Betrayer_Ascendant)*)

Illidan's attempts to destroy the Scourge ended in failure however, and Illidan retreated to Outland in order to escape Kil'jaeden's wrath. He then began a campaign to oust [Magtheridon](Magtheridon) from power in Outland, in order to become the shattered planet's new lord. He bolstered his forces by recruiting Prince [Kael'thas](Kael'thas Sunstrider)'s blood elves. His forces later discovered a surviving [Draenei](Draenei) village who were also fighting against the Burning Legion. The village's inhabitants, ruled by [Akama](Akama), swore allegiance to Illidan in return for him liberating them from orcish and demonic oppression. Ever since then, the draenei tribe were officially aligned with Illidan. (ref: [Gates of the Abyss (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gates_of_the_Abyss_(WC3_BloodElf)))

A portion of Kael'thas' forces remained on Azeroth, occupying the mountain stronghold of [Felstrider Retreat](Felstrider Retreat) in the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands). Led by Viceroy [Eldin Sunstrider](Eldin Sunstrider), a distant cousin of Kael'thas, the Illidari of Felstrider Retreat remained in the Highlands until the arrival of the refugees of the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant). On the Viceroy's orders, [Veloren Brightstar](Veloren Brightstar) was tasked to spy on the refugees of [Brinthilien](Brinthilien) resettling the Highlands. (ref: [quest: A Letter from the Royalty]) [Rommath](Grand Magister Rommath), a loyalist to Kael'thas, has imbedded himself in the nascent [Regency Council](Regency Council) of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas) and has maintained a line of communication between the Prince in Outland and the remaining blood elves in Felstrider Retreat (ref: [Rommath's Orders](Rommath's Orders)) (ref: [quest: An Audience With the Prince]) as Kael'thas continued to seek the path to salvation for his people. Meanwhile, more Illidari gathered in [Felwood](Felwood) at the [Shrine of the Betrayer](Shrine of the Betrayer), led by the demon hunter [Faladar Starsplinter](Faladar Starsplinter), and naga veterans of Illidan's army, the [Tideblade](Tideblade), continue to occupy the [ruins of Nendis](ruins of Nendis) in [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast).
