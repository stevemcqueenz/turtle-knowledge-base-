## Objectives
Travel to Moonwhisper Coast north of Azshara and locate the shimmering lake.

## Description
Now that I regained my strength, my senses are sharp once more, and I can feel the magic of your world far better than before. What I felt, however, was surprising, a sensation that had an aura of familiarity to it. It came from the north, a whirlwind of conflicting energy. Ideals clashing, emotions uncertain and a great change abound. You may have seen it already, beyond the cliffs to the north is a land clad in blue, its cerulean hue draping the coast in serene midnight. In my mind I have the image of a great shimmering lake, there I had felt the sensation the strongest.

<name>, once we have secured the safety of my people, would you travel to the lands north of here and search for this peculiarity I sensed? My heart tells me it is of great importance.
