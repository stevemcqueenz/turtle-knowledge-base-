## Description
You've met many foes on your several adventures through Azeroth, for the first time something in you clicked.

How many more scum do you have to beat, maim and kill so that this twisted world gets its damnable peace?

The Scarlet Crusade has been a personal thorn in your sides as well, maybe it's time to deliver some sort of justice or is it vengeance?

With a table full of tools that will aid you to obtain what you need, you are ready to embrace your demons.
