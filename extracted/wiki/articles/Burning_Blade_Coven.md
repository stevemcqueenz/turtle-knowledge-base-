# Burning Blade Coven

The **Burning Blade Coven** lies within the dusty red mountains that form the northern border of the [Valley of Trials](Valley of Trials). The valley, which serves as a proving ground for aspiring [orcs](orcs) and [trolls](trolls), is no longer as safe as it used to be. [Imp](Imp)s and other weak demons have spilled out from the mouth of the cave and are causing mischief. Some say that there is more than mischief on the minds of stronger demons that inhabit the cave, and that there may be members of the [Burning Blade](Burning Blade) within the abandoned caverns. New adventurers often tackle the dangers of the Coven as their final test before leaving the Valley of Trials. The Burning Blade Coven is led by a warlock called [Yarrog Baneshadow](Yarrog Baneshadow); he has become dangerous because he possesses a powerful artifact known as the [Burning Blade Medallion](Burning Blade Medallion).
