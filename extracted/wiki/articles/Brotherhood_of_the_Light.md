# Brotherhood of the Light

The **Brotherhood of the Light** is the military brawn of the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn), focusing on opposing the forces of the [Lich King](Lich King). Brotherhood of the Light representatives can be seen in [Light's Hope Chapel](Light's Hope Chapel).
