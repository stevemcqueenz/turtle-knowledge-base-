Male [Aesir](Aesir) [Titan](Titan). He is the leader of the [Pantheon](Pantheon) and brother to [Sargeras](Sargeras). Consort to [Eonar](Eonar) the Lifebinder. His metallic skin is platinum and eyes glow yellow. His hand holds a large staff with a Lightning symbol atop and electricity flows around the staff. He has long Hair and beard reaching his waist. Aman'Thul rules the Pantheon -- the Titan High council. He is the oldest and wisest of the Titan race. He and his brother Sargeras set to create countless worlds across the [Twisting Nether](Twisting Nether) and created vast races. Aman'Thul blessed [Nozdormu](Nozdormu) the [Dragon Aspect](Dragon Aspect) with power over Time to be watchful of [Azeroth](Azeroth)'s myriad of time paths and destiny -- protecting the integrity of its timelines.
