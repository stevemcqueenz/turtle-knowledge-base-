# Survival

-Survival** is a secondary professions in Turtle WoW which enables players to create a number of tools to aid them in overcoming obstacles and taming the wilderness.

## Overview
  *Survivalists practice outdoor skills that aid them in surviving the dangerous wilds of Azeroth. With the proper materials, they can create shelter and heat sources to survive the untamed wilderness.*

  *Survival is a secondary profession - anyone can train as a survivalist, no matter how many other professions they've devoted themselves to.*

  *Torches and tents can be created almost anywhere, as long as you have the appropriate ingredients - unlit torches (for torches) and linen cloth, simple wood and some sturdy rope (for tents).*

## Uses
Survival acts as a secondary profession that draws from other primary professions and supplements them with advantages for players who raise their Survival skill further. For example, skilled Survivalists can create powerful tools that can slow, root, or even trap opponents. They can also craft bags suitable for the three gathering professions, as well as containers for fish and meat.

Survivalists also benefit from the gardening feature, allowing them to grow their own food that can provide them with various stat increases that can be paired with bonuses provided from food created with [Cooking](Cooking). Gardening is unlocked by completing the quest [quest: profession=survival], which is available from [Mortimer Stansfield](Mortimer Stansfield) outside [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest) and [Kern Mosshoof](Kern Mosshoof) in [Bloodhoof Village](Bloodhoof Village) in [Mulgore](Mulgore).

## Resource gathering: Woodcutting
Survival is paired with a gathering activity: Woodcutting. Directly tied to your Survival skill, Woodcutting lets you activate [Find Trees](Find Trees), a tracker on your minimap (mutually exclusive with other trackers like [Find Minerals](Find Minerals)). Find Trees is automatically learned alongside Apprentice Survival and the Woodcutting action requires a [Woodcutting Axe](Woodcutting Axe) to be performed, which can be purchased from Survivalist Supplies vendors. 

In order to be harvested, trees require a minimum Survival skill according to the zone they are located in. Starter zone trees begin at skill 15, which can, in turn, be attained by crafting [Dim Torch](Dim Torch)es. Unlike other gathering professions, Woodcutting does not grant any skill points in Survival. Performing Woodcutting on a tree yields one or multiple [Simple Wood](Simple Wood) (which cannot be purchased from General Goods vendors anymore), [Simple Leaf](Simple Leaf)s, and occasionally [herbs](Herbalism), edible mushrooms/berries, and vendor trash.

## Skill level guide
To begin your survivalist career, seek out the Survival trainer for your faction. There are no prerequisites for learning Survival other than the 10 copper you'll be charged. Once you've acquired the Survival skill, you can begin making Dim Torches immediately and your potential skill level will be 75. ### Training locations
Trainers are located within or just outside of capital cities and in several other population centers. Consult the [list of trainers](Survival trainers) or ask a guard for directions.

  -Expert**
Horde players can purchase Expert training from [Swampwalker Krug](Swampwalker Krug) in [Stonard](Stonard) in [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows), while Alliance players can purchase it from [Nerean Stagtree](Nerean Stagtree) in [Nijel's Point](Nijel's Point) in [Desolace](Desolace).

  -Artisan**
Reaching Artisan level requires the completion of the quest [quest: profession=survival] from [Rufus Hardwick](Rufus Hardwick) at [Nesingwary's Expedition](Nesingwary's Expedition) in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale).
