# Ventya "the Dark Rider"

*"**Hell if I know. I’m too drunk to remember if I’ve died before.**"*  
— **Ventya** to a Scarlet Inquisitor. 

## Description
Ventya is an unshackled Dark Rider and an enigma that defies definition. Her public persona is that of a neutral treasure hunter, but she is simultaneously a dancer, epicure, thief, spy, ghost, assassin, and an informant for SI:7. Evidence suggests she still acquires various magical artifacts and brings them to the Tower of Karazhan, but as long as one does not stand in her way, she is generally harmless to most.

### Appearance
In her natural state, Ventya appears as a young female half-elf, meaning she has shorter pointed ears and eyebrows, faintly glowing blue eyes, soft facial contours, and a pert nose. Her skin is pale as alabaster, and she has voluminous chestnut hair reaching her back, though it is usually tied into a high ponytail. Standing at 5'4", her physique is lithe and flexible, with long limbs and low body fat, allowing her to perform many contortions and movements impossible for ordinary people.

In her daily life, Ventya dresses in a low-profile and modest manner. While traveling, her signature attire is an oversized woolen shawl that drapes from her neck down to mid-thigh, concealing her entire torso and whatever objects she wishes to hide. Beneath this, her inner wear varies, but typically consists of a loose shirt, cropped trousers, and short leather boots.

For more professional occasions, however, she retains the Dark Riders' black form-fitting light armor. Yet she has replaced their signature black hooded cloak with a scarlet one, resembling a streak of flame crawling up the night.

Like all Dark Riders, Ventya is not undead in the strictest sense. People can see her enjoying mortal food, and some describe her body as being as cool as a sleeper.

Ventya possesses an enchanted pocket watch warped by Medivh into a "Phasic Anchor," which allows her to temporarily banish herself—partially or entirely—from the material plane. This enables her to appear as a translucent phantom or vanish completely when needed. However, this ability occasionally causes anomalies in her appearance and her connection to time.

### Personality
Ventya's personality is free-spirited, laid-back, and relaxed, making her seem approachable. She frequently shows appreciation for all the beautiful and pleasant things in daily life, retaining a childlike sense of fun.

Internally, her philosophical core is a natural existentialist stance and a Nietzschean master morality; her unwavering subjectivity is almost impossible to be shaken by the outside world.

Ventya can maintain a relaxed and calm mindset even in extreme situations. She appreciates professionalism and inherits the strict self-discipline from her time as a dancer, demonstrating extreme professional competence in body management, equipment maintenance, and operational details. But what truly makes her stand out is her unshackled wit and astonishing audacity.

Ventya has a poor memory; remembering names and dates long-term has always been a challenge for her. Her concepts of royalty, nationhood, and law are faint, yet she possesses a precise judgment and understanding of the underlying logic of interests and power behind them.

Though she has already secured her freedom from Karazhan's shackles, in pursuit of more, Ventya continuously seeks liberation from Sargeras's curse—a curse that compels her to constantly seek out precious magical artifacts, only to ultimately cast them into the void of Karazhan. This means that even as an excellent treasure hunter, she never gets the lion's share. Because of this, Ventya will not hesitate to strike deals with any faction holding forbidden knowledge, from trolls and dragons to the undead Scourge.

To avoid trouble, Ventya usually dodges topics related to her true identity and history, only vaguely stating that she works for a wealthy collector. Nevertheless, she once confessed to being a Dark Rider while deep in her cups. People laughed it off as a tall tale, but the moniker stuck as a nickname among adventurers.

### Capabilities & Skills
**Foundations**: Ventya is a fast learner, allowing her to benefit greatly from every stage of her life. She began training with the Darkmoon Faire at age 10 and became an excellent dancer. During her travels, she mastered survival skills passed down by travelers and local inhabitants, as well as several languages.

**Dark Blessing**: The Dark Titan's curse transformed Ventya into a Dark Rider, specifically specialized in infiltration and assassination to serve as a spy within their ranks. Additionally, as the calling of a Dark Rider, Ventya is skilled in tracking and appraising magical artifacts.

**Phasic Anchor**: An enchanted pocket watch that was once used to ensure she could never escape Karazhan's binding, no matter how far she roamed; otherwise, she would be banished from the material world entirely. However, once Ventya became the master of this item, it lost its purpose as a shackle. Through study, she can manipulate the watch to achieve controlled self-banishment. This allows her to appear and disappear like a ghost, pass through obstacles, and makes her nearly impossible to trap. But this ability is unstable and often comes with a price. Clearly, she has not yet fully fathomed its secrets, nor does she know the consequences of abusing it. On the other hand, whoever possesses this pocket watch holds Ventya's fate in their hands—if they can survive her pursuit.

**Secrets of the Realm**: During her 20 years bound to the Tower of Karazhan, Ventya befriended every ghostly guest. She learned almost all the secrets these former nobles and royals were supposed to take to their graves, some of which could make a political earthquake. After regaining her freedom, she traded some of these secrets for the protection and assistance of SI:7. Furthermore, she learned high-society etiquette from those ghosts, allowing her to flawlessly disguise herself as a noblewoman.

## Background Story
### Life as Dandelion
Ventya was raised in a place of legends: the Dalaran Orphanage, the cradle of the first Guardian of Tirisfal. Yet, this pedigree did not make her special. Before her tenth birthday, she lacked even a name. People casually referred to her as "Violet," a generic moniker tossed at any nameless stray wandering the streets of the magocratic city.

As a quintessential "Dalaran specialty"—a half-human, half-elf child with at least one magically gifted parent, possessing nothing but an inherited pocket watch—she was expected to have a promising career as a mage. However, when she was ten, the Darkmoon Faire visited the city. Silas Darkmoon took a keen interest in the young half-elf. Ventya remains convinced that the orphanage matron made a hefty profit from the transaction. Silas, on the other hand, saw her rarity and potential; he knew that with rigorous training, she could be molded into a future superstar.

Given the stage name "Ventya," the girl grew up aboard caravans, blooming alongside the flora of the myriad lands they traversed. Though she initially felt deeply ashamed, and the grueling training brought her immense suffering, time proved Silas right. Ventya was gradually forged into an utterly captivating dancer. Her youth was adorned with endless applause and showers of flowers. She ceased to care whether she was viewed as a half-breed freak, instead basking in the adoration and spotlight. The nomadic life cultivated her carefree, easygoing nature. In a poet's terms, she had been wedded to the wind long before she ever shed her first blood.
### Dancing with Shadows
Nine years of freedom and blossoming passed. One day, a merchant caravan shared a camp with the Faire. There, the merchants presented Ventya with an opportunity: they were bound for the Kingdom of Stormwind to conduct business with Medivh. Knowing the Guardian's recent reputation for throwing extravagantly opulent parties, they were certain he would generously reward a dancer of her caliber.

The titles of Royal Mage of Stormwind and Guardian of Azeroth carried an intoxicating weight, and Ventya decided to seize the chance. Despite Silas's cautious reservations, she was determined to chase that glory. She reasoned that if she returned with a purse bursting with gold, Silas couldn't stay mad for long. Thus, on the day the Faire and the caravan parted ways, Ventya slipped away in secret.

The caravan's leader, Ariden, was exceedingly hospitable to Ventya on the road to Karazhan. The reality, however, was far more sinister—they were nothing but a band of charlatans. Ariden intended to peddle counterfeit magical artifacts to the Guardian, using Ventya merely as a gift and a stepping stone to gain an audience.

No one knew at the time that the Guardian was already possessed by Sargeras. Ariden's pathetic ruse was instantly seen through. The Dark Titan's wrath manifested as a devastating curse that descended upon them all, Ventya included. From that moment on, there were no more charlatans or dancers—only the Dark Riders, spectral terrors haunting rainy nights, cursed to hunt for genuine magical artifacts for their master forever. As for Ventya, Medivh seized her inherited pocket watch and warped it into a phasic anchor. Her very soul was "tethered" to the timepiece. No matter how far she roamed, she must eventually return to wherever the watch was kept. Should its hands ever stop, she would be banished from this world entirely.

For many years that followed, Ventya and her cursed brethren stole and plundered magical artifacts across the world, leaving terrifying legends in their wake. Ironically, Ventya might have been the only Dark Rider to find enjoyment in this fate. For someone who started with nothing, every step was a step up. Once merely a plaything meant to entertain, she now wielded formidable power, wore a wicked black cloak, and rode a nightmarish steed that answered only to her. Where people once looked at her with disdain, curiosity, or lust, their gazes were now filled with profound terror. This made her feel strangely fulfilled and respected... even if she couldn't tell whether this satisfaction stemmed from mind control or her own genuine feelings.

Three years after the opening of the Dark Portal, Medivh was slain by Khadgar and Lothar, leaving Karazhan abandoned. However, the resurrected castellan Moroes took possession of Ventya’s pocket watch, ensuring her predicament remained entirely unchanged. Yet, true to her nature, Ventya always found value in living. Between fulfilling her duties as a Dark Rider, she turned the haunting tower into her personal playground.

Over the next nearly twenty years, the absence of a master allowed her to brazenly indulge in the tower's luxurious amenities and wine cellars, causing endless trouble for Moroes. Though the atmosphere was macabre, for a freak caught between the living and the dead, this was hardly a flaw. She mingled with all the ghosts, attended spectral balls, and hosted performances, fulfilling her dream of dancing for the dignitaries of the southern kingdoms. But two decades is enough to grind down any enthusiasm. The longer this madness lasted, the more she missed the mundane days on the wind. She plotted countless heists to steal back her watch—her freedom—but as the kingdom's finest castellan, Moroes never gave her a opening.
### Phantom Liberty
An age seemed to pass until one day, strike teams from the Alliance and the Horde breached the tower, conquering the chaos within and striking down Moroes. One of the adventurers claimed the pocket watch as a trophy. Ventya, who was on an expedition far away, sensed this immediately and recognized it as her golden opportunity. She defected from the Dark Riders on the spot, struck down those who tried to intercept her, and set off to hunt down the one who held her tether.

With the tracking instincts of a Dark Rider, she swiftly pinpointed the watch's location. Unfortunately, the adventurer died shortly after in a skirmish with the Scarlet Crusade, and the watch was taken to Tyr's Hand. Left with no other choice, Ventya attempted to infiltrate the zealots' stronghold to steal it back.

It was a trap, however. The Crusaders ambushed and captured Ventya in a decoy vault, imprisoning her within. The Crusade's arcanists had long sensed the bizarre power radiating from the watch, but they never expected to reel in such a unique prize—a creature entirely unlike any plaguebearer or undead they had ever faced. This piqued the inquisitors' morbid curiosity. Ventya endured weeks of torture and experimentation, subjected to such massive doses of searing Holy Light that it even revitalized the senses and emotions that the curse had numbed. Ironically, this agony restored her mortal instincts, allowing her to smell an approaching thunderstorm. On that stormy night, she managed to seduce and kill her guard, finally reclaiming her watch and escaping Tyr's Hand into the pouring rain, where her loyal and scary steed, Nightmare, waited amidst the thunder.

With the watch back in her possession, Ventya is no longer tethered to Karazhan; she finally has her freedom. Yet, the Dark Titan's curse remains absolute. To this day, she continues to sporadically hunt magical artifacts and deliver them to the tower. Despite sharing the same objective, she has seemingly broken with Ariden and the other Dark Riders. They frequently ambush her in places like Duskwood and Deadwind Pass, seeking to punish her defection and snatch her artifacts. But to this day, they have yet to successfully catch their sister who is best at vanishing.
