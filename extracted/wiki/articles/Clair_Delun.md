# Clair Delun

Clair Delun, a [Human](Human) mage from Lakeshire, was once a student of frost magic. She is the wife of the paladin [Zaynn the Vengeful](Zaynn the Vengeful). Together, they run their apple cider business called **“Five Stars Apple Cider”**.

## **Appearance and Personality**
Clair is renowned for her beauty, particularly her striking outer features. She has straight, shoulder-length brown hair. Her captivating hazel eyes, full red lips, rosy cheeks, heart-shaped face, and sculpted figure contribute to her allure.

As for her attire, Clair’s primary outfit consists of a long maroon long sleeved dress with a white, long puffy-sleeved shirt underneath. A brown linen belt cinches her waist, and she completes the ensemble with dark brown linen boots—all meticulously crafted by her own skilled hands.

Clair is compassionate, upbeat, and joyful. She finds comfort in books, her warm heart beating to the rhythm of the pages. In the face of danger, she maintains a hopeful and positive outlook, cheering others when possible and always seeking the good in people. Yet, she remains discerning, able to identify evil. When faced with threats, Clair wishes to help in any way she can, but she is morally opposed to violence and views it as a last resort. Clair loves nature, particularly flowers.
## **Story**
Clair lived in a small house in Lakeshire. She had once resided with her mother and helped tend to their family shop in their village. During the Orc invasion of Stormwind, she tragically lost her father, a footman who fought valiantly to defend the kingdom. Like many other humans in Stormwind, Clair sought refuge to the north, near the town of Southshore. After the Second War concluded with an Alliance victory, she and her mother returned to Redridge. With the kingdom repopulated and peace restored, Clair decided to learn magic for self-defense, as her father’s absence had left her and her mother vulnerable.

Clair took a job as a barmaid at a local tavern in her hometown. It was during this time that she met a paladin named Zaynn, who was a frequent guest at the tavern. The two soon became close, and in the year 606 K.C., after six months of courtship, Zaynn and Clair became betrothed and were married shortly thereafter.

The couple settled in Lakeshire, where they lived relatively untouched by the major events of the world for several years. A few years after the Third War, Clair followed her husband to reclaim his land in the Arathi Highlands, where she helped him run their apple cider production.
