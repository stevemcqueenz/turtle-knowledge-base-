The **Flame of Dagnoth** is a [fire elemental](fire elemental) located in [Grim Hollow](Grim Hollow) in the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches). It is the manifestation of the corruption within the [Flame of Dagnoth](Flame of Dagnoth (object)), an ancient well of dwarven magic. [Yor'thegg the Wise](Yor'thegg the Wise) seeks to expel the evil within the flame and destroy it to snuff out the corruption.

- Blast Wave
