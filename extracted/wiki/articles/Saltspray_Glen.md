# Saltspray Glen

[Saltspray Glen](Saltspray Glen) is a small area in the northwestern [Wetlands](Wetlands) bordered by [Bluegill Marsh](Bluegill Marsh) to the south, [Ironbeard's Tomb](Ironbeard's Tomb) to the east, and [Baradin Bay](Baradin Bay) to the north and west. Like [Black Channel Marsh](Black Channel Marsh), [Whelgar's Excavation Site](Whelgar's Excavation Site), and [Raptor Ridge](Raptor Ridge), Saltspray Glen is crawling with the numerous [raptor](raptor)s who nest here. The glen is so named for the ease with which the ocean throws seawater inland here when there is a strong wind blowing off from the bay.
