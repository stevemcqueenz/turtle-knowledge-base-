# Garona

Garona was a half [Orc](Orc), half [Human](Human) assassin that served as personal assistant to [Gul'dan](Gul'dan) just before and during the [First War](First War).  Prior to the [Horde](Horde)'s final attack on [Stormwind](Stormwind) Keep she personally assassinated King Llane of Azeroth.
