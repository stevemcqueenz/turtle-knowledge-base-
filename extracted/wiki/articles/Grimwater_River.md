# Grimwater River

-Grimwater River** (ref: [quest: Miregill Distraction]) is is a river that runs from [Lake Kithas](Lake Kithas) in the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches) and empties into the [Forbidding Sea](Forbidding Sea). Led by [Gargill the Butcher](Gargill the Butcher), a group of [Miregill](Miregill) [murloc](murloc)s has gathered along Grimwater's southern bank, threatening the local dwarven fishermen.
