Lady **Miralin Wavesinger** is a current member of the [Regency Council](Regency Council) of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas). When Lord [Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn) made a motion before the Council to be named Lord-Regent of Quel'Thalas following the retaking of [Shalandis Isle](Shalandis Isle) by the [Silver Covenant](Silver Covenant), Miralin voted in his favor.
