# Echo Ridge Mine

**Echo Ridge Mine** is a mine located in northwestern [Northshire Valley](Northshire Valley) within [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest). It has recently been taken over by [kobolds](kobolds), much to the frustration of the humans living in the area.

The kobolds inhabiting the mine are needed for a few [quest](quest)s. The mine is quite simple to maneuver through and the quest should be easy, as the kobolds are defensive and will not attack you unless you attack them. If you follow the path to the back of the mine, you will find a larger room; in which a chest spawns and usually has a few [copper](copper) to help with the finances and maybe a new [weapon](weapon). It is worth taking a quick peak to see if it's there.
