# Vendor

# Overview
Any [NPC](NPC) who buys and sells [item](item)s. [Trainer](Trainer)s are not technically vendors, even though they sell [recipe](recipe)s, since they do not buy anything.

Also sometimes used as a verb for selling to a vendor, as in, "I'm just going to *vendor* this axe, since I can't use it."

## Types of Vendors
- General Goods
- Trade Supplies
- Armor
- Weapon
- Blacksmithing Supplies
- Fishing Supplies
