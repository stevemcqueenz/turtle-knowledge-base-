The **Wildhammer clan** is a dwarf clan currently centered mainly in the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands) and the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches). They are renowned gryphon riders and lead the [Wildhammer dwarves](Wildhammer dwarves) as a whole. They are led by High Thane [Falstad Wildhammer](Falstad Wildhammer).

Since they were driven from the keep of Grim Batol, the Wildhammer dwarves have resided in isolated territories and prized their independence above all else. Yet, when their Bronzebeard cousins and their Alliance allies have found themselves threatened by orcs during the [Second War](Second War), or undead during the [Third War](Third War), the gruff and free-willed Wildhammers have stepped in to help out.

## History
### War of the Three Hammers
, former home of the Wildhammers.]]
Just prior to the [War of the Three Hammers](War of the Three Hammers), the Wildhammer clan, ruled by Thane [Khardros Wildhammer](Khardros Wildhammer), inhabited the foothills and crags around the base of [Ironforge](Ironforge). After Modimus died, a civil war broke out between the [Bronzebeard clan](Bronzebeard clan), the Wildhammer clan, and the [Dark Iron clan](Dark Iron clan) for control of the kingdom. The Wildhammer clan was unsuccessful in wresting control of Ironforge. Banished from Ironforge by the Bronzebeards, Khardros and his Wildhammer warriors traveled north and founded their own kingdom within the distant peak of [Grim Batol](Grim Batol). There, the Wildhammers thrived and rebuilt their stores of treasure.

[Thaurissan](Thaurissan), leader of the Dark Iron clan, and his Dark Irons vowed revenge against Ironforge. Thaurissan and his sorceress wife, [Modgud](Modgud), launched a two-pronged assault against both Ironforge and Grim Batol, intent on claiming all of Khaz Modan for their own. As Modgud confronted the enemy warriors, she used her powers to strike fear into their hearts. Shadows moved at her command, and dark things crawled up from the depths of the earth to stalk the Wildhammers in their own halls. Eventually Modgud broke through the gates and laid siege to the fortress itself. The Wildhammers fought desperately, Khardros himself wading through the roiling masses to slay the sorceress queen. With their queen lost, the Dark Irons fled before the fury of the Wildhammers. They raced south toward their king's stronghold, only to meet the armies of Ironforge, which had come to aid Grim Batol after [Madoran Bronzebeard](Madoran Bronzebeard) had led his clan to a decisive victory over Thaurissan's sorcerous army. Crushed between two armies, the remaining Dark Iron forces were utterly destroyed.

The combined armies of Ironforge and Grim Batol then turned south, intent on destroying Thaurissan and his Dark Irons once and for all. They had not gone far when Thaurissan's fury resulted in a spell of cataclysmic proportions. Seeking to summon a supernatural minion that would ensure his victory, Thaurissan called upon the ancient powers sleeping beneath the world. To his shock, and ultimately his doom, the creature that emerged was more terrible than any nightmare he could have imagined. [Ragnaros](Ragnaros) the Firelord, immortal lord of all fire elementals, had been banished by the [Titan](Titan)s when the world was young. Now, freed by Thaurissan's call, Ragnaros erupted into being once again. Ragnaros' apocalyptic rebirth into Azeroth shattered the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains) and created [a raging volcano](Blackrock Mountain) at the center of the devastation. Horrified at the devastation and the fires spreading across the southern mountains, Madoran and Khardros halted their armies and hastily turned back towards their kingdoms, unwilling to face the awesome wrath of Ragnaros.

Once the immediate Dark Iron threat was eliminated, the Wildhammers returned home to Grim Batol. However, the death of the Modgud had left an evil stain on the mountain fortress, and the Wildhammers found it uninhabitable. Though Madoran offered the Wildhammers a place to live within the borders of Ironforge, the Wildhammers steadfastly refused. Khardros took his people north towards the lands of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). Settling within the lush forests of the Hinterlands, the Wildhammers crafted the city of Aerie Peak, where the Wildhammers grew closer to nature and even bonded with the mighty gryphons of the area. Seeking to retain relations and trade with their cousins, the dwarves of Ironforge constructed two massive arches, the [Thandol Span](Thandol Span), to bridge the gap between [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) and [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). Bolstered by mutual trade, the two kingdoms prospered. After the deaths of Madoran and Khardros, their sons jointly commissioned two great statues in honor of their fathers, [standing guard](Valley of Kings) over [the pass](Stonewrought Pass) into the southlands, which had become volcanic in the wake of Ragnaros' scorching presence. They served as both a warning to all who would attack the dwarven kingdoms, and as a reminder of what price the Dark Irons paid for their crimes. (ref: [War of the Three Hammers](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/War_of_the_Three_Hammers_(History_of_Warcraft)))

Though most Wildhammers settled in what would soon become Aerie Peak, the dwarves of [Kalvanost](Kalvanost) and [Dun Kithas](Dun Kithas) instead continued their search, parting from their brethren and often straying into human territories. The mighty Sky King of Kalvanost, [Morgryn](Morgryn), made war upon the humans of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)) and [Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)), reaping their wealth to restore his own. The fairer Sky King of Dun Kithas, [Redoran](Redoran), flew instead from thence far into the west, until he came to the [Gilnean](Gilneas (kingdom)) city of [Gelnor](Gelnor). There, he and his people settled after being gifted a place in Gelnor and to Redoran a seat at the court of Queen [Elaena Greymane](Elaena Greymane). (ref: [The Dwarves of Gelnor](The Dwarves of Gelnor)) Others clinged to their holdings in the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches), though in time, the Wildhammers of the Grim Reaches would come to forget much of their Wildhammer lineage.

### Second War
.]]
Following the ravaging of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) during the [Second War](Second War), the [Horde](Old Horde) pushed farther north into the [Wetlands](Wetlands), taking [Dun Algaz](Dun Algaz), [Dun Modr](Dun Modr), and [Grim Batol](Grim Batol) and converting them into fortresses for the Horde. Grim Batol became the primary base for the land offensive and was also converted into a hatchery for the enslaved [red dragonflight](red dragonflight). [Thandol Span](Thandol Span) was a pivotal battleground in the Second War, constantly shifting in possession between the two factions.

From the ominous and foreboding peaks of the Hinterlands and [Northeron](Northeron) came the [dwarven](Dwarf) gryphon riders. Mounted upon their legendary beasts and wielding the mystic stormhammers forged deep within the secret chambers of their aviaries, these feral dwarves feared no enemy - and relied upon no friend. They had allied themselves only with the [elves](High elf) of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas) at the time, distrusting both their [dwarven cousins](Bronzebeard dwarf) and the [human](human)s alike. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Alliance Air Units](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Alliance_Air_Units)*, Gryphon Rider)

During the Second War, Commander [Baggoth Ironmantle](Baggoth Ironmantle) was the commander in charge of the Wildhammer defenders of the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches), fighting the orcs of the [Dragonmaw clan](Dragonmaw clan) until he was felled during the Dragonmaw's retreat. After his death, his personal standard, the [Banner of Baggoth](Banner of Baggoth), was later adopted as a whole by the Wildhammer clan (ref:  [33] [The Dragonmaw War (3)](The Dragonmaw War (3))) and is flown in Aerie Peak.

### Battle for Grim Batol
Falstad Wildhammer, a noble gryphon rider of the Wildhammer clan of [Hinterlands](Hinterlands), commanded a small flight of such riders who patrolled the shores of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) for any hostile draconic activity after the [Second War](Second War). Falstad and his comrades protected the town of [Hasic](Hasic) from certain doom and then transported human mage [Rhonin](Rhonin) and his companions across the [Great Sea](Great Sea) to [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan). During the journey, however, the group was ambushed and Falstad's kinsmen were killed. Nevertheless, at the incentive of [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner), Falstad continued to Khaz Modan. Later, with the help of a clan of [Bronzebeard dwarves](Bronzebeard dwarf), Falstad reached [Grim Batol](Grim Batol) and fought in the final battle there to free the Red Dragon Queen [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza). (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, chapter 21)

[Kurdran Wildhammer](Kurdran Wildhammer), Gryphon Master of the Aerie Peak, went to [Draenor](Draenor) as part of the [Alliance Expedition](Alliance Expedition). Kurdran and his gryphon riders participated in the [Invasion of Draenor](Invasion of Draenor) and were one of the heroes willing to sacrifice their lives to safeguard Azeroth. Kurdran Wildhammer would later have a statue in his likeness built in the [Valley of Heroes](Valley of Heroes) to honor him for his heroic deeds. (ref: [Kurdran Wildhammer's statue description](Kurdran Wildhammer#Valley of Heroes monument))

### Third War
The daring dwarves of the Wildhammer responded once again to answer the call to war during the [Third War](Third War). They brought the mighty gryphons of the [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak) to aid the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron) in its time of need. Armed with their trusty, lightning-powered stormhammers, the fearless wild dwarves sought to keep the skies of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) free from enemy forces. The proud gryphons share their riders' implacable resolve and stand as noble symbols of the Alliance's fortitude. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* manual, *[Human Units](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Gryphon_Rider)*, Gryphon Rider)

Following the scourging of Lordaeron by the corrupted [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil), the Alliance of Lordaeron crumbled with the death of [King Terenas Menethil II](Terenas Menethil II). Its remains in Lordaeron were later rallied by [Grand Marshal Othmar Garithos](Othmar Garithos) under the [Alliance Remnants](Alliance Remnants), with both [Bronzebeard](Bronzebeard clan) and Wildhammer [dwarves](Dwarf) among their ranks. (ref: [Dreadlord's Fall (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Dreadlord%27s_Fall_(WC3_Undead))) However, Othmar's terrible leadership and disdain of the non-human races led to his undoing and all who followed him, including the Wildhammer riders. (ref: They are no more present in [A New Power in Lordaeron](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_New_Power_in_Lordaeron_(WC3_Undead)).)

### World of Warcraft
, capital of the Wildhammer dwarves.]]
Years after the [Third War](Third War), the Wildhammer dwarves continued to prosper in their home of [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak) in the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands), now led by Falstad Wildhammer. The most immediate threat to their security came from the east in the form of the [Witherbark tribe](Witherbark tribe), [Vilebranch](Vilebranch tribe) [trolls](Forest troll), and the [Horde](Horde)-aligned [Revantusk](Revantusk tribe) trolls. The rugged terrain of the Hinterlands was not only home to the Wildhammer dwarves and their gryphons, but also to these hostile forest troll tribes.

The Wildhammers of Aerie Peak were not part of the reformed [Alliance](Alliance) after the [Third War](Third War), but were still friendly and helpful to Alliance adventurers who have proven their worth to them. (ref:  [45] [Troll Necklace Bounty](Troll Necklace Bounty)) However, the Wildhammer Dwarves of Aerie Peak did strike an alliance with the [high elves](High elf) of [Quel'Danil](Quel'Danil Lodge), much to the ire of their common enemy, the [Revantusk](Revantusk) trolls. (ref:  [48] [Message to the Wildhammer](Message to the Wildhammer))

Considering the presence of [Aerie Gryphon](Aerie Gryphon)s in [Alterac Valley](Alterac Valley), it seems an expedition from the Wildhammer clan of [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak) participated in the battle or lent aid in some capacity.

The Wildhammers of the Grim Reaches, who have forgotten much of their clan's heritage over the years, faced a great peril when the region was invaded anew by the [Dragonmaw clan](Dragonmaw clan), forcing the farmers out of their homes and evacuate to [Dun Kithas](Dun Kithas). (ref:  [32] [Provisions for War](Provisions for War)) [Stolgaz Keep](Stolgaz Keep) was besieged by the orcs, who focused their efforts on it until it fell in their hands. (ref: [Historian Sternhand#Quotes](Historian Sternhand#Quotes)) Even [Baggoth's Wall](Baggoth's Wall), which has stood for years, has begun to crumble from years of siege and neglect. (ref:  [32] [Repairing Baggoth's Wall](Repairing Baggoth's Wall))

## Reputation
Despite having their own in-game reputation, only the members of the clan in the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands) are part of that faction. The Wildhammers in the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches) not tagged as such, though quests with them still give reputation with the faction.

### Faction description
*Though not a part of the [Alliance](Alliance), the [dwarves](Wildhammer dwarf) of [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak) have opened their doors to it and hold the [Horde](Horde) as enemies.*

## Patch changes
- **Patch 1.5.0 (2005-06-07):** The Wildhammer Clan in the Hinterlands is now a peaceful faction to the Alliance, meaning Alliance players cannot intentionally declare war on them.
