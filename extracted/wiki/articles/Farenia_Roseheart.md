Lady **Farenia Roseheart** is a current member of the [Regency Council](Regency Council) of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas). The youngest member of the Council, she is a staunch supporter of [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner), being the only member of the Council to vote in her favor when Lord [Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn) made a motion to be named Lord-Regent of Quel'Thalas following the retaking of [Shalandis Isle](Shalandis Isle). (ref: [A GOOD DAY — Everlook Broadcasting Co. Radio Play Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u90BCZfygz0))
