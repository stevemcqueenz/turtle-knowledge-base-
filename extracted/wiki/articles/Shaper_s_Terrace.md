# Shaper's Terrace

The **Shaper's Terrace** is an area located in the [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater). It is devoid of NPCs.

## Notes and trivia
- It is possible to jump up to the Shaper's Terrace using wallclimbing. Alternatively, a [Riding Gryphon](Riding Gryphon) can be found at the <Farmers Market> guild base located west of [Marshal's Refuge](Marshal's Refuge).[39.0, 11.8]
- The Shaper's Terrace was formerly home to the [Keepers of Outerend](Keepers of Outerend), who were removed in an unknown update.
