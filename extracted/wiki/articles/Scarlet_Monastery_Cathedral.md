## Dungeon denizens
The Cathedral, the center-left entrance, consists of two sections: the [Chapel Gardens](Chapel Gardens); and the [Crusader's Chapel](Crusader's Chapel) with the bosses [Scarlet Commander Mograine](Scarlet Commander Mograine) and [High Inquisitor Whitemane](High Inquisitor Whitemane). [High Inquisitor Fairbanks](High Inquisitor Fairbanks) is hidden in a concealed chamber of the Crusader's Chapel.
