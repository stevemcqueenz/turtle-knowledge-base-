# Ruins of Stardust

The **Ruins of Stardust** are located in southwestern [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), south of [Astranaar](Astranaar) and west of [the Talondeep Path](the Talondeep Path). The ruins are situated on a lake island, and several large [bog beast](bog beast)s roam the ruins, corrupted by the lake's tainted waters. Much like [Iris Lake](Iris Lake), the ruins have been exposed to the demonic rot from [Felwood](Felwood), and the area is tinted a sickly yellowish green color. The most notable exception is the stardust shrubs scattered about the area. They are unmarred by the taint, and are known to contain powerful healing properties if imbibed.
