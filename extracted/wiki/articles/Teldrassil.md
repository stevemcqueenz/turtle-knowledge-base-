**Teldrassil** (meaning "Crown of the Earth" in [Darnassian](Darnassian)) is a [World Tree](World Tree) situated on the island of **Kalidar**, off of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor)'s northwestern coast. (ref: https://web.archive.org/web/20051128220650/http://www.blizzard.com/wow/townhall/kalidar.shtml) Created shortly after the [Third War](Third War) by the [night elves](night elf), Teldrassil serves as their home and primary base of operations, its thick crown housing the capital city of [Darnassus](Darnassus). Other major settlements on the tree include [Rut'theran Village](Rut'theran Village), [Dolanaar](Dolanaar) and [Shadowglen](Shadowglen). Owing to its size, Teldrassil possesses its own unique ecosystem with a various flora, fauna, and vast twilit forests.

## History
After the cataclysmic events that followed the [invasion](Third War) of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), [Malfurion](Malfurion Stormrage) went missing. Archdruid [Fandral Staghelm](Fandral Staghelm) took over the leadership of the night elven druids, convincing the [Circle of Ancients](Circle of Ancients) in [Darkshore](Darkshore) that it was time for the night elven people to rebuild the World Tree and regain their cherished immortality. However, his plan failed due to the fact that [Nozdormu](Nozdormu), the source of the night elves immortality, did not bless the tree.

The Circle of Ancients and powerful druids combined their powers to grow an immense tree in the [Veiled Sea](Veiled Sea). They called the tree Teldrassil, meaning "crown of the earth" in [Darnassian](Darnassian). The night elves made their new home in the boughs of Teldrassil, creating a forest of mighty trees and flowing rivers, bathed in the endless night that fell over the lands of the elves from the dawn of time. 

However, the calm of the enchanted forest has slowly fallen into the shadow of a sinister presence. Crazed [furbolg](furbolg)s and fouler beasts have appeared in Teldrassil, occupying all the attention of the vigilant [Sentinels](Sentinels) and the wise [druid](druid)s to ensure the safety of their new home.

## Geography
The majority of Teldrassil's upper reaches is open forest and it resembles a mid-sized forested island. Darnassus takes up a large chunk on the western side of this island, while smaller cities dot the remaining forest. All forms of terrain exist here, including streams, full-sized lakes, and a few hills that might be considered mountains.

The entire [zone](zone) of Teldrassil is in fact one huge tree, grown on an island off the coast of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). As such, there is no [ore](ore) to speak of here and the night elves are not known to be [blacksmith](Smith)s or [miner](miner)s. However, because the tree is so full of life, [herb](herb)s are numerous here.

Teldrassil contains no [dungeon](dungeon)s, [raid dungeon](raid dungeon)s, [micro dungeon](micro dungeon)s, or [battleground](battleground)s. It is the starting area for the [night elves](night elf) and contains a very sheltered 1-5 leveling area, [Shadowglen](Shadowglen). The night elf [capital city](capital city) of [Darnassus](Darnassus) can also be found in this zone, though the travel hub is actually located at [Rut'theran Village](Rut'theran Village) (accessible by portal from Darnassus).
 
Although Teldrassil is only the size of a mid-sized island in width, you must also take into account the sheer size in height that rivals any other island. It has been described as an "island continent". Another reference in the book and other sources simply call it an island.

### Travel hubs
Though the flight path to Teldrassil is technically in [Rut'theran Village](Rut'theran Village), the village is connected to [Darnassus](Darnassus) by a [portal](portal) and is indeed the only way to get into the rest of the zone. Players can also reach Rut'theran Village by boat from [Auberdine](Auberdine), which is the only way for Horde players to get onto the island.
