# Gates of Ironforge

The **Gates of Ironforge** (or the **Ironforge Main Gate**) (ref: Sign post in Ironforge) lead from the lands of [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh) into the city of [Ironforge](Ironforge) proper. They are a grand sight, and are visible from far across Dun Morogh. They can be reached by heading north up the great ramp in central Dun Morogh, or southwest from the [Commons](Commons).
