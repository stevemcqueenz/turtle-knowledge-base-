# Brill Town Hall

The **Brill Town Hall** is the most prominent structure in the town of [Brill](Brill) in central [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades). [Magistrate Sevren](Magistrate Sevren) leads the town from his podium, issuing orders and tasks to willing [Forsaken](Forsaken) subjects. The town hall is also notable because it is the only town hall in the realms known to be run by the [Undead](Forsaken).
