## Objectives
Gather the Old Crypt Key, and the Mark of Karazhan from around the ruins of Karazhan for Kor'gan in Stonard.
- [Mark of Karazhan](Mark of Karazhan)
- [Old Crypt Key](Old Crypt Key)

## Description
If we are to reforge the key, then we will need the items that Gunther Arcanus had hinted to. I have no doubts in my mind that both of these items can be found lingering around the tower.

The first item we shall acquire is the key itself, there are rumors of a sickly, pale human who emerges when not a living soul is about, to tend the grounds around Karazhan. It would be from him, that the Old Crypt Key can be found, find him, and claim it for our purposes, he should be around.

Lastly, a Mark of Karazhan will be required, of which there are not many left on this mortal world. I have heard tales of a Captain lingering in the caves of The Master's Cellar. Captain Rothynn and his twin brother were both given one long ago, he should still have one.

When these items are obtained, return to me, and I can forge the key.

## Rewards
You will receive:
- 6150 XP
- +250 [reputation](reputation) with [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar (faction))

## Progress
Be dedicated in your searching, if we are to destroy this evil, then we must act fast.

## Completion
So, the stories were correct, the items have been obtained, with the Pendant of Ardan, the Mark of Karazhan, and the key itself, I can begin to work my magic.

## Notes
Both of the NPCs are found in the [Master's Cellar](Master's Cellar) in the ruined homes just before the tower entrance, with each of the NPCs on opposite ends of the area from one another. [Groundskeeper Jacoby](Groundskeeper Jacoby) can be found at the very end of the wine cellar's right-most passage, whereas [Captain Rothynn](Captain Rothynn) haunts the underground caves beneath the collapsed cellar. You can skip some of the mobs in the caves by hugging the left wall and then dropping down into a small crevasse in the floor and continuing along the way again, keeping to the left.
