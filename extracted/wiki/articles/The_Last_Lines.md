<The journal features dedicated listings of sightings, observations, shift schedules and oddities encountered during the few hours the corps was stationed on the plateau. Furthermore, it appears that a small force of Thrall's Horde has settled on one of the smaller islands. Whatever they are planning on this island remains a conundrum for now.
