The bloodthirsty corsairs of the **Bloodsail Buccaneers** (also known as the **Bloodsail Pirates**, (ref: [Archived page](https://web.archive.org/web/20060203033409/http://worldofwarcraft.com/community/ingameevents.html)) or simply the **Bloodsail**) (ref:  [42] [Abandoned Equipment](Abandoned Equipment)) are the bane of many a merchant in the high seas. They are sworn enemies of the [Blackwater Raiders](Blackwater Raiders) of [Booty Bay](Booty Bay).
