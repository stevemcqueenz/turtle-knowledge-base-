# Grim Hollow

**The Grim Hollow** is a wooded swamp located in the southern [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches). As of late, a darkness has seeped into the Hollow. The spirits of the dead interred in the [Tomb of Ancestors](Tomb of Ancestors) walk again, (ref: [quest: The Grim Hollow]) the red dragonkin that patrol the marsh are ensnared by the same corruption that poisons the land, (ref: [quest: Tomb of Ancestors]) and even the wildlife is overgrown and sickly - the spiders in particular swarm the swamp trails like they own the place. (ref: [quest: Creeping Trouble]) The mist hangs thick, and the shadows feel like they're watching travelers.
