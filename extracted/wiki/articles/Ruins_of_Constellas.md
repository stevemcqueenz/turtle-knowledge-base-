# Ruins of Constellas

The **Ruins of Constellas** is an area of [Felwood](Felwood).  [Xavathras](Xavathras), and his group of Jadefire [satyr](satyr), inhabit the area.  Judging by the remnants of the architecture, and the existence of a [moonwell](moonwell), it used to be one of the many [night elven](night elven) settlements near [Mount Hyjal](Mount Hyjal).  This settlement was presumably destroyed in the [Great Sundering](Great Sundering).  The moonwell has since become corrupted with [Fel](Fel) energy, and is being cultivated by Xavathras on behalf of [Xavaric](Xavaric).
