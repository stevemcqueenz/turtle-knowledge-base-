# Stagnant Oasis

The **Stagnant Oasis** is an oasis located between the [Merchant Coast](Merchant Coast) and the [Gold Road](Gold Road) 55 42 The Barrens (g/s/c). In addition to [Kolkar](Kolkar) [centaur](centaur)s camped on its fringes and other indigenous oasis flora, it is also home to several [Lost Barrens Kodo](Lost Barrens Kodo)s, which due to their weakened state and solitary nature are much easier to hunt than the [kodo](kodo) of the [Field of Giants](Field of Giants) to the south.
The Stagnant Oasis can be fished for [Deviate Fish](Deviate Fish) which can be valuable when cooked into [Savory Deviate Delight](Savory Deviate Delight)s.
