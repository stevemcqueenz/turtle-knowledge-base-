-Grand Inquisitor Isillien** is a [human](human) priest and the spiritual heart of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) in the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands). Along with Highlord [Alexandros Mograine](Alexandros Mograine) and [High General Abbendis](High General Abbendis), he was one of the original founders of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade). (ref: [Corrupted Ashbringer/Scarlet Monastery Event](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Corrupted_Ashbringer/Scarlet_Monastery_Event))

During his time in the Crusade, he had taken on [Taelan Fordring](Taelan Fordring) as his pupil and had envisioned grand plans for him. However, Taelan eventually came to realize just how far the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) had fallen from its noble purpose, and defected. As he made his way down the road from [Hearthglen](Hearthglen) to meet his father by the forest's edge, he was met by Isillien, who had been charged by [Grand Crusader Dathrohan](Grand Crusader Dathrohan) with eliminating him and his newfound allies. He was willing to do this for pleasure, not of obligation or duty; for he considered the Fordring bloodline weak. The Inquisitor stroked down Taelan. Not long after, [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring) arrived and, upon seeing his son's body, flew into a rage and attacked the Crusader, killing him. (ref: [quest: In Dreams])

## Abilities
- **Dominate Mind** — Takes control of a humanoid enemy up to level 25 for 15 sec.
- **Mana Burn** — Hits nearby enemies with anti-mana bolts. For each point of mana consumed from a target, that target takes 1 damage.
- **Mind Blast** — Inflicts Shadow damage to an enemy, but causes a high amount of threat.
