# Command Center

The [Alliance](Alliance) **Command Center** is the base of operations for [Stormwind](Stormwind)'s army officers and [warrior](warrior) trainers. Although it normally houses a large number of soldiers and commanders, most of the city's army has been sent away to distant battlefields, and the Command Center keeps only a skeleton crew of hardened veterans to train the city's militia. The three famous trainers are [Ander Germaine](Ander Germaine), [Wu Shen](Wu Shen), and [Ilsa Corbin](Ilsa Corbin), with Ander having the responsibility of being the leader in charge, and Ilsa being in command of mission assignments.
