# Riftwalker

-Riftwalkers** are practitioners of **riftwalking**, an ancient and sacred [draenei](draenei) magical art that allows them to tear open gateways through the [Twisting Nether](Twisting Nether). (ref:  [52] [Learn Of My Past](Learn Of My Past)) When the [orcish Horde](orcish Horde) ravaged the world of [Draenor](Draenor), the draenei of the [Tribe of Sanv](Tribe of Sanv) opened a rift to the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth) to save themselves and [another tribe](Lost Ones), finding a new home in the [Harborage](Harborage) and the [Fallow Sanctuary](Fallow Sanctuary). Meanwhile, the draenei of the [Moro'gai tribe](Moro'gai tribe) also ventured to Azeroth, finding a new home in the [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast).

Riftwalking requires a large amount of energy, and [draenethyst](draenethyst) is often used as a substitute for power by less experienced riftwalkers. (ref:  Riftwalkers use canes infused with echoes of power as implements to channel their riftwalking.<ref> [54D] [The Riftwalker's Cane](The Riftwalker's Cane))

As riftwalking is taxing on the body and mind due to being exposed to the currents of the Twisting Nether, the draenei have developed recipes to combat the side effects of prolonged riftwalking and accelerate a riftwalker's mental recovery. (ref:  [52] [Rift Fatigue: Body](Rift Fatigue: Body)) (ref:  [52] [Rift Fatigue: Mind](Rift Fatigue: Mind))

Interrupting a riftwalker is in most cases a death sentence, as the riftwalker is thrown through the Twisting Nether when their focus is interrupted.
