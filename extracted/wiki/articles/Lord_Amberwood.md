**Lord Percival Amberwood** is a [human](human) quest giver located in [Amberwood Keep](Amberwood Keep) in [Northwind](Northwind). He is the fourth descendant of the Amberwood lineage and oversees the region as a whole. All citizens of Northwind answer to him and his wise decisions. Beloved by many, his way of ruling follows a more calculated way compared to that of his father. (ref: [The Nobles of Northwind](The Nobles of Northwind))
