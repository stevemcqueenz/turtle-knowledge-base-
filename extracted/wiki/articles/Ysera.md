-Ysera the Dreamer** is one of the five [Dragon Aspects](Dragon Aspects) and the leader of the [green dragonflight](green dragonflight).

## Biography
### The Charge of the Dragonflights
Once the Titans felt satisfied with their creation upon Azeroth, they left to roam the cosmos in search of new worlds on which they would shape order from chaos, creating new life throughout a myriad of worlds. Before their departure from Azeroth however, the Titans empowered five dragons to become stewards of that world. Their purpose: to guard Azeroth as the Dragon Aspects, protecting the Titans' creation.

[Eonar](Eonar), the titan patron of all life, blessed [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza)'s younger sister, the lithe green dragon Ysera, with a portion of nature's influence. Ysera fell into an eternal trance, bound to the waking Dream of Creation. Known as the Dreamer, she would watch over the growing wilds of the world from her verdant realm, the [Emerald Dream](Emerald Dream).<ref>[Charge of the Dragonflights](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Charge_of_the_Dragonflights))

### The War of the Ancients
Ysera was one of the Dragon Aspects that gave some of her power to the [Demon Soul](Demon Soul), not knowing that [Neltharion](Neltharion) would use it to betray them. (ref: 

When her dear friend [Malorne](Malorne) died to the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), Ysera came to a screeching halt in the middle of combat and opened her eyes with a deafening shriek, one of the few times she had ever opened her eyes in history. As she tore through the carnage to her friend, thousands of demons were thrown in all directions with her paws and wings. Her rampage continued, making her way through to her goal, until she came still, her chin on Malorne's head; sobs could be heard as She of the Dreaming mourned the White Stag's end.

After the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients), Alexstrasza planted a magical acorn from the Mother Tree [G'Hanir](G'Hanir) within the second Well of Eternity, created by [Illidan](Illidan). The Tree grew large until it reached the heavens and was crowned [Nordrassil](Nordrassil). Ysera enchanted the tree so that it would be bound to the Emerald Dream.

### The Day of the Dragon
At the behest of [Krasus](Krasus), Ysera, [Malygos](Malygos), and [Nozdormu](Nozdormu) grudgingly agreed to come out of their lairs and fight Deathwing. Due to the power they had given to the Demon Soul, they were no match for the Destroyer - until the mage [Rhonin](Rhonin) was able to take the Demon Soul from [Nekros Skullcrusher](Nekros Skullcrusher) and destroy it, releasing the powers the dragons had given to it ten thousand years earlier. With their powers restored, and the newly-freed Alexstrasza joining the fight, Deathwing was forced to retreat.

### World of Warcraft
In recent times, Ysera's most trusted lieutenants, [Lethon](Lethon), [Emeriss](Emeriss), [Taerar](Taerar) and [Ysondre](Ysondre) have been warped by a dark new power within the Emerald Dream. Now these wayward sentinels have passed through the Great Trees into Azeroth, intending to spread madness and terror throughout the mortal kingdoms. Even the mightiest of adventurers would be well advised to give the dragons a wide berth, or suffer the consequences of their misguided wrath. Ysera herself continued to fight the Nightmare in the Dream alongside [Malfurion Stormrage](Malfurion Stormrage) and [Cenarius](Cenarius),<ref> [60R] [The Nightmare Manifests](The Nightmare Manifests)) and appeared before [Ralathius](Ralathius) as she initiated a sacred ritual to ensure the safe passage of adventurers into the [Emerald Sanctum](Emerald Sanctum) in order to stop the Awakening. (ref:  [60] [Into the Dream V](Into the Dream V))
