# Starbreeze Village

[Starbreeze Village](Starbreeze Village) 63 57 Teldrassil (g/s/c) lies south of [Shadowglen](Shadowglen) and east of [Dolanaar](Dolanaar) in [Teldrassil](Teldrassil). Once a [night elf](night elf) [village](village), Starbreeze is now overrun by [corrupted furbolg](corrupted furbolg)s, an indication of the demonic infection that bleeds into Teldrassil.
