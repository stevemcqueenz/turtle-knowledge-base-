# Arathi tribe

The **Arathi** were a tribe of humans who were able to unite the various tribes and found the nation of [Arathor](Arathor) and its city-state capital of [Strom](Strom). (ref: [Arathor and the Troll Wars](Arathor and the Troll Wars)) The Arathi originates from rebels of the [Azotha](Azotha) kingdom of [Corthan](Corthan) fleeing north from their nation as they collapsed and reverted to a tribal state. (ref: [Gheor from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1086439363199635568) - "The Kingdom of Corthan predates the Arathor Empire. Corthan mentions rebels going up north of the now Badlands and implies they have gone back into a tribal state - thus being the first step towards the Empire of Arathor.") 

Following the fracturing of that kingdom, the descendants of the original Arathi tribe travelled south to found the [kingdom of Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)). (ref: [The Seven Kingdoms](The Seven Kingdoms)) The last descendent of the Arathi bloodline was Sir [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar).
