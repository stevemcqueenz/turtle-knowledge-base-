**Kel'Thuzad** was a powerful [human](human) mage, an archmage of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor), and member of the [Council of Six](Council of Six) until his interest in the dark arts caused him to be stripped of his rank and holdings, and exiled from [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)) by the Archmage [Antonidas](Antonidas). Driven by the voice in his head, he traveled to [Northrend](Northrend) and climbed the [Frozen Throne](Frozen Throne) to offer his soul to the [Lich King](Lich King).

Now a necromancer of the [Scourge](Scourge), Kel'Thuzad returned to [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) and founded in secret the [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned), recruiting cultists among the nobility and the lower classes of the kingdoms with promises of immortality. Years later, he became one of the principal agents of the Lich King responsible for spreading the plague of undeath across Lordaeron, (ref: <ref name="Scourge">*Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, [Undead History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#The_Forming_of_the_Scourge), The Forming of the Scourge) resulting in his death at the hands of the paladin and Prince [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil). When Arthas became a death knight and started the Scourging of Lordaeron, he resurrected Kel'Thuzad as a lich with the power of the Sunwell, so he could summon Archimonde the Defiler into Azeroth during the Third War.

Following Arthas's departure for [Northrend](Northrend), and his ascension as the new Lich King, Kel'Thuzad was left to command the Scourge in Lordaeron from the floating necropolis of Naxxramas. After making a deal with the nathrezim Balnazzar, he trapped the Highlord of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) [Alexandros Mograine](Alexandros Mograine) at [Stratholme](Stratholme), and recovered his body to make him a death knight and one of the [Four Horsemen](Four Horsemen). For years he held control of the Plaguelands and fought against the forces of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade), the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn), the [Alliance](Alliance), and the [Horde](Horde) until his defeat at the hands of adventurers.

## Biography
### The Call of the Lich King
Kel'Thuzad was one of the greatest Archmagi of [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)). He was one of the members of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) and was once a dear friend of the Archmage [Antonidas](Antonidas). However, his lust to delve into the dark arts of necromancy made him an outcast amongst his fellow wizards. (ref: [Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos manual#Kel'Thuzad](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Kel'Thuzad))

There were a handful of powerful individuals, scattered across the world, who heard the Lich King's mental summons from Northrend. Most notable of them was the Archmage Kel'Thuzad, of the magical nation of [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)). Kel'Thuzad, one of the senior members of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) — Dalaran's ruling council — had been considered a maverick for years due to his insistence on studying the forbidden arts of necromancy. Driven to learn all he could of the magical world and its shadowy wonders, he was frustrated by what he saw as his peers' outmoded and unimaginative precepts. Upon hearing the powerful summons from Northrend, the Archmage bent all of his considerable will to communing with the mysterious voice. Convinced that the Kirin Tor was too squeamish to seize the power and knowledge inherent in the dark arts, he vowed to learn what he could from the immensely powerful Lich King. (ref: 

At some point prior to the Third War, Antonidas learned that Kel'Thuzad had been experimenting with necromancy by reanimating dead rats. Fearing for the safety and reputation of Dalaran, Antonidas had Kel'Thuzad's properties searched, and all items tainted by dark magic were confiscated and destroyed. Antonidas warned Kel'Thuzad that he would be stripped of his rank and holdings and exiled from both Dalaran and Lordaeron, if he did not abandon his "madness".<ref name="RtD">*[Road to Damnation](Road to Damnation)*) Forsaking his fortune and prestigious political standing, Kel'Thuzad abandoned the ways of the Kirin Tor and left Dalaran forever. Prodded by the Lich King's persistent voice in his mind, he sold his vast holdings and stored away his fortunes. Travelling alone over many leagues of both land and sea, he finally reached the frozen shores of Northrend. Intent on reaching Icecrown and offering his services to the Lich King, the Archmage passed through the ravaged, war-torn ruins of [Azjol-Nerub](Azjol-Nerub). Kel'Thuzad saw firsthand the scope and ferocity of [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul)'s power. He began to believe that allying himself with the mysterious Lich King would not only be wise, but potentially fruitful. (ref: 

Kel'Thuzad met with [Anub'arak](Anub'arak), who told him of all the power he could wield. Frightened by the horrors that he saw in Naxxramas, Kel'Thuzad teleported out and tried to escape. Unfortunately, he was surrounded by wraiths and was brought back inside.

After long months of trekking through the harsh arctic wastelands, Kel'Thuzad finally reached the dark glacier of [Icecrown](Icecrown). He boldly approached Ner'zhul's dark citadel and was shocked when the silent undead guardsmen let him pass as though he was expected. Kel'Thuzad descended deep into the cold earth and found his way down to the bottom of the glacier. There, in the endless cavern of ice and shadows, he prostrated himself before the Frozen Throne and offered his soul to the dark lord of the dead.

To help the Archmage accomplish his mission, Ner'zhul left Kel'Thuzad's humanity intact. The aged, yet still charismatic wizard was charged with using his powers of illusion and persuasion to lull the downtrodden, disenfranchised masses of Lordaeron into a confidence. Once he had their attention, he would offer them a new vision of what society could be – and a new figurehead to call their king.

### The Cult of the Damned
Over the span of three years, he used his fortune and intellect to gather a clandestine brotherhood of like-minded men and women. The brotherhood, which he called the Cult of the Damned, promised its acolytes social equality and eternal life on Azeroth in exchange for their service and obedience to Ner'zhul. As the months passed, Kel'Thuzad found many eager volunteers for his new cult amongst the tired, overburdened laborers of Lordaeron. Surprisingly, Kel'Thuzad's goal to pervert the citizens' faith in the Holy Light towards belief in Ner'zhul's dark shadow was easily attained. As the Cult of the Damned grew in size and influence, Kel'Thuzad made sure to keep its workings secret from the authorities of Lordaeron at every turn.

On [Caer Darrow](Caer Darrow), Kel'Thuzad made a deal with the [Barov](Barov) family and some of his cultists moved into the [Scholomance](Scholomance).<ref>[Eva Sarkhoff#Quotes](Eva Sarkhoff#Quotes))

With Kel'Thuzad's success in Lordaeron, the Lich King made the final preparations for his assault against human civilization. Placing his plague energies into a number of portable artifacts called plague cauldrons, Ner'zhul ordered Kel'Thuzad to transport the cauldrons to Lordaeron where they would be hidden within various cult-controlled villages. The cauldrons, protected by the loyal cultists, would then act as plague generators, sending the plague seeping out across the unsuspecting farmlands and cities of northern Lordaeron. (ref: 

The Lich King's plan worked perfectly. Many of Lordaeron's northern villages were contaminated almost immediately. Just as in Northrend, the citizens who contracted the plague died and arose as the Lich King's willing slaves. The cultists under Kel'Thuzad were eager to die and be raised again in their dark lord's service. They exulted in the prospect of immortality through undeath. As the plague spread, more and more feral zombies arose in the northlands. Kel'Thuzad looked upon the Lich King's growing army and named it the Scourge – for soon, it would march upon the gates of Lordaeron and scour humanity from the face of the world.

### The Scourge of Lordaeron
Kel'Thuzad, upon the Lich King's instruction, oversaw the infection of the small town of [Brill](Brill), where he was discovered by [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore) and [Prince Arthas](Prince Arthas).<ref>[Ravages of the Plague (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Ravages_of_the_Plague_(WC3_Human))) From there, he quickly fled, and they followed him back to [Andorhal](Andorhal), where he told Arthas of [Mal'ganis](Mal'ganis) in [Stratholme](Stratholme). Arthas, in a rage, hunted Kel'Thuzad down and killed him. But they were not finished yet. (ref: [The Cult of the Damned (WC3_Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Cult_of_the_Damned_(WC3_Human)))

Kel'Thuzad waited patiently as a ghostly apparition until his protector, Gavinrad the Dire, was slain by a now-corrupted Arthas, and his remains were reclaimed. He appeared only to Arthas, and told him that he was not to trust the [dreadlord](dreadlord)s, promising to explain all of the Lich King's plans to Arthas once he walked the earth again in [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas). (ref: [Digging up the Dead (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Digging_up_the_Dead_(WC3_Undead)))

Kel'Thuzad watched over Arthas as he made the slow procession to the [Sunwell](Sunwell) in [Silvermoon](Silvermoon) against the stratagems of [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner). (ref: [Into the Realm Eternal (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Into_the_Realm_Eternal_(WC3_Undead))) Eventually, Arthas ravaged his way through the two Elfgates (ref: [Key of the Three Moons (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Key_of_the_Three_Moons_(WC3_Undead))) and finally breached Silvermoon itself. With the help of [Tichondrius](Tichondrius), Kel'Thuzad was reborn into the terrifying form of a [lich](lich). (ref: [The Fall of Silvermoon (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fall_of_Silvermoon_(WC3_Undead)))

As they traveled to Alterac, Kel'Thuzad explained that the Scourge was merely the harbinger of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), and that he would summon the first of the [demon](demon)s, the [Eredar](Eredar) warlock, [Archimonde](Archimonde), to the mortal world. (ref: [The Revelation (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Revelation_(WC3_Undead))) After destroying the [orc](orc)s guarding the Demon Gate, Kel'Thuzad communed with the dark lord, who told him that he must go to [Dalaran](Dalaran) and steal the [Book of Medivh](Book of Medivh) from Dalaran. (ref: [Blackrock & Roll, Too! (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Blackrock_%26_Roll,_Too!_(WC3_Undead)))

After Arthas led the attack on Dalaran and killed [Antonidas](Antonidas), the book was retrieved, (ref: [The Siege of Dalaran (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Siege_of_Dalaran_(WC3_Undead))) and Kel'Thuzad began the lengthy summoning process, with Arthas defending him from the many attacks of Dalaran and the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor). Finally, all was complete, and Archimonde was set loose upon the world. His first act was to promote [Tichondrius](Tichondrius) to leader of the Scourge, rendering Arthas and Kel'Thuzad of little use. However, Kel'Thuzad, confident in the Lich King's grand design, disappeared amidst the chaos of Dalaran's destruction. (ref: [Under the Burning Sky (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Under_the_Burning_Sky_(WC3_Undead)))

Kel'Thuzad resurfaced as the Legion raked across the Plaguelands, and in the aftermath of the invasion, he remained in the Capital City as one of the Lich King's lieutenants. Arthas had gone to Kalimdor on Ner'zhul's command to find the rogue demon hunter, Illidan, leaving only Kel'Thuzad and the banshee Sylvanas Windrunner remaining in his stead. However, both knew that the Legion was defeated long before the dreadlords who remained in Lordaeron did. (ref: [King Arthas (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/King_Arthas_(WC3_Undead)))

Arthas returned in a fury and chased the dreadlords out of the capital, and Kel'Thuzad was relieved to see him. With the Legion's generals gone, they turned to the human villages who were escaping to the mountain passes. Kel'Thuzad thought that perhaps they would be a fitting sacrifice to the Lich King and led his necromancers into battle against the paladin Magroth the Defender and his men protecting the human refugees. But as they cut a swath through the human forces, Arthas suffered some violent seizures, and Kel'Thuzad considered recalling their forces, but the King forbade it, and they continued on their macabre mission. In the aftermath, Arthas received a vision from Ner'zhul, who told him to return to Northrend. (ref:  Kel'Thuzad immediately prepared for his departure, but they were abruptly ambushed by the dreadlords, and separated. The dreadlords unleashed their forces against Arthas, but Kel'Thuzad found his own way out of the city.<ref>[The Flight from Lordaeron (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Flight_from_Lordaeron_(WC3_Undead)))

Kel'Thuzad later came upon Arthas in the clutches of Sylvanas and her wicked banshees. She was about to strike when Kel'Thuzad unleashed his forces against her and her sisters. With the banshees dead, Sylvanas was forced to retreat. Kel'Thuzad escorted Arthas to the shore, where he had prepared a fleet of ships for his departure. Arthas asked Kel'Thuzad, as his most loyal servant and trusted friend, to remain in Lordaeron and ensure that his legacy endured. Kel'Thuzad swore on what was left of his life to carry out that mission at any cost. (ref: [Sylvanas' Farewell (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Sylvanas%27_Farewell_(WC3_Undead))) While Sylvanas and her Forsaken claimed the Tirisfal Glades, Kel'Thuzad returned to the Eastern Plaguelands, which he ruled from the flying necropolis Naxxramas. (ref: 

### Shadow of the Necropolis
Kel'Thuzad began to seek out and reform [Atiesh, Greatstaff of the Guardian](Atiesh, Greatstaff of the Guardian) - the very staff once held by [Medivh](Medivh). To this end, he had acquired the [Staff Head of Atiesh](Staff Head of Atiesh) and had already collected several of the staff's fragments - which were being held by his most trusted lieutenants.<ref>[Archmage Angela Dosantos#Quotes](Archmage Angela Dosantos#Quotes))

As the Lich King stirred once more, Kel'Thuzad plotted the [Scourge Invasion](Scourge Invasion) from the flying citadel of Naxxramas, leading to Scourge activity in the Plaguelands increasing ten-fold and attacks being reported as far away as [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). (ref: [Commander Eligor Dawnbringer#Quotes](Commander Eligor Dawnbringer#Quotes)) Kel'Thuzard amassing for another attack led to a coalition between the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn), the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade), the [Cenarion Circle](Cenarion Circle), and the [Earthen Ring](Earthen Ring) to stop him. Ultimately, champions of the Argent Dawn ventured into Naxxramas and vanquished the lich.

After his defeat, [his phylactery](The Phylactery of Kel'Thuzad) was delivered to [Father Inigo Montoy](Father Inigo Montoy) at [Light's Hope Chapel](Light's Hope Chapel).
