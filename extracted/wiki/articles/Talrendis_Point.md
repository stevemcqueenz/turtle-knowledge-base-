-Talrendis Point** is an [Alliance](Alliance) outpost located in southwestern [Azshara](Azshara). It is populated mostly by [night elves](night elves), but also famously houses a [friendly](friendly) [tauren](tauren), [Loh'atu](Loh'atu).

Like [Valormok](Valormok), Talrendis Point is a frontier town and offers only the barest of necessities.
