I think it would be in both of our best interests while you're here rooting around and, bringing justice or however to work together. You see, the Frostmane have a rare tablet I was looking to acquire, and they've been a bit slow in handing it over, it is called the Tablet of Kaz'gan, located somewhere here in these corridors...
