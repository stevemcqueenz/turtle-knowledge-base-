**Mug'thol** was an [ogre](ogre) from [Draenor](Draenor) who fought during the [Second War](Second War) in the orcish [Horde](Horde). When the Horde was defeated and scattered, Mug'thol joined the [Stonemaul](Stonemaul) Ogres under the rule of [Kor'gall](Kor'gall).

Mug'thol led a small group of ogres in the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands), and found himself caught in the middle of a battle between [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner) and the [Dreadlord](Dreadlord) [Varimathras](Varimathras). 

Sylvanas, needing to bolster her forces, raided Mug'thol's camp. Mug'thol warned her that she would fall, but Sylvanas had one of her [banshee](banshee) sisters possess the hapless ogre, who then declared that his allegiance was now to the "pretty ghost."

Later Mug'Thol and his ogre warband was sent to [Alterac](Alterac) to retrieve the [Crown of Will](Crown of Will) for Lady Sylvanas. Unfortunately, instead of returning it, he decided to try it on. The Crown of Will enabled him to resist the possession placed upon him to serve Lady Sylvanas and he defected, establishing himself and his band of ogres in the ruins of Alterac as the [Crushridge](Crushridge) ogres.
