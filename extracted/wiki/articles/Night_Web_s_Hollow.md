# Night Web's Hollow

**Night Web's Hollow** is a small valley and mine just northwest of [Deathknell](Deathknell) in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades). Both the outdoors vale and the indoors mine is infested with [spider](spider)s, which skitter among the shadows and lurk from their webbed corners. Young [Forsaken](Forsaken) usually test their mettle against the numerous spiders and sometimes venture deep into the mine to uncover its dusty secrets.

Outside the mine, [Young Night Web Spider](Young Night Web Spider)s wander about. The mine itself houses many adult [Night Web Spider](Night Web Spider)s. In the deepest recesses of the mine, the [Night Web Matriarch](Night Web Matriarch) sits.
