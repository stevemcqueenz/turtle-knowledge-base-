# Giant

A type of creature or [mob](mob) in the game similar to [humanoids](Humanoid), but of the very large sort (above 3 meters/yards or so) and larger than most [Ogre](Ogre)s. This includes most [semi-intelligent](semi-intelligent) large humanoids and some [intelligent](intelligent). Most giants are [elite](elite).
