# Old Town

-Old Town** is [Stormwind City](Stormwind City)'s most ancient section, and many parts of it still predate the reconstruction of the city and its razing by the orcs during the events of the [First War](First War). It is also the location of the city's most renowned taverns, such as the [Pig and Whistle Tavern](Pig and Whistle Tavern). Crime is also a constant problem in this run-down area. It's a rustic, down-trodden place, but many Stormwind citizens feel it has its own unique charm. It is the residential area for most of the poor folk of the city.

The [warrior](warrior)s and [rogue](rogue)s of Stormwind are trained in the barracks, which include the [Alliance](Alliance) [Command Center](Command Center), the [officer's barracks](Champions' Hall) (that sell all the [PvP](PvP) gear for the alliance), and the headquarters of [SI:7](SI:7), the Stormwind Alliance organization that deals with intelligence. One of the leaders of the [Stormwind Assassins](Stormwind Assassins) also leads SI:7, [Master Mathias Shaw](Master Mathias Shaw).

Some don't know of him, but there is an [NPC](NPC) named [Kendor Kabonka](Kendor Kabonka) upstairs in the "Pig and Whistle", where you can buy plenty of [cooking](cooking) [recipe](recipe)s. Good for those who either didn't train cooking for whatever reason, or for those who haven't been able to find certain recipes.

To get to Old Town, you can head northeast from [the Trade District](the Trade District), south from [Stormwind Keep](Stormwind Keep), or southeast from [the Dwarven District](the Dwarven District).
