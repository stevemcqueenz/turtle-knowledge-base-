# Dreadlord Insurgents

After [Archimonde](Archimonde)'s defeat at the [Battle of Mount Hyjal](Battle of Mount Hyjal), there were several demonic forces still scattered across [Azeroth](Azeroth (world)). Some remained unaware of the defeat of their masters. Many of the dreadlords in [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) in particular did not know of what had unfolded across the sea, despite a span of many months.

[Arthas](Arthas) returned to Lordaeron to claim his throne, but found that the [Scourge](Scourge) was under the dominion of the dreadlord jailors. When Archimonde had been summoned into Azeroth, [Tichondrius](Tichondrius) had asked that the Scourge be given to him, and thus it was the dreadlords who were the new custodians of the undead.

However, without Archimonde, the dreadlords were exposed. Arthas drove them away, for the time being. Unfortunately for him, the [Lich King](Lich King) was growing weaker. Control over the Scourge was lost, for the most part.

The **Dreadlord Insurgents** were the result of the [Lich King](Lich King)'s will weakening, coupled with manipulative power. Most of the undead who were freed were in turn enslaved by the dreadlord custodians who were controlling the [Scourge](Scourge) in Lordaeron. [Balnazzar](Balnazzar), [Detheroc](Detheroc) and [Varimathras](Varimathras) were the leaders of this Insurgency.

The splintering of the Scourge was to free [Sylvanas](Sylvanas), with whom the dreadlords made a pact. With the assistance of Sylvanas, the Dreadlord Insurgents prepared a trap for Arthas. Arthas was ambushed in [Capital City](Capital City) and was separated from his majordomo, [Kel'thuzad](Kel'thuzad). Despite this, he survived the onslaught and Sylvanas' assassination attempts, and sailed to [Northrend](Northrend).

This left a power vacuum in Lordaeron. There were three undead factions at large. One was the Scourge under Kel'thuzad's control. Another was the precursor to the [Forsaken](Forsaken); Sylvanas and her banshees. Finally there were the Dreadlord Insurgents, retaining control over large unholy armies, including the mind controlled soldiers of the [Lordaeron Resistance](Lordaeron Resistance) under [Garithos](Garithos).

Kel'thuzad chose not to interfere and was not seen for the most part of the Scourge Civil War. Sylvanas, on the other hand, was offered a place in the new order that was being forged by the Insurgents. She refused, and Varimathras launched an assault upon her base. Sylvanas improvised, and possessed local creatures using banshees to turn them against the Insurgents. In this manner she swayed [Zul'rogg](Zul'rogg), [Mug'thol](Mug'thol), [Blackthorn](Blackthorn) and possibly the [Murloc Puddle Lord](Murloc Puddle Lord). This gave her several factions of beasts to throw against Varimathras. Eventually, she was successful, and Varimathras joined her in exchange for his life.

The Dreadlord Insurgents still controlled much of Lordaeron, however. Detheroc's pet Garithos was protecting his master within a fortified city. With her banshees at hand, Sylvanas possessed the human gate guards and turned them on one another, allowing her army into the city by cover of night. While the soldiers of the Lordaeron Resistance slept, Sylvanas set about killing her enemies and taking the city. By the time they were alerted to her presence, it was too late, and the city was soon taken and Detheroc torn to bloody shreds.

Ultimately, it was in Capital City that the Dreadlord Insurgency met its end. Sylvanas forged an alliance with the [Lordaeron Resistance](Lordaeron Resistance), promising Garithos that he could have Capital City in exchange for help in killing Balnazzar. Garithos agreed, and despite tensions, Sylvanas and the Resistance stormed Balnazzar's fortifications.
