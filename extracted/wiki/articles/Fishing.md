# Fishing

## Being a fisherman
  -What's good about fishing?** 
- It's a relaxing past-time, a break from adventuring, a great source of food, helps get ingredients for cooking/alchemy, and interesting loot (such as chest). This profession is a great for hunters, providing a cheap supply of pet food.
  -What type or category is Fishing?** 
- Secondary. It does NOT count against your limit of two (2) Primary Professions. Take all three (3) Secondary Professions. 
- Gathering.

## **The act of fishing**
  -How do I fish?** 
- Train in Apprentice Fishing 
- Buy a fishing pole and equip it. 
- Stand next to water and cast with you Fishing Icon. (Found in your skill list ("P") and can be dropped on your action bar)
  -Can I “Autoloot” Fishing?** 
- Yes, if you Shift+Right Click on the bobber, when it "splashes" the item will automatically go into you backpack or bags. No more having to "loot" your pole.

  -I get a message that says my skill isn't high enough to fish.** 
-Certain rivers and lakes are considered higher fishing skills. If you get this message you will have to go back to a lower level zone and catch some fish there first.
  -Why do I keep getting a message, that I can't attack that?** 
-You are hitting the attack button, instead of the fishing icon. Don't feel to bad, I've done it.
  -What does the message, "There was nothing on your hook" mean?** 
-This means that you clicked on the bobber at the wrong time.
  -What does the message, "The fish got away" mean?** 
-This means that clicked on the bobber at the RIGHT TIME, but you don't really have the skill to fish there.
-Go back to a lower level zone or use lures to raise your skill level.

  -Does my fishing skill determine what I can catch?** 

-No. Though your skill determines where you are able to fish. See above.

  -I have found a fish that I can hold in my off-hand. Is there any purpose beyond humor to holding a fish in my off-hand?** 

-Some special fish, such as a 22-pound catfish, can be held in your off-hand. Place them there to brag about your catch to other people. Hit "Z" to sheath/equip weapons til you are showing off your fish. My hunter looks nice with an axe and fish.
  -I caught a bloated fish, what up with this?** 

-Some fish can be bloated. Look at it and it should read 'right click to open.' If you right-click on them you can loot them lick a chest or clam.
  -I found a nice chest, how do I open it?**  

-Rogues that are level 15 and have completed the quest can pick locks. They need to practice to skill up in this, so they should be glad to help you out.
-Blacksmiths can make and use skeleton keys, which are used used up when you unlock the chest.
-The level of skill or type of key required will be dependent on the type of chest.
-If you can find one to do this for you, start a trade and put the item in the do not trade spot.
  -I heard about some one catching the One Ring, what's up with that?** 

-1 Ring is an actual item that can be caught fishing. It is an ultra rare drop that adds +1 many stats and has the description "Not quite as good as the 2 Ring"

  -Why am I not getting any more skill-ups?** 

- You have reached the highest skill points for your Rank. Go train to the next rank.
  -At what skill level do I no longer get fishing skill ups from this zone?**  

-You can skill to 300 in the newbie zone.
  -I am ready to train in Expert, who can train me?** 

- You need to buy the Expert Fishing - The Bass and You (Cost 1 gold) from the Old Man Heming in Stranglethorn Vale (Booty Bay) on the bottom level of the dock underneath the Alliance Wingrider vendor. The book cost 1 gold. Heming is inside Booty Bay, on the southern portion of the docks, on a lower level of docks. Just jump in the water, go up the ramp and he is in the building to the right.
  -What is the quickest way to get to get to Booty Bay at lower levels?** 

-Alliance: Swim south from Westfall till you get to booty bay. Stay away from the shore to avoid getting agro. Once you have bought your book make sure to get the flight path from the griffons at the top of the structure. Now you can either fly home or use your stone.
-Horde: Take the boat from Ratchet or by swimming down from Grom'Gol, which connects by zeppelin with Undercity and Orgrimmar.
  -I am ready to train in Artisan, where is an Artisan Trainer?**   

-You learn Artisan level by completing the following quest.
- Once you have reached level 40 and have 225 in Fishing. (I repeat you need to be at least level 40 and have maxed your skill at 225.)
-Alliance Start: Starts with Grimnur Stonebrand in Ironforge (The Forlorn Cavern)
-Horde Start: Start with Lumak in Orgrimmar (Valley of Honor).
- Both send you to  speak with Nat Pagle in Dustwallow Marsh (Tidefury Cove, south west of Theramore Island). Approach from NW to avoid higher level mobs. From Theramore Island you can swim directly west from the corner of the dock.
-Nat Pagle wants you to four different fish; Feralas Ahi, Savage Coast Blue Sailfin, Sar'theris Striker, & Misty Reed Mahi Mahi.
-Feralas Ahi is a rare drop in Feralas (Verdantis River). Bring some lures for this zone.
-Savage Coast Blue Sailfin is a rare drop in Stranglethorn Vale (The Savage Coast)
-Sar'theris Striker is a rare drop in Desolace (Sar'theris Strand)
-Misty Reed Mahi Mahi. is a rare drop in Swamp of Sorrows (Misty Reed Strand)

## Effects of Time on Fishing
There are 4 separate intervals of fishing times. These intervals are 6am-12pm, 12pm-6pm, 6pm-12am, and 12am-6am. During this time the drop rates of certain fish varies, for example, Sunscale Salmon and Nightfin Snapper. According to experimentation, the cycle appears to be the following:

  -*6am-12pm** - Nightfin and Sunscale
  -*12pm-6pm** - Sunscale only
  -*6pm-12am** - Nightfin and Sunscale
  -*12am-6am** - Nightfin only

Nightfin Snapper has the highest drop rate from 12am-6am, but is never found between 12pm-6pm. On the other hand, Sunscale Salmon is never found between 12am-6am, but drops quite well from 12pm-6pm.
