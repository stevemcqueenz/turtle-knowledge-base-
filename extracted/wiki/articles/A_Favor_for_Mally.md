## Objectives
Gather 3 Vials of Sunscale Blood for Mally O'Flor at Ratchet in the Barrens for the information Thrall seeks. 
- [Vial of Sunscale Blood](Vial of Sunscale Blood) (3)

## Description
So, Thrall sent you huh?

Interesting, I was sent here to replace another, but I never expected to be contacted. The last two before me were never spoken to in regard to anything.

I work for an organization that has deep ties with Stormwind and has its eyes all over the world, so to say.

I will share the information I hold, but I need a favor from you and your kin beforehand. I am here for another purpose, one I need completed in short time, but do not fret, it is within the region.

In the Barrens can be found a very specific type of raptor, Sunscale. Some of our agents have been looking to gather vials of their blood. Three in total for archival purposes.

Gather me those vials, and the information is yours.
