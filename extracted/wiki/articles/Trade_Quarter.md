# Trade Quarter

The **Trade Quarter** is the central area and hub of trade of the [Undercity](Undercity), located just beyond the lifts to the [Ruins of Lordaeron](Ruins of Lordaeron). The center of the Quarter houses the bank, and most of the shops of the Undercity lie around the bank on a series of levels. All other parts of the city can be reached through this quarter via winding passages into the [Canals](Canals).

Facilities:
- An [Innkeeper](Innkeeper)
- A [Bank](Bank)
- A Horde [auction house](Auction House) (outer ring)
- A Bat Handler
- [Cooking](Cooking) trainer
- [Guildmaster](Guildmaster)
