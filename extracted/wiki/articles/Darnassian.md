## Some Darnassian Phrases
These phrases cannot be directly translated or even broken into translatable words, as no lexicon of Darnassian has been made avaliable- the words in the previous section were either directly translated, or could have been confirmed by analysis. While translation of these phrases are guesswork, the general idea of the phrase is easy to guess while listening. 

- *Enshu-falah-nah*, elven goodbye; said by Illidan when he said goodbye to Furion and Tyrande.
- *Asha'falah*, elven goodbye.
- *Anu'dora*, an agreement or confirmation.
- *Az'thero'dalah'dor*, some form of confirmation- used often by Shan'do Stormrage
- *Ana'duna thera*, battle cry.
- *Ashra thoraman?*, often used by Priestesses of the Moon (*Eternity's End*, et al).

## Darnassian Primer
According to [Brann Bronzebeard](Brann Bronzebeard), here are a few of the more common night elven phrases, translated into common;

- *Ande'thoras-ethil.*="May your troubles be diminished." (A farewell.), said by Malfurion when Illidan searched for Tyrande (**The Brothers Stormrage**, *Terror of the Tides* campaign).
- "Andu-falah-dor!" = "Let balance be restored!" (A war cry.)
- "Ash Karath!" Do it!
- "Bandu Thoribas! = "Prepare to fight!" (A challenge to an enemy.)
- "Elune-Adore." = "Elune be with you." (A greeting.)
(*Eternity's End* campaign).
- "Fandu-dath-belore?" = "Who goes there?"
- "Ishnu-alah." = "Good fortune to you" (A greeting.)
(*Eternity's End*, et al).
- "Ishnu-dal-dieb." = "Good fortune to your family." (A greeting.)
(*Eternity's End*, et al).
- "shan'do" = "Honored Teacher" (A title and term of respect.)
- "thero'shan" = "Honored student" (A title and term of respect.)
- "Tor ilisar'thera'nal!" = "Let our enemies beware!" (A war cry.), shouted by Maiev before joining battle with the Naga.

## Darnassian Relatives
It should also be noted that the Night Elves, the High Elves, the Blood Elves and the Naga use similar languages. High elves use a derivative language known as [Thalassian](Thalassian), and Blood Elves use a newly created language, [Sindassi](Sindassi), while Naga use the language, [Nazja](Nazja).  This relationship correlates to the evolution of those races from the Night Elves. Thalassian and Nazja appear to be similar enough to Darnassian; Prince [Kael'thas](Kael'thas) used "Ishnu-alah" in the same usage as the Night Elves, and Lady [Vashj](Vashj) greeted Kael with "Ishnu-dal-dieb" in the same manner that [Maiev](Maiev) did. Moreover, the word "dorei" is clearly in the current language of the elves, as the "Blood Elves" translates to "Sin'dorei." As High Elves live very long lives and the Naga are apparently immortal (barring death in combat) their languages might simply be Darnassian of a slightly different dialect, just as an English-speaker from Britain itself would speak a different dialect from an English-speaker from America or Australia- usage over ten thousand years would change only slightly for speakers of that lifespan.

Note that the term Darnassian may have been only recently applied to the ancient Night Elven tongue, as the city of [Darnassus](Darnassus), after which it is named, was only recently established.  Of course, it may be the city that is named after the dialect, or even that the word "darnass" may mean something.
