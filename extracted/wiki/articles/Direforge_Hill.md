# Direforge Hill

[Direforge Hill](Direforge Hill) is located in northeastern [Wetlands](Wetlands), just southeast of [Dun Modr](Dun Modr). It is typically where one can find the [warlock](warlock) [Balgaras the Foul](Balgaras the Foul) and several other [dark iron dwarves](dark iron dwarves) dispersed into several smallish camps.
