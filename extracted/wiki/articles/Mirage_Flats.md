# Mirage Flats

The **Mirage Flats** is located east of [Dustbelch Grotto](Dustbelch Grotto) in the [Badlands](Badlands). It is populated primarily by [Giant Buzzard](Giant Buzzard)s, [Ridge Stalker Patriarch](Ridge Stalker Patriarch)s, and [Rabid Crag Coyote](Rabid Crag Coyote)s.
