# Southshore Town Hall

The [Southshore Town Hall](Southshore Town Hall) is an important structure to the remants of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), as it, along with the one at [Hillsbrad](Hillsbrad) is the only one to still belong to the [human](human)s, as all the others have fallen to the [Syndicate](Syndicate) or the [undead](undead). [Magistrate Henry Maleb](Magistrate Henry Maleb) is the town's ruler, and holds his office in this building.
