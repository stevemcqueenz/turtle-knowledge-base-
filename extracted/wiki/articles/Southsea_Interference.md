## Objectives
Slay the Southsea Captains located on the Southsea Sandbar for Colonel Hardinus in Caelan's Rest. 
- [Captain Ironhoof](Captain Ironhoof) slain
- [Captain Blackeye](Captain Blackeye) slain
- [Captain Saltbeard](Captain Saltbeard) slain

## Description
With each sash collected, the Southsea are weakened, but not destroyed. Their strength comes from the captains of each ship and crew. A man with great charisma and promises of wealth, fortune, and adventure can easily rile up a group with no future ahead of them.

It is these individuals that we will need to target—the captains of the Southsea Freebooters. You shall find them to the southwest along a dangerous strip of sand called The Southsea Sandbar. Be cautious, it is the basecamp of the pirates on both Gilijim, and Lapidis.

You shall find the captains upon the boats there. Enter each and kill the leaders.

## Rewards
- 6350 [XP](XP)

## Progress
Once the leaders are dead, the pirates will be sent into a state of chaos to establish new leadership.

## Completion
The Admiral would be proud of everything you've done. I have no doubt it will take the Southsea many weeks before someone is their new leader, and even then, they will be very much weaker than they were before. Cheers, the dead of the fleet have been avenged.
