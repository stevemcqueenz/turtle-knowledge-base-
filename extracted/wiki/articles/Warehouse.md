# Warehouse

The **Warehouse** is the fourth area of the [Scarlet Monastery Armory](Scarlet Monastery Armory). It lies within the [Crusader's Armory](Crusader's Armory), and is where [Armory Quartermaster Daghelm](Armory Quartermaster Daghelm) diligently maintains the armory's warehouse and resources.
