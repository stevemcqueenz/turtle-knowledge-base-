# Wraith

A spectral being, or a type of [ghost](ghost). Unlike [banshee](banshee)s who appear to be formed only from the souls of undead women, wraiths can be of either gender. They also appear to be of either elven or human origin most of the time. Wraiths appear as partially see-through, bluish shapes with a roughly humanoid shape, although notably missing legs.
