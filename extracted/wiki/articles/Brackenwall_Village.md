# Brackenwall Village

**Brackenwall Village**, named for its proximity to the [Brackenwall](Brackenwall), is a small [Horde](Horde)-affiliated outpost in northwest [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh). It is run and populated mainly by [ogres](ogres), and its population is approximately a hundred denizens including the wind rider [wyverns](wyverns).

Brackenwall is the oldest Horde presence in Dustwallow Marsh. It is also the home of the [Stonemaul](Stonemaul) ogres, who had maintained a village of their own but were driven out by agents of the [Black Dragonflight](Black Dragonflight). The Stonemaul ogres have remained allied with the Horde since the [Third War](Third War), when the half-ogre [Rexxar](Rexxar) defeated their leader in combat and took command of the tribe. [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore)’s loyalists harass Brackenwall, but [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore) keeps her own troops reined in. Most of the Horde members here try to trade with [Theramore](Theramore) peacefully, but there are a few that organize groups to attack travelers.

Brackenwall is unique in that it is the only Horde town that isn't of the traditional Horde races. The village was built by the [Stonemaul](Stonemaul) [ogres](ogres) after their original village was ruined by the black dragons to the south. The Stonemaul have been allies of the Horde ever since [Rexxar](Rexxar) bested their brutal chieftain, [Kor'gall](Kor'gall), and assumed the title for his own. After the Stonemauls aided in the battle against Admiral Proudmoore's naval fleets, Rexxar handed over leadership of the clan to [Mok'Morokk](Mok'Morokk).
