# Ursan Heights

The **Ursan Heights** is a secluded Furbolg refuge (ref: [New Zones & World Edits](https://web.archive.org/web/20260514170802/https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps). Archived from [the original](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps) on 2026-05-14. Retrieved on 2026-29-08.) located at [Teldrassil](Teldrassil). 

Ursan Heights is located southwest of [the Pools of Arlithrien](Pools of Arlithrien) in [Teldrassil](Teldrassil). 

It's the home of the Ursan Forbolg tribe. (Ursan Furbolg, Ursan Ambusher, Ursan Marauder and Ursan Shaman.)<gallery>
