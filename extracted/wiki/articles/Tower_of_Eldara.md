# Tower of Eldara

The **Tower of Eldara** was once part of the extensive [night elven](night elven) civilization that stretched across most of what is now called [Azshara](Azshara). It is located on the very tip of land northeast of the [Bay of Storms](Bay of Storms), and the sandy beaches near the tower are heavily patrolled by [murloc](murloc)s, [makrura](makrura), and [hydra](hydra)s. The tower itself stands closed and abandoned.
