# Worm

A slithering, segmented beast sometimes with what appears to be a tiny pair of limbs near its face.  Almost all worms are found inside cave-themed instances such as [Maraudon](Maraudon) or [Ragefire Chasm](Ragefire Chasm), but a few types of tiny worms live inside [zombie](zombie)s.  Strangely, some species of worms are skinnable, while others are not.

Giant Dredge Worms can be found in [Silithus](Silithus).
