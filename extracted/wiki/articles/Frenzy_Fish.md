# Frenzy (Fish)

A piranha-like [fish](fish) found mostly in tropical regions. They are *not* caught with the [Fishing](Fishing) profession but actually are [mob](mob)s that reside in the water and may attack you when you are going for a [swim](swim). Depending upon the [zone](zone), their level can range from around 12 to 60 [elite](elite).

[Player](Player)s that win first place in the [Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza](Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza) can recieve a [trinket](trinket), the Hook of the Master Angler ([ThottBot](http://www.thottbot.com/?i=51848)), which allows them to transform into a Frenzy.
