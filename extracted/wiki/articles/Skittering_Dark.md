# Skittering Dark

The ominous sounding **Skittering Dark** is the name for an abandoned mine and the surrounding forest in northwest [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest). It is so named for the numerous spiders which inhabit the mine and its entrance. Among the spiders of the mine is one particularly nasty matriarch called [Krethis Shadowspinner](Krethis Shadowspinner). 

The spiders of the Skittering Dark are valued for their [Skittering Blood](Skittering Blood), which the [Forsaken](Forsaken) [Apothecaries](Apothecaries) seek for their concoctions. Studies have proven that the blood, combined with a grizzled bear heart, forms a deadly elixir.
