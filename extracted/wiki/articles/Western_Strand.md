# Western Strand

The **Western Strand** is a stretch of coastline in the southwest of [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills), complementary to the [Eastern Strand](Eastern Strand) further east. The Western Strand is home to the many [Torn Fin](Torn Fin) [murloc](murloc)s who have built small camps along the shore.
