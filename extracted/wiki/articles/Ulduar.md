# Ulduar

With [Uldum](Uldum) and [Uldaman](Uldaman), there is a third base of the [Titan](Titan)s named **Ulduar**. A fourth location, [Maraudon](Maraudon), is the location of [Terramok](Terramok), an ancient titan vault. A fifth location, a storage facility, is located under [Bael Modan](Bael Modan). Ulduar is located at [The Storm Peaks](The Storm Peaks) of [Northrend](Northrend). It is controlled by [storm giant](storm giant)s, and their minions the [crystal golem](crystal golem)s.
