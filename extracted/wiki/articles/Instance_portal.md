# Instance portal

{| style="background:transparent;"
|-
|

||&nbsp;
|
An **instance portal** is sparkling field that marks the entrance to an [instance](instance).

The color of the portal indicates the type of instance:
- <font color="blue">Blue</font> Instance Portal: [Group](Group) (5 or less)
- <font color="green">Green</font> Instance Portal: [Raid](Raid) (10 or more)
- <font color="red">Red</font> Instance Portal: [PvP](PvP) Battlezone ([battleground](battleground))
