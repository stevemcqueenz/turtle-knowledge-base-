# Darkwhisper Gorge

-Darkwhisper Gorge** 60 75 Winterspring (g/s/c) is an area in the far south of [Winterspring](Winterspring) which consists of a network of tunnels and chasms. This area is inhabited by [elite](elite) [demons](demons) of an approximate five-man difficulty, although easily solable by a level 70.

This treacherous cavern lies along Winterspring Valley's southern slope. Poisonous green vapours bubble out of acid pools deep within the bottom of the gorge. Malevolent sounds echo from a cave at the furthest end of the gorge, leading some night elves to believe that demons may have taken refuge in the toxic depths.

The demons in this area are important to a number of quests, unlike their [Blasted Lands](Blasted Lands) counterparts. The terrain is considerably grim and foreboding - the presence of poisonous green vapors bubbling from acid pools in the bottom of the gorge is warning enough to many travellers to keep out. Despite the hazards, this area possess several valuable resources, including [rich thorium vein](rich thorium vein)s.

Notably, the Hederine demons which inhabit this area drop , a quest item for the [warlock](warlock) quest , the second quest in the chain which leads to the acquisition of the [doomguard](Doomguard (warlock minion)).

Also, the [Hederine Slayers](Hederine Slayers) and the [Hederine Initiate](Hederine Initiate)s have a very small chance (0.5%) to drop , a quest item in the epic [priest](priest) quest to obtain .

An [imp](imp), [Vi'el](Vi'el), lives in a cave in the Gorge. Among other “exotic reagents", he sells several items of particular note:
- (3-5) s=10 (g/s/c)
- (1) s=80 (g/s/c)
- g=40 (g/s/c) (Required for the [Dungeon Set 2](Dungeon Set 2) upgrade quest)
