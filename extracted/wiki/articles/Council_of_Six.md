# Council of Six

The **Council of Six**, (ref: [Khadgar's Journal](Khadgar's Journal)) (ref: [Magistrate Modera#Quotes](Magistrate Modera#Quotes)) also known as the **Six**, (ref: [Archmage Angela Dosantos#Quotes](Archmage Angela Dosantos#Quotes)) is the name given to the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) high council of [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)). These six individuals were the leaders of both the city and all territories under the control of Dalaran. The Six were usually the most senior, but not necessarily the most gifted. They were chosen by several means, magic but one of them. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, chapter 1)

Traditionally, the membership of the Council of Six was kept a strict secret. When someone met with the Council officially, it was always in a special, enchanted chamber, the Chamber of the Air, a room with no visible walls — the gray stone floor with its central diamond symbol stood beneath an open sky that shifted and changed rapidly, as if the time sped past within the chamber.
