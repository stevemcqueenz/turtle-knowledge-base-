# Venture Company Camp

The **Venture Company Camp** is located within a valley in the central mountain range of Stonetalon Mountains. It was the starting area for [goblin](Goblin (playable)) players until the release of [patch 1.17.1](patch 1.17.1), after which it was replaced with [Blackstone Island](Blackstone Island).

Originally ran by a ragged [Venture Company](Venture Company) crew led by Foreman [Nert Blastentom](Nert Blastentom), the crew ran afoul of faulty equipment, night elf environmentalists and cursed ruins in their mission, and deserted to join the Horde, where they formed the [Durotar Labor Union](Durotar Labor Union). The Venture Company have finally arrived to check on Blastentom and his crew's mission, to find no trace of their (now-ex) employees. They've now dug open a way to the valley to find a clue to their whereabouts and to finish the mission that was tasked to them all that time ago: Drain the land of all of its resources.
