# Dreadmist Den

-Dreadmist Den** is a [Burning Blade](Burning Blade) cultist holding located within [Dreadmist Peak](Dreadmist Peak). It contains the [Demon Seed](Demon Seed), an invaluable artifact to the Cult. The den is perpetually shrouded in a dark red mist that taints the peak and cloaks the nefarious demon worshippers who live there in eternal twilight.
