The **Cult of Forgotten Shadow** (ref: [quest: Taming the Beast|class=hunter|race=undead]) (also called the **Church of the Forgotten Shadows**) (ref: [Moriena from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/810850778838990859/1396856368669003827) - "Undead priests use profane teachings of the Church of the Forgotten Shadows and are not technically using the Holy Light") is the twisted faith of the undead priests of the Forsaken, a dark interpretation of the Holy Light tainted by their tortured existences. (ref: [The World of Warcraft Townhall/Classes#The Priest](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_World_of_Warcraft_Townhall/Classes#The_Priest))

Priests of the Forgotten Shadow believe that the faiths they held in life have failed them. They pride themselves on having survived through will alone and believe that faith in themselves is what separates them from others, and that with their powers they will cause great change in Azeroth and become spiritual leaders for the weak and outcast. (ref: [quest: Hallowed Scroll|class=priest|race=undead])

## Notes and trivia
- Unlike followers of the [Church of Light](Church of Light), adepts of the Forgotten Shadow use profane teachings and are not technically using the Holy Light.
- Undead hunters use incantation that was devised by the Cult of Forgotten Shadow to soothe the mind of a beast and alter its perception of the hunter as being vibrant and healthy, as their condition would normally repel beasts away, stricken with terror and distress by what they are.
