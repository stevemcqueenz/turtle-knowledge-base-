# Darkmist Cavern

**Darkmist Cavern** is the name for a small [mine](mine) in northwest [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh) that has become completely overrun with the terrifyingly large [Darkmist Spider](Darkmist Spider)s. The inhabitants of Dustwallow can't seem to agree on whether the spiders were named after the cavern or if it's the other way around. There are ruins of small houses outside the mine, suggesting there might have previously been a small settlement there.

["Swamp Eye" Jarl]("Swamp Eye" Jarl) sends adventurers here in order to collect spider eyes for some soup.
