# Grim Batol Memorial

The **Grim Batol Memorial** is a monument to the fallen [Wildhammer dwarves](Wildhammer dwarves) of [Grim Batol](Grim Batol) erected on a cliff overlooking the [Ruins of Stolgaz Keep](Ruins of Stolgaz Keep) in the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches).
