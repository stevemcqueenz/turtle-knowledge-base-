# Northridge Lumber Camp

A lumbermill operated by the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) in the northern part of the the Western Plaguelands, close to [Hearthglen](Hearthglen).  Both agents of the [Alliance](Alliance) and of the [Horde](Horde) realize the importance of this outpost, and send adventurers out to gather termites to wreak havoc on the Scarlet Crusade's precious lumber supply.
