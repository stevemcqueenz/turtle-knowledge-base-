The **Bilgerats** are a pirate crew from [Plunder Isle](Plunder Isle) that were led by Captain [Barbaros](Barbaros). They met an ill fate when Barbaros led his crew to the accursed island of [Balor](Balor) in search of treasure aboard *[The Rat's Nest](The Rat's Nest)*, having stolen age-old secrets from [Falrevere](Falrevere). Although they established [a compound](Bilgerat Compound) on one of the northern islands of Balor, Captain Barbaros and several of his men were ambushed by orcs of the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) and dragged to [Stormwrought Castle](Stormwrought Castle). (ref: [Logbook of The Rat's Nest](Logbook of The Rat's Nest))

[Second Mate O'Donovan](Second Mate O'Donovan) and [Jade](Jade) returned to the compound, where they planned to return to the castle to free Barbaros and the rest of the party, but by that point Barbaros had already been used to house the soul of [Deathlord Tidebane](Deathlord Tidebane) (ref: [Moriena from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1462715515360378940) - "He's Deathlord Tidebane") and the rest of the captured pirates were experimented on by the [Shadow Council](Shadow Council) and turned into oozes.
