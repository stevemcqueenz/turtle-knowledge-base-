# Foothill Caverns

__NOTOC__

  -The Foothill Caverns** is a cave found in [Darrow Hill](Darrow Hill) in [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills). It is here that the [Flame of Azel](Flame of Azel) and [Flame of Veraz](Flame of Veraz) were located. (ref: [Helcular's Revenge (2)](Helcular's Revenge (2))) The caverns are also home to [Alterac Granite](Alterac Granite).

[Helcular](Helcular) used the cave as his lair to conduct his transformation into a [lich](lich) when a [human](human) lynch mob discovered his ritual lair in the hills and brutally killed him. Afterward, [yeti](yeti)s overtook the lair. (ref: [Helcular's Revenge](Helcular's Revenge))
