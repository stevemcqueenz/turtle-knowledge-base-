# Dawnstone Plans (object)

The **Dawnstone Plans** are blacksmithing plans drafted by the late Gilnean smith [Adaman](Adaman) that are located inside [Adaman Armor](Adaman Armor) in [Gilneas City](Gilneas City). His old friend [Therum Deepforge](Therum Deepforge) asked adventurers to retrieve them so that his findings could be properly recorded in both Stormwind and Ironforge. (ref: [quest: Behind The Wall])

## Objective of
Contains [Dawnstone Plans](Dawnstone Plans) for the quest [quest: Behind The Wall].
