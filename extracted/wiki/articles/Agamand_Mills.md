# Agamand Mills

The **Agamand Mills**, formerly a prosperous farmstead run by the Agamand family, is now the major outpost of the [Scourge](Scourge) in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades).

## Background
When the Scourge attacked Lordaeron, the Agamands decided to hold fast to their home, and ordered their farmhands to remain on the property to defend it. This effort proved to be in vain; the Agamands and their farmhands were massacred by the Scourge, and most of them arisen in their service...however, a select few have broken away, and become Forsaken instead.

## Monsters & Related NPC's
- [Devlin Agamand](Devlin Agamand) - The youngest of the siblings and the first to fall under the influence of the Scourge, it was Devlin who first allowed the undead access to the Mills. Because of his betrayal, Devlin was outcast by his siblings and now wanders the outskirts of the Mills.
- [Thurman Agamand](Thurman Agamand) - Devlin's brother.
- [Gregor Agamand](Gregor Agamand) - Devlin and Thurman's father.
- [Nissa Agamand](Nissa Agamand) - Gregor's wife.
- [Captain Dargol](Captain Dargol) - An agent of the Scourge who guards the [Agamand Family Crypt](Agamand Family Crypt).
- [Coleman Farthing](Coleman Farthing) - A former farmhand for the Agamands, Coleman is bitter toward them all and wishes revenge for forcing him and the other employees to stand guard on the property only to be slaughtered and resurrected as undead minions.
- [Yvette Farthing](Yvette Farthing) - The sister of Coleman Farthing who now resides in the [Gallow's End Tavern](Gallow's End Tavern) in [Brill](Brill) with her brother. She shared a secret romance with Thurman Agamand, who is now lost to the Scourge.
