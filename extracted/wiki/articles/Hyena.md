# Hyena

Hyenas are large [dog](dog)-like [beast](beast)s found in the most barren areas of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor).  They are most dangerous when travelling in packs.  Hyenas often have an ability to inflict dangerous diseases on those who fight them.  [Centaur](Centaur) [hunter](hunter)s in the [Barrens](Barrens) and [Desolace](Desolace) often keep them as [pet](pet)s.  Hyenas are [skinnable](skinnable).  Found in [The Barrens](The Barrens), [Desolace](Desolace), [Tanaris](Tanaris), the [Blasted Lands](Blasted Lands), the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery), the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands), and [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles)

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Have medium HP, medium armor, and medium damage.
- Eats only fruit and meat.
- Can learn [Bite](Bite), [Dash](Dash), [Growl](Growl), and [Cower](Cower).  They have yet to recieve their species skill.
- [Ravage](Ravage (Hyena)) (1.3 attack speed) and Scarlet Tracking Hound (1.5 attack speed) are good choices for a pet.
- Typically not very popular as a hunter pet.
