# Wildmane Water Well

The **Wildmane Water Well** is located just north of [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff).43 13 Mulgore (g/s/c) It is the northernmost of the tauren people's sacred water wells. This well had been taken over by the [Venture Company](Venture Company) in order to control the water supply but were run off by the tauren
