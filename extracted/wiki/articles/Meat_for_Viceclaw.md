## Objectives
Bring 10 chunks of Turtle Meat to Ekka at Sandmoon Village in Tanaris.
- [Turtle Meat](Turtle Meat) (10)

## Description
Ya be the one who be pokin' in our village! Ya better get yourself useful or else Ekka feed ya to da Viceclaw!

Viceclaw be likin' good, tough meat. Da turtles from da shore be his favorite. Ekka can't go dere, da goblins would shoot her to death! Bring Viceclaw da meat from dem, and as a reward he will spare you!

<Ekka laughs maniacally>

Good Viceclaw, good.

## Rewards
You will receive:
- 3000 [XP](XP)

## Progress
You can find da turtles on the shore, next to da port.

## Completion
Good, turtle meat for Viceclaw! Ya be useful outsider. Ya can stay, Ekka be likin' ya.

## Notes
**Upon completion**
   **Ekka says:** Viceclaw my dear. Da feast today!
   Viceclaw: <cackles>
   **Ekka says:** Good boy.
