# Ironforge Airfields

The **Ironforge Airfields** is an airfield located in a high mountain valley northwest of the [dwarven](dwarf) capital of [Ironforge](Ironforge). It has two landing strips, and several gnome pilots are there standing next to their planes or tinkering with them. Around the airfield, there are a series of dwarven buildings and structures. Mountaineers from Ironforge patrol the area, and some members of the [Stormpike Guard](Stormpike Guard) can be found here too. Further north, near the [Wetlands](Wetlands), there is a crashed gnome plane along with some bones.
