# Mountain giant

These monstrous creatures, crafted by the [Titan](Titan)s when the world was young, are composed of living stone and stout flora. Benevolent and solitary by nature, the mountain giants have awakened to find that the tranquil world they once helped to shape has become a hectic battlefield of fire and sorrow. Now the mountain giants have pledged their courage and strength to the [night elves](night elves)' cause -- and stand ready to banish the last remnants of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) from the world. These massive melee units that can take incredible amounts of punishment from attackers.

Mountain Giants measure a towering 20 feet tall and weigh nearly 12,000 pounds. Their natural life spans measure into the thousands of years.
There are many different kinds, made of stone, ice, earth and even lava.

The Mountain Giants of stone have northwestern Feralas as their main area, where they go under the names of **Cliff Giant** and **Land Walker**.

The ancient cliff giants of the southern region of [Azshara](Azshara) consume the rock and buildings of the land for sustenance. The excrement from their consumption is a highly malleable and extremely strong crystal known as [Azsharite](Azsharite).
The formations litter the southern arc, but few are ever recovered due to the extremely violent nature of the giants.
Many heroes recover enough of the Azsharite for the creation of powerful weapon's body.

Despite being creations of the Titans, some Mountain Giants, like [Golemagg The Incinerator](Golemagg The Incinerator) and [Baron Kazum](Baron Kazum) serve the [Elemental Lieutenants](Elemental Lieutenants) of the [Old Gods](Old Gods).
## The Third War
While they didn't participate in the third war, they did stand by their Night Elf allies in the aftermath.
