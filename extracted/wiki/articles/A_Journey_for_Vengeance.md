## Description
Stonetalon.

The name brings anger to my core, this is the place my brother died in his fight during the Third War.

While I served in Ironforge he sought glory in these lands, but he did not find what he sought. His tale deserves to be heard, when you are ready, speak with me, and I will recount it.
