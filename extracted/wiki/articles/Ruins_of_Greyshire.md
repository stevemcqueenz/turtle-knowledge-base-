# Ruins of Greyshire

The **Ruins of Greyshire** are what remains of the town of **Greyshire**, located in [Gilneas](Gilneas). The town was ravaged and destroyed when a Scourge warhost led by the lich [Naze the Eternal](Naze the Eternal) broke through the [Greymane Wall](Greymane Wall) at what is now known as [Oldrock Pass](Oldrock Pass). The Scourge were soundly defeated by the Royal Army and pushed out of Gilneas, but Greyshire was destroyed in the attack. (ref: [quest: Scouring Greyshire]) The town, as well as the neighboring [Westgate Tower](Westgate Tower), are now infested with the lingering undead remnants. The only remaining inhabitants are located at the [Shademore Tavern](Shademore Tavern), located on the southern end of the village.
