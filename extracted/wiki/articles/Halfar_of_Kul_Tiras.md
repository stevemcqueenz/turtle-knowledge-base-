Sir **Halfar of Kul Tiras** is a human located at the [Jousting Grounds](Jousting Grounds) in [Northwind](Northwind). One of the participants in the Northwind jousting tournament representing the [kingdom of Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)), he faces off against [the Black Knight](the Black Knight).
