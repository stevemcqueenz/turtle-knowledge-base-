# Crypt (Northwind)

The [Northwind](Northwind) **Crypt** is located within the [Abbey Gardens](Abbey Gardens). Unlike the rest of the Abbey grounds, it is not infested with undead from the burnt church, but it is inhabited by the [Forsaken](Forsaken) [Triston Lancaster](Triston Lancaster) and the skeleton [Count Femur](Count Femur).
