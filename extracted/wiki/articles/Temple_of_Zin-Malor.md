# Temple of Zin-Malor

-Temple of Zin-Malor** (aka **Elune's temple**) is an ancient building situated in the [Ruins of Eldarath](Ruins of Eldarath). It was once a prominent [night elven](night elven) temple devoted to the worship of their goddess, [Elune](Elune). Its similarity to the nearby [Temple of Arkkoran](Temple of Arkkoran) is striking, as they both have a very similar design to the current [Temple of the Moon](Temple of the Moon) in [Darnassus](Darnassus). The temple is currently occupied by the [Spitelash](Spitelash) tribe of [naga](naga) under the leadership of [Warlord Krellian](Warlord Krellian), who himself inhabits the temple in a place of honor.
