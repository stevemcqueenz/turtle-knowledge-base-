# Klaven's Tower

**Klaven's Tower**, named after its rogue [Defias](Defias) master [Klaven Mortwake](Klaven Mortwake), lies at the southeastern edge of [Westfall](Westfall) near the river and north of the [Dagger Hills](Dagger Hills). [SI:7](SI:7) has been scouting this tower for some time and sometimes seeks help from adventurous rogues to sneak in and gather intelligence. It is a well known launching point for [Defias](Defias) activity such that it has also been called the "Defias Tower". Klaven's Tower is used, along with [Jerod's Landing](Jerod's Landing) in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), in the smuggling of stolen goods from [Redridge](Redridge). (ref: [quest: class=rogue])
