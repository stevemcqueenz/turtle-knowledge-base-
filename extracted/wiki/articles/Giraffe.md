# Giraffe

Giraffes are [neutral](neutral) [beast](beast)s that are common in the [Barrens](Barrens) in central [Kalimdor](Kalimdor).

There are two types of giraffe. Barrens Giraffe, and Wandering Barrens Giraffe. The Barrens Giraffes are generally lower in level (11-16) than the wandering variety (16-19). Wandering Giraffes are mostly found close to [Camp Taurajo](Camp Taurajo). The lower variety are found a bit farther North. 

## Traits
- Giraffe corpses are [skinnable](skinnable).
