# Chimaera Roost Vale

**Chimaera Roost Vale** is a valley hidden south of the [Lower Wilds](Lower Wilds) in [Feralas](Feralas). Once a stronghold of the [night elves](Night elf), (ref: [Velos Sharpstrike#Quotes](Velos Sharpstrike#Quotes)) the vale has since fallen into ruin, almost completely abandoned save for the [Venomlash](Venomlash Chimaera) and [Dunebound Chimaera](Dunebound Chimaera) that roam the area.

[Velos Sharpstrike](Velos Sharpstrike), together with his chimaera companion [Voltra](Voltra) and apprentice [Lataro Swifthoof](Lataro Swifthoof), have recently re-settled in the vale, hoping to restore the night elven stronghold to its former glory.

## History
Millennia ago, the night elves entered into an agreement with the wild chimaera, who agreed to fly the elves into combat in times of war. One figure, known as [Chimaeran](Chimaeran), was gifted with training and riding the chimaera with unparalleled grace.  (ref: [quest: Harness of Chimaeran]) After entering the pact with the chimaera, the night elves built a fortified roost in the vale to grow and train chimaera for riding, and the stronghold became a vital point of defense for the elves. (ref: 

At some point in time, a brutal war between the night elves and the [Maraudine](Maraudine) centaur occurred, wherein the centaur stole Chimaeran’s legendary harness and hid it away in Maraudon.

Though the practice of constructing chimaera roosts was still in use up to at least the Third War,<ref>Chimaera roosts from Warcraft 3.) Chimaera Roost Vale was eventually abandoned after the war. ### Mysteries of Azeroth
Velos Sharpstrike has reclaimed the vale alongside the chimaera Voltra and tauren Lataro Swifthoof in an effort to revive the roost and train more chimaera. He directs adventurers to retrieve Chimaeran’s long-lost harness to preserve the chimaera-rider’s legacy.
