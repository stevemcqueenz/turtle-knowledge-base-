# Elemental

The Elementals are/were the minions of the [Old Gods](Old Gods), commanded by 4 Elemental Lieutenants: [Ragnaros the Firelord](Ragnaros), [Therazane the Stonemother](Therazane), [Al'Akir the Windlord](Al'Akir), and [Neptulon the Tidehunter](Neptulon). When the Old Gods were defeated and imprisoned by the [Titan](Titan)s, the Titans banished all elementals to the [Elemental Plane](Elemental Plane). Over the centuries, they have been finding ways to return to Azeroth, and now run rampant in many areas, though it's usually only the weakest of their ranks that are able to slip through the "cracks". [Ragnaros](Ragnaros) has found a way to project his being into the [Molten Core](Molten Core), though he is but a shadow of his true self there.

Some intelligent creatures of Azeroth have found ways to summon elementals and bind them to their will. [Human](Human) [mage](mage)s are fond of summoning Water Elementals for their brute force and imposing size.

The 4 lieutenants, seemingly no longer under the Old Gods' control, are finding new ways to wage war against each other, and it's possible that one or more of their factions are working for the good of Azeroth. Though they fight each other, they still maintain an [Abyssal High Council](Abyssal High Council).

The elementals seem to have a complex royal power hierarchy. The most powerful elemental beings have titles such as Baron, Lord, and Duke. The closest, most powerful offspring of the Elemental Lieutenants are even known as Princes and Princesses.

  - *

The elementals are potent and volatile beings of pure elemental energies. Elementals were the beings that once served the [Old Gods](Old Gods) when they ruled [Azeroth](Azeroth). After the fell of the Old Gods, the elementals were banished into the [Elemental Plane](Elemental Plane). They are often used by [mages](mages), and other powerful beings as servants and guards. In order to exist on the physical plane, they must be bound by specially enchanted bracers that keeps them under control and intact. If the magic of the bracers is disrupted, usually by prolonged physical or magical attack, they immediately dissipate. There are four types of elementals, each corresponding to the basic elements of the world: earth, fire, wind and water. Each elemental tends to be immune to damage from their element type. Fire elementals for instance, are immune to fire damage.

All but the most powerful elementals are mindless beings, who carry out tasks at the whim of their masters. Recently however, more and more elementals have begun appearing on Azeroth, serving an as yet unidentified purpose.

# Air Elemental
Elemental creatures made of wind, dust, storms, and weather.

- Attacks are frequently nature-based, and are usually immune or strongly resistant to Nature magic.  They also tend to attack faster than other elementals.
- Their home/domain on the [Elemental Plane](Elemental Plane) is known as [The Skywall](Skywall).
- The leader of the wind elementals is [Al'Akir](Al'Akir)
- The most powerful Air Elemental currently in World of Warcraft is Prince Thunderaan.

Noteworthy Air Elementals

- [Prince Thunderaan](Thunderaan)
- [Huricanian](Huricanian)
- [The Windreaver](Elemental Invasions)
- [Cyclonian](Cyclonian)

# Earth Elemental
Elemental creatures made of the rock, dust, and energy of the earth itself.
- Some earth elemental attacks do nature damage, but most are physical.  Earth Elementals also have the highest armor rating of the elementals.
- The leader of the earth elementals is [Therazane](Therazane)
- Their home/domain on the [Elemental Plane](Elemental Plane) is known as [Deephome](Deephome).
- The most powerful Earth Elemental currently in World of Warcraft is Garr.

Noteworthy Earth Elementals
- [Lokholar the Ice Lord](Lokholar the Ice Lord)
- [Myzrael](Myzrael)
- [Avalanchion](Elemental Invasions)
- [Lord Roccor](Lord Roccor)
- [Smoldar](Smoldar)
- [Garr](Garr)
- [Princess Theradras](Theradras)

# Fire Elemental
Elemental creatures made of fire, lava, magma rock, and other burning and molten elements.
- The leader of the fire elementals is [Ragnaros](Ragnaros)
- Fire elementals mainly do fire damage.
- Their home/domain on the [Elemental Plane](Elemental Plane) is known as [The Firelands](Firelands).

# Water Elemental
Elemental creatures made of water, ice, and various other liquids.  Some water elementals have become corrupted, appearing green instead of blue.
- The leader of the water elementals is [Neptulon](Neptulon)
- [Duke Hydraxis](Duke Hydraxis) is a noteable water elemental, who gives you several quests to kill Fire and Earth elementals.
- Water elementals commonly do frost or nature damage.
- Their home/domain on the [Elemental Plane](Elemental Plane) is known as [The Abyssal Maw](Abyssal Maw).

# Magic Elemental
Magic-based Elementals are not true Elementals, as are [Fire](:Category:Fire Elemental), [Earth](:Category:Earth Elemental), [Air](:Category:Air Elemental), and [Water](:Category:Water Elemental) elementals; they were never a part of the [Old Gods](Old Gods)' armies. They exist in Azeroth as physical manifestations of pure arcane magic, or even of [mana](mana) energy itself. Sometimes, they can also be Shadow-based, as is the case with [Voidwalker](Voidwalker)s.
- Magic elementals commonly do arcane damage or Shadow, depending on their base form.
