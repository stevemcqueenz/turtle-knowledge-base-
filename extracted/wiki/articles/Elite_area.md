# Elite area

**Elite areas** (aka **non-instanced dungeons**, **micro dungeons**, and **half dungeons**) are areas in a non-instanced zone full of [elite creature](elite creature)s. These usually require a [group](group) to complete and are often the focus of group [quests](quests).

With the current rarity of elite mobs, the terms mentioned above could be used to refer to any non-instanced questing area that is separated from its surroundings (caves, crypts, buildings, etc).

Current non-instanced dungeons include, by zone:
