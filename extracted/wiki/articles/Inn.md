# Inn

An **inn** is generally any establishment with an [innkeeper](innkeeper) that may serve as a binding point for [:hearthstone](:hearthstone)s. In a broader sense, inns often contain many more defining amenities including [drink](Drink vendors) and [food](food) [vendor](vendor)s, [stable master](stable master)s, [mailbox](mailbox)es, [trainer](trainer)s, and [quest giver](quest giver)s, among others.
