*This article is about the character biography. For the boss tactics, see [Zuluhed the Whacked (tactics)](Zuluhed the Whacked (tactics)).*

  -Zuluhed the Whacked** is the chieftain of the [Dragonmaw clan](Dragonmaw clan). Though he was one of the few remaining orc shamans within the [Horde](Horde) in Azeroth, he did not embrace his true heritage and still practiced the dark magics prescribed by [Gul'dan](Gul'dan) and the [Shadow Council](Shadow Council). He and his clan were some of [Blackhand](Blackhand)'s strongest supporters during his reign as Warchief, and after his demise he honored his clan's fealty to Blackhand by pledging it into the service of the [Black Tooth Grin clan](Black Tooth Grin clan) led by his sons, [Rend](Rend Blackhand) and [Maim Blackhand](Maim Blackhand). (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Clans of the Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Dragonmaw_Clan)*, Dragonmaw Clan)

Through unknown means, Zuluhed came into possession of what he called the [Demon Soul](Demon Soul), and it is through its power that Rend and Maim masterminded the capture of the Dragon Queen, [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza). (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Air Units of the Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Air_Units_of_the_Horde)*) He entrusted the Demon Soul to [Nekros Skullcrusher](Nekros Skullcrusher), one of the few Warlocks who had survived [Doomhammer](Orgrim Doomhammer)'s purge. Meanwhile, Alexstrasza, threatened with the death of her children, unwillingly gave the Horde an endless supply of her deadly progeny. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*)

When the war turned for the worse, all of Dragonmaw collected at Grim Batol, making sure that no human force could rescue the Dragon Queen. However, when Deathwing, Alextrasza's hated enemy, made a daring attempt to assassinate her, he unwittingly freed the Dragon Queen. Nekros, along with much of the Dragonmaw clan, were destroyed by Alexstrasza after she was freed. (ref: 

After the survivors of the Dragonmaw clan were rounded up by the Alliance and ushered into the internment camps following the battle of Grim Batol, Zuluhed disappeared in search of the remnants of the Demon Soul. He returned only when he managed to reclaim a shard of the Demon Soul from the clutches of the Red dragonflight,<ref>[THE NEXT PATCH FOR CLASSIC+ | Turtle WoW Patch 1.18.0: Scars of the Past](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5gy_3Dy5nXE)) claiming that it had not been destroyed by the wizard [Rhonin](Rhonin). From the [Dragonmaw Retreat](Dragonmaw Retreat), Zuluhed rallied his clan, who had been freed by [Thrall](Thrall) during the liberation of the orcs, (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, *[Orcish History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Day_of_the_Dragon)*, Day of the Dragon) (ref: [The Battle of Grim Batol](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Battle_of_Grim_Batol_(History_of_Warcraft))) against all those who opposed them (ref: [NEW CLASSIC+ DUNGEON: Dragonmaw Retreat — Turtle WoW Patch 1.18 Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V0ql8IneiCk)) under the banner of the [Dark Horde](Dark Horde), led by Rend. However, the Dragonmaw was not so unified, and prospective leaders such as [Korlag Doomsong](Korlag Doomsong) refused to answer Zuluhed's call due to him abandoning his clan to find the Demon Soul while the Dragonmaw fought tooth and nail for survival. (ref:  [35D] [Letter from Korlag Doomsong](Letter from Korlag Doomsong))
