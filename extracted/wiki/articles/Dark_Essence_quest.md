## Description
There is one final material that I require before our tasks is complete. To the south of the region lays a place called The Grim Hollow. It is a desolate, and corrupted land that has been twisted by a dark and unknown power.

This power is what I seek, and it can be claimed by the spirits of dwarves that now haunt the hollow itself. Slay them, and return to me a Dark Essence from their remains.
