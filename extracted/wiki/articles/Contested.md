# Contested

A **contested territory** is any zone on a [PvP server](PvP server) where players' PvP flags are immediately turned on upon entering. Any [player](player) can be attacked by a player of an opposing faction while in these zones. Some contested territories are [faction](faction)-affiliated (for example, some will only feature [Alliance](Alliance) cities and NPC, while other will do same for the [Horde](Horde)), though a big number of contested zone either favor both or none of them.

About two thirds of all zones accessible by all players are considered contested. From that, about half the zones are faction-affiliated. Most notably contested zones include [Ashenvale](Ashenvale) in [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) and [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands) in [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms). Generally, once a player on a PvP server reaches level 20, they will be prone to start [quest](quest)ing in contested territories. Above level 30, all worthy quests will only lead there.

[Neutral](Neutral) cities may be located within these zones where Alliance and Horde coexist, such as [Gadgetzan](Gadgetzan) and [Booty Bay](Booty Bay). Trying to do [PvP](PvP) while near a guard in a neutral city will result in the guard becoming hostile to whoever started the fight.  Both Alliance and Horde must behave while within earshot of a guard, as they'll do a little more than ask you to take it outside. Even indirect participation in a fight (i.e. healing one of the combatants) will flag the healer for PvP and bring the guards down on the player.

[Battleground](Battleground)s are also contested territories, regardless of the realm type you are playing on.
