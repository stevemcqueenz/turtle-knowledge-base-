# Private Hendel

]]
[Private Hendel](Private Hendel) is a level 35 [quest giver](quest giver) located just southwest of [North Point Tower](North Point Tower) in the [contested](contested) territory of [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh).

He starts the quest  [38] [The Missing Diplomat](Quest:The Missing Diplomat).

See [List of Dustwallow Marsh NPCs](List of Dustwallow Marsh NPCs).

## Killing Private Hendel
At the end of [The Missing Diplomat](The Missing Diplomat) quest chain, you must kill Private Hendel. He is guarded by two level 35 accomplices. Immediately after you accept Hendel's challenge to fight, his accomplices will attack you. There is a trick to killing Hendel and his accomplices: immediately after accepting Hendel's challenge run to the edge of the entrance ramp to nearby North Point tower. It's only a few steps away. Hendel's accomplices will follow you to the edge of the tower -- make sure you don't outrun them. When you get to the entrance ramp of the tower, guards from North Point Tower will come down the ramp to assist you. Once they've taken care of the accomplices, you can go back for Hendel.

If you fail to kill Hendel, it will at first appear that you're not given a second chance to do so. Right-clicking on Hendel no longer triggers his challenge. To fix this, abandon The Missing Diplomat quest, then right-click on Hendel to re-create the quest.
