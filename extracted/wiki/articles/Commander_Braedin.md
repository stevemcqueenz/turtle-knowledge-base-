**Commander Braedin** is a [high elf](high elf) quest giver located at the [Silver Covenant Camp](Silver Covenant Camp) in the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands). He is [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner)'s right hand man in the [Silver Covenant](Silver Covenant) and was placed in charge of cleansing the lands of any perils that could endanger the refugees seeking sanctuary (ref: [quest: Wyrms of the Highlands]) and keeping watch over the blood elves' mountain stronghold of [Felstrider Retreat](Felstrider Retreat). (ref: [quest: Felstrider Retreat])

Despite his young age, Braedin carried himself with the calm weight of command and remained loyal to Vereesa through every loss and slight that the Silver Covenant endured. When [Shalandis Isle](Shalandis Isle) was successfully taken by the Silver Covenant, he announced to Vereesa that the [Regency Council](Regency Council) awaited her in the main pavilion on the island. (ref: *[A Good Day](A Good Day)*)
