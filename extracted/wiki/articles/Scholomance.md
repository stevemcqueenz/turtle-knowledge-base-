# History
The Scholomance was founded by the rogue wizard [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) nearly ten years ago. His [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned) renovated the ancient crypts beneath Caer Darrow (an old Arathorian fortress) and turned them into a school for necromancy. The Cult used this place to train its mortal agents and also prepare to unleash the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath) upon [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). It has been brewing with young talented wizards and adepts all this time. No one in Lordaeron knew. Originally, the Barov family (of [Alterac](Alterac Mountains)) owned the land and gave it to the Cult in exchange for the [Lich King](Lich King)'s blessing of immortality. Now the Barovs remain as undead monsters. One of the Kel'Thuzad's mightiest agents, the Ravenian, also lives within the recesses of the school---ever ready to devour those students who show any sign of weakness. The Lich Ras Frostwhisper rules over the site and guards it in the Scourge's name, but the mortal necromancer, Darkmaster Gandling, serves as headmaster of the school.

Recently, the Scholomance was invaded and shut down by groups of intrepid adventurers from both the [Alliance](Alliance) and [Horde](Horde). With the devastation of the primary training facility for [necromancer](necromancer)s of the [Scourge](Scourge), it is likely that the forces of [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) in [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) have suffered yet another extremely damaging blow to their ironclad grip on the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands). Only time will tell whether the Scourge can recover from such a setback.

  *The Scholomance is housed within a series of crypts that lie beneath the ruined keep of [Caer Darrow](Caer Darrow). Once owned by the noble [Barov](Barov) family, Caer Darrow fell to ruin following the [Second War](Second War). As the wizard [Kel'thuzad](Kel'thuzad) enlisted followers for his [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned) he would often promise immortality in exchange for serving his [Lich King](Lich King). The Barov family fell to Kel'thuzad's charismatic influence and donated the keep and its crypts to the [Scourge](Scourge). The cultists then killed the Barovs and turned the ancient crypts into a school for necromancy known as the Scholomance. Though Kel'thuzad no longer resides in the crypts, devoted cultists and instructors still remain. The powerful lich, [Ras Frostwhisper](Ras Frostwhisper), rules over the site and guards it in the Scourge's name - while the mortal necromancer, Darkmaster Gandling, serves as the school's insidious headmaster.*
