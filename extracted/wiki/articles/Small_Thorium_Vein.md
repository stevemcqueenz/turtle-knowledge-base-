# Small Thorium Vein

-Small Thorium Veins** can be found primarily in zones that are in the 50–60 level range.

## Contents
With [Mining](Mining) (245), these veins can be looted for:

- [Thorium Ore](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Thorium_Ore)
- [Dense Stone](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Dense_Stone) (uncommon)
- [Azerothian Diamond](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Azerothian_Diamond) (very rare)
- [Black Vitriol](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Vitriol) (very rare)
- [Blue Sapphire](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Sapphire) (very rare)
- [Huge Emerald](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Huge_Emerald) (very rare)
- [Large Opal](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Large_Opal) (very rare)
- [Star Ruby](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Ruby) (very rare)
