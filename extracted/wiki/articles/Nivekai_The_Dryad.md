# Nivekai The Dryad

## Nivekai The Dryad
Nivekai is an odd creature. 

While many of the other Dryad prefer to keep to the forests in Kalimdor, Nivekai loves to travel. From the shores of Darkshore to the stone walls of Ironforge, Nivekai will appear anywhere and everywhere- even places one would find difficult to imagine a Dryad being.

Where she appears, one will often find music or poetry about valiant souls or epic heroics. A Bard by trade Nivakai seeks any and all stories to share among her and her friends.

#### **Abilities**
Nivekai is naturally drawn to nature magic and has proven to be a natural shapeshifter that takes on rather unorthodox forms. she has  been witnessed turning into both a Harpy and a Kaldorei, on-top of shifting into an Orca when she wishes to swim. 

As a Druid, Nivekai has access to many healing techniques and often prefers healing to harming people. Though when pressed she will not hesitate to - in her words- 'Maul Someone'

  -Limitations the Author has put on the character in RP**

  -*Dreamwalk***: Dryad according to their wiki can slip into the dream at will and use it as a means to teleport anywhere. I found this to OP to use in casual RP combat, and to easily abuseable.  I have written a short story based on another RP where Nive was banned from using the dream for such things.

  -*Magic Immunity/Easily corrupted*** : Dryad have magic immunity but we often see Dryad shaped by their environments. I have decided that there are just locations icly that Nive cannot go, and certain people or magics Nive cannot fight. She may be immune to most magic, but she is far easier to corrupt by other means. (E.G. forcing her to drink demons blood, or if she spent to long in a place like Mauradon)--- PS, Enchanted weapons dont count, you could totally stab Nive with a flaming knife and it would hurt her just like everyone els.

##### Dryad Racials from the WoW RPG
- +2 spirit/wisdom
- Quadruped: +4 against trip checks, can carry 1 1/2 more than other medium bipeds 
- Base land speed: 40
- Medium creature
- low light vision: Starlight, torchlight, moonlight, a Dryad can see twice as far as a human and retain the ability to discern detail and color
- Fey: Cannot be targeted by spells or effects that are designated to effect humanoids such as 'hold person'
- Immortal: does not age, cannot die of old age. Still can be killed by normal means such as a sword or bow.
- Tough Hide: +1 ac
- inhuman frame: Armor costs more to make for this character

  -Dryad creature class features**

- Spell resistance: Dryad of 8th level or below have spell resistance
- Magic Immunity: At 9th level, A Dryad becomes immune to all spell or spell like effects that allows spell resistance
- Dispel Magic: At 3rd level a Dryad gains the ability to cast Dispel Magic at will. This can only be done (dryad level +1) times a day.
- Poison: At 4th level a Dryad's weapon is coated in poison that weakens a foes Str by 1d2
