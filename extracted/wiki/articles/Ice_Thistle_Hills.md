# Ice Thistle Hills

The **Ice Thistle Hills** is a large area just southeast of [Everlook](Everlook). It is the home of the [Ice Thistle](Ice Thistle) [yeti](yeti), who live in and around a cave some way up in the hill, and in the surrounding area. Adventurers often come here to find [Thick Yeti Fur](Thick Yeti Fur)s and [Pristine Yeti Horn](Pristine Yeti Horn)s for their quests.

This area is a popular place to farm leather, as the yeti also drop money and "humanoid" drops (excepting cloth) such as poor quality armor. One can also find [Icecap](Icecap)s around the outdoor area, [Thorium](Thorium) veins inside the cave, and, if one is very lucky, a [Black Lotus](Black Lotus) at the lip of the cave.
