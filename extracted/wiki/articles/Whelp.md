# Whelp

A very young [dragon](dragon), often recently hatched.  In most cases, if someone finds a whelp, there will often be a larger [drake](drake) or even a fully grown [dragon](dragon) nearby.

Whelps can be found in:
- [Badlands](Badlands) (Black)
- [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire) (Black)
- [Blackwing Lair](Blackwing Lair) (Black, Blue, Bronze, Green, Red)
- [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes) (Black)
- [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh) (Black)
- [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains) (Black)
- [Scholomance](Scholomance) (Diseased)
- [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows) (Green)
- [Wetlands](Wetlands) (Red)
- [Winterspring](Winterspring) (Blue)
