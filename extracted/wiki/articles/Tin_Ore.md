# Tin Ore

**Tin Ore** is the second-easiest [ore](ore) to [mine](mining). It is mined from [Tin Vein](Tin Vein)s. You need 65 [mining](mining) skill to mine it. It's mostly used to create the metal alloy [Bronze](Bronze Bar).
