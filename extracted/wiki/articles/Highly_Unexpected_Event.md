## Description
It's done! Quite boring little thing but you know gnomes, blah, I hate gnomes. No refinement when it comes to these things, just look at this thing, the perfect way to steal stuff, and not to mention increase your own profit!

This thing was only supposed to shrink the statues, but guess what, it can now enlarge them too! Anyway let's be done with it yeah, no reason to wait any longer.

Or so you'd think, sooner than later the statue began to shrink, no weighing less, and could easily be placed in your backpack.
