**Emerald Sanctum** is an instance raid located in [Hyjal](Hyjal). A fog of corruption has descended upon the Emerald Dream, twisting the morals and intentions of even the noblest and purest. The corrupted Wakener is preparing to send a premature call of awakening; if not stopped, his kin will arise and go on a frenzied rampage across Azeroth.

## Attunement
In order enter the instance portal of the Emerald Sanctum, the player needs to have the [Gemstone of Ysera](Gemstone of Ysera), which can be acquired through the quest chain "Into the Dream":
- [Into the Dream I](Into the Dream I)
  - Starts and ends at [Ralathius](Ralathius) in [Nordanaar](Nordanaar), [Hyjal](Hyjal)
  - Simply listen to Ralathius' story
- [Into the Dream II](Into the Dream II)
  - Starts and ends at [Ralathius](Ralathius) in [Nordanaar](Nordanaar), [Hyjal](Hyjal)
  - Collect 3 [Nightmare Effigies](Nightmare Effigy) from the Satyrs in Hyjal
- [Into the Dream III](Into the Dream III)
  - Starts at [Ralathius](Ralathius) in [Nordanaar](Nordanaar), [Hyjal](Hyjal)
  - Ends at [Itharius](Itharius) in the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows)
  - Collect the following: 
  - *An [Overloaded Arcane Prism](Overloaded Arcane Prism) (from an [Arcane Torrent](Arcane Torrent) in [Dire Maul](Dire Maul) West)
  - *A [Slumberer's Shard](Slumberer's Shard) (from [Weaver](Weaver) in [Sunken Temple](Sunken Temple))
  - *A [Binding Fragment](Binding Fragment) (from an elite [Cliff Breaker](Cliff Breaker) giant in [Azshara](Azshara))
  - *An [Arcanite Rod](Arcanite Rod) (made by [blacksmiths](Blackmithing) or purchasable for 150 gold from [Vorthos Everbreeze](Vorthos Everbreeze) in Nordanaar)
- [Into the Dream IV](Into the Dream IV)
  - Starts at [Itharius](Itharius) in the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows)
  - Ends at [Ralathius](Ralathius) in [Nordanaar](Nordanaar), [Hyjal](Hyjal)
  - Simply travel back to Ralathius
- [Into the Dream V](Into the Dream V)
  - Starts and ends at [Ralathius](Ralathius) in [Nordanaar](Nordanaar), [Hyjal](Hyjal)
  - Protect Ralathius during the short event against 4 [Nightmare Apparition](Nightmare Apparition)s (non-elite)
- [Into the Dream VI](Into the Dream VI)
  - Starts and ends at [Ralathius](Ralathius) in [Nordanaar](Nordanaar), [Hyjal](Hyjal)
  - Loot the [Charge of Ysera](Charge of Ysera) from the drake [Larannikus](Larannikus) (level 61 elite, CC-immune) in [Hyjal](Hyjal)
