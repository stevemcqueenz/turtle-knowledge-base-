# Main Hall (Northshire)

The **Main Hall** of [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey), located in [Northshire Valley](Northshire Valley) in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), serves as the entry of the abbey and grants access to the tower, [Hall of Arms](Hall of Arms (Northshire)), and [Library Wing](Library Wing (Northshire)). This is also where young [human](human) [adventurer](adventurer)s receive their first quests.
