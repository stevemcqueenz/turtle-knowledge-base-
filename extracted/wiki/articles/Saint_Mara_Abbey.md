# Saint Mara Abbey

**Saint Mara Abbey** is a ruined abbey located in northern [Northwind](Northwind), named for Lady [Mara Fordragon](Mara Fordragon). The abbey was burned down under mysterious circumstances during the yearly gospel where the clergy would remember all the dead of the lands, the abbot making it his duty to speak every name. During the ceremony, the gospel was interrupted by [a nun](The Wailing Nun) screaming through the halls about a fire before collapsing and dying. It is unknown as to how the abbey was set on fire; some believe that it was the gnolls who set the abbey ablaze, others believe it was the wrath of the Light, and some believe that the nun accidentally started the fire herself. (ref: [quest: Remember the Dead])
