-Corthan** (or the **Kingdom of Corthan** (ref: [quest: A King's Plea])) was an ancient kingdom of [Azotha](Azotha) (ref: [Moriena from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1274061839482753157) (dead link) - "Corthan was a kingdom of the Azotha") (ref: [The History of Corthan](The History of Corthan)) situated in the present-day region of the [Badlands](Badlands). They were a warlike people (ref:  who conquered and subjugated the lesser tribes of the area. Corthan's ruler was called the Marauder King; those who took up this mantle were also thereafter called Corthan.<ref>[Gheor from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1086438286467272736) (dead link) - "It is basically implied that all high priests and their children are named after Zeth. The FK's name is Zeth, and his daughters name is Zetharia. At one point in time the duty of High Priest was given to his daugther - they abandon their old names when they get these titles -, same as Corthan.")

Corthan maintained an ironclad grip on the region through continuous warfare. Despite their ferocity and skill in combat, the fallout of a love feud between the last Marauder King and the Keeper of of Zeth's daughter, [Zetharia](Zetharia), ultimately spelled the doom of the nation, when the latter called forth the dark forces of the [Lord of Everlasting Death](God of Everlasting Death), who ensnared Corthan's inhabitants in a curse of undeath forever.

## History
Corthan was formed from a group of Azotha. (ref:  Its birth predates the rise of [Arathor](Arathi Empire); it is suggested that the Arathi dynasty may have been established by rebels who fled north from Corthan.<ref>[Gheor from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1086439363199635568) (dead link) - "The Kingdom of Corthan predates the Arathor Empire. Corthan mentions rebels going up north of the now Badlands and implies they have gone back into a tribal state - thus being the first step towards the Empire of Arathor.") 

The land of Corthan was ruled by many kings, who each had the strength to vie for, and dominate, the region, with "a fist of iron, and strength eternal."  Corthan would go on to weather many storms, including drought, disease, and famine. The nation also warred with its neighbors constantly to maintain its dominion over the land; this penchant for warfare, however, would end up weakening the principles of its people over time. (ref: 

Corthan was still conquering neighboring tribes by the time of the last [Marauder King Corthan](Marauder King Corthan), who solidified his rule by offering amnesty to the defeated chieftains and warlords of the area in exchange for their loyalty.<ref>Events of [quest: The Land of Kings].) Corthan would later enter a betrothal to Zetharia, the daughter of the Keeper of Zeth, who resided in the [High Temple of Zeth](Ruins of Zeth). While Zetharia doted upon the Marauder King, the arrangement soon fell apart when Corthan brought his commoner mistress with him on the day of his wedding ceremony. (ref: Events of [quest: The Curse of Zetharia].) 

Heartbroken and enraged by Corthan's unrequited love, Zetharia confined herself to her bedroom for days, where she consulted many tomes. On the day of Corthan's marriage to his commoner sweetheart, Zetharia emerged from her room. Having made a necromantic pact with the Lord of Everlasting Death, Zetharia cursed the Marauder King, his new wife, and his very kingdom to suffer an eternity of undeath, for all time. 

The kingdom once possessed a powerful Azotha artifact called the [Hand of Corthan](Hand of Corthan). Long after Corthan's fall, [Dark Bishop Mordren](Dark Bishop Mordren) sent Horde adventurers to retrieve the ancient artifact. (ref: [quest: For the Collection])

## People and culture
### Religion
Zeth appears to have been the religion of Corthan. All high priests and their children are named after Zeth (similarly to the naming tradition of each Marauder King being called Corthan). While the specifics are unknown, the worship centers around an entity called the "Lord of the Living Flame." The [Ruins of Zeth](Ruins of Zeth) was once the high temple of Zeth, and it is there where the flame of kings is located, under the care of the Keeper.

## Speculation
- One of the runes in the Molten Core is called the Rune of Zeth; it is possible that the Lord of Everlasting Flame is Ragnaros.
