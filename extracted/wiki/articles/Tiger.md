# Tiger

A variety of [Cat](Cat), tigers are found in the Echo Isles of [Durotar](Durotar) and all over [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale).  Most tigers are orange with black stripes, but there are a few white tigers, the most famous being King Bangalash.
