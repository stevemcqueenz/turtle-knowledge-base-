# Starfall Village

**Starfall Village** is a small outpost of [Alliance](Alliance) [vendor](vendor)s and [quest giver](quest giver)s in [Winterspring](Winterspring). It is found along the main road, northwest of [Everlook](Everlook) and south of [Frostsaber Rock](Frostsaber Rock). It contains an all-but empty [barrow den](barrow den), a [night elven](night elven) lodge, and also a smaller house, though the village's inhabitants prefer to stay within the more defensible lodge.

The following NPCs can be found here:
- [Lyranne Feathersong](Lyranne Feathersong) - food and drink
- [Natheril Raincaller](Natheril Raincaller) - general goods
- [Syurana](Syurana) - trade goods
- [Wynd Nightchaser](Wynd Nightchaser) - quests
- [Jaron Stoneshaper](Jaron Stoneshaper) - quests
- [Grum Redbeard](Grum Redbeard)
