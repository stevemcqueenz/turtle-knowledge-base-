## Objectives
Enter [Gilneas City](Gilneas City) and slay [Genn Greymane](Genn Greymane), then bring his head to [Blackthorn](Blackthorn) at [Blackthorn's Camp](Blackthorn's Camp) in [Gilneas](Gilneas).
- [Genn Greymane's Head](Genn Greymane's Head)

## Description
Storytime is over. The time to act is at hand. I have read the report you retrieved from Livia, and I have decided how best to proceed.

King Genn Greymane was ever a force to be reckoned with in the old Alliance of Lordaeron. His strength and shrewdness were only matched by his stubbornness and pride. He cannot be allowed a to wrest control over his crumbling kingdom once more.

Venture into his city with as many comrades as you can find and bring me his head.

## Progress
The King of Gilneas was once a force to be reckoned with. Though his power and grip over the kingdom has diminished, he will be no easy prey.

## Completion
Wonderful! You have proven yourself a capable agent of the Dark Lady.

Though your tale of what happened in the city troubles me... If Genn Greymane was truly behind the rebellion, we may have inadvertently aided our enemies. That, however, is neither your fault nor your concern.

We will take it from here. You are dismissed.
