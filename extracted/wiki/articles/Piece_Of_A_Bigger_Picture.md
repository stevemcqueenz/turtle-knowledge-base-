## Objectives
Return with the needed reagents to Hydromancer Finnigan in the SI:7 Outpost on Balor.
- [Finnigan's Notebook](Finnigan's Notebook)
- [Reflecting Crystal](Reflecting Crystal)
- [Greater Astral Essence](Greater Astral Essence) (3)

## Description
The pearl you found possesses tidal energy, meaning its mere presence controls the flow of the ocean and the temper of the sky. Seeing as it is damaged to a great amount, it would explain how the tempest is so unbound around here. How truly fascinating to see such a relic on this desolate island. Has it always been here? We can worry about this later, however. First and foremost we need to level the rampant energy within the pearl.

I can subside the pearl's unbridled power with a spell, but I require a few ingredients for that. A reflecting crystal, facetted and sharp. The Stone Maw basilisks near the northern river in the Stranglethorn Vale create these stones as they age. Bring me the biggest you can find. I also need my old spellbook, which I gifted to my good friend Loremaster Dibbs in Southshore of the Hillsbrad Foothills. Tell him I sent you, and he will gladly return it to me. To complete everything, bring me also three greater astral essences.
