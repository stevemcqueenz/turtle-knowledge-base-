## Description
If dere is one who could see our struggle, it be Vol'jin of da Darkspear. Like no otha he be understanding how is it to be outcast and lose da homeland.My kind be not welcome in Horde lands, but maybe ya be able to show our new ways.

Travel to Orgrimmar, and speak to Vol'jin. Tell him what be happening in Zul'Farrak and offer our assistance.

## Notes
**Speaking with Vol'jin**

  I be Vol'jin, son of Sen'jin. It be a long time since we fled our lands of the Darkspear Isles, but here in Durotar we found a new home and future alongside the Horde.

  Our tribe be resilient, and no matter how the river flow, the stones remain. Stay away from the Voodoo, and may the Loa forever guide ya steps mon.

  For the Darkspear and for the Horde!

  > Gossip: *I have been sent on behalf of the Sandfury, they have been driven from their home.*

  We, Darkspear, had to flee our homeland twice. Once, the Gurubashi drove us from Stranglethorn, and then the naga drove us away from the Isles. We know the feeling of loss.

  > Gossip: *The Sandfury in Sandmoon Village remind me of the Darkspear.*

  I see it too. Yet I have trouble believing their intents. Among the few Troll tribes left in Kalimdor, the Sandfury had a terrible reputation, even before Ukorz took over.

  > Gossip: *I don't know them well, but they welcomed me in their village like a friend. They had to leave Zul'Farrak because of persecution and despotism of their leader. They ran away, because they saw what is happening there. They saw the darkness, the corruption.*

  The Horde welcomes anyone who does not fit anywhere else. Thrall welcomed the Darkspear in spite of Troll history with the Second Horde. I cannot leave them, my kind, in the desert.

  I be not saying they join the Horde yet, but we will help. And stop the madness in Zul'Farrak.
