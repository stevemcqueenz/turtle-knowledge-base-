# Razorwind Canyon

The **Razorwind Canyon** is the proper nesting grounds for the [Dustwind](Dustwind) [harpies](harpy) of [Durotar](Durotar). Adventurers had better prepare themselves for battle before heading into this area, as the aggressive bird women are quite numerous through the canyon, and can quickly subdue or kill unwary travelers.
