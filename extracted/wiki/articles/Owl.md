# Owl

A broad-faced, nocturnal bird known to hunt rodents and reptiles. **Owls** look different from most birds in that they have wide faces, short curved beaks and large, front-facing eyes.

A [Player](Player) can purchase two types of owls as "vanity pets" from a [vendor](Vendor) before the gates to [Darnassus](Darnassus):
- [Great Horned Owl](Great Horned Owl)
- [Hawk Owl](Hawk Owl)
