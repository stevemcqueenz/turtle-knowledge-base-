# Draenor

Originally, there were many intelligent races on the world of Draenor, most powerful of which were the [Orc](Orc)s. The Orcs lived in a quiet, shamanistic society until power-hungry leaders made a pact with the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) which turned the Orcs into bloodthirsty conquerors. They decimated every other race on Draenor before the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal) was opened, allowing most of the Orcs to leave for new lands to conquer.

Eventually, the power of the Dark Portal twisted and tore Draenor apart, leaving the barely-hospitable Outland. Some creatures managed to survive: some of the remaining Orcs, Ogres, and the [Draenei](Draenei).

The [Night Elf](Night Elf) Demon [Illidan](Illidan) and many of the [Naga](Naga) and [Blood Elves](Blood Elf) also fled to Draenor/Outland after their defeat in [Northrend](Northrend) at the hands of [Prince Arthas](Prince Arthas)/The [Lich King](Lich King).
