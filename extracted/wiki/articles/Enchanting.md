## Disenchanting
Magical elements (shards, essences and dusts) are needed as Enchanting ingredients; all of which can be created with disenchanting.  Some magical elements can be found as loot or purchased from the Auction House or Vendors in limited quantities.

### Materials
The type and quantity of materials produced from disenchanting varies by item type, level and quality.

**Dusts**

Dusts usually are extracted from armors, but also occasionally found from weapons.

- [Strange Dust] - Disenchanted from level 1 to 20 items
- Level 11-15: Much strange dust
- [Soul Dust] - Disenchanted from level 21 to 30 items
- Level 26-30: Much soul dust
- [Vision Dust] - Disenchanted from level 31 to 40 items
- Level 36-40: Much vision dust
- [Dream Dust] - Disenchanted from level 41 to 50 items
- Level 46-50: Much dream dust
- Illusion Dust - Disenchanted from level 51 to 60 items
- Level 56-60: Much illusion dust

**Essences**

Essences usually are extracted from weapons, but also occasionally found from armors. Uniquely among the enchanting ingredients, these can be changed from lesser to greater and vice versa, at a ratio of 3 lesser to 1 greater of the same kind. This may be done either because a formula calls for the other size, or simply to save bag space (as the conversion is reversible without loss).

- [Lesser Magic Essence] - Disenchanted from level 1 to 10 items
- [Greater Magic Essence] - Disenchanted from level 11 to 15 items
- [Lesser Astral Essence] - Disenchanted from level 16 to 20 items (armor only)
- [Greater Astral Essence] - Disenchanted from level 21 to 25 items (armor only)
- [Lesser Mystic Essence] - Disenchanted from level 26 to 30 items
- [Greater Mystic Essence] - Disenchanted from level 31 to 35 items
- [Lesser Nether Essence] - Disenchanted from level 36 to 40 items
- [Greater Nether Essence] - Disenchanted from level 41 to 45 items
- [Lesser Eternal Essence] - Disenchanted from level 46 to 50 items
- [Greater Eternal Essence] - Disenchanted from level 51 to 60 items

**Shards**

Shards are normally disenchanted from blue or better items, but have a small chance of coming from green items.

- [Small Glimmering Shard] - Disenchanted from level 1 to 20 items
- [Large Glimmering Shard] - Disenchanted from level 21 to 25 items
- [Small Glowing Shard] - Disenchanted from level 26 to 30 items
- [Large Glowing Shard] - Disenchanted from level 31 to 35 items
- [Small Radiant Shard] - Disenchanted from level 36 to 40 items
- [Large Radiant Shard] - Disenchanted from level 41 to 45 items
- [Small Brilliant Shard] - Disenchanted from level 46 to 50 items
- [Large Brilliant Shard] - Disenchanted from level 51 to 60 items

**Nexus Crystals**

Nexus Crystals are obtained by disenchanting Epic (purple) items, and sometimes come from Rare (blue) items also.

- [Nexus Crystal] - Disenchanted from level 51-60 items

## Enchanted Glows
Enchanters may enchant weapons for an additional bonus. Many of these enchants give off a glowing aura once the enchant is applied to the weapon.

- Check this link to learn what enchants applies glowing auras: [Glowchart](https://web.archive.org/web/20080406121914/http://www.glowchart.com/index.cfm?glows)
