# Draenethyst

-Draenethyst** are gems originally native to [Draenor](Draenor) that hold great cultural importance to the [draenei](draenei) due to their otherworldly power. (ref: [THE MIREBEAST — Everlook Broadcasting Co. Radio Play Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2fhEAUpBBLU)) It is said to be the lifeblood of Draenor, and less experienced [riftwalker](riftwalker)s make use of it as reagents when tearing open portals into the [Twisting Nether](Twisting Nether). (ref: [quest: Learn Of My Past]) Draenethyst is also sought out by the [high elves](high elves) of the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant), as the latent magical energies in them provide sustenance for their magical addiction. (ref: [Andanil Sunsworn#Quotes](Andanil Sunsworn#Quotes)) While the draenei had brought many of the crystals with them during their riftwalk to Azeroth, their arrival caused draenethyst to coalesce from the remaining rift magic and it began to crystalize on its own, grafting itself on the new world.

The Shard of Aka'Sha was a particularly powerful crystal of draenethyst that was stolen from the [Harborage](Harborage) by the draenei [Den'borr](Den'borr). When confronted by his fellow tribesmen, he refused to come to his senses. When his brethren attemempted to reclaim it from him, he was attacked by an enraged bog beast, and in the process they were fused into one being by the Shard of Aka'Sha acting of its own volition - the two warping into a creature that the draenei of Harborage named the Mirebeast. The Shard of Aka'Sha was later retrieved when Den'borr and the bog beast were separated once more when they fought an adventuring party seeking a cure for their companion's otherworldly sickness.
