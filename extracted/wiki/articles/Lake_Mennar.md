# Lake Mennar

-Lake Mennar** 39 81 Azshara (g/s/c) is one of the few places in the world where the [blue dragonflight](blue dragonflight) can be found outside of [Northrend](Northrend). The only comparable place is [Mazthoril](Mazthoril) in [Winterspring](Winterspring). Although most of the region on all sides of the lake is inhabited by [dragonspawn](dragonspawn), the actual lake itself is free from danger, and can provide solace and refreshment to those who are brave enough to get through the perimeter dragonkin.
