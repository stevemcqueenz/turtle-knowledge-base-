# Shimmer Ridge

-Shimmer Ridge** is located in northern [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), west of the [Gates of Ironforge](Gates of Ironforge). The ridge is inhabited by [Frostmane](Frostmane clan) [troll](troll)s, who grow [Shimmerweed](Shimmerweed) and dwell in two camps connected by a bridge. Young adventurers seek Shimmer Ridge out in order to collect the shimmer weed for an especially tasty ale recipe.
