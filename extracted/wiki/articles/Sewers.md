# Sewers

The **Sewers** are a side entrance to the [Undercity](Undercity), accessible from the west of the main gate to [the Ruins of Lordaeron](the Ruins of Lordaeron).  This is often the preferred entrance to the city by [Alliance](Alliance) who wish to attack the city, bypassing the main entrance.
