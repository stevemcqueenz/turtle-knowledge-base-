**Jed Runewatcher** is a rare spawn [orc](orc) in [Upper Blackrock Spire](Upper Blackrock Spire). He may wander in the room of [Goraluk Anvilcrack](Goraluk Anvilcrack) or in the Furnace (near [The Beast](The Beast)).  It is possible to use the <code>/target</code> command from the entrance of [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire) to check whether he is spawned [Goraluk Anvilcrack](Goraluk Anvilcrack)'s room, but you must be <i>past</i> Blackrock Stadium (where you fight [Rend Blackhand](Rend Blackhand) before <code>/target</code> will work if he is spawned in the Furnace.

He's a member of the [Blackhand Legion](Blackhand Legion).
