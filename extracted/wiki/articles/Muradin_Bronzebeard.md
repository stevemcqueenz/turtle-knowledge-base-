**Muradin Bronzebeard** was the younger brother of the [dwarven](Dwarf) king [Magni Bronzebeard](Magni Bronzebeard), and the older brother of the renowned explorer [Brann Bronzebeard](Brann Bronzebeard). A hero of the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron) during the [Second War](Second War), he later served as ambassador to [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)) until the [Third War](Third War), where he also befriended the young [Prince Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil) and trained him in combat. (ref: [Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos Game Manual#Human History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Human_History)) Muradin was one of the first dwarves to establish the [Explorer's Guild](Explorer's Guild), a group dedicated to archaeology and exploration of the unknown world. (ref: [Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos manual#Muradin Bronzebeard](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Muradin_Bronzebeard)) Muradin perished in [Northrend](Northrend) when Arthas claimed the runeblade [Frostmourne](Frostmourne) during his quest to destroy the dreadlord [Mal'Ganis](Mal'Ganis). (ref: [Frostmourne (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Frostmourne_(WC3_Human)))
