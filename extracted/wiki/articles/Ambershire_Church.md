# Ambershire Church

The **Ambershire Church** (ref: [quest: The Grateful Dead]) is the chapel and graveyard of [Ambershire](Ambershire), located to the north of the town. [Sister Argent](Sister Argent) spends her days here educating the children of Northwind with [Brother Graham](Brother Graham)'s assistance.
