# Sungrass

**Sungrass** is an [Herb](Herb) that can be gathered with [Herbalism](Herbalism) (230).

## Source
Sungrass can be found most frequently in [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles), and [Badlands](Badlands). It has also been seen in [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes), [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater), and [Tanaris](Tanaris). Sungrass is most commonly found in open areas.
