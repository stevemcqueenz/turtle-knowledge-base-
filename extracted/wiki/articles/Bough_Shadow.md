# Bough Shadow

**Bough Shadow** 90.3 40.1 Ashenvale (g/s/c) is located in the northeastern corner of [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), just beyond [Forest Song](Forest Song). This place contains one of the portals to the [Emerald Dream](Emerald Dream). Although the Emerald Dream is not implemented yet, the guardians [Phantim](Phantim) and [Dreamstalker](Dreamstalker), along with other [dragonkin](dragonkin) guard it.

One of the four corrupted green [Dragons of Nightmare](Dragons of Nightmare) - [Emeriss](Emeriss), [Lethon](Lethon), [Taerar](Taerar), or [Ysondre](Ysondre), will randomly spawn here on occasion. They are tough and require a [raid](raid) to take down.
