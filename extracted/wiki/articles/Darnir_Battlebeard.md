**Darnir Battlebeard** was a [dwarf](dwarf) member of the [Human Expedition](Human Expedition) to [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) and brother of [Grumnir Battlebeard](Grumnir Battlebeard). Wielding the [Battlebeard Axe](Battlebeard Axe), he was a powerful warrior who set off for [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) during the [Third War](Third War) and made a name for himself battling the Horde. He was killed by the [orc](orc) [Scartusk](Scartusk) as the Alliance and Horde forces established a truce to head towards Hyjal, and Scartusk claimed the Battlebeard family axe for his own.
