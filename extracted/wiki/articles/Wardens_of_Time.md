The **Wardens of Time** are a group of the [Bronze dragonflight](Bronze dragonflight) that accepts the aid of any mortal that has earned access to the [Caverns of Time](Caverns of Time). Their goal is to undo the mysterious time anomalies that have recently begun to appear across the timeways.

## Reputation
At honored, mobs in [Caverns of Time: Black Morass](Caverns of Time: Black Morass) will no longer grant reputation. At revered, bosses in Caverns of Time: Black Morass also no longer grand reputation.
