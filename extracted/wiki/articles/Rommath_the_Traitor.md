## Objectives
Report to Grand Magister Rommath. He can be found on the top of the Regency Tower.

## Description
With the current situation in the [Regency Council](Regency Council), my hands are bound. No one but [Lor'themar](Lor'themar) would believe me, even with the letter in our possession. They would undoubtedly perceive my actions as an attempt to usurp the title.

However, all is not lost. If you can convince Rommath that you are, indeed, a delegation from [Felstrider Retreat](Felstrider Retreat), he may inadvertently reveal more about his plans. The letter is exceedingly vague, and this approach may unveil additional details. Go, but exercise caution to avoid exposing your true identity. Rommath is unpredictable, and he could dispose of you in the blink of an eye.

He is on the top of the tower.
