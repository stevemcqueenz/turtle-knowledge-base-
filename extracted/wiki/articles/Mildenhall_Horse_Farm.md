# Mildenhall Horse Farm

The **Mildenhall Horse Farm** is a stable located to the southwest of [Ambershire](Ambershire) in [Northwind](Northwind). Ran by the Geringt family, they have served the Mildenhalls for many generations. (ref: [Horsekeeper Geringt#Quotes](Horsekeeper Geringt#Quotes)) Along with the [Crawford Winery](Crawford Winery), it is a part of the agricultural aspects of Northwind that are overseen by [Lady Josephine Mildenhall](Lady Josephine Mildenhall). They take care of all the contestants' steeds for the [Northwind Joust](Jousting Grounds). (ref: [quest: To Look A Gift Horse In The Mouth])

Players can purchase their riding skills and both the normal and epic [human](human) [mount](mount)s here.
