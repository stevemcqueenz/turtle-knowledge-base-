## Description
It has been a long, and exhausting journey. I have fought many battles, and vanquished many foes.

Before I left my home, I yearned to behold the Cathedral of Light following its restoration. Instead, I was told I would be sent here, rather than to the Embassy in Stormwind. I will not lie to say it hurt me, for I fear I may not see it at the end of all this.

I would still like to pay homage to the Cathedral, however, and I ask you, friend, to do it for me.

Bring this tome containing all of my deeds to the Cathedral of Stormwind, and let it be recorded, lest I fall unremembered on this forsaken edge of the world.
