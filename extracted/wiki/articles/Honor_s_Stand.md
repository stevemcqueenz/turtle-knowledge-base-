# Honor's Stand

Guarding the west road between [Sun Rock Retreat](Sun Rock Retreat) and the [Crossroads](Crossroads), Honor's Stand [39,25] consists of a number of burrows, some badly damaged. It has, in the past, been the location for many great battles between the Horde and its enemies. The orcs stationed here, including peons tasked with repairing the buildings, are very much conscious of the outpost's illustrious, if short, history, though recently they have been beset by attacks mounted by the [Kolkar](Kolkar) [Centaur](Centaur).
