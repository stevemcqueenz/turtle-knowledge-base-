The **Burning Steppes** are located north of the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains) and south of the [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge). As such, they hold the only accessible land passage from the kingdom of [Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)) in the south to [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) and [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) in the north. Now virtually abandoned by Stormwind's protection, the Burning Steppes is controlled by the [orc](orc)ish [Blackrock clan](Blackrock clan), minions of the [black dragonflight](black dragonflight), and agents of [Firelord Ragnaros](Ragnaros). With the constant volcanic activity and flows of lava, the weather is perpetually hot and dry, and the land is absent of widespread vegetation.

The Blackrock orcs have long maintained a lasting settlement around the volcano known as Blackrock Stronghold, staying hidden from the road and always keeping watch for Alliance attacks. These orcs are more typical of the type of the First and Second wars rather than the current [Horde](Horde) in [Kalimdor](Kalimdor): Bloodthirsty and savage, they mine the mountains around Blackrock Spire for metals for their weapons and armor. In recent years, they have begun spreading outward, frequently terrorizing the [Alliance](Alliance)-held region of the Redridge Mountains to the south. They have allied with the [Firegut](Firegut) ogres on the southern end of the steppes as well as the [black dragonflight](black dragonflight) in preparation for a violent territorial expansion into lands protected by Stormwind.

## Geography
Lacking the grace and beauty of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan)'s peaks, the Burning Steppes' mountains show the force of the energy that twisted them. The very rock melted when the volcano was formed, and lava changed the surrounding areas forever. The Blackrock Mountain is awe-inspiring in its sheer mass and the obvious devastation it's brought, while the rest of the land attempts to recover from the damage done by the summoning of the Firelord, [Ragnaros](Ragnaros), in which the mountain was birthed.

On the western end of the ridge that divides the steppes from the Searing Gorge sits the impossibly high Blackrock Mountain, an active volcano birthed over 200 years ago. The steppes are mostly under the control of the Black Dragons and their Blackrock orc allies. Within the northeastern mountains stands the renegade Blackrock outpost of [Karfang Hold](Karfang Hold). To the north lay the [Ruins of Thaurissan](Ruins of Thaurissan), the Dark Iron clan's old capital city, as well as [Flame Crest](Flame Crest), a small independent outpost. In the southeast, the Alliance clings to the scarred ruins of the town now known as [Morgan's Vigil](Morgan's Vigil).

The [Hateforge Quarry](Hateforge Quarry) is an instanced dungeon that is located in the Burning Steppes themselves. In addition, the [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain) zone offers quite a few other high-level dungeons.
