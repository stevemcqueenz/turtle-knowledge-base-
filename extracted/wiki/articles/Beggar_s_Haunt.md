# Beggar's Haunt

-Beggar's Haunt** is an abandoned scout tower of the [Stormwind Guard](Stormwind Guard) located in northeastern [Duskwood](Duskwood), east of [Manor Mistmantle](Manor Mistmantle) and north of the road that leads into [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass). The [Horde](Horde)-aligned tower holds two agents of the [Forsaken](Forsaken) — [Deathstalker Zraedus](Deathstalker Zraedus) and a member of the [Royal Apothecary Society](Royal Apothecary Society) named [Apothecary Faustin](Apothecary Faustin). Both are level 40 and often attack approaching Alliance players that explore a little to close and only being around 20 - 30. A twisting path leads from the tower to a small pool and waterfall, near which rests an unmarked grave with two large statues similar to the ones on the Dark Portal (See [Knight's Hollow](Knight's Hollow)).
