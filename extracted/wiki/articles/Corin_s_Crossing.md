# Corin's Crossing

[Corin's Crossing](Corin's Crossing) used to be a fairly prosperous crossroads trading town.  Surrounded by the farmlands of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) and located strategically at a crossroads connecting [Andorhal](Andorhal), [Stratholme](Stratholme), and [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand), Corin's Crossing was a common stop for traders and travelers.  After the [Scourge](Scourge) turned the breadbasket of Lordaeron into the toxic [Plaguelands](Plaguelands), Corin's Crossing has become an outpost of the Scourge.  Travelers are advised to give the town a wide berth, as it is crawling with minions of the Scourge (levels 55+).  Even rogues using stealth are advised not to attempt to sneak through the town as there are dark shades present that can detect invisible units.
