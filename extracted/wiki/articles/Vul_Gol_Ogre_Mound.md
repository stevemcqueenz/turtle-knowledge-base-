# Vul'Gol Ogre Mound

The **Vul'Gol Ogre Mound** is a small basin in southern [Duskwood](Duskwood). It is completely overrun by [Splinter Fist](Splinter Fist) [ogre](ogre)s led by [Zzarc'Vul](Zzarc'Vul), after which the mound was named. It is located just to the southwest of where the main road through Duskwood splits and runs south into [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). Humans used to live in the basin, as evidenced by the ruined farmhouses.
