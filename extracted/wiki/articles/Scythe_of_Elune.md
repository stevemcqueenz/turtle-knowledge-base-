# Scythe of Elune

The **Scythe of Elune** is an ancient thing, a branch, twisted and gnarled of wood that could be older than even the [Kaldorei](Kaldorei). [Velinde Starsong](Velinde Starsong) was granted it from [Elune](Elune) (maybe she shuold only think, that it was a gift of Elune..), to fight against the [demon](demon)s in [Felwood](Felwood) and [Ashenvale](Ashenvale). By further channeling the energy of the Scythe, the barriers of time and space weakened, and she was able to draw the [worgen](worgen) into the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth). First, they followed her into battle, and with their brutish strength at her command, they tore into the demons of Felwood. Later, she noticed, that though she has not summoned additional worgen, their numbers continue to increase. It is as though the Scythe no longer requires her intervention for the summoning process. By searching for [Arugal](Arugal) and asking him for help, she lost the Scythe within a cave in [Duskwood](Duskwood). It is known, that the dark [mage](mage) [Morganth](Morganth) and Dark Riders from [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass) are now searching for it.
