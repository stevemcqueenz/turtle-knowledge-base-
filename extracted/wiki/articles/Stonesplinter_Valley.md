# Stonesplinter Valley

[Stonesplinter Valley](Stonesplinter Valley) is a valley carved among the mountains in southwestern [Loch Modan](Loch Modan).  While once a pristine setting, the valley is now crawling with hostile [trogg](trogg)s. Adventurers wishing to befriend the local [dwarves](dwarves) will find plenty of work hunting the foul humanoids, and the most ambitious heroes strike deep into the heart of the valley in attempts to rid the region of the trogg leader, [Grawmug](Grawmug).
