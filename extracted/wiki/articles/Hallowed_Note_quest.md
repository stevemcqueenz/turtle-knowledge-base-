## Progress
The Celestial Siblings guide your very first steps to me, <name>. It is I who will watch over you and teach you what I can as you complete your training here in the Red Cloud Mesa.

I trust you are eager to see the world for yourself and perhaps even walk a pilgrimage to the Moonwhisper Coast. Moonhoof Village holds three scrolls of prayer, each devoted to one of the Siblings.

## Completion
It will be many cycles before you are ready to guide your hooves there, my friend. Your training resumes. Seek me once you have gained more experience in battle.

To be a devoted worshipper of the Celestial Siblings places you beyond the bounds of tribe. You are cherished by the eyes and the tear of the Earthmother. Whatever path you walk from now on, at least one of them will always be by your side.
