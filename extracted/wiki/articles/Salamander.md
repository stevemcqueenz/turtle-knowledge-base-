# Salamander

In the depths of [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain) (known as [Molten Core](Molten Core)), there exists a very [Naga](Naga)-like group of creatures called the **Flamewakers**. They have a similar serpentine form, though they are adapted to living amongst the heat and flame of their home, rather than the watery places the Naga inhabit. 

All Flamewakers appear to live within [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain). [Ambassador Flamelash](Ambassador Flamelash) is the first of the Flamewaker adventurers are likely to encounter, as he is located in [Blackrock Depths](Blackrock Depths). Many of the Bosses in [Molten Core](Molten Core) are Flamewaker, including [Ragnaros](Ragnaros)' chief lieutenant, [Majordomo Executus](Majordomo Executus).

## The Race Name
Although there has been much discussion over the name of the race, it has been confirmed by Luke Johnson, the developer of the World of Warcraft RPG that the correct name is Flamewaker, and a description of the creatures will appear in the upcoming new Monster Guide for the WoW RPG. [http://forums.white-wolf.com/viewtopic.php?t=26455]

*Flamewakers* are the only name ascribed to them in-game. Flamewaker, Flamewaker Priest, Flamewaker Protector, Flamewaker Elite, and Flamewaker Healer are names of mobs holding this model. Instead of a racial name, the name *Flamewaker* could be a tribal name only, such as Dark Iron dwarves which are called sometimes simply Dark Irons, although they are indeed dwarves by race.

*Salamander*: the model used in the game takes this name. However, this name only appears in the game in connection to items dropped only by these creatures. [Salamander](Salamander)s in the Warcraft universe are actually either kodo-like creatures found in Warcraft 3, or type of fire elemental originating from the Elemental plane  and are not currently in World of Warcraft. This terminology is also a nod to real-life "fire salamanders", who have the tendency to hibernate in logs during winter and awaken to escape when the logs are burned for firewood.

*Fire Naga* is not supported anywhere in the game, and has been labelled purely out of a similar model. No evidence outside their aesthetic appearance suggests any relation between the two races.
