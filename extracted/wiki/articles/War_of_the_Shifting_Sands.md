# War of the Shifting Sands

The **War of the Shifting Sands** was a major conflict that took place in southern [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) between an allied campaign of [night elves](night elves) and [dragon](dragon)s and the immense armies of the [qiraji](qiraji) and lesser [silithid](silithid).
