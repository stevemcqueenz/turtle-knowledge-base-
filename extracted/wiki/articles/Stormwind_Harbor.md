# Stormwind Harbor

-Stormwind Harbor** is a large port and shipyard, consisting of a series of wharfs, dry docks, and fortifications located in the northwestern area of [Stormwind City](Stormwind City), with the access point set between the [Park](Park) and [Cathedral Square](Cathedral Square). The Kul Tiran vessel *Old Tide* is moored in the harbor and the embassy of [Davenburg](Davenburg) lies within it, and the northern gate leads to the [Merchant's Highroad](Merchant's Highroad) and further into [Northwind](Northwind).

Stormwind Harbor itself is one of the major ports for transport to [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), thereby replacing the [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor) ↔ [Auberdine](Auberdine) line connecting the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms) to Kalimdor; the line to Auberdine now connects directly to Stormwind City, allowing Night Elf players to avoid the "death run" through the Wetlands. The riding gryphon [Steelwing](Steelwing) also offers passage to the [SI:7 Outpost](SI:7 Outpost) on the island of [Balor](Balor).

## Notes
- Along with [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas), Stormwind City is the only other capital city containing a harbor within the city boundaries; sea access to [Darnassus](Darnassus) is through [Rut'theran Village](Rut'theran Village), which is not located within the city itself. Stormwind and Alah'Thalas are also the only capital cities with an open waterfront within its city boundaries.
