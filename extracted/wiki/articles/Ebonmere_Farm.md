# Ebonmere Farm

The **Ebonmere Farm** is one of the many farms of [Gilneas](Gilneas) that have been abandoned in the midst of the [Gilnean civil war](Gilnean civil war). It is overran by [Vilewing Batling](Vilewing Batling)s.
