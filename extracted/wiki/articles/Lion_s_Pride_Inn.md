# Lion's Pride Inn

-Lion's Pride Inn** (also known as the **Goldshire Inn**) (ref: [Journey of Brann](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Journey_of_Brann)) is an [inn](inn) found in the town of [Goldshire](Goldshire), in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest). According to [Innkeeper Farley](Innkeeper Farley), the inn's motto is "Rest and relaxation for the tired and cold." (ref:  [5] [Rest and Relaxation](Rest and Relaxation))

Lion's Pride offers a wide range of services from class trainers and quest givers to drinks aplenty.
