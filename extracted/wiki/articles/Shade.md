# Shade

A disembodied spirit and a type of [ghost](ghost). These often restless spirits are mostly found in the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands), other [undead](undead)-infested areas, and the village surrounding the mysterious tower of [Karazhan](Karazhan). Because of they do not have a real body, some shades are immune to physical damage.

Due to their invisibility, shades are employed by the [Scourge](Scourge) as spies and scouts. They are created from the sacrifice of [acolyte](acolyte)s of the [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned)
