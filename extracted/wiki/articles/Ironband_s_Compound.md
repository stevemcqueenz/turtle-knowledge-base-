# Ironband's Compound

-Ironband's Compound** lies south of [Helm's Bed Lake](Helm's Bed Lake) in eastern [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), and is a base of operations for a band of [Dark Iron dwarves](Dark Iron clan) who readily assault anyone unfortunate enough to wander too close. It was formerly inhabited by [Sturgy Ironband](Sturgy Ironband).

Dwarven [paladin](paladin)s come to the compound in order to aid a fallen dwarf and secure evidence of the Dark Irons' presence for [Ironforge](Ironforge) in [quest: The Tome of Divinity|class=paladin|race=dwarf].
