# Hamuul Runetotem

Arch Druid [Hamuul Runetotem](Hamuul Runetotem) is a level 62 [quest giver](quest giver) located on [Elder Rise](Elder Rise) in the [tauren](tauren) city of [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff).

## Lore
Hamuul Runetotem met [Malfurion Stormrage](Malfurion Stormrage) during the recent invasion of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) and befriended him. The [night elf](night elf) instructed the tauren, awakening an innate affinity for [druid](druid (lore))ism.  Despite opposition from some night elves, such as [Fandral Staghelm](Fandral Staghelm), Runetotem became the first individual belonging to a non-elven race to be accepted as a member of the [Cenarion Circle](Cenarion Circle). He shared his wisdom with his tauren brethren and thus reintroduced the knowledge of the druids, which had been forgotten for eons, to his people.

Hamuul has a daughter, [Bashana](Bashana Runetotem), who is a Cenarion Circle member like her father.
There was also a Tauren chieftain called Taur Runetotem that helped the Horde during the Third War.
