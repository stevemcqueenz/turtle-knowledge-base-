# Sea giant

Gentle, aquatic proto-beings created by the [Titan](Titan)s to help them dredge out the seas and lift the land from the sea floor of [Azeroth](Azeroth). 

They stand around 18 feet tall and weigh about 1,000 pounds.

They seldom make their way to the surface world, instead preferring the isolation of the ocean bottom. They enjoy the worship of [murloc](murloc)s, but are the constant enemy of the [naga](naga). Of late, sea giants have taken to hunting out these corruptors, which has brought them closer and closer to the surface world.

Nomadic creatures, sea giants do not maintain lairs like others of their kind. Instead, they drag their belongings and wealth along with them in huge sacks crafted from the sails of sunken ships.

Few remain, and those that do may attack you if you encounter them.

The Sea Giants of [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale) are particularly malevolent: they are known for deliberately sinking ships and stealing their loot.
