## Objectives
Find Brave Greenhorn and heal his wounds using Lesser Heal (Rank 2). Afterwards, grant him Power Word: Fortitude and then return to Pargan Starstrider in Bloodhoof Village, Mulgore.
- Heal and fortify Brave Greenhorn

## Description
Let us put your teachings to the test. South of this tent, past the circle of totems where our warriors train and beneath the shadows and foliage of trees you will find Brave Greenhorn. He looms, wounded, keeping guard over the Winterhoof Water Well which has been run over by the Venture Co.

While he was ordered simply to observe he was none the wiser and chose to do the opposite. As such he started a fight with the Venture Co. and managed to flee by the skin of his teeth. Your task is to find him, heal him and reinforce his spirit through Fortitude and remind him, he has allies he can rely upon. So that he may return to the village and muster a force to face the enemy ahead.

Do this and when you return we will speak of Mu’sha.

## Rewards
You will receive:
- [Celestial Garments](Celestial Garments)
- 270 XP

## Progress
You may see healing and enhancing your allies as extensions of both An’she and Mu’sha. Where the rays of An’she warmly soothe a wound, the white bloom in the night sky during each of Mu’sha’s cycles acts as a guide through the darkness. Lo’sho can soothe, but he cannot heal. His gifts to you bend and mend the mind, not the body.

## Completion
You have done well. To commemorate your first trial, I will reward you with one of my old robes. As promised, let me speak of Mu’sha. One of our longest allies has been the night elves, who, as their name implies, are children of the Night and, as such, children of Elune, the name they have given Mu’sha. Through our years of connection, some tauren have sensed a trace of favoritism. If we are the children of the Earthmother, then the night elves are the children of Mu’sha, and she may seem inclined to favor them. That does not mean she neglects us in any way, yet a hint of jealousy lingers among some worshipers.

In truth, it has helped us bond with the night elves and find fraternity with them. But to speak plainly, Mu’sha has always pointed the way, and if you choose to follow it, do so. You do not need any explanation; she won’t try to convince you. Of the three, she is the most free, if you will. Her path is her own, and you can either come along or stay behind.
