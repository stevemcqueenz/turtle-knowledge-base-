# Stonemaul Tribe

The Stonemaul Tribe is a group of Ogres residing at [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh).

## Notes and trivia
- Although the faction had originally been announced in the notes for [patch 1.16.0](patch 1.16.0), no content was ever developed for them and no NPCs were ever attributed to the faction.
