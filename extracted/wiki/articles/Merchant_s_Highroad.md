# Merchant's Highroad

The **Merchant's Highroad** is the road leading from [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) to [Northwind](Northwind). [Gnoll](Gnoll)s from the [Amberpaw Hideout](Amberpaw Hideout) prey on travelers along the road.
