# Ramstein the Gorger

Ramstein is a mostly mindless [abomination](abomination) living in the burning city of [Stratholme](Stratholme) under the command of the [Scourge](Scourge). Like all abominations he is not particulary intelligent in most cases, however, and relying on his sense of tactics is an exceptionally poor idea.
He is fearless, tenacious, and resilient, capable of pressing his adventage in combat for hours without growing weary or weak.
He is responsible for the death of thousands, including [Nathanos Blightcaller](Nathanos Blightcaller). And now Nathanos must have vengeance on that which took from him his life.
