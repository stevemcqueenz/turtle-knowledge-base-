## Objectives
Bring Akh Z'ador in Azshara three Siren Brains for his ‘mental recovery'.
-[Siren Brain](Siren Brain) (3)

## Description
Before we can tackle the saving of my tribe, we need to prepare, both myself and my belongings. Riftwalking is very taxing on the user's mind, an interrupted riftwalk even more so. Without my mental strength returned, a rescue will not be possible.

My people have discovered that a creature's brain can accelerate one's mental recovery immensely, if devoured. The more intelligent the beast, the quicker you feel renewed. Down at the shore I saw serpent people slither around, some of them casting spells on their own. Bring me a few undamaged brains from these spellcasters.

Let us hope your world's species are as smart as I envision them to be.
