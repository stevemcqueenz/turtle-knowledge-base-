# Swiftthistle

**Swiftthistle** is a [herb](herb) [crafting reagent](crafting reagent). Unlike most herbs, Swiftthistle is not gathered in herb nodes, but instead has a chance to be gathered when picking [Briarthorn](Briarthorn) and [Mageroyal](Mageroyal). It is also rarely dropped by a number of [mobs](mobs).
