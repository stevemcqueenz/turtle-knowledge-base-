*This article is about the human nation. For the ruins of the city, see [Dalaran](Dalaran). For the in-game reputation faction, see [Dalaran (faction)](Dalaran (faction)).*

  -Dalaran** (officially the **Magocracy of Dalaran**, (ref: *[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*) and also referred to as the **City State of Dalaran**) (ref: [Magus Orelius#Quotes](Magus Orelius#Quotes)) is one of the [seven human nations](Seven Kingdoms) in the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), located near the southern coast of [Lordamere Lake](Lordamere Lake) in Lordaeron. It is a small, magocratic nation originally founded as a city-state by magi of the Empire of Arathor. It is ruled by the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor), an elite cadre of the most powerful wizards in the known world, under the leadership of the [Council of Six](Council of Six).
