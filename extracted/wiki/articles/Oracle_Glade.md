# Oracle Glade

The **Oracle Glade** is a shrine in the northwestern area of [Teldrassil](Teldrassil). One of the sacred [moonwell](moonwell)s lies there, as well as the magical [Oracle Tree](Oracle Tree).

The Oracle Glade is the site of an ancient and mysterious tree shaped liked a [bear](bear) with an [eagle](eagle) flying from its back. This bizarre tree emerged without warning, and the [Cenarion Circle](Cenarion Circle) studies it for clues. The Circle believes that when the tree reaches the appropriate state of evolution, it may divulge information regarding the origin of the second [World Tree](World Tree) from which it grew.

[Harpies](Harpies) have recently moved into the Oracle Glade, and a foul blight has corrupted the matron of the Teldrassil [spider](spider)s, [Lady Sathrah](Lady Sathrah), who can sometimes be found in the far corners of the glade.
