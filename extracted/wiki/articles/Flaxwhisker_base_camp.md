# Flaxwhisker base camp

A base located at the peak of [Icepoint Rock](Icepoint Rock) was established by the gnomes of the [Flaxwhisker Front](Flaxwhisker Front) after one of their reconnaissance aircrafts flew over the area. (ref: [Flight Logbook](Flight Logbook)) The last gnome to tend the camp was left stranded after his flying machine was grounded by the harsh weathers for which it was not built for, resulting in his fuel reserves and the Spanflux Capacitor freezing over. Due to the fall of Gnomeregan and the landing pads in Azshara having gone derelict or been taken over by murlocs, (ref:  [50] [The Flight Logbook](The Flight Logbook)) the Flaxwhisker pilot received no aid and his food reserves were depleated over the course of fifty nine days, and thus he starved to death after attempting one last time to start up the flying machine with the last of his fuel. (ref: [Weathered Journal](Weathered Journal))
