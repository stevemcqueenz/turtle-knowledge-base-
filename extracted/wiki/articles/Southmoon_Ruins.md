# Southmoon Ruins

The **Southmoon Ruins** is an area of ancient troll ruins, in [Tanaris](Tanaris), now taken over by the [Dunemaul](Dunemaul) [ogre](ogre)s from their stronghold [Dunemaul Compound](Dunemaul Compound). [Eastmoon Ruins](Eastmoon Ruins) is also occupied by Dunemaul ogres.

Southmoon Ruins are just northeast of [Uldum](Uldum).
