# Ironbeard's Tomb

[Ironbeard's Tomb](Ironbeard's Tomb) is located just west of the road to [Dun Modr](Dun Modr) in north-central [Wetlands](Wetlands). The Ironbeard clan's crypt has been defiled by [ooze](ooze)s in black, crimson, and monstrous varieties, and none who wish to bury their dead come here any longer. Adventurers often journey to the tomb in hopes of locating [Sida](Sida)'s lost bag, which can rarely be found on any given ooze in the area.
