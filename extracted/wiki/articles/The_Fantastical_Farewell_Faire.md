## Objectives
Speak to Wiz Silverpocket at the Farewell Faire north of Moonhoof Village in Moonwhisper Coast. A Farewell Faire Barker in any capital city can bring you there with ease.

## Description
I extend my greetings to you, <Class>! Have you heard of the Farewell Faire in Moonwhisper Coast? It is a special occasion where all citizens of Azeroth gather round and reminisce about the wonderful memories they have made with their loved ones, friends and even strangers! We celebrate this feast to show gratitude towards our past and to look forward into the future with steadfast resolve. If you wish to join the festivities, speak to me again, and I will transport you there with a little magic trick of mine.

## Rewards
You will receive:
- [Turtle Coin](Turtle Coin) (5)

## Completion
Hello, hello! Like I always say, the more the merrier! Check out the booths, our dancefloor and take a visit to the pier and plant some lanterns into the water! Here, have this coin for a starter! Gather more and you'll be able to purchase wonderful souvenirs from my brother Wez right next to me!
