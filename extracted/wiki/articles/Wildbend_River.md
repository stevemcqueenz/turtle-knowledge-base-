# Wildbend River

The **Wildbend River** is a tainted river in the southern part of [Darkshore](Darkshore), near the [Grove of the Ancients](Grove of the Ancients). It is a continuation of [Bloodvenom River](Bloodvenom River) from Felwood as it cascades down the mountains to Darkshore.
