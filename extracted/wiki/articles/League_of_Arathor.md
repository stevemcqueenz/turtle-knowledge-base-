# League of Arathor

An organisation in support of the [Alliance](Alliance), based out of the [Arathi Basin](Arathi Basin).

Amongst other tasks, they have taken it upon themselves to help supply the Alliance forces where needed. 

Their members include all manner of Alliance races.
