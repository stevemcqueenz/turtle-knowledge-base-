# Mistvale Valley

**Mistvale Valley** is a valley in the southern part of Stranglethorn Vale inhabited by the great Mistvale [gorilla](gorilla)s. Adventurers often come through, looking to find [Mistvale Giblets](Mistvale Giblets), [Gorilla Fang](Gorilla Fang)s, or the very rare [Aged Gorilla Sinew](Aged Gorilla Sinew).
