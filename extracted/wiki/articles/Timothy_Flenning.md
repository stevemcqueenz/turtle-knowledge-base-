-Timothy Flenning** was a human boy from [Northwind](Northwind), where he lived with his mother [Judith](Judith Flenning) and sister [Sara](Sara Flenning). While Judith was away from the house, he and Sara were kidnapped (ref: [quest: Empty Houses]) by orcs of the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) that sought to take them to their hideout on the island of [Balor](Balor).

Along with many other captives, Timothy was experimented upon by the Stormreavers, resulting in his body liquifying and dissolving into ooze. Timothy's remains were later absorbed into the [Remains of the Innocent](Remains of the Innocent), deep beneath [Stormwrought Castle](Stormwrought Castle). Upon the destruction of the amalgamation, all that was left of Timothy was his [Etched Toy Sword](Etched Toy Sword). (ref: [quest: All That Is Left])
