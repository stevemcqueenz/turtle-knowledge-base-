# Wretched Ghetto

The **Wretched Ghetto** lies to the east of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas). It is the refuge of the [withered](withered), elves who have succumbed to their addiction to the arcane in the absence of the [Sunwell](Sunwell). Cast aside by elven society, they eke a meager living in their hovel under the leadership of [Tenris Mirkblood](Tenris Mirkblood), the self-styled king of the Withered.
