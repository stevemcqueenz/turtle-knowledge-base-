# Survival (historical)

*This article is about the original iteration of the Survival profession. For the modern profession, see [Survival](Survival).*

  -Survival** is a secondary professions in Turtle WoW which enables players to create a number of tools to aid them in overcoming obstacles and taming the wilderness.

## Overview
  *Survivalists practice outdoor skills that aid them in surviving the dangerous wilds of Azeroth. With the proper materials, they can create shelter and heat sources to survive the untamed wilderness.*

  *Survival is a secondary profession - anyone can train as a survivalist, no matter how many other professions they've devoted themselves to.*

  *Torches and tents can be created almost anywhere, as long as you have the appropriate ingredients - unlit torches (for torches) and linen cloth, simple wood and some sturdy rope (for tents).*

## Uses
The primary highlight of the Survival skill is the [Traveler's Tent](Traveler's Tent), which provides all players increased rested experience at a higher rate than an inn. Rested experience remains at the standard cap of [current level XP needed to level] x 1.5.

Survivalists also benefit from the gardening feature, allowing them to grow their own food that can provide them with various stat increases that can be paired with bonuses provided from food created with [Cooking](Cooking). Gardening is unlocked by completing the quest [quest: profession=survival], which is available from [Mortimer Stansfield](Mortimer Stansfield) outside [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest) and [Kern Mosshoof](Kern Mosshoof) in [Bloodhoof Village](Bloodhoof Village) in [Mulgore](Mulgore).

To begin your survivalist career, seek out [Rufus Hardwick](Rufus Hardwick) at [Nesingwary's Expedition](Nesingwary's Expedition) in northern [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). In order to begin learning Survival, you must be level 5 and pay 1 silver. Once you've acquired the Survival skill, you can begin making Dim Torches immediately and your potential skill level will be 75. Thereafter, consult the following table as you progress in Survival skill.

### Training locations
The only survival trainer is Rufus Hardwick at Nesingwary's Expedition in Stranglethorn Vale. Rufus Hardwick teaches both ranks of Survival.

The only recipe *not* taught by Rufus Hardwick is the [Simple Wooden Planter](Simple Wooden Planter), which is instead taught by gardening trainers outside [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest) and in [Bloodhoof Village](Bloodhoof Village) in [Mulgore](Mulgore) upon completing the quest [quest: profession=survival].

Gardening can only be done in gardening zones located outside [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest) and in [Bloodhoof Village](Bloodhoof Village) in [Mulgore](Mulgore).

## Notes and trivia
- Originally, to begin your survivalist career, one had to seek out [Sir S. J. Erlgadin](Sir S. J. Erlgadin) at Nesingwary's Expedition in northern [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), who offered the quest [quest: profession=survival]. The quest had a level five minimum requirement and required obtaining a [Wooden Club](Wooden Club), a [Sturdy Rope](Sturdy Rope) from [Jaquilina Dramet](Jaquilina Dramet) at Nesingwary's Camp and [Cheap Goblin's Oil](Cheap Goblin's Oil) from [Mazk Snipeshot](Mazk Snipeshot) in [Booty Bay](Booty Bay). Training was also done in a non-traditional manner, as Rufus Hardwick sold Survival recipes instead of teaching them, and Journeyman Survival was automatically learned upon reaching the appropriate skill level.
