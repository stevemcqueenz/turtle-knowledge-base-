# Venture Company

The Venture Trading Company is a [Hostile](Hostile) faction that operates in the [Mulgore](Mulgore), [The Barrens](The Barrens), and [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains) regions of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), as well as [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale) in the eastern kingdoms. Largely run and managed by [Goblins](Goblin), it employs a few [Gnomes](Gnome) in high engineering positions. It provides employment to all of the sentient races of Azeroth as laborers, even (possibly especially) the less intelligent ones such as [Ogres](Ogre) and [Gnolls](Gnoll).

The Company operates a mine in eastern  Mulgore and harasses the sacred wells of the [Tauren](Tauren) in that region.  You can obtain related [quest](quest)s in and around [Bloodhoof Village](Bloodhoof Village).

The goblin-run Venture Company moved even into the Stonetalon Mountains, chopping down trees and burning huge stretches of forest.
