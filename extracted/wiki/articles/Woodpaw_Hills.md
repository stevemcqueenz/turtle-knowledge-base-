# Woodpaw Hills

**Woodpaw Hills** is an area, densely inhabited by the [Woodpaw tribe](Woodpaw tribe) of [gnoll](gnoll)s, in the southeastern part of [Feralas](Feralas), north of the [Writhing Deep](Writhing Deep).
