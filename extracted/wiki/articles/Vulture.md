# Vulture

A large, carnivorous, scavenger bird that also goes by the name "Buzzard" or "Carrion Bird". They usually have featherless heads and broad, slow-flapping wingspans.  Vultures are sometimes found lurking about the bones of dead beasts and ill-managed graveyards.  They can be found in [Mulgore](Mulgore), [Westfall](Westfall), [Tanaris](Tanaris), the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), the [Badlands](Badlands), the [Blasted Lands](Blasted Lands), [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles), [Desolace](Desolace), and the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains).  

Additional notes:
- Vultures are [hostile](hostile) [beast](beast)s that will attack [player](player)s that venture too close.
- Most vultures have a knock-down ability called Swoop that interrupts attacks by characters and requires time for them to get up after being knocked down.

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Have medium HP, medium armor, and medium damage.
- Eats only Meat and Fish.
- Can learn [Dive](Dive), [Bite](Bite), [Claw](Claw), [Cower](Cower), [Growl](Growl), and their special skill, [Screech.](Screech.) 
- Excellent as a pet, due to their variety of skills, their area of effect taunt (Screech), and their overall stats.  The Spiteflayer, a rare spawn in the Blasted Lands, has a blazing 1.2 attack speed.
- Not seen very often as a Hunter pet.
