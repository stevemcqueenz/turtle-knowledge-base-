# South Seas

A portion of the [Great Sea](Great Sea) surrounding the southern tip of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), mostly noted by travelers on the southern coasts of [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale) and the port of [Booty Bay](Booty Bay). [Undermine](Undermine) is said to lie somewere in its vast unknown waters.

The South Seas also extends to [Tanaris](Tanaris). Of note are two islands southeast of [Lands End Beach](Lands End Beach). Although not visible on the normal overworld map, their location is apparent in an external map viewer. Like normal islands, they are surrounded by shallow water. However, it is impossible to get there by swimming alone, as [fatigue](fatigue) prevent you.

Using any combination of healing, epic mouths, sprinting, soul stones, resurrection, swiftness potions, and travel forms, it is possible to visit these islands. The two are identical in geography, with one being turned 90 degrees from the other. They are populated by Fire Rocs, hyenas, scorpions, and pirates.

On the second island is a mound of wood and shovels, possibly denoting a buried treasure.

At one point in the game, there used to be a bottle on the islands that was from the development team. It read, "What the heck are you doing here?" It's only remnants is the shadow cast by the bottle. The bottle itself, however, is missing.
