  **Mally O'Flor says:** While things seem to be going fairly well, it is quite obvious to many that Kul Tiras is still very stubborn on their ideals and traditions, their sanctity of honor, and respect for the fallen is on par with many of the most noble paladin.
  **Mally O'Flor says:** As of current, there has been no consensus, Stormwind is not looking to be dragged into a war without good reason. I am under good authority and good information to inform of no cooperation between Kul Tiras, and the Alliance in this matter. This 'invasion' you are facing is, by our eyes, up to Thrall to handle as he sees fit.
  **Mally O'Flor says:** The SI:7 has given me clear authority to pass along this information for the better interest of both parties. Bring this to your leader, and let him know.
