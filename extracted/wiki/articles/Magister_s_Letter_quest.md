## Description
<name>, the fall of our homeland has affected us all. But, even if our education down this path was cut short, there are still ways to learn.

Recently, someone left a letter on the desk and it was addressed to you.

Perhaps you should read it and see what it says?

## Rewards
You will receive:
- 400 XP
- +150 [reputation](reputation) with [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant)

## Progress
The Magisterium is the most prestigious group that a mage can be a part of.

## Completion
Ah, <name>, I take it you're here to become my apprentice?

If so, then know that I will not coddle you. You will be serving under a proud member of the Magisterium.

As such, you will be taught what I deem necessary for your progress. Continue to perform adequately and I shall continue to guide you.

Eventually, you will be fit enough for the Magisterium.

Talk to me again once you are prepared.
