**Stormwind** is the faction associated with [Stormwind City](Stormwind City), the capital of the [Alliance](Alliance) [human](human)s as well as the [Kingdom of Stormwind](Kingdom of Stormwind). It is located in the northwestern part of [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest). It is ruled by the young child king [Anduin Wrynn](Anduin Wrynn), regent Highlord [Bolvar Fordragon](Bolvar Fordragon), and [Lady Katrana Prestor](Lady Katrana Prestor).

## Reputation
[Clavicus Knavingham](Clavicus Knavingham) has the repeatable cloth quests to achieve a higher reputation with Stormwind. In return for exalted reputation, non-human players are able to ride [horse](horse)s.

Most quests associated with Stormwind come from the surrounding areas of Elwynn Forest, [Westfall](Westfall), [Duskwood](Duskwood), [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains) and [Northwind](Northwind).

## Rewards
At exalted, [Valiant](Valiant) offers a tabard and a unique epic mount, and non-Stormwind-human Alliance races are able to purchase and ride Horse mounts (Stormwind humans can ride Stormwind horses regardless of faction reputation). Stormwind is also unique in that it is the only racial player faction in the game to have more than one vendor available for their mounts.

Stormwind horses are bought from [Gregor MacVince](Gregor MacVince) in [Theramore](Theramore), [Katie Hunter](Katie Hunter) at [Eastvale Logging Camp](Eastvale Logging Camp), [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), [Unger Statforth](Unger Statforth) at [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor) in the [Wetlands](Wetlands) and [Nadia Geringt](Nadia Geringt) at [Mildenhall Horse Farm](Mildenhall Horse Farm) in [Northwind](Northwind). Please note that only Unger Statforth sells the [Black Stallion](Black Stallion), and Gregor MacVince does not give a Stormwind faction discount due to being affiliated with [Theramore](Theramore (faction)). Unarmored horses are available starting at level 40, with armored horses available at level 60.
