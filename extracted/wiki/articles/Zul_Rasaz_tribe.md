# Zul'Rasaz tribe

The **Zul'Rasaz tribe**, (ref: [New Zones & World Edits](https://web.archive.org/web/20260514170802/https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps). Archived from [the original](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps) on 2026-05-14. Retrieved on 2026-29-08.) also referred to as the **Wildtusk**, (ref: [Shadow Hunter Yin'do#Quotes](Shadow Hunter Yin'do#Quotes)) are a tribe of [forest troll](forest troll)s that inhabit the mountains between [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands) and the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands), formerly inhabiting the troll city of [Zul'Rasaz](Ruins of Zul'Rasaz). After the Amani empire was broken, the people of Zul'Rasaz fled into the mountains where they flourished for generations, maintaining a positive relationship with the local elementals and nurturing their growth. However, one of their own, [Speaker Gan'to](Speaker Gan'to), had fallen to the corruption of the Witherbark's lust for power. (ref: [quest: The Speaker's Betrayal]) The Zul'Rasaz tribe was razed and the survivors rebuilt a home in [Wildtusk Village](Wildtusk Village), eventually allying with the [Horde](Horde) for revenge against the traitor and the Witherbark.
