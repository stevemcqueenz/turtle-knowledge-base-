The **Tower of Ilgalar** is a mage tower in the eastern part of the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains) that was constructed by [Morganth](Morganth), a powerful sorcerer who controls the [Shadowhide clan](Shadowhide clan). After being exiled from the [Mage Quarter](Mage Quarter) of [Stormwind](Stormwind), he took up residence here where he continues his study of the dark arts.

By using a magical artifact in the [Tower of Azora](Tower of Azora) called the Eye of Azora, you are able to see the Tower of Ilgalar and its surroundings. There is, however, no such device in the Tower of Ilgalar. Throughout the Tower of Ilgalar are dragon bones hung on display. On the top level, a small alchemy table also holds a human skull and a dragon skull. It is not known what interest Morganth has in these relics.
