# Azotha

The **Azotha** were an ancient civilization to which [humanity](humanity) traces its early roots to. (ref: [Gheor from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/466626998593388579/838277039987097600) (dead link) - "The Azotha are the first men") (ref: [Lhian from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1085159575046606850) (dead link) - "well, azotha are ancient predecessor to humans")

A shamanistic culture, (ref: [Moriena from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/895751435797536788/1273330923764715654) (dead link) - "The current human predecessors, the Azotha, had shamanistic traits in their culture") they had established nations such as the kingdom of [Corthan](Corthan) in the modern day [Badlands](Badlands) (ref: [Moriena from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1274061839482753157) (dead link) - "Corthan was a kingdom of the Azotha") (ref: [The History of Corthan](The History of Corthan)) and waged war against the Zandalari. (ref: [quest: Azotha Gold]) However, the Azotha would later decline after the Sundering, and Corthan would eventually collapse and revert to a tribal state, with rebels fleeing north of the Badlands where they are believed to have established the Arathi dynasty. (ref: [Gheor from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1086439363199635568) (dead link) - "The Kingdom of Corthan predates the Arathor Empire. Corthan mentions rebels going up north of the now Badlands and implies they have gone back into a tribal state - thus being the first step towards the Empire of Arathor.")
