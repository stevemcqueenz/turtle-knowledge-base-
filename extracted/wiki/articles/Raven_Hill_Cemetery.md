# Raven Hill Cemetery

-Raven Hill Cemetery** is the sprawling graveyard located north of the abandoned town of [Raven Hill](Raven Hill) in [Duskwood](Duskwood). The cemetery is crawling with scores of [undead](undead) as well as massive [spider](spider)s and gangs of grave robbers who will attack anyone interrupting their morbid tasks. The hill in the northwest corner of the cemetery is [Forlorn Rowe](Forlorn Rowe), once the home of [Morbent Fel](Morbent Fel). Through the cemetery, the undead [Mor'Ladim](Mor'Ladim) patrols the cemetery and spells certain doom for adventurers he catches unaware.

At the northeast corner, past the quarter of the cemetery containing the entrance to [Dawning Woods Catacombs](Dawning Woods Catacombs), is the small shack of the Hermit [Abercrombie](Abercrombie).

The cemetery wasn't always so big, growing to dominate much of the area only after the region's corruption by dark forces. It is also currently abandoned, but wasn't always as such. When [Morgan Ladimore](Morgan Ladimore) came back from the [Third War](Third War) and saw the grave of his [wife](Lys Ladimore) and children, he wept and murdered a trio of the cemetery's attendants. (ref: [The Story of Morgan Ladimore](The Story of Morgan Ladimore))

## Known departed
-[Morgan Ladimore](Morgan Ladimore) was buried here after he committed suicide. 
-[Lys Ladimore](Lys Ladimore) along with two of her children are buried here.
-[Eliza](Eliza), Bride of the Embalmer once resided here.
