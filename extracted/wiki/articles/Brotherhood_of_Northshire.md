The **Brotherhood of Northshire** (formerly known as the **Holy Order of Northshire Clerics**) is a clerical order, comprised of clerics, priests, and paladins based at the [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey), in the [Northshire Valley](Northshire Valley) of [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest). It is a branch of the [Church of Light](Church of Light) dedicated to the cause of the [Kingdom of Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)).

For decades, the Holy Order of Northshire Clerics supported the citizens of the kingdom and ensured the protection of the realm alongside the [Stormwind Army](Stormwind Army). During the [First War](First War), the clerics were slaughtered by the orcs of the [Old Horde](Old Horde), their sacrifice for the kingdom would later inspire the Abbot of Northshire, Archbishop [Alonsus Faol](Alonsus Faol), to create the [Order of the Silver Hand](Order of the Silver Hand) during the [Second War](Second War).

Following the victory of the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron), Alonsus Faol restored the clerical order as the "Brotherhood of Northshire". Since then, its members continue to support the Church of Light and the people of Stormwind, notably by recruiting adventurers to deal with the threats to the kingdom, and by ensuring the training of young paladins of the Silver Hand and young priests of the clergy.
