# Death Knight

-Death Knights** were once virtuous defenders of [Human](Human)ity. However, once the [Paladin ranks](Knights of the Silver Hand) were disbanded by the failing [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron), many of these [holy warriors](Paladin) traveled to the quarantined lands to ease the suffering of those left within the [plague-ridden colonies](Plaguelands). Though the Paladins were immune to disease of any kind, they were persecuted by the general populace who believed that they had been infected by the foul [plague](Plague of Undeath). A small band of Paladins, embittered by society's cruelty, traveled north to find the plague's source. These renegade Paladins succumbed to bitter hatred over the course of their grueling quest. When they finally reached [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul)'s [icy fortress](Frozen Throne) in [Northrend](Northrend) they had become dark and brooding. The [Lich King](Lich King) offered them untold power in exchange for their services and loyalty. The weary, vengeful warriors accepted his dark pact, and although they retained their humanity, their twisted souls were bound to his evil will for all time. Bestowed with black, [vampiric Runeblades](Vampiric runeblade) and [shadowy steeds](Deathcharger), Death Knights serve as the [Scourge](Scourge)'s mightiest generals. (ref: )
