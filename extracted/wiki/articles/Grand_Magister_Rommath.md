Lord **Rommath** is the Grand Magister of the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant), one of the members of the [Regency Council](Regency Council) of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas), as well as the most powerful Magister after the fall on Quel'Thalas at the hands of the undead Scourge.

## Biography
Rommath was a member of the Magisterium, the Quel'dorei's traditional order of magi.

After the fall of Quel'Thalas, he eventually joined the Silvermoon Remnant. When Alah'Thalas was expanded beyond an outpost into a city, and the Thalassian Highlands resettled by high elf refugees, the surviving nobles pushed to remove Vereesa Windrunner from her position as Speaker of the Silvermoon Remnant, establishing the [Regency Council](Regency Council) in which Rommath would take part in along with Vereesa, Lor'themar Theron and other nobles.

Although Prince [Kael'thas Sunstrider](Kael'thas Sunstrider) is considered to be a traitor by the Remnant, Rommath remains secretly loyal to the Prince and keeps in contact with him, maintaining a line of communication between the Prince in Outland and the remaining blood elves in [Felstrider Retreat](Felstrider Retreat) (ref: [Rommath's Orders](Rommath's Orders)) (ref: [quest: An Audience With the Prince]) as Kael'thas continues to seek the path to salvation for his people.

When Lord [Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn) made a motion before the Council to be named Lord-Regent of Quel'Thalas following the retaking of [Shalandis Isle](Shalandis Isle) by the [Silver Covenant](Silver Covenant), Rommath voted in his favor.
