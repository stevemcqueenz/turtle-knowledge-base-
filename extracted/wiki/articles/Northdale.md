# Northdale

[Northdale](Northdale) is a town in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), north of [Light's Hope Chapel](Light's Hope Chapel) and southeast of the entrance to [Quel'thalas](Quel'thalas). It is where [Taelan Fordring](Taelan Fordring) renounced the [Order of the Silver Hand](Order of the Silver Hand) and joined the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade). The ruins of the town is inhabited by banshees and invisible eyeless watchers, and the nearby lake is populated by corrupted [water elemental](water elemental)s.
