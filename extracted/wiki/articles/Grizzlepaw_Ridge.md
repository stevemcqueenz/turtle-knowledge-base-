# Grizzlepaw Ridge

[Grizzlepaw Ridge](Grizzlepaw Ridge) is a mountainous area located south of [Thelsamar](Thelsamar) and east of [Stonesplinter Valley](Stonesplinter Valley) in the [dwarven](dwarven) territory of [Loch Modan](Loch Modan). It is inhabited by a large pack of [bear](bear)s, and most notably, the ferocious [Ol' Sooty](Ol' Sooty), who occasionally wanders down from his lair to the base of the valley, pouncing upon anyone unfortunate to irritate him.
