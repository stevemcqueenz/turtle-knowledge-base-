# Stoutlager Inn

The [Stoutlager Inn](Stoutlager Inn) in [Thelsamar](Thelsamar) features a few quest givers and several vendors, among them an innkeeper with the usual services. The inn is a popular place to spend some quality time with one's guildmates, drinking and chatting. It is one of the most popular places to go in [Loch Modan](Loch Modan).
