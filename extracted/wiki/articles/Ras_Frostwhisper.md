# Ras Frostwhisper

**Ras Frostwhisper** is the [Scourge](Scourge) [Lich](Lich) overseer of everything that happens in [Scholomance](Scholomance), he watches and teaches all those who rise to the position of 'Dark Master'.

Ras Frostwhisper was once a human from [Strom](Strom), who turned himself over to the [Lich King](Lich King) when the [Scourge](Scourge) invaded.  Upon the ground in [Stratholme](Stratholme), within the Slaughterhouse, Ras Frostwhisper took a dagger to his own neck and cut from one side to the other-- all the time with a smile upon his face.  His corpse fell onto [Menethil's Gift](Menethil's Gift) and it is there that the [Lich King](Lich King), [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul) raised his fallen corpse as a [Lich](Lich).
