# Northridge Point

**Northridge Point** is a watchtower located in northern [Northwind](Northwind) that has been taken over by a warband of [orc](orc)s of the [Blackrock clan](Blackrock clan) led by [Shuni Steeltooth](Shuni Steeltooth) that established a foothold in the region on the orders of [Overlord Throkk](Overlord Throkk). (ref: [Throkk's Command](Throkk's Command))
