The **blue dragonflight** is a draconic organization led by the eponymous [blue dragon](blue dragon)s to uphold their creed of managing and watching over the flow of magic on Azeroth. After the ordering of the planet, the titan [Norgannon](Norgannon) blessed [Malygos](Malygos) and his flight with power over the arcane and empowering him as guardian of magic and hidden arcanum. (ref: [Charge of the Dragonflights](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Charge_of_the_Dragonflights)) The Lord of Magic ruled the flight until the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients), when the crazed [Deathwing](Deathwing) used the Demon Soul to wipe out the majority of the blue dragons, leaving only Malygos both weakened and depressed with precious few eggs left. (ref: [War of the Ancients Trilogy](War of the Ancients Trilogy)) The flight then slowly but surely recovered over the millennia.

## Culture
### Nomenclature
Members of the blue dragonflight most often have names ending in "gos" for males, or "gosa" for females. Sometimes, synonyms of the word "blue", or terms relating to spellcasting find their way into their names as well.

### Membership
The blue dragonflight is primarily made up of, as the name implies, blue dragonkin. These include the blue dragons themselves, but also blue-scaled variants of drakonid and dragonspawn.

## History
### The Charge of the Dragonflights
Once the [Titan](Titan)s felt satisfied with their creation upon [Azeroth](Azeroth), they left to roam the cosmos in search of new worlds on which they would shape order from chaos, creating new life throughout a myriad of worlds. Before their departure from Azeroth however, the Titans empowered five dragons to become stewards of that world. Their purpose: to guard Azeroth as the Dragon Aspects, protecting the Titans' creation.

[Norgannon](Norgannon), the Titan lore keeper and master-magician, granted the blue dragon, [Malygos](Malygos), a portion of his vast power. From then on, Malygos would be known as the Spell-Weaver, the guardian of magic and hidden arcanum. (ref: 

### War of the Ancients
During the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients) when [Queen Azshara](Queen Azshara) and her Highborne opened a portal in the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity) to allow the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) access to the world of Azeroth, the Dragon Aspects congregated at the lair of Alexstrasza to discuss the coming invasion and what part they should play in defending the world. Neltharion proposed that to end the invasion of demons, the dragonflights should create a weapon with which they could eradicate the demon armies.

[Neltharion](Neltharion) persuaded Malygos to aid him in convincing the other Dragon Aspects to lend a part of their power to create the weapon, the [Dragon Soul](Dragon Soul). The artifact was a simple unassuming golden disc created by Neltharion. The Dragon Soul was to be used as a weapon against the demons. All the Dragon Aspects and their flights contributed a bit of their power in creating the Dragon Soul with the exception of Neltharion. Through the disk, Neltharion was able to magically take control of all other dragons including the other Dragon Aspects.

During one of the final battles the Dragon Aspects called all of their flights together and flew toward the battle where the Ancients had already perished and only night elves were holding back the Demons. With ease, a crazed Neltharion, now known as Deathwing, destroyed both demons and night elves by using the Dragon Soul — now known as the Demon Soul — against them. The other Aspects tried to stop Neltharion. Malygos's flight surrounded Deathwing in an attempt to take the Demon Soul from him. With a single command, they were engulfed by the magic of Demon Soul and were pulverized. Malygos was also terribly injured, both mentally and physically, but did not suffer the same fate as his flight.

Krasus helped ease this loss by going to Malygos' lair, taking eggs that still had life and storing them somewhere unknown; at the end of the war, he handed them over to Nozdormu.

### After the Sundering
Though Malygos was rarely seen after his retreat to the far north,<ref>[Legacy of the Aspects](Legacy of the Aspects)) mighty blue wyrms, such as [Sapphiron](Sapphiron) and [Azuregos](Azuregos), continued to search for and hoard artifacts of great magical power, keeping them from inexperienced hands. (ref: [The Return to Northrend (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Return_to_Northrend_(WC3_Undead))) (ref: [Game Guide/Raid Areas](https://web.archive.org/web/20051218072738/http://www.worldofwarcraft.com/info/basics/raidarea.html))

A thousand years before modern times, the almost-extinct blue dragons still managed to lend their aid to the [night elves](night elves) and the [bronze dragonflight](bronze dragonflight) during the [War of the Shifting Sands](War of the Shifting Sands). Led by [Arygos](Arygos), a child of Malygos, the blues fought the [silithid](silithid) and their [qiraji](qiraji) masters furiously and ultimately helped push them back to their city, where they were sealed away from the world. The dragons suffered few casualties, but the progeny of Malygos, Arygos, was believed lost, having charged deep into [Ahn'Qiraj](Ahn'Qiraj) during the final push. After the war, a shard of the [Scepter of the Shifting Sands](Scepter of the Shifting Sands) was entrusted to the blue wyrm [Azuregos](Azuregos) by Anachronos the bronze. (ref: *[The War of the Shifting Sands](The War of the Shifting Sands (short story))*) Unbeknownst to his flight, Arygos, along with Caelestrasz and Merithra, was not dead, simply captured. The dark master of Ahn'Qiraj used the tortured dragons to power his new creations: [Moam](Moam) and the other [obsidian destroyer](obsidian destroyer)s. (ref: [Game Guide/Monsters of Ahn'Qiraj/Moam](https://web.archive.org/web/20060427205417/http://wow-europe.com/en/info/basics/ahnqiraj/moam.html)) (ref: [Game Guide/Monsters of Ahn'Qiraj/Obsidian Destroyers](https://web.archive.org/web/20060428080912/http://wow-europe.com/en/info/basics/ahnqiraj/obsidian.html))

### Battle of Grim Batol
During the [Second War](Second War), [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza) was kidnapped by the [Dragonmaw](Dragonmaw clan) orcs and taken to [Grim Batol](Grim Batol). Her younger consort [Korialstrasz](Korialstrasz), known as Krasus when disguised as a [high elf](high elf), later visited Malygos to seek his aid in an attempt to free Alexstrasza. Although Malygos initially refused, Krasus convinced Malygos to assist him by revealing that [Deathwing](Deathwing) was still alive and that Deathwing had indirectly led the Dragonmaw orcs to find the [Demon Soul](Demon Soul). Krasus finally said that Alexstrasza could use her powers over life to grant Malygos a new dragonflight. Seeing an opportunity to avenge the death of his flight, see their resurrection, and redeem himself, Malygos agreed to rejoin the world and confront Deathwing. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*)

After Deathwing was defeated and the Demon Soul destroyed, Malygos seemed to reclaim the vitality of his former self. [Rhonin](Rhonin) the mage destroyed the artifact using a scale of Deathwing and with a spell, all the magic and power of the Demon Soul was transferred back to the other Dragon Aspects, returning their full power to them. Although still a sparsely populated dragonflight, the blue brood grows slowly, giving hope to Malygos. (ref: 

### Third War
Like the other dragonflights, the blue dragonflight did not play any significant role in the events of the Third War. [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil), first death knight of the [Lich King](Lich King), did, however, invade the icy lair of the blue dragon [Sapphiron](Sapphiron), where the wyrm had hoarded a great treasure and artifacts of significant power. The champion of the Lich King, assisted by the Crypt lord [Anub'arak](Anub'arak), quickly dispatched Sapphiron's brood and slew the ancient servant of Malygos, raising him in undeath as a mighty frost wyrm.

During the siege of Dalaran, two blue drakes were ironically held prisoners in Dalaran, being transformed into flying sheep.<ref>[The Siege of Dalaran (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Siege_of_Dalaran_(WC3_Undead)))

### World of Warcraft
[Arygos](Arygos), thought to have been lost during the [War of the Shifting Sands](War of the Shifting Sands), was found alive inside [Ahn'Qiraj](Ahn'Qiraj), but as a slave of [C'Thun](C'Thun), along with [Caelestrasz](Caelestrasz) and [Merithra](Merithra). Arygos' sister [Andorgos](Andorgos) came to Ahn'Qiraj after sensing her trapped brethren within, but dared not travel into the [temple](Temple of Ahn'Qiraj) for fear of falling under the [Old God](Old God)'s control. (ref: [quest: Enigma Circlet|class=mage])

[Azuregos](Azuregos) and his brood of [dragonspawn](dragonspawn) once guarded the ruins of the ancient [night elven](Night elf) city of [Eldarath](Eldarath) in [Azshara](Azshara). The city was rumored to hold many powerful [Highborne](Highborne) artifacts scattered throughout the ruins, and Azuregos would stop at nothing to safeguard these artifacts from falling into [mortal](mortal) hands. (ref:  At one point, he was sought out for the [Blue Scepter Shard](Blue Scepter Shard), only to tell the adventurers that he had passed it along to an [empowered minow](Maws) ages ago to spare himself the hassle.<ref>[quest: Azuregos's Magical Ledger])

The icy caverns of [Mazthoril](Mazthoril) in [Winterspring](Winterspring) are teeming with blue dragonkin under the leadership of the Matron Protectorate [Haleh](Haleh), [General Colbatann](General Colbatann), and the drake-champion [Scryer](Scryer). These dragonkin are fierce enemies of the [black dragonflight](black dragonflight) and desire to strike out against its new leaders; [Nefarian](Nefarian) and [Onyxia](Onyxia). At one point, they came under attack by Nefarian's dragonriders, coming in the darkness of night, and kidnapping whelps, such as [Awbee](Awbee), to be used in experiments to create a new chromatic dragonflight. (ref: [Awbee#Quotes](Awbee#Quotes))

While the [Dragon Aspects](Dragon Aspects) insist that the flights should stay out of the affairs of mortals, Haleh could not obey such order when learned of the horrors committed to her kind, and thus tasked the adventurers to make sure that Nefarian and his [chromatic dragonflight](chromatic dragonflight) were destroyed. (ref: [The Matron Protectorate quest chain](The Matron Protectorate quest chain)) When [Onyxia](Onyxia)'s deception was uncovered, the [Dragon's Eye](Fragment of the Dragon's Eye) she used was broken in the ensuing fight. (ref: [quest: The Dragon's Eye]) Using the blood of [General Drakkisath](General Drakkisath), Haleh transformed the broken pendant into the [Drakefire Amulet](Drakefire Amulet), which [Alliance](Alliance) heroes use to bypass the powerful wards protecting [Onyxia's Lair](Onyxia's Lair). (ref: [quest: Drakefire Amulet])

### Mysteries of Azeroth
Citing mortal foolishness that brought the [Great Sundering](Great Sundering), the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) and all that came with it, Lord Malygos decided that mortals were no longer worthy of the responsibility of magic, and thus on his command the blue flight began taking measures to deprive the mortal races of the Arcane, dampening magic within various regions of Azeroth. (ref: [quest: Through a Glimmering Light]) Amongst the affected regions were [Azshara](Azshara), where the energy within the region has been slowly fading away, to the alarm of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) of [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)). (ref: [quest: The Azshara Dampening]) (ref: [quest: The Dampening Mystery]) At the Kirin Tor's behest, [Lieutenant Azsalus](Lieutenant Azsalus) was slain to put an end to the dampening. (ref: [quest: The Dampening Must End])

[Ley-Watcher Incantigos](Ley-Watcher Incantigos), seeking to make a name for himself within the flight by advancing the cause of the great dampening, departed for the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms). There, he sought out the chaotic power and unstable leylines that ran through [Karazhan](Karazhan) in order to harness and untangle them in order to earn the right to meet Malygos. (ref: [quest: Asleep Under Snow])

For unknown purposes, the blue dragonflight called down an arcane meteor on Azeroth, which impacted in [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast) in what is now known as [Starshard Cradle](Starshard Cradle). (ref: [Moriena from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/895751435797536788/1496783160149540866) - "its a meteor called down by the blue dragons") Following the meteor crash, a brood of the azure flight led by the blue dragon [Avranagos](Avranagos) occupied the island of [Vysnagosa's Rest](Vysnagosa's Rest), while the blue drake [Starcaller](Starcaller)'s forces at the crater drove away anything that came too close to the impact site - adventurers, scholars and even the naga learned to give it a wide berth. (ref: [quest: A Star That Calls Back])
