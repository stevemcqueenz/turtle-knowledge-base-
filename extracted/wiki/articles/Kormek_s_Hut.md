# Kormek's Hut

**Kormek's Hut** is a small hovel in north-central [Desolace](Desolace)(62,39). It is currently inhabited by [Willow](Willow), formerly of the [Twilight's Hammer](Twilight's Hammer), and [Bibbly F'utzbuckle](Bibbly F'utzbuckle) who greatly enjoys dancing around in the pen outside. Judging by the hut's architecture and by the linguistic feel of Kormek's name, it is likely that he was a [centaur](centaur), possibly of a rogue affiliation. Willow no doubt chased him off in order to secure the hut for herself. Kormek may be the [Centaur Pariah](Centaur Pariah), who is homeless and roaming around southern Desolace.
