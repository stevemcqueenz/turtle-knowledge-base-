# Mazthoril

-Mazthoril** is a huge and deep icy cavern - populated by the Cobalt Brood. Within these caverns lies the true stronghold of the [blue dragon](blue dragon)s, the forces of [General Colbatann](General Colbatann). Guarding this stronghold is an entire legion of [dragonspawn](dragonspawn) sworn to protect Mazthoril. Night elf forces - including those rare few [wintersaber](wintersaber) riders who can tame the feral frostsaber cats - often clash with the dragonspawn legion, using blade and fang in an attempt to rid themselves of the malevolent dragons.

The blue dragons here are primarily on watch for activity from the black dragonflight, although there have recently been concerns that the blue dragonflight will be needed to assist the dragons at Mount Hyjal if the demons in Darkwhisper Gorge attack. Since Deathwing's disappearance after the battle of Grim Batol, the black dragonflight's members have been busy fighting each other for dominance. To this end, the blue dragonflight organizes an army here, lead by the dragonspawn General Cobaltann, to protect the world from the increasing threat of the black dragons. Specifically, Cobaltann focuses on the protection of dragon eggs, which are being stolen by the blacks for some evil purpose.

Their matriarch being the radiant [Haleh](Haleh) - only encountered in [high elf](high elf) form at the peak of the cavern, and accessible by a portal rune in the cavern floor. It is she who completes the key to [Onyxia's Lair](Onyxia's Lair) for [Alliance](Alliance) heroes. Haleh has four powerful [drake](drake)s who guard Mazthoril, and leads a sizable army of highly sorcerous spawn and young whelps. [Spellmaw](Spellmaw) is the brood's main patrol, and wanders a slow path around a good third of the lower section of [Winterspring](Winterspring). [Manaclaw](Manaclaw) and [Scryer](Scryer) guard the runes at the rear of the cavern. [Azurous](Azurous) patrols nearby regions in Winterspring for threats against the forces of [Malygos](Malygos). [General Colbatann](General Colbatann), a notable general of the blue flight, has also been spotted near Mazthoril.
