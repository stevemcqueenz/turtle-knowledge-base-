# Slickwick Oil Rig

The **Slickwick Oil Rig** is an oil field located in southern [Tanaris](Tanaris) and is the home of new goblin operations for the [Durotar Labor Union](Durotar Labor Union). Fueled by their lust of greed, the DLU is looking to strike it big once again.
