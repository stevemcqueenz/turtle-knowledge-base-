The **Argent Dawn** (referred to as simply the **Dawn**) (ref: [Gregor Greystone#Quotes](Gregor Greystone#Quotes)) is an organization focused on protecting [Azeroth](Azeroth) from agencies that seek to destroy it, such as the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) and the [Scourge](Scourge). As a worldwide organization maintaining a status of neutrality, the Argent Dawn is not a political body, nor do their members ever wish to become one. Their cause is to fight swiftly and mercilessly against any element of evil that surfaced in Azeroth. To that end, they call both the [Alliance](Alliance) and the [Horde](Horde) allies in the cause of stamping out evil and therefore recruit from all races without distinction. (ref: [Argent Officer Pureheart#Quotes](Argent Officer Pureheart#Quotes)) Their base camps can be found across all the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands) as well as in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), while their agents are also present in [Stormwind City](Stormwind City), [Darnassus](Darnassus), and [Everlook](Everlook).

## Background
  ***S**ome say that it is always darkest just before the dawn. Whenever darkness falls, the light of hope will drive away the shadows; wherever the forces of evil work in secret, the brave men and women of the Argent Dawn will be there to thwart their dark designs. The Argent Dawn is an organization that makes no distinction between Alliance or Horde, but instead tries to unite the people of Azeroth in the fight against evil. Though the agents of the Argent Dawn are always wary of the sinister and the wicked in all their various disguises, they have recently focused their efforts on a region of Azeroth where evil has run unchecked for a long time: the Plaguelands of Lordaeron. Detachments of the Argent Dawn are guarding the entrances to the Plaguelands at the Bulwark between the Tirisfal Glades and the western Plaguelands, and at Chillwind Point, a narrow pass leading south from the eastern Plaguelands to the Alterac Mountains. They have also gained a foothold deep in the eastern Plaguelands at the Light's Hope Chapel, east of Corin’s Crossing.*

  *The Argent Dawn is always looking for more fighters to join their cause, and the numbers of those wearing the Argent Dawn Commission, the organization’s insignia, are growing each day. Adventurers seeking to aid the Argent Dawn by rooting out the Scourge’s corruption in the Plaguelands and taking on the Lich King’s powerful servants in the Scholomance and Stratholme will be well rewarded; the organization has many powerful enchanters among their ranks who craft special enhancements that provide the wearer with added protection against all forms of magical attacks. The Argent Dawn also offers those who have proven their dedication to their cause insight into their craftsmen’s designs, enabling these adventurers to craft the armors the Argent Dawn created in their ongoing campaign against evil.* (ref: [World of Warcraft - Classic Factions - Argent Dawn](https://web.archive.org/web/20060218080014/http://www.wow-europe.com/en/info/basics/factions/argent/index.html) (Retrieved 21st Feb 2006))

## History
### Founding
The Argent Dawn was originally formed as a splinter faction of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade). The Scarlet Crusade was formed by Highlord [Alexandros Mograine](Alexandros Mograine), [High General Abbendis](High General Abbendis) and [Grand Inquisitor Isillien](Grand Inquisitor Isillien) during the [Third War](Third War). Under Mograine's leadership, the Scarlet Crusade was at first a noble order, and Mograine was dubbed the Ashbringer as it would be all that remained of the undead after he would walk into whole battalions of undead and emerge unscathed. However, Mograine proved to be the lynchpin of the Crusade, and when joined by [Saidan Dathrohan](Saidan Dathrohan) - who was in fact impersonated by the dreadlord [Balnazzar](Balnazzar) - corruption began to spread throughout the Crusade, planting the seeds of madness and insane zealotry that the order became infamous for. (ref: [Corrupted Ashbringer/Scarlet Monastery Event](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Corrupted_Ashbringer/Scarlet_Monastery_Event))

Becoming Grand Crusader of the Scarlet Crusade, Dathrohan made a bargain with [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) and arranged for Alexandros to be ambushed during a foray in [Stratholme](Stratholme). While the undead attack was not enough, it exhausted him enough that his son [Renault](Renault Mograine) was able to claim the Ashbringer and drive it through his father's heart while his back was turned. The heinous act of betrayal corrupted the blade and led to Mograine's own corruption as a death knight of the Scourge and champion of Kel'Thuzad. (ref: 

[High Inquisitor Fairbanks](High Inquisitor Fairbanks), who had bore witness to the Highlord's death, returned to the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery) - much to Renault's dismay. Shouting and screaming, he told the tale of the lesser Mograine's betrayal to any and all who would hear him. While Fairbanks was murdered in cold blood for his actions and dragged to a hidden chamber of the Monastery Cathedral, some did listen to his words, and thus they separated from the Crusade to form the Argent Dawn.

During the [Scourge Invasion](Scourge Invasion), several regions of [Azeroth](Azeroth) came under attack by Scourge forces. Subsequently, the Argent Dawn organized a worldwide counter to the undead invasion, keeping an eye out for any [necropolis](necropolis) sightings and passing on their information to all adventurers willing to aid them in their struggle. As more and more invasion attempts were beaten back by the defenders, the Argent Dawn was able to bestow increasingly more powerful blessings upon those fighting the invaders. Those who wished to take up arms against the undead invaders could speak with a representative of the organization to learn what regions needed help and how the defense was holding up. When necropolises appeared outside the [Alliance](Alliance) and [Horde](Horde) capitals, the Argent Dawn heeded their desperate call for help, setting up camps inside the besieged cities and rallying the defense against the undead. (ref: [Archived page](http://web.archive.org/web/20060826233658/http://www.worldofwarcraft.com:80/info/events/scourgeinvasion/))

Regardless of their influence, the Argent Dawn did not have the manpower of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade), and could not stand alone against the [Scourge](Scourge). A sub-faction of the Dawn, named the [Brotherhood of the Light](Brotherhood of the Light) under the lead of [Commander Eligor Dawnbringer](Commander Eligor Dawnbringer), worked to keep relations between both organizations from growing violent. (ref: [quest: Echoes of War]) As such, they formed an uneasy alliance in order to focus their combined strength against [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad)'s forces in the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands).

When they learned that the [necromancer](necromancer)s of the [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned) were creating their own [plagued dragon](plagued dragon)s in [Scholomance](Scholomance), they worked to destroy the creatures before they could be deployed. (ref: [quest: Plagued Hatchlings])

Despite their tenuous alliance, [Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff](Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff) condemns the Scarlet Crusade as blasphemers and directs adventurers to slay [Archivist Galford](Archivist Galford) and burn down his archives, in order to take away their most valued documents and punish them for their heretical zealotry. (ref: [quest: The Archivist]) When the heroes confronted [Grand Crusader Dathrohan](Grand Crusader Dathrohan), they discovered that was being impersonated by the [nathrezim](nathrezim) [Balnazzar](Balnazzar), and brought back his [head](Head of Balnazzar) as proof of the demon's masquerade. This discovery shocked Zverenhoff who pondered what the ramifications of Balnazzar's corruption would have on the zealots should the truth come out. (ref: [quest: The Truth Comes Crashing Down]) Duke Zverenhoff later ordered the heroes to slay [Baron Rivendare](Baron Rivendare), (ref: [quest: Above and Beyond]) and those who accomplished such a task were rewarded by [Lord Maxwell Tyrosus](Lord Maxwell Tyrosus) himself with an access to the [Argent Hold](Argent Hold). (ref: [quest: The Argent Hold])

## Reputation
Reputation with the Argent Dawn can be gained in four ways.

### Killing Scourge
The primary method of gaining favor is to kill undead Scourge across the Plaguelands, [Scholomance](Scholomance), [Stratholme](Stratholme) and [Naxxramas](Naxxramas). Non-elite mobs will stop giving reputation once halfway through friendly, save for [Shadowmage](Shadowmage)s that continue even though honored. Elite mobs stop giving reputation once halfway through honored. It is recommended to max out reputation via killing before progressing on to Scourgestone quests.

### Scourgestones
At level 50, players will be able to receive missions from the Argent Dawn. [Argent Officer Pureheart](Argent Officer Pureheart) at [Chillwind Camp](Chillwind Camp) and [Argent Officer Garush](Argent Officer Garush) at the [Bulwark](Bulwark) can give out an [Argent Dawn Commission](Argent Dawn Commission) trinket that allows players to loot [Scourgestone](Scourgestone)s when killing agents of the Scourge throughout the [Western](Western Plaguelands) and [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands) while equipped. Scourgestones can be turned in at the Bulwark, Chillwind Camp, or Light's Hope Chapel to raise your reputation with the Dawn.

The quest [quest: The Active Agent] rewards the player with either [Seal of the Dawn](Seal of the Dawn) or [Rune of the Dawn](Rune of the Dawn), each acting as alternative trinkets to the Argent Dawn Commendation while granting extra bonuses against the undead. [Mark of the Champion](Mark of the Champion), from [quest: The Fall of Kel'Thuzad], is another powerful alternative.

There are three types of Scourgestones, each requiring a different amount to be able to be turned in for reputation and an [Argent Dawn Valor Token](Argent Dawn Valor Token), which can be used either to further increase your reputation or used as currency to buy special items at higher reputation levels.
- [Minion's Scourgestone](Minion's Scourgestone) - Collect 20
- [Invader's Scourgestone](Invader's Scourgestone) - Collect 10
- [Corruptor's Scourgestone](Corruptor's Scourgestone) - Collect 1

### Cauldron Quests
Upon completion of the Plague Cauldrons questline, you will be able to obtain a [Vitreous Focuser](Vitreous Focuser) from either  in [Chillwind Camp](Chillwind Camp) or  at the [Bulwark](Bulwark), which will allow you to collect Osseous Agitators, Somatic Intensifiers, and Ectoplasmic Resonators. These, combined with runecloth and an [Arcane Quickener](Arcane Quickener) purchased from the same NPC, will allow you to change the composition of the plague that the cauldrons create into something that negatively affects the Scourge, which will earn you additional reputation with the Argent Dawn.

The four cauldrons are located at:
- [Felstone Field](Felstone Field)
- [Dalson's Tears](Dalson's Tears)
- [Writhing Haunt](Writhing Haunt)
- [Gahrron's Withering](Gahrron's Withering)

### Healthy Dragon Scales
Once you have completed the entire Egg-Freezing quest line (which starts with [Tinkee Steamboil](http://www.thottbot.com/?n=7956) in the Burning Steppes and eventually takes you to [Betina Bigglezink](http://www.thottbot.com/?n=4715) in the Eastern Plaguelands), you will be able to receive [Healthy Dragon Scales](http://www.thottbot.com/?i=21977) as drops from the Plagued Whelps in [Scholomance](Scholomance). As only a few may drop per run and the favor received is equal to that of a Valor Token or turning in a load of Scourgestones, it is not a very productive way to farm reputation.
