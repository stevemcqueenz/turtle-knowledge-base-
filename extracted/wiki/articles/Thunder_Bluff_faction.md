**Thunder Bluff** is the faction of the [Tauren](Tauren) capital city [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff) located in the northern part of the region of [Mulgore](Mulgore). The whole of the city is built on bluffs several hundred feet above the surrounding landscape, and is accessible by elevators on the southwestern and northeastern sides.

## Reputation
[Rumstag Proudstrider](Rumstag Proudstrider) has the Thunder Bluff repeatable cloth quests used by non-tauren Horde players to obtain the right to ride [kodo](kodo)s at exalted.

Surrounding zones Mulgore and [The Barrens](The Barrens) have the most quests for gaining reputation with Thunder Bluff. 

## NPCs
NPCs aligned with the Thunder Bluff faction are mostly found in [Desolace](Desolace), [Feralas](Feralas), [Mulgore](Mulgore), [The Barrens](The Barrens), [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles) and [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff). Less common areas include [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), [Felwood](Felwood) and [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains).

## Rewards
Tauren can ride [kodo mounts](kodo mounts) by default, with a normal mount at level 30. Epic kodo mounts can be taught to ride at level 60.

Other Horde races who are  with Thunder Bluff can also learn to ride kodo mounts.
