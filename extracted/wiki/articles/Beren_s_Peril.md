# Beren's Peril

-Beren's Peril** is located [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest) due south of [Ambermill](Ambermill), between the main road and the side-road coming from Ambermill in the hills. Before the coming of the plague, this cave was home to creatures of the forest, and was sometimes used as a retreat for the wizards of [Dalaran](Dalaran). When the [Scourge](Scourge) invaded [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), however, the cave's creatures were butchered and consumed by ravenous ghouls and skeletal warriors. It has remained in the dark grip of the undead ever since, and poses a threat to the nearby [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) camps as well as to travelers using the road below.
