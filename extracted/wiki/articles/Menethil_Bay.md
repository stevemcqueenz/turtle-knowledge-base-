# Menethil Bay

[Menethil Bay](Menethil Bay) is a portion of the [Great Sea](Great Sea) off the west coast of central [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), mostly noted by travelers on the western coast of the [Wetlands](Wetlands) around [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor). It is crawling with [crocolisks](crocolisks) of various ages and sizes, often hunted for their fine leather.
