# Writhing Deep

The **Writhing Deep** is the site of a [silithid](silithid) infestation in southeastern [Feralas](Feralas). It is a specific area for several quests in the zone. [Raschal the Courier](Raschal the Courier) seem to somehow found his way down the hive.

It could also be considered to be Hive Zukk'ash, a minor silithid hive, considering the name of the silithid infesting the area.
