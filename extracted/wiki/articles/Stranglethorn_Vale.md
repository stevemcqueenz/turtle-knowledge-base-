**Stranglethorn Vale** (also simply called **Stranglethorn**), sometimes spelled **Stranglethorne**, (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* manual, *[Orc Units](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Wyvern_Rider)*, Wyvern Rider) is a vast jungle at the southern tip of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), south of [Duskwood](Duskwood).

The Vale was the primary center of the trolls of the [Gurubashi Empire](Gurubashi Empire), and their cities were dotted all over the region. After the Empire's fall the cities of the troll empire fell into ruin, although they are still populated by trolls. In recent years, the [Human](Human)s of [Stormwind](Stormwind) attempted to forge trade routes through this savage jungle. Though they managed to establish the small port town of [Blackwater Cove](Blackwater Cove), they were quickly overwhelmed by a crazed pack of Trolls and driven back to their homeland. In the wake of the Humans' departure, the crafty Goblin Trade Princes arrived on the jungle's shores and quickly set up their mining and deforesting operations. The Goblins have maintained a tenuous hold over these remote facilities, but they remain threatened by the Jungle Trolls' growing obsession with purging their ancient lands of any and all foreign invaders.

Stranglethorn is as deadly as it is beautiful. It is inhabited by a large variety of beasts and hostile creatures that low level characters should not face alone. The primary sapient inhabitants are the [Bloodscalp](Bloodscalp tribe) and [Skullsplitter](Skullsplitter tribe) trolls, in the northwest and east respectively. The troll city of [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub) sits in a valley in the northeast, which was once the capital of Troll civilization in Stranglethorn. In the north is a small compound controlled by the renegade [Colonel Kurzen](Colonel Kurzen). Other inhabitants include an encampment of [ogre](ogre)s. Off the southeast coast is [Jaguero Isle](Jaguero Isle), inhabited by the enormous gorilla [King Mukla](King Mukla). There is a renegade group of sailors, known as the [Bloodsail Buccaneers](Bloodsail Buccaneers), who have built camps on the southwestern and southern shores of Stranglethorn Vale. A small population of [naga](naga) can be found in the southwest, and [murloc](murloc)s inhabit some underwater ruins in the northwest. The [Venture Company](Venture Company) also has two mining operations in Stranglethorn. In the northwest, the [Zandalar Tribe](Zandalar Tribe) has its base in [Yojamba Isle](Yojamba Isle). The water gate from the [Deadmines](Deadmines) can also been seen north of Yojamba Isle, though it cannot be entered.

The Green Hills of Stranglethorn and Big Game Hunter quests also now reward different items upon completion.
