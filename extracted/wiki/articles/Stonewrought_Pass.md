# Stonewrought Pass

The **Stonewrought Pass** is a tunnel in [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge) that leads northeast to [Loch Modan](Loch Modan). The pass is located at the northern end of [Dustfire Valley](Dustfire Valley), and ends at the [Valley of Kings](Valley of Kings).
