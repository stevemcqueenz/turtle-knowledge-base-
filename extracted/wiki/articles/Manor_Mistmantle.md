# Manor Mistmantle

-Manor Mistmantle** is the family stead of the Mistmantle family. It is located in northeastern [Duskwood](Duskwood), north of [Darkshire](Darkshire) and west of [Beggar's Haunt](Beggar's Haunt). The lone survivor of the Mistmantle legacy is [Stalvan Mistmantle](Stalvan Mistmantle), a former school teacher and personal tutor who is now an agent of the [undead](undead). The manor grounds are rotten and overrun by Stalvan's [ghoul](ghoul)ish minions. The only sign of life on the property is a single flower in the garden, known as the [Tear of Tilloa](Tear of Tilloa).
