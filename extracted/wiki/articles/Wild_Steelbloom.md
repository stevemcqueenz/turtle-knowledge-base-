# Wild Steelbloom

-Wild Steelbloom** is an [Herb](Herb) that can be gathered with [Herbalism](Herbalism) (115), or can be bought in limited quantities from selected [Vendors](Vendors).

## Source
Wild Steelbloom can be found on steep hills. It is known for being unusually difficult to reach, usually requiring more navigation around steep slopes in order to reach it. It can be found in the following zones - [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains), [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), [Badlands](Badlands), [The Barrens](The Barrens), [Desolace](Desolace), [Duskwood](Duskwood), [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills), [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains), [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles) and [Wetlands](Wetlands).
