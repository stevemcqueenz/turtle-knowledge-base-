**Aviana** was a winged demigoddess, mistress of the Mother Tree [G'Hanir](G'Hanir).  She was killed in the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients).

## Gift of Goddesshood
Aviana was formerly a mere raven of the wild. One day [Elune](Elune) summoned the raven to her for a special delivery. The raven sent the message of Elune to [Cenarius](Cenarius). Doing her job well for years, Elune asked the raven to serve as messenger to other deities and [eternals](eternals). Each deity would greet and reward Aviana with special gifts required to complete her tasks. These gifts evolved the raven, and she became an equal to the deities.

## Interactions with Mortals
Aviana was known to many races including [trolls](trolls), [elves](elf), [tauren](tauren), and [dwarves](dwarves), often taking the form of those races to spy from within. 

Aviana grew curious of the [night elves](night elf) in particular, and joined the temple of Elune in the form of an night elf.  When [Azshara](Queen_Azshara) and her servants delved deeper into the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity)’s magic, Aviana could sense the corruption of the reckless use of its magics, and fled.

## War of the Ancients
During the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients), Aviana served as a tactical spy of Cenarius, who sent her to watch over the [demons](demons) of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) and act as messenger. When humans or Night Elves were in danger, Aviana would take the form of a soldier or friendly shape to help other elves get away from [Doomguard](Doomguard)s and [Felhunter](Felhunter)s that were tracking and chasing them. Her duties were vital to save many lives.

During this conflict Aviana was killed when a large group of demons attacked her. The [Doomguard](Doomguard) surrounded her and pierced her body with their spears. As the blood spilled from her dying body, the demons that came in contact with it became infested with its blessed powers and were purified. When Aviana died, her realm tree G'Hanir died with her. 

According to the [War of the Ancients Trilogy](War of the Ancients Trilogy) by writer Richard A. Knaak, Aviana is also the mother of the [Harpy](Harpy) race. Although according to some legends some harpies are corrupted night elves.
