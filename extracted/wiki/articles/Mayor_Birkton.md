-Mayor Lydia Birkton** (ref: [quest: Deathcap And Widow's Frill]) is a [human](human) quest giver located in [Ambershire](Ambershire) in [Northwind](Northwind). Prior to becoming mayor of Ambershire, she was part of [the Witch of Northwind](the Witch of Northwind)'s coven.
