-Pysan the Righteous** is a [human](human) located in [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast).

## Quotes
  -Gossip**
I am the great Pysan!

Known far and wide as the Righteous to my allies, and the Vengeant to my enemies! I have battled dragons atop mountains, rescued maidens from towers both high and precarious, and struck down evildoers too numerous to count!

I have faced the dreaded hydra Trioch and lived to tell the tale, ventured into the darkest depths of Maraudon, and brought justice to the wicked lurking in Razorfen Downs!

Tales of my deeds spread far and wide, sung by bards in every tavern and hall!

And yet... ahem... despite all this glory, I must ask - have you, by some miracle, happened to see my sword anywhere?

## Trivia
- Pysan's name and dialogue is in reference to [Pysan's Lost Girdle](Pysan's Lost Girdle) and [Pysan's Old Greatsword](Pysan's Old Greatsword), respectively rare Bind on Equip drops from [Maraudon](Maraudon) and [Razorfen Downs](Razorfen Downs), respectively. [Pysan's New Greatsword](Pysan's New Greatsword) was added to the game as a drop from [Trioch the Devourer](Trioch the Devourer) in [Timbermaw Hold](Timbermaw Hold (raid)).
