# Mystic Ward

**The Mystic Ward** was established for the study of magic. It features a number of merchants that cater to mages, as well as [trainers](trainers) for [mage](mage)s, [warlocks](warlocks), [paladin](paladin)s, and [priest](priest)s, who reside in the eldritch [Hall of Mysteries](Hall of Mysteries). A number of very powerful magic users reside within this area, and there are a few shops that sell magical goods.

To get to the Mystic Ward, you can head north from the [Commons](Commons), northwest from the [Great Forge](Great Forge), or west from the [Forlorn Cavern](Forlorn Cavern).
