# Tor'kren Farm

Located near [Thunder Ridge](Thunder Ridge) in northwestern [Durotar](Durotar), **Tor'kren Farm** hasn't seen as much profit as other [pig farm](pig farm)s in the area. Previously run by [Misha Tor'kren](Misha Tor'kren) and her son, the fate of the farm is uncertain after the younger orc ventured out to hunt [crocolisk](crocolisk)s at the [Southfury River](Southfury River).
