# Xavian

-Xavian** is located in northeastern [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), north of [Satyrnaar](Satyrnaar) and west of [Forest Song](Forest Song). It is one of the three Ashenvale [satyr](satyr) settlements, the other two being [Satyrnaar](Satyrnaar) and [Night Run](Night Run). Of the three, Xavian seems to be the center of satyr political power in Ashenvale, as both [Prince Raze](Prince Raze) and [Geltharis](Geltharis) live here. It is also the site of a [dryad](dryad) attack, and questing adventurers will find the mortally wounded [Anilia](Anilia) lying on the ground here.

Xavian is named after [Xavius](Xavius), one of the night elves responsible for summoning the burning legion through a portal manifested from the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity) in [Zin-Azshari](Zin-Azshari) in what became later known as the War of the Ancients. [Sargeras](Sargeras) transformed Xavius into the first satyr when he became unhappy with his progress with the portal. With as close as Xavian is to Azshara, it's easy to assume that this is why it's named so.
