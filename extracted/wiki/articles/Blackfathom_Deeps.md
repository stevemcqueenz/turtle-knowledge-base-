# History
  *Situated along the [Zoram Strand](Zoram Strand) of [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), Blackfathom Deeps was once a glorious temple dedicated to the night elves' moon-goddess, [Elune](Elune). However, the great Sundering shattered the temple - sinking it beneath the waves of the [Veiled Sea](Veiled Sea). There it remained untouched - until, drawn by its ancient power - the [naga](naga) and [satyr](satyr) emerged to plumb its secrets. Legends hold that the ancient beast, [Aku'mai](Aku'mai), has taken up residence within the temple's ruins. Aku'mai, a favored pet of the primordial Old Gods, has preyed upon the area ever since. Drawn to Aku'mai's presence, the cult known as the [Twilight's Hammer](Twilight's Hammer) has also come to bask in the Old Gods' evil presence.*
