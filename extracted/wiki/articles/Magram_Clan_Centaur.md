# Magram Clan Centaur

The Magram Clan centaurs are one of four groups of centaurs in the [Desolace](Desolace) region.  The Magram are in the far eastern end of the zone.

There is a quest that requires you to gain friendly relations with either the Magram or the Gelkis.  To gain faction with the Magram, you must kill a LOT of Gelkis, and vice versa.  The quests have several steps and those steps differ slightly depending on which faction you choose to ally yourself with.
