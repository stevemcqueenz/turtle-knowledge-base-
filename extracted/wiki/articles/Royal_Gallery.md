# Royal Gallery

The **Royal Gallery** is beyond the small courtyard park, next to the [Royal Library](Royal Library). The gallery features grandoise statues of such illustrious personas as [Mara Fordragon](Mara Fordragon), [King Llane](King Llane), and [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore).
