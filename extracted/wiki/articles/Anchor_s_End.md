Slay 8 Expedition Sailors, 6 Expedition Clerics, and 10 Expedition Soldiers for Warbringer Dar'nakk in Razor Hill.

## Description
There is an island south of the Echo Isles the pinkskins have come to call Anchor's Edge.

The time for planning and talking is over.

Travel to Anchor's Edge with as many comrades as you can find and kill every Kul Tiran armed with either blade or spell. Take the rest prisoner. That is my command!

GO FORTH FOR THE HORDE, FOR BLOOD AND THUNDER!

## Progress
Have they been overcome, have they been destroyed?!

## Completion
If my duties did not keep me here, I would have gladly joined you in the thrill of battle.

You have delivered the Warchief's reckoning to our enemies. For that, the whole Horde shall sing your praise.
