# Terrordale

[Terrordale](Terrordale), nestling in the northwest corner of [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), is now a ghost town, one of the first to be overrun by the [Scourge](Scourge). It lies west of [Plaguewood](Plaguewood) and [Stratholme](Stratholme), near the entrance to a tunnel that leads to the banks of the [Thondroril River](Thondroril River).

Much like the town of [Darkshire](Darkshire) may once have been called [Sunnydale](Sunnydale), [Terrordale](Terrordale) likely had a much less morbid name before the invasion of the [Scourge](Scourge). The game and its associated [lore](lore) give no clues as to the haunted town's previous name.

No facilities in [Terrordale](Terrordale) remain for players to use, aside for one delusional fungi merchant, but there are a few quests to be obtained or carried out here.

This area is some particular interest to those that are collecting [Crypt Fiend Parts](Crypt Fiend Parts), as [Crypt Fiend](Crypt Fiend)s are very common in Terrordale and its nearby cave, [Terrorweb Tunnel](Terrorweb Tunnel).  Wandering the withered grounds of this area is also the neutral level 60 [Plagued Swine](Plagued Swine), considered a very good choice as a pet for Hunters.
