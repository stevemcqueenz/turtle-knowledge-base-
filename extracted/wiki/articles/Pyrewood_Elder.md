**Pyrewood Elders** are [human](human)s found in [Pyrewood Village](Pyrewood Village) in [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest). At night, they transform into their [worgen](worgen) form as **[Moonrage Elder](Moonrage Elder)s**.

## Abilities
- **Lesser Heal** — Heal your target for 143 to 165.
- **Shadow Word: Pain** — A word of darkness that causes 60 Shadow damage over 18 sec.

## Objective of
- Drop [Pyrewood Shackle](Pyrewood Shackle) for [quest: Arugal's Folly].

## Notes
- They are affiliated with [Stormwind](Stormwind (faction)) during daytime to make them unattackable by Alliance players.
