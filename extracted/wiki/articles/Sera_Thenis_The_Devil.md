# Sera'Thenis, The Devil

You see a wanted poster, plastered around various towns in **Kalimdor**. There are numerous faces on it, each differing greatly more than the last; one has white short hair, another has purple hair with a ponytail. In the middle, however, is one with black hair that seems to be the most likely description. The bounty is a staggering 800 gold! You decide to read the text:

-"WANTED: "**The Devil**": notorious Kaldorei murderer and man of many faces, has been wanted for the last 30 years, throughout Northern Kalimdor. The individual known as "**The Devil**" is in hiding somewhere. Despite his numerous crimes which include kidnapping, thievery, and murder he is yet to be caught. It is a known fact he changes his face at a whim, and his current whereabouts are unknown, as is his Allegiance.  What his motives are, if there are any, is also unknown."*

Company president, cold-blooded killer, thieving bastard, gang-leader and more; this is one strange elf. His real name is ***Cynthras Starfire***, however few know of this fact. Currently going by the name of ***Sera'Thenis Uldreiyn***, or just Sera, the 376 year old wanted kaldorei is hiding out in Stormwind. He is the leader of [The Unseen / Whitestone Trading Co.](The Unseen / Whitestone Trading Co.) an estemmed trading company... and a syndicate of killers and thieves.

## **History**
#### **Overview**
A man with multiple identites, he has created fake stories so he will not get caught. The entire persona of *Sera'Thenis* is a fake, made to hide himself in Stormwind away from the law in Kalimdor. His real identity is that of *Cynthras Starfire*, a poor elf who lived a lowly and pitiful life of violence in Auberdine. This stands in stark contrast to that of *Sera'Thenis Uldreiyn*, a rich merchants son that has lived a noble and good-willed life, albeit it with tragedy.
