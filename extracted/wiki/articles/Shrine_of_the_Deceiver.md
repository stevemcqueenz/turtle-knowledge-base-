# Shrine of the Deceiver

**Shrine of the Deceiver** is a corrupted barrow den located at [Jaedenar](Jaedenar) in [Felwood](Felwood). It is named for the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion)'s lord - [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden) the Deceiver. The shrine is filled with warlocks of the [Shadow Council](Shadow Council) and is a dangerous place to enter for the unprepared.
