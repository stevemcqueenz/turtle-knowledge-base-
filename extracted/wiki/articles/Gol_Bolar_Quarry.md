# Gol'Bolar Quarry

**Gol'Bolar Quarry** is a large dig site found in southeastern [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh). It is the location of a small dwarven mining camp where young dwarves and gnomes can receive missions to exterminate the [trogg](trogg)s who have overrun the excavation site and the [Gol'Bolar Quarry Mine](Gol'Bolar Quarry Mine).  There are also some [vendor](vendor)s and [profession](profession) [trainer](trainer)s here ready to aid fresh-faced adventurers in any way they can.
