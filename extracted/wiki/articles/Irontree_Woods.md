# Irontree Woods

**Irontree Woods** lies in northern [Felwood](Felwood). Once a peaceful forest glade, it has been corrupted like the rest of Felwood by the demonic presence of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion). Three [Ancients](Ancients) ([Hastat](Hastat the Ancient), [Stoma](Stoma the Ancient) and [Vartrus](Vartrus the Ancient)), the great living trees of the [night elves](Night Elf), have been petrified and remain permanently entombed within the Irontree Woods. Crazed [treant](treant)s, twisted [bog beast](bog beast)s, ferocious wildlife, and corrupted [water elemental](water elemental)s also roam the woods, attacking any who venture near. This is also the place where you can start (or finish) the hunter level 60 epic quest.
