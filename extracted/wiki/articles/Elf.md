# Elf

There are 3 different races of Elf in Warcraft. Elves were once believed to be the first race to awaken on Azeroth, however, some ancient histories suggest that Elves were originally [Troll](Troll)s who discovered the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity), and were transformed by its powers. There are also other creatures who are branches of the Night Elf line: [Naga](Naga) and [Satyr](Satyr).

## Trivial Information
The Elves of Warcraft are inspired by the elves of Tolkien's writings, who were known as the Eldar or the Quendi.  In Warcraft, like in JRRT's works, the elves are one of the oldest races, and became divided into subraces over time.  In Tolkien's works, the first race to split were the Avari (the unwilling), who feared the messenger Orome and his invitation and fled into shadow, being twisted by Morgoth into the race of Orcs, a transformation that is reflected in Warcraft with the Trolls.

The second split race of the elves were the Moriquendi, the dark elves, who despite their name were not sinister beings but merely did not undertake the final journey from Middle Earth to the blessed realm of Valinor and thus never saw the true light of the trees Telperion and Laurelin.  

The Moriquendi lived in darkness, but were sociable.  The greys dealt closely with the newly risen race of Dwarfs, and eventually with the few of the race of men they deemed worthy of respect, while the silvans and others lived in more distant and isolated regions.

The third split in the race of elves occured in Valinor.  Three sects of elves existed in the blessed realm: the Teleri, the Vanyar, and Noldor.  The Noldor above all the others were the proudest and in a way most like men.  They were passionate beings and were driven to wrath by Morgoth, forsaking the Valar's invitation to bring battle to the great enemy, and slaying many of their Teleri kin in the process.  The Noldor would assimiliate into the races of elves in Middle Earth, and become close friends for a time with the noblest of the humans in the battles that followed.  

In Warcraft, the Night Elves take after the Vanyar, Teleri, and the Silvan Moriquendi; they live in close harmony with the world, drawing strength from the more primal forces of the Earth and mourning the destruction wrought by the other races.  

The Blood Elves take after the Noldor and the Grey Moriquendi.  The Warcraft Highborne discovered magic and inadvertently attracted the Burning Legion, a battle that roughly translates to the wars of the first age between the Valar and Melkor over the fate of the elves, after which the Highbourn rejoined the Night Elves and Tolkien's elves journed to Valinor.  Then, the Highborne tampered again with magic, and were sent forth in exile, much as the Noldor were exiled after the kinslaying.  They fell in with the humans and fought in the the Second War for Azeroth, much as the Noldor joined in the fight for Middle Earth.
