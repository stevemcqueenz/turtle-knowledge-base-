# Fire Plume Ridge

Fire Plume Ridge is the volcano subzone in the middle of [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater). Lava flows down the sides of the mountain, where [fire elementals](fire elemental) of lvl 51-54 can be found. A mighty fire elemental, [Blazerunner](Blazerunner), lives at its peak.  During [Elemental Invasions](Elemental Invasions), more fire elementals and [Baron Charr](Baron Charr) appear here.  

This area is plentiful in [Mountain Silversage](Mountain Silversage) and [Thorium](Thorium) veins, and is also a favored spot for farming [Essence of Fire](Essence of Fire).

The hottest place in the ridge is apparently 428,000 degrees Kraklenheit. (Krakle' Thermometer says: Holy Krakle! The temperature is 428,000 degrees Kraklenheit!)
