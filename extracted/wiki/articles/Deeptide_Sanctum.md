# Deeptide Sanctum

The **Deeptide Sanctum** is a naga-infested island located off the shore of [Gillijim's Isle](Gillijim's Isle). When the [Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet](Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet) attempted to make berth on the island of Gillijim, the Deeptide naga attacked them, destroying the high elven vessels at [Faelon's Folly](Faelon's Folly) and, in spite of the Kul Tirans' victory, forced them to relocated to [Lapidis Isle](Lapidis Isle) further to the north. (ref: [quest: Drowning Deeptide])
