Lord **Uther the Lightbringer**, or Sire **Uther Lightbringer**, was the first of the paladins of the [Knights of the Silver Hand](Knights of the Silver Hand). Once a [cleric of Northshire](Holy Order of Northshire Clerics) and a follower of the [Church of Light](Church of Light), under the spiritual advising of [Alonsus Faol](Alonsus Faol), he became the Supreme leader of the Silver Hand during the [Second War](Second War), and led his brothers in arms against the [Orcish Horde](Orcish Horde). After the war, Uther and his paladins kept the peace in Lordaeron for years, settling civil disputes and quelling demi-human uprisings throughout the realm. (ref:  A hero of the Alliance of Lordaeron, and a faithful ally of King [Terenas Menethil II](Terenas Menethil II), he became the master of Prince [Arthas](Arthas) and made him a paladin of the order. He was held to be the wisest and most noble of all the holy paladins.<ref name="OBaH">*[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*)

During the Third War, Uther fought the undead [Scourge](Scourge) across Lordaeron and investigated the source of the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath). When Arthas ordered the culling of [Stratholme](Stratholme), Uther refused to purge the city and its infected citizens and departed with his men to warn the king after the Silver Hand was suspended by the prince. During the fall of Lordaeron, Uther was betrayed and murdered by beloved pupil, while defending the urn carrying the ashes of King Terenas at [Andorhal](Andorhal).

## History
### Second War and aftermath
Apprentice to the aged Archbishop [Alonsus Faol](Alonsus Faol) who was the leader of the [Holy Order of Northshire Clerics](Holy Order of Northshire Clerics) that guided the kingdom of Azeroth during the [First War](First War), Uther learned first hand that faith alone would not defeat the terrible Horde. Many of the Clerics were slain during the First War, and so the Archbishop devoted himself to rebuilding the Order as to provide the citizens of Lordaeron with hope and faith. Knowing that this war with the unholy Orcs could not end soon, Uther discerned that the Archbishop's new Order must be able to defend the people as well as heal them. Thus, young Uther took up sword and shield and issued the call to the most pious amongst the Knights of Lordaeron. Many great Knights heeded Uther's beckoning and joined the Order which became known as the [Knights of the Silver Hand](Knights of the Silver Hand). With the strength of arms - and faith - possessed by these Paladins, Uther hoped to bear witness to the free people of the Northlands that the Orcish Horde can be cast back into the stygian pits that spawned them. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Legends of the Land](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Sire_Uther_Lightbringer)*, Sire Uther Lightbringer) Uther himself, dubbed "the Lightbringer", was anointed as the first of the paladins in the [Alonsus Chapel](Alonsus Chapel) in [Stratholme](Stratholme), (ref: [Aurius#Quotes](Aurius#Quotes)) and the Silver Hand was thus born with him as their leader. The knight [Turalyon](Turalyon) was one of the first to be trained by Uther as a paladin. (ref: 

With his paladins keeping vigilant watch over the northlands, Uther later embarked on the island of [Caer Darrow](Caer Darrow) on a mission to aid and comfort those suffering from the war. Along the way, he was waylaid by orcs of the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) and renegade [Alteraci](Alterac (kingdom)) sailors.<ref>[The Battle at Darrowmere (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Battle_at_Darrowmere_(WC2_Human))) After the naval battle, some of the sailors were captured and Uther uncovered a conspiracy that would reveal that Alterac had betrayed the Alliance. During questioning in Lordaeron's capital, they confessed their nation's treachery to the Alliance, leading to Alterac's eventual ruin. (ref: [Betrayal and the Destruction of Alterac (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Betrayal_and_the_Destruction_of_Alterac_(WC2_Human)))

The [Burning Blade clan](Burning Blade clan) was later routed and ultimately destroyed by troops under Uther's command during the Final Battle at the Portal. (ref: *Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal* manual, [The Aftermath of the Second War](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Beyond_the_Dark_Portal_manual#The_Aftermath_of_the_Second_War))

### Of Blood and Honor
Years later, Uther led the court of [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring) in [Stratholme](Stratholme). After he heard the statements of Tirion as well as [Saidan Dathrohan](Saidan Dathrohan) and [Barthilas](Barthilas), he managed to convince the jurors - Archmage [Antonidas](Antonidas), Archbishop [Faol](Alonsus Faol), Prince [Arthas](Arthas), and Admiral [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore) - to give Tirion another chance in light of his long years of service to Lordaeron and its allied kingdoms: to reaffirm his commitment to the Alliance and forsake his vow of honor to [Eitrigg](Eitrigg), which would grant Tirion a full pardon. While Tirion reaffirmed his commitment to the Alliance, however, he could not throw away his vow to Eitrigg. Disappointed with this answer, Uther sentenced Tirion to be cut off from the Light, the paladinship and to be sent into exile. Because of their friendship, however, Uther's last courtesy to the former paladin was that his family, [Karandra](Karandra Fordring) and [Taelan](Taelan Fordring), did not have to be exiled. (ref: 

### Third War and death
After nearly thirteen years of peace, the rumors of war began to circulate once again. The King's agents reported that a young, upstart warchief had arisen and rallied the few remaining orc clans into an elite fighting force. The young warchief was intent on tearing down the internment camps and freeing his people from their bondage. The "[new horde](Horde)" as it was dubbed, had brazenly attacked Stratholme in an attempt to rescue one of its captured warriors. The horde even destroyed [Durnholde](Durnholde) – the fortress that oversaw the security of the internment camps – and murdered the officers who ran it. To contain the orcish threat, King Terenas sent Uther and his paladins to quell the warchief's uprising, but the crafty orcs could never be found. The young warchief proved to be something of a tactical genius – and evaded Uther's best efforts to corral his hit and run attacks. Along with young Prince Arthas, Uther was sent to [Strahnbrad](Strahnbrad) to defend the town from orcish raids. While Arthas and his men attended to the defense of Strahnbrad, Uther moved on the orcs' encampment.<ref>[The Defense of Strahnbrad (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Defense_of_Strahnbrad_(WC3_Human))) Arthas joined him later, and with Uther's help, Arthas managed to destroy the encampment and kill the Blackrock blademaster. (ref: [Blackrock & Roll (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Blackrock_and_Roll_(WC3_Human)))

When [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) and his [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned) released the plague of undeath upon Lordaeron, Uther was to visit Caer Darrow once again, (ref: [Caer Darrow Citizen#Quotes](Caer Darrow Citizen#Quotes)) but the island fell to the plague before that.

Uther would later come across [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore), who had been sent by Arthas to find him when the pair had come across an army of undead approaching [Hearthglen](Hearthglen). Arthas held out with his forces for as long as he could until Uther arrived with reinforcements and saved the village. (ref: [March of the Scourge (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/March_of_the_Scourge_(WC3_Human))) Arthas quickly left for Stratholme, where he hoped to engage [Mal'Ganis](Mal'Ganis). When Uther followed him, he then learned the truth about the Plague of Undeath: it was not merely a means of mass murder, but rather the means of turning innocent townsfolk into [undead](undead) creatures. Upon arrival to Stratholme, Arthas had found that the grain had already been distributed, and ordered Uther and his knights to [purge](Culling of Stratholme) the entire city. Horrified, Uther rebuked him by saying he would not follow such an order even if Arthas were already king. Proclaiming that Uther had committed treason, Arthas relieved him of his command and suspended his paladins from service. Several soldiers left with Uther — as did Jaina — while those who remained loyal to Arthas joined him in slaughtering the infected citizens. (ref: [The Culling (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Culling_(WC3_Human)))

Following the purge, Uther informed King Terenas Menethil of his son's actions in Stratholme, and convinced the king to recall his son's expedition. An emissary was ordered to find him and order him to return from Northrend. However, Arthas lied to his own men and destroyed their ships in order to force them to complete their mission. (ref: [Dissension (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Dissension_(WC3_Human))) The prince finally did return later, though he was somehow different. Nevertheless, Lordaeron's capital erupted in a festival to celebrate their returning hero. The festivity turned to horror as Arthas proceeded into the throne room and impaled his father on the runeblade [Frostmourne](Frostmourne). (ref: [Arthas' Betrayal (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Arthas%27_Betrayal_(WC3_Human)))

Days after the death of Terenas, he had been ceremonially cremated and ensconced within a magical urn that Uther guarded personally. Uther ordered [Gavinrad the Dire](Gavinrad the Dire) and his [Champions of Peace](Champions of Peace) to guard the cemetery near Andorhal, where the remains of Kel'Thuzad were buried. Arthas eventually came in search of the necromancer's remains, slaying the paladin along with [Ballador the Bright](Ballador the Bright) and [Sage Truthbearer](Sage Truthbearer). Uther was faced with Arthas himself, who came in search of the urn. He explained to Arthas that the urn held the ashes of his father, and the two then engaged in a fight, and eventually Uther fell, telling Arthas he hoped there was a special place in hell waiting for him. Arthas replied that they would never know because he intended to live forever, then delivered the killing blow before seizing the urn. (ref: [Digging up the Dead (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Digging_up_the_Dead_(WC3_Undead)))

His body was later recovered and buried in a great tomb built for him to the south of the ruins of Andorhal. Located in the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands), east of [Chillwind Camp](Chillwind Camp), the tomb was the only light in a land of darkness and was vigilantly guarded by its caretaker [High Priest Thel'danis](High Priest Thel'danis).
