# Infernal

## Background
These mindless giants of flame and fury are summoned by warlocks and [Nathrezims](Nathrezim). Falling to earth as green, molten meteorites from the [Twisting Nether](Twisting Nether), infernals exist
only to destroy every living thing in their path. Though their lifespans are
limited, the mighty infernals have been known to destroy entire cities before
their energies dissipated back into the Great Dark Beyond.  [Archimonde](Archimonde) was known to summon massive amounts of infernals at once.

## In Game
The **Infernal** is a [pet](Pet) available to the [Warlock](Warlock) at level 50 after completing a quest line in [Felwood](Felwood).

The fifth [pet](Pet) available to a [Warlock](Warlock) after a [Felhunter](Felhunter). The next pet available is the [Doomguard](Doomguard). 

The warlock must summon an Infernal and use [Enslave Demon](Enslave Demon) to make it a pet, but the spell has an erratic duration and the warlock may lose control.

The Infernal requires an [Infernal Stone](Infernal Stone) to summon.

The Infernal can [tank](Tank) better than a [Voidwalker](Voidwalker), but also has an [AoE](AoE) [DoT](DoT) [ability](Ability).

## Infernal Tips
Remember to cast [Curse of Shadow](Curse of Shadow) on your Infernal before enslaving it, this will greatly increase the time you can keep him under control.

If you [Banish](Banish) the Infernal a few times when it breaks free, this seems to allow you to control it longer on a re-enslave.

## Infernal Abilites
He can do a nice melee attack.

He has an [AoE](AoE) buff that works similar to [Fire Shield](Fire Shield) on him. When he is hit, he reflects 20-30 or so fire damage to surrounding enemies. Note: This works when he is banished, so stay back.
