**Talvash del Kissel** is a [gnome](gnome) [quest giver](quest giver) located in the [Mystic Ward](Mystic Ward) in [Ironforge](Ironforge). He is a good friend of [Mayva Togview](Mayva Togview), (ref: [quest: Mastering Goldsmithing|profession=jewelcrafting]) and his tutor in jewelcrafting and goldsmithing was the mysterious highborne known as [the Artificer](the Artificer). (ref: 

He was a jeweler in [Gnomeregan](Gnomeregan) and specializes in goldsmithing. Though skilled and passionate about his work, after the fall of Gnomeregan he was reduced to renting out a home in Ironforge, and his business suffered when a competitor started a rumor that his jewelry was cursed,<ref>[quest: Back to Uldaman]) resulting in him falling in debt, experience difficulties when trying to engage in good and honest trade in the city, (ref: [quest: profession=jewelcrafting]) and often requiring to go through various means to pay his rent and appease his landlord. (ref: [quest: profession=jewelcrafting]) Through unfortunate coincidence, many of his clients have met unfortunate ends, including the paladin whose death on a self-appointed quest to clear out the "evil taint" in [Uldaman](Uldaman) first started the rumors and a mage who purchased a spell-enhancing necklace for a venture into an ill-fated [Gilneas City](Gilneas City).
