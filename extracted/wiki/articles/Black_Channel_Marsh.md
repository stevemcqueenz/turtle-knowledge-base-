# Black Channel Marsh

[Black Channel Marsh](Black Channel Marsh) is located in the southwestern [Wetlands](Wetlands). It is mostly noted by travelers on the road to [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor), and inexperienced adventurers are advised to take care because the marsh holds numerous [raptor](raptor) lairs, and some of the hungrier beasts wander quite close to the road.
