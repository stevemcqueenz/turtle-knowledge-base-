# Tomb of Ancestors

The **Tomb of Ancestors** is an ancient burial ground tucked away in the south of the [Grim Hollow](Grim Hollow), in the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches). As old as Grim Batol itself, the [Wildhammer dwarves](Wildhammer dwarves) laid their dead to rest there ages ago and carved their names in runestone and memory. However, a darkness has seeped into the Hollow as of late, and the dead aren't resting like they should. Spirits are drifting through the fog, restless. (ref: [quest: The Grim Hollow]) Deep within the tomb, the [skardyn](skardyn) sorcerer [Thromrar Darkflame](Thromrar Darkflame) performs a dark ritual, ensnaring the minds of the red dragonkin that patrol the swamp and raising the fallen ancestors of the Wildhammer, a herald of [greater darkness](Grim Batol).
