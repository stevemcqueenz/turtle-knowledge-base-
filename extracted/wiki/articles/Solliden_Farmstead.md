# Solliden Farmstead

The **Solliden Farmstead** in eastern [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades) is one of the only places in northern Lordaeron where the native farmfolk survive to this day, untainted by the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath). The farmers have united with the zealous [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) for protection against the [Forsaken](Forsaken) and the [Scourge](Scourge).

The Sollidens nurture themselves on the large pumpkins they grow in their patch, and supplement it with meat from the woods whenever they have a chance. Their proximity to both the [Agamand Mills](Agamand Mills) and [Deathknell](Deathknell) leave them little room to do so, however, as undead frequently wander near and attack. 

[Farmer Solliden](Farmer Solliden) still leads the farm himself.

The humans at **Solliden Farmstead** attack anyone they see, including fellow humans and other [Alliance](Alliance) travelers.  Perhaps the nearby [Scarlet](Scarlet Crusade) forces have brainwashed them to do so.
