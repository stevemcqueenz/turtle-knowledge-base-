# Haldarr Encampment

The **Haldarr Encampment**20 61 Azshara (g/s/c) was located in western [Azshara](Azshara), northeast of [Shadowsong Shrine](Shadowsong Shrine) and southwest of old [Valormok](Valormok). It was densely populated by [Haldarr](Haldarr) [satyr](satyr), the death of which is an objective of a quest.

[Sentinel Keldara Sunblade](Sentinel Keldara Sunblade) laid injured, captured or was consorting here.
