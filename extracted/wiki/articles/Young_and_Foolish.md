## Description
My agents have reported that a Scarlet caravan has started moving through various towns.

From what we know they have already gone to Lakeshire, Darkshire, Menethil and Southshore.

My only assumption is that whoever took over the Crusade after the events that transpired the Scarlet Monastery, Hearthglen and Stratholme has realised they've been running short on men.

I want you to go and speak to the people of Lakeshire. The locals should have at least seen something.
