# Twin Colossals

**The Twin Colossals** are a pair of high vertical mountains, rising up on the west side of [Feralas](Feralas). The road from [Feralas](Feralas) to [Desolace](Desolace) runs between these two mountains. (Some individuals conjecture that these are the stumps of what were once trees that have since eroded and become indiscernible from the surrounding rock and dirt)

They are one of the most striking landmarks of the area and inspire awe in whoever beholds them. 

At the base of the Western Colossal, there is a [Night Elf](Night Elf) female, [Marli Wishrunner](Marli Wishrunner), who gives you the opportunity to visit the top of the Eastern Colossal. She also informs players that they will have to find their own way down.
Accepting to be brought up will result in a teleport to the top of the mountain.

At the top there is a good view of the surrounding area, a small lake where one can fish (one wonders how this is possible), and a Night Elf male, [Kalin Windflight](Kalin Windflight), who sells parachutes to leave the mountain for 20 silver each.

The pair are apparently in on a kind of scam, for to get down, one needs the parachute. 
The tricky part is that the parachute is temporary, so if a player opens it too fast, it will be "gone" before one reaches the ground... resulting in a speedy demise.
First-time visitors often open the parachute at the beginning of the jump and as a result fall to their doom somewhere halfway. It is suspected that the pair then proceeds to loot these unfortunate souls.

A [Mage](Mage) is of course not required to buy the parachute, as they can use [Slow Fall](Slow Fall) to get down.
A [Priest](Priest) can use [Levitate](Levitate) to the same effect.
A [Paladin](Paladin) can cast [Divine Shield](Divine Shield) before hitting the ground to shield him/herself from falling damage.
Any [Engineer](Engineer) may get around this scam with the use of a [Parachute Cloak](Parachute Cloak). 
Also, players who have revered or higher reputation with the [Sha'tari Skyguard](Sha'tari Skyguard) can use either a [Skyguard's Drape](Skyguard's Drape) or a [Skywitch's Drape](Skywitch's Drape) as a parachute to avoid the fall.

It is possible to jump on the south side of the mountain, then use the parachute to land in the pond located just next south of the Eastern Colossal, to avoid death from a massive fall.

The Western Colossal is not accessible.
