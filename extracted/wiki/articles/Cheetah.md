# Cheetah

A species of big [cat](cat) found in [The Barrens](The Barrens), the [Badlands](Badlands), the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows), and [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles).  [Druids](Druids) can shapeshift into [Travel Form](Travel Form), which looks exactly like a **cheetah**. So far, although these [mob](mob)s *look like* a cheetah, none of them are named as such.
