# Tranquil Gardens Cemetery

The **Tranquil Gardens Cemetery** lies slightly southeast of [Darkshire](Darkshire) in [Duskwood](Duskwood). While the presence of many [undead](undead) has made the cemetery anything but tranquil, the undead here are the weakest you will find in Duskwood. The first quests the [Night Watch](Night Watch) give you involve dispatching the undead in this area. The [Insane Ghoul](Insane Ghoul) is also located here, either inside the ruined [church](church) or wandering about outside. Just north of the cemetery lies [Blind Mary](Blind Mary)'s house.
