# Lake Kel'Theril

-Lake Kel'Theril** is a frozen lake in central [Winterspring](Winterspring). The shores of the lake are home to [the Ruins of Kel'Theril](the Ruins of Kel'Theril), which along with the lake itself is populated by many tormented spirits of the [Highborne](Highborne). Adventurers come here to hunt ectoplasm for their tier 0.5 armor quests.

During the periodic [Elemental Invasions](Elemental Invasions) [Princess Tempestria](Princess Tempestria) spawn here.
