-Lakeshire** is a human township that sits on the banks of [Lake Everstill](Lake Everstill) in the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains). The town is led by [Magistrate Solomon](Magistrate Solomon) and the [Court of Lakeshire](Court of Lakeshire), while [Marshal Marris](Marshal Marris) and the [Stormwind Guard](Stormwind Guard) are in charge of the security. The town contains a town hall, an inn, [Verner Osgood](Verner Osgood)'s stables, (ref: [quest: A Baying of Gnolls]) and Chef Breanna's restaurant-house. Recently, the Everstill Bridge was destroyed by the marauding [Blackrock](Blackrock clan) [orc](orc)s.

Like [Westfall](Westfall) and [Duskwood](Duskwood), Redridge Mountains is also under the theoretical protection of the [Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)) army and, like them, has been left mostly to its own devices. However, there is a small guard platoon situated there and the area is still relatively quiet. The nearby [Stonewatch Keep](Stonewatch Keep) has been overrun by orcs that sometimes stage small raids into the city.
