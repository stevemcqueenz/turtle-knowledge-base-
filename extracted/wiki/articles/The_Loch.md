# The Loch

**The Loch** is the single most prominent feature of the [dwarven](dwarven) territory of [Loch Modan](Loch Modan). It is the largest body of water on the continent of Azeroth, and third in size in the entire known world after the [Lordamere](Lordamere) and the [Darrowmere](Darrowmere). Fed by an underground source, the loch was originally much smaller, but more than doubled its size after the dwarves of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) built the [Stonewrought Dam](Stonewrought Dam) at the loch's northern end. Three islands and three islets dot the loch's waters, and remnants of the second war can still be found scattered about the bottom.
