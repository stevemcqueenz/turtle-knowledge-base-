# Stonewatch Keep

-Stonewatch Keep** is the main structure of the human stronghold of [Stonewatch](Stonewatch), which also includes the [Stonewatch Tower](Stonewatch Tower). Originally, Stonewatch used to be controlled by the humans of [Lakeshire](Lakeshire). One day however, the warlock [Morganth](Morganth) made an alliance with the [Blackrock orcs](Blackrock clan) led by [Gath'Ilzogg](Gath'Ilzogg), and together they combined their forces to invade Redridge. Fueled by the evil magic of Morganth, the Blackrock orcs attacked Stonewatch Keep in Redridge with horrific savagery. Ultimately, they captured Stonewatch Keep, but shortly after their victory, Morganth turned on his orcish cohorts. He constructed an arcane tower, the [Tower of Ilgalar](Tower of Ilgalar), and used his powers to build a small army of [Shadowhide](Shadowhide clan) gnolls led by [Lieutenant Fangore](Lieutenant Fangore), to serve his malicious bidding. Both orc and man have fallen victim to the brutes that serve Morganth. (ref: [The State of Lakeshire](The State of Lakeshire))

Left to fend for themselves, the orcs of the Blackrock clan maintained control of the keep, which was overseen by the Blackrock warlord [Gath'Ilzogg](Gath'Ilzogg) and his trusted lieutenant, [Tharil'zun](Tharil'zun). Having taken [Corporal Keeshan](Corporal Keeshan) of the [Stormwind Army](Stormwind Army) prisoner during the battle, they dragged him away to their encampments further north. (ref: [Missing: Corporal Keeshan](Missing: Corporal Keeshan))

[Magistrate Solomon](Magistrate Solomon) has put a bounty on Gath'Ilzogg (ref:  [26+] [Wanted: Gath'Ilzogg (quest)](Wanted: Gath'Ilzogg (quest))) while [Marshal Marris](Marshal Marris) of [Lakeshire](Lakeshire) enlists the aid of [Alliance](Alliance) [adventurer](adventurer)s to hunt down and kill Tharil'zun for his misdeeds. (ref:  [25] [Tharil'zun (quest)](Tharil'zun (quest)))
