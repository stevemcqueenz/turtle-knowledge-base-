The **Rebel Camp** is a camp of former men of [Colonel Kurzen](Colonel Kurzen), who managed to escape the madness that befell the Colonel and most of his followers. The camp is situated in the northernmost part of [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), just west of the road leading south from [Duskwood](Duskwood) and northwest of [Kurzen's Compound](Kurzen's Compound). The soldiers here have their task set: To overthrow their former master, Kurzen.
