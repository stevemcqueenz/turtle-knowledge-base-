-Revantusk Village** is the only visitable settlement of the [Revantusk tribe](Revantusk tribe) of [forest troll](forest troll)s. You can find the [port](Harbor) village on the coast of the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands).

## History
Following the defeat of the [Old Horde](Old Horde) during the [Second War](Second War), the Horde was shattered, and many forest trolls deserted their former orc allies without a moment's hesitation. The Revantusks had little choice other than to flee as well, for their numbers were far too few to liberate the captive orcs, particularly when the orcs themselves seemed uninterested in freedom. Because they continued to feel some loyalty toward the Horde, the Revantusks were the target of many attacks from other forest trolls. Eventually, the Revantusk tribe relocated to the coast of the Hinterlands, where they hoped to avoid further notice, and established Revantusk Village.

In time, the village learned that a new Horde had been assembled by Warchief [Thrall](Thrall), who had turned his people back to their shamanistic traditions and established a nation of their own. In response, the Revantusk immediately dispatched its speediest runners to make contact with the orcs. Thrall and the Revantusk ultimately agreed to a pact of friendship and mutual assistance.

Due to the tribe's small size and the Revantusks' pact with Thrall, all other forest trolls regard the Revantusk trolls as weaklings and enemies. Similarly, the honorable Revantusks regard other forest trolls as traitors to the New Horde. A long-standing enmity exists between the Revantusk tribe and the Wildhammer clan of dwarves, who dwell in the Hinterlands and are considered enemies of the Horde. (ref: [Troll Compendium: Revantusk Tribe](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Troll_Compendium/Forest_Troll_Tribes#Revantusk_Tribe))

### World of Warcraft
During a bold midnight raid on Revantusk Village by the [Vilebranch tribe](Vilebranch tribe), the likes of [Elder Torntusk](Elder Torntusk) and [Guard Captain Malkhor](Huntsman Malkhor) were taken prisoner and brought to [Jintha'Alor](Jintha'Alor). While Malkhor was executed, [Vile Priestess Hexx](Vile Priestess Hexx) sought to sacrifice Elder Torntusk to the faceless Blood God, [Hakkar the Soulflayer](Hakkar the Soulflayer). (ref: [quest: Kidnapped Elder Torntusk!]) (ref: [quest: Job Opening: Guard Captain of Revantusk Village]) However, Horde [adventurer](adventurer)s were able to rescue Torntusk, and the chieftain returned to Revantusk Village. (ref: [quest: Return to Primal Torntusk])

The [Saltwater Snapjaw](Saltwater Snapjaw)s menaced the village making fishing a difficult task, by stealing bait and breaking fishing lines. (ref: [quest: Snapjaws, Mon!])
