**Renzik "The Shiv"** is a [goblin](goblin) located in [Old Town](Old Town) in [Stormwind City](Stormwind City). He is a member of the [SI:7](SI:7) acting as [Master Mathias Shaw](Master Mathias Shaw)'s second in command. (ref: [quest: SI:7|class=rogue]) Renzik has connection both to the [Hidden Circle](Hidden Circle) in [Ironforge](Ironforge) as well as the [rogue](rogue)s in [Darnassus](Darnassus).

Up-and-coming [rogue](rogue) [adventurer](adventurer)s who catch the attention of [SI:7](SI:7) are directed to meet with Renzik within the [SI:7 headquarters](SI:7 (Stormwind City)) in the [Old Town](Old Town) of [Stormwind City](Stormwind City). (ref: <ref>[quest: class=rogue]) (ref: [quest: class=rogue]) He sends these recruits to [Lucius](Lucius) in the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains) for training, so that they wouldn't die while on missions for SI:7. (ref: [quest: class=rogue])
