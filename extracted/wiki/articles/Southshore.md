**Southshore** (or **South Shore**) (ref: *World of Warcraft: Game Manual*) is an [Alliance](Alliance) port town located in the region of [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills). It is a quiet port town and one of the few pockets of human civilization that existed in Lordaeron, maintaining their allegiance to the [Alliance](Alliance). A few survivors of the Third War and adventurers call this town home.

## History
### Second War
After fleeing the [Horde](Old Horde) which had destroyed their kingdom during the [First War](First War), the refugees of [Azeroth](Stormwind (kingdom)) settled near Southshore, pledging their loyalty to the recently formed [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron). (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Nations of the Alliance](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Azeroth)*, Azeroth) Southshore was transformed into a busy seaport during the [Second War](Second War). (ref: [Southshore (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Southshore_(WC2_Human))) Its maritime area was rich with oil, which was also coveted by the Horde. (ref: [Southshore (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Southshore_(WC2_Orc)))

Despite its new status as a military port, Southshore was eventually attacked and taken by the rampaging Horde. (ref: [Tol Barad (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Tol_Barad_(WC2_Human))) Sometime after the war, Southshore was rebuilt by the Alliance.

## Significance
For the lower-level Alliance players (those not seasoned enough to dare entering the Western and Eastern Plaguelands), Southshore is one of two of the northernmost Alliance havens- the other being [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak). For utilitarian purposes, Southshore far outstrips Aerie Peak considering the array of its services and its location as well as being the drop-off point for players beginning the long trek on foot to the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery). But it is far less safe, sometimes being the location of raids going between the town and its Horde counterpart, Tarren Mill, to the North.

An NPC [quest giver](quest giver) named [Marshal Redpath](Marshal Redpath) stands outside the Southshore Town Hall. It is unknown if he is related to the Redpaths whose history is so interlaced with the Eastern Plaguelands. Inside the inn, [Lieutenant Farren Orinelle](Lieutenant Farren Orinelle) mocks Redpath for telling him to take care of the local [murloc](murloc)s.
