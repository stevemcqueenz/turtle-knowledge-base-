# Tents (bot)

<blockquote>*"There are 2 tents in the Goldshire."*</blockquote>Tents is a level 1 Human Rogue character who keeps track of the number of tents in Goldshire in Elwynn Forest. Whispering him with "Tents?" or typing "Tents?" in the World chat will elicit an automated response from Tents with the number of tents in Goldshire.

### History
Tents used to track number of tents in Gurubashi Arena, as both horde and alliance had teleporters in their capital city that would take them there, so it was a common meeting point.
