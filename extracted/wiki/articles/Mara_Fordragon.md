Lady **Mara Fordragon** was the High Clerist of the Kingdom of Stormwind during the First War. She became a patron of the Stormwindian refugees who fled to Lordaeron after the war. A statue of Lady Fordragon stands in the Royal Gallery of Stormwind Keep, while [Saint Mara Abbey](Saint Mara Abbey) in [Northwind](Northwind) was named after her.
