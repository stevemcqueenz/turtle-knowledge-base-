# Terrace of Repose

The **Terrace of Repose** is located on the grounds just north of the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery) entrance. It is one of the very few places in northern [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) where the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath) has not reached, and the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) have been able to grow green hedges and maintain a fountain with water that is fresh rather than stagnant like the rest of [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades).
