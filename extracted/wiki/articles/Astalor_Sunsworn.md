Lord **Astalor Sunsworn** is the Lord-Regent of Quel'Thalas, (ref:  the patriarch of House Sunsworn, and a current member of the [Regency Council](Regency Council) of [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas). One of the surviving nobles of Quel'Thalas, Lord Sunsworn is amongst those most opposed to [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner), having aspirations to ascend to the throne himself and establish his own dynasty to replace House Sunstrider as rulers of the Quel'dorei.

## Biography
Unlike other nobles of Silvermoon, Lord Sunsworn had kept his coffers in the vaults of [Ironforge](Ironforge), allowing him to maintain his vast fortunes even after the fall of Quel'Thalas. He would host lavish feasts for his fellow lords and ladies, though despite his claims that he opened his doors to the less fortunate as well, he would requisition supplies meant for the impoverished of Quel'Thalas for feasts meant for his own supporters.<ref>[quest: A Feast for the Poor])

### Scars of the Past
Lord Sunsworn was the one who convinced the Regency Council to open up [Sunstrider Court](Sunstrider Court), a grand coliseum that was built within Alah'Thlas, to mercenaries, champions, and wanderers from all corners of Azeroth, each hoping to prove their strength, earn gold, or fight for something greater. (ref: [Patch 1.18.0#NEW ARENA — SUNSTRIDER COURT](Patch 1.18.0#NEW ARENA — SUNSTRIDER COURT))

### Nightmares of Ursol
In search of salvation for the Quel'dorei's magical addiction, Lord Sunsworn sent his cousin [Andanil](Andanil Sunsworn) to [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast) with an expedition in search of [draenethyst](draenethyst). (ref: [Andanil Sunsworn#Quotes](Andanil Sunsworn#Quotes)) However, the expedition force did not send word back in weeks, prompting him to send an adventurer in search of Andanil. (ref: [quest: Sunsworn Expedition])

### *[A Good Day](A Good Day)*
Following a campaign by the [Silver Covenant](Silver Covenant) to retake [Shalandis Isle](Shalandis Isle), Lord Sunsworn exposed Vereesa Windrunner's funding for her campaign as having come from diverted funds intended for their people in exile and taxes that were owed to the Council, undermining her attempt at reclaiming her position as Speaker. As the ground that had been taken could not be forfeited to the Scourge after the matter had been disclosed, he made a motion before the Regency Council to have him be named Lord-Regent of Quel'Thalas, ostensibly to provide a strong leader to the people of Quel'Thalas in the dark times to come. With the five votes required from his fellow council members, he was named Lord-Regent of Quel'Thalas.
