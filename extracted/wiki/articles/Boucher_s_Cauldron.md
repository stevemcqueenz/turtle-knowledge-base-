# Boucher's Cauldron

-Boucher's Cauldron** is an [alchemy](alchemy) shop located in [Booty Bay](Booty Bay) in the [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). It's run by [Glyx Brewright](Glyx Brewright) and [Jaxin Chong](Jaxin Chong).

## Notes
[Keldric Boucher](Keldric Boucher) and [Kyra Boucher](Kyra Boucher) are alchemist suppliers that work in [Pestle's Apothecary](Pestle's Apothecary) of [Stormwind City](Stormwind City).
