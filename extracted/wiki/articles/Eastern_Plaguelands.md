The **Eastern Plaguelands** is the more heavily devastated half of the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands), the remnants of the [Kingdom of Lordaeron](Kingdom of Lordaeron). They are located east of the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands) and north of the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands).

The city of [Stratholme](Stratholme), located on the Eastern Plaguelands' northern edge, played an important role for the Scourge during the [Third War](Third War): It was there that [Prince Arthas](Arthas Menethil) of Lordaeron enacted the [Culling of Stratholme](Culling of Stratholme), marking a point of no return in his fall to darkness. After the war, the Eastern Plaguelands became the center of Scourge power in Lordaeron, with Stratholme acting as their southern capital under the rule of the lich [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad).

The area is roamed by mutant creatures and soldiers of the [Scourge](Scourge), while members of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) fight a constant battle to drive the Scourge out of [Stratholme](Stratholme) from their Chapel and key base at [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand). Only a small camp maintained by the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn) remains friendly to all, welcoming adventurers that would lend a strong arm or spell. Located at [Light's Hope Chapel](Light's Hope Chapel), this staging area serves as a last bastion of Light in a land blanketed in darkness.

## Geography
The Eastern Plaguelands contain a normal instanced dungeon, [Stratholme](Stratholme), as well as the raid dungeon [Naxxramas](Naxxramas). The micro dungeon [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand) can also be found here. [Light's Hope Chapel](Light's Hope Chapel) serves as a travel hub for both the Horde and the Alliance. There are no battlegrounds in this zone, though it is a world PvP area.

## Patch changes
- (Patch 1.17.1|note=Zone expanded; [Forlorn Summit](Forlorn Summit) added. Pathway into the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands) added.)
- (Patch 1.16.0|note=Path to the [Scarlet Enclave](Scarlet Enclave) added.)
- **Patch 1.6.0 (2005-07-12):**
  - The Eastern Plaguelands have been converted over to the new graveyard system. The system will place your character's spirit at the closest allowed graveyard in the zone.
  - A neutral graveyard has been added in the Eastern Plaguelands near the northwestern shore of Blackwood Lake. Characters that die in The Fungal Vale will continue to use the Darrowshire  graveyard due to the mountains between it and the new graveyard. Additionally, characters dying in Stratholme will now use the new Blackwood Lake graveyard.
- **Patch 1.4.0 (2005-04-19):** Players can no longer kill creatures with ranged attacks while the mobs are unable to damage the player when standing atop the cauldrons in Eastern Plaguelands.
- **Patch 1.1.0 (2004-11-07):** Eastern and Western Plaguelands have dozens of new quests.
