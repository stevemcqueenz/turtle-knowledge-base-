# Greatwood Vale

The **Greatwood Vale** is a forested valley in the foothills of the [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains). Linking the dry expanse of the [Barrens](Barrens) to the mountains proper, the fertile recesses of the vale have been a hunting and camping region for the [Tauren](Tauren) tribes for millennia. Recently, the [Grimtotem Tribe](Grimtotem) has attacked and destroyed the Tauren loyal to the Horde in the region, burning [Camp Aparaje](Camp Aparaje) to the ground and murdering most of the inhabitants. Horde agents have been sending adventurers into the vale to assault the Grimtotems there.
