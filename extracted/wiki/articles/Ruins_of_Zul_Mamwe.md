The **Ruins of Zul'Mamwe** is a [troll](troll) ruin located south of the [Mosh'Ogg Ogre Mound](Mosh'Ogg Ogre Mound). It is surrounded by mountains on all sides except north. The second major troll tribe of [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), the [Skullsplitters](Skullsplitter tribe), inhabit Zul'Mamwe, led by their vicious chieftain, [Ana'thek the Cruel](Ana'thek the Cruel).
