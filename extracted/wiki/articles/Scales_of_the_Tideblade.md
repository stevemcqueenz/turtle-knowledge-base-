## Objectives
Bring 30 Tideblade Scales to Sentinel Commander Silverstreak at Narvalis Point.
- [Tideblade Scale](Tideblade Scale) (30)

## Description
The naga have infested the ruins of Nendis like parasites. Once it was a quiet settlement of our people. Now the streets are flooded with serpents loyal to the Betrayer. Their warriors call themselves the Tideblade. Veterans of the army Illidan unleashed when he first struck these shores.

We have skirmished with them for weeks, but they are persistent. For every naga we slay, two more seem to slither out of the surf. If we are to weaken their presence here, we must thin their ranks directly.

Go to the ruins of Nendis and hunt the Tideblade naga. Bring me thirty of their scales as proof of their deaths.
