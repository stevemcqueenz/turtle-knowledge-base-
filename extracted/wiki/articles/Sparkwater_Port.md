# Sparkwater Port

-Sparkwater Port** is a harbor located on the eastern coast of [Durotar](Durotar). It stands as a bold testament to the [Durotar Labor Union](Durotar Labor Union)'s rise within the Horde, where the fires of industry blaze brightly as goblins forge their fortunes along the shore.
