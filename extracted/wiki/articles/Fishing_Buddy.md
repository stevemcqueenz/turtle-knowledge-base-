# Fishing Buddy

Fishing Buddy helps with everything you wanted support for in your fishing endeavors. Clothing, fish information, etc.

## How to install
Either clone the repository to your WoW/Interface/Add-ons folder, or download manually via GitHub (click on **<> Code** -> Download ZIP. Do not forget to rename the directory to "FishingBuddy" afterwards.

## Git links
- [**(SeVeN7000)**](https://github.com/SeVeN7000/FishingBuddy)
- [**(ilithyia)**](https://github.com/ilithyia-addons/FishingBuddy)

## Commands
- Use /fishingbuddy or /fb toggle the display of the Fishing Buddy window
- Use /fb help print the list of Fishing Buddy commands 
- Use /fb switch wear your chosen fishing outfit, or change back
**Usage**

- Alt-clicking on an item in your outfit display will clear it.
- Right-click for cast.
- A double-click will force another cast early.
- Shift+right-click on the bobber, when it "splashes" the item will automatically go into you backpack or bags.

## Features
- Easy Cast' lets you right-click to cast your line. 
- Use the same mouse button for looting and casting. A double-click will force another cast early
- Fish Watcher' lets you see the fish you've caught here before (or the fish that you've caught in the current session)
- Display your current skill level and the time since you started fishing
- Choose your fishing outfit and change into it easily
- Support for automatically adding a lure to your fishing pole
- Full Titan Panel support
- myAddOns support
- Preliminary support for tracking 'cycle fish' such as the Nightfin Snapper so you know when to go looking for them
- It also displays your 'style points'
