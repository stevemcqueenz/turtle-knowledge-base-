# Khasya Tanarian

## Khasya Tanarian
Khasya is a wandering sorceress seeking fortune and pleasure throughout Azeroth. Her talents in evocation and divination spells are impressive, but she doesn't have the arrogance like the mages of Dalaran, which makes it easy for people to get along with her.

#### **Occupations**
Khasya is not only a natural spellcaster, but has also received private magic training since childhood. Her spellcasting abilities have been tested in Azeroth's harshest environments, which proven her worth countless times.

Before becoming a freelance adventurer, Khasya was employed by the Gadgetzan Water Company, a subsidiary of the Steamwheedle Cartel. Khasya has creatively used reactions between various elements to determine the location of water veins, plays a role in the discovery of many underground water sources in the desert.

#### **Looks**
Khasya has the exotic appearance of Southsea people. Her eyes were as dark as black pearl, and her hair as silky and dark as nightsaber fur. Khasya's skin is bronzed, smooth and hydrated. Her figure is not plump, but she has attractive curves.

Khasya prefers simple, breathable clothing and does not like to wrap herself too tightly. She likes gold jewelry, the most obvious of which are the two gold bracelets on her right wrist. When she casts a spell, they collide and make crisp sound.

Khasya smokes sungrass and licorice from a long pipe, it was such a long-standing habit that it made her voice slightly rapsy.

#### **Personality**
Khasya is witty, foxy and sarcastic, even acquaintances have trouble telling which of her words are serious. But she believed it effectively weeded out the fribbles and idiots around her. 

Khasya is as free and searing as the desert wind, and it is hard to be tied down by any rules. However, she is no more interested in breaking the law than she is in understanding its authority, so whenever she arrived in a new land, she didn't waste much time in jail. Khasya doesn't like conflict, but is quite vengeful. In battle, Khasya is confident and cunning, with no sense of honor.

Khasya has a great enthusiasm for things or people that interest her, although it may be short-lived like candle light, the passion is always like fire - takes all and gives all. This foehn of attraction had attracted many people to her, a few are capable enough to be her companions, fewer of them interested her, and even fewer could have kept her interested after the bonfire had gone out. Although risks always follow this lifestyle, Khasya has her own way out of these messes. Some people think she is emotionally selfish.

Khasya is a money-obsessed person who lacks long-term vision. Nothing seems to impress her except material things. But she is not a miser. After making some money, she often shares enjoyments generously with her companions, and then quickly becomes poor again, and then looks for the next opportunity to make a fortune, and the cycle starts again. 

## Backstory
Khasya may have been the first human she knew born on Tanaris. In the year she was born, even the pirates from the Southsea had not yet landed on this dry land.

It all starts with the story of Khasya's parents, and it may not be credible - she has a father who only be honesty when chanting spells and a mother who doesn't know what honesty is, but in any case, this is the only way she knows her origins...Well, she doesn't really care about that too.

Khasya's father is said to be a sorcerer from Dalaran. He took part in the second war, but was unhappy with the authorities because he was forced to hand over some valuable artifacts he had looted from horde warlocks. So in 8 ADP he organized a group of rogue mages to heist the vault of Dalaran. They didn't get the loot they were looking for, but the fact that Teron Gorefiend stole the Eye of Dalaran at the very same night made the heist team a suspect for high treason. Khasya's father escaped from Dalaran overnight and boarded an owler ship heading south.

The ship's destination was originally Booty Bay, but off Khaz Modan they were attacked by Southsea pirates, and as if that wasn't enough, the pirate ship was followed by Dragonmaw riders, and the red dragons then attacked the two tied-up ships. In the chaos of the fire, Khasya's father tried to teleport to escape, but at this time, a she-pirate swooped at him and disrupted his chanting. As a result, the two entangled morons were teleported to the desert of Tanaris. No body knows how they walked from the center of the desert to Steamwheedle Port alive. According to their saying, "then poff, here you are".

As a rare talent of the Steamwheedle Cartel, Khasya's father quickly gained a place in Gadgetzan, as for mother, she robbed the midwife as soon as she could jumped off the delivery bed. 

All in all, Khasya grew up in the goblin community, inherited her spellcasting talent from her father, and her appearance is almost exactly the same as her mother. Growing up, Khasya's father taught her everything he had learned throughout his life in Dalaran; her mother taught her how to survive in a sinister world and the cream of Southsea philosophy. 

The successful family education made Khasya to throw a fireball into the face of a strange big man at the age of 11 , that was the first time she saw anyone bigger than goblins or her parents at the shore - This year, the wastewanders landed on the coast of Tanaris.

As Khasya grew up, she began to take her father's place in the Gadgetzan Water Company. In fact, long before Khasya grew up, she had been deep into the desert several times with goblin exploration teams. She soon proved that she was more talented than her father. With her help, the company discovered more water sources in the desert and set up pumping stations, so that the desert could support a larger population. Khasya was also inevitably involved in many battles with beasts, Qiraji, wastewanders, and sand trolls. All this made her stronger and made her rich at a young age.

With the end of the Third War, the rise of the Alliance and the Horde in Kalimdor made trade in the south active again. Many outsiders appeared in Gadgetzan, bringing news to Khasya about the wider world. Taking this as an opportunity, Khasya ended her career of working for goblins, became an adventurer, and began to learn the pleasure that the whole world could bring her.
