# Valley of Kings

The [Valley of Kings](Valley of Kings) is located in the southwestern corner of the [dwarven](dwarven) territory of [Loch Modan](Loch Modan), just west of [Stonesplinter Valley](Stonesplinter Valley). When going to or from [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh) by the [South Gate Pass](South Gate Pass), adventurers pass through a piece of this verdant valley, which also is an important throughway to the dangerous region known as the [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge). The gate to the gorge remains under lock and guard, and adventurers have to prove their power to [mountaineer Pebblebitty](mountaineer Pebblebitty) before she will grant them full access through the gate.
