The **Scarlet Crusade** (also known as the **Scarlet Brotherhood** (ref:   [40D] [Rituals of Power (quest)](Rituals of Power (quest))) or simply the **Crusade**) is a zealous religious order dedicated to the eradication of the undead. They are a major adversary of the Forsaken and they maintain several bastions throughout the former lands of Lordaeron, namely the Scarlet Monastery, Hearthglen, Tyr's Hand and a large temple within Stratholme, wherein it is headquartered.

## History
### Founding and corruption
The Scarlet Crusade was formed by Highlord [Alexandros Mograine](Alexandros Mograine), [High General Abbendis](High General Abbendis) and [Grand Inquisitor Isillien](Grand Inquisitor Isillien) during the [Third War](Third War). Under Mograine's leadership, the Scarlet Crusade was at first a noble order, and Mograine was dubbed the Ashbringer as it would be all that remained of the undead after he would walk into whole battalions of undead and emerge unscathed. However, Mograine proved to be the lynchpin of the Crusade, and when joined by [Saidan Dathrohan](Saidan Dathrohan) - who was in fact impersonated by the dreadlord [Balnazzar](Balnazzar) - corruption began to spread throughout the Crusade, planting the seeds of madness and insane zealotry that the order became infamous for. (ref: [Corrupted Ashbringer/Scarlet Monastery Event](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Corrupted_Ashbringer/Scarlet_Monastery_Event))

Becoming Grand Crusader of the Scarlet Crusade, Dathrohan made a bargain with [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) and arranged for Alexandros to be ambushed during a foray in [Stratholme](Stratholme). While the undead attack was not enough, it exhausted him enough that his son [Renault](Renault Mograine) was able to claim the Ashbringer and drive it through his father's heart while his back was turned. The heinous act of betrayal corrupted the blade and led to Mograine's own corruption as a death knight of the Scourge and champion of Kel'Thuzad. (ref: 

[High Inquisitor Fairbanks](High Inquisitor Fairbanks), who had bore witness to the Highlord's death, returned to the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery) - much to Renault's dismay. Shouting and screaming, he told the tale of the lesser Mograine's betrayal to any and all who would hear him. While Fairbanks was murdered in cold blood for his actions and dragged to a hidden chamber of the Monastery Cathedral, some did listen to his words, and thus they separated from the Crusade to form the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn).

### World of Warcraft
At first, the Scarlet Crusade counted members from citizens and races of all over the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron), including high elves of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas), dwarves of the [Alterac Mountains](Alterac Mountains) and human citizens of [Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)), [Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)), [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)) and [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)).<ref>Hall of Champions statues) However, as the Scarlet Crusade continued to cling to their scattered holdings, obsessed with eradicating the undead and retaking their once-beautiful homeland, (ref: *World of Warcraft* Game Manual) the Crusaders became intolerant of all non-human races, regardless of alliance or affiliation. They started believing that any and all outsiders were potential carriers of the undead plague - and must be destroyed. (ref: [Game Guide/World Dungeons#The Scarlet Monastery](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Guide/World_Dungeons#The_Scarlet_Monastery))

At some point after the formation of the Scarlet Crusade, the Scarlet Monastery was attacked by the Scourge at least twice in the Summertide Assaults. (ref: [Ferren Marcus](Ferren Marcus)'s statue inscription in the Scarlet Monastery) The Crusade also attempted a strike against the Scourge base in Northrend, and built a fleet specifically for that purpose. The attempt failed, although the Crusaders reached as far as the mouth of Icecrown Glacier. There were many casualties among the high ranks of the Crusade, including its first Captain General, and first Chief Assassin. Its first and only Grand Admiral, [Barean Westwind](Admiral Barean Westwind), was presumed deceased as well, but later resurfaced in the [Scarlet Enclave](Scarlet Enclave).

The Scarlet Crusade, unwilling to rest until every undead - Lich King's Scourge or no - is destroyed, hunted down the Forsaken, and the Forsaken of [Brill](Brill) have fended off their incursions, (ref:  [4] [The Scarlet Crusade (quest)](The Scarlet Crusade (quest))) retaliated in kind, and tested their poisons on [Captured Scarlet Zealot](Captured Scarlet Zealot)s. (ref:  [7] [Fields of Grief (2)](Fields of Grief (2))) The Forsaken in turn enlisted Horde adventurers to aid Nathanos Blightcaller in conducting strategic strikes against the Crusade in Tyr's Hand in order to soften them up for the assault on Stratholme. (ref:  [60G] [The Crimson Courier](The Crimson Courier)) Nathanos directed adventurers to slay Demetria, the chief advisor to Grand Crusader Saidan Dathrohan. (ref:  [60G] [The Scarlet Oracle, Demetria](The Scarlet Oracle, Demetria))

Meanwhile, [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring) enlisted the aid of adventurers to redeem his son [Taelan Fordring](Taelan Fordring), who had risen through the ranks of the Scarlet Crusade and became their Highlord. (ref:  [56] [Redemption (quest)](Redemption (quest))) Tirion had the adventurer collect numerous mementos (ref:  [57] [Of Forgotten Memories](Of Forgotten Memories)) (ref:  [58] [Of Lost Honor](Of Lost Honor)) (ref:  [60D] [Of Love and Family](Of Love and Family)) and deliver them to Taelan in order to restore his faith in honor and nobility. (ref:  [60] [Scarlet Subterfuge](Scarlet Subterfuge)) Tirion's plan worked; Taelan was moved by the mementos and desires to reunite with his father so they can both become heroes of the Alliance and of Lordaeron. Taelan and the adventurer sneak out of Hearthglen to regroup with Tirion, but they were caught by [Grand Inquisitor Isillien](Grand Inquisitor Isillien) who murdered Taelan for his desertion on the Grand Crusader's orders. Tirion Fordring slew Isillien in retribution while the adventurer dispatched Isillien's Crimson Elites. In the aftermath of the battle, Taelan's death spurred Tirion to reform the [Order of the Silver Hand](Order of the Silver Hand) so that evil, regardless of its politics or pleasantries, is extinguished from the world. (ref:  [60+] [In Dreams](In Dreams))

In Stratholme, the undead [Scourge](Scourge) — led by the powerful lich, [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad), was locked in battle with a contingent of Scarlet Crusaders, led by [Grand Crusader Dathrohan](Grand Crusader Dathrohan). Both forces held portions of the ravaged city, and both sides were locked in constant, violent combat. (ref: [Game Guide/World Dungeons#Stratholme](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Guide/World_Dungeons#Stratholme)) The [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn) has been sending adventurers into Stratholme to contend with both factions. Despite their tenuous alliance, [Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff](Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff) of the Argent Dawn condemned the Scarlet Crusade as blasphemers and directed adventurers to slay [Archivist Galford](Archivist Galford) and burn down his archives in order to take away the Scarlet Crusade's most valued documents and punish them for their heretical zealotry. (ref:  [60D] [The Archivist](The Archivist)) Adventurers eventually confronted Grand Crusader Dathrohan and during their battle with him, uncovered that he was really the [nathrezim](nathrezim) [Balnazzar](Balnazzar) in disguise. Adventurers sent the [Head of Balnazzar](Head of Balnazzar) to Duke Nicholas Zverenhoff as proof of Balnazzar's evil masquerade. Zverenhoff was shocked that the Crusade was manipulated by such an evil force and pondered what the ramifications of Balnazzar's corruption would have on the Scarlet Crusade should the truth come out. (ref:  [60D] [The Truth Comes Crashing Down](The Truth Comes Crashing Down))

Some Crusaders under the leadership of Scarlet Commander Marjhan allied with the Argent Dawn at [Light's Hope Chapel](Light's Hope Chapel) in order to attack [Naxxramas](Naxxramas), though the temporary truce was not sanctioned by the Grand Crusader. (ref: [Scarlet Commander Marjhan#Quotes](Scarlet Commander Marjhan#Quotes))

Following the events that transpired in the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery), [Hearthglen](Hearthglen) and [Stratholme](Stratholme), High General Abbendis has risen as the sole leader of the Scarlet Crusade. Under her command, her followers were tasked with recruiting young men and women, such as [Vladeus Springriver](Vladeus Springriver), to replenish the Crusade's numbers by tricking them into joining their cause. (ref:  [60] [The Price Of Information](The Price Of Information)) A Scarlet caravan passed through the various towns of the [kingdom of Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)), having passed through [Lakeshire](Lakeshire), [Darkshire](Darkshire), [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor) and [Southshore](Southshore) on its way north. (ref:  [60] [Young and Foolish](Young and Foolish))
