# Iris Lake

**Iris Lake** was located in north-central [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), east of [Astranaar](Astranaar) and west of [Shael'dryn](Shael'dryn)'s [moonwell](Moonwell (Ashenvale)). It was one of the many areas of the [night elven](night elven) forest which fell to the corruption of [Felwood](Felwood). The water of the lake was a sickly yellowish green, and twisted [bog beast](bog beast)s roamed the area, attacking anyone who visited seeking one of [Elune's Tear](Elune's Tear)s.
