The **Dragonmaw clan** is one of the orcish clans dating back to the time of the rise of the Horde, before the Warlocks. Under the command of the shaman [Zuluhed the Whacked](Zuluhed the Whacked), the Dragonmaws swore fealty to [Blackhand the Destroyer](Blackhand) with the other clans of the Horde upon Blackhand's ascension to Warchief. When Blackhand was assassinated by [Orgrim Doomhammer](Orgrim Doomhammer), Zuluhed and the Dragonmaws maintained their fealty to Blackhand by pledging their loyalty to his sons, [Rend](Rend Blackhand) and [Maim](Maim Blackhand), Chieftains of the [Black Tooth Grin clan](Black Tooth Grin clan). (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Clans of the Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Dragonmaw_Clan)*, Dragonmaw Clan)

## History
Manipulated by the Black Scourge, [Deathwing the Destroyer](Deathwing), the Dragonmaws found an artifact of immense power known as the [Demon Soul](Demon Soul) - created by Deathwing himself ten thousand years earlier, but forever forbidden from his grasp as the result of the spellwork of the other Aspects. Zuluhed entrusted the Demon Soul to a warrior and warlock named [Nekros Skullcrusher](Nekros Skullcrusher), who used it to capture the Dragonqueen Alexstrasza - much to his surprise, and certainly to Alexstrasza's. The Dragonmaws used Alexstrasza's young to fight for the Horde - and fight they did, through much of the Second War. Operating out of the abandoned Wildhammer capital of [Grim Batol](Grim Batol), the Dragonmaws maintained their control over Alexstrasza and her flight after the Horde was defeated, using them to raid Alliance ports in the surrounding [Wetlands](Wetlands). (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*)

The Kirin Tor high councillor Krasus - the humanoid form of the red dragon [Korialstrasz](Korialstrasz), one of [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza)'s consorts - sent the mage [Rhonin](Rhonin) to free Alexstrasza from the Dragonmaws by somehow destroying the Demon Soul - which, using one of Deathwing's scales, he did. In their fury, Alexstrasza and her dragons tore Grim Batol apart and incinerated much of the Dragonmaw clan, including Nekros, who was eaten. (ref: 

After the survivors of the Dragonmaw clan were rounded up by the Alliance and ushered into the internment camps following the battle of Grim Batol, Zuluhed disappeared in search of the remnants of the Demon Soul. He returned only when he managed to reclaim a shard of the Demon Soul from the clutches of the Red dragonflight,<ref>[THE NEXT PATCH FOR CLASSIC+ | Turtle WoW Patch 1.18.0: Scars of the Past](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5gy_3Dy5nXE)) claiming that it had not been destroyed by the wizard [Rhonin](Rhonin). From the [Dragonmaw Retreat](Dragonmaw Retreat), Zuluhed rallied his clan, who had been freed by [Thrall](Thrall) during the liberation of the orcs, (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, *[Orcish History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Day_of_the_Dragon)*, Day of the Dragon) (ref: [The Battle of Grim Batol](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Battle_of_Grim_Batol_(History_of_Warcraft))) against all those who opposed them (ref: [NEW CLASSIC+ DUNGEON: Dragonmaw Retreat — Turtle WoW Patch 1.18 Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V0ql8IneiCk)) under the banner of the [Dark Horde](Dark Horde), led by [Rend Blackhand](Rend Blackhand). However, the Dragonmaw was not so unified. While Nekros' son [Nek'rosh](Nek'rosh) led elements of the clan in the Wetlands outside of Grim Batol, and another group of Dragonmaw warriors was stationed within the [Dragonmaw Garrison](Dragonmaw Garrison) in [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire)'s [Blackwing Lair](Blackwing Lair) as part of the [Blackwing Cabal](Blackwing Cabal) protecting [Grethok the Controller](Grethok the Controller), other prospective leaders such as [Korlag Doomsong](Korlag Doomsong) refused to answer Zuluhed's call due to him abandoning his clan to find the Demon Soul while the Dragonmaw fought tooth and nail for survival. (ref:  [35D] [Letter from Korlag Doomsong](Letter from Korlag Doomsong))

At least one member of the Dragonmaw clan, [Gor'dush Jawtaker](Gor'dush Jawtaker), flies the colors of the [Horde](Horde) as a part of the [Scalereaver Warband](Scalereaver Warband). (ref: [Gor'dush Jawtaker#Quotes](Gor'dush Jawtaker#Quotes))
