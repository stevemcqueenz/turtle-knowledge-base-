The **Alliance Remnants** (ref: [Shards of the Alliance (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Shards_of_the_Alliance_(WC3_NightElf))) (ref: [The Ruins of Dalaran (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ruins_of_Dalaran_(WC3_NightElf))) (ref: [Misconceptions (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Misconceptions_(WC3_BloodElf))) (normally referred to as simply the **Alliance** (ref: [Dreadlord's Fall (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Dreadlord%27s_Fall_(WC3_Undead))) (ref: [A New Power in Lordaeron (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_New_Power_in_Lordaeron_(WC3_Undead))) (ref: [Captain Silas' Letter](Captain Silas' Letter)) or the **Remnants**, (ref: [quest: A Call to Aid]) also known as the **Lordaeron Remnants**) (ref: [New Zones & World Edits](https://web.archive.org/web/20260514170802/https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps). Archived from [the original](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps) on 2026-05-14. Retrieved on 2026-29-08.) are a force that has operated since the aftermath of the [Third War](Third War) and consisted of [human](human)s, [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor), [blood elves](Blood elf) and [dwarves](Dwarf) (under both Bronzebeard (ref:  and Wildhammer) formerly led by Grand Marshal [Othmar Garithos](Othmar Garithos) and Prince [Kael'thas Sunstrider](Kael'thas Sunstrider).

Following the death of Grand Marshal Garithos, the Remnants remain an [Alliance](Alliance)-friendly faction located in the [Tirisfal Uplands](Tirisfal Uplands) of the [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades). The survivors of the Army of Lordaeron are led by [Captain Silas](Captain Silas) and base out of the [Remnants Camp](Remnants Camp), a derelict former outpost of the Army of Lordaeron.<ref name="Gheor">[Gheor from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/528228771594240030/1090029823910477845) (dead link) - "The Tirisfal Uplands humans are what's left of his army.") In addition to their main camp, they also hold the [Shatteridge Tower](Shatteridge Tower) and the [Garrick Stead](Garrick Stead).

## History
### Background
During the Third War, [Dalaran](Dalaran) was destroyed by [Archimonde](Archimonde), (ref: [The Destruction of Dalaran (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Destruction_of_Dalaran_(WC3_Undead))) but many of the Burning Legion demons went to Kalimdor and were defeated. (ref: [Twilight of the Gods (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_of_the_Gods_(WC3_NightElf))) By the time of the Third War, [Othmar Garithos](Othmar Garithos) had risen to the rank of Grand Marshal. As the highest-ranking survivor of Lordaeron's military after the Scourge's rampage across Lordaeron, he recruited what forces he could muster from both volunteers and conscripted civilians. In addition to his human army, Garithos' forces comprised forces sent as aid from neighboring non-human states such as Ironforge. The healthiest blood elves led by [Kael'thas Sunstrider](Kael'thas Sunstrider), prince of the ruined [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas (kingdom)), also joined his army. Garithos did not enjoy having to work with the blood elves (ref: [A Dark Covenant (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_Dark_Covenant_(WC3_BloodElf))) or [dwarves](Dwarf), (ref:  believing that the [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron) should never have accepted the elves in the first place. Lord Garithos marshaled the survivors of the Alliance of Lordaeron and managed to retake Dalaran.

In order to survive, the Remnants and particularly the blood elves hid caches of gold, lumber and artifacts such as magical small buildings all over western Lordaeron.

### Blood elves
The Alliance Remnants were stationed throughout Silverpine Forest. Prince Kael'thas and his blood elves were located near the shores of the Great Sea, though they were attacked by the Scourge and would've perished were it not for the help of the night elves [Tyrande Whisperwind](Tyrande Whisperwind) and [Maiev Shadowsong](Maiev Shadowsong). They returned to Dalaran but were scolded by Garithos for their tardiness and ordered them to repair three observatories. After the repairs, Lord Garithos went to the front lines and left Kael'thas to await orders. The next day an emissary sent by Garithos ordered the withdrawal of the human and dwarven troops from Kael'thas' command and sent them to the front lines. Then the dreadlord [Dalvengyr](Dalvengyr) and an undead army launched an attack on Kael'thas' bases. When the blood elves were going to be defeated, Lady Vashj and her naga appeared and helped Kael'thas fend off the undead. The combined armies attacked the undead and managed to kill Dalvengyr, but later Garithos arrived and saw the naga. He condemned the blood elves for consorting with naga as an act of treason and imprisoned the blood elves; planning to execute them later.

### Divided
The Alliance Remnants were thus divided into two groups: the human and dwarves loyal to Garithos and Kael'thas' forces, and the blood elves who were imprisoned.

Imprisoned and sentenced to death, the blood elves were rescued by Vashj. Kael'thas fought his way throughout the dungeons of Dalaran against Garithos' Enforcers and his former friend Jailor [Kassan](Kassan). The blood elves planned to reopen the portal opened by [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) that brought Archimonde into Azeroth and redirect it to [Outland](Outland), where they would then meet [Illidan](Illidan Stormrage).<ref>[The Dungeons of Dalaran (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dungeons_of_Dalaran_(WC3_BloodElf))) However, they needed to keep Garithos' Elite Guard at bay while his men crossed the portal. Kael'thas, Vashj, and some engineers planned a defense and managed to hold off Garithos until everyone made it through the portal. (ref: [The Crossing (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Crossing_(WC3_BloodElf)))

### Mind-controlled
Sometime after the blood elves escaped, and [Arthas](Arthas Menethil) left to defend the [Lich King](Lich King), Garithos and his forces were launching offensives into the Plaguelands, and soon after they were bewitched and mind-controlled by [Detheroc](Detheroc). Along with Detheroc's undead the Alliance Remnants fought the forces of [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner) and [Varimathras](Varimathras), but were eventually freed from Detheroc's enslavement. (ref:  Sylvanas then proposed an alliance with Garithos, to which he was offended by such an offer as Sylvanas had been part of the undead army that drove the humans out of Lordaeron in the first place. However, running out of military options, he reluctantly agreed to her terms: Garithos' army will aid her in her assault on [Balnazzar](Balnazzar) in exchange for control of Capital City. Garithos assembled his forces and met with Sylvanas outside of her forward base.

### Betrayed
The elves had left the Alliance Remnants and the Wildhammer dwarves were seemingly annihilated,<ref>The Wildhammer are not in [A New Power in Lordaeron](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_New_Power_in_Lordaeron_(WC3_Undead)).) with just humans and Bronzebeard dwarves left; yet the Alliance persisted and went to fight for the [Capital City](Capital City). (ref: 

Sylvanas, Varimathras, and Garithos planned to attack the Capital City. They knew that Balnazzar could only be defeated through attrition, so Sylvanas decided that they would assault his army from two fronts. Garithos' men would attack from the rear, while Sylvanas' forces would attack from the front. During the battle, Garithos' was informed that his dwarven reinforcements were lost. Infuriated, he went out to search for them. When he found them, he insulted them for their incompetence and commanded them to help him in the battle. With both armies attacking from two fronts, Balnazzar's Forces, the Dreadlord Insurgents, and the Dreadlord Loyalists were all defeated.

Balnazzar was "killed" by his brother Varimathras. After this, Garithos ordered Sylvanas and her undead "wretched animals" to leave his city. Sylvanas, however, turned on Garithos and ordered Varimathras to kill him, which the dreadlord gladly did.

Following the death of Grand Marshal Garithos, the survivors of his army rallied under the command of [Captain Silas](Captain Silas), nestling themselves in the [Tirisfal Uplands](Tirisfal Uplands) where they continued battling the Forsaken and attempted to make contact with the kingdom of Stormwind. However, the Remnants' request for food, supplies and soldiers was denied by [General Marcus Jonathan](General Marcus Jonathan).<ref>[quest: A Call to Aid])
