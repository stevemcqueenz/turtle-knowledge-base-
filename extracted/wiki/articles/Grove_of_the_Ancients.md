# Grove of the Ancients

The Grove of the Ancients42 79 Darkshore (g/s/c) is a sacred ruin of an old [night elven](night elven) temple, where [Onu](Onu), an [Ancient of Lore](Ancient of Lore), resides together with a pair of merchants and a sleeping [druid](druid).
The grove lies in the southeastern part of [Darkshore](Darkshore), nestled near the mountains, and is built around the surging [Wildbend River](Wildbend River).
The [Circle of Ancients](Circle of Ancients) meets here.
