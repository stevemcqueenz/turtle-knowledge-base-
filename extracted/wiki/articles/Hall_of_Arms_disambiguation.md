# Hall of Arms (disambiguation)

A **Hall of Arms** is a building or room typically associated with martial and military pursuits. The following are the locations across [Azeroth](Azeroth) that are known as a *Hall of Arms*:

- [Hall of Arms, Northshire Abbey](Hall of Arms (Northshire)); part of the famed [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey) in [Stormwind](Kingdom of Stormwind)'s [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest). 
- [Hall of Arms, Ironforge](Hall of Arms); headquarters of [Ironforge](Ironforge (faction))'s armed forces, located in the [Military Ward](Military Ward). 
- [Hall of Arms, Tyr's Hand Abbey](Hall of Arms (Tyr's Hand)); belonging to the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) as part of [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand) in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands).
