# Scuttle Coast

The [Scuttle Coast](Scuttle Coast) [61, 52] is a portion of the [Great Sea](Great Sea) off the east coast of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), mostly noted by travelers on the east coast of [Durotar](Durotar) southeast of [Razor Hill](Razor Hill) near [Tiragarde Keep](Tiragarde Keep). The coast is littered with ships from [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras), smashed upon the rocks as a result of the great battles fought between the [orcs](orcs) and Admiral [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore)'s fleet. The orcs are still salvaging bits and pieces of [gnome](gnome) technology from the stores of the ships.

The name may be in reference to either the large ships that have been scuttled just off shore, or to the [crab](crab)s and [makrura](makrura)s that scuttle along the shore.
