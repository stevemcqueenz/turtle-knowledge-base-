# Ruins of Ravenwind

The **Ruins of Ravenwind** 41 12 Feralas (g/s/c) are the remains of ancient [Kaldorei](Kaldorei) civilization in northern [Feralas](Feralas), specifically the [Highborne](Highborne) (much like [Dire Maul](Dire Maul)).  It is currently overrun with [Northspring](Northspring) [Harpies](Harpy).

A quest from [Feathermoon Stronghold](Feathermoon Stronghold) sends [Alliance](Alliance) players to this ruin in search of several magic flames and an altar to recharge the [Stave of Equinex](Stave of Equinex).

[Shay Leafrunner](Shay Leafrunner), a rather absent-minded young [Night Elf](Night Elf) girl, can also be found here.  She can be escorted back to her guardian friend, [Rockbiter](Rockbiter), a [mountain giant](mountain giant) to the south of the ruins. (Rockbiter is a reference to Never Ending Story. A reference that becomes even more obvious when you read the quest [dialog](http://www.thottbot.com/?qu=2844))

A ways north of the ruins, players can run into [Miblon Snarltooth](Miblon Snarltooth), a hungry [Gnoll](Gnoll) that zealously guards a small nursery of [evoroot](evoroot).  He has a taste for meat.

There are also a group of Jaedemir dragons near the ruins, who are bent on protecting the large tree located at the center. These dragons make it very difficult for lower level characters to complete the Feathermoon quest, unless they stick to the pond while looking for the flames. If you must travel into that area, the dragonkin will not persue players swimming in the lake, even if previously aggroed. Players more familiar with the quest will know where the flames are right away, avoiding the dragons altogether.
