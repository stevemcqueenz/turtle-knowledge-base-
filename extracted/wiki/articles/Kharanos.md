-Kharanos** is a bustling little town nestled among the mountain peaks of [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh). The town is surrounded by the stark wintry wilds of Dun Morogh, so adventure is a common commodity and some of the less tenacious folk in town might pay good coin to complete various tasks in the surrounding region. Kharanos is home to a large forge, a gnomish [workshop](workshop), and the famous [Thunderbrew Distillery](Thunderbrew Distillery), where adventurers can sit down and drink some of the best ale available in the world.

For all its quaint charm, Kharanos is a hub of military activity, defending against all manner of threats in the region. Directly to the west is a large cavern inhabited by fierce [wendigo](wendigo). To the northwest, the [Frostmane](Frostmane clan) [trolls](Ice troll) have rooted themselves atop [Shimmer Ridge](Shimmer Ridge). Kharanos is where all new recruits fresh from [Anvilmar](Anvilmar) report to begin their adventures in earnest.
