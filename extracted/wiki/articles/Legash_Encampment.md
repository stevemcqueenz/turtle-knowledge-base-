# Legash Encampment

The **Legash Encampment** is located in northern [Azshara](Azshara), east of [Ursolan](Ursolan) and north of the [Thalassian Base Camp](Thalassian Base Camp). It is occupied by [Legashi](Legashi) [satyr](satyr).
