The **Explorers' League**, also known as **Explorers' Guild**, (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos Game Manual*) (ref: <ref>[quest: Into The Temple of Atal'Hakkar]) (ref: [Prospector Ironband#Quotes](Prospector Ironband#Quotes)) **Explorer's League** or simply the **League**, (ref: [quest: The Absent Minded Prospector]) is an organization dedicated to researching the origin of the dwarven race. They have many archaeological dig-sites in all corners of Azeroth, and their main base of operations is located in the [Hall of Explorers](Hall of Explorers) in [Ironforge](Ironforge).

## History
The dwarves once unearthed a series of ruins that held the key to the secrets of their lost heritage. Driven to discover the truth about his people's fabled origins, King Magni Bronzebeard ordered that the dwarves shift their industry from mining and engineering to that of archaeology. He helped to create the famed Explorers' Guild of Ironforge, a group utterly devoted to plumbing the secrets of the ancient world and delving out the truth of the dwarves' fabled existence. (ref: 

At some point, the League discovered [Uldaman](Uldaman). The father of the [Wildhammer clan](Wildhammer clan)'s [Gryphon Master Talonaxe](Gryphon Master Talonaxe) was among them.<ref>[quest: Gryphon Master Talonaxe])

### Third War
[Muradin Bronzebeard](Muradin Bronzebeard), brother of King Magni and one of the first dwarves to establish the Explorer's Guild, led an expedition to the frozen wasteland of [Northrend](Northrend) in search of the runeblade [Frostmourne](Frostmourne). (ref: [The Shores of Northrend (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Shores_of_Northrend_(WC3_Human))) They eventually joined forces with the [Expeditionary Forces](Expeditionary Forces) of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)) against the [Scourge](Scourge), but were left leaderless after [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil) recovered Frostmourne and Muradin was killed. (ref: [Frostmourne (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Frostmourne_(WC3_Human)))

The expedition later regrouped under [Baelgun](Baelgun) and took shelter in the [Shadow Web Caverns](Shadow Web Caverns) of Azjol-Nerub, where they engaged in battle with the surviving native nerubians and tried to claim their treasures. When the earthquakes caused by the Eye of Sargeras awakened the faceless ones in the Old Kingdom, Baelgun and the dwarves sealed its gates and vowed to keep the monsters locked inside. The expedition was ultimately destroyed by Arthas—now a death knight—and Anub'arak as they took a shortcut to Icecrown. (ref: [Into the Shadow Web Caverns (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Into_the_Shadow_Web_Caverns_(WC3_Undead)))

### World of Warcraft
Four years after the battle of Mount Hyjal, the League has its presence in several areas across [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan), such as [Gol'Bolar Quarry](Gol'Bolar Quarry) and [Thelsamar](Thelsamar). Moreover, they spread across the world, including [Darkshore](Darkshore) and were interested in the [Temple of Atal'Hakkar](Temple of Atal'Hakkar) in [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows). (ref: [quest: In Search of The Temple])

The League's use of explosives on the land led to conflict with the [tauren](tauren) in [Mulgore](Mulgore), especially in the southern [Barrens](Barrens). (ref: [quest: Dwarven Digging]) During the conflict, a tribe of tauren were mostly killed off by the League's sub-group called [Bael'dun](Bael'dun), led by [Prospector Khazgorm](Prospector Khazgorm). (ref: [quest: Gann's Reclamation]) (ref: 

The League was interested in [Eldarath](Eldarath) in [Azshara](Azshara) and its ancient runes.<ref>[quest: Arcane Runes])

The League continued excavating [Badlands](Badlands) and Uldaman and entered into an open conflict with the [Shadowforge clan](Shadowforge clan).

They believe there are artifacts in [Alterac Valley](Alterac Valley). (ref: [quest: The Battle of Alterac])
