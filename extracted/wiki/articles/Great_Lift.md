# Great Lift

The **Great Lift** is a level 25-27 sub-region located in [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) between the [Barrens](Barrens) and [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles). The Great Lift carries people between the [Barrens](Barrens) and the rocky floor of [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles), far far below.

The two NPCs guarding the Great Lift,  and , will attack [Alliance](Alliance) players on sight despite Moonhorn being a quest-giver and Longrunner being the recipient of a letter for . Most Alliance players who are of a high level enough to survive in the southern [Barrens](Barrens) have no need to kill either NPC — it is pretty easy to simply run past them onto the ramps without being killed, merely hit a few times. Once an Alliance player steps onto the elevator platform, Moonhorn and/or Grish will stop attacking the player and run back up the ramp to their post. In addition, both guards will hesitate before attacking Alliance players who are a significantly higher level than they are (i.e. any player who sees the level numbers in the guards' portraits as grey).
