# The Sentinels and the Long Vigil

<B style="font-weight:normal;background-color:#2b2b2b;">[  [Source Text](WoWWiki:Article_type)  ]</B>

*The Sentinels and the Long Vigil* is a section of the [History of Warcraft](History of Warcraft), Chapter I.

## Text
With the departure of their wayward cousins, the [night elves](night elf) turned their attention back to the safekeeping of their enchanted homeland. The [druid](druid)s, sensing that their time of hibernation was drawing near, prepared to sleep and leave their loved ones and families behind. [Tyrande](Tyrande Whisperwind), who had become the High Priestess of [Elune](Elune), asked her love, [Malfurion](Malfurion), not to leave her for [Ysera](Ysera)'s [Emerald Dream](Emerald Dream). But Malfurion, honor bound to enter the changing Dreamways, bid the priestess farewell and swore that they would never be apart so long as they held true to their love. 

Left alone to protect [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) from the dangers of the new world, Tyrande assembled a powerful fighting force from amongst her night elf sisters. The fearless, highly trained warrior women who pledged themselves to Kalimdor's defense became known as the Sentinels. Though they preferred to patrol the shadowy forests of Ashenvale on their own, they had many allies upon which they could call in times of urgency. 

The demigod [Cenarius](Cenarius) remained nearby in the [Moonglade](Moonglade)s of [Mount Hyjal](Mount Hyjal). His sons, known as the [Keepers of the Grove](Keeper of the Grove), kept close watch on the night elves and regularly helped the [Sentinel](Sentinel)s maintain peace in the land. Even Cenarius' shy daughters, the [dryad](dryad)s, appeared in the open with increasing frequency. 

The task of policing [Ashenvale](Ashenvale) kept Tyrande busy, but without Malfurion at her side, she knew little joy. As the long centuries passed while the druids slept, her fears of a second demonic invasion grew. She could not shake the unnerving feeling that the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) might still be out there, beyond the [Great Dark](Great Dark) of the sky, plotting its revenge upon the night elves and the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth).
