# Stormwrought Castle

*This article is about the castle exterior. For the dungeon subzone, see [Stormwrought Castle (Stormwrought Ruins)](Stormwrought Castle (Stormwrought Ruins)). For the dungeon, see [Stormwrought Ruins](Stormwrought Ruins).*

  -Stormwrought Castle** are the ruins of the former seat of power of the Balor family, destroyed when [Balor](Balor) was taken by the [Old Horde](Old Horde) during the [Second War](Second War) and now occupied by the orcs of the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) and their masters of the [Shadow Council](Shadow Council). A now-ruined monument to [Daria Balor](Daria Balor) stands prominently in the center of the courtyard.

The entrance to the [Stormwrought Ruins](Stormwrought Ruins) dungeon is located here, accessed by climbing up a siege tower to the castle's ramparts.
