-Archduke Keirich** (High German pronunciation: *ˈʃtɛlʊs ˈkaɪʁɪç*), born **Stellus Keirich**, is a female [Blood Death Knight](Blood Death Knight) affiliated with the [Scourge](Scourge). She is a noble scion of the fallen [Kingdom of Lordaeron](Kingdom of Lordaeron) with maternal blood ties to [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras). In the *Chronicle of Myers*, she embodies the tragic elegance of Lordaeron's lost nobility blended with the untamed spirit of the sea, while wielding both runeblade and necrotic song.

## Biography
### Early Life and Resurrection
Stellus Keirich was born in the capital of Lordaeron shortly before the outbreak of the Third War. Her father, Archmage Albert Keirich, was a highborn Lordaeronian noble of ancient Austrian-influenced lineage. Her mother was a woman from Kul Tiras — a seafaring nation known for its deep connection to the Tidemother and the waves. This mixed heritage gave young Stellus a unique blend of continental refinement and maritime resilience.

Condemned to death at birth for reasons shrouded in family secrecy, the infant was saved by her father through a forbidden ritual that combined Lordaeronian necromantic arts with enchanted music. This resurrection not only restored life but transformed Stellus's body from male to female, marking her as one of the most unusual rebirths in the eastern kingdoms. The ritual left a permanent echo of song within her soul — a gift and curse that would define her existence.

### The Fall of Lordaeron and Path to Undeath
As the Scourge ravaged Lordaeron, Stellus witnessed the kingdom she loved crumble under Prince Arthas's betrayal. Already touched by death once, she was claimed by the Lich King's forces and raised as a [Blood Death Knight](Death Knight). Unlike many who lost themselves to the plague, she retained fragments of her noble identity and her mother's seafaring spirit.

Even in service to the Scourge, Archduke Keirich maintains a degree of independence. She often wanders the plague-ridden lands of Tirisfal and the Western Plaguelands, where echoes of old Lordaeron still linger, occasionally venturing toward the coasts that remind her of her Kul Tiran blood.

### The Musician of the Damned
Death could not silence the music that once saved her. Stellus channels necrotic energies through haunting melodies and guitar-like stringed instruments, creating songs that can soothe the restless dead, torment the living, or briefly awaken faint memories in other undead. She is fluent in both the Common tongue of Lordaeron (with a refined High German nobility accent) and the [Kul Tiran language](Kul Tiran (language)) inherited from her mother — a lyrical, wave-like dialect rich in nautical metaphors and rhythmic cadence (often compared by scholars to the flowing poetry of distant eastern isles).

She co-founded the shadowy musical ensemble HOSIBOSI with her wife Hoshino Ryoko and serves as mentor to Shibatou Atsumi, guiding them through both the shadows of undeath and the remnants of mortal artistry.

## Appearance
Archduke Keirich stands approximately 170 cm tall with a graceful yet battle-hardened physique. She has vibrant orange-red hair usually tied in a high right-side ponytail, and glowing ice-blue eyes that betray her Death Knight nature. Her skin is eerily pale and smooth, retaining an almost lifelike quality due to her unique resurrection. She alternates between heavy rune-etched plate armor bearing Lordaeronian crests and flowing garments reminiscent of Kul Tiran seafarer fashion.

## Personality
Keirich is charismatic, resilient, and pragmatic. She carries the refined pride of Lordaeron nobility tempered by the free-spirited resilience of Kul Tiras. The traumas of her birth, transformation, and kingdom's fall have forged a strong will and a quiet melancholy. She is fiercely protective of her family and protégés, yet maintains a detached amusement toward the endless conflicts of the living and the dead.

## Abilities
As a Blood Death Knight, her powers focus on blood magic and self-sustainment:
- **Blood Boil**: Spreads disease and pain through corrupted blood.
- **Death Strike**: Delivers shadow-infused blows that heal her wounds.
- **Rune Tap**: Strengthens her defenses with runic power.

Her mother's Kul Tiran heritage grants her the ability to infuse songs with necrotic magic, allowing performances to influence emotions, drain life force, or offer rare moments of solace to the undead.

## Relationships
- **Albert Keirich (Father)**: The Lordaeronian archmage who performed her resurrection and shaped her early power.
- **Hoshino Ryoko (Wife)**: Her steadfast companion in both life and undeath, sharing musical and personal bonds.
- **Hoshino Atsuko / Hoshino Kadosada (Child)**: The next generation of her unconventional family.
- **Shibatou Atsumi (Protégé)**: A young talent she guides and protects.

## Trivia
- She speaks with a refined High German (Austrian-style) accent in Common, a remnant of her Lordaeronian noble upbringing.
- She is fluent in the Kul Tiran language, often using its rhythmic, sea-inspired phrases in songs or private moments.
- Her mixed Lordaeron-Kul Tiras heritage makes her a bridge between continental nobility and maritime culture, even after becoming undead.
