# Ruins of Kel'Theril

-Not to be confused with [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad).*

  -The Ruins of Kel'Theril** are the remnants of the great [night elven](night elven) city of Kel'Theril. It was destroyed long ago during [the Sundering](the Sundering), and it now lies in complete ruin, haunted by the tormented spirits of the [Highborne](Highborne) who did not survive the disaster.  The ruins lie on the edges of [Lake Kel'Theril](Lake Kel'Theril) in [Winterspring](Winterspring).

There is a [Remorseful Highborne](Remorseful Highborne) here to give a quest to Alliance, seemingly she is part of the alliance herself, which is quite strange.
