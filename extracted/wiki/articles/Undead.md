# Undead

# Overview
The term **undead** refers to all creatures or entities that function normally despite having terminated life functions. Most commonly known as [zombie](zombie)s or [ghost](ghost)s, undead beings are most typically mindless, bloodthirsty fiends hostile toward any living thing that comes across their path.

The undead have appeared all throughout [Azeroth](Azeroth), most commonly in nature as [Revenant](Revenant)s or [wraith](wraith)s. They were used as a combat tool during the [First War](First War) when the [Necrolytes](Necrolytes) of the [Horde](Horde) learned to animate corpses as [skeleton](skeleton) warriors in their fight against the [Alliance](Alliance). Later, the studies of the Necrolytes developed into [Necromancy](Necromancy), the form of magic used very heavily in the [Third War](Third War) when the [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned) spread the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath) across [Lordaeron](Lordaeron).

In modern times, 'undead' refers primarily to either the [Scourge](Scourge) or the [Forsaken](Forsaken), since nearly half of the [human](human) race have since become undead. 

- Cannot be [skinned](skinnable), except for certain undead animals that would otherwise be classified as [Beast](Beast)s (i.e.: [bear](bear)s, [boar](boar)s, [wolves](wolf), etc.).
- Generally are unaffected by mind affecting spells, like [fear](fear), [hibernate](hibernate), [sleep](sleep) or [mind control](mind control).
