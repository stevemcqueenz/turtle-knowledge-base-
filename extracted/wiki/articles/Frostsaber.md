# Frostsaber

Large cat used as a [mount](mount) for the [Night Elves](Night_elf). The Frostsaber is similar in appearance to a real-world [tiger](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger), but is white with black stripes. Frostsabers can also have spots like a [cheetah](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheetah) or [leopard](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leopard). The typical Frostsaber is a color variation of the [Nightsaber](Nightsaber) - the [Nightsaber](Nightsaber) is black with white stripes.  A variety of the Frostsaber is the purple [Wintersaber](Wintersaber).
