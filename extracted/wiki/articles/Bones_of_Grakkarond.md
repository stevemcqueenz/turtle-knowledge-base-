# Bones of Grakkarond

The **Bones of Grakkarond** lie in central [Silithus](Silithus). They are the huge bones of the [Bronze Dragon](Bronze Dragon) [Grakkarond](Grakkarond), who died during the [War of the Shifting Sands](War of the Shifting Sands).

He was slain by [Ossirian the Unscarred](Ossirian the Unscarred).

## Patch changes
- **Patch 1.4.0 (2005-04-19):** The dragon bones just to the southwest of The Swarming Pillar in Silithus have been named the Bones of Grakkarond.
