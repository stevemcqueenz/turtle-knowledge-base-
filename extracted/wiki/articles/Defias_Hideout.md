# Defias Hideout

The **Defias Hideout** is a building in [Moonbrook](Moonbrook) and contains the hidden entrance upstairs that leads into the [Deadmines](Deadmines). It is often unoccupied because of the need for the bandits to be outside protecting the town from outsiders, or down in the mines themselves, working.
