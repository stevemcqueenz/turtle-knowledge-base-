# Gahrron's Withering

**Gahrron's Withering** is located at the eastern end of the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands). Originally the area was a farm, but the place has been taken over by the [Scourge](Scourge). They have placed a large cauldron in the middle of the field which constantly spews disease-filled vapors into the air. The cauldron is guarded by [Cauldron Lord Soulwrath](Cauldron Lord Soulwrath) and other Scourge officers [Haunting Visions](Haunting Vision) and many [Wailing Deaths](Wailing Death) and [Hungering Wraiths](Hungering Wraith). Gahrron's Withering is a popular place for grinding [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn) [reputation](reputation), as the quest involving the [Plague Cauldron](Plague Cauldron) located here provides the best reputation of the four Cauldron Quests.
