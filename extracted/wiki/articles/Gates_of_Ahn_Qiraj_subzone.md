# Gates of Ahn'Qiraj (subzone)

The **Gates of Ahn'Qiraj** is an area located right after the [Scarab Wall](Scarab Wall), in southwestern [Silithus](Silithus). It became available during the [Gates of Ahn'Qiraj](Gates of Ahn'Qiraj) event, allowing access to the instances of the [Temple](Temple of Ahn'Qiraj) and [Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj](Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj).
