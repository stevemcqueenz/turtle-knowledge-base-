General **Turalyon** was a paladin who served the [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron) army with distinction during and immediately after the [Second War](Second War). A knight of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)), he was one of the first paladins trained by [Uther the Lightbringer](Uther the Lightbringer), and was second in command to Lord [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar), the Supreme Allied Commander. When Lord Lothar was struck down in combat with [Orgrim Doomhammer](Orgrim Doomhammer) during the assault on [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire), it was General Turalyon that picked up Lothar's battle standard and broken sword and rallied the army of the Alliance to victory over the Horde, eventually capturing Doomhammer, and driving the orcs back to the Dark Portal.

Only a few years later, General Turalyon was handpicked by King [Terenas Menethil II](Terenas Menethil II) to join an [expeditionary force](Alliance Expedition) through the reformed [Dark Portal](Dark Portal) and into [Draenor](Draenor). There, it was discovered that [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul), the elder shaman of the [Shadowmoon clan](Shadowmoon clan), was planning to use a number of powerful artifacts to open more portals, allowing the Horde to escape and plunder other worlds. The Alliance forces ultimately failed to stop Ner'zhul from opening and escaping into his portals, and the subsequent chaotic energy the portals released began to destroy the planet. Rather than risk Azeroth being damaged by Draenor's destruction, Turalyon selflessly assisted Archmage [Khadgar](Khadgar) in destroying the Dark Portal on Draenor.
