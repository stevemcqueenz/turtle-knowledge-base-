# Blackwood Lake

**Blackwood Lake** is a polluted lake in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands). Its waters are infested by corrupted [water elemental](water elemental)s. Blackwood Lake has been mentioned in the quest item [:Extended Annals of Darrowshire](:Extended Annals of Darrowshire) from the quest [Brother Carlin](Brother Carlin) as being the resting place of [Horgus the Ravager](Horgus the Ravager)'s head.

There is a small Argent Dawn camp here with an [Argent Medic](Argent Medic) tending to infantry.
