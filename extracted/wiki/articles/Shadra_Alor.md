# Shadra'Alor

Built by the [Troll](Troll)s of the [Amani Empire](Amani Empire), [Shadra'Alor](Shadra'Alor) is a temple to the spider god of the [Witherbark](Witherbark) Tribe of [Forest Troll](Forest Troll)s in the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands).
