## Objectives
Speak with Akh Z'ador in the Harborage.

## Description
Much needs to be done, the Harborage is in disarray and many have been injured. You have to excuse me, as I need to tend to my brethren. While any help would be appreciated, I must insist on leaving this to us for now. You have done enough already and it'd be rude to ask for more.

Before you depart however, Akh Z'ador wishes to speak with you again about something urgent. You can find him outside. Enkil Toralar, friend, and good tidings to you.

## Rewards
- [Pure Draenethyst Gemstone](Pure Draenethyst Gemstone)
- 4300 [XP](XP)

## Completion
<name>, I thank you from the bottom of my heart for all you have done for the Harborage. Kas'tal managed to contact me just in time. Any later and I fear for what might've happened. Now, to what I wanted to talk with you about…

<Akh Z'ador presents a flawlessly round and shimmering teal crystal to you unlike anything you've seen before.>

What you see here is a pure draenethyst gemstone, one of the most rare commodities my people valued back on Draenor. Its status is akin to myth, although they are not the fairytale one might assume. Althoug it is just a theory, I assume this one was created between the clash of Tas'dal's and my own rift magic. I want you to have it, as thanks - but also as a promise. The draenei of the Harborage will aid you if needed, for without you, we'd be no more. Enkil Toralar, friend, and may the winds of the Twisting Nether be in your favor.
