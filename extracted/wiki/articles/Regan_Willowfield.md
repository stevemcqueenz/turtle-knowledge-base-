-Regan Willowfield** was an unlanded noble and knight of [Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)) that served as a messenger for the court of King [Genn Greymane](Genn Greymane). At the beginning of the regency of Lord [Mortimer](Regent-Lord Mortimer Harlow) and Lady [Celia Harlow](Regent-Lady Celia Harlow), Lord Regan was tasked with carrying a message to the court of [Lord Darius Ravenwood](Lord Darius Ravenwood) within [Ravenwood Keep](Ravenwood Keep), but was crucified at the rebellious lord's command and his escorts hanged after Viscount [Laurion](Laurion) brought forth a blade that had been bloodied by one of the commanders of the Harlow host camped within Lord Ravenwood's lands, being one of the first casualties of the First Rebellion. (ref: [The Raven's Sentence and the First Rebellion](The Raven's Sentence and the First Rebellion))
