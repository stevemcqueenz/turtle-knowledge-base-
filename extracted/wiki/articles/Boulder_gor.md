# Boulder'gor

[Boulder'gor](Boulder'gor) is a hill found north of [Stromgarde Keep](Stromgarde Keep) in the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). It is the hangout of several [forest troll](forest troll)s, as well as several [ogre](ogre)s of the [Boulderfist](Boulderfist) tribe, based out of the [Boulderfist Outpost](Boulderfist Outpost) which has been carved into the hillside.
