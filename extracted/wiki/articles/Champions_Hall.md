# Champions' Hall

The **Champions' Hall** is the officer's barracks for the [Alliance](Alliance) in [Stormwind](Stormwind). It is located in the south of the Old Town. 

The Horde counterpart of the Champion's Hall is the [Hall of Legends](Hall of Legends) in Orgrimmar.

## Trivia
Several of the humans within the "Champions' Hall" in the Stormwind city barracks are named after the major characters from the [Stargate series](wikipedia:Stargate).
