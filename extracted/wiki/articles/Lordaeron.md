*This article is about the continent. For the [human kingdom](Seven Kingdoms) of the same name, see [Lordaeron (kingdom)](Lordaeron (kingdom)).*

  -Lordaeron** is the northernmost continent of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms). It was named after the [city-state of Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)) by the lords of [Strom](Strom). (ref: [The Seven Kingdoms](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Seven_Kingdoms)) Separated from [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) by a narrow strait, the continent of Lordaeron stretches from the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands) in the south to the [Eversong Wastes](Eversong Wastes) in the far north. Historically, the continent has been populated by [human](human)s in the northern, western and southern regions, [troll](troll)s in the eastern regions; the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands) and [Zul'Aman](Zul'Aman), and [high elves](High elf) in the far-northern [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas).

The climate of Lordaeron is moderate, although the land is rugged and largely mountainous in and around the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands), [Alterac](Alterac Mountains) and [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). The southern, northern, and western regions of [Hillsbrad](Hillsbrad Foothills), [Gilneas](Gilneas), and [Tirisfal](Tirisfal Glades) are characterized by mild forests and farmlands whereas the north-eastern region of [Zul'Aman](Zul'Aman) is covered by a thick and wild forest.
