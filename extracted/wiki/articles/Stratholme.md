# Stratholme

The city of **Stratholme** was the northernmost city of the Kingdom of Lordaeron, and was the city where the Order of the Silver Hand was founded. Stratholme was destroyed during the Third War by the undead Scourge, and has been split between the overzealous Scarlet Crusade led by Grand Crusader Dathrohan, and the Scourge forces led by the death knight Baron Rivendare.

# History
The second largest human city in [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), matched only by the capital, Stratholme was the base of key oil refineries in the [Second War](Second War). In its glory days this jewel of humanity's largest and proudest nation housed more than 50,000 people, and included many well known buildings, from the glorious keep to Fras Siabi's famous tobacco shop.

The city's population was almost completely massacred by [Arthas](Arthas), who believed the citizens had become infected with the Undead plague; the majority of the survivors left with [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore). When Arthas was called back to Northrend, the Lich, [Kel'thuzad](Kel'thuzad) established Stratholme as his capital and constructed his citadel, known as [Naxxramas](Naxxramas), above the ruined towers. Leading [Scourge](Scourge) forces in the city below was a [death knight](death knight) known as [Baron Rivendare](Baron Rivendare), a former noble of Lordaeron who coordinated the efforts of the [Lich King](Lich King) in the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands) until his death at the hands of operatives in the employ of the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn). Today the city houses a few thousand Scarlet Crusade warriors who fight valiantly (or fanatically, depending on who you ask) against the thousands of undead.

  *Once the jewel of northern Lordaeron, the city of Stratholme is where Prince Arthas turned against his mentor, [Uther Lightbringer](Uther Lightbringer), and slaughtered hundreds of his own subjects who were believed to have contracted the dreaded [plague](plague) of undeath. Arthas' downward spiral and ultimate surrender to the Lich King soon followed. The broken city is now inhabited by the undead Scourge -- led by the powerful lich, Kel'thuzad. A contingent of [Scarlet Crusaders](Scarlet Crusade), led by Grand Crusader [Dathrohan](Dathrohan), also holds a portion of the ravaged city. The two sides are locked in constant, violent combat. Those adventurers brave (or foolish) enough to enter Stratholme will be forced to contend with both factions before long. It is said that the city is guarded by three massive watchtowers, as well as powerful [necromancers](necromancer), [banshees](banshee) and [abominations](abomination). There have also been reports of a malefic [Death Knight](Death Knight) riding atop an unholy steed, dispensing indiscriminate wrath on all those who venture within the realm of the Scourge.*

# Summary
[Map at Worldofwar.net](http://www.worldofwar.net/cartography/instances/stratholme.php)
- **Also known as:** Strat, Strath
- **Found in:** [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands)
- 5 Player Cap (Prior to [Patch 1.10](Patch 1.10 (Release Notes)) it was a 10 Player cap)

Stratholme is basically divided into two parts, at least in the minds of players. Scarlet-Side, which goes from the main entrance into the Scarlet Crusade-controlled citadel, and Scourge-Side, which goes from the Service Entrance (which requires a key) to a Scourge Slaughterhouse. However, both "sides" are part of the same instance and players can complete both tracks.
