# Malaka'jin

-Malaka'Jin** is a [Horde](Horde) [camp](camp) in the [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains), [71, 94] just south of the [Grimtotem Post](Grimtotem Post). It houses a small community of [troll](troll) [hunter](hunter)s who make a modest income by selling their catches to passing Horde adventurers. 

The trolls at Malaka'Jin are also mounting covert attacks against the [night elves](night elves) of [Stonetalon Peak](Stonetalon Peak), lead by the efforts of [Witch Doctor Jin'Zil](Witch Doctor Jin'Zil). Inside this troll camp, night elves are being held by the [shaman](shaman), [Jin'Zil](Jin'Zil). He seeks [ingredient](ingredient)s for his [voodoo](voodoo) [potion](potion)s. However, there are not enough trolls here to be a great threat to the night elves. [Brann Bronzebeard](Brann Bronzebeard) once picked off a good number of the trolls here before they noticed him. He felt good for protecting the night elves, but since they were not much of a threat, he did not put more effort into killing them.
