# Angerfang Encampment

The **Angerfang Encampment**, located in the southern [Wetlands](Wetlands), is home to a group of orcs which form the remnants of the [Dragonmaw Clan](Dragonmaw Clan). After the [Second War](Second War), the clan fled to the mountains to escape from the wrath of their former red dragon pets and established the encampment as their new base of operations. Their current plan is to use the encampment as a rallying point to attack [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor). 

## Characters and Items of Interest
-The current leader of the Dragonmaw Clan is [Nek'rosh Skullcrusher](Nek'rosh Skullcrusher), son of [Nekros Skullcrusher](Nekros Skullcrusher) who captured [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza) in order to provide dragon mounts for the [Horde](Horde) during the Second War. Alliance adventurers will be sent for Nek'rosh's head by [Captain Stoutfist](Captain Stoutfist) in the quest [quest: 465].
- The Dragonmaw Orcs are known for their very strong bones. [Velora Nitely](Velora Nitely) will send Horde [Warrior](Warrior)s after their shinbones to create [Brutal Legguards](Brutal Legguards) in the quest [quest: 1841].
- Dragonmaw warlocks are known to use Rods of Channeling, required by [warlock](warlock) players in the quest [quest: 1804].
