**Leonid Barthalomew the Revered** is an [undead](undead) member of the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn), who is revered by his colleagues for the sacrifices he has made. He investigated the return of the powerful [lich](lich), [Ras Frostwhisper](Ras Frostwhisper), in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands). Barthalomew looks at his [undeath](undeath) as a malady, an illness that merely requires treatment. However, he also considers it a great blessing for the Argent Dawn, believing that, as an undead, he is a great vessel to smite the abominations of the [Scourge](Scourge). (ref: [quest: Houses of the Holy])

## History
### Third War
At the time of the Third War, Leonid was a paladin of the [Knights of the Silver Hand](Knights of the Silver Hand). After Prince Arthas declared that the city of [Stratholme](Stratholme) had to be purged, [Uther the Lightbringer](Uther the Lightbringer) and his paladins left [the prince's base](Forlorn Summit). However, Leonid remained behind to protect the innocent people remaining there. When Prince Arthas left for Northrend, the Cult of the Damned infiltrated the refugees that sought refuge in the mountains, and Leonid fell back to [the mine](Widow's Nest) with the remaining refugees. Despite his unwavering devotion to the Light, it was not enough against the hordes of undead. (ref: 

Leonid was killed and later resurrected by the [Lich King](Lich King) as a mindless [undead](undead) of the [Scourge](Scourge). He was later present at Stratholme when [Ras Frostwhisper](Ras Frostwhisper) pledged himself to [Arthas](Arthas) and was transformed into a [lich](lich).<ref name="Menethil's Gift">[quest: Menethil's Gift]) He was freed by the Banshee Queen [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner) but has been loyal to the cause of the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn). (ref: [quest: The Dying, Ras Frostwhisper])

### World of Warcraft
Years after the [Third War](Third War), Leonid can be found in [Light's Hope Chapel](Light's Hope Chapel) in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands) as a member of the Argent Dawn. He helps the adventurers of the [Alliance](Alliance) and [Horde](Horde) to deal with Ras Frostwhisper. (ref:  When he learned about the adventurers helping [Tinkee Steamboil](Tinkee Steamboil) in obtaining frozen [black dragon](black dragon) eggs for [Vectus](Vectus), Leonid tricked Tinkee into sending the adventurers with the eggs back to him<ref>[quest: Leonid Barthalomew]) in order to thwart their experiments on creating the [plagued dragonflight](plagued dragonflight). Subsequently, he has the adventurers deliver the eggs to [Betina Bigglezink](Betina Bigglezink). (ref: [quest: Betina Bigglezink])
