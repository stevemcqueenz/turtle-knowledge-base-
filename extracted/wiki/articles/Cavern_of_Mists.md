# Cavern of Mists

The **Cavern of Mists** (or the **Wailing Cavern**) is a section of the [Wailing Caverns](Wailing Caverns) located just outside the instance. Geysers erupt from the cavern floor, forming a verdant pool in the center of the chamber. The mist from these geysers hangs heavy on the still air. The caverns is dominated by the central pool, and various creatures roam the area, including [raptor](raptor)s, [ooze](ooze)s, [wind serpent](wind serpent)s, [snake](snake)s, and [toad](toad)s. [Trigore the Lasher](Trigore the Lasher), a rare [hydra](hydra), is sometimes seen in the water among the [threshadon](threshadon)s.
