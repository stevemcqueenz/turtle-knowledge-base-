# Hall of Arms (Tyr's Hand)

The **Hall of Arms** wing of [Tyr's Hand Abbey](Tyr's Hand Abbey), located in [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand) in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), was home to numerous militant members of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade). It is situated south of the [Main Hall](Main Hall (Tyr's Hand)), and the [Library Wing](Library Wing (Tyr's Hand)) lies on the opposite side of the abbey. The *Hall of Arms* points towards the east and only slightly north.
