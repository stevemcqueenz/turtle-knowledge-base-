# Magic Quarter

[The Magic Quarter](The Magic Quarter) is located in the northeastern parts of the [Undercity](Undercity). This is where the most arcane-inclined of the [Forsaken](Forsaken) come to research, learn new spells, and practice their arts, under the watchful eye of [Bethor Iceshard](Bethor Iceshard). 

Facilities:
- [Mage](Mage) trainers
- [Warlock](Warlock) and their pets' trainers
- [Tailoring](Tailoring) training to [Expert](Expert) (skill level 225) (inner ring)
- [Portal](Portal) trainer
