-Krazek** is a [goblin](goblin) [quest giver](quest giver) located in the [Salty Sailor Tavern](Salty Sailor Tavern) in [Booty Bay](Booty Bay) in the [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale). He describes himself as secretary to [Baron Revilgaz](Baron Revilgaz), and has been involved in manufacturing mechanical cooking pots under the brand [Krazek's Crock Pot](Krazek's Crock Pot) and delivering supplies and information to various people.
