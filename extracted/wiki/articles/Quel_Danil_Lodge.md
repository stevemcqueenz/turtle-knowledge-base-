# Quel'Danil Lodge

''Not to be confused with [Quel'Lithien Lodge](Quel'Lithien Lodge)

[Quel'Danil Lodge](Quel'Danil Lodge) is the larger of two [High Elf](High Elf) outposts in [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). It acts as an embassy of sorts to continue the remaining High Elves' diplomatic relations with other races.

# Quest Guide
## Quests to Get Before Arriving
- [quest: 2995] - from [Oran Snakewrithe](Oran Snakewrithe) in [Undercity](Undercity)
- [quest: 7841] - from [Otho Moji'ko](Otho Moji'ko) in [Revantusk Village](Revantusk Village)

## Quests to Start
Inside the lodge is a [Witherbark Troll](Witherbark Troll) named [Rin'ji](Rin'ji). Speaking to him begins an escort quest from Quel'Danil southward to [Hiri'Watha](Hiri'Watha). After finishing the escort portion of the quest, head to the island east of [The Overlook Cliffs](The Overlook Cliffs). There will be [Rin'ji's Secret](Rin'ji's Secret).
