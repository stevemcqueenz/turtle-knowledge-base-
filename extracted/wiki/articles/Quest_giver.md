# Quest giver

-Quest givers** are [NPC](NPC)s that provide [quest](quest)s for players. When the quests are completed, the quest giver will usually bestow [XP](XP), or, if the character is the maximum level obtainable, money. Sometimes, quest givers will include an item or two as a token of their thanks. Questgiving NPCs will not usually attack [monsters](monsters) or the opposing [faction](faction) unless they have been attacked first.
