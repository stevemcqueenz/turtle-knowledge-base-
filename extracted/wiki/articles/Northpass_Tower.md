# Northpass Tower

[Northpass Tower](Northpass Tower) is a ruined tower located in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), and an objective in the [Game of Towers](A Game of Towers) world PvP event.  When players capture this tower, memebers of their faction can use an alter located inside the tower, which grants a 30 minute buff that increases the player's hp by 5%.

Northpass is another tower likely to be fought over regularly, as the reward and location are both relevent to groups running [Stratholme](Stratholme). The possible presence of spectral reinforcements from [Eastwall Tower](Eastwall Tower) also make this one of the funner towers to fight over.
