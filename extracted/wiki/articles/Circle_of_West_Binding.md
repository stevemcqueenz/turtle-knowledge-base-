# Circle of West Binding

One of the [Circles of Binding](Circles of Binding) in [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). It lies in the western part of the region, near [Thoradin's Wall](Thoradin's Wall), and is guarded by [fire elemental](fire elemental)s.
