**Falstad Wildhammer**, also known as **Falstad Dragonreaver**, (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, chapter 7) is the High Thane of the [Wildhammer clan](Wildhammer clan). He ruled the Wildhammer clan from [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak) in the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands) following the departure of [Kurdran Wildhammer](Kurdran Wildhammer) and joined his companions [Rhonin](Rhonin) and [Vereesa](Vereesa Windrunner) in freeing the Dragon Queen [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza) from [Grim Batol](Grim Batol).

## Biography
### Day of the Dragon
Falstad Wildhammer was a noble gryphon rider of the [Wildhammer clan](Wildhammer clan) of the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands), commanding a small flight of such riders who patrolled the shores of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) for any hostile draconic activity after the [Second War](Second War). Falstad and his comrades protected the town of [Hasic](Hasic) from certain doom, and then transported human mage [Rhonin](Rhonin) and his companions across the [Great Sea](Great Sea) to [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan). During the journey, however, the group was ambushed and Falstad's kinsmen were killed. Nevertheless, at the incentive of [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner), Falstad continued to Khaz Modan. Later, with the help of a clan of hill dwarves, Falstad reached [Grim Batol](Grim Batol) and fought in the final battle there to free the Red Dragon Queen [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza).

### World of Warcraft
Now High Thane, Falstad appears as a quest giver located at [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak) in the Hinterlands where he deals with the encroaching forest trolls.
