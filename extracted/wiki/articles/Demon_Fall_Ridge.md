# Demon Fall Ridge

-Demon Fall Ridge** is located in [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), just west and above of [Demon Fall Canyon](Demon Fall Canyon). It is home to several kinds of lesser demons, who inhabit both the exterior part of the ridge, as well as the interior of an abandoned [barrow den](barrow den) set into the side of the ridge. In the deepest parts of this den waits [Diathorus the Seeker](Diathorus the Seeker) - a dreadlord and ruler of the demonic conclave in Ashenvale.
