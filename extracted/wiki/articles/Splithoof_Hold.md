# Splithoof Hold

Splithoof Hold is a cave that the [Galak](Galak) [centaur](centaur) tribe has adapted into a stronghold. The entire cave system is populated with centaur. The hold is located in [Splithoof Crag](Splithoof Crag) in [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles).
