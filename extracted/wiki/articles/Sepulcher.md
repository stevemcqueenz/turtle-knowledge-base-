# Sepulcher

As its name suggests, **the Sepulcher** was once nothing more than graveyard along the road to [Lordaeron](Lordaeron)'s Capital City.  Since the end of the [Second Invasion](Second Invasion) however, the enterprising [Forsaken](Forsaken) have converted the old crypt into their base of operations for [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest).  With [Worgen](Worgen) to the south, the [Scourge](Scourge) to the east, and the forces of [Dalaran](Dalaran) to the southeast, it is one of the only places of safety for the Horde in the entire zone.

The Sepulcher has a [Mining](Mining) and [Blacksmithing](Blacksmithing) Trainer, but no [Class Trainers](Class Trainers). It also has an inn, a bat handler, and several vendors and quest givers. Young Forsaken usually arrive here at levels 10-13 when the quests in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades) run out. Unfortunately, the Sepulcher does not offer enough quests to keep going continually until one can head to [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills), and thus, most Forsaken are forced to end up in [the Barrens](the Barrens).
