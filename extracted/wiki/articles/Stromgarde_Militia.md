The **Stromgarde Militia** (ref: [quest: Wanted! Otto and Falconcrest]) is the force protecting [Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)). The crimson-clad defenders are known for the martial discipline in battle and has proven themselves in the [Second War](Second War) which they were able to hold off the entire Horde for quite a while on their own, allowing the rest of the Alliance to mobilize. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*) Possessing a long history of warfare against the trolls, their warriors are well prepared to engage in open combat with all enemies of humanity. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Nations of the Alliance](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Stromgarde)*) Members of the Stromgarde Militia can be identified by their [Stromgarde Badge](Stromgarde Badge)s. (ref: [quest: Stromgarde Badges])

Following the [Third War](Third War) and the fall of Stromgarde, the militia still exists and is based in [Refuge Pointe](Refuge Pointe), while a contingent of their defenders are also firmly entrenched in the [Gallant Square](Gallant Square) district of [Stromgarde Keep](Stromgarde Keep) itself. (ref: [quest: Sigil of Trollbane]) The capital is a battlefield where they face the [Witherbark](Witherbark) trolls, the [Boulderfist](Boulderfist) ogres, and the criminals of the [Syndicate](Syndicate).
