-Anomalus** is the third boss of [Tower of Karazhan](Tower of Karazhan). An avatar of pure magic, (ref: [Turtle WoW Team on Twitter](https://x.com/turtlewowteam/status/1819688372861907427) (2024-08-03)) it is a result of the wild leylines stirring, taking form as a living force driven by vengeance. (ref: [FACE DESOLATION: Tower of Karazhan Raid Trailer | Turtle WoW Vanilla+](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b7z-yAdPchQ)) If not contained, Anomalus threatens to damage the leylines across [Azeroth](Azeroth), and Karazhan itself may crumble to dust.
