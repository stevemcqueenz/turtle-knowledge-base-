# Cut-Throat Alley

-Cut-Throat Alley** was a formerly unpopulated alley of the city of [Stormwind](Stormwind). The alley was technically located in the [Dwarven District](Dwarven District); however, it did not share the smoggy ambiance of the District.

Cut-Throat Alley could only be reached on foot by passing through a shop found on the Dwarven side of the [Canals](Canals (Stormwind City)), just south of the footbridge from [Cathedral Square](Cathedral Square). The alley's only features were a number of crates and barrels, and the only accessible buildings from it were the shop and an empty two-story, one-bedroom residence. It did not connect to the rest of the Dwarven District.

The alley was refurbished and granted to the high elf refugees by the House of Nobles, and was renamed to [Little Silvermoon](Little Silvermoon). (ref: [quest: A New Place in Stormwind] *([Deprecated](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=80220))*) (ref: [quest: Thalassian Diplomacy])
