# Nightmare

Nightmares are a type of demonic creature that lives in the Twisting Nether, that are encountered alone or are ridden by night hag coveys. They are part of the forces of the Burning Legion. Perhaps they include [felsteed](felsteed)s and [dreadsteed](dreadsteed)s, or are similar to them.
