# Rogues' Quarter

The **Rogues' Quarter** is located in the southeastern corner of the [Undercity](Undercity). It is home to many of the [Forsaken's](Forsaken) more shady characters, the rogues' guild, and a few profession trainers.

Facilities:
- [Rogue](Rogue) trainers.
- [Poison](Poison) vendor
- [Leatherworking](Leatherworking) trainers
- [Skinning](Skinning) trainers
- [Engineering](Engineering) trainers
- [First Aid](First Aid) trainers
