## Quotes
### Gossip
Hello, and welcome to Sorrowguard. I am Paladin Janathos, and I keep vigil over this place.

This stronghold was built before the First War as a means to watch the entrance westward into the Kingdom of Azeroth.

It has fallen into disrepair since its fall in the First War.

After its destruction, I was sent here to reclaim it. While we have done repairs and occupied the place, there is always more that can be done. Especially with the darkness of the swamps beside us, and the orcs lurking in Stonard.
