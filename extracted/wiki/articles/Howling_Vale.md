# Howling Vale

**The Howling Vale** 52 37 Ashenvale (g/s/c) is a cave located in north-central [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), just southwest of the border to [Felwood](Felwood). The vale is home to a tribe of [worgen](worgen) called the [Terrowulf](Terrowulf), and they have completely overtaken the ruined Shrine of Mel'Thandris located there. It is the worgen's chilling calls that have given the vale its new name. [Alliance](Alliance) adventurers come here from time to time in search of the legacy of the famed sentinel [Velinde Starsong](Velinde Starsong).
