# Hippogryph

Within [World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft), **hippogryphs** serve as the primary flying public transport [mount](mount) between cities controlled by the [night elves](night elves).  

## Lore
Having sworn allegiance to the night elves in honor of [Cenarius](Cenarius), the magical hippogryphs now patrol the skies above Northern [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), scanning the horizon for invaders. 

Contrary to the common belief, hippogryphs are not simply beasts that act as night elf mounts, as they are highly intelligent. Though they will allow other races to ride them, they are wary of non-elf races; especially since [kobold](kobold)s and [human](human)s have both attempted to enslave hippogryphs.

During the war, these magnificent creatures, with bodies that resemble both stags and ravens, were ridden into battle by [Sentinel](Sentinel) archers. They have since agreed to serve as paid transport for the [Alliance](Alliance), carrying travelers along with them on their patrol routes.

Night elf [sentinel](sentinel)s watch over the hippogryph egs, and the night elves are seen as part of the hippogryph families. Likewise hippogryphs care for the children of the night elves.

## Hippogryphs in Mythology
The Hippogryph is traditionally a magical mix of a [horse](horse) and a [griffon](Gryphon) (which in turn is part eagle). See [Hippogryph](wikipedia:Hippogryph) on [Wikipedia](Wikipedia:Main Page), [The Gryphon in Legend](http://www.gryphonpages.com/legend/legend.htm).

*Note: Within Warcraft, the spelling is "hippogryph", but outside the World of Warcraft, the spelling "hippogriff" is also valid, as "gryphon" is also spelled "griffin" or [griffon](griffon).*
