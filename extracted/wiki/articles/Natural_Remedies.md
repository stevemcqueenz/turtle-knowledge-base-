## Objectives

- [Lingering Fel Energy](Lingering Fel Energy)

## Description
If I am to truly captivate the magic of the region I will require a series of natural materials to amplify my abilities. Our real work is yet to come, but we must prepare nonetheless.

I require some trace of fel energy from the demons of the burning legion, I do know they have a presence in Desolace, seek them out there.

Lastly, I will require the scale of a basilisk, Crystal Spine basilisks to be more specific. This rare breed can be found along the Savage Coast in Stranglethorn Vale, seek them out, and gather me one of their scales.
