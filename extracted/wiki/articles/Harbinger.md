# Harbinger

The ***Harbinger*** was one of three [Blackwater Raiders](Blackwater Raiders) [frigate](frigate)s in search of a lost elven treasure in the reefs of the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). When one of [Professor Phizzlethorpe](Professor Phizzlethorpe)'s contraptions indicated the lost treasure was near [Faldir's Cove](Faldir's Cove), they docked and signaled for the *[Spirit of Silverpine](Spirit of Silverpine)* and the *[Maiden's Folly](Maiden's Folly)* to follow suit. Suddenly, the sea rumbled, the tides lowered, and the two ships crashed into the [Drowned Reef](Drowned Reef). The *Harbinger* and her crew remained stranded in the cove, scared to leave and fearing something lied beneath the waves waiting for them.
