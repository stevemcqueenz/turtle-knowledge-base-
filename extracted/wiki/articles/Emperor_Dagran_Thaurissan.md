# Emperor Dagran Thaurissan

**Emperor Dagran Thaurissan** is the current ruler of the [Dark Iron Clan](Dark Iron Clan), a descendant of Emperor [Thaurissan](Thaurissan) and [Modgud](Modgud). Even as the leader of his clan, he is like all Dark Irons a slave of [Ragnaros](Ragnaros).

He was married to King [Magni Bronzebeard](Magni Bronzebeard)'s daughter, Princess [Moira Bronzebeard](Moira Bronzebeard), and she is pregnant with his child, one who stand to inherit the throne of [Ironforge](Ironforge).  While it was believed that the Princess' affection for Tharissan was the result of a spell, she remains steadfast in her loyalty even following Thaurissan's death.
