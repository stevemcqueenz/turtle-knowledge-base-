-Kargath Bladefist** was the chieftain of the [Shattered Hand clan](Shattered Hand clan). A razor-sharp scythe setting in place of his severed left hand, Kargath was always willing to engage in frenzied combat. His ruthless tactics and single-mindedness have earned him the Chieftainship of the feared Shattered Hand clan. Like [Hellscream](Grommash Hellscream), Kargath waited only for the time his clan cold be unleashed upon the unsuspecting humans to engage in massive bloodshed.

## History
The Shattered Hand had stayed in Draenor when the orcs first crossed the Dark Portal and were spared the defeat that ended the Second War. Under Kargath, the Shattered Hand and the [Thunderlord clan](Thunderlord clan) lent their forces to [Dentarg](Dentarg) to reclaim the [Skull of Gul'dan](Skull of Gul'dan) from a captain of the [Bonechewer clan](Bonechewer clan). (ref: [The Skull of Gul'dan (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Skull_of_Gul%27dan_(WC2_Orc))) Kargath later led a force of Shattered Hand orcs into Azeroth. (ref: [Beyond the Dark Portal (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Beyond_the_Dark_Portal_(WC2_Human))) Kargath's Shattered Hand army defended the Azerothian side of the Dark Portal during the last days of the Invasion of Draenor, and they were left stranded with their chieftain after Ner'zhul abandoned them and the Portal was destroyed by the Alliance. (ref: [The Dark Portal (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dark_Portal_(WC2_Orc)))

The Shattered Hand's status during the interim period between the destruction of the Dark Portal and the fall of the internment camps is unknown; of Kargath's contemporaries, [Kilrogg](Kilrogg Deadeye) of the [Bleeding Hollow](Bleeding Hollow clan) was captured along with his clan, while [Grom Hellscream](Grom Hellscream) of the [Warsong clan](Warsong clan) continued to evade the Alliance. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* Game Manual, *[Orcish History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Day_of_the_Dragon)*, Day of the Dragon) Supported by many of the veteran chieftains, [Thrall](Thrall) ultimately succeeded in revitalizing the Horde and giving his people a new spiritual identity. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* Game Manual, *[Orcish History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Thrall's_Tale)*, Thrall's Tale)

The Shattered Hand still exists, making their home in the [Cleft of Shadow](Cleft of Shadow), located in the orcish capital of [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar).

## Trivia
- The outpost of [Kargath](Kargath) in the [Badlands](Badlands) was named in his honor. In addition, the shore south of Orgrimmar has been dubbed [Bladefist Bay](Bladefist Bay), and the headquarters of the [Warsong Logging Camp](Warsong Logging Camp) in [Ashenvale](Ashenvale) is known as [Kargathia Keep](Kargathia Keep).
