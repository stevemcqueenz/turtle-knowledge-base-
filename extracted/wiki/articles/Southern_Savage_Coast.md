# Southern Savage Coast

The **Southern Savage Coast** is a portion of the [Great Sea](Great Sea) around the southern tip of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), mostly noted by travelers on the southwest coast of [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale) near [Booty Bay](Booty Bay). The border between the [Savage Coast](Savage Coast) and its southern namesake lies due south of [Grom'gol Base Camp](Grom'gol Base Camp) across the bay.
