# Bashal'Aran

To the northeast of [Auberdine](Auberdine) in [Darkshore](Darkshore) you will find the ruins of **Bashal'Aran** .
The ruins are overrun with [demon](demon)ic minions - [sprite](sprite)s and [satyr](satyr)s which have taken up residence in the area. These evil creatures feed upon the magical energies left among the ruins, their powers growing from the continued exposure.
