# Human (playable)

*This article is about the playable race. For human lore, see [Human](Human). For the in-game faction, see [Stormwind (faction)](Stormwind (faction)).*

The **humans** of [Stormwind](Stormwind (faction)) are one of the playable [Alliance](Alliance) races in *World of Warcraft*. They are a resilient breed, having survived an invasion by the savage orcs during the First War. During the Second War, the armies of Stormwind rallied with the rest of the human kingdoms in the Alliance of Lordaeron to reclaim their homeland of Stormwind. After the success of the Second War, the Kingdom of Lordaeron was decimated by the Burning Legion and its population forced to flee to Stormwind, which would consequently become the center of human power on Azeroth.

Humans begin their adventures in the [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey) of [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), which is a part of their kingdom of Stormwind. The capital of said kingdom is the [city of the same name](Stormwind City), and this is also considered the main base of the Alliance itself. Humans are known to ride horses, and their main language is Common. As the leaders of the Alliance, their allies have learned this language from them as well.
