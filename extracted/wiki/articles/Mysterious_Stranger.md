The **Mysterious Stranger** was a [blood elf](blood elf) [quest giver](quest giver) who was located in every starting zone. He was the original starting point for the [hardcore](hardcore) challenge, which can only be begun at level 1. Hardcore characters who reach level 55 may undertake the process for [eternal mortality](Inferno Mode) with him through the [Mysterious Mailbox](Mysterious Mailbox), in [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass).

An **Echo of the Mysterious Stranger** resides in [Medivh's Chambers](Medivh's Chambers) within the [Tower of Karazhan](Tower of Karazhan). Unlike the other echoes that wander the halls, he is not oblivious to the presence of adventurers.
