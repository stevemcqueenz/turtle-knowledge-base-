**Stonard** is the lone [Horde](Horde) outpost located in the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows). It was originally established by the Horde during the [First War](First War), along with a nearby base known as [Rockard](Rockard), as a preliminary base for the invasion of the Kingdom of [Stormwind](Stormwind). It was abandoned in the aftermath of the [Second War](Second War), but has since been re-established as an outpost with the formation of the new Horde and the [orc](orc)s' renewed interest in the area. Regular patrols are sent out, and a number of small orcish encampments have been established nearby to further strengthen the Horde's control over the region. The swamp is still largely an uncharted territory to the orcs, who send [wayfinders](Stonard Wayfinder) and [cartographers](Stonard Cartographer) out to map the region.

It is one of the few orcish communities to still have a [great hall](great hall) in its midst and is protected by two [burrow](burrow)s. Several trainers and vendors can be found at the post. [Mage](Mage)s with sufficient skill can learn to [teleport](teleport) or open a [portal](Portal (spell)) to here.

Stonard is notable for being the location of the Horde master [alchemist](Alchemy) and the only Horde [shaman](shaman) trainer in the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms).
