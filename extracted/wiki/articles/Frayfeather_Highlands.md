# Frayfeather Highlands

The **Frayfeather Highlands** 54 66 Feralas (g/s/c) are the territory of the Frayfeather [hippogryph](hippogryph)s.  Deep into the Frayfeather Highlands, one can find nests containing [Hippogryph Egg](Hippogryph Egg)s to bring back to [Gadgetzan](Gadgetzan)'s Super Egg-O-Matic machine for inspection.
