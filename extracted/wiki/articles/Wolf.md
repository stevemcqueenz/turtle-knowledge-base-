# Wolf

A carnivorous, furry, [canine](canine) [beast](beast) that often runs in packs. Wolves are known to be unable to [swim](Swim) (at least in [World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft)).  Wolves encountered in the wilderness are typically [hostile](hostile) and [attack](aggro) characters that approach them too [closely](Aggro_Radius).

Wolves are indigenous to many parts of Azeroth. The live in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands), [Mulgore](Mulgore), [Duskwood](Duskwood), [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest), the [Badlands](Badlands), [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), [Felwood](Felwood), [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes), [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), [Feralas](Feralas), the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands), [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire), and [Shadowfang Keep](Shadowfang Keep).  They have a wide variety of sub-species and social habits. Though
most wolves are [hostile](hostile) towards humans and demi-humans who encroach upon their territories, a few unique dire wolf packs have befriended the Horde in recent years. There is a larger variety of wolf called [Worg](Worg)s; these are used as the [orc](orc) [mount](mount). [Orc](Orc) [shamans](shamans) seem to hold them in high regard evidenced by the fact that their armor often has a wolf on them.

### Game Value
- At least some wolves start with the [Bite](Bite) special ability when tamed as [hunter](hunter) [pet](pet)s.
- Wolves are [skinnable](skinnable).

### Examples
- Snow Tracker Wolf ([Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh))
- Plains Tracker ([Mulgore](Mulgore))
- [Coyote](Coyote) ([Westfall](Westfall))

## [Ghost Wolf](Ghost Wolf)
[Shaman](Shaman) of moderate ability can change their form into a [Ghost Wolf](Ghost Wolf). This allows them to travel much faster than an average humanoid, but this ritual only works outdoors.

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Has medium damage, medium armor, and medium HP.
- Eats only Meat.
- Can learn [Growl](Growl), [Cower](Cower), [Dash](Dash), [Bite](Bite), and their own skill, [Furious Howl](Furious Howl).
- The Mist Howler, Prairie Wolf Alpha, Deathmaw, Slavering Worg, Bloodaxe Worg, and Mist Howler are relatively popular for their faster swing speeds.  
- A popular pet, due to a variety of colors, some wolves with fast swing speed, Furious Howl, and Dash.
- Worgs, when tamed as a hunter pet, are in the same category as wolves and have the same skills.

## Epic Mount
The epic wolf is buyable in [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar) in the Valley of Honor just outside the Hunter Trainer's building.  The PvP mount is the Black War Wolf.  A famous variety of the epic wolf is the [Frostwolf](Frostwolf) - a huge, rideable wolf availible to members of the [Horde](Horde) exalted with the [Frostwolf Clan](Frostwolf Clan).
