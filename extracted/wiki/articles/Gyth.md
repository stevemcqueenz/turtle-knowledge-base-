# Gyth

A large Chromatic dragon who serves as [Rend Blackhand](Rend Blackhand)'s personal mount.
[Rend Blackhand](Rend Blackhand) and [Nefarian](Nefarian) tested their newly created [Chromatic Dragonflight](Chromatic Dragonflight) against a group of heroes entering [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire), sending wave after wave of the creatures through a gated area. As they were beginning to get worn down [Vaelan](Vaelan) appeared before them, casting a massive healing spell on our entire group before teleporting off again. As still more Dragonkin attacked, Vaelan appeared once again, encouraging them to continue the fight and healing their group with his powerful magic.

Finally, Rend himself joined the fray, astride a huge chromatic drake called Gyth. Together, the two were fearsome, the drake utilizing every type of dragon breath against them while it’s rider swung his massive blade at whoever was near.

Vaelan came to our aid again, but this time in their true form; as [Vaelastraz](Vaelastraz) the Red. The red dragon dwarfed Gyth and Rend, and set upon them with incredible might and fury, their fiery breath forcing Rend to dismount and fight the heroes on foot. Nefarian, furious, threatened the red dragon and finally cast a spell to teleport them out of his lair. The heroes realized later that even the form of Vaelastranz they had seen here was not his real body, but rather just a projection.

With Vaelastranz gone, the heroes were alone against Rend and Gyth, but they were separated now. Gyth was confused without its master to guide it, and the group finished the beast off first, and then turned their attention to Rend. Even with so many of them, Rend was a formidable foe, using all the abilities of a seasoned blademaster to cut away at our ranks. Nevertheless, they eventually cut the warrior down, realizing too late that Nefarian had escaped in the chaos.
