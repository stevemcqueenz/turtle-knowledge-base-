# Stag

Stags are [beasts](beast) very similar to [deer](deer). They are larger animals, taller than humans, and they have antlers. Most **stags** are [neutral](Neutral) when approached. Stags are skinnable. They cannot be tamed by [hunters](hunters) as [pets](pet).

### Examples
- Shadowhorn Stag (Level 20-23, [Ashenvale](Ashenvale))
- Elder Shadowhorn Stag (Level 25-27, [Ashenvale](Ashenvale))
- Antlered Courser (Level 21-23, [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains))
- Mosshoof Stag (Level 49-50, [Azshara](Azshara))
