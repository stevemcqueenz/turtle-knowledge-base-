# Petitioner's Chamber

The **Petitioner's Chamber** (aka **Hall of Petitioners**) is the first room to the right when ascending the ramp into [Stormwind Keep](Stormwind Keep). This is where the public waits when they have sought an audience. Amongst the public attendants in the room include [Caledra Dawnbreeze](Caledra Dawnbreeze), [Count Remington Ridgewell](Count Remington Ridgewell), [Duke Ramon III](Duke Ramon III), [Lord Ivar Pyrewood](Lord Ivar Pyrewood) and [Emissary Godwin Valorcall](Emissary Godwin Valorcall).
