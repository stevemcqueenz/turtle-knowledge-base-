# Hall of Arms

-Were you looking for the [Hall of Arms](Hall of Arms (Northshire)) wing of [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey), or the [Hall of Arms](Hall of Arms (Tyr's Hand)) wing of [Tyr's Hand Abbey](Tyr's Hand Abbey)?*

## General
The **Hall of Arms** is the center of dwarven military power, and houses trainers for warriors and hunters as well as several quest givers. It is located in the [Military Ward](Military Ward) in the [dwarf](dwarf) city of [Ironforge](Ironforge).
