# Murloc

*Aaaaaughibbrgubugbugrguburgle!* ([MP3 sound](http://www.owlboy.com/wowwiki/mMurlocAggroB.mp3), [Shockwave Flash sound](http://forums.worldofwarcraft.com/images-new/murloc_02.swf))

The **murloc** is a bipedal amphibianoid race residing along coastlines, lakeshores and riverbeds, as well as in underwater ruins. Murlocs are distinguished by their bulbous bodies, large mouths lined with row upon row of sharp fangs, and slime-coated skin. Murlocs range in coloration from turquoise to darkish grey, and in height from 3-1/2 feet to 6 feet.

Murloc culture is tribal in nature, and religion is generally shamanistic, with most villages containing a number of oracles and tidehunters, with coastrunners and warriors for defense. Habitations are generally crude huts with peaked roofs, huddled around a body of water. Oddly enough for amphibianoids, they prefer their food cooked, and will happily build campfires or even large bonfires which easily accommodate their favourite dish; the "spit-roasted gnome warlock".

Murloc behaviors toward other races is best characterized as "hostile," and even more so as "unbelievably hostile." Typically, approaching within 100 yards of even one, apparently isolated, murloc will result in the subject letting forth the usual gurgling warcry and dragging forth his entire village of 20, emerging as if by magic, to battle the visitor. Such assaults generally result in a nasty, slimy overkill against the hapless tourist. Indeed, it is nearly impossible for even the most dedicated murloc hunter to get just one to himself -- three or four Murlocs are usually the bare minimum.

Murloc hunting, however, is a time-honored avocation, particularly since murloc fins are a prized delicacy when used for soup broth, and [slimy murloc scale](Slimy Murloc Scale)s are sought after by expert leatherworkers for use in crafting fine armor. In addition, hefty bounties are often placed on certain murlocs, due to their incredibly-pronounced disregard for the sanctity of life, generally-accepted notions of fair play, or simple common decency. Pounding one of them into guava jelly with a large mace leaves you with a feeling of crushing guilt, as you have just killed a helpless creature for your own good.

## History
Murloc history first and foremost is mostly uninteresting and unimportant since most of their own oral tradition is the repetition of unpronouncable forefathers. This renders it completely useless to most people as the murloc language has yet to be deciphered. What is known of murlocs comes from the accounts of the learned people of the world observing these curious creatures.

Though known to the [Night Elves](Night Elves) on [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) for some time, the murlocs are relatively new to the eastern continents of Lordaeron and [Azeroth](Azeroth). It is purported that murlocs are actually a very ancient race of the world, at least as old as the first [Naga](Naga) since the serpentine creatures enslaved a group of deep sea-dwelling murlocs known as the [Mur'gul](Mur'gul). These creatures have steadily been moving inland from their oceanic dwelling places, even adapting to fresh-water lakes and rivers in order to populate areas they would not normally be found in. Though they continue to inhabit more areas of Lordaeron and Azeroth, their supposed intelligence is debatable due to the fact that their gutteral language is impossibly difficult to decipher. However, their use of weaponry and uncanny fighting abilities imply a rather sinister racial intellect.

## Known murloc territories/villages
In these evil times, the murlocs have spread to many various regions of the World of Azeroth. On the western continent of Kalimdor, the murlocs are found with increasing frequency. In dusky [Darkshore](Darkshore) the [Greymist Tribe](Greymist Tribe) makes their homes along the shore and among the wrecks in the shallow seas. The [Saltspittle Tribe](Saltspittle Tribe) lives in and around [Lake Falathim](Lake Falathim) in western [Ashenvale](Ashenvale). For now this seems to be the extent of their territories in Kalimdor.

In beplagued Lordaeron, many murloc tribes have mangaged to avoid the ravages of the undead plague. In the dying [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), the [Vile Fin](Vile Fin) murlocs remain entrenched along the north coast, bravely facing the [Rot Hide](Rot Hide) [gnoll](gnoll)s and persistent [Forsaken](Forsaken). To the south in [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest), the [Vile Fin](Vile Fin)s build huts and villages along the shore and upon the islands of [Lordamere Lake](Lordamere Lake). Southeast of Silverpine are the [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills) which is home to the [Torn Fin Tribe](Torn Fin Tribe). These lonely murlocs eke out an existence on the southern shores of the region.

The [Wetlands](Wetlands) is the only region in [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) that is hospitable to the murlocs' aquatic nature. The vast fens and sticky marshes are home to the [Bluegill Tribe](Bluegill Tribe) who are constantly hunted by the adventurers issuing out of [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor).

Further to the south in Azeroth proper, the murlocs are found in their greatest numbers. The tranquil [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest) is disturbed by an unnamed tribe of murlocs stalking [Crystal Lake](Crystal Lake) and [Stone Cairn Lake](Stone Cairn Lake). To the east in the rugged beauty of the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains), these same murlocs plague the waters of [Lake Everstill](Lake Everstill). In golden [Westfall](Westfall), the murlocs terrorize the [Longshore](Longshore). In [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), an especially vicious breed of murlocs known as the [Saltscale Tribe](Saltscale Tribe) inhabit the [Vile Reef](Vile Reef). Lastly, in the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows), the Marsh murloc tribes live to the south and east of the ancient temple that lies in the fens.

## Famous Murlocs
- [Burgle Eye](Burgle Eye): Level 38 rare. He can be found wandering the coastline Murloc encampments of [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh).
- [Brack](Brack): Level 19 rare. He can be found wandering the beaches of [Westfall](Westfall) to the south.
- [Slark](Slark): Level 15 rare. He can be found wandering the beaches of [Westfall](Westfall) to the north.
- [Gazban](Gazban): Level 38 to 40 elite. He is summoned in [The Vile Reef](The Vile Reef) of [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale) for the quest [The Curse of the Tides](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/quest.html?wquest=611).
- [Gluggle](Gluggle): Level 37 rare. Found in the ruins north of [Grom'gol](Grom'gol) in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale).
- [Gobbler](Gobbler): Level 22. Can be found in [Bluegill Marsh](Bluegill Marsh) in [Wetlands](Wetlands) and is involved in the quest [Claws from the Deep](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/quest.html?wquest=279).
- [Mutanus the Devourer](Mutanus the Devourer): Level 22 elite. Although marked as Humanoid, this thing surely looks like a giant Murloc. He is the end boss of [Wailing Caverns](Wailing Caverns) and can be fought after defeating the 4 fanglords.
- [Old Murk-Eye](Old Murk-Eye): Level 20. Can be found in Westfall. He is needed for the quest [The Coastal Menace](http://www.thottbot.com/?qu=104)
- [Scargil](Scargil): Level 30 rare. Found on the beach west of [Southshore](Southshore) in [Hillsbrad](Hillsbrad).
- [Swamp Talker](Swamp Talker): Level 50 to 56. Found in a cave east of [Stonard](Stonard) in the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows). He is involved in the quest [The Swamp Talker](http://wow.allakhazam.com/db/quest.html?wquest=2623).
- [Cookie](Cookie): Level 20 Elite. Found onboard Edwin VanCleef's ship in the Deadmines. Also he seems to be the ships cook.
