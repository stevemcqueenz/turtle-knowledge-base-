# Silver Coast

The **Silver Coast** is a stretch of coastline to the west of [Gillijim's Isle](Gillijim's Isle). It is the longest shore of the island, leading to the southern coast and the [Broken Reef](Broken Reef). Along its shores is [a high elven shipwreck](Faelon's Folly) that had accompanied the [Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet](Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet).
