The **Scarlet Monastery**, also known simply as the **Monastery**, is a fortified monastery located in the northeast corner of the blighted [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades). Once a cathedral to the Light, the Monastery is now one of four strongholds belonging to the fanatical [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) in the world, the others being the [Scarlet Bastion](Scarlet Bastion) within the ruins of [Stratholme](Stratholme), the town of [Hearthglen](Hearthglen), and the town of [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand).

The Scarlet Monastery is a complex of four instanced dungeons: the [Graveyard](Scarlet Monastery Graveyard), the [Library](Scarlet Monastery Library), the [Armory](Scarlet Monastery Armory) and the [Cathedral](Scarlet Monastery Cathedral).

## History
From the [World Dungeons](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Guide/World_Dungeons) page on the official World of Warcraft Community Site:

   *The Monastery was once a proud bastion of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom))'s priesthood - a center for learning and enlightenment. With the rise of the undead [Scourge](Scourge) during the [Third War](Third War), the peaceful Monastery was converted into a stronghold of the fanatical [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade). The Crusaders are intolerant of all non-human races, regardless of alliance or affiliation. They believe that any and all outsiders are potential carriers of the undead plague - and must be destroyed. Reports indicate that adventurers who enter the monastery are forced to contend with [Scarlet Commander Mograine](Scarlet Commander Mograine) - who commands a large garrison of fanatically devoted warriors. However, the monastery's true master is [High Inquisitor Whitemane](High Inquisitor Whitemane) - a fearsome priestess who possesses the ability to resurrect fallen warriors to do battle in her name.*

Before the Third War, the Monastery served as a seminary for paladins-in-training. (ref: [quest: Mythology of the Titans])

### World of Warcraft
**This section concerns content related to the original *World of Warcraft*.**

[Raleigh the Devout](Raleigh the Devout) believed in the cause of the Scarlet Crusade to rid Azeroth of the undead. As he spent time at the Monastery, he realized that the Crusade's grasp on reality was slipping because they thought everyone was plagued who didn't wear the tabard of the Crusade. Innocent men and women were tortured because they were supposedly plagued.

[Meven Korgal](Meven Korgal) was sent from the Monastery to spy on [Deathknell](Deathknell).
