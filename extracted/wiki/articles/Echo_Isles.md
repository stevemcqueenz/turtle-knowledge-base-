# Echo Isles

The **Echo Isles** [64, 85] are a handful of small islands off the southeastern coast of [Durotar](Durotar). For a while, the [Darkspear trolls](Darkspear trolls) lived here, but when the [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras) fleet attacked their land, they had to flee. They evacuated with the help of the [Mok'Nathal](Mok'Nathal) [Rexxar](Rexxar).

After the [Third War](Third War), when the [Horde](Horde) settled in Durotar, [Thrall](Thrall) made these islands off the southern tip available to the Darkspears. Recently, a Darkspear troll named [Zalazane](Zalazane) decided that his brethren did not need the [orcs](orcs) as allies and began practicing [necromancy](necromancy). He drove the Horde-loyal Darkspears off of the islands, converted followers, and created the [zombie](zombie)s who now inhabit the Echo Isles.

While attempts have been made to oust [Zalazane](Zalazane) and reclaim the islands, his forces are too numerous compared to the weakened [Darkspear tribe](Darkspear tribe). They now live on the coast of mainland Durotar in [Sen'jin Village](Sen'jin Village), in the [Valley of Spirits](Valley of Spirits) within [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar), and in other scattered parts of [Azeroth](Azeroth (world)).

Popular in World of Warcraft as a low-level training ground and as a taming ground for hunters wanting raptor or cat pets.
