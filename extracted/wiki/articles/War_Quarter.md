# War Quarter

The **War Quarter** is located in the northwest corner of the [Undercity](Undercity). This is where the [Forsaken](Forsaken) military is based, and is also home to the city's priest and warrior guilds, as well as a few profession trainers.

Facilities:
- [Priest](Priest) trainers
- [Warrior](Warrior) trainers
- Weapon skill trainer
- [Blacksmithing](Blacksmithing) trainers
- [Mining](Mining) trainers
