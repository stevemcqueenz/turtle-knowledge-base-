# Slithering Scar

The **Slithering Scar** is a [subzone](subzone) in the southern part of [Un'goro Crater](Un'goro Crater) and is home to one of the minor [silithid](silithid) [hives](hives) named **Gorishi**. It may be significant that there are no silithid hives near the Un'Goro [pylons](Power crystals) except in the south, where a southern pylon is mysteriously absent. The [Gorishi Silithid Crystal](Gorishi Silithid Crystal) can be found inside the hive.
