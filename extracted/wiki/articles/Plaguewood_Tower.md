# Plaguewood Tower

**Plaguewood Tower** is a ruined tower located in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), and an objective in the [Game of Towers](A Game of Towers) world PvP event. It is located deep within the [Plaguewood](Plaguewood), the putrid fungal forest outside the gates of [Stratholme](Stratholme). The faction controlling this tower gains access to a ghostly flight master, who will allow players to rapidly fly to any of the other three towers.

Plaguewood Tower is arguably the most useful of the towers in the Eastern Plaguelands, or at the very least the one with the most interesting benefit. The ability to rapidly reinforce any of the other towers is useful, as is the ability to return to [Eastwall Tower](Eastwall Tower), which is right next to the major quest hub [Light's Hope Chapel](Light's Hope Chapel). In addition, it's rather remote location means that once captured, Plaguewood is likely to stay captured.
