# Boulderfist clan

A [tribe](tribe) of [ogre](ogre)s that live throughout the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands) and currently control parts of [Stromgarde](Stromgarde).

Their main holds include [Boulder'gor](Boulder'gor) and [Boulderfist Hall](Boulderfist Hall). 

Notable Boulderfist Ogres:
-Kor'gresh Coldridge
-Or'Kalar
