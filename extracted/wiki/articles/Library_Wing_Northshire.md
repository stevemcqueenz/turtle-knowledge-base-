# Library Wing (Northshire)

*For the Library Wing of [Tyr's Hand Abbey](Tyr's Hand Abbey), see [Library Wing (Tyr's Hand)](Library Wing (Tyr's Hand)).*

The **Library Wing** of [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey), located in [Northshire Valley](Northshire Valley) in [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), is home to the [priest](priest) trainer [Priestess Anetta](Priestess Anetta) and the [mage](mage) trainer [Khelden Bremen](Khelden Bremen) for the least experienced of the [Stormwind Army](Stormwind Army)'s [recruits](Adventurer). It is situated south of the [Main Hall](Main Hall (Northshire)), and the [Hall of Arms](Hall of Arms (Northshire)) lies on the opposite side of the abbey.
