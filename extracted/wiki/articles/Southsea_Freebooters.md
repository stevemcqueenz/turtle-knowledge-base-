# Southsea Freebooters

The Southsea Freebooters are a pirate faction known as the Southsea Pirates, which have been sighted as far south as the coast of Tanaris and reportedly have a hidden base in the [Lost Rigger Cove](Lost Rigger Cove). They are made up of a combination of dark iron dwarves, goblins, humans, gnolls, and trolls.
