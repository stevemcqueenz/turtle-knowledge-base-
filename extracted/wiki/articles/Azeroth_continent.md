The **continent of Azeroth** (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Places of Mystery](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#The_Portal)*, The Portal) is one of the three continents (ref: *World of Warcraft: Game Manual*, pg. 104) (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* manual, pg. 3: "The Alliance forces under Sir Lothar managed to push Doomhammer's clans out of Lordaeron and back into the orc-controlled lands of Azeroth.") that form the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), (ref: *World of Warcraft* manual, pg. 104) lying to the south of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan). (ref: [Grim Batol (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Grim_Batol_(WC2_Human)))
