# Moonwell (Ashenvale)

There are three **Moonwells** in [Ashenvale](Ashenvale). [Shael'dryn](Shael'dryn)'s moonwell 53 46 Ashenvale (g/s/c) is located in the northern  part, east of [Iris Lake](Iris Lake) and west of [Raynewood Retreat](Raynewood Retreat). The southern moonwell 60 72 Ashenvale (g/s/c) is located west of [Silverwing Outpost](Silverwing Outpost) and southeast of [Greenpaw Village](Greenpaw Village). The former is oft visited by [Alliance](Alliance) adventurers who are following the  quest chain, and the latter is sometimes visited by [Horde](Horde) [shaman](shaman)s doing their  quest chain. The third moonwell is located at Raynewood Retreat 59 59 Ashenvale (g/s/c).
