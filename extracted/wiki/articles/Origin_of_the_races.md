# Origin of the races

# Troll/Elf Lineage
[Blizzard](Blizzard)'s history of [Azeroth](Azeroth) does not clearly state that [night elves](night elves) descended from [troll](troll)s, but some [evidence](Troll/Elf_Lineage) does suggest the possibility. Alternatively Night Elves may be descended from [Elune](Elune) herself as some legends state. Which would explain the physical similarities between Elune, Dryads, Night Elves, and Keepers of the Grove.

The [Satyr](Satyr) are the descendents of [Kaldorei](Kaldorei) who allied directly with the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) during the first invasion. These former elves were taken by the [demon](demon)s and twisted into shapes that more accurately reflected their nature.

[Harpies](Harpy) are descended from female elves who betrayed [Queen Azshara](Queen Azshara), Sorceress-Queen of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), and were transformed into hideous bird-creatures as punishment.
-Although there is some reference in the War of the Ancients books by Richard A Knakk, that imply that harpies descended from the demi-goddess [Aviana](Aviana).*

The [High Elves](High Elves) are a race descended from the [Highborne](Highborne) Night Elves who left Kalimdor and settled in the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), founding [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas). When Quel'Thalas and at least 90% of the High Elven race was destroyed by the [Scourge](Scourge), they split into two factions.

Many changed their name to "[Blood Elves](Blood Elves)" in remembrance of their fallen brethren, and would soon become close allies to the [Naga](Naga). Others remained as High Elves, disgusted by Blood Elven values. These stayed true to their old allies, the [Alliance](Alliance), and are scattered among various [cities](city) where they are a clear minority.

When the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity) imploded, sundering the world many Highborne were trapped in the blast, however as their bodies were so infused with magic they did not die but were twisted by the powers they had released, influenced through the limited though still quite powerful will of the [Old Gods](Old Gods), [Azshara](Azshara) and her followers were dragged down beneath the raging sea, cursed &ndash; transformed &ndash; they took on new shapes and became the hateful serpentine [Naga](Naga).

Not all Highborne who were trapped and infused by magic became Naga, others were turned into [Blood Maiden](Blood Maiden)s.

The [Mountain Giant](Mountain Giant)s and the [Sea Giant](Sea Giant)s were crafted by the Titans when the world was young to protect Azeroth.

When the [Titans](Titans) were shaping [Azeroth](Azeroth), they created the [Troggs](Troggs) to assist them. But the Troggs were evil, so the Titans sealed them off. Then the Titans created the [Earthen](Earthen) which continued to shape Azeroth after the Titans left. After the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity) erupted the Earthen sealed themselves off in their cities. Centuries later, the Earthen of [Uldaman](Uldaman) awoke to find that they had changed. Their skin was soft and pink. They decided to leave to the surface, eventually finding the mountain of [Ironforge](Ironforge) which they claimed as their new home. Within Ironforge they made a great altar to their creator Titan [Khaz'goroth](Khaz'goroth). There are three known cities where the Titans made the Earthen: [Uldum](Uldum) in [Tanaris](Tanaris), [Ulduar](Ulduar) in [Northrend](Northrend), and Uldaman in [the Badlands](the Badlands).

The dwarves split up into three factions: The [Wildhammer Clan](Wildhammer Clan), the [Bronzebeard Clan](Bronzebeard Clan) and the [Dark Iron Clan](Dark Iron Clan). The Bronzebeard Clan became the leaders of all the [Dwarven](Dwarven) people. Eventually the Dark Iron Clan became jealous of the Bronzebeard Clan and attempted a coup, which ended unsuccessfully.  They were banished from Ironforge. The Dark Iron Clan retreated to [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire) to plan their revenge. Lead by the [sorcerer-thane](sorcerer-thane) [Thaurissan](Thaurissan) and his wife [Modgud](Modgud), the Dark Iron Clan led a two-pronged attack against Ironforge, the Bronzebeard Clan, [Grim Batol](Grim Batol) and the Wildhammer Clan. The battle at Ironforge was unsuccessfull and the battle at Grim Batol had some margin of success but Modgud fell in the battle, so the Dark Iron Clan retreated to Blackrock Spire with Ironforge and Grim Batol following close after them. At Blackrock Spire Thaurissan attempted to summon a powerful fire elemental to smite the other [Dwarves](Dwarves) but instead freed [Ragnaros](Ragnaros) the Firelord. Upon the Firelord's rebirth the area around the mountain exploded in an apocalyptic fire, devastating the surrounding land and making the [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge) and the [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes). The Firelord took the Dark Iron Clan and made them his. The other Dwarves stopped before the explosion and fled, sealing the only entrance into the Searing Gorge from [Loch Modan](Loch Modan).

There is speculation that the [Flamewaker](Flamewaker) were once Dark Iron Dwarves transformed by the power of Ragnaros. There is no evidence, however, that supports this.

It is not known exactly how the [gnomes](gnomes) came into being, but it is said that they are cousins to the dwarves, but this may not have been literal. One theory of the gnomes heritage is that they were decended from the Titan's giants as the dwarves are decended from the Earthen. This theory is based on the similarity between the form of the Mountain Giants, Sea Giants, and gnomes. Though the giants are many times larger, they have a similarly stocky build to gnomes, with proportionally larger heads and only four fingers per hand.

There are also hints that humans may have decended from some dwarf faction. It could be possible that a faction of earthen left the mountains and grew taller in the open. This is implied in one of the War of the Ancients books where [Rhonin](Rhonin) is described as a tall dwarf.

# Cenarius' Lineage
[Richard A. Knaak](Richard A. Knaak)'s novel [The Sundering](The Sundering) stated that [Ysera](Ysera), the [Dragon Aspect](Dragon Aspect), was [Malorne](Malorne)'s lover and mother of [Cenarius](Cenarius). Later, the author Richard A. Knaak clarified that [Elune](Elune) is Cenarius' biological mother, and Ysera is his adopted mother.

Cenarius has many descendents, the [Keepers of the Grove](Keepers of the Grove), the [dryad](dryad)s, and the [centaur](centaur).

The origin of the centaur is told by the spirit of Keeper [Celebras](Celebras) at [Maraudon](Maraudon) in [Desolace](Desolace). [Remulos](Remulos), one of the sons of Cenarius, fathered Celebras and several dryads.  Jealous of his brother's offspring, [Zaetar](Zaetar) mated with an [Earth Elemental](Earth Elemental) named [Princess Theradras](Princess Theradras). From that unholy love were born the evil centaurs.

While some contend that Cenarius had only two sons, others argue that is not the case.  See: [Keepers of the Grove: Parentage](Keepers of the Grove#Parentage)''.

# The Evolution of the Aqir
The [Aqir](Aqir) were intelligent insectoids, created in the image of the [Silithid](Silithid) by the [Old God](Old God) [C'Thun](C'Thun), that ruled the lands of western Kalimdor during the time of the [Amani](Amani Empire) and [Gurubashi](Gurubashi Empire) Empires.  They were greatly expansionistic and incredibly evil.  They fought against the trolls for thousands of years but were eventually defeated and split into two city-states: [Azjol-Nerub](Azjol-Nerub) and [Ahn'Qiraj](Ahn'Qiraj).

The [Nerubian](Nerubian)s are the descendants of the Aqir that fled to Azjol-Nerub.  These spider-like humanoids reigned on the continent of [Northrend](Northrend) until the [War of the Spider](War of the Spider), when they were tragically defeated by the [Scourge](Scourge) and many were turned into undead [Crypt Fiends](Crypt Fiends).  However, the [Lich King](Lich King) was so impressed by their tenacity that he adopted their architecture for the Scourge, a silent tribute of sorts to the mighty warrior-race.  There are still a few Nerubians on Northrend, waging guerilla warfare on the Scourge, but it is unknown whether this resistance has any kind of centrallized command.

The [Qiraji](Qiraji) are the descendants that fled to Ahn'Qiraj.  Unlike the Nerubians, the Qiraji evolved in many different ways, resulting in a caste system in their society.  The Qiraji waged the [War of the Shifting Sands](War of the Shifting Sands) against the [Night Elves](Night Elves) for many years before the elves, with the aid of the [Bronze Dragonflight](Bronze Dragonflight), trapped the Qiraji behind the [Scarab Wall](Scarab Wall).  Now, 10,000 years later, the [Alliance](Alliance) and the [Horde](Horde) have reopened the [Gates of Ahn'Qiraj](Gates of Ahn'Qiraj) to make sure the Qiraji don't rise up again.

# Corrupted species
Strong demons - the heroes of their kind - can corrupt individuals, tribes, or even whole species. 

While not all the corrupted species were created in this way, the majority were created by demons or the use of demonic magics.

  Night Elves were turned into [Satyr](Satyr) by the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), and then by [Xavius](Xavius), first Satyr. They are transformed by will, unlike other corrupt races, by giving themselves over to the Legion for power.

  [Fel Orc](Fel Orc)s are created in much the same manner as Satyr, by a willful obediance to the Legion. They, however, are not transformed, only given a bloodrage unnatural to their race. This happens from the drinking of a [Pit Lord](Pit Lord)'s blood, or perhaps any demons blood. 

  The two-headed ogres were originally the elite guards of [Gul'dan](Gul'dan). He turned these ogres into ogre-magi using the High Elves runes at [Caer Darrow](Caer Darrow). Using them, along with his demonik magics, the two-headed, ogre magi were created. 

  The [Mur'gul](Mur'gul) are a tribe of [Murloc](Murloc) from deep in the [Maelstrom](Maelstrom). Unlike their cousins, however, they were taken over by the empire of [Nazjatar](Nazjatar), the Naga. Either corrupted from their slavery, or their proximity to an [Old God](Old God), they are now no longer the Murlocs they once were.

  Corrupted Treants and Ancients are caused by the corruption in the land, created by the Burning Legion in their invasion. This changed not only the land, but nearly all inhabitants in it, as well.
