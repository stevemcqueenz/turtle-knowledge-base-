The **Black Citadel** was originally said to be [Magtheridon](Magtheridon)'s fortress in *[Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne](Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne)*. Situated in [Hellfire Peninsula](Hellfire Peninsula).

The Black Citadel was a menacing structure in the [Hellfire Peninsula](Hellfire Peninsula) region of [Outland](Outland). Shortly after the destruction of [Draenor](Draenor) by [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul)'s many portals, a [pit lord](Annihilan) named [Magtheridon](Magtheridon) used the Black Citadel as a throne from which to rule over Outland. Magtheridon used the portals Ner'zhul created to summon members of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), bolstering his armies and hold on Outland.

The half-demon night elf [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage) and his lieutenants - [Lady Vashj](Lady Vashj) of the [Naga](Naga), [Kael'thas Sunstrider](Kael'thas Sunstrider) of the [Blood Elves](Blood Elves) and [Akama](Akama) of the Draenei - led their followers through Outland and laid siege to the Black Citadel following a seemingly successful effort to hide Illidan from his master, [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden), by destroying all access points to Outland from the [Twisting Nether](Twisting Nether). Thinking himself finally safe from Kil'jaeden's wrath, Illidan shifted his goal from simple self-preservation to conquest. He set out to take control of Outland from the cruel Magtheridon and rule over Outland in his place. (ref: [Gates of the Abyss (WC3 BloodElf)](Gates of the Abyss (WC3 BloodElf)))

The Citadel, however, was heavily guarded. Magtheridon controlled a variety of [demon](demon)s, including the corrupted [fel orc](fel orc)s that relentlessly attacked Illidan's forces. [Infernal machine](Infernal machine)s sat silently along the walls to hurl devastating attacks on intruders. A huge abomination named the [Butcher](Butcher (Warcraft III)) lingered in the dark recesses, waiting for prey. Six fel orc [blademaster](blademaster)s were set up to guard: [Bonethirst](Bonethirst), [Maim](Maim (Warcraft III)), [Bloodgrin](Bloodgrin), [Nera'thor](Nera'thor), [Sagra'nel](Sagra'nel) and [Rend](Rend (Warcraft III)). Guarding the final areas were the [Master of Pain](Master of Pain) and [Mistress of Torment](Mistress of Torment) along with an army of Burning Legion minions. In order to get through these defenses, Illidan enlisted Lady Vashj to attack the Citadel by water and Akama to infiltrate Magtheridon's inner chambers through stealth.

Illidan and his company succeeded in defeating Magtheridon and named themselves the new rulers of Outland. The victory was short-lived as Kil'jaeden appeared to remind Illidan that he could not escape the far-reaching gaze of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), not even in the Black Citadel. (ref: [Lord of Outland (WC3 BloodElf)](Lord of Outland (WC3 BloodElf)))

## Trivia

- Black Citadel is also a melee [tileset](tileset) in *The Frozen Throne*.
