# Sardor Isle

-Sardor Isle** 32 53 Feralas (g/s/c) is located west of mainland [Feralas](Feralas). On the island's northern shore stands [Feathermoon Stronghold](Feathermoon Stronghold), led by the [night elf](night elf) general [Shandris Feathermoon](Shandris Feathermoon), head of the [Sentinel Army](Sentinel Army). The night elves keep a watchful eye on the [Isle of Dread](Isle of Dread), located to the south of Sardor Isle, where things most unspeakable now walk the earth.
