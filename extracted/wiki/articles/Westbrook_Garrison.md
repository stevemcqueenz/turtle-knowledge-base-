# Westbrook Garrison

-Westbrook Garrison** is a barracks of the [Stormwind Guard](Stormwind Guard) located in [Forest's Edge](Forest's Edge) on the western end of [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), near the border with [Westfall](Westfall), which is controlled by agents of the [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood) and their dreaded mechanical [harvest golem](harvest golem)s.

Because of the corruption and political instability caused in the kingdom by [Lady Katrana Prestor](Lady Katrana Prestor), the Westbrook Garrison lives through difficult times where they have to deal with an increasing [gnoll](gnoll) and thief activity coming from Westfall. With the bulk of the Stormwind Army abroad, they barely have enough men to keep the roads of the region safe. (ref: [Deputy Rainer#Quotes](Deputy Rainer#Quotes)) At some point, [Deputy Rainer](Deputy Rainer) requested more Stormwind soldiers from [Marshal Dughan](Marshal Dughan) in [Goldshire](Goldshire), who instead sends [Alliance](Alliance) [adventurer](adventurer)s as reinforcements. With their help, the Garrison fought the [Riverpaw pack](Riverpaw pack) who infested the woods south of the guard tower, as they couldn't handle them alone. (ref:  [10] [Riverpaw Gnoll Bounty](Riverpaw Gnoll Bounty))

Several wanted posters at the garrison gave the mission to kill [Hogger](Hogger), the chieftain of the Riverpaw gnolls who has overpowered all attempts at his capture. (ref:  [11+] [WANTED: "Hogger"](WANTED: "Hogger"))
