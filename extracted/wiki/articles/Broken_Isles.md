# Broken Isles

The **Broken Isles** are a mysterious chain of islands, raised up from the seafloor by [Gul'dan](Gul'dan), which are located south of the raging [Maelstrom](Maelstrom). They are located in the [South Seas](South Seas) and include about four large islands and about three small islands.Many ancient ruins can be found on these islands including the once-mighty [night elf](night elf) city of [Suramar](Suramar). Amazingly, the long-lost [Tomb of Sargeras](Tomb of Sargeras) is also located on one of these islands. However, [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage) recently [stole](The Broken Isles) the [Eye of Sargeras](Eye of Sargeras) from the tomb. The water between the islands is known as [Stormreaver Bay](Stormreaver Bay) as this was the location that Gul’dan and his clan anchored their ships when they were exploring the isles. [Balor](Balor) may be a part of the Broken Isles or located near the island chain.

It is stated that 2,000 inhabitants are known to live in the Broken Isles, almost all of them unsavory creatures of various types. They live without a government nor a capital and are under the rule of Tide Priest Harash. There are no major settlements; a few huts and encampments are all that can be found in the way of civilization. They are affiliated with the [naga](naga).

## History
In the distant past [Magna Aegwynn](Aegwynn), Guardian of Tirisfal, battled the [avatar of Sargeras](Sargeras). Knowing that the [dark titan](dark titan)'s corpse still held power, she sought a safe place to entomb it. Aegwynn placed his body in a tomb and cast it deep into the ocean next to the [Maelstrom](Maelstrom) in a [Kaldorei](Kaldorei) ruin she had discovered deep beneath the sea, believing it to be secure.

Only a few remains of a large island in the [Great Sea](Great Sea) were left. These isles were of little strategic value, the largest being little more than a mile in length. The [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) showed an unusual interest in exploring the isles. The forces of the Stormreaver clan and the [Twilight's Hammer clan](Twilight's Hammer clan) moved through the isles en masse towards the end of the [Second War](Second War). It was later discovered that the clans were fortifying a new island upon which [Gul'dan](Gul'dan) had newly raised in order for him to access the [Tomb of Sargeras](Tomb of Sargeras). The newer, larger islands are the Broken Isles of today.

During the [Third War](Third War), [Maiev Shadowsong](Maiev Shadowsong) chased escaped prisoner [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage) here, while the latter searched for the [Eye of Sargeras](Eye of Sargeras). She discovered the remains of [Suramar](Suramar), the ancient [night elf](night elf) capital here, as well as a never-ending battle between the undead remnants of the [Twilight's Hammer](Twilight's Hammer), [Blackrock](Blackrock), and [Stormreaver](Stormreaver) clans. Maiev pursued Illidan into the [Tomb of Sargeras](Tomb of Sargeras), but Illidan found the Eye first and used it to destroy the tomb as well as most of Maiev's forces.
