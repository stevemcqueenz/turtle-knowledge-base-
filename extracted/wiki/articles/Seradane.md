# Seradane

[Seradane](Seradane) is an area in northeastern part of [The Hinterlands](The Hinterlands) which houses one of the four entrances to the [Emerald Dream](Emerald Dream). Many high-level [elite](elite) green [dragon](dragon)s make their home here. One of the [Dragons of Nightmare](Dragons of Nightmare) ([Emeriss](Emeriss), [Lethon](Lethon), [Taerar](Taerar), [Ysondre](Ysondre)) may also appear here.

The skull of an unknown dragon is also to be found lying on top of a hill slightly to the north of the portal.
