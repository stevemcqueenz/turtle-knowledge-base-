# Southwind Village

**Southwind Village** was once a [night elven](night elven) base of operation in [Silithus](Silithus), but was destroyed by the [qiraji](qiraji) during the [War of the Shifting Sands](War of the Shifting Sands). [Valstann Staghelm](Valstann Staghelm), son of [Fandral Staghelm](Fandral Staghelm), led a detatchment of the night elven army to defend the [village](village), but was captured by [General Rajaxx](General Rajaxx) after his troops were slaughtered by the swarming qiraji. Today, Southwind Village has been long since overrun by relentless [silithid](silithid) that infest large parts of that area. The horror of the village's initial sundering still echos through time — the tortured spirits of fallen [druid](Tortured Druid)s and [Sentinel](Tortured Sentinel)s wander the ruins aimlessly, and are led by [Shade of Ambermoon](Shade of Ambermoon).
