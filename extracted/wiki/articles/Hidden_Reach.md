# Hidden Reach

**The Hidden Reach** is the name given to a secret entrance to [Dire Maul](Dire Maul) East which bypasses the usual hazards of getting into the keep from its usual entrance. It is accessed by the [Lariss Pavilion](Lariss Pavilion) located in the forest area north of [Camp Mojache](Camp Mojache). Along the western rim of the pavilion is a downward spiral ramp whose northern area has the door to The Hidden Reach. This door can only be accessed by use of the . Once open, there is a winding tunnel that goes down to an instance portal. Once crossed, the adventurer(s) will be in a corridor that leads to the easternmost portion of [Dire Maul East](Dire Maul East). The area is guarded by [lasher](lasher)s and [satyr](satyr)s.

If one goes through the standard instance portal in the [Broken Commons](Broken Commons) in order to kill [Pusillin](Pusillin) and obtain the Crescent Key, one has to pass through [Warpwood Quarter](Warpwood Quarter) to get to the Hidden Reach. A tunnel leads off near the entrance through a large unnamed area containing [Lethtendris](Lethtendris), [Hydrospawn](Hydrospawn), and [Zevrim Thornhoof](Zevrim Thornhoof) and eventually down into the [Conservatory](Conservatory).
