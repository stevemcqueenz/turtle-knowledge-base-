# Crafting reagent

Some [item](Item) required by a [recipe](Recipe). Sometimes interchangeably used with the term [reagent](Reagent), but **ingredients** are more general, whereas reagents tend to refer to ingredients needed for [spells](Spell).

For a larger list of specific ingredients, see [:Category:Ingredients](:Category:Ingredients).
