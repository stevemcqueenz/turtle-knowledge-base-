*This article is about the city-state and human nation. For the in-game faction, see [Theramore (faction)](Theramore (faction)).*

**Theramore Isle** (also known as **Theramore City** (ref: [A Blaze of Glory (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_Blaze_of_Glory_(WC3_Orc))) or simply **Theramore**) is a [human](human) city (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 8 - *"I rule Theramore, the human city on this continent."*) located in a rocky island east of [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh), as well as the capital of the eponymous city-state.

The importance of Theramore is emphasized by the fact that many class and profession trainers reside here. A mage with sufficient skill can even use teleport and open portals into this city.

## History
### Old Hatreds
Following the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion)'s [defeat](Battle of Mount Hyjal) at [Mount Hyjal](Mount Hyjal), the remaining Alliance forces under [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore) settled in southern [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). Off the eastern coast of [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh), they built the rugged port city of Theramore. There, the humans and their dwarven allies worked to survive in a land that would always be hostile to them. Though the defenders of [Durotar](Durotar) and Theramore kept the tentative truce with one another, the fragile colonial serenity was not meant to last.

The peace between the orcs and humans was shattered by the arrival of a massive Alliance fleet in Kalimdor. The mighty fleet, under the command of Grand Admiral [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore), Jaina's father, had left Lordaeron before [Arthas](Arthas) had destroyed the kingdom. Having sailed for many grueling months, Admiral Proudmoore was searching for any Alliance survivors he could find.

Proudmoore's armada posed a serious threat to the stability of the region. As a renowned hero of the Second War, Jaina's father was a staunch enemy of the Horde, and he was determined to destroy Durotar before the orcs could gain a foothold in the land.

The Grand Admiral forced Jaina to make a terrible decision: support him in battle against the orcs and betray her newfound allies, or fight her own father to maintain the fragile peace that the Alliance and the Horde had finally attained. After much soul-searching, Jaina chose the latter and helped Thrall defeat her crazed father. (ref: [Old Hatreds - The Colonization of Kalimdor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Old_Hatreds_-_The_Colonization_of_Kalimdor))

### Cycle of Hatred
In time, Jaina had built and taken the role of ruler in Theramore. Some citizens came to the city to make a new life for themselves, with crime being of little rate. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 1)

The [demon](demon) named [Zmodlor](Zmodlor) recruited [Margoz](Margoz (human)) into the [Burning Blade](Burning Blade clan) cult, whose goal was to start a full-scale war between orcs and humans again. Margoz's job was to sow anti-orc sentiments in Theramore. His attempts to do so in the [Demonsbane](Demonsbane Inn and Tavern) tavern were very successful.

Jaina's nation and the Horde were at peace for three years. While all was peaceful, some citizens were not satisfied. They complained about the orc lands having better wood to build ships with which they wouldn't trade for. Others even complained about them having better fishing spots and special treatment from the local neutral port: [Ratchet](Ratchet).

After Jaina left for [Durotar](Durotar) to help Thrall with the [thunder lizard](thunder lizard)s, her chamberlain [Kristoff](Kristoff (human)) was left in charge. Under the influence of Zmodlor, he mobilized an army to attack and annihilate the [Horde](Horde) for good. Before another war could start, Lady Jaina Proudmoore, [Aegwynn](Aegwynn), and [Lorena](Lorena) returned to Theramore to confront Kristoff. Jaina exorcised Zmodlor's influence with a bolt of lightning, which killed Kristoff in the process.

After all the commotion, Aegwynn had taken the role of adviser and replaced Kristoff. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, Epilogue)

### World of Warcraft
Despite Jaina's efforts, some people in Theramore continue to desire ill upon the [Horde](Horde). A group of Theramore deserters calling themselves [Daelin's Brigade](Daelin's Brigade), led by [Sellick Voss](Sellick Voss), set up [a hideout](Deserter's Hideout) in the swamps from which they seek to reignite conflict between Theramore and the Horde. (ref: [quest: Daelin's Brigade]) More stray from Theramore with each day, and amongst the deserters that had remained imbedded in Theramore's ranks were [Lieutenant Caldwell](Lieutenant Caldwell) and [Private Hallan](Private Hallan), (ref: [quest: The Betrayal from Within]) who displayed a hatred for Horde and executed [Paval Reethe](Paval Reethe) for alleged involvement with the Horde. As a result of their plotting, the [Shady Rest Inn](Shady Rest Inn) at the edge of the [Barrens](Barrens) was destroyed after the deserters, while drunk, said too much about the [Vengeful Mariner](Vengeful Mariner) to [James "Smiling Jim" Hyal](James Hyal). The deserters, who could not allow this to reach Theramore, burned down the inn. (ref: [quest: Ritual of Divination])

## Organization
#### Leadership
During the first years of [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore)'s leadership, the Lady was aided by her secretary [Duree](Duree) and her chamberlain [Kristoff](Kristoff). The man was left in charge of the city and sat on its throne whenever Jaina was absent. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 13) When it came to military advice, Jaina trusted in [Colonel Lorena](Lorena) more than on her other officers. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 3) After Kristoff betrayed Jaina and allied himself with the demon [Zmodlor](Zmodlor), he was eventually killed and replaced in his role as chamberlain by [Aegwynn](Aegwynn).

[Archmage Tervosh](Archmage Tervosh) and the [night elf](night elf) [Pained](Pained) are both part of Jaina's council.

### People and culture
The population was not only made up of [Human Expedition](Human Expedition) veterans, but also of people from [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) who came after the [Third War](Third War) for political or economical reasons.

Theramore's citizens form a closed community: everyone knows most everyone else, and if they don't, they know someone else who does. Because of that, crime is not a major concern as criminal acts were rare. The Lady's soldiers generally punish crime in a quick and brutal way. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 1 - *True, crime was not a major concern in so closed a community as Theramore—everyone knew most everyone else, and if they didn't, they knew someone else who did—so criminal acts were rare enough. Those that were committed were generally punished quickly and brutally by Lady Proudmoore's soldiers.*)

Theramore's citizens also drink "boar's grog" brewed up north by the [orc](orc)s, corn whiskey, ale and wine. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 1) Theramore cuisine is known for its "bayou" style. (ref: [quest: Morgan Stern])

#### Military forces
The [Theramore Guard](Theramore Guard) is the military organization protecting the city. They are among the finest groups of [Alliance](Alliance) soldiers based in [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) and it is their duty to uphold the laws of the Alliance. (ref: [Captain Garran Vimes#Quotes](Captain Garran Vimes#Quotes))

The [Elite Guard](Elite Guard) is the official protection and personal guard of Lady Jaina Proudmoore. At one occasion, [Kristoff](Kristoff) sent them to [Northwatch Hold](Northwatch Hold) to face the Horde. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapters 13 & 20)

[Northwatch Hold](Northwatch Hold) is under the command of Theramore. Lady Jaina wasn't pleased by the Hold's continued presence but was under considerable pressure to keep it manned despite her stance on war. (ref: *[Cycle of Hatred](Cycle of Hatred)*, chapter 2)

There are also two training teams within the city - [Red Team](Red Team) and [Blue Team](Blue Team).

### Banner of Theramore
The anchor device on Theramore's banner perhaps has a double meaning: most obviously that Theramore is a port city; and that Theramore is a kind of anchor for the Alliance in Kalimdor and will not waver. The banner also displays a solar disk with rays and three stars appear below the anchor. The meaning for those is not certain. Some banners also display the "L"-like symbol of the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron), clearly indicating the city's roots. [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore) - the ruler of Theramore - is the daughter of Lord Admiral [Daelin Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore), former leader of the merchant nation of [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)). The similarity between Theramore's banner and the flag of Kul Tiras stems from this relationship.

The tabard that Theramore guards wear bears the anchor device only. It appears only on the front, and instead of using blue, on the tabard it is gold.
