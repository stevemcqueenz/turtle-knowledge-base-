-Shadowfang Keep**, formerly known as **Silverlaine Keep**, (ref: [The Silverpine Wars](The Silverpine Wars)) is a haunted keep in southern [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest), near [Pyrewood Village](Pyrewood Village). It is the base of operations for the evil archmage [Arugal](Arugal) and his [worgen](worgen) progeny.

## History
From the [World Dungeons](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Guide/World_Dungeons) page on the official World of Warcraft Community Site:
  *During the [Third War](Third War), the wizards of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) battled against the undead armies of the [Scourge](Scourge). When the wizards of [Dalaran](Dalaran) died in battle, they would rise soon after - adding their former might to the growing Scourge. Frustrated by their lack of progress (and against the advice of his peers) the [Archmage](Archmage), [Arugal](Arugal) elected to summon extra-dimensional entities to bolster Dalaran's diminishing ranks.  Arugal's summoning brought the ravenous worgen into the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth). The feral wolf-men slaughtered not only the Scourge, but quickly turned on the wizards themselves.  The worgen sieged the keep of the noble, [Baron Silverlaine](Baron Silverlaine). Situated above the tiny hamlet of [Pyrewood](Pyrewood), the keep quickly fell into shadow and ruin. Driven mad with guilt, Arugal adopted the worgen as his children and retreated to the newly dubbed 'Shadowfang Keep'. It's said he still resides there, protected by his massive pet, Fenrus - and haunted by the vengeful ghost of Baron Silverlaine.*

Pyrewood Village is shadowed to the north by Shadowfang Keep, once a bastion of [Alliance](Alliance) power but now the dark residence of a mad former [mage](mage) of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor), named Arugal. The worgen that stalk the Keep, as well as the cursed villagers of Pyrewood, are the doings of this madman.  During the Third War, Arugal made these worgen as allies but they could not be controlled and turned on him and the other soldiers.  He went mad after this and now accepts them as his "children."  His worgen run rampant throughout much of the forest.

[Jordan Stilwell](Jordan Stilwell) and [Daphne Stilwell](Daphne Stilwell) used to live and work here before the Third War.

During the events of World of Warcraft, agents of the Banshee Queen [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner) were dispatched to both solve the mystery of the enigmatic worgen and ultimately terminate the source of the creatures' infestation in Silverpine.  Eventually they were able to discover that the mad archmage, Arugal, was behind the worgen's presence, and the [Forsaken](Forsaken) command ordered him slain.
