The **Tower of Azora** is a small [mage tower](mage tower) in central [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest). The tower is run by a number of servants of [Azora](Azora), who all appear to be [gnome](gnome)s, and who are in turn presided over by the Archmage of Azora, [Theocritus](Theocritus). The denizens of the tower are in a constant feud with the inhabitants of the [Tower of Ilgalar](Tower of Ilgalar) in [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains), ruled over by the evil sorcerer [Morganth](Morganth). The two towers are constantly spying on one another through magical means.

Inside the tower are several vendors and, most notably, the [Artisan](Artisan) [enchanter](enchanter) for the [Alliance](Alliance).

An interview with the servants of Azora in the [Gadgetzan Times](Gadgetzan Times) reveals that many things straying too near the tower are adversely affected by its magical aura, so the servants often have to battle living buckets and mutant butterflies. (ref: [Gadgetzan Times/The Servants of Azora](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gadgetzan_Times/The_Servants_of_Azora))

## Eye of Azora
Upon the table on the top floor is a magical artifact named the Eye of Azora which allows anyone who touches it to see the surroundings of the Tower of Ilgalar. It is placed there during the quest  [21] [A Watchful Eye](A Watchful Eye).
