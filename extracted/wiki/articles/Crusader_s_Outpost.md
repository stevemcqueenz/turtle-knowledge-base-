# Crusader's Outpost

This **Crusader's Outpost** is a guard tower located in western [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades). It is under the control of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade), who have allied with the farmers of [Solliden Farmstead](Solliden Farmstead) in order to fight back the [Scourge](Scourge) and [Forsaken](Forsaken) who have settlements nearby. This tower is the westernmost holding of the Scarlet Crusade in all of Lordaeron, which also means they are so far from their main base of [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand) that should any trouble befall them, they will not have enough time to wait for reinforcements.  It is one of the four [Crusader's Outpost](Crusader's Outpost) towers in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades).
