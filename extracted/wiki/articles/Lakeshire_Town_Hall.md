# Lakeshire Town Hall

The **Lakeshire Town Hall** in the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains) is an old building that has been diligently refurbished several times. The [town hall](town hall) is commonly the seat of rule by the local magistrate, and the current magistrate, [Solomon](Magistrate Solomon), is a kind and wise ruler. The town's problems with [gnoll](gnoll)s and [Blackrock](Blackrock Clan) orcs has put him in a state of desperation, however, and he readily accepts aid from helpful adventurers.
