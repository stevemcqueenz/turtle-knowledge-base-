# Stagalbog Cave

A large cave located in the [Stagalbog](Stagalbog) in eastern [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows).  Many [murlocs](murlocs) of the Marsh tribe live here, with their leader, the [Swamp Talker](Swamp Talker).
