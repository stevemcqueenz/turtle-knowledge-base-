# Golden Sansam

-Golden Sansam** is an [herb](herb) that can be gathered with [Herbalism](Herbalism) (260). The plant is found mostly in [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater), but also in [Azshara](Azshara), the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands), [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes), [Feralas](Feralas), [Felwood](Felwood), [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands). [Lapidis Isle](Lapidis Isle), & [Gillijim's Isle](Gillijim's Isle).
