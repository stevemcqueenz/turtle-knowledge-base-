## Turtle WoW Pre-BIS guide
You don't need dungeon Pre-BIS on Turtle WoW, but if you want to aim for it, the tools are available. 

Database - https://database.turtlecraft.gg/ for searching any item available on Twow. If you want to filter, you need to search for anything first, and then you have all filters available on the next page. 

AtlasLoot Addon - available from the launcher homepage - an in-game tool to access loot tables and droprates for most items in the game (is updated semi-frequently) 

On Turtle, there are multiple pugs every hour for 
- Zul'gurub (ZG)
- Ruins of ahn'qiraj (AQ20) 
- Lower Karazhan Halls (kara10)
- Molten Core (MC)

These typically do not require any gear checks, and usually a player can soft-reserve 1 or 2 items.

If you don't wish to sit in a dungeon queue, you can find a group yourself or join a raid by typing '/join world' in game.
