The **Arathi Highlands** are located in southeastern [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), east of [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills) and south of [the Hinterlands](Hinterlands). It is a flat but craggy region that has traditionally been the home of the humans of [Arathor](Arathor), who gave the region its name. The main hubs of activity are now [Refuge Pointe](Refuge Pointe) and [Hammerfall](Hammerfall), which houses bases for [Alliance](Alliance) and [Horde](Horde), respectively. The large city of [Stromgarde](Stromgarde) lies in ruins to the southwest, and pockets of [Syndicate](Syndicate) resistance dot the land. The Boulderfist ogres also infest Arathor, and prove to be a constant threat to the [Alliance](Alliance), [Horde](Horde), and [Syndicate](Syndicate) factions vying for supremacy.

The Syndicate, Boulderfist ogres and the beleaguered human defenders of Stromgarde battle for supremacy in this gray, dismal realm. Prince [Galen Trollbane](Galen Trollbane) leads the human defenders, and his mighty city of Stromgarde is a ruined battleground where the three factions wage guerrilla war. To the south is the [Thandol Span](Thandol Span), a massive dwarven construction that bridges the canal between Lordaeron and [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan).

## Geography
The Arathi Highlands have a higher elevation than Khaz Modan's Wetlands, but otherwise the geography is similar. To the north is Thoradin's Wall, now collapsed and gaping wide. South is the Thandol Span, which bridges the gap between Lordaeron and Khaz Modan.

The Arathi Highlands are an area of grassy land in Southeastern [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). Notable features include the great circles of binding, which are protected by elementals and used to keep some unknown entity imprisoned. The region lies just north of the huge canyon running between the highlands and the [Wetlands](Wetlands) to the south, which can only be crossed by the [Thandol Span](Thandol Span). The area is home to the ruins of [Stromgarde Keep](Stromgarde Keep), which has been overrun by [ogre](ogre)s and the [Syndicate](Syndicate). The wildlife of the highlands includes giant [spider](spider)s, [carrion bird](carrion bird)s, and [raptor](raptor)s.

There are no instanced dungeons in the Arathi Highlands, though the ruins of [Stromgarde Keep](Stromgarde Keep) are one of the most well known [micro dungeons](Dungeon) in the game. The [Tower of Arathor](Tower of Arathor) micro dungeon can also be found here. The entrance to the [Arathi Basin](Arathi Basin) battleground can also be found in this zone.
