# Misty Reed Strand

The **Misty Reed Strand** is the gloomy coastline of the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows). There is little along the vast stretch of beach but driftwood, debris, and [crocolisk](crocolisk)s, [crab](crab)s, and [murloc](murloc)s. Adventurers of the [Alliance](Alliance) come here to collect driftwood and slay crabs, and the [Horde](Horde) have several missions to slay the various murlocs spread about the beach.
