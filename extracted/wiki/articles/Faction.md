# Faction

# Overview
A group of usually [racial](Race) but sometimes ideological allies.

Two main factions in [World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft) are the [Alliance](Alliance) and the [Horde](Horde). Players of one of these factions are always at war with the other. Other independent [Neutral](Neutral) factions also exist.

Also see [:Category:Faction](:Category:Faction).
