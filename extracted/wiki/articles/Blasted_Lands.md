The **Blasted Lands** bear that name for a reason: they were twisted by the magic that brought the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal) into being, spewing the rampaging [Horde](Horde) into [Azeroth](Azeroth). 

This wasted desert of red clay is home to the original Dark Portal. The portal still stands, and once led to the orcs' sundered homeworld, [Draenor](Draenor). Nethergarde Keep, a Dalaranian fortress of grim mages, keeps watch over the portal and the demons and ogres that would abuse its power.

Now, the wretched land hosts few inhabitants and no significant settlements. Magic that leaked through the Dark Portal warped the Blasted Lands, leaving an infertile desert landscape. Hardy desert species like Basilisks, Scorpions and Hyenas have since colonized the area. The red rocks of the region do not come by their color naturally. The original tan color was transmuted into the unearthly crimson by searing heat and chaotic energies when the Dark Portal was destroyed.

## Geography
This desolate and barren land has very little in the way of flora and fauna, barring the demons which now wander the plains. The region is particularly notable because the inert Dark Portal can be found here.

There are no instanced dungeons or battlegrounds in this zone, though several micro dungeons can be found here.
