## Notes
**Talking to Falgran Hastil**
What is it you need?

  > Gossip: I am ready for the meeting.

  **Falgran Hastil says:** Kagoro! We are ready.
  **Kagoro says:** Falgran.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** Kagoro, on behalf of the Alliance and the City of Theramore, I would like to apologize for not believing you and my false accusations. We are now certain that the deserters burned the inn.
  **Kagoro says:** I am relieved to hear it. Our investigation of Black Dragons yielded no results. But how did you learn of the truth?
  **Falgran Hastil says:** Through the power of magic, of course. Arts of Divination. We communed with the spirit of Paval Reethe.
  **Kagoro says:** Extraordinary. Incredible sorcery, as always. But... why? Why did they burn the inn?
  **Falgran Hastil says:** Well. This is where it gets a bit complicated. The deserters were regular patrons of the inn. In their drunken state, they confessed... something to the innkeeper. In fear of the information reaching Theramore, they preferred to kill him and leave false evidence.
  **Kagoro says:** The shield... Yes, it makes sense.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** Paval Reethe wanted to stop them. He may have been a deserter, but he had some of his honor still left in his heart.
  **Kagoro says:** That's why they left him in the swamp. But, what was the information that was so precious to the deserters?
  **Falgran Hastil says:** About this, we are not sure. Something about the [Vengeful Mariner](Vengeful Mariner), their rumored true leader. Have you heard about him?
  **Kagoro says:** Vengeful Mariner... The Ogres speak of a haunted sailor terrorizing the shores, but I doubt it's connected.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** We will soon bring justice to the deserters for their crimes, thank you for your information Kagoro, the horde has helped plenty in these lands.
  **Kagoro says:** We believe in justice the same way you alliance do, go with honor friend.
  **Falgran Hastil says:** We should report to Captain Garran Vimes and let him know our discoveries.
