# Cliffspring River

The **Cliffspring River** is a river in the northern part of [Darkshore](Darkshore). It flows from the [Cliffspring Falls](Cliffspring Falls) out to the sea. The river, like so many parts of Darkshore, is tainted by the local [Stormscale](Stormscale tribe) [naga](naga) and local wildlife which drinks from it often become sick.
