# Sorrowmurk

The **Sorrowmurk** is the area of the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows) located just east of the [Pool of Tears](Pool of Tears) and the [Sunken Temple](Sunken Temple). The area is crawling with green [dragonspawn](dragonspawn) and travelers are warned that venturing into the area is extremely hazardous. As if this was not enough, there is also a large green [drake](drake) that patrols the region.
