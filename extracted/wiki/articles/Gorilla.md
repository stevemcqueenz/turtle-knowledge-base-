# Gorilla

Gorillas are a species of ape found only in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), [Feralas](Feralas), and [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater).   They are typically hostile to any travellers that come across them.  An enormous gorilla named King Mukla dwells on Jaguero Island in southeast [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale), where he holds a [Tauren](Tauren) named Princess Poobah captive.  

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Have high HP, medium damage, and low armor.
- Eats only Fruit and Fungus.
- Can learn Cower, Growl, and Bite.  They have yet to recieve their species skill.
- Unpopular as a hunter pet due to their scarceness, lack of skills and fussy diet.
