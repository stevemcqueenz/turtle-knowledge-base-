## Objectives
Slay 10 Expedition Officers, Hydromancer Shaleborn, and Deckmaster Darkhollow for Warbringer Dar'nakk in Razor Hill.

## Description
If we wish to utterly dismantle the Expedition Fleet, then we must crush the head of the snake: Captains, commanders, officers and other figures of interest. The Shattered Hand Clan was kind enough to provide me with a list of their names.

Go to Anchor's Edge, south of the Echo Isles in southeastern Durotar.

Once you arrive, you must look for and kill Hydromancer Shaleborn and Deckmaster Darkhollow, along with any breathing officer on the isle .

Their whole leadership shall crumble!

## Progress
It must be done, their leaders must die. They, more than the luckless footsoldiers they throw at us, are at fault for the persecution of our people.

Do not forget it.

## Completion
It is done, then. With their chain of command gone, it might take months for their expedition to re-organize.

We shall not give them such a luxury.
