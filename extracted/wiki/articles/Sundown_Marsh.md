# Sundown Marsh

[Sundown Marsh](Sundown Marsh) is the deepest part of the [Wetlands](Wetlands), where an odd couple make their home. [Fradd Swiftgear](Fradd Swiftgear) and [Wenna Silkbeard](Wenna Silkbeard) live here in a small stone dwelling with a windmill. They sell peculiar items they come across in the marsh, and also some uncommon recipes. The swampland on all sides are full of aggressive [crocolisks](crocolisks), [gnolls](gnolls), [raptors](raptors), [bog beasts](bog beasts) and [oozes](oozes), which discourage inexperienced adventurers from approaching.
