*This article is about the predecessor organization to the current faction known as the [Alliance](Alliance).*

The **Alliance of Lordaeron** (aka **Lordaeron Alliance**, (ref: *Day of the Dragon*, chapter 1) **Great Alliance**, (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness The Official Strategy Guide*, pg. 93) **Grand Alliance**, (ref: *World of Warcraft* manual, pg. 171: "Even so, when the orcs invaded Azeroth and set out to conquer the human, elven, and dwarven lands, the dwarves offered to join the Grand Alliance.") **Human Alliance**, (ref: *Warcraft II: The Dark Saga* manual, [The Human Alliance](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_The_Dark_Saga_manual#The_Human_Alliance)) or **old Alliance** (ref: [Colonel Hardinus#On-click](Colonel Hardinus#On-click))) was the union of the [seven human kingdoms](Seven Kingdoms), along with the [dwarves](Dwarf) of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) (which also included the [gnomes](Gnome) of [Gnomeregan](Gnomeregan (nation))) and [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak), the [high elves](High elf) of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas (kingdom)), and others with major political influence including the [Church of Light](Church of Light). This coalition formed as a response to the threat of the [Orcish Horde](Old Horde) during the [Second War](Second War).

## History
### Second War
Following the destruction of the [Kingdom of Azeroth](Stormwind (kingdom)) by the [Orcish Horde](Orcish Horde) during the [First War](First War), its survivors fled to [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)). Realizing the threat the [orc](orc)s posed, [Regent Lord Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar), leader of Azeroth's refugees, was able to convince the leaders of the other human kingdoms to unify against the orcs as the Alliance. The [Wildhammer](Wildhammer clan) dwarves of [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak) and high elves also soon joined the Alliance, as well as the gnomes of Gnomeregan and the [Ironforge](Ironforge (kingdom)) dwarves later in the war.

The Church of Light was a strong supporter of Alliance's effort. During the First War, the Clerics of Northshire bravely supported the armies of the kingdom of Stormwind with clerics who healed wounds and channeled devastating powers of Holy Light against their foes. Following the destruction of Stormwind, Archbishop Alonsus Faol was one of the key figures in forming of the Alliance. Having perceived that the pious Clerics of Northshire, who suffered such terrible attrition in the First War, were ill-suited for the dangers of combat, he, along with many of the surviving Clerics of Northshire, sought those of only the greatest virtue among the knighthood of Lordaeron and tutored them in the ways of magic. Christening them the Knights of the Silver Hand, it now rested upon the newly-formed paladins to heal the wounds sustained in combat and to restore faith in the promise of freedom from Orcish tyranny. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Alliance Ground Units](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Alliance_Ground_Units)*, Paladin)

The Alliance — under the leadership of Sir Anduin Lothar as its Supreme Allied Commander — ultimately defeated the [Old Horde](Old Horde) during the battle of [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire). Although Anduin Lothar was slain by [Warchief](Warchief) [Orgrim Doomhammer](Orgrim Doomhammer), his death inspired [Turalyon](Turalyon) to push the Alliance to victory. Turalyon's forces succeeded in destroying the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal), the mystical gateway that connected the orcs to their homeworld of [Draenor](Draenor). Cut off from its reinforcements and fractured by infighting, the Horde finally buckled in upon itself and fell before the might of the Alliance, thus ending the [Second War](Second War).

The scattered orc clans were quickly rounded up and placed within guarded internment camps under the [Alliance Internment Act](Alliance Internment Act). Though it seemed that the Horde had been defeated for good, some remained highly skeptical that peace would last. [Khadgar](Khadgar), now an [Archmage](Archmage) of some renown, convinced the Alliance [high command](High Command) to build the fortress of [Nethergarde Keep](Nethergarde Keep) that would watch over the ruins of the Dark Portal and ensure that there would be no further invasions from Draenor. (ref: [The Alliance of Lordaeron](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Alliance_of_Lordaeron))

After the death of Lothar, the Alliance of Lordaeron was led by King [Terenas Menethil II](Terenas Menethil II). He accepted ambassadors or the monarchs from all member nations and races to his capital. Rarely did the monarchs of the major realms come together. Generally, they spoke through couriers and diplomats, with the occasional state visit thrown in as well. (ref: *Day of the Dragon*, pg. 79) One of the inner council of the Kirin Tor had ever been present at such events (with the exception of one meeting Dalaran was not invited, led by Lord [Daval Prestor](Deathwing), though spied upon by [Krasus](Krasus)). (ref: *Day of the Dragon*, pg. 86 - 87)

### Political crises
Several nations left the Alliance in response to decisions made after the Second War, including King Terenas's institution of a tax to fund the orcish internment camps and fund the rebuilding of Stormwind. (ref: [The Alliance Splinters](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Alliance_Splinters)) Furthermore, there have been [heated debates](Alterac crisis) on what to do with the vacant territories of the treasonous [Kingdom of Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)), with the [Kingdom of Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)) wanting to annex portions of it to their kingdom while others wanted to hand the kingdom whole to an Alterac noble promising increased influence to their kingdoms. (ref: *Day of the Dragon*, pg. 6)

Disagreements over what to do with the lethargic orcs (mostly pertaining to internment camp costs) created tension between some member states of the Alliance of Lordaeron. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* Game Manual, pg. 67) The higher taxes that resulted, along with the high expense of maintaining and operating the numerous orc internment camps, led many leaders - Genn Greymane of Gilneas in particular - to believe that their kingdoms would be better off seceding from the Alliance. Quel'Thalas, led by [King Anasterian Sunstrider](Anasterian Sunstrider), brusquely rescinded their allegiance to the Alliance, stating that the humans' poor leadership had led to the burning of their forests during the Second War. Terenas fought back his impatience and quietly reminded the elves that nothing of Quel'Thalas would have remained if not for the hundreds of valiant humans who'd given their lives to defend it. Nonetheless, the elves stubbornly decided to go their own way. In the wake of the elves' departure, [Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)) and [Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)) seceded as well. (ref: 

However, while Admiral Proudmoore of [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)) also briefly followed suit,<ref>[Assault on Kul Tiras (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_on_Kul_Tiras_(WC2_Orc))) at some point after his nation came under attack by the [Shadowmoon clan](Shadowmoon clan) he and his nation rejoined the Alliance. In the aftermath of the splintering of the Alliance, Kul Tiras resumed its role as a stalwart ally of King Terenas along with Stormwind. Admiral Proudmoore, whose mighty fleets patrolled the trade lanes hunting pirates and marauders, maintained order on the high seas. (ref: [*Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* - A New Generation (manual text)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#A_New_Generation))

### Invasion of Draenor
Under [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul)'s surgical command, the remnant orcs from [Draenor](Draenor) eventually reassembled into the [Horde of Draenor](Horde of Draenor), reopened the Dark Portal, raided several Alliance settlements, and rounded up several Azeroth artifacts that they needed before fleeing back to the safety of Draenor.

King [Terenas](Terenas Menethil II) of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), convinced that the orcs were preparing a new invasion of Azeroth, assembled his most trusted lieutenants. He ordered General [Turalyon](Turalyon) and the archmage, [Khadgar](Khadgar), to lead an [Alliance Expedition](Alliance Expedition) [through the Dark Portal](Invasion of Draenor) to put an end to the orcish threat once and for all. Those recruited into the Expedition included veteran heroes of the Alliance such as [Danath Trollbane](Danath Trollbane), [Kurdran Wildhammer](Kurdran Wildhammer), and [Alleria Windrunner](Alleria Windrunner). The Alliance Expedition, however, never returned because Turalyon and Khadgar agreed to make the ultimate sacrifice by destroying the Dark Portal from their side on Draenor. Though it would cost their lives, and the lives of their companions, they knew that it was the only way to ensure Azeroth's survival. (ref: [https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Invasion_of_Draenor])

### Third War
Years later, an orc gladiator named [Thrall](Thrall) led an orcish insurrection against the orcish internment camps. (ref: [The New Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_New_Horde)) Some Alliance leaders feared the young Warchief and his smaller volatile [Horde](Horde) may pose as dire a threat as the Old Horde did. (ref: [The Warning (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Warning_(WC3_Human))) At the same time, unbeknownst to the Alliance, [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) began forming the [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned) in Alliance lands and have been spreading the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath) in [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). (ref: [Kel'Thuzad and the Forming of the Scourge](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Kel%27Thuzad_and_the_Forming_of_the_Scourge)) [Uther](Uther the Lightbringer) and his fellow [paladin](paladin)s investigated the infected regions in the hope of finding a way to stop the plague. Despite their efforts, the plague continued to spread and threatened to tear the Alliance apart. As the ranks of the undead swept across Lordaeron, [Terenas](Terenas Menethil II)' only son, Prince [Arthas](Arthas Menethil), took up the fight against the [Scourge](Scourge). The Scourge was a difficult foe to beat, for the [undead](undead) ranks swelled with every soldier that fell defending the land. Arthas began employing extreme measures to fight the undead, and his obsession with defeating the Scourge led to him losing his humanity. (ref: [The Scourge of Lordaeron](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Scourge_of_Lordaeron_(History_of_Warcraft)))

Eventually, the Alliance was ravaged by the [Scourge](Scourge) and the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion) during the [Third War](Third War). Terenas led the Alliance — since the end of the Second War — until his murder almost 15 years later at the hands of [Prince Arthas](Arthas Menethil), leading to the collapse of Lordaeron during the scourging of Lordaeron. Arthas attacked Quel'Thalas shortly after, ransacking the elven homeland and nearly scouring the high elves from Azeroth. The survivors of this massacre proclaimed themselves [blood elves](Blood elf) in honor of their peoples' fall, and a group of them joined the [Alliance Remnants](Alliance Remnants) led by [Grand Marshal Garithos](Othmar Garithos) who attempted to reclaim Lordaeron from the undead. The blood elves abandoned the Alliance Remnants due to racism on behalf of its commander and left Azeroth to escape to [Outland](Outland). Grand Marshal Garithos was killed in the following [civil war of the Plaguelands](Plaguelands civil war), and the Alliance Remnants fell apart soon after his death.

### Legacy
Following the Third War, the balance of power among the human kingdoms shifted. With Lordaeron's fall and the devastation of the northern kingdoms, the kingdom of Stormwind arose as a new bastion of leadership and military might in the Alliance. Despite the collapse of the Alliance of Lordaeron, King Magni Bronzebeard and the dwarves of the kingdom of Ironforge remained allies of the modern Alliance, (ref:  [12] [Protecting the Herd](Protecting the Herd)) becoming their staunchest and closest geographical allies.
