# Boulder Lode Mine

The **Boulder Lode Mine** is a mine located in the northeastern section of the [Barrens](Barrens). It is controlled by the [Venture Company](Venture Company) and seems to have once belonged to the [Horde](Horde) judging by the [orc](orc)ish architecture and Horde banners found around the area. [Boss Copperplug](Boss Copperplug) runs this operation.
