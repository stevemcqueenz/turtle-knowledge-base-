# Aerie Peak

[Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak) is a city affiliated with the [Alliance](Alliance) and located in [The Hinterlands](The Hinterlands) (formerly [Northeron](Northeron)), complete with a [Gryphon](Gryphon) master. Also found here is a [Master](Master) [Leatherworking](Leatherworking) trainer.

## History
Aerie Peak is the home of the [Wildhammer](Wildhammer) Dwarves, a faction that broke off from the rest of Dwarven society after the [War of the Three Hammers](War of the Three Hammers).  Because of the horrific underground battles the Wildhammers were forced to fight against the [Dark Iron](Dark Iron) Dwarves during the war, the Wildhammers chose to build most of their dwellings above ground, instead of burrowing into the mountain as is typical of Dwarven designs. 

The Dwarves of Aerie Peak are master Gryphon riders, and their contributions to the Alliance during war cannot be overstated.
