# Northfold Manor

[Northfold Manor](Northfold Manor) is a farm on the north-western outskirts of [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), occupied by forces of the [Syndicate](Syndicate).

A [rare spawn](rare spawn), [Singer](Singer) appears in one of the huts there.
