# Purple Lotus

-Purple Lotus** is an [Herb](Herb) found in [Felwood](Felwood) that can be gathered with [Herbalism](Herbalism) (210).

## Source
Purple Lotus can typically be found around troll, night elf and titan ruins in [Felwood](Felwood). There is a chance that one harvesting a Purple Lotus may find a [Wildvine](Wildvine).
Purple Lotus could also be found in the following zones:
-[Tanaris](Tanaris)
-[Azshara](Azshara)
-[Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale)
-[The Hinterlands](Hinterlands)
-[Feralas](Feralas)
-[Badlands](Badlands)
-[Ashenvale](Ashenvale)
-[Dire Maul](Dire Maul)
-[Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub)
