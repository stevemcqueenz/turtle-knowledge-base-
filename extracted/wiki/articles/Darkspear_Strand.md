# Darkspear Strand

The [Darkspear Strand](Darkspear Strand) [55, 81] is where the [Darkspear](Darkspear) [trolls](trolls) arrived upon their evacuation of the [Echo Isles](Echo Isles). Just yards away from the strand sits [Sen'Jin Village](Sen'Jin Village), where the trolls gathered after fleeing the islands.
