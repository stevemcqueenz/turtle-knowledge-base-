# Vault of Ironforge

The **Vault of Ironforge** is the main [bank](bank) and treasure store in the city of [Ironforge](Ironforge), located just across from the [Auction House](Auction House) in the [Commons](Commons) district. Available to members of the [Alliance](Alliance), the bank's services are overseen by [Dinita](Dinita Stonemantle) and the Stonemantle family.
