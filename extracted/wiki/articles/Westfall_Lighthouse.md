# Westfall Lighthouse

The **Westfall Lighthouse** sits just off the coast on the far southern edge of [Westfall](Westfall). Outside the lighthouse stands the ghostly [Captain Grayson](Captain Grayson), who tasks adventurers with killing the [murloc](murloc)s along the coast. The lighthouse was once staffed by humans, but [Old Murk-Eye](Old Murk-Eye) scared the keeper's family off. (ref: [quest: The Coastal Menace]) They returned and re-lit the flame but Old Murk-Eye coerced the murlocs to raid the Lighthouse with him once again. The second time the family was not so lucky, and before Grayson's eyes they perished helplessly. With no one to keep watch on the flame, the responsibility has fallen upon him. (ref: [quest: Keeper of the Flame])

A beam of light will circle from the top of the lighthouse at night. During [Children's Week](Children's Week), the [Alliance](Alliance) [Human Orphan](Human Orphan) will ask the adventurer to take him here.
