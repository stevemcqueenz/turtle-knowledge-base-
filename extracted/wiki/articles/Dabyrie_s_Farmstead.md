# Dabyrie's Farmstead

A farmstead in northern part of [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), inhabited by local Arathi humans, and protected by Dabyrie Militia.

The people of Dabyrie are part of the Alliance. Their leaders, the Dabyrie family, are Fardel Dabyrie and Kenata Dabyrie.

### Trivia
Many people speculate that the name "Dabyrie" is originated/suggested by World of Warcraft's Lead Producer, Shane (a.k.a. Shahram) Dabiri.
