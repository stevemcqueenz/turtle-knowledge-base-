The **Silver Covenant** is a faction of [high elves](High elf) of the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant) that is loyal to Lady [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner) that formed following her removal as Speaker of the Remnant and the establishment of the [Regency Council](Regency Council). During the resettlement of the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands), Vereesa's right hand, [Commander Braedin](Commander Braedin) was in charge of cleansing the lands of any perils that could endanger the refugees seeking sanctuary, (ref: [quest: Wyrms of the Highlands]) and keeping watch over the [blood elves](Blood elf)' mountain stronghold of [Felstrider Retreat](Felstrider Retreat). (ref: [quest: Felstrider Retreat]) They are primarily in opposition to the [Sunsworn Offensive](Sunsworn Offensive), a faction of the Remnant loyal to [Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn), with whom they clash in [Sunstrider Court](Sunstrider Court) in what they call "friendly competition." (ref: [Patch 1.18.0#NEW ARENA — SUNSTRIDER COURT](Patch 1.18.0#NEW ARENA — SUNSTRIDER COURT))

The Silver Covenant achieved a victory over the Scourge, claiming [Shalandis Isle](Shalandis Isle) as the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant)'s first foothold in Eversong in eight years. However, this victory was bought through the diverting of funds intended for the Regency Council and the people of the Silvermoon Remnant. As such, it undercut the Covenant's victory and sabotaged Vereesa Windrunner's attempt to reclaim her position as Speaker when the matter was brought to light by Lord [Astalor Sunsworn](Astalor Sunsworn), who took advantage of the situation to have himself be named Lord-Regent of Quel'Thalas by the Regency Council. (ref: [A GOOD DAY — Everlook Broadcasting Co. Radio Play Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u90BCZfygz0))
