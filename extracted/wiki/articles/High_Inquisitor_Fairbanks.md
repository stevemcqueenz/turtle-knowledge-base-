# High Inquisitor Fairbanks

High Inquisitor Fairbanks, now an [undead](undead) being, lies hidden in a small chamber only accessible by activating a secret switch doubling as a torch within a room of the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery)'s Cathedral.

Formerly, he was [Highlord Mograine](Highlord Mograine)'s most trusted advisor, and saw [Scarlet Commander Mograine](Scarlet Commander Mograine) kill his own father. He told the story to his fellow Scarlet Crusaders and was murdered for it, then  hidden in one of the most secret areas of the Monastery by (the son) Mograine and his followers. Those crusaders who trusted his story, started the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn).

It is possible that he was High Inquisitor of the Monastery before [High Inquisitor Whitemane](High Inquisitor Whitemane) took over.

High Inquisitor Fairbanks continues the quest of the [Ashbringer](Ashbringer), once the corrupted Ashbringer has been found.
