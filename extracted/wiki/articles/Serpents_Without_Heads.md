## Objectives
Slay Lady Serpentia and Lord Ta'jax in the ruins of Nendis, then return to Sentinel Commander Silverstreak at Narvalis Point.
- [Lady Serpentia](Lady Serpentia) slain
- [Lord Ta'jax](Lord Ta'jax) slain

## Description
You have proven the naga can bleed. Now we make certain they cannot recover. Two commanders direct the Tideblade forces occupying Nendis. Veterans from the day Illidan’s armies burned the town to the ground.

Lady Serpentia.

Lord Ta'jax.

They served as lieutenants during the assault. It was their warbands that swept through the streets while the town burned. They have ruled the ruins ever since.

If the Tideblade are to lose their hold here, those two must fall. Without their leadership the naga will fracture into smaller broods, easier to drive back into the sea.

The naga have ruled those ruins long enough.
