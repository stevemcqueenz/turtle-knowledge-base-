The **Pellars of Wolfswood** (also known as the **Witchwood Pellars** (ref: [quest: The Fallen Ancient])) are an enigmatic order of guardians of the lands of [Gilneas](Gilneas). (ref: [Lurn Five-Pelts#Quotes](Lurn Five-Pelts#Quotes)) Believed to be a myth by many, (ref: [Tess Greymane#Quotes](Tess Greymane#Quotes)) they revere the old ways, specifically the spirit of the Wolf-Serpent - also known as the Wild God [Zalmos](Zalmos) (ref: <ref>[quest: Back to Thalanaar]) - whom they believe will soon return to Azeroth and for whom they make ready for his rebirth and for the cleansing of the lands. (ref: [quest: Deathly Fetters]) The Pellars have settled in the cave where Zalmos died. (ref: [Moonwraith from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/466626998593388579/1435287210000908411) - "But yeah, sadly the Wolf-Serpent is very dead. The cave their settled in is where he died basically.")

During the [Gilnean civil war](Gilnean civil war), the Pellars were approached by Princess [Tess Greymane](Tess Greymane), who followed the tales told to her by her late mother, Queen [Mia Greymane](Mia Greymane), to find help against the corruption that took hold of her father, King [Genn Greymane](Genn Greymane). (ref: [quest: On Legend's Trail]) At the height of the civil war, their healing skills were in high demand as they applied them to the people of Gilneas in need. (ref: [quest: The Serpentroot]) (ref: [quest: The Haunting of Aliattan Anderson])

The Pellars have the ability to partially transform themselves. (ref: [Moonwraith from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/466626998593388579/1435287012713431070) - "Unlike the Coven who are like legit worgen. The Pellars can turn an arm or two, their legs blah blah.") Amongst the artifacts guarded by the Pellars are the Fangs of Zalmos.
