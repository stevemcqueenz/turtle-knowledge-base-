# Far Watch Post

Far Watch Post is a small outpost of the Horde located just west of the bridge between [Durotar](Durotar) and [The Barrens](The Barrens) 62 20 The Barrens (g/s/c). It is made up of a watchtower and an orc burrow with its assortment of guards and helpers. A riding wolf pen watched over by a pair of peons is located a bit to the south, but it contains more chickens than wolves. Overall, this is not a very accommodating spot, but necessary for the protection of Durotar from the [Alliance](Alliance) and the [centaur](centaur) and [quilboar](quilboar) threat of the Barrens.
