## Objectives
Read the Tainted Rune and speak to Thorinn Darkgrovel inside Anvilmar above Coldridge Valley.
- [Tainted Rune](Tainted Rune)

## Description
While you were helping me out, this rune was given to me to pass on to you. Take some time to read it when you have a chance. I'm thinkin' it came from the warlock trainer Alamar. Take a gander at it and go find him inside Anvilmar when you've a chance.

And watch yerself, <name>, your kind ain't too trusted 'round these parts.

## Rewards
You will receive:
- 40 XP

## Progress
Have you read the rune? Excellent!

You standing here before me signals to me that you are willing and eager enough to tread the journey only a few will master; and survive. My clan has seen the weakness that ails our ancient home, the vermin that scurry among the icy fields and dark mountain caves. If our people don't accept any means necessary to exterminate those who do us harm, then Ironforge is doomed to perish.

## Completion
But that is far from any of your problems - yet. You are still a novice, a whelpling tossed into this turbulent world. Yet still, you serve a purpose. To usher in a new age for dwarvenkind. Train in the forbidden arts of the arcane and grow more powerful. Seek my clan members and other mighty warlocks. With their knowledge, the king cannot but recognize that he needs us to save the kingdom.

I may be still at the beginning of my journey too, but let me share what incantations I have learned by myself. Return to me often, and we shall exchange our wisdom of the shadowy arts.
