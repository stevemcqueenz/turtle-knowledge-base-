# High Wilderness

The **High Wilderness** is a vast forest that stretches from the [Forgotten Coast](Forgotten Coast) to [Verdantis River](Verdantis River) in western [Feralas](Feralas). Many creatures can be found in this forest, including but not limited to: [bear](bear)s, [wolves](wolf), [wind serpent](wind serpent)s, [hippogryph](hippogryph)s, [yeti](yeti)s, [gorilla](gorilla)s, [treant](treant)s, and [ogre](ogre)s.}
