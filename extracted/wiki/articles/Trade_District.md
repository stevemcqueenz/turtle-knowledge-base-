# Trade District

The **Trade District** is just inside [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) from the main gate. It is the place in the city where you can get most goods. The Guild Master is to the left in the [Visitors Center](Stormwind Visitor Center) when you come in and can help you make a guild or a guild tabard. The goods that are sold in the Trade District are cheese, arcane goods and reagents, general goods, flowers, cloth/leather/mail armor, bows, guns, and clothes. The [bank](bank), [mailbox](mailbox), and [inn](inn) are on the southwest side of the Trade District. The [Gryphon Master](Flight Master) is up a ramp on the east side. [Patch 1.9](Patch 1.9) added the linked [Auction House](Auction House) in the center of the Trade District.

To get to the Trade District, you can head northeast from the [Valley of Heroes](Valley of Heroes), southwest from [Old Town](Old Town), southeast from the [Cathedral Square](Cathedral Square), or northeast from the [Mage Quarter](Mage Quarter).
