The **Gor'dosh tribe** (ref: [quest: The Maul Orb]) (or **Gor'dosh Ogres**) (ref: [quest: The Gor'dosh Ogres]) are a group of [ogre](ogre)s residing in [Lapidis Isle](Lapidis Isle). They formerly dominated both Lapidis and [Gillijim's Isle](Gillijim's Isle), but the tribe later split, with the Gillijim ogres becoming the [Maul'ogg](Maul'ogg).

Upon the arrival of the [Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet](Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet) on Lapidis Isle, the Gor'dosh made battle with them constantly until they were driven back.
