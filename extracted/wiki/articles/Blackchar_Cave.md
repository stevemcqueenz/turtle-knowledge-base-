# Blackchar Cave

**Blackchar Cave** is a mysterious, sealed-off cave near [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain). This is where players can find [Ironband the Elder](Ironband the Elder) during the [Lunar Festival](Lunar Festival).
