# Further Studies (quest)

Further Studies (https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=40369) is a quest in Darkshore picked up from Nessa Shadowsong in Rut'Theran village on Teldrassil.

The objective of the quest is to gather Fine Crab Carcasses from the crabs of Darkshore, so that Nessa might discern if local corruption has spread to other parts of the local food chain.

The drop rate of these carcasses seems to be quite low, and differ between different crab mobs. The lowest level crabs seem to have next to no drop chance, where level 15-ish crabs seem to be the best to go for. Not enough data to say if the higher level crabs are better.

Tide crawler loot the carcasses at 100%
