# Ruins of Nendis

The **Ruins of Nendis** are what remains of the night elf seaport of **Nendis**, located along the eastern shores of [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast). The town was razed by the [naga](naga) on orders from [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage), who used the port to escape to the [Broken Isles](Broken Isles). [Maiev Shadowsong](Maiev Shadowsong) and the [Watchers](Watchers) pursued Illidan to Nendis during their hunt for the Betrayer, finding the village destroyed. (ref: [Rise of the Naga (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Rise_of_the_Naga_(WC3_NightElf))) To this day, the naga still infest the ruins, calling themselves the [Tideblade](Tideblade) - veterans of Illidan's army led by [Lady Serpentia](Lady Serpentia) and [Lord Ta'jax](Lord Ta'jax), lieutenants of Illidan's forces during the assault. (ref: [quest: Serpents Without Heads])
