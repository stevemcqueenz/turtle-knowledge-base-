# Silk Cloth

A [Silk](Silk) [item](item) [drop](drop) of usually [humanoid](humanoid) [mob](mob)s, not the same as [Spider's Silk](Spider's Silk) or [Thick Spider's Silk](Thick Spider's Silk). Less common than [Wool Cloth](Wool Cloth), but more common than [Mageweave Cloth](Mageweave Cloth). A primary [ingredient](ingredient) of [Tailoring](Tailoring) [recipe](recipe)s, but also used in recipes for [Blacksmithing](Blacksmithing), [Engineering](Engineering), [First Aid](First Aid), and [Leatherworking](Leatherworking).

*Also a [Reagent](Reagent) for various [Disguise](Disguise)s?*

## Simple Uses
Here are some simple uses early on for **Silk Cloth**:
**Blacksmithing**
   [Solid Weightstone](Solid Weightstone), 200, for [blunt](Blunt) weapons with [Solid Stone](Solid Stone)
**Engineering**
   [Discombobulator Ray](Discombobulator Ray), 160
   [Goblin Jumper Cables](Goblin Jumper Cables), 165
   [Solid Dynamite](Solid Dynamite), 175, with [Solid Blasting Powder](Solid Blasting Powder)
   [Iron Grenade](Iron Grenade), 175, with [Iron](Iron) Bars and [Heavy Blasting Powder](Heavy Blasting Powder)
   [Flash Bomb](Flash Bomb), 185, with a [Blue Pearl](Blue Pearl) and [Heavy Blasting Powder](Heavy Blasting Powder)
**First Aid**
   [Silk Bandage](Silk Bandage), 150
   [Heavy Silk Bandage](Heavy Silk Bandage), 180
**Tailoring**
   [Bolt of Silk Cloth](Bolt of Silk Cloth) for use in other recipes
**Reputation**
   Hand in 60 Silk Cloth to a faction quartermaster to gain [Reputation](Reputation#Reputation_from_Cloth_Quartermasters)
