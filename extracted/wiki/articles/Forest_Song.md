# Forest Song

-Forest Song** is located in northeastern [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), just northeast of [Satyrnaar](Satyrnaar), north of the [Warsong Lumber Camp](Warsong Lumber Camp), and southwest of [Bough Shadow](Bough Shadow). It is an ancient [Kaldorei](Kaldorei) ruin, inhabited by a sole night elf - [Kayneth Stillwind](Kayneth Stillwind). Although the scenery is beautiful and no danger appears to be lurking here, there is a certain sense of foreboding about the ruins. The air hangs dark and ominous over the area, and the usual forest sounds are still.
