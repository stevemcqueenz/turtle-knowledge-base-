# Lariss Pavilion

**Lariss Pavilion** is a large outdoor [night elf](night elf) style building in [Feralas](Feralas) just north of [Camp Mojache](Camp Mojache). The nearby [dryad](dryad)s that are usually peaceful have been corrupted by the [Emerald Nightmare](Emerald Nightmare) and now attack anyone. 

The Pavilion is home to a mysterious man named [Azj'Tordin](Azj'Tordin). Apparently, a [Highborne](Highborne) who disagreed with [Prince Tortheldrin](Prince Tortheldrin), he left [Dire Maul](Dire Maul) via means of a backdoor located at the bottom of the Pavilion, but not before an [imp](imp) by the name of [Pusillin](Pusillin) stole his book of incantations.
