The **Warsong Outriders** are the [Horde](Horde) forces of [Warsong Gulch](Warsong Gulch). They are locked in battle against the [Silverwing Sentinels](Silverwing Sentinels).

## Reputation
[Reputation](Reputation) is gained through participation in the [Warsong Gulch](Warsong Gulch) [Battleground](Battleground). You gain 35 reputation each time your side captures a flag. This reputation gain is increased to 45 on [holiday weekends](Battlegrounds holiday).

## Rewards
- Players that have exalted with [The Defilers](The Defilers), [Warsong Outriders](Warsong Outriders) and [Frostwolf Clan](Frostwolf Clan) receive the [title](title) of Conqueror.
