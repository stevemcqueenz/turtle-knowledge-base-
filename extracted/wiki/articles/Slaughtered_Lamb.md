# Slaughtered Lamb

The **Slaughtered Lamb** is a seedy pub in the [Mage Quarter](Stormwind City#The Mage Quarter) of [Stormwind City](Stormwind City). The pub's basement and catacombs serve as host to the city's [warlock](warlock) community and acts as a training ground for new recruits. The top floor is abandoned except for a bartender. Given the pub's name, it seems like the perfect place for warlocks to do their training.
