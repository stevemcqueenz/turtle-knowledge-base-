# Patch 1.18.1

**Patch 1.18.1: Nightmares of Ursol** was released on March 20, 2026.

## Patch notes
### NEW ZONES
- [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast).  Clad in moonlit splendor, this stretch of Kaldorei land is as mysterious as it is bewitched. Spirits of forlorn Highborne haunt the abandoned elven ruins and alien magic permeates the blue forests of this humid shore. An unbound land only a few would willingly call their home, yet it became such a haven for the wandering Tauren Tribe of the Moonhoof, now holding watch over the azure expanses of the chaotic Moonwhisper Coast.

### NEW DUNGEONS
- [Windhorn Canyon](Windhorn Canyon). This ancient canyon has been home to many tauren tribes who have in years past fought for the dominance of its flowing waters and shelter from the dangers of Kalimdor. The cultures and traditions of many have lived within Windhorn Canyon, which can be seen from the ancient shelters carved into the mountainside, to the relics coveted by the Tauren. As of recently the Windhorn Tauren were expelled and driven away by the Grimtotem who have conquered it, and claimed it for their own.
- [Frostmane Hollow](Frostmane Hollow)

### NEW RAIDS
- [Timbermaw Hold](Timbermaw Hold (raid)). As ancient as Kalimdor itself, this enigmatic labyrinthine network of tunnels and caves beneath [Mount Hyjal](Mount Hyjal) has been the home of the Furbolgs since well before the Sundering. Its halls are sacred among the tribes, a place of worship to their progenitors, the twin gods [Ursoc](Ursoc) and [Ursol](Ursol). Nowadays however, only wafts of putrid vapors escape the rotten caverns and whispers of venerating a foul god echo throughout Timbermaw Hold…

### ONYXIA'S LAIR EXTENSION & UPDATES
The broodmother of the Black Dragonflight has fought back many incursions into her lair, each inching closer to her demise. For this reason the security in her lair has been elevated, now featuring new guardians, the commander of her brood as a brand new boss and new tricks for Onyxia herself, the nest is now more perilous than ever. Lucky for you, peril attracts treasure, adding plenty of new equipment as rewards.
- Added two new creatures to Onyxia's Lair: [Onyxian Flamespawn](Onyxian Flamespawn) and [Onyxian Inciter](Onyxian Inciter).
- Added a new section and with it a new optional boss: [Broodcommander Axelus](Broodcommander Axelus).
- Added new mechanics to Onyxia.

### BLACKWING LAIR EXTENSION & UPDATES
Lord Victor Nefarius recently realized that his pitiful minions have been insufficient in preventing interlopers from walking up to his doorstep. Therefore he has taken steps in improving his entourage, with no small amount of help from his newly contracted alchemist.

- Ezzel Darkbrewer might be a goblin of small stature but he is but he is by no means a pushover. With an arsenal of chemical concoctions and a brilliant mind sharpened by many years in the field, he's ready to thwart any intruders stepping into his employer's laboratory. Oh, and don't forget the lumbering ogre he's atop of.
- Added a new mechanic or updated a mechanic for all bosses (with the exception of Chromaggus).
- Added a new section and with it a new optional boss: [Ezzel Darkbrewer](Ezzel Darkbrewer).

- [Sagh's Refuge](Sagh's Refuge). A new quest hub in Thousand Needles for Horde players located in Windhorn Canyon.
- [Ironstone Camp](Ironstone Camp). Updated Ironstone Camp in Thousand Needles with more details.

- [Draenei Exiles](Draenei Exiles) [Neutral]. The Draenei Tribes from Outland that have survived the rigors of warp travel and now find themselves upon new lands. The Sanv and the Moro'gai tribes now reside on Azeroth, brought to the planet by adept riftwalkers. The Draenei Exiles now seek to make a new chapter of prosperity for their people.

### SURVIVAL SKILL OVERHAUL
As our first profession, Survival Skill was held back by limited resources and inexperience. The ideas were fresh and exciting, and while many concepts, such as tents and gardening, received great reception after refinements, the profession ultimately did not live up to its potential.

Survival was originally scrapped during the vanilla development cycle. Because of this, we had a blank slate to work with and could steer the profession toward a role that fits alongside the others. For that reason, we decided to give Survival a more complementary function with an extensive overhaul.

The new, reworked Survival profession will act as a secondary profession that draws from other primary professions and supplements them with advantages for players who raise their Survival skill further. This is reflected in the over 80 new recipes added to the profession. For example, skilled Survivalists can create powerful tools that can slow, root, or even trap opponents. They can also craft bags suitable for the three gathering professions, as well as containers for fish and meat. This provides a unique experience in creating and enhancing synergy between different professions.

To give Survival additional depth, we are linking it to a new gathering activity: Woodcutting. Directly tied to your Survival skill, Woodcutting lets you search forests in the open world for wood and other materials needed for the profession.

Of course, Gardening will also receive several new additions. We will introduce three new crops that players can find in specific zones throughout the world. When fully grown, these crops will grant powerful buffs to ambitious gardeners. Additionally, reports indicate that unwelcome critters have begun infiltrating the crops of unsuspecting adventurers…

With this overhaul, we aim to cement Survival's place among the other professions and elevate it to a one‑of‑a‑kind position not seen before in Warcraft. We hope you'll have plenty of fun scouring the world for materials and using the many tools to increase your chances of surviving the wilds!

#### Tents & Taverns
- Both taverns and tents now grant the rested XP bonus at the same pace.
- Rested XP from killing creatures was reduced to a 50% bonus instead of the previous 100% bonus.
- Tent's rested XP bonus was capped at 25% of a player's rested XP, tents provide no benefit if the player has more than 25% rested XP.
- All taverns except those located in cities can now raise a player's rested XP up to 100%.
