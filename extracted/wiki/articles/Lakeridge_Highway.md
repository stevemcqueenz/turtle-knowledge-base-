# Lakeridge Highway

**Lakeridge Highway** is an old highway that runs along the southern edge of [Lake Everstill](Lake Everstill) in [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains). The highway was originally highly traveled in the days before the [First War](First War).  Since [orc](orc)s have taken over [Stonewatch Keep](Stonewatch Keep) the highway has fallen into disuse.  Now [gnoll](gnoll)s patrol most of the highway and travel on the road is not recommended.
