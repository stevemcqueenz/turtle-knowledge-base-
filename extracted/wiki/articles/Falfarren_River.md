# Falfarren River

The **Falfarren River** runs northeast to southwest across [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), and is considered by many to be the dividing line between the [night elven](night elven) territory under [Astranaar](Astranaar) to the west, and the [orcish](orcish) territory under [Splintertree Post](Splintertree Post) to the east. The water of the river originates at [Mount Hyjal](Mount Hyjal), passes through a series of waterfalls into the lake at [Xavian](Xavian), before it runs between Splintertree and [Night Run](Night Run) southwest. It then runs past the only bridge spanning the Falfarren, continuing between [Silverwing Outpost](Silverwing Outpost) and the southern [moonwell](Moonwell (Ashenvale)) before it slips underground beneath the mountains of the northern [Barrens](Barrens). Several level 26-27 aggressive [Bog Beast](Bog Beast) can be found roaming below the surface of the river at sections around [Felfire Hill](Felfire Hill).
