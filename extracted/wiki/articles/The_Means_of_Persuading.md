## Objectives
Use your persuasion skills to interrogate Vladeus.
- Obtain information from Vladeus

## Description
During your many adventures through Azeroth you have faced several perils and foes, unlike the man present before you.

You are more than sure he's not a wicked sort and chose to approach this lightly, staying true to your nature.

With a table full of choices you pick the best means of persuasion and are ready to begin.

## Notes
  -Speaking with Vladeus Springriver**
What are they going to do to me? I am sorry for all I have done, I really am.

I regret all of it, and given the chance I will surely make amends.

  > Gossip: *Vladeus Springriver, from Lakeshire, is it?*

   Vladeus remains silent while slightly nodding his head, the nod is followed by a cough. You assume his throat was parched after being in chains for so long.

  > Gossip: *Offer water to Vladeus.*

  **Vladeus Springriver says:** Thank you, you're the one that captured me, right? I am grateful you spared my life but there's nothing I can tell you.

  > Gossip: *Please Vladeus, make this easier for both of us. You look like a decent man, the people of Lakeshire wanted me to bring all of you back.*

  *Vladeus cries.*
  **Vladeus Springriver says:** A bit late for that don't you think? Since you took the life of the rest, I have nothing to tell you, the man in the red hood would return home and ki... I have said too much.

  > Gossip: *There is nothing to fear with me at your side, the Alliance will protect your family. The Scarlet Crusade already failed so many times.*

  **Vladeus Springriver says:** Perhaps you are right. I didn't really want it to end this way, we were promised a better life, we... I... I didn't expect they'd make me kill innocents, you have to believe me!

  > Gossip: *I do, take my word for it. Who recruited you and why?*

  **Vladeus Springriver says:** I didn't see his face clearly, he was wearing a red hood and was simply laughing every few minutes. Looking back, it was the stupidest choice I have ever made. As to why they recruited us, I am not really sure. He said we would be taken to a hidden place of the Crusade to be trained, he was complaining that their number was low and since the death of two of their leaders they became broken. I think he mentioned a name, Aventis or Adentis?

  > Gossip: *Abbendis?*

  **Vladeus Springriver says:** Yes, that's the one! Please I don't know anything else, I wish to return home.
