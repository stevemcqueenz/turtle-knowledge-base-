# Thunderhorn Water Well

The **Thunderhorn Water Well** is located in the midst of the grasslands in [Mulgore](Mulgore),44 46 Mulgore (g/s/c) by the north road between [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff) and [Bloodhoof Village](Bloodhoof Village).

This well had been taken over by the [Venture Company](Venture Company) in order to control the water supply but were run off by the tauren.
