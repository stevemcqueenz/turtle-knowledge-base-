# Leper gnome

A corrupted kind of [gnome](gnome).  Almost all leper gnomes are aggressive to both Horde and Alliance and will attack them on sight. Maddened by the Disease some Leper gnomes seem to believe that any player they lay eyes on is a trogg. However, most can still take orders from [Sicco Thermaplugg](Sicco Thermaplugg).

Leper gnomes are found almost exclusively in [Gnomeregan](Gnomeregan), the doomed capital of the gnomes However, there is one leper Gnome NPC named Gazoo who lives in the [Undercity](Undercity). On top of that there are two other leper gnomes who live within the Deeprun Tram.
