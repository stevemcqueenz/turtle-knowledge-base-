The **Searing Gorge** is a contested region in southern [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan). It is home to a group of exceptionally stout [Dark Iron dwarves](Dark Iron dwarf) called the [Thorium Brotherhood](Thorium Brotherhood), who have split from the [Dark Iron clan](Dark Iron clan).

## Geography
There are no instances actually located in the Searing Gorge. However, [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain) lies between it and the [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes). It is home to [Blackrock Depths](Blackrock Depths), [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire), [Molten Core](Molten Core), and [Blackwing Lair](Blackwing Lair).
