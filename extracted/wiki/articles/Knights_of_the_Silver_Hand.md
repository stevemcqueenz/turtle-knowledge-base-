*"Esarus thar no'Darador"—"By Blood and Honor We Serve"*
   — Motto of the Knights of the Silver Hand (ref: [Turalyon#Valley of Heroes Monument](Turalyon#Valley of Heroes Monument)) (ref: *[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*, chapter 2)

The **Knights of the Silver Hand** (ref:  (full name the **Order of the Knights of the Silver Hand**,<ref name="W3 Paladin">*Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* manual, *[Human Hero Units](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Paladin)*, Paladin) often referred to as the **Order of the Silver Hand**, (ref:  the **Paladins of the Silver Hand**,<ref>[The Story of Morgan Ladimore](The Story of Morgan Ladimore)) the **Silver Hand**, (ref: [The Defense of Strahnbrad (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Defense_of_Strahnbrad_(WC3_Human))) (ref: [Blackrock and Roll (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Blackrock_and_Roll_(WC3_Human))) (ref: [Digging up the Dead (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Digging_up_the_Dead_(WC3_Undead))) or the **Paladin Order** (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* Game Manual, pg. 6)) is the order of holy knights, (ref: <ref name="W2paladin">*Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Alliance Ground Units](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Alliance_Ground_Units)*, Paladin) commonly called [paladin](paladin)s, created by Archbishop [Alonsus Faol](Alonsus Faol) as the martial branch of the [Church of Light](Church of Light). Empowered by the Holy Light, they brandish both their warhammers and holy fire in the battle against all who would trample the meek and innocent, (ref:  as they seek to be an example of the Light's teachings, protecting the weak and vanquishing the evil of the world. Led by [Uther the Lightbringer](Uther the Lightbringer), they faced the [Old Horde](Old Horde) alongside the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron) during the [Second War](Second War).

For years, they remained loyal to King [Terenas Menethil II](Terenas Menethil II) and kept the peace in Lordaeron, settling civil disputes and quelling any demi-human uprisings in the realm.<ref name="WCIIINewGeneration>*Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* Game Manual, Human History, [A New Generation](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#A_New_Generation)) (ref: [Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos manual#Uther the Lightbringer](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Uther_the_Lightbringer)) During the Third War, the order was suspended by one of its own members, Prince Arthas Menethil, as Uther refused to purge the city of Stratholme infected by the plague of undeath. Although the paladins continued to fight against the Scourge, their leadership was decimated during the fall of Lordaeron. However, a part of the organization still remained active in the Alliance, as human and dwarf paladins from Stormwind and Ironforge were still referred to as "Knights of the Silver Hand". (ref: [quest: Consecrated Letter|race=human|class=paladin]) (ref: [quest: Consecrated Rune|race=dwarf|class=paladin]) Years later, [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring) came out of his exile and regrouped the remains of the order in Lordaeron to resume the fight against the undead and demons across Azeroth. (ref: 

## History
### Founding of the Order
   *"In the Light, we gather to empower our brother. In its grace, he will be made anew. In its power, he shall educate the masses. In its strength, he shall combat the shadow. And, in its wisdom, he shall lead his brethren to the eternal rewards of paradise."*
   — Archbishop [Alonsus Faol](Alonsus Faol)

The former order led by Archbishop [Alonsus Faol](Alonsus Faol), the [Holy Order of Northshire Clerics](Holy Order of Northshire Clerics), was devastated during the First War. Having perceived that the pious Clerics of Northshire were ill-suited for the dangers of combat. Along with many of the surviving Clerics of Northshire, Archbishop Faol and his apprentice [Uther](Uther the Lightbringer) sought those of only the greatest virtue among the knighthood of Lordaeron and tutored them in the ways of magic. Uther himself, dubbed "the Lightbringer", was anointed as the first of the paladins in the [Alonsus Chapel](Alonsus Chapel) in [Stratholme](Stratholme),<ref>[Aurius#Quotes](Aurius#Quotes)) and the Silver Hand was thus born with him as their leader. (ref: [Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness manual#Sire Uther Lightbringer](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Sire_Uther_Lightbringer)) (ref:  During the Second War, the Knights of the Silver Hand were adorned in armor fashioned from Dawnstone, a rare and highly magical and energy-resistant material<ref>[quest: The Dawnstone Coupler]) that can only be found in the [Dawnstone Mine](Dawnstone Mine) in [Gilneas](Gilneas). (ref: [quest: Legend of Dawnstone])

Subsequently, the paladins were instrumental in winning the war. They quelled a peasant revolt at [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand), (ref: [Tyr's Hand (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Tyr%27s_Hand_(WC2_Human))) and confronted the Horde forces in the city and Tyr's Bay until the orcs razed down the city with a massive naval assault. (ref: [The Razing of Tyr's Hand (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Razing_of_Tyr%27s_Hand_(WC2_Orc))) At the same time, Uther and his knights were watching over the northlands and came to offer comfort to those who were suffering the misfortunes of war on the recently attacked island township of [Caer Darrow](Caer Darrow). During the journey, they destroyed the orc troops still present near the island, (ref: [The Battle at Darrowmere (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Battle_at_Darrowmere_(WC2_Human))) as well as capturing a number of renegade Alterac soldiers and discovering a crew of Alterac sailors that had been assisting the Horde during the battle. (ref: [The Prisoners (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Prisoners_(WC2_Human))) When the Horde managed to reach Lordaeron and began the siege of Capital City, the paladins took part in the defense of the realm under the command of King Terenas Menethil II. (ref: [The Fall of Lordaeron (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fall_of_Lordaeron_(WC2_Orc))) After King [Aiden Perenolde](Aiden Perenolde)'s betrayal was revealed by [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar) and the [Kingdom of Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)) was found guilty of its pact with the Horde, some paladins joined the Alliance troops to save those unjustly held by Perenolde and enlist their aid to launch an attack on Alterac's capital. (ref: [Betrayal and the Destruction of Alterac (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Betrayal_and_the_Destruction_of_Alterac_(WC2_Human)))

### Aftermath of the Second War
As the fires of the Second War died down, the Alliance took aggressive steps to contain the orcish threat. A number of large internment camps, meant to house the captive orcs, were constructed in southern Lordaeron. Guarded by both the paladins and the veteran soldiers of the Alliance, the camps proved to be a great success. (ref: [The Invasion of Draenor](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Invasion_of_Draenor))

The Silver Hand had dedicated themselves to safeguarding the land from orc attacks by directing a few settlements across Lordaeron. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, chapter 4) During these times, the Bastion in Stratholme was considered as the barracks of the organization. The order also began to train new paladins in Stormwind City and Ironforge, swelling its ranks with a number of stout-hearted dwarves (ref: WoW Character Selection Screen: "Although paladins were once exclusively human, a number of stout-hearted dwarves have recently been welcomed into the Order of the Silver Hand.") and high elves. (ref: [quest: Elegant Letter|race=HighElf|class=paladin]) The [Monastery](Scarlet Monastery), a center for learning and enlightenment known as a proud bastion of Lordaeron's priesthood, (ref: [Game Guide/World Dungeons#The Scarlet Monastery](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Guide/World_Dungeons#The_Scarlet_Monastery)) was notably used as a seminary and major training ground for paladins in training. (ref: [quest: Mythology of the Titans])

During the [Alterac crisis](Alterac crisis), Lord [Duncan Senturus](Duncan Senturus) and his paladins of the Silver Hand encountered [Rhonin](Rhonin) and [Vereesa Windrunner](Vereesa Windrunner), sent by the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) to save the Dragonqueen [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza) in [Grim Batol](Grim Batol), and took them to an unnamed settlement of the order about three days from the port of [Hasic](Hasic). Despite their reluctance to cooperate with the mage, they agreed to help them in their quest, escorting them to Hasic which they found in ruins after an attack by a pair of dragon riders of the [Dragonmaw clan](Dragonmaw clan). They subsequently allied with [Falstad Dragonreaver](Falstad Wildhammer) and his wing of gryphon riders in order to reach Khaz Modan, and while en route, Senturus sacrificed himself to save Rhonin and his companions when they were ambushed by Dragonmaw dragonriders. (ref: *Day of the Dragon*, chapter 8)

When Elder Shaman [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul) reopened the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal), paladins defended the [Citadel of Nethergarde](Nethergarde Keep) when the [Horde of Draenor](Horde of Draenor) laid siege to it. (ref: [The Rift Awakened (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Rift_Awakened_(WC2_Orc))) (ref: [The Battle for Nethergarde (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Battle_for_Nethergarde_(WC2_Human))) They confronted the Horde forces when they attacked New Stormwind in search of the Book of Medivh, (ref: [New Stormwind (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/New_Stormwind_(WC2_Orc))) and defended Kul Tiras during the Shadowmoon clan's assaults on the high seas. (ref: [The Seas of Azeroth (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Seas_of_Azeroth_(WC2_Orc))) (ref: [Assault_on_Kul_Tiras_(WC2_Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Assault_on_Kul_Tiras_(WC2_Orc))) However, traitorous knights of Alterac aided the Shadowmoon in destroying Lordaeron and Stromgarde's garrisons along its borders in exchange for the Book of Medivh. (ref: [Alterac (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Alterac_(WC2_Orc)))

Led by General Turalyon, a contingent of knights of the Silver Hand joined the [Alliance Expedition](Alliance Expedition) that ventured beyond the Dark Portal into the orcs' homeworld of [Draenor](Draenor) as a response to the renewed orcish aggression, battling the Horde to stop Ner'zhul's plans to open portals to other worlds. Though they were unable to stop him, they were successful in destroying the portal and saving Azeroth from being harmed by Draenor's violent destruction. (ref: [Once More Unto the Breach (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Once_More_Unto_the_Breach_(WC2_Human))) (ref: [Beyond the Dark Portal (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Beyond_the_Dark_Portal_(WC2_Human))) (ref: [The Bitter Taste of Victory (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Bitter_Taste_of_Victory_(WC2_Human)))

Years passed after the invasion of Draenor as tensions abated and a lasting peace settled over Lordaeron. With his paladins, the Supreme commander Uther the Lightbringer kept the peace in the kingdom, settling civil disputes and quelling demi-human uprisings throughout the realm, while his apprentice [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil) integrated the order at the tender age of nineteen. (ref: 

When Warchief [Thrall](Thrall) began the liberation of the orc clans from the internment camps, King Terenas sent Uther and his paladins to quell the warchief's uprising while revolt against the orc internment camps was about to unfold. However, the crafty orcs could never be found as the young warchief proved to be something of a tactical genius – and evaded Uther's best efforts to corral his hit-and-run attacks.<ref>*Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* Game Manual, Human History, [The Shadows Return](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#The_Shadows_Return)) At the same time, the Silver Hand defended the settlements at the borders of Quel'Thalas from forest troll warbands, where Prince Arthas gained fame for counterattacking and defeating the trolls. (ref: 

At some point, Tirion Fordring's life was saved by the orc Eitrigg. When Eitrigg was captured by Lord Commander [Saidan Dathrohan](Saidan Dathrohan)'s soldiers, Tirion felt he owed a debt of honor and fought to rescue him. For assaulting Alliance soldiers, Tirion was brought to Stratholme to stand trial before the jury composed of Admiral Daelin Proudmoore, Arch-Mage Antonidas, Archbishop Alonsus Faol, and Prince Arthas Menethil. Despite the offer to be freed in exchange for breaking his vow, Tirion refused and Uther had no choice but to excommunicate his old friend from Lordaeron.<ref>*[Of Blood and Honor](Of Blood and Honor)*, chapter 5) The gnome Myranda the Hag, most trusted advisor of Tirion, was later banished after she openly dissented the verdict of the Order of the Silver Hand. (ref: [quest: Find Myranda])

Prior to the Third War, King Terenas was forced to take decisive measures in the face of recent orc uprisings. He ordered Uther with the Knights of the Silver Hand and his son to ride towards southern Lordaeron in order to contain the orcish threat and deal with them once and for all. (ref: [The Defense of Strahnbrad (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Defense_of_Strahnbrad_(WC3_Human)))

### The Scourging of Lordaeron
   *"Lord Uther, by my right of succession and the sovereignty of my crown, I hereby relieve you of your command and suspend your paladins from service."*
   — Prince [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil) (ref: [The Culling (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Culling_(WC3_Human)))

At the beginning of the Third War, Uther the Lightbringer and his paladins investigated the infected regions of Lordaeron to find a way to stop the Plague of Undeath, but despite their efforts, the plague continued to spread and threatened to tear the [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron) apart. While the undead swept across Lordaeron, Prince Arthas Menethil managed to track the shipment of infected grain to Andorhal, where he killed the Cult of the Damned's leader Kel'Thuzad. Frustrated and stymied by the seemingly unstoppable enemy, Arthas took increasingly extreme steps to fight against the Scourge, (ref: [The Scourge of Lordaeron](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Scourge_of_Lordaeron_(History_of_Warcraft))) notably at [Hearthglen](Hearthglen), where he was able to slay those infected before they could spread the disease to the entire town. Shortly after, the undead army laid siege to the town, its defenders barely holding back the undead long enough for the arrival of Uther and his paladins who helped to repel the undead. (ref: [March of the Scourge (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/March_of_the_Scourge_(WC3_Human)))

Horrified, humiliated, and disturbed by his near-defeat, Arthas quickly left for Stratholme, followed by Uther and his paladins, where they found that the city had been infected with the plague by Mal'Ganis, as the necromancer had told him. When Uther refused to follow Arthas' command to kill the infected population of Stratholme before they could die and be reborn as undead, Arthas turned his back on the other paladins, calling their refusal an act of treason, then relieved Uther from command, suspended his paladins from service and sent them away, while he called all those still loyal to Lordaeron to follow their prince. (ref:  After the culling of Stratholme, some former paladins, now called deathlords, also committed the same heinous act in many other cities across Lordaeron.<ref>[Crown of the Deathlord](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Crown_of_the_Deathlord)) (ref: [Shield of the Deathlord](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Shield_of_the_Deathlord))

Following the purge, Prince Arthas took the fleet to Northrend, while the Silver Hand continued to defend Lordaeron villages. Though disenfranchised, the paladins still worked selflessly to protect humanity from the gnawing jaws of evil. (ref:  In the meantime, Arthas tracked the plague's source to Northrend, intending to end its threat forever, only for him to fall prey to the machinations of the Lich King and lost his soul to the cursed runeblade Frostmourne. After he became a death knight, Arthas returned to Lordaeron with his armies of undead to kill his father and destroy Capital City. After the death of Terenas, Uther ordered [Gavinrad the Dire](Gavinrad the Dire) and his [Champions of Peace](Champions of Peace) to guard the cemetery near Andorhal, where the remains of Kel'Thuzad were buried. Despite the horrifying conditions, Andorhal was one of the few places in Lordaeron still under Alliance control. There, Uther and many of the surviving paladins had gathered in the city. When Arthas arrived, Gavinrad refused to give the remains of Kel'Thuzad and was personally killed by the death knight, who then unleashed his undead upon the paladins Ballador the Bright and Sage Truthbearer, commanders of the Champions of Light and the Champions of Truth. In the city of Andorhal, Uther stood against his former apprentice to protect the enchanted Urn of King Terenas, though he failed, falling to Arthas before the scourging of the kingdom continued.<ref>[Digging up the Dead (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Digging_up_the_Dead_(WC3_Undead)))

During the Battle of Darrowshire, a contingent of paladins of the Silver Hand led by [Davil Lightfire](Davil Crokford) allied themselves with the defenders of Darrowshire led by Captain [Joseph Redpath](Joseph Redpath), having learned of the Scourge's impending attack on the town. But despite the paladins' successful efforts in steadying the line and holding back the undead, they ended up being defeated by the death knight [Marduk the Black](Marduk Blackpool) after Davil Lightfire managed to slay the ghoul lord [Horgus the Ravager](Horgus the Ravager), before perishing from his mortal wounds. (ref: [Annals of Darrowshire](Annals of Darrowshire)) (ref: [Extended Annals of Darrowshire](Extended Annals of Darrowshire))

Subsequently, many paladins traveled to quarantined lands to ease the suffering of those left within the plague-ridden colonies, but although they were immune to disease of any kind, they were persecuted by the general populace who believed that they had been infected by the foul plague. Some of these paladins traveled to Northrend. When they finally reached Ner'zhul's icy fortress, embittered by the trials they had to suffer through, they had become dark and brooding. The Lich King offered them untold power in exchange for their services and loyalty. Those fallen paladins, whose souls were twisted and bound to the will of the Frozen Throne, became death knights, the Scourge's mightiest generals. (ref: [Warcraft III - Undead -> Units -> Death Knight](https://classic.battle.net/war3/undead/units/deathknight.shtml))

At the onset of the destruction of Dalaran, some of the paladins joined the [Human Expedition](Human Expedition) to oppose the Burning Legion, notably Duke Lionheart who hunted down the demons left after the death of Mannoroth. (ref: [Enemies at the Gate (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Enemies_at_the_Gate_(WC3_NightElf)))

### Aftermath of the Third War
After Uther's death, [Taelan Fordring](Taelan Fordring) threw down the standard of the Order and renounced all that he had known after he made his final stand in the war torn hamlet of [Northdale](Northdale), believing that there were no others left alive of the Order and that it didn't matter if there were. (ref: [quest: Of Lost Honor])

Several months after the end of the Third War, the surviving paladins joined the last bastions of human resistance. However, some of them were captured by Illidan Stormrage and his naga, and were brought to the ruins of Dalaran. Among them was Magroth the Defender, who was later freed by Malfurion Stormrage and Maiev Shadowsong, and subsequently pledged himself to their cause. (ref: [The Ruins of Dalaran (WC3 NightElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ruins_of_Dalaran_(WC3_NightElf)))

Afterward, detachments of paladins emerged again from the rubble of Lordaeron to form a resistance, protecting those who fled to Dalaran under the control of Grand Marshal Garithos and the Alliance resistance. Some joined the Garithos' Elite Guard, notably one who led a few surviving royal guards. (ref: [The Crossing (WC3 BloodElf)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Crossing_(WC3_BloodElf))) After Archimonde's defeat, the others guarded the ruined Lordaeron palace gardens under the command of Dagren the Orcslayer, Magroth the Defender, and Halahk the Lifebringer, respectively directing the Crippling Force, the Defenders of Justice and the Circle of Holy Light. However, their combined efforts were not enough as they were defeated by Arthas Menethil, Kel'Thuzad, and Sylvanas Windrunner, allowing the Scourge to destroy the resistance. (ref: [King Arthas (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/King_Arthas_(WC3_Undead)))

Among the survivors, some were rallied by Dagren the Orcslayer to join the ranks of the Human Settlers. Under the orders of Lord Admiral Daelin Proudmoore, they traveled to Kalimdor to fight the Horde and locate Jaina Proudmoore. (ref: [Old Hatreds (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Old_Hatreds_(WC3_Orc))) During the invasion of Durotar, Dagren, the Lord Admiral's Chief Petty Officer, and the other paladins were assigned to the protection of Theramore Isle. (ref: [A Blaze of Glory (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_Blaze_of_Glory_(WC3_Orc)))

At some point after the dreadlord [Balnazzar](Balnazzar)'s defeat at the hands of [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner) in the Plaguelands civil war, (ref: [A New Power in Lordaeron (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/A_New_Power_in_Lordaeron_(WC3_Undead))) Lord Commander Saidan Dathrohan's identity was adopted by the dreadlord, who joined the order of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) and began to corrupt it from within. (ref: [Corrupted Ashbringer/Scarlet Monastery Event](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Corrupted_Ashbringer/Scarlet_Monastery_Event))

### World of Warcraft
   *"The death of my son at the hands of these monsters will not pass without incident. Take solace in knowing that the Order is reborn. I now take my place as Highlord of the new Order of the Silver Hand."*
   — Lord [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring) (ref: 

Following the Third War, the Cathedral of Light became the main spiritual hub for the Church of Light and the paladins in the kingdoms of Stormwind and Ironforge, with [Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker](Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker) and [Valgar Highforge](Valgar Highforge) at their head. Upholders of the Holy Light and defenders of the Alliance, these paladins were present from the northern forests of the Tirisfal Glades, fighting back the advance of the Forsaken, to the southern reaches of the Blasted Lands, ceaselessly upholding their vigil against demonic forces from beyond the Dark Portal.<ref>[The World of Warcraft Townhall/Classes#The Paladin](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_World_of_Warcraft_Townhall/Classes#The_Paladin))

After his death, Uther was buried in a great tomb built for him to the south of the ruins of Andorhal, at Sorrow Hill, vigilantly guarded by High Priest Thel'danis, the caretaker of the tomb.

At the [Northshire Abbey](Northshire Abbey), [Marshal McBride](Marshal McBride) marshaled human paladins and adventurers to get rid of the kobolds that infested the Echo Ridge Mine. In Dun Morogh, some dwarf paladins also helped the inhabitants of the [Coldridge Valley](Coldridge Valley). They are later summoned by [Duthorian Rall](Duthorian Rall) and [Tiza Battleforge](Tiza Battleforge), two of the most influential and respected paladins of the Alliance, to continue their training and studies, but also to perform holy trials to show themselves worthy of serving the Light. (ref: [quest: Tome of Divinity|race=human|class=paladin]) (ref: [quest: Tome of Divinity|race=dwarf|class=paladin]) They were then sent on missions to fight the Defias Brotherhood and the Dark Iron clan, (ref: [quest: The Tome of Valor|race=human|class=paladin]) (ref: [quest: The Tome of Divinity|race=dwarf|class=paladin]) but also against the Scourge in the Western Plaguelands. (ref: [quest: Chillwind Camp|class=paladin])

Everyone in the Silver Hand thought that Merideth Carlson was mad for coming up with the idea of a special horse feed destined to nourish spiritual horses. (ref: [quest: Manna-Enriched Horse Feed|class=paladin]) Relationships with the horse breeder were eventually mended, in order to allow the young paladins to be able to obtain their own charger. (ref: [quest: Lord Grayson Shadowbreaker|class=paladin])

When Taelan Fordring was killed by [Grand Inquisitor Isillien](Grand Inquisitor Isillien), his father Tirion avenged him and vowed over his son's body to reform the Silver Hand once more as a force of good dedicated to extinguishing the evil, regardless of its politics or pleasantries, that plagued the world. (ref:  The order was mentioned to be active during the initial assault on Naxxramas. When the tone rose during a meeting between Argent Dawn and the Scarlet Crusade at Light's Hope Chapel, Eligor Dawnbringer threatened Scarlet Commander Marjhan to inform the Silver Hand that they were no longer here on amicable terms, and that Lord Fordring would take great pleasure in seeing to their "atonement".

In the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands), [Ballador's Chapel](Ballador's Chapel) was established and named after the paladin Ballador the Bright, where [Andalideth Suncaller](Andalideth Suncaller) heads the high elven paladins of the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant). The chapel further serves as a haven for those who seek solace in the Light during challenging times.<ref>[quest: A Feast for the Poor])
