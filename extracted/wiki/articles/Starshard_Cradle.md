# Starshard Cradle

**Starshard Cradle** is the site of an arcane meteor that crashed into [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast) that was called down by the [Blue dragonflight](Blue dragonflight). (ref: [Moriena from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/895751435797536788/1496783160149540866) - "its a meteor called down by the blue dragons") After the meteor fell, a brood of the azure flight led by the drake [Starcaller](Starcaller) began to occupy the impact site, driving away anything that comes too close - adventurers, scholars and even the naga have learned to give it a wide berth. (ref: [quest: A Star That Calls Back]) When the meteor fell from the skies, part of the nearby [Servitor's Sanctuary](Servitor's Sanctuary) was also caught in the blast, (ref: [Writings of the Elder](Writings of the Elder)) but the [Fallen Ones](Fallen Ones) yet managed to recover meteor shards for their own. (ref: [quest: Heaven Falling Down])
