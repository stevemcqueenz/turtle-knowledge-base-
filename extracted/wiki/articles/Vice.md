# Vice

The **Vice** is home to the [Deadwind ogres](Deadwind ogres). There are several Deadwind ogre [mounds](Ogre mound) and camps in the area, scattered along the road to [Karazhan](Karazhan) in the southern area of [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass). Nobody knows why the ogres have chosen to settle in this desolate area with nothing but restless spirits and a few [vulture](vulture)s for company.

It should also be noted that [Formula: Enchant Bracer - Superior Strength](Formula: Enchant Bracer - Superior Strength) drops off the [Deadwind Warlock](Deadwind Warlock)s of the area at a near 0.5% chance.
