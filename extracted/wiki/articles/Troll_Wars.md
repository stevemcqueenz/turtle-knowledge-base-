# Troll Wars

The **Troll Wars** were a series of conflicts between the [forest troll](forest troll)s of [Zul'Aman](Zul'Aman) and an alliance between the [human](human)s of [Arathor](Arathor) and [high elves](high elf) of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas), occurring approximately 2,800 years before the coming of the [orc](orc)s to [Azeroth](Azeroth).

The war started because the [High Elves](High Elves) founded the kingdom of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas) over the trolls' sacred ground. There once lay an ancient troll city that was in ruins because of unknown reason.  Elven magic helped to frighten away the superstitious troll warbands and 4,000 years passed before the trolls started the real war.

The human tribes, also suffering at the hands of the trolls, finally banded together to form the first human nation of [Arathor](Arathor). After countless years of being continually besieged by the native trolls, the high elves of eventually sought the aid of the humans of Arathor. The two nations formed an alliance, in which the elves taught the humans the art of magic in exchange for military aid. 

The now strengthened alliance of high elves and humans eventually defeated the forest trolls so completely that the race would never fully recover in the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms).
