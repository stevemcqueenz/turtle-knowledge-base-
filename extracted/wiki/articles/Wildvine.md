# Wildvine

**Wildvine** is an [herb](herb) that can be gathered with [Herbalism](Herbalism) (210). Wildvine is not gathered independently, but rather has a roughly 10% chance to drop while gathering [Purple Lotus](Purple Lotus). It can also be found dropping from the [Skullsplitter](Skullsplitter tribe) trolls in [Stranglethorn Vale](Stranglethorn Vale) or the [Witherbark](Witherbark tribe) and [Vilebranch](Vilebranch) trolls in [The Hinterlands](The Hinterlands).
