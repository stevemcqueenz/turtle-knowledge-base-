-Alah'Thalas** (meaning **Land of Light** in [Thalassian](Thalassian)) (ref: [quest: Assisting the Children of the Sun] *(Deprecated)*), sometimes spelled **Alah'thalas**, is a [high elven](High elf) city situated on a peninsula at the tip of the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands), near Quel'Thalas. It is the largest of the three major concentrations of high elves in [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) and is one of the very last high elven settlements.

## History
Roughly a century before the Third War, high elves of Quel'Thalas founded an outpost and empowered it by an arcane crystal infused with the energies of the Sunwell itself. However, some time later it was abandoned and remained uninhabited until recent time. The outpost was rediscovered after the Third War by the Silvermoon Remnants with information from Dalaran's archives and resettled by Thalassian refugees, now looking for the help of volunteers from the Alliance in their mission to maintain the city and protect the remnants of their once great race.

### Mysteries of Azeroth
Two years after being discovered, the former outpost now stands as an imposing city fueled by the arcane magic that characterizes the High Elves, with the Regency Council as the new government in the absence of a known living member of House Sunstrider after deposing Vereesa Windrunner as its spokesperson.

## Architecture
Unlike Farstrider Lodges across Azeroth that have a kaldorei-like wooden architecture, Alah'Thalas is the only place outside Quel'Thalas that was built with traditional quel'dorei architecture. City structures use arcane magic to operate - with a central crystal as the main power source and smaller crystals to effectively channel the arcane energy from the nexus to the buildings' ley points.

## Points of interest
- **Golden Dawn Institute**: Located at the entrance to the city, the Golden Dawn Institute is a floating building for refugees and travellers from afar.
- **Citadel of the Sun**: Lying over the shores of the highlands is the Citadel of the Sun, formerly the beating heart of Alah'Thalas. Now home to ambassadors of the Alliance, it formerly housed the new Magistry, the headquarters of the Rangers, and the seat of the Speaker.
- **The Bazaar:** Place of Alah'thalas where most commercial buildings are located.
- **Wretched Ghetto:** A place where the "Withered" are contained, they are High Elves who unfortunately gave in to their addiction to magic and are degenerating into monsters.
- **Sunsworn Mansion:** Sunsworn Manor is the mansion in which the nobles of House Sunsworn reside, whose leader wishes to claim the crown as King of Quel'thalas in the absence of Kael'thas Sunstrider.
- **Windrunner Pier:** The Port that connects the City of Alah'thalas with Darkshore is named in honor of the Windrunner Sisters, whose Ranger General, Sylvanas Windrunner, gave her life defending Quel'thalas from the scourge invasion.
- **Regency Spire:** The center of government of the [Silvermoon Remnant](Silvermoon Remnant) in Alah'Thalas, where the [Regency Council](Regency Council) meets and deliberates.
