**Nozdormu the Timeless One** is one of the five [Dragon Aspects](Dragon Aspects) and the leader of the [bronze dragonflight](bronze dragonflight).

## Biography
### The Charge of the Dragonflights
Once the Titans felt satisfied with their creation upon Azeroth, they left to roam the cosmos in search of new worlds on which they would shape order from chaos, creating new life throughout a myriad of worlds. Before their departure from Azeroth however, the Titans empowered five dragons to become stewards of that world. Their purpose: to guard Azeroth as the Dragon Aspects, protecting the Titans' creation.

[Aman'Thul](Aman'Thul), the Highfather of the [Pantheon](Pantheon), bestowed a portion of his cosmic power upon the massive bronze dragon, Nozdormu. The Highfather empowered Nozdormu to guard time itself and police the ever-spinning pathways of fate and destiny. The stoic, honorable Nozdormu became known as the Timeless One.<ref>[Charge of the Dragonflights](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Charge_of_the_Dragonflights))

### The War of the Ancients
Ten thousand years ago, Nozdormu was missing during the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients), as he was trapped in a time anomaly caused by the [Old Gods](Old Gods). During the conflict against the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion), Nozdormu's interests were represented by his consort, [Saridormi](Saridormi), who added a portion of Nozdormu's essence into the [Dragon Soul](Dragon Soul) in his absence, thus imbuing it with his power.

Following the Great Sundering, after he was freed by [Korialstrasz](Korialstrasz) and his allies, Nozdormu, [Alexstrasza](Alexstrasza), and [Ysera](Ysera) recovered the Dragon Soul and were troubled to find that they could not destroy the weapon, neither reclaim the power they had infused into. Instead, they hid it in a remote corner of the world, while enchantments were also placed upon the artifact to prevent [Deathwing](Deathwing) or any other dragon from ever touching it. The trio later visited the [night elven](Night elf) survivors to present the devastated race a gift. The Dragon Queen planted a colossal tree that scraped the heavens, named [Nordrassil](Nordrassil), within the new Well of Eternity. Nozdormu's blessing gave the night elves the gift of immortality, and they would never age or fall prey to sickness or disease.

### The Day of the Dragon
At the behest of [Krasus](Krasus), [Ysera](Ysera), [Malygos](Malygos), and Nozdormu grudgingly agreed to come out of their lairs and fight [Deathwing](Deathwing). Due to the power they had given to the Demon Soul, they were no match for the Destroyer - until the mage [Rhonin](Rhonin) was able to take the Demon Soul from [Nekros Skullcrusher](Nekros Skullcrusher) and destroy it, releasing the powers the dragons had given to it ten thousand years earlier. With their powers restored, and the newly-freed Alexstrasza joining the fight, Deathwing was forced to retreat.

### After the Third War
Shortly after the end of the [Third War](Third War), the [human](human) mage [Rhonin](Rhonin) was called away, once again, by his friend and mentor, the dragon-mage [Krasus](Krasus) of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor). Nozdormu, trapped between different times, had barely managed to contact Krasus and draw his attention to a strange anomaly in the mountains of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). Krasus convinced Rhonin to travel with him to Kalimdor to investigate. Meanwhile, the orcs [Broxigar](Broxigar) and [Gaskal](Gaskal) were sent by their Warchief [Thrall](Thrall) on a similar mission, having caught wind of the strange event taking place nearby. Once there, Krasus, Rhonin, and Brox discovered a 'hole' in time. They were pulled in and ended up 10,000 years in the past, just before the first invasion of the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion). After the war ended and the heroes returned, he had a brief conversation with Krasus, where he thanked him for his assistance and agreed to temporarily watch over the clutch of blue dragon eggs the dragon-mage had saved. This marked one of the few times the Aspect had allowed the timeline to be altered, in the belief that a few blue dragons would be better than no blue dragons and then apologized for the wrongs he would commit in the future
