-Anasterian Sunstrider** was the last [high elven](High elf) king of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas (kingdom)). During the [Second War](Second War), he reluctantly joined the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron), duty-bound to fulfill a pledge of loyalty and friendship to the Arathi bloodline, of whom Sir [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar) was the last. Anasterian was forced to contribute more of Quel'Thalas' armies when the outer forests were attacked by the [Horde](Old Horde). (ref: [The Alliance of Lordaeron](The Alliance of Lordaeron)) Following the war, he withdrew his nation from the Alliance, stating that the humans' poor leadership had led to the burning of the elves' forests during the Second War. (ref: [The Alliance Splinters](The Alliance Splinters))

Still reigning over Quel'Thalas at the time of its fall, Anasterian died along with the [Convocation of Silvermoon](Convocation of Silvermoon).
