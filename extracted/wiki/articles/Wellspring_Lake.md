# Wellspring Lake

**Wellspring Lake** is a small lake northeast of [Darnassus](Darnassus) and southeast of [The Oracle Glade](The Oracle Glade) in [Teldrassil](Teldrassil). Fed by a natural spring deep within the land of the World Tree, this small lake is drained by the [Wellspring River](Wellspring River).  Previously a favored spot for [night elven](night elven) [fishermen](fishing), the lake is now the home of corrupted [timberling](timberling)s. The surrounding area is also a dangerous place, as [nightsaber](nightsaber)s and [spider](spider)s infest the landscape.
