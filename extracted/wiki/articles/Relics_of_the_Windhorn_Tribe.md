## Objectives
Travel into the Windhorn Canyon and recover 8 Windhorn Relics for Sagh at Sagh's Refuge in Thousand Needles.
- [Windhorn Relic](Windhorn Relic) (8)

## Description
I spent the majority of my life with the Windhorn Tribe, and came to respect and value their traditions. With the pillaging of Windhorn Canyon by the Grimtotem, I fear that the history and culture of the Windhorn may fall into obscurity.

When I fled Windhorn Canyon during the raid the only thing that was on my mind was survival. Now I regret not bringing any of the precious relics that dwell in the city into safety. It will only be a matter of time until they are all destroyed, or lost to time.

I ask of you to brave the Windhorn Canyon. Recover any antiques that you may find inside, return them to me and you shall be rewarded.

## Rewards
You will receive:
- [Sagh's Pendant](Sagh's Pendant)
- 2350 XP

## Progress
Tread carefully, the Grimtotem have fortified Windhorn Canyon and claimed it as their own. The large, steep cliffs provide an excellent redoubt for defence. I advise you to bring allies if you wish to help.

## Completion
I spent the entirety of my youth surrounded by such relics. The craftsmanship of the Windhorn is certainly superb.

Without your help, this history would have been lost to time. I thank you, for everything that you have done this day.
