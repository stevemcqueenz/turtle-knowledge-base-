# Steamwheedle Cartel

The Steamwheedle Cartel, founded by [Sam Steamwheedle](Sam Steamwheedle), is the largest and most successful of the [Goblin](Goblin) Cartels in [Undermine](Undermine).  They control bases around the world and have allied themselves with a number of other groups such as the Black Sea Raiders in [Booty Bay](Booty Bay).  The Steamwheedle Cartel has made a great deal of profit from maintaining neutrality in the conflict between the Horde and the Alliance.  Their services are always available to the highest bidder, and they employ anyone regardless of their affiliation if they can produce results.  
They are in direct competition with the [Venture Company](Venture Company), another Cartel, and neither side is above chicanery, sabotage or murder to gain the upper hand in the cut throat world of Goblin economics.

A [Neutral](Neutral) [Faction](Faction) that controls several [cities](City):
- [Booty Bay](Booty Bay)
- [Everlook](Everlook)
- [Gadgetzan](Gadgetzan)
- [Ratchet](Ratchet)
- [Steamwheedle Port](Steamwheedle Port)
