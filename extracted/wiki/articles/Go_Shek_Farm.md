# Go'Shek Farm

A farmstead in south-eastern [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), near [Witherbark Village](Witherbark Village).

## Hints of a New Plague
This once-human farmstead was occupied by Orcs and Forsaken from [Hammerfall](Hammerfall) some time after Third War. Since then, the Forsaken have been using this village as test ground for their mysterious alchemical experiments.

Two agents of Alliance, [Quae](Quae) and [Kinelory](Kinelory), were sent to investigate this issue and with help of some adventurers have first stopped a [Forsaken](Forsaken) courier travelling to [Tarren Mill](Tarren Mill), and then discovered within the village notes and research of [Apothecary Jorell](Apothecary Jorell). It seems the apothecary was working on some new kind of Plague.
