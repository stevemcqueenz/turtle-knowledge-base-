# Shaman guide

-This guide was originally posted in the Turtle WoW forum by "Skegg" in September and October 2025. This wiki page is to ensure it remains accessible.*

I wrote this out as my personal levelling plan after compiling a lot of different tips from discussions in the #shaman channel on the Turtle WoW Discord. Since I noticed a lot of people asking for one, I'll share this in case it helps anyone save time by not having to research the same stuff. I'll be editing this if I come across any more relevant info, and I'd be grateful for any corrections or additions that anyone is willing to make.

Big thanks to the following contributors who provided most of the knowledge compiled here: Squid, Zhafu, Rinzu, Peter Hammer, DemonBoyJr, Monolith, Akos, Not Normal, poto, Haaxor1689, Zaaparou and more (sorry if I forgot any).

## Race
All 3 races are pretty much equally well-performing here, so go with the one you find the most fun or aesthetically pleasing. That said,

- Troll is the best min-max race for PvE (quasi-Bloodlust haste racial for PvE, and hex is decent for PvP)

- Tauren is the best for PvP and the most tanky for leveling (+5% hp, nature resist) - Warstomp is the best PvP racial + Plainsrunning is the biggest QoL racial in the game (saves us 2 talents on imp Ghost Wolf as well)

- Orc is still a decent choice for Enhance PvE (axe skill helps + Blood Fury)

## Gear & Stat Prios
- Stats for tanking while leveling: +stam > +agi > +str > +int (when tanking at 60, +str becomes 2nd most important and +agi third)

- For healing: +int = +hp

- For DPS: +str = +agi > +stam > +int (+str & +agi roughly on par, at 60 +str is more valuable than +agi)

- Stat prios thread: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=21159

- For calculating AP: shamans get 0 AP per agi, 2 AP from str and 1% dodge and 1% crit per 20 agi

- A shield with high armor is a always a top prio, eg. Compendium of the Fallen quest reward -> SM Cath shield -> Gilneas shield / Mara goblin shield / SWV shield. When in doubt between two, choose the higher armor one (it scales with tanking talents that boost armor on shields by 30%, and all items by 15%). You can run an EHP calc just checking your % damage reduction on A shield vs B shield listed on your character sheet: EHP = HP/(1-%DR), then see which one gives you the most EHP. As a quick shortcut, in most cases, 10 armor is roughly equal to 1 stamina

## Talents & Rotation
With this build, you can fill any role in dungeons: tank, DPS or heal. We'll be picking tanking talents, but even if you solely want to DPS, this is arguably still the best build because you do practically the same DPS (the difference is really negligible) + have the benefit of being more tanky. That is, unless you're levelling in a group and don't have to care about mitigation. If you want to skip the tank talents, you could swap out Shield Spec. for Ancestral Knowledge. For healing, all you'll need is some +int gear and you're good to go for dungeons.

### To level 33
- 24p in enh: 5/5 Shield Spec > 2/2 Totemic Alignment > 3/3 Stable Shields > 3/3 Ancestral Guardian (before LS for mitigation) > Lightning Strike (take before AG if threat is a problem) > 1/5 Thundering Strikes > 2/2 Spirit Armor > 4/5 Thundering Strikes > 3/3 Elemental Weapons (first if feeling squishy) > Stormstrike (first if want more threat): (https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=-AoUYALAQDI-)

- Rotation: Rockbiter + Lightning Shield, using Lightning Strike -> Storm Strike -> Earth Shock -> Lightning Strike. You spend your mana at the beginning, then melee the mob down because you want to start the mana regen ASAP. Using totems isn't recommended, though Searing is probably fine if you're sticking in the same location long enough to get value

- Weapon: until Lightning Strike, a high dps dagger is just as good as a slow 2H; after Lightning Strike, a slow 2H is best

- From around level 25, focus on getting a good shield with high armor. At level 28, you can get the Compendium of the Fallen quest from Sage Truthseeker in Thunder Bluff (34.7, 47.8) and do SM: Library for the shield quest reward

- At level 33, get the Jewelcrafting +stam trinkets

### Level 34-44
- 11p in ele: 5/5 Convection > 2/5 Concussion (since few mobs do elemental dmg) OR 2/3 Elemental Warding (for mitigation in Maraudon) > 3/3 Elemental Devastation > Elemental Focus (Clearcasting!): (https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=FQAYI-AoUYALAQDI-) OR  (https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=FACYI-AoUYALAQDI-)

- Between level 35-41, try to get the Totem of Tides from the orc boss in Stormwrought - it's phenomenal for tanking dungeons + farming Lashers

- As a Tauren, at level 40, get Plainsrunning: head to the 2nd village you visit in Mulgore when you make a Tauren. The mount trainer there provides you a quest that leads to STV

### Level 44-50
- 6p in enh: 5/5 Thundering Strikes > 5/5 Element's Grace (raises threat w/o necessarily spending mana):  (https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=FQAYI-AoVYALAQDIAo-) OR (https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=FACYI-AoVYALAQDIAo-)

- Rotation: after getting Elemental Focus at level 44, Frostbrand + Water Shield, using Frost Shock -> Stormstrike -> Lightning Strike

- Optional: between 44-48, farm Earth Elementals in Badlands + occasional Uldaman runs to get the Goblin brainwashing machine for dual speccing (if the spot isn't camped too much, it might be possible to get the Goblin brainwashing machine for dual speccing, though this might be an unrealistic goal)

- Around level 50, it's pretty easy and worth it to get this axe: https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=51892 

- For the level 50 Spirit Totem quest, the trick here is that the Vultures have a higher chance of dropping the Spider Eye and the Black Claw than the spiders and the bears themselves

### Level 51-60
- Respec to 5/5 Ancestral Knowledge: (https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=FQAYI-FAVYALAQDIAo-) OR  (https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=FACYI-FAVYALAQDIAo-)

- 6p in enh: Bloodlust; 3p in ele: 3/3 Reverberation (take this before Flurry for more QoL in managing threat + more frequent spell interrupt); 5p in enh: 5/5 Flurry; 1p in ele: 3/5 Concussion: (https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=FYAYL-FAVYALFQDIAoB-) OR 3/3 Elemental Warding over 3/5 Concussion (Warding = a bit more tanky, but elemental damage is limited; Concussion = more threat):  (https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=FADYL-FAVYALFQDIAoB-) OR for cheaper totems: (https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=FACYL-FAVYALBQDIAoB-Ao)

- Get WS: (https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16790) / [Weapon_Skill](Weapon_Skill)

## Lasher Farming
Here's a video on it by Zaaparou: https://www.youtube.com/embed/o90HvELvCpA Make sure to have a 2nd account with a level 1 -> invite it to the group, make it party lead; once you've finished a run, log out on your shaman, switch over to the 2nd account and reset the dungeon (click on the torch and it'll port you to DM: West entrance). Rotate Nova > Magma > Nova on each pack, heal up & drink between packs; during the pack, you'll have to swap to Earth Shield for 100% pushback protection + do 1-2 Healing Waves on yourself, then switch back to Water Shield. Basically, you do 1 pack at a time. Have Water Shield on, aggro the pack, drop Stoneskin, agility, Healing Stream. When the pack gets close, drop Fire Nova. Then use Magma. Usually, you'll dip to around 50% health at this time, so you can use Earth Shield + cast 1-2 Healing Waves. Switch back to Water Shield, Fire Nova when it's off cooldown, drop Magma again. Usually, the pack will be dead at this time, but sometimes a 3rd Fire Nova totem is needed. You should finish each lasher pack at like 60% health and 40% mana. Talent-wise, you'll need this specific spec with all the talents that increase totem damage (Elemental Weapons in enhance - Flametongue - for +30% damage increase to fire totems) and the ones that transfer totem threat to you (otherwise the Lashers will kill your totems), as well as 12 points in resto for pushback protection: (https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c/1.18.0/shaman?points=FAAQ-AoUYALAQDI-FoAAC). Also make sure to have Totem of Tides from the new Stormwrought dungeon - it deals aoe frost damage every time you are hit while having water shield active. Gear-wise, bring +int/mp5 and +stam gear (e.g. the shield from ST - Eranikus; Princess Dagger - the blue one with +int/mp5; or just some green "of stamina" mace). A good potential farming trinket: Blood-etched Fetish (from RFD - Death Prophet Rakameg, 25% drop rate). After raids are out, stack some vampirism - around 10% (Drillborer Disk and the Rag Flame Trinket and totem). Having herbalism helps the gold you earn because you can potentially get Black Lotus from picking the Dream Foil in there.

## Tanking Rotation
- Goal: to save mana, never generate more threat than needed to maintain aggro - your primary job is to keep aggro, not deal damage. Try to match your healer's mana use rate to keep the pulls rolling as much as you can. It’s much safer to make sure you have the resources (mana) to save a pull that goes south than to spend everything trying to shorten the encounter. So whenever possible, skip using the rotation below and just do auto-attacks + taunt. Or downrank your shocks when you don't need to go all out. Especially avoid the AoE fire totems if possible. Rather than using Earth Shock on cooldown and being caught out with a mob running towards the healer, make sure you can react to any situation that might come up

- Imbue Rockbiter on a 1H and use a shield

- Mark your targets; the party should be focusing on your kill order to not split your threat thin. Put a skull and cross mark and only tag other mobs so the healer doesn't get aggro and keep threat on skull (if someone goes for cross before skull is dead, they can tank it)

- Always ask for Judgment of Wisdom on the skull if there's a pala in the party to help with mana 

- Totems: with >=2 DPS in the party, placing down a Windfury Totem is justified. Especially if they are higher level or well-geared. Just tell them if they rip threat, they tank that mob as a deal. With a pala, Blessing of Salvation (-30% threat generation) can also be placed on the melee DPS in exchange for Windfury. Use Mana Spring only if you're going to stand close to it for >20s

- You can use Lightning Shield for the pull for added threat, switch to Water Shield halfway through and switch back to Lightning Shield whenever Clearcasting procs. If you have Totem of Tides, you can start with Water Shield already during leveling, but Lightning Shield with Lightning Strike is still the best for ST threat

- Pull with rank 1 Lightning Bolt (big range, basically 0 mana) + Chain Lightning (to establish max initial threat). For real max threat, optionally add a Molten Blast after as it has a short cast time which you can fit in the time the mobs run to you

- Running in, you'll want to Stormstrike on target 1, tab switch to Earth Shock target 2, and Lightning Strike target 3. If a target resists nature damage, use Frost Shock instead of Earth Shock

- Tab switch between targets giving each of them an Earth Shock and melee auto attack. When in doubt, place a Stoneclaw Totem (though the threat per mana is great and allows you to passively regen and chain pull). Drop an Earthbind after Stoneclaw expires, if needed

- Use rank 1 Earth Shock to interrupt spells

- AoE threat for >3 mobs: Stoneclaw Totem, or fire totems - Fire Nova and Magma. If targets resist fire damage, Stoneclaw is your only AoE threat

- Oh shit: if a pull was bigger than anticipated or to quickly get aggro off healers: Fire Nova - huge snap threat, but mana intensive

- Mini oh shit: save your taunt to force a mob to attack you when it's attacking your healers

- When a mob gets loose or runs towards a sheeped target, use max rank Lightning Bolt for high threat or Earth Shock for big snap threat

- Healing yourself is a last resort; it's almost always better to pop Earth Shield, a Healing Potion or a Healthstone if you have one. If things get dicey, make sure to use those; never start casting while taking hits

- After a pack is almost dead, move to a new one and Earth Shock one of those mobs to bring in that next pack

- To kite melee mobs & nuke them with casters before they arrive, use Chain Lightning to pull and Earthbind Totem to snare them. Optionally Frost Shock to help kite. Earthbind also works as an "oh shit" button to help a healer catch up

- For Caverns of Time: Black Morass, bring a Druid, Warlock or Hunter to help CC the mobs during those 3/4 Dragon pack pulls leading to the first boss (this dungeon eats every tank; it's not a shaman-specific thing to struggle here). Having a pala friend is huge for devotion aura. When the last boss enrages, find a way to kite it. Aim for approx 6k armor and 4k hp unbuffed before tanking it. It's also recommended to get 4* the best armor kits and initially a fortitude and armor potion + health potion. Some cool items which can help you reach the needed mitigation: Hateforge trinket, even the lvl 33 stamina JC trinket, Hateforge tank neck, shield with the biggest armor you find (preferably 1900+), Helm of Narv (if you wanna buy a BoE), Kara Crypt tank ring, Naglering

-> With the new "Spell AoE tank" build https://octowow.st/talents/shaman?points=FQDYLAADAC-FAVYALBQDIAY-, if you really want to cook some threat on a ST, you can rotate in Fire Nova -> Magma totems and Stoneclaw, like you're doing AoE threat. But it's very expensive to do that so you won't usually maintain that (or need to)

## Enh DPS Rotation
- In dungeons while leveling, use a 2H with Flametongue or WF (*anything but* Rockbiter - at 60 WF) as imbue + Lightning Shield, using Lightning Strike -> Storm Strike, maybe Earth/Flame Shock on bosses -> Lightning Strike

- After Ele Focus/Weapons, use a 2H with Frostbrand + Water Shield, using Frost Shock -> Stormstrike -> Lightning Strike/Shield

- Totems: Strength of Earth, Flametongue, Windfury (once you have it) and Mana Spring. 

- Imbues: Frostbrand for some dungeons or soloing. Windfury is preferred for raiding and what you use when you’ve got a real weapon that you wanna do real numbers with.

- At 60 as you get more crit / focus procs, but be ready to chug mana pots and tea on CD to max damage to sustain Lightning shield longer otherwise you swap to Water shield when you're low on mana. Your mana consumption will also go down in raids, especially if you have ferals around. Blessing of Kings, Mark of the Wild, elixirs like Mongoose and Dreamshard, etc.

- Rotation at 60: use Windfury imbue + Lightning Shield; swap to Water Shield when low on mana. Keep up Lightning Shield. Don’t use Flame Shock on targets that won’t live more than 9-15 seconds. The point at which a Flame Shock becomes more worth than Earth Shock depends on your gear and level, so test on a dummy. At max level it’s always at least 3 ticks (9 seconds). For the rotation, put down totems first -> cast Flame Shock (or Earth Shock if immune) early when running in -> Stormstrike -> Lightning Strike -> Earth Shock (or Flame Shock to reapply debuff), repeat. TL;DR: FS/ES -> SS -> LS, repeat.

## Healing Rotation
- No imbue

- Put down totems that are most beneficial for your group, but only if needed to conserve mana (they have a 30y radius now, so sometimes you won't need to put them down again for a new pack): Strength of Earth, Flametongue, Windfury (once you have it) and Mana Spring (except when you need frost resistance and/or tremor/grounding). Situationally, use poison/disease cleansing, Fire Resistance or even Healing Stream totems

- To keep your tank up in dungeons, use the latest rank of Healing Wave and Lesser Healing Wave, and -2 downranked Healing Wave, eg. r5 HW and LHW + r3 HW. It's also recommended to use r1 Healing Wave sometimes just to trigger Healing Way procs and fish for +armor on tanks

- Chain Heal occasionally if there's lots of cleave dmg on the party

- Once you're raiding, all you really need is Chain Heal rank 1 besides dropping totems within range of your group. Besides that, you can throw out Spirit Link on tanks during enrage phases, eg. use on Maexxna before web wrap

## Totem Use While Solo
- They're not often worth dropping since you’ll be killing faster than the mana is worth to drop them (the mana cast is balanced around your whole party benefiting)

- That said, Earthbind weaving with a Swing timer is worth doing if you care about reducing the damage you take fighting mobs 1v1

- After getting Clearcasting, you can use Searing Totem + Stoneskin occasionally, mainly when it‘s possible to chain pull multiple mobs to the same spot

- To maximize your fire totems' damage when you have Elemental Weapons, you could use the Flametongue imbue - but generally Frostbrand is still superior while leveling

## Earthbind Weaving/Kiting
- Earthbind Totem > Searing Totem > Flame Shock

- Melee, side strafe through them (don't expose your back) to get out of their range while your auto attack is resetting

- As your swing timer is resetting, position to re-enter the mobs' hitbox and swing again

- Re-apply Flame Shock / Lightning shield if efficient

## Profs
- Skinning & LW while leveling since nodes are highly contested on Ambershire atm. And LW allows you to make Stormshroud armor around level 50 (especially the shoulders and hands, they're pre-BiS for enhance), and Devilsaur hands/legs around level 53/55 respectively 

- Alternatively, could take Mining & Blacksmithing as enhance; the Hateforge set is pretty nice around level 50; then after Kara10 comes out, you can get the Towerforge set -> you can also just buy these sets or pieces (chest and legs especially) from AH

- To be more economical, you could skip LW/BS and go straight for herbalism & alchemy from the start

- At ~53 when you start farming lashers, after making Stormshroud, drop LW for herbalism to beef up your gold farming by getting Dream Foil with a chance at Black Lotus

- At 60, herbalism & alchemy for general use and late-game gold (Black Lotus hunting is fun)

- Consider dropping alchemy for engineering - dropping Sappers/Dynamite will help your raid clear faster + if you plan to PvP there’s a lot of utility, even outside of stun grenades

## Spells to Buy
- You can skip all but 1st rank of Resurrection

- Can technically skip Flametongue imbue

- Totems: you likely won't use the nature/frost/fire resistance totems during leveling enough to justify getting those. If you want, you can probably skip all totems besides the Poison/Disease cleansing totems, though for tanking you might want the strength/agi/WF totems. Water totems are optional (pretty weak while leveling but can be nice)

- If you don't plan to tank or play ele, skip the ranged damage spells (but never shocks)

- Everything else you need no matter the spec

## Pre-BiS
- Enh DPS: check out this post in the #shaman channel: https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/810850759658831903/1415067340462362656 Aim for 8% hit for melee. You'll get 3% from talents, and another 1% from Weapon Skill book (250g + quest for permanent 5 weapon skill: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16790 & [Weapon_Skill](Weapon_Skill). So usually, you'll only need to get 4% more from gear/weapon skill. At level 58+ pick up a quest from Shaman trainer in Org, turn in some gems and then kill a mob in Scholomance for Skyfury Helm: https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=7667 (otherwise the Mail helm from Strat, Eyepatch from BRD, or Eyepatch from UBRS are also good options). Take a look at Blacksmithing for the Hateforge Armor set for pre-BiS (https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?itemset=615) and once Kara10 drops the Towerforge Battlegear (https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?itemset=630). Also, check out this video for a general idea: https://www.youtube.com/embed/SgkuSNu-AMQ Later on, follow this explanation for any build trying to get crush/crit immune (= reaching 440 defense) https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_table. More suggested items: pre-BiS mace: https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=83440, Breastplate options: https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?quest=5166; https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60008; https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=14611; https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=13090, shoulders: BM +2% hit one or the Kara 5 one. Arcanite reaper or Bonecrusher are best options for pre-BiS, but could also try Treant's Bane if you are a masochist: https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18538. If you are a double masochist, become exalted in Alterac Valley -Unstoppable Force is great: https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=19323. Eventually, the rule of thumb is to get 30-35% crit, then start stacking AP and +haste (the more haste you get the better)

- Tanking: focus on +stam and armor gear, ignoring defense beyond 15 (with the caveat that you'll still want defense, agility or direct dodge stats when it doesn't compromise stamina or armor to grab it). Generally speaking, just try to get the best threat/defense piece you can for each armor slot because sometimes you need to max your defense out, but sometimes you need a lot of threat, so it’s just dependent on the fight. Shields: SWV shield or the one from Kara5 are acceptable as pre-BiS. The one from BM can probably also be used (less stamina though)

- Resto: Get as much +healing and +int as you can. Just do all the high level dungeons a few times and you should be good, eg. get the legs from DM West, decent options from BRD are the gloves from either Emperor or Moira/Mantle of Lost Hope/Soot Encrusted Footwear/Guiding Stave of Wisdom/Second Wind, Rivendare's Robe from Stratholme UD, Royal Seal of Eldre'Thalas from Dire Maul. As for crafted items, the crafted cloak, and maybe even the crafted chest are great options if you can afford it. Black Morass has the BiS totem until Naxx, and some caster stuff, as well as Kara Crypts. I wouldn't recommend it since it's easy to get similarly strong items in MC/BWL/AQ40, but you could look out for high healing power lvl 54+ green gear on the AH such as Created Cape (you won't need to replace it until the cape from AQ40's final boss), the chest piece and maybe Corehound Belt (optional because costly) - some of those pieces are better than even MC T1 (for example the legs), but it comes at the cost of stam & int (on a badly geared resto shammy, int is pretty important; around a 4k mana pool is recommended - or if your guild clears raids fast enough, you can just drink more pots/tea and oranges between pulls). Enchants to get for resto besides the typically recommended ones outside of T-WoW are enchanted armor kits that give +9 spell power. 2% haste to shoulder enchant, and then 1% haste vs +12 spellpower to helm/legs; ring/neck enchants (+12 healing) from JC. Belt buckle for +10 intellect. Other ideas: the helm and trinket from AD faction, Mindfang from AB exalted, offhand from AV exalted, the shield from BM exalted, totem from BM drop, decent ring from BM faction if Urok LBRS ring evades you, UBRS trinket with AD trinket will be BiS. Strat chest vs BM faction chest could go either way. For weapon, the weaker +healing enchant. Tbh for pre-BiS, it's probably not worth it to enchant gear you're going to replace soon - especially the nitpicky ones like +100hp on chest and such (instead, you can spend the gold on more mana pots and teas). +12 on neck and rings when you have decent ones are worth since it's pretty cheap, then gloves/wrist/weapon if you have good ones

- Ele: check the pinned in #shaman for a doc with ele pre-BiS. Basically need 15% hit on spells, as much int and crit as possible and spd, hit > crit > spd > int. If you have some gold there are two pairs of crafted cloth gloves that are really good, one with crit and one with haste. T1 belt/wrist from AH. The blue crafted Bloodvine set but don’t spend to much gold on stuff you can replace from Kara10. 2% crit quest reward trinket and DM trinket. It won’t be hard to get Onyxia group fairly fast, then you can get the Horde head and a good ring. UBRS has two trinkets and a great cloth head. Gloves of Unwinding Mystery

- You could look at a Classic BiS list and use Atlasloot to check the new dungeons like Kara Crypts (eg. Crown of Shattering, and pre-BiS mace: Boneshatter Maul), Black Morass (eg. a very good mp5 totem), Hateforge Quarry (the Corrosive Core trinket is good if you lack +hit) and Stormwind Vaults (eg. Whip of Encouragement, haste is better if you're hit capped), and see if anything is an upgrade

## Stat Prios at 60

- 1 INT = ~0.05 AP / ~0.10 SP

## Weapon Imbues & Shields
- Rockbiter + Lightning Shield until level 41/42 

- Then Frostbrand becomes better with Elemental Focus + Elemental Weapons talents

- At 60 you’re gonna use Windfury imbue, but you’re still gonna drop Windfury Totem (and Flametongue Totem) for your party even if you don’t benefit from it

## Weapons
- Until you get the Lightning Strike talent, a high-DPS dagger is just as good as a slow 2H

- After getting Lightning Strike, try to have as slow of a 2H as possible

- In general, enhance prefers slow weapons with high top-end dmg, counting for both 1H and 2H
