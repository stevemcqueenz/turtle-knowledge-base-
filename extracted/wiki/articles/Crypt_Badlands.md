# Crypt (Badlands)

The [Badlands](Badlands) **Crypt** is located southeast of [Hammertoe's Digsite](Hammertoe's Digsite), at the top of the hill behind [Sigrun Ironhew](Sigrun Ironhew). Entrance to it is via a natural and unmarked path up the hill. The bottom of the Crypt contains a number of urns, chests, and skeletons, with the centerpiece being an armored skeleton seated on a throne with the [Sword of the Barbarian King](Sword of the Barbarian King) next to it. The ghost of [Marauder King Corthan](Marauder King Corthan) haunts the crypt still, trapped by the Lord of Everlasting Death's curse.
