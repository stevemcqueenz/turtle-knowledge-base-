# Centaur

The centaur are a half-human, half-horse, warlike tribal race. They abound in central and southern [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), primarily in [Desolace](Desolace) and [the Barrens](the Barrens), where they engage in constant war against other centaur tribes.  They are savagery and brutality incarnate. 

## Appearance and Culture
Centaur appear as a creature with a horse body and legs with a humanoid torso, arms and head extending from the front part of where a horse neck and head would normally be placed.   A full grown male centaur can stand over seven feet tall from hooves to head and weigh over a ton. 

Each tribe of centaur is lead by a [Khan](Khan), who is generally a leader of above average strength and intelligence. Some of the clans, if not all, practice cannibalism and will eat the flesh of other sentient races as well, such as [tauren](tauren).

Each tribe has its own unique tribal color, Black, Brown, Green, Red, and Yellow. Members use the colors to mark their weapons and their faces.

## Origin
The centaur were the offspring of [Zaetar](Zaetar), a [keeper of the grove](keeper of the grove) and the eldest son of the demigod [Cenarius](Cenarius), and the earth princess [Theradras](Theradras). They are therefore related to keepers of the grove and dryads through their Cenarion lineage.  After the centaur slew their father, their mother buried his remains in [Maraudon](Maraudon), which became a holy place for the centaur. 

## Conflict with the Horde
[Hatred of the Centaur](Hatred of the Centaur), a [tauren myth](Tauren mythology), tells how the early tauren were driven from their ancestral lands (presumably in [Mulgore](Mulgore)) and forced to wander [the Barrens](the Barrens) of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor).  The tauren claim that the centaur have always existed to scourge the land.

When the [orc](orc)s first arrived in Kalimdor, they found the nomadic tauren under constant attack from the centaur.  Through the aid of the orcs, the tauren were finally able to reclaim their ancestral lands and live in peace once more.  The conflict between the [Horde](Horde) and the centaur continues, however, particularly in the Barrens.  The members of the Horde hate them so intensely that they have taken to skinning the corpses and making rugs out of them.  Such decorations are a common sight in cities such as [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar) and [Thunder Bluff](Thunder Bluff).

The Horde and the [Alliance](Alliance) are both attempting to control the savage centaur by encouraging inter-tribal conflicts, so as to prevent the centaur from banding together and becoming a dangerous force. 

## The Pariah
Despite the hostility each centaur clan has traditionally had for the others, recently a centaur known only as the Pariah, labeled as a heretic by his people, developed plans to unify the clans. Adventurers aided him in his design by entering the holy tomb of [Maraudon](Maraudon) and forging the Amulet of Union, which he intended to use to bring his warring people together as one. Whether or not the Centaur will actually be able to consolidate into a unified, civilized people remains unknown.

The recently redeemed spirit of Zaetar also spoke of hope for a more positive future for his offspring.

## Other Legends
According to [Flavor Lore](Flavor Lore) in the Warcraft III Manual, and [Manual of Monsters](Manual of Monsters) within the world of warcraft is a competing myth for existence of the centaur race which it was theorized that centaurs may have been the direct bastard offspring of [Cenarius](Cenarius). However the sources were also quick to point out that this was only a legend and not necessarily true. 

<blockquote>"Legend holds that the primitive, barbaric centaurs are actually the bastard off-spring of the night elf demigod, Cenarius. Whether or not this is true, it is certain that they are savagery and brutality incarnate.
"</blockquote>

However while the legend ultimately proved to be untrue for the five tribes of centaur living in Kalimdor, there may be some truth to the legend, see [Centaur (Eastern Kingdoms)](Centaur (Eastern Kingdoms)).
