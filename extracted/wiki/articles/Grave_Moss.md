# Grave Moss

-Grave Moss** is an [herb](herb) that can be gathered with [Herbalism](Herbalism) (120). The moss can be found around gravesites in places such as the [Kodo Graveyard](Kodo Graveyard) in [Desolace](Desolace), [Ironbeard's Tomb](Ironbeard's Tomb) in the [Wetlands](Wetlands), [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery) graveyard in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), and the [Raven Hill](Raven Hill) graveyard in [Duskwood](Duskwood).
