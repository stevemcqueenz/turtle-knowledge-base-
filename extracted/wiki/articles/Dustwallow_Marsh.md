-Dustwallow Marsh** (or **Dustwallow Swamp**) (ref:  [43+] [Overlord Mok'Morokk's Concern](Overlord Mok'Morokk's Concern)) is a vast and ancient swamp in the eastern reaches of [Central Kalimdor](Central Kalimdor). Navigation is often extremely difficult and many adventurers have lost their way in this great jungle, where trees obscure the sunlight and [raptor](raptor)s, [spider](spider)s, and [crocolisk](crocolisk)s lie in wait, hunting those brave enough to venture close to their lairs. Although there is little activity in the area, the Alliance and the Horde have recently expanded their influence in the region. The [Alliance](Alliance) city-state of [Theramore](Theramore) is located on a small island east off shore, and the Horde's primary foothold in the area is [Brackenwall Village](Brackenwall Village), the new home of the ogre [Stonemaul clan](Stonemaul clan).

## Geography
The raid dungeon [Onyxia's Lair](Onyxia's Lair) and the micro dungeon [Alcaz Island](Alcaz Island) can be found in this area. No other dungeons or battlegrounds are located here.

  -Micro Dungeon**
-[Alcaz Island](Alcaz Island): This island lies off the coast of the marsh and is patrolled by dangerous monsters (level 57-62 elite). It is inhabited mostly by nagas, though hydras can be found in the water around the island. Under the surface of the island is a heavily guarded prison, where the missing king of Stormwind, [Varian Wrynn](Varian Wrynn) was kept after his abduction by the Defias.
