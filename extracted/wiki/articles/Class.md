# Class

A **class** is the primary adventuring style of a [player character](player character). A character's class determines the [abilities](Ability) and [skill](skill)s they will gain throughout their adventures, and consequently the styles of play available to the character. It determines the types of [weapons and armor](Equipment) they can use, which [attributes](attributes) they will value (and how those attributes function), if they are the [melee](melee), [ranged](ranged), or [caster](caster) type, as well as what combat [role](role)s the character is suitable for. Class also reflects the background themes of a character: whether they have chosen to pursue the dark arts of the [warlock](warlock) or the [Holy Light](Light) of the [paladin](paladin); the bloody honor of the [warrior](warrior) or the arcane knowledge of the [mage](mage).
