Lord **Aiden Perenolde** (ref: [quest: Legacy of Perenolde]) was the king of the nation and kingdom of [Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)) and one of the original founders of the [Alliance of Lordaeron](Alliance of Lordaeron).

Perhaps the most notorious human noble of the [Second War](Second War), Lord Perenolde betrayed the Alliance to the [Orcish Horde](Orcish Horde) in exchange for the safety of Alterac, having come to believe that only the surrender of his forces and his sovereignty would save the lives of his subjects. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Nations of the Alliance](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Alterac)*, Alterac) To this end, he arranged for a peasant revolt in [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand) to cover Horde mining operations there, kidnapped mages from [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)) and arranged for a convoy of high elves passing through Tarren Mill to be ambushed by forest trolls. (ref: [Betrayal and the Destruction of Alterac (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Betrayal_and_the_Destruction_of_Alterac_(WC2_Human)))

When Lord Perenolde's treachery was discovered by the Kirin Tor, the combined might of the Alliance forces, led by Dalaran mages, turned its wrath upon the small kingdom, ruthlessly crushing it as punishment for his treachery.

The fate of Lord Perenolde after the Second War and the Alterac crisis is unknown. His son [Aliden](Aliden Perenolde) fought over the lands of Alterac against Aiden's nephew [Isiden](Isiden Perenolde), though by the end of the Third War, Aliden had taken command of the disenfranchised nobility of Alterac and led them as the [Syndicate](Syndicate), while Isiden remained a guest of House Greymane in Gilneas until he was taken captive by the Harlow regime. A distant cousin of his, [Baron L. Perenolde](Baron Perenolde), has been raised into undeath and has also been seen roaming Gilneas after the opening of the [Greymane Wall](Greymane Wall).
