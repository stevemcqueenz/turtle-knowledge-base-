# Server:Nordanaar EU

**Nordanaar** (originally **Turtle WoW**) is an RP-PvE server, and the original Turtle WoW server prior to the launching of the [Hogger](Server:Hogger SEA) and [Ravenshire](Server:Ravenshire SEA) servers, soon followed by the launching of the [Emerald Dream](Server:Emerald Dream SEA) and [Tel'Abim](Server:Tel'Abim EU) servers.
