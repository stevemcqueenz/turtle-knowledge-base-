# Dustbowl

-The Dustbowl** is a large area of the northwest of [Badlands](Badlands) between [Kargath](Kargath) and the [Valley of Fangs](Valley of Fangs) in the center of the Badlands. It is home to a variety of species including [coyote](coyote)s, [Rock Elemental](Rock Elemental)s, and [buzzard](buzzard)s. Some flora have been able to grow on the arid landscape such as a myriad of cacti and a few trees. This place also contains [Camp Wurg](Camp Wurg).
