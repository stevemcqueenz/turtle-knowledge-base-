Sir **Rudolf of Strom** is a human located at the [Jousting Grounds](Jousting Grounds) in [Northwind](Northwind). One of the participants in the Northwind jousting tournament representing the [kingdom of Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)), he faces off against [Sir Reginald of Stormwind](Sir Reginald of Stormwind).
