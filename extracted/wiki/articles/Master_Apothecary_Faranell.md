-Master Apothecary Faranell** is a [Forsaken](Forsaken) quest giver located in the [Apothecarium](Apothecarium) in the [Undercity](Undercity). As the Master of the Apothecarium, he oversees the most ambitious of their alchemical attempts.<ref name="Guano">[quest: Going, Going, Guano!])

## History
In life, Faranell was an alchemist of great renown and spent the better part of his days studying amongst the learned wizards of Dalaran. He enjoyed nothing more than experimenting with concoctions of every sort, always looking to create a serum for this, an antidote for that. After he died from the plague and rose as one of the Forsaken, his limitless curiosity was twisted by depthless sadism. Faranell became one of the foremost experts on the undead plague, using his skills in service to Queen Sylvanas by crafting alchemical monstrosities and designing newer, more devastating plagues and toxins. ### World of Warcraft
Years after the [Third War](Third War), Faranell worked with other apothecaries on toxins and the [New Plague](New Plague). [Apothecary Renferrel](Apothecary Renferrel) sent a [Horde](Horde) [adventurer](adventurer) with [bear hearts](Grizzled Bear Heart) and [spider blood](Skittering Blood) to Faranell for study their toxic and contaminable properties.<ref>[quest: A Recipe For Death]) Faranell then asked the adventurer to go to [Pyrewood Village](Pyrewood Village) to retrieve the [journal](Berard's Journal) of his former colleague, now the crazed [worgen](worgen) [Apothecary Berard](Apothecary Berard), and bring it to Renferrel. (ref: [quest: A Recipe For Death]) From the journal, Renferrel learned about ingredients that could help in the creation of the new plague. After the adventurer collected moss and tumor from [bog beast](bog beast)s and [murloc](murloc)s, they brought them to Faranell. (ref: [quest: A Recipe For Death])

Later, Faranell worked with [Apothecary Lydon](Apothecary Lydon) on a new elixir. Lydon asked him to apply his colloid of decay on [Mudsnout Composite](Mudsnout Composite), (ref: [quest: Elixir of Agony]) and Faranell then directed the adventurer to gather more reagents in order to activate the contaminating agents in the [Mudsnout Mixture](Mudsnout Mixture). (ref: [quest: Elixir of Agony])

Afterward, Faranell asked for a [Kraul Guano](Kraul Guano) from the [bats](Kraul Bat) in [Razorfen Kraul](Razorfen Kraul). (ref:  With the guano experiment, he wanted to observe what effect two diametrically opposed chemical substances can have on each other, with an added ever-toxic agent. Thus he sent the adventurer to collect hearts of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) members in the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery).<ref>[quest: Hearts of Zeal])

He sent [Orvan Darkeye](Orvan Darkeye) to [Gilneas](Gilneas) on a mission to perfect their new weapon against the enemies of the Horde. After having secured one of the manuscripts on blood magic written by Archmage [Ur](Ur), Orvan sent word back to Faranell that the mission was a success. (ref: [quest: Blood for Blood])

Later, [Lydon](Lydon) sent him [Shadra](Shadra)'s poison for testing. (ref: [quest: Venom to the Undercity])
