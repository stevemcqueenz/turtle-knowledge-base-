# Darrowmere Lake

**Darrowmere Lake** is one of the largest lakes in the known world. It is located in the southwestern part of the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands). At its center is the abandoned island town of [Caer Darrow](Caer Darrow), where the [House of Barov](House of Barov) sits. Inside and deep below the House of Barov is the [Scholomance](Scholomance), the home of the [Cult of the Damned](Cult of the Damned) and countless [undead](undead) minions.

The eastern side of the lake is exposed to a constant breeze which carries with it fresh air blown down from the peaks of the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands). This is where the plaguemist is thinnest in all the plaguelands, and traces of blue sky can even be seen reflected in the large lake.
