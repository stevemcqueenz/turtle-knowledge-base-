Admiral **Caelan** was the former leader of the [Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet](Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet) that was shipwrecked on [Lapidis Isle](Lapidis Isle), and was lost when the fleet was attacked. The outpost of [Caelan's Rest](Caelan's Rest) was named in his honor.
