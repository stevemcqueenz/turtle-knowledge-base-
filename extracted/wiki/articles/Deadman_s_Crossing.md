# Deadman's Crossing

**Deadman's Crossing** is the eastern crossroads leading west to [Duskwood](Duskwood), east towards the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows), and south to [Karazhan](Karazhan) in [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass). It is marked with a hangman's tree still bearing a pair of corpses, as well as a signpost reading "Turn Back!" and "Abandon hope, all ye who enter here."

Despite the forbidding warnings and the high level of monsters in other areas in Deadwind Pass, Deadman's Crossing is perfectly safe for low-level adventurers to run through on their way to the Swamp of Sorrows, provided that they don't stray off the road.
