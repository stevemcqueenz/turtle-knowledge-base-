# Brewnall Village

-Brewnall Village** is a tiny [dwarven](Dwarf) village established in [Chill Breeze Valley](Chill Breeze Valley), near [Gnomeregan](Gnomeregan). It serves as a home for the [Barleybrew](Barleybrew)s, who constantly work to experiment and improve on the venerable dwarven beer brewing tradition. The Barleybrews send young dwarves out on missions to protect the village from wild animals (ref: [quest: A Favor for Evershine]) and seek out secret ingredients for the ultimate ale. (ref: [quest: The Perfect Stout])

## Notes
- Despite being affiliated with the dwarven kingdom of [Ironforge](Ironforge (kingdom)), the village is located in the gnomish territory part of [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh).
- Despite being called a village, the location has no actual buildings and seems more like a camp.
