The **Gilneas Brigade**, commanded by an [unidentified dwarven mountain king](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Mountain_king_(randomized)), is a detachment of troops within the [Human Expedition](Human Expedition) that were led by [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore) to [Kalimdor](Kalimdor). In the mission it appears in, the Brigade is almost entirely made up of dwarven [riflemen](Rifleman) and [mortar operators](Mortar team). (ref: [Cry of the Warsong (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cry_of_the_Warsong_(WC3_Orc))) 

The Gilneas Brigade was formed by Crown Prince [Liam Greymane](Liam Greymane), Lord [Darius Ravenwood](Darius Ravenwood), and Lord [Gerold](Gerold) of [Gelnor](Gelnor), following King [Genn Greymane](Genn Greymane)'s refusal to spare any men for Lady Proudmoore. While Lord Ravenwood readied a fleet in secret, Gerold assembled a force of stout Gelnorian dwarves and men to volunteer as the promised honor guard. Named the Gilneas Brigade by Prince Liam with full honors on the Gilnean shores, the plot to send troops to the Human Expedition was only discovered by King Genn long after the ships, soldiers and supplies were far past the horizon. Harsh punishment fell upon all the plotters, the Crown Prince included, but the warriors found their way into the service of Jaina Proudmoore regardless. At her side they are said to remain loyal unto the bitter end. (ref: [The Prophet's Warnings and the Gilneas Brigade](The Prophet's Warnings and the Gilneas Brigade))

While in service to Jaina the Gilneas Brigade would form an encampment and aid in guarding the passage into the [Stonetalon Peak](Stonetalon Peak)s. Some time later the encampment came under attack from [Grommash Hellscream](Grommash Hellscream) and the [Warsong clan](Warsong clan). In response the brigade struck back against the rest of the [Horde](Horde).

Following the opening of the Greymane Wall by the [Ravenwood Rebellion](Ravenwood Rebellion), the Gilneas Brigade has begun to return home as the call to action is sounded to liberate Gilneas from the tyranny that has taken root within. (ref: [quest: The Brigade is Coming Home])
