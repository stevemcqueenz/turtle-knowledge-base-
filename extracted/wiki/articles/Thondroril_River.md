# Thondroril River

The **Thondroril River** makes up the border between the [Western](Western Plaguelands) and [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands). It runs from near the [Terrorweb Tunnel](Terrorweb Tunnel) to the north and empties out into [Darrowmere Lake](Darrowmere Lake) to the south. Along the river on on both sides, you can find [maggot](maggot)s, [vampire bat](Vampire Bat)s, [plaguebear](plaguebear)s and [spider](spider)s. You can also find [Tirion Fordring](Tirion Fordring) (a level 61 elite quest giver) near a ruined farmhouse on the Eastern Plaguelands side of the river.
