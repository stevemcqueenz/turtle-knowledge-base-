# Elite

Both [quest](quest)s and [mob](mob)s may be flagged as elite.

This generally denotes that the content is more difficult than should be usually expected for its [level](level), and most often requires a group of appropriately levelled players to overcome. Most elite content is very inefficient to [solo](solo).

Elite monsters can have improved [health](health), [damage](damage) or additional [abilities](abilities).

The advantage of completing elite goals is that the rewards generally reflect the added investment and time required. Elite quests tend to have better items or money, and elite mobs will give more experience and loot.
