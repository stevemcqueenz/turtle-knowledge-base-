# Alterac crisis

The **Alterac crisis**, (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, pg. 5) also referred to as the **Alterac Succession Crisis**, (ref: [quest: The Greymane Stone]) was a political dispute between the nations of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)), [Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)), [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)), and [Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)) on what should happen to the traitorous nation of [Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)) and its lands in the aftermath of the [Second War](Second War). The dispute at points threatened to tear the [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron) apart, and the [black dragon](black dragon) [Deathwing](Deathwing) unsuccessfully tried to use the crisis to his advantage to take control of Alterac.

## History
### Background
[Aiden Perenolde](Aiden Perenolde), King of Alterac, had come to believe that only the surrender of his forces and his sovereignty would save the lives of his subjects, and thus betrayed the Alliance to the [Orcish Horde](Orcish Horde) in exchange for the safety of Alterac. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Nations of the Alliance](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Alterac)*, Alterac) To this end, he arranged for a peasant revolt in [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand) to cover Horde mining operations there, kidnapped mages from [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)) and arranged for a convoy of high elves passing through Tarren Mill to be ambushed by forest trolls. (ref: [Betrayal and the Destruction of Alterac (WC2 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Betrayal_and_the_Destruction_of_Alterac_(WC2_Human))) When Lord Perenolde's treachery was discovered by the Kirin Tor, the combined might of the Alliance forces, led by Dalaran mages, turned its wrath upon the small kingdom, ruthlessly crushing it as punishment for his treachery (ref:  and establishing martial law under Lordaeron's forces.<ref name="DotD">*[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*)

### Day of the Dragon
In the aftermath of the Second War, the nations of Lordaeron and Stromgarde maintained garrisons along Alterac's borders. (ref: [Alterac (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Alterac_(WC2_Orc))) King [Thoras Trollbane](Thoras Trollbane), in recognition of Stromgarde's efforts and sacrifices during the conflict, demanded that the eastern portion of Alterac be annexed to Stromgarde. However, King Terenas Menethil II was undecided on what to do, debating the merits of either annexing Alterac to his own kingdom or setting upon its throne a new and more reasonable monarch, one that would be friendly to Lordaeron. (ref:  Lord Perenolde's precarious situation saw his nephew [Isiden](Isiden Perenolde) seeking refuge in Gilneas, where his quest for backing led him to the attention of King [Genn Greymane](Genn Greymane) as the delicate issue of Alteraci succession gained momentum.<ref name="Legacy of Perenolde">[quest: Legacy of Perenolde]) King Greymane's support of Isiden Perenolde drew the ire of [Admiral Proudmoore](Daelin Proudmoore), who was afraid that Gilnean influence in the region would threaten Kul Tiras' naval sovereignty. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, chapter 1)

The black dragon [Deathwing](Deathwing), under the guise of Lord Daval Prestor, manipulated the leaders into making him the future king of Alterac. This worked for a time, but drew the suspicious attention of the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) of [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)), who were conveniently left out of the negotiations by the black dragon. During the crisis, Lord Prestor often frequented [Gilneas City](Gilneas City) and established a close bond with King Greymane, bestowing upon him a gift: a potent artifact known as the [Shard of Midnight](Shard of Midnight). (ref:  Two Alteraci nobles, Lord [Mortimer](Mortimer Harlow) and Lady [Celia Harlow](Celia Harlow), were also sent to Gilneas as envoys of Lord Prestor.

Prestor tried to have the Kirin Tor ostracized by the other Alliance leaders and even secured a betrothal to Lordaeron's princess, [Calia](Calia Menethil), in order to reinforce his political position. Ultimately, however, Deathwing's plans were foiled when he was defeated at the [Battle of Grim Batol](Battle of Grim Batol) and forced into hiding.

### Resolution
With Deathwing's disappearance, his magical influence on the human kings waned away. [Genn Greymane](Genn Greymane) reinstated his protest against Daval Prestor's taking ascension, and even Lord Admiral Proudmoore joined him on this. King Terenas announced that a second check into the so-called noble's background left many questions unanswered, and the wedding was canceled.<ref>*[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, chapter 21)

As a kingdom, Alterac effectively ended when the forces of the Alliance overthrew Lord Perenolde, and its territories were eventually annexed by Stromgarde and Lordaeron. (ref:  At the outset of the [Third War](Third War), [Arthas Menethil](Arthas Menethil) traveled through the [King's Road](King's Road) toward [Strahnbrad](Strahnbrad), and its inhabitants considered him their lord, with the citizens also taking up arms for Lordaeron.<ref>[The Defense of Strahnbrad (WC3 Human)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Defense_of_Strahnbrad_(WC3_Human))) A new resistance against them had appeared, however, in the form of the [Syndicate](Syndicate), led by former Alteraci prince [Aliden Perenolde](Aliden Perenolde) under the teachings of his mentor [Aedelas Blackmoore](Aedelas Blackmoore). (ref: [quest: Blackmoore's Legacy]) Isiden remained in Gilneas even after the closure of the [Greymane Wall](Greymane Wall), and was eventually incarcerated in the [Dryrock Pit](Dryrock Pit) by the Harlows, who had begun acting as regents of Gilneas as King Genn Greymane's health was deteriorating.
