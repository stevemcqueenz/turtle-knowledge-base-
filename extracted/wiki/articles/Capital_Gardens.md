*For the larger dungeon complex, see [Dire Maul](Dire Maul).*

The **Capital Gardens** are the western instance in [Dire Maul](Dire Maul). The area might once have been a pristine city park district, but now the trees stand gray and withered, and the broken streets are full of dangerous [ancient](ancient)s.
