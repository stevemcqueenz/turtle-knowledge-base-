# Coyote

Pronounced 'kai-oh-tee'. A wild [dog](dog) similar to a [wolf](Wolf). In the real world, **coyotes** are smaller with big ears and more dog-like than wolves, but look just like wolves for the most part in World of Warcraft.  Coyotes are common in [Westfall](Westfall).

## Traits
- Coyotes are [hostile](hostile).
- Coyotes often drop coyote [meat](meat).
- Coyote corpses are [skinnable](skinnable).
