# NPC

# Non-Player Character
An NPC or "Non-Player Character" is a slightly hazy term, which is sometimes used in slightly different contexts. Historically it reffered to characters in a role-playing game that were controlled by the Game Master (aka Dungeon Master).

Within the computing context, this definition becomes a bit stretched. Strictly speaking, it can cover any character which is computer-controlled. Typically though, it will be used to either refer to Neutral/Allied computer-controlled entities, or to computer-controlled entities with above-average levels of AI and/or interaction.

Within WoW, all of the vendors, guards, quest givers and other non-attackable mobs (excluding critters) in territories controlled by your faction would probably be considered NPCs. Of course, to members of the opposing faction these may not be friendly, so what's an NPC to a horde character won't necessarily be seen as an NPC by an alliance character!

Attackable entities are normally referred to simply as "Mobs", which is short for "Mobile Objects".
