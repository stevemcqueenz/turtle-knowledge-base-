# Lake Everstill

The serene **Lake Everstill** is a large azure lake located in the center of the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains). The crystal-clear waters of the lake provide a spectacular setting for the sleepy settlement of [Lakeshire](Lakeshire). It is drained by its eastern river which flows near the dark [Stonewatch Keep](Stonewatch Keep). Lake Everstill was once known for its fishing, but recently has become overrun by tribes of [Murloc](Murloc)s.  Adventurers hunting Murlocs in the lake should make sure to keep an eye open for the elite [Lake Thresher](Lake Thresher) that patrols the deeps. The lake is crossed by [Everstill Bridge](Everstill Bridge).

## Fishing
It takes a skill level of 50 to [fish](Fishing) in Lake Everstill.
