# Tower of Karazhan attunement

The [Tower of Karazhan](Tower of Karazhan) raid has two separate attunement questlines. Gaining entry to the upper reaches of the tower requires the [Upper Karazhan Tower Key](Upper Karazhan Tower Key), which grants access to the first five bosses of the raid. However, [the Scepter of Medivh](the Scepter of Medivh) is required to access the last four bosses of the raid.
