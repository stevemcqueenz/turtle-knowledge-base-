## Tank
  -Long effects**

- Spirit of Zanza Binds when picked up Use: Increases the player’s Spirit by 50 and Stamina by 50 for 2 hr.
- Elixir of the Mongoose Use: Increases Agility by 25 and chance to get a critical hit by 2% for 1 hr.
- Ground Scorpok Assay Use: Increases Agility by 25 when consumed. Effect lasts for 60 minutes.

  -CHOOSE ONLY 1 FROM EACH STR/AP CATEGORIES**

  -+STRENGTH**

- Elixir of Giants Use: Increases your Strength by 25 for 1hr.
- JUJU Power Use: Increases the target’s Strength by 30 for 1800 sec.

  -+ ATTACK POWER**:

- Juju Might Use: Increases your  attack power by 40 for 10 min.
- Winterfall Firewater Use: Increases your melee attack power by 35 and size for 20 min.

  -Guardian**

- Elixir of Fortitude Use: Increases maximum health by 120 for 1 hr.
- Elixir of Superior Defense Use: Increases armor by 450 for 1 hr.
- Gift of Arthas Use: Increases resistance to shadow by 10. If an enemy strikes the imbiber, the attacker has a 30% chance of being inflicted with disease that increases their damage taken by 8 for 180 sec. Lasts for 30 min. Does not persist through death.

  -Short Effects**

- Nordanaar Herbal Tea Bind when picked up Use:  Restores 1050 to 1751 health and mana.
- Major Healing Potion Use: Restores 1050 to 1750 health. (2 Min Cooldown).
- Greater Stoneshield Potion Requires level 45 Use: Increases armor by 2000 for 90 sec.
- Free Action Potion Use: Makes you immune to Stun and Movement Impairing effects for the next 30 sec. Does not remove effects already on the imbiber.

  -Food**

- Hardened Mushroom Use: … gain 25 Stamina for 15 min. <nowiki>*</nowiki>stacks with Gurubashi Gumbo
- Le Fishe Au Chocolat Use: … gain 1% dodge and 4 defense for 15 min.
- Gurubashi Gumbo Use: … gain 10 Stamina and 1% reduced chance to be critically hit for 15 min.

  -Strong Alcohol**

- Rumsey Rum Black Label Use: Increases Stamina by 15 for 15 min.
- Medivh’s Merlot* Use: Increases Stamina by 25 for 15 min. <nowiki>*</nowiki>stacks with food buffs
