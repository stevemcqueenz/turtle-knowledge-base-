**Dun Kithas** is an [Alliance](Alliance) town located in the south of the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches).

Following the disastrous [War of the Three Hammers](War of the Three Hammers), many of the dwarven clans sworn to the Wildhammer deserted their old homes of [Grim Batol](Grim Batol), [Kalvanost](Kalvanost) and Dun Kithas, and wandered the northern realms in search of new lands to call their own. The fair Sky King of Dun Kithas, [Redoran](Redoran), flew far into the west with many of his brethren until he came to the [Gilnean](Gilneas (kingdom)) city of [Gelnor](Gelnor). There, he and his people settled after being gifted a place in Gelnor and to Redoran a seat at the court of Queen [Elaena Greymane](Elaena Greymane). (ref: [The Dwarves of Gelnor](The Dwarves of Gelnor))
