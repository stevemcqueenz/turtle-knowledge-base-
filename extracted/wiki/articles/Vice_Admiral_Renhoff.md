### Gossip
I have been sent on behalf of the Lord Admiral to engage in diplomacy with our southern neighbors.

I have been curious to see if the nobility here are true to [the old ways](Alliance of Lordaeron), and to see what respect they hold for the fallen sister kingdoms to the north.

If Kul Tiras and Stormwind are ever to stand side-by-side then brutal truths must be laid out for all to understand bluntly.

There is no truces to be had with the Blackbloods, and no honor to be made standing alongside those that destroyed what once was.
