# Sunwell

The **Sunwell** was a fount of mystical power located in the Sunwell Grove in [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas). It was where the elves drew their immortal powers. (ref: [The Fall of Silvermoon (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fall_of_Silvermoon_(WC3_Undead)))
