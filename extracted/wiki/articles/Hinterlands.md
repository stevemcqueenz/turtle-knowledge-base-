The **Hinterlands**, located in the Northern part of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), are probably most notable for the [Wildhammer dwarves](Wildhammer dwarf) who make their residence at [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak) in the west. The Wildhammers have always been staunch allies of the [Alliance](Alliance). It is from the Hinterlands that these dwarves find and tame their world-renowned [gryphon](gryphon)s. The [Razorbeak gryphon](Razorbeak Gryphon)s roam wild in this region. 

Also in the Hinterlands is one of the last remaining settlements of the [high elves](High elf) in Lordaeron at the [Quel'Danil Lodge](Quel'Danil Lodge). 

The Hinterlands are also home to a large population of [forest troll](forest troll)s, which are divided into three separate tribes. The [Revantusk tribe](Revantusk tribe) is the only one of the three that has declared sides in the Horde-Alliance conflict and support Warchief [Thrall](Thrall) of the [Horde](Horde). The [Vilebranch](Vilebranch) tribe holds residence in the massive troll city of [Jintha'Alor](Jintha'Alor) as well as the [Altar of Zul](Altar of Zul) while the [Witherbark tribe](Witherbark tribe) holds the smallest influence, hailing from the temples of [Shadra'Alor](Shadra'Alor).

In the northern part of the region is the ancient, massive tree, [Seradane](Seradane) where the agents of the [Green dragonflight](Green dragonflight) have been corrupted. A massive portal on the side of the tree connects the servants of the [Aspect](Aspect) [Ysera](Ysera) to her own realm, the [Emerald Dream](Emerald Dream).

## Geography
While there are no instanced dungeons in the Hinterlands, the zone is notable for its huge outdoor micro dungeon, [Jintha'Alor](Jintha'Alor).

The Hinterlands are the location of [Seradane](Seradane), one of the four [Great Tree](Great Tree)s leading to the [Emerald Dream](Emerald Dream) and guarded by the four Emerald Dragons. The three others are in [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), [Duskwood](Duskwood), and [Feralas](Feralas).
