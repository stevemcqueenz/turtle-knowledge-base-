# Stormpike clan

Stormpike clan are a small dwarven clan found in the Alterac Mountains. They have taken a liking to the Alterac Mountains and defend them fiercely against hte trolls and orcs invading hte area. By taking a few troll heads the, the Stormpikes manage to form a close bond with Wildhammer dwarves. An expedition of the Stormpike clan called the [Stormpike Guard](Stormpike Guard) is trying to clear out horde in the [Alterac Valley](Alterac Valley). The stormpike clan is also known for working with the [Wildhammer clan](Wildhammer clan), as well as some of the last [high elves](high elves).
