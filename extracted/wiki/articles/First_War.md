# First War

- Initial battles do not go well for the [Horde](Horde). Expecting an easy victory against a weak opponent, the Horde rushes to assault the fortress of [Stormwind Keep](Stormwind Keep), only to suffer a defeat of catastrophic proportions. Few orcs survive the initial battle.

- [Gul'dan](Gul'dan) invokes shadow magic to enshroud the [Black Morass](Black Morass) in a blinding mist. The mist obscures the retreat of the last remaining [orc](orc)s and prevents the human forces from pursuing them.

- The Horde is humiliated by its unexpected rout, and the tribes swiftly descend into turmoil. Chaos ensues, with factions blaming each other for the Horde’s defeat.

- Gul’dan manipulates a ruthless dictator onto the throne of the Warchief. Known as [Blackhand the Destroyer](Blackhand the Destroyer), the new Warchief rallies the tribes back together again and prepares them for a new assault on [Azeroth](Azeroth).

- [Medivh](Medivh) sends Gul’dan another vision, teasing him with images of the [Tomb of Sargeras](Tomb of Sargeras) and the power it contains.

- The Horde surges into Azeroth again, decimating towns and villages and plundering everything in its path.

- [Durotan](Durotan), chieftain of the [Frostwolf Clan](Frostwolf Clan), is ambushed and slain by rival orcs. The assassins leave Durotan’s infant son for dead, but the young orc is quickly discovered and saved by a band of humans under the command of [Aedelas Blackmoore](Aedelas Blackmoore). The infant is taken as a slave and given the name [Thrall](Thrall).

- Lord [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar) realizes Medivh’s betrayal. He leads a small band of gryphon-mounted warriors to Medivh’s tower and confronts the wizard. Lothar slays Medivh’s body and destroys the spirit of [Sargeras](Sargeras). Medivh’s spirit escapes, however.

- Blackhand the Destroyer is betrayed and slain by [Orgrim Doomhammer](Orgrim Doomhammer), a trusted general and close friend. Doomhammer replaces Blackhand as Warchief of the Horde.

- [Stormwind Keep](Stormwind Keep) falls before the full might of the Horde and is sacked. During the battle, [King Llane](King Llane) is slain by one of Gul’dan’s assassins, the half-orc [Garona](Garona).

- Spies loyal to Orgrim Doomhammer capture and torture Garona. In agony, she reveals the existence of the [Shadow Council](Shadow Council) and directs them to the location of Gul’dan’s warlocks near the ruins of Stormwind Keep.

- Doomhammer dispatches his elite wolfriders to the ruins. They slay or execute most of Guldan’s warlocks and disperse the remaining members of the Shadow Council. Gul’dan pleas for mercy and offers complete submission. Doomhammer accepts and grants him mercy.

- Lord Anduin Lothar concedes that the Kingdom of Stormwind has been lost. He rallies his countrymen and leads them in a desperate retreat across the Great Sea, eventually landing upon the shores of Lordaeron.
