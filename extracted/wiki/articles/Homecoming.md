## Objectives
Bring Elder Krasheen's projection crystal to Sanv K'la in the Harborage in the Swamp of Sorrows.
- [Elder Krasheen's Projection Crystal](Elder Krasheen's Projection Crystal)

## Description
I could have never imagined it. For so long we have lived with the thought of us being the only draenei to have survived the breaking of our homeworld. To think that another tribe has managed to escape to your world as well…

<class>, we must get in contact with them. You may be a stranger to the Moro'gai, but you are acquainted with another draenei tribe, so I place my trust in you. Take this crystal and bring it to their chieftain. It is a projection crystal in which I have stored my image into. Tell them that, and they will know how to proceed.

## Rewards
- 1200 [XP](XP)

## Progress
<name>, it is good to see you again in the Harborage. How can I assist you?

## Completion
A projection crystal... from another draenei?! Please, hand it to me quickly!

## Notes
  -Upon completion**
  Sanv K'la channels energy into the crystal.
  **Image of Elder Krasheen says:** So the Parash'ka spoke true. There really are other draenei in this world.
  **Image of Elder Krasheen says:** I am Elder Krasheen of the Moro'gai. Formerly inhabiting the swamps of Monoogha, we now call the lakes of Moonwhisper Coast our home. It is an honor to meet your tribe as the representative of the Moro'gai.
  **Sanv K'la says:** Okhal-asha, Elder Krasheen. I am Sanv K'la of the Sanv. Your words resonate with my own. Seeing another tribe alive and well sets my heart aflutter.
  **Image of Elder Krasheen says:** The Sanv Tribe? Of the Nham'shana Jungle? We've heard stories of your exploits. It is an honor to see such renowned riftwalkers, even as a projection.
  **Sanv K'la says:** Our tribe is no longer what it once was, we're a mere drop of water in the vast ocean that is the Twisting Nether. But let us continue this conversation is person, Elder Krasheen.
  **Image of Elder Krasheen says:** Well spoke. I will prepare for this occasion and get in contact with you very soon again.
  Elder Krasheen's image fades away.
  **Sanv K'la says:** We are once more in your debt, fair traveler. You have fulfilled a dream I had thought to be nothing more than a delusion of mine. The Sanv tribe will always welcome you as their own. Enkil Toralar.
