# Northlands

The **Northlands** is a name used to refer to different lands in the continent of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron).

The first use of the term was in *[Warcraft II](Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness)*, referring to much, if not all of the continent of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron), including the areas around [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand), [Tyr's Bay](Tyr's Bay), [Darrowmere](Darrowmere Lake), [Caer Darrow](Caer Darrow), [Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)), [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)), and [Alterac](Alterac (kingdom)). (ref: [The Tomb of Sargeras (WC2 Orc)](The Tomb of Sargeras (WC2 Orc))) (ref: [The Great Portal (WC2 Human)](The Great Portal (WC2 Human)))

The term was later used to refer to only the northernmost lands of the kingdom of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)) itself, where cities such as [Stratholme](Stratholme), [Andorhal](Andorhal), [Hearthglen](Hearthglen) and Tyr's Hand are located. (ref: Infered from [The Warning](The Warning), where a diplomat mentions the area as affected by the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath).) The [archmage](archmage) [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) owned several warehouses in the region, where he conducted crude necromantic experiments. His experiments ultimately a failure, the [Kirin Tor](Kirin Tor) confiscated his property, deeming his efforts nefarious. (ref: *[Road to Damnation](Road to Damnation)*, pg. 1) This area was the first region of Lordaeron afflicted with the [Plague of Undeath](Plague of Undeath), and later became known as the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands).
