# Grand Vestibule

The **Grand Vestibule** is the outermost chamber of the [Scarlet Monastery](Scarlet Monastery). It holds the entrances to all of the dungeon's wings, and leads via short passages to the [Chamber of Atonement](Chamber of Atonement) in the graveyard wing, the [Huntsman's Cloister](Huntsman's Cloister) in the library wing, the [Training Grounds](Training Grounds) in the armory wing, and the [Chapel Gardens](Chapel Gardens) in the cathedral wing. The latter two wings are barred by locked doors, and require [the Scarlet Key](the Scarlet Key) to open. 

The vestibule itself is decorated with pillars and statues of scarlet warriors. It is often a contested place, as [Horde](Horde) and [Alliance](Alliance)-affiliated adventurers mindle in the hall with sometimes brutal results.
