# Zhevra

A species of [unicorn](unicorn). It is a horse-like beast common to the [Barrens](Barrens). They are neutral, but will defend themselves with kicks if attacked. Their name is similar to the real-life species, the zebra.

Notable zhevra:
- [Swiftmane](Swiftmane), a legendary zhevra which can run at incredible speeds.

Types of zhevra:
- [Zhevra Charger](Zhevra Charger)
- [Zhevra Courser](Zhevra Courser)
- [Zhevra Runner](Zhevra Runner)
