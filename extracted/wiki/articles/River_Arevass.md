# River Arevass

The **River Arevass** is a [river](river) in [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest), running past [Pyrewood Village](Pyrewood Village). It probably originates from the mountains within Silverpine Forest and flows towards or into [Lordamere Lake](Lordamere Lake).

On the bridge leading out of the village, the [night elf](night elf) [priest](priest)ess [Tyrande Whisperwind](Tyrande Whisperwind), having traveled to [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) to hunt down [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage), made a stand against the [Scourge](Scourge) in order to save a retreating force of [blood elves](Blood elf) led by [Prince Kael'thas](Kael'thas Sunstrider). She succeeded in holding the [undead](undead) back, but was swept downriver as the bridge collapsed.
