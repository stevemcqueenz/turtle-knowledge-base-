# Satyrnaar

**Satyrnaar** is located in northeastern [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), northeast of [Splintertree Post](Splintertree Post) and southwest of [Forest Song](Forest Song). It is one of the three Ashenvale [satyr](satyr) settlements, the other two being [Night Run](Night Run) and [Xavian](Xavian). Of the three, Satyrnaar is the most intact and centrally located, boasting several tents among the ruined buildings as shelter for the satyr who live there. It the easiest of the satyr settlements to get to, as it is located just off the main road from Splintertree towards Azshara. As such, it is also the most contested of the three satyr ruins.
