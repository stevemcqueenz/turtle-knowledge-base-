**Olgra** is an [orc](orc) adventurer and quest giver located just outside of [Maras'ethil](Maras'ethil) in [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast). With her companions [Richard](Richard), [Janero](Janero) and [Venuris](Venuris), she previously adventured into the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows) when they were ambushed by the Mirebeast and Richard was injured, leading her and the rest of her party to seek the Shard of Aka'Sha to cure his otherworldly sickness. (ref: [THE MIREBEAST — Everlook Broadcasting Co. Radio Play Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2fhEAUpBBLU))
