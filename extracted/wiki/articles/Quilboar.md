# Quilboar

Ten thousand years ago - during the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients), the mighty demigod, [Agamaggan](Agamaggan), came forth to battle the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion). Though the colossal [boar](boar) fell in combat, his actions helped save [Azeroth](Azeroth) from ruin. Yet over time, in the areas where his blood fell, massive thorn-ridden vines sprouted from the earth. The quilboar - believed to be the mortal offspring of the mighty god, came to occupy these regions and hold them sacred. The heart of these thorn-colonies was known as the "Razorfen". 

After the War of the Ancients and the subsequent [Sundering](Sundering) of the world, the quilboar surroundings became increasingly hostile. Forced to fight for food and precious land against both [Tauren](Tauren) and [Centaur](Centaur), they developed into aggressive and efficient warriors.

The great mass of [Razorfen Kraul](Razorfen Kraul) was conquered by the old crone, [Charlga Razorflank](Charlga Razorflank). Under her rule, the shamanistic quilboar stage attacks on rival tribes as well as [Horde](Horde) villages.

Crafted  from the same mighty vines as Razorfen Kraul, [Razorfen Downs](Razorfen Downs) is the traditional capital city of the quilboar race.

The two feuding Quilboar tribes encountered in the Barrens are the [Razormane](Razormane) and the [Bristleback](Bristleback). The Quilboar occupying Razorfen Downs under the control of [Amnennar the Coldbringer](Amnennar the Coldbringer) are the Death's Head tribe. Quilboar can be found throughout [Mulgore](Mulgore), [The Barrens](Barrens), and [Durotar](Durotar).
