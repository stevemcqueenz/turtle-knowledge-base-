# Anchors

Anchors are of type FRAMEPOINT, and have the following enumeration:

- TOPLEFT
- TOPRIGHT
- BOTTOMLEFT
- BOTTOMRIGHT
- TOP
- BOTTOM
- LEFT
- RIGHT
- CENTER
