# Jeweled Scepter of Sargeras

A powerful artifact of the [Dark Titan](Dark Titan) [Sargeras](Sargeras), used by him to channel dark energies to open [portal](portal)s to new worlds. A [Daemon](Daemon) on the [Shattered Isles](Shattered Isles) held the Scepter until it was killed by [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul)'s henchmen. Without Sargeras to wield it, Ner'Zhul needed the [Skull of Gul'dan](Skull of Gul'dan) for energy, the [Book of Medivh](Book of Medivh) for the knowledge of how to open a portal and the [Eye of Dalaran](Eye of Dalaran) to secure the planet. The Scepter was used to channel the energies of the Skull using the incantations from the Book, but without the Eye, the portals tore the planet apart. [Khadgar](Khadgar) destroyed the Scepter (along with [Teron Gorefiend](Teron Gorefiend)) a few days before he destroyed the portal.
