## Description
Our brothers and sisters of the Grove of the Moon have begun treading a misguided path towards destruction. They have abandoned Cenarius' teachings, desecrated his enclaves and endangered countless other peaceful druids. As a warden of nature, I cannot let this transgression pass.

<name>, the intruders have laid claim to Elun'aran and the temple above, pillaging its sacred ruins for powerful artifacts. Stop them and return to me afterwards.
