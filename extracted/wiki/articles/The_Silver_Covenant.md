## Description
I would spare you the intricacies of [Quel'dorei](Quel'dorei) politics. Nevertheless, it is imperative that you are informed. Our once-united nation has fractured into smaller factions, engaged in a regrettable bickering over influence and power. Witnessing this discord within our own ranks pains my heart, but it is a tempest we must weather.

<She pauses, a sense of resignation in her eyes.>

However, amidst this turmoil, there are those loyal to the broader vision. They have rallied beneath the banner of the [Silver Covenant](Silver Covenant), their camp positioned beyond the city to the west. I implore you to venture there and seek out Commander Braedin, my esteemed right hand. He has been diligently investigating the matter, and your assistance will be met with gratitude.
