## Objectives
Collect a Refined Aqua Core from the water elementals of Ashenvale for Hydromancer Shaleborn at Anchor's Edge in the Barrens.
- [Refined Aqua Core](Refined Aqua Core)

## Description
It has been a long and arduous campaign for all of us. Through many months of struggle I made do with what implements I brought from Boralus, but my stocks are running dangerously low.

If I am to retain my dominion over water long enough to expedite the fleet on our return journey, I will require a new source.

To the northwest lies the foreign, bewitched forest of Ashenvale. I have scryed the presence of strong, yet volatile elemental spirits within the region's heart, in the Mystral Lake.

Travel there, claim a rare Refined Aqua Core from those beings and Kul Tiras will not soon forget your service.
