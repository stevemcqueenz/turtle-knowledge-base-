# Swamplight Manor

**Swamplight Manor** is a small house located in the [Witch Hill](Witch Hill) region of [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh). It is home to the famous cook, ["Swamp Eye" Jarl]("Swamp Eye" Jarl).
