# First Aid

## **Leveling Up**
This is the way to level up first aid to 300.

- **1-40:** Linen Bandage (1 x Linen Cloth) x 50
- **Skill Level 50:** Train Journeymman First Aid
- **41-80:** Heavy Linen Bandage (2 x Linen Cloth) x 50
- **Skill Level 80:** Train Wool Bandages
- **81-115:** Wool Bandage (1 x Wool Cloth) x 50
- **Skill Level 115:** Train Heavy Wool Bandages
- **116-150:** Heavy Wool Bandage (2 x Wool Cloth) x 50
- Buy **[Expert First Aid - Under Wraps](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=16084)** Requires First Aid (125) For Alliance players, this manual is sold by Deneb Walker, in the Arathi Highlands in Stromgarde Keep. For Horde players, this manual is sold by Balai Lok'Wein, in the Dustwallow Marsh in Brackenwall Village. While visiting this vendor you should consider picking up the Manual: Heavy Silk Bandage, Manual: Mageweave Bandage. You'll need it soon and you can save yourself a trip.
- **Skill Level 151:** Train Silk Bandage  
- **151-180:** Silk Bandage (1 x Silk Cloth) x 50
- **Skill Level 180:** Use the Manual: Heavy Silk Bandage to learn to make Heavy Silk Bandage.
- **181-210:** Heavy Silk Bandage (2 x Silk Cloth) x 50
- **Skill Level 210:** Use the Manual: Mageweave Bandage to learn to make Mageweave Bandage.
- **211-240:** Mageweave Bandage (1 x Mageweave Cloth) x 30
- **Skill Level 225:** Alliance must complete the [**Alliance Trauma**](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=6625) quest series. Horde must complete the **[Horde Trauma](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=6623)** quest series.
- **Skill Level 240:** Alliance must speak to **[Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=12939)** to learn to make [**Heavy Mageweave Bandage**](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=8545). Horde must speak to [**Doctor Gregory Victor**](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=12920) to learn to make [**Heavy Mageweave Bandage**](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=8545).
- **241-260:** [**Heavy Mageweave Bandage**](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=8545) (2 x Mageweave Cloth) x 30
- **Skill Level 260:** Alliance speak to **[Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=12939)** to learn to make [**Runecloth Bandage**](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=14529). Horde speak to [**Doctor Gregory Victor**](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=12920) to learn to make [**Runecloth Bandage**](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=14529).
- **261-290:** [**Runecloth Bandage**](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=14529) (1 x Runecloth) x 50
- **Skill Level 290 to 300:** Alliance speak to **[Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=12939)** to learn to make **[Heavy Runecloth Bandage](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=14530)**. Horde speak to  [**Doctor Gregory Victor**](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=12920) to learn to make **[Heavy Runecloth Bandage](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=14530)**.
- **291-300:**  **[Heavy Runecloth Bandage](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=14530)** (2 x Runecloth) x 20

## **Bandage Heals**
This is the amounts each Bandage Heals

- Linen Bandage = 66 damage
- Heavy Linen Bandage = 114 damage
- Wool Bandage = 161 damage
- Heavy Wool Bandage = 301 damage
- Silk BandageSilk Bandage = 400 damage
- Heavy Silk Bandage = 640 damage
- Mageweave Bandage = 800 damage
- Heavy Mageweave Bandage = 1104 damage
- Runecloth Bandage = 1360 damage
- Heavy Runecloth Bandage = 2000 damage
