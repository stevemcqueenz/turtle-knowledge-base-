# Forgotten Coast

The **Forgotten Coast** is a portion of the western coast in [Feralas](Feralas) bordering the [Veiled Sea](Veiled Sea). A dock exists here where the player can board the *[Feathermoon Ferry](Feathermoon Ferry)*, which then will take you to [Sardor Isle](Sardor Isle).
