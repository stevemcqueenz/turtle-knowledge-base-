# Lake Mereldar

[Lake Mereldar](Lake Mereldar) is a large lake in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), located south of [Corin's Crossing](Corin's Crossing).  This polluted water body is infested by corrupted [water elemental](water elemental)s.
