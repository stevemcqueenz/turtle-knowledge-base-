# Great Sundering

The **Great Sundering** was a cataclysmic event which reshaped the world approximately 10,000 years ago, at the end of the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients), when the [Well of Eternity](Well of Eternity) was destroyed.

Prior to the Sundering, there was only one continent on Azeroth, referred to as [Kalimdor](Kalimdor).  In the catastrophic explosion, eighty percent of the land mass was destroyed, leaving the major continents and scattered islands known today: [Northrend](Northrend), [Undermine](Undermine), the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), and the remnant still referred to as [Kalimdor](Kalimdor).

The site of the Well of Eternity became a swirling vortex of power, known as the [Maelstrom](Maelstrom).
