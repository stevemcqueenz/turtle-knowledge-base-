# Undercity (faction)

Undercity is the faction for the capital city of the Forsaken Undead, [Undercity](Undercity). It is located in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), at the northern edge of the Eastern Kingdoms. The city proper is located under the ruins of the historical City of Lordaeron. To enter it, you will walk through the ruined outer defenses of Lordaeron and the abandoned throneroom, until you reach one of three elevators leading underground.

## Reputation
[Ralston Farnsley](Ralston Farnsley) has the Undercity repeatable cloth quests used by non-Undead Horde players to obtain the right to ride a [skeletal horse](skeletal horse) at exalted.

Surrounding zones [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills), [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest), and Tirisfal Glades have the most quests to earn reputation with Undercity.
