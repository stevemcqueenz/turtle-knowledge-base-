# Nightsaber

Large cat used as a primary [mount](mount) for the [Night Elves](Night_elf). The Nightsaber is similar in appearance to a real-world [tiger](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiger), but is black with white stripes or spotted like a [cheetah](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheetah) or [leopard](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leopard). The [Frostsaber](Frostsaber) is a color variation of the Nightsaber - the [Frostsaber](Frostsaber) is typically white with black stripes.

# Epic Mount
The epic Tiger mount is bought in [Darnassus](Darnassus).  The PVP mount is the Black War Tiger.  A rare epic nightsaber is the [Zulian Tiger](Zulian Tiger), a rare drop from a [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub) boss.
