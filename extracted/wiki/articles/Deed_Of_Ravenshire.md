# Deed Of Ravenshire

## **Deed Of Ravenshire**
The Deed of Ravenshire is a quest item needed for the quest "[*The Deed to Raven*shire](https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=40966&__cf_chl_tk=aPa.XtaTZCsPVAp5nvPExdRGpBQnb5gWs0XWRbdOlkI-1719182096-0.0.1.1-3604)" collected from the dungeon [*Gilneas City*](Gilneas City). The item appears on the table right next to the Family Chest, right after killing both Lady and Lord Harlow.
