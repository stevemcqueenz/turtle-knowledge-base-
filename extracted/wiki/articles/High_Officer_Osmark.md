High Officer **Osmark Dryrock** (ref: [Dossier: Isiden Perenolde](Dossier: Isiden Perenolde)) is a [human](human) located in the [Dryrock Pit](Dryrock Pit) in [Gilneas](Gilneas). He is the overseer of the prison compound and a well-respected Greymane royalist officer. As a token of honor, he was given the [Signet of Silverlaine](Signet of Silverlaine), which had been seized during a siege on [Ravenshire](Ravenshire) along with other possessions belonging to [Baron Caliban Silverlaine](Baron Caliban Silverlaine) and [House Silverlaine](House Silverlaine).
