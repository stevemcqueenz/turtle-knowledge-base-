The **bronze dragonflight** is a draconic organization led by the eponymous [bronze dragon](bronze dragon)s to uphold their creed of managing and watching over the flow of magic on Azeroth. After the ordering of the planet, the titan [Aman'Thul](Aman'Thul) blessed [Nozdormu](Nozdormu) and his flight with the power to guard time itself and police the ever-spinning pathways of fate and destiny. (ref: [Charge of the Dragonflights](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Charge_of_the_Dragonflights))

## Culture
### Nomenclature
Members of the bronze dragonflight occasionally have names ending in "dormu" for males, or "dormi" for females, though [exceptions](Chronormu) exist. Sometimes terms relating to time find their way into their names as well.

### Membership
The bronze dragonflight is primarily made up of, as the name implies, bronze dragonkin. These include the bronze dragons themselves, but also bronze-scaled variants of drakonid and dragonspawn.

## History
### The Charge of the Dragonflights
Once the [Titan](Titan)s felt satisfied with their creation upon [Azeroth](Azeroth), they left to roam the cosmos in search of new worlds on which they would shape order from chaos, creating new life throughout a myriad of worlds. Before their departure from Azeroth however, the Titans empowered five dragons to become stewards of that world. Their purpose: to guard Azeroth as the Dragon Aspects, protecting the Titans' creation.

[Aman'Thul](Aman'Thul), the Highfather of the [Pantheon](Pantheon), bestowed a portion of his cosmic power upon the massive bronze dragon, Nozdormu. The Highfather empowered Nozdormu to guard time itself and police the ever-spinning pathways of fate and destiny. The stoic, honorable Nozdormu became known as the Timeless One.
