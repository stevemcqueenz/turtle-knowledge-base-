# Lord Valthalak

-Abilities**
  Looks like a dragonkin from BWL. Does heavy melee dmg, shadow damage, fire damage and summons spirit assasins. Lord Valethak will go into a rage spamming shadow attacks around 30-40%, he will stop summoning adds from this point. AT 10% he will start launching shadowblot volleys around just like KAzzak does. The spirits have a stunning beam that will end up in a hugh dmg attack (99% of the time instant kill) if they are not killed before the spell ends, wich takes about 10 sec. Also the adds transfer life to the boss.

  - Alliance tactics**
  Kill the adds as soon as they pop-up. A hunter selecting the targets works great. Take a tank and put them in a group with a paladin for devotion aura and a warlock for the extra health. You need to have both a priest and a druid for the buffs (don't forget to let the priest do shadow resistance buff on all). Since you are going to need high dps at the boss and actually need to get there it's recommanded to take a rogue and a mage to fill up 2 of the other spots for crowd control and damage. Rogues don't forget to use Wound Poison to reduce the healing from the adds and keep in mind that a lot of his shadowspells can be interupted with kick. Mages and warlocks aoe the crap out of the adds. The last 2 spots you can fill in randomly, keep in mind though that although a second tank might make the rest of the run go easier, that you need a lot of healing and dps at this boss. My suggestion is a second paladin (they can nuke the undead adds, help with the healing and offtank) and a hunter (great at shooting down those adds and they have a high dps in general). Also drain Lord Valethak's mana it can be done quite easily and it will make him cast a few spells less.

Lord Valthalak is a Dragonspawn summoned in UBRS by a Brazier of Beckoning.  He is the last boss in the [Dungeon_Set_2](Dungeon_Set_2) quest line.
