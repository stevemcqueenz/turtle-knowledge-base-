# Eye of Sargeras

An artifact found in the [Tomb of Sargeras](Tomb of Sargeras) by [Illidan Stormrage](Illidan Stormrage).  The artifact was sought by both Illidan and [Gul'dan](Gul'dan), the former gaining it only through the aid of the [Naga](Naga), the latter being torn apart by crazed demons. We don't know if the Eye was Sargeras's actual eye, or simply an artifact which held his power after the battle with [Aegwynn](Aegwynn).

Eye of Sargeras was used by Illidan in an attempt to destroy the [Frozen Throne](Frozen Throne), but the intervention of [Malfurion](Malfurion) and [Maiev](Maiev) destroyed the Eye instead. We can only hope its powers did not survive.
