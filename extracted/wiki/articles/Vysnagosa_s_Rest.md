# Vysnagosa's Rest

**Vysnagosa's Rest** is an island found off the [Whispering Shores](Whispering Shores) of [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast). After the [Blue dragonflight](Blue dragonflight) called down an arcane meteor that crashed in [Starshard Cradle](Starshard Cradle), (ref: [Moriena from the Turtle WoW Community Discord](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/895751435797536788/1496783160149540866) - "its a meteor called down by the blue dragons") the island was occupied by draconic forces led by the blue dragon [Avranagos](Avranagos).
