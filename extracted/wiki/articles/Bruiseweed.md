# Bruiseweed

-Bruiseweed** is an [Herb](Herb) that can be gathered with [Herbalism](Herbalism). 

## Source
Bruiseweed can be found near structures or occasionally on hillsides. It can also be found in limited supply at [vendor](vendor)s.
