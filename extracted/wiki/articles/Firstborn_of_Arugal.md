The **Firstborn of Arugal** is a rare worgen that can be found wandering the northern farms of [Gilneas](Gilneas) near the [Greymane Wall](Greymane Wall).

## Loot
- [Old Hunter's Boots](Old Hunter's Boots)
- [Explorer's Shoulders](Explorer's Shoulders)
- [Arugal Family Sigil](Arugal Family Sigil)

## Notes
- In comparison to the worgen mobs that occupy northern Gilneas, the Firstborn of Arugal is significantly higher-leveled than them and may catch players unaware during questing.
