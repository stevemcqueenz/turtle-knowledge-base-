-Akh Z'ador** is a [draenei](draenei) [quest giver](quest giver) near [Ursolan](Ursolan) in [Azshara](Azshara). A [riftwalker](riftwalker) and member of the [Tribe of Sanv](Tribe of Sanv), he led his fellow tribesmen and [another tribe](Lost Ones) to Azeroth on the command of his chieftain, [Sanv Tas'dal](Sanv Tas'dal), after the destruction wrought by the [orcish Horde](orcish Horde) on the world of [Draenor](Draenor). Though the draenei established settlements in the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows), some years after the draenei's arrival on Azeroth, Akh Z'ador riftwalked in search of his chieftain and the remainder of his clan. (ref:  Prior to leaving, he directed his apprentice, [Kas'tal](Kas'tal), to survey suspicious fluctuations near the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal).<ref>[quest: Novice In A Barren Land]) To his horror, while riftwalking he discovered that the clan had become servants of the Burning Legion. While he was processing what he had witnessed, Sanv Tas'dal located him within the Nether and interrupted his focus, throwing him back through the Nether and ending up in Azshara.
