-Zul'jin** (also spelled **Zuljin** (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* game manual, *[Legends of the Land](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Zuljin)*, Zuljin)) was a warlord of the [Amani](Amani tribe) forest trolls since before the [Second War](Second War).

## Biography
### Second War
The feared, rogue Troll known as Zuljin had gained a great amount of notoriety over the years. Known best for his daring raids on guarded Elven villages, Zul'jin brought together many of the nomadic Troll warparties and forged them into an intimidating fighting force. The Trolls revered Zuljin and followed his leadership unconditionally. When approached by Doomhammer about joining up with the Horde, Zuljin initially declined. But with the increased tensions between the Trolls and Elves due to the coming war, Zuljin knew that only an alliance with the Orcs would save his people. (ref: 

During the Second War, a band of Human soldiers captured a war party led by Zul'jin and took him and his party to a secret prison near the township of [Hillsbrad](Hillsbrad Fields). On Doomhammer's orders, the Horde freed Zul'jin from the prison and returned him to the trolls' own encampment nearby.<ref>[Raid at Hillsbrad (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Raid_at_Hillsbrad_(WC2_Orc))) Eager to take revenge upon the humans who imprisoned them, Zul'jin and his Trolls then agreed to aid the Horde, supplying axethrowers and destroyers to the Horde war effort. (ref: [Southshore (WC2 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Southshore_(WC2_Orc)))

Following the Second War, Zul'jin disappeared. Many of the Revantusk tribe hold hope that he will one day return, (ref:  [48] [Cannibalistic Cousins](Cannibalistic Cousins)) (ref: [Huntsman Markhor#Quotes](Huntsman Markhor#Quotes)) while the Hakkari believe him to be dead. (ref: Captured Hakkari Zealot says: It shall NEVER happen. We are legion. We are united. Where are your heroes? Vol'jin sits in sanctuary at the side of the young Warchief, unaware. Zul'jin missing, probably dead.)

## Notes and trivia
- Prior to his initial introduction in *Turtle WoW* in patch 1.16, Zul'jin was mentioned several times in the game:
  - Mystic Yayo'jin of the [Revantusk tribe](Revantusk) said: *"If Zul'jin were here, he would have destroyed the whole lot of them with a flick of his wrist - alas, Zul'jin has not yet returned and we are left to our own devices."*
  - Huntsman Markhor of the Revantusk said: *"Zul'jin shall return one day. Mark my words. Until then, we all hold our positions and do what we can to keep the forest troll population intact."*
  - The Captured Hakkari Zealot on Yojamba Isle said: ''"The young races? The infants? Humans? Orcs? Dwarves? You expect to defeat the Hakkari? To destroy a GOD? It shall NEVER happen. We are legion. We are united. Where are your heroes? Vol'jin sits in sanctuary at the side of the young Warchief, unaware. Zul'jin missing, probably dead."
- Troll players in *World of Warcraft* who use the /charge emote will sometimes proclaim "For Zul'jin!" Similarly, Troll Headhunter (Warcraft III) and Troll Batrider (Warcraft III) in *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* yell "Vengeance for Zul'jin" and "Flames for Zul'jin", respectively, despite hailing from the Darkspear tribe.
- Zul'jin was removed from Turtle WoW at an unknown point.
