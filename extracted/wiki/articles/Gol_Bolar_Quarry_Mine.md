# Gol'Bolar Quarry Mine

-Gol'Bolar Quarry Mine** is a deep mine located in the heart of [Gol'Bolar Quarry](Gol'Bolar Quarry). It was once a [dwarven](Dwarf) excavation site but, in the middle of a dig, the dwarves uncovered one of the underground caverns belonging to the [trogg](trogg)s. The troggs pushed the dwarves out, and also took over the quarry outside of the mine. As opposed to the lower-level troggs in the outer quarry, this mine holds some of their strongest numbers which includes their leader, [Hammerspine](Hammerspine). Enter with caution!
