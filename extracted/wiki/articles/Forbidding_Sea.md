# Forbidding Sea

[The Forbidding Sea](The Forbidding Sea) is a largely unexplored portion of the [Great Sea](Great Sea) along the eastern coast of the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms), sometimes noted by travelers on the coasts of [the Hinterlands](the Hinterlands) and [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows). Most of these waters are made by many impassable mountain ranges on the long the eastern coast of the continent, [east of Khaz Modan](east of Khaz Modan).
