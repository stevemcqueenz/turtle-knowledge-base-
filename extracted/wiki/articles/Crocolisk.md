# Crocolisk

Long-snouted reptile with six short legs and a long tail. Crawls low to the ground, more like a [snake](snake) than a [raptor](raptor). Usually found near water, as it swims skillfully.  They appear to be related to the primitive [Diemetradon](Diemetradon)s.  Found in the [Wailing Caverns](Wailing Caverns), [Durotar](Durotar), [Loch Modan](Loch Modan), the [Wetlands](Wetlands), the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows), [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh), [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub), and even [Stormwind](Stormwind) (as the elusive sewer beast).   

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Have high armor, medium damage and low HP.
- Eats only Meat and Fish.
- Can learn [Cower](Cower), [Bite](Bite), and [Cower](Cower).  Crocolisks have yet to get their unique pet skill.
- Not very popular as a hunter pet.
