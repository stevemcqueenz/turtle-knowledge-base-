# Northrend

-Northrend** is the icy, northernmost continent of the world of [Azeroth](Azeroth), and the source of the evil [Scourge](Scourge). It is also the home of Icecrown Glacier, the seat of the malevolent [Lich King](Lich King).

## General info
Often called "the roof of the world", Northrend is a frozen wasteland that lies far to the north. As with the other continents, it was once part of the original [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) landmass that was broken apart during the [War of the Ancients](War of the Ancients). The land that became Northrend drifted far from Azeroth's temperate zone, which killed off a large portion of the remaining wildlife.

A particularly hardy breed of insectoid-humanoids, the [Aqir](Aqir), had survived the destruction.  After thousands of years of evolution, the Aqir became the [Nerubians](Nerubian): a sinister, shadowy race of spider-men.  While a few [humans](humans) and [trolls](trolls) eked out survival in small encampments, their presence was no match for the Nerubian Empire.

Northrend also contains [The Dragonblight](The Dragonblight): an enormous ice plain covered with the bones of thousands of [dragon](dragon)s. As they near death, the great beasts fly to Northrend so that they may die with their ancestors. The [Blue Dragonflight](Blue Dragonflight), led by the enigmatic [Malygos](Malygos), watches over the Dragonblight to ensure that the remains are undisturbed. The blue dragons were successful in their task for many thousands of years, until the coming of the [Lich King](Lich King)...

 After [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul) the [Shaman](Shaman) was captured and tortured by [Kil'jaeden](Kil'jaeden), the demonlord transformed the ancient [Orc](Orc) into a dark being of immense power. Now called the Lich King, Ner'zhul was imprisoned within a block of ice and cast back into [Azeroth](Azeroth). Ner'zhul's prison landed at the base of the mighty [Icecrown Glacier](Icecrown Glacier), by far the largest glacier in Azeroth. From this secret location, the Lich King used his telepathic powers to enslave many of the native life forms, including Ice Trolls and [Wendigo](Wendigo). With these servants, Ner'zhul created a small army for himself. The Nerubians, aware of the Lich King's threat to themselves and the world at large, launched an attack on Ner'zhul's forces. Thus began the [War of the Spider](War of the Spider).

After ten long years of war, the Nerubians were eventually defeated by the Lich King's endless supply of undead warriors and aid from the Legion's Nathrezim. Most of the Nerubians were slain and reanimated as soldiers in the Undead army, while those who remained alive continued a guerrilla war against the lord of the dead. Satisfied that Northrend was his, Ner'zhul began consolidating his forces for the corruption of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron). To this end, the Scourge stole many sets of remains from the Dragonblight, which were then reborn as lethal [Frost Wyrm](Frost Wyrm)s.

By the end of the [Third War](Third War), Northrend had seen the corruption of [Prince Arthas](Prince Arthas), who had followed the [Nathrezim](Nathrezim) [Mal'Ganis](Mal'Ganis) to the cold north in an attempt to stop the [Scourge](Scourge), as well as an invasion attempt by Illidan's [Naga](Naga) and [Blood Elf](Blood Elf) forces, who had hoped to destroy Icecrown and the Lich King. This invasion was [thwarted](Battle of the Four Obelisks) by Arthas and [Anub'arak](Anub'arak) however, and Ner'zhul successfully merged with Arthas to free the Lich King from the Frozen Throne. The Lich King now holds complete domination over Northrend, and the living dare not tread on the frozen continent, especially after a disastrous expedition of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade) which ended in the expeditionary force being decimated.
