# Wiley

-"Wiley"** is a human who was raised in [Westfall](Westfall), Eastern Kingdoms. While the majority of his past isn't public knowledge, there are several records few and far in between detailing excerpts assumed to be of his life. 

Wiley was born in an unknown region in Year 596 of the King's Calendar, but as an infant was taken by his aunt to live a new life in Westfall, hopefully free from the claws of war that grasped the eastern territories of the King's Lands. His aunt raised him to be an upstanding member of society and even a soldier like her brother and his father, whom Wiley knew nothing about. Wiley's aunt had him taught at the Westfall Church before its shutdown, where until the age of 10, he followed a path relatively free of danger or any real risk to his life. 

But Wiley yearned for a life of action, and coupled with his aunt's degrading living situation in the countryside, he wanted to find another way to keep her and himself financially secure. Wiley and his best friend Aaron began to skip classes at the church, sneaking off with other mutual friends to mess around and loiter on the farmers' property and plots around the land. At the age of 12, Aaron confessed to Wiley that he joined the Defias Brotherhood -- which was still early in its creation -- and asked Wiley to join him. Wiley was unsure of a life of crime. He wanted to be rich one day to take care of his aunt, and hopefully find his father -- but the idea of breaking the law put fear in his heart. He declined, and Aaron's new Defias mentor ordered the recruits to make a lesson out of Wiley to show to Aaron what would happen if he ever defected. Wiley was called a noble sympathizer and left bruised and beaten nearly to death by the other kids, left missing for days. From that day on, Wiley never saw Aaron again.

Wiley was found by an older man by the name of Garec -- a retired Kul Tiran sailor, living in Westfall to retire with what little he had from his military days. Since Wiley had no one else in his life, Garec took it upon himself to raise the boy. Wiley felt betrayed by Aaron, and enraged at the Defias for ripping his friend away from his life, motivated to take the fight to them directly. While Garec disagreed and told Wiley that he was too young, he promised he would train him to fend for himself. 

While Wiley learned how to use standard-grade military weaponry by the young age of 14, he favored a bow and arrow, if not for its accuracy but its lack of danger and separation from ever getting nearly killed again. Wiley took up hunting to feed Garec as he grew older and unable to feed himself, returning the favor of being raised by doing good for his new father figure.

At the age of 15, Wiley enlisted in the King's Armies of Stormwind. While he was young, the kid showed promise, and his commanding officer noted that the boy had several qualities worth praise and recognition: 

-"Despite his childish antics within the barracks, Recruit [REDACTED] shows courage, bravery, and honor on the battlefield and in the line of duty. While he isn't as martially inclined as his fellow soldiers yet, the boy is exceptional with a bow and an arrow. We will watch his tenure with great curiosity."*

- Commanding Officer Breckensworth, of the Stormwind Army (Year 611 of the King's Calendar)

#### Present Day (Since 25 ADP/617 KC)
It wasn't long before Wiley was recommended by his CO to become a Private at age 17, and then a Squire at Age 19, before achieving Knighthood status at the young age of 22, by the year 618 of the King's Calendar. 

For the next section, it is almost entirely unknown (ICly, as well as OOCly) where Wiley's whereabouts have been up until recent -- for the past five years, he has not been seen, spotted, or recognized in public. 

Wiley served as a Knight of Stormwind's armies for three years, before being dishonorably discharged under command of his Knight Captain: 

-"Knight [REDACTED] has breached the sworn oath of knighthood by violating the trust, honor, and respect of His Majesty and His Armies. He is indefinitely, indisputably, dishonorably discharged as of 621 K.C."* 

- Knight-Captain Dalonath, of the Stormwind Army (Year 621 King's Calendar)

Without any proper jobs or offers for consistent pay, Wiley begrudgingly turned to a life of crime, eventually learning to enjoy it as the only way he could remember his childhood best friend by, and without any other true option given his legal predicament.
