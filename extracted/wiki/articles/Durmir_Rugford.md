-Durmir Rugford** is a [dwarf](dwarf) quest giver located at the [South Gate Outpost](South Gate Outpost) in [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh). A retired mountaineer, many years ago he founded [Rugford's Mountain Rest](Rugford's Mountain Rest) in the southeastern mountains of Dun Morogh and offered respite and rest for his fellow mountaineers of Ironforge. However, the tavern was one day suddenly overrun by sinister Dark Iron marauders sowing chaos in the dwarven kingdom. Durmir managed to flee to the [South Gate Outpost](South Gate Outpost) with minor injuries, where he seeks aid to recover his belongings left behind.
