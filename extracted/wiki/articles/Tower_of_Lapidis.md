# Tower of Lapidis

The **Tower of Lapidis** is a mage tower in eastern Lapidis Isle. The tower was built by [Arch Hydromancer Lapidis](Arch Hydromancer Lapidis) after the [Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet](Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet) ran ground on the island through the use of Kul Tiran workers and his own water elementals. With his great power, the tower was constructed much before the town of [Caelan's Rest](Caelan's Rest) had gotten its footing, and the Arch Hydromancer proceeded to ally himself with both the trolls of [Zul'Hazu](Zul'Hazu) and the [Gor'dosh Ogres](Gor'dosh Ogres), turning on the Kul Tirans. The tower, guarded by [Twisted Water Elemental](Twisted Water Elemental)s, now looms over Caelan's Rest like a great evil that could destroy them at any minute.
