## Objectives
Infiltrate Stormwrought Castle on Balor and find out who is conspiring with the Stormreaver orcs. Report to Verona Gillian in the SI:7 Outpost on Balor when done.
- [Nethercaller Mahlgus](Nethercaller Mahlgus) slain
- [Warlord Jawbreaker](Warlord Jawbreaker) slain
- [Stormreaver Insignia](Stormreaver Insignia) (20)

## Description
From what I gathered from Spickerspan's findings, I don't believe we are simply dealing with the Stormreaver orcs and their demon friends alone at this point. An operation of this magnitude is too grand for a scattered clan from the Second War. There has to be more at play here. Who else has their grimy little fingers involved in this is our next step. Ascend the island, enter the yard of Stormwrought Castle and thin out their numbers. Search the area for crucial intel we might be missing. Dispatch any high ranking officer you come across, we must be swift and deadly. I myself will send notice to Master Shaw to rally as many agents as we can. Personally I do not expect the King to aid us in this tragedy.

Stay sharp, <class>, I expect sufficient results.
