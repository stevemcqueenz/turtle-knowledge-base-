# Misty Pine Refuge

[Misty Pine Refuge](Misty Pine Refuge) is an outpost building of the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn). It is located in central [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), and currently houses only one agent of the organization - [Father Gavin](Father Gavin).
