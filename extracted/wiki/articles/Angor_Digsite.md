# Angor Digsite

| field | value |
|---|---|
| 1 | Borrin Gloombeard |
| icon | DarkIron Male |

The **Angor Digsite** is an excavation site located in the mountains above [Angor Fortress](Angor Fortress) in the northern [Badlands](Badlands). [Dark Iron dwarves](Dark Iron dwarves), led by [Borrin Gloombeard](Borrin Gloombeard), are scouring the hilltop digging for valuable forgotten artifacts, risking ancient curses.
