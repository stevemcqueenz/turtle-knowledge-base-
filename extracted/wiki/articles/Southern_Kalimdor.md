-Southern Kalimdor** (ref: *World of Warcraft: Game Manual*, pg. 167) (ref: [Bluffwatcher's gossip about fishing in Mulgore](Bluffwatcher#Quotes)) is the southernmost region of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), south of [central Kalimdor](central Kalimdor).

## Notes and trivia
- In Southern Kalimdor there are many areas where [silithid](silithid) occupy the land. The most occupied areas are [Silithus](Silithus) and [Tanaris](Tanaris). The silithid are also seen in [Un'Goro Crater](Un'Goro Crater), the southern [Barrens](Barrens), [Feralas](Feralas), and in the [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles).
- It appears on most maps that Feralas and Thousand Needles are its northernmost zones.
- [Bluffwatcher](Bluffwatcher) guards oddly say that [Mulgore](Mulgore) is part of Southern Kalimdor.
