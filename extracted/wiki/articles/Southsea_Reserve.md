## Description
Oh, to be back on the sea... back on the sea and on the way home. It's been hell just to be left to roam this small part of the island. The only thing we sailors have is grog and each other's company.

Rum is fine, but we've been craving more variety. This is where you're going to come in. The Southsea Pirates have been rather known for their own Southsea Reserve recipe which only they can make. That is, until you go and steal the recipe.

They are currently holed up on a strand of sand called the Southsea Sandbar and the small neighboring patch of sand known as Distillery Island. It's practically a damn factory of the stuff. They have an entire trained crew working around the clock pumping this stuff reserve out.

It's down in the southwest off the coast of Lapidis Isle. You should see several of their ships docked there. The one who invented the recipe is named 'Moonshine' Marty. You'll get it from him.
