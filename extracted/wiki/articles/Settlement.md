# Settlement

**Settlements** are areas were people gather to live, going from small villages of a couple of dozens of people to bustling cities of thousands of souls.

## City
*Cities* are large settlements were people live that are usually much bigger than towns. Normally surrounded by tall walls, cities are protected by their own armies, especially if it is the [capital](capital) of a [nation](nation). It has multiples services, though not always included in all cities, such as [bank](bank)s, [flight path](flight path)s, [inn](inn)s, [auction house](auction house)s and much more. As of patch 1.9.0, all faction capitals should have an [auction house](auction house).

In *[Warcraft III](Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos)* "Cityscape" is a tileset used in single-player and on custom maps.

## Village/Town
*Villages* or *towns* are not large enough to be cities but they have a good amount of buildings and citizens. The distinction between a town and a small settlement can be fuzzy though towns usually have some vendors and flight paths but no banks or auction houses. Inns are often found in them, but it is not a guarantee. Settlements can be found along the roads toward raids, cities and other regions. Other names include *hamlets* etc. In human lands, its denizens are known as [villagers](commoner).

In *[Warcraft III](Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos)* "Village" and "Village Fall" are tilesets used in single-player and on custom maps.

## Camp
*Camps* are small settlements that lack any significant building, though they normally have a flight path. Sometimes, if they are big enough, camps can have [forge](forge)s and other [profession](profession) buildings. Lots of different races have camps scattered all around Azeroth. Other names include *posts*, or *forward camps*. [Tent](Tent)s may be used to set up a camp.
