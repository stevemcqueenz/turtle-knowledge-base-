# Royal Quarter

The **Royal Quarter** of the [Undercity](Undercity) contains the throne room where the Dark Lady [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner), and her general the [dreadlord](dreadlord) [Varimathras](Varimathras) control their burgeoning empire of undeath.  It is protected by a squad of elite royal deathguards.  It can be found right next to the [Apothecarium](Apothecarium).

This is also the home to the [Battlemaster](Battlemaster)s, the NPCs who provide a means to access [Battlegrounds](Battlegrounds).
