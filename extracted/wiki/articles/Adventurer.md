# Adventurer

Adventurers is the lore name for Player Characters.
Although unnamed and numerous, it is thanks to these brave individuals Azeroth was saved numerous of times in recent (and past) times.

## Races and classes
The adventurers mostly come from either of the two larger factions on [Azeroth](Azeroth),being the [Horde](Horde) and the [Alliance](Alliance). 
As such, most of them are of the 5 larger races of each faction.
Namely [Humans](Humans), [Orcs](Orcs), [Forsaken](Forsaken), [Darkspear Trolls](Darkspear Trolls),[Dwarves](Dwarves) ,[Gnomes](Gnomes), [High Elves](High Elves), [Night elves](Night elves), [Goblins](Goblins) and [Tauren](Tauren). 

## Loyalty
Adventurers are mostly loyal to their faction, [Horde](Horde) or [Alliance](Alliance) , but are willing to work for other neutral organizations such as the [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn), [Booty Bay](Booty Bay), etc. for either gold or faction rewards.

## Power
Where the regiment of Orc grunts or Human footmen fail, the adventurers succeed. 
Many are one-man armies, being able to defeat a large number of enemies, whilst even getting stronger. 

## Known actions
Though uncertain why or how, the adventurers saved the day numerous of times in recent times. Examples ranging from stopping Orgrimmar's corruption from [Ragefire Chasm](Ragefire Chasm) to stopping the prisoners escaping in the [Stockades](Stockades).
Also they have aided in re-opening the [Gates of Ahn'Qiraj](Gates of Ahn'Qiraj) and fighting off the Scourge during the [Scourge Invasion](Scourge Invasion).
