# Barleycrest Farmstead

The **Barleycrest Farmstead** is an abandoned farm located in the central [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches). Due to the resurgence of the [Dragonmaw clan](Dragonmaw clan), the Wildhammer farmers of the Grim Reaches have evacuated to [Dun Kithas](Dun Kithas), their fields left fallow. (ref: [quest: Provisions for War])
