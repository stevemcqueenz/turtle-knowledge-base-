# Park

-The Park** is located in the western corner of [Stormwind City](Stormwind City). It was once a district devoted to leisure activities for Stormwind's populace. It has recently been donated as a refuge for visiting [night elves](night elves), who find the comforting presence of nature a welcome respite from the vast stone thoroughfares of Stormwind proper. It is currently the only place in the [Eastern Kingdoms](Eastern Kingdoms) where [druid](druid) trainers reside. The night elves are also working to create a [Moonwell](Moonwell) in the center of the park square.

To get to the Park, you can head southwest from [Cathedral Square](Cathedral Square) or northwest from the [Mage Quarter](Mage Quarter).
