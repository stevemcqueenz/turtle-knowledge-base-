# Hidden Path

Those just beginning to follow the path of the [Shaman](Shaman) are tasked with finding this secret route through the mountains that border the [Valley of Trials](Valley of Trials). Aside from providing [Shaman](Shaman)s with access to the mystical [Spirit Rock](Spirit Rock), the **Hidden Path** provides a good view of [the Barrens](the Barrens) from its highest point. There is also a low point that can be used to get to [Ratchet](Ratchet) or [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar), and could be used by Alliance to get into the Valley of Trials.
