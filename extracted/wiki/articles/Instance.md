# Instance

{| style="background:transparent;"
|-
|

||&nbsp;
|
**World Dungeons** typically have sub-areas called "**Instance Dungeons**", or just "**Instances**". These Instances are special areas in the World of Warcraft where your group or raid party is able to interact with a dungeon privately- you and your party are given your own 'version' of the dungeon upon entering the [Instance Portal](Instance Portal) with its own set of [Mob](Mob)s that are separate from those of any other groups that may be in the same dungeon. 

Instance dungeons tend to feature the most difficult and rewarding content, both in terms of enemies and items, but also in terms of level design. Getting through an instance requires a well-trained and well-balanced group of players that are of an appropriate level for the challenge.
