The **Wetlands** is a large, wet zone below [Stonewrought Dam](Stonewrought Dam) and north of [Loch Modan](Loch Modan), covered with small rivers, lakes, and ponds. Bordered on three sides by mountains, the Wetlands are open to the ocean to the west. Adventurers seeking a route to the Wetlands must either come through sieged [Dun Algaz](Dun Algaz) from Loch Modan or cross the damaged [Thandol Span](Thandol Span) from [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). The northern slopes of Wetlands are generally classed within mountains of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan).

The Wetlands is a vast, cold swamp. Mists cloak the ground, and the overcast sky makes the land a uniform gray. Many Ironforge strongholds once stood in this region, but war has reduced them to rubble. Wild creatures, murlocs and renegade orcs now control the land. Menethil Harbor, a city on the west coast, is the Alliance's primary presence in this dreary region.

## Geography
The Wetlands contain the [Dragonmaw Retreat](Dragonmaw Retreat) instanced dungeon, and the raid [Grim Batol](Grim Batol) is planned to be located here. There are two micro dungeons, the [Dragonmaw Gates](Dragonmaw Gates) and [Dun Modr](Dun Modr), that can also be found in this zone. No battlegrounds exist in this area.
