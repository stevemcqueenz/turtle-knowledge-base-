# Turtle

Similar in appearance but much greater in size than their real-life counterparts, [World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft) **turtles** range in [level](level) from level 16 to over 50 [Elite](Elite). The most important distinction between the different kinds of turtles is which drop [Turtle Scale](Turtle Scale)s and which do not. In general, turtles of a higher level have a better chance of dropping Turtle Scales. The turtles in Dustwallow rarely drop scales while the ones in Tanaris may drop two or three at a time. The turtles in Thousand Needles never drop scales. [Farming](Farming) Turtle Scales is the primary reason to kill a turtle.

Turtles can also be found in the [Lushwater Oasis](Lushwater Oasis) in the [Barrens](Barrens), in the [Blackfathom Deeps](Blackfathom Deeps) [instance](Instance) ([Ashenvale](Ashenvale)), just east of [Southshore](Southshore) by a shallow river which continues up to [Alterac](Alterac), scattered in the Shimmering Flats of [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles), swimming in the murky waters of [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh),  adorning the beaches of [Tanaris](Tanaris), walking in the shallow water of [Marauadon](Marauadon), gathered in the coasts of [Azshara](Azshara), and around [Lordamere Lake](Lordamere Lake) near the [Dalaran Ruins](Dalaran).

Tortoises (land turtles) are pretty much interchangable with turtles in the game, since they all look the same. You can assume it is a tortoise, if it doesn't appear near a large body of water (lake, river, or sea).

Some turtles can grow to enormous sizes. During the Second War, the Orcs used them as submersible war machines. Their shells were so large that several Orcs could fit inside them, and they attached goblin-made rockets to the top of their shells.

Types of turtles:
- Snapjaw
- Sparkleshell
- Mudrock
- Coralshell

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Have high armor, medium HP, and low damage.
- Eats only Fruit and Fungus.
- Can learn [Growl](Growl),[ Cower]( Cower), and [Bite](Bite).  
- Not often seen as a Hunter pet.
