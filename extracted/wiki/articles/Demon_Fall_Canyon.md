# Demon Fall Canyon

-Demon Fall Canyon** is a [fel](fel)-corrupted canyon located in [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), south of [Felfire Hill](Felfire Hill) and just east of [Demon Fall Ridge](Demon Fall Ridge). It earned its name after the titanic clash between the [demon](demon)ic [pit lord](pit lord) [Mannoroth](Mannoroth), and the two [orc](orc)s [Grommash Hellscream](Grommash Hellscream) and [Thrall](Thrall). The pit lord succeeded in knocking Thrall out, but he was not quick enough to escape Grom's axe, which cleaved into Mannoroth's chest.

The demon's death created an explosion of fire and light, which tore the life from Grom as well. The orcs named the site of his sacrifice thusly and many would make pilgrimage there to honor the warrior who had liberated the orc people from the [Burning Legion](Burning Legion)'s [curse](Blood-curse). A [monument to Grom Hellscream](monument to Grom Hellscream) was also erected in his memory. The canyon is now populated by many hostile lesser demons.
