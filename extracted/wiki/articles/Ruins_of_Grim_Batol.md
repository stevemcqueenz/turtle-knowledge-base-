# Ruins of Grim Batol

The **Ruins of Grim Batol** refers to two areas of the same name, one in the [Wetlands](Wetlands) and the other in the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches). In the Wetlands, the Ruins of Grim Batol are the entrance to the halls of the lost mountain city, leading to the entrance of [the raid](Grim Batol (instance)), occupied by the Red dragonflight. In the Grim Reaches, an ancient, destroyed passage in the western hills (ref: [quest: To Cure the Withered]) leads to a forgotten outpost that is part of the outer ruins of Grim Batol and blocked off from the main city, where the shadow-cursed spirits of the Wildhammer defenders led by [Thane Falod Swifthammer](Thane Falod Swifthammer) and the Dark Iron invaders still haunt the ruins. (ref: [quest: Shadow Curse])
