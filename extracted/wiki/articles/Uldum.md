# Uldum

**Uldum** is a titan research facility located in the southern part of the [Valley of the Watchers](Valley of the Watchers), in the desert of [Tanaris](Tanaris).

The gates of Uldum are sealed shut, and the [Stone Watcher of Norgannon](Stone Watcher of Norgannon) claims that entry into the Uldum compound is only allowed for beings that have the [Plates of Uldum](Plates of Uldum). It also claims that Uldum is a research facility for the titans in their continuing efforts to enhance the biosphere of [Azeroth](Azeroth). (ref:  [50] [The Stone Watcher](The Stone Watcher)) Returning with the Plates of Uldum results in the spawning of the world boss [Ostarius](Ostarius), the Gate Keeper, who seals the gates to the compound and engages players upon detecting the [curse of the flesh](curse of the flesh) on them.
