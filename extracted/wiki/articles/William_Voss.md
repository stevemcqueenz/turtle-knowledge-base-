-William Voss** was a renowned alchemist of [Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)) prior to the First War, operating out of his family's home in the city's [Old Town](Old Town). After the end of the Second War and the rebuilding of Stormwind, William assumed his house to be restored, given his importance as Stormwind's most renowned alchemist. However, the [House of Nobles](House of Nobles)' plans for reconstructing the city did not include the rebuilding of the Voss family alchemy shop, leaving the Voss family homeless. Condemning those responsible for his family's fate, William sent his son [Jared](Jared Voss) to join the [Stonemasons](Stonemasons Guild) that shared his disdain for Stormwind's nobility before his death. (ref: [quest: Turning Off The Tap])
