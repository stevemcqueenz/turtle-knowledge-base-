# Crypt (Stromgarde Keep)

The [Stromgarde Keep](Stromgarde Keep) crypt is currently controlled by the [Alliance](Alliance) faction of [human](human)s. It is tended by three caretakers, and is quite small compared to most other crypts in the realms.
