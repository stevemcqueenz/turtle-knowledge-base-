# Nefarian

Also known as:
- Lord Victor Nefarius
- Blackwing

Nefarion (aka Blackwing) resides in his [lair](Blackwing Lair) high in the spires of [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain). There, he and his minions fight a constant battle with [Ragnaros](Ragnaros) and his forces for control of the mountain, and ultimately control of the entire region.

The son of [Deathwing](Deathwing), he has succeeded in creating the Chromatic [Dragonflight](Dragonflight) and seeks to use it to destroy the other dragonflights and destroy [Azeroth](Azeroth).

His human form, Victor Nefarius, has struck up an alliance with [Rend Blackhand](Rend Blackhand), though he only uses him and his clan to further his own goals.

The red dragon Vaelastrasz has sworn to hunt Nefarion until the end of his days.

Nefarion's name comes from the word 'nefarious', which means extremely wicked. Blizzard did their homework in english class =P
