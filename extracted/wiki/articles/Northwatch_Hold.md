# Northwatch Hold

-Northwatch Hold** [62, 54] is a massive [Alliance](Alliance) fortress located between the [Merchant Coast](Merchant Coast) in [the Barrens](the Barrens) and [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh).  It blocks the coastal approaches to Theramore from the north, forcing any [Horde](Horde) assault to travel south and navigate the marshes in order to reach [Theramore Isle](Theramore Isle). It is guarded by a large number of human [Theramore Marine](Theramore Marine)s and [Theramore Preserver](Theramore Preserver)s, all levels 15-17. The large naval base has massive walls and gates, an archery range, a stable, a blacksmith, tents, and two watch towers at the top of the mountain which the fort is located upon. The eastern tower is the home of [Cannoneer Smythe](Cannoneer Smythe) and the western tower is the home of [Cannoneer Whessan](Cannoneer Whessan), both targets of the Horde. There is also a central tower in which a captured [high elf](high elf), [Gilthares Firebough](Gilthares Firebough), is chained to an iron ball and seeks help and rescue by the hands of the [Horde](Horde). At the top of this tower resides [Captain Fairmount](Captain Fairmount), leader of Northwatch Hold, who also is target of the Horde.

The inhabitants of the naval fortress of Northwatch Hold are friendly towards the Alliance, however the area serves no purpose to them. There are no vendors or trainers. The residents of the hold are simply to be killed by the Horde trying to reclaim their land, as well as a vengeful [Captain Thalo'thas Brightsun](Captain Thalo'thas Brightsun) for an unprovoked attack on his ship.
