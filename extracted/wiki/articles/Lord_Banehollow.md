[Lord Banehollow](Lord Banehollow) is a level 59 [quest giver](quest giver) located in [Shadow Hold](Shadow Hold) in the [contested](contested) territory of [Felwood](Felwood). He is an [elite](elite) [Dreadlord](Dreadlord), and the leader of [Jaedenar](Jaedenar), the seat of the [Shadow Council](Shadow Council). He is guarded by a wide variety of warlocks, demons, and his personal attendant [Ur'dan](Ur'dan). Any [Warlock](Warlock) seeking to steal a [Dreadsteed](Dreadsteed) must deal with Banehollow by first drinking a [Shadowy Potion](Shadowy Potion). He assists and directs you in stealing the steed from a rival of his, [Lord Hel'nurath](Lord Hel'nurath) of [Xoroth](Xoroth).  

"Banehollow" is also one of the random names given to the Dreadlord hero in Warcraft III.
