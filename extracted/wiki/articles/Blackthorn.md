-Blackthorn** is a [banshee](banshee)-possessed [human](human) [quest giver](quest giver) located in [Blackthorn's Camp](Blackthorn's Camp) in [Gilneas](Gilneas). He was a bandit lord who was active in the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands) during the [Plaguelands civil war](Plaguelands civil war), at a time when Lordaeron was in ashes after the Scourging and its remaining inhabitants had turned on each other. [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner) encountered Blackthorn's bandits during the battle between her forces and those of [Varimathras](Varimathras). She used one of her banshees to take control of Blackthorn, forcing him and his men to serve her and assist in the battle against Varimathras. (ref: [The Dark Lady (WC3 Undead)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Dark_Lady_(WC3_Undead))) Blackthorn remains possessed by Sylvanas' banshee, who has convinced his band of cutthroats to fight for the Forsaken. Blackthorn's group is usually sent to spy upon the Alliance, as their living status makes them more suited for blending in among humans. (ref: [quest: Quality Time with Blackthorn])
