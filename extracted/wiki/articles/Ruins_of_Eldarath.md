# Ruins of Eldarath

**Ruins of Eldarath** (aka **Eldarath**) is an ancient [night elven](night elven) [city](city) located in central [Azshara](Azshara). The [Great Sundering](Great Sundering) caused most of the city to crumble to the oceans below. A cluster of ruins clings to the eastern cliffs, long shunned by night elves. More recently, treasure hunters and [dwarf](dwarf) exploration teams have made tentative forays into the ruins. They have consistently been repelled by ferocious bands of [naga](Spitelash) who appear to have claimed Eldarath for their own. The powerful warlord [Krellian](Krellian) controls the remaining ruins. It was a former [Temple of Elune](Temple of Zin-Malor).
