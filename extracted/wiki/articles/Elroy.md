-Elroy** was an [SI:7](SI:7) agent assigned to investigate the island of [Balor](Balor). During the course of this mission, he and nine fellow agents were discovered while surveying the perimeter around the [Croaking Plateau](Croaking Plateau) and were taken captive. He, along with [Agents Cherys](Agent Cherys) and [Flynn](Agent Flynn), were dragged to a cave beneath [Stormreaver Spire](Stormreaver Spire) to be experimented on by [Shadowlord Ar'kor](Shadowlord Ar'kor), resulting in his body liquifying and dissolving into ooze until a **Green Puddle** was all that remained of him.
