Princess **Moira Bronzebeard** is the daughter of King [Magni Bronzebeard](Magni Bronzebeard) of [Ironforge](Ironforge (kingdom)).

When [Magistrate Solomon](Magistrate Solomon) of [Lakeshire](Lakeshire) sent envoys to Ironforge to ask for aid against incursions from the Blackrock orcs, Moira volunteered to help and set out toward Lakeshire with twenty-one guardsmen, but as the group was traveling through the [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes), they were ambushed and captured by Dark Iron dwarves. [Kharan Mighthammer](Kharan Mighthammer), the only escorting guard to survive the kidnapping, informed adventurers that came to rescue him that the princess was being held under a magical spell. (ref: [quest: Kharan's Tale]) (ref: [quest: What Is Going On?])

Both King Magni and Warchief Thrall sought to have Moira rescued from Shadowforge City. However, even with Emperor Thaurissan's death, Moira, presumably still suffering from the residual effect of the Emperor's spell, refused to come home and announced that she was pregnant. (ref: <ref>[quest: The Princess's Surprise])
