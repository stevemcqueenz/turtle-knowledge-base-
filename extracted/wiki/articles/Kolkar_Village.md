# Kolkar Village

**Kolkar Village** was the center of power for the [Kolkar clan](Kolkar clan) of [centaur](centaur). The [village](village) located on the eastern margin of [Desolace](Desolace) between [Sargeron](Sargeron) to the north and [Magram Village](Magram Village) to the south was conquered by the [Magram clan](Magram clan). 

The Kolkars were the most prolific of the centaur [clan](clan)s in [Kalimdor](Kalimdor), having villages and outposts in both the [Barrens](Barrens) and [Durotar](Durotar), so a blow to their base of power at the village in Desolace would be a strategic move for their enemies.
