# Forgotten Pools

Located north of the western road out of the [Crossroads](Crossroads), the **Forgotten Pools** are the northernmost of the oases in the [Barrens](Barrens). As with many of these verdant pools, it is patrolled by [Kolkar](Kolkar) [Centaur](Centaur) along with the indigenous wildlife, such as [turtle](turtle)s.
