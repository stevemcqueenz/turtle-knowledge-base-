# Reginald Windsor

A 63+ quest giver NPC, who plays a major role in the Onyxia Quest Chain, resulting in obtaining the [Drakefire Amulet](Drakefire Amulet).

## Story
Marshall [Reginald Windsor](Reginald Windsor) was a legendary knight and commander in his best days. With nerves of thorium, weilding an epic hammer [Ironfel](Ironfel), he has become a living legend in [Stormwind](Stormwind). Later he was assigned a post of commanding officer of Alliance forces in [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes).

As threats of Blackrock Orcs grew, he sent agents to [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain) to investigate. While traveling with the information they gathered, he was ambushed by [Dark Iron](Dark Iron) Dwarves raiding party. Only one of his men, [Ragged John](Ragged John), escaped to tell the tale. Marshall Windsor was captured and imprisoned, his hammer taken by the Emperor Thaurissan himself, and precious information seemed lost.

It was only when a party of adventurers discovered his notes, scattered among major individuals of [Shadowforge](Shadowforge) that Windsor regained hope. Aided by the adventurers, he has released his imprisoned comrades and managed to get his gear back (though not his Ironfel), and then ventured to Stormwind, where despite orders of [Lady Katrana Prestor](Onyxia) he was greeted as a hero he was, and proceeded to reveal Katrana's true identity.
