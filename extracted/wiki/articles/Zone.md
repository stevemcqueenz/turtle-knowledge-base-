# Zone

In [World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft), the [continent](continent)s are divided up into sections known in-game as *zones* or *regions*.  Zones may contain [subzone](subzone)s, [cities](City), [town](town)s, and [instance](instance)s.  For example, the zone [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh) contains the subzone [Coldridge Valley](Coldridge Valley), the city [Ironforge](Ironforge), the town [Kharanos](Kharanos), and the instance [Gnomeregan](Gnomeregan).

For a list of zones with level ranges and which [faction](faction) they favor, see [List of regions](List of regions).
