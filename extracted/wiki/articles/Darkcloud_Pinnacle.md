# Darkcloud Pinnacle

-Darkcloud Pinnacle** is a [village](village) of the [Grimtotem clan](Grimtotem clan) of [tauren](tauren) and located atop the pinnacles in the central area of the [Thousand Needles](Thousand Needles). It is here that a secret correspondence between Grimtotem leaders and an unknown dark power takes place. The leaders of the Grimtotems here are [Arnak Grimtotem](Arnak Grimtotem) and [Harb Foulmountain](Harb Foulmountain). The entry path from the base begins at [31.3, 37].
