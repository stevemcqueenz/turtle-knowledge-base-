# Overlook Cliffs

**The Overlook Cliffs** are a set of steep cliffs that provide a drastic change from the mountainous [Hinterlands](Hinterlands) to the eastern shoreline of the region.

The cliffs are known for their steepness and height. Many travellers have underestimated their height and plummeted to their deaths. The big river that runs through Hinterlands ends here, pouring over the edge in a massive waterfall. This is also the end point for the Hinterlands robot chicken quest.
