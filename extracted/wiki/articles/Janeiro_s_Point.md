# Janeiro's Point

-Janeiro's Point** is a small island just outside [Booty Bay](Booty Bay), upon which a giant statue of a [goblin](goblin) with outstretched arms was built as a beacon to all travelers. 

The [sea giant](sea giant) [Mok'rash the Cleaver](Mok'rash the Cleaver) has chosen the island as his abode.

## Trivia
-The statue and its name are obviously a reference to the [statue of Christ the Redeemer](Wikipedia:Christ the Redeemer (statue)) in [Rio de Janeiro](Wikipedia:Rio de Janeiro), a symbol of the city and of the nation of Brazil.
