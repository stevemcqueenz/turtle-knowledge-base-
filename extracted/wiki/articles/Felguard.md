# Felguard

The **felguard** are the [Legion](Legion)'s basic foot soldiers. They are trained only to fight, so that's about all they do. They operate under the direction of [Doomguard](Doomguard), [Pit Lord](Pit Lord)s and other Legion commanders and generals. Felguard are treated like fodder, driving the Legion's hold on the universe forward like a relentless force of nature.
