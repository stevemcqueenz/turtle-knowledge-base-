The **Grove of the Sun** is a small grove located on the isle of [Tyrandas](Tyrandas), in northeastern [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast). It is one of the two druidic circles in the coast, along with the [Grove of the Moon](Grove of the Moon).
