# Thandol Valley

.]]

**Thandol Valley** is the name of the region surrounding the great bridges of the [Thandol Span](Thandol Span). The bridges are built over the channel between [Baradin Bay](Baradin Bay) and the [Forbidding Sea](Forbidding Sea) that separates [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) from [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan).

## Second War
Following their defeat at [Dun Modr](Dun Modr), the [orc](orc)s were forced to retreat south, across the Thandol Valley to a secondary outpost at [Dun Algaz](Dun Algaz). [Anduin Lothar](Anduin Lothar), hoping to push the orcs even further back into [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan), ordered the destruction of the outpost. (ref: Dun Algaz (WC2 Human))

## Trivia
- The Warcraft II game demo has a mission called Thandol that is situated in [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan). If not a separate location, it could be an earlier, production name for the area.
- The name "Thandol Valley" hasn't been used since *[Warcraft II](Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness)* and might have been retconned out.
- Dun Modr was originally depicted as north of the [Thandol Span](Thandol Span) in older sources including *Warcraft II* and *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*. (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, pg. 180) In *[World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft)* the early concept maps still followed that depiction, but the location of the city was eventually moved south of the Span.
- Similarly, Dun Algaz was originally depicted in *Warcraft II* as on the southern end of the Span, a place currently occupied in *World of Warcraft* by Dun Modr.
- In [this](:File:Eastern Kingdoms concept sketch.jpg) concept map for *[Warcraft II](Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness)*, it is indicated a dam was in the region instead of a bridge. It may have inspired the [Stonewrought Dam](Stonewrought Dam).
