# Augustina Sunblade

## **Augustina Sunblade**
  -Race:** Blood Elf (formerly High Elf)

  -Class:** Paladin (Blood Knight)

  -Nickname:** Red Rose

-"I will conquer the darkness that seeks to consume me."*

### **History:**
Hailing from a lineage of High Elves, Augustina's life took a drastic turn during the Scourge invasion of Quel'Thalas. Witnessing the slaughter of her kin and the defilement of the sacred Sunwell left a lasting mark on her soul. In the aftermath, the survivors took on the name "blood elves," and Augustina became known as "Red Rose" for her unwavering determination and the crimson rose that bloomed amidst the desolation.

Fueled by the loss and the taint of the corrupted Sunwell, Augustina embraced a life of thrilling adventure. She craved the exhilaration of challenging foes, becoming fully dependent on magic to sate the hunger that gnawed at her soul. Carrying her enchanted sword, Destiny, a gift from someone very close to her heart, she sought to prove her worth in the face of darkness.

### **Personality and Demeanor:**
Augustina carries herself with an air of arrogance, a facade to hide the lingering pain of the past and the fear of losing the magic she clings to. In her haughty and confident tone, she demands respect and admiration, believing her noble lineage entitles her to such. Her actions are often driven by the thrill of danger and conquest, as she relishes testing her skills and overcoming formidable adversaries.

### **Motivations and Interests:**
Unlike traditional paladins, Augustina's motivations transcend the pursuit of justice and duty. She seeks to fill the void left by the loss of the Sunwell by satiating her insatiable hunger for magic. Constantly pushing her limits to chase the elusive feeling of wholeness, she delves into the most perilous corners of Azeroth, seeking greater challenges to feed her addiction.

### **Blood Knight:**
Devastated by the loss and driven by a desire for vengeance, Augustina sought solace in the teachings of the Blood Knights. United by their common bond of loss and their dedication to restoring the glory of Quel'Thalas, the Blood Knights trained in the ways of both martial combat and the Light. Under the watchful eye of Lady Liadrin, she honed her skills, seeking strength to protect her people and avenge her family's legacy.

### **Backstory for the Bloody Mask:**
The Bloody Mask holds a dark secret that traces back to one of Augustina's most harrowing adventures. In a remote and cursed land, she faced a malevolent entity whose sinister powers threatened to corrupt the very essence of her being. Through an arduous battle, she managed to subdue the malevolence, but not without paying a steep price. The white mask she once wore, a symbol of her purity, was forever stained with the blood of the malevolent entity, serving as a haunting reminder of the darkness she encountered.

### **Backstory for Destiny:**
A famed sword: Hanging proudly at her side, is an enchanted sword named Destiny. This legendary weapon holds a unique ability, seamlessly shifting between its original form and that of an ancient Greatsword of the Quel'Danil. It bears marks of countless battles. With a hilt wrapped in crimson leather and adorned with intricate golden patterns, the sword feels like an extension of Augustina's being, as if it were a part of her soul. The blade itself is a masterful craftsmanship of enchanted metal, exuding an otherworldly glow as it changes between its forms. Ancient runes line the blade, telling the story of the battles it has witnessed, and the dragon's essence it absorbed during its creation. When Destiny shifts into the Greatsword of the Quel'Danil, a sense of reverence fills the air, as if the spirit of ancient elven warriors courses through the weapon.

### **Backstory for Red Armor:**
In her pursuit of becoming a Blood Knight, Augustina underwent rigorous training, honing her combat skills and mastering the ways of the Light. She proved herself to be a formidable warrior and earned the respect of her peers. As a testament to her dedication and potential, the Blood Knights entrusted her with a remarkable set of red-colored armor, forged with the essence of the Sunwell itself.
