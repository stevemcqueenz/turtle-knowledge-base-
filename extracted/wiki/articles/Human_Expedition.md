The **Human Expedition** was a league of [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron) military and civilian groups that fled to [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) from [Lordaeron](Lordaeron) after the [plague of undeath](plague of undeath) and the [Scourge](Scourge) decimated the kingdoms of [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)), [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas (kingdom)), and [Dalaran](Dalaran (kingdom)). They were led by [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore), who was determined to find a safe haven for her people.

While called the "Human Expedition", the military forces had [high elven](High elf) survivors from [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas) and [dwarves](Dwarf) of [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan) and [Aerie Peak](Aerie Peak).

Following the [Battle of Mount Hyjal](Battle of Mount Hyjal), the majority of the members of these parties settled on [Theramore Isle](Theramore Isle) in [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh) under the wise leadership of [Jaina Proudmoore](Jaina Proudmoore). These forces would soon become part of the army of [Theramore](Theramore).

## Organizations
The Human Expedition was comprised of the following factions, the majority of which were active in [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains): (ref: [Cry of the Warsong (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cry_of_the_Warsong_(WC3_Orc))) (ref: [Where Wyverns Dare (WC3 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Where_Wyverns_Dare_(WC3_Orc)))

- [Lordaeron Brigade](Lordaeron Brigade) (ref:  — A brigade of humans from [Lordaeron](Lordaeron (kingdom)).
- [Lordaeron Corps](Lordaeron Corps) — Lordaeron's elite soldiers guarding the expedition.
- [Gilneas Brigade](Gilneas Brigade) — A brigade of humans from [Gilneas](Gilneas (kingdom)) sent by Prince [Liam Greymane](Liam Greymane) and Lord [Darius Ravenwood](Darius Ravenwood).<ref>[The Prophet's Warnings and the Gilneas Brigade](The Prophet's Warnings and the Gilneas Brigade)) It contained many stout Gelnorian [dwarves](Dwarf) as well.
- [Kul Tiras Elite Corps](Kul Tiras Elite Corps) (ref:  — A force of humans troops from [Kul Tiras](Kul Tiras (kingdom)), likely Jaina's personal guard.
- [Stromgarde Brigade](Stromgarde Brigade) — A brigade of human troops from [Stromgarde](Stromgarde (kingdom)).
- An unknown dwarven contingent of the expedition occupied the settlement of [Bael Hardul](Bael Hardul).<ref>[Patch 1.16.4#Shift in The Tides of Stonetalon](Patch 1.16.4#Shift in The Tides of Stonetalon))

As the factions didn't appear in any other missions, it is assumed that any survivors were assimilated into the Human Expedition which then participated in the missions in [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), [Winterspring](Winterspring), and [Mount Hyjal](Mount Hyjal). (ref: [Eternity's End](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Eternity%27s_End_(WC3_campaign)) campaign)

## Notes
- Opening the [Cry of the Warsong](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cry_of_the_Warsong_(WC3_Orc)) mission in the world editor reveals that the team name for the human factions is Human Expedition.
- It was originally implied in *[Warcraft III](Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos)* that the Expedition was most, if not all, of the survivors of the Eastern Kingdoms. *[The Frozen Throne](Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne)* depicting human and blood elf survivors [still fighting](Alliance Remnants), but it was unclear if they were forgotten stragglers or part of a larger trend. The release of *[World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft)* and its manual confused the issue again by saying that "The few humans who survived the [Third War](Third War) fled south to the protection of Stormwind." (ref: *World of Warcraft: Game Manual*, pg. 168) despite also showing survivors of the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade), [Argent Dawn](Argent Dawn), [Dalaran](Dalaran), and [Southshore](Southshore). Concurrently, the in-game book [Old Hatreds - The Colonization of Kalimdor](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Old_Hatreds_-_The_Colonization_of_Kalimdor) implies that human survivors were in short supply on the Eastern Kingdoms mainland.
