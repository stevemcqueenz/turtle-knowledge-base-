The **Redridge Mountains** (also known as the **Red Ridge Mountains**, (ref: [The Red Ridge Mountains (WC1 Orc)](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Red_Ridge_Mountains_(WC1_Orc))) (ref: [A Torn Journal](A Torn Journal)) or the **Redridge Range** (ref: *[The Last Guardian](The Last Guardian)*, pg. 1) (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* manual, pg. 82)) are located east of [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), northeast of [Duskwood](Duskwood), and south of the [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes). Although it may be considered [contested](contested), [Horde](Horde) characters have no settlements or NPCs, and it is thus a place they use mostly for passing through to reach [Flame Crest](Flame Crest) or [Stonard](Stonard).

An idyllic region of rushing rivers, towering elms, and rising elevations, the Redridge Mountains are under the protection of the [kingdom of Stormwind](Stormwind (kingdom)). On the western bank of [Lake Everstill](Lake Everstill) is the [Alliance](Alliance) town of [Lakeshire](Lakeshire), the central hub for this faction's [adventurer](adventurer)s, offering many services and quests, as well as [flight path](flight path)s. The crossroads known as the [Three Corners](Three Corners), located in the southwest corner of the zone, connects the town of Lakeshire with Elwynn, Duskwood and, via the Lakeridge Highway, [Stonewatch Keep](Stonewatch Keep). The highway itself is quite a dangerous place occupied by [gnolls](gnolls), [murloc](murloc)s, and wild animals.

A force of [Blackrock](Blackrock clan) [orc](orc)s from the Burning Steppes has secured Stonewatch Keep for the [Dark Horde](Dark Horde), and are also in control of the northern roads. There are also a few camps located in Redridge — an orc camp in [Render's Valley](Render's Valley), [Shadowhide](Shadowhide clan) [gnoll](gnoll) camps near the [Tower of Ilgalar](Tower of Ilgalar), and murloc camps near the lake. While the lake's surface appears peaceful enough, murlocs reside beneath the waves, and rumors of sightings of a sea monster persist. In comparison with [Westfall](Westfall), Redridge is still relatively stable.

## Geography
The Redridge Mountains contain no dungeons, raid dungeons, or battlegrounds. The micro dungeon [Stonewatch](Stonewatch) can be found in this zone. The Redridge Range stretches from the zone of the Redridge Mountains to the zone of [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass).

### Redridge Range
The **Redridge Range** (ref: *[The Last Guardian](The Last Guardian)*, pg. 2) is a mountain range in the southern sub-continent of [Azeroth](Azeroth (continent)). It reaches from the [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass) in the south to what's now called the [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge) in the north. Two hundred years before the opening of the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal), the northern reaches of the range were obliterated by the summoning of [Firelord Ragnaros](Ragnaros) and the creation of [Blackrock Mountain](Blackrock Mountain).

## Notes
- Redridge has one of the game's highest level-gaps between zones at its border with the [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes), which is a 30 level jump from 20 to 50. Players are instead directed to [Duskwood](Duskwood) (to the southwest) after completing all the quests here.
- The *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* Game Manual specifically mentions gnolls and kobolds as being present in the Redridge Mountains, despite the game's plot never going there.
