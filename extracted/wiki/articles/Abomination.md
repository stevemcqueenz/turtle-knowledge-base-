# Abomination

An **abomination** is an [undead](undead) ogre-like creature composed of multiple bodies sewn gruesomely together, these huge neanderthals are stretched and stitched skin, broken teeth, massive bloated stomachs, sporting a massive gash where their mixed entrails hang out. They have an additonal limb, and carry huge meat cleavers in their hands. They often serve as the higher-[level](level) [guard](guard)s in [Forsaken](Forsaken) [cities](City).  Many Abominations, aside from their extreme physical strength, also admit a noxious cloud of pestilence and disease from their rotting innards.  This cloud can spread disease or physically weaken anyone who attacks an Abomination.

Abominations also appear strangely joyful. They have a lust for flesh. The abominations are created in Slaughter Houses, found in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands). Those tend to be [Scourge](Scourge)-only however. [Forsaken](Forsaken) abominations are made in [the Apothecarium](the Apothecarium) found in the [Undercity](Undercity), from the caged humans in there. They are mostly employed as guards, however some become Forsaken higher-ups in the military, such as [Lieutenant Murp](Lieutenant Murp), found in [Alterac Valley](Alterac Valley). Important Scourge abominations include [Glutton](Glutton), a constantly hungry abomination found in [Razorfen Downs](Razorfen Downs); [Ramstein](Ramstein) the ruthless guardian of [Baron Rivendare](Baron Rivendare)'s slaughterhouse in [Stratholme](Stratholme); and the titanic [Patchwerk](Patchwerk), whom [Kel'Thuzad](Kel'Thuzad) makes his "Avatar of War."
