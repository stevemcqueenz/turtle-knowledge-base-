# Wendigo

Wendigo are large, furry humanoids that dwell in cold climates; they are common in [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh). See also [Yeti](Yeti). Wendigo often range in levels of 5-7 and are often hunted en masse in central Dun Morogh. Wendigo can be skinned at apprentice level.

The indigenous wendigo of Northrend are exceedingly voracious creatures.
Solitary by nature, wendigo rarely live amongst others of their
kind. Cannibalistic and savage, these hulking, fur-covered humanoids
are highly territorial and do not take kindly to intruders stumbling into
their hunting grounds.
