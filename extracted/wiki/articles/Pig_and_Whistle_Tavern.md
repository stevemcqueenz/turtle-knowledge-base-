# Pig and Whistle Tavern

The **Pig and Whistle Tavern** is a tavern in [Stormwind](Stormwind). Located in the district known as [Old Town](Old Town), the Pig and Whistle is home away from home for the weary [Alliance](Alliance) traveller. [Kendor Kabonka](Kendor Kabonka) can be found here, as well as soldiers recalling the [First](First War) and [Second War](Second War)s. It is also the secret headquarters for Stormwind’s warriors’ guild.

## Inspiration
The "Pig'n Whistle" is the name of [a California bar founded in 1929](http://www.pignwhistle.com/) which was featured in Roman Polanski's 1974 film *Chinatown*.  The "Pig n Whistle" is also the name of [[a bar opened in 1969 in New York City](http://www.pignwhistlets.com/), famous for its friendly people and atmosphere.  Lastly, "Pig and Whistle" was the name of a tavern in the *Dragonlance* series of novels, located in Port Balifor.
