# Wildhammer dwarf

**Wildhammer dwarves**, (ref: [Gryth Thurden#Quotes](Gryth Thurden#Quotes)) also known as **wild dwarves**, (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, pg. 35) (ref: *[Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos](Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos)* [manual](Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos Game Manual), pg. 12) **feral dwarves**, (ref: [Assault on Blackrock Spire (WC2 Human)#Mission briefing](Assault on Blackrock Spire (WC2 Human)#Mission briefing)) **Aerie dwarves** (ref: *[Day of the Dragon](Day of the Dragon)*, chapter 15 - "Then let's be moving on! That mean's ye, too, butterfly!" The last referred to Falstad, who bristled at what apparently had to be a harsh insult to one of the Aerie dwarves.) and **mountain dwarves** are a type of [dwarf](dwarf) currently centered mainly in the [Hinterlands](Hinterlands) and the [Grim Reaches](Grim Reaches).

The Wildhammer dwarves are renowned for their close bond with [gryphon](gryphon)s, leading to numerous successes in the [Second](Second War) and [Third War](Third War)s. The bulk of their people are led by the eponymous [Wildhammer clan](Wildhammer clan). The ancient fortress of [Grim Batol](Grim Batol) was once a massive Wildhammer dwarf city, though it was lost following the [War of the Three Hammers](War of the Three Hammers).
