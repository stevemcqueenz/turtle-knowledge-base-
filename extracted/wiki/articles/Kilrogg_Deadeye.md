-Kilrogg Deadeye** is the chieftain of the [Bleeding Hollow clan](Bleeding Hollow clan). Leading his clan in both the [First](First War) and [Second War](Second War)s, Kilrogg was one of the older and more cunning orc chieftains.

## Background
### First War
As an older chieftain, Kilrogg Deadeye had become more unwilling to throw lives away in combat. In orcish politics older orcs become more pragmatic, which is often mistaken for cowardice by the younger generation. Prior to the sacking of Stormwind, Kilrogg had killed three of his sons and two grandsons already who thought they could rule the clan better. (ref: *The Last Guardian*, pg. 221)

At the start of the First War, the Bleeding Hollow clan participated in the first attack on [Stormwind Keep](Stormwind Keep), along with the [Twilight's Hammer clan](Twilight's Hammer clan) led by [Cho'gall](Cho'gall). This first attack was an unmitigated disaster for the orcs, and Deadeye loudly proclaimed that the attack had failed due to Cho'gall's incompetence. A rift soon grew that threatened to rip the Horde apart before it had even begun its conquest of Azeroth, as the orcs quickly polarized into factions that supported either chieftain. [Gul'dan](Gul'dan) quickly stepped in to quell the problem, installing [Blackhand](Blackhand) as Warchief of the Horde to restore order to the Horde. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[The History of Orcish Ascension](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#The_Mastery_of_Forces_-_Medivh_and_Blackhand)*, The Mastery of Forces - Medivh and Blackhand)

### Second War and aftermath
Kilrogg fought under [Blackhand](Blackhand), and then later under [Orgrim Doomhammer](Orgrim Doomhammer). During the war, his clan was stationed throughout [Khaz Modan](Khaz Modan), where it controlled the Horde's vital mining and oil refining operations. (ref: *Warcraft II: Tides of Darkness* manual, *[Clans of the Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Tides_of_Darkness_manual#Bleeding_Hollow_Clan)*, Bleeding Hollow Clan) After the tide of the Second War turned and the Horde was defeated, Kilrogg managed to lead his clan back through the rift of the [Dark Portal](Dark Portal) and into [Draenor](Draenor). There he was hailed as a hero, and was taken before [Ner'zhul](Ner'zhul). After recounting the events of the Second War to the old shaman, Kilrogg, along with [Grommash Hellscream](Grommash Hellscream) and [Kargath Bladefist](Kargath Bladefist), led the charge back into Azeroth to steal items of power needed to aid Ner'zhul in his scheme to retrieve certain artifacts from Azeroth as to open rifts into other worlds and take the Orcish Hordes to great victories beyond the Dark Portal. (ref: *Warcraft II: Beyond the Dark Portal* game manual, [The Aftermath of the Second War](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_II:_Beyond_the_Dark_Portal_manual#The_Aftermath_of_the_Second_War))

When Ner'zhul used the items to open more portals, Hellscream and Deadeye realized Ner'zhul's mad plans would doom their entire race. They abandoned Ner'zhul and rallied as many orcs as they could find, driving them through the Dark Portal to the questionable safety of Azeroth just before Draenor was destroyed. (ref: [The Invasion of Draenor](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Invasion_of_Draenor)) Their clans passed through just before Archmage [Khadgar](Khadgar) closed it completely. Hellscream and Deadeye hacked their way through the human ranks in a desperate bid for freedom, the Dark Portal exploding behind them. For them, and the remaining orcs on Azeroth, there would be no going back. (ref: <ref>*Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, *[Orcish History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Ner'zhul_and_the_Shadow_Clans)*, Ner'zhul and the Shadow Clans) Though Grom Hellscream and his [Warsong clan](Warsong clan) managed to evade capture, Deadeye and his Bleeding Hollow clan were rounded up and placed in the internment camps in Lordaeron. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* game manual, *[Orcish History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Day_of_the_Dragon)*, Day of the Dragon) (ref: [The Battle of Grim Batol](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_Battle_of_Grim_Batol_(History_of_Warcraft))) The captive clans, including the Bleeding Hollow, were eventually freed by [Thrall](Thrall), son of [Durotan](Durotan). Supported by many of the veteran chieftains, presumably including Kilrogg, Thrall ultimately succeeded in revitalizing the Horde and giving his people a new spiritual identity. (ref: *Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos* Game Manual, *[Orcish History](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos_Game_Manual#Thrall's_Tale)*, Thrall's Tale) (ref: [The New Horde](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/The_New_Horde)) Kilrogg would go on to serve as an advisor to the young warchief, (ref:  and members of his clan would later occupy the outpost of [Grom'gol Base Camp](Grom'gol Base Camp) in the Stranglethorn Vale.<ref>A Bleeding Hollow banner can be found next to [Far Seer Mok'thardin](Far Seer Mok'thardin).)

### Mysteries of Azeroth
On Thrall's orders, (ref:  Kilrogg led his clan to the island of [Balor](Balor), where the Bleeding Hollow established the outpost of [Stormbreaker Point](Stormbreaker Point) from which to hunt the Shadow Council and the remains of the [Stormreaver clan](Stormreaver clan) that had begun to inhabit the ruins once more.
