The **Silvermoon Remnant** are the remaining high elves, still reeling from the loss of their homeland and the Sunwell. A broken people, they have now reunited with their ancestral allies, the humans, which led them to rejoin the Alliance, seeking to reclaim their ancient lands and return their people to their days of former glory.

## Reputation
[Alinerenah Sunsparrow](Alinerenah Sunsparrow) has the repeatable cloth quests to achieve a higher reputation with the Silvermoon Remnant. In return for exalted reputation, non-high elf players are able to ride [unicorn](unicorn)s.

Most quests associated with the Silvermoon Remnant come from the [Thalassian Highlands](Thalassian Highlands) and [Moonwhisper Coast](Moonwhisper Coast). A small selection of quests are also available across [Ironforge](Ironforge), [Stormwind City](Stormwind City) and [Hawk's Vigil](Hawk's Vigil).

## Rewards
At exalted, [Soalara Dawnstar](Soalara Dawnstar) and Alinerenah Sunsparrow offer a tabard, and non-high elven Alliance races are able to purchase and ride Unicorn mounts (high elves can ride Thalassian unicorns regardless of faction reputation). The Silvermoon Remnant is also unique in that it is the only racial player faction in the game to have more than one vendor available for their rewards, with Soalara Dawnstar selling a variety of weapons at revered and Alinerenah Sunsparrow selling capes at honored and armor at revered.

Thalassian unicorns are bought from [Vanira Quel'Belore](Vanira Quel'Belore) in [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas). Unarmored unicorns are available starting at level 40, with armored unicorns available at level 60.
