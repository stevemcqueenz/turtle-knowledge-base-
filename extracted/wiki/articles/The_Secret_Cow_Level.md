# The Secret Cow Level

The cow level is led by Moo-Moo, a celestial beeing in the appearance of a mighty Tauren, wielding the true power of a cow-god, some say. 

Rumors have been flying around about the existence of both; the Moo-Moo Tauren God, and the actual Cow Level. 

Many noble adventurers such as Dêâdshádøw, Bøbktick and other, well known Heroes of Azeroth seeked for the one true God and his Cow-Level.

However, no one succeeded but the rumors still spread around. 

Some said you might reach the cow level playing a naked tauren warrior on hardcore with world pvp on, reaching level 60 just by grinding.
