# Spider

Though many scholars believe that all spiders trace their roots back to the ancient kingdom of [Azjol-Nerub](Azjol-Nerub), the giant spiders of [Kalimdor](Kalimdor) have made a claim to the shadowy corners of [Ashenvale](Ashenvale) Forest. Though not necessarily evil, wild spiders can become fearsome opponents if they feel their nests have been defiled. They are found in [Tirisfal Glades](Tirisfal Glades), the [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes), [Teldrassil](Teldrassil), [Ashenvale](Ashenvale), the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains), [Loch Modan](Loch Modan), [Duskwood](Duskwood), the [Wetlands](Wetlands), the [Stonetalon Mountains](Stonetalon Mountains), the [Western Plaguelands](Western Plaguelands), [Silverpine Forest](Silverpine Forest), the [Hillsbrad Foothills](Hillsbrad Foothills), [Azshara](Azshara), [Blackrock Depths](Blackrock Depths), [Blackrock Spire](Blackrock Spire), [Silithus](Silithus), the [Swamp of Sorrows](Swamp of Sorrows), the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), the [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge), [Dustwallow Marsh](Dustwallow Marsh), and [Zul'Gurub](Zul'Gurub).

During the [First War](First War), Orc Warlocks were adept at summoning these creatures to fight for the Horde.

Spiders are fond of using poison spit and throwing webs upon opponents.  There are three different models of spider: tarantula; and a much larger version of a normal spider; and a miniature spider. The [Smoulderweb Hatchling](Smoulderweb Hatchling), a miniature spider, can be obtained as a small pet.

- Not skinnable.
- Usually drop webbing, spider legs, spinnerets, mandibles (or fangs), or spider meat as loot.

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Has high damage, medium HP, and low armor.
- Eats only Meat.  
- Can learn Cower, Growl, and Bite.  They have yet to recieve their species skill.
- The Spire Spiderling of Lower Blackrock Spire has a fast 1.5 attack speed.
- Spiders are not very popular as Hunter pets.
