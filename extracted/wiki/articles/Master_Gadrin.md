[Master Gadrin](Master Gadrin) is a level 12 [quest giver](quest giver) located in [Sen'jin Village](Sen'jin Village) in the [orc](orc) and [troll](troll) starting zone of [Durotar](Durotar).

Gadrin is one of [Vol'jin](Vol'jin)’s most trusted advisers. Along with a young witch doctor named [Zalazane](Zalazane), Gadrin led the mystical training of many [Darkspear](Darkspear) [priest](priest)s and [mage](mage)s. However, Zalazane went mad from the mystical forces he had unleashed. The crazed witch doctor placed a curse upon the [Echo Isles](Echo Isles) and drove Gadrin and his brethren away. Now Gadrin seeks to end Zalazane’s rampage and put the souls of his cursed brethren to rest.
