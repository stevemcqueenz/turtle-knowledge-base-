# Whelgar's Excavation Site

[Whelgar's Excavation Site](Whelgar's Excavation Site) lies east of [Menethil Harbor](Menethil Harbor) and southwest of [Dun Modr](Dun Modr) in the [Wetlands](Wetlands).  Once a peaceful dwarven digsite, it has been taken over by mottled [raptor](raptor)s. A small group of [dwarves](dwarf) still carve out their living here, on a ridge overlooking the dig site.
