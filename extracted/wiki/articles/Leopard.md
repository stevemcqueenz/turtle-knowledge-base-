# Leopard

- **Race:** [Beast](Beast)s
- **Location:** [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh)

Leopards are a specific sub race of cats that have adepted to the cold climate of the mountain landscape of Dun Morogh, they are also known as [Snow Leopard](Snow Leopard) which suggests other leopards also exist on [Azeroth](Azeroth).

# Hunter
[Hunter](Hunter)s in [World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft) have the ability to tame certain [beast](beast)s.

- **Tamable:** Yes (Cat family)
- **Diet:** Fish, Meat
- **Skills:** Bite, Claw, Dash, Prowl

- **Damage:** +10%
- **Armour:** -5%
- **Health:** -2%
