## Objectives
Travel to Kazon Island and speak with the troll hermit Insom'ni.

## Description
<After studying the Enchanted Brass Key it would appear that Magus Valgon is stumped>.

I do not think I can assist with this, friend, the complexities of the magic may take many weeks to begin to crack, and I think you do not have such time to wait.

I do know one who knows a great deal of magic, hidden away from the world. There is a hermit that remains somewhat hidden upon the island of Kazon, nestled in between Gillijim and Lapidis Isle.

Go there, and find Insom'ni, he is a powerful troll, much more powerful than any I have met.

If there is anyone who could attune this key, it would be him.
