# Cliffspring Falls

The **Cliffspring Falls** are the origin of the [Cliffspring River](Cliffspring River) in [Darkshore](Darkshore).  Once a pure waterfall, the cave that is the source of the falls has become corrupted, like much of the surrounding area. Some believe the corruption is seeping from nearby [Felwood](Felwood) while others believe that members of the [Twilight's Hammer](Twilight's Hammer) cult are responsible.  Members of the cult have been found deep within the caves at the waterfall's source.  The cave is also crawling with [Naga](Stormscale).
