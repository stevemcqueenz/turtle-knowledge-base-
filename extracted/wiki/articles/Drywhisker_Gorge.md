# Drywhisker Gorge

A gorge cutting through the mountains of eastern [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), near [Hammerfall](Hammerfall). At the end of the gorge lies a cave, which is the only known source of [Bloodstone](Bloodstone) ore. It's currently inhabited by [Kobolds](Kobold). Deep within the cave, a mysterious stone shard can be found, in which a piece of essence of princess [Myzrael](Myzrael) was trapped.
