# Patch 1.15.0

-Patch 1.15.0** was released on January 5, 2021.

## Patch notes
- Two new races: Goblins and High Elves & their starting locations
- Two new zones: [Amani'Alor](Amani'Alor) and [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas)
- * Arena 2vs2 (non-rated on release)
- Gambling introduced by fellow Goblins
- New level 60 dungeon: Stormwind Vault
- Over 400 new items for all level brackets
- Two new classes for existing races: Gnome Hunters and Orc Mages
- New mounts, companions & Hunter pets
- General world overhaul with Blizzlike elements
- Cosmetic client enhancements
