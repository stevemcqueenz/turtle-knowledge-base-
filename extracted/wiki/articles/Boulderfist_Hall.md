# Boulderfist Hall

[Boulderfist Hall](Boulderfist Hall) is a fortress of the [Boulderfist](Boulderfist) [ogre](ogre)s in southern [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands), near [Thandol Span](Thandol Span) and [Witherbark Village](Witherbark Village). The caves are strongly fortified, and inside the larger cave you can find chief [Kor'gresh Coldrage](Kor'gresh Coldrage).
