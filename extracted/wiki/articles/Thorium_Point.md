**Thorium Point** is a small, neutral town in northern [Searing Gorge](Searing Gorge) inhabited by the [Thorium Brotherhood](Thorium Brotherhood).  

It has a flight path and the majority of the quests in the Searing Gorge as well as a couple of vendors. It is located northeast of [Firewatch Ridge](Firewatch Ridge) and is populated mostly by neutral [Dark Iron dwarves](Dark Iron dwarf). It is a critical hub for farming [Thorium Brotherhood](Thorium Brotherhood) reputation.

There is a [forge](forge) and [anvil](anvil) at this location.
