# Gilneas Memorial Stone

The **Gilneas Memorial Stone** is a monument located in the center of [Ravenshire](Ravenshire), in [Gilneas](Gilneas). 

## Contents
## Speculation
Presumably, as this memorial was written within Genn's lifetime, it is dedicated to Gilnean soldiers lost either during the [Second War](Second War) or in the Scourge attack by [Naze the Eternal](Naze the Eternal) that saw the destruction of [Greyshire](Ruins of Greyshire).
