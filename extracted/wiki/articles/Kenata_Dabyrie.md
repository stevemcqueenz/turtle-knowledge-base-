**Kenata Dabyrie** is a human found in the [Arathi Highlands](Arathi Highlands). She lives with her two sons [Fardel](Fardel Dabyrie) and [Marcel](Marcel Dabyrie) at the [Dabyrie's Farmstead](Dabyrie's Farmstead). [Genavie Callow](Genavie Callow) wants their heads because they stole her only precious belongings. (ref: [quest: To Steal From Thieves])

## Abilities
- **Disarm** — Disarms an enemy, forcing it to stop wielding its weapon for 10 sec
