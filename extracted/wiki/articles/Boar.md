# Boar

A common, usually furry, cloven-hooved, four-legged [Beast](beast) that is the wild relative of the domesticated pig. Most **boars** are [neutral](Neutral) when approached and are generally omnivorous. They start at around large dog sized and get larger from there.  Some boars are minions of the [Quillboar](Quillboar)s.  Boars are found in [Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh), [Loch Modan](Loch Modan), [Elwynn Forest](Elwynn Forest), the [Redridge Mountains](Redridge Mountains), [Durotar](Durotar), [Mulgore](Mulgore), the [Blasted Lands](Blasted Lands), [Razorfen Kraul](Razorfen Kraul), [Razorfen Downs](Razorfen Downs), [Westfall](Westfall), the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), and [Teldrassil](Teldrassil).

Additional notes:
- Often killed early on for their [food](Food) [ingredient](Ingredient) [drop](Drop)s.
- There are three types of armored boar (the Agam'ars) that dwell in [Razorfen Kraul](Razorfen Kraul); despite appearances, they are tameable by hunters.
- There are also [undead](undead) and [demon](demon) variety of boars, but these are not tameable.  However, the plagued swine of the Eastern Plaguelands looks undead but is a beast.

### Examples
- Crag Boars ([Dun Morogh](Dun Morogh))
- Mountain Boars ([Loch Modan](Loch Modan))
- Goretusks ([Westfall](Westfall))

## As A [Hunter](Hunter) Pet
- Have high HP, high armor, very low damage.
- Eats Bread, Fruit, Meat, Fish, Cheese, and Fungus.
- Can learn [Bite](Bite), [Growl](Growl), [Cower](Cower), [Dash](Dash), and (boar) Charge.  Charge makes the boar run up to the target, immobilizing them for 1 second and adding more power to the boar's next attack.
- Occasionally used by hunters due to their incredible defensive stats, Charge, and their easy diet.  As of 1.9 they have become a great deal more popular due to Charge.  In 1.10 they gained a level 60 member of their family, the plagued swine, which not only has an undead looking skin, but features the last rank of Charge.
