# Shrine of Remulos

The **Shrine of Remulos** is a shrine dedicated to [Keeper Remulos](Keeper Remulos), located in western [Moonglade](Moonglade). It also houses the [night elf](night elf) [Tajarri](Tajarri), who helps young [druid](druid)s along in their quest for the [aquatic form](aquatic form).
