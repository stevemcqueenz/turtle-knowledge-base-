# Bloodvenom Post

-Bloodvenom Post**[34, 52] is a tiny [Horde](Horde) outpost in [Felwood](Felwood) near the [Bloodvenom River](Bloodvenom River). It sits on the high cliffs overlooking [Darkshore](Darkshore) and consists of only a single hut (an inn) with a mailbox, a wind rider master, a few guards, and several npcs. It seems to have been founded by the [Tauren](Tauren) due to the architecture and the Tauren guards.

To find the post, follow the Bloodvenom River west along the south side, starting from the main stone path that runs north and south through Felwood. You will notice a dark trail — simply follow it around the hillside to reach the outpost.
