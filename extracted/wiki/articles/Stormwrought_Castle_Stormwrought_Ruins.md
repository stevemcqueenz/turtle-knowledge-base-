# Stormwrought Castle (Stormwrought Ruins)

*This article is about the dungeon subzone. For the castle exterior, see [Stormwrought Castle](Stormwrought Castle). For the dungeon, see [Stormwrought Ruins](Stormwrought Ruins).*

  -Stormwrought Castle** is the first section of the [Stormwrought Ruins](Stormwrought Ruins) dungeon, accessed from the dungeon's main entrance. It encompasses the castle's [barracks](Stormwrought Barracks), the [kitchen](Stormwrought Kitchen), a [library](Stormwrought Library), a [museum](Stormwrought Museum), [Duke Balor's court](Stormwrought Court), and the [dungeons](Stormwrought Prison). From the prison, the caverns and lower levels of the [Stormwrought Descent](Stormwrought Descent) can be accessed with the [Key to Stormwrought Castle](Key to Stormwrought Castle).
