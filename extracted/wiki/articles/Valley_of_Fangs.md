# Valley of Fangs

The **Valley of Fangs** is a small valley between two massive stone formations located in the central [Badlands](Badlands). It is the location of one of the many small quest camps in the region, this one containing [Martek the Exiled](Martek the Exiled), [Rigglefuzz](Rigglefuzz), and [Jazzrik](Jazzrik). Travel through the valley can be hazardous as it is home to both wolves and cougars.
