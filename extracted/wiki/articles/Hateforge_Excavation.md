# Hateforge Excavation

The **Hateforge Excavation** is a Dark Iron mining site located in the eastern mountains of the [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes). The Shadowforge Miner's Union has set up a [quarry](Hateforge Quarry) in the mountains here.
