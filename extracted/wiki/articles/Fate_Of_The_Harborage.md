## Objectives
Alert Sanv K'la of the imminent danger.
- [Sanv Tas'dal](Sanv Tas'dal) slain

## Description
Quickly, rush to the Harborage and warn Sanv K'la! If we do not evacuate all in time, who knows what horrible tragedy will befall us! I will attempt to contact Master Akh Z'ador in the meantime, hopefully he can aid us sooner than anticipated. <race>, I place all my hopes on you. Perhaps there is still time.

## Rewards
- 8300 [XP](XP)

## Progress
<name>, you are restless. Tell me, could you find Akh Z'ador?

## Completion
I am devastated. Not even in the wildest of dreams could I have imagined this to happen. Sanv Tas'dal, once my mentor as chieftain and noble leader of our tribe, succumbing to the sweet lies of the Burning Legion. If it wasn't for you and Akh Z'ador, then my people and I would have followed him back to Draenor, to live the rest of our lives as drones for the Legion's machinations.

The Harborage is forever in your debt. Words cannot express how heavy my gratitude towards your selflessness lies, so please: Take this symbol of appreciation from all draenei of this humble sanctuary.

  **Sanv Tas'dal says:** Anyone eager to follow me back into the fulfilling embrace of our saviors, come forth! And those who wish to defy me...
