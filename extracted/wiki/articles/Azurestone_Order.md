# Azurestone Order

The **Azurestone Order** is an ancient order of [dwarven](dwarven) mages. Founded long ago when the very caves of [Ironforge](Ironforge) were being mined, they had come across the [Azurestone](Azurestone), an old and magically potent stone through which many of the first dwarven mages had gotten their footing within the realm of arcana.
