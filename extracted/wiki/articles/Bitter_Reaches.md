# Bitter Reaches

The **Bitter Reaches** is the name given to the northeastern region of [Azshara](Azshara). It is densely populated by [hippogryph](hippogryph)s, [ooze](ooze)s and [chimaerae](chimaerae), except along the northern edge where elite [mountain giant](mountain giant)s have displaced most of the wildlife.  Some [herb](herb)s such as [Mountain Silversage](Mountain Silversage) grow here, though sparsely.
