# Black Lotus

A **Black Lotus** is a rare [herb](herb) that can be gathered with [Herbalism](Herbalism) (300).

## Source
Black Lotus can be found in [Winterspring](Winterspring), [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), [Burning Steppes](Burning Steppes), [Silithus](Silithus), and outside the instance portals in [Dire Maul](Dire Maul). It is rare in all of these places and never appears anywhere else.
