# Sen'jin Village

## History
After the [Trolls](Trolls) of the [Darkspear Tribe](Darkspear Tribe) were forced to flee the [Echo Isles](Echo Isles), [Vol'jin](Vol'jin) relocated his people to the southern coast of [Durotar](Durotar).  Vol'jin named the village after his former master, who fell while saving his people from rampaging Murlocs long ago. Vol'jin now resides in [Orgrimmar](Orgrimmar) in order to be on hand should he be needed by [Thrall](Thrall). In his stead, he has left the troll, [Master Gadrin](Master Gadrin) in charge of seeing to the needs of the small village.

## Overview
Usually the first town most [orc](orc)s and [troll](troll)s encounter on their adventures after around level 5. The road from the [Valley of Trials](Valley of Trials) leads east to a crossroads. The path to Sen'jin heads souutheast, while the other path turns north toward [Razor Hill](Razor Hill).
