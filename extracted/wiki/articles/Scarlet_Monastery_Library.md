## Dungeon denizens
The Library, the right-most entrance, consists of four sections: the [Huntsman's Cloister](Huntsman's Cloister), with [Scarlet Beastmaster](Scarlet Beastmaster)s and the boss [Houndmaster Loksey](Houndmaster Loksey); the [Gallery of Treasures](Gallery of Treasures); the [Sanctum of the Fist](Sanctum of the Fist) and the boss [Brother Wystan](Brother Wystan); and the [Athenaeum](Athenaeum (Scarlet Monastery)) with the boss [Arcanist Doan](Arcanist Doan).
