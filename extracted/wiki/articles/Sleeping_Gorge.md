# Sleeping Gorge

The **Sleeping Gorge** is a small chasm in eastern [Deadwind Pass](Deadwind Pass). One of the paths south to [Karazhan](Karazhan) runs through the gorge for a stretch before turning east into the [Vice](Vice).
