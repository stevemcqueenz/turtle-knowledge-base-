# Humanoid

A type of creature or [mob](mob) in the game as distinguished from [beasts](Beast) and [undead](undead). This includes most social creatures that show some form of self-aware, intelligence.

In game terms the important things about humanoids [mobs](mob) are that they:
- Often can [cast](cast) [spell](spell)s.
- Usually run away when at low health.
- Drop [cloth](cloth).
- Cannot be [skinned](skinnable).
- Can be [polymorphed](polymorph) by [mage](mage)s.
- Can be [sapped](sap) by [rogue](rogue)s.
- Can be [feared](fear) by [warlock](warlock)s and [priest](priest)s.
