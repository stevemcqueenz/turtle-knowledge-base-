-Sentinel Hill** is an [Alliance](Alliance) settlement in [Westfall](Westfall) and their last stronghold in the region. It is here that the men and women of the [People's Militia](People's Militia) make their stand against the [Defias Brotherhood](Defias Brotherhood) to keep the land free from corruption after their cause was abandoned by the Stormwind Monarchy.
