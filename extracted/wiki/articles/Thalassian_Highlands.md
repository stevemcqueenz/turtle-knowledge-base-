The **Thalassian Highlands** is the starting zone for [playable high elves](High elf (playable)). 

Located at the remote fringes of [Quel'thalas](Quel'thalas), these lands were initially established as an outpost for [Alah'Thalas](Alah'Thalas). Over time, with the Alliance's assistance, it has developed into a thriving city, complete with its own dock. However, one must not be deceived by the beauty of these lands, for lurking in the shadows are sinister forces, even within the city walls.
