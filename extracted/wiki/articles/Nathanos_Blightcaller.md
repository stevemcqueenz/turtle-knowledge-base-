**Nathanos Blightcaller** (born **Nathanos Marris**) is the champion of the Banshee Queen of the Forsaken, [Sylvanas Windrunner](Sylvanas Windrunner). In life, Nathanos was the first and last of the human ranger lords, trained by the [high elves](high elves) of [Quel'Thalas](Quel'Thalas) in the ways of war and a disciple of Sylvanas herself. Nathanos' accomplishments were unprecedented. He was a tactical genius, responsible for [Alliance](Alliance of Lordaeron) victories spanning a decade of conflict. (ref: [quest: Order Must be Restored]) However, Nathanos had been killed in action in the defense of Lordaeron during the [Third War](Third War), struck down defending his farmstead by the Scourge abomination, [Ramstein the Gorger](Ramstein the Gorger). Nathanos rose as a mindless thrall of the [Scourge](Scourge), bound to the will of the [Lich King](Lich King). Somehow though, deep in his soul, he knew that Sylvanas would come for him, and she did. (ref: [quest: Ramstein])

After he was freed by Sylvanas, Nathanos hunted Ramstein the Gorger across the [Plaguelands](Plaguelands) for months until the abomination fled to the safety of [Stratholme](Stratholme). (ref:  In time, Nathanos returned to his old home, the [Marris Stead](Marris Stead) in the [Eastern Plaguelands](Eastern Plaguelands), with his pet blighthounds on the banshee queen's orders. There he recruited [Horde](Horde) soldiers to fight against corrupted animals such as plaguebats, the [Scourge](Scourge), and the [Scarlet Crusade](Scarlet Crusade)rs of [Tyr's Hand](Tyr's Hand).<ref>[quest: The Scarlet Oracle, Demetria])

Nathanos is secretive regarding personal details of his former life, even sending Horde adventurers to retrieve the Quel'Thalas Registry that contains his file and making sure to inform adventurers that the registry is not to be read. (ref: [quest: The Ranger Lord's Behest])

Nathanos's death did not go unnoticed, nor did the fact that a body had never been found. Master [Mathias Shaw](Mathias Shaw), head of [SI:7](SI:7) - the Stormwind intelligence agency - received some disturbing reports of unusual Forsaken activity in the Eastern Plaguelands, and sent five agents to investigate. Only one of those agents returned, babbling incoherently about "Nathanos" and the word "Blightcaller." He died in his sleep just days after his return, and Alliance adventurers were asked to go investigate their disappearance. (ref: [quest: Honor the Dead]) The only thing SI:7 had to go on was that Nathanos Marris may have been slain by this "Blightcaller," but the truth was far less pleasant. After investigating the grounds around the Marris Stead, adventurers uncovered the ugly reality - Nathanos was the Blightcaller, now a champion of the Banshee Queen. (ref: [quest: The Eastern Plagues]) (ref: [quest: The Blightcaller Cometh]) Once it is determined that Nathanos is the Blightcaller, Highlord [Bolvar Fordragon](Bolvar Fordragon) enlists Alliance adventurers to gather an army and destroy him.
