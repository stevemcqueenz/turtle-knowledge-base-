# Thunderbrew Distillery

The **Thunderbrew Distillery** is a [dwarven](dwarf) tavern in [Kharanos](Kharanos). It features trainers for [warrior](warrior)s, [priest](priest)s, [paladin](paladin)s, [mage](mage)s, [rogue](rogue)s, [cooking](cooking), and [first aid](first aid). It also has several vendors, along with [Innkeeper Belm](Innkeeper Belm) with the usual services. The distillery is known far and wide for its excellent ale.

The Thunderbrew Distillery was founded by [Ragnar](Ragnar Thunderbrew) and [Grimbooze Thunderbrew](Grimbooze Thunderbrew)'s father, [Arkilus Thunderbrew](Arkilus Thunderbrew). (ref: [Ragnar Thunderbrew#Quotes](Ragnar Thunderbrew#Quotes))
