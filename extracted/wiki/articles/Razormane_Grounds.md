# Razormane Grounds

The [Razormane](Razormane) [quilboar](quilboar), dubbed 'pigmen' by the orcs, have been fighting to expand out of the [Barrens](Barrens) and into [Durotar](Durotar). The **Razormane Grounds**, located in central Durotar, are the frontlines of that battle. Bones of dead animals stripped of their flesh to provide for the creatures litter the grounds, and brambles burst out of the red mountains high above. [Orc](Durotar orc)s, [troll](Darkspear tribe)s, and even the occasional [tauren](tauren), [undead](Forsaken) and [goblins](Goblin (playable)) fight to keep the Razormane out of the orcish homeland, but for every one cut down, two seem to emerge from the thickets. The leaders of this quilboar encampement are [Geolord Mottle](Geolord Mottle) and [Captain Flat Tusk](Captain Flat Tusk), captain of the Battleguard.
