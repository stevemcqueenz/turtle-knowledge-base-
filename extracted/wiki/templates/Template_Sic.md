# Template:Sic

From Latin *sic erat scriptum*, meaning "thus was it written". It indicates that the quoted matter has been transcribed exactly as found in the source text, and that an inaccuracy was present in the original quote. For more info on its uses, see [Sic on Wikipedia.](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sic)
