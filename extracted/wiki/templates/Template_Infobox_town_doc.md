  -Infobox town** replaces the "Characteristics" section of most town pages. Feel free to further develop it.

Please note that the fields below all pertain to the *[World of Warcraft](World of Warcraft)* [MMORPG](MMORPG), although some may come from official sources of lore and should be cited.

  - Valid field entries:**

  -*faction:** Alliance, Horde, or Neutral based on the MMORPG only (default Neutral)
  -*name:** town name (default PAGENAME).
  -*ss:** ideal picture of the town [optional]
  -*caption:** screenshot caption [optional]
  -*type:** the type of town. Examples: City, Village, Camp
  -*pop:** total population of the town
  -*lpop:** last known population of the town
  -*race:** races within the town. Population breakdown for each race in parenthesis [optional]. Please use the following separated by line breaks (<nowiki>

  -*frace:** races that used to be in the town [only use if race parameter is used]
  -*government**: list type of government [optional]
  -*leader:** list only if there is a recognized leader of that specific town [optional]
  -*fleader:** a list of former leaders of that specific town [only use if leader parameter is used]
  -*language:** languages within the town [optional]
  -*faith:** faiths within the town [optional]
  -*resource:** resources within the town [optional]
  -*affiliation:** the affiliation of the zone, usually the faction that controls it [optional]
  -*faffiliation:** the former affiliations of the town [only use if affiliation parameter is used]
  -*organizations:** the organizations that hold this town as their base of operations [optional]
  -*loc:** location of zone, or if not known then the general area of zone in relation to the rest of the continent
  -*status:** what is the current status of the town. Examples: Active, abandoned, evacuating, destroyed [optional]
  -*facilities:** used to show the normal facilities [optional]
  -**inn:** has an inn, yes/no
  -**mailbox:** has a mailbox, yes/no
  -**stables:** has a stable master, yes/no
  -**anvil:** has an anvil and forge, yes/no
  -**bank:** has a bank, yes/no
  -**auctions:** has an auction house, yes/no
  -*trainers:** used to show the if town contains trainers [optional]
  -**class:** has a class trainer, yes/no
  -**profession:** has a profession trainer, yes/no
  -*travel:** used to show the traveling options [optional]
  -**flightpath:** has a flight master, yes/no
  -**boat:** has a boat, zeppelin or other mass-transit options, yes/no
  -**portal:** has a permanent portal that leads out, yes/no
  -*source:** where the info came from!
