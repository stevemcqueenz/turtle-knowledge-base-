# Template:Infobox race/doc

<pre></pre>

  - Valid field entries:**

  -*name:** race name.
  -*image:** ideal picture of race (Artwork preferred over in-game screenshots).
  -*type:** what the creature is classified as.
  -*faction:** major groups affiliated with.
  -*character:** the classes associated with this race (keep assumptions to a minimum). 
  -*capital:** capital in which the races calls home.
  -*fcapital:** eventual former capital which the races called home.
  -*leader:** the racial leader or leaders.
  -*fleader:** eventual former racial leader or leaders.
  -*mount:** the creature this race uses as a mount.
  -*homeworld:** where the race lives (ie Azeroth, Draenor, Argus).
  -*environment:** the environment(s) in which the race is often found.
  -*area:** the areas in which they can be found.
  -*language:** the language(s) they speak.
  -*exact:** exact height range.
  -*height:** their average height.
  -*weight:** average weight.
  -*organization:** groups in which they can be found (examples: Flights, pairs, tribes, clans).
  -*alignment:** their alignment (examples: Neural, evil, chaotic evil). RPG-only
  -*source:** source given (should be a lore book or expansion).
