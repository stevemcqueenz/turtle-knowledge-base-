# Leveling Challenges

### Leveling challenges
Prove yourself a great adventurer by completing one or multiple challenges! Get unique rewards such as titles, tabards, companions, mounts and more!

[![Hardcore Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/hardcore_icon-CRQrErcL.png)  ### Hardcore
This is the ultimate test of skill, caution, and sheer stubbornness: level your character to 60 without dying. Not even once. Once you’re level 60, you can participate in raiding and PvP without a risk of losing your character.
Achievement Title — Immortal](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=1636)     ![Craftmaster Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/craftmaster_icon-a_4oKMOC.png)  ### Traveling Craftmaster
No equipments are better than the ones you make yourself!
Level using only equipment you craft.
Achievement Title — Craftmaster

[![Slow And Steady Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/slow_icon-DGzFkKji.png)  ### Slow and Steady
If you really like questing and leveling, play in turtle mode, your creature kill rates will be set to 0.5 rates. Enjoy your journey and receive rewards every 10 levels.](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=482)
[![Level One Lunatic Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/l1l-B1_O8aUE.webp)   ![Hardcore Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/l1l_icon-B9APYD5g.png)  ### Level One Lunatic
During this challenge, you’ll earn exclusive titles to prove just how far a noob can go, all while staying level one:

- Earn 10,000 honorable kills to show you’re more than just fresh meat.
- Reach Exalted with at least one faction to prove that even a tiny hero can have powerful friends.
- Obtain the Arena Master Trinket to demonstrate that even at level one, you’re an unstoppable force of nature.
- Complete 100 quests available to you, earning the respect of tutorial NPCs everywhere.
Achievement Titles — The Wee Warlord, Pint-Sized Politician, Arena's Smallest Smasher,
Hero of Goldshire (Alliance) / Lazy Peon's Foreman (Horde)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=17653)      ![Boaring Adventure Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/boaring_icon-B-wl-uYh.png)  ### Boaring Adventure
In this challenge, leveling up is a real big deal. Experience comes exclusively from slaying boars!
Achievement Title — The Hambringer

[![War Mode Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/warmode_icon-BPPXs0Jc.png)  ### War Mode
You can no longer disable PvP but experience gain from all sources is increased.](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=2&t=20766)    ![Exhaustion Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/exhaustion_icon-PsRWJNOu.png)  ### Exhaustion
No longer gaining rested experience, but your weapon skill gain will be doubled.

![Vagrant Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/vagrant_icon-BKQ8SpKI.png)  ### Vagrant's Endeavor
Live the simple life! You'll be forced to wear nothing but poor or common quality gear until you hit level 60.

Enchanting items is not allowed.
Achievement Title — The Wanderer and a mule to lug around all your newly-unlocked epics!
