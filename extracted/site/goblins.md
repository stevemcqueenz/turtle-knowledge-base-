# Goblins — Turtle WoW

![High Elf Image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/ring_goblin-BMPz6cgH.png)  ## Goblins of the Horde   #### Durotar Labor Union      S     hrewd, greedy, and ruthless, goblins have built a reputation for putting                      profit above all else. While many see them as neutral players in global affairs, history tells a different story — such as the Steamwheedle Cartel's alliance with the Old Horde during the Second War. For goblins, loyalty is a commodity, and every decision is a transaction. Goblins don't celebrate heroes for honor or courage — they admire those who strike the biggest deals, invent the wildest gadgets, or come out on top when everything explodes. They're brilliant engineers, clever traders, and expert sailors, but what truly defines them is their relentless pursuit of opportunity — no matter the cost.

After fleeing Stonetalon, the goblins of the Durotar Labor Union gave in to their greatest weakness: greed. They've taken over a resource-rich island and built a new empire, this time on the backs of hopeful goblins trying to escape the Venture Co., only to end up in the same trap under new management. Now it's your turn to break the cycle, rise through the ranks, and carve out your place in the Union. This new zone introduces an expanded level 1–10 starting experience for goblins.
