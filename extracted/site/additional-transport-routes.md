# Additional Transport Routes — Turtle WoW

### Additional   ### Transport Routes   By creating new transport routes and flight paths, the Horde and Alliance can now access even the farthest corners of the world. Including those undeservedly forgotten.

#### New Flight Paths

**![Small Alliance Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_alliance_small-sNO0Y6rc.webp) Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility**
![Footer Line](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  Located in Dun Morogh, it serves as the new home for the Gnomeregan Exiles. This Alliance town offers familiar amenities like an auction house and bank, as well as general goods vendors.

**![Small Alliance Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_horde_small-B180F7JY.webp) Steepcliff Port**
![Footer Line](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  Ruins of a once-thriving port, now bound—alongside Glenshire—to the will of the Banshee Queen. Aid Duke Nargelas in culling the remaining Scourge and those who still fly the banner of Lordaeron in the name of Othmar Garithos.

**![Small Alliance Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_alliance_small-sNO0Y6rc.webp) Dun Agrath**
![Footer Line](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  A quaint farming village nestled in the high ridges of the Wetlands. Unlocked by discovering Menethil Harbor.

**![Small Horde Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_horde_small-B180F7JY.webp) Mosh'ogg Refuge**
![Footer Line](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  The ogre clan of Mosh’ogg is home to Gilijim’s Isle and locked in an eternal conflict with the remaining troll tribes, struggling for dominance on the island. Unlocked by discovering Booty Bay.

**![Small Alliance Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_alliance_small-sNO0Y6rc.webp) Ironforge Airfield**
![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  Vital for many supply lines within Alliance territories, this icy plateau overlooks Dun Morogh and acts as a base for Ironforge’s mountaineers. Unlocked by discovering Ironforge.

**![Small Alliance Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_alliance_small-sNO0Y6rc.webp) Alah'thalas**
![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  Newly established city in the Northern Eastern Kingdoms that the High Elves now call their home.

**![Small Alliance Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_alliance_small-sNO0Y6rc.webp) Caelan's Rest**
![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  Shipwrecked on the shores of Lapidis Isle, these stout survivors from Kul Tiras fight tooth and nail on this hostile island surrounded by foes and traitors alike. Unlocked by discovering Booty Bay.

**![Small Horde Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_horde_small-B180F7JY.webp) ![Small Alliance Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_alliance_small-sNO0Y6rc.webp) Nordanaar**
![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  As Nordrassil heals from demonic corruption, new threats emerge from the shadows. Druids of Hyjal have gathered in the ancient shrine of Nordanaar and lead the efforts to stop the Emerald Nightmare.

**![Small Alliance Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_alliance_small-sNO0Y6rc.webp) Ravenshire, Gilneas**
![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  The heart of the Ravenwood Rebellion, last bastion of resistance against tyranny of the Greymane dynasty.

**![Small Horde Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_horde_small-B180F7JY.webp) Stillward Church**
![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  Ravaged by the Greymane forces in the past, this abandoned church now serves as a main military base for the Forsaken in Gilneas.

**![Small Alliance Icon](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/icon_alliance_small-sNO0Y6rc.webp) Bael Hardul**
![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/footerline-CqQf1_I8.webp)  Dwarven outpost in Stonetalon Mountains estabilished during the events of Warcraft 3, now reclaimed by the Explorers' League.
