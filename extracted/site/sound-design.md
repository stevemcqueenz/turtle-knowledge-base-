# Sound Design — Turtle WoW

### Turtle WoW Sound Design Team   Sound design has always been a big part of the Warcraft Universe. It helped bring players closer to the stories this world had to offer and enhanced the experience its players had. We aim to keep up with this tradition and provide our players with excellent sound design to match the new content we provide.

### Original Soundtracks  Thanks to our talented artists and musicians (Gabriel Contreras • József Kiss • Charles Lobaugh), we are able to have numerous new soundtracks that you will experience as you embark on your journey through Azeroth.
Warm thanks for the contributions in the past: Ivan Nikolic • Sauli Pulkkinen • Parhaus

#### Sound Design Team
**★Volca**
Hi! I am Volca, the Department Lead of Music & Sound at Turtle WoW. I have been making music since 2006, starting with EDM, and in 2019, I switched to orchestral music. My main inspiration comes from EDM all the way to various cinematic tracks. My favorite video game is World of Warcraft!

**★Bolton**
I'm Bolton, the voice of Zappo Zapblast and a composer for Turtle WoW. Music and games is my passion, the TWoW team inspires me to grow. I draw inspiration from anime, horror, film and jazz-fusion—great music is everywhere, if you look.

**★DJ Dapper**
Bal'a dash, malanore! I'm DJ Dapper, otherwise known as Dappertetu, a voice you hear somewhat frequently on the Everlook Broadcasting Co. Radio through a variety of ads, radio skits, songs, and lore readings as a friendly high elf, sexy narrator, angry spider, and a dying gnome or twelve.

**★Poruu**
Hi! I’m poruu, a composer and music producer from Chile. I joined the project back in 2021. My influences range from John Williams to Radiohead, I LOVE jazz, coffee and fashion. My favourite videogames? Risk of Rain 2, Halo and TW, of course!

**★Ivan**
Heyy there, I’m Ivan, a passionate Music Composer, Producer, and Music Teacher with expertise in classical, jazz, pop, rock, and film scores. With a Bachelor's and Master's in Music, I create music that blends diverse styles, and working on Turtle-Wow has been life-changing and I look forward to new (music) adventures!

#### Unique Character Voices    As we introduce many new characters, we strive to give them more life to make them feel like a part of the world they inhabit. And what better way to do that than to provide them with their own voices.

#### Charles Lobaugh, 'ol Shanky   The community that Turtle WoW fosters is one of teamwork, passion, and care that is almost unprecedented in other games past and present. I'm thankful to be working with such incredible people and extremely humbled by their willingness to let me express myself in my voicework. For Azeroth!

Charles has been a part of our project for many years, breathing life into our new characters with his voice acting skills!
