# Client UI Improvements — Turtle WoW

### Client Improvements   As the development continues and we add more content to the game, we aim to help you better navigate through all the changes. For that reason, our team is hard at work on improving the interface to make your journey more enjoyable.

![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/tradeskill-CcYSBMFX.webp)

**Improved Profession UI**
The profession UI is now larger, having additional options such as **Have materials** and **Provides skill-up**. There is also a search bar along the bottom of the recipe list to save you from the endless scrolling!

![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/postman-NEb-EVJq.webp)

**Items Sent to the Winner**
Items won through group loot will no longer become free-for-all if the roll winner's bags are full. Any rare or higher quality items that are still unlooted upon the corpse despawning will be sent to the winner by mail, instead of being lost forever.

![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/world_map-DbSuT0Z6.webp)

**Minimized World Map**
Your world map can now be minimized by clicking the arrow button next to the close button, allowing a more streamlined experience.

![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/turtlewow_hcchat-BGp3oIsK.webp)

**Hardcore Channel**
You can access hardcore channel by typing a chat command /h now, if you're hardcore player. This channel is disabled at level 60 if you are not an Inferno Mode player and therefore immortal again.

![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/looking-x2NKKeNN.png)

**LFT — Looking For Turtles**
Looking for group? Look for Turtles! We've implemented a queue-formation bulletin board to allow players to form groups easier and faster. No more world chat spam or random groups--party with who you want, when you want. This will not teleport you to the dungeon, it will only help form the group for your adventure!

![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/turtlewow_questlog-BNFFx9lM.webp)

**Extended Quest Log**
The quest log is now larger, giving players enough space to see all their quests at the same time. This does not allow us to bypass the 20 quest limit, which we plan to change in [Turtle WoW 2.0](https://turtlecraft.gg/remastered)

![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/turtlewow_xpgaintoggle-CKEBLtKV.webp)

**XP Gain Toggle on Player's Portrait**
Experience can now be toggled on and off by right-clicking on your character portrait.

![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/turtlewow_collections-DbjxC5rF.webp)

**Pet & Mount Collections**
Rejoice pet collectors for your little ones will no longer have to spend their time at the bank waiting for you. New collection tab can be used to bring all of your pets with you on your numerous journeys. You heard about pulling a rabbit out of the hat. How about pulling a horse out of the spellbook? Amongst other functions, there is a mount tab where you can store all your favorite mounts.

![image](https://turtlecraft.gg/build/assets/turtlewow_battlegroundfinder-SVUQd37u.webp)

**Battleground Finder on the Minimap**
From the sands of the Blood Ring to the timeless conflicts of Sunnyglade Valley, there are many opportunities to earn fame and glory for your faction!
