# Warrior — Protection — Tank ("deep prot", sword and board)

The 31+-point Protection build with a one-hander and shield. The Fury/Prot hybrid tank is a separate playbook (`fury-tank.md`); Arms-based dungeon tanking while leveling is in `leveling.md`.

Eras: pre-1.18.1 (before 2025-10-03), 1.18.1-announced-pre-release (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), post-1.18.1 (from 2026-03-20). Kit revisions: CC2 (1.17.2, 2024-11-01), Dec-2024 revision, CC3 (1.18.0, 2025-08-15), 1.18.1.

## Overview

- **Role**: main tank / off tank in dungeons and raids. Staff intent: "improving Protection leveling builds and opening up new builds for end-game, Shield Slam ... has gained significant damage scaling, and ... the lower half of the tree has been jazzed up with many new threat and damage options" [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102974#p102974). Staff in Jan 2026: "Protection Warriors have been falling behind other tanks. These changes provide targeted improvements, while a more comprehensive review of the Protection talent tree and overall kit is planned for the future" [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **Strengths (sources)**: best mitigation toolkit — Shield Block makes you crit- and crush-immune for the blocked hits ("You are literally critimmune if you are under effect of Shield Block and have usual warrior avoidance stats" [Xudo (player), 2023-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=38228#p38228); "crit and crush immunity if using shield block and shield slam on CD and 10% less damage taken" [Ellifta (player), 2025-01-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115882#p115882)); Shield Wall, Last Stand, Disarm, Shield Slam dispel; the go-to progression tank because tier sets are tank sets and MC drops tank gear ("warriors are the go-to tank for progression raids. even in mostly blue gear they can still tank most of the MC bosses" [Zulnam (player), 2024-10-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103027#p103027)); fear bosses and Maexxna/Loatheb are "just much easier with a warrior than a feral" [Atreidon (player), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102946#p102946); "warrior tanks are good. Their threat generation provides the best gameplay where actually threat management is a thing" [Calli (player), 2024-12-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113382#p113382).
- **Weaknesses (sources)**: single-target threat behind bear and paladin after CC2 ("It's common knowledge by now that warrior tank is severely lacking compared to other tanks in threat department" [Ellifta (player), 2026-02-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167684#p167684); "A bear in dungeons blues can rip threat from a t3 thunderfury protection tank effortlessly without even using taunt" [Bobguy (player), 2025-08-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142932#p142932); "Paladin has a 262.5% threat modifier on holy strike and bear has a whopping 523% of his damage dealth as threat on savage bite" [Atreidon (player), 2024-11-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110503#p110503)); weak AoE threat (Thunder Clap "does 100 damage at max rank without any scaling" [Licata (player), 2026-01-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166667#p166667); "no real AoE threat like other tanks" [Morkahja (player), 2026-02-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167180#p167180)); threat concentrated in Shield Slam so a parried/dodged Shield Slam (boss front: "14% parry chance + a 6.5% dodge chance") is "a devastating loss ... for at least 4.5s" [Eyeburn (player), 2026-03-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170786#p170786) (Xudo replies you still have Revenge and Sunder [Xudo (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170939#p170939) — contested); rage starvation when avoidance is high ("when you dodge/parry you get nothing" [Xronisf79 (player), 2024-01-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85027#p85027)); Shield Block and Improved Shield Slam's block buff overwrite each other instead of stacking ("They cancel each other out" [Ellifta (player), 2026-02-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167684#p167684); [Eyeburn (player), 2026-02-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169309#p169309)); tier sets lack block value + crit together ("none of the warrior tier sets have both of these stats in the same set" [Ellifta (player), 2026-01-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164933#p164933)).
- **Content**: dungeons — fine but "you don't need to invest a single point into the prot tree until late game" [SvenS2 (player), 2026-04-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175303#p175303); raids — main tank for MC–BWL progression and fear/Maexxna-type fights; late raids (Naxx/Kara40) contested vs bear/paladin; PvP — a meme spec with a niche (stuns, Shield Slam dispel, 7k HP) [Manletow (player), 2024-12-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112703#p112703); hardcore — favored for dungeon-only leveling (see `leveling.md`).
- **Community standing**: **contested** through 2024–2026. Positive: "Sword and board warrior is the meta now. Shieldslam runs laps around bloodthirst when it comes to threat ... for 40 man raids, sword and board is the way to go now. More threat and more survivability!" [Atreidon (player), 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108893#p108893) (raid leader of a "content Clear guild" who moved two fury-tank MTs to sword and board [p=108957](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108957#p108957)); "Deep Prot's threat generation after CC2 is higher than Furry Prot's" [Herods (player), 2025-05-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130503#p130503); "is that why speedruns bring warrior tanks?" [amanagor (player), 2024-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112552#p112552). Negative: "warrior is currently the worst tank of the game. You're actually gimping your raid to even bring one unless they are an outstanding tank. Druids and paladins are the meta now" [Biteyou (player), 2024-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112545#p112545); "our GM in Mortality swapped over to Druid because warrior is in such a bad state for tanking" [Gildark (player), 2024-12-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112686#p112686); "deep prot is a dead spec and even dtactics is garbage" [Eyeburn (player), 2026-02-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169309#p169309). Bots: a prot warrior is always accepted as a dungeon tank; in raids expect to be compared with bears/paladins on threat and to be asked to tank fear/single-target bosses.

## Stat priority

- **Defense** to remove crits; "With 370 defence in Naxx, in my experience this leads to 1 crit per boss fight and usually 4-5 crushing blows" *in Battle Stance without Shield Block* [Ellifta (player), 2025-01-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115882#p115882). Exact cap: not stated on Turtle ("Linearizing Weapon Skill vs Defense Skill" was announced [dev blog, 2024-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13840); numbers gap). Vanilla baseline (not from turtlecraft.gg): 440 defense removes boss crits.
- **Hit and weapon skill** matter because warrior threat "relies primarily on white hits" and Sunder/Revenge/Shield Slam can miss/be parried: "weaponskill is therefor invaluable to a warrior tank" [Cheruscan (player), 2025-07-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142315#p142315); +5% hit from talents was a repeated wish [Calli (player), 2024-06-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97903#p97903). Weapon skill does not reduce boss parry (Xudo quoting Daaki, Fury playbook).
- **Stamina / effective health** and armor for raid bosses ("Being fan of Effective Health ... reach minimum required defense cap and then invest into EH" [Bogyone (player), 2024-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85806#p85806)); tank gear at 7k unbuffed is cited for PvP prot (Manletow).
- **Block value + crit** is what deep prot "was meant to be itemized around" (Ellifta, T2 thread) — Shield Slam scales with block value and AP.
- **Dungeon set**: "having a specific Dungeon Tanking set really feels great" — T1 + dungeon blues for rage income [Zahnfee (player), 2024-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85564#p85564); more avoidance = less rage.
- Leveling: Stamina, then whatever gives rage; see `leveling.md`.

## Single-target rotation (boss)

Kit: Dec-2024 → 1.18.1 (Revenge 6 s CD, Shield Slam 6 s minus 1.5 s talented, Concussion Blow 20 s / 0 rage / +10 rage, Improved Shield Slam 4.5 s block buff).

**Opener**: (Charge in Battle Stance if pulling → Defensive Stance with Tactical Mastery) or ranged pull; **Bloodrage** before the pull (it puts you in combat and gives rage; "/cast Bloodrage /yell I AM RANGE PULLING ... /cast Shoot" [Gdawg45 (player), 2022-12-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31058#p31058)); first GCDs: **Concussion Blow → Shield Slam** ("opening with Concussion Blow + Shield Slam would result in significantly more threat" [Taralej90 (player), 2025-08-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146132#p146132); staff design: Concussion Blow "used for maximizing single target opener/snap threat as the first global cooldown"), then **Sunder Armor** to 5 and **Demoralizing Shout** early ("Early in the encounter is it recommended to use Demoralizing Shout to reduce damage taken and to generate threat" — Gdawg45).

**Priority**:
1. **Shield Slam** on cooldown (biggest threat; also 50% (+25/50% Gag Order) magic dispel).
2. **Revenge** whenever available after a block/dodge/parry (Reprisal refunds the rage; "If you hit a mob with a revenge you will not lose aggro 99% of the time" [Sixgun (player), 2023-04-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41025#p41025)). Revenge needs Defensive Stance.
3. **Shield Block** on cooldown for crit/crush immunity and a guaranteed Revenge ("Shield block gives you a guaranteed revenge proc, it is worth it, use it" — Sixgun); mind the overwrite with Improved Shield Slam's block buff: "You should not have to play piano and watch whether boss managed to hit you or not to be crush immune" (Ellifta) — in practice use Shield Block right after the Improved-Shield-Slam charge has been consumed, or accept the overwrite.
4. **Sunder Armor** until 5 stacks, then as the filler ("Then, when I have rage, sunder... You spend 10-2 rage..." — Xudo on low-level Shield Block; sunder-spam is the accepted deep-prot filler: "I'm spamming sunder" [Matches (player), 2024-12-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114231#p114231)).
5. **Heroic Strike** as the rage dump at high rage (on next swing; "If you are using Heroic Strike as a tank, you are dumping rage" — Arkaris); Improved Heroic Strike is why some take 3 Arms points.
6. **Concussion Blow** on cooldown after the opener (20 s; free rage since CC3) — it is threat, not only a stun (boss-immune to the stun but "does twice the amount of threat when the target is immune" was only a player wish [Isvya (player), 2024-11-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109791#p109791)).
7. Thunder Clap once per pull for the attack-speed debuff (usable in Defensive Stance).
8. Execute phase: with Defensive Tactics swap to Battle/Berserker Stance and spam Execute ("This talent allows you to exit def stance and spam execute when boss has less than 20% of hp" [Xudo (player), 2025-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115711#p115711)); Eyeburn notes threat is rarely an issue at that point anyway.

**Defensive Tactics stance choice** (Ellifta, 2025-01-08): Battle Stance = "+7-8% more threat gained per point of damage dealt compared to defensive stance, 10% more damage ... roughly ends up to 15-20% more threat overall", Overpower/Execute available, but no Shield Block crit/crush immunity — use on threat-critical bosses ("Vaelstratz or Maexna"); Defensive Stance = crit/crush immune, 10% less damage taken — "twin emps, Chromaggus or Patchwerk"; Berserker Stance = +3% crit, Whirlwind for AoE threat, fear immunity via Berserker Rage. Math: Defensive Stance with Defiance 5/5 = 1.30 × 1.15 = 1.495 threat per damage; with Defensive Tactics 3/3 in another stance = (1.495 − 1) × 1.8 + 1 = 1.891, times the hidden 0.8 Battle/Berserker threat modifier = 1.512 [Ellifta (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156473#p156473) (measured with TWThreat 1.3; 1.18.1 Defiance 20% changes the numbers — not recomputed by anyone). Whether Whirlwind can be used with a shield equipped is contested in one thread (Captain4k no / Morkahja "I use it often" [Morkahja (player), 2026-02-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167704#p167704)).

## Multi-target / AoE

- **2–3 targets (dungeon pack)**: Charge the first → Defensive Stance → **Thunder Clap** ("For AoE tanking you do want to spam tclap as your main aoe threat generator ... tabbing sunder is no longer necessary nor recommended" [Syrathegreat (player), 2023-05-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47322#p47322); the thread's other voice: "Some dudes say that you can charge in and literally spam tclap ... Viable, however, not optimal" [Sinrek (player), 2023-09-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=61271#p61271)) → **Demoralizing Shout** ("Demo Shout does help with threat in AoE pulls" — Sixgun) → tab through mobs with Revenge/Sunder/Shield Slam: "For the rest of the engagement I tab between the mobs and use Revenge as much as possible while using Shield Block on CD" (Gdawg45); "Use a threat meter, tabbing between targets mid-fight to decide where you are low, and which mob needs another revenge/sunder" (Sixgun). Whirlwind in Berserker Stance with Defensive Tactics is an AoE-threat option (Xudo, Morkahja). Cleave on next swing with 2+ targets.
- **4+ targets**: Thunder Clap (4-target cap per Theeviltpot: "an instant attack that hits 4 people" [Theeviltpot (player), 2026-02-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167206#p167206)), Demoralizing Shout, Challenging Shout as the emergency AoE taunt (Xudo, below); accept that paladins/bears do it better; ask for CC and mark the kill order.
- Skull macro / mark kill order so DPS stack on one target: "making a macro that notifies the party of the kill order"; "mark current target with skull ... build some threat on skull, tab target to other enemies, use 1 or 2 skills and go back to skull" [Reploidrocsa (player), 2023-09-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=62793#p62793).

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Taunt chain**: "You have 3 Taunt-like abilities. First is taunt. Second is Mocking Blow. Third is Challenging Shout. At level 30 you also get Intercept. When I lose agro and mob run away from me, then I switch to berserker stance, use Intercept, switch to Defensive Stance and use Taunt. It all takes 1 GCD ... When my Taunt is on CD, I switch to Battle Stance and use Mocking blow. When everyting is on CD, I use challenging shout" [Xudo (player), 2023-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40901#p40901). Taunt macro: `/run if not UnitIsUnit('player','targettarget') then CastSpellByName('Taunt'); end` + `/cast Shield Block` (Gdawg45). Intervene to reach a healer who pulled aggro [Akos1896 (player), 2025-01-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116000#p116000); Improved Intervene gives snare immunity.
- **Shield Wall / Last Stand**: emergency; Shield Wall shares the 30-min cooldown with Recklessness/Retaliation and ends if you remove the shield (staff); Improved Shield Wall −5/10 min.
- **Shield Block**: on cooldown when tanking bosses; not needed in low-level dungeons ("You spend 10-2 rage to reduce next attack damage for about 40 points. I think it don't worth it" [Xudo (player), 2023-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40901#p40901)) — Sixgun disagrees (guaranteed Revenge). Contested at low level, consensus at 60.
- **Berserker Rage** for fears (stance dance; Tactical Mastery).
- **Rage**: Bloodrage on cooldown / before pulls; **conserve rage at the end of a pull for the next one** ("Conserve your rage, if you are towards the end of a fight/pull, everything is going fine, then stop using abilities and save that rage for the next pull" — Sixgun; "Best you can do on a rage tank is get in the habit of 'saving' some rage between pulls" [Bob022 (player), 2023-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40865#p40865)); more avoidance/tier gear = less rage, hence the T1-set/dungeon-set advice and the fury-prot temptation; absorb shields on you or the target reduce rage income (Fury playbook). Orc tanks: "DO NOT USE THE RACIAL AS IT WILL REDUCE THE HEALING ON YOU" (Gdawg45; the CC2 Blood Fury has −25% healing 25 s per [Dragunovi (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102974#p102974) racial notes quoted in thread).
- **Downtime**: watch party mana before pulling ("I will usually feel comfortable pulling if everyone is 50% or more mana" — Gdawg45).

## Role strategy

- **Positioning/pulling**: pull back to the group; "You can keep your group back a ways from your pulls and backwards-walk your pulls as you bring them in, allowing you crucial seconds to build a threat lead" (Bob022); line-of-sight pulls for casters; face mobs away from the party; control runner direction — "ALL Runners Run AWAY FROM Whatever Holds Their Aggro" so stand so the line through the mob ends at a wall, never at another pack [Roxanneflowers (player), 2020-07-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7046#p7046).
- **Interrupts**: Shield Bash on casters, especially heals ("If it attempts to heal itself I shield bash it AS FAST AS POSSIBLE" — Gdawg45); at low level Shield Bash is also the best threat button ("Shield Bash generates 180 bonus threat at all levels ... your opener as a tank, for only 10 rage" [Redmagejoe (player), 2023-05-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47619#p47619)); Pummel in Battle/Berserker stance; Concussion Blow as a second interrupt on casters.
- **Leadership**: mark targets, call CC, communicate ("the tank job does often demand a pretty good typing ability" — Bob022); let a suicidal DPS die rather than the healer ("Some people can't be saved because they do stupid things. Let them die and keep your healer safe" — Xudo).
- **Threat management**: use a threat meter (TWThreat — Ellifta); build a lead before DPS opens ("condition dps to only start attacking AFTER the tank has established a threat buffer" [Cheruscan (player), 2026-03-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170673#p170673)); if a Shield Slam is parried at the pull, be ready to Taunt (Xudo).
- **Raid assignments**: fear bosses (Onyxia, Magmadar, Nefarian, Yauj, Gluth) and Maexxna/Loatheb go to the warrior (Atreidon); bring a shield even as fury-prot for those (`fury-tank.md`).
- **Camera/targets**: max camera distance, Tab through adjacent targets, keybind marks (Xudo, Bob022); UnitXP addon for "smart targeting" [Bordar (player), 2025-06-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135485#p135485).
- Gear swaps by stance with Outfitter or Roid-Macros `/equip` `/equipoh` (macro threads, `leveling.md`).

## Gear, enchants and consumables

- Tier: "You should never ever wear full T1, T2 or T3 if you want to hold threat against people with purple gear ... stick with T2 shoulders ... T2 legs and boots are also strong ... use off-pieces like ES helmet instead of T2 helmet or T2 DPS chest instead of T2 tank chest" [Ellifta (player), 2026-01-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164933#p164933) (single source, Kara40-geared prot main); T1 set bonus gives rage ("now that i got the set bonus from the T1 i have plenty of rage" [Xronisf79 (player), 2024-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85704#p85704)); "they changed warrior tanks so that original t1, t2 and t3 are not crap anymore" [Noephix (player), 2025-06-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135310#p135310); Kara40 T3.5 is one mixed set for both roles — repeated complaint, no staff answer [Davdes1 (player), 2025-06-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133988#p133988).
- Weapons: Thunderfury for threat (the proc's slow only on the main target; damage jumps contested) [t=22555]; "Deep prot with tundetfury counter enh shaman" (PvP) [ferundal (player), 2026-02-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168167#p168167).
- Shield spikes: "Thorium Shield spike ... Each use blocks damage twice and damages opponent twice for only 10 rage" (pre-CC2, when Shield Block had 2 charges) [Unangwata (player), 2020-11-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8311#p8311).
- Lifegiving Gem (BWL) for PvP/HP (Terrydavis, Springboards — `arms-pvp.md`).
- Royal Seal of Eldre'thalas (Warrior): 30 AP + 8 Defense [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105276#p105276).
- Consumables/enchants: gap — no prot-specific list in the Warrior forum.

## Common mistakes

- Going deep Prot before 60 and expecting it to help: "Prior to raids, you do not need to be Prot in order to tank well" [Redmagejoe (player), 2023-03-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37550#p37550) (consensus; see `leveling.md`).
- Spamming Heroic Strike as a prot filler instead of Sunder/Revenge/Shield Slam (Arkaris, Matches); HS swings give no rage.
- Skipping Demoralizing Shout / Thunder Clap on packs (Roxanneflowers, Sixgun, SvenS2: "Cast demo shout, spam sunder and don't rely too much on thunderclap").
- Dumping all rage at the end of a pull (Sixgun, Bob022).
- Tanking in Battle Stance with Defensive Tactics on hard-hitting bosses (loses Shield Block immunity — Ellifta's own caveat: "1 crit per boss fight and usually 4-5 crushing blows").
- Overwriting Improved Shield Slam's block charge with Shield Block carelessly (Ellifta, Eyeburn).
- Orc Blood Fury while tanked/healed (Gdawg45).
- Chasing every runner / pointing runners at the next pack (Roxanneflowers).
- Fighting the healer's mana: pulling below 50% healer mana (Gdawg45).
- Using Taunt on cooldown "for threat" — Taunt is a fix, not a threat tool; Improved Taunt "for losers" (Xudo) / for new tanks (Xudo 2022).
