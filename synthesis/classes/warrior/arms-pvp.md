# Warrior — Arms — PvP (battlegrounds / world PvP), with Fury and Protection PvP notes

Arms is the PvP spec the forum agrees on for the Mortal Strike debuff; Fury is the post-CC3 "self-sustain / lone wolf" alternative; Protection PvP is a meme with two real tools. The forum's PvP threads are mostly complaint threads ("warrior is F-tier") answered by "get gear and a pocket healer" — the playbook keeps both sides and marks them contested.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Within pre-1.18.1 the kit changed at CC2 (1.17.2, 2024-11-01), the December 2024 revision, and CC3 (1.18.0, 2025-08-15). PvP-relevant kit facts changed at every step (Hamstring cooldown added at CC2, removed at 1.18.1; Counterattack → Master Strike at CC3; Improved Berserker Rage and Blood Drinker rework at CC3; PvP dampening in 1.18.0, made class-specific in 1.18.1).

## Stat priority (PvP)

1. **Stamina first** — the one gearing rule several experienced posters agree on: "put on the tanking t3 pieces+lifegiving gem and STAMINA enchants/armor kits. That will let you get 7k hp unbuffed ... 10k hp with gem buffed. No other class can bring you down 1v1 pound for pound" [Springboards (player), 2024-10-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104533#p104533); "going stamina on gear instead of glass 3.8k-4.5k hp warrior you should be 5.5k-6k unbuffed, stamina enchants tanking neck tanking rings tank gear etc" [Springboards (player), 2025-12-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162626#p162626); "you need the bwl trinket that gives you 20% more hp" [Terrydavis (player), 2026-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171279#p171279) — identified as Lifegiving Gem [Magicknight (player), 2026-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171300#p171300). Arms enables this: "you scale off weapon damage more, this allows the warrior to equip more stamina heavy gear" [Citramate (player), 2023-05-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47208#p47208).
2. **Weapon damage (top-end)** — "your base damage on weapon like BRE/MoM/DEI/severance should be more than enough" [Springboards (player), 2025-12-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162626#p162626); "you need an endgame weapon" [Terrydavis (player), 2026-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171279#p171279); "a Good weapon is a CTUN AXE" [Boeuff (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171438#p171438).
3. Hit/crit/AP after that — no PvP-specific numbers in the sources. Gap. No PvP stat weights or resilience-like stat exists ("Need resilience" is a wish [Bloodline1x9 (player), 2025-05-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130241#p130241)).

## Single-target "rotation" (PvP priority)

PvP has no rotation; the sources give openers and principles.

1. **Open**: Charge (out of combat only — "Not being able to charge while in combat is completely dumb" [Kerebrien (player), 2022-09-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24464#p24464); casters "cast some nothing spell like pain or counterspell at max range to get you in combat and deny you the ability to enter the fight with any kind of momentum" [Snoof (player), 2025-09-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152740#p152740)) or Intercept (Berserker Stance) if already in combat → **Hamstring** immediately (no cooldown since 1.18.1; 6 s cooldown from CC2 to 1.18.0) → Bloodrage before approaching a rogue "to prevent Sap" then Demoralizing Shout to break stealth [Xudo (player), 2024-11-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105917#p105917).
2. **Mortal Strike** on cooldown (healing debuff is the reason to be Arms); **Overpower** on dodge; **Execute** under 20% (no cooldown since CC3).
3. **Master Strike** as the situational CC: dagger swap for a silence on casters, axe for the 4 s root, sword for disarm (staff definition; Cheruscan/Snoof above). Snoof's caveat: "trying to make an ability do that many different things is just not a good idea ... axes being the 'correct' option" [Snoof (player), 2025-09-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152740#p152740).
4. **Pummel** (Battle Stance since CC2) as the interrupt; Shield Bash if you carry a shield.
5. **Disarm** vs melee (keep 20 rage via Tactical Mastery — Xudo above); **Intimidating Shout** as the escape/peel (unreliable fear "especially if you're alliance going up against Will of the Forsaken and tremor totems" [Snoof (player), 2025-09-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152740#p152740)).
6. **Polearm kiting** (with 5/5 Master of Arms): Hamstring then hold 7 yd range against other melee [Manletow (player), 2024-10-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103527#p103527) — single source.
7. Do not stand in a caster's kiting pattern; "abuse line of sight ... he is kiting the mages not letting them kite him" [Springboards (player), 2024-10-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104533#p104533).

Level-19 bracket specifics (Xudo's matchup list: paladins ignore Hamstring, hunters Wing Clip back, priests SW:P you into combat before Charge, shamans kite around Earthbind, mages Frost Nova after Charge, locks dot-and-kite, druids Faerie Fire + root) [Xudo (player), 2025-10-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155546#p155546); rage is plentiful at 19 ("white attack for 100 damage grant ~18 rage") and Pummel/Slam are good there [Xudo (player), 2024-11-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105917#p105917).

## Role strategy

- Play next to your healers and dispellers; your value is MS on their kill target and Hamstring/Disarm/fear peels (Atreidon p=162631, p=164520; Thaladar p=151245).
- Soak: with stamina gear you are the target that costs the enemy mana ("enemy players spend mana to kill him. Meanwhile he was healed from other side" [Xudo (player), 2025-10-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155546#p155546); Manletow p=108331).
- Avoid enhancement shamans head-on: "enhance shaman always outmelee'd warrior.always.no exceptions ... use your toolkit like fear, disarm and stack stamina/kite" [Springboards (player), 2025-12-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164130#p164130); Windfury "has no internal cd" and Stormstrike forces procs [Springboards (player), 2025-12-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164133#p164133). Counter-suggestion: deep prot with Thunderfury (ferundal, single source).
- Dampening era (1.18.0+): "nobody is scared of physical damage. This puts warriors in the position of easily kited, easily bursted, and easily survivable" [Eyeburn (player), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169850#p169850) — single source, pre-release of the class-specific dampening numbers.
- Race: Tauren (War Stomp, HP), Gnome (escape), Night Elf (Shadowmeld, nerfed) [Springboards (player), 2025-12-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162626#p162626); Orc stun resist nerfed at CC2 (Manletow p=103527).
