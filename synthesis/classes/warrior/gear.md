# Warrior — Gear guide (by spec, role and level bracket)

Machine-readable version: `structured/classes/warrior/gear.yaml`. Sources: the Warrior forum (f40) BiS-request threads, matching threads in General, the existing class playbooks' "Gear, enchants and consumables" sections, and the 1.18.1 itemization changelog.

**Headline finding, repeated in every source read for this guide: no maintained Turtle-specific pre-raid or raid BiS list exists for any Warrior spec.** Every "Pre-BiS/BiS list?" thread in the Warrior forum ([t4804](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=4804), [t19772](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=19772), [t14987](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=14987)) and in General ([t13556](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13556), [t18604](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=18604)) ends with players saying no such list exists: "To my knowledge no one has publicly posted a T-WoW pre-raid bis list" — [Philandros (player), 2022-11-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28796#p28796); "I maintain a classic warrior sim with the changes made for turtle... this will be far better than any pre-simmed bis and prebis lists could ever do for you" — [Ugoboom (player), 2023-02-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35760#p35760) (`https://zebouski.github.io/WarriorSim-TurtleWoW/`, community tool, not read further). Every table below is built from named-item fragments scattered across many threads, not a curated list — treat ranks as directional.

Raid-tier bracket mapping used below (inferred from repeated context in the sources, not a stated table anywhere): **raid-t1 = Molten Core, raid-t2 = Blackwing Lair, raid-t2.5 = Ahn'Qiraj 40, raid-t3 = Naxxramas, raid-t3.5 = Kara40** (Tower of Karazhan, Turtle-custom).

## Gaps

- No pre-raid-60 or raid-tier item list exists for Warrior Protection tank in the sources — only scattered stat-priority and set-bonus mentions.
- No leveling gear (leveling-10-29 / leveling-30-49 / leveling-50-59) exists for any Warrior spec. `synthesis/classes/warrior/leveling.md` covers talents and playstyle only — zero named items. A bot has to fall back to generic "best available armor of the right type" logic.
- Runeblade of Baron Rivendare vs. Dal'Rend's swords (a named 2H-vs-DW weapon question) got only trinket answers (Hand of Justice, Rune of the Guard Captain, Blackhand's Breadth); the weapon question itself is unanswered in the source thread.
- Kel'Thuzad mace, Dark Edge of Insanity and Severance are named only as "end weapons" with no slot/stat detail; Severance's custom-vs-vanilla status is unconfirmed.
- Dreadslayer (Kara40 mini-set) is named as part of a T3.5 BiS combo but no individual item names or slots are given anywhere in the corpus.
- "The best gloves for fury warriors are mail" (Balake, 2020-07-02) is an old one-line claim with no item name and no clear kit context — not usable as a current recommendation.
- No enchant list exists for any Warrior spec/role. The only enchant facts found: Windfury Totem interacts with sharpening stones but grants no bonus AP for the extra attack ([Xudo (player), 2024-10-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103287#p103287)); world buffs give a 20-40% DPS increase ([Abiozan (player), 2024-12-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112930#p112930)). Neither is a per-slot enchant recommendation.
- No PvP-specific gear list exists for pure Fury or pure Protection; the PvP section above is explicitly shared across all three specs because the forum does not split PvP gear discussion by spec.
- "Meph leather chest" (Kara40) is recorded exactly as written by the source; Warrior plate vs. this item's stated "leather" armor type is not reconciled anywhere in the corpus.
