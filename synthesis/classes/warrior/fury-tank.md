# Warrior — Fury/Protection hybrid — Tank ("fury-prot")

Covers the dual-wield / Death Wish "fury-prot" tank that was the vanilla and pre-Class-Changes-2 Turtle raid-tank meta, and what is left of it after Class Changes 2 (patch 1.17.2, live 2024-11-01). Deep Protection is a separate playbook (`protection-tank.md`); the Defensive Tactics "battle-stance prot" build lives there because it keeps a shield and a 31+ point Protection core.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Within pre-1.18.1 the kit changed at CC2 (1.17.2, 2024-11-01), the December 2024 revision, and CC3 (1.18.0, 2025-08-15). This spec is the one most affected by CC2: most sources written before 2024-11 describe a build that the sources after 2024-11 call nerfed.

## Overview

- **Role**: tank (raid main/off-tank) using Fury damage talents for threat instead of Protection mitigation talents.
- **What it is (sources)**: "Fury tanks were not about getting more damage from dual wield. They were about getting more damage from Enrage sit-proc, Death Wish, Flurry and Improved Execute. You also had to gear up in DPS plate (lot of crit and agility, but also with stamina and armor). You still had to use shield and 1/3 improved shield block to stay crit and crush immune" [Xudo (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170985#p170985) (describing vanilla; his reference build is a vanilla classicdb link, not a Turtle one). Threat comes from damage: "the furry prot can build a lead in threat then equip a shield for mitigation" [Wilsonsds (player), 2023-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77951#p77951).
- **Community standing, pre-CC2 (before 2024-11-01) — consensus**: the raid-tank meta. "Basically it's the same meta as classic, for some bosses you may need to tank with a shield but if you can survive, fury prot dual wield is better. Weapon skill is also required for minmax" [Velite (player), 2021-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11727#p11727); "All tanks with prebis and better goes fury/prot spec" [Fixaren1 (player), 2023-05-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44704#p44704); "fury prot. Which is already too good" [Kairion (player), 2023-03-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37698#p37698); "Warrior (At least fury and fury/prot) warrior is the best DPS and best tank at endgame with gear" [Bigsmerf (player), 2023-09-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64561#p64561); "That's why no one plays full prot and use Fury-Prot with no shield for more threat" [Grizb37 (player), 2024-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85578#p85578); "From late raids they fall short to fury-prot because everything becomes about the threat you generate" [Akos1896 (player), 2024-01-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85054#p85054). Predicted nerf: "What Warriors deserve and will likely get ... are nerfs to Fury and buffs to Prot to kill the Fury/Prot meta" [Redmagejoe (player), 2023-05-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=44653#p44653).
- **Community standing, post-CC2 (from 2024-11-01) — consensus that it was nerfed; contested whether it is still worth playing**: "Furyprot was intentionally nerfed, because normal prot was not viable in raids. Why do you want to play exactly furyprot? Why not deepprot?" [Xudo (player), 2024-12-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114018#p114018); "Sword and board warrior is the meta now. Shieldslam runs laps around bloodthirst when it comes to threat ... The only reason to run a fury tank nowadays is his better dps if there is nothing to tank" [Atreidon (player), 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108893#p108893); a raid leader's report: "two of our maintanks come with a bloodthirst build in the new patch. Not only were they constantly eating crits, but also their threat was unbearably bad, even with tf" [Atreidon (player), 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108957#p108957); "Fury prot was nerfed, and that spec didn't use traditional warrior tier sets anyway" [Ellifta (player), 2026-01-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164933#p164933); "So furytanks are greatly nerfed in turtle compared to vanilla and dualwielding is not a good idea for tanking" [Xudo (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170985#p170985). Still recommended by some: "if your healers can take it, learning how to fury tank is the way to go until you start getting toward Naxx level gear ... furytank is better for threat generation" [Arkaris (player), 2025-02-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118658#p118658); "I play both fury and deep prot tank and both have very similar threat gen, its basically come down to RNG and fury's using deathwish (which is very inadvisible and puts alot of strain on healers in many fights)" [Cheruscan (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170960#p170960); the consistency argument for it — fast/fast dual wield spreads threat over many hits so a single parry hurts less than a parried Shield Slam [Eyeburn (player), 2026-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170891#p170891), [Eyeburn (player), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171671#p171671).
- **Content**: raids only. "Furytank is a thing, but for raids, not for 5 man dungeons" [Xudo (player), 2023-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40901#p40901) vs "just play fury-prot without a shield in Dungeons, and your Threat will be fine" [Inovatu (player), 2024-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85571#p85571) — contested (both pre-CC2). Healer cost: "Become a druid like tank but much worse than an actual druid. Say hello to crushing blows and strain the healers" [Calli (player), 2024-03-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89995#p89995).
- **Post-1.18.1**: no source re-evaluates fury-prot after 2026-03-20. The 1.18.1 Shield Slam/Defiance buffs (see `protection-tank.md`) all favour deep Protection. Gap.

## Official kit changes that hit this spec (staff)

- CC2 (2024-10-12) [Dragunovi (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102974#p102974): off-hand no longer auto-hits while Heroic Strike is queued; Improved Shield Block removed (block power moved into Improved Shield Slam, which needs 20+ Protection points); Shield Slam moved to row 5 and scales with AP and block value; Bloodthirst self-damage (later removed); Sunder Armor cost 10 rage.
- 2024-11-01 [Dragunovi (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105804#p105804): Enrage 25% → 15%; Bloodrage activates Enrage.
- 2024-12-20 [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460): Bloodthirst no longer self-damages (so no Enrage proc from it), 35% AP; Enrage 4–20%; Defensive Tactics 60/120/180%.
- CC3 (2025-07-25) [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159): Execute cooldown removed; Improved Berserker Rage; Bloodthirst 10% movement speed.
- 1.18.1 (2026-01-23) [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071): Defiance 4–20%; Shield Slam 20% AP and 75% threat modifier — only usable with a shield.
- Player-reported mechanics relevant to the spec: the Enrage "sit proc" (sitting to get crit) was removed on Turtle "long before 2022" [Xudo (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170985#p170985) (he cites threads t=5809 and t=6082 — not verified here); block charges from Shield Slam and Shield Block cancel each other, which Ellifta reads as deliberate: "I know the point was to prevent Fury prot from being crit-crush immune" [Ellifta (player), 2026-02-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167684#p167684).

## Stat priority

Sources are thin and pre-CC2 unless noted.

1. **Hit** — same cap as DPS (9% for bosses; see `fury-melee-dps.md`); "greatly hit dependent" is said of deep prot [Wilsonsds (player), 2023-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77951#p77951) and applies more to a dual-wielder (DW miss chance 24% before hit — [Eyeburn (player), 2025-08-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147408#p147408)).
2. **Weapon skill** — "Weapon skill is also required for minmax" [Velite (player), 2021-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11727#p11727) (pre-CC2; "Turtle devs changed weaponskill formula, but it is unclear how it influences parry chance" [Xudo (player), 2026-03-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170790#p170790)).
3. **Crit, agility, then stamina and armor** — "DPS plate (lot of crit and agility, but also with stamina and armor)" [Xudo (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170985#p170985) (vanilla description). Crit matters more for Enrage/Flurry uptime.
4. **Defense / crit immunity** — the spec does not reach crit/crush immunity without a shield; "Not only were they constantly eating crits" [Atreidon (player), 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108957#p108957); Ellifta's deep-prot numbers (370 Defense at Naxx still "1 crit per boss fight and usually 4-5 crushing blows" when not in Defensive Stance with Shield Block) [Ellifta (player), 2025-01-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115882#p115882).

Stat weights: none published. Gap.

## Single-target rotation (raid boss)

Reconstructed from the descriptions above; no source posts a fury-prot priority list. Vanilla-era logic marked as such.

1. Pull: Charge/Intercept in, **Bloodrage** (activates Enrage since 2024-11-01), Death Wish if the healers can afford it ("very inadvisible and puts alot of strain on healers in many fights" [Cheruscan (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170960#p170960)).
2. **Sunder Armor** ×5 first — fixed-threat abilities are "huge part of warrior threat" [Xudo (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170985#p170985).
3. **Bloodthirst** on cooldown (the build's reason to exist; note it no longer procs Enrage since 2024-12-20).
4. **Heroic Strike** as the rage dump on every swing — after CC2 the off-hand is no longer guaranteed to land while HS is queued [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102974#p102974); Xudo suggests devs could "remove additional threat from Heroic Strike and fury threat will be nerfed enough" [Xudo (player), 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108854#p108854) — i.e. HS's bonus threat is what makes the spec work.
5. **Revenge** only in Defensive Stance; **Execute** under 20% (no cooldown since CC3; Improved Execute is one of the spec's named threat talents).
6. Once the threat lead is safe, **equip a shield** for mitigation ("build a lead in threat then equip a shield" [Wilsonsds (player), 2023-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77951#p77951); "for some bosses you may need to tank with a shield" [Velite (player), 2021-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11727#p11727)). With Improved Shield Slam talented, Shield Slam gives the block charge Xudo calls necessary [Xudo (player), 2024-12-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114018#p114018).
7. Stance: Defensive Stance for the 10% damage reduction and Revenge/Taunt; Berserker Stance is what a Fury build is tuned for but gives 10% more damage taken and no Taunt — sources do not settle which stance a post-CC2 fury-prot tanks in (gap). Taunt chain when threat slips: Intercept → Defensive Stance → Taunt in one GCD, then Mocking Blow, then Challenging Shout [Xudo (player), 2023-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40901#p40901).

## Multi-target / AoE

- 2–3 targets: Cleave (Improved Cleave is one of the three fury threat talents named by Xudo) + tab Sunder; Thunder Clap is usable in Defensive Stance on Turtle [Lavrute (player), 2024-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113482#p113482).
- 4+: Whirlwind in Berserker Stance, Challenging Shout as the emergency AoE taunt. Wilsonsds notes fury-prot "have problens with fear and aoe fights" [Wilsonsds (player), 2023-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77951#p77951); Morkahja proposes fury-prot become the "AoE tank" identity, which shows it is not that today [Morkahja (player), 2026-02-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167689#p167689).
- Threshold: none stated. Gap.

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Death Wish**: the spec's threat cooldown; +damage taken. Use only with healer agreement (Cheruscan above). Xudo calls it "questionable" post-CC2 [Xudo (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170985#p170985).
- **Bloodrage**: on cooldown, and it now starts Enrage [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-11-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105804#p105804).
- **Berserker Rage**: fear immunity is one of the spec's advantages ("nice mechanics to avoid fear" [Wilsonsds (player), 2023-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77951#p77951)); Improved Berserker Rage exists since CC3.
- **Shield Wall / Last Stand**: Shield Wall ends if the shield is unequipped (CC2 note; see `protection-tank.md`), so swap the shield in before using it. Last Stand needs 11+ Protection points — whether the hybrid takes it is unanswered (Kwannox's question).
- **Rage**: "Fury probably has higher rage generation than full Prot would" [Bob022 (player), 2023-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40865#p40865); "they have a great rage generation" [Wilsonsds (player), 2023-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77951#p77951) — both pre-CC2; the CC2 rage formula changed how gear maps to rage (staff post above). Save rage between pulls (Bob022).

## Role strategy

- Raid main tank in MC/BWL/AQ gear when your healers accept crits and crushes; deep Protection once "you start getting toward Naxx level gear" [Arkaris (player), 2025-02-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118658#p118658). For 40-man raids after CC2 the raid-leader sources say sword-and-board (Atreidon above).
- The spec's real edge after CC2 is consistency (many hits, no single ability to be parried) and DPS when nothing needs tanking (Eyeburn, Atreidon above).
- Do not bring it to 5-man dungeons as your first tank spec: "very inconvenient ... you lose your utilities" [Xudo (player), 2023-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40901#p40901). Contested (Inovatu).
- Leveling relevance: a Fury leveler can "occasionally tank dungeons if you equip shield" [Xudo (player), 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108854#p108854); SvenS2 tanks all leveling dungeons in Fury "without a shield in most cases" [SvenS2 (player), 2026-04-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175326#p175326) (post-1.18.1).

## Gear, enchants and consumables

- Gear in DPS plate with stamina/armor (Xudo, vanilla description); "that spec didn't use traditional warrior tier sets anyway" [Ellifta (player), 2026-01-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164933#p164933).
- Weapons: fast/fast dual wield for hit volume [Eyeburn (player), 2026-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170891#p170891); Thunderfury ("tf") was on the tanks Atreidon describes and did not save their threat [Atreidon (player), 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108957#p108957).
- Keep a shield on a swap macro (see the equipoh/Outfitter macros in `protection-tank.md`).
- Enchants and consumables: no fury-prot-specific list in the sources. Gap. Use the DPS list from `fury-melee-dps.md` for threat and the tank list from `protection-tank.md` for survival.

## Common mistakes

- Copying a vanilla furyprot build: Improved Shield Block is gone and the Enrage sit-proc is gone [Xudo (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170985#p170985).
- Main-tanking a 40-man in a Bloodthirst build after CC2: "constantly eating crits ... threat was unbearably bad" [Atreidon (player), 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108957#p108957).
- Popping Death Wish without telling the healers [Cheruscan (player), 2026-03-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170960#p170960).
- Expecting Shield Slam + Shield Block charges to stack for crush immunity — they cancel [Ellifta (player), 2026-02-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167684#p167684).
- Bringing the spec to low-level dungeons instead of Arms/Prot utility (Xudo p=40901; contested).
