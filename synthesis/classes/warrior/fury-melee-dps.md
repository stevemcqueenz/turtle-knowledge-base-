# Warrior — Fury — Melee DPS (raid / dungeon)

Covers dual-wield Fury (the default raid spec) and the two-handed "2H Fury / Slam" variant that the sources treat as a Fury build after Class Changes 3 (July 2025). Arms is a separate playbook (`arms-melee-dps.md`) because the sources argue about which is better and the rotations differ.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Within pre-1.18.1 the Warrior kit changed three times: Class Changes 2 ("CC2", 1.17.2, live 2024-11-01), the December 2024 revision, and Class Changes 3 ("CC3", 1.18.0, live 2025-08-15). Every recommendation below says which kit it was written for.

## Overview

- **Role**: melee DPS; the class's raid-DPS spec. Also the historical basis of the "fury-prot" tank (see `fury-tank.md`).
- **Strengths (sources)**: best-scaling DPS in the game once geared — "Their gear scaling is ridiculous so yes they get very powerful, best tank and best DPS" [Harkus (player), 2023-09-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64560#p64560); "Warrior (At least fury and fury/prot) warrior is the best DPS and best tank at endgame with gear" [Bigsmerf (player), 2023-09-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64561#p64561); staff in March 2025: "All Warrior specializations are currently in a strong position ... Given Warriors' well-documented scaling potential, the new raid loot may further enhance their power" [Torta (staff), 2025-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124504#p124504). Raid utility beyond damage: Sunder Armor for the whole raid, Battle Shout, reliable interrupts, and the ability to off-tank adds in DPS gear [Atreidon (player), 2024-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112515#p112515); "even dps warriors can and often do OT, pick up mobs etc ... Not to mention Battle shout, 200AP is huge!" [Heretic1987 (player), 2026-02-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167515#p167515).
- **Weaknesses (sources)**: extremely gear dependent ("Warriors are THE most gear dependent class in the game" [Roxanneflowers (player), 2020-10-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8310#p8310)); dual wield needs hit gear before it works ("switch to fury when you get 9% hit" [Reploidrocsa (player), 2023-10-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67965#p67965)); "fury in general is more gear dependant than arms" [Cheruscan (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156442#p156442); no self-sustain outside Blood Craze / Blood Drinker; bad at solo farming ("War is the worst class, by far, to 'solo farm'" [Idontcareatall (player), 2024-11-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105723#p105723)).
- **Content**: raids (MC → Kara40) — favored. Dungeons at 60 — fine, but Arms' Sweeping Strikes is preferred for cleave (see Arms playbook). Leveling — contested (see `leveling.md`). PvP — Fury is the "lone wolf / self-healing" spec after CC3 (see `arms-pvp.md`). Hardcore — no Fury-specific hardcore advice in the sources beyond leveling.
- **Community standing**: **favored** raid DPS spec (consensus 2023–2025): "Fury takes you there, arms will not" [Akos1896 (player), 2024-05-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95013#p95013); "Not having flurry is not viable for dps that's pretty much the whole story" [Justgens (player), 2023-07-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53805#p53805). **Contested since mid-2025**: "Arms is definitely up there with fury, maybe 5% behind in MC, BWL and Naxx but definitely better than fury in AQ40 and especially Kara40 where every boss has bazillion adds" [Ellifta (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139087#p139087); "Arms warrior is already the best DPS spec in game. Check out turtlogs" [Noephix (player), 2025-06-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135319#p135319); and in Feb 2026 a player calls "2h fury, in which you slam a lot" the meta build [Warrior37 (player), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168301#p168301). Other players: "2h fury isnt a spec anymore. If you use twohanders go arms" [Atreidon (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156441#p156441) vs "2h fury uses Slam" [Eyeburn (player), 2025-10-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157054#p157054) and "Wake up Neo! Missed CC3?" [armsgirl (player), 2025-10-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157453#p157453). Bots: default to DW Fury in raids; treat 2H Fury (Slam) as an equally accepted alternative post-CC3; treat Arms as accepted in AQ40/Kara40.
- Reputation problem: warriors are envied/over-represented in raids ("582 warriors logged in naxx, and 317 paladins" [amanagor (player), 2024-12-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112676#p112676); guilds "have at least 6-7 slots open for warrior dps" [amanagor (player), 2024-12-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111704#p111704)); expect "warrior envy" threads and do not gloat on meters.

- Post-1.18.1 hotfix 2026-03-21, [Torta (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608): "Flurry will now properly be consumed when attacking" (the line does not name the class).
- Player claim about Flurry's size: "they nerfed flurry. 15% attack speed now, for five whole talent points still" [Bigsmerf (player), 2024-12-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111670#p111670) — single source; no staff line in the extracted posts confirms the number.

### Patch validity

- CC2-era statements (Nov 2024 – Jul 2025) about Bloodthirst self-damage/Enrage procs are **obsolete** since 2024-12-20.
- CC3 (2025-08-15) changed Execute (no cooldown), Improved Execute (rage cost), Blood Drinker (unconditional heal under Enrage/Death Wish/Recklessness) and Flurry→Slam. Anything about "Reckless Execute" or the Execute cooldown predates CC3.
- 1.18.1 changed nothing structural for DW Fury; it renamed Improved Whirlwind to Ravager, fixed Flurry's Slam cast reduction (2H nerf) and changed T1/T3 set bonuses. No source after 2026-03-20 re-evaluates the Fury raid build; treat the CC3 build as valid for 1.18.1 with those caveats.

## Stat priority

- **Hit**: 9% for raid bosses (level 63) so abilities never miss; white hits still miss with dual wield: "You don't need the 9% immediately. This is the end-value for lvl 63 (Raidbosses) and ensures that your skills don't miss. Whitehits will still miss ... For lvl 60 dungeons ~5% are fine" [Tomberry (player), 2023-10-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73184#p73184); "You get a huge power spike the moment you get 9% hit so your abilities won't miss ... won't come until you're lv 60 with some hit gear from dungeons and 20 man raids" [Reploidrocsa (player), 2023-09-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64581#p64581). Consensus. With racial/item weapon skill the cap is lower: "+5 weapon skill races hit cap = 6% for yellow attacks, while 300 weapon skill hit cap = 9%" [Struki (player), 2023-04-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40417#p40417) (posed as a question; vanilla baseline). Two-handers: "14% hit rating with a 2hander? you need 8% max" [Cheruscan (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156442#p156442) (single source; the exact 2H cap on Turtle's linearized weapon-skill formula is a gap — staff announced "Linearizing Weapon Skill vs Defense Skill" [Torta (staff) dev blog, 2024-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13840) without publishing the table in the extracted posts).
- **Dual wield miss**: "DW adds a 19% chance to miss on both hands, combined with the base 5%, that's 24%. The new talent in fury increases ONLY your OH hit chance by 10%" [Eyeburn (player), 2025-08-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147408#p147408) (vanilla-baseline numbers restated for Turtle).
- **Weapon skill**: still matters; Human/Orc/Goblin racials nerfed but kept; +weapon-skill items added (Edgemaster's alternatives, Ashkin Belt, Ouro gauntlets) [Syrathegreat (player), 2023-06-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49836#p49836), [1.17.2 final itemization changelog (staff), 2024](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=15404). Weapon-skill effect chart: "10 skill will save the 2H warrior the need for 3% gear hit, and a modest portion of their damage (white glancing blows) will improve by 30%" [Rokit (player), 2023-05-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46107#p46107); "+7 ... is just a mere ~2% shy of highest diminishing returns" [Trymv1 (player), 2023-05-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46167#p46167). Weapon skill does **not** reduce boss parry on Turtle per testing ("parry chance remains 14%, whereas dodge chance does decrease to roughly 4.6%" — quoted from Daaki 2023 by [Xudo (player), 2026-03-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170790#p170790)).
- **After hit**: crit and AP/Strength — "once you start stacking crit and attack power you'll have more and more rage so you'll be able to spend in more and more abilities" (Reploidrocsa, above); "figuring out how much crit = hit = str = agi, is not straightforward, which is why this sim is useful" (community wiki Warrior page). Fury "needs crit to matter" [Eyeburn (player), 2025-08-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149483#p149483). Exact stat weights: **gap** — no post gives numbers; the forum defers to the sim.
- **Weapon choice**: slow main hand, fast off hand is the tradition; on Turtle the reason (HS queue off-hand trick) is gone, "The current answer is probably better rage generation still" [Voodoochile (player), 2025-07-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141043#p141043); Unbridled Wrath procs per hit so a fast OH procs it more [Voodoochile (player), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141443#p141443). Single source.
- **Spirit**: T3 has Spirit; "spirit scales super good on warriors" [Reploidrocsa (player), 2024-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106146#p106146) vs "Spirit is a Dead stat" [Melfrato1 (player), 2025-06-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135336#p135336) — contested; it is a downtime/regen stat, not a DPS stat.
- **While leveling**: see `leveling.md` — Strength/Stamina, weapon damage first; hit gear scarce so DW is punished.

## Single-target rotation (raid, DW Fury)

The forum gives no single authoritative Fury priority list for the CC3 kit. Assembled from the Arms thread (which covers stance, Sunder, execute phase) and macro threads:

**Opener**: Charge (if out of combat) → Bloodrage (activates Enrage since 2024-11-01) → Sunder Armor first if you are a warrior in a raid: "if you fight raid bosses, every warrior should always use it once as the first ability. 5 stacks of sunders on a mob increase not only your dps by about 15-20%, but also the dps of every other physical dps!" [Atreidon (player), 2025-03-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122809#p122809) → Berserker Stance, Death Wish when the fight starts (no source gives exact timing; see cooldowns).

**Priority** (stay in Berserker Stance: "You should be in Berserker Stance all the time to benefit from the extra Crit ... Battle Stance offers nothing and Defensive Stance instead lowers your output" [Wolf of Rage (player), 2025-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122609#p122609)):

1. Target ≤ 20% HP → **Execute** with surplus rage (see execute phase). Improved Execute makes it cost 2/5 less; no cooldown since CC3.
2. **Bloodthirst** on cooldown ("A fury dps warrior have: Bloodthirst - Whirlwind - Overpower - Hamstring -/ Slam, as skill hit" [Toffe (player), 2024-10-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104303#p104303), written on CC2 launch).
3. **Whirlwind** on cooldown ("You should be using Whirlwind off Cooldown" — Wolf of Rage, above; Ravager/Improved Whirlwind shortens it by up to 2 s).
4. **Sunder Armor** as the filler if the boss has < 5 stacks or the tank is not a warrior: "use sunder armor as a filler over hamsteing if the target doesnt yet hav 5 sunders or if the tank is not a warrior ... you can use it just fine in berserker stance" (Atreidon, above). Sunder is also the official proc-fishing filler since CC2 ("elevating Sunder Armor as a tool to trigger weapon effects" [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102974#p102974)).
5. **Hamstring** as an instant proc-fishing filler (Windfury Totem, Hand of Justice, Sword spec) — pre-1.18.1 it had a 6 s cooldown; **1.18.1 removed the cooldown** so it is spammable again [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071). Contested detail: "Sunder Armor ... will not trigger Sword Specialization" [Wolf of Rage (player), 2025-03-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122819#p122819) vs "SA is a physical attack that can proc on hit everything any other attack can proc: OP, WF, swordspec etc." [armsgirl (player), 2025-05-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129704#p129704). Note Master of Arms (Sword) is an Arms talent, irrelevant to pure Fury.
6. **Heroic Strike** only as a rage dump at high rage: "only ever really bother with Heroic Strike as a last-resort Rage dump if you have nothing available and your Rage is at 70 or more" (Wolf of Rage, above). A player DPS macro uses `[rage>55] Whirlwind`, `[rage>42] Heroic Strike`, else Bloodthirst [Bigbearimpact (player), 2024-05-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96085#p96085) and a SuperMacro variant that HS at >42 rage and WW at >55 only while target HP > 21% [Ericnew (player), 2024-07-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100095#p100095) — player macros, not tested by the thread. Remember HS-queued swings generate no rage and the off-hand no longer auto-hits during the queue (CC2).
7. Keep **Battle Shout** up (Improved Shouts lengthens it); Demoralizing Shout if nobody else applies it.

**Execute phase (< 20%)**: "Yes, and you should prefer to use your biggest Cooldowns during that period (Human Racial for extra Crit OR Recklessness; Bloodrage ...), and time the Executes so that you're getting the most out of the Rage that you use up -- swing and then use Execute, since you might get a Crit for fat Rage ... allowing a max-burst Execute" [Wolf of Rage (player), 2025-03-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122923#p122923) (written for Arms with the old Precision Cut refund; the "swing, then Execute" timing applies to Fury too). Fury-specific: "Under 20% hp Fury stops using filler skills and spends all its surplus rage on Execute instead of dumping with Heroic Strike and using fillers for Windfury. There's a talent deep in Fury that helps that by reducing the base cost so even with the misses inherent to dual wield you can Execute your rage down to 0 and still be able to use it again after 1.5sec of autoattacks" [Licata (player), 2026-01-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166667#p166667).

**2H Fury (Slam) single target**: "Auto attack -> Slam -> Instant : Repeat. If you do not have the rage for both slam and an instant, skip the slam. Install a swing timer mod. Slam should be casted immediately after your AA goes off ... Don't ever touch heroic strike" (vanilla-era Blizzard-forum text quoted approvingly by [Xudo (player), 2025-01-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116686#p116686)). Since Dec 2024 Slam **continues** the swing timer and can be cast while moving, so "you will almost always have to use it just about immediately upon swinging. You may have enough time for one Ability (1 GCD, 1.5s wait time)" [Wolf of Rage (player), 2025-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122609#p122609); "go arms tree and learn to use slam between your auto timer this is the optimal and maybe only way to do decent dps as 2h warr" [marvian (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156445#p156445). Slam's rage is spent on cast completion and the following white hit usually refunds about the same [Hemdall (player), 2025-08-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146665#p146665). Swing-timer Slam macro (SP_SwingTimer `st_timer`, SuperMacro): [Tacticalnelf (player), 2024-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100433#p100433) — casts Slam only when `st_timer > UnitAttackSpeed*0.9`.

## Multi-target / AoE

- **2–3 targets**: Whirlwind on cooldown; Cleave instead of Heroic Strike as the on-next-swing (Ravager cuts Cleave's cost by 1/2/3 in 1.18.1); Sunder only to 50% HP on trash: "for ordinary enemies ... you should consider using Sunder only up to 50% remaining Health" [Wolf of Rage (player), 2025-03-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122819#p122819). Sweeping Strikes is Arms-only; DW Fury has no cleave burst — a stated reason Arms wins in AQ40/Kara40 (Ellifta, above).
- **4+ targets**: same buttons; warriors "excel in dps when fighting multiple enemies" per Atreidon (above), but the sources give no 4+ specific priority for Fury. Gap. Be aware of threat: cleaving warriors are the classic offenders ("Good dps will watch their own aggro, avoid cleave and AE damage" [Ishilu (player), 2023-05-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47618#p47618)).

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Death Wish** (1/1 talent): use on pull or execute phase; no exact rule in the sources. Blood Drinker heals during it (CC3).
- **Recklessness** (shares a 30-min cooldown with Retaliation and Shield Wall — players ask to unlink them [Zamera (player), 2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141635#p141635), [Guilliman (player), 2023-05-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48015#p48015); not changed by staff in the extracted threads): "use your biggest Cooldowns during [execute]" (Wolf of Rage, above); "every 30mins you become a literal god with your big cooldown" [Cheruscan (player), 2025-09-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152804#p152804). Improved Disciplines (Arms) cuts 4/8/12 min.
- **Bloodrage**: on cooldown; activates Enrage (staff 2024-11-01) and gives rage before the pull; costs health.
- **Berserker Rage**: fear break; Improved Berserker Rage (CC3) adds 5/10 rage and 50/100% freedom on use — more a PvP pick.
- **Rage**: new formula since CC2 — "We have plenty of rage. I did few duels and my rage bar was full. On lvl 19 white attack for 100 damage grant ~18 rage" [Xudo (player), 2024-11-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105917#p105917). Rage decay out of combat: 2 rage per 2 s. T1 3-set (1.18.1): 10% per ability hit to gain 15 rage. **No rage from absorbed hits**: "Not being able to generate rage on shields is absolutely crippling" [Snoof (player), 2025-09-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152740#p152740) — contested by "Twow gave you ability to generate rage against aborbs and immunities" [Ataika (player), 2025-07-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140665#p140665). Unresolved.
- **Downtime**: warriors eat after most fights when solo ("need to eat constantly" — Reploidrocsa, above); in raids irrelevant.

## Role strategy

- Stand behind the boss (parry/block avoidance is a tank problem: "DPS stand behind the boss, they cant be parried or blocked at all" [Cheruscan (player), 2026-03-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170725#p170725)).
- Apply/maintain 5 Sunders when the tank is not a warrior (Atreidon, above); Battle Shout for the group.
- Interrupt with Pummel (Berserker Stance) — warriors "can kick reliably with less sacrifice to their dps" (Atreidon, above).
- Off-tank adds in DPS gear when needed: "DPS warriors just save the raid by tanking any trashnob just in dps gear" (Atreidon, above); "even dps warriors can and often do OT, pick up mobs etc. For ex in MC warr and druid dps would be preferable to rogues because of both Garr and Majordomo fights" [Heretic1987 (player), 2026-02-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167515#p167515).
- Watch threat; do not overtake a prot warrior early (their snap threat can be lost to a parried Shield Slam — see `protection-tank.md`).
- Weapon swaps: swapping resets the swing timer and triggers a GCD, so avoid mid-rotation swaps [Licata (player), 2026-01-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166667#p166667).

## Common mistakes (bots must avoid)

- Dual wielding without hit gear / before ~9% hit or before level ~50 (consensus; see stat priority and `leveling.md`).
- Using Heroic Strike as a rotational ability instead of a ≥70-rage dump (Wolf of Rage, above); queuing HS starves rage (HS swings generate none — [Roxanneflowers (player), 2020-07-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7046#p7046)).
- Not sundering because "it's a Defensive Stance ability" ("Many warriors think it can only be used in defensive stance" — Atreidon, above).
- Going 2H while specced deep Fury without Improved Slam / 2H spec, or with 14% hit on a 2H (the Turtlelover case).
- Taking Blood Craze / Blood Drinker in a raid build (contested; skip for pure raid DPS per Atreidon).
- Whirlwind/Cleave on CC'd targets; Deep Wounds breaks Intimidating Shout/sap: "the bleed can break hard CC" (Syrathegreat, above).
- Swapping weapons mid-fight for Master Strike effects (resets swing timer — Licata, above).
- Being loud about meters: "Make warriors do less dmg to be more in line with EVERYBODY else" [Shamma (player), 2022-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29437#p29437) — the community is sensitive about warrior stacking.
