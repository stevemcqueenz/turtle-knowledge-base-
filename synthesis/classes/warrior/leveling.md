# Warrior — Leveling 1–60 (and Hardcore)

Covers the three leveling approaches the forum actually argues about: **2H Arms** (the majority recommendation), **dual-wield Fury** (the alternative that became viable with Class Changes 2 and Blood Drinker/Blood Craze), and **Protection via dungeons** (recommended against by most, defended by a few). Sources span 2022–2026 and three kit versions; every recommendation names its kit.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Kit versions inside pre-1.18.1: pre-CC2 (before 2024-11-01), CC2 (1.17.2, 2024-11-01: new rage formula, dual wield at level 10, Slam at 14, Hamstring 6 s cooldown, talent trees rebuilt [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102974#p102974)), the 2024-12-20 revision (Decisive Strike removed, Slam 2.5 s cast usable while moving [Jamey (staff), 2024-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460)), CC3 (1.18.0, 2025-08-15: Master Strike, Blood Drinker rework, Execute cooldown removed [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159)), 1.18.1 (Hamstring cooldown removed [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)).

## Build B — DW Fury (from ~level 10, or switch at 40–50)

## Respec points

- Level ~30: Arms → Prot if you commit to dungeon tanking [Czasku (player), 2024-11-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106723#p106723); Zhilo tried Prot at 30 and went back [Zhilo (player), 2023-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37911#p37911).
- Level 40: Mortal Strike/Bloodthirst; Arms → DW Fury if you have crit (Eyeburn p=149483) or Fury → try the other spec (Morkahja p=167706); armsgirl's route ends at 40 and switches to a 60-style build.
- Level ~50: DW Fury becomes viable with hit gear even for Arms-leaning posters (armsgirl p=149087).
- 60: pick the raid build (`fury-melee-dps.md`, `arms-melee-dps.md`, `protection-tank.md`). Respec is cheap: "The respec cap is pretty cheap on Turtle" [Zhilo (player), 2023-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37911#p37911) (no number given; gap).

## Skill training priority (what to skip when poor)

- "Warrior is an expensive class. Heavier armor costs more to repair. You have more weapon skills to train then anyone" [Berettadin (player), 2025-05-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130489#p130489).
- Rank-1-only list: "If you are short on gold, you can learn only first rank of following skills: Hamstring, Overpower, Thunderclap, Mocking blow, Intercept, Shield Bash, Pummel. Additional ranks add more damage, but don't increase utility" [Xudo (player), 2023-02-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36342#p36342). Caveat: "caution when only learning rank 1 thunderclap for tanking. The reason why its so good for tanking is because it has a threat multiplier on its damage ... try to not skimp on tclap ranks" [Kairion (player), 2023-02-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36357#p36357); Improved Thunder Clap increases its damage (101 → 123/141/162) [Redmagejoe (player), 2023-04-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42540#p42540). Overpower ranks matter more now that Improved Overpower is in every Arms route — the rank-1 list predates CC2.
- Slam at 14 costs more than expected: "it's not a big loss if you don't train it, since it is worth using only with a very slow weapon to dump rage, heroic strike will do better in any case at lower levels" [Macbetto (player), 2025-01-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115543#p115543) (before the 2025-07 Slam buffs).
- Always train: Sunder Armor, Rend, Heroic Strike (max ranks), Battle Shout, Demoralizing Shout, Charge, Bloodrage, Berserker Rage, stances, Execute, Mortal Strike/Bloodthirst, Whirlwind, Cleave (dungeons). Shield Bash is the best low-level tank opener: "Shield Bash generates 180 bonus threat at all levels ... for only 10 rage" [Redmagejoe (player), 2023-05-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47619#p47619) (drops off 34+).
- Weapon skills: train all; "anything other than Human, Orc, or Goblin will require weapon skill items at endgame" [Syrathegreat (player), 2023-05-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47323#p47323) (pre-CC2; CC2 smoothed weapon skill — "these are subject to change in the upcoming major patch, so i would not choose a human warrior now for the weapon skill racial alone" [Zulnam (player), 2024-10-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103027#p103027)).

## Playstyle

- Opener (2H Arms): "charge -> sunder amor (which is often better than heroic strike) -> rend and sunder armor again ... Overpower is very OP and it proccs really often ... keep your Battle Shout up" [Yasara (player), 2024-11-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108095#p108095); DW Fury: "Opening is Rend, Demoralizing Shout, Sunder Armor, and HS when you can afford it" [Berettadin (player), 2025-05-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130489#p130489); Execute is situational ("starting a new fight ... with 0 rage and charge on CD is lame" — Yasara).
- **Hamstring kiting — contested**: for: "Art of arms leveling is in hamstring kiting. You charge+hamstring, deal melee hit and run from slowed mob while your attack is recharging ... ~30% reduction of incoming damage" [Xudo (player), 2024-11-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108097#p108097); "Use a slow twohander, apply hamstring, kite the slowed mob until your next autoattack is ready, apply sunder armor as you deal the autoattack, bandage if needed, repeat" [Borefficz (player), 2023-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60465#p60465); [Turbosaxophonic (player), 2024-12-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113433#p113433) (Charge > Hamstring > auto > strafe > Rend). Against: "Hamstring kiting is great against higher level opponents but completely unsuitable for normal leveling ... combat duration x 3 but incoming damage - 20%" [Yasara (player), 2024-11-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108106#p108106). Note: written during the 6 s Hamstring cooldown era; the cooldown is gone since 1.18.1.
- Mob selection: "Try to do quests where the enemys are 2-3 levels below your own level ... And try to fight mobs 1 vs 1" [Yasara (player), 2024-11-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108095#p108095); "Choose mobs 2 or 3 levels lower than yours" [Tacticalnelf (player), 2023-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60467#p60467); "Do not fight more then one mob at a time or mobs that are 2+ levels above you" [Monmothma21 (player), 2022-01-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15955#p15955); green quests to dodge the hit penalty (Xudo p=147440).
- Weapons: "keep your weapon up2date because they are everything for warrior. Make sure your weapons are as slow as possible. Also have a shield and a fast 1handweapon (speed 1,x) rdy" [Yasara (player), 2024-11-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108095#p108095); "Staves are actually most reliable 2h weapon during leveling. Almost every dungeon has a quest with staff in reward" [Xudo (player), 2023-04-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40276#p40276); "If you can tank, then you get steady supply of blue swords in quest rewards since WC. 2h weapons have to be crafted or unreliably looted" [Xudo (player), 2025-08-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147440#p147440); Whirlwind Axe at 30 (Hctwowfan/Atreidon/Czasku above).
- Dungeon tanking while leveling: any spec, shield only for hard hitters ("On Edwin Van Cleef or Verdan the Everliving ... I would probably put on the shield, but trash mobs can mostly be tanked without" [Oomentaloo (player), 2025-09-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150752#p150752)); "Cast demo shout, spam sunder and don't rely too much on thunderclap" [SvenS2 (player), 2026-04-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173980#p173980) vs "spam tclap as your main aoe threat generator" [Syrathegreat (player), 2023-05-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47322#p47322) — contested; taunt chain and keybinding advice in [Xudo (player), 2023-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40901#p40901). Dungeon XP: "you get full xp with turtle glyph in dungeons" [Xudo (player), 2023-09-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60711#p60711); "They massively reduced the exp gain if you are grouped up with high level players" [Kairion (player), 2023-09-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60577#p60577).
- Level 20 is the turning point: "Suffer through until level 20 and you are golden" [Monmothma21 (player), 2022-01-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15955#p15955).
- Macros: `/startattack` + ability macros [Yasara (player), 2024-11-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108095#p108095); weapon/shield swap and Shield Block-with-swap macros [Oomentaloo (player), 2025-09-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150752#p150752); repo wiki page `Warrior_Macros`.

- Race: Troll regen/spirit (SvenS2), Tauren for HC ("tauren might be your best option defensively. Warstomp gives some breathing room" [Bigsmerf (player), 2024-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96765#p96765)); "Best - dwarf and orc" [Feomatar (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171481#p171481) vs looks-based picks [Turboman (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171516#p171516), [Springboards (player), 2026-03-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170772#p170772) — contested, mostly cosmetic.

## Hardcore differences

- Escape tools are the issue: Hamstring "your only early game escape tool" and it could miss/was on cooldown [Zahnfee (player), 2025-02-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119855#p119855) (cooldown gone since 1.18.1); Piercing Howl as the no-cooldown AoE snare [Likaleo (player), 2025-02-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119858#p119858); "Running away is always better then dying ... strafe untill you are far enough. Otherwise they can Daze you" [Monmothma21 (player), 2022-01-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15955#p15955); "Shit hits the fan, piercehown+stopwatch+run potions" [Wilsonsds (player), 2023-08-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56444#p56444).
- Prot's Shield Wall/Last Stand as HC insurance (Bloodline1x9 p=37690); Tauren War Stomp (Bigsmerf p=96765).
- Dungeon-only HC: pick Horde because RFC opens earlier than Alliance's first dungeon at 18 [Darktifa (player), 2024-06-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96590#p96590).
- Experience report: 8 HC warriors to 60, "fury to 60, arms to 60, mix and match", slow 2H + Slam [Hctwowfan (player), 2025-10-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157895#p157895); HC leveler's Defensive Tactics analysis [Taralej90 (player), 2025-08-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146132#p146132).
- Save potions for emergencies; keep food and bandages (Monmothma21).

## Common mistakes

- Dual wielding early without hit gear (Grizb37, Mazeperception, armsgirl).
- Going deep Protection before 60 for solo questing (Redmagejoe, Kairion, Zhilo).
- Fighting yellow/orange mobs or 2+ at once (Yasara, Monmothma21, Tacticalnelf).
- Letting the weapon fall behind (Yasara, Monmothma21).
- Skipping First Aid/Cooking (Berettadin).
- Copying pre-CC2 talent lists (Xudo p=28808, Kobemaru, Wilsonsds) into the CC2/CC3 tree.
- Forgetting Hamstring on runners ("besides forgetting to Hamstring things that run off and come back with friends there's not a lot to go wrong" [Berettadin (player), 2025-05-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130489#p130489)).
