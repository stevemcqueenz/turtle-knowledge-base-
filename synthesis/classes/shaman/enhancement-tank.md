# Shaman — Enhancement tank ("warden shaman")

Turtle-specific role: shield + one-hander + Rockbiter Weapon, built on the CC2 (1.17.2) tanking talents (Ancestral Guardian, Spirit Armor, Totemic Alignment, Shield Specialization), the baseline taunt **Earthshaker Slam**, the elemental shields and Lightning Strike. Sources: Mimiwarmini's guide (2024-12-02, edited 113 times to 2026-03-22, title "Shaman Tank Guide 20/03/2026 v1.18.1"), Akos1896's leveling/2-tank write-ups (2024-11/12), Skegg's compiled guide (2025-09), the 1.18.1 tank threads, and the staff posts. Eras are marked.

## Overview

- **Role**: dungeon tank (all levels) and off-tank / alternative main tank in raids; highest threat among tanks in the guide's view, weakest mitigation.
- **Community standing**: **niche and contested**. Staff design intent: "The Enhancement tank builds also got some new tools to help them properly fulfil a tank role in levelling and early end-game content" — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102972#p102972); 1.18.0: "Enhancement Tanks often struggle with threat generation, sometimes to the point some of them opt in to use a two-handed weapon instead of the conventional shield ... improvements to threat generation from Rockbiter Weapon and Spirit Armor" — [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159); the 1.18.1 post says "Tank Enhancement and spell-power–focused Enhancement ... are getting more meaningful updates to their core talents" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071). Players: "Shaman tanks are designed to be 100% viable tanks during leveling. They can be used at raids, too but they are less optimal there. The lack of a defensive CD becomes a pain point" — [Akos1896 (player), 2024-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107678#p107678); "As a tank shaman you can't expect raid spots ... in practice they are objectively the weakest tank at raiding level" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117662#p117662); "the strength of the shaman tank allows it to generate a greater threat than any other tank, whether in AoE or single-target situations" — [Mimiwarmini (player, guide writer), 2024-12-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110875#p110875); "Shaman already tanked KT on tel abim get good" — [J0rsche (player), 2024-12-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113255#p113255); "myself have tanked content all the way through Naxx" — [Azuredrak0 (player), 2025-05-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130226#p130226); hostile: "The best guide for shaman tank, is to not play it" — [amanagor (player), 2024-12-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110876#p110876); "Full P1 paladin will be better than bis geared shaman tank in threat/surv" — [Dezhan (player), 2024-12-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112407#p112407).
- **Strengths** (guide, consensus): huge single- and multi-target threat, ranged pulls, the best anti-magic toolkit (Elemental Warding, resistance totems, T2.5), two interrupts (Earth Shock, Grounding), kiting via Earthbind/Frost Shock, Ankh, same damage as a DPS.
- **Weaknesses** (consensus): no defensive cooldown ("You will never have a defensive CD. Nearest thing you can get is swapping in the Hakkar trinket" — [Akos1896 (player), 2024-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112575#p112575)); mana; mail and almost no defense gear ("If you sort the database for 'Mail' armor and enter the filters 'Stamina 1,' 'Agility 1,' and 'Defense 1,' you get a mere 5 items displayed (excluding the T2.5 Set)" — [Crabsi (player), 2025-10-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156833#p156833)); crit/crush immunity effectively unreachable ("even we say they gave shaman 20 in talent and unlock ZG buff, you wont get capped (440) but around 425 in bis" — [Dezhan (player), 2024-11-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109832#p109832)); the Windfury dilemma (below); early levels are "extra squishy ... until around early 30s" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123022#p123022).
- **Content**: leveling dungeons (strong from ~33), 60 dungeons and Lower Karazhan / MC / Onyxia (Mimiwarmini's tested pre-raid and "T1 BiS" lists), AQ40+ "you def can but it's really hard" (Akos1896 2024-11-12). Black Morass needs ~6k armor / 4k HP unbuffed (Skegg).

## Stat priority (60)

- **Tankiness**: "Stamina>Agilty>Dodge>Defense"; **threat**: "Hit>Agility>PowerSpell>Critical>Strength" — [Mimiwarmini](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110875#p110875) (2026-03-22 edition). Guide targets for pre-raid: def min 20, hit min 5%, Sta min 160 (Tauren → 4,000 HP; 7,500 buffed), Agi min 100, armor min 6,000 ("aiming for a 55% reduction on boss lvl 63 in full buff and enchantment, i.e. 7050 armor").
- Guide caps: physical hit cap 8%, spell hit cap 17% (boss 63), 5 weapon skill = 1% hit; 1 Str = 2 AP and 20 Str = 1 block value; 20 Agi = 1% crit = 1% dodge, 1 Agi = 2 armor; 1 Sta = 10 HP (10.5 Tauren); 1 defense = 0.04% miss/dodge/parry/block and −0.04% crit chance (cap 140 defense above base for level 63); crush = 150% damage, avoidance 102.4% to push it off — [Mimiwarmini](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110875#p110875). Wiki `Shaman guide`: crush/crit immune = 440 defense (vanilla-baseline link).
- **Agility vs Strength**: "With the Rockbiter buff which gives us 653 Ap, it's as if we had 326.5 Strength, plus the Agi increases our threat and our tankiness, so I think the agi is more interesting" — Mimiwarmini; Skegg: "tanking while leveling: +stam > +agi > +str > +int (when tanking at 60, +str becomes 2nd most important and +agi third)"; Akos1896: "stam>agi>melee crit>haste> dodge/parry>strength>int" (2024-07, pre-CC2) — **contested** (Agi vs Str second).
- **Spell power is not negligible**: "the SP is not a negligible statistic and that it is close to the gain obtained with the AP ... SP CAN become a better statistic if we integrate it into a rotation with Lightning Shield" — Mimiwarmini (section IX). Hybrid tank weapons were buffed in 1.18.1 for exactly this (Dragunovi above).
- **Int**: only until mana is under control ("At the start int can almost be as important as stam ... As people learn what to use when or even downrank here and there, int falls behind agi/str" — [Akos1896, 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149742#p149742)).
- Leveling: "focus on +stam and armor gear, ignoring defense beyond 15 ... 10 armor is roughly equal to 1 stamina" (Skegg; the obsidian brooch / JC +11 Sta trinkets at 33 — [Akos1896, 2024-12-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111623#p111623)).

## Threat model (guide numbers, Rockbiter 653 AP, no gear)

TPS per source, single target (Mimiwarmini, 2025-08 edition): auto-attack ~154 (slow and fast weapon equal); Earth Shock max 212; Flame Shock 126; Frost Shock 129; Lightning Shield 96; Lightning Strike 25–40 base, 83–98 with Lightning Shield, 38–53 with Water Shield, 33–48 with Earth Shield; Stormstrike 35–56; Stoneclaw 98; Magma 49; Fire Nova 36–40; Searing 17–23; Water Shield 31; Earth Shield 18; Healing Stream 2.5; Mana Spring 3.6. Conclusion: "a slow weapon will give us more Threat than a fast weapon" and "playing a dagger for speed is a nonsense" (Rockbiter no longer adds flat threat per hit) — [Mimiwarmini](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110875#p110875). Threat rules used: 1 damage = 1 threat, healing 0.25, mana gained 0.5; melee needs 110% / ranged 130% of the tank's threat to pull. Mana per second at full rotation: 143.5 untalented, 61.4 with Elemental Focus/Convection/Tidal Focus, **−26.3 (net positive) with Water Shield + Mana Spring**; "Any AP point above 936 will cause us to generate mana" with Lightning Strike under Water Shield (18:1 ratio). Elemental Focus availability at 38% melee crit ≈ 85% per rotation.

## Single-target rotation

**Consensus (Mimiwarmini, Akos1896, Skegg):**

Pre-pull: Rockbiter on a slow one-hander, shield equipped; Lightning Shield (or Water Shield if mana is the constraint); Mana Spring or Healing Stream, Grace of Air or Windfury, Stoneskin or Strength of Earth ("Before pull Lightning Shield > Mana Spring Totem > Grace of Air Totem > Stoneskin Totem > regenerate your mana" — Mimiwarmini boss pull).

1. **Pull**: Lightning Bolt R1 ("warrior arrow", near-zero mana) for a plain pull; **max-rank Lightning Bolt ×2 or Chain Lightning** for maximum initial threat; optionally Molten Blast (2-s cast) while they run in; against Nature-resistant mobs "Molten Blast > Molten Blast > Molten Blast > Flame Shock" — [Mimiwarmini](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110875#p110875), [Akos1896, 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149742#p149742). Boss: "Pull > Searing Totem > BloodLust > Lightning Bolt > Chain Lightning > Stormstrike > Earth Shock > Lightning Strike > Water Shield" (Mimiwarmini).
2. **Stormstrike** as the first melee action ("Just greet them folks with a stormstrike for initial threat" — Akos1896 2024-11-12).
3. **Earth Shock max rank** under the Stormstrike buff — "has a hidden effect which multiplies the threat it generates by 1.5"; "use max rank almost always" post-CC2 (Akos1896 2024-11-12). Use Frost Shock instead if the target resists Nature; Flame Shock "gives a 'long term threat' on a target ... the dot portion is useful on enemies that reset threat" — [Akos1896, 2024-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112610#p112610).
4. **Lightning Strike** on cooldown with the shield that fits the moment: Lightning Shield = threat, Water Shield = mana, Earth Shield = survival ("Are my defensives high enough? -> If no, use earth shield ... Is threat okay? -> If no, go for lightning shield ... confident in survival and threat? -> Go for water shield" — [Akos1896, 2024-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107678#p107678)).
5. Loop "Stormstrike > Earth Shock > Lightning Strike > Earth Shock > refresh shield" (Mimiwarmini "Rotate Spell of a normal target"); with Reverberation 3/3 a second Earth Shock fits every other Stormstrike cycle (Akos1896).
6. **Do less when ahead**: "never generate more threat than needed to maintain aggro ... whenever possible, skip using the rotation below and just do auto-attacks + taunt. Or downrank your shocks" — Skegg; Bigsmerf: max-rank Earth Shock as "pseudo taunt", second-highest rank as the steady generator, rank 1 for interrupts — [Bigsmerf (player), 2025-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123019#p123019).
7. **Lightning Shield for silence-heavy mobs**: "rush there with a lightning shield on. Silence is preventing eating up too much mana anyway" — [Akos1896, 2024-12-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112289#p112289).
8. **Taunt (Earthshaker Slam)** only when a mob attacks someone else; follow with Earth Shock ("For effectively taunt => Earthshaker Slam > Earth Shock" — Mimiwarmini). It "Has no effect if the target is already attacking you" (staff).

## Multi-target / AoE rotation

- **2–3 targets** (consensus): mark skull/cross; run in and "Stormstrike on target 1, tab switch to Earth Shock target 2, and Lightning Strike target 3 ... Tab switch between targets giving each of them an Earth Shock and melee auto attack" — Skegg; "Up to 3 mobs, you are fine without threat totems" — [Akos1896, 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149742#p149742). Chain Lightning pull establishes threat on all three.
- **4+ targets**: **Stoneclaw Totem** (best AoE threat, 30-s CD; "When in doubt, place a Stoneclaw Totem ... Drop an Earthbind after Stoneclaw expires, if needed" — Skegg) then **Fire Nova Totem** timed to explode as the pack arrives (LOS pulls: "Chain Lightning > Fire Nova Totem"), then **Magma Totem**; Mimiwarmini's AoE sequence: "Stoneclaw Totem > Fire Nova Totem > Magma Totem > Recal Totemic > Stoneskin Totem > Fire Nova Totem > Magma Totem"; pulling an extra pack while Magma is up: "Earth shock (pack sweater) > Recal Totemic > Fire Nova Totem > Magma Totem". Fire-resistant packs: "Stoneclaw is your only AoE threat" (Skegg). Magma is the biggest mana burner — downrank it when threat is safe (Akos1896 2024-12-02).
- **Emergency**: "Fire Nova - huge snap threat, but mana intensive"; save Earthshaker Slam for a mob on the healer; "When a mob gets loose or runs towards a sheeped target, use max rank Lightning Bolt for high threat or Earth Shock for big snap threat" (Skegg). Ally pulls a pack: "Earthbind Totem > Nova Fire Totem > Chain Lightning" (Mimiwarmini).
- **AoE threshold**: totems from 4 mobs ("Casters shouldn't really aoe unless there are 3+ mobs. In case of three mobs only, marking skull and using your shocks, SS and LS on tabbed targets should do the trick" — [Akos1896, 2024-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112575#p112575)).

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Bloodlust**: "As a tank, this ability is for you. Use it when you immediately need a threat spike" — Akos1896 2024-11-12; Troll Berserking is the second haste button (Akos1896 2024-12-13).
- **Mana**: Water Shield by default; Mana Spring only if you stay > 20 s in place and always while the party drinks; Totemic Recall after every fight; Judgement of Wisdom on skull; 2 Clearcasting charges from every crit; Nordanaar tea; Major Rejuvenation Potion; the guide's rule "It's much safer to make sure you have the resources (mana) to save a pull that goes south than to spend everything trying to shorten the encounter" (Skegg). Drink when you cannot open the next pack safely — Akos1896: "you confidently pull a pack with 20% mana" once frugal.
- **Survival buttons** (no CD exists): Earth Shield + Grace of Air + Stoneskin + Healing Stream ("defensive toolset"), Healing Potion / Healthstone, Whipper Root Tuber, defensive weapon swap ("keeping at least two (enchanted) weapons. One for threat, one for survival" — Akos1896 2024-07-10), Juju Escape (5% dodge 10 s), Hakkar trinket, Ankh. "never start casting while taking hits" (Skegg: avoidance is 0 while casting — Mimiwarmini).
- **Racials**: Tauren (+5% HP, War Stomp, Totemic Slam −25% attack speed 8 s) or Troll (Berserking) recommended; Orc "probably the worst choice" for tanking though axe skill helps melee threat — [Akos1896, 2024-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112575#p112575) (consensus with Akos 2025-09-01; Skegg says all three are fine).

## Role strategy

- **Overthreat**: the guide's central concept — threat needed = mob HP / total DPS × highest DPS − highest DPS / 1.1; anything more is wasted mana; "If this happens, don't hesitate, ask the weakest dps to make an effort, and ask your strongest dps to calm down" — Mimiwarmini.
- **Marks**: keep one skull up at all times, re-mark immediately; mark patrols while drinking — Akos1896 2024-12-13.
- **Pull types** (Akos1896 2024-12-13): LOS pull with a ranged spell for caster packs; run-in with Shock/SS/LS on three targets; chain-pull the next pack while the current one dies; walk packs toward the next pull with Earthbind/Frost Shock so melee mobs alternate walking and attacking.
- **Totem policy for tanks** ("what totems are absolutely needed" not "what is best"): Grace of Air by default for dodge/crit; **Windfury only if ≥ 2 melee DPS and they accept threat responsibility** ("Just tell them if they rip threat, they tank that mob as a deal" — Skegg; "With a pala, Blessing of Salvation ... can also be placed on the melee DPS in exchange for Windfury"); Strength of Earth when you want mobs dead fast; Stoneskin only for many small hitters; Tremor when fear/sleep/charm; Grounding for nasty instant casters (Hakkar, Kara Crypt mobs); Tranquil Air left behind for over-eager casters; Searing only when no CC/patrol nearby; Flametongue "just say no" — [Akos1896, 2024-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107678#p107678), [2024-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112575#p112575). The rule that Windfury Totem gives the shaman nothing while Rockbiter is on is staff (imbue priority); Imonobor's claim that it now stacks ([2024-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107710#p107710)) contradicts the staff post and Akos/Taralej90 — treat as wrong.
- **Interrupts**: Earth Shock R1 and Grounding Totem; Hex (Troll) on a Black Morass phase-2 dragon spawn (Akos1896 2024-12-06).

## Common mistakes

1. Over-threating and going oom (both guides).
2. Casting (Lightning Bolt, heals) while being hit — no avoidance during casts (Mimiwarmini; Skegg).
3. Dropping Windfury for melee who then out-threat you, or using Flametongue Totem at the cost of a fire threat totem (Akos1896).
4. Fast daggers — pre-CC2 habit (Akos1896 2024-12-13; Mimiwarmini).
5. Using Stormstrike expecting the old debuff (Imonobor); taunting the first mob instead of Stormstriking it (Akos1896).
6. Tanking red/high-orange dungeons or SM before the level-33 stamina trinkets (Akos1896).
7. Taking Tidal Focus / Call of Flame / Flame Guidance into raids (Akos1896).
8. Searing Totem near CC or patrols (Akos1896, pagu).
9. Trying mid-combat 2H swaps for Stormstrike (Atreidon).
10. Post-1.18.1: relying on Earthen Bulwark from strikes — reported not working through 2026-04-07 (Travoltino).
