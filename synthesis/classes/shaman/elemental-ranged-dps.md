# Shaman — Elemental ranged DPS (raids and dungeons)

Playbook for Elemental on Turtle WoW after Class Changes 2 (1.17.2), the 1.18.0 rework (Electrify → Elemental Fury, new Elemental Mastery, Earthquake, Molten Blast, Improved Molten Blast; live 2025-08-15) and patch 1.18.1 (2026-03-20: Call of Earth, Earthquake +15% / 18 s, Elemental Weapons Flametongue now needs melee hits, relic-swap GCD). Two sub-styles existed in the sources: **Nature ("lightning") Elemental**, the baseline, and **Fire Elemental** (Molten Blast spam with Elemental Weapons Flametongue), which 1.18.1 deliberately weakened. Eras are marked.

## Overview

- **Role**: ranged caster in the melee group (for Windfury), single-target Lightning Bolt/Chain Lightning with a Flame Shock maintained by Molten Blast, strong cleave (Chain Lightning, Fire Nova/Magma, Earthquake), off-heals, Purge, Earth Shock interrupt.
- **Community standing**: **contested**; consensus that Elemental is taken but bottom-half single-target. "Mana issues are there only early on when your gear is bad. I dont need to use mana pots in most fights in nax/kara40. Dps wise you are middle of the pack. We just lack some useful caster totem. Due to this most guilds will only have 1-2 max ele shamans" — [Agentsmith (player), 2025-06-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134625#p134625); "Ele is okay. You really want a thunderfury tank. You're probably forced to use WF totem" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117662#p117662); post-1.18.1: "elemental unfortunately is a very weak spec in raid environments ... Only elemental shaman and to an extent boomkin lack this support [Curse of Elements/Shadow] ... in aoe encounters, we can mostly hold our own. But when it comes to single target dps, you will never be in the top 10 if your raid knows what they are doing" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-04-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174001#p174001); "1.5k dps seems to be where the ceiling is. Meanwhile rogues top end is a cool 1.1k dps higher" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172156#p172156); "your damage in raids will be in bottom half of dps meters ... In cleave/aoe situations you can show good numbers" — [pagu (player), 2026-04-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174156#p174156); "As ele shaman I'm in bottom dps table, behind me it's usually balance druid" — [Mdzielak (player), 2026-04-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175015#p175015). Staff (2024-12-20): "We are generally satisfied with how elemental has risen among the other classes as a very capable and efficient multi-target damage-dealer while not falling behind on its single-target capabilities" — [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460).
- **Strengths**: cleave/AoE, utility (totems, Purge, Grounding, off-heals), "You can play any DPS spec you want" — [Bigsmerf (player), 2025-06-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135886#p135886).
- **Weaknesses** (consensus): no raid debuff amplifies Nature ("Elemental shamans DPS is roughly 66% nature damage ... we deal about 18.4% less damage compared to someone using only fire or shadowspells" — [Atreidon, 2026-04-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174801#p174801)); crit-dependent mana ("clearcasting needs crits to work ... in a very bad situation i had almost 0 crits on onyxia and was oom in the middle of 2nd phase" — [pagu (player), 2025-09-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151552#p151552)); pushback ("Shaman has no guaranteed pushback immunity for Molten Blast. The best we have under realistic situations is 80%" — Atreidon 2026-04-11); totems give the caster nothing ("our totems do nothing for us" — [Kokuyoo (player), 2026-03-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170416#p170416)); Earthquake nerfed/bugged in 1.18.1 (below).
- **Content**: raids (cleave fights best: Gnarlmoon, Incantagos per Atreidon), dungeons, farming (Lasher farm — see `leveling.md`); PvP — see `elemental-pvp.md`.

## Talent build

No source lists a full point-by-point 60 Elemental build; the standard is deep Elemental with Enhancement filler.

- **Named core (consensus)**: Convection 5, Concussion 5, Elemental Devastation 3 (needed toward the 17% spell-hit cap), Elemental Focus, Call of Thunder 5, Reverberation 3, Call of Flame 3, Improved Fire Totems 2 (Searing), Improved Molten Blast 2, Elemental Fury 2, Elemental Mastery, Lightning Mastery 5, Earthquake; Enhancement: Ancestral Knowledge 5, Thundering Strikes / Stable Shields as reachable. Skegg's leveling Elemental filler shows the 11-point Elemental Focus path; Aluevera's PvP 31/17/0 (see `elemental-pvp.md`) is the only explicit split in text.
- **Fire Elemental (pre-1.18.1 only)**: Elemental Weapons 3 in Enhancement + Call of Flame, Molten Blast spam; "Now fire elemental shaman with molten blast is top DPS" — [Elisleris (player), 2025-09-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152498#p152498); "Fire spec ele ... has good 1 target dps ... dont need to track fire shock duration ... hard to oom ... disadvantage - alot of fire immune bosses in mc/bwl+ony" — [pagu (player), 2025-09-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151552#p151552). Fire-immune/resistant list for MC/Ony/BWL (Duids: Ragnaros, Onyxia, Vaelastrasz, Firemaw, Ebonroc, Flamegor, Nefarian immune; Lucifron 93, Gehennas 186, Shazzrah 93, Sulfuron 93, Golemagg 186, Majordomo 95 fire resist) — [Duids (player), 2025-09-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153164#p153164). 1.18.1 made the +30% conditional on melee hits, so "in 1.18.1 ill go back to nature for sure" — [pagu (player), 2026-01-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166819#p166819) (**superseded**).
- **Talents the sources say to skip**: Eye of the Storm/old pushback talent ("the worst antipushback for pve" — pagu 2025-09-13; replaced by Call of Earth in 1.18.1 — Atreidon still rates it 80% at best); Nature's Grace (Resto) only for over-aggro.
- **1.18.1 impact**: Call of Earth added (Earth Shield charges + pushback; ES stacks replenished by offensive Nature spells per the 03-19 revision), Earthquake +15% / 18 s (then reported broken), Elemental Fury covers Frost. No post-release build posted (valid_for_1181 = unknown).

## Stat priority (60)

**Consensus** (Skegg/Discord, Duids' spreadsheet, Atreidon, Kokuyoo):

1. **Spell hit to 15–17%** — "Basically need 15% hit on spells, as much int and crit as possible and spd, hit > crit > spd > int" — [Skegg (player, guide writer), 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149715#p149715); the tank guide states "Magic hard cap ... = 17% hit (on boss lvl 63)" — [Mimiwarmini (player)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110875#p110875); Elemental Devastation gives 3%; T3 4-set +3% overcaps ("Full T3 has 6% + 3% from setbonus. Combined with the Elemental Devastation we are at 12% without Jewelry" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175219#p175219)).
2. **Crit** — mana engine; "for a crit based class, what's the point of a tier that give mostly haste? Haste can be bad if your crit is not high enough" — [Kokuyoo (player), 2026-04-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174964#p174964).
3. **Haste**, capped: "Haste is an incredibly powerful stat on elemental shaman for single target ... 17% is all we need to gain 25% more Damage. Unfortunately, that also marks the Hardcap of how much haste is Useful to us. More haste will just make our Lightning Bolts faster than global cooldown" — Atreidon 2026-04-14 (Lightning Mastery interaction; T3.5 5-set gives 10%).
4. **Spell power / Int** ("as much int and crit as possible" — Skegg; "around a 4k mana pool is recommended" for resto applies to fresh Ele too per pagu).
5. **Attack power** is a real but tiny stat for Flame Shock: "roughly 0.012 dps per attackpower or 0.02 if you snapshot ... it's easily justified to drop those consumables" — Atreidon 2026-04-11.
6. Spell penetration / −resistance items: Don Rodrigo's Band "does apply to all five schools of magic ... non-immune bosses have 15 default nature res ... it also helps with glancing (half-resisted) hits. This is something you need up to the point Lower Karazhan enchants are available or you have a Thunderfury tank" — [Duids (player), 2025-09-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153069#p153069) (single source).

Set choices: "All endgame eles ive seen dont run 8 pc t3. Some do 6pc. But most are 4pc ... searing totem despite having buffed range it is still very weak" — [Agentsmith (player), 2025-06-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136616#p136616) (pre-totem-hit/crit change). Atreidon's tier review (2026-04-14, post-1.18.1): T1 bonuses "largely irrelevant" and haste-loaded; T2 3-set (+6 s Flame Shock) and 8-set (20% free half-damage Lightning Bolt, ~8% LB DPS) good, 5-set outdated; T2.5 (Stormcaller) 5-set makes Chain Lightning worth using single-target ("deals 15% increased damage if it hits only one target"); T3 2/4/6 fine, 8 poor; **T3.5 (Stormhowl) 5-set is the real power** ("Elemental Mastery infuses you with the Stormwolf's Cunning, increasing your casting speed by 10% for 12 sec. This effect is refreshed when you critically hit an enemy with a nature spell ... it will likely remain the set to wear if you can keep the buff going") but "you rather let MB drop than this Setbonus" and "The 5 Piece T3.5 Setbonus is overstatted and suggests Elemental is actually serviceable, which is only true with exactly 5 pieces T3.5" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175219#p175219) (single, detailed source).

## Single-target rotation

**Consensus (Skegg/Discord, Atreidon 2026-04-11, pagu, Mdzielak):**

Pre-pull: totems (Strength of Earth, Windfury, Flametongue for the melee group; Mana Spring; Searing Totem on the boss), Water Shield (mana) or Earth Shield (pushback; with Call of Earth in 1.18.1), Brilliant Wizard Oil.

1. **Flame Shock** on the boss (snapshot it: "Good shamans use active trinkets, elemental mastery and time it with nightfall procs on the boss for maximum DOT damage. If it falls off, your cooldowns remain, forcing you to reapply a much weaker Flame Shock" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-04-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174801#p174801)). Flame Shock lasts 15 s (+6 s with T2 3-set). Use the Totem of Rage relic for the application if you own it; swapping back now costs a GCD (Atreidon; Jamey 2026-01-23).
2. **Lightning Bolt** as filler; **Chain Lightning on cooldown** when mana allows: "rotation for max dps on single target should look like 3LB+CL or 4LB+CL depending on your haste (and ofc keeping flameshock on with MB). CL is 1.5s cast and has higher dmg output than LB. That's the theory - quite mana hungry one i'd say. Spamming LB is more mana conservative but your dps is lower" — [Mdzielak (player), 2026-04-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175273#p175273). Without T2.5's 5-set, CL single-target is marginal (Atreidon).
3. **Molten Blast when Flame Shock has ≤ 3 s left** ("Ideally, you want to hit the target with MB in the last 3 seconds before the Flame Shock DOT expires ... Refreshing Flame Shock to early with Molten Blast is a DPS loss"); it is "a relatively slow traveling projectile", can be pushed back and can miss (1% even at cap), so "either cast 2 Molten Blasts back to back towards the end of the DOT duration ... or accepting it might fall of and manually reapply a weaker Flame Shock" — Atreidon 2026-04-11. Track the DoT with Cursive + SuperWoW ("It should work with Cursive - make sure you have the latest update and use it with SuperWoW" — [Skegg (player), 2025-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150999#p150999)). With 5/5 T3.5 skip Molten Blast entirely ("once you reach your full 3.5 set, You don't use Molten blast cause it doesnt refresh the 3.5 bonus" — Kokuyoo 2026-04-12).
4. **Earthquake** on cooldown only when it will not delay the T3.5 buff and the target will live > 4 s (post-1.18.1 sources call it worse than a Lightning Bolt; pre-1.18.1 it was "worth it I guess" — Kokuyoo 2026-02-16).
5. **Elemental Mastery** on cooldown (3 min, 10 s +15% / −20% mana): "using it on cooldown, you save about 120 mana and deal 0.75 times a Lightning Bolts worth of damage every 3 Minutes" — Atreidon 2026-04-14; align it with the Flame Shock application and with T3.5 (it starts the 10% haste).
6. **Movement**: Earth Shock (20 yd) or Flame Shock (30 yd with Call of Flame) while moving; "In fights that have a movement element to them, we need to be within 20 yards range, so we can use Earth Shock while moving, as using Flame Shock while moving replaces a likely better snapshotted DOT" — Atreidon 2026-04-11.
7. **Nature-immune targets** (Dragons of Nightmare, Chromaggus/Incantagos per Kokuyoo): Molten Blast spam + Flame Shock + Searing/Magma.
8. **Pre-1.18.0 note**: the Electrify opener ("cast 2 Rank 1 LBs to build 2 stacks in 3 seconds") is obsolete since Elemental Fury became passive on 2025-08-15.

Elemental Fire (pre-1.18.1, superseded): Flame Shock → Molten Blast spam; "has 1 spell, shocks, totems ... no rotation, just spam 1 button" (pagu 2025-09-13).

Spell ranks: Lightning Bolt R1 for pulls/threat control; downrank Lightning Bolt when oom in bad gear ("be carefull with chain lightning and sometimes downrank lightning bolt" — pagu 2026-04-06).

## Multi-target / AoE rotation

- **2–3 targets**: Chain Lightning on cooldown (3 targets, −30% per bounce; +1 target with T2.5 5-set), Flame Shock on the longest-lived, Lightning Bolt filler; Fire Nova only if you can stand in melee.
- **4+ targets**: "We wanna run into the pack to drop firenova and afterwards magma totem" — Atreidon 2026-04-11; Earthquake "has a place" for hard packs or packs you cannot approach, but "our primary target dying too early cancels the spell, our primary target running out of the pack places the AOE away ... and should the primary target resist, the entire damage from this ability is 0!"; "for most content, you are better off just running in, dropping a firenova, maybe a sapper and dont waste time trying to cast EQ" — Atreidon 2026-04-11. Fire totems now use your spell hit/crit (staff 2025-08-26) and Elemental Fury doubles their crit bonus (1.18.0). pagu's pre-1.18.1 warning: "if your target dies before cast finished, its canceled" (EQ) — [pagu, 2025-09-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151552#p151552).
- Lasher farming (DM East, level 52+): Fire Nova > Magma > Fire Nova with Water/Earth Shield swaps and Totem of Tides — see `leveling.md`.

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Elemental Mastery** (3 min): on cooldown, aligned with Flame Shock snapshot / T3.5 (above). Its old guaranteed-crit form is gone (1.18.0).
- **Ancestral Swiftness** (Resto, if taken): instant Chain Heal/Healing Wave for emergencies, or an instant CL at −25% (PvP mostly).
- **Mana**: Water Shield by default; "Both fire elemental and regular elemental shaman can easily sustain their mana-needs with manaspring totem, potions and tea even in the longest of fights without usage of watershield ... Being oom after a fight is not an issue. Being oom in a fight is" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168359#p168359); low gear: "Until you get good gear and alot of spell crit mana will be main issue. So you have to use alot of potions and tea" — pagu 2026-04-06; "leveling as ele shaman means drink half of you life" — pagu 2025-09-13. T3.5 3-set raises Clearcasting to 70% (Atreidon: irrelevant that late). Drink after every pack in dungeons ("Ele needs a lot of drinks" — [Ironbob (player), 2025-11-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159080#p159080)).
- **Racials**: Troll Berserking ("the best min-max race for PvE (quasi-Bloodlust haste racial for PvE" — Skegg); Orc Blood Fury also adds spell damage (staff batch); Hex is PvP/utility only.
- **Relics** (pre-1.18.1 swapping now GCD-gated): Totem of the Storm vs Crackling Thunder — "if you deal ~625 dps or more with straight lb casting, crackling thunder starts to overtake storm. But beware this only works to its fullest effect if you have 9% or less combined haste" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-06-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135891#p135891); post-1.18.1 "ele bis totem is still a blue boe" and Eruption/Rage are moot without swapping (Kokuyoo 2026-04-12); Atreidon uses Storm or Rage over Eruption/Crackling Thunder (2026-04-11).

## Role strategy

- **Group**: the melee group for Windfury ("As ele i always just wf totem bot for tank party" — [pagu, 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146924#p146924)); if the raid has spare shamans, a caster group with Tranquil Air (Akos1896 2025-09-13).
- **Totems**: Windfury + Strength of Earth + Flametongue + Mana Spring for the group; Searing on bosses only ("with extended range so much risk it pull extra pack, so i use it very rare, only on bosses" — pagu 2025-09-13); Tremor/Poison/Disease/Fire Resistance as needed; Grounding on Hakkar near the boss (Akos1896 resto guide).
- **Threat**: Nature's Grace (Resto) reduces Nature threat; Chain Lightning openers on trash pull threat off shaman/paladin tanks — wait 1–2 s (Akos1896 tank notes).
- **Pushback**: Earth Shield (30%, +70% with Call of Earth 2/2 in 1.18.1; T1 5-set +10%) on raid-wide-damage fights; "there are alot of packs and bosses where all raid gets damage. and shaman casts constantly pushed back" — pagu 2025-09-13.
- **Purge** (8-s CD): plan it for known buffs (Black Morass first boss, AQ20 second boss adds — Akos1896).
- **Off-healing**: Chain Heal R1 when nothing to nuke; Ancestral Swiftness emergency heal if talented.
- **Positioning for Flametongue-Elemental Weapons (1.18.1)**: only in melee range does the +30% Fire apply — staff intent; Atreidon calls it "the incredibly sweaty playpattern of imbue switching".

## Common mistakes

1. Letting Flame Shock drop and re-applying an unsnapshotted one; refreshing it early with Molten Blast (Atreidon).
2. Casting Earthquake on a target that will die or move; casting it at all in most 1.18.1 AoE (Atreidon, pagu).
3. Spamming Chain Lightning in bad gear and going oom mid-fight (pagu).
4. Over-capping hit (8/8 T3 + Devastation) or over-capping haste past ~17% with T3.5 (Atreidon).
5. Using Searing Totem near CC or patrols (pagu, Akos1896).
6. Relying on Eye of the Storm (pre-1.18.1) instead of Earth Shield on pushback fights (pagu).
7. Fire Elemental into MC/Ony/BWL fire-immune bosses (Duids/Boomcat).
8. Molten Blast while wearing 5/5 T3.5 (Kokuyoo).
9. Casting from outside 20 yd on movement fights so no shock is available (Atreidon).
10. Expecting Windfury/Flametongue totems to help you — they buff the party only (Kokuyoo, pagu).
