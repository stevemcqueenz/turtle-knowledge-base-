# Shaman — leveling guide (1–60)

Built from Skegg's "Shaman Leveling & Pre-BiS Guide" (2025-09-01, compiled from the Discord #shaman channel, preserved on the community wiki), Akos1896's warden-shaman leveling diary (2024-12-13), Mimiwarmini's dungeon-by-level tank chapter, the older "Spellhancement" guide (2023-10, pre-CC2), and Q&A threads through 2026-04. All talent orders use Turtle talent names after CC2 (1.17.2). Eras are marked; nothing here changed in 1.18.1 except Lightning Shield mana (−25%), Flurry, Elemental Weapons and Water Shield's Lightning Strike ratio.

## Spec choice

- **Enhancement (tank-capable build) is the consensus leveling spec**: "Enha going faster. Ele needs a lot of drinks" — [Ironbob (player), 2025-11-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159080#p159080); "Elemental is viable but it won't feel very good. You'll definitely be better off going enhancement, which is a pretty decent proper tank for dungeons too" — [Bigsmerf (player), 2025-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117631#p117631); "With this build, you can fill any role in dungeons: tank, DPS or heal. We'll be picking tanking talents, but even if you solely want to DPS, this is arguably still the best build because you do practically the same DPS ... If you want to skip the tank talents, you could swap out Shield Spec. for Ancestral Knowledge. For healing, all you'll need is some +int gear" — [Skegg (player, guide writer), 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149715#p149715).
- **Elemental Focus (11 Elemental points) is the single most important leveling talent** (consensus since 2023): "The most impactfull talent for leveling is the 11th point in elemental that proc clearcasting. Pair this with a fast weapon and a shield or a fast 2h" — [Kargal (player), 2023-08-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57082#p57082); "Try speccing 11 point into elemental while leveling. It proccs off of physical crits too and if you only shock with the cost reduction, shamans actually has very few manaproblems" — [Kairion (player), 2023-08-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55561#p55561). Post-CC2 the order flipped for many: "Grab lightning strike and stormstrike first, get elemental focus afterwards" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-03-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121471#p121471); "You just need to decide whether you want to rush for Stormstrike first or go into Elemental for Elemental Focus and your 3% hit first since both are extremely good" — [Mekunekud (player), 2024-12-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112209#p112209).
- **Race** (Skegg, consensus): Troll = best PvE min-max (Berserking, Hex ok in PvP); Tauren = best PvP and tankiest (+5% HP, War Stomp, Plainsrunning at 40 "saves us 2 talents on imp Ghost Wolf"); Orc = decent Enhancement (axes, Blood Fury). For tanking Akos1896 rates Orc lowest, Troll/Tauren best ([2024-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112575#p112575)).

**Respec points**: 33 (Shield Spec → Ancestral Knowledge, once the two JC/obsidian +11 Sta trinkets exist; Akos1896) and 51 (Skegg); dual spec via the Goblin Brainwashing Device if farmed 44–48 in Badlands/Uldaman (Skegg, "might be an unrealistic goal").

## Skill training priority

- "You can skip all but 1st rank of Resurrection. Can technically skip Flametongue imbue. Totems: you likely won't use the nature/frost/fire resistance totems during leveling enough to justify getting those. If you want, you can probably skip all totems besides the Poison/Disease cleansing totems, though for tanking you might want the strength/agi/WF totems. Water totems are optional ... If you don't plan to tank or play ele, skip the ranged damage spells (but never shocks). Everything else you need no matter the spec" — Skegg.
- Water Shield at 26 (R1), Earth Shield at 40 (R1); Totemic Recall at 10; Lightning Strike ranks at 20/38/56 (talent + trainer, ranks were missing from trainers until the 2026-03-25 hotfix); Stormstrike single rank at 30; Fire Nova R5 at 52 unlocks Lasher farming (Skegg). Molten Blast ranks from 20 (staff 2025-07-25). Bloodlust talent; Spirit totem quest at 50 ("the Vultures have a higher chance of dropping the Spider Eye and the Black Claw" — Skegg); racial quests at 40 (Feral Spirit / Hex / Totemic Slam — staff batch).
- Keep several Earth Shock ranks on the bars (max, mid, R1) — Akos1896/Bigsmerf.

## Playstyle 10–60

- **Imbue and shield**: "Rockbiter + Lightning Shield until level 41/42. Then Frostbrand becomes better with Elemental Focus + Elemental Weapons talents. At 60 you're gonna use Windfury imbue" — Skegg; "Rockbiter remains the best weapon imbue for leveling due to its consistency and raw output. The 10% dmg reduction from Elemental Weapons is also always active" — [Taralej90 (player), 2025-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126159#p126159) (that −10% became Earthen Bulwark in 1.18.1 — the "always active" part no longer holds). "the Water Shield helps you solo faster than Lightning Shield or Earth Shield. (However, test it for yourself because the Water Shield was nerfed slightly in 1.18.1)" — [Riiya (player), 2026-04-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174099#p174099).
- **Weapon**: "until Lightning Strike, a high dps dagger is just as good as a slow 2H; after Lightning Strike, a slow 2H is best" (Skegg, solo); "Use 2h once you get windfury weapon imbue ... Most important is keeping your 2hander up to date" — [amanagor (player), 2026-04-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173827#p173827); **contested**: "I found myself and other shamans prefer 1H + shield for soloing all the way up to 60. The better damage just does not compensate the time lost for getting mana back" — Riiya 2026-04-05.
- **Kill rotation** (Skegg): to 44 "Rockbiter + Lightning Shield, using Lightning Strike -> Storm Strike -> Earth Shock -> Lightning Strike. You spend your mana at the beginning, then melee the mob down because you want to start the mana regen ASAP"; from 44 "Frostbrand + Water Shield, using Frost Shock -> Stormstrike -> Lightning Strike". Pull with Lightning Bolt R1. "Using totems isn't recommended, though Searing is probably fine if you're sticking in the same location" (Skegg); "Searing totem is very nice in itself. It's mana efficient ... but you'd move on to new targets before you reap the benefits" (Riiya).
- **Mobs at once**: one at a time solo; "you can kill 10+ mobs with a rest" with on-hit Nature/Fire items feeding Clearcasting — [Terryleelolorz (player), 2023-12-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81505#p81505) (pre-CC2 spellhance).
- **Downtime**: "Earthbind weaving with a Swing timer is worth doing if you care about reducing the damage you take fighting mobs 1v1": Earthbind > Searing > Flame Shock, strafe out during the swing reset, re-enter to swing (Skegg). "Mana will be a problem, only put down totems you really wanna use" — [Akos1896 (player), 2024-07-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99781#p99781). Carry water; Water Shield from 26 "gets easier" (Riiya).
- **Threat while grouped as DPS**: Rockbiter and Earth Shock "give increased threat. If you aggro mobs too often, consider using other shocks" (Akos1896 2024-07-18).
- **Old spellhance path (2023-10, pre-CC2, single source, partly obsolete)**: fast 1H + shield, Flametongue, Elemental Focus first, Shield Spec/Improved Ghost Wolf/Thundering Strikes, Improved Lightning Shield + Thunderhead (both removed in CC2), Call of Thunder/Elemental Devastation/Elemental Fury; items with "+3 Nature damage" / "+2 Fire damage" on hit (Thunder Lizard's Hide 81308, Flameskin Gloves 81267, Leggings of Fiery Temper 61505, Lavadrenched Chainmail 80704, Repaired Electro-Lantern 65030, Storm Gauntlets, Incendosaur set); Hand of Edward the Odd at 57; Blade of Eternal Darkness — [Hartmut (player), 2023-10-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69447#p69447), [Imonobor (player), 2023-12-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81617#p81617). The on-hit-item Clearcasting trick still works mechanically (Elemental Focus procs from any Fire/Frost/Nature crit — staff), the talents named do not.

## Stat priority while leveling and key upgrades

- "Stats for tanking while leveling: +stam > +agi > +str > +int ... For DPS: +str = +agi > +stam > +int ... For healing: +int = +hp"; "A shield with high armor is always a top prio ... 10 armor is roughly equal to 1 stamina" — Skegg.
- Upgrades by level (Skegg, Akos1896, Mimiwarmini): level 15 quest shield (`quest=40390`, Mimiwarmini) and Cobalt Buckler (5302); Deviate Scale Belt / Deviate Hide Pack (WC quests); Seal of Sylvanas (SFK quest); Triprunner Dungarees (Gnomeregan "Rig Wars"), Marbled Buckler and Monkey Ring (RFK quests); Compendium of the Fallen (28, Sage Truthseeker in Thunder Bluff 34.7,47.8, SM Library) → SM Cathedral shield → Gilneas / Maraudon goblin / SWV shield; Chain of the Crusader set or Tough Scorpid (LW) from SM; Divino-matic Rod (ZF quest); **level 33: two Jewelcrafting +stam trinkets** (obsidian brooch, ~5–6 g each, non-unique — Akos1896 2024-12-06); **35–41: Totem of Tides** (Stormwrought orc boss, "phenomenal for tanking dungeons + farming Lashers"); level-40 racial quests and Plainsrunning; ~50 axe `item=51892`; Stormshroud shoulders/hands (LW ~50), Devilsaur hands/legs (53/55), Hateforge set (BS ~50) then Towerforge (Kara10); 58+ Skyfury Helm quest (`quest=7667`, Scholomance); Thunder Lizard's Hide (Sparkwater Port vendor, ~8 g, limited stock) for on-hit Nature procs (Hartmut).
- Enchants/consumables while leveling: armor kits, fortitude/armor potions and health potions before Black Morass (Skegg).

## Hardcore-mode differences

Only fragments exist (gap): a hardcore Ele shaman at 30+ was "really strong, interesting and fun to play" compared with a fury warrior — [Darktifa (player), 2023-08-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55563#p55563) (pre-CC2); Akos1896's general tank rule "avoid red dungeons and I usually reconsider if it's okay for me going into an orange one" ([2024-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112575#p112575)); Earthbind + run as the exit strategy; Ankh is not a hardcore safety net in the sources (unstated). No hardcore-specific shaman thread was found in the extracted forums.

## Common leveling mistakes (from the sources)

1. Dropping every totem every fight (Skegg, Akos1896, Riiya).
2. Tanking SM/oranges before 33 without the stamina trinkets (Akos1896).
3. Taking Elemental first when you intend to tank early, or skipping Elemental Focus entirely (both camps agree it arrives by ~44).
4. Windfury imbue before you own a real 2H; Rockbiter on a DPS at 60 (Skegg, Taralej90).
5. Casting Lightning Bolts instead of meleeing (mana) — "Most of damage was melee. LB used only to pull enemies" — [Xudo (player), 2023-08-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58176#p58176).
6. Buying every spell rank (skip Resurrection ranks, resistance totems — Skegg).
7. Ghost Wolf with Earth Shield expectations (not castable in wolf).
