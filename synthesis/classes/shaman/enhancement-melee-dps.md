# Shaman — Enhancement melee DPS (raids and dungeons)

Playbook for two-handed Windfury Enhancement on Turtle WoW after Class Changes 2 (patch 1.17.2, live 2024-10/11), the 1.18.0 adjustments (2025-08-15) and patch 1.18.1 (2026-03-20). A second, minority playstyle — **"spellhance"** (one-hander + shield, Frostbrand/Flametongue, spell power gear, Electrify/Elemental Fury) — is documented where the sources cover it. Every claim is cited and dated; **pre-1.18.1**, **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03..2026-03-19) and **post-1.18.1** are marked.

## Overview

- **Role**: two-handed melee DPS whose damage is auto-attacks + Windfury procs + three instants (Stormstrike, Lightning Strike, a Shock) with Lightning Shield charges as a fourth damage source; brings Windfury / Strength of Earth / Flametongue / Mana Spring totems and self-Bloodlust.
- **Community standing**: **contested** between "very viable" and "far behind warriors". For: "nummerically speaking enhancement is one of the big winners of the patch what pve is concerned" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-03-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121471#p121471); "sub 2 hour naxx clears ... with enhancement shaman near the top" — [Drubarrymooer (player), 2025-01-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115650#p115650); "Enhancement shaman is better in terms of dps, can be in upper half" — [pagu (player), 2026-04-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174156#p174156) (post-1.18.1); "Enh has simple rotation with huge dps compared to elemental" — [Mdzielak (player), 2026-04-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173707#p173707). Against: "a bis warrior does over 30% more dps than a bis ele and 38% more dps than a bis enha" — [amanagor (player), 2025-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115753#p115753); "Enh Shaman remain relatively weak in the grand scheme of DPS'ers, particularly on Nordanaar with late-game scaling" — [Phx (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173678#p173678). Staff view: "Enhancement, thanks to a steady supply of cautious buffs, the class is rising as a top contender for high single-target damage-dealing during its windows of power, namely Bloodlust ... Future efforts will be devoted to carefully tuning the class and bring it on par with other melee single-target damage-dealers" — [Jamey (staff), 2024-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460).
- **Raid spot**: always available because of Windfury Totem, but guilds prefer Restoration: "Most guilds want shamans but they'd prefer said shaman to be resto. Still, you can easily find raid spots as ele or enh" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-01-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115603#p115603); "Raids really like to see 1 WF totem in every melee party" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-01-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115643#p115643); "They are designed as mostly single-target focused, burst DPS, meaning that in shorter windows they can output disgusting damage. Very good for farmed contents, not really effective for progression" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117662#p117662).
- **Strengths**: WF procs that scale with gear ([Taralej90, 2025-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126159#p126159)), self-Bloodlust burst window, cleave via Fire Nova / Chain Lightning ("shamans has some of the best cleave damage assets in his kit. Both chain lightning as well as fire nova totem" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-04-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173712#p173712)), party utility.
- **Weaknesses**: RNG ("Very RNG-dependent — some fights, you top the meters, others, you don't" — [Franktank (player), 2025-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124808#p124808)); threat ("your damage output doesn't match the threat output ... it's not difficult to pull threat even if you're, say, not even top 3 on the meter" — [Phx (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173678#p173678); "Threat Magnet - beg for salv" — Franktank above); mana outside raids ("an interesting build now built around being oom" — [Akos1896 (player), 2024-12-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113240#p113240)); the Windfury Totem you drop does nothing for you while an imbue is active (staff rule below); Enhancement has no real AoE of its own ([Phx, 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173678#p173678), contested by Atreidon above).
- **Content**: raids MC–Naxx/Kara40 and dungeons; leveling — see `leveling.md`; PvP — see the PvP section below.

## Stat priority (60)

**Consensus** (Skegg's compiled guide, Taralej90, Atreidon, Akos1896, wiki `Shaman guide`):

1. **Weapon skill to 315** ("If you're a melee class, then you want to go for the hard cap of 315, period" — [Taralej90 (player), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147005#p147005); "the +5 weapon skill got nerfed to +3, and the weapon skill glancing damage cap is now 315, not 308" — [Crake (player), 2025-09-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151912#p151912)). Sources: Orc racial +3, the 250 g weapon-skill book quest (+5, `t=16790`), items (+6/7/8). Glancing per the tank guide: 35% proc, −35% damage, "We can reduce Glancing damage reduction by a maximum of 30% or 15 weapon skill points" — [Mimiwarmini (player), 2024-12-02 (edited to 2026-03-22)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110875#p110875).
2. **Hit to 8%** — "The hit cap on T-WoW is 8 instead of 9 because the useless hidden 1% from vanilla has been removed" — Taralej90 above; "Aim for 8% hit for melee. You'll get 3% from talents, and another 1% from Weapon Skill book ... So usually, you'll only need to get 4% more from gear/weapon skill" — [Skegg (player, guide writer), 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149715#p149715). Spell hit for shocks comes from Elemental Devastation procs; the tank guide measured "the devastation talent increases the hit spell by 12% under proc" — [Mimiwarmini (player)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110875#p110875).
3. **Crit** — "At early gear levels, you severely lack crit to keep Flurry and Clearcasting up, which are the bread and butter of your DPS. Beyond T2 you should just go for Str" — Taralej90 above; "the rule of thumb is to get 30-35% crit, then start stacking AP and +haste (the more haste you get the better)" — Skegg above.
4. **Strength > Agility** at 60 ("Str > 2 AP due to it scaling with talents and buffs" — Taralej90; "shamans get 0 AP per agi, 2 AP from str and 1% dodge and 1% crit per 20 agi" — Skegg).
5. **Haste** after crit (Skegg above).
6. **Stamina before Int/Spirit/mp5** — "Pretend mp5 doesnt exist ... Spirit intellect and mp5 should all never prioritized over stamina. You are still a relatively flimsy melee if you rock a twohander" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147011#p147011); Akos1896 disagrees only for levelers: "LS does AP scaling and before having a good-enough gear (most levelers) it costs mana, doesn't recharge it" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147014#p147014).

Equivalence table (Skegg, compiled from the Discord #shaman channel; "1 X = AP / SP" — single source, pre-1.18.1): 1% phys hit ≈ 29.09 AP (to the 8% cap); 1% phys crit ≈ 20.69 AP (cap 54%); 1 weapon skill ≈ 16.66 AP; 1% haste ≈ 10.64 AP; 1 Str = 2.3 AP; 1 Agi ≈ 1.13 AP; 1 spell hit ≈ 9.13 AP; 1 spell crit ≈ 2.70 AP; 1 SP ≈ 0.54 AP; 1 armor pen ≈ 0.34 AP; 1 spell pen ≈ 3.12 AP; 1 Int ≈ 0.05 AP — [Skegg (player, guide writer), 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149715#p149715) (also on the community wiki `Shaman guide`, revision 2026-07-03).

**T3 hit overcap**: "Why do we have 6% hit on our enhance t3? ... assuming we're using t3 and our talents, no jewelry or trinkets, we're at 9% hit ... things like the t2 Chest, Boots, and Gloves are better than the t3 versions because they have haste. Only the t3 helm has haste" — [Tipper (player), 2025-08-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145460#p145460); "6 piece is useless ... no one cares about t3 anymore, t3.5 is where its at" — [Therlx (player), 2025-08-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146036#p146036).

**Spellhance stats** (single source): "Attack Power > AGI > Spell Power > Hit/Crit% ... AP seems king after the Shaman rework" — [Franktank (player), 2025-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124808#p124808).

While leveling: "+str = +agi > +stam > +int (+str & +agi roughly on par, at 60 +str is more valuable than +agi)" — Skegg above.

## Weapon imbue and shield choice

- **Windfury Weapon at 60 for raids (consensus)**: Taralej90's 10-min dummy test (pre-1.18.1): vs level-60 dummy Rockbiter 178 / **Windfury 220** / Flametongue 178 / Frostbrand 178 / Frostbrand+WF totem 230 DPS; vs boss dummy Windfury 167 / Flametongue 138 / Frostbrand 151 / Frostbrand+WF totem 167. "Windfury comes out on top in almost every situation. It also scales with the rest of your gear, unlike all other weapon imbues" — [Taralej90 (player), 2025-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126159#p126159). "Windfury is preferred for raiding" — Skegg. In 1.18.1 WF went to 25% per hit ([Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)) — no post-release imbue test exists.
- **Frostbrand + Frost Shock** for dungeons/soloing and for one-hand builds ("Nope for 1h damage FB ench + Frost shock still the best, but with wf and ft totem it is even better" — [pagu (player), 2025-05-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130407#p130407)); useless on bosses because "bosses are immune to the effect of Frost Shock" — Taralej90 above; "Frostbrand utterly pointless once you stop leveling" — [Travoltino (player), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172162#p172162).
- **Flametongue** only with Elemental Weapons and fire-totem play; after 1.18.1 the +30% Fire bonus needs a melee hit first: "you usually want to apply Flame Shock while running in to your target. But you loose 30% of it's potential damage if you didn't wait for a hit to proc it. Similarly, if you get parried or dodged, the buff might fade ... it's peak comedy, that using a weapon oil and WF + FT Totems is now outperforming Flametongue imbue" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-04-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174801#p174801) (post-1.18.1).
- **Rockbiter never as DPS** ("anything but Rockbiter - at 60 WF" — Skegg) except leveling (Taralej90: "Rockbiter remains the best weapon imbue for leveling").
- **Shield**: Lightning Shield for damage, Water Shield when mana is low: "use Windfury imbue + Lightning Shield; swap to Water Shield when low on mana. Keep up Lightning Shield" — Skegg; "chug mana pots and tea on CD to max damage to sustain Lightning shield longer otherwise you swap to Water shield" — Skegg. Water Shield with Lightning Strike returns "130 mana (without attackpower) 20 attackpower gives 1 mana" (18 AP per mana since 1.18.1) — [Dezhan (player), 2024-11-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106681#p106681); Atreidon: "You can generate thousands of mana per minute with watershield and lightning strike" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147011#p147011) (contested by Akos1896 for low gear, same thread).
- **Weapon**: slowest 2H with the highest top-end ("After getting Lightning Strike, try to have as slow of a 2H as possible ... enhance prefers slow weapons with high top-end dmg" — Skegg). Pre-raid: Arcanite Reaper or Bonecrusher, Treant's Bane, Unstoppable Force (AV exalted), Boneshatter Maul (Kara Crypts), Dreadforge Retaliator (BRD), Sleeping Willow (UBRS) — Skegg; [Toxo (player), 2025-09-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151884#p151884). Nightfall as "a signature Enh weapon" — Taralej90. Hand of Edward the Odd for spellhance ("was made for this build — fast attacks = more Frostbrand procs + instant CLs" — Franktank).

## Single-target rotation

**Consensus (Skegg's guide = Discord #shaman compilation, Franktank, Atreidon):**

Pre-pull: Windfury Weapon on the 2H (1 h duration), Lightning Shield (9 charges with Stable Shields), totems down (Strength of Earth, Windfury, Flametongue, Mana Spring — Skegg). Do not pre-Bloodlust; it is a 30-s self buff (see cooldowns).

1. **Running in: Flame Shock** (30 yd range via Call of Flame only if talented; otherwise 20 yd) "or Earth Shock if immune" — "Don't use Flame Shock on targets that won't live more than 9-15 seconds ... At max level it's always at least 3 ticks (9 seconds)" — [Skegg (player, guide writer), 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149715#p149715). Flame Shock lasts 15 s since 2024-12-20.
2. **Stormstrike** on cooldown (8 s) — grants the +25% Nature buff (2 charges) that Earth Shock and Lightning Shield charges consume.
3. **Lightning Strike** on cooldown (9 s) with Lightning Shield up.
4. **Shock on cooldown (6 s; 5 s with Reverberation 3/3)**: Earth Shock while the Stormstrike buff is up; re-apply Flame Shock when it has < 3 s left or has dropped ("Earth Shock (or Flame Shock to reapply debuff), repeat. TL;DR: FS/ES -> SS -> LS, repeat" — Skegg). Staff intent for 1.18.0: "We're slightly buffing Stormstrike's bonus effect to move Earth Shock higher in priority" — [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159).
5. **Never hold Stormstrike or Lightning Strike for a Windfury window**: "If you hold a stormstrike until wf is ready, you on average get 0.2 autoattacks worth of damage. Every time you cast the ability you gain ~1 auto attacks worth regardless of wf cd. So its never worth holding your stormstrike or lightning strike for more than 1/5 of their cooldown" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-07-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141835#p141835). Taralej90's contrary tip (delay strikes to just after the auto-attack because a WF proc on a strike resets the swing timer) is **contested**; Atreidon's post is later and addresses DPS, Taralej90's is a mechanics note.
6. **Clearcasting**: with Elemental Focus every crit (melee or spell) gives 2 charges of −60% mana; since 2025-03-30 they also discount Stormstrike and Lightning Strike, so simply keep casting on cooldown — no source recommends fishing for procs at 60.
7. **Re-cast Lightning Shield** when charges run out (Lightning Strike eats one per use); swap to Water Shield below roughly the mana you need for the rest of the fight (no source gives a %).

**Spellhance/Electrify variant (single source, Franktank)**: "1. Chain Lightning or Lightning Bolt opener for Electrify stacks ... You can also cast 2 Rank 1 LBs to build 2 stacks in 3 seconds. 2. Frost Shock for guaranteed Frostbrand crits (or Earth Shock if running WF). 3. Spam auto attacks, Lightning Strike, Stormstrike. 4. If using Hand of Edward the Odd, spam CL when it procs" — [Franktank (player), 2025-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124808#p124808). Since 1.18.0 Electrify is a passive Elemental Fury, so step 1 is obsolete; the Frost Shock + Frostbrand core still applies only to non-boss targets.

**Spell ranks**: Earth Shock R1 is the interrupt (0.5× threat vs max rank per the tank/resto guides); Lightning Bolt R1 (~0.5 s cast per Manletow) was the Electrify stack builder — irrelevant after 1.18.0.

## Multi-target / AoE rotation

- **2–3 targets**: keep the single-target loop on the kill target; the sources give no spread rotation for DPS. Chain Lightning is "worth considering even as a melee" ([Atreidon (player), 2026-04-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173712#p173712)) but costs a 2.5-s cast (1.5 s per Mdzielak on Ele) and 490 mana.
- **4+ targets**: run in, drop **Fire Nova Totem** (triggers GCD, 15-s CD; 413–459 base, +30% fire with Elemental Weapons Flametongue after a melee hit) then **Magma Totem** (650 mana), plus Chain Lightning; "We wanna run into the pack to drop firenova and afterwards magma totem" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-04-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174801#p174801). Both totems now inherit the shaman's spell hit/crit (staff 2025-07-25/08-26). Recall totems (Totemic Recall, 25% refund per totem, +15% with Totemic Mastery) after the pack.
- Fire totems scale with spell power, not AP: "There's no scaling on your Fire totems unless you're Elemental or some Spellhance hybrid" — [Phx (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173678#p173678).
- Threat: Fire Nova is "huge snap threat" (Skegg's tank notes) — do not open with it on trash when the tank is a shaman or paladin without Salvation.

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Bloodlust** (300-s CD, 395 mana per the tank guide): 20% haste for 30 s on yourself; your melee crits give the party +8% for 6 s. Use it at the start of a boss fight once Flame Shock/Stormstrike are up, and again on cooldown; "top contender ... during its windows of power, namely Bloodlust" — [Jamey (staff), 2024-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460). With Troll Berserking it is "an almost bloodlust on a smaller CD" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149742#p149742).
- **Racials**: Orc Blood Fury (+AP, +SP, −25% healing 25 s; staff 2024-10-12 batch) and axe skill — "Orc is still a decent choice for Enhance PvE (axe skill helps + Blood Fury)" — Skegg; Taralej90 ranks Orc first for WF glancing reasons; estebanmgn's tests put Orc ahead of Troll in short fights, Troll ahead only past ~22 s with a hypothetical racial buff — [estebanmgn (player), 2025-04-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126341#p126341) (single source). Feral Spirit (Orc, 5-min CD) wolves scale with the shaman's damage since 2023-05-24 and no longer with AP since CC2; Manletow's "equip a Rockbiter 2H before summoning" trick ([Manletow (player), 2024-06-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96568#p96568)) predates that removal — **outdated**.
- **Mana**: "be ready to chug mana pots and tea on CD to max damage to sustain Lightning shield longer otherwise you swap to Water shield when you're low on mana. Your mana consumption will also go down in raids, especially if you have ferals around. Blessing of Kings, Mark of the Wild, elixirs like Mongoose and Dreamshard" — Skegg. Mana Spring Totem for the party; Judgement of Wisdom from a paladin if available. Water Shield + Lightning Strike math (tank guide): LS costs 0 mana with Water Shield at 936 AP ("Any AP point above 936 will cause us to generate mana") — [Mimiwarmini (player)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110875#p110875) (18:1 ratio; written for tanks, applies to DPS with Water Shield).
- **Drink** between pulls in dungeons when below the mana for one full pack; in raids use Nordanaar Herbal Tea and Major Mana Potions on cooldown (Skegg).

## Role strategy

- **Positioning**: behind the boss (parries/dodges waste WF procs — [Voodoochile (player), 2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141504#p141504)); in the melee group so your Windfury/Strength of Earth/Flametongue totems (30 yd) cover them.
- **Threat**: take Calming Winds 3/3 (25%) and ask for Blessing of Salvation; Earth Shock keeps its 1.5× threat modifier and is not covered by Calming Winds ("Calming Winds ... ours doesn't include Earth Shock" — [Phx (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173678#p173678); "Many fights i cant even use frostshocks on cd without taking aggro (without Salvation)" — [Dezhan (player), 2024-11-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106681#p106681)). Never use Rockbiter in a raid as DPS (+35% threat).
- **Interrupts**: Earth Shock R1 (6-s/5-s CD) and Grounding Totem (20-s CD, GCD, breaks on CC); Purge has an 8-s CD since 1.18.0 (revision) — plan purges, do not spam.
- **Totem duty**: Windfury + Strength of Earth (+ Flametongue, Mana Spring) in the melee group; "At 60 you're gonna use Windfury imbue, but you're still gonna drop Windfury Totem (and Flametongue Totem) for your party even if you don't benefit from it" — Skegg. Grace of Air instead of Windfury only for hunter/druid/tank groups — see [Mimiwarmini (player), 2026-02-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168008#p168008) vs [Elesion (player), 2026-02-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168045#p168045) (bears prefer WF for rage) — **contested**.
- **Relic**: Totem of the Stonebreaker (ES) over Totem of Crackling Thunder (Kara10): "Crackling thunder used to be really good, now its numbers are pathetic ... 1.6% haste on LS ... stonebreaker is probably the better choice" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-06-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135891#p135891). - **PvP tactics (Enhancement)** — sources treat it as burst-then-kited: "Enhance sham has gotten a few new skills/combo that increased their burst but are easily kited in pvp. If you are able to get to the target you have a fair shot and with gear can 1 shot clothies with a lucky WF proc" — [Evolve (player), 2025-05-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129436#p129436); "They seem to be dependant on Stormstrike though so they do huge damage on their first global then have to wait 8 sec for CD. Very predictable burst" — [Forumdweller (player), 2025-05-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129441#p129441); "Enh shaman with 2h is the squishiest melee ... Tank shaman with a shield is good, but dont have burst ... Maybe B" — [pagu (player), 2025-05-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130405#p130405); "2H ehna has the same weakness as before: no hard CCS, no defensive cds, no way to properly reset a fight, extremely weak to kiting. Lightning Strike "can also be used while silenced" — [amanagor (player), 2025-05-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131960#p131960). ## Common mistakes

1. Using Rockbiter or Earth Shock spam as DPS without Salvation — threat (Dezhan, Phx above).
2. Holding Stormstrike/Lightning Strike for Windfury (Atreidon above).
3. Flame Shock on targets that die in < 9 s (Skegg).
4. Wearing 8/8 T3 for the bonuses while overcapping hit (Tipper).
5. Expecting Windfury Totem to buff yourself while an imbue is on (staff rule); or using Flametongue imbue instead of WF + Flametongue Totem + oil at 60 (Atreidon 2026-04-11).
6. Frostbrand on bosses (immune to Frost Shock's slow, so no guaranteed crits) — Taralej90.
7. Bloodlust before Flame Shock/Stormstrike are set up, or before you have crit to feed its party effect (Atreidon 2025-03-03 on mana/crit).
8. Chasing mp5/Int/Spirit gear (Atreidon 2025-08-19).
9. Dropping totems every pull while soloing ("They're not often worth dropping since you'll be killing faster than the mana is worth" — Skegg).
10. Post-1.18.1: assuming Flurry works from white crits — as of 2026-03-24 players still reported it did not, and Elemental Weapons' Windfury haste may override it (Splitsec, Travoltino).
