# Shaman — Elemental PvP (battlegrounds, with the ele/enh "battle caster" hybrid)

The PvP sources treat Elemental separately from PvE because the CC2 talent Electrify (2024-10 .. 2025-08) and the 1.18.0 Elemental Mastery / Ancestral Swiftness changes removed the guaranteed-crit burst opener; the resulting meta build is a **31/17/0 Elemental/Enhancement hybrid** (Lightning Strike, Ancestral Guardian, Spirit Armor, Improved Ghost Wolf) that trades Nature's Swiftness for armor and a silence-proof melee hit. Enhancement PvP notes are in `enhancement-melee-dps.md`; deep-Resto PvP has one favourable source (Manletow) and is not written up. Source quality: **weak–medium** (one detailed playstyle post, many standing arguments).

## Overview

- **Community standing**: **contested** in the extreme. Pro-nerf voices call shamans the strongest PvP class ("1.18.0 was golden patch for Shamans. In my opinion it was the most powerful class in PvP" — [Zvyrhol (player), 2026-03-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171249#p171249); "Current ele is significantly stronger than current enha" — [amanagor (player), 2025-05-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130008#p130008); "ele and enh are godtier. they are able to 2shot anything if properly geared" — [Volkyte (player), 2025-08-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147963#p147963)); Elemental mains call pure Ele "cooked" ("Ele sucks, you have no way of healing yourself through hunter+pet barrage ... They removed the guaranteed crit. You can't get natures swiftness comfortably anymore" — [Thelwald (player), 2025-11-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158461#p158461); "Ele: not that great and honestly just a downgrade from vanilla" — [ForumGentleman (player), 2025-05-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130102#p130102)). The hybrid is the meta both sides describe: "the point of the spec is to be a high dmg caster that also happens to have 9k armor and a big hp pull in full t3" — [amanagor (player), 2025-05-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131960#p131960); "Tanky shaman are fire one, enh/ele hybrid with hilarous tank talents and high % dogde. Normal elemental build (ele/resto) with shield have just ~50%dr from armor" — [Mdzielak (player), 2026-03-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171242#p171242).
- **Staff direction**: Electrify was "to mitigate the overall burstiness Elemental Shaman has in PvP"; 1.18.0 restored Elemental Fury's crit bonus but reworked Elemental Mastery "to deal its overall damage over a longer period of time instead of in one giant burst", made Purge 8-s CD, Grounding break on CC with a 20-s CD, and Nature's Swiftness → Ancestral Swiftness with −25% on damaging spells — [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25 (+ revision)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159). 1.18.1: PvP dampening "will now have dynamic reductions of Magical and Physical damage based on your class / spec" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169701#p169701) (Shaman values not published); Earthquake +15%; Elemental Weapons' Rockbiter turned into Earthen Bulwark explicitly to stop "non-Enhancement specializations from using the effect as a passive benefit which led to them being more durable, especially in Player vs Player environments" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **Strengths**: Chain Lightning burst (a BiS feral reports "2k Chain lighting with 1.5second cast, and another 1000+ Shock" — [Wosator (player), 2026-03-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171236#p171236)), Earth Shock interrupt every 5–6 s, Grounding, Purge, Frost Shock/Earthbind slows, heals, mail + shield + Ancestral Guardian armor/dodge, Ghost Wolf.
- **Weaknesses**: no hard CC (Hex 5-min CD, 4 s — "Honestly i wasted my money" — [Grinched (player), 2026-03-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169946#p169946)), no defensive cooldown, Nature school lock = death ("shaman is the least counter-spell protected caster with only grounding as a defence mechanism" — [Ataika (player), 2024-11-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109178#p109178)), hard-countered by warlocks ("Play a warlock" — [Voodoochile (player), 2025-06-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137448#p137448)), arcane mages ("If you do not ground Arcane Rupture he will delete 80% of your health" — Ataika).

## Talent build

- **Aluevera's 31/17/0 hybrid (single detailed source, pre-1.18.0 Electrify era, 2025-05-21)**: Elemental Mastery ("for those big crits" — old version), Reverberation ("short CD on shocks 6s->5s"), no fire-totem talents, Lightning Strike, "all the armor i can get" (Ancestral Guardian, Spirit Armor), Improved Ghost Wolf; "u go for 31/17/0. u skip talents like call of flames and improved nova and dump all points into improved shocks" — [Aluevera (player), 2025-05-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132316#p132316), [2025-06-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134228#p134228). Since 1.18.0 Elemental Mastery is a 10-s +15% buff and Electrify is passive — the point list still fits (Elemental Fury 2 pts replaced Electrify 1 pt; Reverberation dropped from 5 to 3 pts).
- **Stable Shields is contested for PvP**: "Stable shields is an awful talent for pvp ... only after 18 seconds in combat do you gain any value ... you should only ever skill it if you grab it for lightning strike. And lightning strike want you to wear slow weapons" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-05-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131896#p131896); "Stable Shields is good for PVP. Constantly getting hit by stupid things that waste those few precious charges Such as AV NPC archers" — [Manletow (player), 2025-05-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131910#p131910); Aluevera: "improved elemental shields are really nice for push back reduction as well 3 charges are gone asap".
- **Ancestral Guardian is the talent the other side wants nerfed**: "a Shaman spends only three points to gain 6% dodge plus 15% increased armor from items ... roughly equivalent to 8–10 talent points worth of stats" — [Sylbrosa (player), 2026-01-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165721#p165721); "tanking talents should be deeper in Enhancement tree" — [Kerenis (player), 2026-01-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165723#p165723). Not changed in 1.18.1.
- **Ele/Resto** (Nature's Swiftness → Ancestral Swiftness at −25% damage) is the "support" variant: "If u want to heal most of the bg stay back and be support play ele/restro. if u find ur self alone on the BG or just want to play more offensive go ele/enhance" — Aluevera. "if you are really serious about PVP, you should still grab nature swiftness anyways" — ForumGentleman 2025-05-07 (pre-rename).
- Earthquake is "not a useful PvP Capstone, yet the game actively punishes us for wanting to spend the talentpoints effectively for PvP" (Ancestral Swiftness −25%) — [Atreidon (player), 2026-04-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174801#p174801).

## Stat priority

- "u are still ele so u prio SP"; stamina pieces from quest rings/necks ("up to 5.4k HP" in full T2.5); swap to the Kara10 reflect shield against casters; Blood Guard boots + Improved Ghost Wolf "almost at 60% mount speed" — Aluevera 2025-05-21 (single source).
- Spell penetration matters against Nature-resist gear (Don Rodrigo's Band applies to all schools — Duids, see `elemental-ranged-dps.md`); pagu asks for "more spellhit, because amount of resists kills me" (PvE) — no PvP hit target stated (gap).
- "we also have resi - it lower %dmg from crits. But there's not much source of it yet" — Mdzielak 2026-03-18 (1.18.1 PvP sets add resilience; values not stated for shaman).

## Single-target "rotation" (Aluevera's playstyle, single source)

1. "stay back as far as u can and cast as much as possible like normal ele does" — Lightning Bolt / Chain Lightning; Flame Shock at 30 yd (Call of Flame) to open on approach.
2. Against a class with a silence/kick: "get in shock range and only rely on ur shocks and LB R1"; keep Grounding Totem down before the enemy's key cast (Ataika: Arcane Rupture).
3. Melee reaches you before you have cast: "stay with earth shield and cast 2 LB r1 swap to Lightning shield can shock lightning strike till u get away or (more likely) target dies" — Aluevera. Lightning Strike works while silenced (amanagor 2025-05-19) and "ignores silence and most of its dmg armor" (Aluevera).
4. Earth Shock to interrupt heals/nukes (5-s CD with Reverberation); Frost Shock + Earthbind to peel/kite; Purge buffs (8-s CD — plan it: "Purge spam was so bad" is why the CD exists, per 1.18.1-thread posters).
5. Heal yourself with Earth Shield up (30% pushback, +70% with Call of Earth in 1.18.1); Water Shield when oom.
6. Pre-1.18.0 Electrify management ("Lightning Bolt R1 is ur favorite spell u press it every time to keep ur electrify stacks up" — Aluevera; "Use Rank 1 LB to get Electrified easily (less than .5 sec cast time)" — [Manletow (player), 2024-11-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109183#p109183)) is **obsolete** since 2025-08-15.
7. Elemental Mastery (1.18.0+): a 10-s +15% window — pop it before a Chain Lightning + shock combo; no PvP source post-1.18.0 describes its use (gap).

Support role when undergeared: "Avoid 1vs1, assist your strong teammates ... Dispel enemy buffs, heal allies if needed" — [Xudo (player), 2023-11-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76808#p76808); Manletow's "conservative supporter" list: Purge, emergency heals, interrupts, Grounding, Chain Lightning on weakened groups ([2024-11-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109183#p109183)).

## Multi-target

Chain Lightning "on groups (especially weakened groups)" (Manletow); Earthquake's slow patch is "entirely irrelevant" (Atreidon) but its splash/aftershock hit clumps; Fire Nova at flags/graveyards (no source).

## Cooldowns and resources

- Grounding Totem: 20-s CD, breaks on the first CC it eats (1.18.0); "Grounding needs to be used strategically" (Akos1896).
- Ancestral Swiftness (if Ele/Resto): instant max-rank heal or a −25% instant CL.
- Racials: Tauren War Stomp "the best PvP racial" (Skegg) and "gigantic tauren leeway" (Springboards, disputed by Atreidon: "Twow has almost no melee leeway. 5 yards"); Troll Hex "decent for PvP" (Skegg) vs "terrible" (Grinched); Orc Blood Fury + Feral Spirit wolves "seem great for any spec" ([Gladeshadow (player), 2025-05-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130013#p130013)).
- Mana: Water Shield; Mana Spring at a held node; potions.
- PvP set bonuses (1.18.1): "+15% movespeed in wolfform that is tied to the boots" — [PvP 1.18.1 thread poster, 2026-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=24132) (from the staff post's discussion; exact set text not extracted).

## Role strategy

- Kiting: Earthbind + Frost Shock, Ghost Wolf; against melee "Trying to melee a shaman as a cat is a big mistake ... They are tanky, they can heal faster and more efficient than you, purge your heals and dish out insane bursts" — [Turboman (player), 2026-03-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171254#p171254).
- Interrupt priority: healers and arcane mages (Ataika).
- Objective play: hold nodes with Grounding/Tremor/Earthbind; Chain Heal support in AV ("Deep Resto is now viable (at least in AV) as you can be a proper Chain Heal master" — [Manletow (player), 2024-11-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108342#p108342), contradicted by amanagor in the same thread).

## Gear

Full T2.5 (Stormcaller) with stamina jewelry (Aluevera); T3 for "9k armor and a big hp pull" (amanagor); Kara10 reflect shield vs casters; Blood Guard boots; 1.18.1 PvP sets with resilience (values not in sources). No shaman PvP BiS list exists (gap; the 10-59 twink spreadsheet by Brisspvp — [2023-10-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=68018#p68018) — covers Enhancement twinks only).

## Common mistakes

1. Free-casting Lightning Bolts into a kick/silence class instead of shock range (Aluevera).
2. Missing Grounding on Arcane Rupture / big casts (Ataika).
3. Leaving Earth Shield off when melee arrives (Aluevera, Thelwald).
4. Spamming Purge into the 8-s CD; wasting Grounding (breaks on CC).
5. Outranging your own totems (30 yd) when playing far back (Aluevera's "Downsides").
6. Using Hex as a CC opener (5-min CD, 4 s, pushback still applies — Grinched).
7. Trying to burst from zero as pure Ele without Elemental Mastery/Fury planning (Bjorn88: "Electrify effectively means you start every combat at 50-66% of your power level" — obsolete but the burst-window logic remains).

### Matchups (cross-read `#pvp-general`)

- **Warrior** favoured (Frost Shock + Earthbind + Earth Shock every 5-6 s); **Protection warrior** is the one warrior spec claimed to counter shaman [[d:pvp-general#1067844135320559616]], [[d:pvp-general#1418626701457625119]].
- **Rogue** contested: shaman tools bully rogues, but an equally geared rogue bursts a 6k-armour/4k-HP shaman in a cheap shot + kidney if the trinket is down [[d:shaman#1401123087209992254]], [[d:pvp-general#1419603728960458762]].
- **Mage** losing/hard: Counterspell locks the shaman out of damage *and* healing since everything is Nature [[d:pvp-general#1081905593008259082]], [[d:shaman#1403451583622021192]].
- **Warlock** losing: Curse of Tongues hard-counters the kit; warlock and rogue are the two most cited counters [[d:pvp-general#1292034196495138889]], [[d:pvp-general#1138134954014933022]].
- **Hunter** contested; **priest** favoured (Purge, then burst); **druid** strong; **paladin** hard-countered by design at consensus but contested at the top end [[d:pvp-general#1082820346035576913]], [[d:pvp-general#1366083256025485352]], [[d:shaman#1341319733600976896]], [[d:pvp-general#1112115354303475752]], [[d:pvp-general#1371851334738907257]].

### Racials, engineering and gear

- **Tauren** War Stomp is the best PvP utility and combos with an Iron Grenade for a 4-s stun [[d:shaman#1188211554357887036]]; **Orc** Hardiness is the anti-paladin/anti-engineering pick [[d:shaman#1246590462454796299]]; **Troll** Berserking + Hex (castable moving and while Nature-locked) is called the best shaman PvP racial by some — race ranking stays **contested** [[d:shaman#1404506212363665439]].
- **Engineering is the only profession giving player power**; grenades fix the no-hard-CC problem (Gnomish/HFQ net), alongside Insignia, Rocket Helm, Magic Dust, Shard of Nightmare, Hyjal Pipe, Tidal Charm, Nifty Stopwatch; FAPs/LIPs decide fights [[d:shaman#1277660705008386109]], [[d:shaman#1280180094823825481]], [[d:shaman#1406779021345423465]].
- **Spell penetration** is high value vs nature-resist gear (~20 resistance -> +5.2% damage, ~75 SP equivalent) [[d:shaman#1282723201184432140]]. **8/8 T3's shock buff** is what made ele infamous pre-1.18.1 [[d:shaman#1359859058848432269]].

### Post-1.18.1 state

- **Resilience is observed to blunt burst**: "in full PvP gear you basically just take normal hits"; NS 75% x dampening 0.8 = **60%** damage [[d:shaman#1491924441113038908]], [[d:shaman#1491921578483646464]], [[d:shaman#1467127442731958463]].
- **Rockbiter -> Earthen Bulwark** was explicitly intended to stop non-Enhancement specs using passive PvP DR; players report it did not stop resto tankiness [[d:shaman#1485997307144048690]], [[d:shaman#1487726640682369034]].
- **Still contested and unresolved**: Ele standing ("top 2 pvp spec" vs "losing ~80% of 1v1s" vs "really meh, no pushback resistance") [[d:shaman#1373661275296632934]], [[d:shaman#1054026335502221322]], [[d:shaman#1454796130146586747]]; Resto PvP mostly negative with one positive 2023 claim plus the 13/17/21 spec [[d:shaman#920766236185493524]], [[d:shaman#1405903764476657758]], [[d:shaman#1155156739201699981]]; the Enh attrition minority is stronger here than the forum file's flat "weak" [[d:shaman#1204951203620986921]], [[d:pvp-general#1353324094954934314]]. No post-2026-03-20 re-tested build, rotation or stat priority exists, and shaman dampening/resilience numbers were never published [[d:shaman#1474075242195583087]], [[d:shaman#1477242197513601168]].
