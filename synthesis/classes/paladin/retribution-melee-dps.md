# Paladin — Retribution melee DPS (raids and dungeons)

Playbook for Retribution on Turtle WoW after Class Changes 2 (patch 1.17.2, live 2024-10/11) with the 1.18.0 (2025-08) and 1.18.1 (2026-03-20) adjustments. Two gearing philosophies coexist and the forum treats them as different specs: **AP Ret** (Strength/crit/Seal of Command, two-hander) and **Spell/hybrid Ret** ("spelladin": Seal of Righteousness, spell power gear, Consecration spam). Both are covered; every claim is cited and dated.

## Overview

- **Role**: two-handed melee DPS with raid utility (blessings, Cleanse, Hand of Freedom/Protection/Sacrifice, emergency off-tank and off-heals).
- **Community standing**: **contested**. Staff: "Yes! Paladins are seen in all three specs in raiding." — [Akalix (staff), 2025-02-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118876#p118876). Top-end players: "I'm BiS in a high tier raiding guild and usually end up top 5 in overall dps on a good night, Definitely viable." — [Gildark (player), 2025-03-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123742#p123742); "one of my guild ret paladins ends up top 5 dps among other wars and one top mage" — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2025-03-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122449#p122449). Against: "If you look at the rankings for k40 dps, aside from the one obvious outlier ret dps isn't even in the top 350 spots" — [Kord2998 (player), 2026-02-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169085#p169085); "AP ret can pull MAYBE 1750 DPS as of now on Patchwerk which is quite a lot behind of other classes, and on multi target fights (Majority of Kara40) it is dumpstered hard" — [Ellifta (player), 2026-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169165#p169165). Staff acknowledged the AP side: "Retribution paladins that leaned into attack power have been underperforming for the last few patches" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **Strengths**: respectable single-target damage, best-in-class utility ("Retpaladin offers respectable damage, emergency tank capabilities, dispell support and judgements for his raid ... Having the ability to transform into a healer on the spot" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-03-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122447#p122447)); threat is no longer an issue after CC2 ("Threat is finally fixed" — [Eulogy (player), 2024-11-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106958#p106958)) and Divine Shield drops threat ("he can drop threat with divine shield" — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2025-03-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122449#p122449)).
- **Weaknesses**: no cleave; long ramp-up (3 Zeal stacks + Holy Might ≈ 20 s, buffs last 30 s/20 s — [Kord2998 (player), 2026-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169188#p169188)); mana dependence on Seal/Judgement of Wisdom that punishes target swapping ("Ret paladin not only doesn't have cleave but it also can't afford to change target often because of needing wisdom to sustain mana, this goes double for AP ret" — [Ellifta (player), 2026-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169165#p169165)); buff-cap issues in raids (see Mistakes).
- **Content**: dungeons and MC–Naxx raids fine; Kara40 (cleave-heavy) is the weak spot per the 2026 threads above. Levelling: see `leveling.md`.

## Single-target rotation

Opener and buff management (consensus across the rotation threads and the macro authors):
1. Pre-cast blessings; Sanctity Aura (spell build) or Retribution Aura; Seal up before the first swing; `/startattack`.
2. **Judgement** as you arrive (Judgement of Wisdom for mana on long fights; Judgement of Command/Righteousness when mana is fine — sources use Wisdom "for sustain" and swap only when full).
3. **Crusader Strike** ×3 (every 6 s) to reach 3 Zeal, then **Holy Strike** once for Holy Might (20 s), then Crusader Strike until Holy Might has < 6 s left, Holy Strike again — the pattern encoded in the macros: "This will use CS until you get 3 stacks of Zeal then HS for buff and continue CSing until your buff is less than 6s duration" — [Gildark (player), 2025-06-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134690#p134690); RoidMacros form `/cast [mybuff:Holy_Might<6] Holy Strike; Crusader Strike` — [Elisleris (player), 2025-07-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139413#p139413); castsequence form `/castsequence reset=17 Holy Strike(Rank 8), Crusader Strike(Rank 5), Crusader Strike(Rank 5)` — [Rindexxx (player), 2025-07-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139772#p139772); the ExAutoCSHS addon (`/AutoCS openHS prioZeal exorcism`) — [excin (player), 2025-07-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139403#p139403). Some open with Holy Strike ("open with hs for buff, then spam CS for max Zeal, throw in judgement in the meantime" — [Vidnar (player), 2024-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106111#p106111)).
4. **Judgement** on cooldown, re-seal immediately after; watch the swing timer so the re-seal lands before the next white hit ("You need to watch a swing timer, for Judging/reapplying the Seal" — [Siltrim (player), 2024-12-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111583#p111583)).
5. **Exorcism** on cooldown vs Undead/Demons (Naxx, Scholo/Strat): "in Naxx you at least get to throw Exorcism into the mix" — [Eulogy (player), 2024-11-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106958#p106958).
6. **Hammer of Wrath** below 20% (no swing reset since 2025-04-01).
7. **Repentance** on bosses for the "repent" tick (30 s; 1.18.1 makes it scale with 8% AP) — no player-side priority given; macro users put `/cast [nocooldown:Repentance] Repentance` in the strike macro ([Elisleris (player), 2025-07-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139413#p139413)).
8. Free GCDs: Cleanse/utility — "Use your extra GCDs while judgement and crusader strike are on CD to assist your raid (eg: dispel)" — [Steakhouse (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106418#p106418).

Spell/hybrid variant (T2.5 era): Seal of Righteousness + Improved SoR, max-rank **Consecration spam**, +damage-on-hit items (jewelcrafter cloth bracers "with 4 fire proc on hit. Triggers unlimited mana"), Wrath of Cenarius "procs with consecration for 80% up time" — [Icedcream (player), 2025-07-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138117#p138117) (single source). "In K40, most spell rets wouldn't even touch [Holy Strike]" — [excin (player), 2025-10-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157278#p157278).

Which strike when: for Ret, "Crusader Strike is your go-to choice for damage ... you need to use Holy Strike purely for the strength buff" — [Aalena (player), 2024-12-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113215#p113215); the trade-off is explicit: "you need to check is it better to keep up the 20% str buff but losing some damage from crusader strike in favor of holy strike OR it's not worth it at all" — [Erhog (player), 2024-12-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113384#p113384). Low-level/dungeon exception: "Holy Strike party regeneration is too good to waste CDs on Crusader Strike" — [Kerenis (player), 2025-10-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157245#p157245).

## Multi-target / AoE

- **2–3 targets**: stay on one target for Wisdom; Judgement of Righteousness/Command on the secondary if it will live; Consecration only if mana is comfortable — "you dont use consecration cause you will run out of mana" — [Vidnar (player), 2024-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106083#p106083) (AP build).
- **4+ targets**: Consecration is the only AoE; 1.18.1 front-loaded its damage explicitly "to help with ... AoE damage capabilities of Retribution" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071). Spell build: max-rank Consecration on cooldown with Wisdom sustain ([Icedcream (player), 2025-07-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138117#p138117)). Lower ranks to save mana: "keybind lower rank spell like consecration. Learn your guilds clear speed and understand when to use max rank and when low rank" — [Snadileoboy (player), 2026-03-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169921#p169921).
- Do not rebuild Zeal on trash that dies fast: "Totally useless on trash packs, since everything dies too fast to ramp up Zeal" — [Kerenis (player), 2024-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106096#p106096); use Holy Strike for party mana/health instead.

## Role strategy

- Stand behind the target (no parry/dodge), keep the swing timer visible (SP_SwingTimer addon in the guide), keep both buffs up, judge on cooldown, and spend free GCDs on Cleanse/Freedom/BoP for the raid.
- Off-tank when asked: Righteous Fury on removes Vengeance's threat penalty — [Torta (staff), 2025-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124504#p124504).

## Common mistakes

- Going AP with hybrid gear or SP with an AP weapon ("hybrid needs different style weapon") — [Snadileoboy (player), 2026-03-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169921#p169921).
- Under hit cap / low weapon skill: glancing blows and misses were the diagnosed cause of "bottom of the damage done list" — [Scootiesana (player), 2026-03-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169915#p169915).
- Target-swapping off the Wisdom-judged mob and running dry — [Ellifta (player), 2026-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169165#p169165).
- Spamming Consecration as AP Ret — [Vidnar (player), 2024-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106166#p106166).
- Letting Holy Might / Zeal expire; relying on buff-icon macros at the raid buff cap — [excin (player), 2025-07-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142117#p142117).
- Judging right before a white swing so the seal is not back up for the hit — [Unangwata (player), 2020-10-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8305#p8305) (pre-CC2 but the seal/judgement mechanic is unchanged).
- Expecting Fiery Weapon or +damage items to proc Seal of Command — [Atreidon (player), 2026-01-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164504#p164504).
