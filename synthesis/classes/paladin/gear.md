# Paladin — Gear guide (by spec, role and level bracket)

Machine-readable version: `structured/classes/paladin/gear.yaml`. Sources: the Paladin forum (f41) BiS-request threads, the Itemization forum's relic list and 1.18.1 changelog, and the existing class playbooks' "Gear, enchants and consumables" sections.

**No Turtle-specific pre-raid or raid BiS list exists for any Paladin spec.** Every "is there a BiS list" thread ([t8738](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=8738), [t18785](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=18785), [t17662](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=17662), [t21851](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=21851), [t23183](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=23183), [t19282](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=19282), [t4568](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=4568)) is unanswered or answered with "ask Discord" / "use a vanilla guide". The one detailed pre-raid list found ([t393](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=393), Protection tank) is a 2019 repost of a generic vanilla guide, explicitly declared outdated by a player in 2025: "Its outdated. Tankpaladin works vastly different now and requires other gear as a result. Additionally turtlewow has added many new better fitting items for palatanks" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-07-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140261#p140261). It is kept below, with that caveat on every item, because it is the single most complete named-item list found for any Paladin spec.

## Gaps

- No Turtle-specific pre-raid gear list exists for Holy or Retribution; the only rich pre-raid list found (Protection tank) is a 2019 vanilla repost explicitly declared outdated in 2025.
- Retribution's t19669 thread named 5 melee items only by raw `database.turtlecraft.gg` item IDs (83440, 83462, 83463, 80674, 60550) with no item names in the post text — could not be resolved without a live database lookup, which this guide is barred from performing.
- "4HM" referenced in the Ret T2.5-vs-T3 stat debate (t18310) is an unexplained acronym, not resolved to an item name anywhere in the sources.
- "Zandalar Freethinker's" is named as part of the Ret raid kit with no item type or slot given.
- No leveling gear list (leveling-10-29 / leveling-30-49) exists for any Paladin spec; only Holy's leveling-50-59 dungeon list (from t14711) was found. Retribution and Protection leveling gear is entirely uncovered.
- No PvP gear list exists for Holy PvP, or for Retribution/Protection PvP beyond the single Fishbringer mention under Protection.
- Enchant lists exist only for Holy and Protection (from a single 2022 guide, last edited 2024-12-04); no enchant list exists for Retribution beyond a brief fragment noting the AP-vs-SP enchant choice per slot is explicitly unresolved in the sources.
- Several Libram relics from the staff relic-list post (Libram of Veracity, Libram of the Resolute, Libram of the Justicar, and the vanilla Libram of Fervor change) were not assigned to a specific spec/bracket above because their best-fit spec is ambiguous (general utility, PvP, or shared) — see [t8229](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=8229) directly for the full list.
