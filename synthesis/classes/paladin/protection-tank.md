# Paladin — Protection tank (dungeons and raids)

Playbook for a sword-and-board Protection Paladin on Turtle WoW, built from the Paladin forum (f=41), the staff class-change posts and the 1.18.1 notes. Everything is cited; where the sources disagree or predate a change, it is marked. Post-1.18.1 sources (after 2026-03-20) are thin: the forum extraction ends in early April 2026, so most play advice is **pre-1.18.1** or **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** and is checked against the official 1.18.1 changes below.

## Overview

- **Role**: main tank or off-tank with a one-hand weapon and shield, Righteous Fury on, threat from Holy damage (Holy Strike, Consecration, Holy Shield blocks, Seal/Judgement of Righteousness) rather than weapon damage.
- **Strengths (consensus)**: best multi-target threat of all tanks and the easiest dungeon tank; near-infinite mana with Seal/Judgement of Wisdom and Shield Specialization blocks; a real taunt (Hand of Reckoning) that is talented to almost never miss. "Paladins have the best multi target threat + their taunt has an extremely low chance to miss if talented for it + they are also the highest in dps among all tank specs" — [Cheruscan (player), 2025-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123148#p123148). "Prot paladin is already extremely buffed. They are the best dungeon tanks and they are perfectly fine to tank all contents (except for some raid bosses who really punish mana using tanks)" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123046#p123046). "Paladin tanking is the easiest of all tanks, in my opinion. Consecration is your bread and butter ability and does 80% of the work for you" — [Captain4k (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165936#p165936).
- **Weaknesses (consensus)**: no ranged pull (use engineering, a friend, or consumables — see Role strategy); no defensive stance and weaker single-target scaling into late raids; caster mobs are the one thing solo Prot struggles with ("prot only struggles against caster mobs" — [Zulnam (player), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135007#p135007)); mana-punishing raid bosses.
- **Content**: excels at leveling by AoE grinding from ~30, 5-man dungeons, MC/BWL-era raids and Tower of Karazhan (staff: "Protection Paladins are thriving in Tower of Karazhan, with strong threat generation, consistent mitigation, and reliable utility" — [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159)). High-end raid scaling was the recognised weak spot until 1.18.1: "Protection Paladins were extremely strong during leveling and early endgame but scaled poorly in high-end raids. These changes slightly reduce early power while significantly improving late-game scaling" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **Community standing**: **favored** for dungeons and mid raids (consensus above); **contested** for top-end raids in late 2025 — "A tank shaman in t2 gear out threats t3 pallys (in full naxx/some k40 bis threat gear) in both single target and aoe" — [Kord2998 (player), 2025-12-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162511#p162511); "Prot paladin threat is still bottom tier even with that set bonus" — [Escaton1 (player), 2025-12-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162493#p162493). Staff answer to that was the 1.18.1 scaling buffs (see below). Staff also state all three specs are raid-viable: "Paladins are seen in all three specs in raiding." — [Akalix (staff), 2025-02-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118876#p118876).

## Stat priority (60)

Two stat sets, swapped per fight (consensus among the experienced posters):

> "You build sets based on caps: A set that guarantees full avoidance (every attack is dodged parried or blocked). A set of plate gear and as much spellpower as possible to maximize threat. And you change accordingly to fight. Usually, trash and weak hitting boses you go threat set while unknown bosses or hard hitting ones you go avoidance." — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166168#p166168)

Survival priority (single detailed source): "Stam until you can survive the tier you are in ... Defense is nice but not mandatory just imagine 5 defense is 1% mitig, its almost as good as 1% dodge ... 18 agi is about 1% dodge and 1% crit in raid scenario (coze of kings) so i usualy go 15 agi>5def ... block chance until you reach crush cap with holy shield is nice to have". Threat priority: "Spell power, agi because of crit, strength (but its the worst stat you can get at the moment and its only Attack power)" — [Everran (player), 2025-08-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143606#p143606).

- **Spell power** is the threat stat: "Spellpower is most important for pally tank right?" — [Snoodydood (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165977#p165977), answered with the two-set rule above. Consecration, Holy Shield block damage, Holy Strike, Seal/Judgement of Righteousness all scale with SP — [Azcron (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153809#p153809). 1.18.1 raised Holy Strike's SP coefficient from 43% to 71% — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **Strength**: contested. "Strenght wont improve your threat in the slightest. All your aggro comes from holy damage" — [Kerenis (player), 2025-10-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153860#p153860) vs. "It increases your block damage, and block damage directly boosts the damage from Holy Shield ... every block with Holy Shield hits harder when your block value is higher" — [Azcron (player), 2025-10-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153912#p153912). Since 1.18.1 Holy Strike also has a weapon-damage component (40% at rank 8), which no forum source has yet evaluated.
- **Crit-immunity defense cap**: "Vanilla Crit Cap is 440 Defense Skill (420 if you are running Anticipation) -- Which is often not worth reaching when you consider what you have to sacrifice" — [Jendaille (player), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148436#p148436) `(single source; the poster hedges "if my memory serves"; general vanilla numbers, not a Turtle-specific test)`. Anticipation gives 7/14/20 Defense — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102969#p102969).
- **Crush cap**: staff intended Holy Shield (45% block) + a little block on gear to reach "crush immunity" — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102969#p102969). Pre-1.18.1 Righteous Strikes' Zeal block chance helped; **1.18.1 removed that** (Zealous Defence instead), so block chance must now come from Holy Shield, Redoubt procs and gear. No post-1.18.1 source gives the new numbers (gap).
- **Weapon**: fast one-hander. "as a tankpaladin faster weapons generate much much more mana with wisdom. So slow weapons are generally avoided" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-07-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=138797#p138797). "always use fast weapons (with +spelldam as a nice to have)! Weapon Damage is not important for threat, only Crusader Strike scales with weapon damage. But try to keep Zeal up! Faster hitting=more Mana!" — [Schlaechter (player), 2025-10-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153945#p153945). Pre-1.18.1 Holy Strike was "weapon agnostic" — [Crake (player), 2025-08-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147650#p147650) — **superseded** by 1.18.1's weapon-damage component (fast weapons still favoured for mana; no post-1.18.1 source re-evaluates).
- **"Adds X damage to melee attacks" items** trigger Judgement of Wisdom an extra time per swing: "if you want to spam max rank consecration +dmg triggering JoW an additional time is a must if you dont want to sip teas on CD" — [Cheruscan (player), 2025-09-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150774#p150774); item list in [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457): Repaired Electro-Lantern (65030), Storm Gauntlets (12632), Fiery Plate Gauntlets (12631), Flameskin Gloves (81267), Lavadrenched Chainmail (80704), Incendosaur Skin Pauldrons/Gloves/Boots (60572/60582/60568), Fiery Retributer (19968), Crimson Spellblade (81018), Blazefury Medallion (17111), Leggings of Fiery Temper (61505), Ring of Electrical Binding (61332). Note: items whose extra elemental damage is white "+X–Y Fire Damage" on the weapon line (Smoldering Brand, Thunderfury) do **not** count — [Atreidon (player), 2024-09-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101314#p101314).
- **Leveling**: Stamina/armor first, Intellect ("...of the Eagle") welcome, Strength unimportant — see `leveling.md` ([Azcron (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153775#p153775), [Schlaechter (player), 2025-10-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153945#p153945), [Isvya (player), 2023-09-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67010#p67010)).

## Single-target rotation (boss / one mob)

Before the pull: Righteous Fury on ("If you died, first thing is to reapply it"), aura on (Retribution Aura for threat, Devotion if squishy), blessings up with ≥1 min left, Blessing of Sanctuary or Kings on self, Holy Shield cast while running in — [Akos1896 (player), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166176#p166176), [Getplucked (player), 2023-07-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52649#p52649).

Opener (consensus of the three tanking write-ups):
1. Seal of Wisdom up; **Judgement of Wisdom** on the target as it arrives ("Prepare the seal and judge your main target with it (usually wisdom so you have a passive mana battery on the battlefield)") — [Akos1896 (player), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166176#p166176).
2. **Holy Strike** immediately ("target skull and press Holy Strike so you hit it as soon as you're in range") — [Getplucked (player), 2023-07-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52649#p52649).
3. Re-seal (Wisdom on bosses; Seal of Righteousness for extra threat on bosses — "Bosses JoW, can use SoR for more threat, but high-rank consecrate+hs is generally enough" — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457)).
4. Boss pull with Bulwark of the Righteous if DPS open hard: "Currently I end up using it every Time at Pull when I fight a boss because DPS can't hold their feet still" — [Melfrato1 (player), 2025-06-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135289#p135289) (single source; the design intent is snap threat OR a defensive — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2025-06-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135285#p135285)).

Priority list:
1. **Holy Shield** whenever it is off cooldown ("Ensure holy shield is always on"; "keep Holy Shield on cooldown") — consensus.
2. **Holy Strike** whenever the shared 6-s strike cooldown is ready and Zeal is at 3 stacks with > ~6 s left ("Just press holy strike every 6 seconds and consecration every 8 seconds while maintaining seal of wisdom" — [excin (player), 2025-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123491#p123491)).
3. **Crusader Strike** instead of Holy Strike only to build Zeal to 3 stacks and to refresh it when it is about to fall off ("If you're in prot, then just check if zeal is at 3 stacks, if it's not use CS, if it is check if it has less than 6 seconds left, in which case refresh it, otherwise just use HS" — [Crake (player), 2025-10-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157229#p157229); "Right now, I just use Crusader Strike to keep Zeal up, and otherwise Holy Strike" — [Tankmeister (player), 2025-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150949#p150949)). Zeal lasts 30 s. Short fights: "For shorter fights you won't need to bother with it - just spam holy strike" — [Erhog (player), 2024-12-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113384#p113384). **1.18.1** adds a reason to weave Crusader Strike before a big hit: it grants Zealous Defence (next blocked attack −6..30% damage for 10 s, 5/5 Righteous Strikes) — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071) (no player source on this yet).
4. **Judgement** on cooldown (every ~10 s) — re-judge Wisdom whenever it fell off or a new target is taken; re-seal after every judgement.
5. **Consecration** only if mana allows on single target; it is not needed to hold one mob ("Don't bother with Consecrate unless you're tanking 2+ mobs. I've seen people drop Consecrate for a single enemy, and that's where all their mana is going" — [Redmagejoe (player), 2022-08-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23920#p23920), pre-CC2 but repeated by [Getplucked (player), 2023-07-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52649#p52649)). With Judgement of Wisdom plus +damage items, "you can use Consecrate more often without worrying about draining your mana" (same post).
6. **Exorcism** on Undead/Demon bosses for damage only — its threat is −70% since 2024-12-20 — [Jamey (staff), 2024-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460).
7. **Hand of Reckoning** when a mob leaves you (10-s CD); Hand of Protection on the DPS as an emergency alternative — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457).

Downranking: "dont forget you can cast low level versions of spells" — [Terrydavis (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165976#p165976); "Rank1 holy strike is usually just fine to keep threat, saves alot of mana" — [Isvya (player), 2023-09-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67010#p67010) (pre-CC2 Holy Strike; still cited for low levels). With SP gear "you can eventually use different ranks of consec" — [Akos1896 (player), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166176#p166176).

## Multi-target / AoE

- **2 mobs**: mark skull; Holy Strike skull, Judgement of Righteousness/Wisdom + Seal auto-attack on the second, "Swap back and forth between autoattacking the two mobs, focusing more on skull"; no Consecration needed — [Getplucked (player), 2023-07-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52649#p52649).
- **3+ mobs**: Consecration is the AoE tool: "If there are 3+ mobs, consec makes wonders"; "consec anytime but mostly in case of 3+ mobs" — [Akos1896 (player), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166176#p166176); "I only ever used Consecrate with 3+ mobs" — [Getplucked (player), 2023-07-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52649#p52649). Keep Holy Shield up (every block is Holy damage + mana), Holy Strike the skull, tab through the rest to spread Judgement/Seal hits ("You can keep threat by tabbing between the mobs"). Big pulls when mana-rich: "Drop Highest Rank Consecrate, followed by R1" — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457). 1.18.1's front-loaded Consecration makes the first ticks the strongest ("On the first tick, Consecration deals 156% of the 1.18 base value") — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **AoE threshold** used by the sources: Consecration from 3 targets; 2 targets are handled by strikes + judgement + tabbing.
- **Solo AoE farming** (open world/Strat): Seal of Wisdom, body pull, Holy Shield, line-of-sight to bunch casters, Consecration, then Holy Light/Flash of Light on yourself under Concentration Aura, re-judge Wisdom when the judged mob dies — [Aeternusdoleo (player), 2023-06-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51774#p51774); Spiritual Focus + Concentration Aura so self-heals are not pushed back — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2023-06-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49725#p49725), [Bryonus (player), 2023-06-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50330#p50330). "Prot pally solo stratholme farm started with them doing only one boss and now they do like 4bosses" — [Snadileoboy (player), 2025-12-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164037#p164037).

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Bulwark of the Righteous** (5 min): either snap threat at a boss pull or hold it as a −30% damage cooldown for the dangerous phase; the 5-minute cooldown means most players "end up using it way less than I'd like" — [Glaive (player), 2025-06-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtocic.php?p=136673#p136673). Decide per fight; a tank main says "Paladins threat is way too good as is. We dont need a low cd shieldslam" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-06-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136693#p136693).
- **Divine Protection / Divine Shield**: Divine Shield now reduces your damage dealt by 60% instead of stopping attacks — [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159) — so a bubble no longer drops threat entirely but will cost threat; no tank-specific source on bubble use.
- **Hand of Protection** on a DPS who pulled — a standard emergency ([Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457)).
- **Mana**: Seal of Wisdom + Judgement of Wisdom on the main target, blocks (Shield Specialization 2% mana per block, 5-s ICD), Holy Strike's mana return, and +damage items. "With Seal of Wisdom and Judgement of Wisdom you never go out of mana so you just go without eating and drinking" — [Dissasster (player), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134989#p134989). Between pulls: "If only one enemy remains use it to regen mana by autoattack ... so next pull you have plenty of resource to go all out the first seconds" — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2025-01-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166451#p166451). Drink only when the group drinks; "Key as a tank is to keep threat using as few abilities as needed. That way you save mana for later and have a higher pace overall" — [Akos1896 (player), 2026-01-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166407#p166407). Emergency: a Human's Quel'dorei/High Elf racial mana is discussed for healers only; Prot relies on Wisdom.
- **Raid mana note**: some raid bosses "really punish mana using tanks" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123046#p123046) — bring mana consumables (Brilliant Mana Oil, mana potions/teas) for those.

## Role strategy

- **Marks**: keybind raid marks, put Skull on a healer/caster mob; DPS kill skull while you gather the rest — [Atreidon (player), 2026-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165826#p165826), [Reploidrocsa (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165909#p165909). Marks are hotkeyable in the keybind settings — [Atreidon (player), 2026-01-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166494#p166494).
- **Pulling** (no ranged pull): body pull, learn your body-pull range; or have a DPS pull to you through your Consecration; or consumables — Ez-Thro Dynamite, Explosive Rockets, Magic Candle (Elwynn kobolds, 1-min CD), Torch of Holy Flame from the Morbent Fel chain, engineering bombs — [Atreidon (player), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147010#p147010), [Jendaille (player), 2025-08-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147332#p147332), [Wolf of Rage (player), 2025-03-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123340#p123340). "If someone pulls for you, make sure you drop a consecration to intercept, and always hide behind a corner if the pull includes ranged mobs" — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165909#p165909). Holy Shock (21 Holy) works as a ranged pull since 1.18.0 restored its damage — [Atreidon (player), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147010#p147010).
- **Positioning**: turn mobs so DPS attack from behind ("mobs and players can't parry, dodge or block when attacked from behind") and so fleeing mobs run away from the pack — [SvenS2 (player), 2026-03-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171780#p171780).
- **Blessings**: Salvation on high-threat DPS (not on hunters who feign, not on low-threat classes); Kings on yourself, else Wisdom/Sanctuary by mana need — [Getplucked (player), 2023-07-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52649#p52649), [Akos1896 (player), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166176#p166176). Salvation is 25% since 2025-04-01 — [Torta (staff), 2025-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124504#p124504).
- **Threat meter** and don't over-spend: "In general, there's no point in pumping more threat on a mob if it's going to die before the DPS will pull threat" — [Getplucked (player), 2023-07-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52649#p52649).
- **Healer etiquette**: never pull with the healer out of range or low on mana ("Is your healer in range? Does your healer have a decent amount of mana? Do paladin buffs have at least 1 minute remaining?") — same post; "be mindful of time between pulls ... if you drink up between every pull, DPS can become restless" — [Akos1896 (player), 2026-01-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166407#p166407).
- **Off-tanking as Ret**: Vengeance's threat reduction is off under Righteous Fury — [Torta (staff), 2025-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124504#p124504); Ret "emergency tank capabilities" are cited as a Ret raid asset — [Atreidon (player), 2025-03-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122447#p122447).

## Common mistakes (what players criticise)

- Spamming Consecration on single targets and going OOM: "just mindlessly spamming consecrate and OOMing themselves" — [Getplucked (player), 2023-07-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52649#p52649).
- Tanking without a seal up / without Righteous Fury / without Holy Shield on cooldown — same post; [Akos1896 (player), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166176#p166176).
- Using Crusader Strike as a threat button; it is a Zeal maintainer (pre-1.18.1) — [Azcron (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153809#p153809); "I wouldn't recommend using it in a Prot build as it's a mana waste".
- Letting Zeal drop and rebuilding 3 stacks mid-fight — [Aalena (player), 2024-12-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113215#p113215).
- Pulling while the healer drinks / not letting the group recover — [Masklingt (player), 2026-01-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166405#p166405) (the other paladin in his group did this).
- Stacking Strength gear "because it's plate" instead of Stamina/SP (contested value, see stats).
- Not marking targets; not using Salvation on DPS.
