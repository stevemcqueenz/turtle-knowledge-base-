# Paladin — Holy healer (melee "Blessed Strikes" healer and caster variant)

Playbook for Holy on Turtle WoW after Class Changes 2 (1.17.2), the 1.18.0 Daybreak/Holy Shock rework (2025-07/08) and the 1.18.1 Daybreak nerf (live 2026-03-20). Holy on Turtle is a **melee-weaving healer**: Holy Strike heals 4 wounded allies within 10 yards, Crusader Strike resets Holy Shock, and Flash of Light / Holy Light fill the gaps. A pure ranged "Flash of Light spam" style still works and is what some raiders prefer; the forum treats the melee style as the stronger one in 2025–2026 (contested; both sides quoted).

## Overview

- **Role**: raid/dungeon healer in plate; tank healer by design ("Holy Paladin by design was meant as a healer excelling in single target, tank healing" — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102969#p102969)) that became a strong raid healer through Holy Strike.
- **Community standing**: **favored (2026)**, **contested (mid-2025)**. "Holy paladins are the best healers in the server at the moment by a big shot thanks to their melee healing ability ... >30% of my healing in Kara40 comes from melee healing" — [Ellifta (player, self-described server #1 K40 healer, R13), 2026-03-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170300#p170300); "Our melee holy paladins do insane amounts of healing. It's very viable, just new" — [Gildark (player), 2025-07-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140787#p140787). Against (mid-2025, before the 1.18.0 buffs): "out of the top 30 healers for Kara40, only two are holy paladins" and Flash of Light's 42% coefficient loses to Chain Heal — [Soymage (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139025#p139025); "holy paladin is gonna have to stick with naxx gear ... paladin is in a very bad place" — [Nrgsa (player), 2025-07-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140751#p140751). Staff acknowledged the pre-1.18.0 gap ("The melee-focused Holy Paladin playstyle has been underperforming in terms of raw output, and we are introducing buffs" — [Jamey (staff), 2024-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460)) and then the over-correction ("the raw power of Daybreak—especially when combined with the Tier 3.5 set bonus—was significantly too high" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)).
- **Strengths**: mana that never runs out (Seal of Wisdom melee, Illumination, Holy Strike's mana return: "It lets me keep my mana bar completely full" — [Beardozer (player), 2025-12-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161979#p161979)); half healing threat ("Paladin heals only deal half threat compared to any other healers" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139095#p139095)); one-GCD Cleanse; instant Holy Shock; blessings; plate.
- **Weaknesses**: melee mechanics (silences, stuns, cleaves) hit you; "Paladin is the single worst healer scaling with haste" and crit/mp5 are weak stats — [Atreidon (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139095#p139095), [Soymage (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139025#p139025).
- **Content**: dungeons, all raids incl. Kara40; strongest where the raid stacks and takes AoE (see the fight list in Role strategy).

## Stat priority (60)

- **Healing power** first ("No issues here. A great stat for everyone") — [Soymage (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139025#p139025); Holy Strike "scales with healing gear" — [Beardozer (player), 2025-12-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161979#p161979); Blessed Strikes "has absolutely crazy scaling with addheal. A large chunk of which comes from the weaponslot specifically" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139095#p139095).
- **Plate with item armor** because of Ironclad ("synchronizes with how your talents push you towards wearing plate" — [Ellifta (player), 2026-03-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170300#p170300)); only flat item armor counts — [Elisleris (player), 2025-06-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136662#p136662).
- **Spell crit**: applies Daybreak, procs Illumination, and Mending Light uses spell crit; Holy Shock crit from Divine Favor. Valued but "Crit has never been that good for healers ... Overhealing is a huge problem" — [Soymage (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139025#p139025).
- **Haste**: nearly useless (Flash of Light sits on the GCD) — [Soymage (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139025#p139025), [Atreidon (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139095#p139095). Zeal (Crusader Strike) gives casting speed.
- **Mp5 / Spirit**: low value; "Spirit is almost useless as holydin" — [Metaalverf (player), 2025-09-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151262#p151262); but "we have %mana continues regenerating while casting as an item stat, and paladin healing t2.5 also grants 15% mana regen while casting. Human spirit bonus also grants 5% regen as well ... At the end of the day though, you probably aren't ooming as a paladin either way" — [Crake (player), 2025-09-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151365#p151365).
- **Hit**: not needed for the heals (see above).
- **Race**: High Elf (mana racial) vs Human (spirit/weapon skill) — contested: "Holy - High Elf" ([Gildark (player), 2025-09-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151263#p151263)) vs "Human Best for all roles" ([Snadileoboy (player), 2025-09-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151269#p151269)); guide: High Elf for "PvE Caster" — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457).
- **Levelling**: Intellect over Stamina for a self-healing class ("Potentially Intellect grants you more health than Stamina does") and Spirit to skip drinking — [Unangwata (player), 2020-10-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8305#p8305) (old; Holy levelling is not recommended by later sources — see `leveling.md`).

## Single-target (tank) healing priority

Setup: Seal of Wisdom (mana on every swing/strike), Blessing of Light on the tank (also buffs Holy Shock), Concentration Aura when you are in melee and taking pushback, Retribution/Devotion otherwise (no source states an aura rule; Concentration is the guide's "Main Healing Aura 22+" — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457)).

1. **Judgement of Wisdom/Light on the boss on cooldown** — each judgement makes the next Holy Light 1 s faster (Holy Judgement 3/3): "judge bosses on CD to have a 1.5 sec Holy Light" — [Ellifta (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139090#p139090). Re-seal after.
2. **Holy Light (1.5 s after a judgement)** for big tank damage; Flash of Light otherwise ("Flash of Light: 428 - 494 (42%) ... It is the primary way of healing as a paladin" — [Soymage (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139115#p139115)). Example maths: Holy Light rank 9 with 1000 +healing, Healing Light 3/3 and the old Daybreak = 2.5/3.5×1000 + 1590×1.12×1.20 ≈ 2851 — [Aqdill (player), 2025-07-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139457#p139457) (formula illustration, pre-1.18.0 Daybreak).
3. **Holy Shock** whenever it is available and someone needs an instant heal; with Divine Favor 5/5 it crits 50% more often, applies Daybreak on crit, and can self-reset.
4. **Crusader Strike** when Holy Shock is on cooldown and you are in melee: it resets Holy Shock (Blessed Strikes) — "Timing Crusader Strike to refresh Holy Shock vs. using Holy Strike for healing/mana management feels meaningful" — [Tankmeister (player), 2025-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150949#p150949). Zeal stacks speed your casts.
5. **Holy Strike** on cooldown when 2+ people within 10 yd are wounded, or simply for mana ("I see HS as less of an AOE heal and more as a way to keep my mana topped off" — [Beardozer (player), 2025-12-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161979#p161979)).
6. **Lay on Hands** as the emergency ("it is possible to come back from 0 mana after a 'Lay on Hands'" — [Cosmico (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139100#p139100)); Hand of Sacrifice / Hand of Protection / Divine Shield as tank-saves and self-saves (no source gives thresholds — gap).
7. **Spell ranks**: sources do not give a downranking table for Holy; Flash of Light rank 60 (428–493, 180 mana) is the filler. Gap.

## Multi-target / raid healing

- **Holy Strike on cooldown whenever raid damage goes out and you are stacked with melee** — the whole 1.18-era Holy playbook: "Cast HS on CD and fill the rest in with FoL" — [Azcron (player), 2025-07-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140887#p140887); "on some bosses like Anomolous and Kruul it's over 55% of their healing" — [Ellifta (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139090#p139090).
- **Where to use it** (Ellifta's fight list, 2026-03-07, single expert source): MC — Majordomo, Lucifron after curses, Sulfuron, Magmadar when fires hurt, Shazzrah after curses ("doesn't incur spell lock btw"), Ragnaros phase 2, trash AoE; BWL — Razorgore after stomp, Vaelastrasz on CD, trash on CD, Broodlord after stomp, Firemaw non-stop, Flamegor enrage; AQ40 — every trash pack, Skeram arcane explosion, bug trio/Viscidus poison bolts, non-stop on Sartura, Ouro popcorn; Naxx — Anub knockback, Faerlina fire/poison, Maexxna spiders, Grobbulus spew/slime, Gluth start, non-stop on Thaddius, Loatheb, Sapphiron and KT; Kara40 — Gnarlmoon after blood moon, Ley Watcher arcane rain and add explosions, on CD on Anomalus and Medivh, Bishop shadow-bolt AoE, non-stop on the Knight, Kruul between the 4-s melee timer, Sanv add phase, Mephistroth, Rupturan phase 2 — [Ellifta (player), 2026-03-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170300#p170300).
- **Where NOT to melee** (Atreidon's list, 2025-08-11, single expert source): MC Gehennas, Garr adds, Shazzrah, Sulfuron (AoE stun), Majordomo (fire novas), Ragnaros (knockback on mana users); BWL Razorgore (melee CC), Broodlord (knockback), Chromaggus, Nefarian (fear); AQ Bug Trio, Sartura, Twins, C'Thun — "know the boss mechanics and only go in if you know for certain its an upside because while it is better to have the option to use blessed strikes, if you do it in the wrong fights you kill yourself AND others" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-08-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144592#p144592). Note the two lists disagree on Shazzrah, Majordomo, Sartura and Ragnaros (**contested**; Ellifta's is the later post and from a top parser; Atreidon's is the safer default for a bot).
- Dungeons: melee healing "is a gamble that can backfire spectacularly" where mobs shield-slam, silence or fear — [Atreidon (player), 2025-08-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144592#p144592); Scholomance tutors' AoE silence as the example — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-08-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144559#p144559).
- AoE threshold: Holy Strike whenever ≥2 nearby allies are wounded (it heals 4); otherwise single-target casts.

## Cooldowns and resources

- Mana: Seal of Wisdom + Holy Strike keep you full; use Illumination crits; Human/T2.5 regen-while-casting exists but is not needed; "Paladins only have Daybreak" as a burst tool — no long cooldown healing CD besides Lay on Hands and Divine Favor (now passive). Drink only with the group.
- Hand of Sacrifice: damage transferred no longer breaks CC — [Jamey (staff), 2025-08-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145249#p145249). Hand of Freedom 24-s CD.
- Divine Shield: −60% damage dealt but heals are unaffected (the "bubble-heal" is the PvP staple, see `holy-pvp.md`).

## Role strategy

- Position behind the boss in the melee clump when the fight allows (lists above); swap to range for the disallowed fights.
- Judge on cooldown for the fast Holy Light; keep Seal of Wisdom up; keep Blessing of Light on tanks.
- Cleanse: one GCD removes magic, poison and disease — "dispell of most type of debuffs from a single target in one gcd" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139095#p139095) (Purify does not cleanse curses — [Make Purify clean curses too t16515](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16515)).
- Support weapons: Nightfall / Dark Iron Sunderer in melee "for dps optimisations" at a healing cost — [Atreidon (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139095#p139095) (contested value).
- Blessings: Improved Blessings (Ret row 1) is where Improved Wisdom lives; assign with PallyPower (Turtle fork linked in the guide).

## Common mistakes

- Standing at range and only spamming Flash of Light in AoE fights: "Spamming FoL is not the way to heal as a paladin" — [Ellifta (player), 2025-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=139090#p139090).
- Meleeing on the wrong bosses (knockbacks, AoE stun/silence, fear) — [Atreidon (player), 2025-08-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144592#p144592).
- Forgetting to judge (loses the 1.5-s Holy Light) or letting Seal of Wisdom lapse (mana).
- Expecting Ironclad from Devotion Aura/enchants — only item armor counts — [Elisleris (player), 2025-06-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136662#p136662).
- Stacking haste or mp5 gear.
- Trying to heal or buff warlock demons — it does not work (player report above).
