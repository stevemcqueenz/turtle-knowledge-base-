# Paladin — Leveling guide (1–60)

Sources are the Paladin forum's guide thread (Unchallenged, updated 2024-12-04 for 1.17.2), 2025 levelling threads, and the staff class-change posts for what is trainable when. Two viable paths: **Protection** (AoE grinding, dungeons, hardcore-safe) and **Retribution** (questing). Holy levelling is not recommended by any post-CC2 source.

## Which spec

- **Consensus**: Ret for questing, Prot for grinding packs and dungeons. "If you want to mainly run around doing quests - ret. If you want to grind packs of mobs - prot." — [Noephix (player), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135039#p135039); "prot is a much better spec to level since it's more mana efficient than the other two specs ... you can easily fight 5 at a time" — [Zulnam (player), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135007#p135007); "Ret works pretty good but I've seen prot pallies lvl well with AoE grinding too" — [Thearturq (player), 2025-08-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144431#p144431); "technically prot is bis for leveling with shield spike/eagle gear and dmg reflect but thats is more advanced territory so i would go ret" — [Springboards (player), 2025-07-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140079#p140079).
- **When to go Prot**: "i think its best to wait until around lvl 30 and then respec into tanking its very weak early on!" — [Terrydavis (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165976#p165976); "Im leveling as holy/ret til 30, and respeccing as soon as I can get Holy Shield" — [Steakhouse (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106313#p106313); "I switched to Prot at lvl 30 and I have to say I really enjoyed it. Less waiting time for dungeons, for quests where you are asked to kill X mobs, just pull 3-5 of them" — [Dissasster (player), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134989#p134989). The guide's Prot orders start at 10 (Redoubt first) — [Unchallenged (player, guide writer), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457). **Respec points**: one respec around 30 (Holy Shield) if you levelled Ret/Holy first; a second at 60 for the raid role. Staff note that Prot was "extremely strong during leveling and early endgame" and 1.18.1 "slightly reduce[s] early power" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- Pallies "CAN'T tank until level 16 at the earliest. This is when you get Righteous Fury" — [Thefishyone (player), 2022-04-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17965#p17965); Consecration is baseline at 20 — [Torta (staff), 2022-07-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20251#p20251); "We already struggle on packs until level 20 when we get consecration" — [Catyngla (player), 2025-10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156127#p156127).

Is the 1.18.1 tree different? Talent positions are unchanged; Righteous Strikes changed effect (Zealous Defence), Crusader Strike rank 5 100% WD, Holy Strike ranks reworked (see below). No source has posted a post-1.18.1 levelling order.

## Skill training priority (guide + staff rank tables)

- **Seals**: Seal of Righteousness for weapons 3.4 speed or faster; Seal of Command (Ret talent at 30) "3.5 or Slower (ONLY USE/TRAIN Rank 1 To Conserve Mana till 60, Same Weapon DMG)"; Seal of Wisdom (ranks 18/28/38 — the level-18 and 28 ranks are Turtle additions [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102969#p102969)); Seal of Light at 28; **skip** Seal of Justice and Seal of the Crusader — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457).
- **Strikes**: Holy Strike rank 1 at 4 from the trainer (no quest), ranks at 12/20/28/36/44/52/60 (1.18.1 table: R1 25% WD + 11–13 … R8 40% WD + 129–146) — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071); Crusader Strike ranks 8/20/30/40/50 (35/50/65/80/100% WD after 1.18.1) — [Jamey (staff), 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108776#p108776), [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071). High Elf starting trainer Andalideth Suncaller teaches Crusader Strike rank 1 — [Torta (staff), 2025-03-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121678#p121678). One 2025 post says "get crusader strike (i think you need to kill hogger as a class quest)" — [Springboards (player), 2025-07-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140079#p140079) — **contradicted** by the staff post (trainer at 8); treat Hogger as a quest, not a requirement.
- **Hand of Reckoning** at 10 — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102969#p102969) (a player recalls "between lvl 10-20" — [Azcron (player), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147006#p147006)).
- Train always: Judgement, Divine Protection, Hammer of Justice, Purify, Lay on Hands, Holy Light, Flash of Light, Divine Shield, Cleanse, Hammer of Wrath, Warhorse/Charger, Consecration (20), Blessing of Sanctuary (Prot), Righteous Fury (16, Prot/dungeons), Hand of Protection/Freedom, Blessing of Wisdom/Might; auras: Devotion until 16, Retribution Aura from 16, Concentration Aura from 22 for healing; skip resistance auras except situationally; **skip on Hardcore**: Redemption and Divine Intervention — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457).
- Rank skipping: Seal of Command rank 1 only (guide); lower-rank Holy Strike/Consecration for mana while tanking ([Isvya (player), 2023-09-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67010#p67010), [Terrydavis (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165976#p165976)).

## Playstyle by bracket

- **1–30 (any spec)**: Seal of Righteousness; Blessing of Might/Wisdom; max-rank Holy Strike ("It's FREE Recovers Mana Cost + JoW"); Consecration on 2+ targets; finish with Judgement of Righteousness; re-seal — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457). "Judging too often can speed kill time, but also eats more mana" (same). A judgement at 10 does ~25 Holy damage; Holy Strike about the same; Crusader Strike about double — [Oldenwisp (player), 2025-10-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156080#p156080) (he argues the strikes are overtuned for levelling; others disagree — contested).
- **Crusader Strike vs Holy Strike while levelling** (Ret): "If you're able to keep your killing streak going, it's great to have 3 stacks of CS, then keep using HS. CS will do more damage than HS will but ... drains mana. Just do CS once in a while to keep it up. If you feel like you run out of mana during it, don't sit down and drink. Pop Seal of Wisdom and spend time on one mob to get your mana back" — [Aalena (player), 2025-08-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147231#p147231). The asker found Holy Strike alone faster overall because of zero downtime — [Xqsme (player), 2025-08-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147228#p147228). Dungeon DPS at low level: "most pallies only use Holy Strike and stay right behind mages and rogues in dps" — [Kerenis (player), 2025-10-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157245#p157245).
- **20+ Prot open world**: Blessing of Sanctuary, max-rank Holy Strike, Seal of Righteousness (or Wisdom/Light on 3+ mobs), auto-attack, Holy Shield on Redoubt procs ("Blocks will Regen Cost of HS"), Judgement of Righteousness to finish — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457).
- **30+ Prot dungeons**: Seal of Wisdom 100%, judge Wisdom, re-seal, highest-rank Consecration then rank 1, Holy Shield on procs/cooldown, auto until low; bosses: JoW, optionally SoR for threat — same post; full dungeon routine in `protection-tank.md`.
- **30+ Ret**: Blessing of Might/Wisdom, Seal of Command rank 1, max-rank Holy Strike, auto-attack, Judgement of Command between autos; "A harder Target can be Stunned/Judged for more damage/burst" — same post.
- **AoE grinding (Prot, ~40+)**: it is "an endgame farming thing" before you have BRD/Strat/Scholo gear per [Werefox (player), 2023-06-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49784#p49784) (contested: others level with 4-mob pulls of mobs 2–3 levels below — [Getplucked (player), 2023-06-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49816#p49816)). Routine: Spiritual Focus + Concentration Aura, Seal of Wisdom with a fast weapon (Jang'thraze from ZF), shield spike, Holy Shield, body/LoS pull to bunch mobs, Consecration, self-heal at ~50% HP, re-judge Wisdom whenever the judged mob dies, Seal of Light when health matters — [Bryonus (player), 2023-06-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=50330#p50330), [Aeternusdoleo (player), 2023-06-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51774#p51774), [Noephix (player), 2025-05-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130687#p130687). Farm spots named: WPL graveyard undead around 50 — [Dissasster (player), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134989#p134989); Stratholme at 60 (56 cadavers crit for ~300 without defense) — [Rindexxx (player), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148311#p148311). Consecration flags you for PvP if it hits enemy players — [Eithius16 (player), 2022-09-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24307#p24307).
- **Downtime**: Seal/Judgement of Wisdom instead of drinking; Blessing of Wisdom on yourself; tents outside cities for rested XP — [Springboards (player), 2025-07-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140080#p140080); Divine Shield/Hand of Protection + bandages to heal for free — [Unangwata (player), 2020-10-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8305#p8305).
- Use a swing timer and start-attack macros; the guide's macros: `/script if (not PlayerFrame.inCombat) then AttackTarget() end` + `/cast Crusader Strike`; seal-or-judge macros — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457); community wiki macro page (Consecration rank 1 out of combat, seal/judge toggles, Crusader Strike stack check): https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Paladin_Macros (community, not official).

## Stats and gear while levelling

- **Prot**: "Prot pally is really all about stamina and strength. Once you hit level 20 and pick up Seal of Wisdom, mana just isn't the issue ... And if you happen to come across gear with spell damage, that's worth considering too" — [Azcron (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153775#p153775); "Go Mail/later Plate with Stam ... '...of the Eagle' Greens with Stam/Int, take them. But Stamina and high Armor is all you need ... allways use fast weapons (with +spelldam as a nice to have)" — [Schlaechter (player), 2025-10-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153945#p153945); "Doesn't matter at all during leveling. Just take whatever piece of equipment you can find that provides a lot of stats and armor" — [Idontcareatall (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153815#p153815); "use eagle gear (stam int) over stam str for a higher mana pool and larger return from shield block talent" — [Isvya (player), 2023-09-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67010#p67010). Guide: Int/Stam (solo) or Stam/Int (dungeons). Strength for threat is **contested** (see `protection-tank.md`).
- **Ret**: Str/Stam/Agi (guide); "Buy a green or blue 2hander every 5-10 levels on the auction house. Buy 'bear' modifier gear with stamina and strength" and discount blues such as items 13041 and 2299 — [Springboards (player), 2025-07-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140080#p140080). Judge weapons by DPS/speed, not by white "+fire damage" lines — [Bigsmerf (player), 2024-09-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101311#p101311), [Atreidon (player), 2024-09-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101314#p101314).
- **Key upgrades**: "adds X damage to melee attacks" pieces for extra Judgement of Wisdom procs — Lavadrenched Chainmail (80704, Bazzalan in RFC, usable at 38, soloable), Incendosaur Skin Boots (60568, Hateforge Quarry, 51), Fiery Plate Gauntlets (12631, crafted, 54): "Wearing all three of them will effectively get you EIGHT chances to proc Judgement of Wisdom on every single melee swing" — [Getplucked (player), 2023-07-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52649#p52649); Gilneas City ring with "+3 Nature Damage" attack — [Aalena (player), 2025-08-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147231#p147231); "when struck" items such as item 17774 — [Schlaechter (player), 2025-10-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153945#p153945); Crescent Grove furbolg trinket (thorns), Naglering, Heart of the Scale for reactive damage — [Mekunekud (player), 2022-09-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24400#p24400) (old).
- **Ret two-handers by level** (2019 list, updated to the Turtle DB 2021-11-08; pre-CC2 but item-only): 18–20 Smite's Mighty Hammer (7230); 20–29 Verigan's Fist (6953); 29–34 Corpsemaker (6687); 34–44 Bonebiter (6830); 44–50 The Rockpounder (9413); 50 Lightforged Blade (20504, paladin quest); 51–58 Ice Barbed Spear (19106); 58–60 Arcanite Reaper (12784); 60 The Unstoppable Force (19323) — [Jcarrill0 (player), 2019-02-13 (edited 2021-11-08)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2079#p2079); plus item 6327 at 32 ("i usually dont replace it until 45+" — [Chlothar (player), 2019-05-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2991#p2991)) and Pendulum of Doom (9425, BoE) — [Chlothar (player), 2020-04-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5786#p5786).
- **Race**: Human for melee (weapon skill, Perception crit), High Elf for casters, Dwarf for PvP (guide); "Prot - Dwarf/Human, Ret - Human, Holy - High Elf" — [Gildark (player), 2025-09-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151263#p151263); contested ("Dorf for all purposes" — [Turboman (player), 2025-08-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144807#p144807)).

## Route

- Zones: only generic advice exists — Elwynn/Westfall or Loch Modan at 10, tents outside Stormwind — [Springboards (player), 2025-07-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140080#p140080); Wetlands/Duskwood at 20–30 as favourite questing zones — [Sylveria (player), 2025-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123029#p123029). **Gap**: no full zone route.
- Dungeons: Prot makes group finding instant ("Less waiting time for dungeons" — Dissasster); RFC solo at 38 for the chest; Gilneas City around 48 (a player keeps an XP-frozen 48 Prot for it — [Sylveria (player), 2025-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123029#p123029)); Hateforge Quarry at 54–55; ZF for Jang'thraze; RFD/Uldaman gear "and it will come naturally" — [Werefox (player), 2023-06-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49850#p49850).
- Professions: Engineering + Mining (bombs for pulls, target dummy, goggles, trinkets; "100% no debate" for tanks — [Terrydavis (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165976#p165976), [Thefishyone (player), 2022-04-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17965#p17965)); Blacksmithing for shield spikes/sharpening stones and Runed Copper → Green Iron → Steel Plate armor (43–45+), "Not as useful SC with access to AH, but on HC very useful"; Alchemy/Herbalism "Highly Recommended on HC" (swiftness, invisibility, Limited Invulnerability, Petrification potions) — [Unchallenged (player), 2022-10-18 (edited 2024-12-04)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27457#p27457); Herbalism for gold ("lasher farm") — [Snadileoboy (player), 2025-10-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154394#p154394).

## Hardcore differences

- "Prot Paladins is the safer choice for HC Players. 20-30 is pretty straightforward you'll do just as much damage as a ret paladin, only will have a shield to block to regen mana per block, and can make your dungeon groups pretty quick" — [Unchallenged (player), 2024-04-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92853#p92853).
- Artisan (crafted-only) + Hardcore: "Definitely go ret as Artisan. And remember that dead enemies deal no damage" (only one crafted shield exists) — [Noephix (player), 2025-05-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130687#p130687).
- Skip Redemption/Divine Intervention (guide); keep Divine Shield/Lay on Hands/Hand of Protection for escapes; Alchemy for invisibility/Limited Invulnerability potions; do not Consecrate near enemy players (PvP flag).
- A 2022 HC build (`https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin/5U-5IV3U1Z6-50C`) is pre-CC2 — [Mrkrissatan (player), 2022-02-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16846#p16846) (obsolete).
