# Warlock gear guide: pre-raid, raid and PvP by spec

Structured data: `structured/classes/warlock/gear.yaml`. See `synthesis/CONVENTIONS.md` for citation format.

**Read this first**: no source gives a complete slot-by-slot raid-tier BiS list for any Warlock spec. The closest
thing is a single player's flat, unordered Affliction gear list posted after 1.18.1 — the poster calls his own list
"dated." Forum threads that directly asked for a BiS list (`t6834` "Warlock Pre-BIS and BIS", `t20477` "preraid
gearing", `t15091` "Gear guide for new 60?", `t22994` "Warlock itemization") mostly got "there's pretty much no need"
or a dead Google-Sheets link back, not an answer. **Demonology and Destruction PvP have zero gear sources at all.**
Treat this document as a starting point.

## Affliction — ranged-dps

### Pre-raid-60

No slot-by-slot list exists; only route advice and one unnamed item-ID list. Agreement: **single**.

Route advice: "Join a guild that is looking for a warlock and you will get the bloodvine set free of charge if you
bring the mats. Do Sunken Temple once while leveling for the Soul Harvester, it's almost as good as pre-raid bis but
very easy to get. When you reach 60 spam Stormwind Vaults since you can enslave the fire/frost imp ... get your bis
trinket, wand and ring (reward from the tome quest for Alliance) as well some random caster gear. Do LBRS once as a
10-man raid to get the Eye of the Beast quest, start your MC/BWL/Ony attunement, finish UBRS for attunements and Eye
of the Beast ... Green Felcloth and 'of shadow wrath' armor is insanely good for how little it costs" —
[Grubi450 (player), 2023-05-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48162#p48162). "People sleep on +Shadow
Wrath Greens" — [Grizb37 (player), 2026-04-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174185#p174185). A 2023
Discord-sourced Turtle item list gives only numeric IDs with one-word source tags, **no item names**: 51755 (quest
reward), 65003 (crafted), 83468 (Karazhan Crypts), 60501 (Stormwind Vault), 60551 (Hateforge Quarry), 83465 (Karazhan
Crypts), 60622 (Stormwind Vault quest reward, Alliance only) —
[Mustachiocat (player), 2023-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45182#p45182) — not listed as
structured items here because neither the name nor the exact slot is given. "Pre-raid isn't all that important ...
besides hit cap, maybe some fire/arcane resist pots" —
[Bigsmerf (player), 2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141507#p141507). Bloodvine was nerfed
before August 2023 — [Pngu (player), 2023-08-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56028#p56028).

**Enchants** (community wiki Warlock page, not official — no post-level citations exist for these): gloves Shadow
Power; legs/head Hoodoo Hex or Arcanum of Focus; weapon and bracer Spell Power; cloak Subtlety; shoulder Zandalar
Signet of Mojo; rings/neck Arcane Emerald Gemstone; belt Dreamsteel Belt Buckle; chest/boots Enchanted Armor Kit. The
ZG head/leg enchant gives Warlocks 18 spell power + 10 stamina (Mages get 1% hit instead) —
[Maladron (player), 2023-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=62052#p62052).

**Consumables**: Flask of Supreme Power; fire/arcane protection potions per raid; Greater Holy Protection Potion
("300-500 hp buff vs paladins" in PvP) — [Erhog (player), 2023-11-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78940#p78940);
Healthstone (Major, shares tea/potion cooldown); Felstone (3% leech, default personal stone for Affliction);
Voidstone if threat is a problem; Potion of Quickness is explicitly called worthless for a spec whose spells are
instant or channelled — [Daftpunkerlol88 (player), 2024-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107603#p107603).

**Stat notes**: Affliction alternative sets exist for T1, T2, T2.5 (Doomcaller's alt), T3 (Plagueheart Attire) and
T3.5 (Nathrezim) with haste instead of crit —
[Dragunovi (staff), 2023-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71030#p71030). T2.5's 5-set summons a
Doomguard that "doesn't fight at all" and "exists 5-10 sec, deals 90 dmg," replacing your existing demon — players
wear 4/5 to avoid it — [Aeotald, 2026-01-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166386#p166386);
[Lavr, 2025-06-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137318#p137318). 1.18.1: T3 4-set Curse of Agony
+100% first 4 ticks; T3.5 3-set 25% Siphon Life return —
[Dragunovi (staff), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168272#p168272). 1.18.1 added a
Rogue/Warlock class-weapon quest chain starting in Timbermaw Hold, "similarly to Benediction/Anathema" — same post;
no source describes the reward.

## Demonology — ranged-dps

No Demonology tier set exists — requested by a player in the itemization thread —
[Krotux (player), 2024-01-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86261#p86261) — staff acknowledged only,
without committing to add one — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-01-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86264#p86264).
Use the SM/Ruin (crit/hit) gear from Destruction (Shadow) below. Black Book trinket is named for fighting/enslaving
greater demons (source does not give a citation beyond the playbook itself). Hourglass Sand removes Brood Affliction:
Bronze from your demon since 1.18.1 — [Dragunovi (staff), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168272#p168272).
Consumables: Infernal Stones, Demonic Figurines, Soul Shards for demon summoning; Healthstones for the player only
(demons cannot use player consumables).

## Gaps

- No source gives a complete slot-by-slot raid-tier BiS list for any Warlock spec — the closest is a single player's
  flat, unordered, self-described-as-"dated" Affliction list (5mman, post-1.18.1), plus tier-set bonus text with a
  handful of named pieces (Plagueheart Raiment/Handwraps, Nathrezim set) and item IDs with no names (Mustachiocat's
  2023 Discord-sourced list: items 51755, 65003, 83468, 60501, 60551, 83465, 60622).
- Demonology and Destruction PvP have zero gear sources at all — only pet/CC tactics.
- No Warlock-forum thread gives a leveling item list (`t15091` "Gear guide for new 60?" got zero replies);
  `leveling.md` states this explicitly as a gap and tells bots to fall back to general upgrade heuristics rather than
  an invented list.
- The 1.18.1 Rogue/Warlock class-weapon quest chain (Timbermaw Hold, described as similar to Benediction/Anathema)
  has no reward name or stats in any source — a genuine gap, not an oversight here.
- Almost no item above has a numeric item ID; where IDs exist (Mustachiocat's list) the corresponding item names were
  never posted.
- Enchant recommendations are drawn from the community wiki's Warlock page (authority: wiki, least reliable tier) via
  the citing playbook, not from a forum post — no post-level citations exist for them.
