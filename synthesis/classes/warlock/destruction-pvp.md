# Warlock — Destruction — PvP (Conflagrate / NF-Conflagrate)

The battleground and Blood Ring burst build: deep Destruction (Conflagrate, Shadowburn, Pyroclasm, Improved Soul Fire) with Affliction points for Curse of Exhaustion and/or Nightfall. The Soul Link builds are in `demonology-pvp.md`; Affliction PvP is a contested side-note in `affliction-ranged-dps.md`. This file also holds the class-wide PvP toolkit notes (fear, Death Coil, pets, races) because the sources discuss them here.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). PvP-relevant kit revisions: CC2 (live 2024-11-01: Conflagrate no longer needs/consumes Immolate; Curse of Exhaustion 30% at row 3 for every spec), CC3 (live 2025-08-15: Curse of Exhaustion 10% base + Improved 15/30% at row 5; Spellstone on-use; Fel Concentration affects Fear), 1.18.1 (live 2026-03-20: curse costs, Curse of Tongues, dynamic PvP dampening).

## Overview

- **Role**: glass-cannon burst caster with the class's CC (Fear, Howl of Terror, Death Coil, Succubus Seduction) and, since CC2, a 36-yard slow.
- **Strengths (sources)**: "nf/conflag destro lock has some of the highest burst in the game" [Springboards (player), 2025-05-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129967#p129967); "adding CoE to destro changed warlock to an absolute god mode ... coe vs warrior pre charge absolutely destroys them; shamans can literally do nothing in 1v1; even rogues aren't a problem anymore since you can trinket stun into coil+coe+dot" [Trinaxyo ("Zergursoul") (player), 2024-12-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114434#p114434); "Warlock is insane, especially destro right now. It just has very high skill cap" [Rektoshrekto123 (player), 2024-12-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114503#p114503); Soul Fire "already oneshots even geared players if it crits" [amanagor (player), 2025-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115752#p115752); Pyroclasm stun on Conflagrate "every 10 sec if u lucky" [Majestik51 (player), 2023-11-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78898#p78898).
- **Weaknesses (sources)**: "Glascannon at its best ... ## Official kit — PvP-relevant

- CC2: Conflagrate no longer consumes Immolate, 1-s GCD (Dragunovi 2024-10-12) — "now every autistic chimp is able to conflagrate its target" [Ataika (player), 2025-04-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127315#p127315); "The weird thing in new patch is Congflag no more consumes Immo., which basiclly means you lose the Immo in PVP ,or you lose Charm" [Sharders (player), 2024-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108596#p108596) (Immolate ticks break Seduction). Curse of Exhaustion 30% at row 3, Improved Debilitating Curses +10/20% (so 50%). Soul Fire ranks from 30.
- CC3: Curse of Exhaustion 10% base, Improved +15/30% at row 5 ("too easily accessible by other specializations which provided them with oppressive levels of control. We're now making its full power only accessible to Affliction warlocks" [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159)) — Akarui computed the new maximum as 40% [Akarui (player), 2025-07-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142150#p142150); Spellstone became an on-use magic dispel + absorb (level × 12) sharing the Healthstone cooldown; Fel Concentration 2 pts also prevents pushback on Fear/Howl; Power Overwhelming breaks pet CC; Soul Fire 114% SP.
- 1.18.1: Curse of Exhaustion 190 mana at 60 (from 110); Curse of Tongues R1 30% / R2 240 mana; Curse of Recklessness 1-s GCD "for the fear-juggling technique"; Felguard Axe Toss 2 s, Legion Strike −25% healing; Soul Link 20%; Enslave −50% healing; Searing Pain 2.0-s cast / 57% SP / +20% base; **PvP dampening** "will now have dynamic reductions of Magical and Physical damage based on your class / spec instead of a flat number" [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168) (per-spec values not published in the extract — gap).
- Long-running bug: Felhunter **Spell Lock** silence "never lasts 3 seconds as tooltip says, it looks like 1 second duration max" [Ataika (player), 2023-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78130#p78130); still reported 2025-09-26 [Forumdweller (player)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153349#p153349) with bug-report IDs 6299/1556/1236/13512; one player thought it "stealth fixed" in May 2025 [Forumdweller (player), 2025-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132118#p132118). Contested/unresolved.
- Other bugs used against you: Dampen Magic halving DoT damage (bug 5631, [Paybacksz (player), 2024-03-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91420#p91420)); demons stop chasing stealthed targets by design ("Turtle wow changed this bug for balance purposes" [Ataika (player), 2024-01-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84191#p84191)); Paranoia not detecting stealth [Lilith23 (player), 2024-02-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87634#p87634).

## Stat priority

- Stamina first ("PvP is not PvE, survivability is better than raw damage. So everyone stack on stamina until they reach some comfortable amount to not die instantly" [Xudo (player), 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108896#p108896)), then Shadow/Fire spell damage ("of shadow wrath" greens are cheap: "Chest with +23 shadow spell damage costs 3g" — Xudo), crit for Soul Fire/Conflagrate one-shots; hit is less important than in PvE (level-60 targets). Vampirism items/Felstone for sustain (wiki list; "Now i see these 100 heals on mhy 3k crits" — Ataika on Felstone, 2025-04-18, i.e. it is small).

## Opener and priority (1v1 / small-scale)

Sources: Tbcb (Blood Ring), Twospooky, Trinaxyo, Delalama (vs Paladin), Springboards (vs BM hunter), Vrankz.

**Opener**: 1) Succubus Lesser Invisibility → Seduction on the target or the healer ("You start invis, getting a spell off. Fear, Immo, Soulfire probably into an instant cast" — Tbcb; "Seduce 1st target, fear 2nd target, nuke 3rd target" [Twospooky (player), 2024-12-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111151#p111151)). 2) Curse of Exhaustion (if talented) or Curse of Tongues on casters / Curse of Agony on a target that will not be dispelled. 3) Soul Fire (hard-cast on a seduced/feared target — "Improved Soul Fire ... After seduction as opener"). 4) Immolate → Conflagrate → Shadowburn.

**Priority**:
1. Target in Conflagrate range with Immolate up and Conflagrate ready → Conflagrate (Pyroclasm stun chance).
2. Target below ~500 health (Shadowburn rank 6 threshold in Ulukay's macro) or fleeing → Shadowburn.
3. Nightfall proc → Shadow Bolt (NF build).
4. Melee on you: Death Coil (3-min CD; "Death coil if they deal 2000 damage" — Twospooky) → back away → Fear (Fel Concentration helps pushback; against WotF/Fear Ward expect failure) → Curse of Exhaustion (talented) → re-establish range. "Death Coil, walk the opposite way with melee snares on you, they try to chase but slowed by 50% too so they can't" (Halfgeek9, pre-CC3 numbers).
5. Immolate missing → Immolate (breaks Seduction on that target — only on your kill target).
6. Soul Fire ready and target controlled → Soul Fire.
7. Filler: Searing Pain (1.5 s with Bane) — after 1.18.1 it "benefit[s] from haste effects" and hits ~20% harder; still resisted by pushback — "you cannot even cast searing pains its just better to use souldrain" (Vrankz, pre-1.18.1, contested).
8. Healthstone / Spellstone (shared CD; Spellstone dispels magic on you and absorbs level × 12), Holy Protection potion vs Paladins (Erhog), engineering grenades to land casts ("if you do not tryhard with grenades/absorbs/potions" [Ataika (player), 2024-12-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111182#p111182)).

**Match-ups**: Paladin — "use felhunter to eat HoJ then use felhunter silence to lock them out of holy casts. Once HoJ and Repentance are on CD, they're defenseless against fear" [Delalama (player), 2023-10-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74709#p74709) (Spell Lock silence is bugged — see above); BM Hunter — kill the pet first (Springboards); Priest — Felhunter Devour Magic on autocast "can singlehandedly make you win ... perma dispel his renew and bubble" [Forumdweller (player), 2025-05-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130077#p130077); Rogue — "you have to kill the pet basically" logic reversed: "pray that some lame rogue isn't going to cheap shot kidney thistle tea evis sapper charge you when you have felhunter out" [Forumdweller (player), 2025-05-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129896#p129896); Undead — WotF removes Fear and Seduction ("leaving only 2 minute control from deth coil" [Twinking (player), 2024-02-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87488#p87488)).

## Battlegrounds

- "Charge on the bridge -> Press my buttons -> Win/Lose" in Blood Ring (Tbcb); in BGs "you get focused by war/paladin ... and you die within 2 hits" [Konstantin19 (player), 2024-12-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111178#p111178). Stay behind the line, seduce/fear the diver, Conflagrate/Shadowburn low targets, Curse of Tongues healers (1.18.1 nerfed rank 1 to 30% but rank 2 keeps its value at 240 mana).

## Common mistakes

- Hard-casting Fear on a melee target ("it takes 6 seconds to cast it against melees" [Forumdweller (player), 2025-04-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127471#p127471)); use Death Coil/Seduce first, Howl of Terror only with Fel Concentration.
- Immolate on a seduced target (breaks Seduction).
- Using the Dreadsteed in combat zones (interruptible, silence-able: "you can't cast like death coil in shadow school spell, this makes you a punchbag" [Sharders (player), 2024-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108612#p108612)).
- Running out of shards for Shadowburn/Soul Fire/pet ("there are times you can die simply because you don't have soul shards" — Forumdweller 2025-05-06).
- Wrong pet for the match-up ("their problem as always since classic, is having the wrong pet out vs the class you are fighting" [Halfgeek9 (player), 2025-05-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130398#p130398)); Fel Domination is a 5-min Demonology talent, not baseline.
- Spamming Curse of Exhaustion after 1.18.1 without watching mana (190 per cast; daleking0518's post-release complaint).
- Fighting Undead with Fear/Seduce as your only plan; assuming Spell Lock silences.
