# Warlock — Leveling guide (1–60)

Sources are the Warlock forum's leveling threads ("Warlock leveling tips" t16295, "Warlock affliction leveling mini guide (PvE)" t23778, "Get Sick or Get Demons" t15119, "Warlock best race" t19168, "Macros for leveling" t13648, "Demonology leveling/raid spec" t18544, "Why Alliance lock is the least played class" t11525), the Hardcore Lounge profession thread ("First HC, profession advice" t4856), and the CC2 staff post (Dragunovi, 2024-10-12, `t15157` p=102973) for baseline spell numbers. All dates UTC; eras as in the other Warlock files: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20).

No Turtle-specific leveling guide thread predates CC2 usefully: the oldest detailed build post in the forum (`t1188`, 2020, "Drain Soul Linked Spirit 15/31/5") uses talents removed or reworked by CC2 (Improved Healthstone, Improved Imp/Voidwalker/Succubus, Improved Enslave Demon, old Demonic Sacrifice) — **obsolete, not used below**. ## Which spec

**Consensus: Affliction is the default leveling spec; Demonology is the safer/tankier alternative, especially for Hardcore; Destruction is not recommended.**

- "the main difference between Affliction and Demonology for leveling is that one is good and the other is bad. Affliction gives you a lot of tools for leveling that will make your life easier. In contrast demonology gives you very little" — [Zulnam (player), 2024-10-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102810#p102810).
- "affliction is definitely better for leveling if you play it right. Demo is better for soloing elites and the better choice for hardcore as it is more survivable, being able to quick summon your demon is a life saver ... With affliction you can multi dot and drain tank a mob yourself while your void is dealing with another" — [Eversongwoods (player), 2024-10-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102794#p102794).
- "Leveling build depending on gear available for you. Affli is faster with decent items (alt or you have friends to backup or smth) while demo is safe and solid way to leveling even without any items. Destro is the worst." — [Erhog (player), 2025-03-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121852#p121852).
- "The general rule is Affli/Demo mixes are best for leveling ... The best vanilla spec to level for warlock is called 'Drain Tanking' and it assumes going deep into Affli ... With the new changes and faster ticks from Affli I imagine drain tanking would be even stronger now for leveling" — [Zulnam (player), 2025-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118801#p118801) (build link below).
- Contested detail on threat talents: "Personally I don't think you need Nether Studies. This is a new talent and lock had no issues with threat on their VW even before this" — [Zulnam (player), 2025-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118801#p118801) vs "Aim for nether studies ... This talent is important because it allows your void to keep agro pretty well" — [Ataika (player), 2024-12-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114546#p114546).
- Demonology's endgame value is separately contested (raid/PvP viability) — see `demonology-ranged-dps.md` and `demonology-pvp.md`; while leveling, sources treat it only as a survivability/tanky pick, not a damage pick.
- Bots: default to Affliction; switch to (or blend in) Demonology for Hardcore or when soloing elites.

## Skill training priority

- **Drain Soul** was reworked in CC2 into the class's core leveling/soloing tool: "Drains the soul of the target, causing X Shadow damage over 6 sec ... dealt slowly at first, and builds up ... If the target dies while being drained ... the caster gains a Soul Shard." Damage: R1 (10) 110, R2 (24) 280, R3 (38) 460, R4 (52) 760, R5 (60, new rank) 950, each tick 16.67% SP scaling. Mana: R1 55→90, R2 125→235, R3 210→405, R4 290→540, R5 655 — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102973#p102973). Train every rank; it is a primary rotation piece in both leveling guides above ("drain soul/drain life/dark harvest on tougher mobs" — Nuninho32/chappelles).
- **Soul Fire**: gained two new early ranks and shifted level requirements — R1 (30) 175 mana / 453–571 dmg, R2 (38) 260 mana / 544–685, R3 (46, was 48) 305 mana / 623–784, R4 (54, was 56) 335 mana / 703–883; scales 125% of Spell Power; cooldown cut from 60 s to 30 s — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102973#p102973). Train current ranks (the old rank levels moved).
- **Corruption**: baseline cast time cut from 2 s to 1.5 s (and further to instant at 5/5 Improved Corruption) — same source. Train Improved Corruption first per both leveling talent orders above.
- **Curse of Weakness**: reworked to a single rank, −15% attack speed instead of a flat damage reduction — same source; do not waste gold on multiple ranks (only one exists).
- **Searing Pain**: lost its threat-generation penalty in CC2 ("compensated by the new Wrathstone") and later (1.18.1) had its cast time raised to 2.0 s / 57% SP scaling / +20% base damage (see `synthesis/1.18.1-master-changelog.md` and `destruction-fire-ranged-dps.md`); no leveling source names it as a core leveling spell — used mainly by Fire/Destruction-leaning levelers.
- **Wand**: "up to lvl 40, your best damaging spell is wand. You don't need to spend mana on shadowbolts if your wand has better DPS and don't suffer pushback from mobs." — [Xudo (player), 2024-10-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102809#p102809). Train and upgrade it; the affliction leveling guide explicitly slots "wand" into the kill sequence for essential-spell newcomers — [funeh (player), 2025-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118779#p118779).
- **Shadow Bolt**: skippable while leveling Affliction per Eversongwoods above ("I wouldnt bother with nightfall or ever cast a shadowbolt while leveling, could save some gold and not train it"), but used as a Nightfall-proc filler in the primary 1.18.1-announced build and by at least one player at 32 ("I usually start my rotation with agony, immolation, corruption, wand, sometimes shadowbolt" — [Snipesta (player), 2025-02-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119257#p119257)). **Contested** whether to train it early.
- **Drain Soul rank choice**: "I've just been using rank 1 DS because of the mana difference compared to rank 2 ... I also have warmode enabled so, when im versing the opposite faction I always use rank 2 because it does a shitload of damage." — [Snipesta (player), 2025-02-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119257#p119257) — rank-down for mana efficiency in pure PvE, keep the top rank trained for PvP/warmode.
- **Voidwalker abilities**: "The most important vw ability is torment, make sure to get the new books when available and have it right-clicked so he auto uses it." — [Eversongwoods (player), 2025-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118780#p118780). Voidwalker threat scaling is stepped: "the voidwalker threat ability upgrades every ten levels (10, 20, etc) so it might be decent at holding threat when it gets a new rank and terrible at levels 17-19, 27-29, etc" — [chappelles/Nuninho32, 2026-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166748#p166748) (compared to Hunter pet Growl by the same author). No source gives a full Voidwalker/Imp/Succubus ability list with levels — **gap**.

## Leveling playstyle

- **Pulling / mobs at once**: "Warlocks can relatively easily 'juggle' 2-3 mobs at a time; applying DoTs on all mobs, fearing one mob, tanking the second, pet handling the third. Keep in mind that with the changes to fel concentration, AoE fearing with howl of terror would be much easier." — [chappelles/Nuninho32, 2026-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166748#p166748). Demonic Sacrifice build pulls similarly: "I can easily pull 2 or 3 mobs at a time, dot them all up, tank all the damage while wanding them down, and then go do it again and be full hp by the time I reach the new mobs" — [Browntown22 (player), 2025-03-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121816#p121816).
- **Kill rotation (Affliction)**: "Corruption → curse of agony → drain soul/drain life/dark harvest (on tougher mobs) → shadow bolt whenever you get a nightfall proc." — [chappelles/Nuninho32, 2026-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166748#p166748). A tested variant at level 32: "agony, immolation, corruption, wand, sometimes shadowbolt but mostly wand then end it with drain soul" — [Snipesta (player), 2025-02-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119257#p119257). A newcomer's loop: "Agony, corruption, inmolation and then either wand or shadowbolt" — [funeh (player), 2025-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118779#p118779).
- **Drain tanking**: a named vanilla-derived Affliction technique — "The best vanilla spec to level for warlock is called 'Drain Tanking' and it assumes going deep into Affli. ... With the new changes and faster ticks from Affli I imagine drain tanking would be even stronger now for leveling." — [Zulnam (player), 2025-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118801#p118801). No source gives its exact talent list on the current tree (the linked PDF pre-dates CC2/CC3 and cannot be verified from the extract) — **gap**.
- **Demonic Sacrifice tanking loop (Demonology, contested niche)**: see build section above; described as letting you "just wand them down" while immune to death from small pulls, at the cost of pet DPS.
- **Downtime**: no dedicated Warlock-forum post on mana management while leveling beyond the talent notes above (Fel Concentration for uninterrupted drains, Improved Life Tap for mana-from-health efficiency, Improved Drains for extra Drain Mana return). Life Tap is implied as the default filler ("used ... instead of just Life Tap" is how the removed Dark Pact community-feedback thread frames the CC2-era mana loop — see `demonology-*` files) but no leveling-specific quote gives explicit HP/mana thresholds — **gap**.
- **Macros**: no Turtle-specific one-button leveling macro was produced in the sources; the thread that asked was answered only with links to Nostalrius-forum vanilla macros and a suggestion to use the "roid macros" addon: "1) pet attack 2) dots 3) siphon life. if u got roid macros addon, is not that hard to put them all together in 1 cast." — [Majestik51 (player), 2024-05-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94513#p94513). Treat any specific macro syntax as **not from turtlecraft.gg**.

## Stat priority while leveling

- "stam > int > shadow or spell power. Spirit isn't bad at low levels, under 20." — [Eversongwoods (player), 2025-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118806#p118806).
- On trading Spirit for Stamina: "trading spirit for stamina is not really viable if you have low attributes overall. Also I suspect that this talent works only for *base* stats, not for improved with gear." — [Xudo (player), 2024-10-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102809#p102809) (referring to the Demonic Embrace-style Spirit→Stamina conversion talent).
- Affliction's kit is argued to matter more than raw stats at low level: "reduce enemy chance to resist your spells, refund mana after kill, slow curse (almost every elite can be killed by kiting)" — same Xudo post — and "up to lvl 40, your best damaging spell is wand ... don't suffer pushback from mobs", so hit/pushback resistance on gear is not chased as hard as at 60.
- No source gives numeric hit-cap or resist-cap targets for leveling (contrast the 60 stat-priority sections in the role playbooks); this is a **gap**.

## Professions

No profession advice exists in the Warlock forum itself; the only sourced guidance is from the Hardcore Lounge, but its reasoning (survivability, soloing) generalizes to any Warlock who wants a self-sufficient leveling profession pair:

- "there are a lot of people that fall into think either alch/herb or engi/mining are the ideal HC professions ... As a warlock, I chose alchemy. This is because they don't have sprint or other great escape mechanisms, so a swiftness potion is always appreciated. Troll blood elixirs when combined with demon armor provides a bunch of health regeneration ... Engineering bombs would take time away from casting, so they wouldn't be very useful. Target dummies are ok, but warlocks already have pets to help manage agro as needed." Alchemy is also self-sufficient for a solo player, unlike Enchanting (needs a rod, and Uldaman for high-level recipes) or Engineering (needs leather from Leatherworkers/Skinners) — [Gladeshadow (player), 2022-11-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29282#p29282).
- Correction/contested: "Troll's blood does not stack with Demon Armor. Demon Armor is better and whether you have a troll's blood on you or not is irrelevant ... The only really good utility pot that the warlock needs from alchemy (imo) is swiftness pot. You can get 2-3 stacks of those in your teens and just keep them and use them when needed, not all the time. They will last you till 60." — [Shamma (player), 2022-11-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29285#p29285), disputed back by Gladeshadow citing (non-Turtle) Wowhead comments — [Gladeshadow (player), 2022-11-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29340#p29340) — **contested, unresolved in the sources**.
- Trading for potions instead of leveling Alchemy yourself is reported impractical while solo: "it becomes increasingly improbable as you level up ... When I was in the 50 range, I knew 2 alchemists in my trading range ... never traded." — [Gladeshadow (player), 2022-11-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29283#p29283).
- This is a general Hardcore-oriented, single-thread recommendation, not Warlock-forum consensus; no source discusses Tailoring/Enchanting, Herbalism/Alchemy tradeskill synergy with Warlock bag space, or profession choice for non-Hardcore leveling specifically — partial **gap**.
