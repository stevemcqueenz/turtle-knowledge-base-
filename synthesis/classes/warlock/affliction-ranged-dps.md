# Warlock — Affliction — Ranged DPS (raid / dungeon)

Covers the post-Class-Changes-2 "Affliction Drain" build (Dark Harvest capstone, Malediction, Soul Siphon, Rapid Deterioration). Pre-CC2 Affliction (Dark Pact, Amplify Curse, Improved Drain Soul) no longer exists on Turtle; statements about it are marked obsolete. PvP use of Affliction is treated in a short section here because the sources do not treat it as a distinct build.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Within pre-1.18.1 the Warlock kit changed several times: Class Changes 2 ("CC2", 1.17.2, announced 2024-10-12, live 2024-11-01), the 2024-11-20 and 2024-12-20 revisions, the 2025-03-30 round, and Class Changes 3 ("CC3", 1.18.0, announced 2025-07-25, live 2025-08-15). Every recommendation below says which kit it was written for.

## Overview

- **Role**: ranged DPS built on damage-over-time upkeep plus channelled drains (Drain Soul, Dark Harvest), with Nightfall-proc Shadow Bolts. Curses for the raid (Curse of Shadow / Curse of the Elements) are still expected from the Warlock; Malediction lets Curse of Agony coexist with them.
- **Strengths (sources)**: the easiest Warlock spec to gear — "gearing for it is much easier thanks to the 10% hit from talents. All you need is 5-6% hit and as much haste as you can get and it performs really well" [Spritzbube (player), 2026-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169207#p169207); the highest sustained single-target of the three specs in early-2026 dummy tests: "lacking 3% hit to being hit capped, i am pulling 450 DPS easy as a Dark harvest affliction warlock on elite lvl 63 dummy ... SM/RUIN ... 250 DPS" [Guiltyhaomarush (player), 2026-02-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168196#p168196); does not depend on crit or on a pet; Nightfall Shadow Bolts are "guaranteed to land" since 2024-12-20 [Jamey (staff), 2024-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460).
- **Weaknesses (sources)**: trash — "Affliction Warlock's main pain point" per staff, which is why Malediction now auto-applies Curse of Agony when you cast a support curse [Torta (staff), 2025-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124504#p124504); "in Raids most mobs will be dead before you dots can even tick" [Grizb37 (player), 2026-02-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168199#p168199); very mana hungry with Dark Pact removed ("The biggest drawback of turtle aff is that dark pact has been removed ... replaced with another very mana hungry channeled spell" [Wrathweaver (player), 2025-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122630#p122630)); channels are punished by pushback and boss knockbacks (Anub'Rekhan knock-up, Faerlina Rain of Fire, Maexxna web wrap — [LastyK (player), 2024-11-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107778#p107778)); wants a Shadow Priest's Shadow Weaving to shine: "for raids - u need shadow priest debuff for good dps" [Akarui (player), 2026-02-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168899#p168899).
- **Content**: raids at 60 — accepted, and by 2026 the forum's default PvE recommendation ("everyone in pve will tell you to switch to affliction with sacrifice demon" [Momohomo (player), 2026-04-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174180#p174180)). Dungeons — good (see `leveling.md` for the low-level dungeon pattern). Solo/farming — "a strong soloing build" (Grizb37, above). PvP — contested (see below). Hardcore — no Affliction-specific hardcore advice beyond leveling.
- **Community standing**: **favored for PvE since late 2025 / early 2026** (consensus of the 2026 posts: Spritzbube, Guiltyhaomarush, Momohomo, Grizb37; and the wiki lists "Affliction Drain" as a raid spec); **contested before that**: staff called it above-average only after the Dec-2024 fixes [Jamey (staff), 2024-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460); in Nov 2024 the top Chinese-server raider wrote "what Affliction can do, Shadow Destruction can also do ... and even performs better" [LastyK (player), 2024-11-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107778#p107778) and in March 2025 a full-T3 Affliction raider found "the DPS output in AQ40 and Naxx is severely lacking compared to DS/Ruin and Fire" [Bardicdm (player), 2025-03-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122244#p122244). Bots: use Affliction as the default raid spec from 2026 on; expect older raiders to still call SM/Ruin the "real" spec.

## Official kit (staff, pre-1.18.1 unless noted)

- CC2 (2024-10-12), [Dragunovi (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102973#p102973): **Drain Soul** reworked into a real damage spell (Rank 5 at 60, 950 damage over 6 s, 16.67% SP per tick, new mana costs); **Corruption** 1.5 s cast (Improved Corruption 0.3 s/point, instant at 5/5); **Curse of Weakness** single rank, −15% attack speed; Improved Curse of Weakness and Improved Drain Soul removed; new **Improved Debilitating Curses**, **Resilient Shadows**; **Improved Drains** (Drain Mana +5/10%); **Improved Curse of Agony** 3/6/10% and prerequisite for **Malediction** ("Your Curse of Agony can now be used alongside your other curses, excluding Curse of Doom"); Fel Concentration also affects Dark Harvest; **Curse of Exhaustion** moved to row 3, slow 30%; Amplify Curse and Improved Curse of Exhaustion removed; **Nightfall** now from Corruption, Dark Harvest and all drains, 3 ranks 1/3/5%; new **Rapid Deterioration** (row 5, 2 pts: +6% Affliction cast speed; haste speeds DoT ticks at 50/100% efficiency, shortening duration); new **Soul Siphon** (3 pts: Drain Soul/Dark Harvest/Death Coil +2/3/4% per Affliction effect, max 4); **Dark Pact removed**; new capstone **Dark Harvest** (700/900/1150 Shadow over 8 s, ticks of your Affliction DoTs 20% faster while channelling, 30 s cooldown, flat mana).
- 2024-11-20, [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108776#p108776): Drain Soul 20% SP scaling, −20% mana, flat ticks (build-up removed); Dark Harvest 25% SP; **Nightfall 2 points, 2/4%**.
- 2024-12-20, [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460): "Affliction is receiving various damage increases"; **Resilient Shadows** reworked to dispel resistance 3/6/9%; **Improved Drains** now +5/10% effectiveness of Drain Soul, Drain Life, Drain Mana; **Nightfall Shadow Bolts guaranteed to land**; **Soul Siphon 2/4/6%** per effect.
- 2025-03-30, [Torta (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124504#p124504): Resilient Shadows 5/10/15%; **Malediction also makes Curse of Recklessness/Elements/Shadow apply your highest Curse of Agony** ("To combat lengthy setup time during Dungeon trash encounters"); **Siphon Life 100% total SP scaling** (was 50%); **Dark Harvest 30% haste and resets its cooldown if the target dies during the channel**.
- CC3 (2025-07-25), [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159): **Drain Soul mana made dynamic** (R5: 135 initial + 65/s, 525 over 6 s — "having such high cost for sometimes only 1 or 2 seconds of uptime was unnecessarily punishing"); **Fel Concentration 2 points, all Affliction spells** (also Fear/Howl); Improved Debilitating Curses renamed Improved Curse of Weakness (no CoEx part); **Curse of Exhaustion base 10%**, new **Improved Curse of Exhaustion** +15/30% (row 5); new Sinister Pursuit (row 3, later row 1); **Shadow Vulnerability (ex Improved Shadow Bolt) can now be applied by Drain Soul ticks**; Pet Command: Take Position.
- 1.18.1 (announced 2026-01-23, live 2026-03-20), [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071): **Curse of Exhaustion mana 8% → 14% base (110 → 190)**; **Curse of Tongues** R1 50% → 30%, R2 mana 110 → 240 ("cost increase better matching their impact"); **Curse of Shadow / Curse of the Elements / Curse of Recklessness GCD 1.5 s → 1 s** (revision 2026-01-26); **Sinister Pursuit moved to Demonology as a 2-point pet-speed talent; Master Conjuror removed**; **Affliction T3 4-set: Curse of Agony deals 100% more damage for its first 4 ticks**; **Affliction T3.5 3-set: Siphon Life mana (and health) return 50% → 25%**; Ritual of Souls 3 shards.
- Post-release player report (unresolved): "Please reduce the mana cost of Affliction Warlock spells! In PvP, the mana consumption is way too high" [daleking0518 (player), 2026-03-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171717#p171717) — the only cost increases in the staff notes are the two curses above.

### Patch validity

- Anything mentioning Dark Pact, Amplify Curse, Improved Drain Soul (mana regen) or 5-point Fel Concentration is **pre-CC2 / obsolete** (e.g. [Roxanneflowers (player), 2020-07-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7010#p7010)).
- Builds from Nov 2024 – Jul 2025 predate CC3's Fel Concentration (5 → 2 points) and Curse of Exhaustion split (10% base + Improved 15/30%).
- 1.18.1 removed Master Conjuror and moved Sinister Pursuit; otherwise the chappelles/Justgreens allocation stands. No post after 2026-03-20 re-evaluates the Affliction raid build; treat the 1.18.0 build as valid for 1.18.1 with those two edits.

## Stat priority

- **Hit**: Suppression covers 10% for Affliction spells; players run "5-6% hit" on gear (Spritzbube, above). Shadow Bolt (Nightfall procs) does not benefit from Suppression, but Nightfall bolts cannot miss since 2024-12-20 (staff, above). Whether **Dark Harvest** is affected by hit is an open question: "With 15% hit, only Darkharvest misses 3 out of 10. Bad luck always on darkharvest or does hit not affect Darkharvest?" [Akarui (player), 2025-08-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147762#p147762); "I don't understand why it even has a resist chance at all" [Toxo (player), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148484#p148484). Gap. The 16% cap against level-63 bosses is vanilla baseline, not from turtlecraft.gg.
- **Haste** vs raw spell power: the wiki says "Stat Priority: Haste and Hit" (community wiki Warlock page); Spritzbube: "as much haste as you can get". The direct question "is Affli better with haste or full sp?" got no answer [Horcos (player), 2025-10-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157151#p157151) — gap. Haste mechanics on Turtle: Rapid Deterioration's 6% is multiplicative with gear haste and "the game works in 0.04 second ticks, and the game rounds up to the next full tick" [Atreidon (player), 2025-10-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156605#p156605) (answering a measured discrepancy table by [Veddel (player), 2025-10-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156602#p156602)); haste shortens DoT duration so you refresh more often ("DOTs will tick faster, and you'll find it nearly impossible to find a suitable moment to channel an 8-second spell" — LastyK, above); pushback on a hasted channel costs whole ticks: "Each tick drain soul = 0.91 sec. First pushing back - minus 1 sec. its full one tick and one unfinished, so its 2 ticks lost" [Akarui (player), 2025-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150902#p150902).
- **Crit / Intellect**: useless — "we dont really want spellcrit" [Kentorianx (player), 2025-01-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115165#p115165); "Its spell crit - useless for us, need more spd/haste/some hit" [Lavr (player), 2025-06-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137349#p137349); DoTs do not crit on Turtle (LastyK, above). Consensus.
- **Stamina**: valued because "A warlocks healthpool IS their mana" (Mitzter, above) and the Affliction sets are stamina-poor ("tier 2.5 affliction set has 0 stamina" — Kentorianx, above; "I would be running around on naxx with 2k health"). Consensus.
- **Spirit**: not a DPS stat. A 2020 build tried to exploit the 5-second rule with Spirit but found that on Turtle mana regen from Spirit is also suppressed **during** channels ("imposing a similar condition of (the same?) -100% debuff while channeling") [Roxanneflowers (player), 2020-09-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7547#p7547) — single source, never re-tested; Justgreens123 (2025): "Spirit about worthless, as you get 0 regen in combat (unless troll)".
- Vampirism (% of damage returned as healing) exists as a Turtle stat on several items and on Felstone (community wiki Warlock page lists a "Vampirism" gear set); Felstone is 3% leech, improved by Improved Stones [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102973#p102973). A player reports Improved Stones showing only 2% Firestone crit instead of 4% [Guiltyhaomarush (player), 2026-03-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170140#p170140) and "2 points in the improved stones talent gives 4% instead of 6%" [Oddly (player), 2026-04-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174409#p174409) — unresolved.

## Single-target rotation (raid boss)

Sources: wiki "Affliction Drain" ("Curse, Corruption, Siphon Life, Dark Harvest when its up otherwise Drain Soul"), [Justgreens123 (player), 2025-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159958#p159958) ("Generally rotation is CoA, Corruption, siphon life, soul drain. Can corruption first if you want nightfall to trigger"), [Eversongwoods (player), 2025-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118780#p118780) ("Use sl,agony,curse of shadows,corruption and either drain soul or dot up the next target"), Akarui's dummy tests (below).

**Opener** (boss, you are the curse Warlock): 1) Curse of Shadow or Curse of the Elements as assigned (1-s GCD since 1.18.1); with Malediction this also puts your highest Curse of Agony on the target. 2) Corruption (instant). 3) Siphon Life. 4) Immolate is **not** in the Affliction list; Campy adds it as a generic Warlock filler but his build is Destruction-heavy [Campy-TWForum (player), 2025-03-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121650#p121650) — do not copy. 5) Drain Soul.

**Priority list**:
1. If a Nightfall proc (Shadow Trance) is up: cast Shadow Bolt (instant, cannot miss). Whether to interrupt a Drain Soul / Dark Harvest channel for it is an open question the sources raise but do not answer (LastyK, above; "Its so stupid when u wait this proc a year but then u get 3-5 in row each tick" [Akarui (player), 2025-10-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156075#p156075)). Bot rule: break a Drain Soul channel for a proc; do not break Dark Harvest.
2. If the assigned support curse is missing: recast it (Malediction re-applies Curse of Agony with it).
3. If Corruption is missing or about to expire (haste shortens it — refresh "when needed" is the only guidance): recast Corruption.
4. If Siphon Life is missing: recast (keeps Soul Siphon at its 4-effect maximum together with Corruption, Curse of Agony and the support curse; "The debuff effects caused by the drain spells do not contribute" — Dragunovi CC2 post).
5. If Curse of Agony is missing and you are not the curse Warlock: Curse of Agony.
6. Dark Harvest if off cooldown — **contested**: Akarui measured "with drain soul + dots dps 8-10% more than with darkharvest+dots and ful t2.5 set" [Akarui (player), 2025-09-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153453#p153453), confirmed by "not using Dark Harvest makes you deal more dmg in the long run lol" [Horcos (player), 2025-10-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155815#p155815) and Justgreens123 (above); versus Guiltyhaomarush's 450 DPS "as a Dark harvest affliction warlock" (2026-02-14) and the wiki rotation. Akarui's own earlier finding was the opposite before CC3 ("With Darkharvest, the DPS on the boss dummy was 5-10% more than if you just throw DoTs and spam Drain Soul. With the 1.18 update, a useful talent appeared in the Destro tree ... now ... 2-5% more without" — 2025-08-22). Bot rule: skip Dark Harvest on bosses unless you can guarantee an uninterrupted 8-s channel; always use it on a target about to die (cooldown resets on kill, staff 2025-03-30).
7. Otherwise: Drain Soul (highest rank). "Drain soul scales alot better at low levels" [Grizb37 (player), 2026-04-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174185#p174185); "drain soul is better than sb in every way as a filler" (Eversongwoods, 2025-02-03). Drain Soul ticks can apply Shadow Vulnerability (CC3).
8. Life Tap when mana is below the cost of your next cycle; never hard-cast Shadow Bolt.

**Ranks**: drop Drain Soul rank only while leveling for mana ("I've just been using rank 1 DS because of the mana difference compared to rank 2" [Snipesta (player), 2025-02-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119257#p119257)); at 60 the dynamic per-second cost makes partial channels cheap (CC3). Rank-1 Corruption is the accepted "reflect breaker" on Karazhan mobs: "You can use it as kara reflect breaker but in case you are nightfall - rank 1 corruption is better" [Ataika (player), 2025-07-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137857#p137857).

## Multi-target / AoE rotation

- **2–3 targets (dungeon trash)**: "dot everything up, once it has good threat, so your dots can be ticking on mobs that arent focused (not just 1 mob at a time). Then drain soul spam on the main target, you do not use shadow bolt" (Grizb37, 2026-04-06). Cast a support curse on each target if you have Malediction (auto Curse of Agony), then Corruption on each, then drain the kill target. Curse of Agony "is end-loaded" so multi-dotting it "rarely pulls aggro" [Nahath (player), 2023-08-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58725#p58725) (pre-CC2 tooltip; the ramp-up still exists — "the incremental damage of Curse of Agony ... makes this curse perform poorly on trash mobs", LastyK).
- **4+ targets**: Affliction has no AoE of its own; the class AoE is Rain of Fire / Hellfire. "for 3 or more trash mobs, it's AOE Rain of Fire or occasionally ... I will Hellfire the trash (make sure the healer is aware)" (Campy, 2025-03-04). Hellfire on Turtle: 1.18.1 made it cheap (R3 325 mana + 65/s) and 6.66% SP per tick, Wrathstone cuts its self-damage by 30% but adds 40% Fire threat (Dragunovi CC2; Jamey 1.18.1). Rain of Fire ticks every 1 s since CC3. Ask the tank first: "I always ask the tank/s if they mind AOE, most are cool with it as long as you give a few seconds to build threat" (Campy).
- Expect trash to die before DoTs pay off; the staff's own framing is that Affliction should "better keep up ... namely on trash encounters" after the Malediction change (Torta 2025-03-30), not lead it.

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Mana**: Life Tap is the engine ("Life tap for affliction is very important in the rotation. And for this, you need a lot of HP" [Akarui (player), 2025-07-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137847#p137847)); Improved Life Tap 2/2 and Shadow Mastery both scale it. T3.5 3-set gives 25% (was 50%) of Siphon Life drain back as mana since 1.18.1. Felstone leech heals the Warlock and the demon. Dark Pact is gone — a player explains you cannot summon the Imp as a battery any more (Arthurice, 2025-09-02). Tea shares the Healthstone cooldown [Ataika (player), 2023-12-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79754#p79754) — decide per fight whether the Healthstone or the tea is your emergency button.
- **Soul Shards**: stack to 3 since CC2; Drain Soul on anything that gives XP/honor yields a shard; bots should keep a capped count — "shardcap addon IS working. But since here on twow shards are stacking to 3, if you want to have max 9 shards in inventory, you need to set shardcap at 3" [Milenski (player), 2025-03-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124319#p124319); shard-deleting macros exist on the community wiki (Warlock Macros page) and in [Roxanneflowers (player), 2020-07-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7011#p7011). Shards can be lost when the pet despawns on flight paths — "I'm pretty sure you have to dismiss your summoned pet first to get the soul shard back" [Drubarrymooer (player), 2025-08-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148976#p148976). Ritual of Souls costs 3 shards since 1.18.1. Soul Bags give a 5–25% chance not to consume a reagent (CC2).
- **Drinking**: the class rarely drinks at 60 — "it's possible to have infinite sustainability as a Warlock (especially as Affliction). It feels amazing to never have to eat/drink while leveling!" [Finarfin (player), 2024-01-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84947#p84947). Drink only when both health and mana are low after a Life Tap chain.
- **Dark Harvest** (30 s CD) — see rotation; reset on kill.
- **Shadow Ward** on shadow-casting bosses (Campy). **Death Coil** as a self-heal / emergency (3-min CD). **Drain Life** is a niche self-heal: "99% of the time you will not use it in PVE. Only in rare situatins when healers cannot heal you and Death Coil on CD" [Akarui (player), 2025-07-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140392#p140392); counter-view: "drain life is great with voidwalker or ... when your potion, tea and healthstone are on cd ... It also conveniently bypasses 50% healing debuff from infernal" [Sharmaine (player), 2025-07-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140405#p140405).
- **Class mount**: Dreadsteed costs mana and breaks the 5-second rule; raiders use another mount for repeated mounting ("If you care for performance you never use class mounts" [Atreidon (player), 2024-06-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97966#p97966)) — contested by casual players who use it anyway.

## Role strategy

- **Curse duty**: two or more Warlocks split Curse of Shadow / Curse of the Elements / Curse of Recklessness; Malediction means each of them still gets a Curse of Agony. Old advice that Siphon Life is skipped in raids "because both damage and healing cause threat" [Atreidon (player), 2023-12-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81272#p81272) predates the debuff-limit removal and the Soul Siphon talent and is obsolete for Affliction.
- **Threat**: Affliction "hasn`t crits and it is much easier to control the threat" (Akarui, 2025-07-18). Options if needed: Voidstone (−10% spell threat, learned at 52, CC3 revision), sacrificed Succubus (−10%), Paladin Salvation, and the TWThreat meter ("instal TWT treat meter, ask pala about salva" — Akarui). Succubus Soothing Kiss reduces only the Succubus's threat, not yours [Hctwowfan (player), 2025-07-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140401#p140401) — despite the Dec-2024 tooltip saying it reduces both; treat as contested/bugged.
- **Pet choice in raids**: Imp (Blood Pact for the party) if it can survive; otherwise Demonic Sacrifice. "I always take my Imp in Dungeons and Raids, Blood Pack buffs all" (Campy). Pets die to raid AoE despite Avoidance (80% AoE reduction, also cleave since 1.18.1); on Four Horsemen marks, Thaddius polarity and Sapphiron's ice they die regardless [Erhog (player), 2025-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126753#p126753). Use **Pet Command: Take Position** (CC3) to park the Imp out of AoE and behind the boss.
- **Movement fights**: DoTs and instant Curse of Agony are the mobile damage; Grim Reach (+range) is taken for this; do not start Dark Harvest before a knockback.
- **Dispel-heavy PvE** (none noted) and **PvP**: Resilient Shadows 15% is "only +15% dispell protection which doesnt do that much" (Grizb37, 2026-02-14). Affliction in PvP is **contested**: "in BG's all your dots will be instantly dispelled especially because every game has like 5+ paladins" (Grizb37) and "affliction is not very good" for PvP [Eversongwoods (player), 2025-02-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118995#p118995) versus "warlocks are OP in bg, they fucking melt people" [Terrydavis (player), 2026-02-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169469#p169469) and "Affliction, if played properly, is a complete neusance that is hard to reach, incredibly mobile as they can just dot and gtfo" [Mitzter (player), 2025-06-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135904#p135904). Affliction PvP relies on Curse of Exhaustion for kiting; after CC3 cut it to 40% total ("Fine for Destro to not have 50% CoEx, but Affliction deep spec needs it" [Halfgeek9 (player), 2025-08-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143162#p143162)) and 1.18.1 raised its cost, it is weaker again. See `destruction-pvp.md` for the PvP toolkit that is shared.

## Common mistakes (what players criticize)

- Hard-casting Shadow Bolt as Affliction — "you do not use shadow bolt, it takes too long to wind up" (Grizb37); Nightfall procs only.
- Casting Dark Harvest into a knockback / AoE and losing the 30-s cooldown (LastyK's Naxx list).
- Not keeping Siphon Life up (Soul Siphon loses a stack); refreshing DoTs early "costs a GCD" each time and wastes mana [Yggd (player), 2025-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115748#p115748).
- Using Curse of Agony instead of the assigned raid curse when you are the only Warlock — Malediction gives both, so never drop Curse of Shadow/Elements.
- Hellfire / Inferno in dungeons without warning: "I had LBRS with t2 warlock who was constantly dying because he spammed Inferno on every mob pack. We wiped like 6 times" (f2 Announcements, [Anonymous player, 2024](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=12931)) — Infernal and Felguard turn hostile when control ends and are "consider[ed] griefing if you have them in a city" [Boeuff (player), 2025-01-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115350#p115350).
- Pet on aggressive near a boss — the Imp pulled Kruul before the pull; "Sounds like it was set on aggressive" [Grizb37 (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165986#p165986). Keep pets passive/defensive on pulls.
- Wearing Attire for the set bonus while dying to AoE — pick stamina where the sets are poor (Kentorianx, Justgreens).
- Letting shards overflow the bags; not keeping a shard for Soulstone/Healthstone/pet re-summon ("there are times you can die simply because you don't have soul shards" [Forumdweller (player), 2025-05-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129896#p129896)).
- Relying on Curse of Weakness — "you can safely throw this garbage out of the spell bar" (Akarui, 2025-07-02); "It doesnt stack with other attack speed slows" [Atreidon (player), 2025-04-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127400#p127400). Consensus that it is a last-resort or reflect-breaker only.
