# Hunter — Marksmanship — Ranged DPS (raid / dungeon)

The class's "traditional" ranged spec. Covers the deep-MM raid build and the BM/MM hybrid that raids used before 1.18.1. Beast Mastery raid DPS is in `beast-mastery-ranged-dps.md`, melee Survival in `survival-melee-dps.md`, PvP in `marksmanship-pvp.md`, pets in `pets.md`.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). The ranged kit changed at Class Changes 2 ("CC2", 1.17.2, live 2024-11-01: Trueshot renamed Steady Shot and moved into the MM tree), was left alone in Class Changes 3 ("CC3", 1.18.0, live 2025-08-15: "We believe Marksmanship is in a good spot"), and was rebuilt in 1.18.1 (live 2026-03-20: Steady Shot and Trueshot Aura baseline, Aimed Shot keystone, Experimental Ammunition, Lock and Load). **Every pre-2026-03-20 build and rotation below is for the old tree; the post-1.18.1 section is thin because the server closed 2026-05-15 and few players wrote guides in the eight weeks between.**

## Overview

- **Role**: single-target ranged physical DPS with a party buff (Trueshot Aura) and raid utility (pulling, kiting, Tranquilizing Shot, Freezing/Frost Trap via Feign Death).
- **Strengths (sources)**: safest DPS position ("sitting back and spamming Trueshot synchronously with Autoshots creates much higher DPS with no risk of getting hit by melee-ranged spells like Cleaves or Tail Swipes" [Steakhouse (player), 2024-01-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83337#p83337)); scales with haste better than other physical classes because "autoshot + steadyshot represent 90% of our total damage against single target" [Juzam (player, guild theorycrafter), 2024-10-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104588#p104588); party-wide Trueshot Aura ("a feral friend got more than 200AP out of the trueshot aura" [Barwat1 (player), 2025-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125834#p125834)); a ranged-CC toolkit that melee Survival loses.
- **Weaknesses (sources)**: "MM shines on single target bosses and lacks aoe in general" (Barwat1, above); Volley "by far the worst AOE spell in the game" before 1.18.1 (Juzam, above); non-scaling Arcane Shot/Serpent Sting/Volley before 1.18.1 ("Arcane shot deals max 183 damage" [Vrag (player), 2026-01-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164294#p164294)); must stand still ("multishot requiring us to stand still for an instant is clunky" [Soulforged23 (player), 2026-01-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165600#p165600)); mana-hungry with T3-independent regen ("Hunter is a class that cannot reliably regain mana without proper set bonuses" [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)); melee buffs (Battle Shout, Blessing of Might, Windfury, Demonslaying elixir) do not affect ranged [Cabum (player), 2025-07-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141781#p141781).
- **Content**: raids — the default ranged raid spec until mid-2025, then **contested** against Survival; dungeons at 60 — fine, Survival preferred for cleave; leveling — second to BM (see `leveling.md`); PvP — see `marksmanship-pvp.md`; hardcore — no MM-specific hardcore advice in the sources.
- **Community standing** (time-ordered, all player claims unless marked):
  - Dec 2024 – Jan 2025, pre-CC3: **favored**. "Naxx logs are showing mark as like... Top five DPS right now or something, and survival is actually alright too ... ## Stat priority (60)

- **Hit**: 9% to cap on level-63 bosses at 300 weapon skill; **6% at 305 skill** (racial +5 bows/guns: Troll/High Elf bows, Dwarf guns) [Reptilewow (player), 2023-06-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49072#p49072); 305 → 6%, 313 → ~5.5%, 315 → 5% (Trueaim Gauntlets +8 skill, Sash of the Grand Hunt +2) [Condor22 (player), 2023-05-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46292#p46292) (posed as a question, vanilla-baseline math; staff never published Turtle's linearized weapon-skill table); a BiS-list author uses 7% as the practical minimum "with the weapon skill book and changes to hit chance calculations on Turtle" [ajax_808 (player), 2024-12-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115058#p115058). "1% hit is always a (slightly more than) 1% damage increase until you reach hit cap" [Juzam (player), 2024-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107692#p107692). Do not over-cap: T3 set + Naxx hit gear already caps a High Elf bow user ("2hit + 4hit +5 Bows ... restricting me to never add any Ring, Neck or Trinket that gives +hit") [Alfonso (player), 2023-08-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56809#p56809) (1.17.0 itemization thread).
- **Haste** (the Turtle-specific stat): "1% haste is around 0.9% damage increase on bosses (single target) and 0.85% damage increase overall"; no diminishing returns; "once you're in full naxx gear, because of your very high crit rate, 1% haste will become as good as 1% crit" [Juzam, 2024-10-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104588#p104588). Quiver/pouch haste and Swift Aspects procs count [Skurridizo, 2025-11-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160791#p160791). Rapid Fire cuts Steady Shot cast "from 1.5 to 1.1" (same post). Haste does **not** shorten Aimed Shot from quiver haste (Juzam) — 1.18.1 explicitly made Rapid Fire cut Aimed/Steady cast by 40% (staff).
- **Crit**: ~0.9% damage per 1% for a raid-buffed Naxx hunter; slight diminishing returns as crit rises, "there is no such things as 'soft crit cap'" (Juzam, above). 53 agi = 1% crit, 1 agi = 2 RAP (same).
- **Attack power / agility**: measure your own AP-per-1%: unequip an AP item, read ranged DPS in the character panel, add hidden AP buffs (110 Hunter's Mark, 200 vs undead from stones); Juzam needed ~34 AP per 1% at 340 DPS [Juzam, 2024-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107692#p107692). Spreadsheet weights used by a BiS-list builder: 1 agi = 3 AP-eq, 1% crit = 30 AP-eq, 1 AP = 1 [ajax_808, 2024-12-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115058#p115058).
- **Haste vs AP enchants**: with mid gear the 23-agi enchant set beats 3% haste; in full T3 haste wins for auto-shot only; with the ZG/Naxx/15-agi enchant set vs 5% haste, AP wins even in BiS (auto-shot-only model) [Nurincarinun (player), 2023-11-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74308#p74308) (pre-Steady-Shot model; single source). "2% Haste edges out 30AP in full T3, but can be worse with lesser gear" [Bgnome (player), 2025-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117570#p117570).
- **Armor penetration**: "generally weak" [Balake (player), 2023-04-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43447#p43447); "does not yield good returns generally, so is largely ignored" (Bgnome, above).
- **Spell power**: irrelevant for MM (Serpent/Arcane/Volley scaled with SP pre-1.18.1 but "if you increase SP on your stuff, you'll lose RAP" — Juzam); T2.5 spell power is dead weight for MM.
- **Intellect/Spirit/MP5**: mana matters in long fights ("Surv Hunter already had problems with mana" is Survival; for MM "I've never run out of mana ... Efficiency + Steady Shot is just 77 mana" [Skurridizo, 2025-09-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150362#p150362)); Juzam calls T3 MP5 "useless" for itemization cost. Post-1.18.1 Aspect of the Viper replaces drinking between pulls (see Cooldowns).
- **While leveling**: agility/stamina and weapon DPS; see `leveling.md`.

## Single-target rotation

### Pre-1.18.1 (CC2/CC3 kit) — consensus

Core loop: **Auto Shot → Steady Shot → Auto Shot → Steady Shot**, never delaying the auto: "Steady Shot(it was Trueshot pre CC2) damage is normalized to 2.8 weapon speed ... That's why we had to pay attention to not clip Auto Shots" [Calli (player), 2025-07-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140872#p140872); "AA>Trueshot>AA>Trueshot ... depending on your weaponspeed" [Atreidon (player), 2025-07-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140730#p140730), confirmed [Bigsmerf, 2025-07-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140739#p140739); "Mana efficiency. Steady Shot deals good damage and costs far less than Arcane Shot or Aimed Shot" [SvenS2 (player), 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149801#p149801).

Ordered priority (raid boss, weapon speed ≥ 3.2):
1. Opener: send pet (attack key, not a combined macro), Hunter's Mark, Aimed Shot as the pull opener only if the tank has threat ("Aimed shot is now an opener just like Ambush" [Juzam, 2023-12-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83213#p83213); "You can use it as an opener, but once the fight starts, you're better off just using autoshots and Steady Shot" [Soulforged23, 2026-01-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165600#p165600)).
2. Serpent Sting once on long fights (≥ 30 s) if mana allows — "Serpent sting i used in longer fights" [Frantsel (player), 2024-01-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83443#p83443); skip on trash and when mana is short [Jinxes (player), 2025-11-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160638#p160638). Contested: Steakhouse says drop it once geared.
3. Multi-Shot on cooldown (10 s), placed right after a Steady Shot so it does not delay the auto: "AA>Steady>Multi>AA>... with crossbows, AA>Multi/Steady>AA>... with faster weapons" [Calli, 2025-07-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140872#p140872); "autoshot > trueshot > multi-shot > auto shot > trueshot > arcane shot > auto shot ... you need the slowest weapon. 3.20 or above" [Vimgreen (player), 2023-09-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66802#p66802); with weapon speed < 3.3: AA MS AA SS AA SS; > 3.3: AA SS MS AA SS [Raypulsif (player) quoting a video, 2025-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115771#p115771).
4. Steady Shot immediately after every Auto Shot; if a Steady would delay the next auto (fast weapon, Rapid Fire + Swift Aspects active), let the auto fire first — with 3.2 speed "you only have 29 miliseconds to not clip your autoattacks" under both haste buffs [Borefficz (player), 2023-08-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56712#p56712).
5. Arcane Shot: only while moving or as a filler with spare mana in PvP; "for most cases it's fine to simply take Arcane Shot off your Action Bars" [Steakhouse, 2024-01-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83337#p83337).
6. Do not re-cast Aimed Shot mid-fight ("I removed my aimed shot completely from the action bar" [Vrag, 2025-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159412#p159412)).

Use a swing-timer addon: Quiver (`https://github.com/SabineWren/Quiver`, download the release not the repo zip [Ltxtwo (player), 2025-03-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122514#p122514)), YaHT Turtle fork with Steady Shot renamed [Chocobodx (player), 2025-01-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116815#p116815), or BearCastBar [Deviator9421 (player), 2023-06-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49849#p49849). Spammable auto-shot macro (no toggle-off): `/run if not UnitCanAttack("player","target")then TargetNearestEnemy()end if not IsAutoRepeatAction(36)then CastSpellByName"Auto Shot"end` (36 = your Auto Shot action slot) [Cochise (player), 2024-09-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101994#p101994).

Player observations after 2026-03-20: "Steady Shot is one of your main sources of damage" [Lane13 (player), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171531#p171531); "Arcane Shot is pretty good now. [Baek (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171502#p171502) (the staff text says the ammunition types **cycle** Fire→Arcane→Nature; Baek and [Tomogwarrior (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171526#p171526) perceived it as random — **contested/unclear**); Lock and Load's line AoE has "an angle of only 5 degrees" and is hard to use in PvE except on a stationary tank target [Baek, 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171856#p171856); "Aimed Shot cast bar deleted since 1.18" thread title (t=21097) — no content extracted beyond the title.

Derived priority for bots (each line's basis in brackets):
1. Keep Auto Shot going; never clip it [consensus, unchanged].
2. **Aimed Shot on cooldown** (2 s cast, 20 s cd with Swiftshot 3/3; 1 s cast when Lock and Load is up) — cast it right after an auto, like Steady [staff tooltip; Rapid Fire −40% cast].
3. **Lock and Load proc** (Aimed cooldown reset): cast Aimed immediately, line up the target behind the boss/other mobs if possible [staff].
4. Spend the Experimental Ammunition buff before it cycles (1 min): **Arcane** → Arcane Shot; **Nature** → Serpent Sting (+100%, −240 armor for 15 s); **Fire** → Multi-Shot on ≥ 2 targets [staff]. Bots should read the buff name; treat the order as Fire→Arcane→Nature per staff but tolerate randomness per players.
5. Multi-Shot on cooldown when it fits between autos [pre-1.18.1 consensus].
6. Arcane Shot on cooldown when mana ≥ 40% (it now scales with weapon damage and is usable while moving) [Lane13 + staff].
7. Steady Shot as the filler after every auto [Lane13; consensus].
8. Serpent Sting only when Nature ammunition is up or the fight is long [pre-1.18.1 consensus; mana −30% in 1.18.1].
9. Volley (3 s cast, −2 s with Barrage 3/3) only on ≥ 4 targets [staff; pre-1.18.1 "less than 8 targets it's actually a loss of dps" — Juzam — for the old channeled version].

## Multi-target / AoE

- 2–3 targets: Multi-Shot on cooldown, keep the single-target loop otherwise; Serpent Sting spread only if mana is plentiful.
- 4+ targets, pre-1.18.1: Volley "is only decent for the packs of small spiders who patrol, few trashs groups before patchwerk and before Faerlina ... only increase your damage by like 20%" (Naxx-geared) [Juzam, 2024-10-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104588#p104588); MM "has 0 aoe" [Jiav (player), 2025-07-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141842#p141842). Raids that valued cleave used Survival instead (see Survival playbook).
- 4+ targets, post-1.18.1: Volley cast (330 mana, 6% AP) plus Fire-ammunition Multi-Shot; no player has benchmarked it. **Gap.**
- The Explosive Trap / in-combat traps are Survival-only (Untamed Trapper); MM can only trap via Feign Death out of combat.

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Rapid Fire**: use at the start of every boss and on cooldown when the fight lasts > 15 s; it speeds Steady Shot casts ("1.5 to 1.1") and, post-1.18.1, Aimed Shot by 40%; watch clipping with a fast weapon (Borefficz, above). Do not waste it while moving [Springboards (player), 2025-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159342#p159342).
- **Trinkets**: ZG trinket resets major hunter cooldowns (double Multi/Arcane) [Azuredrak0 (player), 2024-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91303#p91303); Devilsaur Eye / Jom Gabbar windows (Nurincarinun); Arcane Infused Gem (BWL) is "worthless" outside cleave [Calli (player), 2023-09-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66732#p66732); Sigil of Ancient Accord "for mm wortless" [Foteinos (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156533#p156533).
- **Mana**: five-second rule — "bundle your spells into bursts, and then just use your potent autoattacks for a bit when you need to regen" [Atreidon (player), 2026-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166255#p166255); raid regen from Blessing of Wisdom, mana totem, mana potions, tea, mana oil (Skurridizo, above). Post-1.18.1 **Aspect of the Viper** (56): 5% max mana / 5 s, no cost, but it replaces Hawk — "you need aspect of the hawk for good dmg ... the measily 5% mana reg wont do anything if you keep up your full dmg rotation" [Cheruscan (player), 2026-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166287#p166287); "The viper aspect does not have time to restore Mana if you inflict damage constantly" [Baek, 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171540#p171540); good between pulls / after Feign Death in BGs [Tomogwarrior, 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171553#p171553). Bot rule: Viper only when mana < 20% and no cooldown burst is planned, or out of combat instead of drinking; swap back to Hawk before the pull (aspect swaps cost mana and a GCD — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123634#p123634)).
- **Feign Death**: threat drop every 30 s; put the pet on follow/passive first (see `pets.md`); Feign Death + trap needs two presses [Vliqta (player), 2022-10-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26783#p26783).
- **Drink** when out of combat below ~40% mana; hunters "have 40-50% less mana" than casters [Ragetto (player), 2024-10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103865#p103865).

## Role strategy

- Stand at max range (35 yd baseline, +Hawk Eye) outside the 8-yd dead zone; move only between autos ("You can stand still for 0,5 sec at 40 yd distance, drop that 'rotation', kite rinse an repeat" [Staywhimsy (player), 2025-03-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122276#p122276)).
- Raid jobs beyond damage: "FD to give those valuable seconds to the healers... frost trapping the zombies on Gluth while watching when to tq shot... kiting jobs" [Calli (player), 2024-01-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83530#p83530); Tranquilizing Shot on MC/BWL/AQ frenzies ("intended to be used on certain mc/bwl/aq bosses" [Hctwowfan (player), 2023-08-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58887#p58887)); pulling and Distracting Shot; CC "can be reliably done by 1-2 MM hunter if you don't get unlucky resists on FD" (Barwat1).
- Trueshot Aura is party-wide: raids may put the hunter in a melee group; "TO minmax hunter you have to put them in their own group with feral buff ... and 77 agi totem instead of windfury" [Ironfist13 (player), 2025-07-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141812#p141812). Post-1.18.1 the aura is baseline for every hunter.
- Pet in raids: contributes "4-6% dmg on single target" for a Naxx MM [Cabum (player), 2025-04-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126994#p126994) or "around 17% damage without Coordinated Assault and about 19% with" in instances [Ahinevich (player), 2026-03-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172610#p172610) (contested numbers); keep it alive with Take Position, or run petless with **Alone Against the World** (SV row 2, +3/6% damage with no pet, 1.18.1) — "intentionally weaker than actually doing the legwork and controlling a pet" [Atreidon, 2026-03-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172629#p172629); "Not having ur pet is never better for ur dmg in any raids, ever" [Sbingo (player), 2026-04-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173794#p173794). Bot rule: use the pet; take the petless talent only for fights where the pet dies at the pull.
- Threat: Piercing Shots' bleed generates no threat (staff, CC2); Aimed Shot openers create an "undesirable" threat spike [Atreidon, 2023-12-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83232#p83232).

## Common mistakes (bots must avoid)

1. Clipping Auto Shot with Steady Shot ("Autoshot never fires as the recast of Steady seems to match the cast time" [Carns (player), 2025-02-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119747#p119747)).
2. Casting Aimed Shot mid-fight pre-1.18.1 or standing still in Aimed casts while a boss cleaves.
3. Wasting mana on Serpent Sting/Arcane on trash that dies in 3 s (Jinxes).
4. Combined "pet attack + auto shot" macros in dungeons — pet body-pulls (Turtlebisque, Calli).
5. Forgetting the pet is out during pulls ("Raidlead yelling 'no pets'" — Cheruscan) or letting it die in cleaves/Shadowflame instead of using Take Position.
6. Running out of ammo, or carrying only one ammo type when a weapon swap drops (Justaddwater).
7. Needing on melee weapons/leather ("item sink that will roll need on all items" — Idontcareatall; "Hunters would primarily roll for stat sticks" [SvenS2, 2026-04-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173875#p173875)).
8. Feigning with the pet still attacking (stays in combat); FD/trap macro failures — "Stop using noob macros and learn to control your character" [Skurridizo, 2026-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169225#p169225).
9. Not feeding before bosses; sitting in Aspect of the Viper during burst.
10. Swapping aspects fluidly (each swap costs mana and stops regen for 5 s) [Solveit (player), 2026-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166243#p166243).
