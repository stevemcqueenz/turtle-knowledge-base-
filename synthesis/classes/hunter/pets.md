# Hunter — Pet management (all specs)

Supporting document for every hunter playbook in this folder. Covers pet choice by spec and content, pet talents/abilities (training points, Claw vs Bite, family abilities), feeding/happiness/loyalty, positioning, threat and the Turtle-specific rules (Take Position, no swapping in combat, Avoidance). All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Within pre-1.18.1 the pet kit changed at Class Changes 2 ("CC2", 1.17.2, announced 2024-10-12, live 2024-11-01) and Class Changes 3 ("CC3", 1.18.0, announced 2025-07-25, live 2025-08-15).

## Learning and training abilities

- Learn a family ability by taming a beast that has it, putting the ability on autocast and letting it use it "5 seconds or 5 minutes" until the chat says you learned it; then teach it via Beast Training [Maesus (player), 2023-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39636#p39636); "It usually doesn't take more than a few uses" [Oinkboinkdoink (player), 2022-10-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27380#p27380). Lower ranks cannot be learned after a higher rank [Bogyone (player), 2022-11-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28293#p28293).
- Training points: `pet level x (loyalty - 1)`, max 300 at 60/loyalty 6; a fresh tame has 0 minus the cost of known abilities (hence "-4" on a pet with Claw 1); "Your pet will not be able to learn every available ability and passive ever. Max stamina and max armor already take up most of your max tp" [Hctwowfan (player), 2025-09-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151904#p151904), [2026-02-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169306#p169306). Spread resistances across pets (one fire, one frost, one nature, one arcane) — same source.
- Loyalty rises only if happiness is green/yellow **and** the pet gains XP; standing in town does nothing (Hctwowfan, above). A player reports no loyalty gain over 3 levels at 125% happiness — [Realdecker (player), 2023-08-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55842#p55842) (single, unresolved).
- **Claw vs Bite** (contested, both eras): Bite is stronger per focus with a cooldown, Claw is weak but spammable [Atreidon (player), 2026-03-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172305#p172305); both on a cat = 124 → 139 DPS on a dummy over 1 minute [Glavorli (player), 2022-11-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28237#p28237); Claw focus-starves Growl/poison — "claw will focus-starve pet & result in possible missed/delayed poison stack applications" [Daoc2001 (player), 2026-03-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172391#p172391); "bite>claw, because the focus remains on other skills with the same dps" [Ahinevich (player), 2026-03-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172621#p172621). Bot rule: on a pet with a family focus-spender (Screech, Scorpid Poison, Lightning Breath) skip Claw; on a plain cat/raptor use Bite (+Claw only when not tanking).
- Never teach Cower "Not a single justification" [Shamma (player), 2022-10-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27414#p27414) — but in raids with a threat-hugging pet, Cower is the intended threat dump; it is "too weak" per [Lane13 (player), 2026-04-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174020#p174020) (contested).
- Growl is a flat-threat ability, not a taunt; keep it on autocast [Bob022 (player), 2024-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108662#p108662).

## Feeding, happiness, loyalty

- Food buff ticks +35/+17/+8 happiness every 2 s for 20 s (350/170/80 total) depending on food level; eating is cancelled by combat; happiness drops ~50 per 6 minutes idle, -50 on Dismiss, -10 when walking out of range (use Stay instead of Dismiss), -350 on death; mounting does not reduce it [Amyloriley (player), 2025-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126672#p126672). Eating takes 30 s and is interrupted by combat [Hctwowfan (player), 2023-08-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55845#p55845).
- Diet table (community wiki `https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_Pets`): Bat fruit/fungus; Bear everything; Boar everything; Carrion Bird meat; Cat meat/fish; Crab fruit/fish/fungus/bread; Crocolisk meat/fish; Gorilla fruit/fungus; Hyena meat/fruit; Owl meat; Raptor meat; Scorpid meat; Spider meat; Tallstrider fruit/fungus/bread; Turtle fruit/fish/fungus; Wind Serpent fish/cheese/bread; Wolf meat. Foxes eat meat and fruit only [Cyborgjiro (player), 2025-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123394#p123394). Mana wyrm owners "had to learn to fish" [Agentpothead (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171442#p171442).
- Raid rule: feed before every boss even if already Happy — "my pet losses went from very happy to happy, and i lose a lot of DPS in the process, since that i always feed my pet before starting bosses" [Nalos (player), 2025-11-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160580#p160580).
- Only Scrolls and Jujus can be used on pets; none add spell power [Skurridizo, 2026-03-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170748#p170748); Juju Might / Juju Power for the pet are on the raid consumable list [Likaleo (player), 2024-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91592#p91592).
- Feed macro (feeds slot 1 of the backpack; keep food there): `/run if HasPetUI() then if UnitIsDead('pet') then CastSpellByName('Revive Pet') elseif (UnitHealth('pet')/UnitHealthMax('pet')*100)<75 then CastSpellByName('Mend Pet') else CastSpellByName('Feed Pet') PickupContainerItem(0,1) end end` [Snigery (player), 2020-09-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7660#p7660). MetaHunt's Feed-O-Matic module automates food choice [Metasploit, 2026-02-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169538#p169538).

## Positioning, survival, threat

- Mend Pet is "extremely inefficient": 5 s channel, 480 mana, 1225 healing, 20 yd range; healers rarely show pet frames, so "the most valuable skill becomes: perfect pet positioning" [Skurridizo, 2026-03-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170469#p170469). Improved Mend Pet +40% and a cleanse chance (staff, CC2).
- Use **Pet Command: Take Position** in combat to keep the pet out of cleaves/Shadowflame (BWL) — it sometimes walks slowly to the spot [Nalos, 2025-11-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160580#p160580), [Lane13, 2026-03-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170522#p170522). Pets attack from the centre of a hitbox on Nefarian, BWL drakes, Ragnaros, Omen, Solnius (not Onyxia) and get cleaved [Lane13, 2026-03-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170490#p170490).
- Avoidance does not reduce damage that is "calculated individually" (void zones, Thaddius tesla charges, Kruul's and Chess knights' cleaves, Heigan explosions) [Skurridizo, 2026-03-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170543#p170543); Lane13 disputes that cleaves should count as AoE (same thread). A 2024 report measured ~50% reduction instead of 80% [Dragonfury (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106255#p106255) — unresolved.
- Dungeon rule: keep the pet on **passive** and use an explicit attack key; a combined "auto shot + pet attack" macro "won't end well" because the pet body-pulls [Turtlebisque (player), 2023-12-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82499#p82499), [Calli (player), 2024-04-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=93467#p93467). Pet Dismiss and Eyes of the Beast + Stay are "essential for dungeon runs where you have to jump through ledges" [SvenS2 (player), 2026-04-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173875#p173875).
- Nefarian's hunter class call was made "more expensive for hunters" in 1.18.1 (details not in notes) [Suspicious (player), 2026-03-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171720#p171720).
- Raid threat with Scorpid + Kill Command (post-1.18.1): "Scorpions poison deals bonus threat. Kill command on top of that hits very hard"; fixes: Blessing of Salvation on the pet, swap to a wind serpent, or put BM hunters in a Tranquil Air group [Atreidon (player), 2026-04-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174014#p174014); or "dismiss the pet and recall it right after to reset all its threat" [Lane13, 2026-04-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174020#p174020).
- Before Feign Death put the pet on **follow + passive** or you stay in combat: "You have to put pet on follow just before you feign death and trap" [Annoying (player), 2023-04-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42584#p42584); `/script if UnitAffectingCombat("player") then CastSpellByName("Feign Death"); PetFollow(); PetPassiveMode();else CastSpellByName("Freezing Trap");end` [Annoying, 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108933#p108933).
- Off-tanking: "BM hunter is usually top DPS throughout leveling dungeons ... the pet can serve as a clutch off-tank" [SvenS2, 2026-04-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173875#p173875); nobody in the sources claims pets tank raids.
