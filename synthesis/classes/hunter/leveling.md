# Hunter — Leveling guide (1–60)

Covers the consensus Beast Mastery leveling path, the Survival-melee alternative, pet choice while leveling, talent order, skill training, playstyle, professions, farming and hardcore notes. Pet mechanics in detail: `pets.md`. Endgame respec targets: `marksmanship-ranged-dps.md`, `beast-mastery-ranged-dps.md`, `survival-melee-dps.md`.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Talent names changed at CC2 (2024-11-01), CC3 (2025-08-15) and 1.18.1 (2026-03-20); every talent order below says which tree it was written on.

## Which spec

- **Beast Mastery — consensus**: "BM is much better in my opinion for leveling" [Lavrute (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106293#p106293); "Definitely BM. Imp revive and Intimidation are incredible in particular. With the 8 first defensive talents you nearly double your pet's survivability. It's safer in HC, and also faster in terms of xp/hour" [Steakhouse (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106303#p106303); "BM is the best spec (across all classes) for leveling" [Ragetto (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106305#p106305); "BM Hunters are still the best at leveling imo, and most leveling hunters I see are BM" [SvenS2 (player), 2025-05-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130862#p130862); "Whatever the changes, pure BM for leveling is imo always the go to, with around lv50 being your respec threshold" [SvenS2, 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171464#p171464); "It's not about speed or power, it's about how chill you can play it. BM is the let me watch this video while I auto-attack and have my pet deal with the target" [SvenS2, 2025-08-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149103#p149103). Dissent: "you can level effectively in any spec" [Steakhouse, 2025-02-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118591#p118591); "paladins chain pulling 4-5 mobs at once, mages aoe level and warlocks are just equally as powerful killing machines" [Jiav (player), 2025-08-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149091#p149091). On the CC3 kit one player felt the pet "squishier" [Novelus (player), 2025-08-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148983#p148983) — Endurance Training/Thick Hide were nerfed in CC3 and restored in 1.18.1 (staff).
- **Survival (melee) — alternative, weak before 40**: "Leveling : Weak before 40, but still playable" [Laysson (player), 2025-05-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131127#p131127); "levelling is boring before level 40. At level 40 you get the 'untamed trapper' talent ... After lvl 40 survival melee becomes very fun" [Vladis (player), 2025-08-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143781#p143781); "My advice is to level as BM or Marksman instead and respec Survival at 40" [zer0g3 (player), 2025-05-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131122#p131122); "Just leveled from 1 to 45 as survival ... it feels amazing ... BUT! The spec only really starts to feel really natural past 40" [Upsetlobster (player), 2025-05-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131298#p131298); "Survival still isn't good for leveling, not until you get some much deeper talents" (post-1.18.1) [SvenS2, 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171498#p171498). Advantages listed: highest Agility/Stamina, in-combat traps to escape, Deterrence, Improved Feign Death, AoE, and "Their defense skills can always be kept at the maximum level" [Terryleelolorz (player), 2025-04-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125145#p125145).
- **Marksmanship**: "go marks if you want strong ranged attacks" (Steakhouse); no one recommends it over BM for leveling; petless MM leveling is "completely useless ... During leveling, the pet (for any spec) is not just about damage, but also about tactics" [Ahinevich (player), 2026-03-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172610#p172610).
- Respec cost: "Your first two respecs are cheap, so don't worry about min-maxing" [SvenS2, 2025-08-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146760#p146760) vs "If changing talents and buying skills wasn't so expensive ... I'd love to experiment but simply cannot afford it" [funeh (player), 2025-02-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118631#p118631).

## Survival (melee) talent orders

- CC2/CC3 tree: BM rows 1–2 first (Endurance/Thick Hide/Improved Mend Pet), then Survival to Untamed Trapper at 40 with Lightning Reflexes; Lacerate (CC3) arrives "at level 30 I think" (Vladis). "In the end, you must go at least 34 points into the survival tree to deal good damage" (Vladis).
- Pre-CC2 melee order (obsolete talents, kept for history): 14 Improved Raptor Strike, 16 Savage Strikes, 21 Parry/Deflection, 22 Deterrence, 27–32 Improved Aspect of the Monkey, 34–39 Improved Wing Clip, 37 Surefooted, 40 Counterattack [Xudo (player), 2023-04-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41903#p41903). Counterattack no longer exists.

## Pet while leveling

- Boar: "Boars are a good go to. Boar Charge helps them quickly pile up some threat ... Boars also have a wide diet" [SvenS2, 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171447#p171447); "For leveling, Boars and Carrion Birds are generally your best choice" [SvenS2, 2025-05-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131377#p131377).
- Bird with Screech (owl/eagle/bat/carrion bird): "Owl/eagle/bat is the best pet for leveling simply due to Screech generating a disgusting amount of single target threat ... AoE threat for all mobs in pet's melee range ... once you're in your 50s, you're going to wish you got an owl" [Borefficz (player), 2023-08-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55429#p55429) (boars have a 90% damage modifier vs cats 110%, owls/bats 107% — same post); "A bird with Screech has no match for nonstop pull and AoE aggro" [Kerenis (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171466#p171466); "Screech is still very powerful ... between eagle/screech and spider to run away in case you have to" (HC) [Jiav (player), 2025-08-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148017#p148017).
- Fast cat (Feral Mountain Lion, Hillsbrad): Dash, Bite + Claw (SvenS2, 2026-03-20). Scorpid Death Flayer (lvl 11 Durotar): "Tanky as hell, 1.2 attack speed and scorpid poison slaps" [Plagues2 (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171448#p171448) (Petopia says 1.6 — SvenS2). Raptor for damage [Hemphiz (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171441#p171441). "Literally any Hunter pet works well" [Daoc2001 (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171463#p171463). Early on "pets have stupidly OP damage at low levels so you won't have issues with threat" (Borefficz).
- Level-10 quest chain: "Taming the Beast" (three temporary tames) → "Training the Beast" in the capital teaches Beast Training, Feed Pet, Revive Pet [Maesus (player), 2023-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39636#p39636); High Elves get it from Damilara Sunsorrow in Alah'thalas (misnamed "Taming the Beast"), turning in to Halduron Bringwing [Mekk (player), 2025-08-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143531#p143531) — one player still could not get it (Ghysal94); fallback: Goldshire/Kharanos/Dolanaar quest givers (Mekk). Keep Growl on autocast; feed to green; loyalty comes from XP while fed (`pets.md`).

## Skill training

- Trainers: Steady Shot ranks at 20/30/40/50/60 (baseline from 1.18.1; talent rank 1 at 20 before), Aimed Shot at 20 (baseline since CC2), Aspect of the Wolf ranks track Hawk's levels and "The 7th rank ... is also acquirable out in the world" [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102967#p102967); Aspect of the Viper at 56 (1.18.1); Beast Lore at 24 (Kerbees). Bows for undead: Orgrimmar weapon master [Kairion (player), 2023-07-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54919#p54919); hunters can learn every weapon except maces and wands [Qixel (player), 2021-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9770#p9770).
- Ranks players skip: Arcane Shot ranks once Steady is available (pre-1.18.1: "stop learning new ranks of it as soon as you have access to Trueshot" [Steakhouse, 2024-01-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83337#p83337)); Aimed Shot ranks (pre-1.18.1: removed from bars — Vrag); keep rank 1 Arcane Shot as a cheap Coordinated Assault trigger ("always used lvl 1 arcain shot to trigger the pet buff" [Plagues2, 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171488#p171488)); rank 1 Viper Sting for dispel baiting. Post-1.18.1 Arcane Shot scales with weapon damage, so train it again (Lane13).
- Ammo: train the vendor ammo of your bracket; "Being in a zone cleaning up green elite quests felt rough when they didn't have the current level ammo" [Trollrayzac (player), 2025-02-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121131#p121131); a database search `https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?items=2.3` lists leveling bows/guns/crossbows [basednoob (player), 2025-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124714#p124714).

## Playstyle

- Pull: pet attack (explicit key, not a macro), Hunter's Mark (skip on trash that dies fast — Jinxes), Serpent Sting, Auto Shot; a rank-1 Arcane Shot or Steady Shot to proc Coordinated Assault (CC3+); "Steady Shot is one of your main sources of damage. It's worth using if you won't pull aggro from the pet. Avoid spamming it too much though to prevent going oom" [Lane13 (player), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171531#p171531); Arcane Shot while kiting [Lane13, 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171561#p171561).
- Mobs at once: with BM defensive talents the pet holds 2 mobs while you Multi-Shot/Serpent spread (Steakhouse); "If you keep up your bite and charge ranks current they don't seem to have an issue" holding threat [Rinarch, 2024-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108602#p108602). Aggro problems only start "before you get MC weapons/gun" [Glavorli (player), 2022-11-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28262#p28262).
- Movement: Aspect of the Cheetah between quest mobs — "When your clearing quest mobs you don't need hawk on for the AP keep up Cheetah and just mow them down" (Rinarch); Cheetah dazes on damage.
- Downtime/mana: five-second rule — burst spells then auto-shoot; "You can send pet and drink while pet kills mob" [amanagor (player), 2026-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166261#p166261); "of the falcon" gear (int+agi) if oom [Springboards (player), 2026-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166247#p166247); spirit gear ("Slap on some spirit gear, and you'll have 0 down time" — SvenS2, 2026-03-20); Aspect of the Viper only at 56 ("useless for leveling" [Grizb37 (player), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171558#p171558)). Contested: mid-level mana "substantially WORSE" [Solveit (player), 2026-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166243#p166243) vs "there is not much to use mana on a mid-level hunter" (Springboards).
- Damage curve: "Until midgame hunters are incredibly OP but pet becomes less and less relevant" [Akos1896 (player), 2025-11-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160216#p160216) (contested by Solveit); "your pet may be equal to or out dps you until your mid 50s" [SvenS2, 2025-11-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160692#p160692).
- Melee-Survival leveling rotation (2023, pre-CC2 but the structure survives): "Immolation Trap or Explosive Trap - Pull mob with pet and land him on trap - Raptor Strike on cd - Wing Clip ... Refresh trap => pet passive => feign death => immolation or explosive trap"; AoE: Explosive Trap first, iron grenades [Therrius4121 (player), 2023-04-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41891#p41891); tank yourself with Aspect of the Monkey, use a slow 2H staff, "melee kite with Wing Clip like warriors do" (Xudo, 2023). After 40 (Untamed Trapper) traps drop in combat.
- Dungeons: pet on passive; Eyes of the Beast + Stay for ledges (SvenS2); Feign Death drops threat; do not need on melee weapons.
- Kiting elites in the open world is "much harder" than on retail but possible (Concussive Shot, Cheetah, Growl off) [Allwynd01 (player), 2022-09-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26325#p26325).

## Stat priority and gear while leveling

- Agility, stamina, then intellect/spirit for downtime; "of the falcon" pieces (Springboards). Weapon DPS of the ranged weapon is the main damage stat; e.g. a Hillsbrad quest crossbow at 16.6 DPS at level 38 [Throgmar (player), 2025-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124666#p124666). Melee Survival: slow 2H with agility/strength.
- Key upgrades named: Beastmaster's (T0.5) pieces at 60, Maraudon ring (soloable), AV ring, Rhok'delar quest (works). Quiver ranged haste from early on.

## Route, professions, farming

- Route: the sources give no zone-by-zone hunter route (**gap**). Turtle custom zones with special pets: Thalassian Highlands (Mana Wyrm level 6–7, Crimson Lynx), Gillijim's/Lapidis Isle (48–53 crabs/cats/gorillas/serpents), Tel'Abim (53–60 cobras/gorillas), Gilneas (39–45 wolves/bats/spiders) — community wiki `https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Hunter_Pets`.
- Professions: Engineering + Mining for iron grenades as an interrupt/AoE ("at the end of a dungeon iron grenade are 7%-10% of my total damage") and Alchemy + Herbalism for HC healing potions; First Aid mandatory [Therrius4121, 2023-04-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41891#p41891); "Alchemy for HC is important because of Swiftness potion" (Xudo); Engineering for Thorium ammo (a non-engineer "has to buy Thorium Shells from the AH" [Maesus, 2023-04-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40458#p40458)); Skinning pairs with beast farming (Bigsmerf).
- Farming at 60: Silithus scorpids/worms near Cenarion Hold (rugged leather, buff-food meat, vendor greys) [Bigsmerf, 2024-04-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92700#p92700); Naxx/ZG trash "way better gold" than DM tribute [amanagor, 2025-02-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118907#p118907); DM tribute solo works ("jumped down and FD (you need to be out LOS) and if Cho'rush is priest i just resetted" [Mecanno (player), 2023-10-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71791#p71791)) but selling the chest loot "is 100% against TOS" [Drubarrymooer (player), 2023-10-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=68920#p68920) and Cho'rush's priest loadout cannot be drained (bug 8327, FrankFankledank); DME lashers from 54 as Survival (amanagor, 2025-05-14); Devilsaur/solo dungeons as on vanilla [Gelinho (player), 2025-08-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145712#p145712).

## Hardcore differences

- Take the BM defensive talents first: "It's safer in HC" (Steakhouse, 2024-11-04); Improved Revive Pet for in-combat revives.
- Pet: bird with Screech for AoE threat, or a spider "to run away in case you have to" (Jiav, 2025-08-24).
- Alchemy for Swiftness Potions (Xudo); First Aid.
- Tracking is unreliable across custom map seams: "Just died as 42 HC attacked by 2 Org guards not showing at all on my detect humanoids" [Styopa1 (player), 2023-08-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57979#p57979).
- Do not tame ZG bats/rare pets in HC ("it's just very difficult and risky if you're HC" [Steakhouse, 2023-11-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78816#p78816)).
- Melee-hunter HC: asked in 2022, answered only with a link to the 2020 guide (t4098) — **gap**. Whether a pet tamed at 60 levels with HC or normal XP — asked, unanswered [Myth (player), 2020-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5691#p5691).
