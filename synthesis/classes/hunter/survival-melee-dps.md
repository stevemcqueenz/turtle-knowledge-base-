# Hunter — Survival — Melee DPS (raid / dungeon / farming)

Turtle's melee hunter. Survival was rebuilt as a melee tree in Class Changes 2 ("CC2", 1.17.2, live 2024-11-01), tuned in Dec 2024 / Mar 2025, changed again in Class Changes 3 ("CC3", 1.18.0, live 2025-08-15: Rapid Strikes removed, Lacerate added, Vicious Strikes nerfed) and touched lightly in 1.18.1 (live 2026-03-20: Lacerate buff, Surefooted dual-wield hit, Vicious Strikes damage restored, Alone Against the World). Pets in `pets.md`; leveling in `leveling.md`; PvP in `marksmanship-pvp.md` (Survival PvP is a short section there).

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20).

## Overview

- **Role**: melee physical DPS with fire-trap AoE; keeps its ranged kit for emergencies ("A Hunter is still a Hunter, even specialized for close combat, your ranged playstyle remains fully available whenever you need it. Albeit significantly lower damage" [zer0g3 (player), 2025-05-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131245#p131245)).
- **Strengths (sources)**: "one of the best aoe class rivaling warlocks and mages while having pretty much comparable single target" [Barwat1 (player), 2025-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125834#p125834); "especially strong cleave rivalling if not eclipsing warriors" [Bigsmerf (player), 2025-08-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143785#p143785); in-combat Freezing/Frost Traps ("its ace in the hole is its utility ... will save wipes many a times" [Steakhouse (player), 2025-04-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127584#p127584)); benefits from melee buffs/weapons the ranged specs cannot use ("Windfury, strength totem, battle shout, and blessing of might" [Cabum (player), 2025-07-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141781#p141781)); "Survival is much better than MM [for farming] because at some point your gear just gets too strong and your pet will never keep aggro" (Barwat1); good defence skill while leveling and highest Agility/Stamina of the three specs [Terryleelolorz (player), 2025-04-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125145#p125145).
- **Weaknesses (sources)**: mana — "Surv Hunter already had problems with mana, now he becomes OOM in just 35-45 seconds, even with a paladin and a sham in the group" [Sarthrethal (player), 2025-08-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147658#p147658); "im drinking every pack" [Grawerok (player), 2025-10-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153885#p153885); fire traps are spells — "Traps count as spells, so you might lack heavily in the damage department in early raids, where fire resist is just leaking out" (Bigsmerf), "half of MC and BWL bosses have full immunity to fire damage" (Grawerok); threat — "forget about your pet holding aggro as Survival" (zer0g3), "Survival quite struggles with threat issues" [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159); almost no raid utility ("in raids survival hunters bring exactly zero utility" [Goblinlover (player), 2025-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132137#p132137)); Tranq Shot from melee is unreliable (Barwat1); competes with warriors/rogues for weapons and with druids/shamans for leather [SvenS2 (player), 2026-04-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173875#p173875); weak before level 40 (see `leveling.md`).
- **Content**: raids — **favored** hunter raid spec from ~Apr 2025 to the shutdown ("Surv with switching on MM for some boss (Kel`Thuzad and Kruul)" [Thricew (player), 2025-11-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158027#p158027)); dungeons — favored ("survival hunter will be good in 5-man dungeon" [Hhplayer (player), 2025-05-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131301#p131301)); solo farming/DM tribute — favored ("its your pet that does the pulling, and you then kill using explosive trap ... with surival spec letting you place them in combat" [amanagor (player), 2025-02-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118911#p118911)); leveling — weak before 40; PvP — "melee fights right now is who smoke the other first, but harder for you without a stun ... Ranged: you are as crap as a warrior without charge" [Laysson (player), 2025-05-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131127#p131127); hardcore — one 2022 question with no answer (t4098).
- **Community standing**: **favored for raid DPS but contested as a concept**. For: "Survival is far from a meme spec, it is performing to the highest of levels with one survival hunter getting to the top 10 of best hunter dps in naxx" (Barwat1, Apr 2025); "Survival was quickly approaching the troubling level of almost as much DPS as a Mage" [FrankFankledank (player), 2025-08-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147702#p147702); "melee hunter spec outputs twice as much as MM with a 1 button macro" [Kerbees (player), 2025-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159405#p159405). Against: "Now surv can't compete with mage and other popular dps specs with the same gear" after CC3 (Sarthrethal, −25% in BWL); many ranged mains resent it ("we are 'forced' into melee if we want to be useful at raids" [Calli (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156416#p156416); "Remove Hunter Carve Ability" thread t15877, 64 posts). Staff (Dec 2024): "Survival excels at handling bigger groups of enemies" [Jamey (staff), 2024-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460).

## Stat priority

- **Agility first** — Lightning Reflexes converts 100% of agility to melee AP on top of the 1 AP/agi baseline ("Lightning reflexes make the current Hunter gear also very good for melee hunter" [Juzam (player), 2024-10-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104588#p104588)); "survival scales really well with agility which is a bread and butter hunter stat" (Bigsmerf); melee hunters also want Strength and benefit from "certain druid and shaman leather gear" (SvenS2, 2026-04-03).
- **Melee AP** also scales fire traps (Untamed Trapper): a Naxx-geared melee hunter "will get 2K4+" MAP; Explosive Trap ≈ 787 hit + 147/tick + 144 sting ticks = "1516 damage per target (252 dps per target)" over 6 s [Juzam, 2024-10-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104588#p104588); Immolation Trap "300+ fire" per tick deep SV [Vladis, 2025-08-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146445#p146445), "1.3k+ dmg immolation traps" [Hctwowfan (player), 2025-09-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151131#p151131).
- **Hit**: melee hit cap matters; the tree gives Surefooted 3% (+3% for dual wield since 1.18.1); T3.5's heavy +hit "is for Surv, since surv spec dont use ZG enchant, goes for +8agi on head/legs. And no hit on weapons" [Cabum (player), 2025-06-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134728#p134728). The exact 2H/DW melee hit caps on Turtle are not stated by a Turtle source (a 2021 vanilla-era guide gives "Dual-Wield Melee Hit Cap (12%)" [Yutilk/Caperfin (player), 2021-10-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12933#p12933) — vanilla baseline, pre-CC2).
- **Crit**: Savage Strikes 6% + Killer Instinct crit damage; Lacerate needs a crit to be usable.
- **Weapon**: 2H "by far the better choice" (zer0g3, May 2025) — "one auto attack out of two will be instead a raptor's Strike and will not be subject to Glancing" (Juzam); after CC3 Mongoose Bite hits with both weapons and "two one-handed weapons is now almost on par with a 2h-er" [Thricew, 2025-08-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147693#p147693); 1.18.1 adds DW hit. Bot default: 2H. "Weapon skill has no impact for pvp" but "Orc is the supreme race for survival" (axe skill) [Gildark (player), 2025-06-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137032#p137032). The Eye of Nerub "does get to be melee Survival's best in slot" [FrankFankledank, 2025-04-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126982#p126982).
- **Spell power** helps stings/chili with Stinging Nettle only ("sp cant buff traps but it can stings and chili" [Foteinos (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156533#p156533)) — niche; a "demon hunter" spell-power/Blade of Eternal Darkness build exists but its author says "the mm hunter will have more dps in t2 - t3 gear" [Twinking (player), 2025-01-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117348#p117348).
- Ranged weapon slot: still equip a real bow/gun for pulls, Tranq and Feign-trap kiting (zer0g3); throwing weapons are a leveling-only shortcut.

## Single-target rotation (CC3/1.18.1 kit)

Pre-pull: Aspect of the Wolf (rank 7 from the world); pet on the target first; Hunter's Mark does **not** help melee ("The ability specifies ranged attack power" — zer0g3); Serpent Sting is applied for free by Mongoose Bite (Stinging Nettle).

1. **Immolation Trap** under the target (in combat via Untamed Trapper) — the single-target fire DoT; refresh when it expires ("your immo trap fire dot starts to do some rally nice damage because it scales from MAP" — Vladis). On fire-immune bosses skip traps entirely (Grawerok).
2. **Raptor Strike** on cooldown (queues on the next swing; Vicious Strikes −1 s).
3. **Mongoose Bite** on cooldown ("Mongoose Bite is on 4sec CD" with Vicious Strikes — Laysson) — refreshes the Stinging Nettle sting.
4. **Lacerate** whenever usable (after a crit; 10 s cd), from the side of the boss where possible (+15% post-1.18.1); many judge it weak ("at the very bottom of the dps meter, it is lower than a PET" — Sarthrethal; "Lacerate is underpowered af for its cost" [Hctwowfan, 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149725#p149725)) but on-cd use is what the players did ("It procs on CD and it was used on CD" — Sarthrethal). Skip it when mana < 30%.
5. **Wing Clip** between strikes only if a proc effect is wanted ("Phantom Strike" triggers Windfury/HoJ/Dragonbreath Chili [Yutilk, 2021-10-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12933#p12933) — vanilla-era guide, mechanics not re-verified on the CC2+ kit) and mana allows.
6. Stay **behind** the target ("make sure to always stay behind the mob you're fighting" [SvenS2, 2025-08-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146760#p146760)); Feign Death when threat is high (Improved Feign Death 8% — staff raised it because "Survival quite struggles with threat").
7. Rapid Fire on cooldown (melee haste since CC3).
8. Melee-hunter macro pattern for the strikes (one button, Raptor + Mongoose): "/run if (not PlayerFrame.inCombat) then AttackTarget() end /cast Raptor Strike /cast Mongoose Bite" [Ihavenoclothes (player), 2024-02-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87692#p87692); the Kerbees "1 button macro" remark confirms this is how raiders play it.

The old dodge-based rotation (Mongoose only after a dodge, Counterattack after a parry, "you need to be tanking in melee" [Roxanneflowers (player), 2020-07-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7029#p7029)) is **obsolete since CC2**.

## Multi-target / AoE

- 2–3 targets: **Carve** on cooldown (10 s, 5 targets, 10-yd cone; shares cd with Multi-Shot) plus Explosive Trap; Immolation on the main target.
- 4+ targets: **Explosive Trap** first (stings everything hit), Carve on cd, refresh Explosive when it expires; "Survival is the best AoE" claims above. Fire-resistant/immune trash (early raids) — fall back to Carve and Multi-Shot. "Traps do decent damage ... but they are mana expensive" (Roxanneflowers, old kit — mana concern still true: Resourcefulness, Untamed Trapper −20%, T3 8-set).
- Solo/farm: pet pulls, trap kills — DM tribute "you could do it naked, its your pet that does the pulling, and you then kill using explosive trap" (amanagor); DME lashers from 54 (amanagor, 2025-05-14). DM tribute is bugged with Cho'rush's priest loadout [FrankFankledank, 2025-02-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119109#p119109).

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Deterrence** (SV row 3): +25% dodge/parry 10 s — "a flat 50% chance to COMPLETELY NEGATE any melee attack ... really great to have in PvE" [Bigsmerf, 2025-07-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142264#p142264); use when you pull threat.
- **In-combat traps**: Freezing Trap for an add, Frost Trap for kiting — "Being able to Freeze Trap or Frost Trap in combat is incredibly useful and will save wipes" (Steakhouse); trap dropping "sometimes bug out and just won't drop" (Bigsmerf).
- **Mana**: the spec's limiting resource — Resourcefulness 5/5, Untamed Trapper −20% (staff), T3 8-set; mana potions/tea; Aspect of the Viper (56+, 1.18.1) out of combat instead of drinking, never during the rotation (Wolf is needed for melee AP). "Used to have significant mana issues before they reduced the costs. Now it's just mana issues" (zer0g3).
- Feign Death every 30 s if the tank is weak; Improved Feign Death 2/2.
- Pet: Mend Pet is pointless mid-fight; let the pet be a threat sponge on adds (Screecher) or an extra DPS (wolf/raptor).

## Role strategy

- Position on the boss's side/rear; move out for Tranq Shot only if the raid has no MM hunter ("It's very unreliable to tranq as a melee hunter as you need to move out of the deadzone" — Barwat1) — bots: leave Tranq to MM hunters when present.
- Threat: you generate "quite a bit of aggro" (SvenS2); Feign Death, Deterrence, stay behind; pet cannot tank for you.
- Pulling: Survival's pull range is nerfed (no Hawk Eye) — let another class pull or use the pet.
- Raid buff placement: the melee group (Windfury, Battle Shout, Blessing of Might) benefits Survival, unlike MM.

## Gear, enchants and consumables

- Weapons: best 2H available (Halberd of the Bronze Defender is "a pre bis item [that] deals the ~same damage than Nerubian slavemaker" [Calli (player), 2025-10-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155899#p155899)); The Eye of Nerub (Naxx) BiS melee weapon for Survival (FrankFankledank); melee-only items: Hand of Justice, Wrath of Cenarius/"wot cloak", Chronobreaker ("good items that are melee only and kinda op" — Kerbees). Ranged stat stick still needed for pulls/Tranq.
- Enchants: +8 agi head/legs (not ZG) [Cabum, 2025-06-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134728#p134728); 2H +25 agi; melee weapon enchants/oils proc with Wing Clip.
- Sets: T3.5 Ravenstalker 5-set (+5% Quick Shots haste, −1 s Carve/Multi) "was made for Melee hunter in mind" (Cabum); T1 8-set +15% Carve; T2 8-set Detect Weakness; T3 8-set mana on strikes (staff, 1.17.2 itemization).
- Consumables: melee consumes that MM cannot use — strength potions, Winterfall Firewater, Elixir of Demonslaying (Juzam; Cabum); Dragonbreath Chili and Shadow Oil scale with spell power for the Nettle build (Twinking); mana potions/tea; Sigil of Ancient Accord trinket "good just for packs ... for nettle spec" (Foteinos).

## Common mistakes

1. Trapping fire-immune bosses / wasting mana on traps in MC/BWL (Grawerok, Bigsmerf).
2. Ripping threat by standing in front / not feigning; expecting the pet to hold aggro (zer0g3, Jomm thread).
3. Leveling as Survival before 40 and complaining ("Not very good early levelling. Likely better off playing BM" — Bigsmerf).
4. Skipping Untamed Trapper or Lightning Reflexes (Vladis).
5. Rolling need on every melee weapon and leather piece (SvenS2, Idontcareatall).
6. Going oom by spamming Lacerate/traps on trash that dies in seconds; not drinking between packs (Rd: "I have to drink after every pack").
7. Trying to Tranq from melee and losing the enrage window (Barwat1).
