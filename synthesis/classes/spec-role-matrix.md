# Spec × role matrix — every class, every role (Turtle WoW, as of 1.18.1)

Cross-class index built **only** from the nine class folders in this repo (`synthesis/classes/<class>/README.md`, the 47 spec×role playbooks and the nine `leveling.md` files) plus their YAML twins in `structured/classes/`. Conventions: [`../CONVENTIONS.md`](../CONVENTIONS.md). Machine-readable twin: [`../../structured/classes/spec-role-matrix.yaml`](../../structured/classes/spec-role-matrix.yaml).

No vanilla assumptions are used to decide viability: Turtle rewrote the class kits three times inside the extraction window (Class Changes 2 = 1.17.2, staff post 2024-10-12, live 2024-11-01; Class Changes 3 = 1.18.0, staff post 2025-07-25, live 2025-08-15; 1.18.1, staff post [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071) with revision blocks 2026-01-26 and 2026-03-19, live 2026-03-20), and made several non-vanilla roles real — Enhancement Shaman tank, melee Survival Hunter, melee-weaving Holy Paladin, Subtlety support Rogue, Firelock, Smite Priest. Where sources say a role does not exist for a class, this file says **not viable** and cites the source, rather than inferring it.

**Roles** are the six named in the task: tank, healer, melee DPS, ranged DPS, PvP, leveling. **Standing** is one of `favored` (the community's default pick for that class+role), `alternative` (a real, played choice with a stated reason to pick it), `niche` (a small, specific use-case or a handful of practitioners), `not viable` (sources say the class cannot fill the role). **Agreement** is `consensus` / `single source` / `contested` per `CONVENTIONS.md`.

**Era labels**: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). The forum extractions end between 2026-04-06 and 2026-04-18; the servers went offline 2026-05-15, so *every* post-1.18.1 standing claim in this repo rests on at most eight weeks of player reaction and no published raid logs — see [Merged gaps](#merged-gaps-across-all-nine-classes).

### Cross-class role summaries

- **Tanks**: four classes have a real tank role — Warrior (Protection), Paladin (Protection), Druid (Feral/Bear) and, uniquely to Turtle, Shaman (Enhancement "warden"). Rogue tanking exists but is staff-curbed and niche; Priest caster-tanking is two anecdotes; Mage "tanking" is kiting; Hunter and Warlock cannot tank. The 1.18.1 tank picture is a deliberate re-shuffle: Bear threat was cut (Maul −15%, Savage Bite −12% threat modifier, "Feral Bears have been overly prominent in their threat output, leaving little room for other tanks to shine" — dev comment quoted in `druid/README.md`), Prot Paladin got late-raid scaling, Prot Warrior got Shield Slam R5/Defiance help with "a more comprehensive review … planned for the future" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **Healers**: five classes — Priest (Holy), Shaman (Restoration), Paladin (Holy), Druid (Restoration), plus Priest/Discipline explicitly *not* a healing archetype post-CC2. Turtle's distinctive healer is the melee-weaving Holy Paladin.
- **Melee DPS**: Warrior, Rogue, Paladin, Druid (Cat), Shaman (Enhancement) and — non-vanilla — Hunter (Survival).
- **Ranged DPS**: Mage, Warlock, Hunter, Priest, Shaman (Elemental), Druid (Balance). Paladin lost its ranged option when Shockadin was removed in 1.17.2.
- **PvP**: every class has at least one documented PvP build; the classes the sources most often call strong are Paladin, Shaman and Rogue, and the ones most often called weak are Warrior, Hunter (until 1.18.1) and Druid. This ranking is **contested everywhere** and per-class PvP dampening numbers for 1.18.1 were never published.
- **Leveling**: every class has a favored leveling spec; only Warrior, Rogue and Mage have genuinely contested leveling choices.

**1.18.1 effects.** Tank: Maul threat −15% and Savage Bite threat −12%, the change that drives the post-patch complaint "Bear sucks because threat was the only thing going for them and now it's gone … Now there's literally no reason to bring a bear tank over warrior or paladin" — [Noephix (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173102#p173102), answered sarcastically with "Bear is so unplayable that out of the fastest 10 Kara guilds only 8 of them are using a bear as maintank" — [Velanyr (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173162#p173162) (**contested, single detailed source on each side, no logs**). Melee DPS/Restoration: **no direct 1.18.1 talent or ability changes** beyond the shared threat nerf; the standing shift is relative — "OK, in 1.18.1 feral is no longer the worst DPS spec - moonkin is" — [Noephix (player), 2026-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172923#p172923).

BM is the uncontested leveling spec — "BM is the best spec (across all classes) for leveling" — [Ragetto (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106305#p106305) — but a raid problem, because "Only Spirit Bond actually scales with gear. **1.18.1 effects.** The 1.18.1 rewrite is the largest of any class here: Aimed Shot became the MM row-3 keystone with Experimental Ammunition and the Lock and Load capstone; Trueshot Aura and Steady Shot went baseline; Bestial Wrath returned and Kill Command was reworked to a TBC-style capstone (revised 2026-03-19 to 80% of pet AP); Baited Shot was removed; Survival got Alone Against the World and had Vicious Strikes restored. Reception is thin and split: "I like the updated BM. It's much, much better now" — [SvenS2 (player), 2026-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171498#p171498) vs "MM will remain at the bottom of the list in PVE" — [Baek (player), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171538#p171538). PvP is the one role that clearly improved, because pets ignore the new dampening: "Hunters are strong now, BM pets aren't affected by dampening so BM + Survival are doing pretty well" — [Froghijab420 (player), 2026-04-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175013#p175013) (**player observation; no staff confirmation that this is intended — see gaps**).

## 4. How to read a cell

1. A **standing** is a community judgement, not a measurement. Only two numeric claims in this whole matrix are backed by anything resembling data (Patchwerk/turtlogs rankings quoted second-hand, and one warlock dummy test); everything else is argued.
2. A **contested** marker is never to be resolved by the reader. Present both sides with their dates — several standings flipped when the kit changed, and at least one author (amanagor on Frost Mage) reversed himself.
3. **Staff statements outrank player claims** about intended behaviour, but staff never published: per-class PvP dampening numbers, crit/crush-immunity Defense values, stat weights, or any spec ranking.
4. If a role/spec pair is not in the matrix, no source in this repo treats it as a thing. Answer "the sources do not say" rather than reasoning from vanilla.
