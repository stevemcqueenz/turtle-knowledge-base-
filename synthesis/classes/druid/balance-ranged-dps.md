# Druid — Balance ranged DPS (Moonkin)

Playbook for the "Boomkin" caster DPS spec. Class Changes 2 (2024-10-12) gave Balance its defining **Eclipse** mechanic and a Moonfire/Insect-Swarm/Wrath/Starfire four-button rotation; 1.18.1 changed Eclipse's proc mechanism and reworked Balance of All Things and Moonfury, with the headline Eclipse proc-chance change landing in the 2026-03-19 revision of p=166071 — right at patch release, so post-1.18.1 forum reaction is thin (a handful of threads, most echoing the same author). Sources are unusually detailed for this spec: two long, well-reasoned single-author write-ups (Tarsis pre-1.18.1, Scattyau post-1.18.1-announcement) anchor most of the rotation and stat-priority detail below and are corroborated by many shorter replies.

## Overview

- **Role**: ranged caster DPS via Wrath/Starfire with Moonfire/Insect Swarm DoTs, built around the **Eclipse** capstone that grants a temporary damage buff to one school after landing a proc from the other. Brings raid utility: one of only two classes that can decurse, Moonkin Aura (crit aura), Innervate, and (with talents) a battle resurrection.
- **Strengths**: strong raid utility (see above); competitive-to-strong leveling and questing damage via Entangling Roots CC (see `leveling.md`); scales well with gear/crit into T3/T3.5 — "Really coming online around AQ40 gear and then really starts to ascend when you start hitting set bonuses in T3 and T3.5" — [Noobikscube (player), 2025-06-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134706#p134706).
- **Weaknesses (consensus)**: Eclipse's RNG punishes rather than rewards — "every single RNG talent feels like you have a ticking clock that is wasting away while you're locked into casting something else, or they're inconsequential until you just happened to NOT get them" — [Scattyau (player), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159149#p159149); mana-hungry rotation with more buttons than most casters; DoT ticks and Hurricane **do not proc trinket effects** (Scythe, Clackora, Sigil) even post-1.18.1's new Fortune stat — [Auralys (player), 2026-03-23/26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172021#p172021); no on-demand burst cooldown, unlike every other caster DPS spec.
- **Content**: raids (contested — see standing), leveling/solo (strong, see `leveling.md`), PvP (see Role strategy and `balance-pvp.md`), dungeon AoE trash (weak — see Multi-target).
- **Community standing — pre-1.18.1**: **contested**, split between "competitive with proper play" and "structurally bottom-tier." "Balance Druid DPS is bottom of the DPS tier... its literally bottom of the barrel, not even mediocre" — [Halfgeek9 (player), 2025-05-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131905#p131905), citing top Kara40 boomkin logs around 940 DPS against top-mage logs around 1200-1400. Counter: "the class scales incredibly well... anyone saying Boomkin is bottom of the barrel is misinformed" with logs showing "12th/93 highest among all casters and 34th/230 across all dps" for the week cited — [Noobikscube (player), 2025-06-08/10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134706#p134706). A full-BIS Boomkin at perfect (impossible) 100% Eclipse-proc play was calculated to still lose to an average-geared Fire Shaman on a target dummy — [Scattyau (player), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159149#p159149) (**single detailed source, heavily agreed-with in replies but not independently log-verified**).
- **Community standing — post-1.18.1**: worse, largely because the Starfire-cast-time nerf inside the reworked Balance of All Things landed alongside the Eclipse proc-chance change: "we lost 20% spell haste on Starfire for 9% crit that doesn't scale eclipse - 3 second starfires feel like complete shit" — [Froghijab420 (player), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172180#p172180). "1.18.1 moonkin rework was supposed to be a slight buff — but ended up being ~15% dps nerf to already low-tier spec, resulting in moonkin becoming the worst 'proper' dps spec in the game. Below even cat" — [Noephix (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173102#p173102) (**single source, uncorroborated with logs**). A T3-geared main "parked" their boomkin in Orgrimmar after trying the new rotation once — [Trevor89 (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173305#p173305).

## Talent build

No source decodes a full 60-point list; the shape given by the deepest post-CC2 write-up:

- **~35-38 points committed to core Balance talents** (all damage/hit/crit/range/cast-time/Moonkin Form/Balance of All Things/Eclipse talents) with only **2-3 flex points**, split between **Gale Winds** (raid AoE-slow utility, no personal damage) and **Guidance of the Dream** (interrupt/pushback avoidance — even fully talented it "still brings you to 76% during an enraged owlkin proc, so you still get pushback") — [Scattyau (player), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159149#p159149).
- **~13 points into Restoration for Genesis** ("+5/10/15% periodic damage/healing," reduced from an 18-point requirement in an earlier revision) — same source.
- **Moonfury** is criticized as a weak early investment ("flat damage early in the tree on a tier with 10 other points") that a redesign should fold into Moonkin Form — same source; **official 1.18.1 change increased its bonus to 4/8/12%** ([Jamey (staff), 2026-01-26 revision](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)), so this specific complaint predates and is partially answered by the patch.
- **Leveling/PvP variants differ** — see `leveling.md` and `balance-pvp.md` for their own point orders (30-0-21 caster-kite PvP build, 19-11-21 melee-hybrid PvP build, and various leveling links).
- No source links a decoded talents.turtlecraft.gg calculator string for the current 60 raid build (**gap**, same limitation noted throughout every other Druid role and the paladin playbook).

## Stat priority

- **Crit over Haste, strongly**: "1% crit equal to around 4-5% Haste. It might even be worse" because Haste only affects Starfire (the two DoTs don't scale with it, and Wrath's cast time is already GCD-capped at 0 haste under the pre-1.18.1 spell-haste GCD rules), while Crit "triple-dips by increasing damage per cast, proccing Nature's Grace for far better haste, and also increasing the Eclipse multiplier" — [Scattyau (player), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159149#p159149) (a correction noted in the same thread: the spell GCD floor is 1.0s base / 0.5s talented, so Haste still benefits Wrath somewhat, but the crit-over-haste ranking stands). Item design nonetheless loads Balance gear (Atiesh, T3 8-set) with Haste — same source, calling this a mismatch.
- **Hit**: notably hard to reach cap from set gear alone — "on the entire 9 piece T3 set, there is only 4% spell hit. Unless you go out of your way to use Loetheb's staff or the regalia staff from AQ40, I'm not even sure we CAN get to hit cap" — [Dalijon (player), 2024-11-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110618#p110618); several top players "sacrific[e] a few % of hit (even on bosses) for better crit chance" as a deliberate trade — [kierova (player), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135108#p135108). A post-1.18.1 complaint asks for Natural Weapons to give 10% spell hit instead of the melee-oriented split it currently has — [Jizzus (player), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172261#p172261) (**single source suggestion, not an implemented change**).
- **Spell power**: baseline scaling stat, no numeric weight given (**gap**).
- **No numeric caps given for spell penetration** (**gap**, unlike the general vanilla-baseline figures cited elsewhere; not from Turtle sources).

## Single-target rotation

The rotation is **consensus in shape**, contested only in feel/reward:

1. Apply and maintain **Moonfire** and **Insect Swarm** on the target (both extended to 18-second durations under Class Changes 2).
2. Chain-cast **Wrath** while "fishing" for a Lunar Eclipse proc, or **Starfire** while fishing for a Nature Eclipse proc, depending on which Eclipse buff you currently hold or are chasing: "Previously, the balance druid rotation was keeping up moonfire (1st button) and pressing starfire (2nd button)... The current balance druid rotation is moonfire (1st), insect swarm (2nd), wrath (3rd), starfire (4th)" — [kierova (player), 2025-06-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134911#p134911).
3. **Eclipse proc mechanics** — this changed twice:
   - Pre-1.18.1 (1.18.0 rework, per `synthesis/turtle-vs-vanilla-timeline.md`): Wrath crits/hits had a 30% chance and Starfire a 50% chance to proc their Eclipse buff, worth "10% + 60% of spell crit" bonus damage rather than a flat 25/30%.
   - **1.18.1 (official, revision dated 2026-03-19)**: "Damage from Wrath now has a 40% chance and Damage from Starfire now has a 60% chance to proc their beneficial effects" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-03-19 revision of p166071](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
   - Each Eclipse buff lasts 15 seconds with a separate 30-second cooldown per school; both cannot be active simultaneously.
4. **Stopcasting**: because Eclipse windows are short and the proc can land mid-cast, "optimal gameplay" frequently means cancelling a cast already in progress to switch to the newly-buffed spell rather than let the current cast finish and waste part of the window — described as the single biggest "feel" complaint about the spec both pre- and post-1.18.1 — [Tarsis (player), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135044#p135044); [Auralys (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172020#p172020).
5. **Balance of All Things** (3 points as of 1.18.1, down from 5): Wrath cast on an Insect-Swarm'd target refunds 10/20/30% of its mana cost; Starfire gets a +3/6/9% (revised 2026-01-26 from an initial 2/4/6%) crit-chance bonus against a Moonfire'd target — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23/26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071). Community verdict pre-revision: "There is so much to hate here... they recharge randomly and unpredictably, with a 30% [chance] on each DOT tick" — [Scattyau (player), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159149#p159149).
6. **DoT-refresh tension**: because Insect Swarm and Moonfire have near-identical durations and both accumulate procs, optimal play sometimes clips one DoT early to refresh it under an active Eclipse bonus, desyncing the two — same source.

## Multi-target / AoE rotation

- **Hurricane** is the AoE tool (channeled, spell-power scaling, cooldown halved under CC2); weak for fast-dying open-world trash because mobs often die before Eclipse/DoT setup pays off — "Mob killing in open world, they die too fast to fully benefit from Eclipse... you spend the majority of your time with a -30% damage debuff" — [Tarsis (player), 2025-06-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135251#p135251).
- Same author notes trash-pack dotting requires first spamming Wrath to trigger the Lunar Eclipse before Moonfire application pays off, by which point trash is often already dead — same source.
- No raid-AoE priority list or target threshold given by any source (**gap**).

## Cooldowns and resources

- **No on-demand burst cooldown** — repeatedly cited as the spec's single biggest structural gap versus every other caster: "unlike others, we don't even have a real burst cooldown to stabilize our damage or create a strong window" — [Auralys (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172020#p172020); "any other class have dps cooldowns on demand that they can use at the correct time during a fight... the moonkin doesnt" — [Tarsis (player), 2025-06-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135618#p135618).
- **Mana management**: consumables carry the spec through fights (see Gear below); Sylvan Blessing (100% mana regen while casting after a kill, 15s) helps while leveling/questing; Innervate is a group-support cooldown, generally given to healers rather than self-used in raids per general Druid etiquette threads (see `restoration-healer.md`).

## Role strategy

- **Raid utility**: one of only two decurse classes; brings Moonkin Aura (crit) and, if talented, battle rez and mana return to a healer. Several posters argue the spec's raid value should be judged on this utility, not the damage meter alone — "By design the Druid identity IS THE support class... he supports other classes with auras and spells, if a key player dies he can resurrect him, restore mana to a healer that ran out, removes curses and poisons" — [Tarsis (player), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135044#p135044) (**contested** — opposing view: "Boomkin in raid goes pew pew, and damage meter numbers good so moonkin in exceptional spot" is dismissed by the same author as insufficient justification).
- **PvP**: see `balance-pvp.md` for the dedicated dot-kite build and rotation; Eclipse is widely reported as far weaker in PvP than PvE because sustained standing-still casting invites interrupts and burst — "if you want to heal and is not arcane damage boost time your opponent doesn't have to choose between interrupting your damage or wait for your heal/cc to go off, he just interrupts your nature school and you are stuck with the arcane school that now does 30% less damage" — [Tarsis (player), 2025-04-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126009#p126009).
- **Dungeon healing flex**: full Balance can competently heal 5-mans if geared for it — "Every druid spec can heal without a problem up to late game dungeons so long as you have spare gear for it. As a balance druid you get caster gear anyways so that isn't even a problem to worry about" — [Bigsmerf (player), 2025-04-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126323#p126323).

## Gear, enchants and consumables

- **Idols**: two named options, contested trade-off. A BoE idol giving "17% Moonfire damage that scales with Spellpower" is calculated as roughly a 4% total-DPS gain (Moonfire being ~22% of total damage) and is called "BIS forever" despite a low market price (~20g at time of posting); the alternative, **Ebb and Flow** (reduces Starfire cast time), "feels great casting faster Starfires" but is calculated as weaker in practice since Starfire is "our 4th best spell" — [Scattyau (player), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159149#p159149).
- **Consumables** (consensus list for raid mana sustain): Major Mana Potion, Nordanaar Herbal Tea, Dark Rune, Innervate, Nightfin Soup, Mageblood Potion — [kierova (player), 2025-06-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134911#p134911); corroborated (mana/dark rune/tea) by [Jizzus (player), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172261#p172261) for the post-1.18.1 patch.
- **T3/T3.5 tier set**: hit budget is thin (only 4% spell hit across the full T3 set — see Stat priority); the T3.5 5-piece Wrath-mana-refund synergy with Balance of All Things "increases this lacklustre effect by 50%, so you can feel so little, slightly more! Drop your Wrath down to 37 mana, 3 times!" (sarcastic) — [Scattyau (player), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159149#p159149).
- **Twink/leveling gear list**: a dedicated "49 Balance Druid — Twink talents and Gear list" thread exists (t16360) but was not read in depth for this playbook — **gap, follow up if a twinking guide is needed**.
- **No full pre-raid BiS list found in sources** — gap, consistent with every other Druid role covered here.
- Community (non-staff) tools: "Balance Druid DPS Calculator" Google Sheet and a `pbrigade.gitlab.io` gear-planner link with a pre-built Balance Druid BiS export are both linked from the wiki (`extracted/wiki/articles/Druid.md`) — community-maintained, not staff-verified.

## Common mistakes

- Standing still to hard-cast Starfire in PvP — invites interrupts and burst; use the hit-n-run pattern from `balance-pvp.md` instead.
- Letting a cast finish after an Eclipse proc lands mid-cast instead of cancelling and switching (a real, if unintuitive, DPS gain).
- Gearing pure spell-power/haste while ignoring the crit-over-haste relationship and the thin hit budget on tier gear.
- Treating trash-pack Moonfire/Hurricane as free AoE — most trash dies before the DoT/Eclipse investment pays off.
- Assuming DoT ticks or Hurricane will proc on-hit trinkets (Scythe, Clackora, Sigil, Fortune-stat items) — they don't.
- Judging the spec purely on the damage meter without accounting for decurse/utility uptime, which several sources argue is core to the spec's actual raid value.
