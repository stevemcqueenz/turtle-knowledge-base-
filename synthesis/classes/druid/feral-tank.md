# Druid — Feral tank (Bear/Dire Bear, dungeons and raids)

Playbook for a Bear-form tank on Turtle WoW, built from the Druid forum (f=42), the Class Changes 2 staff posts (2024-10-12) and the 1.18.1 patch notes (p=166071, revised 2026-01-26 and 2026-03-19). Sources are thicker for pre-1.18.1 stat priority and mechanics than for post-1.18.1 play; the 1.18.1 threat nerfs are official but the forum's reaction to them (post-2026-03-20) is a single vocal author corroborated by several short replies, not an independent re-test. Mark accordingly throughout.

## Overview

- **Role**: main tank or off-tank in Bear/Dire Bear Form, threat from Maul (auto-attack-timer rage dump), Swipe (AoE), Savage Bite (rage dump/snap threat) and Demoralizing Roar/Faerie Fire (Feral); Frenzied Regeneration and Barkskin (Feral) for self-healing/mitigation cooldowns.
- **Strengths (consensus, pre-1.18.1)**: cannot be polymorphed, hexed or disarmed; immune to Nefarian-style effects that only key off "warrior" mechanics (though Nefarian's Bellowing Roar/druid call specifically counters bears — see Common mistakes); scales threat with gear because Maul/Savage Bite/Swipe are damage multipliers rather than warrior-style flat threat add-ons — "we still have the most physical mitigation and if geared right can actually output the most threat-per-second" — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58705#p58705). "100% viable. I've MT'd almost the entirety of MC, BWL, AQ40, and most of Naxx" — [Drubarrymooer (player), 2023-09-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64890#p64890). "It's completely viable and sometimes optimal in basically all content from dungeons to raids... Druid always has been and always will be a great tanking class" — [Bigsmerf (player), 2023-08-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58715#p58715).
- **Weaknesses (consensus)**: no −10% magic-damage stance (unlike warrior Defensive Stance), no reliable interrupt against stun-immune bosses (Bash is a stun, useless on stun-immune targets), cannot stance-dance out of fears (Ony/Nefarian fights), and Nefarian's druid-specific call "pretty much entirely prevents druids from tanking him" — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58705#p58705). Cannot parry or block, so Defense rating is worth less than for a warrior/paladin ("Defense is 40% less effective here since you can't parry nor block" — [Sunken (player), 2025-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117585#p117585)) and true crit/crush immunity is unreachable the way it is for shield tanks.
- **Content**: dungeon main tank (near-universal), raid main/off-tank through Naxx-equivalent content (**contested for the hardest fights only**: "For Naxx... there are some select fights where bears have their place... I have not heard of a druid main-tanking all of Naxx but that doesn't mean it doesn't exist" — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58705#p58705)); solo/open-world tanking of elites.
- **Community standing — pre-1.18.1**: **favored/consensus-viable**. Bear was explicitly called out by staff as overperforming going into 1.18.1: "While both Bear and Cat are performing well, Bear even more than we imagined" — [Jammyzz quoting the staff Dec-2024 patch notes (player), 2024-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113723#p113723).
- **Community standing — post-1.18.1**: **contested, trending negative**, single detailed source with several agreeing short replies. "Bear sucks because threat was the only thing going for them and now it's gone... Bears take over 2x the damage while having only around ~20% more HP than warriors... can't parry or block and is subject to crits and crushing blows... Bear snap threat is way worse than paladin's... Now there's literally no reason to bring a bear tank over warrior or paladin" — [Noephix (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173102#p173102). Counter-argument: "Bear is so unplayable that out of the fastest 10 Kara guilds only 8 of them are using a bear as maintank" (sarcastic — i.e. still 8/10) — [Velanyr (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173162#p173162), to which Noephix replies "In 3 months not a single one will. Takes time for people to catch up with bear nerfs" — [Noephix (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173218#p173218). "Feral is dogshit in PvP mate... they nerfed Bear into the ground so its basically just a punching bag, and does zero damage, because of the Savage Bite nerf back in December" — [Grizb37 (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171995#p171995).

## Talent build

No source publishes a full decoded post-1.18.1 point list (talents.turtlecraft.gg links only, not decodable from text — same gap as the paladin playbook). Points named as mandatory/near-mandatory by multiple sources:

- **Omen of Clarity** and **Furor** — take by ~level 20/25 while leveling and keep at 60; "Always go Omen of Clarity first by Lv20, then into Furor by Lv25. The clearcasting procs very often and gives you tons of threat when tanking from all the free swipes you get in bear form" — [Captain4k (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165907#p165907).
- **Feral Instinct** (threat), **Thick Hide** (armor), **Blood Frenzy** + **Primal Fury** (attack speed/rage on crit) — cookie-cutter tank staples across every build cited.
- **Sylvan Blessing** — flagged as an underrated must-have for tank sustain: "A lot of people ignore [Sylvan Blessing] because it is worded weirdly. But it's a really important talent that helps keep you from going out of mana when you heal yourself in between mobs. This minimizes your down time" — [Captain4k (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165907#p165907).
- **Ancient Brutality** — **contested value**. Rage-on-dodge and (pre-2024-12-20) a vampirism component made it strong for tanking dodge-heavy fights, but the December 2024 rework removed the bear vampirism and left only the rage generation, which the community judged undertuned: "removing the Vampirism part of it completely kills the talent for Bears... perhaps 2/4/6 would be better" — [Jammyzz (player), 2024-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113723#p113723). Still wanted for the dodge synergy: "You actually want a fair amount of dodge because of ancient brutality" — [Drubarrymooer (player), 2025-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115691#p115691).
- **Feral Adrenaline — avoid**. Near-unanimous criticism: "3 talent points for 45% dodge every 30 seconds is not good, also dodge doesn't push crits and crushes off the table, if you have 95% dodge then every attack from the boss that lands will be either a Crit or crush... No druid I know takes this talent" — [Jammyzz (player), 2024-12-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112877#p112877). "Bears can't get crit immunity, so the talent smoothes out peaks of critical damage" is the lone defense — [Xudo (player), 2024-12-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112876#p112876) — but even the staff's Dec-2024 dodge-chance buff (15/30/45% → 20/40/60%) didn't fix the complaint, since "the dodge chance was not the issue, the long 30 second ICD is" — [Jammyzz (player), 2024-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113723#p113723), echoed [2025-05-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133058#p133058).
- A meta tank build link (**talent trees unchanged by 1.18.1**, so this is still valid): [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=BSAaAI-FAYYRaDQQIFQB-Ao](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165907#p165907) — [Captain4k (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165907#p165907).
- Older (2022-2023, pre-Class-Changes-2, **superseded**) hybrid tank/heal builds like Kairion's "cookiecutter druid tank build" ([p28953](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28953#p28953)) used a different talent tree shape and should not be used as-is.

**Patch validity**: talent tree unchanged by 1.18.1; only the Maul/Savage Bite threat *modifiers* changed (see Cooldowns/threat below), so pre-1.18.1 talent choices are **still current** for 1.18.1.

## Stat priority

**Contested — no single agreed ordering**, but the framework used by the top reply in the main stat-priority thread is widely echoed:

> "I tend to look at stats in 2 broad categories: 1) Physical Mitigation — armor, dodge, defense. 2) Threat — hit, crit, attack power, haste. Depending on the task at hand you need to (re)balance those two... in 5-person instances lean heavier toward mitigation... in a raid where you have your own 5 healers you can slack on mitigation and go heavier into threat." — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60018#p60018)

Specific point callouts (mostly consensus):

- **Armor**: "iconic bear stat but once you get half-way decent gear you'll be drowning in it. Hitting the armor cap is quite easy to do on Turtle" — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60018#p60018); target "around 11-12k Armor" then deprioritize — [Inovatu (player), 2025-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117477#p117477).
- **Stamina**: always good, general survivability — consensus across all threads.
- **Defense**: **contested/low priority**. "Defense seems much too high on your list. It does very little for Druids (we don't block and we don't parry) making it an incredibly expensive way to boost dodge and add a little crit mitigation" — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60018#p60018); "Defense is 40% less effective here since you can't parry nor block" — [Sunken (player), 2025-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117585#p117585). Situational exception: stack it for fights that hit hard with limited healing — "Some bosses in bwl can hit you very hard if you aren't careful, so does twins in aq40 and bosses with limited healing like Erennius, Maexxna or Loatheb" — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2025-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115706#p115706).
- **Hit**: important for threat; effective melee hit cap on Turtle is **8%** — [Inovatu (player), 2025-03-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121590#p121590) — reduced to **5% with Natural Weapons (3/3, +3% hit)** — [Eyeburn (player), 2025-03-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121689#p121689). Hitting above cap "does absolutely nothing" and cannot counteract dodge/parry, which are governed by defense rating vs. weapon skill, not hit rating — [Atreidon (player), 2025-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121385#p121385).
- **Haste/attack speed**: **undervalued by newer players but rated highly** by veterans, because Maul rides the (slow, 2.5s) auto-attack timer: "Haste is completely missing and actually one of the best threat-generation stats... item budget wise 2% melee haste is equivalent to 1% melee crit and you can get a lot more threat out of 2% melee haste than 1% melee crit" — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60018#p60018); "Attackspeed is really, really good for Bears because more Attackspeed the faster you hit with Maul" — [Inovatu (player), 2025-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117477#p117477).
- **Crit**: better than assumed — "Crit seems too low on your list... melee crits double the damage and all our main threat skills are implemented as multipliers... a crit actually means double threat for us. And the synergy with Primal Fury is always nice" — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60018#p60018).
- **Agility/Dodge**: wanted for Ancient Brutality synergy but **do not itemize for raw dodge** — "Dont go for Dodge, go for Agility if you have to" — [Inovatu (player), 2025-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117477#p117477).
- Fight-dependent split, two schools cited by the same thread: general trash/most bosses → **hit until cap, agility, stamina**; hard-hitting/low-healing bosses → **hit until cap, defense, stamina, armor** — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2025-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115706#p115706).
- Community stat-weight tools (unofficial, not staff-verified): the **VanillaRatingBuster** addon with Taladril's community stat-weight spreadsheet, plus an updated Turtle-specific version by "AngryCat" shared in the Discord druid channel — [krylorandrax (player), 2025-01-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116362#p116362). A generic wowhead/warcrafttavern vanilla bear-tank guide was called "dated" once Class Changes 2 landed — [Kethmandoo (player), 2025-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117463#p117463), responding to [Dr Thrax (player), 2025-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115719#p115719).

**Not found in sources**: exact crit-immunity or crush-cap Defense values for bears on Turtle (the paladin playbook's 440/420-Defense number is a vanilla-baseline figure, not druid-specific, and no source ports it to bears).

## Single-target rotation

No source gives a numbered priority list as explicit as the paladin playbook's; the pieces named across threads are:

1. Pull with **Faerie Fire (Feral)** rank 1 (cheap, high threat, applies armor debuff) — [Nuninho32 thread advice, Terrydavis (player), 2026-01-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165608#p165608).
2. **Maul** on cooldown/auto-attack timer as the primary rage dump and main threat tool.
3. **Savage Bite** as a rage dump and snap-threat cooldown, but note the pre-1.18.1 warning that it is Bear's only instant single-target burst tool and is now on a longer cooldown (6s → 9s as of Dec 2024) — "if just one of these misses, waiting 9 seconds for another will feel godawful" — [Jammyzz (player), 2024-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113723#p113723). **1.18.1 additionally cut Savage Bite's threat modifier by 12%** (official, [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)).
4. **Demoralizing Roar** to reduce incoming physical damage and (in PvP/against stealthed rogues) pull enemies out of stealth — [Xudo (player), 2025-10-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156325#p156325).
5. Downtime rage → **Enrage** (armor debuff on self, separate from the rage-gain buff since Class Changes 2) when threat is secure.

## Multi-target / AoE rotation

- **Swipe** is the core AoE threat tool; it scales with 4% of Attack Power as of the Nov-2024 rework (down from 8% at launch of Class Changes 2, then 6%, "This was 8% originally, this just feels unwarranted" — [Jammyzz (player), 2024-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113723#p113723); final value per `synthesis/turtle-vs-vanilla-timeline.md`: 8% → 6% → 4% AP).
- Faerie Fire (Feral) each new add to keep the armor debuff up and help hold aggro before Swipe catches up.
- Demoralizing Roar on pull for the raw damage-reduction; it also functions as an AoE stealth-detector against rogues.

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Frenzied Regeneration**: converts rage to health (10 rage/s, 6/7/8% of Stamina per point of rage by rank); cooldown is 6 minutes pre-1.18.1 (reduced from 3 in the original Class Changes 2 pass, then only trimmed to 5 minutes in Dec 2024, which the community judged too small a fix: "no one will really feel a 1 minute cooldown decrease... reduce cooldown back to baseline 3 minutes or add 10% healing" — [Jammyzz (player), 2024-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113723#p113723)).
- **Barkskin (Feral)**: learned at 44/52/60; next 5/10/15 melee hits take 50% less damage, 20% movement slow, 12s duration, 10-minute cooldown; a niche panic button, not part of the normal rotation.
- **Ancient Brutality**: passive rage generator on dodge; treat as sustained resource generation rather than an on-demand cooldown (see Talent build for its contested tuning history).
- Rage management: no source gives a numeric "pool" target; the consensus is to keep Swipe/Maul/Savage Bite flowing and not to worry about rage starvation once Omen of Clarity/Furor/Blood Frenzy are talented.
- **Official 1.18.1 threat change**: Maul's threat modifier is reduced by 15% and Savage Bite's by 12%, explicitly to rein in bear threat dominance — "Feral Bears have been overly prominent in their threat output, leaving little room for other tanks to shine" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071). No compensating buff shipped in 1.18.1.

## Role strategy

- **Threat**: the class's biggest edge is that its core threat tools scale multiplicatively with gear (unlike warrior flat-threat abilities), so threat improves faster than expected as gear improves — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58705#p58705). Post-1.18.1 this edge is explicitly smaller (see Cooldowns).
- **Mechanical gaps to plan around**: no interrupt against stun-immune casters, no −10% magic-damage stance, cannot stance-dance fears; Nefarian's bear-specific call effectively removes druids from that fight — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58705#p58705).
- **Raid role competition**: in guilds stacked with geared/experienced warriors, bears are more likely to off-tank than main-tank purely for social/optimization reasons rather than mechanical ones: "geared and experienced warriors sign up as tanks and you have to find a spot" — [Elesion (player), 2023-08-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58705#p58705). Post-1.18.1, Noephix argues this has hardened into "no reason to bring a bear tank over warrior or paladin" — [2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173102#p173102) — but the same thread notes 8/10 top Kara guilds were still running bear maintanks at the time of posting — [Velanyr (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173162#p173162).
- **PvP note**: feral bear PvP damage was hit hard by the same Savage Bite threat/damage trims, and a talent that previously granted +15% Savage Bite damage was "silently removed" per player report — "they even silently removed a talent to give 15% dmg to Savage bite" — [Wosator (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171996#p171996) (**single source, unconfirmed by an official patch line — flag as unverified**).

So the best current bear set in game by far is... PvP t3. Because pvp sets are naturally made with proper stats. ## Common mistakes

- Itemizing hard for raw Defense rating instead of Hit/Armor/Stamina, given Druids get no parry/block benefit from it.
- Chasing raw Dodge% instead of Agility-as-a-side-effect; Ancient Brutality rewards dodge procs but is not worth gearing dodge directly for.
- Taking **Feral Adrenaline** — near-unanimous "don't take it" advice due to the 30-second internal cooldown gutting its value.
- Treating Savage Bite as a spammable threat button post-1.18.1; its cooldown (9s) and reduced threat modifier make it a supplement, not the core rotation.
- Pulling without Faerie Fire's armor debuff, or without Demoralizing Roar against physical-heavy packs.
- Assuming bear can tank every fight equally: Nefarian and other fear-heavy/stance-dance-dependent encounters are specifically worse for bears — plan to swap tanks or specs for those pulls.
