# Druid — Leveling (1–60), incl. Hardcore

Feral is the consensus leveling spec; balance is the alternative; resto leveling is not recommended by any source. This file also covers the Turtle-specific quests (Bear Form, Swift Travel Form) and Hardcore notes.

Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Leveling-relevant kit changes: Moonkin Form at 30 (batch 2, 2024-10-12); Ferocious Bite R1 at 24; Feral Swiftness talent (batch 2); Reshift at 60 (1.18.0); form-to-form shifting (2025-03-30). 1.18.1 changed nothing for leveling (Maul/Savage Bite threat only).

## Which spec

- **Feral (consensus)**: "feral druids dont scale with gear, much at all. Your forms will carry you level to level especially once you get cat form, you hit lightspeed" [Biggamehuntard (player, long-time druid), 2026-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170843#p170843) (1.18.1-announced era); "Undisputed kings of soloing content" [Elesion, 2024-01-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83440#p83440); "Druids aren't too talent dependent, you could throw all ur points into resto and still lvl fine as a cat/bear" [Portholes (player, HC 60), 2023-11-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77120#p77120).
- **Balance (alternative)**: "Currently leveling a druid and switching back and forth between balance and feral. Overall feral still seems a bit stronger at my level (40+) but balance is still viable" [Fornow (player), 2024-11-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109616#p109616); balance is slower (t17441 per summary); mana is the issue at low level ("the mana issue is just to bad" [Rhohin, 2024-11-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109564#p109564)); pre-batch-2 balance leveling to 51 reported by [Daoc2001, 2024-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108779#p108779).
- Bear for leveling: only for elites/dungeons (t18036 per summary); "for solo leveling stamina is not important at all so you might want to keep 2 gear sets" [Terrydavis (player), 2026-01-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165608#p165608).
- Race: Tauren for tanking (5% stamina, War Stomp) [Captain4k, 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165907#p165907); Night Elf "everything else" [Turboman, 2026-01-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165651#p165651); "matters more who you wanna play with faction wise" (Biggamehuntard) — **contested/preference**.

## Balance leveling build

- "The best balance spec for leveling is pretty much going to fill out the entire balance tree minus: gale winds (hurricane slows attack speed), improved nature's grasp (just the 1 in initial point is enough), only 2/3 into natural shape shifter. This leave you with 5 points which could well go into Furor from Resto tree. The instant rage upon entering bear from is very useful for emergency stuns/charges. Going deeper into restoration tree seems like a trap ... Sylvan blessing is amazing, when chain killing enemies it has near 100% up time" (Fornow 2024-11-24; 40-level allocation posted as an image).
- Solo-grind full balance `https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=piaZYKFYZLFQBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA%3D` [Bigsmerf, 2024-11-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109551#p109551); Imonobor solo/group links [Imonobor, 2024-11-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=109622#p109622) ("Get a goblin brainwashing device to swap").
- Studbucket's 13 resto / rest balance (image) with Templar85's tweak: "Instead 3/3 Owlkin Frenzy put 1 and have 2/2 Sylvan Blessing. 2/3 Reflection and 1 point on Swiftmend. It is a good burst heal which might needed while leveling" [Templar85, 2025-01-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116254#p116254).
- "Moonglow" hybrid (no Moonkin) for solo + dungeon flex: Daoc2001 2024-11-20 (Genesis synergy with DoTs and HoTs; prioritize Spirit).

## Skill training

- "before you reach lvl 40 only learn the skills you need! this is very important or you will go to 0 money" (Terrydavis 2026-01-19); "fairy fire lvl 1 is good enough for pulling"; train the HoT: "start hard packs and bosses with your own HOT on you". No source lists specific ranks to skip beyond Faerie Fire R1 (gap; `(vanilla baseline, not from turtlecraft.gg)`: keep Cat Form, Claw/Shred, Rip, Rake, Bear Form, Maul, Swipe, Demoralizing Roar, Rejuvenation, Regrowth, Healing Touch current; skip Wrath/Starfire ranks when feral).
- Omen of Clarity must be cast (30-min buff) — "he has to activate it, its not passiv like in wotlk" [Inovatu, 2023-07-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54938#p54938).

## Playstyle

- Cat: front-Shred is not possible; Claw + Rake + Rip/FB on single mobs; "1v1 front shred mobs in cat" was Hctwowfan's phrasing for the Improved-Shred question — sources otherwise assume Claw in front. Tiger's Fury + Blood Frenzy as available.
- Multi-pull (10–40): "range pull 2-3 mobs with a DoT like Moonfire or Insect Swarm, switch to Bear and Swipe to cleave em down" (Chev03); HC version: "herd up 3/4 mobs and swipe em down in bear or 1v1 front shred mobs in cat" (Hctwowfan). Macro `/startattack` + `/cast Swipe` needs CleveRoid Macros (Captain4k).
- Balance: "open with wrath from max range and before the projectile hits another wrath or starfire, insect swarm, move backward during global cool down, moonfire, move backwards" (Fornow); "pull with 2-3 Wrath casts, Moonfire, Insect Swarm & then melee it ... A yellow mob dies right before our updated 18 sec DOTs finish" (Daoc2001).
- Elites: Nature's Grasp (castable in bear, Xudo); War Stomp + Regrowth (Jammyxx, PvP file); Bear + own HoT.
- Stealth on custom mobs is unreliable (t17529 per summary).
- Dungeon tanking while leveling: stamina set, Omen for free Swipes, Sylvan Blessing to avoid drinking (Captain4k); "make sure to play dungeons fast ... some of the newly added dungeons are extremely long for their level" (Terrydavis).

## Stat priority and key gear while leveling

- "feral druids dont scale with gear, much at all" — greens/quest rewards suffice (Biggamehuntard). Tank set: stamina; "bracer +9 stamina enchant only costs 1 gold ... cloak +70 armor is only 60s silver ... armor kits from leather working are also all under 1g" (Terrydavis).
- Level-50 class quest rewards are "pretty mediocre" [Zulnam, 2024-01-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83527#p83527).
- Balance: Spirit for no downtime (Daoc2001); Feralheart pre-raid set is SP/hit/crit (staff 2023-10-17, `balance-ranged-dps.md`).
- Trinkets for the 53+ stage listed by Schwarzschild (items 11832/20525/11819, t3754 per summary).

## Route (zones, dungeons, professions)

- "Grinding dungeons doesnt help you level faster. At all. Open world grinding and quests especially when rested and using tents ... will level you faster than any dungeon will, but if you do make sure to grab all the dungeon quests you can" (Biggamehuntard 2026-03-15). Contrast: tank-leveling through dungeons is the premise of Captain4k/Terrydavis/Chev03 (t23659) — **contested by goal** (speed vs. tanking practice).
- Professions: gathering for the "lazy lonely druid" (Xudo 2023-11-13); herbalism/mining Tauren bonus (Drubarrymooer 2023-05-01). Leatherworking armor kits (Terrydavis). Engineering for MCP was the old endgame reason (obsolete).
- **Bear Form quest** (level 10, Turtle-custom difficulty): tips in t15197 — roots + Wrath (per summary; no single quotable post extracted).
- **Swift Travel Form** questline (batch 2, [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102966#p102966): 'Mount Hyjal In Turmoil' at Loganaar): steps per [Deeno (player), 2025-05-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129590#p129590): 1. 'Mount Hyjal In Turmoil' (Loganaar, Moonglade → Nordanaar); 2. 'Shadowed Spectre' (west of Darkhollow Pass, Hyjal); 3. 'The Son Of Cenarius' (20 Bright Dream Shards to Keeper Remulos); 4. 'Tethered Memories' (Bough of Cenarius from Dreamstalker in Bough Shadow, Ashenvale; Crystalline Ray of Moonlight from Venethas in a cave north of Everlook; Jade Scale of the Dreamer from Solnius in Emerald Sanctum raid); 5. 'The White Stag'; 6. 'Woven Dreams' (10 Viridian Mushrooms, Jademir Lake, Feralas); 7. 'The Eternal Sleeper' (Seradane, Hinterlands); 8. 'Under the Vibrant Moonlight' (Twilight Grove, Duskwood). Requires an ES raid kill — a level-60 questline.
- Zones: no druid-specific route posted (gap).

## Hardcore differences

- "Don't go HC playing druid" (Sleeplust, t19665 per summary) vs. Portholes (HC 60) and Hctwowfan's HC build — **contested**.
- HC build: Feral to Feline Swiftness (21) for escape speed → Omen → feral; Improved Thorns optional for bear multi-pulls; skip Furor (Hctwowfan 2024-05-23).
- Survival tools: Nature's Grasp (Xudo), War Stomp + Regrowth (Jammyxx: "leveling up as HC, it saved me quite alot"), Travel Form, bear + HoT before hard packs (Terrydavis), 2 gear sets.
- Stealth unreliable on custom mobs (t17529) — do not rely on Prowl to skip.
