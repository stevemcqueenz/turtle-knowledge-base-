# Druid — Leveling guide (1–60)

Compiled from Druid-forum leveling threads. The dominant, near-unanimous recommendation is to level as **Feral (Cat, with detours into Bear for AoE/elites)**; Balance and a hybrid tank/heal build are viable alternatives with their own trade-offs. No source gives a full zone-by-zone route or a level-by-level skill-training table with exact gold costs, so those sections are thinner than the build/playstyle guidance — flagged as gaps below.

## Which build

- **Feral (favored/consensus)**: fastest solo leveling and the standard recommendation for anyone planning to tank dungeons along the way. "Grinding dungeons doesnt help you level faster. At all. Open world grinding and quests especially when rested and using tents... will level you faster than any dungeon will... The reason for that is simple: feral druids dont scale with gear, much at all. Your forms will carry you level to level especially once you get cat form" — [Biggamehuntard (player), 2026-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170843#p170843).
- **Full Balance (viable alternative)**: "Moonkin for me is the most enjoyable to level, because as long as your enemy is not immune to Entangling Roots, he is dead" — [Tarsis (player), 2025-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126107#p126107). Slower than Cat due to mana regen downtime but considered "nice" and can heal any dungeon along the way — [Ironbob (player), 2025-02-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119155#p119155); [Bigsmerf (player), 2025-04-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126323#p126323).
- **Hybrid tank/heal (dungeon-focused)**: recommended specifically for Hardcore or anyone planning to level primarily in dungeon groups, since Resto is hard to solo-level and tanks are always in demand: "I'd suggest leveling as tank if you want to make your life easier. It's very hard to level as Resto and tanks are in short supply" — [Cyrin23 (player), 2024-11-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107212#p107212).
- **Race — contested**: "Night elf is true druidic race, tauren is a gimmick introduced for balancing reasons. If you're a pvp minmaxer = then choose tauren, everything else = night elf" — [Turboman (player), 2026-01-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165651#p165651), countered by "Tauren is always the better option due to their racial ability that grants 5% extra Stamina, which are tanks most important stat. Tauren also have a much bigger bear form model" for a tanking build — [Captain4k (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165907#p165907). Night Elf's Shadowmeld is called out as a PvP-leveling perk ("its pretty funny casting starfire while shadowmelded") — [Biggamehuntard (player), 2026-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170843#p170843). **No consensus** — pick by role (Tauren for a dedicated tank, Night Elf otherwise/for PvP openers).

## Talent point order by level, 10–60

No source gives a single authoritative point-by-point table for the post-Class-Changes-2 talent tree; the pieces given, combined:

- **~Level 10-20 (Feral leveling path)**: put points toward the Cat Form movement-speed talent (Feral Swiftness/Feline Swiftness) as early as possible — "you wanna rush the additional movement speed in cat form talent as fast as you can, i think level 20/21ish is when you can get em" — [Biggamehuntard (player), 2026-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170843#p170843), echoed by an older (pre-CC2, structurally still directionally valid) build: "go feral to Feline Swiftness so you got a nice +30% cat movement speed at 21" — [Hctwowfan (player), 2024-05-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95535#p95535).
- **~Level 20-25**: pick up **Omen of Clarity** ("Then switch to balance to get Omen of Clarity asap" — same source) and **Furor** shortly after — "Always go Omen of Clarity first by Lv20, then into Furor by Lv25. The clearcasting procs very often and gives you tons of threat when tanking from all the free swipes you get in bear form. It also gives you free claws/shreds in cat form" — [Captain4k (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165907#p165907). A minority view delays Omen even further: "I don't think I grabbed Omen until I could grab the bleed->energy talent both" — [Biggamehuntard (player), 2026-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170843#p170843).
- **~Level 24+**: consider **Sylvan Blessing** (1 point) for self-sustain between fights — [Chev03 (player), 2026-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165791#p165791); an older thread also suggests 1/3 Owlkin Frenzy plus 2/2 Sylvan Blessing over a full 3/3 Owlkin investment while leveling as Balance — [Templar85 (player), 2025-01-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116254#p116254).
- **~Level 30**: Swift Travel Form becomes available via the questline 'Mount Hyjal In Turmoil' (started at Loganaar in Moonglade) — official baseline change ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102966#p102966)). "Once you receive travelform, reset and get omen" — [Turboman (player), 2026-01-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165651#p165651) — i.e. some players deliberately delay a full respec until Travel Form is in hand.
- **Beyond 30, decision point (Feral)**: choose between AoE bear farming (3/3 Improved Thorns pre-CC2 equivalent, herd-and-Swipe play) or single-target Cat front-shredding, and skip/delay Improved Shred if not front-shredding much — [Hctwowfan (player), 2024-05-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95535#p95535). Note Bear's own damage doesn't come online until the rage-on-crit talent (~40+) and Savage Bite (54) — [Snkx (player), 2025-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122982#p122982).
- **Level 44/52/60**: Barkskin (Feral) is learned automatically with new ranks at these levels — official ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102966#p102966)).
- **Respeccing**: "you will respec at 60" is treated as a given for Balance levelers, who are told not to worry about filling out Vengeance/Nature's Reach or other endgame-only talents while leveling — [Tarsis (player), 2025-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126107#p126107).
- **No source gives a complete point-by-point table through 60** for any build — treat the above as anchors, not an exhaustive list (**gap**, matching the paladin playbook's equivalent finding).

## Skill training priority

- **Watch your gold before level 40**: "before you reach lvl 40 only learn the skills you need! this is very important or you will go to 0 money" — [Terrydavis (player), 2026-01-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165608#p165608).
- **Faerie Fire rank 1 is sufficient for pulling** through most of leveling — same source.
- **No specific skipped-rank table** is given by any source (which ranks of Wrath/Starfire/Rejuvenation etc. are worth skipping) — **gap**.

## Leveling playstyle

- **Pulling and kill pattern (Feral)**: "range pull 2-3 mobs with a DoT like Moonfire or Insect Swarm" then "switch to Bear and Swipe to cleave em down" on lower levels for speed — [Chev03 (player), 2026-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165791#p165791). At higher levels/more geared play, **single-target Cat is reported faster than Bear AoE farming**: "You don't level in bear form unless you want to spend 5 times longer killing each mob" — [Snkx (player), 2025-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122982#p122982) (contested by players who enjoy the 3-mob-Swipe playstyle regardless of raw speed — [Hctwuckles/slak, 2025-03-19/23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123470#p123470)). Bear's practical uses while leveling: hard-hitting elites, quest objectives that need tankiness, and general survivability buffer — [Dr Thrax (player), 2025-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123476#p123476).
- **Downtime**: keep a self-HoT running before pulling hard packs or bosses — "you also want to skill the healing over time and start hard packs and bosses with your own HOT on you" — [Terrydavis (player), 2026-01-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165608#p165608).
- **Balance playstyle**: "hot yourself and fight in bear" for the earliest levels (before ~20) if geared for it, then root-kite with Entangling Roots on 2+ pulls while DoTing others; use Hibernate instead of roots against Beasts; keep mana topped with a spirit-focused build and, once available, cast Wrath more freely thanks to Turtle's ~70% spell-pushback avoidance ("I found that Twow have added 70% chance to avoid interruption. You can try to spam-cast Wrath" — [Xudo (player), 2025-02-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119170#p119170)).
- **Fastest-XP mode**: for players optimizing pure leveling speed, questing is explicitly deprioritized in favor of Cat-form mob grinding — "If you look to lvl up as quick as possible, then skip questing and go cat mob grinding" — [Dr Thrax (player), 2025-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123476#p123476), consistent with the "open world grinding and quests... will level you faster than any dungeon will" guidance above.
- **Macros**: a simple `/startattack` + `/cast <ability>` macro (auto-targets nearest enemy and starts the swing timer before the special) is recommended for every physical ability — [Chev03 (player), 2026-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165791#p165791), confirmed as needing a helper addon such as **CleveRoid Macros** to function on the 1.12 client — [Captain4k (player), 2026-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165907#p165907).

## Stats and gear while leveling

- **Feral**: "don't forget to spec into [Thick Hide] for the extra Armor. Healers will thank you and its generally useful. Many cat druids are squishy for missing out on it or forgetting to upgrade their items" — [Chev03 (player), 2026-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165791#p165791). One point in **Feral Instinct** is enough early, more only if threat is a problem — same source. If tanking dungeons while solo-leveling in gear that isn't stamina-heavy, consider keeping two gear sets ("for solo leveling stamina is not important at all so you might want to keep 2 gear sets" — [Terrydavis (player), 2026-01-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165608#p165608)). Cheap early enchants: a +9 Stamina bracer enchant (~1g) and a +70 Armor cloak enchant (~60s) are called good value for tanking, alongside cheap Leatherworking armor kits — same source.
- **Balance**: don't stress about matching "caster" leather (Int/Spirit) precisely — "pick anything with int/spirit" — [Tarsis (player), 2025-04-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126107#p126107).
- **Professions**: Alchemy is called out for mana-potion access on a Balance leveler ("if you like alchemy, mana potions are good to always have at hand, but whatever profession you feel like is fine" — same source); Herbalism/Alchemy also suggested for a tanking leveler's self-sufficiency — [Geojak (player), 2022-10-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26875#p26875). No source gives a single "best" profession pairing across builds (**gap**).

- **Spec choice matters more**: tanking is explicitly recommended over Resto for Hardcore/dungeon-leveling because "it's very hard to level as Resto and tanks are in short supply" — [Cyrin23 (player), 2024-11-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107212#p107212).
