# Druid — PvP (Feral flag carrier / Balance 30/0/21 / Restoration)

Battlegrounds, Blood Ring and arena play at 60. The sources treat druid PvP as one topic with three specs, so this is one playbook with three build sections. PvE playbooks: `feral-melee-dps.md`, `feral-tank.md`, `balance-ranged-dps.md`, `restoration-healer.md`.

Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). PvP-relevant kit changes: Frenzied Regeneration nerfed in batch 2 (2024-10-12: 6/7/8% stamina per point, 6-min then 5-min CD); consumables usable in forms (batch 2) and jujus/bandages (1.18.0); Barkskin (Feral) in form (batch 2); 1.18.1 adds class-dependent PvP dampening [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071); Blind became a physical debuff in 1.18 ("Before 1.18 you could abolish psn right after trinket and remove the blind. Now blind is a physical debuff" [Isvya (player), 2025-10-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157381#p157381)).

## Overview

- **Role**: flag carrier / point defender (bear), hit-and-run DoT kiter (moonkin 30/0/21), support healer (resto).
- **Community standing — contested, by role:**
  - Objective play: "Druid is the strongest class in the game for carrying flag in warsong, and so highly sought after in premades. Boomkin is also extremly strong in ab premades, and is also one of the best specs in the game for bloodring" [amanagor (player), 2026-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165799#p165799) (1.18.1-announced era); "10k+ HP, Frenzied Regen, double the armor of a warrior, and being able to shift in and out of forms to dispel movement debuffs makes them by far the best flag carriers in WSG and also very good point defenders in AB. Feral Charge has half the cooldown of warrior Intercept" [Jstansberry (player), 2023-10-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74040#p74040); "Feral is a top tier Blood Ring pick and hard counters most melees" [Gantulga (player), 2023-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75762#p75762).
  - 1v1 / kill power: "Feral Druid is C-tier in PVP. They can last a long time no doubt, but lack finishing power. And their Cat/Bear forms are lacking in generally useful abilities such as an interrupt" [Manletow (player), 2023-10-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73796#p73796); "The class has no meaningful cc and only has a delayed interrupt ... it cannot use pots/trinkets/etc without breaking form. These things alone make the class C-tier" [Mackylol (player), 2023-11-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76016#p76016) (pre-batch-2 — consumables-in-form has since changed); "Out of all three hybrids, druids are at the bottom of the list in pvp, moonkins especially - mediocre damage, no good def buttons, super weak against other casters. Every class has a spot in objective oriented teamplay" [Turboman, 2026-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165866#p165866).
  - Templar85's numeric tiers (1 worst – 5 best), pre-CC2 → CC2: Bear BG 5 / Arena 5* → BG 3 / Arena 3–4; Cat BG 2–3 / Arena 1–2 → BG 3 / Arena 2–3; Moonkin BG 3 / Arena 2 → BG 4 / Arena 3; Healer BG 3 / Arena 2–3 → BG 3–4 / Arena 3; Tree BG 1–2 / Arena 1* → BG 2 / Arena 1 [Templar85 (player), 2024-05-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95354#p95354) and [2024-11-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110263#p110263) (pre-1.18.1).

## Rotation / priorities

### Bear flag carrier (WSG) — sources' rules
1. Bear form, keep HoTs up before the pickup; Feral Charge to close gaps; shift out/in to clear snares ("shift in and out of forms to dispel movement debuffs", Jstansberry).
2. Frenzied Regeneration when focused — but "Be careful with that bearheal, it eats all you rage" (Frantsel); post-batch-2 it "is healing around 25% health" with full rage [Xfraz, 2024-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107713#p107713).
3. Barkskin (Feral) in form; Bash/War Stomp as interrupt (Jstansberry: "Bear has both Feral Charge and Bash to interrupt"; Manletow: "not reliable interrupts").
4. Travel Form to run when disengaged; Swift Travel Form questline (see `leveling.md`).
5. Bear damage is negligible — "Bear hits like a wet noodle ... paladin will cut through your armor with Holy Strike and casters nuke you down as bear" [Grizb37, 2024-05-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95356#p95356).

### Cat burst
Ravage/Shred openers on casters with high crit (Grizb37 2024-01-17); Pounce stun; shift to bear when focused ("in PvP Feral is forced into bear because everyone seems to focus you as cat", Grizb37 2024-05-21).

### Moonkin kite
Moonfire + Insect Swarm, Entangling Roots (outdoors), Wrath while kiting, HoTs on self, Nature's Grasp, Travel/Bear as needed (Turboman 2025-08-05). Moonfire damage reality check: "you'd a full spellpower build to deal 450-500 moonfire dmg with all enchantments" (Turboman 2025-11-22).

### Rogue opener survival (any spec, caster form)
"LAP, fairy fire, NG, Barkskin, rejuv, motw-regrowth (optional), moonfire/swarm and then create distance. I also keep crunchy bread for blind removal ... even if i perform this line of counter actions perfectly - the rogue (envenom one) can simply outdamage everything i do" (Turboman 2025-10-27); "Be in bearform or stealth if you expect to be attacked. Use orb of deception ... Use feral barkskin. Stun" (Frantsel). Trinket the Kidney Shot, then expect Blind (Isvya).

## Cooldowns

Frenzied Regeneration (5-min post-batch-2), Berserk, Barkskin (Feral), Feral Charge (15 s per Zahnfee), Nature's Grasp, Nature's Swiftness, Innervate for attrition, War Stomp, PvP trinket, LIP/FAP/consumables usable in form since batch 2 ("hendrishift has some built in macros" for the old restriction [Drubarrymooer, 2023-11-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76058#p76058)).

## Common mistakes

1. Staying in caster form when a rogue is expected (Frantsel; Thaladar's thread consensus "you need to be in bearform or you're dead").
2. Sitting in bear expecting to kill anything (Grizb37 2024-05-21).
3. Playing moonkin as a turret (Turboman).
4. Full spell-power moonkin gearing instead of stamina (Turboman 2025-11-22).
5. Skipping the PvP set bonus for higher-ilvl PvE gear (Frantsel; Turboman).
6. Emptying rage into Frenzied Regeneration while still needing Maul/Bash (Frantsel).
