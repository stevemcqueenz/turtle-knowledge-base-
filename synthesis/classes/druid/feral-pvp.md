# Druid — Feral PvP (Cat/Bear toolkit)

PvP-specific playbook for Feral Druid. Sources are **weak-to-medium**: one older (2024, pre-Class-Changes-2) "unkillable" toolkit thread whose talent build is superseded, plus post-1.18.1 case-study complaints that are detailed but single-author. Treat the tactical toolkit description (HoTs + bleeds + interrupts + dispel) as durable, and the specific talent build/power level as outdated.

## Overview

- **Role**: dual-form PvP — Cat Form for burst/openers (stealth Pounce/Ravage), Bear Form for survivability/CC-immunity windows and Charge-interrupts, with the caster kit (HoTs, dispel, roots) available whenever untransformed. **Two distinct sub-roles** are treated separately in the sources: objective play (Bear as flag carrier/point defender) and 1v1/duel power (weaker, see below).
- **Objective play — consensus strong, the spec's clearest PvP niche**: "10k+ HP, Frenzied Regen, double the armor of a warrior, and being able to shift in and out of forms to dispel movement debuffs makes them by far the best flag carriers in WSG and also very good point defenders in AB. Feral Charge has half the cooldown of warrior Intercept" — [Jstansberry (player), 2023-10-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74040#p74040). "Feral is a top tier Blood Ring pick and hard counters most melees" — [Gantulga (player), 2023-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=75762#p75762). Still cited post-1.18.1-announcement: "Druid is the strongest class in the game for carrying flag in warsong, and so highly sought after in premades" — [amanagor (player), 2026-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165799#p165799).
- **1v1/duel power — consensus weak** (pre-Class-Changes-2 assessment, not re-tested post-CC2 in sources): "Feral Druid is C-tier in PVP. They can last a long time no doubt, but lack finishing power. And their Cat/Bear forms are lacking in generally useful abilities such as an interrupt" — [Manletow (player), 2023-10-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73796#p73796). "The class has no meaningful cc and only has a delayed interrupt... it cannot use pots/trinkets/etc without breaking form. These things alone make the class C-tier" — [Mackylol (player), 2023-11-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76016#p76016) (note: the consumables-in-form restriction this cites was removed under Class Changes 2, so this specific complaint is now stale). A pre-1.18.1 numeric tier estimate (1=worst, 5=best) rates Bear BG 5/Arena 5 pre-CC2 dropping to BG 3/Arena 3-4 post-CC2, and Cat BG 2-3/Arena 1-2 pre-CC2 rising to BG 3/Arena 2-3 post-CC2 — [Templar85 (player), 2024-05-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95354#p95354) and [2024-11-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110263#p110263) (**single source, self-assigned tier letters, not independently corroborated**).
- **Bear-form damage reality (pre-CC2, directionally still cited post-1.18.1 given the Savage Bite nerfs)**: "Bear hits like a wet noodle... paladin will cut through your armor with Holy Strike and casters nuke you down as bear" — [Grizb37 (player), 2024-05-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95356#p95356), who also notes Cat is usually forced into Bear anyway once focused: "in PvP Feral is forced into bear because everyone seems to focus you as cat" — same source.
- **Standing — contested, trending negative post-1.18.1**: Pre-Class-Changes-2, a player argued a geared druid using its "whole toolkit" (HoTs, bleeds, Innervate, charge-interrupts, dispel) could out-sustain most non-burst classes 1v1 — [Zahnfee (player), 2024-01-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84932#p84932) — but this was disputed in the same thread ("with the talent tree you posted you get raped by lots of classes" — [Holyhorrorr (player), 2024-01-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84979#p84979)) and a Ret Paladin burst combo (Holy Strike → Seal of Command → Judgement, then Hammer of Justice → Repentance to lock out Frenzied Regen) was given as a hard counter — [Springboards (player), 2024-01-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84993#p84993). Post-1.18.1, standing is reported as much worse for the Bear half of the toolkit specifically: "Feral is dogshit in PvP mate... they nerfed Bear into the ground so its basically just a punching bag, and does zero damage, because of the Savage Bite nerf back in December" — [Grizb37 (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171995#p171995). A detailed multi-matchup case study (vs. Ele/Enh Shaman, Assassination Rogue, Demo/Affliction Lock, Discipline Priest) concludes Feral can barely bring any of these opponents below 50% HP even when opening the fight, while taking devastating burst in return — [Wosator (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171992#p171992) (**single detailed source, contested in-thread**: "you don't have slow target and stun target (cat)... you are really bad at pvp, twow make druid from a meme class to top 1 pvp class" — [shadowchaos (player), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172071#p172071)).
- **Content**: dueling/open-world (contested, see above), BG objective play (Cat specifically called out as strong for stealth flag-running — see Role strategy); arena not covered (**gap**).

- General post-CC2 guidance (no full point list given): "Better to go full feral with omen for burst dmg, can destroy casters with ravage/shred high Crit chance... by not going HotW you lose alot of HP in bear and mana pool. Also lose 11% Crit from talents + not having the speed increase in cat form is annoying in PVP" — [Grizb37 (player), 2024-01-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85018#p85018) — i.e. **Heart of the Wild and Feral Swiftness are treated as PvP essentials**, alongside Omen of Clarity for burst sustain.

## Rotation / tactics

The toolkit described (pre-CC2 source, mechanically still available but power level not re-verified against current numbers):

- **Openers**: Pounce (stealth stun) or Ravage (heavy frontloaded damage from stealth) from Cat Form.
- **Sustain loop**: HoTs (Rejuvenation/Regrowth) plus Innervate to out-heal non-burst damage; dispel poisons/curses/magic by shifting form (form-shift clears the effect) — "i ll insta outheal your damage with swiftmend, charge interrupt your drain or fear cast whenever i can and wait for you to be oom while dotting you up" — [Zahnfee (player), 2024-01-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84976#p84976).
- **Interrupts**: Feral Charge (stun + gap-close, ~15-second cooldown pre-CC2 per [Zahnfee (player), 2024-01-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84932#p84932)) used proactively against casters mid-cast, not just defensively; "Bear has both Feral Charge and Bash to interrupt" — [Jstansberry (player), 2023-10-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=74040#p74040), disputed as "not reliable interrupts" by [Manletow (player), 2023-10-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73796#p73796).
- **Bear Form flag-carrier loop (WSG/AB objective play)**: pick up the flag/hold the point in Bear Form with HoTs pre-applied; shift in and out of forms specifically to shake movement-impairing debuffs/snares (a form-shift clears them); use Frenzied Regeneration when focused, but manage rage carefully — "Be careful with that bearheal, it eats all you rage" — [Frantsel (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156428#p156428); post-Class-Changes-2 Frenzied Regeneration was reported healing "around 25% health" with full rage — [Xfraz (player), 2024-11-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107713#p107713); use Travel Form (or Swift Travel Form once trained, see `leveling.md`) to disengage and run once clear.
- **Bear Form as a panic button (duel/arena context)**: switch to Bear to survive burst, apply Demoralizing Roar, and re-engage once healed — general pattern referenced across multiple threads, not a single dedicated source. **Do not expect to kill anything while sitting in Bear** — it is a survival/objective tool, not a damage tool, per the Bear-damage-reality quotes above.
- **Post-1.18.1 caution**: Bear-form damage output specifically is reported as gutted by the cumulative Savage Bite nerfs (Dec-2024 threat/damage trims plus the 1.18.1 threat-modifier cut), so leaning on Bear for offense rather than pure defense/CC-break/objective-holding is no longer supported by the sources — [Grizb37 (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171995#p171995).
- **1.18.1 system-wide PvP change**: the PvP Dampening debuff (introduced 1.18.0) became class/spec-dependent instead of a flat number for all classes in 1.18.1, intended to let staff tune viability per spec without player-target exceptions — official ([Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)); exact Feral-specific dampening values are not published in the sources read (**gap**).

## Common mistakes

- Relying on the pre-Class-Changes-2 "unkillable" build/expectations without accounting for the talent tree rework and subsequent nerfs.
- Trying to out-damage burst classes (Rogue openers, Warlock fear→dot→drain chains, Paladin Holy Strike/Seal of Command burst) in a straight damage race instead of using the CC-break/heal/disengage loop.
- Neglecting Heart of the Wild and Feral Swiftness in a PvP build — cited as essential for both HP/mana pool and cat-form mobility.
- Expecting Bear Form to carry offense post-1.18.1; treat it as a defensive/CC-immunity/objective-holding tool only until further nerfs are walked back or countered by gear.
- Sitting in Bear Form expecting to actually kill an opponent, rather than using it purely to survive/hold an objective.
- Dumping all rage into Frenzied Regeneration when Maul/Bash still need it for pressure or interrupts.
