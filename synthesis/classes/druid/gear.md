# Druid — Gear guide (by spec, role and level bracket)

Machine-readable version: `structured/classes/druid/gear.yaml`. Sources: the Druid forum (f42), the Itemization forum's relic list and 1.18.1 changelog, and the existing class playbooks' "Gear, enchants and consumables" sections.

**No maintained Turtle-specific pre-raid or raid BiS list exists for any Druid spec/role**, matching the finding for Warrior and Paladin. The strongest single source found is a level-49 "twink" gear list for Balance ([t16360](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16360)) — unusually complete, but it names a bracket ceiling, not a leveling route.

### Pre-raid-60

"Balance gear is nonexistent" — Balance-specific leather+spellpower gear barely exists before Tier 1: "There is not a single piece in a dungeon that is leather and has +spellpower on it... you can either use X wrath pieces with no other stats off the auction house, neglecting either arcane or nature, use all cloth pieces, or wait until Molten Core and tier 1." — [Datadragon (player), 2023-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35702#p35702)

| Slot | Item | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| chest (full set) | Ironfeather set | Leatherworking | "Excellent for the time you can pick it up" |

Also named: a Scarlet Monastery trash staff drop ("fantastic"); Crescent Grove's Crescent Sigil trinket ("quite potent", see leveling-30-49 above); the Embrace of the Fang set from Wailing Caverns (some early nature/spelldamage). Consensus: leveling caster spellpower gear is scarce for every class, not uniquely absent for Druid, but Balance-specific pieces are "few and far between." — [Kairion (player), 2023-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35704#p35704)

### Raid-t2.5

"In fact, tier 2.5 from AQ40 got reworked and is now a full boomkin set with lots of hit, spell power and spell penetration" — corroborated by the staff announcement that alternative Tier 2.5 sets (including Balance's, built from the legacy T2.5 set) were added in 1.17.2. — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2023-10-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70239#p70239); [Dragunovi (staff), 2023-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71030#p71030)

— [Dragunovi (staff), 2023-07-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52799#p52799)

Wolfshead Helm / Wolfsheart enchant + Furor is the historic powershifting enabler, still functional post-1.18.1. — [Dragunovi, 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102966#p102966); [Zvyrhol/Noephix (players), 2026-03-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172992#p172992)

"Item devs strike once again, making bear t3 choke-full of useless and low-value stats like intellect or defense (bear cant parry or block, remember?). So the best current bear set in game by far is... PvP t3. Because pvp sets are naturally made with proper stats. Yes, bears have to spend months upon months on BGs to get their pve BiS set." Not independently corroborated with a gear list — treat as a lead, not a confirmed recommendation. — [Noephix (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173102#p173102)

## Gaps

- No pre-raid-60 gear item list exists for Feral DPS, Feral tank (beyond one trinket), or Restoration; only Balance has a rich leveling list (twink-specific, not a leveling route) and a discussion thread naming crafted/dungeon options rather than a ranked list.
- The Kara40 armor-ignoring trinket nerfed in 1.18.1 (Feral DPS) is never named in the sources that discuss it.
- "Smoking Heart of the Mountain" was asked about as a possible Feral tank trinket but never confirmed or denied.
- The level-29 twink PvP gear list for Feral Druid, Hunter and Rogue ([t22403](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=22403)) names every item only via raw `database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=N` links with no item names in the post text — unusable without a live database lookup; not included above.
- "That new Wailing Caverns idol?" appears in the leveling-30-49 Balance list as an open question with no name resolved.
- No enchant list exists for any Druid spec/role beyond the single Wolfshead Helm mention for Feral and the leveling-30-49 Balance thread's unanswered enchant question.
- No raid-t1 or raid-t3.5 gear exists for Feral tank, and no raid-tier gear at all exists for Restoration beyond the T3.5 set-bonus design complaint (not a gear recommendation).
- Faction-mirrored item names in the leveling-30-49 Balance list are not reproduced in full above; see [t16360](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16360) directly for both factions' exact names.
