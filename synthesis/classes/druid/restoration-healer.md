# Druid — Restoration healer (HoT/Tree of Life and Healing Touch builds)

Playbook for the Restoration healing spec. Class Changes 2 (2024-10-12) pushed Restoration toward a "healing-over-time" identity via the Tree of Life Form and the new Efflorescence passive, while trying to solve the classic anti-synergy of multiple resto druids overwriting each other's HoTs. Sources split cleanly into two build philosophies — Moonglow/Healing Touch and Tree of Life/HoT — argued about at length but never resolved into a single "best" answer; PvP survivability is a separate, much more negative topic (see `restoration-pvp.md`).

## Overview

- **Role**: raid/dungeon healer, either via a HoT-stacking Tree of Life build (Rejuvenation/Regrowth/Swiftmend/Tranquility) or a Moonglow-enabled Healing Touch build with Nature's Grace/Aessina's Bloom synergy.
- **Strengths (consensus)**: mana efficiency, strong single-target and (via Aessina's Bloom + an existing Regrowth) throughput healing — "Aessina's Bloom really makes the throughput feel great, especially if a tree is already HoTing your target. I regularly get high or on top of the meters in raids up to naxx because of it" — [Bayanni (player), 2025-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155621#p155621). Spirit scales the whole party's regen, not just the caster's, making it a stronger stat than its "personal log" impact suggests (see Stat priority). "In raid content, resto druids are amazing — as long as we are the *only* resto druid... that one resto druid is a game changer, and no raid should be without" — [Lynxkitten (player), 2025-10-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155593#p155593).
- **Weaknesses (consensus)**: "we cannot compensate for an undergeared tank, where other healers sometimes can. This is because we have less up-front healing options, and our mana sustain can be tricky" in dungeon content — same source; **raid-slot scarcity**: "There is no discernable use to a second resto druid, compared to that raid slot being filled by virtually any other healer" — same source; Tree of Life Form cuts off Healing Touch and all offensive/utility casting while active, and slows movement 20% — a long-running complaint (see Talent build); PvP survivability is weak (see `restoration-pvp.md`).
- **Content**: raids (strong, capacity-limited to ~1-2 slots), dungeons (good but not carry-tier), leveling (viable, see `leveling.md`), PvP (weak — see `restoration-pvp.md`).
- **Community standing**: **consensus that the spec is functional but under-documented** — "I can hardly find any info or guides though on what to do or how to spec" is a repeated new-player complaint — [Mastrshake (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153729#p153729); [Hasenn117f (player), 2025-08-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145447#p145447). Post-1.18.1: "resto sucks because there's only 1 slot for a resto druid in 40-man raid. Maybe 2, if they coordinate. But more than 2 — and they start griefing each other by overwriting each other's heals. Dev team's attempts to fix the problem didn't really help. And resto also sucks because holy priest does resto's 'speciality' (heal over time) much better" — [Noephix (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173102#p173102) (**single detailed source**, partially contradicted in the same thread by "Resto Druid aint that bad ngl when you stack druids the healing gets shared 3 ways but still decent" — [Froizerk (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173300#p173300)).

## Talent build

Two build philosophies, both actively played; **no source decodes a single definitive 60-point list**, only talent-calculator links and named-talent descriptions:

- **Tree of Life / HoT build**: centers on Rejuvenation/Regrowth spam plus Swiftmend, with **Efflorescence** (level 60, Tree-Form-only passive: any time your Regrowth/Rejuvenation is about to be replaced, it instantly heals for a portion of the remaining duration) as the capstone payoff. Community build link: [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=BgAbAI--FAFABbFFALFQB](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156226#p156226) — [Bayanni (player), 2025-10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156226#p156226), who also suggests swapping 2/2 Improved Tranquility for 2/2 Nature's Reach depending on raid needs (Naxx/AQ40 range utility vs. a stronger emergency cooldown).
- **Moonglow / Healing Touch build**: keeps Healing Touch as a spam-able direct heal, uses Moonglow for its mana-cost reduction while casting HT, and leans on **Aessina's Bloom** (Healing Touch on a Regrowth-affected target has a 30/60% chance to instantly heal for 6 seconds of Regrowth). Community build links: [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=oACYQKCYAI--FAoABLFDQAF](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147488#p147488) — [Skyfont (player), 2025-08-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147488#p147488); another variant [https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=BgYbAKAYAI--FAoABbFFQI](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156226#p156226) — [Bayanni (player), 2025-10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156226#p156226).
- **A third, less-tested "deep resto without Tree or Moonglow" route** was flagged as promising but unresolved in the sources read — [Bayanni (player), 2025-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155621#p155621) (**gap** — no follow-up confirmation found).
- **Contested QoL complaint (still open as of 1.18.1)**: **Nature's Focus** (spell-pushback-avoidance talent) costs a full 5 points for 70% protection on the non-Tree build, versus a 2-point cost for equivalent protection on every other healer class, and its cost locks a HT-build druid out of also taking both Regrowth and HT talents — [Bikicsunaj (player), 2026-02-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168231#p168231). No staff response found in sources.
- **Efflorescence confusion**: several players didn't realize it requires Tree of Life Form and is trainer-taught rather than a talent-tree entry — "requares tree form? seriously? On what basis was this strange decision made?" — [Zend (player), 2024-11-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105927#p105927); "Go visit a class trainer. Took me a week of tanking without Feral version of Barkskin before I had noticed lol" — [Drishby (player), 2025-03-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122605#p122605) (about a different trainer-taught spell, same confusion pattern). At least one player reported it not functioning at all — [PikxDizzy (player), 2025-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122961#p122961) (**single report, unconfirmed bug**).
- **1.18.1 tier-set complaint (post-patch)**: the T3.5 5-set "Blooming Bud" (boosts Swiftmend) was reworked with only a 20-second linger, "which only realistically affects the tanks to get the proc (240 healing once getting hit)"; the poster argues the set bonus philosophy (lower Swiftmend CD for more procs of a low-impact buff) no longer coheres and proposes reworking it into a Rejuvenation/Regrowth-crit-style proc instead — [Froizerk (player), 2026-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172927#p172927) (**single source suggestion, not an implemented change**).

## Stat priority

- **Spirit**: undervalued by personal healing logs but mechanically strong because of its party-wide effect: "1 spirit is 0.2 healing to every member of your party, so in a way every point of spirit is 1sp, very strong even if your personal logs don't reflect it" — [Bayanni (player), 2025-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155621#p155621) — cited as a reason to favor the Tree build early in a raid tier when spirit-heavy healing gear has less competition.
- **Downranking**: no numeric spell-rank table is given, but the community rule of thumb for choosing a Regrowth rank is: "spam regrowth with full mana and no buffs/consumables. If your mana lasts 45 seconds or more without any external help/no innervate/no potions you're good for 90% of the fights with that rank. If your mana lasts more than 1 min 15 secs use a higher rank and repeat until you're between 45 secs and 1 min 15 secs" — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153757#p153757). Max-rank Regrowth/Healing Touch is reserved for emergency top-ups; downranked casts are the sustained-healing default — same source.
- **No numeric weights for Healing Power or Intellect** are given by any source (**gap**).

## Single-target (tank) healing priority

- **HT build**: max-rank Healing Touch for emergency top-ups, downranked Healing Touch (per the 45s–75s mana-duration rule above) as the sustained tank heal, Regrowth layered on top when Aessina's Bloom is talented and a Regrowth is already present from any druid — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153732#p153732); [Bayanni (player), 2025-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155621#p155621).
- **Tree build**: "replace healing touch spam with regrowht spam" once raid-geared, since Tree Form locks out Healing Touch entirely — [Reploidrocsa (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153732#p153732). Tree is explicitly weaker as a **dungeon** tank-healer than as a raid healer: "Tree is good but limited due to not having a solid repeatable heal for tanks. They're solid for a raid but I'd hold off using them as dungeon healers unless you're stacked and your HoTs alone can keep the MT up" — [Bayanni (player), 2025-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155621#p155621).
- **Swiftmend**: instant-cast emergency heal, core to both builds; a "cancel Tree + cast Nature's Swiftness + cast heal" macro pattern is discussed in dedicated macro threads (t13046, not read in depth for this playbook — **gap**).

## Multi-target / raid healing

- **Tranquility**: completely reworked under Class Changes 2 — cooldown 5 → 30 minutes, mana cost doubled, tick rate 2s → 1s, baseline threat removed, now hits the **entire raid** instead of just the caster's party, and rank-1-through-4 durations are only 4/6/8/10 seconds respectively (official, [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102966#p102966)). The rework's stated goal was to make Tranquility "affect all raid members in close range of the druid, at a significantly decreased healing power and increased cooldown" so that "only Restoration druids will be able to unlock Tranquility's true potential" — same source. A dedicated thread ("Tranquility is too OP", 42 posts, t15878) argued the rework over/undershot this goal in both directions; not read in full for this playbook — **gap, revisit if raid-cooldown tuning detail is needed**.
- **HoT-overwrite anti-synergy**: the core design tension the Class Changes 2 patch tried to solve — a second druid's Rejuvenation/Regrowth *replaces* rather than stacks with the first druid's, and Efflorescence's instant-heal-on-replace was the intended mitigation, but players still find it painful when gear is uneven: "It is really a pain to be a less geared druid trying to get some healing done while the other druid doesn't have to care about anything... if you can't apply your hots it really hurts" — [Syndraela (player), 2025-02-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120862#p120862), who requests druids be allowed to overwrite each other's HoTs even with weaker versions specifically to trigger Efflorescence — unresolved as of the sources read.

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Innervate**: by general Druid-community convention (referenced across multiple threads, not a single dedicated source) this is given to another healer or the caster's own mana-hungry raid role rather than self-used by a DPS-spec druid; no single authoritative thread quantifies the exact etiquette (**gap**).
- **Mana**: managed primarily through downranking (see Stat priority) and consumables; no Restoration-specific consumable list distinct from the general caster list was found (see `balance-ranged-dps.md`'s Gear section for the shared Major Mana Potion / Nordanaar Herbal Tea / Dark Rune / Nightfin Soup / Mageblood Potion list — **assume it applies to Restoration too, not independently confirmed for this spec — flag as inferred, not sourced directly**).
- **Swiftmend**: emergency mana-free-ish burst heal (consumes a HoT), core cooldown for both builds.

## Role strategy

- **Raid-slot planning**: bring at most one (rarely two, coordinated) Restoration druids to a raid; a third overlapping resto druid actively griefs the others' healing via HoT overwrite — [Noephix (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173102#p173102); [Lynxkitten (player), 2025-10-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155593#p155593).
- **Build choice by raid tier/competition**: favor the Tree/HoT build early in a raid tier (less competition for high-Spirit gear) and be willing to switch to Moonglow/HT if a second Tree druid joins or the tank needs more repeatable direct healing — [Bayanni (player), 2025-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155621#p155621).
- **Dungeon healing**: a Healing Touch-leaning build (even a full-Balance druid with spare healing gear, per `balance-ranged-dps.md`) is generally safer for undergeared/PUG dungeon groups than committing to Tree Form, given Tree's lack of a repeatable strong direct heal.
- **PvP**: see `restoration-pvp.md` — Restoration is reported as one of the weakest PvP healer specs on the server.

## Gear, enchants and consumables

- **Chronometer** addon recommended for tracking own HoT durations — [Pdwow (player), 2025-04-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126491#p126491); Cursive or DoTimer suggested as alternatives that "don't bug out in raids" — [Noephix (player), 2025-04-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128462#p128462).
- **PvP gear as a PvE starting point**: the leather "Abyssal" set from the Auction House was recommended over the early PvE blue set specifically because the blue set wastes stat points on Strength/Agility, while Abyssal gives Stamina/Intellect/spell power — [Halfgeek9 (player), 2025-05-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131901#p131901); the Moonheart PvP set was separately asked about as a starting point but not conclusively endorsed (**gap**) — [Krltbr (player), 2025-05-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131891#p131891).
- Community (non-staff) resources linked from the wiki: a "Turtle WoW Restoration Druid Overview" write-up (`https://rentry.co/kiv7ntyx`) and a pbrigade.gitlab.io gear-planner Restoration Druid BiS export — community-maintained, not staff-verified, content not independently read for this playbook (**gap**).
- **No pre-raid BiS list found in the forum sources** — gap, consistent with every other Druid role covered here.

## Common mistakes

- Bringing more than one or two Restoration druids to a 40-man raid without explicit HoT-assignment coordination.
- Spamming max-rank heals instead of downranking to the mana-sustainable rank for the fight length.
- Trying to tank-heal a dungeon in full Tree Form without a strong repeatable direct heal backup.
- Forgetting Efflorescence must be trained from a class trainer and requires Tree of Life Form to function.
- Assuming a second (or third) druid's weaker HoT will stack instead of overwrite.
