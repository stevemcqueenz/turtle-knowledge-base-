# Rogue — Combat — Melee DPS (raid / dungeon; daggers and swords)

Covers Combat as the geared raid-DPS spec: the **Combat Daggers / Backstab** build (the forum's "endgame meta" 20/31/0 as of late 2025) and the **Combat Swords/Axes/Maces (Sinister Strike)** variant. Assassination ("Noxious Assault") is the alternative raid spec with its own playbook (`assassination-melee-dps.md`); the sources rank them by gear level and disagree at the top end.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Kit milestones inside pre-1.18.1: CC2 (2024-10-12), 2024-12-20 round, 2025-03-30 round, 1.18.0 (2025-07-25 post; live 2025-08-15).

## Overview

- **Role**: melee single-target DPS with the class's only cleave (Blade Flurry) and burst (Adrenaline Rush). Staff: "This specialization is the bread and butter of all Rogues in the original Vanilla and in the current iteration of class changes" [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102971#p102971).
- **Strengths (sources)**: scales with gear far better than Assassination — "combat is running around with 700 agi and 1.3s energy ticks, dealing 2k dps" [Crake (player), 2026-03-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172282#p172282) (post-1.18.1); "20/31/0 is the endgame meta build atm for pve dps ... reach out to Premium, Perceptionz, Hedonist, or Xiaowaifu. They're all pretty solid dudes and regularly top the charts" [Bayanni (player), 2025-09-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153716#p153716); physical damage "always works" against poison-immune raid mobs [Finpin (player), 2025-04-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127477#p127477); reliable cleave and simple rotation ("Combat should be the easiest one" [Rachdingue (player), 2025-09-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151387#p151387)).
- **Weaknesses (sources)**: gear- and hit-dependent; behind Fury/Arms warriors at every gear tier pre-1.18.0 ("around an 18% DPS difference with Warriors beating Rogues at all tiers of play" [Bigbosc (player), 2025-03-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122845#p122845)); Backstab needs positioning ("Combat dagger/sub is a bit more a pain in the ass cause you have to front backstab" [Rachdingue (player), 2025-09-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152024#p152024)); weak while leveling since 1.18.0 (see `leveling.md`); the 1.18.1 Opportunity nerf specifically targets "Combat dagger rogues ... performing tad better than with any other weapons" [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **Content**: raids at AQ40/Naxx/Kara40 gear — favored; dungeons at 60 — fine (cleave); leveling — contested (Combat Swords was the classic default, now called "Easily the weakest option I've tried" by one 1.18.0 leveler [Sslushee (player), 2025-11-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158547#p158547)); PvP — not used ("nobody uses combat rogue in pvp" [Crake (player), 2025-10-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154534#p154534)).
- **Community standing**: **favored at high gear, alternative below it** (consensus late 2025 → 2026): "AQ + gear Combat backstab spec" [Grizb37 (player), 2025-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158295#p158295); post-1.18.1 "Go COMBAT u n00bs, and learn rotations" [Mdzielak (player), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171653#p171653) vs "combat is not harder or more complex ... combat rogue has been overplayed for years" [Thesuperwitch (player), 2026-03-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172799#p172799). Earlier (2025-05): "Combat is actually still stronger than assass. Somehow" [Bigsmerf (player), 2025-05-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130645#p130645); "Around 90+% of Rogues players play Combat Spec" [Finpin, 2025-04-19]. Raid-log claim: "Rogues are among the top parsers of any single target boss in naxx, except for the ones where the mechanics handicap melees" [Kibuum (player), 2025-02-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120498#p120498) (Patchwerk excluded; "Posting parses and removing trash AND warriors is peak dishonesty" [Gantulga (player), 2025-02-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120733#p120733) — contested).
- Reputation: warriors and paladins are seen as the favored classes; rogues "bring nothing to the table" for raid buffs (consensus 2023–2025; e.g. [Exaltus (player), 2025-05-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132748#p132748)) — Improved Expose Armor is "our best buff" [Bigbosc (player), 2025-03-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122847#p122847).

## Official kit (staff)

- CC2 [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102971#p102971): Improved Sinister Strike baseline (SS 40 energy) → Swift Strikes (removed in 1.18.0); **Improved Backstab also gives 15/30/45% chance of an extra combo point**; Setup moved to Combat row 3; Blade Flurry moved to row 3 without 20% haste; Mace Specialization → **Concussive Blows** (stun only); **Close Quarters Combat** 2 points, daggers/fists/maces +2/5% crit; **Surprise Attack** keystone (after a dodge; 2024-12-20: instant, 25% AP, 10-s CD, 10 energy; cannot be blocked/dodged/parried since 2024-11-01); **Hack and Slash** (swords+axes, 2/5% extra attack, 2 points); **Blade Rush**; Weapon Expertise includes maces; Aggression 3/6/10% incl. Riposte/Surprise Attack; Endurance removed (Sprint/Evasion 4 min baseline); Sword/Dagger Specialization → Hack and Slash / CQC.
- 2024-12-20 [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460): Relentless Strikes 20 energy + stacking 5%/stack finisher damage (5 stacks, 30 s); finishers −5 energy; **Adrenaline Rush 3 min**; Blade Rush −15/30 s BF cooldown and +10/20% attack speed (pre-1.18.0 version).
- 1.18.0 [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159): **Blade Flurry baseline at 30 as a toggle** (no duration, 4-s cooldown, −30% energy regeneration, −20% damage while active); Swift Strikes removed; Improved Expose Armor → Combat row 1 (revision 2025-08-14: **Opportunity → Combat row 1**, Improved Gouge and Improved Expose Armor → Subtlety row 1); **Blade Rush → row 6: "+2/5% melee attack speed and reduces the time between your Energy regeneration ticks by an amount equal to your Agility"**; Combat Expertise → row 5; Lethality/Remorseless include Surprise Attack.
- **1.18.1** [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071): **Opportunity 4/8/12/16/20% → 3/6/9/12/15%** ("Combat dagger rogues are performing tad better than with any other weapons, this nerf will bring them more in line"); **Blade Flurry energy penalty 30% → 20%**; poison low-rank scaling reduced (Instant VI unchanged at 5% AP).
- Hotfixes: Riposte no longer works on unarmed enemies after a January 2025 change per a player ("does not work for me, on all enemies who 'do not have a weapon in the main hand'" [Dzikcrakehall (player), 2025-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117517#p117517), bug report 11334) — player report, no staff line in the corpus; "Weapon Expertise" gives +3/+5 weapon skill, not 3/5% — confirmed by a GM in-game per the poster after the website calculator showed % [Nightmonster (player), 2023-05-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43985#p43985).

## Stat priority

- **Hit first**: 9% special-attack cap ("the 9% hit cap is easy to be reached with some +hit gear" [Nightmonster, 2023-05-01]; "warriors & rogues dont go for the insane 27% hitcap for dualwielding" [player in f63, 2024-11]); "if you're above hitcap, but not pushing crit cap, then hit is effectively a near nil increase ... Inversely if you're at the crit cap, Crit is now useless and Agility loses a huge chunk of its value, but Hit is now a premium" [Poxfox (player), 2024-06-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96625#p96625).
- **Weapon skill 305/315**: "with raical bonus (300+5) ... 9% hit cap is easy to be reached"; "you can get lv1 weapon mastery for 308 weapon skill (optimum)" [Nightmonster, 2023-05-01]; "The higher the skill the more you push misses and glancing blows off the hit table" [Shananagans, 2025-10-13].
- **Agility / crit / AP**: agility now also drives energy regeneration through Blade Rush — measured "With 165 agi a tick took aprox 1,7-1,8 seconds. So I'd say 100 agi = 0.1seconds" [Rachdingue (player), 2025-09-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151549#p151549) (single measurement; Shananagans' earlier guess "1 agility = 1 ms" was withdrawn as a guess [2025-09-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151509#p151509)); Crake's post-1.18.1 "700 agi and 1.3s energy ticks" matches ~0.1 s per 100 agi. Crit cap must be watched: "Going over crit cap has to be monitored more closely than combat (unless your are running close to BIS gear)" [Shananagans, 2025-09-12]; "I was at my crit cap of 28%,( without buffs)" at 57 in dungeon gear (Shananagans, 2025-09-13) — player figure.
- **Haste**: "The general consensus for most melees seems to be that an equal amount of crit outperforms an equal amount of haste, as haste only affects autohits" [Atreidon (player), 2024-04-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92096#p92096); for rogues "its worse than bhb [Blackhand's Breadth], since you have flurry up all the time ... and neither energy regen nor style dmg is impacted by haste" [Atreidon, same day](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92099#p92099). A Turtle-updated rogue DPS spreadsheet is pinned in the Rogue section of the Turtle WoW Discord [Likaleo (player), 2024-05-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94963#p94963) (not in corpus).
- **Armor penetration**: "ARP is a wild card stat that doesn't even do anything in many cases" [Gantulga (player), 2024-05-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95023#p95023).
- **Leveling**: Precision first, then whatever; see `leveling.md`.

## Single-target rotation (daggers)

1. Opener from stealth if the pull allows: Garrote ("Garote is normal boss opener" [ValiantBV (player), 2025-03-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122337#p122337)) or Ambush/Cheap Shot; in raids usually none ("Nowadays players must wait out of stealth for tank to pull" [Artashir (player), 2025-11-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160776#p160776)).
2. **Backstab** is the only filler: "You are combat DAGGER. Ghostly strike and Sinister strike are absolutely awful while using daggers. Your filler ability is backstab!" [Atreidon (player), 2025-04-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128331#p128331). Stay behind the target.
3. **Slice and Dice** up at all times (first finisher, 2–5 points; "I don't see a mention of slice and dice... you are using slice and dice, yes?" [Stealiebones (player), 2025-04-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128330#p128330)); "their damage in PvE is built mostly on boosting their white damage by Expose Armor and Slice&Dice. Eviscerate is used when (de)buffs from those finishers are up and not going to fade soon" [player in f63, 2024-12].
4. **Expose Armor** (Improved) if no warrior keeps Sunder up; otherwise skip (consensus).
5. **Rupture** at 5 points on long fights ("I know you are using Rupture and not Eviscerates" [Shananagans, 2025-12-13]; "keep up Rupture on boss and Eviscerate for spare combo points" [ValiantBV, 2025-03-12]); **Eviscerate** at 5 when the target will die before Rupture pays off or it is bleed-immune.
6. **Surprise Attack** whenever it lights up (after a dodge) — macro it ahead of the builder: "/cast Surprise Attack /cast Sinister Strike ... It will cast SA if available, otherwise cast SS" [madferret (player), 2025-07-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141662#p141662); Roid-Macros buff-conditional version by [Elisleris (player), 2025-03-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123951#p123951).
7. Poisons: Instant on both hands by default (raid mobs are often poison-immune anyway); Dissolvent/Corrosive only on Elemental/Mechanical/Giant/Undead post-1.18.1 (see `assassination-melee-dps.md` for the poison table); the Naxx question "consecrated sharpening stones or some combination of poison" has no answer in the corpus (gap).

Swords variant: Sinister Strike replaces Backstab; everything else identical (Yasara; Roxanneflowers' 2020 build discussion). A typical wrong rotation that under-performed (275 vs 350–425 DPS in similar gear): "BS, S&D, Ghostly Strike/SS, Evisc" [RecenRogue (player), 2025-04-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128007#p128007) — the fix was "backstab only" and better macros ("Something that seperates good dps from bad most of the time comes down to optimal macros" [Gildark (player), 2025-04-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128334#p128334)).

## Multi-target / AoE

- 2–3 targets: **toggle Blade Flurry on** — it is the class's cleave and "imho has changed in 1.18 for the better for combat rogue. no cool down and you can toggle it on and off" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-08-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148289#p148289); toggle it **off** as soon as only one target remains (−20% damage, −20% energy while active, staff). Spam-safe macros: "/cast [nomybuff:Blade_Flurry] Blade Flurry" (on) and "/cast [mybuff:Blade_Flurry] Blade Flurry" (off), Roid Macros required [Snkx (player), 2025-11-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160336#p160336). Pre-1.18.0 habit "/cast blade flurry /cast adrenaline rush" [Balake (player), 2023-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60501#p60501) still applies for burst windows.
- 4+ targets: still only Blade Flurry (hits one extra target); rogues "are the only class without any baseline aoe" [Derrurak (player), 2023-06-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49203#p49203) — accept low AoE parses; grenades/Oil of Immolation are the only pad.
- Combat is "the AoE spec" only relatively: "If you really, really want some form of AoE, play combat" [SvenS2 (player), 2025-05-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132963#p132963).

## Cooldowns and resources

- **Adrenaline Rush** (3 min): on pull after SnD/Expose are up, and on every cleave pack with Blade Flurry; "you rely much on Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush to pump out damage for a short period of time" [Finpin, 2025-04-19].
- **Blade Flurry**: a toggle, not a cooldown — see AoE; keep it off on single target.
- **Energy**: 20 per 2 s baseline, faster with agility via Blade Rush (above); Relentless Strikes returns 20 on 5-point finishers; Darkmantle 4-piece "lets you actually press buttons more often" [Sagabal (player), 2025-05-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132721#p132721); Thistle Tea; T3.5 Trickster 5-set proc measured at "15% instead of 20% as description" post-1.18.1 [Hcyd (player), 2026-03-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172499#p172499) (single report, unanswered).
- **Vanish** = second trinket set/opener in raids ("we can access 4 trinkets on a fight with vanish" [Bigbosc, 2025-03-18]); Evasion/Sprint 4 min (staff CC2); Preparation is not in this build.
- **Threat**: Feint; Vanish as an aggro reset (Shananagans' TankPlates colour rule).

## Role strategy

- Behind the target at all times (Backstab); on fights that force front positioning switch to Sinister Strike or expect a big loss.
- Interrupt casters with Kick (Deadly Throw as ranged backup, shares Kick's cooldown).
- Raid utility rogues do bring: Improved Expose Armor ("largest armor drop in the game" [Jinkarasu (player), 2025-08-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148542#p148542)), Distract for skips, disarming traps (BWL Suppression Room: "Only time you might want more is suppression room in BWL" [Bayanni (player), 2023-10-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70147#p70147)), Vanish to avoid mechanics ("avoid certain mechanics like Webspray on Maexxna" [Bigbosc, 2025-03-18]).
- Riposte in dungeons when tanking-adjacent (needs a parry and an armed target).
- Combo points persist per target since CC2; "Combo points are kept on Target A when you switch to Target B ... if you apply a Combo Point to Target B, your points on Target A will be lost" [Redmagejoe (player), 2024-11-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107038#p107038) — so Kick/Gouge on an add costs nothing, but a builder on the add wipes the boss's points.

## Common mistakes (bots must avoid)

- Using Sinister Strike or Ghostly Strike with daggers (Atreidon).
- Forgetting Slice and Dice (Stealiebones).
- Leaving Blade Flurry on during single target, or toggling it off/on with a spam macro (Snkx's separate on/off macros).
- Applying a builder to an add (wipes the boss's combo points) — use Kick/Gouge only.
- Not being hit-capped before stacking crit (Poxfox); ignoring crit cap with world-buff-free raids is not an issue, but with raid buffs at Naxx gear it is (Shananagans).
- Expecting to top Patchwerk-style meters against warriors pre-1.18.1 (Bigbosc's 18%); bragging about parses that exclude warriors (Gantulga).
- Using Blade Flurry to level (energy starvation: "1.18 absolutely fucked blade flurry ... only ever gets better very much later on when you got agility based increase in energy reg ticks" [Hctwowfan (player), 2025-09-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151505#p151505)).

For the Combat-dagger Envenom build, **Deadly sims better than Instant** because Envenom maintains the stacks [[d:rogue#1484976580479418600]] (single source).
- **Weapon speed**: ~1.3 is wanted for poison-proc volume; there is exactly one BiS exception, the 1.8-speed spellstrike build [[d:rogue#1446546660032385118]].
- **Blade Flurry** was "trash until ~800 agility" for leveling/under-geared play; 1.18.1's energy-penalty cut (30%→20%) is what made it a real cleave tool [[d:rogue#1465059753037136125]].

### Rotation and AoE practice

- **24/27/0 Combat-envenom**: the same shape as the old Combat-dagger rotation but **no Adrenaline Rush, and Envenom replaces Rupture** [[d:rogue#1488169766530318456]]; the Backstab form is Backstab for CP, 1-CP Envenom upkeep, 5-CP Eviscerate [[d:rogue#1485641027560673372]]; the Sinister-Strike "evis-spam" form (SS + 1-CP Envenom/SnD, then 1-CP Eviscerate) sims best but is called "the most braindead spec" and not everyone can reproduce the sim [[d:rogue#1486038172336001174]], [[d:rogue#1485960977337024592]] (contested).
- **Never use Sinister Strike with daggers** — macro a sword/mace/fist weapon swap [[d:rogue#1424148190663278846]].
- **Blade Flurry** stays the only cleave and a toggle (-20% energy/-20% damage post-1.18.1); turn it off at one target [[d:rogue#1429240041577644186]]. On trash, use fast-kill poisons (double Instant/Dissolvent) and Rupture may be skipped [[d:rogue#1418643088213016756]].
- **Threat**: Assassination/Combat burst threat is a defining problem — 3 Noxious Assaults from full energy is ~4k threat in 3 s; counterplay is wait 5–7k tank threat, Feint weave, cloak enchant, **Vanish as a second trinket-proc window and a threat reset** [[d:rogue#1409455377640194100]], [[d:rogue#1407474034349969448]].
- **Encounters**: pre-1.18.1 Naxx plan (AR on Spider wing, Envenom on Abom, AR after Thaddius, Military, Envenom for Plague/Loatheb/Sapphiron, AR for KT) [[d:rogue#1418275819721195584]]; **Ambush openers on Patchwerk get you killed** — open Evasion + Ghostly Strike into SnD [[d:rogue#1409285859248832592]].

- Assassination BiS weapon path is **Alcor's + Distracting Dagger until Naxx** [[d:rogue#1410197464362782761]], [[d:rogue#1409767850373025802]]; pre-raid alts 2× Stormfist, Distracting+Stormfist, Finkle's, Julie's, The Ripper, Bonescraper [[d:rogue#1407120619732209734]]; the ladder Julie → Alcor → MC caster dagger → ZG caster dagger → (BWL) → Timbermaw class weapon → THC [[d:rogue#1472663678384865544]]. **Fang of Venoxis was buffed to 1.3 speed** and is called the best available assassination dagger [[d:rogue#1486869810053775370]] (single source).
