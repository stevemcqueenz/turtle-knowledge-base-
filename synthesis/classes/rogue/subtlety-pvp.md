# Rogue — Subtlety — PvP (Hemorrhage/Preparation and Ambush/Backstab dagger builds)

The forum treats rogue PvP as its own topic with two Subtlety-based builds (the classic **Hemo/Prep** control build and the **Ambush/Backstab dagger** burst build) and one Assassination build (`assassination-pvp.md`). This file covers the two Subtlety builds, battleground roles, matchups, races and PvP gear.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Kit milestones affecting PvP: CC2 (Hemorrhage normalised/2%, Crippling II 60%, Wound Poison rework, Sprint/Evasion 4 min, combo points persist), 1.18.0 (Quickfooted +7%, Blind is Physical, Blackjack, Preparation 7 min, Cloaked in Shadows), and the server-side "PvP dampening" / battleground damage-reduction debuff and Insignia changes referenced by players (not in the rogue sources as staff text).

## Overview

- **Role**: control-and-burst duelist and objective player (flag rooms, ninja caps, EFC hunting).
- **Strengths (sources)**: "You have far more ability to control and lock down with pvp spec rogue, the energy management is better, you can build up to a full 5 combo point kidney with 100% reliability ... you have all of your cooldowns TWICE" [Crake (player), 2025-10-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154550#p154550); "a bis rogue will bring a bis geared ele with tank talents down to 30% health in the first stunlock and with a blind follow up, only way to not die in cc chain is to use trinket + lip on second stunlock, or LAP" [amanagor (player), 2025-02-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119488#p119488); "as a rogue you have the benedit of not needing to take bad matchups" [Atreidon (player), 2025-06-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137578#p137578).
- **Weaknesses (sources)**: no DoT removal ("rogue is way too vulnerable to dots in PvP" [Gantulga (player), 2023-12-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79632#p79632)); loses to paladins, ferals (bear), and orc stun resists; stealth is janky on Turtle ("It feels like the tick rate for noticing other players in stealth is like 1 second" [Crake (player), 2025-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158381#p158381); mobs see through stealth [Grizb37 (player), 2025-11-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158386#p158386)); pets keep chasing after Vanish ("Stealth will not get rid of pets chasing you. But vanish will" [Kairion (player), 2023-03-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36946#p36946)); Insignias remove Blind/stuns.
- **Community standing — contested over time**: 2023–early 2025 "rogue is the worst class for pvp on twow" [Feomatar (player), 2023-12-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80230#p80230), "Rogue is lower mid tier in PvP here" [Gantulga (player), 2023-12-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80259#p80259) vs "rogue is actually quite good in pvp currently. Its just a skill issue with most rogues not understanding their role in bgs" [amanagor (player), 2025-02-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119499#p119499). From 1.18.0 (Aug 2025) the outside view flips: "rogue was also buffed into total faceroll territory" [Terrydavis (player), 2025-10-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156307#p156307); "Shaman, Pala, Rogues are good classes for PVP" [Tomogwarrior (player), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171638#p171638) (post-1.18.1). Rogues themselves say the real killers are hemo/prep players, not poison rogues: "Every time I've died to a semi competent rogue in pvp in the last week or so, it's been a hemo rogue, not a noxious assault rogue" [Crake, 2025-10-06].

## Talent builds

### Hemo/Prep control build (1.18.0 tree)

"The pure vanilla pvp sub rogue talent build is still perfectly doable, with a few bonus extras" — link `https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=YoaAKADYYI--FLCYYBCARC` [Crake (player), 2025-10-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153977#p153977). Verbal content from Crake's matchup posts: **Hemorrhage** ("a 35 energy combo point builder when fully talented" — 40 base −5 Bloody Mess), **Dirty Deeds** ("reduces your cost of cheap shot by 20 energy"), **Initiative** ("increases the number of combo points that cheap shot generates from 2 to 3"), **Blackjack/Improved Sap** ("the ability to remain stealthed when sapping"), **Preparation**, plus Improved Sprint from Combat and Elusiveness. Muffanzo's older advice (CC1 tree): "if you don't take master of deception for pvp you are going to have a bad time" (now Camouflage); "Setup could be dropped and put into serrated blades giving you the ability to pick up hemo which costs less energy then sinister strike and combos very well with dirty deeds"; "I don't understand the point of going that deep into combat if you are not going to pick up riposte as well which can be very good in pvp"; links `talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue/KXC6U1-I-U2X02K86` (enough hit) / `CX06O-IC5-U2X02K86` (needs hit) [Muffanzo (player), 2023-05-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46112#p46112) (pre-CC2 codes). Other named PvP talents: Improved Gouge (5-s gouge = restealth or bandage window) and Elusiveness in the leveling PvP path [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149395#p149395); "improve throwing instantly applying crippling is really solid improve sap prep is really solid combat silence on kick or just reposte when you parry disarming is solid since 90 % of the PVPer don't use a chain" [boeuf (player), 2025-05-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129671#p129671). Weapons: "Using slow weapon on mainhand and fast offhand" [Gottabe (player), 2026-04-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174771#p174771).

## Stat priority

Not stated numerically anywhere. Implicit: stamina (PvP BiS list is T3/T3.5 heavy), hit to not miss openers, crit for Ambush, agility; stealth level items for the opener war (Boots of Displacement, Cloak of the Dark Veil, X-52 Stealth Ocular Implants) and stealth-detection items (Catseye Ultra Goggles "gives 18 points of stealth detection while Sightless Leather Hood only gives 10" [Thisisreallydumb (player), 2025-03-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123159#p123159)). New 1.18.1 PvP items with "reducing dot and crit dmg" stats exist (neck, cape, belt, rings) — whether to swap T3 for them is an open question [Gottabe, 2026-04-11]. Gap.

## Opener / kill sequence (single target)

Consensus shape across Crake, Shananagans and amanagor:

1. **Approach in stealth from behind**; Distract to turn the target; Sap on a second enemy first if Blackjack (restealth) is taken. "Never stealth close to a mobs face ... Approaching from behind yields better results ... Use distract" [Atreidon (player), 2025-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158352#p158352) (about mobs; the same detection jank applies to players per Crake).
2. **Cheap Shot** (40 energy with Dirty Deeds; 3 CP with Initiative) → **Hemorrhage** ×1–2 → **Kidney Shot** at 5 points (Crake's "full 5 combo point kidney with 100% reliability"). Dagger build instead: **Ambush** (near-100% crit with talents; "Ambush deals 2x your Backstab damage, and if it doesn't crit you get an energy refund" [SvenS2 (player), 2025-12-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162071#p162071)) → Gouge → Backstab → Kidney/Eviscerate; Shananagans' Mark-for-Death fantasy line: "Mark for Death - Gouge - Vanish - Ambush - Kidney shot - Thistle tea - Backstab - Backstab - Gouge".
3. **Thistle Tea** in the stun for the extra builder.
4. If the target trinkets: **Blind** (Physical now, "not easily cleansable" — staff 1.18.0), bandage or restealth (Improved Gouge window), re-open; Preparation to redo Blind/Vanish/Sprint/Cold Blood ("if blind gets trinketed, you can re-apply it" — Crake).
5. **Keep DoTs off targets you plan to CC**: "Do not use a DOT, damage over time effect ie rupture, deadly poison or a weapon or trinket that puts a dot on your target ... Use double Instant poison or Instant main hand and crippling off hand" [Shananagans, 2025-08-30]; "bleeds may work but you cant hold him at cc(blinds,gouge,grenade etc.) while your bleeds up" [Caesa, 2025-07-14].
6. **Cleanse before Vanish**: "restorative potion, anti venoms, elixirs of poison resistance, and jungle remedy to cleanse as much debuffs off yourself as possible then vanish" [Balake (player), 2023-12-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79624#p79624); Dwarves get Stoneform for poison/disease/bleed [Dannyp92 (player), 2023-12-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79623#p79623).
7. Note "Vanish is also useless when in melee range. If I'm being attacked and vanish, I get broken out immediately" [Madmane (player), 2025-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158351#p158351) — Sprint/Gouge distance first.

Poisons for PvP: Instant main hand + Crippling off hand (Shananagans; Gottabe's "hemo/envenom ... crippling + instant"), Mind-numbing vs casters, Wound vs healers ("We might finally able to penetrate bear druids" [Edgarek (player), 2024-10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103870#p103870)); off-hand procs are rarer by design (Atreidon); Deadly Throw + Throwing Weapon Specialization applies the off-hand poison at range ("improve deadly throw with crippling is one of the most cheesy thing you can use in PVP" [Boeuff (player), 2025-05-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131536#p131536)).

## Multi-target

Combo points persist per target since CC2 (Kick/Gouge/Blind on a second target no longer wipes them — the old loss was the class's main PvP complaint [Huma (player), 2023-04-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42912#p42912)); "It's always good to have Blind on a mouse over macro" (Shananagans; roid-macros needed for @mouseover [Werefox (player), 2023-04-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41265#p41265)). Smoke Bomb is "extremely situational at best" (Tankmeister). Engineering: grenades, Goblin Rocket Helm, Thornpod/Siege Bomber AoE stuns on the BiS list.

## Battleground roles (amanagor, consensus with FrankFankledank)

"In av rogue is good at harassing, recapping bunkers/ or capping towers in advance. In ab they are good at getting ninja caps, especially if 2 rogues play together. In warsong they are good at defending the flag room and making the efc pop cds to get out. They are also necessary to catch an enemy efc ... Rogues are not made for a mid fight" [amanagor (player), 2025-02-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119499#p119499); "Rogues get piled on the moment they show themselves anywhere near the bulk of the group and so are relegated to flag room sapping and attacking AV towers" [FrankFankledank (player), 2025-02-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119497#p119497). "competitive pvp in vanilla and turtle wow is not 2V2, its bgs. Specficaly wsg and AB ... i much rather face a premade comp without a rogue than a premade comp with 1 rogue" [amanagor (player), 2025-02-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120802#p120802).

## Matchups

- Feral druid: avoid; "You wont win fights against feral druids (and paladins) unless they play it pisspoor ... stall out the fight until someone with magic damage can come help you" [Atreidon, 2025-06-30]; fight indoors "where their roots, travel form, and cat passive speed doesn't come into play" [Bayanni (player), 2025-06-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137625#p137625); Catseye goggles/elixir to catch cat form, Restorative Potion for Faerie Fire, "they just need a Faerie fire" (Caesa). Post-1.18.0 counter-view: "Rogues MELT druids. Even in bearform" [Frantsel (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156428#p156428) (poison build).
- Warrior: "Rogue is warrior's one particular 1v1 'counter'" [Bigsmerf (player), 2024-12-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114197#p114197); "the go-to for killing a prot warrior was garotte into hemo to rack up 5 stacks of deadly poison into rupture ... while you sit in their deadzone with your 80% poison slow" (pre-CC2 crippling values) [Crake (player), 2025-10-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153991#p153991).
- Paladin: bad matchup (Atreidon); "Only thing could help - consumables" vs stuns [Gottabe (player), 2024-07-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99343#p99343).
- Priest: with the BG damage debuff a "4.3k HP ... Naxx gear" priest survived a Cheap Shot→Kidney combo before 1.18.0 but not after [Zvyrhol (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153745#p153745).
- Hunters/pets: pets follow you through Vanish (bug; ticket 2891); "Hunters are super strong vs rogues specially if your orc" [Donall (player), 2023-08-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56666#p56666).

## Common mistakes

- Fighting in the mid-field zerg instead of objectives (amanagor/FrankFankledank).
- Opening on ferals/paladins/geared warriors with cooldowns up (Atreidon).
- DoTs or DoT poisons on a target you need to Blind/Gouge/Sap (Shananagans, Caesa).
- Vanishing with DoTs on and in melee range (Balake, Madmane).
- Playing a Noxious/zug build and expecting control: "They're super predictable, they have no means of locking me down" [Crake (player), 2025-10-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154788#p154788).
- Assuming stealth hides you at short range (detection jank).
