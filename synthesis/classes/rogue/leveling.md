# Rogue — Leveling guide (1–60)

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Leveling advice below is almost entirely 1.18.0-era (Aug 2025 – Mar 2026); 1.18.1 did not touch the early-game abilities used here (Ambush, Backstab, Remorseless Attacks, Camouflage, Precision) and only lightly reduced Opportunity and low-rank poison scaling (see `assassination-melee-dps.md` and `combat-melee-dps.md`). Endgame builds (what to respec into at 58–60) are covered in `combat-melee-dps.md`, `assassination-melee-dps.md` and `subtlety-melee-dps.md`; PvP leveling in `assassination-pvp.md` / `subtlety-pvp.md`.

## Which spec

Three leveling paths are documented, and the forum's own consensus shifted after the 1.18.0 patch (2025-08-15):

- **Ambush/Backstab (Subtlety→Combat hybrid, dagger)** — the most repeated recommendation from 2025-08 onward. "Regardless if it's retail, or private sever I have always lvled up to 56-58 ambushing and backstabbing" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-08-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148075#p148075); "Out of all the leveling specs I have tried, this is the most fun and most rogueish" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-10-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157987#p157987); "Ambush rogue is a great leveling spec. I have 0 issues with taking down mobs. 1 Ambush brings a mob down to 50% HP" [SvenS2 (player), 2025-10-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157947#p157947); "Most rogue-ish - ambush" [Noephix (player), 2025-05-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132954#p132954).
- **Combat Swords/Axes/Maces (Sinister Strike)** — the "tried and true" vanilla default, still recommended for ease of gearing: "Easiest to gear - combat swords" [Noephix, 2025-05-25]; "Combat swords is usually the best and simplest way for leveling" [Rachdingue (player), 2025-09-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152024#p152024); "Many dungeons feature good blue sword from quests. So I think that going combat+sword is most reliable option" [Xudo (player), 2025-03-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123674#p123674). But post-1.18.0 several levelers call it the weakest option: "Easily the weakest option I've tried — and it's not even close. Leveling as Combat felt rough enough that I swapped back to daggers after just a few hours" [Sslushee (player), 2025-11-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158547#p158547); this is **contested** — Rachdingue and Xudo still recommend it, Sslushee found it weak with the current tree.
- **Assassination (Noxious Assault) from level 40** — not a full 10–60 path (Noxious Assault is the level-40 capstone) but the dominant recommendation for anyone questing/dungeoning from 40 onward: "Just play Assaa… on 40 You have Nox assault. This skill hit with poisons like a truck… Rogue right now is very powerfull" [Bless18 (player), 2025-09-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152349#p152349); "Mobs are melting … I'm killing mobs about 75% faster than I was with my Ambush/Backstab build" after respeccing at 40 [Sslushee, 2025-11-06]. Trade-off: loses the early Ambush/Camouflage/Precision spike.
- Subtlety full-support (`subtlety-melee-dps.md`'s build) is explicitly **not** recommended solo: "I do not recommend going full sub if you are solo leveling some big talents rely on your party" [Rachdingue (player), 2025-09-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152174#p152174); "Sub is terrible right now. It has never been a PvE spec" [SvenS2 (player), 2025-10-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157944#p157944).

Bracket-level roadmap combining spec with content, from an experienced PvE/PvP rogue: "15 - 40 ambush or 1 cp evis spam. 40-60 noxious assault and envenom. Mc - AQ gear noxious assault and envenom. AQ + gear Combat backstab spec" [Grizb37 (player), 2025-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158295#p158295). This is a single-source but internally consistent frame used throughout this file: **Ambush/Backstab 10–40 → (optionally) Assassination/Noxious Assault 40–60/dungeons → respec to the raid spec you want at 58–60**.

## Talent orders (10–60)

No single post gives every point from 10 to 60; the table below is the union of the two most detailed, mutually consistent guide posts. All levels/points are as literally stated by the sources; use with the talent calculator (`talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue`) to confirm current prerequisites.

### Continuation for players who stay Ambush/Backstab past 44 instead of respeccing to Assassination at 40 [Shananagans, 2025-08-30 / 2025-12-06]

| Level | Tree | Talent | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 45–47 | Assassination | Improved Poisons | 3/3 |
| 48 | Assassination | Envenom | 1/1 |
| 49–51 | Assassination | Efficient Poisons | 3/3 |
| 52–53 | Assassination | Vigor | 2/2 |
| 54–58 | Assassination | Seal Fate | 5/5 |

By this point Camouflage and Improved Ambush are both still kept; "we don't have +5% hit from Precision and we don't have Improved Blade Tactics", which Shananagans accepts because "by now you should have picked up a few pieces of gear that has hit % on them" (Blackstone Ring, Tarnished Elven Ring, Stromshroud Armor set +2% hit, Rune of the Wildhammer Clan +1%). Alternative weave if hit feels bad: alternate 1 point Efficient Poisons / 1 point Precision from 49–54, then Vigor 55–56. **Note**: this continuation predates the level-40 "respec straight into full Noxious Assault" advice that later posters (Sslushee, Bless18) prefer — it is presented by its own author as one option among several, not the only path.

### Alternative dungeon-oriented order, 10–35 [SvenS2 (player), 2025-11-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158613#p158613)

| Level | Talent | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 10–19 | Camouflage 2, Improved Gouge 3, Improved Ambush 3, Remorseless Attacks 2 | 10 |
| 20–29 | Opportunity 5, Improved Backstab 3, Camouflage (to 5/5) 2 | 10 |
| 30–35 | Camouflage (finish) 1, Precision 5 | 6 |

"From here on out you can experiment with other talents. I recommend specting into things that further boost your Ambush ... Malice, Murder, Lethality, Serrated Blades and Initiative. For dungeons, some extra points in Improved Blade Tactics can be quite useful." Rotation for this build: "Ambush -> Gauge -> Backstab -> Eviscerate. If the target is not dead, use Sinister Strike or repeat the Gauge -> Backstab rotation." This build front-loads **Improved Gouge** and **Precision** (hit) earlier than Shananagans' order — the two sources disagree on whether Precision belongs before or after level 44 (**contested**, gear-dependent).

### Older mixed order (pre-1.18.0 wording, same shape) [Terryleelolorz (player), 2025-03-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=123708#p123708)

Assassination Remorseless Attacks 2/2 → Subtlety Opportunity 5/5, Improved Ambush 3/3 → Combat Swift Strikes 2/2 (**removed in the 1.18.0 revision** — skip), Improved Gouge 3/3 or Lightning Reflexes 3/5, Improved Backstab 3/3, Precision 3/5 → Subtlety Camouflage 5/5. Superseded where it conflicts with the two builds above (Swift Strikes talent no longer exists).

### Combat Swords leveling (traditional default)

No full point-by-point order is given; sourced fragments: "Try to get Riposte asap. It's very strong while leveling" [Yasara (player), 2024-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=108703#p108703), with a 31-point PvE sword-build link (`https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=AAAAAAAAAAAAAAaAoYIBAoBSCYBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA%3D`) predating the 1.18.0 revision; "the shimmy dance for Frontstabbing" (Opportunity → Improved Backstab → Lethality, grabbing Malice and Remorseless as prerequisites) is offered as a dagger-frontstab alternative "near 40", or "Riposte rush with Precision after is a standard faux-warrior Combat option" [Trymv1 (player), 2023-06-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49275#p49275). Full point-by-point order for Combat Swords past ~30 is a **gap**.

### Respec points and why

- **Level 40**: capstone Noxious Assault becomes available; multiple 2025-09..11 posts recommend switching straight to Assassination here if going that route rather than waiting (Sslushee's own account: switched at 40, "I don't miss Precision at all... killing mobs about 75% faster"). The true Assassination build "doesn't really come into it's own till level 43" (Precision) "and better if you hold off till your 50's" if you want Precision along with the poison talents [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151001#p151001) — **contested** timing between "respec at 40 and accept missing hit" and "wait to 50s for Precision".
- **Level 58**: the standard point across sources ("At level 58, you should re-spec... this gives you 2 levels of practice with the new build" [Shananagans, 2025-12-06]) to fully commit to the level-60 raid/PvP spec while still leveling.
- Respec is assumed cheap/expected: "Don't worry about a build so much while you're leveling as you'll most likely respec at 60" [Devildog (player), 2025-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120087#p120087).

## Skill training priority

The sources say very little about which spell *ranks* to skip; most training advice is about weapon skill and class-quest timing rather than spell ranks:

- **Weapon skill training/books**: a book-quest chain gives +5 to a chosen weapon skill ("If you would like to read more on how to do the weapon book quest to get + damage to your wepon skill here is a great post about it" [Shananagans, 2025-09-01], referencing thread t16790, not itself extracted here — gap on the exact quest steps); weapon skill is "capped at 315" per player testing (Shananagans).
- **Poisons**: the poison-crafting skill and the first ranks of Instant/Deadly Poison come from a level-20 class-quest chain (Alliance SI:7/Westfall tower; Horde Cleft of Shadow/Barrens tower — community wiki, not staff-verified in this corpus); "And don't do your poison class quest before you get Vanish" [Sigune (player), 2025-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132085#p132085) (Hardcore-motivated but a sound general caution — the quest sends you into a dangerous pocket). Higher poison ranks (e.g. Deadly Poison V, Handbook-taught Corrosive Poison II) come from vendors/drops rather than trainers (staff CC2 post, see `assassination-melee-dps.md`).
- **Sap**: the level-55 Manual of Improved Sap IV is called out by a player-suggestion thread as a high-investment, situational skill in group content ("Puts a rogue at much more risk, and might cost a corpse run to the whole group... Hardcore moment" [player, f47 t3645, 2022]) — treat it as optional for pure leveling.
- **Class quests**: "Do your class quests at a higher level" [Mac (player), 2022-09-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26396#p26396) — a Hardcore-forum tip that generalizes: rogue class quests (Disguise, poison chain, Deadly Throw-era weapon quests) are not rushed the moment they unlock.
- Exact "train rank X, skip rank Y" tables for Sinister Strike/Eviscerate/Rupture/etc. are not present anywhere in the corpus — **gap**; assume "train every new rank of your core abilities, skip niche ones (Expose Armor, Distract) until they matter."

## Playstyle by bracket

### Stealth and pulling mechanics (Turtle-specific, applies at every bracket)

Turtle's faster server tick changes stealth math versus vanilla: "Turtle wow serverticks are luch much faster than they were in classic wow (0.04seconds vs 0.4seconds) ... If a mob notices you close but hasnt qute spotted you yet, he turn for you and will attack you if you dont back away quickly." Practical rules from a leveling rogue's own testing [Atreidon (player), 2025-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158352#p158352):

1. Never approach a mob's face in stealth — their forward detection is stronger than the "grace period" classic players expect; approach from behind.
2. Use Distract against mobs that might turn while you approach.
3. Don't linger in melee range before opening — "Shiftloot pickpoket and opener. Dont give the mob time to react."
4. Never run through a mob's hitbox — no stealth level hides you inside it.
5. Some mobs (packs of dogs/hyenas, bats, anything with "tracker" in its name) have drastically higher detection and can see through stealth almost entirely — learn to avoid them rather than sneak them.

This detection jank is corroborated independently in PvP context ("the tick rate for noticing other players in stealth is like 1 second" [Crake (player), 2025-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158381#p158381); "This has been like this since i started in 2022. It needs fixing because its just ridiculous that not using stealth to sneak past lower lvl mobs is better than using the stealth ability" [Grizb37 (player), 2025-11-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158386#p158386)) — bots should not assume vanilla-classic stealth ranges.

### Kill rotation (Ambush/Backstab build)

"Alrighty then... You lickity spit to your target and ambush (45% chance to crit) you sinister strike 3-4 times and it dies. You hit stealth and again lickity split to you next target but this time you have the remorseless attack buff which adds 40% crit to your already 45% ambush crit buff" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-08-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148075#p148075). Fuller version once Gouge/pirouette is available: "Ambush into gouge, pool a little energy and pirouette to the back side and backstab. I now have 3-4 combo points. (1) how much health they have left, not much? Eviscerate, call it a day, get back in stealth and do it again. (2) They have more health than I like, Check to see if crippling up on mob, rupture and kite ... as soon as rupture runs it course, gouge, pirouette to backside and backstab" [Shananagans, 2025-08-24/2025-08-30]. Stealth's cooldown is 5 s, so with Camouflage's 15% move-speed-in-stealth bonus a rogue can usually chain-pull without downtime: "Your cool down on stealth is 5 sec... this is my Kung Fu and it is strong" (same post). "1 Ambush brings a mob down to 50% HP" even without full crit talents [SvenS2, 2025-10-31].

### Kill rotation (Assassination/Noxious Assault, from 40)

"Open each fight with cheap or garrote, then Envenom and spam Nox… You wont find time for SnD or Evi cuz Nox just melt enemies… Apply Instant poison on both as fast as possible daggers (they could be even White) and just spam Nox with envenom" [Bless18 (player), 2025-09-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152349#p152349); short version for dungeons: "Cheap Shot → Envenom → Slice & Dice (off the Ruthlessness point) → Noxious Assault twice → Dead" [Sslushee (player), 2025-11-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158547#p158547). Double Instant Poison is preferred over Deadly at this stage: "double Instant poison (at least at 40) trumps both instant/deadly and deadly/deadly... the 5 stack cap on deadly ends up hindering your dps" [Sslushee, 2025-11-06].

### Downtime and energy

Energy regeneration is the class's main leveling complaint: "waiting for that energy tick is so damn boring. I wish they added something like Combat Potency talent, but baseline" [Laysson (player), 2025-05-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132677#p132677); "This is exactly what is refraining me to level up a Rogue" (same post). Practical fixes while leveling: pool energy rather than mash buttons; use Thistle Tea ("We have thistle tea :P" [Aulisia (player), 2025-06-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136129#p136129)); Blade Rush (Combat, row 6 since 1.18.0) reduces the time between energy ticks by an amount tied to Agility, "a must have talent for combat... the only way (not including gear) to get more energy regeneration back" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-09-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151497#p151497) — but "Dont use blade flurry while leveling, it is absolute trash until you get like 800 agility" [Exaltus (player), 2025-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149418#p149418), so Blade Flurry itself is not an AoE/energy tool at leveling gear levels.

### Multiple mobs / packs

Rogue has no baseline AoE ("rogues 'are the only class without any baseline aoe'" [Derrurak (player), 2023-06-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49203#p49203)); the class is built around 1v1 pulls. Combo points persist per target since Class Changes 2 but are still built one target at a time, so packs are handled by CC (Gouge/Blind/Sap) and picking targets off, not cleave. "If you really, really want some form of AoE, play combat" (relatively — Blade Flurry toggle) [SvenS2 (player), 2025-05-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132963#p132963). See `combat-melee-dps.md` and `assassination-melee-dps.md` for the (limited) AoE toolkit at 60.

## Stats and gear while leveling

- **Hit/Precision**: front-loaded in the SvenS2 order (level 30–35), deferred to the high-40s/50s in Shananagans' order because early gear rarely has hit and the Ambush/Backstab build "attacks from behind" reducing the practical need — **contested timing**, both agree it matters eventually.
- **Weapon choice**: daggers throughout for the Ambush/Backstab path ("you just need to get some daggers and don't let anyone fool you, they are out there... Deadmines has a great dagger, Shadowfang Keep has a great dagger... you can even use caster daggers as long as they are slow and has a higher damage range than the weapon you are carrying" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-08-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148289#p148289)); for Combat Swords, dungeon quest swords are called "reliable" (Xudo, above).
- **Enchants**: for Assassination-from-40, Agility (+15/weapon) is preferred by the endgame guide over Crusader for the Seal Fate synergy (see `assassination-melee-dps.md`); Lifesteal vs Agility is discussed only at the endgame-gear level in that file, not specifically for leveling — treat as a **gap** for leveling-specific enchant choice.
- **Poisons while leveling/soloing**: no DoT poisons on a target you plan to control — "Do not use a DOT, damage over time effect ie rupture, deadly poison or a weapon or trinket that puts a dot on your target. Use double Instant poison or Instant main hand and crippling off hand" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149402#p149402); against humanoids that flee, "Instant main hand, off hand crippling" [Shananagans, 2025-08-24].
- **Addons** recommended by leveling posters: SuperWoW (fixes multi-caster targeting so you can tell which caster is actually casting) with TankPlates (nameplate colour = threat level: red safe, yellow ease off, green Vanish, blue = CC'd, don't break it) [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149464#p149464); TrinketMenu for swapping trinkets out of combat; EasyPoisons/EzPoison for one-click poison application, "Also work with the new poisons, Dissolvent and Corrosive" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158306#p158306).

## Route

## Profession pairings

- **Engineering** is repeatedly named the class's best pairing, for both PvE and PvP survivability: "engineering is probably the best for rogues" [B3tabob (player), 2022-08-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23187#p23187) (question about Leatherworking as an alternative went unanswered — **gap**); "reroll engineering if you plan to play pvp seriously" [Likaleo (player), 2024-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86159#p86159). A Hardcore-specific endorsement gives the mechanism: "I went engi on my rogue (also immortal) in HC since they pickpocket more than enough health potions and sprint is better than a swiftness potion while also being on a different cooldown... the energy system means I can use bombs when out of energy in order to damage and stun simultaneously while energy regenerates" [Gladeshadow (player), 2022-11-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29282#p29282).
- **Alchemy + Herbalism** is the self-sufficient/solo alternative: "I already have alchemy and herbalism at max level and make gold pretty easily" [Vlad1994 (player), 2024-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86115#p86115); a Hardcore rogue player explicitly swaps away from Engineering to avoid a specific trap (see Common mistakes below): "For rogue, pick alchemy+herbalism instead" [Sigune (player), 2025-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132085#p132085).
- Leatherworking and other professions are not evaluated anywhere in the corpus for Rogue — **gap**.

## Hardcore-mode differences

Rogue is discussed as one of the easier Hardcore classes because of its escape kit, in contrast to Warrior:

- "If you have atleast little experience as rogue, go for it. i rarely died as rogue, mostly due to pvp warmode. You have alot of CC and defensive CDs [Evasion, Vanish]. Make sure you level first aid ASAP, you can use bandages in combat --> gouge into bandage" [Horyss (player), 2022-09-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26386#p26386); "Do your class quests at a higher level" [Mac (player), 2022-09-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26396#p26396).
- "They are indeed squishier, but have more ways to avoid or escape a fight. Vanish, Sprint, Evasion and various CC options" [SvenS2 (player), 2025-05-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131970#p131970); "Rogue has many ways of getting out of bad situations from blind, gouge, kidney punch, mace stun, vanish, sprint, distract, sap, as well as some ways to double-down on sketchy pulls with evasion and blade flurry, or even preparation to reset your cooldowns, and all of this topped with the fact that rogues can stealth to avoid combat. They still suffer from the same gear dependency that warriors do, but they have far more tools at their disposal for when things inevitably go wrong" [Voodoochile (player), 2025-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132032#p132032).
- A player with both an "immortal warrior and an immortal rogue": "rogue is way easier than warrior... Next, Rogue is way less gear reliant than warrior... make an Ambush build instead. You are going to crit for half of the mob health from Ambush with 100% crit chance due to the Remorseless Attacks and Improved Ambush talents. I am currently leveling a vagrant artisan rogue with level 5 copper daggers as my main weapons, and this build is still going strong at level 53" [Sigune (player), 2025-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132085#p132085) — i.e. the Ambush/Backstab leveling build above is explicitly the recommended Hardcore build, and it tolerates poor weapons better than Combat does.
- Specific Hardcore cautions from the same post: "Once you get to level 22 as rogue and get Vanish, you are set to success. Before that, be super careful.
