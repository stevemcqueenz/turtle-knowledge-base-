# Rogue — Combat/avoidance — Tank ("rogue tank", niche and staff-curbed)

Turtle added two custom rogue tools in 2022 (**Flourish** — a finisher giving +20% parry — and **Agitating Poison** — a level-60 threat poison), which created an avoidance-based rogue tank. Staff then curbed it (40-s cooldown on Flourish, 1.17.0). The sources are **contested** on whether it is viable today: a small group still tanks with it in 2026, most players call it a meme, and staff explicitly said rogues "are not going to be true tanks". This file records what the practitioners say so a bot can imitate one where a group tolerates it; it is not an endorsement of the role for raids.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Nothing in 1.18.0/1.18.1 changed Flourish or Agitating Poison except charges (2026-03-03) and Blade Flurry becoming baseline.

## Overview

- **Role**: avoidance off-tank/dungeon tank; raid main-tank only on a handful of melee-only bosses (historically Patchwerk hateful strikes, Loatheb, Razuvious start, Twin Emperors, AQ40 trash — [Hodenmaik (player), 2023-08-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55641#p55641); "our first kill of twins in aq 40 was saved by a rogue who took over aggro and tanked the boss to completion" [Terrydavis (player), 2026-02-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168122#p168122)).
- **How it works (practitioner)**: "With Flourish active I can easily keep myself at 75% or higher avoidance in T1-T2 equivalent gear ... Evasion can then be used as a cooldown that will make the rogue completely unhittable by melee attacks ... Riposte is usable by a tank rogue nearly on cooldown, meaning that targets will be disarmed for several seconds" [Trismegistos (player, tanks SM→BWL), 2023-05-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45534#p45534). "Flourish ... not only reduces damage taken, it also grants the rogue Riposte procs ... and parry haste, which further increases auto attack damage and poison procs, allowing for Agitating Poison to build up threat" [Trismegistos (player), 2023-08-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55820#p55820).
- **Weaknesses (consensus)**: no taunt, no on-demand threat, no spell mitigation, low armor/HP, stun-vulnerable, single-target combo points ("Rogue is absolutely not viable for crowd tanking because you need to build combo points on single target" [Xudo (player, warrior tank), 2022-09-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25185#p25185)); "you either die instantly or do not require any healing at all" [Torta (staff), 1.17.0](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868); threat is a Nature poison so **poison-immune mobs cannot be tanked** ("a surprising number of enemies are immune to it ... comparable to Paladins suddenly losing Righteous Fury" [Lomikare1 (player), 2026-02-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167928#p167928)); a holy priest's single Smite pulls aggro from an AQ40-geared rogue tank [Valari (player), 2025-04-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128159#p128159).
- **Community standing**: **niche, contested**. Pro: "rogue tanking is on good spot. We dont need any new gear. You can gear in any phase and still have nice avoidance ... We just need a physical version of Agitating poison" [Lomikare1, 2026-02-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168126#p168126); "it's a fun and unique mechanic ... It's never going to be an entirely viable option, but it can definitely make group play more fun" [madferret (player), 2026-03-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169666#p169666). Contra: "Rogues should not be tanks" [amanagor (player), 2026-02-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167931#p167931); "Stop trying to make rogue tanking a thing" [Getplucked (player), 2023-06-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49193#p49193); "Rogue tank was gutted intently a long time ago ... all that avoidance gear ... rotting in the bank" [Gantulga (player), 2025-04-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128220#p128220); "hunters and rogues are not going to be tanks" (player summary of the CC2 staff posts, f63 2024-10).

## Official history (staff)

- 2021-11-11, reply to "Flourish (20% Parry) is almost the exact amount of avoidance needed to reach a 100% avoidance Rogue tank": "İntentional, no clue who can pull off rogue tanking, but they should have the required mitigation tool now." [Dragunovi (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13766#p13766).
- 1.16.1 batch 1 [Torta (staff), 2022-07-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20251#p20251): **Agitating Poison** (60): "Each strike has a 20% chance of poisoning the enemy which instantly inflicts 67 to 85 Nature damage, and causes additional threat. 115 charges." Flourish (42, 25 energy, +20% parry 3–7 s by combo point — per the timeline digest).
- 1.17.0 [Torta (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868): "Flourish now has a 40 second cooldown to avoid cases where the Rogue would become avoidance capped. Developer Note: Rogue tanking, while a cool and unique concept, has one major flaw. With avoidance based tanks, you either die instantly or do not require any healing at all. It leads to some encounters being trivialised, such as Patchwerk. It has also been brought to our attention that some crafty rogues use flourish to farm some end game bosses alone or in a much smaller group than intended." Cooldown fixed 2023-10-03 [Torta (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=68406#p68406) (a player saw "6.67 min" before the fix [Scruffles (player), 2023-10-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69755#p69755)).
- 2023-07-05: "Rogues can no longer dodge or parry attacks by Raid bosses if they are attempting to solo it." [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52323#p52323); players read this as an 80% avoidance cap "when 'solo'" that also hit small groups ("any group of 10 or less people can't reach avoidance cap ... can't tank anything in UBRS" [Bayanni (player), 2023-08-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55692#p55692); "Your avoidance is capped at 80%" [Bayanni (player), 2023-09-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=64839#p64839)). Removed: "Removed a deprecated safeguard that would kick in when a Rogue attempted to solo a world boss." [Torta (staff), 2024-06-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97844#p97844).
- 2024-04-01 (April Fools thread "2024 March 32"): "Rogues can now tank much easier with 50% increased baseline threat generation" [Akalix (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91795#p91795) — a joke post; not a real change.
- Agitating Poison: no spell-power scaling [Torta (staff), 2025-06-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134147#p134147); "now correctly uses charges as intended instead of lasting indefinitely" [Shadowsong (staff), 2026-03-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169965#p169965); Throwing Weapon Specialization + Deadly Throw can apply an off-hand Agitating Poison at range [Torta (staff), 2025-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124967#p124967).
- The "3000 gold an hour" farming rumour used to justify the Flourish nerf is disputed by rogues: "At most you got 120g/h from HFQ disenchant boss runs" [Gantulga (player), 2023-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70371#p70371); "You need a shaman and a paladin with you the whole time" [Bayanni (player), 2023-10-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70147#p70147).

## Stat priority (practitioners)

Avoidance (dodge, parry, defense) → stamina → threat stats (hit, agility, weapon skill for poison procs) → resistances for caster bosses. "A 60 rogue with some mediocre gear should sit at around 70% avoidance with flourish up and talented properly, and getting some good gear should bring you close to 85-90% avoidance and evasion being up bringing them well above the 102% avoidance requirement to not be crushed" [Ssai (player), 2022-09-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25123#p25123) (theorycraft, pre-1.17.0 Flourish); a full-mitigation set is "about 0.8% above cap" [Derrurak (player), 2023-06-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49177#p49177). "There are two general situations of tanking ... You have to gear up from the blues and be viable option for chosen role all the time" [Xudo, 2022-09-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25346#p25346).

## Single-target rotation / threat

1. Pre-pull: Agitating Poison on the main hand (and off-hand, or Deadly Throw with TWS for a ranged pull); Slice and Dice and Flourish up from stealth if possible.
2. "I do not expect to get more than one or two combo points before I'm swapping targets, meaning that I will be using them exclusively to refresh Slice and Dice & Flourish, or to snap a two-second Kidney Shot to stop a runner" [Trismegistos, 2023-05-11]; on bosses "juggling Flourish and Slice and Dice uptime while trying to weave in one or two 5-combo Eviscerates" [Trismegistos, 2023-08-04].
3. Riposte on every parry (threat + disarm); Sinister Strike as builder; Ghostly Strike for +dodge windows.
4. Evasion as the "shield wall" (unhittable by melee for 15 s; 4-min cooldown baseline since CC2, 3.5 min with the old Endurance talent).
5. Engineering fills the gaps: "target dummies have an area taunt ... grenades are great for area threat during fights (though your parry and dodge chance drop to 0% for the duration of the cast)" [Trismegistos, 2023-05-11].
6. Poison-immune targets: cannot hold threat; hand off. Casters: resistance gear/engineering reflectors; "it is fully within the rogue's capabilities to tank bosses like Baron Geddon and Shazzrah" (Trismegistos).

## Multi-target

Not viable as a crowd/pull tank: "Rogue is absolutely not viable for crowd tanking because you need to build combo points on single target. If you switch the target then you lose acquired combo points" [Xudo (player), 2022-09-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25185#p25185) — combo points persist per target since CC2, which softens this (Kick/Gouge on an add no longer wipes the main target's points), but a rogue tank still cannot build threat on several adds at once the way a Sunder/Consecration tank can. Practical compromise: change targets every 1–2 combo points to spread Agitating Poison procs across a pack, and toggle the baseline Blade Flurry (since 1.18.0) to hit a second target at −20% energy regeneration; do not expect to hold a full pull.

## Cooldowns

- **Flourish**: the core mitigation finisher, but capped at a 40-s cooldown since 1.17.0 specifically to stop permanent avoidance uptime (staff note above) — plan pulls around it, do not expect it up every fight.
- **Evasion**: unhittable-by-melee cooldown, 4 min baseline since CC2 (3.5 min with the old Endurance talent) — use it as the "oh-shit" button on a dangerous swing, not on pull.
- **Riposte**: not a cooldown but only usable after a parry; treat it as a bonus threat/disarm proc rather than something to plan around.
- **Preparation**: resets Vanish/Blind/Sprint if talented, not Flourish or Evasion; of limited use to the tank build (it is a Subtlety talent competing with tank-relevant Combat/utility points).
- **Vanish**: aggro reset only in genuine emergencies; it does not help hold threat.

## Role strategy

- Take dungeon and off-tank roles, melee-only bosses, and emergency pickups; never volunteer for tank-swap or stun-heavy fights ("If the boss uses any form of stun or other hard crowd control, you can't tank. If taunting the boss is necessary, you can't tank" [Hodenmaik, 2023-08-03]).
- Expect party friction (amanagor/Sylveria replies) and healers who dislike spiky avoidance tanking (Kairion: "being completely irrelevant as a healer in a fight is unfun" [2023-08-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=55661#p55661)).
- Level 60 only: Agitating Poison has no lower ranks ("I'm leveling a rogue now and would like to experiment with tanking but I really can't because of the level 60 req." [Glaive (player), 2025-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151003#p151003)).

## Common mistakes

- Attempting raid bosses that stun, fear, taunt-swap or are poison-immune.
- Standing in front of casters expecting avoidance to help (it does not).
- Casting grenades while being hit (avoidance drops to 0 during the cast — Trismegistos).
- Relying on Flourish uptime: it has a 40-s cooldown; plan Evasion/Ghostly Strike around it.
- Believing the April-Fools "50% baseline threat" note.
