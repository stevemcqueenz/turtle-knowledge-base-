# Rogue — Assassination — PvP ("Noxious/poison zug" build)

The 1.18.0 Assassination kit produced a PvP variant that trades control for raw poison damage. Sources agree it is strong against plate/armor and weak against players who survive the opener; it triggered the loudest "nerf rogues" threads of late 2025. See `subtlety-pvp.md` for the control builds and shared PvP material (races, BG roles, gear).

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20).

## Overview

- **Role**: burst/pressure melee in BGs and world PvP; "Poison rogues are a pve spec. They pump damage, but are terrible at control. It is the equivalent of getting beat up by a combat spec rogue back in vanilla. If they outgear you, they will destroy you, but if you can survive the burst, they will flop instantly" [Crake (player), 2025-10-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153979#p153979).
- **Strengths (sources)**: "poison works perfect against plate characters. If you are horde you will be fighting paladins 90% of the time so it's worth it" [Rachdingue (player), 2025-09-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152457#p152457); "Rogues are (outrageously) melting thru Tanks like butter" [Manletow (player), 2025-09-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153725#p153725); "Now they're actually a threat to me in bear form. Before I jus laughed at them with 14k armor" [Grizb37 (player, feral), 2025-10-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153923#p153923); "good damage against plate, but no prep and slows. I guess for BG's it's viable" [Gottabe (player), 2026-04-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174771#p174771) (post-1.18.1); gear-agnostic ("fresh 60s should not be completely cucked out of participating in pvp" — Crake defending it).
- **Weaknesses (sources)**: "as an assassination rogue, I sacrifice control (won't get a max kidney shot, won't get slow from crippling poison)" [Tankmeister (player), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154255#p154255); "Assassination rogues are just glass cannons without much control, and still do less damage than arcane mages, and they need to be in melee range ... good luck Pvping with no crippling, and mind numbing for casters. its just a zug spec that dies to anyone with half a brain cell" [Snkx (player), 2025-10-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154789#p154789); fast attacks "suffer heavily from thorns effects" and can be blocked to 0 damage so the poison cannot proc [Crake (player), 2025-10-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154550#p154550).
- **Community standing**: **contested — "broken" to non-rogues, "zug spec" to rogues**. Non-rogue: "Rogues are totally broken atm" [Aristany (player), 2025-10-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156423#p156423); "One or two dissolvent rogues and your holy pal is done for" [Turboman (player), 2026-03-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170555#p170555); "No rogue nerf yet? ... A plain -30% damage should be ok" [Manolothegreat (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173527#p173527) (post-1.18.1). Rogue side: "the only difference between instant and dissolvent is nature vs physical, at the expense of less damage. People in pvp don't stack tonnes of nature resist, so instant poison is 100% the better option" [Crake, 2025-10-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154534#p154534). Staff reaction: 1.18.1 poison low-rank scaling cuts, Noxious Assault 30% AP, Taste for Blood nerf and the Physical-poison creature restriction (see `assassination-melee-dps.md`), plus PvP dampening referenced by players ("Everyone give up playing rogues in pvp after adding pvp dampening?" [Gottabe (player), 2026-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175159#p175159), unanswered).

## Talent build

Same as the PvE Noxious Assault build (`assassination-melee-dps.md`): deep Assassination to Noxious Assault, Precision, Dual Wield Specialization; no Preparation, no Dirty Deeds ("if a rogue is specced into noxious assault, that means they DONT have dirty deeds OR preparation. This means their cheap shot is 60 energy, not 40" [Crake, 2025-10-02]). "for pvp you want Seal of Fate" [Kerenis (player), 2025-09-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152446#p152446) (single source; contradicts the PvE advice to drop it at low crit). Gottabe's post-1.18.1 description: "Noxious assault build, with fast daggers, both Instant poisons"; a hybrid "Hemo/Evenom ... like classic hemo, but with evenom (cripling + instant poison), have prep ... Using slow weapon on mainhand and fast offhand. I belive this build better against clothies" [Gottabe, 2026-04-11] — the hybrid needs Envenom at Assassination row 5 plus deep Subtlety; no link given.

Patch validity: 1.18.0 builds; 1.18.1 nerfed the numbers (NA 30% AP, Taste for Blood) and restricted Dissolvent/Corrosive to Elementals/Mechanicals/Giants/Undead, so the "dissolvent vs plate" advice is **superseded** (players are plate, not giants) — post-patch, Instant is the only damage poison vs players, and a feral reports Instant hitting "220-250/hit" [Wosator (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171992#p171992).

## Stat priority

Fast daggers (1.3) for proc rate; hit; crit/agility; stamina. Same reasoning as PvE (Jinkarasu, Shananagans); no PvP-specific weights. Gap.

## Opener and kill sequence (Crake's energy accounting, 1.18.0, pre-PvP-dampening)

"60 energy 4 second stun cheap shot -> 4 second stun = 2 energy ticks, so they get to 90 energy (sin rogues have 110 energy) meaning they can pop 2 noxious assaults, however then they wouldn't have any energy left over for a kidney, so they need to cheap shot -> noxious assault -> kidney shot at 4 combo points, meaning they lose out on a 6 second kidney, and instead only get a 4 second. That's 2 more energy ticks, with an 80% chance to refund the kidney, so they get at most another 40 energy ... giving another 2 noxious assaults ... each noxious assualt does approximately 300-400 armor-affected damage, plus two lots of 250ish poison damage ... about 800 damage total against an armored opponent ... That's 2400 damage, plus auto attacks ... 12 autos at 30% proc chance ... So you'll end up taking ~3150 - 3400 damage total. Yes that's a lot. Except the rogue is now at 0 energy. And you're not stunned" [Crake (player), 2025-10-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153979#p153979).

Practical sequence (union of Crake and Tankmeister):

1. Cheap Shot from stealth (60 energy).
2. **Envenom at 1–2 points immediately** ("I cheap shot -> pop envenom immediately, and refresh when needed" [Tankmeister, 2025-10-03]) — Crake notes this "will completely cuck your combo points and make your kidney do jack all" (4–5 s Kidney instead of 6): choose Envenom for damage vs Kidney length for control.
3. Noxious Assault ×2 → Kidney Shot at 4 points → Noxious Assault ×2 → Blind if trinketed (no Preparation to redo it).
4. Thistle Tea for one more Noxious Assault in the stun.
5. If the target survives at 0 energy: Sprint/Vanish out — "if you can survive the burst, they will flop instantly".

Poisons: Instant/Instant (Crake, Snkx); Dissolvent only vs Undead/Elemental/Giant/Mechanical after 1.18.1 (players are none of these); Crippling in the off-hand costs damage but restores a slow — Gottabe's hemo/envenom hybrid does this.

## Multi-target

None; the build has no cleave use in PvP; combo points persist per target (CC2).

## Role strategy

- Pick fights against plate/high-armor targets and healers who cannot kite; avoid casters that can root/kite and ferals that survive the opener.
- BG objectives as for any rogue (see `subtlety-pvp.md`).
- Expect insignia/trinket plays: with no Preparation, one trinket ends the control — commit only when the opener can kill.
- Duel etiquette in the sources: warriors complain about "acid poison"; players advise Blessing of Sanctuary, blocking fast hits to 0 and thorns as counters (Crake, 2025-10-06).

## Gear

As PvE Assassination plus PvP stamina pieces; Brisspvp's PvP BiS list is dagger-oriented (Kingsfall MH, Harbinger of Doom OH) but written before 1.18.0 — for this build the sources prefer 1.3-speed daggers over slow Naxx daggers (Shananagans). New 1.18.1 PvP items with DoT/crit reduction exist (Gottabe) — untested in the corpus.

## Common mistakes

- Spending combo points on Envenom then expecting a full Kidney (Crake).
- Using Dissolvent on players post-1.18.1 (it no longer applies).
- Fighting a target with thorns/Retribution Aura/Blessing of Sanctuary with 1.3 daggers (Crake).
- Staying in melee at 0 energy instead of resetting.
