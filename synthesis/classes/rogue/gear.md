# Rogue — Gear Guide

Gear recommendations by spec, role and bracket, with where to obtain each item where the sources state it. See synthesis/classes/rogue/*.md for full talent/rotation playbooks; this file covers gear only. Raid tier mapping: T1=Molten Core, T2=Blackwing Lair, T2.5=AQ40, T3=Naxxramas, T3.5=Karazhan 40.

Sources consulted: synthesis/classes/rogue/assassination-melee-dps.md, synthesis/classes/rogue/assassination-pvp.md, synthesis/classes/rogue/combat-melee-dps.md, synthesis/classes/rogue/combat-tank.md, synthesis/classes/rogue/subtlety-melee-dps.md, synthesis/classes/rogue/subtlety-pvp.md, synthesis/classes/rogue/leveling.md, structured/classes/rogue/*.yaml, https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=23023, https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=13918, https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=14264, https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=16538, structured/patches/1.18.1-changes.yaml

## Caveats
- Raid tier mapping used below (per structured/glossary.jsonl): T1=Molten Core, T2=Blackwing Lair, T2.5=AQ40, T3=Naxxramas, T3.5=Karazhan 40 (the Trickster set).
- The richest single item-level list in the corpus is Brisspvp's 8/9-Naxx-plus-AQ40-belt PvP BiS list (subtlety-pvp), reused here for both Subtlety and (partly) Combat PvP.
- Assassination gear is drawn mainly from Shananagans' 2025 guide-writer posts, which explicitly frame themselves as opinion ('jmho', 'IMHO') rather than a measured BiS list; treated as single-source/guide-writer authority throughout.
- Combat-tank gear (Ssai's 2022 avoidance list) predates Class Changes 2 (which removed Setup, Sleight of Hand and Endurance) and uses vanilla item names with no stated Turtle-specific verification; kept for reference only, flagged accordingly.
- The 10/19 Twink Rogue PvP BiS List (t14264) and BiS list thread (t13918) point to external Google Sheets/Discord spreadsheets not captured as text in this corpus; their item-level content could not be extracted.

**Stat priority**: "Speed of Daggers, + to Daggers, Critical Strike" — dagger speed dominates over raw damage/DPS rating because poison procs (not weapon damage) drive Assassination's output; weapon skill (capped at 315) is valued for pushing misses/glances off the table, indirectly raising poison-proc uptime. Two 1.3-speed daggers are preferred over one slow high-damage dagger; Flurry Axe x2 was tested and rejected because compensating for their 0.2s-slower speed would need a 15% extra-attack proc chance that Turtle's axes don't have.

- No dedicated raid-t1/raid-t2/raid-t2.5 item lists for Assassination or Combat beyond the general sets progression (Darkmantle/Stormshroud -> Bonescythe/Trickster) and a handful of MC/BWL-adjacent items; the corpus jumps from pre-raid to Naxx/Kara40 gearing.

- The Rogue class-weapon questline added in 1.18.1 (Timbermaw Hold, starter quest 42029, starter item 42307 from Peroth'arn) has no stated weapon stats in the extracted posts.
- 10/19 Twink PvP BiS List (t14264) and the general 'BiS list' thread (t13918) point to external Google Sheets and a Discord-pinned spreadsheet; their contents are not text in this corpus and could not be captured.
