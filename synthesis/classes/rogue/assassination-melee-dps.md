# Rogue — Assassination — Melee DPS (raid / dungeon, "Noxious Assault / poison rogue")

Covers the 1.18.0+ Assassination build built around **Noxious Assault**, **Envenom**, **Vigor** and fast daggers ("poison rogue", "NA rogue", "assa"). The pre-1.18.0 Assassination variants (Seal Fate/Backstab, "Envenom raiding" with Backstab) are covered as historical variants. Combat is a separate playbook (`combat-melee-dps.md`); the two are the raid-DPS alternatives and the forum argues about which is better, so both are written up.

All dates UTC. Eras: **pre-1.18.1** (before 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), **post-1.18.1** (from 2026-03-20). Within pre-1.18.1 the Assassination tree changed four times: Class Changes 2 (CC2, 2024-10-12 post, live 1.17.2), the 2024-12-20 poison-scaling round, the 2025-03-30 round (Vile Poisons/Envenom buffs, Deadly Throw rework) and 1.18.0 (2025-07-25 post, live 2025-08-15: Noxious Assault, Vigor rework, Dissolvent Poison, Blade Flurry baseline). 1.18.1 then nerfed the spec (poison scaling at low ranks, Taste for Blood, Noxious Assault) and restricted the Physical poisons. Every recommendation says which kit it was written for.

## Overview

- **Role**: melee DPS. The spec's identity per staff is "the Poison rogue": fast weapons, poisons on both hands, Noxious Assault as combo builder ("a new combo point generating abilities that constantly applies your poisons and works well with all weapon types" [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159)).
- **Strengths (sources)**: very high damage at low gear — "absurdly high dps even at low gear levels" [Crake (player), 2025-09-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152736#p152736); "Once you get noxious assault and the new poisons you feel so powerful" [Teerack (player), 2025-09-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150223#p150223); "Mobs are melting ... killing mobs about 75% faster than ... my Ambush/Backstab build ... topping DPS meters in every dungeon" [Sslushee (player), 2025-11-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158547#p158547). Not position dependent: "You don't need positioning to hit your attacks (not like backstab or ambush)" [Rachdingue (player), 2025-09-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152457#p152457). Physical poisons (Corrosive/Dissolvent) bypass armor and Nature immunity — but see the 1.18.1 restriction below.
- **Weaknesses (sources)**: flat gear scaling — "they start at 500dps in pure white gear, but only go up to about 700dps in full nax gear (around 1k dps in full nax gear + raid buffs and debuffs on boss). Meanwhile combat is running around with 700 agi and 1.3s energy ticks, dealing 2k dps" [Crake (player), 2026-03-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172282#p172282) (post-1.18.1); poison immunity/resistance of many raid mobs [Finpin (player), 2025-04-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127477#p127477); the spec "needs a lot of setup before you can get to your full damage output potential. You need to have S&D and Envenom up" (Finpin, same post); burns poison charges very fast on 1.3-speed daggers [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149679#p149679); Blade Flurry is a poor cleave for it ("Blade Flurry can be use by Assassination and Sub but it seems to be a bigger energy drain for those 2 specs. I wont use it on my Assassination rogue" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-08-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148289#p148289)); few fast endgame daggers ("There are so few 1.3/1.4 speed daggers in the game" [Sslushee (player), 2025-12-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162338#p162338)).
- **Content**: dungeons and MC–AQ-tier raids — favored (see standing); Naxx/Kara40-geared raiding — contested vs Combat; leveling from 40 — favored (see `leveling.md`); PvP — a "zug" burst variant exists (see `assassination-pvp.md`); hardcore — no specific advice in the sources.
- **Community standing**: **favored for fresh/mid-geared raiders, contested at the top end** (consensus that this is the ordering, from 2025-09 to 2026-03). The gearing ladder given by an experienced PvPer/PvEer: "15 - 40 ambush or 1 cp evis spam. 40-60 noxious assault and envenom. Mc - AQ gear noxious assault and envenom. AQ + gear Combat backstab spec." [Grizb37 (player), 2025-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158295#p158295). Same ladder by Crake: "This is definitely the way to go until you get up to 40% crit or higher in end game raiding" (about dropping Seal Fate) [Crake (player), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159151#p159151). Counter-view from raiders: "noxious assault is terrible, backstab is way more dmg... you just can't really put that point anywhere else" [Edem (player), 2025-11-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161043#p161043); the 2025-09 "endgame meta build" posted with named top parsers is 20/31/0 Combat, not Assassination [Bayanni (player), 2025-09-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=153716#p153716).
- **Post-1.18.1 status (contested)**: thread "Assa build is dead 4 raiding in patch 1.18.1?" — "im rock bottom at dps, im completely useless" [Microtwist (player), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171651#p171651); "Go COMBAT u n00bs ... throw out 1.3 speed mainhands" [Mdzielak (player), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171653#p171653); "Assaa with 1.3 felt fun to me, but with the poisons change, its too much messing about changing all the time" [Snkx (player), 2026-03-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172809#p172809); vs. "Noxious assault rogues got the biggest nerf in this patch ... Leave the entry level rogue spec alone" [Crake (player), 2026-03-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172282#p172282). A hunter complains rogues do "500dps+ with only white 1.30 daggers from vendors" post-patch [Topmoo (player), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172191#p172191), and a feral druid reports post-patch Instant Poison "hit me 220-250/hit ... its like 700dps from poisons only" in PvP [Wosator (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171992#p171992). Staff position: the class was "a bit overperformant in certain areas" and 1.18.1 makes "targeted numerical adjustments ... without changing how the class fundamentally plays" [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- Reputation: before 1.18.0 the forum consensus was that Rogue was the weakest DPS on Turtle and Assassination "a meme"; from 1.18.0 (Aug 2025) the complaint flips to "rogues are broken" from other classes (see `README.md` gaps and `assassination-pvp.md`). Bots should expect both framings depending on date.

## Official kit (staff) — what the build is made of

Read `../../1.18.1-master-changelog.md` (Classes → Rogue) for the 1.18.1 entries; the pre-1.18.1 chain is:

- CC2 [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102971#p102971): **combo points persist across targets** ("reset when applying combo points on a new target"); One-Handed Axes; Sinister Strike 40 energy; **Corrosive Poison** (Physical DoT, 30% chance, 100/128 dmg over 12 s, 5 stacks, 105 charges, R2 from Handbook of Deadly Poison V); Sprint/Evasion 4 min; Wound Poison −5% healing/stack; Crippling R2 60%; Assassination: Remorseless Attacks 40 s, Ruthlessness 33/66/100%, Improved Blade Tactics (SnD/Flourish +15/30/45%), Vile Poisons and Improved Poisons 3 points, **Taste for Blood** (new, Rupture-triggered damage buff), **Efficient Poisons** (−4/8/12% resist, 15/30/45% chance not to consume a charge), Vigor 2 points, **Envenom** capstone (finisher, +25% poison chance/effect for 12–28 s, 25 energy; "Effectiveness means ... damage for damage dealing poisons, slow amount for Crippling Poison and healing reduction for Wound Poison").
- 2024-12-20 [Jamey (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460): "The damage output of Rogue hasn't quite met our expectations ... we'll be adding scaling to class-specific poisons"; **Relentless Strikes: 20 energy (was 25) and +5% finisher damage per stack, 5 stacks, 30 s**; **all finishers −5 energy**; **Instant Poison +5% AP; Deadly and Corrosive +2% AP per tick (8% per full stack)**; Adrenaline Rush 3 min.
- 2025-03-30 [Torta (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124504#p124504): Vile Poisons 10/20/30%; **Envenom 30%** (was 25%); Deadly Throw reworked (no combo points, 40 energy, 100% weapon damage, shares Kick's cooldown, 3-s interrupt); Throwing Weapon Specialization = 50/100% chance to apply the **off-hand** poison with Deadly Throw. Hotfix: with TWS "Deadly Poison" on the main hand now correctly lets the throw apply Agitating/Instant from the off-hand [Torta (staff), 2025-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124967#p124967).
- 1.18.0 [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159): baseline **Quickfooted** (+7% speed at 30), **Blade Flurry baseline at 30 as a toggle** (−30% energy regeneration, −20% damage, 4-s cooldown), **Dissolvent Poison** (instant Physical, ignores armor, 20% chance; R1 lvl 52 88–113, R2 lvl 60 106–142, 4.75% AP), Blind is Physical; Assassination: Remorseless Attacks and Lethality include Noxious Assault/Surprise Attack (revision: "Combo Point generating ability"), **Corrosive AP scaling −5%** ("the original poison variant now pulls ahead by a noticeable amount"), TWS row 3, **Taste for Blood row 4: +1/2/3% Physical damage per combo point and Rupture +2/4/6 s** (fixed to scale with combo points on 2025-08-15 [Torta (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145822#p145822)), **Envenom row 5**, **Vigor: no cooldown, 2 energy per poison application** ("this still equals a big increase in energy gained"), capstone **Noxious Assault** (both weapons, 35% AP + 30/55/85/105, applies both poisons, 1 combo point, 45 energy, no cooldown; ranks at 40 (talent), 48, 56, 60 via book — the Handbook IV was fixed on 2025-08-15). Revision 2025-08-14: Opportunity to Combat row 1; Improved Gouge and Improved Expose Armor to Subtlety row 1.
- **1.18.1** [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071): low-rank poison scaling removed/reduced (Deadly R1–R3 none, R4 1%/tick; Instant R1 none, R2–R3 1%, R4–R5 2%; Corrosive R1 (56) 1%/tick; Dissolvent R1 (52) 2%) — "smooths out that curve while preserving endgame effectiveness"; **Blade Flurry energy penalty 30% → 20%**; **Taste for Blood 1/2/3% → 1/1.5/2% and no longer increases Corrosive/Dissolvent damage**; **Noxious Assault 35% → 30% AP**. Revision 2026-03-19 (same post): **Taste for Blood is now a 2-point talent, +4/6 s duration, +1/2% damage**; **Corrosive and Dissolvent Poison only apply to Elementals, Mechanicals, Giants and Undead** (first announced in the 1.18.1 preliminary notes [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). Post-release hotfix: "Fixed damage calculations for stackable spells (e.g., Scorpid Poison, Deadly Poison, etc.)" [Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139).
- Poison plumbing fixes worth knowing: "Poisons at max stack now properly take Envenom into account" [Torta (staff), 2024-11-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106599#p106599); Deadly and Corrosive "no longer mutually exclusive when applied by different Rogues" [Torta (staff), 2024-12-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111130#p111130); Band of Ancient Lethality's poison bonus stacks with Improved Poisons [Torta (staff), 2025-06-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134147#p134147); Eviscerate and Rupture "now also correctly scale with Attack Power bonuses against specific creature types" [Torta (staff), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159221#p159221); Corrosive could bypass invulnerability, fixed [Torta (staff), 2025-07-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140939#p140939); Dissolvent vs elementals fixed 2025-08-15.

### Patch validity

Builds above are 1.18.0 (Aug–Dec 2025). 1.18.1 changed: Taste for Blood (2 points, weaker, no Physical-poison scaling), Noxious Assault 30% AP, Blade Flurry −20% energy, and the poison target restriction. No post-1.18.1 source posts a revised full build; the thread t24364 only argues whether to switch to Combat. Treat the talent allocation as still valid except Taste for Blood (now 2 points, and its value is contested), and treat the DPS ranking as **contested post-1.18.1**.

## Stat priority

- **60, Noxious Assault build (Shananagans, 2025-09-01, 1.18.0)**: "Assassination isn't your combat swords mentality of piling on the attack power. It is a balance between dagger speed, armor haste and crit. Attack power is at the bottom of my list" — i.e. **weapon speed (1.3–1.4 daggers) > hit to cap for the content > crit / agility > haste on gear (diminishing) > AP**. "Just my Humble 2 cents: Speed of Daggers, + to Daggers, Critical Strike" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162667#p162667). Reason: "weapon speed doesn't matter for noxious assault, but it DOES matter for how frequently you can apply poisons through auto attacks. More swings = more procs = more damage. And assa is like 50%+ poison damage" [Jinkarasu (player), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148372#p148372) (Noxious Assault "doesn't use weapon damage values at all. It's a % of your total attack power"). Consensus among the 1.18.0 posters (Jinkarasu, Kerenis, Shananagans, Sslushee, Crake). Post-1.18.1 counter-claim: "throw out 1.3 speed mainhands" [Mdzielak, 2026-03-21] — single source, no numbers.
- **Weapon skill**: "+6 to daggers helps push glancing blows lower, which means more poison procs that turns into energy return" (Distracting Dagger, Mugger's Belt); "The weapon skill is capped at 315" [Shananagans, 2025-09-01] — the cap number is the poster's belief, not a staff statement.
- **Hit**: 5% from Precision plus gear; "you only need 4-5%, you are doing dungeons not raiding" [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-11-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161013#p161013); for raids the forum uses the vanilla 9% special-attack cap ("9% hit cap can be reach" [Nightmonster (player), 2023-05-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=43985#p43985)) `(vanilla baseline, not a staff number)`.
- **Haste**: "Be careful of stacking armor haste due to diminishing returns. Right now I feel like I'm in the sweet spot dagger speed with gear at 1.25, with slice and dice up is .97 and with the Stormshroud Set proc it's .87. I tried adding a ring that gave 3% haste to melee and barely budged the needle at .86" [Shananagans, 2025-09-01].
- **Crit vs poisons**: poisons do not crit and crit does not help them — "crit only works for white and yellow hits, but those are not crucial for envenom spec" [Frantsel (player), 2025-06-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135716#p135716); this is why Sslushee drops Seal Fate. Crit still feeds Seal Fate/Relentless when high.
- **Crit cap**: "1% crit is = to 29 agility" and the cap "100% - (base miss % - your hit %) - (base dodge %) - (base parry %) - (glancing blow %)" via the BetterCharacterStats addon [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-09-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151559#p151559) — player arithmetic, unverified.
- **Leveling**: same priorities, but Precision can wait (see `leveling.md`); Sslushee at 40: "This build isn't crit-dependent in the slightest".

## Single-target rotation (1.18.0 kit; unchanged by 1.18.1 except Taste for Blood)

Opener and priority as written by the two guide-style posters (consensus between them, dummy-tested by Shananagans):

1. **Opener**: any combo-point opener — "I'm not worried about which opener I use. I want a combo point, period ... you wont garrote a stone elemental for example" [Shananagans, 2025-09-01]. In dungeons/solo: Cheap Shot (Sslushee) or Garrote/Ambush. On bosses immune to stun: Garrote if it bleeds, else Noxious Assault.
2. **Envenom at 1 combo point immediately** ("I hit envenom to get the poison buff going") — "You're not supposed to wait for full combo points to pop it - I cheap shot -> pop envenom immediately, and refresh when needed" [Tankmeister (player), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154255#p154255).
3. **Slice and Dice at 1 point** off the Ruthlessness combo point ("since it is a finishing move it has 100% of the time so far, granted me another combo point, I hit slice and dice").
4. **Noxious Assault** as the only builder until 5 combo points; "it takes using Noxious Assault 10-12 seconds to acquire 5 combo points".
5. **Keep Envenom and SnD desynchronised by 10–12 s**: refresh the one with 1–2 s left at 5 points, then use the Ruthlessness point on the other; "the next 5 combo points as you can probably guess will go to slice and dice ... it just flip flop back and forth". Rule: "THEY HAVE TO STAY UP".
6. **Only when both buffs have >10 s** and you have 3–5 points: **Eviscerate** (Shananagans' default, keeps CC usable) or **Rupture** (his raid/dungeon variant; "Some have mentioned Rupture does more damage"); Crake's raw-Lua rotation macro uses **Rupture at 5 points** when Envenom and SnD are up, otherwise Envenom (if missing) → SnD (if missing) → Noxious Assault [Crake (player), 2025-10-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157229#p157229).
7. **Pool energy; do not mash**: "Pool your energy and don't mash buttons. You will gain more dps having patience" [Shananagans, 2025-09-01]. Expect ~50% of damage from poisons, 25–27% autos, 15–17% Noxious Assault, 6–7% Eviscerate (dummy test, same post).

Opener for short fights (level 40 dungeon version): "Cheap Shot → Envenom → Slice & Dice (off the Ruthlessness point) → Noxious Assault twice → Dead" [Sslushee, 2025-11-06]. Trash: "you basically use one mob to acivate each since they just die to quick" [Finpin, 2025-04-19] — accept that the first mob of a pack is spent setting up Envenom/SnD.

Expose Armor: only if no warrior sunders (Shananagans, 2025-09-09); Bigbosc (pre-1.18.0) warns that using Expose Armor in this spec means you "simply can't ever push a damaging finishing move".

## Multi-target / AoE

- 2–3 targets: **do not toggle Blade Flurry** in this spec by default — it costs 20% energy regeneration (30% before 1.18.1) and 20% damage, which starves Vigor/Noxious Assault: Shananagans won't use it on his Assassination rogue [2025-08-25]; "Dont use blade flurry while leveling, it is absolute trash until you get like 800 agility" [Exaltus (player), 2025-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149418#p149418); "In the multi-target scene, it used to be 200%, but now it is estimated that it can only be 170% or lower" [killend (player), 2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141570#p141570). Single-source counter: Combat rogues use it freely (see `combat-melee-dps.md`). Instead: tab-swap Noxious Assault between two targets only if both live long enough to keep poisons ticking; otherwise stay on one.
- 4+ targets: no rogue AoE exists ("They've said they won't add true AoE to rogues. The best we'll get is Blade Flurry" [Bayanni (player), 2025-06-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134713#p134713)). Consumable padding: Oil of Immolation ("250 damage over 15 seconds ... for each mob in a 3 yard radius") and Dragonbreath Chili [Shananagans, 2025-09-01]; engineering grenades (see PvP/tank files). Bots should not chase AoE parses on this spec.

- Trinket swapping between pulls with TrinketMenu (Shananagans p149464) is a common habit, not a requirement.

## Role strategy

- Stand behind the target anyway (glancing/parry avoidance; Ambush/Backstab openers), though NA itself has no positional requirement.
- Threat: the class's threat management "is only surpassed by hunters" [Atreidon (player), 2025-02-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120497#p120497); Feint when a threat addon (TankPlates/SuperWoW) shows yellow, Vanish on green [Shananagans (guide writer), 2025-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149464#p149464).
- Interrupts: Kick; Deadly Throw is a ranged 3-s interrupt sharing Kick's cooldown (staff, 2025-03-30) — use it when the caster is out of reach.
- Poison-immune or resistant bosses: swap to Dissolvent/Corrosive only if the target is Elemental/Mechanical/Giant/Undead (post-1.18.1), otherwise the spec is weak there: "ALL Elementals and other partly magic immune creatures/bosses (e.g. emperors, 50% of mc bosses) will just eat your poisons" [Finpin, 2025-04-19]. Fire-immune targets ignore Alcor's Sunrazor procs (Shananagans, 2025-12-13).
- Stealth is unreliable on Turtle (mobs detect from further than expected; see `leveling.md` and Atreidon's tips) — do not rely on Sap pulls in pugs ("Tanks won't wait for your little show anyway" [Akos1896 (player), 2024-10-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103039#p103039)).

## Common mistakes (bots must avoid)

- Using slow "purple" daggers because of the Combat mindset: "we see these beautiful Purple, slow Daggers drop and we drool ... Assassination as we know now is all about the speed" [Shananagans, 2025-12-13].
- Letting Envenom or Slice and Dice drop, or refreshing both at the same time so they expire together (Shananagans' 10–12 s separation rule).
- Waiting for 5 points before the first Envenom (Tankmeister).
- Running Deadly/Corrosive with fast daggers: "the 5 stack cap on deadly ends up hindering your dps" (Sslushee); post-1.18.1, running Dissolvent/Corrosive on anything but Elemental/Mechanical/Giant/Undead (they do not apply).
- Toggling Blade Flurry on and forgetting it (energy starvation).
- Running out of poison charges mid-fight (carry spares; weapon-swap macro).
- Using DoT poisons/Rupture on a target you intend to Gouge/Blind/Sap.
- Ignoring hit: "if you are missing more because you don't have Precision, you damage is going into the toilet".
- Spamming at low energy instead of pooling.
- Expecting AoE parses or top single-target parses in Naxx gear (Crake's 700 vs 2k).

### Mechanics and rotation plumbing

- **Taste for Blood** is a self-buff from Rupture, refreshed at any combo-point value (a 1-CP Rupture gives the full window), and buffs Dissolvent [[d:rogue#1458132484637065343]], [[d:rogue#1432424536262967338]]; it can be triggered off a bleed-immune target to then Eviscerate [[d:rogue#1461858045247225907]]. The 1.18.0 value was "15% damage plus way more energy" [[d:rogue#1404489673018970132]]; 1.18.1 makes it a 2-point talent (+4/6 s, +1/2%).
- **The pre-1.18.1 Discord rotation** refines this file's: priority is maintain TfB > Slice and Dice > dump spare CP into Eviscerate/Envenom [[d:rogue#1406754701407551599]]; opener **1-CP SnD → 1-CP Envenom → 5-CP Rupture (TfB) → refresh 1-CP Envenom/SnD → 5-CP Eviscerate** [[d:rogue#1407474034349969448]], [[d:rogue#1407740396452450416]]; once energy regen leaves 5–10 s of buff uptime, drop Rupture entirely [[d:rogue#1456432270717092092]]. Opener is Garrote (no damage/no threat) or just Noxious; skip stun openers on immune bosses [[d:rogue#1424187035299614801]], [[d:rogue#1407819960079745106]].
- **Noxious Assault** triggers off-hand weapon procs (Crusader, Tempest's Rage) [[d:rogue#1406322984389443594]] but in 1.18.1 **does not proc poisons** and does not use your weapon for effects [[d:rogue#1484557265733419091]], [[d:rogue#1494836426171355327]]. Its proc behaviour is contested [[d:rogue#1418737911167320245]], [[d:rogue#1417770415522316370]].
- **Envenom weapon-swap setup**: stack with secondary Deadly/Corrosive weapons, then swap to primary Instant weapons (Combat runs MH Deadly / OH Instant; Envenom the reverse) [[d:rogue#1324452398273400996]], [[d:rogue#1334918223589539880]]; swaps cost a GCD [[d:rogue#1485762675714691072]]. 1.18.1 Envenom is a maintenance buff holding the Deadly stack, not a damage finisher; **Deadly sims better than Instant** for combat-dagger Envenom [[d:rogue#1484976580479418600]].
- **Poisons proc only from weapon hits** (not Sap/Cheap Shot/Kidney) [[d:rogue#872201163557195858]]; glancing blows still proc, misses do not [[d:rogue#1324752410052395121]].
- **Combo points** persist per target since CC2 but are lost on switch; a builder on an add wipes the boss's points [[d:rogue#1314430049042042911]]; Ruthlessness can drift a CP to a new target [[d:rogue#1260216495535816844]]; 1-CP Eviscerate farms Relentless Strikes [[d:rogue#1405569716176814090]].
- **Thistle Tea** optimally at exactly 0 energy with a free GCD [[d:rogue#1342009613259903067]]; **haste compounds multiplicatively** across gear/enchants/talents (Epickalen's pinned formula) [[d:rogue#1387271666970787983]].

- **Weapon speed**: ~1.3 for poison-proc volume, 1.8 only for the spellstrike exception [[d:rogue#1446546660032385118]]; each +0.1 s costs ~3 dps early and ~12 dps at top rank [[d:rogue#1461866998521921710]], [[d:rogue#1437156474810794097]]; **Fang of Venoxis was buffed to 1.3 speed** [[d:rogue#1486869810053775370]].
- **Stat cheat-sheet** (Epickalen): 15 agi = 1 haste, 1 haste = 1 crit = 1 hit = 25 AP, agi ≈1.5 AP with energy regen / ≈1.2 AP without [[d:rogue#1449907721582280765]]; 8% yellow hit cap and 5 weapon skill = 1% hit [[d:rogue#1407624625059270699]], [[d:rogue#1407635403632279602]]; aim ~14% hit to avoid crit-capping [[d:rogue#1450373303486124083]]. EP estimates exist only as a community sheet [[d:rogue#1324843454261366877]].
- **Weapon skill is softened post-1.18**: for Assassination "weapon skill matters very little" [[d:rogue#1407120295483019406]]; Noxious uses AP, so only autos benefit [[d:rogue#1406925028288761956]] (contested).
- **Gear**: Darkmantle + Stormshroud energy procs remain the pre-raid engine, but Assassination has little progression beyond Darkmantle [[d:rogue#1484549475212202108]]; post-1.18.1 combat-envenom wants 6/8 Darkmantle + 2/8 T2 (then a 3rd T2) [[d:rogue#1488910311829278750]]; Trinkets: Kiss of the Spider / Crest of Retribution for both specs, plus DFT, Whip, Heart of Dreams, Jom Gabbar, Venomous Totem, Slayer's Crest, Mark of the Champion [[d:rogue#1410196128783138877]], [[d:rogue#1488007182871035974]]; the Kara40 thrown "rock" is BiS ranged for all rogues [[d:rogue#1410360888715776252]]. Tempest's Rage off-hand is a ~15% dps loss for Assassination [[d:rogue#1442409907285393500]].
- **Consumables**: Concoction of the Emerald Mongoose for spell+physical crit [[d:rogue#1410790635442671830]]; Potion of Quickness shares a CD with protection potions, Thistle Tea with Health Stones [[d:rogue#1421159028498759741]]; full multiplicative haste-source list [[d:rogue#1415123962169393345]].
- **Class weapon**: the 1.18.1 Timbermaw-Hold rogue class weapon is a fast dagger and was not yet obtainable on the fresh realm at release [[d:rogue#1472744220052684952]], [[d:rogue#1472663678384865544]], [[d:rogue#1472732383538188330]]; stats absent from both corpora (gap).

### Leveling route

**Combat/dagger to 40 → respec Assassination (Noxious Assault) at 40 → respec to the endgame spec at 58–60** [[d:rogue#1425614628385325138]], [[d:rogue#1455712360433189117]], [[d:rogue#1458113459639156888]]. The level-40 respec decodes to exactly **31/0/0 with Noxious Assault 1** [[d:rogue#1475759154089033738]], and the pinned leveling build to **20/5/26** [[d:rogue#1431531176333541396]]. Dagger progression: Big Bronze Knife → Deadly Bronze Poniard → Meteor Shard / Broad-Bladed Knife → The Ziggler / Tigerbane → Fiendish Skiv → Gut Ripper → Barman Shanker [[d:rogue#1286056902975029313]], [[d:rogue#1324107314747408587]]; sword milestones Vanquisher's Sword → Sword of Omen/Serenity → Thrash Blade [[d:rogue#1165510276062384150]]; **frontstabbing** is the practical dagger-leveling technique, at the cost of exposure [[d:rogue#1131138509588140062]], [[d:rogue#1310955868933586985]].
