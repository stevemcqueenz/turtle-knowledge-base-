# Mage — Arcane — PvP (battlegrounds, world PvP)

Source quality: **weak**. The sources are complaint threads and rebuttals about Arcane burst after 1.17.2, plus two opaque build links. No rotation guide or stat list exists; what follows is the tactic pattern the threads describe.

## Overview

- **Standing: strong/"overpowered" burst spec in BGs since 1.17.2 (consensus among both complainers and defenders that the burst exists; contested whether it is a problem).**
  - "Arcane is realy bursty and that is very good at killing wsg flag Carrier and when you attack as team" [Likaleo, 2025-03-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122357#p122357).
  - "During PvP you go arcane (high dps, channeled spell to ignore LOS, 0% pushback, instant cast pyroblast)" [Cheruscan, 2025-11-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158193#p158193).
  - "arcane missiles is a no cd no cast time spell that does 3000-4000 damage around corners with a huge starting range and an infinite total range … i see blue geared arcane mages go 15:0 in bgs all the fucking time" [Terrydavis, 2025-12-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162458#p162458).
  - "if you look at the Arcane and Fire mages who just make use of the Base Line abilities shared with Frost, you notice very quickly that these specs are favored in PvP as well" [Eluaria, 2026-02-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167923#p167923).
  - Complaints: PoM + Arcane Rupture crit that duplicated twice for 4432 ([Manletow, 2024-11-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107344#p107344)); "any decently geared mage is doing atleast 3k dmg with pom rupture, 5k if he gets lucky, 6-7k if hes mega geared, 1.5k dmg per missile tick" [Forumdweller, 2025-06-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134850#p134850); "arcane mage gameplay consisting on blinking ahead and pressing one click on arcane missiles" (PvP-horizon thread player). Rebuttals: the double-duplicate crit is "somewhere around 1% or lower" [Kibuum, 2024-11-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107372#p107372) / "2% every 3 mins on a well geared mage (650-700SP) assuming around 30% crit" [Bahamutxd, 2024-12-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114805#p114805); "Maybe dont let arcane mage freecast on you" [Likaleo, 2024-12-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114472#p114472); "PoM Mages deleting people like the glass cannon they are has been happening for 20 years … its now Rupture & pray for RNG with spell Windfury" [Halfgeek9, 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134982#p134982).
  - Staff response: Arcane Rupture −10% SP scaling (2024-12-20) and lower-rank base damage (2025-03-30) — Manletow: "you will only get hit for 4000 dmg instantly instead of 4400" ([2024-12-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114495#p114495)). 1.18.1 makes PvP dampening class-dependent with numbers unpublished ([Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)).
- Weaknesses: glass cannon (Halfgeek9), 30 yd range, kickable — "An arcane mage that gets kicked on arcane doesnt do 20% less damage, it does 90% less damage and loses its ability to use its main defencives, polymorph, mana shield, blink" (cross-forum player remark); Felhunter/Intensity counters missiles pushback ([Atreidon, 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135012#p135012)); Arcane Power's death condition ("the 'Suicide Button'" — Manletow) and "Arcane atleast struggles to pick up ice block" (Forumdweller) — although he also complains an Arcane mage "has access to 2 ICE BLOCKS" via Cold Snap ([2025-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132193#p132193)) — contested/unclear how deep into Frost that build goes.

## Tactics (as described by the threads)

1. Open from range or after Polymorph: **Presence of Mind → Arcane Rupture (instant) → Arcane Missiles** ("He did, POM (3m cooldown) Arcane Rupture -> Arcane Missile" [Halfgeek9, 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134966#p134966)); "so u just poly anyone then open them with pyro pom pyro" is the older PoM-Pyro pattern ([Forumdweller](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131107#p131107)).
2. Missiles follow targets that break line of sight after the channel starts (Terrydavis, [Boeuff, 2024-12-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114456#p114456): "you turn a corner that mage who sits in a pack of people gets to do 2k to 5k to you while ur LOS"; Forumdweller claims the range extends to 50 yd once channelling — single source, unverified).
3. Blink forward to close to 30 yd, then channel ("Blink in and does 5k dmg" [Forumdweller, 2025-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132087#p132087)).
4. Arcane Surge is available "after one of your spells are fully or partially resisted" — enemy resistances feed it ("Arcane Surge isnt limited to 'Arcane Resist' its literally ALL resistances" Manletow 2025-10-27).
5. Cooldown stacking: Arcane Power + Mind Quickening Gem "for 60% haste and zg trinket for double damage" (Forumdweller's sarcastic plan, [2025-05-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131096#p131096)); remember AP cannot be cancelled and kills below 10% mana — Mana Shield spam drains the same pool.
6. Defensive: Mana Shield (spammable), Blink, Ice Block if reached, "PoM is an insane utility that can substitute ice block" (Forumdweller). If Arcane is silenced you lose Polymorph, Mana Shield, Blink too (cross-forum remark).

## Stat priority

Not documented for PvP. See `arcane-ranged-dps.md` for PvE weights; a warrior-forum player notes Arcane's kit "scale[s] with haste and is a sought after stat" on the alt tier sets.

## Common mistakes

1. Using Arcane Power while Mana Shield is eating mana (death at 10%).
2. Channelling Missiles into a Felhunter/Intensity warlock expecting pushback to matter (Atreidon).
3. Starting the PoM-Rupture opener without Polymorph or range — "Maybe dont let arcane mage freecast on you" is the counter-advice; the mirror is that a bot should not freecast into an un-CC'd melee.
4. Fighting shamans at mid range (Earth Shock range; amanagor's duel claim).

## Discord additions (post-1.18.1; some claims 1.18.1-announced-pre-release)

The archived `#mage` Discord adds PvP reports from the **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19) and **post-1.18.1** windows, plus a stat priority. Era tags: **pre-1.18.1**, **1.18.1-announced-pre-release**, **post-1.18.1**.

- **Damage dampening is real and reported as severe:** "cone of cold is supposed to hit for ~350 … i hit someone for 71 … with 700 spellpower" [[d:mage#1485311972139335833]]; "you need at least 13–15 frostbolts to kill the average pvp geared melee" [[d:mage#1491110087388037304]] (**post-1.18.1, consensus** that the reports exist; the dampening values were never published).
- **Arcane PoM → Rupture → Missiles is the kill pattern:** "PoM+Trinket+Arcane Rupture one shots when it crits, doubles and instability keep you going" [[d:mage#1491794197773156493]]; Missiles follow the Rupture because "the range/LoS is all sorts of broken" [[d:mage#1491794484428537888]]; it surprises warlocks [[d:mage#1485360017107451977]] (**post-1.18.1, consensus**).
- **Deep Frost is weak post-nerf** — the "better to play deep arcane with iceblock" recommendation is **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-12-06): "frost sucks in pvp now … better to play deep arcane with iceblock" [[d:mage#1446952036661723289]]; the Icicles complaint is **post-1.18.1** (2026-04-08): "there's no point playing deep frost anymore unless you fc icicles nerf hit too hard" [[d:mage#1491498337088573571]]. Mages still play Frost for control in team fights [[d:mage#1491543407754219811]].
- **Stat priority PvP: crit > spell power > haste, plus solid stamina** [[d:mage#1446156953347686441]] (**1.18.1-announced-pre-release**, single source).
- **Free Action Potion is the standard melee counter to mage** [[d:mage#1446915765461581934]].
- **Matchups/races:** paladins/shamans dominate team fights [[d:mage#1491110398357798913]]; rogues/shamans top the meta [[d:mage#1430916238699397321]]; vs warriors use Dampen Magic + deadzone Intercept [[d:mage#1491500856984600709]]; warlocks can one-shot [[d:mage#1485360017107451977]]. Gnome Escape Artist [[d:mage#1043563538557448302]], Undead WotF [[d:mage#1493740921232031906]], Orc/Troll damage [[d:mage#1447253910875017238]], High Elf speedrun mana [[d:mage#1494293691227963503]].
