# Mage gear guide: pre-raid, raid and PvP by spec

Structured data: `structured/classes/mage/gear.yaml`. See `synthesis/CONVENTIONS.md` for citation format.

**Read this first**: the only itemized pre-raid list for Mage comes from the community wiki (authority: wiki, the
least reliable tier — see `AGENTS.md` §0) — the wiki itself admits there is "no up-to-date pre-raid BiS, you can just
jump right into raids." Three forum threads asking for a Mage BiS list (`t13188`, `t18636`, `t22994`) got no useful
answers. **No raid-tier slot-by-slot BiS list exists for any Mage spec anywhere in the corpus.** Arcane PvP has zero
gear sources at all. Treat everything below as a starting point, not a verified list.

**Enchants**: shoulder — 2% haste (single-target) or the ZG "Sapphiron"-type enchant (more AoE-flexible); weapon —
Brilliant Wizard Oil. **Consumables**: Mana Tea (Hyjal), Greater/Major Mana Potion, Alchemist's Stone.

**Stat priority**: hit to 16% (10% talent + 6% gear) first — "20SP = 14 DPS, 1 Haste = 9.1 DPS, 1 Crit = 9.1 DPS ... I
never go over 16% hit total" — [Ashafares (player), 2025-12-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162161#p162161) —
then haste ≈ spell power (~17-20 SP per 1% haste), then crit. Ignore set bonuses in favor of raw spell power + hit
per Keds' pre-1.18.1 testing (full T1 with Improved Mind Blast 0/5 ≈ full T2 with Improved Mind Blast 4/5). Spell
penetration: "useful for PvP but I can't think of any PvE fight where it would be worth gearing towards" —
[Mackylol (player), 2024-02-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88341#p88341).

### Raid tiers — fragments only

No per-slot armor list exists. Arcane gets its own T1/T2/T3 alternate sets (Arcanist/Netherwind/Frostfire) with Arcane
spell power and haste after a 2025-01-28 retune (e.g. Frostfire Vestments 41 Arcane SP + 2% haste; Netherwind
Epaulets 1% haste) — [Torta (staff), 2025-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118168#p118168). T3
pieces come from Archmage Angela Dosantos at Light's Hope; T1/T2 from an NPC on the Blackrock Mountain chains;
existing pieces convert at Feid Rota in Ratchet ("SWAPPING WILL REMOVE ANY ENCHANTS YOU HAD" — wiki). T3.5 "of the
Guardian" 3-set Mirror Magic was fixed in 1.18.1 (+10% Arcane on rewind). BWL-tier (post-1.18.1, single source):
"BWL's BiS is a combination of 5-Set and whatever else you can find. Ring of Sulfuras, Sigil of Ancient Accord,
Ornate Bloodstone Dagger for non fire immune bosses" —
[Eluaria (player), 2026-04-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174946#p174946), replying to a note that
"there is apparently nothing like an Icyveins for TW" —
[Andrewd93 (player), 2026-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173144#p173144). Ornate Bloodstone
Dagger "can no longer trigger from effects that deal non-Physical damage without a cast" in 1.18.1 —
[Dragunovi (staff), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168272#p168272). No per-piece T1-T3.5
armor names given anywhere — **gap**.

### Raid tiers — fragments only, contested bug status

Regular (non-Arcane) T1/T2/T3 sets are used. Frost/Fire T3.5 3-set: "proc chance increased from 10% to 35% and
replicated damage increased from 10% to 20%. Now has a 4 second cooldown" —
[1.18.0 itemization changelog (staff), t20103](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=20103); players reported
it broken repeatedly through late 2025 (bug-report id 18568) and the 1.18.1 notes only confirm Mirror Magic "now
triggers correctly" — **whether the Fire 5-set itself is fixed is unknown**. Loop of Unceasing Frost's fire-damage
increase stacks with Improved Scorch (changelog, 2024-12-11). No per-piece item names given — **gap**.

## Frost — pvp

**No named armor pieces exist for Frost PvP.** Consumables: Greater Fire Protection Potion ("blocks 2600 dmg, and
you can drink it up to an hour early", 2-min CD; recommended against Fire/PoM-Pyro mages) —
[Atolius (player), 2025-06-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134628#p134628); Zanza potion (~3g) —
[Springboards (player), 2024-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107470#p107470).

**Stat notes**: no Turtle-specific PvP stat weights exist; a direct question went unanswered —
[Perkamov (player), 2025-12-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161504#p161504). Stamina is valued:
"USE STAMINA ... full t3+life gem+stamina enchants" (advice given to a warrior fighting mages) —
[Springboards (player), 2024-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107471#p107471); a well-geared mage
has "~3k HP in PvP gear" — [Xiangyu (player), 2025-08-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148046#p148046).
Spell penetration is "useful for PvP" —
[Mackylol (player), 2024-02-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88341#p88341), but resistance gear
itself is rare in PvP — [Manletow (player), 2025-10-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157292#p157292).

## Gaps

- No source gives a complete slot-by-slot raid-tier (T1 through T3.5) BiS list for any Mage spec — only tier-set
  bonus text and, for Arcane post-1.18.1, a single post naming two trinkets and one weapon for BWL.
- Arcane PvP has zero gear/stat sources — the playbook explicitly notes "no rotation guide or stat list exists."
- Frost PvP has no named armor pieces at all — only two consumables and general stamina/spell-penetration advice.
- No leveling gear list exists for Mage in any bracket — `leveling.md` states this as an explicit gap; only Int >
  Stam stat priority and two unnamed early items (Amber Rings, a battlemage staff) are given.
- Almost every pre-raid item above has no item ID — sources give names and (usually) a drop location, but not a
  database ID, except the Kara10 items (61251-61450 range) that one 2023 forum post links directly.
- Sorcerer's Regalia (T0.5) individual piece names are not given anywhere; referenced only as a set.
