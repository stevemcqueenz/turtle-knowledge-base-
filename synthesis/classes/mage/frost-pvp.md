# Mage — Frost — PvP (battlegrounds, world PvP, duels)

Source quality: **weak-to-medium**. No source posts a Frost PvP talent allocation for the current (1.17.2+) tree; the material is opinion threads about balance, race choice, a few macros and staff PvP-relevant changes. Everything predating the 1.17.2 Shatter nerf (2024-11-01) describes a stronger Frost.

## Overview

- **Role.** Control and survival caster: Frost Nova / Frostbite freezes → Shatter-boosted Frostbolt crits, Cone of Cold and Frostbolt slows, Ice Barrier, Ice Block (+ Cold Snap), Blink, Polymorph, Counterspell. Staff regard Frost as "already a strong contender" in PvP, which is why Icicles cannot slow and roots the caster ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102968#p102968)).
- **Standing: historically the mage PvP spec; since 1.17.2 contested against Arcane's burst.**
  - "Frost makes you realy good at 1v1 and gives you high amount of control and survival" [Likaleo, 2025-03-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122357#p122357).
  - "the best spec in the game for pvp in warsonng /Ab you know its not a kill based game? try using your toolkit to actually win the game by peeling and being annoying … They have not nerfed the most op part about frost, which is rank 1 frostbolt." [amanagor, 2024-12-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111502#p111502).
  - "I wouldn't call mage overpowered at all, they're pretty nicely balanced if anything as their damage output is limited to shatter combos which puts them in melee range almost and requires cooldowns." [Gantulga, 2023-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82156#p82156) (pre-1.17.2).
  - "Frost is okay in Arena PvP, but doesn't really hold up in BGs, considering Arcane and Fire just outburst and outsustain it by a lot. Frost's only saving grace is the utility it provides" [Eluaria, 2026-02-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167923#p167923); "if you play frost mage in pvp at this point you are genuinely retarded anyway, its dead" [Terrydavis, 2025-12-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162458#p162458); "There is no reason to play frost in either pvp or pve … nerfed shatter" [amanagor, 2026-02-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167933#p167933) — the same author who praised it in 2024 (**his view changed after 1.18.0**).
  - Complaint side (Manletow, a self-described PvP shaman player, many threads 2023–2025): Frost "is overpowered in PvP (and PvE) so their 'bonus damage' should be nerfed from 100% → 50%" ([2023-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82136#p82136)); "kamikaze" mages Blink into groups → Frost Nova → Cone of Cold → Arcane Explosion spam ([2024-09-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101677#p101677)); Blink 15 s "basically a pvp trinket" and Mana Shield spam make mages "deceptively tanky with like 12k hp once t2.5+" [Springboards, 2025-10-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155433#p155433). Counter-voice: "Frost mage is an absolute non-issue compared to actually broken specs" [Gantulga, 2024-09-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=101680#p101680).

## Rotation / tactics

- **Shatter combo:** freeze (Frost Nova, Frostbite proc) → max-rank Frostbolt (Shatter +35% crit); the rank-1/rank-10 macro switches rank on a frozen target (see `frost-ranged-dps.md`). Rank-1 Frostbolt spam for cheap slows is "the most op part about frost" (amanagor 2024-12-05).
- **Peel/objective play:** slows (Frostbolt, Cone of Cold, Frost Armor chill), Nova, Polymorph, Counterspell — "win the game by peeling and being annoying" (amanagor).
- **Escape:** Blink (breaks stuns/roots and the Icicles root), Ice Block, Cold Snap → second Block/Nova/Barrier; Blink from >20 m height fails ("blink in place" — [Atreidon, 2025-04-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126305#p126305)); Blink through closed doors removed 2025-12-24 and "an exploit with Blink" fixed 2026-02-25 (changelogs). Ice Block macro: `/stopcasting /cancelaura Ice Block /stopcasting /cast Ice Block` (wiki General FAQ).
- **Mana Shield** as a second health bar (Springboards, Forumdweller); Improved Mana Shield 13/25%.
- **AoE bomb:** Blink in → Frost Nova → Cone of Cold → Arcane Explosion spam finishes "at least a couple 'weakened' players" (Manletow's description of what Frost mages do; he considers it bad design).
- **Consumables:** Greater Fire Protection Potion "blocks 2600 dmg. and you can drink it up to an hour early", 2-min CD — advised *against* Fire/PoM-Pyro mages ([Atolius, 2025-06-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134628#p134628)); Zanza potion 3g ([Springboards, 2024-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=107470#p107470)); cheap consumables exist to dismount and to remove Polymorph ([Sharmaine, 2025-10-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154580#p154580)); Dampen Magic was "bugged (negates too much)" in Dec 2023 ([Ataika](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82144#p82144)) — later reworked to −3%/−2% flat (staff 2025-03-30).

[Best mage race for PvP thread, 2024-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89723#p89723): Gladeshadow — Alliance: Gnome (Escape Artist 1 min, Expansive Mind), Human (Perception), Dwarf (Stoneform vs rogues); Horde: Forsaken (WotF vs fear and engineering items), Orc (stun resist; Turtle: −15% stun duration per the timeline); Goblin Exit Strategy "terrible" for mages; Troll Berserking "really a PvE build" ([p89754](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89754#p89754)). Springboards: Orc "due to thousands of paladins", Dwarf over Gnome because Escape Artist has a cast time and UD rogues are common ([p89756](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89756#p89756)). Ataika: undead because warlocks counter mages. High Elf Quel'dorei Meditation "not truly pvp relevant" (Gladeshadow); "Will i ever feel disadvantaged for not choosing a High elf" — "Nope, not at all" ([Terexin, 2025-07-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137872#p137872), who recommends Orc for the Blood Fury spell power).

## Common mistakes

1. Casting Icicles in PvP expecting damage (rooted, no slow; useless per Gattare; only relevant if Flash Freeze procs off a permanently immune target — no longer off FAP/BoF).
2. Standing at mid range vs shamans/melee — cast at max range (Forumdweller) or expect to be shocked (amanagor).
3. Blinking while high in the air (blinks in place — Atreidon; "Blink when you are low").
4. Wasting Improved Counterspell on a target in Ice Block — Counterspell macro checks for Ice Block (wiki).
5. Letting Mana Shield drain you to zero — "if you get hit for 500 … you're left OOM at full health and are useless" ([Imonobor, 2023-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82226#p82226), pre-rework numbers).
