# Priest gear guide: pre-raid, raid, leveling and PvP by spec

Structured data: `structured/classes/priest/gear.yaml`. Sources are cited per row; see `synthesis/CONVENTIONS.md`
for citation format. Item IDs and drop bosses are copied from `database.turtlecraft.gg` links players posted in the
cited threads — treat them as player-reported, not staff-verified against a client DBC (see `AGENTS.md` §3-4).

**Biggest gap up front**: only Holy healer has a full slot-by-slot pre-raid list in the sources (one guide thread).
Discipline and Shadow pre-raid gear is fragments, and **no Priest spec has a complete slot-by-slot raid-tier
(T1-T3.5) BiS list anywhere in the corpus** — only tier-set bonus text and a few isolated named items. PvP gear is
even thinner (two named weapons total). See "Gaps" at the end.

**Enchants** (Langorn): head/legs Arcanum of Focus +12 healing (18330); feet Enchanted Armor Kit +9 healing (61183);
chest Enchanted Armor Kit or +4 all stats; hands +30 healing; weapon +55 healing; bracers +23 healing; rings/neck
Beautiful Diamond Gemstone +12 healing (56010). Minimum recommended: weapon, hands, bracers.

**Consumables** (Langorn + Sharmaine): Major Mana Potion (13444) mandatory; Tea with Sugar (15723) / Nordanaar Herbal
Tea (61675); Dark Rune (20520) / Demonic Rune (12662, shares CD with tea); Brilliant Mana Oil (20748); Empowering
Herbal Salad (83309, rare) or Nightfin/Sagefish; Flask of Distilled Wisdom (13511); Spirit of Zanza (20079, ZG
Revered); Mageblood Potion (20007).

**Stat priority** (Langorn): Healing Power > Spell Crit (150% heal crits, +25% target armor on a crit heal) >
Intellect (mana + crit) > Spirit (healing power + mp5 via talents; mind the 5-second rule) > Mp5 > Stamina.

### Raid tiers (T1–T3.5) — no per-slot list exists

No source gives a slot-by-slot Holy raid BiS list. What exists is tier-set commentary, **contested** in places:

- **T1**: "when it comes to Holy priest, T1 raid set is pretty much garbage so don't bother collecting it" — [Langorn (player), 2025-09-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=152325#p152325).
- **T2**: full 8-piece set valued pre-1.18.1 "for the Rank 5 Renew proc on Greater Heal" combined with the Hazzrah Charm from a ZG Madness event — [Hawkbutt (player), 2025-04-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125153#p125153).
- **T2.5**: pre-1.18.1 bonuses were called "decoration" — [Astrallizard (player), 2025-11-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=156737#p156737). Reworked in 1.18.1: 3-set makes critical Heal/Greater Heal effects raise Spirit by 80 for 10s; 5-set gives Lightwell +5 yards range and +5 charges — [Dragunovi (staff), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168272#p168272).
- **T3.5**: **contested** — "3-Piece 'Regen' Bonus: Pay 300+ mana to get 48 mana back ... 5-Piece Flash Heal 'Buff' ... We're forced to wear T2 3-piece + junk Naxx rings/necks" — [Solaire0726 (player), 2025-08-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=142435#p142435) vs. "[it's] another tool" — [Maxitaxii (player), 2025-04-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128268#p128268).
- Atiesh (Priest) grants 150 spell power — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105276#p105276). This is a legendary-quest staff, not a raid-tier drop.

No source names individual T1/T2/T2.5/T3/T3.5 pieces for Holy — **gap**.

**Consumables**: Major Mana Potion; Nordanaar Herbal Tea / Tea with Sugar, Dark/Demonic Runes, Brilliant Wizard Oil,

[Tess322 (player), 2025-05-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=131166#p131166).

**Stat priority**: spell hit to 10% from gear (Piercing Light gives 6% more, 16% total) is top priority — "spec needs
10% from items ... +spellhit (weight19 if undercap) vs +crit (weight7)" — Tscosomaz, as above; a second player
independently gives up to 16% for bosses, "always 1% miss" — [Zvyrhol (player), 2023-12-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81512#p81512).
"After T0.5 there is nothing, unless offsets or T2.5" — Tscosomaz — T1/T2 sets carry no spell hit and are explicitly
called unusable for the spec.

### Raid-t2.5

The original T2.5 (Oracle) set was converted pre-1.18.1 into a full Discipline/Smite set: Holy spell power
33/27/39/34/27 across the five pieces, +1% hit on robe and trousers, +1% crit on tiara/mantle —
[Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105276#p105276). Players call it weaker
than BWL/AQ/Naxx off-set pieces (Leggings of Polarity, Boots of Epiphany) and object to it dealing only Holy damage —
"no justifiable reason for T2.5 to only be Holy damage" — [Mcpewpew (player), 2024-12-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113348#p113348).
1.18.1 5-set rework: Chastise grants 40 spell power and cuts Smite/Holy Fire mana cost 15% for 12s, doubled on a
critical strike — [Dragunovi (staff), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168272#p168272). No
source names the individual T2.5 pieces or their non-Holy-spell-power stats — **gap**.

## Gaps

- No source gives a complete slot-by-slot raid-tier (T1 through T3.5) BiS list for any Priest spec — only tier-set
  bonus text, a handful of isolated named items (Coronet/Shoulderpads/Pants of Faith for Shadow T3), and prose route
  advice. True across Holy, Discipline and Shadow.
- No PvP itemization list beyond two named weapons (Soulseeker, Time Frozen Bow) across both Discipline-pvp and
  Shadow-pvp; stamina-piece recommendations name only vague descriptions ("ragnaros str cloak", "str tanking warrior
  ring from aq40", "naxx 24 stam ring", "nefarian tanking neck"), not exact item names.
- No leveling gear bracket exists for Holy (not a recommended leveling spec per the sources) or for level ranges
  30-49/50-59 for any spec; only the level-5-29 wand progression (Lesser → Greater Magic Wand) is concretely sourced.
- The `shadow-ranged-dps.md` claim that a "Karazhan set" reduces Mind Blast cooldown (attributed only to
  "Zitronenhaze") could not be traced to a specific post ID in the extracted forum data, so it was omitted here
  rather than cited without a resolvable URL.
- Item IDs for many named items are not given in any source: Nightwind Leggings, Band of Servitude, Bloodvine set
  pieces, Soulseeker, Time Frozen Bow, the mooncloth fallback boots, Coronet/Shoulderpads/Pants of Faith.
- Discipline T2.5 and Shadow T2.5/T3.5 raid sets have known set-bonus text (itemization changelog) but no per-piece
  item names anywhere in the sources.
- `database.turtlecraft.gg` item links quoted by players (mostly Langorn's guide) are player-reported, not
  staff-confirmed or cross-checked against a client DBC; treat item IDs and drop locations here as provisional
  pending local-server verification (see `AGENTS.md` §3-4).
