# Priest — Shadow PvP (battlegrounds, world PvP, duels)

Era markers as in `holy-healer.md`. PvP opinions swing with each class-change round; dates matter.

## Overview

- **Role**: mid-range DoT/channel caster with self-sustain (Vampiric Embrace), instant fear, silence and Blackout stuns. "Shadow is the most busted spec in PvP. You do so much damage with so much survability and self heal for 30% of the dmg you deal" — [Grizb37 (player), 2024-10-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104602#p104602) (pre-CC2 state); "If you have the gear, shadowpriest is among the absolutely strongest classes for 1v1" — [Atreidon (player), 2024-07-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98901#p98901).
- **Timeline of power** (contested at every step):
  - Pre-CC2 (≤2024-10): considered overpowered; "Spriests were nerfed hard because pvpers qq'ing" — [Drubarrymooer (player), 2025-03-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=122835#p122835).
  - CC2 (2024-10 .. 2025-07): VE got a 2 s (then 1.5 s) cast, Pain Spike scaling halved, Blackout 3 → 2 s, VT nerfed, Blessed Concentration moved away; "hard-casting VE ruins the whole playstyle" — [Caracole (player), 2024-12-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112248#p112248); "Shadow Priest are probably the worst class for PvP in Turtle wow" — [Wiljorxp (player), 2025-05-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132274#p132274) vs "spriests are strong in PvP ... All it takes is a lucky blackout proc to delete someone" — [Mitzter (player), 2025-05-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132294#p132294).
  - 1.18.0 (2025-07/08): VE instant again — [Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159); Pain Spike scaling 43% (2025-04-01) — [Torta (staff), 2025-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124504#p124504).
  - 1.18.1 (2026-03-20): Blackout to row 2 (from row 1), Shadow Weaving becomes a self-buff, Pain Spike CD 30 s — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071) (+revisions). Reception: "Potential change of 1.18.1 is end of shadow priest pvp" — [shadowchaos (player), 2026-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166721#p166721) vs "Even with this change, Shadow will be similar as now" — [Zvyrhol (player), 2026-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166733#p166733). The Blackout move mostly hurts hybrid priests who took 5 points for it: "this is more debilitation for disc and holy ... rip low level troll priest with blackout and shadowguard" — [Springboards (player), 2026-01-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166894#p166894).
- **Strengths**: instant AoE fear, Silence, Blackout, tankiness ("you are 1 of the most tankiest classes in the game with insane armor, instant fear warlocks are jealous of(which cannot be shadow reflected), instant silence and blackout procs" — [Springboards (player), 2024-10-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104697#p104697)); Pain Spike + Mind Blast burst ("2-3k burstdmg (MB +Spike)" — [Atreidon (player), 2025-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124839#p124839)).
- **Weaknesses**: short range ("SP is a close range spell caster in pvp (mind flay, psychic scream and Mind blast failing if out of range due to it being instant cast)" — [Skattberg (player), 2025-01-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117120#p117120)); fear countered by Fear Ward (now every priest), WotF, trinkets ("now that everyone and their grandma has fear remove our only defensive ability ... does nothing" — [Terrydavis (player), 2025-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124944#p124944)); mana ("It's not fun to be spending 50% or more of your time in BGs just drinking" — [Thaladar (player), 2025-09-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=151247#p151247)); Mind Flay breaks on freedom effects (Skattberg 2025-09-10).
- **Standing**: contested; the Disc/Smite spec is often preferred for priest PvP since CC2 ("Every priest is playing disc spec in pvp over shadow" — [Forumdweller (player), 2025-04-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128453#p128453); "They made shadow priest weaker than disc one" — [Ataika (player), 2024-12-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112258#p112258)).

## Stat priority (PvP)

- Spell hit **3%** vs level-60 players (Heroclastus 2024-10-08; Grizb37 2024-10-10); "4% spell hit is required in PvP against lv 60 players with 300 defence to have 99%" — [Zvyrhol (player), 2024-10-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102656#p102656) (contested 3 vs 4%).
- Spell penetration 1:1 vs resistances (Heroclastus 2024-10-08); Forsaken +10 SR, mage armor, Mark of the Wild raise resists (Zvyrhol 2024-10-06).
- **Stamina** heavily: "stack lots of stamina(e.g ragnaros str cloak as stupid as it sounds, str tanking warrior ring from aq40,naxx 24 stam ring,nefarian tanking neck, etc) ... Stamina is your only defence and its better to die with 0 mana than keep dying with full mana" — [Springboards (player), 2024-07-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98805#p98805) (written for Disc; "spriests will never use tier sets on pvp bc leaves you too squishy" — [Kentorianx (player), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168308#p168308)).
- Spell power (damage and healing) for VE/PW:S; +spelldmg gear "also boosts our healing abilities" (Frantsel).
- Raid gear is PvP gear (no resilience yet in 1.18.0; PvP Dampening debuff introduced 1.18.0, dynamic per class/spec in 1.18.1 — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)).

## Single-target sequence (duel / focus target)

Opener (from range, ~20-24 yd): PW:S self → `Vampiric Embrace` (instant since 1.18.0) → `Shadow Word: Pain` → `Mind Blast` → `Mind Flay` (slow) → `Pain Spike` when the target is low ("Shadowpriest ... outlast ~80% and then finish you off with an instant" — Atreidon 2025-04-01; Pain Spike ≈ 627 non-crit / 940 crit at 600 SP with Shadowform + 3 stacks — same post).

Priority:
1. Keep `Shadow Word: Pain` up (dispel bait: rank 1 SW:P re-applied to eat dispels; low-rank spam "should never have been in the game" per [Snkx (player), 2025-02-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120250#p120250), so expect it to be seen as cheap).
2. `Mind Blast` on cooldown (main burst; Inner Focus for a free crit).
3. `Mind Flay` when in 20-24 yd; stop if the target has freedom/druid shapeshift (Skattberg).
4. `Pain Spike` (30 s CD) as finisher; useless if it does not kill ("if Pain Spike doesn't kill then it achieves... nothing" — [Hawkbutt (player), 2025-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124794#p124794)).
5. Undead: `Devouring Plague` (mana-heavy; "with burst meta it's not even worth using" as Smite — [Zvyrhol (player), 2025-09-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150297#p150297); Shadow "might be more useful"); use with Inner Focus — [Volkyte (player), 2025-08-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146134#p146134).
6. Troll: keep `Shadowguard` up always (no CD; "once geared ... shadowguard starts hitting..ALOT ... plus it can also stun ranged attackers" — [Springboards (player), 2025-11-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160917#p160917)); `Hex of Weakness` rank 1 as dispel bait, max rank vs healers (−20% healing; paladins cannot cleanse curses) — [Halfgeek9 (player), 2025-05-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=133280#p133280).
7. Night Elf: `Starshards` through line of sight ("the channel effect going through LOS" — [Thaladar (player), 2025-08-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143498#p143498)) and as kick bait ("if a rogue kicks your starshards you're free to cast shadow and holy spells" — [Steakhouse (player), 2024-10-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104860#p104860)).

## Multi-target

- DoT everything in range with SW:P; `Psychic Scream` when 2+ melee are on you; `Holy Nova` rank 1 spam to flush stealth ("A rank 1 spammed goes a long way to prevent being opened on" — [Itsbacon (player), 2025-08-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143823#p143823)) if Holy Nova is talented; `Mana Burn` on enemy healers only if Improved Mana Burn taken (3 s cast otherwise — [Thaladar (player), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141217#p141217)).
- Dispel Magic on paladin seals/blessings and mage/druid buffs — a priest can "purge every 1,5sec ... the whole BG" — [player, 2026-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=22214) (1.18.1 release thread; also debated in the paladin forum [t=23094](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=23094)).

## Cooldowns and resources

- `Psychic Scream` (Improved: shorter CD) — the only reliable escape; assume trinket/WotF/Fear Ward.
- `Silence` (20 yd base): interrupt heals/casters (Frantsel; Skattberg).
- `Power Word: Shield` on self every 15 s (Weakened Soul); castable in Shadowform (staff 2024-10-21).
- `Fade` 1.18.1: CD −3/6 s with Improved Fade; no PvP effect in sources.
- Mana: drink whenever out of combat; "when i run out of mana, i just go suicide and respawn at full mana" (Crake) vs buff costs after rez (Thaladar 2025-10-02). Carry Nordanaar Herbal Tea ("the cheapest consumable for pvping" — Atreidon 2024-07-08).
- Race: Troll (Shadowguard, Hex, Berserking) is the Horde consensus for BG play vs paladins ("Troll, no contest" — Halfgeek9 2025-08-16; Springboards, Ataika, Volkyte agree); Undead for WotF + Fear Ward ("having both WOTF and Fear Ward is absolutely gamebreaking" — [Thaladar (player), 2025-08-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146103#p146103)) — **contested**. Alliance: Dwarf (Stoneform removes poison/bleed/disease off-GCD, Avatar, Desperate Prayer) — [Turboman (player), 2025-08-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145204#p145204); Night Elf for Shadowmeld/Starshards/bow — [Thaladar (player), 2025-08-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145223#p145223); "Any alliance priest is weak. Try it out, its fun!" — [Konstantin19 (player), 2025-05-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132509#p132509).

## Role strategy / tactics

- Stay at 20-24 yd (Mind Flay/Silence range), never max range; pillar/LoS with Starshards (NE).
- Versus hunters: "If the hunter can get away quick enough, they'll live. But if I can land a MB and swp, yeah they're dead before Viper sting kills my mana" (Drubarrymooer 2024-02-07); in group fights the hunter's drains win the long game — [Ragetto (player), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91676#p91676).
- Versus melee: PW:S, fear, Blackout luck; "As a priest, you will die in seconds to any melee making it up to you" — [Mayson (player), 2025-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124872#p124872).
- Versus dispellers (paladins, priests): expect SW:P/VE removed; "Devouring Plague is CLEANSED" (Halfgeek9); Shadow Weaving pre-1.18.1 protected DoTs (post-1.18.1 it is a self-buff — Nrgsa objects).
- Group play: shield/off-heal allies ("you can drop out of shadowform and pump flashheals" — Frantsel); dispel enemy buffs constantly.
- Chastise is unavailable to Shadow (Disc capstone).

## Gear and consumables

Stamina + spell power raid pieces (Springboards list above); Time Frozen Bow for NE (Blackduck4, see `discipline-pvp.md`); Nordanaar Herbal Tea; PvP Dampening is dynamic per spec in 1.18.1 (staff) — no numbers in sources.

## Common mistakes

- Opening with Mind Flay at max range (out of range / pushed back).
- Wasting Psychic Scream into Fear Ward/WotF/trinket; fear an undead only after WotF is burned.
- Casting VE mid-melee pressure (pre-1.18.0 cast time); post-1.18.0 apply it before engaging.
- Pain Spike early (heals back if the target survives 5 s).
- Ignoring stamina in favor of tier sets (Springboards, Kentorianx).
- Never dispelling / never shielding ("Half of them never even dispelled or shielded" — Snkx 2025-03-30).
