# Priest — Leveling guide (1-60), incl. Hardcore and race notes

Era markers as in `holy-healer.md`. The most-referenced leveling guide (Aykhanchik, 2022) predates Class Changes 2; its talent names and links are outdated ("Can you update this guide for 1.17.2 ? links dont work anymore" — [Nuninho32 (player), 2025-01-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115669#p115669)). The only complete post-CC2 talent order is Langorn's Discipline leveling build (2025-09).

## Wands and Spirit Tap (the two consensus talents)

- "Wand Specialization and Spirit Tap are the only must have talents to have" — [Aykhanchik (guide writer; 52-post guide t=2484, repeatedly requested for updates), 2022-09-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24431#p24431). "Wands as always when levelling your priest are your best friend (there are wand merchants selling white ones(!))" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-01-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164458#p164458). "At lower levels, the Enchanting wands are insanely OP and often do more DPS than your spells" — [Getplucked (player), 2023-07-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53458#p53458). "as soon as you hit level 5, try to get a Lesser Magic Wand (item 11287)" (Aykhanchik); "crucial to get a wand ASAP and upgrade it to Greater Magic Wand ASAP. I bought mine on the AH for about 60 silver" — [Reomc1 (player), 2025-02-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121113#p121113).
- Wand Specialization is 2 points on Turtle (+12/25% damage, +5/10% hit, mana on hit equal to your level — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102970#p102970)); it also affects Night Elf bows since 2024-12-20 — [Jamey (staff), 2024-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=113460#p113460).
- Spirit Tap value is **contested** post-CC2: "after using the wand talent in version 1.17.2, it will restore mana. This makes the Spirit Tap talent not very practical" — [Terryleelolorz (player, HC), 2025-03-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121460#p121460); "Spirit tap is key, and if leveling disc or holy, your first 5 points will be in this" (Ortogs 2023); in duos "tell your buddy to let you last hit where possible" — [Gargadon (player), 2021-01-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9142#p9142). 1.18.1: Spirit Tap "Now also triggers on Mind Blast critical strikes" — [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-26 revision](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- Quivers do not speed wands on Turtle — [Aykhanchik, 2022-12-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30952#p30952), tested by [Kiersteadmo (player), 2023-06-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=48397#p48397).

## Build B — Shadow leveling

- Order: Wand Specialization 2 → Spirit Tap 5 → Improved SW:P 2 → Shadow Focus 3 ("5/5 Shadow Focus is overkill, 3/5 is sufficient") → Mind Flay → Shadow Weaving → VE → Darkness → Shadowform at 40; skip Improved VE solo ("Vampiric Embrace costs 40 mana, you need to deal at least 1k shadow damage just to get the mana of Vampiric Embrace back"), put spare points into Improved Shield and Fortitude — [Hctwowfan (player), 2024-10-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103013#p103013) (reply to an HC Human shadow priest; the OP's classicdb link `bVZZEMxtcxRt` is pre-CC2). Ortogs 2023: "After that you can get 10% hit ... making sure clutch spells land like psychic scream, MC and Mind Soothe. Then 2 points in swp". Sylvestro1 (2026-01) asked the same question about Shadow Affinity/Imp SW:P — unanswered — [Sylvestro1 (player), 2026-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166181#p166181).
- Hardcore variant: "I Recommend Wand Spec, followed by spirit tap and something that evolves into: `talents.turtlecraft.gg/priest?points=QoAKRb--FACYQ` Or similar by the time you hit 40. Casting other dmgspells than sw:p before you ding 40 is most of the time not worth the mana. Just wandweave and consider respeccing for shadowform lvl 42. Before that, the extra surviability you get from a few points in disc are much better imo" — [Atreidon (player), 2026-01-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164458#p164458).
- 1.18.1 note: the first 5 Shadow points are now Spirit Tap or Improved Mind Blast; Blackout is row 2 — [Jamey (staff), 2026-03-19 revision](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071); "Blackout being in first or 2nd row makes more sense. anything further will be damaging to any hybrid priests. The changes to spirit tap are good though!" — [Kairi (player), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169720#p169720).
- Respec point: 40-42 for Shadowform (Atreidon); before that most sources say Disc points are better.

## Build D — Night Elf Starshards ("Arcane priest")

- 2020 original 21/25/5 (classicdb `bxgMsM0oZbxtMcbVZx`): Unbreakable Will 5, Silent Resolve 5, Imp Fortitude 2, Martyrdom 2, Inner Focus, Meditation 3, Mental Agility 2, Divine Spirit / Healing Focus 2, Holy Specialization 5, Divine Fury 5, Holy Nova, Inspiration 2, Improved Healing 3, Searing Light 2 (old), Spiritual Guidance 5 / Spirit Tap 5; rotation "Holy Fire, Starshards, melee hit … repeat" (10-10.5 s) — [Roxanneflowers (player; staff-endorsed: "I needed something like this" — Dragunovi (staff), 2020-07-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6905#p6905). ## Kill rotation and playstyle (solo)

- Disc/Smite (Langorn): "Holy Fire -> Smite -> Shadow Word Pain -> Wand till death of the monster"; Mind Blast optional; multi-pull: "Fear your enemies and start focusing one ... shield yourself ... Heal yourself"; elites: "Drop a lightwell [pre-1.18.1 click version], shield yourself before the pull and each time your shield CD is up ... fear on CD to refresh shadow word pain and holy fire. Heal yourself with renew and the lightwell".
- Shadow (Ortogs 2023, consensus): "EVERY priest rotation should be mindblast or Holy fire as a starter, SW:P, mindflay or smite, then WAND ... The wand with swp ticking lets you get out of the five second rule". Shadow-specific cost (Aykhanchik 2022): "Mind Flay has ticks, you lose em if you get hit ... You apply PW: Shield before Mind Flay ... You drop Shadowform to heal yourself and then put it back on = you waste more mana".
- SW:P debate: skip it on 1v1 non-elites ("a 'DPS LOSS' and a waste of mana ... by the time shadow word pain does its first tick of damage you could have done more damage" — Nymdorl 2022-02-02) vs "throwing sw:pain on top of an auto wand is simply more damage done hence a faster kill" (Aykhanchik 2022-02-02) — **contested**; bots: SW:P only on elites/multi-pulls or when the wand is weak.
- Mobs at once: 1, pulling with PW:S up; Mind Control a mob inside a big pack to have others kill it, then reset (Aykhanchik).
- Downtime: exploit the 5-second rule (wand last 20-30% — Daoc2001 2024-10-25); "You either wand everything to death or sit and drink every 3-4 mobs" (Getplucked 2023-07-14).
- Melee weaving (optional): "Wand and melee uses separate swing timers"; Inner Fire AP buffs melee; macros for Shoot/Attack on mouse wheel — [Ibux (player), 2023-02-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=35235#p35235); "Spell casts will trigger melee swing CD, even if they are instant cast. If you cast a spell, use your wand" — [Cookieboy (player), 2023-11-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79217#p79217); Inner Fire now grants spell damage, not AP (staff CC2), so AP-based weaving advice is pre-CC2.
- Night Elf bow: shoot does not trigger the GCD; wand/bow swap macro; bow deadzone means no shooting in melee — Thaladar 2025-08-07, Imonobor 2024-10-20; most players still wand while leveling (Steakhouse 2024-10-18).
- Dungeons: heal in any spec (Ishilu, Hawkbutt); Renew downranked on DPS, PW:S for aggro pulls (Ironhorse 2025-04-19, Akos 2025-04-19); Mind Blast in a group "is basically a taunt and you will pull" (Nymdorl 2022-02-02).

## Skill training priority (Aykhanchik 2022, still the only list)

"Must buy/upgrade spells are: Smite, Holy Fire, Shadow Word: Pain, Power Word: Fortitude, Power Word: Shield, Inner Fire, Flash Heal*, Renew, Mind Control*, Prayer of Healing*, Cure Disease, Dispel Magic, Psychic Scream and Fade*." Optional: "Heal, Mind Soothe, Shackle Undead, Mind Vision, Mana Burn, Desperate Prayer" — [Aykhanchik, 2022-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15484#p15484). Nymdorl adds Desperate Prayer as "your number one oh fuck spell" and Mind Blast for shadow-tolerant players. Turtle additions to train: Pain Spike ranks at 30/40/50/60 (all priests, no Shadowform needed), Fear Ward at 20 (all races), Divine Spirit at 30, Lightwell at 40 (one rank in 1.18.1), Smite rank 9 and Shadow Mend from books at 58-60 — staff CC2 post and timeline. Which ranks players skip: not stated beyond "only buy and upgrade specific spells" (gap).

## Stats while leveling

"Stats you wanna prioritize are Spirit>Intelligence>Stamina. Items that give spellpower, spell damage or spell healing are also good ... prioritizing items with high stats to those that give +shadow damage" (Aykhanchik 2022); "Invest in Spirit on your gear, since it increases your MP5" (Amyloriley 2024). Inner Fire's spell-damage version makes it worth casting from 12 (staff CC2). Key upgrades: wands every ~10 levels (Lesser → Greater Magic Wand → dungeon wands; enchanting wands "cheap in the ah" — Nymdorl), Inner Fire ranks, a spirit/int staff.

## Hardcore-mode differences

- Discipline all the way, wand-weave, Spirit Tap optional (Terryleelolorz 2025-03, level 30 HC, heals dungeons).
- Shadow HC: Shadow Focus 3/5, no Imp VE, Imp Shield + Fortitude for safety (Hctwowfan 2024-10); Atreidon's HC/Vagrant advice: Disc survivability points until 40-42, then Shadowform.
- Fear resists are a death risk ("Particularly whee you want a security for bad pulls and fear fesists as a HC" — Atreidon 2026-01-05) → Shadow Focus 2-3 even as Disc; PW:S before every pull; Desperate Prayer (Human/Dwarf).
- Inner Fire "makes a noticeable difference" in hardcore — [Mativh (player), 2022-11-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29117#p29117).
