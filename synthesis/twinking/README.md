# Twinking on Turtle WoW

Twinking — deliberately stopping XP at a battleground bracket ceiling and gearing for PvP — is
community content the rest of this knowledge base does not cover. This guide is built from the
archived Turtle WoW Europe Discord `#twinking` channel (5,026 messages, 2025-02-15 → 2026-05-03)
and its archived talent screenshots. Every claim cites `[[d:twinking#<message_id>]]`; the cited
messages are reproduced verbatim in `structured/discord/evidence-twinking.jsonl`. Machine-readable
data lives in `structured/twinking.yaml`.

**Era tags** follow `../CONVENTIONS.md`: `pre-1.18.1` (before 2025-10-03), `1.18.1-announced-pre-release`
(2025-10-03 → 2026-03-19), `post-1.18.1` (from 2026-03-20). **Agreement** is `consensus`,
`single source` or `contested`. There is no green-name/staff signal in the channel; the two players
who repeatedly post tested, armory-verified itemisation are **Mothra** (29/39 BiS and tier lists) and
**dindunuffinz** (19/29 hunter, paladin and economy numbers) — both are treated as players, not staff
[[d:twinking#1479624368123412501]], [[d:twinking#1471036618226798785]]. General vanilla knowledge
used only for context is labelled `(vanilla baseline, not from Discord)`.

## What twinking is

A twink stops gaining XP at a bracket ceiling and optimises gear, enchants and talents for WPvP,
WSG and AB:

> "A twink is a character who stops leveling for example at 39 and then this person is spending time
> to find the best gear+enchants+talentbuilds for PVP, and then compete in WPVP and in arenas WSG
> and AB" [[d:twinking#1407437366368665783]]

Stated motives (consensus, pre-1.18.1 → 1.18.1-announced-pre-release) [[d:twinking#1364638956439998564]],
[[d:twinking#1364908649365114961]], [[d:twinking#1481605809778589709]], [[d:twinking#1446551289759793203]]:

- Skip the level-60 raid-gear arms race — "at 60 if you're not t2+ geared you get dropped in seconds,
  twinking you can be bis in a week or less" [[d:twinking#1364638956439998564]].
- Farm WSG/AB reputation before level 60 [[d:twinking#1364908649365114961]].
- Play a lower-button PvP game with a smaller gear gap than Naxx-geared 60 PvP [[d:twinking#1481605809778589709]].

**Vocabulary** (1.18.1-announced-pre-release, consensus) [[d:twinking#1412348723056611418]],
[[d:twinking#1455672950475854009]]: *twink* = full XP-locked BiS; *semi-twink* = good blue gear and cheap
enchants while still levelling; *lunatic* = a level 1-9 XP-locked character (a separate community, the
`level-one-lunatic` scope); *grandfathered* / *GF'd* = a piece kept after a quest, level or enchant change
made it newly unobtainable at that level.

## Brackets in use

Turtle keeps vanilla's 10-level brackets. The active twink brackets over this period are
**10-19, 20-29, 30-39, 40-49 and 50-59**, plus a level **1-9** WSG/AB scene. AV is 51-60 and therefore
59-only [[d:twinking#1386437589724299476]].

A recurring answer to "which bracket should I make" is: roll 29, then move the same character through
39 and 49 and keep the one you like [[d:twinking#1382342431525175386]].

**Where the games are.** Population is split across three realms (Nordanaar, Tel'Abim, Ambershire,
launched 2025-09). Nordanaar had the oldest twink community [[d:twinking#1419993480993706024]];
Ambershire had the most levellers and therefore the most frequent queues after launch
[[d:twinking#1485734163280691250]], [[d:twinking#1428180161857060997]]; Tel'Abim is the only PvP
(world-PvP) realm [[d:twinking#1426646467535175803]]. The Ambershire launch fragmented the queue and
killed Nord PvP by late 2025 [[d:twinking#1428066961673289739]], [[d:twinking#1429768767499665419]].

## Per-bracket meta and BiS

### 1-9 (Lunatic)

A separate XP-locked community with its own WSG/AB turn-ins, guild and events; not covered here beyond
the cross-reference to the `level-one-lunatic` scope (consensus/single source) [[d:twinking#1386437589724299476]],
[[d:twinking#1413241575147962428]], [[d:twinking#1490827950931578930]].

### 10-19

**Meta (pre-1.18.1, consensus).** Paladin is S-tier [[d:twinking#1362191649093976246]]; the most OP
classes at 19 are "hunters followed by rogues" [[d:twinking#1422318689524387915]]. Melee dominates and
casters are weak because spell-power coefficients are "HEAVILY reduced below level 20, by vanilla
design" [[d:twinking#1346298771918815322]].
Priests are the best healers [[d:twinking#1422318689524387915]].

**Why paladin is S-tier.** Turtle puts a final-TBC-tier ability, **Crusader Strike, at level 7-8**; a
geared ret paladin "can easily get 600+ crits" at 19 [[d:twinking#1362191649093976246]],
[[d:twinking#1383230595718582273]], [[d:twinking#1346369628921921557]].

**Alliance gear advantage (pre-1.18.1, consensus).** Alliance 19s get "5/5 gloves/tunic and 11% quiver
… the 2 all stat boe trinket and … the 10 ap one", fishing hat/boots, an Alliance-only quest reward,
the "best 2h weapon called Glacial Stone", and — critically — an **enchantable neck**: the WSG neck/ring
are ilvl 23 and cannot take ring enchants, whereas the Alliance Wetlands neck is ilvl 30 and can take the
JC +6 Agility enchant [[d:twinking#1405158689492570152]], [[d:twinking#1405169193439203489]],
[[d:twinking#1405202686474715278]], [[d:twinking#1405202967052816508]], [[d:twinking#1422318689524387915]],
[[d:twinking#1422514577052139592]]. Horde compensation is racials: "tauren is s tier and orc/troll are a+
tier in the bracket as hunters" [[d:twinking#1405169193439203489]].

### 20-29

**Meta (1.18.1-announced-pre-release).** Mothra's 2026-03-07 solo-queue tier list
[[d:twinking#1479624368123412501]]: *Solo carry* = Feral Tank, Frost; *Strong* = Feral DPS, Ret, Holy
Pal, Surv, MM, Combat, Sub, Enh, Shadow; *Useful* = Priest Holy, Disc, Afflic, Druid Resto, Fire, Fury,
Shaman Resto, BM, Arms; *Don't recommend* = Prot Pal, Destro, Prot War, Assassination, Balance, Demo,
Arcane; *Unplayable* = Ele. A separate 29 AB list puts Feral DPS and MM at Very Strong and
Surv/Enh/Ret/Holy Pal/Sub/Druid Resto/Feral Tank as Strong [[d:twinking#1479629989254008895]]. Feral druid
is repeatedly called "probably the strongest spec in the game at 29" [[d:twinking#1488253153186021628]],
[[d:twinking#1490707169463505019]].

**General advice (consensus).** "every class is good since there is no established meta like on 19 …
shamans are very tanky and can purge and hit hard … since on 29 you get so much more new engineering
tools the playing field is equal"; Free Action Potion and Invisibility Potion become available
[[d:twinking#1391624471487709256]]. Alliance still has "vastly better gear" but Horde had better players
[[d:twinking#1413381288378892388]]; the new Stormwrought quest weapon (highest-DPS 1h mace/dagger) is
Alliance-only and "breaking the current 29 bracket balance" [[d:twinking#1413482964159037450]],
[[d:twinking#1419689789254406295]].

### 30-39

**Cost.** 39 is the most expensive bracket to gear: "a 39 hunter needs boe chest, legs, belt, gloves(epic),
boe gun, boe bracers, boe neck, boe epic head … a buttload of coin"; casters need BOE **Staff of Jordan**
(>500g) or an 11-14 dmg dagger + Umbral Crystal and 2× Underworld Bands, which fresh 60s bid up
[[d:twinking#1426552371726782544]]. Gearing 39 "is more expensive than 49, mainly [because] at 39 there
are barely any quest/gear rewards from dungeon above your level bracket"
[[d:twinking#1468562189618249818]].

**Pendulum of Doom (PoD)** is the defining weapon: "pound for pound … maybe the best PvP weapon in the
game", with a huge top-end and ~100% Crusader uptime on instant-heavy classes; the difference to the
next-best 2h is "totally irreconcilable" [[d:twinking#1469006777067765814]],
[[d:twinking#1483797931160244405]], [[d:twinking#1469005865662156851]]. **Widow's Kiss** is the BiS dagger
(~750g on Ambershire) [[d:twinking#1460667673762332918]]. 39 gets "a boatload" of custom trinkets
(4-all-stat, stamina, 24 AP BOE, crit BOE from Stormwrought, Desolace trinkets)
[[d:twinking#1488368275271450624]].

### 40-49

**Why 49 over 39** — the canonical list (Icemang, 2025-02-27, single source): 60% mounts and carrot/car
keys (stack with spurs + riding-speed enchant); 31-point capstone talents; almost all class abilities;
rank 9 vs rank 5; better PvP rep gear; better spell scaling with higher ranks; can 1v1 some 60s; max
potions; unique TWoW items/mounts from Gilneas and Hateforge Quarry; only BRD gear is equippable at 49
but you can farm LBRS/Scholo/Strat for later; and it was fresh/cheap at the time
[[d:twinking#1344596242252038144]]. BRD gear usable at 49 comes from arena bosses, Roccor, Houndmaster
and the early mage/fire bosses [[d:twinking#1344723115107680319]]. 49 gear is mostly low-level-entry
quest/dungeon drops (classic behaviour), so it is **cheaper than 39** [[d:twinking#1468562189618249818]].

### 50-59

59 is played for honor and can equip raid gear. Reported build sources: the tier 0.5 dungeon set and the
DM north tribute axe for warrior [[d:twinking#1492779034789023804]]; ZG enchants and the Hakkar trinket
[[d:twinking#1456582003892813960]]; dungeon-2 or ZG gear in general [[d:twinking#1382336945472016536]].
**Soulforge** is the paladin set [[d:twinking#1477617361384378469]]. Ambershire's `Almost Sixty` Alliance
premade ran daily at rank 11-12, 100-200k honor in a few hours on WSG/AB days; a separate report called
the bracket "unplayable" because premades farm it and games end in 5 minutes
[[d:twinking#1453642847247925359]], [[d:twinking#1475590151559446559]] (**contested**). ZG was
accidentally de-level-locked for a period ("they screwed up zg, its no longer 50"), allowing low-level
entry [[d:twinking#1471160122104348824]].

## Enchants, BoE/BoP and the trade-window rules

This is the most Turtle-specific mechanics area: the rules players tested and repeated.

### Trade-window enchanting

A higher-level character can enchant a lower-level character's **soulbound/BoP** item by trading it, and
the low-level character keeps it (1.18.1-announced-pre-release, consensus)
[[d:twinking#1428257722364854394]], [[d:twinking#1427403694865125447]].

- Naxx shoulder enchant on a BoP shoulder: "Just recently had Naxx enchant applied to a BoP shoulder I
  had. Can be applied through trade window." [[d:twinking#1428257722364854394]]; Saph (Sapphiron)
  enchants confirmed [[d:twinking#1401129840161062912]].
- "+7 dmg … apply the scope using a high level character on the trade window" — the scope itself is
  level-40, applied by a level 40+ through trade [[d:twinking#1391064952508252342]],
  [[d:twinking#1457973737721696370]], [[d:twinking#1458028403608256677]].
- ZG (Zandalar) head/leg enchants and arcanums were historically trade-applicable; by 2026 "not possible
  to get zg enchants anymore" on Ambershire (and AD ones were patched)
  [[d:twinking#1478576918059155609]], [[d:twinking#1477032537007198315]]; a later player says ZG shoulder
  enchants are out but need Zandalar exalted [[d:twinking#1477082896505507900]]. **Contested.**

### Armor kits

Armor kits above your level can be used **if the item's ilvl meets the kit's minimum**, applied by a high
level [[d:twinking#1455672950475854009]], [[d:twinking#1477025612492181675]]. **Thick Armor Kit**
requires item ilvl 25+ (roughly required level + 5) [[d:twinking#1477031129164026049]],
[[d:twinking#1477079144411037818]]. "GF'd armor kits (40 armor/5 stam, before they released the ilvl
update for rings and armor kits)" still exist on old twinks [[d:twinking#1455672950475854009]].

### Ring/neck (JC) enchants and the ilvl gate

Enchantable jewellery must be **item ilvl 25+ or a quest reward**: "Jewelry that can be enchanted are
only those item level 25 above or quest reward" [[d:twinking#1422528611360964751]]; +6 Agility (JC) and
+3 stats are the relevant recipes [[d:twinking#1406062324825063585]], [[d:twinking#1439244902600015905]].
The WSG neck and ring are ilvl 23 and **cannot** be enchanted; the Alliance Wetlands quest neck is ilvl
30 and can, which is why Alliance hunters are materially ahead
[[d:twinking#1405202686474715278]], [[d:twinking#1405169193439203489]]. The Redridge ring quest gives a
second enchantable ring (Alliance) [[d:twinking#1422528526363394069]]; Horde gets a +7 Agility ring
(ilvl 25) that can be enchanted [[d:twinking#1405169193439203489]]. GF'd +6 Agility ring/neck enchants
pre-date the ilvl requirement [[d:twinking#1405158498127314966]].

### Weapon enchants

- **Crusader**: proc rate is PPM-based; a slower weapon has a higher per-swing (and therefore per-instant)
  proc chance. Do **not** put it on an offhand since abilities use the mainhand
  [[d:twinking#1440092660538015948]], [[d:twinking#1440091411025236110]].
- Lifestealing "will proc 3x as often as Crusader" (single-source test by dindunuffinz); Fiery has the
  highest sustained DPS but least burst; LS is better in 1v1/team fights where self-heal matters
  [[d:twinking#1440078289451483258]], [[d:twinking#1478075694587187251]]. **Contested/single source.**
- Fiery/weapon procs penetrate BoP (vanilla) and are favoured vs high-armour paladins; fiery does not
  proc mace stun but Windfury (an attack) can [[d:twinking#1406997556415692932]],
  [[d:twinking#1440117095684640829]].
- 39 "double enchant" trick: two Pendulums of Doom, one Crusader, and swap to the Badlands quest "Fiery
  Blaze" weapon after the Crusader proc [[d:twinking#1487833577860370504]], [[d:twinking#1487839884063539200]].
- A 25 Agility 2h or 15 Agility 1h is common for hunters/rogues; 22 Int and 20 Spirit swap-weapons for
  shaman/mage; weapon chain as the anti-disarm swap [[d:twinking#1439244902600015905]],
  [[d:twinking#1395096177153671338]].

### Professions

**Engineering is mandatory for twinks** ("ENG definitely needed for all twinks")
[[d:twinking#1420281111019524197]], [[d:twinking#1416133409335410740]]. Gnome racial gives the
engineering head and Escape Artist [[d:twinking#1419689789254406295]]. Recommended order: "learn Gnomish
first for the BoP items, then drop it and relevel Goblin for the consumables. You will still be able to
use the BoPs" [[d:twinking#1449205111464263720]]; gnomish trinkets are craft-once, goblin is for sappers
[[d:twinking#1449880391346557158]]. **Goblin Bomb Dispenser is bugged** — it "doesn't scale, has
double-digit HP" [[d:twinking#1359626950057918596]]. Green Lens requires 245 engineering (225 + racial 15
is not enough) [[d:twinking#1422597330804150333]].

## Consumables and world buffs

Flasks require level 50, so they are unavailable in the lower brackets except 50-59
[[d:twinking#1344641732700274780]]. Lower-bracket staples (consensus) [[d:twinking#1395399899599732847]],
[[d:twinking#1367522520856657921]], [[d:twinking#1444045872571613275]], [[d:twinking#1477186978985414688]],
[[d:twinking#1463771368289206283]]: Swiftness Potion (~15s each, "buy 100"), Free Action Potion (~1.6g,
"8-10 potions per bg" for some), Limited Invulnerability Potion, Restorative Potion, **Strong Anti-Venom**
(the answer to rogue crippling poison [[d:twinking#1464274551876747367]]), health/mana/armour/regen
potions, scrolls, murloc nets, sleeping dust, grenades and wizard oil. Higher-level wizard oils (30/40)
and the Darkmoon buff can be applied via trade; dragonslayer/rend world buffs "don't really work or give
dmg" [[d:twinking#1460236314535399444]], [[d:twinking#1472086813572337796]]. "Mongoose is too expensive
for pvp, just use the regular ones for agility" [[d:twinking#1367522173706571827]].

## Speed, movement and jumps

Speed buffs **do not stack**; the game keeps the highest. AB boots 8%, boot enchant 8% and the Zandalar
world buff 10% do not stack with each other, nor with Aspect of the Cheetah, Travel/Cat Form or Ghost
Wolf [[d:twinking#1418855475021353080]], [[d:twinking#1418613730480099508]]. Exceptions that *do* stack or
add: the Night Elf 1% racial, the post-1.18 rogue passive speed (additive, stacks with AB boots) and the
rogue Improved Ghostly Strike 15% active [[d:twinking#1418855475021353080]],
[[d:twinking#1418706927344681003]]. A 2025-08 claim that "athari pants" stacked with boots to >20% on a
paladin was disputed as "not intended game behavior" (**contested**)
[[d:twinking#1409458675382222978]], [[d:twinking#1409545485429112832]].

WSG jumps are a skill differentiator; race/gender affects which jumps are possible, and gnomes famously
cannot do the Alliance "ToT" jumps [[d:twinking#1365983331765714964]], [[d:twinking#1493334100096979146]].
**Safespotting became a bannable PvP offence** in the 2026-01-11 rules update
[[d:twinking#1459955573008109600]], [[d:twinking#1459951630882705469]].

## Twink PvP practice and community

**Queues and bracket health.**

- WSG pops from the minimap PvP button at every level; AB was historically **battlemaster-only** for
  sub-20 (and 1-9), which is why AB barely ran. It was finally made queueable from the minimap (via the
  third-party **PVPWhen** addon) in 2026-03 [[d:twinking#1411025129781203007]],
  [[d:twinking#1486081059115503828]].
- Arena skirmishes/rated can be queued at any bracket at the Gurubashi Arena, but they almost never pop
  below 60 unless organised by hand [[d:twinking#1424885403802140692]], [[d:twinking#1394703829320204380]].
- System BG announcements were removed in 2025-09 and their loss was blamed for dead brackets; players
  filed bug report 16807 to restore them [[d:twinking#1416223332679811182]],
  [[d:twinking#1423755671803006986]], [[d:twinking#1423757637874483231]].
- BG XP was removed for a period, then WSG/AB XP returned; levelling through BGs was popular ("WSG 1 flag
  capture gives about 1.5-2 bars") [[d:twinking#1435949946129092640]], [[d:twinking#1439730335800885368]].
- **Honor/rank caps by level**: 29 caps at rank 3 [[d:twinking#1435855490281373822]]; 39 at rank 5 and 49
  at rank 9 [[d:twinking#1344596242252038144]]; rank 3 gives a 10% vendor/repair discount
  [[d:twinking#1371830684053667901]]. A 2026-04 patch briefly allowed over-cap ranking before ranks were
  reset [[d:twinking#1488874245961416714]].

**Cross-faction BGs.** Introduced around 2025-11 ("Now we have cross faction PVP so you don't know how
and where you end up, what boosted the activity again") [[d:twinking#1440868664617340979]],
[[d:twinking#1436416938237624544]]. It ended the "Alliance twinks dominate one bracket" problem and mixed
teams game-to-game [[d:twinking#1464383700488945822]] but prevented twinks from cross-faction teaming
[[d:twinking#1444098235466649611]].

**Guilds/communities.** The hub guilds were **Kul Tiran Admiralty**, **Warsong Outriders**, **Stinkie
Twinkies**, **Rats**, **Nephilim** and **Dragonmaw Clan** on Nordanaar
[[d:twinking#1409580627510169721]]; `Stinky Twinkies` was "largest and most active twink guild … cross
faction 19-49" [[d:twinking#1341161665806467293]]. On Tel'Abim, **Xanadu** held most Horde twinks
[[d:twinking#1419993480993706024]]. The community ran an organised twink/arena Discord, weekly WSG/AB
nights and WPvP/arena events including Valentine's and Gurubashi "Night of the Loa"
[[d:twinking#1412129467467567247]], [[d:twinking#1429892059107430653]],
[[d:twinking#1340077014886908008]]. A final "twink meet 'n bash" was held 2026-05-02 ahead of the server
sunset [[d:twinking#1500209141182894182]].

**The twink-vs-leveller argument (genuinely contested).**

- *Anti-twink side:* twinks "farm" levellers, the 19 bracket is worst because enchants give the biggest
  boost [[d:twinking#1444185400401723655]]; queues are slow because casual players leave
  [[d:twinking#1413315589887361054]]; a fully-BiS 19 with 60 raid enchants is worse for BG health than any
  jump or consumable [[d:twinking#1459974431643537652]]; "nobody likes twinks unless they play one"
  [[d:twinking#1444378187596304545]].
- *Pro-twink side:* "twinks exist since the start of world of warcraft"; it is the same as a Naxx-geared
  60 vs a fresh 60 [[d:twinking#1444155602363220171]], [[d:twinking#1412795818259255398]]; you can gear a
  twink in a week for cheap [[d:twinking#1364638956439998564]]; what kills queues is faction imbalance,
  not twinks [[d:twinking#1413379312824090655]].
- *Turtle's non-action:* Mothra proposed scaling down low-level enchant power; "this is a classic server
  with a certain degree of vanilla purity and they have been resistant to these sorts of changes"
  [[d:twinking#1446567082484568228]]. A separate-queue proposal was rejected by most respondents as
  unworkable [[d:twinking#1444117282468134942]], [[d:twinking#1444380564932005938]].

## How twinking on Turtle differs from vanilla

1. **Custom talents change the tier list.** Most consequentially, paladins receive a final-TBC-tier
   ability (Crusader Strike) at level 7-8, making them S-tier at 19/29
   [[d:twinking#1362191649093976246]], [[d:twinking#1383230595718582273]]; ret paladins and enh shamans
   are the recurring "hybrids are overloaded" complaint [[d:twinking#1414557993147695134]]. Rogue talents
   are reworked (gouge moved to Sub, cheaper Sinister Strike, new passive speed)
   [[d:twinking#1406861217687011368]], [[d:twinking#1418706927344681003]].
2. **Custom items/dungeons define BiS.** Dragonmaw Retreat (4-piece set + epic neck), Stormwrought
   Descent (quest weapon/chest/trinkets), Windhorn, Hateforge Quarry, Gilneas, Karazhan (K40) and Emerald
   Sanctum for 59s [[d:twinking#1469869335034990845]], [[d:twinking#1488368275271450624]],
   [[d:twinking#1484633260394025110]], [[d:twinking#1344596242252038144]].
3. **Turtle-only enchant economy** — JC ring/neck, Saph/Fortitude shoulder enchants on BoP, armor kits,
   belt buckles, 2% haste/armor-pen [[d:twinking#1439244902600015905]],
   [[d:twinking#1428257722364854394]], [[d:twinking#1477025612492181675]].
4. **Grandfathering is a first-class problem.** GF'd 29 rogue quest dagger, GF'd 6 Agility ring
   enchants, GF'd armor kits, and an Alliance 29 dagger/gun later moved to level 32
   [[d:twinking#1427346103044542556]], [[d:twinking#1405158498127314966]],
   [[d:twinking#1455744155996127263]], [[d:twinking#1420184454768361615]].
5. **Cross-faction BGs** (from ~2025-11) have no vanilla equivalent and reshaped twink organising
   [[d:twinking#1440868664617340979]].
6. **BG dampening buffs/debuffs** were added in 1.18.0 and made **per-spec** in 1.18.1; twinks worried it
   was tuned around 60 BGs and could "totally nuke a few brackets from orbit"
   [[d:twinking#1404430996178210926]], [[d:twinking#1404437493671006321]],
   [[d:twinking#1484355395257634846]], [[d:twinking#1484361717503885433]].
7. **PvP gear was not competitive at 60** through this period; a patch promising T2-equivalent PvP gear
   was announced for 2026-03 but sub-60 characters got no new PvP vendor gear
   [[d:twinking#1481294117865525249]], [[d:twinking#1481301792129159210]],
   [[d:twinking#1487818436728852500]].
8. **Three-realm population split** (and no cross-realm BGs) fragmented queues in a way vanilla did not;
   transfers/merges were requested repeatedly [[d:twinking#1428044052627980308]],
   [[d:twinking#1428066961673289739]].
9. **XP locking itself was disabled** on 2026-04-26 in the late "turned off" batch that also removed
   `/who` and instant rested logout; this effectively ends new twinking
   [[d:twinking#1497695623019757739]].
10. **Warmode** (PvE realms) rather than vanilla PvP-flagging is the world-PvP context; the warmode change
    and Tel'Abim's low population pushed most PvP to BGs [[d:twinking#1437276356437217341]],
    [[d:twinking#1437269969912991794]].

## The end of twinking (2026-04-26)

On **2026-04-26** Turtle disabled XP-off — alongside `/who` and instant rested logout, with temporary
PvP modifiers — in the final "turned off" batch before the server went offline in May 2026
[[d:twinking#1497695623019757739]]. With XP locking gone, new twinks cannot be created: existing
XP-locked characters remain, but the bracket pipelines that gearing depended on (farming a bracket
ceiling, moving a character 29 → 39 → 49 [[d:twinking#1382342431525175386]]) end. The channel's last
community event was the 2026-05-02 twink meet-up [[d:twinking#1500209141182894182]].

## Gaps and cautions

- **No complete, authoritative BiS list per class/bracket exists in-channel.** The lists here are the
  best-tested player lists (Mothra's 29/39, dindunuffinz's 19/29) plus armory names. Several askers were
  told to use classic twink guides and adapt custom pieces [[d:twinking#1386421700605907165]].
- **Armory links are the actual canonical "lists"** and were not captured as inventories: `Persuasive`
  (29 enh shaman), `Shivering` (29 mage), `Zeroeffort` (29 warlock), `Sackslasher` (29 rogue), `Fappies`
  (29 holy paladin), `Saints` (39 priest), `Ghettobooty` (39 shaman), `Huntardess` (29 hunter),
  `Benchmade` (59 warrior), `Colorfuel` (39 elemental-proc druid)
  [[d:twinking#1455988171979358392]], [[d:twinking#1452358416197484695]],
  [[d:twinking#1461398683227783313]], [[d:twinking#1348661291111157871]],
  [[d:twinking#1481735157261009057]], [[d:twinking#1435232123614265415]],
  [[d:twinking#1436584584849391726]], [[d:twinking#1469872329143357573]],
  [[d:twinking#1493271717143908472]], [[d:twinking#1488250913671086261]]. The item database
  (`database.turtlecraft.gg`) was frequently down, so many IDs were never resolved in-channel.
- **Talent-link codes do not decode** against the 1.18.1 client tree (same finding as `#mage`); treat as
  identifiers only.
- Single-source claims not corroborated elsewhere: Lifestealing "3× Crusader proc"
  [[d:twinking#1440078289451483258]]; the Dragonmaw 6-piece proccing weapon enchants (the author himself
  is "97% sure it doesn't", never proven) [[d:twinking#1488249031099416778]].
- Quest/item IDs are almost never given in-channel; the wiki and `structured/content/quests.yaml` are the
  place to resolve the reward names above.
- The channel ends during the server's final weeks; all post-1.18.1 conclusions are short-window and
  cannot be checked against a mature meta.
