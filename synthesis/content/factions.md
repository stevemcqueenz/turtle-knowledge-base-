# Custom factions and reputations

Reputation factions that Turtle WoW added or substantially rebuilt: how reputation is gained, what each rank sells, where the quartermaster stands, and what players report about the grind. Structured data: `structured/content/factions.yaml`.

**Source tiers.** *Official (staff)* = turtlecraft.gg pages and staff forum threads. *Community wiki* = `turtle-wow.fandom.com`, not official — it supplies almost all of the reward tables below, and its prices and item names are community-maintained and possibly stale. *Player* = non-staff forum posts, which are the only source for what the grind actually costs.

**Reputation thresholds** are vanilla-standard on Turtle: Friendly 3,000 → Honored 6,000 → Revered 12,000 → Exalted 21,000 points per rank, with Exalted spanning 999; Honored gives a 10% vendor discount; Exalted unlocks other races' mounts and most tabards — [wiki: Reputation](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Reputation). Turtle-specific reputation mechanics: reputation rewards are **not reduced by character level** (2022-09-11) and all rep gains run at 100% (2023-07-12); the Human racial **Diplomacy** was fixed so it "no longer incorrectly affects reputation loss" — [Torta (staff), 2026-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174367#p174367).

## 2. Faction by faction

### Silvermoon Remnant (Alliance, High Elf racial faction)

**Where the reputation comes from.** The wiki: most quests come from the **Thalassian Highlands** and **Moonwhisper Coast**, with a small selection in **Ironforge**, **Stormwind City** and **Hawk's Vigil**; **Alinerenah Sunsparrow** holds the **repeatable cloth quests** — [wiki: Silvermoon Remnant](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Silvermoon_Remnant).

**What players actually report — consensus and unhappy.** A High Elf who finished every Thalassian Highlands quest, the Tirisfal chain, the Ironforge and Stormwind quests and the Grim Reaches quests was "not even half honored" — [Catyngla (player), 2025-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155668#p155668). Replies: "Cloth, tons of cloth with a repeatable quest in Alah'Thalas (to become exalted, **only runecloth** works)" — [Camthalion88 (player), 2025-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155682#p155682); "I spent around **300–350 g** to go from honored to exalted" — [Azcron (player), 2025-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155715#p155715). An earlier and more detailed estimate: honored → revered costs "around **5,000 Runecloth**… around **380–390 gold** depending on market conditions" — [Habloid (player), 2024-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85754#p85754). **consensus** across four independent posters over two years.

**The removed dailies.** Repeatable Silvermoon Remnant quests existed before the Thalassian Highlands zone was added and were removed with (or around) 1.17.1 — [Escaton1 (player), 2025-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155689#p155689), [Galendor (player), 2024-10-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105669#p105669), [Drinkjoda (player), 2024-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85623#p85623). A player who tracked the item database lists the removed chains: a High Elf girl searching for her mother in Stormwind; establishing relations with the human/dwarf/gnome embassies; helping the night elves against demons; establishing a moonwell in Alah'Thalas; and a Ghostlands chain that was live only briefly — [Elisleris (player), 2024-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85632#p85632). Players repeatedly asked staff to restore dailies — [Dracarusggotham (player), 2024-10-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=105671#p105671), [Habloid (player), 2024-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85754#p85754). **No staff reply in this extraction.** Players also assert the Horde equivalent (Revantusk) kept its dailies, making the two sides asymmetric — [Sylveria (player), 2024-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85752#p85752), [Slicplaya (player), 2025-01-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=117865#p117865). **single-sided claim**, no staff confirmation.

### Durotar Labor Union (Horde, Goblin racial faction)

**Where the reputation comes from (wiki):** quests in **Blackstone Island, Durotar, Stonetalon Mountains and Dustwallow Marsh**; **Vanessa Clang** holds repeatable **engineering-material** quests — [wiki: Durotar Labor Union](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Durotar_Labor_Union). The wiki calls it "the first original faction that was added to Turtle WoW".

**Player-mapped hubs.** Sparkwater Port (Durotar) repeatables, **Powder Town** in Stonetalon Mountains, and — confirmed by staff — **Mudsprocket**: "Mudsprocket had a few faction quests" — [Torta (staff), 2024-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83868#p83868), asked by [Silwarel (player), 2024-01-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83673#p83673). A player adds that Mudsprocket has "**2 infinitely repeatable gathering quests… a total of 300 rep** for completing them both at once", and that a Goblin doing every quest can reach Revered before having to grind — [Snoodydood (player), 2024-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83907#p83907). **single source** for the 300 figure.

**Rocket cars are race-locked.** A Troll at Exalted found he could not buy them — [Freshfunman (player), 2024-02-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88547#p88547) — and players asked for them to be opened to all Horde races while keeping the shredder Goblin-only — [Grizb37 (player), 2024-02-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88804#p88804), [Iridescentmage (player), 2024-10-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=104915#p104915). This **contradicts the wiki**, which says "In return for exalted reputation, **non-goblin players are able to ride rocket cars**" — [wiki: Durotar Labor Union](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Durotar_Labor_Union). **contested**; the player report is dated 2024-02, the wiki page revision 2025-12, so the wiki may describe a later state — no staff post in this extraction settles it. Rocket Cars were "no longer classified as beasts" from 2024-05-29 — [Torta (staff), 2024-05-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95911#p95911).

**Known bug (unresolved in this extraction):** Halloween quests, which grant 150 reputation to every faction of your side, did not grant Durotar Labor Union reputation — [Bargmar (player), 2022-10-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27585#p27585). **single source**, no staff reply.

### Wardens of Time (neutral, Caverns of Time)

Leader **Vairozdormu**, quartermaster **Dronormu** — [wiki: Wardens of Time](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Wardens_of_Time). Official description: Bronze Dragonflight who "accept the aid of any mortal that has earned access to the Caverns of Time" to undo time anomalies — [official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-factions-and-quests).

**Reputation sources, with the diminishing gates (wiki):** repeatable turn-ins give **15 rep** per 1 Corrupted Sand and **175 rep** per 10 Corrupted Sand; bosses give 50/100/50; mobs give 15/5/5/2/5; the Sunnyglade Valley battleground gives 150/10/1. **At Honored, Black Morass trash stops giving reputation; at Revered, Black Morass bosses stop too.**

**The most precise player accounting in this extraction** — [Speedz1337 (guide writer — maintains the pinned custom-quest and Gardening guides), 2023-11-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=78524#p78524):

| Rank band | What still gives rep | Per-run total |
|---|---|---|
| Friendly | Chronar +50, Time-Lord Epochronos +100, Antnormi +50; Infinite Rift-Lord +10; Riftweaver/Riftguard +5; Infinite Whelp +2; 19× Infinite Dragonspawn +5 | **~400 raw rep** |
| Honored | only Chronar +50, Epochronos +100, Antnormi +50 | **200 raw rep** |

Quest names and IDs: *Corrupted Sand* (quest 40340, 15 rep per sand) and *Sand in Bulk* (quest 40341, 175 rep per 10 sand). Stated costs: Honored → Revered needs **520 sand** without run rep; Revered → Exalted needs **1,200 sand**. Sunnyglade Valley gives **+150 per victory**, confirmed independently — [Zvyrhol (player), 2024-10-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102403#p102403). A player calls the early kill-rep cutoff "unnecessary harsh" — [Geojak (player), 2024-10-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102406#p102406); another calls it "one of the easiest rep grinds in the game" — [Phyxius (player), 2024-10-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102507#p102507). **contested** as to difficulty; the numbers themselves are **single source but detailed**.

Rewards are sold by **Dronormu** in the Caverns of Time — [Elisleris (player), 2025-01-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116560#p116560), [Geojak (player), 2023-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54708#p54708). The item list is not reproduced in any extracted post — the players link the item database instead. **Reward list: not found in sources.**

**Balance complaint.** On the fresh realm Ambershire, one player argued the Wardens rep vendor made Black Morass the only worthwhile 60 dungeon: "Wanna get geared → Spam BM → Ignore literally every other high level dungeon… rep vendor makes Scholo loot laughably bad" — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150954#p150954). Others disagreed — [armsgirl (player), 2025-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150956#p150956), [Xudo (player), 2025-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150959#p150959). **contested.**

### Dalaran (Alliance)

Leader Council of Six; quartermaster **Lonum Magicus** — [wiki: Dalaran (faction)](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Dalaran_(faction)).

**Reputation sources (wiki):** repeatable enchanting-material turn-ins — **3× Greater Mystic Essence = 75**, **1× Small Radiant Shard = 100**, **1× Large Brilliant Shard = 200**, **1× Nexus Crystal = 500** — plus roughly **8,125** from one-off quests.

**Player-mapped quests.** "The majority of the Dalaran quests are in **Nethergarde Keep**. There are a few quests in **Azshara** from Magus Bromley, and the Alliance quest chain for **Karazhan Crypts** with Magus Ariden Dusktower also gives Dalaran rep." The same poster reports the intended grind quests at Dalaran in Alterac (Magus Palon, Archmage Talyvanah) were "bugged and aren't repeatable yet" — [Aeliren (player), 2023-02-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36344#p36344). A year later players still asked; "You can't get revered with dalaran yet, no repeatable quest exists" — [Geojak (player), 2023-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54708#p54708), asked by [Greese (player), 2023-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54668#p54668) and [Lipidus (player), 2023-08-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56040#p56040). The **wiki's repeatable-turn-in table therefore describes a later state than these 2023 posts**; **contested**, both dated.

The Blasted Lands *Fel Energy Irregularities I–VII* chain (quest IDs 40438–40444, level 54–60, level requirement 45) is the largest single Dalaran reputation block on the wiki, giving 25 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 300 + **750** and ending in a choice of four **Kirin Tor Shawls** — [wiki: Fel Energy Irregularities I](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Fel_Energy_Irregularities_I), [wiki: Fel Energy Irregularities VII](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Fel_Energy_Irregularities_VII). See `quests-and-questlines.md`.

**Rewards (wiki):** Revered — Boots of the Hermit Magi (7 g 29 s 9 c), Skulker's Gloves (7 g 69 s 26 c), Spellguard's Shield (10 g 53 s 12 c), Pauldrons of Sealed Magics (11 g 29 s 9 c), Girdle of the Warden (7 g 82 s 9 c). Exalted — Dalaran Tabard (5 g), Dalarani Conjurer's Hat (12 g 89 s 22 c), Ring of Flowing Leylines (16 g 59 s 23 c). Timeline note: Dalaran rewards (blues and epics) were added in 1.16.1 with "no new farm".

### Revantusk Trolls (Horde)

Leaders Primal Torntusk / Elder Torntusk; capitals **Amani'Alor** and **Revantusk Village**; theatre Stonetalon Mountains and the Hinterlands — [wiki: Revantusk Trolls](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Revantusk_Trolls). The wiki page has no reputation or reward table.

**Player-mapped grind, three routes** — [Chyk (player), 2023-12-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82964#p82964):

1. repeatable **20 Runecloth → 50 rep** — fastest and most expensive;
2. repeatable quests spread from Ashenvale to Silithus and the Eastern Plaguelands — slow but varied;
3. a repeatable **hippogryph kill quest in the Hinterlands** — "**1.4 g + 150 rep for a 5 minute run**", called the best route if you will not spend hundreds of gold, "but that shit is really boring".

**single source**, answering [Petter600 (player), 2023-12-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82958#p82958). Another player advises doing the dailies while leveling from level 10 to spread the grind — [Oomentaloo (player), 2023-12-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83029#p83029). A dissenting view: "Dont do it. Its not worth it… The raventusk items have no stamina at all" — [Frantsel (player), 2023-12-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83031#p83031). **contested** on whether the rewards justify the grind.

The timeline records the design goal as parity with Silvermoon Remnant: "Friendly in a day, level-10 greens; dailies *Tears of the Poppy* / *The Means to Heal*". Note a 2022 player report that the Amani'Alor reputation NPCs and their building had disappeared after a patch, leaving only the fire quest — [Esefeb (player), 2022-05-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=18896#p18896). **single source, unanswered.**

### Steamwheedle Blood Ring (neutral, PvP)

Official: "A grand arena tournament organized by the Steamwheedle Cartel… great rewards provided by the Arena Masters of the Cartel" — [official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-factions-and-quests). Mechanically it is a **3v3 arena** where reputation is earned "by participating in matches" — [Torta (staff), 2023-10-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71607#p71607). Staff signalled the design in 2021: "Blood Ring is going to be cross-faction and have reputation rewards at some point" — [Torta (staff), 2021-09-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12455#p12455).

Reward track per the timeline: Blood Ring PvP sets (1.16.1), the Exalted title **Blood Ring Champion** plus rings, plus profession recipes gated behind it — **Enchant Gloves – Major Strength**, the **Bloody Belt Buckle** (Blacksmithing) and **Gurubashi Gumbo** (Cooking). Arena quality-of-life changes (pet trinkets disabled, world buffs removed on entry, unlimited mana in the prep phase, soul shards refunded, full health/mana after a match) landed 2023-12 — [Akalix (staff), 2023-12-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80586#p80586). Hardcore characters were no longer forced into the Blood Ring from the 2025-04 build — [Torta (staff), 2025-02-21 (edited; build 7207 live 2025-04-03)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120590#p120590). Detailed PvP handling belongs to the PvP document; only the reputation is covered here.

*Naming caution:* **Blood Ring** is also the name of a South-East Asian PvP realm, so most forum hits are realm chatter — e.g. [Torta (staff), 2025-08-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=146718#p146718).

### Kul Tiras / Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet (Alliance)

Official: "Remnants of Admiral Daelin Proudmoore's Exploratory Fleet, these few survivors that have not been killed by the Horde eke out a dangerous existence" — [official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-factions-and-quests). Leader **Colonel Hardinus** (fleet admiral: Admiral Caelan), base **Caelan's Rest** on Lapidis Isle; the reputation faction was **renamed from "Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet" to "Kul Tiras" in 1.16.1** — [wiki: Kul Tiran Exploratory Fleet](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Kul_Tiran_Exploratory_Fleet).

There is no reward table on the wiki and no forum thread about the grind in this extraction. What is documented is the **quest supply**: nearly every Lapidis Isle quest grants 75–500 Kul Tiras reputation, and the *Tower of Lapidis I–X* chain alone gives 10+50+125+50+125+25+500 — see `quests-and-questlines.md`. Kul Tiras quests also appear in the Barrens (*The Deeds of Sir Danuvis* +300, *The Dragonmaw Conflict* +300, *The Scales of War* +100, *The Power of Aqua* +300), in Stormwind City (*A Sailor's Love* +100, *Charys' Response* +75, *Controlling Sailors* +75) and in Balor (*Ceaseless Storms* +200 and the Hydromancer Finnigan chain) — [wiki: The Deeds of Sir Danuvis](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/The_Deeds_of_Sir_Danuvis), [wiki: Ceaseless Storms](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Ceaseless_Storms), [wiki: A Sailor's Love](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/A_Sailor%27s_Love).

### Kontsuma (neutral, Kamio)

A Turtle-original faction of humans from **Kamio**, a land across the seas, added in 1.17.0 — [wiki: Kontsuma (faction)](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Kontsuma_(faction)). Its representative **Aneka Konko** stands in **Ratchet**. Reputation comes from a small quest set: *Troubles From Distant Lands* (level 13, Barrens, +100, rewards **Music: Winds of Kamio**) and the Tel'Abim chain *Translating The Unknown* → *An Exile's Regret* → *To Heal A Soul* (+25/+25/+250, ending in a choice of **Kabuto of the Senshi** or **Kabuto of the Protector**) — [wiki: Troubles From Distant Lands](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Troubles_From_Distant_Lands), [wiki: To Heal A Soul](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/To_Heal_A_Soul). No reward vendor is documented.

### Draenei Exiles and Earthen Ring (1.18.1)

Both are official 1.18.1 additions — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168):

- **Draenei Exiles [Neutral]** — "Draenei tribes from Outland that survived warp travel; the **Sanv** and the **Moro'gai** tribes now reside on Azeroth, brought by riftwalkers."
- **Earthen Ring [Horde]** — "A group of shamans united for elemental balance, based in the **Stonetalon Mountains**."

Post-release fixes: already-completed quests were made to award Draenei Exiles reputation retroactively, and players can now set **At War** with them; Earthen Ring-aligned NPCs were fixed so Horde players could interact with them; the quest *Glowing Draenethyst Cluster* had its reputation **reduced to 250**; kill reputation for **Timbermaw Hold** was added to Blackroot Furbolgs on Moonwhisper Coast; the quests *The Shadow Guard* and *The Ice Guard* had missing reputation requirements added — [Torta (staff), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608), [Torta (staff), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490), [Torta (staff), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490), [Jamey (staff), 2026-04-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176311#p176311). The retroactivity question had been raised before release — [Aeliren (player), 2025-10-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155010#p155010).

The Swamp of Sorrows / Azshara **Sanv K'la → Akh Z'ador** chain is the documented Draenei Exiles reputation source (+175/+175/+75/+75 per step) — [wiki: The Sanv Charm](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/The_Sanv_Charm), [wiki: Learn Of My Past](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Learn_Of_My_Past). **No reward table exists in any source in this extraction.**

### Argent Dawn (vanilla faction, Turtle changes)

The wiki lists four reputation routes — killing Scourge (non-elites stop halfway through Friendly, except Shadowmages which continue past Honored; elites stop halfway through Honored), **Scourgestones** (Minion's ×20, Invader's ×10, Corruptor's ×1, turned in at the Bulwark, Chillwind Camp or Light's Hope Chapel for reputation and an **Argent Dawn Valor Token**), the **Plague Cauldron** quests at Felstone Field / Dalson's Tears / Writhing Haunt / Gahrron's Withering, and **Healthy Dragon Scales** from Plagued Whelps in Scholomance — [wiki: Argent Dawn](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Argent_Dawn).

Turtle's own changes: **new reputation rewards from Torlath Ironhard in 1.18.0** (wiki), **dungeon reputation for Argent Dawn** added in 1.18.0 (timeline B5), **Glacial/Polar/Icy Scale/Icebane bracer patterns via Argent Dawn** (2024-10-09), **Argent Dawn shoulder enchants gated** (2023-10-05), and a 1.18.1 hotfix adding **missing completion scripts for the WPL cauldron quests** — [Jamey (staff), 2026-04-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176311#p176311).

### Wildhammer Clan (vanilla faction, Turtle content)

The Wildhammers in the **Grim Reaches** [are] not tagged as such, though quests with them still give reputation with the faction" — [wiki: Wildhammer clan](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Wildhammer_clan). The timeline adds **Wildhammer reputation on kill from custom Witherbark trolls (2024-01-03)**. ## 3. Cross-faction reputation mechanics changed by Turtle

From the staff changelog stream, condensed in `synthesis/turtle-vs-vanilla-timeline.md` B5:

- Reputation rewards are **not reduced by character level** (2022-09-11); all reputation gains set to 100% (2023-07-12).
- **Battleground reputation multipliers** (1.16.5); WSG flag capture **108 rep**, AB tick **25 rep** (1.17.0).
- **Dungeon reputation** for Shen'dralar, Argent Dawn, Wardens of Time and Thorium Brotherhood (1.18.0).
- **Wintersaber** training reputation doubled (1.18.0).
- **Gelkis / Magram**: +5 reputation per Maraudine kill (2023-07-05); Desolace centaur clans grant honorary membership (1.16.4).
- **Syndicate** friendly quests (2023-04-26); **Bloodsail** reputation via rum turn-ins (2024-06-04).
- **Darnassus Sentinel** quartermaster added (1.16.1).
- **Shen'dralar**: Ronae'Thalas dailies and Dire Maul reputation — but Ronae'Thalas itself never shipped in this record (see `zones.md` gaps).

## 4. Gaps and unclear points

1. **No reward tables for six custom factions.** Wardens of Time, Steamwheedle Blood Ring, Kul Tiras, Theramore, Revantusk Trolls, Draenei Exiles and Earthen Ring have no item list in any extracted source. Players consistently link `database.turtlecraft.gg` instead of typing the lists out, and that database is not part of this extraction.
2. **No quartermaster locations by coordinate.** The wiki gives a name and a settlement, never an X/Y pair.
3. **Silvermoon Remnant dailies:** removed around 1.17.1, never restored in this record, and staff never answered the repeated requests. Whether the Horde counterpart kept its dailies is a player claim only.
4. **Durotar Labor Union rocket-car access is contested** — a 2024 player report says race-locked, a 2025 wiki revision says open to non-Goblins at Exalted.
5. **Dalaran repeatables are contested** — 2023 players say no repeatable exists and Revered is unreachable; the wiki lists four repeatable turn-ins. No dated staff statement resolves it.
6. **Theramore** appears on the official site and in the wiki faction list but has no article, no reputation description and no forum thread in this extraction.
7. **Reputation totals per rank** are given only as the vanilla-standard thresholds; no source states a custom faction's total requirement except the Wardens of Time sand counts.
8. **The official page's "1,500 quests" figure** is not broken down per faction anywhere.
