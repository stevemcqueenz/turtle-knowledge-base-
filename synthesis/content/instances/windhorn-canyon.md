# Windhorn Canyon (5-player dungeon)

Custom 5-player dungeon in **Thousand Needles**. Added in **patch 1.18.1** (announced 2025-10-03, released 2026-03-20). Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

**Staff description (official):** "New 5-Man Dungeon, level 26-30, in Thousand Needles; **Grimtotem** have expelled the Windhorn Tauren and conquered the canyon." The lore text adds: "The cultures and traditions of many have lived within Windhorn Canyon, which can be seen from the ancient shelters carved into the mountainside, to the relics coveted by the Tauren" — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). A trailer was published on 2025-12-21 — [Torta (staff), 2025-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=163461#p163461).

**Denizens (wiki):** tauren, air elementals, wind serpents, scorpids. The instance has separate exterior and interior maps — [Windhorn Canyon](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Windhorn_Canyon).

### Scripted add spawns (from boss emotes, wiki)

- **Bonespeaker Narlgom** summons **Spirit of Champion Rotag** during the fight ("I have returned…") — [Bonespeaker Narlgom](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Bonespeaker_Narlgom).
- **Chieftain Shalk Blackwind** calls a **Blackwind Bloodguard** at 50 % health ("Chieftain I come to your aid!") — [Chieftain Shalk Blackwind](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Chieftain_Shalk_Blackwind).

### Post-release fix

- "**Ambassador Vortalus** will now properly lose **Engulfing Vortex** if the encounter ends prematurely" (2026-03-31) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490). This is the only Windhorn Canyon hotfix in the extraction and the only source for the Engulfing Vortex ability name.

## 4. Loot themes

- A dungeon set, **Sacred Windhorn** (gloves, shoulders, footwraps, pants, robe, headdress), spread one piece per boss across six of the seven encounters — *(wiki)*, per-boss pages above.
- Three profession recipes: *Plans: Kodoheart Necklace*, *Plans: Ceremonial Belt Buckle*, *Pattern: Grimtotem Bracers* — same.
- The new gear and mounts for 1.18.1 were previewed under "ITEMIZATION — NIGHTMARE OF URSOL GEAR & MOUNTS" — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168).

## 5. Gaps and unclear points

- **No health, damage or duration numbers.**
- **Boss order is inferred from levels**, not documented.

- **Level range** disagrees between staff/wiki (26–30) and the community guide's LFT data (25–33).
- **Quest count** is explicitly unknown on the wiki.
- **NPC and spell IDs**: not found in sources.
