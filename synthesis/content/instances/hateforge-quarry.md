# Hateforge Quarry (5-player dungeon)

Custom 5-player dungeon in the **Burning Steppes**. Added in **patch 1.16.1** (2022-07-24). Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

**Official blurb (patch note):** "Hidden away at the southeastern walls of the **Burning Steppes**, the **Hateforge Quarry** is the Dark Iron dwarves' newest effort to find a new weapon to use against their adversaries. The innocent looking quarry hides an insidious cavern, where the Shadowforge dwarves plot new schemes against all those that oppose them. **Denizens (wiki):** Dark Iron dwarves, leper gnomes (one, Engineer Figgles), trolls, orcs.

## 2. Bosses

Levels are wiki infobox values. Ability and loot lists come from the per-boss wiki pages.

### Corrosis (level 52 elite, water elemental)

No abilities listed. Loot: Sludge-drenched Sabatons, Toxic Ripper, Venomsight Mask, Muckwater Leggings, Corrosive Core — *(wiki)* [Corrosis](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Corrosis).

### Engineer Figgles (level 54 elite, leper gnome)

| Ability | Effect and numbers | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Corrosive Poison** | **657 to 843 Nature damage every 5 sec; armor reduced by 5000; lasts 30 seconds** | *(wiki)* [Engineer Figgles](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Engineer_Figgles) |

This is one of the very few abilities in the whole custom-instance set with published numbers. His aggro line names a companion, "Broody". **Disarm immunity was removed** from him on 2025-06-10 — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135177#p135177).

Loot: The Creator's Goggles, Electroconductive Mitts, Figgle's Coveralls, Wrench of Creation, Techrifle X-TREME 5200, Decoy Dragonling Remote, **Fluorescent Green Mechanostrider** (mount), Fashion Coin. Objective of the level-55 group quest *Hunting Engineer Figgles* — *(wiki)* [Engineer Figgles](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Engineer_Figgles).

### High Foreman Bargul Blackhammer (level 54 elite, Dark Iron dwarf)

No abilities listed; he has idle "work harder" lines that mark him as the overseer of the quarry. **Disarm immunity removed** 2025-06-10 — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135177#p135177).

Loot: Hateforged Cleaver, Visor of Command, Blackhammer Pauldrons, Incendosaur Skin Boots, Taskmaster's Tag, **Manual: Intervene**, Dark Iron Hooked Net, Fashion Coin. Objective of the level-51 group quest *The True High Foreman* — *(wiki)* [High Foreman Bargul Blackhammer](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/High_Foreman_Bargul_Blackhammer).

### Hatereaver Annihilator (level 55 elite, golem)

No abilities listed; his lines are machine-flavoured ("Unidentified intruders, defensive measures, engaged"). Loot: Hatereaver Cog, Flameforged Lugnut, Ragereaver's Helm, Blackstone Stompers, Empowered Power Core, Steamrigged Servohammer, Fashion Coin — *(wiki)* [Hatereaver Annihilator](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Hatereaver_Annihilator).

**Related tuning (official):** "Adjusted procs per minute on **Annihilator**" (2023-10-08) — [Torta (staff), 2023-10-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69525#p69525). (This is the weapon, not the boss, but the two share the name.)

### Har'gesh Doomcaller (level 57 elite, Twilight's Hammer orc, final boss)

**Summons Faceless Terrors** ("The Void hungers for more souls, let it consume you…") — *(wiki)* [Har'gesh Doomcaller](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Har%27gesh_Doomcaller). **Disarm immunity removed** 2025-06-10 — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135177#p135177).

Loot: Toxic Talisman, Call of Shadow, Sacrosanct Epaulets, The Veil of Hatred, Doomcaller Staff, Band of Calamity, Cuffs of Dawnfall, Shawl of End Times, Shadowruned Girdle, Fashion Coin. Objective of *Assaulting Hateforge* (57) and *Of New and Old IV* (57) — *(wiki)* same.

## 3. The Hateforge crafted set

The dungeon drops six blacksmithing plans — **Plans: Hateforge Cuirass, Helmet, Leggings, Belt, Grips, Boots**. The 6-piece **Hateforge Armor** set bonus is **+20 Attack Power, +100 Armor Penetration, +2 % Haste** — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2022-07-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20251#p20251).

A shaman guide recommends the Hateforge set as strong gear "around level 50" for enhancement shamans, either crafted or bought off the auction house — *(player)* [Skegg (player), 2025-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149715#p149715).

## 7. Gaps and unclear points

- **No tactics thread, kill order or positioning advice** exists in the extraction.
- **Four of five bosses have no listed abilities.** Only Engineer Figgles has published numbers (Corrosive Poison).
- **Boss order is not documented** anywhere; the wiki gives no subzone breakdown for this dungeon.
- **Location conflict** (north-eastern vs south-eastern Burning Steppes) between the official website and the official patch note — the patch note and the wiki agree on south-east, so treat the website as the outlier.
- **Level range conflict:** 52–60 (site/wiki) vs a wiki minimum of 48 vs an LFT range of 50–60.
- **Quest count conflict:** 7 named vs 10 claimed.
- **NPC and spell IDs**: not found in sources.
