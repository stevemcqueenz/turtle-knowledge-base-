## 2. Prelate Ironmane (level 22 elite, human ghost)

**Staff description:** "A **spectral priest and preacher of the Light as it was in the Gilnean faith**, haunting the keep's church, seeking peace. **Lay the prelate to rest to purify the keep**" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709), first published in the 1.17.2 preliminary notes [Torta (staff), 2023-12-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82980#p82980) and on [turtlecraft.gg](https://turtlecraft.gg/classic-dungeons-update).

| Abilities (wiki) | Loot (wiki) |
|---|---|
| **Smite**, **Curse of Agony**, **Drain Mana** | Mitre of the First Light, Prelate's Sigil, Hilt of Radiance, Signet of Faded Sermons |

Aggro: "What, you come for confession?"; death: "Free… At last…". He is the objective of the level-22 dungeon quest **Too Late to Prelate** (quest 41366) — *(wiki)* [Prelate Ironmane](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Prelate_Ironmane); quest ID from *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2022-11-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29743#p29743).

**Categorisation fix (official):** "**Prelate Ironmane** and **Spectral Clerics** are now properly categorized as **Undead**" (2025-03-04) — [Torta (staff), 2025-03-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121678#p121678). Before this, undead-specific abilities (Shackle Undead, Exorcism, Turn Undead, holy damage bonuses) would not have worked on the encounter.

The "**Spectral Clerics**" named in that fix are the church trash pack accompanying him — no other source describes them.

## 3. Other Turtle content in Shadowfang Keep

The community dungeon-quest guide lists six custom quests tied to Shadowfang Keep — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2022-11-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29743#p29743):

| Quest | ID | Faction |
|---|---|---|
| The Missing Sorcerer | 60109 | A |
| Into The Jaws | 40281 | H |
| Arugal's Folly | 60108 | A — "the counterpart to the official Horde quest" |
| Too Late to Prelate | 41366 | H |
| **Blood of Vorgendor** | 41378 | A — level 60 **Scythe of Elune** legendary questline |
| **The Wolf, the Crone and the Scythe** | 41381 | H — level 60 Scythe of Elune legendary questline |

The last two connect Shadowfang Keep to the **Scythe of Elune** legendary chain that begins with a drop in Lower Karazhan Halls (see [lower-karazhan-halls.md](lower-karazhan-halls.md)).
