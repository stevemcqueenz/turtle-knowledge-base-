# Gilneas City (5-player dungeon)

Custom 5-player dungeon in the **Gilneas** zone. Added in **patch 1.17.0** (announced 2023-01-21, released 2023-10-03). Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

**Staff description (official):** "Gilneas City is a new level 43-49 dungeon located in its namesake zone, featuring sprawling streets and homes now in disrepair, while the towers that reach for the skies cast their shadows over you as you make your way through the streets to the heart of the city. The dungeon includes **two mini-bosses and five normal bosses**, with plenty of new loot to acquire as you liberate the city" — [Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868).

**Denizens (wiki):** worgen, humans, bats, worgs. The city is contested between Greymane loyalists keeping the districts on lockdown and the **Duskpelt** worgen pack under Packmaster Ragetooth — [Gilneas City](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Gilneas_City).

Wiki pages: [Genn Greymane (tactics)](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Genn_Greymane_(tactics)), [Regent-Lords of Gilneas](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Regent-Lords_of_Gilneas), [Marshal Magnus Greystone](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Marshal_Magnus_Greystone), [Horsemaster Levvin](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Horsemaster_Levvin), [Dustivan Blackcowl](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Dustivan_Blackcowl), [Judge Sutherland](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Judge_Sutherland), [Packmaster Ragetooth](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Packmaster_Ragetooth), [Matthias Holtz](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Matthias_Holtz).

**The Regent-Lords fight is a two-target encounter** with a shared script — their aggro and death emotes address each other, and Mortimer's dying line is "Father…" — *(wiki)* [Regent-Lords of Gilneas](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Regent-Lords_of_Gilneas). The lore twist is that the Harlows, not Genn, ran the tyranny, and Genn is believed by the rebels to have been slain by them — *(wiki)* [Gilneas City](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Gilneas_City). Genn's own emotes ("I did what was necessary", "It was pointless after all") make him a tragic final boss rather than a straightforward villain.

**Genn Greymane's page is titled "(tactics)" on the wiki but contains only an ability list — no tactics text.**

## 3. Loot highlights

From the wiki boss pages: **Wolfblood** and **Mark of the Worgen**, **Greymane Shoulders**, *Formula: Enchant Bracer — Vampirism*, and the **Gilnean Raven** pet (Genn Greymane); the **Greymane Charger** mount (Horsemaster Levvin); the **Greymane Tabard** and Marshal's Blocker (Magnus Greystone); Gavel of Gilnean Justice and The Black Pendant (Judge Sutherland); Hookblade Cleaver and Cloak of the Dark Veil (Dustivan Blackcowl); Orb of Aka'thar (Packmaster Ragetooth); Worgen Hunter Musket (Matthias Holtz).

- Missing skinning loot assigned to Packmaster Ragetooth and both Harlows (2025-06-10) — [Torta (staff), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135177#p135177).
