# Stormwrought Ruins (5-player dungeon)

Custom 5-player dungeon inside **Stormwrought Castle** on the island of **Balor**. Added in **patch 1.18.0** (announced 2025-02-22, released 2025-08-15). Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest and attunement guides; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

## 2. Layout

**Two halves (wiki):** "Due to its length, the dungeon is separated into two parts: **Stormwrought Castle**, which comprises the first five bosses, and **Stormwrought Descent**, which comprises the last seven bosses" — [Stormwrought Ruins](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Stormwrought_Ruins). Staff use the same split when listing trash loot ("the **Castle** section" and "the **Caverns** section") — [Torta (staff), 2025-09-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149960#p149960).

- **Stormwrought Castle:** Stormwrought Barracks, Court, Kitchen, Library, Museum, Prison.
- **Stormwrought Descent:** Crystal Grotto, Deathlord's Sepulcher, Fetid Lake, Rotting Caverns, Sanctum of Blood (containing the Temple of Shadows), Stormwrought Terrace.

### Ighal'for → Mergothid (the final encounter)

**Ighal'for summons Mergothid.** The wiki's Ighal'for page simply points at Mergothid's ability list — [Ighal'for](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Ighal%27for). Staff fixes confirm the two-stage structure: "**Ighal'for will not die prematurely before summoning Mergothid**" (2025-08-19) and "Fixed the Ighal'for encounter not resetting properly after a wipe" (2025-08-26) — [Jamey (staff), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147116#p147116), [Torta (staff), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148454#p148454).

**Mergothid's abilities (wiki):** Flamestrike, Immolate, Curse of Agony, Death Coil, **Bloodstone Ritual**, **Spawn Rifttorn Nether Imps**, **Curse of Mergothid**, **Binding of Mergothid** — [Mergothid](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Mergothid). This is the longest ability list of any custom 5-man boss in the extraction. Loot includes **Eyes of the Beholder** and **Spine of Dentarg**.

- **Which five bosses count as the "Castle" five** is not stated — Chieftain Stormsong's infobox says "Stormwrought Descent" while Dagar and Oronok sit in Castle subzones, so the split above is partly inferred. **Low confidence.**
- **No health or damage numbers**; only Dagar the Glutton's Tendon Rip slow (58 %) is quantified.
- **Boss count** (12 wiki, 13 NPC pages including Ighal'for) is ambiguous because Ighal'for and Mergothid are one encounter.
- **What the Key to Stormwrought Castle actually unlocks** is described only as "shortcuts and access to more bosses" — which bosses is not stated.
- **Level range** disagrees across three sources.
- **NPC and spell IDs**: not found in sources.
