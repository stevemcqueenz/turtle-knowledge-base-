# Karazhan Crypt (5-player dungeon)

Custom level 58–60 5-player dungeon in **Deadwind Pass**, beneath the Karazhan tower. Added in **patch 1.16.0**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest and attunement guides; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

**Official blurb:** "Something is twisting the dead back to life in the forlorn catacombs, find the source so the dead may rest again" — [turtlecraft.gg, Raids and Dungeons](https://turtlecraft.gg/raids-and-dungeons).

### The key questline

Two parallel seven-step chains — *(wiki)* [Karazhan Crypt](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Karazhan_Crypt):

- **Alliance:** *The Mystery of Karazhan I–VII*, ending at **Ariden's Camp**, Deadwind Pass (final step quest ID 40317).
- **Horde:** *The Depths of Karazhan I–VII*, ending at **Stonard**, Swamp of Sorrows (final step quest ID 40310).
- **Neutral:** *No Honor Among Chefs* (41374), given in Lower Karazhan Halls.

Quest IDs from *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2022-11-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29757#p29757); chain start IDs 40311 (Alliance) / 40304 (Horde) from [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120619#p120619).

**Gate change (official, 1.18.0):** "**Karazhan Crypt's gate** and **Arch Hydromancer Lapidis' Magically Sealed Door** now function like normal doors — displaying 'Locked' status and the required key on hover. The former can now be opened with **Lockpicking**" — [Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615).

**Trinket reward inequality (player, contested, no staff answer):** the Alliance version of the chain's trinket reward gives +1 spell hit, +1 spell haste and 15 spell power, while the Horde version is worse; a second player says the Alliance jewellery options are also better (15 Strength/Intellect + 20 Arcane Resist vs 12 / 15) — [GR3ddy (player), 2025-02-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119888#p119888), [FrankFankledank (player), 2025-02-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119889#p119889).

## 2. Layout and bosses

**No wiki boss pages exist** for Karazhan Crypt in this extraction, and no boss list was ever published by staff. The names below are every Karazhan Crypt boss named in any source; **the order is only partly known**.

| Boss | Position | What sources say |
|---|---|---|
| **Marrowspike** | **First boss** | Drops 6 items — five at 18 % and one at 10 % drop chance, totalling 100 % — *(player)* [Lanimil (player), 2022-12-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31595#p31595) |
| **Corpsemuncher** | After the underwater section | Reached by "leaving the water section"; the approach has 4 skeletal guards and 1 frost spectre — *(player)* [Haroone (player), 2021-12-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14969#p14969) |
| **Commander Andreon** | — | Promoted from a normal mob to a **proper boss** in 2023-01-29, "with new abilities, health and damage values and now features 6 new item drops" — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2023-01-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33498#p33498); the loot arrived 2023-01-31 — [Torta (staff), 2023-01-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33639#p33639) |
| **Archlich Enkhraz** | — | Named alongside Alarus as a boss players wanted to drop 2 items instead of 1 — *(player)* [Apimius (player), 2022-08-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20858#p20858) |
| **Alarus** | **Final boss** | Drops the **Staff of Alarus**. "**Alarus will no longer respawn after being killed**" (2025-05-20) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132195#p132195); staff sheathing fix [Torta (staff), 2024-12-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111130#p111130) |

A **Guard Captain** exists as a unique non-boss elite with no loot table, which a player asked to have fixed — *(player)* [Haroone (player), 2021-12-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14969#p14969). Whether that changed is not recorded.

**No ability names or numbers are recorded for any Karazhan Crypt boss anywhere in the extraction.**

### Layout, in the words of a player who ran it at release

The clearest description of the dungeon's shape is a 2021 feedback post — [Haroone (player), 2021-12-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14969#p14969):

- **Entrance** with bats, then a **big drop** into a skeleton room (no way to climb back up).
- A long **underwater section**, part of which grants water breathing (which the poster found inconsistent with the rest).
- **Exit from the water** guarded by 4 skeletal guards + 1 frost spectre, leading to **Corpsemuncher**. Groups were "abusing the swimming aggro reset" here; mana users cannot drink without water walking.
- A **main corridor** after the water with scattered skeletons and zombies; the poster calls the pack placement unstructured and says hugging the wall edge to skip rooms "feels like it's cheating almost".
- **Later rooms** hold the remaining bosses.
- A room with a **great hole** has a heartbeat ambience — *(player)* [Raukodor (player), 2021-09-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=12310#p12310).

The same post's complaints — short respawn timers on the entrance bats and the skeletons after the drop, and the graveyard sending players to **Raven Hill Cemetery in Duskwood** instead of Deadwind Pass — were both later addressed by staff: "Karazhan Crypt should no longer bug out and send the player to Redridge Mountains on death" (2023-12-19) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2023-12-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81778#p81778). (Note the staff note names Redridge, the player named Duskwood; **the two reports may be different bugs**.)

## 3. Difficulty and tuning

- **Health nerf (official):** "Karazhan Crypt creatures and some Stormwind Vault creatures **has been nerfed by 5–10 % in health values** to provide a better dungeon delving experience. We've also touched on most item drops found in the Karazhan Crypt to make these drops more enticing and more relevant to the general dungeon diver" — [Torta (staff), 2023-01-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33498#p33498).
- **Player difficulty reports (contested):** "most high-level group content, including the custom instances Karazhan Crypt and Black Morass (2.0) **can be trio'd by people wearing blue-grade gear**" — [Bob022 (player), 2023-11-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76719#p76719); the same player reports a "nice Karazhan Crypt trip" with a party of three — [Bob022 (player), 2023-05-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47628#p47628). Against that, the 2021 feedback post calls the non-boss encounters and traversal "a slog" while praising the boss fights ([Haroone (player), 2021-12-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14969#p14969)), and a later player says "the actual Crypt part is a bit monotonous" and that the underwater section "is not really challenging, it is rather annoying" — [Akos1896 (player), 2024-02-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=88012#p88012).

## 4. Loot

- **BoE rare drop rate (official):** raised **from 0.0031 %–0.01 % to 0.02 %** across Black Morass, Stormwind Vault and Karazhan Crypt (2024-07-03) — [Jamey (staff), 2024-07-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98410#p98410).
- **Epic shoulders** (item 83464) were reported at **0.25 %** while Black Morass epics were at 1 %, and a player asked for parity — *(player)* [Apimius (player), 2022-08-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20858#p20858). Whether that specific rate changed is not recorded.
- Named items: **Staff of Alarus**; **Battle Bishop's Robes** (cloth with melee/tank stats) — *(player)* [Bartabus (player), 2024-05-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95731#p95731); a **+defence cloth head** item — *(player)* [Akos1896 (player), 2024-05-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95753#p95753); the **"Kara5" neck**, called a good long-term neck for resto shamans — *(player)* [Akos1896 (player), 2024-04-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92081#p92081).
- **Removed set (player complaint, no staff answer):** a Sir Zeliek-style armor set that used to be obtainable in Karazhan Crypt is reported as having been removed, with two parts remaining — [Havenard (player), 2024-12-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114873#p114873).
- Commander Andreon's six new item drops (2023-01-31) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2023-01-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33639#p33639).
- Textures for **Rotten Zombie** and **Risen Crypt Guard** updated 2025-02-21 — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-02-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120590#p120590).

## 5. Gaps and unclear points

- **No boss list, no boss order, no abilities and no numbers.** Karazhan Crypt is the worst-documented custom dungeon in this extraction relative to its age: four boss names are recoverable out of a stated seven.
- **The three unnamed bosses** are not identified in any source.
- **No wiki boss pages exist** for this dungeon.
- **The graveyard bug** has two incompatible descriptions (Duskwood by a player in 2021, Redridge by staff in 2023).
- **Whether the epic shoulder drop rate was ever raised** is not recorded.
- **NPC and spell IDs**: not found in sources.
- **Client-side crashes with the community HD patch:** several players report the game crashing at the same point near the start of Karazhan Crypt while using the Turtle HD Patch-A, "when the mobs used the Death…" — [Arkanae (player), 2025-04-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127927#p127927), [NeyMey (player), 2025-04-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=127940#p127940), [Araphel (player), 2025-05-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129644#p129644). This is a **third-party texture patch**, not official Turtle content, and the underlying spell is never named.
