# Razorfen Downs (vanilla dungeon, extended by Turtle in 1.18.0)

Vanilla 5-player dungeon in the Barrens. Turtle added a new optional area, the **Temple of Agamaggan**, with one new boss, in **patch 1.18.0**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

## 2. Death Prophet Rakameg (level 39 elite, quilboar, Temple of Agamaggan)

| Abilities (wiki) | Loot (wiki) |
|---|---|
| **Shadow Burst**, **Consuming Shadows** | Thornspine, Thorngorged Fleshgloves, **Totem of the Rotten Roots** (shaman relic), Blood-etched Fetish |

Aggro: "**Agamaggan shall be reborn!**"; death: "No! No! I cannot die!" — *(wiki)* [Death Prophet Rakameg](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Prophet_Rakameg).

The encounter's framing — a quilboar death prophet trying to resurrect Agamaggan, allied with the Scourge — extends the vanilla Amnennar/Death's Head storyline rather than replacing it.
