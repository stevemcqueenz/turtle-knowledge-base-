# Blackwing Lair (vanilla raid, extended by Turtle in 1.18.1)

Vanilla 40-player raid in Blackrock Mountain. Turtle **added a new mechanic (or changed one) on every boss except Chromaggus**, added a new optional boss and section, and added a shortcut past the Suppression Room — all in **patch 1.18.1 (released 2026-03-20)**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post; *(player)* = non-staff forum post; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

This file covers **Turtle's changes only**.

## 2. The 1.18.1 extension

### Ezzel Darkbrewer (new optional boss)

**Staff description (official):** "Ezzel Darkbrewer might be a goblin of small stature but he is by no means a pushover. With an arsenal of chemical concoctions and a brilliant mind sharpened by many years in the field, he's ready to thwart any intruders stepping into **his employer's laboratory**. Oh, and don't forget the **lumbering ogre he's atop of**" — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168).

## 3. The Firemaw void-zone bug (open, contested)

**Staff fix attempt:** "**Void Zones should no longer chase players**" — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608). The note does not name a boss.

**Player reports, after that fix:**

- "Firemaw is spawning void zones behind LoS, inside walls, in the air (so impossible to soak), under hay stacks = 100 % objectively invisible, some are 80+ yards away (4 Horsemen is easier fight cuz it's at least consistent)" — [Travoltino (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173564#p173564).
- A week later: "Firemaw spawns invisible void zones (**not LoS — simply invisible**)" — [Travoltino (player), 2026-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174380#p174380).

**No staff reply appears in the extraction**, and no further void-zone fix is listed in the hotfixes after 2026-03-21. Void Zones are also named by hunters as one of the mechanics that kill pets regardless of Avoidance — [Skurridizo (player), 2026-03-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170469#p170469). **Treat as an open bug at the end of the extraction window.**

## 5. Tuning and player reception

| Date | Change | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-02-22 (1.18.0) | Health and damage adjusted for all bosses and creatures in MC, Karazhan (10), Onyxia's Lair and **Blackwing Lair** | *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615) |

**Player verdict after 1.18.1 (single reporter, repeated, no staff reply):** BWL is "WAY overtuned… for the gear that drops there", and the Firemaw void zones make the fight "cointoss RNG or pure bruteforce with +3 raids above mandatory gear" — [Travoltino (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173564#p173564), [Travoltino (player), 2026-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174380#p174380).

**Nefarian class call (player, contested against no staff explanation):** two players in the 1.18.1 release thread say the hunter class call was made "more expensive for hunters" and dislike it — [Suspicious (player), 2026-03-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171720#p171720), [Lane13 (player), 2026-03-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171722#p171722). A hunter thread separately notes pets attack Nefarian and the BWL drakes from minimum instead of maximum range, which combines badly with Nefarian's fears — [Lane13 (player), 2026-03-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170490#p170490).

A pre-1.18.1 player raid guide for Blackwing Lair exists on the forum but predates every change above (2020) — [Gifted's All-in-One Blackwing Lair Raiding Guide, thread t940](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=940).

## 6. Gaps and unclear points

- **The per-boss "new or updated mechanic" list for 1.18.1 was never published.** Only Firemaw's void zones and Nefarian's class-call change are identifiable, and both only from player posts.
- **Ezzel Darkbrewer has no numbers** — no health, no ability values, no phase thresholds beyond the 10 % emote.
- **Where the new laboratory section sits** in the BWL layout is not described.
- **Whether the Firemaw void-zone bug was ever fixed** is unknown; the last word in the extraction is a player report that it was still broken on 2026-04-07.
- **Lockout** for Blackwing Lair on Turtle: not stated in the sources.
