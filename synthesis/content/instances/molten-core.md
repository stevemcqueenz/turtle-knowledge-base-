# Molten Core (vanilla raid, extended and retuned by Turtle)

Vanilla 40-player raid in Blackrock Mountain, **extended with two new areas and three new bosses in patch 1.18.0** and re-tuned across 2025. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post; *(player)* = non-staff forum post; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official; *(vanilla baseline, not from turtlecraft.gg)* = general vanilla knowledge used only as context.

This file covers **Turtle's changes**. The vanilla boss list and vanilla mechanics are not restated except where Turtle changed them.

## 2. The 1.18.0 extension

**Incindis' Lair** — "the hatching grounds of its namesake incendosaur". **Old Shadowforge** — "the first hearth of the Dark Iron Dwarves inside the Blackrock Mountain at its farthest depths, now bereft of life and filled with echoes of a forgotten past." Together they add "3 new bosses and 15+ new items" — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615).

### Incindis (replaces Gehennas)

Gehennas "has retired and returned to the Firelands to live a peaceful life on a ranch with his grandchildren" and was **removed from Molten Core**. Consequences (all official, [Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615)):

- Tier gear previously dropped by Gehennas now drops from **Incindis**.
- The rune associated with Gehennas now requires **Incindis** to be killed before it becomes dousable — i.e. Incindis is now part of the Majordomo rune-dousing sequence.
- The quest **Hands of the Enemy** no longer requires *Hand of Gehennas*.

Later fixes: gold added to Incindis ([Jamey (staff), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147116#p147116)); "Incindis will no longer twitch when hit during combat" ([Torta (staff), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148454#p148454)).

### Smoldaris and Basalthar

- Both could get stuck "in a half-dead state where they could still attack" — fixed 2025-08-15 — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-08-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145822#p145822).
- Gold dropped by both was reduced 2025-08-19 — *(official)* [Jamey (staff), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147116#p147116).
- No mechanics are described in any source. **Gap.**

### Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan (Old Shadowforge)

Fades from players when Thaurissan dies (2025-08-26) | *(official)* [Jamey (staff), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147116#p147116), [Torta (staff), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148454#p148454) |

He is also a quest objective: **Lock Blueprints: Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan** was reclassified as a *raid* quest on 2026-04-07 — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2026-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174367#p174367).

### Other additions

## 4. Golemagg: the flashpoint

**What changed (reconstructed from player reports and staff fixes).** The Core Hounds gained a **fixate** phase: "When boss hit 60 or 50 % hp dogs go 'enrage' and fixate on random people, threat doesn't matter" — [Alovander (player), 2025-08-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148274#p148274). Golemagg's own debuff was changed to **stack fire damage and reduce armor at the same time** — described by a player (Cheruscan) in a post that staff quoted approvingly when replying — [Lhian (staff), 2025-08-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148662#p148662). Golemagg also had his enrage timing changed and restored: "**Golemagg will once again enrage at 10 % health**" (2025-09-02) — [Torta (staff), 2025-09-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149960#p149960).

**The tanking argument (player, contested):**

- The hounds *are* tauntable off their fixated target; the working strategy is 1–2 tanks in phase 1 plus 2 pre-assigned extra tanks/warriors/druids for phase 2 — [Drubarrymooer (player), 2025-08-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148275#p148275), who later reports "we have been doing it with 3–4 tanks since the change with no problem" ([2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148375#p148375)).
- Counter-argument: counting the boss tank and the tank swap from debuff stacks, that is 5–6 tanks in a raid that may only be 20 people — [Crake (player), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148302#p148302), [Zmarotrix (player), 2025-08-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148757#p148757) ("an absurd tanking requirement of at least 6 tanks… a requirement that belongs in Naxxramas, not Molten Core").
- Middle position: you need bodies with a taunt button and some bulk, not six tank *specs* — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148306#p148306).
- The raid leader who opened the thread describes the add threat as unreadable: "one second they suddenly have 10k threat on a mage and they drop all other threat. Another second they completely threat reset to 0" and complains the hounds' slow debuff cannot be removed by Hand of Freedom — [Blackfox (player), 2025-08-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148264#p148264); he adds that the fight also became a soft DPS check because the dogs activate at 50 % and Golemagg's Earthquake at ~10 % pushes melee out ([2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148329#p148329)).

**Staff response (official):** "Golemagg is the only problematic encounter atm… **We'll look at Golemagg this week**" — [Lhian (staff), 2025-08-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148662#p148662). The follow-through, one week later: health −25 % (2025-08-26), enrage restored to 10 %, and "**Golemagg's Core Hounds now move 25 % slower**" (2025-09-02) — [Torta (staff), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148454#p148454), [Torta (staff), 2025-09-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149960#p149960). An earlier fix had already made the core ragers expire with Golemagg's death ([Torta (staff), 2024-05-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95911#p95911)); bleed immunity was removed 2025-06-10 ([Torta (staff), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=135177#p135177)).

## 5. The rework debate (staff intent vs. players)

**Staff intent (official), in full** — [Torta (staff), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148454#p148454):

> "With Patch 1.18.0, we've boosted the overall power of creatures in the open world, dungeons, and raids. This was long overdue: over time, class updates steadily made players stronger… For too long, **Molten Core has been almost a free loot source** — understandable on a progressed server like Nordanaar, but it was too easy for new guilds or players, which made preparation and coordination feel less important. Sure, the gear was nice, but it didn't teach the fundamentals of raiding, causing issues in later content. However, while open world and dungeon tuning has been going as expected, **Molten Core has sparked a lot of discussion**. This week, we're altering numbers of some boss fights while keeping the core challenge intact."

The same post ends with: "Looking ahead, we're also exploring the idea of **hard modes for existing instances**, since there's clearly a lot of interest from the community."

**Player positions (contested):**

- *Against the rework:* the longest argument in the extraction holds that MC "should be clearable by a dedicated group in dungeon blues", that the rework is "the single biggest deviation from the vanilla spirit this server has ever made", and that adding a wing is Vanilla+ while rewriting the original encounters is not — [Zmarotrix (player), 2025-08-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148757#p148757). Supported by [Rashun (player), 2025-08-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148764#p148764) (argues it should have shipped only on the fresh realm Ambershire, not on progressed Nordanaar), [Blackfox (player), 2025-08-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148941#p148941) (agrees with every boss critique except Geddon), and a new healer who says she is "severely disappointed" she will never see pre-patch MC and now hears about "new players wiping constantly" — [Aralise (player), 2025-08-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148768#p148768).
- *For the rework:* "original MC is a joke… after people had figured out the bare bones mechanics it was just getting farmed… the way of overtuning it and then adjusting it down is better, it makes people actually think" — [Cheruscan (player), 2025-08-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148899#p148899). And: "No one pugged MC before the class change patches… Easy mode MC legendaries for every noob was wrong" — [Terrydavis (player), 2025-08-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148945#p148945).
- *Loot-vs-effort:* "All that work for a T1 chest with an unhealthy amount of dump stats or perhaps a mediocre piece of spellpower gear you can upgrade in ZG if you're lucky" — [FrankFankledank (player), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148304#p148304).

**Both sides agree on one point:** the new wing and its loot are good. "The new wing, new bosses, and the loot they provide are fantastic additions… The problem isn't the new content, but the revision of the old" — [Zmarotrix (player), 2025-08-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148757#p148757).

## 7. Gaps and unclear points

- **No published per-boss mechanics changelog for the 1.18.0 rework.** The only detailed description of what changed on Magmadar, Garr, Baron Geddon, Golemagg and Majordomo is a single (well-argued) player post; staff confirmed the *existence* of the changes and their intent but never listed them.
- **Smoldaris and Basalthar have no documented mechanics at all** — only bug fixes and gold adjustments.
- **Incindis' mechanics are undocumented**; only his role in the rune/attunement chain and his tier loot inheritance are recorded.
- **Boss count for the extension** (3 vs 4 names) is unresolved.
- **No health or damage values**, only percentage adjustments relative to unknown baselines.
- **Whether the promised "hard modes for existing instances" ever shipped** for Molten Core: not found in sources.
- **Layout of Incindis' Lair and Old Shadowforge** relative to the vanilla wings: not found in sources.
