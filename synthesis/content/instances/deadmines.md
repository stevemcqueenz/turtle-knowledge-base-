# The Deadmines (vanilla dungeon, extended by Turtle in 1.17.2)

Vanilla 5-player dungeon in Westfall. Turtle added **two new optional areas with one new boss each** in **patch 1.17.2** and adjusted Rhahk'zor's loot. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

**Design principle (official):** "All new locations and encounters are **optional**, providing flexibility for players… This aims at **horizontal progression**, introducing fresh challenges without changing the fundamental dungeon experience too much" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709); [turtlecraft.gg, Classic Dungeons Update](https://turtlecraft.gg/classic-dungeons-update).

## 2. New bosses

### Jared Voss (level 19 elite, Zanzil Laboratory)

**Staff description:** "Oversees a dangerous lab producing **Zanzil's Mixture**. Located in the mine section **before Rhah'nkzor**, presenting a **new first boss encounter** for adventurers who choose the optional route" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709).

| Abilities (wiki) | Loot (wiki) |
|---|---|
| Poison, **Poisonous Stab**, **Poison Cloud**, **Withering Poison** | Chemist's Shawl, Corrosive Vial, Mixologist; **Defias Longbow** added 2026-01-27 |

He drops **Voss' Sizzling Brew** for the quest *Turning Off The Tap* — *(wiki)* [Jared Voss](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Jared_Voss). That quest was recategorised to The Deadmines on 2024-12-10 — *(official)* [Reynolds (staff), 2024-12-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112126#p112126). The Defias Longbow was added to his table 2026-01-27 — *(official)* [Shadowsong (staff), 2026-01-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166661#p166661).

**Name conflict — contested.** The staff patch post spells him "**Jared Vess**" ([Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709), and identically on [turtlecraft.gg](https://turtlecraft.gg/classic-dungeons-update)); the later staff hotfix and the wiki both spell him "**Jared Voss**" ([Shadowsong (staff), 2026-01-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166661#p166661), [wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Jared_Voss)). Treat **Voss** as the live in-game spelling.

His aggro line — "What?! You're **not the ones from Stranglethorn**!" — ties the lab to Zanzil, and his death line is "I was going to make a fortune on this work!"

### Masterpiece Harvester (level 20 elite, Harvest Golem Foundry)

**Staff description:** "A relic from Deadmines' past has laid dormant for long in the **Goblin Factory**, awaiting activation to wreak havoc at the world above" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709). The 1.17.2 preliminary notes said "Goblin **Foundry**" — [Torta (staff), 2023-12-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82980#p82980); the wiki's subzone is the **Harvest Golem Foundry**.

No abilities listed on the wiki. Loot: Craftsman's Pants, Inventor's Mitts, Slag Slugger. He is the objective of the level-19 dungeon quest **The Harvest Golem Mystery** — *(wiki)* [Masterpiece Harvester](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Masterpiece_Harvester).

A player recalls "the Harvest Golem Mystery" as one of the questlines that made the world feel expanded — [Armordillo (player), 2026-04-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175765#p175765).

## 3. Loot changes to existing bosses

**Rhahk'zor (official):** "Rhahk'zor's loot pool has been **adjusted for equal drop chances**, with notable upgrades including his **hammer to Uncommon with armor**, **Rockbiter to a Rare two-handed axe with armor penetration** and a **new pair of Rare mail gauntlets**" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709).

**Edwin VanCleef** is unchanged by Turtle as far as the sources show; his wiki page is flagged `is_turtle_content: false` — [Edwin VanCleef (tactics)](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Edwin_VanCleef_(tactics)).
