# Stormwind Vault (5-player dungeon)

Custom level-60 5-player dungeon underneath **Stormwind City**. Added in **patch 1.16.0**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

### Boss tuning history (official)

- **Arc'tiras' Frost Nova pulse at low health changed from every 4 seconds to every 6 seconds** (2022-08-16) — [Torta (staff), 2022-08-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22582#p22582). Later: "Fixed **Arctiras** using Frost Nova too often" (2025-10-14) — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155594#p155594).
- Arc'tiras' adds aggroing incorrectly, fixed 2022-12-26 — [Torta (staff), 2022-12-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31601#p31601).
- The **door to Arc'tiras now closes once he is engaged** (2023-09-12) — [Torta (staff), 2023-09-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=63377#p63377).
- **Damian's unavoidable instakill:** first softened — "Damian will now inflict damage with a spell instead of instantly killing the highest threat player at low health" (2024-01-23) — then removed entirely: "Removed unavoidable instakill mechanic from **Damian the Ripper**" (2024-12-03) — [Torta (staff), 2024-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85725#p85725), [Torta (staff), 2024-12-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111130#p111130).
- **Creature health nerfed 5–10 %** on "some Stormwind Vault creatures" alongside the Karazhan Crypt pass (2023-01-29) — [Torta (staff), 2023-01-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33498#p33498).

### The Rat Trap

A scripted trap in the dungeon with its own fix trail: issues fixed 2022-09-01; no longer gets stuck if a player enters and leaves too fast (2022-09-27); and "**The Rat Trap in Stormwind Vaults is no longer avoidable once triggered**" (2023-03-08) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2022-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24112#p24112), [Torta (staff), 2022-09-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=26292#p26292), [Jamey (staff), 2023-03-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37124#p37124).

## 6. Gaps and unclear points

- **No player tactics thread** exists for Stormwind Vault in the extraction.
- **Boss loot tables are empty on the wiki pages** in this extraction — only names and quotes survive.
- **Four of six bosses have no listed abilities**; Tham'Grarr's only listed ability is Ice Block, and Arc'tiras' mechanics are known only from bug fixes.
- **Boss order is not documented anywhere** — the subzone assignments above are the only positional information.
- **The secret sewer entrance location** is deliberately not published by staff and is not described by any player post in the extraction.
- **Patch-introduced conflict:** the internal timeline notes that the wiki elsewhere claims 1.15.0 for this dungeon; the staff changelog and the wiki's own patch-changes section both say 1.16.0.
- **Quest count** (4 on the wiki vs 6 in the community guide) is unresolved.
