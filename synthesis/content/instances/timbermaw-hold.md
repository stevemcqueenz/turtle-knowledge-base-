# Timbermaw Hold (20-player raid)

Custom 20-player raid beneath Mount Hyjal, entered from **Azshara**. Added in **patch 1.18.1 (Nightmares of Ursol)**, live from the patch release on **2026-03-20** (a few days later on the older realms). Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post; *(player)* = non-staff forum post; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

> This is the newest instance in the extraction and the thinnest-documented. It opened roughly eight weeks before the announced server shutdown (2026-05-15), so almost no player guides or tactics threads exist. Everything mechanical below comes from staff hotfix notes.

**Lore (official):** "As ancient as Kalimdor itself, this enigmatic labyrinthine network of tunnels and caves beneath Mount Hyjal has been the home of the Furbolgs since well before the Sundering. Its halls are sacred among the tribes, a place of worship to their progenitors, the twin gods Ursoc and Ursol. Nowadays however, only wafts of putrid vapors escape the rotten caverns and whispers of venerating a foul god echo throughout Timbermaw Hold" — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). The wiki reproduces the same text.

**Release schedule (official):** "On the older realms, TMH will open a few days after the patch rollout to give us time to fix the post-launch crashes and critical bugs, as usual" — [Torta (staff), 2026-03-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169695#p169695).

### Entry requirement — contested

A player states the raid "will have a requirement to be at least **neutral with the Timbermaw faction** in order to just enter", and raises the case of rogues who farmed Pet Rock from Azshara furbolgs years earlier and are now *hated*, facing a grind as long as reaching Revered — [Carlculathor (player), 2026-03-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172477#p172477). Another player disputes it: "Where is it written you need at least neutral with Timbermaw to enter? You can still sneak or get summoned right in front of it afaik" — [Travoltino (player), 2026-03-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172541#p172541). **No staff answer in the extraction.** Relevant context: on 2026-03-31 staff *added* a new Timbermaw reputation source — kill rep from **Blackroot Furbolgs** in Moonwhisper Coast — [Torta (staff), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490).

## 3. Difficulty: staff intent vs. player reports

**Staff intent (official):** harder than the existing 20-player raids (ZG, AQ20) — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). The 2026-03-31 hotfix moved in both directions at once: three bosses got *more* health, four got *less* health or damage.

**Player reports (overtuned, single reporter but detailed and repeated):**

- Reacting to the health increase on Karrsh/Rotgrowl/Kodiak: "Cool, raid that drops some **T2.1 equivalent gear** still needs full Naxx or Kara 40 people to complete is getting even harder… Can we get a priority fix on gearing raids please? … nowhere did I read anything resembling 'I want my BWL bosses to be cointoss RNG or pure bruteforce with +3 raids above mandatory gear'" — [Travoltino (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173564#p173564).
- A week later: "Overall difficulty is **WAY overtuned in BWL, Onyx and TMH** for the gear that drops there" — [Travoltino (player), 2026-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174380#p174380).

No staff reply to either post appears in the extraction. The 2026-04-21 scaling change (20-player minimum threshold cut from 12 to 8) is the closest thing to a response, but staff did not connect the two — [Torta (staff), 2026-04-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176101#p176101).

**Positive reception (player):** "Timbermaw Hold has great vibes as do most/all of the content the turtle craft devs have added" — [Ffnabep (player), 2026-04-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174112#p174112).

## 4. Loot

- **Timbermaw tier sets** in light and heavy variants, plus new mounts, were previewed with the 1.18.1 announcement — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168).

Leather, mail and plate **Timbermaw tier boots** had visual bugs, fixed twice (2026-03-31 and again 2026-04-14) — same and [Torta (staff), 2026-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175230#p175230).
- **Corrupted Hide of Ursol** — made to drop for only one player (2026-04-21) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2026-04-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176101#p176101).

- **Gear tier (player claim, not staff):** "T2.1 equivalent gear" — [Travoltino (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173564#p173564).

## 6. Gaps and unclear points

- **Boss order is not known.** No source lists the encounters in sequence, and no map or route description exists beyond the five subzone names.
- **No ability names at all** except the two raid buffs, "Intruder of the Dream" and Peroth'arn's dirk. The staff notes say "several abilities" without naming them.
- **No health, damage, or duration values.**
- **No player tactics thread exists** in the extraction — the raid was live for well under two months before the shutdown announcement.
- **Lockout period unresolved** (player question, no staff answer).
- **Entry requirement unresolved** (contested between two players, no staff answer).
- **The "secret encounter"** is confirmed to exist but is entirely undocumented.
- **Which of the ten named NPCs are bosses vs. mini-bosses** is unclear; the wiki's count of 9 does not map cleanly onto the ten names in the hotfix notes.
