# Blackfathom Deeps (vanilla dungeon, extended by Turtle in 1.18.0)

Vanilla 5-player dungeon in Ashenvale. Turtle added a new optional area, the **Temple of Elune**, with one new boss, in **patch 1.18.0**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

## 2. Velthelaxx the Defiler (level 31 elite, satyr, Temple of Elune)

The wiki page is a **stub**: no abilities and no loot list. Only his emotes survive — aggro "**The power of Elune shall be mine!**", combat "Behold the power of Velthelaxx!", death "I serve in death…" — *(wiki)* [Velthelaxx the Defiler](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Velthelaxx_the_Defiler).

His death line ("I serve in death") and the Legion framing tie him to the same satyr-corruption theme Turtle uses in Crescent Grove and Timbermaw Hold.
