# Wailing Caverns (vanilla dungeon, extended by Turtle in 1.17.2)

Vanilla 5-player dungeon in the Barrens. Turtle added **two new optional areas with one new boss each** in **patch 1.17.2** and upgraded Lady Anacondra's loot. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

## 2. New bosses

### Vangros (level 22 elite, diemetradon, The Overgrowth)

**Staff description:** "A formidable **apex predator** empowered by the rampant life magics of the caverns, lurking in an overgrown sub-cave" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709).

| Abilities (wiki) | Loot (wiki) |
|---|---|
| **Crush Armor**, **Knock Away** | Enraging Waistwraps, Band of the Claw, **Idol of the Apex Predator** (druid relic) |

**Bug fix (official):** "Fixed **Vangros** not respecting threat" (2025-01-14) — [Reynolds (staff), 2025-01-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=116739#p116739). Combined with **Knock Away**, this was an encounter where tanks lost the boss for a year after release.

*(wiki)* [Vangros](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Vangros).

### Zandara Windhoof (level 23 elite, centaur, Kolkar Falls)

**Staff description:** "The Kolkar has sent an expedition to the depths of the caverns and have established a camp with the intention of cementing it as a **stronghold for the centaur**, threatening the Barrens and beyond" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709).

| Abilities (wiki) | Loot (wiki) |
|---|---|
| **Chain Lightning**, **Gust of Wind** | Windborne Sash, Kolkar Marauder Gauntlets, **Totem of Static Charge** (shaman relic) |

She is the objective of the level-23 dungeon quest **Against the Kolkar Dream**. Aggro: "This is our birthright, you have no place here!"; death: "I will never learn the secrets…" — *(wiki)* [Zandara Windhoof](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Zandara_Windhoof).

## 3. Loot changes to existing bosses

**Lady Anacondra (official):** "Lady Anacondra's drops have been **enhanced**, including her **Serpent's Shoulders to Rare** and adding a **new staff tailored for Bear Druids**" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709). The internal timeline records the Serpent's Shoulders as Rare **BoP** and adds that a **Violet Raptor mount** was put on Lady Anacondra in April 2025 — [`../../turtle-vs-vanilla-timeline.md`](../../turtle-vs-vanilla-timeline.md) §B4.
