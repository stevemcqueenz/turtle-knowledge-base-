# Razorfen Kraul (vanilla dungeon, extended by Turtle in 1.18.0)

Vanilla 5-player dungeon in the Barrens. Turtle added a new optional area, **The Brambled Grotto**, with one new boss, in **patch 1.18.0**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

## 2. Rotthorn (level 32 elite, bog beast)

**No abilities are listed on the wiki page.** Loot: Bramblethorn Girdle, Thornlash Branch, Seed of Writhing Growth, **Idol of the Thorned Grove** (druid relic). He drops the **Tainted Brambleheart** for the quest of the same name — *(wiki)* [Rotthorn](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Rotthorn).
