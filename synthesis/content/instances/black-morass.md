# Caverns of Time: Black Morass (5-player dungeon)

Custom level-60 5-player dungeon in the **Caverns of Time, Tanaris**. Added in **patch 1.16.0**, closed at some point, and **reworked and reopened in patch 1.17.0**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

## 2. The 1.17.0 rework

**Staff announcement (official)** — [Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868):

> "**Black Morass (Rework).** It's finally back and better than ever! The Black Morass dungeon is reopened once more, allowing hardy adventurers to venture within the timeways once again."

- "The **Harbinger** and **Infinite Chromie** boss encounters and the **Aqir section** have been revamped to be more time related with new custom spells and effects, with the two bosses being replaced with two totally new encounters: **Drifting Avatar of Time** and **Epidamu**."
- "**Rotmaw** and **Mossheart** are no longer rare bosses and can now always be found in the swamp section of the dungeon, **raising the total boss count to 7**."
- Loot moved: most Infinite Chromie items went to **Epidamu**; **Idol of Savagery** and **Wraps of the Transmogrified** were added to **Rotmaw**; **Totem of the Flowing Timeways** and **Fabric of Time** to **Epidamu**; the epic **Eye of the Abyss** now drops from **Antnormi** at around **1 %**.

## 3. Wardens of Time reputation

**Faction (official, 1.16.0):** "**Wardens of Time.** This specific group of the Bronze Dragonflight will accept the aid of any mortal that have earned access to the Caverns of Time. Their goal is to undo the mysterious time anomalies that have recently begun to appear across the timeways" — [Torta (staff), 2021-11-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14049#p14049).

**How reputation works (official, changed twice):**

1. 1.17.0: "Some of the creatures are now secretly **Infinite Dragonflight agents in disguise**, who will reveal their true form when attacked by players. Those that transform into drakonids will now give reputation to your **entire team**" — [Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868).
2. 2023-07-25 restatement: "**Regular NPCs will no longer increase Wardens of Time reputation when killed by default.** Instead, some of them will secretly be Infinite Dragonflight agents in disguise… Only those that transform into drakonids will give reputation. They will also give reputation to the whole team, not just the killer" — [Torta (staff), 2023-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54660#p54660).
3. Fix: "Fixed Infinite Dragonspawn in Black Morass **not giving 5 reputation per kill up to Friendly** with Wardens of Time" (2023-11-15) — [Jamey (staff), 2023-11-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77428#p77428). This is the only reputation *rate* number in the extraction.
4. 2025-08-13: Caverns of Time: Black Morass was included in the dungeon-reputation mapping as granting **Wardens of Time** — [Torta (staff), 2025-08-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145131#p145131).

## 5. Loot

- **BoE rare drop rate (official):** raised **from 0.0031 %–0.01 % to 0.02 %** across Black Morass, Stormwind Vault and Karazhan Crypt (2024-07-03) — [Jamey (staff), 2024-07-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98410#p98410).
- **Eye of the Abyss** (epic) from Antnormi, ~1 % — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868). A player comparing dungeons says Black Morass epics sit at 1 % while the Karazhan Crypt epic shoulders were at 0.25 % — [Apimius (player), 2022-08-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20858#p20858).
- Rework additions: Idol of Savagery, Wraps of the Transmogrified (Rotmaw); Totem of the Flowing Timeways, Fabric of Time (Epidamu) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868).
- Black Morass BoEs are common enough on the auction house that a player uses them as the benchmark against which Stormwind Vault trash BoEs look absurdly rare — [Badmojo74 (player), 2024-03-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89576#p89576).

## 7. Gaps and unclear points

- **Two of the seven bosses are unnamed** in every source.
- **No ability names, no numbers, no tactics** for any Black Morass boss. There is no player guide for this dungeon in the extraction.
- **Why the dungeon was closed** before the 1.17.0 rework, and for how long, is not explained in any source — only that it was "back".
- **Whether Black Morass was ever restored to the Cartographer requirement** after being removed "for now" in 2023: not found in sources.
- **The Aqir section** is mentioned as revamped but never described.
- **NPC and spell IDs**: not found in sources.
