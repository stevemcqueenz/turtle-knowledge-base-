# Lower Karazhan Halls (10-player raid, "Kara10")

Custom 10-player raid in **Deadwind Pass**, added in **patch 1.17.0**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = a player whose maintained guide thread the community references — here **Speedz1337**, author of the 128-post *Dungeon Quest Guide for Custom Content*, the *Attunements Guide* and the *Karazhan 10 Guide*, all still being edited in April 2026; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

## 2. Layout and bosses

**Structure (guide writer):** four bosses gate the final boss and **can be killed in any order**; Moroes is last. Master Blacksmith Rolfen is optional, killed only for his blacksmithing recipes — [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087).

| Boss | NPC ID | Role | Source of ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brood Queen Araxxna | 61221 | required (spider wing) | *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087) |
| Lord Blackwald II | 61222 | required (preceded by **Dark Rider Champion**) | same |
| Clawlord Howlfang | 61223 | required (worgen wing) | same |
| Grizikil | 61224 | required (imp/grellkin wing) | same |
| Moroes | 61225 | final boss, on the opera stage | same |
| Master Blacksmith Rolfen | 61319 | optional | same |

Layout is "very similar to the lower portion of Karazhan from The Burning Crusade… though the locations of some of the bosses will take you into areas you may not have explored in the TBC version" — *(player)* [Yavamar1 (player), 2025-11-20, quoted in](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160174#p160174).

### Brood Queen Araxxna (spider)

- Spits poison the raid must cleanse — **Abolish Poison** is strongly preferred over paladin cleanse. Summons egg adds that must be killed at once — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087).
- **Poison Cleansing Totem trivialises the fight:** "One shammie makes cleansing much easier, two trivializes the whole mechanics" — *(player)* [Akos1896 (player), 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160089#p160089); the same player calls this a design flaw in the boss ([2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141554#p141554)).
- **Skitterweb Eggs are immune to all crowd control** since 2025-02-04 — *(official)* [Reynolds (staff), 2025-02-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118948#p118948).
- Drops **Araxxna's Husk** (tank item: 140 armor, 15 stamina, 1 % dodge, item 61816) and **Araxxna's Hatchling** pet — *(player)* [Mimiwarmini (player), 2024-12-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=110875#p110875), [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2023-07-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52542#p52542). **Pet drop rate contested:** the guide originally said 20 % ([2023-07-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52542#p52542)); another player says "drops very often, around 30 %" ([Ky6ik (player), 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160125#p160125)).

**Spider-wing trash (guide writer, with spell IDs)** — [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087):

| Mob | Ability | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Skitterweb Darkfang (invisible) | **Venom Influx** (spell 57059) stun; **Darkfang Venom** (57058) stacking 10 %/stack movement + attack-speed poison | Kill first — the stun removes the tank's block/parry/dodge, so healing through the stun outranks dispelling |
| Skitterweb Venomfang (large green, 2 patrol before Araxxna) | **Corrosive Bolt** (44037), **Brood Venom Volley** (57063, stacks) | Mark them; they chain nearby packs. Abolish the volley |
| Skitterweb Crawler | **Leeching Bite** (57056) — −25 % healing taken | Prioritise abolish on tanks |
| Skitterweb Leaper | **Skitter Leap** (57060) charges the furthest player | Low priority |

### Grizikil (grellkin/imp wing)

- **Flamewave** (spell 57091) — a Baron-Geddon-style Inferno to run out of; **Grellkin Rain of Fire** (57093) cast on the raid, also move out — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087).
- **All imps in the corridor and side rooms must be cleared** before pulling, or they join the boss fight — same source.
- Drops the shaman relic **Totem of Crackling Thunder**: "Your Stormstrike and Lightning Bolt have a chance to also grant 15 % attack and casting speed for 8 sec" — *(official)* [Dragunovi (staff), 2023-07-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52799#p52799).

**Grellkin trash:** Channeler casts **Grellfire** (57089) which "can nuke someone down especially if 2 or 3 cast it at once" — interrupt on sight; it also has a damage-immunity blessing and a heal, so banish or kill it first. Shadow Weaver casts **Darkbolt** (57080) plus a dispellable magic mana drain. Primalist casts shocks, a Hurricane to step out of, and a purgeable massive armor buff on allies — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087).

### Clawlord Howlfang (worgen) — the hardest boss

- **A debuff that reduces armor and damage done, removable only by breaking line of sight with the boss.** This makes Howlfang (with Moroes) one of only two fights that strictly require two tanks; **swap at roughly 15 stacks** — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087).
- **Shadowbane Curse** (spell 57084) — tanks must be decursed — added to the guide after [Wsta (player), 2025-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160143#p160143) pointed out the omission; the guide author confirmed the edit ([2025-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160173#p160173)).
- Players call him "the wolf lad" and the only genuinely tough boss here: he "can shred most tanks to pieces if you don't play his fast armor debuff stacking and Tranq shot game nicely" — *(player)* [Toxo (player), 2025-07-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141670#p141670). **Note the two accounts differ on how the stacks are handled** — LoS-drop (guide) vs. taunt rotation + Tranquilizing Shot (Toxo) vs. "a simple tank taunt rotation or a paladin bubble" ([Afraido23 (player), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141323#p141323)). **Contested.**
- **Open bug — taunt resists.** A raid leader reports Howlfang resisting "like 80 % of both tanks' taunts, once 4 times in a row" ([Turney (player), 2025-12-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162969#p162969)); another confirms "something is wrong with that npc" ([Deeno (player), 2025-12-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162983#p162983)); a third suggests the real cause is that Howlfang loses his target while casting, and advises waiting for him to acquire a target before taunting ([Atreidon (player), 2025-12-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162984#p162984)). **No staff response in the extraction.**
- There is a **reset spot above a nearby pillar** — *(player)* [Wsta (player), 2025-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160143#p160143).
- Drops the druid relic **Idol of the Moonfang** — Leader of the Pack / Moonkin Aura crit bonus, buffed from +1 % to +2 % in 1.16.6 — *(player quoting the staff relic list)* [Jammyxx (player), 2023-07-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52873#p52873); relic list itself *(official)* [Dragunovi (staff), 2023-07-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52799#p52799).

**Worgen trash:** Shadowbane Darkcaster — **Darkbolt** (57080), 1000+ damage, uninterruptible, sheep or kill first. Shadowbane Ambusher — stealthed. Shadowbane Ragefang — **Claw Flurry** (57081), bleed on everyone hit. Shadowbane Alpha — enrages, sheep-immune, **Shadowbane Roar** (57079) debuffs nearby targets for **1000 armor, 451 attack power** and lowers shadow resistance; kill last despite that, because its health pool is huge — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087).

### Dark Rider Champion → Lord Blackwald II

- **Dark Rider Champion must die before Lord Blackwald II can be reached** — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087). Staff implemented this as a spawn condition: "In Lower Karazhan Halls, spawn Lord Blackwald II if Dark Rider Champion is dead" — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2023-10-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71678#p71678).
- **Blackwald** summons adds through the fight — "usually no more than 2–3", handled with mage CC or a designated off-tank; **decurse the tanks (important)** — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087). Another player times the summons at "a spooky boy every 30 seconds or so" — *(player)* [Toxo (player), 2025-07-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141670#p141670).
- He drops the paladin relic **Libram of the Faithful** ("Your Consecration reduces damage taken by 5 % for party members inside it") — *(official)* [Dragunovi (staff), 2023-07-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52799#p52799) — and starts the quest **Scythe of the Goddess** (quest 41062) — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087).
- **Exploit history (official):** Blackwald could be made to respawn; safeguards were added and "players who have previously exploited this bug will have the loot from exploited kills taken from them" — [Jamey (staff), 2023-10-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73511#p73511).

### Moroes (final boss, opera stage)

| Mechanic | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AoE silence | Cast on random players, small AoE. **Both healers must stand on opposite edges of the arena so they are never silenced at the same time** | *(player)* [Atreidon (player), 2025-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160165#p160165), folded into the guide by [Speedz1337, 2025-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160175#p160175) |
| Tank stun | Stuns the current main tank regularly; **tanks must be dispelled so they can taunt**, and the fight needs a regular taunt rotation | *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087) |
| Threat | DPS must stay below **both** tanks on threat | same |
| "Pocket sand" | A player describes the signature ability as being thrown at the highest-threat player on roughly a 30 s cadence | *(player)* [Toxo (player), 2025-07-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141670#p141670) |
| Phases | The fight is phased; he must "shift through his phases", is untargetable between them, and is teleported to the stage | *(official, from the bug fixes)* [Torta (staff), 2023-12-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81562#p81562), [Elesion (player), 2024-01-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83531#p83531) |

**Moroes was the raid's long-running bug (official fix trail):** stuck in combat during Phase 2 ([Torta (staff), 2023-10-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69525#p69525)) → not interactable ([Jamey (staff), 2023-10-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73511#p73511)) → "addressed bugs related to Moroes" ([Torta (staff), 2023-11-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=76368#p76368)) → could not shift phases ([Torta (staff), 2023-12-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=81562#p81562)) → "the fight should progress smoothly now" ([Torta (staff), 2024-01-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83488#p83488)) → "Moved Moroes back to the stage" ([Torta (staff), 2024-02-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87728#p87728)). A player asked in-thread whether the "talk to him after the fight and the second fight starts instantly instead of porting Moroes to the stage" bug was included; the answer came from another player, not staff ([Alfonso (player), 2024-01-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83518#p83518), [Elesion (player), 2024-01-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83531#p83531)). Players cited Moroes as the example of "buggy raids" when arguing for a public test realm — [Biteyou (player), 2024-10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103746#p103746).

**Undead trash before Moroes:** Phantom Guardsman (disarm-immune, high armor); Phantom Servant — **Phantom Scream** (57062) AoE silence, a *magic* effect so tanks can be dispelled to taunt, kill priority over everything; Shattercage Magiskull — **Arcane Explosion** (25679) with 30-yard radius, outranged by ranged/healers, stunlockable, one should be shackled — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337, 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087).

Killed for blacksmithing plans: **Plans: Towerforge Demolisher** (item 61805, Hammersmith) plus three Armorsmithing plans making a four-piece **Towerforge** set (helmet, breastplate, pauldrons, two-handed mace) — *(official, set description)* [Dragunovi (staff), 2023-07-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52800#p52800); *(player, item breakdown)* [Elesion (player), 2026-02-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168204#p168204).

## 3. Trash routing and wipe spots

The raid's difficulty is debated almost entirely in terms of trash. A player argues Kara10 "has no spots to be careful at" compared to UBRS — [Zvyrhol (player), 2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141595#p141595) — and another lists six places that can wipe an appropriately-geared raid: the cramped basement with the big green spider patrols; the passage from the ballroom upstairs where packs above aggro from below; the imp packs if you fail to split them; the opera-house entrance with skeleton mage patrols; the well-known jump skip; and the narrow ramp with the three Servants — [Elesion (player), 2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141604#p141604). Zvyrhol answers that in 10+ pugs only the jump-skip spot ever caused a wipe ([2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141633#p141633)). **Contested.**

## 6. Difficulty debate: staff intent vs. players

**Staff intent (official):** "a challenge similar to Molten Core" ([Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868)). In January 2025 staff acted on the "too easy" side of the argument: "**Buffed Lower Karazhan Halls bosses' damage and health values to better match the rewards given, offering a more rewarding challenge**" — [Torta (staff), 2025-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118168#p118168). A broader raid re-tune in 1.18.0 adjusted "health and damage values for all bosses and creatures in Molten Core, **Karazhan (10-player)**, Onyxia's Lair and Blackwing Lair" — [Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615).

**Player positions (contested, mid-2025):**

- *Too easy / badly designed:* "the textbook definition of a tank-and-spank raid… UBRS presents more of a challenge than Kara10", with the Scythe of Elune singled out as a jackpot prize in the easiest raid — [Afraido23 (player), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141323#p141323).
- *Working as designed:* "It's an entry-level raid. The mechanics are perfectly fine… it perfectly hits the spot between UBRS and MC" — [Elesion (player), 2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141546#p141546).
- *Middle:* several parts are complex (Howlfang's stacks, the imp rooms, the Servants' AoE silence, the arcane-AoE skeletons) but the first boss, the spider and Moroes are underwhelming; the real problem is that **loot quality outruns the difficulty**, jumping past ZG and AQ20 — [Akos1896 (player), 2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141554#p141554), agreed by [Toxo (player), 2025-07-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141670#p141670).

**Known open exploit (player, 2026):** after staff blocked fighting Anomalus and Sanv Tas'dal outside their rooms in Kara40, a player noted "eventually you'll fix 2-man Scythe boss on k10 or someone has to snitch that too" — [Wsta (player), 2026-03-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170084#p170084), seconded by [Manolothegreat (player), 2026-03-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170103#p170103). No staff response in the extraction.
