# Scarlet Monastery (vanilla dungeon, extended by Turtle in 1.17.2)

Vanilla 5-player dungeon complex in Tirisfal Glades. Turtle added **one new optional area with one new boss to each of the Graveyard, Library and Armory wings** in **patch 1.17.2**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

## 2. Boss names changed between announcement and release — contested

The turtlecraft.gg **Classic Dungeons Update** page (the 1.17.2 marketing page) and the staff forum notes name **different bosses** for the same three wings:

| Wing | Website page | Staff forum notes and wiki (live names) |
|---|---|---|
| Graveyard | "**The Knight of Naxxramas.** A harbinger of the Dread Citadel enslaved in the prison complex below the torture chambers…" | **Duke Dreadmoore \<Knight of Naxxramas\>** — the website's name became his *title* |
| Library | "**Brother Steelfist.** A masterful monk preparing initiates with martial prowess in the hidden chambers of the Library." | **Brother Wystan** — same description, different name |
| Armory | "**The Nameless Experiment.** A living blade forged from the unholy combination of runeforging and infusion of Light" **and** "**Master Smith Johana.** The craftsman behind the Crusade's powerful armaments" (a second boss "added to the original route") | **Armory Quartermaster Daghelm**, "the diligent maintainer of the warehouse and its resources" |

Website: [turtlecraft.gg, Classic Dungeons Update](https://turtlecraft.gg/classic-dungeons-update). Forum: [Torta (staff), 2023-12-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82980#p82980) and [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709). Wiki: the three pages linked below.

**Reading:** the website page is an earlier draft that was never updated. **The Nameless Experiment and Master Smith Johana do not appear in any staff forum note or on the wiki** — treat them as announced-but-renamed-or-cut. This is the clearest case in the extraction of the official website being stale relative to the patch notes.

## 3. New bosses (live)

### Duke Dreadmoore \<Knight of Naxxramas\> — level 32 elite, Scarlet Prison (Graveyard)

**Staff description:** "a harbinger of the Dread Citadel **enslaved in the prison complex below the torture chambers and experimented on**, waiting for the right moment to break free" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709).

| Abilities (wiki) | Loot (wiki) |
|---|---|
| **Dark Strike**, **Shadow Bolt Volley** | **Introduction to the Dark Arts**, Harbinger Girdle, Slain Sentry's Ring, Ghoulskin Treads |

Aggro: "I am the will of Naxxramas!"; death: "You will learn nothing from me, fools!" — *(wiki)* [Duke Dreadmoore](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Duke_Dreadmoore).

### Brother Wystan — level 36 elite, Sanctum of the Fist (Library)

**Staff description:** "a masterful monk preparing initiates with martial prowess in the **hidden chambers of the Library**" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709).

| Abilities (wiki) | Loot (wiki) |
|---|---|
| **Snap Kick**, **Uppercut**, **Fireball** | **Libram of Radiance** (paladin relic), Wystan's Steel Fist, Swiftstrike Kilt, Pendant of Scarlet Rage |

*(wiki)* [Brother Wystan](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Brother_Wystan).

### Armory Quartermaster Daghelm — level 38 elite, Warehouse (Armory)

**Staff description:** "the diligent maintainer of the warehouse and its resources, ready to deal with the enemies of the Crusade" — [Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709).

| Abilities (wiki) | Loot (wiki) |
|---|---|
| **Armor of Faith**, **Thunderclap**, **Sunder Armor** | Cuffs of Evocation, Balanced Razor Shivs, Loop of Focused Magic, Treads of Renewal |

He drops the **Journal of Basil Frye** for the quest *Reminiscent of Steel* — *(wiki)* [Armory Quartermaster Daghelm](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Armory_Quartermaster_Daghelm).

## 5. Gaps and unclear points

- **The website/forum boss-name conflict is unresolved by any source** — no staff post explains the renames or the fate of "The Nameless Experiment" and "Master Smith Johana".
- **No numbers** for any of the three new bosses; **no tactics threads**.
- **Where each new area branches** from the vanilla wings is described only loosely ("below the torture chambers", "hidden chambers of the Library", "the warehouse").
- **The Cathedral** received two new quests but no new boss; what they are is not named in the sources used here.
- **NPC and spell IDs**: not found in sources.
