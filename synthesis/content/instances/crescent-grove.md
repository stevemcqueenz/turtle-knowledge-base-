# The Crescent Grove (5-player dungeon)

Custom 5-player dungeon in **Ashenvale**, above Mystral Lake. Added in **patch 1.16.0** (2021-11-22 changelog); bosses reworked in **1.18.0**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post / turtlecraft.gg; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

**Official blurb:** "Step foot into a hidden grove under the trees and find and eradicate the source of corruption within" — [turtlecraft.gg, Raids and Dungeons](https://turtlecraft.gg/raids-and-dungeons).

**Lore (wiki):** the grove was a druid retreat until the Groveweald furbolg tribe fled the Foulweald's madness into it, expelling the inhabitants, and then succumbed to that madness themselves. Burning Legion forces under the doomguard **Master Raxxieth** then took root and created the **Vilethorn Scar** — [Crescent Grove](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Crescent_Grove).

**Denizens (wiki):** furbolgs, bears, wraiths, bog beasts, night elves, infernals, satyrs, succubi, one keeper of the grove, one doomguard.

Voice acting: Fenektis the Deceiver, Keeper Ranathos and Grovetender Engryss all have custom voice lines recorded for 1.16.0 — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2021-11-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14049#p14049).

## 3. Loot and quests

**Loot highlights (wiki, per-boss pages linked above):** Groveweald Tribal Necklace, Thornroot Maul, **Horn of Engryss** (Engryss); Bow of the Grove, Circlet of Cenarius, Glademender's Robes (Ranathos); Warden's Vest, Crescent Grove Staff, Thornstrand Sash (the two Wardens, shared table); Circlet of the Crescent Moon, Sentinel's Moonslicer, Crescent Sigil (A'lathea); Betrayer, Trickster's Wraps, Blackflame Wand, Felflame Shard (Fenektis); Crown of Corruption, Slayer's Edge, Ring of Demonic Fury (Raxxieth). Every boss also drops a **Fashion Coin**.

**Item changes (official):**
- **Horn of Engryss**: the on-use Thorns effect was replaced (2022-12-26 changed the Thorns spell; 2025-10-14 removed the on-use Thorns, raised armor 15 → 30, added 8 Stamina and a 3 Nature damage Thorns effect) — [Torta (staff), 2022-12-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=31601#p31601), [Torta (staff), 2025-10-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155594#p155594).
- Scarlet-Monastery-unique items were removed from the Crescent Grove loot table (2024-06-04) — [Torta (staff), 2024-06-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96352#p96352).
- Crescent Grove quest rewards updated 2025-05-06 — [Torta (staff), 2025-05-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129934#p129934).

**Quests (5 per the wiki):** Alliance *The Crescent Grove* (quest 40091) and *Kalanar's Mallet*; Horde *Rooting Out Evil*; plus **The Rampant Groveweald** (level 33 dungeon quest) which needs the **Groveweald Badge** from Grovetender Engryss — *(wiki)* [Crescent Grove](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Crescent_Grove), [Grovetender Engryss](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Grovetender_Engryss); *(guide writer, quest IDs)* [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2022-11-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29745#p29745). *Rooting Out Evil* was mis-categorised until 2023-05-17 — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2023-05-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=46277#p46277).
