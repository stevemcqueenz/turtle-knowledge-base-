# Zul'Farrak (vanilla dungeon, extended by Turtle in 1.18.0)

Vanilla 5-player dungeon in Tanaris. Turtle added **two new optional areas** — the **Farraki Tombs** and the **Farraki Arena** — in **patch 1.18.0**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

## 2. Zel'jeb the Ancient (level 46 elite, dire troll, Farraki Tombs)

**This is the only Turtle boss in the extraction with a fully specified damage-immunity puzzle**, and the wiki even documents the fallback for groups that cannot solve it.

| Ability | Effect and numbers | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Ancient Embalming** | "**Receives 90 % less damage from all sources. Vulnerable to Fire spells and effects.**" | *(wiki)* [Zel'jeb the Ancient](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Zel%27jeb_the_Ancient) |
| **Thunderclap** | — | same |
| **Curse of Years** | Cast at **50 % health** — "Zel'jeb the Ancient calls upon the ancestors of Zul'Farrak!" | same |
| **War Stomp** | — | same |

**Tactics (wiki note):** "Zel'jeb is highly resistant to all damage types save for **Fire**, which will **break his embalming**. In the event where the group has no fire damage dealer, you can acquire the **Flame of Farrak** from a brazier next to **Chief Ukorz Sandscalp**, which has a **ten minute duration**" — *(wiki)* [Zel'jeb the Ancient](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Zel%27jeb_the_Ancient).

He also has a 25 % emote ("I shall live forever!"), implying a second threshold. Loot: Bloodstained Fangblade, Staff of the Bloodbound, Bracers of the Bloodbound, Sash of Ritual Scars. He is the objective of the quest **The Farraki Ancient**.

## 3. The Farraki Arena

A multi-stage gauntlet with a **single wiki page for the whole event** — [Farraki Arena](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Farraki_Arena).

**Champion Razjal the Quick** is the final challenger. His intro line states the structure: "**You challenge me? You'll have to beat my best warriors first.**"

| Combatant | Abilities / emotes | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Kath'zen the Brutal** | aggro "I shall crush your bones…" | *(wiki)* [Farraki Arena](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Farraki_Arena) |
| **Juthza the Cunning** | aggro "I be elusive… You cannot escape me!" | same |
| **Champion Razjal the Quick** | **Scorpid Ritual** ("Be consumed by the sand!"), **Hellfire** ("I am the only champion, not you, not anyone!") | same |

Arena loot: Champion's Sandhelm, **Libram of the Farraki Zealot** (paladin relic), Gilded Blade of Razjal, Talisman of the Sandborn.

**Fix (official):** "Fixed **Champion Razjal the Quick** not always dropping loot" (2025-11-18) — [Torta (staff), 2025-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159994#p159994).

## 5. Gaps and unclear points

- **No health or damage numbers** for any of the new encounters; Ancient Embalming's 90 % reduction is the only quantified value.
- **How many waves the Farraki Arena has** and whether the three named combatants are the whole event is not stated — the wiki treats the arena as one page.
- **No tactics thread** exists for either new area; the Zel'jeb fire mechanic is documented only by the wiki.
- **The two new quests** are not both named; *The Farraki Ancient* is one. The community guide's Zul'Farrak section carries the database IDs — *(guide writer)* [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2022-11-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29747#p29747).
- **Level range** disagrees between the wiki table and the LFT data.
- **NPC and spell IDs**: not found in sources.
