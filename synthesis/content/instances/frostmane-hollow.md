# Frostmane Hollow (5-player dungeon)

Custom low-level 5-player dungeon in **Dun Morogh**. Added in **patch 1.18.1** (announced 2025-10-03, released 2026-03-20). Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post; *(guide writer)* = Speedz1337's maintained dungeon-quest guide; *(player)* = other non-staff posts; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

**Lore (wiki):** "the sanctum of the **Frostmane clan** and a de-facto capital for all of trollkind in Dun Morogh" — [Frostmane Hollow](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Frostmane_Hollow). Frostmane Hollow sits in the same corner of Dun Morogh as the **Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility**; both had minimap artifacts fixed in the 1.18.1 client work — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168).

## 2. Bosses

All five are level 16 elites (wiki infoboxes) despite the 13–20 level band.

| Boss | Type | Abilities (wiki) | Loot / quest link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Handler Oboka** | ice troll | none listed. His aggro line — "**Tan'sha, kill them all!**" — pairs him with the leopard below | (his axe drops from Tan'sha, see next row) |
| **Tan'sha the Sleek** | snow leopard | none listed | Frigid Cloak, **Oboka's Axe**, Vest of the Beastkeeper. Drops *Flawless Leopard Pelt* for the quest **The Finest Pelt** |
| **Battlemaster Ubukaz** | ice troll | none listed | Frostmane Battle Boots, Tribal War Gauntlets, Snowchilled Crest. Objective of **Chieftain Ubukaz** |
| **Kan'za the Seer** | ice troll | **Blizzard** | Frost Seet Dirk, Windcrest Pants |
| **Hailar the Frigid** | ice elemental, **final** | **Frostbolt** | Rak'uhz the Coldbinder, Belt of Binding, Band of Hailar |

Wiki pages: [Handler Oboka](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Handler_Oboka), [Tan'sha the Sleek](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Tan%27sha_the_Sleek), [Battlemaster Ubukaz](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Battlemaster_Ubukaz), [Kan'za the Seer](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Kan%27za_the_Seer), [Hailar the Frigid](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Hailar_the_Frigid).

**Handler Oboka and Tan'sha the Sleek appear to be a paired encounter** — Oboka's only recorded line commands Tan'sha, and Oboka's axe drops from Tan'sha, not from Oboka. That would reconcile the wiki's "4 bosses" count with five boss pages. **Inference, not stated in any source.**

Hailar the Frigid has a scripted 50 % line ("Let the chill overcome…"), so the final fight has at least one phase transition — *(wiki)* [Hailar the Frigid](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Hailar_the_Frigid).

## 6. Gaps and unclear points

- **No written tactics, no run reports.** Frostmane Hollow opened 2026-03-20 and generated almost no forum traffic before the shutdown announcement; the only player content is the video above.
- **Three of five bosses have no listed abilities**; the other two have one each.
- **No health, damage or numbers of any kind.**
- **Boss count** (4 vs 5) is unresolved; the Oboka/Tan'sha pairing above is an inference.
- **Boss order is not documented.**
- **No Horde quests are listed** for the dungeon by the wiki; whether that is a gap in the wiki or in the game is not determinable from the sources.
- **What the Level One Lunatic loot is, and how it is obtained,** is not explained.
- **NPC and spell IDs**: not found in sources.
