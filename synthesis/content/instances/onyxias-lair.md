# Onyxia's Lair (vanilla raid, extended by Turtle in 1.18.1)

Vanilla 40-player raid in Dustwallow Marsh, **extended with a new section, a new optional boss and new Onyxia mechanics in patch 1.18.1 (released 2026-03-20)**. Part of `synthesis/content/instances/` — see the [index](README.md) and [`../dungeons-and-raids.md`](../dungeons-and-raids.md).

**Source tiers:** *(official)* = staff post; *(player)* = non-staff forum post; *(wiki)* = turtle-wow.fandom.com, not official.

This file covers **Turtle's changes only**. Vanilla Onyxia mechanics are not restated.

## 2. The 1.18.1 extension

### Broodcommander Axelus (new optional boss, new section)

*(wiki)* [Broodcommander Axelus](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Broodcommander_Axelus): black dragonspawn, boss, Onyxia's Lair, patch 1.18.1. The wiki page carries only combat quotes — aggro "She must not be disturbed!", death "Broodmother, they're… coming…" — **no ability list**.

- Added as "a new section and with it a new optional boss" — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168).
- **Loot:** he has more than one loot table; **Idol of Equilibrium** was missing from one of them and was restored — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608).
- **Known bug (player, no staff response in the extraction):** "Pets fall below ground when taking the tunnel after the first boss of Onyxia's Lair" — [Lane13 (player), 2026-03-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171787#p171787). (The "first boss" here is Axelus, i.e. the new section precedes Onyxia.)

### Onyxian Hatcher

Not in the announcement, but named in the post-release tuning: "**Significantly reduced health of Onyxian Hatcher**" with the stated reason "Hatchers can sometimes be too tight of a DPS check" — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139). This is the clearest statement in the extraction that the new adds function as a **timed DPS check**.

### Onyxia herself

"Added new mechanics to Onyxia" — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). **The notes never say what they are, and no player post in the extraction describes them.** This is the single largest documentation gap in the 1.18.1 raid changes.

Related fixes:

- **Blink works again in Onyxia's Lair** (2026-03-21) — mages had lost it, presumably as a side effect of the new-mechanics work — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608).
- Pets attack Onyxia from maximum range correctly, unlike Nefarian, the BWL drakes, Ragnaros, Omen and Solnius, where a hunter reports pets running to minimum range — *(player)* [Lane13 (player), 2026-03-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170490#p170490).

- Loot-quantity fixes: double T2 helm/leggings from Onyxia and Ragnaros removed (2023-08-08); Onyxia dropping too much loot fixed (2025-01-07); Onyxia's loot table corrected (2025-05-06); on Tel'Abim PvP she drops 2 tier pieces as intended (2023-11-29) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2023-08-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56424#p56424), [Jamey (staff), 2025-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115795#p115795), [Torta (staff), 2025-05-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=129934#p129934), [Torta (staff), 2023-11-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79223#p79223).
- **Onyxian Whelps** no longer give kill XP (2025-08-13, repeated 2025-12-30) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-08-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145131#p145131), [Torta (staff), 2025-12-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164078#p164078).
- **Onyxia's head** can no longer be turned in a second time after a faction change (2025-11-11) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159221#p159221). The Onyxia trophy head model in Stormwind was fixed in 1.17.2 — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2023-12-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82980#p82980).

## 4. Attunement

- **Amulet of Draconic Subversion** can be re-requested from Myranda the Hag if lost during the Onyxia attunement questline (2026-01-13) — *(official)* [Shadowsong (staff), 2026-01-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165064#p165064).
- **Lady Katrana Prestor** respawn time raised to 10 minutes, with faster respawns preserved for the scripted attunement event, "to prevent duplicated spawns" (2023-08-27) — *(official)* [Torta (staff), 2023-08-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=59286#p59286).
- Players report being stuck on the Onyxia attunement — [thread t14655](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=14655) — and asked in December 2025 whether MC/Onyxia attunements had disappeared — [thread t23285](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=23285).

## 6. Gaps and unclear points

- **Onyxia's new mechanics are completely undocumented.** Staff announced them; nothing in the extraction says what they do.
- **Broodcommander Axelus has no ability list** in any source — only quotes and loot notes.
- **Onyxian Flamespawn and Onyxian Inciter** are named but never described.
- **No health, damage or timer values** anywhere.
- **The new section's layout** — where it branches from the vanilla lair — is only inferable from a bug report about "the tunnel after the first boss".
- **Lockout** for Onyxia's Lair on Turtle: not stated in the sources.
