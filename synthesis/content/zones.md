# Custom and reworked zones

Every outdoor zone, sub-zone and quest hub that Turtle WoW added or rebuilt, from the 2019 "Sands of Time" era through patch 1.18.1 (release 2026-03-20) and its post-release hotfixes. Instances (dungeons and raids) are covered in `synthesis/content/dungeons-and-raids.md`; this file only names them where they sit inside a zone.

**How to read the sources.** Three tiers appear throughout:

- **Official (staff)** — the turtlecraft.gg website pages and staff patch threads on the forum. These give level ranges, patch numbers and design intent.
- **Community wiki** — `turtle-wow.fandom.com`. This is *not* an official source. It supplies most of the sub-zone lists, flight-path pairs, adjacent-zone access notes and resource tables below; treat those as community-maintained and possibly stale.
- **Player** — forum posts by non-staff players.

Every row and sentence says which tier it came from. Where the official site and the wiki disagree on a level range, both are given.

**Coordinates.** Almost no source in this extraction gives in-game X/Y coordinates for zone features. Where a location is stated it is a named sub-zone, not a coordinate pair. See the gaps section.

## 2. Zone by zone

### Blackstone Island — 1–16 (official), Horde/Goblin starting zone

- **Patch** 1.17.1; creature tuning pass 2025-02-18 (per the 1.18.0 timeline entry) — [Torta (staff), 2023-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71005#p71005).
- **Lore/purpose (official):** goblins of the Durotar Labor Union seized a resource-rich island; the player's job is to "break free from the chains of poverty and claim your rightful place in the Durotar Labor Union" — [official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps).
- **Getting there (wiki):** west to Durotar by swimming, or Horde flight path Gazzik's Workshop → Sparkwater Port — [wiki: Blackstone Island](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Blackstone_Island).
- **Sub-zones (wiki):** Rustgate Ridge (capital), Black Ash Coalpits, Black Ash Mine, Gazzik's Workshop, Rustgate Lumber Yard, Venture Co. Slums, The Water Hole.
- **Factions present (wiki):** Durotar Labor Union, Venture Company, Mudpaw tribe. Ruler: Taxxlo Rustgate.
- **Notable NPC:** Feebeld, Survival Trainer (level 50, Rustgate Ridge), added 1.18.1 — [wiki: Feebeld](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Feebeld).
- **Level-range conflict:** official page says 1–16, wiki infobox says 1–10. **contested**, both cited.

### Thalassian Highlands — High Elf starting zone

- **Patch** 1.17.1 (the zone replaced Farstrider Lodge in Loch Modan as the High Elf start, which had been the 1.15.0 start) — [Torta (staff), 2023-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71005#p71005), [Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868).
- **Official description:** an outpost of Alah'Thalas that "has developed into a thriving city, complete with its own dock… This zone serves as the starting point for fledgling High Elves, offering a level 1-10 experience"; the zone band is given as 15–20 — [official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps).
- **Capital:** Alah'Thalas (Alliance capital city, added 1.15.0; merchants added 1.17.2; map rework 1.17.0) — [Torta (staff), 2020-10-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7948#p7948), [wiki: Alah'Thalas](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Alah%27Thalas).
- **Sub-zones (wiki):** Alah'Thalas, Anasterian Park, Ballador's Chapel, Brinthilien, The Farstride (Deepmurk Cave), Felstrider Retreat, Isle of Eternal Autumn, The Last Runestone, Ruins of Nashal'aran, The Scourged Pass, Silver Covenant Camp, Silver Sun Mine, Thaumarium — [wiki: Thalassian Highlands](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Thalassian_Highlands).
- **Travel (wiki):** boat Alah'Thalas → Auberdine (Darkshore); on foot south through the Scourged Pass to the Eastern Plaguelands, or Alliance flight Alah'Thalas ↔ Light's Hope Chapel.
- **Faction:** Silvermoon Remnant (see `factions.md`). Rulers listed by the wiki: Lord-Regent Astalor Sunsworn plus a Regency Council (Vereesa Windrunner, Lor'themar Theron, Grand Magister Rommath, Voren'thal the Scryer, Farenia Roseheart, Miralin Wavesinger) and Viceroy Eldin Sunstrider.
- **Trainers/vendors (wiki, Brinthilien):** Torial Dawnrise (General Goods), Alyssia Solar (Cloth & Leather Armor), Melonius Silvershine (Mail Armor), Malanius Silvershine (Food), plus class trainers Lor'thas the Holy (Paladin), Priestess Maelah Sunsworn (Priest), Leela the Shadow (Rogue), Magister Ala'shor Sunblood (Mage), Ranger Rubinah Sunsworn (Hunter), Valanos Dawnfire (Warrior). Full list in `npcs-and-vendors.md`.
- **Hotfix:** music on the Isle of Eternal Autumn fixed in 1.18.1 — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168).

### Northwind — 28–34

- **Patch** 1.18.0 — [Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615).
- **Official description:** "the cradle of nobility within the human kingdom… Lord Amberwood, Ruler of Northwind, and his fellow nobles struggle to maintain order" — [official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps).
- **Hubs (wiki):** Ambershire (major town: inn, mailbox, stable, anvil, flight path), Amberwood Keep (capital), Birkhaven, Sherwood Quarry, plus Stillheart Port, Mildenhall Horse Farm, Lion's Watch, Crawford Winery, Northwind Logging Camp, Bristlewhisker Cavern, Blackrock Breach, Northridge Point — [wiki: Northwind](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Northwind), [wiki: Ambershire](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Ambershire).
- **Getting there (wiki):** south to Elwynn Forest by swimming through Crystal Falls, or via Stormwind Harbor; flight path Ambershire ↔ Stormwind City; west to Balor by swimming; north-east to Searing Gorge on foot through Sherwood Quarry.
- **Flight path added in 1.18.1:** **Ambershire (Northwind) ↔ Thorium Point (Searing Gorge)** — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168).
- **Factions present (wiki):** Kingdom of Stormwind, Defias Brotherhood, Blackrock clan, Amberpaw pack, Bristlewhisker.
- **Notable NPCs (wiki):** Enchantress Magilou (Expert Enchanter, level 50); The Witch of Northwind (Alchemy trainer, level 50, Witch Coven); Count Femur (level 50 skeleton Blacksmithing vendor, in the Crypt); Horsekeeper Geringt (Riding Trainer) and Nadia Geringt (Horse Breeder / mount vendor) at Mildenhall Horse Farm — see `npcs-and-vendors.md`.
- **Note:** the wiki lists 145 NPC pages for Northwind and 132 pages under "Northwind NPCs" — it is the most densely documented custom zone on the wiki.
- **Naming caution:** *Ambershire* and *Nordanaar* are also the names of two live realms, so most forum hits for those words are realm chatter, not zone reports.

### Balor — 29–34

- **Patch** 1.18.0 — [Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615).
- **Official description:** an accursed island chain west of Elwynn/Westfall, "once a blooming trade port for the kingdom, before falling into the hands of the Old Horde during the First War" and held by Gul'dan's Shadow Council in the Second War — [official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps).
- **Hubs (wiki):** SI:7 Outpost (Alliance, south-east; leader Verona Gillian; inn, flight path), Stormbreaker Point (Horde, east; leader Kilrogg Deadeye; inn, flight path), plus Breezehaven and Bilgerat Compound — [wiki: Balor](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Balor).
- **Flight paths (wiki):** SI:7 Outpost ↔ Sentinel Hill (Westfall); Stormbreaker Point ↔ Grom'gol Base Camp (Stranglethorn Vale).
- **Other access (wiki):** swim from the eastern coast to Westfall or Northwind.
- **Sub-zones (wiki):** Bilgerat Compound, Croaking Plateau, Grahan Estate, Gullwing Wreckage, Langston Orchard, Ruins of Breezehaven, Scurrying Thicket, SI:7 Outpost, Sorrowmore Lake, Stormbreaker Point, Stormreaver Spire, Stormwrought Castle, Treacherous Crags, Vander Farmstead, Windrock Cliffs.
- **Elite areas (wiki):** Stormwrought Castle and Stormreaver Spire.
- **Instance:** Stormwrought Ruins (Castle + Caverns sections) — see the dungeons document.
- **Factions present (wiki):** Stormreaver clan, Shadow Council, Bleeding Hollow clan, SI:7, Bilgerats, Fogwhiskers.
- **Resources (wiki):** Khadgar's Whisker, Fadeleaf, Grave Moss, Liferoot; Silver/Iron/Gold veins; Firefin Snapper, Oily Blackmouth; Bright Wood Tree (Survival lumber).
- **Naming caution:** "Balor" is also a forum username; most forum hits are that user, not the zone.

### Grim Reaches — 33–38

- **Patch** 1.18.0 — [Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615).
- **Official description:** eastern cliffs of the Eastern Kingdoms, contested for decades between the Wildhammer dwarves of Dun Kithas and the Dragonmaw orcs; corruption gathers in the southern swamp, **The Grim Hollows** — [official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/new-locations-and-maps).
- **Hubs (wiki):** Dun Kithas (Alliance town: inn, mailbox, stables, anvil, flight path; Council of Dun Kithas / Sky King Redoran) and Shatterblade Post (Horde outpost: inn, class trainers, flight path) — [wiki: Grim Reaches](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Grim_Reaches), [wiki: Dun Kithas](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Dun_Kithas), [wiki: Shatterblade Post](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Shatterblade_Post).
- **Getting there (wiki):** south-west to Loch Modan on foot through the High Pass, or flight Dun Kithas ↔ Thelsamar; west to the Wetlands on foot through the Dragonmaw Gates.
- **Sub-zones (wiki):** Baggoth's Rampart, Baggoth's Wall, Barleycrest Farmstead, Brangar's Folly, Dun Kithas (Dun Kithas Magistrate), East Ridge Outpost, Geth'kar, Grim Batol Memorial, The Grim Hollow (Tomb of Ancestors), Groldan's Excavation, The High Pass, Lake Kithas, Ruins of Grim Batol, Ruins of Stolgaz Keep, Sal'Galaz Mines, Slatebeard's Forge, Shatterblade Post, Zarm'geth Point, Zarm'geth Stronghold.
- **Related classic edit:** the **Grim Batol gate path** was revealed in 1.18.0 — [Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615).
- **Instance:** Dragonmaw Retreat (wiki places its entrance in the Wetlands; boss Zuluhed the Whacked; Lower Reserve Key) — see the dungeons document.
- **Loot note (staff):** Dragonmaw Bracers/Greaves/Hauberk/Leggings and Stormreaver Boots/Belt had their drop chance raised from 0.11% to 1.35% — [Torta (staff), 2025-09-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149960#p149960).

- **Gillijim's Isle hubs (wiki):** Maul'ogg Refuge (Horde outpost, flight path), Kalkor Point (neutral centaur camp with an anvil), capital Zul'Razar. Sub-zones include The Broken Reef, Deeptide Sanctum, Gillijim Canyon, Gillijim Strand (Deepneck Cove), The Jade Mine, Kazon Island, Maul'ogg Post, Ruins of Zul'Razar, The Silver Coast (Faelon's Folly, Silver Sandbar), The Southsea Sandbar (Distillery Island), The Tangled Wood — [wiki: Gillijim's Isle](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Gillijim%27s_Isle).

- **Questline:** "Tower of Lapidis I–X" is named in the staff changelogs — see `quests-and-questlines.md`.
- **Resources (wiki):** Dreamfoil, Sungrass, Purple Lotus, Golden Sansam, Stranglekelp, Ghost Mushroom (Gillijim); Truesilver and Small Thorium; Tropical Wood Tree.
- **World bosses:** "custom mini world bosses" on Gillijim/Lapidis are noted in the 1.16.0 thread — [Torta (staff), 2021-11-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14049#p14049). No names are given there.
- **Player note:** a player greeted the 1.16.4 island announcement with "Gillijim and Lapidis are good looking zones with enjoyable quests" — [Shamma (player), 2022-11-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30044#p30044). **single source**, opinion.

## 4. World-wide changes that affect every zone

- **Server time offset.** Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms run on different in-game clocks — an 8-hour offset, later 12 hours — [Torta (staff), 2022-10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=27514#p27514).
- **Rare bosses.** 1.16.0 added "many new Rare Bosses lurking in level 40+ zones"; 1.17.0 added a further named list — [Torta (staff), 2021-11-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14049#p14049), [Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868). The names are not reproduced in the extracted staff posts.
- **Outdoor world bosses.** Nerubian Overseer (Eastern Plaguelands, added 2020-05), Ostarius of Uldum (40-player; 20-person minimum to summon from 2023-12-27), Concavius (20+, Shadowbreak Ravine) — [Pompa (player-indexed, writes as team), 2020-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6010#p6010), [Torta (staff), 2021-11-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14049#p14049).
- **Gathering.** Dynamic respawn for high-level herbs and ores (2024-03-19; custom zones 2023-10-18); Black Lotus additional drop chance from Dreamfoil / Mountain Silversage / Plaguebloom / Icecap raised in steps 0.5% → 1% → 2% → 4% → 6%; Rich Thorium shares Thorium spawns; Elemental Earth added to Thorium veins in 1.18.0; gold veins removed from WPL, Un'Goro and Burning Steppes; copper/tin/silver removed from Desolace; **Gemstone Deposits** added (Mining 310). Details and citations in `professions-and-recipes.md` and `drop-rates.md`.
- **Woodcutting trees.** 1.18.1 added Woodcutting nodes across the world tied to Survival; the wiki records the tree tiers per zone (Simple Wood, Bright Wood, Shade Wood, Star Wood, Dead Wood, Tropical Wood) in each zone's Resources section — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168); tree names were renamed "for better immersion" — [Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139).
- **Flight-path hotfix (post-1.18.1):** an awkward transition between Ratchet, Talrendis Point and Shimmering Lake was fixed, and Druid flight masters in Moonglade were fixed — [Torta (staff), 2026-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174367#p174367), [Torta (staff), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608).
- **World fixes in 1.18.1:** ground hole in the Badlands; a water pool that should be lava in the Searing Gorge; minimap artifacts in the Timbermaw Tunnels and Gnomeregan Reclamation Facility; the Noxious Glade slaughterhouse; the Uldaman Cave removed from Bael Modan — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168).
- **High-level mob spawn sharing:** "Some mobs in high level zones now share spawn points" (zones not named) — [Jamey (staff), 2026-04-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176311#p176311).

## 5. Player notes: leveling value, dangers, hardcore

The extracted forum corpus is thin on zone-by-zone leveling advice. What exists:

- **Rested XP and tents (affects every zone, 1.18.1).** Rested XP from kills was cut from a 100% bonus to a 50% bonus; tents cap at 25% rested and all non-city taverns can reach 100% — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). Staff reasoning: the bonus was nerfed "so XP rate isn't permanently doubled in the game" while making rested accessible worldwide, and tent camps at the Stormwind/Orgrimmar gates were "a mess" — [Torta (staff), 2026-02-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168639#p168639), [Torta (staff), 2026-02-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168649#p168649). Players objected — [Saronsen (player), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173332#p173332), [Hctwowfan (player), 2026-02-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168644#p168644). **contested** (staff intent vs. player reception).
- **Travel danger, Stranglethorn Vale.** Reaching Nesingwary's Expedition (the Survival trainer) at low level is repeatedly reported as dangerous: "Going to booty on lvl9 alone is torture, all mobs along the road gets agroed and its impossible to defend" — [Sharq (player), 2024-01-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86439#p86439). A workaround: "you can swim from Westfall to booty bay. It's a straight line, no fatigue, no mobs… 10 to 15min" — [Conelle (player), 2024-12-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=114013#p114013). The Arena Ambassador teleport that older guides used has been removed — [Desuku (player), 2022-09-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=25464#p25464). **consensus** across several posters over three years. This matters for Hardcore characters.
- **Hardcore-specific zone warnings** are **not found in sources** in this extraction. The Hardcore rules themselves (gravestones, login protection, LFT disabled, Inferno Mode at 55) are in the timeline document, not tied to particular zones.

## 6. Gaps and unclear points

1. **No coordinates anywhere.** Neither the staff changelogs nor the wiki infoboxes in this extraction give X/Y coordinates for hubs, flight masters, vendors or rares. Every "location" here is a named sub-zone. The wiki's own NPC pages sometimes state a sub-zone but never a coordinate pair.
2. **Level ranges conflict between official and wiki** for Blackstone Island (1–16 vs 1–10), Thalassian Highlands (15–20 vs 1–10) and Hyjal (56–60 vs 58–60). Both are given; no source reconciles them.
3. **Rare spawns and world bosses are largely unnamed.** The 1.16.0 and 1.17.0 staff threads say rare bosses were added to level-40+ zones and give a "named list" for 1.17.0, but the extracted text does not reproduce the names. Only two outdoor named elites surfaced in the wiki data (Firstborn of Arugal in Gilneas, Father Lycan in Hyjal) plus the three known world bosses.
4. **Ronae'Thalas status unknown.** Announced for 1.17.0 and then "in one of the upcoming patches" in December 2023; no wiki page, no later changelog entry in this extraction.
5. **Tirisfal Uplands and Sparkwater Port have no standalone wiki article** in this extraction, so their sub-zone and facility details come only from the staff threads and the parent zone pages.
6. **Zone-level player guidance is missing.** There are no extracted "best zone to level 30–40" style guides; forum mentions of Tel'Abim, Nordanaar and Ambershire are overwhelmingly about the realms of those names, not the zones. Any zone-by-zone leveling ranking would be invention.
7. **Winter Veil Vale, Sunnyglade Valley and Thorn Gorge** are instanced; their internals belong to the dungeons/raids and PvP documents respectively and are only stubbed here.
8. **The 1.18.1 "New zone" section of the master changelog names only Moonwhisper Coast.** If 1.18.1 added other outdoor areas (for example Ursolan, Maw of Ursoc, Grove of the Sun as standalone map entries) they appear only as wiki sub-zone pages, with no staff citation for their level range.
9. **Faction control of hubs** is taken from wiki infoboxes (`faction: Alliance/Horde/Neutral`); no staff source in this extraction enumerates which hubs are faction-locked.
