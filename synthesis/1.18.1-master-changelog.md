## Headline content (as summarised in the notes)

Moonwhisper Coast (new zone, 50-56), Windhorn Canyon (5-man, 26-30), Frostmane Hollow (5-man, 13-20), Timbermaw Hold (20-man raid "designed to be harder than the existing 20 man raids"), extensions to Onyxia's Lair and Blackwing Lair, new PvP events, Survival profession rework, new items — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168).

## Agreement notes

- Official notes and hotfix lists are **single source** (staff) by nature; where players independently confirm behaviour (e.g. Holy Strike miss bug reported by three players, Flurry/Earthen Bulwark by several Enhancement players, world PvP events absent per several players) the player report is marked and is effectively **consensus** among players, but staff statements about intent outrank them.
- **Contested**: the Tents & Taverns change is defended by staff (immersion, capital-gate camping, XP not permanently doubled) and rejected by several players as a nerf — see the professions section for both sides with dates.
- **Contested**: PvP gear power and honor compensation — staff describe the honor cut as "purely a visual change"; players report the compensation and set power as inadequate.
