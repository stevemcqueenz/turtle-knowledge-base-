## Statements

#### 2021-05-03 — Naxxramas scaling

> No scaling, Naxxramas is intended to be the absolute end to Vanilla ... The Dread Necropolis should require a true army to conquer, not a small strike force.

Clarifies: Naxxramas is meant to stay unscaled as vanilla's absolute end, requiring a full army; future custom raids were argued to stay unscaled too.

[Dragunovi (staff), 2021-05-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=10354#p10354) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Naxxramas caling: Community Poll"

#### 2021-11-10 — Future raids

> Northrend was never announced, Outland is the end for now ... Silvermoon is confirmed to be a raid for Episode 2. The other raid to be confirmed is Karazhan.

Clarifies: Northrend was never announced and Outland is the intended endpoint; Silvermoon and Karazhan were the confirmed Episode 2 raids, with Deatholme planned as an outdoor questing hub.

[Gheor (staff), 2021-11-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13720#p13720) · kind: `roadmap` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "New Dungeons and Raids for Future patches"

#### 2022-08-26 — Raid tuning philosophy

> Ragnaros wipes still happen in newer raiding groups--balancing the lowest end of raids for higher end raiders will cause many issues.

Clarifies: Old raid encounters are not retuned for high-end raiders because newer groups still wipe on them.
