#### 2019-07-01 — Roleplay outfits

#### 2020-03-12 — Transmogrification

> Right now modifications are limited to the same armor type. We are considering removing this limitation at a later point if nothing speaks against it. Some of the custom guild sets are not available for transmogrification, that's a bug which will be fixed in the future.

Clarifies: Transmog design: Fashion Coin currency from a weekly repeatable, same-armor-type restriction, reset is free; custom guild sets not transmoggable is a bug.

#### 2020-03-18 — Portable Meeting Stone

> Initially Portable Meetings Stones were meant as a quick transport to the active locations for role-playing interactions. However, these locations are never really crowded or really used for anything.

Clarifies: Portable Meeting Stones were originally intended as transport to RP hubs; because those hubs went unused, the free teleport was replaced by a reagent-consuming wormhole-style device with a cooldown and cost.

[Torta (staff), 2020-03-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5408#p5408) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Change / Remove Portable Meeting Stones"

#### 2020-03-18 — Teleport items

> The transports you mention are bond to a zone, and also their existence is justified in the lore ... not to the player's inventory. City Protector's Scrolls can be used only by 8 people in total

Clarifies: Distinction drawn between acceptable and unacceptable teleports: zone-bound, lore-justified transports are fine; an inventory-bound free teleport is not.

[Torta (staff), 2020-03-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5435#p5435) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Change / Remove Portable Meeting Stones"

#### 2021-02-01 — Stat budget values

> %1 Dodge is equal to 11.5 stat budget. %1 Parry is equal to 15.65 stat budget ... %1 Block is equal to 5.65 ... %1 Melee Critical Chance is equal to 14 ... %1 Spell Critical Chance is equal to 11.3 ... %1 Melee Hit Chance is equal to 9.55

Clarifies: Turtle's stat-budget table for custom items: 1% Dodge = 11.5, 1% Parry = 15.65 (because of parry haste), 1% Block = 5.65, 1% Melee Crit = 14, 1% Spell Crit = 11.3, 1% Melee Hit = 9.55, 1% Spell Hit = 11, +1 Weapon Skill assumed 3.

[Dragunovi (staff), 2021-02-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=9270#p9270) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "The Ultimate Guide to Itemmaking"

#### 2021-12-02 — Item rebalancing philosophy

> Unique item design is one of the strongest parts of Vanilla ... Even if we want to preserve this feeling there are items that are too weak for their qualities, that end up sharded whenever they drop. This is very prevelant on crafted items too
