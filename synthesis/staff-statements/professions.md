## Statements

#### 2019-05-06 — Survival secondary skill

> We're bringing back another discontinued feature of Classic World of Warcraft: the old Secondary Skill: Survival ... How to build a [Traveler's Tent] to have some rest during your journey. The tent lasts 20 minutes and gives you a resting bonus

Clarifies: Survival is revived as a secondary skill (max 150) giving Dim Torch (+4 spirit party), Fishing Boat (+50 fishing), Traveler's Tent (rested XP) and Bright Campfire (+8 spirit party).

[Torta (staff), 2019-05-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2694#p2694) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.12.1+ — Survival Skill"

#### 2019-05-11 — Survival secondary skill

> Bright Campfire now will correctly give you +1 Survival Skill. Bright Campfire won't trigger PvP mode. Traveler's Tens radius will increase from 10 yards to 15 yards.

Clarifies: Fixes to Survival: Bright Campfire grants +1 Survival skill and no longer triggers PvP mode; tent radius raised from 10 to 15 yards.

[Torta (staff), 2019-05-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2765#p2765) · kind: `bug-confirmed` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.12.1+ — Survival Skill"

#### 2023-02-15 — BoP epic crafts

> All current and future Epic Bind on Pickup profession crafts now require the skill that's required to craft the item to equip. This has been done to avoid meta scenarios where the player could make these items which were intended to be the personal bonus for leveling up the profession

Clarifies: All current and future epic BoP profession crafts require the crafting skill to equip, so players cannot swap professions to collect the personal reward of every profession.

[Torta (staff), 2023-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=34787#p34787) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "2023 — February 15"

#### 2023-06-26 — Tents

> tents are not racial. Yes, they have factional differences in appearance and nothing more. This is the profession you learn in Stranglethorn valley and other than rest points you can't get any buffs.

Clarifies: Tents differ only cosmetically by faction, are learned through the Survival profession in Stranglethorn and grant no buff beyond rest.

[Pepesmite (staff), 2023-06-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51349#p51349) · kind: `working-as-intended` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "More tents?"

#### 2023-07-11 — Belt buckles

> they were never intended to be Blacksmith only and it was merely a case of miscommunication on our part.

Clarifies: Belt buckles were never intended to be blacksmith-only; the restriction was a miscommunication.

[Dragunovi (staff), 2023-07-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=53007#p53007) · kind: `bug-confirmed` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Profession Changes and Additons"

#### 2023-11-15 — Instance mining nodes

> Nodes will now have a prohibitively high respawn timer, similar to other Vanilla dungeons (Wailing Caverns, Blackrock Depths, etc.).

Clarifies: Small Thorium Veins in Hateforge Quarry given a prohibitively long respawn like other vanilla dungeon nodes.

[Jamey (staff), 2023-11-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77428#p77428) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "2023 — November 15"

#### 2024-01-23 — Plaguebloom in Stratholme

> Plaguebloom is now found growing within Stratholme. However, it's important to note that these herb nodes will not respawn, as is typical for instanced content.

Clarifies: Plaguebloom added inside Stratholme with non-respawning nodes, as an economy measure.

[Torta (staff), 2024-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85725#p85725) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "2024 — January 24"

#### 2024-02-06 — Mining nodes

> Rich Thorium Veins now share spawn locations with regular Thorium Veins. This does not include nodes found in instances.

Clarifies: Rich Thorium Veins share spawn points with regular Thorium Veins outside instances.

[Akalix (staff), 2024-02-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=87129#p87129) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "PvP & Economy Update"

#### 2024-03-19 — Dynamic gathering nodes

> we now have moved our herbalism and mining nodes onto a dynamic respawning system ... Resource nodes found inside instances are exempt from this system.

Clarifies: Herbalism, mining and high-tier fishing pools moved to a dynamic respawn system to raise supply and lower consumable prices; instance nodes are exempt.

[Akalix (staff), 2024-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90746#p90746) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Economy Update — Nodes, Trash & Consumables"

#### 2024-04-23 — Black Lotus

> Increased the Black Lotus drop rate from low-level herbs to 4% from 2%.

Clarifies: Black Lotus secondary drop chance from Dreamfoil, Mountain Silversage, Plaguebloom and Icecap raised from 2% to 4% (earlier raised from 0.5% to 1%).
