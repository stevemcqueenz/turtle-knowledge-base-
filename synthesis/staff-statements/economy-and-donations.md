## Statements

#### 2018-09-20 — Auction house

> This is an interesting concept but we don't plan to remove major blizzlike features. Remember, this server is also giving PvE players a new home who rely on the Auction House.

Clarifies: Major blizzlike features such as the auction house will not be removed.

[Torta (staff), 2018-09-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=167#p167) · kind: `policy` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Action House"

#### 2019-03-31 — Shop philosophy

> we tried to find non-gamebreaking, non-p2w options for our shop, players want functional items, not just plain cosmetic items. Most of our new features are free and available for everyone.

Clarifies: Convenience shop items (portable mailbox, auction house) exist because players wanted functional rather than purely cosmetic rewards; the team frames them as non-gamebreaking and non-p2w.

[Torta (staff), 2019-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2413#p2413) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Suggestions"

#### 2019-04-08 — Shop philosophy

> I'll remove portable mailbox from the shop, and repair bot will no longer sell reagents. I wish AH bot to stay tho

Clarifies: After feedback the portable mailbox was pulled from the shop and the repair bot stopped selling reagents; the portable auction house was kept.

[Torta (staff), 2019-04-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2468#p2468) · kind: `policy` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Suggestions"

#### 2021-09-27 — Funding model

> There is absolutely no way a private server can survive with a subscription model ... Also, lawsuit would be immediately at my door. Blizzard is tolerating a lot of things, but this would be just too much.

Clarifies: Why there is a cosmetic shop rather than a subscription: a sub model would collapse the player base and invite legal action.

#### 2023-10-18 — Farming design principle

> High-end instanced farming should ideally require a team of at least two or more players and solo farming with a single class should not surpass the efficiency of group farming with multiple players of different classes. This is to encourage social interaction within the community

Clarifies: Stated principle: high-end instanced farming should need two or more players, and solo single-class farming should not beat mixed group farming.

#### 2024-04-17 — Funding and staffing

> for many years now, everyone here has been paid ... it's safe to say that 99% of our team receives compensation ... even then, we won't resort to introducing pay-to-win elements. The worst-case scenario if we no longer have funding is that development will stall, but the server will continue to run.

Clarifies: Almost the entire ~100-200 person team is paid from donation-shop revenue; recruitment of new modders was stopped for cost reasons; if funding fails, development stalls but the server keeps running and pay-to-win is still excluded.

#### 2024-06-25 — GDKP ban rationale

> we opted to ban GDKP outright rather than fight the losing battle of preventing RMT in a setting where the idea of earned progression is replaced by "affording" progression. Players are free to use whatever loot system they wish, so long as progression isn't being sold.

Clarifies: GDKP was banned outright rather than policed because it is directly linked to gold buying and replaces earned progression with afforded progression; every other loot system is allowed as long as progression is not sold.
