## Statements

#### 2018-09-05 — World PvP toggle

> You can experience the world in a PvP or PvE setting by using our custom, in game command to turn PvP ON or OFF at any time! .pvp off and you're safe! ... Final versions of PvP sets will be available from the start. No waiting until 1.11!

Clarifies: World PvP is opt-in through the .pvp command; final PvP sets available from launch; WSG open from level 1.

[Torta (staff), 2018-09-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14#p14) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Welcome to Turtle WoW!"

#### 2018-09-10 — BG queueing from taverns

> Here on Turtle WoW we prefer a "chaotic" and random system so everyone has a chance to do BGs. The alternate solution would be a command like ".joinbg" which is less lore friendly in our point of view.

Clarifies: Battleground queueing from taverns (rather than a command or battlemasters only) was chosen to keep BGs open to casual players instead of dedicated rankers; a .joinbg command was rejected as less lore-friendly.

[Torta (staff), 2018-09-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=36#p36) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Innkeeper Battleground"

#### 2019-01-10 — Gurubashi Arena tournament

> To prevent group camping, you won't earn Bloody Coins if you are in a group.

Clarifies: The Bloody Tournament gave doubled honor in the Battle Ring and Bloody Coins for kills, with a no-group rule to stop camping; later the honor rate was returned to blizzlike and the item/XP vendor removed.

[Torta (staff), 2019-01-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1812#p1812) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Bloody Tournament"

#### 2019-01-17 — Dishonorable ranks

> If you lower your honor rank to Pariah, nothing will change, apart if having a long way back if you ever decide to get back to positive ranking. If you lower reputation rank with any faction where it's possible, then yes, nobody will trade you anything ... It's mostly visual

Clarifies: Negative honor rank (Pariah) has no gameplay effect on Turtle; only negative faction reputation makes NPCs refuse trade and attack.

[Torta (staff), 2019-01-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1883#p1883) · kind: `working-as-intended` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.12.1+ — Dishonorable Ranks"

#### 2020-09-19 — Server ruleset

> We made this server for PvE folks who don't like World PvP. I highly doubt they'd change their mind. Not worth time implementing :) + with our population it'd be like handing rewards for free.
