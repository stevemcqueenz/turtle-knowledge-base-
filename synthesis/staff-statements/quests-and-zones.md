## Statements

#### 2018-10-10 — Language learning

> Blizzard had a language skill system in one of the Alpha client versions but it was never finished. We will setup as suggested a quest chain if we manage to find a technical solution.

Clarifies: A language-learning system was planned from the start, modelled on an unfinished Blizzard alpha feature, to be delivered as a quest chain if technically feasible.

[Bowser (staff), 2018-10-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=708#p708) · kind: `roadmap` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "About languages and cross-faction chat once more."

#### 2019-10-25 — Mirage Raceway

> I can't recall them saying anything about it, but we've found a number of unused spells related to the raceway in the client files ... Thus, we assumed, it was initially planed as a player controlled raceway.

Clarifies: The player-driven Mirage Raceway was inferred from unused client spells and items (Gnome/Goblin Prize Boxes, Car Keys), not from a Blizzard statement.

[Torta (staff), 2019-10-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4296#p4296) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.12.1+ —  Mirage Raceway"

#### 2021-08-09 — Playable races

> No plans for new playable races yet, it thanks a long time and we still have to complete goblins, having new sets of animations, making all items fit and a lot of other things are to be considered for playable races.

Clarifies: No new playable races were planned in 2021 because each needs animation sets and item fitting; Hyjal was named as a future zone.

[Gheor (staff), 2021-08-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11578#p11578) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "New races and zones"

#### 2021-08-10 — Playable races

> Void Elf is an abomination that future expansion brought so they could add High Elves to Alliance, that's not what we want or need here.

Clarifies: Void Elves and Half-Elves rejected as later-expansion inventions with no place on Turtle.

[Gheor (staff), 2021-08-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11595#p11595) · kind: `policy` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "New races and zones"

#### 2021-12-20 — Hidden content

> Icepoint Rock is a secret location with content that you will have to figure out how to go there yourselves ... It's Mysteries of Azeroth for a reason.

Clarifies: Some locations (Icepoint Rock, a secret sewer entrance to Stormwind Vault) are deliberately undocumented.

[Dragunovi (staff), 2021-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14667#p14667) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.16.0 — Mysteries of Azeroth"

#### 2021-12-20 — Lore gatekeeping

> The Night Elves would not and will not take magic, especially not after the events of the Sundering, we try to look at it from the lore perspective. Night Elf Mages shouldn't exist.

Clarifies: Night Elf Mages rejected on lore grounds: only the corrupted Shen'drelar Highborne use arcane magic after the Sundering.

[Gheor (staff), 2021-12-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14676#p14676) · kind: `policy` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Night Elf Mages"

#### 2022-02-08 — Quest design philosophy

> So not delivering all details on a silver platter is anti-intuitive. Since when do quests need to tell you the exact area you need for the objective to be complete? You forget these quests exist in Vanilla too, you're just too used to the Questie or pfQuest.

Clarifies: Custom quests intentionally withhold exact coordinates; the team argues players are used to Questie/pfQuest rather than vanilla-style directions.

[Gheor (staff), 2022-02-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16839#p16839) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Custom Quests Feedback"

#### 2022-07-11 — Lore discipline

> If we pull up lore out of the magical hat every time it suits us we will only walk in the same wrong direction as others did.

Clarifies: Stated lore discipline: not inventing convenient lore justifications on demand.

[Gheor (staff), 2022-07-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19884#p19884) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Troll Warlocks?"

#### 2025-01-07 — Flight master camping

> Flight Masters will now grow stronger each time they die and respawn to discourage extended farming/camping.

Clarifies: Flight masters grow stronger each time they die and respawn, to discourage camping and farming them.

#### 2025-02-18 — Rare spawn system

> No more than 2 rare mobs are allowed on a location at a time ... This means that the chance of Bjarn appearing is 1/5 ... rare creatures do not have a clear spawn time. It can be really 45 minutes and can be 8 hours.

Clarifies: Rare spawn mechanics: at most two rares per zone at a time chosen from the zone's pool, weighted by zone population, with no fixed respawn timer.
