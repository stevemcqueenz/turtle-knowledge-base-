#### 2018-11-14 — Mode selection window

> Hardcore mode will now only be available up until level 10. If you do not select it by this level, you will remain on normal mode without the option to change ... Riding Skill will not return as hardcore reward.

Clarifies: Hardcore mode selection was limited to level 10 and below; riding skill was ruled out as a hardcore reward.

#### 2019-04-07 — Turtle Mode (Slow & Steady)

> XP from creature kills — both in the open world and dungeons is reduced by 50%. Quest XP stays the same ... Additionally, with the next patch, XP gained from creature kills in dungeons will be halved. This change comes in response to a growing number of players using dungeon speed-leveling to exploit the donation system.

Clarifies: Turtle/Slow & Steady mode rules: 50% reduced creature XP, quest XP unchanged, 5% XP loss on death outside BG/PvP. Later edit halves dungeon creature XP citing donation-system exploitation via dungeon speed-leveling.

[Torta (staff), 2019-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2459#p2459) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Leveling Challenge — Slow & Steady"

#### 2020-06-27 — Rested XP / tents

> Hardcore Mode was not Hardcore at all. Players still could use rested XP from tents and level on x1 instead of going through hardcore adventure. We didn't want to close an important and fun social interaction such as "Tent Party" for Hardcore players so I had to come up with a compromise.

Clarifies: Reason for the Turtle Mode XP changes: rested XP from tents let players level at effectively x1, defeating the challenge; the compromise preserves the social 'Tent Party'.

#### 2026-02-17 — Rested XP / tents

> Logically, a cozy tavern feels way better for a break than a tent out in the woods. The tents at Stormwind and Orgrimmar's gates have been a mess for ages ... you can now get the same rested XP bonus wherever there's a tavern in the world ... Tents in 1.18.1 will act like the new taverns but won't give you the full rested bonus

Clarifies: 1.18.1 moves rested XP from tents to taverns worldwide: a tavern now gives roughly the pre-1.18.1 tent rate, tents give a partial bonus, and offline tavern rested gain is unchanged. Reason: taverns fit the fiction better and the capital-gate tent camps had become a mess.
