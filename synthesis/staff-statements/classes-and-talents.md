## Druid

#### 2021-09-03 — Race/class combos

> Undead Hunter will be a thing with the next patch ... As for Human Hunter we are sadly limited by the 6 classes/race limit that's in the character creation screen ... Everyone has a different way to reason for any class/race combo but there must be some logic and it must not break the lore.

Clarifies: Race/class combinations are gated by lore consistency and the client's six-class character-creation limit; Undead Hunters and Dwarf Mages were approved, Troll and Dwarf Druids rejected as Cataclysm lore.

[Gheor (staff), 2021-09-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=11958#p11958) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Expand available race/class options with expansion"

#### 2023-08-08 — Druid — Tree of Life

> Reduced the healing bonus from Tree of Life aura to only 20% of Spirit.

Clarifies: Tree of Life aura healing bonus reduced to 20% of Spirit.

[Torta (staff), 2023-08-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=56424#p56424) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "2023 — August 9"

#### 2024-10-12 — Druid

> we removed the attack speed slow and moved it to the Balance tree as a talent. This allowed us to reduce Hurricane's cooldown by half, while also strengthening its scaling with the Spell Power stat. Tranquility is an iconic spell from Warcraft 3 ... its World of Warcraft counterpart sadly does not live up to its legacy.

Clarifies: Druid design goals in Class Changes 2: Hurricane's attack-speed slow moved to a Balance talent so its cooldown could be halved and its spell-power scaling raised; Tranquility reworked because the vanilla version does not live up to its Warcraft 3 legacy; feral dodge made to generate rage.

[Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102966#p102966) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.17.2 & Beyond — Class & Gameplay Changes"

#### 2024-10-12 — Druid — Feral tanking

> While blocking generates rage for warriors, dodging does not for druids. Still, they cannot avoid accumulating this stat due to how Agility grants additional dodge chance.

Clarifies: Bear druids get rage from dodge because Agility forces dodge on them while, unlike warrior block, dodge generated nothing; Feral Adrenaline gives a reaction window after a crit.

[Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102966#p102966) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.17.2 & Beyond — Class & Gameplay Changes"

## Paladin

#### 2019-10-26 — Faction class restrictions

> This is not possible without major and very complicated client and server modifications. So the answer is 100% no

Clarifies: Alliance shamans and Horde paladins were ruled out in 2019 as requiring major client and server modification.

#### 2021-11-11 — Paladin — Judgement of Justice taunt

> This is intentional ... the intention was to give them a tool to handle tank swaps, not a free "face me" card basically, like how Druids are the jack of all trades, master of none, hybrids shouldn't be the best at everything and overshadow dedicated specialization classes

Clarifies: Paladin's taunt via Judgement of Justice is deliberately awkward: it is a tank-swap tool of last resort, not a free taunt, so hybrids do not overshadow dedicated tanks.

[Dragunovi (staff), 2021-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=13764#p13764) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Final Changelog for Class Changes coming in 1.16.1"

> 30 minutes would be much longer than I think fits. One example for needing to adapt the buffs would be hunters, as Might/Kings/Wisdom all can be viable depending on the pull.

Clarifies: Extending blessing duration to 30 minutes was resisted because swapping Might/Kings/Wisdom per pull is meaningful; the team preferred to change Paladins elsewhere.

[Akalix (staff), 2022-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23491#p23491) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Pally Buff durations from 5 > 30 min"

> Judgement of Light healing will now be done by the unit that's attacking as is blizzlike. This means the paladin will no longer get flagged for PvP when another flagged player attacks the same mob.

Clarifies: Judgement of Light healing is credited to the attacking unit as in retail vanilla, so the paladin no longer gets PvP-flagged when a flagged player attacks the same mob.

[Torta (staff), 2022-09-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24112#p24112) · kind: `bug-confirmed` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "2022 — August 24-30"

#### 2024-10-12 — Paladin

> The design of the Paladin class in Vanilla is often considered unfinished ... Just before release, both Crusader Strike and Holy Strike were removed ... Holy Strike ... was also quite unbalanced in scenarios with percentage damage increases or in player combat.

Clarifies: Paladin history and rationale: vanilla Paladin is treated as unfinished (Crusader Strike and Holy Strike cut before release); Turtle's restored beta Holy Strike proved unbalanced with percentage damage increases and in PvP, and beta Crusader Strike produced a downrank-spam loop, so both were rebuilt.

[Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102969#p102969) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.17.2 & Beyond — Class & Gameplay Changes"

Clarifies: The Judgement of Justice taunt experiment was judged clunky (two GCDs and heavy mana to return to rotation) and replaced with a baseline paladin taunt.

[Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102969#p102969) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.17.2 & Beyond — Class & Gameplay Changes"

Clarifies: Reckoning was rebuilt around blocking rather than being critically struck, which the team calls a failed vanilla design because tanks do not want to be crit.

[Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102969#p102969) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.17.2 & Beyond — Class & Gameplay Changes"

#### 2023-07-25 — Shaman armor

> That's never going to happen. We do not intend to make drastic changes to the original concept of the classes. Shamans use leather and chain mail thats all.

Clarifies: Shamans will never wear plate; the team refuses drastic changes to the original class concepts, and supports shaman tanking through items instead.

#### 2024-10-12 — Shaman — Windfury Totem

> its Vanilla implementation was too big of a percentage of Shaman's overall output, pushing their personal performance down while it also made other temporary weapon enchants like sharpening stones or rogue poisons irrelevant ... Enhancement performed as an overwhelmingly strong outlier ... by pulling ahead of every single dps spec in the game by several hundreds of damage per second.

Clarifies: Windfury Totem was cut back because in vanilla it made up too much of the shaman's contribution while making other temporary weapon enchants irrelevant; Windfury Weapon was nerfed after Enhancement tested hundreds of DPS ahead of every other spec.

Clarifies: Bloodlust reworked to buff the shaman first with party support scaling off the shaman's own contribution; stated itemization principle that set bonuses should add to a class's strengths, not be their foundation (Stormcaller 5-piece removed).

[Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102972#p102972) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Patch 1.17.2 & Beyond — Class & Gameplay Changes"
