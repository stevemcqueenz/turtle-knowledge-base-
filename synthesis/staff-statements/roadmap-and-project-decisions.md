## Statements

#### 2018-09-05 — Founding design goals

> The main thing in our project will be PvE, role play and communication. We are considering reducing the experience rates for killing mobs (x0.8) ... A shop will be available for cosmetic items ... Nothing will be pay to win or give advantages to a player!

Clarifies: Founding announcement: PvE/RP focus, reduced kill XP, staggered raid release, cosmetic-only shop, opt-in world PvP via .pvp command.

[Torta (staff), 2018-09-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14#p14) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Welcome to Turtle WoW!"

#### 2018-09-14 — Level capping

> this feature would actually force people to play in a timeframe, without taking their time ... Another reason is overpopulation of start areas, which could cause problems for leveling in general -- the game wasn't designed this way

Clarifies: A weekly level cap was considered and rejected: it would force people onto a schedule and overcrowd starting zones, contrary to a project built for unhurried play.

[Torta (staff), 2018-09-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95#p95) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Turtle WoW Questions & AMA — Answers!"

#### 2018-10-03 — XP rates at launch

> The final rates will be the following: Quest rates: x1, Creature kill rates: x0.5, Rested XP bonus: x0.5

Clarifies: Launch rates set from beta: quests x1, creature kills x0.5, rested XP bonus x0.5, with WSG open from the 1-9 bracket.

[Torta (staff), 2018-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=397#p397) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Beta Conclusion & Final Rates"

#### 2018-10-08 — Blizzlike boundary

> the problem is that it will draw us further away from "Blizzlike" gameplay mechanics ... This is in my point of view no option for Turtle WoW because we would start to introduce major Wotlk+ features on a Vanilla server.

Clarifies: Early framing of the project's limits: convenience features risk drifting from blizzlike, cross-faction functions should be made lore-friendly (e.g. learning the other faction's language), and WotLK-era features are off the table.

[Bowser (staff), 2018-10-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=575#p575) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "First impressions/Thoughtful suggestions"

#### 2018-10-27 — Early retention

> Almost 8000 characters have been created, yet fewer than 500 have reached level 20. We must ask — have we made things too slow?

Clarifies: Early retention data drove the rate rethink: about 8,000 characters created but fewer than 500 past level 20, with low rates and lack of cross-faction interaction cited by leavers.

[Torta (staff), 2018-10-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=925#p925) · kind: `reasoning` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Gameplay Experience Feedback"

#### 2018-10-29 — Progressive XP rates

> Experience rates for killing mobs are generally reduced to x0.8 ... Having "grey" quests that give no experience won't be a reason to leave the zone ... they will still give the full experience up to 25 levels of difference.

Clarifies: Progressive kill-XP brackets (0.7-0.9 by level range) were introduced so quests would carry levelling; grey and green quests keep full XP up to 25 levels of difference. Superseded in March 2020 when default rates went to x1 and the brackets were dropped.

[Torta (staff), 2018-10-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=990#p990) · kind: `intent` · era: **pre-1.18.1** · topic: "Progressive Rates"

#### 2019-06-03 — Player housing

> If we ever make player houses, we build them into open world, next to the crowded places such as Goldshire, Razor Hill etc ... So the question is not "How" the question is "Why".

Clarifies: Housing, if built, would be placed in the open world near starting hubs rather than phased or instanced; the team's objection was purpose, not feasibility.

#### 2020-10-03 — Convenience features
