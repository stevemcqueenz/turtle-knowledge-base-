# Level One Lunatic (Turtle WoW challenge)

Discord-sourced guide to Turtle WoW's **Level One Lunatic** challenge, mined from the
archived `#level-one-lunatic` channel (5,605 messages, 444 authors, 2025-02-15 → 2026-05-30).
Every claim carries a `[[d:level-one-lunatic#<id>]]` citation; the cited records are in
`structured/discord/evidence-level-one-lunatic.jsonl`. Era is derived from the message date:
**pre-1.18.1** (< 2025-10-03), **1.18.1-announced-pre-release** (2025-10-03 … 2026-03-19),
**post-1.18.1** (≥ 2026-03-20). Agreement is `consensus`, `single source`, or `contested`.
Authority tiers: Discord has no green-name signal, so **Cysthen** (guild master of
`<Level One Lunatic>` on Nordanaar, forum guide author, main raid organiser) and
**StabsMcKenzy** (co-founder, early guide writer, first *Wee Warlord*) are treated as
guide-writer tier; all other authors are players unless corroborated. This channel is the
sole source for this document; no forum citation is superseded here.

## 1. What the challenge is

| Fact | Detail | Agreement / era | Source |

## 3. Native level-1 kits (all nine classes)

At level 1 a character knows only its **native abilities** plus, at most, **one trainer spell**.
Cysthen's per-class summary [[d:level-one-lunatic#1450210987541266443]] (consensus,
1.18.1-announced-pre-release) is reproduced below.

| Class | Native level-1 abilities |
|---|---|
| Warrior | Heroic Strike, Battle Shout, Shoot |
| Paladin | Seal of Righteousness, Holy Light, Devotion Aura (55 Armor) |
| Hunter | Auto Shot, Raptor Strike, Track Beasts |
| Rogue | Stealth (from trainer), Sinister Strike, Eviscerate |
| Warlock | Demon Armor, Shadow Bolt, Immolate, Imp pet (Firebolt) |
| Mage | Arcane Intellect, Fireball, Ice Armor |
| Priest | Power Word: Fortitude (3 Stamina), Lesser Heal, Smite |
| Druid | Mark of the Wild (25 Armor), Healing Touch, Wrath |
| Shaman | Healing Wave, Lightning Bolt, Rockbiter (29 AP) |

Rogues do **not** start with Stealth — it is a class-trainer pickup — but no other class has a
level-1 trainer spell worth taking [[d:level-one-lunatic#1398980266650898432]] (single source,
pre-1.18.1).

## 4. Scaling constraints that define the meta

| Constraint | Numbers | Agreement / era | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agility → Dodge is class-dependent | +33 Agility gave a **Warrior 13%** Dodge but a **Rogue 29%**; Rogues reach **100%+ Dodge from gear enchants alone**, which is why the meta is "a Rogue challenge" | consensus, pre-1.18.1 | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1343245626569265325]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1344739729329094729]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1350141968356737078]] |
| Spell damage scales badly | Casters underperform in dungeons and spells **resist heavily in PvE** | consensus, pre-1.18.1 | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1347228841646489683]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1376219257691705485]] |
| Healing scales well | Pyropal: "huge scaling, no penalty"; but Paladin rank-1 Holy Light was shadow-nerfed **70% → 20%** coefficient on 2025-04-02 | single source, pre-1.18.1 | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1348200312380592169]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1356980754239717448]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1364436288081498225]] |
| Melee miss is brutal in PvE | **99% miss from the front / 50% from behind**; yellow instant attacks (Sinister Strike, Heroic Strike) hit better; **arrows and Fishing Rods effectively never miss** | consensus, 1.18.1-announced-pre-release | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1397962048205357189]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1423010798586040370]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1420076332842356869]] |
| PvP hit is not penalised like PvE | Tested at level 1 vs a 300-Defense level-60, nearly every white attack from behind landed | single source, 1.18.1-announced-pre-release | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1469520417289932822]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1469529099302076549]] |

The miss model is why **Fishing Rods became the PvE equaliser** for non-Rogue/Hunter classes and
why **Fishing is the single most valuable profession**
([[d:level-one-lunatic#1397962048205357189]]).

## 5. Classes, roles and raid composition

**Tier list:** Rogue, Paladin, Hunter [[d:level-one-lunatic#1354018623194402877]]; for
twink-vs-twink it is "basically just rogues" [[d:level-one-lunatic#1346662308285255852]]; "for the
challenge any class is viable" [[d:level-one-lunatic#1363243605250019339]] (consensus, pre-1.18.1).

**PvE role rundown:** "Hunters are the best dps, Rogues are good dps and needed to avoidance tank,
Any Healing Class … good at Healing, Warriors are good Tanks as Trolls … Casters are in a
questionable spot … Warlock > Smite > Mages [if resists were fixed]"
[[d:level-one-lunatic#1386896431389868124]] (consensus, pre-1.18.1).

| Role | Assessment | Agreement / era | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rogue | Best overall; instant strikes + Stealth; **100% Dodge avoidance tank**, but slower threat | consensus | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1386896431389868124]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1492172258414821466]] |
| Hunter | Top PvE DPS; **60–80% Dodge** in BiS (Night Elf up to 100% with full buffs); arrows do not miss; expensive Feather Arrows for burst | consensus | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1410072455581794435]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1478210926229323930]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1492175946235646174]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1355145395927515227]] |
| Paladin (healer) | Strong PvE healer: rank-1 heals have a **0.25 threat modifier vs 0.5** for other healers; strong Seal spell-power scaling; lowest-threat healer | consensus | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1369361795147370619]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1398456708237365450]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1450211855787098214]] |
| Troll Warrior (tank) | The **only viable Warrior race**: Spirit-stacking off-tank that regenerates health in combat and holds AoE threat via Battle Shout spam; expensive, needs near-BiS + external buffs | consensus | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1421489822354378862]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1492172258414821466]] |
| Healers (HoT throughput) | Ranking **Priest > Druid > Paladin > Shaman** | single source | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1450215473244274688]] |
| Warlock (caster) | Best caster DPS; **Immolate** has the best spell-damage scaling (+4/tick breakpoints, up to +16/cast); Imp Firebolt has 10% scaling but the pet does **not** inherit player spell damage | single source | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1414413087695704154]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1451016902154522624]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1414449422712176712]] |
| Mage | Least impactful overall; niche is PvP survivability/kiting with Ice Armor and WSG flag-running | single source | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1450210219450830959]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1398652196903587980]] |
| Druid (healer) | 2nd-best healing; wants **Tauren** for War Stomp (the bracket's only interrupt) | single source | [[d:level-one-lunatic#1450215473244274688]] |

**Meta comps.** Full-BiS raid: **3 Rogues, 1 Troll Warrior, 4 Hunters, 2 Healers**
[[d:level-one-lunatic#1410072455581794435]]; speed-run: **1 Troll Warrior, 3 Rogues, 2 Paladin
healers with Fishing Rods, 5 Hunters** [[d:level-one-lunatic#1421490487432450090]] (single source,
1.18.1-announced-pre-release).

**Popularity (2025-05-06, least → most):** Druid, Warlock, Mage, Paladin, Shaman, Warrior, Hunter,
Priest, "the vast majority being Rogue" [[d:level-one-lunatic#1369383451811250256]] (single source,
pre-1.18.1).

## 8. Professions

Only **four professions are learnable at level 1: Herbalism, Mining, Skinning, Fishing**
[[d:level-one-lunatic#1439063445868904449]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1397146935902601297]]
(consensus, 1.18.1-announced-pre-release). Crafting professions require **level 5**; Engineering is
not available at level 1 but is used **through mains/alts** for Goblin Rocket Boots, the Voice
Amplification Module, Sniper Scope, and (Horde) Dynamite for reputation
[[d:level-one-lunatic#1478317444354408448]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1398456246134378497]],
[[d:level-one-lunatic#1347140253575549021]].

**Fishing** is the level-1 gold engine and damage equaliser: it can reach **150 skill like level
19s**, and **Stonescale Eels** from Stranglethorn/Azshara fund the enchants. The **Arcanite Fishing
Pole** would be a BiS 2-hander but is considered inappropriate to add
[[d:level-one-lunatic#1439970572019699783]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1375132279076028616]].
Fishing was originally Alliance-only via a bugged trainer that lacked the level requirement, then
given to all trainers [[d:level-one-lunatic#1421531130993049741]],
[[d:level-one-lunatic#1439970572019699783]] (consensus, 1.18.1-announced-pre-release).

### 10.2 Battlegrounds and arena

- Bracket is **1–9 WSG/AB**; BGs historically almost never popped
  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1343272206553321495]]. AB is queued at the **Battlemaster** (not on the
  minimap) and can be forced to pop with enough sign-ups
  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1375133317292097647]] (consensus, pre-1.18.1).
- **AB is preferred over WSG:** "competitive Lunatic PVP in WSG can very easily turn into Flag
  Holding forever, whereas Arathi Basin supports more team play … not to mention better Reputation
  for Diplomat" [[d:level-one-lunatic#1375133988569612288]] (single source, pre-1.18.1).
- **WSG flaggers:** a **Troll Mage with Frost Armor** (high Stamina/Spirit build) was the tested
  best; Mage generally is the best flag runner
  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1355146740428308643]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1350825971254689872]]
  (single source, pre-1.18.1).
- **Dueling:** a full-BiS Paladin wins vs most; Rogue bandaging can stall forever; Hunter Feather
  Arrows are the untested counter [[d:level-one-lunatic#1354018623194402877]] (single source,
  pre-1.18.1).

### 10.3 Post-1.18.0 PvP collapse

Removing Warmode from character creation removed the community's only open-world gameplay loop:
"most Lunatics have no reason to stay online unless they're fishing for Gold or farming
Reputation" [[d:level-one-lunatic#1421070776290902036]]. Kills collapsed; Alliance went from
~200/week to near nothing [[d:level-one-lunatic#1405014881929007175]] (consensus,
1.18.1-announced-pre-release).

## 13. Community and infrastructure

- **Server:** effectively all Lunatics are on **Nordanaar**; the guild `<Level One Lunatic>` is
  based in **Booty Bay** and grew past **300–400** (2025-10) to ~**500** members (2026-03).
  Ambershire/Tel'abim lack the required enchants (15 Agi gloves until AQ phase) and cross-faction
  PvE [[d:level-one-lunatic#1487901304251486309]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1494799174724489457]],
  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1427230626175516744]] (consensus, 1.18.1-announced-pre-release).
- **Schedule:** weekly NA (Monday) and EU (Tuesday) raids; member-hosted PvP events
  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1449780544278761575]].
- **Tooling:** Lunatic raid-log site, vanity-pet leaderboard, guild-Discord class guides, and
  TofuGaming's 5-part YouTube beginner series (2026-04)
  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1458112168334463168]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1500639473447337984]]
  (consensus, post-1.18.1).

  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1489330540040552660]] (single source, post-1.18.1).

## 14. Contested and uncertain

- **Multiboxing is deeply contested.** Cysthen/the guild argue it is essential for PvE viability
  (especially Troll Warrior buffs), fairness toward players who joined under the old rules, and
  normal community play [[d:level-one-lunatic#1395514874791989328]],
  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1411055981210763274]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1462938035665637376]]; players
  such as Destroses/Thirtyseven/Mr. Berold argue it violates the challenge's spirit — "if you aren't
  able to do it on your level one alone, you shouldn't be able to achieve it at all"
  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1395508637413146705]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1395540130659565661]],
  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1400889304309436628]]. Both sides are in the record (contested,
  pre-1.18.1).
- **Horde vs Alliance Exalted asymmetry:** Horde can buy Exalted via Durotar Labor Union Dynamite
  (~400 g, ~9,000 turn-ins); Alliance faced a ~40 h Centaur grind. Cysthen calls the asymmetry
  unfair [[d:level-one-lunatic#1398456246134378497]], [[d:level-one-lunatic#1409969580239093770]]
  (single source, pre-1.18.1).
- **Holy Light coefficient nerf** (70% → 20%, 2025-04-02) is a single-source (Cysthen) claim,
  corroborated by his own healing-math post [[d:level-one-lunatic#1356980754239717448]],
  [[d:level-one-lunatic#1364436288081498225]] (single source, pre-1.18.1).
