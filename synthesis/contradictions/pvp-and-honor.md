## CON-0076 — Honor cut to 10%: "purely a visual change" vs player compensation reports

- **Side A.** "As some Players were gaining hundreds of thousands of Honor a week the above change is simply to keep numbers in line and purely a visual change" — costs were rescaled to match. — [Jamey (staff), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169701#p169701)
- **Side B.** Players report the one-time bonus as inadequate (R10 ~30k, R13 ~60k, R14 ~98k) against set prices of T1 ~5-6k and T2 ~22k per piece, and entry weapons raised by ~10k honor without a note. — [Gnomeoperative (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171910#p171910), [amanagor (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171915#p171915), [Saronsen (player), 2026-03-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172996#p172996)
- **What would settle it.** Vendor prices and honor award values in the 1.18.1 data, compared against the pre-patch values.
- **Assessment (`unresolved`).** Both can be true: the ratio may be preserved while the one-time conversion is not. Unresolved in the corpus; all player numbers are unconfirmed.

## Discord `pvp-general` — systems evidence, era notes and supersession

The numbered entries above are forum-derived. The archived Turtle WoW Europe Discord channel **`#pvp-general`** (266,785 messages, 2021-05-24 → 2025-11-01; scope `staging/pvp-general-a`) adds the pre-1.18.1 systems detail behind several of them. It is **not** a new numbered entry set and is not mirrored in `structured/contradictions.yaml`; the machine-readable form of all of it is [`../../structured/pvp.yaml`](../../structured/pvp.yaml). Citations are `[[d:pvp-general#<id>]]` and resolve in `structured/discord/evidence-pvp-general.jsonl`. Era key: `pre-1.18.1` (< 2025-10-03), `1.18.1-announced-pre-release` (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), `post-1.18.1` (≥ 2026-03-20). **The channel ends 2025-11-01, so it contains no `post-1.18.1` material: every 1.18.1 statement it holds is pre-release.** Agreement: `consensus` | `single source` | `contested`.

### Honour, rank and the ranking economy (related to CON-0078, CON-0079)
- Turtle ran the **vanilla weekly-standings system** with a weekly flush; RP was capped at **~13,000/week** and **R14 needed ~60,000 RP** ("R14 is 60k RP"), so even a perfect top-bracket week cannot finish in fewer than ~5–6 weeks. A multi-R14 player still defends the system ("I love the ranking system"), so "everyone hates ranking" is not a universal consensus. [[d:pvp-general#1115908001480572938]], [[d:pvp-general#1116048119692529774]] (`single source` / `contested`, pre-1.18.1).
- **Decay, not the raw requirement, was the binding constraint** for casual rankers: "Currently 50k/week you are stuck r9 forever"; "increasing the decay would literally make R14 impossible". KB CON-0078 records the 2023-10-01 staff change that stopped decay de-ranking; Discord shows the pre-change squeeze. [[d:pvp-general#1116050834250289262]], [[d:pvp-general#1116040045258883155]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- **Pool boosting mechanics:** each faction had its own bracket ladder; low brackets were padded with **"dummy rankers"** (inactive accounts), a single HK/week was enough to enter the pool, and ~800 dummy rankers sat in the lower brackets in 2022 ("1 hk to get above the 800 dummy rankers"). KB staff say pool boosting is allowed but honor boosting is bannable. [[d:pvp-general#1017095976487505920]], [[d:pvp-general#998635211430567966]], [[d:pvp-general#1033363878735786114]], [[d:pvp-general#1028662404265037824]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- **Rank is level-gated at the low end:** "If you're below a certain level you are capped at rank 3" (exact cut-off unstated). [[d:pvp-general#1422945827495870554]] (`single source`, 1.18.1-announced-pre-release).
- A **no-honour glyph** (honour gain cut to 1–5%) was introduced so already-ranked players could queue without distorting the pool. [[d:pvp-general#921800202749370379]] (staff-register post, pre-1.18.1).
- **Per-kill honour formula** (the KB's listed gap): honour per kill is modified by level difference, victim rank, the number of nearby group/raid members, and a **25% diminishing return for repeated kills of the same player within 24 h**, with **100% → 75% → …** falloff and the award **shared among all nearby players**. Observed values: ~455 honour per WSG flag capture, ~400–500 per open-world War Mode kill. [[d:pvp-general#1212658441215287326]], [[d:pvp-general#1119777672583708753]], [[d:pvp-general#1106205535763714178]], [[d:pvp-general#1059641996056203347]], [[d:pvp-general#1260222976465305642]] (`single source` on coefficients, `consensus` on the DR, pre-1.18.1).

### Battleground objectives and queue behaviour (related to CON-0081)
- **Daily Call to Arms rotation** doubles win honour; on WSG/Blood Ring days the losing side also gets some. By mid-2025 all BGs except Sunnyglade Valley ran regularly; players tracked the rotation with the third-party `turtle-timer` site. Which BG pops depends on the daily — AB/WSG days almost always pop, custom-BG days are random. [[d:pvp-general#1383395973497094226]], [[d:pvp-general#1380460999785578498]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- **WSG flag carriers are slowed 30%** to force contact (the same anti-stall mechanism as the 1.18.0 stacking FC debuff) [[d:pvp-general#1006068996988215387]]. Turtle **staff** give their rationale for the jump/parkour spots that CON-0081 covers player-side: keeping them makes WSG "a glorified parkour gym" that punishes newcomers, removing them upsets veterans, so the middle ground is to **discourage** rather than remove. [[d:pvp-general#1381882667485888514]], [[d:pvp-general#1381891955088166912]] (staff, pre-1.18.1).
- **AV class-agnostic map knowledge:** IBGY is far more defensible than SHGY; Horde warmasters AoE harder than the Alliance NPC versions; Alliance bunkers could be capped from a second-floor weapon rack; the aid tent/SPGY can be safespotted by hugging the mountainside past IWB. AV was the honour-efficiency outlier and was repeatedly nerfed. [[d:pvp-general#1015344576342523935]], [[d:pvp-general#1015391947927588935]], [[d:pvp-general#1015315873084493935]], [[d:pvp-general#1119713704854945983]], [[d:pvp-general#1059889524404129945]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- **Sunnyglade Valley was chronically underplayed** and staff polled the channel on removing it before the 1.18.1 notes disabled it. [[d:pvp-general#1381904693474758696]], [[d:pvp-general#1381904006032658533]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1 / 1.18.1-announced-pre-release).
- **Premade/group-queue status stays contested** (same dispute as the forum "Ghost Queue Mafia"): "Premades are disabled on BGs on Turtle" (2023-04-06) vs "Premades are not disabled" (2023-07-27), with semi-premades and an Alliance premade still reported into 2025. Treat as a changing group-queue policy, not a clean on/off. [[d:pvp-general#1093569073578770566]], [[d:pvp-general#1134177832835240007]], [[d:pvp-general#1089541071136112793]], [[d:pvp-general#1341466559645745223]], [[d:pvp-general#1091769380863483985]] (`contested`, pre-1.18.1).

### Dampening (related to CON-0082)
- Discord quotes the 1.18.0 notes and gives **staff scope clarifications**: the universal BG/arena buff/debuff reduces incoming physical damage, magical damage, healing and shields; it is **not class-specific in 1.18.0** and applies **only to BGs/arenas** (not open world); "Shields"/"Absorbs" means shielding abilities (PW:S etc.), not shield items. Staff rationale: stop PvP being "who can explode the other person instantly first" without handing it to sustain classes; numbers were not known to staff when announced. [[d:pvp-general#1396098240633638942]], [[d:pvp-general#1381838614748594248]], [[d:pvp-general#1381837473058914445]], [[d:pvp-general#1381890754753859654]], [[d:pvp-general#1381890266377621575]], [[d:pvp-general#1381887792241578014]] (staff + player-quoted notes, pre-1.18.1).
- **Player reception was heavily negative:** no visible buff/debuff so players doubted it was active; a bug report asked for the missing −20% healing reduction; players worried about casters OOMing, the interaction with consumes, and the DoT double-nerf with Resilience. It stacks with Resilience. [[d:pvp-general#1406266691415183391]], [[d:pvp-general#1406311150207107165]], [[d:pvp-general#1381903114717761598]], [[d:pvp-general#1381913452435734651]], [[d:pvp-general#1382015304271396914]], [[d:pvp-general#1406279520146690179]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- **Supersession / completion:** the KB's own later staff statement (Jamey, 2026-01-23, [p=166071](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)) makes the reductions dynamic per class/spec in 1.18.1 — exactly what the channel predicted ("dampening is 1.18, honor/gear rework 1.18.1"). **The Discord corpus contains no post-1.18.1 dampening numbers**; keep CON-0082 unresolved and forum-sourced.

### Resilience (related to the "resilience" history)
- **Original Resilience was crit-avoidance** (mostly a PvE tank stat). **~May 2024 Turtle reworked it** to reduce critical-strike damage and DoT damage taken: "Resilience is seeing a complete rework. Instead of reducing your chance to be critically struck, it will instead reduce the damage of critical strikes and damage over time effects on you … as it was mostly utilized by PvE tanks". Corroborated by "When Resilience changes from crit reduction to DoT + Crit Damage…". [[d:pvp-general#1235510685275000842]], [[d:pvp-general#1278439488493977741]] (player quoting the forum change + corroboration, `consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- **Shipped at ~3% per item** (four AB armour pieces ≈ 12% + set bonus; ~15% typical total) and was judged too weak ("15% resilience isn't that great"); the 2024 verdict was "useless in PvP and picked up for cheese in PvE". Some predicted it would make sustain specs unkillable and double-nerf DoTs with dampening. [[d:pvp-general#1283057585733369906]], [[d:pvp-general#1301115279228670014]], [[d:pvp-general#1304464263405899839]], [[d:pvp-general#1301548197700833280]], [[d:pvp-general#1296604348440248393]], [[d:pvp-general#1243988391159136286]], [[d:pvp-general#1301103905538834432]] (`consensus` on the values/verdict, `contested` on the predictions, pre-1.18.1).
- **Supersession:** the Discord description is the 2024 crit-**damage+DoT** version. The later forum staff statement (Torta, 2025-12-02, [p=161356](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161356#p161356)) — "Resilience now reduces the entire critical damage taken, not just the bonus critical damage" — **supersedes** it. Do not present the 2024 description as current.

### CC / diminishing returns (KB gap)
- **Contested.** The usable summary from the channel: fear/charm/polymorph-style CC and snare DR behave vanilla-ly; **stuns sit on a small number of categories** — a beta tester states "Kidney Shot does not share diminishing returns with anything. All other stuns are on another diminishing returns timer", corroborated by separate player claims that HoJ has a DR separate from rogue stuns. **Snare DR** is the vanilla "3 applications then immune" rule. A blunt player claim that "diminishing returns on CC effects isn't a thing" is contradicted by those statements and is best read as "no TBC-style unified category for all CC". Chaining cheap short stuns can consume the stun category and grant effective immunity to the next big stun. **No full category table exists in the corpus.** [[d:pvp-general#1298323517976219660]], [[d:pvp-general#1119216134558863440]], [[d:pvp-general#1117823407543824404]], [[d:pvp-general#1112057125301125240]], [[d:pvp-general#1142229826846064772]], [[d:pvp-general#1128459322834296902]] (`contested`, pre-1.18.1).

### Consumables and engineering (related to CON-0083 context)
- Engineering is treated as near-mandatory: "grenades and reflectors are the meat and potatoes for any pvper"; the named toolbelt is net, rocket helm, rocket boots, gnomish boots, tidal charm, grenade, mind-control cap. **FAP prevents (does not break) slows/roots** so it must be pre-popped; LIP is the anti-melee potion; protection/resistance potions are the standard counter to specific damage. Reflector level requirements in 2022 were frost 47, fire 53, shadow 55. A concrete player proposal (ban FAP/LIP, return to 2v2) is on record; the claim that rated arena "will not allow consums" is **unconfirmed**. [[d:pvp-general#1098200936477888524]], [[d:pvp-general#1090867020880429156]], [[d:pvp-general#1112055482480336987]], [[d:pvp-general#1035765902857207828]], [[d:pvp-general#1030433235940937738]], [[d:pvp-general#1030433592771362846]], [[d:pvp-general#1114083883709911041]], [[d:pvp-general#1016849412451991652]], [[d:pvp-general#1268689704341999656]], [[d:pvp-general#1380953202484056115]] (`consensus` / `contested`, pre-1.18.1).

### World PvP, dishonorable kills and arena (related to CON-0080, CON-0083)
- **War Mode reality:** opt-in glyph/NPC taken mostly for the XP bonus; "90% of people that use it don't even fight back"; organic open-world PvP is "absolutely miniscule" and the population is 80–90% PvE/HC. Asymmetric flagging is the main open-world exploit. [[d:pvp-general#1301687319933227109]], [[d:pvp-general#1288438284854497351]], [[d:pvp-general#1288438002334306314]], [[d:pvp-general#1260222777483333712]] (`consensus` / `single source`, pre-1.18.1).
- **CON-0080 supersession:** the channel reflects the **earlier** rule that DHKs came from **civilian NPCs** and that killing low-level players gave none; the later staff redefinition (Torta, 2023-12-12, [p=80839](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80839#p80839): DHKs for killing grey-level players) **supersedes** it. [[d:pvp-general#1042784841055354930]], [[d:pvp-general#1042785048404967424]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- **Bounty Boards** were unmaintained (Alliance quests pointed at the same inactive player for ~6 months) — substance agrees with the KB's "unconfirmed for gameplay" caveat. [[d:pvp-general#1095357463202643968]] (`single source`, pre-1.18.1).
- **Blood Ring** is Turtle's arena: historically 2v2, later 3v3 on one map; it often failed to pop because rewards were thin (~3 HKs/game). Staff pushed cross-faction Blood Ring internally. Rated arenas were the 1.18.x headline promise. [[d:pvp-general#1381294933796978769]], [[d:pvp-general#1224429862211682434]], [[d:pvp-general#1249389435485819054]], [[d:pvp-general#1281287328178114570]], [[d:pvp-general#1212527345353887795]], [[d:pvp-general#1252023586114375731]], [[d:pvp-general#1403706029795180635]] (`consensus` / `single authority`, pre-1.18.1 / 1.18.1-announced-pre-release).
- **Cross-faction BGs** were announced for 1.18.0 or 1.18.1 and shipped (live 2025-11-04 per KB); mixed-faction **premades** remain impossible (KB staff). [[d:pvp-general#1393688963285254175]], [[d:pvp-general#1333932558303367198]], [[d:pvp-general#1381889760037179444]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1 / 1.18.1-announced-pre-release).

**What Discord cannot settle here.** There is no `post-1.18.1` material: final dampening numbers, honour-as-currency/CP, the three-tier PvP sets, rated-arena rules and the final Resilience behaviour are forum-sourced only and must not be attributed to Discord.
