## CON-0102 — "CP" means combo points or contribution points

- **Side A.** In PvP threads CP is contribution/combat points contributing to honor standing ("top CP earners"). — [Xerron (player), 2019-08-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3715#p3715)
- **Side B.** Elsewhere CP is the rogue/feral combo-point resource. — synthesis/glossary.md (community wiki) — `synthesis/glossary.md`
- **What would settle it.** Not a game-data question; disambiguate by thread context.
- **Assessment (`ambiguous-by-design`).** Both readings are correct in their own threads. Never resolve CP from the token alone.

## CON-0103 — "T2.5" / "T3.5" — official tier names or community shorthand?

- **Side A.** Used as if they were shipped tier names for the intermediate sets between T2/T3 and beyond Naxxramas. — [Tamamo-no-Bae (player), 2019-12-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4697#p4697)
- **Side B.** Also used as a wishlist term for hypothetical content; no staff post in the corpus states them plainly as official set names. — [Catasstrophy (player), 2022-02-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=17082#p17082)
- **What would settle it.** Item set names in the 1.18.1 item-set table: do the sets carry these names, or only community usage?
- **Assessment (`unresolved`).** Unresolved as naming; the items themselves exist and are described in the itemization changelogs.

## CON-0104 — "Hardcore mode" before and after 2021

- **Side A.** Early posts (2018-2020) use "hardcore mode" for the reduced-XP Slow & Steady challenge. — [Torta (staff), 2018-10-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=990#p990)
- **Side B.** Later posts use HC for the permadeath mode with its own ecosystem and rules. — [Akalix (staff), 2023-05-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=47832#p47832)
- **What would settle it.** Challenge/mode flags on characters in the local data.
- **Assessment (`reconciled`).** Resolved by date: read "hardcore" before 2021 as Slow & Steady unless the post says permadeath.
