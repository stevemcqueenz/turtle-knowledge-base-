## CON-0084 — Tents & Taverns: staff rationale vs player "it is a nerf"

- **Side A.** Rested XP moved to taverns worldwide at about the old tent pace; the kill bonus was halved "so XP rate isn't permanently doubled"; tents keep a partial bonus. Reasons given: immersion and capital-gate tent camps. — [Torta (staff), 2026-02-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168639#p168639), [Torta (staff), 2026-02-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168649#p168649)
- **Side B.** Players read the same change as a straight nerf to the rested bonus. — [Hctwowfan (player), 2026-02-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168644#p168644), [Saronsen (player), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173332#p173332)
- **What would settle it.** Rested-XP accrual rate in taverns vs tents, the 25% tent cap and the kill-bonus multiplier in 1.18.1 data.
- **Assessment (`design-dispute`).** Both descriptions are compatible: the bonus rate was halved while access was widened. The factual half is checkable; the value judgement is not.

- **Assessment (`unresolved`).** Unresolved; no staff post reconciles the counts. One seed may pre-date 1.18.1.

## CON-0086 — Jewelcrafting trainers: player guide list vs the official page

- **Side A.** A guide lists Apprentice trainers in Stormwind, Ironforge, Orgrimmar, Undercity, Sparkwater Port plus Expert/Artisan trainers. — [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106310#p106310)
- **Side B.** The official page implies six cities including an Alliance Alah'Thalas trainer, which the guide omits; no wiki NPC page in the extraction is tagged as a Jewelcrafting trainer. — [turtlecraft.gg, Jewelcrafting (official website)](https://turtlecraft.gg/jewelcrafting)
- **What would settle it.** Trainer NPCs with the Jewelcrafting trainer flag in the 1.18.1 creature data.
- **Assessment (`unresolved`).** Unresolved; the guide is the only itemized source and is known to be incomplete.

## CON-0087 — Survival profession: shipped as described vs player-reported gaps

- **Side A.** Reworked Survival: 80+ new recipes, tools that slow/root/trap, gathering bags and containers. — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)
- **Side B.** Players report the Survivalist Skinning Knife being useless (skinning still requires a skinning knife) and Survival recipes showing as "unknown" at the trainer after clearing the cache. — [Dexday (player), 2026-03-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172785#p172785), [Deadcrow (player), 2026-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174264#p174264)
- **What would settle it.** Survival recipe list and trainer spell lists in 1.18.1; check whether the Survivalist Skinning Knife carries a skinning-tool flag.
- **Assessment (`unresolved`).** Unresolved; the recipe list was never published, so the player reports cannot be checked against an intended state from the corpus alone.
