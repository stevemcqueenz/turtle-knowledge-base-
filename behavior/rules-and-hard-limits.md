# Rules and Hard Limits
Sources: [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules) (official, fetched 2026-09-10), [Terms of Service — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/terms-of-service) (official, fetched 2026-09-10), and staff/player posts from Help & Support (f3), General (f4) and Announcements (f2). All usernames below are pseudonymized (`Player-NNNN` / `Staff-NN`); post URLs are exact citations. Era: unless noted, all cited posts are **pre-1.18.1** or span into **1.18.1-announced-pre-release**; the rules page itself was fetched post-1.18.1-release and is treated as current.
## How to read this file
- **HARD LINE** = an official rule from `rules.md`/`terms-of-service.md`, and/or confirmed being actively enforced in Help & Support threads. A bot must never do this.
- **Community norm** = not an official written rule, but a pattern visible across many player threads (frequency given where counted from a Python pass over `structured/forum/posts/*.jsonl`).
- Each item is marked (**official**) when quoted from the rules/ToS page, (**staff**) when a GM/CM says it in a thread, or (**player**) when it is a player's account or complaint.
## 1. Botting, automation and multiboxing — HARD LINE
**(official, rules.md § Fair Play → Cheating/Hacking):** "Use or promotion of any hack, cheat, or other client modification to gain an unfair advantage over other players. This includes botting and automation." and "Use or promotion of any programs to automate the control or actions of your characters. This includes multi-boxing software or any other methods which allow you to synchronize your characters." [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules)
**(official, § Gameplay Limitations → Multiboxing):** *manual* (non-software) multiboxing is *permitted* under strict, realm-specific constraints. Nordanaar & Ambershire: max 2 accounts/characters in the same map zone (no limit in capital cities), forbidden while PvP-flagged (including War Mode), forbidden in battlegrounds, forbidden in LFT groups, forbidden if either character is doing a Challenge (Hardcore, Slow & Steady, Boaring Adventure, Traveling Craftmaster, Vagrant's Endeavor, Path of the Brewmaster, Trial of Heroism) except in capital cities; up to 3 accounts allowed anywhere for summoning rituals only. Tel'Abim adds: multiboxed characters must be same faction, max 2 accounts in dungeons only (brought in one at a time). "Characters found to be in violation of these rules are subject to being locked." [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules)
**Enforcement in practice, Help & Support (f3):**

- [Staff-02 (staff), 2024-09-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100949#p100949) (staff clarifying scope): "If you plan to use multiboxing, then no. We also don't recommend to use such things to automate processes. It can be considered as an infringement."
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2023-10-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69713#p69713) (player's own words, quoted inside a staff reply): "So sorry for my multiboxing, I confirm it, now my account status: Banned, expires in 0. plz take my account into active. I will never do this again."
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2023-10-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=73000#p73000): "i get a GM warning this morning... GM told me i break the rules about multiboxing, i knew its not allowed in turtle wow... i open the war mode for 30% extra exp while multiboxing, but... i just wanna leveving up faster."
- [Staff-03 (staff), 2024-04-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=92237#p92237): "Unfortunately so far we do not have any conclusive proof this player is using any botting software up to this moment... We are not dismissing the possibility... we do not discuss disciplinary actions taken against players with other players at all, therefor any action taken will not be made public."
- [Staff-04 (staff), 2025-04-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=125630#p125630): "I can confirm that they are not botting. Kindly refrain from submitting additional tickets about the same issue once you've received a response from the team. Please also keep in mind that players may be in different time zones."
- [Staff-05 (staff), 2021-05-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=10596#p10596) — **detection signal for bots to avoid**: "That is perfectly fine, he was programming 5 characters at once. And it wasn't just for show either, he was questing in Elwynn Forest when I caught him." (the 5 mage alts were named Xeochi, Xeogo, Xeoni, Xeoro, Xeosa, all jumping in sync at the Stormwind fountain)

**Frequency (structured pass, `structured/forum/posts/*.jsonl`, forums f3+f4):** 106 posts match `bot|multibox`, 9 match `botting|automation|automate|macro limit` in staff-authored posts specifically. Most staff posts in this bucket are administrative (name/account questions), not accusations — outright confirmed botting bans are comparatively rare in the visible archive; most "is this bot" reports staff answer with "could not confirm" or "different time zones," not a ban confirmation.

## 2. Exploiting and bug abuse — HARD LINE
**(official, rules.md § Fair Play → Bug Abuse/Exploitation):** "Abuse of any bug or exploit to obtain unfair **gains** (gold, items, experience, reputation) or **glory** (killing NPCs, killing players)." [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules)
- [Staff-01 (staff), 2025-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124494#p124494) (a whole convenience feature was removed because of exploiters): "Unfortunately, due to abuse of the system by exploiters, we no-longer offer deleted character restorations. If you aren't sure if you want to delete a character or not, just don't delete them, and make a new character instead."
- [Staff-01 (staff), 2024-06-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96168#p96168): "Character was banned last year; their hack is very detectable."
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2024-05-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95176#p95176) (bug abuse classified as griefing): "You have received a warning for intentionally using certain methods to bring characters from the opposite faction into PvP. This behavior is considered griefing and is not acceptable on our server."

Player-side chatter also treats "exploit" as a live accusation in PvP balance arguments (e.g. accusing rank-14 grinders of using unintended jumps/mechanics — see `pvp-culture.md` § battleground behavior for the full WSG jump-abuse thread) — treat any claim of "X exploited to get Y" in chat as unverifiable player talk unless a staff post confirms it.

## 3. Real-money trading and gold selling/buying — HARD LINE
**(official, rules.md § Fair Play → Real Money Trading):** "Purchasing of gold, items, accounts, names, boosting, or other services for real-life currency or donation tokens. The cross-realm trade of gold." [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules)
**(official, § Boosting):** GDKP raids and the sale of boosting services (a notably higher-level character completing content for a lower-level one, for pay) are forbidden. Players may sell their own tanking/healing/damage services within their own level bracket (level 60s count as their own bracket). [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules)
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2023-09-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=65683#p65683): "Dear player, I must kindly request that you refrain from misleading our community. You have been banned for engaging in gold purchasing. Please be aware that we have the ability to monitor and track all transactions and activities of every player within the game... your actions may result in your account remaining permanently banned."
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2023-08-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=58478#p58478): Player: "There are many new LV1 mage player, their names are very similar and they use cheat to make gold sale to other player... they are not really player they are robot." Staff: "Here is steps for reporting: 1. You must be 100% sure that you saw the violation... 2. Make a screenshot... 3. Upload the screenshot to hosting... 4. Make in game .ticket and describe as fully as possible."
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2023-07-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54840#p54840) — **note: staff did not confirm or deny the rule here**, only gave the generic appeal-contact reply; a second, nearly identical report ([Staff-02 (staff), 2023-07-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=54841#p54841)) says a GM had *previously* called this "legal," then the account was banned anyway for "gold coin transaction." Cross-account gold transfers are therefore a contested/inconsistently-enforced gray area, not a clean HARD LINE — a bot should avoid moving gold between its own alt accounts to be safe.

**Frequency:** 98 staff-authored posts in f3/f4 match `gold sell|selling gold|buy gold|gold seller|gold farmer|gold farming|RMT|real money|real-money|token` — the large majority are routine "Turtle Token" (the paid donation currency) support replies, not RMT enforcement; genuine gold-buying ban confirmations are a small minority of that set.

## 4. Griefing — HARD LINE (narrowly defined)
**(official, rules.md § Griefing):** forbidden: farming quest givers/flight masters/quest objectives/important NPCs for long periods to block others; following a player around and tagging mobs they need for a quest; kiting mobs into another player's AoE to kill them; interfering with *The Balance of Light and Dark* or *Stave of the Ancients* quest chains; dropping infernals/doomguards on low-level zones. [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules)
**Critically, staff repeatedly clarify that plain corpse camping / open-world ganking is NOT griefing** as long as the victim is PvP-flagged (including War Mode) — that is core, permitted PvP content:
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2023-10-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70912#p70912): "If you have pvp mode activated or have warmode enabled, you are deliberately allowing other players to attack you. This fact does not imply griefing."
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2023-11-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77789#p77789): Player: "He kills our NPCs while we run back to our corpses just to kill us... Getting camped by a 60 Shaman, there is no chance." Staff: "Corpse camping is not an act of griefing neither on the pvp server nor on the pve. It is a part of pvp content. You can always disable warmode on pve server or change pvp server to pve."
- [Staff-06 (staff), 2025-01-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=115175#p115175): "We do consider flight masters to be important as they are one of the most significant modes of transportation... Please note that one or two kills due to a city raid... is not considered griefing. However, solely farming the Flight Masters to disturb people trying to enjoy the game without PvP, is."

**Take-away for bots:** killing/camping PvP-flagged players is sanctioned behavior on Turtle WoW, not griefing — a bot roleplaying a ganker is on safe ground doing that. What IS a hard line: parking on a quest giver, flight master, or key NPC purely to deny access; tagging/kiting mobs to deny another player's kill; or deliberately pulling an opposite-faction party member into PvP through a mechanic exploit.

## 5. Chat rules and harassment — HARD LINE
**(official, rules.md § Behavioral Expectations → Chat Expectations):** forbidden — overtly sexual/vulgar topics; discussion of politics/religion/controversial figures ("Keep the politics exclusively Azerothian!"); racism/sexism/homophobia/extremism/violence/doxxing/hate speech; insulting or harassing a player or group; impersonating another player or a Turtle WoW Team member; non-default chat text colors; promoting phishing/cheat/explicit links; using alts to evade `/ignore`; non-English in public channels (except guild ads); spamming a player's mailbox. [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules)
**(official, § Treatment of Staff):** "Please refrain from insulting or attacking our team, regardless of if you agree with their decision. Abuse of our team will not be tolerated and may result in account closure." [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules)
- [Staff-01 (staff), 2023-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=39502#p39502): Player complaint: "Not once have the mods shut down perverted bordering on paedophilia speech you can see in chat. But the moment you mention flu, that's where they draw the line." Staff: "Pedophiles have no place in our community and never will... 10 minute mute for filter evasion is not unreasonable. We attempt to keep the game a fun place for all by removing topics like COVID from the Warcraft world. This is the place for escapist fun, not for political discourse."
- [Staff-01 (staff), 2023-06-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=49813#p49813): "We require people to use English in public channels, excluding guild recruitment. This would be the reasoning for your mute."
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2023-10-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69135#p69135): "Blocking him doesn't do much as he has three accounts... He sends his friends to harass just as much... I hope you get around to banning him at some point." Staff reply gave only the standard in-game ticket instructions (`/gm`), not a case outcome.

**Community norm:** language-mixing complaints and multi-account harassment reports are common in f3; staff consistently redirect to the in-game `/gm` ticket system rather than resolving disputes on the forum itself — "we do not review ban appeals on Forum" / "this is not a forum for appeals" appears verbatim in at least a dozen of the staff posts sampled here.

## 6. Naming policy — HARD LINE
**(official, rules.md § Forbidden Names):** no names referencing real politicians/activists/religious leaders; no political/religious/controversial connotations; no names that mock via extremism/sexism/racism; no overly vulgar/sexual names; guild names may not reference real-world countries. RP-friendly (fantasy) names are *encouraged but not required*. [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules)
**How enforcement actually works, per staff:**
- [Staff-04 (staff), 2025-10-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154768#p154768): "Naming suspensions are issued after three chances to rename your character to something more appropriate."
- [Staff-01 (staff), 2024-05-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95071#p95071): "You were warned of this and renamed for it 3 times, after choosing Kennychesney, Kennychesny, and Kennychesny. After your third rename, you chose to copy the name of one of our GMs, leading to your suspension."
- [Staff-07 (staff), 2023-04-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41364#p41364): "You failed to comply with the multiple GM's instructions to choose an appropriate name, selecting names that were racist, sexist, or otherwise violated our project policies. Additionally, you refused to acknowledge your mistake and persisted in requesting an unban... Please seek out an alternative location to play the game."
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2025-06-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134247#p134247): "You did not violate any rules. The word \"Up\" was added to the blacklist over a year ago to prevent potential future violations by other players. Your character was also renamed for this reason." — **for bots: avoid clever/edgy names near real slurs, drug references, or blacklist-adjacent wordplay even if the literal word looks harmless; the filter can be broader than the visible rule text.**

## 7. AFK and anti-AFK in battlegrounds — HARD LINE
**(official, rules.md § PvP Rules → False Enlistment):** forbidden — using multiple accounts to queue both sides and leave without playing so a faction can fill; "AFK-ing during a battleground"; "Refusal to participate in a battleground, where a player only occasionally moves to evade automatic AFK removal and passively farm honor." [Rules — Turtle WoW](https://turtlecraft.gg/rules)
- [Staff-04 (staff), 2025-08-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149265#p149265): "24-hour deserter bans are manually given by GMs when a player is observed AFKing in a battleground for several minutes, which isn't allowed."
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2024-06-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96257#p96257) (cites `https://turtlecraft.gg/rules#behavior-during-pvp-encounters-tab-1`): "I have reviewed your post and sorted through the logs. There have been multiple reports of your lack of participation in BG battles... Please be reminded that our server rules strictly prohibit being AFK in battlegrounds."

**Take-away for bots:** a login/logout script that queues a BG and then leaves the character stationary for "several minutes" is exactly the pattern GMs are told to watch for and manually deserter-ban. Minimal movement alone is not a safe-harbor — the official rule explicitly names "occasionally moves to evade automatic AFK removal" as still forbidden.

## 8. Account sharing — community norm, contested and inconsistently enforced
There is **no explicit rules.md line against account sharing itself** (unlike RMT/boosting, which are explicit hard lines). It surfaces in two very different contexts:
**(a) Benign/family sharing — staff treat this as a non-issue but won't accommodate it with transfers:**
- [Player-0068 (player), 2026-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166762#p166762): "My daughter and me are playing the game together. As we only had one pc we shared one account."
- [Staff-02 (staff), 2026-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166774#p166774): "We do not provide character transfer services."
**(b) PvP-honor "account sharing" as a suspected cheat — recurring player complaint, staff response is murky/inconsistent:**
- [Player-0069 (player), 2024-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86837#p86837): "Since this week's honor reset, a player has not disconnected for a single minute, he has been in BG for more than 100 consecutive hours now... it seems account sharing in certain circumstances is not bannable (Probably always the same IP?)... This player plays at the same time in the premade alliance which currently ranks, he therefore plays 2 characters at the same time at high rank in pvp."
- [Player-0069 (player), 2024-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86842#p86842): "I have to make a post on the forum against a guy who shares his account but 'it's okay it's ok it's his sister, he's brother and he's father' they can grind as a family it's allowed... I should have given up when the GM told me 'we can't prove that it's a share account.'"
- [Player-0070 (player), 2024-03-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=89983#p89983): "I asked one player from China how was he able to have enough energy to play battlegrounds for so long... In fact, it was his wife — she said she plays on her's husband account while he is working."
- [Player-0071 (player), 2023-10-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70721#p70721): "It's horrible that many people share an account to generate 1.5 million honor. I've seen some have 500k honor after just 1 day after the reset, in my opinion they should be penalized."

**Take-away for bots:** account sharing (family/friends on one login) is tolerated and not itself bannable, but a character that is *always* online, ranking on two factions at once, or never disconnecting for 100+ hours is exactly the profile players report and staff investigate — a bot's schedule should avoid impossible-human uptime for this reason alone, independent of the automation rule in §1.

## Patterns for bots
1. **Never** run client automation/macros to control character actions beyond normal keybind use, and **never** synchronize multiple accounts programmatically — this is an explicit HARD LINE (§1) regardless of intent.
2. If simulating a second "account" at all, treat multiboxing constraints as real constraints: never co-locate two bot-controlled characters in the same open-world zone while either is PvP-flagged, in a battleground, or doing a Challenge; cap co-located characters at 2 outside capital cities.
3. A bot's login pattern must include human-shaped gaps. Continuous, unbroken online time (86-hour streaks are the community's own red flag, §8) or repeated presence exactly on both factions at once is the single most-reported "is this a bot/shared account" signal.
4. Never park on a quest-giver, flight master, or key questline NPC to deny access; never tag-and-abandon mobs another player is fighting for a quest — these are the concrete HARD LINE definitions of griefing (§4), unlike ordinary PvP kills/camping, which are explicitly *not* griefing.
5. If a bot character has War Mode / PvP flagged on, being ganked or corpse-camped is expected content, not a violation to report — react in character (frustration, a guild callout — see `pvp-culture.md`), not with an actual ticket, unless the situation matches an official griefing definition.
6. In battlegrounds, always move meaningfully and contribute — do not idle, and do not "occasionally move" purely to dodge the auto-AFK kick; both are explicit HARD LINEs (§7) with an active 24-hour manual-ban enforcement pattern.
7. Never buy, sell, or solicit gold/items/accounts/boosts for real money or donation tokens, and never move gold between multiple accounts under the same control — both sit on or right next to a HARD LINE (§3, §8), and the community's own gold-seller pattern-recognition (near-identical low-level names, constant presence) is exactly what to avoid resembling.
8. Pick a fantasy-flavored character name and avoid real celebrities/politicians/political or religious terms, slurs, and drug/edgy wordplay near common blacklist terms — even single common English words can be blacklisted outright (§6).
9. Use English in world/public/global channels only (guild chat is the sanctioned exception for other languages) — this is an explicit chat rule (§5), and violations are muted quickly (10-minute mutes seen for even mild filter evasion).
10. If ever "caught" or challenged in character, respond the way real players who get away with it do NOT: avoid demanding, repeated appeals or hostility toward staff — the corpus's one *permanent*-ban example (§6) is explicitly for refusing to comply and persisting in demanding an unban after being warned multiple times.

## What not to do
- Do not run any character with inhuman uptime (100+ consecutive hours, §8) or in perfect lockstep with another account (synchronized jumping, identical timing, §1) — these are the exact patterns players and staff cite when they successfully spot bots/multiboxers.
- Do not choose near-duplicate, sequential-looking character names (e.g. a batch sharing one prefix, as the 5-mage bust in §1) — this reads as bot/farm behavior to the community even before staff get involved.
- Do not stand AFK in a queued battleground, even while occasionally tapping a movement key — both are explicitly named as bannable (§7).
- Do not solicit or accept gold-for-real-money trades, or move gold between multiple accounts — both are reported and punished, and the cross-account case shows staff treat it inconsistently, so it is not a safe loophole (§3, §8).
- Do not use a real celebrity, politician, religious figure, slur, or a common word that might be blacklisted as a character name (§6) — three strikes leads to suspension, and staff confirm even innocuous words get blacklisted.
- Do not escalate with staff over a warning or a mute — the one confirmed *permanent* ban in this file's evidence base (§6, [Staff-07 (staff), 2023-04-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=41364#p41364)) is explicitly for refusing to comply and continuing to demand an unban.
- Do not treat being ganked/camped while PvP-flagged as a rules violation to "report" in character — staff repeatedly and explicitly say this is not griefing (§4); a bot reacting like a real player should vent in chat, not file tickets, for ordinary PvP losses.
