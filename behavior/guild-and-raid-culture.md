# Guild and Raid Culture

How Turtle WoW players describe recruitment, raid organization, loot systems and guild drama, in their own words. Sources: forum 23 (Guild Recruitment), 50 (Roleplay Guild Recruitment), 28 (Raids), 4 (General), 37 (Hardcore Lounge). Usernames pseudonymized per `behavior/_aliases.json`; staff keep an authority-visible `Staff-NN` alias.

## Recruitment posts: structure, tone, requirements

Guild Recruitment threads follow a recognizable template: guild name/tag, faction, focus (PvE/PvP/RP/social), raid days & server-time slot, loot system, classes needed, and a contact list of officer names. A very early example sets the pattern:

> "Our guild is not focused on RP but we welcome Rpers to our ranks if they want a welcoming raid environment. [...] Our goal on the server is to progress through all the dungeon and raid content Vanilla WoW has to offer. At a pace that is welcome to someone with obligations outside of WoW."
> — [Player-0072 (player), 2018-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=414#p414), Guild Recruitment, *Guilds & Groups*, pre-1.18.1

A long-running recurring recruitment thread (updated monthly over the guild's life) shows how a guild reports progress and needs at each stage:

> "Recruitment is going well so far. We're currently looking for Warriors (Tank / DPS) and Druids. Could also use another paladin and shaman. We're looking to target July 12th for our first guild raid."
> — [Player-0100 (player), 2022-06-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19288#p19288), Guild Recruitment, *<Chosen> is seeking more members in hopes of becoming a raiding guild.*, pre-1.18.1
> "I just wanted to say that last night <guild> was able to one-shot both Domo and Rag (before the first submerge)! We are now 10/10 in MC, hopefully moving onto BWL within a few weeks. [...] That being said, we still want more to join us!"
> — [Player-0100 (player), 2022-09-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24590#p24590), Guild Recruitment, *<Chosen> is seeking more members in hopes of becoming a raiding guild.*, pre-1.18.1

Recruitment posts routinely state time zone/server-time slots explicitly, since the server is international:

> "Clearing Ony/AQ20/ZG/MC weekly fast, 8PM AEST +10GMT. If you are new, we will hook you up with gear, attunes and leveling if you need it to get you into raid asap."
> — [Player-0110 (player), 2023-01-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33339#p33339), Guild Recruitment, *<East Oceanic Trading Co> is recruiting all Oceanic and Asian-based players! ZG/Ony/AQ20/MC cleared!*, pre-1.18.1
> "20:00-23:00 GMT +1 Thursday & Sunday. [...] We are a mix of EU and NA. Peak online 101."
> — [Player-0118 (player), 2023-04-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42165#p42165), Guild Recruitment, *<Nephilim> PvE PvP Horde Only.*, pre-1.18.1

Some recruitment posts explicitly filter for tone/attitude over skill, a recurring theme in casual-guild recruitment:

> "We don't expect anyone to be the best, actually, if this first time playing. [...] Just be a nice dude/dudette. [...] Guild chat and discord are friendly for all ages."
> — [Player-0118 (player), 2023-04-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42165#p42165), Guild Recruitment, *<Nephilim> PvE PvP Horde Only.*, pre-1.18.1
> "Our goal is simple: steady progress without pressure, a strong guild identity, and a place where members can rely on each other."
> — [Player-0172 (player), 2025-09-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=150884#p150884), Guild Recruitment, *[Ambershire][ENG] <I Lion’s Pride I> — Social • PvE • Tavern Vibes*, pre-1.18.1

## Raid schedules and sign-ups

Guilds post explicit weekly raid-day/time grids and update them as progression advances (new nights added for new raid tiers). Sign-ups happen via Discord (poll/thread) rather than the forum itself, which is used mainly for the standing recruitment post:

> "Raid Days & Current Progress: Mondays: AQ40 & BWL (on farm); Wednesdays: Tower of Karazhan (1/9); Thursdays: Naxxramas (on farm). These are our official raid-days, we also commonly run MC, Ony, AQ20, ZG, Kara10 on off days."
> — [Player-0133 (player), 2023-09-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60818#p60818), Guild Recruitment, *𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐒𝐜𝐨𝐮𝐫𝐠𝐞 — 𝐑𝐚𝐢𝐝 & 𝐑𝐨𝐥𝐞𝐩𝐥𝐚𝐲*, pre-1.18.1
> "We plan to have official guild raids Thursday, Sunday and Tuesdays, 20.00 to 23.00, server time."
> — [Player-0146 (player), 2024-01-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85909#p85909), Guild Recruitment, *<Raven> recruiting raiders*, pre-1.18.1
> "I get the raid started/organized, set raid group compositions for ideal performance, handle master looting, dual box 2 characters, distribute mage food/water, buff the raid with mage+druid buffs, provide flasks/elixirs/potions to the raid, get people to join Discord, and help negotiate disputes."
> — [Player-0081 (player), 2019-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4659#p4659), Raids, *Raid Loot System for 2020*, pre-1.18.1

## Loot systems: DKP, soft reserve, loot council, GDKP

Guilds mix and evolve loot systems as they mature — commonly starting on soft reserve (SR) and migrating to DKP once a stable core forms:

> "Loot system: We will start by using SR until we have a stable core and then switch to a DKP system."
> — [Player-0146 (player), 2024-01-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85909#p85909), Guild Recruitment, *<Raven> recruiting raiders*, pre-1.18.1
> "Soft-reserve system 1x SR > MS > OS"
> — [Player-0145 (player), 2023-11-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77542#p77542), Guild Recruitment, *«Lost in Auberdine» EU/US non-HC*, pre-1.18.1
> "Soft-reserve system changed to Thatsmybis.com system"
> — [Player-0145 (player), 2024-01-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84234#p84234), Guild Recruitment, *«Lost in Auberdine» EU/US non-HC*, pre-1.18.1
> "We utilize HR>SR>MS>OS for all raids. At the beginning of each raid, the raid leader will post and lock all SR (Soft Reserve) links. [...] Participants found selling OS rolled items will be immediately removed from the guild/raid."
> — [Player-0129 (player), 2023-06-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51212#p51212), Guild Recruitment, *<Kul Tiras Third Fleet (RP-PVE)>*, pre-1.18.1
> "Loot: MS>OS +1 with limited LC on select bis items"
> — [Player-0104 (player), 2022-09-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=24963#p24963), Guild Recruitment, *<The Emerald Dream> wants YOU to help stop the Nightmare!*, pre-1.18.1
> "DKP (ms/os) on Tier and random with reset to 0 every 4:th week. Loot council on BiS weapons and trinkets."
> — [Player-0118 (player), 2023-04-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42165#p42165), Guild Recruitment, *<Nephilim> PvE PvP Horde Only.*, pre-1.18.1
> "Für BWL, AQ40, ED und Naxx benutzen wir ein Dkp System namens EP/GP. Für alle anderen Raids wird ein Soft-Lock System namens Sahne-Prio benutzt."
> — [Player-0130 (player), 2023-08-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57856#p57856), Guild Recruitment, *[GER] Die "Schmetterlingsbrigade" sucht Dich!*, pre-1.18.1

Older threads debate DKP vs random-roll vs loot council at length, weighing new-player accessibility against rewarding veteran attendance:

> "DKP system is something that allows you to progresivelly work towards a goal to gather points and head somewhere. [...] nothing discourages a new player more than hearing "Yeah there is some loot for veteran players only"."
> — [Player-0086 (player), 2020-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5879#p5879), Raids, *Veteran vs Newbie Raid Loot Issue*, pre-1.18.1
> "I've had my best experiences with a Loot Council, but you need a bunch of very active and very reliable Class Leaders for that to work."
> — [Player-0082 (player), 2019-12-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4676#p4676), Raids, *Raid Loot System for 2020*, pre-1.18.1

**GDKP is banned by server rule**, a fact that distinguishes Turtle's raid economy from many other private servers. Staff explain the reasoning directly:

> "Concerning why GDKP is banned—it has been heavily directly linked to an increase in RMT/gold buying wherever permitted. In order to maintain the integrity of our server's economies, and ensure fair play, we opted to ban GDKP outright."
> — [Staff-01 (staff), 2024-06-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97794#p97794), Raids, *GDPK, EPGP, HR...*, pre-1.18.1
> "Gdkp's create a gold rush race. The people with the most time and money irl will benefit. [...] It's one of the many reasons I and other's I know quit classic and came here instead."
> — [Player-0018 (player), 2024-06-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97766#p97766), Raids, *GDPK, EPGP, HR...*, pre-1.18.1
> "With GDKP it would probably be faster to get the item you want."
> — [Player-0148 (player), 2024-06-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97765#p97765), Raids, *GDPK, EPGP, HR...*, pre-1.18.1
> "GDKP is banned as per rules. Nobody wants cheaters. Accept the rules of the server to play on the server."
> — [Player-0078 (player), 2024-06-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97789#p97789), Raids, *GDPK, EPGP, HR...*, pre-1.18.1

## Guild drama patterns

Guild drama in the corpus follows two shapes: internal loot disputes escalating publicly, and inter-guild conflict (one guild accusing another of misconduct). A year-long guild collapse ('Sangue Ruim') triggered a public GM-response thread and a debate over how far staff should intervene in internal guild affairs:

> "Griefing around 20 players' work which took 1 year is a high priority case in this virtual realm. [...] Normal guild drama could be ignored and it can be made very clear that if you just wanna make a fuss because you didn't receive a loot after a roll at MC, nothing will happen. But TWOW would also signal that if there is something important, guilds can rely on them."
> — [Player-0142 (player), 2023-10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=72248#p72248), General, *Sangue Ruim Guild drama - GM Response - Skoj*, pre-1.18.1

The Beluga/Blacksmith recipe dispute (see `behavior/group-play.md` for the loot-etiquette angle) also shows the inter-guild-accusation shape: a public callout thread naming a guild by tag, screenshots as evidence, and the accused guild's leadership publicly defending the member:

> "If I were GM of Beluga I would kick you out of the guild myself."
> — [Player-0112 (player), 2023-04-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=40093#p40093), Help & Support, *BELUGA is the LOOT STEALERS Guild*, pre-1.18.1

Internal loot-council favoritism (helm/formula reserved for guild-leader's clique, a PUG member kicked when their reserve conflicted) is documented as a distinct pattern under "Accountability"-style callout posts — see `behavior/group-play.md` Kick / interrupt section for the full example.


## Guild types: casual, hardcore, RP, PvP, national/language

**Casual/social guilds** explicitly market low-pressure pacing:

> "This guild is mainly for players who wish to laugh and have fun, while clearing PvE content smoother than last week."
> — [Player-0146 (player), 2024-01-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=85909#p85909), Guild Recruitment, *<Raven> recruiting raiders*, pre-1.18.1

**Semi-hardcore/progression raiding guilds** state discipline requirements up front:

> "Regelmäßige Teilnahme und aktives Mitwirken am Raid Fortschritt [Regular attendance and active participation in raid progress]; Zuverlässigkeit und Motivation [Reliability and motivation]"
> — [Player-0130 (player), 2023-08-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=57856#p57856), Guild Recruitment, *[GER] Die "Schmetterlingsbrigade" sucht Dich!*, pre-1.18.1

**Roleplay guilds** blend lore-driven guild identity with raid logistics in the same post (see The Scourge, quoted above under recruitment/raid-schedule) — RP and raiding are not treated as separate communities but layered in one guild.


**PvP/Horde-only or faction-locked guilds** state their focus plainly:

> "Nephilim (PVE/PVP) is started up by Morhax/Hornata and Nivea (brothers). We have established our place on Horde side (Horde only guild) 300+ members."
> — [Player-0118 (player), 2023-04-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42165#p42165), Guild Recruitment, *<Nephilim> PvE PvP Horde Only.*, pre-1.18.1

## Patterns for bots

1. **When advertising/joining a guild, state faction, focus (PvE/PvP/RP/social), raid days in server time + a GMT/timezone offset, and loot system** in that order — this is the template every real recruitment post follows.
2. **State loot priority explicitly and don't relitigate it mid-raid.** Use the community's own shorthand: `HR>SR>MS>OS`.
3. **Treat Discord as the operational layer** (sign-ups, SR declarations, voice) and the forum/in-game as the discovery layer — a bot recruiting or asking about guilds should reference "check the Discord" naturally.
4. **When describing a casual guild, lead with pace and friendliness, not clear rate/progression**; when describing a progression guild, lead with attendance/reliability requirements.
5. **If speaking a non-English language, stay fully in that language** for guild-recruitment style content (Spanish "hermandad", German, Ukrainian, Chinese threads are monolingual, not code-switched).
6. **Reference guild bank/guild house as earned progression**, not a given — a bot roleplaying a guild officer should describe them as milestones ("we're 3 months out from a guild house", [Player-0100 (player), 2022-08-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22591#p22591)).
7. **Never suggest or endorse GDKP** — it is banned server-wide; a bot should reflect that as settled policy ([Staff-01 (staff), 2024-06-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=97794#p97794)), not as one option among many.


## What not to do

- Do not run or suggest GDKP; it is against server rules.
- Do not sell off-spec-rolled loot for gold — guilds treat this as a bannable/kickable offense.
- Do not let a raid or officer clique quietly change loot rules mid-run to favor insiders — this is the exact shape of the worst-documented guild drama.
- Do not advertise a guild without stating a concrete raid time and timezone — vague recruitment posts read as low-effort.
- Do not treat a guild bank as free-for-all storage; established guilds gate access by role, main-character status, or "merit."
