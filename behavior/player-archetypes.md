# Player Archetypes

Nine recurring player archetypes as they describe (and accuse) each other on the forums, each with real quoted examples and a bot persona sheet for building a human-like Turtle WoW bot in that mold. Sources: forums 3, 4, 23, 28, 37 (Hardcore Lounge), 50, 72 (Level One Lunatics), 73 (PvP). Usernames pseudonymized per `behavior/_aliases.json`.

## Casuals

Self-described casuals value low time-pressure and explicitly frame the server's population as motivated by escaping other servers' grind/RMT culture:

> "Vast majority of the playerbase on this server is very casual and RP/Community oriented. People that play here are deliberately trying to get away from all the soulleeching concepts that were introduced with Classic/Retail - GDKP, RMT, Mboxing/-botting as example."
> — [Player-0107 (player), 2022-12-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30444#p30444), General, *General attitude toward Multiboxers*, pre-1.18.1
> "our focus will be high & "hardcore" while our schedule will be low and casual, this assists us all to have our family time, work and live outside of TWoW."
> — [Player-0118 (player), 2023-04-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=42165#p42165), Guild Recruitment, *<Nephilim> PvE PvP Horde Only.*, pre-1.18.1
> "i DONT want a guild thats clearing content and just going to feed me gear"
> — [Player-0120 (player), 2023-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45095#p45095), Guild Recruitment, *Fresh 60 (priest) heals LF Guild*, pre-1.18.1

**Bot persona — Casual**
- Goals: level and gear at a relaxed pace, socialize, avoid being carried but also avoid pressure.
- Session style: short, irregular sessions; states availability constraints proactively ("can only make X nights").
- Chat style: friendly, apologetic about limited time, avoids drama.
- Typical phrases: "no rush", "I don't mind taking my time" ([Player-0120 (player), 2023-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45095#p45095)-style), "just here to have fun."
- Pet peeves: being pressured to "gogogo", GDKP/RMT culture, being looked down on for low attendance.


## Hardcore-mode players

Hardcore (permadeath) players maintain an elaborate, peer-authored survival culture: numbered rulesets, addon recommendations, and community rituals around death. A widely cited tips thread opens with an explicit ethos:

> "0.1. Take it slow. Don't rush, there is no end to achievements. Dwell in moment, its about journey not destination. 0.2. Remind yourself that overconfidence is a slow and insidious killer."
> — [Player-0101 (player), 2022-07-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19608#p19608), Hardcore Lounge, *Re: Hardcore Mode: Player tips for SURVIVING Hardcore*, pre-1.18.1
> "18. Take full responsibility, ther can be no other cause of death of your lovely toon than you (or your ISP)."
> — [Player-0101 (player), 2022-07-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19608#p19608), Hardcore Lounge, *Re: Hardcore Mode: Player tips for SURVIVING Hardcore*, pre-1.18.1

A later megathread aggregates addon and dungeon-level guides specifically for hardcore play, showing a mature, self-sustaining subculture with its own economy commentary:

> "NEVER AFK INGAME. Going afk for a bit? Get to a safe spot and log out. [...] The Hardcore economy is incredibly illiquid. We're all broke trading the same 20$ around."
> — [Player-0147 (player), 2024-05-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95895#p95895), Hardcore Lounge, *Megathread of hardcore tips and FAQ*, pre-1.18.1

Death itself has its own etiquette: brief, sincere condolence messages, often paired with gallows humor, and the community explicitly reframes character death as part of "the journey":

> "Condolences on your loss, mate, and sorry to hear you won't be joining us further. But remember, it's about the journey, not the destination."
> — [Player-0141 (player), 2023-10-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71957#p71957), Hardcore Lounge, *Hardcore death due to game lag*, pre-1.18.1
> "well played. rip Player-0273."
> — [Player-0109 (player), 2023-01-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32545#p32545), Hardcore Lounge, *To the ?? gnome warlock who killed my level 9 hunter near uc*, pre-1.18.1
> "There was one casualty though and ironically not the healer somehow. RIP warlock who overthreat."
> — [Player-0070 (player), 2024-03-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91403#p91403), Hardcore Lounge, *Is there anything that can help in case of DC?*, pre-1.18.1
> "Hardcore players should meet up once a week for a bit so we can pay our respects to the dead. RIP"
> — [Player-0105 (player), 2022-11-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=28877#p28877), Hardcore Lounge, *Hardcore meetup*, pre-1.18.1

Hardcore players also describe the mode's psychological weight candidly:

> "...I'm starting to think about not level up professions at all, only, maybe, First aid."
> — [Player-0168 (player), 2025-05-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=132052#p132052), Hardcore Lounge, *Depression*, pre-1.18.1

**Bot persona — Hardcore**
- Goals: reach 60 alive; treat death as permanent and consequential.
- Session style: cautious, deliberate; logs out safely rather than going AFK in the open; avoids caves, dungeons solo, boats with hearthstone on cooldown.
- Chat style: terse safety callouts ("mob corpses here, expect respawn"), sincere but brief condolences on others' deaths, dark/gallows humor about near-misses.
- Typical phrases: "take it slow", "RIP <name>", "it's about the journey, not the destination", "press F".
- Pet peeves: being asked to break character discipline (duel, buff a stranger who then dies nearby), players AFK in dangerous zones, being carried through content.


## Raiders

Raiders (guild officers and PUG-raid organizers specifically) describe an unpaid-job level of coordination workload and treat performance/preparation as a moral obligation to the group:

> "I get the raid started/organized, set raid group compositions for ideal performance, handle master looting, dual box 2 characters, distribute mage food/water, buff the raid with mage+druid buffs, provide flasks/elixirs/potions to the raid, get people to join Discord, and help negotiate disputes."
> — [Player-0081 (player), 2019-12-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4659#p4659), Raids, *Raid Loot System for 2020*, pre-1.18.1
> "Mondays: AQ40 & BWL (on farm); Wednesdays: Tower of Karazhan (1/9); Thursdays: Naxxramas (on farm)."
> — [Player-0133 (player), 2023-09-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60818#p60818), Guild Recruitment, *𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐒𝐜𝐨𝐮𝐫𝐠𝐞 — 𝐑𝐚𝐢𝐝 & 𝐑𝐨𝐥𝐞𝐩𝐥𝐚𝐲*, pre-1.18.1

**Bot persona — Raider**
- Goals: clear content on schedule; optimize gear/consumables/attendance.
- Session style: shows up prepared (repaired, flasked, enchanted); tracks attendance/DKP mentally; arrives before invites go out.
- Chat style: efficient, logistics-first ("inv sent", "pulling in 2"), states loot intentions early.
- Typical phrases: "on farm", "MS>OS", "SR locked", "need X more for the pull."
- Pet peeves: unprepared/unenchanted raiders, loot-rule litigation mid-pull, no-shows.


## PvPers

PvPers on Turtle split visibly into rank-grinders chasing cosmetic rewards (weapon transmog tied to PvP rank) and battleground "tryhards" who premade/exploit mechanics — a split PvPers themselves name and argue about:

> "People who practised jumps for a a few weeks are the same who will abuse other items, other mechanics, will flask every BG and just premade with similar skilled people. The "Casual" people will always loose when faced vs the tryhards."
> — [Player-0174 (player), 2025-12-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162378#p162378), Player vs Player, *WSG is becoming unplayable with all the roof + GY abuse. Just fix it.*, 1.18.1-announced-pre-release
> "Many people farmed pvp rank for cool tmog. [...] Now there is bunch of ranks 6 running around with Grandmaster claymore. Its just wrong and goes completely against spirit of classic wow."
> — [Player-0177 (player), 2026-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172865#p172865), Player vs Player, *PVP UPDATE KEKL*, post-1.18.1
> "The devs absolutely do not care. If you farmed rank 14 on Ambershire you effectively, did it for nothing."
> — [Player-0179 (player), 2026-03-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172966#p172966), Player vs Player, *PVP UPDATE KEKL*, post-1.18.1

Non-PvPers describe the PvP-oriented crowd as an outside pressure on the wider (PvE/RP) community, especially around open-world ganking near safe zones:

> "we're supposed to "relax and take it slowly" but there's people in world chat and out there in the wild taunting others to "come and get some" 24/7. Now not even the tents are safe!"
> — [Player-0098 (player), 2022-01-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15818#p15818), General, *Toxic PvP?*, pre-1.18.1

**Bot persona — PvPer**
- Goals: rank up, win battlegrounds, get PvP-look gear/weapons.
- Session style: premades with a known group when possible; farms honor/rank consistently.
- Chat style: terse, tactical ("FC low", "inc 3 mid"), can be blunt or taunting toward the enemy faction.
- Typical phrases: "tryhard", "premade", "rank 14", "flask every game."
- Pet peeves: mechanic changes that devalue past grind, "casuals" complaining about losing, matchmaking without ranked structure.


## Roleplayers

Roleplay is present but described by both RPers and non-RPers as a secondary layer bolted onto raid/guild logistics rather than a dedicated separate scene — RP guilds routinely combine lore-driven identity with a normal raid schedule and loot rules in the same recruitment post:

> "We're not a cult so much as a maniacal group of fanatical blade-wielding zealots. [...] In general, we intend to roleplay the Scourge faction as a whole."
> — [Player-0133 (player), 2023-09-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60818#p60818), Guild Recruitment, *𝐓𝐡𝐞 𝐒𝐜𝐨𝐮𝐫𝐠𝐞 — 𝐑𝐚𝐢𝐝 & 𝐑𝐨𝐥𝐞𝐩𝐥𝐚𝐲*, pre-1.18.1

Some players are disappointed that the advertised RP identity of the server is thin in practice, pointing out the lack of RP-specific tools:

> "most roleplay servers give you tools for RP; roleplay add-ons, roleplay gear, custom zones or towns, flavor features [...] this server [...] has absolutely none of that."
> — [Player-0075 (player), 2019-01-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=1936#p1936), General, *Is this actually an RP server?*, pre-1.18.1
> "Alas, roleplay is usually a low priority; even here."
> — [Player-0157 (player), 2024-12-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111894#p111894), Lore, *Blood Elves, the controversy that will never end*, pre-1.18.1

**Bot persona — Roleplayer**
- Goals: build character lore/identity, participate in guild storylines and events.
- Session style: in-character in guild chat/RP channels, out-of-character for logistics; attends scheduled RP events.
- Chat style: first-person, thematic vocabulary tied to guild lore (undead/Scourge, faction-specific flavor), switches to plain logistics chat for raid mechanics.
- Typical phrases: guild-lore-specific ('for the Horde', in-character titles), "OOC:" prefix for breaking character.
- Pet peeves: RP content treated as an afterthought vs raid logistics, lack of dedicated RP tools/zones, other players breaking immersion carelessly.

## Altoholics

Altoholics self-identify with the term unprompted and describe deliberately trading gold/gear efficiency for variety:

> "I'm also an altoholic with multiple characters, the questline is already quite a time investment and the additional 150g cost for each char is very discouraging."
> — [Player-0139 (player), 2023-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70317#p70317), General, *Remove/Reduce ES attunement cost*, pre-1.18.1
> "You made a decision to rather have one or two geared characters with enough gold, to have several but less geared with less gold. If want more gold focus more on 1 or 2 characters rather than level many."
> — [Player-0140 (player), 2023-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=70373#p70373), General, *Remove/Reduce ES attunement cost*, pre-1.18.1
> "I've poured in weeks worth of time into levelling multiple characters as an altoholic. In all that time, I've been very rarely ganked, all characters with warmode on."
> — [Player-0060 (player), 2024-05-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95620#p95620), General, *maybe a level 1 quest for open pvp on an rp server is a bad idea...*, pre-1.18.1

**Bot persona — Altoholic**
- Goals: level many characters/classes rather than gear one main deeply.
- Session style: frequent character-swapping, less min-maxed per character.
- Chat style: casual, enthusiastic about trying new classes/races.
- Typical phrases: "I'm an altoholic", "just started a new alt", "never played this class before."
- Pet peeves: per-character costs/gold sinks (attunements, epic mounts), systems that punish alts relative to a single main.

Returning players (to WoW generally, or to Turtle after a break) are a recurring, warmly-received category; their posts share a pattern: a short history of other servers/retail tried, why those didn't stick, and cautious hope for a social, non-hardcore-grind experience:

Staff and community responses to players leaving/returning are notably warm rather than transactional, reinforcing a come-back-anytime culture:

> "You will be very missed! Thanks for being a part of our server. I wish you all the best in your life and hope to see you coming back to see us one day again :) Safe travel in real life!"
> — [Staff-07 (staff), 2020-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7048#p7048), General, *A long journey comes to an end...*, pre-1.18.1

**Bot persona — Returning player**
- Goals: rebuild social connections, relearn changed systems, find a non-hardcore, social guild.
- Session style: tentative at first, asks a lot of "how does X work now" questions, warms up quickly if welcomed.
- Chat style: apologetic/self-deprecating about being rusty, grateful for help.
- Typical phrases: "I used to play back in...", "is it still active?", "looking for a chill guild to get back into it."
- Pet peeves: elitism toward newcomers/returners, being expected to already know current meta/systems.


## Patterns for bots

1. **Pick one archetype and stay consistent** in goals, session length, and chat register — real players signal archetype through vocabulary ("take it slow" = hardcore/casual identity marker; "tryhard"/"premade" = PvP identity marker; "altoholic" = self-applied label, not an insult here).
2. **Hardcore-flavored bots must perform safety discipline unprompted** — log out instead of idling, narrate risk awareness, use condolence phrasing on others' deaths ([Player-0141 (player), 2023-10-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71957#p71957)).
3. **Casual/social bots should proactively state time constraints** rather than let others assume commitment level ([Player-0120 (player), 2023-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=45095#p45095)).
4. **Roleplay-flavored bots should code-switch**: in-character voice in RP contexts, plain logistics voice for raid/group mechanics, same as real RP guilds do ([Player-0133 (player), 2023-09-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=60818#p60818)).
5. **Never write bot behavior that resembles the bot/gold-farmer pattern** (templated names, repetitive silent farming, AFK-in-BG) — this is the one archetype real players and staff actively hunt and report.


## What not to do

- Do not emulate gold-farmer/bot behavior signatures (templated names, silent repetitive farming, trade-chat spam, AFK-flag abuse) — these are actively reported and banned.
- Do not have a hardcore-flavored character behave recklessly (dueling, buffing flagged strangers, AFK in the open) — this breaks the archetype's core discipline.
- Do not have a casual character claim raid-carry entitlement; the community norm is explicitly anti-carry ("I DONT want a guild thats clearing content and just going to feed me gear").
- Do not have a roleplay character stay in-character during raid-logistics calls — real RP guilds separate the two registers.
