## The Discord channel (`#pvp-general`, Turtle WoW Europe)
A second, later body of PvP-culture evidence comes from the archived Turtle WoW Europe Discord channel **`#pvp-general`** (266,785 messages, 2021-05-24 → 2025-11-01). It is player-heavy — balance and matchup chatter dominate — and its culture value is in the **queue/ranking economy, premade disputes, War Mode reality and arena reception** rather than in objective callouts. Authors are pseudonymized here per `synthesis/CONVENTIONS.md`; citations `[[d:pvp-general#id]]` resolve in `structured/discord/evidence-pvp-general.jsonl`. Era tags: `pre-1.18.1` (< 2025-10-03), `1.18.1-announced-pre-release` (2025-10-03 .. 2026-03-19), `post-1.18.1` (≥ 2026-03-20). The corpus ends 2025-11-01, so it contains **no `post-1.18.1` material**. Agreement: `consensus` | `single source` | `contested`.

### Queue behaviour and which battlegrounds actually pop
- A daily **Call to Arms** rotation doubles win honour; on WSG/Blood Ring days the losing side also gets some. By mid-2025 all BGs except Sunnyglade Valley ran regularly, and players tracked the rotation with the third-party `turtle-timer` site [[d:pvp-general#1383395973497094226]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- Which BG pops depends on the daily: on AB or WSG day it almost always pops, an AV can too; on Blood Arena or Sunnyglade day the other BGs pop randomly, "because everyone hates the custom bgs" [[d:pvp-general#1380460999785578498]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- **Sunnyglade Valley was chronically underplayed** — "I honestly like Sunnyglade. Too bad almost nobody queues for it" — and staff polled the channel on removing it before the 1.18.1 notes disabled it [[d:pvp-general#1381904693474758696]], [[d:pvp-general#1381904006032658533]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1 / 1.18.1-announced-pre-release).
- **Faction queue asymmetry:** Turtle staff describe Alliance as having "faster queues but less coordination" [[d:pvp-general#1381889760037179444]] (`single source`, pre-1.18.1). Group queueing was real and easy ("you can group queue bgs ez") [[d:pvp-general#1091769380863483985]] (pre-1.18.1).

### Premades, ghost queueing and queue integrity
- **Premade/group-queue status is contested inside the same year.** "Premades are disabled on BGs on Turtle" [[d:pvp-general#1093569073578770566]] (2023-04-06) vs "Premades are not disabled" [[d:pvp-general#1134177832835240007]] (2023-07-27); players still described "semi premades in AB and WSG" and a persistent Alliance premade into 2025 [[d:pvp-general#1089541071136112793]], [[d:pvp-general#1341466559645745223]] (`contested`, pre-1.18.1). Likely a group-queue size/matching change, not a clean hard disable — consistent with this document's "Ghost Queue Mafia" framing above; keep it contested, do not assert a hard disable.
- The channel repeats the same **ghost queueing** complaint (queuing on an alt / not entering to gain a numbers advantage) that the forum calls the "Ghost Queue Mafia" problem — same dispute, player-side.

### Honour farming and the ranking economy
- Turtle ran the **vanilla weekly-standings honour system** with a weekly flush; RP gains were capped at ~13,000/week and R14 needed ~60,000 RP, so a perfect top-bracket run still took ~5–6 weeks [[d:pvp-general#1115908001480572938]], [[d:pvp-general#1116048119692529774]] (`single source` / contested, pre-1.18.1).
- **Pool boosting in practice:** low brackets were padded with "dummy rankers" (inactive/ghost accounts); a single HK per week was enough to enter the pool, and ~800 dummy rankers sat in the lower brackets in 2022 ("1 hk to get above the 800 dummy rankers") [[d:pvp-general#998635211430567966]], [[d:pvp-general#1033363878735786114]], [[d:pvp-general#1028662404265037824]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- A **no-honour glyph** (honour gain cut to 1–5%) let already-ranked players keep queueing without distorting the pool [[d:pvp-general#921800202749370379]] (staff-register post, pre-1.18.1).
- **Per-kill honour** is reduced by level difference, victim rank, nearby group/raid size, and a **25% diminishing return for repeated kills of the same player within 24 h**, falling to 75% on the second kill and shared among all nearby players [[d:pvp-general#1212658441215287326]], [[d:pvp-general#1119777672583708753]], [[d:pvp-general#1106205535763714178]] (single source on coefficients, consensus on the DR, pre-1.18.1).
- Observed values: **~455 honour per WSG flag capture** [[d:pvp-general#1059641996056203347]] and **~400–500 honour per open-world War Mode kill** [[d:pvp-general#1260222976465305642]] (single source each, pre-1.18.1).
- Staff call the grind degenerate ("the optimal way to rank up is not improving your gameplay, it's increasing how much time you spend grinding honour") and planned "a few requirements of participation to not receive diminished honor from a match" [[d:pvp-general#1381887128253763644]], [[d:pvp-general#1252020200337834046]] (staff, pre-1.18.1).

### World PvP, War Mode and ganking
- **War Mode is opt-in via a glyph/NPC, taken mostly for the XP bonus.** The toggle could be disabled once before level 60 [[d:pvp-general#1301687319933227109]]; "90% of people that use it don't even fight back"; "the amount of organic pvp encounters is absolutely miniscule compared to an actual PvP server … If you level War Mode expect bored 60s … to randomly drive by you" [[d:pvp-general#1288438284854497351]]; and "80–90% ish of other players you will encounter are hardcore or PvE" [[d:pvp-general#1288438002334306314]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- **Asymmetric flagging is the main open-world exploit:** un-flagged PvE players can "charge up their pyroblasts on you from their save pve mode" with no reciprocal risk — the same asymmetry KB staff describe (2023-03-02) [[d:pvp-general#1260222777483333712]] (single source, pre-1.18.1).
- **Dishonourable kills came from civilian NPCs** under the early rule — "Killing low level players does not grant any dhk penalty" — which KB staff later superseded (2023-12-12: DHKs for killing grey-level players) [[d:pvp-general#1042784841055354930]], [[d:pvp-general#1042785048404967424]] (consensus, pre-1.18.1; **superseded by** [Torta (staff), 2023-12-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80839#p80839)).
- **Bounty Boards existed but were unmaintained:** Alliance bounty quests pointed at the same inactive player for ~6 months [[d:pvp-general#1095357463202643968]] (single source, pre-1.18.1) — substance agrees with this document's "unconfirmed for gameplay" caveat; treat as unmaintained, not working.

### Battleground culture and the parkour debate (staff rationale)
- **WSG flag carriers are slowed 30%** to force contact — the same anti-stall mechanism as the 1.18.0 stacking FC debuff [[d:pvp-general#1006068996988215387]] (single source, pre-1.18.1).
- The channel adds Turtle staff's own framing of the **WSG jump/parkour** dispute that this document records player-side: keeping the spots makes WSG "a glorified parkour gym" that punishes newcomers, removing them "make[s] it something some veterans dislike", so the chosen middle ground is to **discourage** the spots rather than remove the maps [[d:pvp-general#1381882667485888514]]; a majority does not want a BG decided by "which team's druid can go the longest without messing up their funny parkour → dash away combo" [[d:pvp-general#1381891955088166912]] (staff, pre-1.18.1).
- Class-agnostic AV map lore seen in-channel (not culture-facing but useful context): IBGY is "infinitely more defensible than SH", Horde warmasters "aoe much harder than the ally npc versions", Alliance bunkers could be capped from a second-floor weapon rack, and the aid tent/SPGY can be safespotted by hugging the mountainside past IWB [[d:pvp-general#1015344576342523935]], [[d:pvp-general#1015391947927588935]], [[d:pvp-general#1015315873084493935]], [[d:pvp-general#1119713704854945983]] (consensus, pre-1.18.1).

### Arena reception and the 1.18.x hype cycle
- **Blood Ring is Turtle's arena**, historically **2v2**, later **3v3** on one map; it often did not pop because rewards were thin (~3 HKs per game, "there isn't enough interest to have 1 game going even when it is the BG of the day") [[d:pvp-general#1381294933796978769]], [[d:pvp-general#1224429862211682434]], [[d:pvp-general#1249389435485819054]], [[d:pvp-general#1281287328178114570]], [[d:pvp-general#1212527345353887795]] (`consensus`, pre-1.18.1).
- Staff pushed **cross-faction Blood Ring** internally — "X-Faction Blood Ring is a big thing I've been saying needs in, but man vanilla hates crossfaction PvP lol" [[d:pvp-general#1252023586114375731]] (staff, pre-1.18.1) — and KB records Blood Ring going cross-faction.
- The channel carries the run-up to the **1.18.x PvP overhaul**: rated arenas and the best PvP gear expected in 1.18.1, honour rework in 1.18.1, PvP aura in 1.18.0 [[d:pvp-general#1403706029795180635]], [[d:pvp-general#1401628696846008361]], [[d:pvp-general#1398256120933384284]] (consensus, 1.18.1-announced-pre-release). ## Patterns for bots
1. If your bot is PvP-flagged/War Mode on, treat being ganked/camped as expected content, not a bug — vent in chat with frustration or dark humor ("got 1-tapped again lol"), don't file an in-character ticket, unless the situation matches an official griefing definition from `rules-and-hard-limits.md` §4.
2. Use real in-BG shorthand: `inc`, `def`, base-name abbreviations (ST/BS/LM/GM for Arathi Basin), and short tactical macros rather than full sentences under pressure.
3. Name-drop specific battlegrounds with their earned reputations: WSG and AB as the reliably-popping "normal" BGs, Sunnyglade Valley and (as of the 1.18.1-era patch) Thorn Gorge as chronically underplayed/"honor isn't worth it" BGs, AV as the map with a live Alliance-disadvantage grievance, Blood Ring as the dueling-arena minigame.
4. When a bot loses a lopsided BG (e.g. facing an obvious premade), a human-like reaction is a queue-dodging or premade complaint ("fair fight" framing), not a rules accusation, unless there's specific evidence (alt accounts, dodgepremade-style timing) — see § Battleground behavior.
5. A dueling-flavored character can reasonably claim informal tournament pedigree or challenge someone to "come duel me" as trash talk in an argument — this is attested forum behavior, not just in-game.
6. Frame War Mode as an explicit risk/reward choice in character talk ("I turned it on for the XP, I know I signed up for getting ganked") rather than as a grievance in itself — that is the dominant, consensus framing across the corpus even among people who dislike being on the receiving end.
7. Cross-faction grouping for quests is a real, sometimes-confusing Turtle-specific mechanic — a bot can plausibly express the same "wait, why did grouping with them flag me" confusion seen in the corpus.

## What not to do
- Do not have a bot "report" ordinary PvP losses (being ganked/camped while flagged) as a rules violation in character — staff explicitly say this is not griefing; real players who do this get told the same thing repeatedly (see `rules-and-hard-limits.md` §4).
- Do not invent a confirmed dev conspiracy or bias claim as settled fact — the AV Alliance-bias and "devs hate premades" claims are attested as contested player opinion only, never staff-confirmed; a human-like bot should voice them as opinion ("feels like...", "I swear..."), not certainty.
- Do not describe Bounty Boards or Dishonorable-Kill consequences as active, working systems — the sampled evidence shows players uncertain whether/where these features are even implemented.
- Do not stage or claim an organized guild revenge campaign as if it were a common, documented pattern — it isn't attested in this corpus; individual venting is the well-evidenced pattern instead.
