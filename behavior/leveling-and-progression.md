## Turtle-specific leveling features

### Slow & Steady

Official mechanic, introduced with Patch 1.18.0 and since revised. Full staff text:

> "If you really love questing and leveling, pick the **Slow & Steady** leveling challenge when creating your character. **Slow & Steady Rules** — XP from creature kills, both in the open world and dungeons, is reduced by 50%. Quest XP stays the same, so completing objectives is still the fastest path forward. You'll lose 5% of your current level's XP on death, everywhere except battlegrounds and PvP." Rewards are handed out every 10 levels (Speedy's Starter Pack at 10 ... Speedy's Victory Chest at 60, including a mount and 200 Donation Tokens).
> — [**Staff-07 (staff)**, 2019-04-07, with an embedded 2025-08-07 update](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2459#p2459)

That same post was later edited to add: with Patch 1.18.0, "XP gained from creature kills in dungeons will be halved. This change comes in response to a growing number of players using dungeon speed-leveling to exploit the donation system." A player objected that this punishes the wrong people ("The increasing painfulness of Slow & Steady to level is a high deterrent to getting 'free' shop points... incredibly obvious attempt to push people to buying points") — [Player-0309 (player), quoted 2025-08-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144665#p144665) — and staff answered directly: "players speed-leveling through dungeons are definitely [not] here for the journey, that's totally the point of Slow & Steady challenge. This change was long overdue... we want players to actually experience[e the content]" — [**Staff-07 (staff)**, same thread, 2025-08-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144665#p144665) (**contested**: player reads it as monetization pressure; staff frame it as closing a speed-leveling exploit — both dated and cited).

A moderator closing an unrelated thread used the mode's name as a catchphrase, showing how far "Slow & Steady" has become community shorthand: "Please stay tuned for the announcements and stay slow and steady as all of our best turtles do." — [Player-0078 (player, thread-closing reply), 2020-11-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=8346#p8346).

### Tents, taverns and rested XP

Tents are a crafted Survival-profession item, central to both roleplay culture and (formerly) a rested-XP exploit:

- Mechanic, explained in a player guide: "For most players, the answer to this question is the Traveler's Tents! These are crafted items that last for 30 minutes. While you stand in or near the tent you gain a significant boost to your rested XP gains. The best part is, the boost from tents can stack!" — [Player-0081 (player), 2019-08-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3641#p3641).
- Staff on why Hardcore mode had to account for tents: "Hardcore Mode was not Hardcore at all. Players still could use rested XP from tents and level on x1 instead of going through hardcore adventure. We didn't want to close an important and fun social interaction such as 'Tent Party' for Hardcore players so I had to come up with a compromise." — [**Staff-07 (staff)**, 2020-06-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=6600#p6600).
- The social/RP side, unprompted: "I love tents. The RP feel when I made one in the Badlands with a campfire. Also the social aspect of it. Simply AWESOME." — [Player-0315 (player), 2022-02-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=16845#p16845), later quoted approvingly by staff in a different reply — [**Staff-02 (staff)**, 2023-06-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51349#p51349).

### LFT (the built-in "Looking For Turtle" dungeon-finder tool)

Praised as a population/QoL feature by suggesters before it existed, then subject to normal queue/etiquette complaints once live:

- Pre-launch ask: "it would be awesome and would definitely boost the server population if you could somehow manage to add a similar to WoTLK dungeon finder [tool]" — [Player-0307 (player), 2019-12-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4725#p4725).
- Real queue-time complaint tying it to a design debate about the death penalty: "Please remove the death penalty in dungeons, no one wants to lose experience as a tank, or be responsible for dying as a healer. The second day I can't get into dungeons. The waiting time is over 50 minutes." — [Player-0305 (player), quoted 2023-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33100#p33100), answered sarcastically: "Forget LFT, roll HC.. U will get into dungeons with ppl who do not like dying." — [Player-0228 (player), same thread](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=33100#p33100).
- Concrete bug/etiquette friction reported directly to staff: "The last several times I've used LFT, it's bugged out. Some issues include: 1. It finds a group, but the 'Let's do this' button is greyed out... 2. It tells us that the party has too many damage dealers (even when it doesn't)... 3. It occasionally messes up roles. It recently formed a group with two healers and no tanks." — [Player-0311 (player), quoted in a staff reply](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66403#p66403), staff reply by [**Staff-02 (staff)**, 2023-09-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66403#p66403).
- "LFT" is also referenced as a world-chat filter keyword alongside LFG/WTS/WTB (see `economy.md`), i.e. players treat it as one more spammy channel tag to filter for or against.

### Rested XP mechanics (general)

Standard rest/inn mechanic, reinforced by staff design commentary above (tents) and by ordinary support requests when it misbehaves: "My problem is that rested xp doesnt rise at all... I usually logout in any Inn or capital city. Even if I log in after three days i gain nothing from rested xp. Is it bug? I have a 32 hunter" — [Player-0313 (player), 2023-12-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83234#p83234). Staff diagnosis, same thread: "Maybe you took the Challenge of the Exhausted?" — [**Staff-02 (staff)**, 2023-12-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83235#p83235) — i.e. the Glyph of Exhaustion opt-out mode (no rested XP by design) is the most common cause of this specific support question.

### Cross-faction grouping

Turtle WoW runs **multiple realms with different cross-faction rules** — this is an important nuance a bot should get right:

- On the main/legacy realm, staff have repeatedly refused cross-faction battleground registration on identity grounds: "enable cross-faction registration for battlegrounds ^ This might sound easy but it's not. Sorry, I'm just not doing it... I'm really against it. It'd destroy what's left of each faction's identities." — [**Staff-07 (staff)**, 2020-09-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=7701#p7701).
- Cross-faction grouping does exist for some content: "There's already cross faction gameplay and paladins and shamans can now be in the same raids" — [Player-0316 (player), quoted 2022-08-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22098#p22098), reply by [**Staff-08 (staff)**, same thread](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=22098#p22098).
- By 2025, the realm split is explicit and staff-confirmed: "For a non-crossfaction server, I recommend Tel'Abim. We do not plan to remove crossfaction from Nordanaar." — [**Staff-01 (staff)**, 2025-06-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137136#p137136). (**staff, current as of mid-2025**: Nordanaar = cross-faction, Tel'Abim = not.)

## Routes and pacing

Time-to-60 numbers vary widely by playstyle, and posters openly disagree with each other about what's "fast" — this is **contested**, not consensus, and every number below is dated and self-reported (no staff/analytics-sourced figure was found):

| Reported time to 60 | Context | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ~130–170 hours | "It takes the average dedicated leveling player about 130-170 hours to get from 1 to 60" | [Player-0136 (player), 2023-11-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=77774#p77774) |
| ~150 hours (6 days /played) | "Assume you play for 6 days to get from 1-60, that is ~150 hours of playtime" | [Player-0303 (player), 2025-07-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137716#p137716) |
| ~72 hours (3 days /played) | "i did my first character when i started on turtle /played 3 days to 60" | [Player-0314 (player), 2024-05-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94600#p94600) |
| 190 hours, Hardcore | "I have an Inferno/Hardcore level 60 character. It took me 190 hours and over a year of real-time investment." | [Player-0310 (player), 2025-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162641#p162641) |

Bracket-level pacing claims from the same 2025-07-01 thread, worth quoting because they disagree with each other in the same conversation: "You can knock out 1-10 in an hour. Two hours if you're going slow." — [Player-0018 (player), quoted](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137716#p137716); countered: "From 1-10 in an hour? Are you a wizard? Even with the use of tents (rested xp) that is quite fast. I think that on average people take 2 to 3 hours to go from 1-10... First dungeon on horde side being at lvl 14-ish, I reckon you can do a dungeon before reaching the 10 hour mark." — [Player-0303 (player), 2025-07-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=137716#p137716).

Milestone/session-reporting style, real example: "I dinged 60 on my warrior and I intended to buy the helm, but the choice is not too obvious if it's going to get changed in the future in an unspecified way." — [Player-0304 (player), 2023-06-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51280#p51280) — note the pattern: the ding itself is stated flatly, in the same breath as the *next* practical gearing decision, not celebrated on its own.

Hardcore-specific leveling-route description (concrete, step-numbered farming loop through low-level zones, ending in a gold total): "...18. Selling everything along the way. At the end of which you should be sitting on about 4-5 gold." — [Player-0102 (player), 2022-07-04, Hardcore Lounge](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=19673#p19673) — a "guaranteed" route the author reports repeating across six characters, all reaching level 20, "most died at various intervals after that."

## Gearing paths at level 60

- **Pre-raid BiS talk exists and is framed around specific boss-drop items** for filling raid roles before a guild's first raid: "to get pre raid bis items like sash of mercy, elemental attuned blade, krol blade and items of that sort so we can fill those pre raid dps/tanking/healing suits etc for our new raiders. Better to have newcomers in boe epics than random greens from questing/world drops, after all it makes raiding overall efficient." — [Player-0308 (player), 2020-03-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=5187#p5187).
- **PvP gear is described as an obligation more than a reward**, a recurring player complaint about the honor/rank system: "you need gear to pvp, but you shouldnt have to pvp in order to pve, and vice versa. I personally play battlegrounds because they are fun, and grind gear/ranks because I am forced to, in order to enjoy the battlegrounds." — [Player-0312 (player), 2021-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14006#p14006).
- **Consumable/flask cost is treated as part of "being geared" for raids**, tying gearing directly into the economy: "shaman MC had several near BiS shamans participating. And they used collectively well over 1000g in consumes, easily." — [Player-0302 (player), 2024-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91055#p91055).

## Profession choices

Concrete class-to-profession pairing statements are rare in this archive (a gap, noted below); what *is* well documented is profession-leveling cost and the mechanical reasons players pick a profession:

- Survival (Turtle-specific) is chosen specifically to unlock Traveler's Tents for the rested-XP synergy described above — see the Player-0081 2019-08-03 quote.
- Mining is picked partly for the ability to self-enchant gathering tools cheaply: "these can also be enchanted if you want... shouldn't cost you more than 2g" — [Player-0276 (player), 2019-02-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2118#p2118).
- Tailoring's 1–300 leveling cost is treated as a known, budgetable gold sink (under 50g, see `economy.md`), which players weigh against the profession's output value.
- A representative "should this profession pull its weight" debate: "I think there's a few different merits in changing some or all of the stuff produced through engineering to be useable either untrained or, as this post suggests, through mastery of something like..." — [Player-0306 (player), 2022-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=23475#p23475).

## Dungeon finder / group etiquette

Beyond the LFT bug/queue complaints above, the clearest etiquette signal in the archive is indirect: players filter LFT messages the same way they filter WTS/WTB spam (see `economy.md`), meaning LFT call-outs in world chat are perceived as noise by non-participants, not as a community bulletin board. No post in the archive uses the literal phrase "dungeon finder etiquette."

## Patterns for bots

1. **Session-report template:** state the ding flatly, then pivot immediately to the next practical decision — `Dinged [N] on my [class]. [next decision/question].` Don't editorialize about the achievement itself.
2. **Pacing-claim template, always hedge with a number and invite disagreement (this is how real threads run):** `You can do 1-10 in about [X] hours[, maybe more/less depending on Y].` Expect and model pushback with a different number from another "player."
3. **Slow & Steady awareness:** a bot roleplaying a veteran should know the rule set cold — "XP from creature kills (open world and dungeons) is reduced by 50%. Quest XP is unaffected. 5% XP loss on death outside BGs/PvP." — and should know it changed once (dungeon-kill XP halved further, patch 1.18.0) specifically to stop donation-shop exploitation via speed-leveling.
4. **Tent-party callout, RP register:** `Anyone want to drop a tent at [location]? Free rested XP.` — matches real usage and the social framing players give it.
5. **Realm-awareness template:** don't assume cross-faction grouping is universal — `Are we on Nordanaar or Tel'Abim?` is a legitimate, sourced disambiguation a knowledgeable player would ask.
6. **Pre-raid gearing checklist phrasing:** name specific BoE items by name when discussing "pre-raid BiS," not just "get better gear" — mirrors how real players talk (see Player-0308 example).

## What not to do (drawn from complaints in these sources)

- Don't suggest cross-faction battleground registration as an easy fix — staff have explicitly and repeatedly rejected it on identity grounds.
- Don't assume LFT works smoothly — real players report greyed-out buttons, role-miscounting, and 50-minute queues; a bot roleplaying a frustrated player has real material to draw on here.
- Don't treat "dungeon speed-leveling" as harmless efficiency — it's specifically what triggered the 1.18.0 Slow & Steady nerf, because staff tied it to donation-shop exploitation.
- Don't invent a single "correct" time-to-60 — real players disagree by more than 2x (72–190 hours) depending on mode (normal vs. Hardcore) and playstyle; a bot should hedge, not state one number as fact.

## Gaps (not found in sources)

- No systematic "class → profession pair" guide/consensus post was found (e.g. a single thread laying out "Warriors: Mining/Blacksmithing, Mages: Tailoring/Enchanting" etc.) — profession choice is discussed piecemeal, tied to specific mechanical payoffs (tents, self-enchants), not as a per-class table.
- The literal phrase "dungeon finder etiquette" does not occur in the archive; etiquette norms had to be inferred from adjacent complaints (queue times, chat-filtering behavior, LFT bug reports).
- No explicit zone-by-level-bracket leveling-route guide (e.g. "10-20: Westfall, then Redridge") was found as a single authoritative thread in the sampled data; leveling-route discussion in the archive is mostly reactive (complaints about population density per bracket) rather than prescriptive.
