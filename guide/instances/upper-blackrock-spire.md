# Upper Blackrock Spire (UBRS)

Vanilla level-60 Blackrock Spire dungeon, up to 10 players. Its main purpose is the Onyxia and Blackwing Lair attunement chain. On Turtle it is entry-level content that guilds run weekly, often alongside Kara10 [[d:priest#1331014250402938972]].

## Tank notes

"Ubrs is a nice entry to managing semi dangerous trash. Mark shit, communicate to other tank and ez" [[d:paladin#1441115195643789375]].

- **Prot paladin in blues with ~80 spell power** can tank it. Have a **ranged pull option** such as dynamite, because some pulls are "super dangerous to face pull especially in the fire orc area" [[d:paladin#1441112018798252196]], [[d:paladin#1441112320804913182]].
- The base damage of Holy Strike and Judgement of Righteousness is enough threat. Bring a Stamina ring, an Arcane Elixir and Wizard Oil [[d:paladin#1441114650434601011]], [[d:paladin#1441114525905457285]], [[d:paladin#1441114942769201392]].
- Fire damage is weak across the Blackrock dungeons [[d:shaman#1457392210541805568]].

## Bosses (Turtle-specific notes)

- **General Drakkisath (final):** a Survival hunter can kite him solo or duo. Open with Aimed Shot + Multi-Shot (maybe Distracting Shot) and drop an Immolation Trap in his path, then run. **Do not keep hitting him to stop a leash reset.** That gets you Conflagrated and killed. Pump initial threat, then stay out of Conflagrate range [[d:hunter#1443767535374958603]], [[d:hunter#1443767590068424806]], [[d:hunter#1443767847972245524]], [[d:hunter#1443767912492961832]]. Drakkisath drops Spellweaver's Turban (25%) [[d:mage#1404107820806307950]].
- **Jed Runewatcher (rare) fishing:** for his trinket, open the door, target through it, and reset if Jed is not there. You have to walk up a little to target him [[d:paladin#1427741841222008923]], [[d:paladin#1427741957848825938]], [[d:paladin#1427742037146341387]].

## Why raiders still come back

- The **UBRS fire-resistance buff** is part of the Firemaw and Molten Core fire-resistance kit [[d:warrior#1449542365701341217]].
