# Naxxramas

**Summary:** The vanilla Tier 3 raid, 40 players, 15 bosses, with no new Turtle bosses but several Turtle-specific quirks. Sapphiron's frost aura is bugged, so most raids skip Frost Resistance gear. Faerlina's room trash is linked to her and ignores the classic corner pull. Greater demons lose their path on the Thaddius jump. Every boss can bleed, and in practice no boss is immune to poisons. Veterans call it "easy but kinda long" [[d:warrior#1459702879584780349]]. The roadblocks are Patchwerk, Heigan, the Four Horsemen and Kel'Thuzad, and gear matters most on Patchwerk [[d:warrior#1449489984410226738]], [[d:warrior#1449490160642162983]].

## Raid-wide preparation

- **Bleeds:** all Naxx bosses can bleed [[d:druid#1461690562099806260]], [[d:druid#1461732289061978378]].
- **Poisons:** no Naxx boss is poison-immune [[d:rogue#1408300574163865730]]. Only Skeletons and Wraiths/Ghosts among the trash resist Nature poisons; Dissolvent/Corrosive work on undead [[d:rogue#1446241221520523469]], [[d:rogue#1446241450210889931]]. Rheinwg's plan: Instant is the safest trash poison (scattered humanoids and beasts); Faerlina, Maexxna and Gothik are the special cases, and Gothik is Dissolvent-only; simplified call: Instant + Deadly 5/4 in the Spider Wing, Dissolvent + Deadly/Corrosive elsewhere [[d:rogue#1484757612271501332]].
- **Undead:** switch to Consecrated Sharpening Stones or Blessed Wizard Oil after the Spider Wing [[d:rogue#1472013120036671579]], [[d:paladin#1462761249325711431]].
- **Resistance potions** (tank view) [[d:warrior#1459717421219975312]]: Shadow for the Four Horsemen and Loatheb; Frost for Sapphiron and Kel'Thuzad; most other bosses need none (Anub'Rekhan's nature damage only matters for the off-tank holding the add stack; Heigan's is avoidable). Greater Fire and Greater Nature Protection do not overwrite each other, so stack both on Faerlina [[d:warrior#1459700289719505184]], [[d:warrior#1459700806465880281]].
- **Frost Resistance is not needed for Sapphiron** on Turtle: the aura ticks irregularly (every 2-4 s) and even during the air phase, so raids use Frost Protection Potions instead; for tanks FR gear only prolongs the fight [[d:warrior#1438234999965876387]], [[d:warrior#1439502357443379220]], [[d:warrior#1439425974226915402]], [[d:warrior#1449484192105631824]]. Some FR still helps non-tank players during progression [[d:warrior#1449481147103842404]].
- **Tanks:** Greater Stoneshield on Patchwerk, Kel'Thuzad, Sapphiron, Loatheb and Faerlina; save your defensive cooldown for Maexxna and Gothik [[d:paladin#1462761249325711431]]. Most fights are "paper threat"; only Sapphiron and Thaddius really test the tank [[d:paladin#1468289569056227339]]. Mitigation gear on Maexxna and Patchwerk (if not MT), Sapphiron; threat gear elsewhere [[d:warrior#1478719837323661353]], [[d:warrior#1454921205864726621]]. Bring Greater Stoneshield potions for the Four Horsemen tanks [[d:warrior#1355788173011320965]]. Flasks are treated as mandatory in progression [[d:warrior#1466476942969540660]].
- **Healers:** a high-output tank survives on proactive healing and coordination (pre-pull armor buffs), not healer count; one guild kept its Sapphiron tank up with 2 dedicated paladins [[d:warrior#1473516533170049307]], [[d:warrior#1473518708193361982]].
- **Pets:** Naxx is easy on pets except when they over-aggro [[d:hunter#1485920686055886930]]. Warlock greater demons do not follow down the ramp to Thaddius (pathing bug unresolved through 1.18.1) [[d:warlock#1462518125135400991]], [[d:warlock#1464002899653824635]].
- **Demonology warlocks:** one pet-per-boss plan — Anub Felguard, Faerlina Infernal, Maexxna Felguard, Noth Felguard, Heigan Infernal, Loatheb/Gluth/Thaddius/Sapphiron/KT SM-Ruin, Razuvious Infernal, Gothik Felguard, Patchwerk Felguard, Grobbulus Infernal [[d:warlock#1352291971313569802]]. Swap off pet specs for Gluth (fear) [[d:warlock#1354862187780378705]].

## Trash

- **Most feared packs:** Death Lord and Deathknight Cavalier [[d:warrior#1428900549134389399]]. They cannot be kicked but an engineering Arcane Bomb silences them (also Necro Knights) [[d:rogue#1465549930344222846]].
- **Faerlina is linked to her room's trash** on Turtle: pulling her pulls the trash; the trash is silence-immune, has room-wide combat and a shortened patrol, so the classic corner pull does not work [[d:mage#1476068135743524978]], [[d:mage#1476632023442391173]], [[d:warrior#1443845679100461148]], [[d:warrior#1443866451357204582]].
- **Speed clears:** pull a whole room into a corner with a Paladin bubble or a warrior "meat shield", burst it with raid AoE, finish with Sappers [[d:mage#1476058959931506862]]. Before Anub'Rekhan: Invisibility Potion pull, a paladin bubble-drags the trash room outside [[d:mage#1475630831304445984]]; a hunter pet can hold the Anub-room adds outside while priests AoE [[d:priest#1494364719472054494]]. Everyone Sappers the first Spider Wing pull [[d:druid#1473384333141217575]], [[d:warlock#1459945548084674623]]; Faerlina's room is the other big Sapper moment [[d:rogue#1450510891677258040]].
- **Spider Wing adds:** use called Challenging Shout rotations rather than one tank's AoE taunt [[d:warrior#1431121542279991370]]; Thunderfury procs "machine gun" threat on spider packs [[d:paladin#1465525958269472779]]. **Crypt Guard webs:** do not spend your taunt GCD just before a web lands; taunt right after [[d:warrior#1459658618160025782]], [[d:warrior#1459661192485404702]].
- **Totem-dropping undead:** Ambush or burst the totem within 2-3 GCDs the moment it spawns [[d:rogue#1312541242441076866]].
- **Gothik trainees** die too fast for a DoT rotation (Drain Soul alone) [[d:warlock#1426240131341357056]]; big Abomination Wing trash is worth Corruption + Curse of Recklessness [[d:warlock#1426240565296631810]]. Some 20k-HP humanoids in the Razuvious area flee if not marked with Curse of Recklessness — **Contested** [[d:warlock#1424143066612236409]], [[d:warlock#1424143424885493890]].
- A Subtlety rogue's Distract on patrols is valued utility [[d:rogue#1427264378079805461]].
- Faerlina's room: if your raid splits up the Shadow Bolt Volleys, a paladin can run in, drop Consecration on the cultists and step back out [[d:paladin#1462767309365051487]].
- **Kel'Thuzad:** engaging him pulls all uncleared trash in the room through the portals [[d:paladin#1444278768322482376]].
