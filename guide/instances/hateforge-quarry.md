# Hateforge Quarry

Turtle-custom 5-man for levels 52-60 in the Burning Steppes. Official difficulty is "Early Blackrock Depths". It is a good first stop at 52+ and a popular solo gold, trinket and Fashion Coin farm at 60.

**Leveling value:** level 52 is "as soon as you can safely go to Hateforge Quarry and early Blackrock Depths" [[d:shaman#1444438508100915261]]. The dungeon drops six Blacksmithing plans for the **Hateforge Armor** set: 6-piece bonus +20 AP, +100 armor penetration, +2% haste ([Torta (staff), 2022-07-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=20251#p20251)).

## Group notes

- **Warrior tanks:** at 54 you will struggle to hold AoE threat against Swipe, Consecration and Multi-Shot. Mark targets, keep mobs off the healer, and let a mage or paladin hold the big packs [[d:warrior#1441907648734957650]], [[d:warrior#1441916755252478004]].
- **Rogues:** the mechanical enemies (Figgles' dragon and the golem) need a different poison from the humanoids. Carry a second set of daggers or swap poisons between pulls [[d:rogue#1489938874258948207]], [[d:rogue#1489939239453069433]].
- **The Overseer patrol** circles the tower and can add to the Foreman fight. Catch him on the staircase coming down from the Foreman, or behind the tower [[d:rogue#1439093300333117521]].

### Har'gesh Doomcaller (final boss)

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **Everyone** | He summons **2 Faceless Terror adds** that "hit quite hard" [[d:rogue#1487903576704684123]]. The boss is inactive until the adds die [[d:rogue#1476960629058437220]]. |
| **DPS** | **The adds are immune to Shadow damage.** Shadow priests and warlocks need another damage type or a burst plan [[d:priest#1469081856380244089]]. One soloer measured about 9k HP per add and about 20k for the boss [[d:priest#1469082517545156783]]. |
| **Tank** | Pick up both adds fast. They hit hard, so burst them down [[d:priest#1469082517545156783]]. |
| **Solo tip** | Save Evasion for the add phase. Blind the last add just before it dies, bandage to full, then pop cooldowns once the boss activates [[d:rogue#1476960629058437220]]. |

## Solo farming at 60

- **Rogue:** vampirism gear plus a Riposte/Assassination build solos the whole route. The loop is the last boss, the Overseer and the gnome, then out and repeat, for about 5 Fashion Coins and 40-60 g/h [[d:rogue#1489491333029822575]]. Another player made at least 100 g/h on Nordanaar doing only last-boss runs; Ambershire rates are lower [[d:rogue#1489642690374140126]], [[d:rogue#1489643647551934685]]. The Onyxia "Dragonslayer" buff plus Enchanting is the suggested setup [[d:rogue#1487903576704684123]]. Killing the bosses also gives weekly-quest crests [[d:rogue#1489491681303859240]].
- **Priest:** soloable by a well-geared Disc or Smite priest; "a hard gear check" [[d:priest#1424296554872963163]]. One priest did it at 5.6k HP / 7k mana with SP and Stamina consumables. The Karazhan Crypt key-chain trinket helps until your gear outgrows it [[d:priest#1469083041581236310]], [[d:priest#1469083717954965594]].
- **Druid:** Hatereaver Annihilator is soloable specced Resto in half-resto/half-feral gear. The risks are going OOM or being stunned outside Bear Form [[d:druid#1426274369683984487]]. For feral farming, mitigation gear beats vampirism [[d:druid#1443995658745807061]], [[d:druid#1443997113951326319]].
- **Warrior:** a Stamina-stacked fury-prot vampirism build solos HFQ [[d:warrior#1449927074566639691]]. A tank set with Force Reactive Disk, vampirism and Dream's Herald can AoE-farm it [[d:warrior#1471949214274556088]] (single source).
