# Molten Core (MC)

**Summary:** The vanilla Tier 1 raid, 40 players, reworked by Turtle. Patch 1.18.0 added two areas (Incindis' Lair, Old Shadowforge) and three new encounters (**Incindis**, the golem pair **Basalthar & Smoldaris**, **Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan**), removed Gehennas, and changed every old boss. It launched badly overtuned and was toned down during 2025. Now full green-geared groups clear it on Nordanaar [[d:rogue#1463675511317467246]]. Most of the raid needs **no Fire Resistance**. Only Ragnaros' and Baron Geddon's tanks do, plus potions on Magmadar, Majordomo and Geddon. Ragnaros, Geddon and the fire-elemental trash are **fire-immune**. Many mobs are **nature- and poison-immune**, so hunters and rogues swap tools almost every pull.

## Boss order (typical)

Incindis → Lucifron → Magmadar → Garr → Shazzrah → Baron Geddon → Golemagg → the twin golems (Basalthar & Smoldaris) → Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan → Sulfuron Harbinger → Majordomo Executus → Ragnaros, from one holy paladin's boss guide [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]].

## Raid-wide preparation

MC is the worst raid for poison swaps; some rogues skip poisons entirely [[d:rogue#1485839409915101225]]. Hunters use a Wind Serpent pet here [[d:hunter#1486441211446431937]] and play Survival but swap to MM for Ragnaros [[d:hunter#1447672710829310054]]. Fire-proc weapons (OBD, Hand of Ragnaros procs) are weak; swap to a non-fire weapon on the immune targets [[d:rogue#1458844048667181099]], [[d:rogue#1460097686123446430]].

### Fire Resistance and consumables

- **DPS and healers need no FR gear**; tanks need it for Ragnaros and possibly Geddon ("however much keeps you alive"; 315 is the cap) [[d:warrior#1444636106682531870]], [[d:warlock#1440431266171191357]].
- Potions: Greater Fire Protection on **Magmadar and Majordomo**, situationally Geddon and Ragnaros; **Greater Arcane Protection on Shazzrah** [[d:rogue#1463441701543870587]], [[d:rogue#1463442057833222281]]. Free Action Potion instead of protection potions on Sulfuron's stun [[d:warrior#1369058081744490558]].
- **MT for Ragnaros:** ~240 FR unbuffed, ~315 buffed with Juju Ember and Fire Resistance Aura (UBRS buff adds more); Dark Iron Helm and Leggings with FR enchants do most of the work [[d:paladin#1481004694053851186]], [[d:paladin#1482011075074592798]], [[d:warrior#1468913491305037998]]. Fire Resistance Aura and an elemental resistance potion do not stack [[d:paladin#1467777346131202230]].
- **Tank HP:** ~6.5k fully buffed is the comfortable floor (the fire-elemental cone in Geddon's room) [[d:paladin#1465280949804732543]]. A rough entry benchmark for tanking Ragnaros: 8k armor, 5k HP unbuffed, 120 FR [[d:shaman#1489054853899620542]] (single source).
- For Majordomo and Geddon tanks: Greater Fire Protection pre-pull and Greater Stoneshield on the pull rather than an FR set [[d:paladin#1460429229513642108]].

## Trash

- **Surgers** (small black golems) charge the farthest target; stack on them [[d:paladin#1439620188809990266]], [[d:paladin#1439620234209132697]].
- Poison-immune trash: surgers, annihilators, fire lords, sons of flame, ghosts, giants, destroyers [[d:rogue#1468988795142803507]], [[d:rogue#1468988942597750835]].
- The four-elemental packs are great Blade Flurry and cleave pulls [[d:rogue#1490340962864599111]]. Imp and dog rooms are easier with a paladin's Consecration [[d:warrior#1490391630102925584]].
- Hunters: MM pulls packs for its range [[d:hunter#1437464685799800873]]. The tameable **Magmalash Scorpid** has several fixed spawn points (most often near the two new bosses, else near Sulfuron or the lava); one at a time, no fresh ID needed; clear MC, stable your pet and hunt it [[d:hunter#1494105695241506888]], [[d:hunter#1494113708224417842]], [[d:hunter#1494109799036489758]].

## Bosses

### Incindis (new, replaces Gehennas)

An incendosaur ("the lizard fight") who lays eggs. The eggs are meant to be killed, but he tends to lay them just as he starts an AoE charge-up and they explode almost at once; groups just tank-and-spank [[d:shaman#1441160975125577869]], [[d:shaman#1441161058453688416]], [[d:shaman#1441162097798152325]]. **When the raid is pulled in, run out** [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]]. An engineering shield is strong here for AoE threat [[d:paladin#1422957111372943360]]. He drops the former Gehennas tier loot ([Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615)).

### Lucifron

- Hits harder than expected because of his two adds [[d:shaman#1445539804170158286]]. Follow marks: first add, second add, boss [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]].
- Mind Control: dispelling an opposite-faction mind-controlled player strips their buffs before the MC; use Restorative Potions or dispel your own faction [[d:priest#1480338698091364373]].
- Reported bug: his adds could not be interrupted for a couple of weeks in January 2026 [[d:rogue#1464188486511755367]] (unconfirmed).

### Magmadar

- Stick to the dog; expect fears into fire patches; Greater Fire Protection before and during [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]]. Lava Bomb pools last 20 s / 40 s ([Torta (staff), 2025-08-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148454#p148454)).
- Hunters can cheese with 2/2 Far Shot, but the boss may walk off [[d:hunter#1477835556577611816]]. Coordinated Assault counts as the hunter's melee hit, so it triggers his hide-reducing debuff [[d:hunter#1447680037573759006]].

### Garr

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| Tank | Common setups: one tank holds Garr in the middle while two tanks split the 8 adds in a corner; full-banish strategies fail because banishing 4+ adds breaks it [[d:paladin#1451282102661283991]], [[d:paladin#1451282609983197396]], [[d:paladin#1451286370722119893]]. The adds hit **physical**, so add tanking is about avoidance and HP [[d:paladin#1452198600678441075]]; they apply Immolate on hit, so wear some FR and use Greater Stoneshield, **not** elemental resistance potions [[d:paladin#1460430597347475662]], [[d:paladin#1460430044118650952]]. Solo add tanking: a fire-immunity trinket, Stoneshields, and a ~5k HP buffer before an add dies (death explosion ~4k fire) [[d:paladin#1470837697546289247]], [[d:paladin#1470839001555664967]]. Too far from Garr enrages the adds, too close shields Garr [[d:paladin#1449029898151919708]]. |
| DPS | Do not stand near small adds when they die [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]] |
| Class jobs | Mages fish Icicle procs off the pack [[d:mage#1467928581001973840]]. Shadow priests AoE all adds evenly and swap to a fire-immunity trinket just before they die [[d:priest#1454601699468509286]], [[d:priest#1454603179659362417]] |

### Shazzrah

- **Counterspell hits even if he faces away** — stop casting just before it [[d:mage#1461713607288164363]], [[d:mage#1461719788773572820]], [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]]. Arcane Explosion radius is 20 yards ([Torta (staff), 2024-07-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100214#p100214)).
- A good shaman-tank assignment: he hits softly and shocks keep threat through his teleports [[d:shaman#1449144466526044191]], [[d:shaman#1449144707102937282]].
- Greater Arcane Protection [[d:rogue#1463442057833222281]]. Warlock Infernals may melt to his arcane (**Contested**) [[d:warlock#1442914859284173051]], [[d:warlock#1442924839840252057]].

### Baron Geddon

- **Living Bomb:** run to the wall; run out of Inferno (paladins can bubble it) [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]]; a tank paladin can bubble off the bomb and cancel to keep aggro [[d:paladin#1369247600242458685]]. 1.18.0 added fire patches ([Zmarotrix (player), 2025-08-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148757#p148757)).
- FR: ~260 recommended, less for experienced groups [[d:paladin#1483437341375860867]], [[d:paladin#1483437712760639600]]; or just walk out of the Hellfire [[d:paladin#1460429728531087485]].
- He drains mana from players near him (keep greater demons away) [[d:warlock#1388886548489044029]]. A holy paladin here is a dispel bot [[d:paladin#1454169199051018291]].

### Golemagg the Incinerator

- The Core Hounds fixate randomly and reset aggro ([Alovander (player), 2025-08-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=148274#p148274)); warriors taunt them for rage and dump it on Golemagg — damage to dogs does not hurt Golemagg [[d:warrior#1437503959152721971]], [[d:warrior#1437512696600727633]]. The hounds were slowed 25% and he enrages at 10% again ([Torta (staff), 2025-09-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149960#p149960)).
- Healers stay on the boss; others handle the dogs [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]]. DPS-spec off-tanks may want a shield here if healers struggle [[d:warrior#1433470964288589844]].

### Basalthar & Smoldaris (new twin golems)

Do not stack and cleave them: keep them apart, facing away from ranged and healers. Attacking the golem with the damage-reduction debuff makes it nuke the raid, so swap between them like Twin Emperors [[d:warrior#1424775608055107654]], [[d:warrior#1424775776842289356]], [[d:warrior#1424776946944053298]]. Healers just swap targets [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]].

### Sorcerer-Thane Thaurissan (new)

- **Rune of Detonation: run out of the rune. Rune of Combustion: run into it** [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]].
- MT: move him out of the electrical-field circle and stand in it yourself to take the parries off the raid (this puts more parries on you) [[d:warrior#1429479733795623062]], [[d:warrior#1429479848341930004]].
- Arcane Barrage knockback reduced ([Jamey (staff), 2025-08-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=147116#p147116)).

### Sulfuron Harbinger

Kill the adds in order, then the boss [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]]. A big cleave pull ("not cleaving there was possibly a crime") [[d:warrior#1446255829065142363]]. Free Action Potion for his stun [[d:warrior#1369058081744490558]].

Doite's strat for 20-25 players [[d:shaman#1474002920419627039]], [[d:shaman#1474003301736648880]], [[d:shaman#1474007555629125654]], [[d:shaman#1474013292954386515]]:

| Role | Job |
|---|---|

| Healers | About 1 per group |
| DPS | Kill order: un-sheeped healers, then broken sheep, then warrior adds |
| Class jobs | **Mages can sheep at most 2 healers**; sheep the two closest to the boss (sheeping his adds still works on Turtle [[d:mage#1424365543967948910]]). | Role | Job |

## Notable loot

- Ragnaros: a rare mount (1.17.2); Sulfuras (Sulfuron Ingots are the bottleneck; join a guild that pools them) [[d:shaman#1426076298023014400]]; T2 legs — paladin/shaman legs could only drop once per kill (a confirmed bug with a fix scheduled) [[d:paladin#1481966240942526635]].
- Majordomo's cloak gives +18 spell power and 1% haste since 1.18 [[d:mage#1432739937173639208]]. An item from Majordomo summons a world boss [[d:paladin#1485274682927218860]].
- T1 still has useful pieces (hit on the gloves) for guilds already in BWL [[d:paladin#1493607352035835904]].

## Common wipes

1. Garr: adds dying next to squishy players, or banishing too many at once [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]], [[d:paladin#1451286370722119893]].
2. Majordomo: broken sheep and add control [[d:warrior#1476388663804821606]].
3. Ragnaros: knockbacks with too little FR on the tank [[d:paladin#1451158265353211914]].
4. The twin golems: cleaving both or hitting the debuffed one [[d:warrior#1424775776842289356]].
