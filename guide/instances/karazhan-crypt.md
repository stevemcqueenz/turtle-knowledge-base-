# Karazhan Crypt

Turtle-custom level 58-60 5-man under Karazhan in Deadwind Pass. Players call it "Kara5" or "Kara Crypts", which is not the same as Kara10 (Lower Karazhan Halls) or Kara40 (Tower of Karazhan) [[d:druid#1395494994810241084]]. Along with Black Morass and Stormwind Vault it is one of the three custom level-60 5-mans. Players rate those three "notably harder than any other end game 5-mans" [[d:druid#1334227414406008892]] and recommend some pre-raid gear first [[d:druid#1354606068122255361]].

**Do the key chain even if someone else opens the door.** Its trinket is "very op for its level" [[d:priest#1425163744375345263]]. Druids prefer it over the Chromie trinket [[d:druid#1439701323921625269]]. One version gives 14 spell power, +1% hit and +1% haste [[d:priest#1469080796454326384]]. The Alliance and Horde versions differ, and players report the Horde one as worse ([GR3ddy (player), 2025-02-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=119888#p119888)). The item IDs are linked in [[d:druid#1454629479719899293]].

## Group notes

- **Tanks:** fresh-60 gear is enough with a good healer, but expect to be hit hard. Use CC where you can [[d:druid#1454114933808697564]]. Crypt is kinder to weak tanks than Black Morass: "you can get in with shit gear and will perform the same as a high gear tank" [[d:shaman#1445574493203333260]].
- **Feral cats:** "Kara crypts is like 80% bleed immunities" [[d:druid#1395690886779895909]]. The small spiders can bleed but the spider boss Hivaxxis cannot [[d:druid#1364193337455673364]], [[d:druid#1366780027542437909]].
- **Stealth runs:** a stealth group can skip a lot, including the resettable "triple mob wall" [[d:druid#1354019310162673674]].

## Trash: the fear room

The room with sand and skeletons "that fear you into more skeletons that fear you into more skeletons" causes more wipes than Black Morass. Players compare it to "troll LBRS except it's worst because fear is toxic" [[d:shaman#1445574039929356309]], [[d:shaman#1445574058518511662]].

Earlier on there is a long underwater section. The exit is guarded by 4 skeletal guards and a frost spectre in front of Corpsemuncher. Mana users cannot drink in the water ([Haroone (player), 2021-12-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=14969#p14969)).

## Loot highlights

- **Feral:** boots and hands (the Rotworn Grips; "really good but an ass to farm"), plus a 2H weapon option [[d:druid#1418979557569990917]], [[d:druid#1424863533145002145]].
- **Hunters:** the spear and gloves (1% hit each) [[d:hunter#1388950972910276661]]; a bow that deals frost damage [[d:hunter#1418965258500046988]]; two leather trash drops worth buying [[d:hunter#1490111388067168298]].
- **Healers:** "mostly kara crypts + Black morass" for resto druid gear [[d:druid#1435927919829454878]].
