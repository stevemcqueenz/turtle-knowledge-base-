# Blackrock Depths

Vanilla level ~52-60 Dark Iron city under Blackrock Mountain. It is the backbone of pre-raid gearing (the tank gear, the Idol of Ferocity) and home to several solo farms: Emperor runs, the arena, and pickpocketing. Kills give Thorium Brotherhood reputation ([Torta (staff), 2025-08-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145131#p145131)).

## Group and leveling notes

- **From 52** you can "safely go to Hateforge Quarry and early Blackrock Depths" [[d:shaman#1444438508100915261]].
- **Tanking the Arena, Angerforge and the Golem Lord** is "a decent way of getting some gear and xp" [[d:shaman#1435023897753026582]].
- **Fire damage is weak here.** Fire/Spellhance shamans underperform across the Blackrock dungeons because of fire resistances and immunities [[d:shaman#1457392210541805568]].
- **Orc 2H DPS warriors** can rush BRD early on weapon skill to grab the Sleeper recipe [[d:warrior#1440459100982087751]], [[d:warrior#1440459604957073428]].

## Notable loot

- **Idol of Ferocity** (cat druid: -3 energy on Claw/Rake) is still BiS [[d:druid#1439095351150903428]], [[d:druid#1492276296808661174]].
- Most pre-raid tank gear: "You get like 80% of your tanking gear from brd" [[d:paladin#1359607379569803304]].

## Emperor Dagran Thaurissan

The sources contain no group tactics. Solo notes follow.

- **Paladin solo Emperor farm:** jump from the balcony and **keep running past Lord Incendius**, or the upstairs mobs follow you. Make the lava jump as soon as possible. In the throne room: Holy Shield → Consecration in melee range of the casters → wait for the first burst → Holy Shock → Crusader Strike → wait for the second burst → Holy Shock → finish the mobs [[d:paladin#1356914410026827868]] (video linked in the source).
- **The Senators' alpha strike:** they cast 3 times in a row. **Line of sight all 3 casts** and they do nothing afterwards. Magic (frost) damage is the worst part of the pulls. Before T3, a solo is only worth it if you really want Ironfoe [[d:paladin#1475907830945939628]], [[d:paladin#1475907959170273390]], [[d:paladin#1475906383114276946]], [[d:paladin#1475908322522697829]].
- **Rogue:** raid-geared rogues solo the torches, the golem, the throne room and the Emperor using target dummies. You can LoS the Emperor behind his throne to avoid pulling the whole room ("pretty fucky") [[d:rogue#1471053877829828724]], [[d:rogue#1471054107728150692]].
- **Hunter:** kill Magmus first, clear the room's adds, then separate the Princess from the Emperor with positioning and Feign Death. Under an hour with practice [[d:hunter#1426724204170182727]], [[d:hunter#1426868795481260145]].
- **Shaman tank:** one cleared the whole throne room solo in MC gear with Earth Shield, but died to the Emperor because "his wife's spells fuckin hurt". No plan for the Princess was found [[d:shaman#1446585484108365928]], [[d:shaman#1446591555862397132]].
- A hunter + warrior multibox with no healer was judged unlikely to work: Thaurissan has high armor and hits hard in his enrage [[d:warrior#1440451033187745974]], [[d:warrior#1440451645430300793]].

## Ring of Law (arena)

- Soloable as Prot paladin in pre-raid gear [[d:paladin#1490852968302510220]], [[d:paladin#1490853001089253446]].
- A deep-BM hunter's carrion bird tanked everything [[d:hunter#1466402262904733855]]. Survival can do it "with some luck in the types of spawns" [[d:hunter#1462949254204227675]].

## Farming

- **Rogue pickpocket route:** Bael'Gar → Incendius → Fineous → jump down to Incendius → arena spectator area → Pyromancer → Angerforge → the bar area. Skip the bar mobs and the prison area, since they give Sturdy rather than Heavy junkboxes. Then take the lava skip to smelt the Dark Iron ore you found, and reset with an alt. About 30 Heavy Junkboxes per run [[d:rogue#1441234944184680590]], [[d:rogue#1441235046118981682]], [[d:rogue#1441235726514655242]]. About 1 in 10-20 pickpockets gets resisted regardless of spell hit [[d:rogue#1454514076511371336]].
- **Paladin AoE farm:** full T1 + 18% vampirism + Force Reactive Disk "cant aoe farm in brd" [[d:paladin#1454257006834880574]].
