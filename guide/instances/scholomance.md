# Scholomance

Vanilla level-60 necromancer academy in Western Plaguelands. Capped at 5 players since 2025-08-15 ([Torta (staff), 2025-08-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145822#p145822)). Kills give Argent Dawn reputation.

## Group notes

- **Ranged-pull everything.** "Scholo is where you want a ranged pull everywhere due to density" [[d:paladin#1435893064710492170]].
- **Undead dungeon kit:** Blessed Wizard Oil plus the Argent Dawn trinket [[d:paladin#1436060738140635238]].
- **Rain of Fire bug:** ground-targeted AoE fails to hit in the basement room of spell-immune undead and in the middle room under the first bridge. Hellfire still works [[d:warlock#1476326861456801874]], [[d:warlock#1476320132774232074]].

## Bosses

- **Rattlegore (rogue solo):** clear the adds nearest him first. One add socially aggros with the boss, so kill it first, then Vanish to reset cooldowns. Pop a **Free Action Potion** 5-10 s into the fight so his stun misses. Cycle Flourish/Ghostly Strike → Evasion → Flourish/Ghostly Strike. It is hard; world buffs or a second rogue help [[d:rogue#1431914254612828233]], [[d:rogue#1430826915555709028]].
- **Marduk (weapon-skill quest):** he is neutral. Attacking him works, but you will probably aggro the whole room. Pulling him alone from a corner used to work, but it is not confirmed now [[d:warrior#1440823863385653328]], [[d:warrior#1440824070840258610]].
- **Darkmaster Gandling (solo):** "If anyone is dumb enough to consider soloing scholo, dont." The Gandling fight took over 40 minutes because of his repeated teleports. The 6 minibosses are easy for scourgestones [[d:paladin#1481547661088657489]].

## Loot

- The **Argent Defender** shield (Scholomance quest reward) beats the Crest options for lasher farming [[d:paladin#1444029248573804717]], [[d:paladin#1444031330470858914]].
- Ras Frostwhisper has 186 frost resistance, so frost casters should expect resists (a community-compiled list, cross-checked from vanilla data) [[d:hunter#1407146805678051390]].
