# Dragonmaw Retreat

Turtle-custom 5-man for roughly levels 25-35 in the eastern Wetlands, added in 1.18.0. It is long, with 13 bosses: "DMR is a humongous dungeon for that level" [[d:paladin#1413208204221087795]]. It is the main source of the Dragonmaw bear set and several leveling weapons.

## Group notes

- **Tank level:** tankable at about 30 with a decent shield, with "only a few sketchy moments with zuluhed" [[d:shaman#1449019965184413788]]. Do RFK first for the Marbled Buckler [[d:shaman#1449020532153385052]]. Another player warns that leveling tanks get rolled unless they go in at about 32 [[d:paladin#1459859989924417686]].
- **Pet tanks work.** A level-32 BM hunter with a gorilla tanked through the dragon boss with a 34 druid healer and sub-30 DPS, with zero wipes [[d:hunter#1421042271238750359]] (single source). Another hunter pet-tanked several bosses, using Aspect of the Monkey plus Disengage and Deterrence [[d:hunter#1480611289528139797]].
- **Difficulty:** "Unknown to many, and those who know it, know it's easy to wipe" [[d:paladin#1432720939677782097]]. Some players found it too easy [[d:druid#1442598792179355729]].

## Loot

- **Dragonmaw bear set:** the BoE pieces drop often. Three pieces are blue BoP. The 2- and 4-piece bonuses are "very good" for leveling bears [[d:druid#1442894531673587763]]. The 6-piece bonus is bad [[d:druid#1450985278226305247]]. Keep the Strength bonus until the 50s [[d:druid#1463647249509646533]].
- **Weapons and shields:** Flamescale Pike (hunter) [[d:hunter#1480631809065881786]]; an +8 spell power shield for paladins that is a low-rate drop [[d:paladin#1444791964812968080]], [[d:paladin#1413207959055503480]]; a staff from Halgan Redbrand [[d:hunter#1408722960315060224]]. A necklace drops at about 1.5% [[d:paladin#1419719450713784443]].
- **No mount** drops here [[d:paladin#1462707975830376499]].
