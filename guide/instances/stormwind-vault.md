# Stormwind Vault

Turtle-custom level-60 5-man beneath Stormwind City. It is short, has no key, and is the easier of the two "raid-equivalent" custom 60 dungeons: "SWV is decently easy, just the last boss can be a slog if people dont know how the boss works" [[d:paladin#1448468876454006846]]. It is also a favorite solo farm (Damian for The Ripper).

## Before you go

- **Fresh-60 gear is enough with a good healer**, but you "will get hit like truck", so CC as much as you can [[d:druid#1454114933808697564]].
- **Rogues:** some mobs are immune to all poisons [[d:rogue#1486679697776578620]].
- **Warlocks:** some demon trash can be enslaved and is "super strong" [[d:warlock#1428855496525480128]].
- **Pull trick:** you can send a pet at the first four bosses and pull them without the connecting trash [[d:warlock#1428858028009979924]]. The old Succubus pull may be patched. Two players in 2026-01 could not get it to work [[d:warlock#1457515601089007677]], [[d:warlock#1458086834751148144]].
- **You must kill every boss.** A door was added, so you can no longer stealth straight to the end. The chest room stays blocked until the last boss dies [[d:rogue#1448338435998744829]], [[d:rogue#1448338744863363234]], [[d:rogue#1446183157014728845]]. A stealth group (for example 2 rogues + 1 druid) can still skip most trash [[d:rogue#1448338860106055833]], [[d:rogue#1448338954968367144]].
- **The Rat Trap** is a scripted trap. It cannot be avoided once triggered ([Jamey (staff), 2023-03-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=37124#p37124)).

### Arc'tiras (final boss)

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **Everyone** | He **mind controls**. Hunters remove their weapons before the fight so a mind-controlled hunter does nothing harmful [[d:hunter#1476148840540274772]]. ## Loot highlights

- **Whip of Encouragement** (healer trinket): "most importantly whip of encouragement" [[d:shaman#1455337266615685272]].
- **Quest rewards:** the Vault quest crossbow, "a good starter xbow" [[d:hunter#1480999389571977379]]. A 1% spell-hit ring quest that just needs a clear [[d:mage#1430916081140109383]]. The mage notes mark this ring Alliance-only, but the cited message does not say so. A caster offhand from a related Hyjal chain that also gives a teleport item [[d:mage#1425428764132638780]].
- **Tank set:** Volkan's neck and chest, Damian's shoulders, Arc'tiras' wrists [[d:paladin#1327978668156518503]].

## Solo farming

- **Rogue (Damian for The Ripper):** use about 8% vampirism and a few points in Parry/Riposte. Open with Ghostly Strike + Flourish, then Evasion, and repeat the defensives. One player farmed him 45 times [[d:rogue#1442034294725017610]]. You need Assassination or Combat for Riposte. Roughly 500+ DPS with ~10% vampirism makes it "somewhat easy" [[d:rogue#1458454505824977001]], [[d:rogue#1458477913664917657]]. Stealth route: sprint past the first creepers, break stealth at the rat-room threshold, and Vanish at the far side [[d:rogue#1488076723198365706]].
- **Whole instance solo:** "Swv is soloable... do 5 resets, takes 25 min" [[d:rogue#1458066064163475622]]. Stealth is inconsistent, because mobs sometimes pull through walls. Invisibility potions + Sprint are an alternative [[d:rogue#1442684537443455088]], [[d:rogue#1442688035165831170]].
- **Hunter (pet tank):** every boss but the last is easy. Arc'tiras' two adds are the problem [[d:hunter#1483817019114524733]], [[d:hunter#1483817477736497243]], [[d:hunter#1476165181850648597]]. Keep armor gear on the pet [[d:hunter#1483819615422517361]].
