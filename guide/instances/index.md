## Raids

All raids are level 60, so the raid tables are alphabetical. Since 2026-04-21 raid scaling is active everywhere: 20-player raids scale down to 8 players, 40-player raids to 16 ([Torta (staff), 2026-04-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176101#p176101)). Community difficulty order (easiest first): Kara10 = ZG = AQ20 > Onyxia > MC > BWL > Emerald Sanctum > AQ40 > Timbermaw Hold > Naxx > Kara40 [[d:druid#1491822859398418473]].
