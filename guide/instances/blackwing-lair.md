# Blackwing Lair (BWL)

**Summary:** The vanilla Tier 2 raid, 40 players, extended in 1.18.1 with a new optional boss (**Ezzel Darkbrewer**), a Suppression Room shortcut, and a new or changed mechanic on every boss except Chromaggus. It is a **warrior-tank and fire-immunity raid**. Vaelastrasz, the three drakes and Nefarian are immune to fire. Firemaw is the one fight where tanks need Fire Resistance, and Nefarian's class calls punish every class. Tanks want **8-9k HP fully buffed** and a Flask of the Titans to avoid being one-shot. Players called the post-1.18.1 tuning "WAY overtuned… for the gear that drops there" ([Travoltino (player), 2026-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174380#p174380)), while one guild chain-wiped two hours on Razorgore at launch as another cleared it all easily [[d:paladin#1485268860126105781]], [[d:paladin#1485268915520274492]].

## Raid-wide preparation

### Immunities

- **Fire-immune:** Vaelastrasz, Firemaw, Ebonroc, Flamegor and Nefarian. Razorgore, Broodlord, Chromaggus, the new ogre/goblin boss and almost all trash take fire [[d:warlock#1488208031111188640]], [[d:warlock#1488208177421095143]], [[d:shaman#1444777299286425631]]. Hunters found Nefarian's first phase vulnerable to fire too [[d:hunter#1480211021858603150]]. "Only 3/8 boss fights work for Fire" [[d:mage#1493999508734873651]]. Survival hunters play a no-trap build here [[d:hunter#1480208996706156644]]; demo warlocks keep near-100% Infernal uptime on the dragons because its physical punches ignore the dragons' resistances [[d:warlock#1424158274630058054]], [[d:warlock#1424158415739027640]].
- **Chromaggus** is not really fire-immune (~15 resist); his gimmick is a magic-damage-reduction buff with random school vulnerabilities [[d:mage#1493999774410608701]].
- **Poisons:** everything is damageable only by Instant/Deadly; Spellbinders are immune to all poisons; Drakonids may carry a high Nature vulnerability. Assassination runs Instant for the whole raid; Combat Instant on trash, Deadly on bosses [[d:rogue#1484755632237187213]], [[d:rogue#1485998623828217956]].
- **Bleeds:** all bosses believed bleedable [[d:druid#1425592735750033458]].
- **Drakonid trash** has very high resistance to all schools except one that varies; Summoner-type adds are spell-immune [[d:druid#1489765347732226209]], [[d:druid#1489765971886477482]].

### Resistance and consumables

- **Fire Resistance matters for tanks only on Firemaw** [[d:paladin#1451158590415966230]], [[d:paladin#1482006597499879537]]. Hunters and other ranged need some FR from MC/BWL gear and Greater Fire Protection Potions [[d:hunter#1425193820148203600]], [[d:hunter#1425205284686663812]].
- **Onyxia Scale Cloak:** needed on Firemaw and Nefarian; equip it at Firemaw and leave it on so you do not forget it for Nefarian [[d:paladin#1451276970275901673]], [[d:paladin#1451276466301177976]]. Melee who never gear-swap should still wear it on the drakes and Nefarian [[d:warrior#1432580991200919656]], [[d:warrior#1432582059502604378]].
- **Tanks:** raid-buffed HP of 8-9k ("BWL bosses can and will just global you" below that) and a Flask of the Titans [[d:paladin#1482013156644622397]]; one tank progressed without flasks [[d:paladin#1482013232515256350]]; the flask first matters on Vaelastrasz, Broodlord and Chromaggus [[d:paladin#1471657583826702366]]. A fully geared paladin was still hit for 105% of max HP [[d:paladin#1476779778932474059]]. Largest single hits: Shadowflame (~5,000 shadow), Broodlord's Mortal Strike (cannot crit or crush), and an enraged Chromaggus' Incinerate (~7,500 fire) [[d:paladin#1466476976100606222]], [[d:paladin#1466477316124446741]]. Raids bring 4-5 tanks [[d:paladin#1435625141366292600]]. BWL is "literally built around warrior tanks": Recklessness on the Vael pull, backup taunts on the drakes, Shield Wall and anti-fear on Chromaggus and Nefarian [[d:warrior#1490529592883023903]]. Swap tank gear per boss [[d:warrior#1446712742307168339]]. Broodlord and Nefarian need a shield [[d:shaman#1487040265381347407]]. Healers must not treat 80% HP as safe against Broodlord and Chromaggus [[d:paladin#1451157983701631006]].
- **Hunter pets:** Fire and Shadow resistance on the pet here [[d:hunter#1485902382322683904]]; pets cannot wear Onyxia cloaks, so keep them out of drake breath [[d:hunter#1484305874158616658]].

### Composition notes

- Shaman tanks find BWL their worst phase (FR itemization) but handle Vaelastrasz and Firemaw; Chromaggus is RNG-heavy for them [[d:shaman#1425350547774509186]], [[d:shaman#1425350696315912294]].
- Warlocks: Doomguard/Infernal underperform Succubus on the fire-immune bosses, except Infernal on Firemaw [[d:warlock#1479597163641307216]], [[d:warlock#1479597279165026538]].

## Trash

- **Suppression Room:** opinions split between "tedious gate" (it "just feels janky overall") and "easy under 10 minutes"; pull mobs out of the suppression zone first [[d:paladin#1436374552673386626]], [[d:paladin#1436373852358836254]], [[d:paladin#1436375022569656360]]. The egg count was halved in a patch [[d:paladin#1436369960996962424]]. A 1.18.1 shortcut skips it after Broodlord ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)); you only need to unlock the skip once per lockout [[d:paladin#1475536427231084736]]. High-risk option for overgeared raids: pushing an orb pulls the whole room at once and stops further whelp spawns [[d:paladin#1445203722568335375]], [[d:paladin#1445203930131988480]].
  - Rogues: bring Thieves' Tools, disable devices, stay stealthed, use distracts [[d:rogue#1483608695249174679]], [[d:rogue#1483613324850495608]]. Stun/interrupt the Spellbinders [[d:rogue#1312541242441076866]]; kite Spellbinders so melee do not kick them too early, and cleanse the poison ticks [[d:mage#1494004230653214802]], [[d:mage#1494002099879415969]].
  - Paladins handle the AoE packs more comfortably than warriors (**Contested**) [[d:warrior#1490391630102925584]], [[d:warrior#1489591327745245304]].
  - A sprint-capable hunter pet (raptor, cat) speeds up pulling the room after Broodlord [[d:hunter#1484476817845780540]], [[d:hunter#1484477028957945928]].
  - Warlocks: channels break if the camera forces keyboard-turning (known bug) [[d:warlock#1425557797764071424]], [[d:warlock#1425565657717542933]].
- **Drakonid / dragon trash** can "global" a tank through Thrash stacking [[d:paladin#1449028341397979281]].
- **Blackwing Alchemists** (Ezzel's lab trash) had respawn fixes ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608)).

### 5-6. Ebonroc and Flamegor

- Both are tauntable; only the MT needs real threat, off-tanks ~25% over the next target as taunt-resist insurance [[d:paladin#1469468280350576650]], [[d:paladin#1469468409518362768]].
- 1.18.1: they summon **suicide fire elementals** that are immune to damage and chase healers, plus a mind control [[d:paladin#1491801890328150097]], [[d:paladin#1491801993633857537]], [[d:paladin#1491802059111006258]].
- One player claims Flamegor now needs the whole raid FR-capped (single source) [[d:warrior#1456660331802919014]].
- Healers: tank-and-spank; do not stand in front [[d:paladin#1465429349154160784]].

### Optional: Ezzel Darkbrewer (1.18.1)

A goblin alchemist riding the ogre **Ton'Raka** in "his employer's laboratory" ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). Named abilities: **Acid Bomb**; Ton'Raka's **charge** ("Bob and weave you fool!"); a distinct 10% line suggests an execute-phase change ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Ezzel_Darkbrewer)). Health raised 2026-03-24; an exploit fixed 2026-03-31 ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490)). Drops Transmutation Alloy (rogue class quest) and the druid **Idol of Acidity** [[d:druid#1484609254613848137]]. No player tactics in the sources.

## Notable loot
