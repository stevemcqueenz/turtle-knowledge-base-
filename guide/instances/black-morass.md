# Caverns of Time: Black Morass

Turtle-custom level-60 5-man, and the hardest dungeon on the server. Players treat it as the fresh-60 tank gear check and compare it to Molten Core: "BM is MC equivalent content" [[d:paladin#1448466072268312646]], "semi heroic, fresh 60 tanks and healers will struggle" [[d:druid#1460298635903238276]]. The loot and the Wardens of Time reputation rewards are among the best pre-raid gear in the game.

## Before you go

- **The tank sets the pace.** "It's just a 'have the gear' as a tank" [[d:shaman#1445574359262560287]]. "If you can survive pulls in there, you're good to tank raids" [[d:druid#1441400547516219402]]. Tanks in greens and blues get "brutalized" [[d:paladin#1463501091973890131]]. If trash still flattens you, go back to other 5-mans for Stamina, armor and avoidance pieces first [[d:paladin#1448466072268312646]].
- **Fresh-60 tanks should not lead with this.** "Generally I wouldn't recommend tanking BM as a fresh 60... Dragons gonna fold you in half" [[d:paladin#1479128320514982019]], [[d:paladin#1479128357588304065]]. Black Morass and Stormwind Vault are the two dungeons where paladins actually need damage reduction like Blessing of Sanctuary [[d:paladin#1446437041347887265]]. "Last boss of BM hits harder than onyxia" [[d:paladin#1483909172868157582]].
- **Bring CC.** A druid (Hibernate) and a priest (Fortitude) is the comp players recommend. Warlock Banish and Fear also work [[d:paladin#1448466675790909510]], [[d:warlock#1432811487621021778]], [[d:shaman#1413584738119323709]].
- **Tanks: full tank talents**, not a leveling hybrid [[d:druid#1484730893624148078]]. Bring Greater Stoneshield Potions [[d:druid#1441393322869395496]], [[d:paladin#1472703383554228326]].
- Get armor trinkets first (Hatereaver Cog from [Hateforge Quarry](hateforge-quarry.md), the Dire Maul Tribute trinket) if the last boss is killing you [[d:druid#1430547261997322330]], [[d:druid#1430553461606580435]] (single source).

## Trash: the dragon packs before the bridge

These packs are the main wipe risk. They are the "3/4 Dragon pack pulls leading to the first boss" [[d:shaman#1413584738119323709]].

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **Tank** | Wear mitigation gear before the bridge. Pull with Hibernate if you are a druid [[d:druid#1441395729242521681]], [[d:druid#1454127781913821380]]. Pop Barkskin (or your equivalent) before the bridge packs [[d:druid#1460287082483421340]]. |
| **CC** | CC one mob per pack. That is the standard advice for a fresh-60 group [[d:paladin#1472702745638211737]]. Even a T2.5 tank with an AQ40 shield still wanted a Sleep on one dragon in the 4-packs [[d:paladin#1472703161687998494]]. Options: druid Hibernate, warlock Banish plus Fear with Curse of Recklessness juggling [[d:warlock#1432811487621021778]]. |
| **DPS** | **Kill the caster first.** There are 2 caster mobs before the bridge, 1 in each pack with it. About 30 s into the pull it lands a "sand-HoJ" stun on the tank, and the rest of the pack then deletes him [[d:paladin#1448464751389577439]]. |
| **Melee** | At the large pack of tornado elementals, "1 of the melee dps always dies" in all-melee groups [[d:druid#1460298386128502868]] (single source). |
| **Healer** | "Miserable dungeon to heal" [[d:shaman#1413584738119323709]]. The 4-dragonkin packs "will sometimes just delete you" [[d:druid#1441393322869395496]]. |

## Bosses

The rework has 7 bosses ([Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868)). The sources name 7: **Chronar**, **Drifting Avatar of Time**, **Epidamu**, **Rotmaw** (crocolisk), **Mossheart** (bog beast), **Epochronos** and **Antnormi** (final boss) [[d:turtle-wiki#1354099759538765966]]. Only Chronar (first) and Antnormi (last) have a known position.

### Chronar (first boss)

- "Chronar's first boss, just gotta get passed a few hard packs" [[d:paladin#1342601089265963121]]. The Black Morass polearm quest sends you to kill him. It rewards the Halberd of the Bronze Defender [[d:paladin#1342600863541100576]], [[d:hunter#1313650537874329662]].
- **He is not immune to slows.** With slows up he is "mega easy". He has a 1-minute Piercing Howl, so carry a PvP trinket [[d:hunter#1313650537874329662]] (single source).
- **Spell reflect.** The tell is a sparkle animation on the boss, not a yell. He also casts an AoE slowing shout that can leave a Felhunter out of range, so keep the pet close [[d:warlock#1443380018540449897]], [[d:warlock#1443748499341443125]]. Warlocks use the Felhunter here [[d:warlock#1352334585371037738]].
- Loot: Monolith Headguard (a tank helm) [[d:paladin#1327978668156518503]].

### The first big dragon boss, phase 2

- In phase 2 of "the first big dragon boss", it summons an add (an echo of a past figure, e.g. "the Lich King") that casts Mortal Coil and a fire AoE. **Hex it** while it is still weak. When the Hex wears off it is almost dead [[d:shaman#1318354711631499295]] (single source, 2024-12). The source does not say which boss this is.

### Rotmaw, Mossheart, Epidamu, Drifting Avatar of Time

- **Rotmaw** (the crocolisk boss) "consumes" the tank, and threat has to be retaken afterwards. That makes it rough for a tank with no taunt, such as a warlock tank [[d:warlock#1379816702786801864]] (single source). He drops the Idol of Savagery (the cat bleed idol) and Wraps of the Transmogrified ([Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868)), [[d:druid#1366528673066520636]], plus Rotmaw's Tooth, a common tanking dagger [[d:warrior#1189357215107252224]].
- **Mossheart** drops Mossheart's Heart, a tank trinket (145 armor, 8 HP5, nature thorns) [[d:paladin#1449287120295428278]].
- **Epidamu** drops Totem of the Flowing Timeways and Fabric of Time ([Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868)), and the shaman Totem of the Calming River [[d:shaman#1244926592791216200]].
- **Drifting Avatar of Sand** (a related creature) cannot be stunned or frozen ([Torta (staff), 2023-10-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=69006#p69006)). Pauldrons of the Timeless drop from it [[d:paladin#1374389115428143104]].
- No tactics for these bosses appear in the sources.

### Antnormi (final boss)

The dragon everyone wipes on. "The end dragon is ridiculously overscaled. It has the highest armor of any enemy in the game... He hits like a truck, he stuns you so your dodge doesn't work, and he enrages" [[d:druid#1441367430122311740]].

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **Tank** | Stack Stamina to survive his Thrash [[d:druid#1430553461606580435]]. He hits for about 2,723 before armor and fears you for about 6 s, over and over [[d:paladin#1472704160095797258]]. A fresh bear at 8k armor and 6k HP needed Greater Stoneshield potions even with a T2-geared healer [[d:paladin#1472703383554228326]]. Another needed Greater Stoneshield plus Barkskin to survive the enrage phase, with a Kara40-geared priest healing [[d:paladin#1463501091973890131]]. |
| **Everyone** | **Line of sight his stun ability**, or you get stunned for about 5 s. Many groups brute-force it without LoS [[d:paladin#1423669403995865160]]. He **enrages at 20%** [[d:paladin#1423668765588983910]]. |
| **Alternative** | Kite him instead of tanking him: "Final boss is a lesson in kiting or thiccccness" [[d:druid#1454127781913821380]], [[d:druid#1460287082483421340]]. Soloers kite him around the tree [[d:hunter#1434822306689187953]]. |
| **Watch out** | Healers and DPS: the sources give no safe spot or DPS cap. Players still ask what they are [[d:mage#1463643919534264443]]. |

- Loot: the epic **Eye of the Abyss** at about 1% ([Torta (staff), 2023-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=32868#p32868)), and the Spear of the Endless Hunt polearm [[d:hunter#1401939608027271311]], [[d:hunter#1360041386963112138]].
- A tank benchmark: "if you can tank the last boss of Black Morass you can probably tank Rag" [[d:shaman#1489072008456310895]].

## Solo farming

- **Mage:** a solo rep farm in all-green gear exists as a video [[d:mage#1438954132487798915]]. With vampirism you can include Blood Drinkers in AoE pulls [[d:mage#1441170272966410382]].
- **Hunter:** soloable for rep and rare drops (video) [[d:hunter#1449108918914781397]]. Wardens of Time rep can be solo farmed [[d:hunter#1483911885194526730]].

## Common wipes

1. The sand-caster stun on the tank in the pre-bridge packs [[d:paladin#1448464751389577439]].
2. No CC on a 4-dragon pack [[d:paladin#1472702745638211737]].
3. Antnormi's enrage at 20% on an undergeared tank [[d:paladin#1423668765588983910]], [[d:paladin#1463501091973890131]].
