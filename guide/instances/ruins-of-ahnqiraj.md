# Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj (AQ20)

**Summary:** The vanilla 20-player raid in Silithus, with no new Turtle bosses. It is an entry-level raid for fresh 60s, alongside Lower Karazhan Halls and Zul'Gurub, but "on the higher edge of challenge level as a fresh 60" [[d:shaman#1453532666514243748]]. Players like it ("aq20 is... good?") [[d:paladin#1439283804438134805]]. Its quirk is **high spell resistance on every boss** (+115/+135/+155), so casters need spell penetration here [[d:mage#1450488624125837452]], [[d:mage#1450527954076434525]]. For 20-player raids one tank named Ossirian and the "raptor" and "tiger" bosses as the main threats (the post may mix in ZG), and said none hit very hard with attentive healers [[d:paladin#1448462237986455632]].

## Raid-wide preparation

- **Spell resistance:** each boss has +115, +135 or +155 to all schools; Moam partially resists almost every spell (155 Shadow Resistance base) [[d:mage#1450488624125837452]], [[d:mage#1450522068750962769]], [[d:priest#1341566757709676545]]. One player summed it up as "every mob has 150 all res" [[d:priest#1341568975720419460]]. Bring spell-penetration gear (much of it drops here).
- **Nature:** roughly 10-20% of AQ20 is nature-immune per a vanilla-based list [[d:hunter#1407146893892386966]].
- **Pets:** healers often forget to heal hunter pets; one hunter had to recall and bandage on Buru [[d:hunter#1444731886374228048]], [[d:hunter#1444731934008934469]].
- **Survival hunters** can facetank trash here [[d:hunter#1462598609374543882]].

## Bosses

Kurinnaxx, General Rajaxx, Moam, Buru the Gorger, Ayamiss the Hunter and Ossirian the Unscarred (vanilla roster; the sources only discuss the ones below).

- **General Rajaxx:** a demonology warlock's summoned Infernal can turn the event NPCs hostile and flag the warlock for PvP (unresolved) [[d:warlock#1486387499973738608]].
- **Moam:** one of the only PvE fights where priests Mana Burn [[d:priest#1463494991207862447]], [[d:priest#1465762246016634922]]. A fast fight; a paladin tanked it on raw threat without taunt [[d:paladin#1156198891650297858]].
- **Ossirian the Unscarred:** the crystal shadow weakness can make a warlock and pet over-aggro by one or two DoTs; Demo is comfortable (threat split with the demon); a Doomguard can use a fire weakness [[d:warlock#1467179921116762345]], [[d:warlock#1467180107834593320]], [[d:warlock#1467180193473888266]]. The raid spreads out, which makes decursing hard [[d:mage#1193827871039422495]]. A shaman's Grounding Totem still eats the "AQ20 cyclone" [[d:shaman#1479859011288436843]]. Blessing of Sacrifice no longer breaks crowd control [[d:paladin#1484538195977961532]].

## Notable loot

- Survival hunters: a belt; MM hunters: a one-hand weapon [[d:hunter#1430482583862513664]].
- Rogues: the AQ20 set (Dagger of Veiled Shadows + Distracting Dagger) and the ring/cloak pieces stay relevant into Naxx [[d:rogue#1442106375164661833]], [[d:rogue#1489360663754182817]].
- Bears: the AQ20 tank set's 3-piece bonus gives a 4-target Swipe [[d:druid#1491449482066399294]].
- Warlocks: the AQ20 3-set ring [[d:warlock#1452170616206790758]].
- A good BoE helmet drops from the trash [[d:paladin#1493607352035835904]]. Moam drops the item for the mage water/orange quest [[d:mage#1306589003566546965]].
- Healer loot is thin (the Ossirian head) [[d:priest#1341568975720419460]].
