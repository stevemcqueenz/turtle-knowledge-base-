# Lower Blackrock Spire (LBRS)

Vanilla level ~55-60 orc/troll/dragonkin dungeon in Blackrock Spire. It is unloved on Turtle: "boring as shit and the gems never drop" [[d:shaman#1435769670409130125]]. Players still run it for Tier 0 pieces, the UBRS key gems and the arrow ammo box.

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 5 |
| Location | Blackrock Mountain (vanilla) |

## Group notes

- **Orc packs before Smolderweb** are the only risky pulls for a prot paladin. Use a ranged pull there [[d:paladin#1435893301231222916]].
- **Priests save a lot of time:** "skipping half the dungeon because you can use troll hex on almost every mob" (Hex of Weakness / Mind Control) [[d:shaman#1435769971316756705]].
- **Warlock tanks** have trouble with spell interrupts here. Bait them out, and keep threat with a fire wand plus Immolate [[d:warlock#1379816702786801864]].
- **Fire damage is weak** across the Blackrock dungeons (fire resistances and immunities) [[d:shaman#1457392210541805568]].

## The packs that end runs (and what worked)

| Pack | What happens | What works |
|---|---|---|
| **The Acolyte room** (the big hall before Omokk) | a dozen casters and their friends; pulling it from the room brought **12** at once and killed the first party that tried | pull it **back to the arrival ramp** in two or three bites, never fight inside the room, kill the casters first |
| **The Omokk hall** | two long ranks (18-19 mobs at the far ends) with walkers crossing; a pull from the middle dragged **22** | pull each rank from its **end**; let the walkers pass before the pull |
| **The War Master's room** (Voone) | the biggest cluster in the dungeon; pulling the boss pack from the walkway pulled the room's west rim in — **11** arrived | clear the rim first, or fight on the room floor; patrols first |
| **The Smolderweb hall** | the hall's rows **all link within about 15 yards** — touching one pulls the whole hall (16 of 17 came in our worst pull, plus walkers) | fight it in **small bites of 3-5** with your feet planted at the **hall mouth**, 20 yards back — never inside the hall. This is where one of our parties lost three wipes in one evening |
| **The shadow pairs** (Vosh'gajin's dais) | the two shadow-priest pairs **wake each other** — a 14-mob fight if you take them together | take them as **two separate pulls from the dais edge**, each fought back in the previous hall |
| **Spire Spiderling packs** (behind the Smolderweb leg) | seven squishy mobs that look like one easy pull and are not; in one of our runs they cost about nine minutes of standing around | fight them as their own pull, or leave them if you only want the bosses |
| **The scorpion bowl** (on the Wyrmthalak leg) | ~30 Spire Scorpids, and a kill is **back in 25 seconds** — pack-by-pack is a treadmill | either burst the cluster in one fight, or stay on the **shelf above the bowl** and walk past it |

[measured]

The draw counts in the table (12, 22, 11, 16 of 17, 14) are the arrivals counted from the round-154 log's join
lines; the hall's "links within about 15 yards" is the plan's assist-plus-call radius over its own rows.

## Boss notes we have measured

- **Omokk** is not the danger — the halls before him are. His own fight is a tank-and-spank.
- **Halycon and Gizrul** are a two-for-one in under five minutes; Gizrul arrives when Halycon dies, so hold the
  group where the fight ends.
- **Wyrmthalak** is a normal final boss; his leg is 11 packs and the packs are the clock.
- The run's time sinks are **big-pull rests and corpse walks**, not boss damage: a group that pulls the halls in
  small bites from the right direction is faster than one that pulls them whole and recovers. [measured]

**The page's one route line is right and should stay:** the orc packs before Smolderweb are the risky pulls — take
them with a ranged pull. Everything above is the detail behind that sentence.

## Key gems

The UBRS key quest needs gems that spawn at 6 possible spots across the whole dungeon, with a very low drop chance. Finding the one spot "is medieval torture" [[d:shaman#1435771318153777273]].

## Last boss and farming

- **Hunter pet-tank speedrun:** a hunter guild clears to the last boss in about 10 minutes, using pure pet tanking (a Screecher or Scorpid) with one hunter spamming Mend Pet. They farm the shoulders for the epic set [[d:hunter#1455955810583838955]].
- **Arrow ammo box:** the arrow counterpart of the Stratholme bullet box is in LBRS. It is BoE and always there on a fresh instance [[d:hunter#1481489849302253692]]. It can be solo farmed "with a few consumes" [[d:hunter#1481507733118124185]].
- **Voone** drops throwing axes that rogues use for kiting builds [[d:rogue#1433013282322776126]].
