# Zul'Farrak

Vanilla level ~44-54 troll dungeon in Tanaris. In 1.18.0 Turtle added the **Farraki Tombs** (Zel'jeb the Ancient) and the **Farraki Arena** gauntlet ([Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615)).

**Damage ramps up here.** "The damage just starts to increase by a large amount around Zul Farrak," so healers want Restoration talents and faster, cheaper heals [[d:shaman#1437274154872344696]].

## Zel'jeb the Ancient (lvl 46, Farraki Tombs)

| | |
|---|---|
| Key mechanic | **Ancient Embalming:** takes 90% less damage from everything, but **fire breaks it** ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Zel%27jeb_the_Ancient)) |
| No fire in the group? | Take the **Flame of Farrak** from the brazier next to Chief Ukorz Sandscalp. It lasts 10 minutes, so grab it just before the pull (wiki) |
| Other abilities | Thunderclap, War Stomp, **Curse of Years** at 50%; another emote at 25% |
| Loot | Bloodstained Fangblade, Staff of the Bloodbound; quest *The Farraki Ancient* |

## Farraki Arena

A gauntlet: **Kath'zen the Brutal** and **Juthza the Cunning**, then **Champion Razjal the Quick**, who casts Scorpid Ritual and **Hellfire** ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Farraki_Arena)). Loot: Champion's Sandhelm, Libram of the Farraki Zealot, Gilded Blade of Razjal. His loot-drop bug was fixed 2025-11-18 ([Torta (staff), 2025-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159994#p159994)).

## Farming

- The old mass-pull solo farms are dead. The ZF pathing exploits are patched [[d:mage#1424770785502953602]], and Turtle added anti-farm mechanics to ZF [[d:paladin#1426765897082867922]]. Pull sizes fell from about 80 to about 20 at best [[d:mage#1441954445670744277]].
- The Shallow Graves infinite-zombie exploit is fixed ([Reynolds (staff), 2024-12-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112126#p112126)).

## Loot
