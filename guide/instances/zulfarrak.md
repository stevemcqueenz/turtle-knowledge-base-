# Zul'Farrak

Vanilla level ~44-54 troll dungeon in Tanaris. In 1.18.0 Turtle added the **Farraki Tombs** (Zel'jeb the Ancient) and the **Farraki Arena** gauntlet ([Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615)).

**Damage ramps up here.** "The damage just starts to increase by a large amount around Zul Farrak," so healers want Restoration talents and faster, cheaper heals [[d:shaman#1437274154872344696]].

## Zel'jeb the Ancient (lvl 46, Farraki Tombs)

| | |
|---|---|
| Key mechanic | **Ancient Embalming:** takes 90% less damage from everything, but **fire breaks it** ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Zel%27jeb_the_Ancient)) |
| No fire in the group? | Take the **Flame of Farrak** from the brazier next to Chief Ukorz Sandscalp. It lasts 10 minutes, so grab it just before the pull (wiki) |
| Other abilities | Thunderclap, War Stomp, **Curse of Years** at 50%; another emote at 25% |
| Loot | Bloodstained Fangblade, Staff of the Bloodbound; quest *The Farraki Ancient* |

## Farraki Arena

A gauntlet: **Kath'zen the Brutal** and **Juthza the Cunning**, then **Champion Razjal the Quick**, who casts Scorpid Ritual and **Hellfire** ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Farraki_Arena)). Loot: Champion's Sandhelm, Libram of the Farraki Zealot, Gilded Blade of Razjal. His loot-drop bug was fixed 2025-11-18 ([Torta (staff), 2025-11-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159994#p159994)).

## The stair event — the clear gate

The page does not mention the event at all, and a run that skips it has not cleared Zul'Farrak.

**How it starts.** One player uses a **Troll Cage** at the top of the pyramid; five freed trolls walk to the stair
top and start the waves. The cage is a **keyed use**: it needs the **Executioner's Key**, which the **Sandfury
Executioner** in front of the cages drops (100% — that is why the route kills him first). The event is triggered by
the cage, not by a boss kill, and on the route above the king is reached after it. [measured]

**The three waves.**

| Wave | What spawns | Where it is fought |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **23 trolls** at the stair base, climbing up in groups of 2, 3, 4 … every 10 s | at the **top** — the party holds the stair head and kills them as they arrive single-file |
| 2 | **24 trolls**, starting 10 s after the last wave-1 add dies | same, at the top |
| 3 | **7 trolls at the base** — Shadowpriest Sezz'ziz, Nekrum Gutchewer and five trash | at the **base**: wave 3 never climbs, and the freed prisoners walk down with the group |

[measured]

**What to do.**

- **Buff and eat before touching a cage** — the waves start as soon as the freed trolls reach the stair top.
- **Hold the top** for waves 1 and 2 and kill the climbing stream where it arrives. Do not walk down to meet it.
- **Use the 10-second gap.** The next wave starts only when the last add of the current wave dies (fleeing adds
  count as alive), so the end of each wave is a free window to drink, top the tank and rebuff.
- **Wave 3 is a normal fight at the base:** adds first, then **Sezz'ziz** (he fears — Psychic Scream — and heals his
  minions, so interrupt and kill him), then **Nekrum** last. Both die within seconds of each other in our runs.
  [measured]
- **Keep the five freed trolls alive.** They fight for you; Weegli is also the only one who can blow open the End
  Door after the event. [measured]
- **There is no fail timer.** A wave stays open until every add is dead; a slow wave is not a wipe. [measured]

**Why groups die here** (from a player run we read): pulling the stream into the healer instead of letting it come
up the stairs; fighting wave 3 up top and getting caught between the two groups; and falling off the stair edge (the
stairs are narrow, and a fall costs the whole climb back). [player video]

## Packs and patrols worth a warning

- The amphitheatre and the temple ring are dense with **patrols that walk into a fight** (two or three per cluster);
  clear the patrol first, or pull the pack back into the room you came from. [measured]
- The **Sandfury packs near Velratha** draw extra mobs if pulled from the water's edge; pull them toward the stairs.
  [measured]
- The **arena gauntlet** is a separate fight each time; do not start it with the group still spread out.

## Farming

- The old mass-pull solo farms are dead. The ZF pathing exploits are patched [[d:mage#1424770785502953602]], and Turtle added anti-farm mechanics to ZF [[d:paladin#1426765897082867922]]. Pull sizes fell from about 80 to about 20 at best [[d:mage#1441954445670744277]].
- The Shallow Graves infinite-zombie exploit is fixed ([Reynolds (staff), 2024-12-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=112126#p112126)).
