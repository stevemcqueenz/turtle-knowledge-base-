# Stratholme

Vanilla level-60 dungeon in Eastern Plaguelands, with a Live (Scarlet) side and an Undead side. Capped at 5 players since 2025-08-15 ([Torta (staff), 2025-08-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145822#p145822)). Kills give Argent Dawn reputation ([Torta (staff), 2025-08-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145131#p145131)). On Turtle it is best known as the **paladin Force Reactive Disk + vampirism AoE gold farm**.

## Group notes

- **Undead side, tanks:** keep priority targets under control. The **banshees in Strat UD cast an AoE silence** [[d:warrior#1441916755252478004]]. Warrior tanks use Demoralizing Shout to put threat on the undead swarms [[d:warrior#1368632839045910719]].
- **Undead dungeons in general:** bring Blessed Wizard Oil and the Argent Dawn trinket "and you're going to cut through packs like butter" [[d:paladin#1436060738140635238]].
- **Magistrate Barthilas:** if you log out mid-run and come back about an hour later, for example after a server reset, he has respawned at the front and you can fight him there [[d:paladin#1432896000124522619]].

## Notable loot

- **Idol of Brutality** (bear) from Strat UD, farmed off the Magistrate [[d:druid#1478015155416862790]].
- **Healers:** a Strat UD chest and amulet with more HP but less regen than the Black Morass pieces [[d:druid#1435928189200236686]].
- **Hunters:** a best-in-slot ammo box (about 20.5 DPS bullets, BoE) sits next to a boss on the Live side and is always there on a fresh instance [[d:hunter#1481489849302253692]].

## Magistrate farm (Idol of Brutality)

He flees and cannot be targeted outside the gate area. Enter through the main gate, open the right-side gate, and kill the mobs in the middle while hugging the wall. Then sneak up to him and spam a damage ability; approached this way he does not flee. Bring disease removal (or Jungle Remedy), or the True-Sight pack spots you. The back door does not work. He resets after about 30 minutes [[d:druid#1478685056133234778]], [[d:druid#1478691433966997564]], [[d:druid#1478692236697927732]], [[d:druid#1478694735546945598]].

## Cannon Master Willey (solo)

He is soloable. The problem is the trash on the way, which sees through stealth. Chain Sprint → Vanish → Preparation → Sprint → Vanish past it [[d:rogue#1436370216752910336]], [[d:rogue#1436370330297045094]].

## Paladin AoE farm (Undead side)

The standard high-end paladin gold farm, ranked hardest of the three after Lashers and ZG bijous: "strat ud for raw gold, zg for bijous or ol' reliable lasher farm" [[d:paladin#1456098380295180389]]. Players estimate it at about 250+ g/h [[d:paladin#1454266113612447954]].

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Vampirism** | About 16%+ on gear because of the 0.7× AoE lifesteal multiplier; 10% after the reduction is the bare minimum, and 12% is noticeably safer [[d:paladin#1480326094812086284]], [[d:paladin#1480326285870764062]]. 14% works if you run into the pack below 50% HP. 16% makes it "completely braindead" [[d:paladin#1489957164272455761]], [[d:paladin#1489965864500461648]]. |
| **Force Reactive Disk** | "Pretty much mandatory" for the vampirism build [[d:paladin#1432869464428515591]]. Engineering for FRD "makes every aoe pull easier" [[d:paladin#1456098669765066832]]. |

**Route and danger points (Grey/Gilfrey):**

1. Cast Blessing of Freedom the moment you open the first gate. Exorcism the far mobs, collect everything, then stop near the wagon/fountain to let vampirism proc before continuing [[d:paladin#1489958889280770071]].
2. The two points where you die: **right after zoning in**, before you have balled up the fountain mobs, and **right after the rat-trap gate**. Waiting for it to open "basically obliterates the deathball", so rush to pull more mobs to lifesteal from [[d:paladin#1489958361733660743]].
3. The whole instance, entrance to entrance, can be one pull that ends with the Unforgiven dead [[d:paladin#1489957647116406895]]. A speed potion for the gate is optional [[d:paladin#1489958015867027486]].
4. "The hardest part... is just surviving until they ball up" inside Consecration + FRD [[d:paladin#1468335125937913856]].

**History and limits:** the old "gigapull" died when vampirism was nerfed and mob damage buffed. Smaller pulls still work [[d:paladin#1467888433358963013]]. T1 gear with 18% vampirism, Dream's Herald and FRD clears only about half the Undead side [[d:paladin#1491659549755707412]]. An MC-geared paladin is "too weak to consistently do strat" [[d:paladin#1454261724952789093]]. On Ambershire it needs a second window with a hunter feign-pulling packs while the paladin holds a corner [[d:paladin#1477055611958202398]], [[d:paladin#1427305261353275464]]. Some veterans call the Strat farm oversaturated [[d:paladin#1427479061155938405]].
