# Zul'Gurub (ZG)

**Summary:** The vanilla 20-player troll raid in Stranglethorn Vale, with no new Turtle bosses. It is one of the three entry raids for a fresh 60, alongside Lower Karazhan Halls and AQ20 [[d:shaman#1453532666514243748]]. The bosses are well known. The Turtle-specific points are these: an optional "hard mode" Hakkar (leave the priest bosses alive), Freezing Trap-immune raptor packs, shadow hunter trash that aggros from double vanilla range, and the troll packs before Jin'do, whose curse multiplies the physical damage a tank takes. ZG is also the server's main mining and solo-farm raid.

## Raid-wide notes

- **Nature:** ZG has essentially no nature-immune bosses per a vanilla-based list [[d:hunter#1407146893892386966]].
- **Rain of Fire** fails to hit in some ZG spots (the last two bat packs by the bat boss, the first left spider pack, the AoE zombies by an idol boss near the panther area) [[d:warlock#1476326140770517073]], [[d:warlock#1476320132774232074]].
- **Grounding Totem** was reported not to work on Hakkar's Mind Control (bug report) [[d:shaman#1360807816633384980]].

## Trash

- **Troll packs before Jin'do** apply a curse that raises physical damage taken by roughly 400-500% and they stun; a solo tank should kite them through a hunter trap, a shaman totem or Piercing Howl, and LIP + Challenging Shout the big pack with slaves [[d:warrior#1399585291693461574]].
- **Raptors** (for example the corner 3-raptor pack in the Hakkari Grounds) are immune to Freezing Trap: use Frost Trap + Scare Beast, or Sleep Dust [[d:hunter#1454336662804107307]], [[d:hunter#1454348119092498534]], [[d:hunter#1456227900276605019]].
- **Shadow hunters** were changed to aggro from about double vanilla range even when only your pet has threat [[d:hunter#1483812534795374663]].
- **Panther pyramid speed pull:** drop from the stairs onto the pack below the bridge and fight the boss while everything runs in, onto a full-mitigation paladin with a Free Action Potion and Force Reactive Disk and 1-2 T3-geared healers — fun but most groups avoid it [[d:paladin#1468029408316227778]], [[d:paladin#1468029495125606544]].

## Bosses

- **Bloodlord Mandokir:** DoT ticks do not seem to count toward his Gaze (single source) [[d:warlock#1492963433069416662]].
- **Jin'do the Hexxer:** see the troll packs above.
- **Spider boss (Mar'li):** often skipped because it takes long, the poison is annoying and the rewards are poor [[d:druid#1479569283418947847]].
- **Hakkar:**
  - **"Hard mode":** do not kill any of the priest bosses first [[d:druid#1479568454234144798]]. Players want unique rewards for it ([Sivent (player), 2024-08-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=100329#p100329)). His stun alone is manageable with decent tanks and DPS [[d:druid#1479569406622437438]].
  - Warlocks used an enslaved Mad Servant to nearly solo-tank him; devs added a healing reduction on enslaved demons to stop it [[d:warlock#1464413341274603797]]. A Doomguard with Felstone healing and Power Overwhelming survived ~30 s tanking him [[d:warlock#1491356701834870824]].

## Notable loot

- Hunters want the ZG head/leg enchant [[d:hunter#1430478630890373250]].
- Ferals: the Belt of Preserved Heads quest reward, Seal and Band of Jin, and Gahz'ranka's Nat Pagle's Fish Terminator / Tigule's Harpoon; most other drops are only good for fresh 60s [[d:druid#1456715469930238002]]. Boomkins find nothing better than MC/Ony/Kara10 gear [[d:druid#1456277632994906265]]. Bear tanks value the fishing-boss trinket and the quest belt [[d:druid#1463190764925223037]].
- Restoration shamans: the Jin'do mace is the main healing main hand [[d:shaman#1457014103880237186]].
- Tanks farm ZG spell-hit pieces for taunt reliability [[d:warrior#1434750016345346089]]; Nat Pagle's Broken Reel is the Four Horsemen taunt trinket [[d:druid#1464013456167669915]].
- Fang of Venoxis was buffed into a rogue main-hand upgrade over Alcor's Sunrazor [[d:rogue#1472656404958412912]], [[d:rogue#1473054088576110875]].

## Farming

- **Mining in ZG** is the best easy gold farm for rogues; AoE mob farming by mages is dead after nerfs [[d:rogue#1489623080711094384]], [[d:mage#1456745859290238977]].
- Hunters solo-kite the pyramid/panther packs, but the sleep-poison range was raised to ~35-40 yards [[d:hunter#1491826077381951550]]. To leave the pyramid, log out, kick yourself from the group, and log back in [[d:hunter#1487164840165511229]].
- Warlocks farm with an enslaved imp after a wall-pull fix; the imp can no longer cross water [[d:warlock#1466553667996876966]], [[d:warlock#1462458323134845124]].
