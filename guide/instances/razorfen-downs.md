# Razorfen Downs

Vanilla level ~35-45 quilboar/Scourge dungeon in the southern Barrens. In 1.18.0 Turtle added the **Temple of Agamaggan**, with **Death Prophet Rakameg** ([Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615)).

## Death Prophet Rakameg (lvl 39)

- Abilities: **Shadow Burst**, **Consuming Shadows**. Loot: Thornspine, Thorngorged Fleshgloves, **Totem of the Rotten Roots** (shaman), Blood-etched Fetish ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Prophet_Rakameg)).

## Route

The page has no route; this is the one our clears run (measured):

**Mordresh Fire Eye → Amnennar the Coldbringer** (final), with **Death Prophet Rakameg** (Turtle, the Temple of
Agamaggan), **Glutton** and **Ragglesnout** as optional stops on the way.

- Our clears take **Mordresh**, **Glutton** (when the route passes him) and **Amnennar**; **Rakameg** and
  **Ragglesnout** were skipped in the runs we measured — a group that wants the Temple of Agamaggan detours for it.
- **The spiral is the run's cost.** The dungeon is a long spiral down to Amnennar; the trash between the two
  required bosses is most of the time, and the page's own skeleton-pack tanking tip (**Demoralizing Shout** for the
  big packs) is the line to keep for it. [measured on bot runs, private 1.18.1-based realm — verified against our plan files, not a run log]

## Boss notes we have measured

- **Mordresh Fire Eye** is the first required boss; the pull that matters is the pack in front of him, not his own
  damage. [measured]
- **Amnennar the Coldbringer** (final) closes the spiral; our clears kill him last, after Glutton when he is taken.
  The page has no tactic line for him. [measured]
- **Rakameg's** kit (Shadow Burst, Consuming Shadows) is on the page; our clears skip the fight, so there is no
  measured tactic for it yet. [measured]

## Notes

- **Warrior tanks:** the skeleton packs are too big to tab-Sunder. Demoralizing Shout puts threat on every mob in range and keeps them off the healer [[d:warrior#1368632839045910719]].
- **Loot:** the Cold Rage Dagger at about 40 [[d:rogue#1324107314747408587]]. The RFD wand no longer has a mage-only class restriction [[d:priest#1407510148943187968]].
