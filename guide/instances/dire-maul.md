# Dire Maul

Vanilla level ~55-60 three-wing dungeon in Feralas: East (DME), West (DMW) and North (DMN, including the "Tribute" run). On Turtle it is the home of the **lasher farm**, the most common solo gold farm across classes. Kills give Shen'dralar reputation. Capped at 5 players since 2025-08-15 ([Torta (staff), 2025-08-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145822#p145822), [Torta (staff), 2025-08-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=145131#p145131)).

## Group notes

- **Ogres are spaced out, so body-pull them** [[d:paladin#1435892955801063465]].
- **DME satyr pack:** one pack pulls the entire top floor if you enslave the imp in it [[d:mage#1435740073952809020]]. A satyr patrols into the lasher room near the back wall [[d:mage#1435733072044888246]].
- **Tribute ogres (Warlock tip):** Immolate 2-3 ogres at a time and tab Searing Pain. The ogre knock-up interrupts Hellfire. On the final Tribute fight, send the Voidwalker onto the second ogre [[d:warlock#1396515922520440964]].
- **DMW ghosts** are the one PvE use for Detect Greater Invisibility [[d:warlock#1478262016820121713]].
- **Dreadsteed quest** (warlock epic mount, DMW): "not too hard" even in a PUG. Bring plenty of soul shards and tell the group in advance, because PUGs often skip it [[d:warlock#1488259139070333009]].

## East (DME): lashers and the jump run

### Bosses on the farm route

- **Hydrospawn** (water elemental, same area as the lashers): soloable by geared priests, paladins and others. Kite him around a pillar with Frost Aura up, or pull him to a wall and burn him [[d:priest#1432728366296858786]], [[d:paladin#1427716920815386837]], [[d:paladin#1427717080249270454]]. He drops a blue to disenchant and a chance at a book. An invisible demon add sometimes drops Felcloth [[d:paladin#1454183269468999691]].
- **Zevrim Thornhoof** (satyr) sacrifices a player. **Shaman:** re-drop Grounding Totem immediately after it is consumed, and pre-place it [[d:shaman#1435010722521288714]], [[d:shaman#1435014446085963999]]. **Rogue:** kick to interrupt his Sacrifice [[d:rogue#1489890399135862874]]. **Warlock:** go Demonology and use an enslaved rogue [[d:warlock#1455943027779436618]]. The old pathing-glitch kite is fixed [[d:warlock#1455942957138972702]].
- **The satyr boss upstairs:** pull him downstairs to the pillar. He cannot fit through the gap, so peek out, get one auto-attack plus Judgement, Exorcism or Crusader Strike, and step back into the gap [[d:paladin#1427718335088295996]], [[d:paladin#1427718442785570907]].
- **Alzzin the Wildshaper (last boss):** he burns mana on hit, so a duo prefers a druid tank + shaman healer over a shaman tank. You can also kite him on the ledge [[d:shaman#1429747984081489921]], [[d:shaman#1429760269650759741]].
- **Full "jump run" order:** lashers → elemental boss → satyr boss upstairs → last boss [[d:paladin#1427716354978484377]]. Thorium nodes after the last boss are "a slog to farm" [[d:paladin#1437837613749768224]].

### Lasher farm: rules that apply to everyone

- **Lashers are Nature-immune.** Force Reactive Disk (nature damage) and Crest of Retribution procs do nothing to them [[d:shaman#1430997994865557658]], [[d:paladin#1467897490769576071]], [[d:paladin#1456566778313572528]].
- **Level:** from about 52-54. Below 56 some packs cannot be reached without aggroing elites [[d:paladin#1417502930247876678]]. A friend being power-levelled there must be 52+ or have innate invisibility so they do not pull [[d:paladin#1450437723134365727]].
- **Income** now comes from herbs rather than vendor greys, so it follows herb prices [[d:shaman#1395550022006866032]]. Herbalism adds about 20% [[d:priest#1420896033189396512]]. Reported rates: about 100 g/h on Nordanaar [[d:shaman#1425738262923972720]]; 80-100 g per lockout run for a geared paladin [[d:paladin#1426000698780291182]]. About 5 lockouts per hour is the cap [[d:shaman#1488144156000911462]].

## West (DMW)

- **Illyanna Ravenoak** (the hunter boss with a bear) drops a blue 19-DPS arrow recipe (BoP, low drop rate) [[d:hunter#1486414327211884574]], [[d:hunter#1486862338882670725]]. **Hunter solo:** hide behind the statue so she comes to you, give the pet a couple of seconds' lead, and Feign Death to pass aggro back to the pet [[d:hunter#1490152762330189914]], [[d:hunter#1490152898267582524]].
- **Shen'dralar reputation solo:** either the East route (lashers + Hydrospawn + demon) or repeatedly killing West's first boss. About 300-400 rep and 2 book drops per run, 3-4 runs per hour. The class dagger and cloak are worth the grind [[d:hunter#1438125601927204885]], [[d:hunter#1438126491958509578]], [[d:hunter#1438126767620755577]].
- The Libram of Focus spell-power head/leg enchant is turned in to the Shen'dralar [[d:paladin#1439161598857183304]].

## North (DMN) and the Tribute run

- **King Gordok charges.** That is intended, and it happened on official servers too. Hunters: do not have Serpent Sting on him when you Feign Death, since the tick pulls him back. Feign Death early [[d:hunter#1424676020530843740]].
- **The priest ogre** Power Word: Shields itself and does not take aggro from damage until the shield is broken. Use Distracting Shot on it [[d:hunter#1471519978598564012]].
- **Solo Tribute:** hunters need **Engineering** to blow the locked door with a Seaforium charge. Skip packs with pet pulls [[d:hunter#1440827721516060672]], [[d:hunter#1440828679885164545]]. Route: pet-pull the first two groups, jump down onto the tent in the long hallway (no aggro), then one Invisibility Potion before the spiders and a second for them [[d:hunter#1390667181142048898]].
- **Contested: can a hunter solo it?** One player on Ambershire found the "Cho'Rush" can out-heal a solo hunter's mana drain [[d:hunter#1434687463108116591]]. Nordanaar players say the mana-regen problem was fixed months ago [[d:hunter#1434703269065785580]], and that Viper Sting on him makes it doable [[d:hunter#1434822133632208928]].
- **Loot:** skipping every boss but the last gives the Tribute chest, which can drop the Tarnished Elven Ring [[d:rogue#1485646505623945478]], [[d:druid#1439574550516990083]]. It is a good source of Roasted Quail pet food and gold [[d:hunter#1448690064799305759]].
- **Darkmantle beads:** the drunk ogre miniboss (up to 3 beads, soloable) and the ogre-mage trash (about 7 per reset) [[d:rogue#1435876864362156103]], [[d:rogue#1435955537756819466]].
