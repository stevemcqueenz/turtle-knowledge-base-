# Tower of Karazhan ("Kara40", "K40", Upper Karazhan)

**Summary:** Turtle's hardest raid (Tier 3.5). An experienced tank's view: "not that much of a step up from Naxx", but every raider must execute mechanics, and small mistakes wipe the raid (one player moving during a 6-second window on the last boss) [[d:paladin#1457969661483290666]], [[d:paladin#1457861795912880161]]. 40 players, 9 encounters. Plan on Naxxramas-level gear, **7-11 healers**, about **200 Arcane Resistance** for Anomalus, a **Shadow Resistance tank** for the Chess Bishop, **2-3 players per magic school** for Incantagos, and spell penetration or a spec swap for Mephistroth. Most bosses are demons (bring Elixir of Demonslaying). About half the bosses are **bleed-immune** and several are immune to specific poisons or schools, so check the immunity table before you pick a spec. The trash kills more raids than some of the bosses.

## Boss order

Keeper Gnarlmoon → Ley-Watcher Incantagos → Anomalus → Echo of Medivh → Chess Event → Sanv Tas'dal → Rupturan the Broken → Kruul → Mephistroth [[d:warlock#1464996618762977505]], [[d:warrior#1447524170836611122]]. Bosses 6-9 are the "Outland bosses" behind the Scepter ([Torta (staff), 2025-04-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126946#p126946)).

One guild's kill times: Gnarlmoon ~3 min, Incantagos ~2, Anomalus ~1.5, Medivh ~1, Chess 2:30-3, Sanv 2:30-3, Rupturan ~3, Kruul 2-2:30, Mephistroth 3-5 depending on phase RNG [[d:paladin#1431942838782001192]] (single source).

Worgen and demon trash lost their poison immunity in a hotfix.

### Arcane Resistance (Anomalus)

- **The number is contested.** At release the minimum was around 225 AR [[d:warrior#1466709085008433254]] (partly because warriors' Sheen of Zanza reflected the prison onto themselves [[d:warrior#1466709112212557927]], [[d:warrior#1466709317834117332]]); by 2026 it was "at most 110", with the whole raid at roughly 240 buffed as the safe target [[d:warrior#1466626780340293682]], [[d:warrior#1466626795133730999]], [[d:warrior#1466626871591440477]]; others say 110 is "griefing hard" [[d:warrior#1487170518976172183]]. Commonly quoted working figures: 190 (one rogue ran it for a month with no wipes) [[d:rogue#1466083702949023869]], 195+ [[d:druid#1492106894800719942]], 200 unbuffed for many guilds [[d:mage#1482669809807396974]], 220 for holy paladins [[d:paladin#1444240875063480410]]. Weirdthing saw different outcomes at the same AR week to week and suspects a random or threat-based targeting component [[d:warrior#1466698439453835385]], [[d:warrior#1466708829088780308]]; the prison seems to pick higher-threat players, so DPS may need less than tanks [[d:druid#1489285323057004565]], [[d:druid#1489295796863242362]]. Staff reduced the requirement on 2025-04-16 ([Torta (staff), 2025-04-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126946#p126946)).
- **How to reach ~200:** the crafted "Cosmic" 4-piece set + Enchanting cloak (+15) + Kara head/leg enchants (+25/+25) + Argent Dawn shoulder enchant (+5) is exactly +200 [[d:mage#1482669809807396974]]; the 4-piece set with the shoulder enchant and no cloak is ~170 [[d:warrior#1469310881891352688]]. Extra pieces: Slivers of Nullification, Brand of Karazhan, an Incantagos turn-in and belt, Anomalus boots [[d:warrior#1469338847325196457]], [[d:warrior#1469338934361194538]].
- **Buffs:** Magic Resistance Potion (no arcane aura or totem exists) [[d:warrior#1458982047979671613]]; Mark of the Wild [[d:warrior#1469310530438299821]]; the Eastern Plaguelands arcane buff from the Champion mind-control trick, known to the devs and left in, lasts 1 hour and is lost on death [[d:priest#1504884269825396807]], [[d:priest#1504898252896600257]], [[d:warlock#1480483435956011048]]; mind-controlled Scarlet Medics give +84 AR [[d:druid#1482008086557491230]].
- **Ley Crystal logistics:** head/leg enchants cost 2 Ley Energy per raider; you must kill Anomalus before the bosses that drop T3.5, so enchant raiders' AR gear before stockpiling recipes [[d:warrior#1465820866540732477]], [[d:warrior#1465821143922376735]], [[d:warrior#1465821381726830845]].

### Consumables by boss

From one raiding tank's list [[d:warrior#1465807734892138659]], [[d:warrior#1465808097770471514]], [[d:warrior#1465809108404801628]]: Greater Fire Protection + Frozen Runes for Rupturan and Mephistroth; Greater Shadow Protection for Kruul and Chess; Restorative Potions for Echo; **Elixir of Demonslaying** for all demon bosses and trash (Gnarlmoon included) [[d:warrior#1465827336783007970]]; Consecrated stones for Echo, Elemental elsewhere; **Arcane Bombs** for Incantagos; target dummies and Limited Invulnerability Potions for Kruul; the MT needs a Frost or Arcane Reflector for the trash before Incantagos. Frost Protection helps on Incantagos' Blizzards [[d:warrior#1463919461554720885]]. Budget ~150 g per speed run, ~300 g for a night of progression wipes [[d:warrior#1451944109781815497]], [[d:warrior#1451944331622744185]].

### Composition

- **Healers:** 7-9 on farm [[d:warrior#1454295977300070692]], [[d:warrior#1454420992926416951]]; 10-12 in progression [[d:warrior#1454295471433584710]]. Raid damage per second: Anomalus ~9k, Kruul ~7.5k (vs. Sapphiron ~7k, Patchwerk ~3.5k) [[d:warrior#1454296556474994833]].
- **Casters of every school** for Incantagos, and off-specs (Elemental, Balance, Shadow) are hard to find [[d:paladin#1438452487966294207]].
- **A Shadow Resistance tank** (warlock or shadow priest, the same player who tanks Twin Emperors) for the Chess Bishop, plus 3-4 other tanks [[d:warrior#1454295471433584710]].
- Paladins: up to 7 in a working raid (typically 3 Prot, 3 Holy) [[d:paladin#1426156614750306395]]. Druids: 1 feral per melee group, 1-4 ferals and 1-3 boomkins [[d:druid#1472319490942570587]].

### Class notes that apply to the whole raid

- **Arcane mages** swap to Fire or Frost for boss 3 and its trash, and many stay Fire for Medivh because there is no time to respec [[d:mage#1430921393029251072]], [[d:mage#1433242094159527936]], [[d:mage#1454850922089021500]].
- **Shadow priests / Affliction warlocks** need 75-110 spell penetration or a Fire spec for Mephistroth [[d:priest#1436138912954454148]], [[d:warlock#1470882688700387389]].
- **Feral cats** lose value: roughly half to two-thirds of the content is bleed-immune [[d:druid#1425528745061519400]], [[d:druid#1465402093685706825]].
- **Demonology warlocks:** greater demons die on most encounters unless micromanaged; Kruul is the exception [[d:warlock#1464983131282083900]], [[d:warlock#1464982840990236928]]. Community demon picks: Gnarlmoon Infernal, Incantagos Felguard, Anomalus Doomguard (Infernal if it dies), Medivh Felguard [[d:warlock#1482836194642362511]]. A pathing bug stops demons hiding behind pillars [[d:warlock#1480292848183148544]].
- **Hunter pets:** Gnarlmoon, Medivh, Sanv and Mephistroth never kill the pet; keep it with you on Anomalus; micro it on Chess, Rupturan and Kruul (details per boss below) [[d:hunter#1397202595583492096]]. Pet survival is much harder here than in Naxx [[d:hunter#1485925913244864676]]. A pet that picked up the Magus debuff once wiped a whole raid [[d:paladin#1445204519259471994]].
- **Rogues:** the Blade Flurry buff often gets stuck on Chess and Incantagos (relog to fix) [[d:rogue#1428255344823636018]], [[d:rogue#1424486188919558175]]. The 32-buff cap hides buffs such as Righteous Fury, Battle Shout and Slice and Dice on fights like Kruul; ban low-value buffs with an addon [[d:rogue#1428254916778004633]], [[d:rogue#1428259749983424523]].
- **Paladins:** do not use the Farraki libram in raid; blacklisting its buffs lags the raid [[d:paladin#1456736206107709662]].
- **Tanks:** one paladin MT's gear per boss: T3 for the ravens, T2.5 for Gnarlmoon, Arcane Resistance for Incantagos and Anomalus, T2.5 for Medivh, Rook and Sanv, T3 full mitigation for Knight and Rupturan, T3 + spell power for Mephistroth [[d:paladin#1483532816535912468]]. Knight and Rupturan require crush immunity (118.4% effective avoidance for a paladin); Rook and Mephistroth strongly encourage it [[d:paladin#1465172802326761748]]. A warrior's first Kara40 should be Deep Tactics rather than Fury Prot [[d:warrior#1495385249302183986]].

## Bosses

### 1. Keeper Gnarlmoon (worgen demon)

The raid splits into a **red** side and a **blue** side; your color comes from where you stand on the pull (raids force it by having the red group stand left and closer) [[d:warrior#1457149632265060383]], [[d:warrior#1457154380120985620]], [[d:warrior#1457154519124410489]]. **Ravens** spawn about every 30 s [[d:warrior#1371573397816873142]] and can only be hurt by players with the matching aura; there is an **owl phase** that is a raid DPS check [[d:rogue#1423566090960900188]], [[d:rogue#1423566170207948851]].

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| Tank | A dedicated AoE tank for the ravens (Prot Paladin, Elemental Shaman or Arms Warrior) [[d:warrior#1454295471433584710]]. The boss tank is "essentially a taunt bot" and needs only some stamina [[d:warrior#1469111929589989407]]. Paladins: pre-place Consecration where ravens group (towards the left of the spawn) and Greater Blessing on spawn [[d:paladin#1361732205692321862]], [[d:paladin#1485541547729621206]]; since 1.18.1 Consecration's front-loaded damage starts on each mob's first hit, so pre-placing is less reliable [[d:paladin#1485639689175302266]], and Blessing spam no longer out-threats a resto druid's HoTs [[d:paladin#1449767594717610005]]. A Jewel Crystal + Sapper gives snap threat [[d:paladin#1431960511850745907]] (another paladin says fire and sappers do not work here — **Contested** [[d:paladin#1361732205692321862]]) |
| Healers | Red-side healers remove Blessing of Salvation so they do not drag ravens [[d:paladin#1470219458680459356]]. Good fight for HoTs [[d:druid#1379865239633395823]]; T3.5 Flash/Renew over T2 Greater Heal for priests [[d:priest#1454630442161405973]] |

### 2. Ley-Watcher Incantagos (blue drakonid)

Five **Affinity** adds, one per school (fire, frost, shadow, nature, arcane), each of which must die to damage of its own school inside a short window ([Torta (staff), 2025-04-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126056#p126056)). A player put it at "40k HP in 15 seconds" per affinity ([Sirracha (player), 2025-04-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126572#p126572)). Staff: "downed with as low as 2 on some elements, and with 3 on all" ([Akalix (staff), 2025-04-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126582#p126582)). Healers and hybrids count (holy priests on shadow, ferals and resto druids on nature) ([Hadjo (player), 2025-04-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126644#p126644)).

| Role | Job |

### 3. Anomalus (arcane elemental)

Arcane-damage burst fight with **bombs**, an **Arcane Prison** that checks Arcane Resistance, and a threat-resetting charge. One of two fights with no adds to DPS [[d:warrior#1371573397816873142]].

Three tanks are enough [[d:warrior#1494786659894431784]]. Dodge and parry prevent **Manabound Strikes** stacks; blocked or resisted hits still stack, so a bear is a good third tank [[d:warrior#1494787179401052161]], [[d:warrior#1494788788633735331]]. ## Trash

- **Before Gnarlmoon:** the first pack (3 ghosts and a bat) is harmless; later vampiric bats AoE-silence and must die after the Darkbringers [[d:paladin#1490083505026240602]], [[d:paladin#1490083599171453059]]. **Darkbringers** raise a shield ~12 s after the pull and queue a one-shot ~15 s after the pull: a **green** shield means magic immunity (needs a physical kick), **purple** means physical immunity (needs a magic interrupt); the shield cast itself cannot be interrupted, and most K40 mobs are stun-immune. The big pack before Gnarlmoon has three; assign interrupters per marker (e.g. rogue on green, mage on purple), or kill them before any shield [[d:paladin#1490083855833497660]], [[d:paladin#1490084192959332475]], [[d:paladin#1490084246843297803]], [[d:paladin#1490084397817528431]], [[d:paladin#1490084601320706248]], [[d:paladin#1490084919119188151]], [[d:paladin#1490084993559433398]], [[d:paladin#1490085104226144387]].
- **After Gnarlmoon:** Fear Ward the tanks before the blue-dragon trash; you have ~15 s to kill the Guards before the fear [[d:paladin#1490089494328770621]], [[d:paladin#1490089742593949696]]. Before Incantagos focus the Guards (their armor debuff can reach 8k armor), keep healers away from Suppressors, have shamans purge spell 57635 off Guards and the big drakes, kill any big blue crystals, and pull carefully around patrols [[d:paladin#1490086664843034856]].
- **Later trash:** Banish the big elemental whose stacking AoE pulse kills at 4 stacks (or outrange it), and Shackle the undead that can one-shot the raid if people move [[d:paladin#1490085707849535579]].
- **Trash is more dangerous than several bosses:** single mobs cast ~3k spells, add pulls can crit ~6k with AoE [[d:paladin#1457078638267863214]], [[d:paladin#1457078702474133688]].
- **Pre-Anomalus bombs:** Manascale Suppressor's *Unstabilized Mana* (+100% cast speed, explodes for ~5k after 10 s) is safe to dispel; Disrupted Arcane Elemental's *Mana Buildup* should be dispelled; Unstable Arcane Elemental's *Unstable Mana* (drains 600 mana and deals 600 per second for 10 s) must **not** be dispelled [[d:mage#1475624746149482586]].
- **Pre-Medivh packs** cause the most rage: wipes come from execution errors (extra packs pulled, healers out of LoS, pets pulling a third pack) [[d:paladin#1471659691519115327]]. Large elementals on the Medivh slopes carry a stacking group DoT aura; pulling onto the slope breaks LoS to healers [[d:paladin#1430109804713480193]].
- **Uninterruptible casters** that an Arcane Bomb can silence: Death Lord, Deathknight Cavalier, Necro Knight, Ley Seekers, Desolate Invader, Ima'ghaol [[d:rogue#1465549930344222846]].
- After Chess, Arcane-Bomb the demon trash before it casts Death Coil or Agony [[d:warrior#1474205907490570271]].
- Skipping the top-of-tower demon pack is seen as exploit-like [[d:paladin#1481778353777152000]]; skipping the squid boss costs a guaranteed Ley Crystal for T3.5 [[d:paladin#1481779554442809484]].
- **Invisibility skips:** the pack after Chess and possibly the elementals after Anomalus; a hunter usually does it, so skip potions on Chess to have one ready [[d:paladin#1467288980557922555]], [[d:paladin#1467289256396591116]], [[d:paladin#1467289416530657380]].
- Prot paladins often swap to a Vengeance/Necrodin spec for trash and back before the boss [[d:paladin#1465229551486505157]].
