# Emerald Sanctum (ES)

**Summary:** A custom 40-player raid in Hyjal at "Blackwing Lair difficulty" (Tier 2), with two bosses: **Erennius** (mini-boss) and **Solnius the Awakener**. In **normal mode** you kill Erennius first. In **hard mode** you fight Solnius while Erennius stays alive and has to be tanked. The raid is a sleep, dispel and resistance puzzle. Solnius and his trash resist Nature and poisons. Hard mode punishes wrong dispels. The add phase is random and gets much harder with Scalebanes. Hard mode is "manageable by simply bringing more healers" in one caster's view [[d:mage#1493997047924260985]].

## Raid-wide preparation

- **Nature and poisons:** Solnius has 50 Nature and 100 Shadow resistance ([Borefficz (player), 2024-05-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94829#p94829)), [[d:druid#1311112461067489431]], and is "super resistant to poisons" [[d:rogue#1303781478244552806]]; Instant Poison works on ES mobs, with resists [[d:rogue#1485996714866245742]], [[d:rogue#1485998161238560969]]. Solnius is nature/poison-immune enough that Corrosive Poison was added for rogues ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102971#p102971)).
- **Mana:** Solnius is a mana-drain fight, like Kel'Thuzad [[d:druid#1317117705614262373]].
- **Sleeps:** sleep-heavy trash gets dispelled by healers, which cancels a boomkin's Hurricane and wastes its cooldown [[d:druid#1481829573137141878]], [[d:druid#1482975539810533446]]. Erennius' **Dreamstate** is a dispellable Magic sleep ([Torta (staff), 2023-12-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=82980#p82980)).
- **Tank gear:** a Nature Resistance set for hard-mode Erennius is on one tank's list of sets [[d:paladin#1451620232870498354]].

## Trash

- **Whelp packs:** a druid stacking T1 + T2 Thorns bonuses with Demoralizing Roar handles them well [[d:druid#1473218279764922368]]; demo warlocks Hellfire the big packs [[d:warlock#1465519812745625819]].
- Staff removed 5 trash groups in 2023 ([Torta (staff), 2023-12-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=79953#p79953)).
- A reported trash skip: a hunter pulls Solnius, someone damages him, the hunter feigns, and the trash does not follow ([Andyru (player), 2024-05-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=95027#p95027)) — no staff response.

## Bosses

### Erennius (green dragon)

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| Tank | Normal mode: killed first. **Hard mode:** he stays up for the whole fight and needs his own tank and healers. Warrior avoidance is effectively zero here; Nature Resistance helps only a little (≈150 unbuffed from gear, +25 Improved Mark of the Wild, +50 from a resistance potion for 3 min), because he mostly auto-attacks plus a periodic ~30 s sleep [[d:warrior#1488534840277074082]], [[d:warrior#1488534967834382377]], [[d:warrior#1488536244479852554]], [[d:warrior#1488546224717168660]]. His threat drops during hard mode come from a sleep-like effect that avoids the slept target rather than a true wipe [[d:warrior#1437152665581781105]], [[d:warrior#1437154802390925313]]. A paladin generated 65k threat in 3 minutes with Holy Strike, Holy Shield, Consecration and Holy Shock [[d:paladin#1453765536218611772]] |
| Healers | Put the add tank and his healers in their own group; one raid leader's layout: Erennius + add tank — 40 yd — 3 healers — 10 yd — healer + ranged — 20 yd — melee + Solnius + main tank ([Atreidon (player), 2025-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130281#p130281)) |
| Watch out | **Call of Nightmare** ignores line of sight ([Torta (staff), 2023-10-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71678#p71678)); players too far away get slept and pulled to him. LoS strategies have been repeatedly patched ([Atreidon (player), 2025-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130281#p130281)) |

### Solnius the Awakener

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| Tank | Two tanks race threat until he transforms; the second stays close in case the MT eats too many breaths in phase 3 ([Atreidon (player), 2025-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130281#p130281)) |
| Healers | **Hard mode: do not dispel or Tremor the normal 60% attack/cast-speed debuff** — removing it applies **Emerald Rot** (80%). Tremor Totem is fine only against the whelp adds' sleep [[d:shaman#1491595081562456084]], [[d:shaman#1491595432965701825]], [[d:shaman#1491708850120822925]]. Tree druids' mana-drain curses may be removed ([Atreidon (player), 2025-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130281#p130281)) |
| DPS | Melee spread at max range behind him (a random melee AoE punishes clumping). **Add phase at 55-60%:** he becomes untargetable and summons random big dragonkin plus Sanctum Dreamers; kill spellcasters first and the **Sanctum Scalebane** (2-2.5x the effective HP of the others) last ([Borefficz (player), 2024-05-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94829#p94829)). Scalebanes cannot cleave melee who stand directly behind at max range, but one player stepping to the side lets them cleave everyone [[d:shaman#1434438168995958844]] |
| Class jobs | Purge his self-buff (keep Detect Magic on him) ([Atreidon (player), 2025-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130281#p130281)). A paladin picks up the whelps in the add phase |
| Watch out | Hunter pets attack him from minimum range ([Lane13 (player), 2026-03-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=170490#p170490)). Hard mode is "manageable" with more healers [[d:mage#1493997047924260985]] |

## Notable loot

- **Claw of Erennius** — a guaranteed healer mace, normal mode only ([Elisleris (player), 2024-07-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=99769#p99769)).
- **Hard-mode neck** — gives spell hit (initially thought to be melee hit) [[d:mage#1487107403198632047]]; for retribution paladins the Choker of the Emerald Lord and Heart of Dreams are keep-forever items, and hard mode is the neck's source [[d:paladin#1479748234544021608]], [[d:paladin#1479092214746321019]].
- Hunters: a hard-mode bow and a head trinket [[d:hunter#1430478630890373250]].
- **Breath of Solnius** — a strong haste healing trinket for Regrowth-spam druids and shamans [[d:druid#1483107829190496400]].
- The only realistic source of **Dreamscale** [[d:mage#1432716939184177152]].
- **Jade Scale of the Dreamer** (Solnius) for the druid Swift Travel Form quest; Solnius is "BWL-esque and needs a small raid" for that step [[d:druid#1336462815904534630]].
- Emerald Drake mount from Solnius ([Torta (staff), 2025-09-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149960#p149960)); Totem of Calm Cascades (its healing-power scaling never shipped) [[d:shaman#1490930386338054265]].

## Common wipes

1. Hard mode: someone dispels the Solnius debuff into Emerald Rot [[d:shaman#1491595081562456084]].
2. An add phase with two or more Scalebanes ([Borefficz (player), 2024-05-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94829#p94829)).
3. Melee clumping for the random AoE ([Atreidon (player), 2025-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=130281#p130281)).
