# Onyxia's Lair

**Summary:** The vanilla Tier 1 dragon fight, 40 players, extended in 1.18.1. The lair has a new section with an optional boss, **Broodcommander Axelus**, and Onyxia's own fight gained a second dragon, **Cindarion**, plus elite adds and Hatchers. With 1.18.1 the fight went from a formality to a real encounter: "onyxia harder than bwl now" [[d:hunter#1484667362329493574]], "a few hundred percent more damage taken" [[d:druid#1486367890675335318]]. Onyxia is **fire-immune**. Fire Resistance gear for her is "a meme" [[d:paladin#1448816634306494514]]. The key jobs are add tanking, killing the adds before Onyxia lands, and threat after she lands.

## Raid-wide preparation

- **Immunities:** Onyxia and all black dragons are fire-immune, so fire weapon procs do nothing [[d:warrior#1439251654095540264]], [[d:warlock#1432406269402026124]]; she is nature-immune per a vanilla-data list [[d:hunter#1407146893892386966]]. Hunter traps do work on Axelus and his adds [[d:hunter#1484656535996207226]].
- **Fire Resistance:** not worth a set for Onyxia [[d:paladin#1448816634306494514]]; some players felt FR gear was "back on the menu" after 1.18.1 [[d:rogue#1484688013253542018]].
- **Fear:** bears have only one long-cooldown fear break, so they rely on priests [[d:druid#1338495727189626902]]. Hunters can Feign Death her fear (not Nefarian's) [[d:hunter#1486905964799066203]].
- **Unusual tanks:** she has been tanked by an Affliction warlock (Siphon Life, no Soul Link) [[d:warlock#1464371788350754926]], [[d:warlock#1465084641219448905]], an Elemental shaman with no pushback talents [[d:warlock#1465085235250200758]] and a hunter pet in a healing-power set [[d:hunter#1494502397945446480]].

## Bosses

### Broodcommander Axelus (optional, new section)

Kill report from the first guild to kill him on Ambershire [[d:paladin#1484605320193114394]], [[d:paladin#1484604429465423993]]:

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| Trash | Mobs before him give melee a debuff that makes every attack deal AoE fire to nearby players; **paladins and priests dispel it** |
| Tank | He keeps summoning **Warden** adds with large-radius AoE fire; kill them in phase 1 — in phase 2 they come almost nonstop |
| Healers | He **chains two players** (mostly ranged and healers) — damage plus silence. The chains seemed to break more often when the pair moved closer to the boss and his cleave |
| DPS | He "dies" once, resets his health and starts a harder second phase with more adds; focus his summoned adds or he goes "from 0% HP to 100% HP in no time" [[d:rogue#1485398433727647814]] |
| Watch out | At launch one raid of 25 Kara40-geared players wiped twice on the trash and killed it on the third pull [[d:paladin#1485271205194895381]], [[d:paladin#1485271489727959181]]; the trash respawns every time you wipe [[d:paladin#1485271319791534292]]. Only organized guilds were killing him in the first week [[d:paladin#1484606108915073144]]. Aimed Shot's piercing crit can glue an add to a hunter [[d:hunter#1484990820791877804]]. Pets fall below the ground in the tunnel after him ([Lane13 (player), 2026-03-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171787#p171787)) |

### Onyxia

The fight now adds elite adds (Onyxia's health was reduced to compensate) and a second dragon, **Cindarion**. Cindarion flies and throws fireballs on the raid in phase 1, lands and breathes when Onyxia takes off (giving melee a target), and phase 3 adds Hatchers that spawn more whelps [[d:druid#1486363045000122408]], [[d:druid#1486367175957549138]], [[d:druid#1486367299878523031]], [[d:paladin#1484606398749737111]].

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| Tank | **Three tanks:** one on Onyxia, one on the two adds, one keeping the small drake away from the raid while DPS kill it; two tanks work if the add tank is beefy and knows the job [[d:paladin#1485274028657737799]], [[d:paladin#1485274080083968092]], [[d:paladin#1485274145423097856]]. Order: kill the drake first, then move the two adds away while the raid burns Onyxia [[d:paladin#1485271620846227526]]. **Threat:** she drops threat on the top players while flying and lands with the tank's threat effectively reset, while ranged keep theirs; DPS must hold after she lands so the tank can reposition [[d:paladin#1440155885107413032]], [[d:paladin#1440156076216680459]]. Classic phase plan: pull her to the back and build threat; in phase 2 nobody can hold her, so ranged back off; in phase 3 she fears on a timer — use a stance swap or Fear Ward [[d:warrior#1408046028690821161]] |
| Healers | Much more raid damage than before [[d:druid#1486367890675335318]]. A shaman MT benefits from paladins' Judgement of Wisdom [[d:paladin#1485794172735852606]] |
| DPS | **Melee must kill the drake, whelps and breeder before ranged force Onyxia to land**, or it usually wipes [[d:rogue#1485397424171253760]]. Onyxian Hatchers are a DPS check ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139)) |
| Class jobs | Trinkets: ToEP on the pull, the ZG proc trinket again for phase 3 [[d:paladin#1457410735104856199]] |
| Watch out | If fear sends the raid under her, she breathes into it [[d:paladin#1485271707722842312]] |

## Notable loot

- **Axelus:** Idol of Equilibrium (druid) ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608)); paladin librams **Hallowed Ground** (+10% spell hit, +20% Holy Shock damage) and a Seal of Command libram [[d:paladin#1484655722074607756]], [[d:paladin#1488558737147957258]]; a tank shield close to the Elementium shield with a fire-reflect proc [[d:paladin#1485798984277692416]]; an 80 spell power caster staff [[d:warlock#1488194472461336576]]; a polearm liked by ferals [[d:druid#1486345514361557063]]; Totem of Thundercall [[d:shaman#1489329800480034970]].
- **Onyxia:** the neck is easy to get [[d:rogue#1463675504577085654]]; the bag (now 20 slots), the Onyxian Drake mount and possibly the T2 helm [[d:rogue#1477347511990485194]] ([Torta (staff), 2025-09-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=149960#p149960)); Sapphiron's Drape gained +5% Fortune [[d:paladin#1474262471752679518]], [[d:rogue#1477348145816932473]]; a new sword with weapon skill [[d:warrior#1486286668351668326]]. One guild never saw Deathbringer drop [[d:rogue#1463880744752644240]]. The hunter quest's Eye of Onyxia drops ~40% of the time [[d:hunter#1440378631661817966]].
- The Onyxia Scale Cloak is needed for BWL (Firemaw, Nefarian) [[d:paladin#1451276466301177976]].

## Common wipes

1. Ranged force Onyxia down before the adds and drake are dead [[d:rogue#1485397424171253760]].
2. DPS pulling threat right after she lands [[d:paladin#1440155885107413032]].
3. Axelus resetting because his adds were ignored [[d:rogue#1485398433727647814]].
