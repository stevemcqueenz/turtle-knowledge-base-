# Temple of Ahn'Qiraj (AQ40)

**Summary:** The vanilla Tier 2.5 raid, 40 players, with no new Turtle bosses. Veterans rate it easy ("aq40 is really really easy, naxx is easy but kinda long") [[d:warrior#1459702879584780349]], and with gear it takes about an hour [[d:shaman#1473202510201360455]]. Many rate its bosses the best designed in the base game [[d:shaman#1473202618397622453]]. The special jobs are Shadow Resistance tanks for Twin Emperors, frost damage for Viscidus, Nature Resistance only for Viscidus (and optionally Huhuran), and threat control. Twin Emperors are immune to all rogue poisons, and Viscidus to all but frost.

## Raid-wide preparation

- **Poisons:** everything takes only Instant/Deadly, except Twin Emperors (immune to every poison — rogues "feel useless") and Viscidus (use Frost Oil) [[d:rogue#1484756192734478396]], [[d:rogue#1486680147846365315]].
- **Nature:** Viscidus is immune to everything but frost; Huhuran, the Twins and C'Thun have high Nature resistance [[d:hunter#1407146893892386966]]. Boomkins only struggle on Ouro [[d:druid#1457345075204980787]]. BM hunters: Wind Serpent for most bosses, Raptor mid-raid, Scorpid for Ouro and C'Thun [[d:hunter#1485818550646997024]], [[d:hunter#1485818622780637266]]; give pets Nature resistance [[d:hunter#1485902382322683904]].
- **Nature Resistance gear** matters only on Viscidus (~180-200 NR plus a Greater Nature Protection Potion); Huhuran is handled with the Peasant Caller trinket [[d:rogue#1468952515977674978]]. Off-tanks who DPS on Huhuran/Viscidus want 150+ NR [[d:druid#1492107030792503326]]. The ~200 NR melee "requirement" is soft; two Greater Nature Protection Potions can replace it [[d:druid#1396794024718766162]]. Tanks generally prefer threat and stamina over NR [[d:druid#1433813167682748427]]; paladin tanks can use Divine Shield instead [[d:paladin#1432831979186950266]]. The Nightmare Dragon world bosses give the best NR gear [[d:shaman#1492069792633327699]].
- **Sappers:** best used on Viscidus and the scarab trash after the first boss [[d:rogue#1450509177322733661]], [[d:rogue#1450509294704263237]].
- **Healers:** Twin Emperors is the first fight where healer mana really matters [[d:druid#1337778703463088139]].

## Trash

- The **bug tunnel** gauntlet is the Force Reactive Disk's main raid use (avoids daze) [[d:warrior#1475615908603887847]]; shaman tanks still use Totem of Tides there [[d:shaman#1467922585361846448]].
- Kill the worm trash near the early worm boss by switching targets [[d:rogue#1312541242441076866]].
- Large **Anubisaths** with Shadow Flame: MM hunters stand exactly on the deadzone line to dodge the "move into the mob" mechanic while still shooting [[d:hunter#1491059182638727278]]. A two-dog pull used a taunt chain between two warriors, though it was inconsistent [[d:warrior#1437512858312245291]], [[d:warrior#1437513019008483431]].
- The trash after Twin Emperors and the constant knockbacks are the main annoyances [[d:warrior#1459369376359780598]]. Clearing optional Mindslayer packs for reputation and Shard of the Fallen Star is a slog [[d:shaman#1473203189536002283]].

## Bosses

### The Prophet Skeram

Mind Control plus teleports and big character models make target swapping confusing — "if they removed the mind control from him it would be 10 times better" [[d:shaman#1473203024657780736]], [[d:shaman#1473203189536002283]]. A shaman can be the third tank [[d:shaman#1429612004775170120]].

### Bug Trio (Silithid Royalty)

Lots of add wrangling for tanks [[d:warrior#1431446441515286559]]. Some room for AoE [[d:shaman#1450906775879421962]]. A shaman can third-tank it [[d:shaman#1429612004775170120]].

### Princess Huhuran

- Her enrage phase (~30%) sends darts at the closest ~15 targets; the raid uses the **Peasant Caller** NR trinket (a PvP drop farmable at Chillwind Camp) and bursts her through the enrage [[d:warrior#1427058635963367546]], [[d:shaman#1427796907266150500]], [[d:shaman#1427798318276280321]].
- Shamans' Spirit Link helps the tank in the last 30% [[d:shaman#1443098135030136852]], [[d:shaman#1443098263833284640]].
- Huhuran's Stinger is a key Combat rogue item [[d:rogue#1464217579429298351]].

### Ouro

Tanks chase threat and reposition on their turn [[d:warrior#1431446574336311477]]. Greater demons get one-shot, probably by Sandblast or by out-threatening tanks [[d:warlock#1440505939663917116]], [[d:warlock#1440506286553829458]]. Traps often fail on his large hitbox [[d:hunter#1315383703429578752]]. A shaman can third-tank him [[d:shaman#1429612004775170120]].

## Notable loot

- Twin Emperors: an off-hand and neck that are top Arcane mage picks [[d:mage#1420103555297054911]].
- AQ40 gear plus Darkmantle is projected to push Combat rogues past Assassination [[d:rogue#1464215858908762113]]; the gain over BWL is smaller than BWL's over pre-raid [[d:rogue#1490344391515312291]].
