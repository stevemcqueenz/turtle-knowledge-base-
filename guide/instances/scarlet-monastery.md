# Scarlet Monastery

Vanilla four-wing dungeon in Tirisfal Glades. Players run it at about 26 (Graveyard), 29 (Library), 32 (Armory) and 35 (Cathedral) [[d:rogue#1482489440302661712]]. In 1.17.2 Turtle added an optional new boss to three wings ([Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709)). Casters can do Graveyard and Library in one trip at about 33 [[d:mage#1492132178593710091]].

## Cathedral: big pulls are dangerous

- **Scarlet Champions' Holy Strike** hits for 460-500+ holy damage, which ignores armor. It is what kills players trying to pull the whole Cathedral onto Mograine [[d:paladin#1443006788311056487]], [[d:paladin#1443008096246173769]].
- Turtle added anti-farm mechanics to typical farm dungeons "like zf and cath" [[d:paladin#1426765897082867922]]. Do not expect vanilla mass-pull farms to work. One player guesses the full Cathedral room pull is soloable with T3 + vampirism, and "easily doable with FRD" (untested) [[d:paladin#1425569868249563249]].
