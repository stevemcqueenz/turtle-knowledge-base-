# Lower Karazhan Halls ("Kara10", "K10")

**Summary:** A custom 10-player raid in the lower floors of Karazhan, Deadwind Pass, aimed at Molten Core difficulty. Its layout follows TBC's lower Karazhan up to the Opera, with all-custom bosses [[d:druid#1491823898751270933]]. It is entry-level (it ranks with ZG and AQ20 as the easiest raids [[d:druid#1491822859398418473]]) and tankable in pre-raid BiS [[d:warrior#1463944367894036656]]. The one boss that checks you is **Clawlord Howlfang**, who needs a tank swap and cannot be out-geared in pre-raid gear. Beyond that, bring a **decurser** and a **poison cleanser** (a shaman totem trivializes the spider boss). Kara10 drops loot above its difficulty, including the Scythe of Elune legendary starter, and starts the Tower of Karazhan attunement.

## Boss order

Four bosses — **Brood Queen Araxxna** (spider), **Grizikil** (imps), **Clawlord Howlfang** (worgen) and **Lord Blackwald II** (after the Dark Rider Champion) — can be done in any order; **Moroes** on the opera stage is last; **Master Blacksmith Rolfen** is optional ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087)).

## Raid-wide preparation

- **Decurse** the tanks: a missing mage or druid decurser means heavy damage from stacking curses [[d:warrior#1463944675101774018]], [[d:paladin#1448462016845971601]].
- **Poison:** every group wants a shaman for the spider basement [[d:shaman#1429385869264945223]].
- Only Howlfang, the Ghost Knights and Moroes hit hard [[d:warrior#1463944462890700976]]. A shaman makes a good off-tank here thanks to Chain Lightning-pull AoE threat [[d:shaman#1311623214434357341]].

## Bosses

### Clawlord Howlfang (worgen) — the hard one

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| Tank | Everyone in front of him gains a stack of a debuff every second; it strips armor (up to 100%), so for a bear only health and dodge are left, while warriors still parry and block [[d:shaman#1474926708506235000]], [[d:druid#1441006493179838486]], [[d:druid#1441008134192627785]]. **Two tanks swap** — at ~15 stacks per one paladin, at 20 per another; the off-tank waits out of range; Divine Shield sheds your own stacks [[d:paladin#1441935833811914812]], [[d:paladin#1441935992243093716]], [[d:paladin#1448985069954793512]]. Bears can let a DPS soak the early stacks in a corner and take over around 10 [[d:druid#1441009126829457408]]. Solo tanking is possible with plenty of HP and avoidance, strong DPS and alert healers; a warlock solo-tanked him repeatedly [[d:druid#1441011969254621265]], [[d:shaman#1474931984647327744]], [[d:warlock#1400226811492831315]]. He enrages around 50%; swap tanks near 15% in an average group [[d:shaman#1474931509831139480]], [[d:shaman#1474926992658010337]]. Defense rating is a poor readiness measure here [[d:shaman#1474925478329716828]] |
| Healers | Decurse **Shadowbane Curse** on the tanks ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2025-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160173#p160173)). Wipes are usually team error such as missed decurses [[d:paladin#1448462318710296626]] |
| Watch out | He cannot be stat-checked in pre-raid gear [[d:warrior#1463944367894036656]]. Players reported him resisting most taunts in December 2025 ([Turney (player), 2025-12-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=162969#p162969)) |

### Moroes (final boss)

- He stuns the tank and drops threat repeatedly; the other tank must taunt through his disorient, which makes him a good fight for an extra plate off-tank [[d:paladin#1441936643534753876]], [[d:paladin#1453195049016758433]]. Dispel the tanks so they can taunt; DPS stay below both tanks on threat ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087)).
- His AoE silence hits random players; healers stand on opposite edges so both are never silenced ([Atreidon (player), 2025-11-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160165#p160165)).
- The Ghost Servants before him cast an AoE silence; ghosts and skeletons are Exorcism-able [[d:paladin#1441935833811914812]], [[d:paladin#1441936209286009013]].
- "Overall not a hard raid, if you get that dog boss right" [[d:paladin#1441936461522931825]].

### Brood Queen Araxxna (spider)

Cleanse her poison (Abolish Poison over paladin Cleanse); one shaman totem makes it easy, two trivialize it ([Akos1896 (player), 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160089#p160089)). Kill the egg adds at once; they are immune to CC ([Reynolds (staff), 2025-02-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118948#p118948)).

### Grizikil (imps)

Clear every imp in the corridor and side rooms first; run out of **Flamewave** and **Grellkin Rain of Fire** ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087)). One tank calls the third boss the one that "trucks" — pre-pot armor [[d:shaman#1442300283605618791]]. He drops *Guide: Aspect of the Wolf VII* [[d:hunter#1302436867672637460]].

### Lord Blackwald II

Spawns only after the Dark Rider Champion dies ([Torta (staff), 2023-10-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71678#p71678)); summons 2-3 adds (CC or off-tank); decurse the tanks ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087)). Starts *Scythe of the Goddess*. His summoned adds can despawn with loot on them within ~2 minutes [[d:paladin#1481722243578073250]].

## Trash

- Kill order and spell IDs for every trash type are in the community guide ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2025-11-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=160087#p160087)); wipe spots are the spider basement, the ballroom stairs, the imp rooms, the opera entrance and the narrow Servant ramp ([Elesion (player), 2025-07-26](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141604#p141604)).
- Warlock imps refuse to cast without line of sight, for example when the tank fights werewolves in doorways [[d:warlock#1465198561955418367]].
- Hunters can solo-farm the dancing room, dining room, upstairs and kitchen packs for reputation and blue drops; harder than the ZG farm [[d:hunter#1470816977194057770]], [[d:hunter#1470817429386035345]].

## Notable loot

- **Scythe of Elune** (very rare legendary-trinket starter), a strong caster trinket, and boots tied to a key quest [[d:mage#1440017964748247130]]; a trash mob drops the Theramore portal tome [[d:mage#1430206372452241549]].
- The final boss's two-handed mace beats every MC weapon except Sulfuras for enhancement shamans; the **Fist of the Forgotten Order** (Moroes) is a prized Crusader-like fist [[d:shaman#1436890845029859409]], [[d:shaman#1429385869264945223]].
- **Anasterian's Legacy** (Moroes, +4 sword skill off-hand) [[d:warrior#1403958886964134009]]; **Breastplate of Earthen Might**, a long-lasting chest [[d:warrior#1441682252789715024]].
- Loot is roughly MC/ZG level, with one standout per role; for AP retribution only the imp boss's head and a fist weapon [[d:paladin#1434624768916717790]], [[d:paladin#1433305114935951402]].
- Overcharged Ley Energy for T3.5 turn-ins: full-clearing Kara10 beats soloing the entrance mobs; even Moroes drops it only ~70% of the time [[d:rogue#1458185071881359382]], [[d:rogue#1458185621452624106]].

## Quests
