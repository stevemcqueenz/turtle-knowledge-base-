# Blackfathom Deeps

Vanilla level ~20-30 dungeon off the Ashenvale coast. In 1.18.0 Turtle added the **Temple of Elune**, with the satyr **Velthelaxx the Defiler** (lvl 31) ([Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615)). His wiki page is a stub with no abilities or loot ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Velthelaxx_the_Defiler)).

## Leveling value

- **Healers and casters:** the **Gravestone Scepter** from a BFD quest is a guaranteed wand that is stronger than the Deadmines drop and carries you to about 30 [[d:priest#1445621187551035392]], [[d:priest#1436055370169712821]].

## Gaps

- Velthelaxx has no documented abilities, loot or tactics.
