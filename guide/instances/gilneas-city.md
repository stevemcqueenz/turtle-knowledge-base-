# Gilneas City

Turtle-custom 5-man for levels 43-49 in western Gilneas. Official difficulty is "Zul'Farrak". It is tougher than its level suggests: "going to gilneas at any level before 42 is suicide" [[d:shaman#1434690962957009018]]. It rewards a good 1-hour run with the Greymane plate set, the Greymane Charger mount and the Theramore teleport quest item.

## Group notes

- **Tanks: wear a shield.** "In Gilneas the elites and knights at the end of the instance will chunk you if you have 2-3 on you at once. Putting on a shield will make the healer a lot happier" [[d:warrior#1346976477048082472]]. Another tank preferred being Prot-specced with a shield there [[d:warrior#1356429453097570305]].
- **Bring CC.** A level-49 deep-Prot tank in full Steel Plate found LFG runs "taxing on the healers" when the group had no CC. The poster also noticed it got tougher after the Scars patch [[d:warrior#1409942351346008074]].
- **Stealthed worgs** in the zone make stealth detection (Catseye Elixir, Catseye Ultra Goggles, Human Perception) unusually useful [[d:warrior#1363173187730276473]]. One player calls parts "overtuned" and says players frequently lose (hardcore) characters there [[d:warrior#1363161409373995239]].
- **Paladin tanks shine:** Consecration AoE threat outpaces a cat or other non-AoE tanks here [[d:druid#1379088170674688020]]. "Gilneas city is indeed a leveling paladin's wet dream" for the Greymane set [[d:paladin#1347154329907367936]].
