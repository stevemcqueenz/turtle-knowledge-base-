# Sunken Temple (Temple of Atal'Hakkar)

Vanilla level ~50-56 dungeon in the Swamp of Sorrows. On Turtle it is mostly run for the **Avatar of Hakkar**, whose drops were buffed, and as a healer checkpoint while leveling.

## Group notes

- **Resist-heavy.** "Everything resists earth shock in the bottom floor"; shamans call it "emerald sanctum before emerald sanctum was a thing" [[d:shaman#1435767846071308320]], [[d:shaman#1435767885606682824]].
- **Healers:** from Zul'Farrak on, damage ramps up and healers want Resto talents [[d:shaman#1437274154872344696]]. A Shadow-specced priest can still heal every dungeon up to and including Sunken Temple if they stack Spirit gear [[d:priest#1350125463573495819]] (single source).
- **The big green dragonkin packs** are the dangerous part of the run [[d:paladin#1456800479140118590]].
- **Shade of Eranikus:** his sleep cannot be removed by Divine Shield, and the Emerald Sanctum "immune to sleep" shield does not stop it. Blessing of Sacrifice no longer breaks CC [[d:paladin#1484538138247565342]], [[d:paladin#1484538195977961532]].

## Avatar of Hakkar

- **Event:** only killing the "sons of Hakkar" during the event can give a 5th blood. Using the trigger item a second time can bug the next lockout's summon [[d:paladin#1470106694947307703]], [[d:paladin#1470106768620261562]].
- **Egg of Hakkar trick:** pick up the Egg of Hakkar chain (troll witch doctor, Steamwheedle Port). As long as you never turn it in, you can summon the Avatar on every run [[d:mage#1306041340173353011]] (2024-11).
- **Loot:** **Embrace of the Wind Serpent**, buffed on Turtle and "actually kind of good for arcane" (-400 armor, heals 300 over 10 s while active) [[d:mage#1458262042732527780]]. Vestments of the Atal'ai Prophet, a high-Spirit mage chest [[d:mage#1306041340173353011]].

### Solo farming the Avatar

- **Paladin:** go straight to the Avatar. About 10-15 min per run, because the add spawns cannot be sped up [[d:paladin#1428991221858046032]], [[d:paladin#1429001924329472051]]. It works as deep Prot even with 0% vampirism [[d:paladin#1456800712355872994]].
- **Mage:** kite him as Frost, since he is not slow-immune, or duo-box it [[d:mage#1458540137913782433]]. The old jump-back-and-forth safe spot does not work; Cone of Cold kiting does [[d:mage#1489776668569501786]] (the thread mixes ZF and ST).
- **Warlock:** "should be plenty doable as demo, just be mindful and keep your pet alive" [[d:warlock#1460856844548313369]] (untested).
