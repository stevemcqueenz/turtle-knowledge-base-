# World bosses and open-world raid encounters

**Summary:** Turtle keeps the vanilla world bosses (Azuregos, Kazzak, the four Nightmare Dragons) and adds its own, such as Dark Reaver of Karazhan, Nerubian Overseer, Ostarius, Concavius, Cla'ckora and Father Lycan. Since 1.18.1 most can be **summoned** with artifacts from raids plus materials, and you can loot each boss **once per week per character**. The Discord sources contain very few tactics for them. This page collects the resistances, loot and the few fight notes that exist, plus the open-world raid-strength quest bosses (the White Stag, Bovar'kez).

## Summoning and loot rules

- 1.18.1: "Powerful artifacts hidden within raid dungeons, combined with materials found throughout the world, allow summoning a specific World Boss" ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)).
- "Everyone can summon and kill world bosses multiple times per week, but you are eligible for loot on the first kill of a world boss every week on that character" ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-12-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111212#p111212)).
- Summoned corpses despawn after 30 minutes; summoned bosses no longer have proximity aggro ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139), [Torta (staff), 2026-04-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176101#p176101)).
- Known summon sources from players: an item from Majordomo (MC) summoned a boss in the Blasted Lands; the Nerubian Overseer summon drops in Naxx; Kara10 drops the Dark Reaver summon [[d:paladin#1485274682927218860]], [[d:paladin#1485274747708113140]], [[d:paladin#1485275355404304557]]. Once summonable, Nightmare Dragon loot became easy to get [[d:shaman#1492069882307547166]].

## Open-world raid-strength quest bosses

### The White Stag (druid Swift Travel Form chain)

- ~150k HP, ~3,000 unmitigated melee hits; a Shadow Bolt Volley (1,000-1,500) that reaches beyond hunter/mage max range; a melee knockback (you can be knocked off the cliff); a point-blank AoE fear; one ability about every 3 s, no repeats until all three fire [[d:druid#1308406716991541249]].
- **Tactic:** tank plus a shaman with Tremor Totem; the highest DPS stands at max range so the stag ping-pongs between tank and that DPS without reaching the squishy ranged; 1-2 druids root-spam; kite it back to the start after each move [[d:druid#1308406108196438139]]. Drag him uphill away from the cliff faces [[d:druid#1474033564722003968]].
- **Group size:** 8-12 [[d:druid#1479521428821905470]]; about 10 for a fair fight, 1-2 players can cheese it over a long time [[d:druid#1479525332959629506]].

### Bovar'kez (Tower of Karazhan scepter chain, Winterspring)

Needs roughly 20-30 people, a decurser for a Cripple debuff (brutal with Rain of Fire), and a tank swap for a stacking debuff [[d:warrior#1429941194359832607]], [[d:warrior#1429941234121572354]], [[d:warrior#1429941734560890890]], [[d:warrior#1429941780769669273]].

## Gaps

- No tactics in the sources for Dark Reaver, Nerubian Overseer, Ostarius, Concavius, Cla'ckora, Father Lycan, Kazzak or the Nightmare Dragons beyond resistances and loot.
- Which raid drops which summoning artifact is only partly known.
