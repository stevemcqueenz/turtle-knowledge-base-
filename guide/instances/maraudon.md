# Maraudon

Vanilla level ~40-52 dungeon in Desolace. On Turtle it matters mostly as a **level-60 solo farm**. Princess Theradras drops the **Blade of Eternal Darkness** (BoED), a ~1% epic dagger with a mana-refund proc [[d:shaman#1489289875076415550]], [[d:priest#1470920206409924657]]. Maraudon lasher packs are a hunter gold route.

## Group notes

- **Nature-immune mobs** make it awkward for nature-damage tanks such as Enhancement shamans. There is a workaround [[d:shaman#1453209759099392022]].
- **Loot for casters:** the off-hand + dagger combo from the Purple side gives +21 Int and +10 Spirit/Stamina, and is worth holding until 60 [[d:priest#1430936443072675951]].

## Princess Theradras

| | |
|---|---|
| **Turtle differences** | She is **not immune to Nature** here. She "farts" (an AoE dust cloud) even when you are not in melee range, and she throws boulders [[d:druid#1425149570144272434]], [[d:druid#1425152504869687316]]. |
| **Melee / tank** | Melee or DoT her, move out when she starts farting, heal up, then continue [[d:druid#1425152504869687316]]. **Fight her next to the pillar.** If she lands a fear or too many farts, run around the pillar to break line of sight and bandage; you can repeat this indefinitely [[d:rogue#1428696864261144666]]. |
| **Casters** | Do not be casting when she throws a boulder. If you get interrupted you cannot Blink away, and she globals you [[d:mage#1461058467056844993]], [[d:mage#1461049951151980628]]. |
| **Consumables** | Nature Resistance/Protection potion; health potion [[d:rogue#1428661874920128522]], [[d:rogue#1459979036938862602]]. |
| **Reset spot** | The stone right at Princess resets trash you pulled on the way [[d:priest#1475667095776919632]]. |

### Solo methods (level 60)

- **Rogue, melee:** Spellstrike gear plus an **Elemental Slayer** weapon enchant (she is an elemental); "you can do it half naked" [[d:rogue#1427742054963871869]]. Get the Spellstrike gear before farming BoED [[d:rogue#1468494387603312641]]. Use vampirism gear, a Nature Resistance potion and Flourish → Evasion → Flourish, and face-tank her [[d:rogue#1428661874920128522]].
- **Rogue, ranged kite:** Throwing Weapon Specialization + Corrosive Poison via Deadly Throw while running in a circle. There is a dead zone where she stops throwing boulders. Surefooted (+8% run speed) lets you avoid all damage [[d:rogue#1400917933013733386]], [[d:rogue#1411693423848587397]].
- **Druid:** "At 60? Easy." Feral Swiftness works in her room [[d:druid#1425152504869687316]].
- **Priest:** soloable in Shadow. About 5 min per clear in Naxx gear [[d:priest#1470920206409924657]]. **Mage:** soloable as deep Arcane [[d:mage#1461047226603274458]].

### Farming BoED

- The drop is about 1%. Reports range from about 20 runs to 96 and 120+ runs [[d:rogue#1428108576592756796]], [[d:rogue#1459979036938862602]], [[d:priest#1470920206409924657]].
- **Fast reset** (needs a second account): keep an alt as party leader. Kill Princess, log out the main, reset instances on the alt, then log the main back in; it appears at the entrance with a fresh lockout [[d:rogue#1443339738831519824]], [[d:rogue#1443343850650206310]]. Kill the goblin, Princess and the crocolisk each run for about 10 g [[d:rogue#1443343850650206310]]. Hourly instance caps still apply: about 5 runs per hour, and the dungeon cap is separate from the raid cap ([Torta (staff), 2025-04-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=128053#p128053)).

## Hunter lasher and hydra route (level 60)

1. Enter with the Maraudon staff. Head toward Princess, but take the left bridge over the crocolisk-boss pond to the second-to-last room.
2. Use Swiftness Zanza, or alternate Cheetah and Monkey aspects (do not get hit in Cheetah).
3. Tag the lasher packs with rank-1 Serpent Sting on the way back along the same route. Go around the rock elementals; the lashers aggro them for you.
4. Kite everything to the rock formations in the hydra/waterfall room until stacked, then kill it with Explosive Trap [[d:hunter#1436512270237565088]], [[d:hunter#1436513058779300031]].

Rogue gold-rate estimate for Maraudon: 60-100 g/h (2025-08) [[d:rogue#1401936418741424148]].
