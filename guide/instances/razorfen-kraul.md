# Razorfen Kraul

Vanilla level ~25-35 quilboar dungeon in the southern Barrens. In 1.18.0 Turtle added **The Brambled Grotto**, with a new boss, **Rotthorn** ([Torta (staff), 2025-02-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120615#p120615)). Scripting fixes to various mobs followed in 2025-07 ([Torta (staff), 2025-07-22](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=140939#p140939)).

## Rotthorn (lvl 32, bog beast)

- No abilities are listed. Drops Bramblethorn Girdle, Thornlash Branch, Seed of Writhing Growth, **Idol of the Thorned Grove** (druid), and the *Tainted Brambleheart* quest item ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Rotthorn)).

## Route

The page has no boss order. This is the one our clears run (measured):

**Death Speaker Jargba → Aggem Thorncurse → Roogug → Overlord Ramtusk** (his adds first) **→ Charlga Razorflank**
(final).

- **Earthcaller Halmgar** (a rare) is taken when he spawns — our runs see him sometimes and skip him otherwise.
- **Rotthorn** (Turtle, the Brambled Grotto) is **optional in our route** and our clears skip it — a group that
  wants the Idol of the Thorned Grove has to detour into the grotto.
- **Agathelos the Raging** and the **Blind Hunter** are the plan's last two optional stops; our clears skip both.

[measured on bot runs, private 1.18.1-based realm — verified against our plan files, not a run log]

## Packs and patrols worth a warning

- **The quilboar village packs in front of Charlga** are 3-4 mobs with patrols; pull them **back to the huts** and
  never fight in the open — the village is where a run unravels. [measured]
- **The Jargba hall** has walkers crossing the room; clear them first or the boss pull doubles. [measured]
- **The kraul bat packs near the entrance** can chain-pull if the group fights in the open — pull them back into the
  corridor. [measured]
- **Keep the Ramtusk fight out of the boar pens** — the pens' mobs join the fight if the pull drifts into them.
  [measured]
- The **boar pens** and the **bat cave** are on the way; they are the two spots where the route and the trash
  overlap, so pull short rather than deep. [measured]
