# Timbermaw Hold ("TMH", "TMH20")

**Summary:** Turtle's newest raid, 20 players, added in 1.18.1 (Nightmares of Ursol) beneath Mount Hyjal with its entrance in Azshara. Staff designed it "to be harder than the existing 20 man raids" ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). At launch it was **deliberately overtuned**. It dropped sub-Naxx loot but K40-geared guilds needed their gear to clear it, and players expected a nerf to about BWL difficulty [[d:paladin#1487276247112355860]], [[d:warrior#1487850434818211941]], [[d:druid#1491823153943543829]]. The lead designer called it "probably deceptively hard" [[d:paladin#1481628373699526830]]. You cannot pug it from world chat; "it's not about gear it's about cohesion and team synergy" [[d:warrior#1487850640259551474]], [[d:warrior#1487850765232898099]]. Many mobs resist or are immune to **Nature** [[d:shaman#1487278411062837390]], [[d:shaman#1487372824397086790]].

## Bosses

Named encounter NPCs in staff notes: Karrsh the Sentinel, Rotgrowl, Kodiak, Loktanag the Vile, Ormanos the Cracked, Selenaxx Foulheart, Trioch the Devourer, Archdruid Kronn, Ursol, and Peroth'arn (secret) ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490)). Players use nicknames, and the order is not documented. One officer's difficulty ranking, hardest first: "druid > Kronn > elemental > big guy > satyr > chief > hydra > double bear" [[d:paladin#1487557780578762893]]. The first-clear account from one guild [[d:paladin#1487276208281620500]]: first boss one-shot; satyr boss a threat race when ~40 imps spawn; Archdruid complicated (killed on the 3rd try); Chieftain one-shot but 2 hours lost because adds did not respawn after a reset; hydra easy; nature boss hard; hunter boss easy apart from a one-shot mechanic; final boss difficult, with an RP-gated add/mind-control mechanic.

## Trash

Trash hits very hard for its HP ("4k crits on a 5.5k armor target"); an undispellable **spreading disease** jumps between players standing close (punishing for pugs); poison clouds hit a bit too hard; rooms are cramped [[d:paladin#1488146220974342215]], [[d:paladin#1488147198825725963]], [[d:paladin#1488147233407897883]]. Travel between bosses is long — one group needed ~30 minutes to reach each boss, so cooldowns are up for every pull [[d:warrior#1489297377356415197]]. Timbermaw Defenders gained armor 2026-04-21 ([Torta (staff), 2026-04-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176101#p176101)).

## Quests: the douse chain

From an NPC in the bear cave above the raid entrance, at Timbermaw **honored**: (1) kill the first boss; (2) kill the shamans in the raid for totems; (3) kill three outdoor elites (Un'Goro, Feralas, Alcaz Island; 120-220k HP, ~30 min respawn); (4) take a pouch from behind the Archdruid and a flower from behind the nature boss [[d:paladin#1487335242183344168]], [[d:paladin#1487394860108353687]]. The quests are worth ~3-4k reputation plus ~3k from clearing [[d:paladin#1487578956868616232]]. A harder "Eternal Douse" (exalted, 7-day lockout) follows the final boss (single source in the reader notes).

## Notable loot

- Timbermaw tier sets in light and heavy variants; the leather set has a Stag (DPS) and Deer (healing) version [[d:druid#1485832429674434723]]; several set bonuses were fixed 2026-03-31 ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490)).
- Trioch and Loktanag drop sought-after rings/trinkets [[d:rogue#1489985845883572426]]; a blue neck gives +5% Fortune [[d:druid#1488971515633598674]]; a new shaman totem [[d:shaman#1487189315938291814]].
- Corrupted Hide of Ursol drops for one player only ([Torta (staff), 2026-04-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=176101#p176101)).
- Loot is sub-Naxx ("T2.1 equivalent" per a critic: [Travoltino (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173564#p173564)).
