# The Deadmines

Vanilla level ~17-26 Westfall dungeon. Turtle added an optional Zanzil laboratory in the mine and a Harvest Golem Foundry, each with a new boss (1.17.2), and upgraded Rhahk'zor's loot ([Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709)). All the new content is optional.

## Route

The page covers the two Turtle bosses and the loot but has **no route**. This is the one our clears run (measured);
the numbers are the map markers.

| Boss | # | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Jared Voss** | 1 | optional, the Zanzil laboratory in the mine — the page's new first boss |
| **Rhahk'Zor** | 2 | the first required boss |
| **Miner Johnson** | 3 | optional, on the way |
| **Sneed's Shredder** | 4 | required; the shredder is the fight |
| **Sneed** | 5 | required; he leaves the shredder when it dies |
| **Gilnid** | 6 | required |
| **Masterpiece Harvester** | 7 | optional, the Harvest Golem Foundry |
| **Mr. Smite** | 8 | required; behind the Defias Cannon (the gunpowder gate below) |
| **Captain Greenskin** | 9 | required |
| **Cookie** | 10 | optional, the ship's cook |
| **Edwin VanCleef** (final) | 11 | required |

Our clears take the required bosses and skip the optionals; a group that wants Jared Voss, the Harvester or Cookie
adds them where the route passes. [measured on bot runs, private 1.18.1-based realm — verified against our plan
files, not a run log]

**The Defias Cannon is the gate to the ship**: it needs the **Defias Gunpowder**, which is a **chest/object in the
mine**, not a mob drop (our realm's data has no creature carrying it). Without it a group can clear the mine but not
reach Smite, Greenskin and VanCleef. [measured on bot runs, private 1.18.1-based realm — verified against our plan files, not a run log]

Jared Voss's kit is all poison, so a poison cleanse helps. That follows from the ability list; the sources give no tactics.

## Packs and patrols worth a warning

- **The mine's goblin packs** are the first real fights; pull them short — the mine's rooms are close together, and
  a body-pull brings the next room. [measured]
- **The ship's deck** is the run's last third: Smite, Greenskin and VanCleef are all on it, and the deck's packs are
  what join a boss fight if the group fights where the ramp meets the deck. [measured]
- **Mr. Smite** switches weapons mid-fight (the switch, not his damage, is the fight's cost) — treat it as the
  moment to use cooldowns. [classic]
- **VanCleef** is a normal final with adds; the fight's cost is the pull before it, not the boss. [measured]

## Leveling value

- **Healers:** you can enter at level 10. A fully enchanted level-10 twink can heal it [[d:druid#1459162799421784136]].
- **Casters:** "Go deadmines until you get the staff the wand and the ring" [[d:mage#1430913027577745540]]. Cookie's wand (Cookie's Stirring Rod) is a random boss drop [[d:priest#1445621187551035392]].
- **Melee:** Smite's Mighty Hammer at 18 for feral druids [[d:druid#1484464772115206204]]. The Blackened Defias set is mostly BoP. The chest drops only from VanCleef (about 16% per Wowhead) [[d:rogue#1447330131533107362]], [[d:rogue#1447336861185081487]].
- **Hunters:** a level-18 gun [[d:hunter#1490658191065747506]].
