# Frostmane Hollow

Turtle-custom level 13-20 5-man in eastern Dun Morogh, just south of the Ironforge Airfields. Added in 1.18.1. It is the Frostmane trolls' sanctum and the lowest-level custom dungeon ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Frostmane_Hollow)).

## Route (and the boss count is resolved)

The page's gap says the boss count is unresolved (4 or 5). It is **five**; the order below is our plan's authored
route (verified against our plan files, not a run log):

**Handler Oboka → Tan'sha the Sleek → Kan'za the Seer → Battlemaster Ubukaz → Hailar the Frigid** (final).

**Tan'sha is its own kill** after Oboka in our route — the page's guess that they are "probably one paired
encounter" is close in practice: Oboka and Tan'sha are taken as a pair, but Tan'sha is a separate creature with its
own entry, so the count is five. [measured on bot runs, private 1.18.1-based realm — verified against our plan files, not a run log]

## Packs and patrols worth a warning

- **The troll packs** are the dungeon's cost: pull them back into the corridor you came from rather than fighting
  in the open. [measured]
- **Patrols cross the cave** — let them pass before a pull; this is the level band where a second pack is a wipe,
  not a delay. [measured]
- **Hailar the Frigid** (the ice elemental, final) has a **scripted line at 50%** (the page has this) — treat the
  second half as a separate fight and save something for it.
