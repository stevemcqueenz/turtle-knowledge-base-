# Frostmane Hollow

Turtle-custom level 13-20 5-man in eastern Dun Morogh, just south of the Ironforge Airfields. Added in 1.18.1. It is the Frostmane trolls' sanctum and the lowest-level custom dungeon ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Frostmane_Hollow)).
