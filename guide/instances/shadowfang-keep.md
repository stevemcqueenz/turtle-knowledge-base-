# Shadowfang Keep

Vanilla level ~22-30 Silverpine dungeon. Turtle added the optional church with **Prelate Ironmane** (1.17.2), and linked two Scythe of Elune legendary quests to the keep ([Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709), [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2022-11-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29743#p29743)).

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 5 |
| Location | Silverpine Forest (vanilla) |
| Turtle changes | Prelate Ironmane; mob pathing (2024-11-19) and bounding (2025-10-05) fixes; level-60 Scythe of Elune quests *Blood of Vorgendor* (A) and *The Wolf, the Crone and the Scythe* (H) |

## Prelate Ironmane (lvl 22, the keep's church)

- Abilities: **Smite**, **Curse of Agony**, **Drain Mana** ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Prelate_Ironmane)). Casters and healers should watch their mana; that follows from the listed Drain Mana, not from any player report.
- He and his Spectral Clerics have counted as **Undead** since 2025-03-04, so Shackle Undead, Exorcism and Turn Undead work on them ([Torta (staff), 2025-03-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121678#p121678)).
- Loot: Mitre of the First Light, Prelate's Sigil, Hilt of Radiance, Signet of Faded Sermons. Quest *Too Late to Prelate* (Horde).

## Route

The page has no boss order. This is the one our clears run (measured; the numbers are the order our runs kill them
in, which is the keep's climb):

| Boss | # | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Rethilgore** | 1 | the courtyard; the worg pack around him is the first real pull |
| **Razorclaw the Butcher** | 2 | the second stop on the climb |
| **Baron Silverlaine** | 3 | the third |
| **Sever** *(optional)* | 4 | our clears skip it |
| **Prelate Ironmane** *(optional, Turtle)* | 5 | the church — see the page's section; our clears skip it |
| **Commander Springvale** | 6 | the corridor |
| **Odo the Blindwatcher** | 7 | the corridor's end |
| **Deathsworn Captain** *(optional rare)* | 8 | taken when the route passes him |
| **Fenrus the Devourer** | 9 | the ramp |
| **Wolf Master Nandos** | 10 | past the **Sorcerer's Gate** |
| **Archmage Arugal** (final) | 11 | the top |

The keep is **vertical**: the run climbs from the courtyard to Arugal, and the stairs are the route. Two doors are
part of it: the **Courtyard Door** and the **Sorcerer's Gate** (the one before Nandos). [measured on bot runs, private 1.18.1-based realm — verified against our plan files, not a run log]

## Packs and patrols worth a warning

- **The courtyard worg pack** is the first fight, and patrols join it; clear it before the first boss, not during.
  [measured]
- **The corridor patrols between Springvale and Odo** walk the length of the corridor; pull the pack back to the
  corner you came from. [measured]
- **The Fenrus ramp:** pull the ramp pack **down**, not up — fighting on the ramp is how a group eats the next
  pack. [measured]
- **The cell-door event** (the two prisoners) is optional and needs a dialogue; skip it unless someone has the
  quest. [measured]
- The keep's **Undead** (Prelate Ironmane and his Spectral Clerics) can be shackled, exorcised and turned — the
  page's staff note is the one to keep. [classic]

## Leveling notes

- **Shaman tanks:** "level normally until 26 then start out tanking in Shadowfang" [[d:shaman#1448921578992832714]].
- **Druids:** if you do not dispel the Haunted debuff (and the group leaves the ghost alone), Sylvan Blessing procs almost constantly [[d:druid#1436218881344667758]].
- **Rogues:** Meteor Shard from the last boss is a strong dagger for the level [[d:rogue#1324107314747408587]].
