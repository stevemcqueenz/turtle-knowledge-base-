# Shadowfang Keep

Vanilla level ~22-30 Silverpine dungeon. Turtle added the optional church with **Prelate Ironmane** (1.17.2), and linked two Scythe of Elune legendary quests to the keep ([Akalix (staff), 2024-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=91709#p91709), [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2022-11-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=29743#p29743)).

## Prelate Ironmane (lvl 22, the keep's church)

- Abilities: **Smite**, **Curse of Agony**, **Drain Mana** ([wiki](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Prelate_Ironmane)). Casters and healers should watch their mana; that follows from the listed Drain Mana, not from any player report.
- He and his Spectral Clerics have counted as **Undead** since 2025-03-04, so Shackle Undead, Exorcism and Turn Undead work on them ([Torta (staff), 2025-03-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121678#p121678)).
- Loot: Mitre of the First Light, Prelate's Sigil, Hilt of Radiance, Signet of Faded Sermons. Quest *Too Late to Prelate* (Horde).

## Leveling notes

- **Shaman tanks:** "level normally until 26 then start out tanking in Shadowfang" [[d:shaman#1448921578992832714]].
- **Druids:** if you do not dispel the Haunted debuff (and the group leaves the ghost alone), Sylvan Blessing procs almost constantly [[d:druid#1436218881344667758]].
- **Rogues:** Meteor Shard from the last boss is a strong dagger for the level [[d:rogue#1324107314747408587]].
