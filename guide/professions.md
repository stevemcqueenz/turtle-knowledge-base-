# Professions on Turtle WoW 1.18.1

**Recommendation:** on almost every class, level with **two gathering professions** for gold. At 60, drop one and take **Engineering**, which is the only profession that directly adds raid damage or threat (Goblin Sapper Charges, grenades, Battle Chicken, Force Reactive Disk). Keep **Herbalism** if you farm the Dire Maul East lashers, or **Mining** if you farm Zul'Gurub. Take a crafting profession only for a specific BoP item your spec needs, and buy BoE crafts instead of leveling a profession for them. Add **Survival**, the reworked secondary profession, for the slingshot ranged pull, ammo and gathering tools. Class pages: [Druid](classes/druid/professions.md) · [Hunter](classes/hunter/professions.md) · [Mage](classes/mage/professions.md) · [Paladin](classes/paladin/professions.md) · [Priest](classes/priest/professions.md) · [Rogue](classes/rogue/professions.md) · [Shaman](classes/shaman/professions.md) · [Warlock](classes/warlock/professions.md) · [Warrior](classes/warrior/professions.md).

Turtle keeps the vanilla primary professions and adds one new primary profession, **Jewelcrafting** (1.17.2). It also has two custom secondary professions: **Survival**, fully reworked in 1.18.1, and **Gardening**, which needs Survival. On top of that are custom recipes for the vanilla professions and, in 1.18.1, an experimental **crafted-item upgrade** system. Gnomish and Goblin Engineering are "basically the same" as vanilla, apart from new recipes [[d:turtle-wiki#1396381814318436402]]. Profession content is called "kinda neglected on this server" [[d:mage#1488179923918131453]].

## Primary professions

### Engineering

- **What it gives:** Goblin Sapper Charges (off the GCD [[d:mage#1493436328271024149]]), grenades and dynamite, Battle Chicken, target dummies, the Force Reactive Disk (paladin farming and AoE tanking [[d:paladin#1488941850139033770]]), teleporters to Stormwind, Orgrimmar and Everlook [[d:paladin#1460964770491662466]], and parachute cloaks.
- **Turtle-specific:**
  - Battle Chicken no longer procs on demand. It starts a random 30-80 s timer in combat [[d:paladin#1429190224776527903]].
  - The **Facetted Crystal Scope** (+2% ranged crit; schematic from the Draenei Exiles at Revered) [[d:hunter#1484564082014289983]], [[d:hunter#1484680131627716769]].
  - **Enchanted Thorium Shells** (1.18.1 ammo). Staff name Cannon Master Willey as the schematic source ([Torta (staff), 2026-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174367#p174367)), but players still report it not dropping ([Roegnvald (player), 2026-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175240#p175240)). **Contested.**
  - **Caster goggles** in 1.18.1, upgraded with an item from a BWL boss, with a large spell-power proc [[d:priest#1486384331227398216]], [[d:mage#1484999614477373665]].
  - Jewelcrafting's tools (Jewelry Lens at 125, Precision Jewelers Kit at 175, Jewelry Scope at 225) are Engineering crafts ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106310#p106310)).
- **Gnomish vs Goblin:** take Gnomish first and make the BoP items you can't buy ("death ray and battle chicken"), then swap [[d:pvp-general#1390646345500463219]], [[d:pvp-general#1390646544205742100]]. Relearn as Goblin to craft your own Sappers and rocket boots [[d:mage#1488174450477240420]]. Switching specialisation means leveling Engineering again from 1 [[d:warrior#1480987563975114783]], [[d:warrior#1480991021805473904]].
- **Druids:** Sappers and grenades don't work in Cat or Bear Form. Moonkin can use Sappers in form [[d:druid#1425274156005720216]]. Which other Engineering items work in form is **contested** (see the druid page).
- **Cost:** about 100-150g from 1 to 300 [[d:paladin#1489769042435768442]]. Engineering makes no gold [[d:mage#1333961791125852251]].

### Alchemy (+ Herbalism)

- Makes potions, elixirs and flasks, the raid buffs everyone needs [[d:turtle-wiki#1405426596902600754]]. Herbalism + Alchemy is the usual "first character" and gold pair [[d:mage#1451246474187771915]], and "def not alchemy without herba" [[d:shaman#1426604274489622619]].
- Flasks got cheaper when their recipe changed to Golden Sansam, "like 30% cheaper" [[d:pvp-general#1405620816623960255]]. The higher Black Lotus chance and flasks from weekly caches then pushed prices down further [[d:pvp-general#1432106318767984680]].
- Turtle custom recipes: Potion of Quickness, Lucidity Potion, Elixir of Greater Nature Power, Dreamshard Elixir (1.17.0) and Unstable Concoction (1.17.1) ([Dragunovi (staff), 2023-07-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52796#p52796)).
- An Arcanite transmute earns about 6g every 2 days, so it only pays across several alts [[d:paladin#1441717649498308649]].

### Enchanting

- Enchant your own gear and your raid's. Turtle adds custom enchants: Bracer Spell Power (+12), Gloves Arcane/Nature Power (+20), Boots/Bracer Vampirism, Gloves Major Strength and more ([Dragunovi (staff), 2023-07-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52796#p52796)).
- Enchanting supplies the **Enchanted Gemstone Oils** that Jewelcrafting needs ([official site, Jewelcrafting](https://turtlecraft.gg/jewelcrafting)).
- A good leveling partner for Skinning or Tailoring: disenchant outgrown gear as you go [[d:druid#1442170706799497227]], [[d:priest#1477162914807939163]]. You can disenchant at Enchanting skill 1 [[d:rogue#1435154921799548978]].
- Enchanting + Jewelcrafting earns gold if you advertise [[d:mage#1448316389499867206]]. Enchants, Jewelcrafting gems and belt buckles are usually sold with the buyer bringing the mats [[d:code-corner#1461537526685896847]].
- In 1.18.1, Lesser and Greater Mystic Wand need **Bright Wood**, which comes from Woodcutting ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)).

### Jewelcrafting (Turtle-custom, 1.17.2)

- **What it is:** more than 200 crafts: rings, necklaces, staves, trinkets, off-hands, helmets and gemstones. It has two specialisations, chosen at skill 225 and level 40: **Goldsmith** (equipment, and most epics) and **Gemology** (gemstones) ([official site, Jewelcrafting](https://turtlecraft.gg/jewelcrafting)). Gear has no gem slots [[d:turtle-wiki#1396383214792343612]]. Gemstones work "like enchants", and anyone can benefit from them without a Goldsmith specialisation [[d:turtle-wiki#1385337387206578301]].
- **What classes use:**
  - Ring and neck enchants: +6 Arcane SP for mages [[d:mage#1466716028414328852]], +12 healing [[d:shaman#1413241423980920943]], +Agility for rogues [[d:rogue#1490264774276026378]], hit for warlocks [[d:warlock#1475629087467765760]], +3 all stats for warriors [[d:warrior#1440985379653161041]].
  - Tank and vampirism pieces [[d:rogue#1444828435540213761]], [[d:paladin#1465783072804049122]].
  - Resto staves and rings [[d:shaman#1416254106682789898]].
  - Warlocks prefer Gemology [[d:warlock#1427499791755116717]]. Goldsmith items are "just aren't good" for warriors [[d:warrior#1481311548176597146]].
- **Self-sufficient:** you need Mining, plus Enchanting for the gem oils [[d:turtle-wiki#1396161476099837963]]. **Gemstone Deposits** replace thorium veins, need Mining 310 and guarantee an Imperial Topaz ([official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/jewelcrafting)).
- **Leveling:** the only 1-300 route is a player guide (pre-1.18.1, peer-corrected) ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106310#p106310)). Details are in `synthesis/content/professions-and-recipes.md` §3.

### Tailoring

- Mostly for a few BoP items: **Robe of the Archmage** for mages (BiS until BWL, still wearable after you drop the profession) [[d:mage#1434898636461969418]], [[d:mage#1448189554992484483]], Mooncloth for T3 [[d:warlock#1451703869968224377]], and the **Core Felcloth Bag** for warlocks [[d:warlock#1446170701622022285]].
- The good caster crafts are BoE, so buy them: Spellwoven Nobility Drape, Gloves of Unwinding Mystery, Gloves of Spell Mastery [[d:mage#1436110986720317460]], [[d:paladin#1493314491340034048]].
- Mooncloth margins are poor, about 10g per 5-day cooldown [[d:mage#1453809774398541826]].
- Turtle custom patterns include Dreamthread, Cosmic, Flarecore Boots, Gloves of Unwinding Mystery and Robe of Sacrifice ([Dragunovi (staff), 2023-07-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=52796#p52796)).

### Leatherworking (+ Skinning)

- **Rogues:** Dreamhide Leggings (3% haste, BoP) [[d:rogue#1447098170642268170]] and Naxx frost-resistance crafts from Cured Rugged Hide [[d:rogue#1481656932015145232]].
- **Druids:** Leatherworking pieces (Dreamhide) feed the Swift Travel Form quest [[d:druid#1328518855823462525]]. Flamewrath Leggings are cat BiS [[d:druid#1430925528172462080]].
- **Hunters:** no Leatherworking specialisation suits hunters [[d:hunter#1486157530781716631]].
- **Shamans:** it "is kinda ass" unless you refuse to trade [[d:shaman#1462159463711506595]].
- Skinning is called the worst gathering profession for gold [[d:rogue#1494692871163478087]]. A Cured Rugged Hide cooldown still sells for about 19-20g every 72 hours [[d:rogue#1456737096407453808]].

### Blacksmithing (+ Mining)

- **Contested** for warriors: "avoid like the plague" [[d:warrior#1464385054607151125]] against "has some good bits now", mostly pre-raid [[d:warrior#1445158997660536987]].
- Key crafts:
  - Tempered Runeblade (Swordsmith chain), pre-raid BiS for Ret and warriors [[d:paladin#1443679533701005333]], [[d:warrior#1445159094184054927]].
  - Belt buckles [[d:druid#1491824487942062091]].
  - A fear-immunity trinket [[d:rogue#1447106369147768947]].
  - The Fiery Chain fire-resistance set for shaman tanks [[d:shaman#1474856625297690705]].
- **1.18.1 crafted-item upgrades (experimental):** BWL bosses drop upgrade items (e.g. an Arcanite sharpening stone) that raise specific crafted items. The upgraded weapon needs the crafting specialisation to use, e.g. Axesmith for the Reaper or Swordsmith for the Champion ([Dragunovi (staff), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168272#p168272)), [[d:warrior#1490124384684478474]]. Players call it "very niche and mostly just for flavor" ([Elesion (player), 2026-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173602#p173602)).

### Gathering: Herbalism, Mining, Skinning

- **Herbalism** pays best through the **Dire Maul East lashers** (from about level 52-54). Prot paladins are called the best lasher farmers [[d:paladin#1372276033608351847]], [[d:mage#1447768105819439347]].
  - How much Herbalism adds is **contested**. One view is about +20% [[d:priest#1420896033189396512]]; another is "a very minor part" of lasher income [[d:shaman#1473082022149750886]].
  - Herb prices crashed in late 2025 from oversupply [[d:mage#1448314903759687832]], [[d:paladin#1428169924966223992]].
  - Staff raised the lasher herb drop to 4%, and the Black Lotus chance from high-level herbs to 6% (last step 2025-10-21) ([Akalix (staff), 2024-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90746#p90746); see `synthesis/content/professions-and-recipes.md` §7).
  - Druids can herb in Travel Form [[d:druid#1306462082107183164]]. Tauren get +15 Herbalism [[d:shaman#1278437860797710387]].
- **Mining:** the best early gold [[d:hunter#1368570962806706177]]. At 60, naked ZG mining (Arcane Crystal veins) is a consistent farm [[d:rogue#1489623080711094384]], [[d:paladin#1489955311077953687]]. Hateforge Quarry has 2 guaranteed thorium veins on a fast reset [[d:hunter#1484726814533353624]]. **Contested:** one rogue calls Mining "a really bad gold maker" [[d:rogue#1476934750731112661]].
- **Skinning** is the cheap leveling gatherer that pays for training [[d:warlock#1369943361254850593]].
- You can only track one node type at a time [[d:druid#1424684566953853009]].

## Secondary professions

### Survival (Turtle-custom; reworked in 1.18.1)

**What it is.** Survival was added on 2019-05-10 as a secondary skill capped at 150. In **1.18.1** (released 2026-03-20) staff rebuilt it into "a secondary profession drawing from other primary professions". The rework has **80+ new recipes**, tools that **slow, root or trap** opponents, **bags** for the three gathering professions, containers for fish and meat, and **Woodcutting**, a wood-gathering activity tied to Survival skill ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)).

**Before 1.18.1 (historical):**

- The crafts were the Dim Torch (+4 Spirit to the party), Bright Campfire (+8 Spirit), Traveler's Tent (rested XP), Fishing Boat (+50 Fishing), Iron Lantern, Murloc's Flippers, the **Repaired Electro-Lantern** and the Gardening planter ([official site, Survival & Gardening](https://turtlecraft.gg/survival-and-gardening)).
- Tent duration is **contested**: 20 minutes on the official page, 30 minutes in the 2019 guide ([Gifted (guide writer), 2019-08-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=3641#p3641)).
- Leveling: skill 76-150 unlocked automatically, and each Bright Campfire gave a guaranteed point up to 150 [[d:turtle-wiki#1354620357222469813]].
- Uses:
  - Tent-questing was the fastest way to level [[d:warrior#1442629300749865160]], [[d:druid#1457170124162207807]].
  - The Electro-Lantern's melee zap was an off-hand for Resto druid "tree-punching" [[d:druid#1484000652387680387]] and a Nature spellstrike item for paladins [[d:paladin#1442133221960716432]].

**The 1.18.1 rework:**

- **Learning it:** progress was **reset** [[d:paladin#1483855079659278448]].
  - Apprentice is available at level 1 again ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608)).
  - The new trainer is in Stormwind and Orgrimmar, and the old trainer no longer sells wood [[d:turtle-wiki#1485536549629071372]], [[d:turtle-wiki#1484735725424476311]].
  - Journeyman: level 10 / skill 50. Expert: level 20 / skill 150, taught only by Nerean Stagtree (Desolace, Alliance) or Swampwalker Krug (Swamp of Sorrows, Horde). Artisan: level 35 / skill 225, from Rufus Hardwick (Stranglethorn) via *To Survive in the Jungle* ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2026-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172915#p172915); level reduced to 35 by [Torta (staff), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490)).
- **Leveling 1-300:**
  - The one full route is the Speedz1337 guide. It needs a Whittle and a Woodcutting Axe from a Survival Supplier, and states a vendor cost of about 6g 62s. Everything else is gathered by Woodcutting or bought on the AH. The route and wood tiers per zone are in `synthesis/content/professions-and-recipes.md` §1.5a.
  - Torches now grey out at skill 15 [[d:turtle-wiki#1485506514327441489]]. Cutting trees gives no Survival skill [[d:paladin#1488086033387098225]], and trees are gated by Survival skill [[d:hunter#1484976773220401243]].
  - Leveling it is "worse than it was before": some materials are very costly while leveling [[d:druid#1488636882920411228]], [[d:mage#1489907806365683712]].
- **What it gives:**
  - **Simple Slingshot:** a 20-yard ranged pull for melee, learned early [[d:paladin#1484571592011940002]], [[d:paladin#1485538065132032080]]. Paladins love it: "The Slingshot is so good" [[d:paladin#1486707812800270429]].
  - **Arrows and ammo:** the easy tier is about 17.5 DPS [[d:hunter#1484621720001319113]]. It can make "2nd bis arrows", which you can also buy on the AH [[d:hunter#1491810301924868136]], [[d:hunter#1491810332295958778]].
  - **Emergency Parachute** cloak (30-minute cooldown) [[d:hunter#1486969485687525437]].
  - **Throwable Net, Slowing Bolas** (slow and root tools; Speedz1337 guide). Warrior twinks list the nets for PvP [[d:warrior#1490664434526458038]], but one player calls them "disappointing" [[d:warrior#1490665285907386509]]. Bolas can proc Wrath of Cenarius (single source) [[d:paladin#1485279603806441522]].
  - **Gathering trinkets:** the Black Lotus trinket multiplies the Black Lotus chance by 1.5, and the Arcane Crystal trinket adds a flat 2% (single source) [[d:paladin#1489295469812121661]]. Players stack them with the new Fortune flask [[d:paladin#1485539474447532062]]. There is also a skill-300 sickle that raises the Black Lotus chance ([Birtha (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171974#p171974)).
  - **Other crafts:** Nutritious Rations (make permanent Oranges), fishing traps, craftable bows, one-handed white weapons for Vagrant/Traveling Craftsman challenges, and a cloth head that summons a cooking fire ([Elesion (player), 2026-03-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173031#p173031)). Also an early dagger with +1 dagger skill (single source) [[d:rogue#1485549149192589322]].
  - **Tents in 1.18.1:** rested XP from kills fell to a 50% bonus, tents fill only to 25% rested, and non-city taverns reach 100% ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). Players say the nerf "wrecked tents" [[d:warrior#1473326647405969428]], and "Tents are ass the rest is solid to use" [[d:hunter#1491811180287754433]].
- **Who benefits most:**
  - Melee without a ranged pull (paladins, warriors, rogues) for the slingshot. The paladin channel's standard pair is "Engineering and Survival" [[d:paladin#1486706568044220557]].
  - Herbalists and miners for the gathering trinkets and bags.
  - Hunters for ammo, though you can buy it.
  - Hardcore players, where "survival skills like that are super high value" [[d:warrior#1481441495184838849]].
  - Several channels advise leveling it only as far as the items you want, e.g. about 60 for the slingshot [[d:paladin#1486706866519015446]].
  - No source finds raid value for caster classes.

### Gardening (Turtle-custom; needs Survival)

**What it is:**

- Grow crops in planters.
- **Before 1.18.1:** at level 20, take **"You Reap What You Sow"** in Elwynn Forest or Mulgore. It rewards a Shovel and a first Wooden Planter and needs **Survival 75** ([official site, Survival & Gardening](https://turtlecraft.gg/survival-and-gardening)). Trainers are Kern Mosshoof (Bloodhoof Village, Horde) and Mortimer Stansfield (outside Stormwind, Alliance) ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2025-06-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=136320#p136320)), [[d:turtle-wiki#1484696948161249320]].
- **Growth cycle:** one seed per planter, four 9-minute stages (36 minutes). Stage 2 needs Un'Goro Soil; stages 3 and 4 need Refreshing Spring Water. **Planters vanish if you log out or leave the zone** (same guide).
- Since 2025, planters work anywhere except capital cities ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2025-10-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155377#p155377)).

**1.18.1 changes:**

- The Gardening quest was **reset**, so you have to redo it ([Sylveria (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171863#p171863)).
- Staff announced **three new crops** with strong buffs, plus critters that raid crops ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)).
- A player named **four** new seeds (Wildhammer Yam Roots, Wild Grape Seeds, Ordinary Cabbage Seeds, Sandswept Carrot Seeds) ([Sagabal (player), 2026-04-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174505#p174505)). The count is **contested**.

**What it gives:**

- Cheap raid food: Power Mushrooms [[d:warrior#1457451144509919446]], [[d:paladin#1429923597664784405]] and Sour Mountain Berries (+10 Agility, tradeable) for rogues [[d:rogue#1471992248861327554]].
- Crops are also Survival reagents, which makes them pricey: the skill-225 quest uses them [[d:priest#1491897021928571091]].
- Semi-AFK income [[d:paladin#1466088149959577856]]. Lashers pay more (pre-1.18.1), but that farming isn't AFK [[d:priest#1400602839461400637]].
- Before 1.18.1, the quest's Shovel was a strong level-20 one-hander for shamans [[d:shaman#1390708702201253969]].
- **Risk:** planters despawn on a server crash, and GMs don't restore them [[d:turtle-wiki#1490573491806801920]].

**Who benefits most:** raiders who want cheap food, and players who level Survival anyway. No class needs it.

## Race interactions

- **Goblin:** the Prospecting racial grants Mining *and* Jewelcrafting skill ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102965#p102965)). Some Engineering recipes are Goblin-only (rocket boots). The race-change trick is to craft Gnome-only BoP items first, then play Goblin (single source) [[d:mage#1488174450477240420]].
- **Tauren:** +15 Herbalism [[d:shaman#1278437860797710387]].
- **Paladins** can only be Human, Dwarf or High Elf, so no race profession trick applies to them [[d:paladin#1486906729110442084]].

## Gold

- **Dire Maul East lashers** (with Herbalism) are the standard gold farm for mages, paladins, shamans, warlocks and priests from about level 52 [[d:mage#1433513769304068147]], [[d:shaman#1425738262923972720]]. Druids farm them poorly because lashers are Nature-immune [[d:druid#1487294040054431765]].
- **Zul'Gurub** kite and mining farms suit hunters and rogues [[d:hunter#1483746983536492575]], [[d:rogue#1494691538389958868]].
- **Cooldown alts:** transmutes and Mooncloth earn about 200-300g a week (single source) [[d:warlock#1434730417017327709]].
- Warriors struggle to farm gold solo; the usual workaround is grouping up (tank dungeons, PvP, or auto-follow a friend for scraps) [[d:warrior#1440432464240840795]].
