# PvP on Turtle WoW 1.18.1

**Short answer:** in 1.18.1 honor is a **currency** with a **20,000 honor per week cap**. Rank Points come in with each honor gain, and you are promoted the moment you have enough. You buy three tiers of PvP sets from the Rank 6 hall; the top tier also needs **Conquest**, which was still not earnable weeks after release. For honor, play the daily **Call to Arms** battleground, keep your marks for its turn-in, and don't bother with Warmode unless you want to be ganked for +5% XP. For class matchups, specs and PvP talent builds see the class pages: [Druid](classes/druid/pvp.md) · [Hunter](classes/hunter/pvp.md) · [Mage](classes/mage/pvp.md) · [Paladin](classes/paladin/pvp.md) · [Priest](classes/priest/pvp.md) · [Rogue](classes/rogue/pvp.md) · [Shaman](classes/shaman/pvp.md) · [Warlock](classes/warlock/pvp.md) · [Warrior](classes/warrior/pvp.md).

Before you start: Turtle is a PvE-first network. Its players sum it up as "BG's pop often, no world pvp, arenas exist but they are horrible" [[d:pvp-general#1427687377064955967]] and "pvp is side quest here, its PvE server" [[d:pvp-general#1427676930810839152]]. The #pvp-general export ends on 2025-11-01, so everything below that carries only a pvp-general citation dates from before 1.18.1. Where 2026 posts in the class channels or staff forum posts confirm or overturn it, this page says so.

## Honor, rank and PvP gear

### Honor is a currency now

- Since 1.18.1 honor is credited immediately and spent at vendors. There is no longer a weekly standings race for your spot ([Jamey (staff), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169701#p169701)). Players sum it up as: "Rank points decay. Rank doesn't decay. Honor is just a currency, it doesn't decay" [[d:paladin#1487370299837255681]].
- All honor gains were cut to **10%** of their old values, and prices were rescaled to match ([Jamey (staff), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169701#p169701)).
- **Weekly cap: 20,000 honor** [[d:warrior#1487436235617075222]], [[d:paladin#1487444569942655006]]. It resets at the weekly maintenance, which is also when the Rank Point decay hits [[d:paladin#1491001797970624653]].
- **Weekly reset:** the night from Tuesday to Wednesday, EU time [[d:pvp-general#1431662006233989313]], usually after the server restart [[d:pvp-general#1433172440866881567]]. A 2026 post on a Tuesday confirms the cap reset [[d:paladin#1491001797970624653]].
- **How long capping takes:** estimates run from about 13 hours [[d:warrior#1492265733630066709]] to about 20 hours a week [[d:shaman#1491797398027243662]], [[d:rogue#1489318842634080476]]. Sample gains:
  - 15 AB, 3 WSG and 6 AV (mostly won) gave 7,855 honor in a week [[d:warrior#1488606937149870212]].
  - 2 AB wins plus 1 WSG loss gave 348 honor [[d:paladin#1488936985170022400]].
  - A battleground objective is worth 25 honor, or 50 on the bonus day [[d:paladin#1487363142861328445]], [[d:paladin#1487363174914064496]].
- **Transition:** you kept your old rank, and got a one-time honor bonus based on it ([Jamey (staff), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169701#p169701)). For the mid-week launch, players also got half of the old flush RP ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139)).

### What gives honor (and what doesn't)

These rules were posted before 1.18.1. Nothing in 2026 contradicts them, but treat them as possibly stale.

- **Level gap:** you get no honor from a target more than 12 levels below you (a 60 still gets honor from a 48), and grey-level targets give none [[d:pvp-general#1431615418652557343]], [[d:pvp-general#1431615470880166011]], [[d:pvp-general#1431615507458691072]].
- **Who shares a kill:** honor counts if you are in the killer's party, if you are within about 50 yd in a battleground, or if you damaged the target in the open world while not in a party [[d:pvp-general#1431614220197171381]].
- **Level within a bracket:** a character at the top of a bracket gets less honor for killing lower-level players in it [[d:pvp-general#1433325309041705001]], [[d:pvp-general#1433330237860614307]].
- **Same faction:** same-faction kills don't give Honorable Kills [[d:pvp-general#1431261583589244968]]. **Contested:** after cross-faction BGs arrived, Jesse Potter said killing your own faction was giving HKs and honor and needed a fix [[d:pvp-general#1432394716996964483]].
- **Same IP:** killing players connected from your IP gives no honor ([Torta (staff), 2025-02-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=120933#p120933)).
- **Dishonorable Kills:** **Contested.** Players said there were no DHKs on Turtle [[d:pvp-general#1431620458100555776]]. Staff first removed them, then said grey-level kills would give DHKs, then said they were "still disabled" ([Akalix (staff), 2023-12-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80586#p80586), [Torta (staff), 2023-12-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80839#p80839), [Akalix (staff), 2024-01-16](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84944#p84944)).
- **Titles:** 100,000 Honorable Kills gives a title of its own, separate from rank [[d:pvp-general#1432036652934496377]], [[d:pvp-general#1432034233810944090]].
- **Leaving AV early:** you lose only the bonus honor. The honor tab just doesn't update until you reload or change zone [[d:paladin#1487366641565302946]]. Pastor's rule of thumb: "See if it will end in 30 minutes. If not, leave" [[d:paladin#1487367116062724179]].

### Rank and Rank Points

- Every honor gain also gives Rank Points. You are promoted as soon as you reach the next rank's threshold. RP decays every week but cannot take a rank away ([Jamey (staff), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169701#p169701)). The no-de-rank rule dates from 2023 ([Akalix (staff), 2023-10-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=67818#p67818)). **Contested:** after 1.18.1 some players reported being de-ranked or showing Rank 0 ([Xerony (player), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173497#p173497), [Leilar (player), 2026-04-18](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175607#p175607)).
- **Rank 13 → 14** is reported at about 80k honor (hearsay) [[d:rogue#1489318842634080476]].
- **Level caps on rank:** twinks can't out-rank their level, as in vanilla's level limits [[d:twinking#1488787721815068693]]. At levels 40-49 the cap is about Rank 9 [[d:twinking#1488756025107419227]], [[d:pvp-general#1424038389484617738]]. In the 30s it is about Rank 5 [[d:pvp-general#1425436855091855422]], and at 59 you can reach Rank 13 [[d:pvp-general#1424038430488268800]]. For the first three days after the patch you could rank past the cap, and the next maintenance set those ranks back [[d:twinking#1488874245961416714]].
- **The PvP hall and its vendors** need **Rank 6** (Knight on Alliance) to enter [[d:shaman#1491797398027243662]], [[d:paladin#1487365684366409758]]. You can't turn in marks there [[d:pvp-general#1431268942911111300]].

### The 1.18.1 PvP sets

The old two-tier rank sets can no longer be bought (you keep them if you have them). They were replaced by three tiers for each armor type and role: **Tier 1 rare ~ilvl 71, Tier 2 epic ~ilvl 77, Tier 3 epic ~ilvl 92** [[d:shaman#1481374586938327223]]. Players name them **Combatant's and Partisan's** (honor only) and **Veteran's** (honor plus Conquest) [[d:paladin#1487368925636329576]]. Each tier needs the tier before it [[d:warrior#1488605450130685983]].

| Tier | Total cost (Thirtyseven) | Weeks at the 20k cap | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 (rare) | 24,000 honor | ~1 | Fresh-60 viable |
| T2 (epic) | 144,000 honor + the T1 set | ~5 | ~20k honor per piece; players think it needs about Rank 13 [[d:shaman#1487916985818812646]] |
| T3 (epic) | 520,000 honor + the T2 set + 21,000 Conquest | ~18 + ~7 for Conquest | Pieces ~95-100k honor and 3.5-4k Conquest [[d:paladin#1487444477466378362]], [[d:paladin#1488936914995253288]] |

Source for the totals: "the MINIMUM time you can spend is 8+ months, 33 weeks" [[d:warrior#1492266218629890271]]. The new off-set pieces add close to 40k honor more [[d:warrior#1492266218629890271]]. PvP weapons don't need Conquest [[d:shaman#1487929037719470310]], but the new trinket needs 250 Conquest [[d:paladin#1491247941581803591]]. More crit-reduction PvP pieces reached the vendors around 2026-04-08 [[d:mage#1491457261766316192]]. Opinions on the sets range from "utter garbage compared to PVE gear sets" to a T3 piece being the new bear threat BiS ([Moomoobro (player), 2026-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172842#p172842), [[d:druid#1487935338197291078]]). The class pages cover which pieces each class wants.

### Conquest Points

- **Planned sources:** Conquest was announced to come from high rated-arena rating (paid at the weekly flush), from the world PvP events, and from Call to Arms battlegrounds (paid immediately) ([Jamey (staff), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169701#p169701)). The Tier 3 set, the trinket and some off-set pieces need it [[d:paladin#1491247941581803591]], [[d:paladin#1491248376094916618]].
- **Status:** on 2026-04-08, asked whether anyone could earn Conquest, Thirtyseven answered "not available yet" [[d:warrior#1491396589040701561]]. On 2026-04-17 another player said "conquest is disabled anyway" [[d:rogue#1494773836149821752]]. Players expected every Conquest source to arrive in one update together with the world PvP objectives [[d:paladin#1491248861233283142]]. One shaman on 2026-04-17 said he was "working on conquest points for trinket" [[d:shaman#1494682635119759400]], which conflicts with the other reports. Treat Conquest as **not live**; the servers closed on 2026-05-15.

## Battlegrounds

### Queueing and cross-faction

- **Queue from anywhere:** use the crossed-swords icon at the minimap [[d:pvp-general#1427061655082111048]]. In late March 2026 the minimap finder had the wrong level requirements for Arathi Basin and Thorn Gorge; the 2026-03-31 hotfix corrected them ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490)).
- **Cross-faction:** since 4 November 2025, PvE realms can put you on the other faction's team when one side is short ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=157078#p157078)). You can't queue a mixed-faction premade ([Jamey (staff), 2025-11-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=158392#p158392)), and the queue tries same-faction matches first ([Jamey (staff), 2025-11-11](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159238#p159238)). Tel'Abim, the PvP realm, has no cross-faction interaction ([Torta (staff), 2023-09-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66566#p66566)).
- **No combat escape:** you can't enter a battleground while PvP-flagged and in combat ([Akalix (staff), 2024-03-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=90751#p90751)).
- **Faction balance before cross-faction:** Horde queues often took over an hour while Alliance popped instantly [[d:pvp-general#1429522141438808234]]. Players put Horde's pug win rate at about 80% [[d:pvp-general#1427519030746550374]].

### Call to Arms (the daily bonus battleground)

- One battleground a day carries the bonus. You can check which one at the city emissaries or on turtle-timer.vercel.app [[d:pvp-general#1431254059167383613]]. Before 1.18.1 the order was **AV → WSG → AB → Blood Ring → Sunnyglade** [[d:pvp-general#1431906563135897722]].
- **1.18.1:** Sunnyglade Valley was disabled for a rework ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168), [[d:paladin#1473392820168687766]]), and its emissaries were replaced by Thorn Gorge emissaries ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173490#p173490)). That Thorn Gorge takes Sunnyglade's slot in the rotation is an inference; no Discord post confirms it.
- **How to farm it:** on its bonus day, queue only that battleground and save the marks. After the third day (AB), turn them all in for the "greater honor" quest [[d:pvp-general#1431906563135897722]]. The quest pays a fixed amount no matter how many sets of marks you hand in [[d:pvp-general#1432832789706833991]].
- **Where to turn in:** marks go to the capital's king's hall, and the class of the character doesn't matter. You gain reputation and honor [[d:pvp-general#1431263535572324362]].
- **Blood Ring day** comes round about every five days. It is one of the few times people queue for the Blood Ring [[d:pvp-general#1428048134088560752]].

### Warsong Gulch

- **Flag-carrier debuff:** it stacks the longer the carrier holds the flag. At about **18 stacks** the carrier dies in two hits, so it works as a soft timer [[d:pvp-general#1425625682335109200]].
- **Resetting it:** dropping and re-grabbing the flag doesn't clear the stacks; only a capture does [[d:pvp-general#1425267040125587536]], [[d:pvp-general#1425274075252789258]], [[d:pvp-general#1425415543149563974]]. One player adds that about 10 minutes without holding the flag also clears them (single source) [[d:pvp-general#1425415565312397342]].
- **Known bug (Oct 2025):** if both teams return their flags and re-pick them at about the same time, the stacks don't refresh, and the new carrier can start with 10 or more [[d:pvp-general#1423633230497583158]].
- **No game timer:** stalled games have run 50+ minutes [[d:pvp-general#1425625894604509227]].
- **Help you can't give the carrier:**
  - Blessing of Protection and Divine Intervention can't be cast on a carrier unless the flag is dropped manually first ([Torta (staff), 2024-01-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=83488#p83488)).
  - A carrier can stay stealthed, but casting stealth drops the flag ([Torta (staff), 2024-01-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=84147#p84147)).
  - **Dust of Disappearance** was nerfed after it was used to force enemy carriers to drop the flag [[d:pvp-general#1424838030518386789]], [[d:pvp-general#1424838417333620816]].
- **Power-up respawns:** Food 20 s, Berserk 120 s, Speed 150 s ([Torta (staff), 2022-12-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=30582#p30582)).
- **Jump spots:** staff chose to discourage them rather than remove them [[d:pvp-general#1381882667485888514]]. Some Classic jumps don't work on Turtle, and male vs female models can matter [[d:pvp-general#1424453730442678474]], [[d:pvp-general#1424462564875702324]].
- **Picking up the flag:** scroll-wheel pickup is not baseline on the 1.12 client [[d:pvp-general#1429903267063791648]].
- **Hunting the carrier:** set your nameplates to enemy players only, so totems, pets and friendlies don't get in the way [[d:pvp-general#1425610037249839264]]. Class-specific carrier advice is on the class pages.

### Alterac Valley

- AV is the only battleground with an inactivity debuff [[d:pvp-general#1428047992065101834]]. Being AFK in any battleground is against the rules and can get you a suspension ([Pepesmite (staff), 2024-06-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=96257#p96257)).
- Before 1.18.1, AV games could drag on for 1.5-2 hours. The losing side still collected a roughly 3k honor bonus from objectives and kills, so many players didn't try to win [[d:pvp-general#1432900493247512617]], [[d:pvp-general#1432900615079465101]]. Those numbers predate the 10% honor rescale.
- Korrak's health has been cut to a quarter of the original, so one player can kill him ([Torta (staff), 2023-06-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=51551#p51551)).

### Thorn Gorge (new in 1.18.1)

- **Format:** an Eye of the Storm-style 15v15. You capture flags and bases until one side reaches the resource cap ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)).
- **Level:** the minimum was lowered to **31** on 2026-03-24 ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139)). For a while after that you couldn't queue it from the map [[d:twinking#1486168996725129286]].
- **Hotfixes:** power-ups were added on 2026-03-24 ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139)). On 2026-04-14 flag pickup stopped being affected by haste and honor went up 50% ([Torta (staff), 2026-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175230#p175230)).
- There is no shapeshifting while you carry its flag [[d:shaman#1491915996599156970]].

### Rules for every battleground

- **Transmog and illusions:** transmog doesn't show inside battlegrounds [[d:pvp-general#1426680097280229537]], and illusion items can't be used there [[d:pvp-general#1430298117651300392]].
- **Consumables:** they are deliberately lost when you die in a battleground ([Akalix (staff), 2024-02-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=86655#p86655)).
- **Cooldowns:** spell cooldowns reset when you enter a battleground or resurrect in one ([Torta (staff), 2019-11-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=4490#p4490)).
- **Leveling in battlegrounds:** battlegrounds give XP, and the losing side still levels well [[d:pvp-general#1432873068812046467]]. In 1.18.1 players still level in AB on its bonus day [[d:druid#1489039007462719649]]. Hardcore characters get no battleground XP ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=155594#p155594)).
- **Twink brackets:** at 19 and 29, non-twinks are reported to be badly outclassed by twinks [[d:pvp-general#1432290422553575557]].

## Blood Ring and arenas

### Blood Ring

- **What it is:** Turtle's 3v3 arena, with its own reputation, the **Steamwheedle Blood Ring** ([Torta (staff), 2023-10-17](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=71607#p71607)).
- **Arena rules:**
  - World buffs are removed when you enter.
  - Pet trinkets are disabled.
  - Mana is unlimited during the preparation phase.
  - Soul Shards are refunded after the match.
  - Source: [Akalix (staff), 2023-12-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=80586#p80586).
- **Reputation rewards:**
  - The **Bloody Gladiator** helm and shoulders, in plate [[d:warrior#1468646228278509864]] and mail [[d:paladin#1448648481198575636]] versions, plus rings [[d:pvp-general#1420509306675134618]].
  - A Weaponsmith dagger recipe that needs Exalted plus Blood tokens [[d:rogue#1435247367275413587]], [[d:rogue#1458835804813131847]].
  - On Ambershire the rewards were released later than the reputation itself [[d:rogue#1447209945371246754]], [[d:rogue#1458840551221625130]].
- **Title:** **Blood Ring Champion** needs max reputation with the Blood Ring, beyond plain Exalted [[d:pvp-general#1428055615258759281]], [[d:pvp-general#1428056239086243930]]. Exalted with the Steamwheedle Cartel doesn't count [[d:pvp-general#1409893022996627496]].

### Rated arena and skirmishes

- 1.18.0 added a new arena map, 2v2 and rated teams ([Jamey (staff), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134968#p134968)).
- **Before 1.18.1** the arena population was tiny: one player got to rank 1 with a single 10-0 session [[d:pvp-general#1426961124120723577]].
  - Matchmaking often stopped finding games, and the only fix was to disband the team [[d:pvp-general#1426983653514281020]].
  - Overflow arena tokens were supposed to arrive by mail, but after about 100 games one player had received none [[d:pvp-general#1433582342223564841]].
  - Gear swaps and pre-match consumables were allowed [[d:pvp-general#1433505871731626056]].
- **After 1.18.1:** on 2026-03-21 new arena teams couldn't be created, and solo skirmishes kicked you out when the doors opened [[d:level-one-lunatic#1484684233984311408]]. Group queueing for skirmish was fixed on 2026-04-14 ([Torta (staff), 2026-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175230#p175230)). The announced restrictions on cooldowns and consumables in rated arena have no confirmed go-live date ([Jamey (staff), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=169701#p169701)).

## World PvP and Warmode

- **Warmode** makes you permanently PvP-flagged. You toggle it at a capital Battlemaster [[d:pvp-general#1432831679336022056]], and it ends at level 60 ([Torta (staff), 2025-08-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=144045#p144045)).
  - **Bonus:** since the 2025 change it is just +5% XP, and it is no longer offered at character creation [[d:pvp-general#1432758178122305537]]. A 2026 answer confirms the +5% and adds "Youll get ganked" [[d:rogue#1487521284308074528]].
  - **Why it changed:** staff blamed level-1 "Lunatic" players killing brand-new characters during their intro cinematic, and said that experience won't come back ([Torta (staff), 2025-12-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161360#p161360)). Players agree Lunatics were the reason for the change [[d:pvp-general#1428054532427874425]], and say it killed what world PvP was left: "There is no wpvp on Nord anymore" [[d:pvp-general#1432759100751482921]].
  - **Warmode on Tel'Abim:** it isn't available there ([Torta (staff), 2023-09-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=66566#p66566)).
- **How much world PvP there is:** very little. It is "virtually nonexistant on Nordranar" [[d:pvp-general#1430917599864291429]], and one player leveled with Warmode on without meeting any [[d:rogue#1487521354264871114]].
- **Zone PvP quest:** get 5 killing blows in a zone that fits your level, for PvP currency and a lot of XP [[d:pvp-general#1432997302620000358]].
- **Redwall Keep and Southshore vs Tarren Mill:** these 1.18.1 world PvP objectives were meant to give honor and Conquest ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). Redwall Keep is a new fortress in Redridge [[d:pvp-general#1433363691046309908]], so it is a low-level event [[d:paladin#1474417607816773742]]. **Contested:** staff said the events would go live "shortly after the release", but players were still asking where they were on 2026-04-15 ([Gramrock (player), 2026-04-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=175348#p175348)). Treat them as never having gone live.
- **Opting out of PvP on PvE realms:** don't turn on Warmode, don't attack the other faction, avoid PvP quests, and keep your flag off. Battlegrounds and the Blood Ring are opt-in ([Pepesmite (staff), 2023-09-20](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=65951#p65951)).

## Dampening and resilience

- **Dampening in 1.18.0** was flat: player physical damage -15%, magical damage -20%, incoming player healing -20%, absorbs -15%, and mana costs -20% in battlegrounds and arenas ([Jamey (staff), 2025-06-10](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=134968#p134968)).
- **Dampening in 1.18.1** depends on class and spec ([Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)). The numbers were never published, and players asked where to look them up [[d:priest#1488633439455281222]]. Players noticed a dampening change around 2026-04-03 [[d:druid#1489732660891877628]]. BM hunter pets were reported undampened [[d:hunter#1492842290719227914]].
- **Resilience** reduces critical damage and damage-over-time taken. Since December 2025 it cuts the whole critical hit, not just the bonus part ([Torta (staff), 2025-12-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=161356#p161356), [[d:paladin#1484443837576249404]]). In 2026 players said stacked resilience "basically turns crits into normal hits" [[d:shaman#1492727874082570391]], and that it hurts DoT classes most [[d:rogue#1491654819138633758]]. The older "only ~1.3% instead of 3%" bug report (#17804) [[d:pvp-general#1425591367932973089]] predates this fix.
- Per-class effects are on the class pages ([Warlock](classes/warlock/pvp.md), [Hunter](classes/hunter/pvp.md), [Mage](classes/mage/pvp.md)).

## Crashes and Deserter

- **Crash rejoin:** if you crash out of a battleground ("a patch or mod has caused an issue"), don't click the white error box. Open a new launcher (or a second wow.exe) and log in. You go back into the battleground with no Deserter penalty [[d:pvp-general#1433159918965362910]], [[d:pvp-general#1424336060258848849]].
- The trick reportedly didn't work for crashes in Kara40. A raid crash can also strip your consumables every time you die [[d:pvp-general#1424374599759691798]].
- These are pre-1.18.1 reports, and no 2026 post confirms or contradicts them.

## Cross-class kit

- **Engineering:** close to mandatory for PvP. Take reflectors, rocket boots and grenades [[d:pvp-general#1430975536468852869]]. Some players prefer Goblin boots to Gnomish [[d:pvp-general#1430976033527697488]]. See [Professions](professions.md).
- **Lesser Invulnerability Potion:** 6 seconds of immunity to physical damage on about a 2-minute cooldown. Casters use it heavily against melee [[d:pvp-general#1432948173634146487]], [[d:pvp-general#1432945315501183097]].
- **Race picks:**
  - Blind no longer counts as a poison, so Dwarf Stoneform doesn't remove it [[d:pvp-general#1426546515790270565]], [[d:pvp-general#1431723602612457552]].
  - Human Perception is rated the best racial against rogues [[d:pvp-general#1431722830164398170]].
  - The Orc racial was changed to shorten stuns, and some now call it the worst racial [[d:pvp-general#1431723602612457552]].
