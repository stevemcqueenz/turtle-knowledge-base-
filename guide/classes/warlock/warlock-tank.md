# Warlock tanking (niche)

**Recommendation:** warlock tanking is a **niche role, not a real tank spec**. "Warlock tanking is not a supported playstyle outside of a few niche fights like twin emps" [[d:warlock#1433199490776432640]]. The two jobs raids actually give warlocks are:

1. the **caster emperor on Twin Emperors** (AQ40), in shadow-resistance gear;
2. the **Bishop on the Kara40 Chess event** [[d:warlock#1437964606663299103]].

Beyond that, a geared warlock can tank 5-man dungeons as a "void tank". It needs good healers and a patient group.

Tanking got much harder in 1.18.0. Master Demonologist lost the Voidwalker's +20% healing received, which "helped make up for our lack of avoidance stats" [[d:warlock#1447993848247746712]]. Power Overwhelming now costs base health, which killed the old pre-pull trick [[d:warlock#1455660288048369912]]. The class designer reportedly reacted to the idea with "What? Warlock tank? No way that works" [[d:warlock#1452809525798109224]].

## How to play

The tank loop at level 60. The details are in [Pull and threat loop](#pull-and-threat-loop) (5-man), [Threat rotation](#threat-rotation) (Twin Emperors) and [Where it works](#where-it-works).

**5-man "void tank" (0/33/18, Wrathstone up):**

1. **Pull:** send the Voidwalker in with Torment, or run in and chain-DoT with Felstone up.
2. **First GCDs:**
    - Caster or melee pairs: Immolate every target, then tab Searing Pain.
    - Packs: DoT the group, then Hellfire once the mobs converge on you.
3. **Steady threat:** keep Immolate up and cast Searing Pain on the kill target. Every fire spell gets the Wrathstone threat bonus.
4. **Multi-mob threat:** Hellfire keeps the pack. Health Funnel, Felstone, Healthstone and Voidwalker Sacrifice are your only AoE healing-threat tools.
5. **No taunt, so no threat recovery:** keep the group's burst in check. Death Coil or Fear a loose mob that heads for the healer. Against kicks, bait the interrupt and hold threat with a fire wand plus Immolate.
6. **Defensive rules:** Soul Link and the Voidwalker's Master Demonologist bonus carry your damage reduction. Keep the Voidwalker alive with Health Funnel, and use Healthstone. **Contested:** whether potions such as Stoneshield make it practical (see the table above).
7. **Avoid:** tanking before you have Wrathstone, Bane and Soul Link; any content with frequent silences or school lockouts (Stratholme); treating vampirism healing as threat.

**Twin Emperors caster tank (0/17/34, shadow resistance):** after each teleport you need about 5k threat within a few seconds. Cast Immolate → Conflagrate → Searing Pain spam, with Shadow Ward on cooldown. Curse of Doom is an optional burst. Never cast Shadow Bolt, because it has no pushback protection. Park your pet in the middle of the room.

## Twin Emperors: shadow-resistance tank

You tank **Vek'lor**, the caster, whose Shadow Bolts need shadow resistance.

### Shadow resistance

- About **210-220 total shadow resistance** removes the full-damage (non-partial) results from Shadow Bolt. After that, hits cap around 3k [[d:warlock#1472991817623666789]], [[d:warlock#1483897559482237008]].
- **Contested:** some players want 315. L-star calls that "huge overkill" [[d:warlock#1472981481986195597]].
- Gear: the vanilla shadow-resistance set, the Argent Dawn exalted trinket, and Jewelcrafting +8 resistance enchants on rings and neck [[d:warlock#1471907752774471823]].
- Cinamo's sum (1.18.0 tree): 221 from gear + 24 from 3/3 Demonic Aegis Demon Armor + 60 from Prayer of Shadow Protection (does not stack with Mark of the Wild) + 10 from Gift of Arthas = 315 [[d:warlock#1413054952183693343]].
- A Felhunter adds 60 more if you keep it out. The pure-Destruction build gives it up, and with it Soul Link's damage transfer, but gets "crazy threat" [[d:warlock#1475628330479915273]].

### Threat rotation

1. You need about **5k threat within a few seconds** of each teleport [[d:warlock#1483897719197270167]].
2. With Wrathstone and enough resistance: **Immolate → Conflagrate → Searing Pain spam** [[d:warlock#1471908188596076697]].
3. Do not cast Shadow Bolt. It has no pushback protection; Searing Pain does [[d:warlock#1429150214811549817]].
4. **Shadow Ward** on cooldown [[d:warlock#1429150214811549817]].
5. Optional: cast **Curse of Doom** when you pick the boss up after a teleport (or right after the pull Immolate) for extra burst threat [[d:warlock#1429159977519677461]].
6. Keep your pet in the middle of the room, where the raid stacks, to keep it out of the extra damage [[d:warlock#1452223748701818922]].

Summonjutsu's estimate: at 800 spell damage with an Improved-Stones Wrathstone, Searing Pain and Immolate alone generate about 1,200-1,500 threat per second [[d:warlock#1431100232195641478]].

### Gear and stats

- **Armor:** pick higher-armor cloth; armor kits are "cheap and effective for easy early PDR" [[d:warlock#1489010489030606961]].
- **Pushback protection** on Searing Pain and Immolate. Intensity plus the rank-8 PvP gloves reach 105% on Searing Pain [[d:warlock#1378758822390140970]]. The talent plus a paladin's Concentration Aura reaches 100% [[d:warlock#1489012201384579274]].
- **Stamina.** Vampirism gear does **not** generate threat, so do not count it as a threat tool [[d:warlock#1455660770045071423]].
- **Hit** matters for tanking bosses beyond Kara10/ZG-tier content [[d:warlock#1396294018627014767]].

### Pull and threat loop

1. Send the Voidwalker in with Torment, or run in and chain-DoT with Felstone up [[d:warlock#1400959800006676551]].
2. When the pack converges on you, **Hellfire** [[d:warlock#1400959800006676551]].
3. On caster or melee pairs (e.g. 2-3 ogres in BRD), Immolate them all and tab Searing Pain [[d:warlock#1396515922520440964]].
4. Your only AoE *healing* threat tools are Felstone, Healthstone, Voidwalker Sacrifice and Health Funnel [[d:warlock#1455661132072095744]]. Health Funnel creates AoE healing threat [[d:warlock#1455659619736227850]].
5. Against interrupts (LBRS), bait the kick and hold threat with a fire wand plus Immolate [[d:warlock#1379816702786801864]].
6. If you lose a mob, you cannot taunt it back. Keep your group's burst in check and use Death Coil or Fear on the loose mob.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to tank without Wrathstone, Soul Link and pushback protection.
- Casting Shadow Bolt as the Twins tank.
- Expecting vampirism or Felstone healing to hold threat.
- Tanking anything with frequent silences or interrupts.
- Forgetting to recast Demon Armor after changing Demonic Aegis points.
