# Warlock leveling (1-60)

**Recommendation:** level as **Affliction** with the **Voidwalker** tanking. Open with Curse of Agony and Corruption, add Siphon Life on mobs that live long enough, then drain or wand. Juggle 2-3 mobs at once (Voidwalker on one, Fear on one, you on one). Pick **Demonology** instead if this is your first warlock or a Hardcore character. Pick **Fire** only if you mostly run dungeons and can buy spell-power gear. The Affliction talent order below ends exactly on the raid build, so you need no respec at 60.

"Aff for speed, demo for safety" [[d:warlock#1467629269302775993]], [[d:warlock#1470954483730219049]]. Warlocks level so well that you can reach 60 with points spent almost at random, or none at all [[d:warlock#1482484924572368937]], [[d:warlock#1465202788857548871]].

| Spec | When | Why | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Affliction** (default) | Normal leveling, open-world farming | The lowest downtime; faster than Demonology; 10% hit from Suppression | [[d:warlock#1427635265689751583]], [[d:warlock#1449880425072824371]], [[d:warlock#1482498807831728288]] |
| **Demonology** | First warlock, Hardcore, soloing elites | "Slowest but safe and easy": the Voidwalker is tankier and holds threat more consistently, and Demonology relies on stamina and Intellect rather than spell power | [[d:warlock#1449880425072824371]], [[d:warlock#1482497977531498572]], [[d:warlock#1450484253392371753]] |
| **Destruction (Fire)** | Dungeon-heavy leveling on an alt with spell-power gear | Burns elites fast for groups [[d:warlock#1488950593304137951]], but it is mana-hungry, pulls threat off your demon, and gets Soul Fire only at 30 and Conflagrate at 40 [[d:warlock#1478893299388649583]] | [[d:warlock#1471269366153609504]] |

**Contested:** Dreamhaze calls anything but Demonology "pointless" for leveling, because a funnel-healed Voidwalker makes every elite "just a stamina check" [[d:warlock#1441086201783128136]], [[d:warlock#1441087350523564232]]. Most other posters recommend Affliction. For the 1.18.1 "Trial of Heroism" (elite-only XP) challenge, sailorVEVO recommends Affliction for its 10% hit [[d:warlock#1482498807831728288]].

Notes on the order:

- **Levels 10-22** (instant Corruption, hit, Life Tap, drains, Fel Concentration) are the near-universal first picks for every warlock [[d:warlock#1315778311959351417]], [Eversongwoods, 2025-02-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=118774#p118774). Crash.Rush's post-1.18.1 Affliction leveler had 13 points by level 22: Suppression 1, Improved Corruption 5, Improved Life Tap 2, Improved Drains 2, Improved Curse of Agony 3 [[d:warlock#1487115677218115665]].
- **Siphon Life (30)** plus **Malediction (31):** Malediction is mostly mana-saving until you learn Curse of Shadows at 44. Until then, trigger it with a rank-1 Curse of Recklessness [[d:warlock#1459978871561650381]]. Rank-1 Recklessness costs 35 mana and still applies your max-rank Curse of Agony [[d:warlock#1446533749415346187]].
- **Dark Harvest (40)** is weak while leveling, because mobs die too fast [[d:warlock#1435341566393843712]]. Use it on elites.
- **Shadow Vulnerability (45-49)** is a damage gain, but "too inconsistent to give it priority when leveling" [[d:warlock#1490766802442125382]].
- **Levels 50-60** buy the Demonic Sacrifice dip for raiding. Until you sacrifice at 60, Demonic Embrace (stamina) is the only part that helps while leveling. If you want to keep leveling with a pet past 50, spend those points on Demonology pet talents instead and respec at 60.
- **Skip while leveling:** Unleashed Potential ("it doesn't do anything" without crit gear) [[d:warlock#1442890233724141608]]; Sinister Pursuit, Improved Curse of Weakness and Resilient Shadows unless you PvP [[d:warlock#1455489313587466262]].
- **Optional swaps:** for Hardcore or kiting elites, take **Curse of Exhaustion** and **Improved Curse of Exhaustion**: "cheap, spammable, no mob limit" [[d:warlock#1461243133403861003]]. You could take them in place of Grim Reach and Suppression 4-5. Bones takes 2 points in Suppression early instead of the first Nightfall point for fewer resists [[d:warlock#1389932786445713429]].
- **Contested — Demonic Sacrifice while leveling:** some Affliction levelers sacrifice the Voidwalker or Felhunter and face-tank with Siphon Life and Felstone [[d:warlock#1435341566393843712]], [[d:warlock#1457856394010038566]]. L-star says to keep the demon out and not sacrifice [[d:warlock#1434098671196307637]]. Lightemgee argues a sacrificed demon wastes "a second fighting ally" [[d:warlock#1490791390601412738]].

## Pull and kill loop (Affliction, bot-ready)

1. **Send the Voidwalker** (Torment on autocast). Send it to the *next* mob a few seconds before the current one dies, so it builds a threat lead [[d:warlock#1490124228727537755]].
2. **Curse of Agony**, or from 44 **Curse of Shadows** with Malediction [[d:warlock#1429880192163250319]].
3. **Corruption** (instant from level 14).
4. **Siphon Life** if the mob will live long enough [[d:warlock#1429880384040210454]].
5. **Filler:** below level ~34, wand; above that, Drain Life when you need health or Drain Soul for damage [[d:warlock#1490122722783789229]], [[d:warlock#1429880698247975024]]. A level-15 dummy test: CoA → Corruption → Drain Soul held 31.5 DPS sustained, vs 23 with Drain Life and 33 falling to 28 with Shadow Bolt (mana) [[d:warlock#1462037192196096116]].
6. **Nightfall proc → Shadow Bolt.** Otherwise do not hard-cast Shadow Bolt while leveling [[d:warlock#1434098671196307637]].
7. **Life Tap** when you are near full health and below ~50% mana [[d:warlock#1457856394010038566]].
8. **Next mob:** repeat. The Voidwalker's regeneration means you rarely stop [[d:warlock#1427636244212617307]].

**Two or three mobs:** DoT all of them first, then Fear one. Fear breaks on new spell applications, not on DoT ticks, so DoT *before* you Fear [[d:warlock#1387410120182599772]], [[d:warlock#1490117470231461951]]. Keep one on the Voidwalker, one feared, one on you [[d:warlock#1490122825787510875]]. **Curse of Recklessness stops a feared mob from fleeing**; swap to another curse to let it run again [[d:warlock#1490139514566344975]].

Never apply Immolate early in the loop. Immolate is dropped once you have Siphon Life (Crash.Rush stopped at 33) [[d:warlock#1487115677218115665]], and it is never part of the endgame Affliction rotation.

**Demonology loop:** send the Voidwalker, DoT, then Drain Life or wand. Pull the next mob with the Voidwalker once the current one is at half health; Soul Funnel heals it for free [[d:warlock#1441086565534273666]], [[d:warlock#1441087350523564232]].

## Resources and downtime

- **No Dark Pact on Turtle** — the biggest difference from vanilla leveling guides [[d:warlock#1490751044295065693]]. Mana comes from Life Tap; health comes back from Drain Life, Siphon Life and Felstone.
- **Wand vs spells:** **Contested.** "0 reason to wand after like level 20" [[d:warlock#1462085166829797541]] vs "Voidwalker + dots and wand = infinite farm and no aggro" [[d:warlock#1472625100124852244]].
- **Spirit:** **Contested.** "Spirit has 0 value" [[d:warlock#1457542304297648332]]; Sarimal says it helps between pulls outside Hardcore [[d:warlock#1457761351404617739]]; Life Tap keeps you inside the 5-second rule so Spirit rarely kicks in [[d:warlock#1457773817320706243]].
- Keep **Demon Armor** up [[d:warlock#1472625100124852244]].

## Dungeons and group role

- Bring the **Imp** (ranged, stamina buff, Fire Shield, Phase Shift) [[d:warlock#1455572117692354725]], or the **Succubus**; both stay invisible and pull less by accident than the Voidwalker [[d:warlock#1485668056544841840]].
- **Rain of Fire** is the safer AoE; **Hellfire** does more damage but can kill you if you pull threat [[d:warlock#1454137182964154494]].
- Fire Destruction is a "godsend" for groups that want elites burned fast [[d:warlock#1488950593304137951]].
- A Voidwalker with the funnel talents can tank or off-tank low-level dungeons, but it is not beginner-friendly and has trouble with multiple mobs [[d:warlock#1472406090791780585]].
- With enough mana, Affliction can drag 4-5 mobs with Curse of Exhaustion and DoTs [[d:warlock#1452397652628209725]].
- Named leveling-dungeon pickups: the SM Library dagger, SM Graveyard trinket and BFD wand (above) [[d:warlock#1451816965072097432]]. The sources give no full dungeon-by-bracket list.

## AoE leveling and farming (40-60)

- Close to level 50, heavy Demonology into Destruction can AoE-level [[d:warlock#1488834501370056754]]. Otherwise you juggle 2-4 targets with Fear and the pet [[d:warlock#1488834636430708887]].
- **Hyjal snakes** (44-60): vampirism gear, full Demonology, the Maraudon scepter, run right, invisibility potion past the hydra, then Hellfire the snakes pack by pack [[d:warlock#1488835475450892401]]. About 8% vampirism from gear plus the Imp's Felstone buff (14% total) is enough [[d:warlock#1492168429497614609]].
- **Dire Maul East lashers** (58+): enchant rings and neck with vampirism for about 9% with Improved Felstone [[d:warlock#1439250280465305691]]. Tyrona's pull guide covers line-of-sight pulls past the death lashes [[d:warlock#1439256559770271754]].
- **Affliction** farms open-world packs: dot 3-4 mobs, slow them with Curse of Exhaustion and kite [[d:warlock#1464039321358176387]].

## Hardcore

- **Demonology** is the safe pick for a first Hardcore warlock [[d:warlock#1482498807831728288]], [[d:warlock#1450484253392371753]].
- **Soul Funnel 2/2** lets you solo elites head-on when there is no room to fear-kite [[d:warlock#1474062678644232374]].
- Take Curse of Exhaustion plus its improvement [[d:warlock#1461243133403861003]].
- Rank-1 Curse of Recklessness is the cheap anti-flee tool [[d:warlock#1446533749415346187]].
- Krampus and Xandivious are immune to Enslave Demon [[d:warlock#1448126201737777354]].

## Common mistakes

- Opening with Immolate, or hard-casting Shadow Bolt, instead of DoT + wand/drain.
- Clipping Curse of Agony early; its biggest ticks are at the end [[d:warlock#1426230149959127154]].
- Fearing before you DoT, so the DoT application breaks the Fear.
- Letting the Voidwalker fall behind on threat. Send it ahead a few seconds early.
- Taking Unleashed Potential, Sinister Pursuit or Improved Curse of Weakness while leveling.
- Buying pre-raid "BiS" lists at 50 instead of Shadoweave and "of Shadow Wrath" greens.
- Running out of soul shards for Soul Fire, Healthstones and resummons. Shards stack to 3 per slot [[d:warlock#1439148601224859680]].
