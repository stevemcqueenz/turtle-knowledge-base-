# Warlock professions

**Recommendation:** level with a **gathering pair** (Mining + Skinning, or Herbalism + Alchemy), then run **Engineering + Alchemy** (or Engineering + Herbalism) at 60. Engineering is "the no-brainer choice" for endgame and Alchemy "is always good" [[d:warlock#1459733829911711796]], [[d:warlock#1462379476326551625]]. Tailoring is optional. Its one bind-on-pickup payoff is Mooncloth for T3/T3.5, and its best warlock pieces are Bind on Equip, so you can buy them [[d:warlock#1451703869968224377]], [[d:warlock#1451704588708085852]]. If you take Jewelcrafting, choose **Gemology**, not Goldsmithing [[d:warlock#1427499791755116717]], [[d:warlock#1430691620008886382]].

## What each profession gives a warlock

### Engineering

- **The consensus endgame profession.** The main value is Goblin Sapper Charges, the Battle Chicken, and reflectors and grenades for PvP [[d:warlock#1459945410754777183]]. Sappers are "very good" on the Viscidus fight and on Naxx spider trash, and they add a big damage boost when 20+ players use them [[d:warlock#1459945548084674623]]. The Destruction page lists sappers for Naxx spider trash ([Destruction](destruction.md)).
- **Pre-raid head:** the Karazhan-10 engineering helm is an alternative to Crown of Molten Ascension (single source) [[d:warlock#1445941174845309031]].
- **"Selfish" profession:** most of what you make is usable only by engineers, so it has little market value [[d:warlock#1409460988142686310]]. Mima calls it "more so a PvP thing"; in a hardcore endgame guild "sapper charges are a must" [[d:warlock#1419960348370534450]].
- **Gnomish or Goblin:** to min-max, take Gnomish until you have crafted every Gnomish-only item, then unlearn and relearn as Goblin. If you pick only one, take Goblin for sappers (single source) [[d:warlock#1456440940674810089]], [[d:warlock#1456441246049501265]].
- **Training gate:** past skill 150 you need an Artisan-or-higher trainer, for example Roxxik in Orgrimmar, to learn Expert Engineering [[d:warlock#1489409623713779732]], [[d:warlock#1489410399588843631]].

### Alchemy (and Herbalism)

- Make your own consumables. Karen takes Alchemy "for ease of consumes", but an alt can do that job too [[d:warlock#1462377352003715240]]. The raid consumable list (Dreamshard Elixir, Elixir of Shadow Power, Greater Arcane Elixir, Major Mana Potion, protection potions and more) is on [Affliction](affliction.md) [[d:warlock#1427210967053373501]].
- **Transmute cooldown:** "the transmute cd is nice" [[d:warlock#1424719197388144680]]. Alts on transmute and Mooncloth cooldowns are the passive-income plan (see [Gold](#gold)).
- Level-35 mana potions are a cheap Alchemy skill-up, so they are cheap on the AH [[d:warlock#1460311716511350814]].

### Tailoring

- **What is worth it:** Mooncloth for T3 and T3.5 [[d:warlock#1451704588708085852]]. The **Core Felcloth Bag** (MC materials) is the endgame soul bag: it holds shards and demon-summoning reagents, and its reagent-refund chance is 25% [[d:warlock#1446170701622022285]], [[d:warlock#1486424794508431484]], [[d:warlock#1434554589843296416]].
- **BoE, so buy it:** the tailored cloak is good for Demonology and Destruction, and the gloves are BiS for Affliction [[d:warlock#1451703869968224377]]. The haste tailoring gloves from Karazhan-10 are a pre-raid pick [[d:warlock#1445944759339319337]], [[d:warlock#1485892018659000401]]. The Dire Maul-reputation tailored cloak is still used in Kara40 on Demonology (single source) [[d:warlock#1448504100030382266]].
- **BoP robe:** "not like you need it" [[d:warlock#1451703869968224377]]. Some pre-raid prebis items only make sense for a tailor [[d:warlock#1454070157172281354]].
- **Bloodvine** is weak now. Its hit moved onto the individual pieces and its set passive is "not very good" [[d:warlock#1455364169061761241]], [[d:warlock#1455364257167441981]]. Asked about Bloodvine for a non-tailor, biloen runs 3-piece T1 with off-pieces and suggests the Kara10 chest [[d:warlock#1485677363239850156]], [[d:warlock#1485677688302862501]].
- **Leveling:** the **Shadoweave** set lasts from level 37 to 60; buy it if you are not a tailor [[d:warlock#1482501029407756318]].
- **Against it:** "Every guild has someone who can help you make things" [[d:warlock#1424721243248136263]]. Tailoring also uses the same cloth as First Aid bandages, and warlocks lean on bandages for downtime [[d:warlock#1409459823342260338]]. If you do both, level Tailoring and First Aid together, one cloth tier at a time [[d:warlock#1424795592567160867]].

### Jewelcrafting (Turtle custom)

- **Gemology over Goldsmithing.** Gemology makes ring and neck enchants that "always will be in demand". Goldsmithing makes "overly expensive prebis items" that get replaced, and it "becomes increasingly ass as the game progresses" [[d:warlock#1427499791755116717]], [[d:warlock#1430691620008886382]], [[d:warlock#1451728395531452546]]. sailorVEVO: level Goldsmithing only "when ur rich" [[d:warlock#1427500686236450836]]. Enchanted gemstones work "like enchants": anyone can use them, with no specialisation needed — [Akalix (staff), 2025-06-19](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/976193120809123881/1385337387206578301).
- **Warlock uses:** hit rings and neck with JC enchants [[d:warlock#1475629087467765760]]; +8 resistance jewelry enchants for tank-lock resistance sets [[d:warlock#1471907752774471823]]; vampirism enchants on rings and neck for the lasher farm [[d:warlock#1439250280465305691]], [[d:warlock#1492169196166189196]]; a 2% haste JC neck (single source) [[d:warlock#1446866573691191517]].
- Pair it with **Mining** to supply your own materials for staves, rings and trinkets [[d:warlock#1409459823342260338]]. It is expensive to level, but Gemology "will start making money back eventually" [[d:warlock#1427500322120667287]].

### Enchanting

- **Not for leveling.** Mirror lists the problems: it is click-heavy, it needs rods, the Artisan trainer is inside an elite instance, and it only becomes profitable at the very end [[d:warlock#1384527459050852442]].
- At 60 it makes gold if you advertise in chat [[d:warlock#1442670711004074048]]. Vampirism bracer and boot enchants help the lasher-farm set [[d:warlock#1492169260179394602]].

### Leatherworking, Mining, Skinning

- Cured Rugged Hides sell to players buying T3 tokens; Mooncloth and Arcanite Bars "always sell" (single source) [[d:warlock#1482004168993542207]], [[d:warlock#1482004369577873581]].
- Mining + Skinning is the "keep gold flowing to buy consumables" option [[d:warlock#1419960348370534450]].

### Survival and Gardening (secondary professions)

- **1.18.1 rework (official):** Survival now "draws from other primary professions". It has 80+ new recipes, tools that slow, root or trap opponents, bags for the three gathering professions, and Woodcutting — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). Player-reported crafts include Throwable Net, Slowing Bolas, Nutritious Rations, gathering trinkets and a slingshot for ranged pulls — [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2026-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172915#p172915), [Atreidon (player), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172086#p172086). The 1–300 route and trainers are in the [Professions overview](../../professions.md).
- **Warlock channel:** one PvP player says he needs "to level Survival for those nets and other stuff", to handle melee that reach him (single source) [[d:warlock#1487477400106434622]]. The channel does not otherwise discuss Survival or Gardening for warlocks. Take Survival for open-world and PvP nets/bolas if you want them; it has no known raid value.
- **Tents and rested XP (1.18.1):** kills now give 50% rested bonus (was 100%), and tents only fill up to 25% rested — [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). Before 1.18.1, tents gave full rested bonus.
- **Gardening:** needs Survival 75 — [official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/survival-and-gardening). The 1.18.1 overhaul reset the Gardening quest — [Sylveria (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171863#p171863). The warlock channel mentions it only as an alternative to farming [[d:warlock#1465770577125183609]].

## When to pick up and swap

1. **1–60:** a gathering pair (Mining + Skinning, or Herbalism + Alchemy) [[d:warlock#1369943361254850593]], [[d:warlock#1369946819341058088]]. Avoid Enchanting while leveling [[d:warlock#1384527459050852442]]. Buy the Shadoweave set instead of leveling Tailoring for it [[d:warlock#1482501029407756318]].
2. **Around 58–60:** keep Herbalism if you plan to farm the Dire Maul East lashers [[d:warlock#1465755008225251454]].
3. **At 60, raiding:** drop the second gatherer for Engineering. Ghost9626 dropped 300 Enchanting for Engineering [[d:warlock#1439421055432261763]]. Keep Alchemy, or move it to an alt [[d:warlock#1462377352003715240]].
4. **Engineering specialisation:** Gnomish first, then relearn as Goblin (single source) [[d:warlock#1456440940674810089]].

## Race interactions

- **Goblin:** Goblin-only engineering recipes (rocket boots). The trick is to level as a Gnome engineer, craft the BoP items, then switch to Goblin (mage channel, single source) [[d:mage#1488174450477240420]]. Goblin **Prospecting** grants Mining and Jewelcrafting skill — [Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102965#p102965).
- The warlock channel names no other race-profession interaction.

## Gold

- **Dire Maul East lashers** (58+) is the standard warlock farm. With Herbalism for the Dreamfoil it earns about 80–100 g/h on Ambershire, capped at 4 instance resets per hour [[d:warlock#1465755008225251454]], [[d:warlock#1465755128031219929]], [[d:warlock#1490300232930427093]]. **Contested:** one player calls it "generally shit" now that herb prices are low [[d:warlock#1455742043643969751]].
- **Herbs to an Alchemy alt:** make your own consumables and sell the rest [[d:warlock#1434729488171667537]].
- **Cooldown alts:** "5 or more lvl 35 alts with transmute/mooncloth cds" earn a "passive" 200–300 g a week (single source) [[d:warlock#1434730417017327709]]. L-star farms Hyjal satyrs for Felcloth to feed his Mooncloth alts [[d:warlock#1470887952124215529]].
- **Other farms:** a solo ZG route with the Imp (about 200 g/h) [[d:warlock#1466553667996876966]]; Hyjal demon satyrs as Affliction for cloth and shards [[d:warlock#1479253647144517834]]; Moonwhisper Coast elementals [[d:warlock#1492123161888817222]]; MC/ZG raid weeklies [[d:warlock#1491190242328445211]]. Warlock gold-farming is generally rated below hunter, mage and paladin [[d:warlock#1470887370629976186]].
- **Mount:** the level-60 Dreadsteed quest includes epic riding for about 400–450 g, much less than other classes pay. Another warlock can supply materials and save you about 250 g [[d:warlock#1489054932010139688]], [[d:warlock#1494819680614088744]].
