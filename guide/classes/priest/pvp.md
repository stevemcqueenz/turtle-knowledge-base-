# Priest PvP

**Recommendation:** for solo and small-group PvP, play **Discipline (Smite)** for burst and shields, or **Shadow** for sustained pressure and near-undispellable DoTs. Pick by matchup: "both are viable imo. Play what suits you better" [[d:priest#1487776350658891838]]. Do not solo-queue as Holy: "you spend 50% of your time looking at a black & white screen" [[d:priest#1487505164675059807]]. Gear for **stamina**, cap at **3% hit**, and expect a burst meta.

## Spec overview

| Spec | PvP standing | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Discipline (Smite)** | Favoured by many priest PvPers | The highest burst (Holy Fire into instant Smites), stronger shields and off-heals [[d:priest#1487776350658891838]]; ally Chastise breaks Sap and Blind [Isvya, 2024-10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103744#p103744) | More exposed to Curse of Tongues and melee [[d:priest#1487776350658891838]]; off-healing feels weak against Mortal Strike-type debuffs without a 30% healing/mana talent [[d:pvp-general#1432871628789780491]] |
| **Shadow** | Viable | SW:P plus Shadow Weaving applies six debuffs at once, so it is very hard to dispel [[d:priest#1485255007283581048]]; VE sustain; Silence; fear | Short Mind Flay range (20 yd), unreliable stuns, one damage school [[d:priest#1487776350658891838]]; 1.18.1 dampening and resilience cut both crits and DoTs, and VE healing is reduced twice [[d:priest#1491433404577742929]], [[d:priest#1488633169107484733]] |
| **Holy** | BG healer only | Dispels, shields, Ascendance works as a second CC break [[d:priest#1486259006413738166]] | Cannot win fights alone [[d:priest#1485371591184482334]], [[d:priest#1487505164675059807]] |

Both damage specs "suffer from overtuned enhancers and rogues mostly" [[d:priest#1487776350658891838]]. Rogues are the matchup priests complain about most [[d:priest#1426334545799806986]].

Pre-1.18.1 tier lists from `#pvp-general` disagree with each other. One post-CC2 list put Smite in A, Shadow in B and Holy in C [[d:pvp-general#1353324094954934314]]; a 1.18-preview list put Priest in B [[d:pvp-general#1405599106486767717]]. That channel ends in 2025-11, so it has no post-1.18.1 data.

`#pvp-general` also shows Priest was seen as one of the least-played PvP classes on Ambershire ("9/10 games not a priest on horde team") [[d:pvp-general#1423679614970101874]], and Holy struggled to heal through melee 1v1 [[d:pvp-general#1423695441161359392]] — yet other posters called Priest (Smite and Holy alike) overtuned. One tier list put Shadow Priest in the top "Z tier" with Shaman, Rogue, Mage and Paladin, and Smite Priest in "S tier"; a reply suggested Holy Priest belonged in F tier instead [[d:pvp-general#1426339548039872532]], [[d:pvp-general#1426343489796440155]]. Ythor separately rated Smite Priest S tier [[d:pvp-general#1429944347834581072]], and Temu called Smite "more insane than Shaman" on the Tel'Abim server, citing priests "regularly going like 37-3 in AB" [[d:pvp-general#1431213840711942154]], [[d:pvp-general#1431213919019470889]]; Bipst disputed the Smite ranking specifically ("no, smite priest is not s tier. Healer priest is good though") [[d:pvp-general#1430015057277751360]]. On the healer side, dindunuffinz called Priest an overtuned healer — deceptively tanky, spammable shields, and 500-600 Renew ticks that cancel DoTs — citing one geared Horde priest who reportedly did 1,000,000 healing in a single AV and felt "more tanky than any alliance warrior" [[d:pvp-general#1429497713585815652]], [[d:pvp-general#1429499728416211104]], corroborated by a self-described unbiased PvE player [[d:pvp-general#1429446787260743843]]. Ythor also flagged a faction imbalance in fear uptime: Horde priests can chain-fear melee much more reliably than Alliance priests [[d:pvp-general#1429514825221931039]].

## Stats and gear

- **Hit: 3%** against level-60 players is enough, so spare Shadow Focus points can go elsewhere [[d:priest#1400568275561087046]]. **Contested:** 3% [Heroclastus, 2024-10-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102752#p102752) vs 4% [Zvyrhol, 2024-10-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102656#p102656).
- **Stamina > Intellect > Spirit** for a big health and mana pool [[d:priest#1472313528693035219]]. "Stamina is your only defence" [Springboards, 2024-07-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98805#p98805).
- **Burst meta:** "in twow you have burst meta anyways, so you need kara gear for pvp" [[d:priest#1451623968057331732]]. The 1.18.1 PvP T2/T3 sets have *less* stamina and armor than the old rank 12-14 sets [[d:priest#1487406580851806249]], [[d:priest#1487471946298949722]].
- **Power Word: Shield scales with healing power.** Disc PvPers who want bigger shields trade damage for +healing [[d:priest#1493738200147955824]], [Gottabe, 2026-03-31](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173328#p173328).
- **Honor-vendor catch-up on Nordanaar:** Ornate Bloodstone Dagger, Spellwoven Nobility Drape (11 SP, 1% hit), and assorted "of Shadow Wrath" pieces [[d:priest#1451628084242550825]], [[d:priest#1451628390548242645]].
- **Weapons:** Soulseeker (−25 target resistance) for Smite [Zvyrhol, 2024-10-06](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102656#p102656). For Night Elves, a bow is PvP-only utility; Time Frozen Bow is called BiS [Blackduck4, 2025-11-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=159517#p159517), [[d:priest#1473445850352058461]]. The community-nicknamed "BR dagger" (also BiS for Rogues and Mages) is also "very good" for Priests [[d:pvp-general#1433361183188189237]].
- **Trinkets:** the PvP trinket now removes all effects, not just class-specific ones [[d:priest#1440676406890594434]]. Reflectors counter specific classes [[d:pvp-general#1360188212584382646]]. A rogue's Shadow Reflector hard-counters Shadow [[d:priest#1485553306876252212]].
- **Enchants:** no PvP-specific priest enchant list was posted (gap). Use stamina and spell power versions of the PvE enchants.
- **Gear gap:** a forum thread flagged that Priests have no dedicated heal+ PvP set (Druids lack a leather healing PvP set too) [[d:pvp-general#1425076941265965118]].

## Burst and control sequences

### Disc (Smite)

1. Pre-fight: Inner Fire, Enlighten, and Power Word: Shield on yourself.
2. **Opener:** self-`Chastise` → `Inner Fire` → `Holy Fire` (hoping for a crit) → spam `Smite`, using each instant Smite from Searing Light [[d:priest#1428000781478395964]].
3. Instant Smites have a 1.5 s internal cooldown. Weave another spell between them [[d:priest#1452013492961021983]]. One player clocked a Smite-proc chain doing about 5k damage in 3 seconds [[d:pvp-general#1432866944234815609]].
4. Against melee and casters: keep Psychic Scream or Chastise for when a mage channels Arcane Missiles or a warlock casts Dark Harvest. Otherwise shield, dispel, spam Holy Nova and fish for instant Smites [[d:priest#1462905759712018432]].
5. **Chastise as CC or as damage:** if you disorient the target with Chastise, follow with Smite, not Holy Fire. Holy Fire's DoT breaks the disorient [Kobiq, 2024-12-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111295#p111295).
6. **Ally Chastise:** use it to break Sap or Blind on a teammate [Isvya, 2024-10-19](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=103744#p103744). Chastise priority for haste: affliction warlocks and arcane mages > two-hand melee > dual-wield melee [[d:priest#1383364969432875028]].

### Shadow

1. `Power Word: Shield` on yourself → `Vampiric Embrace` → `Shadow Word: Pain` (with Shadow Weaving it lands as six debuffs, very hard to dispel; players suspect this is unintended) [[d:priest#1485255007283581048]], [[d:priest#1485255166646030368]].
2. `Mind Blast` → `Mind Flay` from 20 yd. Against warriors, skip Mind Blast unless it kills: crits give them rage [[d:priest#1485369919938887893]]. With around 450 spell power, Mind Flay reportedly ticks for 500+ damage, and Blackout can proc back-to-back stuns while you channel it — a common melee complaint [[d:pvp-general#1432865502086168700]], [[d:pvp-general#1432865528673992787]], [[d:pvp-general#1432882324210585760]].
3. `Pain Spike` as the finisher: "outlast ~80% and then finish you off with an instant" [Atreidon, 2025-04-01](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124839#p124839).
4. `Psychic Scream` when melee reaches you. Expect trinkets, WotF and Fear Ward. Against a Fury warrior, bait their Berserker Rage (fear/root break, ~30 s cooldown, Battle Stance only) with a Fear, then land an engineering Net right after; Arms warriors lack that break and can be Netted or Feared immediately, but the Fury talent Deathwish reportedly grants fear immunity in any stance [[d:pvp-general#1432168724714229770]], [[d:pvp-general#1432169106680840213]], [[d:pvp-general#1432169367419883642]].
5. `Silence` aggressive casters such as a frost mage trying to Nova and Arcane Explosion a node [[d:pvp-general#1360941242695028736]].

Blackout is PvP-only, and its 2 s stun is considered "almost useless" [[d:priest#1424674352330444830]]. Pairing a Shadow priest with a destruction warlock gives strong burst [[d:priest#1483903073318469703]]. Some `#pvp-general` posters disputed that Shadow was overpowered at all, since it is "literally locked to a single school of spells unless they drop form" and needs an Affliction-style passive, range-abusing playstyle to be effective [[d:pvp-general#1432881878385557626]], [[d:pvp-general#1432882353730093057]].

## Battleground role

- **Healer:** a priest healer is especially valuable on Horde, which otherwise lacks defensive magic dispel [[d:pvp-general#1104024920343720007]]. Defensive dispel beats purge because it removes sheep, fear, DoTs, HoJ, Repentance and slows [[d:pvp-general#1073761850845573140]]. Enemies focus healers at once, so the priority is: keep yourself alive > keep others alive > DPS [[d:priest#1426927342332280923]].
- **Flag carriers:** Shadow's six-debuff SW:P is close to impossible to dispel off a flag carrier [[d:priest#1485287934339977258]]. A premade of 1 Rogue, 2 Priest, 2 Mage with the Mage running flag carrier was reported to win every WSG [[d:pvp-general#1429916187760005280]].
- **Support role:** without needing kills, a Priest's suggested job is to deny the enemy kills through dispels and heals, alongside Rogue peels (Sap/stun-combo/Blind) and Mage/Warlock CC support [[d:pvp-general#1432070443640033403]], [[d:pvp-general#1432069563406618725]], [[d:pvp-general#1432069743187329085]].
- **Stealth detection:** spam rank-1 Holy Nova to avoid being opened on [Itsbacon, 2025-08-08](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=143823#p143823).
- **Mana Burn:** use it on mana-hungry resto shamans and other healers, not on paladins (their heals are cheap) [[d:priest#1465782188216942645]], [[d:priest#1465785204026114217]]. Improved Mana Burn now sits deep in the Shadow tree, out of reach for Holy and Disc [[d:priest#1465620222806593557]].

## Macros

- Self-cast Chastise and keep your target: `/script CastSpellByName("Chastise", 1)` [[d:priest#1495029676945707050]].
- Chastise whatever you target, or yourself if the target is an enemy: `/script if UnitIsEnemy("target") then TargetUnit("player") CastSpellByName("Chastise") TargetLastEnemy() else CastSpellByName("Chastise") end` [Mosttrustworthy, 2025-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=121406#p121406).
- Chastise-request pair: the DPS sends `/w <priest> chastise me now!` and the priest uses `/target <name>` + `/cast Chastise` [[d:priest#1383364969432875028]].

## Common mistakes

- Solo-queueing as Holy [[d:priest#1487505164675059807]].
- Mind Blast crits on warriors [[d:priest#1485369919938887893]].
- Holy Fire after a Chastise disorient (the DoT breaks it) [Kobiq, 2024-12-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=111295#p111295).
- Wasting fear into a trinket, WotF or Fear Ward. The PvP trinket now removes everything [[d:priest#1440676406890594434]].
- Stacking PvE tier sets over stamina [Springboards, 2024-07-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=98805#p98805).
- Expecting any feedback from Unbreakable Will; it gives no notification when it resists a stun [[d:priest#1470579530178494535]].
