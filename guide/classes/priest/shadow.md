# Shadow Priest (ranged DPS)

**Recommendation:** raids bring one Shadow priest for Shadow Weaving (+15% Shadow damage taken) and for Vampiric Embrace healing and mana. Play **0/20/31** (Spiritual Guidance secondary). Reach **16% spell hit**, then stack **shadow spell power**; ignore haste. Keep **SW:P** up, cast **Mind Blast** on cooldown, and fill with **2 Mind Flays**, clipped by hand. Use VE only when the group actually needs mana or healing.

Why:

- **Spiritual Guidance** turns Spirit into spell power. There are not enough points for it and the threat talents together, so the standard build gives up threat reduction [[d:priest#1474088272291565693]].
- **Shadow Focus 3/5** works only if gear brings you to the 16% hit cap. If you are short on hit, use the progression variant below.
- **Spirit Tap** also triggers on Mind Blast crits since 1.18.1. In Kara40 BiS that is a ~90 spell power proc with about 50% uptime, mostly a mana gain [[d:priest#1475981714487771136]], [[d:priest#1465391740797194504]].
- **Improved Mind Blast:** at 4/5 the Mind Blast cooldown is exactly 6 s, the length of 2 Mind Flays. At 5/5 you must clip or end the second Flay early to start Mind Blast [[d:priest#1435036085083181076]], [[d:priest#1435035419053129749]].
- **Holy Nova 1/1** is your only AoE (see AoE).

## Stat priority and caps

1. **Spell hit to 16%** against raid bosses. That is 10% from 5/5 Shadow Focus plus 6% from gear, or 5% plus 1% from a druid's Emerald Blessing [[d:priest#1489093951494426686]], [[d:priest#1429309438174564412]]. At 3/5 Shadow Focus you need 10% from gear.
2. **Spell power** (shadow or general), your only real scaling stat [[d:priest#1436098795644387559]].
3. **Crit:** worth about 3-4 spell power per 1% in 1.18.1, because Mind Blast crits now trigger Spirit Tap [[d:priest#1493685228911333427]], [[d:priest#1493756785851633735]]. Before 1.18.1 it was about 2.5-3 spell power [[d:priest#1475220498450350210]]. Only Mind Blast (and Pain Spike) can crit; Mind Flay and SW:P cannot [[d:priest#1482571193390207036]].
4. **Spirit:** about 4 Spirit ≈ 1 spell power through Spiritual Guidance [[d:priest#1440808869797560330]].
5. **Haste: zero value.** Mind Flay's channel ignores it and it does not shorten the GCD [[d:priest#1450143690969317406]], [[d:priest#1462847606664466595]]. You would need about 13% haste for a fourth Mind Flay tick, which is not viable [[d:priest#1438517763936227338]].

**Spell penetration** matters only against high shadow resistance. Most bosses have 10-40 shadow resistance. Mephistroth has ~200 (~125 after Curse of Shadow), so you need about 75 more removed from gear. Kel'Thuzad has ~100 [[d:priest#1436138912954454148]], [[d:priest#1436470618920063036]]. Mind Flay ticks are binary (each fully lands or fully resists), so being under-penetrated costs you whole ticks [[d:priest#1436260006545719376]]. The formula used by the server core: `HitChance = (96 + SpellHit) × (1 − (Resist − Pen) × 0.15/60)` [[d:priest#1487710174436134943]].

## Single-target rotation

**Pre-pull:** Shadowform, Inner Fire, and Power Word: Shield if you expect pushback.

**Opener:** `Shadow Word: Pain` (plus `Vampiric Embrace` first only if the fight needs it) → `Mind Blast` → `Mind Flay` ×2 [[d:priest#1430465373366648873]], [[d:priest#1471593092183232739]].

Priority (bot-ready):

1. **`Shadow Word: Pain`** if missing and the target will live at least 3 ticks (~9 s) [[d:priest#1489637342825152522]]. Do not clip DoTs early [[d:priest#1468633694993649820]].
2. **`Mind Blast`** on cooldown, but watch threat. A 3.5k Mind Blast crit with VE running generates about 7,200 threat [[d:priest#1459705039181320296]], [[d:priest#1475984733321167092]].
3. **`Mind Flay`** as filler: 2 per Mind Blast cooldown [[d:priest#1451277154942718034]]. Clip the channel by hand right after the last tick you want. Watch the latency band on the cast bar to cancel safely into Mind Blast [[d:priest#1481909198819426459]]. Two 3-tick Flays are more mana-efficient than three 2-tick Flays [[d:priest#1463542235487408269]]. Never stop a Flay by moving; that delay is "REALLY bad" [[d:priest#1494662933056327902]].
4. **`Vampiric Embrace`** only when the group needs mana or healing. Never cast it while Fade is active, and rarely while a trinket is running [[d:priest#1491659277474074716]], [[d:priest#1485766258803867659]]. VE's healing threat is not reduced by Shadow Affinity [[d:priest#1475981605284614224]].
5. **`Fade` before a SW:P refresh** when you wear the T3.5 3-piece (Fade grants +15% Shadow damage for 10 s). With a snapshot-friendly trinket such as Eye of Dormant Corruption, this tested as a net DPS gain [[d:priest#1444088076384338121]], [[d:priest#1441870850482503710]]. On its own, the bonus mostly offsets the GCD Fade costs [[d:priest#1357206800369455124]].
6. **`Pain Spike`** as a finisher. Even as filler it is "better than 0 dps": most bosses reduce healing, so little of its self-heal-back actually lands [[d:priest#1474782366689136730]].
7. **`Devouring Plague`** (Undead): slightly less damage than SW:P for more mana, barely worth a GCD [[d:priest#1490748665776832815]].

**Clipping tools:** Zitronenhaze advises clipping by hand from the start [[d:priest#1494374717791010836]]. Nampower's "queue channeling spells" is easier but slower. **Contested:** Zitronenhaze measured about a 125 ms delay (trainable to ~50 ms) when clipping [[d:priest#1494659622181797990]]; Doomsvilla could not reproduce it on a current Nampower build [[d:priest#1494666651105493052]].

**Trinket order** (example: Remains + Essence + Scythe). Pop the passive-cooldown trinket about 30 s before the pull, then Essence on pull, then Scythe. Using Scythe first locks Essence for a minute [[d:priest#1464777512717648175]]. The Zandalarian Hero Charm lets you snapshot SW:P at your highest spell power [[d:priest#1456726981210275870]].

## AoE

Shadow has no real AoE [[d:priest#1472910100401360992]].

| Targets | Action |
|---|---|
| 2-4 | Single-target the kill target. SW:P a second mob only if it lives at least 3 ticks [[d:priest#1489637342825152522]]. Multi-dotting to carry Shadow Weaving costs 4+ GCDs per pack and rarely pays off in fast groups [[d:priest#1472913815174647820]]. |
| 5+ | `Holy Nova` spam (Holy school, needs the Holy points in the build), ideally in a crit and mp5 "Nova set". It is only worth its damage at 5+ mobs [[d:priest#1463297546909909187]]. Even in full Kara BiS you go OOM after ~9-10 Novas [[d:priest#1465011522345373922]]. |

Shadow tier sets carry shadow-only spell power, which makes Holy Nova even weaker in Shadow gear [[d:priest#1472808353603518781]]. "On spell hit" weapon procs (Blade of Eternal Darkness and similar) fire once per target Nova hits [[d:priest#1463593375658676314]]. On Garr and Rupturan add fights, AoE all adds evenly, then swap to a fire-immunity trinket just before they die [[d:priest#1454603179659362417]].

## Resources, threat and 1.18.1 mechanics

- **Shadowform** gives a passive 15% in-combat mana regen [[d:priest#1436363609696370829]]. Mana is still tight. Sustain means VE plus a mana potion and tea on cooldown, "or you would run dry" [[d:priest#1504891047463882844]]. Teas are cheap (about 1g for 5 from a vendor) and work like Demonic Runes [[d:priest#1488267578773209241]].
- **Shadow Weaving (1.18.1)** stacks on *you* as you cast. Each shadow spell then applies your full stack count to its target, so new targets get the debuff at once [[d:priest#1484726495568986112]]. The debuff lasts 9 s (down from 15 s), which players suspect may be an oversight (same source). How several shadow priests' stacks interact was never confirmed [[d:priest#1479949815843524668]].
- **Fade (1.18.1)** reduces threat by 15% while active and removes a flat amount, but you still gain threat normally, so it only delays a pull [[d:priest#1484604133200891944]].
- **Threat:** Mind Blast's threat multiplier is 170% (down from 200%) [[d:priest#1451618600485130362]]. VE's healing and mana return add threat on top [[d:priest#1459705039181320296]]. Against a fresh tank, open with SW:P and Mind Flay and delay Mind Blast.

## Utility duties

- **Mind Control** (Razuvious, Faerlina): Razuvious needs 16% hit; the T3 2-piece hit bonus counts toward it [[d:priest#1461398705235300475]]. On Faerlina, MC the first add on pull and Silence it at once (5 yd range) to stop Poison Volley. Time later MCs right after a Rain of Fire [[d:priest#1432402678176157847]]. Early MC breaks are usually caused by range, a Tremor Totem, someone dispelling, or a fat-fingered spell [[d:priest#1474928240593342515]]. There is also a small per-tick break chance [[d:priest#1472102462134423593]].
- **Shadow tanking:** with 315+ shadow resistance a Shadow priest can tank the AQ40 Twin Emperors caster twin and Bishop-type casters. The Empowered Shadow Bolt only hits within about 20 yd. Mendeleev calls it "the easiest tank to heal", sustained by Renew and VE [[d:priest#1477507796521058465]], [[d:priest#1477508513457635440]]. Drop the threat-reduction talents and Salvation for it [[d:priest#1441148898981707970]]. A full planned shadow-resistance set (Runed Stygian pieces, Juno's Shadow, Garb of Royal Ascension, Anathema, and more) is posted at [[d:priest#1441244848643375295]]. Shadow priests can also tank the Kara40 Chess event [[d:priest#1428028181935292546]].

## Dungeon and raid notes

- **[Kara40](../../instances/tower-of-karazhan.md):** Anomalus, Gnarlmoon and Kruul are where Shadow shines [[d:priest#1428028181935292546]]. On Mephistroth, penetration matters (see Stats); Eye of Moam can be used twice, on pull and for phase 2 [[d:priest#1436149292875382814]]. His crystals have no shadow resistance, so Holy Nova works on the imps [[d:priest#1436149274801864804]]. The T3.5 debuff is immune on 3 of 9 bosses [[d:priest#1486032102896959539]].
- **Naxxramas:** Heigan is the fight where the 20-yd Mind Flay range hurts [[d:priest#1454802940958347295]]. For Anub'Rekhan trash, pull adds into the Anub room with a hunter pet [[d:priest#1494364719472054494]]. Pre-patch benchmarks from the top parser: Anub'Rekhan 2,030, Maexxna 1,918, Noth 1,905, Loatheb 1,704 and Patchwerk 1,692 DPS [[d:priest#1460389926838468770]].
- **Nefarian ([BWL](../../instances/blackwing-lair.md)):** the classic warning is that keeping VE up through the priest class call can kill your group [Jcarrill0, 2019-02-13](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=2080#p2080). On Turtle, players report the class call only turns direct heals lethal, so VE, Renew and Power Word: Shield are fine to keep running [[d:priest#1452688804170174646]].
- **Maraudon, Princess:** soloable in Shadow by circling. She drops Blade of Eternal Darkness at about 1% [[d:priest#1470881805279170786]], [[d:priest#1475667095776919632]].
- **Razorfen Kraul:** soloable at 60 as Shadow, using Mind Soothe to skip packs [[d:priest#1448220702929846283]].

## Common mistakes

- Keeping VE up all the time (threat and overheal) [[d:priest#1491659277474074716]], [[d:priest#1459716663501914345]].
- Buying haste, or chasing crit over spell power [[d:priest#1450143690969317406]].
- Stopping Mind Flay by moving instead of clipping [[d:priest#1494662933056327902]].
- Dotting mobs that die before 3 ticks [[d:priest#1489637342825152522]].
- Standing past 20 yd (Mind Flay range).
- Wearing the T3.5 5-piece on Anomalus, Rupturan or Chess (immune) [[d:priest#1438552505175773284]].

## Bugs and quirks

- **Mind Control drops Shadowform:** when a boss Mind Controls you, you can end up unable to recast Shadowform. Workarounds: be Undead (break the MC), get CC'd by someone, take a mage portal, or relog [[d:priest#1447894110194368622]], [[d:priest#1458239869145972816]].
- Shadow Weaving can stop proccing after you log out while dead, until you relog [[d:priest#1494477462988394709]], [[d:priest#1493297099394257009]].
- The Shadowform visual fades after mounting. Reapply Shadowform to restore it [[d:priest#1483470810847908002]], [[d:priest#1491256498456756274]].
- After 1.18.1, VE stopped healing warlock pets [[d:priest#1491868819826938076]].
