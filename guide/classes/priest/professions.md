# Priest professions

**Recommendation:** on a first priest, level with **two gathering professions** (Herbalism + Mining or Skinning). Every crafting profession costs gold until 60 [[d:priest#1464014152988233738]], [[d:priest#1420896033189396512]]. At 60, take **Engineering**. It is the only profession that changes how a priest plays [[d:priest#1420896033189396512]], and since 1.18.1 it makes the Kara BiS spell power goggles [[d:priest#1485306217310982476]]. Pair it with **Alchemy**, or keep **Herbalism** for the Dire Maul lasher farm [[d:priest#1484601941563539657]], [[d:priest#1423308328791965809]]. If you level only by healing dungeons, Tailoring + Enchanting also works [[d:priest#1477162914807939163]].

## What each profession gives a priest

### Engineering

- **Spell power goggles (1.18.1, BWL).** A new BWL boss drops an item that upgrades the engineering spell power goggles ("spell goggles extreme plus"). It needs **Engineering 270** [[d:priest#1486384331227398216]]. Zitronenhaze calls the result "kara BiS": the same base spell power as the previous BiS head, plus a "HUGE sp proc" [[d:priest#1485306217310982476]], [[d:priest#1485306317643186276]]. After 1.18.1 he listed the "new engi head" as a Shadow BiS change [[d:priest#1485542978473951332]]. He also rates it above the T3 head's roughly 51 spell power (spirit converted) on average [[d:priest#1490751034438451372]], [[d:priest#1490751127086567485]]. That exchange prompted "engineering became an even more bis profession" [[d:priest#1490751469266276454]].
- **Earlier goggles:** Greendam recalls engineering goggles from Kara10 with **2% haste**, and says Engineering is "super worth to have", unlike Goldsmith Jewelcrafting (single source) [[d:priest#1469690254653915197]]. Haste matters because Discipline and Holy aim for a 10% haste breakpoint (see [Discipline](discipline.md)).
- **Sappers and explosives:** a Shadow priest's trash damage in a raid log was "like 90k … sapper, holy water, jewel" [[d:priest#1460391037108359219]]. Casual players and healers can skip it, because healers are "not in sapper range that often" [[d:priest#1464011266040397948]], [[d:priest#1464011410257346610]].
- **PvP:** engineering Nets against warriors: bait Berserker Rage with Fear, then Net [[d:pvp-general#1432168724714229770]]. Engineering gear also raises PvP performance in general [[d:priest#1465116477958127857]].

**Buy it instead:** tailored items are cheap on the AH, so gold from gathering buys them [[d:priest#1356231640216764578]]. One raider advises skipping Truefaith and Mooncloth pre-BiS altogether and spending the gold on consumables and early raid trinkets [[d:priest#1430564712952889447]]. The **Mooncloth** transmute cooldown is the more valuable of the tailoring cooldowns for gold [[d:priest#1462917080209686782]].

### Enchanting

- **Leveling wands:** "the 2 early enchanting wands have way higher dps than their level and outdps any your spells before lvl20". Greater Magic Wand is the pickup at 13 [[d:priest#1436055370169712821]], [[d:priest#1485480981371555962]]. On the Craftsman challenge, "snag enchanting asap and make a wand" [[d:priest#1486734536824983633]].
- **At 60:** the Enchanting cooldown item (Enchanted Armor Kit) sells better than tailoring's [[d:priest#1462915005862117548]]. Enchanting + Herbalism is "more lucrative" than Tailoring at 60 [[d:priest#1472361964544983050]].
- **Downside:** it costs money while leveling. Making money needs rare recipes (e.g. Crusader) and advertising in a capital [[d:priest#1356232562451681461]].
- **Gap in enchants:** there is no Holy or Shadow spell power glove enchant for priests (mages get one) [[d:priest#1459687027019874426]].

### Alchemy and Herbalism

- **Engineering + Alchemy** is the stated PvE endgame pair [[d:priest#1484601941563539657]]. Alchemy makes your own potions [[d:priest#1476626222048870450]] and covers part of the raid consumable stack on the [Discipline](discipline.md) and [Shadow](shadow.md) pages.
- **Herbalism** is the usual partner for the lasher farm. It adds about 20% extra income there [[d:priest#1420896033189396512]].

### Jewelcrafting (Turtle)

- There are **no Shadow- or Holy-specific gems** [[d:priest#1459687027019874426]]. Greendam judged a Goldsmith-crafted helm "not strong enough to take Goldsmith". Another player pointed out you do not need Goldsmith to equip it, so buy it instead [[d:priest#1469690254653915197]], [[d:priest#1469691543345430568]].
- A Shadow pre-raid ring list includes "next JC ring", with Pure Elementium Band BiS once BWL opens (single source) [[d:priest#1453433162767011893]].
- **Artisan challenge:** priest staves and rings come from Jewelcrafting, which needs Mining. That makes a self-sufficient priest hard; the player who raised it left it unresolved [[d:priest#1317279756009472020]], [[d:priest#1317287215428010147]].

### Survival and Gardening (secondary professions)

**Should a priest take them?** The priest channel gives no class-specific reason. Nobody discusses Bolas, nets or gathering bags for priests. Take Survival for leveling comfort (tents) and the general utility below, not for priest power.

- **Before 1.18.1:** players learned tents themselves from level 5, or used the tents placed outside Orgrimmar and Stormwind to rest while leveling by dungeon [[d:priest#1458569409630306315]], [[d:priest#1458567930651611198]]. Another player suggested taking Survival on a leveling partner so tents go where you farm and you run back to the capital less [[d:priest#1445003840960331846]], [[d:priest#1445003884719378523]]. Greendam called **Gardening** the better semi-AFK gold farm, though lashers pay more [[d:priest#1400599971669217431]], [[d:priest#1400602839461400637]].
- **1.18.1 rework:** Survival now has 80+ recipes, including slow/root/trap tools, gathering bags and Woodcutting [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). Rested bonus from kills fell to **50%**, and tents cap at **25%** rested, while non-city taverns can still fill to 100% (same source). The full 1-300 route is on the [Professions overview](../../professions.md) page.
- **Priest-specific 1.18.1 comment:** Greendam dismissed a new Survival consumable as "only 3%" and said it needs Gardening crops that nobody plants and that the skill-225 quest also uses, so they cost more than gold (single source) [[d:priest#1491897021928571091]]. A player asked whether Survival can craft a wand; nobody answered [[d:priest#1485635954340532244]].

## When to pick up and swap

1. **1-60:** double gathering. Change at 60 "when you have a better idea what you want to do" [[d:priest#1420896033189396512]]. A tailor who is leveling can still make the Stormreaver gloves (20s), Shadoweave (Shadow) and Dreamweave (40-45) [[d:priest#1468366946050506783]], [[d:priest#1442771827331043338]].
2. **At 60:** drop one gathering profession, or Tailoring, for Engineering. Several tailors in the channel did exactly that [[d:priest#1472361964544983050]], [[d:priest#1462906174562373774]].
3. **Engineering 270** before your guild farms BWL, so you can craft the goggle upgrade when it drops [[d:priest#1486384331227398216]].

## Race interactions

The priest channel reports no racial profession bonuses that matter for priests. See the [Professions overview](../../professions.md) for Turtle-wide ones.

## Gold

- **Dire Maul East lashers** are the signature priest farm: Holy Nova rank-1 spam in Disc. Herbalism adds about 20% [[d:priest#1420896033189396512]]. See [Leveling](leveling.md#gold-and-farming) for the route.
- A **DM North / Kara10 trinket-quest loop** makes 60g+ per 5 runs, and more with a second account (single source) [[d:priest#1400189981968039987]].
- **Mining + Skinning:** "hundreds of gold" passively [[d:priest#1476681259039199364]].
- **Buying BoEs at 60** (a few hundred gold) gets a fresh Shadow priest past BWL-geared players, at the cost of Stamina [[d:priest#1466919475549835357]].

## Gaps

- No source gives the goggles' name, stats or the upgrade item's name, only "spell goggles extreme plus", Engineering 270, a BWL drop and a big SP proc.
- Gloves of Unwinding Mystery haste is 2% or 3% depending on the poster. Whether they are BoE is not stated.
- The priest channel does not discuss Gnomish vs Goblin Engineering, or 1.18.1 Survival tools (Bolas, nets, trinkets).
- Jewelcrafting's priest value is thin: one ring mention and one Goldsmith helm.
