# Priest Leveling (1-60)

**Recommendation:** level as a **wand + Smite** priest. Take 2/2 Wand Specialization, then 5/5 Spirit Tap, then go down the Holy tree to 5/5 Spiritual Guidance, then into Discipline. Stack Spirit and wand everything until your early 30s, then chain Holy Fire → Smite ×3. Do **not** level Shadow before 40. If you want Shadow, respec at 42-44 and it becomes very strong from there [[d:priest#1331378617153753258]], [[d:priest#1488200396366286889]], [[d:priest#1489371416963842209]].

## Which spec to level

| Spec | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Smite/Holy hybrid** (recommended) | Fastest 1-60 | Smite is "giga buffed" here compared with vanilla [[d:priest#1426107866238156840]]. Divine Fury (Holy) is your best damage talent, and the same path makes you a capable dungeon healer [[d:priest#1492796559509819492]], [[d:priest#1468394752398594120]]. By about 33, a correctly built priest is a "smite machine gun that never needs to drink" [[d:priest#1461691841031307295]]. |
| **Shadow** | Bad before 40, fine after 40, good after 44 | Shadowform arrives at 40 and Mind Flay rank 4 at 44 [[d:priest#1488657196777734204]], [[d:priest#1343069714557173862]], [[d:priest#1474903366231658617]]. Before that, deep Shadow kills at roughly half Smite's speed after 30 [[d:priest#1427444376367530038]]. Players call it a "demigod" around 43-44 [[d:priest#1489371416963842209]]. |
| **Pure Disc from level 10** | Noob trap | The early damage talents are deep in Disc, while the cast-time and pushback talents Smite needs are in Holy [[d:priest#1331378617153753258]]. |

"Wands very potent early through mid-30s; smiter very strong 1-60; shadow gets good after 40, great after 44, excellent in dungeons/party play" [[d:priest#1435209728266338354]].

## Talent order 10 → 60 (Smite/Holy, recommended)

Order from Lirya, Greendam and Zitronenhaze: Wand Spec → Spirit Tap → Holy Focus → Divinity → Divine Fury → Holy to 5/5 Spiritual Guidance → Discipline [[d:priest#1491061921632227400]], [[d:priest#1490664724591939664]], [[d:priest#1488200396366286889]]. Holy Reach and Holy Nova fill the Holy tier requirement, as in Greendam's pinned leveling image [[d:priest#1345481802198024263]]. Optional changes:

- **Piercing Light earlier.** Put 1-2 points in before Spiritual Guidance if you often fight mobs above your level [[d:priest#1490664724591939664]], [[d:priest#1491061921632227400]]. For leveling you only need a point or so of hit; the 16% cap is a raid concern [[d:priest#1382724302079524936]].
- **Spirit Tap is not sacred** (**contested**). Philes argues its 5 points delay the stronger Discipline talents [[d:priest#1492800285691613284]]. Most advisers keep it for the regen and Spiritual Guidance synergy [[d:priest#1490666290388533311]].
- **Spirit Tap slightly later.** Your wand out-damages your spells early, so you spend little mana then [[d:priest#1490666102873915524]]. Some players take Spirit Tap before Wand Specialization [[d:priest#1468388067369943335]].
- **Disc before Spiritual Guidance** (**contested**). Lirya thinks Purifying Flames (+12% damage) is "probably better than Spiritual Guidance" [[d:priest#1492796559509819492]]. Greendam calls rushing Spiritual Guidance "the optimal build" [[d:priest#1490664724591939664]].
- **Holy Nova at 31** (**contested**). Philes calls it "a one point wonder that is occasionally useful" when you heal 2-3 players and hit about 3 mobs at once [[d:priest#1492802070971945020]], [[d:priest#1492802724482384004]]. Lirya: "very little value while leveling. It is too expensive to solo aoe grind" [[d:priest#1492801039093469195]]. It is also a prerequisite point, so a skipped Holy Nova must go into another Holy row-3 talent.

**Respec points.** At **60**, respec into [Smite 31/20/0](discipline.md), [Holy 14/37/0](holy.md) or [Shadow 0/20/31](shadow.md). To keep farming lashers, the 31/15/5 farming build ([Discipline](discipline.md#variants)) is what Lirya uses at 60 [[d:priest#1492796559509819492]]. Respecs cost at most 5 gold on Turtle (the first is 1g) [[d:priest#1473663196677148788]].

## Gameplay by level

What to press in each bracket on the recommended **Smite/Holy** path (talent order above), plus the **Shadow** path after the respec at 42. Learn levels, costs and cast times are from the 1.18.1 game data. Smite Rank 1 and Lesser Heal Rank 1 are known from level 1. Wand use (`Shoot`) costs no mana, so wanding does not stop the 5-second-rule regen (vanilla baseline). Every bracket has a **Healing (dungeons)** block, because this path heals any leveling dungeon [[d:priest#1468394752398594120]], [[d:priest#1492802348899373076]].

**Rules that hold for every bracket:**

- **Healing ranks:** do not downrank while leveling. Cast the highest rank you have of Lesser Heal, Heal or Greater Heal when the tank needs it, put Renew on the tank, then stop casting so the 5-second rule lets your mana regen [[d:priest#1471253529334382634]]. Pre-cast the big heal and cancel it at about two-thirds of the bar if it is not needed [[d:priest#1490473551717728317]]. +Healing gear does little before about 50 and 200+ healing power, so pick Intellect and Spirit over it [[d:priest#1423198171038748714]], [[d:priest#1442768318845948019]].
- **Power Word: Shield in groups:** do not shield warrior or druid tanks. An absorbed hit gives them no rage, and rage is their threat. Paladin and shaman tanks and DPS are fine to shield [[d:priest#1393311111255822486]], [[d:priest#1393317355932876832]]. Each shield puts Weakened Soul on the target, so you cannot shield it again for a while (vanilla baseline).
- **Healing threat:** Renew ticks can pull mobs the tank has not hit yet. If a new mob joins, move towards the tank [[d:priest#1393317475487060081]], [[d:priest#1393318537497677946]]. Fade on the pull stops loose mobs from running at you [[d:priest#1423720492048842943]].
- **Spirit Tap** (from level 12) triggers only on your killing blow. Snipe the last hit with the wand, Smite or Mind Blast, then chain to the next mob while it runs [[d:priest#1489729086015279258]], [[d:priest#1352935354180571138]].

### Levels 1–9

**New now:** L1 Smite R1, Lesser Heal R1, Power Word: Fortitude R1 · L4 Shadow Word: Pain R1, Lesser Heal R2 · L6 Power Word: Shield R1 (45 mana, 4 s cooldown), Smite R2 · L8 Renew R1, Fade R1. Buy a Lesser Magic Wand at about 5 [[d:priest#1485480981371555962]].

**Single target:**

1. Buff Power Word: Fortitude, and keep it up.
2. Before the wand: `Smite` ×2 to 3, then melee (vanilla baseline).
3. With a wand: `Shadow Word: Pain`, then `Shoot` until the mob dies [[d:priest#1436055370169712821]].
4. `Power Word: Shield` only if the mob hits hard. Heal between pulls with `Lesser Heal`, then drink when you are under about half mana (vanilla baseline).

**Multi-target:** fight one mob at a time. With two mobs: shield yourself, SW:P both, then wand one (vanilla baseline).

**Healing (dungeons):** none yet. The first dungeons open at about 13.

### Levels 10–19

**New now:** L10 Mind Blast R1, Lesser Heal R3 (2.5 s), SW:P R2, Resurrection, and your race's two priest spells (quest) · L12 Inner Fire R1, PW:S R2 · L14 Psychic Scream R1 (30 s cooldown), Cure Disease, Renew R2, Smite R3 · L16 Heal R1 (155 mana, 3 s), Mind Blast R2 · L18 Dispel Magic R1, PW:S R3, SW:P R3. Talents: Wand Specialization (10–11), Spirit Tap (12–16), Holy Focus (17–18), Divinity (19). Buy a Greater Magic Wand at 13 [[d:priest#1468158386821660827]]. Get Cookie's Stirring Rod or Gravestone Scepter at about 18 (see [gear](#weapons-and-gear-by-bracket)).

**Single target (the wand phase):**

1. `Inner Fire` and Fortitude up.
2. `Power Word: Shield` on yourself if the mob hits hard or two are coming (vanilla baseline).
3. `Shadow Word: Pain`.
4. `Shoot` until it dies. From 13, Greater Magic Wand + 2/2 Wand Specialization does more damage than your spells [[d:priest#1468158386821660827]]. Opening with Mind Blast is optional, and it costs mana you would rather keep.
5. Let Spirit Tap refill your mana instead of sitting down. Drink only when a pull starts below about a third of your mana (vanilla baseline).

**Multi-target (2–3 mobs):**

1. `Power Word: Shield` on yourself.
2. `Shadow Word: Pain` on every mob.
3. Wand the one that is lowest.
4. If the shield breaks and more than one mob is on you: `Psychic Scream`, re-shield, heal with `Lesser Heal`, then re-apply SW:P [[d:priest#1320479806118170675]].

Do not pull 3+ on purpose yet: you have no AoE.

**Healing (dungeons): Ragefire Chasm, Wailing Caverns, Deadmines, Shadowfang Keep**

| Situation | Press |
|---|---|
| Tank taking steady damage | `Renew` (max rank). Early dungeons are mostly healable with Renew, with the big heal kept ready for bosses and 3+ mob pulls [[d:priest#1479210123439112252]] |
| Tank low (about half health) | Max-rank `Lesser Heal` (R3), or `Heal` R1 from 16 when damage is heavy. Let the tank drop to about half instead of spamming [[d:priest#1430698110853845104]] |
| Tank about to die | `Power Word: Shield`, then heal. On a warrior tank, only in a real emergency |
| DPS took a hit | `Renew`, or nothing if they are above 70% |
| Poisoned, diseased or feared ally | `Cure Disease` for diseases (14+). From 18, `Dispel Magic` on friendly-player magic debuffs: fears, sleeps, slows, polymorphs (vanilla baseline). Priests cannot remove poisons or curses |

**Contested: Lesser Heal or Heal.** One healer keeps using Lesser Heal while leveling because mana matters more [[d:priest#1489676012013883713]]. Another skips it once Heal is trained [[d:priest#1490472852506542100]]. Heal R1 is learned before level 20, so its +healing scaling is reduced. This barely matters at a level with no +healing gear (vanilla baseline).

### Levels 20–29

**New now:** L20 Holy Fire R1 (3.5 s), Flash Heal R1 (1.5 s, 125 mana), Renew R3, Fear Ward, Inner Fire R2, Fade R2, Shackle Undead, Mind Soothe, and the second racial spells · L22 Heal R2, Smite R4, Mind Blast R3 · L24 Holy Fire R2, PW:S R4, Fortitude R3, Mana Burn · L26 Flash Heal R2, Renew R4, SW:P R4 · L28 Heal R3, Mind Blast R4, Psychic Scream R2. Talents: Divinity (19–21), Divine Fury (22–26), Divinity (27–28), Holy Reach (29–30).

**Single target:**

1. Pre-cast `Holy Fire` at max range.
2. `Smite` while the mob runs in.
3. `Power Word: Shield` if it hits hard.
4. Wand to finish [[d:priest#1448670109785915402]].
5. On a weak mob, keep the wand phase going instead: `Shadow Word: Pain` → wand. **Contested:** Greendam rarely casts Holy Fire or Smite before 30, because Gravestone Scepter out-damages them [[d:priest#1436055370169712821]]. Divine Fury (from 22) makes Smite your strongest damage talent [[d:priest#1492796559509819492]].

**Multi-target (2–3 mobs):** shield → `Holy Fire` on the first mob → `Shadow Word: Pain` on the rest → wand. Keep `Psychic Scream` for when the shield breaks [[d:priest#1320479806118170675]].

**Healing (dungeons): Blackfathom Deeps, Gnomeregan, Razorfen Kraul, Scarlet Monastery**

1. Keep max-rank `Renew` on the tank. Put a Renew on warlocks who Life Tap, rather than ignoring them [[d:priest#1404752891054657586]].
2. When the tank drops, pre-cast max-rank `Heal`, and cancel it at about two-thirds if the tank does not need it [[d:priest#1409428375700770889]].
3. `Flash Heal` only when even Heal spam cannot keep the tank up. It costs more mana and heals less per second [[d:priest#1471253529334382634]].
4. `Power Word: Shield` if the tank will not live until your next cast lands [[d:priest#1409428375700770889]].
5. `Fear Ward` on the tank before fearing bosses [[d:priest#848446155523227678]]. `Shackle Undead` on an extra undead mob in Scarlet Monastery Graveyard or Razorfen Downs (vanilla baseline).
6. **Mana:** a big heal, then 5+ seconds of wanding or nothing, with Renew ticking. Drink between pulls, not during them.

### Levels 30–39

**New now:** L30 Smite R5, Holy Fire R3, PW:S R5, Prayer of Healing R1 (410 mana), Divine Spirit R1, Pain Spike R1 (30 s cooldown), Mind Control, Shadow Protection, Lightwell, Fade R3 · L32 Flash Heal R3, Renew R5, Abolish Disease, Mana Burn R2 · L34 Heal R4, Mind Blast R5, SW:P R5 · L36 Dispel Magic R2, Holy Fire R4, PW:S R6, Fortitude R4, and Holy Nova R3 at the trainer · L38 Flash Heal R4, Renew R6, Smite R6. Talents: Holy Reach 2 (30), Holy Nova (31, 130 mana, instant), Spiritual Guidance (32–36), Piercing Light (37–39).

**Single target (the Smite phase):**

1. `Holy Fire` from max range.
2. `Smite` ×3.
3. Snipe the killing blow with `Smite` or max-rank `Mind Blast` so Spirit Tap procs [[d:priest#1468394752398594120]], [[d:priest#1489729086015279258]].
4. Wand only the last sliver (1–2 shots) [[d:priest#1492796559509819492]].
5. Skip Shadow Word: Pain on normal mobs, because most die before 3 ticks. Keep it for elites and bosses, and cast it while moving [[d:priest#1450846541362499627]], [[d:priest#1489729086015279258]].
6. `Pain Spike` (instant) as a finisher when the mob is nearly dead (see [Discipline](discipline.md#single-target-rotation)).
7. Chain the next mob while Spirit Tap runs. A correctly built priest reaches 33 as "a smite machine gun that never need to drink" [[d:priest#1461691841031307295]].

**Multi-target:**

1. 2 mobs: shield → `Holy Fire` → `Shadow Word: Pain` on the second → Smite the first.
2. Bad pull (3+): `Psychic Scream`, shield, heal, then kill one at a time. Or `Mind Control` one mob so the others fight it [[d:priest#1320479806118170675]].
3. `Holy Nova` (31+) is too expensive to AoE grind with [[d:priest#1492801039093469195]]. Use it only when 2–3 players need healing and about 3 mobs are on you [[d:priest#1492802070971945020]].

**Healing (dungeons): Scarlet Monastery, Razorfen Downs, Uldaman, Zul'Farrak**

Use `Renew` and `Heal`, and skip Lesser Heal. Keep `Power Word: Shield` → `Flash Heal` for emergencies [[d:priest#1490472852506542100]].

| Situation | Press |
|---|---|
| Tank, steady damage | Max-rank `Renew` always up |
| Tank drops to about half health | Pre-cast max-rank `Heal`, and cancel it at two-thirds if not needed [[d:priest#1490473551717728317]] |
| Tank spike | `Power Word: Shield` → `Flash Heal` [[d:priest#1490472852506542100]] |
| 3+ players damaged | `Prayer of Healing`, only if 3+ fully benefit. It is very mana-hungry [[d:priest#1471253529334382634]] |
| Magic debuff on a player (fear, polymorph, slow) | `Dispel Magic`. Rank 2 (36) removes two effects (vanilla baseline) |
| Disease | `Abolish Disease` (32+) |
| Undead extras | `Shackle Undead` |

- **Heal R4 (34) and Flash Heal R3 (32):** these used to be inefficient ranks to skip. 1.18.1 raised their base values (see [spells to train](#spells-to-train-and-skip)). Before the patch, one player measured Heal R3 at 9 healing per mana against 7.5 for R4 [[d:priest#1471311745086783518]].
- **Mana:** Renew on the tank plus a proper pre-cast and cancel gives long regen windows [[d:priest#1490481295694758060]]. Buff `Divine Spirit` on the group (30+).

### Levels 40–49

**New now:** L40 Greater Heal R1 (370 mana, 3 s), Prayer of Healing R2, Divine Spirit R2, Mind Blast R6, Pain Spike R2, Shackle R2, Fade R4 · L42 Holy Fire R5, PW:S R7, SW:P R6, Psychic Scream R3, Shadow Protection R2 · L44 Flash Heal R5, Renew R7, Holy Nova R4, Mind Control R2 · L46 Greater Heal R2, Mind Blast R7, Smite R7 · L48 Holy Fire R6, PW:S R8, Fortitude R5, Prayer of Fortitude R1 (item-taught). Smite/Holy talents: Mental Agility (40–44), Improved Inner Fire (45–46), Inner Focus (47), Meditation (48–49).

**Single target (Smite):** same as 30–39. Open with `Inner Focus` → `Holy Fire` on elites (47+).

**Multi-target (Smite):** same as 30–39.

**Shadow path (respec at 42):** new at the respec: Shadowform, Mind Flay, Vampiric Embrace, Vampiric Touch, Improved SW:P and Improved Mind Blast. Train the Mind Flay ranks you skipped (R2 at 28, R3 at 36; R4 arrives at 44). Shadow is good from 44, when Mind Flay rank 4 is out [[d:priest#1343069714557173862]], [[d:priest#1359043629238325300]]. Before that, early Mind Flay ranks do less damage than a wand [[d:priest#1375835552179556364]].

1. `Shadowform` (it stays on). Pre-cast `Power Word: Shield` on yourself so Mind Flay is not pushed back. A lower rank of PW:S is enough, the one the mob can barely break [[d:priest#1415763183548895383]], [[d:priest#1415765173549006889]].
2. `Mind Blast`.
3. `Shadow Word: Pain`.
4. `Mind Flay` ×2.
5. Wand to finish if another cast is not worth it [[d:priest#1415763183548895383]], or wand while SW:P ticks [[d:priest#1415765173549006889]]. Troll: keep Shadowguard up [[d:priest#1415765173549006889]].
6. **Multi-target:** SW:P on each mob, Mind Flay the kill target, and `Psychic Scream` when they reach you. Shadow has no real AoE [[d:priest#1472909182041849967]].

**Healing (dungeons): Zul'Farrak, Maraudon, Sunken Temple**

1. Max-rank `Renew` on the tank.
2. Pre-cast `Greater Heal` (or max `Heal`) when the tank drops. Cancel it if not needed.
3. `Power Word: Shield` → `Flash Heal` for spikes and for DPS who pull aggro.
4. `Prayer of Healing` R2 for group-wide damage when 3+ benefit.
5. `Inner Focus` (47+) on the heal you need most: a free Greater Heal or Prayer of Healing, then back to wand.
6. **Downranking starts now.** Late leveling and a fresh 60 are when you downrank most, because you are short on gear and sustain [[d:priest#1494888479052533902]]. Greater Heal R1 is usually fine. Do not go below Heal R2, or you get the low-level penalty [[d:priest#1431186254069760070]].
7. **Shadow players:** you can still heal dungeons [[d:priest#1486888659960729700]]. You must leave Shadowform to cast heals (vanilla baseline). Dispel Magic can be set up in dispel addons so that you do not drop form to cure diseases [[d:priest#1391482534935658556]].

### Levels 50–60

**New now:** L50 Flash Heal R6, Renew R8, Prayer of Healing R3, SW:P R7, Inner Fire R5, Divine Spirit R3, Pain Spike R3 · L52 Greater Heal R3, Holy Nova R5, Mind Blast R8, Mind Flay R5 · L54 Holy Fire R7, PW:S R9, Smite R8 · L56 Flash Heal R7, Renew R9, Psychic Scream R4, Shadow Protection R3 · L58 Greater Heal R4, Mind Blast R9, SW:P R8, Shadow Mend R1 (item-taught, 30 s cooldown; the sources say nothing about it yet) · L60 Holy Fire R8, Holy Nova R6, PW:S R10, Prayer of Healing R4, Mind Flay R6, Fortitude R6, Divine Spirit R4, Prayer of Spirit. Items teach Greater Heal R5, Renew R10, Prayer of Healing R5, Smite R9 and Prayer of Fortitude R2. Smite/Holy talents: **Purifying Flames** (50–51: +12% Holy damage for 10 s after Holy Fire), **Searing Light** (52–54: an instant Smite after a crit), Mental Strength (55–57), Enlighten (58), Meditation 3 (59), Force of Will (60).

**Single target (Smite, 52+):**

1. `Holy Fire` (starts Purifying Flames).
2. `Smite` spam. Cast each instant Smite the moment Searing Light procs.
3. Recast Holy Fire when Purifying Flames runs out, on longer fights (the raid loop is Holy Fire → 4 Smites; see [Discipline](discipline.md#single-target-rotation)).
4. Mind Blast or Smite for the killing blow (Spirit Tap). SW:P only while moving [[d:priest#1489729086015279258]].
5. Self-buff `Enlighten` at 58.

**Multi-target (Smite):** Dire Maul East lasher packs from about 50: `Power Word: Shield` on yourself, then `Holy Nova` spam. A full pack takes about 9 rank-1 Novas (~6k mana) [[d:priest#1354596333456986244]]. See [Gold and farming](#gold-and-farming). In normal questing, stay single-target.

**Shadow path:** same loop as 40–49 with the new ranks. Use `Vampiric Embrace` on longer fights and elites for self-healing (see [Shadow](shadow.md#single-target-rotation)).

**Healing (dungeons): Blackrock Depths, Lower Blackrock Spire, Dire Maul, Scholomance, Stratholme**

| Situation | Press |
|---|---|
| Tank, steady damage | Max-rank `Renew` always up. At 60, Bluefear mainly casts Heal R4 with max Renew rolling on the tank [[d:priest#1471253832221589577]]. Another healer lets the tank lose about 40% of health, then casts Heal R4 + max Renew: "about 90% of my healing" in level-60 dungeons [[d:priest#1420844417216155790]] |
| Tank burst, or boss | Pre-cast `Greater Heal` (R1 is usually enough) [[d:priest#1431186254069760070]] |
| DPS random damage | A downranked `Renew` (Bluefear uses R5) [[d:priest#1471253832221589577]] |
| Emergency | `Power Word: Shield` → `Flash Heal`. Flash Heal is mana-expensive, and dungeon damage is low enough that you rarely need it [[d:priest#1428692582585860096]] |
| Group damage | `Inner Focus` + max `Prayer of Healing`, or just R2 Prayer [[d:priest#1471253832221589577]] |
| Dispels | `Dispel Magic` (CC and debuffs on players), `Abolish Disease` (Scholomance, Stratholme) (vanilla baseline) |

## Spells to train (and skip)

- **Must train** (Aykhanchik's list): Smite, Holy Fire, Shadow Word: Pain, Power Word: Fortitude, Power Word: Shield, Inner Fire, Flash Heal, Renew, Mind Control, Prayer of Healing, Cure Disease, Dispel Magic, Psychic Scream, Fade [Aykhanchik, 2022-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15484#p15484). Add Mind Blast; max rank is your last-hit tool for Spirit Tap [[d:priest#1489729086015279258]].
- **Turtle additions:** Fear Ward is baseline for every race [[d:priest#1447464110588428309]]. Also Divine Spirit (30), Pain Spike, and Lightwell (one rank in 1.18.1) [Jamey, 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **Optional:** Heal, Mind Soothe, Shackle Undead, Mind Vision, Mana Burn [Aykhanchik, 2022-01-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=15484#p15484). Mind Vision rank 2 has ~300 yd range against ~100 yd for rank 1 [[d:priest#1428455114288664702]].
- **Healing ranks:** for dungeon healing at 31, use Renew and Heal and skip Lesser Heal. Keep Flash Heal for emergencies [[d:priest#1490472852506542100]]. Heal rank 1 carries a low-level penalty. Before 1.18.1, Heal rank 4, Flash Heal rank 3 and Renew rank 5 were inefficient ranks to skip [[d:priest#1346965636810805339]], [[d:priest#1471314520969576534]]. 1.18.1 raised the base values of Heal rank 4 and Flash Heal ranks 3-7 [Jamey, 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071). Whether Renew rank 5 was fixed is not stated (gap).

## Pull and kill loop (bot-ready)

**Levels 1 to ~20 (wand phase):**

1. `Power Word: Shield` on yourself if the mob hits hard or you are pulling two.
2. `Shadow Word: Pain`.
3. `Shoot` (wand) until it dies [[d:priest#1436055370169712821]].

Wanding beats casting early on. At 13, Greater Magic Wand + 2/2 Wand Specialization out-damages your spells [[d:priest#1468158386821660827]].

**Levels ~20 to 30:**

1. Pre-cast `Holy Fire` at max range.
2. `Smite`.
3. `Power Word: Shield` (rank 3 by 20, rank 4 at 24).
4. Wand to finish [[d:priest#1448670109785915402]].

**Level 30+ (Smite phase):**

1. `Holy Fire` from max range.
2. `Smite` ×3. Snipe the killing blow with Smite or max-rank `Mind Blast` so Spirit Tap procs [[d:priest#1468394752398594120]], [[d:priest#1489729086015279258]].
3. Wand only for the last sliver (1-2 shots) [[d:priest#1492796559509819492]].
4. Drop Shadow Word: Pain after about 30: most mobs die before 3 ticks. Keep it for elites and bosses [[d:priest#1450846541362499627]], [[d:priest#1489729086015279258]]. Once you have Purifying Flames and Searing Light you will rarely cast it, except while moving [[d:priest#1489729086015279258]].
5. Chain straight into the next mob while Spirit Tap is active.

**Elites or multiple mobs:** buff → `Power Word: Shield` → `Holy Fire` → `Shadow Word: Pain` → wand ×2. If the shield breaks: `Psychic Scream` (or Mind Control), then re-shield and heal [[d:priest#1320479806118170675]].

**Melee weaving (optional):** with a slow two-hand staff, melee between wand shots. Wand shots do not reset the swing timer, which adds up to ~30% damage at low levels [[d:priest#1430606679807889511]].

## Mana and downtime

- **Spirit is king while leveling.** It gives regen, doubles with Spirit Tap, and turns into spell power through Spiritual Guidance [[d:priest#1428272774643978312]], [[d:priest#1424846576496545912]]. Spirit Tap is strong for Smite too; with decent gear it regenerates mana nearly as fast as you spend it [[d:priest#1451685637819339005]].
- **Stat order:** Spirit > Intellect > Stamina [[d:priest#1474554827982766111]]. Spell power gear only starts to matter from about 30-35 [[d:priest#1474712461763416276]]. **Contested:** Intellect and Stamina may matter more below 40 if you mostly run dungeons, where you rarely land killing blows [[d:priest#1458073332997161022]]. As a dungeon healer, prioritise healing power > Intellect > Spirit [[d:priest#1473741254092263461]].
- Compare mana costs. Refreshing Renew or Inner Fire can cost more than a Shield. Do not auto-shield every pull [[d:priest#1448687675803566190]].
- Spirit Tap only triggers on your killing blow, so it is weaker in duos with a melee partner [[d:priest#1352935354180571138]].

## Group role while leveling

- On the Wand → Spirit Tap → Holy path you can be top DPS with another healer present, or take over healing if needed [[d:priest#1468394752398594120]].
- **Healing a leveling dungeon:** do not downrank. Cast the max rank of Heal or Greater Heal when the tank drops, then Renew and stop casting to regen under the 5-second rule. Use Flash Heal for non-tanks and emergencies, and Prayer of Healing only when 3+ people benefit [[d:priest#1471253529334382634]]. Pre-cast your heal, then cancel it at about two-thirds if it is not needed [[d:priest#1490473551717728317]].
- **Threat:** Disc and Smite have threat trouble without Silent Resolve [[d:priest#1483043291082653746]]. Use Fade pre-emptively on pulls, so mobs the tank misses do not latch onto your healing threat [[d:priest#1423720492048842943]].

## Professions

- **First character:** double gathering (Herbalism + Mining or Skinning). Crafting professions are gold sinks until 60 [[d:priest#1464014152988233738]]. Tailoring and Enchanting are "traps" while leveling unless you invest in them [[d:priest#1356231640216764578]].
- **Dungeon-leveling priest:** Tailoring + Enchanting (cloth goes to your own gear, plus disenchant income). Herbalism + Alchemy suits hardcore [[d:priest#1476626222048870450]].
- **Endgame:** Engineering is the only profession with real gameplay value, including the 1.18.1 BWL spell power goggles [[d:priest#1420896033189396512]], [[d:priest#1486384331227398216]]. Engineering + Alchemy is the usual PvE pair [[d:priest#1484601941563539657]].

## Gold and farming

- **Dire Maul East lashers** are the signature priest farm from about 50-54: Shield + Holy Nova spam on packs, in Disc or a Holy hybrid [[d:priest#1432727974754521100]], [[d:priest#1436385521482989699]]. Greens handle one pack; two packs at a time work in basic gear, and you will hit the instance lockout [[d:priest#1469118366030630974]], [[d:priest#1476598760577433823]]. A full pack takes about 9 rank-1 Novas (~6k mana) [[d:priest#1354596333456986244]]. Herbalism adds about 20% to the income [[d:priest#1420896033189396512]]. You can skip several packs by using aggro range and reset spots [[d:priest#1463784145590157502]].
- **Hydrospawn** (DM East) and **Princess** (Maraudon) are soloable once geared [[d:priest#1432728962638811288]], [[d:priest#1446229592120758332]].
- **Razorfen Kraul** is soloable at 60 as Shadow using Mind Soothe [[d:priest#1448220702929846283]].

## Hardcore notes

- 2/2 Wand Specialization and 5/5 Spirit Tap are treated as mandatory. Do not go Shadow before Shadowform: Mind Flay has no pushback protection and burns mana once your shield drops [[d:priest#1436917703242944552]].
- Put Disc survivability points first until Shadowform at 40-42. Casting damage spells other than SW:P before 40 is mostly not worth the mana [Atreidon, 2026-01-05](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=164458#p164458).

## Common mistakes

- Leveling Shadow from level 10 [[d:priest#1331378617153753258]].
- Not buying and upgrading wands [[d:priest#1484713006725071071]].
- Dotting every mob after 30 [[d:priest#1450846541362499627]].
- Starting Disc from the top instead of taking the Holy damage talents first [[d:priest#1331378617153753258]].
- Downranking heals in leveling dungeons [[d:priest#1494888479052533902]].
- Respeccing Shadow at exactly 40 and losing Wand Specialization [[d:priest#1331378617153753258]].
