# Paladin professions

**Recommendation:** level with **two gathering professions**. Herbalism + Mining is the usual pair, or Skinning for early silver. At 60, swap one of them for **Engineering**, the only main profession that "benefits you in raids" [[d:paladin#1486890674321096929]], [[d:paladin#1491337706964389908]]. Keep the gathering profession that fits your gold farm: **Herbalism** for the Dire Maul East lashers, **Mining** for Zul'Gurub Arcane Crystal veins [[d:paladin#1460963936063983751]], [[d:paladin#1474034767120044177]]. A Retribution player who wants the pre-raid **Tempered Runeblade** can take Blacksmithing (Swordsmith) long enough to craft it, then drop it [[d:paladin#1443679533701005333]]. Add **Survival** as a secondary skill for the slingshot ranged pull [[d:paladin#1486706568044220557]], [[d:paladin#1486707812800270429]].

| **Raiding: Holy** | Engineering + a gathering profession | Blacksmithing gives Holy little; Engineering lets a Holy player dual-spec Prot and farm | [[d:paladin#1435644240179757229]], [[d:paladin#1472001080534761583]] |
| **PvP** | Engineering + anything | You want Engineering for any endgame content, PvP included (grenades, PvP trinkets) | [[d:paladin#1491337490085445834]], [[d:paladin#1429192271722582210]] |

**How much does Engineering matter?** Most players agree it is the one profession that counts. **Contested** on how much: it is "not required unless you're planning to be even semi serious in raids" [[d:paladin#1429188657431969863]], and "people do put a lot more weight on engi than what it really is" [[d:paladin#1429188825011195965]]. The strongest view is that "every other proffesion exists to serve Engineering, especially on Paladin" [[d:paladin#1456908911972974754]].

## What each profession gives a paladin

### Engineering

- **Goblin Sapper Charges and dynamite.** For Prot they are the snap AoE threat the class otherwise lacks: a Sapper does about 500 threat, the same as Consecration's first tick [[d:paladin#1432881647144931369]], [[d:paladin#1489946008426250290]]. For Ret they add 500-600 damage to each AoE pull [[d:paladin#1428687306520395776]]. They are also the only real raid damage a Prot paladin does [[d:paladin#1382258860475154512]]. Righteous Resolve and Vengeance both raise the self-damage you take from a Sapper [[d:paladin#1429191584603308162]], [[d:paladin#1429208863256875129]].
- **Grenades** for ranged pulls and AoE trash packs [[d:paladin#1466850028583915594]].
- **Force Reactive Disk** (300 Engineering; about 900 armor [[d:paladin#1429208322065829938]]). It is the paladin farming tool: "vampirism+FRD is the latchkey of paladin farming" [[d:paladin#1488941850139033770]]. It opens solo farms that are otherwise impossible, such as Stratholme [[d:paladin#1452279624687026217]], [[d:paladin#1429193593368739912]], and stays useful for AoE tanking up to Kara40 [[d:paladin#1461667214578028668]]. Drawbacks: it breaks "every second or third pull" in Stratholme [[d:paladin#1452284122394398773]], so bring a repair bot [[d:paladin#1452288368078618905]]. Many tanks keep Crest of Retribution on and save the Disk for big pulls [[d:paladin#1493417094266622203]]. It deals Nature damage, so it does nothing against the Nature-immune lashers [[d:paladin#1426580107476336682]], [[d:paladin#1427207119140163584]].
- **Electromagnetic Gigaflux Reactivator:** "too many paladins are sleeping on" it. Use it, swap it out, and the 10-minute shield stays up [[d:paladin#1437750763064524851]], [[d:paladin#1437750988986384444]].
- **Teleporters:** a craftable Stormwind teleport and the Everlook teleporter [[d:paladin#1460964770491662466]], [[d:paladin#1452279624687026217]]. The Gadgetzan teleporter is made obsolete by the Time-Worn Rune [[d:paladin#1452281285040148511]].
- **Mechanical/Battle Chicken:** you cannot force the proc as on Blizzard servers. In combat it starts a random 30-80 s timer, a "substantial nerf" [[d:paladin#1429190224776527903]], [[d:paladin#1429190374836011040]].
- Turtle also has a free summonable repair bot that is not the crafted one [[d:paladin#1437432116139790340]].
- **Cost:** about 100-150g from 1 to 300 [[d:paladin#1489769042435768442]], [[d:paladin#1437831886503280842]].

**Gnomish or Goblin?** Turtle doesn't change the classic answer: the optimal min-max path is still Gnomish first, then Goblin [[d:paladin#1489751046359224510]]. The three usual paths [[d:paladin#1485004797475029003]]:

1. **Gnomish first, then Goblin** (the "optimal" min-max path): craft the Gnomish BoP items such as the Battle Chicken, then unlearn and relevel Goblin to make your own Sappers [[d:paladin#1452284648682946680]], [[d:paladin#1494773475951251556]]. You cannot swap the specialization by itself; you relearn the whole profession from 1 [[d:paladin#1452284784913944719]].
2. **Goblin only**, buying Gnomish items on the AH. A Prot player with no arena plans was told "there aint anything worth anything in gnomish" [[d:paladin#1454182680169283719]].
3. **Gnomish only**, for the Battle Chicken, buying Sappers on the AH [[d:paladin#1485004797475029003]].

Winterspring's Gnomish teleport is preferred over Gadgetzan because it is close to the Caverns of Time teleport. Bag-hungry Gnomish gadgets (Death Ray, Net-o-Matic) mostly stay home, because Turtle has many good trinkets [[d:paladin#1484999525721575466]], [[d:paladin#1485000990427054215]].

### Blacksmithing

- **Retribution: Tempered Runeblade.** It needs 300 Blacksmithing, Weaponsmith, then Swordsmith [[d:paladin#1437834344117833910]]. Craft the Untempered Runeblade to open the quest chain (the dwarf at LHC, after the Winterspring swordsmith quest) [[d:paladin#1444802899929727108]]. The chain's materials cost about 150-200g [[d:paladin#1444674827540496466]]. Finishing the quest chain loses you Argent Dawn reputation [[d:paladin#1433476809902784575]]. It is BiS for Spellret before BWL, and "95% sure tempered beats fist" [[d:paladin#1443679533701005333]], [[d:paladin#1481232023606792295]]. **Contested:** "bis sword for 2 phases" [[d:paladin#1428433981707124838]] versus "the phases are preraid and MC" [[d:paladin#1428437739128492117]]. If you skip Blacksmithing, take the Argent Dawn two-hander [[d:paladin#1491464591585185942]].
- **Axesmith** makes Nightfall [[d:paladin#1437834344117833910]]. Outside a fresh server, one player calls Swordsmithing "essentially pointless" and Axesmithing "the clear winner" (single source) [[d:paladin#1428429025864450058]].
- **1.18.1 upgrades:** BWL drops sharpening stones that raise Blacksmith-crafted weapons to BWL item level [[d:paladin#1484714614574022770]]. You must be a 300 Swordsmith to use the Elementium Champion [[d:paladin#1485576277480964176]]. One player rates it weaker than UTB for similar effort (single source) [[d:paladin#1489792168817066136]]. Staff describe the system in [Dragunovi (staff), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168272#p168272).
- **Other crafts:** Fiery Plate Gauntlets and Storm Gauntlets are Spellret spellstrike hands [[d:paladin#1442133221960716432]]. The Dreamsteel belt buckle is sought after [[d:paladin#1428429500361736223]]. Armorsmithing has nothing useful for endgame. Its first quest-chain trinket is decent, and you don't need to be an Armorsmith to wear it [[d:paladin#1437834253391101972]], [[d:paladin#1438590357724987403]], [[d:paladin#1438590495453483099]].
- **Buy it instead:** you can buy almost every good Blacksmithing item on the AH [[d:paladin#1455267588220387329]]. Crafted leveling gear falls behind unless you farm a lot of ore [[d:paladin#1462227716391698432]].
- **Warning:** changing specialization loses the recipes you learned in the old one [[d:paladin#1425183062290993263]]. Unlearning Blacksmithing resets it to skill 1 [[d:paladin#1462227108733391111]].

### Jewelcrafting (Turtle custom)

- **Value:** only on a main Prot paladin, and only if alts cover your other professions. The Goldsmith neck gives 5% magic damage reduction [[d:paladin#1474768639369543933]], [[d:paladin#1474768692536414379]], [[d:paladin#1476874395367772202]]. Grey's verdict: goldsmithing is "-30%g/hr to lashers for 5% DR on like 3 boss fights" [[d:paladin#1489752616320897264]]. On Ambershire the neck "IS pretty damn good", but switch to Engineering eventually [[d:paladin#1452280583848857620]].
- **Crafts named in the channel:** a 300-JC "HoJ ring" with a fire-damage proc (Goldsmith section; name not given) [[d:paladin#1423873219806625832]], [[d:paladin#1423883256327311370]]. A 3% vampirism JC trinket for vamp farming sets [[d:paladin#1465783072804049122]]. Embergem Cuffs, a Spellret spellstrike bracer [[d:paladin#1442133221960716432]].
- **Weak points:** Goldsmith ring enchants are poor ("Can't even do +6stam") [[d:paladin#1446932911742910474]]. The split into two specializations was "overambitious". Gemology is the half guilds need [[d:paladin#1466853091352514581]], [[d:paladin#1466853700751593472]].
- Leveling route, trainers and the Goldsmith quest chain: [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106310#p106310).

### Herbalism and Mining (gathering)

- **Herbalism:** herb nodes around the lasher courtyard raise lasher-farm output more than Mining does [[d:paladin#1437836869726638171]], [[d:paladin#1382258860475154512]]. It also gives Black Lotus chances in Dire Maul and Stratholme [[d:paladin#1465025682735239312]].
- **Mining:** mainly for Zul'Gurub runs that hit two Arcane Crystal veins [[d:paladin#1437837358195150970]], [[d:paladin#1471539414118174830]]. Outside that it is a "waste" for someone who already has Engineering and Blacksmithing at 300 (single source) [[d:paladin#1482361431885090846]].
- **Skinning:** early silver [[d:paladin#1491337556334481528]]. Rugged Hides sell for about 2g each [[d:paladin#1427126057756000277]].

### Alchemy and Enchanting

- **Alchemy:** "pots to give myself even more 'Oh Shit' buttons" [[d:paladin#1428752133322309786]], plus flasks and transmutes [[d:paladin#1382258860475154512]]. Arcanite transmutes earn about 6g over an Arcane Crystal every 2 days, so they pay only across several alts [[d:paladin#1441717649498308649]], [[d:paladin#1441718719045959680]].
- **Enchanting:** disenchant the greens and blues from BoE farms [[d:paladin#1445312926612197457]], [[d:paladin#1474034767120044177]]. It is worth going all in only if you will collect the AQ/MC and reputation enchants [[d:paladin#1454242525312454757]]. Otherwise a low-level bank alt can disenchant for you [[d:paladin#1465026024936046774]].

### Tailoring and Leatherworking

The channel says little. A Holy player asked whether Tailoring beats Blacksmithing, and the answer was Engineering plus a gold-farm profession [[d:paladin#1435644240179757229]], [[d:paladin#1435648174558810233]]. The crafts paladins want are BoE, so buy them: **Spellwoven Drape of Nobility** (Tailoring BoE) for Ret [[d:paladin#1493314491340034048]], and the **Red Dragonscale** set (Leatherworking), a good starter set for Holy but not mandatory [[d:paladin#1492191563324067942]].

### Survival and Gardening (secondary professions)

**1.18.1 (current).** The 1.18.1 patch reset Survival's skill and recipes; it is now a new profession trained mostly at the trainer near the Stormwind gates [[d:paladin#1486709714703351830]], [[d:paladin#1486709848698785802]]. Staff list tools that slow, root or trap, gathering bags and Woodcutting [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). What paladins use it for:

- **Simple Slingshot:** the ranged pull paladins never had, with a 20-yard range [[d:paladin#1484571592011940002]], [[d:paladin#1486707812800270429]]. You get it early, before skill 80 [[d:paladin#1485538065132032080]]. In the player leveling route it sits at 35-50 [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2026-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172915#p172915).
- **Salves** and other small utility [[d:paladin#1486707228928114808]]. **Throwing Bolas** can proc Wrath of Cenarius (single source) [[d:paladin#1485279603806441522]].
- **Gathering trinkets:** the Black Lotus trinket multiplies the Lotus proc chance by 1.5, and the Arcane Crystal trinket adds a flat 2% (single source) [[d:paladin#1489295469812121661]]. That suits a paladin farmer on lashers or ZG.
- **How far to level it:** "you only need to level survival to like 60" and "I would not really recommend getting high level with survival" [[d:paladin#1486706866519015446]], [[d:paladin#1486711329023856703]]. The standard pair in the channel is "Engineering and Survival" [[d:paladin#1486706568044220557]].
- **Tents:** rested XP from tents is capped at 25% in 1.18.1 [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). Grey read the preview as "basically removes them from the game" [[d:paladin#1473479592638681158]].

**Before 1.18.1 (historical).** Survival was "for tents and an offhand" [[d:paladin#1422860249064538143]]. The offhand was the Repaired Electro-Lantern, a Nature spellstrike item for Spellret [[d:paladin#1442133221960716432]]. Tents filled rested XP in minutes [[d:paladin#1423815571044040714]], but they were "almost always" at the Stormwind entrance, so you didn't need the skill yourself [[d:paladin#1422860536676221008]].

**Gardening.** The paladin channel says little: endgame food and Power Mushrooms come from Gardening [[d:paladin#1429922554436386816]], [[d:paladin#1429923597664784405]], and one player uses semi-AFK Gardening or Alchemy for weekly expenses [[d:paladin#1466088149959577856]]. The 1.18.1 reset also reset the Gardening quest [Sylveria (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171863#p171863).

## When to pick up and swap

1. **1-60:** two gathering professions [[d:paladin#1436709623355932702]]. Take Survival early for the slingshot [[d:paladin#1485538065132032080]].
2. **At 60:** most players level Engineering after hitting 60 [[d:paladin#1491337490085445834]]. If you already have Engineering while leveling, get it to 300 by 60 [[d:paladin#1432722835796656209]]. Keep the gathering profession your farm needs: Herbalism for lashers, Mining for ZG [[d:paladin#1460963936063983751]].
3. **Engineering for lashers:** "Don't go Engi for Lashers. Just do that when you're moving on" to Stratholme and bigger vamp pulls [[d:paladin#1427703031994122332]].
4. **Ret with a Runeblade plan:** Blacksmithing (Swordsmith) until you craft the blade, then drop it [[d:paladin#1443679533701005333]]. Dropping a profession resets it to 1 if you ever retrain it [[d:paladin#1462227108733391111]].

## Race interactions

- **Dwarf:** Blacksmithing is "cultural BiS" [[d:paladin#1455271652781719740]]. That is flavor, not a mechanical bonus.
- **Goblin/Gnome:** paladins cannot be Goblins or Gnomes. Only Human, Dwarf and High Elf can be paladins [[d:paladin#1486906729110442084]].

## Gold

- **Dire Maul East lashers** are the staple from about level 52-54, and "prot paladin is considered the best class for lasher farming" [[d:paladin#1426529078080700417]], [[d:paladin#1372276033608351847]]. Rates depend on the server: about 80g/h on Nordanaar without Herbalism, much less on Ambershire, where herbs sell cheaply [[d:paladin#1437840447136465021]], [[d:paladin#1437841107730825328]]. Some Ambershire reports were 35g/h [[d:paladin#1471652401516646480]]. Prices fell because so many paladins farm lashers, not because of a nerf [[d:paladin#1428169924966223992]].
- **ZG as a miner** is "the most consistent" paladin farm [[d:paladin#1489955311077953687]].
- **Stratholme live** with a Force Reactive Disk and a vamp set [[d:paladin#1452279624687026217]], [[d:paladin#1491306289869885440]].
- **Passive income:** transmute cooldowns across alts and Cured Rugged Hides [[d:paladin#1460006985624719725]], [[d:paladin#1453313097883451444]].

Farm routes and builds: [Leveling: gold farming and AoE](leveling.md#gold-farming-and-aoe).
