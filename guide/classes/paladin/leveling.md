# Leveling a Paladin (1-60)

**Recommendation:** level as **Retribution** if you want to quest and are new to the class; level as **Protection** if you want to tank dungeons all the way up or AoE-grind packs. After 1.18.1 the two are close: "ret probably has a slight edge, but it's not like prot is a crawl anymore" [[d:paladin#1486891618769571880]]; "if u aoe the kill quests, prot will be stronger, if u single target the kill quests, ret will be stronger" [[d:paladin#1451241349411246215]]. If you plan to tank at 60, level as Prot to learn the spec [[d:paladin#1486891905605435533]]. **Do not level as Holy** unless you have a permanent duo partner: "unless you have a buddy with you 24/7 going holy for leveling is bait" [[d:paladin#1455241625717706865]], [[d:paladin#1432380186573279335]].

| Spec | Leveling rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Retribution** | A | Simple, fast single-target questing, no special gear or knowledge needed [[d:paladin#1451241349411246215]], [[d:paladin#1486891618769571880]] |
| **Protection** | A (S for AoE grinders) | "One of the strongest levelling classes" [[d:paladin#1489343848537784440]]; instant dungeon invites; faster than Ret only if you AoE well, which "requires a bit of game knowledge" [[d:paladin#1439960802860863619]], [[d:paladin#1488201519885975693]] |
| **Holy/Prot hybrid** | A for farming | The classic AoE self-sustain route: Seal of Wisdom at 18 plus a fast weapon [[d:paladin#1472511842961063948]]; "Holy prot is one of the best levelers on turtle" [[d:paladin#1493020565580026138]] |
| **Holy** | C | Worst solo experience of the three specs; fine for dungeon healing after 5/5 Blessed Strikes [[d:paladin#1432380186573279335]] |

**Hardcore:** Prot or Holy/Prot is "way safer than retri, at least until you unlock repentance" [[d:paladin#1459860842945445932]], but do not AoE-farm on Hardcore, because one bad pull kills you: "you can't afford an aoe farming mistake as hc" [[d:paladin#1486432542772039881]], [[d:paladin#1436745837156892827]]. Paladin is the recommended class for "slow and steady" Hardcore leveling [[d:paladin#1486415393919598804]].

This follows Mithreon's two leveling checkpoints exactly: "For solo questing you want Seal of Command by lvl 30", then "by lvl 50 you get Vengeance, improved Zeal, even hit from Prot tree" [[d:paladin#1440662105140236349]], [[d:paladin#1440662371684192306]]. After 50 "you can do whatever until lvl 60 and respec into one of the endgame builds" [[d:paladin#1440662571471605780]]. ## Talent order: Protection (dungeon tank and AoE), level 10 → 60

Straight Prot from 10 avoids the respec fee: "it's kind of useless early on but you'll unlock imp.Fury and BoS without having to spend money in the shortest time" [[d:paladin#1469263833855168678]]. Mithreon's priority is "Blessing of Sanctuary asap, then Holy Shield asap", and Improved Righteous Fury as soon as you tank dungeons [[d:paladin#1441406630318047342]], [[d:paladin#1441406789982883951]]. The order ends in the current raid build **11/33/7** (Mithreon's post-patch "general raid prot" [[d:paladin#1491936698689388746]]; "final lvl 60 prot build will be 11/33/7" [[d:paladin#1487610577357639830]]), so no respec is needed at 60. The first 21 points match Jr's pinned level-20 and level-30 codes [[d:paladin#1407454061623513088]] except for the two filler points at 27-28. It is the same order as the `protection-tank` bot playbook.

## Gameplay by level

What to press in each bracket, for both leveling paths above: **Ret** (questing) and **Prot** (packs and dungeons). Learn levels, ranks, costs and cooldowns come from the 1.18.1 game data. Talent abilities are listed at the level the talent orders above take them. Three rules hold at every level:

- **Holy Strike and Crusader Strike share one 6 s cooldown.** You get one strike every 6 s, so choosing a strike means choosing which one to press.
- **Judgement is off the global cooldown** (10 s cooldown). Re-sealing costs a GCD and mana, so every Judgement costs a re-seal.
- **Holy Strike refunds its own mana** and ignores armor. While leveling it is almost always the better strike, because it also heals you [[d:paladin#1467563817193050279]].

Seal of Righteousness rank 1 and Holy Light rank 1 are starting spells (vanilla baseline). The trainer list begins at their next ranks.

### Levels 1–9

**New now:** L1 Devotion Aura · L4 Judgement, Blessing of Might, Holy Strike rank 1 (class quest), Divine Protection · L6 Seal of the Crusader, Holy Light rank 2 (60 mana, 2.5 s), Purify · L8 Crusader Strike, Hammer of Justice (60 s), Hand of Protection (300 s).

**Single target (both specs):**

1. Blessing of Might and Devotion Aura before you pull. Devotion Aura is "BiS for leveling unless you're aoe farming" [[d:paladin#1435218213372694570]].
2. Keep a seal up. **Contested:** Seal of Righteousness "is too much mana for too little benefit" at level 8 [[d:paladin#1426356288169836595]], but another player says it beats Seal of the Crusader early ("at very low level they're extremely close but sor takes it over pretty early") [[d:paladin#1439205873397268641]]. Use SoR if your mana lasts from pull to pull. Never judge Crusader on a mob that dies in a few swings.
3. Auto-attack. Press **Holy Strike** every time the strike cooldown is up. From 8, Crusader Strike is the other option on the same cooldown. Use it only while you are above about half mana [[d:paladin#1473978354691604500]].
4. **Judgement only to finish** a mob under 20% or one that is running away [[d:paladin#1473978111036100679]].
5. Between pulls, Holy Light yourself if needed, then eat or drink.

**Multi-target:** you have no AoE. Pull one mob at a time. If a second one joins, Hammer of Justice it and kill the first. Divine Protection is the emergency button.

**Tanking and healing:** not yet. Righteous Fury comes at 16, and the first dungeons are around 15.

### Levels 10–19

**New now:** L10 Hand of Reckoning (taunt, 10 s, off the GCD), Lay on Hands, Seal of Righteousness rank 2 · L12 Holy Strike rank 2, Redemption (quest) · L14 **Blessing of Wisdom**, Holy Light rank 3 (110 mana) · L16 **Righteous Fury**, **Retribution Aura** · L18 **Seal of Wisdom**, Seal of Righteousness rank 3, Hand of Freedom. Talents: Ret takes Benediction, Deflection and Improved Judgement; Prot takes Redoubt, Precision and Toughness. None of them changes the buttons.

**Ret, single target:**

1. Blessing of Wisdom on yourself from 14 [[d:paladin#1452839978001371166]]. Aura: Devotion Aura with a two-hander ("like half a shield while using a 2h as ret") [[d:paladin#1424485380048027689]], or Retribution Aura from 16 as in the kill loop below.
2. Seal of Righteousness.
3. Holy Strike on cooldown. Crusader Strike on the shared cooldown only while you are above ~50% mana [[d:paladin#1473978354691604500]]. "Going oom is a bigger DPS loss than not using CS" [[d:paladin#1467556801900974396]].
4. Judgement only to finish. Do not re-seal on a dying mob [[d:paladin#1452839704889393349]].
5. From 18: when you are low on mana on the last mob, swap to Seal of Wisdom for a few swings instead of drinking [[d:paladin#1485702321647124602]].

**Prot, solo:** play it like Ret with a shield until 20. Use Blessing of Wisdom "till sanctuary" [[d:paladin#1425264348489711837]]. Prot leveling "kicks in" once you have Seal of Wisdom at 18 [[d:paladin#1485331751587811479]].

**Multi-target:** still single pulls. From 18, Seal of Wisdom lets a Prot handle two mobs without drinking.

**Tanking (dungeons, from 16):** most players say to wait for Consecration at 20 [[d:paladin#1432487344417669121]]. If you tank at 16–19:

1. Turn on Righteous Fury, which only raises threat from Holy damage [[d:paladin#1425262878130180229]], and Devotion or Retribution Aura. Wear a shield in dungeons so the healer's mana lasts [[d:paladin#1457392836151345193]].
2. Put Seal of Righteousness on and mark a skull.
3. Spread threat by tabbing: "Holy strike on mob, press tab, judge other mob, press tab, auto attack with SoR other mob" [[d:paladin#1425262949639131186]]. Hammer of Justice the third [[d:paladin#1463624373092090078]].
4. Hand of Reckoning on anything that leaves you.
5. Emergencies: Divine Protection on yourself, Hand of Protection on a DPS who has aggro, Lay on Hands.

**Healing (dungeons, 15–19):** any spec can heal these; talents do not matter yet [[d:paladin#1481960882652315770]]. "Int on gear and mana pots/mana water is all you'll need" [[d:paladin#1429466401910947848]], and Intellect beats everything else at low level [[d:paladin#1486879089032957962]].

1. **Holy Light rank 3** (L14) is your only real heal. Use rank 2 for small top-ups. "Early on you will spam Holy Light until you get Flash of Light" [[d:paladin#1456972397163843678]].
2. **Holy Strike** when you stand in melee. It heals you and wounded allies near you for no net mana, but it is weak without talents [[d:paladin#1456972397163843678]].
3. **From 18:** Seal of Wisdom, then melee a mob between heals to get mana back [[d:paladin#1395273597840261130]].
4. **Purify** poisons and diseases.
5. **Mana:** stop casting to let mana regenerate (the five-second rule, vanilla baseline) and drink between pulls.

### Levels 20–29

**New now:** L20 **Consecration** rank 1 (120 mana, 8 s cooldown), **Flash of Light** rank 1 (35 mana, 1.5 s), Exorcism (15 s), Holy Strike rank 3, Crusader Strike rank 2. Prot talent Blessing of Sanctuary at 20; Ret talent Pursuit of Justice at 20–21 · L22 **Concentration Aura**, Holy Light rank 4 (190 mana), Seal of Justice, Blessing of Salvation · L24 Hammer of Justice rank 2 · L26 Flash of Light rank 2 (50 mana), Seal of Righteousness rank 4 · L28 Holy Strike rank 4, Exorcism rank 2, Seal of Wisdom rank 2. Talents: Prot takes Improved Righteous Fury (21–23) and Shield Specialization (24–26); Ret takes Conviction, Improved Retribution Aura and Two-Handed Weapon Specialization.

**Ret, single target:** the 10–19 list, plus:

1. **Exorcism** on Undead and Demons, pulled from range or during the fight [[d:paladin#1473954986609344543]].
2. At this level, "just focus on CS and HS when you need healing" [[d:paladin#1491895729139093654]].

**Ret, multi-target:** add Consecration on 3+ mobs only if you have the mana. Ret still mostly kills one mob at a time [[d:paladin#1451241349411246215]].

**Prot, solo AoE (from 20):**

1. Blessing of Sanctuary, Righteous Fury, Retribution Aura [[d:paladin#1488343219153670284]].
2. Seal of Wisdom, then judge Wisdom and keep Seal of Wisdom up [[d:paladin#1467022959960133653]].
3. Pull 3–4 mobs 1–3 levels below you. Pull no casters: "rule n1 of prot paladin leveling is to avoid enemies with spells" [[d:paladin#1478149717362741258]].
4. Drop Consecration before the mobs reach you, then use Holy Strike on cooldown. Consecration's front-load starts when a mob enters it, so pre-casting loses nothing [[d:paladin#1484776924046692372]]. Recast it every 8 s while 3+ mobs are alive.
5. Below ~50% health: Concentration Aura, then Holy Light or Flash of Light on yourself, then back to Retribution Aura [[d:paladin#1426916449129336862]].

**Tanking (dungeons):** from here you can tank properly [[d:paladin#1432487344417669121]].

1. **Buffs:** Retribution Aura, Righteous Fury, Blessing of Wisdom; Blessing of Sanctuary if you are Prot [[d:paladin#1428748823458742342]].
2. **Pull:** pre-cast Seal of Wisdom (trash) or Seal of Righteousness (boss). Run in and drop Consecration *before* you reach the mobs. "If you cast it as you reach them, they _will_ run straight through the consecrate without taking damage" [[d:paladin#1461015656781582367]].
3. **First GCDs:** Holy Strike the skull → Judgement → re-seal → Hand of Reckoning or Hammer of Justice on a runner [[d:paladin#1421451485169975356]].
4. **Steady loop:** Holy Strike on cooldown, Judgement on cooldown, re-seal. Consecration only on 3+ mobs. On two mobs it is not worth the mana, because "Consecration is just there for the 4th target and up" [[d:paladin#1463623306107162715]].
5. **Never Crusader Strike as a tank.** It is physical, so Righteous Fury does not boost it: "In dungeons, always holy strike" [[d:paladin#1465095694464585840]], [[d:paladin#1416457668373119076]].
6. **Mobs leaving you:** a mob runs to anyone who drinks, buffs, or has a heal-over-time on them [[d:paladin#1461017585184276572]]. Taunt it back, or Holy Strike the loose mob.
7. **Mana:** "You should be drinking whenever your healer is" [[d:paladin#1463623306107162715]].

**Healing (dungeons):** in low dungeons, healers "play Flash of Light simulator until you get Blessed Strikes" [[d:paladin#1483859536648474644]].

| Situation | Spell |
|---|---|
| Normal tank damage | Flash of Light rank 1–2 (35/50 mana) on repeat [[d:paladin#1452051332470145065]] |
| Tank at ~60% or lower | Holy Light rank 4 until near full, then back to Flash of Light [[d:paladin#1452052778427088937]] |
| Several people chipped, you are in melee | Holy Strike |
| Pushback from mobs hitting you | Concentration Aura (from 22) |
| Mana | Judge Wisdom → Seal of Wisdom → Holy Strike, then heal in a constant rhythm [[d:paladin#1408151414445834242]]. Avoid max ranks: in dungeons max-rank healing is "a mana waste most of the time" [[d:paladin#1344732617190281337]]. Use "whatever rank keeps people alive without draining your mana before you're ready to be at 0" [[d:paladin#1453345051177521277]] |

Triage: tank first, then yourself, then DPS who pulled aggro. Blessing of Protection a DPS rather than healing them through aggro (vanilla baseline).

### Levels 30–39

**New now:** L30 **Seal of Command** (Ret talent, rank 1), **Holy Shield** (Prot talent, 135 mana, 10 s cooldown), **Holy Shock** (Holy talent; only on the Holy/Prot variant; 280 mana, 20 s cooldown, 20 yd), **Cleanse**, Seal of Light, Consecration rank 2 (205 mana), Crusader Strike rank 3, Holy Light rank 5 (275 mana), Divine Intervention, Lay on Hands rank 2 · L34 **Divine Shield** (300 s), Flash of Light rank 3 (70 mana), Seal of Righteousness rank 5 · L36 Holy Strike rank 5, Exorcism rank 3 · L38 Holy Light rank 6 (365 mana), Holy Shock rank 2, Seal of Wisdom rank 3, Hand of Protection rank 3. Talents: Ret takes Conviction 4–5, Vengeance (33–37) and Vengeful Strikes (38–42); Prot takes Righteous Defense (31–33), Reckoning (34–36) and Righteous Strikes (37–41).

**Ret, single target:**

1. **Seal of Command rank 1** replaces Seal of Righteousness as your seal. Ranks only change the Judgement, so rank 1 is enough [[d:paladin#878187962712596490]].
2. Holy Strike on cooldown to keep Holy Might up. Crusader Strike on the shared cooldown for Zeal while your mana allows [[d:paladin#1491895229068873849]]. Crusader Strike starts to pull ahead "as you get better weapons and vengeful strikes (so after lvl 35)" [[d:paladin#1467556599877996627]].
3. Judgement only to finish a mob.
4. Exorcism on Undead and Demons.

**Ret, multi-target:** unchanged. Seal of Command has little AoE, so fight one mob at a time and add Consecration on 3+ mobs when mana allows.

**Prot, solo AoE:** Holy Shield before every pull. Prot takes off "with holy shield and reckoning, so like 34ish" [[d:paladin#1473436732656582806]]. In green gear, pull 1–2 packs [[d:paladin#1435345130075983932]]. Divine Shield from 34 saves a bad pull.

**Tanking (dungeons):**

1. **Pull:** mark a skull, cast Seal of Righteousness, cast Holy Shield while running in, then Holy Strike and Judgement on the skull and drop Consecration [[d:paladin#1431134534631555173]].
2. **Loop:** Holy Strike on cooldown → Judgement → re-seal → Holy Shield when it drops → Consecration on 3+ mobs.
3. **Mana:** Holy Shield rank 1 "will double your consumption. Feel free to stop using it after the first cast on a pack" [[d:paladin#1463623306107162715]]. Downrank Consecration when packs die fast [[d:paladin#1436793203482296331]]. Seal of Wisdom on trash, Seal of Righteousness on bosses [[d:paladin#1428748823458742342]].
4. **Pack size:** two packs at a time works at 33 unless the healer is low or you are squishy [[d:paladin#1428747262015569951]].
5. **Emergencies:** Divine Shield drops your threat, so use it only to survive. Lay on Hands; Hand of Protection on a DPS who has aggro.

**Healing (dungeons):**

1. **Flash of Light rank 3** (70 mana, from 34) for most healing. Holy Light rank 5 or 6 for big tank damage.
2. **Holy Shock** is the emergency instant heal, but only if you took it (Holy/Prot variant); the Ret and Prot orders above skip it. It is expensive: one level-34 Holy healer reported that "shock + holy light" to keep the tank up in Stormwrought ran them out of mana [[d:paladin#1452050947735031999]].
3. **Holy Strike** in melee for small party heals. The real melee-healing playstyle needs the Holy talents Ironclad (from 26) and 5/5 Blessed Strikes (from 39), so it does not work in the Ret or Prot leveling builds [[d:paladin#1395273597840261130]]. Blessed Strikes raises the heal's scaling from 5% to 30% of healing power [[d:paladin#1446238314255618109]].
4. **Cleanse** (from 30) removes magic, poison and disease.

### Levels 40–49

**New now:** L40 Consecration rank 3 (290 mana), Crusader Strike rank 4, Seal of Command rank 3, Seal of Light rank 2, Hammer of Justice rank 3, Blessing of Light, Blessing of Sanctuary rank 2, Holy Wrath (645 mana, 2 s cast, 60 s), **Warhorse** (quest). Prot talent Bulwark of the Righteous at 42 (300 s) · L42 Flash of Light rank 4 (90 mana), Seal of Righteousness rank 6 · L44 **Hammer of Wrath** (1 s cast, 6 s cooldown), Holy Strike rank 6, Exorcism rank 4 · L46 Holy Light rank 7 (465 mana) · L48 Holy Shock rank 3, Seal of Wisdom rank 4. Talents: Ret finishes Vengeful Strikes, then Redoubt and Precision; Prot takes Divine Intellect, then Improved Seal of Righteousness. Repentance is a level-40 talent, but the Ret order takes it last, at 60.

**Ret, single target:** the 30–39 list. Hammer of Wrath is an optional finisher below 20%; many players skip it because it is low damage for its mana [[d:paladin#1441422778774454365]]. The Gilneas City ring (43–44) is the first spellstrike item and a big mana boost [[d:paladin#1424711943343706183]].

**Prot, solo AoE:** same loop. Bulwark of the Righteous makes bigger packs survivable [[d:paladin#1328381614782152867]]. Fast weapons give more Seal of Wisdom procs to pay for Holy Shield and Consecration [[d:paladin#1494348050561503232]].

**Tanking (dungeons):** same as 30–39. Bulwark is your big defensive cooldown for boss burst or a bad pull.

**Healing (dungeons):** Flash of Light rank 4 as the default, Holy Light rank 7 for the tank, Cleanse and Purify. A Ret or Prot in an Intellect set can heal dungeons "all the way to 50lvl (then it gets significantly worse)" [[d:paladin#1362082588281405560]].

### Levels 50–60

**New now:** L50 Consecration rank 4 (390 mana), Crusader Strike rank 5, Seal of Command rank 4, Seal of Righteousness rank 7, Seal of Light rank 3, Flash of Light rank 5 (115 mana), Holy Shield rank 2, Divine Shield rank 2, Lay on Hands rank 3, Bulwark rank 2 · L52 Holy Strike rank 7, Exorcism rank 5, Hammer of Wrath rank 2, Greater Blessing of Might · L54 Holy Light rank 8 (580 mana), Greater Blessing of Wisdom, Hammer of Justice rank 4 · L56 Holy Shock rank 4 · L58 Flash of Light rank 6 (140 mana), Seal of Righteousness rank 8, Seal of Wisdom rank 5 · L60 Holy Strike rank 8, Consecration rank 5 (505 mana), Flash of Light rank 7 (180 mana), Holy Wrath rank 2, Holy Light rank 9, the other Greater Blessings, Charger (quest). Talents: Ret spends the last points on Deflection, Divine Strength, Divine Intellect and Repentance (60); Prot on Sanctity Aura (53), Benediction and Improved Judgement.

**Ret:** the same loop. After 50 "you can do whatever until lvl 60 and respec" [[d:paladin#1440662571471605780]]. At 60, respec and switch to the [Retribution rotation](retribution.md#how-to-play).

**Prot:** Lashers in Dire Maul East from 52–54 [[d:paladin#1459712646252204133]]. Keep Retribution Aura over Sanctity for AoE farming [[d:paladin#1486673767294963723]].

**Tanking (dungeons):** near 50–60, "you'll only really use SoW to judge then ur all SoR" [[d:paladin#1428222847570153592]]. Holy Strike and Judgement of Righteousness carry single-target threat; Consecration handles packs. At 60 the loop becomes the [Protection rotation](protection.md#how-to-play).

**Healing (dungeons):** Flash of Light rank 5–7 as the default, Holy Light rank 8–9 for big tank hits, Holy Shock rank 4 if talented. At 60, see [Holy](holy.md#how-to-play).

## The kill loop

### Retribution (single target)

"Literally just press holy strike, seal of righteousness, and judgement" [[d:paladin#1467562981163532460]]. In detail:

1. Blessing of Wisdom (from 14, a mana-per-5 buff) [[d:paladin#1452839978001371166]], [[d:paladin#1452840011295887393]]; Retribution Aura.
2. Seal: **Seal of Righteousness before 30, Seal of Command (rank 1) after 30**; Seal of Wisdom for mana, Seal of Light for healing [[d:paladin#1491887233761017987]].
3. Auto-attack. **Holy Strike** to keep Holy Might up, **Crusader Strike** as filler [[d:paladin#1491895229068873849]]; below ~50% mana use Holy Strike instead of Crusader Strike, since it is mana-neutral [[d:paladin#1473978111036100679]].
4. **Judgement only to finish** a mob under 20% [[d:paladin#1473978111036100679]], [[d:paladin#1458518892631822591]]. Do not re-seal or judge a mob that is about to die: "it's better if you spend 3 extra seconds waiting for an auto than to spend 40% of your mana bar then drink for 10s" [[d:paladin#1452839704889393349]], [[d:paladin#1452839874154729573]].
5. Exorcism on Undead and Demons [[d:paladin#1473954986609344543]].
6. Out of mana on the last mob: switch to Seal of Wisdom for a few swings instead of drinking [[d:paladin#1485702321647124602]].

Going out of mana is normal for Ret [[d:paladin#1473978111036100679]]. The fixes: mana potions and tea, less Consecration, Seal/Judgement of Wisdom, a spellstrike enchant on the weapon (Fiery or Lifestealing), and the Benediction talent [[d:paladin#1453485716242829433]].

### Protection (packs and dungeons)

Solo AoE pull (from 20, better from 30):

1. Blessing of Sanctuary, Righteous Fury, Retribution Aura [[d:paladin#1488343219153670284]].
2. Seal of Wisdom, judge Wisdom, keep Seal of Wisdom up [[d:paladin#1467022959960133653]].
3. Pull 3-4 mobs 1-3 levels below you; avoid casters [[d:paladin#1463259492408889355]]. A level-22 can take three level 18-19 mobs [[d:paladin#1459768631129804851]].
4. **Holy Shield**, **Consecration** on cooldown, Holy Strike on cooldown; "everything just sticks to you" [[d:paladin#1467022959960133653]].
5. Low on health: Concentration Aura + Spiritual Focus, Flash of Light or Holy Light, then back to Retribution Aura [[d:paladin#1426916449129336862]].

Pack size by gear (Mithreon): green gear and Holy/Prot, 1-2 packs; green gear with Vampirism, 2-3 [[d:paladin#1435345130075983932]]. Prot "really" takes off with Holy Shield and Reckoning, "so like 34ish" [[d:paladin#1473436732656582806]]; before that, clear camps of 3-4 mobs [[d:paladin#1465096099646935257]].

## Tanking dungeons while leveling

- **When:** from 20, once you have Consecration: "Wait till atleast 20 to tank dungeons" [[d:paladin#1432487344417669121]]. Any spec can tank with Righteous Fury up "as long as your party doesn't go overboard with AOE's" [[d:paladin#1490594732144066661]]; from 15 to 40 your talents barely matter for tanking [[d:paladin#1459864211671744698]].
- **Opener:** precast Seal of Righteousness and Holy Shield, judge the cross, Holy Strike the skull, Consecrate [[d:paladin#1456698478888620197]]. Grey's cookie cutter: "Holy Strike Skull, JoR X, SoR Triangle", and stun the third mob [[d:paladin#1463624373092090078]].
- **Trash:** Dzonathan's routine: Retribution Aura, Righteous Fury, Blessing of Wisdom; prebuff Seal of Wisdom and Holy Shield; Holy Strike + Judgement, Consecrate; re-seal Wisdom and refresh Consecration and Holy Shield; Seal of Righteousness on bosses [[d:paladin#1428748823458742342]].
- **Always Holy Strike in dungeons.** Crusader Strike is physical and loses the Righteous Fury threat bonus: "for dungeons you will be losing on 60%+ threat" [[d:paladin#1465095694464585840]], [[d:paladin#1440804793101582428]].
- **Mana:** Holy Strike and SoR autos are free; Judgement of Righteousness costs about 20 mana per second; rank-1 Holy Shield doubles that, so stop after the first cast on a pack; Consecration is "just there for the 4th target and up" [[d:paladin#1463623306107162715]].
- There is no big baseline AoE snap threat; Righteous Strikes and Improved Righteous Fury, then Goblin Sappers, fill the gap [[d:paladin#1442170315315609640]].
- Ret can heal most leveling dungeons: a paladin with zero talent points can heal every pre-60 dungeon in healing gear [[d:paladin#1446939678459822251]].

## Stats, weapons and gear by bracket

**Stats:** Prot: Stamina, spell power, Intellect, Strength, Agility [[d:paladin#1486596157973991424]]; "for leveling it's really just stam>everything else" [[d:paladin#1455719079875969065]]. Ret: Stamina, Strength, Agility, then spell power, crit or haste if available; "a good weapon and good stam will get you there" [[d:paladin#1446219563279847615]], [[d:paladin#1446219750672830715]]. Spell power items only appear from about level 34 [[d:paladin#1470872634236473518]]. "Everything below 60 is irrelevant", so do not chase bracket BiS [[d:paladin#1446546881508409406]].

**Weapons:** "Weapon is by far the most important thing for ret when leveling" [[d:paladin#1459938463506759832]]. Holy Strike now scales with weapon damage (1.18.1), so older advice that a two-hander adds nothing to Holy Strike is outdated [[d:paladin#1451005498697453790]].

## Gold farming and AoE

- **Lashers** (Dire Maul East) are the class gold staple from 52-54 [[d:paladin#1426529078080700417]], [[d:paladin#1459712646252204133]], [[d:paladin#1437470751639994561]]. They are Nature-immune, so the Force Reactive Disk, thorns and Dream's Herald do nothing there [[d:paladin#1426580107476336682]].
- **Farm specs** after 60: Holy/Prot for low-Vampirism gear, Vengeance Prot (0/26/25) with 8%+ Vampirism [[d:paladin#1454470101863633051]], [[d:paladin#1491414001979228363]].
- **Stratholme and big vamp pulls** need about 16% Vampirism on gear because AoE lifesteal is multiplied by 0.7; 10% is the bare minimum [[d:paladin#1480326094812086284]]. The Force Reactive Disk (300 Engineering) is "the latchkey of paladin farming" but needs frequent repairs [[d:paladin#1488941850139033770]].
- **Grinding spots:** Un'Goro for 50-55 [[d:paladin#1474449175986045160]]; Dire Maul East thrashers for 50-60 [[d:paladin#1436295002459344926]].
- **Rested XP:** the guild tabard teleports you to the guild headquarters inn, where 10 minutes gives 150% rested; many cross-faction guilds use Booty Bay [[d:paladin#1457383395704307893]]. Quests stay green for 25 levels [[d:paladin#1432488112503787521]].

## Class quests and mounts

- **Redemption** at 12, Stormwind Cathedral [[d:paladin#1459353566849532108]]. If you lose the Tome of Divinity, the Ironforge counterpart NPC can give another [[d:paladin#1459400249305993319]].
- **Warhorse** at 40: free mount and riding skill from a Cathedral quest [[d:paladin#1446220359878836254]].
- **Charger** at 60: questline from the Cathedral, about 400g including riding, against 800g+ for other classes [[d:paladin#1446220359878836254]].
- A turtle mount is available from level 18 [[d:paladin#1465059618043465950]].
- **Weapon skill:** the "Ancient Warfare" quest on Fray Island gives +5 weapon skill for one weapon type for 250g [[d:paladin#1432001555271254208]], [[d:paladin#1481676735404572833]].

## Professions while leveling

Pick two of Herbalism, Mining and Skinning while leveling [[d:paladin#1436709623355932702]]. Engineering is the one profession that matters for performance in every role (explosives, sappers, Force Reactive Disk) [[d:paladin#1456908911972974754]]. Blacksmithing is good for making your own leveling gear, but skip its specializations unless you want the Tempered Runeblade [[d:paladin#1456908911972974754]], [[d:paladin#1428433981707124838]]. A Paladin + Priest duo levels very well [[d:paladin#1494344380780253295]].

## Common mistakes

1. **Leveling as Holy without a duo** [[d:paladin#1455241625717706865]].
2. **Using Crusader Strike as your main strike as Prot** or in dungeons [[d:paladin#1440804793101582428]].
3. **Keeping Seal of the Crusader active** [[d:paladin#1474136994195046481]].
4. **Taking Sanctity Aura or stat talents early**: stat talents such as Divine Intellect "do absolutely next to nothing for low-level players" [[d:paladin#1481960882652315770]], and Sanctity Aura is not worth it while leveling [[d:paladin#1456918732579279054]].
5. **Following "17/17/17" meme builds** posted as jokes [[d:paladin#1441415536981311598]].
6. **Judging and re-sealing mobs that are about to die**, then sitting to drink [[d:paladin#1452839704889393349]].
7. **Pulling casters or mobs above your level** as an AoE Prot [[d:paladin#1463259492408889355]].
8. **Using Nature-damage gear (FRD, thorns) on Lashers** [[d:paladin#1426580107476336682]].
9. **AoE farming on Hardcore** [[d:paladin#1486432542772039881]].
