# Leveling a Paladin (1-60)

**Recommendation:** level as **Retribution** if you want to quest and are new to the class; level as **Protection** if you want to tank dungeons all the way up or AoE-grind packs. After 1.18.1 the two are close: "ret probably has a slight edge, but it's not like prot is a crawl anymore" [[d:paladin#1486891618769571880]]; "if u aoe the kill quests, prot will be stronger, if u single target the kill quests, ret will be stronger" [[d:paladin#1451241349411246215]]. If you plan to tank at 60, level as Prot to learn the spec [[d:paladin#1486891905605435533]]. **Do not level as Holy** unless you have a permanent duo partner: "unless you have a buddy with you 24/7 going holy for leveling is bait" [[d:paladin#1455241625717706865]], [[d:paladin#1432380186573279335]].

| Spec | Leveling rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Retribution** | A | Simple, fast single-target questing, no special gear or knowledge needed [[d:paladin#1451241349411246215]], [[d:paladin#1486891618769571880]] |
| **Protection** | A (S for AoE grinders) | "One of the strongest levelling classes" [[d:paladin#1489343848537784440]]; instant dungeon invites; faster than Ret only if you AoE well, which "requires a bit of game knowledge" [[d:paladin#1439960802860863619]], [[d:paladin#1488201519885975693]] |
| **Holy/Prot hybrid** | A for farming | The classic AoE self-sustain route: Seal of Wisdom at 18 plus a fast weapon [[d:paladin#1472511842961063948]]; "Holy prot is one of the best levelers on turtle" [[d:paladin#1493020565580026138]] |
| **Holy** | C | Worst solo experience of the three specs; fine for dungeon healing after 5/5 Blessed Strikes [[d:paladin#1432380186573279335]] |

**Hardcore:** Prot or Holy/Prot is "way safer than retri, at least until you unlock repentance" [[d:paladin#1459860842945445932]], but do not AoE-farm on Hardcore, because one bad pull kills you: "you can't afford an aoe farming mistake as hc" [[d:paladin#1486432542772039881]], [[d:paladin#1436745837156892827]]. Paladin is the recommended class for "slow and steady" Hardcore leveling [[d:paladin#1486415393919598804]].

## Talent order: Retribution (questing), level 10 → 60

This follows Mithreon's two leveling checkpoints exactly: "For solo questing you want Seal of Command by lvl 30", then "by lvl 50 you get Vengeance, improved Zeal, even hit from Prot tree" [[d:paladin#1440662105140236349]], [[d:paladin#1440662371684192306]]. After 50 "you can do whatever until lvl 60 and respec into one of the endgame builds" [[d:paladin#1440662571471605780]]. Stay in the Retribution tree until 5/5 Vengeance; Sanctity Aura is not worth it while leveling [[d:paladin#1456918732579279054]], and Repentance and Sanctity should be your last points [[d:paladin#1456920821196001355]].

## Talent order: Protection (dungeon tank and AoE), level 10 → 60

Straight Prot from 10 avoids the respec fee: "it's kind of useless early on but you'll unlock imp.Fury and BoS without having to spend money in the shortest time" [[d:paladin#1469263833855168678]]. Mithreon's priority is "Blessing of Sanctuary asap, then Holy Shield asap", and Improved Righteous Fury as soon as you tank dungeons [[d:paladin#1441406630318047342]], [[d:paladin#1441406789982883951]]. The order ends in the current raid build **11/33/7** (Mithreon's post-patch "general raid prot" [[d:paladin#1491936698689388746]]; "final lvl 60 prot build will be 11/33/7" [[d:paladin#1487610577357639830]]), so no respec is needed at 60. The first 21 points match Jr's pinned level-20 and level-30 codes [[d:paladin#1407454061623513088]] except for the two filler points at 27-28. It is the same order as the `protection-tank` bot playbook.

## The kill loop

### Retribution (single target)

"Literally just press holy strike, seal of righteousness, and judgement" [[d:paladin#1467562981163532460]]. In detail:

1. Blessing of Wisdom (from 14, a mana-per-5 buff) [[d:paladin#1452839978001371166]], [[d:paladin#1452840011295887393]]; Retribution Aura.
2. Seal: **Seal of Righteousness before 30, Seal of Command (rank 1) after 30**; Seal of Wisdom for mana, Seal of Light for healing [[d:paladin#1491887233761017987]].
3. Auto-attack. **Holy Strike** to keep Holy Might up, **Crusader Strike** as filler [[d:paladin#1491895229068873849]]; below ~50% mana use Holy Strike instead of Crusader Strike, since it is mana-neutral [[d:paladin#1473978111036100679]].
4. **Judgement only to finish** a mob under 20% [[d:paladin#1473978111036100679]], [[d:paladin#1458518892631822591]]. Do not re-seal or judge a mob that is about to die: "it's better if you spend 3 extra seconds waiting for an auto than to spend 40% of your mana bar then drink for 10s" [[d:paladin#1452839704889393349]], [[d:paladin#1452839874154729573]].
5. Exorcism on Undead and Demons [[d:paladin#1473954986609344543]].
6. Out of mana on the last mob: switch to Seal of Wisdom for a few swings instead of drinking [[d:paladin#1485702321647124602]].

Going out of mana is normal for Ret [[d:paladin#1473978111036100679]]. The fixes: mana potions and tea, less Consecration, Seal/Judgement of Wisdom, a spellstrike enchant on the weapon (Fiery or Lifestealing), and the Benediction talent [[d:paladin#1453485716242829433]].

### Protection (packs and dungeons)

Solo AoE pull (from 20, better from 30):

1. Blessing of Sanctuary, Righteous Fury, Retribution Aura [[d:paladin#1488343219153670284]].
2. Seal of Wisdom, judge Wisdom, keep Seal of Wisdom up [[d:paladin#1467022959960133653]].
3. Pull 3-4 mobs 1-3 levels below you; avoid casters [[d:paladin#1463259492408889355]]. A level-22 can take three level 18-19 mobs [[d:paladin#1459768631129804851]].
4. **Holy Shield**, **Consecration** on cooldown, Holy Strike on cooldown; "everything just sticks to you" [[d:paladin#1467022959960133653]].
5. Low on health: Concentration Aura + Spiritual Focus, Flash of Light or Holy Light, then back to Retribution Aura [[d:paladin#1426916449129336862]].

Pack size by gear (Mithreon): green gear and Holy/Prot, 1-2 packs; green gear with Vampirism, 2-3 [[d:paladin#1435345130075983932]]. Prot "really" takes off with Holy Shield and Reckoning, "so like 34ish" [[d:paladin#1473436732656582806]]; before that, clear camps of 3-4 mobs [[d:paladin#1465096099646935257]].

## Tanking dungeons while leveling

- **When:** from 20, once you have Consecration: "Wait till atleast 20 to tank dungeons" [[d:paladin#1432487344417669121]]. Any spec can tank with Righteous Fury up "as long as your party doesn't go overboard with AOE's" [[d:paladin#1490594732144066661]]; from 15 to 40 your talents barely matter for tanking [[d:paladin#1459864211671744698]].
- **Opener:** precast Seal of Righteousness and Holy Shield, judge the cross, Holy Strike the skull, Consecrate [[d:paladin#1456698478888620197]]. Grey's cookie cutter: "Holy Strike Skull, JoR X, SoR Triangle", and stun the third mob [[d:paladin#1463624373092090078]].
- **Trash:** Dzonathan's routine: Retribution Aura, Righteous Fury, Blessing of Wisdom; prebuff Seal of Wisdom and Holy Shield; Holy Strike + Judgement, Consecrate; re-seal Wisdom and refresh Consecration and Holy Shield; Seal of Righteousness on bosses [[d:paladin#1428748823458742342]].
- **Always Holy Strike in dungeons.** Crusader Strike is physical and loses the Righteous Fury threat bonus: "for dungeons you will be losing on 60%+ threat" [[d:paladin#1465095694464585840]], [[d:paladin#1440804793101582428]].
- **Mana:** Holy Strike and SoR autos are free; Judgement of Righteousness costs about 20 mana per second; rank-1 Holy Shield doubles that, so stop after the first cast on a pack; Consecration is "just there for the 4th target and up" [[d:paladin#1463623306107162715]].
- There is no big baseline AoE snap threat; Righteous Strikes and Improved Righteous Fury, then Goblin Sappers, fill the gap [[d:paladin#1442170315315609640]].
- Ret can heal most leveling dungeons: a paladin with zero talent points can heal every pre-60 dungeon in healing gear [[d:paladin#1446939678459822251]].

## Stats, weapons and gear by bracket

**Stats:** Prot: Stamina, spell power, Intellect, Strength, Agility [[d:paladin#1486596157973991424]]; "for leveling it's really just stam>everything else" [[d:paladin#1455719079875969065]]. Ret: Stamina, Strength, Agility, then spell power, crit or haste if available; "a good weapon and good stam will get you there" [[d:paladin#1446219563279847615]], [[d:paladin#1446219750672830715]]. Spell power items only appear from about level 34 [[d:paladin#1470872634236473518]]. "Everything below 60 is irrelevant", so do not chase bracket BiS [[d:paladin#1446546881508409406]].

**Weapons:** "Weapon is by far the most important thing for ret when leveling" [[d:paladin#1459938463506759832]]. Holy Strike now scales with weapon damage (1.18.1), so older advice that a two-hander adds nothing to Holy Strike is outdated [[d:paladin#1451005498697453790]].

## Gold farming and AoE

- **Lashers** (Dire Maul East) are the class gold staple from 52-54 [[d:paladin#1426529078080700417]], [[d:paladin#1459712646252204133]], [[d:paladin#1437470751639994561]]. They are Nature-immune, so the Force Reactive Disk, thorns and Dream's Herald do nothing there [[d:paladin#1426580107476336682]].
- **Farm specs** after 60: Holy/Prot for low-Vampirism gear, Vengeance Prot (0/26/25) with 8%+ Vampirism [[d:paladin#1454470101863633051]], [[d:paladin#1491414001979228363]].
- **Stratholme and big vamp pulls** need about 16% Vampirism on gear because AoE lifesteal is multiplied by 0.7; 10% is the bare minimum [[d:paladin#1480326094812086284]]. The Force Reactive Disk (300 Engineering) is "the latchkey of paladin farming" but needs frequent repairs [[d:paladin#1488941850139033770]].
- **Grinding spots:** Un'Goro for 50-55 [[d:paladin#1474449175986045160]]; Dire Maul East thrashers for 50-60 [[d:paladin#1436295002459344926]].
- **Rested XP:** the guild tabard teleports you to the guild headquarters inn, where 10 minutes gives 150% rested; many cross-faction guilds use Booty Bay [[d:paladin#1457383395704307893]]. Quests stay green for 25 levels [[d:paladin#1432488112503787521]].

## Class quests and mounts

- **Redemption** at 12, Stormwind Cathedral [[d:paladin#1459353566849532108]]. If you lose the Tome of Divinity, the Ironforge counterpart NPC can give another [[d:paladin#1459400249305993319]].
- **Warhorse** at 40: free mount and riding skill from a Cathedral quest [[d:paladin#1446220359878836254]].
- **Charger** at 60: questline from the Cathedral, about 400g including riding, against 800g+ for other classes [[d:paladin#1446220359878836254]].
- A turtle mount is available from level 18 [[d:paladin#1465059618043465950]].
- **Weapon skill:** the "Ancient Warfare" quest on Fray Island gives +5 weapon skill for one weapon type for 250g [[d:paladin#1432001555271254208]], [[d:paladin#1481676735404572833]].

## Professions while leveling

Pick two of Herbalism, Mining and Skinning while leveling [[d:paladin#1436709623355932702]]. Engineering is the one profession that matters for performance in every role (explosives, sappers, Force Reactive Disk) [[d:paladin#1456908911972974754]]. Blacksmithing is good for making your own leveling gear, but skip its specializations unless you want the Tempered Runeblade [[d:paladin#1456908911972974754]], [[d:paladin#1428433981707124838]]. A Paladin + Priest duo levels very well [[d:paladin#1494344380780253295]].

## Common mistakes

1. **Leveling as Holy without a duo** [[d:paladin#1455241625717706865]].
2. **Using Crusader Strike as your main strike as Prot** or in dungeons [[d:paladin#1440804793101582428]].
3. **Keeping Seal of the Crusader active** [[d:paladin#1474136994195046481]].
4. **Taking Sanctity Aura or stat talents early**: stat talents such as Divine Intellect "do absolutely next to nothing for low-level players" [[d:paladin#1481960882652315770]], and Sanctity Aura is not worth it while leveling [[d:paladin#1456918732579279054]].
5. **Following "17/17/17" meme builds** posted as jokes [[d:paladin#1441415536981311598]].
6. **Judging and re-sealing mobs that are about to die**, then sitting to drink [[d:paladin#1452839704889393349]].
7. **Pulling casters or mobs above your level** as an AoE Prot [[d:paladin#1463259492408889355]].
8. **Using Nature-damage gear (FRD, thorns) on Lashers** [[d:paladin#1426580107476336682]].
9. **AoE farming on Hardcore** [[d:paladin#1486432542772039881]].
