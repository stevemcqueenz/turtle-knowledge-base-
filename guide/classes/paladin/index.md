# Paladin

Paladins on Turtle WoW 1.18.1 fill all three roles, and all three are raid-viable: "all 3 specs of pally are fully raid viable, ret is an actual dps spec now" [[d:paladin#1486906522863927467]].

- **Holy** is the strongest spec relative to its competition: "Healer is probably the strongest relative to the other classes competing in that role, but all 3 are good and viable at endgame" [[d:paladin#1489356022106558606]]. 1.18.1 cut its AoE healing by about 10% in Kara40 [[d:paladin#1486468604290535595]], but raids still want two Holy paladins [[d:paladin#1491681710780452874]].
- **Protection** got the biggest buff of the patch: "this patch is amasing for prot" [[d:paladin#1485297028337766544]]; "Ppal threat really got a big boost" [[d:paladin#1485398358750134283]]. It is the best dungeon tank [[d:paladin#1429072615078756352]] and one of the best farmers.
- **Retribution** is a solid mid-pack melee DPS (about 1.8k DPS on Patchwerk-type fights [[d:paladin#1487136530983293162]]) with two builds: **Seal of Righteousness "spellret"** and **Seal of Command "AP Ret"**, plus the niche **Necrodin**.

**New player recommendation:** level as Retribution (or Protection if you want to tank), then pick your role at 60. See [Leveling](leveling.md). "Paladins can play all 3 roles depending on your spec ... Its up to you to choose what you want to play" [[d:paladin#1489354632395358258]].

## What changed for paladins in 1.18.1

1.18.1 went live on 2026-03-20. The class notes: [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).

| Area | Change | What it means in play |
|---|---|---|
| All | **Holy Strike** now deals weapon damage + Holy damage (rank 8: 40% weapon damage + 129-146), spell power scaling 43% → 71% | Holy Strike scales with your weapon and Strength; it opens most fights [[d:paladin#1483567863577968650]] |
| Ret | **Seal of Command 7 → 9 PPM**; Crusader Strike rank 5 90% → 100% weapon damage; Repentance ticks with 8% AP | SoC Ret is competitive again [[d:paladin#1485468559441137884]] |
| Prot | Holy Shield threat 30% → 50%; **Righteous Strikes** now +25% Holy Strike damage and threat, with Crusader Strike granting Zealous Defence | About 10% more threat at high-end gear [[d:paladin#1485350693312397362]] |
| All | **Consecration** +8% and front-loaded (first tick 156% of the old value, last 44%) | Better on fast-dying trash and for pull threat |
| Holy | **Daybreak** 289 → 248 base, 43% → 32% scaling; Holy T3.5 5-piece no longer adds Holy Strike targets | Less AoE healing; "raid ended up jumping too much back" [[d:paladin#1464883313524084769]] |
| Sets | Tier 2.5 3-piece (Ret/Prot) now +15% Judgement of Righteousness/Command; Ret T3.5 5-piece is 4% non-stacking haste; Prot T3.5 bonuses reshuffled | |
| All | **Swapping librams in combat costs a GCD** | Equip one libram per fight |
| Items | New stat **Fortune** (necks, cloaks, trinkets) [[d:paladin#1473599626522136707]]; new **Libram of Ardour** (+1 SoC PPM) [[d:paladin#1488558737147957258]]; Libram of Final Judgement scales with 8% SP ([Dragunovi (staff), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168272#p168272)) | Fortune is weak [[d:paladin#1483896373240926438]] |
| Content | **Timbermaw Hold** (TMH20) raid with Pysan's New Greatsword [[d:paladin#1489516749022953614]]; a new Onyxia's Lair boss | See [Retribution: gear](retribution.md#weapons) |

Launch bugs (Holy Strike healing, Block Value, Righteous Fury snapshots) were hotfixed in the first days ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139)); see the spec pages.

**Fake patch news:** the 2026-04-01 "next patch" post by Lhian (spellstrikes stop proccing, Holy Strike becomes a next-swing attack) was an April Fools joke [[d:paladin#1488852869212798987]], [[d:paladin#1488856415010291794]]. Earlier "leaked" talent trees and changelogs were also fake [[d:paladin#1445618286166544415]], [[d:paladin#1458287685750882425]].

Things you will meet in older guides:

- **Strikes do not trigger seals**; only auto-attacks do [[d:paladin#1432111266134097940]]. Crusader Strike and Holy Strike share a cooldown.
- **Seal twisting no longer works** [[d:paladin#1455597115454787625]].
- **Judgement is a melee attack** (melee hit and crit) [[d:paladin#1433868913984143391]], [[d:paladin#1440955522965573716]].
- **Sanctity Aura lives in the Holy tree** (11-point sink) and every Ret takes it [[d:paladin#1426699161788485723]], [[d:paladin#1435211501437718538]].
- **Seal of Command is a level-30 talent** on Turtle (20 in Classic) [[d:paladin#1459751386014027960]]; Consecration is baseline at 20 [[d:paladin#1456699452277526601]].
- **Hand of Sacrifice no longer breaks crowd control**; Hand of Freedom has a longer cooldown [[d:paladin#1487223373317542039]].
- **Melee hit cap is 8%** against bosses; 5 weapon skill = 1% hit [[d:paladin#1436561953936904222]], [[d:paladin#1488982232482840768]].

## Races and racials

Only **Human, Dwarf and High Elf** can be paladins [[d:paladin#1486906729110442084]]. The differences are small: "The difference is minimal, across the board" [[d:paladin#1468406544013201460]]; for Holy and Prot "race has literally no impact on performance aside some flavor" [[d:paladin#1472437813579612252]].

| Race | Racials that matter | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Human** | Sword and mace skill, which pairs with the Retribution weapon-skill talent and the Fray Island weapon-skill quest [[d:paladin#1442081853526773881]], [[d:paladin#1481676967186006076]]; Perception [[d:paladin#1486397667033616505]] | **Retribution** (the only race choice that really matters) [[d:paladin#1442081853526773881]]. Grey calls the weapon-skill gain "a meme" in practice [[d:paladin#1485785552828633168]] |
| **Dwarf** | Stoneform removes poisons (not Blind) [[d:paladin#1486397667033616505]]; "low key dwarf racial da best in Naxx" [[d:paladin#1441383061228097536]] | Protection and PvP [[d:paladin#1442081853526773881]] |
| **High Elf** | A mana-return racial with a cast time [[d:paladin#1441380558470123521]], [[d:paladin#1441380961966358593]]; the 2% Agility racial "entirely disappears to rounding" [[d:paladin#1427689481980612760]] | Holy: "Helf is arguably bis for healing" [[d:paladin#1494508872017379379]] |


## Professions

- **Level with two of Herbalism, Mining and Skinning** [[d:paladin#1436709623355932702]].
- **At 60, take Engineering.** "The only main profession that benefits you in raids is engineering" [[d:paladin#1486890674321096929]], because "no other proffession offers unique enchants/abilities" [[d:paladin#1437832214326022334]]. It gives Sappers, grenades and, for Prot and farming, the Force Reactive Disk [[d:paladin#1488941850139033770]]. It is "not required unless you're planning to be even semi serious in raids" [[d:paladin#1429188657431969863]]. The second main profession can be anything [[d:paladin#1486890896782790758]]. Keep Herbalism for lashers or Mining for ZG.
- **Retribution:** Blacksmithing (Swordsmith) makes the Tempered Runeblade, a pre-raid Spellret weapon ("gives bis sword for 2 phases of a server") [[d:paladin#1428433981707124838]]. Otherwise Blacksmithing is only a leveling-gear profession [[d:paladin#1456908911972974754]].

Full guide: [Professions](professions.md)
