# Hunter PvP

**Recommendation:** for battlegrounds, play **Marksmanship 0/34/17**: MM down to Lock and Load, Survival down to Survivalist, no Barrage. The **Aimed Shot → Arcane Shot** burst hits armored targets for 2,000+. You also keep full damage when your pet is crowd-controlled. **Beast Mastery** is the easy-mode alternative while pets take no PvP dampening. Survival is strong against warriors and rogues, and weak against casters, paladins and warlocks.

**Contested:** is MM or BM the better PvP spec?

- **MM:** "MM is way better for pvp rn" [[d:hunter#1494177202508402711]], [[d:hunter#1494186047612129440]]. BM becomes "a bit of a deadweight" without Bestial Wrath once a mage or druid CCs the pet, while MM has "more constant damage, waaay more survivability/crowdcontrol" [[d:hunter#1494186537129218099]].
- **BM:** "BM pets are monsters in bgs. basically another player on your team" [[d:hunter#1489662317913440306]]; "you don't need to be very skilled in pvp to have some fun" [[d:hunter#1489577856811143319]].

## Stats and gear

- **Ranged hit 8%**, then Agility and crit (burst). See [Marksmanship](marksmanship.md#stat-priority-and-caps).
- **Stamina + resilience** for BM. The pet gets no resilience and dies quickly when focused [[d:hunter#1492801526450884838]]. An "Improved Rez" pet-revive trinket helps a lot [[d:hunter#1492816153930760323]].
- The T2.5 3-piece bonus (a chance to reapply a Serpent Sting tick, which knocks rogues out of stealth) is rated better than T3's mana bonus for PvP [[d:hunter#1459991763157520414]], [[d:hunter#1459991807696834570]] (single source, before 1.18.1).
- Enchants: as for PvE, plus the +2% crit scope ([Marksmanship](marksmanship.md#enchants-per-slot)).

## Burst and control sequences

**MM burst:** Aimed Shot, then Arcane Shot while Enchanted Ammunition is up. It "is extremely good now vs anything with high ac" [[d:hunter#1485349629468999740]], with Arcane hitting ~1k before crits [[d:hunter#1485325181193814267]]. Multi-Shot is an opener or finisher on a low-HP target [[d:hunter#1492280220827385956]].

1. Open from max range: Aimed Shot → Arcane Shot (Enchanted Ammunition) → Auto → Steady.
2. If a melee closes in: Scatter Shot → step back → Freezing Trap (or Frost Trap + Entrapment) → kite with Concussive Shot.
3. If they reach the dead zone again: Wing Clip, then back off. Deterrence or Feign Death as a last resort.

**BM burst:** Bestial Wrath with trinkets, then Kill Command on the next crit. Devilsaur Tooth guarantees the pet's next crit [[d:hunter#1492013965692108811]]. Send the pet into the backline healer as a "dive tank" [[d:hunter#1492849347954741308]].

**Control rules (Turtle-specific):**

- **Freezing Trap after Scatter Shot lasts only 6 s.** They share diminishing returns, and the 10 s DR from 1.12 was not restored [[d:hunter#1470132967287423119]], [[d:hunter#1470137121443549435]]. Traps can be trinketed [[d:hunter#1429245770460303390]].
- **Entrapment** can root on any tick of the Frost Trap, but only once per trap [[d:hunter#1486483467830300702]], [[d:hunter#1486483314318643230]].
- **Improved Concussive Shot's stun** is a separate spell. It can be resisted and grounded [[d:hunter#1489647841629438005]].
- **Counterspell on a hunter** stops trap drops, even out of Feign Death [[d:pvp-general#1431619747358703727]].
- Wing Clip is on a 3 s cooldown and Counterattack was removed [[d:hunter#1483806089668460695]]. The dead zone remains; players want it removed [[d:hunter#1483804263733006456]].
- Hunter's Mark now reveals stealth only within 15 yards (it was 100) [[d:hunter#1470104812556386417]].

## Battleground role

- **Defend and control.** Traps (and Shadowmeld for Night Elves) hold defensive positions; Viper Sting the healers [[d:pvp-general#1427354322395463682]]. Entrapment locks chokepoints in AV [[d:hunter#1489619404449779883]].
- **Deny movement.** "For WSG though, the #1 best thing a pet can do is keep someone from having freedom of movement. A still player is a dead one" [[d:hunter#1485768500294320150]].
- **Counterplay against you:** enemies focus your pet. A dead pet is quickly resummoned [[d:hunter#1492849347954741308]].
- **PvP pets:** Wind Serpent (damage), Spider (root), Boar (Charge root), Bat (blocks line of sight, AP debuff), Moth (heal + attack-speed debuff) [[d:hunter#1485767924273778748]], [[d:hunter#1485768101940432957]]. A fast pet interrupts casts more often [[d:hunter#1494666708793950338]]. Details: [Pets](pets.md).

## Races

- **Night Elf:** best PvP race "if you can FD+shadowmeld combo" [[d:hunter#1460516471250616360]].
- **Tauren:** War Stomp is "best hunter racial" for stunning someone in your dead zone. It is not a trinket and hits several targets [[d:hunter#1453792390098714936]], [[d:hunter#1453792771377987685]].
- **Gnome:** "the meta pvp race for hunter but no one wants to admit it" [[d:hunter#1456327236729835611]].
- **Orc:** Hardiness now shortens stuns instead of rolling for immunity; players petition for 25% [[d:hunter#1438023759352172544]], [[d:hunter#1438024404788449352]].
- **Undead:** Will of the Forsaken saves your trinket against warlock Seduce and Fear [[d:hunter#1456421765013049395]], [[d:hunter#1426350773872230551]].

## Macros

- Wing Clip in melee, Concussive Shot otherwise [[d:hunter#1484736445028630529]]:

  ```
  /run if CheckInteractDistance("target",3) then CastSpellByName("Wing Clip") else CastSpellByName("Concussive Shot"); end
  ```

- Traps with modifiers, auto-Feign in combat (needs CleveRoidMacros) [[d:hunter#1447259183773913320]]:

  ```
  #showtooltip
  /cast [mod:alt]Explosive Trap; [mod:ctrl] Immolation Trap; [mod:shift] Frost Trap; Freezing Trap
  /cast [combat] Feign Death
  /petfollow [combat]
  ```

- Viper Sting on mana users, Serpent Sting otherwise [[d:hunter#1462394522943094974]]:

  ```
  /run if (UnitPowerType('target')==0) then CastSpellByName("Viper Sting")else CastSpellByName("Serpent Sting")end
  ```
