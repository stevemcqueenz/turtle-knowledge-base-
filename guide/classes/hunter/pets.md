# Hunter pets

**Recommendation:**

- **Raid DPS:** a **Raptor from Zul'Gurub** (Bite + Savage Rend; the only source of Savage Rend rank 6). Train Claw too, and switch to it on bleed-immune bosses.
- **Molten Core:** a Cat or Wind Serpent, because many MC bosses are immune to bleeds and poison.
- **Long boss fights as BM:** a Scorpid is the alternative (contested).
- **Leveling and dungeon threat:** a **Screech bird** (owl, carrion bird or bat).
- **Melee-group support for Survival:** a Wolf.
- **PvP:** a Spider (root) or a Wind Serpent (burst).

In every case, keep the pet happy (green), train its resistances for the raid tier, and never leave Claw on autocast next to a real special ability.

## How to play

The pet-control loop, for every spec.

1. **Before the pull:**
    - The pet is green (happy). Feed it right before the pull; a pet that dies usually stays happy [[d:hunter#1491026532678766684]].
    - Its autocast bar is set: [autocast](#which-abilities-to-autocast). Never leave Claw on next to a real special.
2. **Solo or leveling:**
    - Growl on autocast.
    - The pet pulls. Start shooting at 75–80% [[d:hunter#1466911679668424945]].
    - Mend Pet when it drops low.
    - Level-by-level pets and pet tanking: [Leveling: Gameplay by level](leveling.md#gameplay-by-level).
3. **Group with a tank, or a raid:**
    - Growl off [[d:hunter#1428016626455937097]].
    - Send the pet exactly as the tank hits [[d:hunter#1484689822005067816]].
    - Avoid Defensive mode, which attacks whatever hits a party member [[d:hunter#1471838967711272970]].
    - Use Take Position to park the pet out of cleaves and breaths ([positioning](#positioning-in-raids)).
4. **Pet tanking (BM only):**
    - Growl on, with a Screech pet, Gorilla or Scorpid.
    - Pull threat back with Kill Command + Bite, and use Intimidation to buy time [[d:hunter#1455955199234670673]], [[d:hunter#1421044060180713484]].
    - Keep Mend Pet rolling [[d:hunter#1455955810583838955]].
    - The pet has no taunt. Growl is threat only [[d:hunter#1485140138584576110]].
5. **The pet dies:** reviving mid-fight is usually not worth it with under ~30 s left [[d:hunter#1481941619614814320]].
6. **Between fights:**
    - Refeed at yellow.
    - Do not dismiss with the spell; it halves happiness ([feeding](#feeding-and-happiness)).

**Family damage modifiers** multiply auto attacks and Kill Command (not Coordinated Assault). ## Which abilities to autocast

Pets hold at most 4 active abilities, Growl included [[d:hunter#1435109203156009102]].

1. **Raptor:** Bite + Savage Rend on autocast. Train Claw and use it instead on bleed-immune bosses [[d:hunter#1462729876296695900]], [[d:hunter#1489990661066330222]], [[d:hunter#1491436635840057494]]. There is not always enough focus for Bite and Rend together; Bite on autocast "sort of works" but can delay Rend [[d:hunter#1494815020100489437]].
2. **Claw** is "a focus dump and it's obsolete on any pet that's capable of doing damage" [[d:hunter#1489371621343887422]]. If Claw and Bite are both on autocast, the pet only ever casts Claw [[d:hunter#1451993393159995443]]. On a plain Cat, Bite on cooldown with Claw as filler beat pure Claw, 115 vs 107 DPS [[d:hunter#1491473274444714116]].
3. **Growl:** off in raids so the pet does not pull [[d:hunter#1428016626455937097]]. It is a threat ability, not a taunt [[d:hunter#1485140138584576110]].
4. **Wind Serpent:** cast Lightning Breath manually, not on autocast, so Kill Command crits it [[d:hunter#1453590502342524950]], [[d:hunter#1444975316782288896]].
5. Pets sometimes stop using their abilities. Toggle autocast, or press the ability once [[d:hunter#1494439863271948410]].

## Attack speed

- Pet **damage** is normalized; attack **speed** is not [[d:hunter#1464769543099650089]].
- **PvE with Windfury Totem:** slower is better. Windfury has an internal cooldown, so bigger swings get more from each proc [[d:hunter#1489960530461589527]], [[d:hunter#1494100858055426148]]. The difference between a 1.4 and a 2.0 pet is small [[d:hunter#1491083689948610580]].
- **BM:** faster pets help Frenzy uptime [[d:hunter#1494675024832102421]]. Haste sources on the pet multiply (a 2.0 pet with Frenzy goes to 1.54) [[d:hunter#1437019733932118076]].
- **PvP:** faster pets interrupt casts more often [[d:hunter#1494666708793950338]], [[d:hunter#1494735509564559412]].

## Taming and teaching abilities

1. Complete the level-10 class questline, or you never get Feed Pet [[d:hunter#1492136690561585234]].
2. To learn a higher rank or a new ability:
    1. Stable your pet.
    2. Tame a beast that already knows the ability.
    3. Use it in combat until you see "You have learned X (Rank N)" [[d:hunter#1424076440609226985]], [[d:hunter#1489962914000539829]].
    4. Teach it from Beast Training to any pet that can learn it [[d:hunter#1476645089051934771]].

    Not every beast of a family knows every ability, so you need the *specific* beast [[d:hunter#1447776251388498052]], [[d:hunter#1422684190406606971]].
3. Upgrading a rank costs only the difference in training points [[d:hunter#1492296087522775130]].
4. Good sources:
    - Hinterlands Eagles (35–40) know Screech rank 3 (normally level 48). Do not reset them [[d:hunter#1428475590486524116]].
    - Amani Eagles (level 10, Amani'Alor in Stonetalon) and Westfall Greater Fleshrippers (16–17) have early Screech [[d:hunter#1490087581919084586]], [[d:hunter#1490088205435797574]].
    - Only ZG raptors teach Savage Rend rank 6 [[d:hunter#1494711393981698119]].
    - Dash needs pet level 30 [[d:hunter#1490806796825137273]].
5. Taming ZG raptors: they are immune to Freezing Trap but not to Fear. Isolate one [[d:hunter#1489906331166052382]], [[d:hunter#1454348119092498534]].
6. The MC Magmalash scorpid has several fixed spawn points in a cleared MC. Only one is up at a time [[d:hunter#1494106342732861533]], [[d:hunter#1494113708224417842]].
7. **"Invalid Target" when taming:** check the mob's exact name. The level-10 quest wants "Surf Crawler", not "Pygmy Surf Crawler" [[d:hunter#1446959787786109103]], [[d:hunter#1446965863654621285]]. A pfUI self-cast modifier (for example Alt) can also make you target yourself [[d:hunter#1476016297971286078]].
8. Leveling a pet: it gets XP only from kills that give *you* XP (not from quests or PvP) [[d:hunter#1492091816692940902]], [[d:hunter#1491157779950604449]]. Leveling a pet far below you is a big time sink [[d:hunter#1451187541930807449]]. At 60, room-pulling lashers levels a pet in a few hours [[d:hunter#1492653830301814867]].

## Training points and loyalty

- **Training points = (loyalty level − 1) × pet level**, so 300 at loyalty 6 and level 60 [[d:hunter#1437649559420407933]]. A fresh tame that already knows abilities starts negative; this corrects itself [[d:hunter#1493123429992366100]].
- Spend points on stamina, armor and **resistances for your raid tier**: BWL fire and shadow, AQ nature, Naxx frost and shadow [[d:hunter#1485902382322683904]], [[d:hunter#1485902429374644265]].
- **Loyalty comes from time spent happy (green).** Loyalty 1 → 2 takes about an hour of green [[d:hunter#1439058837796687892]], [[d:hunter#1483226400818335856]]. Max loyalty takes about 6 hours [[d:hunter#1425290040262656090]]. **Contested:** some say the pet must also gain XP. A pet tamed at your own level supposedly gains no loyalty [[d:hunter#1460629863223525600]], but others found it purely time-based [[d:hunter#1462276890864910502]].
- **Display bug:** loyalty keeps rising but does not "ding" until you zone, relog or dismiss and call the pet [[d:hunter#1447443908039544883]], [[d:hunter#1483226400818335856]].
- Stabled pets lose nothing [[d:hunter#1480390441802268783]]. Stable slots: 4 in-game (the 4th costs 250g), the 5th only via a donation glyph [[d:hunter#1490970543191494666]].

## Feeding and happiness

- Food level is what matters. Keep food within about 10 levels of the pet [[d:hunter#1473751131103428739]], [[d:hunter#1446140861565108265]]. Good food gives +35 happiness per tick; check the combat log [[d:hunter#1475298724623290552]], [[d:hunter#1493584160781242380]]. Level 45 vendor food (Roasted Quail) is fine for a level-60 pet [[d:hunter#1465596636104359980]].
- Feeding is a heal-over-time for happiness. Feed one item, wait for it to finish, and refeed once happiness drops to yellow [[d:hunter#1424175273603108866]].
- At loyalty 1 a pet needs feeding every 2–3 kills [[d:hunter#1447238822139002910]]; at loyalty 6, about once an hour [[d:hunter#1425290040262656090]]. A neglected new pet can abandon you [[d:hunter#1445469544406585344]].
- Dismissing with the spell halves happiness [[d:hunter#1491067114516381706]]. Leave the pet on Stay and walk away instead [Amyloriley (player), 2025-04-14](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=126672#p126672).
- Feed right before a pull: a pet that dies usually stays happy [[d:hunter#1491026532678766684]].
- Macros:
    - Feed from the first backpack slot: `/run CastSpellByName("Feed Pet") PickupContainerItem(0,1)` [[d:hunter#1460848696995614790]].
    - All-in-one: `/run local c=CastSpellByName if UnitExists("pet") then if UnitHealth("pet")==0 then c("Revive Pet") elseif GetPetHappiness()~=nil and GetPetHappiness()~=3 then c("Feed Pet") PickupContainerItem(0, 1) else c("Dismiss Pet") end else c("Call Pet") end` [[d:hunter#1460950740997636126]].

## Scaling and threat

- Pets get no baseline scaling from you. **Spirit Bond** (BM) converts your RAP into pet AP and pet spell power [[d:hunter#1491781102560350259]]. Pet spell power comes only from Spirit Bond, never from your spell-power gear [[d:hunter#1491781067378524362]], [[d:hunter#1438910045877178520]].
- Pet crit is capped around 20% [[d:hunter#1491956069902192641]]. About 17 Agility on you gives the pet 1% crit [[d:hunter#1471713498643042359]].
- Pet share of damage: ~11% for MM without Coordinated Assault [[d:hunter#1484549579847499990]]; 10–15% for Survival [[d:hunter#1477437644941164667]]; about half for BM [[d:hunter#1486419920332787763]].
- **Threat:** a pet "will never hold threat against any player" in a raid [[d:hunter#1482126003500220510]]. Only a BM pet tanks anything useful [[d:hunter#1482130880620265534]]. No talent lowers pet threat [[d:hunter#1491029265410691233]]. While leveling as BM, the pet can tank 2–4 mobs [[d:hunter#1492991635598868482]].
- Out of combat, pets regenerate to full in about 2 s [[d:hunter#1465718908936786165]].

## Positioning in raids

- **Take Position** moves the pet to a spot; use it to hide the pet behind Sapphiron's ice block [[d:hunter#1484475363621863516]]. Command Pet can get stuck at walking speed; set Follow, then command again [[d:hunter#1454078094246940745]], [[d:hunter#1454079464626458783]].
- Pets on Passive sometimes still follow the tank in [[d:hunter#1486458878307598491]]. Defensive stance attacks whatever hits anyone in your party, which can pull the boss off the tank's positioning [[d:hunter#1471838967711272970]], [[d:hunter#1471839185123147777]].
- Healers rarely heal pets unless asked [[d:hunter#1454829088924762234]].
- Per-boss notes: see [Beast Mastery](beast-mastery.md#raid-notes).
