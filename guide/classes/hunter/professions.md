# Hunter professions

**Recommendation:** take **Engineering + Mining** and keep it. Hunters call Engineering the best-in-slot profession: it makes the new +2% crit scope, Enchanted Thorium Shells, sappers and a bag of gadgets, and Mining feeds it and pays for your consumables [[d:hunter#1486157633780973589]], [[d:hunter#1486798560052904010]], [[d:hunter#1494351453760917514]]. At 60 some hunters drop Mining for **Herbalism** to farm their own consumable mats [[d:hunter#1493748346026852362]]. For pure gold, **Herbalism + Alchemy** is "BiS for making gold" [[d:hunter#1473024949332742144]]. **Skinning/Leatherworking** fit the class fantasy but are weak at 60 [[d:hunter#1486157530781716631]], [[d:hunter#1493701421554729012]]. Learn the reworked **Survival** secondary profession for your own arrows.

## What each profession gives a hunter

### Engineering (the hunter profession)

- **Facetted Crystal Scope** (+2% ranged crit, permanent). The schematic comes from the **Draenei Exiles** quartermaster at **Revered** (Moonwhisper Coast, new in 1.18.1) [[d:hunter#1484564082014289983]], [[d:hunter#1484680131627716769]]. It takes arcanite mechanisms and about 10 Enchanted Thorium [[d:hunter#1484601959016038493]], and the AH ran out of mats at launch [[d:hunter#1484712384458264697]]. Players tested +1.9% and +2.5% crit over 1,000 shots [[d:hunter#1485556365173850123]], [[d:hunter#1485775614395813978]]. At first it did not show on the weapon or character sheet. BetterCharacterStats was updated on 2026-03-27 to show it [[d:hunter#1486823166675386589]]. It replaces the old +7 damage scope for all ranged specs [[d:hunter#1484567181239783515]]. Engineers sell the craft for mats plus a tip [[d:hunter#1485262226121162993]]. **Contested:** one player still saw no crit gain on 2026-04-11 [[d:hunter#1492504532960415884]]. The older **+3% hit scope** is called "legit garbage", because ranged hunters reach the hit cap easily from gear [[d:hunter#1486358616427462799]], [[d:hunter#1486358835101700236]]. See [Marksmanship enchants](marksmanship.md#enchants-per-slot).
- **Enchanted Thorium Shells** (new 1.18.1 gun ammo) [[d:hunter#1485555500157505657]]. Staff name **Cannon Master Willey** as the schematic's source ([Torta (staff), 2026-04-07](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=174367#p174367)). In the hunter channel players were still asking where it drops weeks after release [[d:hunter#1494005101092929667]]. Craftable ammo sits between Thorium and Doomshot; Doomshot is still BiS [[d:hunter#1485738412853170369]], [[d:hunter#1484724163993145427]].
- **Converting ammo:** make bullets with Engineering, then trade them for arrows at the Ironforge inn [[d:hunter#1449026063417213081]]. On Horde, a Tauren NPC in Orgrimmar who walks between the hunter trainer and the anvil does the same trade for Thorium Shells [[d:hunter#1449031185924624445]].
- **Gadgets:** parachute cloak, Jumper Cables XL, teleporters, target dummies, a repair bot, trinkets, and a Turtle-added epic goggle "especially strong for hunters" [[d:hunter#1320834756333797519]]. The Orgrimmar/Stormwind **Portable Wormhole Generators** let you set your hearthstone somewhere else [[d:hunter#1368639471754547411]].
- **Sappers:** good for Survival [[d:hunter#1489829209055432856]]. **Contested** for ranged: one player "tried sappers once, ended gimping my parse" [[d:hunter#1486504979010289838]].
- **Dire Maul Tribute:** you must be an engineer yourself to blow the locked door when soloing [[d:hunter#1440827721516060672]].
- **Gnomish vs Goblin:** take Gnomish first to build the gadgets, then Goblin for sappers in endgame raiding. Switching loses the Gnomish-only Gadgetstan teleport (pre-1.18.1, single source) [[d:hunter#1316770266394923038]].

### Survival and Gardening (secondary professions)

**Take Survival on every hunter.** It is free, it has no profession slot cost, and since 1.18.1 it crafts hunter ammo.

**The 1.18.1 rework** (release 2026-03-20). Survival now draws from the other professions, has 80+ new recipes, tools that slow, root or trap, gathering bags and fish/meat containers, and Woodcutting ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). What hunters found:

- **Arrows:** Bright Wood, Shade Wood, Smooth Ironfeather and **Starfeather** arrows [[d:hunter#1485738412853170369]]. The easy tier is about **17.5 DPS** [[d:hunter#1484620197053726860]]. One raider uses Doomshot on progression, Starfeather where it fits, and the 17.5 arrows on farm content because "I won't have to ask people to make them for me" [[d:hunter#1486190566013403186]].
- **Starfeather Arrows** need Star Wood (Winterspring) and Ironfeathers (moonkin) [[d:hunter#1486007799799349378]], plus Enchanted Thorium [[d:hunter#1489351848442728459]]. The recipe is a ~6% drop [[d:hunter#1489280099567140904]]. Players say they cost too much work: "Doomshots are now cheaper than starfeathered" [[d:hunter#1491466359065022657]], [[d:hunter#1491467392310575154]].
- **Woodcutting:** trees work like herb nodes, and some need higher Survival skill to cut [[d:hunter#1484976773220401243]]. The Horde trainer is in Orgrimmar near the fishing trainer [[d:hunter#1484654567957467306]]. The skill-by-skill route and tree zones are in [the professions overview](../../professions.md).
- **Skinners** can make a BoP +10 Skinning dagger [[d:hunter#1484630439514603722]]. A forum player called the Survivalist Skinning Knife useless, because skinning still needs a skinning knife ([Dexday (player), 2026-03-27](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172785#p172785)). **Contested.**
- Survival also makes a **parachute cloak**. It is unclear whether it shares a cooldown with the Engineering one [[d:hunter#1486968605827666001]], [[d:hunter#1486969687697920150]].
- One player verdict: "Tents are ass the rest is solid to use" [[d:hunter#1491811180287754433]] (single source).
- Tents were nerfed in 1.18.1 to 25% rested ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). A guild tabard at an inn is said to give rested faster [[d:hunter#1478837459046502430]] (single source).

**Before 1.18.1** Survival was a 150-cap skill of torches, campfires, tents and fishing boats with no hunter-specific value. The rework wiped old progression and the Gardening quest ([professions overview](../../professions.md)).

**Gardening:** the hunter channel only mentions it in passing. Gardening foods are "playing nice" [[d:hunter#1488796155360837642]], and Sandswept Carrots are a Gardening crop [[d:hunter#1487269257984082103]]. There is no hunter-specific case for it.

**Not covered by the hunter channel:** Slowing Bolas, Throwable Nets, the gathering trinkets and the Black Lotus sickle. They are Survival crafts ([professions overview](../../professions.md)), but no hunter reports using them.

### Mining

- Mining is the standard partner for Engineering. You get your own Thorium and Arcanite for shells and the scope, and you mine as you farm [[d:hunter#1486798560052904010]]. On the ZG solo farm you can mine rich thorium; one Survival hunter reportedly made 5,000g+ in about three weeks this way [[d:hunter#1458419731454365770]] (single source). Hateforge Quarry has two guaranteed thorium veins on a ~1.5-minute reset [[d:hunter#1484726814533353624]].

### Herbalism + Alchemy

- Alchemy is "BiS for making gold" [[d:hunter#1473024949332742144]]. Making your own consumables is "very nice" but not a must-have [[d:hunter#1493696302691913899]]. Hunters can only run one tracking spell at a time. Mark nodes with pfQuest and use SuperWoW so the nodes blink [[d:hunter#1424647781594828913]]. DME lasher farming yields Dreamfoil and Gromsblood, with a chance at Black Lotus [[d:hunter#1369161177862766693]].

### Skinning + Leatherworking (skip for raiding)

- None of the three LW specialisations suit hunters. Tribal gives no Agility [[d:hunter#1437698647666983042]], [[d:hunter#1441987288782340188]]. The Devilsaur set is decent pre-raid, but Molten Core replaces it [[d:hunter#1437710825417609347]].
- At 60, LW is mostly the Salt Shaker cooldown and crafting Devilsaur or Hide of the Wild for others [[d:hunter#1473398141155016928]]. Late game it is "really only good for crafting resist gear" [[d:hunter#1486157530781716631]]. Players call it "a dead profession" once leveling is done [[d:hunter#1493701421554729012]].
- Cured Rugged Hide is a Leatherworking cooldown, not a Skinning one [[d:hunter#1424715630904344668]].
- Skinning on the ZG farm is low value: about 3g for two minutes of skinning panthers [[d:hunter#1483781661522002041]].
- Staff announced in February 2026 that crafting professions would be made more useful [[d:hunter#1473364968509542632]]. The 1.18.1 crafted-item upgrade drops (so far only in BWL) may change this later [[d:hunter#1491810852796629003]].

### Jewelcrafting

- Suggested as Jewelcrafting + Mining, an alternative to Engineering + Mining [[d:hunter#1459578636964462816]]. Goldsmithing or Gemology both work; look for BoP patterns you want [[d:hunter#1459605929921220795]].
- **No profession-locked BiS exists.** No Goldsmith item is on a BiS list, and anyone can apply the gems [[d:hunter#1493700080639803462]]. There are no TBC-style profession-only enchants or gems [[d:hunter#1493696302691913899]]. It is a flavour or gold pick, not a DPS one.

### Enchanting

- Optional. It is useful for disenchanting dungeon-farm loot, even at skill 1 [[d:hunter#1446240454399099101]]. An enchanter can add **+3 DPS to any 200 ammo** for 1 Large Brilliant Shard + 5 Arcane Dust [[d:hunter#1485358374832046221]] (single source; the same poster was joking in the message before, so verify in game). Other advice says skip it and park a low-level disenchanting bank alt in a capital instead [[d:hunter#1406747510361297099]].

## When to pick up and swap

1. **Level 1-60:** Mining + Engineering (gadgets while leveling) or Mining + Skinning for gold [[d:hunter#1494351453760917514]], [[d:hunter#1368570962806706177]]. Learn **Survival** early; Apprentice is available at level 1 again after a 1.18.1 hotfix ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608)).
2. **Optional early detour:** Tailoring + Skinning for bags, then switch [[d:hunter#1494347298195509419]].
3. **By 60:** Engineering in one slot [[d:hunter#1494356273880105132]]. Get the Draenei Exiles to Revered for the scope; one player reported about 3-4 hours / ~1,000 kills to Revered [[d:hunter#1484730909587673150]].
4. **At 60:** keep Mining for thorium/shells and ZG farming, or swap it for Herbalism to farm consumable mats [[d:hunter#1493748346026852362]].

Skill thresholds for the scope and shells are not stated in the hunter sources.

## Race interactions

The hunter channel ties no hunter-specific profession bonus to a race. For racials, see [Races and racials](index.md#races-and-racials). Staff made the Goblin racial **Prospecting** grant both Mining and Jewelcrafting skill ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102965#p102965)).

## Gold

- **Farms beat professions** for most hunters. The ZG pyramid/panther kite farm (bijous, coins) is the most efficient once mastered [[d:hunter#1483746983536492575]]. DME lashers are the starter farm from about level 45 [[d:hunter#1470681473496383508]]. DM Tribute (needs Engineering) averages about 20g of vendor loot a run [[d:hunter#1440705530459848878]]. Maraudon averages about 50g an hour [[d:hunter#1467309451294806150]].
- **Contested:** "Proffs are a scam… no proff makes as much as farming mara" [[d:hunter#1467261661227385004]] vs "My profession is making me about 100g a day" [[d:hunter#1467290086688428163]].
- **Ammo is a hunter tax.** Thorium arrow/shell prices doubled in late 2025 [[d:hunter#1450736549468835891]]. Doomshot can be soloed and sells for about 10g a stack [[d:hunter#1486968668637364326]]. Survival arrows did not bring prices down on Tel'Abim [[d:hunter#1491468377854836849]]. Making your own shells (Engineering) or arrows (Survival) is the main profession saving.
- Silithid Carapace Fragments in Silithus were called the best gold on Ambershire [[d:hunter#1491828526238662796]].
