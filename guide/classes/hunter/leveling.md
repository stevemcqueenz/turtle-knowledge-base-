# Hunter leveling (1–60)

**Recommendation:** level as **Beast Mastery** with a pet that tanks: a boar early, a Screech bird or a Raptor later. Use a slow ranged weapon (2.6+ speed) and spam **Steady Shot** (baseline from level 20) between Auto Shots. If you want to raid as Survival, level BM to **40**, then respec into Survival for in-combat traps. Respecs cost at most 5g on Turtle [[d:hunter#1493367329101320192]]. From level 45 on, the fastest route to 60 is soloing the Dire Maul East lasher rooms.

| Spec | Leveling rating | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Beast Mastery** | **S** | "BM till 60, then surv" [[d:hunter#1489400947317805157]]. The pet is a "demigod" that tanks 2–4 mobs [[d:hunter#1492991635598868482]]. "The best pet is 'dude just spec BM and you auto win'" [[d:hunter#1491440356099756155]] |
| Survival | **C before 40, A after 40** | "surv is very good when you can use traps in combat, which comes at lv40. At lv19 BM leveling is much easier" [[d:hunter#1489567654791483513]]. At 40 "my levelling speed became ridiculously faster" [[d:hunter#1455791834709098547]]. It is mana hungry and you do the tanking [[d:hunter#1431196318478630952]] |
| Marksmanship | **B** | Works, but "you can go MM and endlessly kite if you're very bored" [[d:hunter#1491930373582753873]]. MM needs crits for Lock and Load, so it is weak when fresh [[d:hunter#1485396418599587942]] |

**Contested:** Sofie (4 hunters leveled) says the fastest route is MM to 20, then BM talents, then a Survival respec at 40 [[d:hunter#1473497805262815486]]. Thalenar and Dinousek find pure Survival leveling easier [[d:hunter#1482746349647822993]], [[d:hunter#1481331994112557199]]. Hunters level fast either way: 1–60 in "3 days if you're on Tel'abim and know what you're doing" [[d:hunter#1473749536345165907]].

## Talent order 10 → 60 (Beast Mastery)

This order ends on a leveling-oriented **41/10/0** build close to Kekkonen's deep BM [[d:hunter#1488784924965343272]]:

- **Swift Aspects first.** Five points in an Aspect talent is "always a good start" [[d:hunter#1468371627854270629]].
- **Pathfinding early** for travel speed [[d:hunter#1438795111030067252]], [[d:hunter#1460356323211477167]].

- **Coordinated Assault** at 20 (see its threat caveat below).
- **Frenzy** — "dont skip frenzy" [[d:hunter#1486063417503645787]].

### Survival path: respec at 40

Keep the BM order above through level 39. At 40, respec and spend all 31 points in Survival down to Untamed Trapper (in-combat traps, which need 30 points in Survival) [[d:hunter#1438179260715110534]]. Then finish Lightning Reflexes and Vicious Strikes, and add the BM filler of the raid build. Hakuna suggests spending 2 fewer points on pet damage while leveling, for more speed from BM [[d:hunter#1485418636436045835]].

## Gameplay by level

What to press in each bracket on the recommended **Beast Mastery** path. The **Survival path** starts at 40 (see the last two brackets). Learn levels, cooldowns and cast times come from the 1.18.1 game data. Talent levels follow the order above. Each bracket also covers the pet: taming, its ability bar, and whether it can tank a dungeon.

**Rules for every bracket:**

- Pull with the pet, not with a shot. Start shooting when the mob is at about 75–80% health [[d:hunter#1466911679668424945]].
- Growl is a threat ability, not a taunt [[d:hunter#1485140138584576110]]. Keep **Growl on autocast** when solo, and keep **Claw off**, because Claw burns the focus Growl needs [[d:hunter#1468519397273501737]], [[d:hunter#1423554879141580861]].
- At range, you only pull aggro off the pet at 130% of its threat, not 110% [[d:hunter#1478847760009531482]]. "If you are BM and consistently pull aggro from pet, you are doing something wrong" [[d:hunter#1468708226240610437]].
- Only a BM pet tanks anything useful [[d:hunter#1482130880620265534]]. As Survival, pet tanking has "really no chance after lv20" [[d:hunter#1482130793500250194]].
- **In a group with a real tank:** turn Growl off [[d:hunter#1428016626455937097]], and send the pet exactly when the tank hits [[d:hunter#1484689822005067816]]. Do not use Defensive mode, which attacks as soon as anyone in the party gets aggro [[d:hunter#1471838967711272970]].

### Levels 1–9

- **New now:** Auto Shot and Raptor Strike (starting abilities). L4 Aspect of the Monkey, Serpent Sting R1. L6 Arcane Shot R1 (6 s cooldown), Hunter's Mark R1. L8 Concussive Shot (12 s cooldown), Raptor Strike R2. No pet yet.
- **Single target:**
    1. Aspect of the Monkey on (dodge; vanilla baseline).
    2. Auto Shot to pull, then Arcane Shot while the mob runs in.
    3. Concussive Shot (from 8), and moonwalk (strafe backward) away for 4–5 more shots [[d:hunter#1460603346443440260]].
    4. In melee: Raptor Strike on cooldown (6 s, off the GCD) plus auto-attack.
    5. Skip Serpent Sting on normal mobs; it costs too much mana [[d:hunter#1478329003201921158]].
- **Multi-target:** do not pull two. Without a pet, you are the tank (vanilla baseline).

### Levels 10–19

- **New now:** L10 class quest chain: Tame Beast, Call Pet, Dismiss Pet, Feed Pet, Revive Pet, Beast Training, pet Growl R1–R2. Also L10 Aspect of the Hawk R1, Aspect of the Wolf R1, Serpent Sting R2. L12 Mend Pet R1 (heals over 5 s), Distracting Shot R1, Wing Clip R1, Arcane Shot R2. L14 Eyes of the Beast, Eagle Eye, Scare Beast R1. L16 Immolation Trap R1, Mongoose Bite R1, Raptor Strike R3. L18 Multi-Shot R1 (100 mana, 10 s cooldown), Aspect of the Hawk R2, Serpent Sting R3. Talents: Swift Aspects (10–14), Endurance Training (15–19).
- **Pet milestones:**
    - Finish the level-10 chain, or you never get Feed Pet [[d:hunter#1492136690561585234]].
    - Tame a **boar/pig**: it eats anything and is cheap to keep [[d:hunter#1460555634327027715]]. Or tame a Screech bird: Amani Eagle (level 10, Stonetalon) or a Westfall Greater Fleshripper (16–17) [[d:hunter#1490087581919084586]], [[d:hunter#1490088205435797574]].
    - At loyalty 1, feed every 2–3 kills and carry spare food [[d:hunter#1447238822139002910]].
    - Pet bar: Growl on autocast, Bite (or the family skill) on, Claw off.
- **Single target:**
    1. Aspect of the Hawk on.
    2. Send the pet. Wait until the mob is at 75–80%.
    3. Hunter's Mark only on elites and long fights.
    4. Auto Shot, plus Arcane Shot when mana allows.
    5. If the mob turns on you: Concussive Shot, then back off and let Growl take it back. In melee: Raptor Strike, Mongoose Bite, and Wing Clip to step away.
    6. Mend Pet (from 12) when the pet drops low (vanilla baseline). It heals over 5 s; start it early.
- **Multi-target (2 mobs):**
    1. Only with a boar or Screech pet. Screech holds AoE aggro [[d:hunter#1464682851231334645]].
    2. Shoot the pet's target. Do not Multi-Shot: "it basically shortens the fight by 2s on average, but recovering the mana takes longer" [[d:hunter#1440106926347128963]].
    3. On beasts, Scare Beast the second mob (1.5 s cast; vanilla baseline).
    4. Before 30 you have no Feign Death. Do not pull a third.
- **Tanking (dungeons):** let a real tank tank Deadmines and Wailing Caverns. The pet has no taunt, and only Endurance Training so far. No source reports pet-tanking a dungeon this low.

### Levels 20–29

- **New now:** L20 **Steady Shot R1** (1 s cast; baseline), **Aspect of the Cheetah**, Disengage R1, Freezing Trap R1, Mend Pet R2, Arcane Shot R3, Distracting Shot R2. Talent: Coordinated Assault (20). L22 Hunter's Mark R2, Scorpid Sting. L24 Raptor Strike R4, Beast Lore. L26 **Rapid Fire** (5 min cooldown), Immolation Trap R2, Serpent Sting R4. L28 Frost Trap, Arcane Shot R4, Aspect of the Hawk R3, Mend Pet R3. Talents: Pathfinding (21–22), Thick Hide (23–25), Unleashed Fury (26–30). Aimed Shot (a Marksmanship talent) is not on this path, and it is "useless" for BM [[d:hunter#1486063417503645787]].
- **Pet milestones:**
    - Train the new Growl rank at 20 from the pet trainer. It is the only skill learned there, every 10 levels [[d:hunter#1428016626455937097]].
    - Do not let the pet fall far behind your level [[d:hunter#1451187541930807449]].
    - Loyalty rises with time spent happy (green), not with kills. See [Pets](pets.md#training-points-and-loyalty).
- **Single target:**
    1. Aspect of the Cheetah between mobs, Aspect of the Hawk before the pull.
    2. Send the pet. Wait until the mob is at 75–80%.
    3. Auto Shot, then **Steady Shot after each Auto Shot** without clipping. Steady-only leveling "is a big time saver", because you drink less [[d:hunter#1476280176533966899]].
    4. Arcane Shot: a cheap Coordinated Assault trigger [[d:hunter#1474824792220631130]], and your shot while moving.
    5. Rapid Fire (from 26) on elites and dangerous pulls.
    6. Scorpid Sting only to help the pet tank an elite. No Serpent Sting [[d:hunter#1478329003201921158]].
- **Resource rule:** send the pet on the next mob and drink while it fights [amanagor (player), 2026-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166261#p166261).
- **Multi-target (2–3 mobs):**
    1. With Thick Hide, the pet holds 2 mobs, and 3 or 4 is "doable sometimes" [[d:hunter#1492991635598868482]].
    2. Keep shooting the pet's primary target.
    3. Traps can only be placed out of combat before 40. Lay Freezing Trap (20) or Frost Trap (28) before the pull and bring the add over it (vanilla baseline).
- **Tanking (dungeons):** still let the group's tank tank. Growl off, pet in as the tank hits.

### Levels 30–39

- **New now:** L30 **Feign Death** (30 s cooldown), Multi-Shot R2, Steady Shot R2, Mongoose Bite R2, Aspect of the Beast, Distracting Shot R3, Scare Beast R2. L32 Flare, Raptor Strike R5. L34 Explosive Trap R1, Disengage R2, Serpent Sting R5. L36 Arcane Shot R5, Mend Pet R4, Immolation Trap R3, Viper Sting R1. L38 Aspect of the Hawk R4, Wing Clip R2. Talents: Ferocity (31–35), Scent of Blood (36–38), **Bestial Wrath** (39; 18 s, 90 s cooldown, no GCD).
- **Pet milestones:**
    - Pet Dash at pet level 30 [[d:hunter#1490806796825137273]]. New Growl rank at 30.
    - At 35–40, swap the boar for an **eagle** for soloing [[d:hunter#1460555634327027715]]. Hinterlands Eagles already know Screech rank 3; do not reset them [[d:hunter#1428475590486524116]].
- **Single target:**
    1. Same as 20–29.
    2. If the mob turns on you while the pet is alive: Feign Death, and the mob goes back to the pet.
    3. Bestial Wrath (39) on elites.
- **Multi-target (2–4 mobs):**
    1. Pet on the first mob; let Growl or Screech collect the rest.
    2. Bestial Wrath on the big pulls.
    3. Multi-Shot only when mana allows.
    4. If a mob sticks to you: Concussive Shot and kite, or Feign Death.
- **Tanking (dungeons, pet tank):** possible from here, once Endurance Training and Thick Hide are maxed. A level-32 BM with a Gorilla tanked all of [Dragonmaw Retreat](../../instances/dragonmaw-retreat.md) with a level-34 healer (single source; "don't know if I'd recommend this") [[d:hunter#1421042271238750359]].
    1. Pet: Screech bird or Gorilla, with Growl on and Claw off.
    2. Pull by sending the pet. The party waits until the first target is at 75–80%.
    3. The pet has no taunt, so the DPS must watch threat. Aggro they take is "tough to get back" [[d:hunter#1421043685780623422]].
    4. Distracting Shot pulls a loose mob off the healer onto you (a taunt; vanilla baseline). Kite it back to the pet so Growl can take it.
    5. Keep Mend Pet rolling. Healers rarely heal pets unless asked [[d:hunter#1454829088924762234]].
    6. Bad pull: Feign Death to drop your own threat. The pet stays on the mobs.

### Levels 40–49

- **New now:** L40 **Volley R1** (3 s cast, 60 s cooldown), Trueshot Aura, Aspect of the Pack, Steady Shot R3, Hunter's Mark R3, Freezing Trap R2, Raptor Strike R6, Distracting Shot R4. Talent: **Intimidation** (40; 60 s cooldown). L42 Multi-Shot R3, Serpent Sting R6. L43 **Kill Command** (talent; 8 s cooldown, no GCD). L44 Arcane Shot R6, Mend Pet R5, Explosive Trap R2, Mongoose Bite R3. L46 Aspect of the Wild R1, Immolation Trap R4, Viper Sting R2. L48 Aspect of the Hawk R5, Disengage R3, Raptor Strike R7. Talents: Spirit Bond (41–42), Frenzy (44–47), Bestial Precision (48–49).
- **Pet milestones:**
    - New Growl rank at 40.
    - A **Wolf** if you want to solo dungeons to 60 [[d:hunter#1460556031796183122]].
    - Improved Mend Pet is "useless unless soloing dungeons" (Hakuna); **Contested:** Elmgren finds it useful for elite quests too [[d:hunter#1486063417503645787]].
- **Single target:**
    1. Send the pet, wait until 75–80%, then Hunter's Mark on long fights.
    2. **Kill Command** whenever it lights up, only while the pet is in melee range [[d:hunter#1484783149413171350]].
    3. Steady Shot after each Auto Shot.
    4. Arcane Shot when moving.
    5. Bestial Wrath and Rapid Fire on elites.
- **Multi-target:**
    1. Pet on mob 1.
    2. **Intimidation** hands the pet a second mob: "send pet with intimidation on a baddie, and after one growl, send it to another" [Steakhouse (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106303#p106303).
    3. Volley only on 3+ mobs that are all on the pet (it costs 210 mana).
- **Tanking (dungeons, pet tank):** "infinitely easier in turtle wow vs classic" [[d:hunter#1455954880517771487]].
    1. **Kill Command + Bite** to tear aggro back.
    2. Dash + a move command to kite.
    3. Retarget once a mob goes back to the pet. Feign Death, or steal aggro back with the next Kill Command [[d:hunter#1455955199234670673]].
    4. Intimidation's stun buys time to rebuild threat [[d:hunter#1421044060180713484]].
    5. Maraudon for 40–45 [[d:hunter#1460557414997692477]].

### Survival path: levels 40–49

- **New now:** respec at 40 (see [Survival path](#survival-path-respec-at-40)):
    - **Untamed Trapper** lets you place traps in combat.
    - **Carve** (10 s cooldown). Carve Rank 2 has been trainable since 38; buy it right after the respec.
    - **Lacerate** (10 s cooldown).
    - **Deterrence** (10 s, 5 min cooldown).

    Also Raptor Strike R6 (40), Mongoose Bite R3 and Explosive Trap R2 (44), Immolation Trap R4 (46), Raptor Strike R7 (48). Talents: Lightning Reflexes (41–42), Vicious Strikes (43–44), Swift Aspects (45–49).
- **Pet:** a Screech pet for AoE threat [[d:hunter#1334677302021197865]], or a Wolf for dungeon soloing [[d:hunter#1460556031796183122]]. It supports; you tank.
- **Single target** (one spam macro, as on the [Survival](survival.md#single-target-rotation) page):
    1. Pull with the pet or a Steady Shot, and let the mob come to you.
    2. Raptor Strike on cooldown.
    3. Immolation Trap on cooldown, but not on a mob that is about to die [[d:hunter#1451997607705444677]].
    4. Mongoose Bite.
    5. Lacerate, from the side.
    6. Carve.
    7. Wing Clip as the last filler.
- **Multi-target (2–4 mobs):**
    1. Explosive Trap.
    2. Carve.
    3. Raptor Strike, Mongoose Bite, Lacerate.

    Explosive Trap and Carve together are the AoE core [[d:hunter#1429126143629328394]]. For big pulls: "gather all with frost trap down, then kite round the frost trap dropping explosive on CD" [[d:hunter#1463674674289447045]].
- **Resource rule:** drink between pulls. Some Survival levelers dip BM for Coordinated Assault and Swift Aspects because mana is tight [[d:hunter#1484556031722786928]].
- **Defensive:** Deterrence when a pull goes wrong. Feign Death is less reliable in melee range [[d:hunter#1494689825419165921]].
- **Tanking (dungeons):** in groups you are melee DPS. Growl off, and Feign Death early: in melee you pull aggro at 110% of the tank's threat [[d:hunter#1318265159327682571]].

### Survival path: levels 50–60

- **New now:** L54 Explosive Trap R3. L56 Carve R3, Immolation Trap R5, Raptor Strike R8, Aspect of the Viper. L58 Mongoose Bite R4. Talents: Improved Primal Aspects (50–52), Improved Revive Pet (53–54), Coordinated Assault (55), Unleashed Fury (56–60).
- **Single and multi-target:** same as 40–49.
- **Dire Maul East lashers (from 45):**
    1. Place a Frost Trap before the pull.
    2. Let the pet take the initial aggro.
    3. Explosive Trap and Carve on cooldown, with a healing potion ready [[d:hunter#1303915313490034688]].
    4. Kite the room in a big circle [[d:hunter#1486750495560306771]].

    It is doable from 45, harder because of the mobs' aggro range [[d:hunter#1486749925097209856]]. The standard pet is a Wolf [[d:hunter#1460556031796183122]].

## Pull and kill loop (bot-ready)

1. **Aspect of the Cheetah** between mobs. Hawk (or Monkey for melee) before the pull.
2. **Send the pet**, and let it build threat. Start shooting when the mob is at about 75–80% health [[d:hunter#1466911679668424945]].
3. **Hunter's Mark** on longer fights; skip it on trash that dies fast.
4. **Auto Shot**, then **Steady Shot** after each auto. Add **Arcane Shot** for Coordinated Assault procs, and while moving.
5. From 43: **Kill Command** when it lights up, but only while the pet is in melee range [[d:hunter#1484783149413171350]].
6. **2+ mobs:** let the pet hold them; Multi-Shot only if mana allows. Screech pets hold AoE threat [[d:hunter#1464682851231334645]].
7. **Losing aggro, or the pet dies:** Feign Death, or Concussive Shot and kite. Moonwalk (strafe backward) to land 4–5 shots before the mob reaches you [[d:hunter#1460603346443440260]]. With Cheetah you can kite "any elite taking 0 dmg" [[d:hunter#1486068038028759122]].
8. After the kill: loot, feed the pet if it is yellow, drink only when needed. The pet regenerates in about 2 s [[d:hunter#1465718908936786165]].

Survival from 40: Explosive Trap and Carve on packs; Immolation Trap, Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite on single mobs; Aspect of the Monkey [[d:hunter#1429126143629328394]]. AoE farming: gather mobs with a Frost Trap, kite in a circle and drop Explosive Trap on cooldown [[d:hunter#1463674674289447045]].

## Resources and downtime

- Mana is your limit. Steady Shot is the most efficient damage [[d:hunter#1476280176533966899]]. Skip stings and Multi-Shot on normal mobs [[d:hunter#1440106926347128963]].
- Let the pet fight while you drink [amanagor (player), 2026-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166261#p166261).
- For leveling "just stack agility" [[d:hunter#1415655104622624879]]. Strength does nothing for ranged play [[d:hunter#1437980775977848893]], and Intellect is weak [[d:hunter#1447461321879846913]].
- Tents in STV give rested XP [[d:hunter#1311033158782681138]].
- Duo partners: a Paladin (Blessing of Might helps the pet, Judgement of Wisdom gives mana), or a Shaman for a Horde Survival (Windfury) [[d:hunter#1401270769756930299]]. Avoid a Rogue, who wants the same gear [[d:hunter#1430096079503753246]].

The Survival kit makes this quest easy: "being able to drop traps in combat makes the quest trivial" [[d:hunter#1476652145376886937]].

- **Un'Goro:** freeze the dog, fight the woman in melee with Aspect of the Wild [[d:hunter#1433667763246862338]].
- **Silithus (Solenor):** do not root him. Kite with Cheetah and Concussive around the AQ gong or gates [[d:hunter#1476662946397421588]], [[d:hunter#1481369193323692205]].
- **Burning Steppes (Klinfran):** the fallen-tree pathing trick [[d:hunter#1433668188591231077]].
- **Winterspring:** Serpent Sting plus Frost Trap kiting from the road [[d:hunter#1433668490753081344]].
- **Simone (bow/staff quest):** keep the dog in an ice trap; melee, Wing Clip, run out, Viper Sting [[d:hunter#1478067371385291036]], [[d:hunter#1478065451295576125]].

## Group role while leveling

- As DPS: pet on Passive in dungeons; do not steal threat from the tank. Hunters are the natural pullers.
- **Pet tanking** works in lower dungeons with a BM pet and a healer (Dragonmaw Retreat above). Geared level-60 Survival/BM hunters have tanked BRD, LBRS, Scholomance and all of Dire Maul [[d:hunter#1482750075753992422]]. Turtle's level-60 dungeons need more gear [[d:hunter#1482750598414860420]].

## Professions

- **Engineering** is the hunter profession: scopes, ammo, gadgets [[d:hunter#1486157633780973589]], [[d:hunter#1493696302691913899]]. Pair it with Mining [[d:hunter#1493748346026852362]].
- While leveling, Tailoring + Skinning makes your own 20-slot bags [[d:hunter#1494347298195509419]].
- The reworked **Survival** secondary profession lets you craft your own ammo [[d:hunter#1494356273880105132]].
- Leatherworking and Skinning are weak at 60 [[d:hunter#1486157530781716631]].
- Engineering + Enchanting sets you up for Dire Maul Tribute farming [[d:hunter#1466390068523569183]].

- **BM with the defensive talents first.** "It's safer in HC" [Steakhouse (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106303#p106303).

## Common mistakes

- Leveling as melee Survival before level 40 [[d:hunter#1489567654791483513]].
- Leaving Claw on autocast [[d:hunter#1468824531790467166]].
- Serpent Sting on every mob [[d:hunter#1478334984585744547]].
- Taming a pet at your own level: it may gain loyalty very slowly and eat stacks of food (contested) [[d:hunter#1460629863223525600]].
- Leveling a pet 30 levels behind you [[d:hunter#1451187541930807449]].
- Shooting before the pet has threat [[d:hunter#1466911679668424945]].
- Not finishing the level-10 pet quest [[d:hunter#1492136690561585234]].
