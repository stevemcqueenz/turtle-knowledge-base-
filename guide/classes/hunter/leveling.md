# Hunter leveling (1–60)

**Recommendation:** level as **Beast Mastery** with a pet that tanks: a boar early, a Screech bird or a Raptor later. Use a slow ranged weapon (2.6+ speed) and spam **Steady Shot** (baseline from level 20) between Auto Shots. If you want to raid as Survival, level BM to **40**, then respec into Survival for in-combat traps. Respecs cost at most 5g on Turtle [[d:hunter#1493367329101320192]]. From level 45 on, the fastest route to 60 is soloing the Dire Maul East lasher rooms.

|---|---|---|
| **Beast Mastery** | **S** | "BM till 60, then surv" [[d:hunter#1489400947317805157]]. The pet is a "demigod" that tanks 2–4 mobs [[d:hunter#1492991635598868482]]. "The best pet is 'dude just spec BM and you auto win'" [[d:hunter#1491440356099756155]] |
| Survival | **C before 40, A after 40** | "surv is very good when you can use traps in combat, which comes at lv40. At lv19 BM leveling is much easier" [[d:hunter#1489567654791483513]]. | Marksmanship | **B** | Works, but "you can go MM and endlessly kite if you're very bored" [[d:hunter#1491930373582753873]]. MM needs crits for Lock and Load, so it is weak when fresh [[d:hunter#1485396418599587942]] |

**Contested:** Sofie (4 hunters leveled) says the fastest route is MM to 20, then BM talents, then a Survival respec at 40 [[d:hunter#1473497805262815486]]. Thalenar and Dinousek find pure Survival leveling easier [[d:hunter#1482746349647822993]], [[d:hunter#1481331994112557199]]. Hunters level fast either way: 1–60 in "3 days if you're on Tel'abim and know what you're doing" [[d:hunter#1473749536345165907]].

## Talent order 10 → 60 (Beast Mastery)

This order ends on a leveling-oriented **41/10/0** build close to Kekkonen's deep BM [[d:hunter#1488784924965343272]]:

- **Swift Aspects first.** Five points in an Aspect talent is "always a good start" [[d:hunter#1468371627854270629]].
- **Pathfinding early** for travel speed [[d:hunter#1438795111030067252]], [[d:hunter#1460356323211477167]].
- **Endurance Training and Thick Hide** were buffed again in 1.18.1 ("pets have been too fragile in open world content") [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **Coordinated Assault** at 20 (see its threat caveat below).
- **Frenzy** — "dont skip frenzy" [[d:hunter#1486063417503645787]].
- **Intimidation** — "Imp revive and Intimidation are incredible" for leveling [Steakhouse (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106303#p106303).
- **Lethal Shots** once the pet is maxed [[d:hunter#1486063417503645787]].

### Survival path: respec at 40

Keep the BM order above through level 39. At 40, respec and spend all 31 points in Survival down to Untamed Trapper (in-combat traps, which need 30 points in Survival) [[d:hunter#1438179260715110534]]. Then finish Lightning Reflexes and Vicious Strikes, and add the BM filler of the raid build. Hakuna suggests spending 2 fewer points on pet damage while leveling, for more speed from BM [[d:hunter#1485418636436045835]].

## Pull and kill loop (bot-ready)

1. **Aspect of the Cheetah** between mobs. Hawk (or Monkey for melee) before the pull.
2. **Send the pet**, and let it build threat. Start shooting when the mob is at about 75–80% health [[d:hunter#1466911679668424945]].
3. **Hunter's Mark** on longer fights; skip it on trash that dies fast.
4. **Auto Shot**, then **Steady Shot** after each auto. Add **Arcane Shot** for Coordinated Assault procs, and while moving.
5. From 43: **Kill Command** when it lights up, but only while the pet is in melee range [[d:hunter#1484783149413171350]].
6. **2+ mobs:** let the pet hold them; Multi-Shot only if mana allows. Screech pets hold AoE threat [[d:hunter#1464682851231334645]].
7. **Losing aggro, or the pet dies:** Feign Death, or Concussive Shot and kite. Moonwalk (strafe backward) to land 4–5 shots before the mob reaches you [[d:hunter#1460603346443440260]]. With Cheetah you can kite "any elite taking 0 dmg" [[d:hunter#1486068038028759122]].
8. After the kill: loot, feed the pet if it is yellow, drink only when needed. The pet regenerates in about 2 s [[d:hunter#1465718908936786165]].

Survival from 40: Explosive Trap and Carve on packs; Immolation Trap, Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite on single mobs; Aspect of the Monkey [[d:hunter#1429126143629328394]]. AoE farming: gather mobs with a Frost Trap, kite in a circle and drop Explosive Trap on cooldown [[d:hunter#1463674674289447045]].

## Resources and downtime

- Mana is your limit. Steady Shot is the most efficient damage [[d:hunter#1476280176533966899]]. Skip stings and Multi-Shot on normal mobs [[d:hunter#1440106926347128963]].
- Let the pet fight while you drink [amanagor (player), 2026-01-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166261#p166261).
- For leveling "just stack agility" [[d:hunter#1415655104622624879]]. Strength does nothing for ranged play [[d:hunter#1437980775977848893]], and Intellect is weak [[d:hunter#1447461321879846913]].
- Tents in STV give rested XP [[d:hunter#1311033158782681138]].
- Duo partners: a Paladin (Blessing of Might helps the pet, Judgement of Wisdom gives mana), or a Shaman for a Horde Survival (Windfury) [[d:hunter#1401270769756930299]]. Avoid a Rogue, who wants the same gear [[d:hunter#1430096079503753246]].

### Rhok'delar quest

The Survival kit makes this quest easy: "being able to drop traps in combat makes the quest trivial" [[d:hunter#1476652145376886937]].

- **Un'Goro:** freeze the dog, fight the woman in melee with Aspect of the Wild [[d:hunter#1433667763246862338]].
- **Silithus (Solenor):** do not root him. Kite with Cheetah and Concussive around the AQ gong or gates [[d:hunter#1476662946397421588]], [[d:hunter#1481369193323692205]].
- **Burning Steppes (Klinfran):** the fallen-tree pathing trick [[d:hunter#1433668188591231077]].
- **Winterspring:** Serpent Sting plus Frost Trap kiting from the road [[d:hunter#1433668490753081344]].
- **Simone (bow/staff quest):** keep the dog in an ice trap; melee, Wing Clip, run out, Viper Sting [[d:hunter#1478067371385291036]], [[d:hunter#1478065451295576125]].

## Group role while leveling

- As DPS: pet on Passive in dungeons; do not steal threat from the tank. Hunters are the natural pullers.
- **Pet tanking** works in lower dungeons with a BM pet and a healer (Dragonmaw Retreat above). Geared level-60 Survival/BM hunters have tanked BRD, LBRS, Scholomance and all of Dire Maul [[d:hunter#1482750075753992422]]. Turtle's level-60 dungeons need more gear [[d:hunter#1482750598414860420]].

## Professions

- **Engineering** is the hunter profession: scopes, ammo, gadgets [[d:hunter#1486157633780973589]], [[d:hunter#1493696302691913899]]. Pair it with Mining [[d:hunter#1493748346026852362]].
- While leveling, Tailoring + Skinning makes your own 20-slot bags [[d:hunter#1494347298195509419]].
- The reworked **Survival** secondary profession lets you craft your own ammo [[d:hunter#1494356273880105132]].
- Leatherworking and Skinning are weak at 60 [[d:hunter#1486157530781716631]].
- Engineering + Enchanting sets you up for Dire Maul Tribute farming [[d:hunter#1466390068523569183]].

## Hardcore notes

- **BM with the defensive talents first.** "It's safer in HC" [Steakhouse (player), 2024-11-04](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=106303#p106303).
- Pet: a boar (tanky, eats anything) or a Screech bird [[d:hunter#1484749621845561464]].
- Feign Death cannot be resisted out of line of sight [[d:hunter#1445841760583680000]]. Keep a speed potion.

## Common mistakes

- Leveling as melee Survival before level 40 [[d:hunter#1489567654791483513]].
- Leaving Claw on autocast [[d:hunter#1468824531790467166]].
- Serpent Sting on every mob [[d:hunter#1478334984585744547]].
- Taming a pet at your own level: it may gain loyalty very slowly and eat stacks of food (contested) [[d:hunter#1460629863223525600]].
- Leveling a pet 30 levels behind you [[d:hunter#1451187541930807449]].
- Shooting before the pet has threat [[d:hunter#1466911679668424945]].
- Not finishing the level-10 pet quest [[d:hunter#1492136690561585234]].
