# Warrior

**Recommendation:** level as **DW Fury with Blood Drinker**. At 60 you have three routes:

- **DPS:** raid as **Arms** until you have about 23% unbuffed crit, then move to **2H Fury** (or its Sweeping Strikes "Lawnmower" variant) or **DW Fury**.
- **Tank:** main-tank as **Defensive Tactics (DefTac)**. Since the 1.18.1 release it outperforms FuryProt in almost every case, and FuryProt stays the off-tank spec.
- **PvP:** warrior is the weakest class in 1.18.1. Play DW Fury with Blood Drinker, or Arms with a healer.

Turtle's warrior differs a lot from vanilla, so vanilla guides don't transfer:

- 2H Fury is a **Slam** build (Flurry shortens the cast).
- **Blood Drinker** heals off every hit.
- **Defensive Tactics** carries Defiance's threat bonus into Battle and Berserker Stance.
- **Precision Cut** turns the extra rage into bigger Executes.
- Weapon-skill racials and hit caps have been reworked.

## Roles

- **Melee DPS:** 2H Fury, Lawnmower, DW Fury, MS Arms, Death Wish Arms ("Deathcute"). "Realistically there's 5 dps builds" [[d:warrior#1487831038725193870]], [[d:warrior#1487833646160281820]], [[d:warrior#1487834407795691582]].
- **Tank:** DefTac (main tank), FuryProt (off-tank, or a high-threat MT), Death Wish Prot, and Revenge deep prot for fresh 60s and dungeons. Warrior is the single-target "cooldown tank" and ranks behind bear and paladin as a complete package. See [Protection](protection.md).
- **Utility:** Sunder Armor, Battle Shout, Demoralizing Shout, Disarm, Intervene, Thunder Clap, Mortal Strike's healing debuff, and the biggest Executes in the game.

## What changed in 1.18.1

**Release (2026-03-20)**

- **Precision Cut** and **Boundless Anger** swapped places in the Arms tree, and Precision Cut now needs Boundless Anger 3 [[d:warrior#1487614924757799136]].
- **Improved Whirlwind** became **Ravager**, which also cuts Cleave's rage cost [[d:warrior#1464348413503209629]].
- **Tier sets:**
  - T1 Arms/Fury 3-piece: 10% chance on ability hit to gain 15 rage [[d:warrior#1483776919446093864]].
  - T3 4-piece: +4% ability crit damage [[d:warrior#1464347845950963873]].
- **Flurry and Slam:** a patch-day Flurry bug was hotfixed. 5/5 Flurry now makes Slam a 1.92 s cast [[d:warrior#1484635426726609007]], [Torta (staff), 2026-03-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171608#p171608), [[d:warrior#1485731806379639005]]. A shield-block bug was fixed a few days later [Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139).

**Mid-cycle hotfixes (January 2026, still live)**

- **Defiance** now scales 4/8/12/15/20%. **Shield Slam** went from 15% to 20% AP, and its threat modifier from 50% to 75% [[d:warrior#1481378107037323354]], [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071). This is why DefTac became the default main tank.
- **Execute** costs 10 rage instead of 15, a net damage buff [[d:warrior#1467491606910472306]].
- **Sweeping Strikes** can no longer crit, and its extra hits deal 20% of the damage [[d:warrior#1463776864144199833]].
- The paladin **Champion** buffs were removed, so tanks have less threat headroom [[d:warrior#1464368423885869251]].

**After release**

- **Hamstring** has no cooldown again [[d:warrior#1492529486363627603]].
- **Slam** no longer delays your next swing if you start it right after a hit (2026-04) [[d:warrior#1494432213700313169]].
- **Orc Hardiness** and **Iron Will** changed from stun-resist chance to a 15% stun-duration reduction [[d:warrior#1488782865893298207]].

## Races

At the highest parse level "all races are within 1% of each other", so pick what you like [[d:warrior#1494450078235099227]], [[d:warrior#1492621742114607307]]. Weapon-skill racials were cut from +5 to +3, which in practice means about 0.6% hit and 6% glancing damage [[d:warrior#1494455850377678940]]. A +5 weapon-skill quest is open to everyone, so Weapon Mastery racials are "pretty meh now" [[d:warrior#1468401294958923797]].

## Professions

- **Engineering** is "the only profession" that matters for a warrior: Sappers, grenades, dummies and Battle Chicken [[d:warrior#1478662431621517363]], [[d:warrior#1478662676279459840]]. Level it with Mining, then drop Mining for a gold profession [[d:warrior#1451247090532356166]]. Go Gnomish first; switching to Goblin later is mainly to craft your own Sappers [[d:warrior#1431678831491547157]].
- **Blacksmithing** is contested. Some say "avoid like the plague" [[d:warrior#1464385054607151125]]; others say it "has some good bits now", mostly pre-raid [[d:warrior#1445158997660536987]]. The Elementium weapons need Swordsmith (Champion) or Axesmith (Reaper) [[d:warrior#1490124384684478474]].
- **Alchemy** covers your own flasks and Mighty Rage Potions, but an alt can do it [[d:warrior#1444333624458743818]], [[d:warrior#1445159836688978163]].
- **Dual spec** costs 350g at level 50 [[d:warrior#1406228381384572998]]. It is worth getting for DPS/tank or PvE/PvP swaps.
