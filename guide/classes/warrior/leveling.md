# Leveling a Warrior (1-60)

**Recommendation:** level **Fury**. Take Cruelty first and use a two-hander early. Pick up the off-hand hit talent and go **dual wield with Blood Drinker at level 35**; from there you rarely need to eat [[d:warrior#1484524982448160798]], [[d:warrior#1484524607267672074]], [[d:warrior#1493981873561407599]]. The same Fury build tanks leveling dungeons with a shield swapped in: "you will have a better time tanking while leveling as full fury" [[d:warrior#1484652940919701534]].

If you mostly run dungeons as DPS, level **Arms with a slow two-hander** [[d:warrior#1485847747801256157]]. If you want to tank, take Tactical Mastery and Improved Thunder Clap early, and go **Protection only once Shield Slam is available at 30** [[d:warrior#1488808982368878733]], [[d:warrior#1485778015593037934]]. Warrior levels slowly before 30 in every spec [[d:warrior#1493939490828324904]].

| Route | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **DW Fury / Blood Drinker** (recommended) | Solo questing, Hardcore, open-world farming | "dual wield is faster than 2h for leveling on turtle" [[d:warrior#1493981873561407599]]; "for leveling I'd go fury for blood drinker... the new dual wield spec is actually good" [[d:warrior#1482755253173616691]], [[d:warrior#1482755383465476096]] |
| **Arms, 2H** | Dungeon-heavy leveling; multi-mob pulls | Fury does more single-target damage, Arms more when you pull 2+ mobs [[d:warrior#1467164464733491283]]; "if ur doing dungeons then id say 2h arms" [[d:warrior#1485847747801256157]] |
| **Protection from 30** | Tank-queue leveling, Hardcore | Shield Slam at 30 is a "gargantuan power spike" [[d:warrior#1493584245199999037]]; "39 is high enough for prot leveling/tanking to be good" [[d:warrior#1484912284156297296]] |

**Contested:**

- **Arms or Fury before Blood Drinker?** Lipibidy: go Arms until at least 30, because it is "easier to hit things, easier with low crit chance" [[d:warrior#1468413710107410647]], [[d:warrior#1468414186953379942]]. Hayden: "literally you should just play fury the whole way on turtle" [[d:warrior#1484524982448160798]].
- **2H Fury while leveling?** Guts: "If you want 1 build and never swap stay 2h fury the whole time" [[d:warrior#1486645860275322901]]. Kyrsie: "dont go 2h fury while leveling", because Flurry-Slam needs crit you won't have [[d:warrior#1478753288844349461]].

## Going Protection while leveling

- Nothing useful sits in the Protection tree before Shield Slam, so don't spec it before level 30 [[d:warrior#1485778015593037934]], [[d:warrior#1485330030149242911]].
- Until then a shield on an Arms or Fury build tanks better. Improved Shield Slam is reachable at 32 [[d:warrior#1485330030149242911]], [[d:warrior#1485330586129399922]]. Tank early dungeons as Arms with Tactical Mastery and Improved Thunder Clap [[d:warrior#1488808982368878733]].
- From 30, respec Shield Slam, Improved Shield Slam and One-Handed Weapon Specialization, then add Cruelty. Skip Defensive Tactics and Revenge talents while leveling [[d:warrior#1328726339490152500]], [[d:warrior#1465918762124574893]].
- **Contested:** Gibom leveled Prot to 50+ "0 downtime, did not grab food with me the whole way" [[d:warrior#1472862034507862179]]. Lipibidy calls Prot the worst early tree until Shield Slam and the worst tree again at 60 [[d:warrior#1472880640750780509]], [[d:warrior#1472880736397820006]].
- The Burnt Inn quest in Dustwallow Marsh gives a Defender's Shield at 34-35 that lasts to 50+ [[d:warrior#1472862034507862179]].

## Abilities: when to use and what to skip

- **Dual Wield** is available at level 10. You cannot equip a non-shield off-hand until you have it trained, and dual wield needs Fury talents to be worthwhile [[d:warrior#1491555705948213368]], [[d:warrior#1457459561534587071]].
- **Revenge** at 14. **Sunder Armor** comes with the Defensive Stance quest: "don't skip that quest" [[d:warrior#1441201843677167788]].
- **Cleave** at 20 [[d:warrior#1491555705948213368]]. **Bloodthirst** (talent) at 40 in the order above. **Whirlwind** at 36 [[d:warrior#1484031184601350286]].
- **Slam:** skip it before about 36. Its weapon-damage ranks are 60→65% (24), 70→80% (34), 80→90% (44) and 90→100% (54) [[d:warrior#1491503981527044217]]. Cast it right after a two-hander swing or you lose DPS [[d:warrior#1494518552319885393]].
- **Rend:** good below 30 on mobs that live its full duration, "almost entirely useless" after that [[d:warrior#1456710126621818981]]. Don't Rend anything that dies in under 15 s [[d:warrior#1476323710452367442]].
- **Heroic Strike:** rage-inefficient while leveling. Use it only when rage would cap [[d:warrior#1432937946989854780]], [[d:warrior#1481442159596142762]].
- **Don't buy every rank** of every ability; train what you use [[d:warrior#1470347926692691969]].
- **Weapon-skill quests** on Fray Island (where you learn Berserker Stance) cost 250 g per weapon type, cover both the 1H and 2H versions, and give +5 skill. They are "the single most impactful 250 (or 500g) you can/will spend" [[d:warrior#1434575531856232550]], [[d:warrior#1434575792700129301]], [[d:warrior#1487598353213558904]].

## Pull and kill loop (bot-ready)

1. Keep **Battle Shout** up between pulls [[d:warrior#1476323710452367442]], [[d:warrior#1484030990027456573]].
2. **Pull** with Charge on isolated mobs. For dense groups or dungeons, pull with a thrown or ranged weapon [[d:warrior#1476322395361906718]], [[d:warrior#1493937763802677289]].
3. **Demoralizing Shout** on melee mobs; they deal about 25% less damage afterwards [[d:warrior#1493937763802677289]].
4. **Sunder Armor:** usually one on a normal quest mob. Stack up to 5 only on elites or long fights, and never below 50% HP [[d:warrior#1484032312151769159]], [[d:warrior#1484032803472412833]], [[d:warrior#1476322978114568294]].
5. **Bloodthirst > Whirlwind** on cooldown once you have them (Fury) [[d:warrior#1484031184601350286]]. Arms: Mortal Strike, Overpower on procs [[d:warrior#1468414511064285255]].
6. **Heroic Strike or Cleave** only to dump rage [[d:warrior#1481442159596142762]].
7. **Execute** below 20%.
8. **Bloodrage** when you are above about 75% HP, so its self-damage crit procs Enrage [[d:warrior#1485271205001822288]].
9. Pull **2+ green mobs** from about level 30 onward for better experience per hour [[d:warrior#1464993090824700059]], [[d:warrior#1470346908122742926]].

## Downtime and resources

- Level on **green** mobs, carry **bandages**, and level First Aid [[d:warrior#1470346908122742926]]. Resting restores 2% HP per second sitting and 1% standing [[d:warrior#1470463044659449950]].
- **Blood Drinker** self-healing plus on-hit vampirism: with 2× Blood-Etched Fetish and 2× Darkmoon rings, one player "pretty much didnt have to eat from lvl 40-60" [[d:warrior#1485271389693939754]]. Farm Razorfen Downs early for the two Blood-Etched Fetishes and cheap vampirism enchants [[d:warrior#1466450703311569070]].
- **Vampirism is useless at very low levels**: it only heals on hits over 100 damage [[d:warrior#1467100727838113936]].
- **Stats while leveling:** Stamina > Strength > Agility. Hit is good when you find it [[d:warrior#1486362597975654420]].

## Tanking dungeons while leveling

- **Any spec can tank at level.** Arms or Fury with a shield in your bag out-threatens a leveling Prot warrior [[d:warrior#1432864256499388647]], [[d:warrior#1432864298937483405]], [[d:warrior#1424644443755122760]]. 2H Fury tanks easily to about 45 [[d:warrior#1470315145988411517]]. On Hardcore, 2H Arms with a shield swap on bosses works until about 45 [[d:warrior#1469875032087068683]].
- **Be at least the level of the dungeon's final boss** [[d:warrior#1459184897775374477]].
- **Pull:** line-of-sight pull → Bloodrage → Demoralizing Shout → tab-Sunder across the pack. Thunder Clap only with spare rage; it hits 4 targets [[d:warrior#1476325827296887007]]. Charge variant: Charge → swap to Defensive Stance mid-Charge → Demoralizing Shout → Thunder Clap → tab-Sunder [[d:warrior#1446128654332596224]].
- **Mark skull > cross > square.** Warriors can't hold much AoE threat, and an opening Whirlwind on a Fury build helps [[d:warrior#1469884937959243838]]. Your real job is keeping the healer alive; let a DPS who over-pulls tank it briefly [[d:warrior#1446128745810493533]].
- **Sweeping Strikes + Whirlwind** (Arms, from about 36) is the best AoE opener while leveling [[d:warrior#1491885115759001831]]. At low level without Defensive Tactics, Improved Thunder Clap is still "a threat beast" [[d:warrior#1491887719029280941]].

## Hardcore notes

- Always carry Swiftness Potions (and never use another 2-minute potion, so it's always ready) [[d:warrior#1459256861608902768]].
- Engineering target dummies; Obsidian Brooch for stamina [[d:warrior#1459256861608902768]], [[d:warrior#1384529902287720469]].
- Hamstring a single problem mob before running [[d:warrior#1459257399545167893]].
- "Take your time, never enter a structure full of hostiles" [[d:warrior#1459279946345091178]].
- Hamstring has no cooldown in 1.18.1, so kiting while Blood Drinker heals works again [[d:warrior#1484624968045166792]].

## Races and professions for leveling

- **Race** matters little. Troll's regeneration and Berserking make it strong for leveling [[d:warrior#1303430079434588260]], [[d:warrior#1494450078235099227]]. See [Races](index.md#races).
- **Professions:**
  - Mining + Engineering while leveling; drop Mining for a money profession once Engineering is maxed [[d:warrior#1451247090532356166]].
  - Alchemy gives durable leveling value [[d:warrior#1444330106830061751]].
  - Blacksmithing becomes recipe-gated and expensive past 50 [[d:warrior#1477042373648191651]].
- **Battlegrounds** are a fast alternative to questing, at the cost of gold. Arathi Basin gives roughly 4% of a level per capture tick [[d:warrior#1474790113673216010]].

## Common mistakes

- Speccing Protection before Shield Slam [[d:warrior#1485778015593037934]].
- Using Slam before about 36, or casting it mid-swing [[d:warrior#1494518552319885393]].
- Heroic Strike spam at low rage [[d:warrior#1432937946989854780]].
- Dual wielding with bad weapons and no hit, and missing everything [[d:warrior#1487090299212660788]].
- Leveling in dungeons over and over for gear instead of questing [[d:warrior#1432395222012399616]].
- Copying classic talent guides as-is: "classic talents are completely different from turtle talents on every class" [[d:warrior#1457111490510458993]].
