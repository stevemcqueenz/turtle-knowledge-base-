# Leveling a Warrior (1-60)

**Recommendation:** level **Fury**. Take Cruelty first and use a two-hander early. Pick up the off-hand hit talent and go **dual wield with Blood Drinker at level 35**; from there you rarely need to eat [[d:warrior#1484524982448160798]], [[d:warrior#1484524607267672074]], [[d:warrior#1493981873561407599]]. The same Fury build tanks leveling dungeons with a shield swapped in: "you will have a better time tanking while leveling as full fury" [[d:warrior#1484652940919701534]].

If you mostly run dungeons as DPS, level **Arms with a slow two-hander** [[d:warrior#1485847747801256157]]. If you want to tank, take Tactical Mastery and Improved Thunder Clap early, and go **Protection only once Shield Slam is available at 30** [[d:warrior#1488808982368878733]], [[d:warrior#1485778015593037934]]. Warrior levels slowly before 30 in every spec [[d:warrior#1493939490828324904]].

| Route | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **DW Fury / Blood Drinker** (recommended) | Solo questing, Hardcore, open-world farming | "dual wield is faster than 2h for leveling on turtle" [[d:warrior#1493981873561407599]]; "for leveling I'd go fury for blood drinker... the new dual wield spec is actually good" [[d:warrior#1482755253173616691]], [[d:warrior#1482755383465476096]] |
| **Arms, 2H** | Dungeon-heavy leveling; multi-mob pulls | Fury does more single-target damage, Arms more when you pull 2+ mobs [[d:warrior#1467164464733491283]]; "if ur doing dungeons then id say 2h arms" [[d:warrior#1485847747801256157]] |
| **Protection from 30** | Tank-queue leveling, Hardcore | Shield Slam at 30 is a "gargantuan power spike" [[d:warrior#1493584245199999037]]; "39 is high enough for prot leveling/tanking to be good" [[d:warrior#1484912284156297296]] |

**Contested:**

- **Arms or Fury before Blood Drinker?** Lipibidy: go Arms until at least 30, because it is "easier to hit things, easier with low crit chance" [[d:warrior#1468413710107410647]], [[d:warrior#1468414186953379942]]. Hayden: "literally you should just play fury the whole way on turtle" [[d:warrior#1484524982448160798]].
- **2H Fury while leveling?** Guts: "If you want 1 build and never swap stay 2h fury the whole time" [[d:warrior#1486645860275322901]]. Kyrsie: "dont go 2h fury while leveling", because Flurry-Slam needs crit you won't have [[d:warrior#1478753288844349461]].

## Talent order: DW Fury (recommended)

Built from the channel's recommended order: Cruelty → the off-hand hit talent → Blood Craze → Piercing Howl → the bottom of Fury [[d:warrior#1485003724190515250]]. casey's level-30 layout is Cruelty, Dual Wield Specialization, Improved Shouts, Enrage and Death Wish, "then Blood Drinker > Flurry > Improved Berserker Rage and obviously take Bloodthirst at 40" [[d:warrior#1493937763802677289]]. Notes:

- **Before you own two good one-handers**, Unbridled Wrath is better than Dual Wield Specialization. Swap levels 15-19 to Unbridled Wrath and pick up Dual Wield Specialization when you start dual wielding [[d:warrior#1484526882044248178]], [[d:warrior#1484524607267672074]].
- **Blood Craze vs Battlefield Mobility (contested):** Blackjack calls it "garbage, take battlefield mobility instead" [[d:warrior#1484527515908440075]], but it helps burst-heal when soloing elites or pulling packs of three [[d:warrior#1484528135734038720]]. For open-world Blood Drinker leveling, Kyrsie skips Blood Craze [[d:warrior#1470046594215837739]].
- **Hardcore:** Piercing Howl (10 rage, no cooldown) and Improved Berserker Rage (breaks roots) are "a game changer" [[d:warrior#1467146748588986611]]. VeganCourage's HC build keeps both and skips Unbridled Wrath for more Piercing Howl range via Booming Voice [[d:warrior#1469877714730090639]].
- **At 60**, respec to a raid build ([Fury](fury.md) or [Arms](arms.md)). You can respec at any warrior trainer [[d:warrior#1476662488903843943]].

## Gameplay by level

What to press in each bracket, for the recommended **DW Fury** route and the **Arms (no respec)** route, plus how to **tank dungeons** at that level. Learn levels, rage costs and cooldowns are from the 1.18.1 game data. Talent unlocks follow the two talent-order tables above. Stances: **Battle** has Charge, Overpower, Mocking Blow, Retaliation and Sweeping Strikes. **Defensive** has Taunt, Revenge, Shield Block, Disarm, Shield Wall and Intervene. **Berserker** has Whirlwind, Intercept, Berserker Rage and Recklessness. Sunder Armor, Shield Slam, Bloodthirst, Mortal Strike, Demoralizing Shout and the shouts work in any stance (vanilla baseline), and on Turtle, unlike vanilla, Thunder Clap also works in Defensive as well as Battle [[d:warrior#1451088832236748943]].

**Rage through a stance swap:** without Tactical Mastery a swap costs you your rage (vanilla baseline). With 5/5 you keep up to 25 [[d:warrior#1471914865512943708]]. The Arms route has 5/5 by 24; the Fury route only gets it at 44–48. Until then, Fury should pick one stance per pull.

### Levels 1–9

- **New now:** L1 Battle Shout (10 rage); L4 Charge (8–25 yd, generates rage, 15 s cooldown) and Rend Rank 1 (9 s bleed); L6 Thunder Clap Rank 1 (20 rage, 4 s cooldown); L8 Hamstring Rank 1 (10 rage, no cooldown) and Heroic Strike Rank 2. No talents yet.
- **Single target** (both routes; use a cheap vendor two-hander [[d:warrior#1470355530215067730]]):
  1. Battle Shout before the pull [[d:warrior#1476323710452367442]].
  2. Charge an isolated mob. Pull dense groups with a thrown or ranged weapon instead [[d:warrior#1476322395361906718]].
  3. Rend, but only on a mob that will live for the full bleed [[d:warrior#1456710126621818981]].
  4. Auto-attack. Heroic Strike only if rage would cap [[d:warrior#1432937946989854780]]; its swing makes no rage [[d:warrior#1473745241159762074]].
  5. Hamstring a mob that runs (vanilla baseline).
  - **Resources:** keep some rage for the next pull [[d:warrior#1476322395361906718]]. Fight green mobs, one at a time, and bandage instead of eating where you can [[d:warrior#1470346908122742926]].
- **Multi-target:** avoid it. If a second mob joins, Thunder Clap once to slow both and kill one (vanilla baseline).
- **Tanking:** none yet. Defensive Stance, Sunder Armor and Taunt come from the level-10 quest.

### Levels 10–19

- **New now:** L10 Defensive Stance, Sunder Armor Rank 1 and Taunt (quest: "don't skip that quest" [[d:warrior#1441201843677167788]]); L10 Bloodrage (60 s cooldown) and Rend Rank 2; L12 Overpower Rank 1 (5 rage, after a dodge), Shield Bash Rank 1 and Battle Shout Rank 2; L14 Demoralizing Shout Rank 1 and Revenge Rank 1 (5 rage, 6 s cooldown); L16 Mocking Blow Rank 1 (10 rage, 120 s cooldown), Shield Block (10 rage, 5 s) and Heroic Strike Rank 3; L18 Disarm (20 rage, 60 s cooldown) and Thunder Clap Rank 2. **Talents:** Cruelty 10–14 on both routes. Then Dual Wield Specialization 15–19 (Fury; Unbridled Wrath while still on a two-hander), or Improved Rend and 3/5 Tactical Mastery 15–19 (Arms).
- **Single target** (both routes, Battle Stance):
  1. Battle Shout up → Charge (or thrown pull).
  2. Demoralizing Shout on the first GCD from 14; mobs deal about 25% less damage [[d:warrior#1493937763802677289]].
  3. Sunder Armor: one on a normal mob. Stack toward 5 only while the mob is above 50% HP [[d:warrior#1484032312151769159]], [[d:warrior#1476322978114568294]].
  4. Overpower whenever the target dodges. It is "your only weaponskill button for a long time" [[d:warrior#1468414511064285255]].
  5. Rend on long fights; Heroic Strike only near the rage cap.
  6. Bloodrage on the pull for rage (vanilla baseline).
- **Multi-target:** still one mob at a time. On an accidental add: Demoralizing Shout → Thunder Clap → focus one mob; Hamstring a runner.
- **Tanking (Deadmines, Wailing Caverns, Shadowfang Keep):**
  - **Setup:** a two-hander is fine. Keep a shield and a one-hander in your bags on a swap macro for bosses that hit hard [[d:warrior#1473953959264649217]], [[d:warrior#1486382907420573696]]. Be at least the final boss's level [[d:warrior#1459184897775374477]]. Expect to run out of rage while shield-tanking this early [[d:warrior#1481441238308622396]].
  - **Pull:** line-of-sight or ranged pull → Bloodrage → Demoralizing Shout → tab-Sunder across the pack. Thunder Clap only if you already have spare rage [[d:warrior#1476325827296887007]]. Charge variant: Charge → switch to Defensive Stance mid-Charge → Demoralizing Shout → Thunder Clap → tab-Sunder [[d:warrior#1446128654332596224]].
  - **Single-target threat:** Sunder Armor is your rage-efficient filler and Heroic Strike your rage dump [[d:warrior#1474888165402873948]]. Revenge whenever it lights up (Defensive Stance). At this level it hits for almost nothing [[d:warrior#1476315197168685056]], but it only costs 5 rage.
  - **Packs:** mark skull > cross > square [[d:warrior#1469884937959243838]]. Your job is to keep the healer alive, not to hold every mob [[d:warrior#1446128745810493533]].
  - **Taunt / recovery:** Taunt (Defensive Stance, 5 yd, 10 s cooldown) the mob on the healer. It can fail on a crowd-controlled mob [[d:warrior#1430903627610062879]]. From 16, Mocking Blow (Battle Stance, 120 s cooldown) is your backup.
  - **Defensives:** Shield Block with a shield on (16); Disarm a hard-hitting melee boss (18, Defensive Stance).

### Levels 20–29

- **New now:** L20 Cleave Rank 1 (20 rage, on next swing), Intimidating Shout, Retaliation (Battle Stance) and Rend Rank 3; L22 Sunder Armor Rank 2 and Battle Shout Rank 3; L24 Execute Rank 1 (15 rage, target under 20%), Demoralizing Shout Rank 2, Revenge Rank 2 and Heroic Strike Rank 4; L26 Challenging Shout (5 rage, 10 min cooldown), Charge Rank 2 and Mocking Blow Rank 2; L28 Shield Wall (Defensive Stance, needs a shield), Overpower Rank 2 and Thunder Clap Rank 3. **Talents:** Fury gets Improved Shouts 20–24, then Enrage 25–29. Arms gets Improved Thunder Clap 1/3 (20), Improved Charge (21–22), Tactical Mastery 5/5 (23–24), Improved Overpower (25–26) and Deep Wounds (27–29).
- **Single target:**
  1. Battle Shout → Charge → Demoralizing Shout → Sunder Armor (rules above).
  2. **Fury, from 25:** Bloodrage while you are above about 75% HP, so its self-damage procs Enrage [[d:warrior#1485271205001822288]].
  3. Overpower on every dodge (Arms: stronger with Improved Overpower).
  4. Execute below 20%.
  5. Heroic Strike only to dump rage near the cap.
- **Multi-target:** still mostly single pulls. With two mobs, use Cleave instead of Heroic Strike as the dump. Retaliation (30 min cooldown, shared with Shield Wall and Recklessness [[d:warrior#1462506610068029502]]) or Intimidating Shout when a pull goes wrong (vanilla baseline).
- **Tanking (Blackfathom Deeps and the other 20s dungeons):**
  - **Arms route:** by 24 you have Tactical Mastery 5/5 and Improved Thunder Clap, the leveling tank kit: "5/5 tact mastery into 3/3 imp tclap" [[d:warrior#1476319270169219346]]. Thunder Clap works in Defensive Stance and is "very solid aoe threat while leveling" [[d:warrior#1423419033231032330]].
  - **Stance dance (Arms):** Charge in Battle Stance → Defensive Stance for Demoralizing Shout, Thunder Clap, Sunder, Revenge, Taunt and Shield Block → back to Battle Stance for an Overpower or Mocking Blow. Each swap keeps up to 25 rage.
  - **Fury route:** no Tactical Mastery, so stay in Defensive Stance for the whole pull and bank rage from the previous pull (vanilla baseline).
  - **Pack loop:** Thunder Clap first, then tab through the mobs with Sunder, and Cleave when rage is high; expect to chase some with Taunt [[d:warrior#1466175854479409152]].
  - **Emergency:** Challenging Shout (26) when a pack runs to the healer. AoE tanking gets hard while it is on cooldown [[d:warrior#1473015814440358053]]. Shield Wall (28) on a boss that is killing you.

### Levels 30–39

- **New now:** L30 Berserker Stance and Intercept Rank 1 (quest; Berserker Stance, 10 rage, 30 s cooldown), Cleave Rank 2 and Rend Rank 4; L32 Berserker Rage (30 s cooldown), Execute Rank 2, Hamstring Rank 2, Heroic Strike Rank 5, Shield Bash Rank 2 and Battle Shout Rank 4; L34 Sunder Armor Rank 3, Demoralizing Shout Rank 3 and Revenge Rank 3; L36 **Whirlwind** (Berserker Stance, 25 rage, 10 s cooldown) and Mocking Blow Rank 3; L38 Pummel (10 rage, 10 s cooldown), Thunder Clap Rank 4 and Shield Slam Rank 2. **Talents:**
  - **Fury:** Death Wish (30; 10 rage, 3 min cooldown), Blood Craze (31–33), Piercing Howl (34; 10 rage), Blood Drinker (35: start dual wielding), Flurry (36–39).
  - **Arms:** Two-Handed Weapon Specialization (30–32), Impale (33–34), **Sweeping Strikes** (35; Battle Stance, 20 rage, 30 s cooldown), Master Strike (36; 20 rage, 30 s cooldown), Master of Arms (37–41).
  - **Protection** (only if you respec for tanking): **Shield Slam** at 30 (20 rage, 6 s cooldown) [[d:warrior#1493584245199999037]].
- **Single target, DW Fury:**
  1. Battle Shout → Charge (or thrown pull) → Demoralizing Shout → one Sunder Armor.
  2. Bloodrage above 75% HP for Enrage.
  3. From 36, **Whirlwind** on cooldown in Berserker Stance [[d:warrior#1484031184601350286]].
  4. Execute below 20%.
  5. Heroic Strike as the dump; if the mob lives long, Sunder instead [[d:warrior#1484031184601350286]].
  6. Hamstring as filler. With no cooldown, Hamstring-kiting while Blood Drinker heals works [[d:warrior#1484624968045166792]].
  7. Death Wish on an elite or a dangerous pull.
- **Single target, Arms:**
  1. Same opener.
  2. Overpower on every dodge.
  3. Master Strike on cooldown (36+).
  4. Whirlwind on cooldown (Berserker Stance, 36+).
  5. Execute below 20%.
  6. Heroic Strike dump. Skip Slam before about 36, and cast it right after a two-hander swing [[d:warrior#1494518552319885393]].
- **Multi-target:** from about 30, pull 2+ green mobs for better experience per hour [[d:warrior#1034155462163181690]]. Once you have Whirlwind and Cleave, pull 2–3; Sweeping Strikes makes it easier [[d:warrior#1465266565250285838]].
  1. Demoralizing Shout.
  2. **Arms:** Sweeping Strikes in Battle Stance → swap to Berserker Stance → Whirlwind. Then Cleave as the dump.
  3. **Fury:** Whirlwind on cooldown, Cleave as the dump. Piercing Howl slows a runner.
  4. Don't pull more than you can Demoralizing-Shout and Whirlwind down. Retaliation is the panic button.
- **Tanking (Scarlet Monastery, Razorfen Downs, Gilneas City):**
  - **Arms/Fury with a shield or a two-hander:** open with Sweeping Strikes + Whirlwind, the best AoE threat opener at this level [[d:warrior#1491885115759001831]], [[d:warrior#1490378739639517226]]. Then go back to Defensive Stance, tab-Sunder, Cleave and Thunder Clap when rage allows, and Taunt stragglers as their threat runs out [[d:warrior#1418375631888060579]]. Use all three stances: Battle and Defensive for threat, mitigation and taunts, a short Berserker swap for Whirlwind, and keep Mocking Blow on your bars [[d:warrior#1483137699962552451]], [[d:warrior#1483137845337264230]].
  - **Fury without Tactical Mastery:** pool rage in Defensive Stance from the previous pull, spend it on Thunder Clap, Cleave and Sunder down to about 25, then make one stance swap for Whirlwind [[d:warrior#1365157106675617823]].
  - **Protection from 30:** play Shield Slam on cooldown > Revenge when lit > Sunder Armor > Heroic Strike dump, in Defensive Stance with a shield. "From 30 on you can level as prot, less killing speed ofc but basically zero downtime" [[d:warrior#1434300007237943368]]. While leveling, stay in Defensive Stance with Taunt + Revenge rather than chasing Defensive Tactics [[d:warrior#1465922876418035757]]. "39 is high enough for prot leveling/tanking to be good" [[d:warrior#1484912284156297296]].
  - **Interrupts:** Shield Bash (shield) or Pummel (38) on casters; they share a cooldown [[d:warrior#1454776559570518109]].

### Levels 40–49

- **New now:** L40 **Bloodthirst** (Fury talent; 30 rage, 6 s cooldown), **Concussion Blow** (Protection talent; 20 s cooldown, generates rage [[d:warrior#1460410582208479334]]), Execute Rank 3, Cleave Rank 3, Heroic Strike Rank 6 and Rend Rank 5; L42 Battle Shout Rank 5 and Intercept Rank 2; L44 Overpower Rank 3, Revenge Rank 4 and Demoralizing Shout Rank 4; L46 Shield Slam Rank 3, Sunder Armor Rank 4, Charge Rank 3 and Mocking Blow Rank 4; L48 Bloodthirst Rank 2, Mortal Strike Rank 2, Concussion Blow Rank 2, Execute Rank 4, Heroic Strike Rank 7 and Thunder Clap Rank 5. **Talents:**
  - **Fury:** Flurry 5/5 (41), Improved Berserker Rage (42–43), then **Tactical Mastery 44–48**. Stance swaps now keep rage.
  - **Arms:** Master of Arms 5/5 (41), Improved Slam (42–43), Improved Thunder Clap 2/3 (44), **Mortal Strike at 45** (30 rage, 6 s cooldown), then Unbridled Wrath (46–50).
- **Single target, DW Fury:**
  1. Opener as above.
  2. **Bloodthirst > Whirlwind** on cooldown [[d:warrior#1484031184601350286]].
  3. Execute below 20%.
  4. Heroic Strike only when capping; it is a dump, never a choice over a skill [[d:warrior#1477507950644957367]].
  5. Hamstring or Sunder Armor as filler.
  6. Bloodrage (Enrage) and Death Wish for elites.
- **Single target, Arms:**
  1. Opener as above.
  2. Mortal Strike on cooldown (45+).
  3. Whirlwind.
  4. Overpower (a Battle Stance swap now keeps 25 rage).
  5. Master Strike.
  6. Slam right after a swing, if trained (see the note below the brackets).
  7. Execute below 20%.
- **Multi-target:** pull 2–3 as before. Fury: Whirlwind > Bloodthirst, Cleave as the dump. Arms: Sweeping Strikes → Whirlwind → Mortal Strike → Cleave.
- **Tanking (Zul'Farrak, Maraudon, Hateforge Quarry):**
  - **Two-hander tanking gets harder here.** "You can tank in 2h fury easilly untill about 45 lvl" [[d:warrior#1470315145988411517]]. Past 40, one player found an Arms shield tank took too much damage and respecced Prot [[d:warrior#1429822325510897794]].
  - **Protection pug-tank trash order:** mark skull → Charge → Demoralizing Shout → Thunder Clap → Concussion Blow → Shield Slam → Sunders, with Overpower on procs. Stay mostly in Battle Stance on trash, and switch to Defensive Stance on bosses for Revenge and less damage [[d:warrior#1410572688505831454]].
  - **AoE priority on a pack:** Whirlwind > Thunder Clap > Concussion Blow > Shield Slam, Heroic Strike with extra rage, and switch targets as needed [[d:warrior#1449599120640704512]].
  - **Fury tank:** Bloodthirst is now your best single-target threat. With Tactical Mastery from 44, you can stance-dance like Arms.

### Levels 50–60

- **New now:**
  - L50: Recklessness (Berserker Stance, 30 min cooldown shared with Shield Wall and Retaliation), Intervene Rank 1 (item-taught; Defensive Stance, 10 rage, 30 s cooldown, 8–25 yd), Cleave Rank 4, Rend Rank 6.
  - L52: Intercept Rank 3, Shield Bash Rank 3, Battle Shout Rank 6.
  - L54: Bloodthirst Rank 3, Mortal Strike Rank 3, Shield Slam Rank 4, Concussion Blow Rank 3, Revenge Rank 5, Demoralizing Shout Rank 5, Hamstring Rank 3.
  - L56: Execute Rank 5, Heroic Strike Rank 8, Mocking Blow Rank 5.
  - L58: Sunder Armor Rank 5, Thunder Clap Rank 6.
  - L60: Bloodthirst Rank 4, Mortal Strike Rank 4, Concussion Blow Rank 4, Overpower Rank 4, Cleave Rank 5, Rend Rank 7; item-taught Battle Shout Rank 7, Heroic Strike Rank 9, Revenge Rank 6.
  - **Talents:** Fury takes the Arms dip (Improved Rend, Improved Heroic Strike, Deep Wounds, Improved Charge, Impale). Arms takes Improved Shouts (51–55), then Enrage (56–60).
- **Single target / multi-target:** same loops as 40–49. From 50, add Recklessness on a dangerous elite or pull (30 min cooldown, shared with Shield Wall and Retaliation).
- **Tanking (Black Morass, Stratholme, Scholomance, UBRS):**
  - **When to switch:** go full Protection for dungeon-spam and pre-raid ("swap at 55 or so"), then finish leveling through dungeons and attunements [[d:warrior#1424747100738162709]].
  - **Loop:** same as 40–49, with Concussion Blow or Shield Slam as the opener.
  - **Utility:** Intervene (50) to peel for a healer [[d:warrior#1494704075055567070]].
  - **At 60:** pick a raid tank build and follow [Protection → How to play](protection.md#how-to-play).

**Contested: Slam learn level.** The 1.18.1 game data lists every Slam rank (50% → 100% weapon damage) at trainer level 54. A player post gives Rank 2 at 24, Rank 3 at 34, Rank 4 at 44 and Rank 5 at 54 [[d:warrior#1491503981527044217]]. Check your trainer. Either way, Slam is not part of the leveling loop before about 36.

## Going Protection while leveling

- Nothing useful sits in the Protection tree before Shield Slam, so don't spec it before level 30 [[d:warrior#1485778015593037934]], [[d:warrior#1485330030149242911]].
- Until then a shield on an Arms or Fury build tanks better. Improved Shield Slam is reachable at 32 [[d:warrior#1485330030149242911]], [[d:warrior#1485330586129399922]]. Tank early dungeons as Arms with Tactical Mastery and Improved Thunder Clap [[d:warrior#1488808982368878733]].
- From 30, respec Shield Slam, Improved Shield Slam and One-Handed Weapon Specialization, then add Cruelty. Skip Defensive Tactics and Revenge talents while leveling [[d:warrior#1328726339490152500]], [[d:warrior#1465918762124574893]].
- **Contested:** Gibom leveled Prot to 50+ "0 downtime, did not grab food with me the whole way" [[d:warrior#1472862034507862179]]. Lipibidy calls Prot the worst early tree until Shield Slam and the worst tree again at 60 [[d:warrior#1472880640750780509]], [[d:warrior#1472880736397820006]].
- The Burnt Inn quest in Dustwallow Marsh gives a Defender's Shield at 34-35 that lasts to 50+ [[d:warrior#1472862034507862179]].

## Abilities: when to use and what to skip

- **Dual Wield** is available at level 10. You cannot equip a non-shield off-hand until you have it trained, and dual wield needs Fury talents to be worthwhile [[d:warrior#1491555705948213368]], [[d:warrior#1457459561534587071]].
- **Revenge** at 14. **Sunder Armor** comes with the Defensive Stance quest: "don't skip that quest" [[d:warrior#1441201843677167788]].
- **Cleave** at 20 [[d:warrior#1491555705948213368]]. **Bloodthirst** (talent) at 40 in the order above. **Whirlwind** at 36 [[d:warrior#1484031184601350286]].
- **Slam:** skip it before about 36. Its weapon-damage ranks are 60→65% (24), 70→80% (34), 80→90% (44) and 90→100% (54) [[d:warrior#1491503981527044217]]. Cast it right after a two-hander swing or you lose DPS [[d:warrior#1494518552319885393]].
- **Rend:** good below 30 on mobs that live its full duration, "almost entirely useless" after that [[d:warrior#1456710126621818981]]. Don't Rend anything that dies in under 15 s [[d:warrior#1476323710452367442]].
- **Heroic Strike:** rage-inefficient while leveling. Use it only when rage would cap [[d:warrior#1432937946989854780]], [[d:warrior#1481442159596142762]].
- **Don't buy every rank** of every ability; train what you use [[d:warrior#1470347926692691969]].
- **Weapon-skill quests** on Fray Island (where you learn Berserker Stance) cost 250 g per weapon type, cover both the 1H and 2H versions, and give +5 skill. They are "the single most impactful 250 (or 500g) you can/will spend" [[d:warrior#1434575531856232550]], [[d:warrior#1434575792700129301]], [[d:warrior#1487598353213558904]].

## Pull and kill loop (bot-ready)

1. Keep **Battle Shout** up between pulls [[d:warrior#1476323710452367442]], [[d:warrior#1484030990027456573]].
2. **Pull** with Charge on isolated mobs. For dense groups or dungeons, pull with a thrown or ranged weapon [[d:warrior#1476322395361906718]], [[d:warrior#1493937763802677289]].
3. **Demoralizing Shout** on melee mobs; they deal about 25% less damage afterwards [[d:warrior#1493937763802677289]].
4. **Sunder Armor:** usually one on a normal quest mob. Stack up to 5 only on elites or long fights, and never below 50% HP [[d:warrior#1484032312151769159]], [[d:warrior#1484032803472412833]], [[d:warrior#1476322978114568294]].
5. **Bloodthirst > Whirlwind** on cooldown once you have them (Fury) [[d:warrior#1484031184601350286]]. Arms: Mortal Strike, Overpower on procs [[d:warrior#1468414511064285255]].
6. **Heroic Strike or Cleave** only to dump rage [[d:warrior#1481442159596142762]].
7. **Execute** below 20%.
8. **Bloodrage** when you are above about 75% HP, so its self-damage crit procs Enrage [[d:warrior#1485271205001822288]].
9. Pull **2+ green mobs** from about level 30 onward for better experience per hour [[d:warrior#1034155462163181690]], [[d:warrior#1470346908122742926]].

## Downtime and resources

- Level on **green** mobs, carry **bandages**, and level First Aid [[d:warrior#1470346908122742926]]. Resting restores 2% HP per second sitting and 1% standing [[d:warrior#1470463044659449950]].
- **Blood Drinker** self-healing plus on-hit vampirism: with 2× Blood-Etched Fetish and 2× Darkmoon rings, one player "pretty much didnt have to eat from lvl 40-60" [[d:warrior#1485271389693939754]]. Farm Razorfen Downs early for the two Blood-Etched Fetishes and cheap vampirism enchants [[d:warrior#1466450703311569070]].
- **Vampirism is useless at very low levels**: it only heals on hits over 100 damage [[d:warrior#1467100727838113936]].
- **Stats while leveling:** Stamina > Strength > Agility. Hit is good when you find it [[d:warrior#1486362597975654420]].

## Tanking dungeons while leveling

- **Any spec can tank at level.** Arms or Fury with a shield in your bag out-threatens a leveling Prot warrior [[d:warrior#1432864256499388647]], [[d:warrior#1432864298937483405]], [[d:warrior#1424644443755122760]]. 2H Fury tanks easily to about 45 [[d:warrior#1470315145988411517]]. On Hardcore, 2H Arms with a shield swap on bosses works until about 45 [[d:warrior#1469875032087068683]].
- **Be at least the level of the dungeon's final boss** [[d:warrior#1459184897775374477]].
- **Pull:** line-of-sight pull → Bloodrage → Demoralizing Shout → tab-Sunder across the pack. Thunder Clap only with spare rage; it hits 4 targets [[d:warrior#1476325827296887007]]. Charge variant: Charge → swap to Defensive Stance mid-Charge → Demoralizing Shout → Thunder Clap → tab-Sunder [[d:warrior#1446128654332596224]].
- **Mark skull > cross > square.** Warriors can't hold much AoE threat, and an opening Whirlwind on a Fury build helps [[d:warrior#1469884937959243838]]. Your real job is keeping the healer alive; let a DPS who over-pulls tank it briefly [[d:warrior#1446128745810493533]].
- **Sweeping Strikes + Whirlwind** (Arms, from about 36) is the best AoE opener while leveling [[d:warrior#1491885115759001831]]. At low level without Defensive Tactics, Improved Thunder Clap is still "a threat beast" [[d:warrior#1491887719029280941]].

## Hardcore notes

- Always carry Swiftness Potions (and never use another 2-minute potion, so it's always ready) [[d:warrior#1459256861608902768]].
- Engineering target dummies; Obsidian Brooch for stamina [[d:warrior#1459256861608902768]], [[d:warrior#1384529902287720469]].
- Hamstring a single problem mob before running [[d:warrior#1459257399545167893]].
- "Take your time, never enter a structure full of hostiles" [[d:warrior#1459279946345091178]].
- Hamstring has no cooldown in 1.18.1, so kiting while Blood Drinker heals works again [[d:warrior#1484624968045166792]].

## Races and professions for leveling

- **Race** matters little. Troll's regeneration and Berserking make it strong for leveling [[d:warrior#1303430079434588260]], [[d:warrior#1494450078235099227]]. See [Races](index.md#races).
- **Professions:**
  - Mining + Engineering while leveling; drop Mining for a money profession once Engineering is maxed [[d:warrior#1451247090532356166]].
  - Alchemy gives durable leveling value [[d:warrior#1444330106830061751]].
  - Blacksmithing becomes recipe-gated and expensive past 50 [[d:warrior#1477033957517168756]]; buy the mats rather than farming them on a first character [[d:warrior#1477042373648191651]].
- **Battlegrounds** are a fast alternative to questing, at the cost of gold. Arathi Basin gives roughly 4% of a level per capture tick [[d:warrior#1474790113673216010]].

## Common mistakes

- Speccing Protection before Shield Slam [[d:warrior#1485778015593037934]].
- Using Slam before about 36, or casting it mid-swing [[d:warrior#1494518552319885393]].
- Heroic Strike spam at low rage [[d:warrior#1432937946989854780]].
- Dual wielding with bad weapons and no hit, and missing everything [[d:warrior#1487090299212660788]].
- Leveling in dungeons over and over for gear instead of questing [[d:warrior#1432395222012399616]].
- Copying classic talent guides as-is: "classic talents are completely different from turtle talents on every class" [[d:warrior#1457111490510458993]].
