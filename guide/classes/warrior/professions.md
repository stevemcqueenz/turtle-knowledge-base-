# Warrior professions

**Recommendation:** take **Engineering** on every warrior and pair it with **Mining** while you level. Once Engineering is maxed, drop Mining for whatever earns you steady gold (Herbalism, Enchanting, Alchemy) [[d:warrior#1451247090532356166]], [[d:warrior#1464385093215850497]]. Engineering is "the only profession that is" worth it for a warrior, according to a respected tank [[d:warrior#1478662431621517363]], [[d:warrior#1478662676279459840]]. The reasons are **Goblin Sapper Charges** (big raid AoE and threat), **target dummies**, grenades and trinkets. **Blacksmithing** is the only real alternative, and it is **contested**. **Alchemy** is the self-sufficiency pick. It is easiest to level while you level the character, or you can leave it to an alt.

## What each profession gives a warrior

### Engineering (take it)

- **Goblin Sapper Charges** are the main reason to take it. Players drop a Sapper on every pack, at Garr ("A sapper is literally the only thing you need for that pull. Literally better than consecrate"), and at Viscidus ("Sappers is bis for Visc") [[d:warrior#1484621943150739467]], [[d:warrior#1430634477323026604]], [[d:warrior#1470034210168508467]]. Players also use **Dense Dynamite** in raids [[d:warrior#1466733230005682309]].
- **Tank AoE kit:** Sapper + Oil of Immolation + Force Reactive Disk handles AoE threat better than any warrior ability [[d:warrior#1484555105830305853]], [[d:warrior#1484623951752859678]]. See [Protection](protection.md).
- **Target dummies** are the leveling and Hardcore favourite. On Hardcore, skip the basic dummy and go straight to **Advanced Target Dummies**, which "will last you to 60" [[d:warrior#1459256861608902768]], [[d:warrior#1384528747394371598]].
- **Craftable helms** (goggles) come up as a reason for leveling warriors [[d:warrior#1459283546194575496]]. There are **no 1.18.1 engineering goggles for Fury/Arms at 60**, only caster and Retribution goggles. Warriors get sharpening stones instead [[d:warrior#1487523830695071947]], [[d:warrior#1487524305175576739]] (single source).
- **Gnomish vs Goblin:**
  - Most players go **Gnomish** first for the BoP crafted trinkets and items. They respec Goblin later only to craft their own Sappers, and Sappers are cheap on the AH anyway [[d:warrior#1431676977240346750]], [[d:warrior#1431678831491547157]], [[d:warrior#1431679263609847839]].
  - Switching specialisation means unlearning Engineering and leveling it again from scratch. One player does Gnomish on the main and Goblin on an alt for Sappers [[d:warrior#1480987485197701121]], [[d:warrior#1480987563975114783]].
  - **Battle Chicken** (Gnomish) is for "max max max" damage: pop it before the pull, then swap trinkets. The Goblin Bomb Dispenser adds "maybe 0.1%" [[d:warrior#1466732324992581785]], [[d:warrior#1466732469796737024]], [[d:warrior#1466732564776747134]] (single source).
  - Goblin engineering also makes gold and is needed to fully upgrade a **T0.5 (Heroism)** set [[d:warrior#1431674590647816333]], [[d:warrior#1431674753105793044]] (single source).
- **Buy instead?** If you don't raid seriously, buy Sappers on the AH [[d:warrior#1466732801536819294]]. Engineering is "so expensive to grind up" before 60 unless another character funds it [[d:warrior#1444332326292029571]].

### Blacksmithing (contested)

- **Against:** "blacksmith is dogshit, avoid like the plague … go engi and something that makes constant revenue" [[d:warrior#1464385054607151125]], [[d:warrior#1464385093215850497]]. Low-level crafts are overpriced for their materials (10 Arcanite Bars for an entry-dungeon blue) [[d:warrior#1445797300621283461]], [[d:warrior#1445794745896144966]]. It becomes recipe-gated and expensive past level 50, so don't take it on a first character [[d:warrior#1477033957517168756]], [[d:warrior#1477042373648191651]].
- **For:** it "has some good bits now", mostly pre-raid. The Rune-Etched pieces and the **Tempered Runeblade** let you skip some pre-raid gearing. It also gives you "free" Elemental Sharpening Stones [[d:warrior#1445158997660536987]], [[d:warrior#1445159094184054927]], [[d:warrior#1444333624458743818]]. A guild still needs a blacksmith for **belt buckles and sharpening stones** [[d:warrior#1419743914084012143]].
- **Dreamsteel** armor is "kinda meh" after a nerf and with alternative tier sets available [[d:warrior#1445159308408127650]].
- **Elementium Reaper / Elementium Champion** (BWL upgrade of the Arcanite Reaper/Champion) are among the best Fury weapons ([Fury gear](fury.md)):
  - The Reaper needs **Axesmith** and the Champion needs **Swordsmith**. Plate-armor upgrades can be worn by any plate wearer [[d:warrior#1490124384684478474]], [[d:warrior#1490124503861428294]].
  - The upgrade stone is self-use only; you can't upgrade someone else's weapon through the trade window [[d:warrior#1486574899953799360]]. For how the upgrade works, see [the crafted-upgrade notes (forum)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168272#p168272).
- **Specialisation:**
  - **Armorsmith** is "the one that is most useful and makes the most money on average". Most output from either specialisation is BoE [[d:warrior#1360661294138523952]]. For a crafting-only warrior, "armor, always go for high hp as a warr" [[d:warrior#1439926722270330980]].
  - **Weaponsmith** is needed for the **Untempered → Tempered Runeblade** quest chain (the Untempered blade is sacrificed) [[d:warrior#1360661294138523952]].
  - Weaponsmith sub-specialisations lock the character. One blacksmith says they made a lot of gold regardless [[d:warrior#1491669802803269703]] (single source).
- **Leveling crafts:** a custom plate set and a life-leech 2H sword become available in the 40s and "will carry you to late game". Add Rugged Armor Kits for stamina [[d:warrior#1384529902287720469]], [[d:warrior#1458177892881268888]]. Blacksmithing wants Mining alongside it for bars, gems and elemental reagents [[d:warrior#1360851675404963965]].

### Alchemy (+ Herbalism)

- Alchemy is the best profession to "build up while levelling", because you use what you make. Leveling it at 60 is far less efficient [[d:warrior#1304030463933026317]]. Self-made food, elixirs and scrolls speed up kills, and some outputs such as Frost Oil sell [[d:warrior#1444330106830061751]].
- At 60 it makes **Mojo Madness, flasks and your own Mighty Rage Potions** [[d:warrior#1444333624458743818]]. A flask is mandatory for Fury in Naxx ([Fury consumables](fury.md)).
- **Buy or use an alt:** "It's worth having an alch and making your own consumes, but an alt can do that" [[d:warrior#1445159836688978163]]. "Alchemy on a main is lowkey troll" [[d:warrior#1480949252283830354]]. Herbalism on the main feeds a guild alchemist [[d:warrior#1444347771556724928]].

### Jewelcrafting (Turtle-custom)

- **Ring enchant** for Fury: **+3 all stats** from Jewelcrafting, or +Strength [[d:warrior#1440985379653161041]], [[d:warrior#1484959651052392649]]. The enchant is applied by a jewelcrafter; the synthesis notes that enchanted gemstones need no Goldsmith spec to benefit from ([staff note](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/976193120809123881/1385337387206578301)).
- **Obsidian Brooch** is a non-unique +11 Stamina Jewelcrafting trinket, "pretty much bis for leveling hardcore" [[d:warrior#1384529902287720469]]. A crafting-only warrior misses "the stam trinkets from jc and armor kits from leather working" [[d:warrior#1458178066856542406]].
- **Goldsmith vs Gemology:**
  - Goldsmith's warrior-relevant item is a magic-damage-reduction piece, "probably shit except for specific raid scenarios". +stat gems are more broadly useful [[d:warrior#1433098811928088676]], [[d:warrior#1433098992002269305]].
  - A tank who collected rare Goldsmithing recipes says the items "have a lot of flavor" but "currently they sadly just aren't good". He calls one "good for tanking Medivh" [[d:warrior#1481311511187165267]], [[d:warrior#1481311548176597146]], [[d:warrior#1481311596851495202]].
  - A **Goldsmith goblet** trinket is named as an effect-trinket option [[d:warrior#1484398963435114586]], [[d:warrior#1484399110990856223]].
  - The "drop a profession for Goldsmithing to get Talisman of Hindrance" advice was sarcasm ("/s, don't do it") [[d:warrior#1457421218071183360]].

### Leatherworking

- **Chromatic Leggings** are the best-stat pants on Ambershire at the time of that post. They need MC drops plus about 6 Cured Rugged Hides, which are expensive [[d:warrior#1465171596703694950]], [[d:warrior#1465170090986311856]]. Buy them instead; you don't need Leatherworking. Rugged Armor Kits are the leveling stamina boost [[d:warrior#1384529902287720469]].

### Enchanting

- A profession to pick up after dropping Mining [[d:warrior#1451247090532356166]]. It earns a little early gold, but you have to sell enchants yourself [[d:warrior#1444331459207757864]]. Disenchanting "is like lottery" [[d:warrior#1488603830814769233]].

### Survival and Gardening (secondary professions)

- **Before 1.18.1:** Survival was mainly **tents**. Resting in a tent gave rested XP ("15 min under those and get 2x EXP"), and the warrior channel treated tent questing as the fastest way to level [[d:warrior#1432724269145522306]], [[d:warrior#1442629300749865160]].
- **The 1.18.1 rework:** 80+ recipes, Woodcutting, gathering bags, and slow/root/trap tools ([Torta (staff)](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). Rested XP from kills was cut to 50%, and tents only fill up to 25% rested ([same](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). Warriors noted that "they also wrecked tents" and "Bg spam is now king" [[d:warrior#1473326647405969428]], [[d:warrior#1473326713210667060]].
- **Survival for warriors:** there is little class-specific evidence. Survival "perks (nets etc.)" are listed as twink PvP tools alongside Alchemy and Engineering [[d:warrior#1490664434526458038]]. Another warrior "found the nets on survival to be quite dissapointing" [[d:warrior#1490665285907386509]]. **Verdict:** optional. Take it for Hardcore, twinks or fun, not for performance.
- **Gardening:** its **Power Mushrooms** are the cheap alternative to Smoked Desert Dumplings / sandworm meat as warrior raid food [[d:warrior#1429921742431981661]], [[d:warrior#1457451144509919446]], [[d:warrior#1457451230006607914]]. Gardening requires Survival 75 ([official page](https://turtlecraft.gg/survival-and-gardening)). See the [Professions overview](../../professions.md) for how Gardening works.

## When to pick up and swap

1. **Levels 1-~40:** Mining (plus Skinning on Hardcore) to stockpile ore and leather. Light and Heavy Leather feed Engineering's goggles and armor kits, and Mining pays for the Engineering grind [[d:warrior#1405181247650791424]], [[d:warrior#1425089807780151359]], [[d:warrior#1484851300569972928]].
2. **Swap Skinning (or Mining) for Engineering** once you have a few stacks. "Keep your mined crap" [[d:warrior#1425089807780151359]], [[d:warrior#1384528747394371598]].
3. **Engineering maxed:** drop Mining for a gold or cooldown profession [[d:warrior#1451247090532356166]], [[d:warrior#1484850759660212245]].
4. **Specialisation:** choose Gnomish (BoP items, Battle Chicken) unless you only want to craft Sappers. Changing later means leveling Engineering again [[d:warrior#1480987563975114783]].
5. **If you want the Elementium Reaper/Champion**, you must be a 300 Blacksmith with the matching Axesmith/Swordsmith specialisation [[d:warrior#1490124384684478474]].

No skill thresholds are sourced beyond these.

## Race interactions

- **Goblin:** Goblin-only Engineering recipes include rocket boots. The usual plan is Gnomish first, then unlearn and switch to Goblin [[d:mage#1488174450477240420]]. The Goblin racial **Prospecting** grants Mining *and* Jewelcrafting skill ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102965#p102965)).
- The warrior channel has no profession notes on the other races (Gnome, Tauren and so on). See [Races](index.md#races).

## Gold

- Warriors are "extremely poor at making money" solo. Estimates are about 20-30 g/h solo farming, against 100 g+/h for a hunter on the ZG farm [[d:warrior#1440432464240840795]], [[d:warrior#1480961813423194142]]. That makes gathering professions and the AH more important than on other classes: "play any other class" for solo farming, "just oldschool mining and herb, auction flipping etc" instead [[d:warrior#1486993284369748059]] — e.g. buying and reselling Bijous [[d:warrior#1486993519842164797]].
- **Gathering:** mining while fresh [[d:warrior#1424042782267543584]]; Skinning + Herbalism to 60 "made plenty of gold for skills and riding" [[d:warrior#1482203596945621054]]; a tank + healer dungeon duo where the healer has Herbalism/Enchanting is "easy money" [[d:warrior#1471224216149033104]].
- **Crafting:** Goblin engineers can "make sapper charges for yourself and sell the extra" [[d:warrior#1472657347024388247]]. Alchemy converts cheap raw materials into sellable consumables [[d:warrior#1490715082332835841]].
- **Big gold sinks:** the weapon-skill quests (250 g per weapon type) are "the single most impactful 250 (or 500) you can or will spend" [[d:warrior#1463827253531119761]], [[d:warrior#1434575792700129301]]. Dual spec costs 350 g at level 50 [[d:warrior#1406228381384572998]].
- Farming routes (lashers, ZG crocs, Hyjal satyrs, Winterfall) are on the [Leveling](leveling.md) and class pages.
