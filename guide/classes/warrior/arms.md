# Arms Warrior (melee DPS)

**Recommendation:** Arms is the **fresh-60 and progression DPS spec**. It needs no Flurry uptime, so it works in low-crit gear, and it is the better choice until you reach about 23% unbuffed crit [[d:warrior#1469008830225580132]], [[d:warrior#1469009070210941099]]. In endgame raids it trails 2H Fury on most fights, except long fights and cleave fights [[d:warrior#1489363464605663365]], [[d:warrior#1492942371879391282]]. It comes in two builds:

- **MS Arms (35/16/0):** Mortal Strike, Master of Arms and Precision Cut, with Slam as filler. This is the consistent build.
- **Death Wish Arms, "Deathcute" (30/21/0):** skips Mortal Strike to reach Death Wish and Enrage, and puts everything into a buffed Precision Cut Execute. The Execute numbers are huge, but it plays slowly.

If you have the crit for Flurry, [2H Fury or Lawnmower](fury.md) does more real damage.

**Contested: is Arms competitive?** Some players' logs say yes. One Ambershire DW Fury player saw "a large DPS increase" in MC after switching to 31/20 Arms [[d:warrior#1474286981042405529]], and VeganCourage's Arms raidmate "routinely out dps" his 2H Fury on Patchwerk, Thaddius and Loatheb [[d:warrior#1474416275252969701]]. Since the patch, the consensus is that 2H Fury and Lawnmower are ahead ("Lawnmower and 2h fury are still better than arms") [[d:warrior#1484690162494603314]], [[d:warrior#1485299170024620223]]. At AQ40+ gear, MS Arms and 2H Fury give "very similar results depending on fight length" [[d:warrior#1492942371879391282]].

## How to play

A decision loop for a level-60 Arms warrior. The details are in [MS Arms](#ms-arms), [Deathcute](#deathcute), [AoE / cleave](#aoe--cleave) and [Cooldowns](#cooldowns).

1. **Opener:** Charge → Sunder Armor on the first GCD → Bloodrage (Enrage) [[d:warrior#1487841821928198335]].
2. **Single-target loop (MS Arms):**
   1. Mortal Strike on cooldown.
   2. Slam right after each auto with a slow two-hander: "auto -> slam -> something" [[d:warrior#1485948346420953218]].
   3. Whirlwind on cooldown.
   4. Overpower when it procs, if the swap won't throw away rage.
   5. Hamstring as a cheap filler.
   6. Heroic Strike only at the rage cap.
   - **Deathcute:** Slam is the filler until 20%.
3. **Execute phase:** bank rage before 20% and Execute from 20% to 0 [[d:warrior#1494705955731013692]]. Precision Cut turns extra rage into damage. Deathcute: Death Wish + Bloodrage, then spam Execute.
4. **Cooldowns:**
   - **Recklessness** in the Execute phase; it shares a 30-minute cooldown with Shield Wall and Retaliation [[d:warrior#1462506610068029502]].
   - **Bloodrage** on the pull, and again into Execute on fights of 1:15 or longer.
   - **Death Wish** (Deathcute) for the Execute phase.
5. **AoE switch (2+ targets):**
   1. Sweeping Strikes first, in Battle Stance.
   2. Then Mortal Strike, Whirlwind and Cleave.
   3. Don't spend Sweeping Strikes on low-damage buttons.
6. **Resource rule:**
   - A stance swap to Overpower costs 50+ rage about 80–90% of the time [[d:warrior#1486211683738456225]], so only swap with little rage banked.
   - The Heroic Strike swing makes no rage.

## What changed in 1.18.1

- **Precision Cut** now raises the damage per extra rage point spent on Execute by 25/50/75%. It swapped tree positions with Boundless Anger, so Precision Cut now requires 3/3 Boundless Anger [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071), [[d:warrior#1487614924757799136]]. Result: "You drop MoA for enrage now, precision cut got big buffed" [[d:warrior#1484972916595757106]], and "arms got actually good executes back" [[d:warrior#1481504953888542905]].
- **Execute:** **Contested:** spell data still lists all ranks at 15 rage, but players report the cost dropped to 10, with the freed rage becoming damage [[d:warrior#1467491606910472306]]. It scales with Attack Power, armor penetration and rage spent, not with weapon damage [[d:warrior#1484660616991080541]], [[d:warrior#1485080360978092143]].
- **Sweeping Strikes** (January hotfix) no longer double-dips damage modifiers. It hits for 20% of the damage and cannot crit [[d:warrior#1463776864144199833]], [[d:warrior#1485394033395105993]].
- **Hamstring** has no cooldown again [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- The **T1 3-piece** and **T3 4-piece** (+4% ability crit damage) changes apply to Arms as well [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- The pre-1.18 "Execute on a cooldown" change from Class Changes 2 was reverted. The developers said it "forced most Arms players to spec into the Fury tree" [[d:warrior#1398137181406498906]].

## Stat priority and caps

1. **Hit to the yellow cap:** 8% at 300 weapon skill, 5% at 315 [[d:warrior#1457402757693833248]].
2. **Weapon skill 310-315.** Two-Handed Weapon Specialization already gives +3 [[d:warrior#1493323470170488984]], [[d:warrior#1458890331666911403]].
3. **Crit (Agility).** Arms keeps valuing Agility above 34% crit, unlike Fury, which loses Flurry value there [[d:warrior#1467622383731867730]]. Early raiders pick an **axe** for the +5% crit from the Arms weapon talent, to avoid rage starvation [[d:warrior#1470334841907056703]].
4. **Strength / AP.** Mortal Strike, Slam and Whirlwind scale with weapon damage and AP. Execute scales with AP, armor penetration and rage [[d:warrior#1484660616991080541]].
5. **Armor penetration** has its best case on Arms, because Execute scales with it [[d:warrior#1485080360978092143]]. It is still weak against fully debuffed bosses [[d:warrior#1488197833382170635]].
6. **Haste** gives Arms no value on Whirlwind or Sweeping Strikes damage [[d:warrior#1479852699406041250]].

## Single-target rotation

### MS Arms

1. **Opener:** Charge → Sunder Armor on the first GCD → Bloodrage (it procs Enrage because its self-damage counts as a crit) [[d:warrior#1487841821928198335]].
2. **Mortal Strike** on cooldown.
3. **Slam** right after each auto lands. The basic loop is "auto -> slam -> something (hamstring for example)" [[d:warrior#1485948346420953218]]. Use a slow weapon: 3.4 speed clips, 3.6 works, 3.8 is ideal for fitting two Slams around one swing [[d:warrior#1454938660276408574]].
4. **Whirlwind** on cooldown [[d:warrior#1477507600320041145]].
5. **Overpower** when it procs. For Arms it is worth a little more than for 2H Fury: "id prio it over ww as arms (still slam tho)". It is a small consistency gain worth chasing only if you play near the top [[d:warrior#1493216441845940356]]. Watch the stance-swap cost: most of the time a swap to Overpower throws away 50+ rage [[d:warrior#1486211683738456225]].
6. **Hamstring** as a cheap filler GCD; it has no cooldown now [[d:warrior#1485948346420953218]].
7. **Heroic Strike** only when rage would otherwise cap (vanilla baseline). The Heroic Strike swing generates no rage [[d:warrior#1473745241159762074]].
8. **Execute phase (under 20%):** bank rage before 20% so you can Execute from 20% to 0 [[d:warrior#1494705955731013692]]. Precision Cut turns every extra rage point into 25/50/75% more damage [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).

### Deathcute

- **Before 20%:** Slam is your filler ("ur filler is slam") [[d:warrior#1485948244092518560]]. Keep Mortal-Strike-less damage up with Whirlwind, Overpower and Sweeping Strikes on packs.
- **Under 20%:** pool rage, then Death Wish + Bloodrage (Enrage) and **spam Execute** ("execute XD") [[d:warrior#1485948147040649348]]. Expect dead time at 0 rage between Executes [[d:warrior#1486834452662456451]].

## AoE / cleave

- **Sweeping Strikes** first on any 2+ target pull, then Mortal Strike, Whirlwind and Cleave. MS Arms is "strong on trash/cleave via Sweeping Strikes cleave window" every ~3.8 s, or ~2.4 s with Flurry [[d:warrior#1486384393625927822]], [[d:warrior#1486386053546770486]].
- Sweeping Strikes hits the second target for 20% of the damage [[d:warrior#1463776864144199833]]. It does not copy the Mortal Strike debuff [[d:warrior#1473506056574337208]]. It copies an Execute only onto a second target that is also below 20% [[d:warrior#1428909002011447358]].
- Do not spend Sweeping Strikes charges on low-damage buttons such as Hamstring or Pummel (pre-1.18.1 advice) [[d:warrior#1358900982875947129]].
- A dungeon version from a Jan-2026 slow-2H AoE build: Bloodrage → Demoralizing Shout → Sweeping Strikes → Mortal Strike [[d:warrior#1462928828090290229]] (single source).
- On pure trash and cleave, [Lawnmower](fury.md#lawnmower-2h-fury-with-sweeping-strikes-21300) still wins [[d:warrior#1486336808806649927]].

## Cooldowns

- **Death Wish** (Deathcute only): save it for the Execute phase [[d:warrior#1482492282388091114]].
- **Recklessness** in the Execute phase. It shares a 30-minute cooldown with Shield Wall and Retaliation [[d:warrior#1462506610068029502]].
- **Bloodrage** on the pull, and again into Execute on fights of 1:15 or longer [[d:warrior#1433266387962560572]].
- **Kiss of the Spider** is very strong when the Execute phase is the most important part of the fight [[d:warrior#1468275155242586275]], [[d:warrior#1492246963108708543]].

## Gear

Arms uses the same two-handers as 2H Fury (see [Fury → Two-handers](fury.md#two-handers)):

- **BWL phase:** Untamed Blade over Ashkandi (certain) and probably over BRE; BRE can carry you to Naxx [[d:warrior#1481969895246659655]], [[d:warrior#1481972504670244894]].
- **Mortal Strike scales with weapon damage.** Choose between a high-weapon-skill slow weapon and a higher-DPS new one by your raid's armor-debuff coverage [[d:warrior#1467556342804775226]], [[d:warrior#1467565147781599295]].
- **Pre-BiS:** there is no Arms pre-BiS list. Players reuse the Fury list or build their own from the database [[d:warrior#1458552269883707474]].
- **Sets:** the T3 4-piece (+4% ability crit damage) and the T1 3-piece rage proc help Arms as they help Fury [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).

Enchants, stones, consumables, macros and addons are the same as for Fury: see [Fury → Enchants](fury.md#enchants) and [Fury → Consumables](fury.md#consumables-and-buffs). One Arms-specific difference: on gloves, many take **+9 Strength**, but Agility stays good for Arms above 34% crit [[d:warrior#1467622383731867730]].

## Raid notes

- **Kara40 ([Tower of Karazhan](../../instances/tower-of-karazhan.md)):** Arms does relatively better with longer kill times [[d:warrior#1471390505643544607]]. Kruul is a no-adds fight where only the enrage phase below 25% matters, and that is where Arms gets to Execute [[d:warrior#1371573397816873142]].
- **Naxx:** short kill times favor Fury: "arms is gonna stay getting mogged in naxx where everything dies quick" [[d:warrior#1471390505643544607]].

## Common mistakes

- Taking Master of Arms over Enrage in a Precision Cut build [[d:warrior#1484972916595757106]].
- Stance-swapping for Overpower while holding a lot of rage [[d:warrior#1486211683738456225]].
- Playing Deathcute in a raid that kills bosses too fast for the Execute phase to matter [[d:warrior#1486834175318425761]].
- Reading pre-1.18 Arms guides, which assume Execute has a cooldown or rely on the old Precision Cut refund [[d:warrior#1398137181406498906]], [[d:warrior#1403876059283197992]].
