# Combat Rogue (melee DPS)

**Recommendation:** play **Combat Envenom, 24/27/0**. Build combo points with **Backstab** (daggers) or **Sinister Strike** (swords). Keep **1-point Slice and Dice and 1-point Envenom** up, cast Envenom first, and put every other combo point into a **5-point Eviscerate**. Do not use Rupture or Taste for Blood. Stack Agility (Blade Rush speeds up your energy ticks), take a slow, strong main hand and the fastest off hand you can get, and toggle **Blade Flurry** on only while two or more enemies are in reach. Poison both weapons with **Instant Poison** and keep a Dissolvent set for elementals. The 20/31/0 **Adrenaline Rush** build remains an option for short and cleave fights.

**Why it replaced Assassination.** Combat Envenom plays like the old 41/10/0 poison build, except that Backstab or Sinister Strike replaces Noxious Assault as the builder [[d:rogue#1488628288845910149]], [[d:rogue#1488629955783823381]]. You give up a "triple nerfed" Noxious Assault and Seal Fate. In exchange you get Blade Rush (haste plus Agility-scaled energy), 5 weapon skill from Weapon Expertise (which frees a gloves slot), and 5% crit from Close Quarters Combat or Hack and Slash [[d:rogue#1486883475448397895]], [[d:rogue#1489401067983601705]]. After 1.18.1, Assassination lost about 10% while Combat Envenom had only ever been about 5% behind, which put Combat Envenom 5% ahead [[d:rogue#1485568535215476746]].

## How to play

The loop for **Combat Envenom (24/27/0)** at 60. The reasons and the Adrenaline Rush variant are in [Single-target rotation](#single-target-rotation), [AoE and cleave](#aoe-and-cleave) and [Cooldowns](#cooldowns).

1. **Before the pull:** Instant Poison on both weapons, with a Dissolvent pair in your bags for elementals ([Poisons and weapon sets](#poisons-and-weapon-sets)). Put a combo point on a critter or an add, then carry it to the boss with Slice and Dice [[d:rogue#1480688682787667988]].
2. **Opener:** no stealth opener; walking in stealth costs too much time [[d:rogue#1468327294430085130]]. Open with **Backstab** (daggers) or **Sinister Strike** (swords). At 1 combo point cast **Envenom, then Slice and Dice** [[d:rogue#1484581432889442335]].
3. **Single-target loop (top first):**
    1. **Envenom** at 1 combo point if it is missing or about to fall off.
    2. **Slice and Dice** at 1 combo point if it is missing or about to fall off.
    3. **Eviscerate** at 5 combo points, not 1-point spam [[d:rogue#1488169648573907026]].
    4. **Surprise Attack** when usable and at 80 energy or less [[d:rogue#1492170434672595226]].
    5. **Backstab** from behind (daggers) or **Sinister Strike** (swords). Never Sinister Strike with a dagger.
4. **Cooldowns:** Thistle Tea (100 energy) when energy-starved; active trinkets on bosses; **Vanish** when you are about to pass the tank on threat [[d:rogue#1465825152125374744]]. Adrenaline Rush build: Adrenaline Rush right after a 5-point Rupture.
5. **AoE switch:** **2 or more** enemies in melee → **Blade Flurry on**, same rotation. **1** → Blade Flurry off. Blade Flurry hits only one extra target, so big packs stay weak [[d:rogue#1468684315079479351]], [[d:rogue#1470215457813434539]].
6. **Resource rule:** energy is 20 every 2 s, faster with Blade Rush and Agility. The Ruthlessness point pays for the next 1-point refresh. Don't mash finishers [[d:rogue#1479064018260856862]].
7. **Movement and situational:** when the boss faces you, `/cast [behind]Backstab;[nobehind]Sinister Strike` switches your builder [[d:rogue#1487600151974641806]]. Kick casts. Feint only lowers your threat generation; Vanish resets it [[d:rogue#1455361248580468992]].

Why these points:

- **Poison talents over Lethality**: moving points out of Lethality into Vile Poisons and Murder simmed the same or better [[d:rogue#1491115660657557584]]. Efficient Poisons also cuts poison application time (25/50/75%) and saves charges; skipping it costs about 100 DPS in the sim [[d:rogue#1484425282109837392]], [[d:rogue#1488772882057265222]].
- **Improved Backstab** only helps with daggers. With swords, take **Hack and Slash**; it is the stronger talent with Envenom, and only Improved Backstab makes up the gap on daggers [[d:rogue#1489273787026247803]].

## Stat priority and caps

1. **Hit to 8%** for abilities (Backstab, Sinister Strike, Eviscerate) against a level-63 boss. Every point of weapon skill above 300 adds 0.2% hit, which the character sheet does not show [[d:rogue#1461126609196023922]], [[d:rogue#1462102234874511605]]. Precision gives 5%.
2. **Agility.** It gives AP, crit (about 1% per 29 Agility) and, through Blade Rush, energy [[d:rogue#1445046771288899736]]. About 400 raid-buffed Agility is where you start to feel the faster ticks; around 800 you reach close to 1-second ticks [[d:rogue#1462569850768724213]], [[d:rogue#1462133009389195521]]. Grace of Air alone "buffs your dps by as much as getting gear upgrades from a whole raid tier" [[d:rogue#1488184613011460238]].
3. **Crit**, up to the crit cap. Crit matters more for Combat than for Assassination, because your autos and Backstabs crit while poisons only crit from spell crit [[d:rogue#1465627335121309697]]. Once you reach the crit cap, take hit again [[d:rogue#1492968340845760683]].
4. **Hit beyond 8%**: dual wielding adds about 19% miss to both hands' autos, so white hits need about 25% [[d:rogue#1465636991772135540]], [[d:rogue#1475559874728296673]]. Around 16% hit is a comfortable spot where you don't have to worry about the crit cap [[d:rogue#1467164411058983042]].
5. **Haste and AP.** Haste is multiplicative with Slice and Dice [[d:rogue#1387271666970787983]].

## Single-target rotation

### Opener

1. Before the pull, put a combo point on any mob or critter; it stays there. Cast Slice and Dice on the boss to carry it over (combo-point transfer with Ruthlessness) [[d:rogue#1480688682787667988]], [[d:rogue#1458895659951325305]].
2. Open with **Backstab** (daggers) or **Sinister Strike** (swords), not a stealth opener. Walking to the boss in stealth costs too much time [[d:rogue#1468327294430085130]].
3. At 1 combo point: **Envenom**, then **Slice and Dice**. Casting Envenom first is worth about 50 DPS in the sim [[d:rogue#1484581432889442335]], [[d:rogue#1484584436648644618]].

### Priority (Combat Envenom)

1. **Envenom** at 1 combo point if it is missing or about to fall off [[d:rogue#1488169648573907026]].
2. **Slice and Dice** at 1 combo point if it is missing or about to fall off.
3. **Eviscerate** at 5 combo points ("5 cp evis, not spam") [[d:rogue#1488169648573907026]].
4. **Surprise Attack** when usable (after the target dodges). A macro that casts it only below 80 energy avoids wasting energy [[d:rogue#1492170434672595226]].
5. **Backstab** from behind (daggers) or **Sinister Strike** (swords) to build combo points. **Never Sinister Strike with a dagger**: "you are giga griefing yourself" [[d:rogue#1489272040014745860]].
6. Optional gain: when Slice and Dice or Envenom is about to run out and you hold more than 1 point, a 2-4 point Eviscerate first beats spending the extra points on the buff [[d:rogue#1486749401731825879]].

Ruthlessness hands back one combo point after every finisher, so the next buff refresh is usually free [[d:rogue#1478779997878485167]]. A dodged or parried builder costs only about 7 energy [[d:rogue#1480274083160789204]].

### Priority (Adrenaline Rush variant)

1. **Slice and Dice** (any combo points) and a **5-point Rupture** for Taste for Blood; keep both up. "Combat is just 2 buffs" [[d:rogue#1462618589298168065]], [[d:rogue#1484205985273741494]].
2. **Adrenaline Rush** right after a 5-point Rupture, so its Backstabs happen inside the Taste for Blood window [[d:rogue#1424148190663278846]]. Pop it once on the pull if the boss dies within ~3 minutes, twice on longer fights [[d:rogue#1446573952553058305]].
3. **Eviscerate** with spare points only if both buffs have 20+ seconds left, ideally during Adrenaline Rush [[d:rogue#1462637371756384276]].
4. **Backstab** as the builder. Use Sinister Strike only when a buff is about to drop and you need the cheaper point [[d:rogue#1465801330600837316]].

### Positioning notes

- Attacks from behind cannot be parried but can still be dodged [[d:rogue#1453200218559483968]].
- On fights that turn the boss toward you (e.g. Thaddius), swap to a fast weapon and Sinister Strike until you are behind again; macro the swap [[d:rogue#1447254729154494575]].
- `/cast [behind]Backstab;[nobehind]Sinister Strike` (SuperCleveRoidMacros) switches the builder automatically [[d:rogue#1487600151974641806]], [[d:rogue#1487603095763685609]].

## AoE and cleave

**Blade Flurry** is a baseline toggle (level 30). While it is on, your energy regeneration drops by 20% [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071) and your hits cleave **one** extra target [[d:rogue#1468684315079479351]].

| Targets in melee | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Blade Flurry **off**; it lowers your single-target damage [[d:rogue#1473731055390298133]] |
| 2+ | Blade Flurry **on**; still single-target rotation. Very strong on small packs like the four elementals in MC or paired Onyxia dragons [[d:rogue#1491477144294002869]] |
| Many (big trash) | Blade Flurry on; rogue AoE stays weak on large pulls. Sappers help if you care about trash meters [[d:rogue#1470215457813434539]], [[d:rogue#1470215674751090821]] |

- Poisons, weapon procs and bleeds do not hit the second target [[d:rogue#1491115201754300516]], [[d:rogue#1475398289737388193]]. A slow, hard-hitting main hand carries Sinister Strike and white damage into the cleave. Main-hand speed barely matters in single target, but a fast main hand sacrifices Blade Flurry damage [[d:rogue#1487361136171941889]], [[d:rogue#1486738916844503212]].
- On mixed trash, many players leave Instant Poison on both weapons instead of swapping, and still do well with Blade Flurry (MC Shazzrah/Harbinger packs) [[d:rogue#1491477644133531930]].
- The buff-cap problem with Blade Flurry [[d:rogue#1482917430350188646]] was fixed in April 2026 [[d:rogue#1495130453802352766]]. The old macro that cancelled buffs to make room for it [[d:rogue#1482234570983342172]] is no longer needed.

## Resource and threat management

- Base energy is 20 every 2 seconds [[d:rogue#1489263649020182698]]; Blade Rush shortens the tick (above).
- Combo points stay on a target as long as it lives, even if you click away [[d:rogue#1465788380661944452]]. A new combo point on a different target resets them [[d:rogue#1482311939945267351]].
- Double-pressing a finisher can overwrite a 5-point cast with a 1-point one; don't mash finishers [[d:rogue#1479064018260856862]].
- **Threat:** rogues can pull aggro when they pop cooldowns at the start of a fight. Let the tank build threat first, and use Feint or a Vanish in the opener [[d:rogue#1490051271405735976]], [[d:rogue#1490058561286443039]]. Feint only lowers your threat generation; it does not dump threat [[d:rogue#1455361248580468992]].

## Gear

Gear is front-loaded: get Darkmantle first ("Darkmantle darkmantle and darkmantle") [[d:rogue#1488543597283381299]].

### Pre-raid and MC

- **Armor:** 6/8 Darkmantle (T0.5) + 2 T2 pieces, then a third T2 piece as soon as possible. The T2 3-piece bonus beats Stormshroud, which has no Agility [[d:rogue#1488910311829278750]]. Drop Stormshroud by BWL at the latest [[d:rogue#1492171488881803536]]. The Darkmantle chain turns in several pieces at a time: bracers, then belt + gloves, then shoulders + boots + legs, then chest + helm [[d:rogue#1488231373759057931]].
- **Weapons:** Perdition's Blade + Gutgore Ripper was one early Combat Envenom kit (465 unbuffed Agility, 12% haste, 16% hit, 950+ DPS) [[d:rogue#1484974114350301414]], [[d:rogue#1484968715588997191]]. The Ripper (Stormwind Vault, Damian, 1% drop) is the BiS off hand [[d:rogue#1488077115793604770]], [[d:rogue#1482771687664783607]], [[d:rogue#1482761245655302307]]. Core Hound Tooth beats Perdition's Blade in the main hand because Perdition's has no stats [[d:rogue#1494268101695377559]].
- **MC/Ony priorities:** Onyxia's neck, Band of Accuria, the Ragnaros cloak (Cloak of the Shrouded Mists), Aged Core Leather Gloves [[d:rogue#1454949336520589413]], [[d:rogue#1463202914322612416]].

### BWL

- **Armor:** 4-piece Darkmantle (head, shoulders, belt, legs) + 3-piece T2 (boots, chest, bracers) beats full T2.5 on a PvE realm; on Tel'Abim probably not, since you won't reach the Agility breakpoint without Kings and Agility totem [[d:rogue#1490028194282209441]], [[d:rogue#1490028255820906526]], [[d:rogue#1490028326885134450]], [[d:rogue#1490355611957723217]].

### AQ and later

- **Veiled Shadows** (AQ20): the 2-piece (ring + cloak) is strong enough to replace the Shrouded Mist cloak; the 3-piece is only a small gain over Shrouded Mist, and the dagger is strong enough on its own to compete with Death's Sting [[d:rogue#1490346429674164224]], [[d:rogue#1490345569212960838]], [[d:rogue#1490347033037508838]], [[d:rogue#1490346873775460442]]. **Contested:** an AQ setup with no Darkmantle and full Veiled Shadows sims well for Nandieb [[d:rogue#1490326444075909274]]; another sim had Darkmantle slightly ahead [[d:rogue#1490333846095401080]].
- Daggers pull ahead of swords again in AQ because the Veiled Shadows dagger is easy to get [[d:rogue#1493505574674104391]].
- **AQ20 2-piece** (cheaper Slice and Dice) is worth about +1 energy per second [[d:rogue#1477936734263050343]].
- From AQ40, Combat needs the **T2.5 chest, gloves and boots**. The chest matters most; boots are less important [[d:rogue#1478923047317541054]], [[d:rogue#1478923114413817887]].
- **Naxx:** soft-reserve Kingsfall before anything else [[d:rogue#1465088483503767683]]. For Combat Envenom the only Naxx off hand that beats the Ripper is Claw of the Frost Wyrm [[d:rogue#1488164532223217825]].

### Trinkets

- Before DFT: the haste Flask trinket beats Vial, Royal Seal and Whip when paired with the Darkmantle and Hand of Justice procs [[d:rogue#1487596668349321337]].
- DFT is the passive "BiS forever" [[d:rogue#1490393540956524746]]; add an active AP trinket or Badge for boss burst [[d:rogue#1487230426148765727]].
- The new Onyxia trinket (a Windfury-like group proc) is a strong melee-group support pick with a Shaman in the group [[d:rogue#1488214504155250811]].
- Fortune only appears on capes, necks and trinkets [[d:rogue#1474793529476645117]]. It multiplies an item's proc chance (10% → 12.5%) [[d:rogue#1484670147619848192]] and does not affect poisons, set bonuses or talents [[d:rogue#1488220449425985748]].

### Cloak

Shrouded Mist sims above Time-Lord's in most builds [[d:rogue#1488051769400492073]]. With swords, add Taut Dragonhide Boots for sword skill [[d:rogue#1489315015994511533]].

## Poisons and weapon sets

**Rule since 1.18.1:** Dissolvent and Corrosive only land on **Elementals, Mechanicals, Giants and Undead**. Instant and Deadly land on everything except nature-immune targets [[d:rogue#1484513053876949133]], [[d:rogue#1487458269957062890]].

| Situation | Envenom build | Adrenaline Rush build |
|---|---|---|
| Trash (default) | **Instant** both hands | Instant both hands |
| Boss, normal type | Instant both, or Instant MH + Deadly OH (**contested**) | **Deadly V MH + Corrosive II OH** if the boss is Undead/Giant/Mechanical; **Deadly V + Deadly IV** otherwise |
| Nature-immune (elementals) | **Dissolvent** both | Dissolvent MH + Corrosive OH |
| Poison-immune (Twin Emperors, BWL Spellbinders) | swap nothing; no poison lands | same |

- **Contested (Envenom):** Rheinwg's rule is "envenom shouldnt be running with deadly" [[d:rogue#1487595221956038837]]. A quick dummy test had Instant MH + Deadly OH about 35 DPS (~5%) ahead of double Instant [[d:rogue#1487611827947962549]], and Rheinwg later added "Deadly and Cor are actually pretty good for Combat envenom" to his guide [[d:rogue#1484754354433953915]]. Sim your own gear.
- **Adrenaline Rush and Subtlety** do more damage with Deadly V + IV; Envenom procs Instant so often that it overtakes the DoTs [[d:rogue#1491214094685044858]]. Rheinwg's default for DoT specs is Deadly MH + Corrosive OH on bosses and Instant/Dissolvent on trash [[d:rogue#1487771304861106207]]. Deadly V and Corrosive II only come from AQ20 [[d:rogue#1474013595485339831]].
- Deadly IV is flaky: two rogues' Deadly IV share one debuff, and whether one rogue's IV and V stack properly is disputed [[d:rogue#1486069806133284864]], [[d:rogue#1491761435087470743]].
- **Weapon sets:** keep a second, cheap pair (e.g. Julie's Daggers) coated with the other poison type in your bags and swap when the creature type changes [[d:rogue#1484557710065406146]]. A SuperCleveRoidMacros macro can equip the right pair by target type [[d:rogue#1485630246320341053]]. ItemRack cannot tell two identical daggers apart, so use two different items [[d:rogue#1485651207103184927]].
- Poisons and sharpening stones or oils share the same slot [[d:rogue#1491077449381838878]]. Windfury is an aura on Turtle and does not conflict with poisons [[d:rogue#1461058653653045258]].

Per-raid poison plan: see [Assassination → Poisons by raid](assassination.md#poisons-by-raid).

## Macros and addons

- **SuperCleveRoidMacros** (+ SuperWoW, Nampower, UnitXP) is the standard toolkit for conditional macros [[d:rogue#1438755956488929280]], [[d:rogue#1484285172735152338]].
- One-button swords macro (Eviscerate-spam form; change `combo:>1` to `combo:=5` for the 5-point version): `/startattack` · `/cast [nomybuff:"Slice and Dice" combo:=1] Slice and Dice;` · `/cast [nomybuff:envenom combo:=1]Envenom;` · `/cast [usable:"Surprise Attack"]Surprise Attack;` · `/cast [combo:>1] Eviscerate` · `/cast [mypower:>40]Sinister Strike` [[d:rogue#1487080588937658369]].
- Daggers: `#showtooltip Backstab` · `/startattack` · `/cast [myrawpower:<=80]Surprise Attack` · `/cast [behind]Backstab;[nobehind]Sinister Strike` [[d:rogue#1492170434672595226]].
- **Lateral** gives timers for Slice and Dice, Expose Armor, Taste for Blood and Envenom; it has a Nampower version [[d:rogue#1461098707733840091]], [[d:rogue#1463468831220699197]], [[d:rogue#1475441686443196478]]. **DoiteAuras** is a WeakAuras-like alternative [[d:rogue#1452061147892154458]]. **Backstabber** shows whether you are behind the target [[d:rogue#1487605765098311820]], [[d:rogue#1487609477958991913]].
- **TrinketMenu** (newest version) queues a trinket swap that fires on Vanish [[d:rogue#1465833401046007849]], [[d:rogue#1465833733126094973]]. **EzPoison / EasyPoisons** apply poisons in one click; **TW Buy Poisons** buys reagents [[d:rogue#1474729457947508736]], [[d:rogue#1474728667019219024]], [[d:rogue#1474760978431217796]].

## Raid notes

- **MC** is the worst raid for poison swaps (elemental trash next to humanoid bosses) [[d:rogue#1485839409915101225]]; see the per-boss list in [Assassination](assassination.md#poisons-by-raid). Blade Flurry shines on the elemental packs. See [Molten Core](../../instances/molten-core.md).
- **BWL:** everything takes Instant/Deadly; you do not swap poisons in BWL [[d:rogue#1489502688897929307]]. On Nefarian's rogue call, Vanish saves you once; a Living Action Potion or the Onyxia cloak a second time [[d:rogue#1492553897380675604]], [[d:rogue#1492559922045386914]]. See [Blackwing Lair](../../instances/blackwing-lair.md).
- **AQ40:** Twin Emperors are immune to all poisons; use Frost Oils on Viscidus [[d:rogue#1484756192734478396]].
- **Naxx:** Consecrated Sharpening Stones were a pre-patch pick for Combat daggers after the Spider wing [[d:rogue#1472013120036671579]]. Rheinwg's post-patch plan is Deadly V + Corrosive II on most bosses and Deadly V + IV on Faerlina and Maexxna [[d:rogue#1484757612271501332]].
- **Kara40:** swap by section (see the Assassination page). See [Tower of Karazhan](../../instances/tower-of-karazhan.md).

## Common mistakes

- Running Rupture and Taste for Blood in an Envenom build [[d:rogue#1486815192690987191]].
- Casting Sinister Strike with a dagger in the main hand [[d:rogue#1489272040014745860]].
- Leaving Blade Flurry on in single target, or taking a fast main hand when you want to cleave [[d:rogue#1473731055390298133]], [[d:rogue#1487361136171941889]], [[d:rogue#1486738916844503212]].
- Spamming 1-point Eviscerates instead of 5-point ones [[d:rogue#1488169648573907026]].
- Keeping Dissolvent on against Humanoids, Beasts, Dragonkin or Demons, where it now does nothing [[d:rogue#1484513053876949133]].
- Stacking Stormshroud into BWL; it has no Agility [[d:rogue#1488910311829278750]].
- Taking Improved Expose Armor in Combat Envenom. It costs you Efficient Poisons ("the worst out of all rogue specs to run iea"), and one tester ran out of poison charges on every trash pack. If the raid wants a rogue on Expose Armor, the Noxious Assault build is the better fit [[d:rogue#1489309057008537871]], [[d:rogue#1489503869820993676]], [[d:rogue#1489309237078392902]].
