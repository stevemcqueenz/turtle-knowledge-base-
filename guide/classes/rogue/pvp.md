# Rogue PvP

**Recommendation:** play **Assassination (Noxious Assault)** to kill plate and burst in arenas and duels, and **Subtlety (Hemorrhage/Preparation)** for control and outplay. Run **Instant Poison on both weapons**; since 1.18.1 Dissolvent and Corrosive do not work on players, who are Humanoid [[d:rogue#1488217700206641192]]. Keep an off-hand swap with Crippling Poison. Hit cap for abilities is **5%**. Expect a harder time than before: dampening cuts damage and the patch's effect on PvP viability is itself disputed (**contested**, see State below).

Before 1.18.1, Dissolvent Poison was described in PvP as "the new physical dmg that ignores armor", force-procced by the level-40 keystone and dealing "insane dmg" [[d:pvp-general#1423408386569011260]]. A pre-patch `#pvp-general` tier list put rogues in the top "Z tier" [[d:pvp-general#1426339548039872532]]. That build no longer exists.

## How to play

The level-60 PvP decision loop. The full combos are in [Burst and control sequences](#burst-and-control-sequences), poison setup in [Poisons](#poisons), and per-class detail in [Matchups](#matchups). Cooldowns below come from the 1.18.1 client data.

**Assassination (Noxious Assault):**

1. **Opener (stealth):** sneak up → `Cheap Shot` → `Envenom` → mash `Noxious Assault`. Against big health pools (bear druids) use Cheap Shot → Envenom → `Slice and Dice` [[d:rogue#1492308177818095657]], [[d:rogue#1492308239507918908]]. If you face two enemies, `Sap` one first [[d:rogue#1374554824783298670]].
2. **Burst:** spend the Cheap Shot stun building to 5 points with Noxious Assault (`Thistle Tea` if short) → `Kidney Shot` (20 s cooldown) → Noxious until dead. `Cold Blood` (3 min) on a Noxious Assault or a 5-point Eviscerate [[d:rogue#1461753771838406667]], [[d:rogue#1492645046049046699]].
3. **Re-lock:** if they trinket the Kidney, Thistle Tea → `Vanish` (5 min) → Cheap Shot again → Noxious into a 5-point Cold Blood Eviscerate [[d:rogue#1442823947702894734]].
4. **Control chain:** Cheap Shot and Kidney Shot do not share diminishing returns on this client [[d:pvp-general#1426568992855822399]]. Add `Gouge` or `Blind` (5 min) when needed [[d:rogue#1461754174701179098]]. Don't run Deadly Poison if you plan to Blind [[d:rogue#1486568837498273956]].
5. **Defence:** with everything on cooldown, `Evasion` (4 min) survives most melee [[d:rogue#1461754193810292736]]. Save Evasion and `Sprint` (4 min) to escape [[d:rogue#1374554824783298670]].

**Subtlety (Hemorrhage/Preparation):**

1. **Opener (stealth):** `Garrote` → `Rupture` → `Vanish` and let the bleeds work on cloth [[d:rogue#1477046153114550273]], or run the full chain: Garrote → Rupture → `Ghostly Strike` → `Flourish` → Evasion → Sinister Strike to 5 → Kidney Shot → Sinister Strike ×2 → Rupture → Vanish out [[d:rogue#1459141070452555788]].
2. **Burst window:** reopen from Vanish with Cheap Shot while the bleeds tick. The Ambush-dagger variant (Ambush → Kidney → autos → Vanish → Ambush) is only for cloth targets [[d:rogue#1484647270224887849]].
3. **Control chain:** `Preparation` (7 min) resets Vanish and Blind (vanilla baseline: also Sprint and Evasion), giving double Vanish, double Blind and a 5-point Kidney into another 5-point Kidney [[d:rogue#1475972891941273782]], [[d:rogue#1477058992965554306]].
4. **Defence:** Vanish protects from AoE for only one GCD [[d:pvp-general#1424859626725314662]]. Sub rarely kills plate outside long 1v1s with several resets [[d:rogue#1490694521275551874]].

**Both specs:**

- **Target priority:** Assassination kills plate, Subtlety kills cloth and outplays [[d:rogue#1490694521275551874]]. In groups, open on the healer and Sap or Blind the second enemy (vanilla baseline for the healer-first rule).
- **Reset / drink:** Gouge, then bandage while they are incapacitated [[d:rogue#1374554824783298670]]. Against warlocks, kill the pet and reset with full cooldowns after eating [[d:rogue#1436600446537175050]]. DoTs are your worst enemy: there is no Cloak of Shadows, only potions that remove DoTs [[d:rogue#1437279812254175253]].

**Vs class** (full table in [Matchups](#matchups)):

- **Frost Mage:** Sprint plus a Free Action Potion, or no chance [[d:rogue#1475178793286242365]]. Pool energy for `Deadly Throw` to re-apply Crippling and interrupt the Polymorph after a Blink [[d:rogue#1452418744650567854]].
- **Shaman:** always stun them. **Contested:** some call them "easier targets" [[d:rogue#1492644310234038463]], but a 5-6k HP Elemental survives a 5-point Kidney [[d:rogue#1492650162055807126]].
- **Warlock:** kill the pet, or Gouge/Vanish through the Death Coil (with an Engineering Shadow Reflector) [[d:rogue#1436600446537175050]], [[d:rogue#1436600877543723029]].
- **Paladin:** Cheap Shot into their Freedom, Kidney into their trinket, Blind into their bubble; bleed them with Rupture and kite with Crippling [[d:rogue#1363157881326665728]].
- **Warrior, Hunter:** "easy clap" [[d:rogue#1475178793286242365]].

## Stats and caps

- **Hit:** 5% caps specials against players; 6-7% helps autos [[d:rogue#1447086577179168801]]. Autos still need up to 25% [[d:rogue#1489759475630870760]], [[d:rogue#1489759562918527161]].
- **Dampening:** player physical damage is reduced 15% and magic (Nature, so Instant/Deadly) 20% [[d:rogue#1484491208670838905]]. The 5% difference hardly matters [[d:rogue#1484496533486305280]].
- **Resilience gear** is not worth the stats you give up [[d:rogue#1458858931781767320]]. Rheinwg would keep Darkmantle over crit-reduction PvP pieces: Assassination is a glass cannon, so kill faster [[d:rogue#1465445442643951791]].
- **Weapons:** Zaszz wants the fastest 1.3-1.5 weapons for Noxious PvP, where other stats matter less [[d:rogue#1492308470358216777]], [[d:rogue#1492308862005678120]]. The new PvP rewards are poor for every rogue spec; the old R14 dagger was the hardest hitter on Ambershire [[d:rogue#1487374863722352680]], [[d:rogue#1487374982807031958]]. Nandieb rates the new Partisan sword best-in-slot for Sub in BWL, and the 2.9-speed Partisan longsword sims almost equal to CTS [[d:rogue#1487831312332095689]], [[d:rogue#1489240537562222593]]. New PvP armor sits between T3 and T3.5 [[d:rogue#1488881887815405569]]. It carries added Nature resistance, cited as one reason poison damage feels low in PvP [[d:rogue#1487392153855328306]].

## Poisons

- **Instant on both weapons**, always, in battlegrounds [[d:rogue#1492308267395715185]], [[d:rogue#1492635476308394104]].
- Swap an off hand to **Crippling** when needed. Squibble uses Crippling + Wound/Instant, or Mind-Numbing to surprise casters [[d:rogue#1484569403310735430]].
- **Avoid Deadly Poison**: it breaks your own Blind and ramps slowly [[d:rogue#1486568837498273956]].
- Off-hand swap macro, looking for any "Poniard" in your bags (change the name to your Crippling weapon): `/run for i=0,4 do for j=1,GetContainerNumSlots(i) do local l=GetContainerItemLink(i,j) if l and string.find(l,"Poniard") then PickupContainerItem(i,j) EquipCursorItem(17) break end end end` [[d:rogue#1459161884216393904]]. Fixed-slot version: `/script if ( not CursorHasItem() ) then PickupContainerItem(4,3); PickupInventoryItem(17); PickupContainerItem(4,3); end` [[d:rogue#1433690897131900979]].

## Burst and control sequences

**Assassination (Zaszz):** sneak up → **Cheap Shot** → **Envenom** → mash **Noxious Assault** until they die or you can **Kidney Shot** → mash more. Against big health pools (bear druids), Cheap Shot → Envenom → Slice and Dice [[d:rogue#1492308177818095657]], [[d:rogue#1492308239507918908]].

**Assassination, full stun lock (Not):** Cheap Shot → Envenom → Cold Blood + Noxious Assault → Thistle Tea → Noxious Assault ×2 → Kidney Shot → Noxious Assault until dead. If Tea is on cooldown, Kidney with whatever points you have before Cheap Shot ends [[d:rogue#1461753771838406667]], [[d:rogue#1461754013061222613]].

**Without Rupture (post-patch, Nandieb):** Cheap Shot → spam Noxious Assault to 5 points (Thistle Tea if needed; a Seal Fate proc gets you there within the stun) → Kidney Shot → only then Envenom + Slice and Dice [[d:rogue#1492645046049046699]].

**Subtlety (Ionnatra):** Garrote → Rupture → Ghostly Strike → Flourish → **Riposte** (disarms on a dodge/parry proc — the rogue kit has no standalone Disarm spell, only the reactive Riposte talent and the utility Disarm Trap) → Evasion → Sinister Strike to 5 → Kidney Shot → Sinister Strike ×2 → Rupture → Vanish out. Bleeds and poison keep ticking while you reopen, and Preparation lets you repeat it [[d:rogue#1459141070452555788]].

Mechanics to know:

- **Cheap Shot and Kidney Shot do not share diminishing returns** on this 1.12-based client; that changed only in Cataclysm [[d:pvp-general#1426568992855822399]].
- **Blind is no longer a poison.** Poison cleanses, Dwarf Stoneform and cleansing totems do not remove it, and it still resets with Preparation [[d:rogue#1477058992965554306]], [[d:pvp-general#1424807475865059479]], [[d:pvp-general#1430235784371245110]].
- **Vanish** protects from AoE for one GCD [[d:pvp-general#1424859626725314662]].

## Matchups

riku's ranking, hardest first [[d:rogue#1475178793286242365]]:

| Opponent | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Frost Mage** | Hardest. You need Sprint on cooldown plus a Free Action Potion, "otherwise 0% chance" |
| **Fire / Elemental Shaman** (tanky) | Bursts 2.5-3k plus a DoT; you need Tea and a healing potion on cooldown. **Contested:** Nandieb calls Shamans "one of the easier targets because they don't have defensive cooldown" [[d:rogue#1492644310234038463]]; a 5-6k HP Elemental Shaman "won't be dead after 5cp kidney" [[d:rogue#1492650162055807126]]. Dobri's routine: Cheap Shot → Noxious Assault → Gouge → Kidney Shot → Thistle Tea → Noxious ×2 → Gouge → Cold Blood + Eviscerate, or Blind and reopen [[d:rogue#1492634440856375468]] |
| **Warlock, Druid** | Hard; usually needs FAP and most cooldowns |
| **Paladin** (Holy/Prot) | Long, annoying fight; you may have to "kill" them three times through their cooldowns |
| **Melee, Hunter** | "Easy clap" |

Post-patch sentiment: on Ambershire "you win a 1vs1 with full cds against almost any class, locks and mages still own you" [[d:rogue#1489351059657719982]]. Assassination loses to any caster 1v1, while Sub keeps a chance through control [[d:rogue#1489349040733814915]]. Arena favors Assassination (fast single kills), Arathi Basin favors Sub (survivability, delaying caps) [[d:rogue#1465447254419570861]].

## Battleground role

- Rogues struggle in objective BGs because stealth is slow next to mounted players and long cooldowns don't fit short BGs [[d:rogue#1403017685104591021]].
- **Alterac Valley:** stealth-cap bunkers and towers behind enemy lines, solo (or sap/kite) graveyard guards, or pick off stray reinforcements [[d:rogue#1441309314051735603]].
- Ranking: Rank 13 → 14 is about 80k honor, and reaching the 20k honor cap took one player 20+ hours of BGs [[d:rogue#1489318842634080476]].

## Races

Matt Lender's "S tier": Troll, Orc, Undead, Goblin [[d:rogue#1467858387294486659]]. On Alliance: "Nelf for hiding, alliance for finding" [[d:rogue#1475973796681744606]]. Gnome's Escape Artist gets you back on target faster [[d:rogue#1467857162553397298]]. Undead's Will of the Forsaken is a "free get out of jail free card" [[d:rogue#1477426010013302959]].

## Common mistakes

- Running Dissolvent or Corrosive on players; they do nothing since 1.18.1 [[d:rogue#1488217700206641192]].
- Deadly Poison while planning to Blind [[d:rogue#1486568837498273956]].
- Stacking resilience instead of damage [[d:rogue#1458858931781767320]].
