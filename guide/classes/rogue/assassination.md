# Assassination Rogue (poison melee DPS)

**Recommendation:** play deep Assassination (**Noxious Assault, 41/10/0**) while leveling from 40, as a fresh 60, until you have full Darkmantle, and for solo farming and PvP. After that, switch to [Combat Envenom](combat.md). Use the **two fastest weapons** you can find; their damage barely matters. Build combo points with **Noxious Assault**, keep **1-point Envenom and Slice and Dice** up, and dump into **5-point Eviscerate**. Since 1.18.1 you no longer run Rupture or Taste for Blood. **Instant Poison** is your default. Carry a second pair of daggers with **Dissolvent** for the four creature types it still hits.

**What 1.18.1 did to it.** Noxious Assault lost AP scaling (35% → 30%), Taste for Blood no longer boosts Dissolvent or Corrosive, and those two poisons now only work on Elementals, Mechanicals, Giants and Undead [[d:rogue#1464347389597847693]], [[d:rogue#1484513053876949133]]. Nandieb counts three separate nerfs to Noxious Assault in one patch [[d:rogue#1489503815010095134]]. Players measured roughly 10% less damage [[d:rogue#1485568535215476746]]. The type restriction, not the numbers, is why people call the spec dead [[d:rogue#1486702772622725260]].

## How to play

The loop for **Noxious Assault (41/10/0)** at 60. Details: [Single-target rotation](#single-target-rotation) and [Poisons](#poisons).

1. **Before the pull:** **Instant Poison** on both of your fastest weapons. Keep a second pair coated with **Dissolvent** for Elementals, Mechanicals, Giants and Undead ([creature-type rule](#the-creature-type-rule-1181)). Poison charges last about 20 minutes of fighting [[d:rogue#1471162658550972653]].
2. **Opener:** a combo point pre-loaded on the boss and carried over with Slice and Dice, or **Noxious Assault** [[d:rogue#1480688682787667988]]. Solo, open with **Cheap Shot** from Stealth (see [leveling](leveling.md#gameplay-by-level)).
3. **Single-target loop (top first):**
    1. **Envenom** at 1 combo point if it is missing or expiring, then **Slice and Dice** at 1 combo point [[d:rogue#1484581432889442335]].
    2. **Eviscerate** at 5 combo points; **Cold Blood** on it when ready [[d:rogue#1480513960317943900]].
    3. **Noxious Assault** to build. Never Backstab or Sinister Strike [[d:rogue#1436359116774838312]].
    4. **No Rupture** [[d:rogue#1485191077827842271]].
4. **Cooldowns:** Cold Blood (a 5-point Eviscerate, or Noxious Assault into Seal Fate); Vanish for threat resets and trinket swaps; Thistle Tea when energy-starved.
5. **AoE switch:** none. Stay single-target and kill one mob at a time. **Blade Flurry** only for exactly 2 targets, and only if your sim shows a gain (**contested**) [[d:rogue#1456462381906657310]], [[d:rogue#1484686771382915153]].
6. **Resource rule:** the Ruthlessness point after each finisher pays for the 1-point refreshes [[d:rogue#1478779997878485167]]. Weapon swaps cost a GCD in combat [[d:rogue#1445469130453942475]].
7. **Situational:** if the target is **nature-immune**, swap to your Dissolvent pair. If it is **immune to all poisons** (Twin Emperors), keep pressing Noxious Assault; the hit still lands. Carry Kidney Shot and Kick for dangerous trash.

Why these points:

- **Cold Blood** turns one Noxious Assault into 2 combo points with Seal Fate, or guarantees a crit on a big Eviscerate [[d:rogue#1480513960317943900]].
- **Dual Wield Specialization is not worth it** ("spending 10 points for 4% dps") [[d:rogue#1474073699618390233]].
- **Murder 2/2** hits Humanoids, Giants, Beasts and Dragonkin, not Undead or Demons [[d:rogue#1452406989798904032]], [[d:rogue#1407084419067412570]], [[d:rogue#1450579595215638688]]. Nandieb's sim moved the 2 former Taste for Blood points into Murder [[d:rogue#1485191077827842271]].

## Stat priority

1. **Hit to 8%** (yellow cap). "The 8% mark is strictly necessary, anything short of 12-14% just has a lot more misses" [[d:rogue#1460107144035696710]]. Hit also means more auto-attacks land, and only a landed hit can apply poison [[d:rogue#1433557308897497219]].
2. **Weapon speed** (see Gear). It decides how often your poisons proc; at raid gear each 0.1 s of speed is worth roughly 6 poison DPS [[d:rogue#1445034228231438409]], [[d:rogue#1461712872068612106]].
3. **Haste**: no diminishing returns; always ahead of crit for Assassination [[d:rogue#1483699455831314525]].
4. **AP**: Noxious Assault, Eviscerate and poisons all scale with AP; poisons scale with AP only [[d:rogue#1485319521324765194]].
5. **Crit** and **Agility** after that.

Community AP-equivalents for the Noxious spec: haste 25-28, hit 22-24, crit 18-20, Strength 1.1, Agility 1.6-1.7 [[d:rogue#1479182385210855576]]. Crit is worth less than for Combat because poisons can only crit from **spell** crit [[d:rogue#1462562836973879307]], [[d:rogue#1464274332644802631]]. Intellect gives rogues no spell crit [[d:rogue#1462441882771787961]]. Dreamshard Elixir-style spell crit adds little, about 3% crit at best [[d:rogue#1487526224275312802]], [[d:rogue#1487527614045356133]]. Weapon skill matters much less than for Combat, because white damage is a small part of the total [[d:rogue#1470965936382017589]].

Damage profile (pre-patch logs): roughly half poison, 10-20% Noxious Assault, the rest autos and finishers [[d:rogue#1464981849645519055]], [[d:rogue#1458458202071892059]].

## Single-target rotation

1. **Opener:** a combo point pre-loaded on the boss (carry it over with Slice and Dice on a critter), or a Noxious Assault [[d:rogue#1480688682787667988]].
2. **Envenom** at 1 combo point, **then Slice and Dice** at 1 combo point. Envenom first is worth about 50 DPS [[d:rogue#1484581432889442335]], [[d:rogue#1484584436648644618]].
3. **Noxious Assault** to build combo points; never Backstab or Sinister Strike in this spec [[d:rogue#1436359116774838312]], [[d:rogue#1456036053197783113]].
4. **Eviscerate** at 5 combo points; **Cold Blood** on a 5-point Eviscerate, or on a Noxious Assault when a Seal Fate crit gets you to 5 faster [[d:rogue#1480513960317943900]].
5. Refresh Envenom and Slice and Dice at 1 point with the Ruthlessness point after a finisher [[d:rogue#1478779997878485167]].

- **Do not use Rupture.** With Taste for Blood gone from poisons, skipping Rupture sims about 20 DPS higher [[d:rogue#1485191077827842271]].
- **Blade Flurry:** usually a loss for Assassination, because poisons don't reach the second target and you lose energy [[d:rogue#1456462381906657310]], [[d:rogue#1482364537549230080]]. **Contested:** the Combat Gum sim shows it as a gain on some cleave fights [[d:rogue#1484686771382915153]]. Only turn it on for 2 targets, and only if your sim agrees.
- **Poison-immune target:** swap to your other poisoned pair if the target is just nature-immune. If it is immune to all poisons (Twin Emperors), keep going; the Noxious Assault hit still lands.

## Poisons

### How poisons work on Turtle

- **Procs** come from landed weapon hits, so faster weapons proc more often. Poison damage itself is fixed by rank and scales a little with AP [[d:rogue#1456782751251300572]], [[d:rogue#1462220214295859220]]. "+x% chance" talents add flat percentage points (30% → 40%) [[d:rogue#1457087792277487740]]. Your total application chance tops out around 101% with Envenom, talents, trinkets and set bonuses [[d:rogue#1462563753240432905]].
- **AP scaling** (max ranks): Instant ~5% of AP, Dissolvent 4.75%, Deadly and Corrosive about 8% over their full duration [[d:rogue#1481460731823128607]], [[d:rogue#1462220214295859220]]. 1.18.1 cut the low ranks' scaling hard (leveling and pre-AQ20 raiding) [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- **School:** Instant and Deadly are Nature, so they ignore armor but are hurt by Nature resistance and nature immunity. Dissolvent and Corrosive are Physical and also ignore armor [[d:rogue#1484500340672958655]], [[d:rogue#1484586924663509082]].
- **Crits** only come from spell crit [[d:rogue#1462562836973879307]]. **Resists** are possible; Efficient Poisons reduces them [[d:rogue#1451664361738207414]].
- **Deadly Poison** on both hands is one debuff, so pair it with Corrosive or a second rank of Deadly [[d:rogue#1460791100414365989]], [[d:rogue#1460791303813070979]]. Two rogues' Deadly IV share one debuff [[d:rogue#1486069806133284864]].
- **Charges** run out in about 20 minutes of fighting; Efficient Poisons stretches them [[d:rogue#1471162658550972653]], [[d:rogue#1471164313422008432]]. Poisons, sharpening stones and oils share a slot [[d:rogue#1491077449381838878]], [[d:rogue#1437935208790757519]].

### The creature-type rule (1.18.1)

> "Corrosive Poison and Dissolvent Poison now both only apply on the following types: Elementals, Mechanicals, Giants & Undead." [[d:rogue#1484513053876949133]]

| Target type | Instant / Deadly (Nature) | Dissolvent / Corrosive (Physical) |
|---|---|---|
| Humanoid, Beast, Dragonkin, Demon (players are Humanoid) | yes | **no** |
| Undead | yes (Undead are not nature-immune [[d:rogue#1475842408158593137]]) | yes |
| Giant, Mechanical | yes | yes |
| Elemental | usually **immune** (Nature) | yes |
| Immune to all poisons (Twin Emperors, BWL Spellbinders, some Stormwind Vault mobs) | no | no [[d:rogue#1485803638549905508]], [[d:rogue#1485998623828217956]], [[d:rogue#1486679697776578620]] |

A player called the change a bugfix, not a balance pass (Discord has no staff signal, so this is not a confirmed staff statement) [[d:rogue#1485750363192561695]]. The bug report asking to revert it was closed [[d:rogue#1486751781303353415]].

### Weapon and poison swapping

- Keep your **main pair** coated with the poison for the raid's dominant type. Keep a **cheap spare pair** (e.g. two Julie's Daggers from the bank) with the other type, and swap only when you reach an immune pack [[d:rogue#1484557710065406146]].
- Common pairings: Fang of Venoxis + Distracting Dagger with one poison, Alcor's Sunrazor + Distracting Dagger with the other (Fang is unique, Distracting Dagger is not) [[d:rogue#1484562180295622797]], [[d:rogue#1484561657366708255]]. The Ripper's own Corrosive proc is a different spell and ignores the type rule [[d:rogue#1486694475723051110]].
- Swapping weapons costs a GCD in combat [[d:rogue#1445469130453942475]].
- SuperCleveRoidMacros auto-swap by target type [[d:rogue#1485630246320341053]]:

```
/equipmh [type:Undead/Elemental/Giant/Mechanical nomhimbue:Dissolvent_Poison] YourDissolventMHDagger;
/equipoh [type:Undead/Elemental/Giant/Mechanical noohimbue:Dissolvent_Poison] YourDissolventOHDagger;
/equipmh [notype:Undead/Elemental/Giant/Mechanical mhimbue:Dissolvent_Poison] YourInstantMHDagger;
/equipoh [notype:Undead/Elemental/Giant/Mechanical ohimbue:Dissolvent_Poison] YourInstantOHDagger;
```

- ItemRack cannot tell two identical items apart, so don't build both sets from the same dagger [[d:rogue#1485651207103184927]].
- If swapping is too much hassle on a trash-heavy night, some players run no poisons in MC at all [[d:rogue#1486680147846365315]], or stay on Instant through a single immune pack and only switch for a whole immune section [[d:rogue#1484592805975298129]].

## Gear

- **Weapons:** the fastest daggers you can get, because Noxious Assault and poisons ignore weapon damage [[d:rogue#1461709639614730411]]. BiS pairs: **Fang of Venoxis + Distracting Dagger** and **Alcor's Sunrazor + Distracting Dagger** [[d:rogue#1484562180295622797]]. Distracting Dagger's +6 dagger skill makes it the off hand to keep; Alcor's has fallen off post-patch [[d:rogue#1490336125615607939]], [[d:rogue#1490336440494850151]]. Rhaena's post-patch tests ranked Dragonfang > Fang of Venoxis > Alcor's > Perdition's [[d:rogue#1485961375586058282]]. The nerf made slightly slower, higher-DPS daggers "slightly more viable" [[d:rogue#1464584363172696221]]. Off-hand speed matters twice as much as main-hand speed (an off-hand weapon needs ~14 extra DPS to offset 0.1 s of speed) [[d:rogue#1464609074015502528]].
- **Budget:** 1.3-speed Julie's Dagger, Finkle's Skinner, 2× Stormfist, or even white 1.3-speed Poniards from a weapon vendor [[d:rogue#1407366386392502353]], [[d:rogue#1409846930632212511]], [[d:rogue#1458065130758603016]], [[d:rogue#1448746889607512075]]. Gear the rest before chasing weapon damage [[d:rogue#1458066367306534925]].
- **Armor, pre-BWL:** 6-piece Darkmantle + Aged Core Leather Gloves + Clutch of Hivaxxis [[d:rogue#1479955248301736159]]. 4 Darkmantle + 4 Stormshroud shows more on a dummy but scales worse with raid buffs [[d:rogue#1484191393902628886]].
- **BWL:** 4 Darkmantle + 3 T2 (the T2 3-piece adds 5% poison chance) [[d:rogue#1473192838022955171]]; T2 priority is bracers, boots, then chest [[d:rogue#1477712146497404941]].
- **Trinkets:** Hand of Justice, Whip of Encouragement, Royal Seal and Vial of Potent Venoms all sim within ~10 DPS; HoJ + Whip is best [[d:rogue#1484274327829610607]], [[d:rogue#1484275198181113907]]. Extra-swing procs are worth more for Assassination because every swing can proc poison [[d:rogue#1473370328582590707]].
- **Solo farming:** a vampirism set (2× Blood-etched Fetish, Twilight Opal Cascade, vampirism enchants) plus a Riposte spec lets you solo Damian (the Ripper) and Hateforge Quarry bosses [[d:rogue#1427306511188889641]], [[d:rogue#1442034294725017610]], [[d:rogue#1491062888503181413]], [[d:rogue#1491063342805286984]]. See [Hateforge Quarry](../../instances/hateforge-quarry.md).

## Common mistakes

- Running Rupture + Taste for Blood in 1.18.1 [[d:rogue#1486815192690987191]].
- Double Dissolvent everywhere, the pre-1.18.1 habit. It now does nothing to Humanoids, Beasts, Dragonkin and Demons [[d:rogue#1484513053876949133]].
- Using slow "upgrades" such as Gut Ripper; speed beats weapon damage [[d:rogue#1472127122071093311]].
- Using Backstab or Sinister Strike instead of Noxious Assault [[d:rogue#1436359116774838312]].
- Dropping Efficient Poisons [[d:rogue#1488765913862770802]].
- Staying Assassination into AQ40 and Naxx with Darkmantle done. Sim Combat Envenom [[d:rogue#1486733365955788911]].
