# Subtlety Rogue (raid support)

**Recommendation:** bring **one** Subtlety rogue per raid. Its job, in this order: keep a **5-point Improved Expose Armor** on the boss 100% of the time, spam **Hemorrhage** (a raid-wide physical-damage debuff), keep **Tricks of the Trade** rolling on your party with 5-point finishers, and fire **Mark for Death** into a **Shadow of Death** in burst windows (twice per fight with Preparation). Play the **17/0/34** build with a slow, hard-hitting **non-dagger** main hand, and stack Attack Power. Put the Sub rogue in a melee group, ideally with two Shamans.

## What Subtlety brings

Yoridzen's summary: "−2600 armor on boss, +2% phys damage on boss, +4% crit for your group, 16 sec of burst which gives ur group 30% of your ap (or sp for casters and palas)" [[d:rogue#1491442185474801804]].

| Tool | Scope | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Improved Expose Armor** (5 points) | raid (target debuff) | More armor reduction than Sunder; about +5% physical damage for the raid on most bosses [[d:rogue#1492305705246658731]]. Shares the Sunder Armor slot and overwrites it; only one rogue should run it [[d:rogue#1488431348992708709]], [[d:rogue#1491188298201305208]] |
| **Hemorrhage** | raid (target debuff) | Bonus physical damage taken; "literally 4% more damage with good up time" [[d:rogue#1450614096587391179]]. One Sub gets about 54% uptime in real parses [[d:rogue#1450615129086361733]] |
| **Tricks of the Trade** | party | Crit buff from your finishers; 12 s, so aim for a 5-point finisher about every 11 s to keep 2 stacks up [[d:rogue#1483262959709982761]] |
| **Mark for Death** | party | 30% of your AP as AP (and 20% as spell power) for 16 s [[d:rogue#1490442703958446232]], [[d:rogue#1491442185474801804]]; also gives combo points (2 + the Ruthlessness point) [[d:rogue#1490037124706205746]] |
| **Shadow of Death** | you | Finisher that stores damage taken by the target and detonates for up to 250% AP at 5 points; Relentless Strikes applies to it [[d:rogue#1459148633910935572]], [[d:rogue#1458871687805403257]]. It usually detonates "within seconds" [[d:rogue#1448070432795263117]] |
| **Blackjack** (Sap/Blind on the boss) | raid | 10% damage reduction on the boss; the most impactful Sub utility per Rheinwg [[d:rogue#1491937019717484675]], [[d:rogue#1490792019969315028]] |

**How many Subs?** "None are needed, 1 is good, 2 is nice to have": one rogue gets 30-40% Hemorrhage uptime, two get 70-80% [[d:rogue#1485592388910190752]], [[d:rogue#1485593209496277194]]. Only one rogue needs Improved Expose Armor. If two Subs are in the raid, one drops IEA and plays for damage [[d:rogue#1490031709717270529]], [[d:rogue#1490446340730388530]]. A dev put it this way: "sub rogue is incredible support, just the gain from having more than 1 is pretty negligible" [[d:rogue#1331689119759208491]].

**Improved Expose Armor vs Sunder (situational value).** One tester measured IEA as the only armor debuff at about +150 raid DPS over no debuff, but only +35 over a full 5× Sunder stack [[d:rogue#1488705124728700938]]. Its real value depends on whether your warriors sunder. Let warriors Sunder on trash and save IEA for bosses [[d:rogue#1464610137049268285]].

## Stat priority

1. **Hit to 8%** from gear [[d:rogue#1467577072863023172]].
2. **Attack Power** above almost everything. Mark for Death, Shadow of Death and Hemorrhage all scale with it; a steady AP bonus beats a proc [[d:rogue#1475335506890985573]], [[d:rogue#1477815128488411289]].
3. **Energy sources** so you can hold the whole rotation (Darkmantle, Stormshroud or AQ20 set bonuses) [[d:rogue#1475335506890985573]], [[d:rogue#1490807562814361662]].
4. Crit gains value with the T3 set at Naxx [[d:rogue#1465445442643951791]].

## Rotation

### Opener

1. **Garrote** (2 cheap combo points; it always procs Tricks of the Trade). Many raiding Subs forget it [[d:rogue#1488615334016716881]], [[d:rogue#1467572957231448354]]. Use Hemorrhage instead if the boss is bleed-immune [[d:rogue#1492117624568680489]].
2. **Hemorrhage** to 5 points → **Improved Expose Armor** as early as possible [[d:rogue#1490034918212894740]].
3. **Slice and Dice** at 1 point; then Hemorrhage to 5 → **Rupture** for Taste for Blood [[d:rogue#1490034918212894740]], [[d:rogue#1492117624568680489]].
4. Burst: Hemorrhage to 2 points, **Mark for Death** (adds 2 + the Ruthlessness point, tops you up to 5), then **Shadow of Death**, with AP trinkets and potions. Call it out so your group bursts with you [[d:rogue#1490037124706205746]], [[d:rogue#1483262959709982761]].

### Sustain priority

1. **Improved Expose Armor** at 5 points if it has under ~10 s left; refresh it before any other finisher [[d:rogue#1483262959709982761]].
2. A **5-point finisher about every 11 s** to keep Tricks of the Trade up; this matters more than perfect Slice and Dice uptime or Shadow of Death on cooldown [[d:rogue#1483262959709982761]]. Spamming 1-point Eviscerates just to trigger Tricks of the Trade is a mistake [[d:rogue#1488615334016716881]].
3. **Slice and Dice** (low combo points) and **Rupture** (5 points, for Taste for Blood). With 5 points, Rupture first, then Slice and Dice with fewer points [[d:rogue#1472204410842251285]].
4. **Mark for Death → Shadow of Death** on cooldown or in the raid's burst window. After the first one, **Preparation** (7-minute cooldown) resets Mark for a second, e.g. in the execute phase [[d:rogue#1468425398747402240]], [[d:rogue#1469728842221359226]].
5. **Eviscerate** with spare combo points.
6. **Hemorrhage** as the builder. You want to see only Hemorrhage and Ghostly Strike, not Backstab or Sinister Strike [[d:rogue#1468425398747402240]].

Yoridzen's full sequence: Garrote → Hemorrhage ×3 → IEA → Slice and Dice → Hemorrhage ×4 → Rupture → Hemorrhage ×2 → burst (Quickness potion, trinkets) → Mark → Shadow of Death → Preparation → Slice and Dice → Hemorrhage ×2 → Mark → Shadow of Death → Vanish + trinket swap → Garrote → keep IEA, Slice and Dice and Rupture up [[d:rogue#1492117624568680489]].

### Advanced

- Pre-load a combo point on a critter and carry it to the boss with Slice and Dice [[d:rogue#1448107099405156353]].
- Sap the boss before Garrote: it is immune, but Blackjack still applies 10% damage reduction for 8 s. Distract gives −6% hit on the boss [[d:rogue#1483262959709982761]].
- Short Naxx sequences (Noth → Heigan → Loatheb, Patchwerk → Grobbulus → Gluth → Thaddius) leave too little downtime for Preparation, so expect one Mark on some bosses [[d:rogue#1467483628576444477]], [[d:rogue#1468425398747402240]].
- On trash, pop Mark + Shadow of Death on a big pack at the start of the raid, not right before a boss [[d:rogue#1467574568280522968]]. Don't IEA trash or in dungeons [[d:rogue#1459199748748607488]].
- Blade Flurry is generally not for Sub: it costs energy and combo points you need for Tricks of the Trade and Shadow of Death [[d:rogue#1456462707405361326]], [[d:rogue#1479550395453018322]].
- Judge yourself on logs, not DPS: cooldowns used, IEA uptime, Tricks of the Trade uptime, Taste for Blood and Slice and Dice uptime [[d:rogue#1467573603225571431]].

## Group placement

Put the Sub in a melee group; Mark for Death and Tricks of the Trade only reach your party. Suggested groups: two Shamans + Feral + Warrior (a T3.5 Paladin instead of the Feral if crit-capped) [[d:rogue#1488195621587386418]]; when a Hunter + Shaman + 2 Warriors group was proposed, Rheinwg preferred swapping the second Warrior for a second Shaman instead [[d:rogue#1488193479887814738]], [[d:rogue#1488193508723396689]]. A caster group also works, since Mark for Death gives spell power [[d:rogue#1465304392914698356]].

## Gear

- **Main hand: slow, high-damage, not a dagger** ("Sub doesn't play with daggers") [[d:rogue#1482354344031424624]], [[d:rogue#1480381217907019908]]. BWL: the new **Partisan PvP sword** is BiS [[d:rogue#1487831312332095689]], [[d:rogue#1491215526339547367]]; Gressil, then Castigator, are the next best [[d:rogue#1481339424364167373]], [[d:rogue#1481339496749601014]]. Deathbringer suffers from having no fitting +skill off hand [[d:rogue#1487831861160968425]]. Later: Ancient Qiraji Ripper > C'Thun's fist > Castigator > Gressil > Thunderfall [[d:rogue#1465711172220092536]].
- **Off hand:** fast, ideally with weapon skill [[d:rogue#1485463733110182069]], [[d:rogue#1489308882319839393]]. Leave **the Ripper** to an Assassination or Combat Envenom rogue; on Sub it costs you too much damage [[d:rogue#1489408343129850029]].
- **Armor:** Darkmantle for energy early; the AQ20 set is very strong, "he should be using that forever pretty much" [[d:rogue#1488615334016716881]], [[d:rogue#1480381468038660308]]. At Kara40 level, T3 + T3.5 + Veiled Shadows replace Darkmantle's energy [[d:rogue#1490816262866210877]], [[d:rogue#1465023837316972849]]. The Deathdealer 5-piece is not worth soft reserves [[d:rogue#1492602081276723340]]. T2 priority: bracers > belt > chest > boots > legs > shoulders > head > gloves [[d:rogue#1491919846357598268]].
- **Trinkets:** Slayer's Crest over Kiss of the Spider (better with Mark + Shadow of Death) [[d:rogue#1478414333460349001]].
- **Enchants:** Crusader on the main hand; Crusader or Agility on the off hand [[d:rogue#1459216610001682545]], [[d:rogue#1459221182820778015]]. Some run double Crusader and pop Mark for Death on a Crusader proc [[d:rogue#1465622166660714598]]. AP-leaning armor enchants; ZG Agility on head and legs; Zandalar haste, later Sapphiron, on shoulders [[d:rogue#1481776341656277112]], [[d:rogue#1481778011035664454]].
- **Races:** Orc and Goblin for min-maxing [[d:rogue#1494186065173680168]].

## Poisons

Sub runs DoT poisons on bosses: **Deadly V + Deadly IV** on normal bosses, **Deadly MH + Corrosive OH** on Undead/Giant/Mechanical bosses, Instant or Dissolvent on trash [[d:rogue#1491214094685044858]], [[d:rogue#1487771304861106207]]. In one BWL sim, Deadly IV on the main hand beat double Instant for Sub [[d:rogue#1487859474713542868]]. Use double **Mind-Numbing** on Medivh [[d:rogue#1483262942748086434]]. Full rules: [Assassination → Poisons](assassination.md#poisons).

## Common mistakes

- Not running IEA as the raid's only Sub ("pointless") [[d:rogue#1490446340730388530]], or running it alongside another rogue's IEA [[d:rogue#1490031709717270529]].
- Using a dagger, Backstab or Sinister Strike [[d:rogue#1468425398747402240]].
- Skipping Ruthlessness, Improved Blade Tactics or Relentless Strikes [[d:rogue#1467576340415775040]].
- Forgetting Garrote at the start [[d:rogue#1488615334016716881]].
- Running Annihilator in a raid that has a Shart'ateth Axe; they share a debuff slot [[d:rogue#1483262942748086434]].
