# Rogue tank (niche)

**Recommendation:** treat rogue tanking as a **dungeon and off-tank** tool, not a main-tank role. An avoidance rogue can reach 95-97% avoidance and hold many bosses up to Naxx. It has no taunt, weak AoE threat, and its only threat tool (**Agitating Poison**) is Nature damage, so it fails on nature-immune bosses. Play the **33/18/0 poison-avoidance** build. Stack Agility, dodge, parry, Defense and Stamina. Chain Ghostly Strike → Flourish → Evasion to stay unhittable.

## How to play

The loop for the **33/18/0 poison-avoidance tank**. It is a dungeon and off-tank role; you have **no taunt**. Details: [Rotation (avoidance chain)](#rotation-avoidance-chain), [Where it works](#where-it-works).

1. **Pull:** coat your weapons with **Agitating Poison**, or Corrosive on valid targets [[d:rogue#1462134848415338742]]. Pull before the DPS start, because you cannot taunt a mob back.
2. **First GCDs:** **Ghostly Strike → 1-point Flourish**, then the normal threat rotation [[d:rogue#1489567787620896880]]. On a pack, turn **Blade Flurry** on with Ghostly Strike [[d:rogue#1314468356580966400]].
3. **Steady threat loop:** **Noxious Assault** spam with Agitating Poison, and **Envenom** at 1 point for poison threat [[d:rogue#1346462072153702481]]. **Riposte** whenever it is up.
4. **Avoidance chain (keep it rolling):** Ghostly Strike → Flourish → Ghostly Strike → **Evasion** → Ghostly Strike → Flourish → Ghostly Strike [[d:rogue#1471063134339858453]]. Juju Escape extends it [[d:rogue#1464675980151886085]].
5. **Multiple mobs:** there is no AoE threat tool. Cleave with Blade Flurry and swap targets to spread threat [[d:rogue#1479145606654988469]]. On 4-mob packs, **Sap** one and **Blind** one [[d:rogue#1471053347518681149]].
6. **Threat recovery and swaps:** you can't taunt. Hand a boss over by lowering your own threat with Feint or Vanish [[d:rogue#1473169875227639943]]. In a group with a Subtlety rogue tank, Mark for Death → Shadow of Death is a threat burst [[d:rogue#1494762649739853886]].
7. **Defensive rules:** never let Flourish and Ghostly Strike run out at the same time; that is when you get crit [[d:rogue#1471063504709484586]]. Save Evasion for that gap. Stamina covers the spell damage you cannot dodge.
8. **Avoid:** nature-immune bosses (Agitating Poison does nothing to them), taunt-swap fights, heavy spellcasters and big trash pulls [[d:rogue#1473170462539251824]], [[d:rogue#1471059228574879856]].

## Stats and gear

- **Priorities:** Stamina (to survive spells), dodge and parry over crit; Agility and Defense enchants on cloak, legs and head; Defense on neck and trinkets [[d:rogue#1471055767372169417]], [[d:rogue#1471071034932203551]].
- **Conversions:** about 9 Defense or about 14.5 Agility = 1% avoidance [[d:rogue#1435379674661523557]].
- **Target:** 86.8% passive avoidance against bosses, as shown in BetterCharacterStats' "Defenses vs Bosses" tab, fully buffed [[d:rogue#1494743574284075150]]. Kara40-level gear reaches about 95-97% [[d:rogue#1462122391093121096]], [[d:rogue#1464675856575103028]]. Expect about 7200 HP in a K40-level tank set with consumables [[d:rogue#1472949118073704502]].
- **Pieces:** Kings Edict, an Agility throwing weapon, a hit + haste bracer, T3.5 Agility pieces [[d:rogue#1472988390306615460]]; Hand Wraps of Deadwind and Mephistroth's Cunning [[d:rogue#1472988662386921690]]; T2.5 for its Agility [[d:rogue#1472949809081090141]]. Upgrades in order: Windwalker or TMH boots over Bloodfang boots [[d:rogue#1494773008131162372]], Deadwind Wraps [[d:rogue#1494773220858003467]], Dagger of Veiled Shadows [[d:rogue#1494773639109939352]], Fist of the Flamewalker as pre-TMH off hand [[d:rogue#1494773824804360403]].
- **Weapon skill:** an Assassination tank reaches 315 with Thunderfury + Anasterian's Legacy + AQ40 hands [[d:rogue#1472990947301265651]]. **Contested:** Scruff would rather sit at 308 skill ("I just rock the 308 dagger skill and call it a day in tank spec") than give up Agility for weapon-skill gear [[d:rogue#1472989078529245314]].

## Rotation (avoidance chain)

1. **Threat:** Noxious Assault spam with Agitating Poison (or Corrosive on valid targets); Envenom for poison threat [[d:rogue#1462134848415338742]], [[d:rogue#1346462072153702481]].
2. **Avoidance chain:** Ghostly Strike → Flourish → Ghostly Strike → Evasion → Ghostly Strike → Flourish → Ghostly Strike. Flourish is then on cooldown for about 10 s and you can be hit (and crit) [[d:rogue#1471063134339858453]], [[d:rogue#1471063504709484586]]. Weaving Juju Escape lets a K40-geared tank "dodge forever" [[d:rogue#1464675980151886085]].
3. **Riposte** whenever it is up.
4. **Multiple mobs:** there is no AoE threat tool. Cleave with Blade Flurry and swap targets to spread threat [[d:rogue#1479145606654988469]]. Sap one and Blind one on 4-mob packs [[d:rogue#1471053347518681149]].
5. **Tank swaps:** you can't taunt, but you can lower your own threat (Feint, Vanish) so the other tank takes over [[d:rogue#1473169875227639943]].

Mark for Death also helps a tank rogue: about 20% of your AP as party AP and 18% as spell power [[d:rogue#1494762339407495340]], and Shadow of Death turns the party's damage into a burst of threat for the tank [[d:rogue#1494762649739853886]], [[d:rogue#1494762725207834784]].

## Where it works

- **Dungeons:** the main use [[d:rogue#1471626258881450147]]. Raid-geared rogue tanks solo many dungeon bosses (BRD torches, golem and Emperor room, the LBRS last boss) [[d:rogue#1471053877829828724]], [[d:rogue#1494756893061812255]].
- **Raids:** backup-tanked Four Horsemen and BWL dragonkin after the main tank died [[d:rogue#1471665604158296095]]. Instructor Razuvious without mind control [[d:rogue#1471680197454467082]]. Off-tank through Naxx [[d:rogue#1460292961165115455]].
- **Not:** nature-immune bosses (no Agitating Poison threat), taunt-swap fights, heavy spellcasters, large trash pulls [[d:rogue#1473170462539251824]], [[d:rogue#1471059228574879856]].

## Solo farming setup (tank-style)

pxstxll's solo farming kit (a pull of 2 mechanical and 3 humanoid enemies): 16 points in Combat (dodge, parry, hit, Riposte), the rest in Assassination up to Noxious Assault without Taste for Blood. Gear: 6-piece Darkmantle, Aged Core Leather Gloves, Clutch of Hivaxxis, 2× Twilight Opal Cascade, vampirism enchants on rings and neck. Weapons: Ripper/Distracting Dagger with Instant, and Fang of Venoxis/a second Distracting Dagger with Dissolvent in the bags [[d:rogue#1491063342805286984]], [[d:rogue#1491062888503181413]]. Manage Evasion, Flourish and Ghostly Strike on cooldown, and keep health pots and a Stamina Zanza flask on hand [[d:rogue#1491064648630534296]].

## Common mistakes

- Signing up as the main tank for taunt-swap or nature-immune bosses.
- Wearing a DPS set: "you are geared as DPS and your spec is DPS" [[d:rogue#1471055767372169417]].
- Letting Flourish and Ghostly Strike drop at the same time; that is when you get crit [[d:rogue#1471063504709484586]].
