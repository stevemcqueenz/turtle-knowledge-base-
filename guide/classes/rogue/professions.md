# Rogue professions

**Recommendation:** on your first character, level with **Skinning + Leatherworking**: it keeps your leather gear up to date and does not slow you down [[d:rogue#1434325242565623940]], [[d:rogue#1481737585167569086]]. At 60, drop Skinning for **Engineering**. Engineering is the only profession that adds to your damage, mainly through **Goblin Sapper Charges** and the **Gnomish Battle Chicken** [[d:rogue#1459623698632802468]], [[d:rogue#1463477543666581601]]. For gold, take **Mining** (naked ZG mining is the standard rogue farm) plus Enchanting's **Disenchant** [[d:rogue#1489623080711094384]], [[d:rogue#1440513896049016936]]. **Herbalism + Alchemy** works at every stage [[d:rogue#1470528671566663711]]. Take **Lockpicking** and **Cooking** as your secondary skills [[d:rogue#1485717534362570752]].

Professions matter less for a rogue than for many classes. The whole Engineering package is called "very overrated" [[d:rogue#1486342417589342240]], and Alchemy does nothing for poisons, which are their own rogue skill [[d:rogue#1487509381837754471]].

## What each profession gives a rogue

### Engineering

- **Why:** "the only prof that will provide a bonus to your stats" [[d:rogue#1459623698632802468]]. It's "Chicken and Sappers" [[d:rogue#1463477543666581601]]. **Goblin Sapper Charges** are what make it worth taking [[d:rogue#1424840738658717696]], [[d:rogue#1436971401914613790]]. Sappers only matter on packs, if you care about trash meters (see [Combat → AoE](combat.md)).
- **Gnomish Battle Chicken:** "outside of explosives [it] is the only real DPS increase" [[d:rogue#1470780105117667512]]. On Turtle it runs on a fixed trinket timer, about once every 30 minutes, and recent changes removed the old trick of forcing it to proc [[d:rogue#1465511775918358790]], [[d:rogue#1465512376425255006]]. **(single source)**
- **Utility:** Orgrimmar wormhole ("bis"), XL jumper cables, parachute, on-demand stuns, extra invisibility, cheap underwater breathing [[d:rogue#1436973147667697807]], [[d:rogue#1436982001352573061]]. The Death Ray, net and mind-control cap are fun PvP toys but not must-haves [[d:rogue#1470780105117667512]].
- **Contested value.** One veteran says it is "useful for exactly 1 raid" and that bombs are only required for Viscidus unless you speedrun; he still calls it technically BiS "because any improvement is improvement" [[d:rogue#1465493047415537766]], [[d:rogue#1465547933981347882]], [[d:rogue#1465548811223957605]]. Others say most DPS want it [[d:rogue#1465506882163835030]]. "Throw 3 sappers during raid is good use of 300 engineering?" [[d:rogue#1486342507959812339]]
- **Gnomish or Goblin:** level **Gnomish** first and craft the Battle Chicken, then switch to **Goblin** for sappers. The chicken keeps working after the switch [[d:rogue#1453273131988029490]], [[d:rogue#1470772572021850205]]. Before switching, check your items: any item that "requires Goblin/Gnomish Engineering" stops working on the other specialisation [[d:rogue#1443444925214494801]].
- **Buy instead:** Goblin-only items can be bought from other players, but making your own sappers saves money over time [[d:rogue#1470780105117667512]], [[d:rogue#1470775185392271361]]. You need Engineering 250 to *use* Dense Dynamite and Goblin Sapper Charges [[d:rogue#1481656932015145232]].

### Leatherworking (+ Skinning)

- **Dreamhide Leggings** (3% haste, BoP) and the matching belt (1% haste) are LW-only haste pieces [[d:rogue#1447098170642268170]]. The legs appear in current Combat/Assassination BiS lists (T0.5 4p + T2 3p + Dreamhide legs) and beat Bloodmask, but are "such a pain to get" [[d:rogue#1481976698571591701]], [[d:rogue#1483012882466865283]]. For tanking, T2.5 legs beat them because they carry far more Stamina [[d:rogue#1467045795588214855]].
- **Naxx:** Naxx gear needs a lot of **Cured Rugged Hide** (~20 g each). Leatherworking also makes frost-resistance gear for Naxx progression [[d:rogue#1481656932015145232]]. Cured Rugged Hide sells for a guaranteed 19-20 g every 72 hours [[d:rogue#1456737096407453808]].
- It also makes fire-resistance gear and a +spell power leather kit [[d:rogue#1459620504100143276]], plus armor/stamina kits [[d:rogue#1484232365198016612]].
- **Elemental vs Tribal:** Elemental is better for your own gear. Tribal is steadier income (a pre-BiS healing cloak and other druid gear) [[d:rogue#1433705211502788619]]. **(single source)**
- Some BoP recipes let you charge crafting fees of about 100 g each [[d:rogue#1440517107346444418]].

### Enchanting (Disenchant)

- You can disenchant anything at Enchanting skill 1 [[d:rogue#1435154921799548978]]. A 50-silver vendor item becomes 3 g or more in materials [[d:rogue#1429832007135002767]].
- "DE'ing is the most simple reliable way for rogue to make money": you do it on the side while soloing, and level 50-60 greens sometimes give 3 Greater Eternal Essences [[d:rogue#1440513896049016936]]. Farming your T0.5 set with Enchanting leaves you "swimming in money" [[d:rogue#1435148293914103828]].
- Pairs well with Engineering at 60: "Engi for dps, DE to get those mats from unwanted loot" [[d:rogue#1466978864151662757]].
- Most rogue enchants come from Enchanting: everything except the blacksmith belt buckle, the jewelcrafting ring/neck enchants and the quest head/leg/shoulder enchants [[d:rogue#1484232312236544171]].

### Jewelcrafting (Turtle custom)

- **Ring and neck +Agility enchants** are jewelcrafter gem enchants [[d:rogue#1490264774276026378]], [[d:rogue#1484232312236544171]]. A Turtle staff member says enchanted gemstones "are like enchants", so you do not need the Goldsmith specialisation to benefit ([Akalix (staff), 2025-06-19](https://discord.com/channels/466622455805378571/976193120809123881/1385337387206578301)). Buy them from a jeweler.
- **Vampirism / solo kit:** the JC **Twilight Opal Cascade** has 12 Agility instead of 12 Stamina [[d:rogue#1444828435540213761]]. The JC 3% vampirism trinket is "nice, but quite expensive" [[d:rogue#1427306703354859633]], [[d:rogue#1427306844510097619]]. The crafted Dazzling Aquamarine ring with a vampirism enchant is part of the cheap solo set [[d:rogue#1427306511188889641]]. See [Assassination → Solo farming](assassination.md) and [Tank](tank.md).
- **Value:** "decent trinkets necks … but nothing that screams that you should grab it" [[d:rogue#1329039988746092628]]. "There isn't really any choices for rogue atm" in JC [[d:rogue#1459590970646597876]]. Pickpocketed gems feed it [[d:rogue#1456709385601683753]].
- **Gold:** gritted-paper margins are "an easy 1k gold a week" [[d:rogue#1440516692126994586]]. **(single source)**

### Blacksmithing

- **Contested and niche.** The blacksmith belt buckle is the belt enchant [[d:rogue#1484232312236544171]]. The Blacksmithing anti-fear trinket is the one reason a player kept the profession over LW [[d:rogue#1447106369147768947]]. A Sword rogue aims for the Swordsmith runed epic blade [[d:rogue#1484550833512382627]].
- **1.18.1 crafted-weapon upgrades:** BWL drops upgrade items for crafted gear, and you need the matching specialisation to use the result ([Dragunovi (staff), 2026-02-15](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=168272#p168272)). One player speculates that on a fresh server Blacksmithing "might become bis profession for this phase" [[d:rogue#1485567993671847989]] **(single source, speculative)**.
- **Ornate Bloodstone Dagger (OBD, crafted):** the recipe is rare, and crafting fees ran 200-700 g [[d:rogue#1459386611132530748]]. In 1.18.1 **rogue poisons and melee can no longer proc it**. Before the patch it was eating up 8-10% of a rogue's damage [[d:rogue#1479961666312470711]], [[d:rogue#1497981748527628390]], [[d:rogue#1497982046125953146]]. Some still use it as a 1.3-speed dagger [[d:rogue#1486378986090139869]]. Don't spend on one for its proc.

### Herbalism + Alchemy

- Makes "hella money and save[s] you equally as much" through self-made potions [[d:rogue#1424901611431657575]]. Crafting your own flasks is usually cheaper than buying them [[d:rogue#1459623922533404880]].
- It has no poison synergy [[d:rogue#1487509381837754471]]. One raider says Alchemy is "nothing ground breaking since you can buy all those pots" [[d:rogue#1447107205873537227]]. The potion market reportedly crashed because weekly quests flood it with supply [[d:rogue#1465020823076077713]]. Raw herb prices are held down by lasher farmers [[d:rogue#1476934750731112661]].

### Survival and Gardening (secondary professions)

- **Before 1.18.1:** Survival (cap 150) mostly meant **tents** for rested XP. Rogue levelers advised resting under a tent "every 1,5 levels" [[d:rogue#1427947901384130570]]. Gardening required Survival 75 ([official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/survival-and-gardening)).
- **1.18.1 rework:** Survival now draws on the other professions and has 80+ recipes. It adds tools that slow, root or trap opponents, and gathering bags ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). Tents now only reach 25% rested; non-city taverns reach 100% (same source). Survival crafts include a **Simple Slingshot** usable for ranged pulls ([Atreidon (player), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172086#p172086)), Slowing Bolas and Throwable Nets ([Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2026-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172915#p172915)), and parachutes and gathering trinkets ([Elesion (player), 2026-03-29](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=173031#p173031)).
- **Rogue-specific:** Survival has "an early dagger you can craft with +1 dagger skill", worth making while leveling [[d:rogue#1485549149192589322]] **(single source)**. One gold-focused leveler ran Survival as support alongside Herbalism/Alchemy, Fishing and Cooking [[d:rogue#1483608549274947668]]. The channel says nothing about bolas or nets in rogue PvP.
- **Gardening:** **Sour Mountain Berries** (+10 Agility food, tradeable) are grown through Gardening and are a rogue raid food [[d:rogue#1471992248861327554]], [[d:rogue#1429922723299332220]]. Gardening "can be hella lucrative" if you market it [[d:rogue#1440516841901654158]]. One player has not confirmed the berries still work after 1.18.1 [[d:rogue#1490538508249731252]].

### Lockpicking, Pickpocket, Fishing, Cooking

- **Lockpicking** is needed for class quest chains, and it "will make you money as long as you use it" [[d:rogue#1458678446006861885]]. Fastest route: Hillsbrad → Badlands → Tanaris → Eastern Plaguelands/BRD [[d:rogue#1465593498286817362]]. Horde can spam the poison-quest box to about 170 [[d:rogue#1465532927189188710]].
- **Pickpocket: stand still.** Pickpocketing while moving can play the loot sound and chat message without putting anything in your bags [[d:rogue#1476276159917588511]], [[d:rogue#1477651156959367251]], [[d:rogue#1328021378854813750]].
- **Autoloot:** if two addons (for example SuperWoW and another) both have autoloot on, neither loots. Turn it off in one of them [[d:rogue#1476277178340610331]].
- **Pocket resets (contested):** one rogue says dungeon mobs only get new pockets when they die or the instance resets [[d:rogue#1442021488449556571]]; another saw BRD pockets refill after 15-20 minutes AFK [[d:rogue#1442109241069408328]].
- **Drop odds:** about 7-8% of pickpocketed mobs give a Heavy Junkbox, and BoE epics such as Alcor's Sunrazor come from about 0.125% of boxes, so about 800 boxes per epic on average [[d:rogue#1476992018839502951]], [[d:rogue#1476992441956565155]]. Every Heavy Junkbox now also holds a Precise Lock Cylinder (see [Ravenholdt](#ravenholdt-reputation)).
- **Fishing:** Stonescale Eels earned one player 50-70 g/h [[d:rogue#1440517107346444418]]. Fish Oily Blackmouth pools as you pass for Free Action Potions [[d:rogue#1470540414133076091]].

## When to pick up and swap

1. **1-60:** Skinning + Leatherworking, leveled alongside your character [[d:rogue#1470528671566663711]], [[d:rogue#1487516373512093821]]. **Contested:** one player says professions slow leveling and gold is easier to farm at 60 [[d:rogue#1481674325772992537]]. Others got an epic mount two days after hitting 60 through professions, at 30-60 g/h [[d:rogue#1481680568793043105]], [[d:rogue#1481774944802050211]].
2. **50-60:** consider Enchanting for Disenchant [[d:rogue#1481662356282019840]].
3. **At LW 300:** drop Skinning for Engineering. Buy stones on the AH and level to 250 for Dense Dynamite and Sappers [[d:rogue#1481656932015145232]]. Do Gnomish first, then Goblin [[d:rogue#1453273131988029490]].
4. Dropping a crafting profession loses its recipes, so swapping between JC, BS and others means relearning everything [[d:rogue#1481769229115064460]].
5. Alternative: level crafting professions on level-35 alts [[d:rogue#1492885567958941746]], or feed an alt's Leatherworking/Enchanting from your main's gathering [[d:rogue#1494712430889275644]].

## Race interactions

- **Goblin:** the Prospecting racial grants Mining *and* Jewelcrafting skill ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102965#p102965)). The rogue channel names no other race-profession interaction. For racials, see [Races](index.md#races).

## Gold

- **Best:** naked Mining in ZG ("best gold farm for rogues and easiest to do") [[d:rogue#1489623080711094384]], [[d:rogue#1494691538389958868]]; Disenchanting on the side [[d:rogue#1440513896049016936]].
- Skinning is called "probably the worst" gathering profession for gold [[d:rogue#1494692871163478087]], though Rugged Hide now sells for a gold or two each [[d:rogue#1456744094867919022]]. **Contested:** another player calls Mining "a really bad gold maker" [[d:rogue#1476934750731112661]].
- Realistic target: "any farm yielding 40g+ an hour is a decent farm" [[d:rogue#1476988625773396225]]. Soloing the HFQ last bosses with a vampirism setup, plus vendoring and disenchanting, yields about 40-60 g/h and Fashion Coins [[d:rogue#1489491333029822575]].
- Pickpocketing BRD for raw gold is "terrible" per/hour [[d:rogue#1440475227720126636]], [[d:rogue#1440512329543454752]]. It is worth doing for Heavy Junkboxes and BoE epics (see [Pickpocket](#lockpicking-pickpocket-fishing-cooking)).

### Ravenholdt reputation

| Step | How | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral → Friendly | Pickpocket **Syndicate Emblems** from Syndicate humans in Alterac and Hillsbrad and turn them in | [[d:rogue#1482560599085940878]] |
| Killing Syndicate | Gives no Ravenholdt rep on Turtle | [[d:rogue#1482703816540422194]], [[d:rogue#1471620131003306148]] |
| Friendly → Exalted | Turn in **Precise Lock Cylinders**, 10 rep each: about 3,800-3,900 cylinders in total. The turn-in quest needs level 50 | [[d:rogue#1481735559109148754]], [[d:rogue#1482561236284608662]], [[d:rogue#1483180319463969010]] |

- **Where cylinders come from:** every Heavy Junkbox now holds one. They stack to 20 and can be bought and sold on the AH. Only rogues can farm them, but anyone can turn them in [[d:rogue#1482561495752769546]], [[d:rogue#1482561787953021151]], [[d:rogue#1483179818752282834]].
- **Cost:** buying your way to exalted was about 600 g in 2026-02 [[d:rogue#1471626146486685858]]; one rogue spent close to 3k gold on cylinders in 2026-03 [[d:rogue#1481719479070359582]].
- **Rewards:** players still called the rep "essentially useless" in 2026-02 [[d:rogue#1474693288551256246]]. The 2026-02-18 changelog moved the Dusksteel Throwing Blade to John Smith in Ravenholdt Manor [[d:rogue#1474694780922495088]]. The class-weapon chain below does not need Ravenholdt rep [[d:rogue#1488700628141216015]], [[d:rogue#1491517634154467581]].

### Class-weapon questline (1.18.1)

The reward is a matched pair of one-handed swords [[d:rogue#1484634204078870679]]. The starting quest is 42029 on the database [[d:rogue#1489170904809472082]].
1. **Start:** the item drops from the secret last boss of [Timbermaw Hold](../../instances/timbermaw-hold.md), about a 50% drop. One raid member must be Timbermaw exalted and do a quest chain to summon it [[d:rogue#1491874344563900659]], [[d:rogue#1488712161130713108]], [[d:rogue#1491482271553683538]].
2. **Steps players listed:** talk to an NPC in Ravenholdt Manor; sneak into Undercity or Stormwind to pickpocket someone (both NPCs are in throne rooms); kill a few elites; sneak into BRD with a Dark Iron dwarf disguise; kill the new BWL boss and an MC boss; bring 10 Elementium Ore [[d:rogue#1488698671527624869]], [[d:rogue#1488703428283858944]], [[d:rogue#1491874344563900659]], [[d:rogue#1491874449639866469]], [[d:rogue#1488708328094826648]], [[d:rogue#1488708349707944068]].
3. **Bug:** don't kill the MC boss with the MC weekly quest in your log. For one rogue the quest item then didn't drop, and he had to wait a week [[d:rogue#1489082025993306254]].

### Tier 0.5 (Darkmantle) chain

- **Cost:** about 500 g [[d:rogue#1450543564831588476]] to 600-700 g on Nordanaar [[d:rogue#1465773917783195894]]; one player guessed 1-2k [[d:rogue#1465765517158514941]].
- **Lost amulet pieces:** don't delete "Left Piece of Lord Valthalak's Amulet" after its quest; the next step needs it.
