# Rogue

**Recommendation:** level as **Assassination** (Envenom at 30, Noxious Assault at 40) with the fastest daggers you can find. Raid as **Combat Envenom (24/27/0)**, the Combat/Assassination hybrid that uses Backstab (daggers) or Sinister Strike (swords) to build combo points and Envenom instead of Rupture. It is the best PvE spec from pre-raid gear through Kara40 in the community sim. A raid can also use one **Subtlety** rogue for Improved Expose Armor, Hemorrhage and Mark for Death. Keep a second set of poisoned weapons in your bags: since 1.18.1, Dissolvent and Corrosive Poison only work on some creature types.

Turtle WoW reworked all three rogue trees in Class Changes 2 (November 2024) and again in 1.18.0 (Noxious Assault, Dissolvent Poison, a toggled Blade Flurry, Blade Rush). 1.18.1 then nerfed the poison build. Vanilla guides, and most guides written before 2026-03-20, are out of date.

## Roles

- **Melee DPS** (all three trees). Combat Envenom is the current default; deep Assassination is for leveling, fresh 60s and solo play; Combat with Adrenaline Rush is a short-fight alternative [[d:rogue#1486812371602243695]], [[d:rogue#1492075054433960148]], [[d:rogue#1487781074191913032]], [[d:rogue#1491107146111127693]].
- **Raid support** (Subtlety): Improved Expose Armor and Hemorrhage are raid-wide target debuffs; Mark for Death and Tricks of the Trade buff your party [[d:rogue#1487763850211364895]]. One Sub rogue per raid is the norm [[d:rogue#1331689119759208491]].
- **Utility:** Kick, Distract, Sap, Blind, stuns on trash, Vanish for threat resets and trinket swaps, lockpicking.
- **Tank (niche):** an avoidance "rogue tank" can hold many bosses up to Naxx, but it has no taunt and weak AoE threat. See [Rogue tank](tank.md).
- **Healer / ranged:** none.

## What changed in 1.18.1

The balance pass was posted on 2026-01-23 and revised on 2026-03-19; 1.18.1 went live on 2026-03-20 [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).

- **Corrosive and Dissolvent Poison now only apply to Elementals, Mechanicals, Giants and Undead** [[d:rogue#1484513053876949133]]. Instant and Deadly Poison work on everything except nature-immune targets. You now swap poisons (or whole weapon sets) between packs. The community rated this far worse than the damage nerfs [[d:rogue#1486702772622725260]], [[d:rogue#1487458269957062890]]. Players are Humanoid, so the physical poisons are gone from PvP [[d:rogue#1488217700206641192]].
- **Taste for Blood** became a 2-point talent (1/1.5/2% physical damage) and **no longer boosts Corrosive or Dissolvent** [[d:rogue#1484222296192454848]], [[d:rogue#1490094810277613608]]. **Noxious Assault** Attack Power scaling went from 35% to 30% [[d:rogue#1464347389597847693]].
- **Opportunity** 4-20% → 3-15% (aimed at Combat daggers) [[d:rogue#1464387758796837010]]. **Blade Flurry's** energy-regen penalty dropped from 30% to 20% [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071).
- The **low ranks** of Deadly, Instant, Corrosive and Dissolvent lost AP scaling (leveling and pre-AQ20 raiding); max ranks were untouched [Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071), [[d:rogue#1464928738901889057]].
- **Ornate Bloodstone Dagger** no longer procs from non-physical, no-cast effects, which ended the OBD/spellstrike build [[d:rogue#1472655388741472319]]. **Fang of Venoxis** and **Dragonfang Blade** were buffed [[d:rogue#1473054148294344786]], [[d:rogue#1472687870828613775]].
- **Net effect:** Assassination lost roughly 10% and Combat Envenom about 5% of damage, which put Combat Envenom about 5% ahead [[d:rogue#1485568535215476746]]. The new sim rule of thumb is to **never run Rupture and Taste for Blood in an Envenom build** [[d:rogue#1486815192690987191]], [[d:rogue#1488627102281498785]].
- **Hotfixes:** Deadly Poison did double damage for 2-3 days after launch, then was fixed [[d:rogue#1487593806906785873]], [Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139). The Blade Flurry buff-cap bug was fixed in April [[d:rogue#1495130453802352766]].
- **New:** the **Fortune** stat (proc chance on items; capes, necks and trinkets only) [[d:rogue#1474793529476645117]], and a rogue class-weapon questline (a matched pair of one-handed swords) starting in Timbermaw Hold [[d:rogue#1472672496007512127]], [[d:rogue#1484634204078870679]], [[d:rogue#1491482271553683538]].

Before 1.18.1, guides told you to run double Dissolvent everywhere, keep a 5-point Rupture up for Taste for Blood, and use the OBD as your main hand. None of that applies now.

## Professions

- **Leveling:** Skinning + Leatherworking on a first character (self-made leather gear); switch at 60 to whatever you need [[d:rogue#1487516373512093821]].
- **Raiding:** Engineering is the only profession that adds to your character's power (Goblin Sapper Charges, Gnomish Battle Chicken) [[d:rogue#1459623698632802468]]. Level Gnomish first for the Chicken, then switch to Goblin for sappers [[d:rogue#1453273131988029490]]. **Contested:** one veteran calls it "very overrated" for rogues [[d:rogue#1486342417589342240]].
- **Gold:** naked ZG mining is repeatedly called the easiest rogue farm [[d:rogue#1489623080711094384]]. Disenchanting dungeon drops also works [[d:rogue#1440513896049016936]], and so does soloing the Hateforge Quarry and LBRS last bosses for Fashion Coins (~40-60 g/h) [[d:rogue#1489491333029822575]], [[d:rogue#1494756893061812255]]. "Anything yielding 40g+/hour is a decent farm" [[d:rogue#1476988625773396225]].
- **Poisons** are their own rogue-only skill, and Alchemy does not help with them [[d:rogue#1487509381837754471]]. **Lockpicking** is needed for the class quest chains and pays through box opening.

Full guide: [Professions](professions.md)
