# Mage

**Recommendation:** level **Frost**, raid **Arcane** (43/8/0), and keep **Fire** as a second spec for AQ40, Naxx and Kara40, where it can match Arcane. Frost is also the control and PvP spec, and the best spec for soloing and open-world farming. Install **Nampower** on day one; every spec depends on its spell queueing.

Turtle WoW reworked the mage in 1.17.2 ("CC2") and again in 1.18.0. Arcane now plays around the custom **Arcane Rupture** debuff, the baseline **Arcane Surge** and **Temporal Convergence**. Frost has **Icicles** and **Flash Freeze**; Fire has **Hot Streak** and a 4-second, shared **Ignite**. Guides written for vanilla or before 1.17.2 do not apply.

## Roles

- **Ranged DPS** (all three specs). Arcane is the meta: about 80-90% of raiding mages [[d:mage#1488090240580190382]].
- **Utility:** Arcane Intellect, Remove Lesser Curse (heavy decursing in Kara40) [[d:mage#1459267043512549439]], Polymorph, Counterspell, Amplify/Dampen Magic, food, water and portals.
- **Tank (niche):** Arcane mages tank the caster emperor on **Twin Emperors** in shadow-resistance gear, and some tank dungeons for fun. See [Mage tanking](arcane.md#mage-tanking-twin-emperors-and-dungeons).
- **Healer:** none. Mages have no heal spell [[d:mage#1431491476835139594]].

## What changed in 1.18.1

Mages got "one to two sentences every major update" [[d:mage#1480942763909583051]]:

- **Improved Fire Blast** now reduces Fire Blast's GCD (0.3/0.6/1.0 s). Fire Blast became the highest-DPS button for Fire, and the Arcane raid build takes it too [[d:mage#1487204103632191600]], [[d:mage#1486320286940004443]].
- **Ice Barrier:** +10% Frost damage for 60 s after the cast, plus +5% while the shield holds. **Contested:** some call it a straight buff, "still has 5% on shield, just 10% is split off independent" [[d:mage#1466881324055920836]]; others dismiss it as a QoL change, not a real buff, since it "changes almost nothing for frost" [[d:mage#1466941066073870347]].
- **Scythe of Elune**'s Arcane proc cooldown is now enforced, a real loss for Arcane [[d:mage#1488090233068322956]]. **Mirror Magic** (T3.5 3-piece) now triggers from AoE spells [[d:mage#1481363007782129816]].
- **PvP:** class-dependent dampening, and resilience that reduces spell crits, turned mage from "a burst class to more of a control class" [[d:mage#1491118432945573978]]. The PvP gear system was overhauled [[d:mage#1487460403125424229]].
- **Weapon swaps now cost a GCD** [[d:mage#1489371212650909758]]. The **Fortune** stat arrived, but most proc items got internal cooldowns at the same time [[d:mage#1484544659697959032]].
- **Not addressed:** Arcane Surge rework, Frost's mana problems [[d:mage#1464427965990306007]], [[d:mage#1464428023045427210]].

## Professions

- **Level with two gathering professions**; Herbalism + Alchemy is the safest long-run pair [[d:mage#1451246474187771915]].
- **Engineering** is "bis for every class" but not mandatory; for a mage it is mostly **Goblin Sapper Charges** on AoE packs (with Arcane Explosion) [[d:mage#1488178674309267647]], [[d:mage#1438566091365552378]], [[d:mage#1468324299499897119]].
- **Tailoring** only for Robe of the Archmage (BiS until BWL); the other good crafts (Spellwoven Nobility Drape, Gloves of Spell Mastery) can be bought and Mooncloth margins are poor [[d:mage#1434898636461969418]], [[d:mage#1453809774398541826]], [[d:mage#1484608704958431252]], [[d:mage#1473078862274367721]].
- **Enchanting + Jewelcrafting** earn gold if you advertise; JC makes the +6 Arcane SP ring/neck enchant. Profession content is "kinda neglected on this server" [[d:mage#1448316389499867206]], [[d:mage#1466718604044144652]], [[d:mage#1488179923918131453]].

Full guide: [Professions](professions.md)

## Gold

- **Dire Maul East lashers** are the classic mage farm from level 54 up [[d:mage#1447768105819439347]], [[d:mage#1428327478342058077]]. One raider counts it as one of only two well-known gold farms in the game [[d:mage#1433513741743296602]], [[d:mage#1433513769304068147]].
- The Naxx-trash ghoul farm (Plate Scraps) fell in value; ZG bijous are the alternative [[d:mage#1433524597290172567]], [[d:mage#1433526565739954187]]. The old ZF/ZG mass-pull farms are patched [[d:mage#1441954445670744277]].
- Conjured water (level 55) sells on the AH [[d:mage#1489858435792109598]].

## Addons every mage uses
