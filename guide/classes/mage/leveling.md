# Mage Leveling (1-60)

**Recommendation:** level as **Frost**. It is the safest spec (quick-cooldown CC, you can kite almost anything) and the default for Hardcore. At 60, respec to [Arcane](arcane.md) for raids. Train spells from **all three schools**, buy a **wand** early, and gear **Intellect, Stamina and spell power**. Ignore hit until you fight mobs 3 levels above you. Arcane becomes good at level **24** (Arcane Missiles rank 3) and is a fine choice for dungeon-heavy leveling from ~30. Blizzard AoE grinding no longer works on Turtle; AoE farming now means **Frost Nova → Flamestrike → Cone of Cold → Arcane Explosion** kiting.

## Which spec to level

| Spec | Verdict | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Frost** | **Default.** Easy (one main spell), safe (quick CC), strong solo ("you can kite any mob indefinitely"), but slower kills | [[d:mage#1453911881256538223]], [[d:mage#1453911746040565926]]; "for HC frost will be the safest pick" [[d:mage#1484982906039570532]]; "the chill spec to level" [[d:mage#1467437401772392489]] |
| **Arcane** | Viable, but squishy and mana-hungry early. It picks up at **24**, when Arcane Missiles rank 3 does 280 total damage vs 144 for rank 2 [[d:mage#1484552827874578533]], [[d:mage#1483088830335549621]]. Level Fire or Frost "until at least level 24" [[d:mage#1473113689132630157]]; the talents reachable around 35-40 are "what will really make it fun" [[d:mage#1474520947926241343]]. One adviser would not go Arcane "until like 30/40" [[d:mage#1453915381151436801]]. In 35-60 dungeons its mana pool is too small, so Frost or Fire are preferred there [[d:mage#1466569768885813513]] | as cited |
| **Fire** | Fun, gets higher Fireball ranks earlier and has longer range, but is crit-dependent, and crit is scarce at low level [[d:mage#1481215928447537232]], [[d:mage#1456753314074202133]]. Good for normal-sized dungeon pulls [[d:mage#1466570131382468834]] | as cited |

A simple rule from the channel: dying too much → Frost single target; kills too slow → Fire; want to AoE-grind → Frost AoE [[d:mage#1453915381151436801]]. "All 3 can work fine" [[d:mage#1484551865285869688]].

**At 60:** if you will quest, dungeon and PvP, stay deep Frost. If you go straight into raiding, go full Arcane [[d:mage#1492227456306909355]].

## Respecs

- Respecs cost at most **5 g** on Turtle, so experimenting is cheap [[d:mage#1489458239912411217]].
- The **Goblin Brainwashing Device** (~350 g on the AH) stores up to 4 talent builds for instant swaps. Dedicated mages fill all 4: Arcane, Fire, Frost and a tank build [[d:mage#1493987631833284648]], [[d:mage#1480899652470440081]].
- Typical path: Frost to 60 (or Frost/Fire to 24-35, then Arcane) [[d:mage#1429898668089151528]]. At 60, respec for raids.

## Abilities and ranks

- **Train every school.** Fireball before Frostbolt at very low levels, because you unlock higher Fireball ranks sooner [[d:mage#1425113776557916230]]. Frostbolt's cast time grows with rank (1.5 → 1.8 → 2.2 → 2.6 s, capped at 3.0 s from rank 6); this is intended [[d:mage#1485075344477393067]]. Spell coefficients are poor below level 20 [[d:mage#1461543828996489329]].
- **Key unlocks:** Arcane Missiles rank 3 at 24 [[d:mage#1484552827874578533]]; Cone of Cold at 26 [[d:mage#1421201196810633308]], [[d:mage#1431344728032411668]]; Arcane Explosion from 14 [[d:mage#1341020543989710849]]. Arcane Surge ranks come at 32/40/48/56 ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102968#p102968)).
- **Keep rank 1 on your bars:** Frostbolt (cheap slow), Frost Nova, Arcane Explosion (threat-safe, trinket pre-procs) [[d:mage#1479603921264836630]], Blizzard (Flash Freeze fishing at 60) [[d:mage#1430636501141553164]], and two Flamestrike ranks (both DoTs tick) [[d:mage#1479770070598291608]].
- **Arcane Power:** do not use it while leveling. "It will kill u if u cast it when on low man[a]" [[d:mage#1490985845094613123]].
- **Conjure Water** tops out at rank 7 [[d:mage#1426213242006671410]]. The higher-rank conjure quest ("Arcane Refreshment") starts in Dire Maul and ends with a Perfect Obsidian Shard from Moam (AQ20) [[d:mage#1310717436890189904]]. Conjured Mana Oranges come from an AQ20 recipe quest [[d:mage#1429068091421102140]].
- **Portals/teleports** are taught by portal trainers next to the mage trainers [[d:mage#1491761752516333710]]. The Theramore portal tome drops from Kara10 trash [[d:mage#1430206372452241549]].
- "Chilled" is a status effect, not a spell you can train [[d:mage#1445919492650700862]].

## The pull and kill loop

**Frost / general (bot-ready):**

1. Pull with `Frostbolt` from max range. Cast 1-2 more if the mob is slow to arrive [[d:mage#1483131710269952153]], [[d:mage#1462360248256954369]].
2. When the mob reaches melee: `Frost Nova`, then walk to max range [[d:mage#1462783774893936671]].
3. `Frostbolt` again. If it breaks free early and Nova is down, use `Cone of Cold` (rank 1 to save mana) ([Mage Levelling FAQ (wiki)](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Mage_Levelling_FAQ)).
4. Finish with `Fire Blast` (instant) or a wand shot when it is low [[d:mage#1483131710269952153]].
5. If a fight goes bad: `Blink` away, then `Ice Block` (from 30), `Cold Snap` to reset.
6. Eat and drink together after the fight. "Mage is mostly about drinking and eating early game" [[d:mage#1462783774893936671]].

**Fire:** `Fireball` spam, `Fire Blast` while moving or to finish, `Frost Nova` and walk if the mob reaches you ([Mage Levelling FAQ (wiki)](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Mage_Levelling_FAQ)).

**Arcane:** `Arcane Missiles` is your main button (Improved Arcane Missiles removes pushback). Open with `Arcane Rupture` only on high-HP mobs, since it costs a lot of mana ([Mage Levelling FAQ (wiki)](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Mage_Levelling_FAQ)). Up to the 50s it plays as an "Arcane Missiles turret" [[d:mage#1495738138251890708]].

**Getting hit is fine** [[d:mage#1462783774893936671]]. Early on the mage "lacks the tools to kite"; later you handle 3-4 mobs at once [[d:mage#1490050503638515892]]. At very low levels Frostbolt is mainly the slow and Fireball the damage [[d:mage#1489950541500121121]]. Polymorph one mob when you fight two [[d:mage#1489979706982793366]].

## Resources and downtime

- **Water is free** for mages, so Intellect means more fights between drinks [[d:mage#1463949167608070258]].
- **Survival without heals:** mages have no healing spell. Train **First Aid**, and carry cheap AH health potions (or take Herbalism/Alchemy) [[d:mage#1431491476835139594]], [[d:mage#1431648186744700978]]. Eat when you drink [[d:mage#1431491677612146789]].
- **Rested XP:** stand near a player-placed tent outside a city (commonly at the Stormwind/Orgrimmar gates). One tent gives rest in about 10 minutes; more tents are faster [[d:mage#1435614404506030110]], [[d:mage#1435614749885988912]].
- **Battlegrounds** give strong XP and are a viable leveling path once past the first 5-6 levels of a bracket [[d:mage#1446863976481685505]], [[d:mage#1487588671199903825]].

## Stats and gear by bracket

- **Stats:** spell power and Intellect [[d:mage#1424664349888483378]]. **Contested** which comes first: spell power as long as you don't go OOM every fight [[d:mage#1463948932563603541]], or Intellect first for fewer drinks [[d:mage#1463949167608070258]]. Add **Stamina**: "mage is extremely squishy without stamina gear" [[d:mage#1489962476190695554]]. Quest greens at your level make you more durable [[d:mage#1490050765773865121]]. Hit is pointless pre-60 unless you fight +3 mobs [[d:mage#1434201589421314099]]. Around level 25, move toward SP gear, but not all of it, or you will have mana issues [[d:mage#1461538444634362070]], [[d:mage#1461552316368031807]].
- **1-12:** Night Elf and High Elf starting-zone quests give a wand [[d:mage#1424002896781119569]]. Early on, "a good wand actually will do more damage than your spells" [[d:mage#1490061732918329505]]. Low-level wands such as the Greater Magic Wand (AH) hit far above their level [[d:mage#1483131945121480926]].
- **13-25:** dungeons open from about level 13 [[d:mage#1489951665858547742]]. Ragefire Chasm drops a good early wand; several players skip the mage wand quest chain [[d:mage#1425868581173723196]]. Run **Deadmines** "until you get the staff the wand and the ring" [[d:mage#1430913027577745540]].
- **25-45:** wand upgrades Chillnail Splinter (Dustwallow quest chain) or Freezing Shard [[d:mage#1430576252502413352]], [[d:mage#1430576558183022602]]. Run Scarlet Monastery Graveyard + Library together from ~33 [[d:mage#1492132178593710091]].
- **45-60:** Scholomance wand (4 Int / 11 SP); the Black Morass wand (14 arcane SP) if you will play Arcane; a 16-SP Dire Maul West trash wand from the AH [[d:mage#1437900378258669568]], [[d:mage#1456942320204382355]]. The Avatar of Hakkar (Sunken Temple) drops Embrace of the Wind Serpent, buffed on Turtle and good for Arcane [[d:mage#1458262042732527780]].
- **At 60:** see the pre-raid lists on the [Arcane](arcane.md#pre-raid-fresh-60) page.

## AoE farming

**What changed:** Improved Blizzard's slow was cut repeatedly, and mobs now walk through the Blizzard before it finishes. "Blizzard is dead, coc is alive" [[d:mage#1436705656941056132]], [[d:mage#1454615166913089587]]. With Permafrost, 3/3 Improved Blizzard is a 50% slow (40% without) [[d:mage#1441842623957565490]]. **Contested** older figures: 75% → 50% [[d:mage#1431343840966479913]], or 65% → 40% with the duration cut from 2 s to 1.5 s [[d:mage#1430717972997214329]]. Players now use "coc/nova/flamestrike kiting" [[d:mage#1489660017299689473]].

For AoE-farm talents, Akanamu advises maxing Elemental Precision plus the Cone of Cold talents, and picking up Amplify Magic to help pull packs [[d:mage#1443288078230884543]].

**The AoE loop** (bot-ready):

1. Gather the pack (wand, rank-1 Fire Blast or body pull).
2. `Frost Nova` (the pack is rooted).
3. `Flamestrike`, timed to land just before Nova breaks. Mobs break Nova at different times [[d:mage#1436468556274602146]], [[d:mage#1423659276966301849]].
4. `Cone of Cold` as they break free [[d:mage#1431344576542671049]].
5. Kite **in a circle** (backpedalling lets them catch up) and spam `Arcane Explosion` when they catch up. Reapply `Cone of Cold` on cooldown ([Mage Levelling FAQ (wiki)](https://turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Mage_Levelling_FAQ)), [[d:mage#1491253713472589894]].
6. When Nova is back: rank-1 `Frost Nova` → `Flamestrike` → `Cone of Cold` again, using the highest Flamestrike rank your mana allows [[d:mage#1491249152842010624]].

A pure-Frost variant skips Nova and Flamestrike: Cone of Cold + Arcane Explosion only [[d:mage#1436463662087929896]]. Cone of Cold and Arcane Explosion have no target cap [[d:mage#1439042850317799425]], [[d:mage#1439882358940504195]]. About 1% of Cone of Cold casts do nothing to a grouped pack (unexplained bug) [[d:mage#1439307616391135302]]. A walkthrough video exists [[d:mage#1491253713472589894]].

## Professions while leveling

- Double up on gathering (Mining/Herbalism/Skinning) for gold and pick production later [[d:mage#1465738429038858382]], [[d:mage#1465738295186034851]]. Herbalism + Alchemy is the best long-run pair for a first character [[d:mage#1451246474187771915]].
- Engineering matters mainly at 60 (Sappers) [[d:mage#1468324299499897119]].

## Common mistakes

1. Trying to Blizzard-kite packs as in classic [[d:mage#1436705656941056132]].
