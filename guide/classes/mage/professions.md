# Mage professions

**Recommendation:** professions matter less for a mage than for most classes. Pick them for gold and convenience, not for DPS [[d:mage#1308485949897969724]], [[d:mage#1447768568665210990]]. **Level with two gathering professions** (Herbalism is the best for gold) [[d:mage#1465738429038858382]], [[d:mage#1448308194551271475]]. At 60 the usual raiding setup is **Engineering + one other**. Engineering is "bis" but not mandatory, and for a mage it mostly means **Goblin Sapper Charges** on AoE packs [[d:mage#1468324299499897119]], [[d:mage#1451246474187771915]]. If this is your first character, **Herbalism + Alchemy** is the safest long-run pair [[d:mage#1451246474187771915]]. **Tailoring** is worth it only long enough to make **Robe of the Archmage**. After that you can drop it and keep wearing the robe [[d:mage#1448045718920233052]], [[d:mage#1448189554992484483]].

| **Raiding: Fire / Frost** | Engineering + any | Same argument: Sappers on AoE packs. The sources name no spec-specific craft for Fire or Frost | [[d:mage#1488178674309267647]], [[d:mage#1422989631430983700]] |

## What each profession gives a mage

### Engineering

- **Goblin Sapper Charge:** it is off the GCD, so you can weave it between Arcane Explosions [[d:mage#1493436328271024149]]. Each one costs about 4g [[d:mage#1488190245013946489]]. The cooldown is the same 5 minutes as in classic (single source) [[d:mage#1493643324093894888]]. A raider reports Sapper + Arcane Jewel of Wild Magics + Arcane Explosion doing about 50k damage in one GCD on a big Naxx spider pack [[d:mage#1438566091365552378]].
- **1.18.1 goggles:** you craft the new Engineering goggles, then upgrade them with an item from the new BWL boss [[d:mage#1484999614477373665]]. Their proc gives **+200 SP and -10% haste**. That should be strong for AoE and weak for single target, because the haste loss "almost cancels out" the spell power (single source) [[d:mage#1494315992212574228]], [[d:mage#1494315772955066569]].
- **Leveling:** the Green Lens "of Wrath" (+35 damage) needs Engineering and was suggested around level 40 [[d:mage#1437749265530224680]].
- **Utility:** Engineering portals to Gadgetzan and Everlook add "even more portals" [[d:mage#1455634315621826561]], [[d:mage#1453808304160506000]].
- **Gnomish or Goblin?** The mage channel has no answer to this [[d:mage#1488169137459953705]], [[d:mage#1488169284512382987]]. One player suggests a race-change trick: level as a Gnome, craft the Gnome-only BoP items, then switch to Goblin to craft your own rocket boots (single source) [[d:mage#1488174450477240420]].
- The **Goblin Brainwashing Device** (4 saved talent builds, about 350g) comes from Gurubashi Arena or the donation shop, not from Engineering. Most mages who swap specs buy one [[d:mage#1484748330004250786]], [[d:mage#1493987631833284648]], [[d:mage#1480899652470440081]].

- **Buy it instead:** apart from the robe, the good crafts are BoE with thin margins [[d:mage#1448308508092141690]]. Mooncloth saves about 10g per 5-day cooldown (2 Felcloth at about 9-10g each against about 28-30g for a Mooncloth), so buying it is often fine [[d:mage#1453809774398541826]], [[d:mage#1473078862274367721]]. Dreamthread gear is "not worth it" [[d:mage#1436108316446298112]].
- **Contested:** "Tailoring is kind of just a meme" [[d:mage#1448308285307355278]], against "you will need it for T3" (Mooncloth) [[d:mage#1469760928508805150]]. Players expected no new tailoring gear in 1.18.1 [[d:mage#1485191444338577469]]. The 1.18.1 crafted-item upgrade system could change that [[d:mage#1473082085202858287]].

### Jewelcrafting (Turtle-custom)

- **+6 Arcane SP ring/neck enchant**, made by a Jewelcrafter. Arcane uses it on ring and neck. One customer was asked for about 15 Rough Stone + 15 Silk Cloth [[d:mage#1466718604044144652]], [[d:mage#1466727423239524463]].
- **Crystallized Topaz** (+9 Arcane damage on ring/amulet, likely uses Imperial Topaz) exists in 1.18.1, but its recipe source is unknown [[d:mage#1484573170458558534]], [[d:mage#1490456423170183369]], [[d:mage#1488248016761655296]].
- A Jewelcrafting spell-penetration option (+5) is useful only on trash with 15 arcane resistance [[d:mage#1445153386206068898]].
- Jewelcrafting makes "tons of good caster offhands" but no caster main-hand [[d:mage#1449979639907749888]]. Pair it with Enchanting and advertise both [[d:mage#1448316389499867206]], [[d:mage#1448316495372222494]]. Gemology and Enchanting are among the few crafts with lasting endgame demand [[d:mage#1440878940183990322]].

### Alchemy and Herbalism

- You make your own raid consumables. The mage raid kit is Flask of Supreme Power, Greater Arcane Elixir, Elixir of Greater Arcane Power and Dreamshard Elixir (see [Arcane](arcane.md)) [[d:mage#1438508962843463692]]. Others can craft them for you, so Alchemy is a convenience, not a requirement [[d:mage#1448045718920233052]].
- While leveling, a mage has no heal. Take First Aid and buy cheap potions, or make them with Herbalism/Alchemy [[d:mage#1431491476835139594]].

### Enchanting

- It is useful for disenchanting and for collecting rare enchants [[d:mage#1451246962425987173]]. Tailoring + Enchanting is the classic cloth-caster pair [[d:mage#1490063816065679450]]. It earns gold only if you advertise [[d:mage#1448316389499867206]].

### Survival and Gardening (secondary professions)

- **Before 1.18.1:** Survival capped at 150. Its main use was the **Traveler's Tent**, which grants rested XP and stacks with nearby tents. Mages used tents like everyone else ([Speedz1337, 2024-05-09](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94431#p94431), [official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/survival-and-gardening)) [[d:mage#1435614404506030110]], [[d:mage#1435614749885988912]].
- **1.18.1 rework:** Survival now has 80+ recipes that draw on the primary professions. These include slowing, rooting and trapping tools, gathering bags and Woodcutting ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). Players also report gathering trinkets and a skill-300 sickle that raises the Black Lotus chance ([Birtha, 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171974#p171974)). Tents now cap rested XP at 25%, and rested XP gives a 50% bonus instead of 100% ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)).
- **For mages:** the channel says Survival "can be tough" to level after the patch because some materials are expensive, and that Skinning helps because many new Survival recipes use its materials [[d:mage#1489907806365683712]], [[d:mage#1489906883702816820]]. The channel has **no mage-specific case** for Survival tools or for Gardening. Treat both as optional. Gardening needs Survival 75; its crops give food buffs ([official site](https://turtlecraft.gg/survival-and-gardening)).

## When to pick up and swap

1. **1-60:** level two gathering professions. Crafting is not worth much while leveling [[d:mage#1465738429038858382]].
2. **At 60:** swap one gathering profession, usually Herbalism, for Engineering if you raid seriously or PvP [[d:mage#1451246474187771915]].
3. **Tailoring detour:** learn Tailoring, craft Robe of the Archmage, then drop it. The robe stays wearable [[d:mage#1448045718920233052]], [[d:mage#1448189554992484483]]. Crafting your own Mooncloth for T3 is optional, since you can buy it [[d:mage#1489905078356803664]].
4. Skill thresholds for mage-relevant recipes are not stated in the mage sources (see the general [Professions overview](../../professions.md)).

## Race interactions

- **Gnome:** the Engineering racial (vanilla baseline). Gnome-only BoP engineering crafts are the reason for the Gnome-then-Goblin race-change trick (single source) [[d:mage#1488174450477240420]].
- **Goblin:** Goblin-only engineering recipes (rocket boots) [[d:mage#1488174450477240420]]. The Goblin Prospecting racial also grants Jewelcrafting skill ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102965#p102965)).
- **High Elf / Human:** one player says their reputation and enchanting racials "don't up your damage in raid" [[d:mage#1457578016170573874]]. A question on whether the High Elf enchanting bonus matters for crafting went unanswered [[d:mage#1352741135218507928]].

## Gold

- **Herbalism** is the mage's steady gold profession [[d:mage#1448308194551271475]]. Herb prices crashed on Nordanaar in late 2025, because of lasher-farm oversupply and a cheap herb item; Gromsblood sold for 8-15s [[d:mage#1448314903759687832]], [[d:mage#1448315831267233944]]. A mage with 300 Herbalism reports only about 30g/hour in DME [[d:mage#1425452420766498829]].
- **Mining** is a fine second income profession [[d:mage#1453809945454837983]], [[d:mage#1473078862274367721]].
- The class farms (Dire Maul East lashers from level 54, conjured level-55 water on the AH) need no profession. See [Class overview: Gold](index.md#gold) [[d:mage#1447768105819439347]], [[d:mage#1489858435792109598]].
- Engineering "doesn't make gold", and Sappers cost money [[d:mage#1333961791125852251]], [[d:mage#1488190245013946489]].
