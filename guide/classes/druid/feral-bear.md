# Feral Bear (tank)

**Recommendation:** play the **11/35/5 tank build** (Feral Instinct, Thick Hide, Feral Charge, Carnage, Improved Shred, Furor). Get **8% hit**, then gear two sets: a **threat** set (haste, Strength, crit) and a **mitigation** set (Stamina, armor). Open every boss the same way: **Cat Form → Tiger's Fury → Bear Form → Mighty Rage Potion → Maul + Savage Bite**, then **Maul on every swing**, **Savage Bite** on Clearcasting or high rage, **Swipe** on 2+ mobs or at full rage. Bear is still the strongest sustained single-target threat tank, but since 1.18.1 it is weaker in the first seconds of a pull, and it takes more damage than warriors or paladins.

Why:

- **Feral Instinct** (bear threat 130% → 145% [[d:druid#1455708290997686473]]) became a "must have" after the 1.18.1 threat nerf [[d:druid#1464363900014170358]].
- **Thick Hide** is now 3 points and much stronger [[d:druid#1453305363356057736]]. Its armor bonus is multiplied by Dire Bear Form: 100 armor on gear adds about 506 bear armor [[d:druid#1486121788604940519]], [[d:druid#1444864663656333475]]. It does not multiply armor from kits or enchants [[d:druid#1415743918603243622]].
- **Blood Frenzy** gives two 36 s haste windows: Tiger's Fury before the pull, then Enrage [[d:druid#1444972730033377300]].
- **Carnage + Improved Shred** let you deal the best DPS a tank can when your target dies, bleedable or not [[d:druid#1432106087326158961]], [[d:druid#1444972918290383024]].
- **Furor** gives rage on the Cat → Bear pre-pull shift and keeps Reshift useful. "All tank guides/spec suggestions take Furor over Feral Aggression" [[d:druid#1489571705772048576]], [[d:druid#1489572016884416562]].
- **Feral Swiftness is "bait"** for raid tanks [[d:druid#1444972730033377300]]. **Contested:** another player values its 4% dodge at about 6-7% less damage taken for deep tank builds [[d:druid#1438845983743017032]].
- No Demoralizing Roar talent: any warrior's shout beats a druid's roar [[d:druid#1489572016884416562]].

## Stat priority and caps

Keep two sets and swap by fight: bear BiS questions are always "mitigation vs threat" [[d:druid#1446615259614937234]].

1. **Hit to 8%** (5% from gear with Natural Weapons). "Hitcap > everything else, we get parried/dodged/blocked enough as is" [[d:druid#1491029884397424720]]. **Taunt/Growl use spell hit** (16% base miss vs bosses), which matters on Four Horsemen [[d:druid#1463111448652677150]], [[d:druid#1464013456167669915]].
2. **Threat set: haste, Strength, crit.** Haste is "absolutely the best stat by FAR for threat", because Maul rides the swing timer [[d:druid#1415743918603243622]]. About 1% haste ≈ 12 Strength [[d:druid#1432273778221518868]]. One set of weights: AP 1, Agility 1.5, Strength 2.2, Crit 25, Haste 26, Hit 36 [[d:druid#1467897618876334284]]. **Contested: Agility.** "Agi is bait ... triple the threat gains from 3 Haste over 25 Agi" [[d:druid#1473795292779516119]] vs "Agi is superior if you are not crit capped and plan to do Kara" [[d:druid#1474744918055587900]]. Strength gives 2 AP; Agility gives crit, dodge and 2 unscaled armor [[d:druid#1480074298579488909]], [[d:druid#1491364545896382515]]. Bear white-hit crit caps at about 52.5% with 8% hit [[d:druid#1474765599858229310]].
3. **Mitigation set: Stamina, then armor.** Armor on rings, trinkets, neck and weapon is "massive value", because those slots are multiplied by Dire Bear Form (about 3.6x) [[d:druid#1444316992877367408]], [[d:druid#1444350531312029988]]. Look for leather with green (bonus) armor text [[d:druid#1470825500875292966]]. Armor from enchants, kits and food is **not** multiplied [[d:druid#1425973110548463647]], [[d:druid#1473392118826537100]].
4. **Armor has no hard cap on Turtle.** Value is linear to about 16.6-17.3k, still worth it to about 22-23k, then drops sharply [[d:druid#1470162624392986675]], [[d:druid#1438986407376060446]]. 22k armor ≈ 78% reduction against a level-63 boss [[d:druid#1463168783484059688]].
5. **Defense and dodge are weak.** Bears cannot push crits off the table without parry and block; 440 defense needs nearly full T2/T3 and is called a trap [[d:druid#1454812937444393186]], [[d:druid#1428087601268789288]]. **Contested:** dodge. Camp A: near zero or negative, since dodge costs rage and makes damage spikier [[d:druid#1432306133560725606]], [[d:druid#1452942318167654521]]. Camp B: valuable for big trash pulls and with Ancient Brutality's rage-on-dodge [[d:druid#1432302031011581995]], [[d:druid#1445422497188155503]]. Never enchant dodge [[d:druid#1415743918603243622]].

**Fresh-60 enchant priority:** haste > Strength > armor > Stamina [[d:druid#1470888760542498856]].

## Single-target rotation

**Pre-pull (about 10 s before):** Regrowth → Rejuvenation → Cat Form → **Tiger's Fury** → Bear Form [[d:druid#1472398513760637101]]. Tiger's Fury's Blood Frenzy haste survives the shift into Bear [[d:druid#1444967001121357986]]. Furor plus a rage-on-shift head enchant give up to ~15 rage [[d:druid#1472410728739770508]]. Self-HoTs also generate healing threat on everything you enter combat with [[d:druid#1432288944313077811]].

**Pull:** Faerie Fire (Feral), or a ranged pull, then **Mighty Rage Potion** and open **Maul + Savage Bite** [[d:druid#1473036752146006037]], [[d:druid#1474500952965906515]]. Never open with Growl: it does nothing on a mob with no threat list [[d:druid#1432722952813543505]]. Feral Charge generates no threat [[d:druid#1433148306586075292]]. Pop **Enrage** (or Berserk) when the Tiger's Fury Blood Frenzy runs out [[d:druid#1488823880549273600]].

Priority (bot-ready):

1. **`Maul`** queued on every swing; it is the threat engine [[d:druid#1474473590043836720]], [[d:druid#1491404394812407858]].
2. **`Savage Bite`** (6 s cooldown) when you have **Clearcasting** or **rage > 50** (some use 35) [[d:druid#1491404394812407858]], [[d:druid#1491140379490390106]], [[d:druid#1474473590043836720]]. With **T3 8-piece**, use it on every cooldown to keep the damage-reduction stacks [[d:druid#1480481555263787149]].
3. **`Swipe`** if **2+ mobs** or **rage is full** (Sagesse: above 75 rage) [[d:druid#1491404394812407858]], [[d:druid#1491140379490390106]]. On a single target it is only a rage dump with poor threat [[d:druid#1484741482169499689]].
4. **`Faerie Fire (Feral)`** when no other GCD is needed [[d:druid#1474473590043836720]].
5. **`Growl`** only to take back a mob or to answer a double dodge/parry at the pull. Taunt is off the GCD, so you can let a caster open first and taunt past them [[d:druid#1473036752146006037]], [[d:druid#1432367846968655993]].
6. **`Reshift`** (Bear → Bear) for a burst of rage when rage-starved, timed around the swing; do not overdo it, since it costs Maul uptime [[d:druid#1462441542953210112]], [[d:druid#1491141840211021884]].

Maul, Swipe and Savage Bite do not share a GCD [[d:druid#1436445990809899018]]. **Enrage** increases damage taken; skip it when your healers struggle [[d:druid#1477368423267696690]]. While leveling (rage-starved), do not open with Maul; use autos and Swipe after the first Roar [[d:druid#1438127035804291083]].

### The 1.18.1 threat nerf

| Ability | Threat modifier | Real loss |
|---|---|---|
| Maul | 175% → 160% | ≈ 9% on Maul |
| Savage Bite | 225% → 213% | ≈ 5.6% on Bite |

The official note subtracted percentage points, not a relative 15%/12% [[d:druid#1472870879418646640]], [[d:druid#1470128951165718528]]. In practice players measured about 10-20% total loss, worst in the first ~10 s ("the first 10 seconds are a nightmare now") [[d:druid#1484585026158067752]], [[d:druid#1485730370954592399]]. Over a long fight bear still out-threats a warrior once its lead is built [[d:druid#1485732568736337981]]. On Ambershire, some tanks barely noticed it [[d:druid#1485296567123710134]], and dungeon tanking is unaffected [[d:druid#1486190492780728481]].

**Threat help:** Kiss of the Spider (near-essential) and Atiesh [[d:druid#1485732399189987398]]; Salvation on the highest-threat non-tank [[d:druid#1474483202373714137]]. Horde on Tel'Abim has no Salvation and relies on bear threat most [[d:druid#1445011444260274354]].

## AoE threat

Bear AoE is the weakest of the tanks: tab-target and Swipe (3 targets), and focus one mob at a time unless a paladin or shaman is in the group [[d:druid#1443042513668542507]], [[d:druid#1473127606185623666]].

| Pack | Action |
|---|---|
| Any pull | Pre-HoT yourself, **Maul the kill target**, tab and **Swipe** the others, Faerie Fire (Feral) on loose adds [[d:druid#1432288944313077811]]. Mark targets and ask DPS to wait for one Maul [[d:druid#1431667615029399755]] |
| 2-3 mobs | Swipe + Maul; Savage Bite on Clearcasting [[d:druid#1455467667497422879]] |
| 4+ mobs | Split 2-3 mobs per tank [[d:druid#1465725918617145405]]. Pre-cast Hurricane at the front mob so the pack runs through it [[d:druid#1473069183431610428]] |
| Big packs (Naxx skitterers, ES whelps) | **Idol of Evergrowth**: Demoralizing Roar deals your Thorns damage to every mob hit, uncapped (~120 threat per cast; Thorns up to 44 with T1+T2 set bonuses) [[d:druid#1440456904559300621]], [[d:druid#1453413796864594111]], [[d:druid#1473218279764922368]]. It only beats Swipe at about 15 mobs [[d:druid#1448823173322768597]] |

- **Dragonbreath Chili** and **Oil of Immolation** add AoE threat [[d:druid#1473069183431610428]], [[d:druid#1473218279764922368]].
- **Sapper Charges and dynamite knock you out of Bear Form**; they are useless for bear AoE [[d:druid#1473126693303615643]].
- Windfury and Flametongue totems help bear threat a lot; Windfury beats the Agility totem [[d:druid#1479712614409048095]], [[d:druid#1476684742576308315]].
- The AQ20 set lets Swipe hit a 4th target [[d:druid#1479712728137465856]].

## Cooldowns and survival

- **Barkskin** (60 s): cast it in caster form before shifting; the buff persists in Bear Form, at the cost of 20% attack speed [[d:druid#1488905914017906758]], [[d:druid#1432725711852146830]]. **Barkskin (Feral)** has a 10-minute cooldown [[d:druid#1465653427081187378]].
- **Frenzied Regeneration** was nerfed for PvP reasons and heals little [[d:druid#1308006939296403456]].
- **Berserk** is your fear break [[d:druid#1462684604111519827]].
- **Bandages** work in Bear Form on Turtle [[d:druid#1476736618466381834]]; potions and most consumables work in form [[d:druid#1411187673644929034]].
- Bear Form has a built-in 10% lifesteal [[d:druid#1470133081708167262]].
- Emergency: a cat who rips threat can shift to Bear and has about a 50/50 chance to live until the tank taunts [[d:druid#1488141460816527401]].

### Raid

- **Weapon:** Blessed Qiraji War Hammer (AQ40) is BiS even against Kara40 weapons, except for pure-mitigation setups [[d:druid#1432998166176989215]]. Herald of Woe (mace) takes Iron Counterweight [[d:druid#1469260607684350132]]. **Atiesh** now takes Counterweight too (staves were allowed in 1.18.1) [[d:druid#1484615359334973663]]. Forgotten Raven's Mallet is the best Kara40 threat/mitigation weapon without Atiesh [[d:druid#1362819924690604032]].
- **Chest / belt:** T2 chest and belt are "meh"; take the T2.5 chest and the C'Thun belt, with the Dragonhide belt as a stepping stone [[d:druid#1472364814096662731]]. The T2.5 2-piece is strong [[d:druid#1458768233460142122]].
- **AQ40:** Gloves of the Hidden Temple, Ring of Emperor Vek'lor; AQ40 hits harder physically than BWL [[d:druid#1433383761432678400]], [[d:druid#1433385110178103380]].
- **T3:** a mitigation set; 8-piece gives about 8% damage reduction, mainly worth it on high-damage fights like Patchwerk or Loatheb. It does not make you crit-immune [[d:druid#1445014560443727984]], [[d:druid#1492173100714954823]]. The T3 6-piece bonus is weak [[d:druid#1471344095631900837]]. Swap in stamina off-pieces (Four Horsemen legs, C'Thun belt) for the hardest fights [[d:druid#1445014335733633076]].
- **Kara40:** Forgotten Hide Helm ("best threat AND mitigation helm in the game"), Tunic of Demonic Deception (threat), Felforged Nathrezim Veil, Branch of Resolute Defense, Handwraps of Dead Winds (best threat gloves), Loop of Hardened Slate (best mitigation ring) [[d:druid#1362819924690604032]].
- **PvP shoulders:** Veteran's Dragonhide Epaulets out-stat the T2.5 shoulders for threat and need only 6 pieces for the set bonus (85k honor + 3.5k conquest for the set) [[d:druid#1487935338197291078]].
- **Timbermaw Hold:** its 3-piece bonus is "broken" for a threat set [[d:druid#1487247519087071262]].

- **Food:** Danonzo's 2% haste food rated above Power Mushroom for threat [[d:druid#1475836397528289542]]; Hardened Mushroom and Gurubashi Gumbo stack [[d:druid#1467924402665558118]].
- **Defense:** Greater Stoneshield Potion (Black Morass pulls), Limited Invulnerability Potion, Free Action Potion [[d:druid#1441393322869395496]], [[d:druid#1461753276017152021]].

## Dungeon and raid notes

- **[Black Morass](../../instances/black-morass.md):** Hibernate the extra adds in 3- and 4-mob packs (they apply −50% armor, bleeds or stuns if left up) and Barkskin before the bridge. The final boss has the most armor of any NPC and hits very hard [[d:druid#1460287082483421340]], [[d:druid#1460298386128502868]], [[d:druid#1441367430122311740]].
- **[Kara10](../../instances/lower-karazhan-halls.md) Clawlord Howlfang:** his stacking debuff strips all your armor. Let a DPS soak the first stacks, take over near 10 stacks, or bring a warrior off-tank [[d:druid#1441006493179838486]], [[d:druid#1441009126829457408]].
- **Incantagos:** pre-HoT, FF + Roar on pull, Rage Potion, spam Roar until the first two adds die; do not let warriors Bloodrage before you have aggro [[d:druid#1462004193492467736]], [[d:druid#1462017835705307148]].
- **Twin Emperors:** Vek'nilash's Unbalancing Strike cuts 100 defense and causes crushing blows; do not Roar or Swipe the neutral scorpions [[d:druid#1451947426381435022]], [[d:druid#1451950951005945990]].
- **Nefarian** is tankable with a Stoneshield Potion and the ZG trinket; expect spikes on Shadowflame [[d:druid#1469029114102157556]].
- **Four Horsemen:** spell hit for Taunt (Nat Pagle's Broken Reel) [[d:druid#1464013456167669915]].
- **Mephistroth** (Kara40): full boss uptime is hard because of Hellfire; bears still open with the 8-piece set [[d:druid#1492179014100451338]], [[d:druid#1492174032349696231]].

## Common mistakes

1. Opening a pull with Growl, or with Feral Charge for threat [[d:druid#1432722952813543505]], [[d:druid#1433148306586075292]].
2. Using Demoralizing Roar with a warrior in the group [[d:druid#1415743918603243622]].
3. Stacking dodge or defense enchants [[d:druid#1415743918603243622]].
4. Using armor kits: the form does not multiply them [[d:druid#1425973110548463647]].
5. Throwing sappers in Bear Form: they cancel the form [[d:druid#1473126693303615643]].
6. Enraging while healers struggle [[d:druid#1477368423267696690]].
7. Asking healers to shield you; the shield removes your rage from damage taken [[d:druid#1491140379490390106]].
8. Dropping hit below the cap for stats [[d:druid#1491029884397424720]].
