# Druid professions

**Recommendation:** start with **two gathering professions**, because crafting is a money sink early on [[d:druid#1482785431626977433]]. **Herbalism** is the druid's natural gathering skill: you can pick herbs without leaving (Swift) Travel Form [[d:druid#1493356486368170186]], [[d:druid#1306462082107183164]]. At 60, most druids run **Herbalism + Alchemy**, which saves gold on consumables and gives healers their mana potions [[d:druid#1435986342378213439]], [[d:druid#1461063036465840300]]. **Engineering** is the only profession with unique raid tools, and it suits **Balance and Restoration**. It is a poor fit for **ferals**: bombs and sappers do not work in Cat or Bear Form [[d:druid#1485983739757592748]], [[d:druid#1480873418554347611]]. No profession has a must-have druid-only BiS item, so beyond Engineering, choose for gold or convenience [[d:druid#1307438213043847258]], [[d:druid#1435986342378213439]].

## What each profession gives a druid

### Herbalism

- Herb from Travel or Swift Travel Form without shifting out [[d:druid#1493356486368170186]]. "Dual box herbing is my most gold/hour" [[d:druid#1424684219556171828]].
- You can track only one node type at a time, so Herbalism + Mining means switching tracking constantly [[d:druid#1424684566953853009]].
- Herbs keep their value because every flask needs a Black Lotus [[d:druid#1446671350386262180]]. Low-level herbs are "crazy cheap", though; copper from Mining earns more early [[d:druid#1446669772224860221]].
- **Contested:** in January 2026, one veteran dropped Herbalism on two characters. By then "every herb is a couple of silver, and Black Lotus is 18g with 4% drop chance" [[d:druid#1458796043293691984]], [[d:druid#1458842065248190618]]. By April, Dreamfoil and Golden Sansam were "back to like 1g each" [[d:druid#1491445833730883775]]. Check your server's prices.

### Alchemy

- The raid-consumable profession. It "stays valuable/relevant untill lategame" [[d:druid#1472200959798022301]], and healers get mana potions from it [[d:druid#1461063036465840300]].
- Alchemy (or Leatherworking) gives you daily cooldowns and cheaper self-made consumables [[d:druid#1458793391675674717]].
- **Swift Travel Form** needs Greater Dreamless Sleep Potions among its materials. The whole questline costs about 300-600g in items [[d:druid#1328518855823462525]], [[d:druid#1346277171613925477]].

### Engineering

- It is the one profession with unique raid tools, and some call it "the best profession by a mile" [[d:druid#1435986342378213439]], [[d:druid#1485983739757592748]].
- **Forms:** Engineering items work in Moonkin and Tree Form, not in Cat or Bear Form [[d:druid#1480873418554347611]]. A grenade thrown in Bear Form does nothing, although the patch notes claimed otherwise [[d:druid#1431618759629733889]]. Sappers take you out of Bear Form [[d:druid#1473126693303615643]]. **Contested:** one feral says "there is alot of good stuff you can use in and out of form", with only sappers needing you to leave form [[d:druid#1444227823874347118]].
- For ferals, the use is grenades on the pull and the **Gnomish Battle Chicken** haste buff for you and your party [[d:druid#1433909867701080085]], [[d:druid#1431553731278667906]]. Hardcore players value its bombs and target dummies [[d:druid#1438080912117071985]].

### Leatherworking

- One player calls it "the most valuable for us, regardless of spec" [[d:druid#1424489158289784974]]. The later consensus is that it has little late-game value and makes little gold. **Tribal** is the pick for convenient gear and kits while leveling and early at 60 [[d:druid#1465675429674483743]], [[d:druid#1465675579037585500]].
- The leather gear you can craft from your own kills runs "always about 15 levels below you" [[d:druid#1456849610969710622]]. Casters get little from it until around level 32 [[d:druid#1441124577198739679]].
- **Elemental Leatherworking** (a Turtle specialisation) crafts the **Convergence of the Elements** leggings, which are "extra good". The recipe is not trivial to get [[d:druid#1491826525689872485]], [[d:druid#1491827014405980191]]. Cat players also rate the crafted **Flamewrath Leggings** "by alot" the best piece of their set [[d:druid#1430925528172462080]].
- There are BoP recipes for Emerald Sanctum pieces [[d:druid#1486069812726595676]]. Swift Travel Form also needs Dreamhide, a pattern many consider otherwise useless [[d:druid#1328518855823462525]].
- **Buy instead:** Devilsaur leather sells well at first, but the market crashes fast; flipping the raw leather beat crafting it [[d:druid#1434884478261792831]], [[d:druid#1434884861717516348]]. Devilsaur Leggings are the only Devilsaur piece cats rate [[d:druid#1397899404039426151]].

### Jewelcrafting (Turtle-custom)

- Jewelcrafters can enchant rings and necklaces [[d:druid#1491824430819573891]]. Anyone can apply these enchants for you, as with Enchanting [[d:druid#1482117186637008966]].
- Mining + Jewelcrafting gets you "a few BoP BiS rings" [[d:druid#1458793391675674717]]. Resto can buy a crafted healer ring instead; it takes a +12 healing enchant [[d:druid#1456478102682341539]], [[d:druid#1473322069809959114]].
- Among specialisations, "it's gemology all the way"; nobody reported anything positive about Goldsmithing (single source) [[d:druid#1417471465657204808]].
- Leveling it takes a lot of copper; you need Mining or must buy the ore on the AH [[d:druid#1438265182420861204]], [[d:druid#1479841263485456505]].

### Blacksmithing

- Blacksmiths make **belt buckles** [[d:druid#1491824487942062091]]. Ferals use the +10 Strength buckle [[d:druid#1443537204717551616]]. For Resto, the 8 healing power buckle is "like 50% better" than the 10 Intellect one [[d:druid#1475237840618258546]]. You can buy the buckles; you do not need the profession.
- A Blacksmithing trinket gives fear immunity for feral PvP (single source) [[d:druid#1487503178571321416]].

### Tailoring and Enchanting

- **Tailoring:** Gloves of Spell Mastery last a Balance druid until AQ40 (pre-1.18.1 list) [[d:druid#1317869755134251009]]. Dreamthread for Swift Travel Form is another Tailoring pattern few people learn [[d:druid#1328518855823462525]].
- **Enchanting:** a steady leveling income, because you can disenchant outgrown gear at any skill level [[d:druid#1442170706799497227]]. Enchanting + Jewelcrafting is also suggested as a pair [[d:druid#1458794241340735549]].

### Survival and Gardening (secondary professions)

- **Before 1.18.1:** Survival tents gave rested XP. One player called tent-camping the fastest way to level [[d:druid#1457170124162207807]]. Resto players "tree-punched" for mana with a Survival lantern and the BoED dagger: the lantern's melee zap triggered the dagger's shadow damage and mana return. The lantern was a high-level Survival craft ("125 or so iirc") [[d:druid#1484001274214224045]], [[d:druid#1484000652387680387]]. The official list describes the **Repaired Electro-Lantern** as "+4 Spirit ... adds a zap to melee attacks" ([official site, Survival & Gardening](https://turtlecraft.gg/survival-and-gardening)).
- **1.18.1 rework:** Survival got 80+ new recipes. These include slowing, rooting and trapping tools, bags for the three gathering professions, and Woodcutting ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)). A skill-300 crafted sickle raises the chance of finding Black Lotus, which matters to herbalists ([Birtha (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171974#p171974)). Tents are now capped at 25% rested ([Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168)).
- The rework reset progress. After 1.18.1, a feral farming the dagger said he now had to "level survival to get the ton berry lantern", and was told that leveling Survival "is worse than it was before" [[d:druid#1488636746320056483]], [[d:druid#1488636882920411228]].
- **Gardening:** the druid channel says nothing druid-specific about it. See the [Professions overview](../../professions.md).

## When to pick up and swap

1. **Level 1-60:** two gathering professions, such as Herbalism + Skinning or Herbalism + Mining [[d:druid#1482785431626977433]], [[d:druid#1473698687183097857]]. If you take Leatherworking for kits and armor, plan to drop it later [[d:druid#1472200959798022301]]. For a leveling bear, Leatherworking against Alchemy makes a "negligible difference" [[d:druid#1486069812726595676]], [[d:druid#1486083289310826577]].
2. **At 60:** keep Herbalism and add **Alchemy**. On Balance or Resto, take **Engineering** instead if you want its raid tools [[d:druid#1453179590804504667]], [[d:druid#1480873418554347611]].
3. Run cooldown crafts such as Mooncloth, Arcanite Bar and Cured Rugged Hide on alts rather than your main [[d:druid#1490407616311070720]], [[d:druid#1472200959798022301]].

No skill thresholds are stated in the druid sources.

## Race interactions

Only Tauren and Night Elves can be druids. Tauren have the Cultivation racial (vanilla baseline), which one player wanted to use by taking Herbalism + Alchemy [[d:druid#1093309536003174464]]. A joke claims Tauren females and Night Elves are "bis" for ZG mining runs [[d:druid#1482376306158075995]]. The sources name no other race-profession interaction for druids. Racials are covered in the [Class overview](index.md#races-and-racials).

## Gold

- Gathering professions pay very well on Nordanaar [[d:druid#1429759714417443017]].
- **ZG mining runs in Stealth** at 60 are "a decent gold source" [[d:druid#1479837392578941039]], [[d:druid#1482375290112966747]].
- Druids "can't cheese much content". Un'Goro fire elementals are a workable general-purpose farm. Dire Maul lashers are immune to Nature damage, so Thorns and Moonfire do not work on them [[d:druid#1487293647866040403]], [[d:druid#1487294040054431765]].
- Herbing lashers has no competition, but a druid does it much worse than a hunter, mage or paladin [[d:druid#1491446715775979540]], [[d:druid#1491446971364544684]].
- For steady income, keep cooldown alts (a claimed ~80g/day from 10 alts), and add Fishing [[d:druid#1489268439251030107]], [[d:druid#1471926560390119557]].
