# Restoration (healer)

**Recommendation:** play the **18/0/33 Tree of Life build** (Moonglow, Preservation, Swiftmend, Nature's Swiftness, Improved Tranquility). Stack **healing power**, then Spirit. Pre-HoT the tank, keep **Rejuvenation** and **Regrowth** rolling on whoever is taking damage (Rejuv first, so Preservation boosts the Regrowth), finish expiring HoTs with **Swiftmend**, and hold **Nature's Swiftness + Healing Touch** and **Tranquility** for emergencies. A raid wants **one resto druid, two at most**.

Why:

- **Moonglow** reduces the mana cost of Rejuvenation, Regrowth and Healing Touch. **Tranquil Spirit** only reduces Tranquility's cost, not Rejuvenation's [[d:druid#1489735161674469550]], [[d:druid#1495007642379227300]].
- **Preservation** only works if Rejuvenation is on the target **before** Regrowth [[d:druid#1479916881719590912]]. It enables the cheap "low-rank Rejuv, then Regrowth" combo [[d:druid#1464353488849993883]].
- **Swiftmend** consumes whichever of your Rejuvenation or Regrowth has the fewest ticks left, even a low-rank HoT from another druid [[d:druid#1476571037243019437]], [[d:druid#1431102498898186371]].
- **Tree of Life Form** gives the Spirit-based healing aura. Since 1.18.1 the aura updates from your current Spirit instead of snapshotting [[d:druid#1476011073952940083]]. You cannot cast Healing Touch in Tree Form [[d:druid#1463955073251020801]].
- **Omen of Clarity** is **contested** for healers: it procs only from direct casts, not HoT ticks, and is hard to proc in Tree Form [[d:druid#1463959311301480509]], [[d:druid#1491888935587021023]]. Hassle calls it a wasted point [[d:druid#1492160723349012490]]; others use Omen procs as their main mana tool [[d:druid#1483994803665502450]].

## Stat priority

1. **Healing power** is king [[d:druid#1437429730700820692]]. If you mostly spam Rejuvenation, more healing power beats crit and haste, which do nothing for HoTs [[d:druid#1471588934378066002]].
2. **Spirit:** worth about 0.2 healing power for yourself, or about 1 healing power if your aura buffs 5 healers in your group [[d:druid#1480623914840359156]]. It also regenerates mana: druid MP2 = Spirit/5 + 15 [[d:druid#1450410384627601429]].
3. **MP5**: better than Spirit for non-Tree Nature's Grace builds [[d:druid#1437429730700820692]].
4. **Intellect:** about half a healing power; the weakest healing stat [[d:druid#1480623914840359156]].

Scaling (with talents): Regrowth ≈ 20% on the direct heal + ≈ 112% on the HoT (≈ 132% total); Rejuvenation ≈ 101%; Healing Touch ≈ 110% [[d:druid#1469614655952388199]], [[d:druid#1469610624022483035]]. Only direct heals crit; HoTs never do, and haste does not speed up HoT ticks [[d:druid#1442857322341863544]].

**Healing power you need** (one experienced healer's guide): 0 in full blues for MC/ZG/AQ20/Kara10; ~0-300 for BWL; 900+ for Kara40 (he runs 1,600) [[d:druid#1474887980916146206]], [[d:druid#1474911419446067312]].

## Healing rotation

**Before the pull:** Rejuvenation, then Regrowth, on the tank [[d:druid#1428335061941358592]].

Priority (bot-ready):

1. **Emergency (tank below ~30%)**: **`Nature's Swiftness` → max-rank `Healing Touch`** (two key presses; the client cannot combine them in one macro click) [[d:druid#1474354456631050323]]. Or **`Swiftmend`** on a target with a HoT.
2. **`Swiftmend`** on a target whose Rejuvenation or Regrowth is about to expire: "insane healing per mana" [[d:druid#1380837659353546782]].
3. **`Rejuvenation`** on a damaged target without it, before Regrowth (Preservation) [[d:druid#1479916881719590912]].
4. **`Regrowth`** on the tank and on spiky damage. Micro Jackson's split is 60-70% Regrowth [[d:druid#1475240403237081128]]: "Sometimes 20 seconds of HOT is way too long and you're better off with Rejuv. Sometimes damage is spiky and then Regrowth is better" [[d:druid#1471593197053546772]].
5. **Do not HoT full-health targets**: faster healers overwrite the need [[d:druid#1428335061941358592]].
6. **`Tranquility`** when the whole raid is low (30-minute cooldown; about 100k healing per cast) [[d:druid#1487184318668345544]]. You must leave Tree Form to cast it [[d:druid#1360346299471495358]].

**Ranks** (from the pinned guide) [[d:druid#1480084094653169664]]:

| Spell | Rank | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Regrowth | 9 | Tanks and melee |
| Regrowth | 4 | Casters, other healers, pets |
| Rejuvenation | 11 (max) | Default |
| Rejuvenation | 5 | On rogues and ferals, to proc the T3 2-piece bonus |
| Rejuvenation | 1 | On yourself with the T3 2-piece: about 180 mana per 12 s for one GCD [[d:druid#1487133261129060423]] |

- Early in raiding, Regrowth ranks 3/6/9; with better gear 4/7/9. Rank 3 is close to the most mana-efficient [[d:druid#1438994301458841723]].
- Avoid ranks below level 20: they take a healing-power penalty; rank 4+ is usually better [[d:druid#1457650046412787816]].
- On trash use lower ranks; on bosses, with good gear, higher ranks are fine [[d:druid#1430089372677443626]].
- Max-rank Healing Touch is actually better value per mana than HoTs at typical gear; HoTs pull ahead only at endgame healing power and cost reductions [[d:druid#1484189596945223801]].

**Two resto druids:** coordinate. One tree gives the full aura value; a second gives none and overlaps HoTs, so split the work (one Rejuv-only, one Regrowth-only) [[d:druid#1435992271068594188]]. When another druid's HoT replaces yours, your old HoT turns into an instant heal for 50% of its remaining value [[d:druid#1490124201317896244]]. **Contested:** whether that Efflorescence effect needs Tree Form: "works in any form" [[d:druid#1490124547343515648]] vs "locked to Tree of Life form" [[d:druid#1467257280880971900]]. Four resto druids in one raid is "griefing" [[d:druid#1487184546595209217]].

## Mana and cooldowns

- **Innervate:** at about 600 Spirit it gives 100% mana regen for 15 s [[d:druid#1465410896120643595]]. Swap to a Spirit weapon (e.g. Staff of the Dreamer) just before self-Innervate for more mana [[d:druid#1464971421745807653]].
- **Trash** costs more mana than bosses; with the T3.5 40% Meditation pieces you can spam max ranks on bosses [[d:druid#1430089372677443626]].
- Items that give "% of Spirit regen while casting" keep regen going inside the 5-second rule [[d:druid#1472451410561073319]].
- **Abolish Poison** beats Cure Poison for the same mana; it re-dispels every 2 s [[d:druid#1440953305391173723]].
- **Tranquility** can top 300k healing with T3.5, ~2,400 healing power and an Anomalous Bomb; its 30-minute cooldown means 1-2 casts per raid [[d:druid#1476245899612131513]]. Sapphiron's aura ticks interrupt it [[d:druid#1302718473369030768]].

### Trinkets and weapons

- **Eye of the Dead + Breath of Solnius** is a BiS pair; Eye of the Dead does not consume Rejuvenation [[d:druid#1487812496101347429]]. Breath of Solnius suits Regrowth spam on Kruul, Anomalus, Chess, Sapphiron, Thaddius and Gothik [[d:druid#1483107829190496400]]; its uptime is ~35-40% [[d:druid#1487819319336243352]].
- **Warmth of Forgiveness** only as an emergency mana tool once potions and tea are used [[d:druid#1487821433408065556]].
- The **Loop of Infused Renewal** ring (a free Rejuvenation on Healing Touch) suits the HT build: with full T2.5 it gives Healing Touch "a huge boost" [[d:druid#1465111200198164624]], [[d:druid#1465111508617920737]].
- **Beacon of the Emerald Dream** is a good mid-tier staff [[d:druid#1481650793852567647]]. The Grandstaff of the Shendralar does not proc from HoT ticks (bug) [[d:druid#1469260707819425924]].

### Idols

All resto idols are "mediocre/bad" [[d:druid#1421550248354123787]]: Idol of Health (faster Healing Touch; AQ40) [[d:druid#1491894147576107130]], Idol of Longevity (+25 mana per HT), Idol of the Forgotten Wilds (a weak plant heal), Idol of Rejuvenation (+50 to Rejuv). The random "sproutling" heal idol averaged about 100 extra healing per Regrowth in Naxx, mostly overhealing [[d:druid#1432093777656549547]].

- Dreamtonic, Greater Arcane Elixir and the damage flask do **not** add healing power [[d:druid#1491527711003246766]], [[d:druid#1491539290919014541]].

## Addons

- **Puppeteer** raid frames show HoT timers and aggro; after the patch it broke Swiftmend for some until they restarted the client and updated addons [[d:druid#1439057744069136515]], [[d:druid#1486088867739336724]]. **Luna** frames are the alternative [[d:druid#1476703964144730213]].
- In pfUI, enable Unit Frames → Raid → Buff/Debuff Indicators → "Show Time Left"; showing timers on other players may need SuperWoW [[d:druid#1492834230927884419]].
- On buffed tanks your HoTs may be active but invisible past the visible buff cap; track by cast, not by buff [[d:druid#1481258380642353162]].
- HealBot disables its bars in Tree Form unless you patch `HealBot_GetShapeshiftForm()` [[d:druid#1402661674158129222]]. A Smartbuff fork works with Tree Form [[d:druid#1460652143802454178]].

## Raid notes

- **MC** has too little sustained damage for trees to shine; resto gains on paladins from BWL onward [[d:druid#1438997763336700108]].
- **Thaddius:** use speed boots so you can make the jump without leaving Tree Form [[d:druid#1475505763278393526]].
- **Knockbacks** (Magma Surgers, Rupturan adds) cause a 1-2 s movement lock in Tree Form, though you can still cast [[d:druid#1476534754906996786]].
- **Nefarian's druid call** forces Cat Form; you can shift back to Tree during it [[d:druid#1485995298831597688]].
- **Kara40 Anomalus:** priests' Prayer of Healing is the "cheat skill" there, comparable to Tranquility [[d:druid#1492253311011655842]], [[d:druid#1492253519921549393]].
- **Timbermaw Hold portal boss:** druids are immune to the stacking Portal debuff, so guilds send a druid inside [[d:druid#1490024687260926023]].

## Dungeon healing

Both builds heal every 5-man; the Healing Touch build stays viable there [[d:druid#1455277051883028721]]. Tree Form lacks a strong repeatable direct heal for an undergeared tank. While leveling you need no resto talents to heal dungeons (see [Leveling](leveling.md)).

## Common mistakes

1. Bringing more than two resto druids without assigning HoT duties [[d:druid#1487184546595209217]].
2. Casting Regrowth before Rejuvenation, which wastes Preservation [[d:druid#1479916881719590912]].
3. Only ever casting one HoT; Rejuv and Regrowth solve different problems [[d:druid#1471593197053546772]].
4. HoTing full-health targets [[d:druid#1428335061941358592]].
5. Expecting Tranquil Spirit to make Rejuvenation cheaper [[d:druid#1489735161674469550]].
6. Taking Intellect over healing power; Intellect is the weakest healing stat [[d:druid#1437429730700820692]].
7. Keeping the T3.5 5-piece for Blooming Bud after the nerf [[d:druid#1484581881180848279]].
8. Skipping Brilliant Wizard Oil because the character sheet does not show it as healing [[d:druid#1491497781368717581]].
