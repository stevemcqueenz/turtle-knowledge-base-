# Leveling a druid (1-60)

**Recommendation:** level as **Feral**. Take **Feral Swiftness** first for cat movement speed, then at **level 30** (Travel Form) **respec into Omen of Clarity** and continue down the Feral tree; take Furor last. "You can tank every leveling dungeon in this spec. If you equip int/spirit gear you can also heal them just fine" (the pinned, dev-endorsed advice) [[d:druid#1413144334336790652]], [[d:druid#1477056404576665650]]. Balance is a viable, slower alternative. Do not level as Restoration: "Leveling as resto sucks" [[d:druid#1412860092692299806]].

| Spec | Leveling | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Feral** | **S** | Near-zero downtime, movement speed, tanks every dungeon [[d:druid#1427651224240652298]], [[d:druid#1460978795996647568]]; druid leveling is A-tier overall, "right behind hunter and warlock" [[d:druid#1487165021715697816]] |
| **Balance** | **B** | Fun, strong with Moonkin Form + Hurricane at 30 [[d:druid#1473015992664850472]], but mana downtime: roughly half the speed of Feral per one player [[d:druid#1487860224646840442]], "never faster than feral" [[d:druid#1433192801616662620]] |
| **Restoration** | **C** | Much slower; every spec heals dungeons with an Intellect set in the bags [[d:druid#1433843338326442117]], [[d:druid#1475768331045703711]] |

**At 60, respec** into the [Feral Cat](feral-cat.md) raid build (Feral Aggression 4 and Improved Shred instead of Feral Instinct, Feral Swiftness and Feral Charge) or the [Feral Bear](feral-bear.md) build (Thick Hide and Improved Shred instead of Open Wounds and Feral Swiftness). Order variants:

- **Omen first** if you do mostly dungeons or duo with a class that has no early movement speed; the pin allows it [[d:druid#1413144334336790652]]. **Contested:** one test at level 34 found full Feral 89 DPS vs Omen+Feral 78 DPS, but the Omen build had less downtime [[d:druid#1484362464949960935]]. Hotverdomme-Nord goes Improved Wrath, then straight to Omen by ~20 if dungeoning [[d:druid#1491894828227887204]].
- **Sylvan Blessing** is **contested** for feral: "better than nothing" (Micro Jackson) vs skip it, since you regen mana in form anyway (Shardphoenix) [[d:druid#1491813464434413568]], [[d:druid#1491816026214633512]]. Improved Nature's Grasp 4/4 is the alternative filler.
- **Furor earlier** if you like it; it removes the energy-starved pauses [[d:druid#1491229947518390422]]. With the Wolfshead helm enchant (from 55) Furor matters less [[d:druid#1486578769077473405]].
- **Hardcore:** take the raid tank build straight away for safety [[d:druid#1429398197163397252]], or go Balance first for Nature's Grasp and Sylvan Blessing [[d:druid#1324840925905682477]].

## The kill loop (Cat)

Bot-ready, for a single mob:

1. From range, optionally **`Moonfire`** or **`Insect Swarm`** to pull (Insect Swarm is cheaper and lowers the mob's hit chance) [[d:druid#1427914229494710423]].
2. **`Prowl`** → **`Tiger's Fury`** → **`Pounce`** (once learned) → **`Faerie Fire (Feral)`** → **`Rake`** [[d:druid#1489369792446988350]].
3. **`Claw`** until 1-2 combo points on a fast-dying mob, or to 5 on a tougher one, then **`Rip`** [[d:druid#1488330626565144576]], [[d:druid#1489369901381718197]].
4. **`Claw`** to 5 combo points; **`Ferocious Bite`** if it will finish the mob, otherwise keep Clawing [[d:druid#1508522976016859156]]. At low level ignore combo points: Rake + Claw, since Bite costs too much energy [[d:druid#1467987874593898496]].
5. **Behind the mob** (grouped), use **`Shred`** instead of Claw [[d:druid#1489369901381718197]].
6. **Bleed-immune mob:** Shred + Ferocious Bite at 5 combo points [[d:druid#1467979995988361497]].
7. **Low HP after the fight:** shift out, **`Rejuvenation`/`Regrowth`**, shift back. Nature's Grasp is rarely needed [[d:druid#1491814814404710541]].

**Two or three mobs:** pull with a DoT, shift to **Bear**, **Swipe** and tab-Maul ([Chev03, 2026-01-21](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=165791#p165791)). Bear is safer, cat is quicker [[d:druid#1294200215435219027]]. Feral has no reliable slow for fleeing mobs: use Bash, Feral Charge, or kill them fast [[d:druid#1442171109985357835]].

**Downtime:** you regenerate mana in form, so heal, shift back and keep going [[d:druid#1491816026214633512]]. Sylvan Blessing's 15 s of full regen after a killing blow also works in form [[d:druid#1468665948134310054]], [[d:druid#1468666399382831310]].

## The kill loop (Balance)

1. Open with **`Starfire`**, then **`Wrath`, `Wrath`** [[d:druid#1483651737389891746]].
2. On harder mobs: Starfire → Moonfire → **`Entangling Roots`** → Insect Swarm, let the DoTs tick, finish with Wrath or Starfire. Recast Moonfire only on an Omen proc [[d:druid#1315032628642189443]].
3. Use rank-1 Entangling Roots and rank-1 Moonfire for mana efficiency while farming [[d:druid#1483713212246523945]].
4. Melee between casts with Omen of Clarity and Natural Weapons to regain mana [[d:druid#1361762103790407981]].
5. With 2/2 Sylvan Blessing and 1/3 Owlkin Frenzy you "never go OoM" while farming open-world mobs [[d:druid#1484236587574493184]].

Before level 30 a Balance leveler still swaps to Cat or Bear when mana runs low [[d:druid#1492652781780074607]].

## Stats and gear by bracket

- **Feral:** weapon damage, speed and chance-on-hit do not matter in form; only the weapon's stats do [[d:druid#1424255168232423565]]. Agility and Strength are worth about the same while leveling [[d:druid#1447703890131947681]]. "Of the Monkey" usually beats "of the Tiger" unless the Tiger item is much higher level [[d:druid#1480184723455348736]]. Gear barely matters; one player used a level-40s stat stick at 59 [[d:druid#1491889189229170780]].
- **Balance:** Intellect first while questing outdoors on Hardcore; Stamina is good; spell power appears on gear around level 30. Armor matters more once you have Moonkin Form [[d:druid#1486685983071010826]]. "Of the Eagle" items are great [[d:druid#1492652781780074607]].

## Swift Travel Form (level 60)

A 100% speed outdoor travel form, separate from normal Travel Form. It has a 3 s cast, no combat or indoor use, and mount-speed items do not affect it, so it is slower than an enchanted mount [[d:druid#1430979926915547146]], [[d:druid#1472831919191359578]]. It also unlocks a stag glyph for normal Travel Form, lets you carry a party member ("Ride of the Wild"), and lets you gather herbs without shifting [[d:druid#1430981583191670946]], [[d:druid#1493356486368170186]]. It cannot carry the WSG flag [[d:druid#1328807349040975996]].

Questline (dev-posted, 2025-01) [[d:druid#1325097793362071574]], with difficulty notes [[d:druid#1336462815904534630]]:

1. **Mount Hyjal In Turmoil:** speak with Loganaar in Moonglade, travel to Nordanaar. Easy.
2. **Shadowed Spectre:** investigate west of Darkhollow Pass, Hyjal. Easy.
3. **The Son of Cenarius:** 20 Bright Dream Shards to Keeper Remulos, western Moonglade. Shards drop from satyrs near Nordanaar (shared loot) [[d:druid#1484218105428443340]].
4. **Tethered Memories:** Bough of Cenarius (Dreamstalker, Bough Shadow, Ashenvale), Crystalline Ray of Moonlight (Venethas, cave north of Everlook), Jade Scale of the Dreamer (Solnius, [Emerald Sanctum](../../instances/emerald-sanctum.md) raid). The Emerald Sanctum must be open on your realm [[d:druid#1434329117653729431]].
5. **The White Stag:** kill the Spectre (~150k HP, ~3,000 melee hits, a long-range Shadow Bolt Volley, a knockback near a cliff, an AoE fear). Bring ~8-12 players and a Tremor Totem, and drag it uphill away from the cliff [[d:druid#1308406716991541249]], [[d:druid#1479521428821905470]].
6. **Woven Dreams:** 10 Viridian Mushrooms (Jademir Lake, Feralas), 10 Dreamhide, 6 Dreamthread, 20 Greater Dreamless Sleep Potions. The expensive step: about 300-600g, cheaper with guild crafters [[d:druid#1328518855823462525]].
7. **The Eternal Sleeper:** Seradane, Hinterlands. Hits very hard; kite with a friend [[d:druid#1336462886272368722]].
8. **Under the Vibrant Moonlight:** Twilight Grove, Duskwood. Root and nuke from range with a group [[d:druid#1336462886272368722]].

**Solo dragon challenge (Rethevus / Dreamer's Challenge, Moonglade):** by 2026 players describe a solo fight in this chain [[d:druid#1486436692310360248]], [[d:druid#1483917303749021848]]. Three methods work:

- **Caster (easiest):** Sleep the helper add (it makes the dragon CC-immune), Root the dragon, line-of-sight its faerie, and kill the darter adds while standing on them for health and mana. Dispel poison and curse at once (the curse stuns for ~8 s). **Never use DoTs.** Nuke with ~6 Wraths only while it sleeps after an enrage, then re-root and re-sleep [[d:druid#1428350646100426803]], [[d:druid#1406589702651379805]]. Expect about 10-14 minutes, 2 Innervates and 2 mana potions [[d:druid#1483917303749021848]], [[d:druid#1415319581186330736]].
- **Rank-1 Wrath ticks:** root, sleep and chip it with rank-1 Wraths [[d:druid#1483858112401244302]].
- **Bear:** face-tank it with ~10% lifesteal gear, Carnage and a stack of Poison Resistance elixirs (or a shaman's Poison Cleansing Totem) [[d:druid#1407500471509319833]], [[d:druid#1483860000827904020]].

**Ride of the Wild bug:** to be carried, the passenger must put **you** on their Ignore list [[d:druid#1482646024085442671]], [[d:druid#1478841425289543862]]. A passenger takes full fall damage [[d:druid#1475423483113635861]].

## Professions and gold

- Take **two gathering professions** first for gold [[d:druid#1482785431626977433]]. Enchanting (disenchant everything) is also good leveling money [[d:druid#1442170706799497227]].
- Herbalism + Alchemy is a common pair (consumables; herb from Swift Travel Form) [[d:druid#1493356486368170186]]. Skinning + Leatherworking lets you craft armor and kits early [[d:druid#1458793571388887234]]. Leatherworking has little for casters until ~32 [[d:druid#1441124577198739679]].
- Engineering explosives and sappers cannot be used in Cat or Bear Form [[d:druid#1473126693303615643]], [[d:druid#1480873418554347611]].
- **Rested XP:** Survival-profession tents give constant rested XP [[d:druid#1457170124162207807]].
- Farming at 60: Winterspring furbolgs as Balance (~60g/hour; spec `talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=oCaAYCFYJ--FAFAAb`, [Open in talent calculator](https://xian55.github.io/tortoise-db-viewer/?talents=druid&t=500232003025311--50050033)) [[d:druid#1487355446288449566]]; ZG mining with stealth [[d:druid#1479837392578941039]]; Devilsaur leather farmed in Bear Form, though the market crashes fast [[d:druid#1434884478261792831]]. Dire Maul lashers are nature-immune, so Thorns and Moonfire do not work on them [[d:druid#1487294040054431765]].

## Common mistakes

1. Leveling as Restoration [[d:druid#1412860092692299806]].
2. Skipping Omen of Clarity because vanilla guides say it is not worth it while leveling: on Turtle it is [[d:druid#1441203667486380052]].
3. Keeping Feral Swiftness at 30 instead of respeccing into Omen [[d:druid#1413144334336790652]].
4. Buying every spell rank before 20-30 [[d:druid#1466139115409113213]].
5. Farming the Manual Crowd Pummeler for its effect, or judging weapons by DPS [[d:druid#1462513205434650823]], [[d:druid#1424255168232423565]].
6. Tanking Wailing Caverns before Swipe [[d:druid#1442259854059245718]].
7. Waiting for 5 combo points on mobs that die first [[d:druid#1488331103730139168]].
8. Buying the Devilsaur set; it is quickly replaced [[d:druid#1418651437164007614]].
