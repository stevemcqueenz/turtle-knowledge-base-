# Shaman

Shamans on Turtle WoW 1.18.1 are Horde-only (Orc, Troll, Tauren) [[d:shaman#1357283814270963743]], [[d:shaman#1347280969320759306]] and fill every role from three trees:

- **Enhancement** is the melee DPS spec and the easiest way into a raid, because its Windfury, Strength of Earth and Grace of Air totems are the best melee buffs in the game [[d:shaman#1474284705997262973]], [[d:shaman#1474284837190762506]].
- **Tank** (Enhancement with shield talents, the "warden" tank) has the best snap threat of any tank and no defensive cooldown; it is the squishiest tank since 1.18.1.
- **Elemental** is a Nature caster that shines in AoE; the old "fire ele" Molten Blast spec died in 1.18.1.
- **Restoration** is a Chain-Heal and totem healer; it lost ground to Holy Priests in 1.18.1.

**New player recommendation:** level as **Enhancement** (tank-capable talents), tank or DPS your dungeons, and at 60 respec (5 g) into Enhancement DPS, Tank or Restoration. "Enhancement is the only good spec for shaman leveling if you plan to do *any* solo content" [[d:shaman#1427654966088171600]]; experienced raiders advise starting raids as Enhancement and switching to Elemental around AQ40 gear [[d:shaman#1472175286543057079]].

Earlier Turtle changes you will meet in older guides:

- **1.18 (2025-08):** Molten Blast and Earthquake added [[d:shaman#1403298839271575552]]; Purge got an 8-second cooldown, Calming Winds became 8/16/25%, Nature's Swiftness became Ancestral Swiftness [[d:shaman#1405331266064089169]]; Totemic Alignment passes ~90% of totem threat to you [[d:shaman#1405237409704317081]]; Windfury Totem and Flametongue Totem became auras ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102972#p102972)).

## Totems

### Rules

- **Unlocks:** Earth totems from the start; Fire at 10, Water at 20, Air at 30, each after a class quest [[d:shaman#1488288472379949328]].
- **No global cooldown:** a totem only locks out its own element for 1.5 s, so you can weave Tremor or cleansing totems mid-rotation or drop four at once [[d:shaman#1482118867835687032]].
- **All four on one key:** needs the Nampower client mod; then a macro with one `/cast <Totem>` line per element [[d:shaman#1488297140617875517]], [[d:shaman#1466421661237510279]]. Addons: Call of Elements (use the MarcelineVQ fork) [[d:shaman#1426570378683093204]] or Sticks and Stones [[d:shaman#1486746434249429023]].
- **Totemic Mastery** (Resto) makes beneficial totems last 20% longer [[d:shaman#1407666125650788372]].
- **Totem of the Flowing Water** (relic) gives your water totems a party effect, only in combat: Healing Stream +2% healing done/taken, Mana Spring a chance per pulse to restore 1% mana, cleansing totems +10% resist, Fire Resistance −3% fire damage [[d:shaman#1424838222546210907]], [[d:shaman#1425807929390661642]].
- Mana Spring Totem and Blessing of Wisdom stack [[d:shaman#1435354837238681620]]; resistance totems still don't stack with matching paladin auras [[d:shaman#1435344353827491910]].

### Windfury Totem mechanics and "twisting"

- Windfury Totem is an **aura**: main hand only, single rank, **1 extra attack with no bonus AP**, and a **2-second internal cooldown** (confirmed from a 200-proc log sample) [[d:shaman#1477700854071955467]], [[d:shaman#1486885483714711634]].
- It **does nothing for a shaman with a weapon imbue**; any personal imbue blocks both Windfury Totem and Flametongue Totem on you. Without an imbue, the two totems stack with each other [[d:shaman#1479014654599495820]], [[d:shaman#1476947395794436319]]. Your own Windfury Weapon (2 extra attacks, ~300 bonus AP each, no internal cooldown) is far stronger [[d:shaman#1477700854071955467]].
- **Totem twisting doesn't exist on Turtle:** because Windfury is an aura, it stops the moment you drop Grace of Air [[d:shaman#1482118867835687032]], [[d:shaman#1440812785524015145]].
- **Windfury vs Grace of Air:** "if 2 damage dealers benefit from WF, use it. Otherwise use Grace of Air" [[d:shaman#1486730676978843719]]; in a double-shaman group one drops each [[d:shaman#1457772348437692608]].
- **Flametongue Totem** adds ~15-20 DPS to a rogue or warrior and ~30 to a paladin (more Judgement of Wisdom procs) [[d:shaman#1438921706726096976]]. It briefly inherited Flametongue Weapon's new spell-power scaling in April 2026 (bug, fixed) [[d:shaman#1491212596819529851]], [[d:shaman#1494356331543396445]].

### Which totems when

| Group | Earth | Fire | Water | Air |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Melee group (default) | Strength of Earth | Flametongue | Mana Spring | Windfury (or Grace of Air, see rule) |
| Caster group | Strength of Earth or Tremor | Searing | Mana Spring | **Tranquil Air** (−20% threat, multiplicative with Salvation) [[d:shaman#1444541380910125177]], [[d:shaman#1395170289838719048]] |
| Tank, boss | Strength of Earth | Searing/Flametongue | Mana Spring | Windfury/Grace |
| Tank, trash | **Stoneclaw** (needs Totemic Alignment) | Fire Nova, then Magma if needed | as above | as above ([Tank](tank.md#aoe-tanking)) |
| Solo leveling | mostly none; Stoneclaw on multi-pulls, Earthbind vs runners | Searing | — | — [[d:shaman#1482592822082277376]] |

Situational: **Tremor** breaks fear on drop and pulses every 4 s [[d:shaman#1459821631760502889]]; **Grounding** eats one harmful spell (it works on AQ20's Cyclone and some Chess mechanics, does nothing on Twin Emperors) [[d:shaman#1479859011288436843]], [[d:shaman#1479843660471468224]]; **Poison/Disease Cleansing** when afflicted; **resistance totems** on elemental-damage fights. Don't drop Nature Resistance Totem casually: "your melees will prefer death over no windfury" [[d:shaman#1475608230116720834]]. **Stoneskin** applies before armor and is nearly useless on bosses [[d:shaman#1458137175219769549]].

## Professions

- **Level with two gatherers** (Herbalism + Mining or Skinning). At 60, end on **Engineering + Herbalism** [[d:shaman#1389424395415715890]]. Engineering is "the only one that increases dps" for any spec (Sappers, AoE threat, Force Reactive Disk) [[d:shaman#1473598065050128439]], [[d:shaman#1475212516761276660]]. Its teleporters plus Astral Recall give near-mage mobility [[d:shaman#1444613431872454716]].
- **Herbalism** pays through the Dire Maul East lasher farm, ~100 g+/hour on a busy server [[d:shaman#1433512536392601681]], [[d:shaman#1425738262923972720]]. Farming red and black Dragonscales with Skinning is the alternative [[d:shaman#1426246866986602538]].
- **Tanks:** Engineering + Jewelcrafting (BoP spell-damage items) [[d:shaman#1406797526492909569]]. Blacksmithing makes the Fiery Chain fire-resistance set [[d:shaman#1476041815240216626]], [[d:shaman#1474856625297690705]].
- **Skip for their own sake:** Alchemy has no shaman-specific perks [[d:shaman#1471890562008481905]]. Leatherworking "is kinda ass" unless you refuse to trade [[d:shaman#1462159463711506595]].

Full guide: [Professions](professions.md)

## Raid placement and utility

- Every spec ends up in a melee group for Windfury and Strength of Earth; Elemental lacks a caster-group totem of its own [[d:shaman#1483918066323558481]]. The channel's "highest rdps melee group": 2 Enhancement shamans, a two-hand Fury warrior and 2 Combat rogues [[d:shaman#1483970042243580015]].
- Every shaman brings Purge, an interrupt (Earth Shock), cleansing totems and Tremor; Restoration adds Spirit Link.
