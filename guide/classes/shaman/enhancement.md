# Enhancement (melee DPS)

**Recommendation:** play the **17/34/0 two-hander build** with **Windfury Weapon** on the slowest good two-hander you can get. Press **Stormstrike > Lightning Strike > shock** (Flame Shock, then Earth Shock, Earth Shock), keep Lightning Shield up and drop your four group totems. Cap **8% melee hit** (4% from gear after talents and the weapon-skill book), push weapon skill toward 315, then stack **Strength** (worth about twice Agility). "Spellhance" (Flametongue, spell power, 22/29) is a niche AoE variant for theorycrafters, covered below.

Why:

- **Bloodlust** was always the raid capstone: "the norm was to go bloodlust before 1.18" [[d:shaman#1485708017784852786]]. It is a 30-second self-haste window whose melee crits also give your party a short haste buff ([Dragunovi (staff), 2024-10-12](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=102972#p102972), [Torta (staff), 2025-03-30](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=124504#p124504)).
- **Enhancing Totems 2/2** over more fire talents: the Strength of Earth / Grace of Air boost is worth more to a melee group than faster Fire Nova; take Improved Fire Totems for 5-mans instead [[d:shaman#1428788267469246527]], [[d:shaman#1435009884142964746]].
- **Calming Winds 3/3**: you need the threat reduction in raids (see [Threat](#threat)). Tank-spec players who DPS miss only this and Enhancing Totems [[d:shaman#1481298646464069643]].
- **Elemental Focus** (Clearcasting) plus Convection keeps your shocks cheap; Reverberation shortens the shock cooldown.
- **Call of Flame 2** is the flex spot: Not Normal's raid alternative moves those 2 points to Improved Fire Totems, "arguable in terms of where they can go" [[d:shaman#1449124148411498560]].

## Spellhance vs classic Strength enhancement

Two Enhancement styles exist on Turtle:

| | Classic ("physhance") | Spellhance |
|---|---|---|
| Imbue | Windfury Weapon | Flametongue Weapon |
| Talents | 17/34/0 (Bloodlust) | 22/29/0 (Elemental Fury) [[d:shaman#1486379110036013087]] |
| Gear | Strength, AP, crit, hit, weapon skill | Hybrid AP + spell power items, then spell power / spell crit; phys hit capped, spell hit ≤3% from gear [[d:shaman#1445940501084901427]] |
| Rotation | Strikes first | Same buttons, but weave Fire Nova and Searing Totem in single target [[d:shaman#1485047142807634111]], [[d:shaman#1450878251953033358]] |
| Single target | Best | Never beats classic: 10-15% behind before 1.18.1, estimated 5-8% behind once the 1.18.1 fixes land [[d:shaman#1485046277111812196]], [[d:shaman#1481702238333440081]] |
| AoE | Good | Better; "will beat the shit out of physhance on total damage in Kara and ES" [[d:shaman#1481702238333440081]] |
| Raids to avoid | none | MC and BWL (many fire-immune bosses): "dogshit bad in mc/bwl" [[d:shaman#1482286791753994342]] |

Advice: play classic unless you enjoy deep theorycrafting. The self-described "resident spellhance crazy person" does "not recommend spellhance unless you're really into theorycrafting" [[d:shaman#1452097226003185835]].

Spellhance details:

- **Flametongue scaling** (1.18.1, as measured): spell-power coefficient **0.16 + 0.03 × weapon speed** (Frostbrand 0.25 + 0.04 × speed) [[d:shaman#1491208166766284860]]. This change was patch-noted but missing at launch; it landed weeks later [[d:shaman#1488705173353136340]]. Flametongue's damage is boosted by Elemental Weapons, Elemental Fury and Call of Flame; the Flametongue *totem* is not [[d:shaman#1461145861072486586]].
- **Elemental Weapons' Flametongue effect** (+10/20/30% fire totem and spell damage) now only lasts 5 seconds *after a melee hit*, to keep spell Enhancement in melee ([Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071)).
- **Stats:** for 2H spellhance 1 spell power ≈ 1 Strength (about 2.1-2.4 AP) [[d:shaman#1449465961169293506]]. Physical crit is extra valuable because Elemental Devastation procs from it; a third of crit's value comes from that proc [[d:shaman#1426218630554583101]].
- **Weapons:** "typical enh weapons do the trick. Hand of Ragnaros is the dream, I use Maul of the Redeemed Crusader, but Might of Menethil or Shar'tateth would be better" [[d:shaman#1485084222338367518]].
- **Molten Blast weaving with a slow two-hander is not a gain:** the lost swing outweighs it [[d:shaman#1470503895062155500]]. Fast one-hand builds (Hand of Edward the Odd) that weave instant Molten Blasts are a separate playstyle.
- **Rotation (Ordinary Joe):** "Earth Shock > Stormstrike > Lightning Strike, weave in fire totems as mana allows"; spell crit is very good [[d:shaman#1488601959559598201]].
- **Elemental Mastery** is "kinda a trap" for spellhance [[d:shaman#1469030523329577071]]; Elemental Fury 2/2 plus Element's Grace 5/5 does not fit in 51 points [[d:shaman#1469040484159717477]].

## Stat priority and caps

1. **Melee hit to 8%.** You have an 8% miss chance; 3% comes from talents, 1% from buying 5 weapon skill (the weapon-skill book), so **4% from gear** [[d:shaman#1482104891295141980]]. Each 5 weapon skill above 300 is 1% hit [[d:shaman#1428121232133591171]]. Flameguard Gauntlets drop the gear requirement further [[d:shaman#1437197649479733329]]. After AQ40/Naxx gear you are usually over the cap [[d:shaman#1484821791615750164]].
2. **Weapon skill to 315.** 315 removes the glancing-blow penalty; about 60% of your melee damage can glance, which makes weapon skill "ridiculously valuable up until 315, think like 14 AP at least per point" [[d:shaman#1424262640028352624]], [[d:shaman#1491837172561739907]]. Sources: the weapon-skill book, Flameguard Gauntlets (+7 two-hand axe), Ancient Jade Leggings, Orc axe racial. Ancient Jade Leggings are a more efficient source than a Towerforge mace [[d:shaman#1491837060196339722]].
3. **Strength.** 1 Strength ≈ 2.3 AP with Kings; 1 Strength ≈ 2 Agility, always: "str is almost twice as good as agi in terms of dps added per point" [[d:shaman#1461356189739778211]], [[d:shaman#1487779517429710899]]. Agility gives shamans no AP, only crit [[d:shaman#1483119159196782755]].
4. **Crit** ≈ 20-25 AP per 1% [[d:shaman#1453511632058712145]]. Around 30% unbuffed crit gives good Flurry uptime; stacking far past that costs too much Strength [[d:shaman#1445003809419034807]]. Flurry uptime runs 75-95% depending on gear [[d:shaman#1486368501240168488]].
5. **Haste** ≈ 17-18 AP per 1% in pre-Naxx gear [[d:shaman#1488973642187411602]], rising with Kiss of the Spider and the T3.5 5-piece [[d:shaman#1452099338363732142]].
6. **Attack Power** (1 AP = 1).

Low value: spell hit (about 6-8 AP per 1%) [[d:shaman#1450934429567549531]]; armor penetration (1 Strength ≈ 5 ArP) [[d:shaman#1494082990370721939]]; Intellect, Spirit.

Stat-weight tables from community sims (MC gear and BWL gear) are posted at [[d:shaman#1483649276906180628]] and [[d:shaman#1486691372466569246]]; Ordinary Joe's sim site models most set bonuses [[d:shaman#1491099460963401820]].

**Weapon speed:** always the slowest weapon at comparable quality. Stormstrike and Lightning Strike use your real weapon speed (not normalized), and AP adds AP/14 × speed to every swing, so "slower is just always better" [[d:shaman#1469420065966264392]], [[d:shaman#1460368788146098422]]. A 3.8-speed 61 DPS Unstoppable Force beats a 3.3-speed 78 DPS Aspect of Seradane for this reason [[d:shaman#1460369455774437643]].

## Single-target rotation

**Pre-pull:** Windfury Weapon on the weapon, Lightning Shield up (9 charges with Stable Shields), totems down: Strength of Earth, Windfury, Flametongue, Mana Spring (see [Totems](#totems-for-enhancement)).

Priority (Not Normal: "stormstrike > lightning strike > flame shock > earth shock" [[d:shaman#1450924897978023976]]):

1. **Stormstrike** on cooldown. Let your first white swing land on the boss before the first Stormstrike, or a Windfury proc from it can eat your opening auto [[d:shaman#1434594354118524958]].
2. **Lightning Strike** on cooldown with Lightning Shield up. It outdamages either shock and can proc Windfury [[d:shaman#1450925139993559211]].
3. **Shock** on cooldown as one loop: **Flame Shock → Earth Shock → Earth Shock**, repeat, "in all circumstances" [[d:shaman#1489381819735605390]]; Stormstrike before Earth Shock always [[d:shaman#1489380594012918013]]. Flame Shock is also your filler GCD while running in.
   - Spend Stormstrike's charges on Earth Shock, not on shield procs: its "+25% to the next two Nature damage sources" only boosts the Lightning Shield part of Lightning Strike [[d:shaman#1470451742012473396]], [[d:shaman#1486003218008899637]]; an Earth Shock is "way better" use [[d:shaman#1451909730426687579]].
   - Skip Flame Shock on fire-immune targets; use Frost Shock only to lower threat or against Nature-immune targets [[d:shaman#1426778186393583666]].
4. **Auto-attack** in between; use a `/startattack` macro so autos never stop [[d:shaman#1447377019469041694]], [[d:shaman#1447377748460044421]].
5. **Lightning Shield / Water Shield:** Lightning Shield on bosses (with Judgement of Wisdom for mana), Water Shield when mana is short or on trash [[d:shaman#1457507332471783485]]. Water Shield gives mana only when it procs (you are hit, or you Lightning Strike), and it has a 2-second internal cooldown [[d:shaman#1492179380967968960]], [[d:shaman#1492179516481601537]].

Never hold Stormstrike or Lightning Strike waiting for a Windfury window.

**Bot-ready loop:** `if not Windfury Weapon → cast Windfury Weapon` · `if Lightning Shield charges = 0 → Lightning Shield` · `Stormstrike if ready` · `Lightning Strike if ready` · `shock if ready: Flame Shock if missing and target not fire-immune and target lives > 9 s, else Earth Shock` · `auto-attack`.

## Threat

Enhancement is one of the most threat-spiky DPS specs: "threat is not managed when you get a triple windfury crit" [[d:shaman#1356065572231839866]]; slow non-normalized Stormstrike/Lightning Strike hits add to the spikes [[d:shaman#1384859692391600148]].

1. Take **Calming Winds 3/3** (8/16/25% threat reduction since 1.18) ([Jamey (staff), 2025-07-25](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=141159#p141159)).
2. Ask for **Blessing of Salvation + Tranquil Air Totem**; Tranquil Air is multiplicative with Salvation [[d:shaman#1486395760894414898]], [[d:shaman#1444541380910125177]].
3. Near the threat ceiling, replace some Earth Shocks with Frost Shock or Flame Shock [[d:shaman#1486399922365530282]].
4. Carry **Limited Invulnerability Potions**: they don't fix threat but the boss won't target you while they are active [[d:shaman#1489323335161614478]].
5. If you still pull with Salvation up, the tank is usually the problem; your threat is easy to read from your DPS [[d:shaman#1486406025262137467]].

## Totems for Enhancement

Default melee-group set: **Strength of Earth, Windfury, Flametongue, Mana Spring**; swap Strength of Earth for Tremor on fear fights, add cleansing and resistance totems as needed ([Index: Totems](index.md#totems)).

- **Windfury Totem vs Grace of Air:** "if 2 damage dealers benefit from WF, use it, otherwise use Grace of Air" [[d:shaman#1486730676978843719]]; Grace of Air is "straight up better for combat rogues" [[d:shaman#1486731739085799527]].
- Your own totems never buff you while you have an imbue on (see [Imbues](#weapon-imbues-and-oils)). Drop them anyway for the party.

## Consumables

Starter kit: **Elixir of the Mongoose, R.O.I.D.S., Juju Power, Dreamshard Elixir, Elixir of Greater Nature Power, Nordanaar Herbal Tea**; drop Nature Power, then R.O.I.D.S., if gold is short [[d:shaman#1416975393554174043]].

Full kit: Concoction of the Emerald Mongoose, Concoction of the Arcane Giant, Concoction of the Dreamwater, Elixir of Greater Nature Power, Elixir of Greater Firepower, R.O.I.D.S., Flask of the Titans or Supreme Power, Spirit/Swiftness of Zanza, Mageblood Potion, Elixir of Superior Defense, Power Mushroom, Rumsey Rum Black Label or Medivh's Merlot, Juju Flurry, Nordanaar Herbal Tea, Major Mana Potion, Goblin Sapper Charge, Potion of Quickness [[d:shaman#1416980701667922021]].

- Juju Power (30 Strength) beats Elixir of the Giants / Arcane Giant (they don't stack) [[d:shaman#1447630112110411827]].
- Strength food over haste food until you stack a lot of haste [[d:shaman#1429927538578296874]].
- Mana: a paladin's Judgement of Wisdom is the main plan; fewer AoE totems also helps [[d:shaman#1445241671645397115]].

## Macros and addons

- **Lightning Strike rank:** since 1.18.1 macros must call `/cast Lightning Strike(Rank 3)` or they fall back to rank 1 [[d:shaman#1484940392347734207]], [[d:shaman#1484578261911601224]].
- **Strike macro:** `/startattack` + `/cast Stormstrike` (SuperCleveRoidMacros fixes auto-attack toggling off) [[d:shaman#1486529447484985384]].
- **All four totems on one key:** needs **Nampower**; then one `/cast <Totem>` line per element [[d:shaman#1488297140617875517]], [[d:shaman#1466421661237510279]]. Totem addons: Call of Elements (MarcelineVQ fork) [[d:shaman#1426570378683093204]] or Sticks and Stones (`/sns`) [[d:shaman#1486746434249429023]].
- With Nampower, a mashed macro often fires its last line rather than its first; put your top-priority spell last [[d:shaman#1474465145534287892]].
- **DoiteAuras** tracks shields, Flurry, Stormstrike and imbues, even past the 32-buff display cap [[d:shaman#1492473691110379620]].
- **ShaguDPS** only credits totem damage to you with SuperWoW installed [[d:shaman#1485700264278233199]].

## Raid notes

- **Fire-immune bosses:** in MC Baron Geddon and Ragnaros; in BWL everything except Razorgore, Broodlord and Chromaggus [[d:shaman#1427106847072063520]], [[d:shaman#1444777299286425631]]. Drop Flame Shock there.
- **Naxxramas mana-burn fights:** on Heigan lean on Judgement of Wisdom and melee after a burn instead of shocking [[d:shaman#1451610016069976064]].
- **Best melee group** cited by the channel: 2 Enhancement shamans, 1 two-hand Fury warrior, 2 Combat rogues [[d:shaman#1483970042243580015]].
- **Buff cap:** at the buff cap Stormstrike's hidden Earth Shock / Lightning Strike bonus stopped applying after 1.18.1 [[d:shaman#1491016083774115850]].
