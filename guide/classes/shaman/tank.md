# Tank (Enhancement "warden" tank)

**Recommendation:** tank with a **shield and a slow one-hander**, **Rockbiter Weapon** on it and the **15/36/0** tank build. Pull with Chain Lightning, hold packs with **Stoneclaw Totem + Totem of the Tides**, add Fire Nova when your group AoEs, and on single targets press **Earth Shock > Stormstrike > Lightning Strike**. Gear for **Stamina and Armor first** (your shield is the most important item), not Defense or block. You have the best snap threat and no defensive cooldown; you are the squishiest of the four tanks.

## How to play

The level-60 tank loop with the [15/36/0 build](#standard-15360-recommended). Details: [Single target](#single-target-rotation), [AoE tanking](#aoe-tanking), [Totems](#totems-for-tanks), [Earthen Bulwark](#how-the-1181-tank-kit-works).

1. **Setup:** Rockbiter Weapon on a slow one-hander, highest-armor shield, Lightning Shield up. Ask for Power Word: Shield before the pull: the bulwark starts at zero [[d:shaman#1472958174960029842]].
2. **Pull:** Chain Lightning [[d:shaman#1486562928877633637]], or Molten Blast into Chain Lightning for more opening threat [[d:shaman#1438950665572450448]].
3. **First GCDs:** Earth Shock the kill target, Stormstrike, Lightning Strike, auto-attack; on a pack drop Stoneclaw Totem as they arrive.
4. **Steady threat loop:** **Earth Shock > Stormstrike > Lightning Strike** on cooldown, never stop auto-attacking (autos build the bulwark). Swap Lightning Shield to Water Shield at about two thirds mana [[d:shaman#1457483435806294106]].
5. **Multi-mob threat:** Stoneclaw Totem + Totem of the Tides with Water Shield up, tab Earth Shock across the pack; add Fire Nova when the party AoEs; Magma after Nova only as a last resort [[d:shaman#1492243908304830534]], [[d:shaman#1492243972607705210]].
6. **Taunt / threat recovery:** Earthshaker Slam (5 yd, 10 s cooldown, off the global cooldown), then Earth Shock. If the taunt is resisted, say so at once [[d:shaman#1481752700486942720]]. Emergency snap on a whole pack: Fire Nova + Stoneclaw (+ Goblin Sapper Charge).
7. **Defensive rules:** you have no defensive cooldown. Use healthstones, teas and Stoneshield potions [[d:shaman#1480080749754450022]]; tea + mana potion on cooldown for mana [[d:shaman#1471201338535711015]].
8. **Avoid:** hard-casting while being hit (it drops dodge, parry and block to zero) [[d:shaman#1445658999881207950]]; rank 1 Earth Shock for threat (it is only for cheap interrupts); Magma on every pull; Stoneskin Totem on bosses; Stoneclaw without Totemic Alignment.

## How the 1.18.1 tank kit works

**Rockbiter Weapon** is your tank stance. It adds **+35% threat** to everything (since 1.18 a percentage, which is why slow weapons are now best) [[d:shaman#1424460395892048006]], [[d:shaman#1444609987270938768]].

**Earthen Bulwark** (Elemental Weapons 3/3 with Rockbiter, new in 1.18.1) replaced the old flat 10% damage reduction: "Your physical damage builds up an earthen bulwark for 20% of the damage dealt, tripled while you're wearing a shield. The earthen bulwark absorbs 5/10/15% of incoming damage for 8 seconds or until the bulwark mitigates enough damage. The bulwark's durability cannot exceed 20% of your maximum health" ([Jamey (staff), 2026-01-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=166071#p166071), [[d:shaman#1480077263381860416]]).

- It applies **after armor**, then other % reductions, then block value last [[d:shaman#1484538733805179114]].
- It starts at **0 every pull** and drops completely after 8 seconds without a landed auto-attack; you cannot build it at range [[d:shaman#1487946914388508872]], [[d:shaman#1493187800193957978]].
- **Live bug:** it builds only from **white auto-attacks**, not Stormstrike or Lightning Strike. A hotfix note on 2026-03-24 said it builds from melee abilities too ([Torta (staff), 2026-03-24](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172139#p172139)), but tanks still reported autos-only in mid-April; "it's a bug rather than a poor design choice", left unfixed "for 3 weeks" [[d:shaman#1493309560428171430]], [[d:shaman#1493387191328571602]].
- Impact is gear and fight dependent: 80% uptime on Patchwerk but 40-50% on Naxx trash (stuns, fears, disarms) [[d:shaman#1485983090601099455]]; 91% uptime in a Chromaggus log [[d:shaman#1493389625421594714]]; some tanks in full mitigation gear have "trouble keeping up bulwark" in BWL [[d:shaman#1493215880765509722]].
- The change was aimed at PvP (Restoration shamans using the old flat reduction) [[d:shaman#1472960871213826213]]; the dev expected "for actual tanks it shouldn't change much, tiny nerf at most" but "big nerf for spelltanks" [[d:shaman#1464414658818736291]], [[d:shaman#1464414877098573846]].

**T2.5 (the only mail tank set)** lost its old Rockbiter threat bonus: its 5-piece now boosts the bulwark (+25% build-up, +3% absorb). That cost roughly 6% of your threat multiplier (1.48 vs 1.57) [[d:shaman#1486121153046118420]]. "T2.5 is now the mitigation set, all other tier sets are threat sets" [[d:shaman#1485032859906473996]], and tank tier sets no longer carry spell power [[d:shaman#1472681420802752725]].

**No defensive cooldown** exists; community workarounds are healthstones, teas and Stoneshield potions [[d:shaman#1480080749754450022]], [[d:shaman#1441164858207895683]]. Ask for Power Word: Shield before the pull: Stormstrike and your first auto build enough bulwark to absorb 10-15% of a crit almost at once [[d:shaman#1472958174960029842]].

- **Bloodlust** buffs your party; "logs show there's not that much difference between taking Bloodlust" and it buffs your group [[d:shaman#1442091068148551742]].

## Stat priority and caps

1. **Stamina and Armor** (equal). "Main stats will always be ARMOR = STAMINA > AGI"; Defense "is a nice addition but never a focus" [[d:shaman#1474649022043263029]]. Your **shield's armor** is "probably the single most important thing" [[d:shaman#1486559128083103835]]; a bad shield costs ~1000 armor and ~700 HP [[d:shaman#1438949610176512091]]. Armor gains scale EHP linearly [[d:shaman#1471233885038841897]].
2. **Hit to 8% melee** (4% from gear with the weapon-skill book) [[d:shaman#1487191048840020049]]. You cannot dual wield, so hit above 8% does nothing [[d:shaman#1474648717737853049]], [[d:shaman#1474648756384301126]].
3. **Weapon skill to 315** for threat (Stormstrike/Lightning Strike are not normalized); MC gloves + Orc racial + quest book = exactly 315 [[d:shaman#1486861889248952521]].
4. **Threat stats:** Strength > spell power > Agility [[d:shaman#1486559128083103835]]. A couple of points of spell hit help taunt and Earth Shock reliability [[d:shaman#1487831708878508054]].
5. **Agility** for dodge and crit.

Not worth chasing:

- **Defense.** Unless you can reach 440 (crit immunity), "it's better to focus on armor and health" [[d:shaman#1466115983541538979]]; shaman tanks can't reach it in practice [[d:shaman#1490407432403419336]]. Crushing blows stay at 15% regardless of Defense [[d:shaman#1478722202269384705]]; pushing them off needs ~85-88% avoidance, which mail gear can't reach [[d:shaman#1474645833302413427]].
- **Block** and Shield Specialization (above).
- Rugged armor kits beat Defense kits unless they get you to 440 [[d:shaman#1466102519502602346]].

Sample benchmark: a tank at ~8k armor, 5k HP unbuffed and 120 FR asking about MTing Ragnaros was told it should be fine ("if you can tank the last boss of Black Morass you can probably tank Rag") [[d:shaman#1489054853899620542]], [[d:shaman#1489072008456310895]]; ~250 FR is plenty for Geddon and mostly for Ragnaros [[d:shaman#1480121471106945105]].

## Single-target rotation

**Pull:** Chain Lightning ("an incredible tank tool for pulling") [[d:shaman#1486562928877633637]], [[d:shaman#1486563067511963648]], or Molten Blast into Chain Lightning for opening burst threat [[d:shaman#1438950665572450448]]. Lightning Shield for the pull, then Water Shield when mana drops to about two thirds [[d:shaman#1457483435806294106]].

Priority:

1. **Earth Shock** on cooldown: about 40% of your threat [[d:shaman#1453500008375521330]], [[d:shaman#1489000281315475559]].
2. **Stormstrike** on cooldown (buffs the next Earth Shock).
3. **Lightning Strike** on cooldown [[d:shaman#1488136124617724004]].
4. Keep **auto-attacking** (it builds the bulwark and is a big threat share at high gear).
5. Refresh Lightning/Water Shield; tea and mana potion on cooldown [[d:shaman#1471201338535711015]].

Rules:

- Keep **rank 1 Earth Shock** bound only for interrupts when low on mana [[d:shaman#1424427249884463326]].
- **Never hard-cast while being hit**: casting drops your dodge, parry and block to zero [[d:shaman#1445658999881207950]].
- Earth Shield is a poor tank tool (small heals vs 1-2k hits); use Water Shield [[d:shaman#1444651606842015845]], [[d:shaman#1445538289376559134]].
- Call out a **resisted taunt** at once so healers pre-cast [[d:shaman#1481752700486942720]].

**Bot-ready loop:** `on pull: Chain Lightning (or Molten Blast) at range` · `Rockbiter Weapon on MH if missing` · `Earth Shock if ready` · `Stormstrike if ready` · `Lightning Strike if ready` · `auto-attack` · `if mana < 66% and Lightning Shield up → Water Shield` · `taunt (Earthshaker Slam) if target not on you and not resisted`.

## AoE tanking

AoE threat is a cost-vs-need hierarchy (Not Normal) [[d:shaman#1492243908304830534]], [[d:shaman#1492243972607705210]]:

| Situation | Tools |
|---|---|
| Normal pack | **Chain Lightning pull + Stoneclaw Totem + Totem of the Tides** (Water Shield up), tab Earth Shock / strikes |
| Party AoEs hard | Add **Fire Nova Totem** (more up-front than Magma, the standard choice) [[d:shaman#1486561660150747156]] |
| Party still pulls | **Magma Totem after Nova**, "last resort, it will destroy your mana" [[d:shaman#1492243972607705210]]; use it if mobs live a long time [[d:shaman#1485085137086709931]] |
| Emergency snap | Fire Nova + Goblin Sapper Charge + Stoneclaw together [[d:shaman#1438960736440156210]] |

- **Stoneclaw Totem** does not taunt; it pulses about 150 threat every 1.5 s to everything in range, 90% of it credited to you with Totemic Alignment [[d:shaman#1491156093550989312]], [[d:shaman#1491173915328774334]].
- **Totem of the Tides** (relic from [Stormwrought Ruins](../../instances/stormwrought-ruins.md)): AoE frost damage whenever you're hit with Water Shield up; "undisputed best for aoe", especially with **Sigil of Ancient Accord** [[d:shaman#1486563629800226817]], [[d:shaman#1486563170083668059]]. Sigil procs from its damage [[d:shaman#1473298508504236146]] and made up ~7% of one tank's damage on almost every boss [[d:shaman#1473301013757956140]].
- **Improved Fire Totems** is still usually taken despite -50% fire totem threat: the 2-second Fire Nova is "insane" QoL [[d:shaman#1489583151977074851]]; **Contested:** it loses threat at the 9-second mark and some skip it so Magma "pumps" [[d:shaman#1489586957632671804]], [[d:shaman#1475720192146280478]].
- The bulwark is less reliable on trash (stuns, fears, disarms, AoE burst before it builds) [[d:shaman#1485983090601099455]].
