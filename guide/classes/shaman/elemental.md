# Elemental (ranged caster DPS)

**Recommendation:** play **Nature Elemental** with the post-1.18.1 **34/3/14** build. Keep **Flame Shock** up and refresh it with **Molten Blast**, fill with **Lightning Bolt**, and cast **Chain Lightning** on Clearcasting (on cooldown once you are well geared); on packs add **Earthquake** and Chain Lightning. Get **12% spell hit from gear** (16% total), then crit, which is also your mana. Elemental needs gear to come online: new raiders are usually advised to start as Enhancement and switch around AQ40.

The pre-1.18.1 **"fire ele" (Molten Blast spam) spec is gone**: 1.18.1 made Flametongue's fire bonus require melee hits, and the channel describes fire as "folded into" Nature Elemental. Older guides that tell you to spam Molten Blast are outdated.

## Stat priority and caps

1. **Spell hit to 16% total** against bosses. 3% comes from talents, 1% from a druid's Emerald Blessing, so **12% from gear** [[d:shaman#1486439926412087380]], [[d:shaman#1486439783516475604]]. Most raid trash is below level 63, so chasing the cap costs damage there [[d:shaman#1452197253258416331]]. Hit is "not mandatory" [[d:shaman#1452149363148062811]]; 11-14% is the usual target in MC/BWL gear [[d:shaman#1452197667781345322]], and AQ40 gear over-caps easily [[d:shaman#1452326205834530826]].
2. **Crit.** It is your mana (Clearcasting from Elemental Focus): past ~12-13% hit and ~500 spell power, crit becomes more valuable than hit [[d:shaman#1480349166218510400]].
3. **Spell power.**
4. **Haste.**
5. **Spell penetration to 15** (bosses have 15 Nature resistance) if nobody brings Thunderfury; "one of the biggest dps gains from a stat (but not after 15)" [[d:shaman#1452198375448645742]]. Use +spell-pen ring/amulet enchants or a head/leg enchant [[d:shaman#1471268879664939070]].

Weights for gear planners: **hit ≈ 15 SP, crit ≈ 10 SP, haste ≈ 8 SP** [[d:shaman#1481698281204482068]]. Int ≈ 59.5 per 1% spell crit [[d:shaman#1430034612301598800]]. Spirit is dead for shamans [[d:shaman#1434512577865715814]].

## Single-target rotation

Current (bugged-Earthquake) priority, Not Normal: "that's currently the ideal rotation" [[d:shaman#1485686440070479892]]; Ordinary Joe's sim: "flameshock > molten blast (only to refresh flameshock) > earthquake > chainlightning > lightning bolt", with Earthquake "DPS even so you can or can not press it" on one target [[d:shaman#1490703901098250300]], [[d:shaman#1490703968576471180]].

1. **Searing Totem** down (or Flametongue Totem if you sit in a melee group), other totems up.
2. **Flame Shock** on the target (skip on fire-immune bosses). Pop Elemental Mastery and trinkets *before* casting it: Flame Shock snapshots [[d:shaman#1420830381762154628]].
3. **Molten Blast** to refresh Flame Shock before it expires (about every 10-12 s) [[d:shaman#1482850420484210790]]. It refreshes to the full 15 s (+3 with a Zul'Farrak totem, +6 with a set bonus) without losing ticks [[d:shaman#1492512142899937351]], [[d:shaman#1492517323838460035]]. Once you have the T3.5 3-piece you stop using Molten Blast this way [[d:shaman#1485240215345037373]].
4. **Chain Lightning** on Clearcasting; on cooldown when mana allows. It beats Lightning Bolt even single target at equal cast time [[d:shaman#1461195417214779403]]. Don't cast it without Clearcasting while mana is tight [[d:shaman#1484941909620428922]].
5. **Lightning Bolt** filler (about 3 between Chain Lightnings).
6. **Earthquake** on cooldown only when it hits more than one target (see [AoE](#aoe-rotation)).
7. **No shocks as filler**: they cost a GCD and do less than Lightning Bolt; Earth Shock also pulls more threat [[d:shaman#1459503774186999850]], [[d:shaman#1459504235296063682]]. Use Earth Shock only to finish a dying target, to interrupt, or while moving.

**Bot-ready loop:** `if Flame Shock missing on target and target not fire-immune → Flame Shock` · `if Flame Shock remaining < cast time + 1 s → Molten Blast` · `if Clearcasting or mana > 60% → Chain Lightning if ready` · `if ≥2 targets in 10 yd → Earthquake if ready` · `else Lightning Bolt` · `if moving → Earth Shock`.

**Mana:** tea or mana potion every boss pull is normal while gearing [[d:shaman#1469228322410991740]]. Avoid downranking; gear for Clearcasting and use consumables [[d:shaman#1485333169057042605]]; **Contested:** "downranking is way better than oom" [[d:shaman#1485335237851480104]]. A heuristic: downrank if your mana % is below the boss's HP % [[d:shaman#1471574271343067246]].

- Chain Lightning was about 40% of Elemental trash damage in Naxxramas logs [[d:shaman#1319681814687649903]]; Earthquake "just goes brr" on spider-wing trash [[d:shaman#1427050769055485993]].

- Earthquake has a long cast and a big animation [[d:shaman#1463817119270703259]]; start it after the tank has threat, since Earthquake into Chain Lightning on trash "almost certainly" pulls aggro [[d:shaman#1462560298480435231]].

## Totems and group placement

Elemental usually lands in a **melee group** (for Windfury and Strength of Earth), since it has no caster-group totem of its own [[d:shaman#1483918066323558481]]; the real issue is that the ideal melee group wants two shamans [[d:shaman#1483934820835917947]]. In a caster group, **Tranquil Air Totem** is the main reason to bring you [[d:shaman#1395170289838719048]].

Raid support checklist (Dwi) [[d:shaman#1475607369789607957]], [[d:shaman#1475608230116720834]]:

- Play like a ranged DPS, but walk into Earth Shock range about every 2 minutes to drop totems for your melee.
- Earth Shock while moving and to interrupt enemy heals and volleys.
- Use the right resistance totem on elemental-damage fights; refresh Poison/Disease Cleansing totems when party members are afflicted; Tremor Totem for fear fights (drop it ~5.5 s before a telegraphed fear).
- Don't drop Nature Resistance Totem casually: "your melees will prefer death over no windfury".

## Weapon imbues and oils

- As a caster, use a **weapon oil** (Brilliant Wizard Oil) instead of Flametongue: Flametongue's fire bonus now needs melee hits [[d:shaman#1470506986113077521]]. Oils cannot go on a weapon that has an imbue [[d:shaman#1450551190189375691]].

## Raid notes

- **Fire-immune bosses:** MC has Baron Geddon and Ragnaros fully immune and the rest heavily fire-resistant [[d:shaman#1427106847072063520]], [[d:shaman#1433554298909884488]]; in BWL all but Razorgore, Broodlord and Chromaggus are immune [[d:shaman#1444777299286425631]]. Drop Flame Shock/Molten Blast there.
- **Nature resistance: Contested.** "MC and BWL have a decent amount of nature resistance (not immune) and AQ40 has a ton" [[d:shaman#1480348390444236820]] vs "AQ40 doesn't actually have a ton of nature resistant/immune mobs. AQ20 does" [[d:shaman#1480349616464728226]]. [Timbermaw Hold](../../instances/timbermaw-hold.md) mobs are reported highly nature-resistant [[d:shaman#1487278411062837390]].
- **Heigan** is a hard fight for any casting hybrid because of the dance [[d:shaman#1464467599747317800]].
- Kara40 has invulnerability phases (Gnarlmoon, Sanv, Mephistroth) that break DoT snapshots [[d:shaman#1419936345228447794]].

## Common mistakes

1. Playing the old Molten Blast spam spec [[d:shaman#1485240088047652894]].
2. Using Flametongue Weapon as a caster [[d:shaman#1470506986113077521]].
3. Chasing 16% hit in MC/BWL gear at the cost of spell power and crit [[d:shaman#1452149363148062811]].
4. Shock spam as filler [[d:shaman#1459503774186999850]].
5. Chain Lightning without Clearcasting while mana-starved [[d:shaman#1484941909620428922]].
6. Earthquake on a single target while it is bugged [[d:shaman#1485686440070479892]].
7. Flame Shock on fire-immune bosses.
8. Skipping a threat-reduction talent [[d:shaman#1460694375985057803]].
9. Popping Elemental Mastery after Flame Shock (no snapshot) [[d:shaman#1420830381762154628]].
10. Starting raiding as Elemental in poor gear; many advise Enhancement until AQ40 [[d:shaman#1472175286543057079]].
