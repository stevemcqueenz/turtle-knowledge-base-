# PvP

**Recommendation:** shamans are one of the strongest PvP classes because every spec can borrow Enhancement's armor talents and still burst. For battlegrounds play **Restoration 13/17/21** (healer with Enhancement armor talents) or an **Enhancement/Elemental hybrid with 30+ Enhancement points**; **spellhance** (Flametongue, spell power) is the strongest damage style, and "PvE-style" two-hand Windfury Enhancement is weak outside a raid's buffs. Stack **Stamina**, get **5% melee / 3% spell hit**, wear mail and a shield against physical classes, and carry free-action and invulnerability potions.

Server-wide PvP rules (honor as a currency, rank, the 1.18.1 PvP sets, Conquest, battlegrounds, Warmode, dampening and resilience) are on [PvP systems](../../pvp.md).

PvP sources are thinner than PvE and mostly predate 1.18.1 (2026-03-20); post-patch items are marked.

## Where the class stands

- Pre-1.18.1 the #pvp-general tier lists put shaman on top: "Shaman god tier, rogue, smite priest S tier ..." [[d:pvp-general#1429944347834581072]]; "shaman, rogue >>> pala > ..." [[d:pvp-general#1430027610406260776]].
- Anyone competent kills the shaman first: "they have to die first or you're cooked" [[d:shaman#1470853367684141166]].
- "Schrödinger's shaman": weak in PvE, yet in a BG "I can kill half the enemy team in a few GCDs ... all while being unkillable and healing the whole horde team" [[d:shaman#1486002554646167643]].
- **After 1.18.1:** the new **dynamic damage dampening** is spec-dependent ([Jamey (staff), 2026-03-02](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?t=24132), [[d:shaman#1480081322822340679]]); **resilience** "basically turns crits into normal hits" [[d:shaman#1492727874082570391]]; one player finds shaman burst "neutered by dampening and resilience" [[d:shaman#1494663845619437609]]; another sees "Ele, Locks, and Rogues are all top-tier" [[d:shaman#1486821661633282240]].
- **1.18 PvP nerfs still in effect:** Purge has an 8-second cooldown; Nature's Swiftness became Ancestral Swiftness (damage spells cast with it deal 25% less) [[d:shaman#1405331266064089169]]; Grounding Totem's cooldown went up ~33% [[d:shaman#1406007562654056499]].
- The 1.18.1 Rockbiter rework (Earthen Bulwark) was aimed at Restoration PvP tankiness [[d:shaman#1472960871213826213]].

## How to play

The level-60 PvP decision loop. The details are in [Burst and control sequences](#burst-and-control-sequences), [Defensive tools](#defensive-tools), [Battleground role](#battleground-role) and [Matchups](#matchups).

**Damage (spellhance / Enhancement-Elemental hybrid):**

1. **Before the fight:** Lightning Shield up; shield equipped vs Warrior, Rogue, Ret, Feral and Hunter, two-hander only to burst squishies [[d:shaman#1365197906990071882]]. Travel in Ghost Wolf if rogues are around: it makes you a Beast, so you can't be Sapped (but Hibernate works) [[d:shaman#1470853297660366872]], [[d:shaman#1470853835948953652]].
2. **Opener and burst:** shock → two totems → auto → Stormstrike, then Stormstrike-buffed Earth Shock; the whole burst fits in about two GCDs [[d:pvp-general#1425207495894499328]]. Against a warlock or mage in the open, burn your cooldowns at the start or you lose [[d:shaman#1439784444381630594]].
3. **Control chain:** Earth Shock interrupt → **Hex** (Troll, 5-min cooldown) → Earth Shock when the 6 s shock cooldown is back [[d:shaman#1418338864245903492]], [[d:shaman#1471611308167139483]]. Tauren: shock → Totemic Slam/War Stomp → shock, or stomp → self-heal [[d:shaman#1471610841274253365]]. Hexed targets still auto-attack.
4. **Kite melee:** Frost Shock + Earthbind Totem, Fire Nova and Searing Totem, all instant [[d:shaman#1449322892763598910]]. Once Frost Shock hits diminishing returns, switch to Earthbind [[d:shaman#1316790501432627201]].
5. **Defence:** drop Grounding Totem (20 s cooldown) before a Polymorph or a big nuke; it does nothing against rogue abilities [[d:shaman#1425240856520294421]]. Free-action and invulnerability potions are your escape; you have no charge, stealth or sprint [[d:shaman#1445313673366343753]], [[d:shaman#1445315487784243322]]. Flame Shock rogues and cats so they can't re-stealth [[d:shaman#1425663325747806269]].
6. **Target:** healers first, with shocks and Hex; Purge seals, blessings and shields (8 s cooldown, magic buffs only) [[d:pvp-general#1424861955998351412]].
7. **Reset:** break line of sight, then heal yourself; drink out of combat (vanilla baseline).

**Restoration (13/17/21):**

1. **Before the fight:** Earth Shield on yourself and open in Ghost Wolf against rogues [[d:shaman#1470853297660366872]]; Water Shield with Totem of the Tides can knock a close rogue or druid out of stealth [[d:shaman#1470855625066942617]].
2. **Default heals:** Earth Shield lets you heal in people's faces when they have no interrupt [[d:shaman#1494671492556918784]]. Use single-target Healing Wave / Lesser Healing Wave in small fights; Chain Heal only when the team is stacked in a big BG fight [[d:shaman#1470853509183180850]], [[d:shaman#1470853945797640234]].
3. **Triage:** expect to be focused first [[d:shaman#1470853367684141166]]. Keep yourself alive, then the target being trained (vanilla baseline).
4. **Emergency:** Ancestral Swiftness (3-min cooldown) is your one instant heal; every other heal is a hard cast, so a team that tunnels you can lock you out [[d:shaman#1494663845619437609]].
5. **Utility:** Earth Shock the enemy healer's casts; Grounding Totem against CC; Purge enemy buffs; Poison Cleansing Totem no longer removes Blind [[d:shaman#1440024319504683029]].
6. **Mana:** in a big fight, heal until oom from a safe spot; drink between fights (vanilla baseline).

**Vs class** (full table in [Matchups](#matchups)):

- **Warlock:** Curse of Exhaustion out-slows Frost Shock; abuse line of sight [[d:shaman#1469487274608689375]].
- **Rogue:** open in Ghost Wolf, without the Insignia a stunlock kills you [[d:shaman#1468558047394664608]]; shield up, Flame Shock against re-stealth.
- **Mage:** Grounding Totem eats Polymorph and Frostbolt [[d:pvp-general#1423693001900687421]].
- **Druid:** watch for Hibernate while in Ghost Wolf.
- **Paladin:** Purge seals and blessings.
- **Warrior / Hunter:** favourable; shield vs warriors and Weapon Chain vs Disarm [[d:pvp-general#1432642448622096424]].

## Burst and control sequences

**Enhancement burst (two GCDs):** shock → two totems → auto → strike: "dead within that small use of 2 gcds and 4 skills" [[d:pvp-general#1425207495894499328]]; one "went 3.8k > 0 in 1 frame" [[d:pvp-general#1425207182869401712]].

**Silence chain (Troll):** Earth Shock (interrupt) → **Hex** → Earth Shock: "a loooong time of not casting or healing at all" [[d:shaman#1418338864245903492]]. Hexed targets still auto-attack (for half damage) and those autos can push back your casts [[d:shaman#1478442861753012469]]. **Contested:** whether Hex can be cast while moving [[d:shaman#1478469508724297862]], [[d:shaman#1478443461609914368]].

**Elemental kite vs melee:** Frost Shock + Earthbind Totem to slow, Fire Nova and Searing Totem for damage, Stormstrike-buffed Earth Shock; all instants, so kiting stays easy [[d:shaman#1449322892763598910]].

**Shock choice:** Earth Shock to interrupt or burst [[d:shaman#1425663232038670486]]; Flame Shock on rogues and cats to stop re-stealth [[d:shaman#1425663325747806269]].

**Totem autoswap:** Call of Elements' "autoswitch sets in PvP" drops a counter set based on the target's class [[d:shaman#1427562362608812133]].

## Defensive tools

- **Grounding Totem** eats the next harmful spell on a party member, which is why Frostbolts "redirect" onto a totem [[d:pvp-general#1433886319896952853]]; nothing rogues do is affected by it [[d:shaman#1425240856520294421]].
- **Poison Cleansing Totem no longer removes Blind** [[d:shaman#1440024319504683029]].
- **Ghost Wolf vs Sap:** "always start in ghostwolf if you expect rogues, you're a beast and can't be sapped" [[d:shaman#1470853297660366872]], [[d:pvp-general#1424854629287596199]]; but you can be sapped mid-cast [[d:pvp-general#1424857842288033963]]. Ghost Wolf can't be cast indoors [[d:shaman#1435301428565508206]].
- **Water Shield + Totem of the Tides** can break a rogue or druid out of stealth if they get close [[d:shaman#1470855625066942617]].
- **Consumables:** free-action / invulnerability potions make Enhancement "pretty good" [[d:shaman#1445315487784243322]]; PvP boots and rocket boots don't stack with Ghost Wolf [[d:shaman#1434929341359456416]].
- **Purge** strips every magic buff, not curses [[d:pvp-general#1424861955998351412]], [[d:pvp-general#1424862075007664149]].

## Battleground role

- **Healer:** find a safe spot and Chain Heal a stacked team until oom; Chain Heal is for big fights, not duels [[d:shaman#1470853945797640234]], [[d:shaman#1470854282017505365]].
- **Burst/peel:** focus healers with shocks and Hex.
- **Mobility:** Tauren can now use Plainsrunning in battlegrounds (it used to be disabled) [[d:shaman#1471897242477527164]], [[d:shaman#1442387742423126107]]. **Contested:** Improved Ghost Wolf is essential for some (flag running, kiting) [[d:shaman#1442378040276422657]], overrated at 60 for others, especially Elemental with 36-yard range [[d:shaman#1442373329917837434]].

## Gear, enchants and ranks

- **PvP sets (1.18.1):** three tiers per role: rare ilvl ~71, epic ~77, epic ~92 (top tier behind Conquest Points) [[d:shaman#1481374586938327223]]. Set bonuses are the same for all specs (2p +2% crit, 4p +15% Ghost Wolf speed, 6p +35 Stamina, 8p +6% resilience); the healer version adds 30% silence resistance and 40% shorter interrupt lockouts [[d:shaman#1487313403050459249]], [[d:shaman#1487313790658809866]]. The Field Medic set needs Rank 14 for all pieces [[d:shaman#1487437782313078866]].
- **Honor:** Rank 6 is needed to enter the Hall of Legends vendor; the weekly honor cap is 20k (~20 hours) [[d:shaman#1491797398027243662]].
- **Weapon enchant:** Weapon Chain, not Crusader ("crusader its pve shit"): it stops Disarm [[d:pvp-general#1432642117917736961]], [[d:pvp-general#1432642448622096424]].
- **Weapons:** Earthshaker with Windfury: "great stun machine for pvp", sometimes perma-stunning with War Stomp [[d:shaman#1492266580640272445]], [[d:shaman#1492271640388702218]]. The Arathi Basin exalted dagger (1% crit, 40 SP) is the key early caster PvP item [[d:shaman#1441118920240009447]].
- **Relic:** Totem of Rotten Roots (healing reduction) for a shock build [[d:shaman#1431086315038052352]], [[d:shaman#1431095201866190919]].
