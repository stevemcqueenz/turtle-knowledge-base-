# Shaman professions

**Recommendation:** level with two gathering professions: **Herbalism + Mining** or **Herbalism + Skinning**. At 60, once you are farming Dire Maul lashers, drop the second gatherer and take **Engineering**. The end state is **Engineering + Herbalism**, the shaman "meta" pair: Engineering is the only profession that adds player power (Sappers, Dynamite, grenades), and Herbalism raises the gold you make from lasher farming, the shaman's best farm [[d:shaman#1406779021345423465]], [[d:shaman#1389424395415715890]]. If you don't care about min-maxing, professions matter little: "Prof choice is relatively inconsequential in classic, unless you want to PvP" [[d:shaman#1444613292189548606]]. In that case, pick them for gold.

## What each profession gives a shaman

### Engineering

- **Goblin Sapper Charges and Dynamite** give mana-free AoE damage and threat, for tanks and DPS alike [[d:shaman#1406779021345423465]], [[d:shaman#1399735964552659054]]. Tanks can snap a whole pack with Fire Nova + Sapper + Stoneclaw (see [Tank](tank.md)). A low-level, low-gear shaman tank leans on mana potions and "engineering explosives" [[d:shaman#1477599035371290694]].
- **How much Sappers add is contested.** One player calls it "relatively minor", used only because it is the one direct DPS gain [[d:shaman#1439359861098807446]]. In the fastest Kara40 run a Sapper was about 1% of a top DPS player's damage [[d:shaman#1439364997342953692]]. One Sapper was also seen making up 7% of a player's damage on Maexxna [[d:shaman#1439365627100921917]]. Others note shamans already have strong AoE threat without Sappers or Force Reactive Disk [[d:shaman#1428251894127525919]].
- **Force Reactive Disk** is named alongside Sappers as a reason Engineering is "the best prof for basically every spec" [[d:shaman#1428248936015204413]], [[d:shaman#1428249153879806084]].
- **PvP:** stun grenades, Flash Bomb, Goblin Rocket Helmet [[d:shaman#1429839210080370728]]. "Grenades are a great aoe cooldown for any content" [[d:shaman#1421140812758126632]].
- **Travel:** the Gadgetzan and Winterspring teleporters and the Orgrimmar wormhole, plus Astral Recall, give "the best qol" and near-mage mobility [[d:shaman#1444613431872454716]].
- **Goblin vs Gnomish:** "Goblin engineering for sappers are amazing" [[d:shaman#1399735964552659054]] (single source in this channel).
- **Buy the mats.** Don't take Mining just to feed Engineering: "just lasher farm and buy the materials, it's 1000% more efficient" [[d:shaman#1388760262009684049]], [[d:shaman#1389424395415715890]].

### Herbalism

- Herbalism is the gatherer that pairs with the **Dire Maul East lasher farm** (see [Gold](#gold)) [[d:shaman#1425698342167515177]], [[d:shaman#1433512536392601681]].
- **Contested:** how much it adds. Some say the value of a lasher run is "tied to the economy of herbs" [[d:shaman#1395550022006866032]]. Others call Herbalism "a very minor part of lasher farm" [[d:shaman#1473082022149750886]]. On fresh Ambershire it added only "like 5-10% per hour" [[d:shaman#1419175037449015368]].
- **Tauren** get +15 Herbalism, "a nice boost in early game" [[d:shaman#1278437860797710387]].

### Alchemy

- Alchemy has no shaman-specific perk. "Anything an Alch can make you can just buy with gold," while an alchemist can't use Engineering's tools [[d:shaman#1388758403983081616]]. The only alchemy trinket is a BoE, and it is not a tank trinket [[d:shaman#1471890802677514324]].
- Profit follows raid demand: with only MC and Onyxia running "you probably won't make much money" [[d:shaman#1433516345114492938]], [[d:shaman#1435057554798678027]]. On fresh Ambershire potions often sold for less than their herbs [[d:shaman#1419175037449015368]].
- Worth it only with Herbalism feeding it [[d:shaman#1426604274489622619]]. It is a "good, low-friction" leveling pair [[d:shaman#1494197308143829093]].

### Jewelcrafting (Turtle custom)

- **Enchants (Gemology):** ring and neck enchants that "will always be useful" as new gear arrives, and a steady gold earner [[d:shaman#1486946522556792932]], [[d:shaman#1408193447827079169]]. Named examples: +12 healing ring/neck enchants for Restoration [[d:shaman#1413241423980920943]]; +6 SP ring/amulet enchants, which Elemental can use to cover spell penetration more cheaply [[d:shaman#1471267256192471226]]. "Go gemology and sell ring and neck enchants" [[d:shaman#1444513617960767590]].
- **Tank items:** BoP spell-damage pieces such as **Talisman of Hinderance** [[d:shaman#1406797526492909569]], a "strong anti magic damage neck" [[d:shaman#1399735944629977279]], and a BoP trinket that is BiS for certain bosses [[d:shaman#1408076843105648680]]. A Goldsmith-only necklace that reduces all spell damage by 5% was raised as a reason to take it (single source, asked as a question) [[d:shaman#1428246212531982446]]. The JC **+8 defense ring** helps reach crit immunity from AQ40 gear [[d:shaman#1396987368988348497]].
- **Restoration:** an epic BoE pre-raid healing ring, and the epic crafted **Grandstaff of the Shen'dralar Elder** for shamans in the dedicated healer group [[d:shaman#1416254106682789898]], [[d:shaman#1416256033051115644]]. The Gemology staff and ring (Shen'dralar recipe) are "hard to beat" [[d:shaman#1473812266901176493]].
- **Goldsmith staff:** a level-60 Goldsmith staff is an alternative to the Stratholme T0.5 off-hand if you can't get help with that summon [[d:shaman#1414301293195034646]].
- **Downsides:** leveling JC alongside Mining is costly ("making a single opaline illuminator takes so many resources") [[d:shaman#1433515256671502486]], and leveling it 1-60 "was not very pleasant" [[d:shaman#1408192239577989180]]. The route and trainers are in the [Professions overview](../../professions.md).

### Enchanting

- Enchanting needs no gathering partner: any profession or drop that yields greens can be disenchanted [[d:shaman#1429007269663866880]], [[d:shaman#1429008277915566231]]. It is "always a good choice" as a second profession [[d:shaman#1458550747691221034]].
- A bank alt with just enough Enchanting to disenchant BoE drops was a steady, if unspectacular, income on Ambershire [[d:shaman#1435323038991388722]].

### Skinning and Leatherworking

- Skinning funds **Dragonscale farming**: shamans solo red and black dragonkin elites. Red scales earn about as much per hour as lashers but sell slowly; black scales sell fast for less. The Dragonscale belt buckle recipe from Searing Gorge black dragonkin is the jackpot [[d:shaman#1426246866986602538]], [[d:shaman#1426247033739542569]]. Devilsaur leather is another shaman farm on Nordanaar [[d:shaman#1435056833592164442]].
- **Leatherworking** "is kinda ass unless you're just doing some self imposed challenge of no trading" [[d:shaman#1462159463711506595]]. Tribal is the usual pick if you take it [[d:shaman#1462177412178510055]]. Stormshroud shoulders and hands are called pre-BiS for Enhancement at ~50 (single source) [[d:shaman#1417946785510326293]].

### Blacksmithing

- Blacksmithing makes the **Fiery Chain** fire-resistance mail set for tanks [[d:shaman#1474856625297690705]], [[d:shaman#1476041815240216626]]. One Fiery Chain piece plus FR enchants reaches ~300 FR [[d:shaman#1425351478540767233]].
- **Towerforge** (crafted, Kara10 tier) head and shoulders are Enhancement pre-raid pieces with a two-hand weapon-skill set bonus. They are expensive (about 600 g for two pieces), and two of the four need AQ20 Small Obsidian Shards. Buy them rather than taking Blacksmithing [[d:shaman#1442295545300390038]], [[d:shaman#1442296203349065899]], [[d:shaman#1442296420022489089]].
- Otherwise Blacksmithing is weak for a shaman ("only has weapons") [[d:shaman#1396759152088453172]]. **Contested:** "Blacksmithing really doesnt have anything" you can't get elsewhere, apart from raid resistance sets [[d:shaman#1360456924096892938]].

### Tailoring

- Bloodvine's tooltip shows "Requires Tailoring (300)". One reading is that you need Tailoring only for the set bonus, not to equip the pieces (unverified) [[d:shaman#1423303445913272393]]. Leatherworking, Tailoring and Alchemy all have cooldown crafts worth "a tiny passive income" [[d:shaman#1417956944945352817]].

### Fishing and Cooking (secondary)

- Fishing takes no profession slot [[d:shaman#1458550421395214497]]. Oily Blackmouth off the Barrens coast sells while you level; Stonescale Eels sell at 60 [[d:shaman#1458554345393094909]].

### Survival and Gardening (secondary professions)

- **The shaman channel is nearly silent on the 1.18.1 Survival rework.** Players mention "survival lantern" and "the new survival trinkets" only in passing, as proc-trinket fodder [[d:shaman#1491556989220815078]], [[d:shaman#1494169299114397766]]. No source argues shaman-specific value, so treat Survival as optional open-world and gathering quality of life.
- **1.18.1 rework (forum, all classes):** Survival now draws on the primary professions and has 80+ recipes: slowing, rooting and trapping tools, bags for the three gathering professions, fish and meat containers, and Woodcutting [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168). The player 1-300 route includes Slowing Bolas, Throwable Nets, fishing traps and Nutritious Rations [Speedz1337 (guide writer), 2026-03-28](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=172915#p172915). A skill-300 sickle raises the Black Lotus chance [Birtha (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171974#p171974). That is useful for a lasher-farming herbalist, but no shaman source tests it. Tents now give only 25% rested XP [Torta (staff), 2025-10-03](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=154168#p154168).
- **Gardening (pre-1.18.1):** the Gardening quest's shovel was called the best 1-hander at level 20 and "good for tanking" [[d:shaman#1390708702201253969]], [[d:shaman#1439723347150639226]]. The 1.18.1 overhaul reset the Gardening quest, so you must redo it [Sylveria (player), 2026-03-23](https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=171863#p171863).

## When to pick up and swap

1. **1-~50:** two gatherers (Herb + Mining or Herb + Skinning) [[d:shaman#1389424395415715890]]. Alternatively, Skinning + Leatherworking until ~50 for Stormshroud [[d:shaman#1411592191498784788]].
2. **~52-54:** start lasher farming [[d:shaman#1488144215681663127]]. Swap Leatherworking for Herbalism if you had it [[d:shaman#1411592191498784788]].
3. **60:** Herbalism for farming plus **Engineering for raiding**; drop Mining or Skinning and buy the Engineering mats with lasher gold [[d:shaman#1411592191498784788]], [[d:shaman#1389424395415715890]]. Another route: "mining and skinning till 35 then engi and mining" [[d:shaman#1417934930393956454]].
4. At 60 the common end pairs are "engi/herb, engi/jc or engi/ench" [[d:shaman#1408187517160788162]].

On a **fresh server** herb and ore nodes are heavily contested. Players took Skinning, or delayed professions until 60 [[d:shaman#1410896661890207815]].

## Race interactions

Shamans are Orc, Tauren or Troll. The one sourced profession link is **Tauren +15 Herbalism** [[d:shaman#1278437860797710387]]. **Plainsrunning** (Tauren) lets you throw Engineering grenades and drink invisibility potions at mount speed, "pretty OP" for world PvP [[d:shaman#1403992368960766043]], [[d:shaman#1426099162717487155]]. Racials otherwise don't affect profession choice (see [Races](index.md#races-and-racials)).

## Gold

- **Dire Maul East lashers** are the default shaman farm from ~52 [[d:shaman#1488144215681663127]], [[d:shaman#1420480071378010222]]. About 100 g+/hour on Nordanaar [[d:shaman#1425738262923972720]], or ~60 g per 5 lockouts, all 5 done in ~30 minutes [[d:shaman#1395550022006866032]]. Vendor trash is about 3-4 g per run, plus BoEs and herbs [[d:shaman#1473091287032004811]]. Lashers are nature-immune, so **Totem of Tides** is the biggest quality-of-life upgrade [[d:shaman#1426808984995631187]]. Running alt accounts on lashers is not a ToS violation [[d:shaman#1432599354543308930]].
- **Alternatives:** Dragonscale and Devilsaur farming (Skinning), Frostsaber and frost-giant Ekos, Ripe Bananas on Tel'Abim [[d:shaman#1419734720081825972]]. Shamans can also solo ZG [[d:pvp-general#1427693065103212555]].
- **Fresh-server caveat:** on Ambershire lasher profits crashed with herb oversupply and low raid demand [[d:shaman#1435308789216706580]]. Players found steadier returns there from Leatherworking/Tailoring cooldown crafts [[d:shaman#1419175037449015368]].
- **Gold sinks:** dual spec costs 350 g [[d:shaman#1396580889323896945]]. Consumables are expensive, and one player put the weapon-skill quest at about 500 g [[d:shaman#1374036484163633237]].
