# Leveling

**Recommendation:** level as **Enhancement**. Take Enhancement talents down to **Elemental Weapons and Stormstrike** (levels 30-33), then dip **11 points into Elemental for Elemental Focus** (Clearcasting, level 44), then finish Enhancement. From level 44 play **Frostbrand Weapon + Frost Shock + Water Shield**: Elemental Weapons makes Frostbrand procs auto-crit on a Frost-Shocked target, every crit gives Clearcasting, and mana stops being a problem. Keep the armor talents: the same build tanks dungeons, so you get fast queues and can respec at 60 for 5 g.

- "Enhancement is the only good spec for shaman leveling if you plan to do *any* solo content" [[d:shaman#1427654966088171600]]; "enh is faster all the way through 60, it's not even really close" [[d:shaman#1425585959390416976]].
- "Picking ele for any reason pre-40 is griefing yourself, and picking it pre-60 is still pretty sus but manageable" [[d:shaman#1455351699295437043]].
- Resto is "very hard to quest as" [[d:shaman#1469195633037541417]]; you can heal leveling dungeons with Intellect gear and no Resto talents [[d:shaman#1469113500256440371]].
- "Tank shaman hands down best plvler" for dungeon farming [[d:shaman#1480608160799653979]]; "the tanking build is ironically better while levelling anyway, especially pre 40" [[d:shaman#1433887832929472613]].

The consensus path is "putting talents into enh until ele weapons and stormstrike, then start talenting into ele until clearcasting" [[d:shaman#1490747804547813447]], [[d:shaman#1424480794470256773]]. If a talent gives you a new button, take it: the must-haves are Lightning Strike, Elemental Focus and Stormstrike [[d:shaman#1464200808060158111]].

- **10-14 Ancestral Knowledge**: row 1 must be filled first; Shield Specialization "gives you almost nothing, so why would you take it and then have to respec later" [[d:shaman#1455032665261609123]].
- **20 Lightning Strike** (Stable Shields 3/3 is its prerequisite): "lightning strike at 20 w/ 9 lightning shield orbs per cast" [[d:shaman#1407046819237855405]].
- **21-26 Ancestral Guardian and Spirit Armor**: squishiness fades at 21-23 with Ancestral Guardian and is gone with the second Spirit Armor point [[d:shaman#1472679669974175745]]; "armor talents in general are goated while leveling" [[d:shaman#1487165923549909133]]. - **30-33 Elemental Weapons and Stormstrike** [[d:shaman#1437957602431729715]].
- **34-44 Elemental to Elemental Focus** (Convection first for mana).
- **Flurry late**: "flurry is bad for leveling ... you don't crit that much and also fights are short" [[d:shaman#1487165798102470787]]. **Bloodlust last** [[d:shaman#1484700846573748416]].
- **Totemic Alignment** only helps if you tank; a pure-DPS leveler can spend those 2 points elsewhere (Frost's leveling build puts them in crit and swaps to Totemic Alignment only when tanking) [[d:shaman#1463500549491265597]].

## The pull / kill loop

**Levels 10-29 (Rockbiter):** `Rockbiter + Lightning Shield up` → `auto-attack` → `Lightning Strike on cooldown (from 20)` → `heal between mobs with spare mana, drink when low`. Don't spend mana on shocks: their damage per mana "is pretty poor" [[d:shaman#1439724223365910679]].

**Levels 30-43:** `Windfury (or Rockbiter)` → `Stormstrike on cooldown` → `Lightning Strike on cooldown` → `Earth Shock if the mob is about to die or you have spare mana`.

**Levels 44-60 (Frostbrand):**

1. Open with **Frost Shock** (rank 1 is enough to apply the debuff; the proc damage doesn't depend on the shock's rank) [[d:shaman#1437946685006282873]].
2. **Stormstrike**, **Lightning Strike**, auto-attack: every Frostbrand proc on the frosted target is a crit, every crit gives Clearcasting [[d:shaman#1437957602431729715]].
3. Refresh Frost Shock (now nearly free with Clearcasting); keep Water Shield up.

"Anytime you're in solo content, it's frostbrand+frost shock. It's too good not to use" [[d:shaman#1473068432533622794]].

**Totems while soloing:** mostly a waste of mana; Searing Totem is good, Stoneclaw buys time on multi-pulls, Earthbind delays a runner [[d:shaman#1482592822082277376]]. Fire Nova beats Magma for AoE while leveling [[d:shaman#1433891577528455219]].

## Resources and downtime

- Before 26 you have no Water Shield: fight with Rockbiter + Lightning Shield and drink [[d:shaman#1485784437395554515]].
- Lightning Shield when mana is high, Water Shield when low; save looted mana potions for chain pulls [[d:shaman#1456015634986041386]].
- From 44 (Frostbrand + Clearcasting + Water Shield) you have "near infinite mana unless you spam fire totems" [[d:shaman#1487775668908589147]].
- Stat priority while leveling a DPS: Strength ≈ Agility, then Stamina, Intellect; a leveling tank wants Stamina first [[d:shaman#1426594071451013221]], [[d:shaman#1427807560873939010]]. Gearing a leveling tank for Int/Spirit instead "falls apart fast" [[d:shaman#1474617456474460160]].

## Group role while leveling

- **Tank:** from 26 (Water Shield, Magma Totem) it gets comfortable; it is fully online at 44 with Clearcasting, mail and Elemental Weapons [[d:shaman#1459182669022887979]]. Loop: mark a skull, pull with rank 1 Lightning Bolt and line of sight, Stoneclaw + Fire Nova for AoE threat, then Earth Shock / Lightning Strike / Stormstrike; don't spend all your mana, Magma is "a huge mana drain" [[d:shaman#1488885982047768770]], [[d:shaman#1436372753165848626]]. See [Tank](tank.md).
- **DPS:** the tank build DPSes fine; "a series of crits or a chunky windfury will rip off the tank" [[d:shaman#1447643989183303740]].

### Healing dungeons while leveling

Intellect gear and your baseline heals are enough through the 40s [[d:shaman#1469113500256440371]]; by Zul'Farrak and certainly Sunken Temple you want Resto talents [[d:shaman#1437274154872344696]]. Healing Wave carries most dungeons [[d:shaman#1427723124308185148]].

## Elemental leveling (if you insist)

Painful before ~40: mana, pushback and low crit (Elemental Focus rarely procs) [[d:shaman#1426720089268424836]]. **Contested:** one player found Elemental "way lesser DPS and a lot of pushback" even with the best spell-power gear from 25 [[d:shaman#1468574204323430440]]; another "leveled as ele. It was fine" [[d:shaman#1468581745254338600]]. The 1.18.1 Call of Earth change helps: "you might just facetank and cast lightning bolt till it drops, it's your most mana efficient spell" [[d:shaman#1484719392439664833]]. Take Convection first if your crit is low [[d:shaman#1485976858523734117]]; rotation Flame Shock → Molten Blast refresh → Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning on Clearcasting [[d:shaman#1485686440070479892]].

## Races for leveling

Tauren is the comfiest leveler (+5% health, War Stomp, and Plainsrunning, which lets you run at mount speed even while water walking; "plainsrunning is irreplaceable once you've used it") [[d:shaman#1453148334968672441]], [[d:shaman#1484209931807756389]]; Orc is the best DPS race at 60 [[d:shaman#1460373099932422247]]. See [Index: Races](index.md#races-and-racials).

## Common mistakes

1. Going Elemental Focus before Stormstrike/Elemental Weapons ("a terrible choice") [[d:shaman#1424480661623935078]], [[d:shaman#1424480794470256773]].
2. Spamming shocks at low level (mana) [[d:shaman#1424416195003809922]].
3. Rockbiter in dungeons when you aren't tanking [[d:shaman#1455035581167046820]].
4. Buying Magma Totem, Flame Shock and every Lightning Bolt rank [[d:shaman#1436338711972548660]], [[d:shaman#1436341682109943878]].
5. Dropping all four totems on every solo pull [[d:shaman#1482592822082277376]].
6. Tanking Wailing Caverns before your armor talents; start at 26 in Shadowfang Keep [[d:shaman#1448921578992832714]].
7. Staying on Frostbrand at 60 for raids [[d:shaman#1473068658103423186]].
8. Expecting to dual wield (shamans can't) [[d:shaman#1318157235016171553]].
