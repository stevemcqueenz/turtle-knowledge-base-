Don't
—Wear full tier tank sets for threat; T2 shoulders, legs and boots with off-pieces.
—Let Shield Block overwrite the Improved Shield Slam charge unnoticed.
—Leave Defensive Stance without Defensive Tactics.
—Spend all rage before the next pull.
—Copy pre-rework talent lists (Improved Shield Block, Improved Sunder Armor no longer exist).
