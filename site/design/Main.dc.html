10 – 29 · Arms two-hander
Cruelty, Tactical Mastery and Improved Thunder Clap carry early dungeon tanking. Whirlwind Axe at 30.
30 – 59 · Sword and board
Take the defensive row first (Shield Specialization, Anticipation), then threat (Defiance), then Shield Slam at 40. Respec is 5 gold.
Alternative
Stay Arms (or dual-wield Fury) to 60 for faster questing, then respec once into the endgame build.
