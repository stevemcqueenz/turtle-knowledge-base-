01
Tank
ProtectionFavored, contestedDeep Protection with a shield. Best mitigation kit; threat sits behind bear and paladin.
Fury / ProtectionAlternativeThe pre-CC2 raid-tank meta, nerfed on purpose at CC2; still argued for on threat consistency.
02
Melee DPS
Fury, dual wieldFavoredThe raid DPS spec and the best-scaling DPS once geared; needs about 9% hit before dual wield works.
ArmsAlternativePreferred on add-heavy fights and at lower gear; Mortal Strike and Sweeping Strikes. Contested versus Fury since CC3.
Fury, 2H SlamNicheCalled the meta by one player in Feb 2026 and not a spec anymore by another.
03
PvP
ArmsFavoredThe PvP spec because of Mortal Strike; worthless solo, strongest class in PvP with support.
FuryNicheThe lone-wolf Blood Drinker dual-wield self-healing build after CC3; single source.
ProtectionNicheCalled a meme spec with a niche: stuns, Shield Slam dispel, 7k HP. No build published.
04
Leveling
Arms, two-handerFavoredThe majority pick in every era: Sweeping Strikes for AoE, Mortal Strike scales with weapon damage.
Fury, dual wieldAlternativeGrowing since CC2/CC3 on Blood Craze and Blood Drinker sustain; gated by hit.
Protection via dungeonsNicheDungeon-only leveling and instant queues; the majority say Arms tanks leveling dungeons fine.
