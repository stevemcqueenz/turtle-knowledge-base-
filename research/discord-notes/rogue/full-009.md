## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Sub]** No talent changes were originally announced for Subtlety, but Taste for Blood (shared assassination talent used by Sub too) is later confirmed nerfed with "no fix" given to Sub to compensate, which the community felt was unfair — [[d:rogue#1478068033015517391]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-02), [[d:rogue#1482042121073721474]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-12: "Sub got nerfed via TFB, for no reason. It didnt even get a fix. Bad").
- **[Assassination]** Taste for Blood (TFB) nerfed: damage bonus reduced to a 10% max cap, and it also no longer benefits Dissolvent/Corrosive poison (still benefits Corrosive per later correction) — [[d:rogue#1481467774055354530]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-12), [[d:rogue#1481469691565445212]] (Rheinwg: "TFB does affects corrosive"), later reiterated Dissolvent won't benefit from TFB post-patch [[d:rogue#1482703062203367546]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-15).
- **[Assassination]** Poison scaling nerf: Instant Poison scales 5% of AP vs Dissolvent's 4.75% of AP post-patch (previously both benefited from TFB) — [[d:rogue#1481460731823128607]], [[d:rogue#1481467774055354530]] (Matt Lender/Rheinwg, 2026-03-12). As a result, community expects to swap from Dissolvent to Instant Poison for non-immune targets after the patch, keeping Dissolvent/Corrosive only for nature-immune targets — [[d:rogue#1482703062203367546]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-15), confirmed again 2026-03-18: "Instant poison because higher base damage and rupture-buff wont affect dissolvent anymore" [[d:rogue#1483699804520583288]] (Nandieb).
- **[Estimated DPS impact]** Community estimate: Assassination takes roughly an 8-10% (some say 10-15%) overall DPS nerf; considered manageable, not spec-killing — [[d:rogue#1482401344169054413]] (Farban, 2026-03-12), [[d:rogue#1481951826583752825]] (riku: "10-15% nerf on assa", 2026-03-12), [[d:rogue#1483117594142834852]]/[[d:rogue#1483124086220783738]] (Kananaios/GumGuzz, "~10%", 2026-03-14), [[d:rogue#1483692871407239249]] (Istrefi, 2026-03-18: "Rogues are seeing roughly a 10%~ nerf ... nothing will change with their rotation").
- **[Blade of Eternal Darkness/OBD]** Confirmed nerfed specifically so that Rogue poison procs can no longer trigger it (other classes retain full functionality); Alcor's trinket itself is NOT nerfed — [[d:rogue#1479961666312470711]], [[d:rogue#1479961467674689629]] (Istrefi, 2026-03-08). Widely mourned as removing rogues' "most fun weapon" — [[d:rogue#1481443335792234622]] (Matt Lender, 2026-03-12).
- **[Combat]** Combat daggers "got nerfed, kinda deserved," while Sinister Strike (non-dagger Combat) got no fix; Cleave got a small buff — [[d:rogue#1482042121073721474]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-12).
- **[Dev communication]** Dev "Crite" confirmed no more balance changes before the current patch ships, but changes can follow afterward; players report having given "plenty of feedback" already — [[d:rogue#1478074715938750504]] (Crite, 2026-03-02), [[d:rogue#1481406158366576883]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-12).
- **[Community critique]** Matt Lender/Epickalen/Rheinwg/Zoobi extended debate (2026-03-12) on how Assassination should be fixed long-term: proposal to lower flat/early poison damage and raise AP-coefficient scaling instead, and to make Noxious Assault scale (partially) with weapon speed/damage so gear progression matters — [[d:rogue#1481443335792234622]], [[d:rogue#1481449576195035217]], [[d:rogue#1481450781403254815]], [[d:rogue#1481454259504681012]] (2026-03-12); Rheinwg: "i would rather lower the damage early on and making it a good spec later on... long term viability is way better than early viability" [[d:rogue#1481450364648554507]].
- **[Weaponskill/crit interaction]** Weaponskill (WS) gives ~0.04% crit per point of WS, mainhand only, confirmed via item testing (Mugger's Belt) — [[d:rogue#1478062839217262614]], [[d:rogue#1478068227505651864]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-02; corroborated by Epickalen [[d:rogue#1478064739446951966]]); order of item equip can affect whether this is correctly applied client-side (bug) — [[d:rogue#1481702051049377884]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-11).
- **[New raid weapon system]** New rogue class weapon (quest item, teased in a box) is expected either from BWL or a new boss; unconfirmed whether it's daggers, swords, fist, or throwing weapons — extensive community speculation but no concrete info — [[d:rogue#1481338042319900793]], [[d:rogue#1481467354331218023]] (Badknees/Rheinwg, 2026-03-11/12).
- **[Turtle-specific era note]** Dot poisons (Corrosive/Deadly) used to be the best Assassination poisons on Chromaggus specifically prior to 1.18, due to a nature vulnerability/invulnerability mechanic — [[d:rogue#1483654945155584101]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-18).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Leveling→endgame arc]** Consensus: Combat (any weapon type using Sinister Strike, slow weapon) is best while leveling until level 40; Assassination (Noxious Assault, fast daggers) becomes and remains best from 40 through early raiding; Combat daggers eventually overtake Assassination once gear/agility is sufficient (~late AQ40/Naxx) — [[d:rogue#1479581076275073034]] (Rheinwg guide, 2026-03-07), [[d:rogue#1482316915048317041]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-13), [[d:rogue#1482670832588292106]] (Yoridzen, 2026-03-14).
- **[Combat-vs-Assassination breakpoint]** Contested/uncertain exact crossover point. Some say "no one knows where is the breaking point exactly," citing precedent from another server ("South Seas") where Combat overtook Assassination in late AQ40 — [[d:rogue#1478927190476194105]], [[d:rogue#1478926915430649857]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-05). Others estimate Naxx, not before AQ40 — [[d:rogue#1478603160154144922]] (Zoobi, 2026-03-04). After the nerf, Rheinwg expects Combat playable in "early AQ40" — [[d:rogue#1481972531278774315]]; riku disputes that BWL alone gives enough extra agility to flip the matchup — [[d:rogue#1481973127218200648]], [[d:rogue#1481973221677862964]] (2026-03-13).
- **[Ambershire vs Nord divergence]** On the newer Ambershire server (pre-BWL/AQ), meta is Noxious Assault (Assassination) with 4pc Darkmantle/4pc Stormshroud and 1.3-speed daggers; on the older Nordanaar server (further progressed), endgame is still the classic 31/20-style hybrid — [[d:rogue#1479643284946157840]] (Epickalen, 2026-03-07).
- **[PvP]** Noxious Assault (Nox) is the best current PvP rogue build; Hemo-spam struggles against tanky classes (shamans, prot paladins) because many PvP classes on Turtle are unusually tanky compared to vanilla — [[d:rogue#1478232558603997366]], [[d:rogue#1478239052900597850]] (riku, 2026-03-03). Assassination confirmed "on top atm" in PvP as of 2026-03-10 — [[d:rogue#1480532019934134306]] (martinjir1).
- **[Fresh-60/farming]** Assassination strongly recommended for a fresh level-60 doing 5-man dungeons/gearing: "any 1.3 daggers, put poisons on them and that's all" — [[d:rogue#1480924045263245353]] (feeder5, 2026-03-10); reiterated for HC/fresh-60 gearing as "super easy, super strong at fresh 60" — [[d:rogue#1483571078209995034]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-18).
- **[Rogue tank]** Community-built "tank rogue" using Agitating Poison for threat can tank many Naxx bosses (video evidence) but lacks a taunt and has weak AoE threat, so is risky in a raid with high-DPS ("juicer") comps — [[d:rogue#1479139872018137099]], [[d:rogue#1479140876407279677]], [[d:rogue#1479142217166749767]] (Nettlefield/Istrefi, 2026-03-06). Ongoing "TANK ROGUE PROPAGANDA" community campaign to petition devs for a physical version of Agitating Poison via forum posts — [[d:rogue#1478692977504424026]] (Nettlefield, 2026-03-04). By 2026-03-14 Nettlefield reports the tank build is "functional" and can tank in endgame Naxx raids [[d:rogue#1479139872018137099]].
- **[Fun/flavor subjective take]** Assassination's core rotation is viewed as simple ("buff up, press AA, go for coffee"); Subtlety is considered the most button-intensive/fun spec; Combat/Backstab is seen as more positional/skill-expressive — [[d:rogue#1478752514290745436]], [[d:rogue#1478752295561859174]] (Ionnatra, 2026-03-04).
- **[Sub as raid support]** Sub is explicitly a support spec: one Sub rogue per raid is typical (rest go Assa/Combat); a well-played Sub can nonetheless place highly on parses (one of the top 10 Ambershire rogues is Sub) — [[d:rogue#1483258210159693896]] (Vegybird, 2026-03-17), [[d:rogue#1483479006962647263]] (Istrefi, 2026-03-17).
- **["Griefing" debate]** Extended thread (2026-03-15) on whether playing sub-optimal specs (e.g. Combat pre-BIS on Ambershire, full-Ambush builds) constitutes "griefing" the raid: consensus is that playing a worse-but-functional spec in appropriate gear is fine, but showing up under-geared/under-talented (e.g., 0 talent points, prebis Combat instead of Assassination when raid-ready) is considered self-griefing / raid-griefing — [[d:rogue#1482443230233624618]] (Purge), [[d:rogue#1482446301923905547]] (Heffey), [[d:rogue#1482451677083729970]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-03-15).

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[Assassination raid build, pre-patch]** ~41/10/0: Noxious Assault + Seal of Fate (5/5) + Cold Blood (1pt) + 5% hit talent on Combat side — [[d:rogue#1480328644126838846]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-08).
- **[Assassination — Cold Blood vs. Improved Eviscerate]** Recommended 2/2 Improved Eviscerate + 1pt Cold Blood over pure 5% Evis damage, because Cold Blood + Noxious Assault (with Seal of Fate) can grant 2 combo points instead of 1 once per fight, letting you reach 5-point Rupture and improve Taste for Blood uptime — [[d:rogue#1480513960317943900]], [[d:rogue#1480514558064857152]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-09).
- **[Assassination, post-patch adjustment]** Because TFB loses priority after the nerf, Rheinwg plans to drop Seal of Fate (less need for the extra combo point since TFB uptime matters less) and move points instead into Dirty Tricks; if fully geared, thinks this is a net improvement — [[d:rogue#1482118628726935694]], [[d:rogue#1482131071465426986]], [[d:rogue#1482131213966639135]], [[d:rogue#1482131399195758782]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-13/14).
- **[Assassination poisons debate]** Ongoing, unresolved debate: Lethality vs Improved Poisons vs Efficient Poisons — [[d:rogue#1478602374720393258]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-04; open question). By late chunk, Efficient Poisons argued to be "way better than dw spec" for Combat daggers — [[d:rogue#1483248389884739688]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-16).
- **[Combat swords endgame]** 20/31 build: Taste for Blood + Adrenaline Rush core, using sword talents instead of dagger talents — [[d:rogue#1480185731283681330]] (Epickalen, 2026-03-08).
- **[Combat daggers endgame]** With full T3, recommended: wear agi pieces (e.g. T2.5 chest over T3 chest), spam Backstab, maintain SnD/TfB, use Deadly(5)+Corrosive(2) instead of Dissolvent — [[d:rogue#1480446443570462781]], [[d:rogue#1480449204328333353]], [[d:rogue#1480449695544381500]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-09).
- **[Sub support build/talent priorities]** For leveling into Sub-support builds: order is roughly Relentless Strikes → Malice → Lethality/Seal of Fate depending on build; for endgame Sub, want Relentless Strikes and Improved Blade Tactics — [[d:rogue#1482345027060367370]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-13), [[d:rogue#1483175692710445096]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-17).
- **[Sub — Combat/Sub hybrid ("Blade Rush sub") debated]** A hybrid combat/sub build sacrificing Ruthlessness/Relentless Strikes for Blade Rush access was discussed; verdict: not worth it — losing group-support talents (Ruthlessness, Relentless) for Blade Rush is "too much," especially since Taste for Blood already fills that role; suggested a specific talent link (https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=AoYDIgY--FQCYbBBARKQYB) as a middle ground with Tricks of the Trade at 3/5 or 4/5 — [[d:rogue#1480907812270768171]], [[d:rogue#1480909447495811237]], [[d:rogue#1480909899037933726]] (Rheinwg/Matt Lender, 2026-03-09).
- **[Sub Kiss vs Slayer's Crest]** Slayer's Crest preferred over Kiss of the Spider for better synergy with Mark of Death + Shadow (can pop Slayer for the first Mark then get a second Mark+Shadow window within Slayer's duration if fight is long enough; most 5-mans <2min so limited value) — [[d:rogue#1478414333460349001]], [[d:rogue#1478415249802793210]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-02).
- **[Sub — Shadow of Death]** Recommended to grab and use alongside Mark of Death, ideally after TFB triggers; "too big to skip" if not running Rupture — [[d:rogue#1479532923655950466]], [[d:rogue#1482092618644193411]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-06/13). Some players (e.g. Badknees) explicitly skip it as "finicky and not reliable, needs everyone on the same page," preferring a pure SnD/Hemo/Eviscerate/TotT loop — [[d:rogue#1482081308095283362]] (Badknees, 2026-03-13).
- **[Sub — Rupture vs Eviscerate]** For Sub, Rupture's extra damage is "not particularly good, especially on short fights"; better to use Eviscerate on trash unless the full DoT duration can be used — [[d:rogue#1482394912585421042]], [[d:rogue#1482395189703348476]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-14). Istrefi's own Sub priority: 1CP SnD → 1CP Envenom → 5CP Rupture → 1CP SnD → 1CP Envenom → 5CP Evis, noting Rupture's damage buff also boosts Noxious Assault's base damage and is used to build 5-stack Relentless for a bigger 5CP Evis — [[d:rogue#1483690580809879666]], [[d:rogue#1483692292027191356]], [[d:rogue#1483693639237369918]] (Istrefi, 2026-03-18).
- **[Sub weapon choice]** Sub does NOT use daggers — wants a strong slow high-damage mainhand (Deathbringer, Viskag from Onyxia, or Gressil) for big Hemo/finisher hits; fast weapon type doesn't matter for offhand beyond speed — [[d:rogue#1480381217907019908]], [[d:rogue#1482354344031424624]], [[d:rogue#1482078490890338537]] (Rheinwg/Badknees, 2026-03-09/13). AQ20 2/3 tier set called "extremely good" pre-Naxx alongside Darkmantle — [[d:rogue#1480381468038660308]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-09).
- **[BoED/spellstrike build]** Niche Assassination build stacking spellstrike + on-hit poison items so poisons/spellstrike trigger Blade of Eternal Darkness's shadow proc; situationally viable pre-patch but weak overall and getting hard-nerfed (poisons will no longer trigger BOED) — [[d:rogue#1478494226932043920]], [[d:rogue#1480454039035580478]] (Epickalen/Matt Lender, 2026-03-04/09); requires level 49 minimum for BOED itself — [[d:rogue#1480480317037875280]] (Im pro vise, 2026-03-09).

## Leveling
- **[Power spikes]** Assassination gets major power spikes at levels 30 and 40 — [[d:rogue#1479248496262647858]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-06); confirmed elsewhere "Assa also has a powerspike at lvl 30" — [[d:rogue#1482365708724601006]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-12).
- **[Brand-new player rotation]** For total beginners: use Sinister Strike + Slice and Dice at any CP count; avoid Backstab and Eviscerate until later; use a slow, high-DPS mainhand (axe/sword/mace ≥2.60 speed ideally); avoid daggers until level 40; open with Cheap Shot once available to reduce incoming damage — [[d:rogue#1478390151750357075]], [[d:rogue#1478391183498543146]], [[d:rogue#1478390671332081664]], [[d:rogue#1478390323825610794]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-02).
- **[Combat leveling with a tank friend]** Backstab spec viable/fun if a friend tank holds threat; example link for first ~28 levels: https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=CoYAI-FAD- (Combat: Opportunity+Backstab) — [[d:rogue#1478408282933559377]], [[d:rogue#1478408865740619817]] (Matt Lender/Rheinwg, 2026-03-02).
- **[General leveling order]** 8 points into Combat then respec into Assassination once that tree "gets busted"; suggested follow-up order Precision → Lethality → Taste for Blood if doing a lot of dungeons — [[d:rogue#1478408669409443922]], [[d:rogue#1478409904946679870]] (Matt Lender, 2026-03-02).
- **[Standard 40-level swap]** Level as Combat (or slow-weapon Assassination talents) until 40, then respec to Assassination with fast daggers (1.3–1.6 speed) and Noxious Assault; considered "the best way" to level fast because deep Assassination poison damage is disproportionately strong at low level — [[d:rogue#1478339836275462154]] (Antur, 2026-03-02), [[d:rogue#1480215833929187371]], [[d:rogue#1480215937914241237]] (Kyrsie, 2026-03-08). "You can literally spec Assassination talents but use slow weapons/swords until 40 with Noxious Assault as filler" is an alternate hybrid path — [[d:rogue#1481630936326996130]] (Kyrsie, 2026-03-11).
- **[Weapon speed sweet spot pre-40]** 1.4–1.6 speed is the practical sweet spot for Noxious Assault leveling (any gray/white/green weapon works since Noxious ignores weapon damage) — [[d:rogue#1478496355386396954]] (Epickalen, 2026-03-04); reiterated any 1.3-1.4 weapon without enchant "will easily carry you to 60," the poisons do the heavy lifting — [[d:rogue#1483097293434654720]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-16).
- **[Frontstabbing]** Walking through a mob's hitbox (aided by lag/server tick) to land Backstab from the front is a known but finicky leveling technique; considered unreliable/unfun by many, with Combat swords suggested as a much more chill alternative if frontstab isn't enjoyable — [[d:rogue#1479581948023406642]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-07), [[d:rogue#1481629076434321561]] (Badknees, 2026-03-11), [[d:rogue#1483603774005317632]] (Epickalen, 2026-03-18: "I personally found frontstabbing unfun"), [[d:rogue#1481630866823446609]] (Kyrsie: "it works but you'll get sick of it quickly").
- **[Weapon-type matching for Combat leveling]** If leveling Combat via Sinister Strike with sword/axe, use matching weapon types in both hands (don't mix, e.g., dagger MH + sword OH) to avoid wasted weapon-skill investment — [[d:rogue#1479579458192277777]] (Istrefi, 2026-03-07).
- **[Community guide]** Rheinwg's public leveling/build guide (with weapon-choice videos, e.g. frontstabbing) at https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tV2Xq9NIrwaeiotC-CgtuESwj75LdV7fGoGctRxpp98 — referenced repeatedly through the whole chunk as the default answer for "is there a leveling guide" — [[d:rogue#1479580058921337015]], [[d:rogue#1481346816623771709]], [[d:rogue#1483184630990508193]].
- **[Low-level rotation]** Suggested: opener (Ambush/SS/Backstab) → Slice and Dice → spam SS/Backstab → refresh SnD; Ambush = opener; Rupture worse than Eviscerate until Taste for Blood; Expose Armor only situational (long fights) — [[d:rogue#1479922062515503124]], [[d:rogue#1479923143920451656]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-07).
- **[No wrong way]** Multiple experts (Antez, Epickalen, Badknees) repeatedly reassure new players there's no wrong way to level and switching to Assassination is optional flavor, not mandatory, before 60: "you cannot mess up a rogue leveling" — [[d:rogue#1478495114144911590]] (Epickalen, 2026-03-04), "There is no wrong way to level up" — [[d:rogue#1483207183763243171]] (Antez, 2026-03-17).
- **[Professions & leveling speed]** Debate: gathering professions (Herb+Alch, Skinning+LW) don't slow leveling much and add gold; Mining/Engineering and Fishing are considered actual time sinks. Consensus (Nandieb): professions are inefficient to level while leveling the character; better to level professions and farm gold at 60. Others (Vistarrk, Epickalen, Badknees) disagree, citing real gold-per-hour data (30-60g/h) and enjoyment — [[d:rogue#1481674325772992537]], [[d:rogue#1481710641365647533]], [[d:rogue#1481680568793043105]], [[d:rogue#1481774944802050211]] (2026-03-12/13).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Assassination core loop]** Spam Noxious Assault (poison-based, ignores weapon damage) to maintain Envenom, Slice and Dice, and Rupture uptimes; free combo points from Ruthlessness go to Eviscerate — summarized as "Mainly just noxx spam and use 5pt rupture followed by SnD and envenom with the free 1 point returns" — [[d:rogue#1478779997878485167]] (Scruff, 2026-03-04); alternate ordering per Delfuego (2026-03-12): 1pt SnD, 1pt Envenom, 5pt Rupture, Evis until any of SnD/Envenom/Rupture needs renewing — [[d:rogue#1482050386637488323]]; Istrefi's later (post-patch-aware) version: 1CP SnD → 1CP Envenom → 5CP Rupture → 1CP SnD → 1CP Envenom → 5CP Evis — [[d:rogue#1483690580809879666]] (2026-03-18).
- **[Combo generator logic]** If the combo-point generator scales off weapon damage (Sinister Strike, Backstab), use the slowest hard-hitting weapon; if it's Noxious Assault (ignores weapon damage), use the fastest weapon for more poison-proc applications — [[d:rogue#1478246343779549336]] (Epickalen, 2026-03-02).
- **[Sinister Strike/dodge-parry energy refund]** Confirmed and extensively tested: if a combo generator (Sinister Strike, Hemorrhage, Noxious Assault) is dodged/parried, it costs only ~7 energy instead of full cost (~82.5% refund), explaining an old community mystery about "losing 1-2 energy" — tested over ~300 casts by GumGuzz — [[d:rogue#1480274083160789204]], [[d:rogue#1480276507707441434]], [[d:rogue#1480285325006995639]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-08).
- **[Combo-point target-switch mechanic / "CP transfer" technique]** Combo points are tied to a single target; switching targets normally loses points, but using a 1-CP finisher (SnD, Envenom, or Kidney Shot) on a new target while Ruthlessness is talented can generate 1 CP on that new target even after switching — used deliberately to pre-apply SnD/Envenom on a boss right before engage by finishing off the last trash add, or to land a full-duration Kidney Shot on an add while retaining raid-boss combo points conceptually — confirmed via dummy testing and Naxx ghoul-pack use — [[d:rogue#1480688682787667988]], [[d:rogue#1480689081926029462]], [[d:rogue#1480689813349994668]], [[d:rogue#1480700039386104012]] (Midfinger/Rheinwg/hybris, 2026-03-10).
- **[Finisher downgrade bug]** Reported bug: double-pressing a finisher can accidentally downgrade a 5-point Rupture into a 1-point Rupture; community discussed a Lua/macro check (HasBuffByTexture / GetBuffTimeLeftByTexture helper functions shared by GumGuzz) to block a recast if the current buff duration is still long — [[d:rogue#1479064018260856862]], [[d:rogue#1479066940382253117]], [[d:rogue#1479067034456166501]] (Matt Lender/GumGuzz, 2026-03-05).
- **[Hemo vs Sinister Strike math]** Hemorrhage deals 110% weapon damage (114% counting the -armor-esque debuff modifier); Sinister Strike deals base weapon damage + a flat bonus; worked example: 100-damage weapon → Hemo ≈114, SS ≈100+63 depending on rank — [[d:rogue#1483543853356286112]], [[d:rogue#1483543915171811469]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-18). With Improved Hemorrhage (2/2 Bloody Mess), Hemo costs 35 energy vs SS's 40, so 5CP via Hemo needs only 175 energy vs 200 for SS — [[d:rogue#1483551941475958996]] (Vegybird, 2026-03-18) — though per-hit damage math still favors SS slightly at low level per a worked example (72 dmg weapon: Hemo ≈82 at 35 energy vs SS ≈87 at 40 energy) — [[d:rogue#1483547446830174359]], [[d:rogue#1483550291646484682]] (Dinousek, 2026-03-17).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Blade Flurry]** Toggle ability (not on cooldown) that reduces personal damage and energy regen while active in exchange for cleaving; falls off in value once single-target damage/poison uptime is prioritized. Assassination should generally avoid using Blade Flurry because it prevents poison application on the second target and the energy loss "doesn't make up" for it — [[d:rogue#1482364537549230080]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-13), [[d:rogue#1482369323988291584]], [[d:rogue#1482369480439890084]] (Kyrsie, 2026-03-13).
- **[Blade Flurry vs buff-cap bug]** Known issue: Blade Flurry's toggle can conflict with the ~32-buff display cap in long fights; a workaround macro cancels other tracked buffs (Healing Way, Renew, Rejuvenation, Bloodlust, etc.) above a stack/count threshold before toggling Blade Flurry back on — full macro shared by Heffey (2026-03-14) — [[d:rogue#1482234570983342172]], [[d:rogue#1482917430350188646]] (Galundra/Estocada asking if a dev fix is coming, 2026-03-16).
- **[Rogue tank AoE]** Rogue tanks lack strong AoE threat; the community workaround is cleaving and rapidly swapping targets across a pull to maintain threat, since there's no taunt — [[d:rogue#1479145606654988469]], [[d:rogue#1479143372999364822]] (Nettlefield/Istrefi, 2026-03-06).
- **[Sub support AoE limitation]** A Sub-support rogue trying to also use Blade Flurry on trash struggles to keep enough combo points for Tricks of the Trade uptime simultaneously — [[d:rogue#1479550395453018322]] (Scruff, 2026-03-06).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Poison crit]** Poisons cannot normally crit (scale off AP, not crit); Spell Crit sources (mage Atiesh, boomkin, Dreamshard Elixir) can make poisons crit via the spell-crit interaction — [[d:rogue#1478543997378105355]], [[d:rogue#1478544107633639475]] (TwistedMindz, 2026-03-04).
- **[Assassination stat priority]** After hit cap: Hit = Haste > AP > Rest (since poisons can't normally crit) — [[d:rogue#1479179762542055465]] (Vanikeks, 2026-03-07).
- **[AP-equivalent weight estimates, Nox spec]** Community estimate: Haste ≈25-28 AP-equiv, Hit ≈22-24, Crit ≈18-20, STR ≈1.1, AGI ≈1.6-1.7 (agi valued above raw AP-per-point because it also grants ~1% crit per 29 agi, plus dodge) — [[d:rogue#1479182385210855576]], [[d:rogue#1479193182024503386]] (Oshy, 2026-03-06); Epickalen: Agi weighted lower outside Combat — [[d:rogue#1479190907063631997]]. Later reiterated by Nandieb: haste has NO diminishing returns and should always be prioritized over crit as Assassination; 1% haste ≈ 25-30 AP-equivalent (per riku) — [[d:rogue#1483699455831314525]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-18), [[d:rogue#1483106563328573471]] (riku, 2026-03-17).
- **[Yellow hit cap and weaponskill]** Yellow (special-ability) hit cap is 5-8% depending on weaponskill; each weaponskill point gives 0.2% hit (5 WS ≈1% hit, doesn't round up for partial amounts); hit applies to both mainhand and offhand simultaneously — [[d:rogue#1480323880240550142]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-09), [[d:rogue#1478922893240041583]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1483378938742046852]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-17, restated: 0.2%/WS, 27% total hit needed to never miss autoattacks). Weaponskill 315 is the cap for glancing-blow damage reduction (65% dmg at 300 WS → 95% at 315 WS) — [[d:rogue#1483380802283896834]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-17).
- **[Buff/consumable stacking rules]** Same-stat consumables generally don't stack: Mongoose (agi) doesn't stack with Agility Potion; Black Juju (firewater-type) doesn't stack with Winterfall Firewater; Green Juju doesn't stack with Giants elixir; exception: Blasted Lands buff DOES stack with Mongoose/other stat buffs; Agility food (berries) stacks with other agi buffs; R.O.I.D.S/Str/Agi Scrolls do NOT stack with other consumes (Scorpok elixir is fine) — [[d:rogue#1479002958052982927]], [[d:rogue#1479004139613589674]], [[d:rogue#1479004361744056492]], [[d:rogue#1479004671807979530]], [[d:rogue#1479004777621749783]] (GumGuzz/Nandieb, 2026-03-05); [[d:rogue#1482473166994542693]] (riku, 2026-03-15).
- **[Buff cap]** ~32 buff display cap; buffs beyond that still function but can't be tracked/canceled via UI — [[d:rogue#1479003141528748033]], [[d:rogue#1479005670832341074]], [[d:rogue#1479005749836251216]] (GumGuzz/"30"/Nandieb, 2026-03-05).
- **[Dissolvent Poison / Fiery enchant scaling]** Dissolvent Poison does NOT scale with Spell Power (myth debunked; scales from AP) — [[d:rogue#1479924708144189461]], [[d:rogue#1479925112445600001]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-05). Fiery weapon enchant does NOT scale with spell power either (unlike some other lifesteal effects, e.g. Hakkari Manslayer's lifesteal, which scales 1:1 with SP) — [[d:rogue#1480483975859535964]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-09).
- **[Weapon-speed break-even math]** Rule of thumb (level-40 example, not universal): +6 DPS needed on a mainhand 0.1s slower to offset lost poison procs from Assassination Noxious Assault; offhand needs ~double (~12 DPS) since offhand carries an inherent damage penalty — [[d:rogue#1480577573330354247]], [[d:rogue#1480583999301615838]], [[d:rogue#1480584068025417820]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-09).
- **[Weapon normalization]** Instant/special attacks (Backstab, Hemorrhage, etc.) use normalized (average) weapon damage rather than damage scaled directly by weapon speed — confirmed via in-sim testing to match DPS-meter values within 0.2 DPS for either a 1.5-speed or 2.8-speed weapon at equal AP — [[d:rogue#1481579150883029024]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-11), discussion started by Matt Lender comparing Deathbringer vs Edge of Chaos top-end damage — [[d:rogue#1481573904278356040]], [[d:rogue#1481576140744429701]].
- **[Weapon proc mechanics — Servo Arm]** Misplaced Servo Arm is an equip-triggered "aura" (not a chance-on-hit item proc), meaning it can be triggered by ANY of your weapon hits (autoattacks, combo-point builders, Eviscerate, extra-attack procs like Windfury), not just its own weapon's swings; roughly a 15% PPM-scaled aura per Servo Arm (so ~30% combined with 2 equipped); confirmed Noxious Assault also procs it since it's an aura, not an item-hit proc — [[d:rogue#1480251199331762228]], [[d:rogue#1480278051173568542]], [[d:rogue#1481604550883410041]], [[d:rogue#1481607169798574131]], [[d:rogue#1481024192798396678]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-08/09/11).
- **[Extra-attack proc interactions]** Detailed rules from cross-referencing the shaman theorycrafting channel: Windfury only affects mainhand, has an ICD, counts as an autoattack (can miss/dodge/glance); Ironfoe always procs the mainhand weapon (self or other weapon if Ironfoe is offhand), can miss/dodge/glance; Hand of Justice, Thrash Blade, Flurry Axe, and Hack and Slash proc on their own weapon (so an offhand extra-attack swings on offhand) and CANNOT miss or be dodged (possibly can't be parried/blocked, untested from dummy's front); these procs cannot chain-trigger each other — [[d:rogue#1481610202251001968]], [[d:rogue#1481610861746458666]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-11). Hack and Slash confirmed via 1-hour dummy testing to not miss/dodge/parry — [[d:rogue#1480083268798906460]], [[d:rogue#1480303747543466165]], [[d:rogue#1480303890074439774]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-08/09).
- **[Crusader PPM behavior]** Testing suggests Crusader's proc rate scales with haste (faster weapons via haste proc it more often) rather than behaving as a fixed ICD; base weapon speed alone does not change the PPM, but increasing haste increases the effective PPM proportionally — [[d:rogue#1480280410201264462]], [[d:rogue#1480280456413843558]], [[d:rogue#1480604883077038170]] (GumGuzz/Rheinwg, 2026-03-08/09).
- **[Combo generator refund]** Beyond Sinister Strike, Hemorrhage and Noxious Assault also get the ~82.5% energy refund on dodge/parry — [[d:rogue#1480278046329143597]], [[d:rogue#1480278754847621130]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-08; harder to test on Backstab since it requires positioning behind target).
- **[Crit cap mechanic]** Boss-level targets (3 levels above player) apply a 3% crit-chance reduction to attacker's autoattacks; crit cap only applies to autoattacks — once at cap, all autoattacks resolve as crit/glance/miss, never a plain "hit" — [[d:rogue#1483660333305561127]], [[d:rogue#1483660863650005154]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-18).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[Pre-raid/fresh-60]** Standard progression: farm Shadowcraft (T0.5) set + Stormshroud as budget alternative, then Darkmantle questline (~500g) once affordable; buy Stormshroud only as a placeholder if gold allows, otherwise save for Darkmantle + epic mount — [[d:rogue#1479741646886273136]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-07), [[d:rogue#1482822679625990332]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-15).
- **[Darkmantle vs Stormshroud mechanics]** Darkmantle 4-set is a PPM-based energy-return proc (works well with any weapon speed); Stormshroud 4-set is a flat chance-on-hit proc (favors faster weapons, more attacks = more procs) — [[d:rogue#1482407517756850322]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-16). Both sets equippable in early 50s. Recommended default: 6-set Darkmantle + ACLG + Clutch of Hivaxxis is commonly cited as BiS for Assassination pre-BWL — [[d:rogue#1479955248301736159]], [[d:rogue#1480325029416927373]] (Istrefi/Kyrsie, 2026-03-08/09) — though 4pc Darkmantle/4pc Stormshroud is also viable with higher variance/ceiling — [[d:rogue#1479362680568414304]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-06).
- **[Combat gear needs]** Combat needs T2.5 Chest/Gloves/Boots (or equivalents: Gloves of Enforcement/Primordial Burrower, Wormhide Boots) to be competitive; some Naxx pieces are bad for Combat due to 0 agility — [[d:rogue#1478923047317541054]], [[d:rogue#1478930583668391956]] (Rheinwg/Tumballa, 2026-03-05).
- **[Trinket priorities, Assassination]** General order (pre-ES/BWL): Whip > Potent Venoms (surprisingly good on non-immune bosses, more DPS than 2%hit/2%crit/HoJ) > HoJ > 2%hit > 2%crit, but use the 2%hit trinket specifically if not yet yellow-hit-capped — [[d:rogue#1480670451108679864]], [[d:rogue#1480671040295272580]] (Matt Lender, 2026-03-09). Later refinement: any 2 of {Whip, Royal Seal, HoJ, Vial of Potent Venoms} are all "relatively close," with Heart of Dreams/DFT as upgrades — [[d:rogue#1483132857042862080]], [[d:rogue#1483133168419733504]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-14). Royal Seal (2% hit) argued to outperform 2% haste trinkets at low hit% because hit rating amplifies the value of haste too — [[d:rogue#1483339667649855489]], [[d:rogue#1483341099983704127]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-17).
- **[Trinket swap technique]** "Vanish trinket swap": pop an active trinket (e.g. Venomous Totem), let the buff run its course, then Vanish (removes combat briefly) to swap in a different trinket without losing uptime; addons like TrinketMenu automate the swap and handle raid-combat-tick timing; newer TrinketMenu versions allow queuing the swap out of combat via shift-click — [[d:rogue#1479599348001276056]], [[d:rogue#1479599791670431895]], [[d:rogue#1479855141090758718]], [[d:rogue#1482474635193876593]] (2026-03-07/15).
- **[Assassination BWL-transition gear]** With BWL live on Ambershire, cited setup: 4pc Darkmantle + 3pc Bloodfang (or 3pc T2) + MC dagger-skill gloves; belt/neck/finger/trinket BWL items debated (e.g., Dragonhide Belt vs Clutch of Hivaxxis: 19 AP vs 1% haste + 0.27 crit) — [[d:rogue#1480975091373183016]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-10), [[d:rogue#1483178588906983496]] (henbob, 2026-03-17).
- **[Combat/Sword BWL gear]** Deathbringer / Edge of Chaos (13 agi, 2.9 speed) as mainhand candidates; Crystal (2.8 speed axe-type, gives weaponskill + hit) increasingly favored as offhand once available on Amber, since offhand full-damage-penalty makes raw damage less relevant there — [[d:rogue#1481579055747694715]], [[d:rogue#1481579810860830771]] (Matt Lender/Rheinwg, 2026-03-11).
- **[Set-swap math example, Assassination]** Worked comparison: swapping T2 Chest in for offpieces gains ~1.31% crit (poison bonus) but loses 1% hit, ~15 AP (with Kings), and 1% haste — considered a close/marginal call — [[d:rogue#1481008819411620095]], [[d:rogue#1481010553232031764]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-11).
- **[Weapon farm targets]** Notable BiS/near-BiS weapons cited through the chunk: Assassination MH/OH — Julie's Dagger, Distracting Dagger (2% hit from set), Finkle's Skinner, Alcor's Sunrazer, Fang of Venoxis, The Ripper, Deathbringer, Dragonfang Blade (getting buffed next patch to be better than Perdition's Blade) — [[d:rogue#1483420007193903258]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-17), [[d:rogue#1480450493296672842]], [[d:rogue#1481974720378179597]]. Sub/Combat MH — Deathbringer, Viskag (from Onyxia), Gressil, Castigator — [[d:rogue#1481339424364167373]], [[d:rogue#1481339496749601014]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-11); Sub OH — ZG Warblade (+40 AP, long-lasting), or Corehound Tooth as a stopgap — [[d:rogue#1482078827973840946]] (Badknees, 2026-03-13).
- **[Farming difficulty]** Darkmantle described as "one of the worst grinds in the game" (riku needed 50+ Scholomance runs for legs, 40+ for shoulders); Distracting Dagger from Deadmines-West notoriously contested/rare due to rogue competition — [[d:rogue#1480226616314433678]] (riku, 2026-03-08), [[d:rogue#1483556657538662623]]/[[d:rogue#1483556824757047397]] (Dinousek giving up on farming DD, 2026-03-17).
- **[Turtle-specific items]** Notable Turtle-only/rare items: The Ripper offhand (1% drop chance from Damian, Stormwind Vault, soloable at 60 with T0.5 gear) called "insane proc rate," better than a single Annihilator — [[d:rogue#1482760981221216428]], [[d:rogue#1482771687664783607]] (Rheinwg/ohcheese, 2026-03-14); Annihilator and Shart'ateth Axe share a debuff slot and do NOT stack — don't run both together — [[d:rogue#1483262942748086434]] (Vegybird, 2026-03-17).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Weapon enchant choice, Combat]** Crusader on mainhand + 15 Agility on offhand is a common Combat choice; endgame Combat daggers favor double Agility for energy regen and crit; Crusader isn't bad but the "correct" choice depends on spec — [[d:rogue#1478085453457657966]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-02, "double agi for combat, double crus for the other 2"), [[d:rogue#1481157003064901663]] (Epickalen, 2026-03-11), [[d:rogue#1483492324599136581]]/[[d:rogue#1483492585791033365]] (Scruff, 2026-03-17: "15 agi for combat, crusader for assassin").
- **[Weapon enchant choice, Assassination]** Agility valued higher than Crusader once past early leveling, for energy-regen and crit stacking; general guidance to pick one type and double up rather than mixing Crusader MH / Agi OH — [[d:rogue#1480283140462215252]] (Epickalen, 2026-03-08).
- **[Shoulder/leg/head enchants]** Sub BiS: Shoulders — Zandalar tribe haste enchant pre-Naxx, later a Sapphiron enchant (slightly less AP, +crit); Head/Legs — ZG agility enchants (require Zandalar rep) — [[d:rogue#1481776341656277112]], [[d:rogue#1481778011035664454]], [[d:rogue#1481777760396382250]] (Ionnatra/Rheinwg/Badknees, 2026-03-12/13). ZG shoulder-haste (2%) enchant vs Naxx-set alternative debated as roughly equal trade-offs (AP/crit vs haste) — [[d:rogue#1478829616457318773]], [[d:rogue#1478830312296415274]] (Antez/Epickalen, 2026-03-04).
- **[Poison choice by content, pre-patch]** Trash: Dissolvent (armor-ignoring, non-magic, works vs nature immunity/resistance); Bosses immune to nature/poison-type effects: still use Dissolvent since it isn't a true "nature" application in the immunity sense; general combat bosses: swap based on Envenom usage — [[d:rogue#1480259592498712587]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-08). Corrosive is explicitly the DoT version of Dissolvent — [[d:rogue#1483609576820441098]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-18).
- **[Poison choice, Combat]** Deadly(5) + Corrosive(2) recommended for bosses; Dissolvent(2)/Instant on trash — [[d:rogue#1480259592498712587]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-08), reaffirmed [[d:rogue#1483450030827376771]] (Kyrsie, 2026-03-17: "corrosive + deadly on bosses and dissolvent/instant on trash").
- **[Poison choice, post-patch]** Because Dissolvent no longer benefits from Taste for Blood and has slightly lower base AP-scaling (4.75% vs Instant's 5%), Instant Poison becomes preferred on non-immune targets; Dissolvent/Corrosive retained only for nature-immune enemies (elementals, some ghostly undead — not "zombie type" undead) — [[d:rogue#1482687904684966001]] (Punk, 2026-03-14), [[d:rogue#1483699804520583288]], [[d:rogue#1483700622732820611]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-18).
- **[Poison resistance mechanics]** Poison resist chance is comparatively minor even when 6 rogues stacked poisons together in older content ("You dont get too many resist even with instant poison either") — [[d:rogue#1480568786280251596]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-09).
- **[Poisons and Spell Crit]** Poisons can crit only via Spell Crit sources (mage Atiesh, boomkin aura, Dreamshard Elixir); only worth using Dreamshard Elixir as an Assassination spec running Dissolvent — [[d:rogue#1478544563894354134]] (TwistedMindz, 2026-03-04).
- **[Fiery weapon enchant for leveling]** Recommended to invest gold in Fiery Weapon enchant while leveling ("makes leveling much more bearable") even though it doesn't scale with spell power — [[d:rogue#1480480688187768956]] (Im pro vise, 2026-03-09).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Vanish mechanics]** Vanish grants ~1 second of spell immunity, removes the rogue from the threat table/combat, and puts the rogue into advanced stealth (a very large, "+270"-equivalent, stealth modifier bonus); can be used to dodge specific boss mechanics if timed within that 1-second window (e.g., new Four Horsemen mark application, Maexxna's web spray) — [[d:rogue#1479856314137186385]], [[d:rogue#1479858864898834432]] (Epickalen, 2026-03-05). Vanish does put you out of combat briefly for trinket-swap purposes, but a raid-wide damage tick can pull you back into combat before the swap completes — [[d:rogue#1479855141090758718]], [[d:rogue#1479829945101582538]] (Epickalen/Dimbei, 2026-03-06).
- **[Smoke Bomb]** Widely considered a weak/underwhelming talent by the community — reduces damage taken by everyone (both allies and enemies) inside a large radius, requiring precise pre-positioning that's hard to use effectively in most fights; considered inferior to the removed "Dust" ability (reduced threat + increased damage) that Smoke Bomb replaced; suggested improvement would be to make it throwable like Distract — [[d:rogue#1478079345053405264]], [[d:rogue#1478090168207675579]], [[d:rogue#1483562910503403732]] (Ionnatra/Rheinwg, 2026-03-02/2026-03-18). Note: Smoke Bomb debuffs/benefits apply to whoever is standing inside it, friend or foe — [[d:rogue#1483561679160741988]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-18).
- **[Cloak of Shadows]** Considered largely "dogshit" for PvE; some players auto-block it via addon (VCB) because it can buff-cap the character and offers little value; occasionally used situationally for specific fire/spell-damage windows (Ragnaros, Firemaw-type fights, BRD stealth runs, lava swimming) — [[d:rogue#1478347991382233178]] (Matt Lender, 2026-03-02), [[d:rogue#1482923163296727282]], [[d:rogue#1482923580537962647]] (Epickalen, 2026-03-16).
- **[Improved Expose Armor (IEA) raid role]** IEA is widely regarded as a significant raid DPS contribution (roughly a 10% raid DPS increase per Sphinctus) but is described as tedious/unfun to maintain ("brutal," makes the Sub rotation "feel shit") because it competes for combo points/energy against personal damage — [[d:rogue#1483332482450460804]] (Sphinctus, 2026-03-17), [[d:rogue#1478812018189406278]]/[[d:rogue#1478812106236362946]] (Rheinwg/Dalak, 2026-03-04), [[d:rogue#1481453232784933066]] (Matt Lender, 2026-03-12).
- **[Weaponskill quest]** A ~250g dagger-weaponskill quest chain (killing an elite, traveling Kalimdor, visiting Stormwind Vault) raises weaponskill and removes the need for Distracting Dagger's +weaponskill bonus; considered worth doing due to glancing-blow damage value even though gold-costly — [[d:rogue#1481867248468492318]], [[d:rogue#1481875499767824477]], [[d:rogue#1481877343126425701]] (Rumpledstillskin/Epickalen/Istrefi, 2026-03-13). Race-swapping to Goblin can reach 313 weaponskill via racial + gear, letting a player skip the daggers-skill gloves slot — [[d:rogue#1482967174564810925]] (Lika, 2026-03-16), [[d:rogue#1483490715328577639]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-17), [[d:rogue#1483491110494929078]] (Scruff: "it's why rolling goblin is so strong").
- **[Ravenholdt rep grind]** Rogue-specific reputation track: pickpocket Syndicate Emblems (Alterac/Hillsbrad) for Neutral→Friendly, then farm Precise Lock Cylinders from Heavy Junkboxes (recently changed to guarantee 1 cylinder per box and made tradeable/stackable to 20 via AH) for Friendly→Exalted; ~3800-3900 total cylinders needed for Exalted; killing Syndicate no longer grants rep on Turtle (changed from vanilla) — [[d:rogue#1482560599085940878]], [[d:rogue#1482561177971200081]], [[d:rogue#1482561495752769546]], [[d:rogue#1483180319463969010]] (Freohr/Epickalen, 2026-03-15).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Rogue tank build]** Community-built "tank rogue" spec uses Agitating Poison for threat generation; can tank many/most Naxx bosses (video evidence shown by Nettlefield) but explicitly lacks a taunt and has weak AoE threat, making target-swaps risky in high-DPS raid comps — [[d:rogue#1479139872018137099]], [[d:rogue#1479140876407279677]], [[d:rogue#1479142217166749767]], [[d:rogue#1479143372999364822]] (Nettlefield/Istrefi, 2026-03-06/14).
- **[Community petition]** Ongoing "Tank Rogue Propaganda" campaign requesting a physical-damage version of Agitating Poison from devs, linked to forum threads — [[d:rogue#1478692977504424026]] (Nettlefield, 2026-03-04).
- **[AoE threat workaround]** Since there's no taunt, rogue tanks rely on cleave and rapid target-swapping to hold aggro across multiple mobs — [[d:rogue#1479145606654988469]] (Nettlefield, 2026-03-06).
- **[Talent tradeoffs for tank build]** Debate on whether deep Assassination (more threat poison procs) or semi-deep Subtlety (Preparation to reset Evasion) is better for the tank build; Improved Blade Tactics suggested for maximizing Flourish duration/avoidance uptime — [[d:rogue#1479861355644325938]], [[d:rogue#1479882221098635476]], [[d:rogue#1479883099956449438]] (Two raccoons/Kyrsie, 2026-03-06).
- **[Item support for tanking]** A rare item from Sunnyglade BG (disappears after the battleground, lost on death) was floated as a potential tank cooldown, but existence/exact mechanics unconfirmed in-chunk — [[d:rogue#1481572168520171613]], [[d:rogue#1481572477967667305]] (GumGuzz/Rheinwg, 2026-03-12).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Kara40 Ley Watcher]** Nature-affinity elementals are immune to physical damage and thus cannot be poisoned; rogues have no way to apply poison damage to them since poison procs require landing physical damage — [[d:rogue#1481741424486518864]], [[d:rogue#1481741884689748039]], [[d:rogue#1481741951471583354]] (Yappa/Rheinwg, 2026-03-12).
- **[MC poison-immunity bosses]** Most Molten Core bosses have "bleed immunity" affecting Rupture value; only Core Hound (Crocodile-type wording), Golemagg, and Baron Geddon/Thane are cited as NOT bleed-immune, so Rupture is mostly used pre-patch just to build 5-stack Relentless for a bigger Eviscerate rather than for direct DoT damage — [[d:rogue#1483693533687971891]] (Istrefi, 2026-03-18).
- **[Chromaggus]** Has a nature vulnerability/invulnerability mechanic; historically (pre-1.18) DoT poisons (Corrosive/Deadly) were the best Assassination poison choice specifically on this boss — [[d:rogue#1483654817594474506]], [[d:rogue#1483654945155584101]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-18); community speculates Dissolvent may still see use here post-patch — [[d:rogue#1483699804520583288]], [[d:rogue#1483700919890739231]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-18).
- **[Medivh (Kara40) and Sanv]** Mind-Numbing Poison talent gives a cast-speed-reduction debuff; Medivh benefits most from Mind Numbing being kept up; Sanv reportedly does NOT get a casting-time reduction from Mind Numbing (though some raids apply it anyway) — disputed/unclear in chunk — [[d:rogue#1483266947733913791]], [[d:rogue#1483267105498599528]], [[d:rogue#1483267301607477258]], [[d:rogue#1483267760841556108]] (Rheinwg/Vegybird, 2026-03-17).
- **[Loatheb]** Question raised whether double Instant Poison is still used on Loatheb (which grants spellcrit, benefiting Instant Poison's spell-crit interaction); Rheinwg states Instant Poison was "never the meta" here vs Deadly+Corrosive — [[d:rogue#1480258431171563601]], [[d:rogue#1480263419725873252]] (hybris/Rheinwg, 2026-03-09).
- **[Viscidius / Cla'kora]** Debate over Coldrage vs. Pincer's Claw from Cla'kora for the Viscidius fight — the fight is about proc frequency (PPM), not raw damage, since damage doesn't matter for that encounter; GumGuzz tested and leaned toward Coldrage having a better proc rate — [[d:rogue#1480306658096971888]], [[d:rogue#1480306942047031400]], [[d:rogue#1480307240442527865]] (Istrefi/GumGuzz, 2026-03-09).
- **[Kruul]** Cited as a fight where precise Smoke Bomb positioning is especially dangerous/impractical due to boss mechanics (Wail of the Banshee, Blinding Haze) and player-death risk from standing still — [[d:rogue#1478086596204036339]], [[d:rogue#1478086655851364414]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-02). Badge of the Swarmguard remains useful even after acquiring Talisman for consistency on this fight — [[d:rogue#1481293540800598026]] (Heffey, 2026-03-11).
- **[Ruptura/Rupturan]** Cloak of Shadows suggested as a way to avoid Ruptura's "firestrike" mechanic — [[d:rogue#1482923034036666461]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-16).
- **[Thaddius]** Used as a common DPS-testing/parsing benchmark boss throughout the chunk for gear/proc comparisons (Servo Arm, Darkmantle/Stormshroud testing) — [[d:rogue#1478353870362120312]] (Purge, 2026-03-02), [[d:rogue#1480306729651536041]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-09).
- **[BWL Suppression Room]** Rogue's job in the BWL suppression room is primarily utility: bring lockpicking/thieves' tools, keep totems down, stay stealthed, use good pulls/distracts, avoid AoE range, and otherwise do minimal else until the boss — [[d:rogue#1483608695249174679]], [[d:rogue#1483613324850495608]] (Rheinwg/Epickalen, 2026-03-18).
- **[UBRS lockpicking]** Rogues CANNOT lockpick the UBRS door (it has no interactable lock); must farm gemstones or pay someone to open it — the lockable door players may be thinking of is in BRD, not UBRS — [[d:rogue#1479420477129887845]], [[d:rogue#1479442063035727984]], [[d:rogue#1479485073236562010]] (BatistaBallista/Nandieb/Noga, 2026-03-07).
- **[Kara40 combo-point loss oddity]** Bug/quirk report: combo points appear to reset unexpectedly when switching between boss/adds in Kara40 in ways that don't happen in Naxx; partially explained by the intended CP-per-target mechanic, but some anomalies (points seemingly vanishing without a new target switch) remain unexplained/disputed — [[d:rogue#1480677677873500331]], [[d:rogue#1480683977542205631]], [[d:rogue#1480690447650390059]] (Globi/Rheinwg, 2026-03-10).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Race choice]** No hard consensus; commonly cited: Night Elf for the passive attack-speed racial and popular look; Troll for Berserking racial (10-15% attack/cast speed, scaling with missing HP, 3-min CD, 10s duration); Undead and Orc favored aesthetically; overall guidance is "whatever you like to look at is bis" since racial differences are minor at endgame — [[d:rogue#1481819947532423209]] (riku, 2026-03-11), [[d:rogue#1481810266957156392]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1481682704725442590]] (KoffiePa: "I will never understand why people pick a specific race just to get a few more dps at some point... nobody cares").
- **[Goblin weaponskill niche]** Goblin race + gear can reach 313 weaponskill (close to the 315 cap) without needing the dagger-skill gloves slot, freeing that gear slot for stat pieces — [[d:rogue#1482967174564810925]] (Lika, 2026-03-16), [[d:rogue#1483491110494929078]] (Scruff, 2026-03-17).
- **[Professions]** Common picks: Herbalism+Alchemy (gold + self-sufficiency), Skinning+Leatherworking (gold + craftable frost-resist gear for Naxx progression, requires cured rugged hide ~20g each), Mining+Engineering (if planning to use Engineering at 60, e.g. Dense Dynamite / Goblin Sapper Charge). Enchanting is highlighted as valuable while leveling 50-60 specifically to disenchant soulbound greens/blues for gold — [[d:rogue#1481656932015145232]] (Farban, 2026-03-11), [[d:rogue#1481662356282019840]], [[d:rogue#1481663494280577267]] (Matt Lender, 2026-03-11).
- **[Professions vs leveling speed]** Disputed: Nandieb argues professions (esp. Mining/Engineering, Fishing) meaningfully slow leveling and gold is more efficiently farmed at max level; Epickalen/Badknees/Vistarrk counter that gathering professions (Herb/Skinning) don't slow leveling much and can fund an epic mount within days of hitting 60 (30-60g/hour cited) — [[d:rogue#1481674325772992537]], [[d:rogue#1481710641365647533]], [[d:rogue#1481737585167569086]], [[d:rogue#1481774944802050211]], [[d:rogue#1481680568793043105]] (2026-03-12/13).
- **[Gold-farming methods for rogues]** Pickpocketing for Precise Lock Cylinders / lockboxes (Ravenholdt rep, ~3800 cylinders to Exalted); disenchanting soulbound Scholomance/Stratholme drops while farming Darkmantle/Shadowcraft; strong "farms" reportedly yield ~100g/hour; opening 2 "teebus" (an item type) in a day cited as a high-gold method (~250g in ~5 hours) — [[d:rogue#1481902005671956540]], [[d:rogue#1481889456092090490]], [[d:rogue#1481906655460458630]] (Rumpledstillskin/Matt Lender/Epickalen, 2026-03-13). ## Bugs & quirks
- **[Sinister Strike/dodge-parry energy cost bug/quirk]** A dodged/parried Sinister Strike (and similarly Hemorrhage, Noxious Assault) only costs ~7 energy instead of full cost — long-observed anomaly ("losing 1-2 energy") now explained and quantified via extensive dummy testing (~82.5% refund) — [[d:rogue#1480274083160789204]], [[d:rogue#1480276507707441434]] (GumGuzz, 2026-03-08).
- **[Combo-point loss on target switch]** Combo points reset when a new combo point is generated on a different target; can be exploited (see Rotation section) via 1-CP finishers + Ruthlessness to seed a new target with 1 CP without fully losing prior progress — [[d:rogue#1482310710758477869]], [[d:rogue#1482311939945267351]] (Joetje/Jay, 2026-03-14).
- **[Kara40 CP anomaly]** Some players report combo points vanishing unexpectedly in Kara40 in ways that don't occur in Naxx, beyond the intended target-switch mechanic; cause not conclusively identified in-chunk — [[d:rogue#1480677677873500331]], [[d:rogue#1480683977542205631]] (Globi, 2026-03-10).
- **[Finisher point-downgrade bug]** Double-pressing a finisher (e.g., Rupture) can downgrade a higher-point cast (5CP) into a lower one (1CP) if pressed again quickly — modern (retail) clients reportedly block this downgrade but Turtle's 1.12-based client does not — [[d:rogue#1479064018260856862]] (Matt Lender, 2026-03-05).
- **[Char-screen stat display bug]** Character screen sometimes fails to correctly reflect equipped stats/talents (weaponskill/crit values from gear not always updating visually); may depend on gear-equip order — [[d:rogue#1481702051049377884]], [[d:rogue#1481702397397962762]] (GumGuzz/Rheinwg, 2026-03-11).
- **[Ouro Gloves crit anomaly]** Reported that Ouro Gloves gave 0.24% crit in testing that couldn't be fully explained by the known 0.04%-crit-per-weaponskill formula, raising suspicion of a minor bug — [[d:rogue#1478068592472756305]], [[d:rogue#1478069090273988795]] (Rheinwg, 2026-03-02).
- **[Ravenholdt rep change]** Turtle changed vanilla behavior so that killing Syndicate mobs (Hillsbrad/Alterac) no longer grants Ravenholdt reputation (previously did in vanilla per WoWhead classic guide); players who assumed vanilla behavior wasted farming time before learning the correct (cylinder-only) method — [[d:rogue#1482637697360199773]], [[d:rogue#1482638796712312852]], [[d:rogue#1482703816540422194]] (Kirsten/Freohr, 2026-03-15).
- **[Precise Lock Cylinders]** Recently changed on Turtle so Heavy Junkboxes guarantee 1 Precise Cylinder on open (previously non-stacking), now stackable to 20 and tradeable via AH — [[d:rogue#1482561495752769546]] (Freohr, 2026-03-15).
