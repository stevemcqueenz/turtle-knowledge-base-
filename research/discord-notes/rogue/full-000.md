## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Weapon skill]** Weapon-skill formula reworked: skill now scales linearly, no more awkward breakpoints; new quests grant +5 skill books; fighting 3-levels-higher mobs/bosses no longer applies extra 1% hit suppression (hit cap effectively moved from 9% to 8% vs such targets, old "soft cap" of 305 skill removed); each point from 300→315 now gives flat 0.2% hit + 2% reduced glancing penalty; racial weapon skill bonus reduced from +5 to +3. Quoted from official forum post — [[d:rogue#1424481044190728255]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-10-06, quoting forum.turtle-wow.org/viewtopic.php?t=15157).
- **[General]** Rogue was recently buffed and is now considered the top (or near-top) PvE DPS class server-wide for the first time in years, causing widespread nerf discussion; historically rogues were rare (1 rogue per 4-6 warriors/mages) — [[d:rogue#1423733538250821662]], [[d:rogue#1423734019430023380]] (Rosco, 2025-10-03).
- **[PvP]** Rogues "got a free matchup against warrior and pala now"; assassin rogues are considered very strong in PvP due to Dissolvent poison's armor-ignoring damage; disputed whether rogue is the single best PvP class (some say shamans are scarier, casters "shit on" melee) — [[d:rogue#1423412138650763466]], [[d:rogue#1423412190991487027]] (Beldrin, 2025-10-03; disputed by Kyrsie [[d:rogue#1423411851986735125]]).
- **[Nerf speculation]** Community expected nerfs to Taste for Blood (15%→10%?), Adrenaline Rush/Blade Rush, Opportunity, and/or Dissolvent/Corrosive-poison interaction; consensus that dagger assassin (Improved Backstab + energy regen via agi stacking + T3.5 combo bonus) is the most "disgusting" outlier, not combat/sub — [[d:rogue#1423625763197948044]] (Hedonist), [[d:rogue#1423744336771678238]] (Premium (Retired), +1), single source/speculation, unconfirmed by devs as of chunk end.
- **[Blade Flurry]** Reported bug: Blade Flurry buff sometimes gets stuck active with no way to remove except relog, especially when it hits buff-cap; compared to Paladin's Bulwark which handles buff-cap correctly; no bug report filed as of discussion — [[d:rogue#1424486188919558175]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1424488717736743042]] (Mithreon; question unanswered).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Assassination]** Dagger Assassination (poison/Dissolvent build) is the strongest, most gear-independent PvE ST spec in early-mid content (MC/BWL/early Naxx) and dominates AR (Adrenaline Rush) burst windows; widely called close to gear-independent — [[d:rogue#1423979116826460221]] (Weirdthing: "its basically bis", 2025-10-03), [[d:rogue#1424504587309809764]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1424743803478085632]] (Hedonist: "PVP Assa, in PVE combat dagger is S+ tier dps").
- **[Combat]** Combat daggers (Adrenaline Rush + Taste for Blood, backstab-focused) overtakes/out-scales Assassination once enough agility/gear is available (roughly Perdition's Blade/Dragonfang/T2/Onyxia cloak tier, i.e. BWL-ish); by AQ40-tier gear combat is considered the best ST spec — [[d:rogue#1424886774441709618]] (tabtabtab, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1424502366589423697]] (Rosco: "AQ seems to be the time for combat rogues").
- **[Combat vs Assassin, general]** Consensus ranking (Weirdthing): "cb daggers > assa with fast weapons > cb swords > sub" for endgame Naxx-geared play — [[d:rogue#1423980182406168687]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-03). Tabtabtab: "combat outscales assassin by like 50-60% at end-game" but "if you're in dungeon gear, assassin will be significantly better" — [[d:rogue#1423794624870223932]], [[d:rogue#1423795908503666760]] (2025-10-04).
- **[Combat swords]** Combat with slow (~1.7-2.8 speed) swords is viable up to Naxx (playable "until you leave naxx") but loses roughly 300+ dps vs dagger AR at the point comparisons were made; whole sword-combat build effectively "rides on 1 item" (Riftcarver) — [[d:rogue#1423570048437457009]] (Hedonist), [[d:rogue#1423569467224358997]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1423569780526284913]].
- **[Sub]** Subtlety with IEA (Improved Expose Armor) is mainly a support/buff spec, doing modest personal DPS (~1500-2000) but bringing "some of, if not the best raid buffs in the game" via Mark for Death (+~1000 AP for 16s); can shine on short fights (e.g., Anub'Rekhan ~33s), falls off on long fights vs combat — [[d:rogue#1423672536385716354]], [[d:rogue#1423674878720217171]], [[d:rogue#1423673544516042872]] (Zaszz, 2025-10-03). Non-IEA "full push" sub DPS reported around 2600+ needed to be worth it on short fights — [[d:rogue#1423673628184281108]] (Hedonist).
- **[Sub]** K40 (Tower of Karazhan 40) 5-piece tier set bonus called "cracked" for Sub — makes maintaining Slice, Rupture, IEA, and Shadow of Death easy due to excess combo points — [[d:rogue#1423670310778961961]] (Zaszz, 2025-10-03).
- **[Spec choice by content tier]** Hedonist's framing: "In DT [Death's Touch/speedrun] type raids AR dagger is the dominant one, in a bit lower tier raids Envenom Combat is better..., Mid or lower tier raids Assa will do better probably" — [[d:rogue#1423672544506019891]] (Hedonist, 2025-10-03).
- **[Combat leveling]** Combat dagger leveling (1-60) is "not recommended," generally worse than Assassin in every way while leveling; 1-40 combat is "on par" with Assassin, but after 40 "there is no contest," Assassin pulls ahead — [[d:rogue#1423731934546366485]], [[d:rogue#1423743926464151634]] (Rosco, 2025-10-03).
- **[Ambershire-specific]** On the newer/less-geared Ambershire server, combat is far behind assassin due to low available agility from gear (theoretical max armor-agi on Ambershire ~202, current meta pre-bis ~82, vs Nord's near-BiS/T3 players); assassin "is basically trash compared to" combat in reverse is disputed — actually combat is behind due to low agi; assassin remains strong regardless of gear — [[d:rogue#1424527016073629849]] (Rosco, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1424500474761183374]] (Rosco: combat ~100-150 personal dps behind bis-ish assassin, using only alcor+distracting+partial darkmantle).
- **[Contested]** Whether combat daggers can close the 100-150dps gap to assassin with Perdition's Blade access is disputed: Epickalen estimates Perdition's alone is worth ~30dps just from raw stat/weapon-damage upgrade (before spec/style/enchant/buff changes), targeting a ~50-75dps final gap as "good enough" — [[d:rogue#1424523531538006047]], [[d:rogue#1424529090408611954]] (Epickalen, 2025-10-06); Rosco skeptical that 120 extra agi from full gear closes the gap ("i do not think 120 agi is enough...need like 600 or so [more agility] lol") — [[d:rogue#1424528151194632192]], [[d:rogue#1424505306116784249]] (Rosco).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Rogue is "turbo weak" until level ~20-25; power spikes around level 20 — [[d:rogue#1424766664993542154]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1424800319619076158]] (andre).
- **[Combat 1-40]** Recommended leveling path: go straight down Combat DPS talents (no Defense line), then dump remainder into Assassin tree once Adrenaline Rush is reached; frontstab/backstab walk-in technique described: walk into mob so your model overlaps theirs, they turn and back away, backstab then — [[d:rogue#1423742284482220162]], [[d:rogue#1423742502418255893]] (Rosco, 2025-10-03), [[d:rogue#1423743214074073110]] (Suade).
- **[Respec point]** General consensus: level 1-40 combat (Sinister Strike or frontstab) is roughly on par with Assassin; at 40, respec into Assassin (get Noxious Assault) for the rest of leveling to 60, "after 40 there is no contest" — [[d:rogue#1423818108128465058]] (TwistedMindz, talent link: `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/rogue?points=Co--FAY` for "core ambush leveling build, take any talents you want until level 40, respec at 40 to assassin"), [[d:rogue#1423743926464151634]] (Rosco).
- **[Assassin leveling rotation]** Early: 1cp Envenom, 1cp Slice and Dice, 1cp Eviscerate spam (mobs die before 5cp Rupture is worth it); once Noxious Assault is learned (~lvl "GumGuzz" thread), switch to Sinister/Noxious builder into 5cp Rupture, keep buffs up — [[d:rogue#1423927477171912744]] (GumGuzz: reported ~25% dps increase from switching a leveling player to this), [[d:rogue#1424683089900343298]] (Kevo: "1CP Env > 1CP SnD > 1CP Evi spam" while leveling, picked up Blood talent [presumably Bloodletting-type] only at 60 for dungeon).
- **[Poisons while leveling]** Use Instant poisons until level 52; at 52 switch to Dissolvent poison (works against elemental mobs too, more damage with Taste for Blood); avoid elemental mobs (e.g., nature elementals in Un'Goro) until 52/Dissolvent available — [[d:rogue#1423924150078148608]] / [[d:rogue#1424349140007256064]] (Blackjack, "If you are lvl 52 get dissolvent poison"), [[d:rogue#1423347025499095051 n/a]].
- **[Opener while leveling]** At low level, open with Sinister Strike (cheaper) rather than Backstab, since goal is applying poisons ASAP/often rather than raw backstab damage; stealth approach costs positioning time — [[d:rogue#1423929228168003667]] (Nuian, 2025-10-04), [[d:rogue#1423929408921796719]] (GumGuzz).
- **[Weapon choice leveling]** Always prefer fastest daggers available while leveling assassin, but "don't obsess over it" — [[d:rogue#1423744208476307556]] (Rosco, 2025-10-03).
- **[Sub/backstab leveling alt]** Alternative enjoyable leveling build: Improved Backstab then dump remaining points into Subtlety — [[d:rogue#1423931170235613204]] (sailorVEVO, 2025-10-04).
- **[Gnomish vs Goblin Engineering]** Recommend Gnomish Engineering for the Chicken and Death Ray gadgets; Goblin's only benefit is crafting Sapper Charges (can just buy from AH instead); Gnomish also gives Gadgetzan teleport (less useful since Tanaris port already free) vs Goblin's Everlook teleport — [[d:rogue#1424833501223583744]] (Beldrin, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1424831590265262171]] (Heick). Goblin Sapper Charges called BiS-enabling for rogue by some — [[d:rogue#1424840738658717696]] (Epickalen: "What makes engineering bis for rogue is goblin sapper charges").
- **[Gold farming while leveling]** Suggested professions over "raw gold farms" (called bad); pickpocketing and fishing also mentioned as leveling-era gold sources — [[d:rogue#1424630295860219955]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1424441687387930737]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Combat AR dagger]** Backstab > SnD (1cp) > Backstab to 5CP > Rupture > SnD; maintain 5cp Rupture for Taste for Blood buff uptime; use Adrenaline Rush right after getting a 5cp Rupture up to maximize AR-window backstabs; never Sinister Strike with daggers (bad multiplier) — swap to sword/mace/fist via macro if forced to Sinister Strike — [[d:rogue#1424148190663278846]] (Heffey, 2025-10-05).
- **[Combat AR]** After a 5cp Rupture, don't pop AR (you'll cap energy); get Thief's Fortitude/"TFB" (Taste for Blood) up ASAP even via Thistle Tea or AR — [[d:rogue#1423573875119226891]] / [[d:rogue#1423574068371656737]] (Hedonist, 2025-10-03).
- **[Combat AR openers]** On Anub'Rekhan and Faerlina, Blood Fury("BF")-equivalent cooldown popped from the start of the fight — [[d:rogue#1423574326233399348]] (Hedonist).
- **[Assassin]** Rotation is largely energy-neutral/no need to worry about "wasted" energy — treat energy as a means to press abilities, not something to hoard; use Thistle Tea when needed or on Surprise Attack proc — [[d:rogue#1423573056152141896]], [[d:rogue#1423573679631110297]] (Hedonist, 2025-10-03).
- **[Assassin]** Standard rotation loop: 1cp Envenom, 1cp Slice and Dice, then Noxious Assault-builder to 5cp Rupture, then Eviscerate spam / rebuild — [[d:rogue#1424158600296665222]] (PhoenMD: "1 nox 1 envenom 1SnD, 5 nox Rupture, evsicerate when can"), [[d:rogue#1424683089900343298]] (Kevo).
- **[Assassin AR gearing note]** With Sword-AR builds, being at &lt;15 energy is needed to pop AR usefully, which is RNG-dependent and messes with trinket/potion timing ("wait for a moment where you have <15 energy... fucks up your trinkets and pots") — [[d:rogue#1423572090996654121]], [[d:rogue#1423572143748157541]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-03).
- **[Combat/Sword AR]** Dagger AR outperforms Sword AR (e.g. Thunderfury) in most scenarios except very short fights (~30s) where energy-efficiency favors daggers even more; dummy test found daggers ≈ Thunderfury for first 1-1:30, then daggers pull ahead over 3 min — [[d:rogue#1424337035371483278]], [[d:rogue#1424338489285021696]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-05/06).
- **[Weapon choice general]** For Assassination, prioritize weapon speed over raw stats/procs; a pair of "1.3 shit bird weapons" (fast, low-stat) is more consistently high-DPS than slower proc weapons like Tempest's Rage or Thunderfury, since faster weapons = more poison procs = more energy/combo points/Noxious Assaults — [[d:rogue#1423670676702630140]] (Zaszz, 2025-10-03), [[d:rogue#1423671467777720451]].
- **[Envenom-combat]** "Too clean" rotation once mastered (20/31 build) — described by Epickalen as ideal balance — [[d:rogue#1423608056721444875]], [[d:rogue#1423642175916216383]] (Epickalen, 2025-10-03).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Weapon skill / hit]** Post-1.18 formula: skill scales linearly 300→315, each point = 0.2% hit + 2% reduced glancing penalty; ability (yellow) hit cap is 8% at 315 weapon skill vs a level-63 boss, and 5-9% depending on skill per various posts (GumGuzz: 5% at 315, 8% at 300; Epickalen: 8% "iirc" for Noxious, +1% hit per 5 weapon skill over 300) — [[d:rogue#1424834668523557026]] (GumGuzz), [[d:rogue#1424840473423380593]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1424481044190728255]] (Premium (Retired), forum quote).
- **[White hit cap dispute]** Disagreement (Epickalen vs Premium (Retired) vs The Messiah Of Ignite) over whether the dual-wield/weapon-skill-based miss penalty applies to main-hand equally or only offhand, and whether hitcap moves with weapon skill beyond the 8% ability cap; The Messiah Of Ignite states, from log analysis, that main-hand misses are affected equally by the DW miss penalty, contradicting a claim that only offhand is affected — [[d:rogue#1424489281837334570]], [[d:rogue#1424491034016223383]] (The Messiah Of Ignite, 2025-10-06; disputed by Premium (Retired) [[d:rogue#1424490062837579947]], contested/open).
- **[Assassin]** For assassin, hit is valuable because it reduces white-hit misses and thereby increases poison procs (both mainhand/offhand autos, since poisons proc off autos) — [[d:rogue#1424467376698691817]] (Mergence, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1424441578155544657]] (Epickalen: 8% needed "for abilities to not miss," "most of your dps" as assassin).
- **[Assassin]** ~9% (yellow) hit cap target cited for trinket/enchant planning; once hit-capped, stack haste (e.g., 3% haste Whip trinket + another 2% haste trinket considered BiS pairing) — [[d:rogue#1424461940754878689]] (Oshy, 2025-10-06).
- **[Energy regen]** Agility affects energy regen only if speced deep into Combat: ~0.0012 sec shaved off regen tick per agility point (0.0006 for 1-point talent version) — [[d:rogue#1423596352851677277]] (Epickalen, 2025-10-03).
- **[Poison scaling]** Dissolvent Poison scales with 4.75% of Attack Power, plus application chance/attack-speed based; poisons in general do NOT scale with Spellpower or Nature Spellpower, but DO scale with spell crit — [[d:rogue#1423625712182759524]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-03), [[d:rogue#1424294712918937662]] (Rheinwg, 2025-10-06, +1 reaction).
- **[Poison AP scaling vs Blade Rush]** Poison's AP-scaling is described as weak compared to Blade Rush's scaling ("poison ap scaling is a joke compared to blade rush scaling") — [[d:rogue#1423671826600562711]] (Beldrin, 2025-10-03; disputed context, part of nerf debate).
- **[Weapon speed value]** At level 60, each 0.1 speed increase is worth roughly ~7 dps from extra poison procs alone (envenom up); less at lower level (~3-4dps) — [[d:rogue#1423922493298573352]] (GumGuzz, 2025-10-04).
- **[Fire res / spellstrike gear]** "+X Fire damage on weapon attack" (spellstrike-style) items are weak/situational in MC/P1 because many MC bosses have high fire resist or fire immunity; players swap those pieces out for lightning/other element pieces vs Rag, etc. — [[d:rogue#1423991717249421372]] (Akuya/Puddlewhisk, 2025-10-04), [[d:rogue#1424000746290024478]] (GumGuzz: "should be better on like rag n stuff").
- **[Meme spellcrit build]** Off-meta build stacking spellcrit gear to buff Envenom/poison spell-crits reported doing ~1200 dps in pre-bis gear — considered fun/meme, not optimized; optimized path is stack AP/agi rogue gear + Onslaught Bracers/Devilsaur ("OBD") + 1 spellstrike proc item — [[d:rogue#1423653154213335060]] (Purge), [[d:rogue#1423652968879624356]] (Scruff).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Weapon enchant - Assassin]** Double Crusader enchant is the standard/recommended weapon enchant for Assassination (both MH and OH), preferred over Agility enchants: since Assassination's primary damage (Dissolvent, Noxious Assault, Eviscerate) scales off AP not weapon stats, Crusader's burst AP (100 AP for 15s, ~30s/min with two) outweighs Agility's flat +30AP+2%crit constant bonus — [[d:rogue#1425180018782376178]] (Suade, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1425177124096180327]] (Scruff), [[d:rogue#1425941962833268757]] (GumGuzz, 2025-10-09).
- **[Weapon enchant - Combat]** For Combat (daggers or non-daggers), +15 Agility on weapon enchant is generally recommended over Crusader, since Combat benefits from agility-based crit/energy-regen rather than AP bursts; for swords specifically, some recommend Crusader MH / Agi OH — [[d:rogue#1425911378123690024]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-10-09; corrected from an earlier "Crusader for daggers combat" claim after Drox flagged it), [[d:rogue#1426336355432271902]] (Epickalen: "If you plan on using it in combat, agi. If its only ever seeing use in assassin, crusader is good enough").
- **[Poisons - Assassin]** Assassin uses Dissolvent Poison (physical, armor-ignoring, scales 4.75% AP) on both weapons for essentially all content — "Assa: Dissolvent for everything"; despite being "master of poisons," Assassin in practice runs one poison type — [[d:rogue#1425635837923692705]] (Rheinwg, 2025-10-09), [[d:rogue#1424534407917736077]] (Rheinwg, "assa only uses 1 poison").
- **[Poisons - Combat]** Combat: Dissolvent for trash/short fights; Deadly Poison + Corrosive Poison for fights over ~1 minute; Corrosive+Dissolvent mix for poison-immune-adjacent or long fights — [[d:rogue#1424430313257308211]] (Rheinwg, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1425635837923692705]].
- **[Poison leveling timeline]** Use Instant Poison up to level 51; Dissolvent Poison unlocks at level 52 and becomes the standard from then on (also works vs elemental/nature mobs since it deals physical damage) — [[d:rogue#1425406376527659082]] (TwistedMindz, 2025-10-08), [[d:rogue#1424349140007256064]] (Blackjack).
- **[Poison mechanics]** Dissolvent's base damage is very close to Instant Poison's (only ~7-20 dmg difference per proc even with Taste for Blood applied), which the community feels is a balance oversight since Dissolvent's armor-ignore should come with a bigger tradeoff; some argued for reducing Dissolvent's base damage while extending poison coating durations (30 min currently, criticized as too short vs old 1hr) — [[d:rogue#1424531942711365632]] (Blindtongue, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1426046215883063408]] (Blindtongue), [[d:rogue#1426142028487458880]] (Blindtongue: proposed -15/20 max dmg nerf to Dissolvent).
- **[Poison resist]** Dissolvent Poison CAN be resisted by mobs, including some world/outdoor mobs — [[d:rogue#1425780350482911336]] (Unzz, 2025-10-09, confirmed by own testing).
- **[Poison immunity]** Some MC/dungeon bosses and undead (e.g., undead dwarves) are immune to poison; Baron Rivendare-type undead were made immune to poison "for lore" then later changed to be affected by bleeds (a pre-1.18 change, likely to help Feral) — [[d:rogue#1428419599333855283]] (Purge, 2025-10-16), [[d:rogue#1428430208033099837]] (Scruff).
- **[Poison stacking rule]** Corrosive Poison has a damage-stack cap, making Dissolvent preferred at high proc-rate ("AUD: Always Use Dissolvent... you proc way too much to use a poison that has a stack cap") — [[d:rogue#1428478304385761361]] (Scruff, 2025-10-17).
- **[Poison scaling talents]** Taste for Blood adds +15% damage to physical-poison damage (Dissolvent, Instant) and is used by essentially every PvE dagger/poison build — [[d:rogue#1427464301475532860]] (Rheinwg, 2025-10-13).
- **[Weapon oils/stones - Combat]** Sharpening Stones vs poisons for Combat Daggers in Naxx raised as an open question, no clear answer given in this chunk — [[d:rogue#1427687760852029492]] (Drox, 2025-10-13; question unanswered).
- **[Vampiric/lifesteal enchants]** For OBD (self-damaging weapon) users, Vampiric (lifesteal) weapon enchant is a reasonable alternative to Crusader on a farming weapon; PvE main-set consensus is still double Crusader since it also helps offset OBD's self-damage — [[d:rogue#1426959492960096426]] (GumGuzz, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1428524065618133022]] (Purge: "sp enchant is lower dps than crusader and crusader also helps with the self dmg").
- **[Vampirism gear]** BiS-ish easy vampirism pieces for solo/farming builds: 2x Blood Etched Fetish trinkets (RFD), Leeching Fang (BoE, can enchant lifesteal), Dazzling Aquamarine Ring (crafted + vamp enchant), Wolfheart Necklace (from Packmaster Ragetooth, Gilneas City, + vamp enchant); considered unnecessary in group PvE since healers cover it — [[d:rogue#1427306511188889641]] (TwistedMindz, 2025-10-13).
- **[Cloak enchant]** Subtlety cloak enchant (threat reduction, "2% reduction") debated vs +3 Agility — generally considered marginal but can matter for avoiding pulling threat — [[d:rogue#1428432364383764642]] (Suade/Weirdthing, 2025-10-16).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Adrenaline Rush]** For Combat AR-dagger, pop Adrenaline Rush right after building a 5-combo Rupture, to spend the AR window's energy on Backstabs during the resulting Taste for Blood buff; conversely, don't pop AR immediately after refreshing a 5cp Rupture in general play or you risk energy-capping — [[d:rogue#1424148190663278846]] (Heffey), [[d:rogue#1423573875119226891]]/[[d:rogue#1423574068371656737]] (Hedonist, 2025-10-03).
- **[Adrenaline Rush - Sword AR]** For Sword-AR builds, you must be below ~15 energy when popping AR to not waste the burst, which is RNG-dependent and complicates trinket/potion cooldown alignment — a cited downside vs Dagger AR — [[d:rogue#1423572090996654121]], [[d:rogue#1423572143748157541]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-03).
- **[Envenom-Combat vs AR-Combat]** Envenom-Combat build (spending 24-27 points, running Envenom instead of pure Backstab-AR) is praised as strong especially where energy can't be spent fast enough with AR; some feel plain AR still pulls ahead on longer fights — contested — [[d:rogue#1427370993680388097]] (elyina, 2025-10-13), [[d:rogue#1427371140573167860]] (Beldrin).
- **[Vanish trinket-swap tech]** Use Vanish to swap in/out active trinkets mid-combat (e.g., Badge of the Swarmguard + another active trinket, then swap to passive stat trinkets once actives are used), tracked via pfUI Buffbars for cooldowns — [[d:rogue#1423566500312383570]], [[d:rogue#1423567008456507487]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-03).
- **[Threat / aggro]** High-DPS rogues (especially Combat/Assassin post-buff) report frequent threat-capping in undergeared or poorly-tanked groups; common mitigations: Vanish then resume, auto-attack only after threat-capping, Feint (called "dogwater"/weak), positioning/target-switching, and relying on tank Salvation or good tanks — [[d:rogue#1424766320913940480]] (Scruff), [[d:rogue#1425823588815999027]] (TwistedMindz), [[d:rogue#1425823913027440800]] (TwistedMindz: "Feint is absolute dogwater tho").
- **[Sub raid utility]** Subtlety's core raid contributions: Improved Expose Armor (IEA) — better armor-reduction than Sunder Armor, does NOT stack with Sunder but reduces more armor (~5% raid-wide physical dmg increase on the boss) — and Hemorrhage, which is called "huge for raidwide physical damage" since it's a debuff that boosts the whole raid's physical dps; Mark for Death gives ~+1000 AP for 16s to the marked target. Many raids designate exactly one Combat rogue (2 points into IEA, dropping Lethality) rather than a dedicated Sub player — [[d:rogue#1427375152131477667]] (Beldrin), [[d:rogue#1427454101213679626]] (30: "~5% dmg increase for all your physical dps compared to just sunders"), [[d:rogue#1427378572003119175]] (elyina), [[d:rogue#1427397461407825930]] (Epickalen: "Our guild run 0 designated IEA rogues").
- **[Cold Blood]** Considered mostly a "flavor" talent point by several theorycrafters unless the build leans on 5-cp Eviscerate finishers regularly; less valuable for a pure Rupture/Expose Armor rotation — [[d:rogue#1428234089462366238]], [[d:rogue#1428234783691112470]] (GumGuzz, 2025-10-16).
- **[Energy regen scaling]** Energy tick rate is reduced only via Combat-tree "Blade Rush"-type talent investment: ~0.0012 sec less regen delay per point of agility at 2 talent points (0.0006 at 1 point); does not apply outside that talent — [[d:rogue#1423596352851677277]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1426786805562478592]] (Heffey, 2025-10-12).
- **[Movement speed utility]** Rogue's passive movement-speed bonus (7% from a lvl-30 passive, on top of any racial) additively stacks with most other speed buffs/items (Swiftness Potions, boots, Emerald Blessing, etc.), unlike some other classes' movement bonuses which don't stack — full breakdown of all known speed sources posted (Night Elf racial 1%, rogue passive 7%, Emerald Blessing 10%, Enchant Boots-Minor Speed 8%, Swift Boots 40%/15s/1h CD, Swiftness Potion 50%/15s, Swiftness of Zanza 20%/2h, Runeblade of Baron Rivendare 15%, Lynxstep Boots 3%, Triskelion of Roving Elements trinket 8%) — [[d:rogue#1428479302508347393]] (Kahno, 2025-10-17, quoting a wiki-style breakdown).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Sub tank-spec for solo content]** For soloing world elites (e.g., Damian in Karazhan Crypt), a Subtlety "tank spec" stacking agility/dodge, keeping Flourish up (to survive double-Ambush windows) and Riposte for parry-punish, is reported to outlast an Assassin-tank/vampirism-stacking approach on long fights, though Assassin's lifesteal helps survive better overall when it works — [[d:rogue#1423801691656159366]] (Suade, 2025-10-03).
- **[Solo tank combo]** Ghostly Strike + Flourish together, overlapped, described as "budget evasion" for solo-tanking elites (e.g., farming Princess Theradras/BOED); alone, either ability is weak ("wet piece of paper") — [[d:rogue#1428665940102348821]], [[d:rogue#1428666403011039293]] (Epickalen, 2025-10-17).
- **[Threat poisons]** Whether a dedicated "threat poison" build is viable for off-tanking without Taunt was raised as an open question, unanswered in this chunk — [[d:rogue#1425943030623375512]] (Ionnatra, 2025-10-09; question unanswered).

- **[Naxxramas]** On Anub'Rekhan and Faerlina, pop your main damage cooldown (e.g. Blood Fury-equivalent trinket/CD) from the very start of the pull — [[d:rogue#1423574326233399348]] (Hedonist, 2025-10-03). High raid DPS is causing several Naxx bosses to die before their intended mechanics trigger: Anub'Rekhan's Insect Swarm phase is skipped, Maexxna died before ever casting Web Spray — [[d:rogue#1423675298591019071]], [[d:rogue#1423675359550902333]] (Zaszz, 2025-10-03).
- **[Tower of Karazhan (K40)]** Anomalus fight: progression strategy has every raid member reach ~315 Arcane Resist and pop a Chromatic Protection Potion/Flask; one guild cleared it scaled to 31 people this way — [[d:rogue#1423528335014760458]] (Weirdthing), [[d:rogue#1423563337287073862]] (Weirdthing). Gnarlmoon has an "owl" DPS check that gates whether a raid can reasonably progress to Kruul — [[d:rogue#1423566090960900188]], [[d:rogue#1423566170207948851]] (Weirdthing). Mephistroph fight can be "dragged out" to ~12 minutes via kiting if the raid is undergeared, using mana potions to sustain casters — [[d:rogue#1423563606636888105]] (Weirdthing), [[d:rogue#1423564697239814175]] (Weirdthing). Chess event and Incantagos encounters are known to trigger the Blade Flurry stuck-buff bug frequently — [[d:rogue#1428255344823636018]] (Rheinwg, 2025-10-16). The Rupturan trinket's buff ("Draenor's Blessing") and Righteous Fury/Battle Shout can get stuck invisible at the buff cap on fights like Kruul, requiring manual buff-banning via addon (VCB) — [[d:rogue#1428254916778004633]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1428259048469430302]] (30).
- **[Molten Core]** Ragnaros and Golemagg ("Geddon" = Baron Geddon) are immune to bleed effects; Garr is NOT immune to bleeds (a common misconception) — [[d:rogue#1428414659962077214]] (Rheinwg, 2025-10-16). Many MC bosses have high fire resistance/immunity, making "+fire damage on weapon hit" (spellstrike) items weak there; swap those pieces out, they perform better vs non-fire bosses (e.g., Ragnaros aside) — [[d:rogue#1423991717249421372]] (Akuya/Puddlewhisk), [[d:rogue#1424000746290024478]] (GumGuzz).
- **[Utility - Naxx patrols]** A Sub-specced rogue's Distract ability on Naxxramas trash patrols is called one of the more engaging/valuable utility contributions a rogue can bring to a 40-man raid — [[d:rogue#1427264378079805461]] (Scruff, 2025-10-13), [[d:rogue#1427264783295713321]] (Epickalen).
- **[World/solo content]** Princess Theradras (Maraudon) is farmable solo using Spellstrike gear + Elemental Slayer weapon enchant (she's an elemental, takes bonus damage) — described as killable "half naked"; recommended defensive combo is Nature Resistance potion, vampirism gear, and a Flourish→Evasion→Flourish rotation, plus healing potions as needed; pillar-kiting/line-of-sight around a pillar lets you reset/bandage indefinitely if things go wrong — [[d:rogue#1427742054963871869]] (GumGuzz), [[d:rogue#1428661874920128522]] (GumGuzz), [[d:rogue#1428696864261144666]] (Parabolic). Farm times for Blade of Eternal Darkness (BOED) drop varied wildly, from ~20 runs to 900 mobs / 80+ runs for some players — [[d:rogue#1428108576592756796]] (Foreseen), [[d:rogue#1428181702962053224]] (Soulafei).
- **[World/solo content]** Damian (Karazhan Crypt) is soloable at appropriate gear; his double-Ambush window is the main death risk — keep Flourish up around when he's expected to Ambush twice, and hope he doesn't parry your Riposte — [[d:rogue#1423801691656159366]] (Suade, 2025-10-03). A YouTube video tutorial was cited as very helpful for learning the solo fight — [[d:rogue#1425047465811247164]] (Ranjob, 2025-10-07).

## PvP
- **[General]** Post-1.18, Rogue (especially Assassin/Dissolvent) is considered one of the strongest, if not the strongest, PvP classes, with a "free matchup" against Warrior and Paladin now; some argue Shamans or well-played casters can still beat rogues 1v1 — [[d:rogue#1423412138650763466]] (Beldrin, 2025-10-03), disputed by [[d:rogue#1423411851986735125]] (Kyrsie) and [[d:rogue#1423412190991487027]] (Beldrin: "any caster still shits on rogues if they have a brain").
- **[Weapon speed for PvP]** Unlike PvE (fastest weapons win), PvP favors punchier 1.5-1.8 speed weapons since raw white-hit damage matters more in short trades; full 1.3-speed setups are considered weak in PvP because auto-attacks deal negligible damage against geared opponents and poison ramp-up is too slow — mages specifically are very hard to beat with a 1.3-weapon Assassin setup — [[d:rogue#1425315642621562943]], [[d:rogue#1426802377851273298]], [[d:rogue#1426802814004236408]] (Blindtongue, 2025-10-08/11).
- **[Weapon-swap proc tech]** PvP tech: keep fast weapons equipped for sustained damage, but swap in slower proc weapons (Dream's Herald MH + Tempest's Rage OH, or vice versa) to fish for their on-hit procs (AoE slow / DoT), then swap back to normal weapons once procced — useful for AoE slows in WSG tunnel or AV zerg fights — [[d:rogue#1424904767645421778]] (Piercing), [[d:rogue#1424958994329636977]] (Suade, 2025-10-07).
- **[Honor grinding]** PvP rank grinding (r10→r13/r14) achievable within weeks of consistent play; honor-decay bracket system explained: the #1-ranked player's honor total sets a threshold, and players below a fraction of that threshold in their bracket get decayed; best time to push rank is right after top players hit R14 and leave the bracket (bracket empties for 1-2 weeks) — [[d:rogue#1426804411669413959]], [[d:rogue#1426804501813399623]] (Blindtongue, 2025-10-12).
- **[PvP sets]** Rogue PvP set (blue) is considered weak/worse than raid alternatives — "0 agil[ity]"; T0.5 (dungeon set) plus a few PvP pieces gives better dodge (~10%) than the full PvP set; rogue PvP set bonuses are considered the worst among classes — [[d:rogue#1425885818936889436]] (wagehart, 2025-10-09), [[d:rogue#1425896616790986833]], [[d:rogue#1425896761444139240]] (Blindtongue).
- **[Sub PvP]** Row-1 PvP talent takeaways (Nelson): Improved Ambush weak (low weapon dmg + energy cost with fast daggers not worth it), Lethality PvE-only/weak in PvP, Serrated Blades near-useless (armor pen too small to notice), Eviscerate talent decent (pairs with Cold Blood), Improved Gouge "a must" for energy-pooling/restealth resets in 1v1, Improved Expose Armor as filler enables Sap→Cheap Shot→Expose→restealth openers. Suade's counter-build (in active PvP use) instead values Lethality (benefits Noxious too) over Improved Eviscerate, and keeps Improved Ambush for Kingsfall-swap openers vs clothies — [[d:rogue#1424187035299614801]] (Nelson, 2025-10-05), [[d:rogue#1424193013831434353]] (Suade).
- **[Poison choice PvP]** PvP poison usage mostly mirrors PvE (Dissolvent primary) but Mind-Numbing and Crippling Poison are used situationally on a weapon swap; their 30-minute duration (vs Dissolvent's ~1hr) is considered an annoyance since it's easy to forget to reapply — [[d:rogue#1424534850504888351]] (Blindtongue, 2025-10-06).
- **[Race/movement]** In PvP, dead rogues' wisp/ghost spirits move noticeably faster than other classes' ghosts, attributed to the passive rogue speed bonus persisting after death — [[d:rogue#1428523250157097071]] (Epickalen, 2025-10-17).
- **[Open]** How to counter a Warrior or Warlock who opens with a gap-closer + DoT was asked and left largely unanswered/unresolved in this chunk — [[d:rogue#1426547591121735720]] (AC, 2025-10-11; question unanswered, one partial reply about Flourish into stun for warriors [[d:rogue#1426550695024132207]] Purge).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Buff/debuff tracking]** pfUI's Buff Bar module (with a custom whitelist) is commonly used to track SnD, Adrenaline Rush, Taste for Blood, Rupture uptime, etc. — [[d:rogue#1426724764252114975]], [[d:rogue#1426725720172003328]] (Heffey, 2025-10-12). BetterCharacterStats addon can calculate hit% needed against a given target level. MPOWA and CandyDice were also mentioned for buff/cooldown tracking, though MPOWA reportedly has a display bug showing poison buffs as active when they're not — [[d:rogue#1423591545428381761]] (Thonpa, 2025-10-03), [[d:rogue#1426394239134208000]] (feeder5), [[d:rogue#1425046515864305664]] (Azitor-Nordanaar).
- **[Trinket swap]** TrinketMenu addon used to vanish-swap active/passive trinkets mid-fight — [[d:rogue#1423566500312383570]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-03).
- **[Poison-buying addons]** TWBuyPoisons is outdated (doesn't support Dissolvent); community forks/replacements exist: TWBuyPoisonsPlus (github.com/akzkak) and EasyPoisons (github.com/Cinecom/EasyPoisons, by Horyoshi) both one-click-buy the correct reagent quantities for poisons — [[d:rogue#1426129100073996309]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-10), [[d:rogue#1427216833139703939]] (Horyoshi, 2025-10-13). AdvancedTradeSkillWindow addon also auto-purchases vendor reagents for tradeskills generally — [[d:rogue#1427819043829579920]] (Beldrin, 2025-10-15).
- **[One-button rotation addons]** feeder5 built and shared a rotation-automation addon (requires SuperWoW + Nampower) that handles combo builders/finishers, interrupts (Kick/Gouge/Kick Stun), Vanish/Feint logic, poison-reapplication warnings, and a "smart Eviscerate" (only uses Evis if expected damage exceeds target's remaining HP) — reception was mixed/negative, with several experienced players calling one-button rotations "lazy" and testing showing bugs (buffs falling off when more than one finisher type was enabled) — [[d:rogue#1425044970422800435]], [[d:rogue#1425425510007046226]] (feeder5, 2025-10-07), [[d:rogue#1425502149999202314]] (Weirdthing: buggy in testing).
- **[Auto-attack macro]** `/startattack`-equivalent macro for SuperWoW clients: `/run for i=1,120 do if IsCurrentAction(i) then return end end CastSpellByName("Attack")` — [[d:rogue#1427288906759667722]] (Nøj, 2025-10-13).
- **[Pickpocket autoloot]** Enabling AutoLoot in the game options/launcher also autoloots Pick Pocket; a toggle-macro (`/script SetCVar("AutoLootDefault", 1)` before Pick Pocket, then back to 0) is an alternative for players who don't want autoloot on generally — [[d:rogue#1427598144165904414]] (GumGuzz, 2025-10-09), [[d:rogue#1427341395399545002]] (Farban).
- **[Low-FPS/UI addon]** `perf_boost` addon (gitea.com/avitasia/perf_boost) can hide other players/spell-effects to help low-FPS players navigate trash/raid encounters — [[d:rogue#1428451857281454141]] (30, 2025-10-16).
- **[Priority macros]** For combo-point generation, players macro Riposte/Surprise Attack/Sinister Strike in priority order (cast in sequence, engine picks the first available); reported issue that priority macros can misfire and use a lower-priority ability if you spam-click rather than waiting for GCD — [[d:rogue#1426981959011274890]] (attention), [[d:rogue#1426990174285336735]] (RatherDashin), [[d:rogue#1426994584910561391]] (Motke).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Race choice]** No strong consensus on best race; Orc and Human considered "rather equal" for Horde/Alliance respectively; among Horde options Orc/Goblin/Troll seen as close to even. Goblin is specifically called out because it's the only race with a racial bonus to Dagger weapon skill, relevant since Assassin/Combat-dagger BiS revolves around daggers ("bis involves daggers and gobbo are the only race that get a racial dagger skill") — [[d:rogue#1427355257637437613]] (Scruff, 2025-10-13), [[d:rogue#1427337882242125934]] (Minces), [[d:rogue#1427340825813323918]] (GumGuzz).
- **[Professions]** Leatherworking/Skinning recommended as a strong first profession pairing for a new rogue (self-sufficient armor + gold from skins); Herbalism+Alchemy also popular and can "save you [gold] equally as much" via self-made potions — [[d:rogue#1424901611431657575]] (Epickalen, 2025-10-07), [[d:rogue#1425151725467074723]] (Ryker).
- **[Engineering]** Gnomish Engineering preferred over Goblin for the Chicken and Death Ray gadgets; Goblin Engineering's only edge is craftable Sapper Charges, which can simply be bought off the AH instead. Goblin Sapper Charges are still called out by some as effectively "BiS enabling" for rogue burst/utility — [[d:rogue#1424833501223583744]] (Beldrin, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1424840738658717696]] (Epickalen).
- **[Gold making]** Raw open-world grinding for gold is called inefficient ("raw gold farms are ass"); professions (crafting/selling, especially Alchemy potions and high-demand/low-supply combinations) and pickpocketing are preferred. Pickpocket yield scales with level/zone: roughly 0.6-1.5 silver per pocket at level 20 vs 4-7 silver per mob in BRD at level 60. Fishing was reported to net ~2,500g over about 22 (unhurried) days right around hitting level 60 — [[d:rogue#1424441578155544657]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1426572924873343129]] (SilverWF), [[d:rogue#1426573343284527135]] (Farban), [[d:rogue#1425738560455053404]] (Epickalen).
- **[Market economy example]** Julie's Dagger (a popular cheap early-assassin weapon) fluctuated heavily on the Ambershire AH, from ~28-34g up to ~60g within a couple of days due to demand — [[d:rogue#1427354603309105274]] (Serec, 2025-10-13), [[d:rogue#1428010262514110484]] (Parabolic, 2025-10-16).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Blade Flurry stuck buff]** Blade Flurry's buff can get stuck permanently active (can't be toggled off) especially when it collides with the 32-buff display cap; only fix is to relog; frequently triggered on Kara40's Chess event and Incantagos fight; no official bug report filed as of this chunk — [[d:rogue#1424486188919558175]], [[d:rogue#1424488717736743042]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-06), [[d:rogue#1428255344823636018]] (Rheinwg, 2025-10-16).
- **[Buff-cap invisibility, general]** At the 32-buff cap, some buffs (Righteous Fury, Battle Shout, the Rupturan trinket's "Draenor's Blessing", rogue's own SnD/TFB on Kruul) can become invisible/untrackable while still active, requiring workarounds like manually banning low-value buffs via an addon — described as a client-side issue devs "can't do anything about," though some argue buff-consolidation (like a past consumable-combining pass) could help — [[d:rogue#1428259749983424523]] (30, 2025-10-16), [[d:rogue#1428259853721407498]] (The Multiverse).
- **[Darkmantle proc quirk]** The Darkmantle set's energy-restore proc does not trigger from Noxious Assault, because Noxious Assault doesn't register as a weapon "hit" the way normal attacks do; it procs normally off Sinister Strike/Backstab/Eviscerate/autos — [[d:rogue#1427915525920526366]] (GumGuzz, 2025-10-15).
- **[Weapon skill >315, debunked]** Community folklore claimed weapon skill above the 315 cap still gave marginal dps/crit gains; dedicated dummy testing (Andrion: 15,000 combined auto-attacks at 314 vs 319 skill) found no significant difference in hit/dodge, confirming 315 is a hard cap; a separate historical bug (reported "some months ago") did give +2% crit at exactly 316 weapon skill, since apparently fixed — [[d:rogue#1428634425167708180]] (Andrion, 2025-10-17), [[d:rogue#1428581890960392315]] (Rheinwg, describing the old bug).
- **[Poison tooltip/charge bugs]** Reported display bugs: poison buffs sometimes show as active on the buff bar when they are not; poison charge counts can appear "stuck" (showing full charges, e.g. 51, after the poison actually ran out) — [[d:rogue#1423591545428381761]] (Thonpa, 2025-10-03), [[d:rogue#1426177916961685616]] (Imaaaaaa, 2025-10-10).
- **[Emerald Mongoose]** Patch notes claimed a fix to Emerald Mongoose (trinket) to grant 2% spell crit, but in-game testing after the patch still showed no spell crit being granted — [[d:rogue#1428440707617521665]], [[d:rogue#1428440752710488245]] (Purge, 2025-10-17; unresolved/possibly still bugged).
- **[Glancing blow math dispute]** Community disagreement over the exact glancing-blow damage penalty at low weapon skill and how much weapon skill mitigates it: some cite a 65% (35% penalty) vs others a 70% (30% penalty) damage-on-glance baseline at 0 skill, with 2% mitigated per skill point up to 315 (giving 95% damage on glance at cap, i.e., only a 5% loss); no authoritative TWoW source was found in the discussion — [[d:rogue#1428577122011844689]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1428577431706669107]] (Epickalen); contested/open.

## Contested / open questions
- **[Nerf speculation]** Whether/how Assassination (Dissolvent+Taste for Blood interaction) and/or Combat (Blade Rush, Opportunity, dagger scaling) would be nerfed was extensively debated throughout the chunk with no developer confirmation; proposals ranged from a straight Taste for Blood reduction (15%→7-10%) to reworking Noxious Assault's poison-application mechanics to scale with weapon speed — unresolved as of 2025-10-17 — [[d:rogue#1426152580773449858]] (Xil), [[d:rogue#1426147420596600894]] (MistAssassin), [[d:rogue#1427014447729348649]] (Weirdthing: "you think we're not getting nerfed?").
- **[Combat vs Assassin closing the gap]** Whether Perdition's Blade alone is enough agility/gearing for Combat Daggers to close (or nearly close) the ~100-150 dps gap to pre-bis Assassination is disputed: Epickalen estimates Perdition's alone gives ~30 raw dps plus proportional gains, targeting a "good enough" 50-75 dps final gap, while Rosco is skeptical, estimating ~600 extra agility (not the ~120 available from Perdition's-tier gear) would be needed to meaningfully close it — [[d:rogue#1424523531538006047]], [[d:rogue#1424529090408611954]] (Epickalen) vs [[d:rogue#1424528151194632192]], [[d:rogue#1424505306116784249]] (Rosco); still open at chunk end, though later messages (2025-10-13 onward) report combat pulling clearly ahead once T2.5+/Perdition's is reached.
- **[White/dual-wield hit-cap mechanics]** Whether the dual-wield miss penalty applies equally to main-hand and off-hand white swings (not just off-hand) was disputed; The Messiah Of Ignite's log-based analysis and later GumGuzz confirmation both say yes, it affects both hands equally, contradicting an earlier "common misconception" that only offhand is penalized — treated as settled by chunk's end (GumGuzz: "dual wield penalty is for both mh and oh") — [[d:rogue#1424491034016223383]] (The Messiah Of Ignite), [[d:rogue#1427587433498939433]] (GumGuzz, 2025-10-14).
- **[Extra-attack ("chance for extra attack") procs and poisons]** Whether a "chance on hit for an extra attack" effect (e.g., Wing of the Time-Lord's 1% proc) counts as a normal autoattack capable of triggering poison procs was initially uncertain ("that sounds like some sword rogue nonsense... I think the answer is no?") but later confirmed via testing that it does proc poisons like any other autoattack — [[d:rogue#1427512788825149540]] (Epickalen, uncertain), [[d:rogue#1428438020935385118]] (Rheinwg, confirmed: "the extra hit can proc poisons, since is just another autoattack").
- **[Reliable AoE]** Whether Rogue has any reliable AoE damage source (beyond a weak, non-poison-proccing Blade Flurry) was raised and left effectively unanswered — general consensus elsewhere in the chunk is that Rogue (esp. Assassin) is single-target-only with "zero AoE" — [[d:rogue#1424414560672284713]] (SilverWF, 2025-10-06; question unanswered), corroborated by [[d:rogue#1427028408017092629]] (Farban: "strong singletarget, zero aoe").
- **[Threat poison / off-tank viability]** Whether a threat-oriented poison/build lets a rogue reliably off-tank without Taunt was asked and not answered in this chunk — [[d:rogue#1425943030623375512]] (Ionnatra, 2025-10-09; question unanswered).
