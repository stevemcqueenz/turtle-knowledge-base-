## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[assassination]** Consensus: Assassination is by far the strongest/easiest spec at low gear and early raid tiers (fresh 60, MC, early BWL), especially on freshly-opened servers (Ambershire) — a cheap Stormshroud-set rogue can nearly out-DPS a whole raid — [[d:rogue#1453417106849927280]] (Irithyl), [[d:rogue#1453417874885443604]] (riku), [[d:rogue#1455236174011699367]] (Rheinwg: "assa is better than combat early on, until late AQ40 at the very least"), [[d:rogue#1458836116474957917]]/[[d:rogue#1458836189980135424]] (as of 2026-01-08, essentially 100% of grouped rogues on Amber/South Seas run Assassination/Noxious Assault).
- **[combat]** Combat scales much better with agility/gear and is reported to overtake Assassination somewhere in the BWL-to-AQ40 range, though no exact breakpoint is agreed: estimates range from "500 or more agi" to "at least 600 agi, 700-800 better" — [[d:rogue#1458509558136836279]] (Boa), [[d:rogue#1453674895127875666]] (Romvlvs), [[d:rogue#1452384415731810304]] (Rheinwg: "no one knows where the breakpoint is... certainly not on AQ40"). On a mature, well-geared server (Nord), Combat Dagger is called BiS/"the best in raids right now" once agility is high enough that Backstab-Combat generates so much energy it's hard to spend, whereas Sword/Axe/Mace Combat can't dump energy fast enough at very high gear — [[d:rogue#1457872426187624459]], [[d:rogue#1457874383099003001]] (Heffey). Disputed counter-view (Rosco): Assassination's damage ceiling is already "self-nerfed" by the scarcity of fast raid weapons, and hardly anyone on the 7-year-old Nord server still plays it at the top end, so no nerf is warranted — [[d:rogue#1457532277088059571]], [[d:rogue#1457531714615382240]] (disputed by Rheinwg: "plenty of people play assa on nord" [[d:rogue#1457531792075522058]]).
- **[assassination]** Damage-composition clarified/disputed: roughly 80-90% of Assassination's damage is *not* weapon-damage-dependent (Noxious Assault, Eviscerate, Rupture are fixed/AP-scaled; only autos, ~10-20% on Ambershire-tier gear, scale with weapon damage) — [[d:rogue#1458499428577968219]], [[d:rogue#1458490912999997562]] (Nandieb). Counter-data from tested logs/screenshots: more like 55-60% poison, ~30-35% from Auto-Attack + Noxious Assault combined (i.e. weapon-damage-dependent), meaning a fast weapon like Alcor's is "not a must, but does increase your dps" — [[d:rogue#1458458202071892059]] (Ambroz, disputing the "gear doesn't matter" oversimplification).
- **[combat]** Combat Sword specifically is weaker than Combat Dagger ("dagger is absolute cream"); Sinister Strike has no clear niche vs. Backstab at high gear because Combat's energy regen when geared makes SS prone to overflow, and most Combat talents specifically buff Backstab — [[d:rogue#1455329848015851563]], [[d:rogue#1455330228212863049]] (Badknees/Farban), [[d:rogue#1457870349252169873]] (Rheinwg). Community wishlist (not implemented): a non-dagger-specific energy-dump finisher or talent to fix Sword/Axe/Mace Combat's energy overflow at high agility — [[d:rogue#1457885292013813977]] through [[d:rogue#1457900212990251212]] (Epickalen/Treehide/Rheinwg, speculative).
- **[subtlety]** Sub has the lowest personal DPS but is valued for Improved Expose Armor (IEA, +5% raid physical dmg once target has 300+ armor stripped — more armor reduction than Sunder Armor gives), Hemorrhage's armor debuff, and Marked for Death/Shadow of Death; one Sub is usually "enough," and it's "pretty good to have 1 in the raid" though not strictly required to clear content — [[d:rogue#1452384493787680879]] (30, IEA math), [[d:rogue#1457984797275062497]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1454810711191715972]] (Nuian: harder sell on small/early raids). Sub is very unpopular at endgame ("very few people play them") — [[d:rogue#1457982569386737715]] (styr), [[d:rogue#1457977168737865748]] (Mtungawimbo, question).
- **[leveling]** "You can't f--k up a rogue leveling build, it's just unfortunately Noxious is a power spike and forces a meta" — most builds work leveling; no need to switch to Assassination purely for the level-40 Noxious Assault spike — [[d:rogue#1454483953946726454]] (Epickalen, well-received). Assassination has two leveling power spikes: level 30 (Envenom) and level 40 (Noxious Assault); before 30 it plays like Combat but has good talents even earlier — [[d:rogue#1457019856678555731]], [[d:rogue#1457865401189929031]] (Rheinwg). Sub is behind both Combat and Assassination for leveling damage but fully viable with "fun tools" — [[d:rogue#1457041995527885043]] (Rheinwg).

## Leveling
- **[general]** Weapon choice: swords until level 20, daggers take off after that (assumes Assassination-track build) — [[d:rogue#1452317526255075451]] (Nandieb). Poison-unlock quest available at level 20 from the class trainer (Org/SW); dual-wield is trainer-taught, no quest needed — [[d:rogue#1453172522127200276]], [[d:rogue#1455207036533997640]].
- **[MistAssassin's structured guide, +2 reactions]** Levels 10-28: take Combat talents left-to-right (Remorseless Attack → Improved Backstab → Improved Ambush); use the strongest dagger you have after speccing Backstab. Levels 28-39: after Precision, rest is personal preference. Level 40: respec into Assassination. Level 60: final respec — author's update, take 5/5 Seal Fate and Cold Blood over Improved Eviscerate — [[d:rogue#1458113459639156888]].
- **[assassination, 1cp Eviscerate build]** GumGuzz's recommended leveling spec: 1cp Eviscerate spam, staying Assassination to 60; talent screenshot posted — [[d:rogue#1452302730873016551]].
- **[assassination]** Classic Nandieb build: swords until 20, daggers after; at 40 grab all Assassination poison talents (Noxious Assault "REALLY pops off"), then Subtlety up to Blackjack, last 3 points flexible (Lethality/Cold Blood/Seal Fate); good through 60 for dungeons/world/PvP, only respec for raiding — [[d:rogue#1452327445297631242]] through [[d:rogue#1452328692071141617]].
- **[leveling, pure Ambush]** 5/5 Camouflage + Remorseless Attack + Cruelty gives ~70-80%+ crit Ambush on chain-pulled mobs, one-shotting/near-one-shotting most mobs to 40, then pop SS once + Eviscerate; switch to deep Assassination at 40 — [[d:rogue#1453543331966484500]] (Toxicshock_OGHavic), [[d:rogue#1456337144036458597]]. Also strong for world PvP (T'A/warmode): can Cheap-Shot-kill a flagged 60 in PvE gear with 0.9-speed daggers as a "41/10/0" build — [[d:rogue#1456338490861027438]].
- **[leveling opener progression]** Improved Ambush as opener pre-40; Cheap Shot as opener 40-52; from 52 (Dissolvent unlocked) Cheap Shot then "just wreck faces" — [[d:rogue#1458145441609683149]], [[d:rogue#1458145570056310915]] (Calavero).
- **[poisons]** Learn order: Instant + Crippling early; Dissolvent rank 1 at level 52, rank 2 at level 60 — [[d:rogue#1454171985125965834]] (TwistedMindz). Switching to Assassination at 40 is "a huge dps boost" — [[d:rogue#1454172680109428756]]. Noxious Assault's full potential (physical-damage poison that also hits elementals/undead) unlocks at 52 — [[d:rogue#1454485227497062492]] (Epickalen).
- **[leveling weapon choice]** Prioritize weapon speed over weapon damage once poison-focused: even a level-14 vendor dagger + BFD dagger drop out-damaged a level-58 player's slow blues (mob kill time dropped from 2 min to 30s switching Combat→Assassination) — [[d:rogue#1458453915614838856]] through [[d:rogue#1458454295744741568]] (blacksusi); using swords ≥1.8 speed while leveling Assassination is a big enough loss that even 1.3-speed grey daggers out-damage them — [[d:rogue#1457769410730262632]] (Nandieb). Cheap leveling daggers: green Sacrificial Kris ×2 from AH lasts to 60; RFK has a random BoE 1.3-speed dagger that's very strong for leveling Assassination but "scales like ass" into raids — [[d:rogue#1453334104870490202]], [[d:rogue#1455236913337340026]], [[d:rogue#1455237488300785856]]. Tool: filter Turtle DB by item level ~45 min / your level max, sort by speed, to find the best easily-gettable fast weapon — [[d:rogue#1458488311441129502]] (Ionnatra).
- **[leveling weapon type, Combat]** Two viable leveling lines: Combat Daggers (Backstab/frontstab-focused, more damage but needs positioning and groups) vs Combat Non-Daggers (Sinister-Strike-based, smoother solo play, any upgrade usable, but fewer good daggers exist overall) — [[d:rogue#1454485754301644911]] through [[d:rogue#1454486717573632141]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1454556230197706863]] (andre).
- **[Taste for Blood while leveling — worth taking, contested]** Detailed argument (Lightemgee): TFB is worth taking even pre-Noxious because it extends Rupture's bleed duration (+6s at 3/3, see Cooldowns section for exact numbers) — a "free" 1cp Rupture during leveling effectively becomes a 3cp-duration bleed, and combined with Serrated Blades' armor reduction it compounds; Murder (2%) has "so little impact while leveling" that Improved Eviscerate is better in that slot since mobs die fast anyway — [[d:rogue#1458117532002156584]] through [[d:rogue#1458119876865691761]]. Rheinwg agrees TFB shouldn't be skipped, mainly for its use vs. poison-immune enemies (see Rotation section) — [[d:rogue#1458089869749391553]].

Endgame recs: Alchemy/Herbalism solid; Leatherworking/Skinning good (needed for Naxx tier crafting — save cured rugged hides, worth 19-20g guaranteed per 72h); Mining/Engineering for parses (dynamite AoE, bomb CC); Jewelcrafting benefits from pickpocketed gems; Enchanting good for gold via disenchanting boss drops + soloing low-50s 5-mans — [[d:rogue#1452823033373917429]], [[d:rogue#1453273131988029490]], [[d:rogue#1456709177215815825]] through [[d:rogue#1456709385601683753]], [[d:rogue#1456721410880700456]], [[d:rogue#1456726541248499814]]. - **[lockpicking]** No new Turtle-specific zones beyond Redridge→Wetlands→Badlands→Sunken Temple + box-opening; Windshear Mine and a Stonetalon site exist but are mob-dense — [[d:rogue#1453081530707476542]] through [[d:rogue#1453094862269448336]]. ## Rotation: single target
- **[assassination]** Full ST rotation: Noxious Assault (spam) → 1cp Envenom → 1cp Slice and Dice → 3-5cp Rupture (per Seal Fate procs/fight length) → refresh Envenom+SnD → resume Noxious Assault → reapply Rupture before expiry, or Eviscerate when Envenom/SnD both have ~1.5s left — [[d:rogue#1453725480594182164]] (andre). Apply SnD before Envenom so both buffs sync and can be refreshed together — [[d:rogue#1456564160266506442]] (Zllig), [[d:rogue#1456571331628765289]]. Never use Backstab as Assassination — [[d:rogue#1456036053197783113]] (Badknees).
- **[assassination, low level 41]** Nox Assault → 1cp Envenom → 1cp SnD; at 52 with Taste for Blood + Dissolvent, add a 3-5cp Rupture at rotation's end to proc the buff; once energy regen (Stormshroud/Darkmantle) lets you finish a full rotation with 5-10s of buff time to spare, start adding 3-5cp Eviscerates — [[d:rogue#1456432270717092092]] (andre).
- **[assassination, CP dump]** Whether to Eviscerate vs. spam more Noxious Assault at full buff uptime depends on gear: with 5/5 T2.5 + 3/3 Improved Eviscerate, Eviscerate hits hard — cast it when Taste for Blood has ≥9s remaining (Dissolvent scales off the TFB physical-damage bonus); getting to 5cp faster for Rupture uptime is itself a DPS gain, especially early in a fight before all buffs are up — [[d:rogue#1456603861119795230]] (Farban), [[d:rogue#1456615596044058754]] (Nandieb).
- **[assassination, poison-immune targets]** Pre-52 kit: Sinister Strike instead of Noxious Assault, spam Eviscerate, use a stun; still Rupture the target even though poison-immune — Rupture fails to apply the poison but refunds all 5 combo points, grants the Taste for Blood buff anyway, and lets you Eviscerate immediately after with those refunded points — [[d:rogue#1458097139384061952]] through [[d:rogue#1458097139384061952]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1456779097341431961]].
- **[combat, sword]** Not just SS/Backstab spam — uses Sinister Strike, Rupture, Slice and Dice, Eviscerate, and Adrenaline Rush; "little hard to backstab with a sword" — [[d:rogue#1453779948174311515]] (Scruff).
- **[general positioning]** Attacking from behind avoids parries but not dodges (only frontal attacks can be parried) — [[d:rogue#1453199382680830063]], [[d:rogue#1453200218559483968]].
- **[general]** Poison PROC RATE is weapon-speed-based (faster weapon = more procs); poison DAMAGE itself is fixed by talent/rank with a slight AP scaling and is not affected by weapon speed — visible on the ability tooltip — [[d:rogue#1456782751251300572]] through [[d:rogue#1456783002968260668]] (Rheinwg). A stated "10% increased chance to proc poison" talent effect is additive (e.g. 30%→40%), not multiplicative — confirmed via in-game tooltip update — [[d:rogue#1457074841684541462]], [[d:rogue#1457087792277487740]] (GumGuzz).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[assassination]** Blade Flurry is a "noob trap" for Assassination: it cleaves away poison damage (poison procs are ~40%+ of total Assassination damage, mostly from Noxious Assault) and adds an energy penalty, without transferring poison damage to the second target — only worth it against a single stronger/higher-level/elite/boss target you're not tightly timing your rotation around, not as an AoE tool — [[d:rogue#1456462381906657310]] (Lightemgee), [[d:rogue#1458110919421722820]] through [[d:rogue#1458112154426277910]] (Rheinwg/Beldrin).
- **[subtlety]** Also generally not recommended for Sub: ruins damage and energy regen, prevents using Shadow of Death or maintaining Tricks of the Trade; would be worth it only if Hemorrhage's debuff also spread with it (it doesn't) — [[d:rogue#1456462707405361326]] through [[d:rogue#1456463026185310208]] (Rheinwg). Exception: trash packs where you can't land openers/finishers at max CP before adds die anyway, since you only get to hit each add once regardless — [[d:rogue#1456463874256797738]] (Rheinwg). Sub's energy regen doesn't scale with gear the way Combat's does, so spending it on Blade Flurry means fewer Hemorrhages/Hemo debuffs overall — [[d:rogue#1456464253216362607]], [[d:rogue#1456464683950280715]].
- **[combat, wishlist — not implemented]** Speculative ideas floated for giving non-dagger Combat a cleave/AoE niche: cheaper Sinister Strike during Blade Flurry, a sword/mace/fist-specific "gain % damage" talent, or "chance on attack: next attack guaranteed crit" — [[d:rogue#1455361537610092667]] through [[d:rogue#1455361778434310204]] (Rheinwg/Epickalen, not implemented).

- **[hit]** "8% is the magic number" to not miss with special (yellow) attacks is widely repeated, though one player at 310 weapon skill/8% hit still reported misses on Hemorrhage/Expose Armor — [[d:rogue#1453210313590575166]] (Romvlvs), [[d:rogue#1453189720287739978]] (Zeke, disputing in practice). Every point of weapon skill above target defense gives 0.2% hit on top of reduced glancing chance — [[d:rogue#1453071480655974461]] (Lightemgee). Hit is called Assassination's strongest stat (white/auto-attack hits matter a lot since they carry poison procs); green/passive crit is "basically negligible" for Assassination unless already hit-capped — [[d:rogue#1456575020074795039]], [[d:rogue#1456575108138401955]] (disputed by Nandieb: crit still helps via Seal Fate procs — [[d:rogue#1456588787646988320]]).
- **[weapon skill]** 315 is the soft cap; Precision (5, Combat only) + weapon-skill quest (+5) + Death's Sting (talent, +3) + Goblin racial (+3) can total 313-316, letting Combat/Goblin builds skip Aged Core Leggings/Gloves (ACLG, +5 skill) entirely; Assassination players are told to skip ACLG outright ("if you're playing assa dont bother") for an agility item instead — [[d:rogue#1452740283820412978]] (Scruff), [[d:rogue#1453103899186561135]] (Farban), [[d:rogue#1452656557996380212]] (Weirdthing), [[d:rogue#1452663202184826930]]. Missing 2 weapon skill ≈ 6 dps loss for Combat; whether that's worth trading for extra agility/AP elsewhere is disputed — [[d:rogue#1452794198742007941]] (Epickalen).
- **[assassination weapon speed]** Every 0.1 speed increase above a 1.3 baseline costs roughly 6 poison-proc DPS (e.g. Perdition's Blade 1.8 + Fist of the Flamewalker 1.5 ≈ 42 DPS loss vs. dual 1.3s, before haste); overall Assassination output ranges ~600-800+ DPS depending on gear — [[d:rogue#1454557039320895619]] (andre). Rule of thumb for when a slower weapon is worth it (unconfirmed math): it needs meaningfully higher *weapon damage* (not displayed DPS) than a faster alternative, and the loss from being slower is worse the more haste you stack; one unconfirmed estimate needs >7 extra weapon DPS to offset lost procs — no one has found a hard formula — [[d:rogue#1458420745024700499]] through [[d:rogue#1458423316850278450]] (Ionnatra), [[d:rogue#1458425200411541687]], [[d:rogue#1458428061107093600]] (Rheinwg, "you have to check every new weapon you get").
- **[weapon speed by spec]** Assassination wants the fastest weapons in both hands (Noxious Assault ignores weapon damage entirely — pure AP + poison procs); an ability's damage isn't affected by OH speed/damage unless it explicitly hits with both weapons — [[d:rogue#1452522179832053924]] (Not Normal), [[d:rogue#1455236617676656816]] (Rheinwg). Combat (Backstab/SS) and Hemorrhage (Sub, 110% weapon dmg) / Ghostly Strike (Sub, 125%) all want a slow, high-top-end MH — daggers are a poor fit for Sub, non-dagger weapons (sword/axe/mace) deal better Hemo damage — [[d:rogue#1452923528776257617]], [[d:rogue#1456182431836274719]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1456127623041122396]] (Romvlvs). Traditional OH speed targets: ~2.00 for SS users, ~1.80 for daggers, ~1.50 for swords; a 1.50 fist weapon (Saph Fist) works as OH for any spec — [[d:rogue#1453446565984473139]] (Rheinwg). Backstab itself scales stronger the slower your MH is — [[d:rogue#1453446941777203324]].
- **[agility/AP conversion — contested, rough estimates]** "Agility being 4ap per point is a ridiculously high estimate"; more realistic community feel: ~1.2 AP/agi out of combat, ~1.5 AP/agi in combat (crit + Kings + playstyle) — [[d:rogue#1452413640887111720]], [[d:rogue#1454575931418214694]], [[d:rogue#1454592229430526135]] (Epickalen). For Combat: crit/hit/haste ≈25 AP-ish each, agility ≈1.72 AP pre-Blade-Rush-rework and 2-3 AP after; for Assassination: hit/crit ≈25 AP-ish, haste ≈32 AP (1% haste ≈31-32 AP) — vs. only ≈3 AP-equivalent for 1% haste on Combat Daggers — [[d:rogue#1456771001621942497]] (andre), [[d:rogue#1456852397849579722]], [[d:rogue#1456852525519998976]]. Between 8 agility and 1% haste, haste wins "every time" for Assassination; raw agility only preferred over % gear if the differential is large (~30 agi) — [[d:rogue#1456781537226784799]] (andre, math assumes 1.3-speed weapons + Dissolvent II). Crit breakpoints: ~26-28.5 agility ≈ 1% crit (with Kings) — [[d:rogue#1452908538761449493]], [[d:rogue#1453163697496391720]].
- **[combat, Backstab energy economy]** Backstab costs ~50 energy for 2 combo points (~25 energy/cp), notably cheaper than other specs' generators, which need ≥35 energy/cp; Ruthlessness + Relentless Strikes largely solve energy/CP flow issues; Vigor has no cooldown but Honor Among Thieves has a minor (1.5s) internal cooldown — [[d:rogue#1458709866162950256]] through [[d:rogue#1458711740941467731]] (Ionnatra).
- **[combat, Blade Rush]** Old ratio cited: 400 agility × 0.012 = 4.8, giving a 1.52s energy tick (per 20 energy) at that agility — [[d:rogue#1453142479212642567]], [[d:rogue#1453146772091441296]] (Romvlvs).
- **[assassination]** Prioritize haste and hit over crit (single-source suggestion) — [[d:rogue#1454605717507149917]] (Nandieb).
- **[subtlety]** Serrated Blades gives ~30% more Rupture damage via armor penetration (3/3 = 5 armor-pen per level = 300 at level 60, multiply tooltip value × target level; stacks with, and is separate from, Sunder/Expose Armor's armor reduction), but since ~40%+ of Assassination-hybrid Sub damage already ignores 100% of armor via poison, the alternative 5% hit (Precision) is argued to be worth more since it lands more autos (more poison applications) — [[d:rogue#1455376662563651716]] through [[d:rogue#1455377250739290224]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1458162827876372480]] through [[d:rogue#1458165921024704748]] (Jeebs/Beldrin/Ionnatra debate; consensus leaned toward hit).
- **[general]** "1 dps ≈ 14 AP" used as a rough item-comparison rule of thumb — [[d:rogue#1454263193072435221]] (andre). Spirit is believed not to affect rogue energy regen; unconfirmed — [[d:rogue#1452411921042243584]] (unanswered).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[assassination, fresh 60 — strong, concrete recommendation]** Get the fastest weapons possible immediately and ignore weapon damage entirely; don't be lured by a slow (2.0+ speed) "upgrade." Vendor a slow MH for cheap 1.3-speed Poniards, buy Julie's Dagger from AH, or farm Finkle's Skinner (UBRS)/Stormfist/Bonescraper (Stratholme UD) at 60. Even Alcor's/The Ripper are only a "minuscule dps increase" vs. actually getting Darkmantle and the rest of your gear — don't gear Assassination like a normal melee DPS where weapon damage is king — [[d:rogue#1458064201364017296]] through [[d:rogue#1458066367306534925]] (Nandieb).
- **[general]** "You can play combat fresh 60, but seriously, getting 2 1.3 daggers with whatever gear you already have and respec to assa will already give you more damage than farming full pre-bis and playing combat" — [[d:rogue#1458793532859879435]] (Nandieb). Pre-raid path (any spec, per andre): Distracting Dagger, Stormshroud set, a 1.3 MH (Julie's/Finkle's), Whip of Encouragement (SWV), a Darkmantle hit trinket, get hit-capped; from MC/Ony only bother with Onyxia neck, Band of Accuria, Ragnaros Agility Cloak — [[d:rogue#1454949078591869019]], [[d:rogue#1454949336520589413]].
- **[gearing debate: Stormshroud vs. off-pieces]** For Assassination, theoretically Stormshroud is a bit more DPS than swapping to T1/T2/ACLG "off pieces," but it's proc-RNG-dependent per-encounter and Stormshroud's lack of Stamina raises death risk, especially if healers are stretched — dying once or twice can offset the DPS gain; Devilsaur Armor preferred by many as a stats/stamina alternative — [[d:rogue#1458382701747900552]] through [[d:rogue#1458387175308529801]] (Ambroz/Nandieb), [[d:rogue#1458410097062711378]] (Ionnatra). Darkmantle's own proc rate is NOT confirmed/known despite a claimed "5%" figure (disputed) — see Bugs & quirks.
- **[subtlety]** Pre-raid: Darkmantle + Devilsaur, focus on 8% hit (Sub lacks Combat's hit talents) — [[d:rogue#1454201154694287523]], [[d:rogue#1454201248327925812]] (Rheinwg). 4+4 Darkmantle+Stormshroud early, swap toward T2/PVP pieces at BWL — [[d:rogue#1454913150951620659]], [[d:rogue#1454914348249251871]]. From MC/Ony: T2 pants, Band of Accuria, Ragnaros back, Magmadar bow, Onyxia-head necklace, Aged Core Leather Gloves, Emberstone trinket — [[d:rogue#1454915470720504011]], [[d:rogue#1454917470132375624]]. Amber-server tested BiS-ish: 2pc Bloodfang + Windwalker Boots + 5pc Darkmantle beat 4x Stormshroud+4xDM (~750 dps on heroic dummy, alcor+dd) — [[d:rogue#1452683722540191906]] (riku). Darkmantle 4-set called BiS "for a solid chunk of time"; Cadaverous/Devilsaur are good alternates if you can't finish it — [[d:rogue#1452553338108252211]], [[d:rogue#1452528372407336973]], [[d:rogue#1452531297066029159]]. Bloodfang set can out-DPS some AQ40-geared rogues while awaiting full T3 — [[d:rogue#1455291974323015760]] (Badknees).
- **[combat dagger, BWL era]** Reported setups near BWL launch: "4x DM, T1 shoulders, T2 head+legs, ACLG" or per a "modified sheet," 6xDM+Perdition+DD+T2legs+head, or T2legs+T1belt+Perdition+CHT+ACLG — all close in DPS — [[d:rogue#1452691846235951135]] (riku), [[d:rogue#1452746797679116439]] (Scryu). Distracting Dagger (DD) is +6 (not +3) weapon skill and 0.2 speed faster than Fist of the Flamewalker — [[d:rogue#1456730864519221340]] (Threbox).
- **[gear/weapon items]** The Ripper (SWV drop): its armor-shred debuff refreshes rather than stacking, but benefits the whole raid (it's a debuff on the enemy) and "stacks with everything" else — [[d:rogue#1454971278740946944]] through [[d:rogue#1454971986252923033]], [[d:rogue#1454970779136557300]] (Rheinwg); proc rates roughly 25% fast-acting / 5% DoT+armor-debuff, and it lowers armor by 160 (not the tooltip number) — [[d:rogue#1452730417156194458]] (GumGuzz). Annihilator OK as OH early for Sub, but needs 2 copies for full value, which hurts personal damage — [[d:rogue#1454970135134732443]], [[d:rogue#1454970228881752213]]. Satyr Claw is "a meme, too low dps" vs. Julie's — [[d:rogue#1458465271659368458]].
- **[BoED/OBD spellstrike build]** BoED (Blade of Eternal Darkness) build pairs well with Ring of Electrical Binding, Incendosaur set, Gilneas ring, Flamestring bow; using Incendosaur + Embergem Cuffs (craftable) lets you keep 4pc Darkmantle too — [[d:rogue#1456367291871531060]], [[d:rogue#1456367407663939655]] (Nuian). BoED average drop rate estimated ~69 runs (wide variance: some got it in ~45-90 runs, others 300-430+) — [[d:rogue#1455235743197827185]] (andre), [[d:rogue#1456223931353530430]] (Equinox). Every enemy in BWL and some in MC are immune to the OBD/BoED proc — [[d:rogue#1458813725979967632]].
- **[T3/T3.5]** T3.5 crafting requires a token, a material, and 2 crystals (no profession mats) — [[d:rogue#1455391326853005387]] (Rheinwg). Rogue's T3, uniquely among classes, requires paying the crafter gold rather than only materials — a recurring community complaint — [[d:rogue#1455409383898087596]] through [[d:rogue#1455424906748624967]] (Epickalen/Treehide).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[poisons, mechanics]** Dissolvent Poison's proc is weapon-speed-based (not PPM), so a full-poison build wants two max-speed daggers since Noxious Assault ignores weapon damage entirely — [[d:rogue#1456664655203405854]], [[d:rogue#1456665366892904498]] (Rheinwg). All poisons ignore armor, not just Dissolvent (the tooltip line on Dissolvent is described as "made to confuse people") — [[d:rogue#1453053321748152330]] (Rheinwg). Dissolvent is nature damage and won't proc on nature-immune targets (elementals); Instant does proc on them — [[d:rogue#1453018745541890088]] (feeder5). "Double Dissolvent is only useful for Assa" — [[d:rogue#1453021373059432570]] (bobbysalami). Instant vs. Dissolvent damage difference is small (~8) without Taste for Blood; with 3/3 TFB, a 1cp Rupture deals about the same bonus as Dissolvent's edge — [[d:rogue#1453055230508793927]] through [[d:rogue#1453055604661944412]] (Rheinwg). Dissolvent/Instant can both be resisted (otherwise Efficient Poisons would be a wasted talent) — [[d:rogue#1453055808324501596]].
- **[Taste for Blood — exact mechanic]** TFB is not its own spell; it auto-extends Rupture's duration whenever Rupture is cast. Base Rupture duration by combo point (1-5cp): 8/10/12/14/16s; TFB adds seconds per point up to +6s at 3/3 (confirmed via addon spell-duration data) — [[d:rogue#1458132484637065343]] through [[d:rogue#1458133880510087351]] (Doite/Lightemgee). Kidney Shot has no such talent extension; its own base durations by rank/cp are Rank1: 1/2/3/4/5s, Rank2 (2nd rank adds +1s to all): 2/3/4/5/6s — [[d:rogue#1458134221213270056]], [[d:rogue#1458134358308425912]]. With 3/3 TFB, a 5cp Rupture gives +15% damage — [[d:rogue#1455318766366883880]] (Zaszz).
- **[poisons, combos by spec]** Assassination MH+OH: double Dissolvent, or Dissolvent MH + Corrosive OH. Combat: Deadly MH + Corrosive OH (or Corrosive II OH); vs. Deadly-immune (nature-immune) bosses swap to Dissolvent MH + Corrosive OH — [[d:rogue#1453040913973514384]] through [[d:rogue#1453047228523610256]] (Jean Pol/bobbysalami/Heffey). Boss fights: use DoTs (Deadly/Corrosive); trash/instants: use Instant/Dissolvent; on long stationary fights DoTs win, on fights with movement/target-swapping (can't maintain 100% uptime) instants are better — [[d:rogue#1453054422715469837]] (Lightemgee), [[d:rogue#1452386069650735144]] through [[d:rogue#1452386970222202992]] (Rheinwg/Premium Retired).
- **[poisons, PvP]** Cripple + Instant/Dissolvent is standard PvP unless you're Assassination with at least Envenom, in which case go full poison (2x Dissolvent) — [[d:rogue#1458124808360427704]], [[d:rogue#1458125490224103537]]. Crippling is best applied via Deadly Throw (swap to a separate weapon carrying it, rather than eating a MH-poison-uptime loss) — [[d:rogue#1457119702026424497]], [[d:rogue#1457163863957962784]] (Nandieb/Lightemgee). Windfury Totem and MH poison are NOT mutually exclusive on Turtle — always keep poisons up regardless — [[d:rogue#1457094923265376523]] (GumGuzz).
- **[weapon enchants]** Turtle-specific slot enchant guide (Epickalen): haste to head/shoulders/legs; haste to gloves if poor (agility if Combat, though haste is "entirely valid" too); chest 3-stat if replacing soon, 4-stat if not; agility everywhere else — [[d:rogue#1457342736679309465]]. Chest is always 4-stat, rings always 6-agility for pure raid DPS — [[d:rogue#1457286188636573818]] (The Multiverse). ZG idol enchants (head/legs, only 2 drop per ZG raid) less needed by rogues than other classes since rogues have more enchant options — [[d:rogue#1457286638840447109]], [[d:rogue#1457288808700575877]]. Weapon enchants for Combat Dagger: agility on both weapons is the consensus pick (+3 reactions) — [[d:rogue#1453002452768129165]] (Drox); alternatives discussed were 2x Crusader, Superior Striking MH + Crusader OH, or Crusader MH + Agility OH — [[d:rogue#1453628263871156305]] (andre), 15/15 agi both weapons also recommended — [[d:rogue#1453653189126525001]] (Treehide/Verrik).
- **[haste vs. AP/agi enchants by spec]** For Assassination, 1% haste ≈31-32 AP-equivalent — heavily favors haste; for Combat Daggers, 1% haste is only ≈3 AP-equivalent, so the ZG helm/leg 28AP+1%dodge enchant or straight agility can beat haste there — [[d:rogue#1456852397849579722]], [[d:rogue#1456852525519998976]], [[d:rogue#1456822230234300632]]. Two haste enchants (gloves+shoulders) are only outmatched by ZG helm/leg once you have Sapphiron-tier gear — [[d:rogue#1452415837490249850]] (Epickalen). Shoulder haste enchant (2%) requires Hyjal rep: 5x Bright Dream Shard + a Fading Dreamshard Fragment (ES/rare Hyjal drop/AH) — [[d:rogue#1457346154206593141]], [[d:rogue#1457347453354381344]].
- **[Minor Speed / movement]** Boots' Minor Speed enchant is ~8%, stacks additively with Quickfooted, does NOT stack with Emerald Blessing — [[d:rogue#1452630303456755775]], [[d:rogue#1452635874750631966]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Feint]** Widely considered too weak — only reduces threat *generation rate* rather than dumping accumulated threat, leaving players "dangerously close" to pulling threat from Thunderfury tanks even while spamming it; multiple players want a rework (e.g. dump a % of current threat on a short CD) — [[d:rogue#1455331774166204702]], [[d:rogue#1455332150848127160]] (Badknees), [[d:rogue#1455334580973338725]] (riku), [[d:rogue#1455361248580468992]] (Epickalen, clarifying it's a generation-rate debuff not a threat wipe).
- **[Vanish]** Its 5-minute cooldown is seen as too long to double as a reliable "oh sh*t" defensive/threat-dump button — [[d:rogue#1455331774166204702]] (Badknees).
- **[HoJ / extra-attack procs]** Hand of Justice doesn't share a cooldown with Windfury or other extra-attack procs (multiple procs can't trigger off each other, but can both fire off the same auto-attack) — [[d:rogue#1456579331798208599]] (Letitonatebe). On dummy, Whip of Encouragement + HoJ beats Whip + a 2%-haste trinket for Assassination; HoJ recommended mainly for deep-Assassination since "any extra swing is counted" for poison procs — [[d:rogue#1456572715405148276]], [[d:rogue#1456573243199717521]] (Nuian), [[d:rogue#1458424482631913709]] (Ionnatra).
- **[Serrated Blades]** At 3/3, grants flat armor penetration = 5 × your level (300 at level 60); multiply the tooltip number by your level. This stacks with, and is mechanically separate from, Sunder/Expose Armor's armor reduction (pen vs. reduction) — [[d:rogue#1455376662563651716]] through [[d:rogue#1455377250739290224]] (Rheinwg).
- **[tank rogue meme]** Stacking agility for dodge + a "threat poison" (or Tab-targeting with a threat poison for guaranteed top-of-list threat) is a known joke/gimmick, but functionally not real raid tanking — normal-DPS-rotation rogues will only hold aggro for a few seconds against even mediocre tanks before the tank catches up ("congrats, you're tanking now for a whole 4 seconds") — [[d:rogue#1456004204358406338]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1456014522396708864]] (Badknees), [[d:rogue#1456019756397236472]] (Heffey: "functionally a non-starter in a raid environment").
- **[what actually separates top parsers]** Not rotation optimization (Assassination's rotation is simple and forgiving of a 1-2s delay) but target uptime, potion timing, mechanic optimization, and offensive Vanish usage — a "99% perfect rotation alone is really not going to get you anywhere near the numbers of the top rogues" — [[d:rogue#1456309909795766556]] (Scryu, well-argued).
- **[client tech]** Turtle's client (Pepopo/Nampower fork) supports instant-spell queueing near the end of the GCD (`NP_QueueInstantSpells`, default on), letting you press ~200ms early and have it fire the instant the GCD ends — [[d:rogue#1456378647744090212]], [[d:rogue#1456396845067538483]], [[d:rogue#1456397067416113515]] (30/Floccumate).

## Tanking specifics
- **[general]** Rogue "tanking" is essentially a meme/HC-content trick (stack agility for dodge, use a threat poison) rather than viable raid tanking — see Cooldowns/utility section above for full citations. Not viable for real raid boss tanks (e.g. Razuvious-tier) since it lacks Preparation/avoidance gear scaling and any real mitigation kit — [[d:rogue#1456947774141759568]], [[d:rogue#1456948279425237128]] (Weirdthing).

## PvP
- **[general dominance]** Poison damage's armor-ignore property makes Assassination "absolutely faceroll" in PvP on low-progression servers (Ambershire) — "you do not need Preparation"; less extreme but still dominant on Nord — [[d:rogue#1455614843603390658]] through [[d:rogue#1455617558500872394]] (Nandieb). Proposed fix (not implemented): fold poison damage into the Resilience stat (already reduces crit/DoT damage) rather than reworking the whole tree — [[d:rogue#1457006345948758121]] (Nandieb).
- **[talents/PvP-focused points]** Improved Eviscerate and Throwing Weapon Specialization are solid alternatives to Murder's flat 2% for PvP; Throwing spec lets you re-apply Crippling and interrupt Polymorph via Deadly Throw right as a mage blinks out of a stun — [[d:rogue#1452418521735889082]] through [[d:rogue#1452418911353049351]] (Zaszz). Murder (2% vs. Human/Giant/Beast/Dragon) does work on players — [[d:rogue#1452406989798904032]].
- **[PvP builds shared]** Cruor's lvl 60 Perdition-MH PvP build on Ambershire: Improved Sap, Improved Kick, Improved Sprint, Preparation, Improved Throws — [[d:rogue#1457734835224182956]]. Nandieb's open-world/PvP/dungeon build: `https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=AoYDIYbYZAQYB--FDCQYAC` — [[d:rogue#1456677515480268964]]. Ionnatra's Garrote+Rupture/chain-CC PvP concept: `https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c/1.18.0/rogue?points=YQYCI-FAoAK-FACYbISARIQ` — [[d:rogue#1456734003271831704]].
- **[PvP talent tradeoffs]** Can't run Noxious Assault + Precision + Blackjack simultaneously — must choose hit (Precision) vs. Sap utility (Blackjack) — [[d:rogue#1456700046891548754]] (Nandieb). Cold Blood + Seal Fate valued as a fast early-fight combo-point builder toward a quick 5cp Rupture — take the flex point from Lethality rather than Cold Blood — [[d:rogue#1456701714404085952]]. Improved Ghost Strike's movement buff still gives a noticeable (if reduced, ~7%) speed boost useful for kiting after past nerfs; Smoke Bomb is good vs. casters when rooted/frozen if you don't want to spend Vanish/Sprint; Improved Gouge favored for isolated 1v1 world PvP — [[d:rogue#1458174007345152153]] through [[d:rogue#1458175756977832228]] (Ionnatra/Lightemgee).
- **[gear]** Full PvP blue (Rank 10) set is considered one of the worst PvP sets in the game; 4pc Darkmantle + 4pc PvP is preferred by most — [[d:rogue#1454540079690158212]], [[d:rogue#1454649446003642420]] (riku).
- **[mechanics]** Channeled spells (Arcane Missiles etc.) continue hitting even if you break line of sight after the channel starts — this is standard classic/WotLK-era behavior, not a Turtle bug; LoS still stops non-channeled casts like Polymorph/Frostbolt — [[d:rogue#1457120113353429147]] (Nandieb), [[d:rogue#1457127314453168311]] (Lightemgee), [[d:rogue#1457142007008071680]] (Lord Grim). Spells cannot be dodged, only resisted — [[d:rogue#1457120113353429147]].
- **[utility]** Weapon-swap macros for poison variety (e.g., swap to a Crippling-poisoned weapon before Deadly Throw) require quoting item names with apostrophes: `/equip "Vis'kag the Bloodletter"` — [[d:rogue#1457383559827558635]] through [[d:rogue#1457390658519040101]].

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Horde PvE]** Troll (haste racial, best synergy with Assassination's poison-proc-frequency goal) or Goblin (+3 dagger skill, lets you skip ACLG for tier gloves) considered best; Orc an honorable mention (axe skill + Blood Fury AP burst, syncs with Adrenaline Rush for Combat or Shadow of Death/Marked for Death for Sub); overall DPS difference between races is small, aesthetic preference is fine — [[d:rogue#1458412004581834918]], [[d:rogue#1458412178620158075]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1458418355819249810]] (Ionnatra), [[d:rogue#1453123250036019241]] (Romvlvs). Undead valued for Will of the Forsaken (fear break, useful in almost every raid) + 2% dmg to Humanoid/Undead — [[d:rogue#1453064017475014688]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Alliance PvE]** Nelf: haste racial + movement speed good; Human: sword/mace skill bonus good, Perception buff considered good for PvE by some (though on the GCD, so limited rogue value) — [[d:rogue#1453909906594988172]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1453064512667254856]], [[d:rogue#1453064591490945134]]. High Elf gets stronger the more haste you stack (2% agi + on-demand 50 energy active); one player calculates its passive 2% agi only matches Goblin's flat +3 dagger skill / T2 glove value once total agility is very high (~1000) — [[d:rogue#1454251462228971610]], [[d:rogue#1454254278855098546]] (Farban). Dwarf/Gnome have the smallest class benefit; Gnome racial (escape-type ability) is useful in PvP — [[d:rogue#1453063367765000272]], [[d:rogue#1454230813783756881]] (Rheinwg).
- **[professions]** Any profession slows leveling; Alchemy/Herbalism, Leatherworking/Skinning (needed for Naxx crafting), Mining/Engineering (AoE/CC utility), Jewelcrafting (pickpocketed gems), and Enchanting (disenchant boss drops + solo farm low-50s dungeons) are the recommended endgame picks — see Leveling section for full citations.
- **[gold]** Cured Rugged Hide sells for a guaranteed 19-20g every 72 hours; raw rugged-hide skinning nets a guaranteed gold or two per skin now (used to be ~10 silver, implying a market/patch shift) — [[d:rogue#1456737096407453808]] (Farban), [[d:rogue#1456744094867919022]] (Epickalen). Lockpicking is a steady moneymaker if actually used (box-opening) — [[d:rogue#1458678446006861885]] (Badknees). Grinding spots: Azshara Blood Elves (level 50-53, fast kills + gold/runecloth), Winterspring Furbolgs (56+, firewater/cloth/jujus + pickpocket heavy junkboxes, contested area) — [[d:rogue#1456639255412604968]], [[d:rogue#1456639468281794582]] (Farban).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[historical]** Poisons briefly scaled with Spell Power/Spell Crit due to a bug that "lasted 1 day"; poisons are not intended to scale with spellpower or spell crit — [[d:rogue#1453438982275727573]] (Rheinwg).
- **[item exploit]** A quest for a dagger referred to as "Kinrial's" was reportedly available earlier than intended due to a bug; the dagger was later nerfed, but characters who obtained it during the bug window kept the original (stronger) stats — [[d:rogue#1458392225397608533]] (Ambroz).
- **[boss mechanics change]** Attack-speed-reduction and damage-reduction debuffs on bosses from different sources no longer stack — only the single highest value applies now — [[d:rogue#1457701839037403378]] (30).
- **[Smoke Bomb]** Affects both allies and enemies inside its radius (slows/increases miss chance for everyone), so it's a bad choice for saving a raid from an extra trash pull — it also handicaps your own raid — [[d:rogue#1457453882451038528]] through [[d:rogue#1457454631889141997]] (Rheinwg); though it does reduce damage taken for everyone inside, which can help a healer catch up in some cases — [[d:rogue#1457475774226628812]] (Lightemgee). A previous buggy iteration of Smoke Bomb "had the wrong effect" and was fixed — [[d:rogue#1457217855321014463]] (Rheinwg).
- **[unclear/unconfirmed mechanics]** Darkmantle's energy-restore proc chance is not publicly confirmed — a claimed "5%" figure (vs. Stormshroud's claimed "2%") is disputed as possibly wrong, and "no one could answer" the real number when asked directly — [[d:rogue#1454409145993265265]] (Talvian, disputed), [[d:rogue#1454459406707331082]] (Nandieb), [[d:rogue#1458366942942330978]], [[d:rogue#1458372335869493279]] (Nandieb, unanswered again weeks later). Whether Noxious Assault can benefit from physical crit is unconfirmed — [[d:rogue#1458364516323561544]] (Nandieb, unanswered). Whether re-applying Deadly/Corrosive Poison too quickly makes it "skip ticks" is unclear — [[d:rogue#1457810547339821368]] (Lightemgee, unanswered). Whether Tricks of the Trade grants spell crit/spellpower to the target ally is unclear — BetterCharacterStats reportedly doesn't pick up Mark for Death's spellpower bonus even though the tooltip states it — inconclusive testing — [[d:rogue#1458321013241675807]] through [[d:rogue#1458326516164333605]] (Lightemgee/Rheinwg). Whether Shadow of Death can crit is disputed — Rheinwg has never seen it crit and believes it acts like a DoT — [[d:rogue#1458616549098393725]].
