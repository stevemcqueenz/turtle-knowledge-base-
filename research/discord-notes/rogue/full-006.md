## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Live balance patch mid-chunk, 2026-01-23]** Patch notes posted verbatim: "Taste for Blood: Physical damage bonus reduced from 1/2/3% to 1/1.5/2% and no longer increases damage dealt by Corrosive Poison and Dissolvent Poison. Noxious Assault: Attack Power-scaling reduced from 35% to 30%." Also, verbatim: "Opportunity: Damage bonus reduced from 4/8/12/16/20% to 3/6/9/12/15%. Developer Comment: Combat dagger rogues are performing tad better than with any other weapons, this nerf will bring them more in line." Blade Flurry received a small buff, Blade Rush left "intact" — [[d:rogue#1464347389597847693]] (Nuian, 2026-01-23), [[d:rogue#1464387758796837010]] (riku, 2026-01-24, Opportunity note verbatim), [[d:rogue#1464347282890817660]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2026-01-23), [[d:rogue#1464358937691291842]] (Farban, 2026-01-24: "Blade rush: intact, Opportunity: still serviceable").
- **[Patch effect, Assassination]** Community math (napkin, not sim-verified) put the net Assassination nerf around 7-8% since ~50% of Assassination damage is poison (Dissolvent/Corrosive) and TfB no longer buffs those, on top of the Noxious Assault AP-scaling cut from 35% to 30% (a further ~15% reduction to Noxious Assault's own damage per GumGuzz's math); despite the nerf, multiple players (andre, Rheinwg) expect Assassination to remain the strongest spec on Ambershire until AQ40-level agility gear becomes available for Combat — [[d:rogue#1464353031163220151]] (Scryu, 2026-01-24), [[d:rogue#1464354310795297043]] (Scryu, 2026-01-24: "corrosive poison too... bump it up to 8%"), [[d:rogue#1464348073860927602]] (Rheinwg, +2, 2026-01-23), [[d:rogue#1464359188107886672]] (andre, +1, 2026-01-24), [[d:rogue#1464358980796022938]] (GumGuzz, 2026-01-24).
- **[Patch effect, Combat]** Combat Dagger got a small net buff from a Blade Flurry/cleave-related change that "outdoes" the Opportunity nerf; estimated ~7-8% nerf offset by the cleave buff, but the underlying sword-vs-dagger energy-overcap problem (swords waste energy capping while daggers don't) was not addressed — [[d:rogue#1464352017823825964]] (-Z?-, +1, 2026-01-24: "meta spec now is Combat Dagger"), [[d:rogue#1464357160094924862]] (Epickalen, 2026-01-24: "cleave buff outdoes the opportunity nerf"), [[d:rogue#1464352574499983445]] (Weirdthing, +1, 2026-01-24: "if swords didn't have an overcap problem it's within 5% of daggers"), [[d:rogue#1464352671702974717]] (Weirdthing, 2026-01-24).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Post-nerf, further confirmed numbers]** Blade Flurry's energy-regen penalty reduced to 20 (i.e. the ability now costs the wearer less energy regen while active) as part of the same patch — [[d:rogue#1464460222419439824]] (Beeg Yoshi, 2026-01-24). Community tally: "Assa lost Noxious [Assault] 15% [effective] dmg (35→30% AP scaling), lost 15% poison scaling from TFB" — [[d:rogue#1464569867993878677]] (GumGuzz, 2026-01-24). Net effect debated further next day: with TFB now only affecting non-poison damage, and poison being a smaller share of total damage after the nerf, some argue TFB becomes relatively "worth pressing more" (Nandieb) since the non-poison portion of damage it boosts is now a bigger slice; overall TFB nerf now estimated around 5% (down from 15% flat) — [[d:rogue#1464583689563537410]] (Nandieb, +1, 2026-01-24), [[d:rogue#1464584973729398795]] (Nandieb, 2026-01-24).
- **[Post-nerf weapon-speed effect]** Poison-scaling nerf is expected to slightly reduce the value of haste and shift the weapon-speed/DPS formula toward making somewhat slower (1.4-1.5 speed) daggers "more viable" for Assassination, though "not dramatically" — [[d:rogue#1464584363172696221]] (Nandieb, 2026-01-24), [[d:rogue#1464584535005204532]] (Nandieb, 2026-01-24).
- **[Post-nerf BWL rotation math, IEA vs Rupture]** Napkin-math argument for dropping Rupture in favor of Improved Expose Armor uptime in BWL: TFB's 10% bonus applies to only ~50% of an Assassination rogue's damage, so losing all Rupture/TFB uptime is only a ~5% personal DPS loss (², 2.5% loss at an assumed 50% uptime baseline); IEA gives roughly a 5% raid-wide physical damage increase applied to about 50% of the raid's damage type, i.e. ~2.5% raid DPS gain — which "evens out" the personal loss from cutting Rupture uptime to prioritize IEA; countered that this ignores the opportunity cost of speccing into IEA at all, and that Cold-Blooded Eviscerate on short fights remains a big DPS cooldown you don't want to give up; a middle-ground tip: Expose Armor can be slotted in as a finisher in place of Eviscerate without fully dropping Rupture from the rotation — [[d:rogue#1465609959285981292]] (Nandieb, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465610768685994120]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465611957016203328]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465614756684632135]] (GumGuzz, +1, 2026-01-27).
- **[Spec summary, concise]** "Combat Dagger performs best, but only on higher raids (AQ40+) — Backstab is stronger than Sinister Strike because SS has energy-spending problems. Assassination is stronger in early raids because its damage comes from poisons and fast weapons, but it doesn't scale well, so Combat becomes stronger at higher raid tiers" — [[d:rogue#1465626709507182602]] (Nettlefield, 2026-01-27).
- **[Combat crit value]** For Combat Daggers, crit is valued more highly than for Assassination because poisons cannot crit from melee/physical crit rating (only spell crit affects poison crit), whereas Combat's autos/Backstab/Sinister Strike benefit directly from physical crit — [[d:rogue#1465627335121309697]] (Matt Lender, +1, 2026-01-27).
- **[Sub weapon enchant]** Crusader is used on both MH and OH for Sub specifically to maximize the chance of both procs lining up, since Mark for Death (MFD) is popped when Crusader procs for a burst window; the AP-per-minute-vs-flat-agility tradeoff of double Crusader is debated but the proc-timing synergy with MFD is the stated reason for the choice — [[d:rogue#1465622166660714598]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465622624800342118]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-27).

## Rotation: single target

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Dual-wield hit/miss]** Turtle WoW rogues have an additional ~19% dual-wield miss penalty on top of normal miss chance, making hit valuable well past the "soft cap" that vanilla classic rogues traditionally chased; players commonly run anywhere from 8% to 20%+ hit depending on gearing options — [[d:rogue#1465636991772135540]] (GumGuzz, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465643148608012496]] (Farban, 2026-01-27: "hover around 20 [hit] in AQ40 set, one of our best stats for dagger, increases crit cap too").
- **[Hit cap, PvE Assassination]** With 5/5 Precision, the hit cap for yellow/ability attacks is 8%; the cap for white/physical swings is "around 20-something%" — [[d:rogue#1464590114242953434]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-24, links to another pinned explanation), [[d:rogue#1464590142525280420]] (Nuian, 2026-01-24).
- **[General]** Ability hit cap is 8% (Rheinwg: "the only thing you should ALWAYS reach for abilities"); beyond 8%, extra hit only benefits autoattacks, which is still good — [[d:rogue#1464288231825866842]] (Rheinwg, +1, 2026-01-23).
- **[Hit/crit-cap interaction]** Every point of hit increases the crit cap; example given: 8% hit (6% from talent+gear, 2% from weapon skill) → crit cap ~30%; raising hit by 10% via gear raises crit cap by 10% as well — [[d:rogue#1464282453752545361]] (Jean Pol, 2026-01-23), [[d:rogue#1464284351444553903]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-23), [[d:rogue#1464284544159977473]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-23).
- **[Worked example, high hit/weapon skill]** With enough hit/crit stacked, example attack table: Miss 8.5%, Glance 40%, Crit 42%, Dodge 5.5%, Hit 3.15% (parry excluded, assumes attacking from behind) — [[d:rogue#1464286630683873409]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-23).
- **[Haste value, contested]** No consensus on a fixed "haste = X AP" conversion because haste stacks multiplicatively (percentage sources multiply together, not add) and interacts with random procs (Tempest Rage, Kiss of the Spider, Flurry, quickness pots); estimates ranged from ~25-32 AP per 1% haste (andre/Epickalen napkin math) up to ~90-98 AP in one derivation, with the group settling on "anywhere between 28 to 32" as a rough single-handed dagger (1.3 speed) estimate; explicitly called unreliable/"feelscrafting" — [[d:rogue#1463479185933733939]] (Zaszz, 2026-01-21), [[d:rogue#1463479966103507090]] (Zaszz, 2026-01-21), [[d:rogue#1463679144796753973]] (andre, 2026-01-22, long derivation), [[d:rogue#1463633856929206305]] (Epickalen, 2026-01-22: ~32 AP for assa, ~55 AP for 2% haste on combat), disputed as unrealistic by [[d:rogue#1463602036279869470]] (Not, 2026-01-21).
- **[Haste vs hit for enchants]** Debate on head/leg enchant choice: 1% haste vs ZG-style flat enchants is "basically a margin of 3 AP," i.e. nearly equal — [[d:rogue#1463478629487874255]] (andre, 2026-01-21).
- **[Enchant priority list, Assassination]** "Haste > agility": Head 1% haste (Dire Maul Libram), Shoulder 2% haste (Hyjal), Legs 1% haste, Gloves 1% haste (cheaper than 15 agility, nearly equal value); everything else agility — [[d:rogue#1463526672509829221]] (Epickalen, 2026-01-21), disputed by [[d:rogue#1463602036279869470]] (Not, +2, 2026-01-21: prefers 28 AP over 1% haste on head/legs).
- **[Enchant priority, Combat]** Combat needs more hit than Assassination because it relies on white/auto damage, whereas Noxious Poison procs let Assassination get by on 8% hit; "talent +315 [weapon skill] is unironically all you need" — [[d:rogue#1463680133775757312]] (Epickalen, 2026-01-22).
- **[Real-world haste caps]** Realistically ~15-26% passive haste is achievable via trinkets/enchants/boots without crippling the rest of the build (5% from trinkets, 4% from enchants, 2% from boots cited as a rough breakdown); pushing further requires "detrimental sacrifices" — [[d:rogue#1463679707634471110]] (Antez, 2026-01-22), [[d:rogue#1463686027582640138]] (Epickalen, 2026-01-22), [[d:rogue#1463687763428769853]] (Antez, 2026-01-22).
- **[Weapon speed]** For Assassination, faster weapons win even at equal DPS because physical/poison uptime beats slower weapons' proc frequency loss; daggers are chosen over swords primarily for the availability of very fast (1.3-1.4 speed) options, not an inherent speed cap advantage — [[d:rogue#1463984648840417467]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-22), [[d:rogue#1463968190421008570]] (andre, 2026-01-22).
- **[Assassination weapon-speed scaling gripe]** Multiple players want Assassination's Noxious poison-style proc damage to scale with weapon speed so slower/stronger weapons remain viable instead of forcing the fastest weapon always; suggestion that if Noxious scaled with weapon speed, ~1.8 speed weapons could become "fair game" for Assassination instead of forcing sub-1.4 daggers, trading poison procs for more physical damage — [[d:rogue#1464269041551151304]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-23), [[d:rogue#1464270058669998123]] (Rheinwg, +2, 2026-01-23).

For Assassination specifically, white/glancing damage is a small enough share of total damage that "the +0.2% hit from each point of weapon skill is worth infinitely more than anything related to glancing damage"; target is 315 weapon skill on both weapons if mixing types, dropping to 310 only situationally when a Kara40 set bonus outweighs the penalty — [[d:rogue#1464831919345762430]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-25), [[d:rogue#1464832263932870717]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-25), [[d:rogue#1464848857157341384]] (andre, 2026-01-25), [[d:rogue#1464831401604943873]] (Slinky, 2026-01-25).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Threat/vanish]** Rogues manage threat manually via Evasion/Vanish rather than pulling threat reduction from talents; players joke about tanking briefly on Evasion or vanishing when close to pulling aggro — social/anecdotal, not mechanical guidance — [[d:rogue#1463443474123587691]] (Epickalen, 2026-01-21), [[d:rogue#1463443064684150888]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-21).
- **[Badge of the Swarmguard vs Venomous Totem]** Badge applies flat armor penetration regardless of raid debuffs or attack timing, so it scales with the target's armor value — a rough rule cited is 300 armor ≈ 5% physical damage reduction, so against a 900+ armor boss Badge is worth roughly a 15% physical damage increase; Venomous Totem's value is constant/easy to test (count poison procs in 20s, double it, that's the extra DPS) and is better against low/no-armor bosses (e.g. KT, who has 0 armor) where Badge does nothing; both trinkets can be used together and swapped mid-fight via Vanish to bring in Kiss of the Spider afterward, but Kiss can't be reused a second time if kill times are fast (implies a cooldown longer than typical fight length) — [[d:rogue#1465742764678578378]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465749252314497114]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465739294802182328]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-27).
- **[Turtle-specific QoL: combo points]** Combo points now persist on the target as long as it stays alive, even if you click off it — a Turtle-specific change from the old behavior where combo points were lost as soon as you clicked away from the target — [[d:rogue#1465788380661944452]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-27).
- **[Turtle-specific: Blade Flurry baseline]** Blade Flurry is baseline on Turtle WoW (not a talent) — every rogue can learn it from their trainer at level 30 — [[d:rogue#1465788380661944452]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-27).
- **[Combo point overcap discipline, Combat Dagger]** General agreement: Taste for Blood should ideally always be used at exactly 5 combo points; the only exception is fast-dying trash where you won't reach 5 CP in time, in which case use the finisher early anyway to get the buff applied before moving to the next target; on bosses, prefer continuing to Backstab past 4 CP (risking a wasted combo point) rather than switching to a cheaper Sinister Strike/Ghostly Strike just to "cap" at 5 — [[d:rogue#1465777932885688331]] (Farban, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465778812716449873]] (Scruff, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465779286723002539]] (Farban, 2026-01-27).
- **[Vanish, PvE utility]** Vanish's primary PvE use is an aggro/threat reset — use it whenever you're about to pass the tank on the threat meter (commonly during Adrenaline Rush burst windows, but applies to any spec after popping cooldowns and getting a crit chain); it also removes movement-impairing effects (slows, roots) and works even outside actual combat state; a major secondary use is swapping on-use trinkets to passive ones mid-fight — [[d:rogue#1465825152125374744]] (Farban, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1465825442559950990]] (Farban, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1465825846521626935]] (Farban, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1465824624422949110]] (DLX, 2026-01-28, question).
- **[Trinketmenu addon QoL: queued swap]** A recent Trinketmenu addon update lets you queue a trinket swap to fire automatically on your next Vanish/combat-drop: shift+left-click and shift+right-click two trinkets to queue them, which can be set up even before a pull; the queue ignores combat drops shorter than 3 seconds (so a warlock pet re-aggroing you briefly doesn't cancel it); the same queuing works between trash packs; the addon also lets you hide irrelevant trinkets (teleporters, engineering cables, etc.) from the swap menu. Addon credited to "Pepo" — [[d:rogue#1465833401046007849]] (Heffey, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1465833733126094973]] (Heffey, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1465834180465135707]] (Heffey, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1465834359205400834]] (Heffey, +1, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1465836300082090146]] (Heffey, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1465843204455665687]] (Heffey, 2026-01-28).
- **[Macro warning]** Naive priority macros (e.g. always casting Sinister Strike then Surprise Attack) risk overcapping energy if not conditioned on current energy amount; recommended to either keep Surprise Attack on a separate manually-pressed button, or write the macro to check energy before deciding which ability to cast — [[d:rogue#1465780647061622865]] (Heffey, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465780870953697547]] (Heffey, +1, 2026-01-27), [[d:rogue#1465780958304276677]] (Heffey, 2026-01-27).
- **[Trinket management]** Recommended to use the Trinketmenu addon to manage on-use trinket rotations efficiently ("not getting actives and rotating them off CD is just accepting a DPS loss") — [[d:rogue#1463685030973800459]] (Heffey, 2026-01-22), [[d:rogue#1463685194526621824]] (Heffey, 2026-01-22).
- **[Trinket interactions]** ZG Energy trinket historically shared a 30s "used a trinket" cooldown with on-use AP trinkets (unconfirmed if still true, "last tried months ago"); ZG tank trinket's buff persists even after swapping the trinket out; badge-type trinkets don't share a cooldown; strategy of starting a fight with the Energy trinket then Vanish-swapping to Emberstone (or another on-use) mid-fight to get both effects — [[d:rogue#1463342518388129923]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-21), [[d:rogue#1463342390445211668]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-21), [[d:rogue#1463342313613950991]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-21), [[d:rogue#1463343828990693452]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-21).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Dungeon set drop rates, resolved as not-a-bug]** Multiple players (including at least one who was told by a GM to post about it) reported suspiciously low drop rates for Rogue's T0 (Shadowcraft) dungeon set pieces on Ambershire — e.g. one player ran Scholomance 35-40+ times without seeing the leggings, another did 66 runs for boots, another ~200 runs for shoulders, and cited Shadowcraft gloves trading for 100-200g on the AH vs. 3-20g for other classes' dungeon set pieces as evidence. Community consensus (including from players who track drop history) is this is NOT a bug: it's explained by (a) plain bad luck/RNG under geometric distribution — hitting a low-probability drop repeatedly doesn't change per-kill odds, and other players got the same pieces on their very first or 4th-5th kill, and (b) supply/demand — very few groups run the specific bosses needed (e.g. LBRS is rarely farmed) while nearly every Rogue wants the T0.5 set, so market supply is low and demand is high, inflating price disproportionately to actual drop rate; the drop rate of "pants"-slot items specifically has historically been low across multiple classes' T0 sets, not just Rogue's — [[d:rogue#1466052743847215217]] (mehmetkuc, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1466053591058874391]] (Cladrius, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1466055069324808399]] (Hoof, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1466055394483765511]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1466055814044454922]] (Nuian, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1466066600229863539]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-28), [[d:rogue#1466064848080081039]] (Scruff, 2026-01-28).
- **[Quiraji poison mechanic]** Quiraji poison (a Turtle-specific poison type) has no internal cooldown (ICD), unlike some other proc-based poison/weapon effects — [[d:rogue#1465896738475872360]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-28).
