## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Assassination/Combat/Sub]** Patch note (verbatim, quoted repeatedly): "Corrosive Poison and Dissolvent Poison now both only apply on the following types: Elementals, Mechanicals, Giants & Undead." — biggest single change this week — [[d:rogue#1484513053876949133]] [[d:rogue#1484568806998147205]] [[d:rogue#1484568872416841818]] [[d:rogue#1484561030062407831]] (2026-03-20, near-universal negative reaction, dozens of +reactions across thread).
- **[Assassination]** Taste for Blood (TFB) changed from 3 talent points to 2 (freeing 1 extra point), physical damage bonus reduced 1/2/3%→1/1.5/2%, and **no longer increases Corrosive/Dissolvent Poison damage** — [[d:rogue#1484222296192454848]] (Cladrius, 2026-03-19).
- **[General]** Net damage-only impact assessed as modest (~5-10% Assassination, less for Combat/Sub); the poison-*type-restriction* QoL change (forcing weapon/poison swaps per mob-type, mid-pack) is judged far worse by the community than the damage nerf itself — [[d:rogue#1484533914575048828]] (Mardi) [[d:rogue#1485568535215476746]] (Nandieb: "assa does like 10% less damage, but combat envenom was only 5% below all along, so it's 5% ahead now") [[d:rogue#1485744609379221705]] (bug report filed by Scruff, turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=20818).
- **[Assassination]** Real measured DPS drops on dummy: 850→600 unbuffed double-instant (Rhaena) [[d:rogue#1484567232330727476]] [[d:rogue#1484567369325084724]]; 1700→1400 (Zllig) [[d:rogue#1484583376286519307]]; evis-spam 1.1k→700 (Purge) [[d:rogue#1485641175082860544]]; one player reported ~30% of prior Naxx dps [[d:rogue#1485748420990996682]].
- **[Tool]** GumGuzz released "Combat Gum" (combatgum.app) rogue simulator the day before the patch: supports 1.18.1 itemization, Fortune stat, talents/presets, rotation builder, buffs/debuffs, combat logs, trinket/vanish-swap logic, racials, Blade Flurry, most raid bosses, build sharing via URL — [[d:rogue#1484243895675129986]] (+45 reactions, pinned); iterated same week: v2.1 (rotation builder revamp, new BWL items) [[d:rogue#1484725168768286801]], v2.2 (bugfixes) [[d:rogue#1484946182496325722]], mobile QoL + poison-damage fix [[d:rogue#1485059200856883210]].
- **[General]** Confirmed bugfix, not intentional per a GM: the extreme poison-mob-type restriction described above is treated by devs/GMs as a "bugfix," not a new balance pass; community disputes whether it was play-tested at all — [[d:rogue#1485750363192561695]] [[d:rogue#1485634710490190017]] (DesignerDave: "Assassination took the biggest hit... I do see warriors creeping over me again").

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[All specs]** Poisons (Instant/Deadly/Corrosive/Dissolvent) scale purely from Attack Power — no spellpower or "nature damage" scaling applies; poisons can crit, but only from spell crit rating — [[d:rogue#1485319521324765194]] (GumGuzz) [[d:rogue#1484487450733711430]] (Im pro vise).
- **[All specs]** Dissolvent/Corrosive are treated as physical/"extra melee attack" damage (armor-ignoring by their own mechanic, no longer boosted by TFB); Instant/Deadly are Nature-school damage — ignore armor inherently (nature has no armor mitigation) but are reduced by target Nature Resistance and can be resisted/partial-resisted — [[d:rogue#1484500340672958655]] (GumGuzz) [[d:rogue#1484586924663509082]] (Jeebs).
- **[PvP]** Incoming physical damage from players is globally reduced 15%; incoming magical damage (incl. Nature = Instant/Deadly) reduced 20% — cited as the reason Dissolvent was historically slightly favored over Instant in PvP despite Instant's ~4.5% higher base tooltip damage — [[d:rogue#1484491208670838905]] (Thirtyseven, screenshot) [[d:rogue#1484495024509943980]] (contested/nuanced by Jay: "this 5% difference in dampening is irrelevant" [[d:rogue#1484496533486305280]]).
- **[Combat dagger]** Weapon skill soft targets: 310 minimum, 313 good, 315 optimal (removes glancing-blow penalty); +weapon skill retains value for dodge/parry avoidance beyond 315 — [[d:rogue#1484908242193875065]] (Farban) [[d:rogue#1485075808069615737]] (Im pro vise).
- **[General]** 1 Agility ≈ 2.55 Attack Power for Rogues — [[d:rogue#1485420357652582441]] (Badknees) [[d:rogue#1485421649015603201]].
- **[Assassination]** Improved Poisons (flat proc-chance %) generally yields higher expected value than Vile Poisons (+30% poison damage) at typical proc rates: e.g. 20% chance × (150×1.3) = 39 EV vs 30% chance × 150 = 45 EV — [[d:rogue#1485268396365844613]] (Ionnatra math), and higher proc rate also means more Vigor/energy-on-proc uptime — [[d:rogue#1485268599940714548]].
- **[Fortune stat]** New 1.18.1 stat (e.g. +25% Fortune trinkets/cloak); confirmed multiplicative to existing proc chance (10%→12.5%, not +25 points), was initially misreported/tested as additive — [[d:rogue#1484670147619848192]] (GumGuzz) [[d:rogue#1485717139045224560]] (Ionnatra) [[d:rogue#1484580733673799700]] (early additive test, corrected).
- **[Haste theorycrafting, pre-patch]** With equal % haste, slower weapons save more absolute swing time and are easier to push toward the game's hard attack-speed floor (~0.25s) than fast weapons, though the *relative* DPS gain from a given % haste is the same for both — cited approximate haste thresholds ~750 for slow weapons vs ~650 for fast weapons to approach the cap (uncertain/"2am" memory) — [[d:rogue#1483747020157096008]] [[d:rogue#1483747944418250893]] (Epickalen, disputed precision by [[d:rogue#1483749726573690960]] "30": "you dont gain more from slow weps... its the same relative gain").

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
Full per-raid poison-immunity breakdown from Rheinwg's guide (2026-03-20, extensively cited/thanked):
- **[MC, all specs]** Trash: mostly Elementals/Giants → Dissolvent 2; Core Hounds low priority; one "green line" trash section → Instant instead. Bosses immune to Instant/Deadly (need Dissolvent/Corrosive): Garr, Geddon, the Two Golems, the Undead Dwarf. Bosses immune to Dissolvent/Corrosive (need Instant/Deadly): the Salamander boss, the Core Hound boss, Sulfuron, Shazzrah, Majordomo. Golemagg (a Giant) can be damaged by either — [[d:rogue#1484754354433953915]] (Rheinwg).
- **[BWL, all specs]** All enemies damageable only by Instant/Deadly, with 3 exceptions: Spellbinders immune to all poisons (low priority); Drakonoids can have high Nature vulnerability (extra-damage window); Chromaggus alternates high resistance/vulnerability to Nature but only Instant/Deadly work on him regardless. Recommendation: Assassination → Instant the whole raid; Combat → Instant for trash, max-rank Deadly for bosses — [[d:rogue#1484755632237187213]] (+4).
- **[AQ40, all specs]** All enemies damageable only by Instant/Deadly except Twin Emperors, who are immune to all poisons; use Frost Oils on Viscidus — [[d:rogue#1484756192734478396]] (+2).
- **[Naxxramas, all specs]** Trash mostly Undead (safe for both types) but scattered Humanoid/Beast enemies too, so Instant is the safer universal trash pick. Bosses mostly damageable by both, except Faerlina, Maexxna, Gothik: Assassination can run Dissolvent or Instant on those; Combat/Sub should use Deadly V+Corrosive II on most bosses, Deadly V+Deadly IV on Faerlina/Maexxna; Gothik is Dissolvent-only. Simplified callout: Instant+Deadly5/4 for the Spider Wing, Dissolvent+Deadly/Corrosive elsewhere — [[d:rogue#1484757612271501332]] (+2) [[d:rogue#1484722568937078811]].
- **[Kara40/Tower of Karazhan, all specs]** Trash is mixed throughout — pick a poison and prioritize what you can hurt. Before Gnarlmoon: Humanoid/Beast/Undead → Instant. Gnarlmoon→Incantagos: Dragons → Instant. Incantagos→Anomalus: Dragons+Elementals → pick either. After Anomalus: Undead/Elemental/Mechanical → Dissolvent. After Chess: Undead then Humanoid/Demon → Dissolvent then Instant. Bosses: Anomalus, Rupturan, Chess-boss immune to Instant/Deadly → Dissolvent+Corrosive (2+1 per spec). Medivh: any poison works, but Combat should use Mind-Numbing Poison instead, Assassination uses Dissolvent. All other bosses: Deadly+Instant only (Combat/Sub Deadly5+4, Assassination Instant). Edit: Worgen/Demon poison immunity on Kara40 trash appears to have been removed in a later hotfix, easing the swap frequency — [[d:rogue#1484759095419474041]] (+1, with edit).
- **[BWL: Firemaw]** New post-1.18.1 mechanic caused wipes for undergeared/first-raid groups (one server-first group wiped 13 times); Fire Resistance gear recommended for inexperienced raid comps even though not strictly required to clear — [[d:rogue#1484688013253542018]] [[d:rogue#1485228855458402486]] [[d:rogue#1485238430794383511]] (Kananaios/Rhaena).
- **[Onyxia's Lair]** New added boss/mechanic: adds must be killed before ranged DPS forces Onyxia to land, or the fight can reset her to 100% HP even after a near-kill; melee should prioritize killing the dragon/whelps/breeder before ranged forces the landing — [[d:rogue#1485397424171253760]] [[d:rogue#1485398433727647814]] (Ionnatra/Snek).
- **[BWL: Nefarian]** "Rogue Call" can teleport rogues to random spots around Nefarian instead of one cluster, risking cleave death if positioned wrong relative to others; Vanish can be used to safely exit/cancel the call — [[d:rogue#1485401895794114682]] (Maltor) confirmed [[d:rogue#1485408802412040365]] (Badknees).
- **[Kara40: Suppression Room]** Referenced repeatedly as a rogue-unfriendly AoE trash gauntlet (source of "rogue strike" jokes about refusing to clear it); question about whether Improved Stealth prevents pulling Broodlord from stealth there went unanswered — [[d:rogue#1484862503262486659]] (Kananaios) — see Contested.
- **[Dire Maul North]** "DMN Tribute run" — skip earlier bosses, fight only the final one for a treasure chest that can drop a Tarnished Ring (+18 Agi and stamina) — [[d:rogue#1485646505623945478]] (Kananaios).

## PvP
- **[General]** Incoming physical damage from players is reduced 15% overall in PvP; incoming magical damage (incl. Nature = Instant/Deadly poison) is reduced 20% — cited as why Dissolvent (treated as physical) was historically slightly favored over Instant (Nature) in PvP despite Instant's higher tooltip damage — [[d:rogue#1484491208670838905]] (Thirtyseven) — nuance disputed as a "non-factor" by [[d:rogue#1484496533486305280]] (Jay).
- **[General]** Post-patch, Dissolvent/Corrosive no longer work on Humanoid targets (players are Humanoid), effectively removing them from PvP against most classes; net PvP impact assessed as small ("just changing from dissolvent to instant") compared to the PvE impact — [[d:rogue#1485745513163653142]] (Scruff) [[d:rogue#1484479164290433085]] (Im pro vise).
- **[PvP poisons]** Recommended: Instant + Deadly, or Crippling + Wound/Instant/Mind-Numbing depending on matchup — [[d:rogue#1484569403310735430]] (Squibble).
- **[Sub/Assassination]** Subtlety generally regarded the dedicated PvP spec; Ambush-dagger burst rotation vs squishy targets: Ambush → Kidney Shot → auto-attack → Vanish → Ambush; reported Ambush crits ~1500 on Warriors and up to ~2500 unbuffed on cloth — [[d:rogue#1484647270224887849]] [[d:rogue#1484648685923991644]] [[d:rogue#1484506553162661949]] (Ionnatra).
- **[PvP]** Backstab-based PvP builds considered weaker than Ambush/burst builds since Backstab needs perfect positioning + energy simultaneously, hard to guarantee in PvP — [[d:rogue#1484650626221867089]] (Ionnatra).
- **[Sub PvP]** Cloaked in Shadows "works but is kind of weak" per limited testing — [[d:rogue#1484678061646417950]] (Rheinwg) — see Contested for the untested interaction with poison resists.

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Client]** Post-patch, some players saw Dissolvent Poison not consuming charges/dealing 0 damage even on valid targets; traced in at least one case to a corrupted client patch from launching via the .exe instead of the official launcher, requiring a full mod/addon wipe and relaunch through the launcher — [[d:rogue#1484876361184313477]] (Celeez).
- **[Item]** Ornate (trinket) stopped proccing off Corrosive/Dissolvent poison hits after the patch — [[d:rogue#1484556448913690758]] (Purge, dummy-tested).
- **[Item]** Noxious Assault does not count as a weapon swing for on-hit proc purposes (does not proc most trinkets); Servo Arm is the confirmed exception, behaving like an aura rather than a normal proc — running 2x Servo Arm stacks the aura to ~30% total proc chance — [[d:rogue#1483829020742127797]] (GumGuzz).
- **[Mechanic]** Windfury Totem's extra-attack effect shares an internal cooldown (~1.5s) across all sources per Eastern Ring Shaman-Discord sourcing, which caps how much benefit fast dual-wielders get vs 2H users — [[d:rogue#1484276910895661169]] [[d:rogue#1484278097686561089]] (GumGuzz/Epickalen).
- **[Set bonus]** Darkmantle set-bonus proc is PPM-based (~1 PPM); Fortune stat multiplies proc chance rather than adding flat percentage points, contrary to initial in-game testing — [[d:rogue#1484670258324312095]] [[d:rogue#1484669918216585337]] (GumGuzz/KoffiePa).
- **[Tool]** Combat Gum early-week bugs found and fixed: Adrenaline Rush giving 4x energy regen instead of 2x; Heroic Training Dummy resist values defaulting to 15/15/15/15 (needed resave after fix); build-sharing occasionally resetting rotation conditions to 0 — [[d:rogue#1484936850987815043]] [[d:rogue#1485407559991951420]] [[d:rogue#1484933855894048980]] (GumGuzz/Rhaena).
- **[Sub]** IEA (the Sub raid-support debuff) can only be applied by one rogue at a time on a given target — a second Sub's IEA does not add a separate stack — [[d:rogue#1484833009948561540]] [[d:rogue#1484833218879295529]] (Spoils).
