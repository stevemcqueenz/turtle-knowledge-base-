## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Assassination]** Poison rework (Turtle-specific): poisons are now split by creature-type matching. Instant Poison is the generalist option, usable on anything. Dissolving ("Dissolvent") and Corrosive poisons only apply to specific creature types now (previously worked on everything and hit harder); both were also damage-nerfed. This forces frequent poison-swapping in mixed-type trash/boss pulls — [[d:rogue#1485836027250606131]] (2026-03-24), [[d:rogue#1487458269957062890]] (Rheinwg, detailed explanation, 2026-03-28).
- **[Assassination]** The Dissolvent/Corrosive restriction hotfix landed the day before BWL released; widely seen as rushed/untested and the single most-hated change (bug report id=20859, 2nd-highest-upvoted on the tracker at the time, closed without action by a GM) — [[d:rogue#1486726290030592204]], [[d:rogue#1486073142630944899]], [[d:rogue#1486751781303353415]], [[d:rogue#1487470497246613594]].
- **[Assassination]** Noxious Assault (NA) took three separate nerf instances this patch, per Nandieb's breakdown: AP scaling reduced from 35%→30%; the TFB nerf indirectly cost NA another ~5%; and the poison it triggers lost another ~15% because TFB no longer boosts it — [[d:rogue#1489503815010095134]] (2026-04-03), corroborating earlier note [[d:rogue#1486882357804925109]]–[[d:rogue#1486884013452034148]] (2026-03-25). Sim result: full-Darkmantle Combat Envenom 1154 dps vs Noxious Assault 1096 dps — [[d:rogue#1486425755779989514]].
- **[All specs]** TFB (an Assassination-tree talent granting a ~5% haste + energy-regen buff and extending Rupture) was nerfed; consensus this makes TFB a net DPS loss whenever a spec runs Envenom. Community "rogue law #1": never run Rupture+TFB together with Envenom — [[d:rogue#1486822915180593272]], [[d:rogue#1486412462948352150]], [[d:rogue#1486815192690987191]] (Nandieb), reconfirmed as a hard rule with no exceptions found in dozens of sim attempts — [[d:rogue#1486823880004730964]], [[d:rogue#1487435203495596082]], [[d:rogue#1488627102281498785]] ("straight up, there is no case if you do envenom now where rupture will not lose you dps").
- **[Combat/PvP]** Opportunity talent nerfed too, but comparatively minor next to TFB — [[d:rogue#1487473182939283506]], [[d:rogue#1487473499927871498]].
- **[Deadly Poison]** A scaling bug that made Deadly Poison deal double damage for ~2-3 days right after the patch was hotfixed; this was reverting an accidental buff, not an intended nerf ("they didn't nerf deadly, it was just a bug that made it OP") — [[d:rogue#1485931775900520538]], [[d:rogue#1487076174592213113]], [[d:rogue#1487593806906785873]] (confirmed the bug lasted 2-3 days), [[d:rogue#1487598045968465921]].
- **[Assassination]** Deadly Poison IV is bugged/not recommended currently; Deadly Poison V isn't available yet on Ambershire — [[d:rogue#1486822268926558389]], [[d:rogue#1486822583545368736]]. Two rogues' Deadly IV debuffs don't stack independently: a dummy test with 2 rogues found 2 separate Deadly V stacks but only ONE shared Deadly IV debuff — [[d:rogue#1486069806133284864]] (hybris; confirmed unchanged by the nerf).
- **[Sub]** T3.5 5-piece set bonus proc chance tested at 15%, not the listed 20% — [[d:rogue#1486589740122898583]] (Nyxknight).
- **[General]** All specs got a flat damage nerf roughly in line with other classes, but Assassination's poison-matching QoL nerf (not the raw number nerf) is what drives "the spec is dead" sentiment — [[d:rogue#1486702772622725260]], [[d:rogue#1486703016068255825]] ("still good early on, but no niche in later raids").
- **[Itemization]** Rogues got ~0% DPS gain in weapon progression from MC BiS to BWL BiS (aside from the outlier Thunderfury), vs. warriors gaining an estimated >10% over the same span and other classes getting notable stat/health/armor jumps — [[d:rogue#1487474944429260860]], [[d:rogue#1487475066525581373]], [[d:rogue#1487476401954357368]] (disputed as understated by Rosco, single source).
- **[PvP]** New PvP gear carries embedded Nature Resistance suspected of countering rogue poison damage; new PvP daggers are 2.0 speed (bad for poison uptime) with worse min/max and set bonuses than old R14 — [[d:rogue#1487392153855328306]], [[d:rogue#1487400038425428131]], [[d:rogue#1487246054637437040]]. Confirmed later: **Corrosive and Dissolving Poison were removed from PvP entirely** — players are Humanoid, off the affected-type list — [[d:rogue#1488216301460783246]], [[d:rogue#1488217700206641192]] ("basically boned except against clothies"), [[d:rogue#1488560424252084256]], [[d:rogue#1488560526664532019]].
- **[Turtle-specific]** Blade Flurry is now a baseline trainable ability for all rogues (not a talent), toggled on/off anytime at the cost of some damage/energy regen instead of a timed buff — [[d:rogue#1487709614719111279]], [[d:rogue#1487710237699080293]], [[d:rogue#1487711528747536424]]. ## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Combat/Assassination hybrid]** "24/27/0" (Combat/Envenom, i.e. Combat-tree talents replacing deep Combat's Adrenaline Rush path with Envenom instead of Rupture) is repeatedly called the strongest all-around PvE spec this patch, replacing deep Assassination as "the new assa." Dagger variant uses Backstab, sword variant uses Sinister Strike; they perform within ~1-2% of each other — [[d:rogue#1485938127192330280]], [[d:rogue#1486356497477668977]], [[d:rogue#1486812371602243695]], [[d:rogue#1487049116382138449]]. Once Rupture is dropped from both builds, 24/27/0 "plays exactly like" 41/10/0 — the only difference is Backstab/Sinister Strike replacing Noxious Assault as the CP builder — [[d:rogue#1488628288845910149]], [[d:rogue#1488628602244169859]].
- **[Assassination]** Deep Assassination / Noxious Assault (41/10/0) is best pre-Darkmantle / while gearing as a fresh 60; Combat Envenom overtakes it once pre-BiS gear is reached, and NA becomes "obsolete" at full Darkmantle — [[d:rogue#1486733280710492383]]–[[d:rogue#1486733633497600111]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-25), corroborated later even with only Stormshroud+dungeon blues gear — [[d:rogue#1487753480671068181]]–[[d:rogue#1487753628830662778]], [[d:rogue#1489400035488829450]]. Niche narrows to solo play at 60 and PvP — [[d:rogue#1487781074191913032]], [[d:rogue#1487753723605286992]].
- **[Combat]** Pure Combat (Adrenaline Rush, "AR") stays competitive for short fights (until roughly Kara40/late Naxx gear); Combat Envenom overtakes for longer fights and as agility scaling ramps up — [[d:rogue#1487043929131188404]], [[d:rogue#1487046797976273057]], [[d:rogue#1487048604987424798]] (full Kara40 gear, 2-min fight: Combat Envenom beats AR).

- **[Assassination weapon progression]** No fast enough dagger exists for Assassination in any raid tier after BWL except Fang of Venoxis (ZG); little point staying pure Assassination past BWL — [[d:rogue#1487103836798718032]]–[[d:rogue#1487104448428904529]].
- **[Sub]** Widely agreed to "hold its own" in raid DPS via raid-wide buffs/debuffs even though personal damage trails Combat Envenom, and to be the most engaging/complex spec to play (heavy CD/buff management: IEA+SnD+TFB+Shadow+TotT) — [[d:rogue#1486854910220959866]], [[d:rogue#1486855170972319784]], [[d:rogue#1489242817254395997]]–[[d:rogue#1489244712832598247]] ("unironically the most engaging spec to play" — Junak'Salsa). PUG raid leaders frequently don't understand its value, sticking sub rogues in caster groups or complaining about missing Sunder while the sub is maintaining IEA — [[d:rogue#1489244564232601663]], [[d:rogue#1489245082040401981]], [[d:rogue#1489245232435429387]].
- **[Sub weapon comparison]** With comparable weapons Combat Envenom Daggers and Assassination sim very close; the practical choice is whether you'd rather deal with Backstab positioning or with multi-poison swapping — [[d:rogue#1489360695190487230]]. "Play them and find what works best for you" — [[d:rogue#1489360865361661963]].
- **[Server/phase timeline, Nordanaar]** T0.5 (Darkmantle) + Assassination stay best-in-slot roughly until AQ40; fewer rogues stuck with Assassination into Naxx after this patch than did pre-patch (it "was still very good in Naxx" before) — [[d:rogue#1489359840848384090]]–[[d:rogue#1489360663754182817]] (Rheinwg, 2026-04-03).

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[Combat/Envenom hybrid, "24/27/0"]** Core idea: ~24 pts Combat / ~27 pts Assassination / 0 Subtlety. Replaces Noxious Assault with Backstab (daggers) or Sinister Strike (swords); drops Rupture+TFB entirely for Envenom. Example links: pre-raid dagger build https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=AoYDIoDYB-FADoCAAoRCC- — [[d:rogue#1485940082325323889]]; Ambershire example https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=YoaAKoAAAI--DDCYbJSARK — [[d:rogue#1486005557495070720]]; another commonly-shared link https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=AoQDIgDQZA-FADoCAAoRCCA- — [[d:rogue#1487579859365531740]], [[d:rogue#1488606079238537380]]; a Sinister-Strike/sword variant https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/rogue?points=AoYCIoS-FADoCAAoRCCYB- — [[d:rogue#1489373493203042415]].
- **[Trade-off, precisely]** Combat Envenom trades a triple-nerfed Noxious Assault (and Seal of Fate) for: Blade Rush (scales hard with agility, reduces energy-tick time), Expertise, Close Quarters/Hack and Slash, 5 weapon skill (frees a gear slot), and ~5% crit or a 2nd HoJ-equivalent trinket slot on swords — [[d:rogue#1486734529640136795]], [[d:rogue#1486734687584911401]], [[d:rogue#1486883475448397895]], [[d:rogue#1489401067983601705]].
- **[Points allocation]** Always take 3/3 Ruthlessness ("carries rogue builds"); Hack and Slash for swords (better than Close Quarters, especially with Envenom — only Improved Backstab on daggers remedies the imbalance); Improved Backstab is wasted if using swords; 2/2 Murder is specifically for undead-heavy content like BWL, swap those 2 points to Lethality outside BWL; last few points debate for a swords/envenom build: 1 pt Improved Poisons + 2/3 Efficient Poisons beat Aggression, since poisons remain the biggest damage source, and beat Lethality in most scenarios — [[d:rogue#1488612444333932725]]–[[d:rogue#1488612998070272012]], [[d:rogue#1489273787026247803]], [[d:rogue#1486661479674675300]], [[d:rogue#1488497854090711041]].
- **[Sub]** Rheinwg's suggested tweak: move 1 pt from Lethality and Smoke Bomb into Honor Among Thieves row; Smoke Bomb widely considered weak — [[d:rogue#1486006168336990248]], [[d:rogue#1486006199584297120]]. Community wishlist for Sub buffs: "Honor should benefit from ranged attacks, have increased range, and get an ICD reduction; delete Smoke Bomb, give literally anything else" — [[d:rogue#1488577378929348832]] (Rheinwg, +2). Cloaked in Shadows (a stealth-defensive talent) considered "utter trash" because a fully-absorbed DoT tick still breaks stealth (by design); Blackjack gives noticeably more PvE damage reduction instead — [[d:rogue#1487796452175384578]]–[[d:rogue#1487797960921317536]].
- **[Evis-spam variant, contested]** A 3rd 24/27/0 variant spams 1-cp Eviscerate rather than building to 5. Rheinwg reports this as higher DPS than dumping to 5cp; Nandieb repeatedly could not reproduce Evis-spam beating Sinister Strike in the sim, even with claimed-BiS Tempest's Rage/Anasterian's weapons; sim author GumGuzz admits his own earlier guidance here wasn't rigorously tested against this specific comparison — [[d:rogue#1487431272040108072]], [[d:rogue#1485961057985105930]], [[d:rogue#1487421628186103958]]–[[d:rogue#1487440532748763228]] (2026-03-28), unresolved.
- **[Verdict / prediction]** Nandieb: devs nerfed the wrong talents — Envenom itself was the "really busted talent," not TFB/Noxious Assault; predicts Combat Envenom will end up highest DPS spec "start to finish" through Naxx/K40 — [[d:rogue#1487032004913528832]]–[[d:rogue#1487033942774124685]], [[d:rogue#1487473398182314066]]. Most raiders were still slow to adopt it out of comfort/risk-aversion since the sim is new, even after this was demonstrated — [[d:rogue#1487839635999817758]], [[d:rogue#1488590209531773179]]; trend improving by end of chunk: "more and more rogues on ambershire dropping tfb/rupture" — [[d:rogue#1489343867068092568]] (2026-04-03).

## Leveling
- **[General path]** Combat (levels 1-40, easiest/most straightforward) → respec deep Assassination/Noxious Assault (40-60) → Combat Dagger (Combat Envenom) at 60 for raiding — [[d:rogue#1486118643258495016]], [[d:rogue#1485984416881971353]], [[d:rogue#1486346499343388872]], [[d:rogue#1488525446214586398]], [[d:rogue#1488507954251698177]] (multiple independent confirmations across the chunk).

Counterpoint: don't start pure Combat while leveling — "almost no offensive talents until like 30" — [[d:rogue#1486780845422542908]] (Lightemgee).
- **[Hardcore]** Combat recommended for HC leveling for survivability (Riposte) and straightforward play — [[d:rogue#1487707972758016100]], [[d:rogue#1487708730261770362]].
- **[Weapons]** Level 26+: dual Cruel Barb daggers with Fiery weapon enchant (Combat tree) recommended for solo/leveling ease over min-max — [[d:rogue#1486365698631532606]], [[d:rogue#1486367289782571109]]. Low-level Combat-swords weapon options: "Trash" (from a quest killing a Princess, obtainable as low as level 45) and "Hanzo," both good pre-raid blue swords — [[d:rogue#1488321557083197642]]. - **[Hemorrhage]** Considered weak for leveling — its stacks often fall off before consumption, deals less per-energy than Sinister Strike, and enemies die too fast at low level to benefit; usable fine as a main attack with a slow MH per one dissenting view — [[d:rogue#1487511038546018477]], [[d:rogue#1487511818770448516]], [[d:rogue#1487522860925845655]], [[d:rogue#1487523703242887209]] (math: 50 base dmg → Hemo hits for 55, SS for 50+20).

Weapon-skill quest: bring an Ancient Warfare Text + 250g to Fray Island off the coast of Ratchet — [[d:rogue#1488300400711958528]] (+5 weapon skill, permanent, worth prioritizing over a temporary weapon like Teebu's Blazing Longsword, which should be sold instead) — [[d:rogue#1486869224336003112]].
- **[Races]** High Elf: agility racial + a 50-energy channel affected by haste (1.5s base, ~1.4s with haste) — considered BiS for Combat at endgame, "cool for leveling but not so much for endgame" per some — [[d:rogue#1485884295644975215]], [[d:rogue#1485888579199569930]], [[d:rogue#1485884523995463780]]. ## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Blade Flurry]** Baseline ability now (see changes). A slower MH hits harder with Sinister Strike, which "translates to Blade Flurry" cleave damage; a fast MH makes BF not worth pressing in AoE (DPS loss) because poisons don't hit the 2nd BF target — only autoattack/SS/Evis do — [[d:rogue#1487361136171941889]]–[[d:rogue#1487361632869810336]]. Buff-cap interaction bug: Blade Flurry's toggle can cancel other buffs to make room ("the anti blade flurry buffcap thing") — [[d:rogue#1487962782119759883]].
- **[Evis-spam / fast weapons]** A separate 24/27/0 variant that spams 1cp Eviscerate with fast weapons is one of "3 main variations of Combat Envenom" (swords/SS, daggers/BS, fast weapons/Evis) — switch based on weapon availability — [[d:rogue#1487048400565174302]]. Community testing (Purge) suggests the Darkmantle set-bonus proc is not a flat PPM but scales with attack speed (chance × weapon speed), so a slow MH + Evis-spam (near-1-per-second finisher casts) can push effective procs well above what Sinister Strike usage gets — [[d:rogue#1488225811658965163]]–[[d:rogue#1488228941922832485]], confirmed mechanically by GumGuzz: "no real PPM in game, it's flat chance or chance × weapon speed" — [[d:rogue#1488228623696527470]], [[d:rogue#1488234077050900620]].
- **[AoE general]** Uncapped multi-target hits are considered common/overtuned on Turtle (Hunter AoE specifically called out); Combat Dagger/Envenom's relevance is partly propped up by this — [[d:rogue#1487623665674879066]]–[[d:rogue#1487624525335232595]]. No dedicated rogue AoE tool like Fan of Knives exists/was discussed as missing — wishlist item, not implemented.

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit]** Single-weapon ability hit cap is 8% (changed from 9% about a year ago, per Turtle's custom formula); dual-wield (white/auto) hit requirement is 25% — [[d:rogue#1488563371321200962]]–[[d:rogue#1488563604210061413]] (Scruff). Weapon skill contributes to effective hit: roughly every 5 skill over 300 = 1% hit — [[d:rogue#1488564852015497308]]. "You don't get returns on hit after 25%" for dual-wield whites — [[d:rogue#1487223008228806878]]. Rogues effectively never "hit cap" in practice — always want more, especially on Ambershire — [[d:rogue#1488563211472212110]]–[[d:rogue#1488563276509085939]].
- **[Weapon skill / glancing]** Glancing blow reduction from weapon skill still applies and "still gives hit"; BetterCharacterStats addon shows a flat 2% glance-damage reduction per weapon-skill point over the soft cap; glance cap itself is 95% — [[d:rogue#1485984884471238666]], [[d:rogue#1487009363095846982]].
- **[Agility / Blade Rush energy scaling]** 1 agility = 0.0012s reduction to the energy-tick timer (linear): 500 agi ≈ -0.6s, 417 agi ≈ -0.5s, 650 agi ≈ -0.78s — [[d:rogue#1486913418140188802]], [[d:rogue#1488183773852864552]]. Agility considered undervalued by many players: "just getting Grace of Air buffs your dps by as much as a whole raid tier of gear upgrades" — [[d:rogue#1488184312334647346]]–[[d:rogue#1488184613011460238]] (contested nuance — sim also favors ZG enchant/proc gear over pure haste stacking in most cases tested — [[d:rogue#1488184048227586129]]).
- **[Weapon min/max mechanics]** Backstab/Ambush/Hemorrhage/Ghostly Strike scale off a weapon's base min/max damage window (shown pre-speed-normalization), not its "written"/DPS tooltip number — so a slower dagger often has a higher min/max value used by these abilities even if its DPS is lower — [[d:rogue#1487442546782376167]]–[[d:rogue#1487523703242887209]] (Ionnatra, well-received explanation).
- **[Spell power]** Poisons do NOT scale with spell power directly but CAN crit from spell-crit sources; rogues gain no spell crit from stacking Intellect. Benefit from a spell-crit trinket like Dreamshard Elixir is small: <1% dps typically, up to ~3% at best, "not the kind of thing you miss if you don't have it" — [[d:rogue#1487526224275312802]]–[[d:rogue#1487527614045356133]], [[d:rogue#1487588280605343905]]. Items confirmed to scale with SP by the sim author: Dragonbreath Chili (0.33:1), Stormfist proc (1:1), Dream's Herald DoT, Ripper DoT, all lifesteal items e.g. Hakkari's Manslayer (1:1) — [[d:rogue#1488213740242604193]]. BoED (a proc trinket) does NOT scale with spell power — [[d:rogue#1488218796568350924]], [[d:rogue#1488222654438576168]].
- **["Fortune" stat, new this patch]** Increases proc chance of item/trinket effects only — confirmed by community testing NOT to apply to poisons, set bonuses, or talents (a Paladin theorycrafter's tests cited) — [[d:rogue#1488216333324648634]]–[[d:rogue#1488222841852792903]], [[d:rogue#1488219853717508156]], [[d:rogue#1488220449425985748]]. Scaling isn't linear/strong (e.g., a 2% proc item at 100% Fortune → only ~4%); considered a "gimmick stat" for rogues since Fortune items usually cost a +hit slot — [[d:rogue#1488219855927775497]], [[d:rogue#1488219398090260491]].

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[Priority #1]** "Darkmantle darkmantle and darkmantle" — the single most important gearing priority for any rogue spec pre-BWL — [[d:rogue#1488543597283381299]]. Gearing is frontloaded: once you have Darkmantle, "you're cruising" — [[d:rogue#1487579760401055936]]. Recommended pre-BWL split: 6/8 Darkmantle + 2/8 T2, then a 3rd T2 piece ASAP — the T2 set bonus is much better than Stormshroud (which has no agi) — [[d:rogue#1488910311829278750]]. Farming order: you need all 8 pieces eventually, but the quests turn in multiple pieces at once (6 pieces gets the 4pc bonus), so head+chest (the best 2 pieces, worn longest) can be saved for last — [[d:rogue#1487944428915130461]]–[[d:rogue#1487944952712270000]]. Darkmantle T0.5 4pc bonus: chance to restore 35 energy on attacks — [[d:rogue#1488545152346755307]]. Quest turn-in structure: bracers first, then waist+gloves, then shoulders+feet+pants, then tunic+head last — [[d:rogue#1488231373759057931]].
- **[Combat Envenom daggers, BWL]** Weapon progression: (Death's Sting > Pugio >) Dragonfang Blade > Fang of Venoxis = Alcor's Sunrazor > Perdition Blade — [[d:rogue#1489268205263523840]]. Ripper is BiS OH for this build (beats everything up to and including Naxx/Kara40 items) unless another Ripper is already in the raid, and it also boosts the whole raid's damage via a debuff — [[d:rogue#1488077115793604770]]–[[d:rogue#1488077958664028160]], [[d:rogue#1489308517897867274]]. Prioritizing Dragonfang over CTS/Anasterian's helps rogues avoid competing with warriors for the same sword drops — [[d:rogue#1487105836428951603]]–[[d:rogue#1487106731531309057]].
- **[Combat Envenom swords, BWL]** Non-orc BiS ≈ Thunderfury/Anasterian's (outlier, not realistic for everyone) > CTS+Anasterian's (confirmed final BiS excluding TF) > Crystal Blossom/Tempest's Rage — [[d:rogue#1487076086411165897]], [[d:rogue#1487857330489135194]], [[d:rogue#1489366218145009967]]. Nefarian's Axe is only good in MH for Orc (only they reach 315 weapon skill); otherwise lower dps than CTS — [[d:rogue#1489385202622005339]]–[[d:rogue#1489385631166500864]]. "Swords win even against max rank Backstab" per Nandieb's final BWL comparison — [[d:rogue#1489509720464035870]]; caveat: highest Backstab rank isn't available until AQ20, so Ambershire (pre-AQ20) sees Backstab underperform its theoretical ceiling, and Sinister Strike/swords pull ahead "as early as MC tier gear levels" there — [[d:rogue#1489366541291229244]]–[[d:rogue#1489373236717158562]].
- **[Assassination BiS]** Fang of Venoxis (recently buffed, 1.3 speed, from ZG "more of a dungeon than a raid") is the best Assassination dagger — [[d:rogue#1486869810053775370]], [[d:rogue#1486694233233424466]]. Dagger DPS ranking for Assassination per Rhaena's testing: Dragonfang > Fang of Venoxis > Alcor's Sunrazor > Perdition Blade — [[d:rogue#1485961375586058282]]. Slow dagger MH = more Backstab/autohit damage; fast dagger MH = more poison damage — [[d:rogue#1485960901499551845]].
- **[Sub BiS]** New Partisan sword is BiS for Sub in BWL "by quite a big margin" — Deathbringer doesn't sim higher than CTS and suffers from lacking a good-fitting OH, but the new PvP sword beats both, has actual stats (unlike Deathbringer), and pairs well with Crystal/Anasterian's/the new Ony sword as OH — [[d:rogue#1487831312332095689]]–[[d:rogue#1487831861160968425]], [[d:rogue#1487857538194997330]], [[d:rogue#1489404776058716313]]–[[d:rogue#1489406128986001679]]. Nightmare Blade is great for Sub but heavily contested for transmog too — [[d:rogue#1489403270526009476]]–[[d:rogue#1489403565951680523]]. Ripper is NOT BiS for Sub — Sub wants an OH with weapon skill instead, and running Ripper on Sub is "griefing your own damage" (an Assassination/Combat Envenom rogue should carry it for the raid) — [[d:rogue#1489308882319839393]], [[d:rogue#1489408343129850029]]. AQ20 Sub 2-piece set bonus is very strong; "he should be using that forever pretty much" — [[d:rogue#1488615334016716881]].
- **[Cloaks]** Shrouded Mist sims better than Time-Lord's cloak in most builds; the crafted Veiled Shadows set is the best option for Nordanaar players specifically — [[d:rogue#1488051769400492073]]–[[d:rogue#1488060027007799366]], [[d:rogue#1489315015994511533]] (final recommendation: Shrouded Mist + Taut Dragonhide Boots for sword skill if needed).
- **[Trinkets]** Ranking for Combat Envenom pre-DFT: Flask (haste) with DM+HoJ passives > Vial/Royal Seal/Whip, since Flask pairs well with proc-chance passives — [[d:rogue#1487596668349321337]]–[[d:rogue#1487598400936480800]]. Once available: DFT + active AP trinket/badge for boss burst especially during Adrenaline Rush; new Onyxia trinket (windfury-like proc) is a big melee-group support pick, replacing Whip of Encouragement, though personally worse than a permanent-haste trinket — [[d:rogue#1487230426148765727]], [[d:rogue#1488214504155250811]]–[[d:rogue#1488214712960155891]]. New BWL trinket "Flask of Petrified Gold" is the only new item relevant to a fortune/proc-stacking build — [[d:rogue#1488123590263181322]]. New Onyxia side-boss trinket confirmed good for melee synergy with a Shaman in group — [[d:rogue#1488214504155250811]].
- **[World boss item]** A 20% attack-speed mace from Kazzak + Ripper offhand is very strong, "right behind CTS" — [[d:rogue#1487230561209548981]].
- **[Timbermaw Hold]** New fast 1.3-speed dagger drops from Peroth'arn, a summonable secret boss requiring one raid member with Timbermaw Hold exalted reputation; the summon item ("Elementium Lock") is a 50/50 drop alternative to the Warlock class-quest starter item from the regular TMH final boss — [[d:rogue#1488698513238786209]], [[d:rogue#1488931121499738112]], [[d:rogue#1488932327689158706]], [[d:rogue#1488961678119927858]]–[[d:rogue#1488962862708818110]], [[d:rogue#1488975038139990026]]. For pure Assassination, Crusader is preferred over Agi due to more value from haste; results are mixed/interchangeable per-case, should be simmed — [[d:rogue#1487846581943406764]]–[[d:rogue#1487847135809507430]]. For Blade Rush/24-27 builds, 15 agi on weapons preferred — [[d:rogue#1487845174624260187]].
- **[Old tier gear]** T1 (and most of T2 except gloves, which are still an optional good piece) is considered obsolete/vendor-worthy for rogues by late BWL/Timbermaw content — [[d:rogue#1488561297808425092]]–[[d:rogue#1488561540759290048]].

Combatant's (lower) PvP set doesn't grant its set bonus until upgraded to the next tier — [[d:rogue#1488428300236755014]].

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Poison profession]** Poisons are their own rogue-only profession/skill line, entirely separate from Alchemy — no synergy with an Alchemist alt — [[d:rogue#1487509381837754471]], [[d:rogue#1487509463308173352]].
- **[Type matching, core rule]** Instant Poison = generalist, works on everything. Dissolving/Dissolvent and Corrosive poisons are restricted to specific creature types (and were damage-nerfed on top) — must swap poisons per pull/boss based on enemy type — [[d:rogue#1487458269957062890]] (long explainer). Recommended PvE default (Rheinwg, late chunk): Deadly poison MH + Corrosive OH for bosses, Instant/Dissolvent for trash — [[d:rogue#1487771304861106207]]; for combat AR builds want Deadly V/IV on bosses that can't be hit by Corrosive; for Envenom builds want Instant or Dissolvent + Deadly OH — [[d:rogue#1487764176859431014]].
- **[Envenom + poison choice, contested]** Rheinwg's rule of thumb: Envenom shouldn't run with Deadly since "Deadly ticks every 3s, Instant procs off every swing" — recommends double Instant — [[d:rogue#1487595221956038837]]–[[d:rogue#1487595310070104204]]. But repeated sim/log testing (Raleias, Dare) found Instant MH + Deadly OH consistently outperforming double-Instant for Combat Dagger Envenom by a small but real margin (~5% in one quick dummy test: 35 dps difference) — [[d:rogue#1487590819732000788]], [[d:rogue#1487595859532189787]], [[d:rogue#1487611827947962549]]. In one UD(Undead)-heavy dummy scenario Dissolvent II + Deadly beat Instant + Deadly by ~10 dps — attributed by the group to Dissolvent (physical) apparently not being reduced by Nature Resistance the way Instant (nature) is in the sim, though this was never fully resolved — [[d:rogue#1487590819732000788]]–[[d:rogue#1487768025032687637]].
- **[Sub poisons]** All envenom-using specs run Instant or Dissolvent (not Deadly) as a rule, though Combat Envenom specifically showed some better results with Deadly on one hand in later testing — [[d:rogue#1487845019917484133]], [[d:rogue#1487845625578917918]]. For Sub in BWL, Deadly IV on MH actually simmed higher than double Instant in one test, with Crystal edging out Anasterian's as OH in that config — [[d:rogue#1487859474713542868]]–[[d:rogue#1487860645947904313]].
- **[Dungeon/raid-specific immunities]** AQ40 Twin Emperors: immune to ALL poisons — [[d:rogue#1485803638549905508]], [[d:rogue#1486679697776578620]], [[d:rogue#1486680147846365315]]. BWL Chromaggus: rotates which element he's weak/resistant to; Spellbinders (BWL trash): 100% immune to poison — [[d:rogue#1485998623828217956]]. Kara40 (K40): Worgens and Demons are no longer immune to poison as of an update to the guide — [[d:rogue#1487466805852373134]]. Emerald Sanctum (ES): mobs are resistant (not immune) to Instant; Dissolvent got worse there after the poison-restriction hotfix ("Dissolvent doesn't work anymore cause of RP or something") — [[d:rogue#1485996714866245742]]–[[d:rogue#1485998161238560969]]. Stormwind Vault (SMV): "some mobs are immune to all poisons" — [[d:rogue#1486679697776578620]]. MC (Molten Core): worst raid for poison QoL post-nerf — running Envenom there without 2 full weapon sets to swap causes charge shortages every pull; many players simply opt out of poison for MC entirely — [[d:rogue#1485839409915101225]], [[d:rogue#1489499679879073843]]–[[d:rogue#1489502688897929307]] ("MC sucks ass now, we just have to accept that. You don't have to swap poisons in BWL"). Spellbinders (multiple zones) are also immune to Nature/poison damage generally — [[d:rogue#1489504239150694620]].
- **[Learn/access bugs]** Multiple players report learning Deadly Poison V's book but still lacking access to Corrosive Poison II — recurring unresolved access bug — [[d:rogue#1487692787464933468]], [[d:rogue#1487992270614565028]], [[d:rogue#1488281744133198025]].
- **[Poison + gear interactions]** Ripper's on-hit "corrosive poison" proc is a separate spell from the rogue's own Corrosive poison application and is NOT subject to the same creature-type restriction — [[d:rogue#1486694475723051110]]. Poison application timing: applying poison while an Envenom buff is already up reportedly extends the buff's duration (single source, unconfirmed by others) — [[d:rogue#1487428265919385652]].
- **[Community feedback on the mechanic]** Repeated suggestion: make poison application <1s cast, and/or add a Shadow/Fire poison so RP-name concerns (poisoning a robot/rock) go away while regaining generalist coverage ("Haunting Poison," "Burning Poison") — [[d:rogue#1486703469661388800]], [[d:rogue#1487404660515012740]]. Devs' likely motivation per community consensus: nerf PvP "poison rogues" whose Dissolvent ignored armor; the side effect made PvE a "headache for all specs of rogue" — [[d:rogue#1488595601049387189]]–[[d:rogue#1488595792011853864]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Energy basics, new player]** You regenerate 20 energy every 2 seconds baseline; at low level most damage comes from autoattacks, not abilities — prioritize keeping SnD up over spending every point on abilities — [[d:rogue#1489263649020182698]]–[[d:rogue#1489264606131126283]].
- **[IEA vs Sunder — important raid-coordination bug]** Improved Expose Armor (and other similar rogue armor-shred effects like the "Crystal Yield" item) shares the same debuff slot as Sunder Armor and will overwrite it (and vice versa) — confirmed bug-reported (id=21433) but treated by many as by-design; only one rogue in the raid should ever apply it, and even a non-improved application can override an improved Sunder stack if misclicked — [[d:rogue#1488431348992708709]], [[d:rogue#1488620087261925406]]–[[d:rogue#1488623597642842334]], [[d:rogue#1488734745956319345]]–[[d:rogue#1488734932325892206]]. This causes real warrior/rogue tension over threat since Sunder feeds warrior threat — community pushback that warriors should lean on Shield Slam instead — [[d:rogue#1488735030476931093]]–[[d:rogue#1488738879828922549]].
- **[IEA vs no debuff]** Running IEA as the raid's only armor debuff (0 Sunders) was tested at roughly +150 dps to the raid vs 0 debuffs; vs a full 5x Sunder stack it's only about +35 dps net gain, i.e. clearly worse than 5 Sunders alone but a solid pickup when nobody else provides armor debuff — [[d:rogue#1488705124728700938]].
- **[Which spec should run IEA]** Combat Envenom is "the worst out of all rogue specs" to slot IEA into (costs Efficient Poisons, and testing in a live BWL run showed running out of poison charges every trash pack); if a raid wants a rogue to bring IEA support, deep Assassination/Noxious Assault is by far the best fit for it — [[d:rogue#1489309002704883892]]–[[d:rogue#1489309405274050632]], [[d:rogue#1489503843615244288]]–[[d:rogue#1489503869820993676]].
- **[Sub raid utility, detailed]** Mark for Death and Tricks of the Trade are party-wide buffs; Improved Expose Armor and Hemorrhage are raid-wide since they're target debuffs — [[d:rogue#1487763850211364895]]. Optimal group comps mentioned for maximizing Sub's group-wide value: Hunter + Shaman + 2 Warriors, or double-Shaman + Feral + Warrior (swap Feral for a 3.5-set Paladin if crit-capped) — [[d:rogue#1488193212425437266]], [[d:rogue#1488195621587386418]]. "The bigger and more melee-heavy the raid, the bigger Sub's value" — [[d:rogue#1487762897441652859]].
- **[Fist weapons in MH]** Inherently worse for Combat because Hack and Slash (sword talent) is much stronger than Close Quarters (fist talent), especially with Envenom — only Improved Backstab (dagger tree) offsets this kind of gap. A fist weapon "does the damage of a sword but can't do any dagger-related skills" — [[d:rogue#1488583400855044187]]–[[d:rogue#1488594741720518827]], [[d:rogue#1489273389653823599]]–[[d:rogue#1489273787026247803]]. AQ40 fist weapon "Remnants" only sims barely on par with BWL BiS while dagger equivalents (Pugio, Death's Sting) clearly beat it — [[d:rogue#1488587908821684276]].
- **[Solo/utility]** Evasion + Preparation (to reset Evasion's CD) + Evasion again is a common solo/short-fight defensive loop — [[d:rogue#1487176282713362462]]. Full Combat Envenom (25/27/0 area) is talent-point-starved and not a good fit for solo content needing IEA — [[d:rogue#1489504304112074907]].

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[BWL, Chromaggus]** Rotates which damage type he's weak/resistant to during the fight; Spellbinders (BWL trash) are 100% immune to poison — [[d:rogue#1485998623828217956]].

- **[Molten Core]** Elemental trash before humanoid bosses (and vice versa) forces poison swaps almost every pull; considered the worst raid post-patch for poison QoL, to the point some players skip poison there entirely — [[d:rogue#1485839409915101225]], [[d:rogue#1489499679879073843]]–[[d:rogue#1489502688897929307]].
- **[Kara40 ("Tower of Karazhan")]** Frequent creature-type swaps between packs (humanoid/elemental/mechanical/undead roughly every 2 packs) — advice: stick to one poison between bosses since you must swap per-boss anyway; Worgens and Demons were recently un-immunized to poison per the updated guide — [[d:rogue#1487466805852373134]].
- **[Naxxramas]** T2.5 (AQ40) chest and the AQ20 ring/cloak combo remain relevant items even into Naxx progression; combat gear picks in Naxx include Death's Sting, Kingsfall, Pugio; Assassination remained "very good" in Naxx pre-patch — [[d:rogue#1489360663754182817]].
- **[Timbermaw Hold]** Currently considered overtuned for its intended difficulty slot ("should be pre-Naxx content at best") — [[d:rogue#1488192586194751608]]. Secret boss Peroth'arn (requires 1 raid member at Timbermaw exalted to summon) drops a new fast 1.3 dagger and/or the rogue class-quest starter item; disarming the regular final TMH boss after phase 2 also has a related drop — [[d:rogue#1488698513238786209]]–[[d:rogue#1488962862708818110]], [[d:rogue#1489099077416259635]].
- **[Solo world content]** Timbermaw Hold's Quarry bosses can be soloed by a rogue with the Onyxia head buff ("Dragonslayer") + Enchanting; the last boss spawns 2 hard-hitting demons, the golem before it can be skipped — [[d:rogue#1487903576704684123]]. "Damien" (a rare farmable Ripper-dropping boss) can be soloed via stealth gear (Darkmantle boots, a BoE rare ring, Defias mask, Northwind-quest cape): sprint past the first creepers, break stealth at the rat-room threshold, vanish at the far side (sprinting cancels Vanish) — ~5 min once known — [[d:rogue#1488076723198365706]]. Hateforge Quarry (HFQ) and LBRS last bosses are soloable for a Fashion Coin currency + gold using a vamp/dodge gear setup, ~40-60g/hour — [[d:rogue#1489467591033618544]]–[[d:rogue#1489491681303859240]]. A new rare elite "Warbark" (39k HP, roots, ~500/hit) drops a strong cloak and is soloable with healing pots/bandages and Expose Armor even as Noxious Assassination — [[d:rogue#1489312722435702825]]–[[d:rogue#1489313066666426420]].

## PvP
- **[Poison]** Corrosive and Dissolving Poison do not work on players (Humanoid) anymore — only Instant Poison is usable, "basically boned except against clothies" — [[d:rogue#1488216301460783246]]–[[d:rogue#1488217700206641192]]. New PvP gear's embedded Nature Resistance may further reduce poison damage against geared opponents (unconfirmed, open question) — [[d:rogue#1487419470443057284]].
- **[Weapons]** New PvP daggers are 2.0 speed, bad for poison and Assassination playstyles generally — old R14 was better/hardest-hitting on Amber — [[d:rogue#1487373934478360667]]–[[d:rogue#1487374982807031958]]. New PvP swords (Partisan Longsword, 2.9 speed) sim close to CTS (2.6 speed) and are considered decent, easy-to-obtain BiS-adjacent options for Combat Envenom and especially Sub — [[d:rogue#1489240537562222593]], [[d:rogue#1487831312332095689]].
- **[Poison choice for PvP]** Deadly Poison breaks your own Blind and ramps slowly — bad for PvP. Run double Instant with a weapon swap to Crippling as needed — applies to any PvP spec, especially Assassination — [[d:rogue#1486568837498273956]]–[[d:rogue#1486569768377909391]]. Long-standing answer (before the type-removal change): Crippling, Mind-Numbing, and Wound poison — [[d:rogue#1487519804800761927]].
- **[Spec matchups]** Combat: good 1v1 but lacks control. Assassination: weak 1v1 post-nerf, loses to any caster. Sub: best control-based 1v1 spec via heavy Preparation/vanish/CC usage (2x every important CD, faster re-stealth), though it can still lose to hard-hitting classes without perfect play — [[d:rogue#1487234324658126890]], [[d:rogue#1489325713893888161]]–[[d:rogue#1489352451424518374]]. Sub reportedly doesn't play well with daggers; plays Hemorrhage/Ghostly Strike-based instead — [[d:rogue#1487257382294781983]], [[d:rogue#1487255813289545919]].
- **[Detailed Sub PvP talent debate]** Honor Among Thieves vs Vigor debated at length by Nelson: HaT is more reliable (procs off any crit, including white swings), Vigor only pays off once Envenom is already up; the extra Cheap Shot cost without Dirty Deeds makes Vigor's bonus energy less useful in practice; video example linked showing the HaT build vs a well-geared Paladin — [[d:rogue#1487820844712591674]], [[d:rogue#1487824835374485709]]–[[d:rogue#1487831077061001457]].
- **[Rank grind]** Rank 13→14 costs roughly 80k honor after a hotfix nerfed "RP gain" by 66%; hitting the 20k honor cap for R13 access took ~20+ hours of BGs for one player — [[d:rogue#1489318842634080476]], [[d:rogue#1489347995937013961]].
- **[Leveling PvP twink]** Julie's daggers from AH are the cheapest option for a leveling PvP dagger build — [[d:rogue#1487374521693503598]].

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Races]** High Elf: BiS for endgame Combat (agility racial + 50-energy channel affected by haste). Dwarf: good for leveling (Stoneform), praised for RP/aesthetics by some as "BiS rogue" regardless of min-max. Goblin: +weapon skill racial, useful in practice fairly often. Undead: popular RP choice, noted as feeling thematically "right" for rogue by some — [[d:rogue#1485884295644975215]], [[d:rogue#1487529672123351311]], [[d:rogue#1485884922861195265]], [[d:rogue#1487875389161472113]]. For Orc specifically: only race that can reach 315 axe skill, making Nefarian's Axe BiS-only-for-orc — [[d:rogue#1489385631166500864]].
- **[Professions]** Leatherworking + Skinning is a common default pairing, then switch to whatever's needed at 60 — [[d:rogue#1487516373512093821]]. Engineering considered "very overrated" for rogue by the sim author (though some players value the Sappers/toys); Herbalism/Alchemy have no poison synergy since Poisons is its own skill — [[d:rogue#1486342417589342240]]–[[d:rogue#1486342507959812339]], [[d:rogue#1487509381837754471]].
- **[Gold]** ZG/mining or herb farms are common rogue money-makers; making an alt for a different farm, or just playing the AH, are common alternatives — [[d:rogue#1487045768115257375]]–[[d:rogue#1487046797976273057]]. Fashion Coins farmable from soloing LBRS/HFQ last bosses (see Dungeons section) for ~40-60g/hour plus vendor/DE income — [[d:rogue#1489491333029822575]]–[[d:rogue#1489491681303859240]]. - **[Ravenholdt reputation]** Currently has little known benefit beyond a throwing dagger/sword reward and being a prerequisite NPC-gate for part of the new rogue weapon quest chain (must be Exalted to progress); new unused items have sat in the Ravenholdt vendor database for ~2 years, seen by the community as dev neglect — [[d:rogue#1488698816424181830]], [[d:rogue#1488905325431099552]]–[[d:rogue#1488919068038791459]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Sim tool]** CombatGum's "Heroic Dummy" selector sometimes silently reverts to level-60/3000-armor non-heroic dummy stats instead of the correct level-63/4211-armor heroic values — always re-click the Heroic Dummy button to confirm — [[d:rogue#1488171818606002428]], [[d:rogue#1488548270077907045]]–[[d:rogue#1488548449187266601]]. Sim doesn't model creature type/mob-type at all, so any item with a conditional (e.g., beast-only) AP bonus is incorrectly applied to every target — caused Finkle's Skinner and other beast-slaying items to look artificially strong versus Alcor's Sunrazor/Fang of Venoxis in multiple threads — [[d:rogue#1485989381893525525]]–[[d:rogue#1485991504550957186]], [[d:rogue#1488486637586485320]]–[[d:rogue#1488490630958026853]]. Several item proc-chance values were wrong in the sim and got hotfixed after community reports: Eskhandar's Left Claw (0.26%→2.6%), ZG fist weapons (5%→1%) — [[d:rogue#1486271523600728097]], [[d:rogue#1486683120974561444]].
- **[Deadly Poison bug]** Dealt roughly double intended damage for 2-3 days right after the 1.18.1 patch before being hotfixed — several players misattributed the subsequent correction as a fresh "nerf" — [[d:rogue#1487593806906785873]]–[[d:rogue#1487598045968465921]].
- **[Deadly Poison stacking]** Two rogues both applying Deadly Poison V+IV get 2 independent Deadly V debuffs but share a single Deadly IV debuff on the target — [[d:rogue#1486069806133284864]].
- **[IEA/Sunder overwrite]** IEA and the Crystal Yield proc item both occupy the same armor-debuff slot as Sunder Armor and will silently overwrite it (bug report id=21433) — [[d:rogue#1488431348992708709]], [[d:rogue#1488620087261925406]].
- **[Blade Flurry buff cap]** Toggling Blade Flurry can cancel other active buffs to make room under the buff cap ("the anti blade flurry buffcap thing") — [[d:rogue#1487962782119759883]].
- **[Rogue TMH quest chain]** Killing the MC boss tied to its own weekly quest before completing a step of the new rogue quest chain can bug out and prevent the rogue quest item from dropping, forcing a full week's wait — [[d:rogue#1489082025993306254]]–[[d:rogue#1489082112698224651]].
- **[Consumable stacking]** New food buff (2026-03) doesn't stack with some other buffs, e.g. a specific food item doesn't stack with Priest Fortitude (bug report id=21172) — [[d:rogue#1488753242379915375]]–[[d:rogue#1488757056247828611]].
- **[PvP set display]** Horde PvP set tooltips reportedly show incorrect names/set bonuses (e.g., showing the previous tier's bonuses, boots duplicated across tiers) — [[d:rogue#1488729017476579398]]–[[d:rogue#1488729379772039318]].
- **[PvP vendor]** Starter PvP boots are incorrectly rank-locked (bug report id=21473) — [[d:rogue#1489013898500898817]].
- **[Doite Auras]** Taste for Blood tracking intermittently fails to display since the patch, unrelated to buff cap; the buff icon sometimes vanishes with 3s still remaining — unresolved — [[d:rogue#1486134971759595663]], [[d:rogue#1486148007241060372]].
