## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Dissolvent poison reworked: pre-patch usable on every enemy, now only works on "specific enemy types" (mechanical), forcing weapon/poison swapping mid-fight — [[d:rogue#1490095420553302127]], [[d:rogue#1490974462323982366]] (Rheinwg/^_^, 2026-04-05; widely disliked, e.g. +8 reactions on complaint [[d:rogue#1491543257375576196]]).
- **[General]** Taste for Blood (TFB) reduced from 15% to 10% and no longer scales Dissolvent poison — [[d:rogue#1490012067032203435]] (Nelson, 2026-04-04). Also TFB changed from a 3-point talent to a 2-point talent — [[d:rogue#1490094810277613608]] (Rheinwg, 2026-04-05).
- **[General]** Instant poisons must be reapplied on boss/mob types now immune to Dissolvent (e.g. bosses), whereas pre-patch this wasn't needed — [[d:rogue#1490012411674235074]] (Nelson, 2026-04-04).
- **[PvP]** Ambush/Opportunity nerfed roughly 5-7.5% (Opportunity nerf), compounding into a larger effective nerf on crit-heavy hits; multiple players report PvP burst cut roughly in half post-patch (e.g. 5cp Eviscerate down from ~1800→900, Ambush ~2500→1400-2250, Backstab ~1000→600-700) — [[d:rogue#1491646097599500319]], [[d:rogue#1491760886493352137]], [[d:rogue#1491377218977398896]] (Ionnatra, 2026-04-09; disputed as overstated by Killinu [[d:rogue#1491733446861787237]] and Nandieb who says PvP effect of noxious assault build is "quite negligible" [[d:rogue#1490686616086384782]]).
- **[PvP]** Armor-penetration poisons were removed as a damage source, argued to be a real PvP nerf (contested — Ionnatra argues it matters much given nature resist gear + armor on plate; Rhaena counters resistance is rarely relevant) — [[d:rogue#1490371998457663518]], [[d:rogue#1490372877663342784]] (2026-04-05).
- **[General]** Buff-cap bug: exceeding the raid buff cap silently disables extra buffs including Blade Flurry uptime, fixable only by logging out or dying; a fix was reportedly shipped ("2 fixes in one week") — [[d:rogue#1491042040899964969]], [[d:rogue#1491178612358123601]], [[d:rogue#1491186577420980265]] (Rheinwg/Galundra, 2026-04-08).
- **[Bug]** Deadly Poison IV/V had a bug causing 4x damage and a shared-debuff bug where only one rogue's Deadly IV registers damage on a boss; devs fixed the 4x dmg bug quickly but the shared-debuff bug status unclear/still open per bug tracker link — [[d:rogue#1491019171277050019]], [[d:rogue#1491040799864127528]], [[d:rogue#1491775818198683728]] (Galundra/Drox/Kirsten, 2026-04-05/09; contested — Rhaena still sees only one debuff on bosses [[d:rogue#1491761435087470743]]).

- **[General]** Emerald Mongoose trinket previously wasn't granting spell crit as intended; status of the fix unclear/unconfirmed by testing — [[d:rogue#1491078036093927535]], [[d:rogue#1491079801631408240]] (Rheinwg, 2026-04-06).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit cap]** 8% hit needed for special abilities (reduced from 9% via 305 weapon skill), 25%+ needed to cap autoattack hit fully; using only one weapon avoids the dual-wield hit penalty — [[d:rogue#1489759475630870760]], [[d:rogue#1489759562918527161]], [[d:rogue#1489759609093881940]], [[d:rogue#1489789439357222943]], [[d:rogue#1489792392071745778]] (Rheinwg/Dezzyx, 2026-04-03). Note: the old vanilla 1% hit penalty on specials was removed in November 2024 per Rheinwg (era note, pre-chunk change) — [[d:rogue#1489792392071745778]].
- **[Weapon skill]** Each point of weapon skill = 0.2% hit (confirmed multiple times) — [[d:rogue#1490037879932715250]], [[d:rogue#1491171378580291835]] (Nuian/GumGuzz, 2026-04-04/08). 6 extra sword skill = 1.2% hit — [[d:rogue#1491177701577326593]] (Rheinwg, 2026-04-08). +Weapon skill quests (250g each) give a repeatable +5 to a specific weapon type — [[d:rogue#1491940064899366974]], [[d:rogue#1491940017923293375]] (marcusmadnjgidget/Lightemgee, 2026-04-10).
- **[Weapon speed math]** Rule of thumb: 0.1 speed ≈ 5 dps on mainhand white damage; offhand dps counts for half, so compensating for 0.1 less mainhand speed requires ~2.5 extra mainhand dps, but compensating for lost offhand speed requires double the dps difference (e.g. 5 dps needed on MH requires 10 dps on OH to compensate) — [[d:rogue#1489725082799706365]], [[d:rogue#1489726755337011370]], [[d:rogue#1489728255752933578]], [[d:rogue#1489728549404414244]], [[d:rogue#1489729093305106705]] (Kananaios/Rheinwg, 2026-04-04).
- **[Offhand value]** Because offhand physical damage is halved, an offhand upgrade is worth roughly double the raw stat value of the same upgrade on mainhand; mainhand weapon speed matters less than offhand speed since poison/Sinister Strike balance out roughly — [[d:rogue#1489512534875574352]], [[d:rogue#1489512801440497664]] (Nandieb, 2026-04-03).
- **[Combat Envenom, AP vs Agi]** For fight-length-dependent stat weighting: +AP enchants reportedly outperformed Agility on weapons/leg/head enchants in some tests, favoring AP more on ~60s fights vs Agi on ~120s fights (unconfirmed, needs more testing) — [[d:rogue#1491100440224338002]] (pepster, 2026-04-06).
- **[Assassination/Envenom]** At 800 agi (buffed) with daggers, DPS spikes noticeably — [[d:rogue#1490334716082130975]] (Zllig, 2026-04-05). Hit is valued more than Agility when hit is low (e.g., before exalted Signet of the Bronze Dragonflight); 7 agi vs 1% hit debated, with consensus favoring hit at low hit% — [[d:rogue#1490468402018386035]], [[d:rogue#1490469886537760878]], [[d:rogue#1490577228533076080]] (Makelovenot/Nandieb, 2026-04-06).
- **[PvP]** 25%+ hit is the autoattack cap referenced for PvP as well as PvE — see hit-cap bullet above. PvP hit cap for special/yellow abilities is ~5% (lower than PvE's 8%); combined with 1% from 305 weapon skill and 5% from talents, only ~19% total hit from gear is needed to fully hit-cap specials against PvP targets, though this is disputed as a low-priority/niche consideration by experienced PvP players — [[d:rogue#1494604646558203935]], [[d:rogue#1494841880574165134]] (Ionnatra quoting another player/Rheinwg, 2026-04-17; Rheinwg doubts the source "is even a rogue").
- **[Agility vs AP conversion, Combat]** Debated conversion ratio: initial claim of 1:3 agi:AP disputed as far too high; corrected consensus lands around 1.7:1 AP-per-agi baseline for rogues (1 agi ≈ 1.7 AP-equivalent value counting its crit contribution), with a separate estimate valuing combat's agi even higher (~2.0 AP-equivalent) once energy-regen value is included (1.1 raw AP + ~0.7 from crit + energy regen bonus) — [[d:rogue#1492511607216144474]], [[d:rogue#1492515753138589826]], [[d:rogue#1492519884259856555]], [[d:rogue#1492522664852586516]] (Scryu/Lightemgee/Oshy, 2026-04-11).
- **[Crit value]** Crit is worth roughly 25-30 AP per point for rogues generally, slightly more for Assassination due to Seal Fate and increased crit damage on combo builders — [[d:rogue#1492516625650417695]], [[d:rogue#1492518454962425966]], [[d:rogue#1492519193206194337]] (Lightemgee/Istrefi, 2026-04-11).
- **[Weapon skill cap]** 315 weapon skill caps glancing blows at the 95% minimum (max reduction); there is nothing wrong with exceeding 315 despite a rumor to the contrary ("buggy above 316") — [[d:rogue#1494042444231348224]], [[d:rogue#1494044987648901251]] (Leo/Sunache/Rheinwg, 2026-04-15/16; rumor explicitly debunked).
- **[Crit cap formula]** Crit cap = 100 - (dodge+glance, ~45 at 0-weapon-skill-deficit) - miss% (25 minus your hit%); roughly 100-45-7=48% as an example. Dodge from mobs is reduced ~0.1% per weapon skill point above their defense (base ~6.5% at a skill deficit); glancing blows are ~40% fixed; going over your crit cap only gives half value (abilities still benefit, autoattacks do not) — [[d:rogue#1493026212354854912]], [[d:rogue#1493026354592223405]], [[d:rogue#1493026633861566725]], [[d:rogue#1493030675358548078]], [[d:rogue#1492972325614649374]], [[d:rogue#1492972798887465002]] (Rheinwg/30/JohnnyRoten, 2026-04-12/13).
- **[Priority]** As long as you're not past the hit cap, prioritize crit over further hit; once at the crit cap, prioritize hit instead — [[d:rogue#1492968340845760683]] (Rheinwg, 2026-04-12).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Poison meta, current best]** Deadly + Corrosive is currently the best DoT poison combo; Deadly 4+5 if Corrosive unavailable, but Deadly IV is bugged (see 1.18.1 changes) — [[d:rogue#1489784080437477487]] (Rheinwg, 2026-04-03).

Instant/Dissolvent poisons are ahead of Deadly5+Deadly4 by ~5-10 dps in some cases per Purge's testing, contradicted by Vegybird who found D5+D4 doing more damage for envenom combat (30% bonus damage effect suspected) — [[d:rogue#1490347788964200468]], [[d:rogue#1491214375258951770]] (2026-04-05/08; open/contested, more testing planned).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Threat]** Rogues can pull aggro off tanks when popping cooldowns/trinkets at fight start; recommendation is to delay full burst until tank has built initial threat, use Feint if needed, and incorporate Vanish into the opening rotation to manage burst timing — [[d:rogue#1490051271405735976]], [[d:rogue#1490052405956575374]], [[d:rogue#1490058561286443039]] (Lightemgee/Nuian, 2026-04-04).
- **[Sub utility]** Mark for Death gives the whole party 30% of the rogue's AP as AP (or SP for casters/paladins) and 20% of AP as spellpower for 16 seconds — huge utility for melee-heavy parties — [[d:rogue#1490442703958446232]], [[d:rogue#1491442185474801804]] (Lightemgee/Yoridzen, 2026-04-06/08).
- **[Sub utility, PvE]** Blackjack (interrupt/CC utility) considered more raid-impactful than Cloak of Shadows in practice; Blinding Haze is used situationally during burst/heavy-healing phases, not on cooldown — [[d:rogue#1491220836781129949]], [[d:rogue#1491946353436266506]], [[d:rogue#1491946371144487013]] (Rheinwg/Lightemgee, 2026-04-09).
- **[Poison/DoT utility, PvE]** Improved Expose Armor at 5 stacks is the primary raid contribution for Sub rogues — described as "80% of why you're there."
- **[Blade Flurry buff-cap interaction]** Blade Flurry uptime affected by the buff-cap bug (see 1.18.1 changes); this hurt fights requiring toggling Blade Flurry on/off — [[d:rogue#1491187036193951976]] (Rheinwg, 2026-04-08).
- **[Vigor / energy regen]** Darkmantle set bonus energy regen enables more attacks/CP/finishers, called "extremely helpful" for Sub especially; ~70 extra energy per minute from Darkmantle proc uptime is "insanely good" — [[d:rogue#1490792214186557571]], [[d:rogue#1490842267131449436]] (Rheinwg, 2026-04-06/07).

- **[Rogue tank in raids, threat/spec comparison]** Assassination gives better sustained tank threat over longer fights, while Subtlety threat is "bursty" (front-loaded via Mark for Death + Shadow of Death, both scaling off AP/SP); a rogue tank has successfully off-tanked/backup-tanked bosses like Razuvious (Naxx, via priest mind-control break) — [[d:rogue#1494763317238173917]], [[d:rogue#1494763472741863425]], [[d:rogue#1494763599535538338]] (Scruff/Suade, 2026-04-17).
- **[Rogue solo/farm tank build, gear targets]** Target passive avoidance around 86.8% (Defenses-vs-Bosses tab in BetterCharacterStats) fully raid-buffed; key avoidance upgrades in priority: Windwalker Boots (22 agi, 1% dodge) or TMH boots (25 agi, 1% dodge) over Bloodfang boots; Deadwind Wraps over lesser cloaks; Dagger of Veiled Shadows over Broodwarden's Bulwarkblade (+0.376% avoidance); Fist of the Flamewalker as pre-TMH BiS offhand — [[d:rogue#1494743574284075150]], [[d:rogue#1494772610213609543]], [[d:rogue#1494773220858003467]], [[d:rogue#1494773458196762655]], [[d:rogue#1494773639109939352]], [[d:rogue#1494773824804360403]] (Suade/Scruff, 2026-04-17). - **[Rogue tank utility]** Mark for Death gives ~20% AP-based AP and ~18% AP-based SP to the party, which is valuable threat for a tank build (bigger raid numbers = more threat for the tank via Shadow of Death converting party damage into a burst); Totem of Flowing Water pairing is very strong for energy regen on a Sub tank — [[d:rogue#1494762339407495340]], [[d:rogue#1494762833622204597]], [[d:rogue#1494761736769900595]], [[d:rogue#1494761869666422816]] (Scruff, 2026-04-17).

Trick: fast weapons hitting the boss from the front generate parries which reset his swing timer (like HoJ/Hack'n'Slash), potentially useful to punish an undergeared or badly-positioned tank on this fight — [[d:rogue#1492571167993888798]], [[d:rogue#1492572627792035951]], [[d:rogue#1492572692719734794]] (Nandieb, 2026-04-11, joking framing but mechanically accurate).
- **[MC, Firemaw]** Firemaw's Flame Buffet mark stacks can be partially avoided: he's usually pulled from position A and tanked at B, giving ~2 stacks while moving — using the alchemy table for line-of-sight can block 1-2 marks, and (with Bigwigs) Vanishing right after his cast finishes can skip 1 more mark; a +fire-resistance cloak enchant and fire-resist juju can help; heavy FR gear investment is generally not needed for DPS rogues, and Runed Wardstone trinket is called out as sufficient / "all you need" instead of FR gear — [[d:rogue#1494126316293984319]], [[d:rogue#1494128268943560825]], [[d:rogue#1494128677066375260]], [[d:rogue#1494128855827615915]], [[d:rogue#1494129040221667500]], [[d:rogue#1494239558651351192]] (Rheinwg/Jay, 2026-04-16).
- **[Trinket usage, MC Firemaw pair]** Runed Wardstone and Venomous Totem trinkets don't share cooldown with each other or Badge of the Swarmguard; a suggested rotation for the Ebonroc/Flamegor pair is Runed Wardstone popped around 8 stacks on the first, ignore the first hide mechanic, then swap to Venomous Totem when he hides (drops stacks anyway) for the second boss — [[d:rogue#1493668354794192987]], [[d:rogue#1493670191127859313]], [[d:rogue#1494009823791022132]], [[d:rogue#1494009942552743936]] (Rheinwg/Nandieb, 2026-04-14/16).
- **[Vanish-trinket-swap mechanic]** After vanishing to swap an active trinket, there is a 30-second cooldown before you can activate a newly-equipped active trinket, so vanish-swaps generally need to happen well before you want to use the second trinket, not on demand — [[d:rogue#1494011328833261689]] (BDL, 2026-04-16).

## PvP
- **[Noxious Assault PvP build]** Detailed writeup: 18/31/2 or 20/31/0 (depending on whether you want Expose Armor), 5 points Improved Stealth in Sub tree, rest dumped into Assassination; playstyle is sneak up, Cheap Shot, Envenom, mash Noxious Assault until dead or you can Kidney Shot, then mash more; for big-HP targets (e.g. bear druids) use Cheap > Envenom > Slice; most targets die within ~6 seconds of stun + Envenom using 1.3-speed green daggers; use 1.3-1.5 speed weapons (faster is better, other stats matter less); Blade Rush is called "easily the best rogue talent" for its energy return; Thistle Tea can be added for burst; Deadly Throw is situationally useful with a thrown weapon equipped — [[d:rogue#1492304625821421692]] through [[d:rogue#1492310644173574175]] (Zaszz, 2026-04-10).
- **[PvP vs Shaman]** Recommended routine: Cheap Shot, spam Noxious Assault to 5cp (thistle tea if needed, though a Seal Fate proc often reaches 5cp within the stun), Kidney Shot, then Envenom + SnD — shamans are called relatively easy targets since they lack a defensive cooldown — [[d:rogue#1492644310234038463]], [[d:rogue#1492645046049046699]] (Nandieb, 2026-04-12; disputed by The J who says a 5-6k HP elemental shaman "won't be dead" from this alone — [[d:rogue#1492650162055807126]]). Alternate routine from Dobri: Cheap Shot > Noxious Assault > Gouge > Kidney Shot (thistle tea after) > Noxious x2 > Gouge again > Cold Blood + Eviscerate (or Blind, then reopen with Cheap Shot); always run Instant poison on both weapons in BGs, avoid Crippling Poison for the damage loss — [[d:rogue#1492634440856375468]], [[d:rogue#1492635476308394104]] (Dobri, 2026-04-11).
- **[Sub PvP]** Considered viable but plate classes are very hard to kill as Sub outside of extended 1v1s with multiple resets; Assassination/Noxious is called the "plate killer" spec while Sub is the "clothie/outplay killer" spec — [[d:rogue#1490693360753836172]], [[d:rogue#1490693734210207855]], [[d:rogue#1490694521275551874]] (Deleted User/Kyrsie, 2026-04-04). A Hemo/Envenom hybrid Sub build is reported to work reasonably in BGs — [[d:rogue#1490685562124898334]] (Nnorg, 2026-04-06).
- **[Dampening]** Confirmed to reduce PvP damage significantly across the board — described as roughly halving burst damage (see 1.18.1 changes section for specific before/after numbers); community consensus by mid-chunk is that Rogue PvP is in a weak spot post-patch ("not really playable right now" per The J) due to dampening compounding with the poison/Opportunity nerfs — [[d:rogue#1494436247249354773]], [[d:rogue#1494477495171285205]] (The J, 2026-04-17).
- **[PvP gear]** Agility PvP items were reported scarce (mostly Str+Stam PvP gear available), disadvantaging Rogues relative to Warriors in PvP gearing — [[d:rogue#1491338903439937608]] (Ionnatra, 2026-04-09; single source/opinion, not broadly confirmed by others in-chunk).
- **[Historical/era note]** Flourish previously had no cooldown and functioned purely as a combo finisher that increased parry chance (a talent then converted parries into combo points); it was later changed to an 8-minute cooldown ability — [[d:rogue#1497899132008796170]], [[d:rogue#1497937971955896330]] (Epickalen/xarop_pa_toss, 2026-04-26; reminiscence about a pre-chunk/earlier-patch mechanic, confirms an "era" change).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Rotation macro, Combat]** Backstab macro avoiding wasted Surprise Attack near the energy cap: `#showtooltip Backstab` / `/startattack` / `/cast [myrawpower:<=80]Surprise Attack` / `/cast [behind]Backstab;[nobehind]Sinister Strike` — [[d:rogue#1492170434672595226]] (Nuian, 2026-04-11).
- **[Sap macro]** `/run if GetBonusBarOffset() == 0 then CastSpellByName("Stealth") end` / `/run ClearTarget()` / `/console targetNearestDistance 10` / `/run TargetNearestEnemy();if UnitIsEnemy("player","target") and not UnitIsDead("target") then CastSpellByName("Sap") end` / `/cast Sap` — [[d:rogue#1493121675477127289]] (Yoridzen, 2026-04-13).
- **[DoiteAuras]** A "Vanilla WeakAuras Light" addon (github.com/Player-Doite/DoiteAuras) actively updated through the chunk: adds proc-sound alerts for Overpower, Revenge, Surprise Attack, Riposte, Arcane Surge (later also Lacerate), and later a feature to track buffs/debuffs beyond the vanilla client's visible icon cap — [[d:rogue#1490755197700542686]], [[d:rogue#1492473663306338384]], [[d:rogue#1495027658029793511]] (Doite, 2026-04-05/11/17).
- **[ProcWatch]** Used to test weapon/trinket proc rates: strip gear/talents, attack a dummy from behind (autoattack only), find the exact combat-log text the proc generates, then configure ProcWatch to track only that text — [[d:rogue#1491821086587879474]], [[d:rogue#1491821996764299286]], [[d:rogue#1491822174644736072]] (Eepyman, 2026-04-08).
- **[Poison-tracking addons]** EzPoisons / "Easy Poisons" tracks and helps reapply poisons on weapons; a related addon (EZpoison, by Weirdthing on GitHub) added Deadly Poison IV as a standalone trackable poison entry — [[d:rogue#1492876709177852065]], [[d:rogue#1492440608998952960]], [[d:rogue#1493240748735332555]] (Rheinwg/Weirdthing/Snek, 2026-04-06/11/13).
- **[UI addon]** PFUI recommended as the best overall Turtle WoW UI addon, with color-coded aggro indicators on health bars and highly customizable layout; Shaguplates is a known alternative with reported bugs — [[d:rogue#1492530701764132866]], [[d:rogue#1492530910502064239]] (Istrefi, 2026-04-11).
- **[BetterCharacterStats]** Crit-cap display (under a "melee vs bosses" tab) is present in some community-modified versions (e.g. one traced to a "Pepopo978" account) but missing from the default Turtle-Wiki-linked version — worth seeking out the alternate version if you want in-UI crit-cap tracking — [[d:rogue#1493014862278824016]], [[d:rogue#1493026721736294454]], [[d:rogue#1493112346871205988]] (Kuroshin/DrShockalu, 2026-04-13).
- **[Trinket swap]** TrinketMenu addon recommended for vanish-trinket-swap macros/UI — [[d:rogue#1493656084131483899]] (Rheinwg, 2026-04-16).
- **[Sim tool]** combatgum.app (GumGuzz's community sim) is the de facto standard theorycrafting tool for the channel throughout the chunk: supports gear/enchant/talent/consumable/rotation input, preset rotations (e.g. Sub with/without Expose Armor, Envenom Backstab), a "Use suggested talents" button, and per-boss simulation; it does not support importing a character directly from the Armory (manual entry only) — [[d:rogue#1491490892308152402]], [[d:rogue#1493498854287343617]], [[d:rogue#1494612157566947421]], [[d:rogue#1493327081109590026]] (nanolespacko/GumGuzz/oraz1/Quintis, throughout chunk).
- **[Auto-loot pickpocket]** Enable auto-loot in the launcher settings to automatically loot Pick Pocket results — [[d:rogue#1492479991701835972]] (shadefx, 2026-04-12).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Race]** Orc and Goblin suggested as the best min-max races for Sub Rogue — [[d:rogue#1494186065173680168]] (Rheinwg, 2026-04-16; brief/single-source recommendation, no detailed rationale given in-chunk).
- **[Gold farming]** ZG mining (no combat gear required) is repeatedly called the best/easiest public gold farm for rogues — see Dungeons & Raids section. Detailed ZG mining "lockout reset" trick: invite a farming alt from your main (as a raid), enter the instance and farm, then make the alt raid leader, log out on the main, reset all instances on the alt, uninvite the main, and relog the main to get a fresh instance ID — repeat for each farming pass — [[d:rogue#1493555367354044427]] (3x1, 2026-04-13).
- **[Gold farming, professions]** Naked mining in ZG is called the single best rogue gold-maker; Skinning is called probably the worst gold-making gathering profession ("all my skinner guildies are poor as fuck"); Herbalism is only worthwhile with an epic mount in an uncontested area — [[d:rogue#1494691498242080898]], [[d:rogue#1494691538389958868]], [[d:rogue#1494692871163478087]], [[d:rogue#1494692914943496446]] (Purge, 2026-04-17). Suggested dual-character profession loop: main runs Mining+Skinning while playing, sends mats to an alt with Leatherworking+Enchanting, alt crafts greens/blues from mats and disenchants them for profit — [[d:rogue#1494712430889275644]] (Mage, 2026-04-17). Leveling crafting professions on throwaway level-35 alts is recommended as a gold strategy — [[d:rogue#1492885567958941746]] (Yoridzen, 2026-04-12).
- **[Pickpocketing]** Pick Pocket has an innate ~5% chance to fail regardless of gear; items/enchants that grant "+1 level of stealth" effectively raise your stealth skill (e.g. to 305 at level 60) which helps positioning/avoiding detection but does not reduce the pickpocket fail chance; Sub spec (Vanish + Prep loop) is recommended for repeated pickpocketing runs, and LBRS is preferred over BRD since LBRS mobs drop the heavy junkbox from the start — [[d:rogue#1490195546630262856]] (ohcheese, 2026-04-05).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[UI/transmog]** Weapon transmog sheathing (back vs. hip) is reported broken repeatedly across patches — big weapons/glaives that should sheathe on the back often revert to hip/side placement, especially after relogging; described as happening "every patch" — [[d:rogue#1493782396389036062]], [[d:rogue#1493791787431628833]], [[d:rogue#1493793529518887053]], [[d:rogue#1493808746080763996]], [[d:rogue#1493840740319760464]], [[d:rogue#1494065130986668172]] (Erode/Rangtuk/ohcheese/Drox/Aimss/BDL, 2026-04-15/16).
- **[Combat mechanics]** Caster mobs appear to have avoidance (dodge, not parry, per correction) even while casting, which several players consider an unfixed longstanding bug/oddity — [[d:rogue#1495037666469871707]], [[d:rogue#1495053334204252241]], [[d:rogue#1495053927018922004]] (Stimx/The J, 2026-04-17; contested/unclear if intended).
- **[Sim tool bug]** GumGuzz's sim had a rotation-handling bug producing implausible results (e.g. 54.8 finishers in a 30-second fight) for a custom Sub rotation; traced to an issue with how Slice and Dice was being re-selected in the rotation logic — fixed by removing and re-adding SnD in the custom rotation, though the underlying cause in the sim was not fully identified — [[d:rogue#1493248872611315803]], [[d:rogue#1493249385004531824]], [[d:rogue#1493250708227751937]] (GumGuzz/Ionnatra, 2026-04-13).
- **[Talent calculator]** The external talent calculator (talents.turtlecraft.gg) was reported stuck/non-functional for some users at one point in the chunk — [[d:rogue#1494478679278096475]], [[d:rogue#1494478839663824926]] (Nelson/DesignerDave, 2026-04-17).
- See also 1.18.1 changes & their effect section for the buff-cap bug, Deadly Poison IV/V bugs, Mongoose+20agi-food non-stacking, Mark of Fordring neck sim bug, and Emerald Mongoose spell-crit bug; and Gear section for the Eskhandar's Right Claw minion-debuff (confirmed non-cumulative, not a bug but a frequently-misunderstood mechanic).
