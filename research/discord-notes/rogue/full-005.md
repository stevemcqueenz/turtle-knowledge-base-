## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Community reports removal of "Dust of Disappearance" (old threat-drop tool, replaced by Smoke Bomb) in a prior patch; players want it back or an AoE Smoke Bomb rework — [[d:rogue#1458937602806911257]], [[d:rogue#1460262727590350858]] (Ionnatra, 2026-01-09/12; contested design wish, not implemented).
- **[Assassination]** Poison scaling with spellpower/AP confirmed to exist (not shown in tooltip): Deadly and Corrosive poisons scale differently from Instant/Dissolvent — "8% for deadly and cor[rosive], 5% for instant and dissolvent" — [[d:rogue#1460304608583880714]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-12; single source, not officially documented, players had to discover it via testing).
- **[General]** No official nerf has been announced for the next patch; a claimed leaked screenshot mentioning nerfs is unconfirmed — [[d:rogue#1460323338483273789]] (Nuian, 2026-01-12; disputed rumor, "GM has said anything about nerfs afaik, it's all been user speculation").
- **[Combat]** Blade Flurry was reworked at some point: it now reduces the caster's own energy regen while active, and poisons do NOT get replicated onto the second target ("40% of your damage doesn't work"); best practice is to toggle it on only while 2+ enemies are up and off again once down to 1 — [[d:rogue#1462687905603649536]], [[d:rogue#1462688642765164789]] (Rheinwg/Heffey, 2026-01-19; net negative for energy-hungry Sub/Assa-adjacent builds, still a clear gain for Combat since autoattacks are its main damage source [[d:rogue#1462691808504189009]]). Its older version was "1.5 min CD for 20 seconds of 20% haste and +1 additional target" — [[d:rogue#1461479883044818946]], [[d:rogue#1461481895576731793]] (Epickalen/Treehide-Verrik, 2026-01-16).
- **[General]** Since a past "class changes" update, totems grant an aura instead of a temporary weapon enchant, so a Windfury shaman totem no longer competes with poison for the weapon-enchant slot — poison both weapons as normal even with Windfury in the group — [[d:rogue#1461058653653045258]] (Scruff, 2026-01-14).
- **[Sub, proposed/unreleased]** A community-drafted Subtlety rework (Nettlefield, posted to the official Turtle WoW forum with a custom talent calculator: https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=47&t=23584) proposing to remove Smoke Bomb/Blinding Haze and change Ghostly Strike's dodge component is NOT implemented and is contested — see Contested/open questions — [[d:rogue#1460888662181937224]] (2026-01-14).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Assassination]** "Assa is the best spec for early raiding, Combat daggers is the best around naxx, Sinister strike [combat swords] is under Combat daggers at the same lvl" — [[d:rogue#1459534443864657950]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-10; confirmed elsewhere: "Combat is good right now, is just overshadowed by assa on early raiding" [[d:rogue#1460276546383122483]]).
- **[Assassination]** Common assumption for switching Assassination → Combat daggers: around "Full AQ40 gear - Naxx" or roughly 500 agi, though "No-one knows for certain" — [[d:rogue#1459560181292859513]], [[d:rogue#1459559952308764672]] (Rheinwg/JohnnyRoten, 2026-01-09; disputed/unclear threshold).
- **[General]** Assa scaling vs other classes debated: "assa really does double damage of every other class" pre-raid (UBRS), but by MC it's no longer as dominant, and other specs scale better into raid tiers — [[d:rogue#1460302747781038285]] (Nandieb, 2026-01-12; Drox adds "Assa has strong start in lower raid tiers but kinda flatlines after that" [[d:rogue#1460335621238427842]]).
- **[Sub]** Sub with IEA (Improved Expose Armor)/Hemo support build "pretty often wanted for raiding guilds"; "Every raid group wants at least 1 of this" — [[d:rogue#1459146777042358375]], [[d:rogue#1459535202618708091]] (Ionnatra, 2026-01-09).
- **[Sub]** Diminishing returns on running 2 Sub support rogues in one raid: hemo stacks from multiple rogues override/consume each other faster, and only one Improved Expose Armor / Mark of Death is needed; a 2nd sub rogue adds ~30-40% more hemo uptime but no extra Improved Expose Armor value, called a poor trade vs a "proper dps" — [[d:rogue#1458854251492020380]], [[d:rogue#1458854705328296018]], [[d:rogue#1458855237057118376]] (Rheinwg/Dalak/Badknees, 2026-01-08; disputed — Badknees argues Mark for Death support doesn't diminish the same way).
- **[General]** Class-utility debate (andre vs Lightemgee): andre argues rogues have little unique raid utility beyond Kick (Kidney/Cheap Shot rarely used outside Naxx trash/AQ), while Lightemgee counters that rogue defensive CDs (short duration) let rogues survive/off-tank when healers are short, and poison variety (crippling, mind-numbing) has niche value — [[d:rogue#1458945099072405504]], [[d:rogue#1458945545966977145]], [[d:rogue#1458950047604674752]] (2026-01-08/09; unresolved disagreement, both sides argued at length).
- **[Rogue tank]** Viable through Naxx-level raid content as an off-tank/gimmick, but "threat will always be an issue" — [[d:rogue#1460292961165115455]] (Scruff, 2026-01-12; some players leveling dedicated rogue tanks per "meme build discord", unverified).
- **[Combat vs Assassination, endgame]** "Combat is BiS but only with BiS gear... [it] can't compete with Assassin's low gear requirements to make poisons go brr" until then; "Assassination doesn't [really] scale with gear, but with weapon speed, so pre-raid is largely just BiS [already]" — meaning Assassination plateaus early since fast 1.3-speed weapons are cheap, while Combat Daggers needs Naxx-tier gear/weapons to pull ahead. This applies specifically to Combat Daggers — other Combat variants (non-dagger) "are lagging behind a bit" — [[d:rogue#1463056606232187004]], [[d:rogue#1463058487889231934]], [[d:rogue#1463058244229398581]] (GumGuzz/Epickalen, 2026-01-20; widely agreed, not disputed).
- **[Spec-switch breakpoint]** Multiple independent estimates converge on AQ40 gear as the point to consider switching Assassination → Combat: "AQ40 is when you tend to have strong enough gear mixed with strong enough weapons"; agility-wise this is roughly 400-600 buffed agility, "where you start feeling the additional backstabs" (regen timer under ~1.5s) — [[d:rogue#1463315466566238283]], [[d:rogue#1463313733928620139]], [[d:rogue#1463313974610235499]] (Epickalen, 2026-01-20; "as it currently stands you will do more damage in assassination... combat's perfectly viable, assassination is busted" [[d:rogue#1463316082486935563]]).
- **[Log comparison, caveat]** Turtlogs "best boss per player" filtering for a K40 sample (Nordanaar) showed roughly: best Assassination ~1600 dps, best Combat ~2000 dps, best Sub ~1300 dps (some Sub rogues counted still ran IEA/Hemo/Mark support duties) — but logs are explicitly called an unreliable skill proxy (gear, comp, fight length, and even edited/faked logs all skew it) — [[d:rogue#1460754471541014760]], [[d:rogue#1460754909497528457]], [[d:rogue#1460755456120066098]] (Zaszz, 2026-01-14; single source, not verified).

## Leveling
- **[General]** No wrong way to level a rogue; both Assassination and Combat are fine while leveling — "50ish I was able to kill mobs 4 lvl higher, bringing me 700 rested xp for each kill" — [[d:rogue#1459647886101844184]] (rey.1982, 2026-01-11).
- **[Assassination]** Two cited power spikes: level 30 with Envenom (Rheinwg) and level 40/52 with Noxious Assault plus a poison that ignores elemental/undead immunity (Epickalen) — [[d:rogue#1459648734211346666]], [[d:rogue#1459648327795605648]] (2026-01-11; both cited, not mutually exclusive).
- **[Combat]** Leveling early as pure Combat is discouraged by some ("you don't really get any tools that help you kill things" until later gear/tree investment) unless frontstabbing, in which case you get dodge; the tree is mostly defensive early — [[d:rogue#1459648851018514653]], [[d:rogue#1459648936783384727]], [[d:rogue#1459649066849009765]] (Lightemgee/Rheinwg, 2026-01-11).
- **[Sub]** Early Sub considered "extremely strong" while leveling: Expose Armor is a surprising damage spike vs tough mobs, and combined with Serrated Blades gives pure damage; Ghostly Strike's baseline energy reduction helps sustain — [[d:rogue#1459649743482388593]], [[d:rogue#1459649856598704190]] (Lightemgee, 2026-01-11).
- **[Leveling weapons]** Levels 1-40ish: use Swords (bonus AP, common quest/dungeon drops) unless you get a good dagger from Stockades/BFD; Frontstab does not require a specific weapon type. Ambush/Backstab daggers only worthwhile from level ~48+ with a Magram dagger. Combat: once you have Bloody Mess 2/2 (~lvl 31), switch to swords, or go full Sinister Strike with swords from the start — [[d:rogue#1459071581426028651]], [[d:rogue#1459072106305290275]], [[d:rogue#1459888566267150347]], [[d:rogue#1459890242109837402]] (Ionnatra/Rheinwg, 2026-01-09/11).
- **[Assassination leveling build]** Ionnatra's 11/0/22 leveling rotation: open Cheap Shot → Sinister Strike → wait for energy (auto-attack) until Cheap Shot breaks → Ghostly Strike → Flourish → build CP back up to Eviscerate while farming. Poison choice while leveling: stacking (Deadly/Corrosive) on long fights (e.g., bears in Felwood, gorillas near Booty Bay); 2x Instant/Dissolvent on lower-HP/short fights — [[d:rogue#1459069408084496458]], [[d:rogue#1459069865053786154]] (2026-01-09).
- **[Leveling]** Rheinwg's lvl-40 sample spec (image, not transcribed) recommends moving flex points among Lethality/Blade Tactics/Throwing Weapon Spec/Improved Eviscerate/Murder, then prioritizing 5% hit from Combat, then filling Seal of Fate — [[d:rogue#1458915764118753404]], [[d:rogue#1458915959325720666]] (2026-01-08/09).
- **[Sub leveling]** Dagger vs Sword while leveling Sub: go Dagger only if relying on Ambush/Backstab; otherwise switch to Sword once you have Bloody Mess 2/2 — [[d:rogue#1459888566267150347]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-11).
- **[Assassination leveling weapons, detailed]** Main-hand: pre-level 30 use whatever hits hardest for Backstab/Sinister Strike; at level 30 prioritize a fast weapon with good damage; at level 40 prioritize speed above all. Off-hand: always aim for the fastest weapon with good dps, at every level. Weapon type doesn't matter for Assassination at any point — [[d:rogue#1461850430656090112]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-14). Concrete numbers at level 37 (364 AP): poison procs hit for ~107 damage; each 0.10 speed loss costs only ~3 dps from poison procs at that level (much smaller than the ~6-10 dps/0.1 figure seen at raid gear, since the loss scales with AP) — but a slower main-hand can gain ~17 dps from autoattacks to offset it; the Sacrificial Kris ("~3x the dps of a Poniard", ~4g on Nord) is called out as a good early weapon upgrade — [[d:rogue#1461866998521921710]], [[d:rogue#1461869820340473866]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-14; riku disputes the exact 3dps/0.1 figure as too low for it to be consistent with known BiS choices [[d:rogue#1461943218789941328]]).
- **[Leveling, poison immunity]** If a leveling target is immune to poison: (1) with Taste for Blood, most poison-immune enemies aren't bleed-immune, so apply Rupture for its buff then Eviscerate; (2) swap to a different weapon and use Backstab/Sinister Strike instead of Noxious Assault; (3) use sharpening stones. You don't strictly need a 1.4-speed weapon to make this work — [[d:rogue#1461858045247225907]], [[d:rogue#1461858747159810128]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-17).
- **[Leveling weapon breakpoints, concrete]** At level 16 Assassination, 2x 1.4-speed daggers are fine to enchant with a temporary Fiery Weapon enchant "until mid-30s"; after level 40 (Noxious Assault) you need ~1.3 speed weapons for poison procs — weapon quality barely matters at that point, you can even use the level-17 green Poniards. The poison-use quest unlocks at level 20-22. Permanent weapon enchants (like Fiery Weapon) let you still apply poisons; sharpening stones/other temporary buffs do not — [[d:rogue#1462881876204064913]], [[d:rogue#1462882219948376074]], [[d:rogue#1462882626162397377]], [[d:rogue#1462882718223437935]], [[d:rogue#1462882845654777987]], [[d:rogue#1462882938696765665]] (ArcaneLight/Antez, 2026-01-19).
- **[Leveling, frontstab defined]** "Frontstab" = using Backstab on a target that's facing you by standing on top of it and walking side to side, forcing the mob's rotation to desync and sometimes fail to turn fast enough, letting Backstab land from the front — [[d:rogue#1461513495349563457]] (Epickalen, 2026-01-16).
- **[Leveling, weapon path summary]** "Backstab only works with daggers... if you are willing to frontstab, that's better [more flexible]. From level 40 on, Noxious Assault with 2 fast weapons will be much better than either [Sinister Strike or Backstab]" — recommended path: frontstab (any weapon) until 40, then respec deep Assassination and pick up the 2 fastest weapons available regardless of weapon damage; race has no effect on this — [[d:rogue#1463202747015893110]], [[d:rogue#1463204214502326323]], [[d:rogue#1463203356695859311]] (Nandieb, 2026-01-20).
- **[Assassination leveling build, image]** A shared leveling→40 build (1cp-Eviscerate-spam into deep Assassination as soon as possible) is called strong: "out DPS other classes with white 1.3 speed weapons with pre-raid gear... sort of expecting a nerf... so enjoy it while you can"; last 3 points are flavor (Cold Blood, Sprint, Eviscerate, or Improved Expose Armor) — [[d:rogue#1462332938417995939]], [[d:rogue#1462333591634837687]] (GumGuzz, 2026-01-18).
- **[Assassination, weapon-restriction easing]** Only from level 52 onward can an Assassination/Mutilate-style leveler "do whatever content you want" without worrying about weapon-type restrictions — before that, going deep for the big single-target damage increase makes gearing/soloing more restrictive — [[d:rogue#1461006241626063186]], [[d:rogue#1461006339668181131]] (andre, 2026-01-14).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Assassination]** Core priority (Rheinwg): "Nox[ious Assault] > SnD > Envenom > get points for rupture > 5p rupture > maintain your 3 buffs" — 1 point each into SnD/Envenom at the start, more later depending on timing — [[d:rogue#1459518931697012943]], [[d:rogue#1459520104076280039]] (2026-01-09).
- **[Assassination]** If a boss/add is immune to Rupture, apply it anyway for its 15% physical damage buff, OR (per Matt Lender/Rheinwg) if not worth reapplying at 5cp uptime, just Eviscerate the freed-up points instead — [[d:rogue#1459520358188187862]], [[d:rogue#1459523065632395308]], [[d:rogue#1459520893385576469]] (2026-01-09).
- **[Assassination]** Opener variant: "hit -> SnD -> Envenom -> Cold Blood -> hit til 5p -> Rupture", or Cold Blood into Noxious Assault for a guaranteed 2 points to close the gap to 5cp faster — [[d:rogue#1459847734231175384]], [[d:rogue#1459849262002344273]], [[d:rogue#1459849382504431699]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-09).
- **[Assassination 31/20/0 rotation]** Noxious Assault → 1CP SnD → 1CP Envenom → 1CP Rupture → Noxious Assault x3 (+1 CP from Ruthlessness) → 4CP into Rupture → repeat — [[d:rogue#1459923031756509389]] (Mcx, 2026-01-09).
- **[Assassination 41/10/0 rotation]** Same buff setup but Seal of Fate procs let you reach 5cp faster, then weave Noxious Assault → Eviscerate while keeping SnD/Envenom/Rupture up; "overhead of timing the Eviscerate" makes it harder to play — [[d:rogue#1459923031756509389]] (Mcx, 2026-01-09).
- **[Combo points]** CP applied to a target (even a critter) persist basically forever, "even through death" and through restealth; players can pre-load 5cp on a critter before a pull (using Ruthlessness on SnD or opening with Cheap Shot) so the raid boss pull opens with a buffed finisher (e.g., Relentless Strikes-fueled Rupture/Envenom) — [[d:rogue#1458895659951325305]], [[d:rogue#1458895771049922671]], [[d:rogue#1458889363487461417]] (Lightemgee/Ionnatra, 2026-01-08).
- **[Sub]** Shadow of Death (finisher) BiS setup: 1) 5cp Rupture active, 2) AP trinkets active, 3) 5 stacks of Relentless Strikes, 4) Mark of Death applied. At 5cp, Shadow of Death = 250% AP + physical damage modifiers (at least 15% from Rupture) + 25% from Relentless Strikes. Aim to land it around 80% of a raid boss's HP window, then fast Vanish → Garrote → Preparation to reset CDs for another Shadow of Death — [[d:rogue#1459148633910935572]], [[d:rogue#1459149068898009241]], [[d:rogue#1459150180208218316]] (Ionnatra, 2026-01-09).
- **[Sub]** Rupture does NOT buff Mark of Death/Shadow of Death itself (debated but concluded "shadow is a weird spell" / separate from physical Rupture buff) — Relentless Strikes DOES apply to Shadow of Death (confirmed by testing, "Good to know that relentless works on it too") — [[d:rogue#1458856143601406043]], [[d:rogue#1458871687805403257]] (Rheinwg/Ionnatra, 2026-01-08).
- **[Sub]** In practice, boss fights can be too short to build a full Shadow of Death combo (5cp Rupture → 3cp Mark → 5cp Shadow) — a DireMaul test only produced a 2500-3000 Shadow of Death hit per ShaguDPS before the target died — [[d:rogue#1459297806475989062]] (rey.1982, 2026-01-10; open question, no clear fix given besides not always chasing the full combo — Rheinwg: "Don't bother getting it alongside rupture always... use it on cd" [[d:rogue#1459326499525099561]]).
- **[Assassination]** Damage composition: roughly 50-75% of Assassination damage comes from poison depending on gear/report (estimates ranged 50%, 55%, 75% across different speakers) — [[d:rogue#1458955377453437090]], [[d:rogue#1458955489487360103]], [[d:rogue#1458955704554750086]] (andre/Lightemgee, 2026-01-09; disputed exact %).
- **[Assassination]** Deadly Poison does not snapshot — it "updates every time you apply it" — [[d:rogue#1459895901718974629]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-11).
- **[Combat]** "Combat is just 2 buffs [Slice and Dice + Rupture], it's just super fast paced with how much energy gen you have"; rule of thumb: if you can maintain both with 20+ seconds of leeway, spend the overflow on Eviscerate (ideally during Adrenaline Rush), otherwise just keep the 2 buffs up as efficiently as possible — [[d:rogue#1462618589298168065]], [[d:rogue#1462637371756384276]] (Treehide/Verrik/Epickalen, 2026-01-16).
- **[Assassination]** Damage-source framing: Assassination's "big trio" is Dissolvent poison damage (scales off AP and weapon speed/PPM), Noxious Assault (pure AP, ignores weapon entirely), and autoattacks (the only piece affected by weapon dps/armor) — in that order on the meter for most players. This is why weapon speed (not weapon dps) is the dominant gearing lever for the spec — [[d:rogue#1461709639614730411]], [[d:rogue#1461719762190078187]] (Scruff, 2026-01-16).
- **[Assassination]** Poison application itself deals no damage — only the periodic ticks do — and reapplying a DoT poison does not reset/refresh its remaining tick timer (and there's no way to dodge/avoid this even if it did) — [[d:rogue#1462308266120839295]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-18).
- **[Assassination]** Dual Deadly Poison (both hands) does not stack as two separate debuffs — "both contribute to 1 debuff" — which is why Corrosive is used on the off-hand instead, to get a second, independently-stacking DoT — [[d:rogue#1460791100414365989]], [[d:rogue#1460791303813070979]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-13).
- **[Assassination]** Maximum poison-application chance achievable is ~101%: example breakdown (with Envenom, a trinket, Bloodfang 3-set, and a ring) is roughly Envenom 30% + trinket 30% + talents 10% + base poison chance 20% + Bloodfang 3-set 5% + ring 1% ≈ 96%, with some sources pushing it a bit higher. Base Deadly Poison proc chance is 30%. Poisons can only crit via spell crit, never physical crit — [[d:rogue#1462563753240432905]], [[d:rogue#1462563098870419632]], [[d:rogue#1462567724587942114]], [[d:rogue#1462562836973879307]] (Rheinwg/Ionnatra, 2026-01-19).
- **[Sub]** Pre-pull Slice and Dice trick: land a combo point on a nearby trash mob or critter (e.g. via Cheap Shot without killing it), then switch target to the boss and press Slice and Dice — this applies SnD (and can build Relentless Strikes stacks) before the pull even starts. Called out as likely an unintended mechanic — [[d:rogue#1463018619226292316]], [[d:rogue#1463020197161205820]], [[d:rogue#1463022442007101677]] (Heffey, 2026-01-20).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General]** Hit cap for melee is 25% (dual-wield); crit cap varies with hit ("maxxing out at like 60% if you reach hit cap, in BiS you don't"); no known haste cap — [[d:rogue#1460418292563972227]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2026-01-13).
- **[Assassination]** Weapon speed formula: subtract 0.0013 per 1% haste from base weapon speed (e.g., 1.3 speed dagger) to get actual attack speed — [[d:rogue#1459694676981452873]] (Epickalen, 2026-01-09; "formula is pinned" in channel).
- **[Combat]** Weapon skill value: "5 weapon skill means you hit 2% more, cuz 1% hit and 1% less dodge" against boss-level targets (boss dummy has base 7 dodge) — [[d:rogue#1460112458969649310]], [[d:rogue#1460113239655321742]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-11). Weapon skill (via Ouro/Primordial gloves, ~+3 skill) is considered the single highest DPS-increase stat until capped, beating 1% haste, because it reduces glancing blows — [[d:rogue#1459588523362484357]], [[d:rogue#1459588711120502981]] (Epickalen/Zaszz, 2026-01-09).
- **[Stat weights, rough community consensus]** Zaszz: ~15 AP = 1% crit = 1% hit = 1% haste = 5 agility (Combat) / 7 agility (non-Combat) — [[d:rogue#1459967035579699303]] (2026-01-11). Andre (more detailed): Combat — 25 AP ≈ 1% crit/hit/haste, hit prioritized until 10-15% hit; agility "technically 1.72 AP" but treated as ~2 AP due to Blade Rush. Assassination — haste ≈ 31-32 AP equivalent, melee crit less important (but Seal of Fate procs still valued), agility ~20 AP — [[d:rogue#1459973777566863585]] (2026-01-11; both are single-source rough estimates, no authoritative sim tool referenced). Nandieb notes items with unique procs (OBD, BOED) defy these traditional weights — [[d:rogue#1459974467664216197]].
- **[Assassination]** Losing 0.1 weapon speed at endgame costs roughly 6 DPS / ~81 AP-equivalent worth of poison uptime (andre's estimate) — [[d:rogue#1459974365864268011]] (2026-01-11).
- **[Assassination]** Weapon choice: want fast weapons ~1.30 speed (e.g., Julie's/Finkle's Skinner, Alcor's Sunrazor, Distracting Dagger); "Gut Ripper isn't good for assa" (too slow) — [[d:rogue#1459205341740011727]] (Farban, 2026-01-09).
- **[Assassination]** Double Crusader enchant (both weapons) is BiS for Assassination ("big Noxious Assault hits and poisons"); Agility on weapon enchant is for Combat instead — [[d:rogue#1459838903807643649]], [[d:rogue#1459841168404779133]] (Nandieb/Rheinwg, 2026-01-10).
- **[Sub]** Crusader on main-hand agreed; off-hand debated between Agility (standard) and double Crusader ("most flexible enchant" — Rheinwg uses double Crusader) — [[d:rogue#1459216610001682545]], [[d:rogue#1459221182820778015]] (2026-01-09).
- **[Assassination]** Crit importance debated: riku argues crit isn't very important for Assassination (self-heal/potions cover survivability, prefers stamina-leaning rings); Rheinwg disagrees — Noxious Assault, autos, and Eviscerate still benefit from crit, spell-crit trinkets (e.g. Bixxie's) less valuable by comparison — [[d:rogue#1459227133430726707]], [[d:rogue#1459227380903182526]] (2026-01-09; unresolved).
- **[Hit mechanics, detailed]** Full breakdown from Rheinwg: for ABILITIES — 5% hit never misses a same-level enemy, 8% hit never misses an enemy 3 levels above you (1% hit per enemy level above you); every point of weapon skill gives 0.2% hit that is NOT shown in the "hit" stat on the character sheet; enemy dodge/parry are unaffected by hit. For AUTOATTACKS — dual-wielding applies a hit penalty to BOTH weapons' autoattacks (main-hand can miss too); you need 25% hit (counting weapon skill) to fully offset it; hit also raises the autoattack crit cap — [[d:rogue#1461126609196023922]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-14; detailed, unchallenged explanation).
- **[Hit caps, clean summary]** "Hit cap is the same as other specs: 8% for abilities and 25% for autoattacks. Crit [cap] is 55%" — [[d:rogue#1462102234874511605]], [[d:rogue#1462102329745604608]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-17; matches the earlier 55-60%-crit-cap-at-hit-cap figures from 2026-01-13, e.g. crit cap ≈55% while hit-capped, due to bosses' base ~5% dodge chance).
- **[Weapon skill → hit conversion]** Every 5 points of weapon skill above 300 gives 1% hit; at 315 skill you need 8% hit from other sources to be ability-hit-capped, at 313 skill you'd need 10% (i.e. 6% "extra" beyond the 315-skill baseline) — [[d:rogue#1461124215611850982]], [[d:rogue#1461124290354085909]], [[d:rogue#1461124376530255873]], [[d:rogue#1461124439453208659]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-14). 315 weapon skill is the true hard breakpoint — testing 320 (5 over) showed no additional dps ("it definitely doesn't give you more dps") — [[d:rogue#1462796333801935021]], [[d:rogue#1462798464189730941]] (SciencE/Epickalen, 2026-01-19).
- **[Blade Rush / energy-tick scaling]** Blade Rush's energy-tick speedup scales continuously and proportionally with agility (not a threshold), reaching a 1-second tick (from the baseline 2s) around ~800 agility; 400 agility raid-buffed is called the "bare minimum for viability... when you can start to feel the energy tick difference," with the full effect needing much more. Raid-buffed agility with a double-shaman group can reach ~800-850 — [[d:rogue#1462569850768724213]], [[d:rogue#1462569911497920606]], [[d:rogue#1462133009389195521]], [[d:rogue#1462573676514771025]] (GumGuzz/Epickalen/Treehide-Verrik, 2026-01-17/19). Blade Rush's tick reduction is separately quoted as 0.0012s per agility point — [[d:rogue#1460097729928757258]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-10 — see also Talent builds note on not relying on it too early while leveling).
- **[Stat weights, refined]** Scryu, testing near-BiS Amber gear: crit ≈13 AP, hit and haste ≈20 AP each — [[d:rogue#1462523161433083914]] (2026-01-16). Epickalen's rough universal napkin figure is ~25 AP per 1% hit/crit/haste, but for Assassination specifically crit is worth less (closer to ~15 AP) since a large share of its damage (poison, Noxious Assault) can't crit physically — [[d:rogue#1462521857608650774]], [[d:rogue#1462521464266690884]], [[d:rogue#1462522671790035128]] (2026-01-18). Andre separately: hit/crit ≈25 AP, Haste (Assassination) ≈31 AP, and 40 AP of "flat" stats (e.g. from off-set gloves/boots/bracers) is only worth ~23 Agility-equivalent, making non-percentage items weak next to items granting +1% crit or +2% haste — [[d:rogue#1462593565233381631]] (2026-01-19). No one in the channel trusts a single sim tool for these numbers; comparisons are settled with manual 2-minute dummy tests (ShaguDPS + DPSMate screenshots at a fixed timestamp) rather than a formula — [[d:rogue#1461731755915608291]], [[d:rogue#1462050104180474011]], [[d:rogue#1462050196383858844]] (Rheinwg/Scryu, 2026-01-16/17).
- **[Weapon-speed-loss cost, gear-dependent]** At raid BiS gear the accepted rough figure is ~6-10 dps lost per 0.1 speed (estimates ranged 6, then a live dummy test suggested it "should be 10dps per 0.1" — no exact consensus number, everyone agrees it's "more than 6" at endgame) — [[d:rogue#1461712872068612106]], [[d:rogue#1461754553325195324]], [[d:rogue#1461760951513645238]] (Scryu/Rheinwg/Epickalen, 2026-01-16). This scales with AP/gear level — at level 37 with 364 AP the same loss was only ~3 dps — [[d:rogue#1461866998521921710]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-14).
- **[Assassination]** Rogue spell crit (the only way poisons crit) does NOT scale from Intellect on Turtle WoW, confirmed via the BetterCharacterStats addon — [[d:rogue#1462441882771787961]], [[d:rogue#1462442791903953132]] (Nandieb/casey, 2026-01-18).
- **[Haste vs hit, contested]** Haste is multiplicative with existing haste sources (e.g. gear haste stacks with Slice and Dice's 30% haste as 1.2×1.3, not 1.2+0.3), so its marginal value increases the more you already have, unlike hit's flatter marginal value — worked through with real swing-timer math in-channel — but several players (Nandieb, Matt Lender) remained unconvinced this makes haste "strictly" better than hit given you need hit for haste to matter at all; no final resolution — [[d:rogue#1463266940981084313]], [[d:rogue#1463274930589274303]], [[d:rogue#1463281308988997738]], [[d:rogue#1463283293972594698]], [[d:rogue#1463318334551625769]] (2026-01-20/21). Rule-of-thumb hit targets given by Epickalen: ~8% stops missing Sinister Strike/Backstab/Noxious Assault; ~10-15% stops losing a lot of damage to missed autoattacks; ~20-25% pushes you toward the ~50-55% effective crit cap — [[d:rogue#1463330366692528251]] (2026-01-20).

- **[Assassination]** Dissolvent (Instant) poison on both hands is standard, slightly less raw poison damage than mixed loadouts but synergizes with Taste for Blood and avoids needing to swap poisons for immune targets — [[d:rogue#1458877757374206115]], [[d:rogue#1458877883719356417]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-08).
- **[Assassination]** Corrosive (stacking) poison deals damage every ~3 seconds and needs stacks to ramp, while Dissolvent procs on every hit and "outscales corrosive" quickly at high proc rates — [[d:rogue#1458881838826258472]], [[d:rogue#1458881989158371533]] (Rheinwg/Badknees, 2026-01-08).
- **[Assassination]** All poison types can be resisted (not just Corrosive), subject to spell-hit; poison procs need roughly 8%+ hit to avoid excessive resists, and community consensus is "the 8% mark is strictly necessary, anything short of 12-14% just has a lot more misses" — [[d:rogue#1459161492976042094]], [[d:rogue#1460107144035696710]], [[d:rogue#1459860665408684148]] (Ionnatra/Epickalen/Rheinwg, 2026-01-09/12).
- **[Assassination poison scaling]** Deadly and Corrosive poison scale from spellpower/AP at 8%; Instant and Dissolvent scale at 5% (previously undocumented in tooltips, discovered via testing) — [[d:rogue#1460304608583880714]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-12). Confirmed more precisely: "all damaging rogue poisons scale from AP on Turtle. Instant/Dissolvent is 5%, while Deadly/Corrosive is 8% over the full duration of the poison per stack"; Agitating Poison is also ~5% — [[d:rogue#1462220214295859220]], [[d:rogue#1462220646422679704]] (Lightemgee, 2026-01-18).
- **[Combat/Sub poison choice]** Combat uses Deadly+Corrosive on bosses and Dissolvent on trash — periodic poisons tested and confirmed "way better" than Dissolvent for two Combat rogues on the same fight, since Combat's lower proc rate makes guaranteed DoT ticks more valuable than relying on procs — [[d:rogue#1462224070425706561]], [[d:rogue#1462224641320943639]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-18). Sub prefers Corrosive (2+1) over Dissolvent, since Sub doesn't run Envenom and Corrosive's higher proc chance outweighs Dissolvent's per-hit damage; Sub's damage is mostly autoattack, Hemorrhage, and Shadow of Death, with poison only a small slice — [[d:rogue#1460748610848297042]], [[d:rogue#1460748600991547729]], [[d:rogue#1460749306876399616]] (Rheinwg/Zaszz, 2026-01-14).
- **[OBD/BOED proc triggers]** Both are proc'd by poisons AND by "spellstrike" items (e.g. Flamestring bow, Incendosaur set pieces) — [[d:rogue#1461090568825344011]] (GumGuzz, 2026-01-15). OBD has a 1.5s internal cooldown, so only 1-2 spellstrike items add meaningful extra procs beyond that; it still procs fine from poisons alone with zero spellstrike items, and most Ambershire players run it that way to limit self-damage/healer strain — [[d:rogue#1461302512823832719]], [[d:rogue#1461302625835290726]], [[d:rogue#1461304801324306560]] (Nuian, 2026-01-14).
- **[General]** Since a "class changes" update, totems grant an aura rather than a temporary weapon enchant, so Windfury no longer competes with poison for the weapon-enchant slot — apply poisons to both weapons as normal even with a Windfury shaman in the group — [[d:rogue#1461058653653045258]], [[d:rogue#1462717797502029875]] (Scruff/Nuian, 2026-01-14/19).

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[Assassination/OBD-BOED spellstrike]** SP consume stack: Flask (of Supreme Power) + Elixir of Greater Firepower + Dreamshard elixir + Dreamtonic + Greater Arcane Elixir; watch out for the raid buff cap when stacking all of these plus normal raid buffs — one player got buff-capped mid-fight and had to manually unclick buffs to cast SnD/Envenom — [[d:rogue#1459511720065106011]], [[d:rogue#1460042197796913284]] (Rheinwg/Matt Lender, 2026-01-09/12).
- **[Consumable replacements]** Dreamwater replaces Winterfall Firewater and Dreamtonic (doesn't stack with Juju Might either); Arcane Giant replaces Greater Arcane Elixir and Elixir of Giants; Emerald Mongoose replaces Dreamshard and Elixir of the Mongoose — but Emerald Mongoose reportedly isn't functioning correctly (spell-stat page doesn't update with it, unlike Dreamshard) — [[d:rogue#1460050501856002259]], [[d:rogue#1460052377896878141]] (Nuian/Rheinwg, 2026-01-11; contested/possible bug).
- **[Assassination]** BOED (Blade of Eternal Darkness) does not benefit from SP consumes since it doesn't scale with spellpower; OBD (Ornate Bloodstone Dagger) does scale and benefits heavily from SP consumes/spellstrike gear — [[d:rogue#1459511898654380230]], [[d:rogue#1459514652546433024]] (Rheinwg/BDL, 2026-01-09).
- **[Assassination]** Thistle Tea used in the opener lets you Cold Blood into Eviscerate after Rupture without losing tempo — [[d:rogue#1459520201669345502]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-09).
- **[Vamp/solo]** DMF (Darkmoon Faire) Heroism trinket adds meaningfully to a vampirism-based solo-farming setup — [[d:rogue#1459911808902107363]] (Nuian, 2026-01-09).
- **[OBD self-damage]** Scales with spellpower (the same coefficient hitting the caster as it does the target, ~40% SP scaling per one estimate); OBD is a poor choice for solo farming because of this — you can hit yourself for ~400 with SP consumes stacked and need to rely on guild healers to keep you topped — [[d:rogue#1462898040636575775]], [[d:rogue#1461104610810007555]], [[d:rogue#1462574598124994804]], [[d:rogue#1462574810952499315]] (Kolima/Lightemgee/GumGuzz, 2026-01-14/19). Explicitly flagged as risky on Hardcore characters — [[d:rogue#1463250018617004074]] (Beldrin, 2026-01-20).
- **[Spellstrike gearing extras]** Cenarion ring (130 SP, 100% uptime with spellstrike gear, but requires Cenarion Circle exalted rep — not realistic on Ambershire), a Karazhan Crypt trinket (1% haste/1% spellcrit/15 SP on swap), and a tailoring-crafted SP helm (2% haste + 30-40 SP) are cited spellstrike-build pieces; rule of thumb given: "only go for SP items if you can get spellcrit or haste on them" too, not raw SP alone — [[d:rogue#1462488638309798133]], [[d:rogue#1462488939163291712]], [[d:rogue#1462489071300513954]], [[d:rogue#1462489186346209478]] (Purge, 2026-01-18).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[General]** Rogues can hold threat/off-tank temporarily by clicking off Salvation; can generate meaningful TPS via Sinister Strike-dagger builds (~1500 TPS cited without Noxious Assault or poison) or via spammed Noxious Assault + Thistle Tea, but energy-tick limits make sustained on-demand threat hard, especially against enemies immune to nature (blocks Agitating-style off-tank tools) — [[d:rogue#1458946908495151145]], [[d:rogue#1458947320799432859]], [[d:rogue#1458947433030750303]], [[d:rogue#1458947661595152405]] (Lightemgee/Epickalen/andre, 2026-01-09).
- **[Sub]** IEA (Improved Expose Armor) tracking is unreliable with common addons: Mpowa is "super bad" at tracking boss debuffs and has a known bug where refreshing the debuff within the last 30s causes its timer to display incorrectly (shows "2 seconds left" even though the buff persists) — [[d:rogue#1460122471092064427]], [[d:rogue#1460124358071816332]] (Matt Lender, 2026-01-12; open request for a better tracking addon).
- **[Sub]** IEA/Improved Expose Armor is often not worth applying on trash or in most dungeons — Rheinwg: "I dont IEA on dungeons, or during most of trash in raids either", though the talent point is worth keeping for raids since there's nothing better to spend it on — [[d:rogue#1459199748748607488]], [[d:rogue#1459199807762468977]] (2026-01-09).
- **[Sub]** Rupture's armor-penetration/damage buff value is "not particularly big" and its extra armor-pen is often negated — [[d:rogue#1459185056869515501]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-09).
- **[Trinket rotation, TrinketMenu addon]** Rule of thumb: use active/buff trinkets only when the target will live longer than the buff's window, otherwise use passives. Trash: default to passive trinkets since most trash dies too fast, but pop an active on "bigger" adds (e.g. Anubisath adds in AQ40) — good active options are Earthstrike, Molten Emberstone, Venomous Totem (Assassination), and especially Badge of Swarmguard, since its buff lasts far longer than other actives and is the best choice for back-to-back trash pulls (turn off automation on that slot until it expires, then swap back to passive). Bosses: pop your best active/buff trinket (Badge/Slayer's Crest) then Vanish-swap into a passive (DFT/Mark of Champions) or Kiss(/JOM); Shieldrender Talisman may functionally replace Badge and other passives at once since it also procs constantly and gives passive AP + an armor-pen proc. A player's trinket-swap diligence on trash (not just bosses) correlates directly with their position on the damage meter — [[d:rogue#1463141330527059968]], [[d:rogue#1463132211271438388]], [[d:rogue#1463160560827760695]], [[d:rogue#1463161333397590130]], [[d:rogue#1463161876488781884]], [[d:rogue#1463208575848681532]], [[d:rogue#1463208844145594421]] (Farban/Badknees/-Z?-/Scruff/Treehide-Verrik, 2026-01-20).
- **[Assassination]** Vigor (an energy-related talent) does NOT get triggered by "poison-categorized" procs such as Vial of Corrupted/Patent Venoms or The Ripper's poison proc — confirmed non-functional, both currently and when tested independently roughly a year earlier — [[d:rogue#1462771991949086939]], [[d:rogue#1462772877458936046]] (Nettlefield/Rheinwg, 2026-01-19).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Rogue tank]** Some players level dedicated "rogue tank" builds; viable as off-tank through Naxx-tier raid content, but threat generation "will always be an issue" — [[d:rogue#1460276985656774748]], [[d:rogue#1460292961165115455]] (zvarat/Scruff, 2026-01-12; single source, niche/meme build).
- **[Rogue tank, spec split]** Rogue tanking is mostly done via Assassination (which has threat tools via Agitating Poison, a Nature-damage effect — so it struggles against nature-immune bosses) or Combat (avoidance-based); Sub tanking is niche, only used on specific fights like Four Horsemen, and most players don't bother — [[d:rogue#1460907162426671238]] (Nettlefield, 2026-01-14).
- **[Rogue tank, threat]** Rogues can sustain strong TPS via Noxious Assault + Corrosive poison, or Windfury + Corrosive, and can hold aggro on a boss "until it's cut in half" fairly well, but can't easily "tank-swap back" mid-fight — the core problems are threat generation against nature-immune targets and being vulnerable to being one-shot, not damage mitigation per se — [[d:rogue#1462134848415338742]], [[d:rogue#1462135016694878333]], [[d:rogue#1462135334384173170]], [[d:rogue#1462134280795983872]] (Epickalen, 2026-01-17).
- **[Rogue tank, avoidance build]** Players have built dedicated rogue-tank gear sets reaching 95-97% unbuffed avoidance ("86.8[%] is all that's needed" for the goal of near-total physical immunity for a stretch); current rotation for staying near-unhittable chains Ghostly Strike (15s CD, dodge buff) with Flourish (40s CD) and Evasion, leaving a hittable gap once cooldowns run out — [[d:rogue#1462122391093121096]], [[d:rogue#1462131949588975920]], [[d:rogue#1462132417132236880]], [[d:rogue#1462133033439203561]], [[d:rogue#1460990265081725090]], [[d:rogue#1460991087253389342]] (GumGuzz/Scruff/Nettlefield, 2026-01-14/17). An experienced rogue tank (Scruff, who tanks through Naxx and BWL's Chromaggus) opposed a proposed Sub-rework change reducing Flourish's CD to compensate for a Ghostly Strike nerf, arguing it introduces a lethal one-shot RNG window in 40-man content ("spec dead") — [[d:rogue#1460900782181454099]], [[d:rogue#1460901537969602695]], [[d:rogue#1460993026988970044]] (2026-01-14).
- **[Rogue tank]** Badge of Swarmguard is an "insane trinket" for extra armor-shred DPS when a raid has no dedicated IEA rogue; trash mob armor in Naxx/AQ40/K40 is roughly 5.5k, and even after 5x Sunder Armor + Corrosive Poison + Faerie Fire it's only reduced to ~3.3k, so extra armor-shred sources remain valuable on big/tanky adds — [[d:rogue#1461758405382504499]], [[d:rogue#1461758770127306853]], [[d:rogue#1461758873588338839]], [[d:rogue#1461758941439594648]] (Farban/Epickalen/Treehide-Verrik, 2026-01-16).

## PvP
- **[General]** Resilience gear generally not worth it outside dedicated PvP builds — "you lose too many stats, many of the pieces with resilience are mid" — [[d:rogue#1458858931781767320]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-08).
- **[Sub]** PvP execute sequence example: Garrote → Rupture → Ghostly → Flourish → Disarm → Evasion → Sinister Strike to 5cp → Kidney Shot → Sinister Strike ×2 → Rupture → Vanish out; if target lacks Improved Vanish, bleed/poison ticks continue to punish them while you reposition from Vanish for another Cheap Shot opener; can repeat once more via Preparation — [[d:rogue#1459141070452555788]] (Ionnatra, 2026-01-09).
- **[PvP macro]** Off-hand weapon swap macro for crippling-poison procs (swap to a Poniard-type weapon, then swap back to Distracting Dagger once the crippling proc lands): `/run for i=0,4 do for j=1,GetContainerNumSlots(i) do local l=GetContainerItemLink(i,j) if l and string.find(l,"Poniard") then PickupContainerItem(i,j) EquipCursorItem(17) break end end end` — [[d:rogue#1459161884216393904]] (Istrefi, 2026-01-09).
- **[PvP]** Assassination poison damage ignores armor and is considered "very strong" in PvP, but balancing damage against utility talents (Dirty Tricks/Preparation/Blackjack) and an energy-regen talent is an open struggle even for a near-BiS (K40) player — [[d:rogue#1459727289863508010]] (Silentkills, 2026-01-11; question left unanswered in this chunk).
- **[PvP, Assassination opener]** Standard sequence: Cheap Shot → Envenom → Cold Blood Noxious Assault → Thistle Tea → filler → Kidney Shot → Noxious Assault to death; if Tea is on CD, dump remaining combo points into Kidney Shot before Cheap Shot ends, then Noxious Assault to death; use Gouge/Blind situationally, and Evasion to survive when all cooldowns are down — [[d:rogue#1461753771838406667]], [[d:rogue#1461754013061222613]], [[d:rogue#1461754174701179098]], [[d:rogue#1461754193810292736]] (Not, 2026-01-16). Assassination PvP builds are considered flexible — "people will melt as long as you got the right stuff from assa tree" — and some "sweats" use weapon-swap macros to switch poison types mid-fight — [[d:rogue#1461753159893647381]], [[d:rogue#1461753702187532533]] (Nuian/Scryu, 2026-01-16).
- **[PvP]** "Daggers is still bis" for PvP even though other weapon types are more viable this patch than before — [[d:rogue#1461753749935755365]] (Heffey, 2026-01-16).
- **[PvP, race]** No single min-max race for PvP: viable options quoted include Undead, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome, Goblin, Orc, and Human depending on the matchup — [[d:rogue#1463245725055058098]] (Ionnatra, 2026-01-20).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Race]** Undead considered the best race for Naxxramas: its 2% extra damage racial applies since all Naxx bosses except Maexxna are Undead or Humanoid; also called very good in Karazhan — [[d:rogue#1459758769134371051]], [[d:rogue#1459765198385713172]], [[d:rogue#1459765939049463888]] (riku/Matt Lender/Treehide-Verrik, 2026-01-10; confirmed by Treehide/Verrik being a top Nord DPS parser on Undead).
- **[Professions]** Only Engineering provides a direct stat bonus to the character among professions; other professions are mainly gold-saving/income (e.g., crafting your own flasks is cheaper than buying) — [[d:rogue#1459623698632802468]], [[d:rogue#1459623922533404880]] (Rheinwg, 2026-01-10). Leatherworking can craft fire-resist gear and a +spellpower leather kit later on — [[d:rogue#1459620504100143276]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Gold]** Noxious Assault + fast weapons trivializes solo farming: "As rogue you can literally just solo kill a few bosses inside Mara[udon] and you make +60g in 35 min" repeatably per day — [[d:rogue#1459461253050138676]] (Najven, 2026-01-09).
- **[Gold/crafted item]** The Ornate Bloodstone Dagger (OBD) is player-crafted; crafting fee observed at 700g on Alliance side (Tel Abim) vs a more "reasonable" 200g on Horde, since the recipe itself is rare/expensive to unlock — [[d:rogue#1459365932593057802]], [[d:rogue#1459386611132530748]] (Squibble/Purge, 2026-01-09/10).
- **[Race, PvE detailed breakdown]** Alliance: Human ≈ Night Elf as early options, but High Elf ends up with the most agility at true endgame plus a strong active racial (Human currently weak for rogues since Combat Swords lags behind Assassination daggers/later Combat daggers); Dwarf mainly worth it for PvP. Horde: Goblin (dagger weapon-skill racial removes the "do I need weapon-skill gloves" dilemma at BiS and frees a gear slot for haste/crit instead) is called the min-max PvE king by some, with Orc/Troll close behind (Troll's attack-speed racial was heavily nerfed ~50% at some point); weapon-skill racials matter "just [for] hit pre-AQ" since min-maxers mostly run Assassination (weapon-skill-agnostic) until then — [[d:rogue#1463245725055058098]], [[d:rogue#1463260936700690594]], [[d:rogue#1460986498672496782]], [[d:rogue#1460987564650397716]], [[d:rogue#1460988590203998208]] (Ionnatra/Scruff/andre/MistAssassin, 2026-01-14/20). Undead separately called underrated for PvE beyond its known Naxx/Kara racial-damage edge: the Vengeance racial (bonus damage) plus Will of the Forsaken (fear-break, relevant since many raid bosses fear) — [[d:rogue#1463249117906997319]] (Badknees, 2026-01-20).
