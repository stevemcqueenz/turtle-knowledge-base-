## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Turtle WoW remade Improved Backstab: grants a 45% chance to gain an additional combo point on backstab crit (custom, not vanilla) — [[d:rogue#1360640008255574348]] (Eylo, 2025-04-12).
- **[General]** Orc racial changed from stun resist to reduced stun duration (not immunity) — [[d:rogue#1360971823780462692]], [[d:rogue#1361181395459899634]] (Eylo, 2025-04-13/14).
- **[Assassination]** Ruthlessness bugged mid-2025 to give 2 CP instead of 1 per finisher ("2 CP bug"); later fixed — [[d:rogue#1379531144864993512]] (youthe, 2025-06-03), referenced angrily as a regression by [[d:rogue#1385105266529275904]] (wosihlaitiwende, +11, 2025-06-19), [[d:rogue#1398506223137062963]] (wosihlaitiwende, +12, 07-25: "devs understand rogue preferences less than the 2-combo-point bug did").
- **[Dev statement, general]** Lhian (dev): 1.18 rogue changes focus on 3 baseline issues — AoE/cleave capability, energy regeneration, and mobility; each spec then received additional spec-specific changes — [[d:rogue#1385167096198991933]] (+31 reactions, 2025-06-19).
- **[Combat]** 1.18 patch note (posted by Repose): Blade Flurry becomes baseline at level 30, no longer has a duration, CD reduced from 2 min to 4 sec, and becomes a **toggle**: while active it reduces energy generation by 30% and damage dealt by 20%, in exchange for cleave. Dev comment: moves decision-making to "keep it active or not" instead of "use it off CD" — [[d:rogue#1398119446249345044]] (Repose, 2025-07-25). Strongly disputed: many (Epickalen, Skelel, Premium, -Z?-, Cyric) argue this guts Combat's burst-window identity (old BF was paired with Adrenaline Rush/Kiss of the Spider/Quickness/Juju Flurry for a 15-20s haste burst window) and prefer keeping BF's old form or adding a separate haste steroid to Adrenaline Rush instead — [[d:rogue#1398220515637858338]], [[d:rogue#1398676686748258305]], [[d:rogue#1399456146959372308]] (2025-07-25/29).
- **[Assassination]** New capstone talent **Noxious Assault** ("the new mutilate") added to Assassination in 1.18, replacing/relocating older capstones; requires investment discussion (Double Dose debated as dagger-only vs weapon-speed-agnostic) — [[d:rogue#1398647498809147538]], [[d:rogue#1400560122882953296]], [[d:rogue#1400597053612097757]] (Scarefade, DatGuy3, Zaszz, 2025-07-25/08-01). T3/T3.5 set bonuses updated to include Noxious Assault: T3 4pc = crit on Backstab/SS/Hemo/Noxious Assault gives 5 energy; T3 6pc = reduced threat on those + Eviscerate; T3.5 5pc = 20% chance on those to "veil in shadows" — [[d:rogue#1398647498809147538]] (Scarefade, 2025-07-25).
- **[PvP/general]** New finisher **Shadow of Death** ("SoD"): sigil that accumulates a % of damage taken by target over 6s, capped at a % of caster's AP (scaling 50%/10% at 1pt up to 250%/50% at 5pt), then detonates as Physical damage; debated as weak in PvP vs strong in PvE longer fights — [[d:rogue#1398949268659638364]], [[d:rogue#1398951037032992868]], [[d:rogue#1400794941029879850]] (Youfie, Speedz, Nelson, 2025-07-27/08-01).
- **[General]** New poison: **Dissolvent Poison** — 20% chance on hit for instant Physical damage that ignores armor; Rank1 (lvl52) 88-113 dmg/100 charges, Rank2 (lvl60) 106-142 dmg/115 charges, scales 4.75% of AP (5% lower than Instant Poison) — [[d:rogue#1400899269354979560]] (Blindtongue, 2025-08-01).
- **[Combat]** New Stormshroud (T-set) itemization posted, compared old vs new: new adds hit/haste/crit stats and a 4pc proc of +10% dodge/+10 haste/+30 energy for 10s (2% chance on attack) vs old plain crit/dodge/AP bonuses — [[d:rogue#1399143780979970068]] (Epickalen, 2025-07-28).
- **[Tanking]** Rogue-tank starting threat issue "already fixed in 1.18" per community summary; agitating poison still nature damage (immunity problem unresolved) — [[d:rogue#1400442185220096071]] (Nettlefield, 2025-07-31).
- **[Dev-confirmed, pre-release]** Crite (dev): the only rogue change confirmed before 1.18 release is swapping **Opportunity** with **Improved Expose Armor** and **Improved Gouge** on the talent tree (i.e. relocating Opportunity into Combat and moving IEA/Imp Gouge into Sub); other proposed changes not dismissed but held pending post-release performance data — [[d:rogue#1402014856667861023]] (Crite, +10, 2025-08-04). Community reaction very positive — [[d:rogue#1402021340008939530]] (Hedonist, +7, 2025-08-05).
- **[Sub PvP nerf]** Nelson reports Sub/Hemo PvP build was nerfed at 1.18 launch: gains a shield on Vanish and a new finisher that costs 30 energy + all combo points for a damage-multiplier window *or* a 6s stun (mutually exclusive choice), plus +3% Crippling Poison proc chance; loses the old cheap "reset" Vanish, Improved Expose Armor, and Smoke Bomb from the PvP kit — [[d:rogue#1403010064171794631]] (Nelson, 2025-08-08).
- **[Talent rework]** Improved Sap renamed **Blackjack**: absorbs Irritating Agent's functionality, damage-reduction increased 6%→10%, Sap itself can now also apply it, and point cost reduced 3→2 — [[d:rogue#1405490156500422707]] (patch note quoted by mr.nobody, 2025-08-14).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** Community consensus mid-2025: best raid parses generally come from warrior/mage/hunter/warlock; rogue "pretty far down the list" but fine for guilds not min-maxing; rogue's strongest niche is one Sub rogue running Improved Expose Armor (IEA) for raid-wide armor debuff — [[d:rogue#1375099954367827988]] (Zaszz, +0, 2025-05-22, single source but detailed).
- **[Combat]** Combat Sword/Axe is called the top DPS spec/easiest to play pre-1.18 changes; "top single target DPS potential" but considered boring by some — [[d:rogue#1362735401047163032]] (Eylo, 2025-04-18); confirmed as still-top spec post cc2 by [[d:rogue#1389958249662648381]] (Bones, 2025-07-02).
- **[Subtlety]** Sub ("support rogue") viewed as raid-valuable via IEA/Hemo/Tricks of the Trade but very low personal DPS; considered by many "useless in raids" pre-1.18, improved in CC2/1.18 but still contested as a support build with narrow raid slot — [[d:rogue#1381917091451502593]] (Weirdthing, 2025-06-10), [[d:rogue#1387411099887865907]] (Bones: "Sub is viable and desired... unfortunately dependent on group comp", 2025-07-25), disputed by [[d:rogue#1389962281667920006]] (JAMMY: "Sub rogue was useless in raids, now it is useless in PvP too", 2025-07-02).
- **[Subtlety PvP]** Subtlety considered top PvP spec on Turtle — [[d:rogue#1371382582054354984]] (Zarthok referencing common knowledge, 2025-05-12; single source/question).
- **[Assassination]** Assassination AoE build (Envenom/Rupture spam + Blade Flurry) achieves ~1200-1300 sustained dps in BiS gear, "probably a little weaker than combat" — [[d:rogue#1361533376796037320]] (Zaszz, 2025-04-15).
- **[General]** Turtle-specific rework overview (Skadi): rogue AoE "questionable but better than 1.12"; Combat can still pull good single-target; 2 new specs (poison-oriented Assassination, support-type Sub) don't compete with Combat but are "different and fun"; PvP is "a lot worse than 1.12" — [[d:rogue#1362134789360062604]] (Skadi, 2025-04-16).
- **[Raid utility]** By 2025-07, community frames rogue's core problem as: pure DPS spec that isn't top DPS, with the only alternative being a support spec (Sub) most don't enjoy playing; contrasted unfavorably with Enhancement Shaman which brings damage AND utility — [[d:rogue#1398366454658896105]] (Xinio, 2025-07-25).
- **[Talent-tree identity]** Hambaga's assessment (well-received, referenced repeatedly): Assassination aims at "no-guard all-in single-target dps king" via poison/crit but lacks utility/AoE; Sub aims at "force amplification/control"; Combat has "no real identity... this is where the good talents are" — [[d:rogue#1384639491154645053]] (Hambaga, 2025-06-18).
- **[1.18 preview tier list]** Zaszz's post-preview build tier list (2025-08-04): **S-tier** — "31 Assassin/20 Combat" (Envenom+Noxious Assault, keep 5pt Rupture, very fast weapons both hands, instant poison, "fun single-target build"); "20 Assassin/31 Combat" (Taste for Blood, swords/axes/maces, Sinister Strike spam, 5pt Rupture, Deadly+Corrosive poison, "high physical dps"); "31 Sub" (support: 5pt IEA, 5pt SnD, Hemo uptime, Mark for Death/Tricks/Hemo/Expose all raid-wide value, slow weapons, "amazing support build, decent personal dps"). **A+ tier** — "15 Assa/31 Combat/5 Sub" dagger Backstab build (uses Opportunity from Sub); "20 Assa/31 Combat" Backstab-daggers (same but no Opportunity, "slightly worse"); "30 Assa/21 Combat" daggers (Seal Fate + Improved Backstab CP-generation, "fun... a little weaker than alternatives") — [[d:rogue#1401951039950950524]] (Zaszz, 2025-08-04).
- **[1.18 real data, leveling]** Baija's level-40 High Elf test with identical gear (crap greens + 2 blue weapons, fastest available): Combat averaged 95 dps, Assassination averaged 166 dps — [[d:rogue#1408409761396822088]] (Baija, 2025-08-22; single data point, self-reported "real data").
- **[Post-launch consensus]** By late September 2025, community converges on: Assassination is strong and *not* gear-reliant, best right after the lvl-40 capstone, "great early" but gets outperformed by Combat Daggers at higher gear; Combat Daggers is the highest-performing DPS spec once well-geared but requires stacking Agility and good weapons to shine; Combat Swords still strong but behind Daggers; Subtlety remains the dedicated support spec (IEA uptime, Hemo raid-wide physical-damage buff, crit-buff utility, a party shield) and is "not for pumping meters"; all four spec variants (Assa, Combat Dagger, Combat Sword, Sub) are called raid-viable — [[d:rogue#1422584837281419395]] (Scruff, 2025-09-30, detailed consensus summary), corroborated by [[d:rogue#1414542670335840256]] (Scruff, 2025-09-08: "assassin can do good consistent dmg and is not reliant on being heavily geared... combat will outshine assassin once heavily geared"), [[d:rogue#1414511385093738508]] (Blindtongue, 2025-09-08: Assassination is a gear-transition "bridge" spec for players without guild gear-rush support).
- **[Contested]** Whether Assassination has a hard damage ceiling that gear can't meaningfully raise (Blindtongue: "your dmg ceiling won't rise... even with major gear improvements") vs Combat's more linear gear-scaling — disputed, not resolved with data in this chunk — [[d:rogue#1419207766190592040]] (Blindtongue, 2025-09-21), [[d:rogue#1422321890613137488]] (Epickalen, questioning the claim, 2025-09-30).
- **[Combat Sword vs Dagger, 1.18]** Multiple raiders (Hedonist, Premium (Retired)) report Combat Sword (Gressil/The Hungering Cold) parses ~100 dps behind Combat Dagger (Kingsfall/Maexxna) even accounting for weapon-skill loss, in unbuffed dummy tests; attributed partly to Combat Sword needing 10+ "wasted" talent points just to function — [[d:rogue#1406786836160057515]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-08-18), [[d:rogue#1402625419814899763]] (Hedonist, 2025-08-06).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Eylo's leveling weapon-choice framework: One-handed Sword/Mace/Axe = higher solo/overland dps, lower dungeon dps, easier to find weapons, simple; Daggers = higher dungeon dps, lower overland dps, harder to find weapons, has a learning curve. Must-have leveling talents regardless of weapon: Precision 5/5 (5% hit, matters more from ~lvl 20), Remorseless Attacks 2/2 (40% crit after a kill, chains kills), Blade Flurry 1/1 (only AoE), Improved Backstab 3/3 for dagger users (30% crit + 45% chance for extra CP, Turtle-specific rework). Sword/axe/mace path reaches this by lvl 22; dagger path by lvl 25 — [[d:rogue#1360640008255574348]] (Eylo, 2025-04-12).
- **[General]** Eylo's leveling stage 2 (post-lvl22/25): choose between Combat tree (more weapon expertise, better autos/cleave/crit) vs Assassination (more crit, more CP, longer SnD/Relentless Strikes — "do NOT venture further than Relentless Strikes and Ruthlessness") vs PvP-oriented Sub build (Dirty Deeds + Preparation; do not use Hemo with daggers, only with swords). ## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General — Eylo's cap-stone list, unbuffed]** 6% Hit Chance target baseline; 315 weapon skill = 3% hit chance + reduces glancing blow damage; 29.8% crit chance for auto-attack cap-stone. Priority after cap-stones: Hit Chance to 26.4% (hard cap) > Agility > Crit Chance (above 29.8%) > Haste (Assassination) > Attack Power/Strength > Haste (Combat Sword, Combat Dagger, Sub Support) > Armor Penetration > Stamina > Dodge Chance (Combat Swords) > Life Steal > HP5 > Spirit > Intelligence. Hit-chance breakdown vs a boss: 5% block (frontal), 6.5% dodge, 8% miss on yellow/special attacks, 14% parry (frontal), 26.4% miss on white/auto attacks dual-wielding. Crit formula proposed: 100 - Glancing - Dodge - Miss - Parry (0 from behind) + Hit + Crit Suppression (3% at boss lvl 63) — [[d:rogue#1393867339723837522]] (Eylo, 2025-07-13; self-flagged as possibly imperfect, "correct me if wrong").
- **[Contested]** Youfie disputes that hit is strictly more valuable than crit outside of crit-cap scenarios: on a normal 1-roll attack table, +1% hit ≈ +1% crit for white damage mathematically (crit only "wins" due to specials and the crit-suppression cap); asks whether Turtle uses a different (2-roll?) attack-table mechanic — unresolved/open question in this chunk — [[d:rogue#1393894379378184232]], [[d:rogue#1394047560951271596]] (Youfie, 2025-07-13/14).
- **[Haste mechanics]** Haste sources are multiplicative, not additive: each gear/enchant haste instance and each spell/proc haste buff (SnD, Blade Flurry, Kiss of the Spider, Juju Flurry, racials, potions, Bloodlust, etc.) is a separate multiplier on weapon speed: `weapon_speed / (gear_haste1 × gear_haste2 × ... × buff1 × buff2 × ...)`; passive gear/talent haste sums together as one factor, then each spell/proc buff multiplies separately — [[d:rogue#1387271666970787983]] (Epickalen, +2, pinned, 2025-06-25), confirmed methodology by [[d:rogue#1387336782810451998]] (Deleted User, 2025-06-25), math worked example by [[d:rogue#1387236317456240783]] (30/username, 2025-06-25).
- **[Weapon skill / auto-attack math]** Weapon skill's dps value is small (~2-2.5% per point-tier in practice tests) and mainly reduces glancing-blow chance/damage rather than adding raw damage; Papairoh's sheet showed 5 weapon skill ≈ 2.4% dps in near-BiS daggers, mainly because not at hit cap (11% of hits were misses/dodges); real auto-damage share in that setup was 58.7% of total — [[d:rogue#1374189437491085312]] (Papairoh, 2025-05-20); live parse test (Epickalen) found +5 weapon skill (305→310) + 2 agility worth roughly 15 dps in a short/noisy sample, self-flagged as needing a longer test to control variance — [[d:rogue#1399951435751358485]] (Epickalen, 2025-07-30).
- **[Auto-attack damage share]** Rough estimate: auto-attacks are the largest damage component (~40-59% depending on gear/hit), CP-generator (Backstab/Sinister Strike) is next largest, finishers/poisons smaller — used as a talent-prioritization heuristic ("what maximizes autos first, then CP-builder, then the rest") — [[d:rogue#1372931688866381955]] (Hedonist, 2025-05-16).
- **[Weapon speed choice]** Slower MH is preferred because special-attack (Backstab/Sinister Strike) damage scales off weapon damage value, which is higher on slow weapons; faster OH is preferred purely to maximize poison-proc attempts, since OH has no damage-value requirement — [[d:rogue#1362585324806733864]] (Epickalen, 2025-04-18).
- **[Sim/BiS caveat]** Premium (Retired): the community rogue sim used for BiS-list guesstimates is "no longer accurate" (napkin-math, classic-era based); Turtle-specific factors (poison scaling, Surprise Attack, abundant multiplicative haste) mean sim results should be treated skeptically — e.g. no reason to use Consecrated Sharpening Stones since poison scaling overtakes the AP-based stones formula — [[d:rogue#1390775156636651685]], [[d:rogue#1391087534502707232]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-07-04/07-05).
- **[Assassination AP vs Agi itemization]** Silentkills flags that Naxx-tier (T3) gear is heavily AP-itemized (little/no Agility on some pieces), which is fine for AP-scaling specs (Assassination/Sub) but limits Combat (Agi-focused after 1.18 energy-from-agility changes); asks devs for separate Agi-leaning tier pieces — [[d:rogue#1398654095190917181]] (Silentkills, 2025-07-25; open/contested, no resolution in chunk).
- **[Post-launch spec stat priorities, Rheinwg's summary]** Combat: strong (slow) MH + fast OH, stack Agility. Assassination: 2 fast weapons, slightly prioritize AP over Agility. Sub: strong (slow) MH + fast OH, get Darkmantle set (still strong for energy) — [[d:rogue#1408320665139806228]] (Rheinwg, 2025-08-22).
- **[Assassination haste/AP priority, 1.18]** Zaszz: Haste is likely the most important stat for Assassination in 1.18 since energy gain comes from poison procs (attacking faster = more energy); Hit is also stronger than before; Crit is comparatively weaker because Instant Poison crit uses spell-crit chance, not physical crit; Assassination's abilities scale off AP not Agility, so raw AP is favored over Agility for that spec (contrast with Combat, whose Blade Rush energy scales off Agility) — [[d:rogue#1401952572238987294]] (Zaszz, 2025-08-04); largely reaffirmed later in the chunk (see Scruff/Rheinwg spec-priority bullets above) though Papairoh separately notes Agility's energy-generation benefit is non-linear (more Agility = disproportionately more energy gain) and that Windfury's relative contribution shrinks under the new haste/ability-heavy paradigm — [[d:rogue#1405247601355395152]] (Papairoh, 2025-08-13).
- **[Lethality talent math]** Zaszz clarifies Lethality's real value is smaller than its "30%" wording implies: Lethality only boosts the *bonus* crit damage (the extra 100% on top of a normal hit), so a 1000-dmg crit (normal hit would've been 500) becomes 500 + 500×1.3 = 1150, an actual ~13.95% increase, not 30% — still a good talent but often other talents are stronger per point — [[d:rogue#1405902756992122952]] (Zaszz, 2025-08-15).
- **[Compounding haste, extended example]** Multiple small multiplicative haste sources produce "free" bonus haste beyond their nominal sum: 5 sources of 2% haste compound to 1.02^5 ≈ 1.104 (10.4%, not 10%); stacking 30% Slice and Dice with 10% from gear compounds to 1.3×1.104 = 1.43 (43% total, i.e. +3% "free" from having multiple smaller sources rather than one big one) — [[d:rogue#1422230948094808074]] (Zaszz, 2025-09-27).
- **[Weapon-skill dps, updated estimate]** With Naxx lacking a dagger-weapon-skill item counterpart to the sword one, taking Goblin (313 vs. 310 weapon skill baseline) is roughly a 9 dps gain; rule of thumb cited: ~1% haste ≈ 1 dps — [[d:rogue#1404963671049895966]] (Epickalen, 2025-08-13); separate estimate: 5 weapon skill ≈ 15 dps, and 2% haste ≈ 50 AP worth of damage (~3.3 dps) in a controlled dummy test — [[d:rogue#1415562544025698334]], [[d:rogue#1422145837471109241]] (Epickalen, 2025-09-10/09-29).
- **[Combat energy overcap]** With Blade Rush's flat energy-per-agility scaling (tuned around Backstab), high-Agility Combat characters overcap energy regularly on Sinister Strike/Hemorrhage well before Backstab; community suggests the per-point energy value should be reduced (e.g. from a flat rate to a 5-point scaling talent) so non-Backstab builds don't waste energy — [[d:rogue#1408354614776041533]], [[d:rogue#1416253088523751424]], [[d:rogue#1416258743137730723]] (The Multiverse, Blindtongue, 2025-08-22/09-13; community proposal, not dev-confirmed).
- **[Open question]** Whether poison can still apply/proc on a glancing blow (relevant to whether weapon skill matters for poison-heavy Assassination/Dissolvent builds) — raised, not answered in this chunk — [[d:rogue#1418327773184786617]] (Puny, 2025-09-19).
- **[Dual-wield miss penalty clarified]** Common misconception corrected: the dual-wield ~19% extra miss penalty affects **both** mainhand and offhand white-hit chance, not offhand only — confirmed via personal log analysis after observing mainhand autos miss at 9% hit — [[d:rogue#1418550318207205406]], [[d:rogue#1418893981407903745]] (The Messiah Of Ignite, GumGuzz, 2025-09-19/20).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[General]** Poisons generally preferred over sharpening stones for DPS specs due to poison scaling with AP outpacing the flat stone bonuses; "no reason to use Consecrated Sharpening Stones as a rogue" — [[d:rogue#1390775156636651685]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-07-04).
- **[Instant vs stacking poisons]** Debate: instant poisons may perform better than stacking (Deadly) poisons except vs poison-immune bosses, since instant damage "adds up better long-term"; no hard data provided (single source, unconfirmed) — [[d:rogue#1371676043072508035]] (Silentkills, 2025-05-13); Dirtysecrets separately asks how poison talent investment compares to crit-bonus talents for Combat Sword — unanswered with numbers in this chunk, Rheinwg replies testing was informal/dummy-based, no updated sim — [[d:rogue#1376633603429105816]], [[d:rogue#1376654137353306223]] (2025-05-26/27).
- **[Corrosive/Deadly poison]** cdt notes the community had shifted to running Deadly+Corrosive poisons together specifically because "double Deadly" stopped applying 2 separate debuffs after CC2 — implies a Turtle-specific poison-debuff-stacking mechanic change — [[d:rogue#1398170837948825661]] (cdt, +8, 2025-07-25).
- **[Poison scaling]** Poisons scale with player stats on Turtle (confirmed indirectly via haste/AP discussion); Rheinwg notes Improved Poisons' value depends on weapon speed, haste, and how much you need to refresh Deadly Poison stacks after losing them (e.g., from Gouge/Blind usage) — [[d:rogue#1376654137353306223]] (Rheinwg, 2025-05-27).
- **[Weapon oils/temp enchants]** Fiery Weapon enchant + talent for 5% double-hit-chance called out as strong/proccing often on a Thrash Blade — [[d:rogue#1378307764534771803]] (ΠΙΠΗΣ, 2025-05-31; question, unconfirmed by others in-chunk).
- **[New poison]** Dissolvent Poison (see 1.18 changes) — Physical damage that ignores armor, scales 4.75% AP — [[d:rogue#1400899269354979560]] (Blindtongue, 2025-08-01).
- **[Poison-oriented PvP]** Nelson's PvP build stacks fast (1.3 speed) weapons for high instant-poison proc rate (~60%+ of damage from poison on meters, in a lower-population/lower-HP server context) — [[d:rogue#1400849181014229152]], [[d:rogue#1400809076576878693]] (Nelson, 2025-08-01; different-server context, flagged as such by poster).
- **[Instant vs Dissolvent Poison, post-1.18]** Eylo's comparison: Instant Poison hits slightly harder and benefits from Thunderfury's Nature-damage proc, but can't be applied to many MC/Naxx/AQ20/AQ40 elemental-type mobs; Dissolvent Poison benefits from Taste for Blood, Hemorrhage and armor-reduction effects, bypasses type immunities (including Nature immunity), but is weaker against physical-damage-reflect targets — [[d:rogue#1407489396999192586]] (Eylo, 2025-08-20). In practice, Dissolvent Poison is reported to outdamage Instant Poison by a wide margin (bug, not intended scaling — see 1.18 changes/Bugs sections) and became the default choice for most Assassination players by September — [[d:rogue#1421529371184861307]] (Waily, 2025-09-27), [[d:rogue#1406246349627723806]] (Suade, 2025-08-16: Dissolvent also confirmed to ignore armor as advertised and to work on Nature-immune mechanical/elemental mobs).
- **[Poison variety incentive, contested]** Premium (Retired) argues there's no real incentive to use anything but double Dissolvent/Corrosive physical poisons since Taste for Blood specifically benefits those, making Nature-based poisons (Instant, Deadly) largely obsolete for min-maxers; calls for a redesign so different poisons have distinct niches — [[d:rogue#1409816069757206601]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-08-26; open/contested, no dev resolution recorded).
- **[Poison addon updates]** EzPoison-turtle addon lets players pre-set and quickly reapply poisons (with a frame-unlock fix macro provided); community forks (TWBuyPoisonsPlus, TWBuyPoisons) updated to support Dissolvent Poison II — [[d:rogue#1408866010403962883]] (Akuya/Puddlewhisk, 2025-08-23), [[d:rogue#1406909407799021571]], [[d:rogue#1406043930268536843]] (Weirdthing, 2025-08-16/18).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Energy math]** Haste's effect on total energy generated over a fight (worked example by Weirdthing): at ~670 agility (near-BiS) tick rate improves from 2s/20-energy baseline enough to generate roughly 500 more total energy over a 60s fight (850→1350), translating to ~12.5 extra Sinister Strikes, ~2 more finishers, and roughly +183 dps just from the extra autos in that model — [[d:rogue#1399273454708064307]] (Weirdthing, 2025-07-28; theorycraft, unconfirmed by log data in-chunk).
- **[Threat/utility]** Tricks of the Trade (Sub) lasts only 12 seconds, criticized as too short to reliably keep up between finishers, unlike Druid's passive-uptime equivalent (Improved Enrage Aura-style, lasting up to ~30s); repeatedly flagged as needing a buff — [[d:rogue#1399035229251043408]] (Bones, 2025-07-27), [[d:rogue#1386639833623035966]] (TwistedMindz, 2025-06-23, describes having to choose between tank-survival CDs (Blind/Distract) and TotT uptime).
- **[Utility]** Smoke Bomb (20% evasion to bosses/mobs in area) considered near-unusable/bad design since it also helps enemies — [[d:rogue#1399035229251043408]] (Bones, 2025-07-27), [[d:rogue#1381930788077047838]] (Aye, redesign suggestion, 2025-06-10).
- **[Sub kit]** Marked for Death used as an opener (early IEA enabler) or synced with raid CD pop/execute phase; Improved Blind used to help tanks survive boss enrage timers; Dust of Disappearance used on DPS at risk of pulling aggro — [[d:rogue#1384682950896914573]] (Bones, 2025-06-18).
- **[Weapon-swap tech]** Sword rogues typically lack a natural Cold Blood + Ambush setup and need a weapon-swap macro to daggers to use it, at the cost of some talent-point efficiency — [[d:rogue#1361174263813505066]] (Eylo, 2025-04-14).
- **[Blade Rush / energy-tick addons]** Post-1.18, energy tick rate scales with Agility (via the Blade Rush talent) instead of a flat 2s tick; Weirdthing publishes updated forks of pfUI's energytick module, NugEnergy (adds `/nen tickrate` to show current tick rate), and a new addon RogueFocus to correctly display/calculate the new agility-based tick rate — [[d:rogue#1404367665866477655]], [[d:rogue#1405831940288020551]], [[d:rogue#1406043930268536843]] (Weirdthing, 2025-08-11 through 08-16); a separate community member (Mnemozin) independently patched pfUI's energytick module for the same reason — [[d:rogue#1413315170079609003]] (Mnemozin, 2025-09-05).
- **[Kidney Shot resource sequencing, PvP]** Nelson: to land a 5CP Kidney Shot you typically need Cheap Shot plus 2 Hemorrhages (draining most of your energy), Kidney Shot itself refunds 20 energy, leaving room for ~2 more Hemos during the 6s stun; using Shadow of Death instead of Kidney Shot frees more energy for damage but sacrifices the guaranteed stun — [[d:rogue#1405911831624679466]] (Nelson, 2025-08-15).
- **[Vigor]** The Vigor talent's energy-pool cap was noted as capped at 8 seconds' worth of energy at the time, with a buff to it expected — [[d:rogue#1401866527606575144]] (Eylo, 2025-08-04).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Rogue tank, general]** Rogue tanking is viable ("really strong to solo", can tank in AQ40 with the right gear) using Agitating Poison for threat and Flourish (finisher) for 20% parry; main weaknesses: Agitating Poison is Nature damage so poison-immune bosses/mobs (e.g. in MC and other raids) can't be threat-tanked, and rogues lack a taunt so tank-swapping is hard — [[d:rogue#1389101365753544774]], [[d:rogue#1387362091299508286]] (Nettlefield, 2025-06-30/07-08).
- **[Rogue tank, threat vs warrior]** Threat generation reported as noticeably lower than a T3-geared Protection Warrior; without a taunt, tank-swap mechanics can't be executed the normal way — Nettlefield requests a 2nd rank of Agitating Poison with more threat + physical damage type — [[d:rogue#1387362091299508286]] (Nettlefield, 2025-07-08).
- **[Rogue tank, community proposal]** Suggested design: don't give rogue tank a taunt, but let it generate high threat baseline and use Feint/Vanish to intentionally lower threat for tank-swaps ("swap out" instead of "taunt in") — [[d:rogue#1387369102363852832]], [[d:rogue#1400449019645530182]] (Nettlefield, 2025-07-08/07-31); Epickalen agrees survivability (lower armor than a shield warrior) is the bigger issue than threat itself, Thunderfury a common tanking-weapon pickup for rogue tanks — [[d:rogue#1387366196336132219]], [[d:rogue#1387369102363852832]] (Epickalen/Nettlefield, 2025-07-08).
- **[1.18 change]** Rogue-tank's weak starting threat "already fixed in 1.18" per community summary; nature-immunity and tank-swap issues still open — [[d:rogue#1400442185220096071]] (Nettlefield, 2025-07-31).
- **[Gear]** Rogue tank progression example: working toward Thunderfury and Tempest's Rage for threat; wants 3pc Kara40 set bonus, haste gloves/bracers, agility throwing weapon — [[d:rogue#1387732351852740709]] (Nettlefield, 2025-06-26).
- **[Post-1.18 impact]** Toggled Blade Flurry's energy-regen penalty made soloing/tanking content noticeably harder per direct dev feedback: "nearly died pulling a pack of level 50 Felwood satyrs earlier with it and I have nearly full Kara40 BiS" — [[d:rogue#1408350563690745866]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-08-22); same poster recommended devs remove the energy-regen penalty from Blade Flurry and instead tune the damage-reduction side only, specifically citing solo/leveling-content viability — [[d:rogue#1406786836160057515]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-08-18).
- **[Open question]** Whether/how rogue tanking remains viable post-1.18 given the Blade Flurry energy changes was asked directly but not answered with specifics in this chunk — [[d:rogue#1402799534048018544]] (Arts, 2025-08-07).

- **[Naxx]** Debuff-limit management: a player intentionally dropped a self-buff/effect (elemental sharpening stones, a +4% crit consumable) to stay under the raid debuff cap since the lost damage wasn't worth the conflict; prioritizes white-hit-cap then crit — [[d:rogue#1363171765215428751]] (Epickalen, 2025-04-19).
- **[Naxx armor mechanics]** Most Naxx bosses sit at 512 or 262 effective armor after standard debuffs (CoR/FF/IEA) unless the raid also brings a Krul Axe debuff or is fighting Eskhandar's-debuffed content; armor penetration is valuable on most bosses but most sources (enchants) give too little to be worth itemizing for, except Serrated Blades (dagger talent) which is "almost always worth" taking — [[d:rogue#1371923333930549408]] (Papairoh, 2025-05-13).

- **[World content]** Princess (world elite, presumably Dire Maul or similar) trivially soloable using Throwing Weapon Specialization + Corrosive Poison via Deadly Throw, kiting around a dead-zone where she can't use her ranged ability — [[d:rogue#1400917933013733386]] (Suade, 2025-08-01); refined further with Surefooted (+8% run speed) to deadzone the boss's boulder-throw and "fart bomb" abilities and avoid all damage while circle-kiting — [[d:rogue#1411693423848587397]] (Suade, 2025-08-31).
- **[Kara40, dragonkin trash split-pull]** For a room of 4 dragonkin trash (likely the Emerald Sanctum/Kara40 dragonkin pack), a rogue can split the pull: stealth to the side, have the raid precast on the boss and the closest dragonkin only, pop out of stealth to gain aggro from all 4, ensure the raid holds aggro on the intended 2 targets, then Vanish — the other 3 dragonkin reset (must be done before ~80% boss HP) and can be killed at leisure afterward — [[d:rogue#1414878692995502122]] (Epickalen, 2025-09-09).

## PvP
- **[Race tier list, PvP]** Eylo's PvP race ranking with rationale — Alliance: Dwarf best (Stoneform = quasi-Cloak of Shadows, removes bleed/disease/poison incl. Frontstabbing/strafestabbing while playing daggers described as "REALLY powerful" but more active/annoying than many players like — [[d:rogue#1374386086918361208]] (Sabrina, 2025-05-20).
