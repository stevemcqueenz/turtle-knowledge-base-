## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[assassination]** Turtle added two custom poisons, Corrosive (a physical DoT) and Dissolvent (instant physical), as "physical" counterparts to Deadly/Instant so Assassination isn't dead vs. poison-immune enemies; post-52 Assassination uses Dissolvent almost exclusively — [[d:rogue#1429837502092542063]] (Scruff), confirmed [[d:rogue#1431939754752212992]] (Im pro vise). Dissolvent's damage is physical (bypasses Nature immunity) but is still processed as "poison" for talent purposes (gets Taste for Blood's +15%, lasts 2x as long as Instant) — [[d:rogue#1432424536262967338]] (Scruff).
- **[general]** Poisons can now be used in raids without blocking Windfury procs — [[d:rogue#1429155845803085957]] (Romvlvs, +1).
- **[combat]** Feint's usage reportedly "increased by 10000%" after 1.18, implying a buff/rework to its utility — [[d:rogue#1430278690016727050]] (GumGuzz).
- **[combat]** Post-1.18 the Adrenaline Rush (AR) combat-dagger build became stronger relative to Envenom-combat because Blade Flurry got a bigger AR-scaled boost and now performs well on trash, not just as a boss-only CD; before 1.18 Envenom was the preferred trash spec — [[d:rogue#1432060511943524502]] (Illithris).
- **[assassination]** The reworked Assassination tree (Noxious Assault) is described as leaving old AR/Envenom-combat CDs "behind" for raid DPS in current (MC/Ony) content — [[d:rogue#1432297653164576859]] (Scryu). Turtle's Ruthlessness also works differently from classic and "makes finisher rotations much simpler" — [[d:rogue#1432297653164576859]] (Scryu).
- **[assassination, historical bug]** Spellpower was briefly added to Agitating Poison's scaling; it was broken and hotfixed very quickly — [[d:rogue#1430963888710746124]] (Scruff). Historically, one rogue abused a "2-combo Eviscerate" bug combined with spellpower-scaling Agitating Poison; a single resulting parse (rogues briefly 30th overall raid-wide DPS for a week) was cited for months as "proof" rogues didn't need buffs — [[d:rogue#1430987102413127700]] (Epickalen).
- **[general, forward-looking]** As of this chunk (through 2025-11-08), top raiders and channel regulars widely agree Assassination's extreme dependence on weapon speed (via Dissolvent) is a known balance problem likely headed for a rework/nerf, but "nothing anyone with real authority has mentioned" officially yet; a rogue-community item dev contact ("@Crite") reportedly receives frequent nerf suggestions, and players are trying to organize proactive feedback rather than a unilateral nerf, citing Turtle's history of buffing then "nuking" overtuned classes (e.g. Warlock) — [[d:rogue#1436289879968452638]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1436416710587715774]], [[d:rogue#1436437390943191211]] (Treehide/Verrik), [[d:rogue#1436445311198298193]] (Rheinwg). **Not yet implemented — pure community sentiment/rumor as of this chunk's dates.**

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[assassination]** Assassination is "op early game no matter what garbage you put on" and remains the easiest/most gear-independent leveling & early-raid spec — [[d:rogue#1428707790557548685]] (MistAssassin); confirmed repeatedly (e.g. [[d:rogue#1430330515432538263]]).
- **[general, important caveat]** Assassination's apparent dominance is content-stage-dependent: on Ambershire (where only MC/Ony are out), Assassination looks like the top DPS spec; on the more-progressed Nordanaar server, Combat *consistently* out-performs Assassination once players have Naxx/Kara-tier agility gear, and even Mages/Warriors reportedly out-DPS rogues on average there — [[d:rogue#1436461101612011572]] (andre, 2025-11-08). Multiple experts agree 3 pure-DPS specs *should* consistently beat hybrids, but Rheinwg attributes rogue's current oddities to over-reliance on fast weapons rather than stats — [[d:rogue#1436461210131370065]], [[d:rogue#1436461519935111389]].
- **[combat]** Combat (daggers or swords) "catches up" with AQ40-tier gear and can surpass Assassination once well-geared, but the exact crossover point is heavily disputed and was refined several times over the chunk:
  - Early vague guess: ~600 agility raid-buffed+consumed "consensus" — [[d:rogue#1431250843029602406]] (TwistedMindz).
  - Alternate framing: not really an agility threshold at all, but whether your MH dagger does enough Backstab damage; full Darkmantle is already close to T3 agility — [[d:rogue#1430534713679806514]] (Epickalen), reinforced when a live test in full T3+Naxx gear only showed Combat > Assassination *after* accounting for the fact assassination's poison bypasses armor entirely (target dummies have heavy armor, so Combat looks artificially weak there without IEA/Sunder present) — [[d:rogue#1434925922062041169]], [[d:rogue#1434926345284091974]] (Rheinwg/into dust, 2025-11-03).
  - Concrete tested numbers (most reliable, from a top player's own logs): with near-BiS Ambershire gear (6pc Darkmantle, Perdition's, etc.) Combat needs **>650 agility raid-buffed** to be close to Assassination, and only pulls clearly ahead over **~700 agility**; the better your MH dagger, the lower this threshold — Ambershire's bottleneck is dagger availability, not raw agility (700 agi is reachable in a double-shaman group) — [[d:rogue#1436324376344658041]] (Scryu). GumGuzz disputes the gear baseline used (6pc Darkmantle "is not BiS") and says 5pc AQ40 gear alone gives more agility than the whole Naxx T3 set, i.e. Combat starts competing once AQ40 drops — [[d:rogue#1436329778293768192]], [[d:rogue#1436330915277312061]].
  - General agreement Combat "pulls ahead as early as AQ40" — [[d:rogue#1435342800291102783]] (Drox); Assassination remains a good raid off-spec for fights with positioning mechanics that prevent staying on a boss's back — [[d:rogue#1435342699313107068]] (Drox).
- **[combat]** Swords rogue is "barely behind daggers depending on boss and gear," so viable in raids — [[d:rogue#1430214110426960095]] (Weirdthing).
- **[assassination, "spellstrike"/OBD build]** A late-chunk expert thread (Suade, Drox, Zaszz, Blindtongue, Rheinwg) documents that the Ornate Bloodstone Blade (OBD) + Distracting Dagger "spellstrike" Assassination build (needs only OBD + an MC ring) is extremely strong — competitive with or beating full Combat-daggers sets, and its cheap pre-raid weapons are "neck-and-neck" with Naxx-tier weapons; "5 rogues in my guild have copied my gear" — [[d:rogue#1435143340084428851]], [[d:rogue#1435343324125986887]], [[d:rogue#1435344237976748339]] (Suade, 2025-11-03/04). This is widely seen as a symptom of the same weapon-speed-dominance problem and a likely nerf target (see Contested/open questions).
- **[subtlety]** Sub is considered the hardest spec to play well but a very strong support pick: raid-wide Hemorrhage gives ~4% more physical damage, Improved Expose Armor gives ~5-8% more than Sunder, combining to a claimed ~10% more physical raid damage on single-target bosses (disputed by Heffey, who doubts the 10% figure since IEA only applies on bosses) — [[d:rogue#1431385258590208052]], [[d:rogue#1431382065693331476]] (Zaszz/Heffey, 2025-10-25). Sub doesn't stack well with a 2nd sub rogue — [[d:rogue#1431351267292090370]]. It's "worth it for the raid and a different playstyle, but don't expect to top meters," and is only worth a raid slot once the raid's overall DPS floor is already high enough for the % buffs to matter — [[d:rogue#1435978878475567124]] (Soulafei), [[d:rogue#1435980807045906553]] (Purge). A poorly-informed raid leader kicking a sub rogue for low personal DPS is "like kicking a healer for not doing damage" — [[d:rogue#1435997314996244656]] (Scruff).
- **[general]** "Rogues are currently top single-target DPS" and easy to gear for early raids — [[d:rogue#1430673029960175636]] (Epickalen).
- **[races]** Recommended race tiers: Human/High Elf/Goblin/Orc/Troll all "very good"; Night Elf "a bit less"; Gnome and Dwarf "the worst" — [[d:rogue#1430857997671727154]] (Weirdthing), reconfirmed [[d:rogue#1434005965530988595]] (Treehide/Verrik). High Elf's +2% agility racial is not significant enough to matter more than personal preference — [[d:rogue#1430851168879382603]] (Hedonist, +1). Orc's Blood Fury scales with strength (which haste scales with) and is very strong on trash and in guilds with fast kill times; usable even while stealthed — [[d:rogue#1433930506243932302]], [[d:rogue#1433932955457294416]] (Treehide/Verrik), [[d:rogue#1433933171594100847]]. Racials besides Troll Berserking are otherwise "fairly equalized," worth ~1-2% — [[d:rogue#1433928807659733064]] (Epickalen).
- **[PvP]** Assassination is the go-to for beating plate classes ("Nox turns paladin/warrior into a joke of a matchup," citing a T3.5 warrior losing to a level-59 rogue); Sub is preferred for chain-CC/utility; Combat daggers is claimed viable for PvP by Epickalen but disputed as lacking utility by 0r5y — [[d:rogue#1432360854749184000]], [[d:rogue#1432361094273306695]], [[d:rogue#1433373898837327933]], [[d:rogue#1433375497915731978]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[assassination]** Core logic: Noxious Assault + poison application are the main damage sources; keep 5pt Rupture up (adds 15% physical damage) and weave 1pt Slice and Dice, spending spare CP on Envenom — [[d:rogue#1429838431629873182]] (Scruff). SnD before Envenom (not after) so their durations "line up" and fall off together — [[d:rogue#1431269420604456961]] (TwistedMindz). GumGuzz's own dummy-tested rotation (see Gear section for full data): **1cp Envenom → 1cp SnD → at least 4cp Rupture → fit in Eviscerate when possible**, with a good rotation showing ~96%+ uptime on SnD/Taste for Blood/Envenom — [[d:rogue#1433046933362184192]] (GumGuzz screenshot, +4). Community-shared variant: Nox → 1cp SnD → 1cp Envenom → Nox → Nox → 4-5cp Rupture → 1cp SnD → 1cp Envenom → Nox×3 → 5cp Eviscerate → repeat, refined by Quintis to prioritize a 5pt Rupture first and only dump 5cp into Eviscerate if Rupture still has >14s left — [[d:rogue#1430895639339733062]], [[d:rogue#1430896736833900627]].
- **[assassination]** Never use Backstab if playing Noxious Assault Assassination: "you don't go backstab if you go noxious" — [[d:rogue#1436359116774838312]] (GumGuzz), [[d:rogue#1434971886474891479]] (Treehide/Verrik), [[d:rogue#1435036777974075495]] (andre) all agree a mixed Backstab/Noxious build "isn't going to come close to full assa or full combat." Noxious Assault's highest rank deals 105 flat + 35% AP and applies 2 poisons for a net ~41 energy cost after the poison-application energy refund (4 energy back per poison), vs. Backstab's 150% weapon dmg + 225 flat + big crit modifier for 60 energy — [[d:rogue#1434977677114343505]], [[d:rogue#1434977264889757829]] (Ionnatra/Foreseen/Scruff). Eventually (roughly AQ40-tier gear) a Backstab-based build does out-DPS Assassination once well-geared — [[d:rogue#1434978536682164296]] (Scruff), [[d:rogue#1434987412999639051]] (Drox).
- **[assassination]** Weapon-speed math (core, repeated/confirmed constantly): ~7-8 extra "poison dps" per 0.1 reduction in weapon speed; a 1.4-speed/50-"dps" weapon and a 1.3-speed/43-"dps" weapon do the same effective damage once poison procs are counted — [[d:rogue#1430880317618524272]], [[d:rogue#1430948306942689424]] (+2), reconfirmed [[d:rogue#1433328342328803409]]. Because Noxious Assault and Eviscerate don't scale off weapon damage, faster weapons beat higher-"dps" raid weapons — [[d:rogue#1430893207310438400]], [[d:rogue#1430894302845992971]] (Scruff). This is so strong that "a 2.0-speed epic from K40 is worse than a 1.5-speed epic from BWL," and there's effectively no dagger progression at all through MC/BWL/AQ40 for Assassination — only Naxx swords (or the pre-raid OBD/Alcor's-tier daggers) matter — [[d:rogue#1435354770335465493]] (Zaszz), [[d:rogue#1435344862575726592]] (Drox). A theorized (unconfirmed) explanation floated in-channel: Noxious Assault may get ~20% extra damage per +0.10 weapon speed, capped around 1.80 — [[d:rogue#1435351472022880357]] (Rheinwg, presented as theory/observation, not dev-confirmed).
- **[assassination]** Poison AP-scaling: Instant Poison scales 5% of AP, Deadly Poison scales 8% of AP — [[d:rogue#1430917187962667088]] (Rheinwg). Dissolvent gets Taste for Blood's +15% bonus (Instant does not) and lasts 2x as long as Instant — [[d:rogue#1432424536262967338]] (Scruff), [[d:rogue#1430918918553211030]] (Rheinwg, +1). Because nature-type poison damage (Instant/Deadly) inherently bypasses armor like other magic damage, while Dissolvent (physical) is subject to armor mitigation but bypasses Nature *immunity* — even if Dissolvent gets nerfed, Instant Poison remains a valid answer for plate targets since it "ignores plate armor cos it's nature dmg" — [[d:rogue#1436291363388788848]] (GumGuzz).
- **[assassination]** Poison choice by fight length: Deadly+Corrosive generally beats Dissolvent "in the long run"; Dissolvent (no ramp-up) is better for short fights (<~1 min) and add/target-switch fights (e.g. Incantagos); some raiders still use Sharpening Stone OH for Undead bosses specifically since those fights are typically sub-1-minute (Corrosive should out-damage it on longer fights) — [[d:rogue#1430559768942219264]], [[d:rogue#1430560339334009033]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1430368085084471387]] (Treehide/Verrik). On Chromaggus specifically, Deadly may be worth using for his Nature vulnerability — [[d:rogue#1430566011270074378]] (Rheinwg). Post-52, Assassination uses Dissolvent in "99.99%" of scenarios regardless — [[d:rogue#1431939754752212992]] (Im pro vise).
- **[assassination]** Napkin-math value from raid logs: 200 AP ≈ 14 dps; 2% crit ≈ 50 AP; poison is ~4-6% of total DPS in Kara40 — [[d:rogue#1430369946600472686]], [[d:rogue#1430370156642570330]], [[d:rogue#1430369246164160565]] (Epickalen). Spell crit affects poison crit chance but has zero scaling with spell power for poisons — [[d:rogue#1430962042927321231]] (Scruff); "very negligible on poison, but very noticeable on 'on harmful spell hit' proc weapons" like OBD — [[d:rogue#1432298441081622629]] (Epickalen). Melee crit is the recommended long-term priority over spell crit — [[d:rogue#1433239645151563817]] (Epickalen).
- **[assassination]** Weapon skill gives +0.2% hit per point (+5 skill = +1% hit); "8% is your minimum," 12-18% commonly run, 20%+ easy naturally at endgame (26% deemed excessive) — [[d:rogue#1432299358820372551]], [[d:rogue#1431669472535777530]]. 311 weapon skill is easily reached via the 250g weaponskill quest + Distracting Dagger — [[d:rogue#1431171926256255006]] (GumGuzz). Weapon skill is linear on Turtle (same value for the first 5 points as the last 5, up to 315 cap) — [[d:rogue#1433460906599383113]] (Matt Lender).
- **[general]** 315 weapon skill is NOT a hard cap for reducing glancing blows — "you still get hit past that" — [[d:rogue#1431650253643452567]] (GumGuzz; contradicts a commonly repeated channel belief).
- **[general, contested/unresolved]** Long argument on Haste vs. Hit given swing-timer/extra-swing mechanics; no consensus — [[d:rogue#1430079836839350342]] through [[d:rogue#1430089247154507847]] (2025-10-20/21). A separate, very detailed and never-resolved argument about whether the character-sheet crit value is mis-derived from weapon skill — [[d:rogue#1430362641615814808]] through [[d:rogue#1430385813404778578]] (2025-10-21).
- **[general]** Design/bug quirk: it's currently a DPS "gain" for an assassination rogue if Rupture gets dodged or resisted (no combo points wasted); intentional tradeoff so CP-loss-on-dodge doesn't tank IEA uptime, but creates backwards-feeling incentives vs. bleed-immune enemies — [[d:rogue#1435704877245403237]], [[d:rogue#1435706211344318566]] (Clevvy/Scruff).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general]** Two different agility-conversion tables circulated with no resolution: (a) 1 Agi = 2 Armor, 0.07% Dodge, 0.035% Crit, 1 AP — [[d:rogue#1430918015314300938]] (Azitor-Nordanaar, +1); (b) "1 agility = 1.78 AP" from an external table — [[d:rogue#1430907932878573661]] (LamineYamal). **Contested/unclear which is accurate.** A separate, Blade-Rush-specific estimate from a well-regarded rogue (Hedonist) puts agility's value as high as ~4 AP/point (another poster says ~3.4) when Blade Rush is talented — [[d:rogue#1434736521440657632]] (elyina, citing Hedonist).
- **[assassination]** Energy tick speed is cited two different ways: 0.0012 sec reduction per agility (600 agi → 1.28s tick) — [[d:rogue#1428736097210470400]] (TwistedMindz); vs. ~800 agility getting ticks to ~1 second, i.e. ~50% faster than a 2-second base — [[d:rogue#1430500753562406933]], [[d:rogue#1430500897674498158]] (0r5y). **Contested/unclear which formula/base is correct.**
- **[combat]** Rough stat-equivalence: Haste ≈ Crit ≈ Hit ≈ 25 AP each, "just has to be the right balance" — [[d:rogue#1432025351579635772]], reconfirmed with more detail (haste's value increases as you already have hit/crit covered) — [[d:rogue#1436158817447706775]], [[d:rogue#1436159209006956714]] (Epickalen, detailed self-report: built an entire combat set around haste, 25% hit then stacked haste via Time-Frozen Bow, ended near crit cap raid-buffed). Mathematically, Haste has increasing returns while Crit has diminishing returns — [[d:rogue#1436166078215491646]] (Ghordon).
- **[combat]** Agility valued "all inclusive" (energy regen + crit + AP) at roughly 1.5 AP/point — [[d:rogue#1431677367251636426]] (Epickalen). Agility is prioritized primarily for the crit it gives once raid-buffed, more than for energy regen — [[d:rogue#1434736490981625939]] (Epickalen). "Agility is king" for combat gear choices — [[d:rogue#1433922628652896277]] (Treehide/Verrik, +2), and combat should take Agi over minor AP differences on rings/items when the numbers are close — [[d:rogue#1435339525164498945]] (Suade). Caution: overcapping energy is possible with faster weapon types like swords, so agi/energy-regen stacking needs to be weighed against weapon speed — [[d:rogue#1433914468869345311]] (Rheinwg).
- **[general]** Close Quarters Combat (the dagger/mace/fist crit talent) only checks the mainhand weapon type — offhand doesn't matter — confirmed via live testing (Anubisath War Hammer + Crystal Sword of Blossom, both 1.8 speed, level-60 dummy) — [[d:rogue#1430389074383998996]] (Treehide/Verrik), reconfirmed [[d:rogue#1431664744678424617]] (Epickalen).
- **[general]** Hit-cap breakdown (well-corroborated): 8% is the "yellow hit" minimum vs. level-63 bosses; the Combat tree (10 points) gives +5% hit; weapon skill gives +1% hit per 5 points over 300; as a dual-wielder the real "white hit"/glancing-avoidance target is ~24-25% — [[d:rogue#1433550147043983470]] (VoidSpork, +1), [[d:rogue#1433552521854451713]] (Scruff, detailed). You can't apply poison on an auto-attack that misses — [[d:rogue#1433557308897497219]] (Epickalen); white misses also can't proc Windfury or your poisons, hence some players target ~16% hit — [[d:rogue#1433707605368504430]] (Matt Lender). After ~9% hit, hit vs. crit value is close (poisons don't crit from melee crit, only spell crit) — [[d:rogue#1433709524925288458]] (Matt Lender). Weapon skill also raises your crit *cap* as a dual-wielder — [[d:rogue#1435341223245385809]] (Drox), [[d:rogue#1436534663425425629]] (Rheinwg notes a flat 3% crit suppression vs. level-63 bosses).
- **[tanking]** Defense/avoidance conversion for rogue tank sets: ~9 Defense = 1% avoidance; ~14.5 Agility = 1% avoidance; useful for judging whether +defense enchants are worth it vs. agi on a given slot — [[d:rogue#1435379674661523557]], [[d:rogue#1435379898960052364]] (Scruff).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[assassination, dummy-tested comparison]** GumGuzz posted a full pre-raid Assassination BiS comparison (dummy-tested, poisons refreshed until below 10 stacks; 2x Dissolvent, all gear haste-enchanted on head/legs/hands/shoulders, Crusader weapon enchants, Whip + Royal Seal trinkets, Ring of Electrical Binding + Sandswept Ring of Arcanum, Bixxle neck for spellcrit) — [[d:rogue#1433046933362184192]]:
  - Alcor's Sunrazor + Distracting Dagger, 6pc Darkmantle + 2pc Devilsaur + Wing of the Time-Lord cape + Time-Frozen Bow: **725.8-736.8 dps** (Dissolvent ~59%, Auto ~16%, Noxious ~10%, Rupture ~9-10%, Eviscerate ~4-5%).
  - Alcor's Sunrazor + Distracting Dagger, 4pc Darkmantle + 4pc Stormshroud + same cape/bow: **732.1-764 dps** (very similar breakdown, slightly higher due to Stormshroud proc variance).
  - Blade of Eternal Darkness (BOED) + Distracting Dagger, 4pc Darkmantle + 3pc Incendosaur + Thunder Lizard's Hide + Embergem Cuff Wrists + Flamestring Bow: **761.5-762.8 dps** (Dissolvent ~48-49%, Engulfing Shadows proc ~15%, Auto ~12%, Rupture ~8-9%, Noxious ~8%, Fire Strike ~3%, Eviscerate ~3%).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[assassination]** Crusader remains the standard weapon enchant at 60 since weapons are frequently replaced and it's a real DPS proc; combat prefers 15 Agility on both weapons for crit-stacking/energy regen — [[d:rogue#1431173261173194803]] (Epickalen), reconfirmed [[d:rogue#1435130795151986700]] (andre, "haste and AP after hit cap are basically the only secondary stats assa cares about"). Weapon-speed math means Fiery Weapon/Flametongue are not worth it at endgame (mostly leveling/fashion) — [[d:rogue#1431164389645488139]], [[d:rogue#1431166001709449246]] (0r5y).
- **[combat]** Combat shoulder enchant: "Haste all the way," preferred over Armor Pen/ZG idol pre-Naxx — [[d:rogue#1431963112927531150]] (Epickalen, +1). Debate on +8 agility vs. the ZG idol enchant (SS/Backstab dmg proc) for head/legs: Treehide/Verrik's opinion is +8 agility is slightly better, "though they are close" — [[d:rogue#1435344651438653442]] (+1); an informal GPT-assisted estimate put +20 Agility at ~1.8-2.0% DPS vs. +6 Weapon Skill (dagger) at ~3-4% DPS overall — [[d:rogue#1435338658017185924]] (Ionnatra, unverified/informal).
- **[assassination/dissolvent mechanics]** Dissolvent does not proc Vigor, and with an on-hit proc weapon like OBD it only counts toward the initial hit, not the DoT tick — [[d:rogue#1433119651973562539]] (Scruff). - **[general, QoL complaint]** Managing 4+ poison types (Dissolvent, Crippling, Mind-Numbing, Instant) burns a lot of bag space to craft in bulk; multiple players wish for a dedicated poison pouch/keyring, or a stack-size increase from 20 to 40, as a QoL fix; not currently in the game — [[d:rogue#1435359506841211121]] (Scruff, +6), [[d:rogue#1435358516272697525]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1436558431669584085]] (Blindtongue).

- **[assassination]** Elemental Sharpening Stone (spellpower scaling, +) used by some in the OH for undead fights instead of Corrosive Poison, mainly because undead fights are typically sub-1-minute; math napkin: 200 AP ≈ 14 dps, 2% crit ≈ 50 AP, so a stone's crit is only clearly better than Corrosive on very short fights — [[d:rogue#1430368085084471387]], [[d:rogue#1430369946600472686]] (Epickalen/Treehide/Verrik).
- **[assassination]** Confirmed AR (Arcane Resistance) consumable stacking for a specific gearing situation: chest+head enchanted for 110 AR each, Chromatic Flask, and a Magic Resistance Potion; the enchanted cape is also worth >50 AR if you don't want to use a Chromatic flask — [[d:rogue#1430345980561199125]], [[d:rogue#1430346100195196980]] (Epickalen).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[assassination]** Energy tick is reduced by agility (two competing formulas, see Stats section, contested). Vanish is regularly used for trinket-swapping mid-fight (e.g. to swap onto/off Venomous Totem, or after popping Thistle Tea to hide the resulting debuff) — [[d:rogue#1435286127459172364]] (Zaszz), [[d:rogue#1434858242596409394]] ("30"). Vanish can also be used to dodge specific raid-boss mechanics: e.g. Naxx's "Kings Wrath"/4-Horsemen-mark-style abilities have been successfully vanished with timing similar to a Horsemen mark — [[d:rogue#1434939143544115382]] (Heffey); on AQ40's Twin Emperors ("Chess" event) and C'thun encounters, timing a Vanish against specific boss abilities (Kings Wrath, C'thun's chaining beam) is inconsistent/situational — some players prefer trinket-swapping earlier instead, others just spread/walk away rather than Vanish — [[d:rogue#1434927740825505954]], [[d:rogue#1435922156973199411]] (Scruff/Toppings).
- **[combat]** Blade Flurry buff-cap workaround macro (widely thanked, +8): only casts Blade Flurry when under 31 total buffs, first canceling several long, low-priority raid buffs (Healing Way, Ancestral Fortitude, Protective Light, Daybreak, Renew, Rejuvenation, Bloodlust, Crusade):
  ```
  /cast [mybuff:Blade_Flurry] Blade Flurry
  /cancelaura [mybuffcount>30] Healing Way
  /cancelaura [mybuffcount>30] Ancestral Fortitude
  /cancelaura [mybuffcount>30] Protective Light
  /cancelaura [mybuffcount>30] Daybreak
  /cancelaura [mybuffcount>30] Renew
  /cancelaura [mybuffcount>30] Rejuvenation
  /cancelaura [mybuffcount>30] Bloodlust
  /cancelaura [mybuffcount>30] Crusade
  /cast [mybuffcount<31] Blade Flurry
  ```
  Uses the Roid-Macros addon (https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/Roid-Macros) — [[d:rogue#1429240041577644186]] ("30").
- **[subtlety]** Ghostly Strike used for energy savings when moving, on split targets, or in the last seconds of a kill; kept off when everyone is stacked on target and Hemorrhage can be sustained instead — [[d:rogue#1435280553036484648]]-[[d:rogue#1435280684250959943]] (Zaszz). Combo generation for sub: Backstab 1-2cp, Mark of Death 2cp (on CD), Hemorrhage 1cp, Ambush 2cp, plus a talent that returns 1cp per finisher used — [[d:rogue#1434278991828881609]] (Ionnatra).
- **[general]** Armor-reduction mechanics explained with worked example: a mob with 2111 armor reduces incoming damage by 27.7% at 0 reduction; -200 armor (to 1911) drops that to 25.8%, i.e. a 100-damage hit goes from 72.3 to 74.2 effective damage; roughly 250 armor reduction ≈ 4% more damage taken — [[d:rogue#1434855022507593798]] (GumGuzz), [[d:rogue#1434855034117685349]] ("30"). Expose Armor overwrites Sunder Armor for the armor-reduction effect, but warriors can still Sunder afterward purely for threat generation on Turtle; (talented) Expose gives more armor reduction than Sunder; some low-armor bosses don't need Expose at all — [[d:rogue#1434862089775611915]]-[[d:rogue#1434862663287967875]] ("30"/Weirdthing). In a raid, Expose Armor is priority #1 for sub, ahead of SnD — [[d:rogue#1434861589529624576]] (Weirdthing); with T3.5 you can Expose a 2nd/3rd target relatively easily — [[d:rogue#1434861752423546914]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[general]** A threat-generation poison exists specifically for rogue tanking on Turtle: combined with forced procs it gives a decent threat/sec, though lack-of-taunt issues appear if the group has very high DPS; certain fights "accidentally" make rogue tanks the best option, but it's limited — usually the rogue tank is 2nd in threat and just holds the boss until a plate+shield class can taunt; with the right gear/rotation you can reach 100% avoidance, but "that only gets you so far for so long" — [[d:rogue#1435795777355255948]], [[d:rogue#1435796338557063249]] (Epickalen).
- **[general]** For pure solo-tanking builds, Sub is the strongest for double-Evasion uptime, but its damage is too low to actually kill things solo — better to use an Assassination/Riposte build for solo content; for group tanking, prefer Combat/Sub with Prep and max agility for avoidance+dodge — [[d:rogue#1434385212707700816]] (Blindtongue, +1). One tank build (offense-oriented, free Eviscerate spam from gear synergy) reportedly out-damaged a guildmate's full-T3 offensive assassin on some bosses — [[d:rogue#1434386264911777944]].
- **[gear]** Rogue-tank weapon choice debate: Assassination-tank with 1.8-speed weapons vs. Combat-tank with Kingsfall (good low-end damage, near-BiS for avoidance stats); "combat needs something chonkier than 1.8" to be worth it, and it's hard to fit meaningful hit into an avoidance-stacked tank build (as little as 1/5 points in the hit talent) — [[d:rogue#1434414993205625005]], [[d:rogue#1434415943718670386]], [[d:rogue#1434416071808520244]] (Suade/Blindtongue). Kingsfall is BiS for avoidance+damage even though not slow; Servo Arm trinket used situationally for burst-damage-check fights like King Gordok — [[d:rogue#1434417174608609380]], [[d:rogue#1434417437121577090]] (Suade). One tank reports soloing King Gordok with ~50% dodge / 10% parry — [[d:rogue#1434419504032514109]] (Suade). Defense/avoidance conversion: ~9 Defense = 1% avoidance, ~14.5 Agility = 1% avoidance (see Stats section) — [[d:rogue#1435379674661523557]] (Scruff).

- **[assassination/farming]** Voone (LBRS) drops throwing axes used with Improved Throw talent to kite-farm Princess Huhuran safely; facetanking her is a pure gear check but only takes ~2 min vs. ~10 min for the kite method — [[d:rogue#1433013282322776126]] (Maekju), [[d:rogue#1435137926429147147]] (GumGuzz). She is immune to Crippling Poison, so kiting relies on Deadly Throw + Corrosive Poison instead — [[d:rogue#1435124450424127600]], [[d:rogue#1435137943630123108]] (AC/Suade).
- **[general]** Cannon Master Willey (Scarlet Monastery/Stratholme-adjacent "live" content) can be soloed, but the trash on the way there can see through stealth; the workaround is Sprint/Vanish/Prep/Sprint/Vanish chaining to skip past it — [[d:rogue#1436370216752910336]], [[d:rogue#1436370330297045094]] (Scruff).
- **[general]** Rattlegore (Scholomance) can be soloed by clearing the adds nearest him first (one socially aggros with the boss — kill it, then Vanish to reset), popping a Free Action Potion ~5-10s in to avoid his stun, and running a Flourish/Ghostly-Strike → Evasion → Flourish/Ghostly cycle; described as resource-intensive and easier with a 2nd rogue — [[d:rogue#1431914254612828233]] (GumGuzz), [[d:rogue#1430826915555709028]].
- **[MC]** Medivh's fight (Kara40, referenced alongside MC-era discussion) — a "typical AR [Adrenaline Rush] spec" with Deadly/Corrosive poisons is favored since bosses die fast in that content, one player's best parse was ~2.7k with this setup — [[d:rogue#1430375563440423043]] (Heffey). Poison choice on Chromaggus favors Deadly for his Nature vulnerability — [[d:rogue#1430566011270074378]] (Rheinwg). Instant/Dissolvent are preferred vs. Incantagos due to frequent add/target-switching — [[d:rogue#1430560339334009033]] (Rheinwg).
- **[AQ40]** C'thun's chaining beam mechanic: rather than Vanishing it, spreading and simply eating the tick (or walking away entirely from Dark Glare) is recommended, since Vanish timing is unreliable there — [[d:rogue#1435922156973199411]] (Toppings).
- **[general]** Darkmantle bead farming spot: DM North's "drunk ogre" miniboss (up to 3 beads, soloable) and its ogre-mage trash (packs of 2 or alone, ~7 beads/reset reported) — [[d:rogue#1435876864362156103]], [[d:rogue#1435955537756819466]].

## PvP
- **[general]** Cheap Shot kills most classes fast while leveling; Warriors/Paladins are tankier — [[d:rogue#1429203325043085403]] (Urkult). Assassination/Noxious Assault is considered the strongest matchup vs. plate ("turns paladin and warrior into a joke of a matchup"; a clip circulated of a T3.5 warrior losing to a level-59 rogue) — [[d:rogue#1432361094273306695]], [[d:rogue#1432361238083407902]] (Beldrin). Mages are considered a rogue's hardest matchup ("I'd rather fight 3 paladins than 1 really good mage") — [[d:rogue#1435542040669716510]] (Blindtongue).
- **[assassination, PvP weapon speed]** For PvP specifically, several experienced PvPers argue *slower*, harder-hitting weapons can be better than the PvE-optimal 1.3s, since rogues are glass cannons who rely on burst rather than sustain, and Improved Ambush synergizes with bigger hits — [[d:rogue#1433313528856580228]] (j0.j0), [[d:rogue#1433314294698737726]] (Nuian). Blindtongue (heavily-played PvP rogue) reports running a 1.8-speed AQ20 dagger for PvP and barely noticing a DPS difference vs. 1.3s on a dummy (~50 dps difference) once haste is stacked (his weapon effectively swings at ~1.55); he attributes most of the "1.3 is mandatory" sentiment to Dissolvent specifically and argues nerfing just Dissolvent (not weapon speed generally) would restore weapon-damage relevance without complex proc-normalization schemes — [[d:rogue#1436541136440393731]], [[d:rogue#1436543190046998670]], [[d:rogue#1436545324398415903]] — **disputed** by Rheinwg, who notes MC-tier weapons genuinely aren't upgrades over Alcor's even accounting for this.
- **[assassination/spellstrike, PvP]** BOED is considered strong for PvP too if you have a full spellstrike set, letting you PvP effectively without a pocket healer via its self-sustain proc; considered "gimmicky" but "huge" if achievable — [[d:rogue#1435144333140557834]], [[d:rogue#1435144584136233023]] (Suade).
- **[general]** Opener resists are a known, intentional-feeling frustration: roughly a "1 in 4 chance" to have your Cheap Shot/Kidney Shot/Sap resisted, which can wreck an opening combo, especially against Discipline Priests — [[d:rogue#1435740059524403291]] (0r5y), [[d:rogue#1435802502481182741]] (Blindtongue anecdote of 2 resists in one fight); general sentiment that RNG resists "feel bad" in PvP design — [[d:rogue#1435741046708633792]] (The Multiverse).
- **[combat, PvP]** Combat daggers with Perdition's Blade is claimed as a valid start for a PvP backstab build by Epickalen, though 0r5y is skeptical combat has enough utility for PvP — [[d:rogue#1433373898837327933]], [[d:rogue#1433375497915731978]]. GumGuzz's blunt summary: "if you want to actually do something against plates you go assassination, if you want to chainlock people and hit them with pillows you go sub; you don't even need Prep for assassination" — [[d:rogue#1432360854749184000]].
- **[PvP weapon choices]** Assassination PvP weapon progression examples: Julie's Dagger x2 → Bonescraper/Shadowblade/Rotmaw's Tooth/Stormfist tier → Alcor's Sunrazor; MH picks favoring some stamina/base damage (Rotmaw's Tooth) or speed vs. tanky targets (Bonescraper); Ripper considered best PvP OH "until Saph Claw" — [[d:rogue#1433458502461821099]], [[d:rogue#1433692944094986341]], [[d:rogue#1433693452100571147]] (Blindtongue). Weapon enchant for PvP debated: Crusader (burst window synergy with Cold Blood+Eviscerate) vs. Agility; some run double Crusader, others Crusader MH + Agility OH for a higher single-weapon proc rate — [[d:rogue#1435295797112602698]], [[d:rogue#1435297079315398696]] (Matt Lender) — **contested, no consensus**.
- **[macro]** OH weapon-swap macro to apply Crippling Poison on demand mid-fight (put the OH weapon in the last bag, top row, 3rd slot from left):
  ```
  /script if ( not CursorHasItem() ) then PickupContainerItem(4,3); PickupInventoryItem(17); PickupContainerItem(4,3); end
  ```
  — [[d:rogue#1433690897131900979]] (Blindtongue).
- **[general]** Racial notes for PvP: Escape Artist (Gnome) rated ahead of Perception (Human) for overall PvP value since it counters snares/roots broadly (especially vs. Mages), even though Perception gives 2% crit while active and helps spot stealthed enemies — [[d:rogue#1435541953206026311]], [[d:rogue#1435632812345524244]] (Blindtongue). Orc's stun-resist racial may have been changed to reduced stun duration instead of an outright resist chance — reported **uncertain/unconfirmed** by the posters themselves — [[d:rogue#1435671864314695772]], [[d:rogue#1435673496704520307]].
- **[general]** Grinding honor: BG-of-the-day dailies give ~2x honor and are the main "sweat" strategy; AB is considered the most reliable/efficient overall since it's guaranteed to end (WSG can drag on); world-PvP kills in capital cities can be comparable to BG honor/hour, with some ranks worth ~300-500+ honor per kill — [[d:rogue#1436309836454428703]] (riku), [[d:rogue#1436320129653936260]], [[d:rogue#1436320375314055269]] (Blindtongue). Solo/duel kills on higher-ranked players are called out as an easy 300-400 honor per first kill — [[d:rogue#1436310243075166351]] (riku).
- **[general]** Vs. tanky/geared Warlocks: Restoration potion is "the only" answer to sustained DoT damage; classic counter-play is killing their pet to force a reset, then chaining Cheap Shot/Kidney/Blind/Sap; Engineering's Shadow Reflector + Gouge/Vanish during their DoT channel is called a "big momentum gain" — [[d:rogue#1436600446537175050]], [[d:rogue#1436600877543723029]] (The Multiverse). Basic burst-and-reset combo: Ambush, Backstab, Thistle Tea, Backstab, Eviscerate, disengage — [[d:rogue#1436602176699895889]] (0r5y).

## Macros, addons, UI

- **[general]** mPowa is commonly used for tracking buffs/debuffs/combo points (add an aura, name it exactly after the buff/spell, tick "on enemy" for debuffs); alternatives mentioned: SimpleAuras, EzPoison (for poison-charge tracking — click to reapply from the addon rather than opening bags), Cursive (for IEA tracking specifically) — [[d:rogue#1430281445041246373]] (Nyx), [[d:rogue#1430282012668858408]] (Beldrin), [[d:rogue#1432467691423399980]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1433857936806903810]] (Treehide/Verrik).
- **[general]** dpsmate is a commonly-used DPS meter/addon suite mentioned repeatedly (with UI quirks like not being alphabetically sortable, and continuing to log outside combat unless configured) — [[d:rogue#1430098888911753246]]-[[d:rogue#1430108791310913557]] (Nyx/GumGuzz/TwistedMindz).
- **[general]** Cleveroid/Roid-Macros addon enables advanced conditional macros (e.g. unit-type checks, full rotations bound to one button, buff-count conditionals) — [[d:rogue#1431498857271001149]] (feeder5), [[d:rogue#1429240041577644186]] ("30").
- **[macros]** Pickpocket+Ambush: `/cast Pick Pocket` then `/cast Ambush` — [[d:rogue#1428856416621432872]] (Kahno). Stealth-spam without breaking stealth: bind Stealth to a numbered key (e.g. "1") on the main action bar, and bind your opener (e.g. Cheap Shot) to the *same* number on your stealth-only bar, so mashing that key stealths you first, then opens — [[d:rogue#1435282175929880719]], [[d:rogue#1435282280443547762]] (Zaszz); alternate simple macro: `/cast !Stealth` — [[d:rogue#1434199896126263478]]. Weapon-swap macros to open Ambush with a dagger then switch to a slow weapon for Sinister Strike are given in full in the Leveling section above.
- **[general]** `/db <item or mob name>` (a database-lookup slash command/addon) shows all matching drop locations/vendors on the map — [[d:rogue#1430639099181793362]] (GumGuzz).
- **[general]** RogueTick, Nug/NugEnergy, and NugCombo addons for energy-tick/combo-point tracking are all reported by various users as buggy/non-functional at various points in this chunk (double/inaccurate energy ticks, combo tracker not loading) — [[d:rogue#1434069890410483774]], [[d:rogue#1434193091857748112]], [[d:rogue#1436524373170327724]], [[d:rogue#1435148543005425774]] — no fix identified in-chunk; treat as a known/ongoing addon reliability issue on Turtle's client.

## Bugs & quirks
- **[general]** Stealth detection: NPCs lower level than the player reportedly still detect through 5/5 Improved Stealth + a stealth-bonus cloak — disputed as a positioning issue rather than a bug by GumGuzz (see Leveling section).
- **[general]** Gift of the Xavian (a Satyr self-heal) could not be interrupted with Kick despite appearing castable — explained as it being a non-magical "skill" rather than a spell, so it's uninterruptible by Kick regardless of lockout — [[d:rogue#1428845727768903791]], [[d:rogue#1428850856257655010]] (Im pro vise).
- **[general]** Client-side buff cap (~48 total buffs) causes SnD/Rupture to silently fail to refresh and Blade Flurry to become uncastable on heavily-buffed fights (e.g. Kruul); it's a known limitation "every class" hits, not fixable by devs, only worked around with addons/macros (see Cooldowns section for the macro) — [[d:rogue#1429244010089811999]]-[[d:rogue#1432560431230222469]] ("30"/Dalak).
- **[general]** Historical: a rogue exploited a "2-combo-point Eviscerate" bug combined with a brief, since-hotfixed spellpower-scaling bug on Agitating Poison, producing one outlier parse that got cited for months as evidence rogues "didn't need buffs" — [[d:rogue#1430987102413127700]] (Epickalen).
- **[assassination]** Noxious Assault's tooltip says it "strikes with both weapons," but visually/mechanically it uses one weak "wet noodle" animation reused from Mark of Death — flagged as a cosmetic bug people expect to eventually be fixed (unresolved as of this chunk) — [[d:rogue#1432803247009038531]] (Yale, +1), [[d:rogue#1432830796258873468]] (Rheinwg).
- **[assassination]** OBD's "Burning Hatred" proc was observed dealing a flat, suspiciously low 100 damage against target training dummies specifically (not against real raid bosses, where it worked normally) — reported by one player, but another tested it 2 days earlier and found it "fine," suggesting an intermittent or dummy-specific issue — [[d:rogue#1436201658752434279]], [[d:rogue#1436206891314319421]] (Suade/Purge, 2025-11-06/07).
- **[general]** Transmog: an off-hand-only weapon cannot be transmogged to/from a genuine one-handed weapon (they're mechanically different item types, "held in off-hand" items aren't real weapons) — [[d:rogue#1430810315783667722]], [[d:rogue#1430815471263023206]] (awry). Weapon models are not mirrored between hands (only translated), so visually-asymmetric weapons always look "correct" for only one hand — [[d:rogue#1430682643439026196]] (Tyrindre - Nordanaar).
- **[general]** Design quirk: it is currently a DPS *gain* for an assassination rogue when their Rupture gets dodged or resists (no CP lost) — explained by devs' intent to avoid CP-loss tanking IEA uptime on bleed-immune fights, but feels backwards (see Rotation section).

## Contested / open questions
- **[general]** Will Dissolvent Poison and/or Noxious Assault get nerfed/reworked? As of 2025-11-08 there's strong community expectation (driven by PvP outrage that "a rogue shouldn't melt a warrior") but "nothing anyone with real authority has mentioned" — pure speculation — [[d:rogue#1436289316790997002]], [[d:rogue#1436289879968452638]], [[d:rogue#1436290527933763746]].
- **[general]** Multiple concrete but mutually-exclusive community proposals were floated to fix Assassination's extreme weapon-speed dependence, none implemented: (a) Zaszz's proc-rate normalization around a baseline speed (e.g. 1.8) so faster weapons get slightly less poison-proc chance and slower weapons get more — worked math example given (60%→55% at 1.3 speed, 60%→70% at 2.8 speed) — [[d:rogue#1435370173342416907]]-[[d:rogue#1435373369494339744]]; (b) Rheinwg's simpler idea of scaling Noxious Assault's own damage by +10% per +0.10 weapon speed, tunable via one number without touching itemization or other specs — [[d:rogue#1436554220148101131]] (+1, well-received); (c) making Noxious Assault strike with both weapons Mutilate-style so weapon damage matters again (The Multiverse), disputed by Rheinwg as undermining the spec's whole "doesn't rely on weapon damage" identity; (d) simply speeding up a few existing daggers (Harbinger, Maexxna's Fang, Death's Sting) to 1.2-1.4 for more late-game progression milestones (Toppings). Blindtongue's minority view: just nerf Dissolvent directly and weapon damage becomes relevant again, no complex formula needed — [[d:rogue#1436545324398415903]], [[d:rogue#1436554602756702270]] (disputed by Rheinwg).
- **[general]** Exact Combat-vs-Assassination agility/gear crossover point remains unsettled: estimates range from "~600 agi" (early, vague) to a tested "650-700+ agi, dagger-quality-dependent" (Scryu) to "starts once you get 5pc AQ40" (GumGuzz) to "not really about agility at all" (Epickalen) — see Spec viability section for full citations.
- **[general]** Haste vs. Hit value given swing-timer/extra-swing mechanics — long unresolved argument, no consensus — [[d:rogue#1430079836839350342]]-[[d:rogue#1430089247154507847]].
