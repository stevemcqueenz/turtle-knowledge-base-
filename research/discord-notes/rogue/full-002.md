## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Assassination]** Considered extremely strong for low investment: "insane damage for requiring 0 gear or effort", but doesn't scale as well as combat as gear improves — [[d:rogue#1437508771839610973]] (𝓜𝓲𝓭𝓸𝓴𝓸, 2025-11-10). Benefit: gearing is forgiving — even a Sunrazor (cheap dagger) "can last you till naxx" for assa, unlike other specs — [[d:rogue#1438533349923619016]] (Drox, 2025-11-13).
- **[Combat daggers]** At Naxx-level BiS: ranking is Combat Daggers > Assassination > Combat Swords; Combat Daggers pull further ahead starting AQ40 — [[d:rogue#1438868946403987527]], [[d:rogue#1438533022717841419]] (GumGuzz/Drox, 2025-11-13).
- **[Sub]** Consensus: sub provides ~9-15% party-wide physical damage buff via 5-cp Improved Expose Armor (multiplicative with Sunder, not additive to 15%), making a single sub rogue a net raid DPS gain even though personal DPS lags combat/assa by ~100-200 dps in Naxx gear — [[d:rogue#1438510157213470811]], [[d:rogue#1438513230732460053]], [[d:rogue#1438511176320286855]], [[d:rogue#1438494399683367093]] (Nuian/Wells/𝓜𝓲𝓭𝓸𝓴𝓸, 2025-11-13). Only worth running ~1 sub per raid; bigger raids benefit more from the raid-wide buff — [[d:rogue#1438500576127221993]] (Nuian). Community perception is that few people actually run sub in Amber-phase raids because personal-DPS-focused players undervalue it — [[d:rogue#1438481343775375440]], [[d:rogue#1438485346072395951]] (MouseNo4/Nuian, 2025-11-13).
- **[Combat]** Some claim combat is essentially "for leveling only" (MouseNo4, disputed/opinion) vs it being the best endgame dps spec with daggers per others — [[d:rogue#1438491930412449913]] (2025-11-13); "combat after naxx/aq40 is just straight up better [than assassination]" — [[d:rogue#1438162563115257986]] (Weirdthing, 2025-11-12).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Assassination]** Core endgame loop: keep Slice and Dice, Envenom, and a 5-cp Rupture up at all times, using Noxious Assault as the builder (replacing Sinister Strike); typical order is 1cp SnD → 1cp Envenom → build to 5cp Rupture, cycling back to refresh buffs — [[d:rogue#1439968245800108082]], [[d:rogue#1439982127344128010]] (Praeco/feeder5, 2025-11-17). On normal (non-elite) mobs, 1cp Envenom + 5cp Eviscerate is used instead of Rupture; Rupture is reserved for elites/bosses — [[d:rogue#1439982127344128010]] (feeder5, 2025-11-17).
- **[Assassination]** Rupture is taken not for its own damage, but because it grants +15% physical damage and +15% Dissolvent Poison damage buff (via Taste for Blood) — [[d:rogue#1438911770822447237]] (GumGuzz, 2025-11-14). Taste for Blood is called "unequivocally the best talent" — between two equally-geared rogues, the one with it will out-dps — [[d:rogue#1439686330039664722]] (Heffey, 2025-11-16).
- **[Assassination PvP]** Blindtongue's opener/rotation: vs warriors/druids — Cheap Shot → Rupture → Envenom, sometimes Garrote → Envenom → SnD → Flourish; vs mages — Ambush → Noxious → Cold Blood → Eviscerate; general: ~80% of targets die to Cheap Shot → Rupture → Noxious → 2-3cp Kidney Shot combo, remainder finished off by Rupture ticking as they flee unless they have >4k hp — [[d:rogue#1436612876876906626]], [[d:rogue#1438684301591056414]], [[d:rogue#1436747051357442048]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-08/14).
- **[Assassination PvP]** In a stunlock, use Envenom twice after the first stun for burst; if the fight will run longer, build a full Kidney Shot first — [[d:rogue#1436746244780195952]], [[d:rogue#1436746354645925908]], [[d:rogue#1436746445544751306]] (Bakstabbath-Firemaw/Weirdthing, 2025-11-08).
- **[Combat]** Rotation keeps Taste for Blood/SnD up at all times, backstab-focused: BS → 5cp SnD → BS → 5cp Rupture → BS repeat, using Eviscerate opportunistically in between; some skip Eviscerate at 5cp entirely, others use it while keeping T4/relentless-strikes energy in mind (costs 10 energy but Relentless gives back 20) — [[d:rogue#1440975997993226250]], [[d:rogue#1440989820787626086]], [[d:rogue#1440855937509494947]] (limoseng/Drox/Rheinwg, 2025-11-19/20).
- **[Combat]** Alternate combat-dagger opening priority (Epickalen): 1pt Slice and Dice, 5pt Rupture, then cycle 5pt Slice, with occasional Eviscerate when efficient — [[d:rogue#1441007225882542170]] (2025-11-20).
- **[General]** For assassination, weapon speed matters more than raw weapon dps for the auto-attack/poison-proc engine: "you can pretty much use a 1.3 speed 30 dps dagger and it outperforms a 1.9 speed 66 dps dagger for assassin" — [[d:rogue#1438230065317019668]] (GumGuzz, 2025-11-12); confirmed further that even a fast low-level dagger with full talents/hit-cap will do respectable damage since poison/Noxious make up the bulk of damage, not weapon auto damage — [[d:rogue#1438254910910238811]] (andre, 2025-11-12).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General]** PvE hit caps: 8% hit for special abilities (yellow hit cap), 25% for autoattacks (white hit cap) — [[d:rogue#1441063728538718269]] (Rheinwg, 2025-11-20). PvP hit cap for abilities is ~5%+ depending on target's defense — [[d:rogue#1441067288668274709]] (Rheinwg, 2025-11-20).
- **[General]** Weapon skill gives +0.2% hit per point of weapon skill in addition to reducing glancing blows — [[d:rogue#1441180786555621477]] (GumGuzz/30, 2025-11-21); weapon skill worth ~1-2% dps for combat during boss fights but less important for assassination since autos are a small % of its damage — [[d:rogue#1438186902619685118]] (Rheinwg, 2025-11-12).
- **[General]** Recommended hit progression: aim for ~15% hit while in T1-era gear (not necessarily pre-raid), because you "cannot crit on missed hits" — raising hit to 25% hits an "effective crit cap" of ~55% due to diminishing returns; beyond that, prioritize agility/crit/haste — [[d:rogue#1441229037547552778]] (Epickalen, 2025-11-21).
- **[Assassination]** No agility "soft cap" for energy regen via Blade Rush exists; at very high agi (e.g. 600+) the practical issue becomes overflowing energy from Sinister-Strike-style low energy costs plus procs (AQ20 trinket, Relentless Strikes, T3 set, shaman totems), not the stat itself being capped — [[d:rogue#1440809376116899870]], [[d:rogue#1440811745697595544]], [[d:rogue#1440825557783941130]] (Snikrot/Pilvento & Rheinwg, 2025-11-20). Backstab/daggers don't have this overflow problem as much since Backstab spends more energy per use than Sinister Strike — [[d:rogue#1440825994088022156]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Assassination]** Blade Rush agi-to-energy conversion: roughly 400 agi per 0.5s tick is needed to average 30 energy per 2 average (i.e., agility scaling of energy regen is per-tick, not a flat "regen X per point") — [[d:rogue#1437274916578922548]] (Epickalen, 2025-11-10). Approximate AP-equivalent value of agility while specced into Blade Rush is ~3.4 AP per point of agi (per "trustworthy sources"); special stats (crit/haste/etc.) are believed to be roughly equivalent to ~25 AP each — [[d:rogue#1437253577168126013]], [[d:rogue#1437252122042306611]] (elyina, 2025-11-10; single source, not independently verified in chunk).
- **[Poisons]** Poison damage scales at 5% of AP and can crit from spell crit; spell crit chance from any source (even from int? unclear) affects poison procs, and this is stated to be intentional/longstanding, not a bug scheduled for a fix — [[d:rogue#1438907292656341096]], [[d:rogue#1440775207647318119]] (GumGuzz, 2025-11-14/19). Spell power/nature spell damage does NOT affect poison damage — [[d:rogue#1440909547320377445]] (Rheinwg, 2025-11-19); nature spell damage explicitly confirmed to do nothing for poisons.
- **[Weapon math]** GumGuzz's napkin math for weapon-speed dps loss from poisons (assassination): Dissolvent Poison ~161 avg dmg, Envenom buff ~209 avg dmg, poison proc chance (with envenom+talents) ~60%; average dmg per hit ≈ 206×0.6=123; at 1.0 speed that's 123 dps from poisons, so each 0.1 speed increase costs roughly ~12 dps from poisons (e.g., 1.3 speed ≈ 94.6 dps from poison, 1.4 speed is ~7.3 dps less than 1.3) — [[d:rogue#1437156474810794097]], [[d:rogue#1437160606150033535]] (GumGuzz, 2025-11-09).
- **[Weapon choice]** Off-hand weapon choice math (Assassination): swapping a Qiraji Sacrificial Dagger for a Distracting Dagger loses only ~8 dps in raw weapon dps but gains 0.60 speed, less glancing penalty, and +1.2 hit — net positive since offhand dps matters less than speed for poison procs — [[d:rogue#1436839848047808583]], [[d:rogue#1436840048824815828]] (Rheinwg, 2025-11-09). Swapping to a fast offhand from a slower one nets roughly +40 dps for assassination once accounting for poison procs — [[d:rogue#1436965766720262266]] (GumGuzz, 2025-11-09).

T3 belt preferred over C'thun belt (despite C'thun's higher AP/hit/crit) because T3's agi is amplified by the Blade Rush talent and the belt is needed to hold the T3 4-piece bonus — [[d:rogue#1437254595884617728]], [[d:rogue#1437254920221757563]], [[d:rogue#1437255170399408241]] (Ryker/elyina, 2025-11-10); maintain at least 4pc T3 even in full Kara gear (achievable via ring, belt, bracers + one more slot) — [[d:rogue#1437255405754519552]] (elyina).
- **[Naxx loot priority]** Kingsfall (dagger) is a very strong upgrade for any dagger rogue and generally worth prioritizing to rogues over an "equal standing" warrior since the rogue gets more relative benefit; Maexxna's Fang is a strong backup if Kingsfall is contested — [[d:rogue#1437264413177548892]], [[d:rogue#1437263884556828702]], [[d:rogue#1437264277856587847]] (Treehide/Verrik & elyina, 2025-11-10). Note: warriors also have a DPS version of the T3-tier ring now (unlike vanilla), increasing competition for that item — [[d:rogue#1437263131184201891]], [[d:rogue#1437263884556828702]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2025-11-10).
- **[Combat daggers BiS weapon]** Backstab damage is stated as uniquely benefiting from slow daggers — no other DPS spec wants slow daggers as BiS, so combat daggers face less weapon competition from assassination (which wants fast daggers) though hunters and some warriors may compete for the same stat-stick daggers — [[d:rogue#1440882231789883412]], [[d:rogue#1440890634385887232]], [[d:rogue#1440891930622951425]], [[d:rogue#1440894018459533384]] (Snikrot/Romvlvs/Im pro vise/Epickalen, 2025-11-20).
- **[Weapon-specific ranking, Assassination]** Rheinwg's suggested weapon tier fixes/opinions: Core Hound Tooth, Dragonfang, Death Sting, Pugio should rank above Alcor's; Bloodmoon should be on par with or only slightly worse than THC/Sapphiron fist; Perdition's Blade has "basically 0 stats" and is considered weak despite good speed; Crystal Sword of the Blossom is "fast but too slow for assa" relative to alternatives — [[d:rogue#1441073564114616360]], [[d:rogue#1441074128244445204]], [[d:rogue#1441073747678335076]], [[d:rogue#1441074293730578573]] (2025-11-20; single source/opinion).
- **[Pre-raid weapon]** Cited assassination pre-raid weapon combo options: Alcor's Razor + Distracting Dagger most commonly recommended; budget options are Julie's Dagger or a white 1.3-speed poniard from a weapon vendor (small dps loss vs Alcor's, but very cheap) — [[d:rogue#1437420255180750921]], [[d:rogue#1438127743643422835]] (Foreseen/Andrion, 2025-11-11/12).
- **[Gearing niche]** For a spirit-based "outside-combat regen" gear set (non-combat sustain vs DoTs after fights), Blindtongue compiled a list including gear from Sunken Temple (Jammal'an the Prophet, 33% drop rate for BiS chest and pants) and Hinterlands quest reward; achieved 209 spirit for ~105hp/sec regen while moving, noted as far better than stacking hp5 for out-of-combat sustain — [[d:rogue#1437672028445479033]], [[d:rogue#1438005578063548509]], [[d:rogue#1438038434328612884]], [[d:rogue#1437903534367445002]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-11/12; video demo posted, +7 reactions on a related post).
- **[PvP gear]** Suggested Alliance/Horde-agnostic PvP set: ~2pc PvP set (helm+shoulder for stamina, or AB reputation set) + 4pc Dungeon Master (DM) + mix of raid gear for the remaining slots, or simpler "4 DM / 4 Stormshroud" combo — cited to work well regardless of faction — [[d:rogue#1438169666760478791]], [[d:rogue#1438213395861078097]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-13). PvP set bonus itself (2pc, e.g. -1 sec Gouge cooldown) is considered weak/not worth chasing for its own sake — [[d:rogue#1438213395861078097]] (Blindtongue). Arathi Basin 3-piece armor set gives 9% crit-damage-taken reduction and is recommended for PvP alongside 2pc R12/13 honor gear (+3%) — [[d:rogue#1438333936446078977]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-13).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Poisons]** Standard poison loadout by content: Assassination — double Dissolvent Poison always (scales with Taste for Blood, ignores armor, no enemy immune) — [[d:rogue#1436631359371088003]], [[d:rogue#1439635372249452647]] (Heffey/Pandemon). Combat — Deadly Poison (MH) + Corrosive Poison (OH) for raid bosses (long fights, low Taste for Blood uptime), Dissolvent for trash — [[d:rogue#1436637888455376927]], [[d:rogue#1437770209879654473]] (Heffey/Weirdthing, 2025-11-08/11). Leveling/soloing/questing — double Instant Poison is standard until Dissolvent unlocks at 52 — [[d:rogue#1436638252793597992]], [[d:rogue#1439635662163939378]] (Heffey/Pandemon).
- **[Poisons by instance]** Use Instant Poison (not Dissolvent) specifically in dungeons/raids where mobs resist nature damage: Stratholme Living side, ~90% of BRD (minus elementals), Onyxia, and Dire Maul North; use Dissolvent in Molten Core (nature immune trash everywhere), Scholomance, Dire Maul West, etc. — [[d:rogue#1439635180808573048]], [[d:rogue#1439635372249452647]] (Pandemon, 2025-11-16; single source, seconded loosely by Rheinwg who still argues Dissolvent+TfB usually wins on raw damage even accounting for this — [[d:rogue#1439636672235966614]]).
- **[Poisons, general]** Deadly Poison (DoT) is essentially never used by assassination — no scenario where a DoT beats the instant-proc damage model; also disliked for PvP since it prevents reliably landing Blind/CC follow-ups and conflicts with wanting burst — [[d:rogue#1438929219903164508]], [[d:rogue#1439636026275663924]] (andre/Pandemon, 2025-11-14/16).
- **[Poisons, mechanics]** Poisons apply only on a landed weapon hit (auto or special that swings); a miss means no poison proc chance; on a hit there's a base ~60% chance to proc, boosted by Envenom (+30% chance and +30% damage/effectiveness) and further by Vile Poisons talent — [[d:rogue#1438187334817550456]], [[d:rogue#1439004633275306108]] (Rheinwg/GumGuzz, 2025-11-12/15). Envenom's "+30% effectiveness" also boosts non-damage poison effects like Crippling Poison's speed reduction and Mind-Numbing Poison's cast-speed slow, not just damage — [[d:rogue#1439004843455938671]], [[d:rogue#1439005032501477376]] (Rheinwg, 2025-11-15).
- **[PvP poisons]** Blindtongue's PvP poison setup: 2x Instant if not running Taste for Blood, 2x Dissolvent if running TfB + using Rupture a lot; Crippling Poison best applied via a weapon-swap macro; Mind-Numbing rarely used since clothies die too fast for it to matter (better vs. heavy-caster comps or 1v1); Wound Poison recommended over Mind-Numbing specifically against healers in BGs — [[d:rogue#1437561858071461908]], [[d:rogue#1437562156995313716]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-11).
- **[Enchants]** Weapon enchant standard: Crusader on main-hand, and either Agility (~15 agi) or a second Crusader on off-hand for assassination; double Crusader is viable and only sacrifices the agility if not leaning on melee crit/dodge/energy regen — [[d:rogue#1437965692472397866]], [[d:rogue#1438078584747986944]] (Clevvy/Epickalen, 2025-11-12/13).
- **[Enchants]** Poisons and weapon enchants (permanent) can coexist — a permanent weapon enchant (e.g. Crusader) is separate from the temporary poison/sharpening-stone buff slot; poison and sharpening stone share the SAME buff slot, so you must choose one or the other per weapon — [[d:rogue#1437935208790757519]], [[d:rogue#1437935476735611001]] (GumGuzz/Treehide/Verrik, 2025-11-12).
- **[Item procs]** Annihilator's proc rate is stated as a flat 5% chance (not a fixed PPM as widely rumored); Turtle WoW is said not to use a standardized PPM system for most procs — [[d:rogue#1437064793277337701]], [[d:rogue#1437067202774831188]] (Hogbobson/GumGuzz, 2025-11-09). Noxious Assault does not proc on-hit weapon effects (Crusader, Windfury) and does not itself proc your poisons beyond the poison procs explicitly listed in its own tooltip — [[d:rogue#1438093852895608832]] (GumGuzz, 2025-11-13). Ironfoe/its Horde-side twin's guaranteed extra attacks do NOT proc poisons — [[d:rogue#1440566590226698315]], [[d:rogue#1440576605041983640]] (Final_Ranger/GumGuzz, 2025-11-19; single source).
- **[Poisons, endgame boss choice]** By-boss poison exceptions from Rheinwg (Combat/Sub): default is Deadly+Corrosive for most bosses, but Dissolvent is better on fights under ~1 minute, and specific fights called out as exceptions where Dissolvent (or dissolvent variants) are preferred: Garr, Geddon, Ragnaros, Razorgore, Skeram, Viscidus, C'Thun-twins("Twins"), Gothik, Chess event, Rupturan (Kara40) — [[d:rogue#1444184207319498773]], [[d:rogue#1444184552263389184]] (2025-11-29). Socia adds nuance: for adds/off-target burn (e.g. Meph Shards, Gnarlmoon Owls), faster off-target kill speed (favoring Dissolvent) can matter more for raid DPS than personal single-target DPS — [[d:rogue#1444165455626371172]] (2025-11-29).
- **[Item, dagger]** Dagger of Veiled Shadows (BoP, Rogue-only, one-hand dagger): 69-130 dmg, 1.80 speed (55.3 dps), +15 Agility, +7 Strength, "Improves your chance to hit by 1%." Part of "Emblems of Veiled Shadows" set with Band of Veiled Shadows and Cloak of Veiled Shadows: (2) set = -10 energy cost on Slice and Dice; (3) set = 25% chance on successful poison application to deal 80 Nature damage — [[d:rogue#1443070874675708086]] (Rheinwg, 2025-11-27; item tooltip screenshot). GumGuzz's guild reports their top DPS rogue in Naxx progression uses this dagger + Distracting Dagger OH while running full AQ20 set — [[d:rogue#1442106375164661833]] (2025-11-23).
- **[Item, general]** Tempest's Rage tested in the off-hand for assassination and found disappointing — proc rate felt "almost nonexistent," resulting in a measured ~15% dps loss compared to using a Julie's Dagger instead — [[d:rogue#1442409907285393500]] (Im pro vise, 2025-11-24; single source/personal test).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[World boss]** Admiral Bareon Westwind (Scarlet Enclave world boss): does an instant-cast Frostbolt that can crit for up to ~3.3k (only once the boss is below ~80% hp) and a 2-sec-cast Volley; recommend a Frost Protection Potion, interrupting/kicking or stunning to shut down the Cone of Cold-style spike, and popping Evasion+Flourish defensively — soloable by a well-built leech/vampirism assassination rogue; one soloist used ~15% leech and 2 Greater Fire Protection Potions timed at pull and 50% hp — [[d:rogue#1439921419403137125]], [[d:rogue#1439921457881682032]], [[d:rogue#1440236531611598940]], [[d:rogue#1440255768250028054]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-17/18).
- **[Dungeon solo]** HFQ (Hateforge Quarry) Foreman: soloable with ~30% physical mitigation + ~4% lifesteal per one attempt; a roaming "Overseer" mob patrols and can add to the fight — best pulled/killed separately on the staircase coming down from the Foreman, or behind the tower, before engaging the Foreman itself; time Evasion to cover his hardest-hitting window and use 4-combo-point Ghostly Strike into Flourish for burst — [[d:rogue#1439093300333117521]], [[d:rogue#1439093395338301471]], [[d:rogue#1439100409389121637]], [[d:rogue#1439100693355958313]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-17).
- **[Dungeon solo]** Damian (Stormwind Vault) can be soloed with ~8% vampirism plus a few points into Parry/Riposte; strategy: open with Ghostly Strike + Flourish, then Evasion, repeating defensives; keep your back to a wall when he goes stealth to avoid his Ambush. One player reported farming him 45 times solo for The Ripper this way — [[d:rogue#1442034294725017610]] (BDL, 2025-11-22).
- **[Dungeon mechanics]** Soloing Stormwind Vault (SWV) is described as buggy/inconsistent — sometimes you can sneak through stealthed with no aggro, other times mobs "teleport through a wall" and knock you out of stealth; players note it's hard to reach the last 2 of 5 bosses without a long Vanish cooldown wait, and consider using Invisibility Potions + Sprint as an alternative traversal method — [[d:rogue#1442684537443455088]], [[d:rogue#1442688035165831170]] (Zllig, 2025-11-21).
- **[Dungeon]** Maraudon Princess (Theradras) fast-reset method for repeated BoED/dagger farming: create/use a second account's character, group with your main, kill Princess, transfer party lead to the alt, log out on your main character, reset the instance on the alt (as leader) and kick the main, then log back into the main which now spawns at the instance entrance for a fresh lockout — [[d:rogue#1443339738831519824]], [[d:rogue#1443343850650206310]] (GumGuzz/Najven, 2025-11-27; Najven's variant: only the alt needs to stay leader the whole time — reset instance on the alt after logging out on main, no need to re-invite). Suggested farm route while at it for gold: kill Goblin, Princess, then jump down and kill the Crocolisk before resetting, netting ~10g per run — [[d:rogue#1443343850650206310]] (Najven, 2025-11-27).
- **[Naxx/Kara40]** In active Naxx progression, one guild reports ALL their rogues still run Assassination (not combat) even at that gear level, with weapon choices varying: Dagger of Veiled Shadows + Distracting Dagger OH (full AQ20 set), 2x Bonescrapper (Strat Baron drop) while stacking AP, or Death's Sting — consensus is OBD (Ornate Bloodstone Dagger) should be the best-performing weapon for assassination since it also scales with spell power, at the cost of a self-damage drawback — [[d:rogue#1442106375164661833]], [[d:rogue#1442113862605344769]] (GumGuzz, 2025-11-23).
- **[Kara40 BiS]** Cited combat-dagger Kara40 BiS daggers: Riftcarver/Kingsfall, or Riftcarver/Bloodmoon Sickle if playing an Orc (to make use of the Orc racial's extra AP/hit/weapon skill on offhand) — [[d:rogue#1442177536774902001]], [[d:rogue#1439394765866143846]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2025-11-19/23).
- **[Raid mechanic]** In C'Thun encounter (P2), his back faces the room entrance, making it awkward to consistently land Backstab — noted as a specific tracking/positioning challenge for dagger rogues — [[d:rogue#1438588105413230624]], [[d:rogue#1438588312284827680]], [[d:rogue#1438588364029820998]] (Majo/Beldrin, 2025-11-13).
- **[Raid mechanic]** Kara40 Rook (final boss if done last) can spawn raid-wiping traps — mentioned as a known danger in that fight — [[d:rogue#1441154871288926368]] (Refrigerador, 2025-11-20).
- **[Dungeon]** Pickpocket lockbox farming route through BRD (per Nuian): Bael'Gar → Incendius → Fineous → back to Incendius → arena spectator area → Pyromancer → Angerforge → bar area (skip the bar mobs themselves, they drop only Sturdy not Heavy Junkboxes) → lava skip to smelt Dark Iron ore found along the way and reset with an alt; nets ~30 heavy junkboxes per run — [[d:rogue#1441234944184680590]], [[d:rogue#1441235046118981682]], [[d:rogue#1441235726514655242]] (Nuian, 2025-11-21).
- **[Dungeon mechanic]** Pickpocket loot on dungeon mobs does NOT refresh just by waiting — mobs must be killed or the instance reset (or a full weekly reset) to reset lootable pockets; however, some players report loot pockets can refresh after ~15-20 minutes even without a kill/reset in specific cases (unconfirmed exception) — [[d:rogue#1442021488449556571]], [[d:rogue#1442109241069408328]] (Epickalen/Jay, 2025-11-22; disputed/unclear).

## PvP
- **[Assassination PvP]** Considered the current king of PvP; only real trouble is warlocks (esp. via Fear/DoTs while wearing Will of the Forsaken counters), per Blindtongue: "rogue king of pvp atm... unless you facing a lip popping warlock then you can go fuck yourself" — [[d:rogue#1439698433916010516]], [[d:rogue#1439698523766657025]] (2025-11-17).
- **[Assassination PvP]** Sample deep-assassination burst sequence vs geared (r12-14) targets: Cheap Shot → Noxious → Kidney Shot → Envenom, then Thistle Tea if they trinket the kidney, then Vanish → Cheap Shot again → Noxious into a 5cp Cold Blood Eviscerate — described as capable of killing most rank 12-14-geared players within/just after a single stunlock unless they Fear Ward/Lichborne (undead priest/DK-style immunities weren't in this game, likely meaning "fap" = Fists of Fury/Free Action Potion, or "lip" = Lifeblood/other CC-breaks) — [[d:rogue#1442823947702894734]], [[d:rogue#1442825712544252024]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-25).
- **[PvP]** DoTs (esp. from casters) are called the rogue's "greatest enemy" in PvP — there is no Cloak-of-Shadows-equivalent ability; the only counters are Rejuvenating-type potions that periodically strip DoTs, or building a spirit-regen gear-swap set for fast recovery between fights — [[d:rogue#1437279812254175253]], [[d:rogue#1437279853857738793]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-10).
- **[PvP gearing]** A dedicated spirit-based swap set (achieving 209 spirit) gives ~105 hp/sec passive regen even out of combat/while moving — used for surviving DoTs after fights, sustaining while carrying flags in WSG, and defending bases in AB; paired with Cannibalize it can take a rogue from 250hp to full in about 8 seconds — [[d:rogue#1437672028445479033]], [[d:rogue#1438212303572172870]], [[d:rogue#1438212863339790417]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-11/13).
- **[PvP, AV]** Rogue tactics for Alterac Valley vary by build: stealth-capping bunkers/towers behind enemy lines to force the enemy to split defense; solo-killing/kiting elite graveyard guards to cap graveyards; picking off stray reinforcements between the lines; or (Blindtongue's favorite, "~100% win rate so far") rushing the enemy's turn-in NPC (reinforcement/upgrade quartermaster) to deny their unit upgrades — requires a tanky build and a spot to pull the NPC away from interruption since it takes ~30 sec to kill; the "mine turn-in" NPC is non-elite/easy, while the Horde-side "Deepforge" armor-scrap NPC is a ~33k hp elite that hits hard but is stunnable, pullable by pulling it between the fort and the mountain, and sometimes drags 2 guards with it (Evasion + kill the guards first) — [[d:rogue#1441309314051735603]], [[d:rogue#1441309516632293468]], [[d:rogue#1441309769720795137]], [[d:rogue#1441364383145001021]], [[d:rogue#1441364621771673600]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-21).
- **[PvP poisons]** In a stunlock rotation, Cheap Shot → Rupture → Envenom is Blindtongue's default; ~80% of targets die to Cheap Shot → Rupture → Noxious → 2-3cp Kidney Shot without needing a full stunlock, with Rupture often finishing off targets who flee at low hp unless they have >4k hp remaining — [[d:rogue#1436612876876906626]], [[d:rogue#1436747051357442048]], [[d:rogue#1436612926029955192]] (Blindtongue, 2025-11-08).
- **[PvP wishlist / contested]** Blindtongue considers Sap's 65-energy cost too high for PvP, arguing it prevents opening immediately in 1v2 scenarios and that Improved Sap is overrated for battlegrounds since geared opponents will just eat Poison Cleansing Food anyway (single opinion, not universally endorsed) — [[d:rogue#1443806889287618723]], [[d:rogue#1443807081760292964]] (2025-11-28).
- **[PvP build]** A hybrid Combat/Assassination PvP dagger build (21 Assassination / 27 Combat / 3 Subtlety points) was shared as "what I've been running" by GumGuzz in response to a PvP dagger-build request; exact talent choices not transcribable from the icon-only screenshot beyond the point split — [[d:rogue#1437747522343796767]] (2025-11-08; image).
- **[PvP feedback]** Rheinwg critiquing a submitted PvP hybrid build: on Combat, grab 2/2 Blade Rush (was missing, losing energy); Hack and Slash is weak if your only sword is in the offhand — replace with Improved Sprint or Improved Kick; on Subtlety, Blackjack rated more useful than Blinding Haze; and warns Hemorrhage does poor damage with a dagger if combining backstab talents with it — a straight Assassination build is likely to outperform a scattered hybrid for most players — [[d:rogue#1442274935316942859]], [[d:rogue#1442275140724461790]] (2025-11-24).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Races]** Alliance race ranking for rogue (Treehide/Verrik's opinion): Night Elf > High Elf > Human, avoid Gnome/Dwarf if racials matter; Night Elf has higher base agility, +1% haste/movespeed passive, and bonus rep gains; High Elf overtakes Nelf agi only at very endgame (buffed); Human's main draw is Sword and Mace weapon skill, "not really meta atm" — [[d:rogue#1444045067357786264]], [[d:rogue#1444045282613661786]], [[d:rogue#1444045551967670339]], [[d:rogue#1444045946194235474]] (2025-11-28).
- **[Races]** Horde race notes: Orc rogue racial (Blood Fury as an extra active DPS cooldown) is called "really good"; Undead liked for Will of the Forsaken (fear immunity, big vs. warlocks) and gets +2% damage to Humanoid/Undead in Naxx specifically; Goblin gets +5 weapon skill to daggers (engineering-flavored racial) which is called good for PvE dps; base agility at level 60: Night Elf 135, Goblin/Troll/High Elf 132 (Alliance-only comparisons, small overall impact except for extreme min-maxing or leveling-dodge stacking) — [[d:rogue#1440118820781162537]], [[d:rogue#1443287709652357191]], [[d:rogue#1440093573277286473]], [[d:rogue#1443258506693054556]], [[d:rogue#1443258775220781218]], [[d:rogue#1443259241820455014]] (Heffey/Rheinwg/Kyrsie/Scruff, 2025-11-18/26).
- **[Professions]** Engineering for rogues is considered mostly a PvP/utility profession, not a raw-dps pick: main value is Goblin Sapper Charges, plus wormholes (Org wormhole "is BiS" for travel), XL jumper cables, parachutes, on-demand stuns, extra invis, and cheap underwater breathing; ~90% of the practical value comes from sapper charges specifically — [[d:rogue#1436971401914613790]], [[d:rogue#1436973147667697807]], [[d:rogue#1436982001352573061]] (Epickalen/GumGuzz/andre, 2025-11-09). Engineering is rated the profession for "damage" among gold-making choices, though its gold value is described as "meta" but "nowhere near impactful" — [[d:rogue#1442601364839727358]], [[d:rogue#1442601477607653580]] (Rheinwg/Epickalen, 2025-11-24).
- **[Professions]** Weapon skill quest books (not just for Warrior/Hunter — usable by Rogues in Turtle WoW) cost ~250g, require killing a specific dungeon boss, and grant +5 weapon skill for the completed weapon type; getting weapon skill to ~315 (the Turtle WoW weapon skill cap) recovers nearly all glancing-blow damage loss, with anything 310-315 considered "usually okay" — [[d:rogue#1441862950620237874]], [[d:rogue#1441871810529595402]], [[d:rogue#1441871990750183555]] (Epickalen, 2025-11-21).
- **[Gold-making]** Best rogue gold-making methods per community consensus: disenchanting (leveling Enchanting to at least skill 1, then DE dungeon-boss greens/blues while running content — called "the most simple reliable way for rogue to make money," especially valuable on 50-60 greens that can proc Greater Eternal Essence) — [[d:rogue#1440513896049016936]], [[d:rogue#1440512478256693269]]; solo farming ZG for arcanite/mining (~100-120g/hr) — [[d:rogue#1440323203032682681]], [[d:rogue#1440512478256693269]] (Purge, 2025-11-19); rogue and Survival Hunter cited as the two best gold-farming classes overall on Turtle WoW — [[d:rogue#1440323203032682681]], [[d:rogue#1440591673053679736]] (Purge, 2025-11-19). BRD pickpocketing for raw gold specifically is called low-value/not worth it despite reputation as a rogue gold method — [[d:rogue#1440475227720126636]], [[d:rogue#1440512329543454752]] (MouseNo4/Purge, 2025-11-19); it's better suited for farming a specific BoE weapon (e.g. Alcor's) than for pure gold.
- **[Gold-making]** Selling on the AH: no cut is taken when an item successfully sells (only the up-front deposit, which is lost if the auction expires unsold) — [[d:rogue#1441575854869057656]] (Epickalen, 2025-11-22).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Bug]** A quest-reward dagger from the Alliance Balor camp questline ("Kinrial's Scalpel," obtainable ~level 32-36) was found to have bugged/mismatched stats — database and in-game white-damage range implied ~50-63 dps (endgame-tier) rather than its intended low-level value; confirmed by observing white-hit damage exceeding the tooltip's stated top-end (over 54 dmg on a listed 28-134 range); a GM ticket did not result in a fix and the GM reportedly didn't recognize the bug; later observed the item was hotfixed down from ~63 dps to ~51 dps but remained far above intended level-appropriate values — [[d:rogue#1441813652742344816]], [[d:rogue#1443811938269139055]], [[d:rogue#1443814183371276381]], [[d:rogue#1443869220499881984]], [[d:rogue#1443883338187145396]] (Tater Salad/Blindtongue/Dodging/Soju, 2025-11-21/28).
- **[Bug]** A "x100%" red-font display bug sometimes appears on the character sheet after losing raid buffs — cosmetic/visual only (a floating-point display error); fixable by relogging, `/reload`, or a zone change/loading screen — [[d:rogue#1443820360314392667]], [[d:rogue#1443821228468076615]], [[d:rogue#1443856294468653287]] (Tater Salad/Blindtongue/Epickalen, 2025-11-28).

- **[Quirk]** Stealth detection by NPCs feels inconsistent even when stacking many "improved stealth" items (capes, rings, boots, trinkets) — some players report no discernible difference in patrol/guard detection ranges regardless of stealth-level gear stacked, particularly against Undead patrols in Stratholme, though it appears to work reliably for at least one other rogue observed staying undetected — [[d:rogue#1436795935999918274]], [[d:rogue#1436796399391084617]], [[d:rogue#1436797996921655386]], [[d:rogue#1436803273725317333]] (wasik/Blindtongue, 2025-11-08; anecdotal/unconfirmed).
