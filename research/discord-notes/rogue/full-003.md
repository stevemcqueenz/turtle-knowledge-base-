## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[assassination]** Stacking poisons (dissolvent/corrosive, physical-damage-amp interaction) received a "scaling fix" ~weeks before 2025-12-01 giving roughly +150 DPS; widely seen as the reason Assassination is currently dominant — [[d:rogue#1444626328547627250]], [[d:rogue#1445057095291506698]] (2025-12-01, single-source claim, disputed nerf-target discussion follows).
- **[assassination]** Community speculation (not confirmed by devs) that the eventual balance fix will be "physical damage amp no longer applies to dissolvent/corrosive," predicted to cost Assassination ~1-2% DPS since other specs use it less — [[d:rogue#1445057264867344465]], [[d:rogue#1445809510110462053]], [[d:rogue#1445809997803028491]] (2025-12-01/12-03; contested/unconfirmed).
- **[general]** Weapon skill mechanic on Turtle differs from vanilla: hit-from-weapon-skill scales linearly instead of vanilla's breakpoint system — [[d:rogue#1445469946992922704]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-02).
- **[general]** +5 weapon skill per weapon type purchasable via quest/training for 250g each — [[d:rogue#1445470354154979529]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-02).
- **[general, Turtle-specific]** Ruthlessness on Turtle gives a 100% chance to generate a combo point (vs. vanilla's 60% chance), effectively giving a "permanent" free combo point whenever the talent is taken — explicitly called out as a Turtle change, enabling the combo-point-transfer pull/opener tech described earlier — [[d:rogue#1451736486822154462]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-20).
- **[general hit]** Confirmed Turtle changed weapon-skill/hit formula "about 1 year ago": in vanilla there was an extra flat 1% hit suppression baked in (hence old guides recommending 9% hit), but on Turtle the real requirement is 8% — [[d:rogue#1451951116387352758]], [[d:rogue#1451951212378193941]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-20/21).
- **[general]** Murder talent (bonus damage vs. specific target types) does not affect Undead or Demons, making it largely useless for most of Naxxramas and Karazhan40 content since those are heavily undead/demon-populated — [[d:rogue#1450579595215638688]] (Blackjack, 2025-12-17).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[assassination vs combat]** Assassination (Noxious Assault + dual fast weapons) is the strongest leveling and endgame PvE spec through most of the current raid tier; consensus is it remains ahead of Combat until roughly Naxxramas/late AQ gear, with the exact breakpoint contested — [[d:rogue#1445164536998137911]] (Beldrin: "assa is competitive until early aq40"), [[d:rogue#1445175838973628416]] (Zaszz: "combat starts to scale better around early nax, late aq agi gear levels"), [[d:rogue#1445335012604903541]] (Andrion: assa better than combat until naxx even pre-1.8), [[d:rogue#1445871816596787422]] (Rheinwg: "data shows Assa is still king even on AQ40" citing South America realm which is further progressed), [[d:rogue#1446235902492807301]]/[[d:rogue#1446237389398868144]] (Treehide/Verrik: "Combat daggers in nax+ is the strongest dps in the game").
- **[combat]** Backstab/dagger Combat becomes the top rogue DPS spec once gear reaches Naxx/Kara40 level and high agility is available (~400+ agi buffed); top combat rogues stack agility and go "Blade Rush maxxing" — [[d:rogue#1446754212301242409]], [[d:rogue#1446759184350842992]], [[d:rogue#1447529821457481839]] (2025-12-04/12-08).
- **[combat]** Sword/Axe Combat (non-dagger) is viable but weaker than dagger combat; best usable axe/sword is worse than best dagger, and the "Hack and Slash" (axe/sword) side of the combat tree is seen as a trap/requires committing to daggers otherwise — [[d:rogue#1444847421942136893]], [[d:rogue#1444856361296072714]], [[d:rogue#1444848143106834432]] (Rheinwg/Romu, 2025-12-01).
- **[assassination]** No single-target boss should realistically be fully poison-immune in current content (Naxx); poison immunity is rare — only ooze/slime add-mechanics or specific undead resistances matter, and stacking poisons (dissolvent/corrosive) work on undead unlike standard nature poison — [[d:rogue#1446241221520523469]], [[d:rogue#1446242894980714506]] (Lightemgee/Treehide, 2025-12-05).
- **[sub]** Subtlety is described as a support/utility spec now (Expose Armor + Shadow Mark buffs raid AP/SP), considered the hardest spec to play well, valued by raids but few people choose to main it — [[d:rogue#1446231812920578129]] (GumGuzz), [[d:rogue#1445527877717331968]] (Lightemgee: "30% of AP to party AP and 20% of AP to party SP is very strong").
- **[leveling]** Assassination becomes the best leveling spec from ~level 35-40 onward due to Noxious Assault (unlocked at 40) being weapon-damage independent and giving a large power spike; before 40, Combat/frontstab (ambush+backstab) or slow-weapon Sinister Strike builds are common — [[d:rogue#1444285672453050370]], [[d:rogue#1444473523790680115]], [[d:rogue#1446188256487145543]], [[d:rogue#1446845873882530004]] (Antez: "did it until 46 myself... assa does 3 times the damage" after switching, +confirmation).
- **[sub raid representation]** Zaszz's ranking-site analysis: only 11 Sub-specced rogues appear across the top Naxxramas parses of the last 5 weeks, out of 71 total rogue entries of any spec — cited as evidence Sub is underrepresented relative to its raid value; disputed by other players (30, Heffey) who argue top-parsing guilds/rogues largely do NOT run a dedicated Sub rogue and still clear content fine — [[d:rogue#1450610826494087229]], [[d:rogue#1450610945230508173]], [[d:rogue#1450611461180231760]], [[d:rogue#1450613374181183679]], [[d:rogue#1450613665249362043]] (2025-12-17; contested).
- **[sub]** Hemorrhage's raid-wide debuff (bonus physical damage taken) is estimated at "literally 4% more damage with good uptime" for all physical attackers in the raid — [[d:rogue#1450614096587391179]] (Zaszz, 2025-12-17); real-world observed Hemo debuff uptime with only 1 Sub rogue in a raid is around 54%, rising to 70-80% with 2 Sub rogues coordinating (uptime hurt by target-swapping fights and downtime) — [[d:rogue#1450615129086361733]], [[d:rogue#1450615299752726702]] (Zaszz).
- **[sub]** A Sub rogue is described as capable of personal parses competitive with mid-pack combat rogues while still maintaining IEA/Hemo/Mark for Death support — one player cites reaching rank ~22 in server-wide Naxx (no-Gothik) personal-DPS rankings across all rogue specs while playing Sub, and 1760 personal DPS on Patchwerk — [[d:rogue#1448120168411824279]], [[d:rogue#1448119596614815868]], [[d:rogue#1448120259847389254]] (Zaszz, 2025-12-09).
- **[sub]** Undead racial (Will of the Forsaken / Vengeance passive) called strong for rogues; the server's top-parsing rogue (Nord) is reportedly Undead, pushing back against assumptions that Undead is a weak PvE rogue race choice — [[d:rogue#1450534266336444610]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2025-12-16).

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[assassination]** "3 pretty much obligatory talents" referenced for an Assassination dagger build via talent calculator link `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/rogue?points=YoCAAoDYB-FADoCAAoBSCQ-` — [[d:rogue#1445476719216824531]], [[d:rogue#1445477199351386136]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-02); notes you must choose between reaching Blade Rush and taking Seal of Fate, and Seal of Fate is weaker at low crit — [[d:rogue#1445478012521943202]], [[d:rogue#1445478081924960376]].
- **[combat/backstab]** "Standard backstab build" talent link: `https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c/1.18.0/rogue?points=AoYDIoY-FADoCAAoRCCYB-` — [[d:rogue#1446561906490867914]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2025-12-05).
- **[assassination]** For raiding Naxx, "Talents are as straightforward as they can be: grab AR (Adrenaline Rush) in Combat and Taste for Blood in Assassin" — [[d:rogue#1445415184763060375]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-02).
- **[combat AR vs envenom]** For combat-dagger raiders, AR (Adrenaline Rush) spec wins over Envenom spec on pure raw damage in most cases; Envenom spec has good damage and "would shine on longer fights" — [[d:rogue#1444687276994068591]] (Heffey, 2025-11-30).
- **[assassination]** Standard AP/haste-stacking build for endgame PvE: Murder + Cold Blood + 2-3 points Lethality; Lethality contributes little unless crit/AP is high — [[d:rogue#1444865543692746843]], [[d:rogue#1444868134753538099]] (Purge/Rheinwg, 2025-12-01); with only ~750 AP, Lethality benefit estimated "marginal at best" [[d:rogue#1444904784325902378]] (Blindtongue).
- **[general leveling default]** ~41/10/0 point split suggested once at 60 with sub-raid gear, built around the assassination "stun mob → SnD → Envenom → mash Noxious Assault" combo, using Ruthlessness to keep 1 combo point after finishers — [[d:rogue#1446197875037175841]] (Zaszz, 2025-12-04).
- **[PvP assassination]** Example PvP-oriented Assassination spec: 2/3 Efficient Poisons (helps land ranged Crippling Poison vs poison resist on some classes), 3/3 Taste for Blood (mainly for Rupture duration), 5/5 Dodge, no Imp Ambush, no Lethality (favoring haste build over AP); at ~710 AP Lethality deemed not worth it vs 5/5 Seal of Fate — [[d:rogue#1447061179456426168]], [[d:rogue#1447061440753303745]], [[d:rogue#1447061837706297445]] (Blindtongue, 2025-12-07).
- **[deep assassination]** Sample raid/dungeon deep-assa spec elements confirmed by Rheinwg: needs Vigor, Improved Poisons, Taste for Blood; 5/5 Precision and 5/5 Dodge recommended, no Backstab talent investment (not used as deep assa), points in Combat tree taken only up to Precision — [[d:rogue#1447298399895425205]], [[d:rogue#1447299096548479037]], [[d:rogue#1447299309799346348]] (Rheinwg/Nandieb, 2025-12-07).
- **[general]** Respec cost caps at 5g on Turtle (not the vanilla 50g), so players are encouraged to experiment with builds while leveling — [[d:rogue#1447553589470822441]] (GumGuzz, 2025-12-08).
- **[combat dagger]** T3 4-piece set bonus (5 energy on crit) is heavily disputed but multiple experienced players (Heffey, Treehide/Verrik) insist it's very strong for Backstab specifically because Backstab crits 85-90% of the time raid-buffed, making the "5 energy per crit" into near-permanent extra energy regen; skeptics (Epickalen) value it as only ~24 AP-equivalent by their own math — [[d:rogue#1447810570765078630]], [[d:rogue#1447964975107805367]], [[d:rogue#1447769853430595594]], [[d:rogue#1447767206091034699]] (2025-12-09).
- **[combat dagger]** Hand Wraps of Deadwinds vs T3 gloves (Aged Core Leather Gloves as alt weapon-skill option) is an active unresolved gearing debate; Deadwinds gives more raw stats (agi/str) but loses the T3 4pc set bonus; consensus leans toward keeping T3 4pc unless neck slot is already covered elsewhere — [[d:rogue#1447951844793123049]], [[d:rogue#1447958907854258196]], [[d:rogue#1447959679102750781]], [[d:rogue#1447966633376092270]] (Scruff/Heffey, 2025-12-09).
- **[assassination]** Deep Assassination talent guidance: do not invest in full Dual Wield Specialization (too deep into the tree, ~5 points into "non-damage" investment); instead put points into Seal of Fate (improves Taste for Blood uptime) and Murder if raiding BWL — Lethality less rewarding early since Noxious Assault doesn't crit much at low AP — [[d:rogue#1448720881760211005]], [[d:rogue#1448721982265884734]], [[d:rogue#1448722036305428512]], [[d:rogue#1448722864240066622]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-11).
- **[assassination]** Cold Blood: debated whether worth taking for PvE Assassination; andre says "zero reason" to take it for PvE, but Rheinwg counters it's a guaranteed crit + Taste for Blood uptime worth ~5% Eviscerate damage tradeoff; Beldrin notes Eviscerate isn't used much as Assassination anyway (context-dependent, contested) — [[d:rogue#1449120813277249771]], [[d:rogue#1449127205174116385]], [[d:rogue#1449128834459762851]] (2025-12-12/12-13).
- **[assassination poisons]** Poison talent choice for combat/envenom daggers: prefer Vile Poisons (3/3, +30% dmg on procs) over Improved Poisons (+10% proc chance) — math shown: on 100 base hits/50 procs, Vile ≈ +15 "extra procs" worth of damage vs Improved's +10 extra procs; one player (Heffey) instead ran 3/3 Improved + 2/3 Vile in practice — [[d:rogue#1449925927889932413]], [[d:rogue#1449926969213845675]], [[d:rogue#1449932445322579998]] (Rheinwg/Heffey, 2025-12-13).
- **[assassination]** Taste for Blood self-buff persists even if the target that granted the combo points is now dead (buff applies to self, not target) — confirmed by Rheinwg — [[d:rogue#1449084291958050957]], [[d:rogue#1449091541061730505]] (2025-12-11). At low levels TfB is valued because it lets you land a "free" 5-point Rupture on a poison-immune enemy for the buff effect without losing combo point value; at level 60 every build runs it (near-mandatory) — [[d:rogue#1449091680514084986]], [[d:rogue#1449092234120270057]] (Treehide/Verrik).
- **[leveling]** Confirmed pre-relentless-strikes leveling order: use a slow mainhand with Sinister Strike until poisons unlock, then transition to fast daggers once poisons are up, prioritizing fast weapons once Envenom is learned; sample leveling talent build linked: `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/rogue?points=YoYDIADQB--` — [[d:rogue#1449046010994102373]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-13).
- **[leveling]** One player's controlled test at level 40 disputes the "1cp eviscerate spam beats Sinister Strike" leveling advice when using a slow, high-DPS AH-bought weapon — reported 20-30% more damage just Sinister-Strike-spamming with a slow weapon vs. Eviscerate-spam; suggested explanation is Eviscerate-spam is meant for weapon-speed independence, not raw damage, so it only "wins" when your weapon is bad or when playing daggers — [[d:rogue#1447934338791374858]], [[d:rogue#1447965017835311147]], [[d:rogue#1447967484681388114]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-09; contested/unresolved for AH-geared alts).
- **[leveling]** At level 40 with minimal gear (2x Sacrificial Kris + Instant Poison III), one player parsed >300 DPS on a dummy — cited as evidence the post-40 Noxious/poison spike is very strong even ungeared — [[d:rogue#1449442851615146074]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-13).

## Leveling
- **[general]** Standard leveling rotation regardless of build: use combo-point generator (Sinister Strike/Backstab/Noxious/Hemorrhage/Ghostly Strike) → keep Slice and Dice up → keep Envenom up (if talented) → dump combo points with Eviscerate when buffs don't need refreshing — [[d:rogue#1444846610017157141]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-01).
- **[assassination]** From ~level 35-40, Assassination is easily the best leveling spec: "uses dual fast weapons, doesn't need gear, gets all its damage from instant poison, stun mob, envenom, slice and dice, watch mob melt"; struggles vs nature/poison-immune mobs since no alt poison is available until high level — [[d:rogue#1446188256487145543]], [[d:rogue#1446188361671905290]], [[d:rogue#1446198015235850354]] (Zaszz, 2025-12-04).
- **[leveling <40]** Pre-40 options: (1) Combat/slow-dagger Ambush-Frontstab spec ("go combat or slow dagger ambush frontstab spec until [level] 40"; can't reliably kill nature-immune mobs until 52) — [[d:rogue#1444285672453050370]] (casey); (2) 1cp Eviscerate spam via Ruthlessness from level 17-18 (1 Sinister Strike → 1cp SnD → 1cp Eviscerate spam) — [[d:rogue#1447473413311565887]], [[d:rogue#1447473642202992693]] (GumGuzz); (3) improved-ambush-first Sub-leaning build then move into Assassination for crit (Opportunity) — [[d:rogue#1446692474239189185]], [[d:rogue#1446692605780955278]] (Simpson).
- **[leveling 1-40]** After getting Ruthlessness/Envenom: assassination-from-start rotation is "1cp SnD, 1cp Envenom, 1cp Eviscerate spam"; after 40 add Noxious to build to 5cp Rupture (if Dissolvent poisons available) or Eviscerate — [[d:rogue#1444556284190916698]], [[d:rogue#1444556519923384361]] (GumGuzz, 2025-11-30).
- **[leveling]** Recommended leveling build post-17: 1 Sinister Strike → 1cp SnD → 1cp Eviscerate spam (screenshotted build referenced) — [[d:rogue#1447473413311565887]] (GumGuzz, 2025-12-08); once Envenom obtained, add 1cp Envenom into the SnD/Eviscerate loop — [[d:rogue#1447476935620825160]].
- **[leveling]** Frontstabbing (Ambush + Improved Ambush based, positional) works fine up to level 40 for many players; unreliable without a free-spinning mouse wheel for strafing — [[d:rogue#1446841206037549087]] (Vale Grumby); after 40, Assassination outdamages frontstab roughly 3x, prompting a respec — [[d:rogue#1446845873882530004]], [[d:rogue#1446846122696900648]] (Antez).
- **[leveling]** Stealth-detection stacking (Camouflage + gear +stealth) is reportedly buggy/unreliable for evading PvE mobs even at 8.4 total levels of stealth bonus, though it works better in PvP — [[d:rogue#1446354536301072476]], [[d:rogue#1446364676479782922]], [[d:rogue#1446369388889374904]] (Blindtongue/Epickalen, 2025-12-05).
- **[leveling]** Hit is called "the most important stat until you get like 9% in pve" while leveling — having hit lets you fight/kill higher-level mobs; without it you feel the impact of higher-level mobs much more — [[d:rogue#1444368536825761852]], [[d:rogue#1444368746079850586]], [[d:rogue#1444368813071011920]] (Vale Grumby, 2025-11-29). A good early hit item: neck from Balor dungeon — [[d:rogue#1444368193903919227]].
- **[leveling gear]** Stat priority while leveling for low-level items: "+7 stam & +20 attack power" preferred over "+5 agi" — [[d:rogue#1445028336664580247]] (Hrimnir, +1 reaction, 2025-12-01).
- **[leveling weapons]** Gutripper (level 40 quest dagger) is a strong, widely recommended MH dagger upgrade at 40 despite being pricey, usable through 50-60; alternatives include Gutgore, Sunrazor at 60 — [[d:rogue#1445806282832482417]], [[d:rogue#1445807757562478784]] (Lightemgee/Epickalen, 2025-12-03). Tooltip on some level-40 quest daggers overstates DPS (bug) — shows ~55+ but actual is ~32, though live parses reportedly do show 130-160 dmg autohits at level 40 — [[d:rogue#1444712574527733820]], [[d:rogue#1444721446235541755]], [[d:rogue#1444796630422257725]] (2025-11-30, contested/possibly separate bugged item).
- **[leveling weapons 40-60]** No clear list of 1.3-speed dagger progression 40-60 known to the channel; Julie's Dagger available at level 55; a 1.2-speed white claw at level ~28 mentioned as filler — [[d:rogue#1447101198644674722]], [[d:rogue#1447101457940615211]], [[d:rogue#1447101654280310887]] (Nandieb/Blindtongue, 2025-12-07; open question).
- **[assassination leveling stat]** Weapon speed math for leveling gear: rule of thumb "0.1 speed = 6 dps from poisons," so a 1.3-speed 40dps weapon is worth more than a 2.0-speed 82dps weapon — [[d:rogue#1445034228231438409]], [[d:rogue#1445034405369479302]] (GumGuzz, 2025-12-01; refined later to 6-8 dps per 0.1 speed) — [[d:rogue#1447182487389339740]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[assassination]** Endgame ST rotation: apply Deadly Poison MH / Corrosive Poison OH for most bosses; use Dissolvent (both hands) on trash/short fights; for bosses immune to Deadly Poison (e.g., Loatheb/"chess", Anomalus), use Corrosive II MH + Corrosive I OH — [[d:rogue#1444213839339196581]] (Heffey), [[d:rogue#1444686074046578700]], [[d:rogue#1444689095090503760]], [[d:rogue#1444691243778183350]] (Heffey, 2025-11-29/11-30).
- **[assassination]** Core finisher priority: 1cp Envenom (kept up as buff) → SnD kept up → build to 5cp Rupture with Noxious Assault when Dissolvent poisons are available, otherwise Eviscerate — [[d:rogue#1444556519923384361]].
- **[assassination]** Relentless Strikes (energy refund on finishers) does NOT affect the Envenom buff, but DOES affect Rupture; Noxious Assault fully replaces Sinister Strike, costs 1 more energy but nets +4 with 2/2 Vigor — [[d:rogue#1446429943574827213]] (feeder5, 2025-12-06). NA damage is roughly 50% poison-based, so poison uptime matters heavily for Assassination overall damage — [[d:rogue#1446430491002536039]].
- **[assassination]** Because Noxious Assault ignores weapon damage (unlike SS/Hemo/Backstab), the mainhand can be a fast weapon without losing NA damage; this is why fast/fast dual-wield is standard for Assassination — [[d:rogue#1446499106989277214]] (Rheinwg), [[d:rogue#1445049793469747212]] (Rheinwg, "Noxx doesnt take weapon damage into account unlike SS/Hemo/BS").
- **[combat backstab]** For fights requiring facing changes (e.g., Thaddius phase mechanic), if using a slow MH weapon (Castigator, Remnants, Ancient Qiraji Ripper, Hatchet), weapon-swap to a fast weapon and use Sinister Strike while facing wrong way, swap back to Backstab when able — [[d:rogue#1447254729154494575]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2025-12-08), macro'd for convenience — [[d:rogue#1447262211751018819]].
- **[combat sword]** Naxx combat-sword poison choice: Deadly MH + Corrosive OH for bosses, Dissolvent for trash/short fights; combat doesn't get enough poison procs for Dissolvent to beat Deadly+Corrosive over time — [[d:rogue#1446092891863908352]], [[d:rogue#1446119907149353103]], [[d:rogue#1446119984442118204]], [[d:rogue#1447145690072617042]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-04/12-07).
- **[combo point transfer tech]** Combo points can be built on one target (e.g., add) then transferred to the boss: use SnD on the new target and Ruthlessness generates a fresh combo point on it, allowing a "free" opener like Kidney Shot without needing Cheap Shot setup on the boss itself — [[d:rogue#1445083689376682217]] (Rheinwg +3 reactions), [[d:rogue#1445118483875500153]], [[d:rogue#1445119487128113264]] (2025-12-01/12-02).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general hit]** Hit reduces miss chance for autoattacks and abilities. At 5% hit, abilities won't miss a same-level target (the "usual cap" while leveling); each level of target above you needs +1% hit. At max level (bosses lvl 63) the yellow-hit cap is 8% — [[d:rogue#1444369360360706130]], [[d:rogue#1444369433622609950]] (Rheinwg, 2025-11-29). Auto-attacks can still miss up to 25% hit (separate, larger threshold) — [[d:rogue#1444369581362778283]].
- **[general hit]** Hit soft cap for abilities against bosses (lvl63) confirmed multiple times as 8% (300 weapon skill) or 5% at 315 weapon skill (weapon skill gives 1% hit per 5 skill / 0.2% per point); race does not change this cap, only how much weapon skill you innately have — [[d:rogue#1444859273036431451]], [[d:rogue#1447162896839872676]], [[d:rogue#1447163186640851076]], [[d:rogue#1447171350497198173]] (Rheinwg/GumGuzz/Epickalen, 2025-12-01/12-07). Add ~19% miss to white MH/OH hits from the dual-wield penalty — [[d:rogue#1447162896839872676]].
- **[general]** Weapon skill also affects glancing blow damage, capping out at 315 weapon skill — [[d:rogue#1444560092098854912]] (GumGuzz).
- **[general]** Weapon skill above 315 is not harmful, just wasted itemization ("depends on what you sacrifice to get there — crit/ap/hit") — [[d:rogue#1445966177703694508]], [[d:rogue#1445972828431056996]] (Heffey/Epickalen, 2025-12-04).
- **[general PvP hit]** PvP hit cap is 5% (lower than PvE); 6-7% gives a bit more security on auto-attacks — [[d:rogue#1447086285087969351]], [[d:rogue#1447086577179168801]] (Blindtongue, 2025-12-07).
- **[general AP]** Agility and Strength give the same amount of AP to rogues (1 AP per point); a full crit is granted every 29 agility — [[d:rogue#1445046771288899736]] (Lightemgee, +2, 2025-12-01).
- **[assassination weapon math]** Weapon-speed-to-DPS tradeoff for poison-scaling specs: roughly 6-8 DPS-tooltip-equivalent per 0.1 speed reduction (e.g., 1.3spd/40dps ≈ 1.4spd/46-48dps); an earlier "18 dps per 0.1 speed" estimate was called inflated — [[d:rogue#1445034228231438409]], [[d:rogue#1447182487389339740]], [[d:rogue#1447182658466480289]] (GumGuzz, 2025-12-01/12-07). Weapon DPS itself does not affect Noxious Assault's flat damage, only poison procs do — [[d:rogue#1447186139520700427]].
- **[combat]** For Combat, 400+ buffed agility considered "a great start," roughly worth 0.5s off energy regen ticks; no real agility overcap for combat — [[d:rogue#1446757949757329520]] (Epickalen, 2025-12-05).
- **[assassination enchant]** Crusader enchant (proc AP + heal) favored over flat +15 Agility per weapon for Assassination because fast weapons proc it often and poison damage scales with AP; Agility enchant favored for Combat since combat uses slower weapons (fewer procs) and combat energy scales with agility — [[d:rogue#1446752620411748372]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2025-12-04). Crusader described as PPM-based, not swing-speed dependent, though haste/extra attacks interact with proc rate — [[d:rogue#1446789536280481832]], [[d:rogue#1446803370152165480]] (Weirdthing/Dodging, 2025-12-05).
- **[general]** Armor penetration considered weak for rogues generally — most raid bosses have low armor and Serrated Blades talent already grants ~300 armor penetration — [[d:rogue#1446862371913207850]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-06), though disputed by MistAssassin who argues last bits of armor are worth stripping more (unresolved) — [[d:rogue#1446974961066574046]], [[d:rogue#1446975074400862298]].
- **[assassination]** Vampirism gearing: 1% Vampiric Embrace-type leech gives 1% of damage dealt back as HP-per-second-equivalent (e.g., 400 dps × 1% = 4hp/sec, 240 HP over a 1-min fight) — [[d:rogue#1446931118967488613]] (GumGuzz, 2025-12-06). PvP players generally prefer offensive stun/burst stats over vamp — [[d:rogue#1446931350446669845]].
- **[general hit]** Weapon skill on Turtle gives 1% hit per 5 weapon skill points (confirmed again) — [[d:rogue#1448335000897585313]] (Lightemgee, 2025-12-10).
- **[general]** Confirmed again: hit soft cap for landing abilities on a level-63 boss is 8% (with 5/5 combat Precision-type talent reducing this); going higher than 8% is still fine ("always good") — [[d:rogue#1449417933720911892]], [[d:rogue#1449421299574378657]] (Graves/Rheinwg, 2025-12-13). Note: this is distinct from auto-attack miss which can still occur even with ability-hit capped — parry/dodge chances are separate and stack on top of hit/miss — [[d:rogue#1448684401050517594]], [[d:rogue#1448684743653589044]] (Rheinwg/Lightemgee, 2025-12-10, re: Expose Armor resist confusion).
- **[assassination]** Reinforced: For Assassination, Haste > Crit in value because poisons don't benefit from melee crit chance at all — [[d:rogue#1449360749460717622]], [[d:rogue#1449361487754825829]] (Nandieb/Lightemgee, 2025-12-13). Assassination "doesn't value anything other than speed, application, and AP" — [[d:rogue#1449692802328952875]] (Epickalen, 2025-12-14).
- **[general cheat-sheet]** Community stat-value rule of thumb (Epickalen, self-described "cheatsheet," not verified/consensus): 15 agi = 1% haste-equivalent; 1% haste = 1% crit = 1% hit = 25 AP in relative value; agi roughly worth 1.5 AP including energy regen value, 1.2 AP excluding it — [[d:rogue#1449907721582280765]] (2025-12-15; single-source estimate, not broadly confirmed).
- **[assassination]** Weapon speed-to-AP conversion estimate for haste theorycrafting: 0.1 speed ≈ 6 poison DPS (per GumGuzz's earlier figure), extrapolated to roughly 1% haste ≈ 0.8 poison DPS, though the implied "37 AP per 1% haste" from one calculation was flagged by andre as "utterly absurd," suggesting a flaw in the math chain — unresolved — [[d:rogue#1449920575274352805]], [[d:rogue#1449921902637223996]], [[d:rogue#1449922343593050134]] (2025-12-15; open/contested numbers).
- **[assassination]** Deep-assassination weapon-speed-to-DPS conversion reaffirmed at ~8 DPS per 0.1 speed difference; example given: Perdition's Blade at 1.8 speed/58.3 "flat dps" loses ~40 DPS-equivalent value versus a 1.3-speed weapon — [[d:rogue#1448983295697223720]] (Equinox, 2025-12-11).
- **[general]** Detailed hit/crit interaction explanation (Rheinwg, 2025-12-16): there are no true diminishing returns on hit between 8% and 25%; more hit (1) reduces autoattack miss chance and (2) raises the "crit cap" ceiling for autoattacks. Recommended target is ~14% hit at typical raid gear levels to avoid getting crit-capped on autoattacks while still benefiting fully from crit; going higher is fine in higher-tier raids — [[d:rogue#1450373303486124083]], [[d:rogue#1450373548534403142]] (2025-12-16). Glancing blow damage is purely a function of weapon skill, unrelated to hit — [[d:rogue#1450373548534403142]].
- **[general]** More detailed hit-cap breakdown (Epickalen, 2025-12-16): base ability hit cap is 8%; weapon skill 300→315 grants up to +3% (0.2%/point); rogue talents (Precision-line) add up to another 5% hit; recommended target during early raid-40 tiers is ~15% total hit rating "since less often you miss is more often you crit." At 15% hit, autoattack crit-cap ceiling is ~45%; at 25% hit it's ~55% — [[d:rogue#1450291246420590774]], [[d:rogue#1450415480824266873]] (2025-12-16).
- **[general]** Crit vs. hit priority is spec/weapon dependent, not a single universal breakpoint — Backstab benefits far more from crit than Assassination does, since roughly half of Assassination's damage (poisons) cannot crit at all — [[d:rogue#1450502737740435487]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-16).
- **[general dual-wield]** Equipping a second (offhand) weapon sets base miss chance to roughly 28% for BOTH weapons' white-damage swings (the dual-wield miss penalty applies globally, not just to the offhand); Dual Wield Specialization/offhand-damage talents only restore offhand damage to 75%, not 100% — [[d:rogue#1450582298117275698]], [[d:rogue#1450583066794917990]], [[d:rogue#1450583133664706612]] (Zaszz, 2025-12-16). This means hit-stacking has roughly double the value on a rogue compared to a single-weapon class, since it fixes both weapons' miss chance at once — [[d:rogue#1450582298117275698]], [[d:rogue#1450583276216520736]] (Zaszz/GumGuzz).
- **[general crit cap]** ~40% of white/auto-attack swings against a raid boss always "glance" (reduced-damage hit) regardless of weapon skill — weapon skill only improves the damage dealt by glancing blows, not whether they occur; a raid boss also retains a dodge/parry chance even when attacked from behind, further eating into crit uptime — [[d:rogue#1450583891273584662]], [[d:rogue#1450583987847172176]], [[d:rogue#1450583992796713102]] (Zaszz/Rheinwg, 2025-12-16). Backstab's crit chance has a hard 100% cap (cannot be "overcapped" past 100%, unlike autoattack crit which has room past the practical cap due to integer/attack-table mechanics); Backstab's crit-damage modifier while a target has Loatheb's spore-crit buff is roughly +145% — [[d:rogue#1450583987847172176]], [[d:rogue#1450584100585869423]], [[d:rogue#1450584414886166629]] (Epickalen, 2025-12-16).
- **[general]** Weapon-skill mismatch between mainhand and offhand: only the MAINHAND's weapon skill/hit matters for landing special abilities (Hemorrhage, Eviscerate, Shadow of Death, etc.) since the dual-wield miss penalty (and its interaction with weapon skill) applies only to auto-attacks — tested directly (8 seconds of Hemorrhage spam with exactly 8% hit and 300 mainhand weapon skill, zero misses) — [[d:rogue#1451950425581420705]], [[d:rogue#1451950628522954873]], [[d:rogue#1451950685817143297]], [[d:rogue#1451950970836881622]] (MistAssassin/Rheinwg, 2025-12-20). This mainly matters for Sub rogues optimizing Precision/hit talent investment, since other specs generally sit above 8% anyway — [[d:rogue#1451951298655031297]], [[d:rogue#1451951560983580772]].
- **[assassination poisons]** Dissolvent/Instant poison application can apparently be resisted like a spell effect, tied to spell-hit rather than melee hit — confusing/contested community understanding, with Rheinwg calling the relevant tooltip line "that stupid line confusing people again" and hoping it gets removed/clarified — [[d:rogue#1451664631696199792]], [[d:rogue#1451664773648355448]], [[d:rogue#1451665048064888864]] (Farban/Rheinwg, 2025-12-19). Efficient Poisons talent (reduces poison resist chance) is valued partly for this reason, giving meaningful extra damage beyond its surface "QoL" framing — [[d:rogue#1451664361738207414]] (Rheinwg).
- **[pickpocket]** Pickpocket resist chance is tied to spell hit; without any spell hit gear, resist chance against high-level (63+) mobs is roughly 1/20 (5%), dropping to about 1/100 (1%) with enough spell hit to cap around 16% spell hit; spell hit cap against lower-level (60ish) mobs is much lower (~3%) — [[d:rogue#1448641308595064892]], [[d:rogue#1448642322827841552]], [[d:rogue#1451162888176930856]], [[d:rogue#1451162965922549873]], [[d:rogue#1451163145723842707]] (Floccumate/Kolima, 2025-12-10/12-19). BRD dwarves reportedly changed to dogs with higher detection at some point, possibly to curb pickpocket botting — [[d:rogue#1451157184581861521]], [[d:rogue#1451157406678646886]] (Ragul/Jacquouilles, 2025-12-19).
- **[weapon enchant]** For combat dagger, +15 Agility enchant confirmed to outperform +5 weapon damage on mainhand via direct in-game A/B testing ("I've done it both ways, 15 agi wins out") — [[d:rogue#1451300019750699049]], [[d:rogue#1451304714669920266]] (Ignatum/Heffey, 2025-12-18/19).
- **[assassination enchant]** Double Crusader vs. +15 Agility OH debated again: with Slice and Dice + Stormshroud procs + ~16% base haste pushing effective speed under 0.8s, Crusader's proc uptime on dual daggers approaches "always up / sometimes double-stacked," valued at nearly 100 AP-equivalent — [[d:rogue#1452001603543502892]], [[d:rogue#1452001920888606854]] (andre/Epickalen, 2025-12-21).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[poisons]** Deadly Poison and Corrosive Poison remain the core Assassination/Combat boss poisons; Dissolvent Poison used for trash/short fights and on bosses immune to Deadly (chess/Anomalus-style immunities) via Corrosive II MH + Corrosive I OH — [[d:rogue#1444213839339196581]], [[d:rogue#1444691243778183350]] (Heffey, 2025-11-29/11-30).
- **[poisons]** As pure Assassination (with Envenom talent), always use Dissolvent (or Instant Poison if Dissolvent not yet learned); periodic-damage poisons (Deadly/Corrosive) are situational only (e.g., swap for burst mid-fight if hiding from a boss mechanic like Chromaggus) — [[d:rogue#1446892696097521776]], [[d:rogue#1446893662309978266]], [[d:rogue#1446893772930678945]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-06). Extra procs of periodic poisons are wasted; extra procs of instant/stacking poisons (Dissolvent/Instant) deal more damage — [[d:rogue#1446893875443798169]].
- **[imbue: weapon speed]** For Assassination fast weapons "always win" over slow with equal DPS, because more attacks = more poison procs; there's a sweet spot around 1.5-1.8 speed weapons for endgame damage before falling too far behind on procs — [[d:rogue#1444417000813236275]], [[d:rogue#1444418457939480708]] (Rheinwg/Blindtongue, 2025-11-29/11-30). While leveling, 1.3-speed weapons are called most optimal, becoming less critical at endgame due to burstier fights — [[d:rogue#1444419382259355840]] (Blindtongue).
- **[enchants]** Crusader enchant (proc AP + self-heal) is favored for Assassination due to fast weapons proccing it often and poisons scaling off AP; Agility enchant favored for slower Combat weapons — [[d:rogue#1446752620411748372]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2025-12-04); double Crusader is popular for Assassination — [[d:rogue#1446590372670341201]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-05).
- **[enchants]** Striking enchants (flat weapon damage) considered a budget alternative to Crusader for poison-priority Assassination builds when gold-constrained — [[d:rogue#1447090520768774205]] (Blindtongue, 2025-12-07).
- **[enchants haste sources]** Notable +haste enchant/item sources for stacking haste on Assassination/PvP: Librams on head/pants, 2% Hyjal shoulder enchant, 1% gloves enchant, 2% or 3% cloak enchants (Shawl / Felwood Boon of Nemiroth quest reward), Windwalker Boots craftable (2%), Dreamhide Leggings (LW, 3%, BoP) with matching belt (1%), AQ20 3-set ring bonus (1% haste, obtained while farming the AQ20 dagger), Engineering haste helm (2%, plus agi/stam) — [[d:rogue#1447098170642268170]], [[d:rogue#1447098573094129666]] (Blindtongue, 2025-12-07).
- **[vamp enchants]** Life-leech/lifesteal weapon enchant available; ~8-10% leech felt more valuable in practice than 18% by one player, with Darkmoon Deck: Heroism + Crusaders needed for real sustain — [[d:rogue#1444828779045326858]] (Blindtongue, 2025-12-01).
- **[assassination poisons]** Dissolvent (Instant/physical) poison does scale with Attack Power, though weakly (~5%) per Lightemgee's correction to GumGuzz's initial claim of "0% scaling" — contested/unresolved, GumGuzz says he'll need to test — [[d:rogue#1449360306873700416]], [[d:rogue#1449360514064060499]], [[d:rogue#1449364290959183985]] (2025-12-13).
- **[assassination poisons]** OBD (spellstrike proc item set) confirmed to have ~42% spell-power scaling and a 25% proc chance; BOED has a 10% proc chance and, per one claim, does scale slightly with spell crit (contested, "0% scaling" also claimed) — [[d:rogue#1449364290959183985]], [[d:rogue#1449367195695054979]] (GumGuzz/Zllig, 2025-12-13).
- **[fire-immune/spellstrike]** Confirmed by testing that on-hit proc trinkets like Sigil of Ancient Accord and Embrace of the Wind Serpent do NOT proc from spellstrike-triggered damage (BoED/OBD); wording like "landing damage spells" is inconsistent/unreliable across items — general rule given: "always assume a proc doesn't work with spellstrikes" — [[d:rogue#1448444855050174587]], [[d:rogue#1448445326372507759]], [[d:rogue#1448446609284403220]], [[d:rogue#1448449438925324311]] (Beldrin/Lightemgee, 2025-12-11). BoED itself is confirmed to work properly and is used on Ambershire — [[d:rogue#1448447582979490016]].
- **[fire-immune BiS]** Incendosaur set essential for a "real" BOED spellstrike raiding build (30% extra proc-related damage achievable with 4pc T0.5 + 3pc Incendosaur + embergem cuffs + rings + back + a now-BoP bow); a lighter/partial version (2 spellstrike pieces + BoED only) still nets a meaningful 6-8% of overall damage plus 1-2% lightning damage in a full MC clear — [[d:rogue#1450166734534213743]], [[d:rogue#1450167731998425098]], [[d:rogue#1450170261952528525]] (Najven, 2025-12-14). Many MC mobs are fire-immune, reducing BOED's overall value there — [[d:rogue#1450166734534213743]].
- **[general]** Poison-immunity workaround: certain fire-damage on-hit sets (Incendosaur) can still proc through fire immunity in some cases though this contradicts the OBD/Burning Hatred fire-immune-boss findings from earlier in the chunk — treat as contested (see Contested section).
- **[assassination weapon enchants]** For Julie's Daggers, Crusader vs Fiery enchant debated with no clear resolution given in-chunk — [[d:rogue#1449534200914120846]] (Istrefi, 2025-12-14; open).
- **[general]** Concoction (Mongoose-type consumable) with 2% spell crit noted as "strictly better than normal Mongoose" but expensive — used to push spell-crit-based haste-adjacent builds (e.g., OBD hit/crit optimization) — [[d:rogue#1449371236667752580]], [[d:rogue#1449371645134504009]] (Akuya/Zllig, 2025-12-13).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[assassination]** Adrenaline Rush (AR) usage timing depends on guild kill time: pop once at pull if kill time <~3min, twice (once early, once near end) if fight runs longer than 3 minutes even if less efficient — [[d:rogue#1446573952553058305]] (Heffey, 2025-12-05).
- **[assassination]** Combo-point-transfer tech (see Rotation ST) lets a rogue enter combat with pre-built combo points on the boss via SnD+Ruthlessness, enabling immediate high-value finishers like Kidney Shot without an on-target opener — [[d:rogue#1445083689376682217]] (Rheinwg, +3 reactions).
- **[general]** Weapon-swap macros trigger a GCD when used in combat, but not out of combat (tested) — [[d:rogue#1445530249419817082]] (Skinnypete, 2025-12-03).
- **[general]** Vigor talent (2/2) refunds energy on finishers, enabling near-permanent Envenom/SnD uptime — [[d:rogue#1444704287207850146]] (Badknees, 2025-11-30).
- **[general]** Ghostly Strike (Sub talent finisher-replacement generator) is worth using on cooldown for Sub rogues if the debuff/buff isn't about to drop — it does more damage and costs less energy than the alternative — [[d:rogue#1446533050451103949]], [[d:rogue#1446541264181788764]], [[d:rogue#1446544561928274073]] (Island/Lightemgee/Rheinwg, 2025-12-05).
- **[sub]** Improved Ghostly Strike gives the rogue 15% movement speed (not the tooltip-implied 6%) when using Ghostly Strike, plus 6% MS to the party and an energy cost reduction; stacks with the rogue's passive 7% MS — tested live via dummy-to-doorway sprint timing by Zaszz — [[d:rogue#1450013616161030308]], [[d:rogue#1450013635878453359]], [[d:rogue#1450013671404077179]], [[d:rogue#1450014175828119562]], [[d:rogue#1450014542133329931]] (2025-12-15). Note: party's 6% MS from this talent likely doesn't stack additively with common boot MS enchants at high end since boot enchants alone often exceed it — [[d:rogue#1449985119506006139]], [[d:rogue#1450037928016220233]], [[d:rogue#1450038155448422431]] (Lightemgee, 2025-12-14/15; open/uncertain stacking).
- **[general]** Bash (stun effect) does NOT stack with Fairy Fire-type effects — confirmed by Rheinwg — [[d:rogue#1448401511196393736]] (2025-12-10).
- **[rogue tanking]** A niche "rogue tank" build/setup exists and is actively played by at least one experienced player (Scruff): claims near-BiS rogue tank gear, hitting ~96% passive avoidance when fully raid-buffed with improved totems; uses Juju Escape as an emergency cooldown; AOE threat is effectively nonexistent for rogue tanks (single-target holding only, e.g., "hold a boss as long as possible") — [[d:rogue#1448062200899043370]], [[d:rogue#1448067670628110436]], [[d:rogue#1448067950958477474]], [[d:rogue#1448068354349990029]] (Romvlvs/Scruff/GumGuzz/Lightemgee, 2025-12-09). No formal taunt ability for rogues; devs have reportedly stated they don't want rogue tanking to be "immortal" — [[d:rogue#1448063039701254328]], [[d:rogue#1448063570888753153]] (Lightemgee, 2025-12-09).
- **[rogue tanking]** Avoidance stacking notes for the rogue tank build: 15 agility = 1% dodge (very efficient scaling); a talent exists that grants combo points when avoiding damage (dodge/parry proc); weapon choice (e.g., Kingsfall) and agility enchants count toward dodge; food (Chocolate Fish + Sweet Mountain Berries combo) plus 2 weapon-enchant dodge sources can add up to 6% dodge — [[d:rogue#1448067109283168377]], [[d:rogue#1448067148952899696]], [[d:rogue#1448067646091428012]], [[d:rogue#1448068921423953990]], [[d:rogue#1448069105260429475]] (Lightemgee/Scruff, 2025-12-09).
- **[sub]** Smoke Bomb talent is widely considered clunky/bad: 35 energy cost, requires very precise positioning to affect only the tank (not the boss), and risks accidentally helping the boss or removing raid threat entirely; community wishlist is either a rework (throwable, or a flat -10% threat reduction for allies inside) or outright removal in favor of a different talent — [[d:rogue#1448089005919834232]], [[d:rogue#1448089047041052672]], [[d:rogue#1448089459542327399]], [[d:rogue#1448091790446956584]], [[d:rogue#1448092074162262117]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-09). One niche confirmed use: Maexxna's web-wrap mechanic in Naxx, though still hard to time — [[d:rogue#1448093210483228684]] (Zllig).
- **[sub]** Devs reportedly buffed/shortened Adrenaline Rush's cooldown ("its cd is so short now"), making AR compelling to grab even alongside Envenom-oriented builds, though Envenom is said to beat AR specifically in longer fights — [[d:rogue#1447961501230043289]], [[d:rogue#1447961790406201445]] (Scruff/Galedar, 2025-12-09).
- **[consumables]** Several rogue-relevant consumables share hidden cooldowns with each other: Thistle Tea and Frozen Runes interact asymmetrically — using Frozen Runes puts Thistle Tea on cooldown, but using Thistle Tea does NOT put Frozen Runes on cooldown (order matters) — [[d:rogue#1450512236371247104]], [[d:rogue#1450512451345973472]] (Rheinwg, 2025-12-16; unverified further by the asker). Healthstones and Thistle Tea were also mentioned as sharing a cooldown, forcing a choice between healing and energy during 4-man content — [[d:rogue#1450512076073336894]] (Scruff). Sappers (Goblin Sapper Charges) and Holy Water share a cooldown — [[d:rogue#1450511796338430054]] (Farban).
- **[raid utility]** Sapper Charges (AoE damage consumable) have limited but clear value spots in AQ40/Naxx: Viscidus and the post-first-boss scarab trash in AQ40, small adds at Razorgore, and stacking many sappers on Naxx's Faerlina room trash pull are cited as the main efficient uses; generally only ~3-4 raid-wide "sapper moments" exist per typical raid — [[d:rogue#1450509151410192595]], [[d:rogue#1450509177322733661]], [[d:rogue#1450510891677258040]], [[d:rogue#1450509294704263237]] (Rheinwg/Badknees/Scruff, 2025-12-16). BWL is mostly fire-immune, reducing Sapper value there — [[d:rogue#1450508991275729031]] (Rheinwg).
- **[assassination]** Assassination threat generation is disproportionately high relative to damage — one warrior tank reports needing to spam taunt continuously against an Assassination rogue doing only ~25% more damage than other classes but generating roughly 3x the threat; attributed to poison damage not being mitigated/reduced the way some threat-reduction mechanics expect, and to the raw damage spike from Noxious Assault — [[d:rogue#1451409313020117072]], [[d:rogue#1451409563076137071]], [[d:rogue#1451409678251855903]], [[d:rogue#1451418957088030720]] (Nako, 2025-12-19). Standard mitigation: rogues should be proactively using Feint/Vanish-swap and threat meters; well-played rogues who use these tools "are never noticed" on threat — [[d:rogue#1451495222029582388]] (Farban, 2025-12-19).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Naxxramas general]** ~90% of Naxx enemies are undead but most are NOT poison-immune; Dissolvent/Corrosive poisons (physical-damage type) work fine on undead — only Skeletons and Wraiths/Ghosts are immune to standard (nature) poison — [[d:rogue#1446241221520523469]], [[d:rogue#1446241450210889931]] (Lightemgee, 2025-12-05).
- **[Naxx: Gothik]** Gothik the Harvester is "the only fight where you really need to use double Dissolvent as combat" — [[d:rogue#1446241677613465671]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2025-12-05).
- **[Naxx: Grobbulus/Noth]** On fights involving slimes/oozes (Grobbulus, Noth), rogues are usually cleaving off the boss rather than directly targeting poison-immune adds, so poison-immunity rarely matters directly — [[d:rogue#1446242801753784483]] (Treehide/Verrik, 2025-12-05).
- **[Naxx: Thaddius]** When Thaddius or similar bosses force facing changes, weapon-swap from a slow MH (Castigator/Remnants/Ancient Qiraji Ripper/Hatchet) to a fast weapon and Sinister Strike while unable to backstab, then swap back — [[d:rogue#1447254729154494575]], [[d:rogue#1447252575521996830]] (Treehide/Verrik/Kuboo, 2025-12-08).

## PvP
- **[general]** PvP hit soft cap is 5%; there's no fixed "best" rotation in PvP — players emphasize adapting on the fly over set combos — [[d:rogue#1444638096279998707]], [[d:rogue#1444680772001992911]] (Rexx/Blindtongue, 2025-11-30); confirmed hit cap 5%, 6-7% helps auto-attacks land more — [[d:rogue#1447086285087969351]] (Blindtongue, 2025-12-07).
- **[assassination PvP]** Detailed PvP gearing philosophy (Blindtongue, extensive thread 2025-12-07): prefer punchier/slower weapons (1.5-1.8 speed) over max-speed weapons for PvP because fights are too short for fast weapons' poison procs to outweigh bigger per-hit damage; fast weapons are "for boss fighting," not PvP — [[d:rogue#1447089609657028619]], [[d:rogue#1447089767341752391]], [[d:rogue#1447125416187002920]], [[d:rogue#1447125883751108648]].
- **[assassination PvP]** Talent adjustments for PvP vs PvE (Blindtongue's build): drop Improved Ambush when well-geared (still useful vs clothies for burst though — later "def retain imp ambush" for clothies), keep 5/5 Dodge for brawling survivability, favor haste-stacking over AP/Lethality when AP is below ~1100 — [[d:rogue#1447061179456426168]], [[d:rogue#1447064122616320042]], [[d:rogue#1447067912031441000]] (2025-12-07).
- **[PvP gearing]** For lower pvp-rank/undergeared players, prioritize Agility+Crit gear over hit-heavy blues since agility/str losses from pvp blues aren't offset by their AP; example weapon options: Rotmaw's MH + Julie's OH, or Claw of Infinite MH + Rotmaw's OH — [[d:rogue#1447081384433815613]], [[d:rogue#1447089047091544166]], [[d:rogue#1447115872702304361]] (Blindtongue, 2025-12-07).
- **[PvP gold/honor]** Best honor-per-hour method cited: spam daily BG plus Arathi Basin regardless of preference ("most honor per hour"); world-PvP camping Stormwind for 3 weeks yielded 50-100k honor/day at R14 push — [[d:rogue#1447078643070406656]], [[d:rogue#1447080224947966106]] (Olivertwist/Blindtongue, 2025-12-07).
- **[PvP envenom/prep]** An "envenom / prep build" is mentioned as especially good for WSG flag-carrying but requires much more gear investment to be competitive — [[d:rogue#1447082390802661386]] (Blindtongue, 2025-12-07).
- **[R14 gear]** At Rank 14, most PvP offhand weapons are itemized at 2.9 speed, seemingly recycled from warrior itemization, considered a poor fit for rogues ("no benefit in using a slow offhand") — [[d:rogue#1444417127271366739]], [[d:rogue#1444417263703953620]] (Blindtongue/Rheinwg, 2025-11-29).
- **[PvP openers]** Facing mechanics: Backstab fails if target has turned to face the attempted "back" (i.e., if they turned around while casting, their back may now be toward the front); not a bug, a mechanic — [[d:rogue#1447019057592406159]], [[d:rogue#1447020454379257907]] (Suade/Weirdthing, 2025-12-07).
- **[PvP combo transfer]** Combo-point transfer via Cheap Shot on one target then Kidney Shot after using SnD-transfer tech is discussed as a way to bank a strong opener, though duration may only last 1 combo point's worth — [[d:rogue#1445118483875500153]], [[d:rogue#1445119179261870232]], [[d:rogue#1445119487128113264]] (Lightemgee/Rheinwg, 2025-12-02).
- **[PvP]** Rough class spec distribution estimate in PvP per Blindtongue: roughly 80% Assassination, 15% Hemorrhage(Sub/Combat-Hemo), 5% Backstab among active PvP rogues — [[d:rogue#1447874252987564093]] (2025-12-09; anecdotal, single source).
