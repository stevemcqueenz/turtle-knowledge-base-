## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
(Note: this chunk's discussion mostly predates 1.18.1 and covers the "CC2" class-rework patch of Nov 2024, which the community treats as the precursor state. Recorded as background/era context; changes explicitly still-current as of 1.18.1 are not distinguishable from this chunk alone, but the underlying math/mechanics discussed generally still apply unless superseded.)
- **[General]** CC2 patch reworked all 3 rogue trees: Combat got baseline Ghostly Strike, Blade Flurry cooldown halved, new reactive ability Surprise Attack; Assassination reworked around poisons/Envenom; Subtlety turned into a raid-support "buff bot" spec (Honor Among Thieves, Improved Expose Armor/IEA, Tricks of the Trade, Marked/Death Mark, Hemorrhage as raid-wide physical damage debuff) — [[d:rogue#1302091771559546991]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-02), [[d:rogue#1303504233508634727]] (Kemeia, 2024-11-06).
- **[Mechanics]** Combo points no longer drop on target swap unless generating a new combo point on the new target (persistence change) — [[d:rogue#1302091771559546991]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-02); confirmed still valued later as a major QoL win — [[d:rogue#1318658590457860167]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-13).
- **[Mechanics]** Ruthlessness reworked to 100% chance to retain a combo point after a finisher (previously chance-based), and can "drift" the retained combo point to a different target when the finisher is next used there — [[d:rogue#1260216495535816844]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-07-09; 📌PINNED), [[d:rogue#1318658590457860167]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-13; called "the biggest QoL upgrade I've ever seen").
- **[Bugs at patch launch]** Deadly Poison V no longer applies from both weapons (only 1 debuff instead of 2); Corrosive Poison and Deadly Poison overwrite each other rather than stacking; different rogues' Deadly/Corrosive stacks overwrite each other; Corrosive Poison rank 2 uncraftable/unlearnable despite having Deadly V — [[d:rogue#1302601508423991377]] (The Messiah Of Ignite, 2024-11-03; links to bug reports #8700, #8703, #8673), [[d:rogue#1306575136089964565]] (Nettlefield, 2024-11-14). Devs later confirmed intent was that different rogues' poisons should NOT be mutually exclusive — [[d:rogue#1313605231434662038]] (RUTHINAS quoting patch note, 2024-12-04). Stichling later found a working Corrosive rank 2 — [[d:rogue#1316682823457046591]] (Stichling🥷🏼, 2024-12-12).
- **[Bugs]** Hemorrhage debuff tooltip doesn't update after learning Bloody Mess (visual only); Efficient Poisons talent's "45% chance to not consume a charge" description doesn't map to how Envenom actually works; Surprise Attack reportedly doesn't trigger from off-hand dodges — [[d:rogue#1306405050125652031]] (BloodPhobos, 2024-11-14; single source, unconfirmed).
- **[Bugs]** Deadly Throw does not benefit from Ruthlessness or proc Relentless Strikes — confirmed as a bug by devs, to be fixed; Relentless Strikes correctly not proccing on a missed finisher is intended — [[d:rogue#1318831548010725446]] (Premium (Retired) quoting dev, 2024-12-17). Deadly Throw + Throwing Weapon Specialization caused 100% self-interrupt — [[d:rogue#1306405050125652031]] (BloodPhobos, 2024-11-14).
- **[Design change]** Hemorrhage moved from row 5 to row 3, reduced to 1 rank, changed from flat damage to +2% physical damage debuff (50 charges, up from 30), energy cost raised 35→45, now subject to weapon normalization; dev comment: intended as a support tool that scales with gear — [[d:rogue#1310205066585571388]] (Gurfark quoting patch notes, 2024-11-24).
- **[Design change]** Surprise Attack (new Combat ability): usable only after target dodges your attack, deals ~120-149% weapon damage on next MH swing (not instant), costs 10 energy, grants 1 combo point; widely criticized as low DPE and clunky since it consumes energy immediately but only fires on next MH swing, sometimes getting cancelled by other abilities — [[d:rogue#1303069468469235874]]/[[d:rogue#1306054406868832297]] (multiple, Nov 2024), math by Weirdthing/Papairoh below.
- **[Design change]** Sprint/Evasion cooldown reduced from 5 min to 4 min — [[d:rogue#1303551841392398356]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-06).
- **[Design change]** Mace stun proc chance reduced from 6% to 5%; Crippling Poison slow reduced 70%→50% — [[d:rogue#1308565626339459193]] (BloodPhobos, 2024-11-20; disputed as unwarranted nerfs).
- **[Sentiment]** Widespread community view (many posters) that rogue DPS fell well behind Warrior/Mage/Hunter after CC2 due to lack of resource/ability scaling with gear; devs said rogue "is being worked on" repeatedly through Dec 2024 — [[d:rogue#1306240899939962890]] (Nab, 2024-11-19; cites turtlogs.com ranking: rogue dropped from 43rd to 119th place overall between quarters), [[d:rogue#1315362039832514622]] (Kantrul quoting dev design intent, 2024-12-08), [[d:rogue#1314542611851247687]] (Lhian, 2024-12-06; +13), [[d:rogue#1316870860904075374]] (Lhian, 2024-12-13).
- **[Balance pass, Dec 2024/Jan 2025]** A first round of post-CC2 balance changes shipped that added AP scaling to poisons and made Surprise Attack instant-cast; community view was this meaningfully helped (Assassination patchwerk parse went from 1039 to 1129 dps for one poster, a ~15% jump attributed mostly to the poison change) but Combat remained ahead and Subtlety was said to need a separate later pass — [[d:rogue#1327442817446051892]] (Hesk, 2025-01-11), [[d:rogue#1323040098769436714]] (Zaszz, 2024-12-30), [[d:rogue#1319759300163539044]] (Lhian, 2024-12-21: "if its about subtlety, we will revisit the spec at a later date").
- **[Relentless Strikes rework]** Changed so every finisher costs a flat 5 energy less (rather than an average ~5 energy refund per combo point spent on crit): net effect is a bigger discount at low combo points (9/13/17/21 energy saved at 1-4 CP, vs previous 5/10/15/20) and unchanged value at 5 CP — described as "at worst neutral, at best a buff" — [[d:rogue#1320490662272958516]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-23).
- **[Blade Flurry cooldown churn]** Iterated multiple times: originally 2 min high-haste version, changed to 1 min with less attack-speed bonus (widely preferred — cleanly aligned with 2-min activatable trinket cooldowns), then to 1.5 min with attack speed restored (criticized because 1.5 min doesn't evenly divide most trinket CDs, effectively wasting the extra 30s), with players requesting Adrenaline Rush be 4 min / Blade Flurry 1 min instead for cleaner CD alignment — [[d:rogue#1320605627851997224]] (-Z?-, 2024-12-23), [[d:rogue#1320608523435905054]] (-Z?-, 2024-12-23), [[d:rogue#1327431637817884785]] and [[d:rogue#1327431859583455316]] (-Z?-, 2025-01-11).
- **[Weapon skill formula, later patch]** Weapon skill scaling made fully linear: every point of skill = 0.2% hit + 2% reduced glancing-blow penalty (30% max glancing reduction total); the +5 weapon-skill training book costs ~300g; racial weapon-skill bonuses (e.g. Orc axes, Human sword/mace) reduced from +5 to +3, reportedly because rogues could otherwise sit at 315 skill "by existing" — [[d:rogue#1330365330635358392]] (Epickalen, 2025-01-19), corroborated — [[d:rogue#1332919208379678783]] (Epickalen, 2025-01-26: notes 308 used to be the practical breakpoint, 310 diminishing returns, before smoothing).
- **[Hit-cap change, ~March 2025]** Per posted patch notes: fighting bosses/mobs 3+ levels above you no longer applies an extra 1% hit suppression, moving the boss hit cap from 9% to 8%; the old "soft cap" of 305 weapon skill (which existed only to counteract that suppression) no longer exists; weapon skill's benefit between 300-304 and 305-308 was smoothed so every point gives the same flat 0.2% hit / 2% glancing reduction — [[d:rogue#1346601561182179389]] (Premium (Retired) quoting patch notes, 2025-03-05).
- **[Late-chunk standing, March 2025]** Zaszz's forum-feedback summary: comparing top-1, top-20 and top-50 Warrior vs Rogue parses shows a consistent ~18% DPS gap in Warrior's favor; describes devs' changes so far (poison scaling, poison+Windfury interaction, Assassination and Subtlety both being raid-viable at all) as "enormously positive" even though more warrior-parity work is wanted — [[d:rogue#1353457604638146573]] (Zaszz, 2025-03-23).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[PvE]** Combat (Swords/Axes) widely regarded the safest/strongest all-around PvE spec: best average damage, best defensive tools (parry/dodge, Riposte, Flourish) and cooldowns — [[d:rogue#1305256869396222014]] (Frumie, 2024-11-10), [[d:rogue#1301998614062563369]] (Premium (Retired), 2024-11-01: predicted Assassination good early via Corrosive Poison but falling off hard by AQ40/Naxx+).
- **[PvE]** Optimal raid Combat build per Premium (Retired): swords, minor debate over Lethality (armor pen, ~9%/2.43% overall dmg via crit) vs Serrated Blades (~5% overall physical dmg increase, ~2x stronger per Papairoh's math since applies to non-crit white damage too) — [[d:rogue#1301999736840130592]] (Premium (Retired), 2024-11-02, talent links included), [[d:rogue#1308076709765185546]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-17; +2).
- **[PvE]** Combat Daggers vs Combat Swords: daggers saw the biggest CC2 buff to combo-point generation (Surprise Attack + Improved Backstab), narrowing the gap with swords, but swords still considered slightly stronger overall due to Aggression + Surprise Attack synergy — [[d:rogue#1303501783880171600]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-06).
- **[PvE]** Assassination: limited by non-scaling poison damage; considered a "meme"/early-game-only spec by many; Envenom seen as underwhelming ("makes my poison ticks go from 171 to 210") — [[d:rogue#1306058593291997234]] (Premium (Retired), 2024-11-10), [[d:rogue#1316349838602010634]] (Nab, 2024-12-11). Zaszz reported Assassination fun and capable of ~1300 DPS on Patchwerk with a lucky parse and perfect comp — [[d:rogue#1313989969756291154]] (Zaszz, 2024-12-05; single source, optimistic outlier).
- **[PvE Support]** Subtlety reworked into a party/raid support spec (IEA armor debuff, Hemorrhage physical dmg debuff, Tricks of the Trade crit buff, Marked for Death/Death Mark burst window). Heavily contested: proponents say it meaningfully raises other DPS (2 warriors measured +12k and +9k dps gain with support) — [[d:rogue#1307294797849034774]] (Nettlefield, 2024-11-16); critics call it energy-starved, low personal DPS (~550 dps behind top warriors even w/ full support buffs stacked), and mechanically frustrating (Death Mark can be dodged/missed/parried, wasting a 10-min CD; only 6-sec party buff) — [[d:rogue#1302456141158023168]] (-Z?-, 2024-11-03; +0), [[d:rogue#1310360691118440479]] (Papairoh citing a raid log, 2024-11-25). Disputed: RUTHINAS argues Sub is "greatly underestimated," citing that 50 Hemorrhage charges of +4% physical damage totals to 200% extra damage across a raid if fully consumed — [[d:rogue#1310322950129319969]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-25).
- **[PvE]** Rogue's raid utility (Improved Expose Armor ~5% raid physical dmg buff, interrupt, Distract) considered weaker than Warrior's free Sunder Armor (~35-40% physical dmg buff) which costs no talent points/combo points — [[d:rogue#1310350720721092758]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-25), [[d:rogue#1315729988766662666]] (Papairoh, 2024-12-09).
- **[Leveling]** All 3 reworked trees considered leveling-viable; Combat/Backstab-Subtlety hybrids suited to group leveling, pure Combat swords/axes fastest/smoothest due to abundant quest-reward weapon upgrades — [[d:rogue#1260216495535816844]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-07-09; 📌PINNED), [[d:rogue#1310955868933586985]] (Tropeco, 2024-12-01).
- **[PvP]** Rogues considered strong/top-tier in PvP by several posters (stun-lock, resets, cooldown-dependent burst) — [[d:rogue#1309924759722790995]] (deca, 2024-11-23; +8: "Rogues are the most dominant class in PvP right now"), disputed by a poster asking for nerfs — [[d:rogue#1311418939808546966]] (deca, 2024-11-28; +9 "We're already hella strong"), [[d:rogue#1314611169645822002]] (Holbart, 2024-12-06; single source complaint).
- **[Combat Sword vs Dagger, endgame, later chunk]** Zaszz (2025-01-17): Combat Swords is currently the highest DPS spec both single-target and AoE; Daggers has no clear DPS niche over swords at that time but is still viable and more fun for some (Seal Fate dagger playstyle) — [[d:rogue#1329826864226897920]] (Zaszz, 2025-01-17). Detailed pros/cons list from Eylo (2025-04-12): Swords have higher sustained/longer-fight DPS, smoother talent tree, best open-world leveling, easier PvP retalent; Daggers have higher burst/short-fight DPS, extra combo point chance off Backstab, higher crit, easier to get in raids (less contested loot), more engaging rotation, but lower DPS in long high-end fights — [[d:rogue#1360255660201082970]] (Eylo, 2025-04-12).
- **[Full spec breakdown, Eylo, near end of chunk]** Rogue has 6 practical PvE "specs": Combat Sword/Axe (high sustain, easy, boring), Combat Dagger (high burst, engaging, medium difficulty), Assassination (mediocre sustain, poison procs fun, hard difficulty), Subtlety Support (low personal dps, helps allies, medium-hard), Combat Mace (high sustain, satisfying crits, easy), Seal Fate Dagger (high sustain, extra combo points off crits, gear-dependent on ~30% crit, medium difficulty) — [[d:rogue#1360572395647402118]] (Eylo, 2025-04-12).
- **[Envenom/Assassination trajectory]** By March-April 2025, Assassination Envenom considered raid-viable and close to Combat in single-target (Zaszz: 1600 dps on Noth reported by Epickalen; devs credited for poison + Windfury overlap and general poison scaling fixes) though still limited by widespread poison immunities in some fights (see Dungeons section) — [[d:rogue#1342689792609095802]] (Epickalen, 2025-02-22), [[d:rogue#1353457604638146573]] (Zaszz, 2025-03-23).
- **[Subtlety by chunk's end]** Some posters (e.g. returning players) express renewed enthusiasm for Subtlety support as a novel, non-"spam Slice and Dice/Backstab" playstyle; Lhian (dev) states "sub rogue is incredible support, just the gain from having more than 1 is pretty negligible" (i.e. only 1 Sub rogue per raid is valuable) — [[d:rogue#1331689119759208491]] (Lhian, 2025-01-22), [[d:rogue#1358949678301053210]] (Bones, 2025-04-08).

## Leveling
- **[General]** RUTHINAS's pinned leveling primer: no single "ideal" leveling build; key talents are Improved Gouge (lets you bandage safely mid-fight, even vs 2-3 mobs combined with Blind/Kidney Shot), Lightning Reflexes + Deflection (avoidance synergizes with Riposte/Setup), Improved Dual Wield + Precision (core Combat DPS/hit), Ruthlessness (100% combo retain + "drift" to new target), Improved Blade Tactics, Relentless Strikes, Improved Ambush (big opener) — [[d:rogue#1260216495535816844]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-07-09; 📌PINNED +1).
- **[Rotation basics, pinned]** Core loop: keep Slice and Dice up near-constantly, refresh at 1-2 combo points post-opener for energy efficiency; use Rupture over Eviscerate if target will live long enough for the DoT to matter, else Eviscerate near death; use Kidney Shot on non-immune trash to relieve tank/healer pressure; use Flourish (parry buff) vs multi-target-melee or knockback mobs — [[d:rogue#1260222269108785162]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-07-09; 📌PINNED).
- **[Combat-dagger leveling build, exact order]** Improved Gouge → 2/5 Lightning Reflexes → Precision → Blade Flurry → Improved Backstab → (optional Remorseless Attacks if solo, else skip to Subtlety) → Opportunity → Improved Ambush → 1/5 Deflection (parry > dodge due to "parry haste") → Dual Wield Specialization → Blade Rush → Close Quarters Combat → Malice → Ruthlessness → Improved Blade Tactics → Relentless Strikes → Lethality; leftover points to Improved Sprint. Respec at 60 — [[d:rogue#1312496085784072232]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-30; detailed, exact talent order).
- **[Combat/Assassination leveling order, general]** Go for Precision, Riposte, Dual Wield Spec, Blade Flurry ASAP; after Adrenaline Rush head into Ruthlessness, Improved Blade Tactics, Relentless Strikes (Assassination); Lightning Reflexes + Setup pair well but optional; Remorseless Attacks less reliable while leveling in dungeons (competing for last hits) — [[d:rogue#1304919725226332285]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-10), [[d:rogue#1309688946456002580]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-23), [[d:rogue#1305260908741660836]] (Frumie build link, 2024-11-11: `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/rogue?points=CoABAAAAAAAAAAYQooIBAoBSAYBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA%3D`, up to level ~48; take Remorseless Strikes, Blade Flurry + upgrade, only one weapon-spec line).
- **[Weapon choice while leveling]** Before level 20 (no poisons) offhand speed matters less; after choosing a weapon specialization (Hack & Slash or Close Quarters Combat), keep MH slower and OH faster matching your weapon-spec choice — [[d:rogue#1310436315921125448]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-25). Good quest-reward swords: Scarlet Monastery rewards, Vanquisher Sword, Thrash Blade; Combat swords/axes progression very smooth due to abundant dungeon quest rewards — [[d:rogue#1310326879861084251]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-25), [[d:rogue#1310955868933586985]] (Tropeco, 2024-12-01).
- **[Frontstabbing]** A technique of exploiting position detection to Backstab while a mob is facing you; usable solo but "unwieldy"/inconsistent, causes energy overcap or missed Blade Flurry windows; not necessary if using an Ambush/Improved Ambush-heavy Subtlety build instead — [[d:rogue#1260216495535816844]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-07-09), [[d:rogue#1310955868933586985]] (Tropeco, 2024-12-01), [[d:rogue#1312496085784072232]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-30).
- **[Hit/miss vs lower-level mobs]** Vs an even-level target: 5% miss chance on yellow (special) attacks, 24% on white/auto attacks while dual wielding; vs a raid boss (skull, +3 levels): 8% yellow miss, 27% white miss while DW; getting to 5/5 Precision (or equivalent hit) while leveling is enough to not miss yellow attacks against even-con mobs — [[d:rogue#1305275399256670310]] (Frumie, 2024-11-11; notes these numbers are current as of the most recent Turtle patch, differ slightly from vanilla's 9% boss miss "for complicated reasons").

## Rotation: single target
- **[Combat/general]** Small-combo-point finishers are more energy-efficient for maintaining buffs (Slice and Dice, Envenom): a 1-combo-point SnD costs ~65-75 energy for 9s uptime (~7.2-8.3 energy/sec of duration); a 5-combo SnD costs 225 energy for 21s (~10.7 energy/sec); each extra combo point buys diminishing extra duration seconds while its build cost stays high — [[d:rogue#1307983356675358721]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-18), full GCD/energy-efficiency breakdown — [[d:rogue#1316419254165438515]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-11), continued — [[d:rogue#1316419770953764946]] and [[d:rogue#1316420781156073552]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-11).
- **[Combat/CP economy]** A single combo point costs 35/40/60 energy depending on the generator used (Sinister Strike/Backstab/etc.), with talents modifying this (Improved Backstab ≈46.5 energy/CP on average; Seal Fate scales with crit chance) — [[d:rogue#1316419254165438515]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-11).
- **[Combat, dagger]** Backstab DPE math (Weirdthing, using a specific weapon example): Surprise Attack ≈25 DPE (raw, later found flawed — corrected below), Backstab ≈15.2 DPE using ((149×1.5+225)×1.2×1.7)/60 — [[d:rogue#1303289579528388609]] (Weirdthing, 2024-11-05; author flags his own SA math as wrong in-message).
- **[Combat, dagger, corrected math]** Papairoh's corrected DPE using BiS-ish stats (45% crit, 16% hit, 315 weapon skill, 2150 AP, 408 MH weapon damage, 1.8 speed): Surprise Attack ≈17.95 damage/energy; Backstab ≈35.4 damage/energy (75% crit, 20% hit assumed, effective 56.25 energy cost after 5-energy Relentless Strikes refund on crit). Conclusion: Surprise Attack is a combo-point-generation tool, not a DPS gain over Backstab — use it mainly when needing cheap combo points, not for raw damage — [[d:rogue#1303383844820353045]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-05), [[d:rogue#1303384613892128800]] and [[d:rogue#1303385534206578770]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-05).
- **[Combat swords]** Sword rogues do ~50-60% of total damage from white/auto damage in raids; auto attacks have a 40% chance to glance a raid boss with reduced damage; each point of weapon skill reduces glancing penalty by ~2%, so 15 points of weapon skill above the cap negates it — reaching 315 weapon skill essentially caps glancing loss — [[d:rogue#1307045815096705054]] (Zaszz, 2024-11-15).
- **[Assassination/Combat, Eviscerate vs Backstab efficiency]** With Relentless Strikes, a 5-point Eviscerate is more energy-efficient than a Backstab in almost all cases (exception below ~3 combo points depending on gear); dagger rogues can sustain a 5-point Eviscerate and 5-point SnD every 30s — [[d:rogue#1303808140495683645]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-06), [[d:rogue#1310387126159806526]] general 5cp Evisc/SnD every 30s cadence — [[d:rogue#1310409876060770314]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-25).
- **[Combat, weapon swap]** Poison stacking rotation: use Deadly Poison + Instant Poison, then swap weapons once Deadly hits 5 stacks to a second Instant-Poison weapon pair to keep the Instant proc chance active while refreshing Deadly less often; ideally maintain 4 weapons (2 Deadly, 2 Instant) — [[d:rogue#1306575136089964565]] (Nettlefield, 2024-11-14), [[d:rogue#1310112423755386921]] (Darg, 2024-11-24).
- **[Hemorrhage damage formula]** `Lethality = 0.06 × Lethality Rank`; `Sinister Calling = 0.01 × Sinister Calling Rank`; `Damage Factor = 1.10 × (1 + Sinister Calling)`; `Critical Factor = 2 + Lethality`; normalization factor 2.4 for swords, 1.7 for daggers; `Normal Hemo Damage = [(AP/14 × Normalization) + Weapon Damage] × Damage Factor`; `Crit Hemo Damage = Normal × Critical Factor` — [[d:rogue#1315766135999959142]] (Zaszz, 2024-12-09). Normalization helps fast/dagger weapons more, but since a slow MH is usually still BiS (for Windfury/sword-spec procs) normalization rarely matters in practice except with unusually fast raid MH weapons — [[d:rogue#1315766327906144377]] (Papairoh, 2024-12-09).
- **[Envenom vs Rupture, dummy test]** Weirdthing: with limited energy/CP you can only maintain ~80% uptime on one of Envenom or Rupture, not both without losing Relentless Strikes procs; Rupture (with Deadly/Corrosive Poison) outperformed Envenom (best with double Instant Poison) on both a small target dummy and a boss dummy — [[d:rogue#1318113823277711370]] (Weirdthing, 2024-12-16).
- **[Combat Dagger vs Assassination Envenom, dummy test]** Combat build ~20 dps ahead of an Envenom/Assassination build on a 312-317 weapon-skill boss dummy over 3-min fights (530 vs ~510 dps in first minute due to Adrenaline Rush burst); small dummy roughly equal — [[d:rogue#1316840250059788339]] (Weirdthing, 2024-12-12), corroborated — [[d:rogue#1316682823457046591]] (Stichling🥷🏼, 2024-12-12: "Combat Dagger vs. Envenom are quite equal, with only a bit of an advantage for Envenom").
- **[Envenom vs HfB (Hemo-for-Blood?)/Rupture, extended dummy test]** Stichling🥷🏼's ~2-hour test on lvl-60 dummy (no buffs, 145s runs, IP+DP poisons): 3rd/worst was HfB+Serrated Blades (no Envenom) build that used to be best pre-patch; 2nd best was "Boss Envenom"; best was Envenom-only + Serrated Blades with all poison talents and no HfB, keeping Relentless Strikes uptime and 20-energy regen near 100% via mostly 4-CP SnD/Envenom and Rupture only when it didn't collide — [[d:rogue#1323577585472639048]] (Stichling🥷🏼, 2024-12-31).
- **[Basic single-target loop, RUTHINAS's own play]** Open, then immediately Slice and Dice, then Envenom (using the free Ruthlessness combo point), then Rupture for Taste for Blood; refresh whichever of the three buffs is closest to expiring as close to 0s remaining as possible, regardless of current combo points; spend all other time on Sinister Strike — [[d:rogue#1344440373543698526]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-02-27).
- **[Combat macro, priority SS over SA]** Weirdthing's SuperMacro function prioritizes Sinister Strike over Surprise Attack when energy ≥60 (i.e., under Adrenaline Rush) and reverses priority otherwise, to avoid losing SA's energy investment — [[d:rogue#1320706483775864832]] (Weirdthing, 2024-12-23), updated version incorporating a Riposte check — [[d:rogue#1352690975918526556]] (Weirdthing, 2025-03-21).
- **[Windfury/proc weapon-speed interaction]** Windfury Totem (and similar "extra attack" procs) has an internal cooldown, so a slower main-hand weapon benefits slightly more per proc since its higher per-hit damage isn't diluted by proc frequency; the same logic applies generally to any static-interval proc effect — [[d:rogue#1338606595952742522]] (Epickalen, 2025-02-11).
- **[Poison DPS testing evolving through balance passes]** Multiple rounds of dummy testing on Improved Poisons vs Vile Poisons and Instant/Deadly/Corrosive combos as poison mechanics changed: Weirdthing found Improved Poisons + Instant/Deadly V (swords) or double-Instant (daggers) consistently best across several re-tests — [[d:rogue#1319962255965818893]] and [[d:rogue#1319962906690846835]] (Weirdthing, 2024-12-22), [[d:rogue#1320728223478452244]] (Weirdthing, 2024-12-23), later re-confirmed with AP-scaled poisons: Improved Poisons IP/IP vs Vile Poisons close (~400-1000 dmg difference over a fight depending on combo), "you can't go wrong with either" — [[d:rogue#1356658037045072054]] (Weirdthing, 2025-04-01). After AP scaling was added, a 5% Improved Poisons proc-rate bump was estimated by Weirdthing to add ~128 DPS in a poison-focused build (from ~174 to ~182 Instant Poison procs over 3 minutes at 1800 AP) — [[d:rogue#1356227773991227402]] (Weirdthing, 2025-03-31).
- **[Hemorrhage raid-impact math, restated]** Hemorrhage's 50-charge, 4%-physical-damage-per-stack debuff is mathematically equivalent to the raid dealing 2x the average physical-hit damage as bonus damage every time the full debuff is consumed (e.g. 500 avg hit → 1000 bonus dmg per full consumption); RUTHINAS argues this makes Hemorrhage the single highest damage-per-energy ability in the class despite not showing on personal damage meters — [[d:rogue#1323408798563565628]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-31), reiterated — [[d:rogue#1331680433070407791]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-01-21), [[d:rogue#1339496277347532923]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-02-13), and by a class ambassador — [[d:rogue#1343195208988233798]] (Dracilli quoting Meme Spec Discord's Subtlety writeup, 2025-02-23).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Combat]** Blade Flurry is the only real AoE tool (haste-based cleave to a second target), on halved cooldown after CC2, but energy/combo-point system makes trash pulls of 3-4+ mobs awkward compared to single-target fights unless mobs are split; multiple posters wanted a dedicated AoE resource or a Fan of Knives-style ability — [[d:rogue#1306232120586403861]] (Fluxi, 2024-11-13; +6), [[d:rogue#1306054406868832297]] (Premium (Retired), 2024-11-13).
- **[Tank AoE threat]** For rogue off-tanking/tanking multiple mobs: open with Blade Flurry + Ghostly Strike, chain Sinister Strikes + Setup points across targets, finish combo with Flourish (party-wide parry buff, no CP cost) as Ghostly Strike is about to expire, then swap targets, losing some combo points deliberately to spread threat; Engineering grenades/target dummies help clump/peel — [[d:rogue#1314468356580966400]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-06).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit]** Yellow (special) ability miss chance vs even-level mob: 5%; vs a raid boss (+3 levels): 8% (vs vanilla's 9%, "for complicated reasons," Turtle-specific). White/auto miss while dual-wielding: 24% vs even mob, 27% vs boss. Being hit-capped on yellow attacks is stated as the single most important DPS stat, above crit/haste/AP, since a miss wastes a GCD and energy — [[d:rogue#1305275399256670310]] (Frumie, 2024-11-11), [[d:rogue#1315930632303542342]] (Rheinwg, 2024-12-10).
- **[Weapon skill]** 315 weapon skill is the new glancing-blow cap on Turtle (up from vanilla's typical numbers); each point of weapon skill above the old baseline reduces glancing damage penalty by ~2%; matters heavily for sword rogues since 40-60% of Combat-sword damage is white/auto — [[d:rogue#1304572547240562708]] (glommer question, 2024-11-09), [[d:rogue#1307045815096705054]] (Zaszz, 2024-11-15).
- **[Armor penetration]** Armor pen % must be "inverse-converted" to get real damage increase: e.g. reducing effective armor mitigation from 55% to 45% is a ~18.2% damage increase, not a flat 10 points — formula: `(1-new%)/(1-old%)` — [[d:rogue#1304314632596492288]] (Darg, 2024-11-08), clarified — [[d:rogue#1304504058098483320]] (Amphion, 2024-11-08).
- **[Haste]** Haste is multiplicative on swing timer, not simple subtraction: attack speed = base speed / (1 + haste1) / (1 + haste2) / ...; e.g. a 2.0-speed weapon with 2% talent haste, 2% gear haste, and 30% SnD becomes 2.0/(1.02×1.02×1.3) ≈ 1.48s — [[d:rogue#1306780270137053244]] (Darg, 2024-11-15), confirmed/extended — [[d:rogue#1306830805674492046]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-15).
- **[Poison scaling]** Poisons (Deadly, Instant, Corrosive) do NOT scale with attack power or spellpower on Turtle as of this patch — a recurring, widely-repeated complaint and the primary reason Assassination/poison builds were seen as falling off at higher gear levels — [[d:rogue#1306058593291997234]] (Premium (Retired), 2024-11-10), [[d:rogue#1315703693361942540]] (Darg, 2024-12-09: "poisons are fine not scaling with anything. The problem is that they just refuse to make new ranks to keep up with content").
- **[Debuff slots]** UI can only show ~half of the up to 64 debuffs applied to a boss; sunder/poison stacks may be active but not visually shown due to the UI limit — recommend SuperWoW + AdvancedCombatLogger addons and reading wowcombatlog.txt to verify — [[d:rogue#1305495321048121384]] (Nab, 2024-11-11).
- **[Weapon skill, precise formula]** Confirmed multiple times: each point of weapon skill above base gives a flat +0.2% hit chance and -2% glancing-blow damage penalty (up to the 30% max glancing reduction); 315 skill is the practical cap post-changes (up from an earlier informal ~308-310 breakpoint) — [[d:rogue#1330365330635358392]] (Epickalen, 2025-01-19), [[d:rogue#1334621125602185298]] (Epickalen, 2025-01-31: with Weapon Expertise (+5) and the Fray Island book (+3), a Combat rogue without Precision needs only 6% hit from gear; with Precision (5/5), only ~1% hit from gear needed at 310 skill).
- **[Dual-wield miss mechanics clarified]** The 24%/27% dual-wield miss penalty applies to BOTH main-hand and off-hand autos; testing with only a main-hand weapon equipped (no offhand) does not reproduce this penalty since you aren't actually dual-wielding — a commonly repeated testing mistake — [[d:rogue#1356320991583797490]] (Perceptionz, 2025-04-01).
- **[Attack-speed/haste value scaling]** Early on (dagger-heavy, low gear) auto-attacks are roughly 1/3 of total damage so 1% haste ≈ 0.33% DPS; at higher gear levels autos become closer to half of total damage, so the same 1% haste becomes worth ≈ 0.5% DPS — haste is one of the only stats whose relative value increases with better gear for rogues — [[d:rogue#1357345684390678568]] and [[d:rogue#1357346453504131163]] (Epickalen, 2025-04-03), similar estimate — [[d:rogue#1360012623005089872]] (Epickalen, 2025-04-10).
- **[Crusader proc rate note]** Crusader (weapon enchant) may proc less often on faster main-hand weapons if it is Proc-Per-Minute (PPM) based, since fewer yellow/special attacks trigger it relative to a slower weapon's fewer-but-bigger swings; stated as uncertain/unconfirmed by the poster — [[d:rogue#1332706825920970752]] (Gurfark, 2025-01-25; single source, hedged).

- **[Race stat comparison, Combat Dagger, detailed sim]** Eylo's manual math for Night Elf vs High Elf (28 Agi/18 Str vs 20 Agi/24 Str starting stats, plus High Elf's +2% agi passive): Night Elf nets ~99 AP-equivalent (24.75 dmg-equivalent) vs High Elf's ~78.5 AP-equivalent base + ~29 AP-equivalent from the 2% bonus at 500 Agi (total ~26.87 dmg-equivalent) — concludes Night Elf wins by roughly 1 DPS at endgame BiS, though High Elf's on-use racial grants 50 energy over 5s on a 3-min CD (with a 1.5s cast) as a situational tool; both are close enough that either is fine — [[d:rogue#1360380765971677327]] (Eylo, 2025-04-12).
- **[Race choice, later chunk, general]** Racial ability differences are considered minor/flavor overall except in niche builds (e.g. Human Berserking anti-synergizes with Ornate Bloodstone Dagger-style spellstrike stacking builds "for reasons"); pick for aesthetics/animation enjoyment as the practical recommendation — [[d:rogue#1328879584749555733]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-01-15). Race-by-optimal-spec table from Eylo: Human → Combat Sword + Sub Support; Dwarf → open-world PvP; Night Elf → Combat Dagger + Assassination; Gnome → premade BGs; High Elf → Combat Dagger + Assassination; Orc → Combat Axe, Sub Support, PvP; Undead → PvP; Troll → Assassination; Goblin → Combat Dagger, Assassination, Sub Support — [[d:rogue#1360228587449614468]] (Eylo, 2025-04-12).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Poison application rules, disputed/bugged at this time]** Consensus attempt: MH always Instant Poison; OH Deadly Poison for long fights (Combat) or always Deadly (Assassination); Corrosive Poison used vs poison-resistant/immune targets (elementals, mechanicals) — cited bosses: Garr, Ragnaros, Twin Emperors, Solnius; Deadly and Corrosive do NOT stack (overwrite), a known bug at the time — [[d:rogue#1306867413865857034]] (Buddy_Shawn, 2024-11-14), Solnius specifically poison-resistant — [[d:rogue#1303781478244552806]] (Scruff, 2024-11-06).
- **[Poison, math]** Improved Poisons (+10% proc rate) generally beats Vile Poisons (+20% flat poison damage) for Instant Poison: base 20% proc + Envenom's 25% = 45%; +10% from Improved Poisons → 55%, a 22.2% relative increase to proc rate/damage — larger than Vile Poisons' flat 20%. For Deadly/Corrosive stacking to 5, Improved Poisons gets you to full stacks 33% faster but extra procs past 5 stacks are wasted, so on long fights swap toward more Vile Poisons once capped — [[d:rogue#1314369622715404318]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-06), [[d:rogue#1317795596827889685]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-15).
- **[Corrosive Poison]** Requires a book costing 150g (rebuy needed even if previously read, a bug); rank 2 required Deadly Poison V known — [[d:rogue#1304191264388026440]] (Taktakhere, 2024-11-08), [[d:rogue#1302601508423991377]] (Messiah of Ignite, 2024-11-03; bug #8673).
- **[Poison scaling]** No AP/SP scaling on poison damage; multiple proposals to add AP or spellpower scaling to poisons discussed but not implemented in this window — [[d:rogue#1310664712018595850]] (shadowlink250, 2024-11-25), scaling-via-spellpower/nature-power specifically preferred by RUTHINAS as a way to create a distinct "poison specialist" gearing path — [[d:rogue#1318180870472863774]] and [[d:rogue#1318186842092212335]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-11).
- **[Poison scaling added later]** A subsequent balance pass (Jan 2025) added AP scaling to poisons; described as a major, well-received fix that pushed Assassination Envenom into raid viability — [[d:rogue#1327442817446051892]] (Hesk, 2025-01-11), [[d:rogue#1353457604638146573]] (Zaszz, 2025-03-23; also credits a poison+Windfury interaction fix). Weirdthing's later math example: with 1800 AP, Instant Poison's proc damage grew to ~267 dmg (0.05% of AP added) — used in a ~128 DPS estimate for a 5% Improved Poisons bump — [[d:rogue#1356227773991227402]] (Weirdthing, 2025-03-31).
- **[Envenom scaling limits]** Even after AP scaling, Envenom-focused Agitating Poison threat/damage does NOT get the AP-scaling benefit itself (Vile Poisons only affects the damage portion, not bonus threat, and Agitating Poison doesn't scale with AP) — noted specifically for rogue tanks — [[d:rogue#1346542096923754549]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-03-04).
- **[Poison uptime/logistics]** A recurring complaint for newer/casual players: poisons require manual reapplication and weapon-swapping to get full value (switching Instant for trash, periodic Deadly/Corrosive for bosses, keeping Corrosive for immune enemies), which is seen as more tedious than "buff and forget" mechanics on other classes; poisons made mostly via Pickpocketing reagents while leveling — [[d:rogue#1348710019528523928]] (Rheinwg, 2025-03-10), [[d:rogue#1354940256964710531]] (Epickalen, 2025-03-28).
- **[Poison immunity while leveling]** Elemental-type mobs are poison-immune throughout leveling until Corrosive Poison is obtained, a real cost for players wanting to stay in a poison-Assassination identity while leveling — [[d:rogue#1328584038872776796]] (Blindtongue, 2025-01-14).
- **[Non-primary poisons]** Beyond the primary damage poison, useful utility poisons while leveling: Crippling Poison (great vs targets that flee at low HP), Mind-Numbing Poison, Wound Poison — typically carry 1 stack of each utility poison plus 2 stacks of the primary damage poison; expect roughly a 5% damage increase from using any poison at all vs none — [[d:rogue#1322714869669167179]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-29).
- **[Weapon-swap Envenom rotation, refined]** Ideal Envenom setup: 2 "secondary" weapons carrying Deadly + Corrosive (used to open and stack poisons) and 2 "main" weapons carrying Instant Poison (used for the bulk of the fight); swap to secondaries only to refresh Deadly/Corrosive stacks — makes little sense outside dedicated Envenom builds since getting to 5 stacks reliably fast is otherwise not worth the complexity — [[d:rogue#1324452398273400996]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-01-03).

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[Combat/general DPS consumable stack]** For maximal Combat DPS: flask, Juju Flurry (haste, "actually fixed to not be a slight debuff on this server"), Potion of Quickness, Kiss of the Spider trinket, Oil of Immolation; Troll racial Berserking (10-15% attack speed) stacks with the above; with Tempest's Rage + Eskhandar's set, near-100% uptime on multiple haste procs is achievable, though those specific weapons are said to be low base damage (best used pre-BWL/AQ40) — [[d:rogue#1310692077008588864]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-26), [[d:rogue#1340348681463009280]] and [[d:rogue#1340360073687339049]] (RUTHINAS/Epickalen, 2025-02-15).
- **[Haste sources, general]** Slice and Dice = +30% attack speed; Blade Flurry = +20% attack speed if talent-improved; Troll Berserking = 10-15%; Flourish (level 42, +20% parry) grants extra attacks via "parry haste" similar to a temporary attack-speed increase — [[d:rogue#1340348681463009280]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-02-15).
- **[Bloodfang set for burst, PvP]** A theorycrafted burst window stacking poison-chance sources: 30% base + 10% Improved Poisons + 3-piece Bloodfang (5%) + 25% Envenom + 30% "Venomous Tongue" trinket/proc = up to 100% poison-application chance for 20 seconds, paired with ~100% attack-speed uptime on fast (1.5-1.7 speed) weapons via Eshkandar's Claw + Tempest's Rage — a min-maxed burst PvP concept, not confirmed tested in raid — [[d:rogue#1321784433849794614]] (Blindtongue, 2024-12-26; single source, theorycraft only).
- **[Ornate Bloodstone Dagger build]** A niche spellstrike-stacking OH build: works best paired with a fast (~1.3 speed) MH dagger under Slice and Dice so the OH's spellstrike proc chance approaches "every swing"; scales from spellpower (unlike Blade of Eternal Darkness, which doesn't scale from SP but procs more reliably off a 1-sec ICD); best with a healer able to HoT the rogue, theoretically up to 250 dps from spellstrike procs alone if they land constantly, though real uptime is lower due to swing-timer misalignment — [[d:rogue#1321328850323640371]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-25), [[d:rogue#1337376054817456210]] (Gurfark, 2025-02-07).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Energy]** The central, recurring class complaint: Energy does not scale with gear the way Warrior Rage does, causing Rogue DPS to fall behind at high gear levels; multiple proposed fixes discussed (none confirmed implemented in this window): energy refund on ability crits (~50%), haste affecting energy regen, spirit-based energy regen, Honor Among Thieves energy return increase — [[d:rogue#1309936716609486928]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-24), [[d:rogue#1315703693361942540]] (Darg, 2024-12-09), [[d:rogue#1315586096209592331]] (Weirdthing, 2024-12-09: names 3 possible scaling fixes — better AP-from-agility ratio, better energy regen, poisons/abilities scaling better with AP).
- **[Honor Among Thieves]** Sub talent granting energy on party crits; described as a very strong energy source in physical-damage-heavy groups — RUTHINAS reports generating 1000-1500 extra energy over a single dungeon run (Crescent Grove), equivalent to ~9-10 Adrenaline Rush casts — [[d:rogue#1314582142109351956]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-06), [[d:rogue#1318642387920355328]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-17).
- **[Tanking utility]** Rogues have no traditional taunt; Distract/target dummies (Engineering) used as a threat/clumping tool instead; some bosses that require hard taunt swaps (e.g. drakes) are called out as encounters rogues can't fully tank — [[d:rogue#1313288726012624978]] (Scruff, 2024-12-03), Envenom (with Agitating Poison) used as a threat-generation substitute for taunt at level 60 — [[d:rogue#1314468356580966400]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-06).
- **[Gouge]** Improved Gouge (talented, 5.5s duration) used to safely bandage (regenerating ~55 energy during the effect), reposition for a Backstab, block a mob from hitting an ally, interrupt a cast, or disengage — [[d:rogue#1310433604328751185]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-25).
- **[Stealth mechanics]** Shadowmeld (Night Elf) can reportedly be used to eat/drink while remaining hidden if timed after Vanish/stealth cancel, allowing a stealthed re-engage at higher HP — proposed technique, not fully confirmed by testing in this chunk — [[d:rogue#1310772927150559385]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-25; single source, untested claim/question).
- **[Distract vs Wail]** Distract is a mass/AoE-targetable ability on a 30-second cooldown requiring no farming, best for trash-pull threat management; Wail is a single-target consumable item farmed from a level-60 instance sharing a long cooldown with similar items, better suited as a boss debuff (often used by rogue tanks to bridge avoidance-cooldown gaps) — both can be used together — [[d:rogue#1321168386725511220]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-24).
- **[Adrenaline Rush timing]** Time Adrenaline Rush activation to just after the halfway point between two energy ticks, to capture an extra tick at the increased regen rate before it would have ticked anyway — [[d:rogue#1341007171168632832]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-02-17).
- **[Blade Flurry target count]** Prefer to reserve Blade Flurry for 3+-mob pulls; on 2-mob pulls the first target is often dead before the buff's value is realized — [[d:rogue#1341007171168632832]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-02-17).
- **[Badge of the Swarmguard]** Shares a 3-minute cooldown with Adrenaline Rush; community view is it's generally better saved for trash packs than bosses, but timing depends on whether a boss pull is imminent — [[d:rogue#1351190544746942464]] (-Z?-, 2025-03-17; question, no clear consensus answer given in this chunk).
- **[Thistle Tea]** Shares its cooldown pool with all other "tea" consumables and Hearthstone; multiple community requests to reduce it to 2 minutes (matching most other class-neutral CDs) or add a level-60 variant with a shorter CD were made but not confirmed implemented in this chunk — [[d:rogue#1319869641958359104]] (Premium (Retired), 2024-12-20), [[d:rogue#1320135885567823923]] (Premium (Retired), 2024-12-22). Optimal use is when a GCD is free and energy has just hit exactly 0 — [[d:rogue#1342009613259903067]] (Zaszz, 2025-02-20).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Viability]** Rogues can tank all dungeons and many raid encounters up to AQ40 using a Combat-heavy build with defensive talents layered in; Turtle-specific tools Flourish (parry-proc party buff) and Agitating Poison (threat) make this notably stronger than vanilla — [[d:rogue#1313084964258058261]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-02), [[d:rogue#1314470616279027796]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-06).
- **[Build]** Rogue tank prioritizes Riposte, Blade Flurry, Setup (Combat) then Envenom/Agitating Poison (Assassination, unlocked at 60) for threat; deliberately breaks the "always maximize combo points" habit — small SnD refreshes and frequent target swaps to spread threat are preferred over big single-target combos — [[d:rogue#1314468356580966400]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-06).
- **[Ghostly Strike]** Made baseline in CC2, significantly strengthening rogue tanking (extra dodge chance) — [[d:rogue#1313084964258058261]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-02). Reported bug: Ghostly Strike (Improved) sometimes removes Evasion and vice versa, possibly tied to Improved Ghostly Strike's dodge bonus only applying if the ability lands — [[d:rogue#1313413675893264424]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-03; unresolved/disputed, RUTHINAS initially said no bug then retracted).
- **[Seal Fate as tank]** RUTHINAS's own dummy trials (in tank gear, ~24% crit, no Improved Backstab) found Seal Fate not terrible for maintaining SnD/Envenom/Taste for Blood buffs even while frequently losing combo points to target swaps — [[d:rogue#1318158352706179123]], [[d:rogue#1318159143961362485]], [[d:rogue#1318170989326110730]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-11; single source, self-reported testing).
- **[No-taunt philosophy]** RUTHINAS argues rogue tanks don't need every-tank-has-a-taunt design: Envenom + Agitating Poison threat is high enough to simply "overtake" other tanks for aggro, and being under the avoidance cap is fine for most (not all) encounters — [[d:rogue#1324859865545445447]] and [[d:rogue#1324861665640185957]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-01-04), [[d:rogue#1327772188740288555]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-01-12: points to a dedicated tank-rogue channel on the "Meme Spec" Discord, `https://discord.gg/4czt2zyk`, for current meta).
- **[Rogue-tank key CC2 wins]** Summarized by RUTHINAS: shorter Blade Flurry CD helps leveling tanks (can skip Engineering, though still recommended); Subtlety avoidance buffs (especially stacked with another rogue); baseline Ghostly Strike; 100% Ruthlessness (called "massive" for tank energy/combo economy); Envenom at 60 giving "insane" threat when paired with Agitating Poison and fast weapons — [[d:rogue#1324868474002214962]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-01-04).
- **[Two tanking archetypes]** "Full avoidance" (cooldown-chaining to become periodically unhittable, frees healer attention entirely) vs "high avoidance" (armor/stamina-based, takes occasional hits but easier to gear and more spell-resistant); both viable, full avoidance requires very specific gear pieces — [[d:rogue#1346561690636128298]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-03-04).
- **[Combat build for leveling tank threat]** Damage = threat while leveling; prioritize avoidance talents, Riposte, Blade Flurry (multi-target threat, pair with Engineering grenades/target dummies), Setup for combo points, Adrenaline Rush as a threat cooldown; at 60, either add Improved Poisons for Agitating Poison threat within an existing build, or fully respec into a dedicated Envenom poisoner build for threat — [[d:rogue#1332944053943406615]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-01-26), [[d:rogue#1340346444640551005]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-02-15), [[d:rogue#1346540267414749337]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-03-04).
- **[Assassination tank build, later chunk]** Nettlefield's tank setup: deep Assassination for all poison-improving talents plus Envenom (major threat), relying on Agitating Poison rather than white-attack threat (which is minimized since avoidance/defense is prioritized instead); dips into Combat for dodge/parry/Riposte/Setup; skips Subtlety entirely — [[d:rogue#1346462072153702481]] (Nettlefield, 2025-03-04).
- **[Rogue tanking, endgame viability, later chunk]** Zaszz reports both a "pure dodge" setup and a "very good threat" (Envenom) setup are viable for different content; rogue tanks take the least damage of any tank class in the game, offsetting the lack of taunt somewhat; weakness remains vs poison-immune, stun-immune, or threat-drop-heavy mechanics — [[d:rogue#1342396444451147787]] (Zaszz, 2025-02-21), [[d:rogue#1347445311035150449]] (Zaszz, 2025-03-07), [[d:rogue#1326270778026491959]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-01-07: notes a skilled rogue can even solo-tank UBRS, potentially with a solo healer).
- **[Rogue tanking, dissenting view]** Premium (Retired) disputes rogue tanking's viability for leveling/new players specifically: leveling tank talents don't mitigate enough to be worth the DPS loss vs going glass-cannon, and finding groups willing to accept a rogue tank (especially with newer players unfamiliar with the concept) is harder — [[d:rogue#1346544529238392874]] and [[d:rogue#1346546108805025813]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-03-04); RUTHINAS disagrees, saying it's more about understanding the tank role generally than the rogue class specifically — [[d:rogue#1346545591806722058]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-03-04). Premium (Retired) separately states devs seem to want to "bury" rogue tanking design-wise, calling it easily fixable with a taunt + Corrosive-enabled Agitating Poison — [[d:rogue#1339278236353302642]] (Premium (Retired), 2025-02-12; single source, dev intent unconfirmed).

## PvP
- **[General standing]** Multiple posters call Rogue top-tier/dominant in PvP (stunlock, resets, cooldown-dependent burst windows) — [[d:rogue#1309924759722790995]] (deca, 2024-11-23; +8), disputed by nerf requests — [[d:rogue#1314611169645822002]] (Holbart, 2024-12-06).
- **[CC2 PvP changes]** Blind and Free Action Potion (FAP) became strong vs Warriors specifically because Warriors rely on dispels rather than immunity effects to escape movement-impair effects post-CC2; any "break" effect got relatively stronger — [[d:rogue#1302015001833373788]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-02).
- **[Dueling/1v1]** Rogues are "all cooldowns" in 1v1 — cannot reliably win without burst CDs available (roughly a 10-min cycle); considered balanced around this limitation, unlike e.g. Shaman which has no CDs at all — [[d:rogue#1309982700966383757]] (hiiklaas, 2024-11-24, responding to Papairoh 1309981759894716417).
- **[Vs Paladin]** Rogue vs Paladin duels are hard: need a full CC chain plus a cooldown to force a bubble; if trinket is on cooldown vs a Retribution paladin, a single Hammer of Justice can be lethal — [[d:rogue#1313623927003676722]] (dumbledood., 2024-12-04; single source, self-assessed as possibly a skill/gear issue).
- **[Deadly Throw in PvP]** Valued for its unique interaction: can cast Deadly Throw then immediately regular Throw with no cooldown between them; only finisher that doesn't get a Ruthlessness combo-point-retention effect (likely intentional to prevent PvP throw-spam) — [[d:rogue#1313603320119820309]] (Gurfark, 2024-12-04).
- **[Gear]** Improved dispel resistance from Vile Poisons is explicitly a PvP-only feature of that talent (no PvE effect) — [[d:rogue#1317795596827889685]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-15).
- **[BGs]** Rogues good at solo objective defense (e.g. Warsong Gulch flag room) and force-multiplying via stealth/engagement selection in larger BGs like Alterac Valley, though DoTs are a major weakness in extended fights — [[d:rogue#1310322950129319969]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-25: regularly holds WSG flag room solo vs duos), [[d:rogue#1309807217108717610]] (Gurfark, 2024-11-23).
- **[1v1 opener combo, Assassination]** A widely-effective non-stunlock 1v1 combo: Garrote → Envenom → Slice and Dice → engage a few seconds → Kidney Shot or Vanish+Cheap Shot mid-fight to re-lock once buffs are running; the deceptive opener (no visible stun) reportedly baits opponents into not popping defensive cooldowns early since they don't feel immediately endangered — [[d:rogue#1328191512265097297]] (Blindtongue, 2025-01-13), [[d:rogue#1328282085835800649]] and [[d:rogue#1328283594556444714]] (Blindtongue, 2025-01-13), corroborated by another player switching to poison-focused play — [[d:rogue#1328535268713566228]] (ravagetwo, 2025-01-14).
- **[1v1 stunlock combo, alternate]** Cheap Shot → Sinister Strike → Kidney Shot → Envenom → Slice and Dice → Thistle Tea → Vanish → Cheap Shot → Rupture is cited as capable of killing "99% of players" 1v1 without them getting to act, with Garrote/Rupture ticks remaining even if the target escapes — [[d:rogue#1328191512265097297]] (Blindtongue, 2025-01-13).
- **[Assassination PvP kiting]** Crippling Poison + fast weapons + Zandalarian Hero Medallion (stacking on-hit proc) used for extended kiting/wear-down vs Warriors, though Warlocks (Curse of Exhaustion counters Crippling) and Hunters (post-nerf) are harder matchups for a Sub/poison hybrid than before — [[d:rogue#1328535268713566228]] (ravagetwo, 2025-01-14).
- **[Sword/Axe PvP build, Riposte emphasis]** Full PvP build recommendation strongly emphasizing Riposte (deals damage, cheaper energy than Hemo, disarms the target on proc — very strong vs Warriors, Rogues, Hunters (parrying pet autos), Paladins/Enhancement Shaman, Warlocks/Druids (parrying pet/shapeshifted attacks); diminishing returns prevent repeated disarms like stuns) — [[d:rogue#1360271908829925446]] (Eylo, 2025-04-12).
- **[Improved Stealth for PvP]** Called "mandatory" for any PvP-focused Combat build — considered worth far more than the small DPS from other 5-point alternatives — [[d:rogue#1330782017712820274]] (Blindtongue, 2025-01-20).
- **[Undead race for PvP]** Undead was considered the go-to PvP rogue race for a period, valued for fear immunity/resistance to being stunned-adjacent CC and Cannibalize sustain, though noted the racial's use is more of a "free action potion" substitute than a Quickness-potion substitute — [[d:rogue#1336798758972821645]] (Epickalen, 2025-02-06).
- **[HC leveling PvP/survival build notes]** For Hardcore leveling: Remorseless Attacks (crit) considered near-mandatory, Riposte valuable for survival, Precision a must in any mode, Blade Flurry helps survivability (not just damage), Improved Sprint for escapes; best Alliance HC races Dwarf or Gnome, Horde Goblin (extra "sprint"-like racial) or Orc (shorter stun duration) — [[d:rogue#1360325720752001295]] (Eylo, 2025-03-19).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Buff/debuff tracking]** CandyDice recommended as a clutter-free Slice-and-Dice-style buff timer; Modified Power Auras for visual/audio proc alerts when exact duration doesn't matter — [[d:rogue#1316599533068881931]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-12).
- **[Combat text]** MikScrollingBattleText addon usable to visually track "auras"/floating text as a manual Surprise-Attack-proc indicator (via Interface > Advanced Options > Show Auras), or build a custom WeakAuras-like trigger for it — [[d:rogue#1307156539731345448]] (Galundra/Estocada, 2024-11-16).
- **[Logging]** SuperWoW + AdvancedCombatLogger addons recommended to verify debuff application (e.g. Deadly Poison stacks) beyond the UI's ~64-debuff display cap by reading wowcombatlog.txt directly — [[d:rogue#1305495321048121384]] (Nab, 2024-11-11).
- **[Macros]** "roid macros" and "super macros" addons can create complex conditional one-button rotation macros that closely approximate simmed play (not perfectly, but close) — [[d:rogue#1310707981096063167]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-26).
- **[Surprise Attack]** Do not macro Surprise Attack together with Sinister Strike — Sinister Strike (instant) takes priority and will consume energy first, causing Surprise Attack to be lost if you go energy-dry before your next MH swing — [[d:rogue#1310336326293389403]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-25).
- **[Target-of-target macro]** For Dust of Disappearance (Sub): use a unit-frame addon's target-of-target display or a macro that switches to target-of-target, casts Dust, then reverts, to reliably debuff whatever has aggro — [[d:rogue#1302476719504228383]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-11-03). Alternative: a mouseover macro on the friendly unit frame you want to Dust, more flexible than fixed party-frame bindings and works on target-of-target too — [[d:rogue#1336589408425476167]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-02-05).
- **[Weapon-swap addons]** ItemRack or Outfitter recommended to auto-swap to a dagger while entering Stealth (for Ambush/Backstab openers) and back to a non-dagger weapon on leaving stealth, especially for Subtlety Improved-Ambush builds; a stealth-detection check (`IsEquipped`/`ItemRack_IsSetEquipped`) macro pattern is given for conditionally casting Ambush only when the dagger set is equipped — [[d:rogue#1321919652246458448]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-26), [[d:rogue#1332886113441812553]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-01-26).
- **[Rotation macro examples]** A `/startattack` SuperMacro that checks for Slice and Dice via `UnitBuff` and casts it if missing and combo points ≥1, otherwise casts Sinister Strike then Riposte on cooldown — [[d:rogue#1327648170242605171]] (HumbleRyan, 2025-01-11; RUTHINAS cautions such macros can be actively harmful if conditions don't line up, e.g. triggering SnD right before a forced movement phase — [[d:rogue#1327753066841112629]]). A separate SuperMacro function conditionally prioritizes Sinister Strike vs Surprise Attack based on current energy (≥60 = SS first) — [[d:rogue#1320700231242879020]] and [[d:rogue#1320706483775864832]] (Weirdthing, 2024-12-23), later extended to include a Riposte check — [[d:rogue#1352690975918526556]] (Weirdthing, 2025-03-21). Azure's simpler variant removes conditionals for finishers (all cost less than Sinister Strike, so no need to gate them) — [[d:rogue#1339682215428362371]] (Azure, 2025-02-13).
- **[Proc alert addon]** ProcDoc — lightweight addon showing pulsing visual alerts for procs like Riposte, Remorseless Attacks, and other class buffs/abilities; auto-detects procs with no setup, configurable via `/procdoc`. GitHub: `https://github.com/wsmaxcy/ProcDoc` — [[d:rogue#1329339972485906454]] (Clempton, 2025-01-16).
- **[LFT/dungeon-queue note]** The dungeon-finder tool ("LFT") checks class-role combinations and dungeon level ranges server-side (not purely client-side), meaning rogue tank queuing via addon hacks around level requirements does not fully bypass server checks — [[d:rogue#1324534734696742922]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-01-03).

## Races, professions, gold
- See race breakdown under Gear section above — [[d:rogue#1317570989575507980]] (Rheinwg, 2024-12-14).
- **[Professions]** First Aid mandatory, Cooking helpful, Engineering very helpful for tanks/AoE (target dummies, grenades), Skinning a reliable early gold source — [[d:rogue#1305256869396222014]] (Frumie, 2024-11-10). Blacksmithing called "obviously mandatory" for self-crafted weapon upgrades while leveling (esp. daggers) — [[d:rogue#1309800281818529853]] (BobberyJimbo, 2024-11-23; single source, Artisan/Boaring-challenge context).
- **[Respec cost]** Respec capped at 5 gold on Turtle (much cheaper than retail scaling), encouraging build experimentation — [[d:rogue#1318627758011846698]] (RUTHINAS, 2024-12-17), reaffirmed — [[d:rogue#1341002067279351809]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-02-17: also notes a 350g multi-spec device exists for even easier build swapping).
- **[Leveling speed items]** "Slow & Steady" glyph recommended for players who want to experience all of the added leveling content (new quests, new instance wings, new dungeons/zones); "tents" can be used to skip past certain level ranges, and tents + Warmode grant a large XP multiplier for players who just want to reach 60 fast (at the cost of gank exposure) — [[d:rogue#1341004427175268435]] (RUTHINAS, 2025-02-17).
- **[Professions, additional]** Engineering valued for HC play specifically (extra stuns/escape tools via grenades); Alchemy for potions as a "second life" and battle elixirs; Skinning for self-sufficient gold; Enchanting needs greens disenchanted (often paired with Tailoring for cloth→enchanting mats), of marginal value unless playing a cloth-wearer; Jewelcrafting has some rogue-usable trinkets/necks but nothing essential — [[d:rogue#1329020698542346291]] and [[d:rogue#1329039988746092628]] (Epickalen, 2025-01-15).
- **[Pickpocketing mechanic note]** Pickpocketing while moving is reportedly semi-bugged: it plays the success sound and loot message but doesn't actually grant gold/items, though a subsequent attempt (presumably once actually stopped or lined up correctly) does work — [[d:rogue#1328021378854813750]] (Amphion, 2025-01-12; single source, unconfirmed root cause).

- SS (Sinister Strike) energy cost briefly bugged at 35 energy for about a week (base is higher) — [[d:rogue#1316869558811754547]] (Premium (Retired), 2024-12-13).
- Deadly Throw self-interrupts frequently even while standing still, seemingly unrelated to actual interruption (bug #4261 referenced) — [[d:rogue#1318404514956705954]] (Blindtongue, 2024-12-17).
