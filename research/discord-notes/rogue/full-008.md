## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** Consensus progression: Assassination is strongest early/pre-raid; Combat overtakes once well-geared (historically "not until late AQ40" on the original server, per Rheinwg); Sub is a support/utility spec, not a raid-DPS spec — [[d:rogue#1474354178288779457]] (Rheinwg); also stated post-1.18.1, Combat and Assassination are expected to be "closer" but Assassination still best early — [[d:rogue#1473474891973132475]]/[[d:rogue#1473474986147581953]].
- **[Combat]** "SS Combat" (sword/mace/fist, non-backstab) is considered close to unplayable in early gear because its key scaling talent Blade Rush needs high agility + a good weapon simultaneously; only becomes strong very late (AQ40/Naxx) — [[d:rogue#1474475921049260063]], [[d:rogue#1474476383857016854]], [[d:rogue#1474483069179138171]]. Backstab/dagger Combat performs well throughout due to Opportunity/Improved Backstab.
- **[Assassination]** "Assa is here to bridge you to the insane lategame [Combat] spec and fall off" — [[d:rogue#1474477993756852528]] (MistAssassin); disputed by others who still rate Assassination competitive with 4pc Darkmantle/4pc Stormshroud (Oshy: 20-30% ahead of next-best class in raid pre-nerf) — [[d:rogue#1472753083976122439]].
- **[Sub]** Value comes almost entirely from Improved Expose Armor (IEA) support utility, not damage; "if your raid is shit assa is best, if your raid is cracked sub [is best]" — [[d:rogue#1474093482430697492]] (MistAssassin); "there is always a place for a sub rogue even if their gear is a bit on lowish side because iea is just that good" — [[d:rogue#1473775566493716503]] (Farban). Sub is considered weak/boring for solo leveling — [[d:rogue#1474408939960668322]] (Rheinwg: "Sub is kind of shit for lvling").
- **[General]** Warrior (Fury) still outperforms all Rogue specs in raw raid DPS after buffs/consumables in most discussed logs; community frustration about being "shafted" relative to warriors is a recurring theme — [[d:rogue#1472754754429124662]], [[d:rogue#1472758784316276908]], [[d:rogue#1472751317653913600]]; per MistAssassin, Combat also loses to Fury Warrior at endgame in some comparisons — [[d:rogue#1472754962001035431]].
- **[PvE]** Hemorrhage (Hemo) is explicitly called a "PvE support spec" on Turtle (not primarily a leveling/raid DPS driver) — [[d:rogue#1474090359792468141]] (Rheinwg).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Respec cost is capped at 5g, making experimentation cheap — [[d:rogue#1473626828609687573]] (GumGuzz).
- **[General]** Standard leveling advice: level as Combat (dagger/Ambush or sword) until level 40, then respec into deep Assassination for Noxious Assault, described as "a huge powerspike"; use the fastest weapon available (not necessarily daggers) once going Noxious — [[d:rogue#1473625706658529290]] (GumGuzz), [[d:rogue#1474403082132656223]], [[d:rogue#1474403392611815476]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Sub leveling]** Ambush/dagger Sub leveling: one-shots/near-kills green mobs via Ambush→Gouge→Backstab; falls off around level 40 as enemies get tougher; Hemo good vs heavily-armored targets due to bleed; overall "more fluid" than Combat but Combat is "more straightforward and better" for groups — [[d:rogue#1473738639510077490]] (Nnorg).
- **[Combat leveling]** "Combat until 52 makes a lot of fun" — [[d:rogue#1473716568998416395]] (Vistarrk); leveling as Combat swords with emphasis on Ambush effectiveness works fine using any fast MH dagger + any fast OH weapon — [[d:rogue#1474466866612142284]] (Warpig_); dual-dagger with slower MH / faster OH recommended pre-40 [[d:rogue#1474117060924670165]] (ipeepeeonu); OH speed matters less until "full assassin build at 40" — [[d:rogue#1474117671174934770]] (Kolima).
- **[Combat leveling]** Blade Flurry makes AoE leveling trivial once obtained — [[d:rogue#1474400046857584650]] (DesignerDave); combat-sword leveling doesn't require facing/positioning, unlike dagger builds, and can solo most content by endgame — [[d:rogue#1474399991874453649]].
- **[Leveling]** Poisons unlocked via a quest from the rogue trainer in your capital city at level 20 — [[d:rogue#1474197835162587198]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Leveling finishers]** Recommended leveling finisher usage: Slice and Dice at low CP to boost autoattack, Kidney Shot for stuns, Eviscerate for cheap instant damage; spec finishers Envenom (Assa) / Shadow of Death (Sub, group burst); de-prioritize Expose Armor (situational, useful vs bosses without a warrior) and Rupture (mobs usually die before payoff, but grants Taste for Blood) — [[d:rogue#1474363294687957103]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Combo point generator ranking, fully talented]** Backstab > Noxious Assault > Ghostly Strike (fully talented) > Hemorrhage (fully talented) > Sinister Strike, in terms of damage-per-energy; SS deals less damage per energy spent than any spec-specific generator — [[d:rogue#1474364925727084626]], [[d:rogue#1474366370849493002]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Leveling]** Hemorrhage (110% weapon damage, 45 energy, reduced to 35 with 2/2 Bloody Mess) compared unfavorably to Sinister Strike (100% weapon damage + flat bonus, 40 energy) at low level — extra Hemo damage is "barely noticeable" for the energy cost without full Bloody Mess investment — [[d:rogue#1474410353059430402]], [[d:rogue#1474413675984060457]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Race choice]** Racial notes for leveling/general play: Goblin has dagger skill + a stealth-related racial; Troll has an active haste ability (good for haste stacking); Night Elf (Nelf) has passive haste, movement speed, dodge, and extra stealth detection avoidance — [[d:rogue#1473442974535782492]] (Epickalen). Orc gives a free AP racial, considered fine for Rogue — [[d:rogue#1473597473590345849]] (GumGuzz).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Assassination]** Noxious Assault does not scale off weapon damage at all — your weapon is "essentially a stat stick with speed for poison procs"; contested whether it can trigger on-hit/chance-on-hit "spellstrike" procs: Epickalen says "your chance on hits dont proc with nox," but GumGuzz says it does proc spellstrike (Epickalen concedes "all hits do... should proc it twice per tooltip") — [[d:rogue#1473405343177375764]], [[d:rogue#1473405474836578435]], [[d:rogue#1473405496126865522]], [[d:rogue#1473405611541794986]].
- **[Assassination]** Backstab is considered BiS for late-gearing Assassination once weapon damage/auto-attack contribution is high enough, outshining Noxious Assault; early-60 Noxious is fine, but "nox outshines" later — [[d:rogue#1473405474836578435]] (Epickalen). Countered by consensus that Backstab requires positioning ("frontstabbing" avoided by many) and Noxious remains the mainstream endgame choice for most Assassination players.
- **[Assassination]** Do not use Sinister Strike with a dagger MH — use Backstab instead; live dummy test (rey.1982, DPSMate, 3 reruns, ~1 min fights) confirmed Backstab autoattack contribution was ~2k higher per minute with clearly higher overall dps than SS in the same setup — [[d:rogue#1473661307944304721]], [[d:rogue#1473672417917403146]].
- **[Assassination]** Eviscerate deals flat/fixed base damage regardless of weapon — "you do the same damage with hungering cold or a stick in your mainhand" (no weapon-damage component) — [[d:rogue#1473964968746680340]] (GumGuzz), confirmed by Matt Lender testing.
- **[General]** Sample raid Assassination poison rotation cited by a player (context ambiguous whether Assassination or Hemo-labeled question): Noxious → Slice and Dice → Envenom → Noxious to 5cp → Rupture → Noxious to 3cp → Envenom/SnD, maintaining both finishers at 2-4cp, and refreshing Taste for Blood only at 5cp — [[d:rogue#1474090918809440433]], [[d:rogue#1474091062502097089]] (Yoridzen; single source, unclear spec label).
- **[Combat]** Basic beginner rotation: Sinister Strike + keep Slice and Dice up, dump combo points into Eviscerate ("if it'll kill it, if not, keep S&D up") — [[d:rogue#1474400856945201225]] (DesignerDave).
- **[Combat]** Backstab-Combat rotation for raids: Backstab spam, keep Rupture and Slice and Dice up; considered by some the "easiest rotation in the game," disputed by others as no easier than other classes — [[d:rogue#1473593546534555780]] (Nettlefield), [[d:rogue#1473594829111037964]]/[[d:rogue#1473595011084980264]] (Weirdthing, disagreeing).
- **[Combat]** Macro for opener/rotation flow: `/run --CastSpellByName("Backstab") /startattack /cast [mypower<70 nomybuff:Adrenaline_Rush] Surprise Attack /cast [nomybuff:Adrenaline_Rush] {SIN}; Backstab` — casts Surprise Attack conditionally based on energy threshold and Adrenaline Rush buff status — [[d:rogue#1472810344786165880]] (Treehide/Verrik).
- **[Combat]** Blade Flurry does not proc poisons; considered a DPS loss to leave on outside AoE — turn it on to dump energy in a burst then off — [[d:rogue#1473730945688277115]], [[d:rogue#1473731055390298133]], [[d:rogue#1473731104682020886]] (Jeebs). "Don't use Blade Flurry as Assa" — [[d:rogue#1473732229585703107]] (Rheinwg).
- **[General]** Energy tick formula: base 2s tick reduced by Agility × 0.0012 per point; e.g. 500 Agility → 1.4s energy tick — [[d:rogue#1474384784255094998]] (ohcheese, with worked example); rough rule of thumb cited elsewhere as "~820 agi ≈ 1s off tick" (approximate, unconfirmed) — [[d:rogue#1472984052528054432]] (GumGuzz).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General]** Hit rating targets: 8% needed for special/yellow abilities to stop missing; roughly 15% needed to stop white/auto-attack misses for most players; 24% needed to eliminate the extra dual-wield miss penalty; 25% is the "effective crit cap" threshold — [[d:rogue#1473784574814191843]], [[d:rogue#1473784669102149807]] (Epickalen/Ionnatra). Clarification: the 8% soft cap is for yellow (special) hits only; hit rating above 8% raises hit for BOTH MH and OH white attacks, not just OH — [[d:rogue#1473261033115226153]] (GumGuzz), correcting a misconception [[d:rogue#1473260548207415296]] (Ionnatra).
- **[Assassination]** For PvE, 15 Agility ≈ roughly equal to 1% Hit or 1% attack speed/haste at typical Ambershire gear levels; opinions split, with some favoring haste once past ~8% hit — [[d:rogue#1473217989007638622]] (Epickalen: "15 agi is roughly equiv" to 1 hit); riku recommends "1 hit and 15 agi obv" over 1% attack speed when already at only 8% hit ("8 hit is really low") — [[d:rogue#1473247929727979581]].
- **[General]** Haste value debate: haste is multiplicative with no diminishing returns per point, unlike hit/agi; disagreement on whether 1% haste beats 30 AP or 8 agi at current (Ambershire, pre-BWL) gear levels — Nandieb/community lean towards haste being undervalued when raid buffs (Bloodlust/Lionheart) are present but overvalued without them; GumGuzz argues 30 AP > 1% haste because of poison/Noxious AP-scaling and haste's small absolute time saved at these gear levels — [[d:rogue#1474324590309150924]], [[d:rogue#1474326653885743124]], [[d:rogue#1473217427230949487]].
- **[General]** Extra-attack procs (Hand of Justice/Wing of the Time-Lord "extra swing" effects) do not miss/dodge/parry-resist in the same way — testing (turtlogs-style parse analysis by Matt Lender/GumGuzz/axelfrog/Rheinwg) suggests these procs "don't miss," though axelfrog notes HoJ/Time-Lord procs CAN be dodged/parried — [[d:rogue#1473347365493805216]], [[d:rogue#1473350958753775628]] (disputed).
- **[General]** Value of 1% extra-swing (HoJ/Time-Lord) estimated at roughly 0.5% haste-equivalent (≈10-20 AP) for dual-wield, since MH procs give full value but OH procs average only 0.5 value due to randomness within swing timer; Wing of the Time-Lord (1% extra swing) valued similar to or slightly below Ragnaros cape (extra AP+crit) — [[d:rogue#1473593666839773308]], [[d:rogue#1473598074948550757]] (Nandieb's detailed derivation).
- **[Assassination]** Extra-swing procs matter more for Assassination specifically because they grant extra chances to proc poisons on the additional swing, beyond their raw damage value — [[d:rogue#1473370328582590707]] (Nandieb).
- **[General]** Weapon skill: 315 skill caps glancing blow reduction at the "official" 99%; a third-party addon ("Better Character Stats") claims stacking weapon skill up to 349 (daggers) reduces glancing penalty further to 163% (vs 95% baseline cap), disputed/uncertain which model is correct — [[d:rogue#1474386526199877692]], [[d:rogue#1474393380883533949]] (ohcheese/GumGuzz).
- **[General]** Dual-wield adds ~19% additional miss chance vs. single-wield hit tables (the commonly referenced Turtle wiki hit-cap table is for single-wielding only) — [[d:rogue#1474365736192311326]] (GumGuzz), re: [[d:rogue#1474363604156420139]] (Queek asking about the Turtle wiki Weapon Skill page).
- **[General]** Poisons scale with Attack Power (not Spell Power); scaling is described as extremely weak ("might as well have no scaling at all") — [[d:rogue#1472808501997731840]] (Irithyl); Dissolvent Poison specifically deals non-cast Physical damage (relevant to the OBD nerf interaction above).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[General]** Weapon enchant: Crusader confirmed as still the best/easiest weapon enchant, better than flat +Agility — [[d:rogue#1474144201993093374]] (Rheinwg); reaffirmed for Sub specifically — [[d:rogue#1476667611419774997]] (Rheinwg).
- **[General]** Recommended armor enchants: Arcanum of Rapidity (haste) for head, a minor Speed enchant for boots (movement speed enchant stacks with the rogue's innate passive move-speed bonus) — [[d:rogue#1476605424743612549]], [[d:rogue#1476617489315270677]] (Warpig_/Kirsten). Rogues have an innate 7% passive movement speed bonus (not 10% as sometimes assumed) — [[d:rogue#1476786298944749678]] (Epickalen).
- **[General]** Move-speed boot enchant argued to out-DPS an Agility boot enchant in actual raid conditions (getting into melee range / repositioning faster on mechanics matters more than the marginal agi), even though Agility wins in a static target-dummy test — [[d:rogue#1476732206419411075]], [[d:rogue#1476732504676372520]] (Nandieb).
- **[General]** Haste stacking mechanics: Slice and Dice, Juju Flurry, and Quickness potions all apply as independent multipliers onto your base weapon speed (each creates its own multiplier which is applied to the base, not to your already-hasted current speed) — [[d:rogue#1476796510619439164]], [[d:rogue#1476796801570046042]] (Epickalen); this multiplicative stacking means haste's apparent diminishing returns are largely offset when multiple haste sources are combined.
- **[Poisons, general]** Poisons scale off Attack Power only (not Spell Power); scaling described as extremely weak — [[d:rogue#1472808501997731840]] (Irithyl); Dissolvent Poison deals non-cast Physical damage (key to the OBD interaction, see 1.18.1 changes).
- **[Poisons]** Standard poison choice by fight length/target: Deadly Poison + Corrosive Poison (both DoTs) for longer fights/bosses (need time to tick, ~10% extra damage over sharpening stones per Rheinwg's estimate); Instant Poison + Dissolvent Poison for short fights and trash (instant damage, no ramp-up needed) — repeated consensus across many dates — [[d:rogue#1474180834331201536]], [[d:rogue#1475173830283759757]], [[d:rogue#1475175470105362633]], [[d:rogue#1475441467592802345]], [[d:rogue#1477857337632100503]] (Rheinwg). Note: the last rank of Deadly/Corrosive isn't obtainable until AQ20 — [[d:rogue#1474013595485339831]].
- **[Assassination]** Standard Assassination raid poison: Dissolvent Poison II on both weapons — [[d:rogue#1475517575571181841]] (Warpig_); post-1.18.1 AP-scaling nerf hits Dissolvent-based Assassination hardest (see 1.18.1 changes).
- **[General]** Nature-damage-immune enemies (mostly Elementals, not Undead): switch to Instant Poison (a Physical-damage poison), unlocked via a quest at level 52 — [[d:rogue#1475782150795296809]] (GumGuzz).
- **[General]** Poisons are not classified as spells: pickpocket-resist chance is unrelated to hit rating per most players' understanding (a resisted pickpocket is not mitigated by spell hit) — debated without firm resolution — [[d:rogue#1476233457138139302]], [[d:rogue#1477643556582850560]], [[d:rogue#1477643822031966271]] (GumGuzz/Nandieb); also, poisons cannot be instantly (re)applied via spell-insta-cast tricks like Edward the Odd, "because poisons are not spells" — [[d:rogue#1474893455887958157]] (GumGuzz).
- **[PvP]** Blind is no longer classified as a poison effect in Turtle, so old vanilla "trinket the poison to escape Blind" tricks no longer work — [[d:rogue#1477058992965554306]] (Blackjack).
- **[Combat]** Shoulder enchant for Combat Dagger: Might of the Scourge (crit) vs. 2x Haste enchant are very close in value; lean haste if already stacking haste, otherwise user preference — [[d:rogue#1477870820192686132]], [[d:rogue#1477878934405382164]] (Epickalen).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Energy]** Base energy tick is 2s, reduced by Agility × 0.0012 per point (e.g., 500 Agi → 1.4s tick); rough rule of thumb elsewhere cited as "~820 agi ≈ 1s tick" (unconfirmed/approximate) — [[d:rogue#1474384784255094998]] (ohcheese, exact formula), [[d:rogue#1472984052528054432]] (GumGuzz, rough estimate).
- **[Combat]** Blade Flurry (Turtle-specific tuning): hits an additional ~2 nearby enemies with white damage only — explicitly does NOT proc poisons, weapon on-hit procs, or bleeds — [[d:rogue#1475398289737388193]] (Vanikeks). It is a single-target DPS loss for Combat (no poison procs) — dump energy in AoE bursts, then disable — [[d:rogue#1473730945688277115]] (Jeebs); never use as Assassination — [[d:rogue#1473732229585703107]] (Rheinwg); usable/worth it on some single-target boss fights if you have spare energy and SnD/Taste for Blood already up (e.g. Incantagos) — [[d:rogue#1473766652620308550]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Bug/Dev response]** Blade Flurry can get "stuck on" past the buff cap and won't fall off. Dev Crite confirmed a debuff-slot fix was technically impossible ("hard to make a toggle debuff") but committed to reclassifying Blade Flurry as priority so it gets pushed to the front of the buff bar (similar to how weapon imbues are handled), avoiding the cap issue — [[d:rogue#1476967215889649695]] (Crite, direct dev quote), community optimistic — [[d:rogue#1476969496198774979]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Utility]** Improved Expose Armor (IEA) is the primary reason Sub has raid value; keep it up as directed by raid lead even at the cost of personal DPS, but that's "not really an excuse to do shit damage" — a Sub player should still gear/play well — [[d:rogue#1477807893150699724]] (Rheinwg).
- **[Utility]** Smoke Bomb affects everyone (players and mobs) standing inside its radius, including allies — considered mostly a "trolling tool" with very low adoption (~5% of Sub builds); only real use case is emergency survival when about to take lethal damage while solo, "never in raid" since it debuffs your own team too — [[d:rogue#1476320964093677791]], [[d:rogue#1476321448040595568]], [[d:rogue#1476322079384015011]], [[d:rogue#1476322206417031179]], [[d:rogue#1476360357344972931]], [[d:rogue#1476360462609416222]] (Ionnatra/Scruff/Lightemgee).
- **[Utility]** A Sub utility ability (25 energy) throws a "pouch of vanishing powder" at an ally: reduces their threat by 20% and reduces their stealth-detection chance for 5s; when it ends/is cancelled, the ally's next damaging spell/ability deals 15% increased damage. - **[Historical]** Hemorrhage energy-cost history: 35 energy pre-"CC2" patch; changed to 45 energy after CC2 with Bloody Mess reducing it 5/point; most recently changed to 40 base energy with Bloody Mess reducing it by an unspecified amount per point (2/5 points invested cited) — [[d:rogue#1476700611864035552]] (Rheinwg).

- **[Macro]** Weapon-swap-on-stealth macro concept for Ambush builds (swap in a slow hard-hitting dagger on stealth entry, swap to a normal MH for Sinister Strike afterward) — functional but requires multiple presses/practice — [[d:rogue#1476553436060323851]], [[d:rogue#1476553436060323851]], [[d:rogue#1476595084509188288]] (Søren/Antez).
- **[Technique]** "Frontstabbing" (using Backstab while soloing without ideal positioning): step past the mob's 180-degree frontal arc, then spam Backstab; mobs naturally turn to present their back if you stand directly under them, so alternating W/S (forward/backward) reliably forces this positioning — unlike A/D strafing, which merely hopes the mob turns correctly — [[d:rogue#1477010620908966072]], [[d:rogue#1477010769970462863]], [[d:rogue#1477011750669783264]] (Ravenwood).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Races]** Racial weapon-skill stacking: every racial grants +3 weapon skill (for the race's associated weapon types); pairing racial + a matching +3 weapon item (e.g. Death's Sting dagger) + the Combat weapon-spec talent (+5) + the weapon skill book (+5) can reach 316 skill without needing Aged Core Leather Gloves — [[d:rogue#1476043751855095858]]-[[d:rogue#1476044190616916141]] (Zaszz). Race/weapon associations: Human = sword+mace, Goblin = dagger+mace, Orc = axe, (implied Gnome/Dwarf = mace/gun per standard vanilla associations, not explicitly stated). Goblin and Dwarf/Gnome racials are considered the weakest for a Rogue overall — [[d:rogue#1476045271908618291]] (Zaszz), [[d:rogue#1476527166425399442]]/[[d:rogue#1476527937598787618]] (Rheinwg on Dwarf).
- **[Races]** Racial ability notes: Goblin — dagger weapon-skill bonus + a distinct stealth animation/mini-sprint; Troll — active Berserking-like haste ability, good for haste-stacking builds; Night Elf — passive haste + movement speed + dodge + extra stealth (harder to detect); Orc — free AP-boosting active racial + axe skill; Human — sword/mace skill + better stealth *detection* (finding other stealthed players); Undead — Will of the Forsaken (CC immunity + self-heal) and a self-directed dark humor about eating enemies; High/Blood Elf — Arcane Torrent/mana-drain style utility and a passive "meditation" effect described as acting like a mini Adrenaline Rush — [[d:rogue#1473442974535782492]] (Epickalen), [[d:rogue#1476044719799926874]], [[d:rogue#1476044719799926874]] (Epickalen/Zaszz), [[d:rogue#1473551709216182272]] (ipeepeeonu), [[d:rogue#1475974307451633816]], [[d:rogue#1477417064518914181]] (Lightemgee).
- **[Races]** For PvP specifically, Night Elf is favored for stealth/evasion utility and Undead for CC-immunity/self-heal/racial fear resistance — see PvP section.
- **[Professions/gold]** Mining rated a poor gold-maker for a rogue; Herbalism is "ok-ish" if paired with Alchemy, but raw herb value is suppressed by other classes efficiently farming Ashenvale-style "lasher" mobs for bulk herbs — [[d:rogue#1476934750731112661]] (Nandieb).
- **[Gold]** Realistic solo gold-farming benchmark on Ambershire: "anything yielding 40g+/hour is a decent farm"; claims of 100g/h+ farms are largely exaggerated or an unshared niche — [[d:rogue#1476988625773396225]] (Nandieb). Best overall early-60 farm suggested: completing all quests in each level-50+ zone — [[d:rogue#1476987527679447050]].
- **[Gold]** Pickpocketing math: heavy junkboxes drop from ~7-8% of pickpocketed mobs; BoE epics (e.g. Alcor's Sunrazer) drop from junkboxes at ~0.125%, meaning ~800 junkboxes needed on average for one such epic — pickpocketing is viable low-level income but not recommended as a main gold source at 60 — [[d:rogue#1476991508866666517]], [[d:rogue#1476992018839502951]], [[d:rogue#1476992441956565155]], [[d:rogue#1476213969063772292]] (Nandieb/Rheinwg). Best pickpocket-box farming spots mentioned: BRD elites, the craftsman near the Fire Elemental boss in BRD, and Eastern Plaguelands zombies — [[d:rogue#1476218892304257185]], [[d:rogue#1476220240072282144]] (Epickalen).
- **[Gold]** Pickpocketing tips: stand still while pickpocketing or looted items (not just gold) may fail to register even though chat shows a loot message; disable autoloot in one addon if running two (e.g. SuperWoW autoloot conflicts with another addon's autoloot) — [[d:rogue#1476276159917588511]] (Rahnd2137), [[d:rogue#1477651156959367251]], [[d:rogue#1476277178340610331]] (feeder5).
- **[Gold]** Darkmantle full questline cost estimate: roughly 300-500g total on Ambershire (100-200g more on Nordanaar), but considered worthwhile given it's used across 3 raid tiers — [[d:rogue#1477908758364684298]] (Nandieb), [[d:rogue#1477602443796942890]] (2 weeks to 2 months of farming is realistic).
