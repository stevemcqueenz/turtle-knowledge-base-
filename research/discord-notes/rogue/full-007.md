## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Patch nerfs Taste for Blood (no longer boosts poison damage, only autoattacks/Noxious Assault), Opportunity, and Blade Flurry energy generation; estimates converge on roughly 8-10% overall DPS loss, worst for Assassination poison scaling — [[d:rogue#1466159615573233892]] (Drox) [[d:rogue#1466178883307966609]] (Epickalen, "~8%, not incl. BF active buff") [[d:rogue#1468354067523834081]] (Rheinwg, "~10% dps decrease on all stages of lvl 60") [[d:rogue#1471196326871175464]] (Jeebs: poisons ≈60% of Assa's total damage, TFB's poison-damage boost was ~15% of that → roughly an 8% net DPS loss from the TFB change alone).
- **[Sub]** Community consensus the Sub nerf (via the TFB/Dissolvent change) was unwarranted collateral damage since Sub was never top of any bracket — disputed by Epickalen ("if it affects all 3 specs equally it isn't spec-specific"). — [[d:rogue#1466180965226516683]] [[d:rogue#1466181147422621879]] [[d:rogue#1466181369028808858]]
- **[Assa]** Dissolvent Poison losing its Taste for Blood interaction seen as likely a bug/oversight — devs reportedly said Dissolvent was only meant to be a weaker instant option vs poison-immune bosses, not scale with TFB — [[d:rogue#1466381844445397146]] [[d:rogue#1466381939467358362]]. Post-nerf, Instant Poison keeps benefiting from TFB and becomes the preferred choice over Dissolvent — [[d:rogue#1471196792329601191]]
- **[Sub/Combat]** Confirmed the patch separately nerfed the LOWER ranks of DoT poisons — Corrosive rank 1 and Deadly rank 4 — which are exactly the ranks Sub/Combat rogues (who rely on DoT poisons, unlike Assassination's instant procs) are stuck using pre-AQ20 (Corrosive 2 / Deadly 5 only drop in AQ20); called out as nerfing Sub/Combat's early-raiding poison damage "for no reason" — [[d:rogue#1472577766934315139]] [[d:rogue#1472578020664541204]] [[d:rogue#1472579280298049741]] [[d:rogue#1472579388880195634]]
- **[Assa]** New patch note: Ornate Bloodstone Dagger (OBD) "can no longer trigger from effects that deal non-Physical damage without a cast," effectively killing spellstrike-gear synergy with OBD (spellstrike gear doesn't deal physical damage, so it can't proc it even via poison procs either) — [[d:rogue#1472655388741472319]] [[d:rogue#1472658827114713301]] [[d:rogue#1472658966424191098]] [[d:rogue#1472659393517719786]]
- **[Gear]** Fang of Venoxis (a "caster" dagger) buffed to be a new pre-raid-tier upgrade over Alcor's Sunrazor — same speed, more dps, and Alcor's proc considered "absolute dogshit" by comparison; drew mixed reception since it's oddly a caster-itemized weapon becoming rogue BiS — [[d:rogue#1472656404958412912]] [[d:rogue#1472662478361071869]] [[d:rogue#1472662667876499467]] [[d:rogue#1472662941299118080]]
- **[New stat]** A new "Fortune" stat/proc-related item affix is being introduced; speculation it makes procs more consistent; community expects a "stack Fortune + every proc item" build to emerge, with skepticism it'll survive balancing unless warriors/paladins also like it — [[d:rogue#1472666725391663288]] [[d:rogue#1472666821227315326]] [[d:rogue#1472666931269079081]]
- **[Community feedback]** Multiple forum-feedback proposals discussed live with dev "Crite" present: replace Smoke Bomb with an "Improved Bleeding" energy-return talent, adjust Honor Among Thieves range/ICD — acknowledged but not resolved in-chunk — [[d:rogue#1466404660154990593]] [[d:rogue#1466406991219134719]] [[d:rogue#1466408496961552723]]. Also a live, multi-day forum petition ("Simple fix to rogue tank threat problems") asking for a Physical-damage version of Agitating Poison for rogue tanks — unresolved — [[d:rogue#1471424475231682611]] [[d:rogue#1471736045120000123]] [[d:rogue#1472148631518838814]]
- **[Design critique]** Recurring community complaint that Assassination and Combat require almost entirely disjoint gear (fast+haste/AP vs slow+agi/crit), so switching specs discards most of a player's gear; Rheinwg argues the fix is giving each spec its own niche within the same raid tier rather than letting one spec dominate per tier; Nandieb explicitly disagrees, arguing spec-swapping across tiers is healthy for "class identity" — [[d:rogue#1472395610001903667]] [[d:rogue#1472395801811746891]] [[d:rogue#1472575938863693914]] [[d:rogue#1472576011991388193]] [[d:rogue#1472587173021876257]]
- **[Design suggestion]** Ravenwood proposes reworking Noxious Assault to scale off weapon damage (e.g., daggers 150%, other weapons 100%) instead of pure AP, so slow/heavy weapons could also be Assassination-viable rather than pigeonholing the whole class into ~3 specific weapons — unimplemented suggestion — [[d:rogue#1472663362943975515]] [[d:rogue#1472664176563589342]]

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Leveling]** Combat (frontstab or sword+parry) until level 40, then respec to Assassination with Noxious Assault; consistent, widely repeated advice — [[d:rogue#1466112390977290387]] (feeder5) [[d:rogue#1467167112924762239]] (Rheinwg, +1) [[d:rogue#1468677302392193241]] (Farban, detailed) [[d:rogue#1471154940901855430]] (Nettlefield: "shines when you can pick up Noxious Assault, but even Envenom is good").
- **[Assa]** "Assa at lvl 40 no doubt" for HC leveling — does 2-3x its prior damage the instant you hit 40 (Noxious Assault) — [[d:rogue#1466461838811201597]] [[d:rogue#1466461862756352204]]
- **[Sub, leveling]** Widely called a poor leveling tree; best approach if going Sub anyway is to just grab Improved Ambush and spend the rest of your points elsewhere — [[d:rogue#1469437469174010059]] [[d:rogue#1472641522657792105]] [[d:rogue#1472641901139198044]]. One player pointed out this contradicts the channel's own pinned leveling guide (which recommends Sub); Rheinwg's blunt response: "Whoever pinned it doesn't know about rogues" — [[d:rogue#1472656133809508383]] [[d:rogue#1472659577937072425]]
- **[Combat vs Assa, endgame]** Consensus crossover point where Combat Daggers overtakes Assassination is "late AQ40 – Naxx," matching where it happened on the older "South Seas" server progression — [[d:rogue#1466587313701130462]] [[d:rogue#1472386328292360232]]. Confirmed even via dummy tests using Gressil to emulate a R14 weapon on Combat, which still couldn't beat an Assassination rogue — [[d:rogue#1467696967072350250]] [[d:rogue#1467697134978728199]]. **Caveat raised late in the chunk**: high-level target dummies have unusually high armor, which structurally favors Assassination (poisons ignore armor) in all these dummy comparisons — treat all dummy-based Combat-vs-Assa test results in this chunk with that caveat — [[d:rogue#1471535883109928990]] [[d:rogue#1471535948322832538]]
- **[Combat vs Assa, BWL]** No new Combat weapons appear in BWL (barring Rank 14 PvP weapons); Combat's cleave/BF value is low there (few cleave fights) and poison max ranks aren't out yet either — "99% sure assa > combat" in BWL — [[d:rogue#1466378887402356790]] [[d:rogue#1466379181205098591]]. Some Ambershire MC logs already show a few Combat rogues matching/exceeding Assa dps using Adrenaline Rush + Felstriker in short fights, but this is disputed as a small, possibly-skewed sample (log site/sorting issues, short-fight bias inflating AR value) — [[d:rogue#1471308737305116714]] [[d:rogue#1471314000863170726]] [[d:rogue#1471470458242339010]] ("rogues don't play combat on Ambershire, they're all assassin").
- **[Sub]** Sub can reach ~1500 dps in short high-execution windows but as low as 300s dps when the rotation doesn't come together; widely called one of the hardest specs to play well — [[d:rogue#1467945090948399209]] [[d:rogue#1467594810214060164]]
- **[Sub]** Parsing close to Combat/Assa requires landing double Mark of Death + double Prep, which many Naxx bosses don't allow (esp. Noth→Heigan→Loatheb and Patchwerk→Grobbulus→Gluth→Thaddius sequences, where Sub's CDs desync badly) — [[d:rogue#1467482542268612805]] [[d:rogue#1467483628576444477]] [[d:rogue#1467483745908166739]]
- **[Sub raid value]** Bringing a Sub in MC still helps raid dps as long as fights last >25s (enough to land a Prep-assisted full rotation) — [[d:rogue#1467788287929225389]]. Sub's value scales with raid melee count (Hemo/IEA benefit multiple melees) and raid size: "10-20 person raids aren't that good for sub rogue, 40-person raids are" — [[d:rogue#1470762993628479673]] [[d:rogue#1470770081553449064]]
- **[Stormshroud vs Darkmantle, disputed]** Long-running disagreement whether Stormshroud (SS) or Darkmantle (DM) is BiS for combat daggers on Ambershire. riku: SS "isn't that good, not BIS," good only for a fresh-60 with spare gold; 6xDM+2 good pieces beats full Stormshroud in his tests — [[d:rogue#1467212039440171190]] [[d:rogue#1467464384820543542]] [[d:rogue#1467215868126826587]]. Counter: SS's 10% haste flat-proc set bonus can spike very high with good RNG and benefits from haste consumes/Bloodlust; "it does a lot more dps than people like to admit, shitty stats but good dmg" — [[d:rogue#1467216112088514591]] [[d:rogue#1467481100082479146]] [[d:rogue#1471972901032038410]]. Testing methodology itself disputed (small sample of "top" parses, dummy-test setups differ) — [[d:rogue#1467432856119808165]] [[d:rogue#1467480927461445694]]. On Ambershire, needing the Darkmantle chest+helm specifically "in almost every scenario right now" — [[d:rogue#1471972688015921456]] [[d:rogue#1471972735251906823]]

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[Assa raid build, common]** "41 Assassination / 10 Combat" (or similar split) is the most commonly used current raid Assassination build on Ambershire, per multiple shared talent-builder links; essentially unchanged between "early raid" and later "endgame" versions — [[d:rogue#1471522415212499189]] [[d:rogue#1472537931066376357]]
- **[Assa raid build, detailed]** Rheinwg's recommended raiding build prioritizes Seal of Fate (to increase Taste for Blood uptime) over Dual Wield spec ("too expensive to get"); skip Opportunity since Noxious Assault is your only combo-point generator; Cold Blood and Murder optional — Murder is very good in BWL but falls off after — [[d:rogue#1466548682412265759]] [[d:rogue#1466548743804420217]] [[d:rogue#1466548895155880108]] [[d:rogue#1466549112009916436]]
- **[Assa, post-TFB-nerf adjustment]** Once TFB no longer boosts poison damage, combo points become less valuable (Rupture/Seal of Fate's main payoff shrinks), so the community leans toward dropping Seal of Fate in favor of Dual Wield Specialization while KEEPING Taste for Blood itself (it still buffs Noxious Assault and autoattacks) — [[d:rogue#1471192007480181131]] [[d:rogue#1471194326091563203]] [[d:rogue#1471196189012660256]] [[d:rogue#1471197397064351991]]
- **[Combat Daggers raid build]** "20/31 Taste for Blood / Adrenaline Rush" build; maintaining 5-point TFB and Slice and Dice uptime is described as the key skill — [[d:rogue#1470227980163747850]] [[d:rogue#1472002125423837334]]
- **[Combat, tank-adjacent riposte build]** A Combat riposte-focused spec (spam-macro: Riposte if ready → Ghostly Strike if ready → Noxious Assault otherwise) worked well in mixed T1/T2 gear with double Stormfists, also noted as strong for soloing the "Damian" world boss — [[d:rogue#1471589015286190121]] [[d:rogue#1471600410023428208]]
- **[Sub struggling-player diagnosis]** For a sub player parsing poorly, Rheinwg's checklist: must have Ruthlessness, Blade Tactics-line and Relentless Strikes talents (missing these costs a lot of energy/combo points); Taste for Blood can be skipped if you can't maintain it; only need 8% hit as Sub (get the 2% shortfall from the Darkmantle set trinket rather than talents) — [[d:rogue#1467576340415775040]] [[d:rogue#1467577072863023172]] [[d:rogue#1467578023233585222]]
- **[PvP builds]** A shared Assassination PvP build: 1pt Efficient Poison for the PvP spell-hit cap; 3pt Improved Ambush useful only if running Perdition's Blade to open on cloth targets (otherwise move those points to 1pt Improved Gouge + 2pt Serrated Blades); Rupture's PvP value is mainly for LOS/melee-kiting fights, not for stopping a Vanish reliably — [[d:rogue#1471530033066807332]] [[d:rogue#1471530374764040329]] [[d:rogue#1471532214566256774]] [[d:rogue#1471533526703669344]]
- **[Frontstab/dagger leveling build]** No up-to-date community talent-calculator link exists (old links dead); general order: grab Opportunity, Improved Backstab, and hit talents first, then fill remaining points from other trees — [[d:rogue#1467593247055548603]]
- **[Sub "DPS" build]** A "Sub DPS" build simply means skipping Improved Expose Armor (IEA); move the 2 points that would go to Lethality into ToT instead, since the alternative use for those points is Camouflage anyway — [[d:rogue#1466401775849312329]] [[d:rogue#1466402332282458277]] [[d:rogue#1466402431213637642]]

## Leveling
- **[General]** Quests give full XP up to 25 levels above the quest's listed level, making smooth solo leveling viable — [[d:rogue#1466114142657184030]]
- **[Combat→Assa path, detailed]** Combat (front-stab or sword) with defensive talents until Noxious Assault unlocks (~40), then Assassination all the way to 60; early raiding can also be played as Assa since Combat needs gear to outscale it — [[d:rogue#1466113744852615261]] [[d:rogue#1466113859399188724]]. Combat needs too much Agi to shine; Assa only needs haste + swift weapons, Sub needs Agi + slow weapons — [[d:rogue#1466114209728434400]]
- **[Detailed Assa leveling path]** Invest in the Assassination tree; ~30 Envenom gives a power spike; ~40 Noxious Assault causes dps to "shoot up high"; stay Assassination long past 60 until Naxx (or AQ40) daggers close the gap with Combat. Prioritize weapon speed over stats/damage once you have Envenom (look for 1.3-speed weapons); later with high-end daggers, stack Agility for faster energy ticks and Backstab spam — [[d:rogue#1467303106353303672]]
- **[Consolidated leveling guide]** Farban's guide: start Combat, respec to Assassination + Noxious Assault at 40, play Assa until ~AQ40. Get avoidance talents + Riposte at the start, pick up hit along the way. Take all poison-boosting talents plus Taste for Blood, finish the Combat tree for dodge/hit — [[d:rogue#1468677302392193241]]
- **[Assa leveling gearing philosophy]** "Approach Assa gearing more like a caster than a melee": prioritize AP over raw weapon damage, and haste/weapon-speed over crit (since Noxious Assault's combo-generation ignores weapon damage and scales only off AP). Suggested overall priority: Haste > Agility > AP > Hit > Crit > Stamina — [[d:rogue#1472140808970043413]] [[d:rogue#1472141889451987037]] [[d:rogue#1472159175722143841]] [[d:rogue#1472127519929929751]]
- **[Poison by level, Assa]** Instant Poison works fine from level 40 onward (only real downside: elementals resist it too, same as Dissolvent — not worth worrying about); Dissolvent (available ~52) gives a "huge boost," and that's the point Rupture enters the rotation for Taste for Blood upkeep — [[d:rogue#1472481935623327805]] [[d:rogue#1472124801773600820]] [[d:rogue#1472125310764847197]] [[d:rogue#1467137811193794662]]
- **[Talent order, leveling Assa]** Pick up Ruthlessness and Relentless Strikes (take points from Lethality); max Taste for Blood before Cold Blood/Seal of Fate, or skip those to dip into Sub for QoL (Camouflage, Gouge, Blackjack) after ~31 points in Assassination — [[d:rogue#1472125040211263633]] [[d:rogue#1472125639443087493]] [[d:rogue#1472125844351619122]]. Gut Ripper (a slower high-damage dagger) is specifically BAD for Assassination leveling — speed and poison procs matter, not weapon damage — [[d:rogue#1472127122071093311]] [[d:rogue#1472127384001187914]]
- **[Frontstab]** Technique: stand on the mob and strafe right-to-left to land Backstab from the front; usable until ~40 then switch to Nox, or alternatively use swords + parry instead — [[d:rogue#1466114956285050953]] [[d:rogue#1466116046455967766]] [[d:rogue#1466445846219325550]] [[d:rogue#1466445914452394029]]. Frontstab leveling is fun grouped/in dungeons but feels slow solo — [[d:rogue#1467589646086050045]]. A frontstab macro also lets you go daggers even while soloing — [[d:rogue#1472121785335025664]]
- **[Early fast daggers, pre-40]** Slip's Dagger (~15g, Horde-only questline) is a fast dagger option before Julie's/Distracting Dagger become relevant — [[d:rogue#1472628685810176081]] [[d:rogue#1472630840902025408]]. Level 14 "Poniards" exist even earlier but their damage is so low it's usually better to accept a 0.1s-slower weapon for +10 more dps instead — [[d:rogue#1472652238945255474]] [[d:rogue#1472652812591698034]]
- **[Talent priorities while leveling]** Get Envenom and Noxious Assault as early as possible; Relentless Strikes and Ruthlessness are priority picks; a throwing-weapon build is fun but optional — [[d:rogue#1466446154349674566]] [[d:rogue#1466446236231008378]] [[d:rogue#1466446349833867478]]
- **[Leveling rotation, Noxious build]** 1cp Envenom → 1cp Slice and Dice → 5cp Rupture; spicier variant swaps 5cp Expose Armor in for Eviscerate; only Eviscerate when Rupture/Envenom/SnD buffs are already up — [[d:rogue#1468113304156115005]] [[d:rogue#1468115101352722661]] [[d:rogue#1468115264464752670]] [[d:rogue#1468115588265283605]]
- **[1cp Eviscerate spam]** Still a solid, "chill" leveling build with Envenom + SnD upkeep at low levels; big additional power spike at 40 with Noxious Assault; at 52, physical poisons scale with Taste for Blood for Rupture too — [[d:rogue#1467136265127198846]] [[d:rogue#1467136451337650310]] [[d:rogue#1467136857845534933]] [[d:rogue#1467137706374201497]]
- **[Very early rotation (<25)]** Spam Sinister Strike (or Backstab in group fights with a dagger MH) and Eviscerate to finish; once available, Improved Slice and Dice 3/3 becomes #1 priority to maintain; treat Envenom (~40) like "an extra Slice and Dice" — [[d:rogue#1470309621099925627]] [[d:rogue#1470309775123415110]] [[d:rogue#1468518863179350068]] [[d:rogue#1468519698168807576]]. Low-level energy starvation is normal/unavoidable ("that's the neat part, you don't [solve it]"); most damage still comes from autoattacks — [[d:rogue#1470278842949046403]] [[d:rogue#1470280229107925074]]
- **[Weapon choice while leveling]** Check required level, then DPS (higher-dps items are usually better regardless of speed); for the mainhand, prefer a higher top-end damage roll over pure DPS since Backstab scales off that roll; offhand wants fast/good-dps, but "while leveling anything will do" — [[d:rogue#1468205667075751947]]. Daggers make good fast offhands even paired with a sword mainhand (loses Backstab access); a sword mainhand instead boosts Sinister Strike since swords roll higher damage ranges — [[d:rogue#1468206548814925967]] [[d:rogue#1468206622517100686]]
- **[Weapon upgrade cadence]** Upgrade daggers "whenever you can" while leveling; database (turtlecraft.gg) lists all daggers by level but isn't perfectly curated — some low-level entries are simply bad — [[d:rogue#1467957906455531640]] [[d:rogue#1467974367588913305]]
- **[No hidden stealth crit]** Contrary to a common belief, attacking from Stealth does NOT give a hidden bonus crit chance on abilities like Backstab — that's why dedicated stealth openers (Ambush, Cheap Shot) exist — [[d:rogue#1468488526562590895]] [[d:rogue#1468488816460435607]]
- **[Close Quarters Combat mechanic]** A dagger/mace/fist weapon in the MAINHAND grants a weapon-crit bonus (name: "Close Quarters Combat"-type talent) that also applies to the offhand regardless of its weapon type; separately, Hack and Slash only procs from sword/axe damage — [[d:rogue#1470287318219886715]]

## Rotation: single target
- **[Sub]** Rheinwg's detailed opener/maintenance rotation: Garrote → Slice and Dice → Hemorrhage/Ghostly Strike to 5cp → Rupture → Hemo/Ghostly to 3cp then Mark (of Death) → Shadow of Death → thereafter maintain Taste for Blood + SnD, always dumping at 5cp to guarantee the party-wide TotT proc; Eviscerate when you get the chance; line up AP trinkets with Shadow of Death; Vanish before using Preparation; want Shadow, Mark, and Vanish all on cooldown before popping Prep — [[d:rogue#1466422873806733362]]
- **[Sub openers]** Openers with 100% chance to proc TotT = Garrote; Cheap Shot also works (e.g., on ghouls in Naxx) though less common — [[d:rogue#1467572957231448354]] [[d:rogue#1467573161955688613]]
- **[Sub, low CP vs 5CP finisher priority]** With 5 combo points, use Rupture and refresh Slice and Dice with fewer points afterward, rather than the reverse — [[d:rogue#1472204410842251285]] [[d:rogue#1472199980231033025]]
- **[Sub, simple leveling/quick rotation]** For a simpler build: Noxious Assault → Envenom → Slice and Dice → Envenom(5cp) → Slice and Dice(5cp) → Rupture(5cp) → maintain; for anything short-lived, skip straight to Nox → 1cp Envenom → 1cp SnD → 5cp Rupture → 1cp Envenom → 1cp SnD, fitting in Eviscerate as needed (since the boss often dies before Rupture ticks out) — [[d:rogue#1471803160140972165]] [[d:rogue#1471834516669730952]]
- **[Combat Dagger opener]** Backstab is the standard opener over Garrote-into-stealth (closing to stealth-range costs too much time); when using swords, opener is Sinister Strike into Eviscerate — [[d:rogue#1468327294430085130]] [[d:rogue#1468325231415267419]]
- **[Assa on swords]** When respecced Assassination but still using swords, rotation is Noxious Assault, SnD, Envenom; Rupture is "a tool" used only occasionally — [[d:rogue#1468326608052945168]] [[d:rogue#1468326769852547314]]
- **[Assa vs immune targets]** Only change rotation when the enemy is immune to poisons (switch to a non-poison weapon and Sinister Strike); poisons should otherwise be applied to both weapons regardless of spec — [[d:rogue#1466481711406907599]] [[d:rogue#1468328954120376443]] [[d:rogue#1468329153060409671]]
- **[Sinister Strike vs Ghostly Strike]** Both generate 1 combo point; Ghostly Strike scales off weapon damage and grants some dodge (more defensive), so with a high-damage weapon Ghostly Strike out-damages Sinister Strike — [[d:rogue#1471210600188084365]] [[d:rogue#1471212965410635854]] [[d:rogue#1471234408567804026]] [[d:rogue#1471244508921335999]]
- **[Assa DPS metric]** "DPS is not a good metric" for evaluating your own play — check logs for: are all CDs used, IEA maintained the whole fight, TotT uptime, TFB uptime, SnD uptime — [[d:rogue#1467573603225571431]] [[d:rogue#1467573920906612801]]
- **[Advanced tech: combo-point carryover]** Casting Slice and Dice on a NEW target while you have combo points on your current (still-alive) target transfers/scales the CP count into the new SnD application; confirmed working reliably, used to pre-stack Relentless Strikes energy between pulls — [[d:rogue#1469666367895765144]] [[d:rogue#1471202624857706711]] [[d:rogue#1471203923862749468]] [[d:rogue#1471219593577103586]]

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit]** 8% is the bare-minimum "always hit with abilities" threshold. Autoattack hit-cap ("yellow"/white cap) is around 24-25% with both MH+OH at 300+ weapon skill; 5 extra weapon skill points = 1% additional hit at 300 baseline — [[d:rogue#1467163880126939319]] [[d:rogue#1468885914770997311]] [[d:rogue#1468889166220886178]]. Practical target: 15-16% total hit gives a high-enough crit cap through Naxx without over-sacrificing other stats; getting to 25% is probably unrealistic without giving up set bonuses — [[d:rogue#1467164411058983042]] [[d:rogue#1470710966323904522]] [[d:rogue#1470711096015978548]] [[d:rogue#1470711304447590508]]
- **[Hit vs Crit by spec]** For autoattacks, hit and crit are practically equal value, but crit also affects abilities while hit doesn't beyond 8%; as Assassination, prioritize hit (more hits = more poison procs, poisons can't crit); as Combat, prioritize crit unless you need hit for the crit-cap — [[d:rogue#1467164741066686494]] [[d:rogue#1467164893135241289]] [[d:rogue#1467165033384382576]]
- **[Hit vs Haste, Assa]** Given a choice (once past 8% hit), favor haste: 1% hit = 1/100 previously-missed hits now land, but 3% haste = 3 more attacks per 100 (more poison-proc chances), and haste is multiplicative so it compounds with existing haste sources (e.g. Slice and Dice's own 30% attack-speed buff) — [[d:rogue#1470443919069413578]] [[d:rogue#1470446165756608605]] [[d:rogue#1470446607643185426]] [[d:rogue#1470447662061850706]]. "Haste is best in a vacuum but fight duration matters — shorter fights reduce its value; ~150 autoattacks/minute is a good baseline for estimating hit vs haste for Assassination" — [[d:rogue#1470954658468986910]]
- **[Enchant priority]** By spec: Assassination → haste + AP; Combat → agility; Sub → AP — [[d:rogue#1469994953131167875]]. General slot rule: Head 1% haste, shoulder 2% haste (Sigil of Quickness), pants 1% haste, gloves 1% haste OR 15 agi (preference), everything else agility — [[d:rogue#1466517277687025862]] [[d:rogue#1467175127388258529]]. Haste enchant sources: Minor Haste (gloves), Hyjal 2% haste shoulder enchant (craftable via quest+mats), Dire Maul 1% haste enchants for legs/head (need BoP mats: Libram + Blood of Heroes, farmable in WPL/EPL, or buy the enchant service ~50g each) — [[d:rogue#1469995697590894633]] [[d:rogue#1469997005651578974]] [[d:rogue#1469998106278887498]]
- **[Haste value]** Haste scales with gear (unlike flat stats); ~2% haste was worth roughly 50-55 AP by one estimate — [[d:rogue#1466516895149850644]]. Simonize (haste enchant) weighed ~17.83 AP-equivalent vs Zandalar Signet's 30 AP — haste slightly wins due to multiplicative scaling — [[d:rogue#1466191518061695017]] [[d:rogue#1466231628614467584]]
- **[Agi/Str → AP]** Both Agility and Strength convert to 1 AP each (applies to Sub too); with Kings you effectively get ~10% more out of those stats (e.g. 30 agi ≈ 33 AP after Kings) — only prioritize flat AP items when the difference is substantial — [[d:rogue#1466204878161313956]] [[d:rogue#1467580558396882995]] [[d:rogue#1467580841847820370]] [[d:rogue#1467581618746163322]]. A separate, more informal weight heuristic given elsewhere: "1 agi ≈ 2 AP, 1 str ≈ 1 AP, but 1 agi > 2 AP in practice due to the added crit" — a looser rule of thumb than the direct 1:1 conversion above — [[d:rogue#1469537575529087028]] [[d:rogue#1470097203207999611]]
- **[Weapon skill — Turtle-specific, linear system]** Confirmed NOT the vanilla 305/308 breakpoint system: every weapon-skill point gives 2% reduction to the glancing-blow damage penalty AND 0.2% additional hit chance. The glancing benefit caps at 315 skill (base glance penalty ~35% reduced to ~5% at 315 = a ~-30% swing, but in overall dps terms this nets under 0.5% since glancing occurs ~40% of white hits and white damage is only ~20% of total); the HIT portion of weapon skill has NO cap and keeps scaling past 315 — [[d:rogue#1470964213743620261]] [[d:rogue#1471017076666667019]] [[d:rogue#1471040844323750048]] [[d:rogue#1470116805963157527]] [[d:rogue#1470119269114056704]] (dodge chance also reduced 0.1%/point). At BiS, Combat Daggers naturally land ~310 skill; reaching 315 via weapon-skill gloves isn't worth it — [[d:rogue#1470964816096002161]] [[d:rogue#1470964947101028352]]. Assassination cares less about weapon skill overall since poison, not white damage, carries the spec — [[d:rogue#1470965936382017589]]. See also turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_Skill — [[d:rogue#1468261072346812479]]
- **[Weapon-skill quest priority]** The BiS weapon for Sub and Sinister-Strike/Combat-Swords is a MACE, so the mace weapon-skill quest is very much worth doing — [[d:rogue#1469413990533828721]]
- **[Blade Rush energy formula]** Confirmed: −0.0012 seconds off the energy-tick interval per point of Agility (base regen is 20 energy per 2 seconds). At 500 agi that's a 0.6s reduction (≈20 energy per 1.4s); every 100 agi ≈ −0.12s per tick — [[d:rogue#1472370682389336338]] [[d:rogue#1472371546344652861]] [[d:rogue#1472377391195357205]] [[d:rogue#1472378545165566125]]
- **[Combat agi at BiS]** MC-phase BiS-geared combat daggers hover around low-to-mid 400s agi unbuffed, ~500 with consumes+Kings, up to ~450-460 unbuffed achievable on Ambershire by one estimate; at full BiS, energy-tick reduction from stacking further agi is roughly half a second total — diminishing/negligible value outside specific mechanics fights beyond that point — [[d:rogue#1466613267605491712]] [[d:rogue#1466613335507206341]] [[d:rogue#1466613608656932986]] [[d:rogue#1471345380225581188]]
- **[Weapon enchants: Crusader vs Agility]** No good reason to "split" enchants (1x Crusader + 1x Agi) since Crusader stacks across weapons — go full Agi (for crit/energy) or double Crusader (for uptime); Crusader averaged ~53% uptime in one 10-min dummy test, valued roughly equal to 53 AP vs 15 agi + 0.5% crit + minor energy regen from full agi — [[d:rogue#1466924757298708592]] [[d:rogue#1466929558581411840]] [[d:rogue#1466944740615258223]]. Crusader has near-100% uptime on fast (1.8-1.9 speed) daggers so proc-rate isn't a concern there — [[d:rogue#1467767469354520817]]. Rule of thumb: Crusader for Assassination (cheaper, high uptime on fast daggers, and its 100 Str proc scales AP), Agility for Combat once chasing crit-cap/energy-regen maxing — [[d:rogue#1469642744195584041]] [[d:rogue#1469643160467800084]] [[d:rogue#1470787192698961981]] [[d:rogue#1469806135610577028]]
- **[Contested: Fiery/Lifestealing PPM vs Crusader]** One player argues Lifestealing/Fiery procs far more often (6-8 PPM) than Crusader (~1 PPM), and questions whether Crusader's steady ~25 AP-equivalent beats the higher-frequency (if smaller) alternate procs, especially for Ambush/Backstab builds; Rheinwg is skeptical specifically of Fiery ("kind of trash") and notes Crusader also heals and buffs everything you do, not just raw AP — unresolved, single-thread debate — [[d:rogue#1470671361746796696]] [[d:rogue#1470690155420455024]] [[d:rogue#1470731648017498164]] [[d:rogue#1470770621767094496]]

- **[Zanza]** Spirit of Zanza works in raid and persists through death, acting like a cheap mini-flask — [[d:rogue#1468618210264879288]] [[d:rogue#1468626095870968022]]
- **[MC Fire Resistance]** Full fire-resist gear not required; just pop Greater Fire Resistance potions on specific bosses — [[d:rogue#1466865787557118077]] [[d:rogue#1466871272490139770]]

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Prep]** Preparation's baseline cooldown is 7 minutes (per Rheinwg's detailed sub-rogue breakdown); an earlier, less-clear claim about a build achieving 3-min Prep via gear was not substantiated — [[d:rogue#1468425398747402240]] [[d:rogue#1466419785360343214]]
- **[Expose Armor mechanic]** Expose Armor's debuff VALUE is static; only duration scales with combo points invested (same pattern as SnD/Envenom) — [[d:rogue#1467846276602269819]]. Regular (non-improved) Expose Armor is "just as good as Sunder [Armor]" as a raid debuff — [[d:rogue#1470050391356866665]]
- **[Blackjack]** A specific weapon/item provides a 10% raid boss damage-reduction debuff — useful Sub raid-utility tool to coordinate with the raid — [[d:rogue#1468425398747402240]]
- **[Mark of Death / Prep interaction]** Prep resets Mark of Death's cooldown; if you can only land one Mark per fight, time it with execute phase or when raid CDs pop; with Prep you can use Mark early then reset the CD for a second use later — [[d:rogue#1469728842221359226]] [[d:rogue#1469728901772218418]]
- **[Trinket-swap macros/addons]** TrinketMenu/ItemRack let you queue a trinket swap that fires automatically on Vanish or when combat drops; newer addon versions allow queueing the swap even while still in combat (Shift+Click) and ignore combat shorter than 3 seconds (avoids spurious swaps from brief pet aggro) — [[d:rogue#1470511767355392021]] [[d:rogue#1470515846630604874]] [[d:rogue#1470516084544241863]] [[d:rogue#1470516525663387822]]
- **[Racial utility]** Undead's 2% bonus damage to Undead/Humanoid targets is relevant on: a dog boss in MC, Nefarian, Onyxia, the AQ40 Bug Trio and Huhuran, Gluth, Naxx Military Wing trash, Gothik, the Chess event, and Mephistroth — [[d:rogue#1467866527155093524]]. Gnome's Escape Artist (breaks fear/charm/roots) repeatedly called out for getting players "back on bosses faster than other rogues/warriors" — [[d:rogue#1467857162553397298]]
- **[Stealth detection quirks]** Some mobs have increased stealth detection (BRD dogs, AQ40 Mind Flayers) — testable by comparing aggro range in/out of stealth; Gilneas zone and the High Elf starting zone are "bugged" and effectively cheat against stealth — [[d:rogue#1469338037065351409]] [[d:rogue#1469305659995586600]] [[d:rogue#1469334445814452235]]

## Tanking specifics
- **[Feasibility, general]** Rogue tanking is real but bounded/situational, not a full MT replacement: rogues have been used to off-tank or "impromptu tank" specific fights (4 Horsemen, BWL dragonkin trash, Instructor Razuvious in Naxx without needing Mind Control tactics), surviving multiple seconds/rotations with 2+ healers focused on them after a main tank dies — [[d:rogue#1471665604158296095]] [[d:rogue#1471680197454467082]] [[d:rogue#1471270833686184029]] [[d:rogue#1471273194789933126]]. Some claim it's the ONE consistent death-source even for an otherwise-strong survivability build (e.g., wiping repeatedly to Razuvious specifically) — [[d:rogue#1471735079679164630]]
- **[Solo/duo tank capability]** Raid-geared rogue tanks can solo many dungeon bosses (e.g., BRD torch/golem/emperor-room encounters, with LOS tricks to avoid pulling the whole Emperor room); 3 rogues with no healer/tank can clear "most dungeon content," sneaking past unmanageable trash — [[d:rogue#1471053877829828724]] [[d:rogue#1471054107728150692]] [[d:rogue#1471050271571316850]] [[d:rogue#1471052484217667617]]
- **[Rogue-tank stat priorities]** Stamina (to survive spells), Dodge/Parry over Crit on gear; Defense rating on cloak/legs/head enchants alongside Agility; trinkets/neck slotted for Defense to boost avoidance — [[d:rogue#1471055767372169417]] [[d:rogue#1471071034932203551]]. Full-avoidance rotation cited: Ghostly Strike → Flourish → Ghostly Strike → Evasion → Ghostly Strike → Flourish → Ghostly Strike, but Flourish then goes on ~10s CD leaving a window where you CAN be hit/crit — reaching this requires real Defense investment beyond Agility alone — [[d:rogue#1471063134339858453]] [[d:rogue#1471063504709484586]] [[d:rogue#1471064164943270020]]
- **[Threat problem, core debate]** Deep-Subtlety "Prep tank" builds sacrifice Envenom/Noxious Assault, leaving poor threat; rogues have NO taunt, so a tank-spec rogue must out-threat other tank classes purely via its rotation, while wearing survival stats instead of Hit/Crit/AP — measured as low as ~800 unbuffed dummy dps for a DPS-spec backstab build, implying even less for tank-spec gear — [[d:rogue#1471050038665941053]] [[d:rogue#1471058077380907121]] [[d:rogue#1471436368373878905]] [[d:rogue#1471437089097912321]]. Counter-view (Epickalen): reasonably high TPS is achievable via Sinister Strike spam + poison/Tfury autoattack baselines without a "full rotation"; "rogue having threat problems is propaganda" — [[d:rogue#1471058580563169438]] [[d:rogue#1471425077223620619]]
- **[Agitating Poison, the recurring pain point]** The tank-oriented "Agitating Poison" deals Nature damage, so it's useless on the many poison-immune/nature-immune bosses (same ~50% of bosses affected as regular poisons); best current rogue-tank spec is a mix of Assassination+Combat (not deep Subtlety) specifically to compensate; a multi-day forum petition campaign ran through this period asking devs for a Physical-damage equivalent ("simple fix to rogue tank threat problems") — repeatedly posted, unresolved by end of chunk — [[d:rogue#1471053347518681149]] [[d:rogue#1471429041604268146]] [[d:rogue#1471434414192853043]] [[d:rogue#1471424475231682611]] [[d:rogue#1471736045120000123]] [[d:rogue#1472148631518838814]]
- **[Community-speculated dev history]** Some players believe Turtle devs attempted a fuller "rogue tanking" project and scrapped/limited it after internal testing showed balance concerns (100% avoidance windows via Prep/Flourish chains being too strong) — unconfirmed community speculation, not a dev statement — [[d:rogue#1471054598759256104]] [[d:rogue#1471054824136114258]] [[d:rogue#1471068312267002067]] [[d:rogue#1471055772057210970]]
- **[Weaknesses]** Squishy against magic-damage bosses and large trash pulls; no Cleave-style AoE threat tool without Agitating Poison; can Sap 1 + Blind 1 to manage 4-target packs manually — [[d:rogue#1471053347518681149]] [[d:rogue#1471048246989422837]] [[d:rogue#1471059228574879856]]
- **[Availability]** GM/community confirmation that rogue tanking is possible mainly for dungeons; some "mad lads" do limited raid off-tanking — [[d:rogue#1471626258881450147]]

## PvP
- **[General]** Rogue is considered strong in PvP currently ("smashing dps meters and smashing ppl in pvp") despite a common belief the class is undertuned — [[d:rogue#1470905641043492916]] [[d:rogue#1470908322831138827]]. Turtle's Assassination reportedly plays more like WotLK Assassination than vanilla Assassination — [[d:rogue#1467088493166727212]] [[d:rogue#1467091140132999220]]
- **[Weapon choice]** A slow mainhand generally underperforms in PvP for Assassination since many tanky classes (paladins, shamans, warriors) take near-0 damage from big slow hits anyway; there's an argument for macro-swapping to a slow/high-damage MH specifically vs cloth targets — [[d:rogue#1466506340175183934]] [[d:rogue#1466506744426397718]] [[d:rogue#1466506819068363046]]. Disputed: one player found slow main (Blackjack) + fist offhand (Stormfist) "looked better" than two fast weapons for PvP Assa/Sub — countered that unless you're one-shotting the target, a slow MH is worse — [[d:rogue#1466478235922727089]] [[d:rogue#1466479500912230434]]
- **[Twink rotation, lvl 39]** Garrote → Hemorrhage → Rupture → Vanish → Cheap Shot → Ghostly Strike → Eviscerate, using 2x Cold daggers, Instant Poison, and 2x Lifesteal weapon enchant — [[d:rogue#1468121383321211106]] [[d:rogue#1468122525040119996]]
- **[Assa PvP build]** Shared build: 1pt Efficient Poison for the PvP spell-hit cap; 3pt Improved Ambush only worth it with a Perdition's-Blade-style opener on cloth targets (otherwise move those points to 1pt Improved Gouge + 2pt Serrated Blades) — [[d:rogue#1471530033066807332]] [[d:rogue#1471530374764040329]]
- **[Rupture in PvP]** Its main PvP use case is during an LOS/melee-kite fight, not as Vanish-detection/prevention (timing a Vanish correctly or gaining distance beats it anyway) — [[d:rogue#1471532214566256774]] [[d:rogue#1471533526703669344]]
- **[Racials for PvP]** Alliance rogue racials broadly seen as weak versus Horde options; Troll/Orc/Undead/Goblin called "S tier" by one player — [[d:rogue#1467858387294486659]] [[d:rogue#1467863460967743645]]. Gnome's Escape Artist (fear/charm/root break) called out repeatedly as a strong defensive PvP racial — [[d:rogue#1467857162553397298]]
