## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[PvE tier]** Arcane is the clear #1 PvE raid spec — "30-40% stronger than frost for PvE," consistent because RNG evens out over many casts; in a top-100 Kara mage-parse sample, ~90 were arcane, ~8 fire, ~2 frost — [[d:mage#1464614790407127153]], [[d:mage#1468709653184643163]], [[d:mage#1465737132768231517]].
- **[Fire]** Fire "does more damage than arcane in perfect/favorable conditions" and top fire parses can beat arcane, but has much higher variance (ignite RNG); ignite is shared across fire mages in logs, so too many fire mages in one raid hurts individual (and sometimes total) fire dps — sweet spot is generally 2 fire mages, occasionally 3 works with the 1.18.1 fireblast fix — [[d:mage#1465727467707957526]], [[d:mage#1465737132768231517]], [[d:mage#1468713916501852311]], [[d:mage#1468714021040558204]]. Fire is very gear-dependent and weak before AQ40/Naxx due to widespread fire-resistant/immune mobs before then — [[d:mage#1468879512749080669]].
- **[Frost]** Frost is broadly agreed to be the weakest PvE spec — pure RNG based on Frostbite's static 15% proc chance with no scaling, "handcuffed" because buffing it would also affect PvP; considered mainly the PvP spec — [[d:mage#1464359850497806632]], [[d:mage#1465579211405135894]], [[d:mage#1464614790407127153]], [[d:mage#1465722140178251950]]. Frost has good burst but doesn't scale; competitive with arcane only in the earliest raid tiers (per Leery) — [[d:mage#1464997486350569646]].
- **[Solo/duo play]** Solo fire mage still "pumps" and is viable; getting even 4% haste lets solo fire chain Fireball→Fireblast→Fireball without dropping ignite — [[d:mage#1465729418818490542]], [[d:mage#1464389538037235878]].
- **[Leveling/dungeons]** For 35-60 dungeon leveling, Frost or Fire recommended over Arcane (Arcane's mana pool too small); Frost better for big AoE pulls, Fire for smaller/normal pulls — [[d:mage#1466569768885813513]], [[d:mage#1466570131382468834]]. For leveling generally, Fire and Arcane suit single-target, Frost suits AoE — [[d:mage#1464803186480119971]].
- **[Raiding, by server]** Shared summary for BWL-era Ambershire: "Arcane is great, frost is good, fire is trolling in BWL" — [[d:mage#1471509289276412018]]. Nord/TA data broadly agrees Arcane leads ("meta is arcane," ~90% of top-100 Kara mage parses) — [[d:mage#1467235543531257886]], [[d:mage#1468709653184643163]] — though Luden TA argues Arcane's consistency comes purely from rolling the RNG dice so many times it evens out, and personally feels "Arcane just needs tuned down" relative to Fire's ceiling — [[d:mage#1468702989257670758]], [[d:mage#1468703616474022042]], [[d:mage#1468706332378009684]]. Counter-view (Lightemgee, TA server): "Frost performs just fine, and in fact does competitive DPS with arcane" — contested given the Nord-wide parse data above — [[d:mage#1477405415108251649]].
- **[New player guidance]** For raiding specifically, Arcane is called the easiest spec to gear into; Frost/Fire both require noticeably better gear to perform — [[d:mage#1471836459270017075]].

## Leveling
- Mage is weak at very low levels (little mana/utility), gets progressively easier with level and gear — [[d:mage#1462244387135422666]].
- Basic kiting pattern for low levels: Frostbolt → Fireball spam; Frost Nova when a mob reaches melee range, run to max distance, repeat; Fire Blast as an execution/finisher when mob is low or you're kiting — [[d:mage#1462360248256954369]].
- Getting **3/3 Frostbite + 3/3 Permafrost** (available by level 20) is described as a major leveling power spike, letting mages sometimes kill mobs before they arrive — [[d:mage#1462784163781546026]].
- **Accelerated Arcana** worth taking ASAP while leveling for just 1 point: 5% haste plus reduced Rupture/Evocation cooldown — [[d:mage#1462476048695427183]], [[d:mage#1462477308878393611]].
- Stat priority while leveling debated: focus SP if mana allows (Shared); prioritize Int to survive more fights before needing to drink, since water is free for mages (Lyans) — [[d:mage#1463948932563603541]], [[d:mage#1463949167608070258]], [[d:mage#1463949287590203422]].
- Spell coefficients for spells below level 20 are poor ("shit"); normal above that — [[d:mage#1461543828996489329]], [[d:mage#1461553333247541280]].
- Around level 25, prioritize SP gear generally, but watch for mana issues from switching too much gear — [[d:mage#1461530503835553877]], [[d:mage#1461538444634362070]], [[d:mage#1461552316368031807]].
- For dungeon leveling 35-60, Frost/Fire preferred over Arcane; Frost for anticipated big pulls, Fire for normal-sized pulls — [[d:mage#1466569572705636443]], [[d:mage#1466569768885813513]], [[d:mage#1466570131382468834]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane is considered weak/boring to level below ~level 24, since Rank 3 of Arcane Missiles (learned at 24) does double the total damage of Rank 2 (280 vs. 144) — recommendation is to level Fire or Frost until at least 24 before trying Arcane, and that the talents reachable around 35-40 (e.g. Resonance Cascade) are what make Arcane actually fun — [[d:mage#1473113689132630157]], [[d:mage#1473616960746029118]], [[d:mage#1474520947926241343]], [[d:mage#1474545381307056188]], [[d:mage#1478672876948164668]].
- **[Frost]** Frost is called "the chill spec to level" and the safest/most reliable choice overall — especially recommended for classic-style solo questing without AoE grinding, since no spec dependency issue arises without needing to farm crowds — [[d:mage#1467437401772392489]], [[d:mage#1471836459270017075]], [[d:mage#1474427809933037658]]. Fire/Arcane are said to be somewhat harder than Frost before ~level 25 but "nothing impossible" — [[d:mage#1467437862151520256]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Arcane, detailed]** Full boss rotation shared by Oaks (with logs linked): `Talisman of Power > Rupture > Missiles > (if Clearcasting proc → Surge, popping Presence of Mind while Surge's GCD is active) > Missiles > Rupture > pop MQG + Arcane Power while GCD active > back into missile spam; once haste is high enough, skip Surge but keep Rupture up` — [[d:mage#1468008389048008928]] (Oaks, 2026-02-03).
- **[Arcane]** Use Arcane Surge (cancelling Arcane Missiles) especially during the Arcane Rupture debuff window; Surge has an ~3-4s usability window; below ~50% mana, wait until the last second to cast Surge, above that cancel Missiles immediately — [[d:mage#1467199924109312040]], [[d:mage#1467200863721357393]], [[d:mage#1470639887412760758]]/[[d:mage#1470715482523828370]] (want max hit for reliable Surge procs off partial resists).
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Surge becomes a net DPS loss above roughly 20-25% haste due to GCD lock (old 1.12 client can't dynamically shrink the GCD with haste) — [[d:mage#1467183525995479132]], [[d:mage#1467184035255160852]], [[d:mage#1466188046163644428]]. Hard test-confirmed caps: Arcane Rupture stops improving above 58.3% haste (cast time would go below GCD); Arcane Missiles has a hard 100% haste cap (in-game tested) — [[d:mage#1470553379427188847]], [[d:mage#1470566698372300801]], [[d:mage#1470570972758409454]].
- **[Arcane]** Gem (ZG on-use int trinket)/Arcane Power/Berserking sequencing: pop Gem first into Arcane Power (so AP benefits from Gem's cooldown reduction), spend haste consumes during Gem, and use AP with one Missile cast remaining on Gem's timer — [[d:mage#1469363343566307470]], [[d:mage#1469365656561647831]].
- **[Fire]** Multiple proposed/used ST rotations: `Fireblast → 4x Scorch → Fireblast → (hotstreak Pyroblast if available) → Fireblast if available → Fireball`; Akanamu's solo-fire variant: `2x Scorch → Fireblast → Scorch → Fireball → Fireblast → repeat` — [[d:mage#1466154744287596809]], [[d:mage#1466158008231919788]]. Always Pyroblast immediately after a Fireblast crit (hot streak) — [[d:mage#1465406147258220657]], [[d:mage#1466159495410356374]].
- **[Fire]** Opener tech: fire off a Fireblast while a Fireball is still in flight to bank extra Fire Vulnerability stacks before the Fireball lands (used at shard-phase on Meph, general max-range openers) — [[d:mage#1466186378617290815]], [[d:mage#1466186616308760890]].
- **[Fire]** With multiple fire mages, coordinate: one player intentionally drops a weak opening ignite via Scorch→Fireblast so a stronger shared ignite can be built afterward; with more delay/desync some ignites may "stick" despite the drop attempt — [[d:mage#1466198674735497530]], [[d:mage#1466198857120616482]], [[d:mage#1466199087517929482]].
- **[Fire]** Fire Vulnerability ("scorch vuln," 5 stacks, 15% dmg, 30s duration) should be applied *before* building your ignite-generating crit, not after — it modifies the stored ignite damage at the moment of the crit (confirmed via worked math by Aest/jc473: crit-then-debuff = lower ignite than debuff-then-crit even though the direct-hit damage ends up equal) — [[d:mage#1466208148367999270]], [[d:mage#1466212364125999157]], [[d:mage#1466212460716626084]], [[d:mage#1466213805527797860]], [[d:mage#1466214293728137430]]. Ignite then also re-checks Fire Vuln on each tick (effectively "double dips": both the crit's own damage and the ignite ticks benefit) — [[d:mage#1466210151127187660]], [[d:mage#1466210184572571742]].
- **[Fire]** Fire Vulnerability lasts 30s and refreshes/stacks to 5 on subsequent Fireblasts; question of exact refresh timing was raised but not fully resolved in-chunk — [[d:mage#1467254399242993706]] (contested, low detail).
- **[Fire]** With MQG active, just spam Fireball (skip Fireblast) per Aest, though this is described as "more complicated than people realize" — [[d:mage#1470857074983440394]], [[d:mage#1470858267944292523]], [[d:mage#1470887256418947274]].
- **[Fire]** MQG usage guidance: often better saved for specific fights (e.g. Medivh in Kara40, Faerlina/Heigan in Naxx) rather than every pull, since fast kill times mean less total value; sequencing MQG vs. trinket procs (Scythe of Elune) around fight openers discussed at length without firm consensus — [[d:mage#1470890829055787194]], [[d:mage#1470891220409651418]], [[d:mage#1470893796047720479]].
- **[Frost]** ST opener after the Frost Barrier QoL change: Frostbolt → Icicles → Frost Nova → Icicles (ZeroRanger) — [[d:mage#1466891208532955228]].
- **[Frost]** Alternative frost rotation (oh, self-described "about BiS for frost"): `Icicles > Nova > Icicles > Cold Snap > Icicles > Nova > Icicles > then Frostbolt until Nova is off CD`, with an occasional Cone of Cold before Cold Snap — [[d:mage#1466899456141299763]].
- **[Frost]** Icicles without the Flash Freeze talent isn't worth casting proactively — better to Frostbolt first for a chance to proc Flash Freeze, since raw Frostbolt DPS is actually higher than Icicles (5s cast vs. 2.5-3s, and Icicles' single tick ~400 dmg is less than an average Frostbolt hit) — [[d:mage#1466900446844031099]], [[d:mage#1466902599302385665]], [[d:mage#1466901178720845825]]. The Frostbolt cast-time talent reduces it from 3s to 2.5s — [[d:mage#1466904371899142441]].
- **[Frost]** Fishing for Icicle procs with only 1 point in Improved Blizzard rank 1 is "insanely good" on certain fights (e.g. Garr - can spam Icicles onto boss while fishing procs off a 5-mob pack); roughly break-even on a generic 5-mob pack — [[d:mage#1467928297353773188]], [[d:mage#1467928499997245481]], [[d:mage#1467928581001973840]].
- **[Fire]** Post-fix ST opener tested on Patchwerk (Pepopo, once Fireblast applies the same Fire-Vuln debuff as Scorch): `Fireblast → Scorch → Scorch → Scorch → Fireball → Fireblast`; trying to further "extend" a big ignite by throwing in extra Scorches is a DPS loss — solo fire cannot keep ignite rolling consistently within its 4s window anyway, so average damage is better served by just maximizing Fireball/Fireblast/Pyroblast casts rather than stalling on Scorch to "protect" a big ignite — [[d:mage#1478881200079769690]], [[d:mage#1478897999286505503]], [[d:mage#1478905379802058992]].
- **[Fire]** Ignite mechanics explained in detail (biloen): a crit applies Ignite, which deals 40% of that crit's damage again as a DoT over 4 seconds; Ignite can stack up to 5 (storing the value of up to 5 recent crits); any further crit — even while already at 5 stacks — resets the DoT's duration back to 4s; if no crit lands within that 4s window the Ignite "drops." Rule of thumb crit thresholds to keep an ignite rolling: a Fireball→Fireblast cadence needs ~50% average crit; a Scorch→Scorch→Fireblast cadence needs only ~33% — [[d:mage#1478705573376819295]], [[d:mage#1478707474113757357]].
- **[Fire]** Pyroblast (Hot Streak) cast-timing guidance: cast at 5 stacks normally; drop to 4 stacks either when the boss is close to dying (to bank more of Pyroblast's DoT before the kill) or right after a Fireblast specifically to avoid risking an ignite drop — [[d:mage#1479141574372757517]], [[d:mage#1479141684251070686]].
- **[Any]** Presence of Mind (PoM) reliably fails to grant its free-cast buff when macroed together with another spell cast in quick succession — better cast standalone; on the arcane T3.5 5-piece set, popping PoM then immediately `/cancelaura Presence of Mind` grants the set's 6-second free-mana effect without needing to spend PoM's charge on an actual spell — [[d:mage#1479120295553142976]], [[d:mage#1479226041414320284]], [[d:mage#1479230663373881344]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Fire]** Flamestrike proc-chance mechanics for on-hit effects (e.g. sigil trinkets): currently (post-change) counts per enemy hit, not per cast, meaning AoE spells like Flamestrike get many more proc "rolls" in big pulls; this was not always the mechanic (changed at some point) — [[d:mage#1461134941373993032]], [[d:mage#1461135638643343605]], [[d:mage#1461139019747561582]]. Flamestrike's 2.5s cast + a sigil's 2s ICD means near-constant proc uptime on big trash pulls (e.g. Strat/Sholo) — [[d:mage#1461136111911960619]]. DoT ticks (ignite) do NOT count as "direct damaging spells" for these on-hit procs — [[d:mage#1461142455247442155]].
- **[Fire]** Flamestrike vs AE spam for trash: situational — worth it when mobs will stay grouped and alive a while (e.g. pre-Gnarlmoon and post-Anomalus mage/conjurer packs in K40); if the raid melts trash fast, "just AE + Blastwave" is better; decision should weigh whether tank/melee will keep mobs stationary long enough — [[d:mage#1465749962976657460]], [[d:mage#1465750162348707850]], [[d:mage#1468725989961109605]], [[d:mage#1468726138787332261]], [[d:mage#1468726359546138676]].
- **[Fire]** Blast Wave, despite being AoE, does NOT crit multiple targets independently the way Flamestrike does — a player at 100% crit (10-stack Combustion) only saw 1 of 9 mobs crit from Blast Wave — [[d:mage#1465281368929075272]], [[d:mage#1465293476231909590]] (bug/quirk, single source, unconfirmed root cause).
- **[Fire]** Flare Core 6-piece set (ZG) analysis: needs large packs to be worth it — under 10 mobs skip it; ~8% of damage at 10 mobs; ~40%+ of damage at 25+ mobs; at 40-60 mob pulls (e.g. Dragonmaw Retreat/Stormrought duo'd with a bear tank) it reached ~50% of total damage; causes major client-side lag/stutter from procs, mitigated by hiding effects with the Perfboost DLL mod; has quadratic (not linear) scaling with mob count, so it shines only at very large scale; even with BOED equipped, mana becomes an issue on smaller packs — [[d:mage#1468412814086508635]], [[d:mage#1468413321119010988]], [[d:mage#1463991961185685769]], [[d:mage#1463992121735254037]], [[d:mage#1463993710915354696]], [[d:mage#1463994135064084564]], [[d:mage#1463994282372239537]]. Cannot be used on Rag/Baron encounters, which drags down its average parse value — [[d:mage#1462139517640380506]].
- **[Arcane]** Stat-weight sims (Turtle Arcane wiki) show Hit is worth ~0 SP-equivalent for AoE because most trash isn't boss-level and mages are usually already hit-capped from talents alone; Haste likewise contributes little to AE — [[d:mage#1463263470022823976]], [[d:mage#1463265344985763956]]. Crit is called the best AoE stat generally — [[d:mage#1465056521728823319]].
- **[Any]** Sigil of Ancient Accord (ZG trinket) is considered the best passive AoE trinket, but its burst threat on trash risks pulling aggro; pair it with Fetish of the Sand Reaver (non-damaging, doesn't share cooldown with damage trinkets) to safely enable big pulls without ripping threat — [[d:mage#1466062638076264510]], [[d:mage#1466062733458800836]], [[d:mage#1466064206678724791]], [[d:mage#1466083915121950754]], [[d:mage#1466084186090901597]] (risk noted: instant-popping Sigil on pull has a real chance to still cause a rip if a mob has low tank threat).
- **[Any]** Blade of Eternal Darkness (BOED) procs on-hit per enemy struck (good for AoE, can proc multiple times per cast if no ICD unlike other trinkets with ICDs) but only from the initial spell hit, not from DoT ticks (e.g. Flamestrike's follow-up ticks don't trigger it) — best paired with Cone of Cold / Arcane Explosion; mainly valued for its mana-regen proc rather than its ~100 dmg proc when farming (≈1.5% of dps on a 5-mob pull) — [[d:mage#1463943781983453308]], [[d:mage#1463948419520270370]], [[d:mage#1467268581526995066]], [[d:mage#1467315344589852712]], [[d:mage#1467324597824126977]], [[d:mage#1467327660572147843]]. ## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Any]** Spell hit vs. a mob 3 levels higher: base hit chance is 83%, and the maximum achievable hit chance is capped at 99% (never a true 100% vs. higher-level targets) — this is why "16% hit" (6% from gear + 10% from a maxed Arcane Focus talent, or equivalent) is called the practical hit cap; running only 15% hit still misses ~2% of casts, not a clean 1%, because the system doesn't finely accumulate the remaining sliver — [[d:mage#1475293772999954594]], [[d:mage#1469210115834904714]], [[d:mage#1469211364387065897]], [[d:mage#1475288739503935622]], [[d:mage#1475273384702116010]]. Turtle's druid "Emerald Blessing" raid buff supplies +1% hit, so some players plan around only 15% personal hit — [[d:mage#1475297169300000808]], [[d:mage#1475297341744615575]].
- **[Arcane]** Haste-to-SP conversion: roughly 10 SP per 1% haste at pre-raid gear, rising to ~18-20 SP per 1% near-BiS (haste's value increases with gear level because it scales your existing damage/cast-speed multiplicatively) — [[d:mage#1466052950182072431]], [[d:mage#1469497305261539368]], [[d:mage#1469500061661528167]], [[d:mage#1469497320063107297]].
- **[Any]** Haste sources stack multiplicatively, not additively: e.g. 10% gear haste × 30% Arcane Power × 3% Juju Flurry × 5% Potion of Quickness ≈ ×1.546 total, meaning the two consumables effectively add ~11% rather than a flat 8% on top of AP — [[d:mage#1472017047591325747]], [[d:mage#1472016248442196123]].
- **[Arcane]** Confirmed hard/soft haste caps (in-game tested, not just simmed): Arcane Missiles channel time hard-caps at 100% haste; Arcane Rupture's cast time hits the GCD floor and stops improving at 58.3% haste — [[d:mage#1470548267199631360]], [[d:mage#1470553379427188847]], [[d:mage#1470566698372300801]], [[d:mage#1470570972758409454]], [[d:mage#1468035315628244993]].
- **[Arcane]** Accelerated Arcana (and its warlock equivalent) reduces arcane spell *cooldowns* by a haste-scaled factor but is NOT itself a "haste" stat — it doesn't register on haste-tracking addons and doesn't stack with true haste sources for cast-speed purposes — [[d:mage#1470121421459034406]], [[d:mage#1472024436671778946]], [[d:mage#1472024544976965725]]. MQG and other haste sources do not reduce the Global Cooldown on this server (haste didn't reduce GCD until later expansions) — [[d:mage#1470101411839741973]], [[d:mage#1470104251031093320]].
- **[Any]** A spell's crit and hit rolls are independent and a miss "takes precedence" — a spell can technically crit and then miss (undetectable without packet-level inspection), disproving the idea that "crits can't miss" — [[d:mage#1474089494973255771]], [[d:mage#1474089701974737127]], [[d:mage#1474092726617243709]], [[d:mage#1474092799384354878]].
- **[Arcane]** New "Fortune" stat (announced for an upcoming itemization patch) increases the proc chance of non-class-specific chance-based item effects; will appear only on necks, cloaks, and trinkets. Community sims (Ocra, using an arcane mage with several proc items) found it weak: 5% Fortune ≈ only 3.3-9.6 SP-equivalent depending on trinket loadout, with diminishing returns as base proc uptime rises (ICDs limit the benefit) — [[d:mage#1472658864334966825]], [[d:mage#1474044889829474414]], [[d:mage#1474048208576446546]], [[d:mage#1474057956550119715]], [[d:mage#1474061637685215374]]. Contested: some argue a strong dedicated high-Fortune cloak (~20%+) could be worth using for a few fights once Sigil is owned, most others expect flat stats to keep winning — [[d:mage#1476521572247273547]], [[d:mage#1476521776870719550]], [[d:mage#1476522173962256425]], [[d:mage#1476522648694427689]], [[d:mage#1476540112853794928]].
- **[Arcane]** T3.5 arcane 3-piece set bonus (Improved Presence of Mind) sims at roughly a 3% DPS increase in both single-target and multi-target, equivalent to about 60 SP (ST) / 180 SP (multi-target) — [[d:mage#1479126581791293564]] (Ocra). The bonus is also reported buggy: it often fails to trigger the Improved PoM buff for no clear reason, and even when it works there's a ~500ms delay — [[d:mage#1474801846119043146]], [[d:mage#1478832544794546248]], [[d:mage#1474823099936538646]] (partly attributed to consuming PoM's free cast too fast).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Fire]** MQG usage priority: best saved for longer fights or ones with a forced pre-pull window to pre-proc it (Hardmode KT specifically called out as ideal); under ~2 minutes, other on-use trinkets can out-value MQG per arcane-wiki sims — [[d:mage#1472414395211583659]], [[d:mage#1472414543908044964]], [[d:mage#1472414583082979492]], [[d:mage#1472414700041015298]]. For fire specifically, MQG is often saved for specific fights only (e.g. Medivh in Kara40, Faerlina/Heigan in Naxx) rather than every pull — [[d:mage#1470890829055787194]].
- **[Any]** Iceblock (and similarly Ice Barrier's immunity-adjacent effects) do NOT reset threat/aggro the way Rogue Vanish does — it functions like Blessing of Protection/Lay on Hands: you're immune to damage but stay on the threat table, and damage dealt while immune still generates threat — [[d:mage#1475235079826706482]], [[d:mage#1475235315546591466]], [[d:mage#1475241261320372458]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Rupture is coded as a "debuff" on the target but functionally behaves like a snapshotting buff — it doesn't re-check caster stats each tick the way a normal debuff would — a known engine quirk that players account for when planning snapshotting/recast timing — [[d:mage#1466215944908181731]], [[d:mage#1466216553677586652]], [[d:mage#1466216688612802715]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Power's mana-drain/death risk remains a real danger even in good gear (players report dying with seconds left on AP if they play aggressively with recasts); the community wants either the death risk removed while keeping mana drain, or the reduced mana regen during AP removed so potions/teas don't feel wasted; running 6-piece T3 arcane or a +1s-missile-duration belt noticeably improves AP survivability/mana economy — [[d:mage#1479235845629153411]], [[d:mage#1479236008708149268]], [[d:mage#1479236252019593349]], [[d:mage#1479236870184636547]], [[d:mage#1479247225744785532]], [[d:mage#1474776252912111881]].
- **[Fire]** Bigwigs' "magetools" module supports a shareable "drop ignite" alert command any fire mage can trigger to warn the raid's other fire mages when a low-value ignite is rolling and should be intentionally reset — [[d:mage#1478706351755759742]], [[d:mage#1478710529290932245]], [[d:mage#1478721646943670332]], [[d:mage#1478732973145854043]].

- **[Fire]** Bigwigs "magetools" tracks ignite tick damage, ignite owner, and scorch/fire-vuln stacks, plus supports the shareable "drop ignite" alert command (`/bw extra magetools ignitedroptrigger`) — [[d:mage#1461756872313802899]], [[d:mage#1461758394238242907]], [[d:mage#1461761884368797788]], [[d:mage#1478706351755759742]].
- **[Any]** Nampower (sanctioned client-side DLL) removes the vanilla 1.12 client's cast-latency/round-trip penalty via spell queueing, letting spells queue back-to-back instead of costing latency+cast time each; also enables double-tap-to-cancel for clipping Arcane Missiles (to recast for haste snapshotting or reapply Temporal Convergence/Rupture); recommended for any high-ping player. Buffer/queue timing should be aligned to the average server tick (default 55ms), not to personal ping — [[d:mage#1461147889727181017]], [[d:mage#1461148664217993439]], [[d:mage#1461827788163125349]], [[d:mage#1461828094137471028]], [[d:mage#1464305024912982126]], [[d:mage#1464346484177768653]].

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Any]** Race is broadly agreed to barely matter for PvE performance except at the very top of parse rankings — pick based on preferred animations — [[d:mage#1470177129005318416]], [[d:mage#1470179517640216769]], [[d:mage#1472416834786431077]].
- **[Orc, contested]** Orc racial (10% haste, single source's characterization) valued at roughly 140 SP-equivalent (10% haste × ~14 SP/%) vs. Troll's flat ~60 SP racial, argued to be more broadly useful than Human's 2% dmg-vs-humanoid or Gnome's talents — [[d:mage#1470174602725687448]]; countered that Troll may be better early before hitting the haste cap without it — [[d:mage#1470175788044128368]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Fire]** Blast Wave, despite hitting multiple targets, does not roll a separate crit per target the way Flamestrike does — a player at 100% crit (10-stack Combustion) still saw only 1 of 9 mobs crit — [[d:mage#1465281368929075272]], [[d:mage#1465293476231909590]] (single source, unconfirmed root cause).
- **[Frost]** Flash Freeze/Icicles casts can be fully denied (rooted with zero cast progress, no cast even starting) if the player is damaged at the exact instant of casting, feeling like more than normal spell-pushback — unresolved, single report — [[d:mage#1474564050959597790]], [[d:mage#1474564102142693448]], [[d:mage#1474564173068369950]], [[d:mage#1474564348440608770]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Power occasionally appears to "vanish" mid-fight (notably on Kruul while self-decursing) with no dispel recorded in the combat log; most likely a visual buff-cap issue combined with the player's castbar addon (e.g. OCB) failing to detect the still-active buff for haste math — not an actual dispel, since the player couldn't manually cancel the "missing" buff either — [[d:mage#1474776252912111881]], [[d:mage#1474776926987223203]], [[d:mage#1474781592638718135]], [[d:mage#1474782336985075915]], [[d:mage#1474786710419280060]].
- **[Any]** Wand-shoot macro-spam behaves differently from Hunter Auto Shot: wand shots only remain "repeatable" while standing still (flashing red only when stationary), while Hunter's Auto Shot continues while moving — confirmed to be intended behavior, not a bug — [[d:mage#1468335783466238094]], [[d:mage#1468337202013667532]], [[d:mage#1468375258380636190]].
- **[Any]** Blade of Eternal Darkness (BOED) no longer procs from the Arcane Instability talent or from "duplicated"/cascaded spell effects (fixed in an earlier balance pass); it also previously incorrectly proc'd from DoT ticks (Fireball's DoT, ignite), which was fixed a while back too — [[d:mage#1474103991372742782]], [[d:mage#1474124732658880623]], [[d:mage#1474125344779931800]], [[d:mage#1476489820732657735]], [[d:mage#1476489942761738290]].
- **[Any]** True Band of Sulfuras (TBoS) only procs off spells with an actual damaging cast, not off DoT ticks/applications lacking an initial damage component — confirmed via official Turtle bug-tracker entries (IDs 11348, 17037); this is why affliction Warlocks largely stopped using it while Arcane/Fire mages (whose main spells all have on-cast damage) still benefit — [[d:mage#1478491464970277046]], [[d:mage#1478492520005767285]], [[d:mage#1478506975242227993]].
- **[Any]** OBD's spellstrike-style proc is reported to be losing the ability to trigger off non-cast-time (instant, e.g. Fireblast) spells in an upcoming patch, per the same wording used for a Paladin/Rogue/Elemental-Shaman nerf; arcane mages expect minimal impact since they mostly proc it off Arcane Missiles casts, with only a possible minor loss if it was also benefiting from Arcane Instability/duplicated-cast interactions — [[d:mage#1473810881086361694]], [[d:mage#1473811500769743131]], [[d:mage#1473811804995194961]], [[d:mage#1473811897936773121]], [[d:mage#1473812151201300744]], [[d:mage#1472705983666196501]], [[d:mage#1472741437769781359]].
- **[Arcane]** T3.5 arcane 5-piece set bonus frequently fails to grant its Improved Presence of Mind buff for no clear trigger condition, with a consistent ~500ms delay when it does trigger — [[d:mage#1474801846119043146]] (see also Stats section).
