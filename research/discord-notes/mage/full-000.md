## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Two large past class-rework patches are referred to as "CC1" and "CC2"; future changes are expected to be tweaks rather than full reworks — [[d:mage#1428498493047308310]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-17; single source).
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Rupture's spellpower coefficient was nerfed from 1.0 to 0.9 in this patch window; full coefficient table posted: Arcane Missile 0.328, Arcane Surge 0.65, Arcane Rupture 0.9 (was 1.0), Arcane Explosion 0.143, Blast Wave 0.129, Flamestrike(r6) 0.157 (+0.02 dot), Fireball 1.0, Pyroblast 1.0, Scorch 0.4285, Fireblast 0.4285, Frostbolt 0.814, Cone of Cold 0.129, Icicle 0.4 — [[d:mage#1428150507456954478]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-16), [[d:mage#1428151178247667723]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-16), [[d:mage#1428161559246405695]] (Pepopo, confirms 1.0→0.9).
- **[Arcane]** Gloves of Unwinding Mystery: patch notes announced spell power cut 22→9, but the live client kept showing/behaving as 22 SP for most of this period (confirmed by naked-vs-geared dummy testing); a bug report was filed, and by mid-November some claim the nerf was reversed/undone — contested, see Contested section — [[d:mage#1427776479936254052]] (Sellma, 2025-10-15), [[d:mage#1430624823142252684]] (alyflex, 2025-10-22, dummy test showing +22 still applies), [[d:mage#1431234432450433135]] (alyflex, 2025-10-24, bug report #18049), [[d:mage#1437586838574338108]] (A Wro, 2025-11-11).
- **[Fire]** Ignite duration was cut (referenced historically as 6s→4s across patches); a lone fire mage can barely sustain Ignite uptime alone post-nerf, making 2-3 coordinated fire mages the practical requirement for good fire ST parses — [[d:mage#1430521459318718547]] (Promey, 2025-10-22), [[d:mage#1433239394294173726]] (Aest, 2025-10-30), [[d:mage#1432008524841484509]] (Izzo, 2025-10-26; fire wiki flagged as outdated re: this).
- **[AoE/Leveling]** Improved Blizzard's slow was nerfed; Luden TA states 75%→50%, Lucius separately claims 65%→40% with duration cut 2s→1.5s (figures disagree, not reconciled) — [[d:mage#1431343840966479913]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-24), [[d:mage#1430717972997214329]] (Lucius, 2025-10-23). Separately, the Zul'Farrak mob-pathing exploit used for mage AoE/solo farming was patched out — [[d:mage#1424770785502953602]] (Pijan, 2025-10-06).
- **[Class balance]** New rogue poison scaling with agility was cited as the main driver of rogues overtaking mages on the meters; ~30-40% of rogue damage now comes from this poison — [[d:mage#1428491673155014668]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-17), [[d:mage#1428496473624023111]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-17).
- **[General]** Vampirism ("damage returned as healing") was nerfed hard for AoE interactions a few patches prior to this chunk; now only meaningfully felt at 8%+ vamp on big AoE pulls — [[d:mage#1431828663984193717]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-10-26), [[d:mage#1431829569043693619]] (ThatFatCat, 2025-10-26).
- **[Consumables]** Dreamtonic (int consumable) now persists through death (a recent change, previously it did not) — [[d:mage#1435777760722288804]] (Promey, 2025-11-06).
- **[Arcane]** BoED (proc trinket/weapon) multi-proc-per-cast was broken (only 1 proc per spell cast) for roughly 2 months starting around last December, then restored to intended multi-proc behavior — [[d:mage#1432494979816755371]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-28), [[d:mage#1432500563265978553]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-28).
- **[Mechanics]** Per-school "Scythe of Elune" damage-taken debuffs were renamed uniquely in 1.18 (Elune's Wrath, Elune's Ire, etc.) but still overwrite each other because they share an internal category — acknowledged as unintended by a dev-adjacent source — [[d:mage#1428739855277490349]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-17), [[d:mage#1428740189362323547]] (Aest, 2025-10-17).
- **[Mechanics]** Official dev comment on Resonance Cascade (Arcane self-trigger): hard-capped at 4 duplications, only rolls once per Arcane Explosion cast regardless of number of targets hit, and duplicated casts do not trigger on-hit item/spell effects — [[d:mage#1431347127828873318]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-24).
- **[Bug/regression]** Around 2025-11-05, several weapon procs with an explicit 0% SP coefficient (meant to be flat, non-scaling, e.g. Sulfuras/Cobalt Crusher-style procs) began using a default instant-cast 0.4285 coefficient and incorrectly scaling with spell power — [[d:mage#1435452592292626545]] (Beldrin, 2025-11-05), [[d:mage#1435461681055662222]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-05).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** All three specs are broadly described as raid-viable across MC/BWL/Kara-tier content, but opinions diverge sharply on how "viable" differs from "ideal" — [[d:mage#1425931393220874301]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-09; Ony/MC/BWL frost+arcane both fine, fire suffers due to fire resist/immunity), [[d:mage#1430996379983020132]] (Lightemgee, 2025-10-23, "pretty much every spec is raid viable now").
- **[Arcane]** Arcane is repeatedly called the most consistent, least gear-dependent, and (for much of this chunk) top or near-top raid DPS spec — respec alone gives a big DPS jump even in poor gear — [[d:mage#1426190199460987002]] (Boomwick, 2025-10-10), [[d:mage#1435006162264260638]] (Vurby, 2025-11-04, "Arcane is consistent good damage").
- **[Frost]** Frost is consistently called the weakest of the three specs for raid DPS: bad mana sustain outside long/slow fights, poor AoE scaling vs Arcane, worse single-target ceiling, and anti-synergy between Ice Barrier and constant incoming AoE damage — [[d:mage#1429247884469076119]] (Akanamu, 2025-10-19), [[d:mage#1429249488408805417]] (**, 2025-10-19, "frost is ok in slow raids... below 1:20 starts degrading"), [[d:mage#1436587791533215807]] (Aest, 2025-11-08, "frost is the one that's in need of help the most right now").
- **[Fire]** Fire has the highest ceiling/most variable output: needs 2-3 coordinated fire mages to sustain Ignite, is nearly unusable in MC/BWL due to fire resistance (Ragnaros and Geddon are fully fire-immune; most BWL bosses are highly fire-resistant, only Chromaggus/Broodlord are viable) — [[d:mage#1435006162264260638]] (Vurby, 2025-11-04), [[d:mage#1438828834047791187]] (Shared, 2025-11-14), [[d:mage#1438835648080515102]] (Arle, 2025-11-14).
- **[General]** By late October/November, community sentiment shifted: mage (mostly arcane) is felt to have dropped from top DPS to roughly 4th-5th behind rogues, warriors, and fire shamans in raid parses, though mage still leads pure AoE potential — [[d:mage#1432314463138222130]] (Izzo, 2025-10-27), [[d:mage#1432080792271720539]] (Pinkkee, 2025-10-26, "We're like 5th best dps in naxx. Ret paladins 4th").
- **[General]** Turtlogs (turtlogs.com/pve/ranking) used to compare live spec DPS rankings by realm — [[d:mage#1438846129323114578]] (scarletrage745, 2025-11-14).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Long-running debate on fastest leveling spec: the old FAQ claim "Frost is fastest for both single-target and AoE" is repeatedly questioned; several posters say Arcane becomes fine once Arcane Missiles rank 3 unlocks (~level 24, ~2x rank-2 damage) — [[d:mage#1424025328938123264]] (iusedisc, 2025-10-04; question), [[d:mage#1424230326451961951]] (Valaen, 2025-10-05), [[d:mage#1424047539581227029]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-10-04, disputes frost as the historical main damage spec even leveling).
- **[Arcane leveling]** Arcane Missiles are mana-inefficient and get heavy pushback before ~level 30, so most recommend leveling Frost or Fire first and respeccing to Arcane around level 24-35 — [[d:mage#1429898668089151528]] (Arle, 2025-10-20, respec 30-35), [[d:mage#1429900713751744512]] (Arle, 2025-10-20), [[d:mage#1427707632239640677]] (Astraeya, 2025-10-14, "Frost is better for survival and mana. Arcane hits harder").
- **[AoE leveling]** Blizzard-based AoE farming is considered dead/heavily nerfed: slow reduced (see Changes section) and mobs now walk through the AoE circle before it ends, wasting mana — the new standard rotation is Frost Nova → Flamestrike → Cone of Cold → Arcane Explosion (repeat), or a pure-frost Cone of Cold + Arcane Explosion loop without Nova — [[d:mage#1423659276966301849]] (Akanamu, 2025-10-03), [[d:mage#1431344576542671049]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-24), [[d:mage#1438219818116448277]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-11-12), [[d:mage#1436705656941056132]] (Izzo, 2025-11-08, "blizzard is dead, coc is alive").
- **[AoE leveling]** No confirmed target cap on Cone of Cold or Arcane Explosion — claims of an AoE cap are called "propaganda"; one user slowed an entire ZG spider pack with CoC — [[d:mage#1439032972274565295]] (Amber, 2025-11-15; question), [[d:mage#1439042850317799425]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-15), [[d:mage#1439882358940504195]] (Boomwick, 2025-11-17, "Theres no limit").
- **[Leveling stats]** While leveling, prioritize Spellpower and Intellect; don't chase spell hit until fighting dungeon mobs 2-3 levels above you — [[d:mage#1424664349888483378]] (Lightemgee, 2025-10-06), [[d:mage#1434201589421314099]] (Izzo, 2025-11-01).
- **[Leveling weapons]** Use Fireball (not Frostbolt) at very low level since it unlocks a higher available rank sooner — [[d:mage#1425113776557916230]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-07). Early wand sources: Night Elf/High Elf starting-zone quests (level 1-5) grant a wand — [[d:mage#1424002896781119569]] (Valaen, 2025-10-04). RFD (Ragefire Chasm/dungeon) drops a good early wand; several posters say skip the mage wand-quest chain entirely — [[d:mage#1425868581173723196]] (Arle, 2025-10-09). Mid-level wand upgrades: Chillnail Splinter (Duskwallow quest chain, 1.4 speed) or Freezing Shard (1.3 speed) — [[d:mage#1430576252502413352]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-22), [[d:mage#1430576558183022602]] (Shared, 2025-10-22); a Scholomance wand (4 int/11 SP) is also called out as an upgrade — [[d:mage#1437900378258669568]] (Luden TA, 2025-11-12).
- **[Dungeon leveling]** Deadmines run is recommended early for its staff/wand/ring drops — [[d:mage#1430913027577745540]] (Mr. Smite, 2025-10-23). Zul'Farrak solo-kiting via Cone of Cold is a known but pathing-exploit-patched method (see Changes) — [[d:mage#1424770785502953602]] (Pijan, 2025-10-06); newer non-exploit solo methods are covered in community video guides — [[d:mage#1436212102053232694]] (Xanadu Plays, 2025-11-07).
- **[Rested XP]** Rested bonus is obtained by resting in an inn OR standing near a player-placed tent outside a city (commonly at the Stormwind/Orgrimmar gates); one tent grants rest in roughly 10 minutes, more tents speed it up — [[d:mage#1435614404506030110]] (Afraido, 2025-11-05), [[d:mage#1435614570176843807]] (Afraido, 2025-11-05), [[d:mage#1435614749885988912]] (Afraido, 2025-11-05).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Arcane]** Core loop: keep the Arcane Rupture debuff up as continuously as possible, filling with Arcane Missiles; interrupt Missiles to cast Arcane Surge only when it procs AND you are below the haste threshold where Surge is worth it (see Stats section); never use Surge or Presence of Mind during an active Arcane Power or MQG window — [[d:mage#1432389374464491550]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-27), [[d:mage#1432389709166018630]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-27), [[d:mage#1437417456741257217]] (Shared, 2025-11-10), [[d:mage#1437417525922107525]] (Shared, 2025-11-10).
- **[Arcane]** Snapshot mechanics (confirmed via testing): the Arcane Rupture buff and any active haste effects are applied to a Missiles channel at the moment each individual missile is CAST, not when it lands or hits the target; distance to the target therefore doesn't affect DPS except right before the mob dies — [[d:mage#1432868307014389871]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-29, corrects his own earlier belief), [[d:mage#1433051780781965333]] (Boomwick, 2025-10-29, confirms via testing), [[d:mage#1432782155703910400]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-28), [[d:mage#1433092512242991247]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-29, "all buffs and debuffs are calculated when a projectile spell is cast not when it lands... true for REOS and Nightfall" too).
- **[Arcane]** Cast-time and buff snapshotting: casting Arcane Missiles right at the last moment of a haste buff (e.g. Arcane Power/MQG) locks in that buff's haste for the ENTIRE channel; by contrast, spellpower affects each missile individually as it resolves, not snapshotted at cast — [[d:mage#1423688683667587102]] (Izzo, 2025-10-03), [[d:mage#1423689410678882511]] (Izzo, 2025-10-03).
- **[Arcane]** Surge/Rupture haste breakpoint: sim testing shows Arcane Surge is only a DPS gain below roughly 20-25% total haste; above that, interrupting a Missiles channel to cast Surge is a net DPS LOSS since Surge doesn't benefit from haste the way Missiles do — [[d:mage#1432774260597854248]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-28, raw sim data at 0/10/20/25/30 haste), [[d:mage#1432774464717979739]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-28, detailed breakdown), [[d:mage#1432921455678459954]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-29, breakpoint corrected/moved to 25% after fixing a rupture-snapshot bug in the sim).
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Missiles has a hard floor of 0.475s per missile tick with Accelerated Arcana talent (0.5s/tick without it); confirmed repeatedly via nampower debug logs (which print true server-side channel duration, e.g. `Channel start: Arcane Missiles duration 5640`), including a direct test hitting 0.470s (attributed to rounding, not a lower true floor) — [[d:mage#1434891188720373840]] (Dontlookatme, 2025-11-03), [[d:mage#1427648060527218769]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-14), [[d:mage#1435069155135983779]] (Pinkkee, 2025-11-04, "PROVEN... waste of haste consumes to pop it all together"), [[d:mage#1435816278827859969]] (Nitrous, 2025-11-06, confirms 2.8s is the visible floor).
- **[Arcane]** The visible Arcane Missiles cast bar (e.g. showing "2.8s" with the T3 belt) is a rounded client-side display, not the true server tick timing; stacking additional haste past that visual point does not reduce true tick time further once you're at the hard floor — [[d:mage#1435064340419055696]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-04), [[d:mage#1434784124392181791]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-03).
- **[Arcane]** Haste-cooldown stacking vs. spreading: sim data shows spreading MQG/AP/Juju/Quickness/Berserking across the fight gives slightly higher DPS below the missile-tick haste cap, but once combined haste nears ~66.6% (where Arcane Rupture's cast time hits the 1.5s GCD floor), combining them stops being a loss — [[d:mage#1436113705480884345]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-07, sim table at 0/10/15/20/30 haste), [[d:mage#1436119153554493563]] (Izzo, 2025-11-07, breakpoint at 15%), [[d:mage#1436147009815052299]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-07, ~66% combined haste), [[d:mage#1436162002673209383]] (Akanamu, 2025-11-07). Rupture cast time below 1.5s is a DPS LOSS because Rupture is bound by its own GCD, so extra haste there just wastes buff uptime waiting on the GCD — [[d:mage#1435147040400212041]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-04).
- **[Arcane]** Recommended cooldown order: pop MQG first, then Arcane Power on the tail end of MQG, to snapshot MQG's haste onto AP and shorten AP's effective cooldown (haste reduces spell cooldowns) — [[d:mage#1435874307745255489]] (Kolima, 2025-11-06), [[d:mage#1435875517730394184]] (Pinkkee, 2025-11-06), [[d:mage#1434898288036806860]] (Shared, 2025-11-03, "hitting arcane power on the tail end of MQG, and firing only one double dipped missile").
- **[Arcane]** End-of-buff decision: with AP/MQG about to expire, most experienced players cast one more Missiles volley to fully use the remaining buff rather than overlapping the Rupture refresh; Temporal Convergence (free instant Rupture proc) has its own 15s base cooldown, so casting it too early sacrifices uptime — sim difference between the two approaches is small — [[d:mage#1440019823751991417]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-17), [[d:mage#1440020691893227641]] (Akanamu, 2025-11-17), [[d:mage#1440027197036564510]] (Akanamu, 2025-11-17), [[d:mage#1440036230807945298]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-17, "difference is pretty minimal").
- **[Fire]** Solo-fire ST rotation: build Ignite via Fireball/Fireblast (Fireblast applies a Scorch debuff stack), then maintain with Scorch + Fireblast once Ignite is stacked; keeping a 4-second Ignite alive solo is hard post-nerf, so 2-3 fire mages coordinating hand-offs is standard for good fire numbers — [[d:mage#1430553498608599163]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-22), [[d:mage#1436842451150176307]] (Aest, 2025-11-09, "fireblast applies a stack of scorch"), [[d:mage#1430510992273707119]] (Promey, 2025-10-22).
- **[Frost]** Core ST kit: Frostbolt, Ice Barrier, Icicles (ideally via Flash Freeze proc), Frost Nova, Cone of Cold when in melee range — [[d:mage#1435806920245117042]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-06). Un-procced Icicles is worse DPS than Frostbolt below ~240 spellpower (icicles DPS ≈ 272 + 0.08×SP over its channel vs. frostbolt DPS ≈ 214.2 + 0.32×SP), but a Flash Freeze-procced Icicles is always better regardless of spellpower — [[d:mage#1430677070396133528]] (Akanamu, 2025-10-23, full math), [[d:mage#1430676081240834068]] (Aest, 2025-10-23), [[d:mage#1430676160114851912]] (Schmitticus, 2025-10-23).
- **[Frost]** Casting Icicles roots the caster for the channel and deals extra self-damage if hit while rooted; canceling early still leaves you rooted for the full duration unless you Blink out — [[d:mage#1428745165132140588]] (Aest, 2025-10-17), [[d:mage#1430673305886658611]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-23).
- **[Arcane]** Sigil of Ancient Accord (MC trinket) procs off each individual Arcane Missile cast independently (2s internal cooldown per source), rolls only once per Arcane Explosion cast, and carries meaningful spellpower scaling (~60 SP average value at roughly half-Naxx gear on single target) — [[d:mage#1430570351980577061]] (Shared, 2025-10-22), [[d:mage#1431355604143968319]] (ralleballe, 2025-10-24; question), [[d:mage#1431361308632285247]] (Dontlookatme, 2025-10-24, confirms 2s ICD + SP scaling, no known exact coefficient).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Frost/AoE]** Current-meta AoE combo: Frost Nova → Flamestrike → Cone of Cold → Arcane Explosion, timing Flamestrike to land right before Nova naturally breaks — [[d:mage#1423659276966301849]] (Akanamu, 2025-10-03), [[d:mage#1436468556274602146]] (Luden TA, 2025-11-08). A pure-frost variant without Nova (Cone of Cold + Arcane Explosion only) is also commonly used — [[d:mage#1436463662087929896]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-11-08).
- **[AoE]** Blizzard is considered dead for both leveling and endgame trash AoE: poor damage:mana ratio, mobs now walk through the zone before it expires — Blast Wave alone has ~2.5x more base damage, so coefficients matter little for AoE — [[d:mage#1428380119524839545]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-16), [[d:mage#1436705656941056132]] (Izzo, 2025-11-08).
- **[Arcane/AoE]** Arcane's 10% talent-based hit is usually sufficient vs. sub-boss-level trash/adds, so gearing extra hit specifically for AoE pulls is rarely necessary — [[d:mage#1426251077547855962]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-10), [[d:mage#1426257933364236330]] (Kolima, 2025-10-10).
- **[Endgame raid AoE]** Sapper Charges + Jewel of Wild Magics (Arcane version) + Arcane Explosion combo can deal roughly 50k damage in a single GCD on a big trash pack — [[d:mage#1438566091365552378]] (Akanamu, 2025-11-13). Jewel of Wild Magics tooltip data (posted in full): Frost — 491-540 frost dmg to all within 10yd + 50% slow/+15% time-between-attacks for 10s; Fire — 491-540 fire dmg + 100 dmg/2s dot for 6s; Arcane — 491-540 dmg (tooltip mislabels school as fire) + 3% cast speed and +50 spell damage for 12s; Holy — 491-540 dmg + 350-450 party heal; roughly 15% SP coefficient on the initial hit, ~10% on the fire dot — [[d:mage#1429985150594318488]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-21), [[d:mage#1429985696550223973]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-21).
- **[AoE]** BoED-type weapon proc can multi-proc per Arcane Explosion cast (see Changes for its bugged period); the Spellwoven Nobility Drape cloak is favored specifically for AoE-spam uptime (~40-50% uptime single-target, much higher on sustained AoE) but is risky to wear during Arcane Power due to its int proc raising max mana and thus the mana-threshold death risk — [[d:mage#1432482999592550584]] (Akanamu, 2025-10-28), [[d:mage#1432446319083130921]] (Mondalv, 2025-10-27, 40-50% ST uptime test), [[d:mage#1430718310395416677]] (ThatFatCat, 2025-10-23, "It will kill you if you're using arcane power and it procs"), [[d:mage#1432741659484880966]] (Boomwick/Pinkkee thread, 2025-10-28).
- **[Gold farming/AoE]** Naxx-trash "ghoul farm": pull all 3 ghoul packs plus slimes together, Cone of Cold then immediate Frost Nova on any resist, repeat; fragile to resist RNG chains — [[d:mage#1433524597290172567]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-10-30), [[d:mage#1433515177109618709]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-30, notes it's usually done duo). ZG bijou farm via a panther-to-tiger pull is an alternative once the Naxx-trash farm's gold value dropped; Vampirism gear helps if including the Blood Drinker packs — [[d:mage#1433526565739954187]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-10-30), [[d:mage#1433527810332426290]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-10-30), [[d:mage#1433528197106110475]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-10-30, notes Axe Trolls were "fixed" to actually throw/stun now).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General]** Spell hit needed vs. a level-63 raid boss is 16% (universal caster hit cap on Turtle); the hit table for smaller level differences is 3%/4%/5%/16% for same/+1/+2/+3 levels — [[d:mage#1425882367599443998]] (Dontlookatme, 2025-10-09), [[d:mage#1439719592636190943]] (sosqua9-, 2025-11-17, restates raid-boss cap and talent contributions).
- **[Arcane vs Frost/Fire hit]** Arcane needs only ~5-6% hit from gear/buffs to reach the 16% cap (10% comes from talents); Frost/Fire need ~9-10% from gear (6% from talents) — [[d:mage#1425934816251220068]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-09), [[d:mage#1435957541216714812]] (Izzo, 2025-11-06).
- **[Arcane]** Haste is arcane's premium stat; community-derived value estimates range from ~10 SP/1% haste at low gear (~300 SP) up to ~13-16 SP/1% haste at high (Naxx/K40) gear, since haste's value scales up with the more of it you already have — [[d:mage#1433284768568447088]] (Aest, 2025-10-30, at ~300 SP: 1 haste≈10sp, 1 hit≈8sp, 1 crit≈8sp), [[d:mage#1430520539532754955]] (Sellma quoting the arcane FAQ, 2025-10-22, "1 haste ~ 13 sp"), [[d:mage#1432714419451854858]] (Shared, 2025-10-28, "closer to 15sp depending on how much you have"), [[d:mage#1438515180328714331]] (Shared, 2025-11-13, "roughly around 15-16SP"), [[d:mage#1434200148472496199]] (Izzo, 2025-11-01, "3 haste better than 100sp for 15sec if fight >1min... haste increasing in value the more you have").
- **[Arcane]** Crit is comparatively low-value for arcane specifically because Arcane Missiles' base crit multiplier is low pre-Arcane-Potency, and its damage-per-cast is spread thin; roughly 1 crit% ≈ 10 SP at ~1000 SP/40% crit — after the Rupture coefficient nerf (1.0→0.9), crit became even less valuable relative to raw SP on missile damage — [[d:mage#1428155109044523091]] (Shared, 2025-10-16), [[d:mage#1428154828420546692]] (Lightemgee, 2025-10-16).
- **[Fire]** Crit is much more valuable for fire (~25 SP per 1% crit) due to Ignite/crit synergy; fire mages should build ~40 crit — [[d:mage#1434207386968785018]] (Snipe, 2025-11-01), [[d:mage#1428164655452262464]] (Lightemgee, 2025-10-16).
- **[General]** Intellect gives bonus mana and a small amount of crit (56:1 int-to-crit ratio) but does NOT give spellpower directly (aside from a minor arcane-talent conversion); it's a secondary stat once mana isn't an issue — [[d:mage#1432007732663287899]] (Izzo, 2025-10-26), [[d:mage#1433284538133385326]] (Aest, 2025-10-30), [[d:mage#1429246473077522452]] (Lightemgee, 2025-10-19, "Int doesn't give spellpower").
- **[General]** The 5-second mana-regen rule (no spirit-based mana regen for 5s after a hostile cast) DOES apply on Turtle, so pure spirit gives little in-combat value unless you also stack %-mana-regen-while-casting (talents/gear); this is why arcane's alternate-tier pieces carry heavy spirit — [[d:mage#1427390424439586907]] (PBnJames, 2025-10-14), [[d:mage#1427391889421631618]] (PBnJames, 2025-10-14).
- **[Arcane mana regen]** Arcane gets 20% mana-regen-while-casting from talents + 30% from Mage Armor baseline; Tier 2 Arcane's 3-piece bonus raises Mage Armor's regen further. Tested actual values: no Mage Armor = 14% mana-regen-while-casting, regular Mage Armor = 30%, T2-3pc Mage Armor = 37-38% (community had assumed 60-65%, disputed and corrected downward) — [[d:mage#1438912530411163748]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-14, direct test numbers), [[d:mage#1438907872539967681]] (Luden TA, 2025-11-14, "45 percent from tier2 3set... I was misremembering"). The server hard-caps total mana-regen-while-casting at 100% (Pepopo posted the relevant server code) — [[d:mage#1438238977558577287]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-12).
- **[Arcane haste caps]** With Accelerated Arcana + the T3/T2 arcane belt, roughly 16% combined gear/consumable haste is needed to reach the Arcane Missiles tick floor (see Rotation section); Arcane Rupture's own cast time hits its 1.5s GCD floor at roughly 66.6% combined haste, beyond which more haste stops helping Rupture — [[d:mage#1434731485113483274]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-03, math: 1.33×1.3×x=2 → x≈16), [[d:mage#1436147009815052299]] (Pepopo, 2025-11-07).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Power lets you fully cast roughly 2 Arcane Missiles volleys before it expires, plus a possible Temporal Convergence-procced free Rupture; it carries a real risk of oom-death for undergeared players — recommended to pop only at full mana, avoid using Surge/PoM during it, and hold off training the Arcane Power talent point until level 50+ while leveling — [[d:mage#1424365348836212796]] (Akanamu, 2025-10-05), [[d:mage#1426874118862540870]] (Arle, 2025-10-12), [[d:mage#1425887461287923763]] (Dontlookatme, 2025-10-09), [[d:mage#1439011377133129900]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-11-15, "Pop it at full mana only and you got no problems").
- **[Arcane threat]** Multiple threat-reduction sources (talents/Salvation/gear) stack MULTIPLICATIVELY, not additively: e.g. 40% arcane talent reduction × 25% Salvation × 3% cloak = 1-(0.6×0.75×0.97) ≈ 57% effective reduction, not the naive 68% additive sum — this makes small standalone % threat-reduction items nearly worthless once large multipliers already apply — [[d:mage#1427355221683601468]] (alyflex, 2025-10-13; question), [[d:mage#1427356537147363509]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-13, full math), [[d:mage#1427357099175710811]] (alyflex, 2025-10-13).
- **[Utility]** The community repeatedly petitions (as an ongoing joke/campaign) for a "threat wand" to help mage tanking; unresolved throughout this entire chunk — [[d:mage#1423623439725367447]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-10-03), [[d:mage#1423630396398108722]] (Shared, 2025-10-03, "John Turtle told me 50% threat wand on the new 20man" — single source, never confirmed live in-chunk).
- **[Human racial]** Perception (human racial) now shares the global cooldown, so the old "macro Perception + Arcane Power together" trick no longer works — [[d:mage#1428203316231536680]] (Snipe, 2025-10-16; question), [[d:mage#1435318769214820452]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-04, "Perception is currently on the gcd").
- **[Addon/utility]** Nampower's spell-queueing setting measurably increases DPS by hiding latency and preventing accidental Arcane Missiles-clipping on back-to-back casts; the "DoubleCastToEndChannelEarly" advanced setting lets you double-tap Surge/Rupture during a Missiles channel to cleanly interrupt and cast immediately, replacing the need for stopcast macros — [[d:mage#1425224352827314196]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-08), [[d:mage#1425228792883974224]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-08), [[d:mage#1425147765234864289]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-07), [[d:mage#1429150174311219391]] (Shared, 2025-10-18).
- **[Frost utility]** Freezing an otherwise freeze-immune raid boss with Icicles resets Icicles' own cooldown, letting you re-cast it up to 5x faster once — this is frost's burst-damage payoff on freeze-immune targets instead of the CC — [[d:mage#1430291530333028524]] (Lightemgee, 2025-10-22), [[d:mage#1430291707630719087]] (Lightemgee, 2025-10-22).
- **[Frost utility]** Flash Freeze only procs when the target is freeze-immune; without the Frostbite talent it can only proc off Frost Nova, whereas Frostbite lets it also proc off Frostbolt/Cone of Cold; best way to farm procs in raid is spamming Frost Nova/CoC on AoE or fishing with Rank-1 Blizzard on trash — [[d:mage#1430636403699617793]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-22), [[d:mage#1430636501141553164]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-22), [[d:mage#1430637056815796386]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-22).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Mage Tank, niche]** There is no formal "mage tank" role/queue; it's a player-driven niche mostly done in 5-mans (especially Black Morass) and never on raid bosses — [[d:mage#1432345887195140107]] (Astraeya, 2025-10-27, "There is no mage tank" re: raids), [[d:mage#1434373911037874381]] (Snipe, 2025-11-02).
- **[Mage Tank build]** ZeroRanger's approach: started with a level-10-PvP-rank set early on, prioritizes raw stamina above all else if unsure, does NOT run Vampirism gear (relies on the group healer instead), uses Lesser Invisibility Potions (LIP) and Fire/Frost Protection Potions to survive burst, and claims ~21.5% avoidance (natural 5% miss chance + dodge) as a rough analog to a warrior's armor mitigation; a fully consumed/buffed mage tank reaches "a bit over 10k HP" — [[d:mage#1436832251165020293]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-11-09), [[d:mage#1436832388989714604]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-11-09), [[d:mage#1433623162997968997]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-10-31), [[d:mage#1433792746061303912]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-10-31), [[d:mage#1434374123764580603]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-11-02, "No Vampirism that's what the healer in the group is for").
- **[Mage Tank rotation]** Suggested single-target boss-pull opener for mage-tanking: Fireball to pull, then Fire Blast → Counterspell → Frost Nova → Flash Freeze'd Icicles — [[d:mage#1436829216975884409]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-11-09), [[d:mage#1436829277541892186]] (ZeroRanger, 2025-11-09).

## Healing specifics
- **[General]** Mage has no dedicated healing spell at any level; defensive kit is limited to shields (Ice Barrier, Mana Shield, Frost/Fire Ward) and Frost Nova/Blink for kiting; new players are advised to buy cheap AH health potions or take Alchemy/Herbalism and to rely on First Aid bandages rather than expecting self-healing — [[d:mage#1431491476835139594]] (Dishonor, 2025-10-25), [[d:mage#1431491677612146789]] (Dishonor, 2025-10-25), [[d:mage#1431648186744700978]] (Arle, 2025-10-25, recommends training First Aid).

## PvP
- **[General]** Mage PvP is described as "rock-paper-scissors": mages dominate melee via kiting/CC/burst but get farmed by most ranged classes/hunters; Frost is the most consistently-performing PvP spec overall, while Fire/Arcane can occasionally land a big burst one-shot but are riskier and squishier — [[d:mage#1440109079191355525]] (andre, 2025-11-18). Arcane specifically is summarized as "bursty but super squishy" — [[d:mage#1439706644022824960]] (Akanamu, 2025-11-17).
- **[Frost/Icicles]** Icicles (and Arcane Missiles) are 30-yard channels that, once locked onto a target, can continue hitting through walls/floors/terrain if line-of-sight was established at cast time — a long-standing, multiply-corroborated PvP complaint — [[d:mage#1430671883379937393]] (Zestitcha, 2025-10-23), [[d:mage#1430672015546908713]] (Akanamu, 2025-10-23, "has this not always been a tech in vanilla with arcane missiles and all channelled spells?"), [[d:mage#1435856743098875954]] (Izzo, 2025-11-06, "missiles were always 30 yard").
- **[Fire PvP]** Fire is considered weak in PvP: no +100% crit damage talent (unlike other schools' burst tools), Fireblast's 6s CD hits softer than a shaman's Fire Shock or boomkin's Moonfire, with the ~10% stun chance being its main redeeming trait — [[d:mage#1439285653048266803]] (Kao, 2025-11-15).
- **[Frost/Fire hybrid PvP]** A fire/frost hybrid trades pure damage for survivability/utility, but the two schools' key PvP talents compete for the same points — you cannot take Ice Shard/Shatter (frost) alongside Blast Wave + Ice Block (fire) simultaneously — [[d:mage#1439286554035224778]] (Kao, 2025-11-15), [[d:mage#1439286608523431956]] (Kao, 2025-11-15).
- **[Frost vs Feral Druid]** Mage generally loses 1v1 to a feral druid due to low mage HP and the post-shift stun a feral gets after trinketing out of Polymorph; realistic play is to trinket and create distance, and even then the outcome often comes down to crit RNG — [[d:mage#1435385304252022965]] (A Wro, 2025-11-05), [[d:mage#1435390397903016119]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-05), [[d:mage#1435390604174557195]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-05).
- **[Frost vs Paladin]** Polymorph still lands on a paladin who has already used Blessing/Hand of Freedom, since Freedom doesn't grant Polymorph immunity, only movement-effect immunity; Improved Hand of Freedom has a 15s duration/24s cooldown, so the counterplay is to poly through that window and burst right after it ends — [[d:mage#1435125334109454426]] (Afraido, 2025-11-04), [[d:mage#1435317013924020464]] (Izzo, 2025-11-04).
- **[Race choice]** Troll is called the best overall mage race for the on-demand ~10% haste racial (Berserking); Orc is also strong, with an on-demand spellpower racial that scales with level (corrected from an earlier "120 SP" guess to a smaller per-level value by Boomwick); Gnome is the best Alliance-only pick via its Intellect racial (only ~0.5-0.75% extra crit at level 60, described as minor); Human's Perception racial is undermined now that Perception shares the GCD; Dwarf's Stoneform is called situationally weak for a class that rarely dies to poison/bleed; High Elf is worth considering specifically for mana issues — [[d:mage#1435320211040374794]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-04), [[d:mage#1435366671635320953]] (Boomwick, 2025-11-05, corrects the Orc racial value), [[d:mage#1435318769214820452]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-04), [[d:mage#1435319188573913160]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-04), [[d:mage#1435319453930885201]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-04).
- **[General]** Free Action Potions (FAPs) have high demand/price specifically because warriors and others use them to counter mage crowd control in PvP — [[d:mage#1435053691676790784]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-04), [[d:mage#1435058624438337536]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-04).
- **[General]** Suggested PvP target/CC priority: kill/CC a pure spellcaster opponent's main damage source then finish them; against non-casters, kite indefinitely; a warlock with Felhunter out loses all defensive options if a second player joins the fight against them — [[d:mage#1435373606396887183]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-05), [[d:mage#1435373701355798719]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-05), [[d:mage#1435373821669408860]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-05).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Core tool]** Nampower (client-side mod) plus the separate "Nampower Settings" addon is the community-standard tool for arcane/spellcasting: enables spell queueing (reduces latency loss, prevents accidental Missile-clipping) and exposes settings like "DoubleCastToEndChannelEarly" for cleanly interrupting Missiles to cast Surge/Rupture — [[d:mage#1425224352827314196]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-08), [[d:mage#1425228792883974224]] (Luden TA, 2025-10-08), [[d:mage#1425147765234864289]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-07). Its debug log exposes the true server-calculated channel duration in milliseconds (e.g. `Channel start: Arcane Missiles duration 5640`), useful for verifying real haste bypassing client rounding — [[d:mage#1427648060527218769]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-14). CAUTION: at least one third-party "nampower" download link was flagged by VirusTotal with 22 detections for containing a trojan — verify the source/official fork before installing — [[d:mage#1427700145075650692]] (Zero, 2025-10-14).
- **[Macros]** SuperWoW + Roid Macros/Cleveroids are required for advanced macro scripting (conditional casts, mouseover) since vanilla client macros are limited on their own — [[d:mage#1424409058131185695]] (Brad, 2025-10-05). `/run SpellStopCasting()` works as a stopcast replacement without SuperWoW — [[d:mage#1432536084197146817]] (Wizzer, 2025-10-28). Mouseover decurse via pfUI: `/pfcast Remove Lesser Curse` — [[d:mage#1425245576164937728]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-08); the Rinse addon auto-targets for you if that's sufficient — [[d:mage#1425239966459629629]] (Pepopo, 2025-10-08).

- **[Addons]** Proc-tracking addons for mage: ProcDoc (removed from its original GitHub, community re-hosts zips), ArcaneSurgeWarning (`github.com/AllinGaming/ArcaneSurgeWarning`), Mpowa (configurable for TBoS/Ancient Accord/Surge/Rupture tracking), MSBT (resist alerts + low-mana sound), DebuffFilter (`github.com/vashin1/DebuffFilter`, filters Arcane Power/Temporal Convergence and similar buffs from nameplates) — [[d:mage#1437016429294780517]] (Eubo, 2025-11-09), [[d:mage#1437079258232262757]] (iRebelzZz, 2025-11-09), [[d:mage#1437170301510221824]] (Eubo, 2025-11-10).
