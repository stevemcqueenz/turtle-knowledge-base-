## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Community consensus is mage got almost no attention in 1.18.1: "sum total of class balance mage will receive... for the next class balance patch you will have to wait around a year" and "Mages get one to two sentences every major update" — [[d:mage#1480990838015135939]] (Blem, 2026-03-10), [[d:mage#1480942763909583051]] (Promey, 2026-03-10), confirmed post-release: "Nothing for mage is changing... Frost is getting better damage, that's all" [[d:mage#1484356989961240717]] (Lightemgee, 2026-03-20).
- **[Fire]** Fireblast reworked/buffed with lower cooldown — becomes "the highest dps spell you can press" for every mage spec, forcing a rotation change (weave Fireblast every ~2 Fireballs instead of using it only as a mobility/finisher tool) — [[d:mage#1486320286940004443]] (Akanamu, 2026-03-25), [[d:mage#1487203134898966600]] (Luden TA, 2026-03-28), player reaction "the fire blast change felt very nice" [[d:mage#1484888194536308876]] (Leery, Pibble Disciple, 2026-03-21).
- **[Frost]** Ice Barrier gains a separate +10% damage buff while the barrier holds — deemed a minor, "not enough" QoL fix that doesn't address frost's raid identity; buff is lost as soon as the barrier is depleted, and fire mostly absorbs the incoming damage via preemptive Fire Ward anyway — [[d:mage#1484357049490997268]] (Lightemgee, 2026-03-20), [[d:mage#1484696403149193316]] (Luux, bug: buff seemed to disappear as soon as shield popped, 2026-03-21).
- **[Arcane]** T3.5 3-piece "Mirror Magic" set bonus tooltip clarified: initial hits from Flamestrike/CoC/Frost Nova/Blast Wave/Arcane Explosion can "rewind" for +10% dmg on Arcane spells vs +20% on Frost/Fire — Arcane's rewind was specifically nerfed from 20%→10% in a recent patch while Frost/Fire kept 20% — [[d:mage#1481363007782129816]] (Pepopo, 2026-03-11), [[d:mage#1481380073650262237]] (Dontlookatme, confirms it changed "the most recent patch notes", 2026-03-11).
- **[Arcane]** Scythe of Elune's "Elune's Wrath" proc had its internal cooldown restored/enforced (~15s per Akanamu); previously Arcane mages could refresh/overwrite the Scythe debuff constantly via high proc-rate Arcane spells, making it near-permanent uptime — now nerfed, a "considerable power loss for arcane" — [[d:mage#1484542176338772069]] (alyflex asks, 2026-03-21), [[d:mage#1487149928877789285]] (Akanamu confirms 15s ICD + "before it would basically be up super often", 2026-03-27), [[d:mage#1488090233068322956]] (Aest, "the scythe arcane cd bugfix ends up being a considerable power loss for arcane", 2026-03-30).
- **[General]** New "Fortune" stat introduced (increases proc chance of on-hit/on-cast effects) but most notable proc trinkets/items (Scythe, Sphere of the Endless Gulch, Bindings) were simultaneously given internal cooldowns, capping how much Fortune actually helps — "ICD just puts an upper bound on the uptime you can get; more fortune will still push you closer to that upper bound" — [[d:mage#1484544659697959032]] (Akanamu, 2026-03-21). Best fortune breakpoint judged to be ~3-4 equipped proc-chance items before it's worth a dedicated fortune trinket/cloak — [[d:mage#1488702935037902950]] (Varnoc, 2026-04-01).
- **[General]** PvP gear/points system fully overhauled this patch; prior rank 13/14 grinders feel shortchanged vs. new players who can buy equivalent new-system gear cheaply — [[d:mage#1487460403125424229]] (Gees, 2026-03-28).
- **[General]** Weapon-swapping now costs a GCD (confirmed mechanic post-patch), killing the old "swap to OBD/proc weapon and swap back" min-max trick except right after a global you'd spend anyway — [[d:mage#1489371212650909758]] (limbless, 2026-04-03).

## Leveling
- **[General]** All three specs are viable for leveling; Frost remains the default "safe" recommendation, especially for Hardcore (HC) players, due to control/survivability (Frost Nova, slows) — [[d:mage#1481177979039252520]] (sosqua9-, 2026-03-11), [[d:mage#1484982906039570532]] (Valaen, "for HC frost will be the safest pick", 2026-03-21), [[d:mage#1490985845094613123]] (sosqua9-, warns against taking/using Arcane Power while leveling HC — "it will kill u if u cast it when on low man", 2026-04-07).
- **[Frost AoE]** Blizzard-based AoE farming was nerfed this era (Improved Blizzard slow reduced; talent calc shows 20/30/40% but in-game shows different numbers with Permafrost baked in) and is largely abandoned; current AoE-farm method is Cone of Cold (10-yd) kiting in circles + Frost Nova + finish with rank-1 Arcane Explosion — [[d:mage#1483257969180278916]] (VILLAIN, 2026-03-17), [[d:mage#1489660017299689473]] (Gees, "Blizzard slow is nerfed hard... aoe farm tactic is therefore focused on coc/nova/flamestrike kiting", 2026-04-03), worked example video/walkthrough — [[d:mage#1491249152842010624]] (Aridhil, 2026-04-08), [[d:mage#1491253713472589894]] (Aridhil, example video link, 2026-04-08).
- **[Arcane]** Viable from level 1 (some level it from the start), but mana-hungry early; real damage breakpoint is level 24-26 when Arcane Missiles rank 3 unlocks (280 total dmg vs. rank 2's 144) — [[d:mage#1483088830335549621]] (Sellma, 2026-03-16), [[d:mage#1484552827874578533]] (Valaen, exact numbers, 2026-03-21), [[d:mage#1483096095390302392]] (ClubbingSealCub, "26 is when you get a new rank of arcane missiles and your dmg gets really good", 2026-03-16).
- **[Fire]** Gets higher Fireball ranks 2 levels earlier than Frost gets Frostbolt upgrades, plus longer range; considered fun but weaker than frost/arcane early — [[d:mage#1481215928447537232]] (Bigmagik, 2026-03-11).
- **[General]** Basic early rotation: Frostbolt (x1-2) to slow, Fireblast as it closes, wand to finish; low-level wands (esp. "Greater Magic Wand" from AH) hit far above their level requirement, a known quirk not a bug — [[d:mage#1483131710269952153]] (Kolima, 2026-03-16), [[d:mage#1483131945121480926]] (Kolima, "they should've been required lvl 14 and 23 respectively with its dmg output", 2026-03-16).
- **[Frost]** Frostbolt cast time scales by rank: 1.5→1.8→2.2→2.6s, capping at 3.0s base for any rank above 5 — confirmed as intended, unchanged mechanic, not a recent nerf (a returning player thought otherwise) — [[d:mage#1485075272922435726]] (biloen, 2026-03-22), [[d:mage#1485056879594962944]] (Aest, 2026-03-22).
- **[AoE spots]** ZG crocs given a "Charge" ability making them immune to slow/root, killing the old croc AoE-farm spot specifically for mages — [[d:mage#1489770174784737430]] (Lightemgee, 2026-04-04); Sunken Temple (ZF) AoE farming still works via CoC-kite but the old "jump back and forth" safe-spot exploit no longer works there — [[d:mage#1489776668569501786]] (Leery, Pibble Disciple, 2026-04-04); Raven Hill Cemetery ghouls used for AoE farming but killing them spawns "worms" adds, a caveat for that spot — [[d:mage#1491439861532459028]] (Starfox, 2026-04-08).

- **[Arcane]** Loop = maintain Arcane Rupture debuff while spamming Arcane Missiles, weave Arcane Surge as filler/finisher (subject to the haste-threshold rule above), burn Arcane Power windows, and cancel Missiles early with nampower's double-tap-cancel or a `/stopcasting` macro to immediately fire Rupture/Surge — verbatim priority list under Talent builds above; macro example: `/script ChannelStopCastingNextTick(); /cast Arcane Rupture` — [[d:mage#1486008678262771782]] (Insta, 2026-03-24).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Arcane]** Hit cap 16%; talents give 10%; need 6% from gear/buffs, or as little as 3% from gear with ES/druid buff (+1%) plus ZG head+leg enchants (+2%) — [[d:mage#1484992783017312256]] (Beta, exact math, 2026-03-21), [[d:mage#1480164287745101894]] (Sellma, earlier confirmation, 2026-03-08).
- **[Fire/Frost]** Hit cap 16%; talents give 6%; typically need ~7-9% from gear (e.g. "6% talents + 2% zg + 1% druid buff = 9%, so need 7% more from gear") — [[d:mage#1484935527709806603]] (Beta, 2026-03-21).
- **[Stat weights]** Arcane: 1% Crit ≈ 10 SP (wiki/arcane FAQ) — [[d:mage#1479596305633513554]] (PBnJames, 2026-03-07). Fire: 1% Crit ≈ 25 SP at typical BiS levels, scales with existing gear — [[d:mage#1479598294987440392]] (Jûun Amelie, 2026-03-07), [[d:mage#1493652965158944868]] (Aest, "at current kara bis it's slightly above 25", 2026-04-14). Late-game rough rule: 6% haste is the single biggest DPS breakpoint for Arcane; beyond it, roughly 1% crit or haste ≈ 14 SP — [[d:mage#1491470885104910406]] (Beta, 2026-04-08).
- **[Arcane]** Missiles channel softcap breakpoints: 2.82s channel with T2/T3 belt bonus, or 2.35s with other belts — further haste stops adding extra ticks within a channel past that point (Accelerated Arcana talent reduces base cast time, not haste, hence 2.82/2.35 rather than 3/2.5s) — [[d:mage#1484613083992162345]] (Mynie, 2026-03-24), [[d:mage#1484613633404305640]] (Mynie, clarifies talent vs. haste, 2026-03-24).
- **[Mechanics]** Haste is multiplicative per source (talents × gear pieces individually × trinket procs × Arcane Power × set bonus), not additive — worked example: TBoS 1.05 × Whip 1.03 × talents 1.05 × ~8 gear pieces ~1.01 each × AP 1.30 × 5pc T3 1.05 ≈ 167% cast speed (≈60% of base cast time) — [[d:mage#1489407016483946638]] (Izzo, 2026-04-03), confirmed "every piece is multiplicated individually. It's really unintuitive" — [[d:mage#1489538696377008290]] (Izzo, 2026-04-03).
- **[Mechanics]** Diminishing returns explanation: crit suffers DR near 100% (a flat +10% crit at 50% crit only nets +6.66% relative increase), while haste compounds multiplicatively without that DR — so heavy haste stacking increases the relative value of SP/crit over further haste — [[d:mage#1490437173990785265]] (Ocra, 2026-04-05).
- **[Ignite]** Debuff has a visible stack cap (48) that can make addons show "no gain" on a cast that landed; nampower + BigWigs "MageTools V2" tracks past the visible cap — combat-log proof given on Loatheb — [[d:mage#1484285532308771007]] (Pepopo, 2026-03-20), [[d:mage#1484297069148770464]] (Pepopo, verification steps, 2026-03-20).
- **[Arcane]** Gear-comparison example (T2 5-piece vs. best single items): losing the 5-piece costs ~33 SP-equivalent of raw stats but the set bonus's haste-on-crit (~5% haste, ~30% uptime) + extra Missile tick + mana regen make the 5-piece the better overall choice — [[d:mage#1484514817438908476]] (Zatyg, worked math, 2026-03-21), confirmed conclusion — [[d:mage#1484515007650730175]] (Zatyg, 2026-03-21).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Arcane Power]** Verbatim tooltip quoted: "Instant, 3 min cooldown. When activated, your spells deal 30% more damage while costing 30% more mana to cast. This effect lasts 15 sec." Community wants a stronger/older version back; considered too mana-hungry to use safely below ~4500-5000 mana pool, so it's effectively unused until endgame — [[d:mage#1487513454637219900]] (Dan1tello, tooltip, +4, 2026-03-28), [[d:mage#1491890195384242276]] (Beta, mana-pool guidance, 2026-04-09), [[d:mage#1491893029655810078]] (PBnJames, proposes a ranked/scaling version so it's usable pre-60, 2026-04-10).
- **[Arcane]** Combining AP + MQG: recommendation is to pop AP right at the end of MQG's duration (not simultaneously) for a shorter effective AP cooldown and possible second use later; players with low base haste (~7% from gear) sometimes just pop both together and it "feels good" — [[d:mage#1484204264791216199]] (Luden TA, 2026-03-19), [[d:mage#1484207112706986094]] (Akanamu confirms, 2026-03-19), [[d:mage#1484233383541477518]] (Luden TA, low-haste counterexample, 2026-03-19).
- **[Threat tools]** No mage-specific "reduce my own threat" consumable exists; mitigation options are Paladin BoP, Limited Invincibility Potion (LIP, requires level 45), Eye of Divinity ("Eye of Dim") trinket's aggro-reduction on pull, and simply downranking AoE spells — [[d:mage#1488102164122439780]] (sosqua9-, 2026-03-30), [[d:mage#1488124351633952878]] (Aest, "Eye of dim my beloved", 2026-03-30).
- **[Trinket shared CDs]** Using an "active" on-use trinket puts the whole active-trinket pool on a shared CD (usually matching that trinket's own buff duration — most are ~20s, Scythe is 1 minute); a large confirmed list of ~45 shared-CD trinkets was posted (Brutal Hauberk, Six Demon Bag, MQG, TOEP, Zandalarian Hero Medallion/Charm, The Scythe of Elune, etc. — full list in source message); passive/on-proc trinkets like Fetish of the Sandreaver and Eye of Dim do NOT share this pool with active DPS trinkets — [[d:mage#1483841497789894828]] (Ocra, full list, 2026-03-18), [[d:mage#1483829710646542336]] (Leery, Pibble Disciple, confirms fetish/eye of dim exempt, 2026-03-18).
- **[Missiles weave macro]** `/script ChannelStopCastingNextTick(); /cast Arcane Rupture` cuts Missiles short cleanly to fire Rupture/Surge; nampower also has a built-in double-tap-to-cancel setting; timing matters — cancel right after a missile tick lands — [[d:mage#1486008678262771782]] (Insta, 2026-03-24), [[d:mage#1486010713355849778]] (Insta, timing note, 2026-03-24).
- **[Utility]** Detect Magic used specifically to check Vulnerabilities on BWL dragons and identify spells cast by AQ40 giants — [[d:mage#1488164422840221836]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-30).
- **[Mechanics]** Blink does NOT break Naxx-style web-wrap/shackle debuffs (teleport ≠ the movement type that breaks it) — tested live by jc473; contrary to a common belief among mages who cited it as an "excuse" when they broke shackle a different way — [[d:mage#1494656562172526847]] (jc473, 2026-04-17), also see Bugs & quirks for Blink being broken in the new BWL boss room.

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Kara40, Anomalus]** Arcane-immune; mages typically respec Frost/Fire for the fight rather than doing near-zero damage as Arcane — [[d:mage#1484538774741586031]] (Sellma, 2026-03-21), [[d:mage#1484539756682547423]] (Akanamu, "i prefer to arcane missiles anomalus... show him whos boss" — some do it anyway despite low dmg, 2026-03-21).
- **[Kara40, trash/gear check]** Requires 200 Arcane Resistance (AR) unbuffed for many guilds; a "Cosmic" tailoring gear-planner set + Enchanting Cloak (+15 AR) + Kara head/leg enchants (+25/+25 AR) + AD-rep shoulder enchant (+5 AR) hits exactly +200 AR — [[d:mage#1482669809807396974]] (Sellma, full build + links, 2026-03-14).
- **[Kara40, KT event]** Whoever enters the arena first triggers a "teleport the raid into the green circle" mechanic for anyone standing outside; a secret rod/lever exists in a side room but is skipped by just entering; no loot-affecting hard mode exists, a toggle only makes phase 1 faster — [[d:mage#1483241280824803430]] (biloen, 2026-03-17), [[d:mage#1483245474050736218]] (Pepopo, on hard mode, 2026-03-17).
- **[Kara40, Chess event]** Fetish of the Sandreaver synergizes with high-TPS tanks for arcane AoE trash; Ancient Accord's proc timing is bad on Chess due to forced downtime — pre-proccing it before the event start lines up better — [[d:mage#1484175858213326868]] (Akanamu, 2026-03-19).
- **[Naxx, Thaddius]** Long-CD proc trinkets (Gulch) are a bad pick due to forced downtime around the polarity-swap transition — two failed procs cited around the transition in a guild's log — [[d:mage#1483950782603726898]] (Bigmagik, 2026-03-19), [[d:mage#1483954011119026228]] (Akanamu, "not rlly a good trinket to use on thaddius", 2026-03-19).
- **[Naxx, Loatheb]** Apparent "missing" ignite ticks in logs are usually the visible-stack-cap display quirk (see Stats section), not a real debuff cap bug; genuine drops are more often partial-resists or non-crit Fireballs — [[d:mage#1484282237393440778]] (Luden TA, log analysis, 2026-03-19).
- **[Naxx, Sapphiron]** Fully immune to frost damage despite only 15 base Frost Resist (not merely "highly resistant" as in later WotLK — a WotLK wiki quote was mistakenly cited) — [[d:mage#1494089881943543971]] (30, correction, 2026-04-16).
- **[BWL general]** Vael and Nefarian 100% fire immune; other big drakes (Firemaw, Ebonroc, Flamegor) effectively immune too — "only 3/8 boss fights work for Fire" — [[d:mage#1493999508734873651]] (jc473, 2026-04-15); Firemaw specifically recommends mages wear Fire Resist gear even as non-fire casters, at some DPS cost — [[d:mage#1493999900881453177]] (jc473, 2026-04-15); Chromaggus is not truly fire-immune (only ~15 res), its real gimmick is a magic-damage-reduction buff with random elemental vulnerabilities to track — [[d:mage#1493999774410608701]] (biloen, 2026-04-15).
- **[BWL, Nefarian]** 2-phase fight relevant for High Elf's mana-regen racial timing (one of the only fights biloen uses it as Arcane) — [[d:mage#1494293964608638978]] (biloen, 2026-04-16).
- **[Shackle-break mechanic (custom fight)]** Ice Block clears the pre-cast shackle-break debuff if used while the ability is being cast (same as Rogue Vanish); using it after the debuff is applied does not help; Blink itself does not break the debuff, though one player's Blink-out "worked" because it was pre-cast — [[d:mage#1494656562172526847]] (jc473, 2026-04-17), [[d:mage#1494660928053051462]] (Promey, Vanish/Maexxna-web-wrap analogy, 2026-04-17).
- **[Techie packs]** Poison ticks stack fast and should be cleansed; use plenty of room, assign a dedicated interrupter per Spellbinder-type add, and don't let melee engage Spellbinders early — [[d:mage#1494002099879415969]] (limbless, 2026-04-15), [[d:mage#1494004230653214802]] (Luden TA, kiting spellbinders for controlled interrupts, 2026-04-15).
- **[General progression]** Fresh-60 path: MC/ZG first, then attunements for Onyxia/Emerald Sanctum(ES)/BWL, Black Morass rep grind for early gear + 20-slot bag, Lower/Upper Karazhan (K10/K40) and ZG for portal-unlock tomes (Theramore portal drops from K10 mobs) — [[d:mage#1490969006855688223]] (Shared, 2026-04-01), [[d:mage#1481691617810452522]] (biloen, raid-tier overview for a brand new player, 2026-03-12).
- **[Emerald Sanctum HM]** Manageable by simply bringing more healers, per some ("just bring a *real* number of healers") — [[d:mage#1493997047924260985]] (limbless, 2026-04-15); ES Hard Mode neck reward, initially mis-thought to give melee hit, confirmed to actually be Spell Hit as well — [[d:mage#1487107403198632047]] (Akanamu, correction, 2026-03-27).
