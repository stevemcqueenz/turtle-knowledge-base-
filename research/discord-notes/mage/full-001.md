## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Arcane]** Broad consensus Arcane is the strongest overall PvE spec across tiers, slightly ahead of Fire, and always best for AoE; gets 10% free hit from its own talent vs 6% for Frost/Fire, giving far more gearing leeway — [[d:mage#1443246253180981309]], [[d:mage#1454588431886848223]], [[d:mage#1443246787186921494]] (+several reactions across thread).
- **[Fire]** Fire can match/exceed Arcane on specific fights (confirmed strong on Loatheb) if run with 2+ fire mages to keep Ignite rolling, but is gear-dependent, weak solo/undergeared, and functionally invalidated in Ony/MC/BWL (~90% fire immune/resistant) and much of BRD/UBRS — [[d:mage#1444812675610710076]], [[d:mage#1444812933698814095]], [[d:mage#1452408736839634994]], [[d:mage#1453078085619679375]] ("bringing fire into MC is straight up trolling").
- **[Frost]** Weakest at endgame single-target due to Flash Freeze/Icicles RNG, but has the best defensive kit (Ice Barrier) and is fine for leveling/dungeons/PvP; "not unplayable," a good frost player can beat a mediocre arcane one — [[d:mage#1448375436475105502]], [[d:mage#1443247840188174558]], [[d:mage#1443250739030786168]]. Frost also does extra damage to many Frost-weak MC trash and can chain Nova/CoC/Icicles for big trash burst — [[d:mage#1448401911437988012]].
- **[Fire]** Fire viable content (pre-Naxx): ZG, Kara (10/40 selectively), AQ20/40 (though AQ40 "isn't the most fire-friendly raid"); dragons in BWL mostly fire-immune except Broodlord/Chromaggus — [[d:mage#1450520418581483572]], [[d:mage#1450478366330458194]], [[d:mage#1453290864183869510]].
- **[Frost, PvP]** Frost nerfed for PvP (Shatter nerf, can no longer proc Icicles off FAP/Freedom-immune targets); one player argues deep Arcane + Iceblock is now better than Frost for 60 PvP — single source: [[d:mage#1446952036661723289]], [[d:mage#1446952283760885957]].
- **[General]** Community view that "casters have dogshit scaling compared to melee," with Survival Hunter and Assassination Rogue seen as far above all mage specs at the top end (single source, +reactions) — [[d:mage#1443281033876471931]], [[d:mage#1443281124485759111]], [[d:mage#1443261559253237931]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane considered close to overtuned/"needs a small tune-down" by some (fear of clunky nerfs), disputed by Akanamu who "doesn't understand why people always call for arcane to be tuned down" — disagreement recorded: [[d:mage#1443253199036219574]] vs [[d:mage#1443279299493757110]].

## Leveling
- **[General]** Frost is the easiest/safest leveling spec (one main spell, quick-CD CC, indefinite kiting) but slow; Arcane is squishier early and only becomes strong once mid-tree Arcane talents (Instability/Cascade-type) are online around level ~20; recommend staying Frost until ~30-40 unless doing full AoE farming — [[d:mage#1453911881256538223]], [[d:mage#1453911746040565926]], [[d:mage#1453915381151436801]] (andre: dying too much → Frost ST, killing too slow → Fire, want AoE clear → Frost AoE).
- **[AoE leveling]** Blizzard has been significantly nerfed for slow/kiting on Turtle and can no longer sustain AoE-kite farming; leveling AoE now uses Cone of Cold + Arcane Explosion instead — [[d:mage#1444122351988899923]], [[d:mage#1454615166913089587]], [[d:mage#1457060687959560325]] (confirmed multiple times, +reactions).
- **[AoE leveling]** Cone of Cold learned around level 22-24 per one player's memory; before that, AoE leveling "sucks ass... until you get to the mid 40s" — [[d:mage#1455951139588804671]], [[d:mage#1455970380178133042]], [[d:mage#1455966023823130825]] (mobs "close the distance too fast" with Blizzard alone).
- **[AoE leveling]** Recommended AoE-farm talents: max Elemental Precision (hit) + CoC-duration talents, and pick up Amplify Magic to help pull packs — [[d:mage#1443288078230884543]] (Akanamu, re: Dire Maul lashers).
- **[Gold/leveling]** DME (Dire Maul East) "lasher farm," pulled pack-by-pack, is the dominant leveling/gold farm from roughly level 54 through 60+; attempting it as low as level 45 is risky (limited space, elite pulls) — [[d:mage#1447768105819439347]], [[d:mage#1456417925119606899]] (Zatyg).
- **[ZG/ZF]** Zul'Gurub and Zul'Farrak AoE-farm routes from classic no longer work: ZF graveyard solo farm and ZG's old axe-thrower/panther pulls are patched (axe throwers now have a shredding ranged ability, crocs immune to slow/root and charge-stun); pull sizes dropped from ~80 mobs to ~20 at best — [[d:mage#1441954445670744277]], [[d:mage#1441954813465067743]], [[d:mage#1455759684509372688]] (mob pathing/kiting also generally changed on Turtle vs classic).
- **[Fire leveling]** Fire is strong but crit-dependent, which is scarce at low level; still "fun in rotation" once some gear is acquired — [[d:mage#1456753314074202133]] (Simbuca, +reply).
- **[PvP leveling]** Leveling almost entirely via BGs is viable once past the rough first 5-6 levels of each bracket for a caster — [[d:mage#1446863976481685505]], [[d:mage#1446864371627065469]] (peter_hammer).
- **[Talents]** Chilled is a status effect applied by frost spells, not a trainable spell — clears up trainer-window confusion — [[d:mage#1445919492650700862]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Arcane]** Core priority: maximize Arcane Rupture debuff uptime — cancel an Arcane Missiles channel to reapply Rupture the instant it falls off; otherwise avoid clipping missiles (each clip wastes channel time) — [[d:mage#1450105782422212681]], [[d:mage#1448804571337199667]] (Akanamu/Luden TA, detailed step-by-step).
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Surge usage is haste-dependent: at low haste, press it whenever available (without clipping an about-to-refresh Rupture); once total haste from gear+buffs passes roughly 20-30%, stop pressing it outside of using it as a fight-ending "finisher" (values cited ranged 20%, 25%, and up to 45% depending on rupture-buff state — no single agreed number) — [[d:mage#1445446595352068128]], [[d:mage#1445446288085749830]], [[d:mage#1450105782422212681]], [[d:mage#1450171194333466695]]. Consensus: never Surge or PoM-Rupture during Arcane Power — [[d:mage#1445445751722213489]] (disputed slightly by Pepopo: still a small DPS gain below the haste threshold, [[d:mage#1445446288085749830]]).
- **[Arcane]** Presence of Mind (PoM) is mainly used to instant-cast Arcane Rupture (or fit it inside one GCD); opening with it risks pulling threat, so many hold it — [[d:mage#1460643726241042527]], [[d:mage#1460643847016022066]].
- **[Fire]** FAQ opener still considered roughly accurate: Fire Blast → 4x Scorch → Fire Blast → then (Hot Streak) Pyroblast if available → Fire Blast if available → Fireball; Scorch-extending Ignite is situational/hard to pull off consistently ("only real way to get long ignites is luck or lots of fire mages") — [[d:mage#1442123676437840002]], [[d:mage#1442163106590490808]].
- **[Fire]** For the Fireball→Fireblast→Fireball loop, the 2nd Fireball must land within 4 seconds of the first to refresh Ignite properly; server tick adds delay requiring ~3-4% haste buffer — [[d:mage#1442162744219402351]]. Getting a Fireball+Scorch into a 4s window needs ~13% haste (impractical from normal gearing), plus ~3% more to offset lag — [[d:mage#1442152195066957994]], [[d:mage#1442152674215596134]].
- **[Frost]** ST rotation is Frostbolt spam, weaving in Icicles whenever Flash Freeze procs; favorite burst combo: precast Frostbolt → Cone of Cold → Icicles (if FF procs) → Frost Nova → Icicles, described as "insanely big burst in a 6 second window" — [[d:mage#1448401911437988012]] (Luden TA).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Arcane]** Trash/pack AoE is Arcane Explosion-centric; "uptime and trinket usage" are the two pillars of arcane AoE damage — [[d:mage#1459931392916848844]] (Akanamu).
- **[Arcane]** For AoE farming pre-endgame gear, one suggestion is dropping Fire points into Arcane and using Arcane Explosion instead of a Fire/Frost Flamestrike-CoC combo, since Shatter/Flamestrike synergy was nerfed and inconsistent — [[d:mage#1440420063734927530]], [[d:mage#1440418791459913839]], [[d:mage#1440419949251395765]].
- **[Frost]** Raid AoE: Cone of Cold on cooldown when in range, flipping to rank-1 Blizzard on medium packs of immune mobs purely to refresh Icicles procs on a skull target ("fish on cooldown, not for damage") — [[d:mage#1443219267058139311]], [[d:mage#1443142692707237909]].
- **[General AoE farm]** Community-devised big pulls use a "trash set" mixing crit gear + threat-reduction trinkets (Eye of Diminution, Sphere of the Endless Gulch, Sigil of Ancient Accord swapped in per pack) to squeeze in extra Arcane Explosions without pulling aggro — [[d:mage#1459930226862719016]], [[d:mage#1459930333259501846]] (Akanamu: 1 extra AE from threat headroom ≈ 3-8k extra damage per pull).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[All specs]** Spell hit cap is 16% total for casters vs level-63 raid bosses; Arcane's talent (Arcane Focus-equivalent) gives 6% freeing up only 10% needed from gear, while Fire/Frost's Elemental Precision also gives 6% — [[d:mage#1441463238863618078]], [[d:mage#1442665382656741376]], [[d:mage#1447246590791123069]], [[d:mage#1450432503675355157]]. With a party Druid's 1% hit buff (Emerald Blessing) you can be 1% under the raid hit cap from gear — [[d:mage#1460514317786022029]], [[d:mage#1447258943226253445]].
- **[Leveling/dungeon]** Outside raid-boss content only ~4% total hit is needed to cap against a mob 1 level above you, so chasing 16% is a waste while leveling — [[d:mage#1445414544120742009]], [[d:mage#1445415186075746374]].
- **[Arcane, haste]** Sim data (Pepopo) shows a haste breakpoint around 5% gear haste where the % of Arcane Missiles casts landing under the Rupture buff jumps from ~59% to ~63%, a smaller bump around 9-11% (to ~65-66%); beyond that only relevant if enough haste lets a raid squeeze in a 2nd Arcane Power in a fight — [[d:mage#1445470431342493757]], [[d:mage#1445470500670410802]], [[d:mage#1445471099847573514]].
- **[Arcane, hard cap]** Arcane Missiles is hard-capped at 470ms per missile tick, tied to the game's only known 100% haste cap (0.5 UNIT_MOD_CAST_SPEED); confirmed server-side (not client-only) via a log where Rupture went instant under high haste while Missiles stayed capped at 470ms — [[d:mage#1455648566583234765]], [[d:mage#1455679324152791156]] (Ocra).
- **[Arcane, sim weights]** At K40/Naxx gear: 1 haste (multiplicative) ≈ 16.84 SP, 1 crit ≈ 13.64 SP (Ocra's own character sim) — [[d:mage#1455643319211135210]]. At fresh-60/MC gear on Ambershire: hit ≈ crit ≈ haste ≈ 12 SP (Lyans) — [[d:mage#1450435312131117117]]. Fight-length dependent example (Ocra): 30s fight → crit 13.18 SP / haste 13 SP; 1 min → 13.3 / 14.32; 3 min → 13.09 / 16.27 — [[d:mage#1459917390610694440]].
- **[Arcane]** Rule of thumb: 1 crit ≥ 10 SP for a mage (crit is a multiplier, scales up with more SP) — [[d:mage#1445970866776178871]] (Lightemgee), context/nuance from Akanamu that value depends on gear level and AoE segments — [[d:mage#1445970218533781697]], [[d:mage#1445767225943527511]] (each 1% crit ≈ 1/(1+c)% dmg increase, additive — lower-crit characters get more value per point).
- **[Int/crit/SP conversion]** crit% ≈ 3.70 + INT/(14.77 + 0.65×level); rough shorthand "≈63 Int = 1% crit" from the Mage FAQ — [[d:mage#1452567265689079850]], [[d:mage#1446156797982277804]]. Separate figure: 1 Int ≈ 0.0186 crit ≈ 0.186 SP for single-target, gear-dependent — [[d:mage#1451972269726503053]].
- **[Spell pen]** Spell Penetration is largely a dump stat: the passive Arcane talent gives 10 (corrected from an initial "20" claim) spell pen; most raid bosses have only 15 penetrable resist plus a 24-point unpenetrable "invisible" resist floor from the level-63 gap; Curse of Shadows/Elements (75 pen) plus the talent covers virtually everything except KT's 200 Frost resist in Naxx and AQ20 bosses (115-155 resist); spell pen also cannot bring resist below 0 — [[d:mage#1445191284775059601]], [[d:mage#1450392648216088629]], [[d:mage#1450392717476630539]], [[d:mage#1442563815504871494]].
- **[Consumable fixes]** Haste food is actually 2% not the stale-DB 3% (confirmed by live testing) — [[d:mage#1457742414260469862]]. Potion of Quickness confirmed still 5% not 6% — [[d:mage#1457731688846331925]] (Akanamu/Ocra live-tested both).
- **[Overcharged Belt vs Mana Igniting Cord]** Contested: Lightemgee argues the Overcharged Belt (+1s Arcane Missiles) is strictly better for mana sustain since the extra missile gives more regen time and another proc chance, and the Mana Igniting Cord (crit, no spirit) is worse for that purpose — [[d:mage#1448133948281454733]], [[d:mage#1448134483311071413]], [[d:mage#1448135141485449226]]; sosqua9- counters that Mana Igniting is BiS for AoE farming until AQ40 due to the crit — [[d:mage#1448131741058601042]].

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[Cloak]** Spellwoven Nobility Drape (crafted tailoring) is BiS for AoE and widely recommended, though contested by a couple of players as overpriced/overrated — [[d:mage#1440797087347507350]], [[d:mage#1450289972031655947]], disputed: [[d:mage#1440806322210738278]], [[d:mage#1440807200661442660]].
- **[Gloves]** Gloves of Unwinding Mystery (crafted BoE, tailoring) is BiS gloves until Naxx/K40; some argue Khadgar's Guidance overtakes it once available due to multi-target scaling — [[d:mage#1441409167171653714]], [[d:mage#1442998052628730026]], disputed: [[d:mage#1459701260885295116]] (Ocra), [[d:mage#1459589933340557508]] (sosqua9-: "khadgar wins because most fights are aoe, 2crit>2haste").
- **[Rings]** True Band of Sulfuras (BRD, needs Sulfuron Ingot from MC) and Wrath of Cenarius (Cenarion Circle badge ring) are the two core Arcane rings, both obtainable before BWL; "most highest parsers run both" — [[d:mage#1441065422639202365]], [[d:mage#1441067520319819879]], [[d:mage#1458931700431061044]]. Wrath of Cenarius vs Meph(istroth) ring are near-sidegrades on ST (±0.2-1% dps depending on gear/haste); WoC slightly ahead when it can also proc off Resonance Cascade — [[d:mage#1449205791830704312]], [[d:mage#1449206362369163366]], [[d:mage#1449207838772691118]]. Dragonslayer's Signet preferred for Frost trash, BM rep ring for Arcane bosses (and vice versa for trash per Akanamu) — [[d:mage#1452870741476507689]], [[d:mage#1453036717719421119]].
- **[Trinkets]** Sigil of Ancient Accord (MC) is near-universally rated BiS/near-BiS passive Arcane trinket for both AoE and single target across almost every raid tier through K40, despite a 2s ICD reducing its value somewhat — [[d:mage#1442108135228706847]], [[d:mage#1450544585909473492]], [[d:mage#1454118562821046314]], [[d:mage#1460795636071796910]].
- **[Trinkets, on-use]** TOEP-style burst SP trinkets are best on fights under ~90s-2min; passive haste (e.g. Whip of Encouragement) or flat-SP passives pull ahead on longer fights; extensive math debate on whether to use MQG/AP together or stagger cooldowns — [[d:mage#1450907650261782661]], [[d:mage#1450909300221284462]], [[d:mage#1450912532507394190]].
- **[Belt]** Memory of the Last Guardian (T3, +1s Arcane Missiles) is valued for the mana efficiency and haste-snapshot window it opens, vs keeping the T3 belt set bonus — [[d:mage#1441428635637776455]], [[d:mage#1447962553199235122]], [[d:mage#1447973641823191132]].
- **[Weapon]** Scythe of Elune (K40) is regarded as the single best mage item — grants +2% hit, +2% crit, +40 SP, procs "Elune's Infusion" (500-650 arcane dmg) once per Arcane Missiles cast (not per missile), and a 10-min-CD group-wide +10% haste summon buff for 8s; worth roughly 10-20% DPS per raid-log estimate — [[d:mage#1445772450162933955]], [[d:mage#1451141209245487114]], [[d:mage#1448660805338792018]] (group-wide, not raid-wide), full effect table posted at [[d:mage#1459265057140510781]].
- **[Weapon, proc]** Ornate Bloodstone Dagger (OBD) gives an SP-scaling (~40%) fire-damage proc, contributing ~6-9% of total boss damage per one player's parses, roughly Wraithblade-tier or better even at lower ilvl, but can push back spellcasts (esp. bad mid-Rupture cast) — [[d:mage#1457474614925201662]], [[d:mage#1457480349285089413]], [[d:mage#1458294971655524489]], [[d:mage#1459213683602231609]] ("easily BiS on Ambershire's current phase").
- **[Set bonuses]** T2 5-piece Arcanist set bonus is superb for Arcane single target and BiS until Naxx — [[d:mage#1447215133288955966]], [[d:mage#1447603250147754004]]. T3 6-piece (mana return on crit during AP) enables popping Arcane Power twice in some K40 fights; T3 8-piece (works only on Rupture+Surge) still sims close to old Naxx BiS despite being easier to acquire — [[d:mage#1457729590523985951]], [[d:mage#1457730007081418965]], [[d:mage#1454122826100641893]].
- **[Set bonuses]** The "of the Lost" 3pc-in-4-item mana-regen set additionally reduces aggro and, via its summoned ghost DoT, reduces enemy damage taken by 5% with ~50%+ uptime — worth ~8-10% of one player's total damage on Tel'abim — [[d:mage#1450239707777732840]], [[d:mage#1450242204214886613]], [[d:mage#1450296511438131282]].
- **[Set bonuses]** AQ40 T2.5 5-piece bonus (10% dmg after a full Evocation channel) is widely considered bad/impractical since it forces dropping T2 5pc for minimal payoff — [[d:mage#1451229944003952731]], [[d:mage#1451231698074210315]]. ZG 5-piece Fire bonus (-0.5s Flamestrike cast) called "atrociously bad" by one player but demonstrated as strong in practice via Akanamu's parses/videos (disagreement) — [[d:mage#1448440888895209557]] vs [[d:mage#1448472583329812531]], [[d:mage#1448472249584713890]].
- **[T1 mixing]** Common Phase-3 strategy mixes 3 pieces of normal T1 Arcanist with 3 pieces of the "new" Arcane-flavor T1 to get both the 3-set bonuses — [[d:mage#1446934697572827176]], [[d:mage#1459636702841798772]].
- **[Naxx bis caveat]** Community-made BiS spreadsheets/lists are considered inaccurate at the margins (opaque stat weights, no active-trinket handling); a detailed line-by-line critique lists Khadgar's Guidance > Gloves of Unwinding Mystery, Eyestalk Waist Cord > Memory of the Last Guardian for multi-target, Doomfinger > Hellflame for multi-target, and missing common trinket swaps — [[d:mage#1459905729036025857]] (Ocra, extensive).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Gloves]** Arcane Power glove enchant (+20 SP equivalent) beats the 1% Haste glove enchant at essentially all realistic SP levels for arcane — [[d:mage#1442497826968834208]], [[d:mage#1490... ]] see also [[d:mage#1450174257798709330]] (andre's full enchant list).
- **[Pants]** ZG 1% hit pants enchant is stated to "never be replaced" by the 1% haste enchant — works identically well for all specs, trash and boss — [[d:mage#1442503244319424684]], [[d:mage#1459313148459749387]].
- **[Head/Legs]** Kara10/Kara25 spell-penetration enchant on helm/legs is generally a waste (see Stats section on resist floors) except specific edge cases — [[d:mage#1445153386206068898]].
- **[Cloak]** Cloak enchant options limited to a minor 2% threat enchant pre-AQ; the 20 spell-power glove enchant and cloak Subtlety enchant only unlock at AQ release — [[d:mage#1450496482242199753]], [[d:mage#1450515315703414886]].
- **[Weapon]** +30 Spell Damage weapon enchant standard while using a placeholder weapon (BM/AQ20 rep sword) before a good weapon drop — [[d:mage#1443588471649468578]], [[d:mage#1443594056612450454]].
- **[Armor kits]** Enchanted Armor Kit (+9 SP, Leatherworking, obtained via turning in Hyjal dreamshards) is better than the base Dragonmaw Armor Kit (+5 SP, plain BoE) — commonly confused — [[d:mage#1450175422204608676]], [[d:mage#1450175980818927616]], [[d:mage#1450176942786744444]].
- **[Full arcane enchant kit]** andre's summarized "BiS enchants on Ambershire": Haste on shoulders/legs/helm, SP on bracers, Arcane Power on gloves, +30 SP on weapon, +6 Arcane SP gems, Dragonmaw/Enchanted kit elsewhere — [[d:mage#1450174257798709330]] (largely endorsed, minor clarification on kit tiers).

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[Stacking rule]** General rule: one flask + one food + one drink + ALL elixirs (they all stack with each other) + one oil + one zanza — [[d:mage#1452290109851570352]] (Izzo). Spell-power consumables all stack with each other — [[d:mage#1452289222361874563]] (Gees).
- **[Stacking]** Elixir of the Sages does not stack with certain other Int buffs, but the Blasted Lands Int buff and Medivh's Merlot Blue do stack with the "AI" arcane-intellect buff — [[d:mage#1452289222361874563]]. Dreamtonic, Elixir of Greater Arcane Power, and Elixir of Arcane Power all stack together — [[d:mage#1446074397415702630]], [[d:mage#1446151593933279373]].
- **[Cost]** Non-flask consumables alone (Dreamtonic, Dreamshard Elixir, Elixir of Greater Arcane Power) can add ~150-160 SP and ~3% crit cheaply, and are sometimes rewarded free from daily dungeon boxes — [[d:mage#1446168602330796285]], [[d:mage#1446169071954690172]]. Full max consumables can add up to ~350 SP and ~3.5% crit for a caster — [[d:mage#1448805852877291642]] (Luden TA).
- **[Cost]** Brilliant Wizard Oil and Greater Arcane Elixir, previously expensive, both now drop from weekly reward boxes and are cheap — [[d:mage#1446176966515491063]], [[d:mage#1446180078286536804]], [[d:mage#1446180494420087026]].
- **[Sustain]** Mageblood potion (mp5) and Nordanaar Tea recommended for mana sustain; Herbal Tea shares a cooldown with Demonic Rune but not with mana/health/rejuvenation potions — [[d:mage#1450233916266119258]], [[d:mage#1450240166676664443]].
- **[Haste cooldown stacking]** Juju Flurry usable up to ~12% gear haste, Potion of Quickness up to ~10%, both together up to ~7% before you're wasting value against the 66%/100% haste caps — [[d:mage#1457504190761930835]] (Pepopo); a community addon (HasteDisplay) shows green/yellow/red for whether popping either would exceed those caps — [[d:mage#1457729034229256347]], [[d:mage#1457729144153702453]].
- **[CD sequencing]** For burst windows, only stack every cooldown together (AP+MQG+Juju+Quickness) on fights under ~30s; otherwise spread cooldowns; the goal is to bring Rupture's cast time down toward but not under the GCD (~1.5s) — [[d:mage#1457546495405326430]], [[d:mage#1457490531600044235]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Evocation]** Mana regen is granted on server mana ticks (~2s intervals); best uptime is achieved by casting Evocation right before a tick, since the 4th tick lands with ~2s left on the channel and you can cancel early rather than waiting for the final (non-regenerating) portion; regen boost is roughly 800-1600% (uncertain exact %) — [[d:mage#1452736983209083033]], [[d:mage#1452737294057345024]], [[d:mage#1452737536861671496]] (Luden TA).
- **[Arcane Power mana]** With T3 6-piece (mana return on crit during AP) and the 1%/sec drain (changed from 2%/sec), popping AP at pull can leave you above 90% mana 20 seconds later — [[d:mage#1450222590395416707]] (UncleBruce).
- **[Threat/utility]** Amplify Magic (Magic Attunement talent) gives a blanket ~4% magic damage amp usable on named trash (Anubisath Sentinels, Qiraji Mindslavers, Firelords) and on allies (boosts a Warlock's Life Tap, and helps healers on physical-heavy fights); does not work on raid bosses — [[d:mage#1447691992959156344]], [[d:mage#1447692305715822734]], [[d:mage#1447692718942982155]], [[d:mage#1450226713089933394]] (bugged for raid-boss use per Dinousek's testing).
- **[PvP utility]** Amplify Magic on an enemy also puts them "in combat" early, useful to prevent a Warrior charge — [[d:mage#1446915962497531912]], [[d:mage#1446917417509851338]].
- **[Threat]** Mage threat generally not a raid concern; server threat is described as "overtuned," meaning a competent tank rarely loses aggro to an opener — [[d:mage#1443298618323898552]], [[d:mage#1443298718110716025]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[Arcane mage tank]** Mage-tanking is a real, if niche, Turtle strategy: must be Arcane spec (no spell pushback on Arcane Missiles vs Fire's 70%-avoid/Frost's full pushback), and must respec out of Arcane's ~40% threat-reduction talents to reliably hold aggro — [[d:mage#1442604213749416036]], [[d:mage#1459269604542251039]].
- **[Twin Emperors]** Twin Emperors (AQ40) is commonly mage-tanked; needs heavy Shadow Resistance — a Priest's Shadow Resist buff + Mark of the Wild are required to break the 335 resist breakpoint — [[d:mage#1447836216199483434]] (Anima). Anima's example tank gearset (screenshot): Netherwind Crown, Eidolon Talisman (+8 Shadow Res), Arcanist Mantle (+5), Cloak of Untold Secrets (+10), Runed Stygian Belt/Leggings/Boots (+4/+10/+15 stam), two Ring of the Elder Warden (+8 each), Grimoire of Grells + Neltharion's Tear trinkets, Brimstone Staff — totaling substantial Shadow Resistance and 6,180 unbuffed HP — [[d:mage#1456832657047687359]] (image).
- **[General mage tank]** ZeroRanger (community's recurring "Mage Tank") has tanked Naxx trash, Twins, and Ouro; uses Ice Barrier + Burrower's Shell for ~1,800 extra effective HP and stresses that pull knowledge matters more than gear for mage-tanking dungeons — [[d:mage#1442527720302051499]], [[d:mage#1443294606681833472]], [[d:mage#1443294717298217155]], [[d:mage#1443224820031684689]].
- **[Solo/duo tank-kiting]** Mages can kite/duo-tank open-world content: Princess Theradras soloable as Arcane via kiting with Fire Blast/Surge between her dust fields, careful not to be casting during her boulder mechanic (an interrupt there disables Blink, since it's an Arcane spell) — [[d:mage#1448460582872158258]], [[d:mage#1461049951151980628]], [[d:mage#1461058467056844993]], [[d:mage#1448422834303926343]].

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[MC/Ony/BWL]** ~90% of these three raids' enemies are fire immune/resistant, making Fire "straight up trolling" to bring — [[d:mage#1452408736839634994]], [[d:mage#1453077957806395443]], [[d:mage#1453078085619679375]].
- **[AQ20]** Every boss has unusually high spell resistance — Moam partial-resists nearly every spell (has 80+ resist from Curse of Shadows plus the 24 unpenetrable floor); other bosses sit at 115/135/155 resist, explaining the abundance of pen gear dropping there — [[d:mage#1450488624125837452]], [[d:mage#1450522068750962769]], [[d:mage#1450527954076434525]].
- **[K40 general]** Trash between the 2nd and 3rd boss, plus the 3rd boss (Anomalus), are Arcane-immune, forcing arcane mages to swap Frost or Fire for that segment; Frost dies less there via Ice Barrier but does noticeably less damage than Fire — [[d:mage#1454543299540025457]], [[d:mage#1454850922089021500]], [[d:mage#1454900783270203566]].
- **[K40 Mephistroth]** 2nd-best K40 fight for Fire per one raider; roughly one dedicated decurser needed — [[d:mage#1440788776791183471]], [[d:mage#1459267389530312787]].
- **[K40 Gnarlmoon/Gnarl]** Trinket meta: Sigil best for AoE/padding on the ravens; REOS+Jewel of Wild Magics ("Rop") for the blue-side adds, Sigil+REOS for raven side; Mark of the Champion debated since spell power reportedly doesn't apply to the raven/owl adds (only the demon Gnarl itself benefits) — [[d:mage#1445588446814142485]], [[d:mage#1445594715675299840]], [[d:mage#1445602953036693667]], [[d:mage#1445609429209972909]].
- **[K40 Kruul/Chess]** Kruul: melee struggle past 25%, casters (Fire Shaman, Arcane Mage) carry damage; heavy decurse load throughout, with Chess needing near-continuous decursing — [[d:mage#1459267767692820635]], [[d:mage#1459267043512549439]].
- **[Naxx Loatheb]** Fire mage confirmed strong ("Fire is better on Loatheb for sure") — [[d:mage#1444812675610710076]] (Gees).
- **[Naxx Four Horsemen]** Long fight favors REOS (2 min CD, pop twice) over MQG (5 min CD); The Restrained Essence of Sapphiron liked for its shorter CD between MQG windows — [[d:mage#1457492358173167616]], [[d:mage#1459939111707349266]].
- **[Sunken Temple / DME]** Avatar of Hakkar drops Embrace of the Wind Serpent, buffed on Turtle to be actually good for Arcane (-400 armor, self-heal 300 over 10s, ~15%/spell proc chance); farmable by kiting as Frost or duo-boxing — [[d:mage#1458262042732527780]], [[d:mage#1458540137913782433]].
- **[Black Morass]** Solo-farmable by mage for rep/gear; Vampirism talent lets you pull Blood Drinkers into AoE pulls safely — [[d:mage#1441169102881296407]], [[d:mage#1441170272966410382]].
- **[ZG]** Mage AoE farming is dead post-nerf; best remaining ZG-adjacent income is mining, not AoE farming — [[d:mage#1456745859290238977]], [[d:mage#1456717838864613487]].
- **[Maraudon]** Princess Theradras soloable by Arcane (see Tanking above); the Scepter run itself is harder solo now due to pathing changes even though soloing Princess alone is unchanged — [[d:mage#1461047226603274458]], [[d:mage#1461047618963505183]].

## PvP
- **[Arcane]** Arcane PvP is a "suicide spec" — you one/two/three-shot a target then die quickly ("dipping your balls in sulfuric acid") — [[d:mage#1443727154788569311]], [[d:mage#1443727007606247547]].
- **[Frost]** Frost is more survivable vs Rogues/Druids abusing Free Action Potions (FAPs), but is considered weaker post-nerf; some argue deep Arcane + Iceblock now outperforms Frost for open PvP at 60 (single voice) — [[d:mage#1443727300083318986]], [[d:mage#1446952283760885957]].
- **[Openers]** Frost PvP combo: Polymorph → Frost Nova (note: Polymorph heals the target to full) → max-range long-cast Frostbolt; rank-1 Frostbolt used to quickly slow/create distance before the full-rank cast — [[d:mage#1444727016837615803]], [[d:mage#1444727256906731660]], [[d:mage#1444727606204305489]].
- **[Openers]** Hybrid PvP combo: Amplify Magic on enemy → rank-1 Frostbolt to slow → Arcane Rupture → Arcane Missiles — [[d:mage#1446916002456797397]] (BLS).
- **[Gear]** Even top (R14) PvP players mostly wear PvE T3/T3.5 gear in BGs rather than the dedicated honor set; the PvP set is considered not worth grinding for — [[d:mage#1455144927947391067]] (BLS).
- **[Design/balance]** One theory holds Frost's raid power is deliberately capped because it "would slap too hard in PvP" if buffed to Arcane/Fire's raid level — [[d:mage#1459270048844873943]], [[d:mage#1459270213957845185]] (Promey).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Essential]** Nampower (client mod) + "Nampower Settings" companion addon is considered essential: enables spell queuing (fewer missed casts from latency/server tick), perfect no-clip Arcane Missiles cancel (double-tap setting), quickcast for ground AoE spells, and a large DPS increase reported anywhere from ~10% to ~25% (one case: 25% more missile casts) — [[d:mage#1448786950369841286]], [[d:mage#1448805283513241610]], [[d:mage#1448806409830662207]].
- **[Surge tracking]** ProcDoc/MPowa addons for tracking Arcane Surge require the max-rank Surge spell to be placed directly on an action bar (not only inside a macro) or the addon won't detect it; must re-place it after learning a new rank — [[d:mage#1449982327651438603]], [[d:mage#1450217026307096616]], [[d:mage#1450221463691858201]]. Alternative: ArcaneSurgeWarning addon (`github.com/AllinGaming/ArcaneSurgeWarning`) — [[d:mage#1442806736888008847]].
- **[Frost macro]** Icicles-priority macro: `/cast [mybuff:Flash_Freeze] Icicles; Frostbolt` — must use `mybuff` (not `buff`) or it silently fails to replace in the cast queue — [[d:mage#1454246436718776330]], [[d:mage#1455275709462351873]].
- **[Fire macro]** Reactive Arcane Surge + Fire Blast macro (needs Roid-Macros addon): `#showtooltip Fire Blast` / `/cast [reactive:Arcane_Surge]Arcane Surge;Fire Blast` — [[d:mage#1454567964413264118]] (Pepopo).
- **[Decurse]** Decursive is unreliable for some players; Rinse recommended as a more configurable alternative decurse addon — [[d:mage#1451325243925991454]] (Luden TA).
- **[Custom addons]** HasteDisplay (Akanamu/Timanaku) shows green/yellow/red for whether a haste consumable would push you past 66%/100% haste — `github.com/Timanaku/HasteDisplay` — [[d:mage#1457729034229256347]]. DoiteAuras tracks proc windows (incl. Arcane Surge) WeakAuras-style — `github.com/Player-Doite/DoiteAuras` — [[d:mage#1460665429709029376]]. aDF tracks curses/Ignite/boss debuffs, preferred by some over BigWigs — `github.com/Zebouski/aDF/tree/masterturtle` — [[d:mage#1461120242817433701]].
- **[Stat addons]** StatCompare 2.0 and BetterCharacterStats both used to track live stats; StatCompare is lighter-weight and used in big raids for quick hit% checks; BetterCharacterStats is "not 100% accurate" and doesn't correctly track Scythe of Elune's hit/crit contribution — [[d:mage#1448291745145294919]], [[d:mage#1448312591209332748]], [[d:mage#1457727944179978437]].
- **[Planning tools]** pbrigade.gitlab.io gear planner is the community-standard gear tool (custom stat weights) but has no real sim and doesn't value active trinkets — [[d:mage#1449052230971031633]], [[d:mage#1459908471028842668]]. A Python-based sim tool (mage-specific, wowsims-style) exists with a slower packaged .exe UI for non-programmers; considered more accurate than the gear planner for fight-length/trinket-specific comparisons — [[d:mage#1453684594476843028]], [[d:mage#1458096414772166797]], [[d:mage#1460317994566811719]].

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Races]** Race choice is largely cosmetic below the top guilds; for min-maxing Horde's Orc (60 SP macro-able racial, though possibly nerfed to ~1 SP/level), Troll (Berserking haste racial), and Undead are favored, Undead's value depends on enemy warlock/priest presence in BGs — [[d:mage#1447253910875017238]], [[d:mage#1448829998055034950]], [[d:mage#1447419216222228540]] (disputed Orc racial value, "isn't orc 120sp, 200% of level").
- **[Races]** Alliance Human's racial (2% crit for 18s, once per activation) is seen as low value relative to Gnome/Dwarf for min-maxers; several players say they regret picking Human for this reason — [[d:mage#1443092037086875648]], [[d:mage#1443101766240440521]], [[d:mage#1443109817974521918]].
- **[Professions]** Tailoring is largely a "meme" profession for mages — mainly useful for Robe of the Archmage (craftable then droppable) and the two key BoE gloves; most other tailoring crafts have poor profit margins (mooncloth: ~18g mats for a 28g item, ~10g margin per 5-day cooldown) — [[d:mage#1448308285307355278]], [[d:mage#1453809774398541826]].
- **[Professions]** Engineering is considered the profession that most directly helps PvE mage play (though its main use is just Sappers); Enchanting + Jewelcrafting recommended together as ongoing gold-makers if willing to advertise; Alchemy/Herbalism recommended as the best long-run gold-saving/making combo for a first character — [[d:mage#1448045718920233052]], [[d:mage#1448316389499867206]], [[d:mage#1451246474187771915]].
- **[Gold]** DME Lasher farm (pulled 1-by-1) is the dominant mage gold farm post-ZG-nerf from ~level 54-60+ — [[d:mage#1446760895735922738]], [[d:mage#1446797783603482796]]. Black Morass is solo-farmable for rep and Arcane Essence gold, though a farming-guide video crashed Arcane Essence prices from ~12-15g to ~2-3g on Ambershire — [[d:mage#1442925609498972281]], [[d:mage#1442927495719682148]], [[d:mage#1442927522546454529]]. Herb prices crashed on Nordanaar due to lasher-farm oversupply and DME nodes — [[d:mage#1448314903759687832]], [[d:mage#1448315831267233944]].
- **[Gold]** Blade of Eternal Darkness (Maraudon) remains farmable/valuable for its vamp/resist-pen niche even after a server-side nerf — [[d:mage#1447775654367199243]], [[d:mage#1447777914840940584]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Fixed]** "Target is invalid"/"spell not ready"/"target isn't in front of you" errors when casting Arcane Missiles on bosses/trash were mostly fixed by Nampower months ago; remaining cases traced to "spy" addons (e.g. HonorSpy) silently changing target — [[d:mage#1441884263044747445]], [[d:mage#1441889688557326396]].
- **[Open]** Long-standing Arcane Rupture cast-failure bug still has an open bug report (id 18566) — [[d:mage#1440856176827830465]].
- **[Movement/cast bug]** A movement-cancel changelog change broke canceling Arcane Missiles/Icicles without moving first; disabling Nampower fixed it for one affected user (2025-11-28) — [[d:mage#1443754585658495096]], [[d:mage#1443762269203861586]].
- **[Set-effect bug]** The mirror-spell tier effect (Rewind-type set proc) triggers once per spell CAST, not once per target hit, despite the tooltip implying a full "rewind"; documented with a 15-target AoE hit that only produced one proc, subject of an extended bug-report thread — [[d:mage#1440974894509588531]], [[d:mage#1440977007793143818]], [[d:mage#1440980123926134866]] (R3XI3L).
- **[Display bug]** Elixir of Greater Arcane Power's stats don't display on the character stats page even though the elixir applies correctly on the backend — "the stats page is bugged, not the elixir" — [[d:mage#1446370634069311518]], [[d:mage#1446408784670101507]].
- **[Display bug]** BetterCharacterStats mis-tracks Scythe of Elune's hit/crit contribution, causing apparent crit-% discrepancies when swapping gear (verified by hand-math to actually be correct once Scythe's tracking bug is accounted for) — [[d:mage#1457695855288123559]], [[d:mage#1457730277173362791]].
- **[Not a bug]** Evocation's final tick appearing to "not complete" is expected behavior tied to server mana-tick timing; best practice is to cancel it manually just before the channel ends once your ticks have landed — [[d:mage#1452400804542812221]], [[d:mage#1452733084590936156]], [[d:mage#1452736983209083033]].
- **[Unresolved]** Whether a Frost Nova cast on a Banish-immune (demon) target still procs the Icicles talent is unresolved: Banish grants a spell-school immunity, not the MECHANIC_ROOT immunity Icicles is thought to require, so Pepopo guesses it won't proc but recommends testing — [[d:mage#1449136303617736876]], [[d:mage#1449167876274782269]].
