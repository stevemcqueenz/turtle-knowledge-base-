## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Mage]** Summary of the 1.18 patch for mages (posted retrospectively): "Slight nerf to Arcane. Huge buff to Fire — Ignite is now good, Flamestrike is good, lots of mana-regen buffs to the tree. No change for Frost except Magic Absorption moved to arcane tree row 1 (−5 points needed)" — [[d:mage#1406125529999999057]] (Arcitec, 2025-08-16).
- **[Fire]** Ignite duration reduced from 6s to 4s in 1.18, reverting an earlier 6s buff; developer comment: 6s caused diminishing returns after 1-2 fire mages (extra mages contributed nothing once 5 stacks hit) and forced solo fire into scorch-spam; 4s is meant to let the stack rotate/refresh among more mages and raises the effective soft cap to 3-4 fire mages — [[d:mage#1408703016089026683]] [[d:mage#1408840428572119162]] [[d:mage#1408844581470081167]].
- **[Fire]** Threat from Ignite is now shared between all mages who contributed to a stack (previously all threat went to the mage who "owned" the stack) — [[d:mage#1408703016089026683]].
- **[Fire]** Fireball/Flamestrike mana costs reduced across ranks (e.g. R12 Fireball 410→295 mana, R6 Flamestrike 990→680) and Pyroblast GCD cut from 1.5s to 1.0s, ahead of the 1.18 fire buff pass — [[d:mage#1356338011217985616]].
- **[Fire]** Fireblast now applies "Fire Vulnerability" itself (previously only Scorch), and Blast Wave's cooldown was reduced — both called out as good QoL wins post-patch — [[d:mage#1407768129475579927]] [[d:mage#1408319117114605679]].
- **[Frost]** "Ragnaros is now immune to Freeze effects" — patch note called out, affecting Flash Freeze/Frostbite proc availability on that boss — [[d:mage#1384620010525425845]].
- **[Arcane]** T3 6-set + Arcane Power interaction bug (haste bonus not applying while draining mana/killing the caster) was fixed on live — [[d:mage#1331671205366988910]] (prior bug report: [[d:mage#1331456006194073692]]).
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Missiles duration+haste ("deadzone") interaction bug acknowledged fixed by devs — [[d:mage#1322175958056173568]]; Arcane Surge fixed to also work with Resonance Cascade duplicates at the same time — [[d:mage#1322176046300401714]].
- **[Arcane]** CC2-era changes (late 2024): Arcane Rupture spellpower scaling reduced 100%→90%; Hot Streak cast-time reduction per stack increased 0.5s→1s but max stacks cut 9→5, "to make Hot Streak more attainable" — [[d:mage#1319504367912747032]] (Abbe relaying official patch notes).
- **[Arcane]** Mid-2025: Arcane Power's baseline mana-drain/regen penalty was detached from requiring T3 6-piece (previously AP was nearly unusable without the T3 bonus that removed the 75% regen penalty); this was controversial — some felt it weakened Arcane's identity/gear reward, others (esp. PvP) said it made Horde Arcane nearly unplayable without Kings/Wisdom buffs — [[d:mage#1356274313325445243]] [[d:mage#1356399590890541418]] [[d:mage#1356488144941350953]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Rupture spellpower scaling further nerfed (~4% per-missile damage loss, ~2.5-3% overall rotation DPS loss per math from Cutty Mink) around mid-2025 — [[d:mage#1398144957990047804]] [[d:mage#1398393866788536340]] [[d:mage#1398118041325404312]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Leveling]** Frost is repeatedly called the safest/most reliable leveling spec across the whole chunk (survivability via slows/Ice Barrier); Arcane is also viable and considered the lowest-effort/least-punishing rotation; Fire is the weakest leveling choice due to slow single-target damage and low crit at low levels — [[d:mage#1394357186272169984]] [[d:mage#1346247508061454407]] [[d:mage#1319048282579796071]] [[d:mage#1341020543989710849]].
- **[Raiding, 2024-11 CC2 launch]** Community backlash: all three specs seen as worse than pre-CC2 baseline; "every mage spec is worse than they were before the patch" — [[d:mage#1303801683918454885]] (+4 reactions), corroborated by multiple posters ([[d:mage#1303045080407474196]], [[d:mage#1309135732987920486]]).
- **[Arcane]** By 2025, widely regarded as the strongest/most flexible raiding spec ("fewest limitations of the three", "arcane is playable to non-t3 players with mana ruby/pots") — [[d:mage#1403407382327066747]] [[d:mage#1356274313325445243]]; but early on (Nov 2024-early 2025) considered mana-starved, risky (could die from Arcane Power at low mana pre-fix), and range-limited (30yd Arcane Missiles) — [[d:mage#1302000736519065621]] [[d:mage#1331456006194073692]].
- **[Fire]** Considered "terrible spot" through most of 2025 pre-1.18: forced into "solo fire" (one geared fire mage tunneling Ignite) because Ignite couldn't be reliably shared/refreshed by multiple mages, heavy mana problems, talent-point starved (needs ~13 pts in Arcane tree + 3 in Frost for sustain/hit) — [[d:mage#1344259381889466368]] [[d:mage#1356272097214730262]] [[d:mage#1321259296654098523]]. Confirmed later (1.18) that group Fire performs well again but solo Fire still has issues (Fireblast drops Ignite if not chained fast enough) — [[d:mage#1421234156758892544]] [[d:mage#1392943760500985978]].
- **[Frost]** "On par with arcane pre-naxx content" but heavily proc-dependent (icicles/flash freeze RNG causes huge fight-to-fight variance) — [[d:mage#1307015620088561665]] [[d:mage#1309345311315066881]]. AoE-frost identity (Improved Blizzard slow) was nerfed repeatedly (75%→~60%→50%) making Cone of Cold + Arcane Explosion kiting the preferred AoE method instead — [[d:mage#1323323814364577813]] [[d:mage#1334485615823028234]].
- **[General]** "All three mage specs are fine" / "all three are viable" is the recurring 2025 consensus once buffs landed — [[d:mage#1327825868441128981]] [[d:mage#1341020543989710849]] [[d:mage#1394357186272169984]].
- **[PvP]** Fire PvP considered weak (Pyroblast ~2.5k vs Destro Soulfire ~4k+; Ignite duration nerf hurt PvP burst specifically) — [[d:mage#1360284351757156413]]. Arcane PvP (Horde) called "unplayable" without Kings/Wisdom after the AP mana-regen change — [[d:mage#1356399590890541418]] (single source, contested by no direct rebuttal shown).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Arcane]** Priority order confirmed repeatedly: Arcane Surge (proc, instant) > Arcane Rupture (on CD/off cooldown) > Arcane Missiles (filler/channel); don't recast Rupture if the debuff is already up, only refresh it — [[d:mage#1415043367238242314]] [[d:mage#1329604300879691839]]. One-button smart macros exist to sequence Surge→Rupture→Missiles automatically — [[d:mage#1373948890109448257]] [[d:mage#1408832993656832093]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Missiles channels 5 missiles baseline (1s interval, or 0.95s with the 5% Accelerated Arcana talent reduction applied before haste). The T2/T3/Black Morass belt adds a flat, haste-unaffected +1s to channel duration, creating a "deadzone" at the end where no extra missile fires unless a haste breakpoint is crossed; cancel the channel right after the last missile lands rather than waiting out the deadzone — extensive formula given by RUTHINAS (interrupt-timing method) — [[d:mage#1308921530599604224]] [[d:mage#1305952609122783293]].
- **[Arcane]** Practical breakpoints: 100% multiplicative haste → 7th missile (~3.5s channel); ~200% haste → 8th missile; formula `h = 0.95n/8` for n missiles in Accelerated-Arcana 8s frame (worked example: 42.5% haste for 12 missiles = 2 full casts) — [[d:mage#1306123368373944351]] [[d:mage#1383252249886720096]].
- **[Arcane]** With T3 belt equipped, haste-capped cooldown-window cast time floors at ~2.8s (or ~2.4s with Eyestalk trinket) — [[d:mage#1394676523641864252]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Surge is worth casting/canceling missiles for below ~25% haste; above that (and especially with MQG active, ~20%+ haste) canceling AM to cast Surge becomes a marginal/negative DPS trade — [[d:mage#1371465450466181221]] [[d:mage#1332096020821049466]] [[d:mage#1421232547035349133]] (contested: Aest argues Arcane Surge scaling is anti-synergistic with Arcane's haste-stacking identity and needs a haste-scaling fix).
- **[Arcane]** Ornate Bloodstone Dagger (20% raw proc, ~1s internal CD, ~14-17% effective proc with Missiles/Surge/Rupture) generally beats a plain spellpower dagger except when its knockback interrupts Rupture uptime at range — [[d:mage#1348786852550676491]] [[d:mage#1349070589200961669]] [[d:mage#1349076573919383612]].
- **[Fire]** Rotation: stack Scorch debuff to 5x on pull → Combustion → Pyroblast (if specced) → on-use trinket (MQG/REOS/TOEP) → Fireball spam; weave Fireblast on CD once Improved Fireblast is talented; refresh Scorch just before the debuff falls off; weave Pyroblast at 5 Hot Streak stacks if talented; solo fire may switch to pure Scorch+Fireblast spam to chase/hold a lucky Ignite — [[d:mage#1343693738320334869]] [[d:mage#1374397423970943048]].
- **[Fire]** Ignite: only one Ignite DoT per target; first mage to crit "owns" it and gets all further damage credit; any mage's crit refreshes the duration and (until 5 stacks) adds more stored damage; at 5 stacks further crits only refresh duration — [[d:mage#1370070493058826240]].
- **[Frost]** Rotation is largely Frostbolt spam, fishing for Icicles procs off Flash Freeze; Frost Nova is worked into the raid rotation specifically to enable Flash Freeze/Icicles procs on freeze-immune raid bosses — [[d:mage#1406244522978967552]] [[d:mage#1304548776014512209]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Arcane/mixed]** Preferred modern AoE loop: Cone of Cold → Arcane Explosion x5 (or x6 with good spacing/timing) → Cone of Cold again; accept 1-2 resists, use Vampirism if getting hit, save Nova for emergencies unless going for a Shatter combo — [[d:mage#1336777512046301216]].
- **[Mixed]** Alternate kiting method: Nova to group mobs → position at cone-range sweet spot → Flamestrike then instantly Cone of Cold → kite in a circle spamming Arcane Explosion (mobs stay in the Flamestrike patch if you circle rather than flee) → reposition to Cone of Cold again to reslow — [[d:mage#1310764519487963177]].
- **[Frost]** Improved Blizzard's slow was nerfed hard over successive patches (75%→~60/65%→50%), making Blizzard/Flamestrike "useless" for AoE farming compared to instant, cheap, mobile Arcane Explosion — widely repeated complaint — [[d:mage#1323323814364577813]] [[d:mage#1334485615823028234]] [[d:mage#1318658804405112912]].
- **[Fire]** AoE is Fire's weak point: Flamestrike/Blast Wave have long casts and heavy mana cost for comparatively little payoff; Fire is "the only spec forced to choose between single-target and AoE DPS while ooming faster doing both" — [[d:mage#1319539463122059334]] [[d:mage#1319551184989847584].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General]** Spell crit: 53.77 Intellect = 1% mage spell crit on Turtle — [[d:mage#1343104058382221364]].
- **[General]** Crit vs spell power tradeoff: a 1% crit chance increase is worth roughly `spell_avg_dmg × 0.01/(1+crit)` extra average damage per cast — crit's marginal value falls off the more crit you already have; a 1% multiplicative haste increase gives ≈1% more damage-over-time regardless of current haste (doesn't fall off the same way) — [[d:mage#1327260304903770173]] [[d:mage#1395907881995604080]].
- **[Arcane]** No single "stat priority" exists for Arcane/Frost — instead there are gear-state-dependent weights; Hit is slightly better than Crit only against level-63 targets; Haste is strong single-target but ~useless for AoE (Arcane Explosion doesn't benefit the same way); all three (hit/crit/haste) are roughly equal until you're fully raid-buffed with good haste gear, at which point haste pulls ahead — [[d:mage#1392204369419702352]].
- **[Arcane]** Rule-of-thumb haste-to-spellpower conversion (~13 sp per 1% haste) is explicitly NOT a fixed constant — it depends on current hit/crit/sp levels and falls off at high haste once you're limited by GCD or lose access to Arcane Surge — [[d:mage#1345855109778903223]].
- **[Arcane]** Max theoretical gear+talent haste catalogued slot-by-slot (head/neck/shoulder/back/wrist/hand/ring/trinket/weapon/offhand/food/T1-3pc/talent tree) sums to ~34% — [[d:mage#1332361652519043112]] (Happybara).
- **[General]** Partial-resist math: Turtle uses the vmangos interpolation table (e.g. 10 resist = 0% chance of 75% partial, 20 resist = 1% chance); negative resistance (Arcane Focus talent) only lowers a target's resistance to 0, it cannot remove level-based resistance from higher-level mobs — [[d:mage#1327332058464522270]] [[d:mage#1339819706592526419]].
- **[Arcane]** T3/T3.5 belt "extra second" mechanic changes effective haste-value math; canceling channels optimally (see Rotation section) recovers the DPS loss from the belt while gaining mana efficiency — [[d:mage#1308954094039339048]].
- **[Fire]** Haste jump at 3% breakpoint gave an "insane" DPS jump in one Pepopo sim (scorch→fireball rotation, 1119→1244 dps between 2% and 3% haste) attributed to hitting a cast-time rounding threshold — [[d:mage#1314697291390652549]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Power: 35% haste, ~40% of current mana consumed on activation, base 75% mana-regen reduction while active; could kill the caster if mana hit 0 during it before a fix — [[d:mage#1302020963717611612]] [[d:mage#1331456006194073692]] (fixed: [[d:mage#1331671205366988910]]).
- **[Arcane]** The 5-second mana-regen rule: regen ticks happen every 2s from load-in, independent of casting; any mana spend disables the "at rest" bonus regen for 5s; channeled Arcane Missiles casts longer than 5s create a window where a regen tick can land mid-channel if timed right — full worked explanation — [[d:mage#1306107321197395968]] [[d:mage#1306111554646966272]] [[d:mage#1323969341670166539]].
- **[Arcane]** Mind Quickening Gem + Arcane Power + on-use trinkets are typically popped together at the start of a burst window; smart macros exist to sequence trinket-use before Arcane Power and prevent AP firing without MQG active — [[d:mage#1344729273440796833]] [[d:mage#1344731444806942791]].
- **[Fire]** Mana sustain requires ~13 points in the Arcane tree (Arcane Concentration, Magic Absorption, sometimes Arcane Meditation) at the cost of core Fire talents (Improved Fireblast, Improved Flamestrike, Incinerate, Hot Streak) — a persistent talent-tax complaint — [[d:mage#1321259296654098523]].
- **[Utility]** Wand Specialization talent widely considered a wasted investment at endgame; recommended to put points into Arcane Impact/Flame Throwing/Blast Wave instead — [[d:mage#1402671229508386917]] [[d:mage#1362744058686214294]] (contested by leveling context where wand-weaving is more relevant).
- **[Utility]** Amplify Magic / Dampen Magic (Magic Attunement talent) generally considered too weak/situational to be worth a GCD in raid — repeated buff requests (raise from base 2%/1% to 3%/2% with the talent doubling it) not confirmed implemented — [[d:mage#1312521237460680705]] [[d:mage#1321263078569934953]] (contested/open).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Kara40]** Gnarlmoon: cannot pop MQG, Arcane Power, or Evocation while under his "blue buff" debuff — noted as a DPS-check mechanic — [[d:mage#1357570440318550026]].
- **[Naxx]** Kel'Thuzad has 200 frost resistance; frost mages stack spell-penetration gear (including duplicate head/legs) specifically for this fight to avoid heavy resists — [[d:mage#1319914227477250068]].
- **[Kara40]** Anomalus drops mage bracers with a very long (~30s) internal cooldown, making them barely an upgrade (or a downgrade for slow-casting Fire/Frost) versus Naxx bracers — [[d:mage#1362738270538170488]].
- **[Kara40]** Mephistroth ("Meph") drops a caster staff debated as an Arcane-mage loot priority alternative to Desecration+Sapphiron off-hand; some argued Affliction Warlocks should be prioritized on the same weapon since it's their only upgrade path, others argued Arcane mages deserve equal priority for the haste — unresolved/contested — [[d:mage#1406379426941501592]].
- **[BWL]** Thaddius and other continuous-raid-damage fights make Ice Barrier uptime (and thus Frost's 15% damage buff) hard to maintain; ranged-only fights like Heigan leave Frost Nova/Flash-Freeze windows mostly RNG — [[d:mage#1357116456890011739]].
- **[ZG]** Egg of Hakkar questline (start from troll witch doctor, Steamwheedle Port) lets you re-summon Avatar of Hakkar repeatedly without turning in the quest, farming spirit gear; Avatar drops the highest-spirit chest available to mages (Vestments of the Atal'ai Prophet) — [[d:mage#1306041340173353011]].
- **[AQ40]** Twin Emperors drop an offhand and neck considered top Arcane BiS picks (offhand nearly as good as the Sapphiron one from Naxx) — [[d:mage#1420103555297054911]].
- **[C'Thun]** Negative-resistance/partial-resist example logged from a Warlock's Corruption tick against C'Thun, used to illustrate partial-resist mechanics generally applicable to mage spells too — [[d:mage#1327356140811718737]].
- **[General]** Naxx is the raid where Fire's mana/Ignite-sharing problems are most visible due to fight length; multiple posters reference Naxx specifically when describing Fire's issues — [[d:mage#1319655979809378385]] [[d:mage#1339419351538729064]].

## PvP
- **[Arcane]** Horde-side Arcane PvP called "unplayable" without Kings/Wisdom once the AP baseline mana-regen removal was taken away from the general population change — a "high risk, high reward" complaint (single source) — [[d:mage#1356399590890541418]].
- **[Fire]** Fire PvP burst (Pyroblast ~2.5k) is well below Destruction Warlock's Soul Fire (4k+); the Ignite duration/window nerf specifically hurt Fire's PvP identity ("revert ignite change just for pvp") — [[d:mage#1360284351757156413]].
- **[Frost]** Deep-frost PvP builds (Improved Cone of Cold) questioned once Icicles/Flash Freeze (target-must-be-freeze-immune, i.e. mostly a PvE mechanic) don't translate well to PvP; players unsure whether to run frost, arcane, or a fire/frost hybrid for PvP — [[d:mage#1318240427635114056]].
- **[PvP tactic]** Vs. Shaman: Sheep + Fireball/Scorch to bait Counterspell, then commit to Frost spells; direct engagement is otherwise very unfavorable for mage due to shaman's self-heal/utility — [[d:mage#1397964439583461376]].

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Tool]** Nampower (by Pepopo, github pepopo978/nampower) — DLL providing cast queueing and precise cast-cancel timing, essential for Arcane Missiles breakpoint optimization; requires a loader like VanillaFixes or UnitXP_SP3 — [[d:mage#1364212120471994469]] [[d:mage#1306498256020176896]].
- **[Tool]** ProcDoc addon (Clempton, github wsmaxcy/ProcDoc) — pulsing visual alerts for procs (Clearcasting, Netherwind Focus, Temporal Convergence, Flash Freeze, Arcane Surge) — [[d:mage#1329340364716249138]].
- **[Tool]** ArcaneSurgeWarning addon (github AllinGaming/ArcaneSurgeWarning) — dedicated Arcane Surge proc timer, recommended over ProcDoc for that specific proc — [[d:mage#1376140782838480957]].
- **[Tool]** MagePlates addon (beastie, github quadcrown/MagePlates) — UnitXP-based nameplate AoE-range indicators, with added talent/tier-bonus detection (Arctic Reach frost range, Netherwind 5pc AE range) — requires SuperWoW + UnitXP AOE-distance build — [[d:mage#1332853192496775218]] [[d:mage#1333185444346724412]].
- **[Cast bar]** Quartz addon confirmed to correctly show Arcane Missiles channel ticks even when macro-cast (unlike Punschrulle, which loses tick display with macros) — [[d:mage#1382392481785577654]].
- **[Macro]** One-button Surge>Rupture>Missiles priority macros documented and iterated on by the community (SuperMacro/roid-macro based); example Lua smart-function for prioritizing trinket-then-Arcane-Power ordering given by RUTHINAS — [[d:mage#1373931321298260070]] [[d:mage#1344731444806942791]].
- **[Macro]** Ice Block / Cold Snap toggle macro (Lua) that casts Cold Snap first if Ice Block is on CD, otherwise Ice Block, or cancels Ice Block if already active — [[d:mage#1384150519450304604]].
- **[Wiki]** Community-maintained Mage FAQ (turtle-wow.fandom.com/wiki/Mage_FAQ), pinned, covers stat priorities, BiS by spec, leveling builds, and haste mechanics — treated as the canonical reference throughout the chunk — [[d:mage#1331688071405506610]] [[d:mage#1406244522978967552]].
- **[Bug/quirk]** Addons that silently inspect other players (e.g. Honorspy-style "NotifyInspect") can change your server-side target and fail to restore it, causing Arcane Missiles' "target must be in front of you" / cast-fail errors, since AM uses whatever target the server currently has rather than your visible target — [[d:mage#1409585295376257167]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Arcane]** T3 6-set + Arcane Power: haste bonus failed to apply while wearing the 6-piece set (mana still drained −40% and regen still cut −75% with zero compensating haste) — reported as "made worse" by a related fix, then hotfixed same week — [[d:mage#1331456006194073692]] [[d:mage#1331671205366988910]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Missiles + T3/Black Morass belt "deadzone": the belt's flat +1s duration doesn't scale with haste, so at non-breakpoint haste values the final second of the channel fires no missiles — extensively documented, eventually addressed — [[d:mage#1308921530599604224]] [[d:mage#1322175958056173568]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Surge does not proc from Resonance Cascade-duplicated spells even when the duplicate is resisted — flagged as a likely bug affecting proc-tracking addons — [[d:mage#1336846470044975125]].
- **[Arcane]** Resonance Cascade duplication behaves inconsistently in combat logs (sometimes double-procs, sometimes chains further than the documented 4-duplication cap) — [[d:mage#1308653968150495304]] [[d:mage#1305945923687551007]].
- **[Arcane]** Dampen Magic and Amplify Magic could both be active simultaneously by casting one as a "debuff" (on enemy/duel target) and the other as a "buff" (on party/self) — filed as a bug/possible exploit — [[d:mage#1304633454163398737]] (bug report #9459).
- **[Arcane]** "Target needs to be in front of you" / "ability not ready" cast failures on Arcane Missiles/Icicles, often caused by other addons silently swapping the server-side target (see Macros section); workaround is deselect/reselect target — [[d:mage#1404140184349573203]] [[d:mage#1405834479150567425]] [[d:mage#1405881735903838280]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Missiles visual/animation desync: missiles visually bunch up and fire late/all-at-once relative to the actual cast timing, especially with the HD texture pack enabled or on certain (e.g. goblin) models — [[d:mage#1413132994075426958]] [[d:mage#1418542043692798022]].
- **[General]** Wrath of Cenarius on-hit proc does not stack with spellpower consumables — filed as bug #10404 — [[d:mage#1314595098666733568]].
- **[Arcane]** "Coalesced Mana" buff tick amount changed unexpectedly between patches (238-239 mana per tick observed) — [[d:mage#1357051094571683932]].
- **[Arcane]** Arcane Rupture crit+Resonance-Cascade-duplicate interaction produces damage numbers that don't match the expected 50%/100% multipliers — flagged as possibly buggy — [[d:mage#1320401816675946598]].
