## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret]** Ret is "fully raid viable" but not top-tier DPS; needs a lot of build effort/gear/consumables to perform — a badly-played Ret does much worse relative to potential than a badly-played Rogue or Warrior. — [[d:paladin#1451839907554394164]] (Destroses, 2025-12-18); [[d:paladin#1452251692539052075]], [[d:paladin#1452251962501369957]] (Silverhand/Mithreon, 2025-12-19)

- **[Ret]** AP SoC pros: simple/straightforward, higher burst potential; cons: mana problems, worse scaling, little/no AoE. SP SoR pros: higher & more consistent DPS, decent AoE, few mana problems, better mana economy, less gear competition; cons: counter-intuitive itemization, limited weapon choices. — [[d:paladin#1452020679821099078]] (Adamant, 2025-12-20, well received); confirmed by [[d:paladin#1452019332686020734]] (Grey)

- **[Holy]** Holy melee-healing at BiS gear is described as the best healing spec this patch, top of raid healing meters; has the best mana economy of all healers if crit is high, plus strong utility (stun, taunt, Freedom, Cleanse, Bubble, LoH, BoP). — [[d:paladin#1451691998326034533]], [[d:paladin#1451692272784506890]], [[d:paladin#1451693822366056518]], [[d:paladin#1451694074850443274]] (Breaddy/Mithreon, 2025-12-18)

## Leveling
- **[General]** Recommended leveling specs by role: Ret for leveling/questing (esp. new players), Prot for dungeon spam / AoE grinding, Holy for PvP-focused leveling; a mixed "ret/prot hybrid" approach is also common early on. — [[d:paladin#1450890164888014960]] (Raugtreefell, 2025-12-17); disputed nuance below
- **[Leveling]** For single-target quest killing Ret is faster; for AoE-pull questing/farming Prot is faster; Prot has no meaningful downtime if played correctly (full resource economy via Seal/Judgement of Wisdom). New/unfamiliar players are advised to just play Ret since AoE pulling requires game/mechanic knowledge to be actually faster. — [[d:paladin#1451241349411246215]], [[d:paladin#1450890850706919586]], [[d:paladin#1451243947056955392]] (Destroses/Beldrin/Dodging, 2025-12-17/18)
- **[Leveling, Prot]** At level 20, switch to 1H+shield; a 2H weapon does not increase Holy Strike damage (flat, ~43% SP-scaling ability, does not scale with weapon damage), so 1H+shield is strictly better once available, and Holy Shield (~level 30) requires a shield anyway. — [[d:paladin#1451005498697453790]] (gastro, 2025-12-17)
- **[Leveling]** Holy Strike vs Crusader Strike while leveling: Holy Strike ignores armor, is mana-neutral/refunds its own cost, and heals the caster — generally stronger, especially against armored/high-level targets; Crusader Strike is reduced 30-40% by armor, costs mana, and gives no self-heal. Exception: with a very slow weapon and freshly-upgraded CS rank, CS can pull ahead temporarily. — [[d:paladin#1451168243208159332]], [[d:paladin#1451168380529672233]], [[d:paladin#1451168488147259574]], [[d:paladin#1451169191465058416]] (Grey/Arle, 2025-12-18)
- **[Leveling, Prot dungeon tanking]** Use Retribution Aura for AoE pulls, Sanctity Aura for single-target; tanking is almost exclusively Seal of Righteousness, reseal/judge inside the swing timer for threat, using Seal/Judgement of Wisdom for mana; switch to Seal of Command around level 30 for AoE grinding since it's weapon-damage based and matches Str-heavy leveling gear. — [[d:paladin#1452256705986695281]], [[d:paladin#1452256746176516328]], [[d:paladin#1452256868666703874]], [[d:paladin#1452257860582113390]] (Destroses/Mithreon, 2025-12-19)
- **[Leveling, Holy]** Holy is explicitly "not a leveling tree" / weak for solo leveling; if leveling as Holy, spam rank-appropriate Flash of Light to keep a tank topped, switch to Holy Light only if the tank drops below ~60% then go back to FoL; the melee-healer ("Blessed Strikes") playstyle only becomes effective once that talent is available higher in the Holy tree, so it doesn't work the same at low levels. — [[d:paladin#1452051332470145065]], [[d:paladin#1452052778427088937]] (Dodging/Beldrin, 2025-12-19); [[d:paladin#1451950613205090395]], [[d:paladin#1451951004210823270]] (Khas/Dodging, 2025-12-19)
- **[Leveling]** A "epic holy leveling build" was posted as a screenshot: a 31-point pure-Holy allocation (7 rows deep into the Holy tree) with guidance to level as Holy, then after level 40 pick up Ironclad Will and move remaining points into the Ret tree. — [[d:paladin#1451798758672044107]] (Dodging, 2025-12-18; image transcribed)
- **[Leveling, Ret]** For a Ret leveler who also wants to PvP, a suggested checkpoint build at level 30 spends 21 of 50 possible points already deep into the Retribution tree (screenshot posted as a "start with this" starting point, further points/finishing build left to the player). — [[d:paladin#1453194276031954994]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-24; image transcribed)
- **[Leveling]** No 2H "tank" leveling path really exists — at level 30, speccing Prot grants Holy Shield which requires a shield, forcing the 1H+shield switch. — [[d:paladin#1452248505295831233]], [[d:paladin#1452248652931399710]] (Arle/Mima, 2025-12-19)
- **[Leveling, hit table]** Melee hit chance from weapon skill: 8% base at 300 skill, each point above reduces required hit by 0.2% (so 7% at 305, 6% at 310, 5% at 315); at 308 you technically need 6.4% hit, so round up to 7% rather than 6% to avoid leaving 0.4% unaccounted for. Devs reportedly said only 8% hit needed to be capped after "CC2" (a Turtle content-cycle/patch milestone), with roughly 8% chance to miss bosses at 300 skill confirmed by testing. — [[d:paladin#1451473692491120703]], [[d:paladin#1451477655944695819]], [[d:paladin#1451489199143387197]], [[d:paladin#1451489421135183982]], [[d:paladin#1452038290344775865]], [[d:paladin#1452038718977343488]] (Mithreon/Aelgagar/Dodging, 2025-12-18/21)

## Rotation: single target
- **[Ret]** General rotation loop described (vs. rogue's simpler NA/S&D spam): pre-pull seal, then Holy Strike (6s CD) → 1.5s GCD → Judgement (8s CD) → Consecration (8s CD) → 1.5s GCD → repeat, managing buffs throughout. — [[d:paladin#1451115878463570071]] (Mccnl1ght, 2025-12-17)
- **[Ret]** Seal twisting (SoC → SoR before attacking) is technically possible in Turtle but inefficient since it burns a GCD normally used for other abilities: cast Seal of Command with auto-attack off, then Seal of Righteousness with a start-attack macro. Tested to get up to 12 Judgement of Wisdom procs this way, but needs T2 3pc + Gilneas ring + Wrath of Cenarius (sacrificing ~37 SP on the ring slot) to be "remotely worth it"; with Windfury in the party it's better to just run SoR + T2.5 in PvP. If SoC's ~0.5s proc delay (originally added as a "twisting helper") is ever removed, multi-SoC-per-swing-with-Windfury seal twisting could return. — [[d:paladin#1452434874731593880]], [[d:paladin#1452435020932452464]], [[d:paladin#1452435230240538635]], [[d:paladin#1452435791044411412]], [[d:paladin#1452435831661793301]], [[d:paladin#1452436228820570202]], [[d:paladin#1452436708128854088]], [[d:paladin#1452436777012039959]], [[d:paladin#1452436855709761617]] (Jesse Potter/Mithreon, 2025-12-22)
- **[Ret, SP scaling]** Roughly all Ret abilities besides autos scale with SP: cited example "95 SP ≈ +30 Crusader Strike dmg, +40 Holy Strike dmg," plus benefits to Exorcism, Holy Wrath, Consecration, and some SoC/JoC damage — SP matters even for AP/SoC builds. — [[d:paladin#1451710513602891867]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-18)
- **[Ret, crit target]** For AP Ret, effective crit "cap" ≈55% (40% glancing + ~5% dodge on white hits eat into it), with bosses applying ~2% crit suppression at 315 weapon skill; recommended crit breakpoints to sit at are ~33% or ~51% raid-buffed (51% only reachable in full BiS with Druid/Rogue crit buffs) — values in between (33-51%) are considered a bad spot; near-BiS players land around 38-42% buffed in practice. — [[d:paladin#1451700642140655648]], [[d:paladin#1451701098866806916]], [[d:paladin#1451701230995898491]], [[d:paladin#1451701506691694633]], [[d:paladin#1451703879359135936]], [[d:paladin#1451703916172546109]], [[d:paladin#1451704292900606053]] (Excinerus/Jesse Potter, 2025-12-18)
- **[Ret, hit gearing]** Cited Ret hit% budget: 1% from weapon-skill quest, 3% from talent, 1% from 0.5T shoulders, 1% from Onyxia neck, 1% from a ring, 1% from a trinket = 8% total (example build). Band of Accuria + Onyxia neck alone give 3% for 2 slots; T1 3-piece Ret set bonus gives 3% hit + a Judgement damage bonus; T1 Ret gloves specifically can be worn cross-spec by Prot for hit while gearing. — [[d:paladin#1450886946149630062]], [[d:paladin#1451960280966889613]], [[d:paladin#1451960562421469326]], [[d:paladin#1451969941963149476]] (Raugtreefell/Jesse Potter/Arle, 2025-12-17/19)
- **[Ret, gearing/AP]** AP Ret: Strength-only enchants preferred by some over "+3 all stats" because Kings + Divine Strength give a large multiplicative Str bonus (cited as ~40-45% total, confirmed multiplicative by testing); others prefer "+3 all stats"/stamina for survivability trade-offs — disagreement noted. — [[d:paladin#1451716464540123391]], [[d:paladin#1451742176374423742]] (silverthornn/Adamant, 2025-12-18); [[d:paladin#1452424891914649650]], [[d:paladin#1452423306228797592]] (Jesse Potter/Olivaar of Moments, 2025-12-20)
- **[Ret]** SoC (AP) cares mostly about Crit until a target of ~33% (near-BiS players sit ~38-42% buffed per the crit-target bullet above); almost all other secondary stats are close to negligible for SoC once crit priority is met. — [[d:paladin#1452428329876459532]], [[d:paladin#1452429082397507746]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-20)

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot, leveling/farming]** AoE farming/leveling loop: keep Seal of Righteousness (or Seal of Command once available ~lvl 30) up, drop Consecration on cooldown, use Retribution Aura, and lean on Blessing of Sanctuary + Consecration for AoE mitigation/threat; Blessing of Sanctuary's 30-40% damage reduction is called out as a big comfort/safety factor when farming multi-mob pulls (e.g. Lasher farming). — [[d:paladin#1451241349411246215]], [[d:paladin#1453314506582130740]], [[d:paladin#1453314608654848193]] (Destroses/Grey, 2025-12-17/24)
- **[Prot, dungeon AoE threat]** On AoE trash, Holy Shield's rank doesn't matter for threat if mobs aren't already hitting you — it's just filler threat stacked on top of Retribution Aura and Consecration; use max rank unless mana is a specific concern. — [[d:paladin#1450900179048861696]], [[d:paladin#1450900243125239898]], [[d:paladin#1450900439565340835]] (Grey, 2025-12-16)
- **[Prot, dungeon threat]** Dungeon (non-raid) tanking loop cited: Hammer of Justice one target, Holy Strike another, Judgement of Righteousness a third, dropping Consecration, marking skull and letting DPS go ham on everything except skull so the tank only needs to hold the rest; HS+JoR is usually enough passive threat (Holy Shield + Consecration) to keep targets from ripping aggro before they die. — [[d:paladin#1451515029110390826]], [[d:paladin#1451514883941208196]], [[d:paladin#1451513624790044802]] (Mima/tripleXDengine, 2025-12-19); [[d:paladin#1451515288955650139]] (Arle)
- **[Prot]** On MC's Garr fight, adds deal physical (not fire) damage, so a Prot tank handling multiple Garr adds needs a lot of physical avoidance more than fire resist. — [[d:paladin#1452198600678441075]], [[d:paladin#1452199226019680256]] (Mima/Dodging, 2025-12-19)

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Good stats: Stamina, Agility, Spellpower, Crit. Worst stats: Strength, Spirit. — [[d:paladin#1450944994423603432]], [[d:paladin#1450945158190334074]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-18)
- **[Prot]** General stat priority: enough HP to survive the encounter, then dump everything into spellpower, then hit cap if achievable on top of that; armor/defense beyond what naturally comes with upgrades isn't worth chasing specifically. — [[d:paladin#1451153707625615402]] (Grey, 2025-12-18)
- **[Prot]** Defense: 1 point ≈ 0.04% dodge/parry/miss/block each; its only unique effect is reducing the frequency of crits taken from bosses. Comparison: 20 agility (1% dodge + 1% crit) ≈ 8 defense (0.32% dodge/parry/miss) in raw avoidance value. — [[d:paladin#1451155120997007421]] (Grey, 2025-12-18)
- **[Prot, threat scaling]** Spelldamage needed to hold threat scales with the raid's DPS gear level: roughly ~150 spelldmg sufficient in MC, ~200 spelldmg recommended for BWL (rule of thumb, not a hard number). — [[d:paladin#1451156545273462915]], [[d:paladin#1451156701368811583]], [[d:paladin#1451156983720972431]] (MalvinP/Grey, 2025-12-18)
- **[Prot, HP targets]** Recommended raid-buffed HP floors: ≥7-8k for Broodlord (Mortal Strike ~5k + ~1k auto) and Chromaggus (Incinerate up to ~7.2k during enrage, needs hunter Tranquilizing Shot); healers should not assume 80% HP is safe headroom against these hits. — [[d:paladin#1451157117145972746]], [[d:paladin#1451157640683061289]], [[d:paladin#1451157784170070127]], [[d:paladin#1451157983701631006]], [[d:paladin#1451159037453533257]] (Grey, 2025-12-18)
- **[Prot, weapon skill]** Don't push melee weapon skill above 315 on any melee spec — 315 is the glancing-blow-reduction cap; going beyond it (e.g. vs level-1 target dummies/critters) can even push crit chance toward 100% due to a known quirk, but this has no practical raid benefit. Autos: 300 skill has 40% of autos reduced by 40% (glancing), 305 skill reduces that same 40%-of-autos penalty to 25%. — [[d:paladin#1450854719495995554]], [[d:paladin#1450855245222772797]], [[d:paladin#1450856511625494780]], [[d:paladin#1450856634933837929]], [[d:paladin#1450857110861779085]], [[d:paladin#1450868536988663889]] (Dodging/Grey, 2025-12-16)
- **[Melee hit table]** 8% hit needed at 300 weapon skill; each point of skill above 300 reduces the requirement by 0.2% (7% @305, 6% @310, 5% @315); non-multiples-of-5 skill are awkward since hit doesn't come in fractional amounts off gear (e.g. 308 needs 6.4%, round up to 7%). ~8% chance to miss a boss confirmed by testing at 300 skill. — [[d:paladin#1451473692491120703]], [[d:paladin#1451477655944695819]], [[d:paladin#1451489199143387197]], [[d:paladin#1451489421135183982]], [[d:paladin#1452038290344775865]], [[d:paladin#1452038718977343488]], [[d:paladin#1453138109377024090]], [[d:paladin#1453144437927710800]] (Mithreon/Aelgagar/Dodging/Mccnl1ght, 2025-12-18/21/24)
- **[Ret]** Auto-attack/on-hit item procs (e.g. Hand of Justice-style trinkets and cloak procs) are rolled as independent chances per swing, not summed into one combined roll — tested with ~15k logged autos and never saw a "double proc" on the same swing; two on-hit items just each get their own independent shot per hit. — [[d:paladin#1452650085522604153]], [[d:paladin#1452650207035785338]], [[d:paladin#1452650446215843992]], [[d:paladin#1452971962195509248]] (Dodging, 2025-12-19/22)
- **[Ret]** Haste does not scale Crusader/Holy Strike or Consecration; it only speeds up autos and, indirectly, Seal-of-Righteousness/Seal-of-Command procs-per-minute — considered a weak, low-priority stat for Ret until gear is quite good (rule of thumb: start valuing haste once unbuffed SP is around 400+). — [[d:paladin#1451337274951467028]], [[d:paladin#1451337365263220919]], [[d:paladin#1451337658369577143]], [[d:paladin#1451367512833786109]] (Sylvaira/gastro, 2025-12-18)
- **[Prot]** Haste is a minor but real threat contributor (autos are ~500 of a cited 1800 total TPS breakdown including SoR/FTT/Thunderfury) but is hard to itemize for deliberately, since gearing choices should prioritize raising minimum SoR/JoR hits; like Strength and (to a lesser extent) Crit, haste/attack-speed stats only affect 1-3 abilities vs. Spellpower affecting the whole kit. — [[d:paladin#1451626959611236413]], [[d:paladin#1451628831889821836]], [[d:paladin#1451629118331555910]] (Grey, 2025-12-18)
- **[Prot]** Armor Pen is weak for Prot since ~95% of Prot's threat is holy damage, not physical. — [[d:paladin#1452123626827677697]], [[d:paladin#1452123879488098326]], [[d:paladin#1452646161612673086]] (Not Normal/Jesse Potter, 2025-12-19)

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Prot]** Righteous Fury macro shared: `#showtooltip Holy Shield` / `/cast [nomybuff:Righteous_Fury] Righteous Fury` / `/cast [mod:shift nocooldown:"Holy_Shield"]Holy Shield(Rank 1)` / `/cast [nocooldown:"Holy_Shield"]Holy Shield; [notargeting:player]Hand of Reckoning` — auto-applies Righteous Fury if missing, and has taunt (Hand of Reckoning) built in via shift-modifier logic, so be careful about accidentally taunting the wrong mob. — [[d:paladin#1450872709037949048]] (Grey, 2025-12-16)
- **[Prot]** Holy Shield Rank 1 is commonly downranked to save mana: using Rank 3 over Rank 1 costs roughly the mana-equivalent of a Blessing of Wisdom application for only ~6.25 extra "dps" of block value, i.e. ~37.5 mp5 more expensive for R3 (R3 costs ~105 mp5 vs. R1's ~67.5 mp5). - **[Judgement/Blessing sharing]** Talents that improve a Judgement or Seal debuff (e.g. Improved Seal of the Crusader) affect the debuff applied to the target, not the paladin who benefits from it — so the improved effect only appears if the paladin WITH the talent is the one who applies that Judgement; multi-paladin groups should coordinate who judges/blesses what so the right talents are used on the right effects. — [[d:paladin#1452602940903788544]], [[d:paladin#1452605164371312782]], [[d:paladin#1452605294491205725]], [[d:paladin#1452605420173787146]], [[d:paladin#1452605595940294686]] (silverthornn/Silverhand, 2025-12-22)
- **[Multi-paladin pug coordination]** In pugs, judge/blessing assignment usually has to be negotiated by whisper/DM (e.g. "can you do JoW/JoTC first GCD") since there's no reliable automated way to force other paladins' judgement choices; PallyPower's judge-assignment feature "doesn't really do anything" mechanically, but its presence is treated as a proxy for competence in pug culture. Tradeoffs are common (e.g. one paladin judges Wisdom on trash in exchange for the other judging Wisdom on skulls, or judging on bosses). — [[d:paladin#1453042078790451270]], [[d:paladin#1453043258325340374]], [[d:paladin#1453043636832043069]], [[d:paladin#1453043979334586540]], [[d:paladin#1453044088244015249]], [[d:paladin#1453044447246942298]], [[d:paladin#1453045613708054598]], [[d:paladin#1453046186889183313]] (L-star/Seviry/Arle/Mithreon, 2025-12-23)
- **[Holy]** In raid, Holy paladins are advised not to spend GCDs on Crusader Strike/Consecration — the healing rotation is Holy Shock on cooldown plus Flash of Light, with occasional melee auto-attacking (kept up via Seal of Wisdom/Judgement of Wisdom) squeezed into otherwise-empty GCD gaps for extra mana/threat-free value; Judgement of Wisdom should stay on one (typically a big/long-lived) target in raids with few big targets (e.g. MC), while Seal of Wisdom (no Judgement swap) is better for zones with many short-lived trash (e.g. Kara10). — [[d:paladin#1453131750644383784]], [[d:paladin#1452685490892570666]], [[d:paladin#1452727170827943976]], [[d:paladin#1452727228852080661]], [[d:paladin#1452727302759776367]] (Jesse Potter/Arle/Silverhand, 2025-12-19/24)
- **[Holy Strike healing]** Turtle-specific: Holy Strike's self-heal-adjacent effect actually heals ALL nearby allies, not just party members — this began as an unintended "bug" that the devs kept as a feature because it couldn't easily be fixed; consecration-related libram tooltips still incorrectly describe their effect as "group"-based when it actually applies to everyone nearby. — [[d:paladin#1453348542893523036]], [[d:paladin#1453348696854102026]], [[d:paladin#1453348726260371566]], [[d:paladin#1453351051129389108]], [[d:paladin#1453351291488178228]], [[d:paladin#1453355294003302430]], [[d:paladin#1453355420210167820]] (Destroses/Pastor/Rhiina/Lhian [dev], 2025-12-23/24)
- **[Ret leveling mana]** Mana-efficiency tips while leveling: don't reseal/judge a mob that's about to die (e.g. at 20% HP); it's better to wait a few extra seconds for a free auto-attack than to burn 40% of your mana bar and need to sit and drink for 10s; Blessing of Wisdom gives better overall uptime than Blessing of Might despite Might "looking" better, because more mana means fewer downtime drinking breaks. General advice for a fresh Ret complaining about mana: use mana pots/tea on cooldown, use Consecration less, keep Seal/Judgement of Wisdom up, put a spellstrike enchant on the weapon, and take the Benediction talent. — [[d:paladin#1452839704889393349]], [[d:paladin#1452839825118986505]], [[d:paladin#1452840081328181328]], [[d:paladin#1452839874154729573]], [[d:paladin#1453485716242829433]] (Beldrin/Destroses/Dodging, 2025-12-22/25)

## Tanking specifics
(see also Stats/caps and Gear sections above for Prot stat priorities, hit%, and FR breakpoints)
- **[Prot]** Gearing a tank has no single clean upgrade path the way DPS/healer gearing does — it's about staying adaptive with swappable pieces for threat vs. mitigation vs. a specific resistance need, rather than a straight best-in-slot chase. — [[d:paladin#1452714680547016936]] (Dodging, 2025-12-19)
- **[Prot]** Long list of situational Prot gear sets a well-geared raider is expected to keep on hand: T2.5 (threat), T3 (AoE mitigation), T3.5 (single-target mitigation), a fire-resist set (Rag/Firemaw), an Arcane-resist set (Incantagos/Anomalus in Kara40), a frost-resist set (for a future Sapphiron), a nature-resist set (Erennius Hard Mode), a dedicated mitigation-accessories set, a dedicated SP-accessories set, Thunderfury, an SP weapon (for nature-resistant mobs/bosses and AoE), a Strathholme sword (for quick Judgement-of-Wisdom weapon swaps), Fist of Death (crush immunity with T2.5/T3.5), Force Reactive Disk (AoE snap threat), Scaleshield (fear-immunity swap when priest coverage is thin on Nefarian), an AP-Ret set and an SP-Ret set (whichever seal — SoR or SoC — is weak that patch), a high-HP AP-Ret and high-HP SP-Ret set (for Kruul), a Holy set, and a high-HP Holy set — plus spare hit pieces for whenever a tier-gear itemization mistake removes needed hit. — [[d:paladin#1451620232870498354]] (Grey, 2025-12-18; hyperbolic but grounded in real item names/encounters — treat individual set claims as illustrative, not literal requirements)
- **[Prot, K40 Anomalus]** Anomalus's physical melee damage is small (roughly 1000-1000 range, about once per 2 seconds after cast-time factored) — smaller threat than a "non-threatening" boss's basic auto — so a Leather Arcane-Resist set (rather than Plate AR) is perfectly fine to tank Anomalus in, especially with a shield and/or more than one tank sharing duty. Priority stats for Anomalus: Stamina, Arcane Resist, and enough threat to hold aggro until the first bomb goes off; avoidance helps if solo-tanking. ~280 AR fully buffed was reported as sufficient gap to safely dump threat for bombs; there's no fixed tank order — whoever is 2nd on the threat table gets ~6 seconds to pump threat before falling back to a corner. — [[d:paladin#1453285637435424799]], [[d:paladin#1453285902263517316]], [[d:paladin#1453293907269124136]], [[d:paladin#1453294097531015242]], [[d:paladin#1453294225364881553]], [[d:paladin#1453294569700724848]], [[d:paladin#1453294637094666270]], [[d:paladin#1453295067325272085]] (Mioko/dusty/Grey, 2025-12-23)
- **[Prot, K40 Rupturan]** The MT needs to stand further than 20 yards from Rupturan to properly hold the fight's aggro/threat mechanic (specific mechanism not detailed in chat, but confirmed as a "found out this week" fix that resolved a threat problem). — [[d:paladin#1453324999011078288]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-24)
- **[Prot, farming]** Blessing of Sanctuary is called out as a big quality-of-life/safety pick for solo farming multi-mob pulls (e.g. Lasher farming): its 30-40% damage reduction is compared to getting "both Libram of the Faithful and a Thorium Shield Spike" worth of effect for a 30-minute buff, and is often the first thing cut to save buff slots even though it's still valuable against bosses too. — [[d:paladin#1453314506582130740]], [[d:paladin#1453314608654848193]], [[d:paladin#1453316398401978430]] (Grey, 2025-12-24)

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Holy melee-healing (using Holy Strike's heal-on-hit plus Holy Shock/Flash of Light) is called the best healing spec this patch at BiS gear, topping raid healing meters, with the best mana economy of any healer when crit is high; also brings strong utility: stun, taunt (via Reckoning-style interactions), Freedom, Cleanse, Bubble, Lay on Hands, Blessing of Protection. — [[d:paladin#1451691998326034533]], [[d:paladin#1451692272784506890]], [[d:paladin#1451693822366056518]], [[d:paladin#1451693881815990467]], [[d:paladin#1451694074850443274]] (Breaddy/Mithreon, 2025-12-18)
- **[Holy]** "Effective healing"/HPS as a skill metric is repeatedly dismissed by multiple experienced players as misleading for Holy Paladins, since good tank mitigation (lower raid damage taken) directly suppresses a healer's measured throughput; padding effective healing (e.g. via Holy Strike on AoE trash) is explicitly contrasted unfavorably against "useful" healing like Cleanse uptime on dispellable debuffs. — [[d:paladin#1450952340612841646]], [[d:paladin#1450952657182265405]], [[d:paladin#1450955772463939604]] (Beldrin/Jesse Potter, 2025-12-18)
- **[Holy leveling]** Leveling as Holy is weak solo: spam a rank-appropriate Flash of Light to keep a tank topped, switching to Holy Light only if the tank drops to ~60% HP or lower, then back to FoL once topped; the melee-healer ("Blessed Strikes") playstyle doesn't work the same at low levels since it depends on a talent unlocked later in the Holy tree. — [[d:paladin#1452050947735031999]], [[d:paladin#1452051332470145065]], [[d:paladin#1452052778427088937]], [[d:paladin#1451950613205090395]], [[d:paladin#1451951004210823270]] (Blackbiospark/Dodging/Beldrin/Khas, 2025-12-19)
- **[Holy]** Ironclad Will is judged not worth prioritizing on gear/talents — only ~140 extra heal power even in T3 gear — versus crit-stacking for Holy Shock/Flash and to proc Mending Light off Holy Strike ("Daybreak" effect); Improved Devotion Aura, Toughness, and other bonus-armor sources do NOT count toward Ironclad Will's healing scaling (it only reads base gear armor), a known misleading-tooltip issue devs have been made aware of (alongside Improved Seal of Righteousness and Reckoning as other bad-tooltip offenders). — [[d:paladin#1451966256755507334]], [[d:paladin#1452253580978290742]], [[d:paladin#1452254579725963376]], [[d:paladin#1452256168289501357]], [[d:paladin#1452256009140830290]] (Nystolan/Mithreon/EmilyJ/Destroses, 2025-12-19)
- **[Holy, mana]** 3/5 Illumination with ~30% crit is sufficient to never drop below 30% mana in essentially any content except Kara40, even with no mana flask; a full Strat run can reportedly be completed without drinking once using just Seal of Wisdom (not even Judgement of Wisdom) kept on a "fat"/high-value target. Holy is repeatedly called one of, or the, best mana-economy healer at the endgame. — [[d:paladin#1451675254115274915]], [[d:paladin#1451676036642373795]], [[d:paladin#1452656736950288495]], [[d:paladin#1452659685646798994]], [[d:paladin#1452653209389891868]], [[d:paladin#1452653286472548555]] (Mithreon/Faithroll, 2025-12-20/22)
- **[Holy, raid rotation]** Don't spend GCDs on Crusader Strike/Consecration as a raid healer — the loop is Holy Shock on CD plus Flash of Light, filling otherwise-idle GCDs with a melee auto (kept mana-neutral/positive via Seal/Judgement of Wisdom) rather than pure spellcasting. — [[d:paladin#1453131750644383784]], [[d:paladin#1452685490892570666]] (Jesse Potter/Arle, 2025-12-19/24)
- **[Holy, Shockadin build tested]** "Shockadin" (Ret gear + Holy Shock-focused Holy talents used for DPS) was tested and found not worth it: needs a minimum of ~3 spellstrike items plus ~10% spell hit just to keep casting it sustainably in a raid DPS role, and even then the mana:damage ratio comes out roughly 1:1. On Nordanaar, top Spelladin/AP-Ret DPS was cited around ~1700 at the high end, while a Shockadin struggled to sustain 700-800 DPS on Patchwerk while going OOM about halfway through the fight — roughly half the output of a proper Ret build. Resetting Holy Shock's cooldown via Crusader Strike isn't worth it either — a plain Holy Strike simply hits harder; the practical use of Shock in a Holy-Prot hybrid setup is just squeezing in a shock or two on downtime bosses (e.g. Medivh) when off tanking-duty during trash. — [[d:paladin#1453315656153038950]], [[d:paladin#1453316538676547704]], [[d:paladin#1453317011651432601]], [[d:paladin#1453317319978647567]], [[d:paladin#1453317414346559499]], [[d:paladin#1453317522098229303]], [[d:paladin#1453317663987335201]], [[d:paladin#1453318096835313696]], [[d:paladin#1453318641046261893]], [[d:paladin#1453318884256911370]] (dusty/Excinerus/L-star, 2025-12-24)
- **[Holy]** Healing rank management example: a lower-geared Holy paladin (~600 HP pool era of gear) used Flash of Light rank 5 and Holy Light rank 6, with the general rule to use whatever rank keeps people alive without draining mana faster than the healer is comfortable drinking to replace. — [[d:paladin#1453344772478468216]], [[d:paladin#1453345051177521277]] (Mima/Grey, 2025-12-22)
