## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Prot]** Prot paladin is "high floor, low ceiling": a decent prot pally can be the best tank in an average guild, but best prot paladins don't compete with best bears; genuinely bad prot pallies are worse than any tank except a bear that forgets to shift — [[d:paladin#1479612800409145557]] (Silverhand, +0), [[d:paladin#1479613620584452166]].
- **[Prot]** Mid-to-average guilds ("75th percentile kill times") rarely have threat problems as prot; issues mostly appear in top ~1% speedrun guilds pushing extreme dps — [[d:paladin#1479603450752012359]], [[d:paladin#1479604283954565150]] (Grey describing himself and angels1m as the two prots who complain most, for opposite reasons: angels1m OT's a top guild vs. top players; Grey has to push top-1% threat in a mid guild with some very strong players).
- **[Ret]** All ret builds are now "hybrid" — the real choice is SoR ("SP"-leaning, more strength, more consistent) vs SoC ("AP"-leaning, more spellpower); SoR generally favored until T2.5/UTB access, "SoR ret is the better performing of the 2" pre-patch per Destroses; expected to shift closer to parity after the 1.18.1 SoC PPM buff (7→9, +libram) — [[d:paladin#1479273676015669300]] (Grey full gearing rundown), [[d:paladin#1479836216894423113]], [[d:paladin#1479836587230494934]].
- **[Ret]** Necrodin build ("Of the Lost" set + procs, uses both T2 3pc ret and prot bonuses) only comes fully online once both T2 3-piece bonuses are held simultaneously; niche/build-around, viable but not mainstream BiS — [[d:paladin#1481026943385342084]], [[d:paladin#1481244507336081509]], [[d:paladin#1481248954028003541]].
- **[Ret]** Shockadin (Holy Shock DPS build for Ret/Prot) is explicitly called a "meme spec": low mana efficiency, 16% miss chance on Holy Shock even with 8% gear hit, worse than just using Repentance for the GCD; "shock prot feels so damn good, but it just doesn't perform as good as it feels" — [[d:paladin#1481266234233327746]], [[d:paladin#1481266894215446569]], [[d:paladin#1481274133617573960]], [[d:paladin#1481275237160063096]].
- **[Ret]** Community sentiment: Ret is felt to be treated as an "afterthought," with the strongest current gap between top and average ret parses attributed partly to ramp-up time (getting Zeal stacks + Holy Might up before a fast pull) rather than pure skill — [[d:paladin#1481937881596559471]] (Southshore Crusader Vidnar), [[d:paladin#1481935203877388298]] (angels1m: "how many rets struggle to maintain zeal before boss pull, wasting 30 sec"), disputed by Destroses/Mithreon who note ret is "one of the better classic+ specs" overall — [[d:paladin#1481939354795642930]].
- **[Ret]** Ret paladin considered "the garbage disposal class" for gearing — near any item with STR/SP/crit/hit is usable, unlike most classes; extreme flexibility praised and mocked simultaneously — [[d:paladin#1479274925867929791]], [[d:paladin#1479277095204028697]] (Grey).
- **[Prot vs other tanks]** Grey's informal tier list (current → after patch): current SSS Druid(well)/SS Druid(avg)/S Druid(poor)+Shaman(well)/A Druid(v.poor)+Paladin(well)+Shaman(avg)/B Paladin(avg)+Warrior(well)/C Paladin(poor)+Warrior(avg)/D Shaman(poor)/E Warrior(poor); after patch, Paladin moves up to the "A" tier when played well (tied with Shaman well) but Druid/Bear remains the undisputed top tank regardless of patch — [[d:paladin#1482765714761842728]]; Silverhand agrees bears "were so considerably above the rest of the pack they'll still be the best tanks" even after paladin buffs — [[d:paladin#1482765166343880786]].

## Leveling
- **[Prot]** Solo-leveling/farming build at level 50-53 (e.g. Lasher farming): "0/33/10 for 5 parry goes a long way," shield "of blocking," Vampirism helps sustain, prioritize armor/defense — [[d:paladin#1479722938423971932]] (Baelin). Concrete talent link and approach shared: go Prot side first, then put remaining points into Holy as far as level allows; gear stacked around Stam/Int — [[d:paladin#1479736968719106173]] (Zaviel).
- **[Prot leveling]** Stat priority while leveling: fast weapon, stamina and intellect, spellpower where available (trinkets, some quest rewards, gear from level 40-50+) — [[d:paladin#1480699497687421038]] (Arle).
- **[General]** Base-stat scaling talents (e.g. Divine Intellect) and Sanctity Aura's holy-damage boost are "effectively useless" below level 50 due to tiny stat totals at that level; most 5-man dungeons up to ~level 25 can be healed in any spec — [[d:paladin#1481960882652315770]] (McSavage).
- **[Prot/Holy leveling]** Recommended weapon: usable through level 60 is fine (e.g. Thrashblade) though upgrades exist along the way — [[d:paladin#1479687638423441508]], [[d:paladin#1479687755633266840]] (Grey).
- **[General leveling]** Holy Strike is the default leveling filler: bypasses armor, is free (mana-neutral via proc/threat utility), gives minor group utility; Crusader Strike may do slightly more raw damage in some cases but overall kills slower due to extra drinking downtime — [[d:paladin#1479780305417277440]] (keungzhe).
- **[Leveling path]** After Westfall: partial Redridge now + more later, or Darkshore/Loch Modan; Redridge can be fully completed around level 23-25 — [[d:paladin#1479520387791585411]] (Badknees).
- **[Prot fresh-60 gearing]** No formal guide exists; a community Google Doc BiS list "floats around." Cheap early gear: Soulforge Gloves/Belt/Bracers (BoE, affordable), Crimson Spellblade from Stratholme Live (rare), any ~2200-armor shield (Draconian Deflector or Black Morass shield), otherwise gear like a warrior (highest armor+stam plate) until reaching K10/ZG/Black Morass paladin gear — [[d:paladin#1479134461537943714]] (Grey). Don't overinvest in spellpower early — most SP comes from consumes/enchants and base ability damage is already high; a large threat nerf to fresh-60 prot was expected within 30 days to reduce overtuned threat — [[d:paladin#1479135203413721262]] (Grey).
- **[Prot fresh-60]** Black Morass (BM) offers good rep/gear (spellpower + defense pieces, "need on" viable) but is not recommended to tank as a fresh 60 — "Dragons gonna fold you in half"; a gearing path of running BM to exalted while picking up all available tank gear is otherwise solid — [[d:paladin#1479128320514982019]], [[d:paladin#1479128357588304065]], [[d:paladin#1479134149330866197]] (Junak'Salsa/Seviry).
- **[Prot pre-raid libram]** No good libram exists before raids except the Scarlet Monastery one, which gives a free 1% crit on Holy Strike — [[d:paladin#1479242363275706573]], [[d:paladin#1479242399954768084]], [[d:paladin#1479245713073377312]] (Silverhand/dumbid).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Prot]** Core single-target rotation (Grey): precast Holy Shield before pulling the boss; use Holy Strike on cooldown, never delay it; use JoR ~2 seconds after HS, ideally during the GCD of another cast (often Holy Shield since its cooldown frequently overlaps with HS); refresh SoR the next global after JoR; fill remaining globals with Consecration (rank 5 until threat is established, then rank 1 to save mana) — [[d:paladin#1479624488675971323]].
- **[Prot]** Priority nuance depending on goal: mitigation-focused = HS > Strike+JoR > Reseal > Consecration; threat-focused = Strike+JoR > Reseal > Consecration > HS; the only time to delay/skip HS is when survival, not threat, is the concern (use Holy Shield instead) — [[d:paladin#1482784084332642345]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1482782844395720785]] (Silverhand). Consensus is HS should be used at exactly its cooldown rather than delayed, even if it costs a 0.5s GCD "tax" elsewhere — [[d:paladin#1482782298461048864]], [[d:paladin#1482782880370528257]] (Kindousen).
- **[Ret]** Fundamental seal-uptime rule: every auto-attack must land with an active seal — judge inside the swing window, then reseal in time for the next auto; never white-swing without a seal up — [[d:paladin#1481671197186850826]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1481671317223768166]] (forsooth confirming).
- **[Ret]** Judgement timing matters more than avoiding auto-clipping: make sure Judgement lands after an auto (or ~0.5-1s before the next auto) so the auto itself benefits from the seal (or gets a SoC proc chance); this is more important than worrying about Crusader Strike overlapping with autos/Windfury — [[d:paladin#1480913696963166301]], [[d:paladin#1480913792052367411]], [[d:paladin#1480917882505396336]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret, SoR]** Full SoR rotation guidance (Grey, since "no SoR rets answered" previously): keep Holy Might and Zeal up; don't let Strikes/Judgements sit unused too long (extending a 6s CD into a 9s CD is rarely worth waiting for a "perfect" window); wait ~2s after a Windfury proc before using the next strike/judge; refresh seals between autos; Consecration/Exorcism don't proc Windfury, so only avoid delaying HS/CS/JoR for them and avoid hitting 0 mana before combat ends — [[d:paladin#1483022433265913865]]. Open with HS for the 20% strength buff, then spam CS until the strength buff needs refreshing — [[d:paladin#1483023120410345554]]. Some SoR rets skip HS entirely on fast-dying trash since the buff doesn't have time to pay off — [[d:paladin#1483022982992363601]].
- **[Ret, post-1.18.1]** Post-patch Holy Strike will out-damage an unsundered Crusader Strike, so prioritize HS on trash; HS also grants 20 Strength (~26%) making the next HS stronger; on bosses, open with HS then prioritize Crusader Strike while maintaining Holy Might since HS gets "sundered" (debuffed on repeat use against same target) — [[d:paladin#1482251821417365697]], [[d:paladin#1482251974044029019]], [[d:paladin#1482252114809061530]], [[d:paladin#1482252392622723175]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret, Necrodin]** For 1H Necrodin, Holy Strike does more damage than Crusader Strike (contrary to naive assumption); CS is used to stack/maintain Zeal, but HS should still be pressed on cooldown — [[d:paladin#1482395480133468251]] (Arle), confirmed empirically by Iseut whose logs showed HS beating CS despite spamming more CS — [[d:paladin#1482393874180739194]].
- **[Ret]** Seal/Zeal priority rule of thumb: always refresh Zeal duration first if stacks are already up; with SoC, prioritize Holy Might over Zeal (SoC/2H scale with weapon damage); with SoR, prioritize Zeal stacks over Holy Might (SoR doesn't scale with weapon damage but haste increases proc/attack count) — [[d:paladin#1482302289338433606]] (Nazeth).
- **[Holy]** Melee-range Holy Strike healing (for AoE Mending Light + Daybreak) should be avoided on certain fights and used aggressively on others: "100% do it on Heigan, definitely do it on Patchwerk (ignore melee complaints), Loatheb only if your raid leader wants a specific number" — [[d:paladin#1482319532700991570]] (Dodging); default back to ranged Flash of Light spam when melee isn't safe — [[d:paladin#1482310661760356354]] (gastro).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot]** AoE/threat-focused priority: Consecration > Strike+JoR > Reseal > Holy Strike (i.e., front-load Consecration before single-target strikes on trash) — [[d:paladin#1482784084332642345]] (Mithreon).
- **[Prot, leveling/dungeon]** Simple dungeon-tanking rotation: Holy Strike for the buff, Crusader Strike, Judge on cooldown, Repentance on bosses, Consecrate on packs (mana permitting), repeat — [[d:paladin#1479180234996846734]] (gastro).
- **[Prot, multi-target]** For 3+ adds: Holy Strike the first target, taunt the second, stun the third; rely on offtanks to peel, or rebuild threat on lost targets and taunt back the skull target, or run a Look-Into-my-eyes(LIP)+AoE-taunt rotation — [[d:paladin#1482780158388731974]], [[d:paladin#1482780515454156851]] (Kindousen/Arle).
- **[Prot]** Consecration rank management: it's one of the few spells worth downranking under mana pressure (along with Holy Shield) — [[d:paladin#1479848590192152698]], [[d:paladin#1479850014628122726]].
- **[Prot, farming]** AoE/vampirism-based trash farming (e.g. Stratholme) needs ~16%+ vampirism on gear due to a 0.7x AoE lifesteal multiplier; 10% is the bare minimum, 12%+ is noticeably safer/faster — [[d:paladin#1480326094812086284]], [[d:paladin#1480326285870764062]] (Grey).
- **[Ret, AoE build]** Suggested AoE/farming talent split: 0/26/25 (vs. 11/8/32 for standard ret DPS) — [[d:paladin#1481929505831653376]] (Destroses).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Threat stat priority: SP > hit > crit > agility (20 agi ≈ 1 crit) > strength — but "very arbitrary," you'll know what you personally lack — [[d:paladin#1482717762697564242]], [[d:paladin#1482717916985032705]] (Mithreon).
- **[Prot]** Mitigation stat priority disputed: a posted list of "Def Skill > Anything else" and "Hit > Stam > Armor > Block/Dodge > SP > Parry > Def" was called "retarded" by Grey — defense-skill-over-stamina and block-over-everything don't apply on Turtle the way they do in TBC/WotLK because threat isn't free and stamina isn't cost-reduced here; any scenario requiring heavy mitigation thinking will global you below 8k HP anyway, and reaching 8k HP is already a huge investment. For threat gearing Grey would rank stamina over strength, but itemization variety is so poor it's rarely an actual choice — [[d:paladin#1482716835135881479]] (Raugtreefell/Starkss posted list), [[d:paladin#1482717394987257926]], [[d:paladin#1482733588586823741]] (Grey).
- **[Prot]** HP thresholds: aim for 7,500-8,000 HP unbuffed (no titans) ASAP; ~7.2k HP is the first threshold that meaningfully reduces "global" deaths (e.g. Vael's breath DoT + flame aura + auto attack together will kill a paladin with 12k armor/7k HP in one global) — [[d:paladin#1479149805857931274]], [[d:paladin#1479150376237273343]] (Grey).
- **[Prot, BWL]** BWL bosses will "just global you" below ~8k-9k HP; Chromaggus can curse you half a second before Incinerate and hit for 8k — [[d:paladin#1482013156644622397]], [[d:paladin#1482013401893965947]] (Grey).
- **[Prot]** Crush-immunity math example (Meph): 10,555 pre-armor hit; 14k armor gives 70% DR (→3,166); a further 10% DR (→2,850); at 8k HP, that leaves 5,150; a 0.4x parry-haste-crit for 5,700 finishes you (from 100% to 0% in half a second); chance of parry haste ~1-in-6, chance of a crit in T2.5/T3.5 gear ~1-in-31.25, i.e. roughly once every 3 kills without crush immunity — [[d:paladin#1482770093904629905]], [[d:paladin#1482770981436002344]] (Grey). Disputed by angels1m, who argues holy shield uptime and healer reliability matter more than chasing crush immunity, and that giving up a trinket slot for it isn't worth it in T3.5 — [[d:paladin#1482770428094320841]], [[d:paladin#1482772515272003727]], [[d:paladin#1482775120341635185]]; Grey/Kindousen maintain avoiding crushes should be a high priority for an MT — [[d:paladin#1482776196826796113]].
- **[Prot]** Defense/avoidance is far less accessible/important on Turtle than in TBC/WotLK — use "however much your gear happens to have"; 440 defense is just above 22% avoidance-from-defense (over the 300 base) and is "psychotic" to itemize for on purpose — it's only useful for AQ40's bug tunnel to avoid being dazed while running ahead — [[d:paladin#1482006533805047830]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1482777932928909354]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1482777907280740393]] (Kindousen).
- **[Prot]** Crit value for tank threat: at ~1,000 SP, 1% crit ≈ 8 SP in TPS value, so a 28 SP neck ≈ 5-6% crit worth of TPS at MC/BWL gear levels; raw SP stacking remains the single most powerful threat stat — 25% crit (fully-buffed T3.5/T2.5) is noticeably better than 18% (T3) but never at the cost of SP — [[d:paladin#1480245364299927552]], [[d:paladin#1480245763958378516]] (Grey). Separately, 9 defense ≈ 1% crit — [[d:paladin#1481493128954511391]] (Grey); parry rating's cost-per-percent was reduced to 13.35 in patch 1.17 — [[d:paladin#1481499627252613190]] (Grey, citing a Turtle forum post).
- **[Ret]** SoC proc-chance formula: 100 × 7 × SwingSpeed / 60 (i.e., a flat percentage stays constant regardless of speed changes from haste — haste just gives more attacks/rolls, not a higher per-swing chance); if the SoC libram adds 2 PPM (from base 7 to 9, or 9 to 11), that's roughly a 70% proc chance per auto with sufficiently fast weapons — [[d:paladin#1482537964130013314]], [[d:paladin#1482538141129904128]], [[d:paladin#1482538286613401873]], [[d:paladin#1482538939800879176]] (Mithreon).
- **[Prot]** Weapon-speed tradeoff for tanks: faster weapons are noticeably better for JoW mana regen; slower weapons are noticeably better for Reckoning/SoR/Windfury threat; in practice wear whichever 1H has the most spellpower, usually landing around 1.8-2.2 speed — [[d:paladin#1480432819544264754]] (Grey).
- **[Ret]** Weapon-skill/hit sourcing: Human racial gives +3 sword/mace skill, +3 more from a talent (Precision); a Fray Island quest (Ratchet's "Ancient Warfare" text turn-in, ~250g) grants further weapon skill, worth ~1% hit per 5 skill — combined this can get a Human ret to +6% hit from skill alone before gear — [[d:paladin#1481676735404572833]], [[d:paladin#1481676788043354319]], [[d:paladin#1481676857395904672]], [[d:paladin#1481676967186006076]].
- **[Ret]** Spellcrit's mechanical relevance is limited: it only affects Ashbringer's proc, Exorcism, and Holy Wrath — [[d:paladin#1482263530567635096]] (Mithreon).
- **[Ret]** Haste has hard diminishing returns from swing-timer granularity: extra haste only matters if it produces a full additional swing over a fight's duration; partial fractions of a swing (e.g. 3/4 of a swing remaining) are wasted, so ideal haste should be tuned per boss-fight length via trinkets/consumables rather than gear/enchants alone — [[d:paladin#1482266634373824594]] through [[d:paladin#1482270680807374975]] (Mithreon).
- **[Ret]** Item-budget/enchant value calculator built by Destroses and heavily modified by Grey for evaluating gear tradeoffs (weight^1.5 formula per stat, with defense converting at roughly 2.4:1) — [[d:paladin#1481492714443772117]], [[d:paladin#1481492942148599868]], [[d:paladin#1482846252243615836]].
- **[Contested]** Post-1.18.1 Holy Strike SP coefficient of 71% (vs weapon damage) is called "a mistake" by Grey — 73% would give a "clean" 100% after talents vs. 71%'s awkward 97.6% — [[d:paladin#1483015273878523935]], [[d:paladin#1483015489126011021]], [[d:paladin#1483015667824197755]].

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[Prot]** T2.5 vs T3 vs T3.5 tank-set tradeoffs: T2.5 is the best threat set (more crit/SP) but is "squishy" with poor avoidance; T3 has by far the best raw eHP/block-value profile and is BiS specifically for fights with frequent big hits relative to max HP (BWL drakes, Chromaggus, Broodlord, Twins, Patchwerk, Sapphiron, Thaddius, KT, Rupturan, Meph) and fights where block value/eHP beat avoidance (Maexxna, Loatheb, Four Horsemen); T3.5 is already BiS overall with the heaviest stats — [[d:paladin#1481047236501569690]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1480649997195284492]] (Ash: T2.5 "kind bad now," T3 "more survivable," T3.5 "already BIS").
- **[Prot/Ret]** "T2.5 ret = T2.5 prot" in stats (same stamina, armor, JoR-bonus set piece) — the only difference is prot's version trades some strength for slightly more SP/avoidance — [[d:paladin#1481613534625403033]], [[d:paladin#1481613746991530004]], [[d:paladin#1481613868357910619]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot]** Mixing set pieces: keep 2 T2.5/T3.5 pieces (wrist+belt) for the 2-set bonus even when running mostly T3, since wrist is otherwise a "dead" itemization slot — [[d:paladin#1480313978910413050]] (angels1m/Arle math on the swap, +2% hit/1% crit/+3% crit bonus vs the lost stamina).
- **[Prot]** Loot priority in progression guilds: BiS generally goes to the most vital raid players first (e.g. Naxx's Desecration going to progression-critical tanks before regular tanks) — [[d:paladin#1481395271484182728]], [[d:paladin#1481395832488984706]] (Mithreon).
- **[Prot, weapons]** Thunderfury remains BiS overall for prot: best average threat AND a large mitigation increase (~20% reduced damage taken from boss auto-attacks); Desecration/SP weapons are better specifically for maximizing minimum-opener damage (important when an offtank without TF would otherwise lose the initial threat race) — [[d:paladin#1480850790955618345]] (Grey).
- **[Prot, weapons]** Wraith Blade vs End of Dreams (EoD) post-1.18.1: Wraith Blade wins by a small margin (~0.4% lower opening burst / ~30 TPS for EoD per Grey's manual math); an independent theorycraft sim (Proph) found a larger gap of 1.8-2.5% higher TPS for Wraith Blade, more pronounced at lower gear levels — both agree the difference is "marginal," not "pool noodle" as one poster claimed — [[d:paladin#1481411198103584999]], [[d:paladin#1481412836600053864]], [[d:paladin#1481414109240234075]], [[d:paladin#1481414375783792760]].
- **[Prot]** Comet Signaller vs Desecration vs Thunderfury vs Wraith Blade DPS-scenario numbers from a community "ChatGPT sim" (treated skeptically) both pre- and post-1.18.1 HS/weapon-dps changes were posted for comparison — [[d:paladin#1481396294898683944]] (IronResolve).
- **[Ret]** T0.5 (Soulforge) 4-piece bonus procs "a lot" — tested empirically at ~3 PPM regardless of weapon speed (3.6-speed and 1.5-speed weapons converged near 3 PPM over 5-minute tests); however SoR ("rider" attacks) does NOT double-proc it the way SoC does, nor does it proc Bonechopper's double-wound effect, likely a deliberate targeted change — [[d:paladin#1479982597420482764]] through [[d:paladin#1480037219854188544]], [[d:paladin#1480483582798467245]] (Destroses/PerchyBrown testing).

- **[Prot]** Ring enchant: 6 stamina is generally the most valuable ring enchant; some (Grey) prefer 3-all-stats for the extra avoidance/crit/int; 5 SP is BiS for pure threat-pushing but the difference is rarely noticeable — [[d:paladin#1479149537040929063]] (Grey).
- **[Necrodin/Ret]** Shoulder enchant (ZG) for BWL-phase necrodin: SP is covered by the T0.5 4-piece bonus, so favor stamina since paladins don't typically go for mitigation gearing until late AQ40 — [[d:paladin#1480351899470397625]] (Mithreon).
- **[PvP]** Weapon enchant tradeoff: for prot, the Crusader proc's Strength buff is "basically a dead stat," so 30 SP is strictly better; for ret PvP burst, 100 Str from Crusader is strong but needs proc uptime to matter, and 30 SP is still generally favored as more consistent — [[d:paladin#1482087360437944380]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1482413538004697225]] (Mithreon).
- **[PvP, Ret]** Icy Chill weapon enchant used by at least one top ret PvPer (Jesse Potter, on "DEoI") despite being a meme-adjacent pick — [[d:paladin#1481013221509959712]], [[d:paladin#1481046222193168538]].

- **[Farming]** Vampirism weapon enchant on tank gear is worth it specifically for AoE dungeon farming (Stratholme/HFQ) when paired with an FRD-style build, but not recommended if you actually tank raids on that set — [[d:paladin#1482398177003835677]], [[d:paladin#1482398604898336868]] (Iseut).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Ret]** Nightfall (the weapon with a raid-wide "Vulnerability" damage-taken debuff) is a major raid-DPS tool: on one example fight it contributed 622k total raid damage in a single pull; the recommendation from multiple experienced players is to rotate the carrier role across several support-capable players (surv hunters, enh shamans, ret paladins, offtanks) rather than pigeonholing one person, since forcing one player to do it every week burns them out and — in loot-council guilds focused on parses — the carrier is rarely prioritized for upgrades — [[d:paladin#1481765165325353053]] (theoloras/warkrak), [[d:paladin#1481767255548104786]], [[d:paladin#1481767392727273634]], [[d:paladin#1481769022738399335]], [[d:paladin#1481770084077932695]] (30/Kyklutch/Iseut). In a caster-heavy raid, an enh shaman generally benefits more from carrying Nightfall than a ret paladin, assuming equal player skill — [[d:paladin#1481765604074459217]], [[d:paladin#1481765761830752266]] (Iseut). A dedicated third-party log site/tool can isolate and label Nightfall's specific damage contribution separately from a player's raw personal DPS — [[d:paladin#1481766137531338753]], [[d:paladin#1481766243529916668]], [[d:paladin#1481775179460771941]].
- **[Prot]** JoW (Judgement of Wisdom) mana-regen synergy: SoR/JoR triggers JoW, and spellstrike procs also trigger JoW — cited as the core reason prot's mana sustain is workable at all, distinct from some other private servers' paladin designs — [[d:paladin#1482439117626409174]], [[d:paladin#1482439280839229501]] (theoloras/warkrak).
- **[General]** Reckoning was reworked from classic: current tooltip/patch text is "30% chance upon being missed to gain an extra attack" — [[d:paladin#1482089831206096936]] (quoted by Xiyanzhe). It behaves like an improved Parry Haste: it finishes your current swing timer and queues the next attack rather than literally stacking bonus attacks like vanilla Reckoning; it isn't strictly melee-range-gated (can trigger off blocking a ranged attack) though mounted-triggering is unconfirmed by direct testing — [[d:paladin#1482499405956382971]], [[d:paladin#1482499995159498802]], [[d:paladin#1482500079372996759]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1482472271422427348]] (Mithreon: "It just finishes your current swing and queues the next").
- **[Design/contested]** Seal-twisting is confirmed removed/impossible in the current implementation; a long argument (2026-03-13) pits nostalgia for classic seal-twisting and stacking Reckoning (Xiyanzhe, calling the current class "gutted") against the majority view (Jesse Potter, Silverhand, Arle) that twisting was largely a netcode-batching exploit not worth balancing around, and that current-era Turtle Paladin's instant-cast, less RNG-dependent kit is the intended design direction — [[d:paladin#1482076144244101264]] through [[d:paladin#1482104974929432766]] and [[d:paladin#1482084048804511917]] (Silverhand: "trying to balance a class around dogshit netcode (seal twisting) is genuine ogre behaviour").
- **[Prot]** Exorcism's threat-reduction nerf remains active even with Righteous Fury up (unlike Vengeance, which is exempted from its own threat penalty under Righteous Fury) — [[d:paladin#1479918368470470849]], [[d:paladin#1479920653040750843]] (Light/Silverhand).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Design]** The paladin "class call" mechanic on Nefarian is being reworked — Lhian confirms the old version was "badly designed," new icons suggest a "negative blessings" debuff cast onto the raid (unconfirmed speculation) — [[d:paladin#1481436037984157797]] (Lhian), [[d:paladin#1481572971289120768]] (Destroses guess).
- **[Prot]** Naked/unbuffed baseline: a completely naked, unbuffed prot paladin can hold threat off someone doing ~1,000 DPS — [[d:paladin#1482781726324297728]] (Grey).
- **[Design/contested]** Class-design flaw noted by Grey: other tanks have redundant threat generation if one attack misses (bear has a second attack option, shaman has ~8 possible openers, warrior has strong unglanceable autos as backup), but "if paladin misses HS, all we have is JoR" — a single miss can meaningfully cost a paladin tank a mob to a DPS — [[d:paladin#1482749421589368886]] (Grey).
- **[Prot]** A generic tank consumable checklist (posted by a shaman, cross-referenced by prot players) recommends Scorpok Assay over "roids"/Long Juice Cocktail (which don't stack with Zanza's); see Consumables section for full stacking caveats — [[d:paladin#1482455676935606362]] (Zappinu), [[d:paladin#1482455817251721530]] (Dodging).
- **[Prot, Naxx]** Avoidance-cap edge cases: Patchwerk soak duty only needs armor and stamina (not avoidance); avoidance is more valuable for Sapphiron, KT (Four Horsemen), and Thaddius progression — [[d:paladin#1481325987525558366]] (Achmodeus asking), [[d:paladin#1481326252962091282]], [[d:paladin#1481326386420650186]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot, BWL]** Running a Titans (health) flask is considered a baseline expectation for a serious progression tank in BWL; using an SP flask instead is framed by Grey as "a huge investment" of 150 SP specifically for paladins/shamans (since warriors/druids simply have an empty slot if not using Titans, with no opportunity cost besides gold), while Arle argues it's not that large an investment once stats plateau and good healers reduce risk — an open disagreement, not resolved — [[d:paladin#1482013232515256350]], [[d:paladin#1482746304462459025]], [[d:paladin#1482746530099363966]], [[d:paladin#1482746739797790961]].
- **[Prot, BWL]** Broodlord's Mortal Strike hits for ~5,500-6,000 plus a ~1,000-1,500 auto and Blastwave, none of it affected by defense — [[d:paladin#1482006928174481488]], [[d:paladin#1482006982457298999]] (Grey).
- **[Prot]** Full example threat/mitigation gear sets for K40/Naxx tanking were shared as screenshots by Grey and angels1m, illustrating SP/crit-stacked "full threatmaxx" setups vs. more balanced setups — [[d:paladin#1482778000545284106]], [[d:paladin#1482777924724850789]] (images; stat philosophy already covered in the Stats section above).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Mending Light (the Holy Strike-linked AoE heal) only hits up to 5 additional targets within a limited radius around the paladin during melee-range Holy Strike casts — "if you holy strike 3 steps too far in a direction, you won't hit everyone possible" — [[d:paladin#1479412476926885929]] (Jesse Potter), extensive real-log confusion about why a raid full of melee wasn't getting expected 1:5 HS:Mending-Light ratios — [[d:paladin#1479410369788252182]] through [[d:paladin#1479418614376300554]] (Beardozer/Mithreon).
- **[Holy]** Mending Light is a smart heal: it will not fire on undamaged targets — instead it refunds mana and deals strike damage to the mob if nobody nearby needs healing — [[d:paladin#1479415903203491956]], [[d:paladin#1479416125493350586]] (Mithreon).
- **[Holy]** Holy Strike's healing/Holy Might buff application goes out even if the strike itself misses, dodges, or parries, and even through Silence — [[d:paladin#1479418525092282468]], [[d:paladin#1479418539931861103]] (Jesse Potter/Mithreon).
- **[Holy]** Ironclad talent: converts a percentage of armor from gear (not from Devotion Aura's added armor) into healing bonus — 1 point = 1% of gear armor, 2 points = 2%; a reported "bug" where 1pt and 2pt gave the same healing bonus number was traced to the Better Character Stats addon's display, not an actual talent bug; the tooltip is being clarified in 1.18.1 to explicitly say "only armor from items" — [[d:paladin#1481753498126385173]] through [[d:paladin#1481759917378371766]] (Moorea/Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1482689843719966833]], [[d:paladin#1482690572643733504]] (lionheart6957/Seviry).
- **[1.18.1 change, Holy]** Daybreak (crit-to-heal-bonus conversion) is being nerfed: currently worth ~15 (effective heal value) in BWL-phase gear, expected to drop to ~10-12 post-patch — [[d:paladin#1481995116087607476]], [[d:paladin#1481996625403576341]] (Mithreon).
- **[Holy]** Returning-player build: 40/0/11, holy-striking in melee to AoE-heal and apply Daybreak, filling with Flash of Light at range when melee isn't safe — [[d:paladin#1482306403606593597]], [[d:paladin#1482306779936198801]] (Destroses); default to ranged FoL spam on fights where melee isn't possible, which is only a handful of fights — [[d:paladin#1482310568739209408]], [[d:paladin#1482310661760356354]] (gastro).
- **[Holy]** Spiritual Focus (pushback resistance) provides only 70% at 2/2; Concentration Aura provides the remaining 30% needed for full pushback immunity — [[d:paladin#1481035898484424846]], [[d:paladin#1481036193402716170]] (Moorea/Arle).
- **[Gear]** Verdant Dreamer's Breastplate vs. Reed Worn Tunic for holy: the mana-regen-on-cast benefit of Verdant Dreamer's is "super small" even with a Human's +5% racial bonus, but considered a fine pickup regardless — [[d:paladin#1482969383054737468]] (woobie).
- **[Gear, fresh 60 holy]** Alternatives to the Red Dragonscale set exist and are "better," alongside Dawnbringer Shoulders — [[d:paladin#1479528732795605286]], [[d:paladin#1479533372844933151]] (Numi).

## PvP
- **[General]** PvP success for paladins is framed around utility and timing rather than raw burst: heal/cleanse/Freedom allies, smart BoP usage, playing around cooldown burst windows, and a "bubble out then reengage" pattern — [[d:paladin#1481664096163074098]] (Dodging), [[d:paladin#1481664216443392121]] (Dodging: "heal cleanse freedom your friends use bop smart").
- **[Ret PvP]** Core kit: SoC with stam/SP/crit stats, capitalizing on Judgement burst windows, and a big two-hander — [[d:paladin#1481664240270970996]] (Mithreon).
- **[Holy PvP]** Needs 6k+ HP; plays either as melee harassment (using CS resets off Shock) or ranged healing with target prioritization; melee classes that can't catch a kiting holy paladin deal no damage to them — [[d:paladin#1481664712130166965]], [[d:paladin#1481664792728174644]], [[d:paladin#1481664931005857886]] (Mithreon).
- **[PvP, gear]** For pure PvP DPS, T2.5/T3.5 3-set combos (rather than full 5-set) pair well with the Judgement-execute-style libram; PvP/conquest gear is expected to outstat and replace T-gear in PvP contexts next patch due to stamina — [[d:paladin#1481665621958725803]], [[d:paladin#1481719067814662245]] (Jesse Potter/Silverhand). Example PvP gear set (image) shared by Jesse Potter as a work-in-progress — [[d:paladin#1481666333220536423]].
- **[PvP, gear, new tier]** New PvP legs example (see Gear section image transcription): "Veteran's Lamellar Legplates," stam/int/agi/str-heavy plate with +2% crit and +21 spelldmg/healing — [[d:paladin#1482026399442407464]]. New PvP tank-oriented tier is stat-heavy (25% more stam than T3.5, 10% more than T3, +35 more on the 6-piece) but is expected to be worse on both threat and mitigation than true PvE tank gear — [[d:paladin#1481379905722908724]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1481318835494588417]] (angels1m: "this gear 100% loses in threat").
- **[1.18.1 change, PvP]** Judgement of Justice becomes a taunt (confirmed by Lhian) — big requested ret PvP utility change, though some argue current Judgement's 10-yard range (vs. TBC's 15) and Pursuit of Justice's 8% (vs. TBC's 15%) move speed keep it from being "OP" — [[d:paladin#1481428000867356684]] (Lhian), [[d:paladin#1481547155540807691]] (Excinerus), [[d:paladin#1482622536725565470]] (Excinerus detailed range/speed comparison).
