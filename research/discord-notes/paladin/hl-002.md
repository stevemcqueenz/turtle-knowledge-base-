## Leveling
- **[Ret→Prot leveling]** Talent-calculator links by level (verbatim), Ret first then swapping to Prot at 20 (1g respec, pick up a shield): L1-19 Ret `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=FAAo--`; L20 Prot swap `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=-AoYCB-`; L30 `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=-AoYFZYAAB-`; L40 `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=AoQ-AoYFZYAAZ-`; L50 `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=AoaAZBAQ-AoYFZYAAZ-` — [[d:paladin#1421216725705298042]] (single source).
- **[Prot leveling]** Suggested leveling-dungeon tank routine: tank at the dungeon's level cap (e.g. Deadmines around level 24); keep Ret Aura and Righteous Fury up; pre-buff Seal of Wisdom and Holy Shield before pulling; open with Holy Strike > Judgement > rebuff Seal of Wisdom; drop Consecration if more than one mob is up and rebuff Holy Shield as needed; set up unit-frame click-cast macros so Blessing of Protection can be thrown on healers/mages who pull aggro; carry an Engineering item or Ez-Throw dynamite for a ranged pull option; use a sub-2.0-speed main-hand weapon for faster mana return from Judgement/Seal of Wisdom (Spellstrike enchant helps) — [[d:paladin#1421451485169975356]] (single source).
- **[Prot/Holy hybrid leveling]** Recommended to level Protection until the capstone talent, then move into Holy for Spiritual Focus; grab Holy Shield as early as possible; self-healing with no pushback (paired with Concentration Aura) is very strong for solo leveling and for backing up undergeared healers in dungeons — [[d:paladin#1422234838219489370]] (single source).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Ret]** Suggested single-target rotation: Judgement of Wisdom if no other paladin is providing it (else Judgement of the Crusader); swap libram from a Fervor-type libram if desired (any libram is fine — it's mostly a stat boost since the Judgement effect is likely already on the boss from another paladin); Crusader Strike + Seal of Command + auto-attack; weave in Holy Strike for a Holy Might proc once the Crusader weapon-enchant has procced; Crusader Strike until Holy Might falls off; Judgement on cooldown; reapply Holy Might roughly every 4th strike — [[d:paladin#1422448983334191125]] (+1 reaction).
- **[Ret]** "Spellret" differs from AP Ret mainly in gearing/rotation emphasis: favor proc-based weapons (they scale off spell power), weight Consecration higher in the priority, and use Seal of Righteousness over Seal of Command; at higher gear levels, Holy Strike is dropped from the rotation entirely — [[d:paladin#1421379000122609735]] (single source).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot, leveling]** When facing more than one mob while tanking, drop Consecration and rebuff Holy Shield as needed to hold aggro on the group — [[d:paladin#1421451485169975356]] (single source).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General]** Exact level-60 paladin spell-crit formula (sourced from vmangos core code, `Player.cpp` line 5388): `crit% = 3.7 + Int / (14.77 + 0.65 * level)`; at level 60 this works out to roughly 53.77 Intellect per 1% spell crit — [[d:paladin#1421396523823927376]] (single source, cites server source code).
- **[Prot, leveling]** For mana-starved leveling/tanking, prefer a main-hand weapon with sub-2.0 speed to maximize mana return ticks from Judgement of Wisdom / Seal of Wisdom; the Spellstrike weapon enchant helps this further — [[d:paladin#1421451485169975356]] (single source).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[Any spec, trinket mechanics]** Sigil of Ancient Accord (toy) scales with spell power at roughly 16.6% spell-power scaling per the tester's math; it procs off Vengeance, off "bonus damage" items (rings, neck, lantern-type items), and off Holy damage from the T2 tier set's 3-piece bonus; it also procs off Exorcism and off the *first tick only* of Consecration — [[d:paladin#1420705851223572593]], [[d:paladin#1420711187577831434]] (single source, self-tested).
- **[Prot]** Weapon/trinket recommendations for a prot paladin waiting on a Thunderfury roll: Azuresong Blade is a strong pick if not yet exalted with Keepers of Time; an engineering-crafted shield is very strong for trash and for MC bosses Garr/Harbinger/Majordomo plus a newer "lizard boss" (AoE threat), for its stats and a minor reflect-damage effect (get the schematic from a guildmate if unclaimed); a fire-on-hit ring is good against non-fire-immune targets as another on-hit source for Judgement of Wisdom mana return / Vengeance generation (if Veng-Prot); the libram "from the Dwarf Ghost man" is strong in execute phase — its damage is separate, can crit, and scales, giving good threat against a boss's execute phase despite Judgement's 8-10s cooldown — [[d:paladin#1422957111372943360]] (single source, responding to [[d:paladin#1422834910854643783]]).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Any spec]** Shadow Oil and Frost Oil do not proc the Sigil of Ancient Accord toy, nor do weapon-proc effects like Thunderfury or Ashbringer, nor do standard weapon enchants including Fiery Blaze — [[d:paladin#1420711187577831434]] (single source).
- **[Prot, leveling]** Spellstrike weapon enchant helps mana regen from Judgement/Seal of Wisdom when paired with a fast main-hand — [[d:paladin#1421451485169975356]] (single source).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Any spec, PvP]** Blessing of Freedom removes snares and slows; Blessing of Sacrifice breaks soft CC (Polymorph, Blind, Scatter Shot, Seduce, etc.) by transferring the damage that ends it; Cleanse removes magic, disease, and poison effects; Blessing of Protection clears physical CC such as Sap and Rocket Helm (and even physical-ability-based fears); Divine Shield removes everything, including curses — [[d:paladin#1423030856326451230]] (single source).
- **[Ret, PvP]** Paladin's core PvP weakness is lacking a gap closer, forcing it to absorb the opponent's opening burst/CC before it can respond; Reckoning is framed as the payoff for surviving that window — accumulated charges convert into a large burst once the paladin closes or breaks free — [[d:paladin#1423030856326451230]] (single source).

## PvP
- **[Any spec]** Paladin's CC-break/immunity kit — Freedom (snare/slow removal), Sacrifice (soft-CC break via damage transfer), Cleanse (magic/disease/poison), Protection (physical CC removal), Divine Shield (removes everything incl. curses) — plus trinket/consumable options: Mithril Insignia grants 30s fear immunity, Free Action Potion grants 30s stun immunity; core weakness remains no gap closer, so the paladin eats the opening exchange before it can act, with Reckoning rewarding that patience once it closes — [[d:paladin#1423030856326451230]] (single source).
- **[Ret]** Turtle's Retribution redesign is criticized as forced/ramp-up-focused for BG balance reasons, leaving Ret without mobility or a gap closer; poster speculates the main PvP pressure tool available is landing fast Seal of Command procs plus a Blessing of Freedom assist for teammates, since a mage or rogue can otherwise lock the paladin down before it reaches the fight — [[d:paladin#1423068561882091602]], [[d:paladin#1423071437266358272]] (single source, opinion).
- **[Paladin vs. Shaman utility, PvP]** Contested framing of Paladin's remaining PvP value relative to Shaman totems (single-target guaranteed buffs/dispel/immunity vs. positional, contested totem buffs); see Spec viability section for both sides — [[d:paladin#1420442981378424998]], [[d:paladin#1420452511369789501]], [[d:paladin#1423030856326451230]].
