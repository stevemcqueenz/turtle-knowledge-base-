## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
(All entries below are pre-1.18.1; they document the CC2 rework and its follow-up balance patches, which are the most recent confirmed Paladin mechanics in this chunk.)
- **[All specs]** CC2 (~2024-11-01) gave every spec two instant melee attacks on one shared 6s cooldown: Crusader Strike (physical, procs "Zeal" stacks giving haste/block) and Holy Strike (holy damage, procs "Holy Might", threat, and — for Holy — an AoE heal and Holy Shock cooldown reset). This was the single most contested design of the whole period: called "clunky"/"goofy" by many — [[d:paladin#1302272388712628225]] (+9), [[d:paladin#1302515950746337290]] (JakeMF), request to revert [[d:paladin#1311096192754651136]]; others found the new rotation more engaging than pure CS-spam — [[d:paladin#1302382333315252296]] (+3), [[d:paladin#1303120671831883826]].
- **[Holy]** Holy Shock lost its damage component entirely and became heal-only; its old self-reset chance (5% + healing power/100) was proposed for removal in favor of Crusader-Strike-only resets — [[d:paladin#1304051463265325096]] (LHK). Community repeatedly asked for the damage component back ("Shockadin") without success as of 2025-02-14 — [[d:paladin#1305757812521046099]] (+4), [[d:paladin#1327364474323206225]].
- **[Holy]** Illumination was nerfed from a flat 60% mana-on-crit-refund down a scale described as "12/24/36/48/60%" (5 talent points) instead of the old flat value; a 3-point 20/40/60% alternative was suggested but not adopted in-chunk — [[d:paladin#1304067941611999282]] (Rerolladin), confirmed as a major complaint by [[d:paladin#1307147091310678096]] (+1).
- **[Holy]** Ironclad (2-point talent) lets Holy wear plate/shield with spell power effectively (a long-requested fix to "cloth spelladin healer" itemization); very well received — [[d:paladin#1313314220556685383]] (Shokisan, "finally... don't you dare say ironclad isn't balanced"), [[d:paladin#1317151966341759028]] (RIP holyprot: "awesome... helps move hpal away from teal dress").
- **[Prot]** Major nerf patch 2024-12-20 hit Protection hard: Holy Shield cooldown 10s→12s, spell-power scaling 33%→15%, threat 45%→30%; Exorcism threat −70%; Ret/Prot T3 6-piece bonus reduced to "only reduces Exorcism cooldown"; Righteous Strikes (Holy Strike bonus threat) cut from 10/20/30/40/50% to 3/6/9/12/15% — verbatim patch text [[d:paladin#1319640578996179016]] (Stundza), reaction [[d:paladin#1319537823455645806]].
- **[Prot]** After heavy backlash, Lhian reverted the Holy Shield CD back to 10s and raised Holy Strike's threat modifier the same day — [[d:paladin#1319684785672687716]] (+44); the Exorcism threat nerf and Shadow/Frost Oil SP-scaling removal were NOT reverted and remained sore points through Feb 2025 — [[d:paladin#1320464999663144991]] (Verdict), [[d:paladin#1326259711447793684]] (Istenneveben, still asking in Jan 2025).
- **[Ret]** Crusader Strike was normalized to a fixed weapon speed (penalizing slow weapons), its double-dip with Vengeance was fixed, and its base damage/Vengeful-(formerly Crusading-)Strikes talent were further nerfed in the same Dec-20 patch — [[d:paladin#1320551770237436026]] (Adamant), [[d:paladin#1319631168588742666]] (Alban itemized list of nerfs).
- **[Ret]** Shadow Oil's spell-power scaling was removed entirely, killing "Spelladin" (full-SP Ret/Hybrid) viability; repeatedly requested to be reverted or ICD-gated instead, unresolved as of 2025-02-14 — [[d:paladin#1320551770237436026]], [[d:paladin#1326259711447793684]], [[d:paladin#1313625050544607334]] ("Spelladin is beaten into pulp... melee Holy is a slave to T1").
- **[Weapon skill]** Server-wide (not paladin-specific but affects hit/threat planning): weapon skill formula changed to linear scaling, the old 305 "soft cap" was removed, hit suppression vs 3-level-higher mobs moved the effective hit cap from 9% to 8%, and racial weapon-skill bonuses were reduced from +5 to +3 — forum link and summary [[d:paladin#1307822046620680212]] (theoloras/warkrak, 2024-11-18).
- **[Gear]** Late-Jan-2025 balance pass adjusted specific tier pieces: Judgement (Prot) Handguards swapped 1% Physical Hit→1% Block; Judgement Greaves Str 9→14, Block 2%→1%, Def 7→5; Avenger's Helmet lost 1% Physical Hit; Redemption (Ret) Pauldrons 25 Int→Strength; Redemption Bindings Haste 2%→1%; Redemption Gauntlets 16 Agi→Stamina; Redemption Leggings +18 Stam, Agi 26→17, Int 23→15; Redemption Sabatons +20 Str/+18 Stam, removed 60 AP; Redemption Greaves SP 12→18, removed 14 Block Value — [[d:paladin#1333804733546237952]] (Iseut, verbatim changelog).
- **[T1 Ret]** The T1 Ret 3-piece set bonus was fixed to correctly buff Judgement of Righteousness and Judgement of Command as intended (was previously only affecting one/neither); Lhian said the JoR/JoC spell-power coefficient would be increased to compensate — [[d:paladin#1328870966448619550]] (dusty), [[d:paladin#1328882105408819333]] (Lhian).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret]** Post-CC2, AP/Hybrid Ret is widely reported as the strongest and most reliable Paladin spec for raiding; full "Spelladin" (pure spell-power Ret) is considered dead/non-viable after the Shadow Oil SP-scaling removal and SoR nerfs — [[d:paladin#1314555143458652190]] (KEANU), [[d:paladin#1313625050544607334]], contested by Istenneveben who kept arguing Spelladin/hybrid-AoE has untapped potential — [[d:paladin#1312881977258545322]].
- **[Prot]** Prot tanking is judged the weakest of the three "real" MT options (Warrior, Bear, Prot Paladin): lowest threat, most restrictive gearing (near-full BiS needed to be worth bringing), lowest HP, least role flexibility — [[d:paladin#1339714435861975090]] (Grey, single-source but detailed and unrebutted at chunk's end); earlier in the cycle (pre Dec-20 nerf) Prot was considered *over*tuned on threat/damage — [[d:paladin#1315630237865742447]] (Verdict), [[d:paladin#1319622857336291399]] (solis).
- **[Holy]** Melee "Blessed Strikes" Holy healing (using Crusader/Holy Strike + Holy Shock resets in melee) is repeatedly assessed as mathematically incapable of beating ranged Flash-of-Light spam over a full raid — "mathematically constrained... to 1/4 the healing of flash of light spamming" [[d:paladin#1317140214086635558]] (RIP holyprot); situationally useful boss-by-boss rather than as a full-raid strategy — [[d:paladin#1323288964660199499]] (LHK), [[d:paladin#1320701298357698631]] (Thalrinel).
- **[Holy]** Holy brings comparatively little unique raid utility versus Shamans (totems), Priests (Enlighten), Druids (brez/innervate); Daybreak (5% HP on target) seen as weak by comparison — [[d:paladin#1309468890413142077]] (LHK), [[d:paladin#1311136573697425419]].
- **[Leveling]** Leveling as Holy is reported as viable and very sustainable (near-zero downtime, mana never an issue via Seal of Wisdom crits) but slower kill speed than Ret/Prot — [[d:paladin#1319034136509284424]], [[d:paladin#1319039144998998087]] (Sinister Minister, HC-verified).
- **[Leveling]** Leveling Prot is now recommended over Ret by several posters because it's very survivable and virtually guarantees dungeon-tank invites; Sanctuary/Holy Shield were moved earlier in the tree and Seal of Wisdom was significantly buffed and made available at level 18 — [[d:paladin#1327120310540959816]] (Drekalo).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Melee Holy leveling: gear like a priest (1h+shield, Int/SP) until Ironclad, then mix cloth/plate; put on Seal of Wisdom and spam Holy Strike whenever below ~40% mana to refill (never need to sit and drink in dungeons); use Flash of Light to top a tank, Holy Light (1.5s with the CD-reduction talent) if someone gets chunked — [[d:paladin#1319035555194998884]], [[d:paladin#1319038448681746432]], [[d:paladin#1319039144998998087]] (Sinister Minister, HC).
- **[Prot leveling]** As of CC2, Prot is recommended for leveling because Wisdom got "giga boosted" and is trainable earlier (level 18) and Sanctuary/Holy Shield moved to a lower tier; Holy Strike does as much damage as Crusader Strike with a 2H weapon until higher level; example: level 37 Prot can AoE 5 same-level mobs for ~2k XP in 30–40s using rested XP — [[d:paladin#1327120310540959816]] (Drekalo).
- **[Dual-set leveling route]** Go Ret first for Str/Stam (solo) and Str/Agi (dungeon) gear up to level 30 (Seal of Command), then pick up Holy Judgement (→ level 41); at that point you can still tank/heal/DPS any dungeon on the same build; only specialize further into Holy or Prot after Stockade/BRD-tier dungeons — [[d:paladin#1336838893131137176]] (Drekalo).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Ret, post-CC2]** Recommended ST rotation on bosses: keep Judgement of Wisdom/Crusader up + Repentance; spam Crusader Strike + Judgement of Command, timing so it doesn't overlap your swing timer if Windfury is present (doesn't matter if not); on long fights, weave a Holy Strike roughly every 20s to keep Holy Might up (skip on trash); add Exorcism vs undead and Consecration if mana allows — [[d:paladin#1316734246576066631]] (Anborn, "after CC2").
- **[Prot, ST]** Holy Strike for the 20% Str buff (Holy Might) when convenient; Crusader Strike priority on cooldown only if it lines up with your (Windfury) swing timer; Judgement on cooldown when it won't clip your swing; Exorcism and Consecration on cooldown; use Repentance even though its damage credits the tank — [[d:paladin#1315736805055660152]] (Lightbourne).
- **[Dungeon tank, simple]** Basic leveling/dungeon tank loop: apply Seal of Righteousness or Command depending on weapon speed, Crusader Strike to 3 stacks of Zeal, keep Crusader-Striking, swap to Wisdom for mana as needed, Consecrate for 3+ mob packs — essentially a 3-button rotation even at 60 — [[d:paladin#1313192898027720714]] (Giuvanna).
- **[Dungeon opener]** Seal of Wisdom → Judgement → Seal of Righteousness → Consecration → Holy/Crusader Strike as opener on bosses; maintain SoR + Consecration afterward — [[d:paladin#1307425846109409402]] (Theslothone, low-level).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot AoE]** Pre-cast Holy Strike → Consecrate → Judge Wisdom → keep up Wisdom/Light/SoR (≈90% of aggro comes from Consecrate) → Holy Strike again; drop SoR "nukes" on low-threat adds; spam Holy Strike/Consecrate since a 5+ mob pack will usually eat a charge somewhere — [[d:paladin#1318893821899309108]] (saschabat).
- **[Leveling AoE]** Bulwark of Righteousness helps AoE grinding survivability even though not mandatory; recommended for reliably pulling/killing bigger packs while leveling — [[d:paladin#1328381614782152867]] (dusty).
- **[Chow/wolf-pack kite (Deadmines-style trash)]** Kite a wolf pack (the "chows") in a circle around a grated floor opening; the paladin (Holy spec) spams Greater Blessing of Kings on warriors (most numerous class, counts hunter pets too), keeps Righteous Fury up, must NOT have Blessing of Salvation on, and drops Consecrate on cooldown for the pack to run through; a Ret paladin should not do this role due to Vengeance's threat reduction — [[d:paladin#1312830044091056168]] (RIP holyprot).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** General stat priority: Spell Power first, then 8% hit, then Agility/Crit (whichever is the bigger value on the item); if using Thunderfury + Shadow Oil, prioritize Spell Hit then Spell Crit; Attack Speed/Spell Hit/Crit are otherwise minor tuning stats — [[d:paladin#1305687800565469268]] (Verdict).
- **[Ret]** Crit is uniquely valuable because it's the only stat that boosts *all* Paladin damaging abilities (SP boosts part of the kit, Str/AP another part, haste only autos and SoC) — [[d:paladin#1334102874699075695]] (Regalius).
- **[Holy]** Crit (via Illumination mana-on-crit and Daybreak procs) is the #1 stat for sustain; stacking raw +healing beyond tier is less valuable than crit for sustained fights — [[d:paladin#1332072881789603881]] (dusty).
- **[SoR scaling history]** Seal of Righteousness's per-second spellpower coefficient was nerfed from vanilla's ~12.5%/sec (45% at 3.6 speed) down to ~4.16%/sec, then partially restored to ~8.3%/sec (30% at 3.6 speed) as of Jan 2025 — still considered underwhelming relative to Seal of Command — [[d:paladin#1329621370279170048]] (Grey).
- **[Defense]** Defense-rating math: subtract your flat level-60 base defense (300) from your total defense skill and multiply by 0.04 to get combined miss/dodge/parry/block % granted; e.g. 69 defense from gear = 2.76% each — [[d:paladin#1327016831382454293]] (Drekalo).
- **[Armor DR]** Armor has steep diminishing returns for plate tanks already at 7–8k armor: gaining a whole T1-chest-piece's worth of armor (735) only yields ~2% physical damage reduction at that range — [[d:paladin#1322696015614181460]] (saschabat, with linked spreadsheet).
- **[Attack table]** Turtle uses the standard vanilla attack-table order: Miss, Dodge, Parry, Glancing Blow (players only), Block, Critical Hit, Crushing Blow (mobs only), Ordinary Hit; only player yellow attacks use the two-roll system — [[d:paladin#1318133596653092926]] (Drekalo, unconfirmed by a dev but unrebutted).
- **[Weapon skill]** See "1.18.1 changes" — linear hit/glancing scaling, 8% effective hit cap vs +3-level mobs, no more 305 soft cap, racial weapon-skill bonus 5→3 — [[d:paladin#1307822046620680212]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Threat]** Prot's threat kit leans very heavily on Holy Strike; if the opening Holy Strike misses/is-defended, the tank has almost nothing else to fall back on (Holy Shield/Reckoning/Bulwark all require existing threat/incoming hits first) — [[d:paladin#1331462354998657075]] (dusty).
- **[Threat]** Righteous Fury only boosts holy-school threat, so physical Crusader Strike/auto-attack threat lags well behind Holy Strike/SoR/Consecration/Judgement for aggro purposes — implied by multiple Prot threat-rotation discussions, e.g. [[d:paladin#1313524338053480468]] (Nnorg question), [[d:paladin#1314506630435438592]] (Mayhem, "SoR/JoR and Holy Strike are the only two abilities that build threat for us on non-undeads").
- **[Mana]** Seal of Wisdom + Judgement of Wisdom is the primary mana-sustain tool for both Prot and Holy; tanks report effectively never needing to drink when running SoW on trash — [[d:paladin#1313529242205556806]] (Anborn), [[d:paladin#1313664266087432242]] (Nnorg).
- **[Buff management]** Because Seal duration is short and refreshing it costs a GCD, and Judgement-of-Light/Wisdom uptime competes with re-sealing, Holy players report significant "timer management" overhead (judge → must reseal or you accidentally judge the wrong seal) — [[d:paladin#1328760973904969823]] (Alban).
- **[Purge]** Seal of Command was made fully dispellable again post-CC2, making Shaman Purge spam a major PvP problem for Paladins (loses Seal + associated procs); repeatedly flagged to devs without in-chunk resolution — [[d:paladin#1319672073685172315]], [[d:paladin#1328498136972267531]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[Threat opener]** Example threat opener: pre-pull Seal of Command + Holy Shield, Judge SoC, Holy Strike, Consecrate, reseal SoC, Judge, Holy Strike, Consecrate, Holy Shield — [[d:paladin#1325965050548322394]] (Abuelo/Greiz), with open questions about whether Consecrate snapshots spell power at cast or updates live.
- **[Gear philosophy]** Prot gearing is a trade-off between avoidance/crush-immunity (favored by T2.5, at the cost of very low HP) and full T3 (safer, reaches avoidance cap, more HP, but noticeably less damage vs non-undead and lower mana pool) — [[d:paladin#1320383972206968842]] (Verdict).
- **[Redoubt bug]** Redoubt (block-chance talent, nerfed to 10% proc chance) reportedly stops proccing entirely after swapping from 1h+shield to a 2H weapon and back, until the character re-logs — [[d:paladin#1310120232093880362]] (Holidae, tested repeatedly at level 21).
- **[Class identity]** A recurring dev-adjacent design comment: Paladin/Bear are meant to be "glass cannon" high-threat/high-damage tanks while Warrior is the safe generalist — "Warriors are uncrushable with cooldowns... Bears... have all the armor... Paladins die" — [[d:paladin#1319551977755246605]] (Drekalo, paraphrasing dev intent).
- **[Comparative standing, end of chunk]** As of 2025-02-14, Prot Paladin is described as viable but the worst of the three main tanks — lowest threat, most restrictive gearing requirement (needs 5/5 T2.5, 8/8 T3, Thunderfury, and thousands of gold in threat consumables to be worth running over a Bear) — [[d:paladin#1339714435861975090]] (Grey).

## Healing specifics
- **[Melee Holy mechanic]** Blessed Strikes talent ties a group AoE heal to Holy Strike and a Holy Shock cooldown reset to Crusader Strike, intentionally pulling Holy into melee range; devs stated intent was "an alternative playstyle... more risky gameplay in exchange for instant heals and higher Daybreak uptime," explicitly not meant as a damage/healing hybrid — [[d:paladin#1304051400971522068]] (LHK quoting the designer).
- **[Numbers]** Healing math example: at 10s intervals, FoL-rank-7 spam does ~2850 healing for 1200 mana (2.375 heal/mana); "judging for Holy Light" (5 FoL + 1 HL + SoR + Judgement) does ~3730 healing for 1653 mana (2.25 heal/mana) — i.e. roughly comparable efficiency, FoL spam slightly ahead — [[d:paladin#1302671505406300192]] (vegeta1k95).
- **[Ranges/positioning]** Many important raid auras (Paladin auras, Atiesh, Druid tree aura) are only 30–40y, so standing in melee for Blessed Strikes can drop you out of range of buffs other healers rely on — [[d:paladin#1304051463265325096]] (LHK); Holy Strike/Holy Shock range limited to ~10-20y is a frequent complaint — [[d:paladin#1322383171479801907]] (LHK, "cooler if it was 15-20y instead of 10y").
- **[Bug]** Holy Strike reportedly deals no healing at all on a miss, wasting the whole GCD — [[d:paladin#1325781868695654443]] (Alban).
- **[Priority]** Multi-paladin raid buff priority as reported: Kings/Salvation > Wisdom/Might > (Sanctity >) Light; only start assigning Light once you have 4+ Paladins in raid — [[d:paladin#1337347417741463593]] (Netharia).
- **[Sustain build]** Zendin's Holy tank-healer hybrid (30 Holy/21 Prot, blue gear): 100% Consecrate/rank-1-Holy-Shield uptime, 57% armor, 120 block/hit, sustainable 60–80 HPS via Holy Shock/Holy Strike indefinitely, 120–180 HPS via Crusader-Strike-reset Holy Shock but slowly OOMs — [[d:paladin#1327444937205354548]].

## PvP
- **[Ret burst]** Ret PvP reports mixed: JotC can be dodged, CS underwhelming (some crits only 700-800), Holy Strike "still useless" in PvP, and Paladins can be one-shot by Warlock Soulfire — [[d:paladin#1308790783880466465]] (Alban).
- **[Purge]** Seal of Command being fully dispellable again post-CC2 makes coordinated Shaman Purge spam extremely punishing for Paladins in PvP (loses seal + burst window); requested fix (short Purge CD, or protecting seals) unresolved — [[d:paladin#1319672073685172315]], [[d:paladin#1328498136972267531]] (dusty, proposes Purge CD tied to a matching Cleanse CD to avoid PvE side-effects).

- **[Holy PvP]** A leveling/PvP Holy build (SoW for sustain, talented silence/fear resist via Improved Concentration Aura + Holy tree stun/fear resist) reportedly lets a Holy paladin "fly under the radar" since opponents assume Prot and don't prioritize it, then land 1.5s Holy Light heals under Holy Judgement — [[d:paladin#1328564839785693237]] (Ruth).
- **[Bug]** A player-reported bug benefiting Paladins "massively" in PvP was flagged for a community vote to fix, with a bug-report link — [[d:paladin#1314294872487890994]] (lmao); not resolved in-chunk.
