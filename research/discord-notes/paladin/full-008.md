- **[Ret]** For PvE, SP (spellpower) Ret is recommended; for PvP, AP Ret is recommended — [[d:paladin#1444240526873460737]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-29).

- **[Leveling Ret]** Standard leveling/early Ret build referenced as **11/33/7** — [[d:paladin#1442342263341449419]] (Destroses, 2025-11-24, answering a leveling-SoR-vs-SoC question).
- **[Ret hit build]** Build picking up full 3% hit if needed: `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=AoAoB-AoQFYYCAZYAoB-AoQ` — [[d:paladin#1442343938597060649]] (Destroses, 2025-11-23).
- **[Prot hit note]** For a 305 weapon-skill target, Prot needs 7% total hit (talents + gear); ~4% must come from gear — [[d:paladin#1441911016505082008]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-22).
- **[Def cap]** Anti-crit defense cap is 440; gearing to the full avoidance cap is unnecessary until T2.5 — [[d:paladin#1441911160252272741]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-22).
- **[Prot deep-talent choice]** For Prot's third talent tree investment, prefer Holy tree for Sanctity Aura unless another Paladin in your party reliably brings it; if you can guarantee a Sanctity-Aura Paladin, invest in Ret tree instead for parry and reduced Judgement cooldown — [[d:paladin#1444052312036479056]], [[d:paladin#1444053772836929667]] (Silverhand, 2025-11-28).
- **[Prot Holy Str vs Int]** For Prot's Holy-tree points, take Holy Intellect over Holy Strength — Strength gives little value to a Prot Paladin while Intellect adds mana; Exorcism, Holy Wrath, and Holy spells use spell crit, relevant even while tanking — [[d:paladin#1444050041747673178]], [[d:paladin#1444050152133361734]], [[d:paladin#1444052043810734192]] (Silverhand, 2025-11-28).
- **[Prot taunt talent]** Hand of Reckoning (talented ranged taunt) requires 16% spell hit to not miss; the Holy tree's spell-hit talent (8%) plus Precision (3%) helps reach this without needing external hit-rating stacking the way Warriors/Druids do for their taunts — [[d:paladin#1444054453471936532]], [[d:paladin#1444054517606776946]], [[d:paladin#1444054598774952076]] (Silverhand, 2025-11-28).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Leveling spec choice: SoC Ret for 1-on-1 questing; Prot for AoE farming/bigger pulls or dungeon leveling; general questing favors Ret — [[d:paladin#1441795623165759598]], [[d:paladin#1441795679424086066]] (Mithreon/Destroses, 2025-11-22).
- **[Ret]** SoR is confirmed the standard leveling seal choice over SoC even with the SoC talent point invested, per community consensus (question asked, answered with the 11/33/7 build) — [[d:paladin#1442342188083052645]], [[d:paladin#1442342263341449419]] (chri5p/Destroses, 2025-11-24).
- **[Ret]** Leveling/open-world solo play is described as when Ret should feel most powerful; underperformance vs 2+ mobs typically traced to poor mana management, seal/judgement macro setup, or wrong strike usage — [[d:paladin#1441807766523220039]], [[d:paladin#1441807500763594814]], [[d:paladin#1441807236262527087]] (Jesse Potter/Excinerus, 2025-11-22).
- **[Mob tagging leveling route]** Suggested route through level ~35 while leveling professions: SW → Tram → IF → airfields → walk down mountain → Menethil → boat to Southshore → Hinterlands, visiting Hinterlands/Feralas around level 30 for profession leveling to 250/275 — [[d:paladin#1441810127224180766]], [[d:paladin#1441809971665698937]] (Dzonathan/Jesse Potter, 2025-11-22).
- **[Leveling tactic]** Body-pull small gnoll camps, Holy Shock to heal, let it aggro the "tank," then Consecrate — a known efficient leveling loop — [[d:paladin#1441810563801022656]] (Dzonathan, 2025-11-22).
- **[Prot leveling/AoE]** No sizeable AoE snap threat baseline; use Righteous Strikes + Improved Righteous Fury talents, plus Improved Retribution Aura for chip damage; Goblin Sappers for snap AoE burst when mob-tagging — [[d:paladin#1442170315315609640]], [[d:paladin#1442170549051592896]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-24). Holy Strike is bread-and-butter single target; Judgement of Righteousness (JoR) can be thrown on a second target to hold threat — [[d:paladin#1442171060555481309]].
- **[Prot leveling]** Sanctuary Aura is decent for AoE tanking threat (better than Kings/Might for big pulls) but its threat contribution is otherwise small; not needed in raids, may waste buff slot given Prot/Ret buff-cap issues — [[d:paladin#1442172931005354065]], [[d:paladin#1442173430689562714]], [[d:paladin#1442173495831171195]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-24).
- **[Prot dungeon-tank leveling build]** A typical level-40 Prot tree hits Righteous Strikes, Holy Shield, Shield Specialization, Precision, and Improved Righteous Fury as core talents, with the single Reckoning point being incidental filler on the way down the tree; Holy Strike is essentially never worth using over Crusader/Righteous Strikes at this stage — Righteous Fury governs threat, Holy Strike deals holy damage, Crusader Strike deals physical damage — [[d:paladin#1444111666311725186]], [[d:paladin#1444111764370489375]], [[d:paladin#1444112069837328456]], [[d:paladin#1444112633509711974]], [[d:paladin#1444112827920154705]] (Dodging, 2025-11-29).
- **[Leveling stats]** While leveling, stack Stamina/Intellect/Spellpower regardless of spec; if dual-speccing Ret, Str still has value for Ret (more so for AP/SoC builds, less so for SoR) but is not a dead stat like it is for Prot — [[d:paladin#1444113893105471651]], [[d:paladin#1444114241895399546]] (Destroses, 2025-11-29).
- **[Leveling AoE build]** For AoE leveling as Prot: keep Holy Shield up, use available "Eagle" gear upgrades for stam/int, Seal/Judgement of Wisdom (SoW/JoW) for mana sustain, Seal/Judgement of Light (SoL/JoL) for self-healing — [[d:paladin#1443559941540679700]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-27).
- **[Leveling difficulty note]** Ret is reported to feel notably weaker in the 50s bracket (mobs slower to kill, incoming damage spikes) versus earlier brackets; general community read is this is a normal difficulty spike, not spec-specific, with heavy weapon-dependency for Ret at that stage (compare: Assassination Rogues are called out as an exception that doesn't suffer this as much); around level 50+ Prot Paladins commonly switch to farming Lasher packs ("lasher central in Prot") — [[d:paladin#1443375363085303889]], [[d:paladin#1443375777176355032]], [[d:paladin#1443375777034010654]] (KO.NC/Beldrin/Mithreon, 2025-11-27).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Ret]** Strike priority always after auto-attack (AA) damage on single target. SoR build order: AA → SoR → CS (Crusader Strike) → JoR. SoC build order: AA → CS → SoC → JoC — [[d:paladin#1441855607173939331]], [[d:paladin#1441856370814222528]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-22).
- **[Ret AP]** Ap-only build: 3 Crusader Strikes before a Holy Strike to build/use the strength buff (Holy Might-type buff from HS) — [[d:paladin#1442440496135012404]], [[d:paladin#1442440737877790882]] (Iseut/gastro, 2025-11-24). Crusader Strike is the higher-damage strike for Ret generally, use over Holy Strike except during leveling exceptions — [[d:paladin#1442440382205136958]], [[d:paladin#1442440496135012404]] (gastro, 2025-11-24).
- **[SP Ret]** SP ret generally just uses CS spam (not the 3xCS-then-HS rule); judgement doesn't scale off AP so no benefit to the HS buff for SP builds — [[d:paladin#1442271201153257492]] (dusty, 2025-11-23).
- **[SP Ret opener]** dusty opens bosses with Holy Strike while Sunder Armor/armor debuffs are still stacking (HS bypasses armor); once armor debuffs are fully up, CS wins on damage. On fights with melee downtime, re-open with HS after debuffs drop again — [[d:paladin#1442271368300335275]], [[d:paladin#1442271509807759390]], [[d:paladin#1442280345767514132]] (dusty, 2025-11-23/24; disputed partly by "30," who says in most raids sunders are up quickly enough that this barely matters [[d:paladin#1442271529831366731]]).
- **[Prot]** Tank rotation debate: keep 3 Crusader Strike stacks up at all times (more hits for Seals) vs only using Holy Strike — community answer: only Holy Strike, using Crusader loses too much threat — [[d:paladin#1442099192599154748]], [[d:paladin#1442099385784598583]] (Aethanol/Destroses, 2025-11-23).
- **[Prot opener]** Suggested tank opener: pre-cast Seal of Righteousness (SoR), pre-cast Holy Shield, Judge, Holy Strike, Consecration, recast SoR, recast Holy Shield, Judge, Holy Strike (question posed, not explicitly confirmed by an expert reply in-chunk) — [[d:paladin#1441899186722312272]] (Abuelo/Greiz, 2025-11-22; question unanswered).
- **[Weapon mana macro]** A "lazy script" auto-swaps weapon based on mana: equip Fist of Stone (mana regen) below 50% mana, equip Thrash Blade (spellpower) above 90% mana, swapping only after a completed swing during an active GCD to avoid lost swing time — `equipMainHand=17943-ifGlobalCooldown-ifSwinged-ifPlayer<50%mana` / `equipMainHand=17705-ifGlobalCooldown-ifSwinged-ifPlayer>90%mana` — [[d:paladin#1442415266440155239]], [[d:paladin#1442415326326161488]], [[d:paladin#1442415596569628814]] (Dzonathan, 2025-11-24).
- **[Ret full rotation]** Confirmed rotation for raiding Ret: Judge Seal of the Crusader on pull, keep SoR up, Judge SoR then reseal inside the swing timer, spam Crusader Strike, use Holy Strike periodically for Holy Might buff uptime; Repentance on cooldown, Exorcism on cooldown, Hammer of Wrath not worth using; Consecration used on 2+ targets (or single target if excess mana with nothing else to press); biggest damage loss source is failing to keep SoR up on every swing — [[d:paladin#1443969857745063956]], [[d:paladin#1443970033523884253]], [[d:paladin#1443970156505206875]], [[d:paladin#1443970313699201144]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-28).
- **[Ret weapon-mana swap]** A modified libram/weapon swap script swaps between Fist of Stone (mana) at <50% mana and a spellpower weapon at >90% mana, timed to the swing without wasting a GCD — [[d:paladin#1442935142678139013]] (Purec, 2025-11-24; single source, similar to Dzonathan's earlier script above).
- **[Ret single-target overhead]** dusty confirms Holy Strike's value on bosses is mainly at pull (before Sunders are up) and on fights with melee downtime; otherwise CS wins once armor debuffs land — testing this must be done vs a raid-realistic (non-zero-armor) target dummy since bare dummies overstate SP builds' relative gain by omitting Sunder/Faerie Fire — [[d:paladin#1442309089874022520]], [[d:paladin#1442309299719377077]], [[d:paladin#1442309371999551488]] (dusty, 2025-11-23).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot]** No sizeable AoE snap threat baseline (community persistently requests "AoE snap threat" as a fix); rely on Consecration, Righteous Strikes + Improved Righteous Fury talents, and Improved Retribution Aura for chip damage; Goblin Sappers help provide snap AoE burst for mob-tagging in the open world — [[d:paladin#1442170315315609640]], [[d:paladin#1442170549051592896]], [[d:paladin#1442460198643044454]] (Mithreon/dusty, 2025-11-24).

- **[Prot AoE gearing]** FRD (Frost Rune Device / a fire-damage AoE proc trinket, engineering) ticks multiple times per second and is considered strong for stacked AoE packs (e.g. the Gnarl encounter and its Whelp adds), described as "better than a Consecration on a warrior" pound-for-pound, but requires ~400+ SP investment to be worthwhile and is situational (best on 2-3 specific fights) — [[d:paladin#1442457914047725578]], [[d:paladin#1442458278956371969]], [[d:paladin#1442459732471447632]], [[d:paladin#1442459812045787297]] (Excinerus/dusty/angels1m, 2025-11-24).

## Stats, caps, weights

- **[Hit]** Paladins have few spellhit sources; main use-case needing spellhit is Taunt — [[d:paladin#1441910780038742130]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-22).
- **[Prot hit]** 305 weapon skill requires 7% total hit for a tank; 4% must come from gear. Def cap for anti-crit is 440; full avoidance cap not needed until T2.5 — [[d:paladin#1441911016505082008]], [[d:paladin#1441911160252272741]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-22).
- **[Item budget]** Devs confirmed 1 Strength = 1 Intellect = 1 Spirit in the itemization budget system; Ret/Enhance Shaman T3 chest items are called "way over budget" (e.g. 5 haste rated far higher value than its nominal budget cost implies) — [[d:paladin#1441951462833000478]], [[d:paladin#1441951615283101798]], [[d:paladin#1441950047586619522]], [[d:paladin#1441950090473242786]] (Lhian/Destroses, 2025-11-23).
- **[SoC crit threshold]** SoC needs ~50% crit for its SP+STR conversion to match SoR's pure SP conversion in damage — [[d:paladin#1442144437659631656]] (Excinerus, 2025-11-23).
- **[Armor pen]** ArP sources for Ret are limited to T3 boots (100 ArP) and a few weapon on-hit effects (40-75 ArP); most bosses sit far from 0 armor even with Sunder/Faerie Fire/Improved Expose Armor stacked (commonly ~266 armor remaining, some bosses like Patchwerk-analog much chunkier) — [[d:paladin#1442453107173883954]], [[d:paladin#1442442457861062656]], [[d:paladin#1442444581466804379]] (Excinerus/30/Stunfisk, 2025-11-24).
- **[Fire res]** For MC arcane/fire content, ~220 unbuffed arcane resist recommended (200 minimum, not recommended lower); gear list: Otherworld pieces, item 55534, quest 41078 (Arcane Fortitude enchant), item 18171 (shoulders), spell 57117 (cloak) — [[d:paladin#1441912291833675999]], [[d:paladin#1441912792390176963]], [[d:paladin#1441912904227094569]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-22).
- **[Prot stat priority]** Overall Prot stat priority is Spellpower > Stamina >> everything else, except on a handful of very hard-hitting fights or fights requiring resistance gear, where avoidance/mitigation takes over — [[d:paladin#1443432832797245542]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-27).
- **[Prot defense stat]** The only fight in the game where the raw Defense number (crush immunity) specifically matters is Twin Emperors in AQ40; otherwise prioritize *avoidance* (combined block+dodge+parry+miss chance) over raw Defense rating — [[d:paladin#1443432240339091537]], [[d:paladin#1443432451899785357]], [[d:paladin#1443432508569026651]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-27).
- **[Prot Str/Block Value]** Strength is called a near-useless/"dead" stat for Prot Paladins — its only function is converting to Block Value, at a poor conversion rate disputed as either 20:1 or 50:1 Strength-to-Block-Value (Turtle-specific value unclear/contested); Silverhand and dusty agree the existence of Str on Prot tier gear is a design mistake ("only exists as an excuse to put strength on Prot gear... which should never be done") — [[d:paladin#1443337822386786374]], [[d:paladin#1443384915889623073]], [[d:paladin#1443398448945696990]], [[d:paladin#1443405282225950793]], [[d:paladin#1443405754278215730]], [[d:paladin#1443406172534214790]] (gastro/Silverhand/dusty, 2025-11-27; contested exact ratio).
- **[Ret AP scaling]** Ret with an AP-focused BiS setup has roughly 20% more HP than an SP-focused BiS Ret, while SP Ret does roughly 20% more damage — a rough tradeoff heuristic, not a precise simulated number — [[d:paladin#1441800555612602389]], [[d:paladin#1441800656334885066]] (Mithreon/Jesse Potter, 2025-11-22).
- **[Holy healing power scaling]** Rank 1 Flash of Light: ~28 Spellpower increases the heal by roughly 7-8 (tested via weapon-enchant A/B comparison: 235 SP vs 207 SP); a +30 Spellpower weapon enchant was separately measured to add ~12-13 healing to Rank 1 Flash of Light — indicating a substantially <1:1 SP-to-healing coefficient at low ranks — [[d:paladin#1443374429232042088]], [[d:paladin#1443374908229943317]], [[d:paladin#1443375065356828695]] (Dodging/Beldrin, 2025-11-27).
- **[Holy Strike heal]** Community testing/reports (unresolved in-chunk) on whether Holy Strike's Mending Light heal-back scales with melee crit or spell crit, and whether Holy Strike/Crusader Strike count as "spells" for spell-hit/spell-crit purposes — treated as an open mechanical question by multiple posters — [[d:paladin#1443411000823910513]], [[d:paladin#1442630227112099981]] (K24/Atronen, 2025-11-25; question unanswered in-chunk).

- **[Spellstrike mechanics deep dive]** Regular "adds X school damage" spellstrike items only proc a narrow allow-list: Judgement of Wisdom (mana), Vengeance stacks, and Wrath of Cenarius ring (and possibly T0.5 4pc); Ret/Prot T2 3pc set bonus ("Divine Strike") is a full spell cast on every melee hit and therefore procs *everything* that responds to spell landings (e.g. Sigil of Ancient Accord), unlike regular spellstrikes — confirmed via combat-log breakdown: an auto hits, then spellstrike-1 and spellstrike-2 both tick for ~3 each as separate "spells." Because Seal of Righteousness (SoR) is itself coded as a melee hit, having SoR up effectively doubles all spellstrike procs — [[d:paladin#1443223755467001866]], [[d:paladin#1443225325260767363]], [[d:paladin#1443225809782571172]], [[d:paladin#1443226001562800248]], [[d:paladin#1443226391410642995]], [[d:paladin#1443226724283187342]], [[d:paladin#1443234513156313150]], [[d:paladin#1443234806283505736]], [[d:paladin#1443235496162623600]], [[d:paladin#1443235955304697857]] (Mithreon/Beldrin/Destroses, 2025-11-24; Destroses correction: T2 3pc should NOT proc anything that only procs from physical attacks [[d:paladin#1443227576708497589]]).
- **[Sigil of Ancient Accord]** Procs off: first tick of Consecration, Exorcism, Holy Wrath, and the Ret/Prot T2 3pc set bonus; does NOT proc from other fire-damage spellstrike items (Embergem Cuffs, etc.) — [[d:paladin#1441924609904480286]], [[d:paladin#1443241461922402527]], [[d:paladin#1443242167052271758]], [[d:paladin#1443242239219208192]], [[d:paladin#1444464365813235774]] (Destroses/angels1m/Nesushka/Pastor, 2025-11-22/23/30; confirmed twice independently).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Ret weapon enchant]** For a 2h Ret weapon, Crusader (proc) enchant is generally recommended over a flat +30 Spellpower enchant — Crusader's Strength buff synergizes with Divine Strength/Blessing of Kings scaling on top of raw AP, and has better effective uptime thanks to Seal of Righteousness/Zeal stack interactions; confirmed correct by guide author Istenneveben, and "way ahead even for overall dps, not just single target" — [[d:paladin#1443933904577892434]], [[d:paladin#1444186808379506789]], [[d:paladin#1444192615573487687]], [[d:paladin#1444192690332897352]], [[d:paladin#1444192866480951437]] (Jesse Potter/Lazepally/Istenneveben, 2025-11-27/29).
- **[Ret weapon enchant alt]** Other weapon-enchant options for Ret 2h: Lifesteal-type if mana/Vengeance uptime is needed, or +30 SP if running a SoR (spellpower) build — [[d:paladin#1443933904577892434]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-11-27).
- **[Prot weapon enchant]** Wizard Oil recommended over Elemental Sharpening Stones for prot/spellret weapons, mainly because it's much cheaper (not competing with Warriors/Rogues for stones) despite comparable value; Crusader enchant is fine to use on a 2h for Prot too — [[d:paladin#1442372996726521917]], [[d:paladin#1442373382158024714]], [[d:paladin#1442373524324094014]], [[d:paladin#1443933599802851448]] (dusty/fastdino/Jesse Potter, 2025-11-23/27).
- **[Prot enchants]** Prot belt-buckle enchant: agi, not Str (Str is a dead stat for Prot) — [[d:paladin#1443381908527648950]], [[d:paladin#1443384915889623073]] (Arm4n/Beldrin, 2025-11-27). Soulforge gloves/belt: Agi (not SP/Str) is the recommended enchant per community consensus — [[d:paladin#1443992846477688974]], [[d:paladin#1443993441460555849]] (Zaxx/Dodging, 2025-11-28).
- **[Prot leg/head enchant]** For AP Ret head/legs enchant choice, take Strength (18 Str = 36 AP applied to everything except Consecration) over the 1%/2% attack-speed enchant, since 2% haste equates to roughly "2 more attacks every 100 swings" on autos only and rarely matters — [[d:paladin#1442286611663556759]] (dusty, 2025-11-23).
- **[Prot enchant priority]** For Prot hands (9 Str) vs a 15 Agi option, and other slot choices, Vengeance ramp-up (wanting crit) is cited as a reason Agi/crit enchants can matter more than raw stat enchants; guide context matters (a "real classic" guide's Str-favoring advice doesn't apply directly on Turtle) — [[d:paladin#1442287073716469953]], [[d:paladin#1442287475262361631]], [[d:paladin#1442287562222862418]] (Akihiro/dusty, 2025-11-24).
- **[Vamp/Life-tap enchants]** Cloak of Flames can be swapped for the Kara10 vamp cloak for a solo/vampirism build; note that return/reflected damage does NOT proc healing from vampirism-type effects — [[d:paladin#1443239291072417953]], [[d:paladin#1443239407753891882]] (Beldrin/Tsubasa, 2025-11-24).
- **[Weapon proc stacking]** Wizard/Brilliant Wizard Oil (a spellpower weapon buff) does work alongside Windfury Totem (WFT is now an Aura, not a weapon imbue, so it doesn't conflict) — [[d:paladin#1443921661219967106]], [[d:paladin#1443921742543192074]], [[d:paladin#1443921771668570142]] (El Jefe/Mithreon/Destroses, 2025-11-28).
- **[Elemental Slayer]** Elemental Slayer weapon enchant is confirmed to boost the damage of a shield's on-block proc effect (e.g. Crest of Retribution); see Dungeons section for the lasher-farm gear caveat (Lashers resist Nature damage) — [[d:paladin#1444028104304492575]], [[d:paladin#1444028404247564489]], [[d:paladin#1444028520186773706]], [[d:paladin#1444028578822881413]] (theoloras/warkrak, 2025-11-29).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Reckoning]** Reckoning's tooltip is misleading: it does not guarantee an extra swing; it gives a chance to finish the current swing timer on a block and queue the next swing faster. Still useful for AoE since more frequent swings/blocks means more frequent Seal of Wisdom/Judgement of Wisdom procs — [[d:paladin#1443333572063789156]], [[d:paladin#1443333810208116776]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-27).
- **[Judgement utility]** Judgements are refreshed by any of the paladin's own subsequent attacks (auto/strike); some community members debated whether Crusader Strike/other paladins' attacks refresh Judgement of Wisdom/Righteousness on the target, but consensus leaned toward each paladin's own attacks refreshing their own Judgement — [[d:paladin#1442110011399606313]], [[d:paladin#1442110114634010704]], [[d:paladin#1442110246821826662]], [[d:paladin#1442110259673169920]] (Mithreon/Adamant/Aelgagar, 2025-11-23; not fully resolved — contested).
- **[Raid support via Judgement]** Judgements are described as an under-recognized form of raid support: they provide a passing debuff granting mana regen, minor healing for melee, and Holy damage bonus for Prot/other Paladins and casters like Smite Priests hitting the target; with at least one Prot and one Ret in a raid, 2 of 3 Judgement types can usually stay up continuously — [[d:paladin#1442109792221921413]], [[d:paladin#1442109896509362337]], [[d:paladin#1442111011284062280]], [[d:paladin#1442111351592976414]] (Adamant/Mithreon, 2025-11-23).
- **[Libram swapping]** Libram (relic) hot-swapping mid-combat (e.g. swapping to Final Judgement libram right before Judgement, then back to another libram) is possible without a GCD if timed to spellbatching; Silverhand calls unpenalized swapping "essentially an exploit" that should be disabled or penalized — [[d:paladin#1441939552095961159]], [[d:paladin#1442867474058969150]] (Excinerus/Silverhand/Mithreon, 2025-11-23).
- **[Libram addon]** An updated libram-swap addon (`github.com/jvaneg/Libramswap`) removes the need for manual macros; some users report occasional lost GCDs with libram-swap addons/macros depending on setup — [[d:paladin#1443904837371560043]], [[d:paladin#1443923640616878100]], [[d:paladin#1443965723188662342]] (Mithreon/pipillaaa, 2025-11-28/29).
- **[Reseal/Judge macro]** Community macro/script examples to Judge-and-reseal in one keystroke, or to only cast Seal of Righteousness if not already buffed (avoiding wasted global cooldowns from double-sealing): `/script if buffed("Seal of Righteousness") then CastSpellByName("Crusader Strike") else CastSpellByName("Seal of Righteousness") end` and CleveRoid-style scripts like `sealRight-ifNotPlayerHasBuff=sealRight` / `judge-ifPlayerHasBuff=sealRight` — [[d:paladin#1444410988509138974]], [[d:paladin#1444411827243974676]] (Vik/Faithroll, 2025-11-29). A Judge does not recast Seal if you Judge within the same GCD window, avoiding a common macro pitfall — [[d:paladin#1444409509853401198]] (Dodging, 2025-11-29).
- **[Threat: Sunder priority]** Sunder Armor should be applied first-global by Warriors even on trash that dies in ~2 globals, mainly as habit reinforcement for boss fights where it matters; on fast-dying trash the actual DPS impact of waiting for Sunders is negligible — [[d:paladin#1442289355069587526]], [[d:paladin#1442290742361067570]] (angels1m/dusty, 2025-11-23).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Prot hit/threat caps]** 305 weapon-skill target requires 7% total hit (4% from gear) to never miss as MT; anti-crit Defense cap is 440; full avoidance cap unnecessary until T2.5 gear — [[d:paladin#1441911016505082008]], [[d:paladin#1441911160252272741]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-22, repeated from Stats section for tank-specific context).
- **[AQ40 avoidance target]** By AQ40, Prot should reach 102.4% avoidance under Holy Shield (i.e. effectively uncrushable), since incoming damage is sizeably bigger than in BWL — [[d:paladin#1443030427659927673]], [[d:paladin#1443030508153081998]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-26).
- **[Prot resist gearing]** MT-ing MC requires fire resist on a couple of specific bosses (amount not specified, guides from vanilla/classic apply since resist mechanics didn't change) — [[d:paladin#1441911357120446654]], [[d:paladin#1441911476733476945]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-22). For Kara40, ~220 unbuffed Arcane Resist recommended (200 minimum) — see Stats section for the gear list.
- **[Prot threat gradient]** MT gearing gradient once hit-capped: best weapon > best shield > then set bonuses and set stats; SP is the primary threat stat, Stamina next, Strength is dead — [[d:paladin#1443031532829675520]], [[d:paladin#1443033037041307801]], [[d:paladin#1443081249353895966]] (Xytras/Mithreon/gastro, 2025-11-26/27).
- **[Prot threat philosophy]** MT wants max threat (or at least enough that DPS can't pull off the tank); OT (off-tank on adds/secondary targets) can trade threat for more mitigation if the MT alone can hold with margin to spare — [[d:paladin#1443030752026558555]], [[d:paladin#1443030995895980122]], [[d:paladin#1443031435920543845]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-26).
- **[Prot Righteous Fury]** Toggle Righteous Fury off when off-tanking in MC to avoid ripping threat from the main tank, especially with strong current-patch Prot threat output — [[d:paladin#1442647267759755397]], [[d:paladin#1442647380825477120]] (Silverhand/Tsubasa, 2025-11-25).
- **[Prot AoE tank note]** Consecration is described as doing "80% of the tank job" for AoE dungeon packs; Prot Paladins are considered one of the best low-effort AoE dungeon tanks, alongside strong pull-threat via Sanctuary Aura — [[d:paladin#1442831708561997896]], [[d:paladin#1442828109161168946]] (angels1m, 2025-11-25).
- **[Scarlet Monastery hazard]** Scarlet Champions' "Holy Strike" converts a full auto-attack into 100% Holy damage that bypasses all armor (unresistable unless magic-resisted), hitting for 460-500+; historically countered with a Flame Retardant Device (engineering, absorbs/reduces the hit) or by simply dropping Skullflame Shield for a Fire Resistance Device (FRD) — Tier gear does not help since the damage type ignores armor entirely — [[d:paladin#1443006788311056487]], [[d:paladin#1443006907186020434]], [[d:paladin#1443007920613883924]], [[d:paladin#1443008083486970017]], [[d:paladin#1443008096246173769]] (George/gastro/Beldrin/Jesse Potter/Sylvaira, 2025-11-26).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy Strike + Daybreak]** Holy Strike's heal-back (Mending Light) can crit, and crits proc Daybreak on the target hit, which then spreads as free AoE healing — described as "a beyond broken mechanic" and "a ton of free raid healing"; not maximizing Holy Strike + Daybreak uptime while playing melee-Holy is "griefing yourself" per dusty — [[d:paladin#1442201760893632554]], [[d:paladin#1442201933543768146]], [[d:paladin#1442202313111371919]], [[d:paladin#1442202445353586718]] (dusty, 2025-11-23).
- **[Melee Holy design tension]** dusty argues melee-Holy should ideally function purely off Holy Strike + Seal of Light/Holy Judgement (pure combat orientation) rather than also having access to Flash of Light, calling the current hybrid design "a fraud"; separately, Daybreak can proc the Red Dragonscale set's 4pc bonus (undocumented interaction reported) — [[d:paladin#1442203774872719573]], [[d:paladin#1442213465564512378]] (dusty/Deleted User, 2025-11-23).
- **[Holy rotation]** Basic Holy healing rotation described as: Holy Strike, fill with (Rank 1) Flash of Light, repeat — [[d:paladin#1444276924997304433]] (Destroses, 2025-11-29).
- **[Holy trinket note]** Loop of Infused Renewal ring only procs off Holy Light (10% chance for a separate Rank 3 Holy Light clone heal, which can crit separately); not worth using for Paladins until/unless a Holy Light-focused build exists, since Paladins currently melee-heal via Holy Strike, not Holy Light — [[d:paladin#1442882170564640851]], [[d:paladin#1442882382305689620]], [[d:paladin#1442882443819618324]] (Kohhzee/Mithreon, 2025-11-24).
- **[Holy libram tradeoff]** Flash of Light (FoL) libram (raw, not healing-power-scaled, ~28 HP-equivalent on Rank 1 FoL) is preferred over the Consecration healing libram for raiding — the Consecration libram gives a heal + 5 Str to allies standing in Consecration but costs 2 buff slots, making it effectively unusable in a raid near the buff cap — [[d:paladin#1443764358843732061]], [[d:paladin#1443765370950516888]], [[d:paladin#1443766704491729069]], [[d:paladin#1443771606064304270]], [[d:paladin#1443788212370673819]], [[d:paladin#1443790585088708745]] (Račke/HolyAngel/Lazepally/Destroses/Illithris, 2025-11-27).
- **[Deep-Holy talent choice for Prot]** When speccing into Holy as Prot's 3rd tree, take Holy Intellect over Holy Strength (Str gives little value, Int adds mana; Exorcism/Holy Wrath/healing spells all use spell crit) — see Talent builds section — [[d:paladin#1444050041747673178]], [[d:paladin#1444052043810734192]] (Silverhand, 2025-11-28).
- **[Holy set/gear notes]** Holy T1 5pc is still considered good, especially paired with Wildgrowth-type items; Holy set choice can flex between T1 and T2 5pc cheaply since T1 shoulders/legs are nearly identical to T2 (see Gear section); T3.5 5pc for Ret/Holy is described as bugged and 3.5's set bonus stole a large share of the class's "power budget" per community read — [[d:paladin#1442200837203034243]], [[d:paladin#1442471471543881789]] (Mithreon/Stunfisk, 2025-11-23/24).
- **[Nerf risk]** Multiple posters flag Mending Light + Daybreak as likely nerf targets for over-delivering free AoE healing; disputed by others who feel Holy is currently only "mediocre" and needs no nerfs (see 1.18.1 changes section) — [[d:paladin#1443596169623113852]], [[d:paladin#1444440036761276528]] (Mithreon/Faithroll, 2025-11-27/29).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Emerald Sanctum / "the dog boss"]** (Turtle-custom raid content referenced alongside K40 discussion) — the dog boss should be tanked with 2 tanks who swap around 15 stacks of a debuff; one tank should stay outside its range to avoid stacking; Bubble (Divine Shield) can be used to clear the stacking debuff and continue tanking. Ghost/skeleton adds in the same raid apply a silence. The final boss applies a temporary disorient, requiring the other tank to taunt. Overall considered "not a hard raid" if the dog boss mechanic is executed correctly — [[d:paladin#1441935833811914812]], [[d:paladin#1441935992243093716]], [[d:paladin#1441936209286009013]], [[d:paladin#1441936461522931825]], [[d:paladin#1441936643534753876]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-23).
- **[Kara40 ("Tower of Karazhan")]** New MC-tier and Kara40 bosses commonly feature knockback or suck-in mechanics (community-observed pattern, not a specific boss callout) — [[d:paladin#1441821046348185620]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-22). Kara40 requires ~220 unbuffed Arcane Resist for Holy Paladins on relevant fights, minimum 200 (see Stats section for the gear list). By T2.5/T2.5+3p, tanks reach ~7.5k HP overall in current Prot gearing, described as the necessary tradeoff for adequate threat — [[d:paladin#1444240875063480410]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-29).
- **[Scarlet Monastery Cathedral]** Scarlet Champions' Holy Strike ability hits for 460-500+ unresistable holy damage that bypasses armor entirely; historically countered by Flame Retardant Device or swapping Skullflame Shield out for an FRD trinket; without T3 gear this can one-shot squishier characters attempting a full pull — see Tanking section for full detail — [[d:paladin#1443006788311056487]] through [[d:paladin#1443008096246173769]] (multiple, 2025-11-26).
- **[Naxx trash design critique]** When engaging Kel'Thuzad (KT), all previously-uncleared trash in the room rushes through portals; some trash is intentionally relocated to disrupt known skip routes — community expects a redesign of Naxx trash/instructor fight order when Ambershire reaches that phase, mirroring changes made for BWL — [[d:paladin#1444278472510935122]], [[d:paladin#1444278593306890334]], [[d:paladin#1444278768322482376]], [[d:paladin#1444279054890045481]] (Mithreon/Mima, 2025-11-29).
- **[Lasher farming]** Ancient/Felwood-style Lasher packs are a common gold/herb farm for level 50+ Paladins (Prot spec, using Consecration + mobility); Lashers are immune to Nature damage, so Crest of Retribution-type Nature-damage shield procs are wasted on them — a physical-damage shield proc works better there, and the Argent Dawn shield / Argent Defender (Scholomance quest reward) are both cited as outperforming both crest options for lasher farming — [[d:paladin#1444027429382520974]], [[d:paladin#1444028104304492575]], [[d:paladin#1444050152133361734]], [[d:paladin#1444029131275894907]], [[d:paladin#1444029248573804717]], [[d:paladin#1444031193627623608]], [[d:paladin#1444031330470858914]], [[d:paladin#1444031542216229015]] (Beldrin/theoloras/Istenneveben/Ythor, 2025-11-29).
- **[BWL threat gearing example]** A community-shared "BiS threat setup for Prot BWL tier" gear planner link (see Gear section citation for the URL) was posted as a rough baseline for full-threat Prot gearing entering BWL — [[d:paladin#1443193468653666316]] (Destroses, 2025-11-27).

## PvP
- **[Ret PvP weakness]** Rets are "quite weak" in PvP currently; 20% PvP damage dampening means Ret deals too little damage for the mana it costs, forcing frequent Divine Shield + heavy mana-cost healing, after which the Ret gets re-focused and killed anyway — [[d:paladin#1441758774980382780]], [[d:paladin#1441760566657028186]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-22).
- **[Ret PvP purges]** Freedom, seals, and stuns applied by a Paladin are all magic-type effects and thus easily purged/dispelled by Shamans (Freedom in particular is purgeable despite its cooldown); this makes stunning as Ret weak against multi-Shaman groups — [[d:paladin#1441760867635953716]], [[d:paladin#1441763664133881946]], [[d:paladin#1441763812943331380]] (Mithreon/Faithroll, 2025-11-22).
- **[Ret vs classes]** Ret is considered a reasonably good matchup vs Mages 1v1 if it can close the gap before being noticed, and notably strong against AFK players; against premade/organized Shaman-heavy groups Rets struggle heavily (anecdote: a single Mage kited 3 Rets for 2 minutes) — [[d:paladin#1441764012780945488]], [[d:paladin#1441764115252252827]], [[d:paladin#1441764406278098945]], [[d:paladin#1441764762571640945]] (Mithreon/Faithroll/Julian, 2025-11-22).
- **[Bug/nerf]** Blessing of Sacrifice's redirected damage no longer breaks Crowd Control effects — a deliberate nerf that the community strongly dislikes, calling it an "undercut of skill expression"; occurred around the same time Freedom's cooldown was increased by ~2s — [[d:paladin#1441767886023692468]], [[d:paladin#1443942083659300975]], [[d:paladin#1443942099832668386]], [[d:paladin#1443942254011088930]], [[d:paladin#1443942544831549501]], [[d:paladin#1443507286801776720]], [[d:paladin#1443510233677697096]] (Skripter/Julian/Pastor/theoloras/Jesse Potter, 2025-11-22/27).
- **[Horde-only CC-break bug]** A Horde-only cheap consumable ("poisoned bread"-type item) breaks any damage-based CC (Sheep, Blind, Sap) via 1-second poison ticks doing only ~5-10 damage each, essentially a free CC break widely seen as broken/unaddressed by devs (bug report referenced) — [[d:paladin#1441767886023692468]], [[d:paladin#1441768321367146556]], [[d:paladin#1441768711517245532]], [[d:paladin#1441769239504490579]], [[d:paladin#1441769924564357246]] (Pastor, 2025-11-22).
- **[Stronghold Gauntlets]** Ret paladins frequently wear Stronghold Gauntlets while perma-BG'ing, tolerating the 20% dampening tradeoff for consistent PvP presence — [[d:paladin#1441763421199794176]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-11-22).
- **[Best PvP paladin spec]** Community consensus: Holy Paladin (melee-healer hybrid) is where Paladin PvP performance actually shines — "Rets are ass, hpally is where paladin pvp is at" — thanks to a frequently-resetting Holy Shock that can crit for 2k+ with 50%+ crit chance — [[d:paladin#1443973737056698448]], [[d:paladin#1443973163389292564]], [[d:paladin#1443973263607857275]], [[d:paladin#1443973375012765696]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-27).
- **[PvP gearing]** For Ret PvP, AP Ret is recommended over SP Ret (SP Ret is the PvE recommendation) — [[d:paladin#1444240526873460737]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-29).
- **[PvP meta commentary]** Community view: PvP should not drive PvE class/item balance decisions (multiple posters argue for separating the two), citing PvE-nerfed stamina on tier gear and PvP-driven item nerfs (e.g. -8 Stamina on certain weapons) as evidence of poor cross-balance decisions — [[d:paladin#1442925928215871619]], [[d:paladin#1442922613755351080]], [[d:paladin#1443514605077794867]], [[d:paladin#1443515330004516984]] (Jesse Potter/Beldrin/Destroses, 2025-11-24/27; contested/open design debate, not a dev-confirmed direction).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[QuickHeal macro]** A "QuickHeal" macro/addon for casting Holy Strike's heal component is described as a strong QoL tool, though buggy for automated Holy Light casting after Shocks (users report it casting Holy Light unpredictably and had to disable it); the automation is also seen by some as detrimental to "real" healing skill/parses since Holy Paladins have a lot to micromanage — [[d:paladin#1441856376874860636]], [[d:paladin#1441856567954903202]], [[d:paladin#1441872450374733836]], [[d:paladin#1442853136179134517]] (Faithroll/Mithreon/Sookie, 2025-11-22/25).
- **[Threat/nameplate addons]** A user reported pfUI's built-in threat coloring (red/yellow/green) not updating correctly in combat and asked for alternative nameplate addon recommendations for tanks — question posed but left unanswered in-chunk — [[d:paladin#1442984812058247360]] (Thunderdoomz, 2025-11-26; question unanswered).
- **[DPS meter addon]** ShaguDPS is used by at least one veteran player over addons that show running averages, for its display of desired metrics — [[d:paladin#1442062476693737575]], [[d:paladin#1442062511720628245]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-11-23).
- **[Libram swap addon]** `github.com/jvaneg/Libramswap` is an updated libram-swap tool that doesn't require a manual macro; older/heavier macro addons (SuperCleveRoidMacros) can cause noticeable in-game microfreezes on libram swap if the player also runs real-time stat-calculating addons; `pfDebug` addon can help pinpoint which addon causes such freezes — [[d:paladin#1443904837371560043]], [[d:paladin#1444432779470176478]], [[d:paladin#1444447108848550049]], [[d:paladin#1444449202603036785]], [[d:paladin#1444449259213553826]] (Mithreon/Pastor, 2025-11-28/30).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Race choice]** For pure min-maxing: Human is good for all 3 specs and specifically pairs well with Ret's weapon-skill talent racial synergy; Dwarf is favored for Prot and PvP; Helf (High Elf) is favored for Holy (mana regen); overall the difference between races is described as small except for the Human Ret weapon-skill interaction — [[d:paladin#1442081853526773881]] (Destroses, 2025-11-23).
- **[Professions]** Engineering is a strong general pick for Prot Paladins; Swordsmithing/Blacksmithing (Weaponsmith path) is required to craft/temper the Tempered Runeblade weapon, best-in-slot for Spellret before BWL — can be dropped after crafting it if not otherwise wanted — [[d:paladin#1443679454072279242]], [[d:paladin#1443679456202981407]], [[d:paladin#1443679533701005333]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-27).
- **[Gold farming]** Common Paladin gold farms: Lasher farming (level 50+, Prot spec, Consecration-based — crappy gray-item vendor trash alone adds up to ~30g/hour); Water Essence farming from Felwood elemental pools; grinding the "Field Duty" quest repeatedly for Cenarion Circle rep and turn-in gold (one player reported ~20k reputation just from repeating the envelope quest, faster than killing mobs for rep) — [[d:paladin#1443009226787127386]], [[d:paladin#1443009363450003527]], [[d:paladin#1443009827973370019]], [[d:paladin#1442498487231971409]], [[d:paladin#1442498570002632868]] (Mithreon/Pastor, 2025-11-26).
- **[Arcanite bar farming]** Arcanite Bar transmute (Alchemy, 2-day cooldown) profit margins are thin (~3-6g over an Arcane Crystal) unless running multiple alt transmuters; running Zul'Gurum for player-farmed Arcane Crystals (e.g. via a Druid/multibox) to feed the arcanite farm, or using the 10-minute-cooldown Elemental transmutes on a single character, are suggested alternatives — [[d:paladin#1441717649498308649]], [[d:paladin#1441717886052859995]], [[d:paladin#1441718719045959680]] (gastro/Jesse Potter/Mithreon, 2025-11-22).
- **[WoC ring farming]** Wrath of Cenarius ring is obtainable via ~30 hours of Cenarion Circle-adjacent quest grinding (only unlocked on Ambershire after a recent patch); considered a worthwhile, "not psycho," permanent +132 Spellpower investment once you understand the grind, though overwhelming on a first attempt — [[d:paladin#1442857725020340336]], [[d:paladin#1442857513694527520]], [[d:paladin#1442857294571503739]] (Destroses/Beldrin, 2025-11-24).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Bug]** Succubus Seduction works through Divine Shield — an open bug report referenced as nearly 2 years old with no fix — [[d:paladin#1441782806823833712]], [[d:paladin#1441787681003540510]] (Pastor/Faithroll, 2025-11-22).
- **[Bug]** Mount-speed buff can exceed the buff cap and get "stuck," leaving the player permanently mounted and unable to dismount until it resolves itself — [[d:paladin#1442238192211333262]] (Excinerus, 2025-11-23).
- **[Quirk]** Judgement cannot be cast while Silenced despite being a ranged physical/magic hybrid attack that uses melee hit/crit; conversely, Holy Light's heal still goes off while the caster is Silenced — both called out as inconsistent/baffling by the community — [[d:paladin#1441932646513905787]], [[d:paladin#1441933181585588244]], [[d:paladin#1441933671429964030]] (Excinerus/Mithreon, 2025-11-23).
- **[Quirk]** Libram (relic) hot-swapping can be executed without triggering a GCD if timed to server spellbatching, which Silverhand calls "essentially an exploit" enabled by addon/macro precision that a normal player wouldn't replicate manually — [[d:paladin#1441939552095961159]] (Silverhand, 2025-11-23).
- **[Quirk]** T2 3pc "Divine Strike" is a full spell-cast proc that (unlike regular spellstrike items, which only proc a narrow allow-list of JoW/Vengeance/WoC) procs *everything* that responds to spell landings, including Sigil of Ancient Accord — a known, deliberate-but-unusual exception in the itemization system (see Gear section for full mechanic breakdown) — [[d:paladin#1443226001562800248]], [[d:paladin#1443226724283187342]] (Beldrin, 2025-11-24).
- **[Quirk/exploit note]** WFT (Windfury Totem) procs are counted server-side as regular auto-attacks (not a distinguishable special attack type), meaning any interaction that treats "auto attacks" specially (like Seal procs) cannot currently distinguish a real swing from a WFT-triggered swing without a large, currently-unplanned server code change — [[d:paladin#1441990453833502740]], [[d:paladin#1441992610339225791]], [[d:paladin#1442085324363792518]] (Excinerus/Mithreon, 2025-11-23).
- **[Bug]** Stormstrike (Enhancement Shaman) reportedly has a 4% chance to proc Windfury twice — once from the Stormstrike hit itself, once from applying its debuff — an apparent unintended double-proc oversight highlighted by paladins comparing melee proc fairness — [[d:paladin#1442046366409818133]], [[d:paladin#1442049701372366981]], [[d:paladin#1442049950472212520]] (Izmo/Fat Pibble with Air Pods, 2025-11-23; single source, unconfirmed by a dev).
- **[Design note/quirk]** Reckoning's tooltip is misleading — it does not grant a guaranteed extra attack; it only lets the current swing finish on a block and queues the next swing (see Cooldowns section) — [[d:paladin#1443333572063789156]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-27).
- **[Quirk]** Dwarf's Stoneform racial reportedly can no longer clear Blind (Rogue) since Blind was changed to a physical-type effect, meaning it also cannot be cleansed off other players by any Paladin cleanse either — [[d:paladin#1443507638079062100]], [[d:paladin#1443508598973005884]], [[d:paladin#1443508926548152411]] (Faithroll/theoloras, 2025-11-27; disputed exact mechanic/possibly a recent unannounced change).
