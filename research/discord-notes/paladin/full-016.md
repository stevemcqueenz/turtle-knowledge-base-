## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Prot]** Full preview patch-note text posted for Protection: Holy Shield's additional threat modifier increased from 30% to 50%; Righteous Strikes reworked to "Increases the damage and threat of your Holy Strike by 5/10/15/20/25%. Crusader Strike now grants you Zealous Defence for 10 seconds. Zealous Defence reduces the damage of your next blocked attack by 6/12/18/24/30%." Holy Strike now scales with weapon damage; SP scaling raised from 43% to 71%. Per-rank values: R1 (lvl4) 25% wpn+11-13 holy; R2 (lvl12) 25%+22-28; R3 (lvl20) 30%+34-38; R4 (lvl28) 30%+52-58; R5 (lvl36) 35%+71-77; R6 (lvl44) 35%+91-98; R7 (lvl52) 40%+106-118; R8 (lvl60, max) 40% weapon damage + 129-146 Holy damage. Dev comment: "Protection Paladins were extremely strong during leveling and early endgame but scaled poorly in high-end raids. These changes slightly reduce early power while significantly improving late-game scaling." — [[d:paladin#1465008595815891047]] (goyut1, 2026-01-25; posted verbatim from forum patch notes).
- **[Prot]** The patch-note wording ("part of its damage as Physical") was an error; Lhian confirmed Holy Strike's weapon-damage component is still full Holy damage, just now also benefiting from Righteous Fury/Sanctity Aura and weapon damage scaling — [[d:paladin#1465167759364329655]] (Adamant relaying Lhian), [[d:paladin#1465385495558361280]] (Lhian, 2026-01-26, "the changelog entry for holy strike was corrected"), confirmed again [[d:paladin#1465387172042379264]] (Mouretsu, 2026-01-26).
- **[Prot]** Overall class summary as posted: Holy — nerfed (Daybreak nerf + AoE healing nerf); Prot — buffed; Ret — buffed (SoC/SoR buffs) — [[d:paladin#1464989748354482367]] (Destroses, 2026-01-25, +1).
- **[Prot]** Threat math consensus (Proph, Grey, Aelgagar, angels1m, Dzonathan): the Holy Strike rework raises the break-even spellpower where the change is a net threat gain. With ~150 base weapon damage the break-even is roughly 500-600 SP; with ~300 base weapon damage it's roughly 200-350 SP. Below ~400 SP, tanks are likely to see a net TPS **decrease** from Holy Strike; above naxx/aq40-level gear it's a **modest to strong buff** (roughly 20-40% more Holy Strike damage at "peak spellpower" gear, e.g. Grey's ~1250→~1700 dmg) — [[d:paladin#1465164605390065786]], [[d:paladin#1465172142042910882]], [[d:paladin#1465178546069700618]], [[d:paladin#1465248862418505829]] (Proph/Grey, 2026-01-25; extended multi-message derivation, single coherent source, cross-checked by several posters).
- **[Prot]** Consecration is getting a frontloading buff, but **only the base damage is frontloaded, not the scaling** — confirmed as a real (if underwhelming) improvement, roughly a flat ~+30 base damage on the first tick. Several posters (Aelgagar, Mithreon, Grey) had expected the SP scaling to also be frontloaded per "leaks" and were disappointed it wasn't — [[d:paladin#1465195404768317515]], [[d:paladin#1465507481886130342]] (Grey/Lhian, 2026-01-26/27; Lhian confirms per-tick differing coefficients would be a code nightmare, not planned).
- **[Prot]** T2.5 3-set bonus is being moved from Crusader Strike proc to Judgement (JoR), intentionally shifting some prot opening threat onto Judgement — [[d:paladin#1465222071071146016]] (Aelgagar, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot]** T3.5 gets +4% damage reduction, but loses the ability to libram-swap for a similar DR proc mid-fight, making it roughly a wash ("losing libram swaps so you're also losing a potential 4% DR") — [[d:paladin#1465239485733277716]], [[d:paladin#1465240148785496145]] (dusty, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot]** "Champion" buff (priest capstone that granted 5% raid-wide damage reduction, and separately a damage buff) is being removed entirely. Extensive disputed thread over whether this is a real tank nerf: pro-nerf (Anakinn) argues paladins lose survivability with no compensation until gear/stat itemization catches up; contra (angels1m, Grey, Silverhand) argue the 5% DR was "shouldn't exist" design debt and that geared tanks won't need to trade threat gear for it — disputed, no resolution — [[d:paladin#1465039005015081165]] through [[d:paladin#1465049928995962903]] (Anakinn vs angels1m/Grey, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot]** Righteous Fury math clarified: base Holy threat mod is 60%, Improved Righteous Fury adds +75% of that base (i.e. +45 percentage points, additive not multiplicative), for a total 105% bonus multiplier on Holy damage/threat — [[d:paladin#1465243231678763155]], [[d:paladin#1465243452450144348]] (Jesse Potter/Seviry, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot]** Crusader Strike (prot 100% weapon dmg + 161 + 0.20×SP, no threat mod, normalized 2.4 for 1h sword/axe/mace, 1.7 dagger, 3.3 2h) remains effectively unusable in the normal prot rotation because it shares a cooldown with Holy Strike, which does dramatically more threat — Lhian personally states he has "given up on even considering crusader strike to be part of normal prot rotation" and sees "a niche" for it (e.g. Knight fight, or as an emergency mitigation button after the Zealous Defence change) but no rework planned this patch — [[d:paladin#1465262990554300527]], [[d:paladin#1465263048779501674]], [[d:paladin#1465263224898326714]] (Lhian, 2026-01-26).
- **[Ret]** No new paladin ability/talent designs in the revised notes beyond the CS/Repentance/Holy Strike buffs already known; Lhian confirms "there may be some extra tuning closer to release, but no new designs" — [[d:paladin#1465505210389495839]], [[d:paladin#1465507352102043911]] (Lhian, 2026-01-27).
- **[Holy]** Holy nerf accepted as reasonable by most (Daybreak/AoE heal nerf), unlike more contested nerfs to other classes; some individual holy paladins reportedly considering quitting or respeccing away from Holy 3.5 due to the change, though itemization on Holy 3.5 gear is still described as "bonkers" good regardless of set-bonus feelings — [[d:paladin#1465507581278687292]], [[d:paladin#1465508279206547651]], [[d:paladin#1465508805814255679]] (Jesse Potter/Lhian, 2026-01-27).
- **[Ret]** Sigil of the Ancient Accord (trinket) confirmed to proc only off T2 3-piece bonus procs and off direct-cast spells (Holy Shock, Exorcism, probably Holy Wrath); it does NOT proc off spellstrike weapon procs like Anomalus bracers — tested and confirmed by "angel" — [[d:paladin#1465136762094358548]], [[d:paladin#1465237854387961988]], [[d:paladin#1465248732818968627]] (Zasa/Destroses/angels1m, 2026-01-25/26).
- **[Prot]** The new Crusader Strike "Zealous Defence" buff (from reworked Righteous Strikes) only reduces the damage of the *next single blocked hit* by up to 30% (a dodge/parry/miss doesn't consume it, only an actual block does); Lhian confirms it reduces the incoming hit's damage directly (like other %DR), not block value, and this appears to apply before other %DR — [[d:paladin#1465833695175901347]], [[d:paladin#1465381825126859002]] (Excinerus/Lhian, 2026-01-26/28). Compared to the current CS bonus (permanent +30% Shield Block Value while stacked, real average mitigation ~100-120 damage per block per angels1m's math), the new version scales with *boss hit size* instead of the player's fixed block value cap (~150 BV max), making it stronger against big single hits (e.g. blocks a Sapphiron/Rupturan/Meph-tier hit for ~30% off) but weaker/more swingy as a "one shot" defensive cooldown rather than a sustained mitigation buff — disputed whether it's a net upgrade, Excinerus calls it "ass" for wasting the CD on a subsequent dodge; Grey argues it's fine because the buff persists until you actually land a block — [[d:paladin#1465833531308642486]], [[d:paladin#1465834303790518343]], [[d:paladin#1465835093947060359]] (Excinerus/Grey/angels1m, 2026-01-28).
- **[Prot]** Community design-constraint analysis (Grey, unconfirmed by Lhian directly but uncontested) for why Holy Strike itself had to be the one buffed rather than other threat tools: can't decouple the CS/HS shared cooldown; can't reduce the cooldown (would buff Ret's Crusader Strike too); can't buff SoR scaling or Judgement (JoR) further because that collaterally buffs Spellret; can't buff Consecration scaling (buffs AoE tanking/Ret, doesn't help single-target); can't buff Holy Shield (same AoE/Ret collateral issue, and doesn't help offtanks); can't buff Exorcism (Undead-only niche) — leaving "just buff Holy Strike's formula" as the only lever without adding an entirely new cooldown ability — [[d:paladin#1466083699090133074]] (Grey, 2026-01-28).
- **[Prot]** Open technical question: will Holy Strike's new weapon-damage component be *normalized* (like Crusader Strike's weapon-damage is) or use raw weapon damage? Jesse Potter relays that Lhian "assumes yes" it will be normalized but doesn't personally code abilities so isn't 100% sure; Aelgagar notes normalizing weapon-damage abilities is the established design pattern, so an unnormalized release would likely be considered unintended and get patched — unresolved at time of chunk — [[d:paladin#1466077161545928705]], [[d:paladin#1466077551104360498]], [[d:paladin#1466077712534733006]], [[d:paladin#1466077794370064473]] (Mithreon/Jesse Potter/Aelgagar, 2026-01-28).
- **[Prot]** Seal of Command proc-rate buff quantified: proc chance = (100/60) × weapon swing time × PPM. Going from 7 PPM to 9 PPM turns a 3.8-speed weapon (e.g. Maul of Momentum) from ~44.3% to ~57% proc chance per swing — i.e. roughly 1-in-3 swings procs today vs. roughly 1-in-2 after the patch, about a 33% increase in proc *rate* (some debate over whether that's a 28% or 33% output increase, converging on ~33%) — [[d:paladin#1466430997825327124]], [[d:paladin#1466427159290712236]], [[d:paladin#1466469148996538661]] (Kairion/Jesse Potter, 2026-01-27/28).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret]** Three named ret archetypes: **Spellret** (stack spell damage over almost everything else), **Necrodin** (T2 3pc prot+ret synergy build using "The Lost" set summon-ghosts bonus), **Shockadin** (Holy/Ret using Holy Shock for damage, currently underwhelming) — [[d:paladin#1465163837308407952]] (Adamant, 2026-01-25).
- **[Ret]** Spellret (SoR) vs Command/AP ret (SoC): currently same single-target damage potential, but spellret has better mana sustain/proccing and a huge AoE advantage from SP scaling Consecration; post-patch spellret keeps the mana/AoE edge and may only be "slightly lower" ST if the Holy Strike change isn't overtuned. SoC's only advantage is an easier gearing path (no need for pure-SP items) — [[d:paladin#1465407836824797339]], [[d:paladin#1465408137958785156]], [[d:paladin#1465408296482508954]] (Excinerus, 2026-01-26; single detailed source).
- **[Ret]** SoC (Seal of Command) is described as "lottery" gameplay — needs to both proc and crit, and on short fights the RNG is completely unreliable; multiple posters (Excinerus, Grey, Lhian via forum) have suggested splitting the proc into two smaller hits to reduce variance — [[d:paladin#1465409556351025266]], [[d:paladin#1465015821448249637]] (Flammekat/Excinerus, 2026-01-25/26; unresolved suggestion, not confirmed for patch).
- **[Prot]** Prot Paladin is broadly agreed to remain an offtank in top guilds because bears (Feral Druid) are stronger; the 1.18.1 threat buffs are viewed as meaningful QoL but not enough to flip that ("these threat changes are not big enough for bears OR paladins... that I foresee that changing in competitive guilds") — [[d:paladin#1465182177024282674]], [[d:paladin#1465182243352743977]] (Proph, 2026-01-25; broadly agreed, some disagreement on how big bears' lead actually is, e.g. [[d:paladin#1465220285006610453]]).
- **[Prot]** Paladin tank scaling problem stated as: from naked to #1 K40-BiS parse, prot's main threat ability (Holy Strike) only grows ~3.06x (424→~1300 dmg) vs. Fire Mage's Fireball growing ~3.3x (650→2150), i.e. paladin scales worse across gear progression than top DPS specs — [[d:paladin#1465180967265239231]] (Grey, 2026-01-25).
- **[Ret]** AP ret vs SP ret tension: SP/hybrid rets already farm WoC; some players try to convince AP rets to farm WoC too via "sp propaganda", with the joke that "ap ret is just warrior cosplay" — [[d:paladin#1465125250969899029]], [[d:paladin#1465125329176629600]] (Wilhelm/donnyhathawayenjoyer, 2026-01-25).
- **[Ret]** Overall Ret Paladin server reputation: "pretty good by the standards of most of the server" but speedrun/top-end guilds consider Ret "non-meta" — [[d:paladin#1466647259058929664]], [[d:paladin#1466647586554384469]] (Grey, 2026-01-30).
- **[Ret]** The class's overall design is described (by an experienced prot/theorycrafter) as "basically just a regular Enhancement Shaman but in a new context, with slightly shifted priorities" — i.e. Prot Paladin ≈ SoR Ret with a shield — [[d:paladin#1466658237410705491]] (Grey, 2026-01-30; single opinion, illustrative not literal).

## Leveling
- **[Prot]** At level 20 (fresh respec to prot), recommended rotation: always use Holy Strike; solo, Judgement Seal of Wisdom then kill with Seal of Righteousness (or just stay on Seal of Wisdom/Seal of Light once available if fighting many mobs); in dungeons, mostly Seal of Wisdom to avoid running OOM and pull pack after pack — [[d:paladin#1465095038680830125]] (Pastor, 2026-01-26).
- **[Prot]** For strikes while leveling, use your highest-damage strike; in dungeons always Holy Strike — [[d:paladin#1465095694464585840]] (Arle, 2026-01-26, +1).
- **[Prot]** AoE while leveling: can start doing small-pack AoE (3-4 mobs) even before Holy Shield, though it becomes much easier after getting Holy Shield — [[d:paladin#1465096099646935257]] (Arle, 2026-01-26).
- **[General]** Free turtle mount available via a quest at the Darkmoon Faire starting around level 18 ("Tortas egg" quest); gives ~9% movement speed initially, scaling further per a linked message — [[d:paladin#1465059618043465950]], [[d:paladin#1465059986181853216]], [[d:paladin#1465059855608971306]] (Arle, 2026-01-25).
- **[General]** Leveling macro tip: use `/startattack` in your strike macros to avoid interrupting auto-attack; requires the CleverIODMacros addon (Cleveroids) for `/startattack` to function correctly — [[d:paladin#1465096327376539823]], [[d:paladin#1465096654838562866]], [[d:paladin#1465097098813898756]] (Meta23/Pastor/Excinerus, 2026-01-26).
- **[Contested]** Prot leveling aura choice at level 20 (Retribution Aura vs Devotion Aura) asked but not answered in this window — [[d:paladin#1465098631672496309]] (Korn, 2026-01-26; question unanswered).
- **[Professions]** For a paladin planning to tank later while leveling/farming gold: Engineering strongly recommended (sappers + Field Repair Bot needed since Consecration's design is "effectively balanced around the assumption you're using both"); Herbalism useful for Black Lotus farming in Dire Maul/Stratholme; Mining more niche (ZG and other farms); Blacksmithing mainly useful for a couple of BoP transmog items only. Disenchanting is best offloaded to a bank alt; a level-60 rogue alt is ideal for lockpicking + disenchanting loot — [[d:paladin#1465025682735239312]], [[d:paladin#1465026024936046774]] (Grey, 2026-01-25).
- **[Contested]** Best leveling seal for Ret: Grey argues Seal of Righteousness (SoR) is generally optimal for leveling because it's gear-agnostic (works with any weapon), whereas Seal of Command (SoC) needs a slow, high-strength weapon or you're "griefing yourself"; Jesse Potter argues SoC has always been better for Ret leveling in practice because low-level gear gives "fuck all SP" to boost SoR, and this becomes even more true post-1.18.1 since Holy Strike — free to cast — also gains weapon-damage scaling, making a slow/hard-hitting weapon doubly valuable. No resolution reached — [[d:paladin#1466784078421557269]], [[d:paladin#1466784207161397289]], [[d:paladin#1466784257820065976]], [[d:paladin#1466784957845475483]] (Grey/Jesse Potter, 2026-01-30).
- **[Leveling]** Crossover point for Sanctity Aura to out-value Improved Retribution Aura is roughly ~700 SP — below that (e.g. while solo farming/leveling) Improved Retribution Aura is better; some players consider a dedicated "solo farming" talent spec that goes into Ret for Improved Retribution Aura rather than running their raid Sanctity-Aura prot/holy build — [[d:paladin#1466023428325904477]], [[d:paladin#1466023772069822544]], [[d:paladin#1466023904295260386]] (Mima/Jesse Potter, 2026-01-28).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Prot]** Prot single-target rotation described as: Holy Strike on cooldown, Judgement, Consecration, Holy Shield block-charges — this is essentially the entire "rotation," confirmed by multiple posters as having "0 choice" since Crusader Strike shares Holy Strike's cooldown and is never worth pressing over it — [[d:paladin#1465257361684824187]] (Sock, 2026-01-26), [[d:paladin#1465246363888390227]] (dusty, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot]** Estimated threat split at peak output (pre-patch, geared): ~800 tps Holy Strike, ~400 tps Judgement (JoR), ~350 tps SoR (via weapon), ~150 tps autos, out of ~1600-1700 total tps window vs. a warrior's Saberslash+Deathwish+Enrage+trinkets peak — [[d:paladin#1465259977299202080]] (Grey, 2026-01-25). angels1m separately estimated ~35% threat boost to Holy Strike, ~34% to Judgement, and ~80% boost to Holy Shield threat post-patch, with some loss from a TF-proc threat interaction — [[d:paladin#1465260649603928146]] (angels1m, 2026-01-25; single source, unconfirmed).
- **[Ret]** Repentance is valued as a Ret rotational/utility tool even outside PvE damage rotations for PvP peel; not used purely as damage filler.
- **[Ret]** Haste vs. Crit for SoC-based Ret: one experienced player reports switching from a crit-stacking build (Gloves of the Tortured Martyr, Vengeance Wrists, Cloak of Dominion, Don Julio ring) to a haste-focused build and getting better practical results (tanks perceiving him as closer to threat-capping, healers noticing more instant-heal "sniping"), though the haste benefit wasn't subjectively noticeable below ~12% buffed haste; rule of thumb cited: 1% haste ≈ 0.03s swing-time reduction, and haste breakpoints matter more than raw % (a rough, self-described "botched," calc suggests ~8%/28%/41%/50% buffed haste on a 3.8-speed weapon are where you gain an extra auto+Windfury Totem proc+SoC proc simultaneously in a given minute) — [[d:paladin#1466658593439879228]], [[d:paladin#1466795138541031618]], [[d:paladin#1466795439687991337]], [[d:paladin#1466795694739165296]] (Jesse Potter/Mithreon, 2026-01-28; single detailed source, self-flagged as approximate).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot]** Consecration remains the core AoE threat tool; frontloading only affects the base-damage portion of the first tick (see changes section), not the SP-scaling portion, so it's a modest early-threat help rather than a big AoE buff — [[d:paladin#1465195698617188617]] (Silverhand, 2026-01-26).
- **[Prot]** In current (pre-patch) numbers, full-BiS-spellpower Consecration does ~150 damage per tick (or ~100 in a mitigation set); Grey's suggested rework (frontload scaling too) would raise this to ~225/~150 respectively, for ~460/~310 instant threat — enough to out-threat a Rejuvenation/food pull but still not an "AoE tank menace" vs a warrior/mage going all-in with sappers and immolation oils — [[d:paladin#1465054796603064411]] (Grey, 2026-01-25; hypothetical, not implemented).
- **[Ret]** Spellpower-scaling Consecration on a Spellret build gives a "huge AOE advantage" over Command/AP builds, cited repeatedly as SoR's main edge over SoC both pre- and post-patch — [[d:paladin#1465407836824797339]] (Excinerus, 2026-01-26).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Target avoidance number ("crush immunity") vs. melee Twin (K40) using Unbalancing Strike (-100 defense debuff, i.e. -16% combined avoidance / +4% crit-taken) requires **118.4%** effective avoidance, vs the normal 102.4% threshold — even a maximally mitigation-stacked T3 set (Gluth's Collar, Nordrassil Ring, Vek'lor Ring, Styleen's, Glyph of Deflection, all avoidance-enchanted) barely clears this with only ~1-2% to spare, using gear that in some cases drops from the boss itself; the Parry talent line (Anticipation/Deflection, 8.2% avoidance) is recommended during progression to make the number achievable at all — [[d:paladin#1465416367967572164]], [[d:paladin#1465418223225798708]] (Grey, 2026-01-26).
- **[Prot]** Prot should stay avoidance-capped (fully buffed) until the raid's DPS reach K40 gear, per Grey: "t2.5/t3 vs t2.5/t3 - paladin has comfortable threat lead; t3.5 vs t3.5 - paladin needs to sweat, hard" — [[d:paladin#1465171549215789326]] (Grey, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot]** Paladin gear inherently has poor avoidance itemization vs. other tanks: K40 helm gives only 0.6 avoidance vs bear's 1.25 and warrior's 1.25-5 — [[d:paladin#1465415180614635695]] (Grey, 2026-01-26).
- **[Prot]** Example SP stacking for prot tank (t2.5 set, Mark of the Champion): 613 unbuffed against undead base, +132 WoC, +150 flask, +35 dreamtonic, +35 GAE (Greater Arcane Elixir), +15 dreamshard, +60 oil, +140 JoTC, +130 Essence of Sapphiron, +55 Remains pet = 1180 stable, peaks at 1365 — [[d:paladin#1465279185848238143]] (Grey, 2026-01-26). A separate Grey breakdown gave 579 from gear +132 WoC +130 sapph active +55 remains +150 flask +35 dreamtonic +35 GAE +36 brilliant wizard oil +15 dreamshard +140 JoTC — [[d:paladin#1465050716438790277]] (Grey, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot]** Judgement of the Crusader gives +140 SP to the whole raid (or +161 with Improved Judgement talent on the caster), even without SP food/consumables; only reliable if applied early in a fight, less useful on trash/fast-dying bosses — [[d:paladin#1465195089839263814]], [[d:paladin#1465202654962782269]] (Silverhand/Proph, 2026-01-26).
- **[General]** Str now has a small but real place on prot gear post-patch because Holy Strike's weapon-damage component scales with AP (dev intent per Turtle team), but the amount is still small relative to full-holy scaling — repeatedly debated as still "not enough to be worth 24 str with no stam/avoidance" — [[d:paladin#1465241302663954651]], [[d:paladin#1465242685848817829]] (dusty/Grey, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot]** Concrete post-patch strength values: 30 Str = 1.5 Shield Block Value + 4.97 Holy damage on Holy Strike only; T3's current 71 Str = 3.55 SBV + 11.76 Holy Strike damage. For comparison, 12 SP alone gives about the same Holy Strike damage as that 30 Str, but SP additionally boosts SoR, Consecration, and Judgement, so SP still vastly outperforms Str per point even post-patch — [[d:paladin#1465828487091978445]], [[d:paladin#1465828943457288414]], [[d:paladin#1465829022490427434]] (Excinerus/Grey, 2026-01-28).
- **[Prot]** Block Value (SBV) is a rare stat in vanilla-style itemization — Styleen's Impenetrable Cuirass was reportedly the first item ever to carry it, and most SBV items in Turtle are Paladin-oriented — [[d:paladin#1465827614940725248]], [[d:paladin#1465827991195226307]] (Grey, 2026-01-28).
- **[Prot]** Avoidance scales non-linearly near the cap: going from 98% to 99% total avoidance means taking, on average, 50% less damage (not 1% less) — [[d:paladin#1465995223309094973]] (Drekalo, 2026-01-27).
- **[Prot]** "Def cap" is described as a misunderstanding — the real target is total avoidance percentage, not a raw Defense rating number; any Parry/Dodge/Block rating contributes toward the same avoidance total. Silverhand states the target is 100% avoidance against level-63 mobs (or 102.4% against level 60), which is 55% avoidance before Holy Shield's contribution (57.4% against level 60) — note this specific 100%-vs-lvl63 framing differs from the more commonly cited 102.4%-vs-level-63 breakpoint used elsewhere in this chunk (e.g. crush-immunity math above), possibly reflecting a mob-level mixup by the poster — [[d:paladin#1465748254464409813]], [[d:paladin#1465748329257500672]], [[d:paladin#1465748476632498463]] (Silverhand, 2026-01-27).
- **[Prot]** Neltharion's Tear trinket gives +44 SP (the highest flat SP available from any pre-K40 trinket, for non-Undead/Demon targets) plus a reduced chance for your taunt to be resisted; its SP also benefits Exorcism, Consecration, and weapon procs like Thunderfury since those scale off SP too — [[d:paladin#1465925725180792985]], [[d:paladin#1465928153007521843]] (Not Normal/Aelgagar, 2026-01-26).

Only one weapon-enhancement consumable (stone OR oil) can be active at a time regardless; weapon oils in general have been nerfed, but Brilliant Wizard Oil is still recommended over Elemental Sharpening Stone specifically for tanking — [[d:paladin#1466354962886820054]], [[d:paladin#1466355155216633919]], [[d:paladin#1466355191417933874]], [[d:paladin#1466355243049816199]], [[d:paladin#1466356260977770668]], [[d:paladin#1466357706796306473]] (Hogbobson/Aelgagar/Destroses, 2026-01-28).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Prot]** Mana economy: prot mana consumption at "full blast" (heavy strike/judgement/consecrate spam) is very high with no built-in regen mechanic, but can be reduced to near-zero with good seal/spell management; spellstrike gear is not required to sustain WoC uptime for a well-managed prot rotation — [[d:paladin#1465527305915994346]], [[d:paladin#1465527376351068293]], [[d:paladin#1465527530973958195]] (Grey/dusty, 2026-01-26/27).
- **[Prot]** "Brainwasher"/respec-swap strategies (e.g. swapping into Vengeprot Necrodin for trash then back to normal Prot for boss fights) are used by slower-paced guilds but avoided by speedrunning guilds since a single mid-raid respec swap can cost minutes multiplied across the raid ("2hr naxx becomes 2hr 6min... 4 hours of collective timewaste across 40 people") — [[d:paladin#1465229551486505157]], [[d:paladin#1465230141583261866]] (gastro/Grey, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot]** Vengeprot (Vengeance spec) Necrodin is used situationally for trash tanking/DPS between bosses (e.g. Kara40 Anomalus trash → swap back to Prot for "Echo Infernals"); vengeprot also gives higher avoidance than expected in some setups — [[d:paladin#1465229551486505157]], [[d:paladin#1465229799739097119]], [[d:paladin#1465234219625087080]] (gastro/Seviry, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot]** Nightfall (NF) is commonly used by prot paladins to "pretend DPS" on fights where they aren't tanking (e.g. BWL Infernals encounter, where the "tank" is just holding adds, not doing real damage) — described as a way for prots to occasionally deal damage without raid judgment — [[d:paladin#1465227194950225920]], [[d:paladin#1465228697501040826]], [[d:paladin#1465228866913308818]] (dusty/Aelgagar, 2026-01-25).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Detailed BWL boss-by-boss healing guide (paladin holy healer perspective), by fight order: Boss1 can stun in melee — use Free Action Potion or bubble if close, otherwise reactive "whack-a-mole" tank healing. Boss2 — classic "3 Flash of Light + 1 Holy Shock" rotation while ticking an AoE heal on the raid; if you get the Adrenaline debuff, run to the left column and hide behind it (Bubble does not stop the debuff). Boss3 — stand where ranged stand to avoid cleave; preceding trash "Supression room with whelps" is a mana gauntlet, watch mana. Boss4 — stand with ranged, track your debuff, use Holy Shock on the active tank when the fire breath is incoming. Boss5/Boss6 — tank-and-spank, heal the active tank / don't stand in front (Boss6 AoEs the raid). Boss7 (Chromaggus) — dispel all debuffs, hide behind cover when others hide; Hourglass Sand dispels the "golden" debuff (otherwise repeated stuns); expect to need ~3 Sands for the pull. Final boss — heal the active tank, don't stand in front; if feared into the boss, bubble (roughly 4/5 times you still get breathed and die) — [[d:paladin#1465429349154160784]] (Mithreon, 2026-01-27; single detailed source, well received, +bubble tip below).
- **[Holy]** Chromaggus tech: you can bubble one of the bronze/gold debuffs yourself to save an Hourglass Sand and sell the saved Sand on the AH — [[d:paladin#1465430385386193027]] (Beldrin, 2026-01-27, +5 reactions).
- **[Holy]** A Paladin healer spamming Flash of Light is called out as the ideal healer for the "Shadowflame"-type mechanic (5000 immediate shadow dmg + 2000/2s dot) because Flash of Light isn't blocked by Corruption effects or forced-cat-form style silences the way other heals can be — [[d:paladin#1465004078382842137]] (Grey, 2026-01-25).
- **[Holy]** Naxx Maexxna Divine Intervention (DI) strategy: pre-pull a Paladin DIs a healer, who pops out mid-first-webwrap to heal the MT; assign a (possibly the same) Paladin to DI the same healer again before every subsequent webwrap; bonus points if that Paladin has a Soulstone active on themselves so they can self-res after DI'ing (ideally timed so the Soulstone res also refunds ~2k mana). A Holy Paladin with the Improved Lay on Hands talent should also LoH the tank just before a webwrap for a 30% armor buff that lasts the ~2-minute Web/Enrage window — [[d:paladin#1465797002422911018]], [[d:paladin#1466097683629342991]], [[d:paladin#1466097719415410709]], [[d:paladin#1466097904178692189]] (Grey/Jesse Potter/Aelgagar, 2026-01-26/28).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Prot]** Engineering (sappers/FRD), Herbalism (Black Lotus in Dire Maul/Stratholme), Mining (niche, ZG), Blacksmithing (mostly BoP transmog only) — see Leveling section for full detail and citation.
- **[General]** Disenchanting best handled via a low-level bank alt; a rogue alt is ideal for combining lockpicking (lockboxes) with disenchanting since one character can do both — [[d:paladin#1465026024936046774]] (Grey, 2026-01-25).
- **[Prot/farming]** Field Repair Bot Deluxe (FRD) + Vampirism (lifesteal) gear lets a paladin solo/duo-farm large single-pulls (e.g. full Scarlet Monastery Cathedral/Graveyard, Stratholme "strat live") for gold, but the FRD's charge depletes fast under big pulls — roughly 30 seconds for a full SM Cathedral/Graveyard pull — and repair costs add up quickly; mid-combat repairs are possible via a Repair Bot (separate item from FRD) without needing to stop; running 2-4 FRDs simultaneously is used by serious farmers but considered janky/annoying — [[d:paladin#1465772663304618009]], [[d:paladin#1465772915839602752]], [[d:paladin#1465777544908374099]], [[d:paladin#1465778028675334338]] (Torbz/Beldrin/Grey, 2026-01-27).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Bug]** Concoction of the Arcane Giant / Concoction of Dreamwater SP-portion non-stacking bug — see Consumables section (confirmed, reported since Nov 2025, still present).
- **[Bug]** Some stat displays (e.g. character sheet SP) don't visibly update immediately when certain set-bonus procs (T0.5) trigger, even though the effect is applied — attributed to general vanilla-client stat-display quirks rather than the proc not working — [[d:paladin#1465006503391133882]], [[d:paladin#1465006742587969592]] (Kayno/Silverhand, 2026-01-25).
- **[Bug]** Holy Strike's original 1.18.1 patch-note text incorrectly said it deals "part of its damage as Physical damage" — this was a documentation error (Lhian corrected it); the ability is fully Holy damage — see Changes section.
- **[Bug]** Improved Devotion Aura and Improved Retribution Aura talent bonuses do not actually apply until you manually re-cast (refresh) the aura after taking the talent — a long-standing, reported-but-unfixed bug; practical consequence is you must remember to refresh your aura after every respec or you silently lose the talent's bonus — [[d:paladin#1465965614307872901]], [[d:paladin#1465966589852782829]], [[d:paladin#1465966728864338003]] (Leo, 2026-01-27).
- **[Bug]** Consecration (and other persistent "object"-type spells like Flamestrike's DoT) roll a resist chance on every tick, against every target, and always have — but resisted ticks only started showing up in the combat log recently (a Turtle logging fix shortly before Christmas 2025); previously a resisted tick just silently vanished from the log. Confirmed via 3000+ ticks of dummy-testing by Destroses and independently via long-term leveling-alt observation by Grey (comparing consistent tick counts at level 60 vs. variable counts on low-level alts) — [[d:paladin#1465964135782289530]], [[d:paladin#1465965049284661412]], [[d:paladin#1465930310075285556]] (Grey/Destroses, 2026-01-27/28).
- **[Bug/unclear]** Whether the Precision talent's +3% spell hit is actually applying to Consecration's resist chance is in question — Destroses saw streaks of consecutive resisted Consecrate ticks in testing suggesting it might not apply, but also has other logs suggesting it does — inconclusive — [[d:paladin#1465930666972549130]], [[d:paladin#1465931023421411378]] (Destroses, 2026-01-26).
- **[Bug/unclear]** Judgement of Righteousness (and Judgement generally) appears to never miss in logs, despite being stated as using melee-hit rules (can't be dodged/parried, but "intended" to use melee hit per Aelgagar) — a player noted this while combat-log sleuthing and got no clear confirmation whether it's a bug or working as designed — [[d:paladin#1466583042507145392]], [[d:paladin#1466589054954110997]], [[d:paladin#1466590413962871012]], [[d:paladin#1466591364270522429]] (Not Normal/Aelgagar, 2026-01-30).
