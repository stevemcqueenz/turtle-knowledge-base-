## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Bug, dated 2025-11-05]** A patch meant only to fix "effects from items such as Runeblade of Baron Rivendare now properly reapply after resurrection" accidentally made nearly all on-hit "spellstrike" item effects (Equip: Adds X elemental dmg to melee attacks), weapon reflect procs (Hand of Ragnaros), and even Sanctuary scale drastically with SP for a few hours — e.g. a Lightning Strike ring proc jumped to 33 dmg/hit, a BoE hunter got ~720 dps from spellstrikes alone, Hand of Ragnaros reflect got "juiced" — widely assumed a decimal/multiplier bug, not intended, and hotfixed the same day — [[d:paladin#1435434616143085658]], [[d:paladin#1435438469294985256]], [[d:paladin#1435443200075763793]], [[d:paladin#1435446578419601469]], [[d:paladin#1435475464675393538]] (confirmed fixed same day). Dream's Herald weapon came out of it still broken (base damage apparently deleted) even after the hotfix — [[d:paladin#1435482937805115454]].
- **[Bug]** Separately, Seal of Righteousness measured doing ~40% less damage for 1-2 hours right after that maintenance before self-correcting; not a lasting nerf — [[d:paladin#1435985318820970527]], [[d:paladin#1435985604423975055]].
- **[Bug]** Also same maintenance window: an FRD/Skullflame-Shield-style "no durability loss on failed proc" farming exploit ("FRD cheese") was removed, and durability now depletes normally — this previously enabled very large uninterrupted Stratholme pulls; Skullflame Shield now needs a repair bot or a swap shield for long farm sessions — [[d:paladin#1435431768319594581]], [[d:paladin#1436031341958267013]], [[d:paladin#1436035252404621512]].
- **[General]** Judgement is coded as a melee attack, not a spell — deliberately, since in old patch 1.12 Judgement being spell-coded forced Paladins to itemize spell hit, melee hit, and melee stats simultaneously ("basically impossible to itemize"); one of the core reasons Ret was bad back then — [[d:paladin#1433868913984143391]] (Wulf), [[d:paladin#1433870392367517787]] [[d:paladin#1433871682195427388]] (Dodging).
- **[Ret]** SoR was deliberately buffed/made SP-usable in a past patch partly to reduce SoC's dominance, which incidentally improved everything else with SP scaling but left AP-scaling abilities (Repentance, Hammer of Wrath) behind; SP on tier gear was also nerfed, and Dragonbreath Chili's SP-scaling coefficient was cut from 100% to 33% around the same time ("triple whammy" for AP/hybrid) — [[d:paladin#1435646857282981998]], [[d:paladin#1435648569314115734]], [[d:paladin#1435651567251488869]].
- **[Prot]** Reckoning does not function entirely as designer Lhian intended, but is still considered good, especially for AoE/leveling — [[d:paladin#1434217950201774171]] (theoloras/warkrak).
- **[PvP]** Divine Shield was nerfed to the point some feel Divine Protection is now the better defensive choice; Blessing of Freedom also got an increased cooldown, widely believed to be a PvP-motivated nerf — [[d:paladin#1434448892124074045]] [[d:paladin#1434451030116143307]] (Southshore Crusader Vidnar), [[d:paladin#1434452224259395654]] (Mithreon).
- **[General]** Consensus complaint: Paladin seals/auras/blessings are locked behind talent capstones while Shaman totems are largely baseline; many veterans want all Seals/Auras/Blessings baseline — [[d:paladin#1433376676351574106]] (dusty), [[d:paladin#1434447802145968168]] [[d:paladin#1434533537784270858]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1434534422127972463]] (Destroses).
- **[Threat]** Salvation (threat reduction) was nerfed relatively recently — [[d:paladin#1434538018731065454]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Dev direction, live in-channel Q&A]** Lhian confirmed he is reviewing ALL paladin tier-set bonuses for the next patch and invited tags on ones needing change — [[d:paladin#1435001401062326322]] [[d:paladin#1435001489847222394]]. He explicitly named Prot T2.5's 3-piece (+12% Holy Strike damage) as a prime example of a "problematic" bonus (too automatically strong), and said he's targeting sets that "actively harm gearing," not weak/situational ones — nerfing a bonus purely to force a gearing path is "literally the opposite" of his goal — [[d:paladin#1435019192536535080]] [[d:paladin#1435024461282676879]]. He confirmed Prot's core problem (Holy Strike carrying almost all threat) is tied to Righteous Fury and overall threat design and "not an easily solvable problem" via set bonuses alone, but said "changing the T2.5 bonus would be a step" toward moving threat off Holy Strike into the rest of the kit — [[d:paladin#1435030814604918954]] [[d:paladin#1435030968699453520]] [[d:paladin#1435032864457625641]]. He also confirmed devs won't add a Seal-of-Blood-style (TBC) guaranteed proc-on-every-swing mechanic, which is why SoC won't get a "damage every swing" rework — [[d:paladin#1435227613978296330]] (Jesse Potter relaying Lhian), and confirmed a future patch aims to buff SoC specifically, exact scope unknown — [[d:paladin#1435153151371251723]]. He also confirmed "not all set bonuses have to be good [for everything]" — some are intentionally situational/flavor — [[d:paladin#1435024002782461953]].
- **[Dev direction]** Community (Destroses) pushed back that Prot/Ret are uniquely tier-dependent since, unlike other tank/dps specs, there are almost no good non-tier "off pieces" for Paladin threat gearing — Dodging agreed this is a real gap devs should address alongside any set-bonus nerfs — [[d:paladin#1435024756775719064]] [[d:paladin#1435024953157357629]].
- **[Design philosophy]** Lhian: "as long as Strikes share a CD, they shouldn't both be Single Target" — a stated constraint on any Holy Strike/Crusader Strike redesign, and he has said he doesn't want Holy Cleave made baseline — [[d:paladin#1435991324556922881]] [[d:paladin#1435991486482092084]] (Jesse Potter relaying).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret]** Both SP/SoR ("spellret") and AP/SoC are viable; SP/SoR pulls ahead as gear improves: ~within 5% at Ambershire-tier gear, 10%+ at Nordanaar/K40-tier gear per raid — [[d:paladin#1433446944214093875]] (Beldrin), [[d:paladin#1433447400848101509]] [[d:paladin#1433448301717360680]] (Mithreon/Beldrin). AP gets relatively better once BWL drops — [[d:paladin#1433448566935912570]]. Recommendation for new players: play spellret until Kara40 gear — [[d:paladin#1433448725375614986]]. On Nordanaar the gap "isn't really considerable," but on Ambershire ("TA") it's much larger because SoC really suffers without Windfury Totem access — [[d:paladin#1435075885500727467]] (dusty).
- **[Ret]** Concrete Naxx parse comparison (Jesse Potter, mid-progress Nordanaar): top SoC parser ≈1491 dps vs top SoR parser ≈1658 dps; SoC generally ~200 dps behind the average SoR user — [[d:paladin#1435206281316007947]] [[d:paladin#1435206095982301196]]. SoC alone still does solid damage (1k–1.5k dps) but hundreds of dps behind SoR; SoR needs Scythe of the Harvest/a proc weapon + full consumables + Windfury buff to beat SoC — without those, SoC does better on average — [[d:paladin#1433447593144225864]] [[d:paladin#1433450503726960641]].
- **[Ret]** "AP ret even at Kara40 is weaker than hybrid ret" — [[d:paladin#1433848439283781763]] (Iseut).
- **[Ret]** Necrodin (aka "the Lost set" build): stacks two different T2 3-piece spellstrike bonuses (ret + prot) to fish extra procs every swing; considered a real, competitive build once BWL is available (not just meme), possibly the best ret build for that phase. BiS variant: proc weapon (e.g. TF-tier) + Lost set (custom Turtle gear) + T2 3p ret/prot + Sigil of Ancient Accord (MC trinket) — [[d:paladin#1433464437414559746]] [[d:paladin#1433473685896953877]] [[d:paladin#1433473755807748186]] [[d:paladin#1435159293988175923]]. Reference log: a Nordanaar ret using this build topped parses on last K40 boss — https://turtlogs.com/viewer/50147/base?history_state=0 — [[d:paladin#1433464772296310816]].
- **[Ret]** Ret T1 8-piece and T2 8-piece set bonuses both considered "trash" — [[d:paladin#1433858510570913943]] [[d:paladin#1433858651528892446]]. T1 is generally "not good," so pre-raid BiS remains BiS until BWL; T1 3-piece is mainly taken for the 3% hit with SoC, not the bonus itself — [[d:paladin#1433680434704679045]] [[d:paladin#1434495996989018184]] [[d:paladin#1434496108138201108]]. T2 3-piece is required to make SoR strong — [[d:paladin#1434497262985154671]]. T2.5 3-piece was/is considered the "pinnacle" of ret gear until K40 in some builds — [[d:paladin#1434541700088332389]]. T2's 5-piece was nerfed (~2-3 months after Kara launched, per Beldrin) and is now "completely useless"; only the 3-piece is worth using — [[d:paladin#1434740783063371837]] [[d:paladin#1435037783499604049]].
- **[Ret PvP]** AP ret on Ambershire considered solid/top of the pack, though nothing beats Shaman currently; disputed whether ret/rogue actually beat shaman on top parses (single strong players can skew perception) — [[d:paladin#1433898382535163965]] [[d:paladin#1433899362433634335]] [[d:paladin#1433900212652347495]].
- **[Prot]** Community consensus: Bear (Feral Druid) tank is far ahead of Prot Paladin on both threat and mitigation; even a fully mitigation-specced bear reportedly out-threats a fully threat-specced prot pal — [[d:paladin#1433719255890923590]] [[d:paladin#1433735254555754506]] [[d:paladin#1433739462575390781]]. angels1m's numeric breakdown: T3 prot 2/8 good, 4/8 "worse than any other prot set bonus in the game," 6/8 "trash after the Exo nerf," 8/8 exists mainly to compensate lack of SP on prot pieces — [[d:paladin#1435023650704068699]].
- **[Melee-heal hybrid]** Hybrid ret/holy "melee healer" builds are not worth it — bad at both DPS and healing (gear conflicts, poor Nightfall uptime while weaving) — [[d:paladin#1433682724174041231]] [[d:paladin#1433682750073868288]] [[d:paladin#1433689671371718738]].
- **[PvP]** Shockadin (Holy/Ret hybrid) exists as a rare meme PvP spec: burst is "laughable" but if geared correctly (not all-in on SP) it's very hard to kill; fun/flexible for leveling but not optimal at any endgame stage — [[d:paladin#1435036177555128450]] [[d:paladin#1435036278617014423]] [[d:paladin#1435036437828604095]] [[d:paladin#1435036576928370768]].
- **[General]** Overall state as of early Nov: "Paladin is in an 'ok' state, no one thinks it's OP... a semi-working tangle of jank," but "dissent is forming for Ret and Prot" specifically — [[d:paladin#1435596781248577658]] [[d:paladin#1435596963549810819]]. ## Leveling
- **[Prot]** Stat priority at very low level (e.g. level 19-20 Deadmines): Stamina then Intellect; big power spike at level 20 with Consecration, though it doesn't directly fix incoming-damage problems — [[d:paladin#1434250349149028517]]. Consolidated later advice (PerchyBrown/gastro): Stamina + Strength (or Stam/Int/SP) primary; maintain Blessing of Wisdom on self; open with a threat spell then sit in Seal of Wisdom; "maintaining a constant mana pool is more important than a high mana pool"; Consecration is your main damage dealer and is expensive so Seal/Judgement of Wisdom is essential; add Spell Power once a few levels in — [[d:paladin#1435861925065130065]]…[[d:paladin#1435863907460710581]]; Agility "won't hurt but shouldn't be prioritized" — [[d:paladin#1435867217110503575]]. Paladin damage (even Prot's) is entirely Holy-school spell damage, not physical, which is why Agi/weapon stats matter less — [[d:paladin#1435744659853803611]].
- **[Prot]** Deep-prot dungeon tanking at 40-54: over-stacking spellpower early hurts survivability; crushing blows become a factor once tanking mobs several levels above you — [[d:paladin#1434104805705384006]] [[d:paladin#1434105286381142048]] [[d:paladin#1434106689354989699]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Prot]** As Main Tank: pop one Crusader Strike (CS) for the Righteous Strikes talent buff, then spam Holy Strike (HS) for threat since CS generates less threat than HS; as Off-Tank (2nd on threat), can favor HS occasionally for the buff and lean more on CS — [[d:paladin#1434271787859837028]] [[d:paladin#1434273240657957097]] (Domy).
- **[Ret]** Zeal stacks (from Crusader Strike use) grant extra seal-proc chance; 3 stacks ≈ +9% seal procs — [[d:paladin#1434277788629598238]] (single source).
- **[Ret]** Crusader Strike deals more overall damage to average (lower-armor) targets; Holy Strike (ignores armor, holy damage) is only better against very high-armor targets (e.g. 10k+ armor in PvP) — [[d:paladin#1434391765665710232]] [[d:paladin#1434406149104336989]]. Deliberate design: HS has little/no weapon-damage scaling so it can't outright replace CS as in the pre-"CC2" era — [[d:paladin#1434396625069019249]] [[d:paladin#1434396883949850684]].
- **[Ret]** SoC proc chance/PPM is calculated off base weapon speed, unaffected by haste — Zeal's haste does not hurt SoC proc rate — [[d:paladin#1434390991317500028]] [[d:paladin#1434391161895780413]].
- **[Ret]** SoC has a fixed ~0.5s proc delay after landing; likely a relic to preserve seal-twisting relevance rather than a bug — [[d:paladin#1434502235487866900]] [[d:paladin#1434502434268643398]] [[d:paladin#1434502644772245626]]. Seal twisting exists on Turtle WoW but is confirmed NOT a DPS increase, unlike classic/TBC — [[d:paladin#1434497802070655096]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit/weapon skill]** Melee hit cap for raid bosses is 8% total; each point of weapon skill above 300 is worth 0.2% hit and 2% glancing-damage reduction (glancing improves up to 315 skill, past which there is no benefit); base miss chance vs a boss is 8%, so gear+talents+weapon skill must cover that — [[d:paladin#1435130681322901625]] [[d:paladin#1435130910981886074]] [[d:paladin#1435131153295085609]] [[d:paladin#1435853816766533697]] [[d:paladin#1435853838874710077]] [[d:paladin#1435854144521900195]]. No benefit to weapon skill above 315 for Paladins specifically (glancing-blows-beat-crits theory only applies, if at all, to Warriors/Rogues) — [[d:paladin#1434672393715978332]] [[d:paladin#1434673882270269450]].
- **[Prot hit]** Total needed ≈8% (3.4% for humans due to the 1.6% hit from 308 weapon skill racial+book); breakdown: 3% from Precision talent, 1% from the weapon-skill book quest, ~4% from gear. Recommended gear path: Darksoul Leggings (crafted), Helm of the Executioner (Stratholme, Baron Rivendare), Ancient Warfare Text quests for all 1h weapons (gives both 1H and 2H skill) — [[d:paladin#1434791257128964227]] [[d:paladin#1434791440810246245]] [[d:paladin#1434792820081819690]] [[d:paladin#1434927836296249597]]. Reaffirmed later: assuming the book, you need 4% from gear — [[d:paladin#1435226145233703003]].
- **[Stat by role]** Holy uses spell crit; Prot and Ret want melee crit — [[d:paladin#1435227490888187995]].
- **[Ret]** Spell hit only affects Holy Shock, Exorcism, and Holy Wrath for spellret, so it barely matters for the core rotation — BUT it also governs whether elemental weapon procs land (life-drain from Ashbringer, Hand of Ragnaros proc, other "spellstrike" elemental procs), and Holy Water/Sapper/Dense Dynamite — this is why spellrets value spell-hit/SP trinkets like Neltharion's Tear even though they don't need spell hit for their Seals/Strikes — [[d:paladin#1435800592680357899]] [[d:paladin#1435805642144223304]] [[d:paladin#1435805821220028556]] [[d:paladin#1435846369553416223]] [[d:paladin#1435811856974352527]]. JoR (Judgement of Righteousness) used to require spell hit but that was changed months ago (Judgement is melee-coded) — [[d:paladin#1435811978705768448]].
- **[Ret]** Consecration damage scales exclusively with spellpower — [[d:paladin#1433447470066827264]].
- **[Ret]** SoR counts as a second melee hit, doubling the proc rate of on-hit spellstrike effects vs auto-attack alone — [[d:paladin#1433519811396243668]] [[d:paladin#1433492835516289225]].
- **[Mechanics]** "Spellstrike" = generic term for "Equip: Adds X <school> damage to melee attacks" gear effects; each instance procs individually per melee hit, and even two same-school effects (e.g. two rings with fire on-hit) proc completely independently — [[d:paladin#1433521371945893949]] [[d:paladin#1433521484520886402]] [[d:paladin#1433521699537555536]].
- **[Mechanics]** Only the T2 3-piece set bonus is coded as a "full spell," so it — almost uniquely among spellstrike effects — can proc other spell-triggered equipment effects (Sigil of Ancient Accord, WoC, Bindings of Contained Magic); ordinary on-hit spellstrike items mostly only proc Judgement of Wisdom/Seal of Wisdom mana return, Vengeance, and (per later confirmation) WoC, BoED, OBD, and True Band of Sulfuras specifically — [[d:paladin#1433679443192184902]] [[d:paladin#1433679506442293269]] [[d:paladin#1433885487038202008]] [[d:paladin#1435341706198253730]] [[d:paladin#1435830120752480367]]. Whether normal spellstrikes proc the Blue Dragon set is unconfirmed — [[d:paladin#1435829417468362826]] [[d:paladin#1435829569046319286]]. One item (id 61277, "adds 3 holy dmg to melee attacks," ~1 PPM) is itself coded to trigger spells, so it also procs Sigil of Ancient Accord like T2 3p does — [[d:paladin#1434497944781983765]] [[d:paladin#1434498159475687527]].
- **[Mechanics]** Flat weapon-damage enchants (e.g. "+3 weapon damage") do NOT proc spellstrike-type effects — only green "Equip: Adds X elemental damage to melee attacks" tooltips count. Fiery Weapon enchant does count as a real spellstrike (procs JoW etc.); Drillborer Disk's on-hit is a "thorns"/reflect effect (procs WoC ring, JoW, Vengeance) but does NOT trigger extra Seal of Command/SoR procs — that needs a genuine extra-attack trigger — [[d:paladin#1435609124292526080]] [[d:paladin#1435610778064523405]] [[d:paladin#1435611019811622992]] [[d:paladin#1435608459491147887]] [[d:paladin#1435610468285939734]] [[d:paladin#1435611243179409571]].
- **[Mechanics]** Spellstrike ("adds X dmg to melee attacks") does not proc Seal of Command; it triggers JoW and ring-type spellpower procs, but not Seals — [[d:paladin#1434215105754566787]].
- **[Mechanics]** Anything that directly hits the target procs Judgement of Wisdom's mana return; Consecration, Sapper, Holy Water, and Grenades do NOT proc it — [[d:paladin#1435258186847223899]] [[d:paladin#1435258234251382845]].
- **[Mechanics]** Lightning damage on melee attacks (weapon enchants) does not proc HoJ, Dark Edge of Insanity, or Windfury-style extra attacks on Turtle (nor in original classic — old "batching" made it look like it did, similar to Fiery weapon appearing to proc SoC) — [[d:paladin#1435488822384394322]].
- **[Mechanics]** Sigil of Ancient Accord full proc table (posted verbatim by Supal): procs from Vengeance ✔; bonus-damage spellstrike items (rings/neck/lantern) — Fire ✔, Lightning ✔, Holy/T2-3p ✔; T1 full prot 8/8p ✔; Oils — Shadow ✘, Frost ✘; weapon on-hit effects (TF/Ashbringer etc.) ✘; Spells — Exorcism ✔, Holy Wrath ✔, Consecration ✔ (first tick only); Seals ✘; Judgement ✘; Holy Strike ✘; Crusader Strike ✘; Dragonbreath Chili ✘; armor procs ✘; enchants ✘; pet abilities ✘; Libram of Final Judgement ✔; Grenades/Crystal Charge ✔; Holy Water ✘; spell-scaling coefficient 16.6% — [[d:paladin#1434041643358752779]]. Proc chance is low (roughly once per 8s attempt window at best) and the damage is Arcane, so it does work on Nature-immune Lashers — [[d:paladin#1435607942694043851]] [[d:paladin#1435607993436737596]].
- **[Sanctity Aura]** Increases Holy damage only, NOT healing; was in the Ret tree in classic, moved on Turtle "for reasons" — [[d:paladin#1435211336371142738]] [[d:paladin#1435211386056872049]] [[d:paladin#1435211501437718538]].
- **[Defense]** 25 Defense skill = +1% dodge, +1% parry, +1% block chance — [[d:paladin#1435674950798151782]]. Daze-immunity uses the same defense-skill threshold as crit-immunity; the threshold scales down by the mob's level relative to 60 — [[d:paladin#1434956668872954018]] [[d:paladin#1434954382084018376]]. Defense does not affect chance to be missed in PvP (only relevant vs bosses) — [[d:paladin#1435722208587550780]].
- **[Ret]** Vampirism (lifesteal) has no hard cap, but a practical sweet spot around 13-14% (achievable via 2 rings + trinket + full enchants) without sacrificing further gear slots — [[d:paladin#1434951291372572763]] [[d:paladin#1434951604032897066]].
- **[Stat weight]** Rough community estimate: ~1% haste ≈ ~0.96 stat budget points on comparable items — [[d:paladin#1434514458566201484]]; methodology and conclusions widely disputed even by its author (budgets look inconsistent/over-generous, "the system isn't used at all") — [[d:paladin#1434515017209872404]] [[d:paladin#1434519899354435727]] — treat as speculative, single-source.
- **[Stat priority]** For spellret gear, prioritize hit over small crit/other bonuses if not hit-capped — [[d:paladin#1433810256768798740]].
- **[Gear]** Blessing of Might currently grants 155 AP; will rise to 185 AP (222 AP with the 5/5 talent) once AQ40 releases — [[d:paladin#1434536880849948774]] [[d:paladin#1434537917946007654]].
- **[Item stat "crit damage reduction"]** A stat reading "reduces damage from crits and DoTs by 3%" replaced an older stat that reduced chance to BE crit by 1%; the new version is considered a weak PvP-oriented stat with poor itemization budget — [[d:paladin#1434895132569636915]] [[d:paladin#1434895227306639603]] [[d:paladin#1434901096610005254]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Divine Shield vs Divine Protection]** Divine Shield lasts longer, blocks essentially everything, but reduces the paladin's own spell damage output and costs more mana; Divine Protection is shorter, disables the paladin's own melee/physical attacks while active but doesn't reduce spell damage and costs much less mana — for Holy, Divine Shield is often preferred since it still allows Holy Strike/casting during it — [[d:paladin#1434634554920140982]] [[d:paladin#1434634880045682799]].
- **[Blessing priority]** In a group/raid, damage-increase Blessings (Might/Kings-type) are the default choice to boost tank threat; Blessing of Light is low priority — used mainly if the tank is otherwise unhealable — [[d:paladin#1434641181828055110]] [[d:paladin#1434643887296872734]] [[d:paladin#1434643947195596811]]; low-gear group: throw BoL on the tank for a huge +healing effect; well-geared group: give DPS buffs and Salvation the DPS instead — [[d:paladin#1434176544107925716]].
- **[Librams]** Sigil of Ancient Accord-style item table for what does/doesn't proc it is under Stats above. Libram of the Farraki Zealot's Consecration-heal effect heals literally everyone including the paladin, not just party — [[d:paladin#1435107960215965888]]. Libram of Final Judgement adds an after-Judgement burn, procs at 35% (vs ~20% for the "HoW" libram alternative), adds 2-3k damage/fight — [[d:paladin#1434061135182692454]] [[d:paladin#1434063087148994642]]. Community wants Librams made non-swappable mid-combat so devs can safely make stronger ones (recurring wishlist) — [[d:paladin#1434074054477746308]] [[d:paladin#1434444237998653580]] [[d:paladin#1435115492770578515]]. Libram of Virtue makes Holy Smite count as a spell (uncertain if it has spell-power scaling) — [[d:paladin#1434063825451352205]] [[d:paladin#1434067582700355654]].
- **[Prot]** SBV (Shield Block Value) is currently NOT a threat stat for Paladin (unlike Prot Warrior, where it is); community wishes it became one, and that it could also scale Holy Shield/Blessing of Sanctuary damage — unimplemented wishlist — [[d:paladin#1435596062311186584]] [[d:paladin#1435596454579409008]].
- **[Consumables/mana macro]** Cast a self-buff-cancel macro (e.g. cancel Divine Protection to swap into Divine Shield, since they share a CD) — see Macros section for exact script.

## Tanking specifics
- **[Threat design]** Prot's threat is concentrated almost entirely in Holy Strike; a Holy Strike whiff falls back only to a (weaker) SoR proc, unlike Warrior where a Shield Slam whiff falls back to Heroic Strike — dev-acknowledged design gap tied to Righteous Fury's core threat mechanic, not easily fixed by set bonuses alone — [[d:paladin#1435031697103126549]] [[d:paladin#1435030814604918954]].
- **[Threat]** Prot MT rotation: pop one CS for the Righteous Strikes buff, then spam HS; OT/2nd-on-threat can lean more on CS — [[d:paladin#1434271787859837028]] [[d:paladin#1434273240657957097]].
- **[Threat]** Prot still wants spellpower specifically because Righteous Fury and Holy Strike scale with it — [[d:paladin#1433463778074165360]].
- **[Comparisons]** Bear (Feral) tanks are widely viewed as the strongest tank on the server, out-threating even a fully threat-specced Prot Pala while running full mitigation gear; Prot war also seen as edging ahead of Prot Pala on threat — [[d:paladin#1433719255890923590]] [[d:paladin#1433735254555754506]] [[d:paladin#1433720177006084136]].
- **[Reckoning]** Strong for AoE tanking (blocks frequently at low level, procs a lot) but questionable design at endgame — [[d:paladin#1434219864364421200]] [[d:paladin#1434270061123731548]].
- **[Threat trinkets]** For Onyxia head reward, the DEF trinket vs +spell ring is a real choice; also keep at least one dedicated threat trinket like ToEP/ZHC/Tear alongside mitigation trinkets (Styleen's, Glyph, DM Book), and Reed of UBRS / corrupted Reed from Emerald Sanctum are alternatives — [[d:paladin#1434385486952141022]] [[d:paladin#1434388105338814474]].
- **[K40 tactic]** Bulwark (defensive CD) can't always cover both a Noth-teleport-phase pull AND Heigan on the same lockout given cooldown length vs fight timers — plan Bulwark usage per-pull — [[d:paladin#1435022167816405143]].
- **[BWL tanking]** BWL doesn't usually require solo main-tanking — expect 4-5 other tanks sharing duties — [[d:paladin#1435625141366292600]].
- **[Ironclad]** Only scales off an item's BASE armor value — does NOT benefit from Devotion Aura or the Prot armor talent (a common source of confusion since tooltips don't specify "base") — [[d:paladin#1433636384807194706]] [[d:paladin#1433636385578942564]] [[d:paladin#1433637058974584862]].
- **[2H tanking]** A 2H "sword and board"-less Prot tank build is not viable — Prot's mitigation kit (Shield Block Value etc.) is tuned around having a shield in hand — [[d:paladin#1433434277193711739]] [[d:paladin#1433435202805305396]].
- **[Utility]** Devotion Aura is still considered BiS for leveling (unless AoE farming) and some Prot paladins still run it as OT in raids — [[d:paladin#1435218213372694570]].

## Healing specifics
- **[Low-gear raid healing crash course, Mithreon]** Build: https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=AoaAYRFQpAYoB--FoQAAI. Rotation: spam Flash of Light on the active tank(s); if you have the talent that cuts Holy Light's cast to 1.5s after using Judgement, use that reduced-cast Holy Light on badly damaged allies; use Holy Shock (shorter range) as an instant, sparingly, to top people off. When the whole raid takes damage, mentally split the raid in half and heal that half like whack-a-mole rather than trying to track everyone. Mana: in low gear expect to use mana pots, Nordanaar Herbal Tea, and Mage's Oranges out of combat; well-geared healers can go 3 minutes of FoL spam without drinking; the classic newbie mistake is spamming full Holy Light (chews mana far too fast). Priority: keep the tank(s) alive above all else — if the tank dies, mobs go down the threat list killing everyone else — [[d:paladin#1436009932502597705]] [[d:paladin#1436010077046837280]] [[d:paladin#1436011418313953350]] [[d:paladin#1436011675357810781]] [[d:paladin#1436012968784429220]] [[d:paladin#1436013333919830126]].
- **[Holy]** Holy Strike is the paladin's de facto AoE heal but requires melee range, careful timing, and tracking how many injured allies are nearby — takes practice — [[d:paladin#1436010077046837280]].
- **[Holy]** Holy Judgement talent used as a reliable "pocket emergency heal" precisely because it doesn't depend on a crit like Holy Shock does (Holy Shock's ~40%-ish crit-dependency means it "misses big" a large fraction of the time); Holy Light crits are about 2x the size of Holy Shock crits — [[d:paladin#1434669512518533302]] [[d:paladin#1434670528110526534]] [[d:paladin#1434670243841835181]]. Its primary use case is PvP — [[d:paladin#1434670264599183401]].
- **[Holy]** Current Holy playstyle criticized as "mostly a caster that stands in melee and presses Holy Strike every 5.5-6s, then casts FoL" rather than true melee healing; HS/CS share a cooldown and the design pushes Holy toward this "semi-melee" style. Daybreak+Holy Strike healing is considered overtuned/free AoE healing by some, arguably "worse than Chain Heal ever was," a departure from Holy's original efficient-single-target-throughput identity — [[d:paladin#1433690127170797578]] [[d:paladin#1433690824406859877]] [[d:paladin#1433692791552217160]] [[d:paladin#1433693076609826898]] [[d:paladin#1433693571034382366]].
- **[Holy healing utility]** Cleanse has a GCD and single-target only, compared to Shaman totems (off-GCD, group-wide, 40yd range) — seen as a real utility gap — [[d:paladin#1433416402701783163]].
- **[Holy gear]** Blacksmithing offers "basically nothing" to Holy Paladins — Tailoring is a better profession pick; general gold-farming pairing recommendation is Engineering + a profession suited to your specific farm (e.g. Herbalism for Lashers) — [[d:paladin#1435644240179757229]] [[d:paladin#1435648174558810233]].
- **[Holy talent feedback to Lhian, Mithreon's detailed writeup]** T1 3p good; T1 5p could be QoL instead of spellcrit (hpals already have 15%+ crit by that point); T1 8p was heavily nerfed and needs a redesign given melee-healing changes. T2 3p good (don't nerf); T2 5p has no real place since there's no standalone "Holy Light spec" anymore (Mending Light+Daybreak replaced HL-spam); T2 8p is "worse Daybreak now," needs review. T2.5 3p mana-return-on-cast is redundant with Illumination+crit; T2.5 5p decent but wants Mending Light/Daybreak synergy; lots of Spirit itemization seems PvP-backline-oriented. T3 2p great (don't nerf); T3 4p mid/not needed (raids often carry multiple LoH paladins already); T3 6p bonus interactions with multi-spec buff slots can be annoying (e.g. unwanted armor buff to Warriors); T3 8p heal-on-cleanse too weak/niche for a Naxx-tier set — [[d:paladin#1435007720355926186]].

## PvP
- **[Divine Shield/Protection]** See Cooldowns section for the mechanical difference; Divine Shield nerf discussion under 1.18.1 changes.
- **[Repentance]** Has a narrow niche use in PvP tied to targets that would otherwise be immune to a Sap-style effect — described as "the only time to use Repent in PvP" — [[d:paladin#1434416484175712357]] [[d:paladin#1434416944110637077]].
- **[Ret PvP]** AP ret with SoC favored for burst (Judgement of Command does double damage on stunned targets); with a slow weapon, a lucky triple-crit can kill in one global; Deathbringer (1h weapon) makes 1h AP ret PvP viable/fun — [[d:paladin#1435043535899590837]] [[d:paladin#1435043642799558746]] [[d:paladin#1435042699886592141]] [[d:paladin#1435043815642759180]].
- **[Damage reduction in PvP]** Holy Strike does far less than its tooltip in PvP due to a flat PvP damage reduction (~180-200 actual with 0 SP) — [[d:paladin#1435492770986045]→][[d:paladin#1434451192770986045]].
- **[Rogue matchup]** "Dissolvent" rogue poison/effect bypasses Block entirely (only Dodge/Parry mitigate it), has no weapon-speed/damage scaling on the rogue's end, and is not reduced by Protection Potions — a known hard PvP weakness for Prot Paladins vs Rogues — [[d:paladin#1435717695189422100]] [[d:paladin#1435719385900912670]] [[d:paladin#1435719789988286678]] [[d:paladin#1435719848331317328]] [[d:paladin#1435720450100564068]].
- **[Bug window]** During the Nov-5 spellstrike-scaling bug, weapon reflect procs (e.g. Hand of Ragnaros) became powerful enough to reliably kill rogues/melee attacking a paladin — purely a temporary bug, not representative — see 1.18.1 changes.
- **[Race]** Human vs High Elf paladin is not a big difference; ret physical/hybrid builds want the extra 1% hit human racials effectively provide once gear is good; polearm users don't benefit from the 2h racial/talent damage bonus and still need to itemize hit separately since there's no weapon-skill offset for polearms — [[d:paladin#1433390478857867456]] [[d:paladin#1433390800737275955]] [[d:paladin#1433391068526805043]] [[d:paladin#1433391274081386516]]. Some race choices are noted as better specifically vs Rogues and Druids in PvP (context: racial utility, not detailed further) — [[d:paladin#1435289451302621335]].
- **[Cross-faction WSG bug, 2025-11-05]** A same-day cross-faction BG test caused visual bugs (players rendered as the opposite faction, e.g. "Orc Paladin"), blank/missing debuffs for players without the launcher updated, and reported general PvP instability — flagged as still being tested/unstable — [[d:paladin#1435451356742815794]] [[d:paladin#1435452612475359353]] [[d:paladin#1435525329299963978]] [[d:paladin#1435532303655899166]].
