## Rotation: single target
- **[Ret/SoR & SoC]** Judgement priority with one paladin: Judgement of Wisdom (JoW) on cooldown for mana; with a second (holy) paladin present, try to have them "enslave" to Wisdom so the DPS ret can run Judgement of the Crusader (JoTC) instead; use Judgement of Command (JoC) on cooldown but never judge right before your auto-attack lands or you'll lose seal uptime on that swing — [[d:paladin#1492067023998554164]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret/SoR]** Consecration is not worth using until high-tier gear because the damage-per-mana isn't good enough; with enough spellpower scaling on Consecration it becomes worth spamming, which is why some SoR spellstrike builds want the mana-return item (spellstrike ring/bracer) that other ret builds skip — [[d:paladin#1492066318159970314]], [[d:paladin#1492066398770429992]], [[d:paladin#1492067835780792451]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret/SoR]** Consecration's Holy Strike-equivalent scaling: Holy Strike has a 0.71 spellpower coefficient (used in a tank threat comparison, but confirms the scaling value) — [[d:paladin#1493038688790253639]] (gastro).
- **[Ret]** Seal choice with the Pysan trinket/proc item: using Seal of the Crusader instead of Seal of Righteousness "essentially nullifies Pysan's proc" — [[d:paladin#1493319533329387520]] (Beldrin).
- **[Ret]** Judgement of the Crusader is useful for longer fights (bosses/big elites); the Seal of the Crusader buff itself is "pretty terrible"; Hammer of Justice/stuns are always fine to use but best value interrupting a spellcaster — [[d:paladin#1492574827746300175]], [[d:paladin#1492574880837664869]], [[d:paladin#1492575059686985810]] (Silverhand).
- **[Ret/SoC]** Libram of the Eternal Tower vs Libram of Ardour for Seal of Command: Eternal Tower (cooldown reduction) only pays off once a fight runs meaningfully longer than ~60s (time saved yields one extra Holy Strike/Crusader Strike used on CD); Ardour is preferred generally because it always gives a chance at an extra proc even in short fights — [[d:paladin#1493046992148893806]] (Destroses), [[d:paladin#1493055347043074118]], [[d:paladin#1493055386016678000]] (Jesse Potter: uses Ardour "all the time").
- **[Ret/SoR "Lost" build]** The Lost proc (multiple "ghost" procs from Seal of Righteousness) is RNG-based, e.g. "sometimes 4 ghosts, sometimes 2"; wants the fastest weapon possible — Crimson Spellblade (1.90 speed) is too slow; Flurry Axe (1.50 + extra-attack proc) is 2nd-best but expensive; Anasterian's Legacy (K10) is a good budget option; fire-school Spellstrike procs do NOT trigger Lost/Sigil, only holy-school spellstrike procs do — [[d:paladin#1493296247409217707]] (Wilhelm).
- **[Ret, baseline mechanic]** Crusader Strike (CS) and Holy Strike (HS) share the same cooldown/global "attack ability" slot — you pick one of the two per cooldown, plus auto-attack and periodic seal refresh; at low level Holy Strike is effectively free because it refunds the mana it costs (+3 HP/+5 mana baseline at very low level, scaling with SP/Healing Power, and halved when it heals yourself vs. an ally) — [[d:paladin#1493593157190025436]], [[d:paladin#1493595401440202852]], [[d:paladin#1493595988122800179]] (Jesse Potter). CS deals 0 healing and has a mana cost (unlike HS) — [[d:paladin#1493596073116303490]] (Silverhand).
- **[Ret]** CS vs HS priority is driven by buff uptime, not raw hit damage: once you have max Zeal and Holy Might stacks, the goal is to keep both buffs up; most players alternate CS/HS because letting either buff fall off is a big DPS loss — "the damage done by CS and HS are secondary to the effects they bring" — [[d:paladin#1494198526383751270]], [[d:paladin#1494198613004521502]] (Protoman). Concretely: with high aggro on a "dragon"/high-armor target, some go HS-CS-HS-CS; otherwise build CS stacks then spam Holy Strike, which reportedly hits harder for geared SP ret — [[d:paladin#1494174489712525312]], [[d:paladin#1494174866151313418]] (Larive). Paladins running "The Lost" set may drop Crusader Strike from the rotation entirely because it isn't more damage than Holy Strike or Hammer of Wrath in that setup; Hammer of Wrath usage on execute-range targets varies by player preference — [[d:paladin#1494189514070163477]], [[d:paladin#1494193077403779183]] (forsooth/Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret, final rotation summary]** General-purpose SP/AP ret single-target loop as of this chunk: Seal → Judgement → reseal → Consecration, auto-attacking until Judgement is back off cooldown, with CS/HS layered in to maintain buffs; exact CS/HS mix depends on target armor, your SP, and weapon damage — e.g. sometimes 1 HS then spam CS, sometimes 1 HS → 2 CS → 1 HS → 1 CS → spam HS with CS only to refresh Zeal; the goal is to keep both the HS and CS buffs active and spam whichever ability hits harder — [[d:paladin#1495906695132483664]], [[d:paladin#1495912090865504497]], [[d:paladin#1495912323821473832]], [[d:paladin#1495912560325824543]], [[d:paladin#1495912649358180472]] (Istenneveben).
- **[Ret/hit cap]** Hit is the top priority stat to avoid ("missing is the worst dps loss"); target ~8% hit (from all sources); +5 weapon skill = 1% hit; on Turtle, 3 weapon skill ≈ 0.6% hit (an imperfect/worse-than-1:1 conversion). As Human with the weapon-skill talent (Precision) and skill book you get 11 weapon skill (2% hit) + Precision's 3% hit = 5% hit before gear. Precision's spell-hit component (3%) was reportedly fixed and helps Consecration/proc reliability. The weapon-skill book comes from a Fray Island quest chain (~250g + a quest item), and must be repeated per weapon type (separate 250g quest for axe, mace, sword, etc.) — [[d:paladin#1493596580358394057]] through [[d:paladin#1493599151370535003]], [[d:paladin#1493602119347998800]] (Jesse Potter/Peberro). Easy sources of +hit: 2% from head (Mask of the Unforgiven or the helm from Baron Rivendare) or shoulders (Defiler's from Blackrock Mountain, or the Dalaran rep shoulders); 1% from the Onyxia neck or Ogre Beads of Might (though the Ony neck is hard to get on Ambershire) — [[d:paladin#1493597868035149976]], [[d:paladin#1493598004928581755]], [[d:paladin#1493598243148402712]] (Peberro/Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret]** AP-ret math: 300 Strength converts to roughly 1200 Attack Power (via Blessing of Might/Divine Strength-type multipliers), which is why Untamed Blade (a high-Str proc weapon) can outperform SP weapons for AP ret despite not stacking spellpower — [[d:paladin#1493595800100671668]] (Jesse Potter).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Ret/SoR]** SP enchants (helmet +12 SP, pants +12 SP, gloves +9 SP) generally beat 1% Haste enchants for spellret/hybrid SoR, "use haste on all slots if you swing Nightfall though" (Nightfall's proc benefits from faster weapon swings) — [[d:paladin#1492370392973578261]], [[d:paladin#1492380383113777264]] (theoloras/warkrak).
- **[Ret]** Weapon enchant choice: 30 SP enchant if the weapon is your main hand; Rift Tear (proc) enchant if the weapon is purely for AoE farming — Rift Tear is noisy and low DPS overall, weaker than SP for anything but pure AoE — [[d:paladin#1492387256437182555]] (Destroses), [[d:paladin#1492983706791710943]] (Moorea, ~4% dmg, underwhelming), [[d:paladin#1493022254626508831]] (Moorea).
- **[Prot]** Belt enchant: 10 Str vs 6 Agi — for Prot, the difference is minor and Agi is far cheaper (~1-2g vs ~60g); 12 Str gives only minor extra Holy Strike damage (less than one shield-block-value) since prot doesn't benefit from Divine Strength multiplier, while 6 Agi gives 12 armor, 0.3% crit, 0.3% dodge; for Ret the Str option is clearly better because Ret does get the Divine Strength multiplier — [[d:paladin#1492410170419183737]], [[d:paladin#1492431581384540161]] (gastro), [[d:paladin#1492667984320270528]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1492666857885274294]] (gastro), [[d:paladin#1492668085142945853]] (gastro).
- **[Prot]** Str's effective value is boosted by multipliers from Kings/Divine Strength/Holy Strike scaling — noted as a caveat to the above, though the overall belt-enchant conclusion for prot didn't change — [[d:paladin#1492479290292310057]] (João Caju).
- **[Prot]** Defense/avoidance: guides advising hitting the defense cap for raiding (esp. MC) were called into question — one view: to hit the (unspecified) "440 crit cap" you must sacrifice every slot's defense, and Holy Shield's avoidance cap is unreachable anyway, so full-threat itemization may be preferred over defense-stacking — single source, unclear terminology, contested with the "Defense is a noob trap" talent advice above — [[d:paladin#1492685619065782342]] (Cadpat, question), [[d:paladin#1492691336325890158]] (Magik, answer).
- **[Prot]** T2.5 vs T3.5 shoulder/leg mix-and-match comparison (with concrete stat deltas): T3.5 shoulders + T2.5 legs = 37.4 AP, 47 SP, 2.695% crit, 1 haste, 1 hit, 4.615 avoidance, 49.5 stam, 1543 armor; T2.5 shoulders + T3.5 legs = 54 SP, 2.53% crit, 1 hit, 4.29 avoidance, 52.8 stam, 1557 armor. Net: T3.5-shoulder variant trades SP for avoidance; the SP-heavier combo is recommended for threat since "37 AP is like 6 weapon damage for Desecration or 2.3 [Holy Strike] damage; 8 SP outperforms it considerably" — [[d:paladin#1492881083904294953]], [[d:paladin#1492907380294287470]], [[d:paladin#1492912850321014804]], [[d:paladin#1492916897728172195]] (Mithreon).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Prot]** Talent priorities: Toughness (armor% from items) over Anticipation (Defense skill) — "Defense is a noob trap"; by the time max avoidance matters (AQ40, and even then questionably) your gear alone gets you there without sacrificing Toughness's mitigation — [[d:paladin#1492790077800059020]] through [[d:paladin#1492791630443450499]] (Krooks/Raugtreefell/Destroses).
- **[Prot]** T2.5/T3.5 mix-and-match for shoulders/legs (concrete stat comparison) — see Stats section above; recommendation leans toward the higher-SP combo (T2.5 shoulders + T3.5 legs) since 8 extra SP outweighs the AP/avoidance from the alternative combo for threat purposes — [[d:paladin#1492907380294287470]] through [[d:paladin#1492916897728172195]] (Mithreon).
- **[Prot]** Threat/gearing debates (T0.5 vs T1 8-piece, and double-spellstrike Ret+Prot T2 vs 4pc Soulforge) are contested — see Gear section above for full detail and citations.
- **[Prot]** Belt/enchant stat priority favors Agility over Strength for Prot (cheap, minor avoidance/armor/crit) since Prot doesn't get the Divine Strength Str multiplier that Ret does — see Stats section for full numbers and citations.
- **[Prot leveling/farming]** Solo/leveling tank builds: fast weapons are preferred for more Seal of Wisdom procs (to sustain mana for Holy Shield/Consecration spam); Stamina + Vampirism is a strong early stat combo, letting a solo Prot pull 6-8 mobs in the open world with a healer along; Ret Aura adds damage once tanking basics are covered. Once around level 40+, high pull counts are achievable without Vampirism or a healer; if you do have a pocket healer, Vampirism becomes "kinda useless" and Int/more Stam (bigger mana pool, better Shield Spec mana return) is preferred instead. Some solo Prot levelers run zero Int and never go OOM thanks to Seal of Wisdom alone — [[d:paladin#1494348050561503232]] through [[d:paladin#1494356882218029196]] (Vastus/Istenneveben).
- **[Prot farming]** Fresh-60 gold-farming as Holy/Prot: spec Holy-Prot and farm Firewing/"lashers" (fire elementals in Blackrock/Searing Gorge-type content) — doable even under-geared starting around level 52-54. Two gearing approaches: (1) Lesser Elemental Slaying weapon enchant + non-nature Thorns items (lashers are immune to Nature damage, so Nature-damage Thorns items don't work) — a spiked shield helps too; or (2) straight Vampirism gear, where ~8% vamp supports two pack-pulls at level 54. A Holy Shock/Holy Shield reset build also works for this from as low as level 52, with Concentration Aura and faster Holy Light (via Judgement) helping survive — [[d:paladin#1494565833727283322]] through [[d:paladin#1494817238136656102]] (Voorshead/Peberro/Magik/Drekalo).
- **[Prot]** Deadmines pre-Consecration: tankable with a good group; in Hardcore, better to go Ret or wait for Consecration since overpulling matters; at level 42+ it's tankable solo-pull-wise but watch for body-pulls, and remember you can Line of Sight (LoS) problem mobs — [[d:paladin#1494777140732756080]], [[d:paladin#1494778509724221470]], [[d:paladin#1494808253102690414]] (Kaltau/Riverax/Magik).
- **[Prot solo]** A geared Prot/Necrodin-adjacent paladin can solo most 5-man content on Nordanaar, including all of BRD, Stratholme, Scholomance, and LBRS (minus the troll boss); a documented Scholomance solo gold-farm route: clear the first 4 mobs, drop into the back corner of the big room (~3 pulls depending on gear), invis-potion into Rattlegore's room for a key + Shadow Skins (→ Arcanum of Focus), invis back out, use Dawn's Gambit for extra boss kills, clear the 6 minibosses (skip Darkmaster unless very confident — soloable but a ~30 min fight), then solo Ras with ~150 Frost Resistance (needs all cooldowns/pots) — yields ~2 Arcanums + ~10 Scourgestones/shards per run, no need to engage mana-burn mobs — [[d:paladin#1495342108138012722]] (keungzhe).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy, melee-holy build]** Detailed melee-holy playstyle: get Ironclad and the Holy Strike improvement talents; Judgement reduces Holy Light cast time. Rotation: run into melee, Judge Wisdom on the target, Seal of Wisdom, spam Flash of Light, hit Holy Strike on cooldown (a large AoE heal to everyone in melee range — at least ~30% of total healing depending on tier); the reduced-cast-time Holy Light from Judgement can be cast about as fast as Flash of Light, roughly once per judgement/pack. Some mobs silence in melee — learn which. In raids, running into melee means learning more mechanics, but paladins have a built-in "ignore mechanic" button (bubble); stay ranged for certain packs/bosses regardless. Ironclad changes what pre-BiS/BiS gear looks like and enables melee survivability — [[d:paladin#1492813841057448060]] (PraylorSwift). Confirmed independently: "melee holy is the same as ordinary holy, you just become aware that pressing Holy Strike between Flash of Lights is better than only doing Flash of Lights" — [[d:paladin#1492796874128887839]] (L-star). Later community reflection (as server was closing) reinforces concrete numbers: ~60% crit chance achievable on Holy Shock, Judgement making Holy Light cast as fast as Flash of Light "at times," Consecration usable when healing is comfortable, and Holy paladins doing 8-10% of total damage in 5-mans on top of healing — [[d:paladin#1495124235805265921]] (yeesh).
- **[Holy]** Ironclad talent gives healing power scaled from armor, which structurally favors a 1h+shield holy build over 2h holy (shields grant far more Ironclad healing power than any comparable stat gain from a 2h weapon); extensive player debate (largely a wishlist, not implemented) proposed making Holy Strike/Seal of Wisdom/Seal of Light scale with weapon damage for 2h viability, but the counter-argument (1h/shield gives more armor, healing power, raw stats, PvP survivability, and more seal-proc opportunities) won out as the status quo — [[d:paladin#1492967066523926709]] through [[d:paladin#1493022254626508831]] (yeesh/gastro/dumbid/Mithreon). Contested/open — no dev confirmation of any change.
- **[Holy]** Leveling stat priority: Int is the main stat, with Crit and Healing Power becoming relevant later; Spirit is considered bad for paladins. Gear can mix cloth/leather/plate early, but plate becomes better once Ironclad is learned (healing power from armor) — [[d:paladin#1492964365911986377]], [[d:paladin#1492967040535757031]] (gastro).
- **[Holy]** How far spellret ("soulforge") gear can carry a healer: on Nordanaar, viable as a 2nd/3rd healer through BWL+ in pugs (there are enough undergeared/green-carried DPS around), and even in AQ40 if a guild is desperate for a body — but heals and mana pool are only about 2/3 as strong as with proper T2.5+ gear, and the gap can reach ~50% in a full endgame comparison; 8 major slots on int/healing-power-light spellret gear is a real cost to the raid — [[d:paladin#1493084847021883453]] (bela).
- **[Holy]** BiS-adjacent off-set pieces on Ambershire (BWL/ZG unavailable): Azuregos drops a 92-healing-power helm considered one of the best available (better than T2 helm's 65 healing power, though T2 helm enables the 3-piece T2 set bonus of +57 healing, making the choice non-trivial in a set-bonus context); Lethon (dragon boss) drops a helm of similar/slightly-better value; a "crab boss" world-boss shield is decent but slightly worse than the Vael shield; two decent leather chest options exist from a "Nerub" source; a cloak alternative to Hide of the Wild exists from a world boss — [[d:paladin#1493605764957339698]] through [[d:paladin#1493619042429436037]] (bela/Infertile/PraylorSwift/drago). Recommended holy BiS-ish combo for this phase: 3-piece T2 + 3-piece T1 + Wild Growth + Empowered Leggings — [[d:paladin#1493614832933666866]], [[d:paladin#1493614832933666866]] (Infertile).
- **[Holy]** T1 vs T2 shoulders for holy: "almost a joke" — T2 (with Ironclad + 3pc bonus context) is far better than T1 for healers — [[d:paladin#1493607903284822198]] (bela). Holy's own T1/T2 tier pieces are considered mediocre before T2.5, when good off-set pieces (accepting cloth/leather) become competitive — [[d:paladin#1493605696074153985]] (bela).
- **[Holy]** A "3 holy damage on melee attacks" minor proc effect does not scale with SP directly, but it does proc Judgement of Wisdom and other on-hit effects — [[d:paladin#1493609297228070992]] (bela).
- **[Holy PvP]** Holy Paladin in PvP is a different playstyle from Ret, not strictly worse: a good Ret will outperform a bad Holy in raw damage, but a good Holy Paladin is "one of the most obnoxious things" in BGs since it can heal to full from an opener if it avoids CC; early/undergeared, Ret does very little damage so healer may be the better early PvP choice — [[d:paladin#1493989325069746187]] through [[d:paladin#1493991206630195361]] (Vastus/Mithreon).
- **[Holy]** Haste target for Holy: don't go above ~3-4% Haste because Flash of Light's cast time already matches the global cooldown at that point (further Haste is wasted on FoL specifically) — [[d:paladin#1493729787582349402]], [[d:paladin#1493729928125087845]] (minefrost/Infertile).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[General]** Race recommendation for paladin: "all are fine" — Human is probably best for pure output due to the 2% crit racial; High Elf is arguably BiS specifically for healing; Dwarf gives a minor, situational Defense-on-CD bonus, not very impactful; Ret/Prot get only a "tiny bonus" from most racials since paladin (Holy Strike) already isn't subject to the glancing-blow penalty that makes some racials matter more for other classes — [[d:paladin#1494492026685882388]] through [[d:paladin#1494526129195978983]] (Ryver/dumbid).
- **[Leveling]** Paladin + Priest duo leveling is strong: a Prot Paladin takes little damage early, and a Shadow Priest's trickle healing/mana return lets the pair sustain a faster pace and let the paladin pull more; Disc Priest is also a strong, simple pairing (can DPS and heal simultaneously) — both considered excellent 2-person leveling comps — [[d:paladin#1494344380780253295]] through [[d:paladin#1494345756604108852]] (Istrefi).
- **[Leveling/rep]** Faction rep vendor gear notes — see Leveling section above (Elf/Dalaran/Silvermoon rep gear and cost).
- **[Professions]** Engineering path question for a leveling Prot Paladin with 200+ Engineering (Gnome vs. Goblin): recommended approach is to level Gnome engineering first (build on the BoP items it unlocks), then relevel to Goblin — [[d:paladin#1494771532843450590]], [[d:paladin#1494773475951251556]] (Avenyah/Nobody-86).
- **[Gold/farming]** Fresh-60 gold farming as Prot: spec Holy-Prot and farm "lashers" (see Tanking specifics above for the build); Force Reactive Disk (300 Engineering) is a strong AoE-farming cooldown but has costly repair bills, so many keep Crest of Retribution as the default and save Force Reactive Disk for big pulls/emergencies — [[d:paladin#1493416805618946248]], [[d:paladin#1493417094266622203]] (Kindousen/Moorea).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Major, 1.18.1]** Flametongue Totem (FTT) spellpower-scaling bug — see "1.18.1 changes" section above for full detail and citations; status was unstable across this chunk: reported present and causing 6-20% of damage on some fights (varying hugely by fire resistance on the specific boss — BWL's largely fire-resistant/immune bosses show much less effect, ~6%, than trash-heavy content, ~13-20%), reported "fixed post reset" on 2026-04-15 by one player, but as late as 2026-04-15 evening other players still saw zero FTT contribution in logs and questioned whether a PTR fix was pulled from production before shipping — genuinely unresolved/contested within this chunk.
- **[Ret]** "Hippo" 3-piece proc effect (item/set nickname as used in chat) has multiple confirmed quirks from dedicated player testing: does not scale with SP; does scale with Vengeance; can crit (and grants Vengeance on crit); can proc Wrath of Cenarius; can proc from Crusader Strike but NOT Holy Strike; is consumed by Crusader Strike, Holy Strike, and extra attacks; cannot proc Ring of Burning Talons or Sigil; is not consumed by parry/dodge/miss; has no ICD. Suspected bug: Seal of Righteousness auto-attack hits do not generate a proc charge but DO consume one — described as "not proccing from seals but being consumed by them (worst of all worlds)." Base (non-seal) proc rate measured at ~7.8% over a 30-minute test with a 3.7-speed weapon (60 procs) — [[d:paladin#1494962664261025803]], [[d:paladin#1494963514173816902]], [[d:paladin#1494964215113580575]], [[d:paladin#1494966627416932462]], [[d:paladin#1494967920638562474]] (Protoman, methodology cross-checked by Destroses).
- **[Ret/Necrodin]** Summoned "guardian" adds (from The Lost/Necrodin procs) can independently pull trash aggro while wandering, notably aggroing the golems near the Molten Core entrance and running around the room — [[d:paladin#1494411608993042633]] onward (Mithreon).
- **[Prot loot]** Paladin item drop rates for certain slots are anomalously low and were partly confirmed as bugged: a detailed community drop-rate audit (20,000 kills across 2 tests) found Molten Core boss (Garr) drop weights of ~13% for the paladin Talisman, ~6% for the paladin Bindings, and only ~8% (effectively lower in practice) for T1 Paladin Shoulders, because Talisman/Bindings have independent loot-group rolls while Shoulders must "beat" three ~20%-weighted competing items on a shared table; the Garr Bindings' higher relative weight is inflated further by another legendary sharing that table. The Shaman/Paladin T2 Legs drop-rate bug was already confirmed fixed by devs following community reports; the T1 Paladin Shoulders issue was still being pursued as of this chunk — [[d:paladin#1492983229467066689]] through [[d:paladin#1493010678083096626]] (Rindal/Shardphoenix/Peberro, initial reports), [[d:paladin#1494444905538256926]] through [[d:paladin#1494449913914851469]] (Lyrazia/Istenneveben/"30", detailed follow-up audit).
- **[Ret]** Zeal talent buff-bar display bug: the Zeal buff tooltip/icon (and character sheet via at least one addon) shows the base value (e.g. 9% for 3 stacks) instead of the talent-boosted value (e.g. 15%), even though the talent's effect is actually applied — "the buff bar doesn't update with the talent, same as every other buff that goes up with talents" — cosmetic only, not a functional bug — [[d:paladin#1492703999151964282]] through [[d:paladin#1492710490353303643]] (clockwiz/Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret]** Holy Strike / Crusader Strike "Smart Healing" targeting was changed: Holy Strike's AoE heal no longer prioritizes the most-damaged target, only whether the target is damaged at all — described as "by design," part of a broader removal of "smart" heal targeting from the game — [[d:paladin#1492525393683677365]], [[d:paladin#1492528383014469794]], [[d:paladin#1492528432146288820]] (Sprotte/Mithreon).
- **[General]** HoJ (Hammer of Justice) and a "Cloak" proc effect share the same underlying spell ID, so they can never proc together at exactly the same instant — equipping the cloak effectively raises HoJ's own proc chance to 3% instead of adding a fully separate proc source; separately, "Timeless Strike" reportedly uses the same icon as HoJ (cosmetic only) — [[d:paladin#1493041035423649833]], [[d:paladin#1493041430388805643]], [[d:paladin#1493102838367256709]], [[d:paladin#1493102952309719162]] (Spaghett Victim #1/Destroses/dusty). This was disputed by Protoman as possibly wrong spell-ID terminology but not fully resolved — [[d:paladin#1493101132384567402]].
- **[Ret]** T2 3-piece "spellstrike" only triggers a specific whitelist of on-harmful-spell-cast effects (Judgement of Wisdom, Weapon of the Council, Vengeance, and similar), NOT generic "on-hit" procs — it does not add extra Untamed Blade procs directly, though the faster Vengeance stacking it causes was observed to raise dummy-test DPS indirectly — [[d:paladin#1493665044133712088]] through [[d:paladin#1493669661890314473]] (ImplodingFruitz/Mithreon). Separately, Seal of Righteousness itself (extra weapon swings) does increase Untamed Blade proc count — [[d:paladin#1493697709398294579]] (gastro).
- **[Item]** Empowered Sulfuras ring only grants cast-speed (not useful for Ret) — [[d:paladin#1493607666382274792]] (bela).
- **[Item]** Daybreak (weapon/proc) can crit but will not re-apply/refresh itself once active — [[d:paladin#1493797633750466733]], [[d:paladin#1493816794438434937]] (Mendeleev/Infertile).
- **[Item]** A "1% chance to crit with attacks and spells" effect on an AQ20 SP shoulder item reportedly does not actually work for spells (only attacks) — [[d:paladin#1493418347612868619]], [[d:paladin#1493419553202507866]], [[d:paladin#1493419612430143599]] (Kindousen/Purge).
