## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** SoR and Consecration were both nerfed ~33% (CC1→CC2 balance pass); Holy Shield's threat nerf disguised as buff (now needs ~450 SP to match old threat-per-block); Holy Strike lost weapon/AP scaling but became instant with 40% shorter CD; Vengeance also removed, further reducing scaling of everything above — [[d:paladin#1340894628651466874]] (Grey, 2025-02-17).
- **[Prot]** Consecration threat math: at 1000 total SP, CC1 did ~415 TPS vs CC2 doing ~290 TPS — a ~45% threat reduction — [[d:paladin#1351520211739611210]] (Grey, 2025-03-11).
- **[General]** Holy Shock: damage portion confirmed will NOT return with Kara(40) release — [[d:paladin#1348776531568099348]] (Lhian, 2025-03-11).
- **[Ret]** SoR spellstrike-proc bug (SoR wasn't proccing on-hit/spellstrike items) confirmed to be fixed "next restart" — [[d:paladin#1349700327288475708]] (Lhian, 2025-03-13); later "Imp SoR fixed?" follow-up — [[d:paladin#1356848283250589836]].
- **[Ret]** Repentance: starting next restart, Repentance damage applies even if the attack is dodged/parried/blocked — [[d:paladin#1353449023561924639]] (Lhian +13, 2025-03-23).
- **[General]** Talent tree reshuffle for Ret announced ("reshuffling in ret talents in balance update") — [[d:paladin#1349682716991295541]] (Lhian +7, 2025-03-13); also confirmed as "continued tuning" — [[d:paladin#1349684549654937600]].
- **[General]** Around 2025-03-31/04-01 patch: Vindication buffed, Vengeance no longer removes threat when using Righteous Fury, SoR got buffed (though T3 is AP-focused so Ret may not switch), Hammer of Wrath improved for PvE, Grounding Totem added (shaman) — [[d:paladin#1356189208053551185]] (Alban, 2025-03-31).
- **[Prot]** Holy Shield/Bulwark math: a paladin under Bulwark takes 2.8x as much damage as a warrior under Shield Wall (75% reduction only nets 250 vs Shield Wall's 30%→700 out of 1000) — [[d:paladin#1339910436375498793]] (Grey, 2025-02-14).
- **[Ret]** Eye for an Eye swapped positions with Two-Handed Weapon Specialization in the talent tree — developer comment cited: fixes "awkward pathing" in 4th row, improves accessibility/leveling — [[d:paladin#1356246877711896636]] (Cruelia, quoting patch notes, 2025-04-01).
- **[Holy]** Rank 1 Holy Light was shadow-nerfed (109 healing: 115→66 noncrit) — not on changelog, community upset (low-level "Lunatic Paladin" build) — [[d:paladin#1356821149408952412]], [[d:paladin#1358429215036411946]] (Cysthen, 2025-04-02/06). - **[Ret]** Boot speed enchant gives 8% running speed in TWoW (vs 5% retail) — noted as a good investment despite costing 2 talent points elsewhere for comparison — [[d:paladin#1351204428451151935]] (Excinerus, 2025-03-17).
- **[Item]** Ashbringer questline: Corrupted Ashbringer stats posted (350-525 dmg, Speed 3.80, +55 Str -30 Sta, +3% crit all spells/attacks, on-hit: steal 475-525 life from up to 5 enemies granting 30 Str/Agi per enemy stacking to 5) — [[d:paladin#1350933425820799098]] (theoloras/warkrak, 2025-03-17); new enchant "Grand Crusader" (heal 350-450 + 120 Str for 20s on melee hit proc) also added — [[d:paladin#1350934676293947423]]; cleansing/purifying Ashbringer confirmed obtainable in Kara40 — [[d:paladin#1356440420669984879]], [[d:paladin#1356557656470458430]] (Lhian, 2025-04-01).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Prot]** Prot Paladin confirmed by dev as "a perfectly viable tank," though acknowledged not to need immediate further changes — [[d:paladin#1340773356575789147]] (Lhian +6, 2025-02-16); countered by community that WoC (Wrath of Cenarius, an item) needs a nerf for balance — [[d:paladin#1340773969275523249]] (Kyrsie, disputed).
- **[Prot]** Prot paladin has lowest HP/armor and weakest mitigation/defensive CDs among tanks per community consensus, contrasted with Warriors' ability to go uncrushable — [[d:paladin#1340783996279459951]] (Domnall, 2025-02-17).
- **[Prot vs Bear/Warrior]** Feral (bear) tanking seen as more suitable overall: better snap/AoE threat via items like WoC on pull, huge HP/rage pool, decent gear from dungeons; Prot Paladin described as "a Mana class with no gear options outside of tear sets," slow tempo — [[d:paladin#1352075359662768168]] (angels1m, 2025-03-20).
- **[Ret]** Consensus: Ret damage is highly guild-dependent — "the more raid dps your guild puts out, the worse the [ret] spec gets" (less time to stack Zeal/Consecration ticks) — [[d:paladin#1341550567612481616]] (R, 2025-02-19).
- **[Ret]** PvE: "Ret can do decent damage in Naxx because 90% of mobs are undead" (Seal/Judgement of Righteousness bonus), but this isn't representative of other raids (Kara40 unknown mob types) — [[d:paladin#1341104560261042347]] (Alban, 2025-02-17).
- **[Ret]** Three ret build archetypes as of April: Spellret (SoR + high-SP 1H like Wraithblade/TF, non-tier SP gear), Hybrid (SoR + Scythe or Ashbringer, SP off-pieces + T2.5 5-set incl. Visc gloves, "best parse and highest consistent dps for all dps builds"), AP-Ret (SoC + full T3 8-set with AP off-pieces, plays like a warrior) — [[d:paladin#1361820371313229844]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-04-16). Rule: 2H weapon → always Crusader Strike; 1H weapon → always Holy Strike.
- **[Ret]** Community frustration: ret rotation reduced to "Is HS buff on? - **[Holy]** Holy Paladin considered "easier to play and more impactful at low gear level," very powerful for effective healing in raids like Naxx, though Priest is more efficient/diverse and easier to gear; Shaman is "most boring but completely broken" for healing — [[d:paladin#1350019074914127892]] (Zblougi, 2025-03-14).
- **[Ret]** Ret has "no group utility," no Avenging Wrath ("This isn't Blizzard") — [[d:paladin#1356297283259601017]] (dusty, 2025-03-31).
- **[Prot]** Warriors' complaints about "OP" consecrate/Holy Strike threat framed by Grey as based on rare best-case procs (20% HS crit chains) while ignoring mana cost (1/3 of mana pool) and lower HP than warriors; used as evidence prot threat is actually mediocre, not overpowered — [[d:paladin#1351514966800470067]] (Grey, 2025-03-17, +context from replies).
- **[Ret]** Zeal stacking mechanic (buff-slot competition): Zeal and Vengeance each take 3 buff slots; rets can be near buff cap (16/32) by 2nd rotation from paladin buffs alone (3 zeal, 3 vengeance, seal, holy might, auras, blessings), causing dropped stacks — [[d:paladin#1348433115181224088]] (Excinerus, 2025-03-10).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Safest/easiest leveling route: go Prot tree first — take everything shield/tankiness-related, get Sanctuary talents up to Holy Shield; use Sanctity Aura + Ret Aura + Consecration to AoE farm and solo quests autonomously. After that, branch to Ret (movement speed + damage) or Holy (sustain/self-heal for soloing elite quests) — [[d:paladin#1362375717777838100]] (Zblougi, 2025-04-17).
- **[Leveling seals]** For damage use Seal of Righteousness; for HP sustain use Seal of Light (when low) + Judge for group regen; for mana sustain use Seal of Wisdom + Judge for group regen (best for AoE farming/avoiding downtime); against single tough/boss targets, judge Seal of the Crusader then switch to Seal of Righteousness (never keep SotC active); Ret tree unlocks Seal of Command for 2H damage. Other seals mostly PvP-only — [[d:paladin#1362380123008925898]] (Zblougi, 2025-04-17).
- **[Leveling]** Righteous Strikes and Bulwark mainly useful for dungeon tanking, not mandatory while leveling solo; Kings not worth taking pre-endgame (Benediction of Wisdom usually better); saved Holy points can go to Int/better Holy Light for sustain — [[d:paladin#1362386986886758601]] (Zblougi, 2025-04-17).
- **[Leveling]** Mixed Prot/Holy while leveling is "very efficient," lets a paladin do both tank and heal roles in dungeons; pure endgame Ret/Holy specs won't have any Prot points, so mixing is leveling-specific advice — [[d:paladin#1344732617190281337]] (Zblougi, 2025-02-27).
- **[Leveling]** In dungeons while leveling: go melee to regen mana with Seal of Wisdom; Holy Strike helps healing output; if low Int gear, put Benediction of Light on the tank; avoid max-rank heals (mana waste) — [[d:paladin#1344732617190281337]] (Zblougi, 2025-02-27).
- **[Leveling zones]** Suggested farm/leveling spots (source claims Chinese LazyScript community guide, imported): Lvl 20-22 Westfall Gnolls (avoid magic mobs); 22-25 Wetlands gnolls (south); 25-30 Wetlands gnolls (mid); 30-33 Duskwood cave ghouls (rare elite present, drops accessories); 33-35 Duskwood Werewolves; 35-36 Theramore shield quest → Crescent Glades for mail gear; 36-45 Dustwallow Marsh murlocs — [[d:paladin#1361640176664973505]] (Dzonathan, 2025-04-15; single source, unverified in-channel).
- **[Leveling]** New player leveling: 3-seal juggling (Wisdom/Righteous/Crusader) plus Holy Strike/Judgement/HoJ/Crusader Strike flagged by an expert responder as likely too much complexity too early; recommended to simplify — [[d:paladin#1342231345581264896]] (Vireca question, 2025-02-21; no single definitive reply captured as a fix, folded into general early-level guidance above).
- **[Leveling]** After level 40, paladins can wear plate — [[d:paladin#1345780005581492265]] (Mihnea question, 2025-03-02; confirmed implicitly by later replies about switching to plate at 54, see Zblougi 1346466347055448136).
- **[Leveling]** Once Ironclad talent obtained, switch to plate prioritizing Int; not much plate is available pre-raid besides expensive items — mail/leather with heal/int stats recommended until ~level 54 when dungeon-quality plate becomes available — [[d:paladin#1346466347055448136]] (Zblougi, 2025-03-04).
- **[Leveling]** Early leveling weapon choice: prioritize armor/stamina on tanking plate pieces before worrying about avoidance/spellpower once eHP is built — [[d:paladin#1352846277918064760]] (Grey, 2025-03-22, re: Kara plate).
- **[Leveling]** For Prot leveling weapon: prioritize a fast 1H with +int/stam for a bigger mana pool (compensates for lack of Seal of Wisdom access early and high Consecration cost); for Ret leveling weapon: use the slowest 2H available (prioritize DPS) and rank-1 Seal of Command to conserve mana; get 1-2 on-hit (spellstrike) items once Seal of Wisdom is available for extra mana — [[d:paladin#1359979949905150102]] (Istenneveben, 2025-04-10).
- **[Leveling/Tank]** With right gear/talents, a paladin can tank dungeons even without prior experience; big tank shortage means groups will accommodate; alternatively gathering an int gear set lets Ret-specced paladins heal dungeons up to ~level 50 (gets "significantly worse" after) — [[d:paladin#1362082588281405560]] (Anborn, 2025-04-16).

- **[Ret]** Basic endgame rotation: keep Crusader Strike on CD, upkeep 3 stacks of Zeal at all times; use Judgement of Wisdom and spam Holy Strike to sustain mana; Hammer of Justice + Holy Light to heal self if needed in combat, else free-cast Holy Light out of combat; Divine Shield/Hand of Protection/Divine Protection are escape tools only — [[d:paladin#1351037201189699596]] (ᶠᵒʳᵉᵗᵒˡᵈˡᵉᵍᵉⁿᵈˢ, 2025-03-13).
- **[Ret]** Weapon-speed rule of thumb: 2H weapon → use Crusader Strike; 1H weapon → use Holy Strike — [[d:paladin#1361820371313229844]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-04-16).
- **[Prot tanking]** "For tanking, click Crusader Strike. Drag it off your bar. Forget it ever existed" — Prot uses Holy Strike (magical strike) for threat almost exclusively; Crusader Strike (physical strike, haste-focused) rarely worth using in Prot — [[d:paladin#1357049786640891954]] (dusty, 2025-04-02); corroborated: "Prot: Uses the magical strike [Holy Strike] for threat, nearly never a reason to use the other" — [[d:paladin#1348579326278242336]] (Sarvesh, 2025-03-10). Holy spec uses the physical strike (Crusader Strike) for its haste-refresh, rarely the other.
- **[Prot]** Consecration mana-management macro (LUA function checks mana pool, keeps ~800 mana reserve, casts highest affordable Consecration rank) to avoid mana downtime — [[d:paladin#1347349949792256075]] (Excinerus, 2025-03-07).
- **[Prot]** Holy Shield "self-attack sustain" macro: checks if Holy Shield is off CD and casts it, else self-attacks with rank-1 Crusader Strike (via Attack in slot 26) purely to sustain haste buff stacks; requires a living attackable target; noted as exploit-adjacent workaround pending a fix — [[d:paladin#1350991922105417911]] (Morchog, 2025-03-17).
- **[Prot opener]** Detailed Prot dungeon-trash and boss openers: Trash — precast Seal of Wisdom + Holy Shield, walk up, Holy Strike+Judge, Consecrate (if 2+ mobs or high Int), reapply Seal of Wisdom, then Holy Shield > Consecrate > Holy Strike loop, judge+reseal on new targets. Boss — precast Seal of Wisdom + Holy Shield, walk up, Holy Strike+Judge, switch to Seal of Righteousness, then Holy Shield > Consecrate > Holy Strike+Judge > Seal of Righteousness repeat. With 5/5 Reckoning + 1 spellstrike item (e.g. Fiery Plate Gauntlets), the boss opener can be used for trash too — [[d:paladin#1366525445666897921]], [[d:paladin#1370280012397154424]] (Dzonathan, 2025-04-29/05-09).
- **[Ret endgame]** Detailed AP Ret single-target rotation once Vengeful Strikes is talented: judge Crusader, Seal of Command, Holy Strike, Crusader Strike x2, Holy Strike, then continue keeping Zeal/Holy Might up while casting Consecration; on trash, Seal of Command + spam Crusader Strike + Consecration (skip Holy Strike since trash dies too fast for it to matter) — [[d:paladin#1374412650649485333]] (Iseut, 2025-05-20).
- **[Prot threat priority]** Prot threat rotation preference confirmed multiple times: judge Crusader (damage) or Wisdom (mana) then use Seal of Righteousness for damage/threat and judge again thereafter; using Seal of Crusader as your maintained seal is a DPS/threat loss even with the faster attacks — [[d:paladin#1366067018964406322]] (HolyMoose, 2025-04-27).
- **[Ret]** CS vs HS threat-focused critique: "The problem with CS is the threat... CS needs to do equivalent threat to HS while also not being Holy damage" — because CS is physical, it doesn't benefit from Sanctity/threat-boosting-from-holy-damage mechanics the way HS does — [[d:paladin#1353274555283673119]] (dusty, 2025-03-23).
- **[Holy melee]** Theodore's (top-5 Turtlogs Holy Paladin, 5/9 Kara40) detailed melee-healing rotation and effective-healing-per-cast data: averages ~900 effective healing per Flash of Light, ~1700 per Holy Shock, ~2400 per Holy Strike (vs. ~2100 for a Shaman's Chain Heal) — with the Libram of the Faithful (Holy Strike also grants Crusader Strike access while maintaining Holy Strike frequency, Zeal haste, and a Holy Shock reset), rotation becomes: Wisdom-judge in one GCD, Holy Shock, Crusader Strike, then a 1.3s Holy Light/Shock combo, then repeated Holy Strike rotations (flash-flash-flash-strike or 2-flash-strike, weaving in Holy Shock resets); haste lets a melee healer reposition between GCDs without losing a heal. Notes heal crits use spell crit not melee crit, and heals land even on immune/banished (miss) targets — [[d:paladin#1379171818123952168]], [[d:paladin#1379171957181780139]] (Theodore, 2025-06-02).
- **[Holy]** Simpler rule of thumb for Holy Strike vs Crusader Strike+Holy Shock: pressing Holy Strike costs 1 GCD for a ~1000-per-target 5-man heal with no mana cost; pressing Crusader Strike requires a follow-up Holy Shock (2 GCDs total) for a 70% chance to heal one target for ~2k (with Daybreak chance) — "half of the time it's not even a choice, just press Holy Strike" — [[d:paladin#1379490296194269214]] (Zblougi, 2025-06-03).
- **[Holy]** General melee-healing target-count rule: use Holy Strike when there are 3+ injured targets within ~10-13yd (not critically low), otherwise spam FoL while Holy Strike is on CD (max ~3 FoL per Holy Strike); judge Seal of Light onto the boss once, especially with 2pc T3 — [[d:paladin#1399695295524110427]] (Mithreon, 2025-07-31).
- **[Shockadin PvP]** Suggested "shockadin" PvP rotation: Seal of the Crusader → Judgement of the Crusader → Seal of Righteousness → Holy Shock → Crusader Strike (to reset Holy Shock if on CD) → Holy Shock → Crusader Strike (repeat) → finisher/low-mana: Judgement of Righteousness → low mana: Seal of Wisdom + Holy Strike — posted as a question, unconfirmed by an expert in-chunk (see Contested/open questions) — [[d:paladin#1407663127499112530]], [[d:paladin#1407670406898520076]] (McBe, 2025-08-20).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot/dungeon]** Dungeon AoE rotation: Consecration on CD + Holy Strike; high armor + shield for mitigation against 6+ mobs; AoE healing during farming done via Holy Shock/Flash of Light/Holy Light supported by Concentration Aura, Holy Judgement (Judgement buff) and Spiritual Focus talent; optional Hand of Edward the Odd + SoR combo to proc it often for instant Holy Lights — [[d:paladin#1354755245623869450]] (Dzonathan, 2025-03-27).
- **[Prot]** AoE tanking (e.g., Gnarlmoon raven adds) tips: remove Blessing of Salvation from healers so they can help pull threat; pre-cast Consecration at the spot where ravens will be grouped; use Group Blessing of Kings or Salvation (or worst-case Blessing of Light) for initial threat the moment adds spawn — [[d:paladin#1361732205692321862]] (angels1m, 2025-04-14).
- **[Prot]** AoE threat comparison: Ret Aura (imp) at 1,000 SP does ~38.4 TPS; Holy Strike at 400 SP gives ~36.64 TPS (with Righteous Strikes math); roughly breaks even around MC gear levels for single-target threat, but for AoE farming Ret Aura only competes with Consecration and breaks even around ~700 SP (rarely reached outside full-consumed raid gear) — [[d:paladin#1347069038924398703]] (Grey, 2025-03-06).
- **[Prot]** Main tool for handling AoE aggro theft: Thunderfury + Sapper Charges, or simply asking DPS to wait ~2-3 seconds before attacking; Consecration alone (even at 1100 SP + Sanctity Aura + Enlighten) is only ~340 TPS — [[d:paladin#1347480583781093377]] (Grey, 2025-03-07).
- **[Prot AoE leveling]** Standard 30/21/0 AoE farming routine: judge Wisdom, reapply Seal of Wisdom, then Holy Shield on CD, Holy Strike and Crusader Strike as needed on CD, reapply Seal of Wisdom on self when it drops; for AoE specifically, pull more than one mob and add Consecration; keep Retribution Aura up and Blessing of Sanctuary active — [[d:paladin#1385666080877318175]] (Scubasage, 2025-06-19).
- **[Prot AoE leveling]** Never use Crusader Strike while AoE tanking — bonus threat only comes from Holy damage and CS is physical; ask DPS to hold off a couple seconds so reflective/Consecrate threat can establish; use Ret Aura; spam Consecration on pulls; keep Seal+Judgement of Wisdom with a fast weapon for indefinite mana — [[d:paladin#1416457668373119076]] (Arle, 2025-09-13).
- **[Prot AoE farming]** FRD (Field of Retribution/RFD-style) vampiric farming: there's a breakpoint where enough mobs attacking make you effectively invulnerable (unless stunned); danger is the initial pull and closing out the fight — insufficient mobs early means insufficient healing, and uneven mob deaths near the end can be fatal below critical mass — [[d:paladin#1385298126373523586]] (Spooky Boots, 2025-06-19). Also recommended: don't do FRD vamp farming for beginners due to risk from melee mobs; start with 1 pack LOS'd using Consecration + a 30/21/0 HS/HS survival build, scale to 2 then 3+ packs with Vengeance Prot once comfortable — [[d:paladin#1363584583970324681]] (Illithris, 2025-04-20).
- **[Prot threat, off-tank]** When off-tanking (not first in kill order), a 5-10 second head start on mitigation-focused play is usually enough threat buffer; when actively chasing threat on a boss, ensure sufficient TPS instead — flexibility should live in gear sets, not talent switching — [[d:paladin#1365386824801063025]] (Ascuz, 2025-04-25).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Ret]** STR > STA > AGI/INT stat priority for Ret; hybrid SoR builds want spell hit/crit, AP builds want AP gear — [[d:paladin#1352758355428638832]] (dai, 2025-03-22).
- **[Holy]** Crit becomes the stat to prioritize for Holy Paladin (via Daybreak proc mechanics) — [[d:paladin#1340412100660559932]] (Lhian +3, 2025-02-16).
- **[Holy]** Healing power vs crit tradeoff: below ~400 +Healing, chase +Healing; above ~700-800 +Healing (not an exact number), spell crit becomes more valuable — [[d:paladin#1353758969583108247]] (Thalrinel, 2025-03-24).
- **[Spell hit mechanics]** Every spell needs spell hit to land; even at cap (16%) there's still 1% full-resist chance; unlike other schools, Holy damage never gets partial resists once it hits, so paladins don't need spell penetration (unlike Shadow) — [[d:paladin#1342555530622795837]] (Istenneveben, 2025-02-21).
- **[Prot]** Strength on tier gear provides negligible value for tanks: full T1 set gives 88 Str = only 4.4 block value and 12.57 TPS across the entire set, less than the 13.8 TPS from a single 18-SP tier helm piece alone (SP contributes to Holy Strike, Holy Shield, Consecrate, and SoR threat) — [[d:paladin#1351877947161837662]] (Grey, 2025-03-19).
- **[Prot]** Mana economy critique: Ret/Prot Paladin (and Hunter/Rogue/Arcane Mage) don't get better resource economy as gear improves, unlike most classes (e.g., Holy Paladin refunds more mana from higher crit, Warriors/Bears generate more rage from higher white damage) — [[d:paladin#1352069352723189800]] (Grey, 2025-03-20).
- **[Prot]** Downranking spells doesn't help fresh-60 paladins (still poor regen/SP); near-BiS casters/healers can go nearly indefinitely without drinking due to spirit/mp5, but Prot/Ret paladin stay stuck at "fresh-60 mana economy" even at high gear — [[d:paladin#1352233300290306089]] (Grey, 2025-03-20).
- **[Prot]** Avoidance cap question (fresh 60 Prot at 346 defense, ~79% avoidance w/ Holy Shield) — unresolved in chunk whether cap is reachable pre-T3; only reply was "just get TF bro" (see Contested/open questions) — [[d:paladin#1351309725916074017]] (Elivane question, 2025-03-18; [[d:paladin#1351319220994048010]] Leo, non-answer).
- **[Reference, pinned]** Full spell SP-scaling coefficient table (as of ~2025-07-04, pre the July 25 nerf pass): 1H Seal of Righteousness 0.092×WeaponSpeed×SP; 2H SoR 0.125×WeaponSpeed×SP; Judgement of Righteousness 0.71×SP; Seal of Command 0.24×SP + 0.7×WpnDmg; Judgement of Command 0.43×SP + 0.15×AP; Repentance 0.5×SP + 0.08×AP; Holy Strike 0.71×SP + 0.4×WpnDmg; Hammer of Wrath 0.43×SP; Bulwark 0.43×SP; Exorcism 0.43×SP; Crusader Strike 0.2×SP + WpnDmg; Holy Wrath 0.19×SP; Holy Shield (per charge) 0.15×SP; Consecration (across all 8 ticks) 0.66×SP; Retribution Aura 0.03×SP; Holy Light 0.71×SP; Flash of Light 0.43×SP; Holy Shock (dmg or heal) 0.43×SP; Daybreak proc 0.32×SP; Mending Light (Holy Strike heal effect) 0.05×SP — [[d:paladin#1390647300568911932]] (Mithreon, pinned +8, 2025-07-04; corroborated by an earlier % breakdown credited to forum user Clothar — [[d:paladin#1390622335085711453]]). NOTE: the 2025-07-25 patch subsequently reduced Crusader Strike's SP coefficient 33%→20% and 2H SoR 12.8%→12.5%×WeaponSpeed (see 1.18.1 changes).
- **[Physical hit]** Since Class Changes 2 (CC2), physical hit cap is 8% total; each point of bonus weapon skill (from talents/racials/quest) gives 0.2% hit (so 5 weapon skill = 1% hit); with the Fray Island weapon-skill quest done + Precision talented, a non-human needs ~4% hit from gear (3% for a human using a 2H sword/mace, from the extra +6 weapon skill of talent+racial) — [[d:paladin#1385462966928805938]] (Spooky Boots, 2025-06-20).
- **[Prot]** Weapon-skill/glancing-blow math: going from 303→310 weapon skill is a flat 5.6% white-damage improvement from reduced glancing-blow damage alone — used to compare weapon choices (e.g. Herald of Woe's 77.8 flat DPS + 0.81% crit vs Obsidian Edged Blade's 70.7 flat damage) — [[d:paladin#1390411069754708158]] (Fjordain, 2025-07-04).
- **[Avoidance mechanics]** Avoidance "table" priority: dodge/parry/block rolls are prioritized top-to-bottom on the attack table, so having dodge+parry+block sum over 100% pushes the lowest-priority (block) off the table with zero value — e.g. 30% dodge + 40% parry + 50% block = 20% of block chance wasted — [[d:paladin#1405305965489815664]] (Fjordain, 2025-08-14).
- **[Prot]** Strength→AP conversion with buffs: 1 Str becomes effectively 1.4 Str (2.8 AP) when accounting for +10% Kings and +10% Divine Strength (first-row Holy talent); with Holy Might active (+20% Str) it's effectively 2.4x (some debate whether keeping Holy Might up is worth the DPS tradeoff at low Str stacking) — [[d:paladin#1356782508951474236]] (Adamant, 2025-04-02).
- **[Ret]** Melee crit clarification: crit chance is a proportion of swings that don't already Miss/get Dodged/Glance — 40% crit does NOT mean 40% of all swings crit — [[d:paladin#1394036208153268377]] (Fjordain, 2025-07-13).
- **[Ret]** UTB (Untamed Blade) proc synergy: Seal of Righteousness has much higher uptime with UTB than Seal of Command because SoR triggers on every auto attack (behaves like a melee attack), while SoC only procs ~30-40% of the time despite also counting as a melee attack — [[d:paladin#1408897599892754595]] (Adamant, 2025-08-23).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Prot]** Mana economy structural weakness: Prot/Ret Paladin (along with Hunter/Rogue/Arcane Mage) don't get better resource economy as gear improves, unlike most classes — Holy Paladin refunds more mana from crit, Warriors/Bears generate more rage from higher white damage, etc. — [[d:paladin#1352069352723189800]] (Grey, 2025-03-20; see also Stats section).
- **[Prot]** Taunt is considered the best in the game: 11% miss chance baseline (talented) with 10yd range, and gear Prot already wants for SP threat (tear, wraith blade, ES neck, C'thun ring) happens to carry hit; ranked ahead of Shaman's conditional 12% free-hit taunt, and Warrior/Bear tied for 3rd — [[d:paladin#1351245020925268089]] (Grey, 2025-03-17).
- **[Prot]** Hand of Reckoning (taunt) macro to only fire on targets not already attacking you: `/script if UnitExists("target") and UnitExists("targettarget") and UnitName("targettarget") ~= UnitName("player") then CastSpellByName("Hand of Reckoning") end` — posted as found-online, unconfirmed reliability, with a caution that spamming it plus a healer immediately re-pulling aggro can backfire — [[d:paladin#1411687708484702328]] (Tweak, question, 2025-08-31; unanswered — see Contested/open questions).
- **[Prot]** Greater Blessing of Kings/Sanctuary generate raid-wide threat distributed among all mobs currently in combat with the raid (more people buffed = more total threat generated, split across all engaged mobs) — [[d:paladin#1389891276471799841]] (angels1m, 2025-07-02).
- **[General]** Righteous Fury toggling: `/unbuff [noequipped:Shields]Righteous Fury` macro (via CleveRoidMacros) intended to auto-remove RF when switching to DPS unless a shield is equipped was reported broken (conditional not working) by multiple players — unresolved bug in-chunk — [[d:paladin#1390101164271472692]], [[d:paladin#1390810276235841628]] (Alban, 2025-07-03/05); Lhian later confirmed notifying a developer about a related Righteous Fury issue — [[d:paladin#1406580595714293804]] (Lhian, 2025-08-16).
- **[Ret]** Buff-cap mechanics affecting group play: raids with too many "group auras" (Sanctity, Leader of the Pack, multiple Hunter auras, multiple totems) at the buff cap can cause a paladin's own self-buffs to be dropped/unable to recast due to aura priority — a community (non-dev-confirmed) observation, not a documented mechanic — [[d:paladin#1377408441911349309]] (theoloras/warkrak, 2025-05-29).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Prot]** Basic threat-building toolkit for new tanks: Righteous Fury (passive threat mod) + Retribution Aura + Consecration + Holy Shield constantly, Seal of Wisdom/Righteousness + Judgement on single targets, Holy Strike as main threat spell — a player reporting persistent aggro loss despite following this was told there's no single silver bullet; likely a gear/level issue (see Leveling) — [[d:paladin#1377662286809796689]] (Fisher question, 2025-05-29; unresolved, see Contested/open questions).
- **[Prot]** Defense/hit gearing checklist for a paladin starting to raid: non-human Prot needs 9% total physical hit (3% from talents + weapon-skill quest for ~3% more + remaining from gear); prioritize getting to 8% hit as a first gearing goal since most Prot threat comes from Holy Strike and misses are costly — [[d:paladin#1377497050018611260]] (Don question), [[d:paladin#1390340164542398574]] (Destroses, "You are going to want to get to 8% hit. That is a prio since most of our threat comes from holy strike").
- **[Prot]** Threat meter recommendations/issues: KTM (Kong Threat Meter) doesn't register Improved Righteous Fury even though it should; TWT (Turtle WoW Threat?) registers it and shows values matching logs, per multiple experienced tanks comparing the same gear producing different in-game threat feel — open compatibility issue, no resolution in-chunk — [[d:paladin#1408467978994843658]] (Mithreon, 2025-08-22; see Contested/open questions).
- **[Prot]** Weapon speed choice for tanking debated: fast 1H favors more frequent Seal of Wisdom procs for mana return (Crusader Strike doesn't scale off weapon damage so speed doesn't matter there), while a slower 1H gives more Crusader Strike weapon-damage-scaled threat when stacking Zeal — most threat still comes from Holy Strike regardless — [[d:paladin#1410823330285748234]] (Homtanks question, 2025-08-29; not definitively answered in chunk).
- **[Prot]** Onyxia/Baron Geddon divine-shield tactic: as MT tanking a mechanic like Baron Geddon's "bomb," Divine Shield can be used to remove the debuff/damage and immediately cancelled to still taunt and retain aggro — practical MT technique — [[d:paladin#1369247600242458685]] (Citizen Chris, 2025-05-06).
- **[Prot]** Solid stat targets suggested for a new-to-tanking Prot at 46: prioritize stam/armor, then defense=avoidance, then str/int; keeping SoW up and spamming Holy Shield/Consecration solves mana issues; vampirism gear valued highly for sustain (see Stats/Consumables) — [[d:paladin#1376502748564033556]] (Britney, 2025-05-26, question thread; general community consensus reflected across many similar Q&As in this chunk).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Melee healing is Turtle WoW's signature Holy Paladin playstyle: FoL remains the "bread and butter" baseline, balanced by encounters that reward melee healing (Holy Strike/Crusader Strike/Holy Shock combo) — no single global guide exists as of mid-2025 per community consensus, though the spec is considered "very fun... with a lot of optimizations" — [[d:paladin#1376852150205808692]] (Zblougi, 2025-05-27).
- **[Holy]** Melee healing shines specifically on predictable-damage encounters (e.g. Loatheb with a well-mitigated tank trivializes the healer mechanic; Mephistroth felt "really safe" to melee-heal, with Mending Light hitting 33.8% of effective healing there); some bosses (e.g. Rupturan) don't allow it at all — [[d:paladin#1382265813800714351]] (Iseut, 2025-06-11), [[d:paladin#1373985305526538301]] (Iseut, 2025-05-19).
- **[Holy]** Melee healing is explicitly NOT a DPS-while-healing playstyle: "You wont do any DPS if you want to heal, and if you truly want to dps you'll be both a very bad healer and a very bad dps" — it's refreshing gameplay but can be frustrating on encounters where it's unplayable (forced to one-button FoL spam) — [[d:paladin#1382266577516236891]] (Zblougi, 2025-06-11).
- **[Holy]** Mana economy for melee Holy: keeping up Seal of Wisdom while meleeing largely trivializes mana management (a single proc "almost pays for itself"); using Holy Shock then Crusader Strike back-to-back (two instants) triggers an auto-attack too, doubling SoW proc chances — [[d:paladin#1384397336393809921]] (Sarvesh, 2025-06-17). Rank-1 Crusader Strike gives the same Holy Shock CD reset as max rank for 100 less mana — [[d:paladin#1384397336393809921]].
- **[Holy]** Comparative mana economy: Holy Paladin with 60% Illumination is worse baseline than a "normal" caster-healer but has Seal of Wisdom access in melee, largely trivializing the issue if melee-healing is viable on the fight; Resto Druid has worse mana economy overall (has to stop and drink) but can Nature's Swiftness/rely on HoTs to catch up; Hpal has more "gimmicky" tools (Hand of Protection/Freedom) that reward proactive play — [[d:paladin#1404208543556698284]] (Illithris, 2025-08-11).
- **[Holy]** JoL (Judgement of Light) described repeatedly as "the most broken and underrated spell in the kit" — top healing-parse chart in any server where it's tracked, so devs deliberately keep it untracked/excluded from meters to avoid a forced nerf — [[d:paladin#1349705167393783909]] (Zblougi), [[d:paladin#1386689089637711922]] (Zblougi, 2025-06-23).
- **[Holy leveling]** Early rotation for leveling Holy healers in dungeons: judge Wisdom, reapply Seal of Wisdom, Holy Strike to heal a bit while triple-dipping Wisdom procs, then Holy Light/Flash/Holy Strike in a constant rhythm; judge on cooldown to speed up Holy Light casts via Judgement-triggered cast-time reduction; watch for undergeared/2H tanks early on — [[d:paladin#1408151414445834242]] (Bello, 2025-08-20).
- **[Holy]** Resurrection spell reminder: Holy Paladins should complete their class resurrection quest before queuing as a healer — should not be missing it by level 42 — [[d:paladin#1400094685632004096]] (TheEgyptianCat, +12, 2025-07-29).

## PvP
- **[General]** Sanctified Command talent (previously protected Seals from being purged/dispelled) was removed; developer comment (quoted) said Holy Strike now provides equivalent mana-restoration support, and promised to "closely monitor paladin matchups against Purge" and make Seals undispellable if too oppressive — community repeatedly noted, months/a year later, that Purge remained extremely oppressive against Paladins with no follow-through — [[d:paladin#1378797143321608262]] (Alban, quoting the dev comment, 2025-06-01), reiterated [[d:paladin#1399386939991527434]] (tripleXDengine, "10 months later prolly still monitoring").

- **[PvP]** Repentance (Ret CC) has fairly short range, making it a weaker kiting counter than hoped; Hand of Freedom is the only anti-kite tool paladins have (no sprint/charge/slow) and is frequently purged — [[d:paladin#1351124693352185886]] (Alban, 2025-03-17).
- **[PvP]** Ret PvP burst technique: seal-twisting SoC/SoR is possible but "your burst in pvp will be quite a casino" — [[d:paladin#1403089892434640927]] (Mithreon, 2025-08-07).
- **[PvP]** Historical seal-twisting viability across eras: worked in OG vanilla (nothing else to do, "auto-attack simulator"), worked in TBC (no abundant haste, no Windfury-off-abilities), but does NOT work well on Turtle now due to 5.5s main-ability CDs, incentive to Consecration-spam, and Windfury proccing off all melee attacks/judgements — leaves no time to twist — [[d:paladin#1382717207594799184]] (iso, 2025-06-12).
- **[PvP]** Ret defensive/utility toolkit valued highly in dungeon/group PvE-adjacent play even by non-mains: heals when healer OOMs, Hand of Protection on squishies, tanking a few adds on a botched pull — "a run saving machine" — [[d:paladin#1419446394288209970]] (Vazdeverence, 2025-09-22).
- **[PvP]** Alliance BG win-rate cited multiple times at 41% as a counter-argument to "Paladin is OP" claims, since Paladins comprise roughly 40% of Alliance's population/teams — [[d:paladin#1398238594191331460]] (Alban, 2025-07-25).
