## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** No formal paladin/class changes had been announced as of 2025-10-21; expected reveal timing ~2 months out based on prior CC1/CC2 patch cadence (both revealed in October) — [[d:paladin#1430102974935339009]], [[d:paladin#1430103819827544064]] (speculation, unconfirmed).
- **[Ret/Prot]** Dev Lhian reportedly mentioned wanting more SoC proc-rate (PPM) and more AP scaling on Judgement of Command; current SoC is ~7 PPM — [[d:paladin#1430239765378105558]], [[d:paladin#1430241150710583538]] (unconfirmed intent, not yet shipped).
- **[PvP]** Repentance previously could delay a FAP'd (immune) flag carrier by 6s; "now it does nothing" per Jesse Potter, implying a mechanic change nerfed this interaction — [[d:paladin#1430425762976235651]] (single source, exact patch not specified).
- **[Ret/Prot]** dusty states as a heads-up (single source, presented as fact rather than speculation): "all seals, blessings, and auras are being moved to baseline" — [[d:paladin#1431181375674908713]] (unconfirmed by a second source in this chunk; treat as reported intent, not shipped).
- **[Holy]** "Sanctified Wrath" was a real talent design by dev Lhian that was cut before shipping in CC2 — [[d:paladin#1431518416916713473]] (Destroses).
- **[Prot/Ret era note]** Between CC1 and CC2, Holy Strike changed from an auto-attack-replacing "on next swing" enhancer into its own standalone instant attack; this broke the viability of 2H tank/hybrid builds that were tried in CC1 — [[d:paladin#1431510211994058812]] (Destroses). Separately, Crusader Strike became a 6s-CD ability that grants Zeal (previously spammable on GCD only) — [[d:paladin#1430674284464050290]] (billienomates).
- **[Ret capstone]** A Ret set-bonus/proc effect was nerfed from 50% down to 15% for being "very strong" (exact effect not fully specified, likely Judgement of Command/Vengeance-adjacent) — [[d:paladin#1430876678770528296]] (Destroses, single source).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Prot]** Widely criticized threat kit: Holy Strike is described as the only real threat-generating ability; Crusader Strike's physical damage doesn't benefit from Righteous Fury threat multiplier; Consecration threat is below Mage/Warlock AoE; item scaling from T2 to T3.5 is only ~10-15% better — detailed critique by Laigeer, self-described from a design-analysis angle — [[d:paladin#1430212267978395809]] (confirmed/expanded by angels1m's independent threat math, [[d:paladin#1430212099887595701]]).
- **[Prot]** Naxxramas is considered one of the best raids for Prot paladins to perform in — [[d:paladin#1430195088046297341]] (angels1m).
- **[Ret]** Disagreement on current state: dusty says "Ret is pretty much fine," with its main problem being AP-Ret's dependence on Windfury (unavailable on PvP/Horde-mirrored realms) rather than the spec itself — [[d:paladin#1430390501378363504]], [[d:paladin#1430390572719407185]]. Istenneveben counters that Ret can pull 1000+ single-target DPS in pre-raid BiS and isn't in a bad spot — [[d:paladin#1430377998875365436]], [[d:paladin#1430308508137689171]]. Contrast: Wulf reports only ~650 average dps on a well-geared Ambershire (PvP realm) Ret post-nerf, feeling weaker than a "very crappy geared" pre-nerf Nordanaar test character — [[d:paladin#1430354259496665219]] (disputed).
- **[Ret]** Pure AP (warrior-style) gearing is "definitely behind" hybrid/SP gearing for Ret — [[d:paladin#1430285193670955138]] (Beldrin, single source but stated plainly as known fact).
- **[Ret]** SoC (Seal of Command) spec is called the "meme build" until buffed, by Jesse Potter (+3 reactions), yet is also stated to be the best-performing Ret build in scenarios without Windfury access, while SoR/AP-ret is best with WF — [[d:paladin#1430403919120695346]].

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[Holy]** Example 60-point Holy build shared by Mithreon: https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=AoaAZSFQpAYoB--FoAAAI — described as workable "if you don't need PoJ, Improved Judgement, and have a [strong] Smite Priest in your party" — [[d:paladin#1430171648392433664]].
- **[Holy]** Concentration Aura gives 35% pushback resistance baseline (corrected from an initial guess of 30%); the improvement talent raises it to 50% concentration + 15% silence-resist chance — [[d:paladin#1430171416397086851]], [[d:paladin#1430171521850150993]] (Jesse Potter). Spiritual Focus + base Concentration Aura already reach 105% combined pushback resistance, so the talent point is mainly worth it only if facing silences or if party members are stuck at ~50-65% pushback resistance from other sources — [[d:paladin#1430171337036533800]].
- **[Prot/Holy]** Imp Blessing of Protection (BoP) is not generally needed but is prioritized for use on mages who pull threat on every AoE pack; reduces BoP cooldown — [[d:paladin#1430170082432253995]], [[d:paladin#1430170265060642836]].
- **[Prot/Ret]** Excinerus notes the Prot and Ret talent trees don't have enough "good" talents to fill 51 points without dead picks, unlike Holy which has enough for 53 — [[d:paladin#1430316521774776483]] (corroborated later at [[d:paladin#1430550591704137900]]: "you literally can't put all your talent points in Ret, even taking the unusable stuff"). Nobody takes Improved Devotion Aura — [[d:paladin#1430317004556079276]].
- **[Prot]** Two named endgame Prot talent build links shared by Mithreon: "holyprot" — https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=AoaAYQDQB-AoYFZbAAZY- and "vengeprot" — https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=-AoYFZYAAZQ-AoQoVIAAF — [[d:paladin#1431549623503290418]].
- **[Prot]** If speccing into Righteous Strikes, take at least 3 points in Reckoning as well — [[d:paladin#1431190419148116071]] (Jesse Potter); nobody builds fully around Reckoning alone as a standalone strategy — [[d:paladin#1431190654092050453]].
- **[Prot]** Righteous Strikes (5/5) is recommended despite Strength-to-Block-Value being a weak conversion, because it's still considered "a really decent talent choice" overall — [[d:paladin#1430721277806514206]] (angels1m/wasik).
- **[PvE]** Vindication does not work on bosses, making it a dead talent choice for PvE content; it functions only in PvP — [[d:paladin#1431510211994058812]] (Destroses).
- **[Prot]** Defense rating is generally treated as a low-priority/"useless" stat by Turtle Prot players, since crit immunity against bosses comes from Holy Shield uptime rather than base Defense; players top up Defense only if a small amount (e.g. ~7) is needed to close the gap to full crit immunity while Holy Shield is active — [[d:paladin#1430723444592476232]], [[d:paladin#1430726846080749690]], [[d:paladin#1430729600010289205]] (angels1m/Destroses).

## Leveling
- **[Ret]** For pure questing while leveling, use a 2H weapon (Ret spec); for dungeons/AoE grinding, use 1H+shield (Prot spec) — [[d:paladin#1430141997511086180]] (Destroses).
- **[Leveling]** Mace of Devotion (Northwind) is the standout 2H leveling weapon known to Destroses (mostly played Holy/Prot) — [[d:paladin#1430142534650429441]]; obtainable via a simple level-26 quest rather than the long chain (Chop Chop) — [[d:paladin#1430053260265525290]]; it grants bonus Holy spell power and is called "insanely high damage for its level" while soloing (Jesse Potter) — [[d:paladin#1430051491657420892]].
- **[Leveling]** A notable two-handed sword is available around level 34 from Stormwrought — [[d:paladin#1430112855075586048]] (Alexander).
- **[Leveling]** "Of the Eagle" affix gear recommended while leveling Holy/Prot — [[d:paladin#1430078883016736820]] (Jesse Potter clarifying the affix name).
- **[Prot leveling]** Holy Shield acquisition timing gates whether you can AoE-farm/tank dungeons; if just questing, there's no need to rush Holy Shield — [[d:paladin#1430051754392948846]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Holy leveling]** For a player planning to heal at 60, it's recommended to still level as Prot with a healing gear set on the side rather than leveling pure Holy — Holy gains nothing extra from the Prot tree at 60 anyway, and Prot handles solo/offtank duty better while leveling — [[d:paladin#1430316693984383099]] (gastro).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Ret/Prot]** At high haste, optimal ability timing means waiting for the second auto-attack to land before overlapping Judgement/seal procs, to avoid "wasting" an auto without a seal proc — [[d:paladin#1430163208345223179]] (Jesse Potter, describing tight rotation windows).
- **[Ret]** SoC currently procs at ~7 PPM; sustaining SoC's mana cost typically requires 2 equipped spellstrike items, vs. only 1 needed to sustain Seal of Righteousness — [[d:paladin#1430236133295526080]], [[d:paladin#1430241150710583538]] (Excinerus/Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret]** Exorcism currently generates negligible threat for Ret ("does fuck all threat gen now") — [[d:paladin#1430161928050970687]] (Jesse Potter, single source/offhand).
- **[Ret]** Core rotation rule diagnosed for a struggling raider: always have a seal buff active when your white (auto-attack) swing lands — delay casting Judgement if needed so the seal is reapplied only after the auto lands, not before — [[d:paladin#1431380231587102822]]. A logs review found the player had no Seal of Righteousness active for ~50% of melee hits, directly costing damage; even a well-performing comparison Ret wasn't at 100% SoR-on-autos, showing how easy this mistake is to make — [[d:paladin#1431381602033733664]], [[d:paladin#1431381968385478777]] (Beldrin/Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret]** Ability priority: Crusader Strike > Judgement of Righteousness > Consecration, all used on cooldown; Holy Strike is used only roughly every 3rd Crusader Strike cooldown, purely to refresh the Holy Might Str buff, not as a primary damage button (Crusader Strike outdamages Holy Strike by ~1600 in a reviewed log) — [[d:paladin#1431382428382924910]], [[d:paladin#1431382480656666715]], [[d:paladin#1431382385877848228]], [[d:paladin#1431382280017805312]] (Beldrin/theoloras/warkrak/Jesse Potter). Zeal (the buff from stacking Crusader Strikes) should be maintained as close to permanently as possible — [[d:paladin#1431383050394013767]].
- **[Ret]** Judgement is not on the shared GCD, so a seal should always be re-applied immediately after judging — [[d:paladin#1431384631768518818]], [[d:paladin#1431384690564010004]] (Beldrin). Avoid binding multiple spells (e.g. several seals + Judgement) to a single macro button: doing so caused over 20% ability downtime in logs for several raiders due to fumbled seal swaps eating GCDs — [[d:paladin#1431384577359745167]], [[d:paladin#1431384873993633792]] (Excinerus/Beldrin).
- **[Ret]** If your Spell Power is low (undergeared for spellret), prefer Seal of Command over Seal of Righteousness/Seal of the Crusader as your primary seal; Seal of the Crusader (SotC) is generally used only for its Judgement effect, then swapped away — [[d:paladin#1431381172398592173]], [[d:paladin#1431381715384799366]] (theoloras/warkrak).
- **[Ret]** Macro tooling: ExAutoCSHS (github.com/Excinerus/ExAutoCSHS) + ActionBarProfiles (github.com/Siventt/ActionBarProfiles) can automate Crusader Strike/Holy Strike selection based on current Zeal/Might buff state and equipped weapon type (1H vs 2H). Example: `#showtooltip Holy Strike` / `/AutoCS OpenCS PrioZeal Exorcism` executes the standard AP-Ret alternation and casts Exorcism when both strikes are on cooldown. Default logic: with a 2H weapon, build one Zeal stack, then one Holy Strike, then stack Zeal to 3 in a CS-CS-HS pattern refreshing Might roughly every 3rd cast; with a 1H weapon, open with Holy Strike then use one Crusader Strike per 3 Holy Strikes — [[d:paladin#1431383481547493517]], [[d:paladin#1431388191000100945]] (Excinerus).
- **[Ret]** Suggested pre-pull ability sequencing by range while closing on a boss: apply your seal on the pull timer, Exorcism at 30 yards, Repentance at 20 yards, Judge your assigned seal at 10 yards (practice re-sealing immediately after judging), then strike once in melee — [[d:paladin#1431384317849894992]] (Excinerus).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot]** For a 3-target Consecration-tanking scenario, angels1m calculated that a 128 SP gear increase adds roughly 4.8k total threat across Holy Strike + Judgement of Righteousness + Consecration + Exorcism + Seal of Righteousness + Holy Shield combined, comparable to (or exceeding, depending on target count) a Thunderfury proc's ~5100 threat on that same pull — [[d:paladin#1430212099887595701]] (angels1m's own math, self-flagged as possibly imperfect).
- **[Prot]** On multi-target pulls (~4 targets), an SP-stacked weapon is preferred over Thunderfury for AoE threat; TF remains stronger for single-target/opening burst — [[d:paladin#1430206518795567226]], [[d:paladin#1430206786811727874]] (Jesse Potter/angels1m).
- **[Prot]** For pure add/off-tank duty in Molten Core, a single spellstrike source (e.g. just a ring) is enough to sustain mana and threat comfortably; a second spellstrike source (e.g. gloves) isn't required unless main-tanking — confirmed by someone who off-tanked MC with only 1 spellstrike source — [[d:paladin#1431304626988388392]], [[d:paladin#1431304956073345125]], [[d:paladin#1431305000755138632]] (Tsubasa/Wilhelm).
- **[Prot leveling/dungeon]** Suggested dungeon-tank opener: mark the target with a skull, cast Seal of Righteousness, cast Holy Shield while running in, then Holy Strike + Judge the skull target and drop Consecration — [[d:paladin#1431134534631555173]] (Dodging). Seal of Light is essentially useless outside of AoE farming — [[d:paladin#1431134372584489022]] (Destroses). Use Sanctuary Aura (not Devotion) for tanking, and Retribution Aura specifically helps multi-target threat — [[d:paladin#1431139104870891693]], [[d:paladin#1431139240053571687]] (gastro).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Hit: can run as low as 6-7% until Kara40 without much issue on some setups (gastro) — [[d:paladin#1430342775529934950]]; on Ambershire specifically the ~6% hit breakpoint (from a T0.5 shoulder quest costing ~200g) is considered worth it, disputed as "maybe not" on Nordanaar — [[d:paladin#1430343281568387082]] (server-dependent, contested).
- **[Ret]** AP-Ret crit target: 35% unbuffed ideal, 30% if that's not reachable, achieved via agi enchants across most slots — [[d:paladin#1430245594345246891]], [[d:paladin#1430245777753509888]] (Beldrin).
- **[Ret]** Str buff stacking: Kings +10%, Divine Strength +10%, Holy Might +20% are all separate multiplicative/additive str bonuses a raiding Ret should have — [[d:paladin#1430253653435875471]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Prot]** Holy Strike scales ~43% with SP baseline; roughly 980 SP is needed to double base Holy Strike damage via SP scaling alone — [[d:paladin#1430200316548223201]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot]** Typical BiS-adjacent SP totals: dedicated Prot "threat set" runs ~420-430 SP (buffed total cited as 832 SP, "non-UD"); a "T3 offensive setup" (T3 tank gear + caster off-pieces) runs ~390 SP base — [[d:paladin#1430197705304707163]], [[d:paladin#1430198981404659753]] (angels1m).
- **[Turtle itemization]** Holy set carries 8 less stamina than the Prot set; T3.5 Prot shoulders have less stamina than both the equivalent Ret and Holy shoulder pieces — [[d:paladin#1430519695026688092]] (angels1m, +2 reactions, repeated emphasis at [[d:paladin#1430188025081630865]]).
- **[Turtle itemization]** Ret's T2.5/T3.5 shoulders carry more stamina than Prot's equivalent pieces — [[d:paladin#1430187897511739563]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot]** Hit cap for Prot is 8%; no spell hit is needed — [[d:paladin#1430665092298571887]] (gastro), refining the earlier "6-7% is enough until Kara40" comment into a hard cap figure.
- **[Ret]** Concrete spell coefficients discussed: Judgement of Righteousness ~0.71 SP coefficient; Consecration ~0.66 SP coefficient for the entire cast (not per tick, unusually high vs. other classes' AoE DoTs which are typically ~33% spread over their duration, e.g. Rain of Fire/Magma Totem); Judgement of Command assumed (not fully confirmed) to keep its normal ~43% SP coefficient plus a newer +15% AP coefficient — [[d:paladin#1430667129673355365]], [[d:paladin#1430675394356314223]], [[d:paladin#1430675672992448713]], [[d:paladin#1430675790604800092]] (Not Normal/Mithreon/gastro).
- **[Ret]** Seal of Righteousness counts as a melee attack for proc purposes, and can proc Windfury Totem — confirmed mechanic — [[d:paladin#1430670825136066612]] (Mithreon). Seal of Righteousness appears to crit at the reduced ~1.5x "spell-like" multiplier rather than SoC's melee-like multiplier, which is part of why AP-Ret (using SoC) values crit highly — [[d:paladin#1430669415673761955]] (Mithreon, somewhat inferred from context).
- **[Ret]** Achievable haste stacking example for Ret: 15% from Zeal + 5% from T3.5 + 8% from enchants + 25% from the Scrolls trinket = 53%, not counting off-pieces — [[d:paladin#1431164067854422037]] (dusty).
- **[Ret]** Real logged Ashbringer proc damage: ~1500 average in BWL (several Shadow Vulnerability phases active, no Chromaggus), ~1300 average in MC without a Warlock present; Hand of Ragnaros procs on nearly every swing for roughly 350-375 each — the two camps disagreed sharply on how many HoR procs it takes to match one Ashbringer proc (Jesse Potter argued ~8, citing bigger Ashbringer numbers with debuffs; dusty's math from the raw proc sizes suggested the gap was much smaller) — [[d:paladin#1431160867017330738]], [[d:paladin#1431161277278851143]], [[d:paladin#1431159750204063784]], [[d:paladin#1431159824065892375]] (disputed, real numbers on both sides).
- **[Ret]** Damage-modifier stacking on procs: Priest Shadow Vulnerability +15%, Warlock Curse (of Elements/Shadow) +10%, Vengeance +15%, before counting a Nightfall proc — [[d:paladin#1431160055276896399]], [[d:paladin#1431160112113913879]] (Jesse Potter).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[Prot]** Keep the Soulforge (T0.5) 4-piece set bonus over swapping into T1 pieces; wear the T0.5 4-piece and fill remaining slots with T1 or other upgrades — [[d:paladin#1430343446945595563]], [[d:paladin#1430343621785288714]] (gastro).
- **[Prot]** MC/Ony trinket & ring picks for a fresh tank: Fabric of Time (one of the best trinkets available this phase), Force of Will (decent, comparable to a ~30 SP trinket like Briarwood Reed), Ring of Spellpower (MC) as an upgrade, keep Ring of Electrical Binding for spellstrike (drop once T2 3-piece is obtained), Onyxia Blood Talisman (defensive, ~2.84% avoidance), and Talisman of Ephemeral Power worth picking up — [[d:paladin#1430344155409813605]] through [[d:paladin#1430346300792115300]] (gastro); avoid Sizzling Pyrestone Aureole — it doesn't even function on Ragnaros — [[d:paladin#1430345671332073563]] (gastro).
- **[Prot]** Weapon: Blade of Infinite Mysteries (Black Morass reputation) is a better/decent replacement for Azuresong Mageblade due to faster weapon speed improving Judgement of Wisdom proc rate; prioritize other upgrades and get Mageblade last — [[d:paladin#1430347496961347727]], [[d:paladin#1430347662376435733]] (gastro).
- **[Prot]** Quel'Serrar is not worth picking up for stats as Prot — "stats are ass," good only for its transmog model — [[d:paladin#1430438918544818280]] (Destroses).
- **[Prot]** Onyxia Blood Talisman recommended Onyxia reward for a tank, ~2.84% avoidance — [[d:paladin#1430368588803477626]] (Acerious).
- **[Prot]** Bulwark of the Righteous best used situationally: as an opener if Holy Strike gets parried, on add-heavy phases (e.g. Garr adds), or on fights with stacking armor debuffs — rather than on cooldown — [[d:paladin#1430348761053532353]], [[d:paladin#1430350116103000136]], [[d:paladin#1430350721445924894]].
- **[Prot]** Soulforge (T0.5) questline: worth completing even though time-consuming (mainly the T0 grind), since BWL doesn't open for ~2 months, leaving time to finish it; on Ambershire it's a real gear upgrade, on Nordanaar it's essentially only for transmog — [[d:paladin#1430592436828242163]], [[d:paladin#1430593227299225601]], [[d:paladin#1430596585246687363]].
- **[Ret]** Spellstrike items: not strictly "needed," mainly ease mana management (baseline Judgement of Wisdom is otherwise sufficient); most players stop running spellstrike gear once around T2.5 — [[d:paladin#1430328767334846527]], [[d:paladin#1430328908670046289]] (gastro). Each spellstrike item procs once on auto-attack and once more on a Seal of Righteousness/Seal of Command hit; main purposes are proccing Judgement of Wisdom, the Whisper/Woe of Cthun (WoC) ring (once AQ40 is open), and faster Vengeance stack ramp — [[d:paladin#1430335169667141792]] (Destroses). Prot's T2 3-piece spellstrike effect does proc WoC — [[d:paladin#1430199405557977110]] (angels1m).
- **[Ret]** Kiss of the Spider (Queen) trinket called BiS for spellret: ~1% crit + 1% hit, 2 min cooldown — [[d:paladin#1430248290200457346]], [[d:paladin#1430249093443354624]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret]** Using Ashbringer, some raiders prefer MC boots (+5 weapon skill) over the T3.5 5-piece bonus (2% haste) to avoid losing crit; others keep a 4-piece T3 combo (glove/ring/leg + helm) over chasing T3.5 pieces — [[d:paladin#1430248185762287808]], [[d:paladin#1430251665940021330]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Bug/quirk]** T2 tier-token turn-in quest for tmog/vendoring only appears while you still hold the original T2 piece in your bag; once converted to Ret/Prot or vendored, it cannot be recovered even via the quest a second time — [[d:paladin#1430447123194974321]] (Fat Pibble with Air Pods), confirmed [[d:paladin#1430457071488466998]] (Olivaar of Moments).
- **[Bug/quirk]** T2 legs are supposed to drop from two separate loot groups (3 and 5) on Ragnaros, effectively doubling drop odds like for other classes, but the shared Paladin/Shaman tier loot pool appears to have been mis-split on some boss tables (Ragnaros, Chromaggus), leaving Paladin/Shaman T2 legs and gloves noticeably scarcer than other classes' pieces — extended multi-person thread, with reports of 3+ months without a T2 legs/gloves drop on Ambershire — [[d:paladin#1430609436879229132]], [[d:paladin#1430610302063481045]], [[d:paladin#1430611326765367438]], [[d:paladin#1430612529909862511]] (Arle/Dodging/Beldrin).
- **[Ret]** MC-phase gearing guidance for SP-Ret (spellret) beyond the tier set: Ragnaros neck, at least one Ring of Spell Power, Talisman of Ephemeral Power, and Sigil of Ancient Accord (not useful until BWL/T2 3-piece is available, but fine to grab early if uncontested) — [[d:paladin#1431263923427868685]], [[d:paladin#1431264126868525078]] (Beldrin). For weapon, Scythe (of the Harvest) can be swapped for Shadowstrike as an alternative spellret weapon — [[d:paladin#1431263631995306086]] (Mithreon).
- **[Ret]** MC-phase tier gearing split: AP-Ret should run the full Tier 1 set; SP-Ret (spellret) should wear the T0.5 4-piece bonus and fill the rest with Tier 1 — [[d:paladin#1431208658355748886]] (Destroses).

Full SP-oriented cloth-stat gear is not good for a hybrid Ret — [[d:paladin#1431379401915895889]] (Beldrin). Even in an optimized rotation, white hits + Crusader Strike still account for ~30% of total Ret damage, underscoring why seal uptime on autos matters — [[d:paladin#1431379684158869514]] (Beldrin).
- **[Ret]** Weapon notes: Hand of Ragnaros is BiS for both AP-Ret and Spellret at the MC/Ony phase (uncertain how Tempered Runeblade compares to Sulfuras) — [[d:paladin#1430660788296876144]], [[d:paladin#1430661229893910720]] (Atronen/gastro). For a hybrid Ret choosing between Sulfuras and Untamed Blade (UTB): Sulfuras is better if using Seal of Command, but UTB avoids needing to worry about fire-immune targets and can carry a Crusader enchant for a large AP gain — [[d:paladin#1431142880034029740]], [[d:paladin#1431142902637006962]], [[d:paladin#1431143069096607755]] (gastro/Destroses). UTB remains usable all the way up until Ashbringer regardless of seal choice — [[d:paladin#1431299869351612619]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Bug/quirk]** Tempered Runeblade previously had an unintended interaction with Seal of Command that produced ~2k crits; this was fixed ("today I learned they fixed Seal interaction with Runeblade... you won't get 2k crits with SoC now"), though the weapon is still considered BiS to use — [[d:paladin#1430895486633513010]], [[d:paladin#1430895536566960199]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[PvP gear]** Thick Obsidian Breastplate + Leysteel Legguards (+34 stam combined) beats a comparable 2-piece bonus (+20 stam) and adds magic mitigation, useful against multi-caster PvP teams; however, the breastplate's on-hit magic-absorb proc reportedly got weaker over time — it "used to be goated against casters... now barely procs from direct hits," implying it may now trigger mainly off DoT ticks rather than direct spell hits — [[d:paladin#1430806779666763849]], [[d:paladin#1430812970077655060]], [[d:paladin#1430815079854768170]] (Jesse Potter/Southshore Crusader Vidnar).
- **[Prot]** Dalaran reputation (Revered) rewards shoulders/belt/shield worth getting "if you need hit and can spare the gold"; alternative comparable items exist: shields from a Hyjal quest or Black Morass drop (both less stamina than the Dalaran shield), Soulforge (T0.5) is the closest comparable shoulder alternative, and Karazhan Crypt (Turtle-specific dungeon) drops a comparable belt — [[d:paladin#1430881473304727593]], [[d:paladin#1430887408702062652]] (Jesse Potter/Destroses).
- **[Prot]** T1 8-piece set bonus for Prot is considered extremely strong ("absolutely nuts") — worth dropping partial T2 pieces (e.g. T2 pants) to complete it; the T1 2-piece bonus (available on the DPS-stat glove variant) increases Judgement of Righteousness damage, a direct threat gain — [[d:paladin#1430862493483532328]], [[d:paladin#1430862822375559198]], [[d:paladin#1430862255716565072]] (Destroses/angels1m). Holy damage (e.g. from Seal of Righteousness) counts toward certain "spell" interactions and, being Holy-school, gives roughly 200 extra threat per swing under Righteous Fury — [[d:paladin#1430877169797828721]] (Destroses).
- **[Prot]** Sigil of Ancient Accord procs reliably only from the Prot T2 3-piece spellstrike bonus; the T1 8-piece set can also proc it but less reliably — [[d:paladin#1430876352621580370]], [[d:paladin#1430953771600449556]], [[d:paladin#1431454922980986900]] (Destroses/Jesse Potter/Supal). Both a T2 3-piece Ret set and a T2 3-piece Prot set can be worn simultaneously as independent spellstrike sources (they stack), which is required if you want strong uptime on the Sigil proc — [[d:paladin#1430953855981453424]], [[d:paladin#1430954577242492938]] (Jesse Potter).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Prot]** Structured threat-kit critique (Laigeer): Holy Strike is the only ability that meaningfully generates threat; Crusader Strike's physical damage doesn't scale Righteous Fury threat; Strength is a useless stat for Block Value; autoattack generates ~120 threat baseline; Exorcism (15s CD) generates ~300 threat; Consecration's threat is below a Mage's/Warlock's AoE and it also applies the Salvation debuff to the raid; gear scaling from T2 to T3.5 is only 10-15% better — concludes Prot Paladin threat design needs a full overhaul to be raid-competitive with other tanks — [[d:paladin#1430212267978395809]] (single detailed post, echoed by angels1m's independent math [[d:paladin#1430212099887595701]] and [[d:paladin#1430216919826305174]]: "Holy Strike and JoR are your 90% threat combo").
- **[Prot]** Improved Righteous Fury currently only boosts holy-damage-derived threat; a community proposal (unconfirmed) would change it to a flat +10/20/30% threat bonus to all damage types — [[d:paladin#1430213769706995764]], [[d:paladin#1430249067442733227]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[Prot]** angels1m soloed MC as Prot using only "Zanza" gear; BWL tanking is comfortable because taunt is rarely resisted there and there's no Holy resistance on mobs — [[d:paladin#1430195595116679259]], [[d:paladin#1430195829926531236]].
- **[Prot]** Shield Block Value (SBV) is buildable to ~480 in T3 gear, blocking ~25% of Mephistroth's (Kara40) melee damage — [[d:paladin#1430253614105886810]] (Excinerus); Righteous Strikes' SBV bonus is otherwise considered weak ("super bad") — [[d:paladin#1430252546311852292]] (Seviry).
- **[Open question]** A fresh Prot off-tank asked for baseline MC-appropriate stat targets (reported unbuffed: 290 stam / 6422 armor / 331 defense / 63 fire resist, still struggling on Flame Elementals without fire protection potions) — no consensus answer given in this range — [[d:paladin#1430522845779460178]] (Lazepally; question unanswered).
- **[Prot]** Black Morass (BM) is unusually punishing for Prot tanks: dragonkin plus packs of 4 elementals at the start hit very hard, and even AQ40-geared Prot paladins still take heavy damage there; direct comparison to Druid tanks isn't fair since Druid is considered overperforming/"busted" at the time — [[d:paladin#1430718036658098256]], [[d:paladin#1430721409319043074]], [[d:paladin#1430721503862718575]] (angels1m/Destroses). Reported example gearing/talents for a struggling Prot in BM: 4821 HP unbuffed, Str taken in the Holy tree for extra Block Value, 5 points Righteous Strikes / 3 points Reckoning, ~7.9k armor with 5/5 Toughness (~7.1k baseline) — [[d:paladin#1430720688884416633]], [[d:paladin#1430720968430583849]], [[d:paladin#1430721729948291072]] (wasik/angels1m). How you pull matters: running straight into a BM pack can let 3-4 mobs land hits simultaneously, whereas pulling with a ranged ability (Judgement/dynamite) staggers the incoming hits — [[d:paladin#1430722952864858274]], [[d:paladin#1430723057223471244]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot]** Holy Shield's block does not apply to spell-type "autoattacks" from elemental-type mobs (e.g. fire/frost auto-attacks), a real current mechanic limitation raised repeatedly as a pain point for tanking elementals — [[d:paladin#1431196478231023677]], [[d:paladin#1431196889595777039]] (Destroses).
- **[Prot - Kara40]** On the Kruul encounter, tanks can come close to (~25%) losing threat on the Infernal adds because Holy Strike gets parried and Taunt gets resisted; Infernal adds tend to "snap" onto the nearest Priest healer like a magnet, requiring the tank to re-taunt plus Exorcism/Judgement/Holy Strike to hold it, and AoE healing (e.g. Prayer of Healing/Holy Prayer) from a Priest standing elsewhere can pull the Infernal back off the tank — [[d:paladin#1431390507275124887]], [[d:paladin#1431391328301879366]] (angels1m/Excinerus).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Ironclad's healing bonus scales only off a paladin's own base armor value, not from swapping to higher-armor-per-slot cloth items — [[d:paladin#1430310601447444510]] (angels1m), which effectively answers whether cloth gear can help Holy healing via Ironclad (it mostly can't beyond base armor). Toughness talent does NOT affect Ironclad — confirmed twice — [[d:paladin#1430305996810489997]], [[d:paladin#1430313618141876476]] (gastro).
- **[Holy]** At Holy BiS gear, Holy Light can only be cast roughly 20 times before going OOM — mana remains a hard constraint at endgame, making Holy Light function mostly as an "emergency heal" rather than a primary spam spell — [[d:paladin#1430556664322588823]], [[d:paladin#1430475146107551906]] (Mithreon).
- **[Holy]** Full-rank Flash of Light spam causes mana problems for under-geared Holy paladins even in current MC-level progression — [[d:paladin#1430485796451909642]] (Mithreon).
- **[Holy]** Current Holy tier set bonuses (T1 through T3.5) are broadly considered weak/uninspired by raiders discussing them — [[d:paladin#1430473606378557590]] (Mithreon).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Kara40 / Tower of Karazhan]** Trash near the Medivh-area slopes includes large elementals with a stacking group-wide damage-over-time aura; pulling onto the slope can put melee outside line-of-sight of ranged/healers, forcing a choice between healers moving into melee range to heal, or melee dragging the pull back down — cited as a good/bad map-design example — [[d:paladin#1430109804713480193]], [[d:paladin#1430109977938493450]] (Mithreon).
- **[Kara40]** Holy Shield provides 8 charges; threat is manageable in a min-max-heavy raid even for a less-optimized race choice — [[d:paladin#1430187294886727750]] (Excinerus).
- **[Kara40 - Mephistroth]** Healing this fight is demanding: Flash of Light alone falls behind during "Meph zerg" burst phases; a Holy Light + Scrolls setup was tried but still insufficient for some healers — [[d:paladin#1430474819350298715]], [[d:paladin#1430474891177758760]] (Mithreon).
- **[Raid-wide]** Naxxramas is considered one of the best raids for Prot paladin performance — [[d:paladin#1430195088046297341]] (angels1m).
- **[Kara40 / Tower of Karazhan boss overview]** Community summary of Kara40 bosses: Incantagos can be cheesed with a Rogue or Hunter pull; Anomalus can be parry-hasted "from the ass" (from behind, an unintended angle); Medivh devolves into a zerg-down and requires waiting out (or relogging through) a ~4-minute curse afterward; the Chess event has janky line-of-sight; Sanv(il) is essentially a target dummy; Rupturan is well-liked (~7/10), especially with a "heart" strategy; Kruul and Mephistroth (Meph) are both considered "jank" fights — [[d:paladin#1431396800283279580]], [[d:paladin#1431397755980615721]] (Mithreon).
- **[Kara40 - Chess event]** Line-of-sight bug: standing zoomed-out near one particular pillar on the right side of the room can have your view blocked by a wall decoration, causing the "knight" piece to visually disappear; simply stepping slightly onto the pillar and getting elevated ~1 inch can also trigger it — [[d:paladin#1431397229041684530]], [[d:paladin#1431397339293024266]], [[d:paladin#1431397345907445780]], [[d:paladin#1431398509038538844]] (Sylvaira/Excinerus/Mithreon).
- **[AQ40 - Twin Emperors]** With an automated CS/HS macro maintaining Might/Zeal buffs, a Ret/hybrid player can focus purely on target-switch timing during the boss swap, leaving only ~4 seconds "in transit" between the two bosses, using that window to drop a fresh Consecration and Repentance before switching — [[d:paladin#1431390436940972043]] (Excinerus).

## PvP
- **[Ret/Holy]** Paladins currently have no reliable "peel" ability comparable to Warrior's Intimidating Shout, Priest's Psychic Scream+dispel, or Warlock's Fear/Coil — flagged as a class-design gap versus other classes — [[d:paladin#1430426233111445606]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Holy]** Repentance is frequently neutralized in organized PvP: a "FAP" (an immunity/poison food-debuff item) on an enemy flag carrier plus standard peel leaves a Holy paladin with only auto-attack/Crusader Strike, hoping for a delay proc — [[d:paladin#1430425657262735422]] (Jesse Potter). Repentance previously could delay a FAP'd target 6 seconds but "now does nothing," implying a mechanics change reduced its PvP value against immune targets — [[d:paladin#1430425762976235651]].
- **[PvP gear]** The 2-piece PvP set bonus (R8 chest + R7 boots) is called "pretty nice" but costs the Agility bonus otherwise gained from Soulforge boots — inconclusive value trade-off — [[d:paladin#1430425700782837832]], [[d:paladin#1430425747260047432]] (Sock).

## Macros, addons, UI
- LibramSwap requires the Nampower client-side plugin to function correctly for Paladins, and needs a hook into the "cast" command to remain compatible with other cast-macro addons (e.g. SuperDuperMacro) — [[d:paladin#1430128271672082575]] (Agamemnoth), [[d:paladin#1430124873551712256]] (Jesse Potter). LazyScript can auto-cast spells from the spellbook to sidestep manual libram-swap macro writing — [[d:paladin#1430124667653591130]] (Dzonathan).
- PfUI's automatic bag rescanning conflicts with libram/gear-swap macros, causing UI/screen freezes, especially with large (36-slot) bags full of gear; can be mitigated by disabling PfUI's bag component, but that also requires disabling action bars (which trigger their own reagent rescans) — [[d:paladin#1430128713118388234]], [[d:paladin#1430129136361541742]], [[d:paladin#1430129213519953940]] (Pastor/Destroses).
- QuickHeal (QH) reportedly does not work properly for Paladins specifically, worsening at least one healer's output; Puppeteer is used instead — [[d:paladin#1430092362717331568]], [[d:paladin#1430092585028030647]] (Mithreon/dusty).
- Example macros:
  - Libram-swap + Crusader Strike: `/run PaladinCrusaderStrike()` then `/run CastEquipByName("Crusader Strike","Libram of the Eternal Tower")` — [[d:paladin#1430124380490301580]] (Jesse Potter).
  - Conditional Judgement + libram swap: `#showtooltip Judgement` / `/use [@target,hp:<35] Libram of Final Judgement` / `/run CastSpellByName("Judgement")` — [[d:paladin#1430125534729474100]] (Jesse Potter).
  - Seal of Righteousness/Judgement auto-swap macro (buggy): loops `UnitBuff` looking for a "ThunderBolt" buff to decide whether to cast Judgement or Seal of Righteousness; reported to sometimes fail to fire the Judgement branch, requiring a manual click — [[d:paladin#1430185950805360803]] (Don; question/bug, no fix given).
- SoC procs display as white damage text using the Crusader Strike animation (spinning for Dwarves, downward slash for Humans) — useful for visually confirming procs — [[d:paladin#1430276466893000795]], [[d:paladin#1430280153744806099]] (Dodging/Beldrin).
- DPSMate gives more in-depth combat stats than the default Shagu meter bundled with the launcher; running a threat meter alongside a DPS meter is considered essential — [[d:paladin#1430282353602723860]], [[d:paladin#1430281555506626661]] (Dodging).
- UI stack alternative to full PfUI: Luna Unitframes + ShaguPlates (PfUI's nameplates as a standalone addon) for players who only want frames/nameplates — [[d:paladin#1430282879966908526]] (Beldrin); SuperWoW is called foundational, required for accurate nameplate cast bars — [[d:paladin#1430283361779191878]], [[d:paladin#1430283397992943668]].
- Performance in Kara40: disabling MSBT (Mik's Scrolling Battle Text) and non-essential visual effects (friendly spell effects/characters), plus the "perf_boost" addon, meaningfully fixed lag for a raider who nearly gave up on the instance — [[d:paladin#1430277142553428010]] through [[d:paladin#1430277957846569031]] (Beldrin).
- Ret rotation automation: ExAutoCSHS (github.com/Excinerus/ExAutoCSHS) plus ActionBarProfiles (github.com/Siventt/ActionBarProfiles) can auto-select Crusader Strike vs Holy Strike based on current Might/Zeal buff state and detected weapon type (1H/2H) — see Rotation: single target section for the macro syntax and logic — [[d:paladin#1431383481547493517]], [[d:paladin#1431388191000100945]] (Excinerus).
- Binding several different spells (e.g. multiple seals plus Judgement) to one macro button is discouraged — it caused over 20% ability downtime in logs for raiders who fumbled the seal swap and ate a GCD on the wrong seal — [[d:paladin#1431384577359745167]], [[d:paladin#1431384873993633792]] (Excinerus/Beldrin).

- T2 tier-piece conversion is irreversible; the tmog/vendor-recovery quest only shows up while the original T2 item is still in your bag — [[d:paladin#1430447123194974321]], [[d:paladin#1430457071488466998]].
- T2 legs/gloves drop-pool appears mis-split between Paladin and Shaman on some boss tables (Ragnaros, Chromaggus), causing much scarcer drops for these two classes than others on at least one realm — [[d:paladin#1430609436879229132]] through [[d:paladin#1430612529909862511]].
- Sizzling Pyrestone Aureole trinket does not function against Ragnaros — [[d:paladin#1430345671332073563]] (gastro).
- Repentance no longer delays a FAP-immune flag carrier the way it once did — [[d:paladin#1430425762976235651]] (single source, exact cause/patch not specified).
- Tempered Runeblade's unintended Seal of Command interaction (producing ~2k crits) was fixed — see Gear section for details — [[d:paladin#1430895486633513010]].
- Holy Shield's block does not apply against spell-type auto-attacks from elemental mobs (fire/frost "auto-attacks") — see Tanking specifics — [[d:paladin#1431196478231023677]].
- Vindication does not function against boss-type targets (PvE), making it PvP-only — [[d:paladin#1431510211994058812]] (Destroses).
- Bosses can apparently be parried by totems/pets standing behind them, and this "parry from behind" can inadvertently increase boss parry-haste procs beyond intended — [[d:paladin#1431196918817624075]], [[d:paladin#1431197039298871387]], [[d:paladin#1431197356019286017]] (Mithreon/Jesse Potter).
- Kara40 Chess event line-of-sight/camera bug causing the "knight" piece to visually vanish near one pillar — see Dungeons & raids — [[d:paladin#1431397229041684530]].
- There is currently no token-exchange system for T1/T2 tier pieces (unlike T2.5/T3, which do have exchanges), and T3.5 can only be obtained as a single, non-repeatable set with no way to re-acquire or swap it if you respec — flagged as a longevity/flexibility gap for hybrids who want to gear multiple specs — [[d:paladin#1431114143590518867]] (dusty).
