## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Prot]** Holy Strike threat/scaling was deliberately reworked this patch: base value nerfed but scaling with high-end gear boosted, "so we don't see fresh 60 pala doing 80% of threat from bis prot" — [[d:paladin#1485348164021256412]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot]** Holy Strike now factors weapon damage/AP (~50% weapon damage) into its damage via a weapon-normalization term; formula confirmed as `base_weapon_damage + (AP * weapon_speed / 14)` (not the WoD-era `/3.5` someone initially proposed) — [[d:paladin#1485861509094445066]], [[d:paladin#1485864767343034579]] (Proph confirms Grey's math), [[d:paladin#1485866571594465280]]. Consensus (gastro, Grey): impact is real but overhyped — spellpower remains the dominant prot threat stat by a wide margin — [[d:paladin#1485887132974190693]], [[d:paladin#1485860266041278584]].
- **[Prot]** Threat overall ~10% better than pre-patch at high-end gear per Proph's model; break-even point for spellpower value is ~100 SP — [[d:paladin#1485350693312397362]], [[d:paladin#1485352455314083934]].
- **[Bug at launch, fixed same/next day]** Holy Strike/Mending Light missing baseline 5% spellpower scaling, and not healing on miss/dodge/parry — widely reported [[d:paladin#1485234627260977275]] (Athurian), [[d:paladin#1485376665772228618]] (Mithreon), confirmed by Lhian as scheduled for fix [[d:paladin#1485213480679243856]] (+3 reactions), fix confirmed client-side same day but needing a reset [[d:paladin#1485348941204815894]], celebrated as fixed [[d:paladin#1485645164494983330]] ("Hell yeah HS fixed").
- **[Healing math example, post-bug]** Holy Strike (Mending Light) healed 608 on an ally: `(1477*0.25+180)*1.1 ≈ 604`; with full 30% scaling (vs. the bugged missing 5%) it would have healed 685 — i.e. ~15% under-healing from the bug — [[d:paladin#1485634378028810431]], [[d:paladin#1485634518613229599]], [[d:paladin#1485634615531143270]] (Mithreon/Grey).
- **[Holy]** Daybreak (talent/mechanic interacting with Holy Strike healing) was intentionally nerfed this patch as "too strong for vanilla"; separate from the miss/dodge/parry bug — [[d:paladin#1485588954072875130]] (K24), [[d:paladin#1485599978544959528]] (Silverhand).
- **[Bug, root-caused and fixed]** Block Value calculation was inverted for auto-attacks: if an incoming hit exceeded your block value, you took a hit equal to your BV and only "blocked" the excess, instead of blocking the BV and taking the remainder — net effect was MORE block value could mean MORE damage taken on big hits; only affected auto-attacks, not abilities — [[d:paladin#1485255752011747458]] (Debo), [[d:paladin#1485390532489511123]] (Grey's clean writeup used for the bug ticket), confirmed by multiple testers [[d:paladin#1485401846699921459]] (Marfusia). Bug ticket #20881 filed, dev initially couldn't reproduce [[d:paladin#1485389771776987346]]. Lhian personally asked "is this true?" and it was confirmed and root-caused to the Zealous Defense talent, then fixed — [[d:paladin#1485626782563434536]], [[d:paladin#1485629739359014953]], [[d:paladin#1485647863806693447]] (dev: "blame zealous defence"), [[d:paladin#1485647701126152293]] ("its fixed").
- **[Prot]** Consecration reworked: frontloads damage to the early ticks (first tick ~600 threat, second ~475 in Grey's test) without changing tick rate or duration; damage-on-cast (vs. frontload-of-existing-damage) requires the new Consecration Libram — [[d:paladin#1485368911712092332]], [[d:paladin#1485639427173781704]], [[d:paladin#1485639478210072738]]. Frontloading applies per-mob starting from each mob's own first hit taken, which made pre-placing Consecration on incoming adds (e.g. Gnarlmoon ravens) less reliable — a de facto nerf for that playstyle — [[d:paladin#1485639689175302266]], [[d:paladin#1485639188547239946]] (Excinerus).
- **[Bug]** Mending Light's baseline 5% spellpower scaling reportedly missing/changed; disputed whether intentional — [[d:paladin#1485156480305860801]] (Mithreon).
- **[Bug]** Righteous Fury threat "snapshots" the talent build active when it was cast; after a mid-session talent respec, toggling RF off and back on is required to re-apply new talent values — [[d:paladin#1485349101628424263]] (Proph), fix confirmed pending a reset [[d:paladin#1485348941204815894]].
- **[Fortune trinket / set procs, unresolved]** Community reports the Fortune trinket (proc-chance-increase item) does not affect set-bonus procs like "the Lost" 5-piece; devs (Lhian/Dragunov) had not confirmed intent as of this chunk — [[d:paladin#1485252329774256350]], [[d:paladin#1485293591331864696]] (single source, question unanswered).
- **[Weapon tier shakeup]** Desecration and Wraith Blade received a "white DPS" (weapon damage) upgrade this patch; End of Dreams did not, shifting the relative rankings between Blade of Eternal Justice/EoD/Desecration/Wraith Blade for prot threat — [[d:paladin#1485395698688983090]] (theoloras/warkrak), disputed math below.
- **[Bear/Druid tank]** Warrior... err Bear (Druid) Maul threat coefficient reduced ~15% per patch notes, but players tested closer to a ~50% real-world reduction — flagged as possibly an additional bug on top of the intended nerf — [[d:paladin#1485357995968692386]], [[d:paladin#1485358238403657749]] (Dziadmroz/Mithreon), used as context for why prot paladin threat now looks comparatively very strong.
- **[New content]** A new boss was added inside Onyxia's Lair this patch (referred to as "the new boss in Ony"), dropping a shield comparable to BWL's Elementium Shield (plus a fire-spell-reflect proc, ~3 less defense, ~1 less block value, ~40 less armor than the BWL item) — [[d:paladin#1485798984277692416]], [[d:paladin#1485799233071091863]] (Silverhand) — plus new trinkets/goggles/libram/ring drops (see Gear and Cooldowns sections).
- **[Bug/design change, unresolved]** Holy Strike's healing (Mending Light) apparently lost its "smart" injured-target-priority targeting: multiple testers report it healing full-HP targets while a badly-injured ally standing right next to them is ignored — reported to devs as bug #21117 by Mithreon — [[d:paladin#1486270263120101437]]; concrete anecdote of a K10 raider watching Mending Light fully overheal a topped-off ally while a 70%-HP tank nearby didn't move at all — [[d:paladin#1486371961331257454]]. Dev (Lhian, via Mithreon) reportedly confirmed the *complete* random-targeting behavior "shouldn't behave that way" and a fix is pending (either restoring old "check for damage, then lowest HP" logic, or a simpler "just check for damage" fix) — [[d:paladin#1486372378257920020]], [[d:paladin#1486372599004008579]] — but by the end of the chunk (2026-03-26) the community still wasn't sure whether the non-prioritizing behavior itself was fixed, was intentional, or both — [[d:paladin#1486666859435790397]], [[d:paladin#1486669085952708649]]. Real numbers (Infertile, same raid group before/after): pre-patch 49 Mending Light casts → 166 heal instances; post-patch 36 casts → 72 heal instances — [[d:paladin#1486480416746766368]]. Grey's raid-log comparison for one holy paladin: Flash of Light healing rose 200k→275k while Mending Light collapsed 80k→29k, with roughly flat total effective healing (424k→432k) because FoL absorbed the difference — [[d:paladin#1486480151138275421]]. Related, likely intentional design direction: Chain Heal and Lightwell (other classes) also lost "smart" injured-priority targeting this patch, suggesting a broader shift toward "no smart heals" for AoE kits other than Holy Priest's — [[d:paladin#1486490033715286037]], [[d:paladin#1486490142624579694]] (Mithreon).
- **[Design commentary]** Mithreon: "pally was never meant to be an AoE healer, we were bound to be slapped down" regarding paladin AoE-healing nerfs coinciding with Holy Priest buffs — [[d:paladin#1486637274694418523]]; a brief period existed where Holy Strike/Mending Light could hit 6-7 targets for very low cost, which the community itself agreed "was never going to stick around long term" and got hotfixed away — [[d:paladin#1486636512279007283]], [[d:paladin#1486637153189625916]], [[d:paladin#1486664089022365737]].
- **[Confirmed non-bug]** The recurring "Ironclad talent isn't working" reports were traced definitively to out-of-date healing/character-stat addons (e.g. BetterCharacterStats) not recognizing the reworked talent — "Ironclad was never bugged. Update your addons." — [[d:paladin#1486480191315382303]] (Grey), reconfirming earlier addon-based explanations.
- **[Prot]** Daybreak (a crit-related value tied to Ironclad/healing) was nerfed by roughly 25-30% this patch (one player observed 800→600, or 25%; another separately saw a further drop from the T3.5 fix) — [[d:paladin#1486635309947949197]], [[d:paladin#1486635643768541195]] (Mithreon/Mike). K40 overall healing output calculated at roughly -10% after the patch, in line with the T3.5 and Daybreak changes combined — [[d:paladin#1486468604290535595]].
- **[Bug, hotfixed]** A leveling-dungeon 2H "gun-axe" item was found to have a broken proc mechanic: repeatedly hitting a target dummy for ~15 minutes would "charge" the weapon so its proc chained dozens of times per swing (up to ~500 procs/minute observed), eventually pushing solo dummy DPS to ~2500+ and remaining "charged" until the weapon was unequipped or the player logged out — [[d:paladin#1486169134768324718]], [[d:paladin#1486169243425837238]], [[d:paladin#1486169357716422676]]. It reportedly let one Kara10 pug paladin (using it plus Hand of Justice and a BM rep cloak) out-threat a Thunderfury warrior tank — [[d:paladin#1486167771065749574]] — and a PvP clip showed it one-shotting players for ~50% HP per hit — [[d:paladin#1486566627158786080]]. Devs added a 3-second ICD ~4 days after launch ("RIP Gunadin") — [[d:paladin#1486557442228617336]], [[d:paladin#1486558708296192140]]; Lhian's stated design rationale: "vanilla needs to have a certain gear progression - if an item from a leveling dungeon you can get in 20 minutes eclipses best weapons in game in performance with the right setup, that's a problem to address" — [[d:paladin#1486160532271534192]].
- **[Design tension, ongoing]** The paladin T2 3-piece set bonus (procs off any "damaging spell," including auto-attacks/seals coded as spells) is considered by several regulars and at least implicitly by devs to be over-strong/design-limiting, since it lets paladins proc caster-only trinkets/items far more often than their intended audience; Mithreon predicts "there will be no T2 3p after BWL phase anymore, 90% certainty" — [[d:paladin#1486091710462431396]] — while Lhian has so far resisted changing it: "i really dont want to, it enables so much stuff" — [[d:paladin#1486109344486653954]]. It's specifically flagged as important for Ret/TA-server viability (dumbid) but nearly irrelevant for Prot (see Tanking/Stats sections for why) — [[d:paladin#1486093685526495502]], [[d:paladin#1486116260642296082]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Prot]** "Paladin right now [is] best single target and best AoE tank" this patch per raid testing across specs — [[d:paladin#1485372752398385223]] (Dziadmroz), echoed by Proph's ~10% threat-improvement estimate [[d:paladin#1485350693312397362]].
- **[Prot]** Vengeance ("Vengprot") tanking is viable again: mana-refund-driven build adds meaningful threat and works well "if the raid is going fast enough to maintain stacks"; strong in late-game (Naxx) gear, weaker in fresh/lower gear — [[d:paladin#1485665910827257856]], [[d:paladin#1485666100623573242]], [[d:paladin#1485666314675552397]] (Dodging/angels1m). One prot paladin uses Vengeance situationally in Kara10/40 and for almost all of Naxx, doing near-top DPS on trash/some bosses while still MT'ing — [[d:paladin#1485666884774006914]]. Called "a very good 3rd/4th tank build" by Seviry — [[d:paladin#1485755924101468272]], though MetalDude counters 3rd/4th tanks never get loot priority (contested/joking) — [[d:paladin#1485756364298129498]].
- **[Ret]** Necrodin (pet/undead-summon Ret build) is well regarded post-patch: "Necro... is the best thing that happened to ret in a while," credited partly to dev Lhian fighting to keep it — [[d:paladin#1485126252288409610]] (Adamant/dusty). A geared banana-realm (TA) necrodin reportedly outperformed everyone despite missing ~20% haste/crit/AP vs. the other realm — [[d:paladin#1485524647926825071]], [[d:paladin#1485526040783880334]] (Excinerus/Destroses). Some players find necrodin "more braindead than normal ret play" and prefer normal Ret specs if damage were equal (Zaberin, contested) — [[d:paladin#1485526121436155954]].
- **[Ret]** Necrodin drawbacks: needs to sacrifice nearly every item slot (including both trinkets) to be worth it, is less tanky than prot, and burns mana fast using Holy Shock/Crusader Strike (unlike prot, which doesn't run OOM); the summoned ghost/Sigil pet breaks crowd control — [[d:paladin#1485748395241898215]], [[d:paladin#1485748549277843657]] (Pastor).
- **[Prot]** Necrodin/pet builds cannot function as a tank build: "you don't get your pet's threat" (though you do get their DPS), so necrodin "will never be a tank build until the Paladin gets the threat of the pets" — [[d:paladin#1485123737551507476]], [[d:paladin#1485124493843365981]], [[d:paladin#1485124629520580658]] (dusty).
- **[Prot]** Holyprot (hybrid) considered unnecessary for endgame dungeon tanking (Strat/Scholo) if a dedicated healer is available — "just use a regular build" (single-source opinion, question left open by asker Vexx) — [[d:paladin#1485318742354428096]], [[d:paladin#1485319076220895514]].
- **[Holy]** Shockadin ("Holy Shock" spec) is reported threat-capped in MC/BWL by at least one player even with competent tanks — open/contested issue, no resolution in this chunk — [[d:paladin#1485668370882756879]] (Protoman).
- **[Ret]** SoC/AP-Ret is described as "so back" this patch, with a near-BiS SoC ret doing "very respectable damage" per Proph's log review — [[d:paladin#1485468559441137884]], [[d:paladin#1485832835901161472]].
- **[Ret, SoR vs SoC]** By 2026-03-26, community sentiment shifted toward "a real choice between SoR and SoC now" thanks to the new Libram of Ardour (+1 SoC ppm): controlled dummy testing by Aljeron (same pre-K40-BiS spell-ret gear: CAB, WoC, SoAA; only the seal/libram changed) found SoC ~30 dps ahead of SoR with only self-buffs (Imp BoM, Sanctity Aura) applied — [[d:paladin#1486723603952046090]], [[d:paladin#1486545504736379091]], [[d:paladin#1486546803678642188]]. Caveats: SoC is "swingy"/feast-or-famine on short fights and needs a 10-30+ minute sample to be reliable — [[d:paladin#1486548663042838548]], [[d:paladin#1486549091398975588]]; whether raid buffs/debuffs tip it back to SoR was untested as of this chunk. A top SoR-ret DPS on Nordanaar switched to SoC and "enjoys it very much" — [[d:paladin#1486547634943557735]], [[d:paladin#1486556685073125578]] — but skeptics note SoR itself is highly RNG-dependent (WFT/weapon procs) and that regulars may simply prefer SoR builds out of habit — [[d:paladin#1486551302174539826]], [[d:paladin#1486552240289484840]]. Simple seal-choice rule of thumb (Grey): Seal of the Crusader is only ever *judged* for its flat +130ish SP bonus; if you stack spellpower gear run SoR, if you stack strength gear run SoC — [[d:paladin#1486727985540104303]].

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[Ret/"Spellret"]** Glass-cannon Consecration/SP hybrid build (Olivaar of Moments), full breakdown: Holy — Divine Strength 5/5, Improved Seal of Righteousness 5/5, Sanctity Aura 1/1; Protection — Devotion Aura or Redoubt 5/5, Precision 3/3; Retribution — Benediction 5/5, Improved Judgement 2/2, Improved Seal of the Crusader 3/3, Conviction 5/5, Blessing of Kings 1/1, Pursuit of Justice 2/2, Two-Handed Specialization 3/3, Vengeance 5/5, Vengeful Strikes 5/5, Repentance 1/1 — [[d:paladin#1485415655368032338]]. Grey confirms "same as spellret but with 1 point in SoC," and notes Lightbourne has recommended dropping the Protection tree entirely if you can't free up enough hit gear to reach the hit cap — [[d:paladin#1485478074979188846]].
- **[Holy]** No single agreed baseline holy spec exists ("grab everything in holy that looks remotely good, then pick up support from prot/ret"); key points always taken: 31 points into Holy for Blessed Strikes and Holy Shock, plus Improved Blessings, Guardian's Favor, and Kings — [[d:paladin#1485373897602896044]], [[d:paladin#1485374258082218037]] (Grey).
- **[Prot/Vengeance]** Two Vengeance-prot talent-calculator links shared by angels1m: "deal damage when not tanking" build — https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin?points=-AoYEYYCAB-AoQgFIYAFAF ; "maximise threat" build — https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin?points=-AoYEYYCAZIAI-AoQgFIYAF. Choice framed as "either one point in Righteous [Defense] + 5 Vengeance, or 5 Crusader Strike and no Righteous Defense" — [[d:paladin#1485667467706765485]], [[d:paladin#1485667215473643570]].
- **[Prot tanking talents]** Toughness: usually take at least 2/5 to complete the talent row, more if points are left over — [[d:paladin#1485419807129079858]] (Olivaar). Sanctuary: skip if another party/raid member covers threat reduction; it's not as strong as Kings on single-target boss fights — [[d:paladin#1485419583195058176]], [[d:paladin#1485419631580811325]]. Reckoning: 0 points means the effect never triggers at all; beyond 1 point it's a "spend leftover points" talent — [[d:paladin#1485420564549074994]], [[d:paladin#1485420656140091402]]. Some prefer 2/5 Toughness + 3/3 Anticipation instead of the max-Toughness route — [[d:paladin#1485468999541325916]] (Grey).
- **[Prot]** With the strength-focused holy talent (raid buffs now give notable AP/strength via Holy Strike's weapon-damage component), taking the 10% Strength talent in the Holy tree may now beat the 10% Int talent for prot — [[d:paladin#1485377870724464854]] (dumbid); Mithreon floats the same logic possibly applying to Ret — [[d:paladin#1485355282430427276]] (speculative, unresolved).
- **[Prot]** T3.5 5/5 set-bonus users can drop to 4/8 T3 since extra hit on the shoulders offsets the lost hit from the belt, and the resulting 3% damage reduction is arguably better than the stamina given up — [[d:paladin#1485668902527697067]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot MT "cookie cutter" build]** Grey's baseline MT talent build (points can be moved around): running the 10% Strength talent over 10% Int gives slightly higher Holy Strike+Auto threat but worse mana recovery; Blessing/Improved Sanctuary can be dropped for a point in Guardian's Favor, Anticipation, or Improved Hand of Reckoning; points in Toughness can move to the same talents; 2 points can move off Reckoning into those talents or into Improved Lay on Hands — this generally works best for most players; off-tanks may do better running Vengprot — [[d:paladin#1486685580652712007]]. Counter-opinion: Arle argues Improved Retribution Aura (10% extra JoR/SoR damage) shouldn't be dismissed as "far more valuable talents exist elsewhere" — it costs a lot of points and assumes a party member can bring Sanctity Aura instead — [[d:paladin#1486699962929778729]].
- **[Prot]** For AoE-heavy fights, Sanctity Aura only overtakes Improved Retribution Aura once you have roughly 1300 spellpower (not counting Ret Aura's own 3% SP scaling) — [[d:paladin#1486673767294963723]], [[d:paladin#1486673875637899295]] (Destroses). Every SoR-ret running with a prot paladin should ask that paladin to take Improved Retribution Aura; SoC-ret is exempt from this advice since the aura is less convenient for them to pick up — [[d:paladin#1486681067686461552]], [[d:paladin#1486681212117586012]] (Grey).
- **[Holy-Prot solo/leveling build]** dumbid shared a talent-calculator link for a Holy-Prot hybrid aimed at easy solo dungeon farming/leveling: https://www.talent-builder.dev/collections/1.18.1/paladin?points=AoaAYCFAhAAo-AoYFBbAABA- — "get the reset [talent] and Holy Shield and you're golden" — [[d:paladin#1486140396848549939]]. For Hardcore-mode leveling, going full Prot is recommended over a Holyprot AoE-farming build, since HC can't tolerate an AoE-pull mistake — [[d:paladin#1486432542772039881]] (Silverhand); for HC leveling generally, taking Spiritual Focus (pushback resistance) is advised regardless of spec — [[d:paladin#1486417287945785534]] (Magik). Paladin is recommended over warrior for "slow and steady" HC leveling — [[d:paladin#1486415393919598804]] (gastro).

## Leveling
- **[Prot]** Leveling as Prot is viable starting as early as level 10 with no regrets, described as fun for soloing quests otherwise too hard for the character's level — [[d:paladin#1485331418359009380]] (Myzna).
- **[Prot]** Leveling as Prot "kicks in" once you get Seal of Wisdom (exact level not stated) — [[d:paladin#1485331751587811479]] (Magik).
- **[Prot]** Open question, no answer given in-chunk: best weapon progression after Stalvan's Axe for a leveling prot paladin — [[d:paladin#1485219162103091351]] (Antur, unanswered); the Legend of Stalvan quest chain axe (Darkshire, Duskwood) is independently recommended as "a great prot/1h weapon" and worth doing while leveling — [[d:paladin#1486166510048120852]], [[d:paladin#1486735552316440667]] (dumbid/Meriarcy).
- **[Leveling stat priority, Prot]** Rough stat order while leveling: Stamina > Spellpower > Intellect > Strength > Agility; typical leveling gear is mainly stam/int/SP but doesn't show up until later levels; spellpower remains the main threat/damage driver even after the AP/weapon-damage changes, though Strength is "a bit more useful now than before" — [[d:paladin#1486596157973991424]], [[d:paladin#1486596369677287434]], [[d:paladin#1486596503479914516]], [[d:paladin#1486596803636760656]] (gastro). Stamina is called out as the single best stat specifically for leveling via dungeon tanking, since it makes it much easier for healers to keep you alive — [[d:paladin#1486629398080655371]] (ImplodingFruitz). Intellect does **not** increase Consecration's damage — it only lets you cast more of it (mana) and adds spell crit — [[d:paladin#1486472570319540366]], [[d:paladin#1486472947492454400]] (Dodging).
- **[Leveling as first character]** Leveling a Prot paladin tank as a first-ever character is considered totally fine gear-wise — multiple experienced players confirm you don't need level-bracket BiS to level or tank dungeons at that stage, since items become obsolete so fast that farming for them is rarely worth the time (a "fun vs. efficiency" tradeoff, not a requirement) — [[d:paladin#1486734516231078081]], [[d:paladin#1486734639702872147]], [[d:paladin#1486734899468697761]], [[d:paladin#1486735131103334430]], [[d:paladin#1486735559333384192]] (Meriarcy/Dodging/Silverhand/Beldrin). A simple AH-flipping trick for cheap leveling upgrades: use an addon that shows vendor sell price, then buy any item whose AH price is barely above its vendor value — [[d:paladin#1486735584272584814]] (Grey).
- **[Twink note]** Seal of Command becomes usable somewhere around character level 42 (disputed/imprecise memory, not confirmed exactly) — [[d:paladin#1486737142435680317]], [[d:paladin#1486737381817188384]] (Seviry/Grey).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Spellpower remains the dominant tank threat/DPS stat by a wide margin over weapon damage/AP, even after the Holy Strike weapon-damage rework — [[d:paladin#1485835502966804542]] (Proph), [[d:paladin#1485887132974190693]] (gastro), [[d:paladin#1485860266041278584]] (Grey: "Ultimately a spellpower class, but if someone gives me might then hell yeah I get might").
- **[Prot weapon math]** Holy Strike's weapon-damage term uses normalized weapon damage: going from a 1.5-speed 80dps weapon to a 3.0-speed 80dps weapon adds roughly 70 "spellpower-equivalent" worth of damage to Holy Strike; going from 1.8 to 2.4 speed only adds ~28sp-equivalent — an overall ~2% threat swing at typical BiS-level Holy Strike/JoR hits (~4k/2.5k threat), i.e. real but small — [[d:paladin#1485858130440093767]] (Grey).
- **[Prot]** Weapon skill: capped weapon skill only reduces dodge chance by up to ~1.5% and this is disputed as a "major issue" for classes lacking weapon skill (e.g. Feral druids) — [[d:paladin#1485438699524198622]] (Dodging). Whether weapon skill affects parry the same way it affects dodge is disputed — Dodging says no, Grey says yes ("just inverse defense") — [[d:paladin#1485438477331075092]], [[d:paladin#1485439302090621029]].
- **[Ret hit/spellhit budget]** For a caster-hybrid (spellstrike) Ret build with WFT+FTT in party: 1 spellstrike needed with a fast weapon, 2 with a slower one (always 2 if using a 2H weapon); rough overall target cited as ~6 spell hit, 2 spellstrikes, plus 6 hit from gear — [[d:paladin#1485557876465471498]], [[d:paladin#1485558342666686486]], [[d:paladin#1485559496280375306]] (Destroses/Mithreon).
- **[Ret hit]** For melee Ret hit-cap math: roughly 3% from talents + 3% from tier pieces (2x T2 3-piece) + 1% from a book leaves you "1 short" of the cap in one build breakdown — [[d:paladin#1485780934513922129]] (Destroses).
- **[Ret]** Human racial's 3 extra weapon skill (2H sword/mace) only shaves off a small fraction of a percent of miss chance — treated as "a meme," since DPS miss chance can't be reduced below ~5% baseline anyway without parry: "if you're hitting 95% or 94%... doesn't matter" — [[d:paladin#1485785552828633168]], [[d:paladin#1485786330997854269]] (Grey).
- **[Healing]** Ironclad talent math example (bela, testing a fresh 60 in raw gear): Holy Light max rank without spellpower heals 1800-1960 at 148 base defense/0 SP; with 5500 armor (still 0 SP) plus Ironclad it heals 1900-2050 — Ironclad contributing ~110 add-heal at a ~0.7 coefficient (2.5/3.5), i.e. ~80-100 actual added healing, verifiable via a "Brainwasher" addon parse — [[d:paladin#1485600768630329364]].
- **[Prot avoidance]** Block value sourcing from gear (Grey, full breakdown): Paladin T3 alone provides ~166 block value (216 with Shield Spec); a full block-stacking set nets ~396 BV passively (702 with a trinket active), or ~416/722 with full strength buffs; paladin T3 reportedly carries roughly 60% of all the block value available on any equipment in the game — [[d:paladin#1485642249357365299]], [[d:paladin#1485646693046423593]]. Other BV sources: Styleen's item, the T3 ring, Glyph of Deflection (must be activated), all multiplied 1.3x by Shield Spec — [[d:paladin#1485641963280535644]].
- **[Prot weapon enchant]** Spellpower enchant (30 SP) still beats the Crusader (proc-on-hit) weapon enchant "in nearly all circumstances" for tanks — Crusader would need an unrealistic 6-10 ppm to compete — [[d:paladin#1485640320665391275]], [[d:paladin#1485640519500693615]] (Grey/Destroses).
- **[Ret]** "The Lost" (item/set effect) does not scale with the wearer's own spellpower directly — it benefits from spell-amplification effects applied to the target (e.g. a mage's 6-piece T3 debuff) and scales 100% off those — [[d:paladin#1485788202038988800]], [[d:paladin#1485789281770274888]] (Lightemgee).
- **[AP Ret stat targets]** One player's personal AP-Ret stat targets: ~33% crit unbuffed, aiming for 32-33% haste when fully raid-buffed (gear + Zeal + Crusade + Atiesh), with a plan to swap the last 1% of haste enchant for a Strength enchant on head/legs once itemization allows — [[d:paladin#1486344005515087996]], [[d:paladin#1486733424772251713]] (Jesse Potter). Weapon pick note for AP ret: Turalyon's for SoR builds (and usable on prot too), but Worldbreaker is considered better overall (+10 more Str, +1% haste) — [[d:paladin#1486733762485031065]], [[d:paladin#1486733798812024904]].
- **[Prot threat gear philosophy]** T2.5-tier gear dumps most of its stat budget into threat output, whereas T3/T3.5 ("Cloudplate")-tier tank gear dumps its budget mainly into stamina/defense instead — a useful mental model for evaluating why mixing T2/T2.5 pieces into a threat set is often correct even at higher raid tiers — [[d:paladin#1486724494172229743]] (Grey). Wrist slot is called out as having an unusually small stat budget in general, so even modest spellpower on a wrist (e.g. Kara10's) makes it relatively better "threat gear" by comparison — [[d:paladin#1486725238149615667]] (Arle). As of this chunk, prot paladins are missing any dedicated "threat bracer" item entirely and have to dip into cloth gear to fill that gap; other threat slots are reasonably well covered (gloves: Kara40 trash gloves; belt: Turalyon's or several mail belts, plus Mentor's for crit if hit-starved; accessories: general spellpower gear; head/shoulders/chest/legs/boots: T2.5/T3.5) — [[d:paladin#1486722956297240646]], [[d:paladin#1486723345117352176]], [[d:paladin#1486723498243260559]] (Grey).
- **[Prot vs Ret T3 2-piece]** Quick math suggests the Ret T3 2-piece bonus (+4% crit) is only worth taking over T3 Prot bracers + Turalyon's at very high spellpower — breakeven purely on Holy Strike/JoR/Autos is around ~1000 SP, and factoring in Consecration/SoR/Holy Shield/Reckoning/Windfury pushes that breakeven point meaningfully higher — [[d:paladin#1486730927223738549]] (Grey).
- **[Prot]** Why the T2 3-piece proc-heavy set bonus matters far less for Prot than for Ret: Righteous Fury only doubles threat from *holy* damage sources, not from the procced effects the T2 3-piece enables, so prot doesn't benefit from stacking those "proc memes" the way Ret does; Grey: "if we were 1.5x global threat like all 3 of the other tanks we might be more interested in those things" — [[d:paladin#1486670081877348434]] (Grey/Veyron).
- **[Prot Holy Strike ceiling]** Real-world Holy Strike crit numbers reported at BiS-ish gear: ~3300 crits with no Nightfall/Scythe-smite-priest/WoC and stam-enchanted gear, rising toward ~4000 with WoC (+~150), Nightfall (+~350), and ~80 extra SP from enchants; some log-watchers report seeing similar or higher numbers regularly on fights like Thaddeus — [[d:paladin#1486704611472707744]], [[d:paladin#1486705058795229185]], [[d:paladin#1486705386978283720]], [[d:paladin#1486705005179174992]] (Grey/Shardphoenix/Mike). Theoretical absolute ceiling: Holy Strike could exceed 2300 non-crit with a 2H weapon, full SP enchants, and every conceivable raid damage multiplier (Trueshot, Enh Strength of Earth totem, Smite-Scythe priest, Nightfall, Sub rogue, Battle Shout, etc.), making a ~5000 crit "theoretically possible in absolute ideal pure perfect situations" — [[d:paladin#1486750354191159316]], [[d:paladin#1486750501629464606]] (Grey).
- **[Prot set-bonus quirk]** The T2.5 Judgement of Righteousness (JoR) set bonus was found to only add ~20 damage to a ~600 JoR hit, far less than the advertised percentage would suggest at first glance — explained as the bonus multiplying JoR's small *base* damage component only, not the fully spellpower-scaled total (base ~170 undamaged → 170×1.15 ≈ 195) — [[d:paladin#1486703992418603009]], [[d:paladin#1486704257146163350]], [[d:paladin#1486704389480775700]] (angels1m/Grey).
