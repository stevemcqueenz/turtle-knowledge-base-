## Rotation: single target
(see Cooldowns/threat and Healing sections for melee-holy specifics)
- **[Holy melee]** Melee Holy Paladins do NOT need to worry about hit cap for Holy Strike's attached heal: "the heal always goes out" regardless of whether the melee portion hits — [[d:paladin#1439760009096466582]] (Akos, Q), [[d:paladin#1439760087060316180]] (Destroses, A, 2025-11-16).
- **[Ret/mechanics]** Judgement of the Crusader (JotC) does not increase the caster's own spell power — it is a debuff that increases Holy damage taken by the target. Crusader Strike is not Holy damage, so it gets no benefit from JotC even indirectly; Holy Strike (which is Holy damage) does benefit but still underperforms Crusader Strike's 2H physical scaling overall — [[d:paladin#1439757160513933424]], [[d:paladin#1439757708923375689]], [[d:paladin#1439758047173152969]] (dusty, confirmed mechanic, 2025-11-16).
- **[Weapon choice]** On a level-1 training dummy, Claymore + Crusader Strike spam parses far higher than Runeblade + Holy Strike, but this reverses in real raid conditions: dummy armor (~4k) heavily mitigates Crusader Strike's physical damage, while in MC with proper armor debuffs, boss armor drops to ~1k or less (13% DR or lower), narrowing/reversing the gap; dummy also crits more due to level difference — [[d:paladin#1439557337244504225]], [[d:paladin#1439557480878313493]], [[d:paladin#1439558378404712610]], [[d:paladin#1439559858109022268]] (Backstall/Mithreon, 2025-11-16).
- **[Spell Ret]** Opener/priority: open the fight with Holy Strike as the first GCD (it benefits from JoTC and lands before Sunder Armor/armor-debuffs are up), then switch to Crusader Strike for the rest of the fight once armor debuffs are stacked, since CS's physical-damage benefit from sunders generally outweighs Holy Strike's SP scaling for the remainder — confirmed by multiple regulars after back-and-forth — [[d:paladin#1440193252538974301]], [[d:paladin#1440195370712957060]], [[d:paladin#1440195456859639939]] (dusty/Mccnl1ght/Jesse Potter, 2025-11-17). With Crusader Strike stacks/buff and Holy Strike buff both up, prioritize whichever parses higher: Crusader Strike usually wins in raids (scales with weapon damage, benefits from boss armor debuffs) while Holy Strike (scales with SP, not weapon) can win on a high-armor training dummy with enough SP — [[d:paladin#1440327639431778314]], [[d:paladin#1440329935200976966]] (Adamant, 2025-11-18).
- **[Spell Ret, key summary]** "You have Holy Strike and Crusader Strike for Ret, you think there's a choice but 90% of the time it's just Crusader Strike, and for Prot it's just Holy Strike, and lately it's just Holy Strike for Holy [too], because why would you reset the CD on Holy Shock in raids when you can do 5x the healing with Holy Strike" — summarizing actual strike usage per spec — [[d:paladin#1440590927860334663]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-11-19).
- **[Spell Ret, raid vs dummy]** Repeated/confirmed with real numbers: on a target dummy (~4k armor) Holy Strike outparses Crusader Strike, but in a sundered raid, Crusader Strike wins — real example cited from an Ambershire MC Lucifron log: one paladin's Holy Strike crit for 1178 while a normal (non-crit) Crusader Strike hit for 1154 — i.e., CS's normal hit ≈ HS's crit, meaning CS clearly outdamages HS once bosses are sundered; the gap grows as gear/weapon damage increases — [[d:paladin#1440595054476394516]], [[d:paladin#1440595107131687032]], [[d:paladin#1440597606404456508]], [[d:paladin#1440598728175255602]] (gastro/Jesse Potter, 2025-11-19).
- **[Spell Ret, mechanic]** Seal of Command/Judgement/Crusader Strike are all classified as "melee attacks" for on-crit mana-return-talent interactions (not spells) — [[d:paladin#1440588604660256789]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-11-19).
- **[Spell Ret, coefficients]** Crusader Strike has a 20% SP coefficient (in addition to weapon-damage/AP scaling); Holy Strike has a 43% SP coefficient but does not scale with weapon damage at all (normalized) — [[d:paladin#1440194652442464267]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-11-17).
- **[Mechanics, terminology corrected]** Reckoning is commonly (but incorrectly) called "parry haste" in this community. True parry haste in vanilla is a 40% swing-timer reduction on main-hand only. Reckoning is actually "an instant unconditional Windfury-style attack against the target that blocked", which finishes your current swing timer and queues the next — mechanically similar in effect (bigger relative swing-timer value on slower weapons) but a different underlying mechanic; it can also sometimes erroneously trigger at range (~30y), which the community treats as a bug/quirk — [[d:paladin#1440690585437606153]], [[d:paladin#1440691148094967928]], [[d:paladin#1440691516589871215]], [[d:paladin#1440691811638055055]], [[d:paladin#1440692031629430856]] (Silverhand/Arle/Mithreon, 2025-11-19; corrects the earlier "parry haste" framing used elsewhere in this chunk, e.g. [[d:paladin#1439593116301525093]]).

- **[Prot, Lasher-farming build]** Concrete Lasher-farming rotation/gearing (resolves a frequently-asked question in this channel): use a slow weapon with 5/5 Reckoning (let Reckoning do the swing-timer work rather than chasing a fast weapon for more seal procs, contra some outside advice), Seal of Wisdom (has a 20 PPM proc rate — will proc on essentially every hit with a ~3.0s weapon, making Judgement unnecessary for mana), and in practice Seal of Wisdom alone on Lashers gives effectively infinite mana. Rotation: Holy Shield on cooldown, Consecration on cooldown, spam Crusader Strike (with the Holy talent that resets Holy Shock's cooldown on crit); throw a self-Holy Shock in when available; pull/clear one pack at a time until geared enough to pull two — [[d:paladin#1441446113449349295]], [[d:paladin#1441446199419732048]], [[d:paladin#1441446283159273543]], [[d:paladin#1441447150713176185]], [[d:paladin#1441447353042210888]], [[d:paladin#1441447353071702106]], [[d:paladin#1441447571443683348]], [[d:paladin#1441447808761860257]], [[d:paladin#1441447996620538017]], [[d:paladin#1441448069316087882]] (Dzonathan/Silverhand, 2025-11-21).
- **[Prot, AoE macro tip]** Practical AoE-tank flow: keep Holy Shield up, drop Consecration, and run your normal single-target rotation on cooldown otherwise; the main execution hurdle is Judgement+reseal, which can be macro'd into one button — [[d:paladin#1440690436363649155]], [[d:paladin#1440690514063134862]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-19).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Spell Ret]** Crit-to-SP/AP conversion estimates (all single-source/community rules of thumb, disputed in specifics):
  - 1% crit ≈ 30–50 AP depending on personal preference; value drops off between ~33% and ~47–51% buffed crit due to a crit threshold — [[d:paladin#1439907563482382376]] (Jesse Potter).
  - "Feelcraft" estimate: 1% crit for Spell Ret ≈ 10 SP (since JoR/Crusader Strike/Holy Strike are melee-crit-scaling), or ~15 SP once accounting for autoattacks with WF totem — [[d:paladin#1439908279110336623]], [[d:paladin#1439908853306359889]] (Mithreon).
- **[Prot]** Community consensus (dusty, Dodging, Silverhand, angels1m): ignore Defense skill entirely below and at level 60 and prioritize Stamina instead. Reasoning: gear with Defense generally has lower spell power (threat loss); Paladin cannot reach the Defense/avoidance cap without sacrificing too much threat; Paladin already gets avoidance-cap-like coverage from Holy Shield + Block; "there's like one boss [below K40] that genuinely requires 400 defense." Precise mechanic cited: 1 point of Defense skill = +0.04% each to dodge/parry/block/miss/crit-taken (≈0.2% total avoidance per point) — [[d:paladin#1440029317118562436]], [[d:paladin#1440029715283841266]], [[d:paladin#1440030013218099300]], [[d:paladin#1440030275622011013]], [[d:paladin#1440031903687381062]], [[d:paladin#1440033269570605227]] (2025-11-17).
- **[Prot]** Str is a poor stat for Prot Paladin: Holy Strike is a melee attack (benefits from melee hit/crit) but the attack-power-scaling portion of its damage is a small part of overall threat; Attack Power "slightly increases white hit damage but that's a minuscule part of your threat" — [[d:paladin#1439627938755641436]], [[d:paladin#1439628995279847455]], [[d:paladin#1439629214742610141]] (Sadi/Silverhand, 2025-11-16).
- **[Gear itemization complaint]** Ret gear is itemized with "obscene amounts" of Intellect (not very useful for melee-Ret), Prot gear itemized with excess Strength (poor tanking stat) — [[d:paladin#1439849628752416779]] (dusty, opinion, 2025-11-16).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Ret]** Blessing of Freedom does not remove or grant immunity to all slow effects: it removes Thunderclap (AQ20) but not follow-up slows/frenzy effects from the same fight, and does not remove effects like Rasnaal's (or similar boss's) Ice Armor/Cone of Cold/Frostbolt slows or debuffs at all — [[d:paladin#1439744267236016168]], [[d:paladin#1439744579728314571]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-11-16).
- **[Prot]** Holy Shield mana sustain: with Reckoning + Seal of Righteousness procs, mana regenerates enough to recast Holy Shield on cooldown; don't drink mana tea and a mana potion simultaneously — drink them separately to get two distinct "recharge" windows instead of overflow waste — [[d:paladin#1440054288024866896]], [[d:paladin#1440055390921293864]] (dusty, 2025-11-17).
- **[Ret/gear mechanic]** Sigil (MC trinket) only interacts with T2 spellstrike gear specifically, not generic spellstrike items — there is reportedly another (unspecified) way to make it work with other gear — [[d:paladin#1439751928316690624]] (dusty, 2025-11-16). T1's 8/8 set bonus also procs (Holy Spellstrike-like effect) but "not at a busted rate" — [[d:paladin#1440065892737089596]] (Istenneveben, 2025-11-17). Sigil-type spellstrike procs are directly triggered by: T2 Ret 3-set, T2 Prot 3-set, and Exorcism; once you have T2, Crusader Strike/Holy Strike/Judgements can also trigger it indirectly because their accompanying melee/auto component counts as triggering a spellstrike — [[d:paladin#1440901611793809509]], [[d:paladin#1440901942237859871]] (Adamant, 2025-11-19).
- **[Mechanic, confirmed via combat log]** Judgement is coded as a melee attack, so (like Crusader Strike/Holy Strike and their accompanying autos) it can proc melee-attack-triggered items like Hand of Justice's extra-attack and the Cloak of the Warlord/WoT-faction cloak; this was disputed in-channel (one player insisted CS/HS "can't" trigger these) but resolved with posted combat-log lines showing both Holy Strike and Crusader Strike triggering Hand of Justice extra attacks, and the claimant retracted — [[d:paladin#1440955522965573716]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1440968038223450122]], [[d:paladin#1440968131852894290]], [[d:paladin#1440968192183894059]], [[d:paladin#1440968236916015104]], [[d:paladin#1440968304389918822]], [[d:paladin#1440972132405215273]] (30/Mccnl1ght, 2025-11-19/20).
- **[Ret]** Spellstrike-proc items (JoW-style) are valued primarily for mana sustain (how long you can fight before OOM) and for ramping/maintaining Vengeance uptime/crit, not for raw damage; in Molten Core most fights are short enough that spellstrike isn't needed to avoid going oom — [[d:paladin#1439752628170133585]], [[d:paladin#1439752660994621621]], [[d:paladin#1439753004562649251]], [[d:paladin#1439753190961844245]] (2025-11-16).
- **[Spell Ret, SoC mechanic]** Seal of Command rank 1 deals 70% weapon damage on proc; higher ranks give slightly stronger Judgements, useful mainly on short fights — [[d:paladin#1440498273231179918]], [[d:paladin#1440498538424303716]] (Beldrin, 2025-11-19). Judgement of Command has an internal cooldown (~0.5s) specifically to stop it from triggering multiple times off a single Windfury proc window; this ICD in the interaction database was reportedly not known to devs until a player flagged it about two weeks prior — [[d:paladin#1440525984872009912]], [[d:paladin#1440526296860856393]], [[d:paladin#1440526299839070289]] (Squid/Jesse Potter, 2025-11-19).
- **[Spell Ret, technique]** Seal-twisting trick: after Judging with Seal of Command, some players briefly switch to Seal of the Crusader (haste-on-seal) then back to Command to land the next Judgement-of-the-Crusader reapplication faster; described by its user as mainly a QoL/timing tool for JoTC upkeep, not a reliable DPS gain (costs a Command proc chance) — [[d:paladin#1440554074205327492]], [[d:paladin#1440557041142857800]], [[d:paladin#1440557183082434671]], [[d:paladin#1440557312065536010]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-11-19).
- **[Spell Ret, mana]** Playing max-rank Seal of Command/Judgement and max-rank Exorcism, a player reports consistently ending fights with leftover mana, including on fights (e.g., Kel'Thuzad add phases) where Consecration can't be used on cooldown — spell ret mana is generally not tight for a SoR-focused user in that context (contrast with SoC Ret, reported elsewhere as mana-tight, especially in AoE/trash) — [[d:paladin#1440499138436530302]], [[d:paladin#1440499424727011388]], [[d:paladin#1440499676624326716]], [[d:paladin#1440496403645337700]], [[d:paladin#1440496577201442898]] (Jesse Potter/Dodging, 2025-11-19).

"If you are specifically looking for an AoE proc, you aren't going to find one" — [[d:paladin#1441132523479503032]], [[d:paladin#1441132661870559254]], [[d:paladin#1441132796952313982]], [[d:paladin#1441133110103248966]], [[d:paladin#1441133474525351946]] (dusty, 2025-11-20).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Prot]** Prot Paladin is seen as behind other tanks: weakest health/armor combo, mitigation carried mainly by Block, worst raid-buff scaling of any tank (no SP-scaling raid buffs exist; best external buffs are WFT/BoK/MotW/Bear Aura), and worse threat/AoE-threat generation than tank Shaman or Warrior — see Spec viability section for citations, [[d:paladin#1439713753405984941]], [[d:paladin#1440044480173441226]].
- **[Prot]** Defense skill should essentially be ignored (see Stats section) — [[d:paladin#1440029715283841266]].
- **[Prot]** T3.5 Prot's actual raid threat is disputed: some joke it "should not be mentioned in [the same sentence as] full threat," while another (Mithreon) reports a Naxx clear with half the raid at 150+ ping and an off-spec healer with zero threat problems using T3.5 Prot at full threat gear + TF — [[d:paladin#1439943594545774694]], [[d:paladin#1439942795774267473]] (contested, 2025-11-16/17).
- **[Prot]** Onyxia-specific threat troubleshooting (Silverhand, detailed, well received): Onyxia has multiple threat-drop mechanics — she drops the threat of top-of-threat-list players while flying, and lands with the tank's threat effectively reset while ranged DPS who kept attacking during the flying phase retain theirs; her wing-buffet knockback also strips some threat. Unless the tank vastly outgears the raid, holding aggro immediately after she lands is largely dependent on DPS pacing, not tank skill/gear — "Onyxia has multiple threat drop mechanics so if DPS don't respect threat, there's only so much you can do... it's the only boss in the first tier with real threat mechanics." Prot Paladin threat is otherwise "actually very good" in early raids and isn't a real problem until Naxx-level DPS ramp-up — [[d:paladin#1440155885107413032]], [[d:paladin#1440156076216680459]], [[d:paladin#1440156130306293810]], [[d:paladin#1440157084242022481]], [[d:paladin#1440157226579923054]], [[d:paladin#1440159256795025489]] (2025-11-17). One tank (George) reported still losing Ony aggro post-landing with 237 unbuffed SP, Defense-capped, 5k HP, Flask of Supreme Power and full rotation on CD — attributed by the regulars to DPS not respecting the post-land threat window rather than his gear.
- **[Prot]** Reminder: make sure non-tanks (especially threat-heavy specs like Assassination Rogues) have Salvation applied in dungeons/raids to reduce these threat blowouts — [[d:paladin#1440157505794867230]], [[d:paladin#1440157766034522135]] (Silverhand/George, 2025-11-17).
- **[Prot, K40 threat vs Bear/Warrior]** At the Karazhan40 progression level, Prot Paladin threat is described as heavily luck-dependent (relies on not being parried while your SP trinket is on cooldown) and generally losing out to Bear tanks, which scale better via Atiesh, haste, K40 gear upgrades, and Elixir of Demon Slaying (which gives Paladin no value); "Bear can MT with half the BiS missing, against BiS-geared teammates," whereas Prot Paladin "needs threat gear like oxygen" at that progression level and must be geared as well as the best DPS in the raid to hold aggro. In full BiS with consumes, one estimate put Prot Paladin around 8.5k HP (T2.5-level gear alone gives ~7.5k). This contrasts with an earlier-progression (Naxx-level) impression from another player that Prot Paladin threat still felt superior to Warrior at that point — [[d:paladin#1441458327543349279]], [[d:paladin#1441458630254661753]], [[d:paladin#1441458925622001781]], [[d:paladin#1441458986246340638]], [[d:paladin#1441459099903590513]], [[d:paladin#1441459209211220021]], [[d:paladin#1441461743556755617]], [[d:paladin#1441461894555893891]], [[d:paladin#1441462121891233853]] (Artifyce/Mithreon/angels1m, 2025-11-21).
- **[Prot, set-swap mechanic]** T1 and T2 gear pieces can only be converted between spec-versions (e.g., Holy→Prot) a single time from their raid-drop form; once swapped, they're locked. Carrying too many "swappable" items in your bags at once (vs. bank) can cause the item-swap NPC in Ratchet to bug out and hide/fail to show items — keep only the currently-needed swappable piece(s) in your bags and store the rest in the bank. T2.5 and T3 tokens ARE freely re-swappable; T3.5, despite being token-based, is NOT swappable (possibly deliberate, to gather usage data or encourage sustained raid participation) — [[d:paladin#1439812335731605644]], [[d:paladin#1439812557023088782]], [[d:paladin#1439812585666117815]], [[d:paladin#1439812772899586118]], [[d:paladin#1439813221623005256]], [[d:paladin#1439932487768866836]], [[d:paladin#1439932559034417183]], [[d:paladin#1439932828849668148]] (2025-11-16).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy melee, MC guide]** Full boss-by-boss melee-holy-paladin guide for Molten Core (Dzonathan, well received): Lucifron - follow kill order on adds then boss; Magmadar - stick to the dog, expect fear, pop Fire Resist Potion before and again mid-fight (you'll likely get feared into fire); Gehennas - dodge whatever; Garr - don't stand near small adds when they die, otherwise heal boss tank or heal from afar with Flash of Light; Shazzrah - pop an Arcane Resist potion, don't cast during his Counterspell CD window, watch BigWigs; Baron Geddon - pop Fire Resist potion, run away from Inferno, run to the wall if you have the Living Bomb, can Bubble through Inferno; Golemagg - stay on boss, others handle the dogs; Twin Emperors (Sulfuron/Golemagg twins?) - swap targets; Domo... /Majordomo - kill adds in order then boss; Sulfuron Harbinger - kill adds in order then boss; Ragnaros - move away then back in on Knockback, spread out and heal MT/from range, group up when adds spawn — [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]] (2025-11-16).
- **[Holy melee]** Supplementary tips: big Golem-type mobs telegraph a stomp — good Holy Strike-heal opportunity; packs of fire elementals + stone adds (3–4 mobs) can stun and hit hard, randomly repeat-target the same player — Holy Shock + 1.5s Holy Light those players; don't stand in front of "fire dog" (Core Hound) breath; don't melee-heal on a boss if unsure of mechanics, FoL-spam from range is fine — [[d:paladin#1439618048259657831]], [[d:paladin#1439618474069725355]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-16).
- **[Holy melee]** General approach recommended: mirror the melee DPS — if melee runs in, you run in; if they bail, you bail; if one player stays in while others leave, that player is likely parsing (chasing damage), so bail with the group anyway — [[d:paladin#1439619289194823963]] (Dzonathan, 2025-11-16).
- **[Holy melee]** Hit cap does not matter for the heal component of melee attacks (Holy Strike's Mending Light heal always lands even if the melee swing misses) — [[d:paladin#1439760087060316180]] (Destroses, 2025-11-16).
- **[Holy melee, stats]** Stat priority for Holy melee given as Spell Power > Intellect > Armor; Armor's only relevant conversion is via the "Ironclad"-type set bonus/talent, at a rate of Armor × 0.02 = bonus heal power — full BiS gear with Ironclad only nets around 180 heal-power from that conversion ("not that extreme"). Once you're around 1100–1200 crit rating, additional Crit starts outvaluing additional heal-power/bonus-healing — [[d:paladin#1440981051190284372]], [[d:paladin#1440983531898535956]], [[d:paladin#1440984135740030996]], [[d:paladin#1440984887862628483]], [[d:paladin#1440985549208031232]], [[d:paladin#1440986093334827087]] (Saša/gyatt/Mithreon, 2025-11-20).
- **[Holy Strike mechanic]** Holy Strike is a melee attack (benefits from melee hit/crit, uses melee crit tables) but also fires a Mending Light heal + mana replenish that counts as a healing spell for other purposes (e.g., healing-spell interactions) — [[d:paladin#1439627190559047780]], [[d:paladin#1439627387359854683]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-16).

- **[Holy]** Full healing-kit breakdown (Mithreon, detailed, in response to a new-healer question): Flash of Light = short single-target heal, 1.5s cast; Holy Light = long single-target heal, 2.5s cast (hastable to 1.5s after a Judgement, buff persists until used or death); Holy Shock = instant single-target heal, 20s CD with a self-reset chance of roughly 5% + 0.01%×heal-power on crit; Holy Strike = melee-range 5-target smart heal, 6s CD. Any of these can crit into "Daybreak," an aftershock heal worth roughly one Flash of Light that triggers once the next time the healed ally takes damage. Illumination returns mana on heal crits and is described as a very large contributor to Holy's sustain; Daybreak procs are said to inflate a Holy Paladin's healing-done parse significantly relative to other healers — [[d:paladin#1440253873276194857]] (2025-11-18).
- **[Holy, trinket]** Item `https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=55087` is a 4-element random-proc trinket valued highly for Holy: Frost = slow, Fire = DoT zone, Arcane = spell power + haste, Holy = instant-refresh-type effect + heal ("Holy explosion" heals a bit); called "IMO BiS for hpal," with Eye of the Dead as the second-best trinket option; commonly paired with the AQ40 "meteor" trinket, using one on one pull and the other on the next — [[d:paladin#1440354013487829134]], [[d:paladin#1440357406789079092]], [[d:paladin#1440357631750438923]], [[d:paladin#1440358237583970366]], [[d:paladin#1440358529163853954]], [[d:paladin#1440359074007875699]], [[d:paladin#1440359675722727516]] (Mithreon/angels1m, 2025-11-18).
- **[Holy]** Crit-to-heal-power conversion rule of thumb ("feelcraft"): 3% crit ≈ 120 heal-power/bonus-healing — [[d:paladin#1440366950768185457]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-18).
- **[Holy, weapon]** "Death's Bargain" (1H mace) recommended to be kept as Holy Paladin's weapon until a Kara40 (K40) shield-tier mace is available; the Kel'Thuzad-drop mace is judged not worth switching to since it trades crit for heal-power at a bad ratio ("massive gap for just 1 crit") — [[d:paladin#1440368357353455709]], [[d:paladin#1440368747276796055]], [[d:paladin#1440368894811439149]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-18).
- **[Holy]** For a solo Paladin in a raid, self-buff Blessing of Wisdom and put Salvation on everyone else; if you're the only Paladin and playing Prot, use PallyPower to Salvation the raid and keep a 10-min Blessing of Might on yourself — [[d:paladin#1440275124552204319]], [[d:paladin#1440276857407803412]], [[d:paladin#1440296346706776165]] (Destroses/Mithreon/Dzonathan, 2025-11-18). Sanctuary Aura is worth keeping while still doing dungeons, but is "kinda trash" for raiding since other paladins in the raid typically cover the aura — [[d:paladin#1440275124552204319]].
- **[Holy, raid comp opinion]** Debated ideal healer comp (opinion, not confirmed by devs): one view wants ~9-10 healers total — ~5 Resto Shaman, 2 Holy Priest, 2 Holy Paladin, plus a Shadow Priest or extra Holy Priest; a counter-view (dusty) argues Elemental Shaman do the same totem job while being the best raid DPS, and melee-Holy Paladins heal as well or better than Resto Shaman, making the 5x Resto Shaman allocation excessive — [[d:paladin#1440186607662534757]], [[d:paladin#1440187205971611679]], [[d:paladin#1440187474600267828]] (2025-11-17).
- **[Holy, mechanic]** Holy Strike's smart-heal component reportedly has full/guaranteed high crit chance across up to 7 targets simultaneously (some say 5), which several regulars flag as a likely source of Holy's healing-parse dominance and a nerf candidate (e.g., proposals include making Mending Light's crit chance halved, or making Holy Strike cost mana unless a specific talent is taken) — [[d:paladin#1441456293708697640]], [[d:paladin#1441456341133824191]], [[d:paladin#1441456362822434899]], [[d:paladin#1441456707007021190]] (Mithreon/angels1m/Dzonathan, 2025-11-21, community speculation).
- **[Holy]** Multiple Holy Paladins in one raid stack well on AoE raid damage: "5 hpals in a raid, AoE goes off... instant Prayer-of-Healing-equivalent coverage on half the raid at least," a level of simultaneous smart-healing only Priest's Prayer of Healing can otherwise match — but without Priest's offsetting heavy mana cost — [[d:paladin#1441457163863326731]], [[d:paladin#1441457312052281464]], [[d:paladin#1441457368817733892]], [[d:paladin#1441457510644056257]], [[d:paladin#1441457573646569582]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-21).
- **[Holy, expected nerf]** Strong community expectation that Holy Paladin will be nerfed, driven by PvP complaints and Holy topping healer parse charts (attributed to smart-heal AoE overperformance); the T2 3-piece proc-enabling bonus for Holy is also expected to be targeted — [[d:paladin#1441452953407066142]], [[d:paladin#1441453163679846400]], [[d:paladin#1441453473257492681]], [[d:paladin#1441453651783848147]], [[d:paladin#1441454113991950489]] (Mithreon/Beldrin, 2025-11-21).
- **[Holy]** Confirmed strong T3.5 Holy 3-piece set bonus in practice: one player with the bonus did 50% of total raid healing in an AQ20 with 4-5 healers (next-highest healer at 15-20%); the bonus (healing extra targets off Holy Strike/Daybreak) was implemented before the "Daybreak" ability rework and was never revisited afterward, which the community flags as a likely balance oversight — [[d:paladin#1440190746383618179]], [[d:paladin#1440190917691834460]], [[d:paladin#1440190991347875880]], [[d:paladin#1440191565942362326]] (Sookie/Jesse Potter/dusty, 2025-11-17).

- **[Prot, UBRS]** Upper Blackrock Spire is tankable as Prot with modest gear (blue gear + ~80 SP reported as workable); use a ranged pull option (e.g., dynamite) for dangerous packs, especially in the fire-orc area, where face-pulling can be very risky; mark targets to control kill priority; base damage from Holy Strike and Judgement of Righteousness is enough threat to tank the instance; bring a Stamina ring, pop an Arcane Elixir + Wizard Oil; keep your back to a wall throughout since many packs have knockback/fling effects — [[d:paladin#1441111894722220103]], [[d:paladin#1441112018798252196]], [[d:paladin#1441112320804913182]], [[d:paladin#1441112466821222632]], [[d:paladin#1441114525905457285]], [[d:paladin#1441114631308316797]], [[d:paladin#1441114650434601011]], [[d:paladin#1441114942769201392]], [[d:paladin#1441115195643789375]], [[d:paladin#1441115389097672724]], [[d:paladin#1441117536921059479]] (Saša/Dodging/angels1m/Lazepally, 2025-11-20).
- **["Twins" encounter, Prot tanking]** A Paladin can tank one of the "Twins" (warlock-summon-associated add fight): get initial threat by standing closest to the boss with specific talents to pull roughly 11k threat; if the boss knocks you back the wrong direction right before a boss-swap, that's usually a wipe; approach: pop Consecration under yourself as you retreat, then spam Judgement + Holy Shock + blessings to rebuild threat (~10k), and ask DPS to ease off; you must run away immediately if knocked back by the caster-side twin; any damage dealt right after a teleport/"port" mechanic wakes the boss and causes an instant knockback, so hold your Judgement until you're far from the boss. Physical DPS should carry most of the damage; casters should focus adds since Paladin/caster threat generation is comparatively low with modern (smaller, non-30-warrior) raid comps — a real caster class is a better pick than Paladin for a shadow-resist soft-cap role — [[d:paladin#1441119370230366259]], [[d:paladin#1441120593561915512]], [[d:paladin#1441120655121711288]], [[d:paladin#1441120835036385385]], [[d:paladin#1441121121951813733]], [[d:paladin#1441121332262605042]], [[d:paladin#1441121537557135360]], [[d:paladin#1441121771808886855]], [[d:paladin#1441122563190165626]], [[d:paladin#1441122661722882241]], [[d:paladin#1441122989663064185]], [[d:paladin#1441123098815627366]], [[d:paladin#1441123172568141867]], [[d:paladin#1441123560314769509]], [[d:paladin#1441123698005245972]], [[d:paladin#1441123793115418627]], [[d:paladin#1441124207428632658]], [[d:paladin#1441124402304516136]], [[d:paladin#1441124503752015934]], [[d:paladin#1441124625705861262]], [[d:paladin#1441124757469790409]] (Soulafei/Pastor/Dodging, 2025-11-20).
- **[General]** "Surger" add in MC (Lucifron/Magmadar trash?) — described as a small black golem that charges the furthest target; stack on it — [[d:paladin#1439620188809990266]], [[d:paladin#1439620234209132697]] (Dzonathan, 2025-11-16).

## PvP
- **[Prot/Holy hybrid]** dusty plays a "Holy Prot" PvP build using Thunderfury; Reckoning + Seal of Righteousness + TF procs described as "a huge deal" for burst; uses Frost Oil enchant — [[d:paladin#1439840528337207418]], [[d:paladin#1439841448072708199]], [[d:paladin#1439842268453535805]], [[d:paladin#1439837489073487872]] (2025-11-16).
- **[Prot]** Alternate 1H+shield build reported vs Dual-Wield Fury Warriors: Judgement of Light (1H weapon) + shield + Seal of Righteousness, to out-sustain the warrior's Bloodrinker lifesteal and hope Holy Strike crits finish them — [[d:paladin#1439841216589070369]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-11-16).
- **[General]** Community claim (unconfirmed): a dev noted world-buff DPS as a ceiling the playerbase is "far from reaching" — [[d:paladin#1439847039507763260]] (Dodging, single source).
- **[Ret/PvP]** Some paladins run T2 Seal of Righteousness gear with the "Lost"/Accord trinket combo in PvP specifically for its wolf-summon add-on effect, used mainly to prevent Rogues from stealthing rather than for the damage itself; dusty expects this interaction to eventually get nerfed because of PvP usage — [[d:paladin#1440227931325730928]], [[d:paladin#1440228045406605444]], [[d:paladin#1440228432637071440]], [[d:paladin#1440228568524394556]], [[d:paladin#1440228814117535754]] (2025-11-17).
- **[Ret PvP, weakness]** Ret Paladins are widely viewed (including by Horde opponents) as a weak/wasted teammate slot in battlegrounds: no gap closer, pure-melee kiteable, and generally low current damage output; many Ret players reportedly don't even apply Judgement to world/BG bosses (e.g., "18 paladins fighting [an AV boss]... but 0 judgements on them"). The "OP Ret" reputation traces to the CC1 era when Ret burst was real and FOTM Ret stacking (up to 8 in one Arathi Basin) happened — which actually lost fights since ranged Horde could kite them; much of the ongoing complaint is attributed to Warrior players upset they couldn't reliably win 1v1 against Paladins back then — [[d:paladin#1440693390907015248]], [[d:paladin#1440693596868055192]], [[d:paladin#1440694394679332916]], [[d:paladin#1440695003973419287]], [[d:paladin#1440695020989714534]], [[d:paladin#1440696313221222442]], [[d:paladin#1440696411950813336]], [[d:paladin#1440696484495495359]], [[d:paladin#1440696589805949000]], [[d:paladin#1440696653626609664]], [[d:paladin#1440696921374326835]] (Mithreon/Silverhand, 2025-11-19).
- **[Ret PvP, gearing]** BiS/pre-BiS Ret gear sets don't account for survivability beyond the bare minimum; for PvP, add Stamina pieces (T2/T2.5 alone isn't considered enough) until you stop "feeling like paper," rather than following a rigid PvE-oriented gear list; swap out spellstrike items (useless in PvP) for stamina/PvP pieces, except keep T2 3-piece if going SoR since it still procs Sigil-type damage (not needed if playing SoC) — [[d:paladin#1441524752610099302]], [[d:paladin#1441524791424061550]], [[d:paladin#1441524843462918265]], [[d:paladin#1441525072236904669]], [[d:paladin#1441526680865734706]], [[d:paladin#1441526844041068664]], [[d:paladin#1441528784812769361]] (Silverhand/Dodging/Adamant, 2025-11-22).
- **[Ret PvP, philosophy]** General PvP advice: prioritize supporting your team (well-timed Hand of Protection/Freedom on a focused ally) over chasing max personal damage — "a well-placed Hand will matter more than any damage you can do"; SoR is much less bursty than SoC, and burst matters more in PvP — [[d:paladin#1441525444099837992]], [[d:paladin#1441525624140464239]], [[d:paladin#1441525705086079238]], [[d:paladin#1441525849042977040]] (Silverhand, 2025-11-22).
- **[PvP]** "90% of the value of Paladins in PvP is the support anyway" — Holy can provide that while healing, Prot can provide it by being a disruptive nuisance, but Ret is seen as mostly just chasing ranged players and dying; Spell Ret is described as "never meant to do big burst of damage" (that's SoC's role), though 800 Drain Life procs off Corrupted Ashbringer are called "game changing" in duels/BGs; Repentance is Ret's real (if underwhelming) CC tool — [[d:paladin#1441145875756875876]], [[d:paladin#1441145908963184793]], [[d:paladin#1441145952101470392]], [[d:paladin#1441145965737410632]], [[d:paladin#1441146289134764163]] (Adamant/Silverhand, 2025-11-20).
- **[Ret PvP]** Spell Ret is reported to lack enough Stamina to function well in PvP; Ret generally needs to prioritize Stamina to some degree just to survive fights at all — [[d:paladin#1441146500938731622]], [[d:paladin#1441146608862494750]] (gastro, 2025-11-20). is described as extremely strong against plate ("eat plates for breakfast"); Paladins reportedly only stand a real chance against it with Divine Shield + a defensive trinket up — [[d:paladin#1440704598984884274]], [[d:paladin#1440704796448395396]], [[d:paladin#1440704913096048794]] (Adamant, 2025-11-19).
- **[PvP]** "Flamewrath" (a Paladin buff/ability with a damage-reflect effect) can kill melee attackers who hit you while it's active — used by at least one player against Rogues who jumped him in world PvP outside Stratholme, causing them to kill themselves on the reflect — [[d:paladin#1440229107198591016]] (dusty, 2025-11-17, single source/anecdote).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Race, Ret mana]** High Elf's racial (an Arcane Torrent-like mana return, castable but with a cast time) is valued specifically for Ret's mana problems — "if Rets had no mana issues we wouldn't even think about the High Elf racial"; some players preferred an older out-of-combat-mana-regen version and consider the current cast-time version underwhelming/clunky (no spell pushback at least) — [[d:paladin#1441380558470123521]], [[d:paladin#1441380961966358593]], [[d:paladin#1441381509708906516]], [[d:paladin#1441381813703675995]] (Excinerus/Silverhand/Mithreon, 2025-11-21).
- **[Race, Prot]** Dwarf racial (Stoneform, self-cleanse) is called particularly strong for Naxx progression specifically because Shamans in a raid may not reliably drop cleansing totems — [[d:paladin#1441383061228097536]], [[d:paladin#1441383156048597043]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-21).
- **[General]** Standard Blessings last 10 minutes; Greater Blessings (30-minute duration) exist but are only obtainable much later in progression — [[d:paladin#1441225540647387228]], [[d:paladin#1441225745375559760]] (Plank/Adamant, 2025-11-21).
- **[Farming]** A Paladin alt can farm Lashers with reasonably cheap BoE gear starting around level 53, but profitable farming generally wants at least some Molten Core gear — [[d:paladin#1441223425786904708]], [[d:paladin#1441225135108391002]] (andre/Adamant, 2025-11-21).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Gear]** Carrying too many spec-swappable tier items in bags at once can cause the Ratchet tier-swap NPC to bug out/hide items; keep the bank clear of everything except the swappable piece(s) you need right now — [[d:paladin#1439812585666117815]], [[d:paladin#1439812772899586118]] (2025-11-16).
- **[Display]** Armor/Attack Power can display -1 off from actual due to a rounding-type bug on certain races; visual only — [[d:paladin#1439999778703282348]], [[d:paladin#1440001508929437717]] (2025-11-17).
- **[Contested]** Unyielding Faith (fear-resist talent) + shadow resist gear reportedly performs inconsistently — one player claims resisting 0 of 3 fears in a row with ~150 shadow resist + the talent, but resisting fears normally with it all removed; framed sarcastically, unconfirmed as an actual bug — [[d:paladin#1439746501797740696]], [[d:paladin#1439747527413858304]], [[d:paladin#1439747565288427721]] (Jesse Potter/Silverhand, 2025-11-16, single source/anecdote).
