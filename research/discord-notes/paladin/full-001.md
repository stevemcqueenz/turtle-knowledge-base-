## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Ret]** Devs reportedly stated the upcoming patch focuses on fixes, not big class changes, and that Ret is intended to remain "lobotomite melee dps" style (simple/low-complexity) per some devs' stated design goal — [[d:paladin#1427755886121582763]] [[d:paladin#1427757428426145853]] (dusty/Dodging, 2025-10-15; single-source claim).
- **[Ret]** Crusader Strike has been nerfed twice since "CC2": reduced spellpower scaling and normalized its physical damage to a 3.30-speed weapon baseline, so weapon speed itself barely matters for CS damage beyond the weapon's own Str/SP stats — [[d:paladin#1427819705929830410]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-15).
- **[general]** Judgement of Righteousness and Judgement of Command use melee hit/crit tables (not spell hit/crit); this was changed from spell-based to melee-based in "CC2" — [[d:paladin#1427822802299387985]] [[d:paladin#1427792436473757788]] (Jesse Potter/dusty, 2025-10-15).
- **[general]** Judgement base cooldown is 6s, reduced to 5s with Improved Judgement talent — [[d:paladin#1427823350931132568]] [[d:paladin#1427823405654343712]] [[d:paladin#1427823438097158144]] (Beldrin/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-15).
- **[general]** 1.18 raised HP/damage of many world mobs and dungeon packs, e.g. Deadwood/lasher-area mobs got noticeably "beefier" post-patch, harder to burst before OOM — [[d:paladin#1426589438858891315]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-10).
- **[Prot]** Force Reactive Disk changed/known to deal Nature damage, doesn't work on Nature-immune Lashers — [[d:paladin#1426566947729838123]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret]** Weapon-skill hit mechanics: cap changed such that "8 skill" requirement point moved; you need 8% +hit at 300 weapon skill to reach hit cap, every 5 weapon skill points above 315 gives +1% hit, weapon skill cap is 315 — [[d:paladin#1426574385812340766]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-10). Related: glancing blow no longer caps at 308 weapon skill; all scaling now linear with no breakpoints — [[d:paladin#1426575146818211964]] [[d:paladin#1426575352842551317]] (Dodging, 2025-10-10).
- **[Prot]** Devotion Aura's supporting Libram (used to give flat 5% damage reduction) was nerfed; Devo Aura now "pretty much unnoticeable," priest Champion buff can now provide the same 5% DR (at the cost of 5% more damage taken) — [[d:paladin#1426250760588361809]] [[d:paladin#1426252244881248276]] (dusty / angels1m, 2025-10-10).
- **[Holy]** Hyjal and Kara10 zone enchants were bugged (obtainable from wrong slots); some believe leftover enchants from the bug were never removed — [[d:paladin#1426104332025331833]] [[d:paladin#1426105261944475749]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret]** Crusade (T3.5-related buff?) party-stacking is bugged: party members can never get more than 1 stack of the buff and refresh timing is inconsistent — confirmed by 3-guild-member dummy test (does not increase cast/attack speed beyond intended, doesn't refresh correctly); dev (Lhian) says it's "not bugged, i checked" — disputed — [[d:paladin#1426891015121408062]] [[d:paladin#1426891232814043189]] [[d:paladin#1426889619072352256]] (Excinerus vs Lhian, 2025-10-11).
- **[era note]** Consecrate used to be a spammable AoE that ignored reduced-damage effects (used to almost-normally DPS C'Thun with 100+ Consecrate ticks instead of 12), implying later nerf — [[d:paladin#1426146713491603456]] [[d:paladin#1426146769057611847]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10).
- **[era note]** Paladin taunt (Judgement-based) used to be the sole ability exempt from needing melee hit for a taunt effect in "CC1" (Turtle content-cycle 1), later changed — [[d:paladin#1426099387225997413]] (Breaddy, 2025-10-10).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret]** Widely agreed Ret is currently the weakest of the 3 specs; considered an "afterthought"/aftershock spec, hurt by lack of raid utility beyond damage, mana issues, gear itemized without stamina — [[d:paladin#1426172414886281269]] [[d:paladin#1426855775044636805]] [[d:paladin#1426863329477001237]] (Mithreon/Jesse Potter/Excinerus, 2025-10-10/11; broad agreement, +2 reactions).
- **[Ret]** Two Ret builds exist: "AP Ret" (Seal of Command/SoC, early/entry build) and "Spellret"/"SP Ret" (Seal of Righteousness/SoR + spellpower gear + a proc weapon). Standard progression is start AP Ret, transition to SP Ret once you have tier gear and a proc weapon (Scythe of the Havester, Tempered Runeblade, or Shadowstrike) — [[d:paladin#1426617815959474187]] [[d:paladin#1426618359537205458]] [[d:paladin#1426618877797732432]] (bigdaddyd, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret]** SoR generally overtakes SoC in damage around ~300 spellpower, though "weapons matter a lot" and it's not a simple threshold — [[d:paladin#1426269692225392724]] (dusty, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret]** SoR/spellret's core power comes from a proc feedback loop with Windfury Totem (WFT): every SoR proc counts as an extra melee attack that can itself proc WFT, and WFT procs can trigger SoR again, raising effective WFT proc chance from a base ~25% up to 40%+ depending on haste; this loop (and reliance on it) is considered the central "brokenness"/balance problem of Ret, several posters say Ret won't get proper design attention "until the seal-WFT feedback loop is broken" — [[d:paladin#1426854001051435189]] [[d:paladin#1426879045022388347]] [[d:paladin#1426854949211340881]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-11; single-source theorycraft but detailed math, dev Lhian participated in thread).
- **[Ret]** Real parse example (turtlogs.com): a shaman-buffed Ret's 99s fight had 45 autos (18 WFT procs, 40%), 42 seal procs, 14 strikes; without WFT and Flurry-type totem buffs, DPS would drop from that log's total to ~1313.54 dps but the player would still place top 3-4 on the "TA" leaderboard — [[d:paladin#1426877047518789684]] [[d:paladin#1426878144341872692]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-11).
- **[Prot]** Prot Paladin considered strong for solo dungeon-clearing/farming ("absolute busted in dungeons... solo clear spec"), but for raid main-tanking, Bears (Feral Druids) are preferred over Prot Paladins due to higher stamina, better resource/threat management, and no 6s "threat ability gap" — Prot Paladins mostly relegated to off-tanking adds/trash unless the raid lacks a bear — [[d:paladin#1426255165236711454]] [[d:paladin#1426253456053895330]] [[d:paladin#1426253611251535897]] [[d:paladin#1426256116374835251]] (angels1m/dusty, 2025-10-10; broad agreement).
- **[Prot]** Prot Paladin threat/mitigation is heavily balanced around Sanctity Aura being present from a Ret in the group; without a Sanctity feeder, threat is ~30% lower and tanking becomes very difficult — [[d:paladin#1426700652427346142]] [[d:paladin#1426699979199746140]] [[d:paladin#1426700535536554116]] (dusty/Dodging/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-11; confirmed by multiple).
- **[Prot]** Prot's Crusader Strike is considered weak/pointless while tanking; several tanks say they "never use CS" and it "feels weird as a tank" — [[d:paladin#1426945483640934490]] [[d:paladin#1426952118476083406]] [[d:paladin#1426951891962695772]] (Guluguts/Sylvaira, 2025-10-11).
- **[Prot]** Prot is "not fine" per some — threat distribution is poor and the spec is balanced around mechanics (WFT) it doesn't have access to; others say Prot/Holy are fine and only need tuning, with Ret being the truly neglected spec — disputed — [[d:paladin#1426952861912269101]] vs [[d:paladin#1426951001109172366]] (dusty vs Southshore Crusader Vidnar, 2025-10-11).
- **[Holy]** Holy Paladin healing described as extremely easy/low-effort relative to reward ("most no-effort:big-return thing in the game"), largely just Flash of Light spam; considered the "most boring" spec by some — [[d:paladin#1426272914805887058]] [[d:paladin#1426273387059351624]] [[d:paladin#1426286492468121762]] (dusty, 2025-10-10).
- **[Holy]** No good "Holy DPS" spec exists currently; DPS without Vengeance (the Ret capstone-adjacent mechanic) is considered "a painful existence" — [[d:paladin#1426170440157757482]] [[d:paladin#1426170508046504017]] (Deleted User/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10).
- **[PvP]** Paladin in PvP is frequently described as best played as Prot/tank spec ("the class where the go-to spec for PvP is tank"); Ret is considered to lack identity in PvP (no interrupt, no gap closer beyond Repentance, generic stun) — [[d:paladin#1426127950113280002]] [[d:paladin#1426130237938339882]] (Southshore Crusader Vidnar, 2025-10-10).
- **[PvP]** Community perception: Paladins are seen by opponents as "OP" mainly because of Divine Shield/bubble, while the actual damage output is considered mediocre relative to Shaman/Rogue/Warlock burst; several posters argue this reputation is disproportionate — [[d:paladin#1426133934483312773]] (Jesse Potter, +4 reactions, 2025-10-10).

## Leveling
- **[general]** Pinned leveling-guide recommendation cited by community: go full Prot for beginners, or full Ret to reach 60 faster; time lost walking back from spirit healer is treated as a bigger loss than optimal spec choice — [[d:paladin#1426549590022291506]] [[d:paladin#1426550027140071557]] (Weiner johnson, 2025-10-10); Mithreon confirms Prot vs Ret leveling speed difference is not large ("not like you will save a week") — [[d:paladin#1426550953405845534]] (2025-10-10).
- **[leveling]** Holy Strike does not scale with weapon damage at low level; if getting hit hard by melee mobs at low level, swap to 1H+shield instead of a 2H, since Holy Strike damage floor makes weapon choice matter less for melee mitigation early — [[d:paladin#1426174266218516552]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10). Unclear (not leveled that patch by Jesse) at what level Crusader Strike surpasses Holy Strike with a 2H weapon — [[d:paladin#1426174454525984828]] (open question).
- **[leveling]** Seal choice while leveling: Seal of the Crusader (SotC) is "not *terrible*" until about level 18-20 when a 2H weapon is acquired, after which some switch to SoR; Jesse leveled with SotC until ~18 pre-Crusader-Strike/Holy-Strike changes so advice may differ now — [[d:paladin#1426356477454712862]] [[d:paladin#1426356909367099422]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10).
- **[leveling]** Lvl 8: don't overthink seal/judge rotation; using Seal of Righteousness (SoR) is "too much mana for too little benefit" on normal mobs at low level; never use Seal of Command to DPS — only use it for its Judgement — [[d:paladin#1426356200995426334]] [[d:paladin#1426356288169836595]] [[d:paladin#1426356402539987046]] (bigdaddyd, 2025-10-10).
- **[leveling]** Class quests: Redemption quest available at level 12 (a level-10 quest chain only sends you to Stormwind first) — [[d:paladin#1426408844023828591]] [[d:paladin#1426411942926487584]] (Linvain/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10). - **[leveling/AoE]** Lasher farming (Deadwood/lasher elementals) can start around level 51-52, ideally from 54; a starter build at low gear: `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=FoaAYAAQB-AoYFZbAAZY-` (Holy/Prot mix); Ret/Prot ("retprot") build outdamages deep Prot for farming after ~level 30 once Holy Shield is available, and becomes stronger with any Vampiric gear — [[d:paladin#1426529078080700417]] [[d:paladin#1426529338400047225]] [[d:paladin#1426535735980331080]] [[d:paladin#1426536664796237954]] [[d:paladin#1426537708804771860]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-10). ## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general]** Weapon-skill/hit interaction: need 8% +hit at 300 weapon skill to be hit-capped; every 5 weapon-skill points above 315 gives +1% hit; weapon skill cap for avoiding dodge is 315 — [[d:paladin#1426574385812340766]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-10). Being hit-capped matters beyond autos: missing with an auto also prevents SoR/SoC from landing (both require the melee swing to connect) — [[d:paladin#1426586815544168458]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-10).
- **[general]** Hit rating governs miss chance; weapon skill also affects dodge chance (separate from hit rating) — [[d:paladin#1426588534449504277]] [[d:paladin#1426588999060815922]] (DuckYi/Mithreon, 2025-10-10). For farming-tier gear, running 5% hit instead of the full 6% for more spellpower is considered acceptable ("alright... for more sp") — [[d:paladin#1426589277332176987]] [[d:paladin#1426592629512081498]] (DuckYi/Mithreon, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret SP]** SoR generally overtakes SoC around ~300 spellpower, though weapon choice matters a lot and there's no clean universal threshold — [[d:paladin#1426269692225392724]] (dusty, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret]** Faster weapon speed favored for Seal/Judgement of Wisdom procs (JoW) even though SoR's per-hit scaling is smaller on faster weapons — net frequency wins; damage difference between weapon speeds cited as small ("15-25 damage different from speed, if that") — [[d:paladin#1426085059613425684]] [[d:paladin#1426098579801374810]] [[d:paladin#1426098902855319643]] [[d:paladin#1426098979107639468]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret]** Haste is treated as itemized more expensive than crit, but for melee/AP Ret builds haste doesn't affect Strikes, Consecration, or Judgement cooldowns/damage directly (crit does, multiplicatively); for SP/SoR builds haste is estimated to drive 40-50% of damage, and up to 50-80% for AP builds via extra swings — a proposal (not implemented) to give Strikes cooldown-haste scaling was discussed to make haste itemization worth its cost — [[d:paladin#1426867604097728653]] [[d:paladin#1426867914388013086]] [[d:paladin#1426868642569519265]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-11; single-source theorycraft).
- **[Ret]** Crit for melee stacks multiplicatively across multiple crit-% bonus sources, then multiplied again by separate haste-bonus sources; white-hit cap is 55%, crit "ultimate cap" is treated as 100%; haste has no such hard cap — [[d:paladin#1426869967688826930]] [[d:paladin#1426870166347841659]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-11).
- **[Ret]** Community sentiment: Ret/Ele-Shaman-tier items are itemized with 0 stamina by design (to prevent PvP one-shots), leaving Ret conspicuously stamina-starved vs. e.g. Rogue rings with identical other stats but +10 stamina — specific comparison: Band of Redemption (Paladin, +20 Str/+15 Agi/1% speed) vs Bonescythe Ring (Rogue, +10 Sta/+20 Agi/+20 Str/1% hit) — [[d:paladin#1426327156295860275]] [[d:paladin#1426326814938235001]] (theoloras/warkrak, Jesse Potter, 2025-10-11).
- **[Ret]** Consecration and Seal of Righteousness both scale with spellpower, driving SP-Ret's AoE damage higher than SoC/AP-Ret's — [[d:paladin#1426829526502998066]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-11); exact SP-scaling coefficient of Consecration was asked but left unanswered in this chunk — [[d:paladin#1426884422686347305]] (Cat Petter, 2025-10-11; open question).
- **[general]** Armor from armor kits does not increase healing power via Ironclad (Ironclad is armor->something else, unclear exact mechanic, but confirmed armor kits themselves don't add to the Ironclad-scaling value) — [[d:paladin#1426332524384096419]] [[d:paladin#1426332995295645769]] (Ruth/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-11; example: gloves at 615 armor, 2% of 615 relevant, doesn't go up with kits).
- **[general]** Glancing blow mechanics clarified: glancing chance is fixed at ~40% regardless of weapon skill (does not reduce further past 315 skill); weapon skill instead reduces the *damage penalty* of a glancing blow, up to ~95% of a normal hit's damage at the weapon-skill cap (315); roughly 1 weapon-skill point = 0.2% hit — [[d:paladin#1427600075781046272]] [[d:paladin#1427601178270760960]] [[d:paladin#1427599702999957514]] [[d:paladin#1427672870225707300]] [[d:paladin#1427675396874436730]] (Excinerus/Fat Pibble with Air Pods/Jesse Potter/Dodging, 2025-10-15). Only weapon skill (not +hit rating) reduces glancing penalty — [[d:paladin#1427668584527564801]] [[d:paladin#1427672870225707300]] (Ali Fouad/Dodging, 2025-10-15). Recommended target: 315 weapon skill — [[d:paladin#1427675396874436730]] (Dodging, 2025-10-15).
- **[Ret]** Precise ability formulas posted by Excinerus: Seal of Command proc = 114.4% weapon damage + 0.34 spellpower, ignoring target armor; Crusader Strike = 132.87% weapon damage + 0.268 spellpower, subject to target armor — [[d:paladin#1427630261801193502]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-15; single source, precise numeric claim, unconfirmed by a dev in-chunk).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Ret/general]** Sanctity Aura is treated as mandatory for essentially every Ret build regardless of gear/spec (AP or SP), because a Prot tank in the same raid group loses ~30% threat without it; every Ret build is said to spend the same 11-point Holy-tree sink for it — [[d:paladin#1426699161788485723]] [[d:paladin#1426699235566424084]] [[d:paladin#1426699485366452315]] [[d:paladin#1426700652427346142]] (Jesse Potter/Dodging/dusty, 2025-10-11).
- **[Prot]** For raid tanking, Sanctuary Aura is not required, but either Vengeance or Sanctity is; if a Ret is providing Sanctity, the tank should take Vengeance instead — [[d:paladin#1427128630336552990]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-13).
- **[Prot]** Bulwark of Righteousness (Holy Shield-line capstone) is considered a weak/meme-tier capstone — best used as a "backup" when Holy Strike is dodged/parried, or as an emergency threat spike rather than a defensive cooldown; several posters note it was rarely taken even historically because it couldn't trigger retroactively and its threshold made it near-useless in practice — [[d:paladin#1426953613229428928]] [[d:paladin#1426953818800656568]] [[d:paladin#1426954026565763214]] [[d:paladin#1426954910292770866]] [[d:paladin#1427182052083761194]] [[d:paladin#1426956269842858056]] (dusty/Sylvaira/Dodging/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10/13).
- **[general]** Parry Haste: on parrying an attack, the melee swing timer resets and the character gets a near-instant follow-up hit; applies to both 1H and 2H weapons for Paladins (works differently/possibly not at all for dual-wielders); also triggered by the Reckoning talent; this is a universal combat mechanic (any class/NPC that can parry), not Paladin-specific — [[d:paladin#1426614573078810846]] [[d:paladin#1426615201498665132]] [[d:paladin#1426615322789679205]] [[d:paladin#1426617714478289068]] [[d:paladin#1426632461672124638]] [[d:paladin#1426631857071460382]] (Nehol/Mithreon/bigdaddyd/Marten, 2025-10-10; mob positioning tip: attack from behind so mob attacks don't parry-haste the tank).
- **[Prot]** Reckoning does not grant a true "extra attack" — it instead finishes the current swing and queues the next swing early (same underlying mechanic as parry haste); tooltip is considered misleading — [[d:paladin#1426631940022472884]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-11). Old Reckoning (pre-changes) required being crit to trigger, which made it self-defeating as tank gear improved (better-geared tanks get crit less) — [[d:paladin#1427512107452072068]] [[d:paladin#1427512328647213136]] (dusty, 2025-10-13).
- **[Prot threat]** Holy Strike is the primary threat/filler ability for Prot in dungeons and raids; Crusader Strike provides little threat value relative to its haste/block-value bonus and several experienced tanks recommend not using it at all until deep into T3 gear — [[d:paladin#1427367155753685043]] [[d:paladin#1427367488122781929]] [[d:paladin#1426945710984794152]] [[d:paladin#1426952118476083406]] (Infertile/Excinerus/Dodging, 2025-10-11/13).
- **[Prot AoE farm]** Blessing of Sanctuary + Judgement of Wisdom/Light + Seal of Wisdom/Light (swapped based on HP%/mana%) is the standard AoE-farm/leveling rotation; fast weapon + shield with block is recommended, Knightly Sword (AH) is an easy/cheap fast weapon usable until 60 — [[d:paladin#1427141208525770774]] [[d:paladin#1427142177338691674]] [[d:paladin#1427142377079963670]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-13).
- **[general]** SoR's Judgement (spell-type damage) mechanics vs. magic-immune targets: if the target is immune to magic, the Judgement will not deal damage, but it will still use melee hit/crit tables, will still proc weapon effects/enchants, will NOT trigger Grounding Totem, will NOT proc "on harmful spell hit" effects, but WILL proc Spellstrike items — [[d:paladin#1427791869382885376]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-15; detailed single-source breakdown, described in-thread as emblematic of Paladin "jank").
- **[Prot]** Blessing/Aura/Seal management note: Auras and Blessings remain on the global cooldown (unlike some other classes' equivalents); several veteran players argue these should be baseline/free — [[d:paladin#1426731085429080168]] [[d:paladin#1427819393550647397]] (dusty/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-11/15).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Prot]** Sanctity Aura is functionally required for Prot threat: without a Sanctity feeder (self or a Ret in group), Prot loses roughly 30% threat and "will never hold anything" — confirmed by multiple experienced tanks after observing PUG failures with Devotion Aura substituted — [[d:paladin#1426700652427346142]] [[d:paladin#1426701139726041220]] [[d:paladin#1426701573114957946]] (dusty/Dodging, 2025-10-11).
- **[Prot]** Devotion Aura is weak/negligible for mitigation post-nerf (its supporting Libram's flat 5% DR was removed); mainly useful now only when "feeding" (buffing) a co-tanking Bear/Druid — [[d:paladin#1426250760588361809]] [[d:paladin#1426251119751069706]] (dusty, 2025-10-10).
- **[Prot]** Do not stack Defense to the 440 crush-immunity cap on Prot Paladin; prioritize stamina/armor since Prot's HP pool is inherently weak ("no stam, bad dodge, shit armor, our best form of mit is fucking block") — aim for ~102.4% avoidance with Holy Shield active instead — [[d:paladin#1427381998514864334]] [[d:paladin#1427382120631763089]] [[d:paladin#1427379996413919445]] (Destroses/dusty/Excinerus, 2025-10-13).
- **[Prot]** Crusader Strike provides little tanking value (small haste + block-value bonus) versus Holy Strike's direct threat; multiple tanks recommend removing it from the action bar entirely until very high gear (full T3) — [[d:paladin#1427767695465517107]] [[d:paladin#1426945710984794152]] [[d:paladin#1426952118476083406]] (Lazepally/Guluguts/Sylvaira/Excinerus, 2025-10-10/15).
- **[Prot leveling tank tip]** For dungeon tanking while leveling: body-pull, drop Consecration, spam Holy Strike, use Crusader Strike only to maintain its buff stacks, Seal of Wisdom for mana; to snap AoE aggro fast, Holy-Strike the non-primary target and taunt the primary back rather than trying to out-threat instant AoE pulls (compare to warrior Sweeping-Strikes-into-Whirlwind snap threat) — [[d:paladin#1427767321119690782]] [[d:paladin#1427768478483611708]] [[d:paladin#1427768512734036250]] [[d:paladin#1427769071138640003]] (Ash/Lazepally, 2025-10-15).
- **[Prot]** Improved Righteous Fury is recommended over early Sanctity Aura investment while leveling/tanking dungeons pre-60 — [[d:paladin#1427770148072325192]] [[d:paladin#1427770743831400579]] (Ravenhost/gastro, 2025-10-15).
- **[Prot]** Weapon-skill upgrades ("the Book," +5 to a chosen weapon skill via DM class book quest + 250g) mainly help hit-cap ease, but matter little for Prot overall since Prot isn't as punished by glancing blows as Warriors are (autos ≈0.5% of Prot's threat) — [[d:paladin#1427532002487439441]] [[d:paladin#1427534352551641168]] [[d:paladin#1427689614247985192]] [[d:paladin#1427367197130358864]] (dusty/angels1m/Caffeine, 2025-10-13/15).
- **[Prot solo/farm]** For solo Prot farming (non-raid), best bet is Prot pre-raid BiS + 10%+ Vampiric gear ("Vengeprot"); for fastest possible clears go heavier Vengeprot/Vampiric with bigger pulls — [[d:paladin#1427727501387370668]] [[d:paladin#1427728414441341010]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-13).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** No hard cap on healing bonus/spellpower for Holy Paladins ("no cap except what gear numerically limits you to") — full T3 (8-piece) is best for fights requiring heavy dispelling rather than pure healing throughput; 5-6 piece T3 + 3-piece T2 is often preferred otherwise; Viscidus specifically rewards 8-piece T3 — [[d:paladin#1427315096648024064]] [[d:paladin#1427315120412823715]] [[d:paladin#1427315121369124866]] (Mithreon/Dodging, 2025-10-13).
- **[Holy]** Daybreak (proc from melee Holy Strike healing) and Illumination are both expected by community members to get nerfed eventually; melee-holy playstyle ("Holy Strike spam for crit%/Daybreak uptime") is seen by some as having crowded out the previous, more varied melee-holy rotation (Mending Light + Holy Shock resets) that existed pre-1.18 — [[d:paladin#1427751892934725886]] [[d:paladin#1427744723619020870]] [[d:paladin#1427755476971556988]] [[d:paladin#1427760246834725074]] (Mithreon/Illithris, 2025-10-14/15; described as going from a ~30/70 CS/HS-in-melee-healing ratio pre-1.18 to ~10/90 in 1.18). Countered by Sookie: melee holy still requires choosing between Holy Strike and resetting Holy Shock depending on whether damage is spread or concentrated, so it isn't purely "spam HS" — disputed — [[d:paladin#1427756270311571589]] (Sookie, 2025-10-15).
- **[Holy]** Melee-healing (Holy Strike based) is strong specifically because of the Daybreak proc interaction, letting a melee-holy paladin heal in melee range with plate/int/spellcrit gear instead of normal caster-healer itemization; considered by some a "dumb interaction" that shouldn't define the spec, by others (Sookie) as legitimate strong utility — disputed — [[d:paladin#1427744723619020870]] [[d:paladin#1427751910005412052]] [[d:paladin#1427754160035594280]] (Illithris vs Sookie, 2025-10-15).
- **[Holy]** Pure ranged-caster Holy healing can clear Kara40 with Flash of Light spam alone; melee-holy (without T3.5) is considered mainly useful for log-pushing/lower tiers rather than being required — [[d:paladin#1427752206077263912]] [[d:paladin#1427752315796062350]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-15).
- **[Holy]** Mp5 (Mana per 5) is a good stat pickup for Holy but not for Prot or Ret — [[d:paladin#1427702718113386629]] [[d:paladin#1427702792998617250]] (Deleted User/dusty, 2025-10-13).
- **[Holy]** Downranking guidance (recap/expand from earlier chunk): in raids you almost never downrank; higher crit means more Illumination mana return, which can reduce mana-pot reliance further; Holy Shock and 1.5s Holy Light should stay max rank as emergency heals, but Holy Light can be downranked to ~rank 6 for sustained spam on fights with long, predictable tank-damage intervals — [[d:paladin#1426451030203174986]] [[d:paladin#1426451170905296906]] [[d:paladin#1426451644501065738]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-10).
- **[Holy]** Eye of the Dead trinket procs off Holy Strike and each Daybreak proc during its buff window, but Holy Strike consumes a charge regardless of whether the strike itself lands as a heal — considered a design flaw (charge should ideally only consume on a successful heal) — [[d:paladin#1426489313482248292]] [[d:paladin#1426489438598463500]] [[d:paladin#1426489595171700776]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-10).

Has heavy physical damage-reduction (~50%) that punishes melee; final 10-20% of the fight is commonly just burned/ignored via cooldowns (Restoration Potion strat, Bubble/BoP on casters) rather than played mechanically — [[d:paladin#1427391123244056606]] [[d:paladin#1427391696118743100]] [[d:paladin#1427776954047791235]] [[d:paladin#1427777054077882420]] (Breaddy/Excinerus/dusty, 2025-10-13/15). - **[Kara40 boss: Sartura]** Considered "jank": can be stunned mid-fight and then taunted for reduced (confusing) threat interaction — [[d:paladin#1426991057320677566]] [[d:paladin#1427770213566517340]] (Mithreon/Ravenhost, 2025-10-11/13).
- **[Kara40 boss: Chess event]** Considered one of the best-designed fights ("bis fight in there") because multiple people can make mistakes without an instant wipe, unlike other Kara40 mechanics — [[d:paladin#1427393047255322774]] (Breaddy, 2025-10-13). Decursing on this boss is possible but must stop once he returns from the add phase; alternatively use Freedom/Cleanse-Poison abuse ("FAP") to avoid the mechanic depending on raid DPS — [[d:paladin#1427395113432252437]] [[d:paladin#1427395169807896646]] (Breaddy, 2025-10-13).
- **[Kara40 boss: Rupturan]** Considered a "fuck you melee" fight pre-changes; a Felheart-set-interaction change reportedly reduced this issue — [[d:paladin#1427391633724407861]] (Breaddy, 2025-10-13). Every AoE tank scenario in Rupturan reportedly cannot actually be handled solo by a Prot Paladin (used as evidence Prot is not truly a viable "AoE tank" for the hardest add-heavy fights) — [[d:paladin#1426253611251535897]] [[d:paladin#1426253756961652888]] (dusty, 2025-10-10).

A well-optimized run is cited at sub-2h to sub-5min-per-lasher-farm-adjacent efficiency benchmarks depending on group; a "good" Kara40 run is described as landing in the 1:30-1:50 range — [[d:paladin#1427392867785117860]] (Breaddy, 2025-10-13).
- **[Lasher farming instance ("DME")]** Full lasher-farm route for a geared Prot: bubble into a 3-pack, sideways-gather 2 more nearby packs, use a Speed potion to grab a 4th far pack and return (route "3-2-3", ~7 min); alternatively pull all packs on one side via patrol timing then finish the remaining 2, targeting sub-5-min; a "full jump" run (Lashers > elemental boss > satyr boss upstairs > last boss) squeezes in extra loot/mining nodes — [[d:paladin#1426583425686700243]] [[d:paladin#1426584534102708254]] [[d:paladin#1426585014258368664]] [[d:paladin#1427716354978484377]] (Mithreon/Beldrin, 2025-10-10/13). - **[general instance note]** Turtle WoW has added anti-farming mechanics to some traditional farm dungeons (e.g. ZF, Cathedral, possibly Maraudon) to prevent giant AoE pulls — [[d:paladin#1426765897082867922]] (Dodging, 2025-10-10; unconfirmed for Maraudon specifically).
- **[Stratholme]** Dream's Herald weapon is considered "huge" for farming Strat (heals from its DoT for effective damage reduction); can stand in the corner of the gate area and pull using a hunter's pet from a 2nd account/window — [[d:paladin#1426766098317180998]] [[d:paladin#1426766343708872704]] [[d:paladin#1427305261353275464]] (Dodging/Mithreon, 2025-10-10/13). - **[LBRS]** "Jed" (a specific mob, possibly a rare/quest NPC) can be selectively pulled by targeting through the door and resetting if not present ("open door, target, reset if no Jed") — used to farm the "Reed" trinket — [[d:paladin#1427741841222008923]] [[d:paladin#1427741957848825938]] [[d:paladin#1427742037146341387]] [[d:paladin#1427742055505072361]] (Key/gastro/Beldrin, 2025-10-13).

## PvP
- **[PvP general]** Community perception vs. reality: opponents commonly overstate Paladin PvP power (attributing to Paladins abilities/behaviors they don't have, e.g. "on-demand no-side-effect Divine Shield," "double stun and instaheal," "free full-HP heal for 0 mana," a 1-button one-shot) — Jesse Potter compiled a list of misconceptions and rebuttals (e.g., Lay on Hands costs your full current mana and has a much longer cooldown than comparable Druid/Shaman heals; Repentance breaks on damage unlike most fears, and is single-target/60s-cd vs. Psychic Scream's multi-target/45s-cd) — [[d:paladin#1426799607970660444]] [[d:paladin#1426799889899196467]] [[d:paladin#1426799982295650425]] [[d:paladin#1426800158305419428]] [[d:paladin#1426800267588014100]] [[d:paladin#1426800359648788592]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret PvP]** Seal of Command + Holy Shock reset ("fight club spec") is considered strong but underused/underrated in PvP: burst rotation is SoC autos/crits until target is low, Holy Shock to finish via its reset mechanic; risk is going OOM fast — [[d:paladin#1426118845046849576]] [[d:paladin#1426119235922563196]] [[d:paladin#1426119339089727588]] [[d:paladin#1426119473072570410]] (Domnall/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret PvP]** Against multiple Rogues (common Alliance PvP matchup complaint): standard defensive sequence is trinket the Kidney Shot, Bubble/BoP, Repentance and hope they trinket it, Hammer of Justice or wait for Evasion to end then HoJ; without Repentance available (e.g. missed/resisted), kill chance vs. coordinated Rogues drops significantly — [[d:paladin#1426122717635285005]] [[d:paladin#1426122938066665523]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret PvP]** Seal of Justice (SoJ) is a niche but real PvP tool vs. caster/healer targets: procs a 2s stun that doesn't share diminishing returns with other stun types; suggested opener vs. a healing/caster target is SoJ up, swap to SoC, Judge for double proc-chance burst, swap back to SoJ — [[d:paladin#1426722719579377674]] [[d:paladin#1426723808445989067]] [[d:paladin#1426723987551031336]] [[d:paladin#1426726513239392336]] [[d:paladin#1426726771910512792]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-11; niche use, low proc rate).
- **[Ret PvP build]** Community-shared PvP Ret talent link (from SuperDavid): `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/paladin?points=FAaAB--AoQYFKYYFIFAB` (4 unspent points); found Sanctity Aura underwhelming for PvP, considering Improved Devotion -> Precision instead — [[d:paladin#1426481689676349544]] (SuperDavid, 2025-10-10; open/unresolved).
- **[PvP]** Pursuit of Justice (movement-speed talent) is considered to need a buff to ~15% speed to have real PvP impact, since it must beat the common 8% boot-speed-enchant baseline by a meaningful margin to matter — [[d:paladin#1427425649752150036]] [[d:paladin#1426155094855913473]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-10/11; wishlist/contested, not implemented).
- **[PvP]** T3/T3.5 Ret gear intentionally carries ~0 Stamina, widely believed to be a deliberate anti-one-shot-PvP design choice, unlike e.g. Elemental Shaman gear which reportedly keeps full stamina value on equivalent slots — [[d:paladin#1426326814938235001]] [[d:paladin#1426326943023759505]] (Jesse Potter/Excinerus, 2025-10-11; community grievance, disputed as unfair vs. other hybrid classes).
- **[PvP]** Paladin cleanse removes Poison-type effects unconditionally, while comparable Horde tools (only certain totems/Druids) are more restricted, and physical CC (e.g. Blind) is untouched by cleanse — cited as a lopsided Alliance-favoring mechanic by some — [[d:paladin#1427780079966224407]] [[d:paladin#1427780125298266223]] [[d:paladin#1427780155882864741]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-15).
- **[PvP]** A Horde-only self-damaging "pizza" food item is used to break Repentance/CC without a trinket, seen as a significant counter unavailable to Alliance without cross-faction trade — [[d:paladin#1426782152191905832]] [[d:paladin#1427523954624696420]] [[d:paladin#1427524037323788339]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-11/13).
