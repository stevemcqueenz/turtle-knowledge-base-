## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret]** Both SoR (spellret) and SoC (AP ret) are viable; SoR outperforms SoC on boss fights by roughly 10% overall, while SoC is better while leveling since SP can't be stacked early enough for SoR to pull ahead — [[d:paladin#1448259974957367461]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-10); [[d:paladin#1448260053181136937]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-10).
- **[Ret]** Raid-log comparison (Jesse Potter): SoR beats SoC in nearly every damage category except auto-attacks, where SoC's ~200k more auto-damage over a raid "almost evens them out" but SoR still finishes ahead overall (e.g. 289k SoR vs 245k SoC seal-damage totals) — [[d:paladin#1449409823530684617]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-13; single detailed source, not disputed).
- **[Ret]** "Necrodin" (Kara Crypt "Possessions of the Lost" set + double 3pc T2 spellstrike + proc items) is a strong niche AP build, close second to SoR on the main server, but underperforms on very fast (<50s) kills or fights where Sigil can't be used — [[d:paladin#1449746207583899649]] (Adamant, 2025-12-14); [[d:paladin#1449746754802155662]] (Excinerus, 2025-12-14).
- **[Ret]** Severe AP-ret underperformance reports (e.g. losing to a level-56 all-green hunter) are diagnosed by the community as player/gear mistakes, not inherent unviability — current AP ret still does 2.5-4x vanilla ret's ~750 dps baseline — [[d:paladin#1449521874806505512]] (Beldrin, 2025-12-14); [[d:paladin#1449522110908207269]] (Grey, 2025-12-14).
- **[Prot]** Community sentiment: prot paladin is behind Feral (bear) tanks, who are seen as strictly better than Warriors and beating Paladin/Shaman tanks on what matters, while running full DPS gear (~12k HP, ~20k armor, ~40% crit) — [[d:paladin#1448376620074139812]] (Grey, 2025-12-11; community consensus, no pushback).
- **[Prot]** Prot paladin lacks a cleave and AoE taunt, relies on Consecration/Holy Shield/Sanctuary's "minor AoE," and gets no rage-on-hit — termed the "hybrid tax" — [[d:paladin#1447908095182246020]] (Excinerus, 2025-12-09).
- **[Holy]** Holy paladin's perceived overpowered status is tied heavily to T3.5 gear specifically; at T3 + BiS offspec (no T3.5), Holy and Resto Shaman come out roughly equal — [[d:paladin#1449676266247819306]] (Mioko, 2025-12-14); [[d:paladin#1449671820390174852]] (Faithroll, 2025-12-14).
- **[Ret/DPS general]** "Is DPS pally on Turtle WoW playable?" — general consensus: "compared to classic, insane... playable"; high effort, won't top meters in serious groups, but works well enough — [[d:paladin#1450155378452201585]] (Hausa, 2025-12-15); [[d:paladin#1450155601622728796]] (Silverhand, 2025-12-15).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Prot]** Core threat loop: Holy Strike + Judgement of Righteousness immediately on pull, refresh Seal of Righteousness ASAP, then Exorcism, then Consecration whenever it won't delay the above — [[d:paladin#1448378456915054826]] (Grey, 2025-12-11).
- **[Prot]** "Rip Crusader [Strike] off the bar" — CS generates negligible threat for prot ("does dick-all... completely useless") and should essentially never be used to build/regain threat; only marginally useful on big AoE dungeon pulls to refresh the Zeal/Righteous-Strikes Block-Value buff — [[d:paladin#1448371720305119352]] (Arle, 2025-12-10); [[d:paladin#1449054135532912720]] (Grey, 2025-12-13); [[d:paladin#1449027627787616417]] (Grey, 2025-12-12).
- **[Prot]** Concrete comparison: Holy Strike ≈900-1000 non-crit / ≈2.66k baseline threat vs Crusader Strike ≈300-460 / ≈300 baseline threat; rule of thumb — compute how long you'd need to auto-attack to make up the gap before ever pressing CS (Grey's own case: ~51 seconds) — [[d:paladin#1448373074553602151]] (Grey, 2025-12-10); [[d:paladin#1449055288668717186]] (Grey, 2025-12-13).
- **[Prot]** Never use Crusader Strike as prot for a different reason too: it's physical damage, while prot's threat scales off Holy damage, so Spellpower (not weapon damage) is the real threat stat — [[d:paladin#1448075789508808755]] (angels1m, 2025-12-10).
- **[Prot]** Opener: Holy Strike → Judgement of Righteousness → pick next Seal — [[d:paladin#1448075879384088659]] (angels1m, 2025-12-10).
- **[Ret]** Reference "healthy" damage-source breakdown (Jesse Potter): ~40-45% Autos, 15% Crusader Strike, 10-15% Seal of Command, 8% Judgement of Command, remainder split across Consecration/Drain Life/Exorcism/Repentance/Flametongue procs/Holy Strike/consumables — [[d:paladin#1449516189494804532]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-14).
- **[Ret]** SoC mechanic: cast Seal of Command on self, re-judge it 2-3s before expiry (as in classic); wearing spellstrike gear and landing on a JoW target counts as an extra damage instance for JoW/WoC/Vengeance, partially offsetting the seal-management overhead — [[d:paladin#1448262331598635109]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-10).
- **[Ret]** SoC (AP ret) mana is generally fine unless the fight is extremely long or the raid kills very fast; using zero spellstrikes with Consecration-on-CD + Exorcism + tea/pots works for high performers — [[d:paladin#1449760214764097777]] (angels1m, 2025-12-14); [[d:paladin#1449760484189409331]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-14).
- **[Ret]** On-the-fly SoC mana fix for TRASH only (not standard boss advice): swap to Seal of Wisdom for 2-3 Holy-Strike-landed hits to regain mana at a DPS cost — [[d:paladin#1449761431237955654]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-14; flagged by others as trash-only, not blanket advice).
- **[Ret]** Server-dependent rotation nuance: on fast/behind-content Ambershire, use Holy Strike mainly on the opener (short fights, low Sunder stacks); on the main server, check logs of top players for exact rotation since fight length/gear differ — [[d:paladin#1448576616291897434]] (Faithroll, 2025-12-11).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot]** For multi-mob threat: Holy Shield + Consecration, swap in Seal of Wisdom for mana management, spam Holy Strike once threat is secure; never Crusader Strike — [[d:paladin#1448075789508808755]] (angels1m, 2025-12-10).
- **[Prot]** Righteous Strikes (Zeal-stack talent) gives +10% Block Value per Zeal stack (100 BV → 130 BV at 3 stacks); standard play is maintain 3 stacks and spam Holy Strike, using Crusader Strike only to refresh the Zeal buff on big AoE pulls — [[d:paladin#1449027164967145483]] (Grey, 2025-12-12); [[d:paladin#1449027627787616417]] (Grey, 2025-12-12).
- **[Prot]** Mana-efficient triple-pull plan: Holy Strike+JoR locks mob 1 until dead; Taunt+Seal of Righteousness locks mob 2; Hand of Justice stun holds mob 3 long enough to build threat on mob 2 and let Taunt come off CD — total cost cited at 346 mana — [[d:paladin#1448984963306491924]] (Grey, 2025-12-12).
- **[Prot]** Aura swap for AoE: switching to Retribution Aura for heavy AoE pulls helps skip using Crusader Strike for threat; 10% flat threat/damage judged better than a smaller flat periodic-damage aura for single-target — [[d:paladin#1448463718798065748]] (Grey, 2025-12-11); [[d:paladin#1448464123342880960]] (Domy, 2025-12-11).
- **[Ret]** Spellret/hybrid-ret AoE (Consecration/Exorcism-based) is worth it for heavy AoE fights since it doesn't need a food buff and can be refreshed mid-combat, unlike some AP tools — [[d:paladin#1448081400665473095]] (Excinerus, 2025-12-10).
- **[Prot]** Excinerus's proposed (unconfirmed) SoC AoE/mana fix: make the SoC proc a flat 50% chance and split its damage into 2 hits like Windfury, instead of PPM-scaling with weapon speed — [[d:paladin#1449517887424430225]] (Excinerus, 2025-12-14; wishlist, not implemented).
- **[Prot]** Block Value's value scales hard with hit count: a "+30% BV" effect is nearly irrelevant on single-target but can be 50-100% effective damage reduction on large AoE pulls (e.g. full ZG wing pulls) — [[d:paladin#1448373560535027814]] (Silverhand, 2025-12-11); [[d:paladin#1448373826185330741]] (Grey, 2025-12-11).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Defense→avoidance conversion: 8 Defense = 1.28% total avoidance (0.16% per point) — NOT the commonly misremembered "8 Defense = 1%," which is actually dual-wield-mob logic affecting only dodge/parry/miss, not block — [[d:paladin#1448972595528601642]] (Grey, 2025-12-12); [[d:paladin#1448972742434230344]] (Grey, 2025-12-12).
- **[Prot]** "Defense by itself isn't a benchmark worth considering... it's just a function of total avoidance" — track total avoidance % via a stats addon instead of chasing raw Defense — [[d:paladin#1449051831811571873]] (Grey, 2025-12-14).
- **[Prot]** Defense/crush-immunity actually only matters as a hard requirement in ONE fight: Twin Emperors (AQ40), where the "Unbalancing Strike" debuff drops avoidance ~16 percentage points (e.g. 118.4%→102.4%); elsewhere just maximize threat/DPS while staying "reasonably tanky" — [[d:paladin#1448462297507954845]] (Sylvaira, 2025-12-11); [[d:paladin#1448462613003636758]] (Grey, 2025-12-11).
- **[Prot]** Avoidance-cap targets by raid: BWL needs NO specific cap (102.4% only helps vs Fankriss's worms, which can be stunned/kited instead); AQ40 needs 118.4% to stay crush-immune after Unbalancing Strike, and reaching it generally requires full Naxx/Kara-BiS mitigation gear — [[d:paladin#1448971232912478208]] (Grey, 2025-12-12); [[d:paladin#1449003621533483052]] (Grey, 2025-12-12).
- **[Prot]** Concrete 102.4%-avoidance gear/buff breakdown (BWL): 3% Jewelcrafting, 4% K10 helm/leg enchant, 1% cape enchant, 3.4% Mongoose+Scorpok+MotW+Kings buffs, 0.8% buckle, 2% shield-block enchant — leaving only ~23.2% (or ~20% with 3/3 Anticipation) needed from raw gear; key items: Onyxia Blood Talisman, Girdle of the Warden, Leggings of the Elite Guard/Flamewaker Legplates, Boots of Avoidance/Temporal Bronze Boots, Medallion of Steadfast Might, DM Paladin trinket, (if ZG) Overlord's Onyx Band+Crimson set (5.52% combined), Bloodsoaked Gauntlets, Aegis of the Blood God — [[d:paladin#1448969367747428415]] (Grey, 2025-12-12).
- **[Prot]** Concrete 118.4%-avoidance gear combo (AQ40): Jadestone Helmet/Helm of Domination + dodge enchant (4.82-5.86%), Master Dragonslayer's Medallion + block enchant (2.82%), Overlord's Onyx Band+Crimson Band + block enchant (7.6%), Onyxia Blood Talisman (3.14%), Styleen's (7.08%), Elementium Threaded Cape (3%), Chocolate Fish (1.64%) = 30.1% baseline, plus T2 belt/gloves/bracers/shoulders/boots/chest/pants; Aegis of the Blood God helps "cheese" the cap if unlucky elsewhere — [[d:paladin#1448977166011334706]] (Grey, 2025-12-12).
- **[Prot]** Styleen's (trinket) gives 5% flat block + 13 defense-equivalent points (2.08%) = 7.08% total combined avoidance — [[d:paladin#1449002775869653064]] (Grey, 2025-12-12).
- **[Ret]** Hit-cap mechanics: prot needs 8% total hit (3% talents + 1% skill book + 4% gear) vs level-63 bosses; Humans need only 3% from gear thanks to racial weapon skill; going above 315 weapon skill no longer grants bonus crit (patched out on Turtle), though the dodge-reduction benefit past 315 is unconfirmed — [[d:paladin#1450135493386703021]] (Destroses, 2025-12-15); [[d:paladin#1448580606710513805]] (Destroses, 2025-12-11).
- **[Prot]** Real-world hit tolerance: Silverhand runs 2-3% under hit cap "basically forever" with Holy Strike misses being a "complete non-issue"; Dodging tanks MC/BWL/K10 with ~4% miss regularly; hit matters comparatively MORE on pre-raid gear (few total stats per piece) than on raid gear — [[d:paladin#1450137134504743075]] (Silverhand, 2025-12-15); [[d:paladin#1450137095296389351]] (Dodging, 2025-12-15).
- **[Prot]** Dungeons: OK to run 1-2% under hit cap; Raids as MT: aim for full hit cap (missing costs real threat); as OT can run up to ~7% under depending on the fight — [[d:paladin#1450132383419338932]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-15); [[d:paladin#1450132467347623956]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-15).
- **[Ret/Prot]** SoC (Seal of Command) spell-power scaling coefficient measured at ~0.24; Crusader Strike carries a 20% SP coefficient — [[d:paladin#1448139285592146074]] (Excinerus, 2025-12-10); [[d:paladin#1448575456206127206]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-11).
- **[Holy]** A Holy-spell SP scaling cap is referenced at 43% ("an odd number to cap scaling at"); ~1000 SP on a tank in a raid setting is already considered an "absurd" amount with diminishing returns beyond that — [[d:paladin#1448201187022082201]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-10); [[d:paladin#1448201252122005617]] (tripleXDengine, 2025-12-10).

- **[Kara10, Clawfang]** Stack-based taunt swap at 20 stacks between co-tanks (wait out the ~10s taunt CD between swaps); resisted taunts do happen; Bubble (Divine Shield) can shed your own stacks, or reset via Bubble+instant-cancel+re-taunt if willing to risk it — [[d:paladin#1448985069954793512]] (Grey, 2025-12-12); [[d:paladin#1448989187964403773]] (Vik, 2025-12-12).
- **[AQ20]** Main damage threats are Ossirian and the Raptor/Tiger bosses — manageable with attentive healing — [[d:paladin#1448462237986455632]] (Grey, 2025-12-11).
- **[AQ40, Incantagos]** See Cooldowns/threat section above (Blessing-spam pull tactic; 4 stacks of 25% damage reduction on pull).
- **[Black Morass]** Two caster trash mobs before the bridge land a "sand-HoJ" stun on the tank ~30s into the pull if not killed/CC'd first — focus casters first; dragon-type trash/bosses hit very hard, bring mitigation gear + CC liberally; roughly MC-equivalent difficulty, tuned like an unofficial "heroic"; helpful comp is a Priest (Fortitude) + Druid (Sleep) — [[d:paladin#1448464751389577439]] (Grey, 2025-12-11); [[d:paladin#1448465414249255032]] (Grey, 2025-12-11); [[d:paladin#1448466675790909510]] (Grey, 2025-12-11).
- **[Stormwind Vault]** Comparatively easier than Black Morass; only the last boss can be a slog if the group doesn't know it — [[d:paladin#1448468876454006846]] (Destroses, 2025-12-11).
- **[Kara Crypt]** With 0.5-tier gear + Tempered Runeblade, Ret loot priority: "Fist of the Forgotten Order" first, then "Mind's Key"; Kara Crypt has great tank/healing gear but comparatively little for Ret — [[d:paladin#1448526983276531794]] (KO.NC, 2025-12-11); [[d:paladin#1448530086704844882]] (keungzhe, 2025-12-11).
- **[K40 general]** Grey finds Kara40 "not very fun to tank" — too many make-work mechanics and unnecessary tacked-on adds — [[d:paladin#1447998485184905278]] (Grey, 2025-12-09).
- **[Dire Maul lashers]** Can power-level a friend through Lasher farming without them pulling aggro if they have innate invisibility, or are at least level 52 — [[d:paladin#1450436366017101835]] (Trojh, 2025-12-16); [[d:paladin#1450437723134365727]] (Raugtreefell, 2025-12-16).

## PvP
- **[Holy]** For a Rank-14 shield-transmog goal, Holy is the clear recommendation over Ret; R14 grinding effectively forces AV regardless of BG preference — [[d:paladin#1448556717636980866]] (Faithroll, 2025-12-11).
- **[General]** T2.5 is obtainable in ~6 weeks of raiding — judged by the community as a better time investment than grinding to PvP Rank 12/13 gear — [[d:paladin#1450306272938299412]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-16).
- **[Holy]** Holy Shock's damage was fully removed in an earlier patch for PvP balance, briefly restored after pushback, then reduced again ("only a short period where Shock and SoC returned and then damage was removed and reduced"); reason: Auto+SoC+CS+Judgement+Shock combined was "too much instant burst" (the "8k Holy Strike era") — [[d:paladin#1450310427731820675]] (Adamant, 2025-12-16); [[d:paladin#1450310660025090100]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-16).
- **[General sentiment]** Most regulars in this channel are PvE-focused and are annoyed by nerfs to paladin abilities driven purely by PvP balance concerns — [[d:paladin#1450312310257090705]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-16).
- **[Confirmed not planned]** Seal of Blood (a TBC seal) will not be added to Turtle, per Lhian — [[d:paladin#1447850529433718937]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-09). Turtle's design explicitly avoids rewarding seal-twisting — [[d:paladin#1447865702684950569]] (gastro, 2025-12-09).
- **[Prot]** Racial note: Human vs Elf choice makes only ~0.1% difference for non-Ret paladins; matters more specifically for Ret — [[d:paladin#1447935390563434610]] (angels1m, 2025-12-09).
