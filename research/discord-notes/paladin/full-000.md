## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret]** Two Ret sub-builds exist: AP/Strength Ret using Seal of Command (SoC), and Spellpower Ret using Seal of Righteousness (SoR); SoR/spellret is generally regarded as doing more damage once geared (T0.5+), while SoC is simpler/cheaper early and can spike on fast trash/AoE — [[d:paladin#1423773591908126854]], [[d:paladin#1423676609600426066]] (Excinerus/Jesse Potter/Beldrin, 2025-10-03/05).
- **[Ret]** Excinerus's blunt framing: gear like an Arms Warrior early doing ~80% of an AFK Arms Warrior's dps, get T0.5 (D2), then "trash all previous gear and gear like a caster" to eventually match an AFK Fury Warrior — describes Ret ceiling as still below top DPS specs even fully spellstrike-geared — [[d:paladin#1423771995375538258]] thru [[d:paladin#1423772172454989936]] (2025-10-04; single source but detailed, unopposed in thread).
- **[Ret/threat]** Consensus: Paladin (any spec) threat generation cannot keep up with top-tier DPS ("not like a bear"); mid-tier DPS groups feel fine — [[d:paladin#1423728122599178271]], [[d:paladin#1423728643141664828]] (Proph, 2025-10-03).
- **[Prot]** Prot Paladins compared unfavorably to Bear Druids for tanking survivability/soak in current gear ("15k armor druid" out-tanking Prot); multiple complaints that Paladin plate tanks feel squishier than bear/warrior tanks — [[d:paladin#1423730574689964052]], [[d:paladin#1423730973912203376]], [[d:paladin#1424200222371741786]] (2025-10-03; recurring theme, several agree).
- **[Holy/Shockadin]** Holy/Prot hybrid ("Holyprot" / "Shockadin") is viable for solo PvP survivability and dungeon soloing/farming ("sticky and hard to kill") though not for actually killing anything — [[d:paladin#1423413036584276019]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-03).
- **[PvE]** Necrodin build mentioned as a niche use for Cloak/HoJ combo, otherwise considered "cargo culting" by some — [[d:paladin#1424159666144808960]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-05; single source, disputed).
- **[Ret]** There is no third distinct "spellret" spec — endgame Ret is fundamentally a choice between Seal of Righteousness (SoR, spell-power scaling) and Seal of Command (SoC, AP/Strength scaling); "spellret" as a community term has become roughly synonymous with SoR/hybrid builds — [[d:paladin#1426047501638565940]], [[d:paladin#1426048208483782656]] (Jesse Potter/Dodging, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret]** SoR vs SoC performance is close for non-parsing/average players; among top parsers on one server snapshot ~7/10 top Rets were using SoR, in another snapshot only 4/10 were SoC (5th/8th/9th/10th place) — sample too small/early (only weeks into MC) to call definitively — [[d:paladin#1425926030115471380]], [[d:paladin#1425926855630000361]], [[d:paladin#1425426675113398373]] (Dodging, 2025-10-08/10).
- **[Ret]** Playstyle difference: SoC is spiky/RNG-dependent — can spike to ~25k damage in a 30s fight on good Command crits but can also whiff down to ~5k, whereas SoR is consistent (every-swing procs) and enables much higher Consecration uptime/AoE — [[d:paladin#1425927627826532442]], [[d:paladin#1425928741099671715]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-08).
- **[Ambershire]** Whether AP Ret or SP(SoR) Ret is currently ahead specifically on the Ambershire server (lower average SP itemization, fewer Undead/Demon targets in MC) is disputed — some argue AP Ret is stronger there for those reasons, while testing from others suggests "SP is looking superior so far"; general consensus is they are "really close for non-parsers" — [[d:paladin#1424817099469230172]], [[d:paladin#1424817308349763646]], [[d:paladin#1425426675113398373]] (Archr/Dodging, 2025-10-06/10; contested).

## Leveling
- **[Prot leveling]** Standard early leveling rotation: keep Holy Shield and a Seal (Seal of the Righteous or Seal of Wisdom for mana) up, spam Holy Strike; remove Crusader Strike from the bar; use Judgement after Seal of Righteousness for ranged damage, or with Seal of Wisdom to apply a mana-return debuff — [[d:paladin#1423420805202706442]], [[d:paladin#1423420878384922724]], [[d:paladin#1423421177765957696]] (Mithreon/Beldrin, 2025-10-03).
- **[Weapon]** While leveling, weapon is mostly a stat/proc stick — dps/speed largely irrelevant except weapon speed slightly affects mana return frequency; considered low-priority while leveling since you'll be sitting to drink between every pull regardless — [[d:paladin#1423423599506620557]], [[d:paladin#1423423983956791387]], [[d:paladin#1423424434357801123]] (angels1m, 2025-10-03).
- **[Aura choice, leveling]** For AoE/grinding leveling as Ret, use a 2H + Devotion Aura (roughly "half a shield"); for questing, Ret Aura for return damage is more useful; general guidance: "Ret if you want to quest, Prot if you want to just AoE grind level" — [[d:paladin#1424485380048027689]], [[d:paladin#1424485417071149127]], [[d:paladin#1424485560038461601]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-05).
- **[Seals, leveling as Prot]** Recommended active seals while leveling as Prot: Holy Strike + Seal of Righteousness + Judgement for single-target threat; otherwise stack as much holy damage as possible and keep Judgement of Wisdom up (its mana proc also gives some threat) — [[d:paladin#1423727324322332763]] thru [[d:paladin#1423727957230358539]] (Proph, 2025-10-03).
- **[Rested XP]** Tents give rested XP the same as taverns/cities, just fill faster (roughly 15 min to cap vs 24h offline in an inn); tent rested XP fills only up to current level's bar per some, though relative benefit is always worth it per others — [[d:paladin#1423815246690254949]], [[d:paladin#1423815571044040714]], [[d:paladin#1423815587314012331]] (Jesse Potter/Ravenhost, 2025-10-04; contested detail on exact mechanic).
- **[Rested XP]** At low levels (e.g., 7-8), rested XP cap is burned through in 30 min–1 hr of questing — [[d:paladin#1423818211249488095]] (gastro, 2025-10-04).
- **[Talent point advice]** New-player Ret leveling question referenced "redoubt" talent from a build guide; clarified Redoubt is a talent (not a trainer-taught spell) so it's found in the talent tree, not from the class trainer — [[d:paladin#1424200069657264208]], [[d:paladin#1424201894905253971]] (Caelum/Beldrin, 2025-10-05).
- **[Prot leveling, holding aggro pre-Consecration]** Before Consecration is unlocked (level 18+), the recommended way to hold threat on multiple pulled mobs is to tab between targets: Holy Strike on one mob, tab, Judgement on another mob, tab, auto-attack (with Seal of Righteousness up) on a third — repeating this once or twice before a pack reaches you prevents anything from pulling off the tank; continue this pattern for trash even after Consecration is available — [[d:paladin#1425262878130180229]], [[d:paladin#1425262949639131186]], [[d:paladin#1425264171276046429]], [[d:paladin#1425264308060684318]] (gastro/Beldrin, 2025-10-08).
- **[Prot leveling]** Use Blessing of Wisdom on yourself while leveling/tanking until Blessing of Sanctuary becomes available — [[d:paladin#1425264348489711837]] (Dodging, 2025-10-08).
- **[Holy leveling]** Best way to level a Holy Paladin: spec Prot and pull several mobs while questing until roughly level 30-40 (heals don't scale well yet and mana is tight at low level), then respec to Holy once leveling normally — [[d:paladin#1425978068547272715]], [[d:paladin#1425978698930323457]], [[d:paladin#1425978836952158248]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-10).
- **[Leveling gear, prot/AoE]** Budget items called out as carrying an Ambershire "holyprot" leveling/lasher-farm build to 60: Nature Spellstrike ring from Gilneas City (level 43 dungeon; low direct value on Lashers but a huge mana-regen boost for the 45-60 grind) and smithable Fire Spellstrike gloves (requires 54 skill, cheap on AH, ~20g) for early lasher-farm and leveling mana sustain — [[d:paladin#1424711943343706183]] (Maddy, 2025-10-06).
- **[Leveling]** For solo prot leveling, 2H weapons kill faster than 1H+shield but cost more mana per kill; after getting Seal of Wisdom, mana should support pulling 3-4 mobs at once without issue, and by around level 37 characters generally have the tools for big pulls — [[d:paladin#1425191731665698837]], [[d:paladin#1425191812079026247]], [[d:paladin#1425192948882079969]] (Beldrin, 2025-10-07).
- **[PvP leveling]** Blessing choice while leveling in groups: Kings for hunters/mages/locks, Wisdom for healer + self, Might for melee (including Survival Hunters) — [[d:paladin#1425449770645979196]], [[d:paladin#1425450853518807182]] (Pocky/Mithreon, 2025-10-07).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Prot]** Basic single-target: keep Holy Shield + a Seal up, spam Holy Strike; do not use Crusader Strike on the bar for prot — [[d:paladin#1423420805202706442]], [[d:paladin#1423420878384922724]] (2025-10-03).
- **[Ret/SoC melee]** Boss opener discussed: 3x Crusader Strike (CS) then Holy Strike, then pop trinket/flurry/perception as CS comes off cooldown; alternative opener CS-HS-CS-CS also used. Never Judge before you already have "AA and CS" (auto-attack timing/crusader strike) lined up, to avoid losing seal procs — [[d:paladin#1423803338155233332]], [[d:paladin#1423803751730385047]], [[d:paladin#1424101223656591430]] (Jesse Potter/Mangosteado/Flammekat, 2025-10-04/05).
- **[Ret/SoR]** Seal of Righteousness (SoR) uptime is critical: judging (to reapply seal) at the wrong point in the swing timer, or letting SoR fall off before an auto-attack lands, loses significant proc value (~400+ procs per lost swing) — [[d:paladin#1424100821171179683]] (Dodging, 2025-10-04).
- **[Ret/SoR]** Rule of thumb: use SoR/SoC when mana threshold is reached (~2k mana lost) to time seal refresh efficiently — [[d:paladin#1424157375194988655]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-05).
- **[Ret]** Simple summary for casuals: "Holy Strike, and then Holy Strike... oh and Seal of Righteousness I guess" — [[d:paladin#1424233192411496569]], [[d:paladin#1424240050538811392]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-05).
- **[Holy]** Holy healer single-target: Holy Strike on CD when safe, spam Flash of Light (FoL), use Holy Shock for burst heals; keep Seal of Wisdom or Seal of Light up via melee for mana/threat management — [[d:paladin#1424290706947571793]], [[d:paladin#1424290830499315814]] (Iseut, 2025-10-05).
- **[Holy]** After getting Flash of Light, Holy Light is still used as an emergency heal after Judgement (with the Holy talent making it 1.5s cast instead of 2.5s), and/or timed for 2.5s casts that align with predictable boss damage windows (e.g., Patchwerk-style tank damage) to avoid overhealing — [[d:paladin#1424048219045757058]], [[d:paladin#1424048463666085999]], [[d:paladin#1424048606956097537]] (Rafalexxxx/Mithreon, 2025-10-04).
- **[Ret/dungeon]** In 5-man dungeons as Ret DPS, Consecration should still be used (possibly downranked for mana comfort) as long as tank threat and your own mana hold up — [[d:paladin#1423744768889847808]], [[d:paladin#1423745510216302654]] (Wanton Soup/Gnome Mage, 2025-10-03/04). Using Seal of Wisdom specifically to sustain Consecration spam is considered a net DPS loss — [[d:paladin#1423746298577813687]] (Beldrin).
- **[Ret, full SP/SoR opener+rotation]** Detailed SoR Ret rotation (from a top-parsing SoC player summarizing the SoR playbook): apply Blessings, cast your assigned Judgement (Light/Wisdom/Crusader) then re-Judge on cooldown, keep Seal (of Righteousness) up, use Repentance right after your auto-attack swing lands (to not waste the GCD alignment), spam Crusader Strike on cooldown, weave Consecration on cooldown, use Holy Strike roughly every 4th cooldown window for the 20% Strength (Holy Might) buff if desired, pop trinkets timed to land just as an auto/CS comes off cooldown to maximize active-proc uptime, use Perception (Human racial) opportunistically, use Exorcism vs Undead on cooldown, and don't forget consumables — [[d:paladin#1425942761663889428]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-09).
- **[Ret, shorthand SP/SoR rotation]** Shorter summary: keep SoR up, Judgement+reseal on cooldown timed inside the swing window, use Repentance on cooldown, keep the Holy Might buff active via Holy Strike, spam Crusader Strike — [[d:paladin#1425942538052960297]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-09).
- **[Ret]** Seal-swing timing rule of thumb: never Judge (breaking your seal) unless you can get the seal back up before your next auto-attack swing lands, or you lose a swing's worth of seal proc value; a good Ret's biggest differentiator vs a bad one is not whiffing seal uptime around the swing timer — [[d:paladin#1425936779105796167]], [[d:paladin#1425936833560313988]], [[d:paladin#1425929824291127391]] (Dodging/Beldrin, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret, opener]** Seal application order at pull: Seal of the Crusader (for the Physical hit-chance debuff) → Judgement → Seal of Righteousness — [[d:paladin#1424875093808517172]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-07).
- **[Prot]** For Prot threat, Crusader Strike is essentially never used — it's Holy Strike spam essentially all the time — [[d:paladin#1425146529685049437]], [[d:paladin#1425147284282409122]] (Torbz/Dodging, 2025-10-07). Crusader Strike is physical damage; as a tank you want Holy Strike (Holy damage, benefits from Righteous Fury) for threat, not Crusader Strike — [[d:paladin#1425684780460277803]], [[d:paladin#1425684966561288252]], [[d:paladin#1425686228585545768]] (Tripped/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-08).
- **[Prot/tank hit & threat mechanics]** Holy Strike and Judgement of Righteousness (JoR) — despite dealing Holy-school damage — scale their crit/hit chance off **melee** crit and melee hit, not spell crit/hit; spell crit only affects Exorcism, Bulwark of Righteous Might, and spell-type procs like Drain Life. Crusader Strike scales its damage from weapon damage and spell damage combined, but its crit/hit chance is melee-only — [[d:paladin#1424554621929783337]], [[d:paladin#1424555094166474884]], [[d:paladin#1424555150365822979]], [[d:paladin#1424555337528115220]] (noka/Rhiina/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-06).
- **[Prot/tank hit]** For tank hit rating: prioritize melee hit (used by Holy Strike/JoR), not spell hit — spell hit only matters for Taunt/Exorcism/Hammer of Wrath (interruptible-by-silence "spells"); realistic target is ~4% hit from gear (plus ~3% from talents/Precision and ~1% from weapon skill) — [[d:paladin#1425755294394220606]], [[d:paladin#1425756256437665823]], [[d:paladin#1425756663721361460]] (Serpentyn/angels1m, 2025-10-08).

- **[Prot/AoE]** Vengeance-tree/AoE tank builds trade single-target threat cooldowns for AoE survivability tools; Improved Holy Strike is called out as making "too much single target threat" if you need to be mobile, so pure AoE/dungeon-farm builds may skip it in favor of staying in the Prot tree for survivability with Judgement of Wisdom + Holy Strike spam — [[d:paladin#1423554764813107322]] (noka, 2025-10-03).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Ret melee]** Weapon skill: 1 point of weapon skill ≈ 0.2% hit; also affects glancing blow % and frequency — [[d:paladin#1424085504822870177]], [[d:paladin#1424085705826766910]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-04).
- **[Ret/AP-Ret to Spellret transition]** Switch from AP Ret to Spellret around level 58+ once you have ~175 Spell Power with D2 4-set and enough spellstrike items to use Scythe of the Harvest / Vendorstrike — [[d:paladin#1424133275546288199]], [[d:paladin#1424135227080445952]] (Hanzues/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-05).
- **[Ret/SoC vs SoR breakpoint]** ~175 Spell Power (with Scythe/Vendorstrike) is stated as the point where Spellret (SoR) starts to pull ahead of SoC — [[d:paladin#1424060129778602207]], [[d:paladin#1424060264332001411]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-04). Separately, ~300 unbuffed Spell Power on "Nord" (Nordanaar) server cited as the SoR breakpoint vs SoC — [[d:paladin#1424179818282352694]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-05; two different numbers given in different contexts — gear-tier dependent, treat as approximate).
- **[Ret]** Hit cap and crit target: "8% hit, 25% crit then stack either STR/Crit with SoC or SP for SoR" as a rough gearing target — [[d:paladin#1423773591908126854]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-04).
- **[Ret/SoR]** Recommendation to prioritize ~30% crit while buffed for SoR builds; without Vengeance stacks, damage is described as very low regardless of SP — [[d:paladin#1424208451009249420]], [[d:paladin#1424208704651133042]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-05).
- **[Ret/haste]** Haste is considered undervalued/poorly itemized for Paladins: it only affects auto-attacks, glancing damage, and seal procs (not active strikes/judgements), so its effective damage contribution is roughly 40% of nominal value on a good day; ~1% crit is valued at ~1.6 "haste" worth by comparison, but gear itemizes the opposite — [[d:paladin#1423679487354077335]], [[d:paladin#1423680169591312474]], [[d:paladin#1424161442940190892]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-03/05; detailed math thread, single-source theorycrafting, not disputed).
- **[Prot/healing gear]** Rule of thumb for Holy gear itemization vs Ironclad talent (wear plate/mail for healing without losing effectiveness): every 100 Armor ≈ 2 Healing Power equivalent (e.g., 654 armor ≈ 13 healing power); prioritize Int, Spell Crit, and raw HP — Spirit/MP5 said not to matter much because Holy Paladins regen mana via Tea, Mana Pots, SoW>JoW>SoW weaving, and passive mana from crit heals (Daybreak/Illumination) — [[d:paladin#1423964272081502270]] (Dzonathan, 2025-10-04; detailed, single source, unopposed).
- **[Prot]** Whether to wear a leather/cloth healing item over mail/plate with Ironclad: use whichever has higher healing bonus once Ironclad is factored, other stats (e.g. crit) also matter — [[d:paladin#1423928426343043114]], [[d:paladin#1423928706447179869]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-04).
- **[Ret]** Extra elemental/magic damage on weapons (e.g., "5-10 shadow damage" on the "Runeblade") is valued not for its own damage but because it is coded as part of the normal auto-attack, so it hits through Blessing of Protection and other physical-immunity effects, and it can trigger Seal of Command (since SoC triggers off autos only, not off spellstrikes) — [[d:paladin#1424143641383010394]], [[d:paladin#1424149300929368084]], [[d:paladin#1424151397867196526]], [[d:paladin#1424152132604657774]] (Jesse Potter/Dodging, 2025-10-05; some disagreement/confusion on whether it double-procs seals — resolved as no, it's one attack with part of its damage as magic).
- **[Prot/defense]** 440 Defense skill is needed to be uncrittable by (most) bosses — [[d:paladin#1425598148738416721]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-09).
- **[Prot/avoidance]** Under Holy Shield, 102.4%+ total avoidance (miss + dodge + parry + block, from Defense/gear) is the priority breakpoint to become "uncrushable" (immune to crushing-blow damage spikes); this excludes crits; achieving it purely via Defense stacking was only realistically possible in late-TBC-equivalent gearing, not this era — once capped, Block becomes largely worthless and further gearing should prioritize Armor, Stamina, and Dodge/Parry; hitting the cap naturally isn't realistic until Naxx or late-BWL gear (stacking pure +Defense items earlier sacrifices too much Stam/Int/SP) — [[d:paladin#1425598215222460486]], [[d:paladin#1425598594437611642]], [[d:paladin#1425598712230449274]], [[d:paladin#1425599143908343961]], [[d:paladin#1425599872681250929]], [[d:paladin#1425601180863565955]] (Mithreon/StMichael/gastro, 2025-10-09).
- **[Prot]** Reckoning (extra swing on a hit-while-parry-locked) only triggers when the tank is actually hit/takes a swing; confirmed to still work with Ironfoe equipped; a windfury-proc-heavy weapon setup does not inherently block Reckoning procs from working — [[d:paladin#1425608515837362327]], [[d:paladin#1425608654765293589]] (gastro, 2025-10-09).
- **[Prot/threat math]** Bulwark of Righteous Might math example: at 50% chance per hit, generates roughly ~170 extra threat per proc (modest; compared unfavorably to a Soulforge-set upgrade in the same discussion) — [[d:paladin#1424750389319827550]], [[d:paladin#1424750823350599860]] (Beldrin/Dodging, 2025-10-06).
- **[Prot/Zeal-Righteous Strikes SBV math]** The Zeal buff from Righteous Strikes (stacks 3x) multiplies total Shield Block Value (SBV) — from shield base SBV, Strength conversion, and gear bonuses combined — by 1.3x (i.e., +30% SBV) at 3 stacks; example calc using full Naxx-tier gear + Gluth's trinkets + Face of Death totaled ~320 base SBV before the buff, i.e. Righteous Strikes/Zeal would add roughly ~76 extra SBV at that gear level — considered a minor, not "start using Crusader Strike for it" level of value — [[d:paladin#1425974518626910391]], [[d:paladin#1425974934198419556]], [[d:paladin#1425977688010657833]] (Seviry, 2025-10-10).
- **[Ret/AP math]** Rule-of-thumb Str→damage conversion for Paladins: roughly 100 Strength ≈ 97 "weapon damage" equivalent after paladin-specific scaling (e.g., a 300 Str weapon proc like Untamed Blade's is treated as ~297 damage-equivalent); base conversion cited as 7 Strength = 1 weapon damage point for Paladins; buff stacking example: 57 base Str → 68 with Divine Strength (+10%... wait, order matters) → 80 with Kings → 100 with Holy Might (+20% more), which is "technically" ~230 AP but treated as ~200 AP for quick math, then divided by weapon speed (e.g. /3.7) for a per-hit estimate — [[d:paladin#1425530904943657090]], [[d:paladin#1425531664892952689]], [[d:paladin#1425531785382461512]], [[d:paladin#1425770087545049118]] (Jesse Potter/Mithreon, 2025-10-08/09).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Prot]** Sanctuary (Blessing/Aura) debated: it reduces damage taken by a flat amount rather than adding block value; some argue this flat mitigation becomes more impactful as gear/armor increases (tankier characters benefit more from flat reduction relative to incoming hits), others argue it's primarily valuable for its damage component and the flat mitigation is negligible (e.g., 24 damage reduction vs mobs hitting for ~100) — [[d:paladin#1423441883782447214]] thru [[d:paladin#1423444591771652106]] (Jesse Potter vs Seviry/Dodging, 2025-10-03; unresolved disagreement).

- **[Prot]** Reckoning-based tank/DPS hybrid builds are explicitly not recommended: Reckoning is considered too weak ("doodoo ass") currently to justify building around, requiring deep Prot investment just to reach it, with no clear itemization path (e.g., no reason to pick Gressil over BiS weapons like Thunderfury for a Reckoning build) — [[d:paladin#1423434800647241811]] thru [[d:paladin#1423437689235181579]] (Seviry/gastro, 2025-10-03).
- **[Prot]** Weapon speed for tanking: a slower 1H (or 2H) is "substantially better" with Reckoning procs, but since Reckoning isn't build-worthy, this is largely moot; historically "slow 1H Vengeance Prot" was a known high-threat ("meta") spec in earlier eras — [[d:paladin#1423434800647241811]], [[d:paladin#1423438897970413669]] (Seviry/Dodging, 2025-10-03).
- **[Prot]** Engineering is very good for tanks specifically because of Sappers and Grenades (crowd control/burst utility), even without a dedicated engineering requirement for the class — [[d:paladin#1423558562168311849]], [[d:paladin#1423560632552259594]] (noka, 2025-10-03).
- **[Prot]** Question on whether swapping to a 2H weapon when tanking casters improves survivability — asked but left unanswered in the chunk — [[d:paladin#1424470152170176643]] (Saša, 2025-10-05; open question).
- **[Prot]** Holy Shield is widely criticized as a weak/overvalued core mechanic: its threat is a % bonus applied to the reactive block-proc, which itself does near-zero base threat; realistically landing all 5 charges within its 12s cooldown requires taking a hit almost every GCD (only reliably achievable with heavy incoming damage, e.g. from Power Word: Shield-immune sources or after a Vampiric Embrace purge on adds), so most of the time pressing it is a net threat "griefing" relative to Holy Strike; multiple posters ("angels1m", "Rhiina", "dusty") independently call it the worst-value ability in the Prot kit, and note current Holy Shield scaling is weaker than in vanilla-era Paladin — [[d:paladin#1424536926626648064]], [[d:paladin#1424537077147762708]], [[d:paladin#1424537323122458676]], [[d:paladin#1424537482418192394]], [[d:paladin#1424537589196652545]] (angels1m/Rhiina/dusty, 2025-10-05).
- **[Prot]** Crusader Strike and Holy Strike sharing one cooldown is called out repeatedly as a structural design problem for Prot (and Ret/Holy): unlike Shaman's two strikes, which don't share a cooldown and even synergize with each other, Paladin's two strikes always force a "which one is strictly better" situation rather than a real rotational choice — [[d:paladin#1424536668987326556]], [[d:paladin#1424536733789454336]], [[d:paladin#1424536775145291796]] (Jesse Potter/Rhiina/tripleXDengine, 2025-10-05; long-running, unresolved design complaint, recurs later in the chunk on 2025-10-10 as well).
- **[Prot]** Vengeance Prot (a Strength/Reckoning-leaning offtank build) is considered a niche/"delusional" spec for actual main-tanking: it deliberately trades threat for damage (crits reduce Vengeance-stack threat), which is a poor tradeoff for a spec that already struggles with aggro; only reported useful for offtanking, where burst snap-threat matters more than sustained aggro; reportedly at most ~4 players have played it in real top-guild progression raids, and all reportedly agree it isn't viable as a primary tank spec — [[d:paladin#1424543910834864228]], [[d:paladin#1424544104896790630]], [[d:paladin#1424544113830924328]], [[d:paladin#1424544212434812981]], [[d:paladin#1424544394542842026]] (angels1m/Rhiina/tripleXDengine, 2025-10-05).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Rotation: Holy Strike on cooldown when safe; Flash of Light (FoL) spam plus Holy Shock for burst; keep a Judgement seal (Wisdom or Light) applied via melee weaving — [[d:paladin#1424290706947571793]], [[d:paladin#1424290830499315814]] (Iseut, 2025-10-05).
- **[Holy]** Holy Light retained post-FoL as an emergency heal (esp. after Judgement w/ Holy talent making the cast 1.5s instead of 2.5s), or as a planned 2.5s cast timed to predictable heavy-damage windows to avoid overhealing (e.g., tank-and-spank fights) — [[d:paladin#1424048463666085999]], [[d:paladin#1424048606956097537]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-04).
- **[Holy]** Mana sustain relies on Tea, Mana Potions, SoW→JoW→SoW weaving via melee autos between heals, and passive mana return from critical heals (Daybreak/Illumination) — Spirit and MP5 said to not matter much by comparison; prioritize Intellect, Spell Crit, and raw HP — [[d:paladin#1423964272081502270]] (Dzonathan, 2025-10-04).
- **[Holy]** Holy Paladins can wear plate/mail or leather/cloth interchangeably thanks to the Ironclad talent, choosing whichever has the higher healing bonus (+ other relevant stats); no obligation to wear plate — [[d:paladin#1423928426343043114]], [[d:paladin#1423929389233737778]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-04).
- **[Holy]** Red Dragonscale 4-piece set bonus doesn't fall behind in +heal until BWL, but pushes the playstyle toward ranged Flash of Light spam rather than melee weaving; still being evaluated vs "Lawbringer holy" gear in CC2 content — [[d:paladin#1424151691338580240]] (Illithris, 2025-10-05; single source, open evaluation).
- **[Holy, FoL vs HL math]** Detailed community math comparing Flash of Light (FoL) and Holy Light (HL): at ~750-1000 healing power / 20% crit, max-rank FoL averages roughly 570-930 non-crit healing on a 1.5s cast, while max-rank Holy Light averages roughly 950-2090 non-crit healing on a 2.5s cast (numbers vary between posters/gear levels, both reported); Holy Light wins on raw throughput-per-cast if it lands without overheal, but FoL wins on healing-per-mana (HPM) and snipes more precisely in small increments, overheals far less on non-max-damage windows, and generates ~6 more Daybreak procs per minute due to its faster cast (Daybreak averaged ~690 non-crit at the same stats in one measurement) — [[d:paladin#1425088237315162206]], [[d:paladin#1425088879098204240]], [[d:paladin#1425088991128064000]], [[d:paladin#1425089452380000449]], [[d:paladin#1425089548475564133]], [[d:paladin#1425094790739005560]] (Rafalexxxx/Dzonathan/Jesse Potter/Vindaven, 2025-10-07).
- **[Holy, addon-dev recommendation]** From a healing-addon (QuickHeal) developer's testing: default to spamming Flash of Light almost always; reserve Holy Light for rare emergencies — specifically only when the target is critically low (~40% or less) AND you already have the "Holy Judgement" buff (from Judging) that cuts Holy Light's cast to 1.5s — because earning that buff costs 2 GCDs (seal + judge) which is usually a net mana/time loss versus just continuing to spam FoL; also recommends disabling automatic Holy Light casts entirely while out of combat (too mana-inefficient) — [[d:paladin#1425074584926949426]], [[d:paladin#1425100594988843119]], [[d:paladin#1425101563847770112]], [[d:paladin#1425107371482550283]], [[d:paladin#1425107644166701136]] (Vindaven, 2025-10-07). An alternative simpler heuristic suggested: Rank 4 Holy Light while keeping Blessing of Light on your main heal target — [[d:paladin#1425104037560189000]] (Deleted User, 2025-10-07).
- **[Holy, melee healing]** Melee/"holy melee" healing (weaving Holy Strike/Crusader Strike attacks with heals while standing in melee range, often paired with a support weapon like Nightfall) is a genuinely different and harder healing style: it is explicitly not a damage spec, requires a live target in melee range and raid-leader awareness that you won't be spamming ranged heals every GCD, and a single melee-swing GCD can throw off the timing of slower casts (e.g. Greater Heal/Holy Wrath-length casts) enough to matter — [[d:paladin#1424849129678311464]], [[d:paladin#1424850793458171926]] (Marfusia/Illithris, 2025-10-07).
- **[Holy, melee healing]** Holy Strike is valued as an AoE melee-heal tool, hitting up to ~5 nearby people for roughly 1k each, making it efficient during trash/cleave phases if you're in melee range; Holy Shock is generally saved for yourself or for "sniping" low-health targets (including enemy healers in PvP) rather than used reactively as party AoE — [[d:paladin#1425089190051188867]], [[d:paladin#1424851861692747836]], [[d:paladin#1424852211258622073]] (Jesse Potter/Marfusia/Dodging, 2025-10-06/07).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Bug]** Blessing of Protection previously did NOT grant immunity to Blind (a bug); confirmed fixed in this period — [[d:paladin#1424408436044595280]], [[d:paladin#1424416119590228100]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-05).
- **[Bug/quirk]** Elemental/magic-damage weapon procs (Runeblade-style) are coded as part of the base auto-attack (not a separate attack), which is why they bypass Blessing of Protection/physical immunity while still counting once toward proc effects (not double) — [[d:paladin#1424151397867196526]], [[d:paladin#1424152132604657774]] (Dodging, 2025-10-05).
- **[Quirk]** Sigil of Ancient Accord doesn't scale with Spell Power despite being a spell-damage-flavored trinket — [[d:paladin#1423437756863873184]] (gastro, 2025-10-03).
- **[Quirk]** Hand of Reckoning/Judgement of Command stun-bonus-damage debate suggests the bonus damage on stunned targets may be flat (not scaling with AP/SP/talents) rather than a percentage increase — unresolved math disagreement between Jesse Potter and Excinerus — [[d:paladin#1424165990559780864]] thru [[d:paladin#1424168100483432540]] (2025-10-05; contested/unresolved).
- **[Quirk]** Seal of Command (SoC) reportedly cannot be triggered by Crusader Strike or Holy Strike — only base auto-attacks proc it — seen as a design weakness compared to Windfury Totem, which can proc off Shaman special attacks — [[d:paladin#1425968552619278356]], [[d:paladin#1425968681493598228]] (Excinerus/Not Normal, 2025-10-10).
- **[Quirk]** Blessing of Sanctuary's flat damage reduction is applied to incoming damage BEFORE the target's armor mitigation calculation rather than after, which (per Excinerus's math, `1000*50% - 20` vs `(1000-20)*50%`) makes the flat reduction proportionally weaker than it would be if applied post-armor — a mechanical quirk affecting how much the buff is actually worth relative to its tooltip value — [[d:paladin#1425920749469696093]], [[d:paladin#1425921178454855894]], [[d:paladin#1425921568671924225]], [[d:paladin#1425921672338341991]] (Excinerus/Dodging, 2025-10-09).
- **[Quirk]** Mobs with full damage-type immunity (e.g., Ragnaros/Nefarian to Fire) take zero damage from weapon procs of that school regardless of whether the proc itself is "unresistable" — immunity and resistance are separate mechanics; a proc being unresistable (e.g. Sulfuras's proc, fixed on Ambershire) does not bypass outright immunity — [[d:paladin#1424107661405851838]], [[d:paladin#1424107845296849086]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-04).
