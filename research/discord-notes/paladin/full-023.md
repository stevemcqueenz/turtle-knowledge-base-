## Rotation: single target
- **[prot]** Prot paladins do not use Crusader Strike: it's mutually exclusive with Holy Strike, which does "nearly 10x as much threat"; not even worth it to maintain the Zeal 3-stack haste buff, since it'd take ~5 minutes of uninterrupted auto-attacking for the haste gain to outweigh the lost Holy Strikes — prot uses Seal of Righteousness instead — [[d:paladin#1487459302951358536]], [[d:paladin#1487459563707044021]], [[d:paladin#1487459725661573132]] (Grey/Biggsey, 2026-03-28).
- **[prot]** Roughly 30% of a prot tank's total threat comes from auto-attacks, so weapon choice (SP first, see Stats section) still matters incidentally even though Str/AP isn't a priority stat — [[d:paladin#1487460215204089866]] (Grey, 2026-03-28).
- **[prot]** Cooldown-desync note (Libram of Eternal Tower): without Eternal Tower, Holy Strike naturally comes off cooldown at the same time as every 4th Judgement of Righteousness, every 4th Consecration, every 3rd Holy Shield, and every 2nd Exorcism, creating GCD congestion. With Eternal Tower equipped, HS desyncs from all of those (0.5s after every 2nd Holy Shield, 0.5s before every 5th) for a much smoother rotation "playfeel," even without a big net damage gain — [[d:paladin#1487468942699270224]], [[d:paladin#1487469060747821217]] (Grey, 2026-03-28).
- **[holy]** Extensive healing-rotation consensus: Crusader Strike should almost never be mixed into active healing for output — it costs a GCD without healing anyone; "much rather do 2 FoL and 1 [Holy] Shock than 2 Shock and 1 CS"; CS is only used when moving and needing a Holy Shock cooldown reset specifically — [[d:paladin#1488530603669454990]], [[d:paladin#1488530958905774251]], [[d:paladin#1488533588973719592]], [[d:paladin#1488534279020482730]], [[d:paladin#1488534322569936957]] (Infertile/Destroses/Mithreon, 2026-03-30; some, e.g. Numi, still use Shock+CS+Shock to guarantee healing 2 targets at once — [[d:paladin#1488533400729161750]]).
- **[ret/soc]** SoC favors weapons with big raw weapon damage over weapon DPS/attack speed ("SoC likes heavy hitters with big weapon damage, not dps") — [[d:paladin#1488647303521763498]] (Mithreon, 2026-03-30).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[prot]** Standard AoE-leveling/farming talent+rotation kit for prot: Consecration, Retribution Aura, Blessing of Sanctuary, Redoubt, Holy Shield, and a Shield Spike weapon/shield enchant — [[d:paladin#1488343219153670284]] (Destroses, 2026-03-31).
- **[gold/aoe]** Force Reactive Disk (FRD, requires 300 Engineering) has no internal cooldown and releases a damage charge on every incoming hit, so its damage "overlaps" across multiple attacking mobs (e.g. 2 mobs attacking = each takes double what an equivalent Thorns effect would deal); combined with Vampiric-touch life-steal gear it's described as "the latchkey of paladin farming." Downsides: needs frequent repair from heavy AoE pulls (~once per wing in raid) unlike other shields, and it plus Dream's Herald (both Nature damage) do NOT work on Lasher-type mobs, which are Nature-immune. Non-Engineering AoE-farm alternatives: Dream's Herald, Crest of Retribution (no repair bill), Argent Defender, or simply the tankiest shield available — [[d:paladin#1488941850139033770]], [[d:paladin#1488926148560814330]], [[d:paladin#1488940468791148646]], [[d:paladin#1488984070393168034]], [[d:paladin#1488939167013666927]] (Silverhand/Beldrin/gastro, 2026-04-01).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[ret/soc]** SoC stat priority: Crit > Haste > Str > SP > AP > Agi; haste gains value in "jumps" (breakpoints) — [[d:paladin#1487011772266184774]], [[d:paladin#1487011955586760704]] (Mithreon, 2026-03-26).
- **[ret/soc]** Alternative view: for SoC ret, spellpower is lower priority than agility — 20 agi = 1 crit; player reports stacking agi over SP with T3 ret + Ashbringer — [[d:paladin#1487027164204367902]] (Shokisan, 2026-03-27; single source, somewhat disputed by Mithreon's general priority).
- **[ret/soc]** Crit vs SP value is contextual: value of crit fluctuates with AP/SP amount and buff uptime; Onyxia's Tooth Pendant (OTP) considered the "safer bet" until ~35-40% crit, Seething Vine/similar is a scaled-up OTP — [[d:paladin#1487027870021718116]], [[d:paladin#1487028004352823377]], [[d:paladin#1487028392321880096]] (Grey, 2026-03-27).
- **[prot]** SP > AP, "SP is king, AP is just nice to have in addition" for prot as well — recommendation of 30 SP enchant on weapon over Crusader enchant "by a wide margin" — [[d:paladin#1487342522484396174]], [[d:paladin#1487342627023097948]] (Grey, 2026-03-28).
- **[general]** 1% dodge/block/parry ≈ worth 7 defense rating (rule of thumb) — [[d:paladin#1487068890264633504]] (Silverhand, 2026-03-27).
- **[necro/ret]** Necrodin (spellstrike/on-hit build) stat notes: SP focus is generally best even with SoC; some still run mixed SP/agi pieces — [[d:paladin#1487284066918076478]] (Shokisan, 2026-03-28).
- **[hpal]** Holy paladin core stats: INT, spell crit, healing power; mana never an issue with SoW>JoW rotation + Holy Strike + Illumination + mana pots/tea — [[d:paladin#1487359832318148680]] (Dzonathan, 2026-03-28).
- **[hpal]** Holy Might/Kings scaling: the new BWL boss trinket (SoC-proccing item) will scale with Holy Might and Kings while Seal of Command itself does not scale that way — considered a "huge deal" — [[d:paladin#1486777426716655618]] (gastro, 2026-03-26).
- **[ret]** Weapon skill guidance: 310 WS is plenty for hybrid/SoR ret, 315 is good for SoC ret but not strictly needed; general rule of thumb is that going over 315 WS wastes stat budget; 5 weapon skill = 1% hit and scales linearly — [[d:paladin#1488219693293633586]], [[d:paladin#1488232104671051877]], [[d:paladin#1488982232482840768]] (Istenneveben/Dodging, 2026-04-01/02).
- **[prot]** Prot needs essentially no spell hit ("you dont need any spell hit on turtle anymore" — stack SP instead); hit% still helps Consecration, Exorcism vs. Undead, taunt reliability, and procs like TF, but isn't worth gearing for outside of specific trinkets like Nature's Tear — [[d:paladin#1488770847803052143]], [[d:paladin#1488771283314544730]] (theoloras/Jesse Potter, 2026-03-31).
- **[prot]** Recommended prot SP sources: SP trinkets (Nature's Tear-type, Sapphiron essence, Wrath of Cenarius), SP cloak drops (Hakkar/C'Thun/Sapphiron), and everything else basically T-gear/mitigation cloak/neck until K40 — [[d:paladin#1488772079284260996]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-03-31).
- **[prot]** Str/AP is still not a strong individual stat for prot, but "in full raid buffs the AP gains are actually useful now" thanks to this patch's Holy Strike AP-scaling buff — [[d:paladin#1487951069383495861]] (dumbid, 2026-03-30).
- **[ret/ap]** For AP ret, Strength scales linearly with no diminishing returns, unlike Haste which has breakpoints and shows most of its value only over long, sustained fights (little value on trash with 1-2 auto-attacks per pack); a player who learned AP ret from a top parser ("lonsell") recalls being taught Str vastly outweighs every other stat ("only str exists, everything else is just trolling") — [[d:paladin#1489018254000132147]], [[d:paladin#1489020647454675125]] (Istenneveben, 2026-04-02).

- **[prot]** Threat rule of thumb: 1% dodge/block/parry is worth about 7 defense rating in terms of relative value — [[d:paladin#1487068890264633504]] (Silverhand, 2026-03-27).
- **[prot]** For undergeared tanks starting out: prioritize defense over SP if having survivability issues; if threat issues, prioritize SP; general guidance to ask raid leader in guild context, or "threatmax and roll the dice with healers" if pugging — [[d:paladin#1487066524564062218]], [[d:paladin#1487067233644445888]], [[d:paladin#1487068006755471420]], [[d:paladin#1487068529156030534]] (Antez/Silverhand/Dodging/Arle, 2026-03-27).

- **[general]** Weapon skill in PvP: base skill cap is 300 before talents/gear; only matters for hit% if the opposing player has higher defense skill than your weapon skill (e.g., 17 skill vs 20 defense = up to 0.6% miss chance); weapon skill (rating) does not otherwise affect PvP outside this miss mechanic — [[d:paladin#1487285551798157433]], [[d:paladin#1487285661450109080]], [[d:paladin#1487285866685530132]], [[d:paladin#1487277427364200489]] (Jesse Potter/Dodging, 2026-03-28).
- **[prot]** Avoidance-cap target for prot tanks: 102.4% total combined dodge/parry/block/miss is the "uncrittable" threshold; being at ~90% combined while Holy Shield is active is "basically the same" in practice — [[d:paladin#1488125472943509505]], [[d:paladin#1488125629583851582]] (Grey/Arle, 2026-03-31).
- **[prot]** Defense rating philosophy: defense is "bad" itemization-wise in vanilla/TWOW because it's too costly on item budget to max out, and (unlike TBC/Wrath) threat isn't reliant on overtuned threat multipliers, so prot tanks should prioritize stamina/spellpower/armor over stacking defense; going under 400 defense still risks trash crits (~15% of max health), while most raid bosses hit for ~1-1.5k normal / 2-3k crit — [[d:paladin#1488126608115240960]], [[d:paladin#1488131691343249449]], [[d:paladin#1488132401812209797]] (Grey, 2026-03-31).
- **[prot]** Improved Hand of Reckoning is only clearly valuable on specific fights (Four Horsemen progression, and turning a wipe into a kill on Firemaw/Ebonroc/Flamegor); elsewhere (MC/ZG/AQ20/Kara10) it's "kinda meh," and 5/5 Toughness (~500-1000 extra armor, ~2% damage reduction) is a fine, more consistent alternative — [[d:paladin#1488123732537901087]] (Grey, 2026-03-31).
- **[general]** Extra-attack effects (Band of the [?] Marksman-type items, Hand of Justice, etc.) reportedly share an internal cooldown between BM Cloak and Hand of Justice specifically; other such effects are believed independent — [[d:paladin#1487979107303030846]] (Destroses, 2026-03-28; single source, unconfirmed).
- **[general]** Buff-cap mechanic: buffs beyond the visible buff-cap limit still function correctly even when not displayed; this only really matters for classes needing to visually track short-duration self-buffs, but it can cause a bug where an over-cap Divine Shield/Bubble cannot be visually confirmed or cancelled early — [[d:paladin#1489160210328322190]], [[d:paladin#1489161650966757437]], [[d:paladin#1489176890408304701]] (Grey/Silverhand, 2026-04-02).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[TMH20/Timbermaw Hold raid]** New 20-man raid (Timbermaw Hold, "TMH20") launched around 2026-03-27; requires completing a "douse" quest chain (4 quests total, obtained from an NPC inside the bear cave above the Timbermaw raid entrance, requires Timbermaw Honored reputation) before being able to reach the final boss — [[d:paladin#1487335242183344168]], [[d:paladin#1487351034476101745]] (Alexander, 2026-03-28).
- **[TMH20]** Douse questline detail: Step 1 — kill "Crash the Server" (the raid's first boss); Step 2 — kill shamans scattered through the raid to collect Totems; Step 3 — fly out to Un'Goro Crater, Feralas, and Alcaz Island to kill 3 outdoor elites (120k-220k HP each, ~30min respawn); Step 4 — retrieve a quest item from behind the Archdruid boss (a Pouch) and from behind the "Nature Prick" boss (a Flower). The base douse has a 1-hour cooldown (like the old MC douse). A separate, harder "Eternal Douse" requires Timbermaw Hold Exalted reputation and carries a 7-day lockout; it's obtained after clearing the final boss and starting a follow-up quest chain worth ~3-4k additional reputation — [[d:paladin#1487394860108353687]], [[d:paladin#1487393794663125093]], [[d:paladin#1487578956868616232]] (Jesse Potter/Mithreon, 2026-03-27/29).
- **[TMH20]** Boss difficulty ranking (Mithreon, hardest to easiest): Druid (Archdruid) > Kronn > Elemental > "big guy" (Rock giant) > Satyr > Chieftain > Hydra (Trioch) > "double bear" (first boss) — [[d:paladin#1487557780578762893]] (Mithreon, 2026-03-29).
- **[TMH20]** Rock-giant boss mechanics: an AoE stomp, an AoE ground zone ("earthquake popcorn"), a soak mechanic (the whole raid must collapse onto the targeted player before he charges), and periodic slowing adds (kill the big add before small ones; small ones just get cleaved) — [[d:paladin#1487277971017437370]], [[d:paladin#1487278044761686136]], [[d:paladin#1488104732236058654]], [[d:paladin#1488105875007733760]] (theoloras/Mithreon, 2026-03-28/04-01).
- **[TMH20]** Hydra (Trioch) boss: stand to its side and soak "icicles" in two groups when it targets two players; relatively low HP so it dies fast; also periodically AoEs fire onto the raid; drops Pysan's sword — [[d:paladin#1487764686517698734]], [[d:paladin#1487712576589533287]], [[d:paladin#1487764927639847153]] (Mithreon, 2026-03-29).
- **[TMH20]** Poison-cloud boss: requires moving out of the cloud plus periodic Cleanse; the debuff from getting hit is intentionally undispellable-feeling/painful so players "can't get mad" about skipping cleanses; some guilds clear with zero cleansing using defensive cooldowns/Resistance potions instead — [[d:paladin#1487279896110825542]], [[d:paladin#1487280371044323348]], [[d:paladin#1487280431895416832]], [[d:paladin#1487280992866795631]] (theoloras/Eepyman/TRUE HEIR, 2026-03-28).
- **[TMH20]** General raid feel: trash hits very hard for its HP pool (e.g. "4k crits on a 5.5k armor target"), an undispellable spreading-disease debuff chains between players standing too close (very punishing for pugs), poison clouds deal a bit too much damage, and most rooms are cramped/tight — [[d:paladin#1488146220974342215]], [[d:paladin#1488147198825725963]], [[d:paladin#1488147233407897883]], [[d:paladin#1488147274541564045]] (Beldrin/angels1m, 2026-03-30).
