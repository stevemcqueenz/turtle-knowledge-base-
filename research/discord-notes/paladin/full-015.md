## Leveling
- **[Tank/leveling]** Beldrin's leveling advice for a future prot paladin: "use stam/int gear, get prot then holy talents, pull mobs 1-3 levels below yours, don't pull mobs with spells" — [[d:paladin#1463259492408889355]] (Beldrin, 2026-01-20).
- **[General leveling]** A level-by-level talent guide exists in the channel's pinned messages; general leveling advice: wear up-to-date "Of the Eagle" mail gear with a fast 1h weapon, hit Holy Strike on cooldown — [[d:paladin#1463636340942962698]] (Grey, 2026-01-22).
- **[Leveling]** Low-level tank mana management: don't cast unnecessary spells once you have a threat lead (each unused spell ~ 2-4 Seal of Wisdom procs' worth of mana saved); Holy Strike refunds its own cost and is free; SoR autos are free; JoR costs ~20 mana/sec; Holy Shield rank 1 costs 15 mana/sec (doubles consumption, drop after first cast per pack); Consecration is only needed for 4+ targets — [[d:paladin#1463623306107162715]] (Grey, 2026-01-21).
- **[Leveling]** Cookie-cutter low-level tank pull opener: "Holy Strike Skull, JoR X, SoR Triangle" then HS+JoR one mob, SoR the next, stun the third — [[d:paladin#1463624373092090078]] (Grey, 2026-01-22).
- **[Leveling]** Professions for leveling tank paladin: herbalism+alchemy is a better moneymaker; mining+engineering gives more utility (Force Reactive Disk near-required for big dungeon AOE farming and raid AoE threat encounters); mining+engineering ("grenades could be pog for leveling") is not a bad call either — [[d:paladin#1463593231848837352]], [[d:paladin#1463590630029525024]] (gastro/Seviry, 2026-01-21).
- **[Leveling]** Weapon-skill quest note: "Ancient Warfare" weapon-skill text quest bonus stacks additively with Human racial weapon skill — [[d:paladin#1464112233096286424]] (dusty, confirming to WaR, 2026-01-25): 1 weapon skill point ≈ 0.2% hit, 5 skill ≈ 1% hit, 315 skill is the cap for both hit and glancing blows.

## Rotation: single target
- **[Prot]** Recommended trash/single-target tank opener at low level and generally: Consecrate (only if 4+ targets), Holy Strike, Judgement of Righteousness, then auto-attack (SoR) while managing threat lead — see Leveling section citations above.
- **[Prot]** SoR (1h weapon) generates relatively little threat: bringing paladin tps from ~800 to ~2,000 overall, with ~40% of that gain coming from JoR ("only ability that comes anywhere close to scaling 10%/sec"); by contrast bear autos scale "from 1k threat/2s to 6k threat/1.3s" — [[d:paladin#1463509697406439445]] (Grey, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** Weapon speed largely doesn't matter for JoR (pure SP-based); SoR TPS is roughly equivalent between fast (1.9) and slow (2.8) speed weapons because scaling vs. hit-rate cancel out; fast weapons matter more for JoW procs/mana, not threat — [[d:paladin#1464078574897070332]], [[d:paladin#1464079764041109546]] (dusty/Silverhand, 2026-01-23).
- **[Prot]** Holy Strike is the tank's primary/largest threat source; missing it (parry/dodge/miss) causes a big threat swing — [[d:paladin#1464053262981402727]] (Silverhand, 2026-01-23). Sample numbers from full T3.5-geared prot: "consecration ticks 150-200, SoR ~500, autos crit ~1700, Crusader Strike crit ~2k, Judgement ~2.2k" — [[d:paladin#1463087586510770289]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-01-20).
- **[SoC/Ret]** SoR does not deal damage if the paired auto-attack misses/is dodged/parried; SoR itself cannot miss if the auto-attack lands — [[d:paladin#1463077254266355829]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-01-20).

- **[Prot]** Judgement is not on the global cooldown — noted as a caveat for libram-swap macro timing — [[d:paladin#1463240147930841219]] (Pastor, 2026-01-20).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot]** Consecration outthreats Arcane Explosion even at its current output; its main job is to get mobs onto the tank alongside Holy Shield/FRD — [[d:paladin#1464062501959237653]], [[d:paladin#1463180242406608929]] (Grey, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** Consecration cast time (~2s before first tick) is a known pain point; a proposed 1.18.1 fix is unclear on whether the first tick timing moves up — [[d:paladin#1463179337816735875]], [[d:paladin#1463180242406608929]] (Grey, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** Practical AoE trash advice: consecration is a mana sink not needed at good raid pace; e.g., in Naxx only used on ~8 spiders, Faerlina cultists, the 4 ghoul packs, and Construct/Smiths packs — everything else dies in 4-8s single-target — [[d:paladin#1463659247324631080]] (Grey, 2026-01-21).
- **[Prot]** Greater Blessing spam is a real (if clunky) AoE threat tool: each GB cast does 60 threat per buffed player × Righteous Fury multiplier, split evenly among all enemies in combat (Blessing of Wisdom generates 0 threat) — [[d:paladin#1464060242735399034]], [[d:paladin#1464060527260074145]] (Grey, 2026-01-23). Rule of thumb: ~10 players receiving the blessing is "a safe point" to actually hold threat vs healer TPS — [[d:paladin#1464061288727449712]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot]** 3-set T2 sigil (Ancient Accord/Sigil of the Unfathomable Depths style ring) procs off any "damaging spell," including the first tick of Consecration and Exorcism, and T2 3-piece's own proc; it does 5-11% of total damage over a raid depending on investment (typical reported 5-8%) — [[d:paladin#1463295885789364462]], [[d:paladin#1464044720941564060]], [[d:paladin#1464044843033563169]] (Pastor/dusty/Jesse Potter, 2026-01-21/23); noted as weaker for a pure AoE/vamp farming set due to its internal cooldown — [[d:paladin#1463413086609342656]] (Aelgagar).
- **[Prot]** Double T2 3-piece + Sigil trinket combo (via Dream Herald / arcane elemental procs) does "a huge chunk of aoe damage," especially useful for prot AoE tanking — [[d:paladin#1463297546876227724]] (Excinerus, 2026-01-21).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Weapon hit: 4% hit needed on gear (5 weapon skill points from trainer book), 3% is sufficient if Human racial is used — but Human racial bonus doesn't apply to prot as hit toward taunt-relevant spell hit — [[d:paladin#1463873858166722570]], [[d:paladin#1463505809806987384]] (Grey/Jesse Potter, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** Improved Hand of Reckoning gives +8% spell hit to Taunt but does not guarantee it — spells still have a 16% base miss chance at level 60 vs. raid bosses, so guaranteed taunt is not achievable — [[d:paladin#1463558903748100267]], [[d:paladin#1463559071859998733]], [[d:paladin#1463559685050335424]] (Wilhelm/Mithreon, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** Weapon-skill-to-hit conversion: 1 weapon skill point ≈ 0.2% hit; 5 skill ≈ ~1% hit (not exactly, "3 weapon skill isnt 1% hit, it's 0.6% hit"); 315 skill is the hit/glancing cap — [[d:paladin#1463505840601432181]], [[d:paladin#1464112233096286424]] (Jesse Potter/dusty, 2026-01-20/25).
- **[Prot]** Overcapping hit is a low-priority concern ("being 8-9-10% by wearing otherwise-BiS pieces isn't a real problem") vs the risk of losing far more valuable stats (e.g., 37sp ring) to correct a minor hit overage — [[d:paladin#1463168323503259822]] (Grey, 2026-01-20); countered that being overcapped means "dead weight stats," prefer crit over hit — [[d:paladin#1463168691058376889]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot]** Realistic SP progression by content per Grey: full-Naxx-bis with no K40 loot ≈ 350 SP before WoC held threat fine; current T3.5 set ≈ 400 SP before WoC and getting tighter as raid DPS improves — [[d:paladin#1463502326026211443]] (Grey, 2026-01-20). CriticalRick's rough phase estimate (unconfirmed/single source, disputed): MC ~250-350 SP, ZG ~250+, Naxx ~500, Kara40 ~550 — [[d:paladin#1463500226823458932]]; countered "In bis gear you have around 250 sp" and "Uuh no, K40 bis is not 250" — [[d:paladin#1463500434957406221]], [[d:paladin#1463501070104920167]] (Nesushka/Mithreon, contested).
- **[Prot]** T3 (tier 3, mitigation set) itself provides only ~150 SP unbuffed; can be pushed to ~350 with Thunderfury via pure spellpower accessories — [[d:paladin#1464047641796280340]] (Grey/angels1m, 2026-01-23).
- **[Prot]** Full raid-buffed SP breakdown example in T2.5+Desecration: base gear ~600, WoC 132, JoTC 130 (140 pre-talent/libram per Jesse Potter), flask 150, Dreamtonic 35, Greater Arcane Elixir 35, oil 36, Dreamshard 15, essence 130, Remains 55 → total ~1300 SP fully buffed, with ~7.4k HP — [[d:paladin#1463507185853268007]], [[d:paladin#1463507418519699536]] (Grey, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** Paladin gear budget for stamina is deliberately low ("designed as the low HP class, likely for PvP reasons") — works out to ~9 stamina per piece less across 8 pieces vs comparable classes — [[d:paladin#1463362154903245030]] (Grey, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** T1 (mitigation tier) has ~150 HP less than Warrior's T1 (177 vs 190 stam total on paladin vs warrior sets, an example gear comparison) but the paladin T1 8-piece threat bonus is strong/underrated ("criminally overlooked") despite the set's poor mitigation — [[d:paladin#1464043461903913043]], [[d:paladin#1464043987781550302]] (Grey/dusty, 2026-01-22).
- **[Prot]** T2 (mitigation set) full 8-piece avoidance bonus: +2.44 dodge, +2.44 parry, +1.44 block, +1.44 miss, -1.44 crit taken (for comparison, Warrior's mit set gives +3.64 dodge/+2.64 parry/+6.64 block/+1.64 miss/+1.64 anti-crit) — [[d:paladin#1464044457048408237]], [[d:paladin#1464045055105957938]] (Grey, 2026-01-22).
- **[Prot]** T2.5 (threat set) has more avoidance (agility on every piece) but less stamina than T3.5; T3.5 gives more stam/mitigation but is regarded as a worse threat set overall ("t3.5 tries to be a threat set but it's just a mit set with less mit and more crit... with some additional healing received") — [[d:paladin#1463473035959865432]], [[d:paladin#1463504332161941627]], [[d:paladin#1463504420401709169]] (Aelgagar/Grey/Jesse Potter, 2026-01-20).
- **[Ret]** T2.5 is BiS/non-debatable for both spellret and "ape" (AP) ret due to set bonuses — [[d:paladin#1464113164659589288]] (dusty, 2026-01-25). Common spellret endgame progression: 5pc T2.5 + 3pc T2 (for Sigil trigger), some also run 2pc T3; full 3-4pc T3 is used by some SoC/AP builds; T3.5 (5-set, haste) is used by pure-haste-stacking builds ("Some go SoC, some go Haste, some go SoR") — [[d:paladin#1464113587537580268]], [[d:paladin#1464113943449440574]], [[d:paladin#1464114415543648429]] (dusty/Jesse Potter, 2026-01-25).
- **[Ret]** Ret PvE crit target: "if you don't reach 51% crit, you're better off going for 33% unbuffed crit and haste instead" (heavy crit builds need a rogue + feral druid in the group to be worthwhile) — [[d:paladin#1463424900730720481]], [[d:paladin#1463424953268568125]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-01-21).
- **[General]** Arcane Resist (AR) target for Anomalus (Karazhan) fight: 200 unbuffed AR needed (140 unbuffed acceptable if using an MRP-style world buff); ~210-220 is considered ideal when progressing — [[d:paladin#1463880940332781730]], [[d:paladin#1463882587910176789]], [[d:paladin#1463886477191807209]] (Adamant/Beldrin, 2026-01-21).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[Prot]** Gear/threat philosophy on gear-tier "outdatedness": prot paladin threat set (T2.5) piece budgets are described as several raid tiers stale/underbudgeted compared to peers (e.g., "chest 3 raids out of date... hurts especially when it's already low HP by the standard of the raid below it") — [[d:paladin#1463493242019385417]] (Grey, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** Prot mitigation-set picks by fight per Grey: Loatheb → T2.5; Maexxna and Hatefuls (4-horsemen) → T2 (though noted T2.5's extra armor may edge out T2's extra HP even here); T2 helmet has only 6 defense, shoulders only 2% block with no BV, chest has "actually zero mitigation, just raw eHP" — [[d:paladin#1463917710944636979]], [[d:paladin#1463919559307165726]], [[d:paladin#1463918096338129079]] (Grey, 2026-01-21).
- **[Prot]** Good early-mitigation trinket picks: Angelista's Touch over Heavy Dark Iron Ring; avoid Shawl (bad); Onyxia Blood Talisman or the AV trinket recommended over generic options — [[d:paladin#1463938024223281314]], [[d:paladin#1463917227286991091]] (Grey, 2026-01-21).
- **[Prot]** Pre-raid mit progression: t0.5 (4-piece "increases damage and healing" bonus) is very strong and used long-term by many rets and occasionally prots for threat sets; the quest chain starts in Ironforge near the throne room — [[d:paladin#1463923716684517521]], [[d:paladin#1463913204601065513]] (Silverhand/Grey, 2026-01-21).
- **[General]** Black Morass (custom dungeon) is heavily overtuned relative to its (MC-level) loot — "basically BWL level content that drops MC level gear"; t0.5's 4pc bonus is "ridiculously good" — [[d:paladin#1463913204601065513]] (Grey, 2026-01-21).
- **[Prot]** T2 vs T2.5 mit tradeoffs discussed at length; general BiS-threat weapon is Thunderfury (TF) — currently strictly better than Desecration for threat in essentially all situations including sub-5-target AoE (a TF pull-proc roughly doubles 5-target threat); after the 1.18.1 patch Desecration may become better for single target too, but its 20% attack-speed-reduction debuff will likely remain best-in-slot for group mitigation if no one else brings it — [[d:paladin#1464064346807078933]], [[d:paladin#1464067873533202545]], [[d:paladin#1464068476820783237]] (Grey/Jesse Potter/Adamant, 2026-01-23).
- **[Prot]** TF will likely remain BiS mitigation weapon on Ambershire "for the better part of a year" even post-patch since it can't be easily beaten by anything except possibly a future Grim Batol item — [[d:paladin#1464076898165592199]], [[d:paladin#1464077177292193944]] (dusty/Silverhand, 2026-01-23).
- **[Prot]** Weapon proc math: Might of Menethil (MoM, 1374 AP, 2 crit) vs Severance (1267 AP, 2 crit) — the difference is roughly equivalent to 107 more AP elsewhere; MoM nets ~11.5 more DPS with Crusader Strike and ~8.2 more DPS with SoC — used to argue that legendary/rare weapon slots are overvalued relative to gearing the rest of the set — [[d:paladin#1463189997300748535]], [[d:paladin#1463191139036303525]] (Grey, 2026-01-20).
- **[SoC/Ret]** Best-in-slot weapons for SoC (AP ret): MoM into Shar'tateth is BiS; Ashbringer is not BiS for pure SoC damage optimization but is close enough that the gap is "so miniscule it doesn't matter" and it offers cross-build versatility (works for both SoC and spellret) — [[d:paladin#1464120221412626466]], [[d:paladin#1464124911436959815]], [[d:paladin#1464125477491834951]] (dusty/Jesse Potter, 2026-01-25). Ashbringer is unambiguously BiS for spellret (SoR) due to its drain-life scaling with SP — [[d:paladin#1464115907730210836]], [[d:paladin#1464115901204009172]].
- **[Ret]** For weapon choice at level ~60 just past leveling: Fist (Kara10) or Jeklik's weapon (ZG) recommended pickups; Runeblade craftable via Swordsmithing or the Argent Dawn rep weapon (Demonsbane) are good early-endgame options; farm Argent Dawn rep fastest via Scholomance (boss density + scourgestones), Stratholme Undead side also good — [[d:paladin#1463665646553403534]], [[d:paladin#1463690267025604618]] (dusty/gastro, 2026-01-21).
- **[Prot]** Fire Resistance (FR) gear for Garr adds/BWL tanking: Dark Iron Helm + Legs (enchanted) = 110 FR alone; Ony Cloak enchanted = 31 FR; "fiery chain" items ~20+20+25 FR combined; base racial/consumable buffs (aura + juju) give +75 FR; with a UBRS FR buff, a full FR-piece stack can exceed the ~315 cap ("too much") — recommend a "mixed" FR set targeting less than full cap and prioritizing stam/avoidance once base FR from aura+juju+2 pieces covers most of it — [[d:paladin#1463474520936419328]], [[d:paladin#1463475351933157522]], [[d:paladin#1463475803428753560]], [[d:paladin#1463476791942582315]] (Aelgagar/Arle, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** For solo-tanking Garr's Firesworn adds: full mitigation/stam matters more than max FR; Drakefire Amulet (only 10 stam) is not ideal — prefer a higher-stam neck; dedicated healer/dispeller assignment required — [[d:paladin#1463478824091254898]], [[d:paladin#1463477372052570200]], [[d:paladin#1463477890120286405]] (Aelgagar/Pastor, 2026-01-20).

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[General]** Arcane-Giant-style spellpower concoction stacking rule: Concoction of the Arcane Giant does NOT stack its SP bonus with either Dreamwater or Greater Arcane Elixir (confirmed by in-game testing on a target dummy with Flame Shock) — but Dreamtonic's SP bonus DOES stack fine with Arcane Giant, since Dreamtonic isn't classified the same way; Dreamwater's small extra tick boost when combined comes from its AP component (not SP) — [[d:paladin#1463000320459149362]], [[d:paladin#1463578191703703582]], [[d:paladin#1463624759999856822]] (TRUE HEIR/Adamant, 2026-01-20/22; confirmed via screenshots).
- **[General]** Practical consequence: use "Concoction of the Arcane Giant" together with Winterfall Firewater + Dreamtonic (not Dreamwater or Greater Arcane Elixir) for max non-redundant buffs, OR use the separate Dreamwater + Greater Arcane Elixir + Elixir of Giant's Strength combo, but don't mix Arcane-Giant-concoction with Dreamwater/GAE — [[d:paladin#1462994782975426747]], [[d:paladin#1462995375718928425]] (Adamant, 2026-01-20).
- **[General]** Winterfall Firewater's "grow large" visual effect has no confirmed mechanical impact on hitbox/range/LoS based on player testing (attacking, being attacked, ability range, mob follow distance) except that large-model races can be physically blocked from walking through some tight spaces — [[d:paladin#1462997919178424383]], [[d:paladin#1462998196228985014]] (Grey/Jesse Potter, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** Spell-hit trinkets like Mind's Key give only the SP bonus for Holy Strike; they do not affect auto-attack or SoR hit chance — [[d:paladin#1463077201296625771]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-01-20). Spell hit does matter for Consecration and Exorcism damage-landing, and for weapon-proc effects, but is not chased as a stat by prot due to gear scarcity — [[d:paladin#1463078401337200866]], [[d:paladin#1463078515841830999]] (Seviry/Aelgagar, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** Eye of the Dead (holy) trinket: charge consumption confirmed to trigger off Daybreak procs during Mending Light's healing-spell buff window, even if the Daybreak already applied to the target before the trinket buff started (one Mending Light hitting 7 targets with 4 already-active Daybreaks consumed no charge) — [[d:paladin#1463209915760771143]], [[d:paladin#1463210207940313088]], [[d:paladin#1463210238420324506]] (Mithreon/Marfusia, 2026-01-20).
- **[General]** Buff-slot management for macro reliability: Seals/Judgements still function when the player is over the visual buff cap, but judgement-check macros (checking for a seal buff by name) break over cap; a long list of buffs known to cause this includes Divine Spirit, Prayer of Spirit, Inspiration, Ancestral Healing, Healing Way, Arcane Intellect/Brilliance, Thorns, Fire Shield, Ghost Costume, all Blessings (Sanctuary/Wisdom/Salvation, greater and normal), Daybreak, Rejuvenation/Regrowth (druid T3 set bonus names), Renew, Power Word: Shield, Holy Power, Chastise Haste, Proclaim/Empower Champion, Champion's Grace — [[d:paladin#1463164697246171217]] (Grey, 2026-01-20). Recommended addons: VCB (Visual Cap Buffs, blocks set-bonus/talent buffs from taking slots), AutoBuffRemover / ABR (for class buffs like Thorns), Doiteauras / "Weakaura Lite" for tracking buff durations — [[d:paladin#1463157811897831506]], [[d:paladin#1463157946484523008]], [[d:paladin#1463091505479815201]] (Jesse Potter/Seviry, 2026-01-20).
- **[Prot]** Salvation debate: healing paladins generate only 50% threat from heals and ret paladins have Vengeance for threat reduction, so some argue paladins should rarely get Salvation put on them by others; disagreement — Salvation is still considered valuable/necessary because ret paladins CAN rip aggro (bad tank, SoC god-roll on pull, or heavy outgear) — [[d:paladin#1463163171262042257]], [[d:paladin#1463164600374661243]], [[d:paladin#1463164862623518935]] (Aljeron/Beldrin/angels1m, 2026-01-20).
