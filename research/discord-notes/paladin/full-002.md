- **[Prot LazyScript macro]** Community-shared one-button filler macro using the LazyScript addon (github.com/laytya/LazyScript). Single-target form: cancel Blessing of Salvation, apply Blessing of Sanctuary if missing, apply Seal of Righteousness (or Wisdom if mana <60%), Judgement if seal active and target lacks the debuff (mana-gated 20-90%, target >50% hp), Holy Strike, Holy Shield (mana-gated, also refresh if Redoubt is up), reapply Seal of Righteousness if missing and mana >95%. AoE form: same buff logic, then Consecrate, Judgement if SoR active, Seal of Wisdom if no seal, Holy Shield, Holy Strike. Poster notes Holy Strike used instead of Crusader Strike for higher threat — [[d:paladin#1428593456984489985]] (2025-10-16). Jesse Potter explicitly disagrees with one-button macro rotations — [[d:paladin#1428597751637344257]].
- **[Ret]** Consolidated P1 rotation restated several times: keep Seal of Righteousness up at all times, Judge and reseal inside the weapon-swing window, Crusader Strike on CD, use Holy Strike to keep the Holy Might buff up — [[d:paladin#1429231666630758492]], [[d:paladin#1429809968550510734]] (2025-10-18/20).
- **[Ret spellstrike order]** For a fully spellstrike-stacked SoR build, each weapon swing and each seal-damage tick separately roll the full spellstrike chain in this order: weapon damage → Spellstrike Ring → Spellstrike Glove → Spellstrike Neck → Spellstrike Weapon → Enchant, then again for seal damage → Ring → Glove → Neck → Weapon → Enchant — meaning a full spellstrike loadout effectively fires twice per swing (once off the auto, once off the seal proc) — [[d:paladin#1429353544720584777]] (2025-10-18). Multiple "Equip: Adds X Elemental Damage" effects (spellstrike stat sticks) each trigger individually rather than merging into one effect — [[d:paladin#1429353294056394882]] (2025-10-18).
- **[Prot]** Maintaining the Zeal haste buff as Prot costs roughly 1 Crusader Strike per 3 Holy Strikes, estimated at about a 25% threat loss for making that trade — [[d:paladin#1429083097990693047]] (2025-10-17).
- **[Holy]** Full melee-healing playstyle explained: Holy Paladin healing is "pretty much just spamming Flash of Light," even while melee healing — the only difference melee-healing makes is pressing Holy Strike every 5.5-6s; Daybreak procs off Holy Strike, and Judgement of Wisdom returns mana when Holy Strike lands. Holy Shock can be cast while moving. Most fights allow melee-healing, but some (e.g. Heigan) require staying ranged, where melee healing would be "useless and trolling the raid" — [[d:paladin#1429351510759706676]], [[d:paladin#1429351830529376257]], [[d:paladin#1429351897331925012]], [[d:paladin#1429352367400157204]], [[d:paladin#1429375020945113109]] (2025-10-18).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Weapon skill quest raises melee skill to 308 (many prot tanks reportedly don't know this quest exists); each point of weapon skill removes 0.2% miss chance — [[d:paladin#1428098197720531034]], [[d:paladin#1428099672697733311]] (2025-10-15).
- **[Prot]** Hit-cap math at 308 weapon skill: 3% hit from talents + 3% hit from gear leaves ~0.4% miss chance (deemed negligible by some); to fully cap miss chance to 0% needs 4% hit from gear on top of 3% talent hit at 308 skill — [[d:paladin#1428097514103373988]], [[d:paladin#1428097828806328471]], [[d:paladin#1428097933739294912]], [[d:paladin#1428099330216169643]] (2025-10-15).
- **[Prot]** Many progressing Prot tanks are reportedly not actually hit-capped in practice — [[d:paladin#1428098085967499377]], [[d:paladin#1428099145599680683]] (2025-10-15).
- **[Ret/Prot]** Stat priority by build (Jesse Potter): Prot valuing threat wants more SP; Prot valuing mitigation wants Stamina and other defensive stats; Ret going SoR wants more SP; Ret going SoC wants more Strength and Crit than SP — [[d:paladin#1428243977647296626]], [[d:paladin#1428244028339785778]], [[d:paladin#1428244100951576728]] (2025-10-15).
- **[Ret]** Multiple copies of the same "+X elemental damage" trinket/item (e.g. Incendosaur) each proc separately — 3 copies gives (up to) 4 total proc instances per hit — [[d:paladin#1428488461387698396]] (2025-10-17).
- **[Prot]** Weapon-skill hit conversion clarified: 5 weapon skill = 1% hit; at 315 weapon skill (the cap where glancing-blow penalties stop improving) you need 5% hit from other sources to be capped, but at 308 skill you need 6.4% (round to 7%) — a more precise figure than the earlier simplified "3%+3%=capped" claim — [[d:paladin#1429790128997666816]], [[d:paladin#1428802416165261505]] (2025-10-18/19). Humans need only +4 weapon-skill from gear/racial to reach 315; other races need +7, which (outside axes/maces) isn't achievable from a single item — [[d:paladin#1429000615639388230]], [[d:paladin#1429000705967915061]], [[d:paladin#1429000767049695262]] (2025-10-18). Stacking Human racial + book + Edgemaster's enchant gives +15 weapon skill = 3% hit, plus 3% from the Precision talent, leaving only 1 more gear piece needed for full hit cap — [[d:paladin#1429790458661437460]] (2025-10-18).
- **[Prot]** Weapon-skill cap for reducing glancing blows and increasing crit/hit is 315; going past 3% hit from talents to reach 315 total weapon skill is worthwhile because it also frees a gear slot for +1% crit or SP instead of a hit item — [[d:paladin#1428802416165261505]], [[d:paladin#1429000010699112469]], [[d:paladin#1429002803220709478]] (2025-10-18).
- **[Prot]** Full Kara40-progression Prot stat target list: 8% hit cap, 102.4% avoidance while Holy Shield is active, as much Stamina as possible while also maximizing Spell Power, plus meeting encounter-specific resistance requirements (e.g. Fire Resist for Baron and Ragnaros in MC) — [[d:paladin#1429447737610604635]], [[d:paladin#1429447797329100810]] (2025-10-18).
- **[Prot]** Righteous Fury gives a base +60% threat multiplier; Improved Righteous Fury adds 75% of that base (an additional 45 percentage points), for roughly +105% total threat with both — [[d:paladin#1429070988141793311]] (2025-10-18).
- **[Prot]** Strength on Prot gear converts to Holy Shield block value via (reportedly) a 50:1 conversion ratio — a small but real reason Str isn't fully dead on tank gear — [[d:paladin#1429743319373713430]], [[d:paladin#1429743446360461312]] (2025-10-19). Both Strength and Intellect are otherwise considered largely irrelevant "dead" stats on Prot gear — [[d:paladin#1429743057669984266]] (2025-10-19).
- **[Prot]** Flat Armor argued more valuable than avoidance/Defense rating for a newer/still-gearing tank, since avoidance is RNG per-hit while armor reliably reduces every hit; high avoidance values aren't needed before AQ40 — [[d:paladin#1429743197369798756]], [[d:paladin#1429743252839600138]], [[d:paladin#1429743323635253369]], [[d:paladin#1429743401258979359]], [[d:paladin#1429743435304407100]] (2025-10-19).
- **[Ret]** Holy Strike's scaling is split by component: the damage portion scales 0.43 from Spell Power; the self-heal portion scales only 0.05 base (0.3 total with the relevant talent). The heal always procs for a set proportion (~50%) and each Holy Strike heal instance rolls its own separate crit check — [[d:paladin#1429726374540869703]], [[d:paladin#1429726622646538282]], [[d:paladin#1429726761213759598]] (2025-10-18).
- **[Ret]** SoC proc-rate math example (Excinerus): for a 3.6-speed weapon, SoC at 7 PPM procs ~42% of swings dealing 70% weapon damage → ~29.4% average extra weapon damage; for a 3.8-speed weapon at 7 PPM, ~44% proc chance → ~31% extra damage. Haste raises effective PPM, so SoC (a %-weapon-damage proc) gains more value from haste than a flat-damage-with-scaling effect like Ashbringer's proc, and slower weapons benefit more from haste stacking with SoC — [[d:paladin#1429182161612837136]], [[d:paladin#1429182274594672754]], [[d:paladin#1429182764762005524]] (2025-10-17).
- **[Ret]** Haste stacks multiplicatively across separate on-gear haste/extra-attack effects (e.g. 1.01× per +1% effect, 1.02× per +2% effect, etc. multiplied together), rather than summing additively; Zeal's 15% haste multiplies every other haste source. Haste can actually hurt Windfury Totem uptime specifically due to ICD collisions (WFT often already saturated by SoR's double-hit) — [[d:paladin#1429184297423867947]], [[d:paladin#1429184654585499780]], [[d:paladin#1429184790338211911]] (2025-10-17). Whether multiple small "extra attack %" procs (e.g. HoJ plus another 1% proc) merge into a single effect (as in Blizzard's client) or apply as separate multiplicative effects (as suspected on Turtle) was raised as an open question — [[d:paladin#1429183502292619274]], [[d:paladin#1429183641891766444]], [[d:paladin#1429183839003218081]] (2025-10-17).
- **[Ret]** Haste is valued highly by SP-focused Ret players on BiS gear because it works out to roughly 1% extra damage per 1% haste, "due to how seal works" (more swings = more seal procs) — [[d:paladin#1429483366297768097]], [[d:paladin#1429483409054498937]] (2025-10-19).
- **[Ret]** Crit stat targets for SoR spell-ret: aim for roughly 20-30% total melee crit fully consumed and raid-buffed (Druid crit pack, Rogue's Tricks of the Trade); above 30% is "too much Agi enchanted" and unneeded — 20-25% is mainly valuable for quickly stacking Vengeance via Divine Strike crits, since SoR itself doesn't scale much further from crit — [[d:paladin#1430031868757147749]], [[d:paladin#1430032596082888835]], [[d:paladin#1430031065535217696]] (2025-10-21). A well-optimized AP Ret build can reach ~47-48% crit fully consumed/buffed (e.g. via items like Gloves of the Turtle's Mercy [GoTM] and Wristguards of Vengeance rather than Wing of the Timelord+HoJ) — [[d:paladin#1430035497127772210]], [[d:paladin#1430035722613555342]], [[d:paladin#1430036113086615663]] (2025-10-21). T3.5 set pieces give only single-digit % crit boosts individually but each carries ~20-30 SP — [[d:paladin#1430036325125591050]] (2025-10-21).
- **[Ret]** SoR builds don't need to prioritize reaching 315 weapon skill as much as SoC/AP builds, since they care less about glancing-blow frequency/damage and can get their hit from elsewhere instead — [[d:paladin#1430031980652662884]] (2025-10-21).
- **[Holy]** Approximate stat conversion for Holy healbonus at ~1200+ healbonus: 1% spell crit ≈ 38-40 healbonus equivalent; 1% haste ≈ 15-20 healbonus equivalent (haste value is more subjective, depends how much you want to "snipe" heals for parsing) — [[d:paladin#1429167222923984966]], [[d:paladin#1429366184922447913]], [[d:paladin#1429366561847644173]] (2025-10-16/18).
