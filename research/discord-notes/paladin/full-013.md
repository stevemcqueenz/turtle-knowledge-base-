## Rotation: single target
- **[prot]** Opener/threat rotation: pre-cast Holy Shield before entering combat (use the GCD before it's "necessary"); open with Judgement of Righteousness for snap threat; don't cast Judgement of Wisdom until you have a solid threat lead (it sacrifices the threat from JoR) or have a second paladin do JoW instead — Dodging/Jesse Potter [[d:paladin#1456851251349164235]], [[d:paladin#1456851425190744209]], [[d:paladin#1456851647383998607]], [[d:paladin#1456851793370808474]] (2026-01-02).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[prot]** Avoidance mechanic: dodge/parry/block/miss are summed and, if total exceeds 100%, proportionally squished to fit 0-100 (e.g. 40% dodge/15% parry/70% block/10% miss = 135% raw → squished to dodge 0-30%, parry 30-41.1%, block 41.1-92.9%, miss 92.9-100%) — Grey [[d:paladin#1456719654591398069]] (2026-01-02, detailed math, caveated as approximate).
- **[prot]** Block chance is very cheap on the stat budget: 2% raw crit costs roughly the same budget as 5% block chance; ~1% block chance can be added to nearly every slot with minimal budget impact — Grey [[d:paladin#1456716440483664077]], [[d:paladin#1456716531042877551]] (2026-01-02).
- **[prot]** Mitigation roll order disputed: Grey/Seviry say Parry→Dodge→Block (dodging instead of blocking loses the extra threat from Sanctuary/Holy Strike proc-on-block effects); angels1m says it's actually Dodge→Parry→Block — unresolved in-chunk [[d:paladin#1456718384321269812]], [[d:paladin#1456719009314771035]] (2026-01-02).
- **[prot]** Need slightly above 100% avoidance for crit immunity against raid bosses (Lv63): ~102.4% total avoidance vs. exactly 100% against same-level (Lv60) targets — angels1m/Seviry [[d:paladin#1456718679222780085]], [[d:paladin#1456718776694472843]], [[d:paladin#1456718857837478039]] (2026-01-02).

- **[prot]** Daze/defense breakpoint: 140 total defense (gear + talents) makes you daze-immune vs. same-level mobs; subtract 5 defense per level the mob is below you to find the breakpoint for lower-level mobs — Grey [[d:paladin#1457251192957829177]] (2026-01-03).
- **[prot]** Added avoidance % is roughly 1:1 with dodge/parry/block %, and ~0.16 per point of Defense — Mithreon, unconfirmed/unanswered in-chunk [[d:paladin#1457115909125570570]] (2026-01-03).

- **[prot]** Prot gearing/stat-priority framework (Grey, 3-step method): Step 1 — equip Spell Power until you can comfortably hold the boss for the first 10 seconds against your top-threat DPS/offtanks; Step 2 — equip stamina/armor until you can survive 4-5 seconds without a heal; Step 3 — for remaining slots, take the highest combined avoidance (dodge/parry/miss), then fill with Block Value — [[d:paladin#1457743157151404226]] (2026-01-05). - **[prot]** Block Value's real-world mitigation: T3 set + Force Reactive Disk (FoD) gives ~10% damage reduction against Sapphiron/K40 bosses and ~20% against every other Naxx boss, with some ZG bosses often reduced to zero damage; scales up in effectiveness in lower-tier raids, producing smooth/predictable damage intake — but healers can find this "boring" — Grey [[d:paladin#1457824707800862770]] (2026-01-05). - **[prot]** Official/dev stance reported by players (a source of frustration): devs reportedly told Prot Paladins "No, you don't need crush immunity — Bears can't reach it either and survive fine," and separately "No, if you're dying to crushes, gear for crush immunity" — seen as a contradictory pair of responses to community requests for more avoidance/Holy Shield block value — Grey [[d:paladin#1457816931569631352]] (2026-01-05).
- **[general]** Hybrid-class itemization insight: stats that don't functionally overlap (e.g. Strength and Intellect) barely compete for the same item stat-budget pool, so gear with many different stat types (typical of Paladin hybrid gear) can carry a lot of each at a much lower effective "cost" than a single-stat item — Grey [[d:paladin#1458823211134488586]], [[d:paladin#1458825620115951657]] (2026-01-06). This is cited as part of why Paladin tier gear often reads as "spread thin" across many stats rather than being stamina/mitigation-dense like other tanks' gear.
- **[general]** Consecration's spell-damage coefficient is roughly 10 SP = +1 damage per tick at max rank; low ranks (relevant to leveling) have different, lower coefficients than max rank — Jesse Potter [[d:paladin#1459156594133831866]], [[d:paladin#1459157011102040198]]; a full spell-coefficient list is maintained in the channel pins — Destroses [[d:paladin#1459165563489226971]] (2026-01-09).
- **[spellret]** Rough Spell Ret stat priority: spell power > attack speed > physical stats, but don't stack pure SP at the expense of everything else — Iseut [[d:paladin#1459081396164235478]]; in practice ~50% of one player's damage came from SP-scaling sources, with autoattacks at 30.2% and Crusader Strike (partial SP scaling) at 13.6% — prefer balanced tier-set pieces over a pure-SP cloth item [[d:paladin#1459081765388947588]], [[d:paladin#1459081979533197445]] (2026-01-07).

K40 clears take ~1h30-1h45 for efficient guilds; only an estimated 10-20 raid teams server-wide clear it weekly, and well under 10% of the total playerbase (across servers, counting alts) has cleared it — dusty/Jesse Potter/angels1m [[d:paladin#1457941392646213828]], [[d:paladin#1457941646887882808]], [[d:paladin#1457976059952627744]], [[d:paladin#1457969586807771412]] (2026-01-06). Overall K40 is "not that much of a step up from Naxx" — the main difference is that every raid member must be ready to execute mechanics, vs. Naxx where usually only a few pre-assigned people handle them — Grey [[d:paladin#1457969661483290666]] (2026-01-06).
- **[general]** Kruul (K40 boss) is regarded as "the only objectively shit-coded boss... to this day I never saw someone doing it as intended" — angels1m [[d:paladin#1457897496834478307]]; a Paladin's job on Kruul is to wear 300 fire resist and, roughly every 25 seconds, use Exorcism + Judgement to pull another Infernal add out of the melee stack the moment it spawns — Grey [[d:paladin#1457970802442899541]] (2026-01-06). Incantagos's P2 (6-crystal, damage-type-split mechanic) is criticized as overly comp-restrictive; a suggested (unimplemented) fix is reducing it to 3 crystals with paired damage types and slightly more HP — angels1m/Adamant [[d:paladin#1457899316885848277]], [[d:paladin#1457899613913743392]] (2026-01-06).

## PvP
- **[ret]** Ret Paladin PvP viability has declined severely since rogues gained enough burst (via Dissolvent Poison and stun-lock openers) to kill paladins "in greens" within their stun window; solo world-PvP viability used to exist before this — Excinerus [[d:paladin#1456998726626181292]], [[d:paladin#1457176906410623134]] (2026-01-03).
- **[general]** Historical note: Holy Strike used to be extremely powerful in PvP "for like a millisecond of game time," traumatizing a generation of PvP players; combined with Berserking and stacking % damage bonuses, near-one-shots of average-geared players were possible; this incident is cited as the reason Divine Shield was hit with a damage-taken penalty instead of an attack-speed penalty — Dodging/Southshore Crusader Vidnar [[d:paladin#1457049478455623853]], [[d:paladin#1457050386702798979]], [[d:paladin#1457065119174492341]] (2026-01-03).
- **[hybrid]** Suggested "Holy Prot" PvP hybrid build: double T2, Thunderfury, and a sigil trinket; judge Wisdom for infinite mana, Holy Shock yourself, act as support ("more like an MBT" — mana battery) for your team; enemies will fixate on you or hunt for the healer keeping you alive — Excinerus [[d:paladin#1457175546072137914]], [[d:paladin#1457175626850111669]], [[d:paladin#1457175724338450454]], [[d:paladin#1457175810040664249]] (2026-01-03). Divine Sacrifice/reflect called weak in PvP: 9% damage reduction over 10 seconds is too slow to matter by the time you reach a target, and no kill credit is given if the reflect kills the attacker — Excinerus [[d:paladin#1457175191913496608]], [[d:paladin#1457175350537617509]], [[d:paladin#1457175411577590003]] (2026-01-03).
- **[ret]** Suggested PvP macro: cast Crusader Strike if health is below a threshold and Holy Shock is on cooldown, otherwise cast Holy Strike — Excinerus [[d:paladin#1457176499038715914]] (2026-01-03).
- **[pvp talents]** For PvP prot/hybrid builds, take the "broken" rank-2 talent giving 1.03s-cast Holy Lights over the rank-1 hit-chance talent; for PvE, check channel pins for hit-dependent builds — Excinerus [[d:paladin#1457178151737233518]], [[d:paladin#1457178602742354033]] (2026-01-03).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[general]** Simple failsafe escape macro: `/cast Divine Shield` + `/use Hearthstone` — Magik [[d:paladin#1457452077465731114]] (2026-01-05); alternative is using the Hand of Edward (Turtle item) for an instant hearthstone-like escape — dusty [[d:paladin#1457459351764598865]].
- **[general]** VCB (a buff-management addon referenced repeatedly for blacklisting specific buff icons, e.g. the Farraki libram buff or Divine Spirit) is standard in this community for raid buff-slot management — Grey/Mithreon [[d:paladin#1457142522508869667]], [[d:paladin#1457108911248117975]] (2026-01-02/03).
- **[ret]** Suggested PvP macro for damage rotation under Crusader Strike/Holy Shock cooldown logic — see PvP section [[d:paladin#1457176499038715914]].
- **[general]** Cooldown-tracking addons for Paladin abilities (Judgement/Crusader Strike/Consecration icons on the unit frame): Mpowa recommended, though setup can be "ridiculously unstable" before it stabilizes; DoiteAuras (`https://github.com/Player-Doite/DoiteAuras`) recommended as the closest thing to WeakAuras; Simple Auras also suggested as an alternative — Domy/Grey/Lyrazia/Gs [[d:paladin#1458626733300388023]], [[d:paladin#1458630082271645788]], [[d:paladin#1458634973430157446]], [[d:paladin#1458641889535197247]] (2026-01-08).
- **[general]** Libram-swapping addons/macros are a common (if controversial) part of the Paladin meta: the "LibramSwap-Alj" addon (`https://github.com/Jahfonte/LibramSwap-Alj`) auto-swaps librams mid-combat (e.g. Crusader always gets its damage libram, Crusader Strike/Holy Strike always get the reduced-GCD libram, auto-swap to an execute libram near 35% HP, and swap for Freedom/Hand of Justice/Flash of Light too) — Aljeron [[d:paladin#1458459279391461557]]. A second, different libram-swap addon (recommended by dusty) similarly auto-swaps to a "Final Judgement" libram when the target hits ~35% HP and back to Eternal Tower on a Strike, doing the libram-swap, judge, and reseal all within the same GCD, without triggering an extra GCD itself due to how spell batching works — dusty [[d:paladin#1459289993460842735]], [[d:paladin#1459291423579836642]], [[d:paladin#1459291670159036436]]. This addon (and libram swapping generally) requires the SuperWoW client mod, considered "a must-have in raids" — angels1m/Destroses/dusty [[d:paladin#1458475852181540906]], [[d:paladin#1458478992578576384]], [[d:paladin#1459301502769303764]]. Some players report the Final Judgement auto-swap not working reliably without a manual macro calling the addon's function directly — Beldrin/Aelgagar [[d:paladin#1459305169966731294]], [[d:paladin#1459314957962449037]] (2026-01-10). Many players (Jesse Potter, Destroses) dislike libram-swapping as "degenerate" scripting and would prefer librams just have flat stats instead — [[d:paladin#1458461242472796314]], [[d:paladin#1458461393798959288]], [[d:paladin#1458461520794091710]] (2026-01-06).
- **[general]** Some raiders report PallyPower has recently been causing low framerate and disconnects in raid groups; not corroborated by everyone (dusty reports only the older, long-standing issue of it "sometimes just won't work") — Artifyce/dusty [[d:paladin#1457974153767419956]], [[d:paladin#1457982948480647319]] (2026-01-06).
- **[general]** The manuscript quest for extra weapon skill (5 skill of your choice = 1% hit) is a repeatable multi-quest chain (~5 quests ending in killing a tough enemy); you only need to do the first group of quests once, then repeat just the final kill-quest for each additional weapon type you want skill in — Destroses/Jesse Potter [[d:paladin#1459117015502422105]], [[d:paladin#1459117175007739946]], [[d:paladin#1459117273997643849]], [[d:paladin#1459117390767067253]] (2026-01-09).
