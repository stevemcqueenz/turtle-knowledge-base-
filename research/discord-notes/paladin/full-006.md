## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Under CC2 (current content patch referenced), trash/mob HP melts fast, reducing value of ramp-up abilities like Consecration; a poster asked devs (Lhian) to frontload Consecration's damage curve, but devs replied endgame parsing isn't the only environment to balance for — [[d:paladin#1436793203482296331]], [[d:paladin#1436794245171249303]], [[d:paladin#1436794443465494629]].
- **[Spellret/SoC]** Devs "tried to buff SoC's relative power vs Spellret with 1.18 but it fell flat," and are still looking for ways to do it (per Dodging, citing ongoing dev discussion) — [[d:paladin#1436795183902490675]].
- **[T2 3-set]** T2 3-piece Ret bonus (procs a "spellstrike" effect, interacts with Wing of the Timelord and on-spell trinkets like Accord) is confirmed by devs to stay as-is/not be changed, despite being considered possibly unintended — [[d:paladin#1436830991716192390]], [[d:paladin#1436841670938198027]].
- **[Chili/Shadow Oil]** Devs previously said they would not change Chili and Shadow Oil scaling, then later nerfed/removed it anyway — [[d:paladin#1436841557734199377]].
- **[Prot]** Speculation (unconfirmed) that Prot will get a new capstone button and that damage reduction may move to Divine Protection to differentiate it from Divine Shield — [[d:paladin#1436477639299825708]] (single source, speculation).
- **[Reckoning]** Turtle's Reckoning triggers on block (not crit) and functions like "parry-haste but on a block"; considered weak/underwhelming compared to other versions — [[d:paladin#1436436088485842974]], [[d:paladin#1436946684637745163]] (context).
- **[Loot]** Paladin/Shaman Tier 2 legs on Ragnaros can only drop as a single loot slot (not duplicated like other classes' T2 leg slots), making them rarer than other classes' T2 legs — [[d:paladin#1437196001570984036]], [[d:paladin#1437198364394651768]], [[d:paladin#1437203127777624246]] (community-confirmed via loot table digging).
- **[FRD durability]** Force Reactive Disk (FRD) was patched so it now breaks/loses durability more reliably (previously sometimes wouldn't lose durability after dying with it equipped, treated as a bug); repair cost went up as a result — [[d:paladin#1436962955270029313]] (context), [[d:paladin#1437433247666536479]], [[d:paladin#1437505932555849809]].
- **[Dreams Herald]** Devs fixed a bug on Dreams Herald that was reportedly scaling inversely down to zero with spell power — [[d:paladin#1437171120158543934]], [[d:paladin#1437171218015846651]] (+4 reactions, single source/no detail on final fix).
- **[Prot T2.5]** T2.5 3-piece Prot threat bonus is expected to be nerfed from 12% to 6% (matching the already-nerfed Ret version), "with 90% certainty" per Mithreon, pending the December balance preview; devs have reportedly said "we will bring SoC in line with SoR" and then nerfed SP on tier sets instead, a pattern heligaknut calls out as recurring — [[d:paladin#1438152500686360648]], [[d:paladin#1438152724272255098]], [[d:paladin#1438152819331829792]], [[d:paladin#1438158525917302885]].
- **[Prot rebalance]** Per Mithreon citing dev commentary, Prot base ability damage will be reduced but scaling improved in an upcoming pass — [[d:paladin#1437729220347433071]].
- **[SoC buff]** A Seal of Command buff for Ret is expected "next patch" per ongoing dev discussion, but exact contents unconfirmed beyond "tier bonus changes and SoC" — [[d:paladin#1437719065861951488]], [[d:paladin#1437725632732594286]], [[d:paladin#1437731416472289321]].
- **[Champion buff]** The "Champion" buff item/effect is being removed (not reworked); the associated talent and button reportedly stay — [[d:paladin#1437719065861951488]], [[d:paladin#1437720244171833415]].
- **[Turtlogs]** The community parsing site (Turtlogs) now attempts to detect and display whether a Ret Paladin's logs used SoC or SoR, though it's "buggy" and doesn't detect talents (so someone talented into both can be misattributed) — [[d:paladin#1438703755670589480]], [[d:paladin#1438704292138586122]], [[d:paladin#1438704536335286363]].
- **[Bug: T3.5 5pc Ret]** The T3.5 5-piece Ret "Daybreak/Crusade" proc reportedly does not correctly grant party members their own stacks beyond 1, even as the Paladin's own stacks reach 2-3 — reported as likely unsubmitted bug — [[d:paladin#1439186627716055041]], [[d:paladin#1439186844712566815]]. Separately, multiple Paladins in the same raid not stacking Daybreak with each other is described as an intentional (if unpopular) design change — [[d:paladin#1437708204644765778]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret/AP vs Spellret]** Consensus: Spellret (SoR/hybrid using spell power + weapon procs) out-parses AP Ret (SoC/raw stats) at endgame; on Ambershire (behind in gear) AP is "the only reasonable/viable path atm due to available gear," while on Nordanaar/Tel'Abim hybrid spellret is "much more consistent at higher DPS parsing numbers" — [[d:paladin#1436108687826747433]], [[d:paladin#1436108810677780561]]. AP Ret is still called "viable" but does less damage than SP builds — [[d:paladin#1436947880748384338]]. Both benefit from AP/SP to differing degrees — [[d:paladin#1436122626761167020]], [[d:paladin#1436122638140313632]].
- **[Ret]** SoR consistency vs SoC burst: SoR provides guaranteed procs every hit and is "consistent... overall it beats SoC," while SoC is high-variance (can outdps SoR if lucky/crits every proc, but unreliable across a full raid) — [[d:paladin#1436788194564636703]], [[d:paladin#1437073280493420624]] (Mithreon: "SoR has higher sustained damage, but SoC has higher burst").
- **[Ret]** dusty (Make Shock Live Again) argues Ret's split identity (SoC vs SoR/hybrid) is bad design; proposes fully committing Ret to SoC and moving all "spelladin" scaling into Holy (Exorcism/Holy Shock) — single opinion, some agreement (+2 from Destroses) — [[d:paladin#1436866451662176257]], [[d:paladin#1436869978031919147]], [[d:paladin#1436909825886916669]].
- **[Prot]** Vengeance-Prot (threat via Vengeance stacks instead of Righteous Strikes) called "not really worth it" — building Vengeance stacks takes too long vs. reliable Holy Strike+Righteous Strikes threat — [[d:paladin#1436873650182553741]], [[d:paladin#1436873998410715189]], [[d:paladin#1436875648164692050]] (disputed/meme-only: "you can probably make a meme build around vengeance… but you're trolling yourself").
- **[Leveling]** Prot is recommended for leveling (AoE grinding 5 mobs vs 1) over Retribution for smoother/faster leveling and survivability, except in Hardcore mode where big pulls risk death from one mistake — Retribution recommended for HC instead — [[d:paladin#1436477747458347119]], [[d:paladin#1436477838986313849]], [[d:paladin#1436745837156892827]]. Prot leveling AoE grind called "fastest option for pallys" / "Best leveling out there imo" — [[d:paladin#1436712535762931833]], [[d:paladin#1436712556512280618]].
- **[Holy leveling]** Holy Paladin considered "the most do-nothing" playstyle in raids per dusty — [[d:paladin#1437533447521435821]] (opinion/humor, single source).
- **[Necrodin/support build]** A niche "Necrodin" support build exists using Thrash Blade, 3pc Possession of the Lost set, Embrace of the Wind Serpent; relies on T2 3-piece bonus (both Prot and Ret) which triggers on autos; competitive but lower DPS than conventional physical/hybrid unless in full BiS with Wing of the Timelord (WFT/BM cloak) — build details: standard spellret gear base, chest = ST robe or Insomnius, belt = Kara40 belt (summons ghost wolves), weapon = Anti-Ashbringer/Annihilator (for armor pen utility) or Flurry Axe — [[d:paladin#1437308608428773487]], [[d:paladin#1437351104969969737]], [[d:paladin#1437351487704535164]], [[d:paladin#1437352162790346783]], [[d:paladin#1437353723746582548]]. Speculated to become a top Ret performer once BWL/Flurry Axe options are available — [[d:paladin#1437847316659961867]], [[d:paladin#1437847589465624671]].
- **[Ret ST vs AoE]** SoR performs better on AoE overall; SoC performs on par with SoR on single target but only "if you get lucky," since SoC procs by chance rather than every attack — [[d:paladin#1438268451368992890]], [[d:paladin#1438268514589999305]], [[d:paladin#1438268534668005578]], [[d:paladin#1438268637806067835]]. Current server consensus: "spret is a bit better currently, but both specs are good and playable" and devs are on "an endless quest" to bring them in line — [[d:paladin#1438237330795139133]], [[d:paladin#1438238602814554273]], [[d:paladin#1438238776215212114]]. Pre-raid/T1 gear the two are close, but SP starts pulling ahead with better gear (best Ambershire logs come from Tempered Runeblade/spellret users) — [[d:paladin#1438819398663012453]], [[d:paladin#1438819600061169756]], [[d:paladin#1438820192166871050]].
- **[Prot threat-vs-mitigation]** From Naxx onward, a full-mitigation ("full beef") Prot build cannot hold threat — "the name of the game is threat," forcing Prot to gear like "dps with a shield"; full-BiS-threat Prot tops out around 8.5k HP vs. a threat-specced Bear tank's 10k HP + 20k+ armor — [[d:paladin#1438150625543065611]], [[d:paladin#1438150938232623224]], [[d:paladin#1438149510835474513]], [[d:paladin#1438149568348029098]]. Bear (Feral Druid) is the server-meta Main Tank pick over Deep Prot Warrior (Warriors underperform without Thunderfury); Prot Paladin holds up better vs. Bear than Warrior tanks do, due to threat scaling — [[d:paladin#1438151202973028432]], [[d:paladin#1438151366051762277]], [[d:paladin#1438151510621159484]].
- **[Shockadin]** Leveling/playing pure Holy Shock ("Shockadin") is judged not viable because it runs out of mana too fast — [[d:paladin#1437607867996966964]], [[d:paladin#1437609883510898851]]. A raid Holy-Shock-focused build was theorycrafted (talent links posted) but rejected: Holy Shock/Blessed Strikes are deep in the tree, give little DPS gain for the investment, and sacrifice Vengeance/Holy Might/Repentance; Holy Shock's own reset isn't reliable enough to build around without Blessed Strikes — [[d:paladin#1438504409704435743]], [[d:paladin#1438505361408659566]], [[d:paladin#1438506178630914189]], [[d:paladin#1438506248613003355]], [[d:paladin#1438507747560657068]].
- **[Holy tier]** Holy Paladin is "currently the strongest healer" on the server per Dodging — [[d:paladin#1437935236431483101]]. Complaint (Faithroll): Holy Paladin and Resto Shaman are the only healers with zero legendary weapon options — [[d:paladin#1437923418115670138]].
- **[PvP Ret]** Ret's PvP-oriented spec is described as forgiving/"training wheels" with a lot of playstyle freedom, comparable to a low-effort pumper Warrior build — [[d:paladin#1438870450792042581]], [[d:paladin#1438870636591190167]].
- **[Prot leveling talents]** For leveling Prot: prioritize Improved Righteous Fury, Blessing of Sanctuary, Holy Shield; optionally Spiritual Focus (7 pts Holy) for solo healing; Righteous Strikes and Reckoning both good while leveling. Stat priority: Stamina, Intellect, Spellpower, Agility good too; Strength not needed and shouldn't be prioritized — [[d:paladin#1438620364279386212]], [[d:paladin#1438620544583991408]], [[d:paladin#1438620663005839370]], [[d:paladin#1438621849721372742]].

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[Ret PvE]** Discussion around a fantasy/theorycraft custom SotC (Seal of the Crusader talent?) rework by Destroses: instead of judgment damage, SotC converts Zeal stacks for 8% haste per stack (max 3 stacks = 24% from JotC-consumed-Zeal), stacking multiplicatively with base Zeal (15% at 3 stacks) and SotC's own 40% haste, for an effective ~halved attack speed / CS-HS cooldown (~2.94s instead of ~6s) — this is a proposed homebrew change, not live — [[d:paladin#1436564056835100782]], [[d:paladin#1436671685418946590]], [[d:paladin#1436672374769451130]], [[d:paladin#1437030072933417000]] (not implemented in-game, community suggestion).
- **[Prot AoE tank threat build example]** tripleXDengine runs "0/26/25 with vengeance in ret" for AoE-heavy grinding with r13 gear + T3 offpieces + offensive trinkets — [[d:paladin#1437471343905079379]], [[d:paladin#1437471499958091966]].
- **[Aura choice]** Sanctity Aura recommended for single-target raid threat generation if not paired with another Sanctity-aura paladin; if paired with another Sanctity Ret/Prot, foregoing it (via Imp Retribution Aura or other) is fine. Dungeon tank aura choice "doesn't matter that much." Raids favor Sanctity, dungeons favor Improved Retribution Aura — [[d:paladin#1437476055144468632]], [[d:paladin#1437480784046067817]], [[d:paladin#1437482274231947387]], [[d:paladin#1437482767419179098]] (leveling as Holy: take Sanctity Aura).
- **[Prot pull opener]** gastro/Beldrin consensus opener at 60/raid level: Holy Shield pre-pull → Judgement of Righteousness (JoR) → Holy Strike → Consecration → keep Seal of Righteousness (SoR) up most of the time; don't Judgement of Wisdom (JoW) until after opener — [[d:paladin#1436102447406780479]], [[d:paladin#1436103399300010116]], [[d:paladin#1436110448079671348]]. At lower level/gear this can be relaxed (JoW/SoW on trash is fine) — [[d:paladin#1436103228159688775]], [[d:paladin#1436110118084411523]].
- **[Prot detailed opener]** Mithreon: pre-cast Holy Shield, JoR + reseal one target, Holy Strike another, Consecrate, auto-attack a third, taunt fourth (multi-target pull); or if no AoE on the pack: JoR+reseal skull, Holy Strike skull, Consecrate — [[d:paladin#1437090439898599495]], [[d:paladin#1437090527270015049]], [[d:paladin#1437091156960874508]].
- **[PvP Ret specs]** Example point spreads shared: 11/8/32 for a PvE-leaning hybrid, 17/0/34 as a more PvP-focused spread; community stresses PvP specs are "very personal" — [[d:paladin#1438896099019460738]], [[d:paladin#1438896184516149339]], [[d:paladin#1438896478016770138]]. Improved Seal of the Crusader is not worth talenting in PvP since JotC judges are rarely used there; base JotC is still a noticeable boost applied to a tanky target with multiple Paladins present — [[d:paladin#1438896892590424166]], [[d:paladin#1438897099428335687]], [[d:paladin#1438897217174765640]], [[d:paladin#1438897498482671728]].
- **[PvP Ret "frontline bruiser" build]** Detailed hybrid Ret/Holy PvP build (Jesse Potter): gear T2.5 5-set + T3 2-set (swap in 4-set disarm gloves specifically vs. Warriors); optionally spec 3% hit for more offense or Sanctity Aura for +10% holy damage (costs manual aura-swapping mid-fight); talents: normal Ret Repentance, then invest into Holy healing talents for +12% healing and 70% pushback resistance, paired with Concentration Aura + Ironclad. Result: ~1700-2000 unbuffed Holy Light casts at 6.5k HP while still landing ~1k melee crits; playstyle prioritizes healing/peeling for frontline Warriors/Rogues over squishier ranged allies — [[d:paladin#1438847838577754215]], [[d:paladin#1438858149003264110]], [[d:paladin#1438858260546588769]], [[d:paladin#1438858337415463044]], [[d:paladin#1438858396085387418]], [[d:paladin#1438860440259268661]]. Avoid using Judgement of Light/Holy Judgement as Ret in raids since it takes up a buff slot — [[d:paladin#1438858463479595150]].

## Leveling
- **[Prot]** Swap to Prot around level 20, switch to 1H weapon + shield, AoE grind with Consecration + Crusader/Holy Strike, keep Seal of Righteousness up — [[d:paladin#1437356574548037633]], [[d:paladin#1437356948306530314]], [[d:paladin#1437363266836758558]].
- **[Weapon choice leveling]** Fast 1H weapons preferred over slow for leveling procs (JoW benefits from faster weapons; SoR doesn't care about speed) — [[d:paladin#1436456576323158046]]. Specific budget weapon path: Crescent of Forlorn Spirits (lvl 22+, 1.6 speed axe, https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=2044) as a cheaper alternative to Knightly Longsword through the 20s-30s; Knightly Longsword (~1.50 speed, green/uncommon) usable from ~33 to 40-60; Flurry Axe purchasable at 42 to carry to 60 (optional, pricier on Ambershire) — [[d:paladin#1436451838777098300]], [[d:paladin#1436452246824157346]], [[d:paladin#1436743160406609972]].
- **[DME leveling]** Dungeon-of-choice "DME" mentioned for fast leveling 50-60 (via Thrashers, prot spec implied); when dropping into DME, aim right of the big tree to avoid landing near an elite — [[d:paladin#1436295002459344926]], [[d:paladin#1436460513541361735]], [[d:paladin#1436460071902253147]].
- **[Professions leveling]** Recommended: pick 2 gathering professions (herb/mining/skinning) while leveling for gold, then swap one to Engineering (for goblin sappers / repair-bot access) plus whichever gathering prof helps farming (e.g., Herbalism for Lasher farming) — [[d:paladin#1436709623355932702]], [[d:paladin#1436709684177539092]], [[d:paladin#1437232620604887161]], [[d:paladin#1437239245512249476]] ("Level with two gathering profs and you'll be flush with money come 40").

- **[Solo farm]** Level 52-60 "Lasher" farming (Lashers, likely Dire Maul or similar zone) is the standard early gold/xp farm for geared characters; Stratholme farming for well-geared players using Force Reactive Disk (FRD) to pull large groups; requires ~12%+ lifesteal/vampirism, good SP/stamina and defensive stats — [[d:paladin#1436218645905805413]], [[d:paladin#1436222145402241154]], [[d:paladin#1437510379147231422]]. - **[Prot leveling rotation/macro]** Low-level dungeon-tank rotation (dusty): self-buff Righteous Fury + Blessing of Sanctuary; since Taunt and Judgement are both off the GCD, macro Taunt + Holy Strike + Judgement of Righteousness together on the pull, then Consecration/Holy Strike/JoR on cooldown; skip Judgement of the Crusader entirely on trash since it dies too fast for the debuff to matter (plain JoR wins on threat); Seal of Wisdom can substitute for Seal of Righteousness once available, with comparable threat at low level — [[d:paladin#1439393650210508841]], [[d:paladin#1439393719324250185]], [[d:paladin#1439394026016215271]], [[d:paladin#1439399892022726879]]. - **[Leveling seal choice]** Seal of Wisdom for mana, Seal of Light for self-healing while attacking, Seal of Righteousness for more damage; SoR outdamages Seal of the Crusader even at low level (close only very early) and Sanctity Aura doesn't help SotC since it deals no holy damage (SotC only grants attack speed) — [[d:paladin#1439204972376883240]], [[d:paladin#1439205139683606539]], [[d:paladin#1439205656308748298]], [[d:paladin#1439205873397268641]], [[d:paladin#1439206096589033612]]. Crusader Strike vs Holy Strike choice depends on weapon and mana; once Righteous Fury + Improved Righteous Fury are trained, Holy Strike pulls ahead for dungeon threat since it's pure Holy damage (armored targets resist Crusader Strike's physical damage more) — [[d:paladin#1439208524147327147]], [[d:paladin#1439209146670125087]], [[d:paladin#1439209359006634094]].
- **[Leveling tank stats]** For a leveling tank: prioritize Stamina/Intellect; grab spellpower on plate/mail items from quest rewards (including Turtle's custom-zone quests) where available rather than seeking out cloth — [[d:paladin#1439307135748931827]], [[d:paladin#1439307973976391860]], [[d:paladin#1439316626737463576]], [[d:paladin#1439329295276183882]], [[d:paladin#1439329441703657644]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Prot]** Judge Crusader (Seal of the Crusader) on boss/single target, swap to Seal of Wisdom if mana is a problem for self/party — [[d:paladin#1436110118084411523]].
- **[Prot trash management]** Pre-buff Wisdom → judge mob → rebuff Wisdom → mob dies → judge new mob → rebuff, repeating; keep Seal of Wisdom up on trash rather than Righteousness to sustain mana — [[d:paladin#1436676548777345054]], [[d:paladin#1436792398280790159]].
- **[Holy melee/Shockadin identity]** Detailed breakdown (Istenneveben) of why melee-Holy playstyle "feels good" (Holy Strike AoE heal, Holy Might, Crusader Strike Zeal stacking) vs. Ret's abilities being "just charging up DPS" with little frontload; most Ret players still don't understand HS/CS usage even long-term — [[d:paladin#1436906087008698519]], [[d:paladin#1436907012008050770]], [[d:paladin#1436907101762097234]].
- **[Holy rotation, healing]** Basic Holy healing rotation is heavy Flash of Light (FoL) spam with Holy Strike on cooldown; Holy Shock reserved as the emergency "oh-shit" instant heal, Holy Light as the normal backup heal (should ideally be 1.5s cast via the Judgement talent interaction) — [[d:paladin#1437268018316185611]] (joke spam of FoL, +context), [[d:paladin#1436894022730907840]], [[d:paladin#1437271826676846694]], [[d:paladin#1436907214307590215]]. Melee ("Battle-Holy") variant: Crusader Strike resets Holy Shock's cooldown and Holy Strike heals up to 5 targets in 10yd (with Blessed Strikes talented) — most Holy Paladins melee-weave because the healing lost from skipping Holy Strike is "huge" (losing ~100 healing-power-equivalent from not wielding a shield alone); ranged Holy is otherwise the same FoL spam with occasional 1.5s Holy Light/Holy Shock — [[d:paladin#1439170292789280778]], [[d:paladin#1439170416886415391]], [[d:paladin#1437985302445555772]], [[d:paladin#1437992420074590319]].
- **[Ret AP/SoC single target]** Standard AP-Ret rotation: Holy Strike for the Vengeful Strikes buff (only worth it with enough Strength — under ~350 Str it's a DPS loss), Crusader Strike spam, Judgement on cooldown avoiding overlap with autos (to not waste a SoC proc chance), keep Seal of Command up — [[d:paladin#1438269673794703463]], [[d:paladin#1438270236716699788]], [[d:paladin#1438298296530636930]]. Whether Holy Strike is worth using is "debatable for Spelladin" but for Ape/AP Ret "you want the buff" — [[d:paladin#1439392158984437881]], [[d:paladin#1439392237950603448]].
- **[Ret Spellret single target]** For fast-weapon SP/spellret builds, players often spam Crusader Strike and skip Holy Strike (its raw damage is low; SP gear benefits more from the weapon's flat base "AP" via CS) unless using a slow proc weapon like Scythe of the Harvest, where Holy Strike is comparatively better; T3 weapons like Claymore of Unholy Might (no SP scaling but its own +7 weapon skill) favor CS spam too — [[d:paladin#1439553256513736766]], [[d:paladin#1439553479457640541]], [[d:paladin#1439553813173243934]], [[d:paladin#1439554035966414879]].
- **[Prot seal in dungeons]** Seal of Righteousness for single-target threat priority; switch to Seal of Wisdom when ST threat isn't critical and mana is the bottleneck — [[d:paladin#1438580572019757150]], [[d:paladin#1438578951613321256]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot]** Standard prot AoE opener: Holy Shield pre-pull, run in, Holy Strike skull, Judge, Seal of Wisdom, Holy Strike, Holy Shield, Holy Strike loop at low levels; at 60/raiding open with JoR + Holy Strike instead so DPS doesn't rip threat — [[d:paladin#1436101565038596309]], [[d:paladin#1436101728939540530]].
- **[Prot AoE threat]** Consecration is a large chunk of AoE threat but needs 8 full seconds to reach its scaling value; if trash dies in 3-4s, Consecration gives almost no value — its damage scaling curve is considered "broken"/overtuned for full uptime but very strong even at Rank 1 due to that scaling, so downranking Consecration (using Rank 1, cheap with DME libram) is advised when packs die fast — [[d:paladin#1436793203482296331]], [[d:paladin#1436793430897328158]]. Don't Consecrate on only 1-2 enemies dying in <10s — [[d:paladin#1436792665831375060]].
- **[Prot AoE]** In AoE, auto-attack is "less than 50% of your threat"; recommend spreading threat beyond just Consecration when the group is heavy-AoE (e.g., against Shamans with Chain Lightning) — [[d:paladin#1436677185607045232]], [[d:paladin#1437105181161689179]].
- **[Prot AoE tools]** Goblin Sapper Charges recommended for bigger pulls to help hold aggro if DPS can't hold back — [[d:paladin#1437090795336241192]]. No mass taunt exists for Paladins even with perfect play — [[d:paladin#1437101283566686270]].
- **[Prot dungeon pull sequencing]** Open with Taunt on the primary target to fish for an early Holy Shield block; snap threat on ~3 targets can be established before the first Consecration tick even lands; whether Holy Strike or JoR goes to which target depends on relative threat across the pull — always "depends on the situation" — [[d:paladin#1438581300679409719]], [[d:paladin#1438581156021932167]], [[d:paladin#1438581367083368488]], [[d:paladin#1438581604023926885]]. Counterpoint (Arle): dungeon tanks generally don't need snap threat — use a skull mark as a focus target, build threat on the rest, and Taunt the skull target back after a few seconds; opening a pull with Taunt is "a bad idea" in this view — disputed with the above — [[d:paladin#1438586065202974821]], [[d:paladin#1438586498676035645]], [[d:paladin#1438586687537156248]].
- **[Prot vs. AoE caster DPS]** Against a DPS who AoEs immediately on pull (e.g., a mage), pre-position and cast Consecration so mobs run through it before the AoE lands, or open with Consecration + Taunt, then JoR, saving Holy Strike for later — [[d:paladin#1438595413643886643]].
- **[Prot AoE threat reputation, contested]** gastro: "AOE threat is literally what paladins are known for" — [[d:paladin#1439006266289557538]]. Strongly disputed by dusty/Excinerus: Paladins have no AoE taunt and no AoE snap threat "without throwing money at the problem" (FRD/consumables); Warriors are considered better at AoE threat since Whirlwind gets 180% of the Defensive Stance threat bonus, and warrior tank AoE threat also comes heavily from Demoralizing/Battle Shout — [[d:paladin#1439399674250526870]], [[d:paladin#1439400257149730979]], [[d:paladin#1439400608783269979]], [[d:paladin#1439402051514662974]], [[d:paladin#1439060277042876487]], [[d:paladin#1439060568676892795]], [[d:paladin#1439059906534707210]]. Consecration's AoE threat value is entirely dependent on encounter length ("If raid waits 8 seconds for consecration to tick out, it's unmatched") — [[d:paladin#1439086122247651329]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit cap]** 8% hit chance is the cap for both 1H and 2H weapons (dual-wield exception doesn't apply, Paladins can't DW) — [[d:paladin#1436561953936904222]]. For tanking, 6-7% hit is considered sufficient — [[d:paladin#1436600913866395819]]. Weapon skill: 5 weapon skill = 1% hit; after 300 skill, every +1 skill = +0.2% hit up to 315 skill (no bonus after) — [[d:paladin#1437094455130980412]], [[d:paladin#1437095373356273727]]. Humans get +5 weapon skill with sword/mace racial, ending at 311 skill, needing only ~6% hit from other sources to cap — [[d:paladin#1437094495517937784]].
- **[Str/Kings/Holy Might stacking]** Str buffs are multiplicative: x1.1 Blessing of Kings, x1.1 Divine Strength (talent), x1.2 Holy Might (talent) — [[d:paladin#1436321352041627648]].
- **[Haste stacking]** Long, detailed community debate/testing (Hogbobson, Destroses, Excinerus, Mithreon) concludes: equipment/item haste percentages are multiplicative with each other (not additive) — e.g. 2% + 2% from two items yields ~4.04%, not 4% — [[d:paladin#1436339107738685481]], [[d:paladin#1436344070036258896]]. Zeal is confirmed to be 3 separate 5% multiplicative sources (1.05×1.05×1.05, not a flat 15%) — [[d:paladin#1436340559315734589]], [[d:paladin#1436676212717125693]]. Total speed formula: weapon speed / (product of each separate haste-source multiplier) — [[d:paladin#1436674974525161514]], [[d:paladin#1436675573882687548]].
- **[Zeal/Crusading Strikes math example]** With Crusading Strikes: SotC 40% + Zeal x3 15% + JotC 24% = combined ~79% effective haste (theorycraft, not live ability) — [[d:paladin#1436672374769451130]].
- **[Block Value/Zeal]** Zeal's block-value component from Righteous Strikes stacks additively per-stack rather than compounding multiplicatively, per one tester's math (117 base block + 3 stacks → 152.1 if additive vs 155.7 if multiplicative) and includes bonus stats like Strength scaling, not just base values — disputed/being tested, not conclusively resolved in this chunk — [[d:paladin#1436389931755901180]], [[d:paladin#1436390300573634560]], [[d:paladin#1436390482040324331]], [[d:paladin#1436402716820770816]] (Silverhand's competing theory: stacks replace each other, e.g. 30% total rather than 10%×10%×10%, disputed/unresolved).
- **[Block Value from Str]** 1 shield block value (SBV) point gained per 40 Strength — disputed: one user thought it was 20 in vanilla, asked if Turtle changed it to 40; unresolved in chunk — [[d:paladin#1436397048642666599]], [[d:paladin#1436400850921848894]] (contested/unconfirmed).
- **[Holy Shock reset chance]** Holy Shock cooldown reset chance: base 5%, +1% per 100 spellpower/heal bonus (i.e., 0.01% per point of SP/healbonus, not per 1% as sometimes phrased) — [[d:paladin#1436323766492991630]], [[d:paladin#1436328922290851903]], [[d:paladin#1436332688511598692]] (Excinerus notes a %-based roll like 1-(0.99^SP) would be a more "proper" dev implementation, speculative).
- **[SoC scaling]** Seal of Command benefits from both AP and SP (weapon proc has an SP coefficient) but scales at lower efficiency per point than SoR/hybrid builds; SoC procs on-hit like a weapon strike, roughly ~40% (60% less) of the attack count that SoR gets due to how the proc works relative to on-hit weapon effects — [[d:paladin#1436747441134108694]], [[d:paladin#1437204123387826287]].
- **[Blessed Strikes / healing coefficient]** Mending/heal coefficient scaling from the Blessed Strikes talent is additive, not multiplicative: full Blessed Strikes gives ~0.30 Mending Light spell coefficient instead of the base ~0.06 (i.e., 0.05 base + 0.05 × BlessedStrikePoints, not 0.05 × (1 + 0.05×points)) — [[d:paladin#1436298969910083625]], [[d:paladin#1436301336927928320]].
- **[Item enchant note]** Ring enchant gives 9 frost dmg + 5 SP; hand enchant gives 20 holy dmg + 9 SP — cited while discussing whether item budgets should favor "holy damage" over generic "spell power" for class itemization (dev speculation thread, not a confirmed change) — [[d:paladin#1436308575239671900]], [[d:paladin#1436309303819632640]].
- **[Consecrate/HS/JoR SP scaling]** Consecration scales 66% from spellpower, Holy Strike 43%, Judgement of Righteousness (JoR) 71% — [[d:paladin#1437115367167234239]] (single source, cited as useful reference, not contested).
- **[Prot SP threat guideline]** For a fresh level 60 Prot tank, ~100 spellpower is a good starting threat baseline, with well-geared MC-ready tanks around 200+ SP and +6% hit; SP is repeatedly emphasized as the main lever for Prot threat scaling — [[d:paladin#1437109917856038984]], [[d:paladin#1437111549327311049]], [[d:paladin#1437109622711386287]], [[d:paladin#1437115147138236488]].
- **[SP vs Haste vs Str for Ret/SoR]** SP > Str > Haste, in that order, for SoR/hybrid Ret builds: "Haste does not boost abilities' dmg"; SP supports both single-target and AoE and boosts abilities, Str only boosts autos/CS; since a "Chili nerf," Haste "basically only supports auto-attack" — [[d:paladin#1436774610937843802]], [[d:paladin#1436789979748630608]]. Counterpoint (Dodging): with high uptime/Zeal, full haste enchants can add ~20% more swings (100 extra SoR procs per 500 swings in a raid) — disputed, no consensus reached — [[d:paladin#1436786756119822468]], [[d:paladin#1436788393601405109]].
- **[Ability coefficients]** Confirmed damage coefficients: Judgement of Command = 15% AP; Crusader Strike = 90% weapon damage; Seal of Command = 70% weapon damage; Holy Strike = pure Holy damage, scales off SP only — [[d:paladin#1439038792609566720]], [[d:paladin#1439039124836319292]].
- **[Seal of Command PPM]** Seal of Command procs at a flat 7 PPM (not classic-vanilla weapon-speed-based %); per-swing proc chance = 7/60 × weapon speed — [[d:paladin#1439100031922868256]], [[d:paladin#1439422885767942216]], [[d:paladin#1439423031339389050]]. There is no "true PPM" system in vanilla WoW itself (a later-era mechanic), but Turtle's implementation uses PPM-style dynamic-chance formulas regardless of period terminology — [[d:paladin#1438784402514645084]], [[d:paladin#1438799341086179340]].
- **[JoW spellstrike math]** A single spellstrike item source gives roughly 59% average Judgement of Wisdom proc value per activation window (25% chance of 0 procs, 25% chance of a double proc, 50% chance of a single proc) — [[d:paladin#1439550499958296576]].
- **[Righteous Strikes / Zeal block value, real impact]** The extra block value from Righteous Strikes/Zeal is small in absolute terms early game (e.g., 150→200 damage blocked on a 3000 hit) — its real value is the Holy Strike threat bonus, not the block value; Righteous Strikes/Zeal is treated as a dump ability for when Holy Strike can't be used (target temporarily physical- or magic-immune) — [[d:paladin#1438894080477233153]], [[d:paladin#1438894099775099006]], [[d:paladin#1438894159422558293]], [[d:paladin#1438894286925070346]], [[d:paladin#1438894352347955273]].
- **[Weapon skill / hit at 60]** With 311 sword/mace skill (300 base + 5 Human racial), ~6% hit from gear/talents is needed to cap for MC; "just a few missed [hits] per fight can significantly lower your dps" — [[d:paladin#1438605746374705213]], [[d:paladin#1438610289225633972]], [[d:paladin#1438610743758160094]]. Running as a non-Human effectively costs a tank one full hit-cap gear slot's worth of itemization compared to Human — [[d:paladin#1437843153590943785]].
- **[Spellstrike proc scope]** Gear "spellstrike" (chance to deal X elemental damage) procs trigger Judgement of Wisdom, Vengeance, and the Wrath of Cenarius ring — but do NOT trigger Seal of Righteousness/Seal of Command's own on-hit-weapon-effect chain, Ashbringer's Drain Life or other on-hit weapon procs, or Seal/Judgement of Light, since those require true attacks (autos, extra attacks like Windfury) or seal "pseudo-attacks," not spell-type hits — [[d:paladin#1438517783016243361]], [[d:paladin#1438519043815379057]], [[d:paladin#1438555655530352853]], [[d:paladin#1438561509448880129]], [[d:paladin#1438816498851512411]].
- **[Sigil of Ancient Accord proc sources]** Confirmed to proc from: the Ret T2 3-piece spellstrike effect, Consecration's first tick only, and possibly Exorcism/Holy Wrath — confirmed NOT to proc from Seal of Righteousness, Judgement of Righteousness, or ordinary gear spellstrike procs (e.g., Storm Gauntlets) — [[d:paladin#1437915944847540435]], [[d:paladin#1437918132378407004]], [[d:paladin#1437918454966390784]], [[d:paladin#1438407829680685096]], [[d:paladin#1438805139673714739]], [[d:paladin#1438805614141636662]].

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[Ret weapon BiS progression]** Overall power progression (Mithreon): Jeklik's Blade / Fist of the Old God < Demonbane < Scythe of the Harvest < Shadowstrike < Neretzek < Tempered Runeblade < Hand of Ragnaros < Untamed Blade (BWL) < Corrupted Ashbringer — [[d:paladin#1437049959294828740]]. Scythe of the Harvest is BiS "until something better drops" mainly because of guaranteed AH availability + decent PPM proc (vamp/lifesteal), not raw stats; Shadowstrike from MC is a straight upgrade once available — [[d:paladin#1437045589773783041]], [[d:paladin#1437046028594581514]], [[d:paladin#1437047311883702392]]. Tempered Runeblade is craftable/guaranteed (via Blacksmithing) and now roughly on par with Neretzek, described as the "weapon equivalent of the T0.5 set" — [[d:paladin#1436356110041878559]], [[d:paladin#1437046734671974440]], [[d:paladin#1437050259518918676]]. If you already have Runeblade, you can ignore all other weapon upgrades until Untamed Blade drops in BWL — [[d:paladin#1437048158721933483]].
- **[Ashbringer]** (Corrupted/Purified) Ashbringer is BiS for spellret builds because it carries "Drain Life," the only weapon proc that scales 1:1 with spell damage (100% of spell dmg); it beats other options like Might of Menethil (MoM) — [[d:paladin#1436079182928019467]], [[d:paladin#1436080807251804242]]. A separate low-level 2H axe is also claimed to scale 1:1 but unconfirmed by testing — [[d:paladin#1436081123670229073]]. Zulian Slicer possibly also scales similarly but unconfirmed (never seen drop) — [[d:paladin#1436082246430883881]], [[d:paladin#1436082354245337129]]. The Drain Life proc's periodic leech overrides existing leech effects rather than stacking, making it "kinda weak," useful mainly in PvP — [[d:paladin#1436084969746468944]].
- **[Untamed Blade]** Better for AP builds than SP-proc weapons partly due to its 300 Strength on-use/proc effect with good uptime alongside SoR — [[d:paladin#1437068808467517471]].
- **[Ret set swaps]** For switching AP-Ret → Spellret: drop 3pc T1 in favor of 4pc Soulforge (Ret set) for the spellret 4-set bonus; swap Quick Strike Ring for Ring of Electrical Binding (helps Vengeance uptime and JoW procs, but less needed on very short/20s boss fights); T1 3p (head/gloves/boots, the hit% pieces) can be kept alongside partial Soulforge if not going full 4pc — [[d:paladin#1436739557553868892]], [[d:paladin#1436740390924320811]], [[d:paladin#1436740555181654116]], [[d:paladin#1436740663164272672]].
- **[Ret spellret gearing target]** Aim for ~30% buffed crit as spellret/SoR (general endgame amount, may not be reachable in Phase 1); AP stat is still valuable to spellret users even in an SP-focused build — [[d:paladin#1436742530229342320]].
- **[Prot pre-raid/early raid gear]** Prior to K10, 8-piece T1 is the standard Prot BiS reference; T0.5 4-piece is BiS for threat until then; full T1 8p beats 4pc Soulforge for Prot — [[d:paladin#1437056716163256463]], [[d:paladin#1437077852708143115]], [[d:paladin#1437078068203360368]], [[d:paladin#1437078435456356533]].
- **[Prot AQ40 gear]** Suggested threat-BiS direction for AQ40 Prot: 5pc T2.5, Ring of the Fallen God, Wrath of Cenarius (best-in-slot threat trinket, obtainable via reputation/quest grind), Qiraji shield for mitigation — [[d:paladin#1437012888198971482]], [[d:paladin#1437013147696369746]], [[d:paladin#1437221132305432656]].
- **[Prot early-60 gear check]** Example gearing checkpoints: 386 defense / 183 SP unenchanted at fresh 60 considered decent; questions about Syncrestist's Sigil (defense/HP) vs Arcanum of Focus enchant, and whether 2-3pc AQ20 set matters for tanking (unanswered/open in chunk) — [[d:paladin#1437212868809326643]], [[d:paladin#1437213845272924200]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[JoW/SoW mechanics]** Judgement of Wisdom (JoW) has a flat 50% chance to proc per hit — faster weapons proc it more often. Seal of Wisdom (SoW) procs at a fixed 20 procs-per-minute (ppm) rate regardless of weapon speed, meaning it procs more reliably (proportionally) with slower weapons — with a ~3.0 speed weapon, SoW procs on essentially every swing (every 2nd swing roughly, since ~20 swings/min at 3.0 speed vs 20 ppm) — [[d:paladin#1436678404622778581]], [[d:paladin#1436678471542636554]], [[d:paladin#1436678772240683109]], [[d:paladin#1436679759999402166]], [[d:paladin#1436679823681654816]] (Note: total mana gained per minute from SoW is speed-independent since ppm is fixed — only the "per hit" proc rate changes).
- **[SoR proc chain]** Seal of Righteousness (SoR) counts as a melee hit for on-hit weapon procs, so it can proc weapon effects (giving "a second chance per swing to proc weapon on-hits") and also chain with Hand of Justice (HoJ) and Windfury Totem (WFT/shaman totem): a hit can trigger SoR → SoR can retrigger HoJ (if not on ICD) → each triggered HoJ can trigger SoR again → all of this can trigger one Windfury Totem proc at max, which retriggers SoR again — [[d:paladin#1436080975141408818]], [[d:paladin#1436745720567697438]], [[d:paladin#1436746348438356008]], [[d:paladin#1436747173676187698]]. SoC does not chain the same way and "produces 60% less attacks" in this interaction — [[d:paladin#1436747441134108694]].
- **[On-hit weapon procs and SoR/SoC]** On-hit weapon effects (e.g., Drain Life, Dark Strike) generally only proc from melee hits; SoR and SoC both count as a "melee hit" for these purposes, so weapon procs (and trinkets like Sigil of Ancient Accord) can trigger off Seal damage too — [[d:paladin#1437022000194392156]], [[d:paladin#1437023127719645194]].
- **[Holy Might]** Holy Might triggers even if Holy Strike misses (as does the heal it provides); same is true for Zeal triggering off Crusader Strike regardless of hit/miss — [[d:paladin#1436318027212918915]], [[d:paladin#1436318143751389246]].
- **[Vengeance mechanic]** Vengeance is a slow-building stack; on Ret it builds "almost instantly" due to high crit rate, but on Vengeance-Prot the slow ramp makes the mechanic largely non-functional/not worth it as a Prot threat tool — [[d:paladin#1436879089012048014]].
- **[Consecration cast-tick]** Consecration's cast tick still procs on-cast effects like "WoC" (unclear exact ability referenced) and reportedly can also proc Sigil-type effects — [[d:paladin#1436793419426037971]], [[d:paladin#1436793752953028841]] (unclear/context-light, single source).
- **[FRD (Force Reactive Disk) mechanics]** Each Holy Shield block with FRD equipped deals a guaranteed 30 damage to every nearby target; with 4 mobs, that's 120 damage per block; Redoubt procs constantly giving +15% block chance on top, averaging roughly 1 extra block/second, so with 4 mobs FRD can contribute ~30 DPS per target (20-30% of total AoE DPS); main downside is it breaks/needs repair often, requiring a repair bot or carrying 4-5 spare FRDs if not using engineering's own repair item — [[d:paladin#1436285382579978384]] through [[d:paladin#1436286445923995658]]. FRD does NOT proc against Nature-type mobs like Lashers (no proc) — [[d:paladin#1437466144721076364]] (stated, not fully confirmed/tested). Skullwing/Skullflame War Axe (SWR) shield does scale with spellpower as an FRD alternative but has lower armor/block value at level 60 — [[d:paladin#1437435467451596942]].
- **[Macros]** Judgement is not on the global cooldown (GCD), so spamming the Judgement button doesn't waste GCDs, but pre-emptively spamming a judge+seal macro before Judgement is off cooldown will NOT cast Judgement — it will only recast the Seal (SoR) — [[d:paladin#1436352871812829234]], [[d:paladin#1436353505396002967]]. A judge+reseal macro generally cannot fire both in a single keypress in this client; requires double-tapping the button, or use of SuperMacro addon behavior; example judge/seal macro combining `/startattack`, seal cast, judge cast, and a tooltip-show line was shared — [[d:paladin#1436351916022960260]], [[d:paladin#1436352469130018857]], [[d:paladin#1436355223525261385]], [[d:paladin#1436356021256847480]]. A conditional HP-based judge macro (Rank swap under 35% HP) was shared using `/run` + `UseAction` — [[d:paladin#1437426736739647548]].
- **[Libram swapping]** Addons "PaladinLibramSwap"/"LibramSwap" let you equip a libram and cast a spell in the same GCD via macro, e.g. swapping in Libram of Fervor to cast Seal of the Crusader (SotC) then swapping back to Eternal Tower libram before combat — sample macro: `/run PaladinSealoftheCrusader()` + `/run CastEquipByName("Seal of the Crusader","Libram of Fervor")` — [[d:paladin#1437134371114192897]], [[d:paladin#1437134689419657367]], [[d:paladin#1437136177324101742]]. In MC, players debate whether swapping librams mid-fight for JoTC (Judgement of the Crusader?) execute-phase damage is worth it vs. just using Eternal Tower libram throughout — general take: only worth doing in K40 (three Judgements per fight), not worth the hassle in other raids or on trash — [[d:paladin#1437136016224944291]], [[d:paladin#1437136376398348449]], [[d:paladin#1437427781754425424]], [[d:paladin#1437428252561113228]]. Manually swapping librams (or via addon) always triggers the GCD; the addon only avoids wasting it by executing the swap + spell-cast within the same network "batching window" — high-latency players will fail the window and eat the GCD anyway; simply not carrying an unused libram in inventory avoids triggering any swap logic — [[d:paladin#1439384645358846065]], [[d:paladin#1439385056899764275]], [[d:paladin#1439385291918938265]], [[d:paladin#1439385525487141079]], [[d:paladin#1439390330813284403]], [[d:paladin#1439390396185841882]]. Devs are asked repeatedly to lock librams during combat and give librams "actual effects" instead of relying on swap gimmicks — [[d:paladin#1439193782636515338]].
- **[Reckoning + weapon speed]** Reckoning (an extra swing, "parry-haste") pairs well with slow main-hand weapons + Argent Defender trinket for damage, while fast weapons instead favor mana regen via JoW/SoW — [[d:paladin#1438758658061111328]], [[d:paladin#1438759160689987615]], [[d:paladin#1438759493201690738]], [[d:paladin#1438759812266590298]]. With Reckoning procs adding extra swings, a fixed 20-PPM Seal of Wisdom can end up effectively proc'ing well beyond the naive "swings per minute" estimate (one test showed ~30ppm-equivalent SoW procs while using Reckoning) — [[d:paladin#1438760037345660938]], [[d:paladin#1438760196401791099]], [[d:paladin#1438761199729901588]].
- **[Extra-attack effects]** Extra-attack-on-hit effects (Hand of Justice, timelord-cloak-style procs) can be roughly treated as ~1% haste equivalent for damage purposes — [[d:paladin#1437544428557570228]], [[d:paladin#1437822818967490601]].
- **[Spellstrike for mana/threat]** Spellstrike gear is valuable for Vengeance uptime, Judgement of Wisdom mana, and Wrath of Cenarius uptime; for Prot specifically it's "definitely only 'needed' once u have WoC" — [[d:paladin#1437016958120759446]], [[d:paladin#1439179021199081603]].
- **[Repentance]** 60-second cooldown, deals free chip damage and generates no threat so it's safe to use while approaching a target — "you press it once every 60s, it's a lame spell but free damage is free damage" — [[d:paladin#1438268340673052944]], [[d:paladin#1438268353134465074]], [[d:paladin#1438269272844402851]].
- **[Hand of Sacrifice]** Considered "okay" for raiding but don't GCD-lock yourself out of a more useful spell just to cast it — it isn't a lifesaving cooldown; can also cause buff-cap issues in raids with tight buff slots — [[d:paladin#1439035714670166106]], [[d:paladin#1439036349012377650]], [[d:paladin#1439036552914276433]].
- **[Threat % to pull aggro]** Need roughly 110% of the current top-threat value to pull aggro (melee); more distance from the target requires proportionally more threat overage to pull — [[d:paladin#1439212832703909941]], [[d:paladin#1439212930863468696]], [[d:paladin#1439213329414619307]].
- **[ICD design note]** Internal cooldowns exist to cap proc rates, but the server does not check every proc item on every character every tick (too costly); overly-proccy effects instead get their underlying PPM adjusted server-side — [[d:paladin#1437059771831287931]], [[d:paladin#1437059978182791278]].
- **[JoW resist interaction]** Judgement of Wisdom does not proc if the triggering hit was fully resisted, which can break the SoR→HoJ→WFT proc chain described above — [[d:paladin#1436339695142567979]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[Avoidance target]** Need 102.4% avoidance under Holy Shield to avoid Crushing Blows from raid bosses; AQ40's Twin Emperors (melee one) reduces the tank's Defense by 100, making this harder to sustain — [[d:paladin#1438147622589173923]], [[d:paladin#1438147752893874237]], [[d:paladin#1438148055642800249]].
- **[Defense stat]** Defense isn't a "milestone stat" except specifically hitting 400 for the Twins encounter; the uncrit-immune point is ~440 Defense, but a realistically MC-geared Prot sits around 350 without sacrificing SP; full T1 (8-piece) adds 36 Defense — [[d:paladin#1437546087656460498]], [[d:paladin#1437544654777352252]], [[d:paladin#1437544851557187754]].
- **[Threat > mitigation]** A "full beef" mitigation-focused Prot build cannot hold threat at endgame — threat is "the name of the game," not avoidance/HP, once raid DPS is geared — [[d:paladin#1438150625543065611]], [[d:paladin#1438150295635886242]], [[d:paladin#1438150377663889461]].
- **[Non-MT hit requirements]** A Prot tank who isn't the boss/skull-tanking Main Tank can get away with a small miss chance and prioritize threat gear first during progression, unlike Ret or an MT who needs to be fully hit-capped — [[d:paladin#1439178724057813002]].
- **[Ambershire fresh-60 mana sustain]** Mana Pot + Mana Tea recommended for dungeon tanking; it's expected/fine for the tank to drink more often than healers in 5-mans — [[d:paladin#1438578951613321256]], [[d:paladin#1438579524211052545]], [[d:paladin#1438579578338541568]].
- **[Bear vs Prot Paladin vs Deep Prot Warrior]** Bear (Feral Druid) is the current server-meta Main Tank pick; Deep Prot Warriors underperform without Thunderfury; Prot Paladin remains more competitive with Bear than Warrior tanks are, due to Holy Strike/threat scaling — [[d:paladin#1438151202973028432]], [[d:paladin#1438151366051762277]], [[d:paladin#1438151510621159484]].
- **[Ashbringer while tanking]** Ashbringer is usable even on single-target fights like Kruul (accepting its -25 Stamina penalty) with careful positioning (e.g., staying out of the main melee stack so its -stam doesn't compound with raid-wide AoE); the exact stat-budget tradeoff of -25 Stam vs. Might of Menethil's Str/Stam is debated at length, unresolved — [[d:paladin#1437889132146987210]], [[d:paladin#1437889298027253883]], [[d:paladin#1437889946399543508]], [[d:paladin#1437890535586267147]], [[d:paladin#1437890776003645450]] (contested).
- **[Un'Goro Sacrifice solo-farm LoS]** For a solo-farmable Un'Goro elite ("Zev"/Zevrim) whose "Sacrifice" cast must be line-of-sighted: one method uses the broken-pillar base at the edge of the upper platform (hug the edge where "square goes to circle" while it casts); an alternate method uses a narrow gap next to a small pillar near the pool that the boss must path around since it can't fit through — diagrammed via noclip.website — [[d:paladin#1437802635913728162]], [[d:paladin#1437805375687495856]], [[d:paladin#1437807616892862586]], [[d:paladin#1437807719909429349]], [[d:paladin#1437808628391481366]].

## Healing specifics
- **[Melee vs ranged Holy]** Melee ("Battle-Holy"): Crusader Strike resets Holy Shock's cooldown, Holy Strike heals up to 5 targets within 10yd with Blessed Strikes talented; ranged Holy is FoL spam with occasional 1.5s Holy Light/Holy Shock. Encounter-dependent choice — e.g., another melee player is "meh" for some fights like KT — [[d:paladin#1439170292789280778]], [[d:paladin#1439170416886415391]], [[d:paladin#1439170727419842686]].
- **[Why melee-heal]** Most Holy Paladins melee-weave because skipping Holy Strike loses "huge" healing — not wielding a shield alone costs ~100 healing-power-equivalent from Ironclad-style plate scaling — [[d:paladin#1437985302445555772]], [[d:paladin#1437992420074590319]].
- **[Judging while healing]** Holy Shock cooldown reduces to ~1.5s cast on Holy Light via the Judgement talent interaction; a full Holy Shock/CS loop ("shock > cs > shock > cs") is a fun theorized rotation if reset chance were reliable enough, but isn't in the live game without Blessed Strikes — [[d:paladin#1436907214307590215]], [[d:paladin#1437711217643749377]], [[d:paladin#1437713011489112144]] (theorycraft/contested).
- **[Bear-mount analogy]** Holy Paladin is called the current strongest healer on the server, but subjectively "the most do-nothing" playstyle to some, since much of ranged Holy is FoL-spam that leaves attention free — [[d:paladin#1437935236431483101]], [[d:paladin#1437533447521435821]].

## PvP
- **[Ret PvP specs]** Example spreads: 11/8/32 (PvE-leaning), 17/0/34 (more PvP-focused); "PvP specs are very personal" — [[d:paladin#1438896099019460738]], [[d:paladin#1438896184516149339]], [[d:paladin#1438896478016770138]].
- **[Improved SoTC skip in PvP]** Improved Seal of the Crusader isn't worth talenting in PvP since Judgement of the Crusader is rarely applied there; base JotC can still matter vs. a tanky target with multiple Paladins present — [[d:paladin#1438896892590424166]], [[d:paladin#1438897099428335687]], [[d:paladin#1438897217174765640]], [[d:paladin#1438897498482671728]].
- **[Ret "frontline bruiser" hybrid PvP build]** Detailed (Jesse Potter): T2.5 5-set + T3 2-set (swap to 4-set disarm gloves vs. Warriors); 3% hit or Sanctity Aura for +10% holy damage (aura swap cost); talents add Holy healing points (+12% healing, 70% pushback resist) atop normal Ret Repentance, paired with Concentration Aura + Ironclad. Result: ~1700-2000 unbuffed Holy Lights at 6.5k HP while still landing ~1k melee crits; playstyle peels/heals for frontline melee (Warriors/Rogues) over squishier ranged allies. Avoid Judgement of Light in raids as Ret — it costs a buff slot — [[d:paladin#1438847838577754215]], [[d:paladin#1438858149003264110]], [[d:paladin#1438858260546588769]], [[d:paladin#1438858337415463044]], [[d:paladin#1438858396085387418]], [[d:paladin#1438858463479595150]].
- **[SoC PvP reliability complaint]** Proposed community buff: make Seal of Command's proc undodgeable/unparryable, since SoR (guaranteed once triggered) and SoC (RNG, avoidable) behave inconsistently against player defenses — [[d:paladin#1438913855789994146]], [[d:paladin#1438925146227347466]], [[d:paladin#1438925239538159666]].
- **[Verigan's Fist]** Fun/relevant in the 20-29 BG bracket even though it's soon replaced by a dungeon-drop weapon — [[d:paladin#1437918695891402904]], [[d:paladin#1437918865337094225]].
- **[Community wishlist]** Extended, organized list of desired Paladin PvE+PvP changes (Southshore Crusader Vidnar, echoed by Jesse Potter/Destroses): all Blessings baseline; melee hits (not just autos) should trigger Seals; all Seals should deal damage; Auras shouldn't cost a GCD; Divine Shield shouldn't be the default excuse for nerfing everything else; rework Seals/Judgements; more meaningful talent choices; reduce melee Paladin's reliance on Windfury Totem; give Paladins class racials; fix "horrible" itemization vs. required stats; reduce overreliance on the spellstrike-item "gimmick" — [[d:paladin#1439148552247967744]], [[d:paladin#1439150933765263521]], [[d:paladin#1439151447210983474]], [[d:paladin#1439151900023849031]].
- **[WFT dependency]** Long-running complaint that melee Paladin damage is heavily bottlenecked without Windfury Totem support (historically acute on Tel'Abim, where Alliance lacked Shaman); cross-faction BGs have partly mitigated this — [[d:paladin#1436108810677780561]], [[d:paladin#1439409751099179131]], [[d:paladin#1439409578432532622]].
- **[Class racials imbalance]** Paladins have received no class racial abilities while some other classes (Shaman, Priest) have — repeated complaint, no dev commitment — [[d:paladin#1439151900023849031]], [[d:paladin#1439176131503849533]].

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Engineering]** Strongly recommended as close to mandatory for competitive play (goblin sappers, Force Reactive Disk, repair items, Electromagnetic Gigaflux Reactivator) since "no other profession offers unique enchants/abilities"; costs ~150g to power-level 1-300 — [[d:paladin#1437832214326022334]], [[d:paladin#1437831886503280842]]. Electromagnetic Gigaflux Reactivator flagged as underrated: usable, swappable, and you keep the benefit of its 10-minute shield afterward — [[d:paladin#1437750763064524851]], [[d:paladin#1437750988986384444]].
- **[Armorsmithing]** Judged largely useless for endgame — "can't think of anything useful it'd give for endgame" — though its first quest-chain trinket is decent and doesn't require being an Armorsmith to wear — [[d:paladin#1437834253391101972]], [[d:paladin#1438590357724987403]], [[d:paladin#1438590495453483099]].
- **[Swordsmithing/Axesmithing]** Swordsmithing unlocks crafting Tempered Runeblade; Axesmithing unlocks Nightfall — [[d:paladin#1437834344117833910]].
- **[Recommended profession path]** Engineering + one gathering profession is enough; buy the rest — [[d:paladin#1437834551639539773]]. Herbalism specifically boosts Lasher-farm gold output (herb nodes around the farm's courtyard) more than Mining, whose value is mainly tied to Arcane Crystal farming — [[d:paladin#1437836869726638171]], [[d:paladin#1437837358195150970]].
- **[Gold-per-hour reference]** Herbalism gathering estimated ~80g/h on Nordaanar without a herbalism-specific bonus; herb prices much lower on Ambershire (dreamfoil ~10s vs. 60s on Nord; lotus ~8g vs. 30g on Nord) — [[d:paladin#1437840447136465021]], [[d:paladin#1437840543936675850]], [[d:paladin#1437841107730825328]].
- **[Human racial value]** Human's +5 sword/mace weapon-skill racial is specifically valuable for Paladin hit-capping; a non-Human tank effectively gives up one hit-cap gear slot's worth of itemization by lacking it — [[d:paladin#1437843153590943785]].
- **[Free repair-bot]** Turtle WoW has its own repeatable no-cost repair-bot summon item (distinct from a crafted Engineering one), useful for maintaining Force Reactive Disk uptime during farm sessions — [[d:paladin#1437432116139790340]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Sigil of Ancient Accord proc scope]** See Stats section — procs only from T2 3pc spellstrike, Consecration's first tick, and possibly Exorcism/Holy Wrath; not from SoR, JoR, or ordinary gear spellstrikes — [[d:paladin#1437915944847540435]], [[d:paladin#1438407829680685096]].
- **[T2 3pc Ret proc frequency]** Anecdotally procs "a lot more" once triggered after certain gear combos (e.g., pairing with Carapace/CA gear) — behavior not fully understood but the bonus is still well-regarded — [[d:paladin#1438561060872519810]], [[d:paladin#1438561663531089950]].
- **[Dream's Herald]** Previously scaled inversely down to zero with spellpower; hotfixed — [[d:paladin#1437171120158543934]], [[d:paladin#1437171218015846651]].
- **[FRD durability]** Previously sometimes wouldn't lose durability if the wielder died with it equipped; fixed, so it now breaks/needs repair more often — [[d:paladin#1436962955270029313]], [[d:paladin#1437433247666536479]].
- **[Scarlet Crusade passive]** A Scarlet Crusade-set-related passive/aura effect reportedly does not take up a buff slot, unlike most other "hidden" passive effects — [[d:paladin#1437883520553844867]].
- **[Hidden-aura buff cap]** Low-value passive procs/racials/language auras can silently push visible buffs off the buff bar (or be pushed off themselves) once the buff table caps out — a recurring annoyance for melee/hybrid builds carrying many small on-hit procs — [[d:paladin#1436720845484589287]], [[d:paladin#1436721661121400842]], [[d:paladin#1437881765451665481]], [[d:paladin#1437882876887695381]].
- **[T3.5 5pc Ret party-stack bug]** Reportedly doesn't grant party members their own stacks beyond 1 even as the Paladin's own stacks reach 2-3 — see 1.18.1 changes section — [[d:paladin#1439186627716055041]], [[d:paladin#1439186844712566815]].
- **[Scarlet set bonus + Consecration]** One unconfirmed single report that a Scarlet-set-style proc does not trigger from Consecration — [[d:paladin#1439481467473363016]] (unconfirmed, single source).
- **[Enchanted Armor Kit]** Reportedly doesn't apply to Blue Dragonscale boots/gloves/chest, only to the legs, despite the community spellret spreadsheet listing +9 for those pieces — [[d:paladin#1439555624181563412]] (unconfirmed).
