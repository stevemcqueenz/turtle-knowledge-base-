## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret]** Ret DPS assessed as "B tier through all phases," mainly because rogues and warriors are overtuned, not because ret itself is weak — [[d:paladin#1459463667782520842]] (Mithreon, 2026-01-10; +0, general agreement in thread).
- **[Ret]** SoC (Seal of Command) build is "currently underperforming" relative to SoR; SoR's advantage partly structural: only one seal proc per swing is possible even with extra attacks (Windfury etc.), so SoC can't double-dip, while SoR's continuous scaling plus better Exorcism/Consecration scaling keeps it ahead — [[d:paladin#1459981286365073541]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1460190954693853215]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1460188773387210934]] (Mithreon, proc mechanics explainer).
- **[Ret]** "Necrodin" build (melee-holy-strike-proc build) described: uses Lost set items (Ring, Trinket, OH, Neck — 3pc) plus 2x T2 3-set to proc extra Holy Strikes; pairs with Flurry Axe or Thunderfury (TF); commonly paired with Ancient Accord trinket (MC) and Wing of the Time Lord Cloak — [[d:paladin#1460465924460118036]] (HolyAngel, 2026-01-13, detailed description). Best current gear combo cited: "2x T3 3p, the lost set, sunken temple chest and a TF (or a THC)" — [[d:paladin#1460716449470484672]] (Excinerus, 2026-01-13; endorsed "best build ever" by Adamant [[d:paladin#1460718162717704294]]). Scales with attack speed, not spellpower ("all the spell power is placebo, it's all about number of procs per second") — [[d:paladin#1459996172151296274]] (Excinerus). Weapon comparison for this build: TF most consistent in testing; Flurry Axe/Ironfoe comparable via extra procs — [[d:paladin#1459995654305743002]] (dusty), [[d:paladin#1460720453629907166]] (Adamant). Caveat: doesn't scale with future gear/damage buffs, so may fall behind as other classes get buffed further; mainly worth it if not raiding K40 regularly or on a realm without Windfury access (TA) — [[d:paladin#1460730002877452440]] (Pastor), [[d:paladin#1460730772448215071]] (gastro), [[d:paladin#1460730283681648742]] (gastro).

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[Prot]** Cookie-cutter Prot tank build: **11/33/7** (Holy/Prot/Ret) split. Reasoning: 11 Holy points self-supplies Sanctity Aura for its 1.1x damage multiplier; the Prot points maximize Judgement damage via Imp SoR and Imp Judgement — [[d:paladin#1460797887653875714]] (Grey, 2026-01-14). Kings/Blessing of Kings is NOT part of this cookie-cutter build and isn't needed — all Blessings except Wisdom give identical threat for near-identical mana cost, so Blessing choice doesn't need to be Kings — [[d:paladin#1460793879799533618]] (Grey).
  - Reckoning: run at least 1 point, most run 2-3; 4-5 points considered unnecessary for single target but strong when multiple mobs attack — [[d:paladin#1460800337760751882]] (Grey).
  - "Wiggle room" talents to shift based on preference/need: Improved Taunt, Anticipation, Improved Blessings, Toughness — [[d:paladin#1460800337760751882]] (Grey).
  - Case for Improved Blessings over Benediction: Benediction only saves ~5 mana/sec on SoR+Judgement (15% mana reduction), while Imp Blessings gives ~20% more mp5/AP from Wisdom/Might raid-wide — [[d:paladin#1460803899345600523]] (Grey).
  - Anticipation (Prot tree) gives more avoidance per point than Deflection (Ret tree, pure parry) — [[d:paladin#1460802209854787799]] (Silverhand).
  - Grey personally runs raw-armor-focused build now (previously 2/5 Toughness + 3/3 Anticipation) — [[d:paladin#1460800798526279863]] (Grey).
- **[Ret, general leveling PvP]** Suggested AP-focused Ret PvP build (not spellpower-focused): use warrior-style AP gear (Abomination's Cage, Endless Rage, Vengeance Bracers, Chromatic Boots etc.), enough points for Repentance, remaining points into Holy for Spiritual Focus + Improved Holy Light (1.5s cast), and Prot tree for Guardian's Favor; example split **10/7/34**-ish — [[d:paladin#1460037957884055696]] (Leo/Sunache asks), [[d:paladin#1460039423646240779]] (dindunuffinz, single source, unconfirmed by others).
- **[Prot]** Grey's posted "cookie cutter for maximum personal performance" tank build screenshot exists, with note "trade Benediction for Imp Blessings if needed; trade points from Blessing of Sanctuary and Toughness for Anticipation, Imp Hand of Reckoning, Guardian's Favor, etc if needed" — [[d:paladin#1459442077220733080]] (Grey, 2026-01-10; image referenced, talent point numbers not independently confirmed from text).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Righteous Fury unlocks at level 16 (learned via class trainer/quest); the Paladin "revive" (self-res) is obtained via a Tome of Divinity class quest starting near Stormwind Cathedral (Duthorian Rall / Brother Wilhelm); losing/abandoning the book can softlock the chain but a GM or an Ironforge NPC counterpart can restore it — [[d:paladin#1459353566849532108]] (Aelgagar, 2026-01-10), [[d:paladin#1459383332994547928]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1459400249305993319]] (Beldrin).
- **[General]** Seal of Command is available starting level 30 on Turtle (vs. level 20 in classic), making early leveling a bit slower here — [[d:paladin#1459751386014027960]] (Beldrin, 2026-01-10).
- **[Hit/weapon skill]** For a human Spelladin, target 8% hit total: with weapon-skill talents + 2h weapon specialization + human racial (+2% skill→hit) + 3% from Precision talent, only ~3% hit needs to come from gear — [[d:paladin#1459513015924953098]] (Iseut), [[d:paladin#1459514482744102943]] (Jesse Potter: "3% Talent, 11 Skill = 2% hit, so you need 3% on gear"), 2026-01-10.
- **[Ret leveling]** Suggested leveling rotation: Seal of Wisdom → Judgement → Seal of Command (or Righteousness) → auto attack, use Holy Strike on cooldown (free, comparable dmg to Crusader Strike while leveling); Crusader Strike can be better with the right weapon but rarely worth the mana over CS being free — [[d:paladin#1459575136138166282]] (Pastor), [[d:paladin#1459575554377519261]] (Silverhand), 2026-01-10.
- **[Ret leveling]** Getting Seal of Wisdom (and Blessing of Wisdom) largely removes downtime/drinking while leveling as Ret; grabbing a spellhit item or two on top of that keeps you cruising — [[d:paladin#1459575815963672696]] (Isvash), 2026-01-10.
- **[Ret leveling]** Weapon quality is the single most important gear factor for Ret while leveling; twinking a weapon matters far more than twinking other slots. Warrior benefits from twinking even more than Ret does, but twinking a Prot Paladin lets it level "way faster than any warrior spec" — [[d:paladin#1459938463506759832]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1459950897416376425]] (Julio), 2026-01-10.
- **[Leveling tanking]** Ret talents barely hurt low-level tanking (15-40): Redoubt/Holy Shield block little at low block value; concrete example in Razorfen Kraul: Razorfen Earthbreaker hits 240-310 before armor, ~178 avg with 2h (35% DR) vs ~137 with shield (50% DR); with the RFK shield (17 block value), a block saves 15% dmg; combined Redoubt+Holy Shield+Toughness+Blessing of Sanctuary+Righteous Fury only cut overall damage taken by roughly 2-4% each pre-40; toughness/reckoning/righteous defense/righteous strikes become significant around level 40+ — [[d:paladin#1459864211671744698]] (Grey, 2026-01-10, detailed math).
- **[Leveling]** For pulling multiple mobs while leveling: subtract roughly one mob you can safely pull per level below your own vs. mob level (e.g., a level-22 character can handle 3 level 18-19 mobs) — [[d:paladin#1459768631129804851]] (Julio, 2026-01-10).
- **[Leveling dungeons]** Lasher farming (a solo-farm target) realistically starts around level 52-54, especially after dungeon mob damage/health was buffed — [[d:paladin#1459712646252204133]] (Blossom), [[d:paladin#1459712952667078809]] (Destroses), 2026-01-11.
- **[HC leveling]** For Hardcore leveling, Prot or Holy/Prot builds are considered much safer than Retribution, at least until Repentance is unlocked; tanking as Ret is possible but with more damage taken/less threat — [[d:paladin#1459860842945445932]] (orodoet), [[d:paladin#1459861500372979854]] (orodoet), 2026-01-10.
- **[HC leveling]** Recommended cross-faction guild tabard trick for Booty Bay rested XP/trading; check faction rewards (Darnassus, High Elves) — [[d:paladin#1459873239160324219]] (orodoet, 2026-01-10).
- **[HC leveling]** Bubble (Divine Shield) + Hearthstone works as an escape at level 34+ with r1 Divine Shield (12s duration unlocks at 50, but early rank + haste from Zeal can allow ~30-34); regular Hearthstone is affected by haste and is not on the global cooldown — [[d:paladin#1459866751369089129]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1459867646538289300]] (orodoet), [[d:paladin#1459868413412249701]] (Grey), 2026-01-10.
- **[HC leveling]** "Sweet 'N Crunchy Fruit Bread" item breaks all "soft" CC (fear/sap/sheep/etc.) by dealing continuous small self-damage (~10/sec for 30s) — [[d:paladin#1459874451385745602]] (Pastor), 2026-01-10. Note: this bread does NOT trigger "when struck in combat" procs (e.g. Dream's Herald) since it's not a melee strike — [[d:paladin#1459864468627525682]] (Grey, 2026-01-10).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Prot]** Highest-threat Prot rotation: use Holy Strike immediately when it comes off cooldown, never clip it; use Judgement of Righteousness immediately; refresh Seal of Righteousness after Holy Strike but before Consecration/Holy Shield; refresh Consecration; refresh Holy Shield — [[d:paladin#1460619678945902684]] (Grey, 2026-01-13).
- **[Prot]** Never open a pull with Crusader Strike, and generally never use Crusader Strike as Prot: it does much less threat than Holy Strike (physical, reduced by armor, scales poorly with SP, doesn't get the 2.05x Improved Righteous Fury threat multiplier that Holy Strike gets), and its only benefit (Shield Block Value gain via Zeal stacks) doesn't require using CS itself — the Zeal talent's 25% Holy Strike damage/30% threat bonus applies regardless of whether CS was used — [[d:paladin#1459591918815019149]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1459592851791806540]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1459593914418987090]] (Mithreon: "CS is physical damage... scales poorly with SP... doesn't get 2.05x multi... unlike HS"), 2026-01-10.
- **[Ret]** Seal choice/scaling debate: SoR (Seal of Righteousness) ahead of SoC overall due to steady procs on every strike type (Holy Strike, Judgement, SoR itself all count as "melee strikes" for on-strike effects like Dream's Herald), while SoC is capped at one proc per swing even with extra attacks — [[d:paladin#1459361456918233259]] (Adamant: "holy strike, judgement and seal of righteousness count as melee strikes"), [[d:paladin#1460190870723625112]] (Jesse Potter), 2026-01-10/12.
- **[Ret]** Spellstrike items (ring/neck/glove/bracer effects, or weapon "+X fire/frost/etc damage" procs) exist mainly to sustain Judgement of Wisdom (JoW) mana regen and Vengeance uptime, not for their own damage — [[d:paladin#1460666085207576768]] (Adamant, 2026-01-13, detailed). Only the T2 3-set spellstrike effect can proc Wrath of Cenarius (WoC), Judgement of Wisdom-style, and Sigil of Ancient Accord-style trinkets; plain weapon-imbue-style spellstrikes (fire/frost weapon procs) cannot proc WoC. Resolution of an earlier community disagreement (Destroses said "WoC can be proc'd by all types of spellstrikes" [[d:paladin#1459744234101014569]] vs. Iseut/theoloras saying only T2 3p qualifies [[d:paladin#1459742726651383943]]): confirmed later as a bug — T2 3-set's spellstrike is coded to count as BOTH "casting a spell" and "damaging with a spell," while ordinary weapon spellstrikes only count as the latter (damage), which is why only T2 3p can trigger spell-cast-gated effects — [[d:paladin#1460924106487759004]] (Grey, 2026-01-13).
- **[Ret]** First realistic leveling/pre-raid spellstrike item: Gilneas City ring, ~level 43 from a level-46 boss — [[d:paladin#1459767465843097794]] (Destroses), [[d:paladin#1459767930316263434]] (Destroses), 2026-01-10.

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot]** Standard multi-target/AoE dungeon pull: pre-cast Consecrate so it lands where mobs converge on you (see Tanking specifics for full precast timing detail), then work single-target priority (Holy Strike/Judgement/SoR) on top of it; Consecration plus autos/Holy Strike is usually enough to hold a pull after one cast — [[d:paladin#1461408170533589197]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1461409404720123935]] (Grey), 2026-01-15/16. Some tanks run Seal of Wisdom throughout AoE pulls purely to spam Consecration indefinitely without running oom, accepting only a trivial amount of extra AoE threat from SoW itself — [[d:paladin#1461409508860366889]] (Silverhand).
- **[Ret/SoC raid AoE]** Use Consecration when you have a mana surplus and 2+ mobs present; otherwise prioritize the single-target seal/judgement/strike loop — [[d:paladin#1462893175369764917]] (Mithreon, 2026-01-19).
- **[Holy AoE heal]** Holy Strike's Mending Light effect is the main "AoE heal" tool for a melee-weaving Holy Paladin — see Healing specifics for playstyle detail (running alongside the tank, Blessed Strikes scaling, Daybreak procs).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Defense cap: 440 defense for anti-crit (crit immunity); each defense point gives 0.16x contribution toward the anti-crush cap of 102.4% avoidance; Block/Dodge/Parry contribute 1:1 to that avoidance total — [[d:paladin#1459549528884641936]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1459549897798979739]] (Mithreon), 2026-01-10.
- **[Prot]** More precisely: each point of Defense gives 0.04% to each of dodge/parry/block/miss/anti-crit; so 8 Defense = 0.96% "hard avoidance" (dodge/parry/miss) + 0.32% block/anti-crit, while 6 Defense = 0.96% "combined avoidance" (dodge/parry/miss/block), which is the stat relevant to crush immunity (needs 102.4% combined avoidance; defense alone cannot reach crush immunity, only crit immunity) — [[d:paladin#1460803234695479420]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1460803430699237509]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1460803055623737406]] (Grey), 2026-01-14.
- **[Prot]** Mitigation/avoidance is not prioritized until roughly AQ40 (except specific fire-resist checks earlier); target ~102.4% avoidance while Holy Shield is up, timing depends on raid composition — [[d:paladin#1459550725007740969]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1459550748353368284]] (Destroses), [[d:paladin#1459550935629168778]] (Destroses), 2026-01-10.
- **[Prot]** Shield Block Value (SBV) math example: with ~2500 unbuffed HP-equivalent hits, 100 SBV ≈ 4% damage reduction per block, which is not worth sacrificing Holy Strike for Crusader Strike's ~25% more threat — [[d:paladin#1459492108757897219]] (Grey, 2026-01-10, detailed math example, disputes over-valuing SBV from angels1m/Mithreon in same thread).
- **[Prot]** Real raid parse data on SBV/blocking value: one MT logged 127k blocked vs 574k taken over a full Naxx clear; another example: 9.1k blocked of 68k total on Sapphiron (~11%, "very good" on a hard-hitting boss); prot pally in a different Naxx logged blocking 8.2% of all boss damage taken (7% overall), rising to 8-8.3% when MT — [[d:paladin#1459477966206800137]] (Aelgagar), [[d:paladin#1459494119310229565]] (Aelgagar), [[d:paladin#1459479752955003035]] (Mithreon), 2026-01-10.
- **[Cloak enchant]** For a healer, +5 resist-all is argued slightly better than +70 armor on the back enchant: 70 armor is a small % reduction given healer's already-higher armor, while 5 resist gives a small chance to fully nullify a magic debuff (saving a dispel); physical damage is rarer for healers but has fewer other mitigation options, magical damage is more common but easier to counter (potions/dispels) — [[d:paladin#1460076460911100059]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1460051499370418328]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1460052057586143315]] (Grey), 2026-01-12. For Ret/melee, +70 armor back enchant is the only relevant back enchant option since agility/dodge/resist enchants don't apply while meleeing in plate — [[d:paladin#1460047673380966511]] (Grey).
- **[Ret]** Ring enchant: +3 all stats generally preferred/considered comparable to +5 spell power for Ret — [[d:paladin#1460637356473454633]] (Beldrin: "either +3 stats or +5 sp" for spellret jewelry, 2026-01-13).
- **[Prot]** Toughness talent does NOT affect healing power; the "2% of armor as healing power" effect (from a separate item/talent) is calculated only from the base white armor value printed on gear pieces themselves — not from enchants, base character armor, buffs, or consumables — [[d:paladin#1459771471294627891]] (Destroses), [[d:paladin#1460047151777185802]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1460047484880556226]] (Grey), 2026-01-10/12.

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Prot]** Threat rotation priority (see also Rotation: single target): never clip Holy Strike, use Judgement of Righteousness on cooldown, refresh Seal of Righteousness after Holy Strike (before Consecration/Holy Shield), then refresh Consecration and Holy Shield — [[d:paladin#1460619678945902684]] (Grey, 2026-01-13).
- **[Prot]** Shield Specialization's mana-on-block refund is a flat 2% of max mana; Grey's suggested fix (2% of a fixed baseline like 50 mana +1% of max mana) would be MORE consistent across gear levels, not a nerf, since most play time is spent below 5-6k mana where the flat 2% underperforms — math: 2% of 1000/4000/7000 mana = 20/80/140 mana (1 Rank-1 Holy Shield cast per ~40s/10s/5.5s); a "50+1%" model = 60/90/120 mana (1 cast per ~15s/10s/6s); devs have not made this change despite Grey's advocacy, partly due to "regulars" pushback framing any QoL change as ez-mode — [[d:paladin#1460969676082516033]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1460972373527363782]] (Grey, detailed math), 2026-01-14.
- **[Prot dispel]** Paladin is the only class that can Cleanse Poison, Disease, and Curse-type debuffs in a single cast; other classes' dispels instead hit multiple targets or multiple debuffs of the same type — [[d:paladin#1460987740437876787]] (Kaelgard, color coding: Purple=Curse, Green=Poison, Blue=Magic/Dispel, Dark green=Disease, Red=usually undispellable), [[d:paladin#1460988858593448083]] (Grey), 2026-01-14. Precasting dispels (before a mechanic lands) is valuable, e.g. Naxx Spider Wing poison — [[d:paladin#1461076668021211320]] (Thaodan).
- **[Prot]** Blessing of Sanctuary is a strong leveling/dungeon talent (many small-hit mobs) but weak in raids (flat, non-scaling damage reduction, currently -24 per proc, irrelevant against thousand+ damage boss hits) and costs a valuable buff slot — see Spec Viability section for full breakdown and the rumored future scaling change.
- **[Reckoning]** Reckoning (the extra-swing-on-crush/parry talent) DOES proc seal effects (e.g. Judgement of Wisdom mana return), contrary to a long-standing community belief that it only deals "white" (unenchanted) damage — confirmed by Grey's own testing and corroborated by Silverhand's experience of "fourfold JoW procs without spellstrikes" — [[d:paladin#1462215650369077521]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1462215974848692439]] (Silverhand), 2026-01-18.
- **[Mechanic]** Judgement of the Crusader's bonus spell-power-derived damage is calculated as a separate "off-character" value that is NOT affected by percentage damage modifiers like Improved Seal of Righteousness or Sanctity Aura — only the normal on-character SP contribution to Seal of Righteousness gets boosted by those; confirmed by Destroses — [[d:paladin#1461826806310109522]] (Not Normal), [[d:paladin#1461828811900457063]] (Destroses), 2026-01-17.
- **[Mechanic]** Vengeance triggers off ANY crit (melee or spell) — it doesn't distinguish crit type. The Soulforge 4-piece set bonus, however, only procs off actual melee hits, so neither the Gilneas ring's fire proc nor other spellstrikes (which aren't melee hits) trigger it — [[d:paladin#1462450930393026761]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1462451166993453119]] (Grey), 2026-01-18.
- **[Weapon skill]** 305 and 308 weapon skill both provide the same -1% miss chance breakpoint (i.e., 305 is enough, 308 gives no extra benefit until the next breakpoint) — [[d:paladin#1462080796125954068]] (Grey, 2026-01-19).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Prot]** Righteous Fury (learned at level 16) is the tanking prerequisite; central single-target threat tools are Holy Strike and Judgement of Righteousness, with Consecration (learned at 20) making AoE threat significantly easier — [[d:paladin#1460966828607013027]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1460966596980772875]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1460967146044784731]] (Grey), 2026-01-14.
- **[Prot pulling]** Consecration is not a true "AoE taunt": mobs path toward whoever is doing almost any action other than standing still 15ft behind the tank — drinking, buffing, having a HoT/Renew ticking, generating rage/mana, or using a potion will all draw mobs away from the tank. Consecration itself does 0 damage for its first 2 seconds, so it must be pre-cast (dropped as you approach, timed so the first tick lands as mobs arrive) rather than cast after mobs are already in range, or they will run straight through it untouched. At higher levels/faster mob speeds, mobs can cross the whole Consecration radius between ticks, requiring even more precise pre-casting — [[d:paladin#1461015656781582367]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1461017585184276572]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1461018234927976587]] (Grey), 2026-01-14.
- **[Prot dungeon opener]** Standard dungeon-tank opener: drop Consecrate while running in (so it lands where mobs meet you), Holy Strike the first target, Judgement the second, Seal of Righteousness + auto the third; Holy Strike covers its own mana cost, and usually only 1 Consecration cast per pull is needed once Holy Strike + autos are running — [[d:paladin#1461408170533589197]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1461409404720123935]] (Grey), 2026-01-15/16. For pure AoE-heavy pulls, some tanks just run Seal of Wisdom the whole time to spam Consecration forever without oom (trivial extra AoE threat as a side benefit) — [[d:paladin#1461409508860366889]] (Silverhand).
- **[Prot]** Cookie-cutter endgame Prot build is **11/33/7**; see Talent Builds section for the full breakdown (Sanctity self-support, Reckoning 1-3 points, Imp Blessings > Benediction case, wiggle-room talents).
- **[Prot avoidance math]** Defense gives 0.04% to each of dodge/parry/block/miss/anti-crit per point; 8 Defense → 0.96% hard avoidance + 0.32% block/anti-crit, while 6 Defense → 0.96% "combined avoidance" (the stat relevant to the 102.4% crush-immunity threshold); crush immunity itself cannot be reached via Defense alone (only crit immunity can) — see Stats section for full detail — [[d:paladin#1460803234695479420]] (Grey, 2026-01-14).
- **[Prot tank comparison]** Cross-class T3 8-piece tank set comparison (excluding ring/helmet slot as noted): Bear T3 prevents 42% crits, 4.72% avoidance from defense + 15.97% total avoidance (with agility/dodge), 370 AP, 8.25% crit; Paladin T3 prevents 43% crits, 7.32%+1.85%+3% = 12.17% avoidance, 161 SP, 1.85% crit; Warrior T3 prevents 55% crits, 14.24% avoidance, 277 AP, 0% crit. Conclusion: "the bear set is simply, by a wide margin, the best of the tank T3s... in terms of eHP, avoidance, and threat output" — [[d:paladin#1461345980510113848]] (Grey, 2026-01-15, detailed math).
- **[Prot]** Prot Paladin's raw threat/eHP scaling is considered "undertuned" across the board relative to other tanks by mid-late content: T1 has an overtuned 8pc bonus; T2 has low threat itself but base damage is high enough through BWL; T2.5 has effectively no HP (can't be enchanted enough to fix); T3 has neither threat nor avoidance; T3.5 itemization is subpar (helm/shoulder/chest still below Warrior T2.5-equivalent despite a ~40% stamina buff) and lacks good off-pieces — [[d:paladin#1461307583401951387]] (Grey, 2026-01-15, detailed tier-by-tier breakdown). The core late-game problem isn't damage blocked, but that gear lacks enough avoidance to reach crush immunity without sacrificing threat — [[d:paladin#1461307987317620806]] (Grey).
- **[Prot]** Paladin is regarded as possibly the best MC tank currently, with Druid overtaking it once leather gear starts scaling exponentially around the T2.5 tier — [[d:paladin#1462148488006471913]] (Grey, 2026-01-16). Later community read (mid-tier, 2026-01-16): Shaman is one of the best single-target tanks now, Paladin wins at AoE tanking, Druid gets very strong at T3, Warriors fall behind on threat — [[d:paladin#1462152915652448391]] (Ironbob).
- **[Prot mana]** Paladin tank mana economy is considered poor outside of optimal buff support: Grey describes going oom on Thaddius after gearing into T3 (less mp5/spirit) for an unfamiliar healer team, needing to self-apply Judgement of Wisdom and even skip Blessing of Salvation usage — [[d:paladin#1462154488663244840]] (Grey, 2026-01-16).
- **[Prot]** Blocking mechanics: Str does NOT give block chance, only block value (contradicts a rumor that it would) — [[d:paladin#1462711017581576367]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-01-19). Stacking every current source of block value on a paladin yields 766 total block value (1305 with the Sapphiron trinket active) — [[d:paladin#1461400837569118394]] (Grey, 2026-01-15).
- **[Prot]** Maexxna's Web Wrap does NOT fully disable avoidance — Grey confirmed via logs that dodges/misses DO occur while web-wrapped, though no confirmed parries were found across a dozen checked logs — [[d:paladin#1462105230819725444]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1462110397183168578]] (Grey), 2026-01-17.
- **[Prot shields]** Shield recommendations by content: Argent Dawn shield is a mass-AoE shield great for big dungeon pulls/farming (e.g. lashers) but falls off hard in raids; Vault Defender ("budget Drillborer Disc") is the best pre-raid option until a raid shield drops; best general raid shields cited: Sulfuron Aegis (good in MC), ZG/Hakkar shield (good almost everywhere after), Defender of Nordrassil ("Nord shield"); Force Reactive Disc (FRD) strongly preferred over Skullflame Shield for tanking/AoE farming ("please do not ever get skullflame shield, it is noob bait... cannot do mass aoe farms like FRD can") — [[d:paladin#1462326004986150943]] (keungzhe), [[d:paladin#1462334465140326574]] (Seviry), [[d:paladin#1462358770394075217]] (Destroses), [[d:paladin#1462388304124317796]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1462388598509932786]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1461667095128309792]] (gastro), [[d:paladin#1461667214578028668]] (gastro), 2026-01-16/18. FRD is not raid-friendly though — it "lags other players and consumes 2 buff slots they'd rather not waste" — [[d:paladin#1462053855398461450]] (Mithreon, 2026-01-17); it also breaks/needs repair frequently (once per wing in Naxx) but is cheap (~3g for a full repair) and considered the single most gold-efficient damage consumable-equivalent in the game — [[d:paladin#1462089634526789732]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1462090228192776468]] (Grey), 2026-01-17.
- **[Naxx MT tips]** Practical Naxxramas MT checklist from an experienced tank: use Greater Stoneshield Potion on Patchwerk, Kel'Thuzad, Sapphiron, Loatheb, Faerlina; save Bulwark cooldown for Maexxna and Gothik; switch weapon oil from normal to Undead Slaying after the Spider Wing; on Thaddius, start tanking on the left side and get Seal of Wisdom judged onto the right-side target before the polarity switch to avoid oom; don't use Exorcism over Consecration if both are available (Consecration has more threat); for Gluth kiting, wear a full mitigation set since Greater Blessings cost a % of base mana; avoid Stratholme Holy Water during progression (low threat value) — use Dynamite instead — [[d:paladin#1462761249325711431]] (angels1m), [[d:paladin#1462761591672934442]] (angels1m), 2026-01-19.
- **[Mana mechanic]** Greater Blessings (Might, Wisdom, Kings, etc.) cost a % of BASE mana (a fixed value regardless of current mana pool size), not current/total mana — this makes them cheaper relative to a geared-up mana pool as you level; only Blessing of Salvation is confirmed % of base mana among testable examples — the rest (Might/Wisdom/Kings) are flat costs of 250/260/270 — [[d:paladin#1462766648921423945]] (Grey, screenshot comparison), [[d:paladin#1462766939356139600]] (Grey), 2026-01-19.

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Melee-weave healing playstyle: run alongside the tank so Holy Strike's Mending Light effect lands as an AoE heal more often; Flash of Light remains the most-used spell, Holy Strike is woven in for AoE heals when in melee range, Holy Shock is used for quick top-offs, and a fast-cast-rank Holy Light is kept for emergency big heals; a proper raid-frame addon (e.g. Puppeteer) is a major QoL help for this playstyle — [[d:paladin#1461721133782138890]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1461721229412008196]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1461722389120548885]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1461722536919175269]] (Mithreon), 2026-01-16.
- **[Holy]** Healing threat modifiers: Holy Paladin healing threat is reduced by -50% (base) and Holy Light specifically has an additional -70% modifier, making healing threat "nonexistent" as a practical raid concern — [[d:paladin#1461721382441320488]] (Mithreon, 2026-01-16).
- **[Holy melee-heal]** Holy Strike's Mending Light heal scales with the Blessed Strikes talent (100% increased healing + 25% healing power coefficient), can crit, applies Daybreak to multiple targets, and costs 0 mana; guild members reported Holy Strike healing contributing 16-30% of their total healing output once geared with crit/Daybreak/Blessed Strikes — at low level (no crit, no Daybreak, no Blessed Strikes) it's correctly described as weak — [[d:paladin#1461520456732508306]] (Aelgagar), [[d:paladin#1461521137132503180]] (Aelgagar), [[d:paladin#1461521410634813653]] (Aelgagar), [[d:paladin#1461529231451357278]] (gastro), 2026-01-16.
- **[Holy stats]** Prioritize healing power until roughly 1000, then healing power/crit tradeoffs become closer; crit's healing-power-equivalent value rises with gear level, commonly cited at 18-40 healing power equivalent, "low 30s" in Naxx gear — [[d:paladin#1461533285275668622]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1461571356230680710]] (keungzhe), 2026-01-16.
- **[Holy leveling]** While leveling, healing power from armor's white-armor-derived "2% as healing power" effect is basically irrelevant — prioritize Intellect for a bigger mana pool instead; armor-based healing power only becomes meaningful at much higher gear levels — [[d:paladin#1462479863645736981]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1462496386611413043]] (Draconuuse), 2026-01-18. Spirit is described as viable only for a gear-heavy Holy Light-focused build, otherwise largely useless — [[d:paladin#1461069979565686815]] (Numi, 2026-01-14).
- **[Holy vanilla-tree critique]** Grey's view: the vanilla Holy talent tree itself was always weak; Holy Paladin's classic-era reputation was carried entirely by Flash of Light's mana efficiency, not the talent tree — comparison: R1 Flash of Light costs 35 mana for 67-77 healing, vs. R1 Flash Heal (Priest) 125 mana for 202-247, R1 Lesser Healing Wave (Shaman) 105 mana for 170-195 — Flash of Light is worse only at exactly 0 HP targets, better with any healing power at all — [[d:paladin#1461048946515509471]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1461052038766133402]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1461053271312437453]] (Grey), 2026-01-15.
- **[Loatheb]** Holy Strike's Mending Light heal is NOT silenced/blocked by Loatheb's Corrupted Mind debuff, so Holy Strike remains usable for healing through that fight; however, Daybreak will NOT trigger off a Holy Strike crit while silenced — [[d:paladin#1462811299619672281]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1462812780234014886]] (angels1m), [[d:paladin#1462816619020554404]] (Mithreon), 2026-01-19.
- **[Holy leveling rotation]** Leveling heal rotation: bring plenty of mana drinks; use Holy Light while tanking, Flash of Light + Holy Strike during downtime; blessing choice while leveling should be Blessing of Wisdom until switching to Prot, then Blessing of Sanctuary style for AoE pulls — [[d:paladin#1462036450869772330]] (bela), [[d:paladin#1461062983718277243]] (Quot), 2026-01-15/16/17.
- **[Holy relics]** Question about a "best relics for holy paladin" list was raised with no concrete answer given in-thread; librams giving mana-return/vengeance/mending-light interactions were discussed generally but no definitive holy-relic list was posted — [[d:paladin#1462052665553850409]] (Nyshak, 2026-01-17; question unanswered).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[MC/Domo, Geddon, Garr]** See Consumables section: prepop Greater Fire Protection Potion + Greater Stoneshield on pull for Domo and Geddon rather than gearing full FR (fire is only ~20% of intake with good physical mitigation); Geddon's Hellfire can be walked out of; Garr add-tanking needs partial FR for Immolate application, and Greater Stoneshield (not Elemental Resistance potions) is correct there.
- **[MC/Ragnaros, Baron]** Ragnaros and Geddon melee autos are pure fire damage, ignoring armor and normally unblockable — but Paladins can, for unexplained reasons, actually block Ragnaros's melee autos (a Turtle-specific quirk); the amount blocked is minimal (~136 damage over a whole fight in one example) — [[d:paladin#1460804943052148815]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1460805048803131548]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1460805624777543824]] (Aelgagar), 2026-01-14. For Baron and Ragnaros, wear as much FR as possible (unlike Geddon/Domo) — [[d:paladin#1460429550671495249]] (Shokisan), 2026-01-12.
- **[Firemaw]** On Firemaw, use the MC fire-resist trinket to wipe your Nefarian-plague/mark stacks; Paladins can wipe marks twice per fight (once via trinket, once via Divine Shield — careful not to accidentally kill the raid by bubbling through raid damage) — [[d:paladin#1461518062187446428]] (Magik), [[d:paladin#1461518665705853081]] (Aelgagar), [[d:paladin#1461518787839791309]] (Magik), 2026-01-16.
- **[Naxx]** Full MT checklist (Greater Stoneshield timing, Bulwark saved for Maexxna/Gothik, oil swap after Spider Wing, Thaddius polarity Seal-of-Wisdom pre-judge, Consecration>Exorcism, Gluth-kite mitigation gearing, avoid Holy Water for threat) — see Tanking specifics section, [[d:paladin#1462761249325711431]] (angels1m, 2026-01-19).
- **[Naxx/Faerlina]** In the Faerlina trash room, when splitting Shadow Bolt Volley groups, run in and drop a Consecrate on the cultists before stepping back out to avoid the volley, rather than avoiding the pull entirely — [[d:paladin#1462767309365051487]] (Grey, 2026-01-19).
- **[Naxx/Maexxna]** Maexxna can perform two Web Wraps in a fight (players caught off guard by this after only planning cooldowns for one); Web Wrap does not fully disable avoidance (dodges/misses still occur) — [[d:paladin#1462102962041000029]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1462105230819725444]] (Grey), 2026-01-17.
- **[Naxx/Loatheb]** Holy Strike healing (Mending Light) still functions through Loatheb's Corrupted Mind silence, but Daybreak procs do not trigger from a silenced Holy Strike crit — see Healing specifics.
- **[K40/Necrodin]** See Spec Viability section for the full Necrodin build description (Lost set 3pc + double T2 3pc, Ancient Accord trinket, Wing of the Time Lord). Standard/current best gear line: 2x T3 3-piece bonus + Lost set + Sunken Temple chest + Thunderfury (or a THC weapon) — [[d:paladin#1460716449470484672]] (Excinerus, 2026-01-13). Running BOTH the Ret T2 3-set and Prot T2 3-set simultaneously gives two independent "+3 holy damage on melee swing" proc instances (this is the core mechanic Necrodin exploits, not a stacking bug of one effect) — [[d:paladin#1462720445710336157]] (Seviry), [[d:paladin#1462720459911991391]] (Mithreon), 2026-01-19. Insomnius chest is explicitly NOT recommended for Necrodin — use the Sunken Temple chest instead — [[d:paladin#1462041976420696139]] (angels1m), [[d:paladin#1462042024537755841]] (angels1m), 2026-01-17.
- **[General]** Lasher farming (open-world, not a dungeon per se) realistically starts around character level 52-54 post dungeon-mob buffs — see Leveling section.

## PvP
- **[Ret AP PvP build]** Suggested AP-focused Ret PvP build using warrior-style AP gear (Abomination's Cage, Endless Rage, Vengeance Bracers, Chromatic Boots, etc.), enough points for Repentance, remaining points into Holy for Spiritual Focus + Improved Holy Light (targeting the 1.5s-cast Holy Light), and into Prot for Guardian's Favor; example split ~**10/7/34** — [[d:paladin#1460037957884055696]] (Leo/Sunache asks), [[d:paladin#1460039423646240779]] (dindunuffinz, single source/unconfirmed).
- **[Ret PvP, endgame example]** One well-geared AP-Ret PvP player (Jesse Potter, near-BiS with 7k+ HP) describes flexibly swapping between "support" (buffing others) and acting as a front-line bruiser depending on group composition/support available; runs Kings or Sanctity Aura situationally, but avoids running Sanctity in combat too often in favor of Resistance/Concentration Aura value — [[d:paladin#1462808467206045768]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1462808606779904189]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1462808850288738398]] (Jesse Potter), 2026-01-19.
- **[Bug/PvP]** `Loop of Unceasing Frost`'s proc will no longer hit Shamans specifically — a patch-note change discussed as closing a "time dilation"-style exploit/quirk against that class in battlegrounds — [[d:paladin#1460752388175692075]] (Lyrazia asks), [[d:paladin#1460752881966649356]] (Lhian, confirms it hits shamans specially), 2026-01-14.
- **[PvP hit cap]** For Spelladin raiding/PvP hit needs: the vast majority of Paladin abilities are melee-hit based, not spell-hit based, so 8% melee hit is the target; get 1% from the Ancient Warfare weapon-skill text quests, up to 3% more from the Precision talent (Prot tree) — Human paladins with Precision + weapon skill talents need only ~3% hit from gear to cap — [[d:paladin#1462963216060514325]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1462978789628711014]] (Shokisan), 2026-01-20.

- **[Nameplates]** PfUI (with Shagu Tweaks embedded) is a common UI choice with threat-colored nameplates and off-tank options, but has a known issue of nameplates floating off-model for tall enemies (e.g. Molten Giants in MC); "tankplates" mentioned as an alternative addon that positions nameplates better and shows elite status — [[d:paladin#1462712596951076935]] (George), [[d:paladin#1462712842636497001]] (George), [[d:paladin#1462715264436011090]] (angels1m), 2026-01-19.

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Bug, resolved/explained]** T2 3-piece set bonus's "spellstrike" is coded to count as BOTH "casting a spell" and "dealing damage with a spell," unlike ordinary weapon/item spellstrikes which only count as the latter — this is why only T2 3p can proc Wrath of Cenarius, Sigil of Ancient Accord, and other "on direct spell hit"/"on spell cast" effects. Widely believed by devs and players to be an unintentional bug, not a deliberate design choice — [[d:paladin#1460924106487759004]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1460948900113416317]] (Grey, definitive breakdown: "Sigil procs off casting a spell... WoC procs off casting a spell AND dealing damage with a spell... T2 3pc counts as both... Ring of electrical binding only counts as dealing damage with a spell"), 2026-01-13/14. Community is split: some (dusty, angels1m, ethereum) want it "fixed"/removed since it forces 3-6 gear slots into T2 and makes Sigil of Ancient Accord (5%+ of Ret damage) a near-mandatory MC trinket farm; others (Adamant, Raugtreefell, Grey) argue it's fun, not overpowered outside Paladin due to weapon/gear limitations, and shouldn't be nerfed without also opening the mechanic to other classes carefully — [[d:paladin#1460912185923469507]] (dusty), [[d:paladin#1460915765346697371]] (dusty), [[d:paladin#1460913086348267532]] (Raugtreefell), [[d:paladin#1460941292505661556]] (Adamant); Lhian reportedly wants to keep it as-is but hasn't committed either way — [[d:paladin#1460870250110259210]] (Raugtreefell asks), [[d:paladin#1460872794538512476]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1460907305112440905]] (dusty, "murky area" summary).
- **[Bug]** Precision talent (Prot tree, grants spell hit) does NOT appear to work for the Paladin's own holy-damage spells — extensive community Consecration-resist testing (Destroses, ~2400+ ticks) showed no measurable improvement from talenting Precision, and this was echoed by Adamant/angels1m; it MAY still work for granting spell hit to gear-based "direct spell" procs like Sigil of Ancient Accord, Sunken Temple chest, and Insomnius (Adamant measured ~9% total spell hit with Insomnius+Emerald Blessing, consistent with Precision applying there) — reported to devs via a bug report by Destroses — [[d:paladin#1462744268941692959]] (Adamant), [[d:paladin#1462746111868665950]] (Adamant), [[d:paladin#1462755297155350558]] (Destroses), [[d:paladin#1462821520840724584]] (angels1m: "Precision talent doesn't work on holy dmg spells"), [[d:paladin#1462821630148476929]] (Destroses: also didn't work for nature spellstrikes), [[d:paladin#1462821956138307624]] (Destroses, filing bug report), [[d:paladin#1462823425855983701]] (Destroses, directly flags it to Lhian), 2026-01-19/20.
- **[Bug]** Seal-twisting no longer functions: the old ~0.5s grace period where Seal of Command's buff persisted after switching seals is gone; widely believed to be an unintentional side-effect of another fix rather than a deliberate removal, and might be restored — [[d:paladin#1460265608397717598]] (Blackfox), [[d:paladin#1460199096324194345]] (Jesse Potter: "delay on SoC buff is gone (accidentally allegedly)"), 2026-01-12.
- **[Quirk, Turtle-specific]** Paladins can block Ragnaros's (and by extension Firelord-type) melee autos despite those attacks being pure fire damage that should ordinarily bypass blocking entirely — an unexplained Turtle-specific interaction, though the amount actually blocked is negligible (~136 damage across a full Ragnaros fight in one example) — [[d:paladin#1460804943052148815]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1460805048803131548]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1460805624777543824]] (Aelgagar), 2026-01-14.
- **[Bug]** BetterCharacterStats addon does not track active spellpower trinkets in its displayed SP total, leading to confusion about whether TOEP-style trinkets or charm-style buffs are actually applying — [[d:paladin#1459444102855921756]] (Grey, 2026-01-10).
- **[Quirk]** Buff-slot visual cap: exceeding the visual buff-bar cap does not stop buffs from functioning, but it does break some one-button macros that rely on `UnitBuff` scanning to detect active seals/buffs, since those buffs become invisible to the scan past the cap — [[d:paladin#1461393332973342902]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1461405610527428691]] (Grey, "he uses one-button macros, 100% chance"), 2026-01-15/17.
