## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret]** Two viable Ret builds: SoC (Seal of Command) and SoR (Seal of Righteousness) — SoC is better for leveling and solo play, SoR is slightly better for endgame raid DPS — [[d:paladin#1483867158860791910]] (gastro, 2026-03-18; +1 Beldrin agreeing [[d:paladin#1483867152111894669]]).
- **[Ret]** Spellpower ("SP") Ret / "Necrodin" (2H weapon focused on spell-power procs like Ashbringer/Chronobreaker/Ironfoe) requires T2.5+ or K40 gear to not underperform; without that gear it's "dogwater" vs geared warriors/rogues, and "the only tier where necro can outshine SP Ret is probably BWL" — [[d:paladin#1483892011806232636]] (Agnesia, 2026-03-18), [[d:paladin#1483866053439389696]] (keungzhe, 2026-03-18). T2.5 is "required for spellret to perform at all" — [[d:paladin#1483866848129978628]] (gastro, 2026-03-18). Spellret in Naxx BiS without Kara gear tops parses — [[d:paladin#1483866118279139339]] (gastro, 2026-03-18).
- **[Ret]** "Hybrid SoR" and "full spellret" are effectively the same thing on current patch; there's no longer a way to go "lean full spellpower" (that's a holdover term from an old patch) — it's SoR-ret and SoC-ret with varying stat-hybrid degrees — [[d:paladin#1484130623416438886]] (Dodging, 2026-03-18).
- **[Ret]** Even with expected buffs, Ret is still projected behind Rogues/Warriors in raid DPS ("way behind rogues and warriors... rogues and warriors still outdps us on fucking Medivh") — [[d:paladin#1483896015839826083]] (keungzhe, 2026-03-18), [[d:paladin#1483896176892706857]] (Beldrin, 2026-03-18).
- **[Prot]** "Paladin has basically been a noobgod for the last year": bad-DPS raids think Prot Paladin tanks are OP, good-DPS raids know they're underpowered; Bears have been the only viable MT/1OT since August because top-end DPS threat (demo warlock pets, warrior enrage windows peaking 4500-5000+ dps) outpaces what a Paladin can hold onto while staying above ~6500 HP (vs Warrior's 8000 armor) — [[d:paladin#1484146915389079599]] (Grey, 2026-03-19).
- **[Shockadin/Holy DPS]** Detailed critique from Karadra: Shockadin is not currently viable as a real DPS spec — Holy Shock mana cost too high (OOMs in 20-30s even full SP gear), 20s base CD too long, low base damage (~270 at last rank), forced to share gear with Ret (STR+SP), and forced to spend points in Precision (5th-row prot talent) for both melee+spell hit — [[d:paladin#1483554175857070271]] (Karadra, 2026-03-17), [[d:paladin#1483554294652469451]] (Karadra, 2026-03-17). Response from Grey/gastro: this is intentional — Holy Shock's mana cost/scaling is balanced around its *healing* use (Illumination synergy is heal-only), Crusader Strike resets Holy Shock (not Holy Strike) because the spec is built for a healer choosing between ST heal (Crusader→Shock) vs AoE heal (Holy Strike); a real damage-competitive Holy Shock would have to be a "nuke" and would break PvP — [[d:paladin#1483557417471184896]] (Grey, 2026-03-17), [[d:paladin#1483565143051866273]] (Beldrin, 2026-03-18), [[d:paladin#1483560260626157720]] (gastro, 2026-03-17). Small possible buff floated: letting Illumination's mana-return also apply to Holy Shock's damage cast — [[d:paladin#1483562529480511641]] (gastro/Karadra, 2026-03-17), noted by Grey as "somewhat likely" but not guaranteed — [[d:paladin#1484150979942154240]] area / [[d:paladin#1483788...]] — see [[d:paladin#1484568...]]. Disputed/open — see Contested section.
- **[PvP general]** Ret PvP viewed as strong on burst but weak on utility ("no crowd control, no real meat or gap closing"); "shockadin" (Holy/Prot hybrid with shield, using Holy Shock + Hand of Rag/etc.) is viewed as tankier and harder to kill 1v1 for melee — [[d:paladin#1483194352116301946]] (dusty, 2026-03-16), [[d:paladin#1483193618968740051]] (Shokisan, 2026-03-16), [[d:paladin#1483193788594782381]] (Shokisan, 2026-03-16).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Melee hit]** Melee hit-cap table for level 63 mobs vs weapon skill (Grey/discussion): 8% miss at 300 weapon skill, dropping 0.2%/skill point up to 5% at 315 weapon skill — i.e., roughly 1 weapon skill ≈ 0.2% reduced miss chance, consistent with "0.2% hit per weapon skill" — [[d:paladin#1483293633779073024]] (Grey, 2026-03-16), [[d:paladin#1483610983200198727]] (Svarrog question, 2026-03-17, unanswered directly but consistent with Grey's table).
- **[Ret/hit]** Need ~3% hit from gear (rest typically from talents / T-gear / a neck item); paladins do not need full hit-cap for both melee and spell simultaneously — many casters run without spell hitcap, MT tanks routinely run without melee hitcap on some fights (88-90% spell hit "good enough" on certain fights) because other stats are more valuable — [[d:paladin#1483974125755306035]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-03-19), [[d:paladin#1483568788073873508]] (Grey, 2026-03-17).
- **[Prot threat math]** Sigil of the Lost (Ret T2 set bonus item) vs 8-piece Prot T2 threat-set math (Grey): Sigil ≈15.4% proc chance per melee hit for 400 threat = ~61 threat/melee hit; 8pc T2 set bonus ≈50% proc chance per melee hit for 213 threat = ~106.5 threat/melee hit — 8pc set bonus wins decisively, and wearing partial Ret T2 (belt/pants/bracers/helm/shoulders/gloves/chest/boots) for Sigil costs stamina and spellpower better spent on trinkets like TOEP/Briarwood Reed/Nelth's Tear — [[d:paladin#1483516161353056407]] (Grey, 2026-03-17).
- **[Prot threat math, post-patch]** Judgement of Righteousness (JoR) vs Holy Strike (HS) per-cast threat at 1000 SP, with T2.5 3pc=15% JoR / Righteous Strikes 25%dmg+25%threat on HS: JoR ≈253 threat/cast (can't be missed/dodged/parried); HS ≈416.83 threat/cast but can be dodged/parried (~20% of the time, "416x0.8=333" effective average) — HS still favored per-cast due to weapon-damage/AP scaling (e.g., Thunderfury's ~1400 AP raid-buffed adds ~480 extra threat to HS that JoR doesn't get) and its shorter (6s) CD vs JoR's 8s CD (a further 1.33x multiplier) — [[d:paladin#1484152711673942147]] (Grey, 2026-03-19), [[d:paladin#1484153900255674399]] (Grey, 2026-03-19), [[d:paladin#1484154547575459893]] (Grey, 2026-03-19), [[d:paladin#1484155096827691038]] (Grey, 2026-03-19).
- **[Ret/spellret crit]** Spell crit-to-healing-power-equivalent ratio on Ambershire currently around 1:12 (2% spellcrit trinket ≈ as good on average as a 30 healing-power trinket, on a healer build) — but full crit-stacking is not advised since crit can't hit 100% and an unlucky non-crit streak hurts badly — [[d:paladin#1483391571868258396]] (Mithreon, 2026-03-17), [[d:paladin#1483392469856161873]] (Mithreon, 2026-03-17), [[d:paladin#1483392698567364688]] (Mithreon, 2026-03-17).
- **[Spellret gearing]** Reaching 26% melee crit post-consumables+Kings via 3 stat gems, 3 Agi on cloak, 4 stats on chest, plus Mongoose/Scorpok/Elemental Sharpening Stone weapon enchant combo; more Agi enchants on boots/bracers/legs/helm push crit further — [[d:paladin#1483973204140888074]] (Beldrin, 2026-03-19), [[d:paladin#1483973387205345460]] (Beldrin, 2026-03-19), [[d:paladin#1483973492838764697]] (Beldrin, 2026-03-19).
- **[Prot, confirmed patch-day math]** Holy Strike (HS) rework, explained post-launch by Grey: base damage drops from ~300 to ~137, but gains 40% weapon-damage scaling (effectively AP-like scaling, but weapon auto-DPS now matters, unlike pure AP scaling). Net result: HS goes from ~150% of SP as threat (current, no T2.5) / ~168% (current, with old T2.5 HS-dmg bonus) to 250% of SP as threat post-patch — this bakes the old T2.5 3pc bonus into the base kit, so losing that bonus (T2.5 3pc moves to 15% JoR instead) is not really a nerf: "HS going from 41% to 71% [of some damage-mod total] is like an 80% scaling increase" — [[d:paladin#1484384590444626031]] (Grey, 2026-03-20), [[d:paladin#1484382097119645888]] (Grey, 2026-03-20).
- **[Prot, confirmed patch-day math]** Because HS now scales off weapon damage, undergeared/fresh-60 tanks see a real DPS/threat *drop*, while fully-buffed/BiS tanks see a large gain: example given — a naked Prot Paladin with only a 10g "Mass of McGowan" weapon plus Might/Battle Shout sees HS drop from ~1100 (current) to ~806 (post-patch, buffed) or ~683 (completely unbuffed); "if you have any gear or buffs whatsoever... you basically break even." Overall framed as fixing a long-standing problem where fresh-60 Prot Paladins looked overpowered (could hold threat while nearly naked) while raid-BiS Prot Paladins scaled poorly — post-patch it's a "significant nerf as fresh 60 in dungeons, significant buff in full BiS, slight buff in between" — [[d:paladin#1484390780394143784]] (Grey, 2026-03-20), [[d:paladin#1484391142702186496]] (Grey, 2026-03-20).
- **[Prot]** Full BiS improvement estimated at "about 10-20% more Holy Strike damage under normal circumstances, 40-50% more under peak circumstances [buffs/procs active], 15% more JoR damage" — [[d:paladin#1484380776333316167]] (Grey, 2026-03-20). - **[Prot]** Divine Intellect generally beats Divine Strength for Prot: max mana also increases mp5 via Shield Specialization, so a bigger mana pool both lasts longer and regenerates faster; with ~6000 mana, Holy Shield rank 3 roughly breaks even on its own mana cost via Shield Spec procs (rank 1 nets a slight gain). Strength's return is comparatively small in typical Prot gear (Grey: ~370 Str pre-Divine-Might gives only ~5.3 autoattack dps and ~18 threat/~3tps to Holy Strike, vs. Int's ~800 extra mana giving ~16 mana per Shield Spec proc, ~15% effective Holy Shield cost reduction) — holds true from MC through K40 gear levels — [[d:paladin#1484482699644108901]] (Grey, 2026-03-20). - **[Ret/spellret]** Ring of the Fallen God mechanic explained: Judgement of Wisdom has a chance to proc mana return, and a "spellstrike" (an item effect that deals bonus magic damage on melee hit) counts as a separate instance of damage that can also trigger that mana-return chance — the intended use is to run 1+ spellstrike items with JoW to sustain mana at the cost of slightly less raw damage. The T2 3-piece set bonus doubles down on this because its own spellstrike-like proc also counts as a spell "landing," letting it trigger other on-spell-landing effects too. AP/SoC Ret gets far less value from this than SP/SoR Ret, because SoC's abilities don't scale mana cost with spellpower the way SoR's do — [[d:paladin#1485049143448830163]] (Olivaar of Moments, 2026-03-22), [[d:paladin#1485049652511244298]] (Olivaar of Moments, 2026-03-22), [[d:paladin#1485050416197796052]] (Olivaar of Moments, 2026-03-22).
- **[Prot]** Zealous Defense (Crusader Strike's new mitigation effect: 30% damage reduction on your next blocked attack) has a real niche in planned tank-swap scenarios: pre-apply Holy Shield, taunt, Crusader Strike — guarantees 30% less damage on the next incoming hit, buying the healer breathing room; a "crusader → hit → bulwark → bulwark about to end → crusader" cadence can extend a tank's survivability window. Its built-in +25% Holy Strike snap-aggro is also a reasonable alternative to the Vengeance-based global-damage-increase talent for tanks not chasing max threat — [[d:paladin#1484833025177944206]] (bela, 2026-03-21).
- **[Prot]** Community and dev consensus: Crusader Strike has almost no place in normal Prot raid rotations — the main use cases are Patchwerk-style single massive hits, or an OT who must suddenly become MT mid-fight (e.g., a tank-swap boss like BWL's 3rd drake). Per Mithreon, paraphrasing a dev comment, there's "just nothing to do with CS for prot to not make it super niche." For Holy Paladins, Crusader Strike is mainly a PvP tool or used to reset Holy Shock while moving — [[d:paladin#1484825866943987803]] (Mithreon, 2026-03-21), [[d:paladin#1484823910041260132]] (Mithreon, 2026-03-21), [[d:paladin#1484824175431520286]] (Seviry, 2026-03-21).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Holy Strike functions as the AoE-heal tool (large heal, ~6s internal CD, effectively "an instant free Prayer of Healing on a 6-sec cooldown but you have to be in melee range of an enemy") while Flash of Light spam is the single-target/tank-healing tool; Crusader Strike resets Holy Shock (single-target burst heal path) while Holy Strike does not, by design, since Holy Strike is meant to be the AoE-heal branch — [[d:paladin#1484597037365329930]] (Grey, 2026-03-20), [[d:paladin#1484599114355441704]] (Grey, 2026-03-20).
- **[Holy, patch-day]** Widespread, severe complaints (2026-03-20/21) that Holy Paladin healing output collapsed post-patch — reports of 50-70% lower effective healing, Holy Strike self-heals dropping from a pre-patch ~900-1200 to ~500-700, players losing badly on meters to classes they used to beat, several joking about respeccing to Ret — [[d:paladin#1484928533024342057]] (Athurian, 2026-03-21), [[d:paladin#1484931337143058582]] (Athurian, 2026-03-21), [[d:paladin#1484930434029588550]] (Athurian, 2026-03-21), [[d:paladin#1484932297286221924]] (Sookie, 2026-03-21). Two real causes were confirmed by end of chunk: (1) an intentional nerf — Daybreak's base healing cut 16% and its scaling cut 26%, and the T3.5 set bonus's old +40% overall-healing bonus to Holy Strike/Daybreak was removed — [[d:paladin#1484924634599264276]] (Grey, 2026-03-21); (2) an unintentional bug — Mending Light (and likely related Holy Strike heals) missing its baseline ~5% healing-power scaling coefficient, confirmed by Lhian — see Bugs & quirks. The two nerfs stacking together produced a much bigger felt drop than either alone, and player anger was compounded by the scaling bug not being called out in patch notes — [[d:paladin#1484929695828017162]] (Sookie, 2026-03-21).
- **[Holy]** Despite the healing-output complaints, Grey argues Holy Paladin retains outsized raid value independent of the healing meter: blessings, bubbling casters during AoE damage, effectively-infinite-mana tank healing, zero-mana-cost AoE healing (Holy Strike), Nightfall proc utility, and being one of only two classes able to remove magic debuffs from allies while most current raid debuffs are magic — making Holy likely still the most efficient way to deliver Salvation/Kings/Might/Wisdom blessings to a raid even if its personal healing-per-second parse is lower — [[d:paladin#1484938782292840551]] (Grey, 2026-03-21).
- **[Holy DPS/Shockadin]** New "Hallowed Ground" libram (dropped from the new Onyxia Lair boss, Broodcommander Axelus) grants +10% spell hit and +20% damage to Holy Shock — a meaningful, previously-unrequested buff directly addressing Karadra's long-standing Shockadin complaints (see Spec viability section); reaction was strongly positive/surprised ("I didn't see 20% coming, I was thinking 10") — [[d:paladin#1484655722074607756]] (Karadra, 2026-03-20), [[d:paladin#1484759232451711058]] (dusty, 2026-03-21), [[d:paladin#1484654530418311209]] (Stanley, 2026-03-20).
- **[Ret leveling]** For a leveling healer/hybrid: healers in low-level dungeons mostly "play Flash of Light simulator until you get Blessed Strikes"; standard advice for someone maining Holy who also wants to duo/solo-quest is to keep a 2H weapon or sword-and-board Eagle-stat gear rather than committing to a second (Ret) spec — see Leveling section.
