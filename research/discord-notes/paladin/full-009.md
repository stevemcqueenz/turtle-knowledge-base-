- **[general]** Prot's 2.5 and 3.5 set 3-piece bonuses are confirmed being reworked for the coming patch; SoC is also being adjusted — beyond that "all else speculation" — [[d:paladin#1445215573129564191]], [[d:paladin#1445215563914805268]] (Mithreon/Jesse Potter, 2025-12-02; single source, devs did not add detail in-chunk).
- **[ret]** A teased 1.18.1 patch note reads "Vengeance now reduces damage of Seal of Command" — reported as a leak/tease, not confirmed by a dev in this chunk — [[d:paladin#1445373275600326771]] (Blackfox, 2025-12-02; unconfirmed, contrast with a joke "Corrupted Ashbringer -30 stam" leak posted right after that was clearly satirical — [[d:paladin#1445378421009027082]]).
- **[general]** Several talents behave differently from their tooltip text, causing repeated new-player confusion (compiled by Mithreon): Ironclad (only counts base/unbuffed armor from gear, giving +0.02 healbonus per point, not "armor" generally); Reckoning (does not actually grant a literal "extra attack" as worded, functions more like a parry-haste-style proc on block, with an internal cooldown preventing rapid re-procs); Righteous Strikes (block bonus scales per Zeal stack 1-3, not a flat 10%); Repentance (many elites/dungeon bosses are not CC-immune despite assumption, and its cooldown/duration scales with the target's attack speed, so "parry-hasting" a boss to minmax the cooldown is possible but considered unintended/cringe design) — [[d:paladin#1444616173982580746]], [[d:paladin#1444617464792219818]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-30).
- **[general]** Devs reportedly said they might add a talent-tooltip hover/shift-hover detail view to fix these description mismatches — [[d:paladin#1444616866118238339]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-30; single source, unconfirmed timeline).
- **[general]** Lhian confirmed directly (2025-12-03): no major talent rework or ability overhaul is coming ("don't expect crazy new abilities, tree reworks etc"); T2 tier sets are staying unchanged ("if t2 would change i would warn about it beforehand") — [[d:paladin#1445568849071308880]], [[d:paladin#1445577261716869130]] (Lhian, 2025-12-03).
- **[ret]** Confirmed: Ret's T2.5 3-piece bonus (currently +6% Crusader Strike damage) is being replaced with a Judgement-damage (JoR/JoC) bonus, explicitly described by Lhian as intentionally "less impactful" so SoR users aren't forced into it; Prot's T2.5 3-piece (currently a Holy Strike damage bonus) is getting the same JoR/JoC-type bonus, "might be bigger for Prot than for Ret" — [[d:paladin#1446231189483425793]], [[d:paladin#1446234621527195779]], [[d:paladin#1446236970400612532]], [[d:paladin#1446237147014238250]] (Jesse Potter/Mithreon relaying Lhian, 2025-12-05).

Lhian directly confirmed the underlying problem: "i know naked prot paladin is almost as strong as bis geared one... new changes def will help with that" and, asked if Prot should expect a small or big adjustment, replied "you should be stronger with the changes" — [[d:paladin#1446244211170087004]], [[d:paladin#1446374013461991486]], [[d:paladin#1446920433977786480]], [[d:paladin#1446920610306330826]], [[d:paladin#1446921040117629040]], [[d:paladin#1446974833089839166]] (Mithreon/dusty/Grey/Lhian, 2025-12-05/06/07).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[ret]** Retribution (SoR) is roughly "B+ tier dps in Kara" currently after 1.18 nerfs, with a possible future buff — [[d:paladin#1444473222350377031]] (Mithreon, 2025-11-30).
- **[ret]** Ret Paladin is viable in raids but very consumable-dependent ("most expensive" spec/class to play well, ~15 potions per raid) and highly Windfury Totem-dependent — anyone with WF outperforms; without WF it suffers like every other melee — [[d:paladin#1445184143351877854]], [[d:paladin#1445184716788732005]], [[d:paladin#1445184832614436884]] (dusty/Jesse Potter, 2025-12-02).
- **[ret]** SoC (Seal of Command) Ret is "completely viable" and "fine on Nord as long as you have WF," but SoR does more overall single-target damage at 60 — [[d:paladin#1445297694452224081]], [[d:paladin#1445215942228316303]] (Destroses/dusty, 2025-12-02). Disputed: Beldrin calls SoC "utter trash," saying SoC-Ret players effectively only use 2 T3 pieces for the 2p bonus rather than the full set — [[d:paladin#1445220729300975706]], [[d:paladin#1445220909408325692]] (Beldrin/dusty, 2025-12-02).
- **[general]** Holy is seen by several as currently the strongest/most "worth playing" Paladin spec ("feels like the only spec worth playing for paladin is holy atm" / "Not entirely wrong") — [[d:paladin#1445215825047982212]], [[d:paladin#1445215874209546332]] (Domy/dusty, 2025-12-02).
- **[tank]** Rough leveling-tank tier ranking given informally: Druid > Paladin > Warrior > Shaman — [[d:paladin#1444943901340471306]] (gastro, 2025-11-30; single source, leveling context).
- **[tank]** At endgame, Enhancement Shaman tanking is argued to be stronger than Prot Paladin or Warrior on threat and AoE threat, despite devs saying Shaman tanking "wasn't meant to be taken seriously" — [[d:paladin#1444596868825747508]], [[d:paladin#1444596953018007643]] (dusty, 2025-11-30; contested opinion, no rebuttal in chunk).
- **[general]** Paladin set-bonus design is repeatedly called the weakest itemization among classes ("2/3 the stat budget as anybody else," "95% of Paladin sets are shit") — [[d:paladin#1445214738962452562]], [[d:paladin#1444599315841552476]], [[d:paladin#1444606168616931388]] (dusty/Mithreon, 2025-11-30/12-02; recurring complaint, not disputed).
- **[ret]** In BiS gear, Mithreon states SoR (Seal of Righteousness) outperforms SoC (Seal of Command) in every raid tier ("MC - SoR, BWL - SoR, AQ40 - SoR, Naxx - SoR, Kara40 - SoR") because SoR is guaranteed on a landed attack, can apply from both the player's own hit and a simultaneous Windfury Totem attack, and reliably triggers on-hit procs; SoC only has a chance to proc, can be dodged/parried, and cannot double-proc off a single WFT attack — [[d:paladin#1446972080213065804]], [[d:paladin#1446972727511486715]], [[d:paladin#1446973245822599268]], [[d:paladin#1446973363699449959]] (Mithreon/Dodging, 2025-12-07). Disputed: Beldrin says SoR's edge is specifically in Naxx/K40, while SoC is comparatively weak in AQ40 "since there's a ton of AoE padding in that cursed hellhole," and cites a specific well-known player ("Lightbourne") who plays SoC in Naxx/Kara and "pulls decent numbers" — [[d:paladin#1445846769408938106]], [[d:paladin#1445846876581662750]], [[d:paladin#1445842170236899490]] (Beldrin, 2025-12-02).
- **[ret]** SoC vs SoR is also heavily gear-dependent, not just a seal choice: Str/Agi-focused ("AP") gear makes SoC hit hard but makes SoR "hit like a noodle," and vice versa for SP-focused gear — a player doing only 199 SoR damage with a full-AP build was told this was the reason, not the seal itself — [[d:paladin#1445833859273986239]], [[d:paladin#1445838911057428581]], [[d:paladin#1445840418213462088]] (Sylvaira/Beldrin/Faithroll, 2025-12-02). - **[ret]** Ret described elsewhere as "pretty mid," dealing decent damage but extremely consumable-dependent, "competes nowhere near warriors, rogues or mages in Naxx+K40," while conversely being "great for solo play" and very powerful while leveling before "dwindling at end game" — [[d:paladin#1445893238111076476]], [[d:paladin#1445893649270571202]], [[d:paladin#1445894479654682735]], [[d:paladin#1445893651409403924]] (Perrapys, 2025-12-04; single perspective, broadly consistent with other spec-viability bullets above).

- **[prot]** Prot Paladin is called "the go-to for everything leveling related" and "the most broken/popular spec" pre-60, strong enough to largely solo through many dungeons in raid gear, but this popularity/dominance is leveling/dungeon-specific and "falls in line pretty fast" once raiding starts — [[d:paladin#1446532061048606801]], [[d:paladin#1446531537293152447]], [[d:paladin#1446532434832392473]], [[d:paladin#1446532354700214384]] (Arle/Beldrin/angels1m, 2025-12-05).

## Leveling
- **[prot]** Holy Strike, not Crusader Strike, is the main threat/damage tool while leveling as Prot: Holy Strike deals a flat, weapon-independent Holy-damage amount (boosted by Righteous Fury since it's Holy damage) while Crusader Strike scales off weak early auto-attack damage and is not boosted by Righteous Fury; "in most cases the flat holy strike amount will do more dmg than crusader strike" — [[d:paladin#1444946712866193529]], [[d:paladin#1444946375044239531]], [[d:paladin#1444946824086556785]] (gastro, 2025-11-30).
- **[prot]** Weapon choice while leveling: prioritize sub-2.0 (ideally sub-1.8) speed weapons for faster Seal of Wisdom/Judgement mana returns; slow weapons (esp. maces) are explicitly bad for Prot post-rework since Holy Strike no longer scales off weapon damage the way it did in an earlier patch ("CC1") — [[d:paladin#1444478932819644448]], [[d:paladin#1445167305926643833]], [[d:paladin#1445174357394718871]], [[d:paladin#1445177847047000104]] (Mithreon/Arle/dusty, 2025-11-30/12-02).
- **[prot]** Judgement of Wisdom is obtainable starting level 18; recommend doing the Stalvan questline around Duskwood level to pick it up — [[d:paladin#1444945049795428464]], [[d:paladin#1444945133257887775]] (Jesse Potter/gastro, 2025-11-30).
- **[prot]** Key leveling talents/tools: Improved Righteous Fury = "huge threat boost"; Righteous Defense gives 10% damage reduction and is "quite good"; Blessing of Sanctuary worth picking up while leveling; Retribution Aura helps AoE threat while leveling — [[d:paladin#1444947265096519680]], [[d:paladin#1444946995587317760]], [[d:paladin#1444947402040672368]], [[d:paladin#1444947476556808294]] (gastro, 2025-11-30).
- **[general]** Improved Blessings talent doesn't show real value while leveling — its benefit scales with long (raid-boss-length) fights and being multiplied across a full raid, so it's low priority pre-60 — [[d:paladin#1444581389042188361]], [[d:paladin#1444581657897336852]] (dusty, 2025-11-30).

- **[general]** Paladin-specific mount cost advantage: the level-40 mount + riding skill is free via a Stormwind Cathedral quest; the level-60 mount + riding skill costs only ~400g via a questline starting in the Cathedral, versus 800+g for most other classes — [[d:paladin#1446220359878836254]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-04). - **[general]** Gearing below level 60 is widely considered low-priority: "everything below 60 is irrelevant," fast leveling (AoE farming with rested XP) is favored over min-maxing pre-60 gear, and 60 is reachable in under a week — [[d:paladin#1446546881508409406]], [[d:paladin#1446547394853474324]] (Domy, 2025-12-05). ## Rotation: single target
- **[ret/SoR]** Standard SoR opener/rotation confirmed correct by Jesse Potter: Sanctity Aura up, Judge SotC, then SoR / Repentance / CS / Consecration / Judge / SoR repeating, "as long as you're not autoing without a Seal up, everything else can just happen" — [[d:paladin#1444819893349257358]], [[d:paladin#1444820134513475675]] (Achmodeus/Jesse Potter, 2025-12-01).
- **[prot]** Well-optimized Prot Paladins essentially never press Crusader Strike, relying on Holy Strike spam for threat — "Literally never" — [[d:paladin#1445121992494940330]], [[d:paladin#1445122023130136730]] (Mima/dusty, 2025-12-01).
- **[ret]** Debate over whether the "HS-CS cycle" (Holy Strike / Crusader Strike alternating) is optimal for Ret damage: a Chinese video guide's use of it was called "questionable"; per-fight log comparison across ~5 fights (crits excluded) found CS hitting roughly 10-15% harder than HS, though this may be confounded by lack of Sunder Armor / no-Str gear in that data — [[d:paladin#1444753002530476092]], [[d:paladin#1444754885970100265]], [[d:paladin#1444755673228509265]] (Faithroll, 2025-12-01; contested, single video's methodology questioned).
- **[ret/spellret mechanics]** Seal of Righteousness (SoR) functions as a coded "second melee hit": any on-hit ("chance on hit") effects, including spellstrike procs (e.g., +N elemental damage items), get doubled while SoR is up; every one of these procs can also trigger Judgement of Wisdom mana return since JoW procs off both melee attacks and spells — [[d:paladin#1445474473422749728]], [[d:paladin#1445474816965611561]], [[d:paladin#1445474853007261887]], [[d:paladin#1445475170256027801]], [[d:paladin#1445475392239698000]] (gastro, 2025-12-02).
- **[spellret weapon procs]** UTB (Untamed Blade) procs on: white hits, SoR/SoC (treated as auto attacks), and abilities (CS/HS); the 3pc set "Divine Strike" effect counts as a spell, so it only triggers spell-related procs, not weapon procs like Crusader/UTB/Windfury/HoJ — [[d:paladin#1445481472549978213]] (Beldrin, 2025-12-02).
- **[spellret]** Tempered Runeblade's on-hit proc is nominally ~1.2 PPM with ~25% SP scaling, but multiple players report real proc rate feels closer to ~2 PPM (dusty: "something has to be broken"); Faithroll's raid logs show it procs roughly every ~10 seconds in practice — [[d:paladin#1445287499747557447]], [[d:paladin#1445289741187678218]], [[d:paladin#1445292069449896048]] (Destroses/dusty/Faithroll, 2025-12-02; contested/unresolved real PPM).
- **[general]** Weapon proc-per-minute (PPM) rate is calculated off the weapon's *base* speed, not the haste-adjusted swing speed — haste does not change the per-swing proc chance, but increases total swings per minute and thus total procs. This is why haste trinkets/gear are especially valuable for SoC (Seal of Command): they artificially increase swings-per-minute (further amplified with Windfury) and thus SoC's total proc count — [[d:paladin#1446802893611991060]], [[d:paladin#1446802943620943913]], [[d:paladin#1446803105802092617]] (Pastor/Dodging/Jesse Potter, 2025-12-06).
- **[general]** A Judgement effect only needs to be applied once — subsequent auto-attacks on the same target refresh its duration without needing to re-cast Judgement — [[d:paladin#1446688156094304469]] (Aelgagar, 2025-12-05).
- **[ashbringer]** Corrupted Ashbringer's on-hit proc triggers from any form of attack: Judgement (JoR/JoC), Seal procs (SoR/SoC), Hammer of Wrath, Strikes, autos, Windfury Totem-granted attacks, and extra attacks from gear; it has 1:1 SP scaling and roughly 1.6 PPM at base — [[d:paladin#1445875462688604170]], [[d:paladin#1445875644297646271]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-04).
- **[ret/SoR]** Opener sequencing example from a top-parsing SoR player: 2 Zeal stacks, then trinket/Flurry proc, then Holy Strike — using cooldowns roughly 7 seconds into the fight; also waits until the auto-swing timer is under 0.7s remaining before popping an on-use haste effect, to get extra cooldown reduction from the haste — [[d:paladin#1445909839632007261]], [[d:paladin#1445910419515379722]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-04).
- **[ret/SoR]** Opener disagreement: Istenneveben (recognized SoR authority) recommends Holy Strike first, then Crusader Strike for the rest of the fight, "generally speaking," reasoning that combat-start armor debuffs are active and you likely get full value from Holy Might at that point — [[d:paladin#1446901119576969370]], [[d:paladin#1446901329166467232]] (Istenneveben, 2025-12-06). A different player instead opens CS > HS > CS > CS specifically to get buff-cap-relevant procs (e.g. Crusader/UTB) active as early as possible for macro conditions to register correctly, even though it isn't necessarily max-DPS — [[d:paladin#1446172112179036241]], [[d:paladin#1446172292316139531]] (Trojh/Gilfrey, 2025-12-04).
- **[ret/SoR]** A one-button SoR + Judgement + reseal macro shared: `#showtooltip` / `/cast [nomybuff:"Seal of Righteousness"] Seal of Righteousness; Judgement` — [[d:paladin#1445860830838259865]] (gastro, 2025-12-02). A separate conditional macro casts Crusader Strike if the Holy Strike buff is active, otherwise casts Holy Strike, via a `/run` script checking `UnitBuff` — [[d:paladin#1447063651981852823]] (Shokisan, 2025-12-07).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[prot leveling]** For low-level dungeon AoE farming, both Reckoning and Vengeance are worth taking together rather than choosing one — [[d:paladin#1446301381664182374]], [[d:paladin#1446303908241739989]], [[d:paladin#1446306127733329974]] (gyatt/Aelgagar/gastro, 2025-12-04).
- **[ret/SoR]** Since Zeal (a Ret buff/stack mechanic) requires ramping up each pull and has no on-demand burst cooldown (unlike e.g. a Fury Warrior's Death Wish/Bloodrage), players compensate by doing faster pulls so Zeal doesn't fall off between packs, and by using Crusader Strike on freshly-summoned Warlock pets specifically to build/maintain Zeal stacks without wasting the hit on a boss that's already dying — [[d:paladin#1446130009117949982]], [[d:paladin#1446134718155194448]], [[d:paladin#1446134870332936234]], [[d:paladin#1446135643779366974]] (Thaodan/Dodging/angels1m, 2025-12-04).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[spellret]** Core stat priority: Stam/Int/SP, with Agility still valued for melee crit (helps maintain Vengeance uptime); spellret specifically does not value spell crit much — gear tier pieces plus SP accessory slots is the general approach — [[d:paladin#1445300483630829639]], [[d:paladin#1445302494153543732]] (PerchyBrown/Destroses, 2025-12-02).
- **[spellret]** Hit cap breakdown to reach 8% needed: 3% from gear + 3% from the Precision talent + 5 weapon skill from a book/farmed mask (Stormwrought Vault, or the T1 head) leaves the remainder to be covered by a hit pendant — [[d:paladin#1444815394534850590]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-11-30/12-01).
- **[prot]** Prioritize SP over pure mitigation/avoidance stacking once baseline avoidance is adequate; over-stacking avoidance/stamina ("chonk") can actually cause threat problems relative to a well-geared group — one tank reports losing threat until respeccing Prot/Holy for Sanctity Aura and swapping helm/leg/glove/bracer/boot enchants to SP — [[d:paladin#1445471553742704640]], [[d:paladin#1445471617340674059]] (Homtanks, 2025-12-02).
- **[prot]** Spellstrike (on-hit elemental-damage proc items) is not essential for mana with Windfury Totem present; without WF (e.g., dungeon tanking), Seal of Wisdom on trash + switching to SoR mainly only for bosses is the standard approach since applying Judgement/switching seals on trash costs more mana than it returns when mobs die fast — [[d:paladin#1445468956151386214]], [[d:paladin#1445469130441625621]], [[d:paladin#1445469329075470376]] (Silverhand/Isvash, 2025-12-02).
- **[prot]** Weapon skill (+5 WS) threat value estimated at roughly a 10% damage increase specifically on glancing blows, which occur on about 40% of autos — treated as a rough estimate, not confirmed precisely — [[d:paladin#1445413436086550529]] (Destroses, 2025-12-02; single source estimate).
- **[ret/SoC]** Each Seal of Command proc deals 24% of spell power as Holy damage — i.e. SoC has a genuine SP-scaling component despite being an AP/Str-focused seal; as a rough conversion for SoC's overall damage, ~12 SP is considered roughly equivalent to ~8 Str — [[d:paladin#1446645457123151987]], [[d:paladin#1446644685190987941]] (Excinerus, 2025-12-06). SoC gearing overall leans crit/Str/Agi ("gear like a warrior"), with off-pieces crit-focused, while still wanting some SP specifically to boost Consecration's AoE damage — [[d:paladin#1446643878538121348]], [[d:paladin#1446644232751419466]], [[d:paladin#1446644247989194984]], [[d:paladin#1446644386816331856]] (silverthornn/Jesse Potter/Excinerus, 2025-12-06).
- **[ret/SoR]** SoR gearing is described as more open/versatile than commonly assumed: it functions like a "dot" (similarly to Consecration), rewarding SP directly, but a less-SP-focused T2-proc-based build is also viable since SoR reliably triggers those on-hit procs — [[d:paladin#1446661543356792872]], [[d:paladin#1446662023344554054]], [[d:paladin#1446663533227016323]] (dusty, 2025-12-06). SoR performs better with a fast/high-attack-frequency weapon (e.g. Shadowstrike) since more swings means more guaranteed SoR "hits" — [[d:paladin#1446644921590091999]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-06).
- **[ret]** Crit soft-cap heuristic for choosing between crit and Strength enchants: with Mongoose (3%), Kings, Mark of the Wild, a Druid raid buff (3%), and a Rogue raid buff (2-4%) all active, once total crit exceeds ~30%, 25 Str becomes better than more crit for SoR; without most of these buffs stacked, 1% extra crit (to get closer to the ~30% mark) is the better pick — [[d:paladin#1445986573526110218]], [[d:paladin#1445986606455455857]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-04).
- **[ret]** SoR is confirmed to only care about melee crit and melee hit for all of its damage sources, despite its damage scaling with spell power — described as "the best thing devs did with pala" for simplifying stat itemization for Retribution — [[d:paladin#1445793797752488088]], [[d:paladin#1445800233186623548]] (Homtanks/Mioko, 2025-12-02).
- **[ret/SoR food+oils]** Detailed optimization from Istenneveben: use Elemental Sharpening Stone for SoC, Spell Power food/oil for SoR (since most SoR damage comes from auto-attacks, Crusader Strike, and Judgement rather than spell-crit-based sources); "SP is almost always better" than crit-food (Spicy/chili) alternatives — logs showed a crit-food pick contributing only ~1.3% of overall damage — [[d:paladin#1445534898466193560]], [[d:paladin#1445536454003523626]], [[d:paladin#1445538871654875267]], [[d:paladin#1445540980760514674]] (Istenneveben/gastro, 2025-12-03).
- **[general]** TWoW devs have not implemented a Blizzard-style "% of Strength converted to Spell Power" mechanic for Paladins, unlike later official expansions — a repeated community wish, not implemented — [[d:paladin#1447095486208016515]] (Aelgagar, 2025-12-07; wishlist/contested, see also Contested section).
- **[tank]** Block Value (BV) is "widely understood to be useless" by most since there's no practical way to stack enough of it to matter — [[d:paladin#1447105685543194715]], [[d:paladin#1447105736546058352]] (gastro, 2025-12-07). Disputed by one player who claims that when heavily stacked, BV currently makes most sub-BWL bosses hit him for "literally zero" — [[d:paladin#1447105618166026321]] (Grey, 2025-12-07; single source, contradicts the mainstream view).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[general]** Reckoning does not literally grant an "extra attack" as its tooltip states; it functions more like a parry-haste-style proc on block and has an internal cooldown preventing rapid re-procs (one rare combat-log example showed 2 procs ~0.08s apart, considered a fluke) — [[d:paladin#1444616173982580746]], [[d:paladin#1444484876693798922]], [[d:paladin#1444485123566469341]], [[d:paladin#1444485899550462083]] (Mithreon/Destroses/angels1m, 2025-11-30).
- **[prot leveling]** Improved Righteous Fury = major threat boost; Righteous Defense (10% damage reduction) is a strong defensive tool while leveling; Blessing of Sanctuary and Retribution Aura (for AoE threat) also recommended while leveling — [[d:paladin#1444947265096519680]], [[d:paladin#1444946995587317760]], [[d:paladin#1444947402040672368]], [[d:paladin#1444947476556808294]] (gastro, 2025-11-30).
- **[general]** Blessing of Protection (BoP) removes physical forms of CC on the target (Rogue Blind post-rework to "physical" rather than poison type, Warrior Fear, Rogue Stun/Sap) but these can no longer be removed via dispel, only via BoP; one player reported being unable to self-BoP out of Blind for unclear reasons — unresolved bug/edge case — [[d:paladin#1444641600046235769]], [[d:paladin#1444641675493507183]], [[d:paladin#1444641814551466077]], [[d:paladin#1444641959258882210]], [[d:paladin#1444642402135576687]] (Pastor/A.I. Alex, 2025-11-30; open question on the self-BoP failure).
- **[holy melee]** Melee/"Shockadin"-style Holy build core loop: Holy Strike AoE-heals nearby allies and procs Daybreak; Crusader Strike instantly resets Holy Shock's cooldown; Holy Strike's cooldown is timed to fit in the middle of casting 3 Flash of Light heals — [[d:paladin#1445241967645823046]] (Dodging, 2025-12-02).
- **[general]** Judgement of Wisdom procs off both melee attacks and spell casts, not just melee — relevant to spellstrike-proc interactions and Seal of Righteousness's "second melee hit" doubling — [[d:paladin#1445475170256027801]] (gastro, 2025-12-02).
- **[general]** Hand of Sacrifice (HoS) has been nerfed and no longer breaks any form of crowd control on the target it's cast on — [[d:paladin#1446480953072554055]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-04). Its damage-transfer amount at low character level is "virtually unnoticeable" — [[d:paladin#1446479506427744298]] (Dodging, 2025-12-04).
- **[general]** Hammer of Justice (HoJ) does NOT share a cooldown with Windfury Totem — confirmed false that they share a CD — [[d:paladin#1446001753718788218]], [[d:paladin#1446008088397348957]] (Insul/Dodging, 2025-12-04).
- **[holy]** Blessed Strikes talent makes Holy Strike's heal component scale with spell power/heal power at 30%, versus a 5% base scaling without the talent — a major Holy Strike healing-scaling lever — [[d:paladin#1446238314255618109]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-05).
- **[holy]** Holy Strike and Holy Shock share the same cooldown (both 6s) — using either puts both on cooldown; Holy Strike (a full 5-target smart heal) is generally preferred over using Crusader Strike to reset Holy Shock, except CS-into-Holy-Shock is more useful in PvP/open world for single-target burst healing — [[d:paladin#1446238404172845088]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-05).
- **[holy]** Concentration Aura (via a Focus-line talent) grants effective spell-pushback immunity ("with Concentration Aura you will never be interrupted") — a major utility pickup for Holy — [[d:paladin#1446239450840563862]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-05). Debate on whether to also take Kings over Improved Concentration Aura: "if you leave Kings to the Prot Paladins they lose 15% of their threat, and if you leave it to the Ret Paladins they just don't click it ever," so Holy often ends up taking Kings itself, making Improved Concentration Aura comparatively redundant — [[d:paladin#1446878343583633409]], [[d:paladin#1446878213228728474]], [[d:paladin#1446878414899384556]] (Grey/Dodging, 2025-12-05).
- **[ret]** Judgement-effect summary by Seal, confirming which Judgements match their Seal's theme and which don't: Righteousness (dmg → dmg), Command (dmg → more dmg vs. stunned targets), Wisdom (mana → also restores mana for teammates), Light (heal → also heals teammates), Justice (stun proc → stops the target's fleeing/fear); Crusader (haste/AP self-buff but sacrifices weapon damage → increases Holy damage the target takes) is called out as the one Judgement that doesn't thematically match its Seal — [[d:paladin#1445930666133819543]], [[d:paladin#1445930709473562645]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-04).
- **[general]** Judgement of Justice (JoJ) niche use: prevents a target from fleeing in fear; relevant against the small number of fear-prone humanoid mobs at 60 (mostly in Plaguelands) and against a few Naxx trash mobs that run when low; Turn Undead is separately used in PvP against players using the "Lost" set — [[d:paladin#1445931915688284251]], [[d:paladin#1445932996266954772]], [[d:paladin#1445933528029200477]] (Silverhand/Jesse Potter, 2025-12-04).
- **[prot/utility]** Divine Shield/Protection (the 1-hour tank defensive CD) is considered awkwardly weak — not strong enough for a genuine "oh-shit" enrage-fight save (30% still isn't enough to avoid dying on the hardest-hitting fights) but not useless either; its main practical use is trivializing large dungeon pulls, though even that is "already easy with a vamp setup" and thus not a huge factor — [[d:paladin#1446321842708287530]], [[d:paladin#1446322034148769865]], [[d:paladin#1446322213606260808]], [[d:paladin#1446322940093267988]] (dusty/Silverhand, 2025-12-04).
- **[prot]** Resistance Auras (Shadow/Frost/Fire) are considered largely obsolete for group utility since Priests (Shadow Protection) and, especially with cross-faction play, Shaman totems (Frost/Fire Resistance Totem plus additional totems) outclass them — [[d:paladin#1446320892803289168]], [[d:paladin#1446320704176787591]] (dusty/Silverhand, 2025-12-04).
- **[general]** Sanctity Aura is repeatedly criticized as a bad design point because its 10% Holy-damage bonus is functionally mandatory for both Prot and Ret, leaving effectively no real aura choice unless two Paladins are in the same group; a repeated (unimplemented) community suggestion is to bake the 10% into base Holy-school damage and repurpose the aura slot for something else — [[d:paladin#1446327852852056124]], [[d:paladin#1446329585150197762]], [[d:paladin#1446330105642225830]] (Silverhand/dusty, 2025-12-04; wishlist, not confirmed as planned).

## Tanking specifics
- **[prot]** Threat mechanics deep-dive: well-optimized Prot essentially plays "one button" (Holy Strike) for threat; even a fully-buffed Holy Strike with Thunderfury can lose the opening threat race to a well-played Bear or Enhancement Shaman tank, and once that initial lead is lost the rest of the kit (Blessing of Sanctuary/Holy Strike, Holy Shield, Reckoning) isn't strong enough to catch back up — [[d:paladin#1446374569232433153]], [[d:paladin#1446376141001593013]], [[d:paladin#1446376950053605598]] (dusty, 2025-12-04).
- **[prot]** Blessing of Sanctuary's flat damage-absorption is calculated *before* armor mitigation is applied — meaning the higher a tank's armor, the less real (post-mitigation-relevant) value the absorb provides; several players consider this a bug/wrong order of operations, since a flat absorb applied after armor calculation "won't break balance" even in K40-level content — [[d:paladin#1446640677877186560]], [[d:paladin#1446640835637547038]], [[d:paladin#1446640915501551616]] (Excinerus/Silverhand, 2025-12-06). Note: Stoneskin Totem for Shaman is said to scale the same (negatively-with-armor) way — [[d:paladin#1446640943133364376]] (Seviry, 2025-12-06).
- **[prot]** Holy Shield: extremely strong for dungeon tanking even if a charge gets consumed instantly ("insane value" from the AoE protection alone); in raids it functions mainly as a crit-immunity cooldown; it must actually be hit by an incoming attack to generate threat from the block, and threat priority can be lost to Warlock pets in that window — [[d:paladin#1446462148858609706]], [[d:paladin#1446462340466737203]], [[d:paladin#1446462377531801641]] (angels1m, 2025-12-04).
- **[prot]** Real boss burst-damage-window math used to argue Paladin tank HP is too low: Sapphiron can deal ~7800-8400 damage in a 2.4s window (auto ~2600 + cleave ~2600 + frost tick ~600 + a second auto or a Drain Life tick); Vaelastrasz ~6500-7700 in 2.4s (flame breath 3.5k + auto 1.2k + cleave 1.2k + fire nova 600, more with crit/parry-haste); Chromaggus's Incinerate can reach ~8000 in a 2.4s window outside enrage; conclusion drawn is that raid bosses commonly deal 7000-8000 damage in a 2.4s span, so ~8k HP is needed to be "reasonably reliable," which full T3.5 struggles to reach without devoting every enchant/food/ring slot to stacking HP — [[d:paladin#1446860014718554144]], [[d:paladin#1446861186963603607]] (Grey, 2025-12-05).
- **[prot]** Maximum achievable avoidance/anti-crush pre-T3.5-nerf: roughly 57.5% with 6/6 T3.5, Chess Event cape, T3 gloves/bracers, Mephistroth belt, and Face of Death, all avoidance-enchanted — [[d:paladin#1446862122452779131]] (Grey, 2025-12-05).
- **[prot/kara]** Anomalus (Kara40) tanking notes: use T2.5 as the first/primary tank set; front-load threat hard in the first ~30s since healing isn't needed then (healers should focus the MT); the boss needs 40 stacks to reach its 8000-damage max hit, and even then there's only a 1% chance to roll 0% resistance on that hit, so single-tanking the fight (vs the "stack" mechanic) usually isn't worth the risk for the marginal benefit; save Bulwark of the Righteous and a resist trinket (GAPP) for high stack counts (30-32+, depending on kill speed) — [[d:paladin#1446930914725859420]], [[d:paladin#1446931087908405433]], [[d:paladin#1446932033933934622]], [[d:paladin#1446932383768248584]], [[d:paladin#1446933068526129272]] (angels1m/Grey, 2025-12-05).
- **[prot]** A forum proposal to nerf Prot for dungeon tanking (citing "infinite threat/mana" pre-raid) was broadly dismissed by regulars as ignorant of other tank specs' capabilities, though angels1m offers a nuanced read: Prot's leveling/dungeon threat is easy to overtune specifically because Prot's threat abilities scale mostly with SP, and there is no SP access before level 40, so a targeted nerf to Seal of Wisdom + Consecration specifically (the core of easy AoE leveling) is the likely lever, if any — [[d:paladin#1446523300732993546]], [[d:paladin#1446530774483603611]], [[d:paladin#1446531171000516752]] (George/angels1m, 2025-12-05; this is exactly what Lhian's confirmed "slight reduction to leveling holy strike threat" change appears to target — see 1.18.1 changes).

## Healing specifics
- **[holy]** In practice, Flash of Light is used far more than Holy Light even in Kara40, due to better mana efficiency and faster cast time — some players report using zero Holy Light in K40; one raid's logs showed roughly a 1:7 Holy Light-to-Flash-of-Light cast ratio — [[d:paladin#1446993999637254155]], [[d:paladin#1446994139928592424]], [[d:paladin#1446996276322369546]] (Iseut, 2025-12-04/07). Holy Light is used situationally, mainly during haste-buff windows or on a target that's just taken a large (1.5-2k) hit — [[d:paladin#1446993729926991995]], [[d:paladin#1446993979244810301]], [[d:paladin#1446996265110999071]] (Oito/Dodging, 2025-12-04).
- **[holy]** Mending Light gives strong effective healing under constant AoE raid damage, but should be thought of as a damage dampener rather than a burst "save" tool — [[d:paladin#1446996775486492809]], [[d:paladin#1446996862426026015]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-04).
- **[holy]** Rotation optimization: after casting a 1.5s Holy Light, it can be worth spending the next GCD on a Judgement re-application/reseal rather than another Flash of Light — [[d:paladin#1446997676200820840]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-04).
- **[holy]** Holy Paladin heals appear to carry reduced aggro compared to other healers: Flash of Light and Holy Light are stated to have reduced threat (Holy Light specifically ~75% reduced), and in practice Holy Paladins are said to almost never pull adds off the tank even during add-heavy fights, unlike Druid/Shaman healers on the same fights — treated by the channel as a known (if slightly grudging) "vanilla thing," not something to nerf outright — [[d:paladin#1446147858289131632]], [[d:paladin#1446145891042988133]], [[d:paladin#1446148082508107788]], [[d:paladin#1446146386142826547]] (Destroses/Mithreon/Beldrin, 2025-12-04).
- **[holy]** Ironclad does not interact with Improved Devotion Aura in a way that would grant healing scaling from something like Mark of the Wild's armor bonus — asked and confirmed no — [[d:paladin#1446239510731165758]], [[d:paladin#1446239588053028945]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-05).

- **[general]** Hunter/Warlock pets can be hit by raid debuffs like Magus/Unstable Mana and wipe the raid if not accounted for — a pet once got the Magus debuff and wiped a full raid; players want these debuffs blacklisted from applying to pets — [[d:paladin#1445204519259471994]], [[d:paladin#1445204678219399366]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-01).
- **[leveling dungeon]** Gilneas City queue times for a leveling Prot tank (level 44) were under 5 minutes during EU hours on Ambershire — [[d:paladin#1445124505176379566]], [[d:paladin#1445124960090718293]] (goyut1/Silverhand, 2025-12-01).
- **[dungeon]** Black Morass (BM) and Stormwind Vault (SWV) are called out as the two dungeons genuinely hard enough at fresh-60 gear to require real damage-reduction tools like Blessing of Sanctuary; most other dungeons don't need it — [[d:paladin#1446437041347887265]] (angels1m, 2025-12-04).
- **[bwl-prep]** No meaningful BWL-specific preparation is possible ahead of time since existing bosses are getting new mechanics on release anyway; general advice is to look up mechanics/skips once available — [[d:paladin#1444818837190082641]], [[d:paladin#1444819907786182809]] (Mithreon/Jesse Potter, 2025-12-01).

## PvP
- **[holy]** Recommended Holy PvP trinkets and philosophy already covered under Gear/BiS (Shard of Nightmare, Scarab Broach, ES trinket, Burrower's Shell); minimalist consumable use (teas/pots/Frost Oil) and gear swaps preferred over heavy consumable investment — see Gear/BiS and Consumables sections.

- **[holy]** "Pregnant pally" (also called giga-tanky/near-unkillable self-healing Holy) was a notable PvP playstyle, mainly a la TBC-era, that largely died out once Death Knights were introduced (not yet relevant to current Turtle content, background/flavor only) — [[d:paladin#1447002663437865051]], [[d:paladin#1447003165701701886]], [[d:paladin#1447004630260121640]] (Seviry, 2025-12-07; that playstyle's actual damage output is only ~400 per strike/JoR — [[d:paladin#1447003940414554183]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-07)).
- **[general]** Warriors running deep Prot spec + Vampiric Touch in PvP/battlegrounds are reported as extremely tanky and hard for casters/melee alike to kill — a real observed anti-meta comp, not Paladin-specific but relevant context for who out-tanks Paladins in PvP too — [[d:paladin#1446753454717407445]], [[d:paladin#1446753637551575112]], [[d:paladin#1446753703083249738]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-12-04; single anecdote).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[general]** PallyPower is considered an essential/expected addon for any Paladin — playing without it (or refusing to install it) is viewed very negatively by raid groups — [[d:paladin#1444589270344929331]], [[d:paladin#1444589481666678794]], [[d:paladin#1444589959666339882]] (Faithroll/dusty, 2025-11-30).
- **[general]** Libram-swap addon "Libramswap" (`https://github.com/jvaneg/Libramswap`, also a maintained fork at `https://github.com/Profiler781/Libramswap`) can fail to trigger spell-related macro clicks after certain state changes; workaround is ensuring spell names in the addon config use correct capitalization — [[d:paladin#1445079150397489182]], [[d:paladin#1445079598973845634]], [[d:paladin#1446079014904135691]] (Achmodeus/angels1m/Agamemnoth, 2025-11-30/12-04).
- **[general]** Libram swap + GCD interaction: swapping a libram and firing the associated ability within the same "batching window" avoids triggering the GCD; a "cold" swap (separate keypresses with a gap) does trigger the GCD — [[d:paladin#1445229031388745748]] (dusty, 2025-11-30).
- **[general]** Itemrack (gear-swap addon) can bug out mid-raid and simply stop swapping sets — a known issue — [[d:paladin#1445229812162498621]], [[d:paladin#1445229981344206948]] (gyatt, 2025-11-30).
- **[general]** Cleverroids/macro-addon weirdness is most often caused by not having SuperWoW installed — [[d:paladin#1445230774982021140]] (gastro, 2025-11-30). Puppeteer error messages can be silenced via a PFUI setting if unwanted — [[d:paladin#1445232485171593367]] (gyatt, 2025-11-30).
- **[tank]** TWThreat addon has a "tankmode" feature to help identify which specific mob (out of many) is hitting a DPS instead of a tank, though it doesn't let you click-target that mob directly — a feature request for that was raised but not resolved in-chunk — [[d:paladin#1445769850839498893]], [[d:paladin#1445771136125112431]] (Excinerus/Mithreon, 2025-12-03).
- **[holy addon]** "QuickHeal" (a heal-automation addon/script) combined with the "Nampower" addon can cause the paladin to erroneously attempt a self-cast Force-Heal-of-Light after Holy Shock/Holy Strike regardless of the caster's own HP — a specific, diagnosed addon-interaction bug — [[d:paladin#1446400968278409310]], [[d:paladin#1446401130115502131]], [[d:paladin#1446401521322426459]] (Faithroll/Mithreon, 2025-12-04).
- **[general]** BetterCharacterStats addon recommended to check current Spell Power total on your character sheet — [[d:paladin#1446539633033744566]] (Faithroll, 2025-12-05).
- **[ret/SoR]** A one-button SoR + Judgement + reseal macro and a Holy-Strike/Crusader-Strike conditional macro are covered under Rotation: single target.

## Races, professions, gold
- **[general]** Second-profession picks alongside Mining/Blacksmithing for a Ret Paladin: Enchanting (steady gold from disenchanting BoP greens/blues) or Herbalism (marginal help for lasher/vamp farming since herbs also drop from Lashers when known) — no strong consensus, "doesn't matter pretty much" — [[d:paladin#1445312926612197457]], [[d:paladin#1445318719936008213]] (PerchyBrown/Zaxx, 2025-11-30).
- **[general]** Blacksmithing (Master Swordsmith path) is required to craft the Tempered Runeblade; the Untempered Runeblade must be crafted first to unlock the follow-up questline (available via a dwarf NPC at LHC once a Winterspring swordsmith quest is done); total material cost for the line is roughly 150-200g — [[d:paladin#1444802899929727108]], [[d:paladin#1444674827540496466]] (Jesse Potter/KO.NC, 2025-11-30).
- **[general]** Paladin-specific mount-cost advantage (level 40 free, level 60 ~400g vs 800+g for most classes) — see Leveling section.
- **[niche]** Goldsmithing is described as "an entire prof spec that exists solely for a single item" — a ring enchant option relevant to the Anomalus fight — with otherwise poor enchant options (can't even do +6 stam) — [[d:paladin#1446932911742910474]], [[d:paladin#1446933032257851455]] (Grey, 2025-12-05).
- **[economy]** Server-economy notes affecting consumable/gearing costs (Ambershire vs. "Nordanaar"/TA): dungeon-cache loot is flooding the market with mats like Large Brilliant Shards and herbs, crashing prices for Alchemy/Enchanting materials and making some gathering professions comparatively unprofitable; Black Morass reputation-exalted rewards (1H weapon + shield) and Argent Dawn exalted rewards (trinket + other items) remain solid gearing-via-gold/rep paths regardless — [[d:paladin#1444619414187016242]], [[d:paladin#1444619553316278312]], [[d:paladin#1445774313532821644]] (Mithreon/Excinerus, 2025-11-30/12-03; economy specifics are server/time-window-dependent, treat as a snapshot not a durable fact).
