## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[all]** Holy Champion (free +5% damage buff to top DPS, given voluntarily by a priest) is being removed in 1.18.1; server consensus is it was being cheesed via respec-persistence (get buff, respec away and back, keep it) and possibly alt-swapping — extensively debated, no one defended keeping it as-is — [[d:paladin#1466938467656400989]] [[d:paladin#1466939633035116690]] [[d:paladin#1466950787929477367]] (2026-01-31).
- **[prot/ret]** Holy Strike gains 40% weapon damage in 1.18.1. Grey's math as prot: pre-patch ~424 non-SP HS damage (base+Righteous Strikes+Sanctity Aura) vs post-patch ~412 (base+weapon dmg+Righteous Strikes+Sanctity Aura) — roughly breaking even for prot, but spellrets swinging 90dps weapons with hundreds of strength get a substantial damage boost from the same change — [[d:paladin#1467614254910799914]] (2026-02-02).

- **[healing]** Holy Strike will incur a guaranteed GCD after 1.18.1 (already true per a recent changelog); advice against libram-swapping for FoL/HS crit as a healer because of this — [[d:paladin#1467165502320279734]] [[d:paladin#1467165689545756767]] (2026-01-31).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[ret]** Ret PvE DPS is considered "average"/"middle of the pack" among good players; a comparably-geared warrior (T1/T2) does almost the same DPS as a near-BiS Ret according to Jesse Potter's own alt comparison — [[d:paladin#1467813800911568936]] [[d:paladin#1467814142240096392]] (2026-02-01).
- **[ret]** Ret is widely disliked for group invites specifically because it competes with the tank for plate gear, independent of actual DPS output — a long, repeated community complaint (roman's leveling saga) — [[d:paladin#1467330297338003774]] [[d:paladin#1467559384174559262]] (2026-01-31/02-01).
- **[ret/pvp]** Ret PvP is a utility/support role, not a primary damage class; requires more gear/minmaxing/awareness than a warrior to be effective; Judgement of Justice valued for peeling enemy casters/healers off the backline — [[d:paladin#1467835072450134098]] [[d:paladin#1467835514148094087]] [[d:paladin#1467846413848412315]] (2026-02-01). 2H Warrior currently does more damage than 2H Ret — [[d:paladin#1467839220105347072]] (2026-02-01).
- **[ret, contested]** "Necrodin" (a gear set that summons ghosts on spellcast) reportedly outperforms standard ret builds by ~30% at low gear levels / on the PvP server, but underperforms standard builds at higher gear levels — single-source claim, not independently verified in-thread — [[d:paladin#1467631974792429762]] [[d:paladin#1467631976101187805]] (2026-02-02).
- **[prot]** Prot is very mana-efficient, especially post Holy-Strike-buff (near-zero-mana threat generation); "Vengeprot" (prot leaning further into Ret-tree Vengeance talents) is discussed as a viable speedrun AoE-tank niche competing with bear druids for that role — [[d:paladin#1467393182344941598]] [[d:paladin#1467399261321826396]] (2026-01-31).
- **[prot]** Spiritual Attunement is broadly considered bad design for prot mana management (see Tanking section) — [[d:paladin#1467551516947775810]] [[d:paladin#1467554035442913393]] (2026-02-01).
- **[prot, expert opinion]** Grey (self-described MT with a high-DPS raid group) argues prot paladin's core design problem is hyper-specialization forcing all-or-nothing gearing choices: full threat gear leaves ~7k HP/40% avoidance; full-tank gear hard-caps DPS at ~1500; crush immunity without hurting top DPS still leaves ~7k HP and forces awkward group-comp tradeoffs (a holy paladin has to sit in the windfury group, or a tank sits in the tree group, "cucking" someone either way) — [[d:paladin#1469370467524542717]] (2026-02-06). Separately, angels1m argues prot paladin's eHP scales better than any other tank and doesn't require stacking ~1.5k SP like other threat-focused tank specs, and cites strong prior-cycle progression-tanking results for the spec, while conceding it "suffers a fuckton" when the raid's skill/gear spread is wide — [[d:paladin#1469312626063573107]] [[d:paladin#1469314174877302905]] [[d:paladin#1469314466192687331]] (2026-02-06). Both agree there's a long-running perceived gap between the itemization team and the class-design team (cited examples: Exorcism's double-hit change wasting mana, Holy Water changes, Holy Shield scaling changes) — [[d:paladin#1469315190435614736]] [[d:paladin#1469316184896962717]] (2026-02-06).
- **[prot, data point]** A geared prot paladin MT reports 753 DPS tanking Patchwerk in "fairly chonky" gear, offered as a real reference point for why many guilds default to bear druid MTs instead (Proph) — [[d:paladin#1469397860117381286]] [[d:paladin#1469398675230032199]] (2026-02-06).
- **[ret]** Raid demand for paladins (Grey): prot struggles for MT/OT slots because only 1 MT + 2 OT are needed per raid and organized groups already have those filled with whatever's currently meta (not prot). Ret struggles because only ~4 paladins total are useful in a raid for blessings, and Holy is already a strong healer, so choosy groups fill all 4 paladin slots with Holy before considering Ret. Rets are "not overplayed," but the classes every guild specifically needs are warriors, shamans, and warlocks — being competent and likeable reportedly matters more than spec choice for getting picked — [[d:paladin#1468996143668138161]] [[d:paladin#1468980396443303976]] (2026-02-05).

## Rotation: single target
- **[all]** Holy Strike vs Crusader Strike in raids: Prot uses Holy Strike exclusively; Ret uses a blend of Holy Strike and Crusader Strike to maintain buffs/procs — [[d:paladin#1467563817193050279]] (2026-01-31).
- **[ret]** SoR/JoR damage formulas (Grey, cross-checked by Proph's fit): SoR spellpower coefficient is 0.092 per second of weapon speed for 1H weapons, 0.125/sec for 2H; Judgement of Righteousness (JoR) spellpower coefficient is 0.71. SoR base (non-SP) damage scales linearly with weapon speed from a 1.0-speed weapon to a 4.0-speed weapon, fitting roughly to `base ≈ 3 + 18 × weapon_speed` — [[d:paladin#1468296561082499196]] [[d:paladin#1468296708030075045]] [[d:paladin#1468298821556637777]] (2026-02-03).
- **[contested, ret]** Consecration's spellpower coefficient was questioned as ~0.66 currently (vanilla's was 33%); no confirmed final number was given in this chunk — [[d:paladin#1468302112587911199]] [[d:paladin#1468306023466139982]] (2026-02-03).
- **[spellret]** Weapon tier list for spellret (community consensus): Corrupted Ashbringer (CA) > UTB (Untamed Blade?) ≈ Might of Menethil (MoM) for SoR proc value, with CA beating both; CA/UTB > rare AQ40 trash axe > Shadowstrike > Kalimdor's Revenge (AQ40 drop, weak/"doodoo" proc) if nothing else is available — [[d:paladin#1466910887225852207]] [[d:paladin#1468234027877273701]] [[d:paladin#1468240255244304406]] (2026-01-30/02-03). The Emerald Sanctum (ES) mace has a poor proc rate and no scaling, not recommended for spellret — [[d:paladin#1468242584429265024]] (2026-02-03).
- **[spellret]** Shadowstrike (1:1 SP-scaling weapon) is better single-target; Cla'ckora's Anchor's AoE poison proc pulls ahead on large multi-target pulls since it spreads to more targets — choice depends on current spellpower — [[d:paladin#1467246244979019796]] [[d:paladin#1467378989076185324]] (2026-01-31).
- **[ret, contested]** SoC (Seal of Command) crit "breakpoint" theory: proponents (Jesse Potter) argue crit chance should be parked at specific values (33%/42%/52%) because in a short fight you can only land a whole number of crits, so crit % between breakpoints is partially "wasted" — Jesse personally runs 33% unbuffed by design, ~42% raid-buffed, situationally 52% with specific trinkets/enchants (Gift of the Martyr-type item, Wristguards of Vengeance, Sapphiron shoulder enchant), and considers other stats (haste/AP/SP/str) better investments past ~37% crit. Skeptics (Proph, Not Normal) argue this only matters for very short/low-sample fights and regresses to a smooth average over a full raid — actively disputed, no resolution — [[d:paladin#1468068189547139164]] [[d:paladin#1468069653489385505]] [[d:paladin#1468074643964432495]] [[d:paladin#1468086877528526991]] (2026-02-03).
- **[ret]** Achievable crit for ret: passive floor of ~5% base + 5% Conviction talent + ~4% from Mongoose/Mark of the Wild/Kings-type buffs + 3% from Leader of the Pack + ~5% from a double-shaman Agility Totem comp (considered the optimal melee raid comp) — reported real numbers: 37% unbuffed (Beldrin), "easily" 47% raid-buffed (Jesse Potter), up to 52% peak with specific gear swaps but not consistently repeatable raid-to-raid — [[d:paladin#1468298675011850395]] [[d:paladin#1468299389683630081]] [[d:paladin#1468306632751448085]] (2026-02-03).
- **[ret]** Glancing blow floor: at 315 weapon skill, glance chance is fixed at ~40% and glance damage is 95% of a normal hit, unaffected by stacking more crit — this puts an effective "hard cap" on useful crit around 60%. Dodge vs a boss is 6.5% at 300 weapon skill, 5% at 315; there's little reason to push weapon skill past 315 — [[d:paladin#1468298257485529184]] [[d:paladin#1468298430790242592]] [[d:paladin#1468305884600860856]] (2026-02-03).
- **[ret]** Hit-table clarification for spellret (Silverhand): everything except Exorcism, Holy Wrath, and Consecration uses the **melee** hit table, not spell hit — so Holy Strike, Judgement, Crusader Strike, seals and autos don't need spell hit investment; Righteous Fury/taunt is spell hit but "if you have to taunt as spellret, something's already gone wrong." Seal of Command CAN miss (it rolls a separate proc chance and a hit roll) if you aren't melee-hit-capped — [[d:paladin#1469407077146300591]] [[d:paladin#1469407191336353965]] [[d:paladin#1469415069174468669]] [[d:paladin#1469559762046947514]] [[d:paladin#1469563712145723458]] (2026-02-06/07).
- **[ret]** Spellstrike gear (Embergem Cuffs, Ring of Electrical Binding, etc.) does NOT make weapon procs (e.g. a runeblade's drain-life proc) fire more often — it exists purely for mana regen (via Judgement of Wisdom) and faster Vengeance ramp-up. A spellstrike proc only triggers Judgement of Wisdom, Vengeance, and the Wrath of Cenarius ring — not other weapon procs — with the notable exception of the T2 3-piece spellstrike, which is "wacky" and counts as a spell hit that can trigger everything (see Gear and Bugs sections) — [[d:paladin#1469407756371886172]] [[d:paladin#1469407919685767399]] [[d:paladin#1469408069208375479]] (2026-02-06).
- **[ret]** Weapon skill for spellret: little reason to sacrifice gear for the last few points to reach 315 when hit chance is covered from other sources; Human race reportedly reaches 311 weapon skill baseline "with the book" (an unspecified weapon-skill quest reward) — [[d:paladin#1469408208975036670]] [[d:paladin#1469410638651719877]] (2026-02-06). However, for a SoC-focused (AP) ret specifically, going from 311 to 317 weapon skill is worth it since the bulk of SoC-ret damage is still autos, so reducing glancing blows matters more there — [[d:paladin#1469490368008687709]] [[d:paladin#1469490521129881764]] (2026-02-07).
- **[ret]** Haste is a very good stat for ret, especially as gear improves ("more haste means more autos, means more seals, means more procs"), and scales multiplicatively well when combined with the Zeal buff's own haste bonus — [[d:paladin#1469419104703221893]] [[d:paladin#1469419186957717690]] [[d:paladin#1469419312245899488]] (2026-02-06). All individual haste sources stack multiplicatively rather than additively (`attack_time / (1+haste1) / (1+haste2) / ...`), which nets out close to (but not exactly) a non-diminishing effect overall, unlike crit (clearly diminishing relative value at higher %) or additive damage buffs (two 10% damage sources compound to +21%, not +20%) — [[d:paladin#1469423761663987895]] [[d:paladin#1469425160946712616]] [[d:paladin#1469425791841337386]] [[d:paladin#1469426133723254849]] (2026-02-07).
- **[ret]** SoC (Seal of Command) weapon choice: MoM (Might of Menethil) vs Corrupted Ashbringer (CA) — CA's edge over MoM is mainly its +1% hit, not its drain-life proc (which "isn't overly powerful vs SoC"); CA nets an estimated 50k+ extra damage over a raid night plus 800 more HP vs MoM, but has less relative value if not also running the WoC ring. Both weapons' SoC proc chance sit around "1 in 3" currently; CA's 3.80 speed procs SoC slightly more often than a 3.60-speed weapon like MoM — [[d:paladin#1469489336985587897]] [[d:paladin#1469489624354390101]] [[d:paladin#1469490127142260848]] [[d:paladin#1469490210332217582]] [[d:paladin#1469490263658463454]] (2026-02-07).
- **[ret]** Gearing threshold for choosing SoR over SoC: with UTB and only ~139 unbuffed spellpower, Proph says to just use Seal of Command — "I can't think of a single benefit from SoR with that setup." SoR generally doesn't beat SoC in damage until you have the T2.5 5-piece set bonus. UTB+SoC is described as good all the way until getting a Corrupted Ashbringer, and even with CA, SoC remains a solid offspec option; hybrid ret with SoR only pulls further ahead with CA specifically (Proph) — [[d:paladin#1468466625534755112]] [[d:paladin#1468466713258623140]] [[d:paladin#1468467181770510450]] [[d:paladin#1468467491431780404]] (2026-02-04). Mechanically, SoR procs on every swing including extra/bonus attacks, while SoC does not proc on extra attacks (dusty) — [[d:paladin#1468487677170159617]] (2026-02-04).
- **[ret]** SoC's spellpower coefficient is relatively low, roughly ~20% — meaning Str is generally fine to prioritize over SP for a SoC-focused build (it boosts SoC, Judgement, Crusader Strike, and Auto damage more broadly), though SP still contributes to all of those except autos to a lesser degree (Jesse Potter) — [[d:paladin#1468774529676611752]] (2026-02-03).
- **[prot]** Concrete Holy Strike numbers from the 1.18.1 weapon-damage change: "minor nerf at MC gear and below, increasingly large buff at BiS" — one T3.5-geared prot's peak Holy Strike (with trinkets active) is projected to go from ~1300 to ~1700 (Grey) — [[d:paladin#1468650842390528163]] (2026-02-04).
- **[prot, threat opener math]** Grey argues average-TPS sims understate how much "opener" burst threat matters for keeping the first-GCD DPS alive on a pull. With 1000 SP and WITHOUT Righteous Strikes/Improved SoR: SoR(1.9 speed)≈474, JoR≈2002, HS≈1668, Auto≈150 → ≈4294 total opening threat (no crits/Holy Shield/Windfury/Thunderfury). WITH those talents: SoR≈521, JoR≈2202, HS≈2710, Auto≈150 → ≈5583 — roughly a 30% increase to minimum opening threat, even though those talents are individually only ~12.77% (Righteous Strikes) and ~2.55% (Improved SoR) of overall sim'd TPS. Proph agrees short-fight/opener outcomes are dominated by crit/miss variance on both sides, but concurs improved-opener talents matter disproportionately for pull safety specifically — [[d:paladin#1469759391401250948]] [[d:paladin#1469780120448401673]] [[d:paladin#1469780849678352627]] [[d:paladin#1469783156629573705]] [[d:paladin#1469783572708593694]] (2026-02-07/08).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[prot]** For multi-target tanking, Seal of Wisdom + Consecration is far better than Seal of Righteousness ("SoW+Cons >>> SoR on multiple targets"); Holy Strike is mana-free and strong — [[d:paladin#1467497544488718404]] [[d:paladin#1467498747805302805]] (2026-02-01).
- **[prot]** Current AoE mana economy requires precasting Consecration and using Greater Blessings to sustain it through a wave, costing 1000+ mana per wave; the planned "grab on cast" Consecration libram (see 1.18.1 section) would cut this to ~505 mana for a single rank-5 cast — [[d:paladin#1467551516947775810]] (2026-02-01, single source/dev-credited plan).
- **[prot]** AoE farming (Stratholme/"strat") setup: Force Reactive Disc + Vampirism stat ("piss+FRD wombocombo") — mobs are pulled and grouped into the Consecration/FRD radius while vamp heals through incoming damage. ~16% vamp recommended for pack pulling (angels1m). Without vamp, use a Holy Shock-focused build and clear pack-by-pack, which is much slower (~2 hours for what would otherwise be a fast clear, per gastro) — [[d:paladin#1468319980377608356]] [[d:paladin#1468314246537547776]] [[d:paladin#1468330142463033374]] (2026-02-03). Reported real stats considered borderline-viable without vamp: 7.7k armor, 5k HP, 403 defense, 48% avoidance, 310 unbuffed spellpower ("you'll get battered even by a single pack... more of an experimental thing than a gold-farming thing at that level") — [[d:paladin#1468312854036877541]] [[d:paladin#1468317093404606722]] [[d:paladin#1468318647406821645]] (2026-02-03). No amount of avoidance alone survives a 10-pack pull; vamp/healing throughput is what matters for big pulls — [[d:paladin#1468331562004254964]] (2026-02-03).
- **[prot]** Lasher packs (nature-damage-immune mobs, common strat/ZG farm target) specifically require Force Reactive Disc rather than nature-damage AoE — [[d:paladin#1467897490769576071]] (2026-02-01).
- **[contested/wishlist]** Long community debate about Seals (SoR/SoC) counting as weapon hits and thus triggering Windfury Totem/Weapon, creating a strong WFT damage-dependency for ret; various unimplemented fixes discussed (make seals not count as weapon attacks, buff seal proc rates/damage instead, split SoC into 2-4 smaller damage instances for spellstrike triggers) — pure community wishlist, nothing confirmed as changed — [[d:paladin#1467613303772545207]] [[d:paladin#1467614720390594694]] [[d:paladin#1467630878497968139]] (2026-02-02).
- **[prot]** Standard Stratholme/AoE-farm vampirism gear "recipe" (Beldrin): 2x rings enchanted with 2% vamp each (6% total) + a JC trinket or the RFD (Force Reactive Disc?) trinket (5% total) + boots/bracers/shoulder vamp enchants (4% total); optionally a Kara10 cloak and an enchanted neck for +1% each. Vamp is explicitly NOT used for lasher farming specifically — it barely saves time there and you'll hit the lockout cap regardless — [[d:paladin#1469334495802036519]] [[d:paladin#1469339592862728295]] [[d:paladin#1469340030907715809]] [[d:paladin#1469340287477223504]] (2026-02-06).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[prot]** Defense skill gives 0.04% each of block/dodge/parry/miss chance plus 0.04% anti-crit per point — [[d:paladin#1467323261577593054]] (2026-02-01).
- **[prot]** Survivability data point (Grey, self-described as "somewhat of a meme but a reference point"): tanking Sapphiron undergeared (T2.5, no shield), staying above ~5000 HP (~75% of that character's pool) made crits a non-issue; below that threshold crits become dangerous. If dying to crits, get more HP/armor or better healer attention rather than chasing avoidance — [[d:paladin#1467325561373331602]] (2026-02-01).
- **[all]** Weapon skill: no strong reason to push past 315 — dodge vs a boss only drops from 6.5% (300 skill) to 5% (315 skill), diminishing further gains — [[d:paladin#1468305884600860856]] (2026-02-03).
- **[tank]** Fire Resistance Aura does NOT stack with Elemental Resistance potions — confirmed by an incident where a tank expected full stacking FR on Ragnaros, ended up with only 260 total fire resist, and got knocked back 7 times on that pull — [[d:paladin#1467777346131202230]] [[d:paladin#1467777534472229046]] [[d:paladin#1467777559239458949]] (2026-02-01). Separately, FR's knockback-prevention is described as pure RNG independent of resist total: full 315 FR can eat every knockback on one pull while relying on only the totem eats none on another — [[d:paladin#1467874722887897212]] (2026-02-01).
- **[all]** Resist caps: Curses cap at 75% resistance, Auras/totems cap at 60% — relevant when trying to counter Curse of Elements (CoE) magic-damage-taken debuffs — [[d:paladin#1467950657712685218]] (2026-02-01).
- **[ret]** Haste stacks multiplicatively across independent sources (`effective_speed = base / [(1+haste1)(1+haste2)...]`), which produces a near-linear net effect overall despite the multiplicative formula; 100% haste from one source halves the swing timer, it does not zero it — [[d:paladin#1468082487740731524]] [[d:paladin#1468083269730963639]] (2026-02-03, confirms an earlier independent claim by Jesse Potter/Mithreon on 2026-01-31 that "100% haste = 50% speed" — [[d:paladin#1466928933214617602]]). Auto-attack + SoC + WFT combined procs-per-minute jump non-linearly at 13/32/44/53 total haste% post-patch (with Might of Menethil active); jumps beyond that are only 3-4% each — [[d:paladin#1466927072222908480]] (2026-01-31, single source).
- **[prot]** Crush immunity: reported target is roughly 57.5% total avoidance (dodge+parry+block+miss). Agility gives 0.05% dodge per point; Defense gives 0.04% to each of dodge/parry/block/miss/anti-crit per point (per the earlier Defense note). Grey's own numbers show the margin is razor-thin — swapping just two ring enchants from 3-all-stats to 6 stamina or 5 spellpower dropped his avoidance from 57.51% to 57.36%/57.05%, "completely killing the point of the set." Blessing of Kings' avoidance contribution disappears immediately on leaving the group — [[d:paladin#1469278620458549303]] [[d:paladin#1469297877686685768]] [[d:paladin#1469300088055533752]] [[d:paladin#1469303480878633185]] (2026-02-06).
- **[prot]** Grey's list of the only routes to crush immunity, all criticized as poor tradeoffs: devoting ~14/18 gear slots to avoidance (kills DPS); running 11 Holy/20 Ret talents (denies a melee Windfury and denies a healer a talent tree buff — a group-comp cost); swapping trinket to a pure-avoidance option like Styleen's (loses 85-170 SP in one swap); running 2/5 Toughness + 3/3 Anticipation (trades eHP on ~40 non-crushing bosses for avoidance on ~6 crushing ones); or respeccing per-boss (costly, makes the whole raid wait) — [[d:paladin#1469311551243681888]] (2026-02-06). Whether it's worth chasing crush immunity at all for specific fights (e.g. a long Mephistroth-type fight) vs simply stacking more HP/armor is disputed between Grey (situationally yes) and angels1m (generally no, only matters for a handful of fights) — [[d:paladin#1469303800044196013]] [[d:paladin#1469304041199632530]] [[d:paladin#1469307638608298226]] (2026-02-06).
- **[prot]** Paladin's stamina/eHP softness is specifically concentrated in the T2.5-to-T3 gear range: "paper tank" in T2.5 gear, "unkillable" once fully in T3 — described as the exact gear-power window the devs need to balance around (angels1m) — [[d:paladin#1469312507268173928]] (2026-02-06).
- **[prot]** Weapon-skill/avoidance numbers vs a raid boss: Parry is a flat 14% regardless of weapon skill and cannot be lowered by gearing; Dodge has a 6.5% base that drops 0.1% per point of weapon skill. Combined dodge+parry chance while tanking is roughly 20% at 305 weapon skill. The only raid buff/debuff that affects hit chance is a 1% spell-hit buff from "Emerald Blessing" — [[d:paladin#1469154638983860408]] [[d:paladin#1469156488516730942]] [[d:paladin#1469158228800569395]] (2026-02-06).
- **[prot]** Defense stat is "just a stat like any other" below 440 defense skill — Armor is described as the more useful overall tank-progress metric; Defense skill only becomes uniquely important at the 440 uncrittable-vs-bosses threshold — [[d:paladin#1469467220672385065]] [[d:paladin#1469467435127275672]] (2026-02-07). Reference BWL-offtank-readiness stats vetted by the community as "decently beefy": ~5k HP, ~380 defense, ~225 unbuffed SP, ~40% avoidance, ~60% damage reduction, ~8500 armor — bring Greater Stoneshield potions for Broodlord and Chromaggus specifically — [[d:paladin#1469460255552438430]] [[d:paladin#1469460906697035837]] [[d:paladin#1469461311434784922]] [[d:paladin#1469467600592568577]] [[d:paladin#1469467921494315232]] (2026-02-07).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[tank]** Blessing of Sanctuary deals flat holy damage per rank on block (not spellpower-scaled, per Jesse Potter); Silverhand argues the damage-reduction math is backwards for a "prot specialty" blessing (should but doesn't scale positively with armor/SP), and speculates it may be deliberately left weak defensively since it primarily enables AoE farming — [[d:paladin#1467331559550877844]] [[d:paladin#1467962612762280230]] [[d:paladin#1467963118188757147]] [[d:paladin#1467964877808210084]] (2026-01-30/02-03).

- **[ret]** Buff-cap management: cancel low-value incoming buffs (Renew, Rejuvenation, priest/shaman armor buffs, Healing Way stacks) to leave room for higher-value ones; can be automated via the VCB addon's customizable auto-cancel list rather than manually clicking buffs off — [[d:paladin#1467715504692138084]] [[d:paladin#1467715911032111251]] [[d:paladin#1467717499230617600]] (2026-02-01). Extended example cancel-list from Jesse Potter (ret): Holy Power(?), Blessing of Light, Blessing of Sanctuary, Shadow Protection, Spirit, Thorns, Healing Way, Inspiration, an Ancestral-type shaman buff, and a druid "Claw" buff — with armor buffs specifically left unblocked in PvP. He uses the ABR addon over VCB for this because ABR's saved profiles worked reliably for him while VCB's didn't — [[d:paladin#1469539640288608298]] [[d:paladin#1469540379685556316]] [[d:paladin#1469540404637601812]] (2026-02-06). The buff-cap visual limit is the same as vanilla, but functionally unlimited; a few specific bugs occur above 32 active buffs, but "nothing gets lost, and 99% of things continue to work normally above that" (Grey) — [[d:paladin#1468351368078037247]] [[d:paladin#1468351702347550750]] (2026-02-04). Buffs pushed off the visible buff bar by the cap can break any macro/addon logic that checks for that buff's presence (e.g. an auto-strike-selector macro) even though the underlying buff may still be active — the DoiteAuras addon (per its author) shows buffs beyond the visual cap; Consolidated Raid Buffs groups multiple buffs under one dropdown icon to reveal ones that would otherwise be hidden, though it doesn't fix the underlying macro-breaking issue — [[d:paladin#1469531886601769081]] [[d:paladin#1469534733242339359]] [[d:paladin#1469534870463316063]] [[d:paladin#1469535673257038086]] [[d:paladin#1469587474597548154]] (2026-02-06).
- **[all]** Windfury Totem/Weapon (WFT) mechanics as understood by the community: ~20% proc chance per melee hit (including Seal of Righteousness/Seal of Command/Holy Strike/Judgement of Righteousness, all of which count as weapon hits), with roughly a 1.5s internal cooldown ("basically every 2s if you time it"). Reckoning-triggered extra swings are treated as a normal auto attack with seal for WFT-proc purposes. T2 3pc/other spellstrike procs do NOT trigger WFT. Common mental shorthand for its overall DPS effect: treat it as roughly +20% more auto attacks, though this is an approximation, not exact — [[d:paladin#1469785873049583668]] [[d:paladin#1469785946772865155]] [[d:paladin#1469786147692478475]] [[d:paladin#1469786494721065001]] [[d:paladin#1469788428731154576]] (2026-02-08).
- **[all]** Thunderfury proc mechanics (per a knowledgeable poster, "30"): 22.8% proc chance (i.e. 7 PPM, corrected up from a commonly-assumed 6 PPM), with threat calculated as `proc_damage × threatmod + 234 × threatmod`. It has no internal cooldown of its own (unlike WFT) and can proc multiple times on a single swing when combined with Windfury and Seal procs. Notably, prot paladin is called out as "the only tank that doesn't just passively multiply all threat by ~1.5" (i.e. has no baseline threat-modifier multiplier the way some other tank classes do), so Thunderfury's flat +234 threat bonus is comparatively more valuable to a paladin. Its proc itself can trigger Vengeance and possibly WoC/Sigil-type effects (it's "basically a spellstrike" for that purpose), but NOT spellstrikes. Paladins value Thunderfury more for its utility (mana/proc access) than its raw threat, while warriors value it more for threat (especially cleave threat) — [[d:paladin#1469788656863805614]] [[d:paladin#1469788813357355169]] [[d:paladin#1469788966604509228]] [[d:paladin#1469788980764741712]] [[d:paladin#1469789948033896508]] [[d:paladin#1469790039092236590]] [[d:paladin#1469790091814637741]] [[d:paladin#1469790174337568809]] [[d:paladin#1469790403086516246]] (2026-02-08). Simulated post-1.18.1 threat comparison (Proph): without modeling WF's effective haste, Thunderfury trails both Comet Signaller and Desecration as a tank weapon by 0.23% and 1.64% total threat respectively; modeling WF as ~20% effective haste, Thunderfury becomes 1.12% ahead of Comet Signaller but still 0.51% behind Desecration — [[d:paladin#1469834372881317888]] [[d:paladin#1469834733151060111]] [[d:paladin#1469834996062621737]] (2026-02-08).
- **[all]** A weapon's flat bonus elemental damage roll (e.g. Thunderfury's 16-30 nature damage) is added on top of normal auto-attack damage but does not scale with AP and isn't counted as "weapon damage" for ability scaling purposes; in the combat log it's lumped in with the physical damage number. Practical benefit: it still applies (and can trigger seals) against physical-damage-immune targets, e.g. letting a Thunderfury-wielder still do (and threat-generate) something against a physical-immune boss add — described as "basically magical damage that behaves as physical" (30/Jesse Potter/Grey) — [[d:paladin#1469790923406708746]] [[d:paladin#1469791192311926839]] [[d:paladin#1469791292535079144]] [[d:paladin#1469791376349724805]] [[d:paladin#1469791466967535737]] [[d:paladin#1469791561641623764]] [[d:paladin#1469791660128075950]] [[d:paladin#1469799974400167986]] (2026-02-08).
- **[holy/tank]** Blessing of Light vs Blessing of Sanctuary when 4 paladins are in a raid: Mithreon states Blessing of Light is strictly better than Sanctuary if at least 1 holy paladin is in the raid, even before buff-cap considerations — but Grey's separate threat-reduction math suggests Sanctuary's ~5-10% damage reduction against big raid-wide damage sources (Anomalus, Sapphiron, Gnarlmoon-type abilities) can be MORE impactful than Blessing of Light unless roughly half the raid's healers are holy paladins, since flat damage reduction like Sanctuary gets proportionally more valuable as a fight's raid-wide damage-reduction percentage climbs — a genuine disagreement/contested point — [[d:paladin#1468885417226141771]] [[d:paladin#1468966452894765319]] [[d:paladin#1468969080861229281]] (2026-02-05). Proph's threat sim separately estimates Sanctuary contributes ~2.3% of a tank's total threat (assuming Holy Shield is kept up alongside it, single-target, 1 attack per 2s) — [[d:paladin#1469383867843608670]] (2026-02-06). With 4 paladins in a raid, a common blessing-assignment convention is: 1 on Improved Blessing of Might, 1 on Improved Blessing of Wisdom, 1 on Blessing of Kings, 1 on Blessing of Salvation, "then disband" — Improved Might is considered to benefit more specs overall than Improved Wisdom (angels1m/Mithreon) — [[d:paladin#1468968922354286808]] [[d:paladin#1468969642851831937]] (2026-02-05).
- **[tank]** Aura choice for tanking: use Sanctity Aura if nobody else in the raid provides it; if someone else already provides Sanctity, Retribution Aura is a worthwhile secondary pick; Devotion Aura (flat armor) is really only better than Retribution in single-target fights against a slow-attacking boss, since Retribution's on-hit proc damage "amounts to basically fuck all" there, or if survivability/damage-taken is the sole concern and threat/damage don't matter at all (Proph/Silverhand) — [[d:paladin#1469723489400262698]] [[d:paladin#1469726010797981809]] [[d:paladin#1469726092981047296]] (2026-02-07). Proph's sim quantifies aura threat contribution: Retribution Aura ≈2.1% of total tank threat (comparable to the Judgement-cooldown talent or Improved SoR), Sanctity Aura ≈9% of total threat ("Sanctity is king") — recommend running Retribution alongside Sanctity when someone else provides Sanctity and damage taken isn't a concern. Retribution's value is lower for a tank running very high pure-avoidance (vs block), since it was modeled around roughly a 60% chance of being hit per attack; squishier/lower-geared tanks trigger it more often (~65%+) — [[d:paladin#1469733503712104672]] [[d:paladin#1469734061621776477]] [[d:paladin#1469735291806941415]] [[d:paladin#1469736647456456804]] (2026-02-07).
- **[prot]** 2H tanking is viable via raw armor + Improved Righteous Fury rather than avoidance: Grey reports 8,000 HP and 73% damage reduction using Maul of the Redeemed Crusader as a 2H weapon (no shield). Weapon math (Desecration [with shield] vs Maul, at 1500 AP, applied to Holy Strike): Desecration → 60 weapon dmg + 91 spell dmg + 103 AP dmg = ~350 total after Sanctity/Righteous Strikes; Maul → 130 weapon dmg + 28 spell dmg + 141 AP dmg = ~411 total. Despite Maul's higher raw Holy Strike number (+60 dmg, +3.3% SP/sec-equivalent), Grey judges it isn't worth losing Reckoning or the extra ~88 SP contribution to JoR/Consecration that a shield provides, so the shield setup is generally still preferred outside of a few specific fights — [[d:paladin#1469023385584668744]] [[d:paladin#1469023708063600651]] [[d:paladin#1469023878436360326]] (2026-02-05).
- **[prot]** Holy Shield's practical value is disputed: Proph's sim estimates Holy Shield will be ~10-12% of total tank threat post-1.18.1 (14-16% with the T3.5 set bonus), comparable to Consecration, plus another ~3% from Reckoning-triggered extra blocks — [[d:paladin#1469028375040495784]] [[d:paladin#1469029144955326676]] (2026-02-05). Grey pushes back hard: in his experience Holy Shield is often a wasted GCD and mana cost even post-buff — fast-dying single mobs (giants, drakonids, captains, smiths, worgens) don't live long enough for it to matter, and on multi-target pulls the first target often dies before Holy Shield's charges are used up, wasting the mana. He says it's only clearly worth pressing when (a) you're above ~57.4% avoidance and a crit would kill you, or (b) every other cooldown (HS/Judge/Piss/Exorcism) is on cooldown, SoR is already up, and the boss will live 30+ more seconds — [[d:paladin#1469040234657480744]] [[d:paladin#1469041605037129923]] [[d:paladin#1469041779029446762]] (2026-02-06). Holy Shield's block/reflect damage doesn't clearly use either the melee or spell hit/crit table — Grey reports it hasn't crit once across a full week of Naxx logs despite having ~12% spell crit and ~25% melee crit at the time — [[d:paladin#1469369412887773215]] [[d:paladin#1469370492501626941]] [[d:paladin#1469370966638329877]] (2026-02-06).
- **[all]** Weapon skill and taunt: taunt hit chance is already roughly 90-97% just from the Precision talent + normal gear at level 60 vs level-60 mobs, without dedicated Improved Taunt investment. Talent points into taunt reliability are considered a luxury most tanks skip in favor of armor talents, though it's "more useful during progression when fights last longer" (Grey); a counter-opinion holds taunt-hit chance is overrated in general, "tanks have been doing just fine for 20 years without it" (Destroses) — [[d:paladin#1469842723732193291]] [[d:paladin#1469842843668316351]] [[d:paladin#1469842986585292901]] [[d:paladin#1468693348226175212]] (2026-02-06/08).
