## Rotation: single target
- **[prot]** Opener: seal for judgement (Crusader/Wisdom/Light) → Judge + Repent on pull → reseal (to Righteousness) → Holy Strike → Consecration → Crusader Strike/Judgement/Consecration on cooldown — [[d:paladin#1474433644088000745]] (Beldrin).
- **[prot]** TOEP (trinket) should be used at the start of the pull to build a threat lead; can macro it right after Judge — [[d:paladin#1474054220494803171]], [[d:paladin#1474054737547497583]], [[d:paladin#1474055018142240932]] (Silverhand, Cobanana): full example sequence — pop Holy Shield, cast seal, run in, pop TOEP, Judge, Holy Strike, Consecrate.
- **[prot]** Righteousness (SoR) preferred seal while tanking (even pre-60) because it's still needed for Judgement threat, over Seal of Wisdom/Light — [[d:paladin#1475199805188997230]] (Jesse Potter); pre-60, Wisdom is "usually fine" while spamming Consecrate/Holy Shield since mana regen from Wisdom counts as threat on all mobs — [[d:paladin#1475207689314242591]] (Drekalo).
- **[prot]** Prot threat breakdown: Holy Strike hits 1300 dmg → ×1.25 → ×2.05 threat mult = 3331 threat; Crusader Strike hits 200 dmg with no multiplier = 200 threat; typically takes ~20s to build enough threat lead to drop Holy Strike spam — [[d:paladin#1473789581269926042]] (Grey, detailed raid-log-based numbers).
- **[prot]** Auto-attacks contribute roughly half of total prot threat output over a fight; factoring JoR as a byproduct of SoR, Seal of Righteousness (spell) is ~60-70% of total prot threat; SoR itself (excluding autos/JoR) is comparatively low, roughly a single-target Consecrate-equivalent (~350-400 TPS even with Desecration) — [[d:paladin#1475771297228259389]], [[d:paladin#1475771893339390013]] (Grey).
- **[prot]** Switching off Seal of Righteousness for any reason (even briefly) kills single-target threat: loses SoR itself, extra JoW procs, extra Windfury procs, and JoR — [[d:paladin#1475773657925161033]] (Grey).
- **[prot hit]** Below hit cap, missing entirely (not just being dodged/parried) is pure lost threat — recommend capping hit; can get away with 7% hit (instead of full 8%) in some gear situations — [[d:paladin#1474911869847212136]], [[d:paladin#1474912528503934976]], [[d:paladin#1474912611635036161]] (Jesse Potter, K24).
- **[prot hit-skill]** With Frey Island quest reward giving +1% hit per 5 skill, only need 4% hit from gear + 3% from talents; ret human can get to 11 skill (+3 talent +3 racial) needing only 3% gear, 3% talent, 2% skill — [[d:paladin#1475199315193626764]], [[d:paladin#1475199416179757340]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[ret]** Crusader Strike does not scale with Spell Power and is not amplified by Seal of the Crusader Judgement (JotC only affects Holy-school damage; Crusader Strike is Physical school) — [[d:paladin#1475161049971032115]], [[d:paladin#1475164635001917542]], [[d:paladin#1475164868171661494]] (Itadakimanko's question, confirmed by Silverhand/Aelgagar/gastro).
- **[SoR ret]** For SoR, weapon speed matters less because SoR damage scales roughly linearly with speed; the deciding factor is the proc that "scales the hardest," typically Lifedrain-type procs; Hand of Ragnaros (HoR) is more about abusing its high proc rate than raw scaling — [[d:paladin#1475267682629583030]], [[d:paladin#1475267827538727073]], [[d:paladin#1475268146700222726]] (dusty).
- **[ret weapon]** OEB (Obsidian Edged Blade?) 1-in-3 chance to proc SoC per auto vs Demonbane 1-in-2 chance; OEB gives near-zero glancing blow penalty due to weapon skill bonus (was a "huge dps increase" when tested); alternatively, sword-skill gloves/boots from MC remove glancing penalty as human without needing OEB — [[d:paladin#1475258604838850621]], [[d:paladin#1475256470860988657]], [[d:paladin#1475257387484057713]] (Jesse Potter, Nabwek, dusty).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[all]** Hit cap is 8% (5% base miss + 3% dual-wield-esque melee table, i.e. standard raid boss); with the Frey Island quest reward (+1% hit per 5 weapon skill) a tank effectively needs only 4% hit from gear + 3% from talents; human ret with +3 racial and +3 talent weapon skill can drop to needing just 3% gear/3% talent/2% skill — [[d:paladin#1475199315193626764]], [[d:paladin#1475199416179757340]] (Jesse Potter). Prot tanks can get away with 7% instead of full 8% in some setups since misses (unlike dodge/parry) are pure lost threat — [[d:paladin#1474912611635036161]].
- **[prot]** Avoidance math: 1 point of Defense = +0.16% total avoidance (0.04% each to dodge/parry/block/miss chance); the +5% parry talent (e.g. from Anticipation-line) is equivalent to ~31.25 Defense — [[d:paladin#1475190786919497889]], [[d:paladin#1475191446972661861]], [[d:paladin#1476130624635932702]] (Silverhand, orodoet). Below level 60, 5 Defense = 1 "level" of to-hit difficulty for the purposes of being attacked — [[d:paladin#1477031100365803757]] (Silverhand).
- **[prot]** Crush immunity / avoidance-cap target: 102.4% total avoidance (dodge+parry+block+miss) vs. level-63 raid bosses removes crushing blows entirely; 100% is achievable vs level 63 by combining base avoidance/defense with Holy Shield's +45% block chance; getting to ~55% avoidance pre-Holy-Shield is realistic with decent MC/BWL gear + buffs/consumables (e.g. Mongoose + Scorpok Assay + Druid's Mark of the Wild-style paw buff adds ~3% near-permanently) — [[d:paladin#1475189658274107545]], [[d:paladin#1475189955662844125]], [[d:paladin#1475190265361989846]], [[d:paladin#1475190308894670989]] (Silverhand/Drekalo).
- **[prot]** Crits are mutually exclusive with dodge/parry/block/miss in classic-era combat tables — reaching 100% avoidance vs. a level-63 boss makes you effectively crit-immune too (except against player "yellow"/special-attack tables, which use a separate esoteric table and where the traditional 440 Defense "crit immunity" cap still specifically matters — relevant mainly for PvP, not PvE) — [[d:paladin#1477032855212134550]], [[d:paladin#1477032907024498820]], [[d:paladin#1477032987508998145]], [[d:paladin#1477034299399077888]], [[d:paladin#1477034497693323505]] (Silverhand; disputed by Proph who thought WotLK/TBC required separate crush+crit caps, Silverhand says that's not how classic/Turtle works).
- **[prot]** Avoidance cap ("102.4%") is described by several as only really mattering from AQ40 onward; pre-raid and early raids you're better off stacking threat + stamina, since nothing hits hard enough pre-AQ40 to require full avoidance (exception: Twins fight, which can be handled with stamina/armor stacking instead) — [[d:paladin#1477378940875047116]], [[d:paladin#1477379438000734460]], [[d:paladin#1477379444510425251]], [[d:paladin#1477379757967544360]] (Mithreon, Seviry, angels1m).
- **[ret/general stat weights]** No fixed universal stat-weight sheet exists; general guidance: Tier + Spell Power accessories for SoR, Tier + Attack Power accessories for SoC; SoC values crit and haste, SoR values haste more than crit (but still likes some) — [[d:paladin#1477213232740438160]], [[d:paladin#1477213681883414668]] (Destroses; single source, "there are more intricacies" caveat given). Haste's stat-weight is highly item-dependent in one poster's spreadsheet model, ranging from a weight of 6 to 18 depending on the item's other stats/procs — [[d:paladin#1478254321924837517]], [[d:paladin#1478262896537505813]], [[d:paladin#1478268115694522468]] (Destroses; single source, no shared sheet).
- **[prot leveling]** General stat priority for a level-60 prot tank per one answer: Stam, Agi, Spell Power, Int (in that order) — [[d:paladin#1478222888153645197]] (gastro; single source, brief).
- **[SoR ret]** SoR damage scales roughly linearly with weapon speed, so a fast weapon isn't a DPS loss for the seal itself and is more reliable for threat/JoW procs; contrast with SoC which benefits from crit — [[d:paladin#1476230682962296843]], [[d:paladin#1476230783743168563]] (Silverhand).
- **[necrodin]** Weapon proc rates referenced: Flurry Axe 5.5% chance to proc; Thrash Blade 4.5%; Bonereaver's Edge (BRE) 2 procs-per-minute / 11.3% chance per hit — [[d:paladin#1476509434044878952]], [[d:paladin#1476726212910125156]] (Destroses, "30").
- **[necrodin hit]** ~7% hit + 308 weapon skill mentioned as adequate hit setup for a necrodin ret build — [[d:paladin#1476662390954262731]] (Azymuth's question, unanswered directly but implied acceptable).

- **[necrodin]** Full necrodin build requires BOTH Ret T2 3-piece and Prot T2 3-piece (their spellstrike-triggered proc effects are separate instances and stack, doubling ghost/proc consistency but with a shared 3s ICD per source) — [[d:paladin#1477147156996685856]], [[d:paladin#1477147218707349514]], [[d:paladin#1478658607439548478]] (Destroses, dusty, Grey). Sunken Temple (ST) robe (rare drop from the Avatar of Hakkar summon, ~2% drop rate) is a core damage/raid-utility piece for necrodin: applies an armor-reduction debuff (~400 armor) and a ~5% damage-taken-increase debuff to the boss, contributing significant raid DPS beyond its own tooltip — [[d:paladin#1474118846385492010]], [[d:paladin#1476513381942034495]], [[d:paladin#1476513592953278638]] (multiple sources agree it's worth the farm). Best necrodin weapons: Thunderfury (TF) best if available, otherwise Flurry Axe; a slow "extra attack" weapon like Ironfoe is the only slow weapon that performs well (otherwise fast weapons preferred to maximize T2 3p procs per second) — [[d:paladin#1476522904807280821]], [[d:paladin#1476525636091777128]], [[d:paladin#1476511756187730033]], [[d:paladin#1476511835950940281]].
- **[weapons]** Desecration (new K40/BWL-tier weapon) vs Thunderfury for necrodin/SoR: Desecration's raw stats and consistent proc carry it well even in the necrodin build; swap to Desecration for single-target/opener threat, TF for AoE/trash — [[d:paladin#1476534293366050927]], [[d:paladin#1476535218159751281]], [[d:paladin#1475412850636750909]], [[d:paladin#1475410024749863047]] (Adamant, Destroses, Mithreon, Jesse Potter).
- **[ret pre-raid]** Full T1 + Flurry Axe + Skullflame Shield cited as ret pre-raid BiS weapon/set combo — [[d:paladin#1478461319333155069]] (gastro). Sulfuron Hammer (MC) and Untamed Blade (UTB, AQ40) are the main 2H options for SoC/AP ret pre-Naxx; UTB gives ~300 Str, Sulfuron/Hand of Ragnaros (HoR) roughly equal to Ashbringer for SoC due to similar total damage despite lower proc uptime — [[d:paladin#1475082486210887814]], [[d:paladin#1475272762552619109]], [[d:paladin#1475273287662567585]]. AQ40 trash-drop axe and Kara10 mace/sword (e.g. Moroes' sword) also mentioned as accessible 2H/weapon alternatives before UTB — [[d:paladin#1478410029211058290]], [[d:paladin#1476511311989833891]].
- **[SoR ret]** Progression per multiple posters: Soulforge 4-piece → T2.5 5-piece + T2 3-piece (with Sigil of Ancient Accord from MC) → add Ashbringer; T2.5 5/5 is a clear upgrade over 4/8 Soulforge once available, though losing T2 hit/spellstrike pieces (helm/belt/legs) needs compensating gear (e.g. Embergem Cuffs instead of Gilneas ring for hit) — [[d:paladin#1478181884960706630]], [[d:paladin#1478189988729454593]], [[d:paladin#1478193184235327600]], [[d:paladin#1478193240028217465]] (Illithris, Beldrin: ~32.7% crit raid-buffed achievable with agi enchants on blanks). AQ20 (Heart of Dreams "HoD" guaranteed drop, cloak+ring set) considered a strong, cheap (~180g) upgrade path pairing with Wrath of Cenarius (WoC) — [[d:paladin#1478193612473761953]], [[d:paladin#1478193693352530081]], [[d:paladin#1478193784163668129]].
- **[SoR ret]** Full BiS-ish target from T2.5/T2 3p base heading into Naxx: T2 3-piece Ret + Sigil (from MC) is the highest-value remaining item; "The Lost" trinket/set generally not worth full itemizing around unless min-maxing hard (needs both T2 3p's) — [[d:paladin#1475638313808302190]], [[d:paladin#1475645038980497701]], [[d:paladin#1475656478760112391]] (Nesushka, Destroses, Silverhand).
- **[T1/T2 loot]** Only Prot spec truly wants full T1 8/8; Ret and Holy don't need full T1 — [[d:paladin#1475521319968706701]] (dusty). T2 legs/belt/bracers/gloves widely reported as very rare drops for paladin/shaman specifically (see Bugs & quirks section for the loot-table mechanic).
- **[T2.5 vs T3]** Whether T2.5 or T3 (prot) is better depends on raid DPS output — below ~1.5k raid DPS you don't benefit much from T3 over T2.5 — [[d:paladin#1478439039437766742]] (Grey).
- **[prot pre-raid]** Pre-Naxx prot target per Mithreon: 4-piece T0.5, 4% hit from gear (rest from talents/skill), 0-1 spellstrike sources, remaining stats into stam/crit/sp/agi; newly-geared tanks are unlikely to maintank anything and mark-tanking doesn't need much mitigation in MC/BWL — [[d:paladin#1475129511543701658]], [[d:paladin#1475129684189516000]], [[d:paladin#1475130867377508507]] (Arle agreeing: focus threat first, survivability as needed).
- **[prot gear — ES raid]** Emerald Sanctum (ES) gear notes for prot (raid opened on Ambershire ahead of schedule): Choker of the Emerald Lord is BiS neck until K40; Ring of Nordrassil is "the best mitigation ring in the game by a wide margin"; an ES helmet is called the single highest-mitigation armor piece in the game; a 2% block mace is good for niche AoE farms; the Jadestone Shield is BiS until AQ40; the Scaleshield is BiS forever specifically for Nefarian and Solnius Hard Mode (pop it on opener to avoid being slept if the boss is tanked in range); an ES libram is BiS on "Knight," Mephistroth, and other bosses where minimizing crush/crit from stray parry-haste matters; Lasher's Whip and a sleeper's ring are good for tanking "Erennius" — [[d:paladin#1478436514147991813]] (Grey, detailed single post, well-reasoned). Choker of the Emerald Lord and other rings compete with Ring of Nordrassil depending on target damage/block value — [[d:paladin#1478437390954528778]].
- **[prot gear]** OBT trinket (Ony reward, alternative to an SP ring) is "the third highest avoidance trinket in the game," good in full-mitigation sets until K40, and BiS forever specifically for tanking Garr's adds; full-mitigation sets are otherwise mostly situational until late AQ40 — [[d:paladin#1478528792799744112]] (Grey).
- **[trinket design]** Active trinkets (ToEP, ZHC, DIE, Essence, etc.) are best for short fights/openers/trash and stack extremely well with each other and with class cooldowns (esp. Fury Warrior burst windows); passive trinkets generally win for long fights/sustained raid performance unless you can guarantee near-100% uptime management of actives — [[d:paladin#1478426633932116099]], [[d:paladin#1478428052110442649]], [[d:paladin#1478429166410928381]], [[d:paladin#1478431914867036193]] (Grey, Silverhand, detailed consensus). ToEP should be popped at pull start for prot to build an early threat lead, and can be macro'd into the opener — [[d:paladin#1474054220494803171]], [[d:paladin#1474054737547497583]] (Silverhand, Cobanana).
- **[necklace/rep gear]** Leveling/early gear picks: Triune Amulet, Necklace of Calisea (BoE), Taskmaster's Tag (Hateforge Quarry) → Talisman of the Dreamshaper (Hyjal quest) → Shard of Eternity (CoT exalted) is a recommended progression (see Leveling section for full list).
- **[weapon: prot]** For prot weapon choice, spell-power casting weapons (e.g. Dream's Herald) are generally preferred over pure tank-stat weapons since prot threat is "basically 90% spellcaster"; testing found Dream's Herald outperforms weapons under ~40 SP for threat purposes — [[d:paladin#1475773845964066847]], [[d:paladin#1477306468494475325]] (Grey; Purge's informal test).

- **[all]** Rugged Armor Kit (gloves/boots/chest/legs) called strong and underused for leveling; bracers +9 stam enchant (illusion dust) gives ~300 extra HP for cheap when not inflated by speculators — [[d:paladin#1473792640939200827]] (Grey).
- **[ret]** Onyxia pendant enchant choice: 3-stat (crit+stam) if you want survivability/crit, or +6 str if pure DPS-focused — [[d:paladin#1474133037535989791]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[ret]** Head/legs enchant debate: +13 SP vs +1% haste — SP has constant impact regardless of fight length, haste's value depends on fight duration; some (dusty) prefer haste for "haste stacking" playstyle while conceding SP is arguably objectively better — [[d:paladin#1476021915071021211]], [[d:paladin#1476022894139281579]], [[d:paladin#1476023133294039152]].
- **[prot weapon]** SP enchant is the best all-rounder for prot paladin weapon enchant; agi considered for Holy-Strike-crit-focused approaches but SP wins broadly — [[d:paladin#1474930627748040814]] (Iseut).
- **[oils/stones]** Wizard Oil vs Brilliant Wizard Oil (+2% crit) debated for prot: the 2% crit mainly benefits Exorcism/Holy Wrath; some prefer running an Elemental/Consecrated Sharpening Stone for consecration/threat value instead since crit gives diminishing returns for prot the more it's stacked — [[d:paladin#1477339576530899064]], [[d:paladin#1477340137426911294]], [[d:paladin#1477340264921301134]] (Dinousek, Mithreon; unresolved/contested).
- **[cheap leveling enchants]** Dragonmaw armor kits (+5 SP, ~5g) as a budget alternative to full Enchanted Armor Kits (~30g) for pre-raid gear — [[d:paladin#1476539427575824496]] (Wilhelm).
- **[bug/interaction]** T2 3-piece spellstrike is coded as a full "spell" (unlike Judgement/Strikes), making it the only spellstrike source that can trigger Sigil of Ancient Accord — widely called an unintentional-but-kept "bug that's basically a feature" — [[d:paladin#1476064445032824925]], [[d:paladin#1475110066045718599]] (dusty, Pastor).
- **[necro/spellstrike gear]** Multiple spellstrike-item slots exist for stacking Judgement of Wisdom (JoW) procs: 1H weapon (e.g. Fiery Retributor), offhand, rings (Storm Gauntlets / Ring of Electrical Bindings — these DO stack with each other, giving two separate spellstrike triggers, though only one source is "needed"), Kazzak neck, gloves, Incendosaur boots/shoulders — [[d:paladin#1478699098411368497]], [[d:paladin#1478699283648610395]], [[d:paladin#1478701594941063291]] (Nazeth, Destroses, keungzhe).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[prot]** TOEP (The Eye of Peroth'arn or similar trinket) opener sequence: pop Holy Shield, cast seal, run in, pop TOEP, Judge, Holy Strike, Consecration — front-loads a threat lead — [[d:paladin#1474054737547497583]] (Cobanana/Silverhand).
- **[prot]** Auras: rule of thumb — use Sanctity Aura when your Holy Strike/Judgement threat matters (i.e. still building/holding threat); switch to Retribution Aura when Holy Strike threat is no longer relevant and you're just camping Seal of Wisdom for mana, since Ret Aura's damage-per-hit scales up with mob count just as Consecration does — [[d:paladin#1475970308929487029]], [[d:paladin#1475969905588437022]] (Grey, Silverhand).
- **[general]** Judgement of the Crusader (JotC) uptime problems: casting JotC requires reseal-to-SoR + Judge + reseal-back, effectively costing 2 GCDs even though Judgement itself is "off the GCD" in name; multiple posters (dusty especially) report chronic difficulty getting other paladins in a raid to reliably keep Judgement of Wisdom (JoW) up, forcing self-sacrifice of JotC uptime to cover the mana problem — [[d:paladin#1476050559831244840]], [[d:paladin#1476050680707026955]], [[d:paladin#1476053305292488734]] (dusty, Silverhand; recurring frustration, not really "resolved," more a raid-organization problem).
- **[general]** Seals always cost a GCD to apply/reseal in Turtle/Classic (this changed only in later expansions where Judging no longer consumes/requires reseal) — [[d:paladin#1476051035058606110]], [[d:paladin#1476051132290695328]] (dusty).
- **[SoC mechanic]** Seal of Command's swing itself gets delayed by ~0.5s (different animation queued once a proc is determined) but the SoC damage/buff portion does not carry the old delay bug that used to exist (that delay-on-buff bug was "accidentally" removed); this interaction is cited as a reason SoR beats SoC when Windfury Totem is present — [[d:paladin#1477290795047583837]], [[d:paladin#1477291113428815983]], [[d:paladin#1477292686980681932]], [[d:paladin#1477292911279476830]] (Jesse Potter, Mithreon).
- **[SoC math]** SoR gets its proc-eligible "hit" every swing (~effectively every ~37% of a swing-cycle accounting for speed), SoC roughly every other swing but can crit; SoR ≈ 12.4% (of weapon dmg?) scaling per swing vs SoC ≈ 24% about every other swing, plus SoC scales with AP through weapon damage — [[d:paladin#1477523024499576994]], [[d:paladin#1477523143206895669]], [[d:paladin#1477523532547231966]] (Destroses; single detailed source, speculative pending 1.18.1 SoC buff).
- **[macro/mechanic]** Libram swapping will force a GCD in 1.18.1 (see 1.18.1 changes section); "Fervor at pull, then Final Judgement for the rest of the fight" is the recommended simplified approach once swap-spam is no longer viable — [[d:paladin#1475911623125368875]] (dusty).
- **[Hand of Sacrifice]** Widely regarded as a near-dead button post-nerf (used to reduce target's damage taken by 12%/give threat utility; now largely useless outside niche add-tanking situations like sharing Garr-adds damage, worth ~5000 damage total split across a fight) — [[d:paladin#1475239829116682330]], [[d:paladin#1476371962208190645]], [[d:paladin#1476372008219705535]] (Jesse Potter, Silverhand).
- **[threat]** Prot paladin's biggest tools beyond SoR/JoR/autos are Consecration (AoE) and Holy Shield procs; Thunderfury's chance-on-hit is "basically a 0.8x multiplier to holy shield TPS" — [[d:paladin#1475775320744525855]] (Grey).

## Tanking specifics
- **[prot]** Standard prot rotation/opener already covered in Rotation section (seal→judge→repent→reseal→HS→consec→CS/judge/consec on CD).
- **[prot]** Auto attacks provide ~50% of total threat output over a fight; factoring JoR as a byproduct of SoR, the Seal of Righteousness "spell" line accounts for roughly 60-70% of total prot threat — [[d:paladin#1475771297228259389]] (Grey; see Rotation: single target).
- **[prot]** Prot considered "basically 90% spellcaster" for threat purposes (dropping to ~85% post 1.18.1 Holy Strike buffs) — spell power gear (rings/trinkets/neck/cape) wanted 99% of the time over pure mitigation gear except in pug/undergeared healer situations — [[d:paladin#1475773845964066847]], [[d:paladin#1475774204723855433]], [[d:paladin#1475774612876038206]], [[d:paladin#1475774889003581461]] (Grey, Pastor).
- **[prot]** Consecration is central to holding AoE aggro; one Consecration cast is enough to hold all of Ragnaros's adds ("that's all you get") — [[d:paladin#1474897368888639630]] (Beldrin).
- **[prot]** Widely regarded as one of the best AoE tanks / capable MT for essentially any content; "dooming" about prot viability repeatedly pushed back on by multiple posters — prot can top damage meters using Consecration alone, and struggling to hold threat in a low-level dungeon is a player/gear issue, not a class issue — [[d:paladin#1476661204196917288]], [[d:paladin#1476661682905420031]], [[d:paladin#1476669710132052008]] (gastro, several agreeing); minority view: prot paladin is "the worst tank in raid" comparatively, but "not a bad tank by any stretch" — [[d:paladin#1476661851017318431]] (Silverhand).
- **[prot]** Standard tank weapon debate: SP-based weapons (Dream's Herald, etc.) generally beat tank-stat weapons for threat once above a certain SP breakpoint (informally tested: beats weapons under ~40 SP) — see Gear section. Using Quel'Serrar (weapon-swap on the proc, then swap back to threat weapon) as part of a full-mitigation setup was mentioned as a "noticeable difference" claim by a third party but not verified/confirmed by anyone in the thread — [[d:paladin#1476498946074607832]] (gastro relaying, unconfirmed; Contested).
- **[prot]** Hit-cap for holding threat: missing (not dodge/parry, but a pure miss) is 0 threat from Holy Strike/Judgement, so hit is prioritized; 7% is an acceptable floor instead of the full 8% in some gear situations (see Stats section).
- **[prot]** Threat/DPS complaint about needing a range pull: no native ranged pull beyond ~10 yards (melee range); the workaround item https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=51778 is repeatedly recommended by Jesse Potter as the answer to "prot has no range pull," with the caveat it's on a ~60s cooldown — [[d:paladin#1475240862064578602]], [[d:paladin#1475240963252293813]], [[d:paladin#1475241087076405523]].
- **[prot]** Defense/threat priority pre-raid vs endgame: pre-raid, focus stam+threat over avoidance-cap since nothing hits hard enough to require it; avoidance cap starts mattering around AQ40 (see Stats section for the caveats and Twins-fight exception).
- **[prot leveling]** Prot leveling AoE strength unlocks meaningfully with Holy Shield and mana sustain via Seal of Wisdom; before that, avoid enemies with spell-casting (a mob that's casting on you isn't hitting you, so it isn't feeding your Shield Spike/Blessing of Sanctuary/Ret Aura threat-return, and self-healing mobs can't be interrupted by melee-based threat sources) — [[d:paladin#1478149717362741258]], [[d:paladin#1478149913039732799]], [[d:paladin#1478149961593000162]] (Beldrin).
- **[prot leveling]** Prot vs Ret leveling speed roughly equal for single-mob questing, but prot kills 3 mobs 1-vs-3 in about the same time Ret kills them 1-vs-1-vs-1 (i.e. prot AoEs, ret doesn't) — [[d:paladin#1477708470852780243]] (Grey).
- **[MT/OT]** MT paladin buff-blocking: with a bis-Kara buff situation and healers over-buffing, most paladin self-buffs except Blessing of Might/Blessing of Kings should be blocked from the buff bar to conserve slots — [[d:paladin#1476488666867040379]], [[d:paladin#1476488353955319942]] (Mithreon; single source, informal).

## Healing specifics
- **[holy]** Holy Strike is central to Holy Paladin's kit even in melee range: ignores armor, refunds its own mana cost, 6s cooldown, purely spell-school damage (works fine even with a bad weapon); makes prot/any-spec leveling comfortable since even a bad weapon doesn't gate your damage — [[d:paladin#1476795023243608205]] (Grey).
- **[holy]** "Melee holy" build: spam Holy Strike in melee range for a large amount of incidental AoE healing (via Illumination/heal-on-hit or the strike's own heal component), while skipping talents like Toughness/Improved Concentration Aura/Unyielding Faith — practiced with 4/4 Red Dragonscale set bonus for a heal-over-time proc; described by multiple posters as legitimately strong, not a meme — [[d:paladin#1476582585822871582]], [[d:paladin#1476610461678964786]] (error's build description; Numi: "spamming holy strike in melee in raids gives a ton of healing actually").
- **[holy]** Flash of Light (FoL) is extremely mana-efficient at baseline — a 0-talent-invested vanilla paladin can theoretically infinite-cast rank-3 FoL using only Mana Potions/Dark Runes; critical-strike mana refunds (via Illumination) just increase the rank of FoL sustainable, they are not the sole reason infinite-casting works — [[d:paladin#1476289419341467819]] (Grey, correcting a misconception that crit-refund alone explains classic-era "infinite healing" reputation).
- **[holy]** Judgement of Light (JoL) uptime is frequently poor in practice when left to Ret/Prot to maintain — with only a Holy paladin in a 3-paladin raid, JoL was reported up only ~10% of the time — [[d:paladin#1476362324657967246]] (Silverhand).
- **[holy population]** Rough population/representation snapshot from Naxx effective-HPS/DPS logs since 1.18: ~200 distinct Holy paladins logging, versus ~60 (by dps) and ~160 Ret; i.e. roughly 50% Holy / 35% Ret / 15% Prot split among logged paladins — [[d:paladin#1476361313478377624]], [[d:paladin#1476361577795027015]] (Mithreon; single-source, self-reported log read).
- **[bug]** Mending Light's effective range is bugged/inconsistent — testing with a distance-display addon found the real cutoff around 7.6-7.62 yards from the paladin, not the tooltip's stated 10 yards; confirmed present for at least ~1 year without being fixed — [[d:paladin#1478326501693853727]], [[d:paladin#1478361089740902608]], [[d:paladin#1478363418548502661]] (McSavage, tested with a third-party range-display addon; Grey/Mithreon confirm long-standing).
- **[holy PvE gear]** Daybreak (holy spell-crit-related mechanic/item) is getting nerfed soon per patch notes, but spell crit is expected to "remain king" for holy itemization; rough conversion given: 1 spell crit ≈ 25 healing — [[d:paladin#1476195785891909662]] (Stormblesst/odnetniN).
- **[holy macros]** Mouseover Holy Shock macro is tricky under the client's macro/casting engine (pfUI's /pfcast doesn't handle the mouseover-heal-while-hostile-targeted case); a working macro was shared: `/run i=(GetMouseFocus().unit) if i then ClearTarget() CastSpellByName("Holy Shock") SpellTargetUnit(i) TargetLastTarget() else CastSpellByName("Holy Shock") end` — tested working when mousing over a portrait solo, untested in full group — [[d:paladin#1477771144802078720]] (Sidiros).

## PvP
- **[general]** Ret in PvP considered a "half-value" slot: paladin's PvP niche is utility (buffs/blessings/cleanses), but Holy provides the same utility AND can heal, and Prot provides the same utility AND is much tankier — meaning a Ret PvP player provides only half the value of playing another spec/class — [[d:paladin#1476365378287308830]], [[d:paladin#1476365487402254376]] (Silverhand); similar argument made for Ret's PvE "group support" balance identity being redundant with Holy — [[d:paladin#1476365700757983425]] (dusty).
- **[ret PvP]** For Seal choice: Seal of Command (burst-oriented) is generally preferred in PvP because fights don't last long enough for Judgement of the Crusader to matter; people taking Crusader-line points should reportedly take Parry instead — [[d:paladin#1477090352065151047]] (dusty, responding to Starkss's SoC/SoC-vs-crusader question) [[d:paladin#1477089994010132642]]. If using a 1H+shield vs. a warrior (esp. a "blood-drinker"-style build), use Judgement of Light (JoL) instead — [[d:paladin#1477091325848322263]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[PvP vs hunters]** Recommended counters: put on a shield and use line-of-sight (LoS) to break their damage; use a Swiftness Potion to close/disengage; keep Frost Oil on weapon for a near-guaranteed slow proc; farm the net-trinket from Hateforge Quarry (HFQ) and swap to it out of combat/LoS as a burst-disable; alternatively kill the hunter's pet (most hunters disengage/flee once their pet dies) — [[d:paladin#1477997240814534807]], [[d:paladin#1478005878736294043]], [[d:paladin#1478006678690599055]], [[d:paladin#1478007043435663440]], [[d:paladin#1478007087891218495]] (Jesse Potter, TRUE HEIR, Light).
- **[itemization]** New PvP-only ilvl-92 gear previewed for 1.18.1, gated behind rank/Conquest currency (a new weekly-capped, non-decaying honor-like currency from BGs and world-PvP objectives); community expects PvE stat budget to be diluted by PvP stats (stam/"the new avoidance stat"/damage-reduction vs other players) so it should land "slightly above BWL, below AQ/Naxx" in raw PvE power and not be strictly best-in-slot for raiding, intentionally to avoid forcing PvE raiders into PvP — [[d:paladin#1477827608992284742]] (forum link shared), [[d:paladin#1477853543359578213]] (K24, single detailed but speculative post; genuinely Contested/unconfirmed until release).
- **[avoidance/defense]** For general PvP, hit% matters ("it's good if you wanna land your attacks on your enemies") but a dedicated defense-cap investment is not emphasized the way it is for PvE tanking — [[d:paladin#1478191558766493696]], [[d:paladin#1478191654077988915]] (Starkss, Beldrin; light coverage).
- **[19 twink]** No Seal of Command available at level 19 (too low level) — Rock-solid vanilla-era twink builds rely on Righteousness/Wisdom and juju consumables (Flurry, Quickness, Perception) instead — [[d:paladin#1477547358555213914]], [[d:paladin#1477549409188446270]] (Jesse Potter).
