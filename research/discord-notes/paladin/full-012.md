## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Prot]** Holy Shield is being changed to scale from 100% of block value (previously partial); Lhian says this may force removing it or nerfing it massively since it became too strong, which would be "disappointing" — [[d:paladin#1454652176109666478]], confirmed by Lhian himself, 2025-12-28.
- **[General]** Consecration is confirmed getting frontloaded damage in 1.18.1 ("we got confirmation that Cons is getting frontload") — [[d:paladin#1454535833032982559]] (Mithreon, 2025-12-27).
- **[General]** New Librams incoming: a Consecration libram and a "Libram of Command" were teased/leaked by Lhian; SoC and Consecration are getting new "dps librams" — [[d:paladin#1454936505733611530]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1454656009598533685]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1454099213821542512]] (Lhian teasing, 2025-12-26).
- **[General]** Dev-stated current itemization vision (not final, per Destroses): Seal of Righteousness = SP scaling, Seal of Command = AP scaling, Seal of the Crusader = proc-focused; SotC's Judgement effect not being deleted despite suggestions — [[d:paladin#1454051244564811796]] (Destroses, 2025-12-25).
- **[Prot]** Class changes for 1.18.1 are "mostly finalized, still tuning left" per Lhian; a 2h-tanking-related mechanic/talent is coming ("something for enjoyers of it") — [[d:paladin#1454080985883738185]], [[d:paladin#1454081307536658472]] (Lhian, 2025-12-26).
- **[General]** Libram swapping mid-combat: Lhian says swapping is "fine" in principle but should incur a penalty since the GCD it currently costs is trivially avoidable, meaning players will always default to the best libram — [[d:paladin#1454096311405248643]] (Lhian, 2025-12-26).
- **[Dev process]** Lhian: balance patches happen only 0–3x/year because spell-related code changes need a dedicated branch (25-year-old client/server codebase), so scaling ends up "too swingy" instead of incremental, and any overtuned result is on him — [[d:paladin#1455211014793465902]], [[d:paladin#1455211402926100571]] (Lhian, 2025-12-29).
- **[Prot]** Devs are aware Prot Paladin is functionally "a spellret with holy shield equipped" (same spells as spellret ~90%); Lhian's only stated constraint is balancing so "40 prot pallies" doesn't become the raid-comp meta — [[d:paladin#1455212143971664078]], [[d:paladin#1455212411282915450]] (2025-12-29).
- **[Contested]** Community frequently proposes prot-specific damage/threat identity fixes (holy-cleave on Holy Strike/Holy Shield, a unique prot-only damaging ability, Repentance dealing upfront damage) — repeated across many messages, no dev commitment given — [[d:paladin#1453556177878122496]], [[d:paladin#1454027923815399457]], [[d:paladin#1455205804847267952]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Ret]** Ret Paladin is viable raid DPS but consumable-dependent ("ull be spending g on consumes though") — [[d:paladin#1455153340198555752]] (Destroses).
- **[Ret]** SP-based Ret ("SoR/spellret") is regarded as generally better/more consistent than AP Ret currently — [[d:paladin#1455419722668441610]] (Beldrin); Jesse Potter's own naxx damage-meter screenshot shows him mid-pack among DPS, used as evidence AP ret is competitive but not top — [[d:paladin#1455419995520499935]].
- **[Prot]** Prot Paladin threat/tanking is considered strong while content is capped at MC/K10-era gear, but scaling is poor at higher gear levels: "naked prot pally and T3.5 prot pally are barely different" until the 1.18.1 patch — [[d:paladin#1454645125547556997]], [[d:paladin#1454645357341311150]] (Silverhand).
- **[Prot]** At full BIS (T2.5, no trinkets) a prot paladin only reaches ~832 SP, meaning gear gains barely double Holy Strike damage over baseline (800 SP needed to double it) — "so effectively you get same dmg from all ur bis items as u get baseline" — [[d:paladin#1454646881610367192]], [[d:paladin#1454647878621462569]] (angels1m).
- **[Healing tier list]** Community-stated tier for endgame healers: 5 resto shamans, 1 druid, 2 priests, 2 holy paladins is considered the "meta" comp — [[d:paladin#1454859580482785453]] (angels1m); resto druid rated "top 1 healer on Amber" by hps parses, though angels1m disputes hps as a meaningful metric (mentions farming Anomalus/Tranquility inflating numbers, pet-healing penalty distortions) — [[d:paladin#1454863349090357481]], [[d:paladin#1454864658652725441]], disputed.
- **[Group comp]** Paladins are considered a "wasted buff slot" if two are kept in the same group; standard practice is one Holy in tank group (Sanctity Aura) and Ret in DPS group, or finding a non-paladin "sanctity slave" — [[d:paladin#1453858574978650248]], [[d:paladin#1453859979626025031]], [[d:paladin#1453868122187632900]].
- **[Contested]** Whether Ret Paladins should spend the mandatory 11 points in Holy for Sanctity Aura (if no other Sanctity source) vs whether that's crippling class design — ongoing debate, no resolution — [[d:paladin#1453857425663852675]] through [[d:paladin#1453876181278527640]].
- **[Prot]** Holy/Prot hybrid tank builds (sacrificing DPS/threat for self-healing) are viable only for trash/off-tanking, not boss tanking: "if you're tanking bosses there's no point in a hybrid healer build" — [[d:paladin#1454134754701213800]] (Dodging); a full Holy paladin cleared an entire MC as tank once but threat "sucked" — [[d:paladin#1454133739566272542]] (Grey).

## Leveling
- **[Ret leveling]** Priority is SoC ASAP, and 5/5 Parry preferred over 2/2 Improved Judgement early because it's cheaper (mana-wise) than spending on rejudging and parry chance scaling 1-in-20 to 1-in-10 matters a lot with parry haste — [[d:paladin#1453603982999158896]], [[d:paladin#1453604136087060634]], [[d:paladin#1453604234028253349]], [[d:paladin#1453604366937227328]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Ret leveling]** Skipping SoC and putting 11 points in Holy tree while leveling is explicitly called "not a leveling build" — [[d:paladin#1453604949928706181]] (Dodging).
- **[Prot leveling]** Common leveling advice: take Holy Shield, Reckoning, then go into the Holy tree (Arle); most players don't get further than Holy Shield when leveling prot — [[d:paladin#1455329500698247371]], [[d:paladin#1455333681282421011]].
- **[Prot leveling]** Around level 30-40, gearing focus should be stam/int/sp; specific quest call-outs: Missing Diplomat quest gives an SP ring; a Swamp of Sorrows questline gives a plate (not cloth) SP shoulder item usable ~level 40 — [[d:paladin#1453506787562094808]], [[d:paladin#1453509162863431948]], [[d:paladin#1453819951885582510]], [[d:paladin#1453820450374287595]].
- **[Prot leveling]** Grinding spot for level ~31 Alliance Prot Paladin: worgen cave/camp in Duskwood (two spawn areas, cave vs. camp, for crowding) — [[d:paladin#1453781055977820210]], [[d:paladin#1453781476138029219]], [[d:paladin#1453781652156321833]].
- **[Holy leveling]** Going Holy while leveling solo is "bait" unless you have a permanent duo partner — [[d:paladin#1455241625717706865]] (Beldrin); Holy leveling class questions repeatedly discouraged/unanswered.
- **[Leveling dungeons boosting route]** Level-banded dungeon progression cited for boosting/twinking: Deadmines to 20 → Stockades to 30 → RFK/RFD to 40 → Gilneas City/ZF to 47 → Hateforge Quarry 50-55 → Stratholme 55-60; ZF is considered bad; Hateforge preferred to BRD/LBRS for boosting because more mobs can be grouped safely — [[d:paladin#1454262759620608184]], [[d:paladin#1454262475909369888]], [[d:paladin#1454262042247823451]], [[d:paladin#1454262115476045986]].
- **[Boosting]** For boosting, Paladins go Holy for threat so friends can tag mobs without pulling aggro; Consecration is preferred over grenades since grenades can be resisted — [[d:paladin#1454262412940148838]], [[d:paladin#1454262931469635707]], [[d:paladin#1454263186177003630]].
- **[Enchant]** Lifestealing on early leveling weapon (e.g., Knightly Longsword) suggested to "last a long time" — [[d:paladin#1453510033366061240]] (INFECTIOUS asking, unanswered directly / single source).
- **[Leveling rotation, basic]** For a very early leveling rotation (asked at level 13): "activate Seal of Righteousness, press Holy Strike, wait 6 seconds, press Holy Strike... can judge righteousness for more damage at the cost of more mana spent" — [[d:paladin#1456455484881113212]], [[d:paladin#1456455594545516575]] (Grey).
- **[Leveling gear]** No single leveling gear checklist is really necessary ("stam > everything else" while leveling, not worth optimizing); notable go-out-of-your-way pickups: the Gilneas City ring (called "op"), the Gilneas mace for Prot, and plate shoulders with +holy damage from a Swamp of Sorrows quest chain leading into Scarlet Monastery (plate with holy damage is rare); double Golden Goblet trinkets available around level 31 are "overstatted"; Blood-Etched trinkets (level 38, from AH or Jewelcrafting) are not considered better than the Golden Goblet — [[d:paladin#1455719079875969065]], [[d:paladin#1455719210922803322]], [[d:paladin#1455719252958122096]], [[d:paladin#1455719415990587463]], [[d:paladin#1455719542427750650]], [[d:paladin#1455719867779911742]], [[d:paladin#1455719940542697711]], [[d:paladin#1455720411147931819]] (Silverhand/Seviry).
- **[Leveling gear, low level]** At low character level, Head/Shoulder/Neck slots are "special" and have very little armor available in starting-zone cities like Stormwind, so it's normal to find nothing there for those slots — [[d:paladin#1455881466797101076]] (Dodging).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Prot]** Standard prot ST rotation cited: "judge > seal > cs > repent > consecration > repeat" (Beldrin) — [[d:paladin#1454124966768279694]].
- **[Prot]** Crusader Strike use by Prot is broadly considered a mistake/threat risk: haste isn't valuable for prot (parry haste + reckoning already provide swing procs; Shield Block Value scales poorly), and CS costs much more mana than Holy Strike, undermining sustain; only situational uses are on magic-immune targets or a dying add — [[d:paladin#1453812827033698365]], [[d:paladin#1453813546415685836]], [[d:paladin#1453813622294974566]], [[d:paladin#1453813885437214942]], [[d:paladin#1453814187590684833]] (Beldrin/Silverhand consensus). On low-threat Ambershire-tier content (MC/K10) some prots get away with CS spam but it's not recommended going forward — [[d:paladin#1453814369157779578]], [[d:paladin#1453814404943581275]].
- **[Ret/SoC]** SoC damage is calculated before haste is applied, so haste still benefits SoC ret damage output — [[d:paladin#1455414691500134452]] (Destroses).
- **[Ret]** For leveling/low-gear AP Ret DPS macro question: "just do holy strike every 18 seconds if you always want it up" to maintain Holy Strength/Crusader Strike zeal stacks — [[d:paladin#1453729497466798265]] (Olivaar of Moments).
- **[Holy healer melee-weaving]** Holy Paladins are expected to melee-weave between heals in raids: "when shit hits the fan it's FoL/HL/judge for HL/HS" and consecration is pressed "unless things are chill" — [[d:paladin#1454173630698225917]], [[d:paladin#1454173696708317276]] (Mima).
- **[Holy]** Crusader Strike judged onto a target is a healing loss for a Holy paladin — "nah dont crusader if u dont need to, it's a healing loss" — [[d:paladin#1454174059683250278]] (Domy); most groups prefer light judged for buffs, not crusader.
- **[Ret SoC opener]** Jesse Potter's (top-server SoC ret) detailed opener/rotation: "Repent, SoTC, JoTC at the start > SoC Apply > CS > Consecration > CS > Pop perception, Juju and trinket > Holy Strike > Judge on CD, Consecration on CD, Exo on cd vs UD, CS on CD until the third, then do Holy Strike in a pattern"; he deliberately delays popping Kiss/Juju/trinkets a few globals in (not first-GCD) to line them up with max haste/Vengeance/sunders/IEA already applied, similar to a Warrior saving Death Wish — [[d:paladin#1455491776248025123]], [[d:paladin#1455493072661250152]], [[d:paladin#1455493145276977258]].
- **[Ret SoR vs SoC damage]** Jesse Potter (SoC main) reports his own autoattacks do roughly 3x the damage of his Seal of Command hits, and that SoR out-damages SoC overall because "all abilities use SP Coeff, so overall you do more damage because everything does more damage," whereas SoC/AP investment mainly boosts autos (200-300k more autodamage over a fight) while every other ability does a collective ~400k less; SoC BiS ends up around 400 SP vs SoR BiS around 700-800 SP — [[d:paladin#1456523396409196658]], [[d:paladin#1456525622548496384]], [[d:paladin#1456525897019560062]], [[d:paladin#1456526006662856859]]. Destroses (2026-01-02) confirms community consensus: "SoR ret is generally better than SoC ret," though "both SoR and SoC rock tier... differences come to the accessories and weapons," and the SoR-vs-SoC choice (not "SP ret vs AP ret") is the real fork — SoR users take caster off-pieces, SoC users take warrior/rogue off-pieces, but both share the same T-gear/weapon path — [[d:paladin#1456491978056405094]], [[d:paladin#1456493316030992624]], [[d:paladin#1456527031801090213]], [[d:paladin#1456527061962199165]], [[d:paladin#1456527092098269340]], [[d:paladin#1456527158104035348]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot hit]** Prot needs 8% total melee hit: 3% from talent, 1% from the 5-weapon-skill book (costs 250g + a blue AH item + a small quest), remaining 4% from gear — [[d:paladin#1454034211819229277]], [[d:paladin#1454035275197190177]], [[d:paladin#1454035728102326448]] (Destroses).
- **[Prot hit]** Spell hit (as opposed to melee hit) as Prot only matters for Exorcism, Holy Shock (damage), Holy Wrath, Taunt, procs, and Consecration — melee hit/crit is what matters for the base Prot kit otherwise — [[d:paladin#1455105847255633982]], [[d:paladin#1455106045507670129]] (Destroses), [[d:paladin#1455109048335597762]] (Jesse Potter adds procs), [[d:paladin#1455133194499264513]] (Grey adds consecration).
- **[BUG] Spell hit talent not working** — Destroses ran extensive live testing (100s of Consecration casts and Holy Shocks on a dummy) and found resist rates consistent with the 3% talent-based spell hit NOT applying: 13-14% resist observed with 7% total spell hit (3% talent + 4% gear) where ~9-10% resist would be expected if talent worked; concludes "the 3% hit talent isn't giving spell hit," planned to file a bug report to Lhian — [[d:paladin#1454035486187327599]] (setup), [[d:paladin#1455187207898927268]], [[d:paladin#1455220148821819453]], [[d:paladin#1455216665980833802]], [[d:paladin#1455220317717926057]] (Faithroll confirms pattern), [[d:paladin#1455223534216286349]] (plans bug report). This likely also affects the Taunt-hit talent — [[d:paladin#1455221175968858122]].
- **[Consecration mechanics]** Each Consecration tick rolls independently on the hit/resist table (confirmed via testing, not just the initial cast) — resists were observed on ticks 2 and beyond, not just tick 1 — [[d:paladin#1455136021019623434]], [[d:paladin#1455155451623637024]] (Lazepally), [[d:paladin#1455150240452575404]] (Destroses); the visible "resist" text covers both true spell misses (min 1% always) and armor/resistance-type resists indistinguishably, per vanilla-engine convention — [[d:paladin#1455152041105162344]], [[d:paladin#1455152568572313620]] (Grey).
- **[Prot Sigil of the Ancient Accord]** Tested to proc only from Exorcism among baseline Prot abilities; confirmed NOT to proc from Consecration (300 test casts, zero procs) even on multiple enemies — [[d:paladin#1455139233898696705]], [[d:paladin#1455142397339107421]], [[d:paladin#1455179193376575733]], [[d:paladin#1455179366836207636]] (Flaguz). It also procs from T1 8pc set bonus, Holy Wrath, T2 3pc set bonus, Holy Light libram, Execute libram (per Destroses) — [[d:paladin#1455139976760131680]].
- **[Prot avoidance/hit budgets]** SoC Ret gets enough physical hit from gear that the hit talent risks overcapping; general guidance for HC (hardcore) raiding SoC Ret is to gear hit-cap over max stam — [[d:paladin#1453788680253870080]], [[d:paladin#1453789656616275968]] (Beldrin).
- **[Prot damage-reduction talent]** The talent granting a % armor-based damage reduction is considered weak (small flat reduction against big cleaves, ~-40 damage best case) and loses 30% effectiveness without a shield equipped — [[d:paladin#1453791552366444576]], [[d:paladin#1453791597807403008]], [[d:paladin#1453791636961366036]] (Aelgagar/Silverhand/Beldrin).
- **[Ret gearing]** AP Ret enchant guidance while gearing: enchant Agility to shoulders/gear if not yet at 33% crit, otherwise Strength (Str has more overall value for Ret than Agi at higher crit) — [[d:paladin#1455415306041299036]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1455415472462889082]] (Istenneveben).
- **[Prot itemization gripe]** Paladins have their gear budget split across 6-7 stats (Str/Int/Agi/Stam/SP/hit/crit/haste) vs other classes' narrower budgets, resulting in comparatively low HP on paladin gear pieces — recurring community complaint, not a dev-confirmed mechanic change — [[d:paladin#1454916405295054982]], [[d:paladin#1454918235073019974]] (Excinerus/Silverhand), open/contested design gripe, no resolution.
- **[Holy]** Healing debate: MP5 vs Healing Power on a neck — general guidance is that MP5/mana-economy stats matter more if you OOM often in fights, otherwise prioritize raw healing power; crit is argued (Domy) to have more value than MP5 for Holy Paladin overall — [[d:paladin#1454156206477279333]] (Arle), [[d:paladin#1454171230763483209]], [[d:paladin#1454170471196135615]] (Domy), disputed by Mima who reports going OOM regularly on long fights.
- **[Prot stat priority]** Str is called "a dead stat for prot"; stat priority given as SP, Stam, Int, Agi — [[d:paladin#1455609977749377035]], [[d:paladin#1455610107793641635]] (gastro).
- **[Prot weapon enchant]** Crusader weapon enchant "does nothing for prot" — use an SP enchant instead — [[d:paladin#1455604350343839826]] (Beldrin).
- **[Weapon skill mechanics]** 8% is the melee hit cap, obtainable from a mix of gear and weapon skill; each point of weapon skill above 300 grants 0.2% hit, with the bonus ending at 315 skill; once at 8% hit chance from other sources, more weapon skill is unnecessary except to reduce the glancing-blow damage penalty, which improves by 2% per point of weapon skill (glancing reduction stops improving past 315 skill) — [[d:paladin#1455656068913958912]], [[d:paladin#1455656267266916363]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1455449190116364339]] (Destroses, 2% per skill point). The +5 weapon-skill training quest costs 250g — [[d:paladin#1455627892825391345]] (Shucky). Human Ret baseline weapon skill (racial + book + talent) is 311; Ash-Forged Boots give +5 (to 316) but are lost when swapping to T3.5 boots, meaning non-Humans need 2 items to reach 315 or accept sub-315 skill, which is argued as part of why non-Humans lean SoR (glancing damage matters less for a caster-scaling build) — [[d:paladin#1455438403679162493]], [[d:paladin#1455438544251125863]], [[d:paladin#1455438669128138844]] (Jesse Potter). Grey notes even the post-book last 3% melee hit is "painful to get without sacrificing too many other important stats" due to poor Prot hit itemization — [[d:paladin#1455653591284187375]].
- **[Prot hit cap history]** The Prot hit-cap requirement was lowered over time from 9% total, to a book+quest reducing it to 7%, plus a talent reducing it further so only 4% is needed from gear — [[d:paladin#1455439186013323294]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Prot spellhit natural coverage]** Prot doesn't need to itemize for spell hit specifically; strong SP items worn anyway (Wraithblade?, Fallen God pieces, Devoured items, Nelth's Tear, Choker of the Emerald Lord) naturally provide ~9% spell hit once combined with Precision talent, so a dedicated spellhit piece "is just not worth actually wearing" — [[d:paladin#1455539400896417913]], [[d:paladin#1455540030561980498]] (Grey).
- **[Frost resist]** Consensus: do NOT gear Frost Resist for Sapphiron or Kel'Thuzad tanking — "the answer is zero"; wearing any FR at all leads to taking *more* total damage than wearing none, because it costs stam/armor that outweighs the mitigation; if insistent, only 150 (35% reduction) or 315 (75% reduction, the soft cap) are "breakpoint" amounts worth using; instead stack HP (7500 minimum for BWL bosses, 8000 preferred) and rely on Frost resist granted by Proclaim Champion — [[d:paladin#1455534772028837888]], [[d:paladin#1455535236136833169]], [[d:paladin#1455534204690366578]], [[d:paladin#1455537634641449063]], [[d:paladin#1455534277193371701]] (Grey/Dodging/angels1m).
- **[Item stat-budget system]** Turtle WoW items follow a per-ilvl point budget where additional points in the *same* stat cost progressively more (diminishing returns), so multi-stat items are more "efficient" per budget point than single-stat-stacked ones; worked example: an ilvl100 epic chest has ~662-677 total budget, and going from 44 to 48 Stamina alone costs 40 of those points (~6% of the whole budget) — [[d:paladin#1456680739352612947]], [[d:paladin#1456682420681183425]], [[d:paladin#1456684245744615637]] (Grey). Destroses states Lhian confirmed the Ret tier set is "fully within budget," but Destroses's own calculation puts it ~80 budget points short of the Shaman equivalent once haste isn't counted, implying haste is valued close to crit in the model though no exact number was given — [[d:paladin#1456664935126794331]], [[d:paladin#1456665021110030377]].
- **[Ret itemization gripe]** The Ret T3 chest is nominally "overstatted" but wastes points because its budget went into AP instead of Str, making it effectively worse than a plain Str item once Holy Might/Kings/Divine Strength Str-multiplier buffs are factored in — [[d:paladin#1456666424260038656]], [[d:paladin#1456666504069120062]] (Jesse Potter). T2.5 has a 5-piece set bonus but T3 caps at a 4-piece bonus, a known asymmetry — [[d:paladin#1456668412808925337]], [[d:paladin#1456668503644963010]] (Jesse Potter). Ret pre-raid BiS gear is scattered/"chaotic" across many disconnected sources (Red Dragonscale set pieces, Corehound/Hide of the Wild, dungeon drops like Animated Choker, quest rewards like Fordring's Seal, rep rewards like Time-Shifted Wheel), unlike Prot's comparatively consolidated pre-raid set — [[d:paladin#1456682370949447688]] (Mima), [[d:paladin#1456683039915770070]] (Kaedetoki).
- **[Prot shield stat priority]** Grey's detailed shield/ring stat-weight guidance for Prot: prioritize 2% Block on shields essentially always over +7 Stam (trade other stats for stam instead); if you're not already close to being one-shot by the extra 70-80 HP, that HP isn't helping you, whereas 2% Block accumulates large mitigation over a raid; replace 1%-block ring enchants with +6 stam or +3-all once past crush cap; if wearing avoidance purely to hit crush cap, once capped consider swapping that piece for a threat piece instead; consider dropping 5/5 Deflection to 3/5 once crush-capped; the one exception to preferring block is choosing Shield Spike (damage) enchant over block on a dedicated AoE/DPS shield like FRD — [[d:paladin#1456636999325843507]], [[d:paladin#1456637277974298675]].
- **[Enchant tooltip quirk]** Wizard Oil's tooltip says "Spell Damage" but the item also grants Healing Power — a naming/tooltip inconsistency, not a separate effect — [[d:paladin#1456637687288299530]] (Račke).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Prot]** Reckoning mechanics debated at length: it converts a proc into an instant completion of the current swing timer rather than a true bonus "extra attack" appended after normal swing — this makes it weaker/more situational than other extra-attack sources (Windfury, etc.) that trigger off the player's own successful attacks — extended community discussion, semi-resolved that it's "acceptable" given ~3-4 autos per Holy Strike/Judgement — [[d:paladin#1453813622294974566]] through [[d:paladin#1453882242836467764]] (Silverhand/Grey/Dodging/Arle/Destroses).
- **[Prot]** Reckoning is confirmed to proc from spell casts too (not just melee/parries): e.g., Exorcism cast, SoR cast — [[d:paladin#1454083843576565760]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot]** Reckoning triggers off successful blocks and also functions to fish for Thunderfury procs and sustain mana — [[d:paladin#1454857283136786575]] (angels1m), [[d:paladin#1454083435827302503]].
- **[Prot threat]** Threat feels "absolutely fine" on Ambershire (MC/K10-locked content) but this is largely an illusion of the low-gear/low-content stage — scaling into higher tiers does not keep pace — [[d:paladin#1454645125547556997]] (Silverhand).
- **[Prot threat/SoR]** The threat-shifting patch that buffed Seal of Righteousness/nerfed Holy Shield/Holy Strike-based threat (to address prot off-tanks being "useless") is seen as insufficient compensation, since 1H SoR doesn't scale enough to preserve overall threat output ("It is by far the lowest and lamest 'on next melee'") — [[d:paladin#1455195644141633600]], [[d:paladin#1455196033435959399]] (tripleXDengine/Grey).
- **[Prot naxx dps benchmark]** A well-geared prot MT is estimated capable of 900-1000 dps in Naxx if everything connects; multiple prots in the discussion report having hit ~1k dps in Naxx tanking — [[d:paladin#1455203162658050079]], [[d:paladin#1455203253670121756]] (angels1m/Grey).
- **[Prot]** Autoattack + Crusader Strike (both physical) make up "over half your damage" when there's still armor left on bosses (i.e., before full sunder/IEA stacking) — [[d:paladin#1455416619533865174]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Judgement mechanics]** Only 3 Paladin abilities scale with AP; Judgement effects use a flat SP-based bonus (~140 SP-equivalent flat bonus mentioned for the Judge effect); Crusader Strike is normalized on weapon damage (0.9 x base weapon damage, not raw weapon damage) and cannot proc seals, damage-based procs, or refresh judgements — [[d:paladin#1453733547729748140]], [[d:paladin#1453733724087648357]], [[d:paladin#1453733894791630929]], [[d:paladin#1453734511949906141]] (Mithreon).
- **[Crusader Strike damage table]** Full tooltip posted: applies a stacking (up to 5x, 30s) bonus holy damage debuff to target. Rank 1: 25 mana / 12 dmg / 6 bonus holy dmg per stack; Rank 2: 40 mana / 27 dmg / 10 bonus; Rank 3: 55 mana / 46 dmg / 15 bonus; Rank 4: 70 mana / 69 dmg / 22 bonus; Rank 5: 90 mana / 103 dmg / 30 bonus — [[d:paladin#1453755094947594372]] (angels1m).
- **[SoC/haste interaction]** Seal of Command damage is calculated before haste is applied — haste still benefits SoC dps despite the "slow weapon" archetype association — [[d:paladin#1455414691500134452]] (Destroses).
- **[Consecration]** Consecration triggers the on-next-melee holy-damage debuff (Crusader effect) but is itself unaffected by that same debuff — relevant for boss mechanics like Loatheb's spell-lockout since Holy Strike counts as a "cast" that would trigger Loatheb's debuff — [[d:paladin#1455221253823795282]] (Grey).
- **[GCD]** The global cooldown is 1.5 seconds for all classes except Rogue and Druid-in-cat-form, which have a 1-second GCD; Holy Shock's cooldown/reset is a special-cased 1-second exception on Paladin specifically — [[d:paladin#1456593182472011984]] (Destroses), [[d:paladin#1456598910356164658]] (Destroses, Holy Shock exception).
- **[Spellstrike proc mechanics]** Seal of Righteousness, Seal of Command, Crusader Strike, Holy Strike, and Judgements all count as "melee strikes" and can proc spellstrike-type items; only the T2 3-piece set bonus additionally makes these count as a "casted spell," which is what lets them proc caster-only-trigger items like Sigil of the Ancient Accord, the Sunken Temple ("ST") chest, and Insomnius Retribution — [[d:paladin#1456009121894764586]], [[d:paladin#1456009237892169800]], [[d:paladin#1456009315394785533]] (Adamant). Spellstrike items will NOT proc off Seal/Judgement of Light, Seal of Justice, or Seal of Righteousness itself (deliberately, to prevent an infinite proc loop, since SoR already procs spellstrike) — [[d:paladin#1456008725897936979]], [[d:paladin#1456009704361951409]], [[d:paladin#1456009718580641883]] (Mima/Adamant/Silverhand); Judgement of Light proccing spellstrike specifically had to be hotfixed out previously for being too strong — [[d:paladin#1456009062893490177]] (Silverhand).
- **[Seal twisting]** Seal twisting reportedly stopped working/was nerfed at some point without an accompanying changelog entry; when re-tested, twisting into Seal of Command was found to only grant a single extra SoC proc rather than the old "SoR proc + SoC + a second SoR" chain — [[d:paladin#1455596253172989997]], [[d:paladin#1455596351269114071]] (Beldrin), [[d:paladin#1455597115454787625]] (Excinerus).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Prot]** For fresh level-60 Prot Paladins, baseline threat is described as unusually strong "due to baseline of Holy Strike being good," contributing to the perception prot is OP in early content — [[d:paladin#1454644785599217778]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot]** Holy Shield uptime "should" be ~100% on boss fights via mana management from the extra attacks it grants; AoE-farm holy shield uptime is less relevant since charges often go unused pulling large packs — [[d:paladin#1453872651058811011]], [[d:paladin#1453872688790503579]] (Silverhand).
- **[Prot tanking gear thresholds]** A guild target of getting above the ~7-7.5k HP "danger threshold" is cited for T3.5-geared tanks — [[d:paladin#1454275577564631092]] (Grey).
- **[Prot mitigation trinkets]** Best mitigation trinkets by context: raw % mitigation = Styleen's; high-end raid ST (stam + avoidance) = Edict of the Tribunal; mass-pull/boosting = Gift of the Doomsayer ("GoD") — [[d:paladin#1454504686295191756]], [[d:paladin#1454504772160721022]], [[d:paladin#1454504827152367687]] (tripleXDengine).
- **[Prot AoE farming - FRD]** Force Reactive Disk (FRD) used to allow infinite durability via a self-kill exploit, which has been "fixed" multiple times; community treats continued attempts to bypass it as clearly over the exploitation line at this point — [[d:paladin#1454476210355572901]], [[d:paladin#1454476612434137170]], [[d:paladin#1454480173360615480]], [[d:paladin#1454483636618264670]], [[d:paladin#1454483899538346035]] (Destroses/angels1m/Grey).
- **[Prot MT loot]** Guild anecdote: guild considering giving Thunderfury to a warrior off-tank instead of a top-10-parsing prot paladin MT under the rationale that "TF isn't good for prot paladin," which the prot MT strongly disputes as a skill-issue/logic-issue take — [[d:paladin#1454642884300247234]], [[d:paladin#1454644338599399606]], [[d:paladin#1454644391540035626]].
- **[Prot]** SoR/threat generation from autoattack alone (with TF) is calculated at effectively ~400 threat contributed by AA+SoR combined per rotation cycle (tripleXDengine/angels1m back-of-envelope in context of Prayer of Warding/soaking mechanics) — [[d:paladin#1455191866470301758]] (angels1m).
- **[Prot 2H tanking]** Two-handed weapon Prot tanking exists as a known playstyle ("2h tanking jank") and Lhian confirmed something new is coming for players who enjoy it in 1.18.1, though currently the 2H weapon specialization talent lacks +3 polearm skill unlike some other specs — [[d:paladin#1454081152066261163]], [[d:paladin#1454081248283852901]], [[d:paladin#1454851583010410508]], [[d:paladin#1454534687757307986]].
- **[Prot 2H tanking debate]** Extended discussion of the rumored 2H-tank patch: shields are argued to always deserve a niche because they grant ~30% raw damage reduction outright (a "massive" mitigation source), so even if 2H tanking gets meaningfully higher threat, sword-and-board will likely remain correct for many fights, especially full-mitigation AoE tanking (e.g., full ZG pulls) where full-mit S&B is still optimal; the worry cited is a "furyprot"-style outcome where the new option makes the old one strictly worse in all cases — [[d:paladin#1455976330435760149]], [[d:paladin#1455976479195398330]], [[d:paladin#1455977347500212406]], [[d:paladin#1455977497106841611]], [[d:paladin#1455977542652657825]], [[d:paladin#1455977909670903818]] (Silverhand/Grey/Anakinn). Without a shield, full T3.5 Prot can reportedly be one-shot by Sapphiron's melee range, and Mephistroth already "hits like a freight train" even with a shield up — cited as reasons not to give up the shield lightly even if 2H becomes viable — [[d:paladin#1455976767285366926]], [[d:paladin#1455976940954714264]], [[d:paladin#1455540446393536770]] (Grey).
- **[Prot T2 vs threat]** T2 Prot is explicitly a mitigation ("mit") set, not a threat set; if DPS meaningfully outgear the tank (2+ tiers), Prot (like most tanks other than Bear/Shaman) will struggle to hold threat regardless of gear quality, since only Bear and Shaman tank kits can "ignore" a large gear gap — [[d:paladin#1456148928113152151]] (Grey).
- **[Prot threat benchmark, PW soak]** Average sustained TPS for a well-geared (T2.5, full threat gear) BiS Prot MT is roughly 2000-2100 TPS in a "Prayer of Warding"-style soaking context; for comparison a 2.3k dps Rogue generates ~1700 TPS with Salvation up, meaning the tank must clear that to hold aggro — [[d:paladin#1456274390579810419]], [[d:paladin#1456274996413595692]], [[d:paladin#1456275212805996596]] (angels1m/Mithreon).
- **[Prot HP thresholds, BWL]** 7500 HP is called the "bare minimum" for BWL tanking, with 8000 HP preferred — [[d:paladin#1455534204690366578]], [[d:paladin#1455544794045747364]] (Grey).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Holy Paladin healing playstyle is largely melee-weaving between Holy Strike/Consecration and heals — described by multiple as "not totally braindead" if you track boss mechanics well, but at least one experienced player calls it "brain dead" (melee-healed all of Kara40 including Kruul) — [[d:paladin#1455216979265851414]] (Faithroll) vs [[d:paladin#1455217777135845450]] (dusty), disputed.
- **[Holy]** Fights considered "unmeleeable" by some holy paladins: Abyssal Lords' rock-guy add, Ragnaros, Geddon, Onyxia phase 2 — though others (Domy) claim to melee-heal all of these successfully, disagreement on playstyle/gear-dependence — [[d:paladin#1454169105425895527]] (Mima) vs [[d:paladin#1454169185675383018]] (Domy), disputed.
- **[Holy]** Clawfang Ripsaw (UBRS?) tanks are described as a "tank check" fight requiring near-constant max-rank Flash of Light spam from the healer, a known mana sink; general recommendation is other healers/melee should offload the healing rather than relying solely on the Paladin spamming max-rank — [[d:paladin#1454170045214101717]], [[d:paladin#1454170727518441603]], [[d:paladin#1454170373401874496]] (Mima/Domy/Arle).
- **[Holy]** Domy's personal gearing notes (2.5/Neff Tear geared holy paladin): "don't judge often," use the ZF (Scarlet Monastery?) libram with rank-1 Consecration in melee range for group healing top-up, prioritize crit/caster rings/neck over MP5, use rank-1 Holy Light unless a big burst heal on the tank is needed — [[d:paladin#1454169645962756280]], [[d:paladin#1454171937465962598]], [[d:paladin#1454172022602076210]], [[d:paladin#1454170471196135615]].
- **[Holy]** Blessing of Light is described by one player as a blessing they've "not done yet" even at 5/8 T1 — implies it's a low-priority/late-adoption Holy tool — [[d:paladin#1454175160155504693]] (Mima).
- **[Holy raid utility]** A dispel-bot role is called out for a specific fight (Geddon) where melee-healing isn't practical for a Holy paladin because of dispel duty — [[d:paladin#1454169199051018291]] (Mima).
- **[Holy mana]** angels1m's math: with 8 MP5 + 6 Int neck difference, over a 1-minute fight that's ~156 extra mana, and over a 4-minute fight ~384 extra mana from MP5 — argued as low value compared to a single Major Mana Potion (triples mana) — [[d:paladin#1454168475294629970]], [[d:paladin#1454168846201258154]], [[d:paladin#1454169086941728808]] (angels1m).
- **[Holy crit weighting]** 1% spell crit is estimated worth roughly 20-40 "heal bonus" points depending on gear level, used as a rough conversion rate when deciding whether to trade Healing Power for crit — [[d:paladin#1455586091099881542]] (Mithreon).
- **[Holy crit mechanic, Turtle-specific]** A critical heal grants a buff giving the healer's next spells +20% Healing Power and +5% max HP pool, and separately places a "timebomb" on the *target* that was crit-healed, which triggers at the target's next damage instance — described as functioning like a reworked Illumination-style mechanic; exact spell name not given in-chunk — [[d:paladin#1455585456812069050]], [[d:paladin#1455585705534423125]] (Račke/Mithreon).
- **[Holy scaling]** Holy Strike's healing component scales at 0.3 healing-power coefficient, versus Daybreak at 0.43; Mithreon expects meaningful non-Daybreak healing gains to require the Eye of the Dead trinket and T3.5 gear later — [[d:paladin#1455591730635341986]], [[d:paladin#1455591878203277476]].
- **[Holy set utility]** Holy Paladins/priests are considered the least-impacted (best off) healers relative to the ZG-before-BWL delay, since ZG gearing benefits them well (see Gear section) — [[d:paladin#1455850458760347857]] (Mithreon).
- **[Holy AoE grind niche build]** A "Holy Shock + Blessed Strikes reset" self-healing build ("shockadin") is explicitly called very niche/fun-only, mainly a PvP or low-gear solo-farming build, not something to gear seriously toward — [[d:paladin#1455896108633952372]] (angels1m), [[d:paladin#1455896314654097531]] (Faithroll). One player found it surprisingly strong for solo Silithus bug farming/self-healing compared to Ret simply dying — [[d:paladin#1455896060290404623]] (L-star).
- **[Holy pre-raid BiS]** Holy pre-raid BiS is scattered across many sources: a mix of the Red Dragonscale set, Core Hound/Hide of the Wild pieces, dungeon quest rewards (Animated Choker), quest-chain rewards (Fordring's Seal), and rep rewards (Time-Shifted Wheel), among others — a "chaotic" gear-up path compared to Prot's — [[d:paladin#1456682370949447688]] (Mima).

## PvP
- **[Prot]** Prot Paladin is regarded by some non-paladins as strong/"king" in PvP due to Lay on Hands/bubble utility, though this is disputed as PvP participation is low server-wide (~50 players) and many kit choices are said to be PvP-driven despite devs claiming otherwise — [[d:paladin#1453731825401987093]] (TRUE HEIR), [[d:paladin#1454935979180818493]] (Gilnean).

- **[PvP mechanic]** Bubble (Divine Shield/Protection) reduces healing received by 60% while active in addition to its other effects (a recent-ish nerf per discussion) — [[d:paladin#1454939206445957152]] (Seviry).
- **[Contested]** A leaked/rumored upcoming change where Shield Block Value converts to "block a % of damage" is speculated to nerf Prot Paladin specifically in PvP — [[d:paladin#1454936219392938045]] (Seviry), unconfirmed/open question.
