## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Ret/Prot]** Holy Strike is gaining weapon-damage scaling next patch; SP coefficient rising from ~0.43 to ~0.71, base damage reduced but total scales up with gear/weapon dps — [[d:paladin#1470803915565039883]] (Destroses, 2026-02-09), confirmed math by [[d:paladin#1471496079882190962]] (Grey, 2026-02-11: unbuffed HS damage stays same pre/post patch, so AP-buff scaling is pure bonus).
- **[Prot]** Post-patch, raid AP buffs (BoM/BoK/MotW/Battle Shout/Totem of Strength) will contribute as much dmg to Holy Strike as a full round of SP consumables currently does (~85 dmg vs ~84 dmg) — [[d:paladin#1471496079882190962]] (Grey, 2026-02-11; expects it to cut prot's gold/hr consumable costs a lot).
- **[Ret]** Devs (Lhian) intend Ret Paladin to remain a deliberately low-APM melee class; no seal-twisting or libram-twisting desired going forward — [[d:paladin#1470312282868416573]], [[d:paladin#1470312349239087126]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-02-08).
- **[Ret]** The current lack of a ~0.5s GCD delay on libram-swapping ("libram twisting") is confirmed by Lhian to be an unintended bug, not a sanctioned mechanic; likely to be closed — [[d:paladin#1470311635087265854]], [[d:paladin#1470311751517208741]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-02-08). Community expects normal libram swaps (post-pull, on downtime) to remain fine even if twisting is removed — [[d:paladin#1470277098433417331]] (Destroses).
- **[Ret]** SoR got scaling back and JoR became "melee hit" at some past patch (CC2) — this killed pure "spellret" as a distinct build; ret is now hybrid SoR (benefits from both STR and SP) — [[d:paladin#1470538114614427853]], [[d:paladin#1470539660521635851]] (Jesse Potter/Istenneveben, 2026-02-09).
- **[Tank]** Windfury Totem internal cooldown confirmed at 2s, not 1.5s — [[d:paladin#1471513070697451573]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-12).
- **[Consumables]** Concoctions (Dreamwater, Emerald Mongoose+Arcane Giant combo potions) were bugged and not stacking their SP components (some share the same coded "35 SP" aura and overwrite instead of adding) from at least 2026-02-10; devs said a fix was coming "next week" — [[d:paladin#1470717224020086834]]/[[d:paladin#1471500202291626016]] (gastro/Pastor, 2026-02-10/12) — later report suggests it may already be working again by 2026-02-13, unconfirmed as a lasting fix — [[d:paladin#1471946948264202383]] (Olivaar of Moments, 2026-02-13; single source).
- **[General]** No official changelog was published for the week of 2026-02-09/10 while devs worked on itemization changes; patch date rumored as "Feb 27" but not confirmed by devs — [[d:paladin#1471040909805359197]], [[d:paladin#1471118087519473837]] (question unanswered).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Prot]** Prot's talent tree design forces picking only one of several strong, mutually exclusive utility talents (Sanctuary Aura, Kings, Sanctity Aura, Imp Reckoning, Anticipation, Deflection, Conviction) rather than "pick 2 of 3 to improve" like most classes — [[d:paladin#1469843710857449686]], [[d:paladin#1469844216019685610]] (Grey, +1 reaction, 2026-02-08). Disputed: Leo claims "you can easily do sanctuary and kings" together, against Proph/Grey's claim you can't do Sanctity+Kings — [[d:paladin#1469843865774198887]] vs [[d:paladin#1469843974255542334]] (2026-02-08; disputed, unresolved).
- **[Ret]** Necrodin (Ret + Prot hybrid using double T2 3-set bonuses, "Of the Lost" ring set, spellcast-based ghost/wolf pet summons) is viable, if below standard ret DPS: "performs very well, you will do less than standard, but is completely viable"; top pusher Adamant reportedly held a rank-4 Naxx parse spot with it — [[d:paladin#1470316146141040813]], [[d:paladin#1470316685675331697]] (Destroses, 2026-02-09).
- **[Ret]** SoC and SoR end-game BiS builds share ~11 of the same gear pieces; "spellret" as separate pure-SP gearing is not a thing anymore since CC2 changes — [[d:paladin#1470538287960821965]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-02-09).
- **[Ret]** Necrodin damage breakdown: summoned ghosts ~15-25% of damage, wolves ~2-3%, with roughly even split otherwise between summons/autos/spells/abilities depending on weapon and enchant — [[d:paladin#1470164059117846570]], [[d:paladin#1470246088555495659]] (Aelgagar/keungzhe, 2026-02-09). Necrodin is stronger on long stationary fights, weaker on target-swap/burst fights like Razorgore/Twins — [[d:paladin#1470324329031143476]] (keungzhe).
- **[Ret]** Necrodin needs BOTH T2 3-piece sets (Ret+Prot) to work; a single 3pc + pure SP gear "halves your spell effects" since the whole build is based on casting spells (Divine Strike/etc.) to proc ghosts and item effects — [[d:paladin#1470319054094073979]] (keungzhe, 2026-02-09; disputing a claim that 1x3pc+SP gear works just as well).
- **[Tank]** Prot paladin tank DPS/threat roughly comparable to a well-geared feral bear at BiS (~1k dps each in AQ40/Naxx gear), but average bears usually out-DPS average paladins by 30-50% due to more consistent damage intake / rage generation — [[d:paladin#1471631398077993105]], [[d:paladin#1471629245720690728]], [[d:paladin#1471630067607011351]] (Grey/Proph, 2026-02-13). General tank DPS rule: "60% of a dps" except (non-deep) prot warrior, which does more — [[d:paladin#1471628277847298131]], [[d:paladin#1471628573457780808]] (Mithreon/Grey).
- **[Class flavor]** Multiple posters (Southshore Crusader Vidnar, Jesse Potter) note Ret has almost no talents actually themed around "vengeance/retribution" flavor — light chatter, low signal.

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[Prot]** Proph's own build history: used "11/33/7" almost exclusively, then made a second build with more avoidance for periods needing heavy Kings-spam (greater-blessing rotation instead of Sanctity), which also lets him wear threatier/less-avoidance gear while staying crush immune — [[d:paladin#1469845161801678953]] (Proph, 2026-02-08; single source, no full point list given).
- **[Prot/OT]** Aljeron's proposed 2nd-tank AoE-threat build (full T3/K40 geared, less need for defensive CDs): `https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/paladin?points=-AoYCYaCABo-AoQoVIAAF` — drops Righteous Strikes, which costs ~30% single-target Holy Strike threat — [[d:paladin#1470942274300018708]], [[d:paladin#1470943510227058781]], [[d:paladin#1470944487093174375]] (Aljeron/Silverhand, 2026-02-11). Seviry plans to run the identical build as 3rd tank in a speedrun group but adds 1 point in Vengeful Strikes, since a single point already grants the full attack-speed bonus — [[d:paladin#1470962502736019518]] (Seviry, 2026-02-11).
- **[Prot]** Reckoning: 3/5 points yields on average ~1 extra attack every 15s vs a boss; not worth running 5/5 over other talents in most cases; TBC-style Reckoning uptime does not exist here — [[d:paladin#1470578331769508051]] (Grey, 2026-02-09: autos ~220 dmg, SoR ~360 dmg, so each Reckoning proc ≈1k threat with JoW mana/WF proc chances on top). Reckoning appears to have a ~1-1.5s internal cooldown based on swing-timer observation (unconfirmed, vibes-based) — [[d:paladin#1470796875375579311]] (Destroses, 2026-02-08; single source).
- **[Prot]** Simmed threat value (Proph, upcoming class changes): Reckoning ≈ 3-3.5% of total sustained threat; 5/5 Improved SoR ≈ up to 2.8% — [[d:paladin#1470599999220875367]] (Proph, 2026-02-10).
- **[Prot]** Sanctuary Aura: ~2% of a tank's total threat when active on an ordinary boss (comparable to Judgement CD points or the SoR talent points) per Proph's numbers; grants 4% damage reduction vs Sapphiron's Frost Aura and 8% vs Anomalus's Arcane Aura, making it strong raid-wide mitigation especially vs many small hits — [[d:paladin#1470244666330058863]], [[d:paladin#1470241482807181504]] (Proph/Grey, 2026-02-09). Disputed: Aelgagar argues it's not worth self-talenting since most fights are a few big hits rather than many small ones, though he'll use it if another tank brings it — [[d:paladin#1470243770233520384]], [[d:paladin#1470245538455752716]] (Aelgagar, 2026-02-09; disputed by Grey/Proph).
- **[Ret leveling]** The "Ret Aura +25% damage" talent is good while leveling (you get hit) but becomes useless once raiding as a DPS who shouldn't be tanking hits — [[d:paladin#1470135414701428939]], [[d:paladin#1470135499883810888]] (Arle, 2026-02-08). Most ret players prefer Pursuit of Justice over this movement-speed-competing damage talent, using the freed points for +15 stam on boots — [[d:paladin#1470147976151171247]] (Grey, 2026-02-09).
- **[Prot leveling]** For a lowbie prot AoE-grinding, prioritize Intellect (more consecrations) over Strength (block); "Str is bad for blocking, you'll get way more value from int" — [[d:paladin#1471571009923453001]], [[d:paladin#1471571986353356913]] (Lynathel/Seviry, +2 reactions, 2026-02-13).
- **[Holy]** Sanctity Aura vs Kings for Prot: providing your own Sanctity Aura is "incredible value" as prot; for leveling specifically, keep Sanctity Aura — [[d:paladin#1471862675314311230]]/[[d:paladin#1471862675314311230]], [[d:paladin#1471863425066995803]] (Grey, 2026-02-13).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Prot (or Holy/Prot) is the recommended leveling spec; a leveling guide with a prot build is pinned in-channel — [[d:paladin#1470026434159579368]] (Antur, 2026-02-08), [[d:paladin#1471804279470817354]] (gastro, 2026-02-14: "generally best to level as prot or holyprot"; AoE-pull packs faster, easier to solo elites).
- **[Ret]** Basic leveling rotation: Seal of the Crusader → Judgement of the Crusader → Seal of Righteousness → Crusader's Strike → autohit → loop Judgement of Righteousness/Crusader's Strike until dead — [[d:paladin#1470224575672488058]] (№⅟ℋɛɍσ, 2026-02-08; single source, no dissent).
- **[Holy]** Leveling as pure Holy feels weak/incomplete before Holy Shock and Blessed Strikes are unlocked; mostly Flash of Light spam with occasional full Holy Light held in reserve — [[d:paladin#1471313912447238277]], [[d:paladin#1471315091642384475]], [[d:paladin#1471314706106023968]] (Muster For Lupercal/Silverhand, 2026-02-12). Recommendation: go Holy/Prot for Spiritual Focus while leveling rather than pure Holy, since Holy Shock isn't available until later anyway — [[d:paladin#1471776197032673435]], [[d:paladin#1471767452382793789]] (dumbid, 2026-02-13).
- **[SP gear]** Spell power items are essentially irrelevant before level 34; not worth pursuing as a leveling stat outside a few niche pieces — [[d:paladin#1470872634236473518]], [[d:paladin#1470873431930175498]] (Seviry, 2026-02-11).
- **[Weapons]** Recommended early weapons: a green Knightly Longsword off the AH around level 33+ ("kicks ass leveling") — [[d:paladin#1471362716831715499]], [[d:paladin#1471376008635748436]] (Grey/deef, 2026-02-12); Crescent of Forlorn Spirits (axe from the Stalvan questline) lasts almost to level 40 — [[d:paladin#1471346808180707474]] (Kay9Cop, 2026-02-12).
- **[Enchants]** To get Enchant Weapon – Crusader on a leveling weapon: buy the mats yourself, then find a player enchanter who owns the recipe and tip them (no direct AH purchase of the enchant itself) — [[d:paladin#1470261707350999143]] (Aelgagar, 2026-02-08).
- **[Holy/leveling utility]** eagle-gear + vampirism (~5%) plus 5% parry / Improved Ret Aura felt strong for a leveling paladin instead of squeezing in prot/holy hybrid points — [[d:paladin#1470869340734623796]] (bela, 2026-02-10; single source, casual account).
- **[Prot leveling]** Prot AoE grinding doesn't really "take off" until Holy Shield and Reckoning, around level ~34 — [[d:paladin#1471804279470817354]], [[d:paladin#1470836932522147891]] Chud/dusty exchange, 2026-02-15 (dusty: "Not really until you can get Holy Shield and Reckoning. So like 34ish?"). Consecration is learned baseline from a trainer at level 20; AoE pulling is possible from 20 but not efficient, more efficient once Prot (via Holy Shield) — [[d:paladin#1472901589311164490]], [[d:paladin#1472902897300934739]], [[d:paladin#1473034262054961337]], [[d:paladin#1473035686352261192]], [[d:paladin#1473038673397157940]] (Barkis/Pastor/flatearthdsh/Grey/Mithreon, 2026-02-14/16). Mana management via Seal of Wisdom is required when AoE-pulling early.
- **[Leveling stat priority]** Holy leveling: Int and Stam are priority; add Strength if soloing/questing rather than pure dungeon healing — [[d:paladin#1473436363750903901]], [[d:paladin#1473436508378894357]] (dusty, 2026-02-18). Best-known leveling path for years has been Holy/Prot, picking up Seal of Wisdom around level 18 and a fast weapon from Darkshire that lasts a long while — [[d:paladin#1471852362405515477]]/[[d:paladin#1472511842961063948]] (dumbid, 2026-02-14).
- **[Ret leveling]** For pure ret leveling on a PvP-flagged/faction-imbalanced realm ("Tel Abim"), ret is reportedly weak for a long stretch; recommendation is to go Prot or Holy instead — [[d:paladin#1472962763520217231]], [[d:paladin#1472965158329520231]], [[d:paladin#1472965300143001660]] (Mjanni/Purge, 2026-02-16; single-source anecdote, realm-specific).
- **[SoR vs SoC leveling]** SoR is like a mage's Fireball — damage independent of your weapon, so it doesn't require a good weapon to level with; SoC is like a rogue's Sinister Strike — entirely weapon-dependent, good if twinked, bad if undergeared — recommended for a "Craftsman" challenge (blacksmith-only weapons) to run SoR since 2h weapon selection from blacksmithing is sparse levels 30-50 — [[d:paladin#1472511373756862588]], [[d:paladin#1472507806971199593]], [[d:paladin#1472539611728183367]] (Grey/Jesse Potter/gastro, 2026-02-14).
- **[Craftsman-challenge specific]** Blacksmiths can craft only a ~level-5 shield in Turtle, nothing further until 60, so tanking as Craftsman is impractical; recommend leveling Holy/Prot for the "forgiving" nature of a healer over gear-dependent Ret; take Jewelcrafting temporarily (to ~180 skill) for the +12 stam/+13 holy dmg/+12 int trinkets, then drop it for mining — [[d:paladin#1472537049931321477]], [[d:paladin#1472539497064435732]], [[d:paladin#1472540009507852393]], [[d:paladin#1472540594533302457]], [[d:paladin#1472541463215865956]] (gastro, 2026-02-14).
- **[Weapon speed while leveling]** SoR's base damage is slightly higher with a slow weapon than fast, but not by much; its SP scaling is unaffected by weapon speed, only by whether it's 1h (9.7%×speed) or 2h (12.5%×speed) — [[d:paladin#1472535396075180063]] (Light, 2026-02-14; single source, precise numbers, unchallenged).
- **[Fresh 60 prot gearing]** Fastest gearing path: get carried in MC/Kara10 (10/20-man), do Onyxia attunement (also gates MC/BWL attunement), then traditional gearing via BRD/LBRS/UBRS/Scholomance/Stratholme into custom dungeons (Stormwind Vault, Black Morass); Caverns of Time/Black Morass "Wardens of Time" rep yields great items, faster if funneled Sands of Time rep — [[d:paladin#1472549279502700616]], [[d:paladin#1472549513217445931]], [[d:paladin#1472550138877575310]] (Arle, 2026-02-14; a pre-BiS list is in the pins).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Ret/necrodin]** Rotation for necrodin/2h ret with slow weapons: Crusader Strike until 3 Zeal stacks, then spam Holy Strike, refresh Zeal as needed — [[d:paladin#1471135299928199250]] (Beldrin, +1, 2026-02-11); confirmed "HS >> CS for necro after stacks" — [[d:paladin#1471136975883866124]] (keungzhe, +1). CS SP scaling (0.20) is lower than Holy Strike's (0.43 pre-1.18.1), but CS gets AP + base weapon damage on top, so with high SP/AP it can edge ahead — [[d:paladin#1470983558658265188]], [[d:paladin#1470983601972838480]], [[d:paladin#1470983685816975392]] (Destroses/Aelgagar, 2026-02-11). Test CS vs HS on a sundered (armor-reduced) target since CS is physical and benefits more from armor debuffs — [[d:paladin#1471134954464350332]] (Destroses).
- **[Ret standard]** Full rotation: spam Crusader Strike, use Holy Strike to keep the Holy Might buff up, Judgement of Righteousness + reseal Seal of Righteousness inside the swing window, keep SoR always active; use Repentance on non-stunnable targets; use Consecration on 2+ targets or when mana is in surplus. SoC ret substitutes Judgement of Command/Seal of Command for JoR/SoR — [[d:paladin#1471852362405515477]] through [[d:paladin#1471853399300374713]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-11; single detailed source, unchallenged).
- **[Ret]** Judgement should be cast right after your white (auto) swing lands, not before, to guarantee your seal is active for that swing's on-hit effect (e.g. SoR) — [[d:paladin#1471558463229526216]] (theoloras/warkrak, 2026-02-12).
- **[Ret]** General DPS-maximizing checklist from Grey: buy more consumables, shorten the fight for the whole raid, maintain Zeal/Holy Might for the fight duration, don't let a weak filler ability delay a strike that's about to come off CD, don't clip auto attacks with strikes (windfury interaction — wait until after WF's ICD to use JoR if a strike would proc it) — [[d:paladin#1472678616847351819]] (Grey, 2026-02-15).
- **[Holy DPS-filler]** As Holy spec, Holy Strike is used ~98% of the time over Crusader Strike because it's a free AoE "smart heal" that can crit and proc Daybreak — [[d:paladin#1471931304911311039]], [[d:paladin#1471931378869207210]] (Beldrin, 2026-02-11). Holy players use Crusader Strike essentially only "when there's zero AoE damage to heal" — [[d:paladin#1472677287362039878]], [[d:paladin#1472677338494926848]] (Grey/Arle, 2026-02-15).
- **[Holy]** Optimal Loatheb-style single-target healing rotation: Judge (Light), then spam Holy Strike (and Holy Shock) on the boss; tell raid not to heal the tank until your first Lay on Hands — [[d:paladin#1473376383173394493]], [[d:paladin#1473381732593041519]] (Thalrinel/keungzhe, 2026-02-17).
- **[Prot]** Reckoning has roughly a 1-1.5s internal cooldown by "vibes"/swing-timer observation (not confirmed via testing) — [[d:paladin#1470796875375579311]] (Destroses, single source).
- **[Prot]** Weapon speed tradeoffs on threat: faster weapons are better for Judgement of Wisdom mana return and consistent threat; slower weapons are better for Windfury, SoR, Reckoning, and the upcoming (weapon-scaling) Holy Strike — [[d:paladin#1472291743394169056]] (Grey, +1, 2026-02-15). Lok'amir il Romathis is called BiS 1h for prot until Naxx (excluding Thunderfury, which is treated as "goes without saying" BiS/not everyone has it) — [[d:paladin#1472291743394169056]], [[d:paladin#1472294665633140746]] (Grey, 2026-02-15).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Prot]** Consecration provides at most ~30-40% of total AoE threat against 3-5 targets; the primary AoE strategy is applying single-target-style threat to each mob individually while using taunt/stuns to control extras, and in large raids assigning one tank/warrior/feral per mob group — [[d:paladin#1469946685776990285]], [[d:paladin#1469946818933821595]] (Grey, 2026-02-08).
- **[Prot]** Concrete consecration-cast guidance in raids: in MC, only cast consecrate (at all) on the trash in the Geddon/Shazzrah rooms and the corehound 8-packs; in ZG, cast rank-5 consecration once per 3-5 pack; general rule of thumb: cast rank-5 consecration at most 1-2 times per AoE scenario, tuned to feel based on your threat output relative to your group — [[d:paladin#1469947064392618177]], [[d:paladin#1469947075197145244]], [[d:paladin#1469947262431133819]], [[d:paladin#1469947532175216763]] (Grey, +2, 2026-02-08).
- **[Ret AoE]** For heavy-AoE Ret specs, prioritizing Reckoning over Vengeful Strikes' 6% haste is recommended, since Reckoning procs on every block (frequent in AoE) while Zeal/haste ramps too slowly (18s to max stacks) to be worth it for short AoE packs — [[d:paladin#1470793705026879599]], [[d:paladin#1470796647268352275]], [[d:paladin#1470796724749472046]], [[d:paladin#1470796830651715595]] (Destroses/Arle, 2026-02-08).
- **[Ret/necrodin]** Necrodin is weak on trash — recommendation is to run a standard 2h SoR/SoC weapon set on trash and only swap into the necrodin (Of the Lost) set for bosses; keungzhe: viable on BWL/AQ trash given how it's pulled/set up, but not recommended on Naxx or Kara(40) trash — [[d:paladin#1472800680434598134]], [[d:paladin#1472800729499701402]], [[d:paladin#1472801404107100180]] (Destroses/keungzhe, 2026-02-15). Necrodin summoned wolves are riskier for trash pulling than ghosts (wolves run to a new target once their original dies if a same-type mob is nearby); ghosts mainly risk aggroing imps in Sunken Temple/Molten Core-style trash — [[d:paladin#1472800333422923919]] (keungzhe).
- **[Prot AoE gear]** For AoE farming, the more mobs pulled at once, the more valuable avoidance becomes relative to spellpower; lasher farming prefers SP accessories for 1-4 packs but avoidance trinkets for full-packs pulls — [[d:paladin#1473100909524287650]], [[d:paladin#1473101385997488148]] (Grey, 2026-02-17).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Hit cap: 8% at 300 weapon skill, 7% at 305/308 weapon skill; Precision talent provides 3%, so 4% from gear is needed once the weapon-skill quest/book is done — [[d:paladin#1471632460998770943]] (Grey, 2026-02-13). Prot generally only needs 6-7% hit; if reaching 8% costs meaningful stats, settle for 6-7% — [[d:paladin#1470239672809553970]], [[d:paladin#1471634507978244149]] (gastro, 2026-02-08/13).
- **[SoR ret]** Baseline miss chance vs a level-63 boss is 8%; every 5 weapon skill reduces miss by 1% (311 skill ≈ 2.2% hit-equivalent from skill alone); against same-level mobs (not bosses) only 5% hit is needed, no weapon skill required, since defense scales 5/level — [[d:paladin#1471667859276173423]], [[d:paladin#1471795975788953697]], [[d:paladin#1471796060463566900]] (Grey/Jesse Potter, 2026-02-13). SoR relies on racial + talent (Precision) + weapon-skill quest to reach hit cap, since it can't itself reduce the weapon-skill requirement the way melee gear-hit does — [[d:paladin#1471667937873105055]] (dusty).
- **[Prot leveling stat]** Int is prioritized over Strength for a leveling AoE prot: "Str is bad for blocking, you'll get way more value from int" — [[d:paladin#1471571986353356913]] (Seviry, +2, 2026-02-13). Even at 60, Strength currently does very little for prot paladin threat (only minor auto-attack damage); mana sustain (Int) drives more of prot's threat via spells — [[d:paladin#1473407195843530752]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-02-17).
- **[Holy Strike SP scaling]** Current (pre-1.18.1) HS SP coefficient ≈0.43; post-patch ≈0.71×1.1×1.25 ≈ 97.6% of spellpower total, with base damage reduced but weapon damage added — [[d:paladin#1472154514843897889]] (Grey, 2026-02-14; Grey notes he wanted a round 73% coefficient for an even 100.375%, but dev declined). CS SP coefficient ≈0.20.
- **[Threat weights, simmed]** Proph posted simmed stat-weight tables for prot paladin under the upcoming class changes (t3/K40 gear, low-buff scenario, and a second table for ~MC gear/no raid buffs); tables were shared as images/text not fully captured verbatim in this chunk, but included a weight for Block (validated by Not Normal as a reasonable threat source, with the caveat that stacking block past the avoidance cap displaces dodge/parry) — [[d:paladin#1471267740693303327]], [[d:paladin#1471283442887950448]] (Proph/Not Normal, 2026-02-12; content of the table itself not recoverable from text).
- **[Itemization budget]** Every tank item is "over budget" relative to its point cost by design (Hybrid Grace: stats cost less when they serve a role other stats on the item don't already cover) — [[d:paladin#1471988910782415029]], [[d:paladin#1471989042001481863]] (Grey, 2026-02-14). Concrete t3.5 chest example: total budget 622; 8 defense costs 84 (or 232 with 2crit before hybrid grace), providing 1.28% dodge/parry/miss/anti-crit; 23 agility + 1% crit would cost only ~178.4 combined for 1.25% avoidance (after Kings) + 2.25% crit — i.e., agi/crit stats are currently cheaper than defense for similar avoidance value — [[d:paladin#1471988446582280243]] (Grey, 2026-02-14). Paladin tank gear is comparatively worse-itemized than other tank classes' gear (e.g., warrior helm costs ~20% more budget points than the paladin equivalent; shaman tank gear was called out as reportedly much better-budgeted) — [[d:paladin#1471988555029942424]], [[d:paladin#1471988687863545947]], [[d:paladin#1471987522320990332]] (Grey/dusty, 2026-02-14; dusty: "recall seeing an image comparing our current item budgets... we are like the second worst while needing the most" — single source, unverified image not recoverable).
- **[Ret Str vs SP]** With the 1.18.1 class changes, Strength is estimated to be worth "a little less than 1/5th" of Spellpower for Ret threat/DPS purposes — needing ~70 Strength for just a 1% threat increase, which multiple posters (Proph, Grey) call out as weak stat design — [[d:paladin#1471986527763697664]], [[d:paladin#1471975992510910690]] (Proph, 2026-02-14).
- **[New stat: Fortune]** 1.18.1 introduces "Fortune," which reads "Increases your chance to trigger chance-based item effects by X%." It amplifies both PPM-based and flat-percentage item procs multiplicatively (e.g., 5% Fortune turns a 20% proc chance into 21%, not 25%), and is restricted to Necklaces, Cloaks, and Trinkets specifically because of its compounding power with multiple proc items — confirmed via an official itemization-post screenshot — [[d:paladin#1473599626522136707]] (Destroses, 2026-02-18, official text: image transcribed above). Community math/confirmation matches this multiplicative model — [[d:paladin#1472684151558635705]], [[d:paladin#1472685727790928066]], [[d:paladin#1473067696181477439]] (Grey/Beldrin/Arle, 2026-02-15/17). Sapphiron Drape is confirmed to grant 5% Fortune (first known source) — [[d:paladin#1473067879485407413]] (Arle, 2026-02-17). Fortune reportedly does not affect spell-based procs (e.g., SoC's own spell-coded proc), only item-coded chance effects — [[d:paladin#1473251598431158437]]/[[d:paladin#1473251598431158437]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-02-17); whether it affects Windfury Weapon (a shaman weapon-imbue "item-like" proc) was raised as an open question with no confirmed answer in-chunk — [[d:paladin#1473431592436236422]], [[d:paladin#1473432613619503144]] (dusty, 2026-02-18, unanswered).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Blessings]** All greater blessings cost the same mana (60 per target × Righteous Fury multiplier) and provide the same threat modifier, so "Kings spam" (using any greater blessing besides Wisdom) is mechanically interchangeable threat-wise; Kings also grants extra movement speed for utility — [[d:paladin#1469845267145822378]], [[d:paladin#1469845312666599546]], [[d:paladin#1469845313572311080]] (Grey/Proph, 2026-02-08). A remembered (unverified) tier list ranked Kings > Sanctuary > others > Salvation for a prot's own blessing choice, but Proph flags this as possibly inaccurate ("pulled out of someone's ass") — [[d:paladin#1469845709598756997]] (Proph, single source, low confidence).
- **[Blessing talents]** Improved Blessings/Kings talents are essentially mandatory for any non-prot spec at mid-to-endgame gear levels; only a low-geared SoR user benefits from skipping them for the Precision hit talent instead — [[d:paladin#1472621146586480661]], [[d:paladin#1472621224856387818]], [[d:paladin#1472622444815843591]], [[d:paladin#1472622483080216576]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-02-15); Silverhand: "fine to skip as prot, but you're genuinely trolling if you skip it as other specs" — [[d:paladin#1472622173863805139]].
- **[Trinket swapping]** Most trinkets lose their on-use buff the moment you unequip them; exception is Remains of Overwhelming Power (Kara40), which retains its buff through a swap. Standard technique: use trinket A, then swap to an unused trinket B for the next target, cycling as needed — [[d:paladin#1471617621718859949]], [[d:paladin#1471617736579874988]], [[d:paladin#1471618223140245693]], [[d:paladin#1471618440509784216]] (Mithreon/theoloras/Grey, 2026-02-13).
- **[Nefarian call]** Any paladin's Blessing of Protection or Divine Shield/Bubble cancels the paladin-targeting call/gaze mechanic on Nefarian; you don't need to precast it — [[d:paladin#1471659438925549761]] (user "30", +2, 2026-02-13), confirmed further that Blessing of Protection specifically clears the gaze on the affected target — [[d:paladin#1471658804797243627]] (Silverhand, +1).
- **[Ranged pull]** Prot tanks can ranged-pull using the Faerie Fire consumable stone (from Un'Goro) or Un'Goro-crystal grenades — [[d:paladin#1473333198573277365]], [[d:paladin#1473588771478962186]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-02-17).
- **[Buff cap]** Visible buff limit is 32, but a hard limit exists above that; buffs applied past the 32nd become invisible (stop showing an icon) yet still fully function — confirmed via direct example: Zanza's buff + a flask consumed while over the buff limit stayed invisible for 2 hours while still granting their HP/damage effects — [[d:paladin#1470483210432413898]], [[d:paladin#1473442854016782336]]/[[d:paladin#1473442854016782336]] (Grey, 2026-02-09/18). This mainly matters for anything that needs a visible buff icon to function — e.g. Druid Swiftmend fails if its triggering HoT is invisible (33rd+ buff) — [[d:paladin#1470483210432413898]] (Grey). Relogging forces invisible buffs to redisplay, and mounting up while over the buff limit is specifically flagged as risky ("god forbid you mount up when over the buff limit") — [[d:paladin#1470483576272191538]], [[d:paladin#1473442975127437555]] (Grey).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Solo tanking Garr adds]** Achievable with just MC-level gear plus a fire-immunity trinket and Greater Stoneshield Potions; positioning to only pull ranged-type adds (their death explosion is fire damage, ~4k, so keep ~5k HP buffer before finishing one off); having another paladin cast Blessing of Sacrifice on you for the pull helps a lot; Force Reactive Disk (FRD) is optional but can be a handicap if you're not overgeared (loses control over exact add death timing) — [[d:paladin#1470837697546289247]], [[d:paladin#1470839001555664967]], [[d:paladin#1470847368206483599]]/[[d:paladin#1470847368206483599]], [[d:paladin#1470847504407986196]], [[d:paladin#1470848409983385652]] (Tuna/Pastor/Silverhand, 2026-02-10).
- **[Solo tanking Firemaw]** A prot MT described soloing all of Firemaw's adds via positioning against a wall and taunting right after Wing Buffet, calling it "the only way to make that fight fun" for an overgeared raid — [[d:paladin#1469852591918682204]], [[d:paladin#1469852989673046068]] (Grey, 2026-02-08).
- **[Tank DPS comparison]** Well-geared ("BiS'd") tanks of any class comfortably reach ~1k DPS in AQ40/Naxx-level gear; average bears typically out-DPS average prot paladins by 30-50% due to more consistent rage income from being hit, not necessarily better play — [[d:paladin#1471629245720690728]], [[d:paladin#1471630067607011351]], [[d:paladin#1471631398077993105]] (Proph/Grey, 2026-02-13; concrete example: alt bear 485.2 dps/39s vs alt paladin 757.3 dps/41s on Maexxna, but a well-geared bear hit 1051.6 dps/25s vs a well-geared paladin's 1017.7 dps/41s on their best kills). General DPS parity rule: tanks output ~60% of a DPS's damage, except (non-deep) prot warrior, which does more — [[d:paladin#1471628277847298131]], [[d:paladin#1471628573457780808]] (Mithreon/Grey).
- **[Prot infinite mana]** With Blessing of Wisdom + Judgement of Wisdom + Shield Spec's mana recovery, plus Holy Strike being free, a boss-tanking prot paladin's mana bar goes UP over time as long as you avoid wasteful casts (max-rank Holy Shield, max-rank Consecration, etc.); on trash the only mana tools needed are Nordanaar Herbal Tea and Major Mana Potions, and even those can be skipped if you mostly just Holy Strike/Judgement of Righteousness — [[d:paladin#1469939857630695569]], [[d:paladin#1469940162711654472]] (Grey, 2026-02-08). Keep a rank-1 Consecration on your bar at all times: its ~8% SP scaling per tick means in good SP gear (e.g. ~950 unbuffed SP) it can hit for more than a baseline rank-5 tick (~90/tick, 1440 threat for 120 mana) — [[d:paladin#1469935800379506953]] (Grey, 2026-02-08). Below ~30% mana while boss tanking, switch to rank-1 Consecration until back to ~50% — [[d:paladin#1470119581581312222]] (Proph, 2026-02-08).
- **[Prot threat math]** JoR is compared favorably to a warrior's Concussion Blow: with only ~475 SP, 5/5 Improved SoR, and Sanctuary Aura, JoR does as much threat as a 2000 AP warrior's 5/5 One-Handed-Spec, battle-stanced Concussion Blow — [[d:paladin#1471604228664594495]], [[d:paladin#1471605216397885471]] (Grey, +2, 2026-02-13; caveat: crit rates differ between classes, closing the gap somewhat).
- **[Naxx trash threat bar]** Example: Naxx gargoyles need only ~30 DPS worth of damage output to require ~6.6k threat per player after Salvation is applied evenly — trivial for a paladin tank to hold — [[d:paladin#1471599638787391621]] (Grey, 2026-02-13).
- **[Solo Stratholme]** Achievable as Holy spec via Holy Shock reset from Crusader Strike + Seal of Wisdom mana sustain; big pull farming needs T2+ gear, FRD, and 10%+ vampirism; not very gold-efficient due to high repair costs (~160g/hr before a vamp nerf, ~2 repairs needed clearing from the back entrance to Unforgiven, killing the 3 big UD packs alone costs ~-15g) — [[d:paladin#1471539122912104649]], [[d:paladin#1471543209061515385]], [[d:paladin#1471543384077242409]], [[d:paladin#1471543640223518750]] (Pastor/Beldrin, 2026-02-12).
- **[Vagrant/hardcore-style challenges]** Prot is considered decent for the Vagrant Challenge; Blessing of Sanctuary is called "kinda broken" for it; specs that depend heavily on weapon upgrades to do damage (i.e. SoC) are poor challenge picks — [[d:paladin#1471545516751061173]], [[d:paladin#1471545888995414120]] (Beldrin, 2026-02-12).
- **[Prot shock-tank builds]** Grey posted example talent screenshots for a "shock tank" style build: a 21/30/0 (Holy/Prot/Ret) variant explicitly called not recommended for serious progression but "functional for most purposes," and a deeper 34/17/0 Holy/Prot variant recommended specifically for dungeon tanking where less single-target threat is needed — [[d:paladin#1473097673476276245]], [[d:paladin#1473100475137003561]], [[d:paladin#1473100560717713440]] (Grey, 2026-02-17; exact talent names not legible from the screenshots, point totals only).
- **[Vamp gear feels inconsistent]** Multiple players reported vampirism/leech-based sustain (FRD, vamp gear) randomly feeling much weaker than expected after logging in; fix is to re-equip your full gear set (and shield) after logging on — a known old issue also affected Pursuit of Justice not working if talented before logging in — [[d:paladin#1472598188983713856]], [[d:paladin#1472890213679239198]], [[d:paladin#1472890517430997024]], [[d:paladin#1472890892988846254]] (Howler/Jesse Potter/Grey, 2026-02-15).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy stat priority]** Healing power is the top stat, but Crit is also valued (feeds Illumination and Daybreak); Haste is comparatively weak for holy paladins because Flash of Light's 1.5s base cast already matches the paladin GCD, unlike e.g. shaman's longer 2-2.5s casts where haste has more room to matter — [[d:paladin#1471283802721751215]], [[d:paladin#1471284013133205617]], [[d:paladin#1471284116430393487]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-12).
- **[Armor-to-healing talent]** A holy talent (2 points) converts armor on gear into effective healing power at roughly 50 armor = 1 healing power (e.g., a 2500-armor shield ≈ 50 healing power baseline before other stats) — [[d:paladin#1471283527336198185]], [[d:paladin#1471286458932527104]], [[d:paladin#1471286547763433515]] (Mithreon/Silverhand, 2026-02-12); with this talent, gear priority becomes "+healing and high armor" rather than pure int/spirit cloth — [[d:paladin#1471283513360777439]] (Flammekat).
- **[Holy solo-healing capacity]** A T3.5-geared holy paladin can reliably solo-heal a 10-12 man group; 20-man solo healing is less reliable — [[d:paladin#1471615533156925614]], [[d:paladin#1471616618407792920]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-13). Estimated healer counts for K40 (pre-1.18.1 changes): 5-7 healers + 2 shadow priests are typically enough depending on the fight, with 8 healers + 2 spriests considered overkill — [[d:paladin#1471630603601444957]], [[d:paladin#1471630687613485157]], [[d:paladin#1471631047333515305]] (Mithreon/Proph, 2026-02-13).
- **[Holy Strike as heal]** Holy Strike is used by holy specs as a free AoE "smart heal" that can crit and proc Daybreak — used ~98% of the time over Crusader Strike, which is reserved for zero-AoE-damage windows — [[d:paladin#1471931304911311039]], [[d:paladin#1472677287362039878]] (Beldrin/Grey/Arle, 2026-02-11/15).
- **[1.18.1 Holy nerf]** Daybreak's crit-scaling is being reduced ~30% in the class-change pass, which will noticeably lower top-end "parser" Holy Shock/Holy Strike healing numbers, though it's not expected to change raid roster requirements much — [[d:paladin#1472912765759783047]], [[d:paladin#1472949626570277027]], [[d:paladin#1472914903940595722]], [[d:paladin#1472947424510087310]] (Mithreon/angels1m, disputed severity, 2026-02-15/16; Thalrinel separately warns "the majority here are sleeping on how rough these Holy changes are" — [[d:paladin#1472948213555265700]]).
- **[Holy leveling]** Leveling as pure Holy is weak/incomplete before Holy Shock/Blessed Strikes unlock; recommendation is Holy/Prot for Spiritual Focus instead, since Flash of Light spam is the only real tool early — see Leveling section above for full citations.

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Kara40 Ravens]** Sanctuary Aura vs Improved Retribution Aura for ravens-add aggro is debated: total generated threat is fixed regardless of talent, but gets diluted the more mobs are present, so single-target Sanctuary threat isn't as relevant here; stronger argument exists for Improved Ret Aura specifically on Gnarlmoon and Incantagos; the guild's actual strategy is having red-side healers remove Salvation from themselves near ravens so blessing-spam alone (rather than aura choice) keeps ravens off healers — [[d:paladin#1470219458680459356]], [[d:paladin#1470220250296356987]], [[d:paladin#1470220492345446565]], [[d:paladin#1470220595814990024]] (Aelgagar/Claank, 2026-02-08).
- **[Kara40 pre-Medivh packs]** Repeatedly cited as the biggest rage-inducer for guilds: no fixed correct strategy exists, the wipes stem mostly from execution errors (someone pulling extra packs, LoSing healers, or a warlock/elemental shaman pet accidentally pulling a third pack), though pet-pulls have become significantly less common over recent months — [[d:paladin#1471659691519115327]], [[d:paladin#1471660146311823493]] (Grey, 2026-02-13).
- **[Nefarian]** Any paladin's Blessing of Protection or Divine Shield/Bubble cancels the "call"/gaze targeting mechanic without needing to precast it (see Cooldowns section).
- **[BWL]** Vael, Broodlord, and Chromaggus are cited as the first bosses where having a Titans-tier flask genuinely matters; Broodlord's ability to stack a 5k Mortal Strike on top of a 2k crit auto and a Blast Wave is called out as especially dangerous — [[d:paladin#1471657583826702366]], [[d:paladin#1471657743537410232]] (Grey, 2026-02-13).
- **[Sunken Temple / Hakkar event]** Only killing "sons of Hakkar" during the event grants a chance at an extra blood; using the event-trigger item a second time can cause Hakkar's next-lockout summon to bug out — [[d:paladin#1470106694947307703]], [[d:paladin#1470106768620261562]] (angels1m, 2026-02-08).
- **[MC fire resistance]** See Gear section: Dark Iron Helmet + Legs + Onyxia Scale Cloak (all fire-res enchanted) → 201 fire res / ~47.8% average reduction after totem/aura for MT/OT duty; aim for ~280 vs Ragnaros specifically — [[d:paladin#1473110127061237811]] (Grey, 2026-02-17).
- **[Black Morass]** Even in near-BiS gear (T2.5, AQ40 shield) with a well-geared healer, Black Morass's dragon 4-packs before the bridge are dangerous enough to want CC (sleep) on one; on a fresh bear tank (8k armor/6k HP) Greater Stoneshield potions were needed on the final boss even with a T2-geared healer; the two final-boss adds (Magmadar-analog hitting ~2722 before armor with an armor-reducing fire DoT, and a fear-based add hitting similarly) are the main threats; CC one mob per pack is standard advice for a fresh-60 group — [[d:paladin#1472703161687998494]], [[d:paladin#1472703232835850345]], [[d:paladin#1472703383554228326]], [[d:paladin#1472704160095797258]], [[d:paladin#1472702745638211737]] (Grey/Lantere, 2026-02-15).

## PvP
- **[Dampening]** PvP has damage and healing dampeners that make niche builds (e.g. Shockadin) even less viable than they'd otherwise be — [[d:paladin#1469979336156250299]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-08; direct answer to a Shockadin-viability question, no further detail given).
- **[Contacts, not builds]** When asked who plays PvP paladin, the channel could only point to two named players (Excinerus, Lightbourne) rather than share an actual build — no PvP talent template was posted despite being requested at least twice in this chunk (Pescot, Flowers) — [[d:paladin#1471710411983163514]] (Destroses, 2026-02-08; open question, unanswered).
- **[Ret PvP]** Ret paladins on the (PvP) realm reportedly get deleted in 2-3 hits by burst classes, especially shaman, with no strong anti-purge tool; the community's typical answer is "you have a parry talent" / accept the dampening — [[d:paladin#1472659024351592731]], [[d:paladin#1472659580852109515]], [[d:paladin#1472659945802563664]] (Sunmourn/Silverhand, 2026-02-15; casual complaint thread, not a build guide).
- **[Historical]** WFT lost some AP and gained a higher ICD compared to some earlier version; the whole paladin class is described as "balanced around" reliance on Windfury Totem, which is raised as a long-standing PvP/faction-balance complaint (Alliance/paladins on faction-imbalanced or Horde-favoring servers "shafted" without shaman support) — [[d:paladin#1473257013923414048]], [[d:paladin#1473256010763210828]], [[d:paladin#1473256684460708033]] (Jesse Potter/Destroses/Southshore Crusader Vidnar, 2026-02-17; opinion/complaint thread).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Auto-attack]** Turtle's client is based on the actual vanilla client (not Blizzard's later-modified Classic client), so it lacks the "continue attacking after target switch" behavior added in later expansions — after a kill, auto-attack drops and must be manually restarted (via the attack button or another attack ability) — [[d:paladin#1469959136027349156]] (Grey, 2026-02-08). Retail-style mouseover macros don't work; use RoidMacros (or variants) for vanilla-client macro functionality — [[d:paladin#1469959136027349156]] (Grey).
- **[Stat addons]** BetterCharacterStats (get "an actually good fork") is recommended for gear/hit/haste tracking, alongside StatCompare for haste specifically; both can be wrong about weapon-skill-derived hit reduction (addons often don't account for the weapon-skill bonus, requiring manual math) — [[d:paladin#1471632910586216620]], [[d:paladin#1471777441809698938]], [[d:paladin#1471779029399900170]], [[d:paladin#1472212057771479215]], [[d:paladin#1472213440109215858]] (Grey/dumbid/Arle/Kagero, 2026-02-13/15). StatCompare's displayed haste value was called out as possibly wrong/additive rather than multiplicative — [[d:paladin#1472215975888687256]], [[d:paladin#1472219962713313311]] (Excinerus/Mithreon, 2026-02-14).
- **[Conditional macros]** True conditional macros (e.g., "cast Holy Strike if Holy Might has <8s remaining") require RoidMacros/CleverRoidMacros/SuperCleverRoidMacros plus Nampower and SuperWoW — vanilla WoW's native macro system cannot check remaining buff duration; example given: `/cast [mybuff:"Holy_Might"<8] Holy Strike` — [[d:paladin#1471019950943764582]] (Drekalo, 2026-02-11), [[d:paladin#1471535071641997444]], [[d:paladin#1471536812756566037]] (Nazeth/Pastor, 2026-02-12).
- **[Trinket-on-CD macro]** For using an on-use trinket alongside Holy Strike (e.g. REOS trinket): `/cast Holy Strike(Rank 8)` then `/use 13`, requiring SuperMacro (not simply vanilla macro syntax) — [[d:paladin#1471334530467237938]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-02-11).
- **[BoP/Bubble cleanup macro]** To cast Divine Protection/BoP and immediately clear the buff off yourself: `/cast Divine Protection(Rank 1)` followed by a script looping `GetPlayerBuff`/`CancelPlayerBuff` to strip the "Spell_Holy_Restoration" buff texture; alternatively, use the VCB addon bound to instantly remove the buff — [[d:paladin#1473192625556291686]], [[d:paladin#1473192824253055120]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-02-17).
- **[Useful addons]** Puppeteer — mouseover healing and binding BoP/Cleanse — [[d:paladin#1471870338366177280]] (Lazepally, 2026-02-13). PallyPower — tracking/managing 10-min blessing buffs on party/raid — [[d:paladin#1472391911665958992]] (Seviry, 2026-02-14). "What's Training" — checking trainer skill unlocks while leveling — [[d:paladin#1471917059725328484]] (Flammekat, 2026-02-11). DoiteAuras — tracking your own buff durations while leveling — [[d:paladin#1472640810053795910]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-15).
- **[Blessing management habit]** Buff the whole party/raid at once so everyone's 10-min blessing timer lines up, making it easy to track just your own buff's remaining duration instead of everyone's — [[d:paladin#1472388255998808064]] (Silverhand, 2026-02-15).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Race]** For Holy or Prot, race has effectively no performance impact beyond flavor, even at BiS; Ret leans toward Human for the sword/mace weapon-skill racial, but it mainly just smooths early weapon-skill progression rather than being mandatory — [[d:paladin#1472437813579612252]], [[d:paladin#1472437935440793703]], [[d:paladin#1472438028244095060]] (Jesse Potter, 2026-02-15).
- **[Engineering]** Recommended for Holy/dual-spec paladins specifically to enable farming as an offspec — [[d:paladin#1472001080534761583]] (Arle, 2026-02-15).
- **[Gold farming]** Sunken Temple ST-chest farming for necrodin is slow (~8-9 min/run) and only nets a small gold trickle; Lashers farming nets roughly 35g/hr with average vamp/FRD setups, historically up to ~100g/hr before a vampirism nerf and item-price crash; Force Reactive Disk (FRD) is described as near-mandatory for efficient AoE farming outside of Lashers, along with a mobile repair bot — [[d:paladin#1470403247301726376]], [[d:paladin#1471652401516646480]], [[d:paladin#1471654609750724803]], [[d:paladin#1471651192915492969]] (Arle/Flammekat/Nabwek, 2026-02-09/12/13).
- **[ZG mining]** 2 arcane-crystal veins per ZG run via mining is a solid gold source, and specifically recommended over Stratholme farming due to Strat's high repair costs — [[d:paladin#1471539414118174830]], [[d:paladin#1471651831578099864]] (Pastor/Silverhand, 2026-02-12).
- **[Elemental invasions]** Random world spawns (fire/air/water/earth) drop essence items; water and fire are currently the most profitable — [[d:paladin#1471657936043380737]] (Nabwek, 2026-02-13).
- **[Skullflame Shield]** Widely called overhyped/meme-tier by multiple posters (dusty, Silverhand) — better tanking and farming options exist despite its reputation among newer/streamer-influenced players — [[d:paladin#1471656467261165569]], [[d:paladin#1471656403260407869]] (dusty, 2026-02-12).
- **[Craftsman-challenge gearing]** See Leveling section: Blacksmith-only gearing is very weapon-poor from level 30-50; Holy/Prot recommended over Ret for this challenge, plus temporary Jewelcrafting for +12 stam/+13 holy dmg/+12 int trinkets (~180 JC skill) before dropping for mining.

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Buff cap]** Visible cap is 32; extra buffs apply invisibly but still function (see Cooldowns section for the Zanza+Flask example and Swiftmend interaction). Relogging re-displays invisible buffs; mounting while over-cap is specifically flagged as risky.
- **[Concoction stacking bug]** Confirmed and later officially patched — "Fixed an issue causing Alchemy concoctions to not stack correctly" (1.18.1 patch notes) — see Consumables section for the full testing thread and Grey's shared-spell-ID explanation.
- **[Vamp/FRD feels reduced after login]** Multiple reports of leech/lifesteal effects (FRD, vamp gear) randomly feeling far weaker after logging in; fix is re-equipping the full gear set (including shield); an older related bug had Pursuit of Justice not function if it was already talented at login — [[d:paladin#1472598188983713856]], [[d:paladin#1472890892988846254]], [[d:paladin#1472891085326909450]] (Howler/Jesse Potter/Grey, 2026-02-15).
- **[Weapon-speed WFT interaction]** WFT's true internal cooldown (2s, not the assumed 1.5s) means very fast weapons (Thunderfury) can't get consecutive procs, which was previously an unrecognized "popular belief" error about TF's proc optimality — [[d:paladin#1471156719684944004]] through [[d:paladin#1471157031812731047]], [[d:paladin#1472398442709123256]] (Adamant/Destroses, 2026-02-11/15).
- **[Windfury swing "clipping"]** Perceived lower auto-attack damage after Windfury procs is normal (temporary weapon-enchant swing behavior), not a bug — see Consumables section.
- **[Loot table % confusion]** Atlas Loot percentages can sum past 100% because they represent multiple separate loot tables/pools on one mob, not simple flat drop odds — a repeated point of confusion for players trying to infer true drop rates (e.g., a ZF libram farmer questioning repeat drops) — [[d:paladin#1471041350991483022]], [[d:paladin#1471044977428660274]] (TRUE HEIR/Kay9Cop, 2026-02-11; addon/UX confusion, not a game bug).
- **[Necrodin trinket interaction]** Necrodin ghosts don't benefit from Greater Shadow Power Elixir (confirmed tested: "It doesn't. For anyone interested") — [[d:paladin#1472683556705927289]] (Arle, 2026-02-15).
