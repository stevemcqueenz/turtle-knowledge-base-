## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** New **Fortune** stat (Neck/Cloak/Trinket slots only) increases proc chance multiplicatively, confirmed not additive — e.g. 25% Fortune × 5% base proc = 6.25%; for ppm-based procs, convert ppm to per-hit % via weapon speed first — [[d:paladin#1489243794325901342]] (Excinerus), [[d:paladin#1490132689586749611]] (Destroses).
- **[General]** Fortune is confirmed to affect item procs (TF, Flurry Axe, Ironfoe, Pysan's) but does **not** affect a set's own proc trigger rate (T1 8pc, T2 3pc procs are themselves unaffected by Fortune); it does buff the proc chance of *other* items that those set-bonus hits then trigger (e.g. a T2 3pc hit landing improves Sigil of Ancient Accord's own proc chance) — [[d:paladin#1489221331936612474]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1490072654059667497]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Contested]** Whether Fortune affects the "Lost" set (necro summon) procs specifically stayed unresolved all week: some cite a dev confirming on the necro Discord that Fortune does work on set-bonus effects incl. Lost set — [[d:paladin#1490078096198729851]] (theoloras/warkrak retracts a prior "doesn't work" claim) — but Destroses maintains it's "not hard confirmed," and Droplet of Nordrassil currently doesn't get boosted by Fortune (bug or intended, unclear) — [[d:paladin#1490224351025299568]], [[d:paladin#1490224416351588374]], and Seviry later found an old bug report suggesting it *should* work on set bonuses — [[d:paladin#1491486384643379310]].
- **[Seal of Command]** SoC per-swing proc chance was buffed this patch to roughly 9 PPM / ~50% at 3.8 speed (was lower); a new SoC-only libram (Libram of Ardour, +1 PPM) also released — [[d:paladin#1489585274165006507]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1490465807715668209]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1490136296369164309]] (Destroses).
- **[SoC vs SoR]** SoC narrowed the DPS gap with SoR this patch (previously ~200-300 dps behind); on Nord K40 logs a SoC player sat 2nd, ~3 dps behind a SoR player, with a pre-patch SoR player (Lonsell) still ahead overall — [[d:paladin#1490461028348067841]], [[d:paladin#1490461574010372107]], [[d:paladin#1490461802822373696]].
- **[Necrodin/SoR]** SoR itself counts as a full "auto attack" for proc purposes — each swing under SoR effectively rolls twice, and with both T2 3pc (ret+prot) worn you get 4 spellstrike-equivalent triggers per swing — [[d:paladin#1490202199756902430]], [[d:paladin#1490202721737773158]], [[d:paladin#1490218179593764997]] (Destroses).
- **[Holy]** Holy Strike's SP scaling was roughly doubled this patch (from ~45% to ~70%), making it the preferred SoR-SP filler over Crusader Strike for many builds — [[d:paladin#1489458703450116347]] (Destroses).
- **[Bug, major]** Holy Strike's AoE "smart heal" (heals injured targets in range) has been broken for 2+ weeks — instead of healing ~5 people it healed only 1-2. Lhian downplayed it as a small bug; a restart on 04-07/08 "fixed" it, but the fix changed HS from a true smart-heal (prioritizes most-injured) into a "semi-smart" heal that only checks binary damaged/not-damaged state, so it can still land on someone barely hurt while a critical ally stands nearby — and **Lhian confirmed it will not go back to the old fully-smart behavior**, calling that overtuned to begin with. Net result: players report HS healing for noticeably less (e.g. hitting for 2k instead of 3k) and feeling barely better than Flash of Light — [[d:paladin#1490431443078221895]], [[d:paladin#1490433767993511949]] (Sookie), [[d:paladin#1490442443940696288]] (Lhian), [[d:paladin#1491673171286298634]] (Golthor: "still heals full-hp people while there is injured people around"), [[d:paladin#1491683004093960273]] (Destroses confirms it was intentionally made "only semi smart").
- **[Bug, major, root cause found]** Separately, Holy Shock was healing ~20% less than its own tooltip math predicted (e.g. calc'd 1844 crit heal at 1093 healpower vs. observed 1470-1530; base-value-only casts with 0 healpower matched fine, so the healpower coefficient itself was broken, suspected dropped from 0.43 to ~0.26). Root cause confirmed by Mithreon at the very end of the window: **the new Argent Dawn Holy Shock libram itself was introducing the 20% healing loss — equipping it paradoxically restored the correct numbers** — [[d:paladin#1491134213041160342]], [[d:paladin#1491142158554628116]], [[d:paladin#1491684703131013261]], [[d:paladin#1491862816531939328]] (Mithreon: "It's a bug of 20% lost healing that the new AD libram introduced. If you equip it you get back to the original Shock numbers"), [[d:paladin#1491862875927609445]].
- **[Bug, big, temporary]** Flametongue Totem (FTT) accidentally regained full spellpower scaling (formula ≈ instant-cast coefficient, ~40-43%) after a Fiery Weapon enchant coefficient buff intended for 1.18.1 finally shipped late and "leaked" into the totem. This devs had specifically removed a while back to stop a "TF + Lantern/FTT" Paladin meta. It produced absurd numbers for SoR/Necrodin Paladins (single totem contributing 250-650 dps, ~14-38k damage per Patchwerk pull, "top damage source" for some SoR players) and inflated several parses; still not resolved as of 04-09/10 despite a promised hotfix, with one adjustment reported (flat 40% → weapon-speed-based) that didn't fully fix the overperformance. Community wish: keep some FTT SP scaling (~5-15%) but restore FTT/WFT mutual exclusivity — [[d:paladin#1490888484725067949]] (Not Normal), [[d:paladin#1490874285387022426]]–[[d:paladin#1491085085649272892]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1491769384052457575]] (Kedarunzi explains the original design intent), [[d:paladin#1491785663388057755]], [[d:paladin#1491828398287356036]] (still broken 04-09), [[d:paladin#1491943164796211344]] ("T3 already took a hit" from a related nerf).
- **[Bug]** Bosses reportedly can now parry from behind — [[d:paladin#1490447884259164312]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Bug]** Buff-cap: buffs beyond the visible limit still function mechanically, but macros/UI addons (and a few hard-coded interactions) can fail to recognize them; a patch note mid-week added "Buffs exceeding the limit will now become visible once space is available" as a partial fix (screenshot) — [[d:paladin#1490666870653583430]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1491160194061828297]] (Gilfrey, patch-note screenshot), [[d:paladin#1491187372811882496]] (Claank).
- **[Blessing of Sacrifice]** Confirmed intentional: damage taken via Blessing of Sacrifice no longer breaks the target out of CC, changed months ago in anticipation of arenas — [[d:paladin#1489674002254528573]] (theoloras/warkrak).
- **[Reckoning]** Does not store up to 4 charges like classic; on trigger you instantly auto-attack the blocked attacker if within 8 yards (even with autoattack toggled off), functioning like a "parry haste for blocking" instead. Ranged-attack-triggered procs don't carry into melee range. Confirmed working as intended/long-standing by veteran players, designed around PvE not PvP — [[d:paladin#1490576034641346570]] (Xiyanzhe, detailed writeup), [[d:paladin#1490577420443582474]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1490578447603597312]] (Destroses: the reckoning-triggered attack does include seal effects, per old testing). **Seal twisting is confirmed removed / not possible** in Turtle WoW — [[d:paladin#1490036310508048567]] (Gilfrey).
- **[TF history]** Thunderfury's proc rate was raised to 4 PPM in the "Gilneas release patch" then nerfed back to 3 PPM within a week (pre-1.18.1 history, cited for context); TF has no internal cooldown — [[d:paladin#1489800557085593721]], [[d:paladin#1490109349886951615]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Scythe of the Harvest]** Base proc rate was nerfed from 3ppm to 2ppm at some point, and its old Crusader-Strike-fishing interaction was removed — [[d:paladin#1489851836478525510]] (Istenneveben).
- **[Nefarian]** Now casts a "Paladin class call" style debuff set on players mimicking Blessings: reported effects include ~20% more damage taken, a mana drain, ~10% reduced stats, -300 AP, and +50% threat (exact assignment to each debuff uncertain/possibly reversed) — [[d:paladin#1489839107264745582]], [[d:paladin#1489840330592489523]], [[d:paladin#1491880297145434262]].
- **[AP Ret 6pc]** T2.5's old 6-piece set bonus was "obliterated" (nerfed) some time ago; since then almost no Ret goes beyond 4 pieces of that set — [[d:paladin#1491154254562459788]], [[d:paladin#1491154330437288090]] (Beldrin).
- **[Ret history]** In patch 1.18, Ret got SP moved onto tier gear (buff) while Dragonbreath Chili got hit hard, around when Rogue became the dominant PvE DPS; in 1.18.1 Ret is climbing back with buffs while Rogue got a slight nerf — [[d:paladin#1491703593492025465]]–[[d:paladin#1491703796093685892]] (Mithreon).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** All 3 specs (Holy/Prot/Ret) are viable at endgame; Holy is considered the strongest relative to competing classes in its role — [[d:paladin#1489354632395358258]], [[d:paladin#1489356022106558606]] (gastro).
- **[Ret PvE]** Ret DPS in K40 is roughly on par with Druids/Shamans, a bit ahead of Priests (the "1k club" for boss overall dps in a 1:30 K40 run), but behind top specs (Affliction Lock, Arcane Mage, Ele Shaman, Spriest, MM Hunter). Ret's damage is heavily gated by ramp-up time (buffs/procs) that's hard to establish on a fresh pull — [[d:paladin#1489508110749139024]], [[d:paladin#1489512682787704926]], [[d:paladin#1489514178191556740]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1489505194877390890]].
- **[Ret PvP]** Community view: Holy is more valued but Ret is "fine"; the bar is low since many Ret players underperform; Ret PvP needs more setup/lineup than Prot — [[d:paladin#1490035729718313032]], [[d:paladin#1490035837331570892]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1490469589543292928]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1491794360658821320]] (Silverhand: "98% of rets are dead weight" — biased/hyperbolic single source).
- **[Prot PvP]** Deep Prot with Thunderfury is extremely strong even with 0 consumables/no dedicated PvP gear ("top of leaderboard 9/10 matches"); can beat 1v4 physical comps by default in pure PvE tank gear. Community expects a nerf — [[d:paladin#1490469775988494518]], [[d:paladin#1490470082948632746]], [[d:paladin#1490470328374399117]] (Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1490470975664554085]] (Mithreon).
- **[Necrodin]** "Necroshock" (Holy Shock-focused hybrid using the Lost-set summon-ghost proc) is newly viable and strong for both PvE and PvP this patch, reaching 750-800 self-buffed dps on a raid dummy — [[d:paladin#1490162410257125466]], [[d:paladin#1490162750775758929]] (Agnesia).
- **[Contested] Pure "Shockadin" (Holy Shock-spam Ret/hybrid)**: enthusiasts reported strong short-parse numbers this patch (600+ dps undergeared, 61% unbuffed Holy Shock crit chance) after the new AD Holy Shock libram — [[d:paladin#1491510594140901377]], [[d:paladin#1491512719147401237]] (Karadra) — but veteran players (gastro, Beldrin, both self-described 5-year paladin mains) strongly dispute the viability based on 30-second dummy tests, note it won't get Judgement-of-Wisdom/Vengeance benefits, will OOM hard without a 2nd paladin's JoW + a Windfury totem, and say the spec has "already been tested" and found wanting over full (10-min+) parses — [[d:paladin#1491512962421100594]], [[d:paladin#1491513869800177806]], [[d:paladin#1491516594269065338]], [[d:paladin#1491516669305028799]]. A hybrid "half-necrodin" shock build (not pure spam) reported a more modest but credible ~550 dps on a dummy — [[d:paladin#1491517863318786148]] (turdfergeson). Standard SpellRet (SoR) is still considered the stronger overall PvE Ret build; pure Shockadin is "decent now" but "still isn't the best" — [[d:paladin#1491000685909643264]] (Destroses).

## Leveling
- **[Role choice]** Paladin can fill any of the 3 roles while leveling; not recommended to level as Holy (harder); recommended to level as DPS/tank and keep a spare gear set for the eventual raid role since talents matter less while leveling and gear alone can fill most roles — [[d:paladin#1489354632395358258]], [[d:paladin#1489356260376711308]], [[d:paladin#1489358762929295640]], [[d:paladin#1489359051920773120]] (gastro).
- **[Prot leveling]** Prot Paladin is one of the strongest leveling specs — [[d:paladin#1489343848537784440]] (gastro).
- **[Prot leveling, lvl 29]** Gear for Stam/Int/Spellpower; with enough Int you can pull a full group using Seal/Judgement of Light and never go oom (more Int just delays, doesn't remove, the need to regen) — [[d:paladin#1489343059526156389]], [[d:paladin#1489345109035188366]], [[d:paladin#1489345586929864734]] (gastro/Beldrin). SP gear is obtainable this early via enchants: +30 SP weapon enchant (even on a level-1 sword) and +12 SP bracer enchant — [[d:paladin#1489343418550456350]] (L-star).
- **[Ret leveling basics]** New/returning players: keep up Seal + a Blessing, hit Holy Strike when available; use Holy Strike to maintain the Holy Might buff, Crusader Strike as filler; at low level (~29) mostly just alternate CS/HS and use whichever heals when needed; Judgement of Wisdom/Light become the mana/health "bread and butter" once unlocked — [[d:paladin#1489354632395358258]] (bela), [[d:paladin#1491895229068873849]], [[d:paladin#1491895729139093654]], [[d:paladin#1491895973834785009]] (Isvash). Seal of Righteousness is the main leveling seal before level 30; Seal of Command becomes the main seal after 30; Seal of Wisdom for mana; Seal of Light when healing is needed — [[d:paladin#1491887233761017987]] (Mithreon).
- **[Ret leveling mana]** Fresh-60 Ret in dungeons: core mana fix is understanding spellstrikes — items adding magic damage to attacks (e.g. Ring of Electrical Binding) proc off both autos and Seal of Righteousness (double benefit under SoR), and those hits can trigger Judgement of Wisdom for mana — [[d:paladin#1490471390107795607]], [[d:paladin#1490471519263133736]]–[[d:paladin#1490472028107575377]] (Silverhand/Gillarias).
- **[Dungeon role]** New player (lvl 16) leveling 2H DPS asked healer-vs-DD for queue; advice: DD is the "safe bet" unless you already have int-heavy healing gear for most slots — [[d:paladin#1489351485266591854]], [[d:paladin#1489352102902759466]], [[d:paladin#1489352632052088902]] (Dodging).
- **[Twinking, open]** 49 twink Holy Paladin PvP build/gear/talents requested — no concrete answer given; 49 twinking is gated behind a long Magram (Turtle-specific) reputation grind — [[d:paladin#1489230536072499342]], [[d:paladin#1489232951156674664]] (Dodging).
- **[Twink weapons, 49 bracket]** For a lvl-49 2H PvP twink, min-damage matters most (not raw dps or speed alone); named options: Pendulum of Doom (lvl39 BoE axe, Uldaman, contested/rare), Blackrock Slicer (53 BoP — too high level), Brand of Lohengrim (found at the bottom of one of two lakes near a dragon skeleton in Winterspring-like zone, "so-so"), plus cheaper ZF/Maraudon/Uldaman/crafted Thorium Greatsword alternatives, or buying "Kang" off the AH (~100-150g) — [[d:paladin#1491315101179842642]]–[[d:paladin#1491331072020185088]] (various), [[d:paladin#1490596127102337094]] (Antur).
- **[Weapon skill]** Must visit a weapon master in a capital city to learn new weapon types; Paladins can use all melee weapons except daggers and staves — [[d:paladin#1489679888867921961]], [[d:paladin#1489680031461675068]] (Silverhand).
- **[Tanking while leveling]** Any spec can tank with Righteous Fury up as long as the group doesn't overboard AoE-pull; with ≥13 points into Prot for threat talents you can tank effectively with even a 2H equipped if your healer allows — [[d:paladin#1490594732144066661]], [[d:paladin#1490595018640064592]] (Xiyanzhe).
- **[Enemies partially resist Holy dmg above your level]** A leveling mechanic flagged (screenshot); unclear if it interacts correctly with Consecration (may be a bug) — [[d:paladin#1491614195081281647]], [[d:paladin#1491616195504246844]] (Seviry).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Holy]** Base rotation: Holy Strike whenever available, Flash of Light spam as filler, Holy Shock reserved for someone about to die who can't wait on a FoL cast; same rough approach in dungeons and raids, though melee-weaving is "arguably more important for 5-mans than raids" currently — [[d:paladin#1489753323253923850]], [[d:paladin#1489753379583557704]], [[d:paladin#1489754231773593762]] (Grey/Jesse Potter/Xallog).
- **[Holy melee-weaving]** At raid level, Holy Paladins should melee-weave for mana regen; correctly executed, "holy melee paladin never gets out of mana" — Holy Strike weaved with Flash of Light casts is the core loop — [[d:paladin#1489691651713663047]], [[d:paladin#1489691721964065038]] (Mithreon/Ragnardbd).
- **[Holy, lower raids]** In easier raids, melee-weaving gives a roughly free (0-mana) smart heal every 4th global via Holy Strike, can proc Seal of Wisdom or contribute damage via SoR, plus keep up a judgement (Wisdom/Crusade/Light) and Consecration rank 1 with a libram that grants uncapped AoE targets — must know the fight to avoid AoE silence/damage — [[d:paladin#1489557484732682260]] (bela).
- **[Holy, hard-hitting fights]** On heavy-damage fights like Vael, pure cast-heal spam wins: "3 FoL and 1 HS rotate" — casting interrupts autoattack, so autos only land when HS lands — [[d:paladin#1489558047897686076]], [[d:paladin#1489558294707048508]] (Mithreon).
- **[Holy Strike crit mechanics, confirmed]** HS's *damage* crit chance is based on Melee crit; its *healing* portion has its own, separate crit roll based on Spell crit — they are not tied together — [[d:paladin#1492019095888199791]], [[d:paladin#1492019326264283356]] (dusty).
- **[Ret SoR mana]** With no Windfury/Flurry Totem, a 1H weapon needs 1-2 spellstrike procs to sustain mana via JoW; a 2H needs 2-3. Fiery and Lifestealing weapon enchants both count as valid spellstrikes for this ("always been a thing here") — [[d:paladin#1489845996233031700]], [[d:paladin#1489846712100061255]] (theoloras/warkrak). Concrete example: Ring of Electrical Binding + JoW + SoR gives 4 chances at mana per swing (melee hit + ring proc + SoR proc + ring-off-SoR proc), each JoW hit worth +59 mana — [[d:paladin#1491928884722274324]] (Excinerus).
- **[Ret mana escalation]** Grey's mana-fix priority order for a spell-heavy/shock build (stop once fixed — going past step 3 signals a real problem): 1) more teas/mana pots, 2) downrank Consecration to rank 1, 3) use Holy Strike as filler instead of Crusader Strike, 4) stop casting Judgement of Righteousness, 5) stop casting Consecration entirely — [[d:paladin#1489847158504165416]].
- **[Necroshock/Shockadin mana]** Agnesia solved Necroshock's sustain via a Lifestealing weapon enchant + Consecration rank 4 (sweet spot, ~100% mana sustained in a typical parse); Crimson Spellblade also helps — [[d:paladin#1490048685000753172]], [[d:paladin#1490081713198469293]], [[d:paladin#1490209130005397534]]. Separately, when actually playing Shockadin you need a different rotation to sustain Holy Shock's mana cost: use Crusader Strike instead of Holy Strike as filler since CS "refunds itself" — [[d:paladin#1491002673393176678]] (Destroses).
- **[Ret weapon speed]** For SoR/AP Ret, raw weapon damage matters (many abilities scale off it), so slower weapons are generally favored (self-flagged low confidence) — [[d:paladin#1490120798630056057]], [[d:paladin#1490121310620356849]] (Silverhand).
- **[Ambershire SoC weapon progression]** Kara10 mace → Tempered Runeblade → Untamed Blade — [[d:paladin#1489620966634950767]] (bigdaddyd).
- **[SoR weapon]** Untamed Blade (UTB) is the clear current-phase weapon winner for SoR; gyatt: "amazing on necrodin." Flask of Petrified Gold gave UTB ~70% uptime in one test — [[d:paladin#1489550904184143953]], [[d:paladin#1489551182795116574]] (Slime). - **[SoC Ret gearing guide]** (Istenneveben/Jesse Potter, self-described "feelscraft"/vibes, not sim-verified): pick a slow weapon, get hit-capped, target ~30-32.5% unbuffed melee crit, then stack Str; useful pieces include Onslaught Girdle, Quickstrike Ring, SGC, ES trinket, Hand of Justice/scrolls, Ony neck early; enchant Agi instead of Str on head/leg/ring/neck/bracer once near the crit target since raid buffs (Kings, MotW, consumes) cover the rest. - **[SoC Ret weapon path]** Argent Dawn rep weapon "Demonsbane" (needs Exalted AD) is a good starter before Tempered Runeblade; progression then continues Tempered Runeblade → the BWL "Champion" weapon — [[d:paladin#1491996140655087726]], [[d:paladin#1491996507778584596]] (Istenneveben/Jesse Potter).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Stat priority overview]** Prot: Stam + Spellpower + Block Chance (Armor comes naturally from plate, don't need to chase it separately); Ret: Str + Melee Crit or Spellpower depending on build; Holy: Heal Power + Int + Armor + Spell Crit (a talent converts 2% Armor into Heal Power, so caster robes aren't needed) — [[d:paladin#1491443483918077983]], [[d:paladin#1491444217380077619]] (single community answer, not dev-verified). Arle's addendum: don't specifically chase Block since Holy Shield already gives plenty; take good fringe mail pieces for hit-cap (Dalaran rep shoulders called "super good"); focus Stam + SP — [[d:paladin#1491449302688596008]].
- **[Prot]** 1 Spellpower ≈ 1 TPS as a rough rule of thumb, updated to ~1.2 TPS after Holy Strike's SP scaling was buffed this patch — [[d:paladin#1490113496359243938]], [[d:paladin#1490114115052769341]] (Destroses/Silverhand).
- **[Prot]** Str was made "no longer worthless" this patch but "not good" — Holy Strike scales much harder off SP than Str, so don't chase Str; disputed by Racduck, who argues Str is undervalued because it's more abundant on gear than SP in equal amounts, and HS's threat still benefits from it — [[d:paladin#1490385255822135537]], [[d:paladin#1490385353817718795]], [[d:paladin#1490385468116701364]], [[d:paladin#1490384128636616816]].
- **[Prot]** Gearing priority framework: (1) getting one-shot (100→0)? get more Stamina; (2) healers struggling? get more Avoidance; (3) DPS threat-capping/pulling aggro? get more Spellpower — but this is a DPS problem first and lowest tank priority — [[d:paladin#1490383795298504806]], [[d:paladin#1490384299608772768]] (dusty). Simplified mantra: "threat until you're good on threat against your guild DPS > mitigation until your healers can keep you up reliably > fun experiments" — [[d:paladin#1491875120770449479]] (Mithreon).
- **[Prot]** Hit cap matters but is over-emphasized; losing a large SP item (e.g. 37 SP) to chase hit/weapon-skill hurts more than 4 weapon skill is worth — get the +5 weapon-skill book ASAP instead of gear-chasing it — [[d:paladin#1490385793791955155]], [[d:paladin#1490386192615870624]] (Silverhand).
- **[Prot]** Defense is called an "overrated" stat for Paladins specifically; a boss-crit-reduction stat is low value since a geared Prot Paladin "shouldn't be getting crit at all" on bosses anyway — [[d:paladin#1491875755481632848]], [[d:paladin#1491876064673271939]] (gastro). 440 Defense (full Def cap) is not worth chasing generally, only relevant for one specific AQ40 Twin Emperors mechanic, and even that can be worked around with a full 40-man threat-geared group — [[d:paladin#1491228323202990202]], [[d:paladin#1491228491344121866]] (Destroses/dusty), [[d:paladin#1491228862796005446]] (angels1m).
- **[Necroshock]** Higher SP on a heavy-shock build increases variance/risk from undead-type magic resistance — [[d:paladin#1489850500500164750]] (Istenneveben).
- **[Fortune]** Confirmed multiplicative; appears only on Neck/Cloak/Trinket slots — [[d:paladin#1490132689586749611]], [[d:paladin#1489793318945685567]] (Destroses/Jesse Potter).
- **[Stam, prot, general]** Once you're already reasonably geared, Stam contributes little beyond SP-item-side-effects and raw effective health unless you actively pick dodge/parry/block-carrying items — the added Defense on most pieces "doesn't add much" — [[d:paladin#1491340605375385611]] (bela).

- **[Reckoning mechanics]** See 1.18.1 changes above — functions as a "parry haste for blocking," instant-attacks the blocker's target within 8 yards if the trigger came from melee range; does not carry over from a ranged-triggered proc into melee; the reckoning-triggered swing does apply seal effects (tested a long time ago) — [[d:paladin#1490576034641346570]], [[d:paladin#1490578447603597312]].
- **[Sigil of Ancient Accord triggers (Prot)]** T2 3pc, Exorcism, Holy Shock, Holy Wrath all trigger it; T1 8pc (Prot) also triggers it and has an unusually high proc chance vs. the Ret version, which is largely untested — [[d:paladin#1491020792610291812]], [[d:paladin#1491022551961309324]], [[d:paladin#1491023191735406625]] (Destroses/Mithreon/Tatis).
- **[Judgement mechanics]** Judgement and Holy Strike are coded as "melee" for hit/crit% purposes; since Judgement counts as melee it does not soak Grounding Totem or Counterspell/Counter-totem-type effects — Hand of Reckoning specifically does not trigger Grounding Totem — [[d:paladin#1490585367596892212]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:paladin#1490585912751427658]], [[d:paladin#1490585978442616863]] (Xiyanzhe). Consistent with a separate open question about whether Taunt/Judgement use spell-hit or normal-hit (asker suspects normal/melee hit, matching the above) — [[d:paladin#1490789643409686610]] (Dieuw).
- **[VengeProt as OT]** Go Vengeance Prot as off-tank for more DPS/threat: OT doesn't benefit from Reckoning or Holy Shield anyway, so Vengeance shifts more threat into SoR/autos/Consecration at the cost of a slightly weaker Holy Strike (acceptable as OT); benefits more from 2H weapon spec too (extra SoR SP scaling + 6% weapon damage) — [[d:paladin#1489834521783304262]], [[d:paladin#1489834912025673900]] (Grey). One player ran Vengeance Prot for several weeks of BWL and liked it, mainly to squeeze out damage in an otherwise low-damage OT slot — [[d:paladin#1491125115591852254]], [[d:paladin#1491126995994677249]] (Seviry).
- **[2H tanking, assessed negative]** Trying 2H as Prot loses Defense, mana regen, threat from Blessing of Sanctuary/Reckoning, and Block, in exchange for more auto-attack (snap aggro) damage; Warriors benefit far more from 2H (Whirlwind/Sweeping Strikes synergy); Paladins only gain 3 weapon skill + 6% dmg from 2H talents, and the ~100 SP lost from dropping a caster 1H outweighs the snap-threat gain — [[d:paladin#1491915857469640827]] (bela), [[d:paladin#1491917134723416175]] (gastro).
- **[Ranged pull for Prot]** A new ranged-pull tool this patch: "Simple Slingshot" (Requires Survival 30; Use: fires a projectile for 1-2 damage) — a new Turtle-specific Survival secondary-skill recipe usable as a low-damage ranged pull option, previously Prot had none besides body-pulling — [[d:paladin#1491724652714659880]] (screenshot), [[d:paladin#1491724834000998460]], [[d:paladin#1491795715851161791]] (Celeez). "Fallen Shard" is also referenced as an existing throwing-item ranged-pull option — [[d:paladin#1491817973231390801]] (donnyhathawayenjoyer).
- **[Threat comfort, Ambershire]** On Ambershire threat is generally "very comfortable," so tanks can afford bulkier stam/def gear; getting Thunderfury alone makes threat concerns largely vanish, freeing gear choices toward pure survivability — [[d:paladin#1490382340088926208]], [[d:paladin#1490382489586237532]] (Silverhand).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Gearing philosophy]** "Tank gear is basically: threat until you're good on threat against your guild DPS > mitigation until your healers can keep you up reliably > fun experiments" — [[d:paladin#1491875120770449479]] (Mithreon). See Stats section above for the fuller (getting one-shot? more Stam / healers struggling? more Avoidance / DPS pulling aggro? more SP) framework.
- **[Threat math]** 1 SP ≈ 1 TPS rule of thumb, revised to ~1.2 TPS after this patch's Holy Strike SP-scaling buff — [[d:paladin#1490113496359243938]], [[d:paladin#1490114115052769341]].
- **[TF threat testing]** Tested over 5×100-hit samples: no bonus ≈22 procs; +fire-strike ring ≈30; +30% Fortune alone ≈30; ring+30% Fortune ≈34; ring+30% Fortune+SoR ≈74 (small sample, high variance flagged) — [[d:paladin#1490711066596872283]] (Dalana), [[d:paladin#1490711800373710928]] (Silverhand). Separately: TF ≈19% proc rate estimated; +5% Fortune (Sapphiron Drape) → ~20%, roughly +1 extra proc/min under SoR (not counting haste/reckoning/parries); each TF proc ≈534 dmg → ~+9 TPS in the worst case from that 5% Fortune — making Sapphiron Drape (5% Fortune, +3 int, minor resist) roughly on par with Cloak of the Brood Lord (Nefarian drop, +5 armor) as a preference call — [[d:paladin#1490112489776746647]]–[[d:paladin#1490119904534331516]] (Silverhand).
- **[TF]** No internal cooldown, confirmed via observation (up to ~5 procs seen in one auto-attack window) — [[d:paladin#1490109349886951615]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Shar'tateth]** Its armor-shred does **not** stack with Annihilator, but does stack with Sunder Armor — [[d:paladin#1491309206123450409]], [[d:paladin#1491311797313998962]] (Lika).
- **[Shock Prot]** Holy Shock costs less mana per unit time than Judgement of Righteousness (485/20s vs 300/8s), so a 1H + shield Shock-Prot build can sustain mana reasonably; TPS is a bit lower than expected but "not too much so," with better burst/utility; losing Bulwark hurts specifically for Anomalus's late-stack mechanic — [[d:paladin#1489853410055557162]], [[d:paladin#1489854443481600001]] (Grey).
- **[VengeProt / 2H tanking]** See Cooldowns section above.

## Healing specifics
- **[Rotation]** See Rotation: single target above (HS/FoL/Shock base loop, melee-weaving, Vael-style all-cast fights).
- **[Optimal healer comp]** Repeated community consensus: 2 Holy Paladins + 2 Resto Shaman as the core, with 2-3 remaining slots flexible between Shaman/Priest/Druid — [[d:paladin#1491681710780452874]], [[d:paladin#1491174186000056381]] (Mithreon).
- **[Blessing of Light]** Only benefits Holy Paladins directly; not worth using unless there's an excess of paladins to bless (players will auto-cancel it otherwise to save buff-cap room); with only 1-2 Holys, skip it — Blessing of Sanctuary may mitigate more total raid damage than BoL grants those 1-2 Holys in healing, though no hard data exists — [[d:paladin#1490560751939620984]], [[d:paladin#1490561064562196530]], [[d:paladin#1490561136440115372]], [[d:paladin#1490561332620300409]] (dusty/Gilfrey).
- **[Bug/nerf history, cumulative]** As of 04-09, community lists a stack of recent Holy nerfs/bugs this patch: Daybreak proc nerfed, Holy Strike healing nerfed, Holy Strike's smart-heal no longer prioritizing the most injured target, and the Holy Shock scaling bug (see 1.18.1 changes) — [[d:paladin#1491145578518810685]] (ronron). Real reported numbers: one player at 1376 Heal Power (no Blessing of Light) getting ~1k non-crit / 1.5k crit on Holy Shock, versus a theoretical calc suggesting notably higher — [[d:paladin#1491125645244104916]], [[d:paladin#1491126567697387592]] (ronron).
- **[Healing parse culture]** Healing meters are heavily criticized as a real-skill metric — can be cheesed by healing through Tranquility-type effects, healing DPS instead of tanks on Patchwerk-style fights (rule: heal only tanks), and other overhealing games; e.g. one log showed only 269 overheal-adjusted HPS, barely above a shaman — [[d:paladin#1491626807076716584]], [[d:paladin#1491626911942840511]], [[d:paladin#1491627618921873428]] (Sookie/Andant).
- **[Addon controversy]** "QuickHeal" (QH) is a healing addon criticized by multiple players as "borderline scripting" — automates smart target selection/priority so heavily that some feel it removes real decision-making, reportedly let players "outperform hard" without needing to think; some made modified versions hooked to DPS/threat prediction. It's not against server rules (only botting is banned), but several vocal players consider it distasteful and were uninstalling it — [[d:paladin#1491875187006771283]]–[[d:paladin#1491878620124155954]] (Yappa/Infertile/Mithreon).
- **[Fortune trinket for holy, "Star"]** In Sunken Temple content, this item can approach Sigil-level damage/output with the right setup, but needs Spellwoven Drape of Nobility and your fastest weapon (it has a 1-second ICD) — comparison vs Sapph Drape/Flask of Petrified Gold remains open — [[d:paladin#1491892343580659783]], [[d:paladin#1491892505568874527]], [[d:paladin#1491892913519591444]] (Adamant/Seviry).
- **[Trinket, Holy tank cross-use]** Fabric of Time is considered bad for Holy due to its -20 Int penalty — [[d:paladin#1491856291826503922]] (minefrost).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[K40 (Tower of Karazhan) trash]** The first pack (3 spectral workers + a giant bat) does nothing special. Vampiric bats cast AoE silence on nearby players and must be focused down after Darkbringers are pulled. Darkbringers cast a shield before a one-shot: green shield = magic immunity (needs a **physical** interrupt/kick), purple shield = physical immunity (needs a **magic** interrupt/kick); shield has a 12s cast-CD (first cast ~12s after pull), the one-shot is queued ~15s after pull. The big pre-first-boss pack has 3 Darkbringers — assign interrupters per Darkbringer (commonly rogue→green, mage→purple); with enough DPS all 3 marks can die before ever casting a shield — [[d:paladin#1490083505026240602]]–[[d:paladin#1490085104226144387]] (Mithreon/Jesse Potter).
- **[K40 general]** Before the 1st boss, only interrupting Darkbringer casts matters; before the 2nd boss there's nothing to CC/interrupt. After that, remaining CC is Banish on a stacking-AoE-pulse elemental (kills at 4 stacks, hard to heal, better to range/kite it out) and Shackle on undead adds that can wipe the raid if people move — [[d:paladin#1490085707849535579]], [[d:paladin#1490085911663476888]] (Jesse Potter). PUGs on a first prog night can reach deep bosses (Kruul) without necessarily killing them — [[d:paladin#1490085911663476888]].
- **[K40 – Gnarlmoon]** Interrupt the owls during intermission; a completed owl channel can change a random player's color. After Gnarlmoon, put Fear Ward on tanks before the blue-dragon trash (only ~15s to kill Guards before Fear expires); the big flying drake's fear also leaves a lingering ~1-min debuff — [[d:paladin#1490086664843034856]], [[d:paladin#1490089494328770621]], [[d:paladin#1490089742593949696]], [[d:paladin#1490089807764787382]] (Mithreon/Jesse Potter).
- **[K40 – Incantagos]** Focus Guards (they carry a full armor debuff, can reach 8k armor); healers should avoid standing near Suppressors; Shamans purge a debuff (spell id 57635) off Guards and the big flying drakes; also focus any spawning big blue crystals; pulls need care since several units patrol back and forth. During the Incantagos fight itself, don't interrupt adds — use that time to just kill them — [[d:paladin#1490086664843034856]] (Mithreon).
- **[BWL]** Ebonroc and Flamegor summon suicide-fire-elementals that are damage-immune and chase healers. Firemaw has a void-zone/puddle mechanic that can spawn even on the far side of the room and must be actively handled (ignoring it "comes knocking"). Vael applies an additional stacking debuff that damages but boosts stats — [[d:paladin#1491801890328150097]]–[[d:paladin#1491802356197625907]] (Jesse Potter/angels1m/Mithreon).
- **[AQ40 – Twin Emperors]** Full 40-man threat-geared groups can tank Twins without needing to gear specifically for avoidance/def-cap — [[d:paladin#1491228862796005446]] (angels1m).
- **[Naxx clearability in undergeared groups]** On Ambershire, groups clearing MC in "crazy" preraid-BiS numbers with good tank threat and no healer struggles led to speculation Naxx is clearable in preraid BiS (KT flagged as the one questionable low-dps-check fight); people have cleared Naxx in half-green/half-T1-2 groups before — [[d:paladin#1491056712696729710]], [[d:paladin#1491057982727786737]], [[d:paladin#1491058108473278667]] (Istenneveben), [[d:paladin#1491057487703576627]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Strat/Scarlet Monastery solo farm build (Prot Paladin)]** Force Reactive Disc + high Vampirism% is the core loop; recommended ~14-16% vamp minimum (16% described as "completely braindead"/game-plays-itself vs. 14% being "sketchy" especially on small pulls); danger points are right after zoning in (before balling the Atiesh-fountain mobs) and right after the rat-trap gate (mobs get spawned and you're locked in, "obliterates the deathball" while waiting). Use Blessing of Freedom the moment you open the first gate; use Exorcism on far mobs; a speed potion for the gate is optional/"sus" without it. Concrete vamp-set example for 13-16% Vamp: T3 8pc, Gluth neck, Spellpower cloak, 2x Vamp ring, Vamp trinket, KT spellpower trinket, Dream's Herald, Force Reactive Disc — [[d:paladin#1489957164272455761]]–[[d:paladin#1489965864500461648]] (Grey/Gilfrey), [[d:paladin#1491942396915617916]] (Claank), [[d:paladin#1491943067027243038]] (donnyhathawayenjoyer, comfortable at 15% on half-T2/half-T3). Devs are said to prefer nerfing tank gear stats over touching the underlying vamp/FRD farming mechanic — [[d:paladin#1491942975616450690]] (Excinerus). At only T1-tier gear (~18% vamp, Dream's Herald + FRD) only about half the undead side is clearable, requiring an engineering bomb pull, swiftness potion, and bubble as crutches — [[d:paladin#1491656543618207905]], [[d:paladin#1491659549755707412]] (Stormblesst).
- **[Lasher farming (DME-style)]** Gear barely matters (mob damage negligible) — just stack SP; a holy-shock-to-prot-shield-swap hybrid build (Holy Shock → drop into Righteous Defense/Holy Shield) works; Bulwark/Reckoning are useless here. Rule of thumb: lots of vamp gear → lean high-Prot; little vamp → lean full Holy (Holy Shock's reset + a Daybreak crit is huge for survival); don't pull more than 2 packs at once if struggling. Agent Defender Shield (blue, EPL quest reward, chance to proc + on-hit block chance) is recommended for undergeared Lasher farming — [[d:paladin#1489620619959074980]]–[[d:paladin#1489626103218835597]] (bigdaddyd/Light/Mike), [[d:paladin#1490767784135626762]] (Xiyanzhe).
- **[BRD Arena]** Soloable in preraid gear as Prot on Ambershire — [[d:paladin#1490852968302510220]], [[d:paladin#1490853001089253446]] (dusty/Istenneveben).
- **[ZG farming]** Considered the most consistent Paladin gold farm as a miner; running it as Prot with a healer friend clears "almost all the trash" for a combined 200g+/hour — [[d:paladin#1489955311077953687]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1491945839675969587]], [[d:paladin#1491945955866710218]] (donnyhathawayenjoyer).
- **[Stockades farming]** Clearable in 3 pulls under 8 minutes in "crap" level-60 gear using an Argent Dawn 1%/50%-block shield + Thorium Spike enchant; mostly a low-value wool/silk source (~5g/run vendored), useful as filler while waiting for a group — [[d:paladin#1490656952915591281]], [[d:paladin#1490659960747458570]] (Xiyanzhe/bela). Confirmed bug: taking 16+ debuffs at once can overflow them visually into your buff bar slots (reproducible by pulling everything and facing backward so all Rends land) — [[d:paladin#1490665553956704306]] (Xiyanzhe).
- **[Kill-timer culture / raid pacing]** K40 clears are now well under 1 hour partly because devs haven't yet patched out some boss/trash skips; some players want the skips removed to restore the intended ~1.5h pace — [[d:paladin#1491800364427509892]], [[d:paladin#1491800482073677874]], [[d:paladin#1491800765864349836]] (angels1m/Mithreon/Jesse Potter). A "good" Patchwerk kill time is regarded as ~1:20 (fastest logged by chatters); a claimed 1:10 was checked on turtlogs and not found among Paladin-linked logs — [[d:paladin#1491795916603129927]]–[[d:paladin#1491797622699331745]] (angels1m/Destroses/Jesse Potter).

## PvP
- **[Deep Prot dominance]** Deep Prot with Thunderfury is very strong in PvP even with zero consumables and no dedicated PvP gear (reported "top of leaderboard 9/10 matches"); rotation: swap in Libram of Final Judgement to execute targets below 35% HP, and Libram of Hallowed Ground to permanently deny base captures; pure PvE tank gear alone can beat 1v4 physical comps. Community expects an eventual nerf — [[d:paladin#1490469775988494518]]–[[d:paladin#1490470975664554085]] (Silverhand/Mithreon).
- **[Sanctuary]** Can be purged quickly but is valued over keeping a seal up in some builds; it "deletes" Rogues/Cats/DW Warriors — recommended for a PvP Holy Shock build alongside going further into the Shock-reset talent — [[d:paladin#1490468741014618153]], [[d:paladin#1490472138925277224]], [[d:paladin#1490472253706604746]] (Jesse Potter/Silverhand/Mithreon).
- **[Reckoning in PvP]** Community view is split: one detailed writeup argues the current 8-yard/no-charges/"parry haste" implementation removed Paladins' PvP equalizer vs. Hunters/casters closing the gap; veteran players call it long-known, intentional, PvE-first design ("poor man's parry") — see 1.18.1 changes above.
- **[Spellstrike basics for new Ret PvPers]** Items adding magic damage to attacks (e.g. Ring of Electrical Binding) trigger off both autos and Seal of Righteousness (double benefit under SoR) and can proc Judgement of Wisdom — [[d:paladin#1490471519263133736]]–[[d:paladin#1490472028107575377]] (Silverhand).
- **[Judgement of Justice PvP trick]** Judging Justice has a chance to proc your seal on the judgement hit itself, functioning as a ranged stun in PvP; you have to reseal to Justice and judge it for this to trigger — [[d:paladin#1491170906700251276]] (Xiyanzhe).
- **[PvP gear complaints]** New T2-PvP-set 2pc bonus turns a gloves' holy-damage-buff-on-JotC into a set bonus, criticized as low value since JotC has limited PvP usage; T2 PvP chest carries 40 Stamina and is well-liked for PvP; new PvP trinket/rings/belts trade +Defense for %-damage-reduction and heavy Stam, considered fine for tanks since Defense/crit-reduction are already low priority for Paladins — [[d:paladin#1490717267225219093]]–[[d:paladin#1490717529277206619]] (Beldrin/Istenneveben), [[d:paladin#1490764317606547638]] (Xiyanzhe), [[d:paladin#1491875755481632848]] (gastro).
- **[PvP progression state]** Conquest-gated PvP items (250 Conquest, incl. a CC-immunity trinket) exist in the game but Conquest itself has no earning path yet this window — no rated arena, world-PvP events, or weekly BG quest implemented — [[d:paladin#1491332525665157190]], [[d:paladin#1491332859204730993]] (Mithreon/Jesse Potter). Honor cap is 20k/week; weekly raid lockouts reset ~5h after the honor reset and weekly quests ~24h after; there's a known double-reset bug where weeklies grabbed right as they reset Wednesday night get force-removed from the quest log a few hours later (doesn't happen if picked up from the prior week) — [[d:paladin#1490949898588131348]]–[[d:paladin#1490951160356667503]] (Silverhand).
- **[Arena history/"Zookeeper" build]** Pre-Necrodin, a PvP "Zookeeper" arena build stacked multiple summon-proc trinkets/items (since summoned pets don't get arena dampening and soak damage while the paladin bubbles); before trinkets shared cooldowns, 10-15 simultaneous summons per arena match were achievable; bubble's damage/attack-speed interaction (reduces damage, not attack speed) still benefits summon-stacking builds today — [[d:paladin#1490581699376185444]]–[[d:paladin#1490581905093955624]] (Jesse Potter/TRUE HEIR), item reference `database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60790` — [[d:paladin#1490584052841844746]] (Destroses).
- **[PvE gear cross-use for PvP]** Blue PvP healing gear is comparable to, or only slightly weaker than, the Scarlet Monastery ("Strat") healer set for Holy pre-raid BiS purposes — [[d:paladin#1491923339583950989]], [[d:paladin#1491923505741172767]] (Xiyanzhe).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Macro troubleshooting]** A mouseover-cast macro can show greyed-out even when in range if an earlier line in a multi-line macro has an unfulfilled conditional (e.g. Exorcism greyed while Hammer of Wrath, listed first, is on CD) — the game evaluates line-by-line and an early failed conditional can visually greys the whole icon even though later lines still work. Suggested fix line to force a "scan macros for spells" refresh: `/run if nil then CastSpellByName("Flash of Light") end` before the real cast line (works for other spells too) — [[d:paladin#1490055755167174857]] (Grey), [[d:paladin#1490060564037242940]], [[d:paladin#1490060993605140612]] (Gilfrey).
- **[Divine Shield removal macro]** `/run local i=0 g=GetPlayerBuff while not (g(i) == -1) do if(strfind(GetPlayerBuffTexture(g(i)),"Spell_Holy_DivineIntervention"))then CancelPlayerBuff(g(i)) end i=i+1 end CastSpellByName("Divine Shield")` — note you need to tap it twice to fully remove — [[d:paladin#1491972173814960189]], [[d:paladin#1491972229695668346]] (donnyhathawayenjoyer).
- **[Addon: Puppeteer]** Popular healer/raid-frame addon; supports click-casting and mouseover, can be configured to show custom debuffs, range rings (e.g. 20y for Holy Shock) and directional arrows for out-of-range/LoS allies; requires SuperWoW + Nampower + UnitXP to function correctly — [[d:paladin#1489366814373970011]] (Mithreon, showing arrows toward out-of-LoS teammates), [[d:paladin#1491878720015695944]]–[[d:paladin#1491879466807332898]]. Some players reported Holy Shock specifically stopped being recognized by Puppeteer after this patch (spell shows as "unknown"); a few also had `/pfcast` macros stop working for Holy Shock and had to fall back to plain mouseover macros — [[d:paladin#1490131994871468242]] (Fistofate), [[d:paladin#1491860485673451551]], [[d:paladin#1491863757775704196]] (Sookie).
- **[Addon: QuickHeal]** See Healing specifics — controversial "borderline scripting" healing automation addon; not bannable per current rules (only botting is banned) but disliked by several players who felt it removed real decision-making from healing.
- **[Addon: RaidTrinketHelper]** Community-made addon (github.com/Kichmeni/RaidTrinketHelper) for easier use of the new Fetish trinket: dropdown player select + one button that targets the chosen player, casts the trinket, and returns to your previous target — [[d:paladin#1491735847068897471]] (Kichma).
- **[Addon: Interact]** github.com/luskanek/Interact — lets you bind looting/interacting to a key and spam it, fixing the issue where corpse piles from mega-pulls become hard to loot individually — [[d:paladin#1491948488551039027]], [[d:paladin#1491948873772564540]] (Xiyanzhe).
- **[Other addon UI notes]** "Nostalgia" is a UI preset profile; Settings > Appearance > "Force Blizzard Borders" gives a cleaner rounded/stylized look; Action Bar > Gryphons re-enables the classic gryphon bar art — [[d:paladin#1490003610116030775]] (Grey, in a pfUI-adjacent context).
- **[Libram hot-swapping]** Devs reportedly keep librams weak on purpose because they're "combat swappable" — some players script/macro swapping librams mid-fight (e.g. swap to a seal-boosting libram, cast, then swap to a Consecration libram before that cast) by hooking cast events to equip calls between GCDs — [[d:paladin#1491922885462327516]]–[[d:paladin#1491925176001105981]] (Excinerus/Xiyanzhe, the latter calling it "cheating").
- **[No official DPS simulator]** Unlike most other classes, there's no working Paladin DPS simulator for Turtle WoW; building one is considered hard because it needs to model weapon-swing timing and situational ability priority (e.g. Judgement vs Crusader Strike) rather than a flat priority list; several experienced players have private, unshared calculators/sims they don't publish publicly out of concern it would cause more harm than good if released widely — [[d:paladin#1490736985399103649]]–[[d:paladin#1490749196980977807]] (Flensmaster/Xiyanzhe/Silverhand), [[d:paladin#1492002869044580432]], [[d:paladin#1492003228177666110]] (Adamant/dusty).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[New-player profession advice]** Engineering is the go-to endgame profession for both PvE and PvP (most people pick it up after hitting 60); Sappers/dynamite are called near-mandatory for anything beyond casual raiding. While leveling, Skinning (early gold) plus Mining or Herbalism (mid-term gold) is a solid combo; don't bother crafting your own gear while leveling — [[d:paladin#1491337490085445834]], [[d:paladin#1491337556334481528]], [[d:paladin#1491337706964389908]] (Destroses/bela).
- **[Engineering leveling cost]** Getting Engineering to 300 can cost as little as ~100g: run Deadmines for smelting-area parts (to ~150), then Mithril then Thorium; the priciest single reagent is usually the Solid Stone for blasting powder, farmable or cheaply bought — [[d:paladin#1489769042435768442]]–[[d:paladin#1489769374817452214]] (gastro).
- **[Gnomish vs Goblin Engineering]** No real functional difference recommended between the specializations; "optimal" min-max path is to make everything in Gnomish then re-level Goblin later, though this is costly/time-consuming and skippable — Engineering itself at 300 (for Sappers/FRD) is the important part, specialization less so — [[d:paladin#1489751046359224510]], [[d:paladin#1489752616320897264]] (Grey).
- **[Gold farming]** ZG mining is called the most consistent Paladin gold farm; Stratholme ("live strat") farming for random rare drops is preferred by some over steady Lasher farming, "for a prot paladin it's a breeze" with a small power team; running many alts with Leatherworking + Alchemy for steady passive gold is another named approach — [[d:paladin#1489955311077953687]] (Mithreon), [[d:paladin#1491306289869885440]]–[[d:paladin#1491307994812518504]] (Shokisan/Jesse Potter/dumbid).
- **[Race]** High Elf's racial (spell crit) is called a nice fit for Shock Prot's playstyle — [[d:paladin#1489853703895912598]] (Athurian).
- **[Rep mechanic, Turtle-specific]** Rep gains from minor mobs do not stop past Honored/Revered in Turtle WoW the way they did in vanilla (where you had to hold turn-ins until reaching a threshold); best Cenarion Circle rep grind method: kill Twilight's Hammer cultists until you stop gaining rep from them — [[d:paladin#1490779939774005428]]–[[d:paladin#1490786223592177874]] (Xiyanzhe/Tatis).

- **Holy Strike smart-heal nerf/bug** and **Holy Shock 20% healing-loss bug** (root-caused to the new AD Holy Shock libram) — see 1.18.1 changes above for full detail and citations.
- **Flametongue Totem SP-scaling bug** — accidentally regained near-instant-cast SP scaling, produced 250-650+ dps for SoR/Necrodin paladins for most of this window, still not fully resolved by 04-10 — see 1.18.1 changes above.
- **Buff-cap client bug** — buffs past the visual cap still function but can break macros/addons and specific interactions (e.g. a mount-speed buff gained while over-cap could leave you unable to dismount until relog); a partial patch-note fix ("buffs exceeding the limit will now become visible once space is available") shipped mid-week — see 1.18.1 changes above, and [[d:paladin#1491163672956108831]], [[d:paladin#1491164282841464912]] (Beldrin/Istenneveben on the mount-lock bug).
- **Debuff overflow into buff bar** — taking 16+ simultaneous debuffs can visually overflow them into your buff-bar slots; reproducible in Stockades by pulling everything and facing backward so all Rends land — [[d:paladin#1490665553956704306]] (Xiyanzhe).
- **Puppeteer/Holy Shock unknown-spell bug** — after this patch, some players' Puppeteer stopped recognizing Holy Shock ("unknown spell"), and separately some `/pfcast` mouseover macros for Holy Shock broke and had to be replaced with plain mouseover macros — [[d:paladin#1490131994871468242]] (Fistofate), [[d:paladin#1491863757775704196]] (Sookie).
- **Reckoning behaves nothing like its tooltip implies** — see 1.18.1 changes above (functions as an 8-yard "parry haste for blocking," not a 4-charge stacking proc).
- **Rift Tear enchant** was bugged and couldn't be applied to another player's weapon via trade (enchanter had to have it on their own weapon); also does not proc off Bonechopper's wound effect like other weapon enchants do, for unexplained reasons — [[d:paladin#1489847642983895042]], [[d:paladin#1491422157954224290]] (Destroses).
- **Loop of the Lost / Memento of the Lost drop-rate suspicion** — one player reported ~30 K5(?) runs with zero drops despite a database-listed 17% rate, prompting a (unconfirmed, likely just variance) suspicion the drop rate was quietly nerfed — [[d:paladin#1490972816361914409]] (Scooterran).
- **Enemies above your level partially resist Holy damage** — flagged as possibly not working correctly with Consecration ticks specifically (unclear if bug or intended) — [[d:paladin#1491614195081281647]], [[d:paladin#1491616195504246844]] (Seviry).
