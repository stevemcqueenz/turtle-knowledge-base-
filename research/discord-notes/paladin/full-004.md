## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[ret]** Ret AP/SoC performance is heavily Windfury-Totem-dependent ("the whole spec hangs on a shaman totem"); on servers/groups without reliable WFT (e.g. TA/Ambershire), SoC underperforms badly while SoR/SP-focused Ret still functions — [[d:paladin#1432327368281686056]], [[d:paladin#1432321768118288395]], [[d:paladin#1432321591588425788]] (Excinerus/angels1m, 2025-10-26; disputed by no one, but with counterpoint that Nord/WFT-heavy servers see the opposite gearing logic).
- **[ret]** Logged comparison (Kruul, static single-target window): SoR build totaled 913.5 dps (SoR ability line 158.9/s, JoR 160.4/s) vs SoC build 789.7 dps (SoC 116.4/s, JoC 115.7/s) — roughly the "40% more damage" claim — [[d:paladin#1432316416626921482]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-26; image transcribed: top=SoR 140,207 total/913.5 dps, bottom=SoC 121,210 total/789.7 dps). A separate lucky-proc comparison (0-armor Medivh burn) showed SoC briefly ahead (1312.4 dps) vs SoR/Nightfall (1259.1 dps), illustrating high RNG variance — [[d:paladin#1432323903518281780]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-26; disputed/contextual, "could've been just 8 procs instead of 12" [[d:paladin#1432325556296482918]]).
- **[ret]** Necrodin (pet/Drain-Life-proc build) requires the T2 3-piece bonus specifically — no other spellstrike item substitutes; Dreams Herald is an acceptable weapon but there is no formal weapon-progression list for the build. It does viable but slightly-behind-standard-SP-build damage in general, yet "in end game gear it can be on par with proper spellret" — [[d:paladin#1433081645489197056]], [[d:paladin#1433081959369801789]], [[d:paladin#1433081999886651535]] (Destroses/Mithreon, 2025-10-29).
- **[general]** Informal Kara40 DPS-log ranking (single source, not official): of the top 25 overall K40 parses, 13 are Rogues; order is roughly Rogue > Arcane Mage/Elemental Shaman/Warrior > Ret Paladin even with full consumables and a WFT group — [[d:paladin#1433083916540968992]], [[d:paladin#1433082705876226208]], [[d:paladin#1433082735311720559]] (Destroses/Beldrin, 2025-10-29).
- **[ret]** Informal community tier label: "Ret is Tier 0 for Naxx, Tier 2 for Kara40," with a handful of paladins reaching top-10 dps in K40 logs — [[d:paladin#1433009606040358962]], [[d:paladin#1433009897267662939]] (Lydiance, 2025-10-29; single source, no official tier list exists).
- **[ret]** By this point in the patch, pure "Spelladin" (100% SP gearing) is considered no longer feasible; current viable Ret builds are "hybrid leaning AP or SP," while pure "Ape" (melee/AP-only, using T3) remains doable — [[d:paladin#1433254803508826203]], [[d:paladin#1433273488222982256]] (dusty, 2025-10-30).
- **[holy]** Holy Paladin is fully viable for 5-man dungeon healing, described as "a breath of fresh air" versus ranged-caster healers, with positioning (avoiding cleaves/stuns) as the main skill factor — [[d:paladin#1431669452914823251]], [[d:paladin#1431669636474081423]] (Bubbelz, 2025-10-25).
- **[holy]** Melee-healing (Holy Strike-centric healing) is considered strong ("goated") after multiple buffs; Holy Paladins who avoid melee-healing when safe are said to be "kneecapping themselves" — [[d:paladin#1432885521981509752]], [[d:paladin#1432885363898187847]] (Iseut, 2025-10-29).

## Leveling
- **[general]** Baseline leveling rotation: Seal → several Auto-attacks → Judge → reseal — [[d:paladin#1431660824556077086]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-25).
- **[tank]** Prot leveling opener: Seal of Wisdom → approach mob → white hit → Holy Strike/Judge → reseal — [[d:paladin#1432487309562871828]] (Dzonathan, 2025-10-27); wait until level 20 (Concentration talent) before tanking dungeons — [[d:paladin#1432487344417669121]] (lukatus, 2025-10-27).
- **[tank]** Leveling talent order for Prot: skip Heart of the Crusader initially, put 3 points into Improved Parry while leveling, pick up Heart of the Crusader at level 60 instead — [[d:paladin#1431979703228567592]], [[d:paladin#1431979862146678956]] (Destroses, 2025-10-25).
- **[tank]** Improved Devotion Aura vs. the +7 Defense talent point around level 28: raised as an open question with no answer given in this chunk — [[d:paladin#1432669402809110559]] (Aethanol, 2025-10-27; question unanswered).
- **[holy]** Solo leveling as Holy is considered the worst-experience spec of the three; dungeon leveling as Holy is just okay before the 5/5 Blessed Strikes talent and very good afterward (near-instant AoE mini-heal every 6s) — [[d:paladin#1432380186573279335]], [[d:paladin#1432380525183504468]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-27).
- **[holy]** Holy melee leveling avoids gearing for Spirit/MP5/mana-regen entirely by running Seal of Wisdom→Judgement of Wisdom→Seal of Wisdom for mana, freeing rolls onto caster DPS gear with haste/crit/SP; Red Dragonscale (Leatherworking) is BiS pre-raid for this due to the Daybreak interaction — [[d:paladin#1432667492177805395]], [[d:paladin#1432668173072863232]], [[d:paladin#1432669330289725511]] (Dzonathan/Destroses, 2025-10-27).
- **[general]** Weapon-skill-cap quest ("Ancient Warfare Text," Frey Island/Barrens area): costs 250g per attempt, grants +5 weapon-skill cap for one chosen weapon type, repeatable per type until the 555 skill cap is reached — [[d:paladin#1432001555271254208]], [[d:paladin#1432010057423523961]], [[d:paladin#1432010244334289076]], [[d:paladin#1432011434295951512]] (Mithreon/Dodging, 2025-10-25).
- **[general]** An early spellstrike item is obtainable from a Horde-capital-city dungeon quest (accept the corpse-run inconvenience) — [[d:paladin#1432487508347982026]] (Dzonathan, 2025-10-27). Quests stay "green" (full XP value) for 25 levels below the quest's level — [[d:paladin#1432488112503787521]] (Dzonathan, 2025-10-27).
- **[tank]** Rule of thumb for matching level to dungeon while leveling as tank: check the required-equip level on the final boss's drops and enter around that character level — [[d:paladin#1432670346032451585]], [[d:paladin#1432670920182595664]] (Destroses, 2025-10-27).

## Rotation: single target
- **[ret]** Zeal (Holy Might buff) upkeep is critical: letting it fall off to squeeze in one extra Crusader Strike costs 2 lost autos + missed weapon-imbue procs + Seal of Command's 20% Str bonus, so it's more optimal to do 3× Crusader Strike (banking the pre-refresh haste) before using Holy Strike to refresh — [[d:paladin#1431634714304708638]], [[d:paladin#1431634827010113557]], [[d:paladin#1431635561898053743]] (Jesse Potter/Iseut, 2025-10-25).
- **[ret/holy]** Confirmed by direct testing/combat-log proof: Strikes (Crusader Strike and Holy Strike) do **not** proc Seals — only white/auto attacks trigger Seal of Righteousness and similar seal effects; this had been an open forum dispute, resolved here with a screenshot and the confirmation "you also have to auto attack for it to do SoR on the strikes" — [[d:paladin#1432111266134097940]], [[d:paladin#1432111305740779712]], [[d:paladin#1431627474374098944]], [[d:paladin#1432217847412686858]] (Dodging/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-27; +1 reaction, confirmed/no dispute after proof posted).
- **[ret/holy]** Holy Strike's healing (smart heal) lands on the melee group even if the attack itself misses the enemy, as long as an ally is missing health; Crusader Strike still grants a Zeal stack even on a miss — [[d:paladin#1432389400368644117]], [[d:paladin#1432389556380106903]], [[d:paladin#1432391605595148411]] (Mithreon/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-27).
- **[ret]** Seal of Righteousness's extra-damage proc triggers only from auto attacks, not from weapon-enchant procs like Lifestealing — [[d:paladin#1432963266912587796]], [[d:paladin#1432963701840937021]] (SexyMorgana/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-29).
- **[ret]** Judgement crit behavior differs by seal: Judgement of Righteousness crits like a spell (+50% damage), Judgement of Command crits/doubles like a melee ability (×2 damage) — [[d:paladin#1432994994423332938]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-29). Spell-crit stat specifically affects: Drain Life weapon proc, spellstrikes, Sigil of Ancient Accord, Lifestealing/Fiery weapon-enchant procs, and Exorcism/Holy Wrath — not the core strike/Judgement rotation — [[d:paladin#1432995889596731523]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-29).
- **[ret]** With a spell-power weapon like Scythe, favor Holy Strike over Crusader Strike since the weapon sacrifices weapon damage entirely (CS scales off weapon damage); stack maximum spell-power consumables — [[d:paladin#1432446208047054869]], [[d:paladin#1432446482367123598]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-27).
- **[ret]** Weapon progression (AP/hybrid Ret): Scythe/Untempered Runeblade/Destiny → Shadowstrike → UTB (Untempered Runeblade) → Ashbringer — [[d:paladin#1432448022901428335]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-27). Untempered Runeblade (UTB) is considered the top pre/near-AQ40 weapon due to a very high proc rate — testing showed it out-DPS'd Shadowstrike by roughly 100 dps, and even Demonbane beat Shadowstrike at only 250 AP — [[d:paladin#1433104756905349141]], [[d:paladin#1433105275933560983]], [[d:paladin#1433115139871346718]], [[d:paladin#1433175178602614804]] (Istenneveben/Windpuncher, 2025-10-29).
- **[ret]** "SoR still kinda works on TA without WF, SoC doesn't" — restated as: without Windfury, SoC-based rotations do below-tank damage while SoR remains functional — [[d:paladin#1431945536927436872]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-25).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[tank]** Multi-mob pull sequence: prebuff Seal + Holy Shield → run to mobs (optionally throw a grenade first) → White Hit/Holy Strike/Judge on the first target → reseal (Seal of Wisdom) → Consecrate; secure the first mob's threat before moving attention to the next — [[d:paladin#1432676439408508989]], [[d:paladin#1432719902782787645]], [[d:paladin#1432719609118724227]] (Dzonathan/gastro, 2025-10-27).
- **[tank]** Threat-vs-mana tradeoff on pulls: if threat is stable, weave Seal of Wisdom between pulls for mana; if unstable, hold Seal of Righteousness for the extra threat; use Consecration whenever multiple targets are present — [[d:paladin#1432676508857532468]], [[d:paladin#1432676852299989015]] (angels1m, 2025-10-27).
- **[tank]** Consecration alone is not enough to hold multi-mob threat against undisciplined DPS; must actively tab/cycle Holy Strike and Judgement across targets and use taunt to recover mobs that slip off — [[d:paladin#1432718401230602291]], [[d:paladin#1432718505488154745]], [[d:paladin#1432719609118724227]] (gastro/Dzonathan, 2025-10-27).
- **[farming]** Vampirism healing was reduced ~33% specifically for AoE effects at some point ("nerfed"); Force Reactive Disk (FRD) adds so much self-healing during blocks that it's effectively required for reliable large solo pulls (e.g. Stratholme UD 250-mob pulls), even though it isn't strictly mandatory — [[d:paladin#1431731368999649420]], [[d:paladin#1432114111537352935]], [[d:paladin#1432767157502083094]] (Purec/dusty/Dodging, 2025-10-25..28).
- **[farming]** Skullflame Shield is explicitly **not** worth it or necessary for solo BRD/dungeon AoE farming despite the common belief; FRD is the correct shield for most dungeon-grinding farm builds except Deadmines (DM) — [[d:paladin#1432939751626772530]], [[d:paladin#1432939825752838197]], [[d:paladin#1432950398557094021]] (Mccnl1ght/gastro, 2025-10-29).
- **[farming, Turtle-specific]** Thorns/reflect-item stacking for AoE self-damage farming: a Paladin can equip at most 5 thorn-effect items for 24 reflect damage total, versus Hunters who can dual-wield Beastslayer/Elemental-Slayer-enchanted weapons plus items for up to 101 reflect damage — [[d:paladin#1432355923678199809]], [[d:paladin#1432356032948076565]], [[d:paladin#1432357111437721663]], [[d:paladin#1432357657955664006]] (Dzonathan, 2025-10-26).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[tank]** Hit-cap guidance for Prot tanking (dungeons/off-tank raid): "always be hit capped," though missing ~1% may be acceptable if the traded stats are worth it — [[d:paladin#1431989573059084299]] (angels1m, 2025-10-25).
- **[tank]** Crit/crush immunity for tanks is reached primarily through Holy Shield uptime rather than raw Defense rating; a tank is likely to become crush-immune before ever reaching 440 Defense — [[d:paladin#1433168550025232594]], [[d:paladin#1433168691763216537]] (gastro/Seviry, 2025-10-29; single mention of "440" defense as an informal, largely unnecessary soft target — [[d:paladin#1431894091272290374]]).
- **[general]** Spell penetration is a wasted stat for Paladins — nothing in the game currently has Holy resistance — [[d:paladin#1432754074058756218]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-27).
- **[tank/ret]** Stat priority: SP > Agi for tank gear; for Ret, SP feeds Seal of Righteousness while Str/AP feeds Seal of Command — since seals are largely mutually exclusive outside of (low-value) seal-twisting, gearing commits to whichever seal you'll main, most often SoR+SP — [[d:paladin#1432754479903805510]], [[d:paladin#1432754973204156527]], [[d:paladin#1432755303543210196]], [[d:paladin#1432755697950396467]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-27).
- **[holy]** Int vs. +Healing on gear is largely "feelcraft" early on, since most good end-game gear eventually carries both — [[d:paladin#1432756045935149207]], [[d:paladin#1432756493819838577]] (Mithreon, 2025-10-27).
- **[tank]** Weapon speed is largely irrelevant to a level-60 Prot Paladin's own damage (weapon treated as a stat-stick, doesn't scale with weapon dps), but a faster weapon still yields more Judgement-of-Wisdom mana procs — [[d:paladin#1432737292879138958]], [[d:paladin#1432737381273833593]], [[d:paladin#1432738273817788527]] (gastro, 2025-10-27).
- **[ret/holy]** Consecration's total spell-power coefficient is 66%, spread across the 8-second duration (corrects an outdated wiki figure that listed 33%) — [[d:paladin#1432781684952010772]] (Olivaar of Moments, 2025-10-28).
- **[ret]** Lifestealing weapon enchant does not scale with spell power and isn't needed for proc uptime; persistent mana problems from strikes indicate a play-skill issue rather than a gearing gap — [[d:paladin#1432468940595920896]], [[d:paladin#1432469066118992046]] (dusty, 2025-10-28).
- **[holy]** Red Dragonscale (Leatherworking) 4-piece set effect scales with healing power at ~0.4 total (0.1 per tick); it used to be considerably higher before a nerf — [[d:paladin#1432921038190149712]], [[d:paladin#1432921073346809968]] (Destroses, 2025-10-29).
- **[ret]** Sigil of Ancient Accord (MC trinket) contributes only ~3-5% of damage normally without Windfury, but can spike above 50% of a Ret's total Consecration damage when amplified by Windfury's extra hits (up to 16 additional proc chances per WF proc) — [[d:paladin#1432101466021171360]], [[d:paladin#1432101519389360189]], [[d:paladin#1432102118357074042]] (Jesse Potter/Excinerus, 2025-10-27).
- **[ret]** PPM-to-percentage conversion explained: %chance = PPM ÷ (60/weapon-speed-in-seconds), e.g. a 3.4-speed weapon gives 17.6 hits/min, so 1 PPM ≈ 5.7% chance per swing; Wrath of Cenarius (WoC) trinket is effectively ~100% uptime for Ret on Nord with typical current gear/haste under this math — [[d:paladin#1432943557286559804]], [[d:paladin#1432943799893622846]], [[d:paladin#1432943900732948582]] (user "30", 2025-10-29).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[bug]** Reckoning is confirmed still bugged: pre-CC2 it granted multiple stacking charges instead of a single extra attack, and even that behavior was inconsistent/non-functional at times; the current "parry haste" implementation is described by devs (per community relay) as a deliberate band-aid because the intended mechanic "couldn't be implemented properly" on this engine — [[d:paladin#1432478939464339696]], [[d:paladin#1432479161393610803]], [[d:paladin#1432479315898925208]], [[d:paladin#1432479430261084352]] (theoloras/warkrak/Rhiina, 2025-10-27).
- **[general]** Only Hammer of Justice and Repentance are spell-reflectable on a Paladin; Judgement is melee-coded and therefore not reflectable, and most Paladin offense is "Holy damage" (not a resistible/reflectable "Holy spell"), so it is unaffected by most reflect/resist mechanics — [[d:paladin#1432485301300887582]], [[d:paladin#1432486241823363276]], [[d:paladin#1432486314611314698]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-27).
- **[ret]** Naglering does not proc Judgement of Wisdom because a reflected spell isn't counted as an "attack"; the Gilneas City ring correctly triggers JoW instead — [[d:paladin#1432811771940442283]], [[d:paladin#1432811849908359282]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-28).
- **[utility]** Salvation (Blessing of Salvation) opinion: if DPS in a 5-man can only receive one Paladin blessing, Salvation should be the default priority to smooth pulls and let DPS push harder without pulling threat — though some groups are reportedly reluctant to accept it — [[d:paladin#1432828422589255810]], [[d:paladin#1432829232064495819]] (Dinousek/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-29; contested — no opposing view given beyond player reluctance).
- **[utility]** Benediction-style talents/items combined with Improved Blessing of Wisdom can reduce a caster's mana expenditure by roughly 13 mana/sec — [[d:paladin#1432672474868355193]], [[d:paladin#1432673546798104688]] (Dzonathan, 2025-10-27).
- **[tank]** Consecration has a ~1-second delay on its first tick, making it a poor snap-AoE-threat tool; Paladins are described as having no snap AoE threat at all and must instead rely on cycling Holy Strike/Judgement across targets plus taunt — [[d:paladin#1432258144225988659]] (dusty, 2025-10-26).
- **[tank]** In very long fights (e.g. 50-minute K40 clears), skipping Judgement to conserve mana when threat is already stable is considered a legitimate choice, not a rotational error — [[d:paladin#1432676852299989015]], [[d:paladin#1432676932339630160]] (angels1m, 2025-10-27).

## Tanking specifics
- **[tank]** T2.5 3-piece set bonus is described as central/mandatory to current Prot threat output ("feeds the Holy Strike oppression"), and is said to be effectively un-reworkable without breaking Prot's ability to hold threat at all until the spec gets a broader rework — [[d:paladin#1433189325172117585]], [[d:paladin#1433189576096354334]], [[d:paladin#1433189905206349835]] (gastro/dusty, 2025-10-30).
- **[tank]** T2.5 gear itself carries very little stamina/mitigation, forcing tanks to swap between T2.5 (threat, e.g. trash/most bosses) and T3/T2 (survivability, e.g. Golemagg-style heavy hitters) depending on the encounter — [[d:paladin#1432043676078575736]], [[d:paladin#1432044090869944401]], [[d:paladin#1432045062669336593]], [[d:paladin#1433193455256862770]] (Sylvaira/Seviry/dusty, 2025-10-25..30).
- **[tank]** A Prot Paladin without Thunderfury may not be able to generate enough threat in Kara40 until a rare drop from the second-to-last boss — [[d:paladin#1433191690696659064]] (dusty, 2025-10-30; single source/opinion).
- **[tank, farming]** Anecdotal report that 377 Defense + 11% Vampirism was insufficient for a specific Stratholme solo-farm attempt; Force Reactive Disk plus likely a full T3 Prot set are recommended for reliable large-pull solo farming — [[d:paladin#1432869359780368556]], [[d:paladin#1432869779084939334]], [[d:paladin#1432869968055373885]] (Claank/Breaddy, 2025-10-28; inconclusive/anecdotal).
- **[tank]** Black Morass, Stormwind Vault, and Karazhan Crypt are the only level-60 (custom) dungeons that actually require specific gear preparation for a fresh 60 tank; other dungeons (including Stratholme) need "just the spec and a healer" — [[d:paladin#1432137087964151989]], [[d:paladin#1432137574889033888]] (Jesse Potter/Seviry, 2025-10-27).

## Healing specifics
- **[holy]** Endgame single-target/tank Holy rotation: keep Seal of Wisdom up (or Seal of Light if another healer already maintains SoW); Flash of Light is the main heal; use Holy Strike on cooldown except when the Crusader-Strike haste buff (Zeal, 3 stacks) needs refreshing; use Holy Shock as often as possible for spot-heals, using Crusader Strike specifically to reset Holy Shock's cooldown — sequence: FoL → Holy Shock → CS → Holy Shock (likely crits, procs Daybreak) — [[d:paladin#1432895821564870748]], [[d:paladin#1432896090184613980]], [[d:paladin#1432897015569711114]] (Brendar, 2025-10-28).
- **[holy]** Holy Light is mana-inefficient and reserved for fights with heavy, predictable tank damage (e.g. Patchwerk-style); otherwise the FoL/Holy Strike/Holy Shock loop is preferred — [[d:paladin#1432895821564870748]] (Brendar, 2025-10-28).
- **[holy]** Holy Paladin's core strength is mana efficiency: cheap Flash of Light plus mana returned via Seal of Wisdom and Holy Strike — [[d:paladin#1432896090184613980]] (Brendar, 2025-10-28).
- **[holy]** Downranking heals is generally not worthwhile in Turtle: all heal ranks share the same cast time, and with reasonable raid composition/consumables mana usually isn't the bottleneck — [[d:paladin#1432427773472931962]] (Thalrinel, 2025-10-28).
- **[holy]** QuickHeal (healing addon) is criticized as purely reactionary; a skilled human healer who precasts based on incoming damage/aggro will outperform it, especially during predictable heavy-AoE phases (e.g. Anomalus) — [[d:paladin#1432422120318701568]], [[d:paladin#1432422695093669890]] (Sookie, 2025-10-28; +3 reactions).
- **[holy]** Daybreak talent procs from Holy Strike's healing crits (confirmed) and can also trigger Red Dragonscale's 4-piece healing-power-scaling effect — [[d:paladin#1433073573383114863]], [[d:paladin#1432917266571464736]] (Destroses, 2025-10-29).
- **[holy]** Rank-1 Flash of Light is commonly cast on the tank between trash packs purely to cheaply proc a crit and refresh Daybreak uptime — [[d:paladin#1432923730018832555]], [[d:paladin#1432924257616269445]] (Mccnl1ght/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-29).
- **[holy]** Holy Strike is considered "infinitely better" than Crusader Strike for a melee-healing Holy Paladin since it's a large, essentially zero-mana-cost heal; using CS in the Holy rotation is mainly relevant in PvP or niche cases — [[d:paladin#1432885810000302181]], [[d:paladin#1432886415687028756]] (Iseut/Excinerus, 2025-10-29).
- **[holy]** Improved Seal of Righteousness talent does actually scale with spell power as the patch notes claim, per direct testing — resolving repeated community doubt about the talent — [[d:paladin#1433281283571843163]], [[d:paladin#1433281451339813067]] (Silverhand/Destroses, 2025-10-30).

## PvP
- **[pvp]** Divine Shield ("Bubble") is perceived by Horde players as the Paladin's defining "OP" cooldown, but paladins report dying quickly once it drops; treated by paladins themselves as a short reprieve rather than a win condition — [[d:paladin#1433203121185882252]], [[d:paladin#1433208186458083519]] (Bide/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-30).
- **[pvp]** Only Hammer of Justice and Repentance are reflectable on a Paladin; most Paladin damage output (typed as Holy damage rather than a resistible "Holy spell") is unaffected by spell-reflect effects — [[d:paladin#1432485301300887582]], [[d:paladin#1432486314611314698]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-27, see also Cooldowns section).
- **[pvp, myth-busting]** Long-running Horde-side claims that classic-era Crusader Strike hit for "350 damage with no cooldown" or that pre-CC2 Holy Strike crit for 6-8k are explicitly debunked by veteran players; the actual old Crusader Strike hit for ~93 damage plus a Vengeance-% bonus — [[d:paladin#1432112413183836300]], [[d:paladin#1432112470297677894]], [[d:paladin#1432475210447061192]], [[d:paladin#1432475371567190207]] (Rhiina/Jesse Potter/dusty, 2025-10-27).
- **[pvp, AV]** Alliance's Bridge choke point is reported to lose to Horde's Frostwolf Village hold more often, attributed to an easier Horde backdoor route to the left tower plus invisible-wall terrain limiting Alliance flanking options — [[d:paladin#1432051987209392262]], [[d:paladin#1432052099507814636]], [[d:paladin#1432052234094641245]], [[d:paladin#1432052631332720650]] (Dodging/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-26).
- **[pvp]** Cross-faction BGs were being anticipated during this period; the paladin community broadly expects Shaman (Windfury/Earth Shock burst) to dominate once Horde and Alliance queue together, since Horde players allegedly haven't had to deal with playing against Shaman before — [[d:paladin#1432115606798209107]], [[d:paladin#1433214175982653471]], [[d:paladin#1433216785217224764]] (Jesse Potter/dusty, 2025-10-26..30).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[addon]** ModifiedPowerAuras (`github.com/MarcelineVQ/ModifiedPowerAuras`) is the closest available equivalent to WeakAuras for the 1.12 client, though its API differs substantially from retail — [[d:paladin#1433269885248602123]] (Acerious, 2025-10-30).
- **[addon]** "Attack Bar" addon recommended for swing-timer tracking; mounting and immediately dismounting is a known workaround to clear a stuck cosmetic "shadow" visual effect (from buffs like Ashbringer's) without needing to unequip the weapon — [[d:paladin#1432166733921386506]], [[d:paladin#1432168714832187482]], [[d:paladin#1432169003190718504]], [[d:paladin#1432170101016367226]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-27).
- **[macro]** A common solo/leveling auto-attack+reseal+Crusader-Strike macro (using `buffed("Seal of Righteousness")` checks) can break down in raid settings once buff icons exceed the visible buff-icon cap, since the macro reads the visual buff bar rather than the true buff state — [[d:paladin#1431678928031973458]], [[d:paladin#1431679527247020132]], [[d:paladin#1431680583083360481]], [[d:paladin#1431681897741680735]] (Xinbad/Dodging, 2025-10-25).
- **[macro]** Libram-swap macros (e.g. swapping a healing libram in mid-fight for a Consecration libram) work via WoW's spell-batching window, but only reliably at low latency, since both the swap and the spell-cast commands must land in the same batching tick; manually swapping in combat also correctly triggers the GCD, unlike some macro'd swaps — [[d:paladin#1432447311237087313]] (dusty, 2025-10-27).
- **[UI]** ModernMapMarkers addon shows dungeon entrances on the world map, useful for finding groups by zone name — [[d:paladin#1432491232092881019]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-27).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[general]** Race choice for Holy healing is largely cosmetic/minor: Human Spirit gives a small regen edge, High Elf gives a short channeled mana-return ability that "stops being relevant" fairly early in post-60 gearing, Dwarf gets Stoneform (removes bleeds/poisons/disease, brief physical-damage reduction) and Perception; none are considered game-changing for PvE healing — [[d:paladin#1431670602317561917]], [[d:paladin#1431672860560199850]], [[d:paladin#1431673079431561317]], [[d:paladin#1432915022379483287]] (Bubbelz/Dodging/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-25..29).
- **[gold/farming]** Lasher (Dire Maul East) farming remains a common paladin gold-farming activity; a 0/26/25 Vengeance/AoE build is used when a farmer feels too squishy on their default build — [[d:paladin#1433088065621983272]] (Destroses, 2025-10-29).
- **[gold]** Force Reactive Disk / Vampirism-based dungeon solo-farming (Stratholme, etc.) is a recognized gold-making method for tank/hybrid paladins, gated by gear (see Tanking/Dungeons sections for specifics) — [[d:paladin#1432869464428515591]] (Breaddy, 2025-10-28).
- **[professions]** Engineering is recommended for Prot tanks specifically for the Force Reactive Disk shield and Sapper Charges/dynamite for pulling and snap AoE threat, since Paladins otherwise lack snap threat tools — [[d:paladin#1432881647144931369]], [[d:paladin#1432722835796656209]], [[d:paladin#1432722892805767330]] (Excinerus/Dzonathan, 2025-10-28..29).
- **[professions]** Weapon-specialization professions (Weaponsmithing swordsmithing, etc.) gate access to specific weapons (e.g. Untempered Runeblade needs Master Swordsmith, not Armorsmith); switching specializations means re-learning it later, and specialization-specific quest rewards (e.g. Demon Forged Breastplate) may become permanently unavailable if the specialization is dropped — this was raised as an open question without a definitive answer on reacquiring such quest rewards — [[d:paladin#1433165217071894588]], [[d:paladin#1433014541989449770]], [[d:paladin#1433014838703034489]] (Achmodeus/Mithreon/SexyMorgana, 2025-10-29; question partially unanswered).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[bug]** T2 3-piece Ret/Prot set bonus ("Divine Strike") is coded as a full/class spell rather than a normal elemental spellstrike, letting it proc effects other spellstrikes cannot (see Gear section); devs have said it's a bug but will not fix it in the near term — [[d:paladin#1432795297058127913]], [[d:paladin#1432076717660438740]].
- **[bug]** Ret T3.5 5-piece set bonus does not refresh correctly when two 5/5 T3.5 Ret Paladins are in the same party: instead of each getting a fresh stack, their stacks randomly refresh to 1-10 seconds and can't exceed one stack from the other's procs, effectively sabotaging each other; the proc rate is also reported as very low (a 10-minute full-rotation dummy test produced no measurable extra haste/attack) — [[d:paladin#1432825988718268477]], [[d:paladin#1432826125851168768]], [[d:paladin#1432826395058507786]] (Excinerus, 2025-10-29).
- **[bug]** Reckoning's true mechanic (extra attacks on parry) has never worked correctly; it currently functions only via a "parry haste" band-aid, and pre-CC2 it granted multiple charges instead of the intended single extra attack — [[d:paladin#1432478939464339696]], [[d:paladin#1432479161393610803]].
- **[bug]** Atiesh (mage/priest/druid legendary staff) previously required re-equipping after every death; this has been hotfixed, and class-specific stat versions were added for each eligible class rather than a flat power buff — [[d:paladin#1432980985112301618]], [[d:paladin#1432981122828206100]] (Jesse Potter/dusty, 2025-10-29).
- **[quirk]** Rexxar's world-spawn NPC pathing has an intentional ~12-minute "phase out" gap at zone borders (e.g. Feralas/Desolace) rather than a pathing bug — he simply doesn't exist during that window — [[d:paladin#1432542641697980416]], [[d:paladin#1432542894010667068]] (Destroses, 2025-10-27).
