## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** All three specs (BM/MM/Survival) are considered viable/strong after the recent hunter overhaul; "all 3 specs eat very good right now" — [[d:hunter#1491783245203898492]] (limbless), confirmed [[d:hunter#1489408730394136628]] (Kaslux spec breakdown), [[d:hunter#1489291599509848254]] (Wuuc: rotated all 3 specs in one BWL raid).
- **[BM]** Kill Command reworked into "a different spell", usable during auto-shot windup, and stays castable for 4s even while on cooldown once available — near-100% uptime if critting roughly once per 4s — [[d:hunter#1489057648715501738]] [[d:hunter#1490812124006711457]] [[d:hunter#1491029120103219210]]. Old Baited Shot RNG proc mechanic removed entirely, replaced by passive Bestial Wrath trigger — widely seen as a big quality improvement — [[d:hunter#1491783758242779258]] [[d:hunter#1489057580436422777]] [[d:hunter#1492169055321460846]] (Darkaco: "losing Baited Shot was awesome" vs losing its focus-regen was a bummer [[d:hunter#1492168989789392957]]).
- **[Coordinated Assault]** Confirmed to be a melee attack attributed to the *hunter* (not the pet) even though the pet performs it — scales off hunter's own melee AP, not pet AP — [[d:hunter#1489330996787544365]] [[d:hunter#1489092928063148032]]. Threat from CA goes to the hunter, not the pet, so it doesn't help pet tanking/aggro and can make leveling pulls clunkier — [[d:hunter#1491312346742915184]] [[d:hunter#1491308406626324510]] [[d:hunter#1491784533551353896]]. Triggers Windfury Totem only when the hunter is within melee range — [[d:hunter#1490149747766591648]]. Combat-log name is "Strike Together" — [[d:hunter#1489691399082344518]]. Consensus this is a coding bug (should scale off pet AP) that devs patched around via tooltip wording rather than fixing, likely to avoid nerfing Survival, for whom it is the *strongest* (BM, its nominal home, benefits least) — [[d:hunter#1489052783742025779]] [[d:hunter#1491479477958742076]] [[d:hunter#1491479528281870389]]. Pre-1.18.1, CA out-damaged Immolation Trap for Survival — [[d:hunter#1489052741669228644]].
- **[MM]** Arcane Shot buffed and is now a core part of weaving between Steady/Aimed shots — [[d:hunter#1490833482979606616]]. On-crit, Steady/Aimed/Arcane now replace the *next* Steady with a free Aimed Shot (~550 extra dmg), making crit noticeably more valuable relative to haste post-patch — [[d:hunter#1490409235757011155]] (Focus: "crit was enhanced with patch... now instead of being significantly worse than haste before patch, it's slightly better"). Aimed Shot alone has a 1s GCD like a special ability; players are asking devs (Lhian) to give Steady Shot the same treatment — [[d:hunter#1489604240090468505]] [[d:hunter#1492650252853969017]]. Rapid Fire explicitly buffed to double its Aimed-Shot cast-speed reduction — [[d:hunter#1489692709550817407]] [[d:hunter#1489733155719217306]] (measured: 0.81s Aimed cast with RF+Improved Aspects+L&L, 1.22s with Imp Aspects+L&L, 1.94s Imp Aspects alone, 2.15s no procs, vs 2.5s tooltip baseline). BWL crossbow ("Handcannon") reworked from a memetic 2.6 speed to a proper 3.1 — now competitive with Ashjre'thul — [[d:hunter#1492648236651905268]] [[d:hunter#1492648292826218506]].
- **[Bugs, unfixed as of this chunk]** Poison Spit (Cobra/Adder pets) overrides/replaces the hunter's Serpent Sting on the target — reported repeatedly with no fix yet — [[d:hunter#1490021223646756995]] [[d:hunter#1491163901360996466]] [[d:hunter#1491613564480262144]] [[d:hunter#1491960986242912437]]. Moth's unique ability "Pollen Burst" (heal + enemy attack-speed debuff) becomes unlearnable / gets wiped once the pet hits max loyalty, requiring a GM ticket to re-teach — [[d:hunter#1491002478315962462]] [[d:hunter#1492253068366970980]] [[d:hunter#1491010113438355568]] [[d:hunter#1493972355431661709]] (still broken 2026-04-15). Charge (boar) gives flat AP plus should give a 35% bonus, but the % only actually applies ~20% of the time and gets progressively worse/entirely broken on Charge ranks 5–6 — [[d:hunter#1489061312431394856]] [[d:hunter#1489061403749781724]] [[d:hunter#1491455047429783602]]. Ferra's Collar is bugged: it constantly overrides/cancels the Unseen Path (2-set) buff and the 3% pet-damage boost from Beastmaster's Boots/neck items — [[d:hunter#1492167127606759424]] [[d:hunter#1492494095661138141]]. Devilsaur Tooth confirmed to work with Kill Command, forcing the pet's next hit to crit (persists until next hit lands) — [[d:hunter#1492013965692108811]] [[d:hunter#1492071714547302443]]. A 5-piece pet-set trinket effect that summons a Hippogryph ally is bugged — doesn't stay out as intended, inconsistent ~22s ICD — [[d:hunter#1492448991567155311]] [[d:hunter#1492456049679007815]] (bug report filed [[d:hunter#1492476895718277283]]).
- **[Farm nerfs since patch]** ZG solo panther/bear farm: sleeping-poison range increased from ~10-20yd to ~35-40yd, making the farm harder — [[d:hunter#1491826077381951550]]. Kara10 ("Tower of Karazhan") caster-add solo farm: casters used to walk-and-cancel-cast when out of range; now they stop and stand still to finish casts, badly hurting the old kiting reset trick — [[d:hunter#1491818790629937383]] [[d:hunter#1491820130995408986]] [[d:hunter#1491821734377033738]]. Ebonroc (BWL) stealth-pull-into-2nd-suppression-room pre-patch trick: boss mechanics were "slightly changed" in 1.18.1, unclear (unanswered) whether it still works — [[d:hunter#1491574117781213245]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** BM, MM and Survival are all considered raid-viable post-1.18.1 rework; "devs got balance right", differences come mostly from encounter length/mechanics — [[d:hunter#1491404375711809567]] [[d:hunter#1491404422276976640]] [[d:hunter#1489408730394136628]] (Kaslux: BM = ST dmg but needs careful pet positioning + consumes; MM = good ST/AoE, RNG-heavy on trash; SV = best AoE, good ST).
- **[Ambershire progression note]** On a BWL-fresh server, one raider rotated all 3 specs in one clear: BM felt "most reliably good" ST, but got #1 damage on Broodlord as MM off a lucky Lock-and-Load streak — "feels like all specs are viable atm" — [[d:hunter#1489291599509848254]]. Survival specifically underperforming its potential early because so many current bosses have fire resist/immunities — [[d:hunter#1489292976243867808]].
- **[ST vs AoE]** MM "seems better than MM" [sic, better than BM] for most single-target fights per one raider's early read, but MM shines more on trash/AoE with a good comp (Feral, Grace of Air totem) due to RNG-reliance on single target — [[d:hunter#1489295730920390768]]. Volley-opener-on-trash reset interaction (Renatakis' item) makes MM very strong on multi-add pulls — [[d:hunter#1489296042468970627]] [[d:hunter#1489296152112271432]]. Later consensus (post extra weeks/patches): MM and Survival sim about equal on single target; on AoE, Survival cleaves more easily but MM wins "by a mile" if Aimed Shot's cleave line can be lined up through several mobs — [[d:hunter#1491704423427342497]] [[d:hunter#1492458171934183505]].
- **[BM]** Considered extremely strong/"insane" after the overhaul — 5.5k Kill Command crits on Broodlord reported — [[d:hunter#1493004500149993484]]; a BM/MM hunter (Itron) placed #2 on a raid damage meter at 849.9 dps (5.4% of total raid damage) — [[d:hunter#1493005279288234208]]. Its main weakness noted repeatedly: heavy party-composition dependency (buffs/Windfury) for pet scaling — [[d:hunter#1492384227378073682]] (see Contested section).
- **[Leveling]** BM is the overwhelming consensus best leveling spec — simplest to play, tankiest, pet solos most content — [[d:hunter#1490453793328857279]] [[d:hunter#1490455633114435715]] [[d:hunter#1489400947317805157]]. Survival only becomes strong for leveling once the level-40 "traps usable in combat" talent is available; before that, stick to BM — [[d:hunter#1489567207490064384]] [[d:hunter#1489567354877640704]] [[d:hunter#1489567654791483513]]. MM leveling works but means "endlessly kite", seen as the boring/hardmode option — [[d:hunter#1491930373582753873]].
- **[PvP]** BM pets are "monsters in bgs... basically another player on your team" and can solo people while the hunter LOS/kites; BM is also the easiest spec to have fun/win in PvP without much skill — [[d:hunter#1489662317913440306]] [[d:hunter#1489577856811143319]]. MM is a "free kill" once an opponent closes into the deadzone (except vs. Holy Paladins) — [[d:hunter#1489546377963765835]]. Survival PvP is viable and can beat warriors 1v1 with good play, but lacks gap-closers, self-heals, and reliable CC vs. classes; PvP is "almost 100% luck" for hunters overall (crit-dependent burst) per junabii — [[d:hunter#1489552360459206706]] [[d:hunter#1489620614468866290]] [[d:hunter#1491130145858261146]]. Pets currently take/deal 100% damage in PvP with no dampening applied (unlike players), making pet burst (esp. Kill Command) unexpectedly strong for now — expected to get dampened eventually — [[d:hunter#1490803074963476650]] [[d:hunter#1491028211960643695]] [[d:hunter#1492842290719227914]].
- **[Race choice]** Night Elf has the highest base Agility of Alliance races plus 1% haste — considered the top PvE Alliance pick; High Elf gives 2% agi (not 5%, corrected misconception) — [[d:hunter#1491089681234919635]] [[d:hunter#1491594913136115732]]. Orc favored on Horde for 5% pet damage racial + axe skill — good for BM specifically — [[d:hunter#1491089012730106050]] [[d:hunter#1491594913136115732]]. Troll gives +3 bow skill/Berserking; on Turtle, high-elf ranged skill is only +2% agi not a big deal at endgame — [[d:hunter#1491594913136115732]].

## Leveling
- **[Spec/pet]** Beast Mastery is the default leveling spec: tame any Raptor, spec BM, use a slow (2.6+ speed) ranged weapon, and spam Steady Shot — "pretty simple, you can basically do any quest" — [[d:hunter#1489320174745288867]]. While leveling BM, the pet is described as a "demigod" that can tank 2–4 mobs simultaneously with zero downtime for the hunter — [[d:hunter#1492991527394349177]] [[d:hunter#1492991635598868482]] [[d:hunter#1492991704620335164]]. Which pet you pick barely matters for damage while leveling ("the best pet is 'dude just spec BM and you auto win'") — autos carry most of the damage and the pet's main job is tanking, so pick based on looks — [[d:hunter#1491440356099756155]] [[d:hunter#1492990709580435457]] [[d:hunter#1489401113684873267]].
- **[Survival/MM leveling]** Survival only comes online for leveling once you unlock the level-40 talent letting traps be used in combat — before that, stay BM — [[d:hunter#1489567207490064384]] [[d:hunter#1489567354877640704]] [[d:hunter#1489567654791483513]]. MM leveling is possible via constant kiting but is considered slow/tedious by comparison — [[d:hunter#1491930373582753873]].
- **[Coordinated Assault at low level]** Gives extra damage but sends the threat to the *hunter* rather than the pet, which can make pulls clunkier while leveling; still commonly taken since there's nowhere better to spend the points — [[d:hunter#1491784533551353896]] [[d:hunter#1491784691357843567]] [[d:hunter#1490451117174423573]].
- **[Pet ability upgrades]** Learn a new rank of an ability (e.g. Growl) roughly every 10 levels by taming a donor pet that already knows it and using it in combat until you get a "You have learned X (Rank N)" system message — that ability is then teachable to any of your own pets via the pet trainer — [[d:hunter#1489962567022674000]] [[d:hunter#1489962914000539829]] [[d:hunter#1491247586550485003]] [[d:hunter#1491292180793393293]]. Upgrading a rank at the trainer only costs the *difference* between old and new rank's point cost, not the full new cost — no need to "skip" ranks to save points — [[d:hunter#1492295501561856000]] [[d:hunter#1492296087522775130]] [[d:hunter#1492295980652171325]]. Complete the early hunter class questline or you won't unlock the "Feed Pet" ability at all — [[d:hunter#1492136690561585234]].
- **[Good early Screech pets]** Amani Eagles (level 10, Amani'Alor in Stonetalon; Horde gets a portal quest, Alliance can swim from Darkshore around level 12) — [[d:hunter#1490087581919084586]] [[d:hunter#1491549694692954193]]. Greater Fleshrippers in Westfall (level 16-17 carrion bird) also start with Screech rank 1 for Alliance who don't want the long trek — [[d:hunter#1490088205435797574]] [[d:hunter#1491510308991270932]]. A level-35-tameable Hinterlands Eagle carries Screech rank 3 (normally locked to level 45) — a big power spike, worth the trek — [[d:hunter#1492994314786177045]] [[d:hunter#1493146688322277447]].
- **[Boars]** Recommended as a good starter pet for Charge's instant small stun and high initial single-target threat — [[d:hunter#1489518792722874369]] [[d:hunter#1490601448067825745]]. Dash requires the pet to be level 30 — [[d:hunter#1490806796825137273]].
- **[Pet leveling at 60]** Leveling a low-level tamed pet up at max character level is slow; the standard method is to solo-pull full rooms of Lashers (Silithus/etc.) for a few hours, which can level a pet to 60 in that time — [[d:hunter#1489623380570149024]] [[d:hunter#1492653830301814867]] [[d:hunter#1492653956483252265]]; XP-giving dungeons where the pet can be parked safely also work — [[d:hunter#1490359153695264818]]. Pets get XP based on kills that give *the hunter's own level* XP (i.e. green-and-above to the hunter, regardless of the pet's own level) — [[d:hunter#1492091816692940902]] [[d:hunter#1492092209334321152]]; pets get zero XP from PvP kills or quest completion — [[d:hunter#1491157779950604449]] [[d:hunter#1491155382666793182]].
- **[Respec cost]** Talent respec caps at 5g max on Turtle WoW — cheap enough that "wrong" early leveling talent choices are not a big deal — [[d:hunter#1493367319857074418]] [[d:hunter#1493367319857074418]] [[d:hunter#1493378407495372910]].
- **[Early weapons]** Laminated Recurve bow available in Stormwind at level 11; Deadmines gives a level-18 blue gun; Wailing Caverns gives a level-19 bow; Horde generally has better low-level ranged-weapon quest rewards than Alliance — [[d:hunter#1492137881144262666]] [[d:hunter#1490658191065747506]] [[d:hunter#1490660893472722965]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[BM]** Simple/near-optimal loop: Auto Shot → Steady Shot → Arcane Shot (or Multi-Shot) → Auto, weaving Kill Command the instant it procs; commonly played from a single macro chaining Kill Command / Arcane Shot / Steady Shot — [[d:hunter#1493031293343236346]] [[d:hunter#1493029118936219742]] [[d:hunter#1493050238653435955]] [[d:hunter#1490811550188175652]]. Kill Command remains castable for 4 seconds even while on cooldown once it becomes available, so near-100% uptime is achievable if the hunter/pet crits roughly once every 4 seconds — [[d:hunter#1489057648715501738]] [[d:hunter#1490812124006711457]]. KC *can* be cast during Auto Shot wind-up but *cannot* be cast while a Steady Shot is already casting (confirmed via testing; casting it resets the wind-up) — [[d:hunter#1492650379727605830]] [[d:hunter#1492650475517120764]] [[d:hunter#1492656625318887515]].
- **[MM/general]** Standard shot rotation: open with Steady Shot and spam Arcane Shot while it casts for burst, then after each Auto Shot cast Steady, weaving an Arcane Shot in at the end of the Steady cast — the "standard rotation" — [[d:hunter#1489708299913003218]] [[d:hunter#1489708644365897770]] [[d:hunter#1489709233007235133]]. Steady Shot is *not* weapon-speed-normalized like a melee special — it's normalized to a fixed 2.8 base — [[d:hunter#1489556543606231171]] [[d:hunter#1489558856739586161]].
- **[Weapon speed for weaving]** To weave Arcane Shot without ever clipping Steady/Aimed under full haste (Aspects etc.), you want a ranged weapon around 3.3 speed unhasted, which comes down to ~2.5 effective with Aspects/quiver-style haste — the "optimal weapon speed is 3.3" — [[d:hunter#1492170200219652260]] [[d:hunter#1493314225865490462]] [[d:hunter#1492662427202883616]] [[d:hunter#1492662547927662754]].
- **[Haste vs. crit for MM]** Consensus view: haste is a weak/negative stat for the current MM rotation because it doesn't reduce the GCD — more haste just clips more Steady/Aimed casts against the auto-swing timer; its only benefit is more auto shots and slightly better Aspect uptime — [[d:hunter#1491111488214007951]] [[d:hunter#1491112449733165088]] [[d:hunter#1491112890147803268]] [[d:hunter#1490408532057526615]] (Boomsky: "you are completely overvaluing haste, especially with how clip heavy the current MM rotation is"). Contested: Focus argued on the TurtleCraft forums that 10% haste = ~1% more overall dps and made crit more valuable via triggering more haste-boosted auto-Aimed swaps; Oojala/Boomsky disputed the math as inconsistent — [[d:hunter#1490408079089471508]] [[d:hunter#1490409235757011155]] [[d:hunter#1490410061472731157]] [[d:hunter#1490410294206267522]].
- **[Aimed Shot cast time]** Base cast 2.5s; only hunter-specific talents/Rapid Fire reduce it (raw gear-haste% does *not* affect it, unlike Auto Shot) — [[d:hunter#1489692299725115543]] [[d:hunter#1489744714294100178]] [[d:hunter#1489596144974565427]]. Measured cast times: 2.15s with no procs, 1.94s with Improved Aspects alone, 1.22s with Improved Aspects+Lock and Load, 0.81s with Rapid Fire+Improved Aspects+L&L — [[d:hunter#1489733155719217306]]. On crit (Steady/Aimed/Arcane), the *next* Steady Shot is upgraded into a free Aimed Shot (~550 extra damage) — [[d:hunter#1490409235757011155]].
- **[Lock and Load / RNG]** MM's parse variance is dominated by Lock and Load proc luck — "MM hunter top parse is pure luck with Lock and Load" — [[d:hunter#1491122275997585498]] [[d:hunter#1491130145858261146]].
- **[BM pet abilities: Claw vs Bite]** Claw is near-worthless as a focus dump on any pet capable of a real special ability — "obsolete on any pet that's capable of doing damage," has almost no AP scaling — [[d:hunter#1489371621343887422]] [[d:hunter#1491441340062367958]] [[d:hunter#1491441568522174545]]. In a controlled dummy test: pure Claw autocast ≈107 dps vs. Bite-on-cooldown-with-Claw-as-filler ≈115 dps, roughly a 7% gain for the latter — [[d:hunter#1491473274444714116]] [[d:hunter#1491473561058152658]]. Counterpoint (also well received): "it's common knowledge that bite and claw does effectively the same," and Claw can be preferred for total hit-count/Frenzy uptime purposes in some setups (e.g. BT boss with only 1/5 Frenzy talent) — [[d:hunter#1491450985183445002]] [[d:hunter#1491442053937233920]] [[d:hunter#1491452314437812325]].
- **[Savage Rend]** Raptor-family special, far stronger per-focus than Claw/Bite: "in worst case scenario deals almost as much damage as double claw in best case scenario, for 30% lesser focus cost" — [[d:hunter#1490319094292156526]]. Max rank is only taught by ZG raptors — [[d:hunter#1489803889950720100]] [[d:hunter#1489804499924291634]]. Standard raptor loadout is Growl + Bite + Savage Rend on autocast; swap Rend/Bite off (Claw on) vs. bleed-immune targets — [[d:hunter#1489990661066330222]] [[d:hunter#1490356552228671618]] [[d:hunter#1491436635840057494]]. Tested scaling: Savage Rend tooltip base is 27-36 initial hit / 16 per DoT tick; measured ≈13 AP per +1 dmg on DoT ticks and ≈25 AP per +1 initial hit (matching Bite's initial-hit scaling), with the pet's happiness tier (green vs yellow) also multiplying total damage by ~25% — [[d:hunter#1491772472008245368]] [[d:hunter#1491772642255048804]] [[d:hunter#1492483447049027794]] [[d:hunter#1492491859983995003]] [[d:hunter#1492491923674365963]] [[d:hunter#1492488702994415657]].
- **[Pet attack speed vs Windfury]** DPS is normalized regardless of pet attack speed, so at equal DPS a 2.0-speed pet's Windfury proc hits for roughly double a 1.0-speed pet's — since WF has an internal cooldown capping total procs/min, a faster (~2.0) pet gives bigger individual WF hits for the same proc count — [[d:hunter#1489955871776702616]] [[d:hunter#1489960530461589527]] [[d:hunter#1489962086535663696]]. For BM specifically, faster pets are favored for Frenzy uptime; the overall dps difference between a 1.4-speed and a 2.0-speed pet with Windfury is reported as small either way — [[d:hunter#1490799339868786779]] [[d:hunter#1491083689948610580]] [[d:hunter#1491086021683712010]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[MM]** Opening multi-add trash pulls with Volley (paired with the Renatakis item's on-hit focus-cost-reset interaction) is very strong — "OP af with MM" — [[d:hunter#1489296042468970627]] [[d:hunter#1489296152112271432]]. Aimed Shot's cleave line (Lock and Load's "AS cleave") pierces additional targets standing directly between the hunter and the point of impact — very tight (essentially a 1-pixel-wide line) and only hits targets fully in front, not behind, the primary target; enemy height also seems to matter and can cause misses — [[d:hunter#1489189135909523547]] [[d:hunter#1489189786290753576]] [[d:hunter#1489191203164393634]] [[d:hunter#1489191545738494022]] [[d:hunter#1489191837116924026]] [[d:hunter#1489193233543331913]] [[d:hunter#1489194239022727331]]. On good mob alignment, this cleave makes MM AoE beat Survival "by a mile"; otherwise Survival cleaves more consistently — [[d:hunter#1491704423427342497]].
- **[BM]** Gorilla's Thunderstomp deals AoE damage (scales from spell power, coefficient ≈0.21) and applies an AoE attack-power-reduction debuff with high threat value (~189 flat threat per application, scales poorly with AP, better on repeated small hits) — good burst AoE tool, especially pre-popped with Devilsaur Tooth/Bestial Wrath/Blood Fury for a guaranteed big crit — [[d:hunter#1491405605594861578]] [[d:hunter#1491403250887299092]] [[d:hunter#1491403332537811058]] [[d:hunter#1491403469498744902]] [[d:hunter#1492107776921436200]]. Gorilla considered top BM AoE pet and can reach 20% of total raid damage in some fights per Survival hunters using it as a secondary — [[d:hunter#1491456752066236566]] [[d:hunter#1491457222100652166]] (limbless: "gorilla is good for aoe and for massive windfury swings, will be worse than raptor on single target but solid on aoe packs"). Old Crab's "Bubble Barrier" AoE also existed pre-patch but was considered weak/situational — [[d:hunter#1491405618576228503]] [[d:hunter#1491405843080675458]] [[d:hunter#1491405986278146291]].
- **[General]** All 3 specs have Volley as a baseline AoE option (once per minute); considered only "okay-ish" — [[d:hunter#1492997395007668487]]. Survival is regarded as having the best raw AoE of the 3 specs, MM has good but situational AoE (needs alignment for the cleave), BM's AoE is merely "acceptable," worse than a Smite Priest spamming Holy Nova — [[d:hunter#1492997613157617848]] [[d:hunter#1492997804157571284]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit]** Ranged hit cap in raids is 8% ("never miss") — [[d:hunter#1489060054354366524]]. Reiterated ranged hit cap 8.0% with a note that +3 ranged weapon skill gives only ~0.6% hit and is not very impactful — get at least +5 skill — [[d:hunter#1491594913136115732]].
- **[Crit]** Crit is called "hunter's highest value stat" alongside agility, with no risk of "too much crit" the way there is for rogues/warriors, because of how crit scales with agility and Lock and Load procs — [[d:hunter#1489269266690605156]] [[d:hunter#1489269339789201459]] [[d:hunter#1489271520340611112]]. 52 agility ≈ 1% crit for a hunter; any pure-crit stat item is "always valued" for MM — [[d:hunter#1491741421059833897]]. With Kings, ~700 agi only nets a bit over 1% crit from Agi alone (agi-to-crit ratio is low) — [[d:hunter#1489268471681515672]].
- **[Attack speed / normalization]** Steady Shot is normalized to a fixed 2.8 speed baseline regardless of actual weapon speed — [[d:hunter#1489558856739586161]] [[d:hunter#1489556543606231171]].
- **[Spell power]** Full spell-power consumables (incl. Supreme Flask, ~300 SP) add roughly only 1-2% overall MM dps since only Serpent Sting/Arcane Shot (and possibly Volley) scale with SP; logged data: Arcane Shot was only ~9% of a hunter's boss damage in a full Naxx clear — [[d:hunter#1490854183237324840]] [[d:hunter#1491911187439550494]] [[d:hunter#1491912659304583369]]. Pets get *zero* spellpower from the hunter's own gear; the only source of pet spellpower is the ranged-AP→spellpower conversion via Spirit Bond (BM talent) — [[d:hunter#1491781102560350259]] [[d:hunter#1491781361588113600]] [[d:hunter#1491781130524037310]].
- **[Pet family damage modifiers (pre-1.18.1 data, believed unchanged)]** AP coefficients for pet Bite/Claw/Kill Command/Coordinated Assault: Raptor 9%, Cat 9%, Cobra/Snake 13.5%, Wind Serpent 7% — [[d:hunter#1489051025900966028]]. Scorpid's modifier is roughly -6% vs. Snake's +13.5% — a ~19.5% swing in Kill Command damage in the snake's favor — [[d:hunter#1490807317409828956]]. Bear ≈ -9% modifier (should logically be offensive but isn't) — [[d:hunter#1491438007994028082]]; Bear's health bonus is only ~8% ("giagantic") which does not compensate — [[d:hunter#1491438673323888641]]. Community complaint: defensive-flavored pets (turtle, crab, bear, fox) have neither good offense nor big enough defensive bonuses to compensate — [[d:hunter#1491439110303383673]] [[d:hunter#1491438332834615367]].
- **[Poison/Spit vs. AP]** Scorpid Poison and Cobra/Adder Poison Spit scale from the pet's *spellpower* (via Spirit Bond), not AP — so stacking hunter spellpower gear does nothing (pets don't inherit hunter SP directly) — [[d:hunter#1491780001484378246]] [[d:hunter#1491780300085264455]] [[d:hunter#1491781067378524362]].
- **[Pet AP scaling magnitude]** The AP difference between an MC-geared and a Naxx-geared hunter's pet is compared to roughly the swing of having or not having a Blessing of Might — i.e. relatively small compared to gains from raid buff composition — [[d:hunter#1492165399771611166]].
- **[Weapon skill]** Weapon skill caps out at +15 total; a +5 quest reward plus a +7 weapon enchant does *not* reach the cap (need the BM shoulder enchant/book too) — [[d:hunter#1491460401165242378]] [[d:hunter#1491460456500695253]].

- **[Pet-buffing consumables]** BM hunters can stack strength/agility scrolls and melee juju on the *pet* (not just self) to inflate Kill Command crits — reported crits of 7-9k this way, described as "full huntard" — [[d:hunter#1490806608312406098]] [[d:hunter#1490807845078434054]]. Juju Flurry (haste) and Rapid Fire share the same cooldown category, so plan which to use first depending on burst-timing needs — [[d:hunter#1489794162059509780]] [[d:hunter#1489794704836137140]]. Most hunter special-shot cast times have a 0.5s "hidden" component that can't be reduced by haste, so haste stacking hits diminishing practical returns — [[d:hunter#1489792810344517743]] [[d:hunter#1489592311087370260]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Kill Command]** Usable window: stays castable for up to 4 seconds even while "on cooldown" once it becomes available after a crit, so near-100% practical uptime is achievable at moderate crit rates; can be cast mid Auto-Shot windup, but not while Steady Shot is casting — [[d:hunter#1489057648715501738]] [[d:hunter#1490812124006711457]] [[d:hunter#1492650379727605830]] [[d:hunter#1492656625318887515]]. Kill Command's crit is derived from the *pet's* crit chance (it's coded as a pet attack) — [[d:hunter#1491170449982357644]].
- **[Coordinated Assault threat]** Generates threat for the *hunter*, not the pet, since it's a hunter-attributed attack — [[d:hunter#1491312346742915184]] [[d:hunter#1491308406626324510]].
- **[Aspect of the Viper]** Confirmed to generate threat via its mana-tick regen, same mechanism as Shaman Water Shield / Paladin Seal of Wisdom — reported causing random aggro pulls onto the hunter mid-pull; unclear if intended — [[d:hunter#1491713716662833253]] [[d:hunter#1491714117369724928]] [[d:hunter#1491728542373773362]]. Also causes threat during solo Feign-Death-reset farming if not swapped off before FD (relevant for solo elite/panther farming) — [[d:hunter#1489321946599395388]] [[d:hunter#1489059767061184523]].
- **[Screech]** AoE attack-power-reduction debuff from Owl/Bat pets: single-target *damage* portion, but the AP-reduction debuff itself affects all enemies in range — makes it strong for AoE threat-holding while leveling and in some raid AoE, since no pet family otherwise gets a threat-generation bonus — [[d:hunter#1492967973319737506]] [[d:hunter#1492227704362242188]]. Threat value on the database is listed as 189 flat threat per application (scales poorly with AP, more like a flat baseline) — [[d:hunter#1491403250887299092]] [[d:hunter#1491405303378608209]] [[d:hunter#1491405339248033972]].
- **[Devilsaur Tooth]** Works with Kill Command, guaranteeing the pet's next hit crits; the crit-guarantee persists on the pet until its next hit lands, so it can be pre-popped and then triggered again after the pet's first crit — [[d:hunter#1492013965692108811]] [[d:hunter#1492033115118632981]] [[d:hunter#1492033139961368637]].
- **[Stable slots]** Base max is 4 free slots; a 5th requires a real-money donation-shop glyph (Glyph of the Beastkeeper) — the 4th costs 250g in-game — [[d:hunter#1490970543191494666]] [[d:hunter#1490969473480654962]]. Widely requested QoL: more stable slots (players cite wanting 10-20 given growing pet-family count and per-boss pet-swapping needs) — [[d:hunter#1491174759013158912]] [[d:hunter#1492163455728357628]] [[d:hunter#1492163843189899425]].
- **[Pet dismiss/feeding]** Dismissing a pet halves its happiness; a common trick is to leave the pet on Stay far away and let it despawn naturally (no happiness loss) rather than manually dismissing — [[d:hunter#1491067114516381706]] [[d:hunter#1491026532678766684]]. Feeding a pet right before a pull means it can die mid-fight and still count as "happy" afterward (happiness state persists) — [[d:hunter#1490815240936620214]] [[d:hunter#1491026532678766684]]. Pet food that grants 35 happiness/tick vs. 17/tick makes a large practical difference in feeding frequency — value shown in the combat log after feeding — [[d:hunter#1493584069458395236]] [[d:hunter#1493584160781242380]].
- **[Alone Against the World]** The talent granting +6% damage with no pet out (Survival capstone-adjacent) is intentionally not calculated for pet uptime in the community sim, since it's designed to not work with a pet — [[d:hunter#1490701761609076777]]; per TWoW devs it exists mainly as a QoL option for players who don't want to manage a pet in raids, since optimal pet play out-damages it — [[d:hunter#1493000939848728740]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[Naxx boss/pet interactions, per Kaslux, extensively tested]** Keep the pet in a good position generally; Kel'Thuzad and Sapphiron can one-shot the pet if mismanaged — [[d:hunter#1492148713832059001]]. Sapphiron's frost breath insta-kills the pet unless it's put behind the ice-block cover in time; Kel'Thuzad's frost blast damages but doesn't always one-shot the pet — [[d:hunter#1492149309926281358]] [[d:hunter#1492154254079561923]] [[d:hunter#1492154217660416010]] (KT auto-attacks were what actually killed the pet in Kaslux's logs). Heigan: no special pet handling needed. Four Horsemen (4HM): must supervise pet's "mark" positioning carefully, keep it on the exact same spot in the safe zone, and heal it there if no dedicated healer is assigned — [[d:hunter#1492152906202480740]] [[d:hunter#1492153011311611915]] [[d:hunter#1492153108208287783]]. Thaddius: pet does not get the Polarity debuff, so just keep the pet near Thaddius and follow the normal polarity swap order — [[d:hunter#1492153238009548800]]. Blaumeux/Zeliek: the holy chain and void zones never significantly affected Kaslux's pet — [[d:hunter#1492154411173281834]].
- **[Pet threat management, general]** No talent currently reduces pet threat (unlike hunter's own -threat talents), and pets can't be Feign-Death'd to drop aggro — a repeatedly requested QoL fix (-10% pet threat talent, or scaling Growl's base aggro) that doesn't exist yet — [[d:hunter#1491029265410691233]] [[d:hunter#1492155186318147684]] [[d:hunter#1492156387860545667]].
- **[BM off-tanking dungeons]** In dungeons the pet can over-aggro without causing real trouble as long as the healer keeps it topped up; for solo/duo content the pet can tank 2-4 mobs simultaneously while BM-specced — [[d:hunter#1492522963592020089]] [[d:hunter#1492991635598868482]].
- **[Solo pet-tanking elites]** A fully tank-talented pet with 3-piece T1 (or with Viper Aspect for mana sustain) can facetank many "elite" open-world mobs without kiting; even a squishy DPS-spec Raptor can tank things like Frostmaul Giants — [[d:hunter#1493986952620408922]] [[d:hunter#1493987043406250034]] [[d:hunter#1494046920266612921]].

- **[Mend Pet]** Scales from something on the pet (reported healing 500-600 per tick without crits) — specifically scales with pet AP per follow-up testing — [[d:hunter#1490826263865069801]] [[d:hunter#1490833195397025822]]. No known additional gear/talent source currently boosts Mend Pet beyond its base scaling, a gap noted by limbless who wanted to test whether it could solo-carry content by itself — [[d:hunter#1491782111680790755]] [[d:hunter#1491782194438471680]].
- **[Moth's Pollen Burst]** Heals nearby allies for ~300 HP and applies a -20% attack-speed debuff to nearby enemies; heal scales with the *pet's* AP — [[d:hunter#1490818006291451984]] [[d:hunter#1490833195397025822]] see also 1.18.1 bug note above (ability is currently unlearnable/lost at max pet loyalty).
- **[Vampirism/lifesteal for solo survival]** "% of damage returned as healing" items/talents apply only to melee damage, not to traps/DoTs/Serpent Sting — relevant for Survival soloing/leveling builds that stack lifesteal for sustain (one player reported 13% total lifesteal from gear+aspect at level 50) — see Enchants section [[d:hunter#1489360309213724672]] [[d:hunter#1489350033395683501]].

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[BWL, Rupturan]** Phase 2 strat (per Dresche/Sofie): raid stacks on the three adds and burst-kills them together; MM hunters stand right at the edge of the deadzone (one step further than melee range) so Boulder attacks miss them, and on Flamestrike everyone side-steps 10 yards together as a group — "trivializes the second phase almost 100%," turning it into a pure burst-down fight — [[d:hunter#1491057592150134956]] [[d:hunter#1491057909038059752]]. MM is fully viable on Rupturan without losing the pet, in both phases (log linked as proof) — [[d:hunter#1491050771704315974]] [[d:hunter#1491052987236356157]]. The same MM deadzone-edge trick works on AQ40's large Anubisaths with Shadow Flame — stand at the exact deadzone line to dodge the "move into the mob" mechanic while still landing shots — [[d:hunter#1491059182638727278]].
- **[BWL, general]** Nefarian's class-call weapon-destruction mechanic ("the new mechanic") causes large gold loss (or big DPS loss if you preemptively unequip weapons) since it now affects *all* equipped weapons including for dual-wielders — considered "kinda stupid" design; the workaround is to unequip weapons via macro right before the call resolves — [[d:hunter#1490088270590382304]] [[d:hunter#1490144166594744380]] [[d:hunter#1490144504626548817]]. DPS output specifically doesn't matter on the Nefarian encounter itself (implying the fight isn't dps-checked) — [[d:hunter#1490103210059366460]].
- **[Naxx]** See Tanking specifics for full per-boss pet-positioning notes (KT, Sapphiron, 4HM, Thaddius, Blaumeux/Zeliek). Loatheb is a known weak fight for BM: the pet does 50% of BM's damage but can't benefit from the boss's Spore Cloud buff, which only affects players — BM falls behind on this fight as a result — [[d:hunter#1489049723296944198]] [[d:hunter#1489050159705882654]]; Warlock pets by contrast do inherit warlock crit and thus don't suffer the same penalty on Loatheb — [[d:hunter#1492156660737638540]] [[d:hunter#1492156732393132162]]. C'Thun: hunters commonly leave the scorpid on C'Thun itself (to build/maintain poison stacks) and kill tentacles/eye-stalks personally — a fully snapshotted scorpid poison can maintain perma-uptime on C'Thun for good sustained damage — [[d:hunter#1492164517449433190]] [[d:hunter#1492164599313596578]].
- **[Karazhan Crypt / Kara10 ("Tower of Karazhan")]** A recognized solo-farm target for shadow-caster trash; patch changed casters to stop moving while casting (previously they'd walk-and-cancel their casts when kited out of range) — makes the farm much harder now, "zero margin for error" — [[d:hunter#1491814378562129970]] [[d:hunter#1491818790629937383]] [[d:hunter#1491820130995408986]] [[d:hunter#1491821734377033738]]. Farm involves specific pull angles/corners and careful positioning at two marked spots to avoid pulling extra casters — [[d:hunter#1491818593372082256]] [[d:hunter#1491819815013453985]] [[d:hunter#1491820086137458790]].
- **[ZG solo/duo farm]** Doable post-nerf (sleep-poison range increased from ~10-20 to ~35-40 yards) with practice; core techniques are pulling with pet + Furious Howl (Wolf pet) + Feign Death reset, timing safety margins around Wyvern Sting range, using a PvP trinket + limited invulnerability potions as an emergency out — [[d:hunter#1491813464706912308]] [[d:hunter#1491813731288486029]] [[d:hunter#1491826077381951550]]. Reported clear time ~12-25 minutes depending on skill/gear — [[d:hunter#1489209789543350353]] [[d:hunter#1491813793339150418]]. Difficulty ranking given by Sofie, easiest to hardest: Naxx trash farm → ZG → Kara10 — [[d:hunter#1491814378562129970]].
- **[ZG raptor solo-tame]** ZG raptors are immune to Frost Trap; the reliable method is to Fear them (not immune to Fear) after isolating a single raptor from its pack at a specific spot in the southwest corner of the room before the raptor boss, feign-death timing critical to avoid pulling two at once — [[d:hunter#1489903969345081477]] [[d:hunter#1489906331166052382]] [[d:hunter#1489910056399933470]] [[d:hunter#1489910751916331038]].
- **[DMW (Dragonmaw quarry-ish, Hateforge/DMW) arrow-recipe farm]** Second boss can be solo-farmed as BM by hiding behind a statue to bait the boss to approach while the pet holds aggro, then FD to reset threat and re-pull; farmed for a craftable arrow recipe — [[d:hunter#1490147535392739359]] [[d:hunter#1490152762330189914]] [[d:hunter#1490152898267582524]].
- **[Ferra's Collar / arrow recipe bear farm]** Solo-farmable bear boss drops the "Ferra's Collar" trinket (4% pet damage) and a craftable-arrow blueprint; reported drop rates are low and inconsistent per-farmer (one reported the collar on run ~16 of 22, another needed 15 runs for one collar and 29 for the arrow blueprint but got 4 collars total over that span) — [[d:hunter#1489716181324795995]] [[d:hunter#1489716764538306791]] [[d:hunter#1489716923108294676]] [[d:hunter#1489748961282490378]].

## PvP
- **[Spec strength]** BM is the strongest/easiest PvP spec currently: pets solo people while the hunter LOS/kites, and pet damage is not dampened in PvP the way player damage is — [[d:hunter#1489662317913440306]] [[d:hunter#1490803074963476650]] [[d:hunter#1491028211960643695]]. MM is a "free kill" once melee closes to the deadzone; Survival can trade with warriors 1v1 if played well but lacks gap closers, self-heals, and reliable CC and mostly wins via burst — [[d:hunter#1489546377963765835]] [[d:hunter#1489552360459206706]] [[d:hunter#1489620614468866290]].
- **[BM PvP talents]** Coordinated Assault called "one of the best talents, period" for PvP — pet rarely dies before the hunter unless badly mismanaged — [[d:hunter#1489618477168984295]] [[d:hunter#1489618725995941979]]. Entrapment (root on trap-hit) is strong for locking chokepoints, especially in Alterac Valley — [[d:hunter#1489619404449779883]]. Improved Wing Clip is a decent (not amazing) melee-range slow for healers/flag-carriers — [[d:hunter#1489619404449779883]] [[d:hunter#1489645781106294905]].
- **[MM/Survival PvP build]** "MM down to L&L and surv down to Survivalist is BIS," skipping 3/3 Multi-Shot — [[d:hunter#1492275874207105184]]. Multi-Shot is still used as a fast double-hit finisher/opener despite not proccing Lock and Load — [[d:hunter#1492279977125740675]] [[d:hunter#1492280220827385956]].
- **[Pet vs. hunter focus fire]** Focusing the pet is the standard counter to a BM hunter in battlegrounds; junabii argues it's no more punishing than focusing any other class's burst window, and a dead pet is trivially re-summoned — pet is often used as a "dive tank" to draw fire off the hunter, e.g. sent at the enemy backline healer — [[d:hunter#1492848863487328286]] [[d:hunter#1492849347954741308]] [[d:hunter#1492849406070755479]].
- **[PvP resilience/survivability]** Testing suggests stamina + resilience gear is worthwhile for BM in PvP for personal survivability, but the pet itself gets *no* resilience and is thus very fragile and easily focused/killed even under Bestial Wrath — an "Improved Rez" pet-resurrection trinket (+15 agi, faster pet rez) is considered close to mandatory for BM in arena once available — [[d:hunter#1492798156843388978]] [[d:hunter#1492801526450884838]] [[d:hunter#1492816153930760323]].
- **[Mage matchup]** Mages can counter a Bestial-Wrath BM burst by double Ice-Blocking to wait it out, then Frost Nova + deadzone kite; doing so however uses two long CC casts that expose the mage — [[d:hunter#1492850660142612513]] [[d:hunter#1492850912945770506]] [[d:hunter#1492850969669533867]] [[d:hunter#1492851214113706034]]. Kill Command crits reportedly one-shot/near-delete squishy casters post-patch, generating "hate whispers" from mages — [[d:hunter#1492850599396642858]].
- **[Resist mechanics]** Improved Concussive Shot's stun can be resisted (reported happening, and can be Grounding-Totem'd), and resistance chance against hunter pets/hunters specifically increases the more the target fights hunters — [[d:hunter#1489647180842012854]] [[d:hunter#1489647841629438005]] [[d:hunter#1489647965919252633]].

## Contested / open questions
- **[Haste value for MM]** Whether haste is a net positive or negative for the current MM rotation is actively disputed: majority view (Boomsky, The J, limbless) says haste causes more clipping and is weak; Focus argues via forum math that ~10% haste is worth ~1% overall dps and that crit's post-patch buff makes crit stacking better justified — Oojala/Boomsky call his reasoning inconsistent ("crit becomes better which means the crit cloak becomes worse, logic ain't logicing") — [[d:hunter#1491111488214007951]] [[d:hunter#1490408079089471508]] [[d:hunter#1490410061472731157]] — unresolved by end of chunk.
- **[Optimal pet attack speed for MM/Survival with Windfury]** Whether a 1.5-speed or 2.0-speed pet is better for MM/Survival groups with a shaman is repeatedly asked and answered inconsistently — most say the dps difference is small either way, some favor slower pets since MM/Survival lack Frenzy (which "wastes" a fast pet's attack-interval reduction) — [[d:hunter#1490841185067991291]] [[d:hunter#1492542337325469918]] [[d:hunter#1490799952694218782]] — question resurfaces unanswered multiple times ([[d:hunter#1493037568454037634]] [[d:hunter#1493664333257769001]]).
- **[BM vs MM overall raid dps ranking]** No firm consensus — "BM and MM are pretty close tbh, they got balance right... variance will come mostly from differences in encounters" (limbless) vs. others claiming MM pulls ahead with enough gear/bis trinkets (yaboikeenser) — [[d:hunter#1491404375711809567]] [[d:hunter#1492335952436396093]] — treated as still an open question pending more logs.
- **[Whether pets should inherit hunter crit/haste]** Extensively debated design wish (not an existing mechanic): proposals to let pets scale partially off hunter crit/haste (like Warlock pets inheriting crit) to fix BM's late-game crit-capped-at-20%-via-Spirit-Bond scaling problem — no resolution, purely community wishlist/dev-feedback discussion — [[d:hunter#1492155250512236768]] [[d:hunter#1492156021811052775]] [[d:hunter#1492158486728151142]] [[d:hunter#1492159789508071626]].
- **[Savage Rend / Wolf Howl exact AP scaling]** Community was actively crowdsourcing exact scaling coefficients for Savage Rend and Wolf's Furious Howl to feed into Vellasta's sim; partial numbers found (Furious Howl: +1 physical damage per 25 pet AP, melee-only) but Savage Rend's initial-hit scaling required multiple rounds of testing and was still being refined at the end of the chunk — [[d:hunter#1490710371718402218]] [[d:hunter#1490710861105594459]] [[d:hunter#1492483447049027794]] [[d:hunter#1492491772830416986]].
- **[Ebonroc BWL stealth-pull skip post-patch]** Asked whether the pre-1.18.1 stealth-pull-into-2nd-suppression-room trick still works after boss mechanic changes — no answer given in this chunk — [[d:hunter#1491574117781213245]].
- **[Whether ranged AP "stacks" for BM pet damage / which items double-count]** Multiple unresolved item-interaction questions floated without confirmation: does the Onyxia warlock off-hand's 5% pet damage work for hunters ([[d:hunter#1492425676291178536]] [[d:hunter#1492425886824272043]]); does Shieldrender affect the pet ([[d:hunter#1493592665823121529]] [[d:hunter#1493594698558734397]]); does the orc 5% pet-damage racial double-dip on a Hyena's second summoned ally (tested as apparently "still bugged"/unclear) — [[d:hunter#1492186998801301685]] [[d:hunter#1492191878907691089]] [[d:hunter#1492193537259868261]].
