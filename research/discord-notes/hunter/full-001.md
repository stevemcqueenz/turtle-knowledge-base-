## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** Consistent consensus across the chunk: Survival (melee) > Marksmanship > Beast Mastery for raid DPS; BM is buggy and rarely used at endgame despite doing more raw pet-inclusive damage than MM in some comparisons — [[d:hunter#1438139122991497216]], [[d:hunter#1439022684032733195]], [[d:hunter#1441951990396751882]] (2025-11-12/17/23; recurring +many reactions across thread).
- **[SV]** Detailed raid-log comparison: a SV hunter did 10% more total damage than two MM hunters in the same Kara40 raid despite leaving before the last boss (8/9 vs 9/9 bosses), with all three ~95% equally geared (full T3.5, relevant trinkets) — [[d:hunter#1437385735656443966]] (2025-11-10, single detailed source).
- **[MM]** Reasons MM lags behind SV in raids: MM gets no benefit from melee-group buffs (Battle Shout, Windfury Totem) while SV does; MM's raid utility (Trueshot Aura) is real but personal DPS is weak; MM is safer (no melee mechanics) which is its main selling point — [[d:hunter#1437768822114226196]], [[d:hunter#1437482918200217713]], [[d:hunter#1439022769143681095]] (2025-11-10/11).
- **[MM]** Some MM players (junabii) don't want the spec's rotation touched, calling it "already fun and strong" and fearing an "annoying bulky rotation like BM" — [[d:hunter#1440805018059145236]] (2025-11-20; disputed by majority calling MM weak).
- **[BM]** Community tier take (Minces, extended post, +reactions): BM is clunky, requires already being good at MM, and does the least raid DPS; MM is "mid," needs strong raid knowledge and CD timing, "loses to laser chicken" (Balance druid); Survival is the best of the three and easiest, but has weak single-target (even DW) and severe mana issues pre-Naxx, and gets outpaced on AoE by warrior/mage of equal skill — [[d:hunter#1440943795003723806]] (2025-11-19).
- **[General]** Hunter overall is described as "one of the weaker classes" for raid DPS; a fully Naxx-geared hunter can reach top-5 in a raid but starts much lower, and hunters rarely appear in server-wide top-30 parses — [[d:hunter#1439026097751720129]], [[d:hunter#1439026159055671306]], [[d:hunter#1440947140246638693]] (2025-11-15/19).
- **[BM]** BM's core complaint: pet cannot hold aggro once the hunter is well-geared (hunter power scales faster than pet); armor and pet-damage talents were nerfed "not long ago", worsening this — [[d:hunter#1441056946676302028]], [[d:hunter#1441057459857920030]], [[d:hunter#1441064457961869362]] (2025-11-20/21).
- **[BM]** No hunter build fully skips the pet for more DPS — pet is always a net gain — though it can be dismissed for style/challenge — [[d:hunter#1438623232432275740]] (2025-11-14).
- **[MM]** MM praised for one niche: pulling packs from 41 yards with Hawk Eye range talent, invalidating some mechanics and enabling safe pulls (e.g. the pack after Vael in BWL) — [[d:hunter#1437611567360249917]], [[d:hunter#1437599373365084209]] (2025-11-11).
- **[BM]** Extended late-window consensus (Psyker, detailed, +reactions): SV is doing well, MM and BM are "bottom of the pack"; most hunters consider BM too risky/jank for endgame raiding (K40); a given week's K40 logs had zero BM hunters; ~99% of raiding hunters play SV or MM, with top performers often swapping between the two per-boss — [[d:hunter#1444742501121523773]], [[d:hunter#1444742784262078484]], [[d:hunter#1444744693727629322]], [[d:hunter#1444747035625390150]] (2025-11-30/12-01).
- **[BM]** BM's structural raid problem beyond bugs: the pet doesn't scale from the hunter's defensive stats, so a single boss cleave (example given: Kruul in K40) can remove roughly half the pet's HP, forcing frequent repositioning/pulling the pet back — a recurring DPS tax in later-phase content — [[d:hunter#1444749426152444146]], [[d:hunter#1444749815564079254]] (2025-12-01).
- **[BM]** A "full BM hunter" cleared BWL and posted a parse as counter-evidence to "BM is useless" claims; the more common response was that BM isn't literally useless, just still bugged and unfun — one long-time player called it "the worst feeling spec I have ever played in classic," and reported several hunters (including half of a hunter-only guild) quit after the BM rework patch — [[d:hunter#1444505032857751622]], [[d:hunter#1444507668206194761]], [[d:hunter#1444742712707252496]] (2025-11-30).
- **[BM]** Manually micromanaging pet focus/ability casts (rather than leaving abilities on full autocast, which defaults to spamming Claw ~99% of the time) noticeably increases BM pet damage — [[d:hunter#1444509019623526520]], [[d:hunter#1444509163261657220]] (2025-11-30).
- **[SV]** At Kara40, some guilds swap a hunter's spec/weapon mid-raid by boss: e.g. Survival until Rupturan → Marksman for Rupturan → back to Survival before Kruul, or Survival until Rupturan → Marksman for the rest of the raid (as the designated "eye baiter" on Mephistroth) — [[d:hunter#1446047436425400394]] (2025-12-04).

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[MM/BM]** Two "strongest 2-talent combinations for single-target DPS" per Skurridizo el Cazador, with Survival noted as having much better AoE/burst instead: `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/hunter?points=FYQABoIoBCQYB-AoCoQBAo-` and `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/hunter?points=FgQABoAoBCQQ-AoCoQBAoAC-` — [[d:hunter#1438280578603421838]] (2025-11-13).
- **[BM/SV]** No good BM/Survival hybrid exists; the deepest workable hybrid is roughly 17/0/35-ish, since BM's benefits (Untamed Ferocity/Spirit Bond, Lightning Reflexes) require going deep in one tree — [[d:hunter#1436338777944494130]], [[d:hunter#1436339174058758166]], [[d:hunter#1437958839679848510]], [[d:hunter#1437961159839060118]] (2025-11-07/12).
- **[SV]** Untamed Trapper (lets traps be used in combat) requires 30 points spent in the Survival tree — [[d:hunter#1438179260715110534]] (2025-11-13).
- **[SV]** Lightning Reflexes (Survival talent) is required to convert Agility/Strength into meaningful melee Attack Power; without any Survival talent, Hunters get only 1 melee AP per point of Agility or Strength (base) — [[d:hunter#1439030590732243079]], [[d:hunter#1439032562898043113]], [[d:hunter#1440726391716053062]] (2025-11-15/19). With the Survival talent, 1 Agility = 2 melee AP.
- **[BM]** Bestial Discipline (20% cooldown reduction) applies only to a pet's "special family" abilities (e.g. Howl), not to general abilities like bite/claw — [[d:hunter#1441098595406450719]], [[d:hunter#1441099684641767447]] (2025-11-20).
- **[SV]** Removing points from trap talents (Combat Traps, Trap Damage Enhancement) for fire-immune bosses is possible but the Brainwasher Device respec item has a 10-minute cooldown, limiting how often you can flip back; benefit of pulling those points is roughly 4 points into pet damage instead — [[d:hunter#1437148144944152720]], [[d:hunter#1437150169748607006]], [[d:hunter#1437150351093665842]] (2025-11-09).
- **[SV]** With a 2H weapon (as opposed to dual-wield), only put 1 point in Vicious Strikes — the 2nd point does nothing because weapon speed caps how often Raptor Strike can be used; 1/2 also "feels more fluent" in the rotation than 2/2 — [[d:hunter#1447201318476316763]] (2025-12-06).
- **[BM, low gear]** Suggested low-gear BM build: 29-22-0 split (talent-calculator link: `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/hunter?points=FYQABoBoBCQQ-AoCoQBAoAC-`), paired with Scorpion (pre-MC minmax), Wind Serpent (once in MC, if comfortable with Kill Command + Lightning Breath), or Cobra (low-maintenance DPS) — [[d:hunter#1444722642824200243]] (2025-12-01, single source).
- **[Raiding transition]** Suggested point to switch a leveling BM hunter into a raiding spec is "when you start raiding," with 11/35/5 (mostly MM, some BM) suggested as a good raiding spec with a little BM utility retained — [[d:hunter#1444657461951991910]] (2025-11-30, single source, exact tree order unconfirmed).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Easiest leveling spec is widely agreed to be BM ("animal abuse spec", own tank, least downtime); Survival is also viable/fun but more gear-dependent early since it scales with melee weapon damage — [[d:hunter#1435269495236198606]], [[d:hunter#1435275630701842594]], [[d:hunter#1436865817990922271]] (2025-11-04/09).
- **[BM]** Leveling talent order debate: (a) rush 31 points into BM tree, then grab Steady Shot from Marksman, use Baited Shot on cooldown — [[d:hunter#1435341358024888352]] (2025-11-04); (b) go BM first because more ranged damage makes it harder for the pet to hold aggro — [[d:hunter#1437448103757090958]] (2025-11-10); (c) go Steady Shot first, then fill BM tree — this delays Coordinated Assault to level 31 and Kill Command to level 41, but Demonhun argues Steady Shot is "mandatory," while Elmgren counters that early pet damage (e.g. Wind Serpent, whose lightning generates enough damage to avoid overaggro even with growl off) matters more while leveling — [[d:hunter#1438789559067476101]], [[d:hunter#1438792649292386344]], [[d:hunter#1438793496487006228]], [[d:hunter#1438794744812666983]], [[d:hunter#1438795008101580842]] (2025-11-14). Rush the Pathfinding talent early for travel speed regardless — [[d:hunter#1438795111030067252]].
- **[SV]** Around level 35, respec to grab Lightning Reflexes then Untamed Trapper to go full Survival; reported as a DPS jump comparable to getting optimized raid gear on a fresh 60 — [[d:hunter#1440729969121820673]], [[d:hunter#1440730384747855885]] (2025-11-19).
- **[SV]** Fastest known 1-60 route: level BM to ~50, then switch to Survival to solo-farm Dire Maul East lasher room repeatedly, reaching 60 in 1-2 days of medium play — [[d:hunter#1438684909622394971]] (2025-11-14, image build attached).
- **[General]** No 2H ranged weapon can be crafted for the Travelling Craftmaster/Vagrant's Endeavour challenge; best approach is Survival + Blacksmith/Leatherworking, sending mats to craft on demand — [[d:hunter#1439794833026711593]], [[d:hunter#1439806795806806176]], [[d:hunter#1439806923095674930]] (2025-11-17).
- **[General]** For the Vagrant's Endeavour challenge, BM is the recommended spec — [[d:hunter#1436207270987038722]] (2025-11-07).
- **[BM]** Hardcore leveling: BM is still viable post-1.18 despite pet-survivability complaints, "a bit worse but still very good" — [[d:hunter#1436216639489052692]], [[d:hunter#1436220296590921758]] (2025-11-07).
- **[Stats]** No Strength needed for ranged leveling; Agility is the only needed leveling stat for BM/MM — [[d:hunter#1437980775977848893]], [[d:hunter#1438063976117436426]] (2025-11-12).
- **[Weapon skill]** Weapon skill quests are worth doing: every 5 weapon skill points (up to 315) reduces the Hit requirement by 1%, freeing gear slots for other stats; skill also mitigates melee glancing blows — [[d:hunter#1438645089759920290]], [[d:hunter#1438641239175073802]] (2025-11-14).
- **[Pets]** All pets (even elemental ones like Wind Serpent) reach identical base stats at level 60: 136 Str, 100 Agi, 50 Int — [[d:hunter#1438867020556013678]] (2025-11-14).
- **[Pets]** Rares/elites give no better tamed-pet stats; all mobs of the same pet family share identical stat modifiers whether common, rare, or elite — [[d:hunter#1441778916011282473]] (2025-11-22).
- **[General]** Post-patch leveling-spec consensus repeated late in the window: BM until level 40, then switch to Survival — [[d:hunter#1446906876657795112]] (2025-12-06).
- **[BM leveling]** Wind Serpent leveling macro/approach: don't leave Growl/Lightning Breath on full autocast; macro `#showtooltip Lightning Breath(Rank 6)` / `/cast Kill Command` / `/script CastPetAction(4)`; only turn Growl on when actually needed — two Lightning Breath crits alone are often enough threat while leveling — [[d:hunter#1444975316782288896]], [[d:hunter#1445001223915704452]] (2025-12-01).
- **[Pet taming]** When a taming quest gives "Invalid Target," check you're targeting the exact named variant (e.g. "Surf Crawler," not "Pygmy Surf Crawler") — [[d:hunter#1446959787786109103]], [[d:hunter#1446965863654621285]] (2025-12-07).
- **[Bug]** Newly tamed pets can vanish entirely ("you have no pet," no message) if not fed enough in their first hour; this is reported repeatedly and is described as normal hunter-class behavior across servers (not Turtle-specific), tied to low starting ("Rebellious") loyalty burning through happiness fast — feed with level-appropriate food until the pet reaches a few loyalty ranks; a UI addon (pfUI) shows happiness as colored arrows next to the pet portrait, which can be easy to misread — [[d:hunter#1445466727768129599]], [[d:hunter#1445469544406585344]], [[d:hunter#1445469966076874882]], [[d:hunter#1447222831115927695]], [[d:hunter#1447223191830528212]] (2025-12-02/07).

## Rotation: single target
- **[MM/BM]** Steady Shot is described as mandatory for both MM and BM (contributes roughly "a third" of DPS); weaving it between auto shots ("auto > steady > auto > steady", adding baited/multi/arcane/serpent sting between if using a slow weapon) is the core loop — [[d:hunter#1438843725345329223]], [[d:hunter#1438944686151700531]], [[d:hunter#1439021845948006410]] (2025-11-14/15).
- **[MM/BM]** Removing Steady Shot from the rotation costs roughly 30-50% of ranged DPS — [[d:hunter#1439720145160376430]] (2025-11-17); it matters much less for BM since a large share of BM's damage comes from the pet — [[d:hunter#1439735356609007788]], [[d:hunter#1439735644925460522]] (2025-11-16).
- **[MM]** Arcane Shot scales with spell power, not attack power, and is considered a weak/meme ability outside a dedicated spell-power build — [[d:hunter#1439903975078891531]] (2025-11-17).
- **[MM]** Multi Shot: prioritize casting it off cooldown even on single target if there's any chance of adds; otherwise stick to the same steady/auto weave and only delay Multi Shot a few seconds if adds are about to spawn — [[d:hunter#1439273330829037668]], [[d:hunter#1439273413418946611]], [[d:hunter#1439299980455055442]], [[d:hunter#1439304494348369970]] (2025-11-17).
- **[BM leveling]** Early BM rotation while leveling: Hunter's Mark, send pet, open with Kill Command, then Arcane Shot + auto until mob dies (~15s); repeat Kill Command + Arcane Shot if longer; Multi Shot is skipped as too mana-expensive for the DPS gained (~2s faster kill vs. longer mana recovery) — [[d:hunter#1440105658518212659]], [[d:hunter#1440106180675371170]], [[d:hunter#1440106926347128963]] (2025-11-18). Sofie instead recommends respeccing MM while leveling for better mana sustain and using only auto-steady-auto-steady, adding Multi Shot once 60/with multiple mobs — [[d:hunter#1440326918799884331]] (2025-11-18).
- **[BM]** Kill Command bug: only reliably crits (and only one pet ability) on the first cast as the pet approaches at the start of a fight; mid-fight casts are unreliable and waste a GCD/mana — [[d:hunter#1440111133699870730]], [[d:hunter#1440114539621253251]] (2025-11-18). One theory: pets on autocast try to fire off-cooldown abilities even without enough focus, which seems to interfere with Kill Command's crit proc; switching all pet abilities to manual cast made Kill Command and Coordinated Assault crits consistent — [[d:hunter#1441362114286583900]], [[d:hunter#1441362375105314978]] (2025-11-21, single source, worth testing).
- **[BM]** Coordinated Assault ("Strike Together" in combat log) is coded oddly: it counts as the hunter's own attack (adds to hunter aggro, not the pet's), can trigger frost-armor chill effects on the ranged hunter, and does not reliably trigger the pet's 30% attack-speed proc despite scaling off Kill Command crit — [[d:hunter#1440104106608951307]], [[d:hunter#1440455203131363341]], [[d:hunter#1440466089883729960]] (2025-11-18). Despite the bugs it's high value: ~6% of one SV hunter's Naxx-geared DPS, called "the single best 1 talent point you can spend"; for an MM hunter it out-damaged Extra Shot or Piercing Shots — [[d:hunter#1440461873311912088]], [[d:hunter#1440465437618864228]] (2025-11-18).
- **[BM]** Baited Shot doesn't stay castable as long as it's supposed to, on top of not reliably proc'ing off Coordinated Assault crits; workaround until fixed: run a 1.5-speed pet with max points in Frenzy to maximize Baited Shot uptime — [[d:hunter#1440117469124759745]], [[d:hunter#1440118017815089202]] (2025-11-18).
- **[Mechanic]** Steady Shot's true cast time is claimed to be a fixed 1s portion reduced by haste plus a hidden, haste-unaffected 0.5s tacked on afterward (total base 1.675s); Multi Shot's GCD is 1.5s not 1.0s — claimed by Skurridizo el Cazador, later independently confirmed by Boomsky via dummy testing after initially disputing it — [[d:hunter#1441899060213579996]], [[d:hunter#1441905986120978452]] (2025-11-23).
- **[SV]** Mongoose Bite damage = a percentage of weapon damage + a flat amount, dealt as physical damage (mitigated by armor) — [[d:hunter#1440725733185032322]] (2025-11-19).
- **[SV]** Slower weapons are better for Survival: instant attacks (Raptor Strike, Mongoose Bite) scale off weapon damage, so a slower weapon's higher per-hit damage roll increases their damage — [[d:hunter#1440665630075256893]], [[d:hunter#1440666315416404058]], [[d:hunter#1440669671085838466]] (2025-11-19). Countered/nuanced later: the actual top-damage-roll difference between weapon speeds is often small (e.g. 110 vs 106), so the real choice is often between a slow weapon's stat budget vs. a fast weapon's on-hit proc value (e.g. Fiery) — [[d:hunter#1442177085312602162]], [[d:hunter#1442177433879973949]] (2025-11-23).
- **[MM]** Detailed MM weave (Boomsky, +3 reactions): Steady Shot between every auto shot; Aimed Shot only as a precast/opener or timed around a boss immunity/shield dropping; Steady+Multi between autos when both are up, replacing Steady with Multi if using a fast weapon with attack-speed procs; Arcane Shot/Serpent Sting can be squeezed in after a Steady Shot only with a slow weapon, and only once comfortable with the base rotation — [[d:hunter#1444479298634514555]] (2025-11-30). Arcane Shot is called "utterly useless" for damage in the normal MM rotation; Serpent Sting is also skipped — [[d:hunter#1444450272054214859]] (2025-11-30). Scorpid Sting/Wyvern Sting are situational only (Wyvern mainly for Moam in AQ20; Scorpid Sting on strong elites, since many raid bosses resist/are immune to it) — [[d:hunter#1444450849148375191]] (2025-11-30).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[MM/SV]** Hit cap is 8% base for melee/ranged attacks and skills; drops to 7% if you have the weapon skill book; ranged uses the same hit cap as melee — [[d:hunter#1435990248369815633]], [[d:hunter#1435990323611308042]], [[d:hunter#1435990496945377471]] (2025-11-06).
- **[SV/DW]** Dual-wield hit requirement is much higher (one claim: pushed to ~26%, uncertain) due to DW's extra miss chance; while leveling (no weapon skill/hit gear) DW miss chance can exceed 20%, making 3% extra hit relatively meaningless until endgame — [[d:hunter#1435990470747754496]] (uncertain value), [[d:hunter#1440538925885030450]], [[d:hunter#1440543752815906856]] (2025-11-06/18).
- **[Ranged]** Ranged weapon skill only affects hit% (1% per 5 skill); ranged attacks can't glance, so skill does nothing else. A racial +3 bow skill (e.g. High Elf) gives 0.6% hit, vs. +5 giving 1%; pointless once already hit-capped at endgame — [[d:hunter#1436397828997120052]], [[d:hunter#1436413861434626148]], [[d:hunter#1436414126174900386]], [[d:hunter#1436414233171722403]] (2025-11-07).
- **[General]** Attack Power benefits both ranged and melee AP for hunters (it is not split/wasted on off-spec items) — [[d:hunter#1436245039096533072]], [[d:hunter#1436263896922198047]] (2025-11-07).
- **[MM]** Crit-to-AP-equivalent conversion for MM: roughly 30-40 APe per 1% crit depending on gear (35 used as a default); 1 Agility ≈ 2.7 APe is questioned as possibly outdated — crit is suspected to be valued lower on Turtle than in classic since Steady Shot (not Aimed Shot) is the main filler, giving AP more relative value — [[d:hunter#1435543107390410843]], [[d:hunter#1435548367311605810]] (2025-11-05).
- **[Haste]** Weapon/gear haste only improves auto-attack speed; Agility improves all abilities (not just autos) — [[d:hunter#1437291037625155585]] (2025-11-10).
- **[Frenzy mechanic]** Pet haste sources multiply rather than add: Frenzy's 30% haste on a 2.0-speed pet yields 1.54 speed, not 1.40, because haste = base speed / (product of each haste multiplier) — [[d:hunter#1437019733932118076]], [[d:hunter#1437022351152906341]], [[d:hunter#1437032136476725310]] (2025-11-09).
- **[ArP]** Armor Pen value for hunters: use the warrior baseline (1 AP = 3 ArP) but apply roughly a 0.7x multiplier, since traps/stings get no benefit from ArP; ArP's value also scales inversely with target armor and increases with the hunter's own base AP (diminishing/scaling returns, situational) — [[d:hunter#1437469182307340460]], [[d:hunter#1437471531545526382]], [[d:hunter#1437471683870195764]], [[d:hunter#1437471815084802230]], [[d:hunter#1437471934727327976]] (2025-11-10).
- **[Pet stats]** Pet stat scaling mirrors warriors: 1 Strength = 2 AP for the pet; pet ability spell-power scaling only applies if the hunter has the Spirit Bond talent — [[d:hunter#1438910045877178520]], [[d:hunter#1438910250047504434]] (2025-11-14).
- **[MM]** Haste is worth roughly 15-20 AP equivalent for MM, but is easy to "lose" (wasted via clipping) during Rapid Fire/Bloodlust — [[d:hunter#1441596579328036864]], [[d:hunter#1441608283759706132]] (2025-11-21).
- **[Weapon skill]** Precise hit-cap math confirmed twice: baseline is 8% hit needed for 2H/ranged; every 5 weapon skill points reduces the needed hit by 1% (308→7%, 310→6%, 315→5%); going 300→315 weapon skill also raises glancing-blow damage from 65% to 95% of a normal hit, and glancing blows make up roughly 40% of white/auto hits against raid bosses — [[d:hunter#1446986323910201526]], [[d:hunter#1445632351936974919]], [[d:hunter#1445632577397329961]] (2025-12-03/06).
- **[Crit/Agi]** Concrete conversion example (Santa Bear): 53 Agility gives +1% crit and ~110 AP (with Kings + talents); using 1 crit% ≈ 32 AP as a baseline, that puts 1 Agility at roughly 2.7 AP, and ~11.85 Agility ≈ 1% crit in overall value — varies by tier/talents/gear/Blessing of Kings — [[d:hunter#1446085446018011208]], [[d:hunter#1446086997260238971]] (2025-12-04).
- **[ArP formula]** Precise Armor Penetration formula shared by Dresche: `(100 × EffectiveArmorPen) / (TargetArmor + 85 × PlayerLevel − EffectiveArmorPen + 400)`; rule of thumb: ArP's value rises the closer it is to the target's armor value (e.g. 100 ArP vs. 2000 armor has close to zero effect) — [[d:hunter#1446488573040660650]], [[d:hunter#1446490824635318404]] (2025-12-05). Community view: Turtle doesn't have enough ArP sources to meaningfully close the gap the way late-TBC gear/debuffs did; most bosses end up under ~1000 armor once debuffed anyway, so RAP or haste is often preferred over ArP on optional gear slots — [[d:hunter#1446497208672321587]] (2025-12-05).
- **[Haste, SV]** Community consensus estimate: 1% haste ≈ 10-20 effective AP for SV, though very hard to calculate precisely and the value differs completely between SV and MM — [[d:hunter#1446118389289582666]] (2025-12-04).
- **[Haste, MM]** 2% haste is said to beat +6 Agility "every time" unless the hunter's shots are clipping (canceling an auto shot by casting too early) — with good noclip-macro play the difference favors haste; skill/consistency of play matters a lot here — [[d:hunter#1446126698675568680]], [[d:hunter#1446130303088328764]], [[d:hunter#1446130423464722533]] (2025-12-04).
- **[eAP tools]** A community effective-AP (eAP) spreadsheet/table exists (shared by Dresche) for comparing gear; it is phase-dependent (different values for a later-phase server like Nordanaar vs. an earlier-phase one like Ambershire); hit-derived eAP stops increasing once hit-capped, except for 2H1H Survival where residual value is harder to calculate; Turtle's higher shot/swing frequency than classic (Steady Shot spam vs. classic's Aimed-Shot-centric play) makes 1 Agility's eAP slightly higher on Turtle than in classic even without world buffs — [[d:hunter#1446098423022616586]], [[d:hunter#1446109782711861342]], [[d:hunter#1446112643386577078]] (2025-12-04).

- **[Pet loyalty]** Loyalty levels up primarily through time spent at "green" (happy) status while killing non-grey mobs, not directly from XP gained; a bug exists where a newly tamed pet won't gain loyalty until manually dismissed once then re-summoned (fix: tame, feed to happy, manually dismiss) — [[d:hunter#1435342052068954204]], [[d:hunter#1439045578863415428]], [[d:hunter#1439058837796687892]] (2025-11-04/15). - **[Threat]** No confirmed addon exists in this chunk to track hunter-vs-pet threat specifically (asked twice, unanswered) — [[d:hunter#1441074342086705205]], [[d:hunter#1441376796816707705]] (2025-11-20/21).

Coordinated Assault currently counts as damage done by the HUNTER (not the pet), so it can proc things like Improved Aspects/melee attack-speed effects on the hunter, generates aggro on the hunter, and can be chilled by enemy Frost Armor-type debuffs even at range — [[d:hunter#1445050523740016792]], [[d:hunter#1445060688765784105]] (2025-12-01). Mongoose Bite was also found to proc Coordinated Assault, undocumented in its tooltip — [[d:hunter#1443474215142428865]], [[d:hunter#1443511080998666290]] (2025-11-27).
- **[Windfury + Coordinated Assault, tested]** A 6-minute in-game test (Swifteye) found: (1) if only the pet is in Windfury Totem range while the hunter melees a *different* target, Coordinated Assault's WF-triggering attacks buff the pet but the hunter gets no bonus attack; (2) if both pet and hunter are in WFT range and attacking the *same* target, a WF proc off Coordinated Assault gives the HUNTER an extra melee attack — confirmed via deselecting the target after a Raptor Strike and watching the character swing alone off the pet's proc; this gives 2H weapons an edge over dual-wield since the "free" swing hits harder — [[d:hunter#1443520008985772128]], [[d:hunter#1443520748919591073]]. On a ranged Coordinated Assault, Windfury only produces the visual/animation of an extra shot with no actual extra damage — [[d:hunter#1443512301251596399]] (2025-11-27).
- **[Bug]** Kill Command remains unreliable past its first cast per fight; even with 5/5 Frenzy, the pet can still lose its Frenzy attack-speed buff since Kill Command's crit is what's supposed to refresh it reliably — [[d:hunter#1445843818619473973]] (2025-12-03). One theory (single source, worth testing): leaving pet abilities on autocast causes the pet to keep attempting off-cooldown abilities even without enough focus, which seems to interfere with Kill Command's crit proc; switching all pet abilities to fully manual cast made Kill Command/Coordinated Assault crits consistent — [[d:hunter#1441362114286583900]], [[d:hunter#1441362375105314978]] (2025-11-21).
- **[Bug]** Hunter's Mark's ranged-AP-to-attackers bonus (+75 RAP in one tester's case) produced no measurable damage change in controlled dummy testing (40-shot and smaller manual samples), unlike Aspect of the Hawk's own +90 RAP which gave a clear ~+20 damage bump; a bug report was filed (#18750) — [[d:hunter#1444077437561475172]], [[d:hunter#1444083227311735056]], [[d:hunter#1444090176573018223]], [[d:hunter#1444100420866805971]] (2025-11-29).
- **[Pet threat]** General consensus: pet threat generation itself isn't necessarily worse than before, but hunters now deal more personal damage (hence more relative threat) than pets can keep up with; Screech's cooldown/power nerf (see 1.18.1 changes) also makes pet threat generation arguably worse than it used to be — [[d:hunter#1444156155508035706]], [[d:hunter#1444166988032381021]] (2025-11-29). No addon was found in this window that tracks hunter-vs-pet threat specifically when solo (outside a group/raid, threat isn't broadcast for addons to read) — [[d:hunter#1441074342086705205]], [[d:hunter#1444975660551639072]], [[d:hunter#1444976016736129258]] (2025-11-20/12-01).
- **[Pet loyalty, contested]** Two different mechanics are described for loyalty gain: (a) killing non-grey mobs worth ≥5% of the hunter's XP per loyalty level, independent of time — [[d:hunter#1435341679660892293]] (2025-11-04); vs. (b) a later, detailed claim that loyalty is purely time-based (kills don't matter): feed to green/125% happiness, wait ~10 minutes, refeed, repeat — roughly 1 hour of this cycle reaches Loyalty Rank 3 — [[d:hunter#1447249603991830538]] (2025-12-07). Not reconciled in this chunk. Multiple players also report loyalty feels much slower to gain on Turtle now than it used to — [[d:hunter#1444838304892846153]], [[d:hunter#1444879947536666706]] (2025-11-30/12-01).
- **[Pet feeding]** Feeding cadence scales hard with loyalty: at Loyalty 1, a pet needs feeding roughly every 2-3 kills; at Loyalty 6, it barely eats (takes hours to get hungry) — [[d:hunter#1447238822139002910]] (2025-12-07). Food LEVEL (not type/quality) determines happiness gained — cheap low-level food works identically to expensive food as long as it's within the pet's level range — [[d:hunter#1446140861565108265]] (2025-12-04).
- **[Stable slots]** The 3rd stable slot costs gold (250g, up from ~50g for the 2nd); the 5th/last slot requires a real-money purchase; community frustration is high given how slow pet leveling/loyalty is — several want more (cheaper) slots given upcoming new pet families — [[d:hunter#1446233930511679682]], [[d:hunter#1446927399882194954]], [[d:hunter#1446933919093100728]], [[d:hunter#1446934192070983681]] (2025-12-05/06). Bug: if a pet's family is later made untameable by a patch, a stable slot holding that pet becomes permanently unusable/bricked — [[d:hunter#1446927689930768384]] (2025-12-06).
- **[Pet roles, taxonomy]** Consolidated view (junabii, well received): Screech pets = AoE tank/raid support; Gorilla = top AoE DPS + solid dungeon off-tank; Scorpid/Raptor/Cobra(snake) = best single-target raid DPS pets; Wind Serpent/Spider/Boar = often better PvP picks than the raid-DPS pet depending on spec; Wolf/Tallstrider = group support, situationally better depending on comp; there is no single best-in-every-situation pet, even for raid DPS, since it depends on the specific boss's immunities — [[d:hunter#1446923899555152083]] (2025-12-06).
- **[BM pet speed, contested]** Two different, non-reconciled recommendations on pet attack speed for BM: (a) run a 1.5-speed pet with max Frenzy points to keep Baited Shot uptime up given Kill Command/Coordinated Assault bugs (yaboikeenser, workaround-focused) — [[d:hunter#1440118017815089202]] (2025-11-18); vs. (b) BM specifically wants HIGHER attack-speed pets (more crit chances per second) to reduce needed Frenzy investment and to proc Baited Shot more often, so a 1.5-speed Scorpion is actually worse than 2.0 for this reason — except that a Scorpion's poison DoT (spell-scaled via Spirit Bond) is attack-speed-independent and can still make it the best BM pet in raids regardless (Demonhun) — [[d:hunter#1445687935193059399]], [[d:hunter#1445688108166156422]] (2025-12-03).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Maraudon]** Detailed solo route (Dresche, level 60): spawn with the Maraudon staff quest item; take the left bridge past the crocodile boss to the 2nd-to-last room; use Swiftness Zanza or alternate Cheetah/Monkey aspects; pull lasher packs with Serpent Sting rank 1 while running the same route back; evade rock elementals (they'll aggro to lashers, no need to pull them directly); at the hydra/waterfall spawn room, kite hydras around rock formations until stacked, then Explosive Trap them all down; learn the route first without accidental pulls, keep swiftness potions ready, and use 360° jump-shots to pull safely — [[d:hunter#1436512270237565088]], [[d:hunter#1436513058779300031]] (2025-11-08).
- **[DM East]** Solo lasher-room farm: requires Survival spec + the Pathfinding talent (from BM tree) + 1 point in Improved Eyes of the Beast (extra time to pull lashers with the pet); tame a fresh Wind Serpent for Lightning Breath rank 5; pet is used only to pull the room, damage is done by running around dropping Explosive Traps — [[d:hunter#1440821249554256062]], [[d:hunter#1440822715824209971]], [[d:hunter#1440822972821540985]], [[d:hunter#1440823113779515513]], [[d:hunter#1440825746426822696]] (2025-11-20).
- **[DM North]** Engineer-only solo of the last boss: must personally be an Engineer to blow the locked door with a Large Seaforium Charge; run to the boss skipping trash via pet pulls, then "juggle" the boss between hunter and pet using FD; yields ~5 blue + several green items — [[d:hunter#1440827721516060672]], [[d:hunter#1440827890647433447]], [[d:hunter#1440828679885164545]], [[d:hunter#1440828784310747298]], [[d:hunter#1440829068781158553]] (2025-11-20).
- **[MC]** Survival remains competitive DPS in MC despite fire immunities costing roughly 20% damage on Baron Geddon/Ragnaros from lost trap uptime; MM has an equivalent problem (no bleed access), and BM can't use elemental-damage pets against immune bosses either — no spec is untouched by MC/BWL fire immunities — [[d:hunter#1440230226058547412]], [[d:hunter#1440237424608284724]], [[d:hunter#1440237901266030592]], [[d:hunter#1440238282662215710]] (2025-11-17).

- **[Shen'dralar rep]** Solo Dire Maul rep-farm routes for the class dagger/cloak: East route (lashers + Hydrospawn + demon, demon boss being hardest to solo) or West route (repeatedly kill the first boss); yields ~300-400 rep and 2 book drops per run, 3-4 runs/hour — [[d:hunter#1438125601927204885]], [[d:hunter#1438126491958509578]] (2025-11-12). The Shen'dralar dagger and cloak are specifically worth farming rep for — [[d:hunter#1438126767620755577]].

- **[AQ40]** SV pet-management issues become a real liability on fights like Chromaggus and (Turtle's) Twin Emperors — [[d:hunter#1441952820436799549]] (2025-11-23).
