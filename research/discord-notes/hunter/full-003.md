### Datamined/previewed patch notes for the upcoming hunter rework (discussed 2026-01-24 on, not yet live in this chunk)
- **[MM]** New Row-5 keystone **Experimental Ammunition**: "Your Aimed Shot deals an additional 5% of its damage as Fire, Arcane or Nature damage and grants you a benefit depending on the element: Fire Ammunition: your next Multi-Shot hits explode for 20% ranged weapon damage in a 5-yard radius. Arcane Ammunition: your next Arcane Shot deals 100% increased damage and weakens the target's resistance to magic, +3% magic damage taken for 6 sec. Nature Ammunition: your next Serpent Sting deals 100% increased damage and applies a corrosive poison reducing armor by 4/level (240 at level 60) for 15 sec." Effects last 1 minute and cycle Fire→Arcane→Nature automatically; only one type active at a time; devs confirmed it is NOT player-selectable, a cycling personal buff triggered by landing Aimed Shot, consumed on use of the matching ability (or replaced by the next Aimed Shot) — [[d:hunter#1464351466440622133]] (Itron quoting patch notes, 2026-01-24), interpretation debate resolved by [[d:hunter#1464530282085351506]] (Sofie) and dev-comment quote "the goal... is to give unique, thematic effects to existing abilities... encouraging use of Aimed Shot and lesser-used Hunter abilities" [[d:hunter#1464540144383037500]]. Community was near-universally unhappy that the element cannot be chosen/forced, calling it "cumbersome" and RNG-heavy — [[d:hunter#1464417626712445060]] (Roger_Dabbit), [[d:hunter#1464388077895287058]] (Littlechurch), [[d:hunter#1464431513641685053]] (flagtutor); **contested/unresolved even after discussion** — see Contested section.
- **[MM]** New capstone **Lock and Load** (7th row, 1 rank): "Your Steady Shot, Aimed Shot and Arcane Shot critical strikes have a chance to reset the cooldown of Aimed Shot and trigger Lock and Load. Lasts 10 seconds or until you cast Aimed Shot. Lock and Load reduces the cast time of Aimed Shot by 1 second and causes your Aimed Shot to damage all enemies in between you and the target" (a line-piercing shot, not a true AoE burst) — [[d:hunter#1464365372215005398]] (Siphis, 2026-01-24), clarified not full AoE by [[d:hunter#1464358360622039082]] (yaboikeenser). Exact proc chance not stated in patch notes — widely called out as an open question — [[d:hunter#1465111002042597488]] (Sofie), [[d:hunter#1464631464854294640]] (Cabum).
- **[MM]** Aimed Shot and Arcane Shot moved to separate/independent cooldowns (previously shared); Aimed Shot base cooldown ~26 sec, base cast ~3.5 sec (reduced by haste, and by 1 sec more with Lock and Load) — [[d:hunter#1464357502136090705]], [[d:hunter#1464357573313691669]] (Itron, 2026-01-24), [[d:hunter#1465871215309750314]] (Vellasta). Rapid Fire now ALSO reduces Aimed Shot cast time by 40% (in addition to Steady Shot) — [[d:hunter#1464381799554875494]] (Irithyl, 2026-01-24). Steady Shot took a straight 5% damage nerf, Aimed Shot a 10% buff, and all ranged damage a further 5% buff, per patch-note readers — [[d:hunter#1464419300235415567]] (Raggen, 2026-01-24).
- **[MM]** Arcane Shot changed to scale off both ranged weapon/AP damage and Spell Power (community-derived SP coefficients: Arcane Shot ≈43.5%, Serpent Sting ≈20%×5 ticks=100% total, Volley ≈33% of total damage) — [[d:hunter#1465256041922170890]] (Vellasta, 2026-01-25). This was seen as the reason hunters would start using Spell Power consumables; suggested SP stack: Flask of Supreme Power (+150 SP), Emerald Mongoose (+15 SP, melee-AP-flagged buff so it does NOT conflict with the "melee"-restricted Firewater/Mongoose enchant text), Greater Arcane Elixir (+35 SP), Dreamtonic (+35 SP), 2x Wizard Oil (+36 SP each) ≈307 SP total — [[d:hunter#1464876213599338568]] (Vellasta, 2026-01-25).
- **[MM]** Ranged Weapon Specialization talent buffed from 1/2/3/4/5% to 2/4/6/8/10% ranged damage — [[d:hunter#1464363528688500749]] (Itron, 2026-01-24).
- **[MM]** New Row-2 talent **Alone Against the World** (2 ranks): "While you have no pet under your control, all damage dealt is increased by 3/6%" — a permanent, unmanaged, always-on damage boost for playing pet-less ("Lone Wolf" style) — [[d:hunter#1464419300919087360]] (wifed, 2026-01-24), full text also at [[d:hunter#1464544568937807922]] (Raizen). Heavily disputed value (see Contested section).
- **[Survival]** Per a community summary of the leaked notes: "Alone Against the World" with pet is still more DPS than without; Lacerate got a slight buff; Trueshot Aura made baseline for all specs (not just MM) got a HUGE buff, meaning Survival/BM hunters will provide it too — [[d:hunter#1464358047949394104]] (Reyka, 2026-01-24). Steady Shot also becomes baseline for all specs (useful for Survival on fights where you can't be in melee, e.g. Heigan's dance) — [[d:hunter#1466006006126477313]] (Minces, 2026-01-28). Trueshot Aura itself was toned down from the original flat +100 AP to roughly (3% AP + 30 flat AP) at rank 1 (speculated 5%+55 AP at max rank) — debated as a nerf vs vanilla — [[d:hunter#1466005709970870350]], [[d:hunter#1466007167286116405]] (Vellasta/Darkaco, 2026-01-28).
- **[Survival profession]** The Survival tracking/gathering PROFESSION (arrows) is also said to be getting "a big rework" allowing arrow-crafting, per patch-note followers, though no details were public yet as of this chunk — [[d:hunter#1466703227029553165]], [[d:hunter#1466708592869642364]] (Raggen/Vellasta, 2026-01-30).
- **[BM]** Developer comment quoted: "In the last patch, we reworked Beast Mastery, but the changes did not hit the mark. The specialization played awkwardly, so we are redesigning it again with a focus on smoother gameplay and preservation of core Beast Mastery mechanics." Also: "Baited Shot struggled to fit into the Hunter rotation. For that reason, it has been removed, with its core concept preserved within the Scent of Blood talent. - **[BM]** Kill Command becomes the new capstone (replacing/absorbing the old proc identity): quoted tooltip stat block "(8 Second Cooldown, 5% Base Mana Cost, 45 Yard Range, No Global Cooldown)" — [[d:hunter#1464586562300809399]] (Itron, 2026-01-24); deals roughly 50% of pet AP as damage per Kaslux's math, i.e. much less than current Coordinated Assault's output, and unlike current KC it does NOT free the pet from crowd control — [[d:hunter#1465767782074421329]] (Kaslux, 2026-01-27). Widely called an "underwhelming capstone" that does less damage than the 11-point Coordinated Assault talent over time — [[d:hunter#1464394039032807657]] (Darkaco, 2026-01-24); concrete quote: "The new KC as a capstone will be so bad. It'll deal less damage than CA over time" — [[d:hunter#1466483936963203318]] (Darkaco, 2026-01-29).
- **[BM]** **Bestial Wrath returns** as a Row-5, 1-rank talent: "Send your pet into a rage, giving it the Scent of Blood effect for 18 sec. While enraged, the beast does not feel pity or remorse or fear and it cannot be stopped unless killed." (1.5-minute cooldown, 12% base mana cost, no GCD) — [[d:hunter#1465766083138031841]] (Itron quoting patch notes, 2026-01-27). This replaces the current Kill Command's ability to instantly break pet CC; because BW's cooldown is 90 sec vs current KC being spammable, this is judged a significant PvP nerf to BM's pet-CC-immunity uptime — [[d:hunter#1465763251441635463]], [[d:hunter#1465766294841462935]], [[d:hunter#1465766400323752069]] (junabii, 2026-01-27). Whether the passive 15%-proc-chance "Scent of Blood" talent (row 5, non-BW version) ALSO grants CC immunity on its own, or only full Bestial Wrath does, was unresolved/ambiguous in the patch notes and heavily debated without a clear conclusion — [[d:hunter#1466461387340517671]] (Darkaco), [[d:hunter#1466477195693396100]] (Swifteye's reading: only BW itself gives the CC-immunity portion) — **contested, see Contested section**.
- **[BM]** Endurance Training and Thick Hide pet-survivability nerfs (from a prior patch) are being reverted/undone — before those nerfs, pets reportedly had "essentially infinite HP" — [[d:hunter#1464433381931745364]] (ᵀᵉˡˡʸ, 2026-01-24).
- **[BM]** Devs stated intent that the reworked BM tree should support EITHER ranged or melee play (dual-wield melee BM), enabled by Scent of Blood being achievable via fast melee auto-attacks/DW abilities as well as ranged shots — [[d:hunter#1465323401538437294]] (Vellasta, 2026-01-27), [[d:hunter#1464865562864652318]] (Vellasta, 2026-01-25). Kaslux built and tested a melee BM/Survival hybrid on the new leaked talent calculator, arguing that with fast dual-wield weapons Scent of Blood uptime could approach 100%, at which point Bestial Wrath becomes unnecessary — [[d:hunter#1466099196485570733]], [[d:hunter#1466100191714086952]] (Kaslux, 2026-01-28) — but this remained purely theoretical/untested pending patch release.
- **[BM]** Coordinated Assault's melee-AP scaling bug (see below) means Kill Command's damage in the new tree may or may not inherit the same bug ("Kill command scaling with meele ap i can see that coming" — Kaslux only half-joking) — [[d:hunter#1466476672961347872]] (2026-01-29); the community explicitly flagged as an open dev question whether CA will be fixed at all before/with this patch, since the patch notes only covered talent redesign, not bug fixes — [[d:hunter#1464834639465283676]] (Destroses), [[d:hunter#1465112830964531353]] (Kaslux, 2026-01-26; "they havent fixed coordinated assault because they said hunter is getting a rework, but that seems untouched, not even mentioned").
- **[General]** New spell **Aspect of the Viper** (learned at level 56, no mana cost, one aspect active at a time): "regenerating 5% of maximum mana every 5 seconds." Confirmed by players who had played other versions of vanilla-classic-likes that Turtle's version has NO damage-reduction penalty (unlike the SoD version, "5% mana regen, no mana on hit") and is not the TBC version either — [[d:hunter#1464355513331613739]] (Itron), [[d:hunter#1464367885882363965]], [[d:hunter#1464368300711477462]], [[d:hunter#1464369086799675392]] (Dixa, 2026-01-24). General verdict: mediocre for raiding ("no one is using that in raids except noobs, same with lone wolf" — rickety, [[d:hunter#1464367550312874156]]), useful mainly for leveling/farming/chain-pulling trash regen, and its level-56 unlock (rather than earlier) was called out as making early leveling as MM notably more mana-starved once the patch lands — [[d:hunter#1464931551191105713]] (Sofie, 2026-01-25).
- **[Community verdict]** Best single summary of expected post-patch balance, from Dresche (considered a Survival DW authority, see Experts): "BM changes good, but will still fall behind MM and SV in damage, due to not having the good capstone and keystone talents from MM... SV almost unchanged with a slight dmg buff... MM now highest skill ceiling spec, which rewards positioning (through piercing aimed shot) and timing (ammunition cycles and aimed shot CD resets)... 'alone against the world' is a meme addition." Also lists open dev questions at the time: can ammo types be forced or is it a random 1-minute cycle; will Scorpid Sting finally work on raid bosses; will Kill Command be fixed before release; what is the exact Lock-and-Load proc percentage — [[d:hunter#1464573424474132522]] (Dresche, 2026-01-24, +3 reactions). Community sentiment otherwise split: many veteran MM/BM players expect MM to leapfrog Survival ("MM > survival next patch easily" — Itron [[d:hunter#1464358156808228996]]) while others (moldt, Surreal) maintained Survival would remain the strongest hunter spec by far even post-patch, citing that mobs often die before a complex proc rotation can complete — [[d:hunter#1464541088575655988]], [[d:hunter#1464542043530461236]] (2026-01-24).
- **[Coordinated Assault bug — quantified]** Concrete parse-derived numbers shared ahead of the patch: on a well-geared MM hunter's top Patchwerk log, pet abilities alone were ~8-11% of total damage (not counting Coordinated Assault/"Strike Together"); Strike Together itself was measured doing 5-8x more damage than its description implies for MM (bugged to scale off hunter melee AP, benefiting from the hunter's own hit%, crit%, and even proccing Windfury Totem/Frost Shield as if it were a real melee swing) — [[d:hunter#1466181868469616712]], [[d:hunter#1466183350560624661]], [[d:hunter#1464834863076212912]] (yaboikeenser, 2026-01-26/29). For Survival, Strike Together via CA was measured at ~10-11% of total damage (~180-215 dps) at ~2k total dps, roughly double MM's share (~70-80 dps) because Survival's melee AP (~3k buffed) is much higher than MM's (~1.5k buffed) — [[d:hunter#1466184699025297581]], [[d:hunter#1466185062348493024]], [[d:hunter#1466232931424014337]] (yaboikeenser/Kaslux, 2026-01-29). If the bug were ever fixed to scale off pet AP instead, Kaslux estimated true Coordinated Assault would be far weaker (~100-150 dmg per hit instead of ~400-500) — [[d:hunter#1464832105816002611]] (2026-01-25).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Survival]** Consistently the top hunter PvE spec in current content (ZG/MC/Kara40/Naxx): on turtlogs.com Kara40 leaderboards, hunters barely crack top 100 and it's almost always Survival (first MM hunter around rank 256-490 vs multiple Survival higher); Naxx has more MM but Survival still favored — [[d:hunter#1460250313209679954]] (yaboikeenser, 2026-01-12). Reasoning given: MM ranged damage has "0 scaling" from gear multipliers while Survival benefits from many multipliers, so better gear favors Surv more — [[d:hunter#1460254208543686811]], [[d:hunter#1460254309278023967]] (Ionnatra, 2026-01-12).
- **[MM]** Debated as "one of the worse specs" but explicitly "not no good" — parse evidence: on Nordanaar Patchwerk logs, first hunter (surv) #204, first MM hunter #476; on Tel'Abim Patchwerk, first hunter (any spec) #11, first MM #28; one player reports being #3 overall dps / #6 on Patchwerk in their guild as MM — [[d:hunter#1460370585468272926]] (Sofie), [[d:hunter#1460372647069356196]], [[d:hunter#1460372902955188498]], [[d:hunter#1460373326080774259]] (all Sofie, 2026-01-13). Community consensus: MM raid-viable but expect to be outparsed by Survival unless Survival players are undergeared — [[d:hunter#1460369325574586432]] (yaboikeenser, 2026-01-13).
- **[BM]** Weak for raiding by general consensus: "BM is not great for raiding. It will do ok... but surv/mm will always be better" — [[d:hunter#1462142748927987836]] (Rpecial, 2026-01-17). "bm sucks" for PvP/general — [[d:hunter#1461570261592703009]] (Sofie, 2026-01-16). Still considered fine for people who "don't care about being top" — going BM instead of Naxx/Kara wouldn't be "pure trolling", "just fine" in PUGs — [[d:hunter#1461276207084736648]] (sailorVEVO, 2026-01-15). BM is standard for leveling and pet tanking/soloing but "pet spec is giga dead" at endgame per one poster — [[d:hunter#1460440709839454290]] (Bluwu, 2026-01-13).
- **[Survival tanking-trash]** Survival hunters in T3 gear reported facetanking trash in PUG Kara10/ZG/AQ20 successfully due to high dodge + vampiric leech + Carve/Explosive Trap generating huge threat; a well-geared tank is needed to hold aggro over them, warriors especially struggle since their AoE threat tool is weak — [[d:hunter#1462596377325605158]] (ph1LL, 2026-01-19), [[d:hunter#1462598066845515988]] (Sofie), [[d:hunter#1462599460587569205]] (Rpecial).
- **[Leveling]** BM recommended as default leveling/dungeon spec; most switch to Survival or MM at endgame — [[d:hunter#1462176005178265871]] (Sweat Miser, 2026-01-18). Rpecial: leveled BM to ~45-50 then swapped Survival, "staff from wildhammers made survival feel super good" once available — [[d:hunter#1462221281285116129]], [[d:hunter#1462222228514144296]] (2026-01-18). Julio: "Go Beast Mastery all the way, easy leveling" — [[d:hunter#1459565632503943414]] (2026-01-09).
- **[Naxx/K40 parses, mid-January]** Recurring turtlogs.com Patchwerk cross-checks throughout the chunk keep confirming Survival's lead: Tel'Abim top-30 K40-all-bosses only had 2 hunters in the top 100, both presumably Survival; MM's top purely-MM parse in K40 cited around rank 364 — [[d:hunter#1466064754060820675]], [[d:hunter#1466064913469280316]] (Sofie, 2026-01-28), [[d:hunter#1466069871790915680]] (Surreal). A skilled MM player (Sofie) nonetheless reported being top overall DPS in her guild's Naxx and #4 on a 2h40m server-wide Naxx Patchwerk log, illustrating that a good player can still make MM competitive — [[d:hunter#1464543072174997578]], [[d:hunter#1464543769960513546]] (2026-01-24).
- **[BM outlook]** General agreement BM remains the weakest spec for raid DPS even after the announced rework, since Kill Command (its new capstone) does less damage than Coordinated Assault and the tree becomes more passive; several veteran players (Kaslux, Darkaco) argue this isn't necessarily wrong — "I don't think BM *should* outdo the other specs on overall damage... it has its niche as a solo/leveling spec, and its good in PVP against certain targets, and that's fine" (Krulltak) vs. counter-argument that "the go-to leveling spec" precedent (BM in TBC) doesn't preclude high-end viability (Darkaco) — [[d:hunter#1465141880043081908]], [[d:hunter#1465183563291951319]] (2026-01-26), extended debate continues 01-28 (see Contested section for the unresolved argument).

## Leveling
- **[BM]** Standard leveling spec; take Pathfinding early for movement speed before a mount, then invest in pet damage; by the time pet is "beefed up completely" you're near level 50, then finish 50-60 in Dire Maul East on the Lasher farm — [[d:hunter#1460356323211477167]] (Elmgren, 2026-01-12). Avoid going "hybrid" (mixed BM/other) while leveling per Elmgren.
- **[Spec switch]** Recommended: level BM until 40, then respec to Survival or MM for 1g — applies to duo-leveling groups too — [[d:hunter#1462582858001416214]] (Rpecial, 2026-01-19). One poster notes Survival becomes much stronger/more fun once its capstone talent (drop traps mid-combat) is available around level 40, but it is very mana intensive — bring water — [[d:hunter#1462593185728561364]], [[d:hunter#1462594036790726707]], [[d:hunter#1462594392891064372]] (Sisco the Kid/Rpecial, 2026-01-19).
- **[Melee weaving]** Viable and effective up to at least level 50 while leveling as BM even with only rank-1 Raptor Strike (higher ranks waste mana pre-endgame) — [[d:hunter#1459092782592299081]] (Morenth, 2026-01-09). At endgame with high ranged AP from gear, melee weaving is considered no longer worth it ("no way... you gain too much ranged atk power as you gear") — [[d:hunter#1459096287080087667]] (Morenth, 2026-01-09).
- **[Leveling rotation, low level]** Suggested kiting technique for early hunters: open Auto Shot, "moonwalk" (strafe backward) while landing Serpent Sting / instant abilities, stop briefly to fire Auto Shot again, repeat — lets you land 4-5 shots before target reaches melee — [[d:hunter#1460603346443440260]] (Leroy, 2026-01-13, +2), detailed sequence also given by [[d:hunter#1460604992917475503]] (Anyway,I grind).
- **[Pets while leveling]** Recommendation, level 10 on: pig/boar early (cheap food, tanky), swap to eagle/bird around 35-40 for soloing; from level 40+ a Wolf (with Dash) is favored for solo dungeon farming, e.g. Dire Maul East with Explosive Trap — [[d:hunter#1460555634327027715]], [[d:hunter#1460556031796183122]], [[d:hunter#1460556303704260729]] (Vicent, 2026-01-13). Alternative leveling path: 40-45 in Maraudon, 45-60 in Dire Maul — [[d:hunter#1460557414997692477]] (Vicent).
- **[Pets]** Never tame a pet the same level as you — until it can gain XP (which pet loyalty requires per some testers) it will barely gain loyalty, costing huge amounts of food; contested — see Contested section — [[d:hunter#1460629863223525600]] (Ehrenboy93, 2026-01-13, +1).
- **[Weapon skill quests]** An island south of Ratchet (250g + "ancient warfare text") offers a quest granting a permanent +5 weapon skill in a chosen weapon type, and the bonus applies to both the 1H and 2H counterpart of that weapon category — [[d:hunter#1461492019221368894]], [[d:hunter#1461492808459223082]] (Sisco the Kid, 2026-01-16).
- **[Pet choice while leveling, detailed]** Wind Serpents hold the best aggro and have the best up-front burst damage while leveling as BM (instant-cast Lightning Breath fired while running in); Serpents/Raptors are decent second choices; Owls have the best AoE threat via Screech but BM's cleave is weak so that utility is often wasted; Scorpid's poison wants long-lived ("juicy") targets to stack all 5 charges, and most leveling mobs die before that's worth it — avoid pure claw/bite focus-dump pets for leveling; Cobras and Scorpids are tanky but Kill Command doesn't add much value to them — [[d:hunter#1465715534699040768]], [[d:hunter#1465715729813999708]], [[d:hunter#1465711478991749296]], [[d:hunter#1465711523124089111]] (Stunfisk, 2026-01-27). General raid/leveling pet cheatsheet: owl/carrion-bird/bat for leveling and dungeon AoE threat via Screech (also reduces tank damage taken); raptor/wind-serpent/cobra for raid DPS; wolf for melee-group support (also sidesteps poison/bleed immunity issues raptors and cobras run into); Hawkstrider gives a subtle ranged-group buff but is only tameable at low level; Scorpid does the highest overall damage on long (>1 min) boss fights but is weak on trash — [[d:hunter#1464682851231334645]], [[d:hunter#1464682937244188914]], [[d:hunter#1464682974439018587]], [[d:hunter#1464683268166385716]], [[d:hunter#1464683521821114613]] (Krulltak, 2026-01-24).
- **[Pet out-of-combat regen]** Pet HP regen out of combat on Turtle WoW is very fast ("busted") — pets return to full HP within about 2 seconds of not being hit, which reduces how much a pet's raw "tankiness" (e.g. Scorpid vs. Raptor) actually matters while leveling — [[d:hunter#1465718726166053108]], [[d:hunter#1465718908936786165]] (Stunfisk, 2026-01-27).
- **[Pet food/loyalty, endgame pet]** Only level-45+ vendor food (e.g. Roasted Quail) meaningfully feeds a level-60 pet; loyalty gain is tied to time spent in the green/yellow happiness range, not the specific food used — better food just fills the happiness meter faster — [[d:hunter#1465596533570404403]], [[d:hunter#1465596636104359980]], [[d:hunter#1465677437949186090]] (Destroses/Thula Wena, 2026-01-27).
- **[Professions while leveling]** Skinning-for-gold is less necessary than on official Classic because Turtle's constant rested-XP means leveling outpaces gold-earning anyway; recommendation instead is to level Engineering + Enchanting from the start for later Dire Maul Tribute-run farming, or Alchemy for passive income via Arcanite Transmutes — [[d:hunter#1466389496248668210]], [[d:hunter#1466390068523569183]], [[d:hunter#1466390416659189842]] (Elmgren, 2026-01-30).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Survival, melee macro]** Community "one-button" macro shared for melee Survival: `/startattack, /cast Immolation Trap(Rank 5), /cast Raptor Strike(Rank 8), /cast Mongoose Bite(Rank 4), /cast Furious Howl, /cast Lacerate, /cast Carve(Rank 3)` — works with Nampower macro-queue behavior (see bugs) — [[d:hunter#1458939426645217403]] (Suade, 2026-01-09). Simpler variant: `/startattack, /cast Raptor Strike, /cast Lacerate, /cast Mongoose Bite, /cast Wing Clip, /script PetAttack()` — [[d:hunter#1459333313855750356]] (Afraido, 2026-01-09, +1).
- **[BM]** Zim's rotation reported: Kill Command > Sausage Shot(?) > Multi Shot > Steady Shot, autoshot-weaved, using a quiver for weave timing — [[d:hunter#1459237016301732135]] (Zim, 2026-01-09). Kaslux (Survival, "the spec that does most of damage" currently) tips shared to Zim: pop AP trinket before the 5th poison stack when running Scorpid, spam Steady Shot when bursting, ask for Blessing of Might since melee AP buffs the (bugged) Coordinated Assault ability — [[d:hunter#1459238370143244370]], [[d:hunter#1459238633818296361]], [[d:hunter#1459238672921657434]] (Kaslux, 2026-01-09).
- **[Survival, DW opener]** Example combo cited: Kill Command opener > Lightning Breath (pet) > Baited Shot > Lightning Breath > Auto Shot > Multi Shot ("+ orange damage") — reported as "legit like 3k damage in 1 combo" — [[d:hunter#1459993997509525514]] (junabii, 2026-01-11) (this appears to describe a BM-pet-focused burst, cited under Survival/BM crossover discussion of pet-buffing gear).
- **[MM]** Steady Shot / Aimed Shot / Quick Shots weaving discussed at length (see Stats section for haste discussion); rotation is largely auto-shot + Steady Shot weave with cooldown dumps, using Quiver addon's "no clip" macro to avoid clipping Auto Shot: `/run local a, b = Quiver.GetSecondsRemainingShoot(); local c = a and b < -0.25; local f = c and CastSpellByName or Quiver.CastNoClip; f("Steady Shot")` (requires Quiver addon, and a normal Steady Shot button placed somewhere on the action bars) — [[d:hunter#1459516386048086251]] (Zwift, 2026-01-09).
- **[Hunter's Mark macro]** Simple opener macro pattern used broadly: `/script PetAttack(target)`, `/cast Hunter's Mark`, `/cast Auto Shot` (or Arcane Shot) as the raid opener — [[d:hunter#1461693828170780910]] (Raggen, 2026-01-16), [[d:hunter#1462091993382387814]] (2026-01-17).
- **[Raptor pet, MM/Survival]** Raptor should be trained with both Bite and Claw; normal fights use Savage Rend + Bite, but switch to Claw on bosses immune to bleed — [[d:hunter#1462729876296695900]] (Zwift, 2026-01-19).
- **[Survival, comprehensive rotation/consumable checklist]** Detailed unprompted advice for an underperforming DW/2H Survival hunter in MC/BWL gear: mostly spam the ability macro, always Explosive Trap in AoE and Immolation Trap on single target; weapon quality matters a lot (you won't out-damage equally-geared mages/rogues/warriors if outgeared by them); always have Windfury, Mongoose, Ground Scorpok Assay, Elixir of the Giants, Firewater/Juju, Sour Mountain Berry, Elemental Sharpening Stone, and Str/Agi scrolls up; you do NOT need 8% hit if you have weapon-skill bonuses — with 305 weapon skill only 7% hit is needed, with 310 only 6%, with 315 (hit cap) only 5%; swap Hand of Justice for Molten Emberstone or Devilsaur Eye right before bosses (swap back via a Feign Death macro once the trinket's buff window ends); use Potion of Quickness on fights where you don't need other potions; keep Demonic Runes on hand for long fights — [[d:hunter#1463846948200124426]] (Elmgren, 2026-01-22).
- **[MM, weapon-speed-dependent rotation rule of thumb]** From Dresche (extended, detailed breakdown after multi-day community testing/debate): the closer your weapon's "shot speed" (as shown by the Quiver addon) is to 1.0, the more you should ONLY alternate Auto Shot/Steady Shot — Multi Shot's extra 0.5-sec cast (on top of the normal 0.5-sec "bow draw" baked into every Auto Shot cycle) can delay the next Auto Shot if pressed too close to when Auto Shot is about to fire, whereas Steady Shot (instant) never delays it; concretely: 1.00–1.09 shot speed → only Steady + Auto; 1.10–1.19 → weave Multi Shot but never at the cost of clipping/missing a Steady; above 1.20 → always weave Multi Shot when available. During simultaneous Rapid Fire + Quick Shots (high haste), prioritize Multi Shot over Steady Shot whenever it's off cooldown since Multi Shot deals more damage without adding extra downtime in that specific high-haste state — [[d:hunter#1463858995403161744]], [[d:hunter#1463860501007175748]] (Dresche, 2026-01-22), confirmed by [[d:hunter#1463860222790734011]] (Elaale). Sofie independently blind-tested this on a target dummy over 10×2-minute trials with a Slavemaker crossbow: prioritizing Multi Shot immediately after Steady Shot (rather than waiting for Auto Shot) gave 1359 dps vs. 1273 dps doing the reverse — a clear, repeatable win for "MS right after SS, don't wait for auto" — [[d:hunter#1463872378009550939]], [[d:hunter#1463875063039394047]], [[d:hunter#1463875219025297458]] (Sofie, 2026-01-22). Final consensus reached days later after continued confusion: Multi Shot's extra 0.5s cast ONLY delays the next Auto Shot if you cast it right before Auto Shot was about to fire (i.e. the 0.5s window overlaps the auto-shot timer); cast it early enough into the free window between autos and there's no delay at all, which is why many players never noticed any clipping — [[d:hunter#1466811323584352423]], [[d:hunter#1466813505620410675]] (Dresche, 2026-01-30).
- **[MM, ranged weapon speed/dps testing]** Sofie ran back-to-back target-dummy tests (Hunter's Mark kept up, fighting to OOM) across her personal weapon upgrades: Rhok'delar (3.4 speed) → Ashjre'thul (BWL xbow) → Larvae (Naxx bow) → Slavemaker: each upgrade was worth roughly +50 dps on a level-1 dummy, except the final Slavemaker jump which was closer to +100 dps; slower weapons (like 3.4-speed Rhok) have almost zero downtime/clipping but ultimately underperform because top-end damage/DPS matters more, so she "feel[s] pretty confident" recommending hunters simply upgrade ranged weapons by weapon DPS through the tiers — [[d:hunter#1464143573850001601]], [[d:hunter#1464178357276115069]], [[d:hunter#1464182096175173734]] (Sofie, 2026-01-23). Resulting community-endorsed MM ranged weapon tier list: 1) Rain of Spiders, 2) Slavemaker, 3) Soulstring, 4) Ouro/C'thun/Emerald-Sanctum-hardmode bows — with Soulstring, Larvae, "Musket," and Nature's Call treated as roughly equal to each other a tier below Slavemaker — [[d:hunter#1464144100914888830]], [[d:hunter#1464187990765994064]] (Sofie, 2026-01-23).
- **[MM weapon skill/scope math]** A scope's flat "+N dmg" bonus converts to DPS by dividing by weapon speed, and to "AP-equivalent" by multiplying that DPS figure by 14 — [[d:hunter#1463874102799368285]] (mzk, 2026-01-22).
- **[MM, mana/rotation options at high haste]** Raggen questioned whether Steady Shot is even worth using during Rapid Fire; Elaale/Dresche/Sofie/nalae's consensus: still use Steady Shot as the default filler, but replace it with Multi Shot specifically when both Rapid Fire and Quick Shots (the haste proc) are active simultaneously — [[d:hunter#1463111824668098671]], [[d:hunter#1463136629064732682]], [[d:hunter#1463862980042035409]], [[d:hunter#1463867278822211669]] (2026-01-21/22).

Furious Howl (from Wolf pet) is valued specifically for boosting Carve damage in cleave/dungeon scenarios ("that's about it") — [[d:hunter#1460351079484100825]] (Stunfisk, 2026-01-12), disputed value: "isnt furious howl kinda overrated?" — [[d:hunter#1460347976038744198]] (Itron, 2026-01-12).

Concrete AoE farming method (level 40+ Survival, e.g. gnoll/lasher packs): drop a Frost Trap to gather mobs, kite in a circle around the trap dropping Explosive Trap on cooldown, cleave (Carve) when safe; put pet on "whatever" as long as it's attacking and not pulling threat; can use Eyes of the Beast with a sprinting pet to help herd mobs into the pack; not very effective before level ~40-50 (until a decent AoE-enabling weapon/staff is available) — [[d:hunter#1463674674289447045]], [[d:hunter#1463674795135860942]], [[d:hunter#1463675129400918088]], [[d:hunter#1463675322007289907]] (Rpecial, 2026-01-22).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Survival]** Agility-to-effective-AP formula worked out in discussion (Lightning Reflexes: +10% Agi, +100% Agi as melee AP): Effective Agi = Agi×1.10; AP = Effective Agi×2 (so 1 Agi ≈ 2.2 AP "proven by character sheet"); Crit% = (Agi×1.10)/50 → 1 Agi ≈ 0.022% crit; with 1% crit ≈ 34 AP for Survival (2.2 crit multiplier), total ≈ 2.95 effective AP per Agi — [[d:hunter#1459524813667172494]] (Reyka, 2026-01-09). Disputed: Bakingsoda calculated 1 Agi ≈ 3.15 EAP using 1% crit = 32 AP and including Blessing of Kings — [[d:hunter#1459523446290776177]], [[d:hunter#1459525467005779978]] (2026-01-09); no consensus reached, single-source-quality math with no in-game sim tools available — [[d:hunter#1459526697568178330]].
- **[General]** "1% crit = 32 AP" cited as a widely used historical rule of thumb, not confirmed to actually hold on Turtle WoW — [[d:hunter#1459526875327234255]] (Bakingsoda, 2026-01-09). Crit EAP estimated at ~28 in MC/Ony-phase gear, rising to ~40 in later phases per community tables — [[d:hunter#1459536844671811717]] (Zwift, 2026-01-09).
- **[MM]** Stat weights posted (with self-caveat "correct me if wrong"): Hit 8% mandatory, then Agi 2.7, Crit 32, Haste 15 (early gear); shifting toward Agi 3, Crit 40, Haste 30 as crit approaches ~30% in Naxx-tier gear — [[d:hunter#1459547549303181578]] (Sofie, 2026-01-09).
- **[MM/Survival, haste]** MM hunters start with 15% haste baseline from Quiver (the weapon type) itself plus Quick Shots procs, so hit cap then crit is prioritized over haste for most MM players — [[d:hunter#1459891776801345566]] (Sheyba, 2026-01-10). Counter-opinion: haste is "definitely good as MM" and smooths the rotation, some MM players (nalae) stack haste aggressively (up to 2% haste offhand book from SWV) reaching ~24% haste raid-buffed/39% under Quick Shots with kara-level gear + 5pc T3.5 set bonus, describing a near-perfect 1:1 Steady-Shot/Auto-Shot weave — [[d:hunter#1459920520677163039]], [[d:hunter#1459923323076214931]], [[d:hunter#1459940638236610701]] (nalae, 2026-01-10), [[d:hunter#1459934039254503514]] (junabii). Sofie: haste breakpoints matter — not useful to prioritize haste unless already above ~30% crit — [[d:hunter#1461360523877548065]] (2026-01-15). Only one 1% haste enchant exists for gloves (not 2%) — [[d:hunter#1459857456078262433]] (nalae, 2026-01-10).
- **[Survival]** 5% hit is enough at 315 weapon skill (not the full 8/9% sometimes quoted); each 5 points of weapon skill = 1% hit — [[d:hunter#1461152712791031841]] (Hungrig), [[d:hunter#1461204687209496648]] (Sisco the Kid, 2026-01-15).
- **[Survival, weapon speed]** Slower weapons have higher DPS and scale better with abilities (Mongoose Bite etc.) — Sofie recommends slow MH for DW Survival despite rogue/warrior conventions of fast OH — [[d:hunter#1460715123869221005]] (2026-01-13). Thalenar (self-described guru) instead prefers a slow-MH/fast-OH or double-fast proc-heavy sword DW setup, noting ability damage suffers but attack-speed-driven proc uptime compensates — [[d:hunter#1460812871247204484]], [[d:hunter#1460813032560005120]], [[d:hunter#1460813148562001971]] (2026-01-13).
- **[Survival, crit vs weapon oil]** Debate: Consecrated Sharpening Stone (flat AP) vs a hypothetical crit % sharpening stone: ~200 AP is judged to need ~5-6% crit to be equal in value; a heavily geared (full T3.5) Survival hunter estimates their own effective AP-per-1%-crit is closer to 50, which could tip the balance toward crit-based oils — [[d:hunter#1461483407358951692]], [[d:hunter#1461483467903598726]], [[d:hunter#1461487387426426983]] (Sofie/Dresche, 2026-01-16).
- **[Ranged crit soft cap]** No hard ceiling on crit chance stated to exist — [[d:hunter#1461575287056761074]] (Hungrig, 2026-01-16), question asked by [[d:hunter#1461569482794336266]] (Pingmeow/Khanto).
- **[Weapon proc]** A weapon proc (context: pet/hunter melee trinket-like proc, "gives 25 agi ... 2% hit, 150 ap") procs roughly 6 times per fight on average for one melee-focused build (Thalenar); Zwift separately measured ~30% proc uptime on a similar item while playing MM ("like +25 agi" effective), noting Carve gives 5x the proc chance vs Multi-Shot's 3x, and DW likely raises proc uptime further — [[d:hunter#1459436146370285682]] (Thalenar, 2026-01-09), [[d:hunter#1459468847252902005]], [[d:hunter#1459495508023312547]], [[d:hunter#1459496126947397694]] (Zwift, 2026-01-09).
- **[Survival DW vs 2H hit% — tested]** Sofie ran head-to-head testing with identical gear except swapping 2H for DW and found DW clearly better even only barely hit-capped; further controlled test comparing 310 polearm skill (2H) vs 305 axe skill (DW, only 7% hit from gear) still favored DW — "even despite better gear and better weapon, 2H is still not as good as dw," estimated ~99% confidence the result generalizes — [[d:hunter#1465688438832369887]], [[d:hunter#1465688872993423606]], [[d:hunter#1466767637303267582]] (Sofie, 2026-01-27/30). With 315 weapon skill (5% base hit cap) DW needs roughly 13% total hit from gear/talents to be safe; off-hand can still land glancing blows unless main-hand skill is 315 — [[d:hunter#1465658568022691961]] (Raggen), [[d:hunter#1466767100034154547]] (Sofie, 2026-01-27/30). Community rule of thumb for DW: prefer both weapons slow (more value from Swift Aspects/haste procs) though "a fast OH can also be great depending what it is"; daggers specifically are not recommended (too fast for a Survival DW pair) — [[d:hunter#1465687812530770257]], [[d:hunter#1466372650036236376]] (Sofie/Commando, 2026-01-27/30), [[d:hunter#1465653967290630290]] (Sofie: "daggers = fast and we wouldn't want 2 fast weapons").
- **[Survival, weapon-speed-to-AP]** Rule of thumb shared for converting attack speed to effective AP: "usually 1% [attack] speed is said to be 15-20 AP, but with more crit, haste will be more [valuable]" — [[d:hunter#1466775138237415570]] (Sofie, 2026-01-30).
- **[Glancing blow cap — open question]** Whether glancing-blow damage reduction caps out once weapon skill exceeds 315 while miss chance keeps decreasing further was asked and not answered in-chunk — [[d:hunter#1466775446967554249]] (Humanshield, 2026-01-30; open question).
- **[Coordinated Assault interaction]** Elemental Sharpening Stones (4% extra crit) can trigger Flametongue Weapon procs via the (bugged) Coordinated Assault "melee swing," explaining unexpected Flametongue procs showing up on a hunter's own combat log — [[d:hunter#1466234973462724629]], [[d:hunter#1466235245920780339]], [[d:hunter#1466235416465113281]] (Kaslux/Tydorel, 2026-01-29).
- **[Pet stats normalization]** Pet base DPS is normalized across species/models (no more unique bonuses like innate shadow damage or unusually slow walk speed), but attack SPEED itself is not normalized — species like Broken Tooth keep unusually fast swing speeds. Fast pet attack speed is valued mainly for PvP spell-interrupt spam; for PvE, a slower pet attack speed (1.5+) is generally preferred so procs like Windfury (totem) aren't wasted on frequent small hits — [[d:hunter#1464769543099650089]] (Destroses), [[d:hunter#1464773969931669617]] (Sisco the Kid), [[d:hunter#1464788182280179772]] (Midnight Requiem), [[d:hunter#1464788345950568591]] (Krulltak, 2026-01-25).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
