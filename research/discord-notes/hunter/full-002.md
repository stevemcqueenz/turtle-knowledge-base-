## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Raid DPS]** Survival is the top personal-DPS spec for raid, especially AoE; on single target margin is smaller but SV still ahead of MM even on Prince Wanted patchwerk-style fights with zero AoE components — [[d:hunter#1447705660069515535]], [[d:hunter#1448476613904957560]] (Duciel), [[d:hunter#1448478556492398633]].
- **[MM viability, contested]** MM called "perfectly viable" through all of Kara40 by Psyker — brings ranged safety, Trueshot Aura party buff, easier gearing, pulling, instant Tranquilizing Shot; even top SV hunters swap to MM on some fights (e.g., Kruul) — [[d:hunter#1448425859466657872]], [[d:hunter#1448508442418352168]]. Disputed hard by Duciel/Surreal/yaboikeenser: "cope," first MM hunter on K40 logs barely top 300, guilds are pressuring MM hunters to switch to melee for raid AoE — [[d:hunter#1448445791625547826]], [[d:hunter#1448477824834076795]], [[d:hunter#1448491642578407465]].
- **[Sub-spec use]** Common practice: play SV for trash/most bosses, swap to MM for specific fights (e.g., Ragnaros) to use ranged safety/utility — [[d:hunter#1447672710829310054]] (Dresche), [[d:hunter#1448005058234155173]] (Device, also swaps for Rag).
- **[BM]** BM leveling and PvP burst (wind serpent) called strong/fun; BM raid viability seen as behind but "clears content fine"; junabii predicts nerf if Kill Command bug gets fixed — [[d:hunter#1448317369565843650]].
- **[Consensus]** "MM needs buffs, doesn't need to be top DPS to be viable" — many hunters still clear content as MM — [[d:hunter#1448508442418352168]] (Psyker). Suggested fixes discussed: rework Trueshot Aura to raid-wide, fix BM bugs, give MM a new capstone; buff Endless Quiver proc chance (6-8% vs Windfury's 20%); let Multi-Shot hit more targets — [[d:hunter#1448508780202426451]], [[d:hunter#1448518375667142706]], [[d:hunter#1448495337584201728]].

- **[Naxx MM specifically, bottom tier]** yaboikeenser posts comparison logs: an MM hunter gives only a "small buff to melee" and does comparatively little personal damage vs. an Elemental Shaman (big melee buffs + big personal damage) despite both wearing mail; separately, "hunter appears once in the top 100 DPS for Naxx, and it's a melee (Survival) hunter" — MM hunter called effectively "unplayable" this patch by Bluwu ("forces you to be a warrior with a mana bar"), though Bluwu is disputed as comparing only Naxx-BiS-geared outliers — [[d:hunter#1457800696090071101]], [[d:hunter#1457801243438088427]], [[d:hunter#1457813922135674880]], [[d:hunter#1457860159115104451]], [[d:hunter#1457893919143497893]] (Bluwu's caveat).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Hunter (esp. BM) is considered one of the fastest leveling classes; sub-4.5-day 1-60 reported as fast for Turtle; BM can "auto attack and alt-tab" — [[d:hunter#1448029416209911932]], [[d:hunter#1448350178812624907]].
- **[Survival leveling]** Respeccing into Survival around level 50-54 makes leveling "insanely quick" once combat traps are available — [[d:hunter#1448708800298221650]], [[d:hunter#1448715492343681298]].
- **[Stat priority while leveling]** Int is weak on hunter (no spammable mana-cost shots/dots like mage/boomkin); prioritize stam/agi. Spirit is only borderline useful while leveling for out-of-combat regen (or just feign death + mage water) — [[d:hunter#1447461321879846913]], [[d:hunter#1447461351369867295]], [[d:hunter#1447462242466398278]].
- **[Weapon enchant while leveling]** Little haste gear while leveling — 1% haste enchant is close to or beaten by 8 agi enchant; dual wield: Crusader main hand + 15 agi off hand — [[d:hunter#1447462635179343903]], [[d:hunter#1447463136771833906]].
- **[Lasher farming, ~lvl 46-47]** DME lasher packs: at level 46 without gear, low-level hunters "do a slightly annoying full-room pull and drop traps while kiting" rather than fighting 1-2 at a time (video linked) — [[d:hunter#1448378106250264638]], [[d:hunter#1448378728530055269]]. At 60 with gear, pull 1-2 at a time.
- **[Race]** High Elf called a "noob trap" by Thalenar due to low base agility (only overtakes Night Elf's 2% haste bonus around ~600 agi); disputed indirectly — recent patch changed High Elf base stats so it now starts only 3 agi less than NE (~28 base agi for NE hunter, confirmed via in-game check) — [[d:hunter#1447625083672592636]], [[d:hunter#1447636137417113631]], [[d:hunter#1447636381580001352]], [[d:hunter#1447637011891753021]]. Orc vs High Elf for new hunters: Orc has more pet damage, AP buff, less stun duration — [[d:hunter#1447606549668433961]].
- **[Pet taming while leveling]** Newly tamed pets have a bug: loyalty gains don't visibly "ding" until the pet is manually dismissed/recalled or you zone; recommend dismiss+recall immediately after taming to fix loyalty leveling — [[d:hunter#1447443562449993899]], [[d:hunter#1447443908039544883]], [[d:hunter#1447444073991372911]]. Reports vary on time-to-loyalty-2 (some say ~1 hour of "green happy" AFK) — [[d:hunter#1447450616795500554]].
- **[Pet ability training]** Pets need points/loyalty to train higher spell ranks, and the specific rank must be learned from a beast that already knows it (tame it, feed it once, autocast the skill, wait) — [[d:hunter#1447774248109211778]], [[d:hunter#1447776251388498052]], [[d:hunter#1447776486202540042]]. Use the pinned "Beast/pet spreadsheets" to find which mob teaches which rank — [[d:hunter#1447769633624162454]].
- **[Pet stun bug workaround]** If pet-taming quest fails due to pet stun, aggro the pet with Slow Shot, retreat into water, then tame from the water's edge (pets like Tauren-associated mob won't chase into water and can't stun you) — [[d:hunter#1447276163759083540]]. Alternative: use sleep dust from the plague quest, then tame right after — [[d:hunter#1447265044709773322]].
- **[In-combat traps gate at 40]** Survival's capstone (In-Combat/Untamed Trapper-equivalent) is only available at level 40, and multiple experienced players recommend avoiding Survival/melee leveling until then, since traps are "such a massive part of Survival leveling" that going melee before 40 is a much slower, worse experience; the 39→40 "powerspike" from finally getting combat traps is described as drastic — [[d:hunter#1454057857828917331]], [[d:hunter#1454058839711158365]], [[d:hunter#1457829043134009466]] (Rpecial: "Don't level survival. It's not good till trap talents at 40"). In-combat traps let a leveler drop the single most dangerous mob from a 3-5 pack and kill the rest safely — [[d:hunter#1454059340058067047]] (d2klein).
- **[BM = best leveling spec, broad consensus]** Repeated, near-unanimous recommendation that Beast Mastery is the safest/fastest leveling spec/tree regardless of eventual endgame spec — avoids MM's aggro-management problem and Survival's mana issues; some go MM until Steady Shot then swap into BM — [[d:hunter#1453456557256872040]] (junabii: "no spec better for any kind of leveling than bm hunter"), [[d:hunter#1458035319097266208]] (maaadcheddar), [[d:hunter#1457415852642140371]] (Anxious).
- **[Melee/"agile warrior" leveling playstyle]** Thalenar leveled 1-60 as a self-imposed melee-only challenge (no ranged weapon until 20): turn pet Growl off and use it as support DPS while the hunter tanks, use Aspect of the Monkey, Wing Clip + kite when low, and prioritize Planning Ahead early since it extends Freezing Trap duration for taming — found it far more engaging framed as "an agile warrior" rather than "a ranged class with melee capabilities" — [[d:hunter#1454081134714556447]], [[d:hunter#1455790349690601492]], [[d:hunter#1455790736795762828]], [[d:hunter#1455792144940793917]]. Disputed by Sofie, who leveled 3 hunters and did not find pre-40 melee/Survival leveling strong — [[d:hunter#1455792261563547668]].
- **[Pet leveling XP rule]** A pet only gains XP from kills the hunter would gain XP from (a 5-level-ish gray/green cutoff applies); the pet does not need to participate in the kill, just be out/nearby — leveling a pet that has fallen far behind the hunter's level is a significant time sink best avoided unless the pet's family/skin is otherwise unobtainable later — [[d:hunter#1451186258893344922]] (Jay), [[d:hunter#1451187541930807449]] (Jay: don't bother leveling a pet 30 levels behind).
- **[Gold/leveling farms]** Mob-tagging with a BM hunter (send pet, tag, move on) is called an efficient low-effort leveling/farming method; a "boost farming" loop for gold/XP: pull a dungeon pack with your pet, leave the group, tag the same pack with a friend's AoE class, then kill with the hunter for full credit — [[d:hunter#1448701988471570585]], [[d:hunter#1453109693047111895]] (Duciel).
- **[Pet attack-speed normalization]** Pet auto-attack DPS is normalized by attack speed — e.g. a 1.0-speed pet hits for 3x the damage per swing of a 3.0-speed pet for the same overall DPS; slower attack speed is generally preferred for PvE (better Windfury proc value per swing), faster preferred for PvP (more frequent spell-pushback triggers) — [[d:hunter#1451211345792991324]], [[d:hunter#1451211466450665483]], [[d:hunter#1451211626731540611]] (Duciel).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Survival]** Base ST macro/rotation order: Raptor Strike, Mongoose Bite first (priority), then Lacerate before Carve if gear is good, or Carve before Lacerate if gear is worse; traps kept outside the main macro since they're situational to enemy count — [[d:hunter#1447674320733343764]] (Dresche). Simple macro template: `/startattack, /cast Raptor Strike, /cast Mongoose Bite, /cast Carve (or Lacerate), /cast Lacerate (or Carve)`; put Wing Clip only when nothing else is off CD — [[d:hunter#1447675493225857115]].
- **[Survival]** Krawallzugzug's proposed ST rotation with 2H (Spinal Reaper): RS > MS (Mongoose) > Lacerate > Carve, confirmed worth adding Carve to single-target rotation since it's the only ability using weapon damage — [[d:hunter#1448541614208192602]], [[d:hunter#1448546351657914440]] (Thalenar: "yes, carve is worth it to press").
- **[Survival]** Dresche: prioritizes Carve over trap if a mob is about to die in a couple seconds since traps take time to unfold damage; doesn't add Multi-Shot after knockback, just "runs straight back in," occasionally Arcane Shot when far — [[d:hunter#1448582581980499978]], [[d:hunter#1448584116109840447]].
- **[Kill Command usage/melee]** "KC the bites, let the claws auto cast" for pet ability macro efficiency; can manually cast both for full optimization but harder while doing own rotation — [[d:hunter#1449019611239682190]], [[d:hunter#1449021049093095457]].
- **[Macro debate]** Some argue against combining everything into a single "1-button macro" (Thalenar, Dresche: hurts pet control/muscle memory, situational spell use); others (Dresche, junabii) say certain macros (raptor/mongoose/wing clip/carve) make sense combined, but pet attack/follow commands and traps should stay out of macros — [[d:hunter#1448323251267833888]], [[d:hunter#1448319694149779456]]. Dresche self-reports beating MM in MC "with a one button macro that you smash" — [[d:hunter#1447670389969911992]] (context: [[d:hunter#1447670389969911992]]).
- **[MM]** MM rotation described as "shot weaving," considered the most fun rotation by junabii, who hopes it isn't changed by rework — [[d:hunter#1448356261233037475]].
- **[MM, detailed shot-weaving]** Core loop is Auto Shot + Steady Shot + Multi Shot, with Arcane Shot/Serpent Sting/Aimed Shot only weaved in when they won't clip Steady Shot's cast into the next Auto Shot; use a slow ranged weapon to leave enough "red bar" (swing timer) room; Aimed Shot is mainly reserved for pull/opener or for securing threat on an off-tanked/kited add — detailed multi-message walkthrough by yaboikeenser, confirmed by Sofie — [[d:hunter#1455803663531511943]], [[d:hunter#1455804757485551712]], [[d:hunter#1458465257495199857]], [[d:hunter#1458467658503557140]]. Steady Shot's cast time is governed by Haste (not weapon speed) and its damage is normalized like other shots, so "always take the slowest weapon" from classic is now a comfort/timing preference rather than a hard DPS rule, since Aimed Shot is no longer a core rotation piece — [[d:hunter#1449590498590195855]], [[d:hunter#1449590681608781874]] (Boomsky).
- **[MM, Coordinated Assault priority]** Take Coordinated Assault regardless of feelings about its bugs — it's ~5-10% of an MM hunter's total damage for a single talent point, currently amplified by a bug (see Cooldowns/Stats sections) that lets it scale off the hunter's own stacked melee AP/crit — [[d:hunter#1450963049849819236]], [[d:hunter#1450963341366267956]] (Sofie), [[d:hunter#1453016858515996848]] (Kaslux: "at this point not using CA is trolling"), [[d:hunter#1453041516259053628]] (Itron: "absolute 0 reason to not take it as mm").

- **[General/melee]** 1 Strength = 1 melee AP for hunter; 1 Agility = 1 melee AP baseline, but with 5/5 Lightning Reflexes, 1 Agility = 2 melee AP (Lightning Reflexes also gives +10% agility); conclusion: never take Str over Agi once you have 5/5 Lightning Reflexes, since Agi also gives crit/dodge/armor — [[d:hunter#1448969343881969675]] (Valaen), [[d:hunter#1449011854230814784]], [[d:hunter#1449013099871862796]] (Raggen).
- **[Weapon skill / glancing blows]** Weapon skill reduces glancing-blow damage penalty by a flat percent, linearly; at 315 skill, glancing blows do 95% damage; at 311, one player's sword did 87% — [[d:hunter#1449073651067523174]] (Sisco the Kid), confirmed by forum link `forum.turtle-wow.org/viewtopic.php?t=16790` — [[d:hunter#1449074315160060090]]. From 300→315 every point matters for melee glancing (not for ranged, ranged weapons don't glance) — [[d:hunter#1449122082494484584]], [[d:hunter#1449051618673950860]] (Psyker: ranged doesn't glance).
- **[Weapon skill, hit interaction]** At 315 skill you need only 8% hit to hit-cap ranged/2H attacks (1 hit per 5 skill under cap); for 1-handers you'd technically need 27% hit to fully cap, making dual-wield much harder to gear for than 2H — [[d:hunter#1449123190248050780]] (Psyker).
- **[Weapon skill amount needed, contested]** Thalenar argues 315 is "overkill" and you only need the +5 from weapon-master quests; disputed by пиздинг and Psyker who say 315 is needed on 62+ level raid bosses or you lose damage, and Ossirian's Binding's +6 polearm skill is "a bigger damage increase than anything you could get in your belt slot" — [[d:hunter#1449096666937294858]], [[d:hunter#1449107182690898010]], [[d:hunter#1449108724013862952]].
- **[Weapon skill quests]** Each weapon-type skill-up quest is repeatable only once per weapon type — [[d:hunter#1447635848223785021]] (Sofie).
- **[Windfury vs Agility totem, long debate, contested/unresolved]** Extended debate (~50+ messages) whether Windfury Totem or Agility (Grace) totem gives more DPS to a Survival/melee hunter. Claims: WF stronger at endgame K40 gear/buffed numbers per Psyker/30; Agi totem better per Kaslux's informal dummy test with a Survival hunter, boosting traps/Lacerate/Strike Together together. Consensus caveat: Coordinated Assault ("Strike Together") is coded as the hunter's own melee attack (not the pet's), so it CAN proc Windfury from melee — confirmed by Darkaco mechanically and Boomsky — but true ranged autos/Steady Shot do NOT proc WF at all (a melee-attack check fails at range) — [[d:hunter#1448705881926795427]] (Darkaco, most detailed explanation), [[d:hunter#1448512518292115647]] (Psyker: "wf does not work for ranged shots"), disputed briefly by Kaslux [[d:hunter#1448512695325298789]] then resolved against him. No firm consensus reached on which totem nets more overall raid DPS; flagged as needing real controlled A/B raid testing — [[d:hunter#1448515336356888679]], [[d:hunter#1448515491676033188]]. Open ask: WF should be changed to proc off ranged shots/autos too (community wishlist, not implemented) — [[d:hunter#1448512172056641716]], [[d:hunter#1448517337811652659]].
- **[SP scaling]** Spell Power only affects Arcane Shot, Volley, and Serpent Sting on Hunter; Vamp/leech stat has no SP scaling, only scales with more Vamp stat or more damage dealt; Serpent Sting SP scaling is 1:1 across the dot's length, ticks 5 times = 20% scaling per tick — [[d:hunter#1447365267851055369]], [[d:hunter#1447366011438497832]], [[d:hunter#1447366087078580315]] (Destroses). Traps do NOT scale with Spell Power at all, only with melee AP (and only once the Survival capstone talent is taken): ~1.3 dmg per tick per 10 melee AP for Immolation Trap — measured/tested by Sisco the Kid on a dummy — [[d:hunter#1449843004423667935]], [[d:hunter#1453539019177857065]], [[d:hunter#1450447074452836364]] (Destroses, corrects a poster who assumed T2.5's SP would boost traps).
- **[Weapon-skill/hit interaction, repeated & confirmed]** Above 300 weapon skill, each 5 points reduces required hit by 1%: 305→7% hit needed, 310→6%, 315→5% (applies to 2H/ranged) — [[d:hunter#1458284299878138010]] (Sofie), consistent with earlier figures.
- **[Glancing blow mechanics, clarified late in chunk]** Weapon skill does NOT reduce the *frequency* of glancing blows — it only reduces the damage penalty once a glance occurs. Base glance chance can be up to ~40% vs. same-or-higher-level mobs and increases with level gap; only white/auto-attack hits can glance (never crit); at 315 skill vs. a level-63 raid boss, a glancing hit still deals ~95% of normal damage (compare 300 skill ≈ 65% before any weapon-skill investment) — [[d:hunter#1457754894814412852]] (Zhafu), [[d:hunter#1457760072280309762]] (mzk), [[d:hunter#1457761856826638426]] (Thalenar), [[d:hunter#1457763790413107452]], [[d:hunter#1457765455514046616]]. Note: the weapon-skill formula itself was changed on Turtle around patch 1.17, so some players flag their own numbers as needing rechecking — [[d:hunter#1457771463544274955]] (Sofie).
- **[True "no-glance" 315 skill is a min-max trap on early Ambershire]** Reaching a full no-glance 315 weapon skill vs. level-63 bosses this early in the server's progression requires stacking Human racial (+3), Mastery of Swords/Axes-type quest (+5), a weapon-skill trinket line (+4), and Edgemaster's-type gloves (+7) = +19 beyond base — judged not worth "gimping yourself" for (e.g., Human is bad for Survival, Edgemaster's-type items are weak stat-wise) — top Ambershire Survival logger Thalenar instead just accepts glancing on every boss except Baron/Rag/Garr and still ranks near the top — [[d:hunter#1457692437429489665]], [[d:hunter#1457692908043112449]], [[d:hunter#1457693500941406281]].
- **[Dual-wield Survival hit requirement, resolved]** Contrary to a widely-repeated claim that DW needs 22-27% hit to be viable, multiple experienced Survival players confirm DW works fine with only 8% hit and modest (~9) weapon skill, using a slow main-hand + a fast off-hand that carries bonus weapon skill (e.g. ZG trash-mob axe, +2 axe skill); DW is called viable and even better than 2H for single target, with an informal "soft cap" around 16% hit — [[d:hunter#1457570570773467198]], [[d:hunter#1457570768987885610]], [[d:hunter#1457563175665926318]] (Sofie), [[d:hunter#1457602551686889518]] (Leroy notes mongoose bite hits with both weapons, so ideally you'd want the offhand slow too, but fast-with-skill is the practical compromise).
- **[Weapon-damage-scaling abilities favor slow weapons]** For abilities that scale off a weapon's damage roll rather than normalized DPS (Carve, Multi-Shot, and partially Steady Shot's top-end), a slower weapon does more damage per hit even when two weapons show similar tooltip DPS — explained via an Ashwood Bow vs. Raptor's End comparison — [[d:hunter#1456571356836528282]] (Jay), [[d:hunter#1456573265660215403]], [[d:hunter#1456581226692608167]], [[d:hunter#1456586978282704907]] (d2klein, hedged: believes Multi-Shot/Steady/Aimed are the non-normalized ones).
- **[Str vs Agi weighted value, contested math]** For a melee Survival hunter with 5/5 Lightning Reflexes and Blessing of Kings, 1 Agility's total weighted AP value combines the doubled base AP conversion, the Lightning Reflexes/Kings agility% stacking, and a crit-to-AP conversion (~32 AP per 1% crit, ~1/53 agi per 1% crit) — posters land on differing final numbers (2.52 vs. 3.12 AP per agi) due to disagreement over how the percentage bonuses stack, but all agree Agi strictly beats Str once 5/5 Lightning Reflexes is taken — [[d:hunter#1452278607874363502]] (Bakingsoda), [[d:hunter#1452421568796823712]] (Survival for the fittest, disputes the math), [[d:hunter#1452437747749879900]].
- **[Haste value, contested]** ~1% haste ≈ ~20 AP equivalent value is used as a rough community benchmark for Survival gear comparisons, but Haste is repeatedly flagged as one of the *weaker* stats for Survival specifically because it only affects auto-attack frequency, not weapon-damage-scaling abilities like Carve/Mongoose Bite; long-running dispute between Psyker (haste weak, prioritize weapon skill/hit then Agi) and Thalenar (haste is fine — his top-5-Ambershire-Survival logs don't match Psyker's framing) — [[d:hunter#1452273249340755970]], [[d:hunter#1449426261100335207]], [[d:hunter#1449430428237697165]], [[d:hunter#1449434705786310727]].
- **[Trinket/racial non-stacking]** Bloodfury (Orc racial) and Molten Emberstone (MC AP trinket) do NOT stack — only the higher single value applies instead of adding (observed 200 AP instead of an expected 320), reported as possibly unintended — [[d:hunter#1456621926540644466]] (Sheyba), [[d:hunter#1455596166350635048]] (Commando, corroborating a similar effect between Bloodfury and a racial).
- **[Armor Penetration]** Called "the most useless stat on Turtle WoW" — current AP-pen item values are considered far too low to matter and "should be tripled, or more" to be worth reconsidering; the 2% Haste shoulder enchant (Hyjal) is confirmed better than a 40 Armor Pen alternative for both MM and Survival — [[d:hunter#1450167101015982404]] (Reyka), [[d:hunter#1450180330526081106]], [[d:hunter#1450147236599304365]] (Psyker, +6 reactions).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Coordinated Assault / Strike Together mechanics]** Coordinated Assault is coded as the HUNTER's own melee attack, not the pet's — this means it (a) can proc Windfury, (b) can trigger boss debuffs like Magmadar's hide-reducing debuff, (c) generates threat for the HUNTER rather than the pet, worsening pet threat problems even in BM — [[d:hunter#1447680037573759006]], [[d:hunter#1447680222873911338]], [[d:hunter#1448705881926795427]] (Darkaco, primary source).
- **[Baited Shot bug, BM]** Baited Shot's proc window is inconsistent (0.5-5s), doesn't always trigger off pet crits, is worsened by Kill Command bugs; players report canceling Steady Shot to use Baited Shot only for it to fade before landing, wasting a swing — [[d:hunter#1448708379470991394]] (Darkaco, most detailed), [[d:hunter#1448753650238492822]] (Kaslux).
- **[Kill Command, PvE utility]** Kill Command removes CC effects from the pet (e.g., Nefarian's Bellowing Roar fear, Chromaggus's bronze-flight stun) without removing the underlying debuff — valued highly in raid since it's often the only pet-CC-save available; concern that the "leaked" rework locks this CC-cleanse behind Bestial Wrath's 2-min CD instead of Kill Command's shorter CD, which the community sees as a big downgrade — [[d:hunter#1448755403591782420]], [[d:hunter#1448756106737483917]], [[d:hunter#1448756354805141515]], [[d:hunter#1448773547194323086]].
- **[Weapon skill quests]** Learning the sword/axe/etc "dual wield" or weapon-type skill requires visiting the hunter trainer, and each is repeatable only once per weapon type; buying weapon skill up to 315 seen as unnecessary by some (Thalenar) but recommended by others for melee (see Stats section) — [[d:hunter#1447635848223785021]], [[d:hunter#1448004100850712657]].
- **[Pet feeding macro]** `/run CastSpellByName("Feed Pet")PickupContainerItem(0,1)` used as a feed-pet macro/shortcut — [[d:hunter#1447882833866657944]] (Jay). An addon exists on the Turtle WoW addon page (search "hunter"/"pet"/"feed") that automates feeding — [[d:hunter#1447925361580183552]] (Demonhun).
- **[Pet follow macro]** `/script PetFollow();` used to fix "/petfollow doesn't work" issue in a macro — [[d:hunter#1447686191687143445]] (Mr. Smite).
- **[Hunter's Mark + Aimed Shot macro]** Official Turtle WoW hunter macros page has a macro to auto-cast Aspect of the Hawk before Aimed Shot if not already active: `/run local i,x=1,0 while UnitBuff("player",i) do if UnitBuff("player",i)=="Interface\Icons\Spell_Nature_RavenForm" then x=1 end i=i+1 end if x==0 then CastSpellByName("Aspect of the Hawk") else CastSpellByName("Aimed Shot")end` — behavior note: if Cheetah aspect was active, pressing once only casts Aspect of the Hawk (not Aimed Shot); requires a second press — [[d:hunter#1448302055734120611]], [[d:hunter#1448302879474454579]] (confirmed as intended: ensures AotH is active first).
- **[BM bug compilation, most detailed source in chunk]** Kaslux posted a full write-up of BM's three core interacting abilities and their bugs: **Coordinated Assault** ("hitting with Arcane Shot/Steady Shot/Raptor Strike causes pet's next attack within 6s to also deal 30% of pet AP as physical damage, once per 3s") — Bug 1: does not count as pet damage; Bug 2: counts as yellow melee damage, so it CAN trigger Windfury Totem/Flametongue Totem/Hand of Justice-style procs; Bug 3: scales off the HUNTER's own melee AP (drawing the 30% from hunter, not pet, AP) and hunter melee crit chance; Bug 4: Mongoose Bite also incorrectly triggers it (only Raptor Strike should). **Kill Command** ("removes pet CC, guarantees pet's next 2 abilities within 10s to crit +50% crit damage") — Bug 1: a dodged attack can prevent the next ability from being guaranteed to crit; Bug 2: a dodged-but-landed pet ability doesn't visually consume a charge (shows 2 charges when 1 remains); flagged as too RNG-reliant since dodge/parry/resist can waste the whole effect. **Baited Shot** ("instant, 125% weapon dmg, pet gains 5 focus/8s vs. the target, only after a pet crit, 2s ICD on the focus gen") — Bug: gets canceled by casting, sometimes leaving under 0.5-1s to react — [[d:hunter#1453586841000476785]] (Kaslux, full post), corroborated/extended by Destroses (KC also fails to crit Wind Serpent's Lightning Breath when left on auto-cast, only works if manually cast) — [[d:hunter#1453590502342524950]], and by yaboikeenser, who ran an unbuffed heroic-dummy test and found BM performing significantly worse than expected: "after first use it seldom works... this massively devalues BM" — [[d:hunter#1450915296566317147]], [[d:hunter#1450915603849285845]].
- **[Coordinated Assault favors Survival over BM]** Because CA draws from the hunter's own melee AP/crit rather than the pet's, Survival hunters (who already stack melee AP/agility) benefit far more from this BM-tree talent than BM hunters themselves — some logs reportedly show CA outdamaging a Survival hunter's own Immolation Trap — [[d:hunter#1454822559718576295]] (Darkaco), [[d:hunter#1454822679503966210]], [[d:hunter#1454823580918480907]]. A Chinese-community researcher (Vicent) separately claims the CA bug grants a Survival hunter's two melee abilities a 36% Windfury proc chance instead of the normal 20%, calling it "a bigger DPS boost than all buffs received since patch 1.17" — single, untranslated, unverified source — [[d:hunter#1451485473594933268]], [[d:hunter#1451484146290524211]].
- **[CA melee-stat minmax tech]** Because of the CA bug, even ranged MM/BM hunters get real value from requesting Blessing of Might and using Juju Power/Elemental Sharpening Stones/Elixir of Giants on themselves despite being "ranged" — treated as a known, likely-to-be-patched exploit rather than intended design — [[d:hunter#1452611024845934603]] (Kaslux), [[d:hunter#1454087140098179275]], [[d:hunter#1454088114573148371]].
- **[Pet threat/tanking limits]** Pets cannot be made crit-immune or gain bonus avoidance, so they fold quickly against hard raid damage; pet tanking realistically works only for dungeon off-tanking/emergency lifesaving, not raid main-tanking. Owl/Bat Screech was nerfed from a 1s to a 4s cooldown on Turtle (vs. spammable in classic), greatly reducing its AoE-threat value; Gorilla's Thunderstomp AoE is on a longer (~30-40s) cooldown so is more reliable once available but not spammable — [[d:hunter#1455149031322816699]] (Destroses), [[d:hunter#1455314735687008359]] (yaboikeenser), [[d:hunter#1449144817283240079]], [[d:hunter#1455315108686331985]].
- **[Healers don't heal/dispel pets]** Widely reported that healers generally ignore hunter pets (pet frames often aren't shown by default) — the practical workaround is personally bribing/asking guild healers, since there's no in-combat way to heal a pet without channeling Mend Pet (costly GCD/mana); community wishlist includes a passive or damage-triggered pet self-heal — [[d:hunter#1454829088924762234]], [[d:hunter#1454830321315877048]], [[d:hunter#1454831910835716302]], [[d:hunter#1454832406900117564]].
- **[Pet positioning bugs]** Pets can be caught in ground-effect AoEs (e.g., Shadowflame) because Pet Command: Move is unreliable — it can walk instead of run, or mis-target a nearby player instead of the intended location; pets also sometimes path to the center of a mob's hitbox, exposing them to cleaves — [[d:hunter#1454828182825074834]] (Kaslux), [[d:hunter#1454833746342707311]], [[d:hunter#1454082973958934612]] (Darkaco).
- **[Pet-move speed bug, filed]** Command Pet (also affects hunter pets) can get stuck at walking speed toward its target; fixable only by setting the pet to Follow then re-issuing Attack; official bug report filed at `turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=19262` — [[d:hunter#1454078094246940745]], [[d:hunter#1454079464626458783]], [[d:hunter#1454080497536991264]] (Kaslux, confirms it also affects hunters, not just warlocks).
- **[Pet Command: Take Position quirk]** Does not count as a "spell" for casting-while-standing restrictions, so it can be used while sitting/eating/drinking — but feeding the pet cannot be done while sitting — [[d:hunter#1455295359667404830]], [[d:hunter#1455295559551160461]] (Sisco the Kid).
- **[Shot-timing addon]** The community-made "Quiver" addon (GitHub: SabineWren/Quiver) tracks exact shot-timer state and provides a no-clip Steady Shot macro: `/run local a, b = Quiver.GetSecondsRemainingShoot(); local c = a and b < -0.25; local f = c and CastSpellByName or Quiver.CastNoClip; f("Steady Shot")` — called "a must-have for Hunter"; an alternative, "ShotTimer" (GitHub: MarcelineVQ/ShotTimer), gives default per-skill timer commands — [[d:hunter#1455667361721094235]], [[d:hunter#1452302855443845150]] (Sheyba), [[d:hunter#1451177645952860242]] (Duciel).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Pet loyalty]** Newly tamed pets keep gaining loyalty internally but the loyalty-level-up notification/effect doesn't trigger until the pet is manually dismissed (via spell) or you zone/cross a zone barrier — recommend dismiss+recall right after taming — [[d:hunter#1447443562449993899]], [[d:hunter#1447443908039544883]], [[d:hunter#1447444073991372911]], [[d:hunter#1447444905621196920]] (dismissing via any method may reset the affection timer, exact mechanics unclear/quirky per Boomsky).
- **[Pet stable]** Pets getting "stuck" in stable slots (can't retrieve half the slots) is a known/recurring issue; a workaround is dragging a pet directly onto another pet in the stable to force-swap (swapping from no-pet to a pet doesn't work, only pet-to-pet) — [[d:hunter#1447636091946533048]], [[d:hunter#1447637898332737600]] (Garth).
- **[Pet talents]** A newly tamed pet can show "-13 talents" if it was tamed with skills already learned by a previous owner — explained by shn as pre-learned skills reducing available talent points — [[d:hunter#1447912257295089716]], [[d:hunter#1447918205023817798]].
- **[Coordinated Assault crits]** Coordinated Assault crits reportedly do not trigger Baited Shot procs, raised as a possible bug (unanswered/unconfirmed in this range) — [[d:hunter#1447640091445956760]] (Chibbi).
- **[Windfury misfire]** Windfury attempting to proc off Coordinated Assault performs a melee range-check that fails at range, so it "does nothing" for ranged-triggered procs even though the attack is coded as melee — [[d:hunter#1447680037573759006]], [[d:hunter#1448705881926795427]].
- **[Baited Shot]** Inconsistent proc window (0.5-5s) and doesn't always fire off pet crits — see Cooldowns section.
- **[Tribute mana regen]** Question raised whether DM Tribute runs are "still bugged" with mana regen — unanswered in this range — [[d:hunter#1447832038550929439]] (Pharmaka).
- **[Turtle pet]** Turtle-family pet's "Shell Shield" (or similar) reduces its own attack speed while shielded, seen as a design flaw that makes the pet bad despite high mitigation — [[d:hunter#1448037415477444729]] (Kaslux), [[d:hunter#1448037604250357820]] (Boomsky: "turtle might be decent if it had actual threat instead of negative threat").
- **[Wind Serpent skin]** A "Wind Serpent" pet entry with an unused-in-vanilla skin found in the "book of pets" (database.turtle-wow.org/?npc=61480); poster suspects the link/entry may not correspond to an actual tameable in-game beast — raised multiple times, unanswered — [[d:hunter#1447671024962502687]], [[d:hunter#1447911581026619625]].
- **[Rare pet tamability]** A rare centaur-tied pet reportedly cannot be tamed while "controlled by another unit" (tied to a rare NPC's spawn) and disappears when that NPC is killed — [[d:hunter#1447967325906145402]], [[d:hunter#1447967404599545856]].
