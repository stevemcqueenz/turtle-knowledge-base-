## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[MM]** Full rework: Aimed Shot now has a short cast (resets to ~1s cast on a Lock and Load proc), Steady Shot baseline, and a new "Experimental Ammunition" proc system where shots randomly grant Enchanted Ammunition (buffs next Arcane Shot), Poisonous Ammunition (buffs Serpent Sting), or Explosive Ammunition (buffs next Multi-Shot) — [[d:hunter#1486774833995055286]] (Alysia's macro encodes the 3 buffs, 2026-03-27).
- **[MM]** Net raid dps roughly unchanged from pre-patch for the same player on the same content (1679 dps pre-patch vs 1664 dps post-patch, "use best weeks" comparison) despite the rework's complexity — [[d:hunter#1485495893200343161]] (Sofie, 2026-03-23; caveated by Boomsky as "best crit rng of 20+ weeks vs one week" [[d:hunter#1485502583543173262]]).
- **[BM]** Old Baited Shot (crit-triggered pet buff + Focus regen) removed, replaced by Kill Command (big burst hit, no GCD, off pet AP, requires a pet crit to trigger) plus passive "Scent of Blood" (no crit requirement but no Focus regen either). Widely called simpler/more powerful but also "boring"/"macro and forget" — [[d:hunter#1486098305254686841]], [[d:hunter#1486098380701564988]] (Chibbi, 2026-03-25), design counter-arguments from Darkaco/Kantrul at [[d:hunter#1486124828032237740]], [[d:hunter#1486131484669509703]].
- **[BM]** Kill Command formula per community testing: ~80% of pet AP, multiplied by the pet family's damage modifier (Raptor/Cat 1.10, Owl 1.07, Scorpid 0.94); uses the pet's own crit chance, not the hunter's — [[d:hunter#1485483224225546281]] (Darkaco), [[d:hunter#1485789832759349258]] (Focus: Raptor 802 dmg vs Scorpid 685 dmg on matched dummy test, ~17% gap) — [[d:hunter#1485791011937718446]].
- **[BM]** Scorpid Poison was massively overtuned at patch launch (poison ticks scaling to 1-2k+ via an Eye-of-the-Beast AP snapshot exploit before applying the 5th stack), hotfixed within ~18-24h down to roughly 250-500 dmg/tick — [[d:hunter#1485866035889311754]]→[[d:hunter#1485867690877780040]] (2026-03-24), confirmed post-fix numbers at [[d:hunter#1485904705883214075]] (Kaslux, "max of 500... instead of that crazy 1800").
- **[BM/PvP]** Kill Command was flagged by PvP players at patch launch as hitting 1-2k on cloth targets and seeming "bugged"; Darkaco clarified it's working as intended (just very strong from 80% pet-AP scaling) — [[d:hunter#1485449416088223764]], [[d:hunter#1485482802127310973]] (2026-03-23).
- New 2% ranged-crit enchant ("Facetted Crystal Scope," from Draenei-exile rep at Revered) added; visually/tooltip-bugged for several days (didn't show on weapon or in stat addons) but functionally live from day one per independent tests — Sofie: rhok'delar+old scope 26.3% crit vs slavemaker+new scope 28.2% crit over 1000 arrows each — [[d:hunter#1485556365173850123]]; Focus: +2.5% crit over 1000 arrows — [[d:hunter#1485775614395813978]]. Bug report open at id 20716 [[d:hunter#1485556018057314366]]; fixed visually via client hotfix + BetterCharacterStats update by 2026-03-27 [[d:hunter#1486823166675386589]].
- New craftable ammo added: arrows (Bright Wood, Shade Wood, Smooth Ironfeather, Starfeather) and Enchanted Thorium Shells (Engineering bullets) — roughly 17-19 dps, cheaper/easier to gather than old Thorium Arrows (half the bars per stack) but still below Doomshot (~20 dps, still BiS) — [[d:hunter#1485738412853170369]] (Meta's recap, 2026-03-24), [[d:hunter#1486190566013403186]] (Raggen), [[d:hunter#1486008034437369856]].
- New pet family **Moth** (Moonwhisper Coast, new zone): Pollen Burst ability (group heal that scales/can crit on a 30s CD, plus enemy debuff) but persistently buggy — see Bugs section — [[d:hunter#1485547039088447508]] (Meta, 2026-03-23).
- New pet family **Cobra** (Turtle-custom, not on vanilla Petopia — refer to community pet spreadsheet) — poison-spit ignores armor, became relatively stronger than nerfed Scorpid for short fights — [[d:hunter#1485990912461897870]] (Valaen), [[d:hunter#1485785746701946910]] (Tarane).
- Volley confirmed changed from a channeled cast to a 3-second cast (1 second if talented as MM) — this patch, not a bug — [[d:hunter#1486055681734869063]] (Focus).
- Explosive Ammunition (Multi-Shot proc) bugged: intended ~20% of weapon damage per a relayed dev comment, but tests show far less (~10% or a flat ~60 dmg regardless of equipped weapon, even a level-10 white weapon), suggesting it's computing off a normalized/AP value rather than actual weapon damage — [[d:hunter#1485468072860192828]] (relayed dev quote), [[d:hunter#1486218766466547792]] (Vellasta), [[d:hunter#1486226839654629486]]→[[d:hunter#1486228875125067826]] (Vellasta's math breakdown).
- Steady Shot is confirmed fully baseline this patch (no longer an MM talent at all) — [[d:hunter#1487446185630371870]] (Stunfisk, correcting Dresche's guide). Lock and Load's Aimed-Shot-CD reset is confirmed to trigger only on a crit (Steady/Aimed/Arcane); a non-crit does not reset it — [[d:hunter#1487440846071533629]] (Dresche).
- **[Survival]** Patch buffed Lacerate's bleed-side damage, Raptor Strike, and Mongoose Bite; only lowered Coordinated Assault's damage slightly — [[d:hunter#1488832787128647792]] (Swifteye, 2026-04-01).
- Serpent Sting's armor debuff (from Poisonous/"serpent" Ammunition procs) reduces boss armor by 240 and stacks with all other armor debuffs (confirmed) — [[d:hunter#1488876111252160624]] (Vellasta, 2026-03-28).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Survival]** Consensus across the week: Survival remains the strongest overall raid dps spec with a comparatively simple ("one-button") rotation, beating both reworked MM and BM in most logs — [[d:hunter#1485679555783164149]] (Vellasta), [[d:hunter#1486510805460582470]] (Sabagina: "Surv still mogs MM"), [[d:hunter#1486103359432687757]] (dumbid: "BM works, surv still great aoe, mm great st and better aoe").
- **[MM]** Rework judged better but not the feared/hoped "#1 dps spec"; damage roughly matched pre-patch levels in early logs but the rotation became far more RNG/proc-chasing (see Rotation section) — [[d:hunter#1485466176585728211]] (Vellasta), [[d:hunter#1485495893200343161]].
- **[BM]** Rework surprised the community as strong single-target dps ("BM is the winner in this patch" per one poster) though the new 2-button Kill Command playstyle is widely criticized as boring/simple compared to old Baited Shot; talent-tree bloat in the bottom half of the tree persists — [[d:hunter#1485660377009229924]] (Hakuna), [[d:hunter#1485661872089862274]] (Darkaco on Baited Shot's removed design), [[d:hunter#1485662177497976954]] (Destroses, talent bloat, +1).
- **[BM vs MM]** Hakuna's same-day A/B raid test, full T1/epics: BM ≈780 dps vs MM ≈650 dps; MM better only on big AoE packs — [[d:hunter#1486051535312912394]], [[d:hunter#1486052250005671946]].
- **[BM vs MM]** Santa Bear's Naxx boss-only comparison: "BM rank 1, MM rank 2. Very close results," Raptor 2.0 pet, KC crit for 7153 — [[d:hunter#1486419920332787763]]; bhors independently found BM+Scorpid beat MM every time on K40 single-target with full-T3 hunters — [[d:hunter#1486333991916142795]].
- **[MM]** Bobob: "MM is just a slot machine, can win big or do less. Overall MM probably still takes it [vs BM]" depending on fight/rng — [[d:hunter#1486124588764237894]] (disputed by nobody directly, +1).
- **[Ranged overall]** By late in the chunk, Ambershire's top parsers in recent BWL raids were ranged (MM/BM), suggesting ranged can lead but "it's just a lot more work to pull off and people cba [can't be arsed]" — [[d:hunter#1487042069359886406]], [[d:hunter#1487042535863091330]] (Elmgren).
- **[Overall, end-of-chunk verdict]** Barwat's summary as of 2026-04-02: "for overall dps in raid, survival is still best. BM might come in very close second or better for single target, and MM looks to be a little worse, but it's mostly because lock and load and ammunition are a bit clunky" — [[d:hunter#1489032257317507102]] (+consistent with Kalamity/SliceOfBerry guild anecdotes at [[d:hunter#1486889430131408956]]).

## Leveling
- **[General]** BM is the easiest/most "chill" leveling spec — pet tanks/holds threat reliably, lets you shoot from safety; Survival recommended once at 60, or as a more engaging melee-flavored alternative especially past level 40 — [[d:hunter#1485929374065889361]] (Faustmeyer), [[d:hunter#1485933547364810873]] (ThriceWoven), [[d:hunter#1486828708537831436]] (Varnoc's full onboarding answer for a new player).
- **[BM leveling build, ~lvl48]** Don't skip Frenzy; take Swift Aspects; once pet is maxed, put remaining points in Lethal Shots (permanent 5% crit) rather than a 15%-speed proc talent; Aimed Shot is "useless" as BM; Improved Mend Pet is situational (Hakuna: useless unless soloing dungeons; Elmgren disputes — useful for elite quests too); Bestial Discipline useless on non-claw pets; hit doesn't matter while leveling (max useful overleveling is +2 levels, and even that is inefficient) — [[d:hunter#1486063417503645787]] through [[d:hunter#1486068038028759122]] (Hakuna/Elmgren/Icantkite thread, 2026-03-24).
- **[Leveling pets]** Screech-ability pets (Owl/Carrion Bird/Bat/Vulture) recommended for AoE-threat leveling; a Hinterland Eagle can spawn as low as level 34-35 already knowing Screech Rank 3 (normally requires level 48 to train) — [[d:hunter#1486143346475798568]], [[d:hunter#1486403885294817280]] (Valaen). Pet attack speed is normalized (1.0 and 2.0-speed pets deal equal dps); faster pets interrupt enemy casters more often — [[d:hunter#1485884411361628261]] (Kaslux), [[d:hunter#1486404340007567390]] (Roy).
- **[Raptors]** Red ZG raptor (level 60, elite) gives max-rank Rend; Black Raptor (Grim Reaches, levelable from 37) prized mainly for rarity, was the only 2.0-speed raptor before ZG existed — [[d:hunter#1486065326750961726]], [[d:hunter#1486074413647532082]], [[d:hunter#1486074658242564306]].
- **[Moth]** New pet available in Moonwhisper Coast level range; gives a scaling, critable group-wide heal on a 30s CD plus an enemy debuff — strong reported value once functional, but see Bugs section for reliability issues — [[d:hunter#1486740255108370495]] (Dinousek), [[d:hunter#1486741684095287306]] (Rangtuk).
- **[Gold/farming]** Doomshot solo-farm remains the standard high-value ammo/gold route (~30 min run, sell excess at ~10g/stack); requires a near-pixel-perfect pull route, a parachute cloak (Survival talent or Engineering item) to skip a gap, Freezing Trap + Feign Death to split packs, Bestial Wrath to stop the pet being polymorphed, and a Lucidity Potion to avoid hex while looting — [[d:hunter#1486958066199953439]] (Tydorel, detailed walkthrough, 2026-03-27).
- **[New rep]** New Draenei-exile rep vendor (Swamp of Sorrows) sells the crit-scope schematic at Revered, plus new bullet/arrow schematics — [[d:hunter#1485630355993264198]], [[d:hunter#1486033229747323072]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[MM]** Converged priority: Aimed Shot on CD (now a fast cast, resets to ~1s on Lock and Load); weave Steady Shot between casts to fish for Lock and Load; fire Arcane Shot on CD whenever "Enchanted Ammunition" is up (it can also proc Lock and Load itself); skip Multi-Shot on pure single-target (its proc is weak/bugged, not worth the GCD); use Serpent Sting mainly for the raid nature-damage debuff rather than personal dps — [[d:hunter#1486698931491442708]] (Sofie: "Aimed and steady, arcane when it's ammu is up, multi only if multi targets", +2), [[d:hunter#1486217854574399639]] (Raggen), [[d:hunter#1485608605158866965]] (Itron, +1).
- **[MM]** Sofie's final clean summary of the rotation change (2026-03-28): "before was steady->multi->auto->steady->auto etc, now is aimed shot->auto shot->steady shot->arcane shot->auto shot — hunting procs of Lock and Load with aimed shot, ignoring serpent sting ammunition and multi shot ammunition unless aoe situation" — [[d:hunter#1488874340295643166]].
- **[MM, maturing consensus by end of chunk]** Several experienced players converged on largely ignoring the Experimental Ammunition procs altogether and just weaving Arcane Shot whenever possible after Aimed/Steady Shot to fish Lock and Load, since tracking/reacting to the ammo buffs "isn't rewarding enough" and "you can basically ignore it and do pretty much the same dps" — [[d:hunter#1488488059505741957]] (Vellasta), [[d:hunter#1488504859744276562]] (Zwift), [[d:hunter#1488527231008641095]] (The J: "Even serpent isnt worth it imo. Multi def not worth it on boss fights. Just arcane + steady to trigger LnL into aimed shot. Ignore the rest.", +1), [[d:hunter#1488530576230318080]] (Vellasta).
- **[Survival]** Single-target ability priority (two independent, converging answers): Mongoose Bite first, then Raptor Strike, then Immolation Trap, then Lacerate, then Carve, then Wing Clip as filler — [[d:hunter#1488177971733991627]] (Sofie, in German: "für single target: raptor strike, dann immo trap, mongoose, lacerate, carve, wing clip"), corrected/refined by [[d:hunter#1488178738528124948]] (Survival for the fittest, in German: Mongoose Bite first, Raptor Strike second, Carve below Mongoose Bite, Immolation Trap below Raptor/Mongoose). For AoE: Explosive Trap first, then Carve — [[d:hunter#1488177971733991627]]. Never cast Wing Clip if anything else is ready or about to be ready — [[d:hunter#1488178991918485585]] (Sofie).
- **[MM]** Only single-target shots (Steady/Aimed/Arcane) can proc Lock and Load — Multi-Shot cannot — [[d:hunter#1486698734736642089]] (Sofie).
- **[MM]** Widely reported as "stressful"/RNG-heavy: players must simultaneously track the current ammo-proc icon, Serpent Sting timer, and Lock-and-Load/Aimed-Shot readiness; several veteran players say it plays worse or no better than the old rotation despite the added complexity — [[d:hunter#1485676134380343307]] (Aurin, +2), [[d:hunter#1485677879302946906]], [[d:hunter#1485679432034156714]] (Amathaunta).
- **[BM]** Effectively Steady Shot spam (non-clipping) with Kill Command macroed onto every ability (KC has no GCD) and Multi-Shot on CD; widely described as a simple "2-button" spec — [[d:hunter#1485618365513994341]] (Vellasta), [[d:hunter#1486062363688829080]] (Hakuna: "SS spam baked in KC"), [[d:hunter#1486412285546205354]].
- **[Design commentary]** The forced-reactive nature of Enchanted/Poisonous/Explosive Ammunition procs (must consume with Arcane/Serpent/Multi respectively) collides with Aimed Shot's frequent CD resets, making cooldown availability "chaotic and unpredictable" rather than a fixed priority list — [[d:hunter#1486255776367382598]] (Meta's theorycrafting writeup, +2).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[MM]** Volley (3s cast, or 1s with the MM talent) + Multi-Shot is strong on packs; Zwift logged ~2500 dps on a large add pack using 1s-cast Volley + Multi-Shot, placing him "right below mages" — [[d:hunter#1486284331247996989]] (+1).
- **[MM]** Sample AoE loop: "Volley/Aim/Multi/-renataki-/Multi/Volley," described as feeling "wild" — [[d:hunter#1485737439137108059]] (Wuuc).
- **[MM]** Aimed Shot's Lock-and-Load-triggered cleave into nearby targets is inconsistent: "sometimes it cleaves mobs 1m away from target, sometimes it misses half of them in a straight line" — [[d:hunter#1485611062836461618]], [[d:hunter#1485611128162877500]] (Hakuna).
- **[MM]** Multi-Shot's Explosive Ammunition proc is prioritized more highly on AoE packs specifically because crits there are needed to chain into piercing Aimed Shot crits — [[d:hunter#1486257650852302899]], [[d:hunter#1486257843756728320]] (Boomsky).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Ranged/all]** Ranged hit: need 9% hit, or 8% hit + 305 weapon skill, to never miss; each 5 weapon skill = +1% hit and +10% reduced glance damage up to the skill cap of 315 — [[d:hunter#1486358400244777121]] (Zae), [[d:hunter#1485763124253425858]] (dumbid).
- **[Survival, DW]** Melee hit soft cap is 8% (same as 2H) but the true "never miss" hard cap for the off-hand is much higher (~22-24%, unconfirmed exact number); Surefooted grants DW an extra +3% hit vs 2H because the off-hand needs it more — [[d:hunter#1486732672683347988]], [[d:hunter#1486732195593715854]] (Dresche); Surefooted's bonus is reportedly not shown on the character sheet even when functioning correctly per devs — [[d:hunter#1485759515965263924]] (Hakuna: "Lhian said it works, i trust Lhian"), [[d:hunter#1486077656066822288]] ("addon issue").
- **[MM]** Crit-rating eAP value estimated to have risen with this patch (crit now triggers Lock and Load): community converged around ~50 eAP per crit point, up from ~32-40 in earlier tiers — [[d:hunter#1486658244486365204]] (Zwift), [[d:hunter#1486692505604067349]] (Dresche: classic-phase baseline ~40, "should be something between 40 and 50" on Turtle), [[d:hunter#1486705272167858227]] (Dresche, "we are in 50 territory").
- **[MM]** Arcane Shot spell-power scaling per MM talents: ~0.473 extra damage per SP on a normal hit, ~1.0879 on a crit; doubled for Enchanted-Ammunition procs — [[d:hunter#1486034422972284998]] (Varnoc).
- **[Survival]** Roughly 1 Agility ≈ 2.6+ effective AP once Kings + the Agility talent are factored in; a permanent +25 Agility 2H enchant (≈65-75 eAP) judged clearly better than Crusader's proc (100 AP, non-permanent; the claimed ~92.5% uptime is disputed/not confirmed by logs) — [[d:hunter#1485975870316216472]], [[d:hunter#1485976378502283354]] (Elmgren), [[d:hunter#1485978606118375564]] (Elmgren's own dummy test found long stretches without a Crusader proc).
- **[BM]** Kill Command damage scales with the pet family's damage modifier, not just raw pet AP: Raptor (1.10 mod) hit 802 vs Scorpid (0.94 mod) hit 685 on a matched dummy test — a ~17% gap for high-modifier pets — [[d:hunter#1485789832759349258]], [[d:hunter#1485791011937718446]] (Focus).
- **[Survival]** Traps scale with nothing by default; they only scale with Attack Power once the 31-point Survival talent is taken, and never scaled with Spell Power (contrary to some assumptions) — [[d:hunter#1486589864207057069]] (Vellasta: "Yep from the 31-point talent"), [[d:hunter#1486696716060201093]], [[d:hunter#1486703013342216252]] (lwif). The Serpent Sting applied via Mongoose Bite/Wing Clip is the one Survival component that does scale with Spell Power — [[d:hunter#1486579356875886692]] (Vellasta).
- **[MM]** Spell-power consumables give little value: Sofie's Naxx logs show Arcane Shot only ~3-10% of total damage even post-patch, so heavy SP investment (Flask of Supreme Power, SP elixirs) is estimated at only ~2% overall gain — "not worth it for a ton of gold spent" unless already fully BiS — [[d:hunter#1485478239123275908]], [[d:hunter#1485479954698473523]], [[d:hunter#1485485437219770419]] (Vellasta, disputing Sofie's 10% figure with her own 3% finding — single-source discrepancy, unresolved).
- **[All specs, eAP consensus by end of chunk]** Rough community stat-weight consensus (explicitly caveated as unverified/no real sim exists): 1 Agility ≈ 3 eAP, 1 Crit rating ≈ 50-55 eAP, 1 AP = 1 eAP, 1 Haste ≈ 12-14 eAP for MM/BM but 20-40 eAP for Survival (too much haste risks clipping); 1 Hit is worth taking only up to the relevant hit cap; Armor Penetration ≈ 0 eAP if the raid fully debuffs boss armor, or ~60 eAP per 100 ArP if ~1000+ armor remains (most raids under-debuff) — [[d:hunter#1488075516060766330]] (Zwift, "don't take it as gospel"), [[d:hunter#1488442215729594449]] (Survival for the fittest's independent haste/ArP breakdown). On the character sheet specifically, 53 Agility = 1% ranged crit — [[d:hunter#1488074387918688336]] (Sofie). These weights were disputed as coming from an unverifiable "private" spreadsheet — [[d:hunter#1488079776479707166]], [[d:hunter#1488165063033491547]] (Survival for the fittest).
- **[Survival, DW]** A hard cap of 28% hit exists for dual-wield (to always land off-hand autos); ability-miss cap (8%) is separately affected by weapon skill — [[d:hunter#1489021201153134692]] (Barwat). Get weapon skill "as high as you can, to 315" rather than stopping at a small fixed bonus, since roughly 40% of DW Survival's damage comes from white/auto-attack damage — [[d:hunter#1487845942529888279]] (Sofie), [[d:hunter#1486732672683347988]].
- **[MM]** Base weapon dps shown on the tooltip gets scaled up substantially by ammo/talents when actually fired; weapon damage remains one of the single biggest damage scalars for MM, making even small (e.g. +5-6) weapon dps upgrades very significant — [[d:hunter#1488845803907973242]] (nati bati yi), [[d:hunter#1488800618238705736]] (Survival for the fittest: "hunters quit guilds over 5 dps weapon increases that get looted to warriors").
