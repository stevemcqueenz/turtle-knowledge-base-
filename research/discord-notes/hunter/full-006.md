## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Patch 1.18.1 dropped 2026-03-20 (local time varied by region, e.g. 4am vs 5pm) — [[d:hunter#1483638689253953788]] [[d:hunter#1483640110074298408]].
- **[General]** Talents were reset for everyone on patch day — [[d:hunter#1484491232167329855]].
- **[MM]** New capstone talent **Experimental Ammunition** (verbatim): "Your Aimed Shot deals an additional 5% of its damage as Fire, Arcane or Nature damage and grants you a benefit depending on the element: Fire Ammunition: your next Multi-Shot hits explode for 20% ranged weapon damage in 5 yard radius. Arcane Ammunition: your next Arcane Shot deals 100% increased damage and weakens target's resistance to magic, increasing magic damage taken by 3% for 6 seconds. Nature Ammunition: your next Serpent Sting deals 100% increased damage and applies a corrosive poison that reduces target's armor by 4 per level (240 at level 60) for 15 seconds. Effects last 1 minute and cycle Fire→Arcane→Nature; only one active at a time." — [[d:hunter#1484528608927027270]] (Itron, 2026-03-19, quoting patch notes).
- **[MM]** Aimed Shot cast time reduced 3s→2s baseline; under a Lock and Load proc it behaves like a ~1s cast + 0.5s "reload" component that ignores haste (so total ~1.3-1.5s even hasted); 26s base cooldown — [[d:hunter#1484519407429619732]] [[d:hunter#1484519076775858408]] [[d:hunter#1484520798176804925]], confirmed live as "aimed shot feels nuts now" [[d:hunter#1484605203515834419]].
- **[MM]** Lock and Load: 100% proc chance (on qualifying shot crit) to reset/empower Aimed Shot — [[d:hunter#1484523895498215485]] [[d:hunter#1484824260928077844]] [[d:hunter#1484825677902647378]], but only triggers off crits, not raw attacks, so low-crit fresh-60 hunters get poor value from it — [[d:hunter#1485396368804806788]] [[d:hunter#1485396418599587942]].
- **[BM]** Kill Command buffed to scale with 80% of pet's AP, requires a crit to fire, and has a GCD (patch notes say no GCD change) — [[d:hunter#1484195012932669651]] [[d:hunter#1484267358452318298]] [[d:hunter#1484400891422576661]]. Confirmed live hitting very hard: crits of 2000-3400+ reported, e.g. 2092 on a low-geared level-52 in AV, 3100 in a ZG test — [[d:hunter#1485328216867737672]] [[d:hunter#1484610739070308475]] [[d:hunter#1484952030567989539]].
- **[BM]** Coordinated Assault (CA) nerfed ~10% and its tooltip "corrected"/confirmed: scales off hunter **melee attack power** (not pet AP, not RAP), counts as a hunter melee-style attack — procs mage Frost Shield/Ice Barrier, triggers Windfury/Flametongue/Hand of Justice at 41-yard range — [[d:hunter#1484428180612845578]] [[d:hunter#1484427303483080844]] [[d:hunter#1484427693867925596]] [[d:hunter#1484505790428479538]]. This disproportionately benefits Survival (high melee AP) over MM/BM, which the community considers poor design for a Beast Mastery-tree talent — [[d:hunter#1484403113082355834]] [[d:hunter#1484426680561831947]]. Devs reportedly said (per a bug-report reply) they intend to keep CA as hunter (melee) AP rather than pet/RAP — [[d:hunter#1484441717590593556]] [[d:hunter#1484791600323363029]] (contested, see Contested section).
- **[Talents]** Improved Hunter's Mark removed entirely from the Marks tree; nobody missed it ("garbage talent") — [[d:hunter#1484366835540299878]] [[d:hunter#1484371791957856346]] [[d:hunter#1484399000571347146]].
- **[BM/Survival]** New talent **Alone Against the World / Lone Wolf** (Survival, 2nd row): +6% total damage while having no pet — [[d:hunter#1484190388377096366]]. Pet damage (incl. Strike Together) is normally ~10-15% of a hunter's single-target output, so the talent was predicted (and largely confirmed) to be a net DPS loss for anyone who plays their pet well, useful mainly as insurance against high pet mortality or for players who dislike pet micromanagement — [[d:hunter#1483846991493595227]] (Sofie: pet = 14% dmg on Gnarlmoon AoE fight, 12% on Twins) [[d:hunter#1484549579847499990]] (Bobob: pets ~11% of MM dps w/o CA) [[d:hunter#1484443857402724372]] (Kaslux/AI analysis: Lone Wolf = ~10.4% net loss vs pet+procs at 15.4% of output) [[d:hunter#1484665264741027901]] (Sisco the Kid: "for someone who has difficulty keeping their pet alive... it's a godsend. for players who can manage their pet, probably slightly worse").
- **[BM]** "Strike Together" pet-damage talent bugged: scales off **hunter melee AP** instead of pet AP, inflating pet damage for Survival specifically (high melee AP); devs have not acknowledged it as a bug — [[d:hunter#1483848490428661921]] [[d:hunter#1483848657684922562]] [[d:hunter#1483843321024745575]] [[d:hunter#1483848962699165859]].
- **[MM/Survival]** Volley reworked: castable (not channeled), retains its existing spell-power scaling and additionally gains AP scaling — [[d:hunter#1484194643590516910]] [[d:hunter#1484273026777481216]] [[d:hunter#1484315483057553470]]. Actual cast/aim time is 1.5s, not 1s as some assumed (0.5s pre-channel aiming time carried over) — [[d:hunter#1484741389655740437]] [[d:hunter#1484878488740696155]].
- **[Survival]** Vicious Strikes buffed (damage%) — [[d:hunter#1484246792790081738]] [[d:hunter#1484554060194517143]].
- **[General]** New craftable ammo via reworked Survival profession: base craftable arrows/bullets ~17.5-19 dps (worse than Doomshot ~23 dps, better than plain Thorium); an "Enchanted Thorium Shells/Arrows" recipe requires Draenei-rep pattern (several Arcanite Mechanisms + ~10 Enchanted Thorium Bars); any ammo stack can additionally be enchanter-buffed +3dps for 1 Large Brilliant Shard + 5 Arcane Dust — [[d:hunter#1484387163306066000]] [[d:hunter#1484449687770628156]] [[d:hunter#1484598949560979538]] [[d:hunter#1485357293724762112]] [[d:hunter#1485358374832046221]] [[d:hunter#1485359287738826895]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** Pre-patch, hunters as a class were far behind top parses: ~1.5k dps at full Kara40 gear vs. rogues ~3000 dps — [[d:hunter#1484431525347856474]].
- **[General]** Pre-patch consensus: Survival was the best PvE dps spec ("surv scaling still 2good"); MM was predicted to close the gap via Lock and Load/Aimed Shot burst but not necessarily surpass Survival — [[d:hunter#1484299017566421052]] [[d:hunter#1484297516173234286]] [[d:hunter#1484299512771252294]].
- **[BM]** Pre-patch BM raid DPS was ~200 dps behind average MM (400+ behind top MM) on Ambershire logs; BM tree called "turbobugged since August 2025" with several unfixed bugs for 8 months (Baited Shot proc window disappearing/phantom procs, Baited Shot not proccing off Coordinated Assault crits, Kill Command not always giving 100% crit, CA scaling off melee AP) — [[d:hunter#1484346665354789006]] [[d:hunter#1484252707177889864]] [[d:hunter#1484247030086766775]].

- **[Leveling]** Survival considered solid at any level, "really gets going at 40," melee playstyle — [[d:hunter#1483958844609794239]] (+4 reactions).

- **[General, end of chunk]** Rough consensus emerging: "all 3 specs are very playable," Survival still edges out overall but MM is now close and much more engaging; BM's competitiveness is uncertain pending the Scorpid fix — [[d:hunter#1485189556528611368]] [[d:hunter#1485190165025652736]] [[d:hunter#1485443630654296065]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[MM, live post-patch]** Detailed priority from multiple independent testers converges on: **Aimed Shot on cooldown/proc first priority > Arcane Shot (when buffed by Enchanted Ammunition, or on CD to fish crits) > Steady Shot filler**, with Serpent Sting and Multi-Shot both considered weak/skippable on single target — [[d:hunter#1485050636293902387]] (Sofie: "priority should be aimed shot, arcane shot, steady shot, in that order") [[d:hunter#1484648266242195686]] (Kantrul: "not using serpent sting or multi-shot in single target") [[d:hunter#1485049747596120125]] [[d:hunter#1485387819189932214]] (Kantrul's actual rotation: "steady, aimed and arcane shot").
- **[MM, live]** Arcane Shot's Experimental Ammunition buff (+100% dmg) only applies if cast *after* the triggering Aimed Shot has already **landed** — casting it during the arrow's flight time loses the buff; Multi-Shot and Serpent Sting, by contrast, still gain their bonus if cast while the Aimed Shot is still in flight, since their effects resolve on landing rather than at cast — [[d:hunter#1484610058904928467]] [[d:hunter#1485024955962753145]] [[d:hunter#1485030335602495499]]. Practical result: several players (Sofie, yaboikeenser, nati bati yi) settled on "Steady Shot after Aimed, then Arcane/proc-ability right after Steady" to guarantee the aimed shot has landed — [[d:hunter#1484718640443035658]] [[d:hunter#1485089214046539917]] [[d:hunter#1485090580131352757]].
- **[MM, live]** Serpent Sting's Nature/Poisonous Ammunition proc widely considered not worth using for personal DPS despite the buff (mana cost too high, only ~100 extra spell-power-scaled damage over 15s, "100sp = 100 more dmg over 15 seconds... not enough justification") — but its 240 armor-reduction debuff is a net raid-wide DPS gain even at a personal DPS loss — [[d:hunter#1485050638172950610]] [[d:hunter#1485051121843044404]] [[d:hunter#1485053622550134876]] [[d:hunter#1485389859009859644]].
- **[MM, live]** Multi-Shot generally skipped on single target since it can't reset/proc Lock and Load and its Fire-Ammo explosion is bugged (see Bugs); Arcane Shot preferred over Multi-Shot as filler even without its ammo buff since it hits harder and can crit-reset Aimed Shot — [[d:hunter#1485387517455896597]] [[d:hunter#1485344391706185961]].
- **[MM, live complaint]** Rotation widely described as "bloated"/"clunky," requiring tracking 4-5 buttons plus 2 concurrent RNG systems (Lock and Load crit-proc + the 3-way Experimental Ammunition cycle); projectile flight-time desyncs the timing further — [[d:hunter#1484603077678858351]] [[d:hunter#1484603658191638698]] [[d:hunter#1485091689566568621]] (Sofie: standing further from target delays the ammo-proc-visible window, "forces you to go closer to the boss") [[d:hunter#1485092268078665830]].
- **[MM, live]** Auto Shot must still be weaved between casts and clipping it is a real DPS loss; multiple testers reported persistent clipping issues adapting to the new rotation — [[d:hunter#1484614602238066698]] [[d:hunter#1484614715060519063]] [[d:hunter#1485054094434369676]].
- **[MM, community macro helper]** A single "smart" macro that casts Aimed Shot if Lock and Load is up, otherwise the correct buffed spell per active Experimental Ammunition debuff, otherwise Steady Shot, was shared and refined over the chunk — see Macros section.
- **[MM, disputed]** Whether Steady Shot or Aimed Shot is the "main" spell post-patch is disputed: Sofie initially argued Steady Shot remains main since Aimed relies too much on procs — [[d:hunter#1484607125425487872]] — but by end of chunk most testers (Kantrul, Kichimaru, Dresche) treat Aimed-on-cooldown as top priority with Steady as the filler — [[d:hunter#1484981170952474765]] [[d:hunter#1485387819189932214]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[MM, live]** Aimed Shot (piercing) hits everything in a straight line behind the primary target for full damage, and each additional target hit re-procs both Lock and Load and the Experimental Ammunition cycle — but the line-up requirement is strict ("pool table" positioning): targets even slightly off-axis, or 1 yard behind, are missed — [[d:hunter#1484960517842796614]] [[d:hunter#1484829044494172222]] [[d:hunter#1484605004764680303]]. Several testers found this niche inconsistent/underwhelming in real trash pulls despite huge single-hit numbers — [[d:hunter#1485047265394557000]] [[d:hunter#1484812543271764071]] [[d:hunter#1485134563712831658]] (Sofie: "found it way too chaotic to attempt to align targets with piercing aimed shots" during first live MC/BWL raids).
- **[MM, live]** For genuine AoE, Volley (now castable + AP-scaled) is preferred over trying to force Aimed Shot piercing; described as "free +-2k dmg" and reset by the ZG hunter trinket — [[d:hunter#1484723679286923335]] [[d:hunter#1484688430268022955]] [[d:hunter#1485134951522504955]].
- **[MM, live]** Multi-Shot only considered worth using on 3+ targets, and its Fire-Ammo explosion proc is a flat ~50 dmg bug (see Bugs) rather than the stated 20%, further reducing its single-target value — [[d:hunter#1485050636293902387]] (Sofie) [[d:hunter#1485388211885965384]] (Kantrul: "multi-shot only on heavy packs, and even then better off fishing for lock and load").
- **[MM, live]** On K40/Naxx-style content, actual optimal simplified priority for trash found to be **Steady > Aimed > Multi**, with Arcane only weaved in for an Aimed-cooldown-reset or the ammo debuff, since forcing every ammo type on cooldown was a measured DPS loss — [[d:hunter#1485384949455392868]] (Dresche, full raid test writeup).
- **[Two-MM raid comp]** Speculated (untested) that running two MM hunters could maintain better uptime on the Nature-armor debuff and Arcane magic-damage-taken debuff on trash/bosses simultaneously — [[d:hunter#1484582025062125711]] [[d:hunter#1484582794175844493]].
