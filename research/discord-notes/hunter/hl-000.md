## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
(Note: most talent-tree discussion in this chunk predates 1.18.1 and concerns the earlier "CC2"/melee-hunter class-rework patch, ~1.17.x, in Nov 2024-early 2025. Flagged as pre-1.18.1 background/history; only explicitly-dated items are era-tagged below.)
- **[Survival]** Survival was reworked into a melee spec ("CC2") around patch release ~2024-10-25 to 11-01; talents like Mongoose Bite, Carve, and combat-usable traps (Untamed Trapper) became central — [[d:hunter#1301781839450476604]] (Ashtorn, 2024-11-01), [[d:hunter#1321056168696610870]] (Kadash, 2024-12-24, "survival has been converted into the hunter melee spec").
- **[Mongoose Bite]** No longer requires a dodge to use; deals 60% Weapon Damage + flat damage by rank: R1 (lvl16) 60%+25, R2 (lvl30) 60%+45, R3 (lvl44) 60%+75, R4 (lvl58) 60%+115; also resets auto-attack swing timer — [[d:hunter#1305323904524488754]] (KebabAgon, 2024-11-11).
- **[Carve]** Nerfed from 6s CD/60% weapon dmg to 10s CD/50% weapon dmg; widely called overtuned nerf by community — [[d:hunter#1311487128278138952]] (Halgondas, 2024-11-28), disputed as "overdone" with detailed feedback (CD homogenizes Carve with Whirlwind) — [[d:hunter#1308733062078660648]] (Moonshlne, 2024-11-20).
- **[Scorpid Poison]** Buffed: base damage +20%, SP coefficient 0.1/tick, tick interval reduced 2s→1.666s (6 ticks over 10s instead of 5), net ~20% dmg increase — [[d:hunter#1304095901526003733]] (Focus, 2024-11-07); bug-reported for not applying full expected increase — [[d:hunter#1304124594512466003]]/[[d:hunter#1302254320896704552]] (Focus).
- **[Pet scaling]** Devs added SP/AP scaling to pet abilities: Poison Spit (Serpent) 0.25 SP/tick, Lightning Breath (Wind Serpent) 0.5, Scorpid Poison 0.1/tick, Thunderstomp (Gorilla) 0.21 AP, Bite +1 dmg/20 AP, Claw +1 dmg/42 AP — [[d:hunter#1306764069935644672]] (Cosmo_Kid citing dev patch notes, 2024-11-15).
- **[Improved Steady Shot]** Confusion/bug saga: initially worded "25% increased damage," devs clarified they meant "25% increased bonus damage" (unlike wording for Improved Cleave/Barrage), community anger over unclear communication, eventually fixed so it affects full damage not just bonus — [[d:hunter#1308760247556505641]] (Zugolas, 2024-11-20), [[d:hunter#1308750514011504662]] (Moonshlne).
- **[Serpent Sting]** Dev clarification: Serpent Sting applied via trap-triggered mechanisms ignores resistances/immunities by design (talent description to be updated) — [[d:hunter#1319078310403244083]] (Dragunovi, 2024-12-19).
- **[Fox pets]** Foxes moved to their own pet family (no longer "wolf" family) and lost ability to learn Furious Howl; pets tamed/taught before the change kept Howl (grandfathered), causing community fairness complaints — [[d:hunter#1310596997601820722]] (Winterwolf, 2024-11-25), unresolved/repeated — [[d:hunter#1330289079903850576]] (Cinderfella, 2025-01-19; skill removed on respec too).
- **[MM]** Piercing Shots (MM DoT/proc) buffed; overall patch seen as "buffing MM 2x" — [[d:hunter#1318393930672508929]] (Trekoer, 2024-12-17); community view MM became the clearly strongest raid spec post-patch.
- **[1.18 "Nightmares of Ursol" — BM rework]** Bestial Wrath was removed from the BM tree entirely, replaced by a new Row-5 keystone **Kill Command**: "removes any crowd-control effect from your pet, and causes your pet's next 2 abilities within 10s to be guaranteed to critically strike with 50% increased critical strike damage"; 1s GCD (not 1.5s), 5% base mana, 45yd range, 12s CD; dev comment says it differentiates BM from MM and synergizes with pet family abilities and the new Coordinated Assault talent — [[d:hunter#1413526041498091661]] (Kylem quoting patch text, 2025-09-05). Reaction was very split: many mourn the loss of an "iconic" spell and feel BM lost its classic identity/flavor — [[d:hunter#1398129776165916875]] (Rylandar), [[d:hunter#1398161059751399535]] (Petcheese), [[d:hunter#1406144727320891403]] (Advent), [[d:hunter#1407117469868359760]] (Wulfensteinr, all 2025-07/08).
- **[1.18 — Coordinated Assault (new BM passive)]** Procs off Arcane Shot or Steady Shot: pet's next attack within 6s deals bonus physical damage equal to 30% of pet AP (multiplied by pet-family/damage talents and Baited Shot, up to ~47% pet AP), ~3s internal CD; estimated to add ~200-250 dps in BWL-tier gear with good Steady Shot timing — [[d:hunter#1402551870148575304]] (ദenji, 2025-08-06); widely liked ("great, my favorite new thing") — [[d:hunter#1408194032848343201]] (Bobob, 2025-08-22); simplified BM players report it triggers off almost every Steady Shot for ~4% of total damage even without active pet micromanagement — [[d:hunter#1417872152371265576]] (Littlechurch, 2025-09-17). Bug: shares an internal proc conflict with Baited Shot so triggering Coordinated Assault via Arcane Shot can cause a simultaneous Baited Shot proc to be lost — [[d:hunter#1405974413672972459]] (cnas, 2025-08-15).
- **[1.18 — Baited Shot (new BM ability)]** Crit-proc-based pet-buff ability, criticized as poor design vs. Bestial Wrath's guaranteed flavor/burst identity, RNG doesn't fit a ranged rotation well — [[d:hunter#1408194032848343201]] (Bobob), [[d:hunter#1414896934837030973]] (Darkaco, 2025-09-09); shares CD with Arcane Shot causing awkward play — [[d:hunter#1405974413672972459]] (cnas).
- **[1.18 — Survival gets Lacerate]** New melee ability (does more/faster damage from a target's flank per some accounts). Bugs at launch: overwrites Immolation Trap's damage-over-time instead of coexisting — [[d:hunter#1406362566946590771]], [[d:hunter#1406365447015759993]] (Zakiluzza, 2025-08-16); does not stack with Serpent Sting (mutually overwrite) — [[d:hunter#1405935779309555782]] (Goldi), [[d:hunter#1405943577334517790]] (Vladis, 2025-08-15). Reported very low damage post-launch: ~360 dmg + 18 dps DoT at 1600 unbuffed AP in full T2 — [[d:hunter#1407136464285990953]] (natty, 2025-08-19); called "garbage," costs as much mana as Mongoose Bite + Raptor Strike combined for less output than an unbuffed pet — [[d:hunter#1409239181384880169]] (Nergal, 2025-08-25).
- **[1.18 — Survival nerfed overall]** Post-patch testing found Mongoose Bite/Raptor Strike damage ~40% lower across the board and overall dungeon DPS down 30-50% vs pre-patch; MC/BWL bosses are largely immune to Fire (traps) and many are Bleed-immune (Lacerate), compounding the loss for a raid-focused spec — [[d:hunter#1407136464285990953]], [[d:hunter#1407137301599096883]] (natty, 2025-08-19).
- **[1.18 — new pet family abilities]** Every pet family without a unique ability got one on patch day (2025-08-16): Tallstriders — Strider Presence (party regens % health in combat / mana while casting, indefinite); Spiders — Web (root then heavy slow); Hyenas — Packleader (summons a temporary Pack Ally that takes reduced AoE damage, obeys main pet's orders, generates no threat); Bears — Roar of Fortitude (party damage-taken reduction + damage-dealt buff); Crocolisks — Death Roll (heavy slow + rapid damage, more vs low-HP targets); Crabs — Bubble Barrier (damage-absorb shield, AoE Frost damage on break/expiry); Raptors — Savage Rend (bleed, longer duration on crit); existing Cats (Prowl) and Owls (Screech, nerfed slightly this patch) kept theirs — [[d:hunter#1417710203381747802]] (Ionnatra, 2025-09-17; full table also posted by tamimssa [[d:hunter#1407952984398237697]]).
- **[1.18 — new abilities underwhelming/buggy in practice]** ~1 month after launch, several new pet abilities were judged weak: Crab's Bubble Barrier AoE needs multiple mobs already attacking it to matter and does low damage regardless; Crocolisk's Death Roll barely beats auto-attacking and its slow is trivially walked out of; Tallstrider's regen is ~1 HP/5s (negligible) — [[d:hunter#1414695699831980163]] (junabii, 2025-09-08); earlier field-test ranking: Crab "absolute garbage," Hyena "too much CD, no other real damage," Raptor "3rd highest DPS now," Crocolisk "best for PvP if not BM," Tallstrider "BiS for leveling if Survival," Bear "too much CD, not useful," Spiders "good for PvP but not as good as Croco" — [[d:hunter#1407004576703451226]] (Skurridizo, 2025-08-18). Raptor emerged as the standout DPS pet from Savage Rend + high base stats, weak only vs bleed-immune targets (some Naxx bosses) — [[d:hunter#1408663145102442526]] (Valaen, 2025-08-23).
- **[1.18 — Mongoose Bite reworked again]** Now strikes with both weapons when dual-wielding (previously only main-hand); discussed as a real DW buff for Survival even though each hit is still only 60% weapon damage, offset by the very low (~4-4.5s) cooldown — [[d:hunter#1417869577618063452]], [[d:hunter#1417859010505343036]] (Vazdeverence, 2025-09-15/17), [[d:hunter#1412513282279084156]] (Not Normal, 2025-09-02).
- **[1.18 mana]** Patch added rotation complexity (more abilities to weave) without compensating mana efficiency; hunters aren't an Int/Spirit class yet started playing more like a caster; community asked for an Aspect-of-the-Viper-style tool or a scaled-up Efficiency talent, and for BM's Spirit Bond regen component to give mana instead of health — [[d:hunter#1406579540884455525]] (vatie, 2025-08-16).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Raiding]** "BM is the worst hunter raiding spec by far now, MM is the strongest one, survival is good if you have to deal with lots of big packs (dungeons, emerald sanctum)" — [[d:hunter#1304045971033952387]] (Zugolas, 2024-11-07; corroborated widely, e.g. [[d:hunter#1321628933741084692]] Kantrul, [[d:hunter#1330928769267138610]] Bobob).
- **[BM]** "Every point in deep BM is wasted compared to a point in deep MM" — only reason to go BM is the pet-cooldown button; optimized BM build shared: https://talents.turtle-wow.org/hunter?points=FoAAAoAoRCQYBAAoCoQBAoAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA= — [[d:hunter#1330928769267138610]] (Bobob, 2025-01-20).
- **[Survival PvE]** SV does very good AoE/multi-target and decent single-target, but criticized for lacking a stun/interrupt as sole melee spec without one, no raid utility buff (unlike MM's aura), high mana cost, threat issues (110% threshold as melee, FD resisted more often at melee range) — [[d:hunter#1321224824219242629]] (Thraxeus, 2025-01-01, detailed); melee brings "0 utility," worse for limited-melee-spot fights (C'Thun, Four Horsemen, KT) — [[d:hunter#1318265159327682571]] (Zugolas, 2024-12-16).
- **[Survival]** Untamed Trapper (capstone, ~28-35% of SV total damage from traps) is dead weight on fire-immune bosses; repeated, unresolved community complaint that it's bad design — [[d:hunter#1314816167365447781]], [[d:hunter#1314982861216022538]], [[d:hunter#1315752005041524849]] (Maahes, 2024-12-07/09); countered that "not a huge issue... they're fire immune after all," bigger issue is boss hitboxes not triggering traps at all (Ouro, Sapphiron, Thaddius) — [[d:hunter#1315383703429578752]] (Azure, 2024-12-08).
- **[Survival identity]** SV was the vanilla PvP endgame spec; community felt post-rework SV lost PvP identity while other melee (rogue/warrior) got buffed — [[d:hunter#1317960758818246739]] (Meta, 2024-12-16); PvP take: melee hunter has no kick/stun, one defensive on 5-min CD, "suicide bomber with explosive trap" — [[d:hunter#1321626770805362729]] (Oathbreaker, 2024-12-26).
- **[General]** "hunter is easy to play but very hard to master" — basic rotation (auto+multishot+steady) clears all PvE content, but perfect play requires uptime, trinket swaps, pet micro, FD usage, pulls — [[d:hunter#1330522919943536670]] (napkin boi, 2025-01-19; well received).
- **[All specs]** "All 3 specs are decent... Marksman is the best raid DPS, but very few would mind if you came as Beast Master or Survival... Bring the player skill, not the spec" — [[d:hunter#1328266870616494163]] (Arcitec, 2025-01-13).
- **[Raid representation]** In top Naxx speedruns (HKS, Mortality), only 2-3 hunters and all Marksmanship; BM/Survival absent — [[d:hunter#1321727798699626559]] (Thraxeus, 2024-12-26; data point, disputed methodology by responder).
- **[Mid-2025 tier summary, pre-1.18.1]** "BM is easiest to level, but weakest in raids at end game; middling PvP. Survival is the most 'active,' pretty good at leveling and PvP, and a good player can be an absolute nuisance. MM is equivalently strong to Survival at end-game, good PvP, least easy to level" — [[d:hunter#1384206679075197059]] (tabtabtab, 2025-06-16).
- **[Survival vs. MM, pre-1.18.1]** Survival was widely called "strictly superior" to MM in most situations (trash, moving fights, target-switching), possibly excepting fights like Four Horsemen — [[d:hunter#1384898817874464880]] (WyFyL, 2025-06-18); countered in detail: MM has real, situational advantages (no Windfury-slot competition, a real party-wide buff, safer 130%-vs-110% aggro margin, time to FD/trinket-swap before dying to a pulled mob), so SV's dps edge is not "undisputed" outside BiS-geared, ideal-comp raids — [[d:hunter#1384912457490890762]] (Papairoh, 2025-06-18).
- **[Raid utility perception]** Some raid teams view melee hunter (and BM) as replaceable/low-value despite good damage, citing the lack of a Trueshot-Aura-style group contribution — [[d:hunter#1390353007396393083]] (Azure, 2025-07-03); disputed — top guilds reportedly have no issue fitting Survival hunters and their logs show it — [[d:hunter#1390356295541850275]] (napkin boi, 2025-07-03).
- **[BM pet damage share]** With optimal play and a good group comp, a BM hunter's pet can be 40-46% of total BM damage; downside is pets are "dumb" and will stand in bad spots (e.g. getting cleaved/breathed on by Molten Core/Emerald Sanctum mobs) — [[d:hunter#1398074684037402706]] (MonFut, 2025-07-25).
- **[Post-1.18.1, contested]** Within days of each other in the same channel: "survival has been buffed so hard hunters who play range are a joke now" — [[d:hunter#1406307827839926333]] (Bnkyo, 2025-08-16) — vs. "survival is just straight gutted... truly unplayable" in raids due to fire/bleed immunities on MC/BWL bosses — [[d:hunter#1407136464285990953]], [[d:hunter#1407137301599096883]] (natty, 2025-08-19). Anecdotal parse data further down this timeline (mid-Sept) shows a less-geared Survival hunter matching/beating a better-geared MM's damage on the same fight, and much faster gold/rep farming as SV — [[d:hunter#1417869577618063452]] (Signal, 2025-09-17).

## Leveling
- **[BM]** For leveling to 40, take a pet with Screech (best spammable AoE-aggro skill, reduces pet damage taken by debuffing AP) — [[d:hunter#1314941567127126016]] (Lyrazia, 2024-12-07); BM leveling build should avoid Eyes of the Beast, Aspect of the Monkey, Aspect of the Wolf talent investment (little use while leveling); focus pet defensives first then pet damage — [[d:hunter#1306655894892970007]] (TheKohtas, 2024-11-14).
- **[Weapon/race]** Nelf weapon progression example while leveling: vendor bow →lvl11 vendor/AH green→ mid-20s Wetlands raptor bow → early 30s Gnomer gun → mid-30s Crescent Grove bow → ~40 Theramore crossbow (optional questline) → late40s/50s Mara bow → 60 pre-BiS AV Korrak bow or Stormwind Vault quest reward, then Dark Iron Desecrator, then Rhok'delar/Ashjre'thul/Larvae/Nature's Call, then Naxx weapons (Soulstring/Slavemaker) — [[d:hunter#1311230041606062111]] (Boomsky, 2024-11-27).
- **[Survival leveling]** SV can easily AoE-grind 2-3 mobs continuously with a decent weapon: screech pet + AoE trap + Carve + Feign Death; use cheap agi/mana pots — [[d:hunter#1334677302021197865]] (Andrew, 2025-01-31); pre-rework Survival was already considered an underrated leveling spec (Savage-Strikes-buffed Raptor Strike w/ 2H, Deterrence defensive, trap utility) — [[d:hunter#1301992393985232896]] (Diggity Doo, 2024-11-01).
- **[Leveling tip]** Get a tent in STV, stay rested, grind — even at level 40+ can get several levels' worth of rested XP from one tent; melee hunter can AoE-grind rested efficiently after level 40, but mana/reagent costs are high, sell to a pet or grind near a vendor — [[d:hunter#1311033158782681138]], [[d:hunter#1311039000265621564]] (BobberyJimbo, 2024-11-26).
- **[MM leveling]** Steady shot into autoshot spam most efficient single-target rotation once available (~level 27+, full value from level 51 per one account) — [[d:hunter#1310745601498939392]] (Punk, 2024-11-26), [[d:hunter#1321432412625961043]] (Zugolas, 2024-12-25: Aimed Shot "completely worthless" for leveling — too slow/costly vs. fast mob kills; Steady Shot from level 51 was a big DPS boost but requires greater mana pots/spirit scrolls).
- **[Pet leveling]** To teach a pet family skill (e.g. Furious Howl, Grace), stable current pet, tame a temp pet that already knows the skill at required level, use the ability repeatedly until a "learned" chat message appears, then re-swap; distance to old pet doesn't matter — [[d:hunter#1322086154019012619]] (Lundy, 2024-12-27); pet loyalty requires 3 things: happiness (green), time (can be AFK), and kill XP share; ~8 hours of combat time to go loyalty 1→6 by one account — [[d:hunter#1328084283008614570]] (Arcitec, 2025-01-12).
- **[Gold while leveling]** DME Lasher farming as Survival (see Dungeons section) is a common leveling-era gold farm, ~8-14g/8min run — [[d:hunter#1327039234414088263]] (immaculate_capybara_80667, 2025-01-10).
- **[General leveling pattern]** Widely repeated advice: level BM (pet defensives first) to ~40, then branch into MM for Steady Shot; check vendors for bows/guns often since weapon upgrades matter a lot at low level — [[d:hunter#1360510145511030845]] (Cinnamona/Zumatra, 2025-04-12).
- **[Survival leveling, post-CC2/1.18]** Survival is "slow and irritating" from 10-40 but turns a corner hard at 40 once Untamed Trapper (in-combat traps) is available; the +100%-of-agility-as-melee-AP talent also comes online around level 34-39, making SV melee damage competitive — [[d:hunter#1401995396489674832]] (Sisco the Kid, 2025-08-05), [[d:hunter#1373398645025804308]] (Sisco the Kid, 2025-05-18). Downsides while leveling: heavy mana cost from combat traps and very high threat from Explosive Trap (traps scale off melee AP via Untamed Trapper), which is fine solo (enables efficient AoE pulls) but can cause aggro problems in dungeon groups given SV's single defensive and no self-healing — [[d:hunter#1373400274932203591]] (Sisco the Kid, 2025-05-18).
- **[Leveling duo partners]** For a 2-hunter leveling duo, expect to trivialize questing content. For a mixed group: Paladin partner is ideal for Alliance (Blessing of Might benefits the pet, Judgement of Wisdom is a top mana cooldown for a hunter); Shaman partner is ideal for a Horde Survival hunter (Windfury synergizes with melee); avoid Shadow Priest partners since their threat pulls trigger enemies into the hunter's deadzone — [[d:hunter#1401268289321308273]] (Montezul), [[d:hunter#1401270769756930299]] (Zugolas, 2025-08-02).

## Rotation: single target
- **[MM/general]** Base rotation: weave abilities between autoshots without clipping the autoshot cast; priority is Multi-Shot when up, else Steady Shot, both woven autoshot→special→autoshot; add Rapid Fire, Feign Death trinket swaps, Arcane Shot/Serpent Sting in remaining gaps; rotation must adapt to current attack-speed buffs (0 buffs vs Quickshot+Rapid Fire differ) — [[d:hunter#1330644012989808672]] (napkin boi, 2025-01-20; corroborated [[d:hunter#1330610436470608037]] Gees).
- **[BM/MM]** In raids, don't use Aimed Shot at all (limited exceptions); Steady Shot has no cooldown and is main damage filler after every auto shot, even as BM — [[d:hunter#1304045971033952387]] (Zugolas, 2024-11-07).
- **[Survival melee]** Priority for weapon-speed-dependent Raptor Strike (RS, 5s CD) vs Mongoose Bite (MB, 4s CD, resets swing timer): with a slow 2H (~3.0+ speed) MB should be spammed on CD (missing a MB costs a full weapon-dmg+MB hit vs missing RS only costs ~150 dmg); with weapon speed 2.5-2.8s, prioritize RS to avoid resetting swing; formula from Trekoer: if (weaponSpeed × n − 5) ≤ 0.6s for n=1,2,3..., prefer RS over MB, otherwise spam MB — [[d:hunter#1306352203409264802]], [[d:hunter#1318674082954481664]] (Trekoer, 2024-11-14/12-18).
- **[Survival melee]** vatie's DPS-simulator finding: a "balanced" RS-priority rotation with a delay threshold of 2.5s before allowing MB gives ~14% more damage than pure MB-on-CD and ~16% more than pure RS-priority (at 3.56 attack speed) — [[d:hunter#1322247079569129504]] (vatie, 2024-12-27).
- **[Survival melee]** General priority order given repeatedly: Trap (Immolation ST / Explosive AoE) on CD → Raptor Strike → Mongoose Bite (right after auto) → Carve → Wing Clip, filling with auto-attack; "you will be top dps on meter on almost every dungeon boss" — [[d:hunter#1313675145071689728]] (Trekoer, 2024-12-04); similar summarized rotation — [[d:hunter#1321224824219242629]]/[[d:hunter#1326991724974964736]] (Azure, 2025-01-09: time MB right after auto-swing via swing timer addon, start trap DoT ASAP, fill dead air with Trap/Carve/Wing Clip in that priority).
- **[Survival melee, DW vs 2H]** DW makes MB timing less punishing (less clipping penalty), better mana efficiency with 6pc T3, allows double weapon enchants, better use of Quick Strikes/Rapid Fire procs, but requires much more weapon skill investment due to more white-hit damage share; 2H hits harder per swing and needs less hit% for autos (~8% vs 23%, per one unverified claim) — [[d:hunter#1304665158370463845]] (Lundy, 2024-11-09), [[d:hunter#1320121570521382942]] (Azure, 2024-12-22), [[d:hunter#1305705686142947409]] (Thraxeus, 2024-11-12: DW gets more MB/RS uses and higher imp-aspects-haste uptime than 2H).
- **[Melee macro]** Community-shared Lua melee-priority macro logic: cast Raptor Strike if ready, else Mongoose Bite if "safe" (RS CD remaining >4s), else Carve if RS-safe, else Wing Clip if no existing Wing Clip debuff/buff and RS-safe — [[d:hunter#1305158944741920860]] (Trekoer, 2024-11-10; updated with Windfury/Hand of Justice condition [[d:hunter#1313576517007179807]] 2024-12-03).
- **[Attack-speed/haste]** Community testing found increased attack speed did not produce the expected proportional DPS increase in practice, contrary to theory (1% haste should ≈0.9% more auto/steady damage); multiple independent testers (SaggySack, Crulty) reported no statistically clear gain from IAS trinkets/buffs — disputed/unresolved — [[d:hunter#1334726475332452474]], [[d:hunter#1337706557806477337]] (SaggySack, 2025-01-31/02-08), [[d:hunter#1304893005722816625]] (Crulty, 2024-11-09: haste buffs like Quiver/Rapid Fire seem to give less than stated % on paper).
- **[MM, gap-filling under haste]** With Rapid Fire + Quickshot both active, the window before the next autoshot fires shrinks to ~1s (1.5s GCD minus 0.5s autoshot wind-up); best fillers in that window are Steady Shot (115%+57.5 dmg, 1s cast) or Multi-Shot (115%+172.5 dmg, 0.5s cast) — [[d:hunter#1412710924237799475]] (Zugolas, 2025-09-03).
- **[MM example full rotation]** Open with Aimed/Arcane/Serpent Sting depending on target and available time, then Auto→Steady→Auto; weave Multi-Shot after Steady if the weapon is slower, or after Auto if faster; only use Multi-Shot at 2+ mobs if the weapon is very fast; a full-T2 hunter with a decent weapon and a clean rotation reportedly out-damages a T3 hunter with a sloppy one — [[d:hunter#1353385893158977660]] (Dresche, 2025-03-23).
- **[1.18 BM rotation attempts]** Hunter's Mark + pet-in, Kill Command, then Serpent Sting into Baited Shot if available, else Arcane Shot to trigger Coordinated Assault, with Intimidate/Growl for threat management — [[d:hunter#1409127183498215475]] (ᵀᵉˡˡʸ, 2025-08-30); alt approach: Kill Command + Arcane Shot pre-pull (Arcane Shot also triggers the autoshot swing), then standard Auto-Steady weave, treating Baited Shot as a Steady-Shot replacement when it procs rather than reactively clicking it, Multi-Shot only at 3+ targets — [[d:hunter#1408103907112128553]] (Dresche, 2025-08-21); a simplified/lower-effort BM approach skips active Kill Command/Baited Shot micromanagement entirely, dumping points into passive pet damage instead and just running Hunter's Mark+pet-in then Auto/Steady, since Coordinated Assault fires off nearly every Steady Shot anyway (~4% of total damage for one talent point) — [[d:hunter#1417872152371265576]] (Littlechurch, 2025-09-17).
- **[Survival, Vicious Strikes talent math post-1.18]** At typical 2H Survival weapon speeds (3.3-4.0s), Vicious Strikes' Raptor Strike CD reduction gives literally zero extra RS casts (RS still lands roughly every 2nd swing regardless of a 5.0/5.5/6.0s CD); for Mongoose Bite, however, 1 point (CD 4.5s = exactly 3 GCDs) makes MB line up perfectly with a smooth "MB→filler→filler→MB" cycle, while 2 points (CD 4.0s = 2.67 GCDs) yields more casts on paper but breaks that clean rotation flow — [[d:hunter#1417526669299814482]] (Reyka, 2025-09-16).
- **[Survival melee, weapon-speed math]** With a 3.5s weapon and no auto-attack-reset from Mongoose Bite, effective Raptor Strike interval computed at ~7s; a weapon around 2.6s speed theorized as ideal so RS comes off CD just before the 3rd auto swing — [[d:hunter#1350150149430710272]] (Reprobated, 2025-03-14).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit]** Weapon skill reworked ("CC2"): hit cap reduced to 8% (from vanilla 9%/6%), every point of weapon skill gives +0.2% hit; troll's racial +3 bow skill = +0.6% hit; +5 weapon skill obtainable via a level-60 quest for any weapon; Trueaim Gauntlets give +8 ranged weapon skill — [[d:hunter#1309280857516933302]] (Jambon Providentiel, 2024-11-22).
- **[Hit, DW]** Dual-wield hit needs ~20+ hit to avoid missing white attacks (yellow attacks need less, ~8-9); DW hit compensated either by higher off-hand damage or stacking more hit — [[d:hunter#1320901882209898590]] (Trekoer, 2024-12-24).
- **[Weapon damage]** Hunter melee/ranged attacks are not "normalized" — bigger/slower weapons give more effective damage per swing; confirmed by testing — [[d:hunter#1316083545877254164]] (Trekoer, 2024-12-10).
- **[AP math example]** At 2050 AP, 35% crit, 2.3x crit multiplier with Slavemaker+Doomshot: weapon contributes 25.57% of DPS, AP 65.48%, ammo 8.94%; +28 AP → +0.89% total DPS; +1% crit (with 2.3x crit mult) → +0.89% total DPS increase (from 45.5% baseline crit contribution to 46.8%) — [[d:hunter#1306314270878859347]] (Zugolas, 2024-11-13; detailed napkin-math, single source but detailed).
- **[Agility vs attack speed]** Debate/dueling napkin-math on whether 1% attack speed > or < 2 agility for weapon DPS; conclusion flipped between two posts by the same author correcting a formula error — treat as disputed/uncertain — [[d:hunter#1308720930431959111]], [[d:hunter#1308730393981091891]] (DirtyDawg808, 2024-11-20).
- **[Crusader vs Agility, 2H melee]** Napkin math: 25 agi (→27.5 via Lightning Reflexes) ≈ 55 AP ≈ 3.93 dps + ~0.52% crit; Crusader proc (100% uptime) ≈ 100 str → 100 AP ≈ 7.14 dps; concluded Crusader dps edge only offset by ~0.52% crit chance if crit-damage multiplier (via Killer Instinct, +120%) makes crit worth more — inconclusive/rough estimate — [[d:hunter#1312759293979656232]] (Ethaniel, 2024-12-01).
- **[Survival]** Agility strongly favored (2 AP + crit per point); traps scale with AP only (not affected by crit); Str still gives 1:1 AP so isn't dead; crit "still very valuable but not as valuable as for MM" since a big chunk of SV damage (traps) is AP-based not crit-based — [[d:hunter#1320880456287125617]] (Thraxeus, 2024-12-24).
- **[Serpent Sting/Spell Power]** SS scales with spell power and is a meaningful chunk of Survival damage; T2.5 3-piece set bonus gave SP scaling to something (Serpent Sting related) but was reported bugged, working only on first application — [[d:hunter#1310598223315861516]] (Moonshlne, 2024-11-25), [[d:hunter#1331890204876865547]] (Bigsideobeef, 2025-01-23: T2.5 3/5 SP-scaling worth roughly 0.5-0.7% of overall damage in one parse sample).
- **[Volley]** Player found Volley reportedly has a 33% spell coefficient per an external vanilla list; testing a Lesser Wizard Oil (+16 SP) yielded only ~0.8 extra damage/tick — unconfirmed, single source, question left open — [[d:hunter#1326592324339236884]] (Xeonique, 2025-01-08).
- **[Armor penetration]** Community view: AP-pen items don't translate into much damage vs raid-boss armor (thousands of armor) unless stacked with Sunder Armor debuffs; other stats usually better value — [[d:hunter#1336332615472381952]] (bgnome, 2025-02-04); armor-pen enchants/trinkets are only good when target armor is close to the penetration value itself (e.g. ~145) — both near-zero-armor and very-high-armor targets make ArP worse than Agility or other stat options — [[d:hunter#1415661908458999920]] (Dresche, 2025-09-11).
- **[Weapon skill / glancing blows]** 6 weapon skill ≈ 1.2% hit + 18.46% reduced glancing-blow damage (glancing blows occur ~40% of the time on-level content), netting a real ~7.3% overall damage increase — but this only pays off if the weapon type matches the trained skill (e.g. a polearm-skill book only helps if actually using a polearm like Eye of Nerub) — [[d:hunter#1347978384990277725]] (The Mighty Bee, 2025-03-08).
- **[Survival stat conversions]** Stated ratios: 1 Str = 1 melee AP; 30 Agi = 1% crit; 1 Agi = 2 ranged AP baseline; Lightning Reflexes talent doubles the Agi→melee-AP conversion from 1:1 to 2:1 — [[d:hunter#1344911286802186283]] (Shiokster, question, 2025-02-28, uncontested in-thread).
- **[Dual-wield hit/miss]** Multiple sources reconfirm DW needs far more hit investment than 2H: 2H needs only ~8-9% hit to be capped, DW effectively needs up to ~24-26% due to off-hand penalties; one calc showed a fast DW weapon's Superior Striking enchant is worth less than a slow weapon's Fiery Weapon enchant once the ~19% extra DW miss chance is factored in (would need ~1.0 speed to match Fiery Weapon's proc value) — [[d:hunter#1368743148225958018]] (SaggySack, 2025-05-05), [[d:hunter#1357428801641910272]] (Azure, 2025-04-03).
- **[Windfury + pet attack speed simulation]** Monte-Carlo sim (Turtle 1.18 values: 15% WF proc, 6s ICD, 100k trials) found slower pet attack speeds (2.0-2.5s) beat faster ones (1.0-1.9s) for total damage, because the 6s ICD caps proc frequency regardless of speed, so bigger per-hit damage from a slow pet wins out (e.g. 2.3s pet ≈15,025 avg dmg over 120s vs 1.0s pet ≈14,080) — [[d:hunter#1416396978333421652]] (Noctyr, 2025-09-13); explained as pet DPS being server-normalized (slower pets hit proportionally harder), which is exactly why slower pets pair better with Windfury Totem — [[d:hunter#1416397058679640104]] (Thorran, 2025-09-14).
- **[BM Spirit Bond scaling]** Spirit Bond's AP→pet-AP/SP conversion runs through a low fractional coefficient (cited as 0.15, vs. e.g. a spell like Thunderclap's 0.21), meaning hunter AP investment translates poorly into pet damage for BM — [[d:hunter#1410334754787819631]] (oldbummuffin, 2025-08-27).
- **["Dead stats" problem]** Ranged hunters (MM/BM) don't need Stamina (outrange danger + FD available) and Strength doesn't scale their damage, effectively leaving half the normal physical-DPS stat budget (AP/Str/Agi/Crit/Hit/Stam) "dead," making itemization pickier than for other physical DPS — [[d:hunter#1384615596477517925]] (Papairoh, 2025-06-17).
- **[BM stat priority, unresolved]** Talent-reading suggests Agility > AP=Strength > Crit for deep BM, but community anecdotally leans toward the MM-style Agility > Crit > AP=Strength; no hard parse data was shared to settle it — [[d:hunter#1416803493469093989]] (TheRandomControl, 2025-09-14; open question).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Threat]** As melee, hunters face a 110% aggro threshold (vs. 130% for ranged) and no threat-reduction tools; Feign Death is resisted more often at melee range, meaning trinket swaps tied to FD are less reliable — [[d:hunter#1318265159327682571]] (Zugolas, 2024-12-16); FD must be used proactively before pulling aggro since SV hunters lack any flat threat reduction and can die to a boss crit under ~5.5k HP — [[d:hunter#1320880892675096697]] (Thraxeus, 2024-12-24).
- **[BM pet happiness]** A pet that dies and is resurrected returns at low happiness, causing ~20% damage penalty until re-fed/re-happied outside combat; repeatedly requested fix (e.g., Bestial Wrath reviving pet at full happiness) not implemented as of this chunk — [[d:hunter#1323380691852988459]], [[d:hunter#1323443552176439328]], [[d:hunter#1330238316850839676]] (Azure, 2024-12-31/2025-01-18; open/contested design ask).
- **[Feign Death]** Traps require FD to place before combat pre-Untamed Trapper (capstone talent) — earlier changed such that /cast Freezing Trap after 1.17.2 requires an FD-icon workaround; addon trick (Quiver.FdPrepareTrap) shared by Dokka to fix icon swap for pre-combat trap macros — [[d:hunter#1321496680264372286]] (Dokka, 2024-12-25; references bug id 8688, closed by devs without fix).
- **[Pet AI]** Pet ability auto-cast uses a priority system favoring the highest-cost/biggest ability on cooldown, not bar order (matches old vanilla behavior); e.g., Bat auto-casts Bite (10s CD) + Screech together fine, Owl's Claw has no CD so it starves Screech of energy if both auto-cast — [[d:hunter#1328188874228236308]], [[d:hunter#1328234450911363193]] (Arcitec, 2025-01-13).
- **[Master's Bond]** (BM design discussion, proposed/likely a real talent): pet marks target it's attacking with hunter as "Hunted" debuff (10s); hunter ranged damage to marked target explodes the mark into a bleed + pet attack-speed buff, stacking up to 5 — [[d:hunter#1321636568837918772]] (Kantrul, 2024-12-26; described mechanically, unclear if live or proposal).
- **[Kill Command, full 1.18 tooltip]** Row-5 BM keystone (replaces Bestial Wrath): "removes any crowd-control effect from your pet, and causes your pet's next 2 abilities within 10 sec to be guaranteed to critically strike with 50% increased critical strike damage if that ability is capable of striking critically"; GCD reduced to 1s; costs 5% base mana; 45yd range to pet; 12s cooldown — [[d:hunter#1413526041498091661]] (Kylem, quoting patch text, 2025-09-05).
- **[Post-1.18 mana pressure]** More rotation buttons (Kill Command, Baited Shot, Lacerate, Coordinated Assault interactions) increased overall mana usage without a corresponding regen/efficiency buff; suggested fixes floated in-channel include an Aspect-of-the-Viper-style ability or scaling the Efficiency talent up, and converting BM's Spirit Bond self-heal component into mana instead — [[d:hunter#1406579540884455525]] (vatie, 2025-08-16).
- **[Deterrence]** Still valued as an "just in case" defensive even in well-run raids — stacked with Improved Aspect of the Monkey it can reach ~90% avoidance, useful against unpredictable raid mechanics — [[d:hunter#1408483316390428682]] (Sisco the Kid, 2025-08-22); Intimidate (pet stun) and Deterrence both called essentially PvP/solo tools with little raid use on their own — [[d:hunter#1357796238376046703]] (Bubble Train, 2025-04-04).
- **[Pet off-tanking]** A pet can effectively off-tank ~2 targets via a Growl-taunt-swap + Intimidate-stun-swap-back pattern, but beyond 2 targets threat becomes very hard to manage since Screech's threat isn't enough to hold a big pull against melee/caster adds — [[d:hunter#1401631367334203424]] (Montezul, 2025-08-03).

## PvP
- **[General]** Hunters generally lose to Warlocks and Shadow Priests (DoTs ignore kiting) and Mages (easy deadzone), do fairly well vs melee and other casters via kiting; most viable PvP builds go at least 21 into Marksmanship for Scatter Shot to enable Shatter→Feign→Freezing Trap reset loops — [[d:hunter#1308899176649851048]] (Frumie, 2024-11-21).
- **[Survival PvP]** Melee SV lets you drop Freezing/Frost Trap in combat every 15s and hit hard in melee; alternative hybrid: stay ranged, invest only 31 pts in SV, rest in MM for Imp Concussive Shot/Steady Shot/Hawk Eye, keep dropping Freezing Trap — [[d:hunter#1308903013116805234]] (Jambon Providentiel, 2024-11-21).
- **[Survival PvP, weaknesses]** No kick/stun/interrupt, only one defensive (Deterrence?) on a 5-minute CD; melee hunter labeled a "suicide bomber with explosive trap" that dies fast without a healer — [[d:hunter#1321626770805362729]] (Oathbreaker, 2024-12-26); countered that adding more Carve damage wouldn't unbalance PvP since warriors/rogues/paladins still out-damage SV in melee — [[d:hunter#1315800164782575670]] (Zakiluzza, 2024-12-10).
- **[Resisted Hamstring]** Resisting a melee's Hamstring gives a 6s window before they can reapply it (changed from immediate re-try), a kiting-relevant mechanic noted by a mage discussing hunter matchups — [[d:hunter#1323979414525972501]] (Cruelia, 2025-01-01).
- **[Frost Trap]** Frost Trap (39s duration, 15s recharge) + Entrapment talent lets a hunter kill any melee elite (or group) risk-free by kiting around the trap; pre-rework this required resistible Feign Death to place traps mid-combat (1 trap/30s effectively), now easier — [[d:hunter#1314941970204069958]] (Lyrazia, 2024-12-07).
- **[Arena outlook, pre/at-1.18.1]** Concern that once arenas become the primary competitive PvP format, hunters will be outclassed without: Aimed Shot causing a TBC-style silence/interrupt effect, removal of the deadzone, and a revert/buff of the mana-drain nerf applied in patch 1.17.2 — [[d:hunter#1403661175618670695]] (Tyragetto, 2025-08-09).
- **[BM PvP, post-1.18]** Cats favored for BM PvP due to Prowl stealth combined with a new "move pet" command enabling an ambush opener: Prowl (+50% dmg) into a guaranteed-crit (+50% crit dmg via Kill Command) Bite, further amplified by a new 31-point BM talent's instant proc — [[d:hunter#1404566716163555329]] (Focus, 2025-08-12); BM also grants a ~3-second stun via pet Intimidate and hits hard vs. clothies by parking the pet in their face — [[d:hunter#1372782110242246749]] (Krulltak, 2025-05-16).
- **[Racial PvP notes]** Orc, Troll, Tauren, Gnome, Dwarf rated as having strong PvP passives/cooldowns for hunters; High Elf and Night Elf also have good passives; Human, Undead, Goblin bonuses are comparatively less impactful — [[d:hunter#1373574791155548281]] (Lundy, 2025-05-18).
