## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Survival]** Currently considered the strongest DPS spec and best leveling spec on live 1.18.0 build; melee-based, high crit via agility-to-crit conversion talent (~100% agi-derived crit talent referenced) — [[d:hunter#1476906040544071843]], [[d:hunter#1476899900271755284]], [[d:hunter#1478058982642941983]] ("just go surv" advice repeated to multiple askers).
- **[BM]** Considered the easiest/smoothest leveling spec, but weak/uninteresting for raiding; called a "complete mess" for endgame compared to Surv/MM next patch — passive stronger pets, one enrage CD (18s, 90s CD) and a stun (PvP/leveling only) are its only unique tools — [[d:hunter#1476362363920846868]], [[d:hunter#1476345923503128717]], [[d:hunter#1476346193914237010]], [[d:hunter#1476346338416525408]], [[d:hunter#1476346695729156208]].

Post-rework, MM/Coordinated Assault (BM) or hit talent (Survival) both considered strong filler picks for the leftover MM talent points — [[d:hunter#1478015565439307888]].
- **[PvP]** BM and MM both viable for PvP; BM currently strong with Kill Command giving Wind Serpent good burst and BM providing two stuns — [[d:hunter#1477834862122631250]], [[d:hunter#1477837723124629728]].

## Leveling
- **[Race]** High elf or orc recommended for BM/MM (Alliance: high elf edges out night elf once gear/agi scales up, around Tier 3 gear or ~300 agi from gear); Night elf still fine early, has +1% haste (not 2%, corrected) racial. High elf gets +2-3% agi racial. Survival: high elf preferred simply for the agi racial — [[d:hunter#1475591770669584627]], [[d:hunter#1475592575447470211]], [[d:hunter#1477884508136013854]], [[d:hunter#1477885991741161683]], [[d:hunter#1477886065024176188]]. Base agi at level 1: NE 28, HE 25 (+2% agi racial closes gap over time) — [[d:hunter#1477871596017418280]].
- **[BM]** Recommended as easy/smooth leveling spec, with pet AoE-tanking value; general path: deep BM first, then MM after the level-40 capstone, later respec to Survival at endgame if desired — [[d:hunter#1478418340547395718]], [[d:hunter#1475592276460699658]].
- **[Leveling]** "steady shot while leveling" saves drinking downtime significantly ("big time saver") — [[d:hunter#1476280176533966899]].
- **[Leveling]** Respec BM → Survival commonly done around level 45 (example: "lvling as full bm before respeccing into surv at around 45") — [[d:hunter#1476277999216099340]].
- **[Leveling]** Survival strong even leveled 1-60 pure Survival per one player's account (killed the Rhok'delar demon questline bosses solo without issue) — [[d:hunter#1478089943543971840]].
- **[Weapon choice]** For leveling, best-available weapon (highest dmg/agi) usually matters more than 2H vs DW; DW can be marginally better for extra Lacerate procs (though many mobs immune to bleed) — [[d:hunter#1477248930281558142]], [[d:hunter#1477249887807471636]], [[d:hunter#1478140671851495454]], [[d:hunter#1478125957620564029]].
- **[Consumable at level]** Farming/food: lvl 45 food works for lvl 60 pets; lvl 5 food works for pets up to level 20 — [[d:hunter#1475304047803105410]].
- **[Serpent Sting while leveling]** Not recommended while leveling — high mana cost, low damage, inefficient; drop one on bosses in dungeons as an opener only — [[d:hunter#1478329003201921158]], [[d:hunter#1478334984585744547]], [[d:hunter#1478335392234082396]].
- **[Scorpid Sting]** Situational only, mainly to help pet tank elites — not recommended for general leveling — [[d:hunter#1478329003201921158]].
- **[Rhok'delar questline]** Turtle class changes make it much easier: Survival can drop traps in combat, trivializing the quest; classic MM build still works with talent shifts (Aspect of the Wolf's vampirism helps vs. Klinfran); Solenor: don't root him (he needs to fear-kite), 30% cheetah + occasional concussive works; bring mana pots — [[d:hunter#1476652145376886937]], [[d:hunter#1476662946397421588]].
- **[Simone/bow-staff quest]** Keep the dog ice-trapped, melee Simone, wing-clip and run out, viper sting, run back in; occasional fire trap; "Precious" pet no longer deaggroes if trapped-and-fled since a fix — [[d:hunter#1478065451295576125]], [[d:hunter#1478067171971043428]], [[d:hunter#1478067371385291036]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Survival, current 1.18.0]** Priority macro shared: `Immolation Trap(Rank 5) > Lacerate > Carve > Raptor Strike(Rank 8) > Mongoose Bite(Rank 4) > Wing Clip` — [[d:hunter#1476921467542438011]]. Disputed: some question putting Carve before Raptor Strike/Mongoose Bite since Carve is more expensive, does less damage, and doesn't apply Serpent Sting; suggested reason is that Carve doesn't share GCD with Raptor Strike — [[d:hunter#1476925261311246356]], [[d:hunter#1476925405175746665]], [[d:hunter#1476925416534184018]].
- **[Survival macro bug]** Nampower/macro rotation sometimes skips the trap even when off cooldown, casting 3-4 other spells instead; reinstalling nampower did not fully fix it — [[d:hunter#1476926031741845635]], [[d:hunter#1476926654306844682]], [[d:hunter#1476926777917050971]].
- **[MM, current]** Base weave: auto shot - steady shot - arcane shot (filler between autos), with Multi-Shot on cooldown; heavy debate on whether clipping autoshot timers to weave Arcane Shot is a DPS gain or loss (Sofie: clipping >0.5s of auto timer is a clear loss; Kantrul argued for weaving anyway) — [[d:hunter#1478364625040052235]], [[d:hunter#1478373733549998132]], [[d:hunter#1478373897501151252]].
- **[MM, next patch prediction]** Expected weave becomes auto - steady - arcane - auto - steady - auto, replacing steady shots with Arcane Shot/Serpent Sting when procs/ammo buffs are up (enchanted/poisonous ammunition, quick shots/rapid fire); Arcane Shot to be used near-permanently on CD once buffed and able to proc Lock & Load — [[d:hunter#1478363083184279594]], [[d:hunter#1478369549358469241]], [[d:hunter#1478368505706451015]], [[d:hunter#1478369053801185280]].
- **[MM]** Weapon speed matters for weaving: with Rhok'delar (2.9 speed) or similar, swift aspect buff (attack speed ~1.21) makes weaving Arcane Shot without clipping the next auto very hard; slower weapons (BWL crossbow, ES bow at 3.0) allow more weaving room — [[d:hunter#1478365088485343416]], [[d:hunter#1478367904721408140]].
- **[Coordinated Assault opener discussion]** Whether to proc it via Arcane Shot first vs. Raptor Strike on entering melee — situational depending on position/gap-closing — [[d:hunter#1478090314995597466]], [[d:hunter#1478090697994277066]], [[d:hunter#1478091221804126371]].
- **[MM cast mechanics]** Using an ability (e.g., Arcane Shot) delays the next auto shot slightly (~0.05-0.1s) due to GCD interaction — you cannot have autoshot and an instant both "fire at the same time" — [[d:hunter#1478367102825009285]], [[d:hunter#1478367149524520960]].
- **[Attack speed / haste mechanic]** Haste reduces time between autoshots normally (`att spd / (1.0 + haste)`), but for Steady Shot only the 1-second cast portion is scaled by haste — the fixed 0.5s "auto-shot component" of steady shot is NOT affected by haste — [[d:hunter#1477994125315018977]].

- **[MM haste/weave math]** Boomsky's calculation: at a 2.22s swing (~30.6% haste), the "extra shot window" is 1.72s (since the flat 0.5s auto-shot cast is part of the swing timer); Steady Shot at that haste takes `1/1.306+0.5 = 1.26s`; Steady+Multi = 1.76s, already clipping the next auto before accounting for Improved Aspect of the Hawk procs or Rapid Fire — [[d:hunter#1480811290233667696]]. Community consensus (Boomsky, Sofie, Zwift, Bakingsoda): default weave is 1:1 auto→steady→auto→multi(on cd); weaving extra shots (Arcane) is only clean with slower weapons (3.0-3.4 speed) or without a haste proc active — [[d:hunter#1480814713997627415]], [[d:hunter#1480853835785437298]], [[d:hunter#1480869687725260973]]. Disputed by maaadcheddar, who insists deliberately clipping autos to fit extra shots via a haste-stacked build (28% total haste) was his single biggest DPS gain and that he's "consistently on top" in logs; several others (Boomsky, Sofie) argue this is suboptimal and situational at best — [[d:hunter#1480811986559438869]], [[d:hunter#1480817000732164240]], [[d:hunter#1480814054334533848]].
- **[Lock and Load proc math]** Vellasta: firing Steady Shot roughly every 2s at 40% crit → Lock and Load procs on average once per ~10s; at 20% crit, once per ~20s — [[d:hunter#1479393332135465050]], [[d:hunter#1479394639063552010]].
- **[Predicted post-patch priority]** Sofie's guess: 1) Aimed Shot (if Lock and Load up) 2) Serpent Sting/Multi-Shot/Arcane Shot if their ammo buff is up 3) Steady Shot filler — [[d:hunter#1480867419030290442]]. Kalamity's more granular guess: `auto>steady>multi>auto>steady>arcane>auto>steady>auto>steady>arcane>repeat` — [[d:hunter#1481839357575954625]].
- **[Design critique, 1.18.1 MM]** Orb's detailed pre-patch analysis: unclear whether Lock and Load's piercing Aimed Shot needs a literal line-of-fire hitting mob hitboxes (risk of frustrating CC-breaking on MC/Sheep targets, requiring "cancelaura" macros); unclear if ammo buffs persist through rotation or must be manually cancelled to swap; unclear if multi-target Lock and Load pierce-crits can each proc a reset (could enable "classic-warrior-game-bending" AoE Aimed Shot spam if so) — [[d:hunter#1479683944910815353]], echoed with concern later — [[d:hunter#1481170541803999372]], [[d:hunter#1481170877646246020]]. Broader complaint (Orb, Sofie, Thalenar, Boomsky): the new MM design turns Hunter into a static "turret"/caster class, losing the classic mobile ranged-kiting rhythm; Steady Shot's cast-in-place nature is called out as the core culprit — [[d:hunter#1481167036783788097]], [[d:hunter#1481168355703394404]].
- **[Fast weapon weaving]** With a 3.5-speed bow, insert two Steady Shots between autos when no haste procs are up; with Quick Shots up, weave one Arcane Shot after the first Steady in "odd rounds" — `auto - multi/steady - arcane - auto - steady - auto - ...` (Bakingsoda) — [[d:hunter#1483383955683282986]], [[d:hunter#1483387739364659290]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Survival]** Stat priority: Agility (as much as possible) > flat Crit > Haste > Hit (8% if 2H, 13% minimum if dual-wield) — [[d:hunter#1475246044374896640]].
- **[Ranged hit cap]** 8% base ranged hit cap, reduced to 7% with the "305 weapon skill" quest completed — [[d:hunter#1478175832966041633]], [[d:hunter#1478145650775560262]] (weapon skill itself doesn't matter for ranged damage beyond hit%).
- **[Armor Penetration]** Widely called "100% worthless" as a flat-value stat pre-patch since it's a flat amount, not a %, and boss armor values in Turtle are already low post-debuffs (bosses often at 50-150 armor remaining) — makes marginal difference vs. other stats; disputed slightly by Itron ("secretly OP since bosses have so little armor left") — [[d:hunter#1475225515613884546]], [[d:hunter#1475243609019125934]], [[d:hunter#1475247702668214385]], [[d:hunter#1475250788338241556]], [[d:hunter#1475273780023660708]].
- **[Str vs Agi]** "There is not a single scenario in which 1 str is better than 1 agi" for hunters; without Lightning Reflexes talent, 1 agi = 2 AP (both ranged and melee) — with Lightning Reflexes, still 2 AP ranged and melee (i.e., Lightning Reflexes described as a big DPS jump via extra crit conversion, not changing the AP:agi ratio) — [[d:hunter#1475843185803788288]], [[d:hunter#1475850832330096684]], [[d:hunter#1475852095390355476]], [[d:hunter#1475852182392803390]].
- **[Agi vs Haste, Survival]** 8 agi ≈ or better than 1% haste for Survival ("8 agi, no contest") — [[d:hunter#1476801000693039195]], [[d:hunter#1476828157762863185]].
- **[Haste vs AP, general]** 1% haste roughly worth 12-14 AP (context: weaving autos) — [[d:hunter#1475592639628967946]], [[d:hunter#1475592678539268188]].
- **[MM pet damage weighting]** Pet contributes roughly 6-8% of total MM DPS per Sofie's logs (disputed vs. Sheyba's claim of ~20% pet damage share from a specific Naxx log — Sofie pushes back citing normal range 6%, Coordinated Assault ~7-8%) — [[d:hunter#1478225977753469050]], [[d:hunter#1478339231821856930]], [[d:hunter#1478369290666377278]]. Because of this, 5/5 pet damage talent (BM) vs. 2% haste (Survival tree) debated — pet dmg 5/5 argued to be worth ~0.5-0.7% overall for MM vs. roughly comparable or greater value from 2% haste; disputed — [[d:hunter#1478219025891852308]], [[d:hunter#1478225428639514778]], [[d:hunter#1478225977753469050]], [[d:hunter#1478339978974204116]].
- **[Survival pet dmg]** Pet contributes roughly 10-15% of Survival hunter's total damage per Rpecial's testing — [[d:hunter#1477437644941164667]].

- **[Haste value, disputed]** Estimates vary: Thalenar ~20 AP per 1% haste; Vellasta values 1% haste at ~8 agi for MM; Sofie's comparison of bracers (6 agi) vs T3 piece (1% haste) calls them "pretty even"; Bakingsoda/Boomsky insist the true value is complicated and gear/pet-dependent, not a fixed constant — [[d:hunter#1480862456879517818]], [[d:hunter#1480863007277056000]], [[d:hunter#1480861853444866191]], [[d:hunter#1480861901113000107]].

- **[Survival Crit vs Haste, T3+ era]** Once you have 6pc T3, crit is clearly preferred (Raggen); Haste for Survival only benefits auto-attacks, so it's worth roughly 1/3 of a crit-rating point (Vellasta). Worked example (Eye of Nerub trinket choice): 10% haste/11% crit (31.32% total) vs 7% haste/13% crit (33.32% total) — recommendation: take crit — [[d:hunter#1483031941178593281]], [[d:hunter#1483034612103118999]], [[d:hunter#1483029825621987428]], [[d:hunter#1483030269983330304]]. Survival crit soft-cap heuristic (vague, from Sofie): with dungeon/T1 gear, crit worth up to ~35 (rating), rising to ~45-50 with T3+ gear before haste allegedly starts becoming comparatively better again — [[d:hunter#1483120928371118161]].
- **[Coordinated Assault, quantified]** Contributes roughly an additional ~9% of MM's total damage on top of the pet's own ~6% (Sofie's logs: "6% on its own and another 9% with coordinated assault"); for Survival, CA is roughly ~10% of total damage (Kaslux) — [[d:hunter#1478369290666377278]], [[d:hunter#1481120266758127807]]. Because CA is confirmed bugged (scales off the hunter's own melee AP, ~2000+ raid-buffed, rather than the pet's ~1000 AP), fixing the bug is estimated to cut its damage roughly in half — a ~50% nerf for Survival specifically (Boomsky) — [[d:hunter#1481119637109342249]], [[d:hunter#1481120628697469038]], [[d:hunter#1481150040637444126]].
- **[Trueshot Aura, 1.18.1]** Becomes baseline for ALL Hunters at level 40 (any spec): "Increases the attack power of party members within 45 yards by 3%, plus an additional 30" — down-tuned from old spec-locked version; official dev comment (quoted in channel): "Trueshot Aura was a design-limiting ability and an uninteresting capstone... we are making it baseline for all Hunters in a slightly toned-down version" — [[d:hunter#1481179340233248870]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Mana/Focus lore & design]** Long community discussion (largely design speculation, not confirmed dev statement) on why Hunters use mana instead of a Focus resource; references a podcast claim that an original vanilla dev said Focus was planned but never implemented due to time constraints — recorded as a claim, not verified in-chunk — [[d:hunter#1475458502439993436]], [[d:hunter#1475458633620783217]]. No focus system confirmed for Turtle; discussion is purely hypothetical/wishlist — [[d:hunter#1475476518288494685]] ("giving hunters focus is way too far gone from vanilla anyway imo").
- **[Feign Death mechanics]** FD resist chance scales with mob level difference: ~20-30% resist chance at 2 levels above player, up to ~50% at 3+ levels above (one player's anecdotal experience, "not math, subject to bias"); distance from mob and line-of-sight (LoS) also affect FD resist chance — if LoS is broken, FD reportedly cannot fail — [[d:hunter#1476933933563252806]], [[d:hunter#1476934047547789535]], [[d:hunter#1476940245046923427]].
- **[Trinket swapping]** Common addons for swapping trinkets mid-fight via Feign Death: Itemrack (has a queue feature) or the dedicated Trinket Menu addon (kept separate from other gear sets); typical pattern: pop trinket-swap ability, FD with pet-recall macro, then swap — [[d:hunter#1476607766326935653]], [[d:hunter#1476615599835840707]], [[d:hunter#1476615911975944255]], [[d:hunter#1476617520717758609]], [[d:hunter#1477940107360342027]].
- **[Improved Aspect of the Wolf]** Leech talent (6% leech) only applies to melee attacks, NOT to trap damage; separate "leech gear" effects DO work on traps — [[d:hunter#1477733786954240234]], [[d:hunter#1477736679069712495]], [[d:hunter#1477736889611063418]].
- **[Traps scaling]** Survival traps scale off Attack Power, but ONLY after taking the relevant talent — otherwise 0% scaling — [[d:hunter#1476784251721617581]], [[d:hunter#1476797985366212640]].
- **[Vampirism / lifesteal]** Traps do not benefit from the Vampirism-granting weapon/gear effects the same way as normal attacks (asked; answer: gear-based vampirism affects only the aspect-talent's melee component, not traps) — see Improved Wolf citation above.
- **[Coordinated Assault]** 3-second internal cooldown; can be tracked via SuperWoW events (`UNIT_CASTEVENT`, watching for the Strike Together spell ID) using a custom addon — [[d:hunter#1477961469206335568]], [[d:hunter#1477973013008748685]], [[d:hunter#1478081336139972689]], [[d:hunter#1478081456881401996]].

- **[Feign Death range]** FD can resist within 36 yards of target; pet must be set to Passive (not just Recalled) or you won't leave combat when the boss/mob keeps attacking the pet — [[d:hunter#1480876483277881397]].
- **[Pet threat, hard limit]** Pets can NEVER out-threat a player character regardless of spec or tools (confirmed repeatedly) — "no matter the pet, it cannot ever hold threat from you" — [[d:hunter#1482123909858984096]], [[d:hunter#1482126003500220510]]. Pets "can't even tank anything useful unless BM," and even then only via short-duration cooldowns (Intimidation, Growl + the pet's 130% aggro threshold vs. normal 110%) — [[d:hunter#1482130613514272991]], [[d:hunter#1482130880620265534]], [[d:hunter#1478847760009531482]].
- **[Trinket-swap addon mechanics, detailed]** TrinketMenu (jrc13245 GitHub fork recommended over older addon-list copies) auto-swaps trinkets on leaving combat via the `PLAYER_REGEN_ENABLED` event once Feign Death lands; a "priority" flag matters only when 2 active trinkets are queued together; a "delay" setting (set to roughly the trinket's buff duration) prevents the addon from swapping out a trinket while its effect is still active — [[d:hunter#1482731895937700062]], [[d:hunter#1482737198125285561]], [[d:hunter#1482834856533819493]]. On fights with strict pet positioning (e.g., Patchwerk facing the raid, Thaddius ledge-kiting), set pet to Passive rather than Recall during the FD/swap so it doesn't get relocated into a bad spot or parried when you stand back up — [[d:hunter#1482734969326534787]], [[d:hunter#1482738559365677137]].
- **[Pet revive math]** Whether to spend ~4s reviving a dead pet mid-fight depends on remaining fight time: e.g., 1000 pet-dps vs 850 no-pet-dps, a 4s cast costs ~3400 damage (850×4), requiring the revived pet to survive and fight for ~23+ seconds to break even, and even then the pet returns at lower happiness/health and loses all buffs — generally not worth reviving with under ~30 seconds left in a fight (Sofie, Vellasta math) — [[d:hunter#1481937651555631114]], [[d:hunter#1481940747220684916]], [[d:hunter#1481941619614814320]], [[d:hunter#1481941762640449638]].

- **[Traps]** Frost Trap nicknamed "flashbang" because it blinds/disorients nearby allies too, not just enemies — running gag about multiple hunters flashbanging the same corner in BWL — [[d:hunter#1477772715493691543]], [[d:hunter#1477772850692624615]].
- **[Traps]** Common complaint: most classes can dispel Frost Trap almost immediately (~89% of the time per one player), reducing its PvP utility — [[d:hunter#1476455162158780466]].
- **[BM vs MM PvP]** Both viable; BM currently favored for burst via Kill Command (esp. w/ Wind Serpent) plus two stuns available in the tree — [[d:hunter#1477834862122631250]], [[d:hunter#1477837723124629728]].

- **[Dead zone complaints]** Extended late-chunk discussion (03-15/16) on the ranged dead zone (roughly 5-35 yard usable range, meaning 0-5 yards has no ranged option): widely disliked; Kantrul reverses an earlier "keep dead zone for flavor" stance and calls outright for its removal, alongside baseline Wyvern Sting and Counterattack — [[d:hunter#1483100297533587568]], [[d:hunter#1483117728037601381]]. Removing/reducing the dead zone in a past patch elsewhere (2.3-era reference) reportedly made Hunter the most dominant PvP class, cited as the reason TWoW devs are unlikely to touch it — [[d:hunter#1482811319374844037]], [[d:hunter#1482828135744536637]].
- **[Range parity complaint]** Hunters lost their historical range advantage over casters: several caster specs (affliction lock, fire mage) reportedly reach ~35-36 yards vs Hunter's 41-yard baseline (only +6 yards with talents), undermining Hunter's kiting identity in PvP — [[d:hunter#1482759387574571231]], [[d:hunter#1482766266816270447]], [[d:hunter#1483108300999495882]].
- **[Frost Trap / Scatter combo]** Confirmed still functional for PvP CC chains, but opponents can trinket out of the trap, and Hunters lack a "Counterattack" ability (removed from the class at some point, unlike Warriors who kept but rarely use theirs) to punish melee closing distance after a trap breaks — [[d:hunter#1483092888383393979]], [[d:hunter#1483098979494400091]], [[d:hunter#1483099098386272396]], [[d:hunter#1483100723280478320]].
- **[Community PvP state summary]** A player (Baek) describes current Hunter PvP as "very difficult," citing spell-return-time/cast dependency and poor armor penetration against heavy-armor targets; expects 1.18.1 to meaningfully improve things except for dead-zone/server-sync problems in arena, which remain "extremely terrible" — [[d:hunter#1483105387728076900]].
- **[Group vs solo PvP]** General consensus: Hunter is strong in group PvP (classic pairing with an Enhancement Shaman for burst) but weak 1v1/solo — called "high skill, low reward" in PvP; casters can cast through melee standing on top of them while Hunters generally cannot fight effectively at point-blank range — [[d:hunter#1483060308925743206]], [[d:hunter#1483106412476498136]], [[d:hunter#1483076572968914995]].

Sofie (self-described as having leveled 4 hunters) insists MM/BM is unambiguously faster than Survival before level 40, calling pre-40 Survival melee talents "insignificant" to damage output, and gives this as her standard advice to all askers — [[d:hunter#1482743043055685662]], [[d:hunter#1482743260911894538]], [[d:hunter#1482747558077136916]]. Thalenar and Dinousek (both citing extensive multi-character leveling experience) directly disagree, calling Survival leveling "distinctly easier" for them and requiring more skill/planning rather than being slower — [[d:hunter#1482746349647822993]], [[d:hunter#1482746448406642861]], [[d:hunter#1481329191441137836]], [[d:hunter#1481331994112557199]]. Both sides are treated as credible, experienced players by the channel.

Optimists (Ionnatra, Thalenar, Orb) believe a petless MM/AoE build could be "miles better than pet MM," especially in cleave/AoE where the pet contributes little relative to the flat ~6% damage bonus — [[d:hunter#1481165223720390698]], [[d:hunter#1481165546912485487]].
