## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[consumables]** Chimera Chops and Squid Eel Skewer buffs were changed to stack with other buffs, and now yield 2 items per craft (official patch note quoted from forum) — [[d:hunter#1494296665740283934]] [[d:hunter#1494296996629057656]] (Zae/Icantkite, 2026-04-16; +reactions).
- **[BM]** Kill Command tooltip/behavior clarified post-patch: KC is a full pet attack using the pet's own (melee) AP and family modifier; Coordinated Assault is a full hunter attack instead (worse for BM, better relatively for survival); a pre-patch tooltip bug on Coordinated Assault was fixed to reflect actual behavior — [[d:hunter#1495205797716562022]] [[d:hunter#1495206931302711522]] [[d:hunter#1495206996704628907]] (limbless/Darkaco, 2026-04-19).
- **[general]** Racial rebalance: Troll racials nerfed (resource cost not removed/CD not reduced despite other nerfs) "to prevent troll warlocks from being OP," seen as overcorrection driven by Alliance complaints — [[d:hunter#1494901530757955635]] [[d:hunter#1494901787545702461]] (Krulltak, 2026-04-18).
- **[Survival]** "Strike Together" talent was nerfed, which is why survival's stat priority no longer favors haste-focused builds as strongly as before — [[d:hunter#1494831344424063198]] (limbless, 2026-04-18).
- **[BM]** Community complaint that current BM rotation/design is "TBC BM rotation" — considered a step down in uniqueness vs other specs' custom kits — [[d:hunter#1496219546829324429]] (Kantrul, 2026-04-21).

- **[DW]** 11% hit suggested as a target for dual wield (question, seemingly accepted as reasonable in context) — [[d:hunter#1494459349131857981]] [[d:hunter#1494465610652975275]] (Bogenjochen/Sofie, 2026-04-17).

- **[Race]** Orc's Blood Fury gives a flat +120 AP; separately Orc's 5% pet damage bonus is argued to outdo every other hunter racial including High Elf's agility bonus, since pet damage is a large fraction of total damage for BM and at least ~10% for Survival — disputed math: 5% of a 10%-of-total pet contribution is only ~0.5% total DPS gain, though still "free" — [[d:hunter#1496670753788002464]] [[d:hunter#1496948956289171549]] [[d:hunter#1496952215489876081]] [[d:hunter#1496952731212976179]] (Krulltak vs Stunfisk, 2026-04-23; disputed).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[BM]** Kill Command can be used with no pet nearby/no pet actively chasing the target: it deals zero damage but still consumes the global cooldown — confirmed bug, official bug report filed (turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=20736) — [[d:hunter#1494305705392345238]] [[d:hunter#1494307598269288591]] (Willynilly/Darkaco, 2026-04-16).
- **[Survival]** Feign Death resist chance is tied to proximity to the target (closer = worse); Improved Feign Death's 2 points are disputed as unreliable in practice despite intent — [[d:hunter#1494689825419165921]] [[d:hunter#1494691528990261279]] (limbless/FEIGN, 2026-04-16; contested/open).
- **[BM]** Rumor that Spirit Bond generates threat — called into question/mocked as "propaganda" by another player; unresolved — [[d:hunter#1494823362076344410]] [[d:hunter#1494825844315525340]] (limbless/Chubby Piglets, 2026-04-18; contested).
- **[pet mgmt]** Pet loyalty: keep happiness "in the green" via feeding; loyalty gain rate is otherwise the same as fighting any mob; a pet that dies at low loyalty typically needs 2-3 feeds to return to green — [[d:hunter#1494769577098215675]] [[d:hunter#1494770085556916354]] (lwif, 2026-04-17).
- **[pet mgmt]** No known way to speed up pet CD-ability learning beyond fighting; a specific spot in south Northwind (border near Stormwind) lets a hunter walk back and forth across a layer boundary to reset a pet's ability cooldown repeatedly for fast learning of long-CD abilities — [[d:hunter#1494778661432197410]] (Ex, 2026-04-17).
- **[BM post-mortem]** Design critique (post-shutdown reflection): Kill Command needed a threat reduction since tanks often refuse to taunt back from the pet; Growl should have been buffed to compensate; pet balance was skewed too far toward high-modifier pets because of KC — [[d:hunter#1495117564597108827]] [[d:hunter#1495119480152523065]] (Darkaco, 2026-04-19).

## PvP
- **[spec choice]** MM favored over BM for PvP by multiple posters (see Spec viability section) for constant ranged damage, survivability, and CC that isn't lost to pet CC — [[d:hunter#1494177202508402711]] [[d:hunter#1494186537129218099]] [[d:hunter#1494187030912045076]] (The J/Moonshow, 2026-04-16).
- **[pets/PvP]** Faster pet attack speed is valued specifically for PvP because it interrupts enemy spellcasting more often (same total DPS as a moderate-speed pet, but more frequent interrupts); a ~1.3 attack-speed dog-family pet ("in a wasteland near a portal") is called out as a PvP pick — [[d:hunter#1494101237761708194]] [[d:hunter#1494666708793950338]] [[d:hunter#1494667747324592178]] (limbless/Chubby Piglets, 2026-04-16).
- **[attack speed]** Faster pet attack speed does not increase overall DPS (pet DPS is normalized) — it's specifically a PvP tool, not a raid DPS tool — [[d:hunter#1494100642107752488]] [[d:hunter#1494735509564559412]] (limbless/Rpecial, 2026-04-16).
- **[pets/PvP]** For Windfury Totem synergy, a slower-attacking pet is preferred so each swing (including the 2 extra Windfury attacks) hits harder — but this must be balanced against wanting max Frenzy uptime — [[d:hunter#1494100858055426148]] [[d:hunter#1494675024832102421]] (limbless, 2026-04-16).

- **[macro]** A mouseover pet-attack macro (`/petattack [@mouseover,harm,nodead] [harm,nodead]`) reportedly stopped working with Cleveroid 2.4 — plain `/petattack` still targets current target correctly, but the `[@mouseover]` conditional does not; suggested fix `/script PetAttack()` as the first macro line did not resolve the mouseover-specific issue — [[d:hunter#1494385603666641137]] [[d:hunter#1494394306490794178]] [[d:hunter#1494401518634401842]] (Fiky/Daenerys, 2026-04-16; unresolved).
- **[macro]** Request for a spammable auto-shot macro using Cleveroid macros addon went unanswered — [[d:hunter#1494694159242494122]] (Søren, 2026-04-17; open question).
- **[UI]** Question about an addon to permanently show a weapon on the character's back went unanswered — [[d:hunter#1494875763336417341]] (Saša, 2026-04-18; open question).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[race stats]** Base level-1 stat comparisons (screenshots): Night Elf Str21/Agi25/Sta22/Int16/Spirit22/Armor52; High Elf Str17/Agi25/Sta19/Int24/Spirit20/Armor52; Tauren Str25/Agi18/Sta23/Int15/Spirit23/Armor38 — [[d:hunter#1494899617404420267]] [[d:hunter#1494852545791922196]] (Krulltak/Sofie, 2026-04-18); Night Elf's 25 base Agility (28 for hunters, +3 class bonus) is highest among "un-added" races, ignoring the newer High Elf/Goblin — [[d:hunter#1494899756655317095]] (Krulltak, 2026-04-18).
- **[race]** At BiS raid gear the Agility gap between races widens well past the level-1 gap: Dwarf 733 vs High Elf 755 agility in one matched-gear sim comparison — [[d:hunter#1494852458147479766]] (Krulltak, 2026-04-18).
- **[race]** High Elf's agility racial considered too strong by some (Krulltak advocates nerfing it to 1%, or removing it), citing Dwarf/Tauren/Orc all being noticeably behind Helf in AP from the racial alone; not a dev priority for a racial rebalance — [[d:hunter#1494849343381835946]] [[d:hunter#1494852604180824277]] [[d:hunter#1494852787689881711]] (Krulltak, 2026-04-18; opinion, disputed by no one directly but called low priority).
- **[race]** Orc: Blood Fury +120 AP flat, plus 5% pet damage bonus said to outdo any other hunter racial for both BM and Survival (pet is ≥10% of Survival's total damage) — see Stats section for the disputed magnitude — [[d:hunter#1496670753788002464]] [[d:hunter#1496948956289171549]] (Krulltak, 2026-04-23).
- **[professions]** Tailoring + Skinning recommended for self-made 20-slot bags while leveling, before switching to a "real" profession — [[d:hunter#1494347298195509419]] (Chubby Piglets, 2026-04-16).
- **[professions]** Mining/Engineering: fun during leveling (gadgets) and BiS lategame; alternative is to drop Mining for Herbalism/Skinning while leveling since ammo can now be crafted via the Survival tree, keeping Engineering regardless — [[d:hunter#1494351453760917514]] [[d:hunter#1494356273880105132]] (IT Mckee/Ushtik, 2026-04-16).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[BM]** Kill Command usable without a pet nearby: deals no damage but consumes the GCD — confirmed bug, official report filed: turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=20736 — [[d:hunter#1494305705392345238]] [[d:hunter#1494307598269288591]] (Willynilly/Darkaco, 2026-04-16).
- **[pets]** Sunscale Screechers reportedly do NOT have Savage Rend rank 2 (tested directly by reporter, unsure of the proper bug-report channel) — [[d:hunter#1494121166472876162]] [[d:hunter#1494121211918160062]] (orkdork, 2026-04-16; single source).
- **[gear]** Facetted Crystal Scope (2% crit to gun/bow) doesn't show in statcompare or visibly on the weapon, despite a dev (Meflor) claiming a fix two weeks earlier — [[d:hunter#1494171586218168371]] [[d:hunter#1494173489198923827]] (Overdaze, 2026-04-16; unresolved).
- **[pets]** Serpent tamed from Zul'Farrak overwrites the hunter's own Serpent Sting on a mob with its own poison-spit debuff — possibly a spell-naming clash; Scorpid Sting is unaffected by the same tamed serpent — [[d:hunter#1494403965780103380]] (Flatliner, 2026-04-16; unconfirmed/unanswered).
- **[weapon proc]** "Iron/Weapon Counterweight" tooltip differs between Turtle WoW and Wowhead/Classic: TWoW's tooltip omits "staff" from the affected weapon list and doesn't display a "Requires level 12" line, while the Wowhead/Classic tooltip includes staff and the level requirement text differs — whether it affects ranged attack speed (when attached to a melee weapon while using ranged attacks) remains an open/possible visual or functional bug — [[d:hunter#1494407205183815922]] [[d:hunter#1494419088544628888]] [[d:hunter#1494459918978257039]] (GolgothaTheGreen/Dinousek, 2026-04-16/17; unresolved).
- **[pets]** Pets intermittently stop auto-using their trained special abilities (Claw/Bite/Savage Rend etc.) for extended periods (reported: "weeks") with no owner-side toggle change; workaround is to toggle autocast off/on or manually press the ability once; the trigger is believed to be related to resetting pet training points and re-teaching skills, but not fully understood — [[d:hunter#1494435104368361534]] [[d:hunter#1494439863271948410]] [[d:hunter#1494443417902649534]] (Bogenjochen/Darkaco/junabii, 2026-04-17; confirmed by multiple independent reports).
- **[general]** A bug exists with a "croc boss" and the Scent of Blood trinket in "BM" content (context/instance unclear from the message) — [[d:hunter#1494295644960194690]] (Icantkite, 2026-04-16; single source, under-specified).
- **[movement]** Aspect of the Cheetah/Pack-type movement speed bonuses do NOT stack with other movement-speed bonuses (only the highest applies), but mount-speed and swimming-speed bonuses (e.g. Aspect of the Shark/Dolphin/Fish) DO stack with movement bonuses — confirmed via forum post — [[d:hunter#1494860666123845662]] (Darkaco citing forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?p=94080, 2026-04-18).
- **[UI]** Floating combat damage numbers frequently fail to display in real time, forcing reliance on the combat log to check pet/ability damage after the fact — [[d:hunter#1494321120973946910]] [[d:hunter#1494440198342447165]] (junabii, 2026-04-16/17).
- **[sim tool]** Vellasta's community sim reportedly does not account for Scorpid poison snapshotting, making Scorpid look artificially bad in short (120s) simulated fights, and the default 120s fight length "spends" ~15s just setting up the poison stack — [[d:hunter#1494802529857568890]] [[d:hunter#1494802758513983560]] (limbless, 2026-04-18).
