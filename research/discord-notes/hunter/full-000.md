## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[general]** Debuff limit raised from 16 to 64 in this patch/era, so multiple hunters' Serpent Sting no longer overwrite each other's raid debuff slots — [[d:hunter#1424107283280957471]] (steez, 2025-10-04; single source, replying to a question about the old 16-debuff worry).
- **[BM]** Bestial Wrath was removed from Turtle's BM tree in this rework; players consider this a big loss, some say "revert it" — [[d:hunter#1425430835292672082]], [[d:hunter#1425431416929521685]], [[d:hunter#1425434215998029924]] (Vellasta/Faustmeyer, 2025-10-08; disputed how it should be fixed).
- **[general]** Turtle-specific: Survival was reworked into a pure melee spec (Raptor Strike/Mongoose Bite, Lacerate), unlike vanilla ranged-hybrid SV — [[d:hunter#1424012974741393408]], [[d:hunter#1425879372002361475]] (Vellasta/Lean, 2025-10-04/09).
- **[MM]** Direct dev statement screenshot (Turtle forum) on Marksmanship, shared mid-chunk: "We believe Marksmanship is in a good spot. We are considering taking another look at the design of Trueshot Aura and possibly at Volley in a future update but for now we don't believe the specialization needs any change." — this directly contradicts the loud community consensus that MM needs a buff — [[d:hunter#1432708217170825266]] (Vellasta, 2025-10-28; image; see also Contested).
- **[BM]** Kill Command → Baited Shot cooldown-reset interaction is bugged/inconsistent; some report it works, some don't; devs acknowledged but slow; a ticket reportedly got it fixed "next day" for one player — [[d:hunter#1424372457610940466]], [[d:hunter#1424373168390410424]], [[d:hunter#1424375527455723612]], [[d:hunter#1425421980697624667]] (Kekkonen/Fenis/Faustmeyer, 2025-10-05/08; disputed/unresolved).
- **[general]** Coordinated Assault (BM capstone) crit with Kill Command is buggy and doesn't reliably proc Baited Shot despite patch notes claiming a fix — [[d:hunter#1425421980697624667]] (Faustmeyer, 2025-10-08).
- **[general]** Hit cap is normalized server-wide: 8% for ranged/2h weapons, 16% for dual-wield and casters (not 26% as some wikis claim) — [[d:hunter#1425745259954180231]], [[d:hunter#1425882706218192977]] (Duciel/Lean, 2025-10-09; Lean corrects Duciel's initial "26%" claim, Duciel plans in-game testing).
- **[general]** Turtle uses an older (1.12-era) client without some modern macro conditionals, motivating the community's separate Unreal client project; Turtle wiki has macro list adapted for this — [[d:hunter#1423707121580769370]] (gluttyslow, 2025-10-03).
- **[SV/MM]** Scorpid Poison scales off AP (not SP); scaling documented in the 1.18 patch notes on the Turtle forums — [[d:hunter#1424006758409568348]], [[d:hunter#1424006992300736663]] (Dixa, 2025-10-04).
- **[general]** New custom pet-family abilities added; a "Twow All Beasts" pinned spreadsheet documents pet changes for this patch — [[d:hunter#1424245517293977681]], [[d:hunter#1425250556154351646]] (Valaen, 2025-10-04/08).
- **[general]** Posted patch-note excerpt on pet ability AP/SP scaling additions: Screech (Carrion Bird/Owl) now scales with pet AP, +1 dmg per 25 AP, 5s cooldown; Furious Howl (Wolf) scales with pet AP, +1 physical dmg per 25 AP on next melee hit; Charge (Boar) grants the boar bonus AP on its next attack equal to 35% of its AP; Poison Spit (Serpent) +0.25 SP per periodic tick; Lightning Breath (Wind Serpent) +0.5 SP; Scorpid Poison +0.1 SP per tick; Thunderstomp (Gorilla) +0.21 SP; Bite +1 dmg per 20 AP; Claw +1 dmg per 42 AP — [[d:hunter#1428339626451406932]], [[d:hunter#1428339182006173876]] (Zae, 2025-10-16).

- **[general]** Screech was deliberately nerfed by devs to bring it in line with other (non-viable) AoE pet-threat tools rather than buffing those up — "their reasoning was that it was better than the other aoe pet threat options... so now none of them are viable" — [[d:hunter#1429207864634904596]], [[d:hunter#1429208605197860935]] (junabii, 2025-10-19). Screech was later reworked with a 5s cooldown and AP-scaling; still considered the best pet for holding AoE/single-target threat, just not as dominant as before — [[d:hunter#1433366457902829669]], [[d:hunter#1433367001396416562]] (Vellasta, 2025-10-30).
- **[general]** Windfury visually procs on ranged (Coordinated Assault-triggered) attacks with the swirl effect and buff icon, but it is purely cosmetic — it does not grant any extra ranged shots — [[d:hunter#1432667068158836837]], [[d:hunter#1432669633403420683]], [[d:hunter#1432670190298071232]] (_kebabtallrik_/Nutisis/Ela, 2025-10-28).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[SV]** Survival is considered the strongest hunter spec in current PvE, especially AoE/trash (insane cleave/AoE via Carve, high uptime rotation) and competitive single-target — [[d:hunter#1424222489466372137]], [[d:hunter#1424264619727392908]], [[d:hunter#1424225683697303563]], [[d:hunter#1425953600034701503]] (Krulltak/Vellasta/Lean, 2025-10-05; "SV beat MM by 200+ dps on all bosses in ZG" per Lean).
- **[MM]** Marksmanship is considered weak/underwhelming in the current patch — low-impact Trueshot Aura, Aimed Shot considered "a meme", 20-60% behind top classes at high gear per parses — [[d:hunter#1424932546252242975]], [[d:hunter#1425271175189368933]], [[d:hunter#1425640072694206535]], [[d:hunter#1425665558338207856]], [[d:hunter#1426143857522774198]] (tabtabtab/Zent/Elmgren, 2025-10-07/08; broad consensus, disputed only in degree).
- **[BM]** Beast Mastery viewed as broken/unfinished: bugged Kill Command/Baited Shot interactions, unviable in raids, "meme spec"; almost nobody plays it in raids ("no beastmasters in the 60s range") — [[d:hunter#1424907113683091517]], [[d:hunter#1424907184151597258]], [[d:hunter#1424932546252242975]], [[d:hunter#1424975853032443904]], [[d:hunter#1426143857522774198]] (Don/tabtabtab/Ben, 2025-10-07/13; single strong consensus, some nuance that it's fine as a leveling/solo spec).
- **[general]** Overall hunter is seen by several players as underpowered relative to other classes in raids, doing meaningfully lower parsed DPS than the "top" classes and providing comparatively little unique raid utility (Trueshot Aura considered weak reward) — [[d:hunter#1425268765624631296]], [[d:hunter#1425640072694206535]], [[d:hunter#1425756882361909248]] (Zent/tabtabtab, 2025-10-08/09; disputed by Sisco the Kid who argues class asymmetry is expected, but data-point posters maintain hunter underperforms).
- **[general]** In group PvP/1v1, hunter is seen as losing to nearly every melee class (shaman, warrior, rogue, paladin) and to casters (mage deadzone, lock/priest dots outdamage burst heal); only matchup felt "winnable" is vs. another hunter — [[d:hunter#1425274763416174624]], [[d:hunter#1425287154505875497]] (Zent, 2025-10-08; single detailed account, some agreement from Boomsky/Krulltak on specific matchups).
- **[BM]** For leveling, BM is recommended as the beginner-friendly spec — [[d:hunter#1424988413806772245]], [[d:hunter#1424993170806145068]] (Valaen, 2025-10-07).
- **[SV]** For raiding, SV is preferred over MM even on fire-immune bosses (MC/Ony) by experienced players; unless MM player execution is off, SV wins — [[d:hunter#1425531365021192212]], [[d:hunter#1425537704095121488]], [[d:hunter#1425543786678718634]], [[d:hunter#1425545780718080093]] (Jhinzuo/Vellasta/tabtabtab, 2025-10-08; SV double-dips melee buffs like Windfury/Battle Shout, str/agi).
- **[SV/MM/BM]** Later, more detailed raid-parse consensus (late Oct): Survival is "~10x stronger" than ranged even in MC/Ony without fire-trap/bleed access; BM is decent but consistently just under Survival's DPS; MM is "still superior to BM" and a well-played, well-geared MM/SV hunter typically lands top-5-to-top-10 raid DPS in Krulltak's guild — [[d:hunter#1434219271436632348]], [[d:hunter#1434219349358416005]], [[d:hunter#1434606262313811979]], [[d:hunter#1434606463237492797]] (Thalenar/Krulltak, 2025-11-01/02).
- **[MM]** Counter-data point: one MM hunter (Zwift, Ambershire) reports consistently landing top-1 raid DPS on some MC bosses (Ragnaros) at 800-1100 dps, and ranking ~34th-35th server-wide overall — offered as evidence MM isn't universally bottom-tier if itemized/played well — [[d:hunter#1434469829666144266]], [[d:hunter#1434472230066196520]], [[d:hunter#1434835687860994069]] (Zwift, 2025-11-02; single/contested data point vs. broader "MM is weak" consensus).
- **[general]** Personal pet-damage share as % of total hunter DPS scales down with gear: fresh level 60 with weak gear ≈15-20% of damage from pet; drops to ≈10% at Naxx-tier gear — [[d:hunter#1427145509235326996]], [[d:hunter#1434607032576770209]] (Vellasta/Krulltak, 2025-10-13/11-02).
- **[MM]** Community view on hunter's raid "identity": MM is mainly valued for utility (pulling packs at the right time, Tranquilizing Shot, being the designated eye-soaker/off-tank on specific K40 mechanics) rather than for topping meters; some argue this pigeonholes hunter as a "buff bot"/puller with sub-par personal DPS compared to other pure-DPS classes — [[d:hunter#1430115140665086033]], [[d:hunter#1430127149481725972]], [[d:hunter#1430128499712065647]] (Ambroz/Vellasta/Destroses, 2025-10-21).
- **[general]** Design debate (long thread, 2025-11-01): Dresche argues personal-max-DPS choice should "always be Survival, even in MC," and plays MM/BM only situationally (K40 last-boss eye-baiter, Rupturan 2nd threat tank); Thalenar and others push back that ranged hunter is a valid, fun playstyle and shouldn't be forced into melee; broad agreement that ranged hunter needs buffs but "shouldn't scale too much" since it plays at range away from mechanics — [[d:hunter#1434225169207001118]], [[d:hunter#1434227447670509708]], [[d:hunter#1434231522017284216]], [[d:hunter#1434233129186496693]] (Dresche/Thalenar/Dixa, 2025-11-01/02).

## Leveling
- **[general]** Recommended leveling pets: bear or boar early (tanky, can "eat everything"), though neither is meta at endgame; boar until raptor w/ Savage Rend is available — [[d:hunter#1423745087497830492]], [[d:hunter#1423774717399269377]] (SilverWF/Kekkonen, 2025-10-03/04).
- **[general]** Cats are viewed as a strong pick from low level all the way to 60 ("always good") since they don't need replacing — [[d:hunter#1424200412050882652]] (Magala, 2025-10-05).
- **[general]** Pet leveling mechanic: you don't need to replace your main pet for higher-rank abilities — stable your main pet, temporarily tame a beast that already knows the ability rank you want, use it in combat until you "learn" the ability (chat message), then teach it via Beast Training menu to your real pet — [[d:hunter#1424076440609226985]], [[d:hunter#1424212015630520483]], [[d:hunter#1424358952509898783]] (Valaen, 2025-10-04, repeated many times/confirmed).
- **[general]** Pet happiness/loyalty mechanics: feeding is like a HoT for happiness — give one food item at a time, wait for it to finish before feeding again, keep at green, let drop to yellow before re-feeding; overfeeding wastes food; loyalty only builds while happiness stays maxed over time, not from food itself; ~6 hours game time to reach max loyalty, then feed only once/hour — [[d:hunter#1424175273603108866]], [[d:hunter#1425289896830046301]], [[d:hunter#1425290040262656090]] (Nine/Krulltak, 2025-10-05/08).
- **[general]** Different foods (even sub-types like "chunk of boar meat" vs "vulture meat") give wildly different happiness-duration ("fuel") even at similar tiers, tracked via combat log — [[d:hunter#1424892308175847506]], [[d:hunter#1424901803165749359]] (Reiker/SilverWF, 2025-10-07).
- **[SV leveling]** DME (Dire Maul East) "Lasher farm" solo grind recommended for fast leveling through the 50s: needs a wolf pet (to transfer aggro via Wolf Howl when recalling pet), full traps talent build + Pathfinding (BM) + Improved Eyes of the Beast (to scout/pull the room); ~1 level per 4 runs; explosive trap rank 3 unlocks at level 54 — [[d:hunter#1424403777435467928]], [[d:hunter#1424405844849594378]], [[d:hunter#1424406014140088402]], [[d:hunter#1424406352851374203]], [[d:hunter#1424407377771237416]], [[d:hunter#1424408814630731817]] (Elmgren, 2025-10-05).
- **[general]** Dual wield unlock level for hunters on Turtle confirmed to be later than rogue/warrior (who get it at 10); community asked why but no dev answer given in-chunk — [[d:hunter#1424229203724861470]] (Zekeal, 2025-10-05; unanswered, see Contested).
- **[general]** Pet quest for Humans starts in Goldshire — [[d:hunter#1425651439182675978]] (Valaen, 2025-10-08).
- **[general]** Weapon skill progression for melee SV leveling to 60: getting to 315 weapon skill needs later gear (Ouro/AQ20 gloves, Edgemaster's Handguards ~300g); a +5 quest reward and +4 Tempest sword only get to 309; without AQ access, next option is AQ20 gloves — [[d:hunter#1426173164580376667]], [[d:hunter#1426176634846842900]], [[d:hunter#1426178534535073924]], [[d:hunter#1426179358791434343]] (Ismir/Dresche/Duciel, 2025-10-10).
- **[general]** Melee leveling is inefficient before level ~40: "melee hunter is slow until about level 40 when you can set traps in combat... before that it's just hitting things with a weapon, you press like 4 buttons"; level 40 is Survival's leveling power-spike where combining Explosive Trap + Carve gives "hot AoE action" — [[d:hunter#1427496459015819385]], [[d:hunter#1429125909151088763]], [[d:hunter#1429126023584284802]], [[d:hunter#1429126143629328394]] (Krulltak, 2025-10-14/18).
- **[SV]** Survival leveling reports very high burst once traps unlock: one player at level 41 saw Immolation Trap deal ~1000 total damage for 93 mana, Carve matching Multi Shot's damage, and Explosive Trap giving "insane AoE" — described as feeling far stronger than SoD-era melee survival — [[d:hunter#1432336343434006550]], [[d:hunter#1432347792797335575]] (Ambroz/Thalenar, 2025-10-27).
- **[SV]** Survival leveling is more mana-hungry than BM without a healer in group (no Aspect of the Viper); expect to "be the tank 90% of the time" since your own melee damage exceeds pet damage, and to drink often unless partied with a Paladin/Shaman for mana buffs — [[d:hunter#1431196318478630952]], [[d:hunter#1431195393273888788]] (пиздинг, 2025-10-24).
- **[general]** Hinterlands Eagle (level 35-40 mob) has innate Screech Rank 3 despite Screech 3 normally requiring level 48 to teach — tame it and do NOT respec it right away so it keeps the pre-learned Screech 3 — [[d:hunter#1428475590486524116]] (Valaen, 2025-10-17). A low-level Screech source (Rank 1) is also available via a Horde-only quest chain leading to a Revantusk teleport near Stonetalon eagles: https://database.turtle-wow.org/?quest=80300 — [[d:hunter#1430266312034091069]] (Valaen, 2025-10-20).
- **[general]** Community "canned" race-choice answer (repeated near-verbatim by Vellasta multiple times): race barely matters below the 98-100th percentile; if min-maxing: Horde PvE = Orc/Troll, Horde PvP = Undead/Tauren, Alliance PvE = Night Elf/(Human), Alliance PvP = Night Elf/Gnome/Dwarf — [[d:hunter#1427255235381956661]], [[d:hunter#1430452802379055185]] (Vellasta, 2025-10-13/22).
- **[general]** High Elf hunter racial is repeatedly called a "noob trap": the +3 bow skill doesn't even reach a full 1% hit, and the % Agility bonus is misleading because High Elves start with the lowest base Agility of any race (~8 less than Night Elf) — you'd need 400+ total Agility just to match a Night Elf's base, i.e. the 2% racial only adds 1 point of Agility per 50 you already have — [[d:hunter#1426403180195090495]], [[d:hunter#1427325600980537416]], [[d:hunter#1432084141477990491]] (Valaen/Vellasta/Sisco the Kid, 2025-10-11/28).

- **[SV]** Melee SV rotation stated as a priority list, not conditional macro: Mongoose > Raptor [Strike] > Lacerate — [[d:hunter#1423891717098045542]] (Vellasta, 2025-10-04).

- **[MM]** Steady Shot mechanics clarified: it does not pause the auto-shot timer, but you cannot fire an auto shot while Steady Shot is casting — this is "clipping," which should be avoided; the tooltip's "1s cast time" is misleading because every hunter physical shot (auto, aimed, multi, steady) has a hidden extra 0.5s "shooting time," making Steady's real cast ~1.5s; the 1s portion is reduced by haste, so at high haste the GCD can theoretically become the binding constraint (rarely an issue in practice) — [[d:hunter#1429656197627121767]], [[d:hunter#1429656702709530737]], [[d:hunter#1429657701935218729]] (Vellasta, 2025-10-20).
- **[MM]** Standard MM single-target rotation stated by multiple posters: Auto Shot → Steady Shot → filler (Multi Shot > Arcane Shot > Serpent Sting, by priority) → repeat, always timed to not clip the auto shot — [[d:hunter#1429591286469628056]], [[d:hunter#1432805931414192240]], [[d:hunter#1432806033192910899]] (Kalamity/Zed, 2025-10-20/28).

- **[MM]** Multi Shot is claimed to deal more damage than Steady Shot on its own in MM and casts faster; the theoretical best MM filler loop is Auto → Multi → any instant (Serpent Sting/Arcane Shot) → Auto, but this requires strong gear (Swift Reflexes talent, Quick Shots proc, 2% haste shoulder enchant) and specifically benefits from a slow weapon like the Chromaggus Crossbow — [[d:hunter#1432993843564904519]], [[d:hunter#1432994166153150515]], [[d:hunter#1432994363310608526]] (Lean, 2025-10-29).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general]** Hit cap normalized: 8% for ranged weapons/2H, 16% for dual-wield melee and magic classes (not the higher numbers some wikis/sims suggest) — [[d:hunter#1425745259954180231]], [[d:hunter#1425867198630723635]], [[d:hunter#1425882706218192977]] (Duciel/Lean, 2025-10-09; Lean: "hit chance has so little impact after ~12-13% that almost any other stat is more effective").
- **[general]** A ranged weapon-skill "book" purchase/quest reduces required hit by 1% for that weapon type specifically — [[d:hunter#1426219984002220054]], [[d:hunter#1426227336553959464]] (Dresche, 2025-10-10).
- **[general]** Weapon skill above 300 gives 1% reduced miss chance per 5 points (i.e. 315 skill needs only 5% hit from gear instead of 8%), with diminishing returns claimed beyond 315; concrete in-game test: 308 skill + 6% hit gear measured as only 0.4% actual miss chance (player chose to accept that small miss rather than chase the last 1%) — [[d:hunter#1431698706398449804]], [[d:hunter#1431708661914271856]], [[d:hunter#1431907093639598123]] (Duszman/Kalamity/Zwift, 2025-10-25/26).
- **[general]** Warmonger (2H sword, quest reward) gives 3% hit that applies to BOTH ranged and melee attacks, useful as a cheap way to approach the hit cap without spending on hit gear/trinkets — [[d:hunter#1429455327295967452]], [[d:hunter#1429458439066751058]], [[d:hunter#1429462452046921739]] (riodont/Dopemania, 2025-10-19). The only ranged-only hit source noted is the rifle/gun "Scope" item; Duciel recommends always preferring a damage-scope over a hit-scope once 8% hit is reachable from other gear, since "hit is not that hard to get" — [[d:hunter#1429468016298557501]], [[d:hunter#1429468739123941468]], [[d:hunter#1429469097397059677]] (Duciel, 2025-10-19).
- **[general]** Crit value estimates vary by source: rough community math ≈32 effective AP per 1% crit (Krulltak), Duciel's own spreadsheet used ≈24 AP (believes he over-valued it); ranged hunter crit multiplier is roughly 2.3x normal damage (130% bonus) vs. melee/Survival's 2.2x (120% bonus) per Boomsky/Vellasta; since you don't go from 0% crit, the *marginal* value of +1% crit shrinks as your crit rate rises (e.g. ~1% dps gain going 0→1% crit vs ~0.67% going 50→51%) — [[d:hunter#1426382253977763870]], [[d:hunter#1426389989411000391]], [[d:hunter#1426390721807646720]], [[d:hunter#1428675260739354624]] (Krulltak/Boomsky/Duciel, 2025-10-11/17).
- **[SV]** Crit is worth comparatively less to Survival than to MM: Immolation Trap, Serpent Sting, and the Lacerate DoT tick do NOT scale with crit (though Lacerate's initial hit can crit) — roughly 30% of a geared SV hunter's damage doesn't benefit from crit at all, vs. ~100% of MM's non-pet damage; Survival's crit multiplier is also lower (120% vs MM's 130%) — [[d:hunter#1428685173079277679]], [[d:hunter#1428685719597219871]], [[d:hunter#1428692514109522002]], [[d:hunter#1428692941894975560]], [[d:hunter#1428693026867515473]] (Vellasta/Duciel, 2025-10-17, with a Shazzrah parse screenshot as evidence).
- **[general]** Agility-to-AP conversion, accounting for the crit Agility also grants: roughly 2.7 effective AP per Agility (ranged), or ~2.9 with the Survival/BM Agi-scaling talent factored in; a simpler community rule-of-thumb: 6 Agility ≈ 12 AP + ~0.12% crit (a bit more with Blessing of Kings, and slightly more again for Survival's extra scaling) — [[d:hunter#1432056905785933824]], [[d:hunter#1431085437216362597]], [[d:hunter#1431085727982030929]] (Reyka/Lean, 2025-10-24/28). Base rule restated: 1 Agility = 2 ranged AP / 1 melee AP; 1 Strength = 1 AP flat (no bonus multiplier even for Survival) — [[d:hunter#1430242233369759846]] (Vellasta, 2025-10-23).
- **[general]** Pet Agility-to-crit conversion is unknown/unresolved — unclear if a tamed pet's Agility converts to crit using the hunter formula or the warrior formula; Scroll of Agility on a pet is judged not very impactful even in the best case (~1% crit ≈ only ~0.1% overall DPS gain for MM/Survival) — [[d:hunter#1432650984860942438]], [[d:hunter#1432651488194461777]], [[d:hunter#1432651839618420847]], [[d:hunter#1432652615229116496]] (Vellasta, 2025-10-28; open question).
- **[general]** No dedicated Turtle hunter DPS simulator exists as of this chunk; all community stat-weight numbers (MM spreadsheet, Duciel's calc, etc.) are based on manual/napkin-math extrapolation from classic-vanilla simmed values adjusted for Turtle's talent/ability changes, not a true sim — [[d:hunter#1432626286802501652]], [[d:hunter#1432632038841909250]], [[d:hunter#1432632596042354792]] (Dresche/Destroses/Vellasta, 2025-10-28).
- **[SV]** AP from Agility: base is 2 ranged AP / 1 melee AP per point of Agility; Survival gets an extra ~2 AP/agi from a talent, making it ~2.2 AP per Agility total for SV melee — [[d:hunter#1425838786654502913]], [[d:hunter#1425840433103372390]] (Deleted User/Mr. Berold, 2025-10-09).
- **[SV]** Shoulder enchant math (early gear): 8 Agility ≈ 25 AP, 1% Haste ≈ 14 AP-equivalent, so 8 Agi enchant preferred early; expected to shift toward Haste with Naxx/Kara40 (K40) gear — [[d:hunter#1425923164009005187]], [[d:hunter#1425925508322951181]], [[d:hunter#1425927968605081760]], [[d:hunter#1425928063316918412]], [[d:hunter#1425928222826172558]] (Elmgren/Duciel, 2025-10-09).
- **[MM]** Shoulder enchant for MM: naxx-specific enchant is BiS long-term, but 30 AP is recommended over 2% Haste "for now" (pre-Naxx) — [[d:hunter#1425994617106595873]], [[d:hunter#1426010703088582719]] (Kalamity, 2025-10-09).
- **[general]** Weapon skill: going from 309 → 315 skill gives roughly 12% extra damage on glancing blows and 1.2% extra hit chance; glancing blows estimated at ~40% of auto attacks on average — [[d:hunter#1426180243533860956]], [[d:hunter#1426180572002386021]], [[d:hunter#1426180726134669344]] (Duciel, 2025-10-10).
- **[general]** Duciel's personal DPS calc spreadsheet excludes armor penetration, weapon speed effects, extra-hit procs, and weapon skill from its stat weights due to lack of reliable proc-rate data — [[d:hunter#1426186010383093880]], [[d:hunter#1426186589683318804]] (Duciel, 2025-10-10). - **[general]** Concrete miss-rate data point: Duciel's 30-min level-60 dummy test with a 2H weapon, 308/308 weapon skill, and 0% Hit Rating measured a 23.2% miss rate (plus 10.0% glancing, 5.5% dodge, 5.8% crit, 55.5% hit) — illustrates just how punishing being far under the 8% ranged/2H hit cap is — [[d:hunter#1425932719334096906]] (Duciel, 2025-10-10; image).
- **[SV]** Pet raw-damage-output ranking confirmed via a cumulative damage-over-120s chart: Scorpid highest (~21,700 total), then Cobra (~17,300), then Wind Serpent (~15,000), then Cat lowest (~12,800) — assumes the target isn't poison-immune; matches the text-based claim that Scorpid/Cobra outdamage Cat/Raptor when poison lands, with Raptor said to have overtaken Cat specifically after the 1.18 changes — [[d:hunter#1434606056675344597]] (Krulltak, 2025-11-02; image, chart explicitly labeled "pre-1.18").

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[SV]** Melee SV out-damages its own pet so heavily that pet threat isn't really manageable/needed as a concern; "you inevitably do a lot more dmg than your pet in surv, it can't be managed" — [[d:hunter#1424727327924818070]], [[d:hunter#1424723872279826582]] (Pepe/Slaagi, 2025-10-06).
- **[general]** Growl is a high-threat, low-focus ability, not a taunt; pet won't have focus for Bite/Rend simultaneously without Baited Shot; Claw has no cooldown/low focus cost so keeping it on means the other two abilities almost never fire — don't run Bite+Rend together solo if you want Growl threat to hold — [[d:hunter#1423554879141580861]], [[d:hunter#1423555119617933404]], [[d:hunter#1423553995590471690]] (Dixa, 2025-10-03).
- **[general]** Mend Pet: mana-expensive on hunters since pre-raid gear rarely has Int; even with the talent and MC set bonus it barely keeps pace with raid damage on pets — [[d:hunter#1425287502867992658]] (Zent, 2025-10-08).
- **[general]** Feign Death + trinket swap macro: works, but doesn't function reliably if the pet is actively attacking during the FD window (race condition with latency); recall pet first to make the swap consistent; example macro: `/script PetPassiveMode(); /cast Feign Death /equip Hand of Justice` — [[d:hunter#1425102329237213346]], [[d:hunter#1425102521386401905]], [[d:hunter#1425103245293916221]], [[d:hunter#1425904228676796519]] (JohnGood/Vellasta/Elmgren, 2025-10-07/09).
- **[general]** Feign Death does not reliably resist properly at range ("FD doesn't resist if you're far away" per one report) — [[d:hunter#1424676020530843740]] (Elmgren, 2025-10-06; single source).
- **[general]** Serpent Sting still ticking when you Feign Death can cause a mob (e.g. King Gordok in DMT) to re-aggro/charge on the next tick — [[d:hunter#1424676020530843740]], [[d:hunter#1425559400327942337]] (Elmgren/CptGankBawlz, 2025-10-06/08).
- **[general]** Furious Howl (Wolf) has a serious interaction bug with ranged attacks: if the Howl buff triggers while a shot is already in flight, the buff is consumed when that shot lands but its damage bonus is NOT applied — effectively wasting Howl for ranged (MM/BM) hunters almost every time, since a missile is "almost always" in the air; Wolf therefore remains strong for melee Survival in groups but is not recommended for ranged specs — [[d:hunter#1431975871920607364]], [[d:hunter#1431976462998700083]], [[d:hunter#1431976549850157099]], [[d:hunter#1432295110833606696]], [[d:hunter#1432295282045096018]] (Vellasta/Destroses/Dresche, 2025-10-26; someone did prior math showing Wolf is "just barely" worth it over Raptor for MM even counting the group buff, per [[d:hunter#1431977916631093320]]).
- **[general]** Hunter's only defensive "oh-shit" tool is essentially Feign Death (plus Deterrence for melee); hunter lacks a hard interrupt like Counterspell/Kick — the closest options are Intimidation (1-min CD stun, does interrupt), Scatter Shot (disorient, does interrupt), and Improved Concussive Shot (only ~20% chance to stun, unreliable); Survival can also interrupt by landing a Freezing Trap directly on a casting target — [[d:hunter#1433593186936291368]], [[d:hunter#1433593542491770953]], [[d:hunter#1433594855401066496]], [[d:hunter#1433734979845619772]] (Krulltak/Zwift, 2025-10-31).
- **[SV]** Explosive Trap damage scales off melee Attack Power specifically (not ranged AP) per the Survival capstone tooltip, roughly cited as ~10 AP = 1 trap damage — [[d:hunter#1431247680293568592]], [[d:hunter#1431249612005572681]], [[d:hunter#1431251720968863817]] (HB/Destroses/jimb0____, 2025-10-24).
- **[general]** Bear's party buff (damage reduction + 3% party damage bonus) has poor practical uptime: nominally 12s buff / 40s cooldown ≈ 30%, but Duciel's calc puts realistic sustained uptime closer to 10%, meaning the "3% party damage" nets out to roughly 0.3% overall raid DPS gain if used passively (using it on cooldown intentionally helps a bit more) — [[d:hunter#1428030720038666424]], [[d:hunter#1428031151280492654]], [[d:hunter#1428073676263264357]] (Duciel/Lean, 2025-10-15/16).
- **[general]** Party-buff pet comparison: Wolf's Furious Howl (flat physical dmg on next hit, melee-only benefit, bugged for ranged per above) vs. Bear's Roar (dmg reduction + 3% all-damage-type party buff, benefits casters too, poor uptime as above) vs. Tallstrider's aura (20% health regen + 10% mana regen while in combat, passive/always-up) — [[d:hunter#1428022537664860280]], [[d:hunter#1428023307491348662]], [[d:hunter#1428026779175751942]], [[d:hunter#1428027387727315108]] (Deleted User/Demonhun/Destroses, 2025-10-15).
- **[general]** Dismissing a pet (by any means — distance, death, manual, or the "dash reset" trick) resets ALL of its ability cooldowns when re-summoned; deliberately abusing this in raid (e.g. cycling Bear's roar) is generally judged not worth the hassle for the marginal DPS/utility gain versus simply keeping a fixed pet out — [[d:hunter#1426934951810699364]], [[d:hunter#1427441315955871795]], [[d:hunter#1427444530503745659]], [[d:hunter#1427445591507468458]] (Krulltak/Duciel/Boomsky, 2025-10-11/14).

## PvP
- **[SV]** SV performs decently vs. melee classes in PvP; ranged specs do decently vs. non-melee; both struggle vs shaman/paladin — [[d:hunter#1425275899426766930]] (Boomsky, 2025-10-08).
- **[general]** Detailed 1v1 PvP breakdown by class (Zent, single detailed account, extensive but not independently confirmed): Shaman burst (Frost Shock + Stormstrike + Windfury proc) is near-unavoidable lethal; Warrior charge/hamstring/intercept chains you down; Rogue kites you down through stealth openers, cannot out-CC their toolkit; Paladin outruns you (Pursuit of Justice) if in range; Mage blinks in, Frost Nova + kills you in your deadzone, can Polymorph your pet if BM; Warlock/Priest dots plus fear/drain typically kill through Feign/Scatter/Intimidate counters; Druid bear-tanks then shifts cat, or Boomkin dots hit ~20% health per cast; only hunter-vs-hunter felt winnable — [[d:hunter#1425287154505875497]] (Zent, 2025-10-08).
- **[general]** Hunter's deadzone (minimum range where neither melee nor ranged attacks land) and lack of a persistent ranged slow are cited as core PvP weaknesses vs. shaman/warlock slows — [[d:hunter#1425282394247794781]] (Zent, 2025-10-08).
- **[PvP races]** For PvP race choice: Undead (Will/fear immunity), Night Elf (Shadowmeld), Gnome (Escape Artist), Orc (stun resistance) are called out as the generally stronger PvP race picks; Belf (Blood Elf) called "useless"; Goblin racials unclear to the poster — [[d:hunter#1426350773872230551]], [[d:hunter#1426352242033950751]], [[d:hunter#1426352306873438293]] (Kalamity, 2025-10-11).
- **[PvP]** Windfury currently does not affect ranged attacks; multiple players (Vellasta, Krulltak, Duciel) request WF work on ranged weapons (at reduced proc rate), citing PvP relevance (e.g., "let us use sharpening stones on our arrows") — [[d:hunter#1425289048645439609]], [[d:hunter#1425289134444118167]], [[d:hunter#1425372946494656573]] (Krulltak/Vellasta, 2025-10-08; feature request, unimplemented).
- **[PvP]** Turtle's PvP nerfs stacking against hunter CC: Scatter Shot → Freezing Trap chain duration cut from 10s to 6s, trinkets can now break traps/scatter (previously untrinketable), and Diminishing Returns is reportedly implemented incorrectly on Scatter/Freezing, making ranged hunters "way more vulnerable" in PvP than before — [[d:hunter#1429245693913989222]], [[d:hunter#1429245770460303390]], [[d:hunter#1429243730430263447]] (Boomsky, 2025-10-19).
- **[SV PvP]** Melee Survival is described as an "easy target" in group PvP despite doing well 1v1 vs melee via frequent trap access — still recommended to kite/range as much as possible even as SV; SV's real PvP benefit is not being helpless if caught in melee, not raw survivability — [[d:hunter#1430554967990599782]], [[d:hunter#1430606120464023552]], [[d:hunter#1430612195326296095]] (Elmgren/Morenth/Holidae, 2025-10-23).
- **[PvP]** A shaman explicitly notes that any hunter standing still to cast Steady Shot is an easy burst target ("EQ target for me") — cited as evidence of MM's PvP fragility/lack of mobility tools — [[d:hunter#1429360996656615526]] (peter_hammer, 2025-10-19).
