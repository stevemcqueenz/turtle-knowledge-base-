## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Survival/PvP]** Survival hunter strongly beats rogues and warriors due to Immolation Trap, but is weak/mid against spellcasters since Survival has little ranged pressure before casters close distance, and once they're in the "dead zone" Survival has no tools to push them back out unless they misplay — [[d:hunter#1422113602130935921]] (Zakiluzza, 2025-09-29; single source).
- **[BM]** Kill Command's interaction with Thunder Breath and Baited Shot is called "clearly bugged"; players debate best pet choice for a Baited Shot + Kill Command BM build: Scorpid best for sustained DPS but doesn't benefit much from KC since its ability can't crit; Wolf+Howl seen as outdated (pre-1.18) baseline; Cobra pet abilities' crit capability questioned; Raptor's newer ability can crit and may be best but the crit only extends duration by 1s (seen as underwhelming) — [[d:hunter#1423013597423861861]] [[d:hunter#1423014230684340364]] (Ash, 2025-10-01; question unanswered, ties to bug below).

## Leveling
- **[BM/tank]** A level-32 BM hunter with a Gorilla pet successfully tanked Dragonmaw Retreat (Turtle-custom dungeon) for a group with a level-34 feral druid healer (bad gear) and three sub-level-30 DPS, clearing everything including the dragon boss with zero wipes (run ended early only due to a disconnect/LoS issue on the last trash pack) — [[d:hunter#1421042271238750359]] (Vic, 2025-09-26; single source, framed as an experiment, "don't know if I'd recommend this playstyle to people").
- **[pets]** At low character level, hunter pets are described as "essentially mini-players" — pet choice barely matters early on; as the hunter gains gear/skills, pet choice starts to matter more because pets fall off in relative power — [[d:hunter#1422760261755867168]] (Sisco the Kid, 2025-10-01; single source).
- **[pets]** For solo leveling, owls, carrion birds, and bats all have the Screech ability, which generates extra AoE threat (vs. single-target) and is good for holding aggro while solo — [[d:hunter#1422760261755867168]] (Sisco the Kid, 2025-10-01; single source).
- **[pets]** Pet ability learning: a pet only learns an ability by taming a *different* beast that already knows it, using that ability enough to learn it, then teaching it back to your main pet; not all beasts of a family have every skill — check the pinned guide and Petopia for which beasts have the skill you want. One player tamed ~20 striders at level 10-11 without any having "Strider Presence" despite Turtle Database listing it as learned at level 10 — [[d:hunter#1422678922927280190]] [[d:hunter#1422684190406606971]] (Gravemind/Retdiesel, Sisco the Kid, 2025-10-01; confirmed mechanic explanation, single source).

Kara40 — Sanv polearm or Kruul axe, described as interchangeable sidegrades for hunters — [[d:hunter#1421937910466347068]] (Minces, 2025-09-28; single source).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[general]** Serpent Sting is displayed/treated in-game as a "poison" (e.g., Dispel Poison can remove it), but internally does not carry the same database "poison" tag that rogue poisons have — so code changes targeting the poison tag (as applied to rogue poisons) do not affect Serpent Sting even though it behaves like a poison for dispel purposes — [[d:hunter#1420813641506230332]] (Hakuna, 2025-09-25; single source).
- **[BM]** Baited Shot is reported bugged: it should become available after any pet-ability crit, but currently only triggers off physical attack crits (bug report #17221 filed) — [[d:hunter#1423024639118016613]] (Dreaderino, 2025-10-01).
- **[BM]** Scorpid's pet ability reportedly cannot crit, meaning it gains little benefit from Kill Command in a Baited Shot/KC-focused build — [[d:hunter#1423014230684340364]] (Ash, 2025-10-01; single source, phrased as a question/belief, unconfirmed).
- **[pets]** Turtle Database listing for pet ability level-learn (e.g., Strider Presence at level 10) does not guarantee a tamed beast of that species will actually know/teach the ability — confirmed as expected behavior (not all beasts of a family have every skill) rather than a bug — [[d:hunter#1422678922927280190]] [[d:hunter#1422684190406606971]] (Gravemind/Retdiesel, Sisco the Kid, 2025-10-01).
