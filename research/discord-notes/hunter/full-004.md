## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[general]** Bug: Coordinated Assault (CA, a pet-buffing ability triggered off Arcane Shot / Steady Shot / Raptor Strike, not Aimed Shot) scales off the **hunter's** Attack Power instead of the pet's, so high-melee-AP Survival benefits far more than BM from it ("Surv is doing more damage with CA than BM does with BW" — backwards); widely called a bug awaiting fix, expected to be a Survival nerf once fixed — [[d:hunter#1466822820402303108]], [[d:hunter#1466823365439389933]] (+1), [[d:hunter#1468932375026340016]], [[d:hunter#1469339122085658785]]. The CA-triggered extra hit is dealt by the **pet** (confirmed via aggro testing) but its damage is calculated from the hunter's melee AP, not weapon damage (if it were weapon-based, 1h vs 2h would show an obvious gap) — [[d:hunter#1473096533359595611]], [[d:hunter#1473113060599398484]]. Design criticism: it's a lvl 20 ability with almost no combat feedback while leveling, and its damage contribution is large enough that every spec is effectively forced to take it, homogenizing talent choices — [[d:hunter#1473938024915271710]].
- **[MM]** Trueshot Aura becomes baseline for MM next patch — [[d:hunter#1469088860775387360]] (confirmed by Dabcart [[d:hunter#1469088949199835158]]).
- **[MM]** New MM capstone/keystone talent ("Experimental/Elemental Ammunition"): Aimed Shot deals +5% dmg as Fire/Arcane/Nature and grants a cycling 1-min buff (Fire→Arcane→Nature, one active at a time, changes on next Aimed Shot cast rather than after 60s): Fire Ammo makes next Multi-Shot explode for 20% ranged weapon dmg in a 5yd radius; Arcane Ammo makes next Arcane Shot deal +100% dmg and reduce target magic resist (+3% magic dmg taken, 6s); Nature Ammo makes next Serpent Sting deal +100% dmg and apply an armor-reducing poison (4/level, 240 at lvl60, 15s) — verbatim tooltip quoted — [[d:hunter#1469336923666514043]] (Kantrul), timing clarified [[d:hunter#1473776564997980365]] (Elaale). Official (not fan) upcoming talent tree link posted by Destroses: https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/calculator?t0=qPngf49SEV&t1=MS0xmCWYbr&t2=S6Meg6ew-Y — [[d:hunter#1473296527496708237]] (single source, treat as current-at-time-of-chat, may still change before release).

## Leveling
- **[general]** Fastest tested leveling route (Sofie, tried 3-4 times): go MM to start, once Steady Shot unlocks at 20 pick up BM talents, then respec to Survival at level 40 — [[d:hunter#1473497805262815486]].
- **[general]** Spec barely matters while leveling; investing 5 points into an Aspect-line talent early is "always a good start" regardless of chosen spec — [[d:hunter#1468371627854270629]] (Rpecial).
- **[BM]** For BM leveling, wait until the mob is at 75-80% health before opening with ranged shots so the pet can build aggro first; sending pet in and unloading immediately causes threat problems — [[d:hunter#1466911679668424945]] (Thula Wena). Coordinated Assault (learned at 20 alongside Aimed Shot) only triggers off **Arcane Shot, Steady Shot, or Raptor Strike — not Aimed Shot**; leveling BM hunters should proc it with rank-1 Arcane Shot (sustainable on mana) rather than Aimed Shot — [[d:hunter#1474824792220631130]] (Prime), [[d:hunter#1474831874965639188]] (Vulpine). Common practice is to respec for Steady Shot ASAP at level 20 while leveling BM — [[d:hunter#1474832739743039588]] (Holidae).
- **[general]** Leveling weapon choice: always use your current best-available weapon; for Survival there's no fixed DW-vs-2H leveling doctrine (unlike Warrior) — flexible — [[d:hunter#1467154999833399318]], [[d:hunter#1467155885796229385]] (peter_hammer). When choosing between two weapons while leveling, prioritize whichever gives the highest top-end damage/stats; slower weapons hit harder on yellow abilities and reduce autoshot-clip risk, though "slow is not necessarily good at lower levels" since there's less haste to fill the gaps — aim for ~2.7-3.0 speed while leveling — [[d:hunter#1468377824514805780]] (Kalamity), [[d:hunter#1468380271660503050]] (Rpecial), [[d:hunter#1471801080005525504]], [[d:hunter#1471805000534593681]] (Sofie).
- **[general]** Carrion birds (owl/eagle) remain the best leveling pet for AoE threat via Screech — still ~95% uptime even after a +1s CD nerf, and it's the only pet ability with meaningful AoE threat that isn't on a 45s CD — [[d:hunter#1468952204558733516]], [[d:hunter#1468952633757663446]], [[d:hunter#1468952972749832305]] (Itron/Thula Wena/lwif). Common leveling mistake: turning on a pet's "Claw" ability lets it focus-dump so it can't Growl/Screech, causing threat loss — turn off Claw autocast, teach Growl/Screech/Dive or rank-appropriate Bite, and max pet stam/armor — [[d:hunter#1468519397273501737]], [[d:hunter#1468594343551041781]], [[d:hunter#1468824531790467166]] (Unchallenged/Undying). Boar recommended as an easy-to-feed leveling pet since it eats nearly anything — [[d:hunter#1468358454828863620]] (Thula Wena).
- **[general]** Rare/spawn timers: Spirit Fox (Winterspring) and other 2-day-respawn rares reportedly do NOT respawn on server restart — [[d:hunter#1467115828162859193]] (Itron), contra an earlier claim they spawn right after restart — [[d:hunter#1467114423318483027]] (Thula Wena) — contested/unresolved.
- **[general]** Weapon-skill quest: hunters can pay ~250g to buy ranged weapon skill directly, reducing the value of racial ranged-weapon-skill bonuses — [[d:hunter#1468356675227357256]] (Thula Wena). Turtle nerfed the Troll/Human ranged weapon-skill racial from +5 to +3, which is largely wasted since ranged weapons have no glancing-blow benefit from skill (only a small hit% conversion) — [[d:hunter#1468355253505097894]], [[d:hunter#1468355328721555515]], [[d:hunter#1468355833598578851]], [[d:hunter#1468357491401429023]].
- **[Survival]** DME "Lasher" pulls are the best Survival powerlevel farm from level 45+ (once you can enter), with an easier ramp if started at 50+; described as having a learning curve but very consistent once learned — [[d:hunter#1469985707576660130]], [[d:hunter#1469985787042074776]], [[d:hunter#1469986924168216640]] (Vellasta).
- **[general]** Leveling speed anecdote: 1-60 in "3 days if you're on Tel'Abim and know what you're doing" — [[d:hunter#1473749536345165907]] (Sofie, single source/casual estimate).
- **[general]** Pet food: an item's usable food-level is roughly its required-level +10; once a pet outlevels the food's range by more than ~10 levels it stops giving full happiness, so keep the food level within 10 levels of the pet's level — [[d:hunter#1473751131103428739]], [[d:hunter#1473752011261481132]] (Sisco the Kid). Feeding refreshes the "eating" buff duration rather than stacking it — wait for it to finish before re-feeding for max value; the buff still ticks while the pet is passive/not attacking — [[d:hunter#1474996273852583956]] (Skripter), [[d:hunter#1475001472834863227]] (Rangtuk).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general]** Str/Agi/AP conversion (Destroses, precise): STR = 1 AP (melee only — bad for ranged spec, per Rpecial); Agi = 1 AP for ranged, 1 AP for melee normally, doubling to ~2 AP for melee **only** with the Survival Lightning Reflexes talent — [[d:hunter#1473899311916912741]], [[d:hunter#1473923784313540638]]. Precisely quantified elsewhere: without Lightning Reflexes 1 agi = 1 AP; with the talent, 1 agi = 1.1 (agi-equivalent) × 2 = 2.2 AP for melee; losing Lightning Reflexes costs more than 700 AP at Naxx-tier gear — [[d:hunter#1473303463835340901]], [[d:hunter#1473303832703406234]], [[d:hunter#1473305949220634645]] (Chukis).
- **[general]** Crit-to-AP value is contested and gear-dependent: "crit is valued at 30-40 AP for years now, the more AP/gear you have, the more value crit gets" — [[d:hunter#1468098954897129727]], [[d:hunter#1468099607170125824]] (Boomsky); "usually 35 AP for low-geared and 40-45 AP for higher-geared" — [[d:hunter#1472682949529964798]], [[d:hunter#1472683022645067961]] (Sofie) — both saying crit gets more valuable with more gear. Directly disputed by Elaale's calc: for a well-geared 30%-crit hunter, +1% crit ≈ only 20-25 AP, and "the AP equivalent of crit gets LOWER the higher your crit is" (diminishing returns) — [[d:hunter#1472679720838762578]], [[d:hunter#1472685184565641431]]. Boomsky's rebuttal: base AP value itself isn't constant either, and Turtle's different rotations/talents/itemization will shift the exact numbers vs. classic — [[d:hunter#1472693574419218453]]. Sofie notes there's no sim tool on Turtle so no one has *true* math on these stat weights; top hunters reportedly favor haste over crit for longer fights as crit value falls off — [[d:hunter#1472711722237165619]].
- **[DW Survival]** Hit-cap numbers vary widely by source/definition: ~8% yellow-hit / ~23-26% white-hit (no weapon skill) — [[d:hunter#1467801309905158215]] (Destroses), [[d:hunter#1467853912974430293]] (Lean); a full DW soft-cap of 27% hit on white hits vs. bosses — [[d:hunter#1467373076072104149]] (Vellasta); but later in the chunk, "DW is not hit-hungry to be viable," 10-11% is fine at most, with +3% more coming from a DW-specific talent stacking on the existing 3% next patch — [[d:hunter#1472412973795512371]], [[d:hunter#1472413032696119329]], [[d:hunter#1472413106121609378]], [[d:hunter#1472413839617294489]] (Thalenar); ~13% cited elsewhere as a practical DW sweet spot — [[d:hunter#1471825190580129916]] (Rpecial). BM/MM hybrid (Scatter Shot + Intimidation + a Spirit-Bonded pet) is cited as the strongest control-oriented PvP setup — [[d:hunter#1470128511980142696]] (dindunuffinz). Survival is considered weak specifically vs. Paladins, since Freedom clears all of Survival's CC (stings/slows/roots/freeze/traps) — [[d:hunter#1470129243932463337]] (dindunuffinz).
- **[BM]** Current (pre-1.18.1) BM is called "the best BM PvP has ever been" by a longtime player, and is described as fun/strong even though few people know how to play it well since it's a Turtle-specific spec rather than a classic archetype; author claims to lead kill-blows in BGs as BM — [[d:hunter#1470113234332815521]], [[d:hunter#1469783301848830117]], [[d:hunter#1475005407800856604]] (junabii/yaboikeenser). Baited Shot specifically is called out as very strong/fun in current BM PvP, and its planned removal in the rework is unwelcome to PvP players — [[d:hunter#1475005407800856604]], [[d:hunter#1475005507306655805]] (yaboikeenser).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[general]** Quiver is the standard addon for timing Steady Shot around the hidden 0.5s auto-shot cast window; when the timing bar turns red, it's safe to cast Steady/other shots — [[d:hunter#1471408612575285426]], [[d:hunter#1471409619187531880]], [[d:hunter#1471409645141754039]], [[d:hunter#1471455843890237510]] (Sofie/Infertile). HSK ("Hunter Swiss Knife") suggested as an alternative that worked better for at least one user when Quiver's bar looked wrong — [[d:hunter#1472351375961624728]] (Ash). Nampower's spell-queuing feature is known to interfere with/break Quiver's Steady Shot timing macros; can be toggled off per-addon in Nampower's own settings — [[d:hunter#1469980748290457703]], [[d:hunter#1469980930910458086]], [[d:hunter#1469982555330576384]] (Vellasta).
- **[general]** Sample working pet-attack macros: `/script PetAttack(target)` — [[d:hunter#1467182845419061504]] (Rpecial); auto-target + attack: `/script if GetUnitName("target")==nil then TargetNearestEnemy() end` + `/script PetAttack(target)` — [[d:hunter#1467188788597100708]] (Sofie); feign death + trinket swap: `/script PetPassiveMode();` `/cast Feign Death` `/equip Hand of Justice` — [[d:hunter#1468647768565354617]] (Elmgren); feed-pet-from-first-bag-slot: `/run CastSpellByName("Feed Pet")PickupContainerItem(0,1)` — [[d:hunter#1473828964446502922]] (Sofie).
- **[general]** `!`-prefixed macros (e.g. `!Auto Shot`, `!Stealth`) do NOT work on this client with any addon tested — [[d:hunter#1469396307310542911]] (Thula Wena).
- **[general]** Coordinated-Assault-era pet-attack "in range" conditional macro example (RoidMacros/Lua syntax): `/run if IsActionInRange(<slot#>)==1 then PetAttack() end` — [[d:hunter#1471836413283532850]] (Bakingsoda); pet-management guidance: setting pet to Defensive makes it auto-attack the nearest target that attacks the hunter or a party member (not just the hunter), which can cause unwanted pulls in raids if the tank is trying to position a boss alone — recommend Passive + manual send via macro/mouse-wheel-back in those cases — [[d:hunter#1471837809588310138]], [[d:hunter#1471838967711272970]], [[d:hunter#1471839185123147777]], [[d:hunter#1471839443601326242]] (Bakingsoda).
- **[general]** Auto-Shot only engages after a ranged attack is actually used — clicking "Auto Attack" alone defaults to melee; this is a known quirk of the 2006-era client, not something addons fix — [[d:hunter#1474126713330864191]], [[d:hunter#1474126792309739635]], [[d:hunter#1474129043921043730]] (Boomsky).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[general]** Best hunter races per community FAQ (in pins): Night Elf, High Elf, Orc, Troll — [[d:hunter#1471832709163778059]] (Thalenar). NE currently has 3 more base agility than HE on Turtle, and HE's +3 bow-skill racial matters little at endgame since it rarely crosses a hit threshold — [[d:hunter#1468246276612297005]], [[d:hunter#1468246441825800455]] (Vellasta); there is reportedly a crossover point around ~400 agility where HE's agi% racial overtakes NE's flat bonus (uncertain/single source) — [[d:hunter#1468270104323297300]] (Sofie).
- **[general]** Professions: Alchemy is "BiS for making gold"; Engineering is good for raid-utility minmaxing; Leatherworking is currently weak at 60 (mainly the Salt Shaker gold-cooldown item and crafting Devilsaur/Hide-of-the-Wild pieces for others, "no real upgrades" for yourself) — devs have announced plans to make crafting professions more useful in a future update — [[d:hunter#1473024949332742144]] (Sofie), [[d:hunter#1473395396284780544]] (Dresche), [[d:hunter#1473398141155016928]] (Holidae), [[d:hunter#1473364968509542632]] (Elmgren).
- **[general]** Gold farms for Survival: DME Lasher pulls (level 45+, easy to learn, herbs sell well) recommended as a starting farm; ZG solo-kite (bijou) farm is the highest-gph option once you're skilled/geared enough, "definitely don't recommend [it] for new hunters" — [[d:hunter#1470680751149420577]], [[d:hunter#1470681473496383508]], [[d:hunter#1470683604169392179]], [[d:hunter#1470683979970773107]], [[d:hunter#1470684737969455278]] (Vellasta). Mara(udon) farming nets ~50g/hr, RNG-heavy, gear-independent — [[d:hunter#1467309451294806150]] (junabii); contested against a "professions are a scam, no profession earns as much as farming Mara" opinion — [[d:hunter#1467261661227385004]] (junabii) vs. counter-claims of ~100g/day from professions — [[d:hunter#1467290086688428163]] (Rpecial).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[general]** Coordinated Assault scales off hunter melee AP instead of pet AP (see 1.18.1 section) — the single most-discussed hunter bug in this chunk.
- **[BM]** Kill Command's crit doesn't always trigger even though the buff/proc icon shows available; if the triggering attack is dodged or parried, it silently consumes the charge and does nothing — an ongoing, still-unresolved issue as of Feb 2026 — [[d:hunter#1466830588773269725]], [[d:hunter#1466830666326085850]] (Kaslux, original report), reconfirmed independently later in the chunk by two more players — [[d:hunter#1472364005023551499]], [[d:hunter#1472364107205316772]] (Darkaco).
- **[general]** ZG raptors are immune to Freezing Trap on Turtle (a custom/unintended change vs. vanilla), complicating solo-taming/farming strategies that rely on trapping them — [[d:hunter#1471464164341059584]] (Elmgren).
- **[general]** Two erroneous/bugged tames were reportedly never removed by devs: a bear that kept the Crab family's "Bubble Barrier" ability ("Bubble Bear") and a cat with the Death Roll ability, both considered overpowered (especially in PvP) but left in the game — [[d:hunter#1468072729063456991]] (Valaen), [[d:hunter#1468023628124917915]] (Boomsky), [[d:hunter#1468335243839803587]] (IIIIIIII).
- **[general]** Freezing Trap's DR is bugged at 6s instead of the historically-correct 10s from 1.12/Classic 2019, and shares DR with Scatter Shot (see PvP section) — treated as a long-standing unfixed bug, not a deliberate design choice.
- **[general]** Furious Howl (Wolf pet ability) doesn't scale with AP despite the tooltip/intent implying it should — confirmed fix (AP scaling, +1 dmg per 25 AP) coming next patch — [[d:hunter#1470075397352722585]], [[d:hunter#1470075649803812874]] (Elmgren).
- **[general]** Auto Shot does not start just by clicking "Auto Attack" — you must issue an actual ranged attack first — a known quirk of the underlying 2006-era client rather than a Turtle-specific bug — [[d:hunter#1474126713330864191]], [[d:hunter#1474129043921043730]] (Boomsky).
- **[general]** Quiver's Steady Shot cast bar can visually disappear when multiple haste sources stack simultaneously (e.g. Bloodlust + Rapid Fire + Quick Shots, ~90% total haste) — a plausible Quiver rendering/precision limitation at very short cast times rather than an in-game mechanic — first reported [[d:hunter#1469472264465547435]] (Moragil), independently reported again days later — [[d:hunter#1471148434420531251]] (Moragil), [[d:hunter#1471150220577472515]] (lwif).
