## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General PvP tier list]** Community-posted (well-received, +4) PvP tier list: **Z tier** (top): Shaman, Rogue, Mage, Shadow Priest, Paladin. **S tier**: Balance Druid, Smite Priest, Warlock, Melee Hunter. **A tier**: Ret Paladin, Ranged Hunter. **F tier**: Warrior — [[d:pvp-general#1426339548039872532]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-11); a follow-up suggests Holy Priest should also be F tier — [[d:pvp-general#1426343489796440155]] (Liracrime).
- **[Rogue PvP]** Assassination/poison-focused rogue considered heavily overtuned: poison damage is ~50%+ of overall raid/PvP damage output, ignores armor, cannot be resisted; pre-raid rogues do ~1k dps baseline, full BiS/T3 rogues can burst ~3k dmg/second with consumables (as in arena); in a UBRS run, two rogues auto-attacking + Noxious Assault + Dissolvent poison alone (poison = 60% of their damage) were top 1-2 overall dps — [[d:pvp-general#1424859334969397430]] (Pastor), [[d:pvp-general#1424859391198232616]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:pvp-general#1426544263646150737]] (Gnomeburger Orgon), 2025-10-06/11; essentially undisputed. Community realistic ask: "rogues to be nerfed somewhat" and Dampening's damage reduction increased — [[d:pvp-general#1424991330752532572]] (lowlifeopinions).
- **[Rogue PvP]** ~90% of rogue damage comes from poisons, not weapon/AP scaling, so rogues gear for double *fast* weapons (even grey/blue 1.0-1.3 speed daggers) because poison procs off every swing and doesn't scale with AP/weapon skill — faster weapon = more damage; the recommended cheap high-proc-rate weapon is the 1.20-speed fist/dagger from a level-33 quest — [[d:pvp-general#1423694541004734676]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:pvp-general#1426537635207446600]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:pvp-general#1427221835929358376]] (Jesse Potter), 2025-10-03/13.
- **[Rogue PvP]** Dissolvent Poison (level-40 rogue keystone proc) deals physical damage that fully ignores armor (confirmed geared plate/mail players get shredded just as hard as naked targets) and cannot be reduced by Nature Resistance since it's physical, not nature; it can proc twice per hit via Noxious Assault; the only counter is preventing the autoattack itself (stun/disarm/kill the rogue), not resisting or curing poison — [[d:pvp-general#1423408386569011260]] (Eemo), [[d:pvp-general#1424096807268515932]] (Kyrsie), [[d:pvp-general#1423760730213187694]] (Eemo), [[d:pvp-general#1427246080810029127]] (BrewsWillis: even 4k+ armor gets shredded), 2025-10-03/13; as of 2025-10-13 still unfixed ("continue the 600dps shit fiesta").
- **[Rogue PvP]** Suggestion: rogues have stopped using Mind-Numbing/Crippling Poison in favor of spamming Dissolvent; proposal to instead lean rogues into Cloak of Shadows + Shadowstep toolkit rather than raw poison damage — [[d:pvp-general#1424095115890397224]], [[d:pvp-general#1424095578974847069]] (Philalethes, 2025-10-04; single source, unanswered).
- **[Mage PvP]** Arcane Mage burst (Presence of Mind + talent chain + Arcane Missiles channel) is very strong; unlike classic, the channel is fast enough that even brief LoS exposure (~1s) can eat a full channel, making pillar/LoS play less reliable than in classic/TBC — [[d:pvp-general#1423403435470098504]] (Kyrsie), [[d:pvp-general#1424858513510891551]] (Thirtyseven); disputed in degree by lowlifeopinions, who says a full rupture+missiles+procs kill needs ~8s of uninterrupted free-casting minimum — [[d:pvp-general#1423496943488733355]] (2025-10-03/07). Countertip: Mage can beat roughly half of Enhancement Shamans just by Disarming them since most don't carry a backup weapon — [[d:pvp-general#1427584676188520560]], [[d:pvp-general#1427584805431935049]] (limoseng, 2025-10-14).
- **[Warlock PvP]** Warlock's core strength is control (fear, dots), not raw dps; affliction burst (Dark Harvest/Drain Soul) is real but Destruction (esp. with the 3-piece "3.5" tier PvP set) gives better burst — [[d:pvp-general#1425011438090584114]] (lowlifeopinions), [[d:pvp-general#1427391732580089978]] (Bipst), 2025-10-07/14.
- **[Warlock PvP]** Extended, disputed debate/video review of a T3-geared Warlock (+priest assist) vs a top warrior (Berserker Stance + WSG zerker buff = ~20% extra dmg taken, using Burrower's Shell [+900 HP] and Life-Giving Gem [+15% max HP]): warrior survived ~20-23s before dying, warlock ended at ~50% HP. lowlifeopinions argues this shows warlock/affliction single-target dps is NOT especially strong here; Thirtyseven argues 20s is impressive and the warrior used every defensive available — both sides recorded, unresolved — [[d:pvp-general#1424991449404936262]], [[d:pvp-general#1425010660684595264]], [[d:pvp-general#1425013739924619314]], [[d:pvp-general#1425015533228658740]] (2025-10-07).
- **[Warlock PvP]** Warlock is disproportionately valuable/rare on Alliance (few players choose it there); Curse of Tongues (-slower spellcasting) is very strong vs Horde's caster-heavy comps, since Horde relies on mage/druid decurses to clear it — [[d:pvp-general#1427356050306109440]], [[d:pvp-general#1427356400962637824]] (dindunuffinz, 2025-10-13); Kyrsie disputes that Warlock is genuinely that strong on Alliance ("if it were, we'd see more of them") — [[d:pvp-general#1427356582261555452]] (contested).
- **[Warlock PvP]** Warlock's only near-unwinnable matchup is Feral Druid: Feral's stealth-opener (Cheap-Shot-equivalent) is close to guaranteed due to how the stealth-detection cone works, even with a Felhunter out (would require constant 360° turning); Thorn's Pod trinket (removes fear, 1h CD) and Death Coil (as a reflector/fear-break trigger) are the main tools, but Grillbe still reports dying to Ferals "100 to 0 in 3 seconds" as an Alliance lock — [[d:pvp-general#1427356757348454441]], [[d:pvp-general#1427357294449922222]], [[d:pvp-general#1427531411421794354]] (dindunuffinz/Grillbe, 2025-10-13).
- **[Warlock PvP]** Succubus-spec Warlock (Seduce fear-chain into Soulfire) considered near-unbeatable 1v1 without a Restoration Potion or Lucidity Potion; win condition is killing the Succubus pet before it re-casts Seduce, otherwise you get chain-seduced through a Soulfire burst — [[d:pvp-general#1426343844731162684]], [[d:pvp-general#1426343965292236901]], [[d:pvp-general#1426344097974849557]] (Kyrsie, 2025-10-11).
- **[Shaman PvP]** Enhancement Shaman is widely called "busted": burst combos of ~2.3-4.2k damage in 2 GCDs (Flame/Frost Shock → totems → autoattack → Stormstrike) are achievable and described repeatedly as an effective "oneshot" from full HP on squishier targets; a well-geared ("3.5"-tier) Enhance shaman is said capable of a ~5k nuke — [[d:pvp-general#1423710667394912296]] (AxenPunker), [[d:pvp-general#1425207495894499328]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:pvp-general#1425207182869401712]] (Thirtyseven: "3.8k > 0 in 1 frame"), [[d:pvp-general#1425210224914202725]] (Thirtyseven), 2025-10-03/08. Video evidence cited: a fresh level-48 leveling shaman doing near-2k crits, and a fresh level-60 shaman doing guaranteed 3.2k crit combos (1600+1600) "in a second" — [[d:pvp-general#1427319026056429659]] (dindunuffinz, 2025-10-13; single source w/ video link).
- **[Shaman PvP]** Shaman's Lightning Shield auto-procs extra damage (~530) whenever the shaman lands a melee hit — [[d:pvp-general#1425209999571025982]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-08). A talent gives Shaman 30% armor from having a shield equipped (contributing to tankiness); suggestion (unimplemented) to cut it to ~10% and compensate with +20% healing spell power — [[d:pvp-general#1425148312041947186]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-06).
- **[Shaman PvP, disputed]** Long, unresolved debate on whether Shaman is a genuinely overtuned class or a case of Paladin having near-zero "floor" (bad average players) — betcover argues Shaman has an unusually high floor (weak players still "press shocks and one-shot people") while Paladin needs real skill/button-pressing to be good; Gizzmazz/Bstwrath-Gizzy argues a *good* Holy/Prot Paladin outperforms a shaman and the real issue is ~17-25% of the server's population being (mostly mediocre) Paladins, skewing faction comps — [[d:pvp-general#1427519654263394304]], [[d:pvp-general#1427520420713857185]], [[d:pvp-general#1427527976790130792]], [[d:pvp-general#1427414659077505064]] (betcover/Gizzmazz/Bipst, 2025-10-14; genuinely contested, both sides extensively recorded). Community also floated (unimplemented) a per-class BG headcount limit and/or temporarily disabling Paladin character creation to force class variety — [[d:pvp-general#1427414065063395495]], [[d:pvp-general#1427516505159110758]] (dindunuffinz, 2025-10-14).
- **[Shaman/Elemental PvP]** An "Ele tank" hybrid build (Elemental spec taking tanky/defensive talents) is called the most broken variant on the server — can heal, tank melee, and deal ranged dps simultaneously; an "Ele/Enhance" hybrid (giving up the 10% DR talent) is strong vs Paladin/Warrior/Druid if it lands hard casts — [[d:pvp-general#1427538216935886889]], [[d:pvp-general#1427539066362134529]] (limoseng/Tirya, 2025-10-14). A dedicated "Shadow Shaman" spec exists using an AoE fear totem and a Shadow-damage "Molten/Shadow Bolt" hybrid spell (made Shadow-school specifically so it doesn't also benefit from Curse of Elements/Fire Mage's +15% fire damage buff in PvE) — [[d:pvp-general#1426303667228643452]], [[d:pvp-general#1426303713529692224]], [[d:pvp-general#1426303834736824330]] (AxenPunker, 2025-10-11).
- **[Priest PvP]** Priests are the least-played/weakest-participation PvP class on Ambershire; struggle to heal through melee pressure 1v1; Holy Priest is nominated for F-tier alongside Warrior — [[d:pvp-general#1423679614970101874]] (Nandieb), [[d:pvp-general#1423695441161359392]] (Gnomeburger Orgon), [[d:pvp-general#1426343489796440155]] (Liracrime), 2025-10-03/11.
- **[Paladin/Ret PvP]** Ret Paladin's Vengeance mechanic (custom talent) stacks up to 3x on crit, granting +20% physical damage while inflicting -20% armor/-20% resistances on the paladin for 30s per stack; Paladin also has "Zeal," a custom talent giving +15% haste at all times (unlike Warrior's crit-only Flurry) — [[d:pvp-general#1423770058571644990]], [[d:pvp-general#1423770947231547532]] (Kyrsie/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-03). A talent letting Holy Light cast in 1.5s is considered mandatory for PvP Paladins: bait a Shaman Shock, 1.5s Holy Light, Judgement, another 1.5s Holy Light ≈ 3k healing recovered — [[d:pvp-general#1427671149428342906]], [[d:pvp-general#1427672117058474138]], [[d:pvp-general#1427672345136463882]] (Purec/Kyrsie, 2025-10-14); without it, Holy Light was recently nerfed to ~1.4-2k healing, insufficient sustain vs burst meta (dying in 2-3 GCDs, bubble-then-die) — [[d:pvp-general#1427661237667500104]], [[d:pvp-general#1427661237667500104]] (SkillzMFG, 2025-10-14).
- **[Paladin PvP]** Paladin bubble (Divine Shield) chaining by multiple paladins is widely viewed as the single biggest problem in BG/AV objective play — 5-15 "bubbladins" can lock down a flag/graveyard cap near-indefinitely by chaining bubbles roughly every minute; critics say it requires little skill vs other classes' long CC (sap, poly, fear) — [[d:pvp-general#1425208065057099867]], [[d:pvp-general#1425208428682547241]] (Gnomeburger Orgon/Pastor, 2025-10-08); a forum post separately complains paladins can interrupt flag caps with just Bubble and have two ranged stuns (one deals damage), asking for Priest Mass Dispel as an answer — [[d:pvp-general#1425162507101802527]] (Pastor quoting forum, 2025-10-07). Common bad-Ret play pattern noted by veterans: wasting Hammer of Justice on a Grounding Totem instead of the player, then bubble-charging alone into a bad position and getting kited/killed — [[d:pvp-general#1427659181745504359]] (Bipst, 2025-10-14).
- **[Warrior PvP]** Widely called the weakest PvP class (F tier); comparatively low single-target damage (e.g. "wait for another auto to do a mortal for 500"); joked-about "oneshot rotation" is "charge → root → getting kited → Spirit Healer" — [[d:pvp-general#1425206560845725929]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:pvp-general#1427537326594330716]] (limoseng), 2025-10-08/14. Countervailing data point: with good gear a Mortal Strike can crit ~2300, and geared Ret Paladins crit Enhance Shamans for ~1.2k, so raw damage isn't always trivial — the class's real weakness is survivability/mobility vs ranged/caster burst and easy kiting — [[d:pvp-general#1426729916191473784]], [[d:pvp-general#1427541569304657941]] (Hotdiggity/Tirya, 2025-10-12/14). Only Dual-Wield Warrior (using a lifesteal-proc weapon, "Blooddrinker") is reported to perform reasonably well in PvP — [[d:pvp-general#1427997410394902589]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-14).
- **[Contested/summary]** Tirya's summary of the meta: "Ret pala with bubble up is broken, ele shaman is broken, destro lock is broken, arcane mage is broken, rogue is broken... If they're all broken, there's balance" — reflects broad community sentiment that burst/survivability across nearly every non-warrior spec has been pushed very high — [[d:pvp-general#1427538032554283009]] (Tirya, 2025-10-14; opinion, widely echoed).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General PvP]** General PvP gear stat priority: "depends on your class, generally stamina > crit > main stat" — [[d:pvp-general#1424924907820552212]] (Bipst, 2025-10-04).
- **[Mage PvP]** Stamina/crit prioritized for PvP gear; Spirit is useless in PvP because there's no mana regen during combat — [[d:pvp-general#1423705880704319609]] (Nocapskibidi, 2025-10-03).
- **[Rogue PvP]** Rogues stack heavy Stamina for burst survivability; one rank-14 warrior reports ~6.9k stamina achievable — [[d:pvp-general#1423763738779193467]], [[d:pvp-general#1423764040269692928]] (Stormtaurus/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-04).
- **[Bug]** Resilience stat does not work as its tooltip describes: it reduces critical-hit damage by only ~1.3% instead of the stated 3% (tested by repeatedly hitting a target and calculating), while it does correctly reduce DoT damage by the full 3%. An official Turtle bug report was filed: turtle-wow.org/bug-report?id=17804 — [[d:pvp-general#1425591367932973089]], [[d:pvp-general#1426817593095880767]], [[d:pvp-general#1427318514359861378]] (Rocked/Tapka, 2025-10-08/13; multiple confirmations, plus a filed bug report — high confidence).
- **[Turtle mechanic]** "Dampening" (a damage-reduction-over-time mechanic active in both extended arena/duel fights and in regular battlegrounds) exists on Turtle; community requests it be increased further to curb burst; a Troll Warrior's racial health regeneration is NOT reduced by Dampening, making Trolls overperform specifically in low-level (1-29) twink brackets — [[d:pvp-general#1424990540537266269]], [[d:pvp-general#1425920834262007939]], [[d:pvp-general#1428041544543244361]] (lowlifeopinions/NoplanetB/Cysthen, 2025-10-07/15).
- **[Turtle mechanic]** "Melee leeway" affects kiting: characters keep tracking/swinging at targets somewhat beyond normal range, making it harder to kite warriors even with Hamstring applied — [[d:pvp-general#1425014745534042123]] (lowlifeopinions, 2025-10-07). Charge similarly can overshoot/undershoot because it "anticipates" the target's position, and gets thrown off if the target is slowed or moving fast (300+ move speed is very hard to Charge reliably) — [[d:pvp-general#1427576195553169429]], [[d:pvp-general#1427576579113881661]] (Jesse Potter/Pastor, 2025-10-14).

- **[PvP gearing progression, contested]** Community complaint: there is effectively no PvP-only gear progression path — competitive PvP gear requires raiding, which is a problem for players who PvP specifically because they don't have raid time; suggestion (unimplemented) that Rank 12/13 PvP gear should be roughly equal to Tier 2.5/3 raid gear — [[d:pvp-general#1426488616565215244]], [[d:pvp-general#1426639569628958792]], [[d:pvp-general#1426639881219739719]] (Loralius/Purec/olympius_, 2025-10-11). - **[Set bonuses]** Some 2-piece Rank 12 PvP set bonuses are considered efficient stat sticks for PvP on certain classes; the Arathi Basin (AB) PvP set specifically is called a solid option for Warlocks — [[d:pvp-general#1426641812269039776]], [[d:pvp-general#1426641971581161604]] (lowlifeopinions, 2025-10-11).
- **[Server/gear gap]** Ambershire (newer/fresh server) currently lacks some enchants available on Nordanaar, e.g. the 15 Agility to Gloves enchant is not obtainable there yet, which affects twinks/tanks on that realm — [[d:pvp-general#1428064010216931529]] (Cysthen, 2025-10-15).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Rogue PvP]** Rogues favor double *fast* weapons (even cheap grey/blue 1.0-1.3 speed daggers) because ~90% of their damage comes from poison procs that trigger per-swing and don't scale with AP/weapon skill — faster weapon = more poison procs = more damage; a specific 1.20-speed fist/dagger from a level-33 quest is recommended for "zoomie poison dps" leveling builds — [[d:pvp-general#1423694541004734676]], [[d:pvp-general#1426537635207446600]], [[d:pvp-general#1427221835929358376]] (Thirtyseven/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-03/13).
- **[Rogue PvP]** Dissolvent Poison (see Spec viability) ignores armor entirely and is unaffected by Nature Resistance; Noxious Assault can proc it twice per hit — [[d:pvp-general#1423408386569011260]], [[d:pvp-general#1423694672991228016]] (Eemo/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-03).
- **[Paladin/melee PvP weapon setup]** Example high-end weapon proc stack described for fighting a spam-healing tank/healer: C'Thun axe proc, Icy Chill (slow-on-hit weapon enchant), Frost Oil proc, plus Seal of Justice — [[d:pvp-general#1427585547895046144]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-14). Icy Chill specifically preferred over other slow procs because the uptime reliably prevents targets from disengaging — [[d:pvp-general#1427585100068950076]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Shaman PvP]** Windfury Weapon can double-proc back-to-back with no internal cooldown on Turtle (and can rarely triple-proc), but it cannot chain-proc off its own procs the way it could in very early vanilla patches; Windfury Totem procs (2 hits) and Windfury Weapon procs (weapon hits) are separate effects that don't stack multiplicatively — [[d:pvp-general#1426963958769651742]], [[d:pvp-general#1426964382490955856]], [[d:pvp-general#1426964713883045908]] (dindunuffinz/Xerq, 2025-10-11/12).
- **[Contested]** Whether Enhancement Shaman can still "imbue-weave" both Windfury and Rockbiter (like old-style Paladin seal-twisting) on Turtle's spellbatching model is unresolved/unknown to the posters discussing it — [[d:pvp-general#1427316217034838117]], [[d:pvp-general#1427319124144423094]] (dindunuffinz, 2025-10-13; open question).

- **[Survival trinkets]** "Burrower's Shell" (+900 HP) and "Life-Giving Gem" (+15% max HP) are trinkets used by warriors to extend survivability in extreme burst fights — [[d:pvp-general#1425008816709304330]], [[d:pvp-general#1425016225980874794]] (chain/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-07).
- **[Warlock PvP]** Warlock Healthstones can be handed to allies roughly every ~2 minutes — [[d:pvp-general#1424812942028832929]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-06). Fel Domination (reduces pet-summon cast time to 1.5s) and a Soulstone-into-Shadow Bolt combo are noted as strong warlock plays — [[d:pvp-general#1425009815138340914]], [[d:pvp-general#1425013983236329562]] (lowlifeopinions/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-07).

- **[Warrior PvP]** A consumable called "Sheen" (of Zanza?) grants a guaranteed spell-reflect on first use and can reportedly be re-chugged roughly every 4 seconds by players who can afford it; it can even reflect certain raid boss spells (e.g. after "gnalrmoon"/a big dragon boss) — [[d:pvp-general#1427542605595672616]], [[d:pvp-general#1427548144304128121]], [[d:pvp-general#1427549471234850890]] (Jesse Potter/MoneyCow, 2025-10-14; single-source, exact item name uncertain — likely "Sheen of Zanza").

## Tanking specifics
- **[Tank PvP roles]** Discussion of whether tanks have a real PvP role concluded: Protection Paladin can work broadly in BGs, contributing some damage while supporting teammates; Protection Warrior makes a very good flag carrier on Turtle; Druids can also flag-carry without going a strict tank build; all tank-capable specs can spin/hold flags in Arathi Basin and tank mobs in Alterac Valley — [[d:pvp-general#1425550627576217752]] (Pastor, 2025-10-08). Prot Warrior's kit (slows, Concussion Blow, Revenge-triggered stun procs, high mitigation) is cited as already giving some of the "disrupt the damage dealer" utility that a StMichael forum post argued PvP tanks need — [[d:pvp-general#1425561313228230707]], [[d:pvp-general#1425562300399620251]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-08).
- **[Contested]** StMichael's forum post proposed importing Warhammer Online-style baseline tank tools (Guard: redirect 50% dmg from an ally to the tank; AoE Taunt reducing the taunted target's damage to others by 25%; a channeled "Hold the Line" granting nearby allies dodge/resist) as a design direction to give PvP tanks a disruptor role — met with mixed reaction; Pastor notes there's no place for tanks in arena regardless, and Jesse Potter argues Prot Warrior already covers much of the proposed niche via existing kit — [[d:pvp-general#1425548855839035653]] through [[d:pvp-general#1425568470485176352]] (StMichael vs Pastor/Jesse Potter/lowlifeopinions, 2025-10-08; open design discussion, no resolution).
- **[Farming/economy note]** A geared Prot Paladin can reportedly solo-farm content at a level comparable to "AoE farming 10 BiS-geared warriors" worth of mobs, and Shamans can solo Zul'Gurub — raised as relevant to PvP-balance debates because farming prowess funds PvP consumables — [[d:pvp-general#1427693065103212555]], [[d:pvp-general#1427697837130518609]] (Kyrsie/Nandieb, 2025-10-14).

## Healing specifics
- **[Paladin PvP]** Holy Light was nerfed and now heals for roughly 1.4-2k, described as insufficient sustain against the current burst meta (dying within 2-3 GCDs; Bubble often the only real answer, then dying right after it drops) — [[d:pvp-general#1427661237667500104]], [[d:pvp-general#1427661560523784203]] (SkillzMFG, 2025-10-14). Counter-technique: a talent that reduces Holy Light's cast time to 1.5s is called mandatory for PvP Paladins, allowing a bait-Shock → 1.5s Holy Light → Judgement → 1.5s Holy Light cycle for ~3k healing recovered — [[d:pvp-general#1427671149428342906]], [[d:pvp-general#1427672117058474138]] (Purec, 2025-10-14).
- **[Shaman PvP]** Shaman healing intentionally nerfed as of 1.18 (see 1.18.1 changes) — a heal now ticking ~2k/2s is trivial vs ~3.5k/s incoming burst — [[d:pvp-general#1424806300096794755]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-06).
- **[Priest PvP]** Priests struggle to heal through melee pressure 1v1 in the current state — [[d:pvp-general#1423695441161359392]] (Gnomeburger Orgon, 2025-10-03). In non-premade BGs, healers (esp. Priest) get essentially no peels from random teammates, making solo-queue healing miserable regardless of class — this is characterized as more a player/comp issue than a pure class issue — [[d:pvp-general#1427530447658684478]], [[d:pvp-general#1427530515006885918]] (Grillbe, 2025-10-14).
- **[Healer PvP gear]** No leather PvP healing set exists for Druids and no heal+ PvP set exists for Priests (see Gear section) — [[d:pvp-general#1425076941265965118]] (Cenandrah, 2025-10-07).

## PvP
- **[BG mechanic]** WSG's flag-carrier stacking debuff functions as a soft timer: at roughly 18 stacks, the carrier becomes vulnerable to being two-shot, effectively forcing a cap or death before the stack count gets too high; stacks only reset on a successful flag cap, NOT by dropping the flag (confirmed against a claim that a rogue could reset stacks by dropping/re-grabbing) — [[d:pvp-general#1425625682335109200]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:pvp-general#1425267040125587536]], [[d:pvp-general#1425274075252789258]], [[d:pvp-general#1425415543149563974]] (Jesse Potter/Thirtyseven/awry, 2025-10-08). There is no stack cap currently; community wants one added. Some WSG matches with no natural resolution have lasted 50+ minutes, prompting requests for a hard match timer — [[d:pvp-general#1425622199930458162]], [[d:pvp-general#1425625894604509227]] (ormeyer, 2025-10-09).
- **[WSG tactics]** Flag carriers tend to hide/hold at a small number of known, class-dependent spots (e.g. a specific player known to always FC at "ToT"; a druid using the graveyard jump-skip near the instance portal; some warriors defaulting to graveyard); learning these habitual spots is a real skill for hunting enemy FCs — [[d:pvp-general#1425610494772776981]], [[d:pvp-general#1425610622594453595]], [[d:pvp-general#1425610728554889226]] (Jesse Potter, 2025-10-09). Nameplate/addon settings can be configured to only show enemy-player nameplates (hiding totems/pets/friendlies) to reduce visual clutter while flag-hunting — [[d:pvp-general#1425610037249839264]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[WSG mechanics]** Some flag-carrier jump routes/shortcuts that work in Classic vanilla do not work identically on Turtle (e.g. a jump from a wooden platform to the tunnel/roof is impossible here); male vs female character models can matter for making certain jumps — [[d:pvp-general#1424453730442678474]], [[d:pvp-general#1424462228014497842]], [[d:pvp-general#1424462564875702324]] (Grillbe/Pastor, 2025-10-05). There is no way to practice WSG jumps outside of an ongoing real match without self-hosting/GM access — [[d:pvp-general#1427301812750258316]], [[d:pvp-general#1427304288920670328]] (Nandieb/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-13). Skipping the pre-match gate lockout is possible but not condoned as "allowed" — [[d:pvp-general#1425609795150418002]], [[d:pvp-general#1425609880429138001]] (awry/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-09).
- **[Alliance NE Hunter tactic]** Night Elf Hunters can use Shadowmeld to hide (with their cat pet) directly on/near a flag as a defensive/ambush cheese tactic — [[d:pvp-general#1427100132062662668]] (Kyrsie, 2025-10-13; disputed as a legitimate strategy by some, described neutrally by others).
- **[Honor rank system]** Rank progression uses a weekly "RP" (rank points) system approximated as `current_RP*0.8 + weekly_standing_RP ≈ progress`; RP needed per rank scales with total honor earned that week and depends on the size/competitiveness of the active ranking player pool that week (not a fixed honor number) — e.g. Rank 8 needs roughly 30,000-35,000 RP, Rank 3→4 needs roughly 5,000-10,000 RP — [[d:pvp-general#1424740849161601034]], [[d:pvp-general#1424744403418742784]], [[d:pvp-general#1424744910476673035]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-06). The maximum rank you can jump in one week (e.g. Rank 4 → Rank 6) depends on being near the top ~10 players on your faction that week (relative "standing"), per community-referenced tools like vanillawar.com — [[d:pvp-general#1427419010940796928]] (Pastor, 2025-10-14).
- **[Honor rank system]** Weekly RP is capped at 13,000 RP; the honor required to hit that cap varies from roughly 450k to 1 million depending on how many people are actively ranking that week; the higher your current rank, the less RP the same honor grants (diminishing returns); there is NO weekly honor cap on Turtle (unlike vanilla-classic's hard 500k weekly cap on official/emulated servers) — [[d:pvp-general#1424748285645819965]], [[d:pvp-general#1424748682452013057]], [[d:pvp-general#1424749973249200232]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-06), confirmed no-cap by [[d:pvp-general#1425573060341399717]], [[d:pvp-general#1425573105752866817]] (Pastor/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-08).
- **[Honor rank system]** Rank does not decay below your highest achieved rank while inactive — reputation/progress toward the *next* rank decays, but you keep your current rank (e.g. hit Rank 8, stop playing 3 months, remain Rank 8); timing a break right after ranking up avoids any penalty — [[d:pvp-general#1424868588879347713]] (Pastor), [[d:pvp-general#1424869139641663509]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:pvp-general#1425490482536120370]] (SkillzMFG, 2025-10-06/09).
- **[Honor rank system]** Honor/rep gain rate from flag caps and quest turn-ins is the same at level 60 as at lower brackets — [[d:pvp-general#1424670432702763052]], [[d:pvp-general#1424671676909490280]] (Rauber/Pastor, 2025-10-06). Staying at level 49 (twink bracket) caps achievable rank at Rank 9; staying in the 30s bracket caps it around Rank 5 — [[d:pvp-general#1424038389484617738]] (Kyrsie), [[d:pvp-general#1425436855091855422]] (awry, 2025-10-04/08).
- **[Honor rank system]** Reaching Rank 13 as a fresh character regardless of class/faction/race takes only 1-2 weeks of casual play — [[d:pvp-general#1423691136676925543]] (Nandieb, 2025-10-03).
- **[Honor rank system / exploit, contested]** Legitimately reaching Rank 14 is possible but unlikely while competing against dedicated "rankers." Rank-14 pushes reportedly require ~600-800k+ honor/week, achieved by playing 16-17 hours/day for weeks; the grind is described as mentally exhausting to the point that top rankers commonly go semi-AFK ("anchoring" on an objective) late in a session, which degrades game quality for everyone else; some players multi-account/multibox (e.g. 3 computers ranking 6 characters at once) to farm honor and then sell Rank 14 characters for real money (~$500+ each). Account sharing for honor farming is NOT against server rules, though multiboxing itself is reportedly bannable — [[d:pvp-general#1424604648316211312]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:pvp-general#1425137983769673911]], [[d:pvp-general#1425141589692518424]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:pvp-general#1425181425707253852]] (Jesse Potter: multiboxing pvp is bannable), [[d:pvp-general#1428047736858476675]], [[d:pvp-general#1428048072247873610]] (Thirtyseven describing his own R14 grind: 600+ BGs, lost 90%+, still got 650k honor), 2025-10-06/15. An AFK-detection debuff currently exists only in Alterac Valley, not in other BGs, and reports of AFK players don't reliably lead to action — [[d:pvp-general#1428047992065101834]] (Midoko), [[d:pvp-general#1428046911692214372]] (Rhiina), 2025-10-15. Proposed (unimplemented) fixes: a stacking 25%-honor-reduction debuff for detected inactivity lasting 5 hours, and/or a stacking damage-taken debuff to punish objective-camping without contesting — [[d:pvp-general#1428050498228850841]], [[d:pvp-general#1428050650406846496]] (Thirtyseven/dindunuffinz, 2025-10-15).
- **[Honor rank system]** "Poolsize" (the number of standing/rank slots available per faction per bracket) scales with the number of active ranking players that week; exact calculation method is unclear/disputed — one guess is that even a single honor kill counts you into the pool — community observed 12 top-bracket Horde spots on a low-population (~5,000 total pop) Ambershire, which seemed inconsistent with expectations — [[d:pvp-general#1427958356290568192]], [[d:pvp-general#1427959573335248937]], [[d:pvp-general#1427966653211344917]] (Nandieb/Purec, 2025-10-14; open question).
- **[Honor rank system]** A special "Blood Ring Champion" title requires unique max reputation with the Blood Ring faction; Blood Ring (the arena-like duel event) opens roughly every 5 days and is one of the only times PvPers actively queue for it, since it grants bonus honor toward ranking — [[d:pvp-general#1428055615258759281]], [[d:pvp-general#1428056239086243930]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:pvp-general#1428048134088560752]] (lowlifeopinions, 2025-10-15).
- **[Rank/gearing]** Getting a competitive PvP mount is much cheaper via Rank 11 (which grants a mount with 100% speed despite the rider only needing 60%-tier riding skill trained) than earning normal Epic (100%) riding skill (~2-3 days of farming vs ~2-3 months to reach Rank 11); some players deliberately skip training Epic Riding and rely on the Rank 11 mount instead, though this is discouraged as it gimps them before reaching Rank 11 — [[d:pvp-general#1425475882176217119]], [[d:pvp-general#1425476473363369985]], [[d:pvp-general#1425525785678053457]], [[d:pvp-general#1425531956447150192]] (AxenPunker/Cenandrah/Nandieb/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-08). Druid's own epic-flight-form-equivalent quest reportedly requires a full raid group and is much harder to complete than the PvP-mount route — [[d:pvp-general#1425477143764140222]] (SkillzMFG, 2025-10-08).
- **[Faction/comp]** Faction imbalance is a persistent, long-running (since Nostalrius-era) theme: historically Horde pugs beat Alliance pugs ~90-95% of the time, forcing Alliance to organize premades to compete; this dynamic persists unchanged on Turtle — [[d:pvp-general#1427085650028662898]] (dindunuffinz, 2025-10-13). On Turtle specifically, Horde is reported winning ~80-90% of pug BGs, driven by Shaman-stacking vs Alliance's Paladin-heavy comps; Alliance also tends to lack casters (esp. Priests) which lets Horde flag-caps go uncontested more easily — [[d:pvp-general#1427519030746550374]], [[d:pvp-general#1427521811373097001]], [[d:pvp-general#1427526095162314803]] (betcover/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-14).
- **[Comp/Team PvP]** Ret Paladin + Shaman is a strong (not best) 2v2 comp: Shaman gives the Paladin Windfury, Paladin gives Shaman Blessing of Protection — very complementary — but lacks hard control and loses to good Mage/Rogue comps; Mage or Warlock are speculated (unconfirmed) as possibly the best 2s partner for a Ret Paladin — [[d:pvp-general#1424955165294723072]], [[d:pvp-general#1424957793433292953]], [[d:pvp-general#1424945919803129919]] (Bipst/lowlifeopinions, 2025-10-06/07).
- **[Comp/Team PvP]** Warlock is favored as a teammate for maximizing overall kill count, Mage for clutch/disabling utility; Warlock vs Paladin is a more manageable matchup than Warlock vs Shaman — [[d:pvp-general#1424118413147771052]], [[d:pvp-general#1424118458995445943]], [[d:pvp-general#1424120782862155847]] (Kyrsie, 2025-10-04). Against Paladins specifically, "sheep and ignore" is a reliable win pattern for Mages (paladins have no way to prevent the sheep sticking); vs 5+ Shamans, sheep gets repeatedly "ping-ponged" off Grounding Totems, making Shaman-heavy groups a much harder matchup — [[d:pvp-general#1423692207071952996]], [[d:pvp-general#1423693001900687421]] (Dracilli/Jesse Potter, 2025-10-03).
- **[Comp/Team PvP, disputed prediction]** lowlifeopinions predicts (self-flagged as speculative) that heal+dps comps will underperform double-dps comps because damage here is very spiky/burst while healing is reactive: Paladins/Shamans are vulnerable when locked out or CC'd outside of Divine Shield, Priests die easily, and Druid HoTs don't counter burst well — [[d:pvp-general#1424980220653473942]] (2025-10-06/07; explicitly a prediction, not confirmed in-server evidence).
- **[State of PvP, contested/summary]** Newcomer FAQ-style summary given twice independently: "BGs pop often, no world PvP, arenas exist but are horrible, class balance is basically non-existent; PvE content is awesome but if you value PvP more, don't roll here" and "it's literally not a PvP server, PvP is a side quest here, it's a PvE server" — [[d:pvp-general#1427687377064955967]], [[d:pvp-general#1427687509722271866]] (betcover), [[d:pvp-general#1427676930810839152]] (AxenPunker), 2025-10-11/14; widely echoed opinion, not contradicted in this chunk. Turtle's actual competitive-arena population is very small — one poster reports getting "Rank 1" arena standing off a 10-0 session in ~30 minutes because there are so few other teams; roughly only ~10 people were doing arena at the time this was discussed — [[d:pvp-general#1426961124120723577]], [[d:pvp-general#1426971617220755711]], [[d:pvp-general#1427798188953178162]] (Pastor/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-11/15).
- **[Contested]** Extended, unresolved brainstorm on implementing cross-faction battlegrounds (frequently requested, never delivered as of this chunk): open questions include how reputation/pool-size/ranking brackets would work per faction, how to prevent cross-faction premades from farming pugs, and whether an MMR system would be needed for random-faction team assignment; community leans toward keeping reputation/honor pools faction-bound even under cross-faction queuing — [[d:pvp-general#1428028687059521647]] through [[d:pvp-general#1428044451539845291]] (Nandieb/Thirtyseven/Chomps/Pastor, 2025-10-15; no resolution, forum-adjacent).
- **[Contested]** Faction switching/race changing is allowed on Turtle and used by strong players to move to the perceived-stronger side (e.g. a known good Alliance Warlock race-changed to Undead/Horde); called "retarded" as a balance-undermining policy by at least one poster — [[d:pvp-general#1427531907826061313]], [[d:pvp-general#1427532069885710528]] (betcover/Purec, 2025-10-14).
- **[Turtle mechanic]** Level-1 "Lunatic" twink gankers (extreme-investment level-1 characters camping starting zones) became such a problem that it directly drove a nerf to how open-world "Warmode" flagging works, which the community broadly blames for killing what little world PvP existed; several posters argue a narrower fix (level-1 lunatics simply cannot attack non-level-1 players, or placing a Battlemaster/opt-out near starting zones) would have solved it without gutting Warmode for everyone else — [[d:pvp-general#1428054532427874425]], [[d:pvp-general#1428055912236716115]], [[d:pvp-general#1428056454132142213]] (Pastor/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-15; contested but one-sided in this chunk — no one defends the current Warmode design as-is besides cs from GRIZZLY defending Lunatic play itself).
- **[Bug]** Arena matchmaking on Turtle frequently stops finding new opponents entirely, requiring the team to be disbanded and reformed to get a new match — described as a long-standing, unaddressed issue — [[d:pvp-general#1426983653514281020]], [[d:pvp-general#1426984048206811226]] (MoneyCow/1.18.1 WILL SAVE US, 2025-10-12).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Addons]** Common third-party tools referenced for Turtle (1.12-based client) PvP: SuperWoW (needed to see more than 4 buffs/debuffs on default UI, enables more accurate unit info), pfUI (recommended all-in-one replacement for stitching together many addons — clickcasting, buff/debuff whitelisting on frames/nameplates, castbars; ships with an extensive spell-duration lookup table to work around the client's lack of proper DR/duration tracking), VanillaFixes/VanillaTweaks (load DLLs like SuperWoW automatically, run via a VanillaFixes.exe launcher), Nampower, Decursive (helps prioritize which debuff to dispel first), "Interact" (installed by placing Interact.dll beside wow.exe and registering it in dlls.txt, with a matching addon-folder component), and "honorspy turtle" (tracks/estimates weekly ranking honor totals) — [[d:pvp-general#1424898278763397291]], [[d:pvp-general#1425884691327619113]], [[d:pvp-general#1425885542620467321]], [[d:pvp-general#1426269965505396867]] (Dracilli/Pastor/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-04/12).
- **[Client limitation]** The 1.12 client cannot properly implement Diminishing Returns tracking, so addon-shown debuff/CC durations can be inaccurate (e.g. a warrior Charge within the last 15s can extend a debuff's real duration beyond what's displayed); the only reasonably accurate fix is an addon that parses combat-log messages and calculates durations itself (which is what pfUI's large duration-lookup file partially achieves), and even that can't perfectly account for talents that extend durations or variable-combo-point Kidney Shot — [[d:pvp-general#1425837014040973402]], [[d:pvp-general#1425837338734497935]], [[d:pvp-general#1425837486512410625]], [[d:pvp-general#1425838310219190376]] (Pastor, 2025-10-09).
- **[Addon controversy]** WeakAura-style audio/visual alert addons exist that instantly flag things a skilled player would otherwise have to notice manually — e.g. a "foghorn" alert the instant a Flash Bomb is thrown, or instant pop notifications for Divine Shield/trinket use — community is split on whether these are fair (since the underlying event is in the combat log) or effectively an aimbot-tier crutch that removes real skill elements like fakecasting (e.g. the old "Hearthstone trick" for baiting reactions no longer works against WeakAura users) — [[d:pvp-general#1425775938385346641]], [[d:pvp-general#1427357515246735450]], [[d:pvp-general#1427358653677179093]], [[d:pvp-general#1427984095589306468]] (1.18.1 WILL SAVE US/dindunuffinz/Kyrsie, 2025-10-08/13; genuinely contested, unresolved).
- **[Addon]** An "AutoBG" addon exists that automatically queues the player for battlegrounds on login (github.com/JJardine98/_AutoBG), used by some low-population-faction players trying to help games pop — [[d:pvp-general#1428055379593396295]] (Cysthen, 2025-10-15).
- **[Macro]** Standard BG-queue macro/UI note: press the dual-sword icon near the minimap to queue for a BG from anywhere (including while questing), no NPC visit required — [[d:pvp-general#1427061655082111048]] (dindunuffinz, 2025-10-13).
- **[UI note]** Transmog display is disabled inside battlegrounds on Turtle; players instead sometimes appear as illusion-item forms (Cenarius, Dryad, Scourge, Furbolg, Noggenfogger-morphs, etc.), which can make it hard to read enemy attack direction/animations (e.g. a "giant chicken" model obscuring the player underneath) — [[d:pvp-general#1426680097280229537]], [[d:pvp-general#1426682672574496940]], [[d:pvp-general#1426685347399471227]] (Jesse Potter/Thirtyseven/Pastor, 2025-10-12). Transmog itself provides no real gameplay advantage since any WeakAuras/SuperWoW user can see ability pops directly regardless of visual model — [[d:pvp-general#1427983466204631061]], [[d:pvp-general#1427984095589306468]] (awry/dindunuffinz, 2025-10-15).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Race choice]** Dwarf was historically recommended on Alliance specifically to counter rogue poison damage via Stoneform, but this counter is weakened now that rogue Blind is no longer classified as a poison effect (see 1.18.1 changes) — [[d:pvp-general#1426544205789925527]], [[d:pvp-general#1426546515790270565]] (dindunuffinz/1.18.1 WILL SAVE US, 2025-10-11); Dwarf's small racial Stamina/Strength edge over other races is otherwise considered negligible at level 60 (~4 points each) — [[d:pvp-general#1426545537766916126]] (dindunuffinz).
- **[Race choice]** For pure PvP performance, Tauren is recommended over Night Elf on Horde/Alliance-equivalent comparisons specifically because "Horde is stronger" as a faction overall in the current meta — [[d:pvp-general#1427094680042213417]], [[d:pvp-general#1427095513072668863]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-13).
- **[Server/faction choice for PvP]** For someone prioritizing PvP, a fresh/newer server (Ambershire at the time of this chunk) is recommended over the older, more progressed Nordanaar because gear disparity is much smaller there (everyone near fresh-60 stats, only MC-tier raid gear available), whereas Nordanaar has players fully decked in Naxx/BWL/AQ40 gear one-shotting fresh 60s — [[d:pvp-general#1426196317717135391]], [[d:pvp-general#1426196390907875441]] (Kyrsie, 2025-10-10); however, others note Ambershire also develops sharp gear disparities within weeks due to its "no-life" playerbase — [[d:pvp-general#1423630197021999134]] (Nandieb, 2025-10-03).
- **[Gold/farming]** Full Tier 3 raid gear is reportedly achievable within ~2 months of raiding after hitting 60, described as easier than on the newer/more contested Ambershire server due to less loot competition — [[d:pvp-general#1423535275790831704]], [[d:pvp-general#1423535421094101104]] (lowlifeopinions, 2025-10-03).
- **[Gold/farming]** Geared Prot Paladins and Shamans can solo-farm significant PvE content (mentioned: Prot Paladin AoE-farming, Shaman soloing Zul'Gurub), which indirectly funds PvP consumable spending for those classes more easily than for others — [[d:pvp-general#1427693065103212555]], [[d:pvp-general#1427697837130518609]] (Kyrsie/Nandieb, 2025-10-14).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Bug]** Resilience's tooltip-stated 3% critical-damage reduction only actually applies at ~1.3% in practice (full 3% works correctly for DoT damage); officially bug-reported (turtle-wow.org/bug-report?id=17804) — [[d:pvp-general#1425591367932973089]], [[d:pvp-general#1426817593095880767]], [[d:pvp-general#1427318514359861378]] (Rocked/Tapka, 2025-10-08/13).
- **[Bug]** A mace weapon proc effect ("Master Strike," reusing an old C'Thun boss spell) applies a disorient that does not break on damage despite its own tooltip explicitly saying it should — a long-standing unfixed bug — [[d:pvp-general#1427226359003873361]], [[d:pvp-general#1427245634137755728]], [[d:pvp-general#1427239312856191007]] (Jesse Potter/Purec/AxenPunker, 2025-10-08/13).
- **[Bug]** Dampening was badly broken when first introduced: warriors were hitting for up to 15,000 in a single blow and its healing-reduction component didn't function for roughly 5 weeks — [[d:pvp-general#1428041379879325756]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-15). Troll Warrior's racial regeneration is not reduced by Dampening at all, making Trolls overperform specifically in low-level twink brackets where Dampening otherwise matters — [[d:pvp-general#1428041544543244361]] (Cysthen, 2025-10-15); conversely, Dampening does still reduce Fury Warrior's Bloodthirst-linked self-healing talent, which Thirtyseven calls an inconsistent/unintended interaction — [[d:pvp-general#1428042764532322347]] (2025-10-15).
- **[Bug]** WSG flag-carrier stacking debuff can fail to reset if both flags are returned and re-picked-up near-simultaneously, giving a player 10+ stacks immediately — [[d:pvp-general#1423631876052226098]], [[d:pvp-general#1423633230497583158]] (Pastor, 2025-10-03).
- **[Bug]** Arena matchmaking frequently stops finding new opponents outright, requiring a team disband/reform cycle to get games again — [[d:pvp-general#1426983653514281020]] (MoneyCow, 2025-10-12).
- **[Bug/crash workaround]** On a client crash during a raid/BG (frequently in Kara40/Naxxramas), avoid clicking the crash error dialog and instead open a second wow.exe and log in normally to dodge the Deserter debuff; this trick reportedly does NOT work for crashes inside Naxxramas or Kara40, where a crash/death loop can also strip all consumables repeatedly — [[d:pvp-general#1424335537019420672]], [[d:pvp-general#1424336060258848849]], [[d:pvp-general#1424374599759691798]] (NoplanetB/Pastor, 2025-10-05).
- **[Quirk]** WSG matches can run 50+ minutes with no natural resolution since there is no hard timer — [[d:pvp-general#1425625894604509227]], [[d:pvp-general#1425625894604509227]] (ormeyer, 2025-10-09).
- **[Quirk]** Transmog is force-disabled inside battlegrounds and players instead display illusion-item forms (Furbolg, Scourge, Dryad, Cenarius, etc.), sometimes making it genuinely hard to parse enemy attack animations/direction (e.g. a "giant chicken" model) — [[d:pvp-general#1426680097280229537]], [[d:pvp-general#1426682672574496940]] (Jesse Potter/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-12).

## Contested / open questions
- **[Is Shaman overtuned, or is it a class-population/skill-floor problem?]** Extensively debated with no resolution in this chunk: one side argues Shaman has an unusually low skill floor (weak players still perform well) while good Holy/Prot Paladins are undervalued because ~17-25% of the server plays (mostly mediocre) Paladin, skewing perceived balance; the other side insists Shaman is simply overtuned relative to any equally-skilled Paladin — [[d:pvp-general#1427519654263394304]] through [[d:pvp-general#1427528605063450736]] (betcover vs Gizzmazz/Bstwrath-Gizzy, 2025-10-14).
- **[Cross-faction BG implementation]** Widely requested but never implemented as of this chunk; unresolved design questions include how reputation/pool-size/ranking brackets would be split or merged, how to prevent cross-faction premades from farming pugs, and whether MMR-based team balancing would be needed — [[d:pvp-general#1428028687059521647]] (Nandieb, 2025-10-15).
- **[Honor "poolsize" calculation]** Exactly how the weekly per-faction ranking pool size is calculated is unclear to the playerbase; an apparent inconsistency was observed (12 top-bracket Horde spots on a ~5,000-population Ambershire) that nobody in the chunk could explain — [[d:pvp-general#1427958356290568192]] (Nandieb, 2025-10-14; question unanswered).
- **[Resilience/Dampening tuning]** Whether Resilience is "good enough" once its crit-reduction bug is fixed, and whether Dampening's damage reduction should be increased further, are both raised as open tuning questions with no dev response recorded — [[d:pvp-general#1426640089844420619]], [[d:pvp-general#1426640253506158684]] (cs from GRIZZLY/lowlifeopinions, 2025-10-11).
- **[Warrior spell reflection]** Repeatedly requested (Kyrsie, limoseng, others) as a baseline ability to give Warriors a real defensive tool vs casters; never implemented as of this chunk — [[d:pvp-general#1423506745590026282]], [[d:pvp-general#1427542424028450867]].
- **[PvP gearing progression]** Whether PvP gear should be raid-independent (accessible without raiding) is debated with no resolution; contrast between "PvP gear should stand alone, like classic-TBC resilience gear" and "there's currently zero non-raid path to competitive PvP gear" — [[d:pvp-general#1426488616565215244]] through [[d:pvp-general#1426639905626259689]] (Loralius/cs from GRIZZLY/olympius_, 2025-10-11).
- **[Master Strike / mace disorient bug]** Whether this is an intentional design (some argue it's meant to be a strong CC given the mace/weapon investment) or simply an unfixed bug contradicting its own tooltip is unresolved; the prevailing community view is that it's a bug reflecting poor PvP QA — [[d:pvp-general#1427239312856191007]] (Purec, 2025-10-13; question unanswered by any dev-affiliated source in this chunk).
