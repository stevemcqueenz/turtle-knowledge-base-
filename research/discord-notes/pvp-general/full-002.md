- **[General/World PvP]** Upcoming/teased feature: PvP tower objectives in 3 locations, including a new fortress in Redridge, meant to revive world PvP — [[d:pvp-general#1432874589330870435]] (Jesse Potter, +2), [[d:pvp-general#1432874923835003010]] (Ghost9626), [[d:pvp-general#1433363691046309908]].

- **[General/PvP systems]** Cross-faction is already live in arenas but not in battlegrounds as of this chunk; the first cross-faction test caused multi-hour "infinite damage glitches" — [[d:pvp-general#1432850017235767306]], [[d:pvp-general#1432850206562586706]] (Ghost9626).
- **[Paladin]** Paladin T2 3-piece + Sulfuras hammer + Seal of Righteousness interaction (holy proc counted as a spell and doubled by SoR) has been "deemed a bug" by devs but is not being hotfixed, despite devs saying they wouldn't touch some things and doing so anyway — [[d:pvp-general#1432963511310352476]], [[d:pvp-general#1432963608312021130]] (Jesse Potter); Purec expects it fixed once he completes his own 3-piece (+ single source distrust) — [[d:pvp-general#1432964861373054997]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Warrior/Arms]** Arms struggles hard against Shamans: "arms fucking sucks against Shamans" — [[d:pvp-general#1432545990069453000]] (SkillzMFG); partial counter: Arms can do okay vs Enhancement specifically if it can land Disarm often — [[d:pvp-general#1432596969473445950]] (Spiff).
- **[Warrior/Protection]** Deep Protection is considered nearly useless offensively in PvP — "no reason to play prot vs fury (root break and heal) or arms (burst and Ms debuff)... a mediocre fc that fury can do better," only situationally justified for a dispel+silence utility bot or with a Titan's Grip/"TF" gear condition — [[d:pvp-general#1433017284896489524]] (limoseng, question), [[d:pvp-general#1433018154443079741]] (Jesse Potter), [[d:pvp-general#1433031089038954610]], [[d:pvp-general#1433031889727520818]] (Spiff). Counterpoint: a fully deep-Prot warrior can be an unkillable "walking juggernaut" midfielder that locks down casters even doing little damage (cites warrior "Alexxandros") — [[d:pvp-general#1432620070408028191]] (NoplanetB/Thilgand), [[d:pvp-general#1432595864710549605]], [[d:pvp-general#1432595924785299507]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Warrior general]** Overall warrior tier read: "warrior is rock bottom, paladins average, shamans god tier" — [[d:pvp-general#1433954354624466994]] (Ythor, +1); dual-wield warrior is said to perform "decently" in 1v1 — [[d:pvp-general#1433954312962445373]] (Stormtaurus); warrior needs a strong support setup (dispels, 2 healers) and top-end gear/weapon (Kara-level gear, Shieldrender talisman, BRE weapon) to be strong — [[d:pvp-general#1433957317082349668]] (Bipst), [[d:pvp-general#1433973970310267012]], [[d:pvp-general#1433974048752140358]] (dindunuffinz).
- **[Shaman/Elemental]** Considered too tanky/strong: molten/earth shock burst seen as excessive even against focus fire; community consensus favors an Earth Shock damage nerf (20–30%) compensated by a threat modifier buff so PvE elementals aren't gutted — [[d:pvp-general#1432877101744390295]] (lowlifeopinions), [[d:pvp-general#1432877937585029141]] (Ghost9626), [[d:pvp-general#1432878129881288877]] (SkillzMFG).
- **[Shaman/Enhancement]** Weapon Chain (not Crusader enchant) is the PvP-correct choice — "crusader its pve shit" — and lets Enhancement shrug off Disarm attempts (Warriors can't land Disarm on a Weapon Chain-equipped target) — [[d:pvp-general#1432641376398606356]], [[d:pvp-general#1432642117917736961]] (Gluom), [[d:pvp-general#1432642448622096424]], [[d:pvp-general#1432642920632025119]] (limoseng). Once Shamans get T2.5–T3 gear ("AQ+"), a set effect lets Frost Shock → Stormstrike "one shot any and all classes every 12 seconds," at roughly a 20% proc rate ("in classic that's basically 100%") — [[d:pvp-general#1432871847719862332]], [[d:pvp-general#1432872425225191545]], [[d:pvp-general#1432880334919307386]], [[d:pvp-general#1432880459142008912]] (Ghost9626).
- **[Paladin/Holy]** "Holy always been a pve spec" — Holy is mana-starved without gear ("after 5 Greater Heals you're almost oom," relying on a 20% mana-cost reduction to function) but trivial with T2 ("felt like i was cheating," never oom) — [[d:pvp-general#1432871223527997505]], [[d:pvp-general#1432871340951605418]], [[d:pvp-general#1432871354788479108]] (Bipst), [[d:pvp-general#1432871367874838528]].
- **[Paladin/Retribution]** Ret runs on a very small mana pool in PvP — ~2k baseline, ~3k unbuffed with a Ret's own set, and T2.5 (used by most rets) adds ~800 more (~3.8k total); rets play as frontline damage-soakers rather than mana-managed casters — [[d:pvp-general#1432596559308128367]] (question), [[d:pvp-general#1432597845525139506]], [[d:pvp-general#1432598559013994638]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Priest/Discipline]** Disc feels healer-unfriendly without a "30% increase" (talent/effect) — healing constantly feels crippled under Mortal Strike-type debuffs — [[d:pvp-general#1432871628789780491]] (Liracrime).
- **[Priest/Shadow]** Not considered overpowered because Shadow is "literally locked to a single school of spells unless they drop form" — [[d:pvp-general#1432881878385557626]] (Ghost9626); Affliction-style passive/range play is required to be effective — [[d:pvp-general#1432882353730093057]].
- **[Warlock/Demonology]** Ret Paladin + Felguard Warlock cited as possibly the best 2v2 arena comp, because Ret's Divine Protection + Hand of Protection counters other warlocks — [[d:pvp-general#1433954955731140648]] (Pastor). Felguard itself is "super inconsistent" — very strong in some fights, but can be walled off (e.g., standing on the pillar in Blade's Edge Arena) which doesn't work for melee comps — [[d:pvp-general#1433530820173238426]], [[d:pvp-general#1433531063644459049]] (lowlifeopinions).
- **[Rogue]** Anti-heal poison (analogous to TBC Aimed Shot's 50% heal reduction) is not really used by rogues in practice — [[d:pvp-general#1432902686868635658]] (Jesse Potter).

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[General PvP]** Lesser Invulnerability Potion ("LIP") is a major PvP balance complaint: ~2 minute cooldown for 6 seconds of complete physical damage immunity, cheap to buy and spammed constantly by casters/healers against melee, versus a warrior's spell-reflect trinket which is a single-school reflect on a 5-minute cooldown — [[d:pvp-general#1432945315501183097]], [[d:pvp-general#1432947715989311582]], [[d:pvp-general#1432948173634146487]] (betcover). It is not dispellable ("not a magical spell") — [[d:pvp-general#1432575275538841610]] (MoneyCow). Proposed (not implemented) fix floated in chat: delete LIP, add a physical-damage reflector instead (e.g., 100% parry for 6s) — [[d:pvp-general#1432949465546756216]] (Spiff), [[d:pvp-general#1432949560824827995]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Priest]** With ~450 spell power on Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Flay reportedly ticks for 500+ per tick, and the Blackout talent can proc back-to-back stuns while channeling — a notable Warrior/melee complaint about being chain-stunned while already taking heavy DoT/channel damage — [[d:pvp-general#1432865502086168700]], [[d:pvp-general#1432865528673992787]] (Jesse Potter); Bipst was skeptical of the 500/tick figure until shown math — [[d:pvp-general#1432865808249262151]], [[d:pvp-general#1432882324210585760]].
- **[Priest]** Smite Priest ("smite proc") burst is called out as landing ~5k damage in 3 seconds via Smite procs — [[d:pvp-general#1432866944234815609]] (Jesse Potter, single source).

## PvP
- **[Warrior vs Shaman]** Arms is weak vs Shamans generally (see Spec viability); disarming Enhancement Shamans is a key counter when possible — [[d:pvp-general#1432596969473445950]] (Spiff); 90% of non-Warrior 2h-weapon users can be Disarmed, but Weapon Chain grants immunity — [[d:pvp-general#1432642448622096424]] (limoseng).
- **[Elemental Shaman]** Considered too tanky and hard to burst down; players sometimes just CC and ignore very tanky Elemental/Enhancement shamans rather than fight them — [[d:pvp-general#1432595703317925888]] (NoplanetB/Thilgand), [[d:pvp-general#1432595864710549605]] (Jesse Potter). A totem-related proc ("Rekindled Flame" from a totem) was observed critting for ~2k — [[d:pvp-general#1432878024058994749]] (Jesse Potter, single source).
- **[Honor/Rank system]** Turtle uses the vanilla honor/rank pool formula unchanged except that rank can no longer decay below its current level; each rank still requires 5000 ranking points, and where you land in the pool determines ranking-point gain — [[d:pvp-general#1432895708855275520]], [[d:pvp-general#1433317299321831465]] (lowlifeopinions), [[d:pvp-general#1433332934047436951]], [[d:pvp-general#1433333393508012075]] (Jay). Disputed: Duszman claims progress per standing point gets slower at higher ranks on Turtle specifically (vs flat vanilla scaling) — [[d:pvp-general#1432893515439931402]], [[d:pvp-general#1432894026927046736]] — countered by lowlifeopinions attributing the slowness to a smaller PvP population/pool rather than a formula change — [[d:pvp-general#1432895798332358788]], [[d:pvp-general#1432898792419754025]].
- **[Honor/Rank system]** Reaching Rank 14 requires being consistently top ~3 (up to ~10 depending on pool size) among active honor farmers; being at the bottom of that group takes longer than being first — [[d:pvp-general#1433315466742595585]] (question), [[d:pvp-general#1433317539005595709]] (Spiff), [[d:pvp-general#1433330263802249268]] (MoneyCow). As of this chunk, players are progressing past R11 and the first batch of R14s is expected in ~2 weeks, though R12-14 rewards are gated until the BWL phase (phase 4 per an accompanying screenshot) — [[d:pvp-general#1432989915108347924]] (Nandieb), [[d:pvp-general#1432905560033988739]] (Duszman, image).
- **[Honor/Rank system]** BG mark-turn-in honor bonus on "bonus day" is a fixed amount regardless of how many marks (e.g. x3) are turned in at once — [[d:pvp-general#1432830542621179975]] (question), [[d:pvp-general#1432832789706833991]] (Xerq).
- **[Honor/Rank system]** Honor for kills is scaled by relative level within a bracket — a higher-level character within a bracket gets reduced honor for killing lower-level opponents, mirroring how lower-level characters struggle to land hits on higher-level targets — [[d:pvp-general#1433325309041705001]] (question), [[d:pvp-general#1433330237860614307]] (lowlifeopinions).
- **[Honor/Rank system]** Weekly rank reset occurs Wednesday (Tuesday-to-Wednesday, usually tied to server restart) — [[d:pvp-general#1433171524298477698]] (question), [[d:pvp-general#1433172440866881567]] (Meow).
- **[Honor/Rank system]** There is reportedly an NPC that can be paid to disable personal rank gain (useful for farming honor for gear without affecting rank standing); a player whose rank/standing didn't move despite 37k honor at reset may have had this active — [[d:pvp-general#1433298585620975821]] (Swindle), [[d:pvp-general#1433311184303685713]] (Valadorn, unconfirmed).
- **[Honor/Rank system, contested]** "Dishonorable ranks" (Dishonored/Exiled/Outlaw/Pariah, for farming low-level players/civilians — purely visual but requires re-earning lost honor) were apparently announced in a patch note, but at least one player claims the feature "was scrapped completely"; unclear if it's currently live — [[d:pvp-general#1433471444377145415]] (Starships, quoting patch text), [[d:pvp-general#1433477917282799767]] (SkillzMFG, disputed); historically at least one player ("Redridgeboss") reportedly reached the Pariah title by camping low-level players in Redridge — [[d:pvp-general#1433493523516362802]] (Nandieb), [[d:pvp-general#1433508387404120134]], [[d:pvp-general#1433508575921180753]] (Nandieb).
