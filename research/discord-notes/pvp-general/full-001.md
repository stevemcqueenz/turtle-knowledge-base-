- **[Cross-faction — CONFIRMED]** An official forum post confirmed cross-faction Battlegrounds are coming: "players, depending on team imbalance in a given Battleground, may end up fighting for the opposite faction for that game, to increase fairness and reduce queue times" (quoted from the announcement) — [[d:pvp-general#1431787543753396326]], [[d:pvp-general#1432396649132720139]], forum link https://forum.turtle-wow.org/viewtopic.php?t=22577 [[d:pvp-general#1432458169787089057]]. Explicitly framed by the devs as a population/queue-time fix, not a class-balance fix — [[d:pvp-general#1432137251378434192]] (Lovk quoting), [[d:pvp-general#1432137115453620236]] (Beardyman). Exact mechanics unclear/undocumented at chunk end: leading community guess is that only the "overflow" players from the more-populated faction get placed on the other team (so one full-faction team + one mixed team), not a fully randomized/merged pool — [[d:pvp-general#1432276416812159077]] (Nandieb, speculation). Open concerns raised: whether same-faction kills under the temporary mixed team will correctly grant/deny Honor Kills (unfixed as of chunk end, per Jesse Potter) — [[d:pvp-general#1432394716996964483]]. A "wPvP tower objectives at 3 locations" feature was also mentioned as tied to this update — [[d:pvp-general#1432050053916262460]] (Jesse Potter). Community reaction is split: PvP-focused posters largely support it (queue times, faction balance) — [[d:pvp-general#1432089806263029861]] (+9), [[d:pvp-general#1432393420005052597]] (+5); RP-focused opponents object strongly, citing lore/immersion concerns and comparing it unfavorably to a proposed "mercenary" (opt-in reduced-honor cross-faction tag) alternative that wasn't chosen — [[d:pvp-general#1432387374834384916]] (Gnomeburger Orgon), [[d:pvp-general#1432402247769264269]] (+18 reactions, "Unapologetically proud Shammy").

This removed one of Ret Paladin's few tools vs. Elemental Shaman (previously could Purge-lock Ret's seals for free) — [[d:pvp-general#1428261669062180864]] (Purec), [[d:pvp-general#1429598053219958945]] (Kyrsie wants it reverted) vs. [[d:pvp-general#1429598148325543966]] (SkillzMFG: one of the better changes). - **[Shaman — nerf history]** Cumulative Shaman nerfs cited across the chunk: Windfury can no longer proc off itself (no self-chaining), Grounding Totem restored to sharing the GCD (20s CD) after a period where it had no GCD and could be spammed, Electrify removed/nerfed, Orc/Tauren shaman racials nerfed, a "spell batching" nerf, and Nature's Swiftness nerfed — [[d:pvp-general#1431134232293408919]], [[d:pvp-general#1431135544313909259]] (dindunuffinz), [[d:pvp-general#1431741144215322824]] (Nandieb).

## Rotation: single target

- **[Paladin/Ret]** Burst-on-healer combo: when Seal of Justice (stun) procs, follow with Seal of Command Judgement for double damage; chain Seal of Justice stun + a stun-proc weapon (C'Thun's Axe, 3s disorient) since Ret lacks a hard interrupt outside Hammer of Justice/Repentance/a Grenade — [[d:pvp-general#1430216235202777238]], [[d:pvp-general#1429499781864095794]], [[d:pvp-general#1431318898795610112]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Paladin]** Old (historically strong, now considered a relic build) Crusader Strike-spam tactic: since Crusader Strike and Holy Strike didn't share a cooldown, a fast one-hander with multiple Spellstrike-proc items let a Ret spam-interrupt/pushback healers; each Spellstrike proc counts as an individual pushback hit — [[d:pvp-general#1431319961116348466]], [[d:pvp-general#1431319840538361856]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[Rogue]** Standard chain vs. a target: Cheap Shot > Noxious [Assault poison-proc] (repeat) > Kidney Shot > Noxious > Eviscerate; Noxious Assault does 800-1200 dmg per hit (crit-dependent) on top of autoattacks (80-120 phys + ~200-240 poison) — [[d:pvp-general#1431780351897768066]], [[d:pvp-general#1431780998143545425]], [[d:pvp-general#1431781089310937139]] (Jesse Potter). A 5-combo-point Rupture followed by Envenom (using the free combo point Rupture grants) is also cited as a strong finisher pattern — [[d:pvp-general#1431780366720700578]] (Noctissal).
- **[Warrior/Arms vs Hunter]** Open with Intercept+Disarm together (guarantees Disarm lands even if it would otherwise be resisted/dodged separately), then Piercing Howl on the same engagement before they escape; expect a 100% chance of an Explosive Trap on the Hunter's opener — bring a reflector; if you can't reach the Hunter, hit their pet for sustain — [[d:pvp-general#1429580338149064857]], [[d:pvp-general#1429582078873440357]], [[d:pvp-general#1429583147233968230]] (Spiff).
- **[Priest vs Warrior]** vs. a Fury Warrior, bait their Berserker Rage (their fear-break/root-break, ~30s CD, usable only in Berserker Stance) with a Fear first, then land a Net (engineering) right after; vs. Arms (2H, Battle Stance), you can Net immediately since they lack the same root-break, though Arms in Battle Stance can also simply be Feared if they don't swap stance; Deathwish (Fury talent) reportedly grants fear immunity in any stance — [[d:pvp-general#1432168724714229770]], [[d:pvp-general#1432169106680840213]], [[d:pvp-general#1432169367419883642]] (Spiff/Liracrime).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General/Resilience]** Bug: resilience's crit-damage reduction only reduces the *crit portion*, not the *total* hit, making it roughly half as effective as expected (3% resil: expected 1940 from a 2000 crit, actual ~1970; 20% resil: expected 1600, actual ~1800). Filed as bug #17804 — [[d:pvp-general#1428416707600449596]] (Pastor), math [[d:pvp-general#1428417638517968916]], [[d:pvp-general#1428417841912090827]].
- **[General/Resilience]** DoT-tick resilience reduction under-delivers at low values and improves as resilience rises (6% resil → ~4% tick reduction; 9%→~7%; 21%→~20%) — [[d:pvp-general#1428422932392181912]] (Tapka, testing not exhaustive).
- **[General]** Spell hit does not counteract target resistances (resistances scale off level difference; Spell Penetration is needed for that instead). Some "% chance to resist" talent wording for shadow-school spells actually grants Spell Hit Rating specifically to shadow spells (unless changed on Turtle) — [[d:pvp-general#1429052861924511794]], [[d:pvp-general#1429054236242477167]] (lowlifeopinions).
- **[General]** Global PvP damage dampening of roughly 20% is in effect and is regularly cited as reducing burst/sustain (e.g., Warlock Soulfire burst, Warrior rage generation) — [[d:pvp-general#1431769867446390886]] (Noctissal), [[d:pvp-general#1429938922296709352]] (Bipst notes Dampening also hurts Warrior rage gen specifically).
- **[Warrior]** Best two-hander generates 8 rage per swing baseline (cited as an unchanged base mechanic; weapon-speed changes shift this, e.g. ~22-23 rage/auto range referenced) — [[d:pvp-general#1429596348671590471]] (Kyrsie).
- **[Paladin/Holy]** Spiritual Focus + Concentration Aura together give 105% spell-pushback resistance, effectively guaranteeing casts land if timed with the aura up — [[d:pvp-general#1430590994587586620]], [[d:pvp-general#1432115003229475056]] (Jesse Potter/Kyrsie: "anything below 100% pushback resistance feels like 0%").
- **[Movement speed]** Movement-speed bonuses do not stack — the highest value always overwrites the others (e.g., a boot movement enchant does not stack with Pursuit of Justice) — [[d:pvp-general#1430556698069696622]] (Pastor).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Reflector trinkets]** School-specific "Reflector" items (Nature/Fire/Shadow/Arcane) are a core PvP tool. Mechanic: any reflector sends a projectile back to its caster once it *reaches you*, so closing distance reflects it faster; instant/no-travel-time spells (e.g. Rupture) reflect immediately; Arcane Missiles must be waited out per-missile — [[d:pvp-general#1431274586690945155]], [[d:pvp-general#1431274725337862284]] (Jesse Potter). Usage tactics: bait Shaman Earth Shock by popping Nature Reflector then hard-casting (they "shock themself") — noticeable animation, so good Shamans interrupt fast — [[d:pvp-general#1429480802374254612]], [[d:pvp-general#1429481402633556028]]; Fire Reflector took a Shaman from 90%→20% HP off one hard-cast — [[d:pvp-general#1430034446089977958]]; Arcane Reflector can solo-kill a channeling Mage via reflected Arcane Missiles, or reflect a Polymorph back onto the caster while you escape unaffected — [[d:pvp-general#1429481205992263895]], [[d:pvp-general#1430238717053767690]], [[d:pvp-general#1430238790642827317]]. A full defensive stack (Nature Reflector + Scarab [trinket] + Loatheb's reflection + Fire/Nature resist aura) can reach ~170 resist, further boosted to ~224 by swapping to HoR trinket + a Legendary neck (+54 Fire, +24 Nature) — [[d:pvp-general#1431315345657823414]] (Jesse Potter, very detailed).
- **[Bug/quirk]** Arcane Missiles' channel reportedly ignores line-of-sight breaks (keeps hitting through LoS); community wants a damage penalty added for LoS-completed casts — [[d:pvp-general#1429043495846543411]], [[d:pvp-general#1430239289106497678]] (Verlyn/AxenPunker).
- **[Shaman]** Totem rules: Grounding Totem has a GCD + 20s cooldown; Fire Nova Totem has a GCD; other totems (e.g. Searing Totem) have no GCD and can be spammed — called "kinda dumb" — [[d:pvp-general#1429182241992343736]] (AxenPunker). Fire Nova Totem crits near 2k if not interrupted; Searing Totem hits ~400/tick — [[d:pvp-general#1428153361936683139]], [[d:pvp-general#1428153225248505907]]. Visual/targeting complaint: up to ~20 totems can clutter the screen, breaking pet-AI targeting and tab-targeting (needing many tab presses to cycle past totems to the player) — [[d:pvp-general#1432146504205992017]], [[d:pvp-general#1432146803968708664]] (Lovk/dindunuffinz). ## PvP
- **[BG general]** PvP XP tooltip is bugged: displays "0.3%/0.36% XP per 300 points" but actually grants ~3% (10x displayed), making low-effort BG grinding roughly 10x faster than intended for leveling — [[d:pvp-general#1428859351526801598]], [[d:pvp-general#1428860098465239112]] (hei123).
- **[Honor/Rank system — detailed mechanics]** Weekly Honor earns you a percentile "standing" among all players that week; standing (not raw honor) determines Rank Points (RP) gained, on a curve relative to how many others are competing and how much honor they got. RP determines Rank: each rank costs 5,000 RP, max 13,000 RP obtainable per week, and you decay 20% of accumulated RP each week regardless. Net effect: someone with far more honor/HKs than a rival can still gain less RP if the honor pool that week is more competitive; at low ranks, modest-but-consistent honor climbs a little (decay bites less); at high ranks (11+), decay is punishing and requires sustained near-max weekly honor over multiple weeks — [[d:pvp-general#1431888462340030474]], [[d:pvp-general#1431889176281878582]], [[d:pvp-general#1431892681055015032]] (Nandieb, extensive), corroborated/summarized by [[d:pvp-general#1431893140523978832]], [[d:pvp-general#1431893459006001312]], [[d:pvp-general#1431893910917087314]] (Fortystabs(H)). Confirmed separately: you can lose progress within your current rank but cannot drop to a lower rank; ~15 games/week needed to "beat decay" at rank 6 — [[d:pvp-general#1429567563293003797]], [[d:pvp-general#1429569365237432431]] (SkillzMFG/Bipst).
- **[Honor/Rank system]** 100,000 Honorable Kills grants a separate title track independent of Rank — [[d:pvp-general#1432036652934496377]], [[d:pvp-general#1432034233810944090]] (Jesse Potter/MoneyCow). Same-faction kills never grant Honor Kills — [[d:pvp-general#1431261583589244968]] (Cysthen). Damage-based honor counts while in a party, within ~50y in a BG, or in open-world without a party; you must be within 12 levels of the target to get honor from a kill — [[d:pvp-general#1431614220197171381]], [[d:pvp-general#1431615418652557343]] (Jesse Potter). Dishonorable Kills (DHK, for ganking low levels) have been disabled on Turtle for as long as current players recall — [[d:pvp-general#1431620458100555776]], [[d:pvp-general#1431620503683993750]] (Kyrsie). Weekly PvP reset is Tuesday night → Wednesday, EU time — [[d:pvp-general#1431662006233989313]] (Pastor). Honor cap is not a fixed number — it's set relative to whatever the top player earns that week — [[d:pvp-general#1431228403532562515]] (Jesse Potter).
- **[BG bonus-day system]** A daily BG bonus rotates (checkable via turtle-timer.vercel.app or city emissaries): AV → WSG → AB → Bloodring → Sunnyglade. To farm a specific reputation/marks efficiently, queue only that BG on its bonus day, save marks, then turn them in for a bonus "greater honor" quest reward on the relevant day — [[d:pvp-general#1431254059167383613]], [[d:pvp-general#1431906563135897722]] (Jaki/Nandieb). PvP marks are turned in at the capital-city ("King[s]") location regardless of class, granting rep + honor; the separate PvP Hall (requires Rank 6 to enter) does not accept mark turn-ins — [[d:pvp-general#1431263535572324362]], [[d:pvp-general#1431268942911111300]] (limoseng).
- **[Cross-faction]** See "1.18.1 changes" section above for the full confirmed announcement and community reaction.
- **[Twinking/leveling PvP]** Widely reported that levels 19 and 29 are effectively unplayable for non-twinks due to fully-enchanted, consumable-stacked twinks; level 49 used to be the most balanced bracket (enchants mattered less) but is no longer considered fun either — [[d:pvp-general#1432290422553575557]] (Gnomeburger Orgon, detailed). Low/mid-level Ret Paladins are considered especially strong twinks since Crusader Strike is obtainable as low as ~level 20 (self-corrected mid-thread from an initial claim of level 7/30s), well ahead of Shaman's Windfury (Totem) at level 30-32 — [[d:pvp-general#1430210562020606123]], [[d:pvp-general#1430213048236507137]] (dindunuffinz).
- **[BG tactics — WSG]** A premade of "1 Rogue, 2 Priest, 2 Mage" running the Mage as flag carrier (using Mage mobility/Ice Block) is claimed to win every WSG — [[d:pvp-general#1429916187760005280]] (Grillbe). Any Horde player with a Swiftness Potion is called a "god-tier" FC (cited example: 8 Paladins with only +8% run speed can't catch up) — [[d:pvp-general#1430237626253971526]], [[d:pvp-general#1430237741005930697]] (Pastor). Support-oriented leveling-PvP roles recommended for fun without needing kills: Rogue (Sap/stun-combo/Blind to take 2-3 people out of an initial fight even without landing kills), Mage/Warlock (CC support, less escape than Rogue), Priest (deny enemy kills via dispel/heals) — [[d:pvp-general#1432069563406618725]], [[d:pvp-general#1432069743187329085]], [[d:pvp-general#1432070443640033403]].
- **[BG general — faction imbalance]** Horde reported to win ~80% of WSGs by multiple posters (community perception, not official data) — [[d:pvp-general#1428089216277156022]], [[d:pvp-general#1429914942236135577]]. Alliance-side complaint: Alliance BGs frequently face 4+ Shamans (or heavy Priest/Warlock/Mage stacks) — [[d:pvp-general#1430031803552632904]], [[d:pvp-general#1430033469030924478]] (Rhiina/betcover). Horde queue times reported to often exceed an hour while Alliance queues are near-instant — [[d:pvp-general#1429522141438808234]] (Bipst). lowlifeopinions counters that Horde has "overwhelmingly positive win rates in pug-v-pug BGs across vanilla-based servers generally" (not Turtle-specific) — [[d:pvp-general#1432137069626392668]].
- **[World PvP]** Warmode/open-world PvP is reported as virtually nonexistent on the main server (Nordanaar) and likely similarly thin on Ambershire; Tel'Abim (a dedicated PvP-ruleset server) is recommended for those wanting it but even there it's reportedly thin, mostly raid-exit ganking — real vanilla-style world PvP would need whole-faction flagging, not just a handful of players — [[d:pvp-general#1430917599864291429]], [[d:pvp-general#1430924834056962068]], [[d:pvp-general#1430965763233153075]] (Zestitcha/1.18.1 WILL SAVE US/Temu).
- **[Class-specific matchup]** Mage vs Warlock (formulaic at T3+ gear, per lowlifeopinions): opening Arcane Missiles/Rupture should be met by the Lock's Spell Lock (risky if it resists); Mage can Ice Block through the interrupt window; a second Arcane Missiles can be answered with Death Coil if up; if the Lock has a second answer (e.g. double Ice-Block-equivalent), the matchup gets very hard for the Mage. At T3+ with a Scythe (of Elune), a single completed Arcane Missiles channel can win outright — [[d:pvp-general#1430030401585287208]], [[d:pvp-general#1430024181017219153]] (corroborated from the Lock's POV by dindunuffinz).
