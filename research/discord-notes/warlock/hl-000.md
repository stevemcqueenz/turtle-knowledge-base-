## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
(No 1.18.1 content in this chunk — see era note above. The following are the CC2-era patch notes/dev statements actually recorded, kept for trajectory context.)
- **[All specs]** CC2 (launched 2024-11-01) removed Dark Pact (mana-drain-from-pet capstone) and reworked most warlock talent trees; widely experienced as a large net nerf relative to the prior "DS/Ruin" (CC1) meta — [[d:warlock#1308910220348493824]] (Valaen, 2024-11-21), [[d:warlock#1308627662440497163]] (Sarimal).
- **[Demon changes]** Early CC2 patch note: Demon Gate (renamed from Demon Portal) 30 min CD, 4s cast, no longer ritual — drops an object that spawns a Felguard after 6s, 1 min duration; Inferno CD cut 20→10 min, cast 2→1.5s, 30s duration; Ritual of Doom required players 4→2, no longer randomly kills a participant, 1 min duration — [[d:warlock#1302081486765297754]] (Ardonir, 2024-11-02).
- **[Balance patch, mid-Nov 2024]** Searing Pain base damage +10% (all ranks); Drain Soul spellpower scaling 16.67%→20%, mana cost −20% (all ranks), and its damage-buildup-over-duration was removed (flat damage per tick instead); Dark Harvest spellpower scaling 20%→25%; Nightfall changed to a 2-point talent at 2/4% proc chance (was 1/3/5%); Imp's Firebolt coefficients reduced — [[d:warlock#1308629214701944845]] (SkimBeeble copying patch notes, 2024-11-20; confirmed by Ardonir [[d:warlock#1308636881843654759]]).
- **[Greater demons, Dec 2024 patch]** Infernal HP 3544→7500, Felguard HP 3832→8500, Doomguard HP 4517→9500; Unleashed Potential reworked to "direct-damage crits grant a 20s Unleashed Potential stack (refreshed by funnel ticks); each stack gives your demon 3/6/9% of your bonus spell damage, stacks to 5"; Infernal's healing-taken-reduction debuff increased 50%→75% (live/reference value was 15%) — [[d:warlock#1319549210974097450]] (Sarimal quoting patch notes, 2024-12-20).
- **[T2.5 Affliction set nerf, Jan 2025]** Doomcaller's set had armor converted to stamina and stats trimmed across all 5 pieces (e.g. Crown: armor 250→100, +19 Stam, Int 29→28, Hit 2%→1%; Leggings: armor 277→107, +22 Stam, Int 33→29, hit removed) — called "butchered" by the community — [[d:warlock#1333959253500756019]] (2025-01-29).
- **[Late-March 2025 patch, largest single nerf to Demo/SL]** Removed the Demonic Sacrifice + Greater Demon interaction (~flat 6% DPS loss), added a 10% damage-taken-and-dealt reduction while Felguard's MD buff is active, cut Felguard's Legion Strike base scaling 130%→50% weapon damage (partially offset by it now being able to crit and CD 6→4s), and completely removed Black Book trinket pre-pull swapping — [[d:warlock#1356044892131819701]] (Nezu/Papaverine/Mooncrazed, 2025-03-31), corroborated with the "why" reasoning (Black Book was balanced around vanilla's 50 dps demons, not Turtle's ~500 dps demons; Power Overwhelming was always 20%, never 30%) by [[d:warlock#1368533456254013470]] (Deleted User, 2025-05-03).
- **[Dark Harvest change, Apr 2025]** Dark Harvest now refreshes its duration if the target dies mid-cast — flagged by players as clashing with Demonic Sacrifice interactions and gear planning — [[d:warlock#1357102255681175644]] (Karen, 2025-04-03).
- **[Item procs, May 2025]** Multiple on-proc trinkets/items updated to trigger from any successful harmful/helpful spell cast rather than requiring the spell to land direct damage/healing — [[d:warlock#1367595337014968481]] (Ziggie quoting patch notes, 2025-05-02).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[SM/Ruin]** Consistently the "gold standard"/generalist spec across the whole period: "middle of the pack... better than fire but worse than SL on bosses, better than demo but worse than fire on trash" — [[d:warlock#1334240884228886650]] (Nezu, 2025-01-29; +3 reactions).
- **[Destro Fire]** Fast, bursty, no-pet spec; best for trash/dungeons/speedrunning, weaker on long fights, very gear (crit) dependent — [[d:warlock#1334240911860957216]] (Nezu, 2025-01-29).
- **[Soul Link Demo]** After the Dec-2024–Mar-2025 tuning cycle, SL/Demo became the top-parsing spec in Naxx (and expected to stay strong for several Kara40 bosses); high skill-cap (pet micromanagement, cooldown planning) — [[d:warlock#1334241026902065183]] (Nezu, 2025-01-29), confirmed still true 2025-04/05 — [[d:warlock#1364582403041005621]] (Nezu, 2025-04-23).
- **[Affliction/Drain (DH/DS)]** Weakest spec for the entire chunk period, especially on trash (no real AoE, DoTs barely tick before mobs die); still playable/competitive on long boss fights with BiS haste gear, per dedicated Affliction players — [[d:warlock#1330211878022156319]] (Noheartrate, "affli is dead weight without ISB uptime help", 2025-01-18) vs [[d:warlock#1364582403041005621]] (Nezu: "damage isn't abysmal if taken in a vacuum, some say it's BiS for some Kara40 prog fights — I disagree", disputed).
- **[Endgame instance/boss spec routing, repeated by multiple raiders through Apr–May 2025]** Dungeons/ZG/Kara10/AQ20: Fire; MC: SM/Ruin (or Demo); BWL: Demo (SM/Ruin also fine); AQ40: Fire until Ouro, Demo for Ouro/C'thun; Naxx: Demo except Gluth & Thaddius (SM/Ruin, or Affli if BiS); Kara40: all fights unfavorable for Demo — Fire on the last boss (and first, if undergeared), SM/Ruin or Affli elsewhere — [[d:warlock#1376947779481632842]], [[d:warlock#1378713254410322020]] (Nezu, 2025-05-26/06-01).
- **[Demo/Kara40]** Demo specifically weak in Kara40 progression because raid-wide AoE shreds the pet on the first several bosses, forcing constant healer babysitting of the pet — [[d:warlock#1359100959640518686]], [[d:warlock#1359101593320034460]] (Nezu, 2025-04-08).
- **[Melee/wand-weave warlock]** Meme/challenge build only — pet does ~50% of total damage, "fun but not good," never presented as competitive — [[d:warlock#1330358458146623540]] (olly613, 2025-01-19).

## Leveling
- **[All specs, near-universal early pick]** 5/5 Improved Corruption (instant cast) + 2/2 Life Tap + 2/2 Improved Drains is treated as close to mandatory for leveling regardless of eventual spec — [[d:warlock#1315778311959351417]] (sailorVEVO, 2024-12-10), [[d:warlock#1346250973256876053]] (DemonBoyJr, 2025-03-04).
- **[Demo leveling]** With 15 points into Demo for 5/5 Fel Stamina + 2/2 Soul Funnel, a Voidwalker is "effectively immortal" and can pull 3+ mobs at once; Soul Funnel heals 20% of the pet's missing HP per tick, so even a Rank 1 Health Funnel heals 100+ HP/tick — [[d:warlock#1307088266150805636]], [[d:warlock#1307088464440721582]] (Pandion, 2024-11-16; +4 each).
- **[Drain-tank leveling loop]** Instant Corruption + 2/2 Life Tap + 2/2 Improved Drains: send Voidwalker to build threat, dot the target, Life Tap for mana, Drain Life to recover HP — net-positive on both mana and HP while leveling/questing; Dark Pact (mana-from-pet) is removed on Turtle unlike vanilla drain-tank guides — [[d:warlock#1346250973256876053]] (DemonBoyJr, 2025-03-04), [[d:warlock#1355671429781393601]] (Asharu quoting a community doc, 2025-03-30).
- **[Demo leveling caveat]** Demo is the slowest-leveling spec and poor for dungeon groups specifically — trash dies before dots/pet ramp up; Improved Stones "not worth it" while leveling; prefer Voidwalker, grab instant Corruption from Affliction first — [[d:warlock#1372491095308767242]] (Mirror, 2025-05-15).
- **[Affliction/Demo hybrid for multi-elite soloing]** Voidwalker tanks 1–2 elites while dotting, heal via Rank 1 Health Funnel, fear a third mob if needed; for pure leveling speed, go Affliction to 5/5 Fel Concentration then pivot into Demonology pet talents, prioritize Shadoweave Tailoring gear once available — [[d:warlock#1371343966510780539]], [[d:warlock#1371345124067639296]] (sailorVEVO, 2025-05-12); this route is reported to make Houndmaster (BRD) soloable around level 52 for its cloak drop.
- **[Race pick, contested]** Orc (5% pet damage + extra spellpower) vs Gnome (5% Int → indirect pet crit via pet Int scaling) for Demo leveling/pets — no consensus reached — [[d:warlock#1351958254657470536]] (Reprobated, 2025-03-19; single source, unanswered).

## Rotation: single target
- **[SM/Ruin, precise timing]** Example lag-free immolate rotation with Curse of Recklessness/Shadow/Elements: trinket pop at 0s (20s snapshot window) → Corruption 1.5s → Immolate 3s → Shadow Bolt ×5 (2.5s casts, 5.5–18s) → Immolate 19.5s → Corruption refresh; alternate build precasts Immolate 1.5s before pull when running Curse of Doom/Agony — [[d:warlock#1321285069276446811]] (30, 2024-12-24).
- **[Affliction, standard endgame rotation]** Assigned curse → trinket + Curse of Agony → Corruption → Siphon Life → Dark Harvest → Drain Soul, then (haste/trinket-dependent) clip Agony/Corruption to push another snapshotted set before snapshotting the next Drain Soul; commonly abbreviated "DH/DS" — [[d:warlock#1355696295704269001]] (harvestmoon, 2025-03-30).
- **[Destro Fire opener/rotation, tested]** CoE → Soulfire → Immolate → Conflagrate → Shadowburn → Death Coil → Corruption, Searing Pain as filler; measured 697–774 dps across several talent splits (e.g. 0-18-33, 9-0-42) with imp OOM as the limiting factor — [[d:warlock#1302707446929821777]] (Spasmaticus, 2024-11-03).
- **[Destro Fire, Conflagrate usage]** Casting Conflagrate back-to-back is inefficient because it costs extra GCDs re-applying Immolate; better to let Immolate tick fully and only Conflag at the very last moment before it expires — [[d:warlock#1302748061730078811]] (Sarungal, 2024-11-04).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Hellfire, universal AoE tool]** Costs melee range (110% threat threshold for the caster vs a mage's 40% Arcane threat reduction), roots the caster, drains the caster's own HP, and costs ~1.3k mana upfront — repeatedly flagged as needing a buff relative to its downsides — [[d:warlock#1312777697319194707]] (Deleted User, 2024-12-01; +5).
- **[Affliction AoE gap]** Affliction has effectively no real AoE tool beyond Hellfire (no Seed-of-Corruption-style dot spread on Turtle in this period) — cited repeatedly as a top complaint, unresolved in this chunk — [[d:warlock#1320465465373622346]] (Noctissal, 2024-12-22; disputed by Twospooky as "trash gets melted, no meaningful AoE gap" [[d:warlock#1320494286990610603]]).
- **[Rain of Fire vs Blizzard, comparison]** RoF: ~1200 mana for ~900 dmg (75% mana), 2s ticks, only 10%/26% chance of slow/stun split across 4 ticks. Blizzard: ~1400 mana for ~1200 dmg (85% mana), 1s ticks, reliable slow — RoF judged inferior — [[d:warlock#1312785913289506826]] (Deleted User, 2024-12-01; single source).
- **[Leveling cleave]** For dungeon-cleave groups, apply Curse of Recklessness to gauge pull size, or Hellfire where possible, against caster-heavy vs melee-heavy packs — [[d:warlock#1302996694522396753]] (Zulltak/Nagrak, 2024-11-08).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[SM/Ruin]** Base guidance: hit > crit, haste not valued; but exact value is gear-dependent — "the more SP you have, the more valuable hit is" (no fixed conversion) — [[d:warlock#1319750309114941470]] (sertyu222, 2024-12-20).
- **[General stat weights, Feb 2025]** 1% crit ≈ 15–16 SP; 1% hit ≈ 17–18 SP; hit remains better than crit until ~15% hit, at which point hit's marginal value drops to 0 — [[d:warlock#1343823407669706784]], [[d:warlock#1343824464357625926]] (Nezu, 2025-02-25).
- **[Affliction haste value]** ~1 haste ≈ 5 SP at ~350 SP/6% hit gear level, scaling up to ~1 haste ≈ 10 SP at full T3 + consumables; 1 haste is claimed to never exceed ~11 SP of value — [[d:warlock#1366380472921358377]] (RioJnfer, 2025-04-28; single source, disputed elsewhere as haste being weak because it shortens DoT duration without adding ticks, e.g. [[d:warlock#1326494435533393971]] neuroblocker).
- **[Haste formula, dev-confirmed]** Turtle's actual haste formula is `duration × (1 − haste%)`, not `duration / (1 + haste%)` — i.e. 6% haste = base×0.94; confirmed with dev (Haaxor) input — [[d:warlock#1352725761559232572]] (harvestmoon, 2025-03-21).
- **[Affliction stat priority]** Hit (target 16%) > haste > SP; crit is considered a near-dead stat for Affliction since DoTs/drains can't crit under normal talents — [[d:warlock#1355696295704269001]] (harvestmoon, 2025-03-30; disputed at length by Deleted User earlier who argued giving drains crit would be "ridiculous" for game balance [[d:warlock#1312653615869067330]]).
- **[Spell penetration stacking example, SM/Ruin]** K10 head/leg enchants (25 spellpen × 2) + T2.5 shoulders (10) + T2.5 chest (20) + Veil of Eclipse (10) + T3 pants (10) + Ring of Swarming Thought w/ +6 spellpen enchant (26) = 126 total spellpen, plus 75 from Curse of Shadow — [[d:warlock#1369306388597047396]] (Nezu, 2025-05-06).
- **[Innate resist mechanic vs higher-level mobs]** Server code excerpt: `resistModHitChance += (8.0 × levelDiff) × playerLevel / 63.0` for creature targets — extra innate resist scales with level difference and caster level — [[d:warlock#1371893357353369611]] (Pepopo posting server source, 2025-05-13).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[T3/T3.5 itemization]** Warlocks don't need heavy hit investment on Turtle the way classic guides assume (without world buffs you're further from the crit cap); Polarity pants called "significantly better" than T3.5 pants despite the latter's hit — [[d:warlock#1303640407791370241]] (Volcker, 2024-11-06; +1... widely echoed later).
- **[Kara40-era SM/Ruin comparison]** Ysh'golar + T3.5 Legs (71 SP, 2% crit, 4% hit) judged marginally better than T3.5 Head + Polarity (85 SP, 5% crit, 4% hit) for players progressing from Naxx gear — [[d:warlock#1369293121850183702]] (Nezu, 2025-05-06).
- **[Community gear-planning tool]** Shareable Kara40-era gear planner (pepopo978.gitlab.io/twow-gear-planner) with a worked SM/Ruin example reaching ~522 SP (556 shadow), 10 hit, 20.28 crit from T2+T2.5+blues+AQ20 neck/ring/Rockfury bracers/Bloodvine pants (no trinkets/enchants) — [[d:warlock#1376885595384713277]] (Gulolio, 2025-05-26).
- **[Trinkets]** Black Book was "the biggest baddest trinket for Demo by a country mile" before being nerfed ~50% and losing pre-pull-swap viability in the March 2025 patch (see changes section) — [[d:warlock#1368281414302306464]] (Lightemgee, 2025-05-03).
- **[Epic mount weapon: Scythe]** Full proc breakdown: 2% spell hit, 2% spell crit, 40 SP passive; 5% chance on cast of 500–650 arcane damage plus a school-specific debuff (e.g. Shadow: −25% healing taken, 200 mana/150 shadow dmg per second; Fire: +2% crit taken, +400 fire dmg on crit) for 10s, all effects amplified +8% by the scythe's own debuff; separate Summon proc gives party +10% haste for 8s (10 min CD) — [[d:warlock#1370791130290983053]] (Lyrazia, 2025-05-10).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Fel Domination]** Nerfed from ~0.5s to 2s cast time in CC2 — one of the most-hated single changes, cited across the whole chunk as making pet-resummon reactive play much clunkier — [[d:warlock#1305942806799585371]] (Darg, 2024-11-19), [[d:warlock#1304740500359741512]] (Fuggle, 2024-11-16).
- **[Demonic Sacrifice / Greater Demons]** The sac-a-lesser-demon-for-a-buff talent's damage bonus was nerfed repeatedly (15%→~3-9% across patches) and its interaction with Greater Demons was fully removed in the March-2025 patch (see changes), permanently locking Affliction and Destro out of using Infernal/Felguard/Doomguard as minor offensive CDs — [[d:warlock#1359735459315580999]] (Nezu, 2025-04-10).
- **[Greater demon control mechanic]** Summoned via a consumable reagent; control-break is checked on a roughly ~18s interval; Soul Link makes them only break at max duration; once broken free they cannot be re-enslaved by anyone, and the "break free" attacks the summoner by design (an intentional but heavily contested "flavor" drawback) — [[d:warlock#1328393889253425235]] (Zulltak/Nagrak, 2025-01-13).
- **[ISB]** Improved Shadow Bolt's proc mechanic changed repeatedly across the chunk (crit-only → hit-or-crit without stack consumption); the SM/Ruin↔Shadow Priest ISB/Shadow-Weaving synergy was a recurring balance flashpoint the whole period — [[d:warlock#1335103290542784533]] (Deleted User summarizing the current state, 2025-02-01).
- **[Health/Soul Funnel]** Heals the pet for 20% of its missing HP per tick — a very strong, cheap pet-sustain tool even at Rank 1 — [[d:warlock#1307088464440721582]] (Pandion, 2024-11-16).

## PvP
- **[Pet choice]** Succubus (Seduction) recommended as the primary PvP pet — long-range CC/interrupt utility, turn off Lash of Pain; Felhunter only if facing stealth classes; Voidwalker only if running Soul Link with high armor/dodge plus the Voidwalker trinket — [[d:warlock#1315175310509084793]] (Twospooky, 2024-12-08).
- **[Greater demons in PvP]** Infernal favored for group fights (higher DPS, AoE aura, fire immunity, "clubbing"); Doomguard favored for utility (bleed, cripple, AoE stun) against casters or when kiting is needed — [[d:warlock#1377883333459181629]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-05-30).
- **[Warlock PvP fragility]** Warlocks are unusually susceptible to spell pushback/interrupts due to having very little instant damage; Curse of Exhaustion (via a dip into Affliction) recommended over Shadowburn for BG survivability/kiting — [[d:warlock#1377404879655534724]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-05-29).
- **[Matchup notes, post-CC2 buffs to other classes]** Warriors' new fear-immunity and Shaman melee burst (reported 3k crits) cited as difficult post-CC2 PvP matchups — [[d:warlock#1375036251085996062]] (Urnightmare, 2025-05-22; single source).
- **[Resilient Shadows / Howl of Terror]** Both widely called too weak to justify their talent-point cost in PvP (dispel-resist niche is rarely relevant; Howl's range/cast time make it hard to land) — repeated buff requests, no resolution shown in this chunk — [[d:warlock#1325981106645766155]] (lifeless, 2025-01-07), [[d:warlock#1340093945077563526]] (Gamba, 2025-02-15).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[DoT/proc trackers]** DoTimer, Cursive, and "Chronometer/Chronomancer" all recommended as good DoT trackers; Cursive became the de facto community standard and is actively maintained (Dark Harvest hasted-duration support added, dot-display bugs fixed iteratively) — [[d:warlock#1302269741628526723]] (Doombabe, 2024-11-05; +3), [[d:warlock#1326410674405900298]] (Pepopo GitHub branch, 2025-01-08).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Nightfall/Shadow Trance]** Behavior changed so the "next Shadow Bolt guaranteed to land + instant" buff is consumed by the *current* in-progress cast rather than a subsequent one, requiring the caster to cancel and recast to actually benefit — reported as a regression from pre-patch behavior and still unresolved months later — [[d:warlock#1321842601808101417]] (Ryuuk, 2024-12-26), still an issue per [[d:warlock#1345569006492454964]] (RioJnfer, 2025-03-02).
- **[Curse of Doom]** Repeatedly reported broken/bugged over several months; one confirmed interaction bug: another warlock's Curse of Agony (with the Malediction talent) can remove your Curse of Doom — [[d:warlock#1302566924550410261]] (Sarimal, 2024-11-03), recurrence reported [[d:warlock#1327458690525298799]] (30, 2025-01-11).
- **[Felguard summon bug]** Summoning a Felguard while already controlling another pet makes it immediately hostile — confirmed bug, bug report filed (turtle-wow.org/bug-report?id=8928) — [[d:warlock#1302568020409061386]] (Sarimal, 2024-11-03; +5).
- **[Greater demon insta-death bug]** Greater demons can sometimes die instantly to trivial damage (e.g. ~100 damage at 6k HP) if a raid member hits them right as they break free — confirmed bug — [[d:warlock#1365831676499853525]] (Deleted User, 2025-04-27).
- **[Greater demon duration mismatch]** Tooltip-stated duration (e.g. "1 min") frequently doesn't match observed actual duration (e.g. ~3 min with Soul Link); also reported that Hardcore-mode Infernals appear to deal only ~10% of their shown damage values and take ~10x damage — unresolved, unclear if bug or an unlisted HC-specific scaling rule — [[d:warlock#1334526916807364682]] (Cybernoise, 2025-01-24), [[d:warlock#1346304538340229130]] (Rwby, HC-specific, 2025-03-04).
- **[Imp Firebolt auto-cast]** Imp does not continue auto-casting Firebolt after the first cast; must be manually re-triggered each time — reported as likely a bug — [[d:warlock#1323007102700093614]] (Changas, 2024-12-30).
- **[DoT tick anomaly]** Identical Corruption cast (same trinkets/buffs) hit for 2184 without Dark Harvest active vs only 2002 with Dark Harvest active — reported as a possible partial-resist or DH-interaction bug, unresolved — [[d:warlock#1355948393360523577]] (Benched, 2025-03-30).
- **[Enslave/Banish immunity creep]** Several newer-content elite demons (Darkwhisper Gorge, Tainted Scar) are immune to Enslave/Banish despite equivalent vanilla mobs being enslaveable — flagged repeatedly as a design regression — [[d:warlock#1315343156442693683]] (Laz, 2024-12-08).
- **[Channel-interrupt regression]** After a launcher update, casting other spells no longer canceled an active channel (e.g. Hellfire) the way it previously did — reported as a possible launcher-side regression — [[d:warlock#1364188566833594409]] (Decemus, 2025-04-22; single source, unconfirmed cause).

## Contested / open questions
- **["Support tax" narrative]** Devs reportedly told warlocks their lower DPS ceiling vs other pure-DPS classes is deliberate payment for curse/summon utility; heavily disputed by comparing to Sunder Armor/other unpunished support abilities — never resolved in this chunk — [[d:warlock#1320874920585986051]] (Volcker, 2024-12-24; +53), disputed further by [[d:warlock#1320886021713428480]] (Kareni).
- **[Soul Link vs armor mitigation]** Whether Soul Link's pet-damage-transfer % is meaningfully equivalent to physical-mitigation tankiness (e.g. for dungeon tanking) was asked directly and never answered mechanically — [[d:warlock#1321218211789672489]] (Akos, 2024-12-25; question unanswered).
- **[Haste-shortens-vs-extends-ticks debate]** Long-running, unresolved proposal (repeated by neuroblocker, Sarimal, NewYorc, and others across Dec 2024–Apr 2025) that haste should add extra DoT ticks rather than compressing duration, to fix Affliction's mana/GCD problems — never implemented within this chunk's timeframe — [[d:warlock#1330306587901956166]] (neuroblocker, 2025-01-19).
- **[Permanent Greater Demon ask]** Large, sustained community request to make one Greater Demon (commonly proposed: Felguard) a permanent pet for deep Demonology rather than a temporary summon that eventually turns hostile — repeated from Dec 2024 through May 2025 with no confirmed implementation in this chunk — [[d:warlock#1322611595385241653]] (NewYorc, 2024-12-28), still being requested [[d:warlock#1354935423805423827]] (sailorVEVO, 2025-03-28, hoping Kara40 adds new greater demons to solve uptime).
- **[Pet scaling source]** Debate over whether pet spell damage should scale directly from the warlock's own spell power (as Unleashed Potential does) vs indirectly through pet Intellect (as most of the tree does) — argued as unintuitive design, unresolved — [[d:warlock#1338808885863120947]] (neuroblocker, 2025-02-11).
- **[Hardcore-specific Infernal scaling]** Whether Hardcore-mode greater demons have different (weaker) scaling than normal-mode is an open, single-sourced observation with no dev confirmation in this chunk — [[d:warlock#1346304538340229130]] (Rwby, 2025-03-04).
