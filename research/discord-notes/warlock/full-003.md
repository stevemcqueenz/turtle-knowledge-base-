## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
The 1.18.1 patch notes dropped on 2026-01-24 and dominate the back half of this chunk (near-universal negative reception from raiders). Concrete confirmed changes:
- **[Demonology]** Sinister Pursuit reworked into a 2-point Demonology talent giving +5/10% pet move speed (includes greater demons); Master Conjuror talent removed entirely — [[d:warlock#1464417092869554227]] (rengartop quoting patch notes), [[d:warlock#1464353438899900644]].
- **[General]** Ritual of Souls (Soulwell) shard cost raised from 1 to 3 — [[d:warlock#1464417092869554227]], [[d:warlock#1464419987862323364]] (called out as senseless since Master Conjuror scaling made 1 shard→40 healthstones).
- **[General]** Healthstone consolidated from 3 ranks/types down to a single 1200 HP stone — [[d:warlock#1464359329204539466]], [[d:warlock#1464359558309740597]].
- **[Demonology]** Enslave Demon now applies -50% healing-received to the enslaved demon — [[d:warlock#1464417092869554227]]; Master Demonologist's old -8% healing-taken-by-warlock penalty removed and effectively shifted onto Greater/enslaved demons (they already had a healing penalty; net described as worse for pet survivability) — [[d:warlock#1464355641966723365]] (dev comment quoted), disputed value 40% vs 50% [[d:warlock#1464356213998354556]].
- **[Pets]** Felhunter and Infernal armor reduced 30% (~5% less physical damage reduction), framed by players as a PvP-motivated nerf to make Felhunter easier for melee to kill — [[d:warlock#1464417141699772497]], [[d:warlock#1464380540135276677]].
- **[PvP/Demo]** Soul Link reduced from ~25-30% down to 20% — [[d:warlock#1464364102959763558]], [[d:warlock#1464377278073934171]] (community argues it hurts PvE demon survivability for a PvP-motivated change).
- **[Destruction]** Curse of Recklessness GCD reduced 1.5s→1s (only CoR, other curses unchanged, called out as an odd choice) — [[d:warlock#1464369957109366816]], [[d:warlock#1464422924227449056]].
- **[Destruction]** Searing Pain cast time reduced (≈1.5s→1.0s base, tested at ~1.39s with some haste after the change), making it hasteable for the first time — [[d:warlock#1464353163518672928]], [[d:warlock#1464360074796470474]], [[d:warlock#1464359103144001617]].
- **[Destruction]** Hellfire: mana now spread across the channel like Drain Soul instead of paid upfront (so an interrupted channel doesn't waste full mana), plus a small SP-coefficient increase — [[d:warlock#1464350977862795487]], [[d:warlock#1464423131434188800]].
- **[Affliction]** T3.5 3-piece crit-bonus set effect reported nerfed to half its prior (already-small) effect — [[d:warlock#1464384603816198439]] (see also the wording-bug thread in Gear section).
- **[Demonology]** New "Improved Summons" talent added after community backlash the same day: Demonology row 1, 3 points, reduces Ritual of Summoning cast time by 1/2/3s — does NOT remove the multi-clicker requirement for Doomguard — [[d:warlock#1464394846981586995]].
- **[Overall]** Destruction received effectively no meaningful buffs (only the CoR GCD/Searing Pain/Hellfire tweaks above); Demonology took several nerfs (Soul Link, healing reduction, armor) with the movement-speed talent seen as filler; community consensus is Affliction is now the only fully "safe" spec — [[d:warlock#1464357398021148744]], [[d:warlock#1464368251613085756]].
- **[Dev response]** Class-council-adjacent poster "Jamey" defended the changes, stating "Warlock across all major builds have the highest unified performance in PvP" and that most QoL suggestions were "considered and rejected, not ignored" — [[d:warlock#1464426108819603660]]; confirmed warlocks are "not at the bottom" of PvE parses either — [[d:warlock#1464427095248732268]].
- **[Unresolved]** The greater-demon/enslave pathing bug (see Bugs section) was raised repeatedly during the patch-notes discussion and was NOT addressed by 1.18.1 — [[d:warlock#1464011841117098146]], [[d:warlock#1464219304043216896]].
- **[Prior/background changes noted as "since patch X"]** Dark Pact (mana-regen capstone) was removed from the game entirely before this chunk (reasoning debated: weak spell vs. infinite-mana-loop risk via Mana Funnel) — [[d:warlock#1462566039266136075]], [[d:warlock#1464046449535881332]]. Pyroclasm (Aftermath talent) was "borked" roughly a year prior so it now rolls once per 15s Hellfire channel instead of per tick — [[d:warlock#1458587593389113659]]. Power Overwhelming was changed at some point from "40% of total health" to "40% of base health," reducing its pre-pull utility for voidwalker drain-tank setups — [[d:warlock#1455660288048369912]]. Ambershire's Stormwind Vault trash-pull exploit and DME/DM:E path-glitch farms were reportedly hotfixed/changed ("pathing is fixed") — [[d:warlock#1455942957138972702]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Affliction]** Broadly regarded as the safest, most consistently strong PvE spec through K40/Naxx and the default recommendation for both leveling and endgame — [[d:warlock#1459089158302863507]] ("for leveling: affli, for endgame: affli"), [[d:warlock#1461121705778090045]] ("Demo/affliction" pump most currently).
- **[Demonology]** High risk/high reward; punishing on any mistake (yours or the raid's); relies on Felguard (15 min CD), Doomguard (requires 2-3 clickers, ~10s channel), Infernal (5 min CD). Some (Jiva, Nezu) report top-5 DPS in Naxx/Kara40 when played optimally; others (L-star) call it "garbage" outside a few good fights like Anomalus and Patchwork — [[d:warlock#1462167460307275837]] (defense), [[d:warlock#1462166691533291773]] (criticism), [[d:warlock#1464145303249748039]] (Sarimal: cooldowns "near unplayable," risk/reward not there).
- **[SM/Ruin]** Considered dead/underwhelming on Ambershire and generally inferior to Affliction/Demo — [[d:warlock#1456642064484794400]] ("smruin is dead on ambershire"), [[d:warlock#1456333359931330561]] ("sm ruin is garbage"), [[d:warlock#1459181175255077061]] (Tatica: "sm ruin is boring... peepoBed spec after you learn affli/demo").
- **[Destruction/Fire]** Widely regarded as the weakest spec: needs ~16% hit plus heavy crit stacking to function, Searing Pain has poor scaling, both AoE spells (Hellfire/RoF) are weak and can't crit; estimated ~1500 DPS in full BiS+consumes (fire mage support, dragonling) — [[d:warlock#1458485756191576126]]; largely reduced to CoR-bot duty in raids — [[d:warlock#1457200084826591305]] ("busy serving cookies and applying curse of rec for rogues to parse"). Destro deemed "amazing" for AoE clearing large humanoid/undead packs by some — [[d:warlock#1459700448876433418]] (disputed — see Rotation/AoE).
- **[Overall class]** Consensus that warlock underperforms pure-DPS casters/melee: "have less AOE than mage, less single dps than mage, less burst than mage" — [[d:warlock#1457010866208772126]] (+6 reactions); warlock seen mainly as a support class (curses, summons, healthstones) — [[d:warlock#1462224799848861697]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit cap PvE]** ~16% total hit needed for Affliction; with 10% baseline from Suppression you need ~6% more from gear — [[d:warlock#1458088995580936354]]. Demonology targets 12-13% hit (biloen); going lower is "miserable" though survivable per Summonjutsu at 9-10% — [[d:warlock#1456628476969877747]], [[d:warlock#1461122776034185422]].
- **[Hit cap PvP]** ~3% from talents/gear closes most of the gap; there is always an irreducible ~1% miss chance — [[d:warlock#1461813750683402536]], [[d:warlock#1461814469830119708]].
- **[Suppression]** Gives Affliction 10% built-in hit on Ambershire (or ~5-6% on servers with Emerald Blessing) — [[d:warlock#1455335533244055573]], [[d:warlock#1455335598205178000]].
- **[Haste value]** Community table (Taffy, from "Clarg's Sad Zone" DPS spreadsheet): at 0 SP, 1% haste ≈ 10-12 SP; at 500 SP ≈ 18 SP; at 1000 SP ≈ 22-24 SP, i.e. haste value roughly doubles between low and high SP — [[d:warlock#1461749357232787634]] (corroborated separately: "at ~700sp, 1% haste ≈ 18 SP" — [[d:warlock#1456417176297934992]]).
- **[Spell base damage/coefficients]** From the same pinned spreadsheet: Dark Harvest base 1152 (200% SP coefficient, so haste is unusually valuable during its window), Curse of Agony 1212, Corruption 834, Drain Soul 1050, Siphon Life 450 — [[d:warlock#1461878264027938858]] (rqo disputes the accuracy of the Agony/DH numbers specifically — [[d:warlock#1461889696325046418]]; single-source contested figures).
- **[Haste mechanics]** On Turtle, haste shortens DoT/channel duration rather than adding extra ticks — total damage over the full duration stays the same regardless of haste %, it just completes faster, which some regard as a poor implementation for casters — [[d:warlock#1464362461795713262]], [[d:warlock#1464362567693766697]].
- **[Demon scaling]** Pets get NO passive scaling from warlock stats except via specific Demonology talents: Fel Intellect (1 SP per 1 int transferred 1:1), Fel Stamina (50% of warlock stamina), Demonic Precision (hit/crit share), Unleashed Potential (AP from SP) — [[d:warlock#1459240829401960542]], [[d:warlock#1459222543310262293]], [[d:warlock#1458103765990117477]].
- **[Demon AP coefficients]** Succubus gets 40% of her own AP as bonus weapon damage, Felhunter 30%, Voidwalker 20%; Imp's max-rank Firebolt scales off 40% of the Imp's own spell power — [[d:warlock#1455654992517140562]]. Confirmed via live testing (Str of Earth Totem on a Succubus: +77 str → +154 AP → +61.6 dmg over a 2s swing ≈ +30.8 weapon dps, matching the 40% coefficient) — [[d:warlock#1458151449287721067]], [[d:warlock#1458151636534034493]].
- **[Boss resistance]** Bosses (level 63) carry +15 reducible resistance (penetrable via Curse of Elements/Shadows) plus a separate unreducible ~7-7.4% average partial-resist penalty confirmed from parse logs (27,968/(27,968+360,015)=7.2% on one raid; corruption dot alone ~0.6-0.75%) — [[d:warlock#1462908234166698096]], [[d:warlock#1462909488511062224]]. Separately, mobs get a flat 8 unreducible spell resistance per level difference (a level-63 mob = 24 unreducible resist) — [[d:warlock#1462913141879869522]].
- **[DoT resist rate]** DoTs/channeled spells are only partial-resisted at roughly 1/10th the rate of "binary" direct-damage spells like Shadow Bolt, so Affliction can mostly ignore target resistance profiles — [[d:warlock#1462881531612758068]] (Tatica's own DH parse noted a smaller-than-expected gap, so the exact rate for channels is not fully confirmed — [[d:warlock#1462899348328284347]]).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Doomguard]** Ritual of Doom requires 2-3 other players to click/channel for ~10 seconds; frequently griefed by movement/non-participation, a top long-standing complaint; NOT removed in 1.18.1 despite "over a year" of requests — [[d:warlock#1455907288022909141]], [[d:warlock#1464370420374438031]], [[d:warlock#1464387141621780580]]. New Improved Summons talent only shortens the cast time by 1-3s, doesn't remove the clicker requirement — [[d:warlock#1464394846981586995]].
- **[Greater demon cooldowns]** Infernal ~5 min, Felguard ~15 min, Doomguard ~15-30 min; community repeatedly asked for lower CDs (e.g. 5/10/10 or 3/10/10) — [[d:warlock#1455191582054682678]].
- **[Threat]** Greater/enslaved demons get zero threat reduction (no Salvation-equivalent) and will overthreat and die versus an undergeared/slow tank — long-standing unaddressed complaint — [[d:warlock#1460667884022927526]].
- **[Unleashed Potential upkeep]** Rank-1 Health Funnel (cheapest health cost) or Mana Funnel refreshes the UP buff if stacks are lost to a lack of crits or movement — [[d:warlock#1458913059501506673]].
- **[Life Tap / 5SR]** Life Tap only interrupts the 5-second mana-regen tick when it actually spends mana; if a spell's mana cost is fully offset by another effect, it won't interrupt regen (general 5SR behavior confirmed in warlock context) — [[d:warlock#1458261245135163413]].
- **[Master Demonologist / healing]** Formerly gave the warlock -8% healing taken while a greater demon was out; 1.18.1 removed the warlock-facing penalty and effectively shifted a (40-50%, disputed) healing reduction onto Greater/enslaved demons instead — [[d:warlock#1464355641966723365]], [[d:warlock#1464356213998354556]].
- **[Dark Pact]** Permanently removed from the game prior to this chunk; debated whether removed for being underwhelming or to prevent an infinite-mana loop via Mana Funnel — [[d:warlock#1462566039266136075]], [[d:warlock#1464046449535881332]].
- **[Curse of Recklessness GCD]** Reduced 1.5s→1s in 1.18.1 (only this curse, not CoS/CoE), letting a "reck bot" apply it and start DPS 0.5s sooner — [[d:warlock#1464367323036258420]], [[d:warlock#1464369957109366816]].

- **[4-button kill]** A fresh-60 warlock can reportedly kill another fresh-60 target using just Corruption, Curse of Agony, Siphon Life, and Shadowburn, illustrating early/low-gear PvP strength — [[d:warlock#1464376560290107628]], [[d:warlock#1464377279902777416]].
- **[Curse of Exhaustion]** Warlock's main slow, described as a 40% instant ranged slow, considered still strong post-patch — [[d:warlock#1464377628252180717]].
- **[Race for PvP]** Gnome or Forsaken best per Summonjutsu, Human close third due to Perception countering rogue/druid openers — [[d:warlock#1455821507870724242]]. ## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Cursive]** The standard curse/DoT-tracking addon; needs SuperWoW for full multi-target dot tracking; recommended over pfUI/ShaguPlates dot-tracking, which "kills game performance" — [[d:warlock#1458029905500569630]], [[d:warlock#1458030473761656894]].
- **[Necrosis]** Tracks greater/enslaved demon duration and Demon Armor buff rank; forks exist at `github.com/Nedlinin/necrosis-twow` and `github.com/T321us/necrosis-twow`; has a reported bug where it doesn't register an upgraded Demon Armor rank (shows Rank 1 buff icon even when using a higher rank) — [[d:warlock#1459558245856448596]], [[d:warlock#1459504216937594881]]; most current addon links maintained on the community wiki Addons page — [[d:warlock#1459567786639491083]].
- **[Nampower]** Recommended for all casters; considered mandatory at high latency, still beneficial at low latency — [[d:warlock#1458769799638417463]].
- **[Cursive Multicurse macros]** Standard pattern: `/script if not Cursive:Multicurse("Immolate", "HIGHEST_HP", {refreshtime=1,minhp=100,resistsound}) then ... end`; `minhp` filters out low-HP mobs from recast, and "in combat" + 45yd range filters are set via the Cursive minimap panel — [[d:warlock#1461770489113350204]], [[d:warlock#1461767289740329033]], [[d:warlock#1461768466557046980]].
- **[Pet control]** Right-click a pet-bar ability to toggle autocast (bright swirl icon = on, yellow-triangle corners = off/available); default keybind for Pet Follow is "Secondary Action Button 2" (Ctrl+2 by default) — [[d:warlock#1457558294028619923]], [[d:warlock#1456017214582034643]] (see also [[d:warlock#1456008715101540393]]).
- **[UP/demon tracking]** DOITE Auras suggested for tracking Unleashed Potential — [[d:warlock#1464375454076240055]]; a custom UP-tracking addon by Szalor exists but will need rework once UP becomes a formal debuff in 1.18.1 — [[d:warlock#1464373887071551590]], [[d:warlock#1464375224022863872]]. No addon reliably predicts curse/dot time-remaining while accounting for haste (Chronometer insufficient) — [[d:warlock#1461914283032973465]] (question unanswered).
- **[Pet voice mute]** "Be Silent Demon" addon referenced repeatedly as a past solution to mute pet voice lines, but its source/topic is no longer available and no working replacement was found in this chunk — [[d:warlock#1461000530397757534]], [[d:warlock#1461291246138949643]] (question unanswered).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Curse resist, not addon bug]** Cursive appearing to fail to show an applied curse is usually a resist against high boss shadow-resistance (Meph 200, Kruul 75 in Kara40) rather than an addon or debuff-cap issue — [[d:warlock#1454583421522280692]], [[d:warlock#1454607531149234336]].
- **[Unleashed Potential blocked by buffs]** Battle Shout (and reportedly the Dragonslayer proc buff) can block a demon's UP proc from firing ("You fail to cast Unleashed Potential: A more powerful spell is already active"); reported via bug report and ignored — [[d:warlock#1460617907556843620]], [[d:warlock#1460647733760364678]], [[d:warlock#1460658073860444192]].
- **[Command Pet walk-speed bug]** Warlock/hunter pets move at walk speed instead of run speed when relocated via Command Pet; bug-reported, unresolved as of this chunk — [[d:warlock#1460578160138653707]], [[d:warlock#1461396144734339195]].
- **[Channel sound/animation lingering]** Drain Life/Drain Soul reportedly keep playing sound/animation after the cast bar ends with no extra damage dealt — reported as possibly a bug, unconfirmed — [[d:warlock#1456735565662781543]] (question unanswered).
- **[Scythe of Elune name collision]** The Scythe vulnerability debuff shares its name/ID across different casting classes' scythe trinkets (e.g., a Warlock's and a Mage's), so only one can be active on a target at a time, cutting proc uptime for whichever lock/mage applied it last; long-requested unique-name fix, not implemented — [[d:warlock#1455141337681760348]], [[d:warlock#1455142278569005158]], [[d:warlock#1455164985717031009]] (dev, Vrograg, says he tried and it may not have been prioritized by the team).
- **[Icon overlap]** Tainted Blood (Felhunter debuff) shares an icon with Health Funnel, causing addon malfunctions; reported to the community icon-fix project, unresolved as of this chunk — [[d:warlock#1455141337681760348]].
- **[Doomguard summon danger]** Historically could kill the summoner outright; no longer the case — current issue is just people not standing still/canceling the channel — [[d:warlock#1458761686143205561]], [[d:warlock#1458763514461294755]].
- **[Greater demon pathing bug — major, unresolved]** Summoned greater demons (Infernal/Doomguard/Felguard) desync from the player's position, lag behind, and fail to follow into new areas (e.g., won't follow down the ramp to Thaddius in Naxx); worked correctly for ~months, then broke again ~2 resets before 2026-01-23 (~Jan 14); suspected side effect of the healing-reduction change targeting the Mad Servant enslave exploit; world-enslaved demons (e.g. in Hyjal) still path correctly — this remained unfixed through the end-of-chunk 1.18.1 patch notes — [[d:warlock#1462518125135400991]], [[d:warlock#1462670570452680759]], [[d:warlock#1463994716436828286]], [[d:warlock#1464002899653824635]], [[d:warlock#1464413341274603797]].
- **[Healthstone underhealing]** Healthstones reportedly heal ~15% less than tooltip value; bug-reported, no fix timeline given — [[d:warlock#1464094417647435880]] (question unanswered).
- **[T3.5 crit-bonus wording]** See Gear section — likely mis-worded or under-tuned interaction, comparable to a previously-fixed Resilience bug the dev acknowledged but did not apply the same fix here — [[d:warlock#1461037982181101836]], [[d:warlock#1461038839639310593]].
- **[Dark Harvest CD-reset bug]** Dark Harvest's channel is supposed to reset its cooldown if the target dies mid-channel; reported as not working — [[d:warlock#1463553707974332500]], [[d:warlock#1463621746698747904]].
- **[OBD/BoeD patch-note confusion]** A historical patch note referencing a 15%→5% SP-coefficient nerf was misattributed; per direct tester Pepopo, OBD has always been an instant-cast proc scaling ~42.85, and "BoeD" has always been a flat 100 (no scaling) — the patch note in question was "complete nonsense"/mis-worded — [[d:warlock#1458489534244192561]].
- **[Xandivious unenslaveable]** Made unenslaveable at some point; unclear whether intentional design or an undocumented fix, speculated tied to "lock griefing" complaints — [[d:warlock#1457148158122524734]], [[d:warlock#1457149251707732111]] (question unanswered).
- **[Enslave AI overhaul]** A new enslave-AI made enslaved pets (e.g. ZG Mad Servants) refuse to path over water and stop following while the warlock is mounted, constantly re-checking pathing; makes both farming and general use of enslaved pets worse — [[d:warlock#1463693847706996777]], [[d:warlock#1463696759627976786]], [[d:warlock#1463697019133755545]].
