## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Demonology]** Warlock demon Attack Power scaling from patch notes: Voidwalker 20%, Succubus 40%, Felhunter 30%, Infernal 60%, Felguard 60%, Doomguard 30% (of player stats) — [[d:warlock#1439951424405508117]] (Shaunthepr0n, 2025-11-17).
- **[Affliction]** Drain Soul was reworked in 1.18 into a real damage spell (previously "only for shards" in old guides) and is now the highest-DPS, most mana-efficient Affliction spell — [[d:warlock#1445133944977887445]], [[d:warlock#1445134333630349333]] (2025-12-03).
- **[Affliction]** Nightfall can now proc off every Drain Soul tick (not just occasional Shadow Bolt casts as before) — [[d:warlock#1445802861660209324]], confirmed as an 1.18 change [[d:warlock#1445804324461740134]] (2025-12-03).
- **[Demonology]** Power Overwhelming (PO) now removes CC from your pet, since 1.18 — [[d:warlock#1440858777116086273]] (+4 reactions, L-star, 2025-11-18).
- **[Affliction]** A 2-point talent next to Siphon Life ("Rapid Deterioration") converts haste into faster DoT ticks: Rank 1 = 6% haste but only 50% of that applies to DoTs; Rank 2 raises that to 100% — [[d:warlock#1442442344384299103]], [[d:warlock#1442453461735047319]] (Vladizlav, 2025-11-24); worked example: 10% gear haste + this talent effectively = 16% haste for DoT purposes.
- **[Affliction]** Siphon Life's healing-from-damage penalty was removed/changed on Turtle — it now scales 100% off spellpower (previously only 50% because it also heals for 100% of the damage) — [[d:warlock#1446121165465845790]], [[d:warlock#1446130870434926797]] (2025-12-04).
- **[Destruction]** Rain of Fire's effective spellpower coefficient was roughly doubled in 1.18 (ticks faster for more, smaller ticks; per-tick coefficient unchanged, base damage reduced) — [[d:warlock#1452757128652525578]], [[d:warlock#1452757196759892019]], [[d:warlock#1452757240384851988]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-23).
- **[Demonology]** Demonic Sacrifice can no longer be taken alongside greater demons (Infernal/Doomguard/Felguard) — Soul Link spec is now mutually exclusive with sac — [[d:warlock#1443683130627330332]], [[d:warlock#1443683270507233340]] (sailorVEVO, 2025-11-27).
- **[Demonology]** Devs have explicitly stated greater demons will remain temporary (~3 min) by design, with no plans to make them reenslavable in raids — [[d:warlock#1447024371284574330]], [[d:warlock#1452699295651336325]] (Ghost9626, 2025-12-07/22).
- **[General]** Master Demonologist was nerfed to remove secondary effects, including +20% healing received on Voidwalker; this (along with a Vampiric Embrace/heal-amp nerf) effectively killed off warlock-tank viability, which devs reportedly did not consider intentional fallout — [[d:warlock#1447993848247746712]], [[d:warlock#1451304387577380926]], [[d:warlock#1452809525798109224]], [[d:warlock#1452809639581323315]] (2025-12-08/19/22).
- **[Leveling]** Krampus and Xandivious made immune to Enslave Demon — [[d:warlock#1448126201737777354]] (sailorVEVO, 2025-12-10).
- **[Bug/live]** UC Voidwalker "Binding" quest bug (guards insta-kill the summoned voidwalker before the player can tag it) was acknowledged and reportedly fixed the following Wednesday per devs — [[d:warlock#1450964014115979314]], [[d:warlock#1450966147028746333]], [[d:warlock#1453142391392567449]] (2025-12-17/18/24); workaround while broken: abandon the UC quest and pick up the Razor Hill (Durotar) variant instead — [[d:warlock#1451246007760453743]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Tier list]** rqo's widely-cited Ambershire newcomer writeup (bump-for-pin requested): Demo = highest early DPS via pets but overwhelming to learn, doesn't scale as well, needs an organized/healer-cooperative raid; Affliction = close behind, decent early damage, "scaling monster," simple rotation, cheap pre-raid BiS, no mechanics/RNG dependence, but can feel monotonous; SM/Ruin = obsolete; Farming/grinding spec (vamp gear) = best lasher farmer, best solo rep grinder besides hunter, can solo most endgame 5-mans — [[d:warlock#1447000241126641775]] (2025-11-07... actually 2025-12-07).
- **[General]** Consensus across the chunk: Affliction and Demonology are the two strong PvE specs; SM/Ruin is weaker here than in vanilla because Affli/Demo were buffed; Destruction/Fire is the weakest in MC/BWL due to widespread fire immunity/resist and becomes only "alright" by AQ40 — [[d:warlock#1445919814626578532]], [[d:warlock#1445933094757597318]], [[d:warlock#1445940250269450383]] (2025-12-04).
- **[Affliction]** "Affliction is best everywhere at all phases except a few training-dummy bosses like Patchwerk, where Demo with a fast-clearing guild can out-pump via greater demons" — [[d:warlock#1451322946202374194]], similar take [[d:warlock#1452078049595228212]] (2025-12-19/21).
- **[Demonology]** Demo's ceiling is very high in a good, fast, mechanically-tight raid (healers heal the pet, guild pulls on time), but collapses hard in casual/pug raids where pulls are delayed, healers ignore the pet, or wipes cost a summoned greater demon — [[d:warlock#1447019535440937133]], [[d:warlock#1452693343459999847]] (2025-12-07, 2025-12-22; the latter +2 reactions).
- **[Raid comp]** Warlock class is "balanced around an off-meta raid composition of 3-4 locks + 1-2 shadow priests"; without that comp, warlocks can do less damage than tanks, while even with it, warrior/mage/rogue generally outperform — [[d:warlock#1445893433125372016]], [[d:warlock#1445894272124719294]], [[d:warlock#1447623796512002269]], [[d:warlock#1447624741971038410]] (2025-12-04, 2025-12-08).
- **[Destruction]** Fire/Destro capped around ~1300 dps per one assessment, "nice for scythe fire-effect procs" only — [[d:warlock#1447545773049905247]] (Afraido, 2025-12-08); disputed by Duroz, a self-described top fire lock for months, and Not Normal, who argue destro's ceiling is unexplored because too few people play it seriously and none are in full BiS — [[d:warlock#1452778759366377533]], [[d:warlock#1452798378378526771]], [[d:warlock#1452780867859648715]] (2025-12-23).
- **[SM/Ruin]** SM/Ruin "isn't bad, just more gear dependent"; can't use Imp on fights requiring demo/affli spec-swap (e.g. Thaddius, Gnarlmoon), which widens the DPS gap vs Affliction — [[d:warlock#1447637062445437190]], [[d:warlock#1452073026404352143]] (2025-12-08, 2025-12-21).
- **[PvP]** Affliction recommended for open-world/BG PvP once geared (Dark Harvest hits hard, ~5k damage through Fear in ~7s without a dispel); Demo is best for Bloodring/duels (greater demons can't be CC'd and can one-shot); Destro/Conflagration viable once T3-geared, good vs fresh 60s — [[d:warlock#1450362534728040640]], [[d:warlock#1450362753851068497]], [[d:warlock#1450363160979570840]], [[d:warlock#1447378022100635658]] (2025-12-16, 2025-12-08).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Affliction, raid]** Ghost9626's split rotation: Team rotation (build shadow vuln) = Curse + Drain Soul until Shadow Vulnerability procs, then pop Quickness/Juju/Racial cooldowns → Corruption → Siphon Life → Curse of Agony (for a haste snapshot) → Dark Harvest. Selfish rotation = Quickness pot + Juju + Troll/Orc Racial + Corruption + Curse + Siphon Life + DH. Interrupt Drain Soul to fire off Nightfall procs, but never interrupt Dark Harvest — [[d:warlock#1448347515580579950]], [[d:warlock#1448348059900706828]], [[d:warlock#1448348304122450022]] (2025-12-10).
- **[Affliction, general]** With Eye of Dormant Corruption or 5-piece T2: Curse (applies CoA) → Siphon Life → Corruption → Soul/Drain Soul filler → Dark Harvest; reapply CoA/Corruption right after DH ends; use Soul Drain as filler and to fish for Nightfall procs, but "don't lose the last tick." Multi-dotting past 3 targets becomes counterproductive (you spend more time reapplying than using Soul Drain/Dark Harvest) — [[d:warlock#1442926931740983367]], [[d:warlock#1442927152696786994]] (𝐂𝖆𝖑𝖆𝖒𝖎𝖙𝖔𝖘, 2025-11-25).
- **[Affliction]** Don't clip Dark Harvest to chase a Nightfall proc — DH's CD/dot-acceleration is worth more; DO interrupt Drain Soul for Nightfall procs since it has no CD downside — [[d:warlock#1443543987255251007]] (Vladizlav, +3, 2025-11-27).
- **[Affliction]** Dark Harvest sequencing: avoid letting DH tick on a target with no DoTs up; get some Drain Soul ticks in first to build Shadow Vulnerability before firing Dark Harvest — [[d:warlock#1450088955147194389]], [[d:warlock#1450089355610951833]] (2025-12-15).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Affliction, trash]** Sitgarath's per-pack-type approach: standard packs — Corruption on all mobs first, then either CoA + Dark Harvest on the longest-living mob or Reckless if the raid doesn't care about curse assignment; dog packs — a couple of Corruptions while positioning, then Hellfire; destroyer/giant packs — Corruption + Agony on both, Siphon Life on the higher-HP one, then Dark Harvest that mob — [[d:warlock#1442606114054078494]] (2025-11-25).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Affliction]** Haste breakpoints (from a spreadsheet screenshot): ~19% total haste is optimal for the MC phase (gear-only, add the 8% base from the Rapid Deterioration talent to get total), likely holding through BWL; may rise to ~23% in AQ; values between breakpoints are "ineffective" — [[d:warlock#1440439603331858502]] (Bigdots, 2025-11-19); author notes another lock's independent estimate runs ~2 SP higher, for a baseline of ~700 total SP — [[d:warlock#1440439365364088832]].
- **[Affliction]** Rule-of-thumb "1% haste ≈ 13 SP" is only valid around ~310 SP; the more total SP you have, the more each % haste is worth — [[d:warlock#1442363401773777099]] (Sitgarath, 2025-11-24); refined later: ~12 SP early game, 16+ SP late game (Naxx/K40, once buffed SP is ~800-1.2k) — [[d:warlock#1446020637016719370]], [[d:warlock#1446021456780853288]], [[d:warlock#1446022301396111445]], [[d:warlock#1446964604591804516]] (Summonjutsu/Cinamo, 2025-12-05/07); haste is multiplicative, so bigger % chunks are worth more than the same % split across pieces — [[d:warlock#1446022561547685969]].

- **[General]** Hit requirements diverge by spec: your own spells need ~16% hit (vs level-63 raid bosses) to avoid meaningful miss chance; demon attacks/spells only need ~6% hit from the player to cap the demon — [[d:warlock#1447691067821391954]], [[d:warlock#1447691510903603202]] (2025-12-09).
- **[General]** Boss-level (63) innate resistance floor of ~15 (can't be reduced below via spell pen/curses) confirmed still present — [[d:warlock#1445392075645517875]], [[d:warlock#1445424108165927114]] (2025-12-04); spell penetration only reduces resistances, not level-based hit/miss chance, and no pre-AQ40 boss has resistance above what curses reduce — [[d:warlock#1441691611556876369]] (lowlifeopinions, +1, 2025-11-22).
- **[General]** Boss-level partial-resist math example (Lucifron, 200 shadow res reduced by Curse of Shadows to ~100+): about a 33% partial-resist chance remains, so ~1.5% chance of 4 resists in a row is "not that unlikely" — [[d:warlock#1445424108165927114]], [[d:warlock#1445438519437955103]], [[d:warlock#1445438693098655774]] (2025-12-04); spells with secondary effects (e.g. curses) don't get partial-resisted — [[d:warlock#1445439010796474459]].
- **[Theorycraft]** Spell hit/crit appear to function like the physical attack table (roll order: miss/dodge-type outcomes first, then crit) rather than crit being a % of only non-missed casts, per live combat-log data collection (2045 sample landed casts on a target dummy) — [[d:warlock#1447699380160303206]], [[d:warlock#1447701067558162569]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-09); confirmed by Illithris: in 1.12, spells don't use the melee attack table at all — instead separate 0-100 rolls determine hit/resist/crit outcomes — [[d:warlock#1447728610424389735]], [[d:warlock#1447728833670287483]] (cites https://royalgiraffe.github.io/resist-guide).
- **[Destruction]** Searing Pain and Shadowburn both have a base spellpower coefficient of ~0.4285 (1.5s cast / 3.5), since no talent reduces their base cast time — [[d:warlock#1452469128945467544]], [[d:warlock#1452518613817888900]], [[d:warlock#1452518747263860777]], [[d:warlock#1452532815274049640]] (2025-12-21/22).
- **[Destruction]** Haste does not reduce the global cooldown on Turtle even at 30% haste (confirmed via combat log timestamps: Searing Pain casts stayed at ~1.5s intervals) — [[d:warlock#1450573925305024625]], [[d:warlock#1450574639070842880]] (2025-12-16/17).
- **[Affliction]** DoT/Drain spellpower ratio: all DoTs except Immolate get a 100% SP-to-damage ratio (e.g. 500 SP adds ~500 extra damage to an 800-damage-over-30s Agony) — [[d:warlock#1446108383710281860]] (Ghost9626, 2025-12-04).
- **[Demonology]** Demon spells scale from the demon's Intellect (30% of Int becomes demon SP); demon melee/physical attacks scale only from demon level; Unleashed Potential stacks additionally convert the player's own SP into demon AP and SP — [[d:warlock#1445323362774679594]], [[d:warlock#1445353371619033119]] (L-star, 2025-12-02); the pet-AP conversion is believed to scale only off the player's generic SP and Shadow SP (not Fire SP) — [[d:warlock#1452711233580171396]] (2025-12-22).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[General]** Warlocks generate unusually high threat per damage relative to other classes even with extra reduction talents (Salvation-equivalent stacking, Voidstone, Succubus Sacrifice reduction); suspected hidden threat multipliers on certain spells (Health Funnel called out specifically), several open warlock threat bugs sat on the tracker "for a hot minute" — [[d:warlock#1440060392419364976]], [[d:warlock#1440062388425588868]], [[d:warlock#1440062671943766187]], [[d:warlock#1440065544186495126]], [[d:warlock#1440066136635867146]] (2025-11-17).
- **[Design commentary]** Arcane mages get 40% threat reduction from 2 talent points with no downside (plus 10 magic pen), while affliction locks must sacrifice 4% damage/mp5 for 10% threat reduction and also use a stone that reduces another 10% threat, for a total 20% reduction bought at real cost — cited as a core class-design gripe ("IQ issue") — [[d:warlock#1448673530853200014]], [[d:warlock#1448674745355866233]], [[d:warlock#1448675260638433310]] (2025-12-11).
- **[Demonology]** Enslaved/summoned greater demons (Infernal, Doomguard, Felguard) have built-in "anti-grief": the instant they aggro a non-summoning player (e.g. a tank taunt, or a healer generating healing threat) they die immediately; this is intentional design to prevent world griefing on a hardcore-sharing server, and it directly makes Demo's greater-demon uptime fragile in raids — [[d:warlock#1442922075617493094]], [[d:warlock#1442925341881401457]], [[d:warlock#1442925678847852576]] (2025-11-25).
- **[Demonology]** Greater demons despawn if you travel ~1000 yards from the summon point after they break free (roughly the Durotar-coast-to-Org-zeppelin-tower distance) — [[d:warlock#1442922625105006776]] (2025-11-25).
- **[Demonology]** Infernal cannot be re-enslaved once its 3-minute timer ends — you either kill it or it kills you (or you time the kill for right as the timer expires); enslaved (non-greater) demons CAN be re-enslaved once already enslaved before — [[d:warlock#1440630522350145596]], [[d:warlock#1443382068569571399]] (2025-11-16, 2025-11-27).
- **[Demonology]** Soul Link lets you keep a Greater/enslaved demon under control for the full 5-minute duration uninterrupted (without it, a Felguard can break free in as little as ~30s) — [[d:warlock#1443365803100737679]], [[d:warlock#1443371918970454079]], [[d:warlock#1443381751891366010]] (2025-11-27).
- **[Demonology]** Doomguard has a ~10% chance to spawn (free) when it lands a killing blow, but it auto-dispels out of combat from things like critters unless kept in combat (e.g. via Fear) — [[d:warlock#1452721792849285140]], [[d:warlock#1452722800409313480]] (2025-12-22); community requests: guaranteed spawn with Soul Link, or shorter CD, remain unresolved as of this chunk.
- **[Utility]** Health Funnel provides substantially more effective healing to a pet than most raid healers can output, and Turtle made it "20x more effective for demo" — many locks self-heal their demons rather than expect healer attention, especially on healing-intensive phases — [[d:warlock#1443154439518027837]], [[d:warlock#1448706339974025398]], [[d:warlock#1448706449470394368]], [[d:warlock#1448687760004087991]] (2025-11-26, 2025-12-11).
- **[Utility, group]** Felhunter's Devour Magic can be manually used to eat mechanics classified as "physical" rather than spell effects — e.g. Chronar's spell reflect in Naxx (target the caster boss instead of sacrificing the pet, turn off autocast and time it manually), and Smoke Bomb in Kara10 — [[d:warlock#1443502653916315648]], [[d:warlock#1443503239466319924]], [[d:warlock#1443664601941016678]], [[d:warlock#1443665008323203185]], [[d:warlock#1443665230075920478]], [[d:warlock#1443665574927405056]] (2025-11-27).
- **[Curses]** Malediction procs a free Curse of Agony application when you cast Curse of Reckless/Elements/Shadows — you still need to reapply it once it independently falls off — [[d:warlock#1444115940773072966]], [[d:warlock#1444132926831726642]] (2025-11-29); the applied Agony can itself be resisted like the base curse, and works with every curse except Exhaustion, Tongues, and Doom — [[d:warlock#1442719633319792802]] (2025-11-26).
- **[Threat/Demo]** Sunder Armor generates a fixed, non-scaling threat value (~415) regardless of damage dealt, unlike Shield Slam (350 base + scaling damage); DPS applying Sunder while a threat-capped tank holds aggro is recommended — relevant to raids struggling with warlock-generated threat — [[d:warlock#1440763884053991464]], [[d:warlock#1440764108881268736]], [[d:warlock#1440764648470085724]] (2025-11-19).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Warlock tank, general]** Warlock tanking (VW or self-tank via Hellfire) can achieve ~60% physical damage reduction currently, but has no taunt, making threat drops a serious hassle; requires attentive healers/DPS — [[d:warlock#1448374502194020616]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-12-10).
- **[Dungeons]** Stratholme Live and Dead: not doable via spell-lockout/silence for a tank-lock currently, though Stratholme Dead is doable by tanking ghosts with the Voidwalker; Dire Maul North doable, Tribute doable but the final boss can burst-kill a lowgeared-heals group — [[d:warlock#1448374502194020616]], [[d:warlock#1448375760371974256]] (2025-12-10).
- **[Kara10, shadow tank]** Ally lock can tank most of Kara10 with Salvation; one boss requires synced tank swap with the offtank; fire wand from BWL + pull dots should hold threat on servants after grabbing aggro from the offtank — [[d:warlock#1445293825265500221]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-12-02).
- **[Naxx, shadow tank]** Twin Emperors can be tanked (shadow-tank) without any specific talents; positioning your Imp/pet in the middle where the raid stacks avoids extra owl-aura/polarity-debuff damage on the pet — [[d:warlock#1446379363959439423]], [[d:warlock#1452156344630116373]], [[d:warlock#1452167428300869702]], [[d:warlock#1452223748701818922]] (2025-12-05, 2025-12-21).
- **[General]** Master Demonologist's removed +20% Voidwalker healing-received effect (nerfed pre-1.18) previously enabled warlock tanking; without it, tank-lock is "much more of a meme now," though still theoretically doable in 5-mans with heavy-armor cloth stats + Felstone + vamp gear, dotting everything — [[d:warlock#1447993848247746712]], [[d:warlock#1452810865349890263]] (2025-12-08, 2025-12-22).
- **[Design gripe]** Tank-lock lacks any taunt, so it can't reliably regain aggro if it drops; suggestions floated (Void Sac becoming a hard taunt, Soul Fire taunting while Threat Stone active) with no dev commitment — [[d:warlock#1452803222350594260]], [[d:warlock#1452803513011802287]], [[d:warlock#1452803641776803890]] (2025-12-23).

## PvP
- **[General]** Warlocks in PvP must "sit at max range and do their PvE rotation" — classic vanilla positioning closer to the frontline to Curse of Tongues enemy casters isn't viable here because you evaporate too fast — [[d:warlock#1451420578169753755]], [[d:warlock#1451420782289748010]] (2025-12-19).
- **[Affliction]** Dark Harvest is strong once geared ("hits like a truck") but hard-countered by any magic dispel; can dump ~5k damage through a single Fear window (~7s) without a dispel available — [[d:warlock#1450363160979570840]], [[d:warlock#1453625732390453450]], [[d:warlock#1453625813974122621]] (2025-12-16, 2025-12-25).
- **[Destruction]** Race-to-burst PvP combo: Seduction (Succubus) to force a trinket → if not trinketed: Soulfire → Immolate → Conflagrate for a near one-shot; hybrid 26/25/0 recommended over pure Affliction for BGs for more self-peeling — [[d:warlock#1450370894307524801]] (rqo, 2025-12-16).
- **[Class balance debate]** True Band of Sulfuras/Impending Doom bug is arguably "not a bug" for PvP balance reasons — some PvP-focused players actively lobby against fixing Affliction interactions (e.g. Siphon Life sustain) out of PvP balance concerns, which Affliction/PvE-focused players push back against — [[d:warlock#1444104869748281445]], [[d:warlock#1444109487630979122]], [[d:warlock#1444160097612857524]], [[d:warlock#1451024745045819546]] (2025-11-29, 2025-12-08).
- **[Race]** For faction choice: Horde races resist Fear/Charm 50% of the time via racial/faction mechanics — described as mattering "the most," more than specific race choice — [[d:warlock#1448104322419265637]] (L-star, +2, 2025-12-10). Recommended PvP races: Forsaken or Gnome (Dwarf possibly good once released) — [[d:warlock#1450888193900544084]] (2025-12-16); Dwarf's PvP value from Blind resistance dropped after Blind got buffed — [[d:warlock#1450890902640791573]] (2025-12-16).
- **[Utility]** Spellstone in PvP clears all dispellable buffs/magic debuffs on use, including magical silences (though you still can't cast in a silenced school even after clearing it) — [[d:warlock#1454095765600473179]], [[d:warlock#1454095909053792339]] (2025-12-27).

- **[Macro]** Debuff-check DoT-priority macro (works without Roidmacros conflicts once set up correctly): `/run c=CastSpellByName function b(k)for i=1,16 do if strfind(tostring(UnitDebuff("target",i)),k)then return 1 end end end if not b("w_Requ")then c("Siphon Life(Rank 4)")elseif not b("eOfSarg")then c("Curse of Agony(Rank 6)")else c("Corruption(Rank 6)")end` — checks Siphon Life → Curse of Agony → Corruption in priority order; caveat: may misbehave if grouped with another warlock applying the same debuffs — [[d:warlock#1443891568674345074]], [[d:warlock#1443891649364361246]], [[d:warlock#1443891943523352597]] (2025-11-28).

## Contested / open questions
- **[Demonology vs Affliction scaling]** Whether Demonology "scales poorly" with gear compared to Affliction is disputed: rqo argues Demo gets proportionally less value per stat point due to raw base pet damage carrying it early; Nezu/Papaverine/Mooncrazed and Ghost9626 counter that Demo scales very well because hit/crit apply to both player and demon, and via UP+Black Book compounding — question left open/unresolved in-channel — [[d:warlock#1447000241126641775]], [[d:warlock#1447001193996877834]], [[d:warlock#1447017589665566770]], [[d:warlock#1447018628112449626]] (2025-12-07).
- **[Warlock trash AoE weakness]** Long unresolved design debate: is warlock/caster underperformance on fast-dying trash (packs die in 5-10s in Naxx) a "real problem," or is it acceptable because warlock support (curses) and boss damage are fine? No consensus reached; suggestions (2-3x Hellfire dps, trash HP buffs, buffed Drain Soul) floated with no dev commitment as of chunk end — [[d:warlock#1452704445845733683]], [[d:warlock#1452708527087948040]], [[d:warlock#1452755272048181259]], [[d:warlock#1452760199306678526]] (2025-12-22/23; long multi-day thread).
- **[Destruction/Fire ceiling unknown]** Whether Fire/Destro's raw ceiling is meaningfully closer to Arcane Mage than logs currently show is unresolved — Not Normal argues the small playerbase and non-BiS/non-optimal logs make destro's true ceiling "unknowable," while L-star/Afraido maintain observed logs (best fire lock parses ~1.5-2x below Arcane) reflect a real, large gap — [[d:warlock#1452778759366377533]], [[d:warlock#1452780162897674383]], [[d:warlock#1452780867859648715]], [[d:warlock#1452780937518649414]] (2025-12-23); question unanswered.
- **[Tank lock viability]** Whether a real "5th tank" warlock playstyle should be revived (post Master-Demonologist nerf) is an ongoing ask; devs reportedly dismissed it once ("What? Warlock tank? No way that works") — [[d:warlock#1452809525798109224]] — question unanswered/no roadmap commitment.
- **[Threat design]** Whether the warlock threat-reduction "tax" (sacrifice damage/mana AND use a threat-reduction stone, vs Arcane Mage's free 40% reduction) is justified given the class's raid-buff value — actively argued both ways, especially regarding whether Curse of Elements/Shadows/Recklessness "support tax" should offset perceived low personal DPS — [[d:warlock#1448673530853200014]], [[d:warlock#1452709404037218365]], [[d:warlock#1452706801148625138]] (2025-12-11, 2025-12-22); unresolved.
- **[Doomguard/Felguard QoL]** Whether devs will ever loosen the "3 people to summon" / long-CD design for greater demons is an open community ask, repeatedly raised (as recently as 2025-12-26) with no confirmed changes for the 1.18.1 patch as of this chunk — [[d:warlock#1453852262870028288]], [[d:warlock#1454035726206505031]] (2025-12-26/27).
