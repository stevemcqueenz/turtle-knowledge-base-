## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Master Demonologist was reworked: imp now gets -15% spell costs plus 30% of your Int and mana regen (no longer runs out of mana), but the old secondary bonuses (Voidwalker +20% healing taken, +5% imp haste) were removed entirely — [[d:warlock#1423963068815249548]], [[d:warlock#1424124336066793533]], [[d:warlock#1423970864667295804]] (2025-10-04/05).
- **[Demo]** Master Demonologist's remaining bonus is a flat 10% crit for greater demons ("10% crit chance for greaters is massive") — [[d:warlock#1424110154642624546]] (2025-10-04).
- **[AoE]** Rain of Fire was reworked to tick once per second (previously slower); community says its *tick ratio* was adjusted so total scaling across its duration is effectively doubled (~66%) vs Hellfire's ~33% cap, making RoF outperform Hellfire above ~1200 SP, while Hellfire is still better at low SP — [[d:warlock#1425390445621678241]], [[d:warlock#1425396289239121942]], [[d:warlock#1425397735195938847]] (2025-10-08).
- **[General]** Turtle-wide hit change: fighting bosses/mobs 3+ levels above you no longer applies the old 1% physical hit suppression, moving the physical hit cap from 9% to 8% and removing the old 305-skill "soft cap" — [[d:warlock#1428568531829788672]] (30, 2025-10-17).
- **[Item]** Gloves of Unwinding Mystery nerfed from 22 to 9 spell power on the ~2025-10-15 patch; still considered BiS for Affliction afterward due to lack of alternatives — [[d:warlock#1427879767700541552]], [[d:warlock#1427913587942490133]], [[d:warlock#1427929051187773480]].
- **[Item]** True Band of Sulfuras's proc has been repeatedly nerfed/hotfixed: it used to trigger off DoT ticks/self-fire-damage at a bugged ~58% rate; that was "fixed" so it now only triggers on landing/casting fire spells (mainly Immolate's initial cast), tested empirically at ~15% (3 procs / 20 Immolate casts) — [[d:warlock#1428475783067865191]], [[d:warlock#1428483027922849942]] (2025-10-16/17).
- **[Bug/DH]** Dark Harvest has a known bug when multiple Affliction locks are in raid: everyone's dots fall off/expire noticeably early; acknowledged as a real issue by dev-adjacent poster Pepopo but not prioritized for a fix — [[d:warlock#1426241695439261788]], [[d:warlock#1427323500430884924]], [[d:warlock#1425145169375924334]].
- **[Item]** Egan's Blaster was changed/nerfed, ending the "cowboy warlock" playstyle it enabled — [[d:warlock#1427804204751388814]] (2025-10-15).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** Consistent community read on server logs: Warlock is bottom or near-bottom pure-DPS, roughly middling on boss dps in fast raids but "terrible" on trash, an improvement over classic (bottom in both) but still trailing melee/mage/ele shaman — [[d:warlock#1426808816191803422]] (lowlifeopinions), [[d:warlock#1426802938013028373]] (sailorVEVO), [[d:warlock#1427816573283995822]] (Aest, +1 reaction).
- **[Affliction vs SM/Ruin]** Multiple experienced players (L-star, Gilnean) agree Affliction now outperforms SM/Ruin at the top end ("affliction >>>>>> smruin imo" / "this is reality") — [[d:warlock#1425123540495237160]], [[d:warlock#1425123610292523090]].
- **[Spec list]** 4 broadly viable specs per sailorVEVO/Pithius: Affliction (best PvE + leveling), Demonology (great leveling, can be strong PvE but high skill ceiling), SM/Ruin (the standard go-to PvE spec, hit-gear dependent), Fire/Destro (weak in MC/BWL due to fire resist on bosses, strong once geared) — [[d:warlock#1427753831302828113]] (2025-10-15).
- **[Demo]** Demonology needs ~34 points just to function as a build, is described as the highest skill-ceiling spec, "requires 2x the effort and 3x the support" of other casters, and needs careful greater-demon cooldown planning around raid speed — [[d:warlock#1426205889571983581]], [[d:warlock#1426207773171122298]], [[d:warlock#1428796680488091769]].
- **[All specs, Kara40]** Kara40's final boss (Meph/Mephistroth) has ~200-220 shadow resistance, forcing shadow-based warlock specs to respec to Fire for that fight specifically — [[d:warlock#1426673837713657876]], [[d:warlock#1426806369243238410]].
- **[Threat]** Community consensus: warlock's only native threat-reduction is 10% from the Voidstone (via sacrificing Felhunter); unlike most other DPS specs there is no dedicated threat-reduction talent — [[d:warlock#1424108788545360002]] (sailorVEVO), [[d:warlock#1424108748825428028]] (Nezu), disputed as unintentional bug report by [[d:warlock#1428755895122399385]] (L-star).

## Leveling
- **[Affliction]** Recommended as the lowest-downtime leveling spec by multiple experienced players (Gilnean, Wells, sailorVEVO) — [[d:warlock#1427635265689751583]], [[d:warlock#1427622126462173339]].
- **[Demo]** Demonology leveling has little downtime because "demon regen is busted on twow"; Voidwalker scales off the player's HP/mana so it gets noticeably stronger once you have your first green items, but demo talents aren't worth dipping into until ~27 (Unholy Power) or 30 (Power Overwhelming) — [[d:warlock#1427636244212617307]], [[d:warlock#1427628107124310076]], [[d:warlock#1427624617731821580]].
- **[Pet choice]** Voidwalker ("Blueberry") is the general-purpose leveling pet recommendation — can hold a fully-dotted enemy while you pull the next one; Succubus deals much more damage but is squishy/needs babysitting, recommended mainly when partied — [[d:warlock#1427685348158345257]], [[d:warlock#1427685601875853382]], [[d:warlock#1427688247198351411]]. Succubus becomes the main pet choice from level 20 onward once you have some support (near-VW tankiness, much higher damage) — [[d:warlock#1429055507032965211]], [[d:warlock#1429055547847737374]].
- **[Solo/HC]** Infernal (from the Inferno quest, felwood, level 50+) hits extremely hard for a fresh 60 — melee crits ~1200 unbuffed, up to ~2000-3500 with early raid gear; strong enough to 2-shot casters and 2v1/3v1 in world PvP (AB) — [[d:warlock#1429059777215860777]], [[d:warlock#1429059612891418697]], [[d:warlock#1428561954397491210]], [[d:warlock#1429591833981358220]].
- **[Leveling rotation]** fusedpro's Affliction leveling order: Curse of Agony first (biggest damage at end of timer) → Corruption → at level 44+ lead with Curse of Shadows to auto-apply CoA via Malediction → optional Immolate → Siphon Life if the mob will live long enough → Drain Life/Drain Soul filler — [[d:warlock#1429880192163250319]], [[d:warlock#1429880384040210454]], [[d:warlock#1429880698247975024]] (2025-10-20).
- **[Quest]** The elite Infernal for the Inferno quest chain (Felwood, level 50) can be soloed by an Affliction lock via Curse of Exhaustion kiting — [[d:warlock#1429055346684727366]] (Mima, 2025-10-18).
- **[Mechanic]** Demon pet damage scales with the caster's Intellect via a demonology talent that grants % of the player's Int to the pet as its spell-power stat — [[d:warlock#1424050543181168881]], [[d:warlock#1424052747241656460]], [[d:warlock#1429053113666375812]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Affliction]** Standard order confirmed by multiple: apply curse (CoA baseline, or CoS/CoE with Malediction auto-applying CoA) → Corruption → Siphon Life once learned → optional Immolate → Drain Life/Drain Soul filler; only ever cast a DoT if it will tick fully, and never overwrite a DoT (especially Curse of Agony, whose last tick hits hardest) before it's about to expire — [[d:warlock#1424686874001412176]] (L-star), [[d:warlock#1425413794934554684]] (Sehnsucht), [[d:warlock#1425403879792705642]] (Weiner johnson).
- **[Affliction/DH]** Cast a Drain Soul (ideally with Improved Shadow Bolt/ISB debuff up) before Dark Harvest to avoid losing ~20% of DH's damage; with multiple Affliction locks, DH's known bug (see Changes section) causes dots to expire early mid-channel — [[d:warlock#1426242115599335546]], [[d:warlock#1426241435572637808]], [[d:warlock#1426242376074268803]].
- **[SM/Ruin]** Simple raid rotation: apply Corruption + a curse (Agony or the raid's assigned curse), then spam Shadow Bolt — [[d:warlock#1427612150037217431]] (Gilnean).
- **[Demo]** Opener per Gilnean: "curse → immolate → corr → sb spam" or "curse → 2x searing pain → corr → sb spam", then reapply Corruption and spam Shadow Bolt as filler, aiming to stack Unleashed Potential (UP) quickly via crits — [[d:warlock#1428791458672021596]], [[d:warlock#1427743665744511109]].
- **[Demo/UP]** Unleashed Potential gives the demon 15%/stack (45% at 3 stacks) of your higher fire/shadow spell power, both as demon spell damage and a converted Attack Power bonus (roughly 1 AP per ~2.1 spell power) not stated in the tooltip — [[d:warlock#1425954985488941108]], [[d:warlock#1429158486989737994]], [[d:warlock#1430233639983255704]].
- **[Demo/UP snapshot]** Detailed by Szalor: for Succubus, the AP portion of UP snapshots at the 3rd stack and the SP portion at the 1st stack; for Infernal, the incinerate-aura fire damage snapshots from fire SP at the 1st stack and melee damage snapshots as AP from the higher of shadow/fire SP at the 3rd stack — burst cooldowns should be popped once fully stacked — [[d:warlock#1424161635798487214]], [[d:warlock#1424162792226689097]].
- **[Demo]** Searing Pain is used specifically to fish for crits to build UP fast on short/burst windows (e.g. Anub, Noth) despite being a raw DPS loss vs Shadow Bolt outside that use; on longer fights, letting UP proc naturally off Shadow Bolt/ISB uptime is preferred — debated between L-star and Szalor, [[d:warlock#1424167757586370560]], [[d:warlock#1424169326310588547]], [[d:warlock#1427229808689021029]].
- **[Demo]** Demonic Precision converts 30/60/90% of your own spell hit/crit into your demon's melee+spell hit/crit (plus a small 1/2/3% base); demon melee (orange numbers) only needs 8% hit (special-attack table) while demon spells (Lash of Pain, Firebolt, Torment) need the full 16% (spell-hit table) — [[d:warlock#1429946136340463707]], [[d:warlock#1428816200212680816]].
- **[Demo]** Firestone/Devastation fire-spell crit does NOT transfer to the demon's own damage — confirmed by repeated testing, contrary to popular assumption — [[d:warlock#1424158818945732658]], [[d:warlock#1424158969601196204]], [[d:warlock#1424159046881251330]].
- **[Haste mechanic]** Unlike stock vanilla, haste on Turtle reduces DoT/channel duration by a linear formula (`duration = base - base*haste%`), not the usual 1/(1+haste) scaling — confirmed against the actual server source (vmangos) by Pepopo — [[d:warlock#1425213812809797772]], [[d:warlock#1425227492825759764]]. This creates "null points" where +1% haste gives zero benefit due to rounding to the nearest 1% of base cast time, empirically at 12%, 19%, 24%, 28%, 32% haste (see attached table) — [[d:warlock#1425124347109965966]], [[d:warlock#1428284937101901887]] (screenshot).
- **[Stat conversion]** 1% haste ≈ 12-22 spell power depending on total gear level (rises as SP rises); at ~687 buffed SP/27% haste, marginal value is ~18 SP, ~16.3 SP average across 7-27% haste — [[d:warlock#1425129581857476793]], [[d:warlock#1428149844660322326]], [[d:warlock#1429533519209566300]].
- **[Talent]** Rapid Deterioration is a flat +6% haste to Affliction spells (confirmed via code, uses a different aura type than normal haste stacking) — [[d:warlock#1426373626063491158]], [[d:warlock#1425219956148011149]].
- **[Addon/macro]** Single-button "noob" castsequence macros are broadly discouraged (Gilnean, L-star, Stunfisk): they can't check the target's actual debuff state, will misapply/clip dots on resists, and don't detect another lock's existing dot — recommended to bind and manually manage each dot spell — [[d:warlock#1426230149959127154]], [[d:warlock#1425522140253519913]], [[d:warlock#1426220477998825532]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[General]** Hellfire is considered fundamentally weak AoE: ~230 dmg/tick in green gear, only rising to ~250 with tier 3.5 gear, poor SP scaling, high self-generated threat, and requires standing inside the pack — [[d:warlock#1425378228612235426]] (L-star), [[d:warlock#1424110740004012094]], [[d:warlock#1424112552933331146]].
- **[Multidot]** Rule of thumb: only multidot when a target will take some time to die (fewer than 3 targets usually not worth it since the rest die to melee first); apply Agony specifically to targets melee won't reach soon — [[d:warlock#1425401504843436062]], [[d:warlock#1425403128718819388]], [[d:warlock#1425403879792705642]].
- **[Fast trash]** For trash that dies quickly, a single Corruption then Drain Soul (not Dark Harvest, "DH without dots is useless") is preferred over multidotting — [[d:warlock#1426240600117739610]], [[d:warlock#1424142393946669197]] (Dixa).
- **[AoE talent]** Pyroclasm's 26% stun chance is divided across Hellfire's ticks (≈1.7% per tick over 8s), making it a poor pick for AoE crowd control — [[d:warlock#1425309644838146099]], [[d:warlock#1425374165355200582]].
- **[AoE scaling]** AoE spells cap at 33% SP scaling per hit, unlike normal >3.5s-cast spells which get a full 100% scaling bonus — [[d:warlock#1425380406575763509]], [[d:warlock#1425390445621678241]] — and no warlock AoE spell can crit as of this chunk, a recurring, unresolved community request — [[d:warlock#1424112243074793482]], [[d:warlock#1424139834079056062]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit]** Affliction can reach 10% hit purely from Suppression (5/5), the easiest spec to hit-cap; Suppression only affects Affliction spells, so SM/Ruin/Demo/Destro must gear all their hit — [[d:warlock#1428561653750042717]], [[d:warlock#1427339620567220335]] (Gilnean).
- **[Hit targets]** Gilnean's rule of thumb: 12% hit "entry level," 14% "sweet spot," 16% "overkill" for SM/Ruin and Demo — [[d:warlock#1424063923107397807]], [[d:warlock#1428816930474823771]].
- **[Demo hit]** Demon melee only needs 8% hit (special-attack table); demon spells (Lash of Pain/Firebolt/Torment) need the full 16% (spell-hit table); requires Demonic Precision to transfer hit at all — [[d:warlock#1424053307684552777]], [[d:warlock#1428816200212680816]].
- **[Demo crit]** Because Demonic Precision transfers crit to pets, crit is worth roughly 1.6-1.8x its normal value for Demo — [[d:warlock#1428821206009643039]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[General]** Leveling stat priority: spell power > int > stam — [[d:warlock#1425501281073758370]] (Gilnean).
- **[Spell pen]** Spell Penetration is niche/skippable since curses (CoE/CoS) already strip magic resistance for the whole raid — [[d:warlock#1423453573651365938]], [[d:warlock#1423461747917979824]] (sailorVEVO).
- **[Int scaling]** Int does not directly grant spell power on Turtle/vanilla (unlike Cataclysm+ retail) — SP is a separate stat budget entirely, and Int "does nothing" for a warlock's own scaling beyond mana — [[d:warlock#1427831585792721092]], [[d:warlock#1427831883865133220]].

- **[Food]** MP5 food (e.g. Sagefish Delight) is considered largely wasted on warlock; Affliction should prioritize haste food, other specs spell-power food — [[d:warlock#1429951129843794083]] (Lightemgee); Lion Stew (12 spell dmg/15 min) is a cheap leveling/budget option — [[d:warlock#1429947456921145355]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Greater demons]** Infernal/Felguard/Doomguard last exactly 3 minutes under player control before turning hostile unless Enslave Demon-controlled (up to 5 minutes total with Soul Link); greater demons cannot be re-enslaved once control is lost — [[d:warlock#1423978829499601006]] (Deleted User), [[d:warlock#1423978462825283584]].
- **[Cooldowns]** Base summon cooldowns: Inferno 5 min (with talent), Demon Gate (Felguard) 15 min, Ritual of Doom (Doomguard) 30 min — [[d:warlock#1427660678130307232]] (Wells).
- **[Curse of Doom]** Fixed 60-second delay before it detonates; explicitly NOT sped up by Dark Harvest and NOT affected by haste; damage scales with spell power and benefits from Shadow Weaving — [[d:warlock#1427265145914130645]], [[d:warlock#1430201177362010202]] (Summonjutsu: "Nope, Doom is 60 sec no matter what"), [[d:warlock#1430225232077127751]] (Lightemgee: also unaffected by haste).
- **[Threat]** Only native warlock threat-reduction is 10% from the Voidstone via Felhunter sacrifice; Demonology splits threat between warlock and demon so has comparatively fewer threat problems than Affliction/SM-Ruin, especially during Dark Harvest ramp-up — [[d:warlock#1424108788545360002]], [[d:warlock#1428300726068838410]] (L-star).
- **[Threat/heal]** Health/Mana Funnel generates raid threat via the healing the demon receives; Vampiric Embrace healing IS reduced by the same 40% healing-received debuff greater demons suffer (confirmed by testing, though one poster argued it shouldn't be), while the warlock's own Drain Life is not affected the same way ("our saving grace") — [[d:warlock#1424166019815182416]], [[d:warlock#1424165228282646598]], [[d:warlock#1424166215429132508]].
- **[Utility]** Soulwell lets a warlock fill Healthstones for the whole raid at once, a distinctive warlock utility — [[d:warlock#1424859852399710248]] (Lightemgee).
- **[Curse math]** Curse of Recklessness reduces boss armor; using the mitigation table (816 armor = 12.4% mitigation, 516 = 8.2%, 216 = 3.6%, 0 = 0%), stripping ~400 armor is roughly a 5% raid-wide DPS increase — [[d:warlock#1424516582016680058]] (30), [[d:warlock#1424516675834744842]] (Lightemgee).
- **[Bug]** Malediction-applied curses (the free Curse of Agony riding on CoR/CoS/CoE) reportedly don't benefit from your hit% from gear or Suppression, causing extra resists — widely reported, unresolved — [[d:warlock#1427064951046012948]], [[d:warlock#1427065019962495049]] (Nezu).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Twin Emperors]** Summon Felhunter purely for its passive shadow-resist buff and keep it hidden/safe; spam Searing Pain (Shadow Ward on cooldown) rather than Shadow Bolt (no pushback protection makes SB risky); with enough shadow resist from other sources (AD trinket, jewelry enchants), a Fire spec with Conflagrate can replace the Felhunter-buff approach entirely — [[d:warlock#1429150214811549817]], [[d:warlock#1429150802303516762]] (L-star).
- **[Demo tank]** Demo/Voidwalker tanking is considered viable with careful gearing ("its ok deme is playable"); older Firestone-based ranged demo tanking for Twins/Chess has largely been superseded by a Fire-spec approach due to threat concerns on the resist build — [[d:warlock#1428003063037431909]], [[d:warlock#1423985403819462779]] (Szalor), [[d:warlock#1423985428901396615]] (Gilnean).
- **[Void sac]** Sacrificing Felhunter for its shield is used situationally in high-risk solo-survival tanking/dps scenarios when healer support is unreliable (e.g. progression C'Thun as fire spec) — [[d:warlock#1425122194983489546]] (L-star).

## Healing specifics
- **[Support role]** Warlock is not a healer; Shadow Priest is described as "0.5 healer + 0.5 dps," and running 3+ good warlocks in a raid makes bringing a Shadow Priest worthwhile for mana return/Shadow Weaving synergy — [[d:warlock#1428383230297378987]] (Sehnsucht), [[d:warlock#1426207061519372349]] (Nezu).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Kara40, Meph]** Final boss requires standing still for 6 seconds during a mechanic; boss has high shadow resist, forcing shadow-spec locks to swap to Fire for the fight — [[d:warlock#1426673837713657876]], [[d:warlock#1426806369243238410]].
- **[Kara40 trash]** Spider-swarm packs before Anub'Rekhan are called out as one of the only trash pulls in "fast guilds" where Hellfire actually gets used, since normal trash dies too fast for AoE setup to matter — [[d:warlock#1424142631017119774]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[Kara40, circles]** Anomalus-area "Unstable Magic" soak circles instantly kill a demon left standing inside them — pull the pet out before it soaks — [[d:warlock#1429571553225609320]], [[d:warlock#1429573519536291851]] (Gilnean).
- **[Naxx, Gothik trainees]** Squishy trash best handled with Drain Soul alone rather than a full dot rotation, since they die before a full DoT cycle — [[d:warlock#1426240131341357056]] (Sehnsucht/Karen).
- **[Naxx, Abomination Wing]** Big trash worth a Corruption + Curse of Recklessness (mark) then Drain Soul, not Dark Harvest — [[d:warlock#1426240565296631810]], [[d:warlock#1426240600117739610]] (Karen).
- **[Naxx, Razuvious room]** Certain 20k-HP dwarf/human trash will run if not marked with Curse of Recklessness — disputed whether this actually happens (30/Summonjutsu confirm it does; Dixa says never seen it) — [[d:warlock#1424143066612236409]], [[d:warlock#1424144858607517768]], [[d:warlock#1424143424885493890]] (disputed).
- **[AQ40, Twins]** Curse of Doom can be applied right after the teleport/pickup swap to help ramp threat on the newly-picked-up twin — [[d:warlock#1429159977519677461]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[BWL, Suppression room]** Camera/positioning sometimes forces keyboard-turning even though channels (Drain Soul/Life) only tolerate mouse-turning without breaking — acknowledged bug, unresolved — [[d:warlock#1425581946326487142]], [[d:warlock#1425564942404161556]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[BWL/AQ40, big dragons/Firemaw-type]** Physical "punch" damage (Infernal) is unaffected by 90%-reduced-unless-right-school dragon resistances, so a sundered dragon can still take ~2k crit punches; demo prioritizes near-100% Infernal uptime for such fights — [[d:warlock#1424158274630058054]], [[d:warlock#1424158415739027640]], [[d:warlock#1424158535129890837]].
- **[Custom dungeon: Stormwind Vaults]** The first four bosses can be pulled to your pet without also aggroing connecting trash, saving significant time; contains enslavable strong demon trash — [[d:warlock#1428858028009979924]], [[d:warlock#1428855496525480128]].
- **[Farming spots]** Dire Maul East hellcallers and Mount Hyjal elites are noted as strong enslavable demons; for lasher farming and Balor's Dungeon/Princess runs, Demo (Soul Link + Voidwalker) is recommended for survivability, Affliction if slowable elites are present — [[d:warlock#1429060097392509051]], [[d:warlock#1429060016773664971]], [[d:warlock#1428321355052482723]].
- **[Lasher farm route]** Use Voidwalker or a greater demon (not enslaved Satyrs); apply Felstone + vampirism-ring gear, wand-pull packs, Torment/siphon-life some targets, then Hellfire the pack down; resummon (not just dismiss) to refresh the pet's 2-min cooldown; sacrifice Voidwalker for its shield mid-Hellfire-channel if aggro goes wrong — [[d:warlock#1424797988664115290]], [[d:warlock#1424798516433260624]] (Summonjutsu).

## PvP
- **[Destro]** "Deep Destro" is competitive but needs heavy hit% investment to keep up with Affliction; still considered a strong PvP spec via Soul Fire/Conflagrate crits — [[d:warlock#1427445294718779445]] (Pathofexile).
- **[Soul Fire]** Its bonus spell-power scaling (114%) is largely wasted in PvP because PvP damage is globally nerfed 20%, so the buff mostly benefits PvE only — [[d:warlock#1427074606489010260]] (L-star).
- **[Demo/SL PvP]** A 20/31/0-style SL Demo build struggles to out-damage a lifesteal Fury Warrior; greater demons are risky in PvP/progression since dying resets their long cooldowns for the rest of the fight — [[d:warlock#1424128668275445886]], [[d:warlock#1424128569558437989]] (L-star).
- **[Felguard vs Warrior]** Keeping Felguard stacked on top of your own model lets it punish a warrior who tries to melee you (Felstorm) without them being able to avoid it except via rare Intimidating Shout use; Power Overwhelming's damage tick breaks Hamstring — [[d:warlock#1424132713518071920]], [[d:warlock#1424132808812794070]], [[d:warlock#1424132597247770826]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[Seduce/Fear DR]** Seduce shares diminishing returns with Fear but is not broken by fear-specific breaks like Will of the Forsaken/trinket; only generic effects that "explicitly break fear" don't apply — [[d:warlock#1424132365671993464]] (lowlifeopinions), [[d:warlock#1424131974582505643]].
- **[Blood Ring]** Greater-demon Demo bursts hard in Blood Ring/arena but this is seen as bad game design (pillars/rope let ranged safe-spot the Felguard, making it paradoxically weak there) — [[d:warlock#1424130910865068094]] (L-star), [[d:warlock#1424134340052520980]] (lowlifeopinions).
- **[Racials]** Will of the Forsaken only grants 3s immunity on Turtle (nerfed from retail's 5s) and doesn't block stuns, weakening it vs Rogues/Ferals; Orc favored for Horde PvP locks for Blood Fury (60 SP + 25% healing-reduction debuff, roughly "half a Saph trinket") plus a 5% pet-damage racial useful for Demo — [[d:warlock#1424135596372263005]], [[d:warlock#1428341886845714542]], [[d:warlock#1428342040659493005]].
- **[Utility]** Detect Invisibility (via Succubus's Greater Detect Invisible) is rarely used by other locks in PvP despite countering invis-potion gankers; some world-PvP guilds keep it up almost constantly — [[d:warlock#1424135915176988703]], [[d:warlock#1424136222292578444]] (L-star).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[DoT tracking stack]** SuperWoW (client hook) + Cursive (addon) + Nampower (client mod) is the required combo for accurate DoT tracking; Cursive is called the most accurate DoT tracker available but still mistracks Curse of Agony under Malediction and during multi-lock Dark Harvest overlap — [[d:warlock#1426374221868699770]], [[d:warlock#1427254934054899753]] (Sehnsucht).
- **[Shard management]** ShardCapPlus addon (`github.com/akzkak/ShardCapPlus`) auto-manages soul shard counts — [[d:warlock#1426949316034953327]] (fusedpro).
- **[Pet audio]** "Be Silent Demon" addon mutes pet voice lines — [[d:warlock#1424704903447707748]], [[d:warlock#1424705035144532000]].
- **[Proc tracking]** ShadowTranceAlert (`github.com/wsmaxcy/ShadowTranceAlert`) and ProcDoc track Nightfall procs; sound cues are also commonly used but can be unreliable — [[d:warlock#1425508935284166779]], [[d:warlock#1425497273592189039]] (Gilnean).
- **[Action bars]** Bongos addon supports up to 10 action bars, a workaround for the default UI's 5-bar limit — [[d:warlock#1427681771071471697]] (Wells).
- **[Buff tracking]** Powa addon tracks buff/consumable uptime (stones, armor, etc.) in raid — [[d:warlock#1428299630147403817]] (Sehnsucht).
- **[Macro advice]** Single-button castsequence macros are discouraged (see Rotation section); Cursive's scripted `Cursive:Curse(...)` calls are the community-preferred alternative since they check debuff presence first, though they still can't detect another lock's existing dot — [[d:warlock#1424752611633922210]], [[d:warlock#1425522140253519913]] (Weiner johnson).
- **[Channel interrupt]** `/run SpellStopCasting()` interrupts normal casts but not channels; Nampower's `ChannelStopCastingNextTick()` is needed to cleanly cancel a channel (e.g. Drain Soul) on its next tick before casting something else — [[d:warlock#1427513244922155028]], [[d:warlock#1427662298587201657]] (Refrigerador).
- **[Visual aid]** Mik's Scrolling Battle Text is recommended to visually distinguish demon damage by school/color, useful for Unleashed Potential planning — [[d:warlock#1423913874779799554]] (Gilnean).
- **[Trinket swap]** TrinketMenu addon binds and auto-swaps between two trinkets (e.g. ToEP + a passive stat trinket) to manage on-use cooldown rotations — [[d:warlock#1426487005499621416]], [[d:warlock#1429497738344534126]].
- **[Caveat]** Community pet-summon-queue addons LockPort/LockPort_Plus were found (via reading their source) to hardcode priority for their own developer's characters — a caveat when relying on them for group summon queues — [[d:warlock#1429934678135017495]], [[d:warlock#1429934763023663174]] (Verlyn).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Professions]** Herbalism + Engineering recommended combo for PvE/PvP utility (grenades, sappers, Engineering boots of speed, target dummy) — [[d:warlock#1424790364094468126]] (Gilnean). Tailoring generally not recommended (guilds usually have a crafter, and custom Turtle dungeon drops often beat tailored greens); leveling Tailoring alongside First Aid is suggested for reliable bandage material access — [[d:warlock#1424721243248136263]], [[d:warlock#1424795592567160867]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[Jewelcrafting]** Gemology recommended over Goldsmithing — ring enchants stay in demand while Goldsmithing recipes are expensive and often obsoleted; "when rich, level JC and go Goldsmithing" as a late fallback — [[d:warlock#1427499791755116717]], [[d:warlock#1427500322120667287]] (Karina).
- **[Races]** Orc favored for Horde PvP/Demo (5% pet damage + Blood Fury synergy); Troll's Berserking interaction with Rapid Deterioration/haste-on-dots was raised as a question (does it snapshot for the DoT's full duration or update dynamically?) but left unanswered — [[d:warlock#1428342040659493005]], [[d:warlock#1428336835280502885]] (Fuzruk, unresolved).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Pet loss]** Pets occasionally lose all learned abilities and get a new random name after a server crash/restart (e.g. Summonjutsu's imp "Karnar" → "Belqua"); fix is to rebuy all max-rank grimoires — [[d:warlock#1425133859107766353]], [[d:warlock#1425134051630649375]], [[d:warlock#1428133618324209836]].
- **[Pet bar bug]** Greater demons (uniquely, unlike regular pets) don't remember custom pet-action-bar button positions across summons — reported as an active, unfixed bug — [[d:warlock#1423835529673445436]], [[d:warlock#1423835662347669554]] (L-star).
- **[Dark Harvest]** Multi-lock DH causes dots to expire visibly early; acknowledged by Pepopo as known but low-priority — [[d:warlock#1427323500430884924]].
- **[Malediction]** Auto-applied Curse of Agony via Malediction reportedly doesn't get your hit%/Suppression bonus, causing extra resists — unresolved, informally reported — [[d:warlock#1427064951046012948]].
- **[Channel interrupt]** Keyboard-turning interrupts channeled spells (Drain Soul/Drain Life) though mouse-turning does not; acknowledged as a bug vs. true vanilla behavior, unresolved — [[d:warlock#1425557797764071424]], [[d:warlock#1425565657717542933]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[Pet movement]** Pet "move to location" command sometimes locks the pet to walk-speed at short range because it inherits the pet's current move-speed value; can't reposition the pet while casting — [[d:warlock#1426265882711032031]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[Legion Strike]** Filed bug report: pet AI still uses Legion Strike every 6s despite its cooldown being reduced to 4s (`turtle-wow.org/bug-report?id=12798`) — [[d:warlock#1423976149700317214]].
- **[Health Funnel]** First tick lands ~1s after cast instead of instantly, an exploitable/annoying quirk — [[d:warlock#1423991746546896980]], [[d:warlock#1423991840759218238]] (Gilnean: "you can abuse this sooooooooooooo fucking easy").
- **[Proc wording]** At least 4 distinct item-wording styles exist ("harmful spell," "harmful spell land," "landing damaging spell," "direct damaging spell") with inconsistent trigger behavior for DoTs; community consensus is initial DoT application can proc, ticks almost never do unless explicitly worded otherwise, and channels (besides Arcane Missiles, coded as separate casts per bolt) get only one shot at a proc — [[d:warlock#1428450525476819116]] (Summonjutsu), [[d:warlock#1428454021282140221]].
