## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Demo]** Demonology is the highest-parsing warlock spec by a wide margin (turtlelogs cited repeatedly), but requires micromanaging greater-demon cooldowns/positioning/pet health and is much harder to play — [[d:warlock#1472304053924008019]] [[d:warlock#1474401634720415948]] [[d:warlock#1477810248206123151]] (Jameroni/Akanamu/L-star, 2026-02-14/02-20/03-02; widely agreed).
- **[Affliction]** Affliction is the second-best/most consistent spec, considered by some players "brain dead" easy, but has the biggest single power-spike at T3.5 gear; also called weak vs. Balance Druid/Shadow Priest tier by yugi (disputed by Lightemgee who calls affli currently the strongest spec when using cooldowns) — [[d:warlock#1473383232148869355]] [[d:warlock#1473455313460920353]] [[d:warlock#1474680828473901186]] (yugi vs Lightemgee, 2026-02-18; Nezu, 2026-02-21).
- **[SM/Ruin]** Widely regarded as underperforming/neglected — "a relic from when debuff slots were a concern," demonstrably behind affli/demo in rankings; long unresolved thread (W the dev vs. Aest/Akanamu/derrurak/30/L-star) on whether it deserves a dedicated buff — [[d:warlock#1473557350685212805]] [[d:warlock#1474407282962989126]] [[d:warlock#1474419351330357333]] [[d:warlock#1474422419811864762]] (2026-02-18/02-20; unresolved, dev disputes need for spec-specific buff).
- **[Destro/Fire]** Weakest PvE spec overall — "meme spec," good for burst/trash clear speed (undisputed per Duroz) but bad sustained single-target, needs heavy hit+crit gear, causes threat problems, hard-countered by fire immunity/resist bosses and PvP fire-immunity trinkets — [[d:warlock#1467233249444102226]] [[d:warlock#1469458947969909015]] [[d:warlock#1471006193345499381]] (Duroz/dindunuffinz/Afraido, 2026-02-01/02-07/02-11).
- **[Affliction/Destro]** On Meph (K40) high shadow resist + fire-immune adds gimps most warlock specs; dots resist shadow res less than fire spells resist it, so affliction still edges out fire destro there despite the resistance — [[d:warlock#1470882688700387389]] [[d:warlock#1470882969374687373]] [[d:warlock#1470883869208084601]] (yidhra/L-star, 2026-02-11).

## Leveling
- **[General]** "Aff for speed, Demo for safety, Destro for pain" is the standard leveling-spec summary repeated many times — [[d:warlock#1467629269302775993]] [[d:warlock#1470954483730219049]] (Chud, 2026-02-01/02-11).
- **[General]** Voidwalker recommended pickup at level 10 for leveling; Succubus better for dungeons (higher attack speed, no mana cost on melee, invis to sneak up); Imp does good damage but is fragile and needs babysitting — [[d:warlock#1468373119725998112]] [[d:warlock#1468503938209943606]] [[d:warlock#1473231266857095345]] (multiple, 2026-02-04/02-18/02-17).
- **[Demo]** Fel Intellect (demon scales from your Intellect) makes demons scale from early greens; take 3/3 for meaningful leveling DPS — [[d:warlock#1473231182366769246]] [[d:warlock#1474597214780133579]] (sailorVEVO/Not Normal, 2026-02-17/02-20); however testing shows the DPS gain from stacking Intellect at low level is small (~4% DPS for 50 int at lvl25) vs. Stamina's large HP gain, so Stamina often still preferred while leveling/HC — [[d:warlock#1474797610262069336]] [[d:warlock#1474800680769159374]] (ramino1/Not Normal, 2026-02-20; contested, "not really a one or the other" per Not Normal).
- **[Destro]** Full 1-60 fire destro leveling is viable but mana-hungry and VW won't hold aggro against fire-SP gear; typical drink cadence ~6-8 kills; needs pushback-reduction talent and +fire SP greens — [[d:warlock#1471252847294287914]] [[d:warlock#1471269366153609504]] [[d:warlock#1471270125356191926]] (Euro/Not Normal, 2026-02-12).
- **[Demo]** Nether Studies (VW's Torment +45% dmg) is a big leveling threat/damage boost for tanking with Voidwalker; damage-to-threat scaling on Torment tested as linear (like Heroic Strike) — [[d:warlock#1474355384524210293]] [[d:warlock#1474393436734885899]] (L-star/Stunfisk, 2026-02-20).
- **[HC]** Soul Funnel (2/3) lets you solo elites head-on when there's no room to fear-kite; Fel Domination/Master Summoner give faster demon uptime while leveling — [[d:warlock#1474051259693535333]] [[d:warlock#1474062678644232374]] (ramino1/Stunfisk, 2026-02-19).
- **[Leveling]** Demon armor recommended as buff; wanding recommended once you have a tanking pet ("Voidwalker + dots + wand = infinite farm, no aggro") — [[d:warlock#1472625100124852244]] (Quadella, 2026-02-15).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Affliction]** Curse (assigned, e.g. CoS/CoR/CoE, applies Agony via Malediction) → Corruption → Siphon Life (if full duration usable) → Drain Soul, breaking for instant Shadow Bolt on Nightfall proc; Dark Harvest only if dots have ≥7s left and won't be clipped — [[d:warlock#1471139622703726806]] [[d:warlock#1476320386575761539]] (Shortmo/Frolegacy, 2026-02-12/02-26).
- **[Affliction]** Dark Harvest vs plain Drain Soul is close to DPS-neutral or a slight loss outside T2.5 5pc/Shadow Vuln windows — sim (base 640sp/15%crit/16%hit): with Nightfall, DS-only rotation ≈857 dps vs +harvest ≈848 dps at 180s fight — [[d:warlock#1473678492687270104]] [[d:warlock#1473680714368028702]] (biloen/Xi, 2026-02-18; confirmed by both sim and math independently).
- **[SM/Ruin]** Curse (CoS/CoR/CoE) → Curse of Agony → Corruption → Shadow Bolt spam; Shadowburn when can't fit an SB cast; Drain Soul filler only on long-lived targets; Siphon Life on long fights (e.g. Patchwerk) — [[d:warlock#1476505618654302291]] (dobbyatmos, 2026-03-01).
- **[Destro/Fire]** Soul Fire precast/on pull → Immolate → Conflagrate (consumes Immolate) → Searing Pain spam, refresh Immolate, repeat; Corruption is still worth casting as filler since "anything that isn't Searing Pain is a huge dps increase" for fire — [[d:warlock#1467312473039311110]] [[d:warlock#1467326093831704861]] (biloen/L-star, 2026-02-01).
- **[Demo]** Send pet in, curse+corruption (+optional immolate for crit-fishing), spam Shadow Bolt or Searing Pain to build/maintain 3-stack Unleashed Potential, use health/mana funnel to refresh UP before it expires, pop trinket (Black Book)/Power Overwhelming at 3 stacks for burst — [[d:warlock#1466905028320038912]] [[d:warlock#1472322255269662964]] [[d:warlock#1473595062918185033]] (Summonjutsu/Curatewf/L-star, 2026-01-31/02-15/02-18).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General]** Affliction gets 10% hit free from Suppression; typical total hit target 14-16%; SM/Ruin and Destro have no free hit talent and commonly run ~13-16% hit, a frequent point of complaint vs affli's free 10% — [[d:warlock#1466098489497751552]] [[d:warlock#1469458712434311229]] [[d:warlock#1466201591530000486]] (Draino/dindunuffinz/Grease, 2026-01-28/02-07/01-29). Demo can run as low as ~6% hit (dropping Devastation/Shadowburn for Suppression) — [[d:warlock#1466915530299412655]] (Summonjutsu, 2026-01-31).
- **[Affliction]** Rule-of-thumb haste-to-SP value: ~12-16 SP per 1% haste (varies with gear/rotation); at higher SP (T3.5-ish) ~15-20 SP per 1% haste — [[d:warlock#1475801351308050513]] [[d:warlock#1477367991808164042]] (biloen/WL-hunt, 2026-02-24/03-02).
- **[Affliction]** Talented cast/channel-speed reduction (e.g. Bane) is a flat multiplicative reduction applied *after* gear/buff haste (`0.94 * mod_cast`), so it doesn't interact with gear-haste breakpoints the same way — [[d:warlock#1475833637885972480]] [[d:warlock#1475855758535950457]] (biloen, 2026-02-24; "haste breakpoint" charts exist but biloen is skeptical they hold precisely, especially once multiplicative scaling with 12%+ haste kicks in — [[d:warlock#1475880476756414619]]).
- **[Affliction]** DoTs don't crit; haste only affects DoT tick speed via the Rapid Deterioration talent, not duration shortening — [[d:warlock#1469938635573952616]] (Zhafu, 2026-02-08).
- **[General]** Demons get roughly 1 spell power per point of Intellect (higher with Fel Intellect); Torment/Lash of Pain scale at ~40-60% SP coefficient depending on rank (untested precisely, extrapolated) — [[d:warlock#1474597214780133579]] [[d:warlock#1474793606186537162]] (Not Normal, 2026-02-20; single source, unconfirmed exact coefficient).
- **[Tank]** ~210 total Shadow Resistance eliminates all non-partial resists on Shadow Bolt (caps hits at ~3k, "should never take a SB of more than ~3k after 210"); some players insist on 315 SR for Twin-Emperors-type tanking, called overkill by others — [[d:warlock#1472991817623666789]] [[d:warlock#1472999794430578779]] [[d:warlock#1472978648817598617]] [[d:warlock#1472981481986195597]] (Not Normal vs Lufrima/L-star, 2026-02-16; disputed).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Leveling/low-level]** Voidwalker with Health Funnel talents can tank lower-level dungeons solo or off-tank, but is not beginner-friendly and causes aggro spread issues with multiple mobs — [[d:warlock#1472402288982098131]] [[d:warlock#1472406090791780585]] (Bear/Andrion, 2026-02-15).
- **[SR tank]** For Twin-Emperors-style shadow-resist tanking, recommended build is Demonic Sacrifice + Improved Stones + rest into Destruction (to Ruin, for +15 spell crit); gear: vanilla-equivalent SR set + AD exalted trinket + JC SR jewelry enchants (+8 each) — [[d:warlock#1471907752774471823]] [[d:warlock#1475628239480160336]] (L-star/biloen, 2026-02-13/02-24).
- **[SR tank]** ~210 total SR removes non-partial Shadow Bolt resists (caps hits ~3k); Wrathstone + Immolate→Conflagrate + Searing Pain spam is the standard threat rotation, with Shadow Ward on CD; a Felhunter is optional if SR gear is high enough — [[d:warlock#1471908188596076697]] [[d:warlock#1472991817623666789]] (L-star/Not Normal, 2026-02-13/02-16).
- **[Twins]** Felhunter needs Soul Funnel (2/2) healing between teleports since splash damage from bolts can kill it; Feldomination only available once per ~5min, so re-summons must be timed carefully — [[d:warlock#1475214088144552226]] (Summonjutsu, 2026-02-22).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Kara40/Tower of Karazhan – Chess]** Knight has very high cleave; demons must be positioned far behind the target using the (buggy) Pet "Take Position" command; `/petattack` must be re-issued after repositioning or the pet stops attacking — [[d:warlock#1465463647894765712]] (Tapka, 2026-01-27).
- **[Kara40 – Anomalus]** Fire-immune, so mages go Fire; UP-stacking as demo is awkward because the fight is short (~1 min) relative to Searing Pain's crit window; workaround is to pre-stack UP on the trash pack right before the pull, with a trinket snapshot, and a Dynamite crit for a guaranteed stack — [[d:warlock#1465499637963165890]] [[d:warlock#1465499997721071891]] (L-star/Szalor, 2026-01-27).
- **[Kara40 – Mephistroth]** High shadow resist + fire-immune adds gimp most lock specs; some swap to fire destro for the fight; ~75 spell penetration (50 from CoS+2 pen enchants, 25 from gear) needed to beat his resistance — [[d:warlock#1470882688700387389]] [[d:warlock#1466803335737901353]] [[d:warlock#1466803445838119124]] (yidhra/Zitronenhaze, 2026-01-30/02-11).
- **[Kara40 – Kruul]** UP-stacking snapshot tactic used before pull; fight is long enough that lifetap is mandatory even with quickness pot — [[d:warlock#1465503280460333178]] [[d:warlock#1465503751979663534]] (Szalor, 2026-01-27).
- **[Kara40 – Osiris]** Shadow debuff from crystal risks lock+pet double-dotting causing overaggro; Demo is considered relatively chill here because pet+lock DPS splits the threat; Doomguard suggested for a potential fire-weakness matchup — [[d:warlock#1467179921116762345]] [[d:warlock#1467180107834593320]] [[d:warlock#1467180193473888266]] (WL/hunt/Stunfisk, 2026-01-30).
- **[Naxx – Thaddius]** Greater demon does not follow across the gap (open bug for 2+ months at time of writing); park the pet at the middle stack point during Polarity Shift to avoid taking damage; demo underperforms here vs affli (1600 vs 2100 dps cited) partly due to this pathing bug — [[d:warlock#1474519805234249999]] [[d:warlock#1474517364098859120]] (L-star/Nezu, 2026-02-21).
- **[General raid]** Warlock trash DPS is typically bottom of the meter, "lower than tank" in optimized groups; hellfire on trash gives demo locks a job on Emerald Sanctum trash and other large packs to group mobs for burn — [[d:warlock#1467153861729849554]] [[d:warlock#1465519812745625819]] (Sehnsucht/Summonjutsu, 2026-01-27/01-30).
- **[Solo farm – ZG]** Detailed enslaved-imp solo farm route (crocs, sneks, thorium packs, Venoxis-area patrols) yields ~200g/h after devs patched the previous wall-abuse pulling exploit; new pull spots found via ledges/underwater positioning — [[d:warlock#1466553667996876966]] (Qazdur, 2026-01-30).
- **[Solo quest – Suppression]** Summon Voidwalker → sacrifice for shield → immediately resummon another and set passive → start channeling; the boss's beam is a hard taunt that pulls threat to the warlock regardless of pet aggro, so timing banish/channel around his RoF→Stomp pattern matters — [[d:warlock#1466504188065480996]] [[d:warlock#1466508010795827515]] [[d:warlock#1466510912075595836]] (Summonjutsu/Sehnsucht, 2026-01-30).
- **[Gold farm]** Dire Maul East lasher packs + Herbalism (dreamfoil nodes) is a common warlock farm, ~50-100g/h depending on server economy — [[d:warlock#1465755008225251454]] (Dezmar, 2026-01-27).
- **[Bug – Scholomance/ZG]** Rain of Fire (and other ground-target AoE) fails to deal damage in specific spots: Scholo's UD-immune basement room and the room under the first bridge; also several ZG packs (bat packs near bat boss, spider pack, zombie/idol area); suspected line-of-sight/vertical-geometry issue, no bug report found despite searching — [[d:warlock#1476320132774232074]] [[d:warlock#1476326140770517073]] [[d:warlock#1476326861456801874]] (Frolegacy/#1 top Render, 2026-02-25/02-26).
