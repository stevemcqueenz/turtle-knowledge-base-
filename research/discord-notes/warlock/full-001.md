## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Soulfire nerfed, Master Demonology lost several effects (one hurt warlock tanks badly); Spellstone reworked to act like a second healthstone but also strips all magic buffs/debuffs from the user; Void Stone added as the new threat-reduction stone — [[d:warlock#1431716900437496019]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-10-24).
- **[Demo]** Imp Firebolt's Unleashed-Potential SP scaling was nerfed vs. the previous patch because it outshadowed other demons; imp is "still a fair bit stronger than vanilla" — [[d:warlock#1431236800092967006]], [[d:warlock#1431249890155040810]], [[d:warlock#1431249934165737534]] (aegislasoupethomas/Stunfisk, 2025-10-24).
- **[Bug→fix, Affliction]** Dark Harvest was accelerating (hasting) OTHER warlocks' dots on the same target, not just the caster's own — confirmed by testing, bug report filed (turtle-wow.org/bug-report?id=18104), and fixed within this window; extensive debate on whether the fix was a net DPS loss (broke Cursive's timer display) vs. mathematically neutral/a buff (dot damage total unchanged, just compressed) — [[d:warlock#1432117470482792519]] (unfixed 10/27), [[d:warlock#1432166441951695062]] (bug report link), [[d:warlock#1432928134696538122]] (confirmed fixed), [[d:warlock#1432463172907958382]] (math argument it can't be a nerf).
- **[Bug]** Boss debuff-visibility issue (64-visible-debuff client limit) was causing warlock DoTs (esp. Curse of Agony, Dark Harvest) to appear to tick fewer times than expected in raids with many overlapping debuffs (one raid counted 76+); devs said it would be fixed with a future "Turtle 2.0" client, though a later correction says this specific report was actually already fixed in September — [[d:warlock#1430588561404592218]], [[d:warlock#1430601753220485230]], [[d:warlock#1430650111326814329]] (yugi/dev/Lightemgee, 2025-10-22/23).
- **[Item bug]** Gloves of Unwinding Mystery were meant to be nerfed from 22 SP to 9 SP but continued showing/granting 22 SP for weeks; nerf eventually confirmed to have landed ~4-5 weeks after first reported — [[d:warlock#1430625173828141247]], [[d:warlock#1438190296700817498]], [[d:warlock#1438199510303248424]] (2025-10-22, 11-12).
- **[Bug]** Doomguard/Infernal summon ritual: a helper who stops channeling mid-summon now merely wastes reagents instead of dying as in classic ("how it should be: they stop channeling and instantly die") — [[d:warlock#1437801061082730596]], [[d:warlock#1437813565091221566]] (peter_hammer/L-star, 2025-11-11).
- **[Community proposal]** A community-drafted changelist (compiled by 30/Szalor, with input credited from Maven who works with devs) proposed frontloading Corruption/Agony damage, letting Hellfire be cast while moving, a new AoE talent, reworking ISB/Weaving stacking, and cutting demon-summon cast time from 10s to 6s; status/dev reception unresolved as of chunk end — [[d:warlock#1432862948564664380]] area, [[d:warlock#1432865472763920550]], [[d:warlock#1432872763319648356]] (2025-10-29).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** Widely repeated framing: "warlock starts at 125% [of other classes] and stays there, while other classes start at 100% and ramp to 150%" — [[d:warlock#1435054823467647067]] (Summonjutsu quoting sailorVEVO, 2025-11-04; +1s).
- **[Affliction]** Considered the current PvE "meta"/easiest endgame spec: stable non-crit-reliant damage, own 10% Affliction hit from Suppression eases gearing, less loot contention with other casters — [[d:warlock#1433355089749348412]] (Poulpup, 2025-10-29); "sm ruin is poor man's version of affli in 1.18" — [[d:warlock#1434152217736118352]] (L-star, 2025-11-01).
- **[SM/Ruin]** Seen as a fading "relic" spec — "smruin falls off. now its afli -> twist afli (+demo/smruin) -> pure demo if comp/raid timer allow" — [[d:warlock#1433234060938842202]] (Gilnean, 2025-10-29); community also debates SM/Ruin's identity as an awkward hybrid mixing Affliction dots with Destro-style spam — [[d:warlock#1432868338396299336]] (Szalor, disputed by Noctissal same thread).
- **[Demo]** Best on short fights (Black Book trinket + Unleashed Potential window), loses value as fights get longer and against high-armor bosses — [[d:warlock#1432717115730694276]] (Nezu, 2025-10-28); on-par or ahead of Affliction on single target once Black Book + Felguard are available, per boss-log comparisons — [[d:warlock#1436226614659649617]], [[d:warlock#1436228496274751588]] (rqo/Ghost9626, 2025-11-07).
- **[Fire/Destro]** Considered the weakest raiding option due to MC (Ragnaros, Baron Geddon) and BWL (4 bosses) fire immunities — [[d:warlock#1432406269402026124]], [[d:warlock#1432406590664736860]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-10-27); "fire is really bad compared to everything else" — [[d:warlock#1438011518120628295]] (Calamitos, 2025-11-01); disputed by a few who say fire is fine once past fire-immune content — [[d:warlock#1433034269608902728]] (Ghost9626, 2025-10-30).
- **[Meta rotation]** Top players "twist" (respec) between Affliction (default) and Demo per-boss using a Brainwasher device, requiring two full sets of gear (haste for Affli, hit/crit for Demo); called a stupid but necessary meta — [[d:warlock#1433667518836244550]], [[d:warlock#1437656905936932967]] (Alexa/Ghost9626, 2025-10-31, 11-10).
- **[Data points]** One log comparison: top rogue ~900 avg dps, top warlock ~450 avg dps in a Naxx guild's logs — [[d:warlock#1431181013932970067]] (Noctissal, 2025-10-24), disputed as small/biased sample — [[d:warlock#1431182194763763773]] (Nyx); L-star: "warlocks do 20-30% less damage than equally geared pumpers from other classes on average... and terrible on trash" — [[d:warlock#1434654291452362752]] (2025-11-03).
- **[Demo pet share]** All 3 greater demons contribute ~50% of a demo warlock's total damage, lesser demons ~30%; Felguard alone makes up roughly half of parsed damage — [[d:warlock#1433929795196162058]], [[d:warlock#1433918589236150354]] (L-star/Ghost9626, 2025-11-01).
- **[Boss-specific spec choice, Naxx]** Affliction favored on Gnarlmoon (pet travel downtime on separated adds), Rupturan (high armor favors sustained cleave), and Kruul (high armor + pet-positioning difficulty); Demo favored elsewhere — [[d:warlock#1435300266822996021]] (Nezu, 2025-11-04); Demo/Felstorm called the best spec for Anub'Rekhan's AoE — [[d:warlock#1436396975485354085]] (Maven, 2025-11-07).

## Leveling
- **[Affliction]** Core leveling loop: dot 3-4 mobs with Voidwalker tanking/taunting between them, wand for mana regen between mobs, rarely needing Drain Life; talents barely matter at low level since early-tier talents are weak — [[d:warlock#1434098671196307637]], [[d:warlock#1434098742394490940]], [[d:warlock#1438401007209152512]] (L-star/rymkin, 2025-11-01/13).
- **[Demon choice while leveling]** Voidwalker taunt is not a true taunt but a flat threat amount, and falls behind every ~10 levels when a new demon-ability rank unlocks (X7-X9 breakpoints) — [[d:warlock#1433032936160100373]] (peter_hammer, 2025-10-30); options past 40: Demonic Swiftness (Destro) for a faster imp/succubus, Demonology for a tankier Voidwalker with better threat, or Affliction for better drains/curses; don't take Demon Sacrifice while leveling — [[d:warlock#1434098981562093659]] (L-star, 2025-11-01).
- **[General]** Warlock considered one of the strongest leveling classes currently thanks to buffed demons and an improved core kit vs. vanilla — [[d:warlock#1436424931520020570]], [[d:warlock#1436425090735800362]] (sailorVEVO, 2025-11-07).
- **[Facetank build]** Demonic Sacrifice (sacrifice Voidwalker for +9% HP4/damage buff) combined with Siphon Life healing lets an Affliction leveler "facetank"; more common choice once Dark Harvest is picked up around level 43 since DH is weak while leveling (mobs die too fast) — [[d:warlock#1435341566393843712]], [[d:warlock#1435342693294411787]], [[d:warlock#1435342810541850674]] (Sakkaro/Summonjutsu, 2025-11-04).
- **[Sacrifice choice]** Choosing Felhunter-sac (mp5) vs. Voidwalker-sac (HP buffer) depends on total mana pool vs. HP pool ratio — if HP pool dominates, void-sac gives more value; if mana pool dominates, felhunter-sac does — [[d:warlock#1435408995543158945]], [[d:warlock#1435409398171172966]] (Summonjutsu/Stunfisk, 2025-11-04).
- **[Weapons]** Warlocks can use dagger, one-handed sword, and staff — [[d:warlock#1432725004893556767]] (Afraido, 2025-11-01).
- **[Ritual of Summoning]** Learned at level 20; requires 1 soul shard per summon and 2 other players clicking the portal — [[d:warlock#1432018661119496333]] (fusedpro, 2025-10-26).
- **[Gear ramp]** Shadoweave/Stormweave crafted armor recommended from the late 30s/40s through 60 — [[d:warlock#1436747447845130361]], [[d:warlock#1437530629842403550]] (Ghost9626/JP Morgan Intern, 2025-11-08/10).
- **[Lasher farming, level 58+]** Pull the pack, AoE-taunt with Voidwalker, Hellfire, sac the Voidwalker when it switches aggro to you, resummon before the next pack; requires stacked Vampiric Embrace/enchants (~9 vamp achievable via ring/neck enchants + Improved Felstone) to sustain — [[d:warlock#1439246941644132413]], [[d:warlock#1439250280465305691]], [[d:warlock#1439256559770271754]] (Tyrona, 2025-11-15).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Affliction, standard]** Assigned curse → Corruption → Siphon Life → Drain Soul filler until Shadow Vulnerability (renamed ISB) procs off DS, then drop DS and full-cast Dark Harvest; also drop DS to reapply expiring dots and to cast Nightfall procs — [[d:warlock#1435398662426595529]], [[d:warlock#1433667695253000295]], [[d:warlock#1433760676526162071]] (Ghost9626, 2025-10-31/11-05).
- **[Affliction, alt priority]** Siphon Life → CoS+CoA → Corruption → Drain Soul; if the first DS channel procs Shadow Vuln, go Dark Harvest to use up remaining Corruption; otherwise take 2 more DS casts before DH — [[d:warlock#1435583725386731530]] (suddeNslow, 2025-11-05); Calamitos's version: Curse → Siphon Life → Corruption → Drain Soul (interrupted into DH ~40% of the time); once Corruption is extended via T2 set bonus or the Medivh Kara40 trinket, invert Corruption and Siphon Life order — [[d:warlock#1437974669667078194]], [[d:warlock#1437974865163325542]] (2025-11-12).
- **[Cancel-casting]** Cancelling Drain Soul cleanly right after a tick (not mid-tick) via Nampower's "cancel channel only after a tick" option, or by double-tapping the next spell with SuperWoW, is a core DPS optimization, confirmed working by a user who tested it — [[d:warlock#1433442818235564052]], [[d:warlock#1438426294005006356]] (Nezu/Hyasur, 2025-10-31/11-13).
- **[Dark Harvest timing]** Using DH when dots have less than ~5-6s left is a DPS loss, because the accelerated dots then expire mid-channel and force an earlier, awkward reapplication — [[d:warlock#1432456413111914699]], [[d:warlock#1432456554719744080]] (Nezu, 2025-10-28). With 2 warlocks DH-ing simultaneously and full dots (post-DS-fish for ISB), Siphon Life/Curse of Agony/Corruption should each have ~14s/11s/9.5s left at DH start to both fully harvest; with 4 locks DH-ing at once this drops to ~9s/7s/6s — [[d:warlock#1432920385137147926]], [[d:warlock#1432921737812508728]] (Gilnean/Summonjutsu, 2025-10-29).
- **[Dot management]** Never clip dots early — only reapply once they fall off; exception is Siphon Life in its last ~5s with an empty GCD, where you can let it finish and then reapply Agony/Corruption together — [[d:warlock#1433432659555647498]], [[d:warlock#1433433207738466365]] (Nezu, 2025-10-31).
- **[Snapshotting]** Affliction dots snapshot the caster's own buffs (SP trinkets, Orc racial, ZG trinket, haste) at cast time, not target-side debuffs (those apply dynamically) — reapply right after popping an on-use SP cooldown to fit 2 full dot cycles under its window — [[d:warlock#1433431784263254119]], [[d:warlock#1433432574406955119]], [[d:warlock#1433433802893426758]] (peter_hammer/Nezu, 2025-10-31).
- **[Nightfall usage]** Interrupting Drain Soul to cast a Nightfall-proc'd Shadow Bolt is generally correct at low haste, but at 30%+ haste it stops being worth it since DS ticks every ~0.66s for ~1k damage near the end of its channel — [[d:warlock#1433776301222924350]], [[d:warlock#1433776906020327516]] (Nezu, 2025-10-31).
- **[Drain Soul resist behavior]** DS doesn't suffer partial resists the way Shadow Bolt does (only full resist is possible), so average DPS output ends up similar — [[d:warlock#1435725262154236016]], [[d:warlock#1435746558003122186]] (Ghost9626/Lightemgee, 2025-11-06).
- **[Demo]** Rotation centers on keeping Corruption up (to trigger/keep Shadow Vulnerability) plus Immolate for crit-fishing, filled with Shadow Bolt spam while managing Power Overwhelming and pet HP; can weave in Curse of Agony/Doom when not assigned a raid curse — [[d:warlock#1434977948875886684]], [[d:warlock#1434978229558710413]], [[d:warlock#1434978329068441673]] (Dongabhana/Summonjutsu, 2025-11-03).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit cap]** Overall spell hit cap vs. bosses is 16% (there's always a base ~1% miss chance that can't be removed) — [[d:warlock#1436869830090293359]] (Sitgarath, 2025-11-09).
- **[Affliction hit]** Suppression (5/5) gives 10% hit but only to Affliction spells (Corruption, Agony, Drain Life, Drain Soul, Dark Harvest), so only 6% more hit from gear/curse is needed for cap; with an Emerald Blessing druid only 5% more is needed — [[d:warlock#1432224779875778711]], [[d:warlock#1436885169142370437]] (Summonjutsu/Pacyn, 2025-10-27, 11-09).
- **[Demo hit]** Soft cap for demon melee is 6% caster hit (brings the demon to 8% via Demonic Precision, stopping orange-swing misses); pet spell-type attacks (Lash of Pain, Firebolt) and the Infernal's aura still need the full 16% spell hit table; Felguard cleave/felstorm need only ~8% total but still occasionally get dodged at 16%; recommended overall target is 10-12% personal hit while prioritizing crit for UP uptime, moving to 15%+ once Naxx-geared — [[d:warlock#1432774862216101999]], [[d:warlock#1432787125627257014]], [[d:warlock#1432793099888758836]], [[d:warlock#1435711148996562965]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-10-27/11-04).
- **[Haste value scaling]** Haste's SP-equivalent value rises with total spell power: roughly 10-14 SP per 1% haste at 0 SP, ~18-22 SP per 1% at ~500 SP, and ~22-24 SP per 1% at ~1000 SP — [[d:warlock#1432240676749312140]], [[d:warlock#1436926055918076004]], [[d:warlock#1436751783492845763]] (Summonjutsu/Ghost9626, 2025-10-27, 11-08/09).
- **[Crit for Affliction]** Considered "basically 0" or "absolutely useless" value since dots can't crit — haste and SP dominate the priority — [[d:warlock#1436284588421808159]], [[d:warlock#1436921963812622457]] (rqo/Calamitos, 2025-11-07/09).
- **[Demo crit]** The pet gets 90% of the caster's crit + a flat 3% from Demonic Precision; above ~30% caster crit, the demon starts falling slightly behind 1:1 in received crit; pet melee/orange attacks crit for 200% damage (vs. 150% for the player without Ruin), while pet spell-type attacks (Lash of Pain, Firebolt) only crit for 150% even with Ruin talented — [[d:warlock#1432796175735455885]], [[d:warlock#1432798289689837672]], [[d:warlock#1432781317384310854]], [[d:warlock#1432781470979719239]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-10-27).
- **[Hit value example]** For Affliction at ~400 SP / 10% hit, the next 1% hit is worth roughly 16.8 SP by one calculated formula (1/n × avg spell dmg ÷ avg dmg multiplier); disputed by others who feel that's too generous ("1 hit is not worth 35 sp") — [[d:warlock#1435685464026910740]], [[d:warlock#1435686845865394377]], [[d:warlock#1435679770712080484]] (Bigdots/lowlifeopinions, 2025-11-04).
- **[Resistance floor]** Bosses have a level-based resistance minimum that spell penetration cannot bypass — a boss 3 levels above the player has an ~15-point floor that stays even with Curse of Shadow + full spell pen — [[d:warlock#1432869575787024394]], [[d:warlock#1432871177294053406]], [[d:warlock#1435681812117459065]] (30/Summonjutsu, 2025-10-29/11-04).
- **[Spell pen stacking]** Curse of Shadow (-75 resist) stacks additively with gear spell penetration; example calc: 100 spell pen (gear) + 75 (curse) reduces a 200 SR boss most of the way to its floor — [[d:warlock#1432410262748794983]], [[d:warlock#1432410532215918755]], [[d:warlock#1432411934480924792]] (rymkin/Summonjutsu, 2025-10-29).
- **[Tier sets]** Community consensus that T2 set bonuses are noticeably stronger than T3 for Affliction, and T2.5 (especially the chest) is considered best-in-slot right up until T3.5 — [[d:warlock#1434483691656052747]], [[d:warlock#1434484475403702273]], [[d:warlock#1434485863370199063]] (Poulpup/L-star, 2025-11-02).

Sample unbuffed MC-phase demo stat targets: 33.42% crit / 642 SP, or 35.91% crit / 612 SP, both at 6% hit — [[d:warlock#1432787435041062982]] (Bigdots, 2025-10-27); Summonjutsu recommends staying above 500 SP and 25% crit while picking up hit, prioritizing BWL belt/bracers for hit next — [[d:warlock#1432788355820552305]] (2025-10-27).
- **[Gearing philosophy by spec]** Affliction wants +spell damage and +haste; SM/Ruin and Demo want +crit/+hit — [[d:warlock#1433943326096101466]] (L-star, 2025-11-01); general priority quoted as "hit > spell power > leech > stam > int" — [[d:warlock#1434408225725349919]] (Weiner johnson, 2025-10-27).
- **[Trinket comparison]** Whip of Encouragement (3% haste) valued above Blackfire Orb (1% haste/1% crit/15 SP) for Affliction, since crit is near-worthless on dots: roughly 36 SP-equivalent vs. 27 SP-equivalent — [[d:warlock#1437962546748457073]] (Calamitos, 2025-11-12).
- **[True Band of Sulfuras]** Its casting-speed-on-hit proc doesn't trigger from Drain Soul or Dark Harvest ticks (not classified as "direct hit"), making it a weaker upgrade than expected for Affliction — reported as a bug — [[d:warlock#1437513449361113249]], [[d:warlock#1437537999540981922]], [[d:warlock#1437538452261306468]] (Mima/Calamitos, 2025-11-10/11).
- **[Atiesh]** Best suited to SM/Ruin; a dedicated Atiesh-holder gives up personal build flexibility (locked out of Affliction) to provide the staff's raid-wide +30 SP buff, which is worth ~4x more to the raid than the personal DPS the holder loses — considered a real burden by some holders — [[d:warlock#1431641229728419955]], [[d:warlock#1431644786502406345]] (Braszunda/Aest, 2025-10-25).
- **[Robe of Sacrifice]** Explicitly called "straight ass," worse than crafted alternatives at current gear levels — [[d:warlock#1435337510950535249]] (Lightemgee, 2025-11-04).
- **[Pet scaling from gear]** Generic +Spell Power benefits all demon school-specific damage; school-specific SP (e.g. shadow-only gloves) only benefits that school's pet abilities (imp Firebolt = fire only, Succubus Lash of Pain = shadow only); pet Attack Power via Unleashed Potential always uses the caster's HIGHEST spell power school regardless — [[d:warlock#1432589769380204655]], [[d:warlock#1432720871461617757]], [[d:warlock#1432721013233422507]] (Summonjutsu/Nezu, 2025-10-27/28).
- **[Pet buff items]** Dungeon-set pet-damage items (e.g. the 0.5-questline hands/boots) reportedly don't work on Greater demons, only lesser pets — [[d:warlock#1432493570711097346]] (L-star, 2025-10-28).
- **[Raid buffs on demons]** Lesser demons (imp/succ/VW/felhunter) can receive raid-wide AURA buffs (paladin auras, shaman totems, priest Prayer of Fortitude/Spirit, Arcane Brilliance, Gift of the Wild, Battle Shout) as long as they're summoned and in the raid when the buff is applied — but not single-target buffs; Greater demons (Infernal/Doomguard/Felguard) get NO raid buffs at all — [[d:warlock#1438226555762507876]], [[d:warlock#1438200136374161427]] (Summonjutsu/Stunfisk, 2025-11-12). Succubus specifically benefits from Windfury Totem when positioned in melee range — [[d:warlock#1436770733652050103]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-11-08).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Unleashed Potential (UP) mechanics]** Grants the pet a share of the caster's SP as Attack Power (from highest school) and school-specific SP as pet spell power, stacking to 3 on caster spell crit. Empirical testing shows the value snapshots on each stack and only the LAST (3rd) stack's snapshot matters going forward — once at 3 stacks, further crits refresh duration only, not the captured value; best practice for on-use SP trinkets is to activate after already having 2 crit stacks so the buffed SP gets captured on the 3rd — [[d:warlock#1432862948564664380]], [[d:warlock#1433212670722576518]], [[d:warlock#1433212419500408965]] (Bigdots/Summonjutsu, 2025-10-28/30). Whether UP also periodically re-checks SP on a timer (~20s, per one dev conversation) was never conclusively confirmed by testers — [[d:warlock#1432712579859611688]], [[d:warlock#1432713726246654074]] (Nezu/Szalor, 2025-10-28). UP does NOT proc from the demon's own crits, only the caster's direct-damage spell crits — [[d:warlock#1430790163667095562]], [[d:warlock#1439069365223030874]] (Wells/Lightemgee, 2025-10-23, 11-15).
- **[Threat]** Warlock as a class has no dedicated threat-reduction talent (unlike Arcane Mage's 40% talent), a long-running complaint; Greater demons (especially Doomguard/Felguard's Felstorm) can generate enough threat to out-pull well-geared tanks, with reported Felstorm crit ticks of 5.2k — [[d:warlock#1439185233453777047]], [[d:warlock#1439149220518035456]], [[d:warlock#1439154466782969958]] (Szalor, 2025-11-15).
- **[Greater demon duration mechanics]** Controlled Greater demons (Infernal/Doomguard/Felguard) last their full 3-5 minute duration and cannot be re-enslaved once they break free; break-free is checked roughly every 10 seconds, but Soul Link makes the demon immune to these checks for its 3-minute duration; Power Overwhelming can be used to finish off your own demon quickly before it breaks loose (percentage is of current HP, effective while it's still in combat) — [[d:warlock#1435035999351738471]], [[d:warlock#1434988294504185866]], [[d:warlock#1435036795212533892]], [[d:warlock#1435037942048489623]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-11-04).
- **[Summon ritual]** Requires soul shard(s) plus 2 other players channeling into a portal (~10s); a helper who stops channeling early no longer dies (patch change) but wastes the caster's reagents — [[d:warlock#1432018661119496333]], [[d:warlock#1437810809123111053]], [[d:warlock#1437813565091221566]] (fusedpro/Mardoom/L-star, 2025-10-26, 11-11).
- **[Master Summoner]** 2/2 cuts Infernal/Doomguard cooldown from 10m to 5m — called "crucial" and was missing from a commonly-shared demo build — [[d:warlock#1435426129765990520]] (Mima, 2025-11-05).
- **[Addon-assisted cancel-casting]** Nampower's "cancel channel only after a tick" option lets Drain Soul be cancelled cleanly right after a tick lands, avoiding a wasted partial-tick cast; confirmed working in practice — [[d:warlock#1433442818235564052]], [[d:warlock#1438426294005006356]] (Nezu/Hyasur, 2025-10-31, 11-13).

## Tanking specifics
- **[General]** Warlock tanking is unsupported/niche but doable in specific spots: Twin Emperors (AQ40) via Shadow-Resist Destro/SM-Ruin, and the Kara40 Chess event via Bishop-tanking with modest Shadow Resist — [[d:warlock#1433199490776432640]], [[d:warlock#1437964606663299103]], [[d:warlock#1437964707653750989]] (sailorVEVO/Calamitos, 2025-10-30, 11-11).
- **[Twin Emperors build]** Go deep Destro (Improved Stones/Wrathstone) for threat if unbuffed Shadow Resist is high enough (180+ unbuffed → 250+ raid-buffed is enough without needing Master Demonologist/Gift of Arthas/Shadow Prayer/MotW); otherwise just dip into Improved Stones — [[d:warlock#1432836708784668732]], [[d:warlock#1432838036718092431]], [[d:warlock#1432844847089189025]] (Montezul/Gilnean/Summonjutsu, 2025-10-29). Estimated deep-Destro/Wrathstone tps at 800 spell dmg and hit cap: ~1200-2000 tps; a theorized Affliction+Felstone tank build (redot → teleport → immediately harvest → redot → drain soul) could generate ~780-1000+ tps — [[d:warlock#1431100232195641478]], [[d:warlock#1431104738690072606]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-10-24).
- **[Other tanking spots]** Black Morass's final boss is "really really rough" even for a warlock tank; niche off-tanking possible in Kara10/AQ20/ZG trash, not recommended in MC; impossible in live Stratholme due to constant kicks/school locks — [[d:warlock#1433196798947885128]], [[d:warlock#1433199377781883041]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-10-30). Melee "warlock tank" builds (Bloodring Dagger) are a meme, not a real playstyle — [[d:warlock#1433200801391837194]], [[d:warlock#1433201572279615531]] (sailorVEVO/Summonjutsu, 2025-10-30).

## Healing specifics
- Warlock has no dedicated healing spec; self-sustain tools are Healthstones (though some players say they now use Well Fed/Tea more than healthstones), Life Tap (mana-for-HP, worse than it sounds since damage/healing-received modifiers like Amplify Magic or Shadow Ward affect its output), and Health Funnel (feeding own HP to the pet) — considered a large DPS loss to use mid-fight, with Soul Funnel's efficiency talent making it strong for solo/leveling sustain — [[d:warlock#1430549295857143851]], [[d:warlock#1435934793367027774]], [[d:warlock#1438302034624516238]], [[d:warlock#1438706993148989572]] (Stunfisk/Ghost9626/Dreamhaze/Lightemgee, 2025-10-22, 11-06/14).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[MC]** Ragnaros and Baron Geddon are Fire-immune, forcing Destro/Firelock to swap to Shadow Bolt for those fights; rest of MC is fine for Destro except the Fire Elemental (also Shadow) — [[d:warlock#1432406269402026124]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-10-27).

- **[Onyxia]** Ony is Fire-immune as of this window — [[d:warlock#1432406269402026124]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-10-27).
- **[Kara40 Mephistroth]** ~200 Shadow Resistance makes this a "parse-killer" for Affliction/shadow specs; Eye of Moam + Curse of Shadow reduces effective resistance to 5 (requires the trinket, and affliction locks must take turns activating it); adds split immunities — crawlers immune to Shadow, other adds immune to Fire — forcing heavy respec/itemization for this single fight while melee doesn't have to swap; on progression fights disease can kill the tank's pet — [[d:warlock#1430904581222694973]], [[d:warlock#1436159410593337435]], [[d:warlock#1430905859243905135]], [[d:warlock#1431181936709079155]] (Cinamo/Odessyus, Ghost9626, Stephan, 2025-10-23/11-07).
- **[Kara40 Kruul]** Demo/Felguard viable but the boss has very high armor and pets tend to glance while chasing behind it as it turns; long fight duration erodes Demo's early burst advantage; melees can create a safe spot in phase 2 for demons — [[d:warlock#1430906092426231870]], [[d:warlock#1432717115730694276]] (Stephan/Nezu, 2025-10-23/28).
- **[Kara40 Chess event]** Warlock can Bishop-tank with modest Shadow Resist (~100 SR sufficient); either spec works — [[d:warlock#1437964606663299103]], [[d:warlock#1437964606663299103]] (Calamitos, 2025-11-11). Fel Concentration is valuable here because of a constant shadow-damage aura mid-fight that would otherwise interrupt/pushback casts (Drain Soul, Dark Harvest) — [[d:warlock#1430506392711594026]], [[d:warlock#1430506521967591496]] (Rollbluntz, 2025-10-22).
- **[Naxx Anub'Rekhan]** Best AoE fight for Demo — Felstorm is "just too strong" here — [[d:warlock#1436396975485354085]] (Maven, 2025-11-07).
- **[Naxx Gnarlmoon / Rupturan / Kruul]** Affliction favored on Gnarlmoon (pet travel-time downtime on separated adds) and Rupturan (high armor rewards sustained cleave over Demo); Kruul also favors Affliction for the same armor/positioning reasons — [[d:warlock#1435300266822996021]] (Nezu, 2025-11-04).
- **[Naxx spec plan]** A posted per-boss spec plan: Demo for Spider wing, Patchwerk & Grobbulus; Affliction for Gluth & Thaddius; full Demo for Plague wing; full Affliction for the Military wing/4 Horsemen, adjusted by strat and raid comp — [[d:warlock#1438009683439784116]], [[d:warlock#1438011359886446735]] (Mayurii/Calamitos, 2025-11-01).
- **[Black Morass / Wardens of Time]** Key farming dungeon for pre-raid BiS gear; requires Banish and Curse-of-Recklessness-based fear-juggling on the hard-hitting dragon trash at the start; use Fear as CC if the group lacks a Druid's Hibernate — [[d:warlock#1432811487621021778]] (Bigdots/Summonjutsu, 2025-10-27).
- **[Dire Maul East]** Cited as the warlock's best solo gold-farming instance — [[d:warlock#1434729274413420595]] (Ghost9626, 2025-11-03).

## PvP
- **[General standing]** Warlock PvP considered solid for solo/leveling play but weak in organized BG/Arena at 60 due to: no pushback protection outside Affliction channels/Fear, Curse of Exhaustion locked to deep Affliction and nerfed twice recently (from a 50% baseline down to a talented 40%, base now only 10%), no mobility/gap-closer, and no threat-reduction tool — [[d:warlock#1436406776265445607]], [[d:warlock#1436619326273884203]], [[d:warlock#1436501951696080908]] (Summonjutsu/Karen/L-star, 2025-11-07/08).
- **[Affliction PvP]** Standard approach is dotting + kiting, since hardcasting Shadow Bolt vs. melee is largely non-viable due to pushback; Affliction locks are immune to interrupt effects (kick/pummel) while channeling drains — [[d:warlock#1434242633894592725]], [[d:warlock#1432907646901551224]], [[d:warlock#1432907105597395128]] (L-star/Gilnean/Twospooky, 2025-10-23/11-01).
- **[SM/Ruin PvP]** With 4 points to finish Curse of Exhaustion, called the "most versatile effective pvp spec" for open-world/BGs, since it avoids losing Fear-pushback protection (which Master Summoner in Destro/Affli sacrifices) or the class's only reliable instant-damage tool — [[d:warlock#1436410692520644791]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-11-07).
- **[Demo PvP]** Strong specifically in Bloodring/Arena because Demon Gate and Infernal cooldowns reset each match, letting a player burn Felguard then Infernal every match; weak in group PvP (AV) once a Greater demon is down and 2+ opponents show up — [[d:warlock#1436498300542128168]], [[d:warlock#1436498529479692340]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-11-08).
- **[Greater demon combat myths]** Infernal melee damage is Physical (goes through armor), NOT Fire as commonly assumed, correcting a community myth — [[d:warlock#1435054016328630343]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-11-04); Infernal has been observed punching for 3500-5000+ in raid-buffed PvP scenarios — [[d:warlock#1435039653186109500]], [[d:warlock#1435038503183323176]] (Summonjutsu/Zestitcha, 2025-11-04).
- **[Anti-mage tech]** Use Felhunter manually (no autocast) and bait Counterspell by faking a Drain Soul cast or opening with a Fire spell first; Felhunter/Succubus seduce resist enemy Polymorph dispel attempts — [[d:warlock#1435740919579152524]], [[d:warlock#1435779467862605966]] (Ghost9626/Lightemgee, 2025-11-06).
- **[Race choice]** Gnome best for Imp/Ruin builds (int scaling for imp), Troll best for Affliction, Orc best for Soul Link Demo (though the 5% orc racial only applies to melee/orange pet hits and is finicky), Dwarf considered best for tank-lock (stoneskin racial) — [[d:warlock#1431033164113117418]], [[d:warlock#1431035119468155081]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-10-24).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Cursive]** Near-mandatory addon (requires SuperWoW) for accurately tracking dots/curses in raid; supports one-button multi-target curse macros via `/cursive multicurse <Spell>|<Filter>` and automatically skips CC'd targets (sheep/stun/shackle) — https://github.com/pepopo978/Cursive — [[d:warlock#1437658700658769930]] (Calamitos, 2025-11-11).
- **[SuperWoW]** Closed-source (not open source) client-side .dll injector required as the base mod for Cursive and accurate dot tracking; some community distrust since the author won't share source with Turtle devs — [[d:warlock#1434899641832308757]], [[d:warlock#1434900496870674442]] (Karen/Goajos, 2025-11-03).
- **[Nampower]** Enables precise cancel-casting ("Advanced Option → double cast to end channel early" / `ChannelStopCastingNextTick()`), improves spell queuing, and reduces high-ping issues — [[d:warlock#1437010949016915968]], [[d:warlock#1437083041679016018]] (Karen/Refrigerador, 2025-11-09/10).
- **[DoTimer]** Older/simpler alternative that doesn't require SuperWoW; correctly recognizes and shows expiration of your own dots, but doesn't account for other locks' Dark-Harvest-hasted dots — [[d:warlock#1434919469569802323]], [[d:warlock#1434933504529141830]] (Darkseer/Sehnsucht, 2025-11-03).
- **[ShardCap]** Manages soul shard deletion above a threshold when leaving combat (default caps at 12 inventory slots; shards stack to 3 each on Turtle, so this is not literally "12 shards") — [[d:warlock#1439140535532126300]], [[d:warlock#1439148601224859680]] (Szalor, 2025-11-15).
- **[Macro examples]** Single-target SM/Ruin macro combining Cursive + SuperCleveRoidMacros posted by Gortzi — [[d:warlock#1431267309611388978]] (2025-10-28); multi-dot macro with pet-attack/nochanneled conditionals and auto Life Tap posted by Bigdots — [[d:warlock#1433225280427655209]], [[d:warlock#1433225428943900836]] (2025-10-30); pet attack/follow toggle macro using AssistUnit/PetAttack/PetFollow — [[d:warlock#1435629169752150140]] (rqo, 2025-11-05).
- **[Debate]** Some veteran players (Gilnean) discourage sharing full-rotation macros publicly, arguing it stunts new-player skill growth; others insist the class needs the QoL to be playable at all ("refuse to use mods → do shit dps → deadweight in raids") — [[d:warlock#1433228117828305047]], [[d:warlock#1434864291512848434]] (Gilnean/Afraido, 2025-10-30, 11-03).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Dark Harvest cross-lock bug, fixed]** One warlock's DH channel was hasting OTHER warlocks' dots on the shared target, not just the caster's own; confirmed by testing, filed (bug-report?id=18104), and fixed within this window — [[d:warlock#1432158011199328266]] (still present 10/27), [[d:warlock#1432166441951695062]] (bug report link), [[d:warlock#1432928134696538122]] (confirmed fixed 10/29). Extensive disagreement over whether the fix was a net loss (broke Cursive's dot-timer accuracy) or mathematically neutral/a buff (total dot damage unchanged, just compressed to allow earlier reapplication) — [[d:warlock#1432463172907958382]], [[d:warlock#1432918222470123530]] (lowlifeopinions) vs. [[d:warlock#1432918776776622081]] (Gilnean, disputing).
- **[Debuff-cap dot issue]** A 64-visible-debuff client limit was implicated in warlock DoTs (esp. Curse of Agony, Dark Harvest) appearing to tick fewer times than expected in raids with heavy debuff overlap (76+ counted in one raid, worsened by a Scythe-of-Elune trash mob's Moonfire spam); devs' response pointed to a future "Turtle 2.0" client fix, though a later correction says the specific referenced report was actually a different (already-fixed) boss-visibility bug — [[d:warlock#1430588561404592218]], [[d:warlock#1430601753220485230]], [[d:warlock#1430650111326814329]] (yugi/dev/Lightemgee, 2025-10-22/23).
- **[Gloves of Unwinding Mystery]** Intended nerf (22 SP → 9 SP) didn't take effect in-game for an extended period (tooltip and character sheet both still showed 22); eventually confirmed to have landed ~4-5 weeks after first reported — [[d:warlock#1430625173828141247]], [[d:warlock#1430632849488220181]], [[d:warlock#1438199510303248424]] (2025-10-22, 11-12).
- **[Suppression/Malediction interaction]** Suppression's 10% Affliction hit bonus doesn't apply correctly when Curse of Agony auto-reapplies via a Malediction proc, causing unexpected dot-application misses even at nominal hit cap — [[d:warlock#1435342993006788680]], [[d:warlock#1435343693195247648]] (Mima/Summonjutsu, 2025-11-04).
- **[True Band of Sulfuras proc]** Doesn't trigger from Drain Soul/Dark Harvest ticks (not coded as "direct hit" damage), reported as a bug — [[d:warlock#1437513449361113249]], [[d:warlock#1437537999540981922]], [[d:warlock#1437538452261306468]] (Mima/Calamitos, 2025-11-10/11).
- **[Curse resist% bug]** An unspecified curse-resistance bug was reportedly still unfixed as of Oct 29, described as worse than the DH bug that got fixed first — [[d:warlock#1432870103653027871]] (30, 2025-10-29; single source, unresolved).
- **[Pet AI after Fear]** Pets (especially Greater demons) can remain idle/broken after being feared and the fear ending, a long-standing low-priority bug — [[d:warlock#1430987320928243782]], [[d:warlock#1431193043557548032]] (Gilnean/Nezu, 2025-10-23/24).
- **[Command Demon bug]** Reportedly unusable while the warlock is mid-cast, listed by Nezu alongside UP-stack-tracking issues and the 2-person Doomguard-summon requirement as longstanding demo pain points — [[d:warlock#1431194152179204159]] (2025-10-24; single source).
- **[PvP dampening bug]** Warlocks reportedly doing only 1-damage ticks against "bugged" warriors in PvP, suspected a dampening/pvp-gear interaction bug; community upvoting a bug report — [[d:warlock#1439342862734069985]], [[d:warlock#1439346752607092857]] (Ghost9626/lowlifeopinions, 2025-11-16).
