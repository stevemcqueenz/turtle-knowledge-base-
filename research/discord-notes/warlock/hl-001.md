## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[demonology]** Reworked to actually incorporate Greater Demons into the core rotation; demon stats/abilities now scale from warlock stats including spell power — [[d:warlock#1405230719181131838]] (Lightemgee, 2025-08-13).
- **[affliction]** Debuff caps largely removed (no fighting for debuff slots); DoTs scale with haste at high investment; Drain Soul is the primary damage tool between cooldowns — [[d:warlock#1405230719181131838]].
- **[destruction]** No longer limited to only SM/Ruin; Searing Pain no longer generates bonus aggro directly (moved to a separate mechanic) — [[d:warlock#1405230719181131838]].
- **[affliction]** Fel Concentration talent cut from 5 points to 2, now affects all Affliction spells (pushback resist), and also now prevents pushback on Fear/Howl of Terror — [[d:warlock#1398226750961356920]] (Karen, 2025-07-25), [[d:warlock#1405907548908949636]] (Lightemgee, 2025-08-15).
- **[affliction]** New talent Sinister Pursuit (row 3, 3 pts): demon movement speed +5/10/15% for 3s after the demon attacks an Affliction-debuffed target — [[d:warlock#1398226750961356920]].
- **[all specs]** Curse of Exhaustion baseline slow nerfed from 30% to 10%; a new 2-point "Improved Curse of Exhaustion" talent (row 5) restores +15/30% slow, gating full CoEx behind Affliction investment — [[d:warlock#1398226750961356920]], [[d:warlock#1398731734161297469]] (chinik, disputed as an overly harsh nerf to world-PvP kiting for non-Affliction specs).
- **[affliction]** "Improved Debilitating Curses" renamed "Improved Curse of Weakness" and no longer boosts CoEx's slow — [[d:warlock#1398226750961356920]].
- **[demonology/tanking]** Master Demonologist reworked: removed secondary buffs (e.g. Voidwalker's healing-taken bonus); community says this effectively kills the beloved warlock-tanking build, and a request to compensate tanks for the loss (e.g. via Voidwalker threat/shadow damage) went unaddressed as of chunk end — [[d:warlock#1398731987199459489]] (chinik, +1), [[d:warlock#1400373044169670706]] (Therz).
- **[all specs/threat]** Spellstone changed from absorbing magic damage to a 10% threat-reduction effect, then that 10% threat reduction was removed entirely later in the patch cycle — sparked heavy backlash (multiple messages, e.g. +6 reactions) — [[d:warlock#1378862197987479693]] (2025-06-02), [[d:warlock#1398623998517973132]] (Maven, +6), [[d:warlock#1402995837268791437]] (Holliday, +3), [[d:warlock#1398732316506853447]] (chinik). - **[destruction]** Improved Soul Fire fire-damage bonus raised 8%→10%; Soul Entrapment (no-pet damage bonus) raised from 1/2/3% to 2/4/6%; Imp's buff changed from 6% Fire Damage to 4% Spell Damage (now also boosts Corruption); Rain of Fire ticks every 1s instead of 2s (total damage unchanged) — [[d:warlock#1398285910751183040]] (Stephan, 2025-07-25).
- **[affliction]** Drain Soul's mana cost made dynamic instead of charging full cost up front, and Drain Soul can now proc/benefit from the Improved Shadow Bolt amp — [[d:warlock#1398324715826712837]] (Karen), [[d:warlock#1405907548908949636]].

- **[destro/demo/affliction]** Destro solid post-BWL; Deep Demo strong for MC/BWL because Infernal is fire-immune (more uptime); Affliction shines once fights run past ~40s due to consistency/no resists; SM/Ruin a contender through all raid tiers — [[d:warlock#1383462360080973836]] (Summonjutsu, +1), [[d:warlock#1397642107648475247]] (+2).
- **[destro vs sm/ruin math]** At 0/500/1000 spell power, plain Destro Searing-Pain-string edges out SM/Ruin Shadow-Bolt-string (e.g. 1828 vs 1670 dmg at 0 SP, 5428 vs 5894 at 1000 SP favoring Destro-side math as shown), but adding ISB/Shadow Weaving plus Fire debuffs (Scorch/CoE) closes the gap — [[d:warlock#1405243713441235137]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[destro/sm-ruin variant]** A "0/34/17" succubus+felstone build (vs classic imp-only UP/Ruin) performs close to SM/Ruin with much less threat issue since ~30% of damage comes from the succubus (a separate threat entity); Soul Link gives ~50% PDR combined w/ tank talent, and 25% more survivability plus 15% better Hellfire/Infernal damage via PO/Health-Funnel loops — [[d:warlock#1419028619199647825]] (Cinamo/Odessyus, 2025-09-20).
- **[general gearing]** Demo and SM/Ruin share nearly identical gear, making them an easy dual-spec pair; Affliction needs its own gear track; Fire "flops on MC/BWL like all fire specs" due to fire immunities — [[d:warlock#1416489875708055604]] (Cinamo/Odessyus, +2).
- **[destro build split]** Two distinct Destro talent philosophies diverge: 0/34/17 succubus+felstone (uses Infernal/Felguard on bosses, keeps up ISB for other Shadow casters) vs 28(30)/23(21) imp-only (stacks Unleashed Potential via rank-1 Health Funnel refresh every 14s, runs Firestone) — [[d:warlock#1388304879893614785]] (Summonjutsu).

## Leveling
- **[demo, pet DPS test]** At level 60 with minimal gear (241 Int, 62 generic/101 fire/99 shadow SP, 9.16% crit, 2% haste), on lvl-51 test dummies: Imp 163-168 dps, Succubus 83-93, Voidwalker 63.9-70.8, Felhunter 38.8 — Succubus edges out Voidwalker; Felhunter notably underwhelming for demo — [[d:warlock#1380085389464830013]] (L-star, spec link https://talents.turtle-wow.org/warlock?points=AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAoCYBoCDBYFYBAFAVYBIAAAAAAAAA=).
- **[affliction]** Drain-Soul-centric leveling is mana-inefficient because the Voidwalker can't hold aggro well and any pushback removes roughly 50% of the drain channel's value (mana spent, little damage gained), unlike Shadowbolt which keeps full damage even if delayed by pushback — [[d:warlock#1387342907228557417]], [[d:warlock#1387404671135649842]] (jkrejzar).
- **[all specs]** Leveling stat priority while haste gear is scarce: Stamina > Intellect > Spell Power; Fear is reliable on TWoW and mostly breaks on the target taking a *new spell application*, not on DoT ticks — so apply DoTs first, then Fear — [[d:warlock#1387410120182599772]] (Bones).
- **[affliction]** For leveling, 2 points in Suppression recommended over Nightfall/Improved Life Tap — brings resist chance to ~1% vs mobs up to +1 level, a "4% increase" worth detouring from Demonology talents for; especially valuable under Hardcore/Slow&Steady-style challenge rules — [[d:warlock#1389932786445713429]], [[d:warlock#1390009988935716864]] (Bones).
- **[demo/affliction]** Demonic Sacrifice + Curse of Exhaustion combo for solo leveling: open Soulfire/Shadowbolt, kite while casting CoEx, instant Corruption, then nuke with Shadowbolt+Searing Pain, or Fear→Drain Life loop (regens 2% max mana per 4s tick) — [[d:warlock#1381441459076726856]] (Genesis Tenshi).
- **[affliction/demo hybrid]** Path: level Affliction to Curse of Exhaustion, then Demonology up to Demonic Sacrifice, dump remainder into Destruction; sac Felhunter = near-unlimited mana, sac other demons for +9% damage instead — [[d:warlock#1382046312173670511]] (Genesis Tenshi).
- **[demo leveling order, up to lvl 34]** Fel Stamina (tankier Voidwalker/demons) → Nether Studies (VW-only damage+threat) → Master Summoner (utility) → Unholy Power (damage) → Power Overwhelming (damage for HP cost) — [[d:warlock#1387274140515303455]] (sailorVEVO).
- **[gating note]** Under 1.18-preview talent placement, Fel Concentration isn't reachable until level 52 and Improved Drains until level 57, which constrains leveling talent-order planning — [[d:warlock#1390006821569429649]] (Suade).
- **[demo]** Post-1.18-preview advice: level in Demonology, but start in Destro talents first until reaching the imp/succubus cast-time/cooldown reduction talent plus Bane, since those pets get heavy early use — [[d:warlock#1405240486838407271]] (Lightemgee).
- **[general]** Warlock leveling considered very strong on Turtle post-rework: a Demo lock can face-tank most elite quests on the Voidwalker while cycling Drain Life/Siphon Life, leveled 2 days faster than a hunter alt this way — [[d:warlock#1420542061203034224]] (Dixa, 2025-09-25).
- **[demo, ~lvl 36-40]** Skip 5/5 Corruption while leveling (dots do little without spellpower and it delays key Demo talents); prioritize Fel Stamina once Succubus is out (she's squishy); Succubus "solo carries" at this stage — [[d:warlock#1416430364301332673]] (Turreve).
- **[demo]** Post-1.18-preview, Curse of Exhaustion's leveling value is diminished (10% baseline slow) — not worth a talent point/GCD unless also taking Improved CoE — [[d:warlock#1405910260190806088]] (Suade, question, echoes the general CoEx nerf above).
- **[profession]** Enchanting strongly discouraged for a leveling warlock: expensive/slow, depends on a Blacksmith for rods, its trainer sits in an elite-filled instance (inaccessible around level 35 solo), heavy mat/inventory burden, poor profitability until very late, and loses gold vs. vendoring/AH-selling greens directly — [[d:warlock#1384527459050852442]] (Mirror, +1).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[stat weights, general]** Hit is worth "infinite" spell power until the cap (16/15%+something described as "EB"), then worth 0 extra SP; roughly 1% haste ≈ 14-15 SP; roughly 1% crit ≈ 17 SP, though crit is worth close to 0 for Dark-Harvest-style Affliction since it's mostly channels/dots. Worked example: a 3%-haste item (~42-45 SP-equivalent) beats a +23-spell-damage/fire-resist item for Affliction, but the fire-resist item wins for Destro (haste doesn't help Searing Pain's fixed GCD) and for Demo (pet doesn't inherit warlock haste, but does inherit SP/crit) — [[d:warlock#1421918198206304456]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-09-28, +5).
- **[spell power mechanics]** Generic spell power applies to all schools including healing; a school-specific bonus (e.g. "+22 fire spell power") only applies to that school; effective SP for a given spell = generic SP + that school's specific bonus (e.g. 100 generic + 100 shadow-specific = 200 effective SP for Shadow Bolt but only 100 for Searing Pain) — [[d:warlock#1391603438139211908]] (Summonjutsu), confirmed [[d:warlock#1422024938264592456]], [[d:warlock#1409290854975148203]].
- **[tank]** Twin Emperors shadow-resist target: 221 from gear = 24 (3/3 Demon Armor talent) + 60 (Priest's Prayer of Shadow Protection — does NOT stack with Mark of the Wild) + 10 (Gift of Arthas consumable), which caps at 315 total unbuffed; remainder of gear/food should prioritize stamina — [[d:warlock#1413054952183693343]] (Cinamo/Odessyus, 2025-09-04).
- **[affliction]** ~18-20% haste cited as the "soft cap" for a fluent Affliction rotation; a high-progress player switching gear to hit ~20%+ haste over crit-heavy pieces reported it as an improvement — [[d:warlock#1396062155916447755]] (Stichling, question/self-report).
- **[affliction, contested]** A guildmate's claim that "crit scales much better than haste and Affliction caps out at BWL" was raised as a reason to consider respeccing to SM/Ruin — posed as a question, no resolution recorded in chunk — [[d:warlock#1403440979582914570]] (Shandoryl, question unanswered).
- **[caster mechanics]** Casters roll twice: first for hit/miss (minimum ~1% miss + level-difference scaling), then a separate roll for crit if the hit lands; example given: vs. a level-63 target as a level-60 with 0% hit / 100% crit rating, ~17% of casts show as resisted and the remaining ~83% all crit — [[d:warlock#1401835231719329865]] (Deleted User).
- **[destro, petless build]** A True-Sulfuras-focused petless Destro/SM-Ruin build claims Demonic Sacrifice gives 6% more Fire damage by going petless, using Immolate reapplication partly to keep the Sulfuras proc up despite Immolate doing ~25% less threat than a non-crit Shadow Bolt — [[d:warlock#1407422710123991211]] (Cinamo/Odessyus, question, no direct rebuttal in chunk).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[destro/imp build]** Firestone preferred over Felstone if invested in the Improved Stones talent line; otherwise Felstone + Soulfire-on-cooldown if Improved Soulfire was taken instead — [[d:warlock#1388304879893614785]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[tank]** Wrathstone gives all Fire damage a 40% threat multiplier (60% with Improved Stones talent); this applies to Searing Pain, Immolate, Imp's Fire Shield, fire wands, Sapper Charges, Oil of Immolation, and Fiery Weapon procs — it's the core warlock-tank threat tool — [[d:warlock#1410823324090503289]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[affliction, contested]** Whether a 1% haste enchant (head) is better than an 18 spellpower ZG enchant, while a 20 spellpower glove enchant is preferred over 1% haste there — implies 18 SP < 1% haste < 20 SP, but this was posed as an open question in a guide and not confirmed in-chunk — [[d:warlock#1403029893641404467]] (fizzlegoof, question unanswered).

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[general]** Classic-era warlocks benefited less from world buffs than melee classes, cited as one reason Classic warlock logs/parses looked weaker than they "should" — [[d:warlock#1404152049641193494]] (Nezu).
- **[utility]** Fel Cookies (healthstone-tier consumables): each higher rank raises the minimum usable level by 12 as the warlock levels; handing lower-rank cookies down to lower-level group members/dungeon tanks saves them potion/repair gold — [[d:warlock#1397408752847556752]] (Summonjutsu).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[demon mechanics]** Greater Demons (Infernal, Felguard via Demon Gate, Doomguard via Ritual of Doom) all last exactly 3 minutes under control before breaking free; once broken, they attack ONLY the summoning warlock (not random players); if the demon is ever forced to attack another target (taunt, threat off healers, etc.) Turtle WoW's "anti-grief system" instantly kills it rather than letting it hurt someone else — [[d:warlock#1412415297054445610]] (L-star), [[d:warlock#1399132797104423014]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[all specs]** Lesser demons (Imp/Voidwalker/Felhunter/Succubus) are permanent until dismissed, killed, or replaced — [[d:warlock#1399132797104423014]].
- **[demo]** Power Overwhelming damages the warlock's demon proportional to its max HP; pair with Soul Funnel + a couple ticks of post-PO Health Funnel during downtime to keep the pet alive; not recommended while leveling unless running a Void with Consume Shadows — [[d:warlock#1383459083323183135]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[demo]** Master Conjuror talent points also increase Soulstone's returned mana/health on use — [[d:warlock#1388917143453827133]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[threat]** Pre-1.18: Spellstone -10% threat and sacrificing the Succubus were the two warlock threat-reduction tools (backlash when Spellstone's version was removed, see 1.18.1 section); post-1.18 preview a dedicated "Voidstone" (-10% threat) item was confirmed planned — [[d:warlock#1400577657841910014]] (Grim, question), [[d:warlock#1404201474321285121]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[macro/UI]** Demon pets are viewed via Shift+P or the pet tab in the character pane; a demon left on defensive will attack whatever attacks the player, but Succubus/Felhunter may run off to attack whatever pulled the player into combat at fight start — a manual pet-attack macro or Ctrl+1 is recommended to control targeting — [[d:warlock#1382457452879937537]] (Summonjutsu), [[d:warlock#1391214410969649195]].
- **[threat rule of thumb]** Once a warlock reaches ~80-85% of tank threat, start wanding/holding back — except very early in a pull before the tank has sunders up, when a small overpull is more acceptable — [[d:warlock#1388662907415564491]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[utility]** Death Coil is the only pushback-protected interrupt/CC the class has; its 2-minute cooldown is largely justified by its self-heal component, since almost nothing else in the kit has pushback protection besides Searing Pain and the drain spells — [[d:warlock#1399422556074938460]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[utility, complaint]** Warlock has essentially no mobility/self-peel besides an unreliable 3-second-cast Fear (breaks on new spell applications) and the 2-minute Death Coil, unlike Mage's Blink/Nova/Poly/Frost Armor kit — [[d:warlock#1399427460201844872]], [[d:warlock#1399432909311574158]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[affliction/malediction]** Curse of Agony auto-applies alongside Curse of Shadow/Exhaustion/Recklessness (but not Curse of Tongues/Weakness) via the Malediction talent; re-applying any of those three curses will refresh CoA even if undesired — the two effects cannot be decoupled — [[d:warlock#1408053714534793237]] (jo7e).
- **[threat, general]** Warlocks generate threat from up to 6 separate sources simultaneously (dots, felstone healing, wand, direct spells, etc.) versus a tank's ~3, which is cited as the underlying reason for the class's persistent "sit on your hands" playstyle even at comparable gear levels — [[d:warlock#1400883328034279609]], [[d:warlock#1400879047654772746]] (Summonjutsu).

## Tanking specifics
- **[tank]** Summonjutsu's tank guide (forum link https://forum.turtle-wow.org/viewtopic.php?t=19652): effective from level 60 onward with Wrathstone + Soul Link (Intensity helps a lot); has tanked all Dire Maul variants including Tribute and all of BRD; croc boss ("Antinormi") is rough due to losing taunt after being consumed — suggests a 2k-armor potion, mob is kiteable; Kara Crypts 5-man worm boss can emerge from its hole unexpectedly (possibly a bug); LBRS has spell-interrupt issues, bait it out and use a fire wand + Immolate to hold threat since Wrathstone boosts all fire damage including trinket procs like Eternal Flame's fire-thorns aura — [[d:warlock#1379816702786801864]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-06-04).
- **[tank]** Comparable-geared warlock tanks shouldn't be out-threatened by a similarly-geared warrior; higher-armor gear pieces (e.g. Helm of the Transmogrified) generally preferred — [[d:warlock#1383441339777286214]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[tank]** Strengths/weaknesses: no hard taunt; likely the best AoE threat generation in the game via Wrathstone-buffed flame damage (includes wand hits, Fire Shield); needs heavy hit investment to tank raid bosses outside Kara10/ZG-tier content; a Worgen boss is theoretically solo-tankable with no swap needed — [[d:warlock#1396294018627014767]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-07-20).
- **[tank, Twin Emps]** Drop Demon Armor shadow-res points if unneeded, grab Cookie instead, and put 17 points into Demo for Improved Stones if hit/spell-damage is otherwise low; standard Destro/Fire + Wrathstone suffices otherwise — [[d:warlock#1383202005849346160]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[tank, raid coverage]** Post-1.18-preview assessment: MC/BWL/Onyxia fine to tank as Demo or SM/Ruin (fire immunities are the concern elsewhere, not here); Destro fine for ZG, AQ20/AQ40, UBRS (Shadowbolt the fire elemental), Naxx, and Kara40 ("parsing reasonably per turtlogs," though the poster admits low personal confidence there due to gear/skill) — [[d:warlock#1421921624885690419]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-09-28).
- **[tank/pet roles]** Voidwalker is the primary tank-demon (offtank/positioner/pull tool) — its own +10% physical damage reduction stacks with Soul Link's 30% PDR for ~50% unbuffed PDR; Felhunter is called "the most important demon" for silence/interrupt/dispel, including removing debuffs that would otherwise block the warlock's own threat generation — [[d:warlock#1410823324090503289]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-08-29).
- **[tank, requested but unimplemented]** Community asks (not confirmed as implemented in this chunk): Voidwalker's Master Demonologist passive should meaningfully help tanking (e.g. armor contribution); Wrathstone should add shadow damage (countered by speculation that a 6%-hit tank would then be "busted"); Shadowburn should act as a taunt while Wrathstone is active; Fel Stamina's crit-reduction should apply to both pet and warlock — [[d:warlock#1400373044169670706]] (Therz), [[d:warlock#1405267104868995204]] (lowlifeopinions, pushing back that ignoring crushing blows/uncrushable isn't a real fix), [[d:warlock#1409290854975148203]].
- **[tank, bug]** Demon Armor/Demonic Aegis interaction bug: adding armor-talent points while the Demon Armor buff is already active does not retroactively apply the new value — must fully right-click the buff off then rebuff; the ability tooltip shows the correct value even though the active buff icon may not — [[d:warlock#1407105337042931794]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[tank]** Master Demonologist's rework (removing the Voidwalker healing-taken bonus and other secondary demon buffs) is described by the community as effectively ending the dedicated warlock-tanking build — [[d:warlock#1398731987199459489]] (chinik, +1).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Naxxramas, positioning]** Faerlina: drag pet out of Rain of Fire. Thaddius: stack in the middle before Polarity Shift so the demon avoids the lightning. Razuvious: hide pet from the shout, or on the last shout let the imp die instead. Four Horsemen: drag pet out of the void zone. Sapphiron: drag pet out of the blizzard and behind blocks. Kel'Thuzad: drag pet out of the blizzard. Chess Event / trash (AQ40, per same message): drag behind a pillar or it may die if mispositioned. "Rupt & Medivh" content: stay out of flamestrikes — [[d:warlock#1400326250769289267]] (Nezu, 2025-07-30).
- **[Naxx Four Horsemen]** Demon frequently dies to accumulated damage/Mark mechanic; even in a "progressing guild" the demon died on the second horseman — [[d:warlock#1418726573242323167]] (L-star, 2025-09-20).
- **[Naxx Heigan]** Demon usually dies during the dance, or if it's the Imp it gets "burnt dry" — [[d:warlock#1418671899780710432]], [[d:warlock#1418726573242323167]] (L-star).
- **[Naxx Thaddius/Kel'Thuzad-tier trash]** Demon can die to accumulated AoE lightning damage on slower kills; survives more often on fast kills — [[d:warlock#1418726573242323167]] (L-star).
- **[Naxx Loatheb/Gluth/KT-adjacent bosses]** Little point running Demonology on these — greaters die too fast or are useless on trash/boss itself; only worthwhile to drop an Infernal briefly for specific mechanical windows (e.g. when the boss/adds step down) — [[d:warlock#1418726573242323167]] (L-star).
- **[BRD, Ogre trash / Tribute run]** Immolate pulls of 2-3 ogres, tab Searing Pain if DPS can't focus fire; the ogre knock-up costs extra mana to recast Hellfire; on the final boss, send the Void demon onto the second ogre — [[d:warlock#1396515922520440964]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[Twin Emperors]** Warlock can main-tank as early as level 60 with Wrathstone + Soul Link; see tank-build and Stats sections for the specific talent link and 221/315 Shadow Resist target — [[d:warlock#1383202005849346160]], [[d:warlock#1383441339777286214]], [[d:warlock#1413054952183693343]].
- **[Kara10 ("Tower of Karazhan")]** Warlock repeatedly solo-tanked Howlfang there — [[d:warlock#1400226811492831315]] (Summonjutsu, 2025-07-31).
- **[Karazhan Crypts, 5-man]** The worm boss can emerge from its hole to reach players outside expected range (poster unsure if this is a bug) — [[d:warlock#1379816702786801864]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[Anomalus]** For this arcane-heavy encounter, players debated Demonology+Felhunter (60 arcane resist from the pet) vs. Destro(SM/Ruin)+Voidwalker/Felstone self-healing combo — no consensus recorded, framed as a live decision — [[d:warlock#1386680824908349440]] (Pruva - Bazinga, question).
- **[Molten Core, Baron Geddon]** Applies a mana-draining debuff to players standing too close — relevant to greater-demon positioning and Health Funnel range management (20-yard range) — [[d:warlock#1388886548489044029]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[AQ20 Viscidus]** One guild's attempt failed at 1% due to needing more Nature Resistance / frost weapons — [[d:warlock#1383213508581396593]] (fizzlegoof, general progression context, not warlock-specific mechanic).

## PvP
- **[general, post-1.18-preview]** The Curse of Exhaustion nerf (30%→10% baseline) is seen as a major blow to world-PvP kiting/self-peel for all non-Affliction specs; SL/NF (tanky Soul-Link/Nightfall build) is seen as outdated relative to the current burst-meta power creep — geared opponents still delete undergeared warlocks regardless of spec choice — [[d:warlock#1394591408991502498]], [[d:warlock#1394715478206972045]], [[d:warlock#1394880947274514533]] (Suade).
- **[destro, pre-1.18]** Conflagrate+Shadowburn burst could two-shot a geared player in blues; Curse of Exhaustion described as "basically required" for PvP viability at that time — [[d:warlock#1390864415443652741]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[demo, gimmick build]** Deep Demonology PvP trick on fresh Ambershire 60s: sacrifice Voidwalker for the shield buff, then summon Infernal (via PO) combined with a grenade to stun the target — the Infernal can 2-shot most targets and 1-shot clothies on a crit; also strong in dungeons as a fresh 60 (10-15k damage from an Infernal in under a minute before PO kills it) — [[d:warlock#1412448883094716540]], [[d:warlock#1412449625176277042]] (Mima).
- **[general, complaint]** Warlock has almost no self-peel in PvP: only an unreliable, easily-broken 3-second Fear cast and a 2-minute Death Coil; no instant Howl for Affliction; losing Curse of Exhaustion access outside Affliction is seen as a significant class-wide kiting nerf — [[d:warlock#1399432909311574158]], [[d:warlock#1399427460201844872]] (Summonjutsu).
- **[affliction, buff]** The reworked pushback-resist talent (5→2 points) now also prevents pushback on Fear and Howl of Terror, called "a huge buff when you're under pressure in pvp and pve" — [[d:warlock#1405907548908949636]] (Lightemgee).
- **[general, single-source complaint]** One player reported feeling that fear/CC is largely non-functional in some BG matchups on Turtle (Shaman Tremor Totem effectively 0 CD, Hunter pets fear-resistant) — posed as frustration, not corroborated or rebutted elsewhere in the chunk — [[d:warlock#1418322782692966520]] (Connavear, single source).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[macro]** DemonControl (Goajos): mouseover-priority pet-attack/follow Lua function; a later revision adds a `PetFollow()` fallback branch when neither mouseover nor target is attackable — [[d:warlock#1384088702292070531]], improved version [[d:warlock#1407088217047437487]].
- **[macro, Cursive]** Nested-if Cursive macro for Curse of Agony→Corruption→Siphon Life→Shadow Bolt priority, written to correctly account for Malediction's auto-applied CoA — [[d:warlock#1394411972975526041]] (biloen, +1).
- **[macro limitation]** CleveRoidMacros can't natively detect the need to "refresh" a Cursive-managed curse; workaround is nesting the Cursive-cast macro inside a separate CleveRoidMacros macro — [[d:warlock#1394420424137248860]] (Don't ping me, +1).
- **[macro, wand]** Fix for double-press cancelling autorepeat Shoot: `/run for i=1,120 do if IsAutoRepeatAction(i) then return end end CastSpellByName("Shoot")`; alternative is a dedicated "toggle" addon; the Vanilla-Macros GitHub repo has a Warlock-specific macro section — [[d:warlock#1395112623443411066]] (cteven, +1).
- **[macro, soul shards]** Two shared Lua macros to auto-delete excess Soul Shards above a kept-stack count (`MAX_SOUL_SHARD`); one version had a stray extra `end` that broke it, fixed by Trenyc — [[d:warlock#1389222697195671652]] (valthorn), [[d:warlock#1407063315817173126]] (Goajos), bugfix [[d:warlock#1407084024832462858]] (Trenyc).
- **[macro, pfUI]** libdebuff-based "smart Affliction" macro that scans active target debuffs and casts the next missing curse/dot, with a Ctrl-modifier branch for Siphon Life — [[d:warlock#1408059914835988672]] (Goajos), generalized version [[d:warlock#1408063770936348694]].
- **[addon]** ScaledLifeTap (ported from a classic addon collection) auto-selects Life Tap rank by gear/mana/health; the addon UI doesn't work on Turtle but the macro command `/run ScaledLifetap_CastLifeTap()` does, and can be combined with an auto-Demon-Armor check — [[d:warlock#1382311101655679047]] (Deleted User).
- **[macro, bug]** A Zandalarian Hero Charm on-use macro broke after a client patch (".13"): throws "another activity in progress" and casts Soul Fire without using the trinket — [[d:warlock#1400269519141863486]] (L-star, reported to dev Pepopo).
- **[addon]** ProcDoc recommended for tracking Nightfall procs: https://github.com/wsmaxcy/ProcDoc — [[d:warlock#1385432799044960297]] (Zoogle, +3).
- **[addon]** Necrosis has a partial working port on the wiki, but its firestone/spellstone auto-equip logic is broken because Turtle made those consumable instead of equippable; a player is maintaining a personal fork to fix Turtle-specific behavior and wants to add SuperWoW-based DoT tracking since native DoT tracking is "completely broken" — [[d:warlock#1412852565548204102]], [[d:warlock#1412856492691816469]] (Ilithyia).
- **[addon]** MPOWA can build DoT-duration weakauras-like trackers but can't easily account for haste-modified tick speed/duration changes (open limitation, no fix given) — [[d:warlock#1412716375293693982]] (Doodluv, question unanswered).
- **[client CVars]** Channel-queueing behavior controlled by `NP_QueueChannelingSpells` (default 1, enables queuing during channels), `NP_DoubleCastToEndChannelEarly` (default 0, double-casting the same spell within 350ms ends the channel early), `NP_InterruptChannelsOutsideQueueWindow` (default 0); `SpellStopCasting()` also interrupts channels — [[d:warlock#1379209243172929556]] (Pepopo).
- **[technique]** With GCD-aware channel queueing, simply clicking the next spell once the GCD is over cleanly ends a channel (Drain Soul/Dark Harvest) without needing a stop-cast macro — [[d:warlock#1412020999515541585]] (Hyasur, question, technique offered).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[gold/farming]** Warlock-friendly gold routes: Dire Maul: East solo farming (~100 GPH); ZG Bijou farming route (dolls, Hakkari veins, herbs, bijous from tigers/crocodiles); free 60% mount at level 40 and a discounted 100% mount at 60; can solo most non-undead elite quests; good at boosting/twink-leveling alts via vampiric gear in low-level dungeons (note: twink-powerleveling via glyphs is explicitly forbidden) — [[d:warlock#1396757265037656066]] (CheddarBob).
- **[profession fit]** Tailoring said to pair poorly with warlock (competes with First Aid for cloth); Engineering called "selfish" (mostly BoP) but broadly useful; Jewelcrafting (paired with Mining) recommended for self-sourcing staves/rings/trinkets — [[d:warlock#1409459823342260338]], [[d:warlock#1409460988142686310]] (Enginuity).
- **[profession, leveling]** Enchanting discouraged while leveling a warlock — see Leveling section for the full breakdown — [[d:warlock#1384527459050852442]] (Mirror, +1).
- **[population]** Warlock considered one of the least-played classes, cited as a reason gear/loot competition for the role is low ("if a guild is in dire need of warlock the gear doesn't matter") — [[d:warlock#1385189755158921248]] (Karen).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[general]** Soul Shard occasionally auto-adds to the bag with an audible ping even when the bag has zero shards and the player is out of demon range or on a flight when it despawns — described as "seems rather random," cause unresolved — [[d:warlock#1395669594311753811]] (Deleted User).
- **[demo, Doomguard]** Infernal Blade's DoT component doesn't deal damage at all (debuff applies, tick is absent); only the initial 30% fire-melee-damage bonus works, scaling off Doomguard AP, fire SP, and double-dipping crit — [[d:warlock#1408382327322706004]] (Nezu, +7).
- **[general]** Pet Command "Take Position" behaves as an active/GCD-adjacent spell that can't be issued while casting or channeling another spell — [[d:warlock#1408382327322706004]].
- **[affliction, Malediction]** Curse of Agony applied through Malediction doesn't get the hit% bonus from gear or Suppression, causing excessive resists; it also fails to refresh/reapply if the target already has a Malediction-sourced CoA — [[d:warlock#1408382327322706004]].
- **[item proc]** True Band of Sulfuras proc chance was ~58% pre-hotfix (likely bugged), reduced to roughly 12% observed after; only checks on the initial application of curses/dots (not subsequent ticks) but checks every tick for direct fire/shadow hits and fire-dot ticks specifically — [[d:warlock#1385998011657621525]] (Nezu, +9), confirmation [[d:warlock#1391827383702782064]] (Summonjutsu, +1), [[d:warlock#1416150936950276096]] (Cinamo/Odessyus).
- **[item proc, open question]** Whether Chromie's Broken Pocketwatch or True Band of Sulfuras trigger off DoTs / Drain Soul / Dark Harvest was unresolved at chunk end; Blade of Eternal Darkness is confirmed to NOT trigger off those, checked via the same underlying mechanic — [[d:warlock#1419661959187529730]] (Bigdots, question unanswered).
- **[destro, tooltip]** The AQ20 3-piece set bonus tooltip (Immolate cast-time reduction + Conflagrate interaction) was edited mid-testing with ambiguous new wording, leaving it unclear whether Conflagrate deals bonus damage on top of, or instead of, its normal amount — [[d:warlock#1399209176898474077]] (Agitation / Jinxi, question unanswered).
- **[general, timing quirk]** A gear swap or a Demonic Sacrifice buff applied after a projectile spell (e.g. Shadow Bolt) is already cast does NOT retroactively benefit that in-flight cast, because the server doesn't immediately register gear changes — unlike curse/DoT ticks, which do appear to re-check mid-flight (cited as the explanation for Curse of Tongues affecting bats in ZG mid-cast) — [[d:warlock#1381790852350869564]] (Summonjutsu, not 100% certain), [[d:warlock#1382074044144291890]].
- **[tank, buff refresh]** Demon Armor/Demonic Aegis: adding armor-talent points while the Demon Armor buff is already active does not update its stats until the buff is fully removed and reapplied — [[d:warlock#1407105337042931794]] (Summonjutsu).
