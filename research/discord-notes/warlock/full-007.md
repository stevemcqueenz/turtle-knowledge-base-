## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[general]** Fresh-60 consensus: start **Affliction** because Suppression gives 10% spell hit from talents (only ~6% more needed from gear), then transition to SM/Ruin, Demo, or Destro once hit-itemized; switching early "isn't a big DPS loss" and lets you learn the spec — [[d:warlock#1494696547101966597]] [[d:warlock#1493328974787645481]] [[d:warlock#1490901049777721516]] (Shortmo/Fauxnetic/L-star).
- **[demonology]** Demo has a **soft hit cap around 6-8%**: the demon automatically gets ~+3% hit plus ~90% of the warlock's own hit%, so only ~5-6% player hit is needed for the demon to reach its own soft cap — [[d:warlock#1494300694071480412]] [[d:warlock#1494311207350567124]] [[d:warlock#1494313197874970705]] [[d:warlock#1494350275799023756]] (Blackfox/Wildpants thread). Demo is viable well under full hit-cap: "8-10% hit is fine" [[d:warlock#1494849835294134284]] (Lightemgee); disputed-strong claim "hit cap is a meme, demo lock in greens beats naxx-geared affli if played well" — [[d:warlock#1492510081638469772]] (L-star, +1).
- **[demonology]** Demo judged top/strongest raid spec once hit/gear-checked, especially after the "Black Book" trinket drops in BWL; Affliction is better early (MC) due to low ambient crit/hit availability — [[d:warlock#1489707674479104113]] [[d:warlock#1489707780800643238]] [[d:warlock#1489707890246680646]] (Mima).
- **[fire/destro]** Firelock (Shadow/Fire destro) is fine post-BWL but can't compete with drain(affli)/demo; "slightly better than SM/Ruin if you have enough spell hit" — [[d:warlock#1493286788775805198]] (Fauxnetic). Called "big bursty, fun in dungeon, not raid viable atm" [[d:warlock#1493290501674373180]] (Fr6'P, +1) and "temu arcane mage" [[d:warlock#1493300860548153365]] (biloen, +2). Destro specifically performs well on the Kara40 final boss Mephistroph — [[d:warlock#1494334535775682633]] (Rob0t).

- **[general]** AoE leveling possible from ~level 44-60 via heavy Demo→Destro, or typical 2-4 target juggling with fears+pet at lower levels — [[d:warlock#1488833805732286534]] [[d:warlock#1488834501370056754]] [[d:warlock#1488834636430708887]] (Qazdur).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[affliction]** Hit cap is **16%**, entirely from gear — Shadowbolt (and non-affliction spells generally) do not benefit from the Affliction tree's 10% hit talent, so a Suppression-affliction build still needs 16% from gear for Shadowbolt/Dark Harvest/Death Coil-type casts; the Emerald Blessing buff adds +1% hit for everyone, and Curse of Shadow(s) affects hit too — [[d:warlock#1491843936715542548]] [[d:warlock#1491844442049478677]] (Araz/Kris). A destro gear-planner example reached hit-cap "on Amber, not full BiS but doable" needing ~15% from gear with one Suppression-line point — [[d:warlock#1488934843235569815]] [[d:warlock#1488934924147888360]] (PBnJames).
- **[demonology]** Demo soft hit cap ~6-8% (see Spec viability) — demon inherits ~90% of player hit plus a flat +3% baseline — [[d:warlock#1494300694071480412]] [[d:warlock#1494311207350567124]] [[d:warlock#1494313197874970705]] [[d:warlock#1494350275799023756]].
- **[affliction]** Haste is compounding — its SP-equivalent scales with both existing SP and existing haste. At 500 SP / 0% haste, +1% haste ≈ **+13.24 SP** averaged across Affliction spells (Drain Soul ≈50% of total Aff damage); at 500 SP / 10% haste, the next +1% haste ≈ **+14.72 SP** — [[d:warlock#1489759140824748163]] [[d:warlock#1489762952562212954]] [[d:warlock#1489763649416593498]] (Taffy, chart: per-spell SP-equivalent of 1% haste at 0/500/1000 base SP for Dark Harvest/Agony/Corruption/Drain Soul/Siphon Life). A companion table shows Drain Soul's channel duration shortening with haste and flags "wasted" haste bands where the visible tick count doesn't change until the next threshold (e.g. 11%→12% no change, jumps at 13%) — [[d:warlock#1489765045180043306]] [[d:warlock#1489765205863829604]] (Taffy, +2). **Disputed**: extensive community testing found these apparent breakpoints are purely a display-rounding artifact with no real effective-haste cliffs — [[d:warlock#1489889352417935473]] (Nezu/Papaverine/Mooncrazed, +1, "extensively tested").
- **[general]** Rough separate haste-to-SP rule cited: at ~1000 SP, 1% haste ≈ 20 SP — [[d:warlock#1492214613410844753]] (Refrigerador); directly disputed — "haste isn't that great; stacking spell damage provides a greater increase" — [[d:warlock#1492213319711588462]] (yugi). Contested.
- **[affliction]** DoTs and channeled spells cannot crit in this build, so deep Affliction gets **no value from Crit or Intellect**; only Stamina, shadow damage, hit, and haste matter — [[d:warlock#1492247338750836817]] [[d:warlock#1492248746052948131]] [[d:warlock#1492250052742217868]] (Dagorath/Lynathel). Corruption needs 15% haste to reach the 2.55s/tick breakpoint enabling a 7th tick in an 18s duration — [[d:warlock#1492252383856824461]] (biloen).
- **[affliction]** T1 3-piece set bonus (Drain Soul +5% damage) is worth ≈**48-50 spell power** in practice, measured on a training dummy excluding Dark Harvest usage — [[d:warlock#1495056389842931802]] [[d:warlock#1495057004782288936]] (biloen, "48.2 spellpower" precisely).

- **[demonology]** Fel Intellect is better invested in Imp than Voidwalker; demon spell power scales off the demon's own Intellect, which can only be raised via 3/3 Fel Intellect or party/raid-wide buffs (Arcane Brilliance, Blessing of the Wild) — not from the demon's base stat — [[d:warlock#1491197283134341284]] (Summonjutsu); "Fel Intellect effect = demon spell power scales with demon's Int; more Int on the warlock = stronger demon" — [[d:warlock#1492152469722239006]] (Stunfisk).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[threat/design]** Warlock threat design explained in depth: Soothing Kiss reduces 30% of *total accumulated threat* (not a flat wipe like Priest Fade, which also subtracts a flat -600); vanilla warlocks were balanced around low spell hit and RNG-heavy threat, and that underlying design carried over to Turtle despite QoL improvements — [[d:warlock#1490107045024628828]] (Summonjutsu, long). This matches stated dev philosophy that "threat management" is core to warlock identity — [[d:warlock#1490049086643113994]] (biloen). No hard/instant threat wipe exists; players have long asked for either a hard Voidwalker taunt or a Soul-Fire-taunts-on-Wrathstone-equip mechanic, neither implemented as of this chunk — [[d:warlock#1489010911137234985]] [[d:warlock#1489011725347848404]] [[d:warlock#1489013280331792594]] (Lightemgee/Summonjutsu; contested/open).
- **[general]** Positioning trick to reduce pet threat friction: send the Voidwalker/pet a few seconds ahead of the current kill target to bank a threat lead without needing Demonology's threat talent — [[d:warlock#1490124228727537755]] (Chandalen).
- **[demonology]** Greater-demon summon rules: Infernal learnable via quest at level 50+, Doomguard via quest at level 60, Felguard via a BWL item drop at level 60; all three deal significantly more damage than the four permanent demons but only last 3 minutes and then turn hostile unless killed. **Without Soul Link** they have an increasing chance to break free early (unreliable); **with Soul Link** they reliably last close to the full duration — [[d:warlock#1490963153725427792]] [[d:warlock#1490963345967419542]] (sailorVEVO).
- **[bug/demonology]** A broken-loose greater demon gets **deleted** if the raid heals it (or something near it) for more than ~600 HP, or if it takes/deals enough damage while in combat — [[d:warlock#1489971548717580419]] [[d:warlock#1489971626060677290]] [[d:warlock#1489972009587703899]] (Stunfisk); criticized as a bug — players expect it to instead "wreck everything" after breaking free — [[d:warlock#1489972520257061085]] (Oronok).
- **[demonology]** Summoning a greater demon forces Affliction/Destro warlocks (who sacrifice a permanent demon for a buff) to re-sacrifice a fresh permanent demon afterward — an annoying resummon/resac loop — [[d:warlock#1489967871290380401]] [[d:warlock#1489967920590356573]] (Stunfisk).
- **[UI/utility]** Greater-demon and other pet ability hotkeys reset to default positions every time a different demon is summoned, with no way to save a per-demon custom keybind; suggested workaround is a `CastSpellByName()` macro to fire all pet abilities at once — [[d:warlock#1489260709991678163]] [[d:warlock#1489264224944525402]] (Outed/L-star).
- **[demonology]** Unleashed Potential-related on-use spell power trinkets check the SP maximum on the *first* proc stack and the AP total maximum on the *last* stack, for demon-scaling purposes — [[d:warlock#1490120774319603772]] (Summonjutsu).

## Tanking specifics
- **[demonology/tank]** "Void tanking" (drain-tanking, not shadow-tanking) is viable but weak post-nerf, described as an "armorless bear tank"; can reach ~70% PDR by potting Stoneshield every 2 min; only practical once geared with Wrathstone + Soul Link. Requires push-back protection on Searing Pain/Immolate, watching for silence/interrupt/spell-lockout, rotating demon per situation, and prioritizing higher-armor gear (cheap armor kits help early) — [[d:warlock#1489010489030606961]] [[d:warlock#1489010548304646306]] [[d:warlock#1489010739388747827]] (Summonjutsu/Lightemgee).

Proposals (unimplemented as of this chunk): a hard instant Void taunt, or Soul Fire taunting when Wrathstone is equipped — [[d:warlock#1489011725347848404]] [[d:warlock#1489013280331792594]] [[d:warlock#1489010911137234985]] (Lightemgee/Summonjutsu; contested/open). ## PvP
- **[general]** Race guidance: Undead for pure PvP (fear immunity via Will of the Forsaken), Orc for Demo (pet-damage synergy), Troll for Affliction (regen/berserking) — or simply pick preference since the practical gap is marginal — [[d:warlock#1488865404519583745]] (suddeNslow, +2). Orc's Blood Fury gives +120 spell power for 15s (≈ a free trinket) plus 5% pet damage, now considered the best pure-damage race pick after the stun-resist racials (e.g. Human's) were nerfed hard; Will of the Forsaken (Undead) remains the best PvP racial for its fear/CC immunity despite a 30%→15% nerf to one of its effects — [[d:warlock#1491110311535706296]] [[d:warlock#1491111022877216972]] (Cyric). Undead's +2% damage-vs-Humanoid racial explicitly does **not** apply against players — [[d:warlock#1491164815488450784]] (Lightemgee, +1).
- **[affliction]** Affliction is the dominant PvP spec, with Curse of Exhaustion cited as a defining strength; very few Demonology locks are seen in BGs — [[d:warlock#1489224572174602251]] [[d:warlock#1489224625480274061]] (Fuggle the Dampened). Curse of Exhaustion itself was nerfed and is now "hard to use if you aren't Affliction" — [[d:warlock#1492287674206261402]] [[d:warlock#1492351199847120906]] (pyro87/Lightemgee).
- **[pvp/dampening]** Dampening and Resilience double-stack against warlocks specifically because most warlock damage is DoT-based, both of which those mechanics target; a fully T3-geared PvP lock reportedly does close to naked-level damage — [[d:warlock#1490175529209430066]] [[d:warlock#1490651019904946236]] (Summonjutsu/Fuggle; repeatedly disputed as overblown by [[d:warlock#1490666504985903195]] [[d:warlock#1491187985486581912]], Actualrodent/Afraido: "target dies 1-2 sec later, not a big deal"). - **[pvp/ranges]** Cross-class 36-yard-class spell-range comparison shared: Affliction 36yd (all major spells), Frost Mage 36, Fire Mage 41-42, Arcane Mage 30, Smite Priest 36 (extendable to ~41yd via ZG healing-power items — called "crazy OP artillery," though generally not worth itemizing for), Shadow Priest 36, Elemental Shaman 36, Balance Druid 36 — [[d:warlock#1491179322055196702]] [[d:warlock#1491180584301498388]] (Afraido).

Destro is preferred by some over full Affliction in PvP specifically because its slows/stuns/crit burst can secure kills vs. kiting classes like Warriors, where Affliction's sustained-DoT approach struggles — [[d:warlock#1494966922104537178]] (Livius).
- **[contested]** Whether Warriors' Blood Craze + lifesteal + Troll racial can out-heal Affliction's DoT damage (effectively "ignoring" dots) was argued at length without full resolution; Warrior is broadly agreed to be the class that benefits most from the dampening patch — [[d:warlock#1491187257820971149]] [[d:warlock#1491186428787560668]] [[d:warlock#1491184560787165466]] (Ionnatra/Benched).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[general]** Race guidance repeated from PvP section: Undead/Orc/Troll per spec, or personal preference (see PvP) — [[d:warlock#1488865404519583745]].
- **[gold]** Warlock mount quest costs ~400-450g plus ~4 hours of questing at level 60, and awards both the mount summon and epic (100%) riding for less than the ~900g other classes pay standalone for epic riding; another warlock supplying materials (J'eevee's Jar 150g, Xorothian Glyphs 50g, Black Lodestone 50g) saves ~250g — all other quest materials vary by AH price — [[d:warlock#1489054932010139688]] [[d:warlock#1489055061203222768]] [[d:warlock#1494819680614088744]] (vincentfm/Fauxnetic). The Dreadsteed (epic mount via DMW quest chain) cost as little as ~500g total with help vs ~1000g+ solo — dungeon access (DMW/Scholomance) is the bigger practical hurdle than the gold — [[d:warlock#1492529481628385441]] (Lynathel).
- **[profession]** To progress past Engineering skill 150, you must train Expert Engineering from an Artisan+ trainer (e.g., Roxxik in Orgrimmar) — visiting only "an expert" trainer is insufficient — [[d:warlock#1489409623713779732]] [[d:warlock#1489410399588843631]] (Valaen). General profession sentiment: Turtle WoW professions are "not really refined"; for gold, any gathering profession works, while Engineering is cited as the main end-game option for others — [[d:warlock#1490731148475896000]] (Afraido).
- **[gold/farming]** DME lasher farming with a dedicated vamp-life-drain set is efficient (~6 min/run via a 3+1+2+2 pack pull pattern) but capped at 4 instance resets/hour; some substitute pure-Stamina gear + Demo spec + occasional food if bag space for a vamp set isn't available — [[d:warlock#1490300232930427093]] [[d:warlock#1490300383678169118]] [[d:warlock#1490300864563253249]] (Tapka/NikeOne). Moonwhisper Coast elemental farming (elementals, draenethyst formations) is called out as a good alternate source of soul shards + gold — [[d:warlock#1492123161888817222]] (Metsys). MC/ZG weekly raid quests are also a strong, low-effort gold source — [[d:warlock#1491190242328445211]] (Ionnatra).
