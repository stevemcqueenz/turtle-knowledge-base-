## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[general]** 1.18.1 "release" still pending at time of chat; community references "Stay tuned for more info on 1.18.1 (and a release date) in the near future" and jokes it may be delayed like the K40 launch (~4 months late) — [[d:warrior#1451150088444448992]] (Sylvaira, 2025-12-18; single source, unconfirmed date).

- **[dps/design]** Slam was heavily buffed on Turtle: increased damage scaling, made to work while affected by Flurry, and its old vanilla behavior of resetting the swing timer was removed — [[d:warrior#1452744154659557517]] (Dodging, 2025-12-22).
- **[dps/mechanics]** Slam's cast time reduction from gear haste appears to "double dip": Lipibidy calculated their 11% gear haste produced the same 1.57s cast whether computed as flurry-then-haste or the reverse, i.e. Slam benefits from both attack-speed and cast-speed haste sources — [[d:warrior#1452748241178333335]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-22; Dodging says community consensus since 1.18 drop is that Slam double-dips Flurry specifically, disputed by Lipibidy who says that's mechanically unjustified — [[d:warrior#1452744486944899162]]).
- **[tank/threat]** Shield Slam threat was changed from vanilla's flat "damage + 270 bonus threat" to also scale with a ~1.5x threat multiplier on damage (on top of stance/Defiance multipliers), per Sylvaira's reading of the sheet (~2.25x total incl. stance) — [[d:warrior#1451340294107828425]], [[d:warrior#1451341885632155750]], math confirmed by Dodging/Lipibidy: 228 dmg ×1.5(ability)×1.51(stance+defiance)=516 threat — [[d:warrior#1451344867216199681]].
- **[tank]** Windfury Totem changed: now has a 1.5s ICD and no longer grants added AP (per 30's tanksim changelog) — [[d:warrior#1451656148166901912]] (30, 2025-12-19).
- **[general/dw]** Heroic Strike/Cleave "queue" bug was reportedly "fixed" (removed), which the community treats cynically ("fixed" is cope) — hit value discussion changes as a result; Dual Wield Specialization talent (+hit on offhand) was added specifically to compensate for HS-queue removal — [[d:warrior#1451433091288662087]], [[d:warrior#1451433242908426392]] (Nako/Dodging, 2025-12-19).
- **[misc]** Kara40 (Tower of Karazhan) polearm proc previously did not stack with Juju Power — confirmed as long-standing behavior, "it has never stacked" — [[d:warrior#1451953710346408020]] (30, 2025-12-20).
- **[tank]** New Kara40/Naxx/T3.5 set bonuses, off-pieces, weapons are still being added to the community tanksim; as of Dec 22 prot ability implementation, Shield Render/Kings Edict/Badge trinkets not yet modeled — [[d:warrior#1451656148166901912]] (30, 2025-12-19).

## Leveling
- **[general]** Warrior leveling: Turtle changes make some low-30s dungeon tanking possible; Improved Thunderclap (Arms tree) is called the best low-level tanking talent, useable in Defensive Stance in Turtle unlike vanilla, giving +60% threat/reduced cost — [[d:warrior#1451699416548376731]], [[d:warrior#1451700541070774303]] (Koae, 2025-12-20).
- **[leveling order]** Recommended leveling path (Koae): start Arms until ~50+ for the free damage/Sweeping Strikes/Improved Thunderclap value; dungeons are inefficient for leveling in Turtle since mobs hit much harder — quest instead and only run each dungeon once (until SM Armory, run a few times for gear); switch toward Prot only once you want to run dungeons over questing — [[d:warrior#1451698627926950000]], [[d:warrior#1451701137232363630]], [[d:warrior#1451701461942538343]] (Koae, 2025-12-20).
- **[prot leveling]** Prot leveling is slower than Arms/Fury but "doable," and from ~level 30 (Shield Slam unlocks) to ~40 it's on par with or better than other specs for single-target due to Shield Slam's low CD (4.5s) and partial rage refund; not viable for AoE/multi-mob speed, more of a "1v1 questing" style — [[d:warrior#1450673761039679582]] through [[d:warrior#1450679624341131294]] (Lÿenn, 2025-12-17). Lipibidy disputes prot ever keeping pace with Arms/Fury and says it's poor for leveling dungeon-tanking — [[d:warrior#1450675796875411458]] (2025-12-16).
- **[blood drinker / dual wield leveling]** "Blood Drinker" (a Fury talent path) is called the standout for DW leveling: gets much stronger by level 43 ("cruising"), recommended if soloing to 60 without dungeon-tank-queuing, and considered the best spec at 60 for solo/elite farming — [[d:warrior#1451111017034547360]], [[d:warrior#1451111228175810560]], [[d:warrior#1450679925429506108]] (Lipibidy/Kyrsie, 2025-12-17/18).
- **[2h tanking early]** 2H tanking (with a staff, using the 25% Parry buff — the specific talent/ability not named beyond "that 25% Parry Thing with the Staff from WC") is recommended over 1H+shield up to roughly level 50 for early dungeon tanking, citing better initial threat; swap to 1h+shield via macro when taking heavy damage — [[d:warrior#1450785397155172434]], [[d:warrior#1450785531402129470]] (VeganCourage, 2025-12-17). Once Sweeping Strikes and gear come online, 2H tanking becomes strong for dungeons, and Parry-haste from the buff even makes Master Strike a semi-viable damage button — [[d:warrior#1450790018732920872]], [[d:warrior#1450792629171257365]] (Vladizlav, 2025-12-16).
- **[dual wield fury leveling]** DW Fury is safest 40-60 leveling route with Blood Drinker per Kyrsie; good sword drops available from Scarlet Monastery and Razorfen Downs quests support this — [[d:warrior#1450521388128403558]], [[d:warrior#1450522772332609628]] (Kyrsie/Arle, 2025-12-16).
- **[stat prio while leveling]** With limited haste from gear compromises while leveling, prioritize Crit first, Haste later — [[d:warrior#1450529361219420311]] (Neurospicy, 2025-12-16).
- **[leveling weapons]** Vladizlav posted concrete pre-raid/leveling 2H weapon links by type (quest rewards, filterable via Turtle DB "Level req 0-1"): 2H Axe item 51892, 2H Sword item 13982, 2H Mace item 61074, Polearm item 19106, Staff items 17743 and 9683 — [[d:warrior#1451116712714960898]], [[d:warrior#1451136171798429901]] (Vladizlav, 2025-12-18).

## Rotation: single target
- **[2h fury]** Standard 2H Fury single-target priority: weave Slam between weapon swings like "a caster following a castbar with an instant cast" — `Slam > BT > Slam > WW > Slam > Slam, repeat`; if BT/WW are on CD, `Slam > Sunder` to fish for Windfury procs — [[d:warrior#1452742334142873855]], [[d:warrior#1452742539730747513]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-22/31). Community consensus: BT is still generally used ahead of Slam in a "normal" rotation even when Slam hits as hard, because you still need instant abilities to fill the wait for your next auto — [[d:warrior#1455667382033973298]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-31).
- **[2h fury timing]** Detailed swing-timing advice from Kyrsie: Slam should ideally land before your next auto attack lands without heavily delaying the swing; players "fish" for Windfury procs using low-damage filler abilities (Hamstring, Pummel, Master Strike, Sunder) with leftover rage so as not to waste damage on Slam when a WF proc would extend a swing anyway; 2H fury requires much more attention to swing-timer state than DW fury (DW "only manages one Slam per auto") — [[d:warrior#1454991016653226024]], [[d:warrior#1454991675779711139]], [[d:warrior#1454991896500764733]] (Kyrsie, 2025-12-29).
- **[arms 2-slams-per-swing]** Arms build can land 2 Slams within one auto-attack window if wielding a weapon speed of ~3.6+ (3.4 clips, 3.6 works, 3.8 is "ideal") — [[d:warrior#1454938660276408574]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-29).
- **[dw fury]** Fast MH / slow OH is the default recommended DW fury weapon-speed setup, but a significantly higher-DPS fast weapon can outperform a "textbook" slow MH in sims — sim results can look erratic if boss/fight config isn't set up correctly — [[d:warrior#1451573901451595789]], [[d:warrior#1451580295534088333]] (Dodging, 2025-12-19).
- **[dw fury priority]** Community-stated DW Fury priority: Execute > Bloodthirst > Whirlwind > Heroic Strike > Hamstring, with Execute-only during execute phase — [[d:warrior#1451583639702863927]] (Dorfmancer/Flunge, 2025-12-19; confirmed via testing after fixing wrong priority order).
- **[weapon comparisons]** Modrag (Mod) main-hand + Ironfoe (IF) offhand tested as more "consistent" DPS in sim vs IF-MH/Mod-OH which is "swingier" (higher ceiling, higher variance, ~700 vs ~620 dps spread across iterations); the gap narrowed drastically once Windfury Totem was turned off in the sim (WF was inflating variance) — [[d:warrior#1453228804385411212]], [[d:warrior#1453235460355920158]] (Dramafever, 2025-12-24; single-source testing).
- **[fast weapon vs slow]** Debate whether Crul/Hakkari Manslayer (fast axes) are best MH threat weapons for furyprot — Dodging notes Crul feels "kinda slow for a fprot mainhand" despite being an axe, implying fast-weapon preference for rage/HS-queue upkeep — [[d:warrior#1454244341949141176]], [[d:warrior#1454245116251209738]] (2025-12-27).
- **[flurry/slam interaction — bug]** Multiple reports (Yosh, with video clips) that Slam inconsistently consumes Flurry charges even though only auto-attacks should — described by others as likely a UI/animation-timing illusion (the auto and Slam landing near-simultaneously) rather than an actual charge-consumption bug, though not conclusively resolved in this chunk — [[d:warrior#1452069421253263401]] through [[d:warrior#1452083799218323496]] (Yosh/Lokimanor, 2025-12-21; contested/unresolved).
- **[HS queue behavior change]** Heroic Strike queue "auto-unqueues" if your rage falls below the ability's rage cost (recent change); this can break macros that previously relied on HS staying queued even below-cost, requiring closer rage monitoring — [[d:warrior#1455614353712611440]], [[d:warrior#1455638508759941214]] (Kyrsie/Lipibidy, 2025-12-30).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[hit cap]** Melee hit cap vs a level-63 target is 8% (not 9%); Turtle explicitly removed vanilla's raid-boss +1% innate miss chance that some players expect from classic — [[d:warrior#1451430729769680999]], [[d:warrior#1451430955867963474]] (Dodging, 2025-12-19). Weapon skill above 300 reduces hit needed by 0.2% per point, capping at 315 skill; weapon skill also reduces glancing-blow damage penalty — [[d:warrior#1451429993627385989]] (Lÿenn, 2025-12-19).
- **[dual wield hit]** Dual wielding applies a hit penalty to yellow (special-ability) hit chance equal to your white (auto-attack) hit chance when DW; using only one weapon (e.g., unequipping OH) reveals your "true" ability-miss chance, which is otherwise hidden/buggy in the character pane while DW — [[d:warrior#1451432445911105687]], [[d:warrior#1451432684860473374]], [[d:warrior#1451432914238701608]] (Arle/Dodging, 2025-12-19; both MH and OH are penalized while DW). The Dual Wield Specialization talent gives +hit specifically to the offhand to compensate for the removal of the old HS-queue "bug" — [[d:warrior#1451433091288662087]], [[d:warrior#1451433242908426392]] (Nako/Dodging, 2025-12-19).
- **[general dps stat prio]** Lipibidy's rule of thumb for gearing: "Str is good, str+agi extra good, str+crit very good, str+agi+crit super good, str+crit+ap also good" — prioritize hit cap, then look for these stat combos on gear rather than chasing a rigid, fixed prebis list — [[d:warrior#1451418465884704808]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-19; contested by chopperdutch/Lÿenn who want a firmer stat-priority order, see Contested section).
- **[DW fury stat order]** Lÿenn's stated priority for DW fury: Hit cap (8%, adjusted for weapon skill) > Str/AP > Crit/Agi > Haste, with the caveat that if you're already very high in one stat (e.g., 32%+ unbuffed crit) it loses relative value — [[d:warrior#1451429993627385989]] (Lÿenn, 2025-12-19).
- **[str/agi attribute value]** Attribute stats (Str, Agi) are considered better than flat AP/crit-rating in some cases because they scale multiplicatively with Kings (+10%), so any attribute stat can be treated as roughly "10% more" than its raw number suggests — [[d:warrior#1451420399081492553]] (Dodging, 2025-12-19).
- **[2h fury haste]** For 2H Fury, gear Haste vs flat Str/AP: at lower gear levels Str is better; at Kara40-level gear, Haste becomes arguably better since it stacks multiplicatively and the value curve steepens with more existing haste — but the difference in most scenarios is small enough that either choice is "not a bad decision" — [[d:warrior#1452670641634410526]] through [[d:warrior#1452671617128726578]] (Virasan, 2025-12-22).
- **[block value / SBV]** Shield Block Value (SBV) is a low-priority stat that only meaningfully helps threat via Shield Slam's SBV-based damage component; it's not a great scaling stat and mostly relevant at endgame gearing levels, valued more for defensive gearing than as a threat lever — [[d:warrior#1452919711930257520]]/[[d:warrior#1451316778293792799]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-19/23).
- **[defense/avoidance cap]** Uncrushable ("102.4% avoidance") is achieved via Shield Block + Improved Shield Slam (~75% from these alone per Lipibidy) rather than stacking pure Defense; hard Defense-cap chasing for crit-immunity is called a "boomer strat" since Shield Block already prevents crits on bosses where it matters — [[d:warrior#1452840513941012594]], [[d:warrior#1452843626064515196]] (Lipibidy/Black Hat, 2025-12-23).
- **[defense vs threat sim finding]** Community threat-sim testing (30's sim) found adding 80 Defense via a "delta" only lowered TPS by about 1%, suggesting the commonly-repeated claim that "Defense actively kills threat" is overstated once controlling for the fact you're not also giving up other stats to itemize for it — [[d:warrior#1453534570606497841]], [[d:warrior#1453538215746212001]], [[d:warrior#1453565422958940291]] (Nost/30/Dodging, 2025-12-25; caveat from Dodging that a delta-value test doesn't reflect actually swapping gear to get that Defense, so real-world tradeoff still applies).
- **[anticipation talent]** Similarly, sim testing suggested Anticipation's "hate" (crit-avoidance talent seen as low-value/threat-negative) may be overstated — Black Hat notes running 2/3 Anticipation lets him wear less Defense gear on specific fights (e.g. Twins) — [[d:warrior#1453565422958940291]], [[d:warrior#1453565663011405895]] (Black Hat, 2025-12-25).
- **[effective health]** Lipibidy gives a detailed EH explainer: Effective Health = total health modified by real (guaranteed) mitigation like armor/block; it strictly excludes avoidance (dodge/parry/miss) because avoidance is not reliable/guaranteed — EH is used to calculate worst-case swings-to-survive so healers know their margin; became far more important in TBC/WotLK due to burstier boss damage profiles, less relevant in vanilla-style content — [[d:warrior#1452526138751586459]] through [[d:warrior#1452528645540282430]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-22; disputed by Nost who says avoidance IS commonly included in some EH formulas elsewhere, a difference of definition rather than fact).
- **[rage mechanics]** 30 posted the exact rage-generation formula used in the community tanksim: `((((dmg / 230.6) * 7.5) / 1.075) + ((speed * 3.5) / 2.25))` for old/vanilla-style formula, with an offhand variant using different constants (1.75/2.4 in one component) — under investigation/testing whether Thunderfury's proc generates rage at all (testing suggested it may generate 0 rage under some conditions, unclear if a Turtle-specific bug) — [[d:warrior#1452415109589893150]], [[d:warrior#1452415188555927583]], [[d:warrior#1452420835230089247]] (30/Don't ping me, 2025-12-22; unresolved).
- **[weapon skill]** Weapon skill's practical impact on white-hit damage/threat is small: removing "Gift of Arthas" was cited as only a ~30 TPS loss in one furyprot config, and weapon skill mostly affects white-hit damage/associated rage generation, of which fully-geared characters see relatively few — [[d:warrior#1454620694200713430]], [[d:warrior#1454620925394817326]] (Black Hat/30, 2025-12-28).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[general prebis guidance]** No single canonical prebis list exists community-wide; repeated advice is to use AtlasLoot/the Turtle item database with the good-stats heuristic above and simply run: SW Vault (SWV), Black Morass (BM), Karazhan Crypt (KC), Scarlet Monastery/Scholomance/Dire Maul, and BRD as "endgame pre-raid dungeons" — [[d:warrior#1453613945188253869]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-25).
- **[T2.5 "Heroism" set]** Bulwark/Justice/Persistence of the Brotherhood (T3.5 in some contexts, but referenced multiple times as the "3.5" or Heroism-tier set) bonuses: **3pc** "Bulwark of the Brotherhood": reduces damage taken from melee, ranged, and spell attacks by 10% for 10s; **Justice of the Brotherhood**: crit damage +5%; **Persistence of the Brotherhood**: attack speed +10% — [[d:warrior#1450995852943294585]], [[d:warrior#1452524555376525343]] (30, 2025-12-18/22).
- **[T2.5 vs T3]** Wearing full T2.5 (8/8 Heroism) vs T3/T3.5 pieces mostly comes down to raw stats plus set bonus tie-breaking; players are advised to gear for the highest raw stats and let set bonus be the deciding factor rather than chasing full-set bonuses at the expense of stats — [[d:warrior#1450249426265444464]], [[d:warrior#1450250308973494312]] (Nost/Lipibidy, 2025-12-16/27). Off-set pieces like Plated Abomination Ribcage and Carnage Legs are called better than T3 DPS equivalents and are often passed up because DPS warriors chase full-T3 for the set bonus — [[d:warrior#1454281915476480120]] (Nost, 2025-12-27).
- **[T3.5 3pc for tanks]** Community sim testing found the T3.5 3-set threat bonus for tanks is surprisingly small ("only a couple hundred TPS," described in one 16-sec parse test as minimal) — [[d:warrior#1453538392640983203]], [[d:warrior#1453539655726137404]], [[d:warrior#1453747457673527346]] (Weirdthing, 2025-12-25).
- **[Naxx gearing priority for furyprot, per 30]** Detailed slot-by-slot Naxx-vs-Kara40 pre-planning guidance from 30 (community tanksim maintainer): overall value order roughly Kiss of the Spider > THC/MotC > Mentor/T3 DPS ring/legs or Carnage > Vengeance/Stormrage/Sadists > everything else; specifics: replace T2 helm in Kara but T3 DPS helm stays good for furyprot; don't replace neck typically (T3.5 neck rare to get); shoulders/chest/legs/boots have "keep or replace" nuance depending on set-bonus plans (3.5 5pc); Vengeance bracers are a strong minor upgrade; MotC (Mark of the Champion?) is "really really strong" vs Undead/Demons; THC is BiS if you don't have Thunderfury — [[d:warrior#1454279083491066008]], [[d:warrior#1454279380602978459]] (30, 2025-12-27).
- **[weapon choices — 2H]** DFH is regarded as easier to obtain and cheaper than LHH (Lionheart Helm implied context or a specific 2h weapon) while both fill a similar slot value; if you already have one, chasing the other has diminishing value — [[d:warrior#1452810858987126835]], [[d:warrior#1452813256908345344]] (Lipibidy/evella, 2025-12-23).
- **[Viscidus frost weapons]** For the Viscidus encounter, double Coldrage-enchanted daggers are still considered a strong (if not exactly "BiS," possibly outdated) option; alternatives include Bonechopper MH with Icy Weapon enchant + Frostbound Slasher OH (both with Frost Oil), and an offhand dagger dropping from Korrak with an innate chill proc — [[d:warrior#1451615676807512114]] through [[d:warrior#1451616893160128702]] (Wilhelm/Aubreykun, 2025-12-19). Important correction: the "Icy" weapon **enchant** does NOT count as frost damage for Viscidus purposes (must be actual Frost damage, e.g., Frost Oil) — [[d:warrior#1451617406672961547]], [[d:warrior#1451617683626922075]] (Dodging, 2025-12-20). Double Crusader enchant is also suggested to help kill the slimes phase — [[d:warrior#1451627131011596348]] (evella, 2025-12-20).
- **[Naxx trinkets]** For 2H Fury in Naxx, commonly cited trinkets: Kiss (of the Spider), Crest, Champion, Swarmguard, Heart of Dreams, with Earthstrike sometimes fit in; Badge is called "useless for BRE" users; Mark is noted as good for everything except Spider Wing — [[d:warrior#1452021914209095792]], [[d:warrior#1452022278643777536]] (Dodging/Piemelbrie, 2025-12-20). Elemental Sharpening Stone (2% crit-equivalent AP boost) vs Concussion/Consecrated Sharpening Stone tradeoff: ~100 AP is valued over 2% crit once crit is already high enough, and Consecrated Sharpening Stone actually gives 108 AP vs Elemental's 100 AP due to a game-engine quirk ("Blizzard jank") — [[d:warrior#1452022976978485258]], [[d:warrior#1452023090765627586]], [[d:warrior#1452024739521499259]], [[d:warrior#1452026248120238222]] (Piemelbrie/30, 2025-12-20).
- **[Ironfoe]** For a warrior with Ironfoe (IF), sim testing (Dramafever) found IF-MH + Modrag-OH slightly ahead of the reverse in raw average DPS but with much higher variance (proc-driven); the sim's advantage shrinks close to negligible once Windfury is disabled, suggesting most of the average-DPS edge for IF-MH comes from WF-proc synergy rather than a clean baseline advantage — [[d:warrior#1453100486210355441]] through [[d:warrior#1453236154282279064]] (Dramafever/Aubreykun, 2025-12-23/24).
- **[furyprot OH with TF]** For a furyprot warrior using Thunderfury MH, the offhand choice is contested: Riftcraver/Thunderfall paired vs THC (Tempest's Heart's Crest-type item)/Crystal Sword; sim (Khoni's) reportedly shows TF/Riftcarver ~20 TPS ahead of TF/THC, though live testing by others found their furyprot and imaginary-TF configs "tie in every test" — [[d:warrior#1451152811902369886]], [[d:warrior#1455268351956877446]], [[d:warrior#1454625307964477450]] (30/Squid/Black Hat, 2025-12-18/29; contested, results vary by sim/settings).
- **[non-tf furyprot OH]** Without Thunderfury, THC is considered BiS OH for furyprot "if you aren't getting Thunderfury soon" — [[d:warrior#1454275995572899910]], [[d:warrior#1454276455780323369]] (30, 2025-12-27).
- **[arcane resistance for Kara40]** ~170 unbuffed arcane resistance is stated as sufficient for Kara40 content; crafted AR set + cloak covers requirements, with a magic-resist potion beforehand as a supplement — [[d:warrior#1452920367231275142]], [[d:warrior#1452920424856817788]], [[d:warrior#1455356588364796056]] (Squid/30, 2025-12-23/30).
- **[gold/set planning]** Community heuristic: plan your Kara BiS list first, then check which Naxx items overlap/get replaced by Kara items to prioritize which Naxx items are worth the most (won't get replaced) — [[d:warrior#1454276740146008157]] (Nost, 2025-12-27).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[weapon oils/stones]** Consecrated Sharpening Stone gives 108 AP (vs Elemental Sharpening Stone's 100 AP) due to an engine quirk, making it the better choice by raw AP once you don't need the crit; a common pattern is 1 Elemental Sharpening Stone for adds-heavy segments (e.g. Spider Wing) then switch to Consecrated for the rest of a raid — [[d:warrior#1452024739521499259]], [[d:warrior#1452025486611058970]], [[d:warrior#1452026248120238222]] (30/Piemelbrie, 2025-12-20).
- **[shoulder enchants dps]** Best shoulder enchant options for DPS warriors: the ZG AP enchant, the 2% haste enchant, or the Naxx-sourced shoulder enchant — the Naxx one is called the best overall — [[d:warrior#1454575185054404730]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-28).
- **[boot enchants dps]** Boot enchant choice for DPS warriors debated between Agility and other options; if the raid doesn't yet have Emerald Blessing (from Emerald Sanctum), a movement-speed boot enchant is suggested as valuable since it doesn't stack with Emerald Blessing/BT movement speed once you do have it — [[d:warrior#1450624948346552501]], [[d:warrior#1450625732274553002]], [[d:warrior#1450632082786095235]] (Wilhelm/30, 2025-12-17; confirmed non-stacking by VeganCourage's testing — [[d:warrior#1450784642172190752]]).
- **[weapon enchant — crusader vs fiery blaze]** Ongoing question (unanswered in this chunk) about whether enchanting Dream's Herald with Fiery Blaze makes sense for a warrior tank on trash, then swapping to something else for bosses, vs. plain Crusader — [[d:warrior#1455792369931780106]] (hehe haha, 2025-12-31; question unanswered, single source).
- **[gloves enchant]** Threat enchant on gloves (vs 15 Agility) is confirmed via sim to be notably better for threat once boosted by Turtle changes — historically in vanilla it "wasn't needed," implying Turtle buffed the threat-glove enchant's value — [[d:warrior#1453645363838980219]], [[d:warrior#1453649819410370714]], [[d:warrior#1453563555365261435]] (ScoobertDoobert/mark vasko/Nost, 2025-12-25).
- **[glyph of deflection]** Glyph of Deflection (an active proc/trinket-like item for Deep Prot/Dtactics threat) closes much of the threat gap between Dtactics and Furyprot; called "a small Death Wish in terms of threat" and considered nearly mandatory for a competitive Dtactics build — [[d:warrior#1453697669380702241]], [[d:warrior#1453702393660182548]] (Weirdthing, 2025-12-25).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[shield slam threat formula]** Shield Slam threat = damage × ~1.5 (ability multiplier) × ~1.51 (stance/Defiance multiplier, includes Defiance) ≈ 2.25× total multiplier on damage, replacing the old vanilla flat "damage + 270" model; verified via community math: 228 dmg × 1.5 = 342, × 1.51 = ~516 threat — [[d:warrior#1451340294107828425]], [[d:warrior#1451341885632155750]], [[d:warrior#1451344867216199681]] (Sylvaira/Dodging/Lipibidy, 2025-12-19).
- **[revenge threat]** Revenge's flat threat bonus (with talent + T3 2pc) is about 300 flat, but after Defensive Stance's threat multiplier the effective total becomes ~600-700 threat per use; crits on Revenge barely matter since the threat gain isn't much higher — [[d:warrior#1451347363200241825]], [[d:warrior#1451347724833259622]], [[d:warrior#1451347802058920047]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-19).
- **[buff/debuff application threat]** Applying a buff/debuff (e.g., Battle Shout, Demoralizing Shout) generates a fixed ~25 threat per affected target, divided among the number of targets hit — historically in classic there was a bug where this wasn't divided by target count, letting Demo Shout spam generate huge threat on all targets when combined with Improved Blizzard mage pulls for Scarlet Monastery boosting; not stated whether this bug still exists on Turtle — [[d:warrior#1453422367866093569]], [[d:warrior#1453422933526708486]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-24).
- **[log reading / dps vs damage]** Detailed discussion (Lipibidy/30/Black Hat) on why raw "damage done" is a more reliable cross-log comparison metric than DPS: DPS can be "cheesed" by faster kill times inflating the rate, whereas damage done directly reflects boss health removed; DPS remains necessary to compare across different guilds/kill-time contexts (e.g., Maexxna: killing it too fast on Naxx spider wing lowers dps due to less time for spiderling padding) — [[d:warrior#1453915518426677380]] through [[d:warrior#1453960170483744969]] (Lipibidy/30, 2025-12-26).
- **[shield block / improved shield slam interaction]** Shield Block's buff and the buff granted by Improved Shield Slam overlap/consume each other — you can only have one active at a time, and using either one after the other requires "minigaming" the timing to keep uninterrupted crit/crush immunity uptime; some players macro Shield Slam to consume it automatically, at the cost of sometimes overwriting a fresh Shield Block — [[d:warrior#1454146387263488083]], [[d:warrior#1454146680789274635]], [[d:warrior#1454148053496762388]], [[d:warrior#1454148156336902266]] (Black Hat/Seli, 2025-12-26).
- **[deep prot vs bear/shaman threat]** Bear Druid's Maul deals damage × a large threat multiplier off the global cooldown (not a flat bonus like Heroic Strike's damage+bonus model), giving them substantially higher and steadier threat output than warrior tank abilities; Enhancement Shaman's Earth Shock/Lightning Shield-based kit similarly outpaces warrior tank threat, especially in mixed/mitigation gear — [[d:warrior#1454672397000114269]] through [[d:warrior#1454672929378799658]], [[d:warrior#1454889386045866045]] (30/Jakeends, 2025-12-28).
- **[thunderfury proc rage]** Community testing (30, "Don't ping me") on whether Thunderfury's proc generates rage found inconsistent/near-zero rage generation from the proc under certain angle-based no-auto-attack test conditions, versus the expected ~3 rage a 300-damage hit "should" generate via the posted rage formula; unresolved whether this is a Turtle-specific quirk vs intended behavior — [[d:warrior#1452413284560146433]] through [[d:warrior#1452420835230089247]] (2025-12-22).
- **[macro/utility notes]** Sunder Armor and Rend do NOT proc chance-on-hit weapon effects (e.g., Fiery Weapon, Searing Blade) on initial application per community answer — [[d:warrior#1452182638210318380]], [[d:warrior#1452185406614540288]] (styr/Deleted User, 2025-12-21/28; single reply, "thunderclap doesn't crit" given as adjacent fact but Sunder/Rend proc question not explicitly confirmed either way in the visible reply). Pummel and Shield Bash share a cooldown — using Pummel puts Shield Bash on a 10s CD too, so a "swap to shield and Shield Bash when Pummel is on CD" macro strategy does not work as intended — [[d:warrior#1454776217231167621]], [[d:warrior#1454776559570518109]] (jsl, 2025-12-27). Interrupting/countering Medivh (Kara40 boss) via Pummel or Counterspell adds to his enrage stacks and can make him start blowing up tanks faster, so warriors should be selective about when they Pummel him — [[d:warrior#1453091062682751037]] through [[d:warrior#1453091929704108093]] (Squid/Dodging, 2025-12-23).

## Tanking specifics
- **[deep prot vs fury prot TPS data]** Multiple independently-run community threat-sim comparisons in this chunk (using the community "tanksim," settings vary per player/gear):
  - casey: Furyprot ~1300 TPS vs Dtac ~1200 TPS at his gear — [[d:warrior#1453028020045742150]].
  - Black Hat: Deftac 1500-1600 vs Furyprot 1900 TPS (same/less-BiS fury set) — [[d:warrior#1453015389448572928]], [[d:warrior#1453562850860597288]].
  - Weirdthing: Furyprot ~200 TPS above Dtactics, "not as squishy as I would've thought" — [[d:warrior#1453539655726137404]].
  - Dtac impale vs DW "chinaprot": DW furyprot simmed ~200 more TPS than Dtac impale — [[d:warrior#1453544229996597309]].
  - Lÿenn's detailed sim screenshot: FuryProt-Shield TPS 1838/DPS 1035/Threshold-failed 43.3%; FuryProt-DW TPS 2434/DPS 1384/Threshold-failed 16.0%; Dtac TPS 1808/DPS 867/Threshold-failed 24.7% — confirms ranking FuryProt DW > Dtactics > FuryProt Shield for threshold consistency — [[d:warrior#1453694211588096000]] (image viewed, 2025-12-25).
  - Velanir states the sim's key metric is "threshold failed" (chance to not meet a set threat goal), not raw average TPS, and that ranking is FuryProt DW > Dtactics > FuryProt Shield — [[d:warrior#1453701571677524089]], [[d:warrior#1453701631026794668]].
- **[dtactics threat-chase weakness]** Multiple tanks agree Dtactics performs much worse than FuryProt at "chasing" a bad pull/threat-drop recovery: Furyprot has Flurry/Death Wish/self-Enrage/DW/Heroic-spam to burst back to the top, while Dtactics (battle stance, shield) has comparatively few burst tools and can get "cooked" if an opening Concussion Blow + Shield Slam both parry/dodge — [[d:warrior#1453065132341989568]], [[d:warrior#1453067850771337357]], [[d:warrior#1453068194406207488]], [[d:warrior#1453069364562759942]] (Dodging/Velanir/axelfrog, 2025-12-23).
- **[dtactics rage problem]** Dtactics' core weakness identified by 30: mitigation itself hurts your rage generation (dodge/parry/block/miss all generate less/no rage vs a full hit), creating a negative feedback loop where being "tankier" directly reduces threat output — [[d:warrior#1454683570093428828]] (30, 2025-12-28; see also [[d:warrior#1454897113556193455]] from axelfrog).
- **[fury prot vs dtac gearing philosophy]** Long-running community position (Lipibidy) that "Dtactics is a poor substitute for Furyprot and has no real purpose" as currently designed, since Furyprot always outperforms it in threat sims with equivalent threat-optimized gear, and Dtactics gets closer to Deep Prot's threat as you gear it more defensively — [[d:warrior#1452922902344110184]], [[d:warrior#1452934557819998311]], [[d:warrior#1452935232113217678]], [[d:warrior#1454683483971518587]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-23/28; disputed — see Contested section).
- **[T1 3pc set bonus]** T1 tank 3pc bonus ("Whenever you miss with an ability, you gain 15 rage") is judged low-value/"trash" for a hit-capped tank since the entire point of stacking hit/weapon skill is to not miss — [[d:warrior#1455657651374391338]], [[d:warrior#1455657861131796717]] (MooHill/Thirtyseven, 2025-12-31).
- **[uncrushable]** Uncrushable (102.4% avoidance) is achieved for warriors mainly via Shield Block + Improved Shield Slam (~75% combined per Lipibidy), making hard Defense-stacking largely unnecessary — [[d:warrior#1452840513941012594]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-23).
- **[warrior vs bear/paladin/shaman tank comparison]** Kyrsie: warrior has a "greater defensive and single-target toolkit than Paladin," making warrior tank still likely the best choice at the top level of progression despite the meta being dominated by Bear Druid threat; Bear is explicitly described as "not DOA," with a mitigation-geared Bear doing the same threat as a full-threat-geared warrior tank — [[d:warrior#1454662963805028383]], [[d:warrior#1454663028074086627]], [[d:warrior#1454663387731595437]] (Kyrsie, 2025-12-28). Enhancement Shaman tanks are said to be able to wear ~75% avoidance mitigation gear and still out-threat a fully-threat-geared warrior in some cases, with shaman armor talents (Rockbiter 10%, totems) giving huge additive mitigation not available to warriors — [[d:warrior#1454887096748740749]], [[d:warrior#1454889640971603989]], [[d:warrior#1454891382719123641]] (Jakeends/Lipibidy, 2025-12-28; Not Normal's math: shaman armor difference of ~9k in Naxx gear translates to roughly 11% relative mitigation edge, combined with a further net ~5% swing favoring Dstance-warrior-vs-Ostance comparisons but still behind shaman's stacked % reductions).
- **[naxx boss mitigation calls]** Saph and Patchwerk (P&W) are cited as the main Naxx bosses where extra mitigation gearing over threat gearing is recommended; otherwise threat gearing is preferred — [[d:warrior#1454921205864726621]] (Lans, 2025-12-28).
- **[weapon speed for furyprot]** For furyprot MH, a genuinely fast weapon is preferred for threat/rage upkeep (not for extra hits but to avoid rage-dumping delays); e.g., Crul is critiqued as "kinda slow for a fprot mainhand" — [[d:warrior#1454245116251209738]] (Dodging, 2025-12-27).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)

## PvP
- **[general viability]** Recurring, strongly-held community view that warriors are weak in PvP relative to hybrids/casters/rogues/healers; warriors are "squishy" now because other classes deal damage that bypasses armor while warriors themselves lack the burst/utility to compensate, unlike classic vanilla where warriors were durable and scaled with heals despite weak 1v1 — [[d:warrior#1452728250563297290]], [[d:warrior#1452729457637851382]], [[d:warrior#1452729567251791972]], [[d:warrior#1454522174281879644]] (Lipibidy/Virasan, 2025-12-22/29). Counter-view (Zhafu, Thirtyseven): warrior PvP is not universally terrible — best matchup is vs Warlock, weakest vs Paladin/Shaman, and skilled players can still top damage meters — [[d:warrior#1455234542141968621]], [[d:warrior#1455234309672796307]] (2025-12-30).
- **[2h fury pvp build]** Yorv's fast-2H-weapon PvP fury build emphasizing procs, sunder-fishing, mobility, and weapon-swapping for Master Strike over Slam focus — talent link: `https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c/1.18.0/warrior?points=FQRAJYBQ-FoAIBYCqBAVAB-` — [[d:warrior#1451339755848597557]] (Yorv, 2025-12-19).
- **[dw fury pvp]** DW Fury is noted as more self-sufficient in a chaotic PUG free-for-all setting since it doesn't depend on teammates the way Arms does ("Arms needs teammates to exist") — [[d:warrior#1453097606527062350]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-23).
- **[stat priority pvp]** Resilience is valued at 3% crit-damage reduction "per point" in this discussion's shorthand, debated against raw HP/stamina value in a 1h+shield PvP context; no clear resolution, framed as situational (healer support dependent) — [[d:warrior#1455313301239107624]] through [[d:warrior#1455313491408715856]] (Guts/Thirtyseven, 2025-12-30).
- **[gear for pvp]** A described high-end warrior PvP set: Marshal (PvP) gear base, T3.5 helm (valued over T3.5's 1% crit stat since T3.5 gives Agility directly), double T3 rings, T3 bracers, K40 ranged crossbow, AQ40 cloak; T3.5 legs/boots/chest are called usable alternatives if you have enough HP — [[d:warrior#1455316700126777560]], [[d:warrior#1455316469599436905]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-12-30).
- **[shieldrender in pvp]** Shieldrender's proc/3-set bonus is called "really really good" for PvP even though overall stats on the set aren't great for PvP; example crits cited: 4.6k and 11k on players when procced — [[d:warrior#1455330544190492794]] through [[d:warrior#1455330799229337703]] (Guts/Kyrsie/Thirtyseven, 2025-12-30).
- **[pummel/shield bash]** Confirmed: Pummel and Shield Bash share the same interrupt cooldown category — pummeling puts Shield Bash on a 10s CD too, so a "swap to shield to Shield Bash when Pummel is down" strategy against casters does not actually work — [[d:warrior#1454776217231167621]], [[d:warrior#1454776559570518109]], [[d:warrior#1454805081764397191]] (jsl/Bitmap, 2025-12-28; Bitmap adds that 2 talent points are needed to equalize Pummel's lockout duration with Shield Bash's).
- **[taunt in pvp/leveling]** Taunt uses the melee hit table (caps at ~8% miss against same-level, plus an unavoidable minimum ~1% miss floor "no matter what even if you stack 16%" avoidance-reduction gear) — spell-based taunts (Paladin/Shaman) instead use spell-hit and effectively can't miss the same way — [[d:warrior#1455331812959060120]], [[d:warrior#1455332409305469086]] (Dodging, 2025-12-30; contested by Ascuz who says melee hit does nothing for taunt — see Contested section).
- **[dueling caveat]** Kyrsie explicitly warns that classic-WoW PvP balance shouldn't be judged from duels alone (context: warrior 1v1 performance complaints) — [[d:warrior#1455060810710777970]] (Kyrsie, 2025-12-29 [sic, message timestamp under 12/29 block]).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[stance-swap macros]** pfUI automatically swaps action bars/pages based on stance without needing a manual macro — e.g., placing Whirlwind on a bar will auto-switch stance context — [[d:warrior#1452428865598390413]], [[d:warrior#1452429403853295696]] (Black Hat, 2025-12-22). 30 recommends learning to swap stances manually via mouse wheel rather than relying on stance-swap macros — [[d:warrior#1452428970225041571]], [[d:warrior#1452429355979505764]] (30, 2025-12-22).
- **[swing timer / rotation addons]** SP_SwingTimer and SP_Overpower require SuperWoW to function correctly/reliably (they act up without it); without SuperWoW you also won't see swing-timer resets from Windfury procs — [[d:warrior#1454989911781408829]], [[d:warrior#1454990221648334859]], [[d:warrior#1454990303420350707]] (Kyrsie/Dodging, 2025-12-27/29). CleveRoidMacros + SP_SwingTimer (+ SP_Overpower if playing Arms) is a recommended baseline macro/addon combo for an Arms Slam build — [[d:warrior#1454505789053206538]] (Kyrsie, 2025-12-27).
- **[revenge proc tracking]** `IsUsableAction()` on an action-bar slot can be used in a Lua condition to track whether Revenge is currently usable/procced, since the "Defensive State" buff itself is reported as a red herring/vestigial buff on vMangos/Turtle that doesn't actually gate Revenge usability the way players assume — [[d:warrior#1452898973269626992]], [[d:warrior#1452899018551066747]], [[d:warrior#1452899431702593670]] (30/Jakeends/MattB, 2025-12-23).
- **[equip-swap macros]** For unequipping a shield to re-equip an offhand weapon, `/equipslot 17` is the correct slot approach (one user reported issues getting it to work reliably, using the CleaveRoids-adjacent macro addon) — [[d:warrior#1453150690204581948]] (Khad, 2025-12-24; troubleshooting incomplete in this chunk).
- **[roidmacros conditional macros]** For pull-safety, a "nozone" condition can be set in roidmacros/SuperCleveRoids to prevent an automated ability (e.g., Pummel) from firing in specific encounter zones where interrupting is harmful (e.g., Medivh, where interrupting raises his enrage) — [[d:warrior#1453090819660714055]], [[d:warrior#1453090956743147632]] (Squid/Dodging, 2025-12-23).
- **[fishing/filler macros]** Because many warrior filler abilities (Hamstring, Pummel, Sunder) do negligible direct damage, an all-in-one Windfury-fishing macro combining them is considered acceptable practice rather than needing perfect ability-priority logic; Lipibidy notes a full "correct" WF-fishing macro accounting for every proc source would be "a full paragraph" — [[d:warrior#1453091618482557142]], [[d:warrior#1453091929704108093]], [[d:warrior#1453092437189722122]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-23).

- **[race choice]** Consensus across many messages: race choice makes negligible practical difference to warrior threat/DPS/leveling performance in general — [[d:warrior#1451250970637107304]], [[d:warrior#1451250999548186665]], [[d:warrior#1451250970637107304]] (Nako/Dodging, 2025-12-18/28). Exceptions: Troll's Berserking racial gives a noticeable (likely snapshotting) 10% attack-speed opener boost, called out as one of the only racials that's actually noticeable — [[d:warrior#1451303448158011513]], [[d:warrior#1451304166063341870]], [[d:warrior#1451304363770249417]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-19). Troll is also cited as the best threat race by simulation for BiS 2h-axe-era fury/tank setups — [[d:warrior#1453011071983157299]] (VeganCourage, 2025-12-21). - **[gold-making]** Multiple gold-making approaches discussed for fresh-60 warriors: farming vampiric/lasher-type mob camps with a "vamp" (life-leech) itemization set, AH flipping (buying underpriced/dumped items and reselling, e.g. herbs and "e'kos" that go for 2-3s regularly), vendoring stacks of items dumped below vendor price, and running easy dungeons/low raids that don't require consumables — [[d:warrior#1452343963246657678]], [[d:warrior#1452408216322441306]], [[d:warrior#1452410078538760262]] (Aubreykun/Black Hat, 2025-12-21/22). - **[transmog / weapon sheathing]** Only weapons explicitly flagged with an "alternate equip region" (e.g., Dream's Herald, Thunderfury, Crystal Sword) can sheathe on the back; this is a per-item data flag unrelated to transmog, so transmogging a weapon's appearance does NOT change its sheathe placement — a frequently-asked but structurally-limited request — [[d:warrior#1454553675304534119]], [[d:warrior#1454554040015917216]], [[d:warrior#1454554709015658628]], [[d:warrior#1454555805859971205]] (30, 2025-12-27).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[slam haste double-dip — being fixed]** Slam's cast-time reduction is affected by a haste-formula bug: the sim/live behavior is "not normalized" (Slam is a cast, so slow weapons benefit more than with an instant like Mortal Strike) and benefits too much from gear haste, producing e.g. an 11%-haste warrior getting a 1.57s cast instead of the "correct" ~1.73s; TWoW dev "30"/Lhian confirm this is recognized as overtuned and slated for a fix alongside other Fury-tree number tuning, though not guaranteed for the immediate 1.18.1 patch — [[d:warrior#1454577603053883555]], [[d:warrior#1454578274381332618]], [[d:warrior#1455785897701277819]], [[d:warrior#1455786025128431778]] (30/Lipibidy, 2025-12-28/31).
- **[shield slam / improved shield slam overlap]** Shield Block's buff and Improved Shield Slam's granted buff cannot coexist — activating one while the other is up overwrites it, requiring careful sequencing to maintain continuous crit/crush immunity — [[d:warrior#1454146387263488083]], [[d:warrior#1454146680789274635]] (Black Hat, 2025-12-26).
- **[defensive state buff is vestigial]** The "Defensive State" buff (visible via SuperWoW, normally hidden) does not actually gate whether Revenge can be used — 30 states it's a "useless buff on vMangos and Turtle" that could be removed without changing Revenge's actual usability logic — [[d:warrior#1452898973269626992]], [[d:warrior#1452899018551066747]] (30, 2025-12-23).
- **[kara40 mace + juju]** The Kara40 polearm/mace's on-hit proc never stacked with Juju Power — a long-standing, confirmed-intentional (not-a-bug) interaction — [[d:warrior#1451953710346408020]] (30, 2025-12-20).
- **[windfury/thunderfury proc rage inconsistency]** Testing suggested Thunderfury's proc may generate little-to-no rage under certain no-auto-attack test conditions, contrary to the ~3 rage a similar 300-damage hit "should" produce via the standard rage formula — unresolved as a bug vs. intended Turtle-specific mechanic in this chunk — [[d:warrior#1452413284560146433]] through [[d:warrior#1452420835230089247]] (30/Don't ping me, 2025-12-22).
- **[cooldown partial-tick display bug]** Turtle dev Lhian notes the client can't display partial-value cooldown reductions accurately (e.g., a hypothetical "your Rend ticks reduce Whirlwind's cooldown by 1 second" effect) — the underlying reduction can apply but the UI won't reflect it correctly, a known client-side limitation affecting any future proc-based CD-reduction talent design — [[d:warrior#1454685442711748678]], [[d:warrior#1454686023945818233]] (Lhian, 2025-12-28).
- **[rain FPS drop]** Reported FPS drops specifically when weather (rain) renders; suggested workaround is a config tweak (unspecified exact setting) or disabling weather effects via unitXP-type client mods — [[d:warrior#1455610776764158083]], [[d:warrior#1455613974480556062]], [[d:warrior#1455615819294707782]] (MeCute/Kyrsie/Weirdthing, 2025-12-30; not fully resolved).
- **[HS queue "fix"]** The old Heroic Strike/Cleave "queue" exploit was removed ("fixed"), and a subsequent change makes the HS queue auto-drop if rage falls below the ability's cost — this can silently break macros/rotations built around the old always-queued behavior — [[d:warrior#1451433091288662087]], [[d:warrior#1455614353712611440]] (Nako/Kyrsie, 2025-12-19/30).
