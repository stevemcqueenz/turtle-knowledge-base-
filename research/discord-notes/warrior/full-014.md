## Tanking specifics
- **[tank]** Kara40 Anomalus mechanic clarified via combat-log analysis: avoidance (dodge/parry) prevents a stack of the "Manabound Strikes" debuff, while resistance alone does not stop the stack from applying on a hit that still lands; a blocked hit still appears to apply a stack, making pure Armor/Resistance tanking less effective than previously assumed and suggesting Bear (Feral) tanks — who stack Dodge — may be the better choice for a 3rd Anomalus tank — [[d:warrior#1494787179401052161]], [[d:warrior#1494787207406555217]] (30, 2026-04-17), combat log evidence [[d:warrior#1494788788633735331]], [[d:warrior#1494788955533742210]] (30), reaction/agreement [[d:warrior#1494787515763261440]], [[d:warrior#1494787828297765055]] (Black Hat).
