## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[general]** Stated current meta (pre-1.18.1): "warriors are one of the best dps, decent maintanks and excellent offtanks" — [[d:warrior#1476318467266314280]] (Velanir), but this view erodes through the chunk as prot complaints mount.
- **[tank]** Three main tank builds recognized: FuryProt (dual-wield until threat is secured, then swap shield — highest threat, best for offtank), DefTac (fine for most groups), Deep Prot (doesn't scale with gear, mainly for fresh 60s) — [[d:warrior#1475986032737652919]] (Black Hat).
- **[tank]** Deep Prot dps in Phase-1 raids tops out around ~400-408 dps vs FuryProt's ~400-600, "poop compared to fury prot or a paladin" — [[d:warrior#1475526169951731925]] [[d:warrior#1476088859015249975]] (ScoobertDoobert).
- **[tank]** Long-running (8-year) complaint that Thunderclap and Revenge don't scale with gear at all, making prot progressively worse relative to Paladin/Druid tanks as gear improves — [[d:warrior#1475822122612822090]] (Bon) [[d:warrior#1479151649694941356]] (Lipibidy); Revenge/Reprisal (no-dtactics) build explicitly "gets worse with more gear because it has no scaling" — [[d:warrior#1475982264012636334]] (Black Hat).
- **[tank]** Warrior is considered the only tank class that still stacks DPS-statted gear rather than dedicated mitigation gear, because threat (not survival) is the bottleneck; contrasted with Bear (dps leather that's also good mitigation via form) and Paladin (locked into spell-prot tier itemization) — [[d:warrior#1476097028667867220]] [[d:warrior#1476099887270723685]] [[d:warrior#1476111795893829752]] (Lipibidy, +1 agreement).

- **[fury]** 2h Fury build centers on making Slam attack-speed-capped via Flurry; described as having "3 huge steroids" (Enrage/Deathwish/Flurry) that nothing in Arms or Prot competes with — [[d:warrior#1478083363293364345]] (Lipibidy).

## Leveling
- **[leveling/prot]** Prot feels very weak before level 30 (Revenge does "nearly 0 damage" at 16); recommendation is to level with a 2H weapon until ~30, since Prot becomes strong once Shield Slam unlocks at that level — [[d:warrior#1476315197168685056]] [[d:warrior#1476318368708694108]] (Velanir).
- **[leveling/prot]** Dungeon-tanking talents while leveling: 5/5 Tactical Mastery into 3/3 Improved Thunderclap; "improved thunderclap spam will carry you to 60" in dungeons — [[d:warrior#1476318760871657502]] [[d:warrior#1476319270169219346]] (Velanir).
- **[leveling/tank]** Low-level solo/tank rotation: Charge (or ranged pull) → Demoralizing Shout → Sunder Armor (stop once target is below 50% HP or 5 stacks reached — not enough time to recoup investment past that) → don't use Heroic Strike (too rage-hungry) → keep Battle Shout up between pulls; don't Rend anything that will die in <15s — [[d:warrior#1476322395361906718]] [[d:warrior#1476322734605860979]] [[d:warrior#1476322978114568294]] [[d:warrior#1476323089968005130]] [[d:warrior#1476323710452367442]] (Virasan).
- **[leveling/tank dungeons]** LOS-pull, Bloodrage, Demo Shout, Sunder tab-target on multiple mobs; Thunderclap only if rage is already excess when mobs arrive (hits only 4 targets, costly); 5/5 Tactical Mastery is "pretty great long term," Rend is "terrible for dungeons" — [[d:warrior#1476325827296887007]] [[d:warrior#1476326205870575676]] (Virasan).
- **[leveling]** 2H Fury is explicitly **not** recommended while leveling — its whole point is Flurry-accelerated Slam, which needs high crit uptime you won't have pre-60; Arms (2H) or DW Fury (for Blood Craze/Blood Drinker self-healing/regen) recommended instead — [[d:warrior#1478753288844349461]] [[d:warrior#1478940826062290945]] [[d:warrior#1478940933570433205]] (Kyrsie).

## Rotation: single target
- **[furyprot opener]** Enter combat in Defensive Stance, Bloodrage, then BT+HS on the first GCD (start with as much rage banked as possible), pop Death Wish just before the pull; sequence continues with Revenge or Sunder (queuing HS); don't waste early GCDs on Thunderclap/Demo Shout — current warrior threat generation can't afford it — [[d:warrior#1475247386174554132]] [[d:warrior#1475249572606382255]] [[d:warrior#1475249627337986211]] [[d:warrior#1475249694681600101]] [[d:warrior#1475256639379734619]] (30, D.C.V.).
- **[fury]** Core loop is BT > Revenge > Sunder repeating, filling with Heroic Strike only when rage is capping — "you don't have to memorize GCD order" — [[d:warrior#1475254482324553889]] [[d:warrior#1475254591523258519]] (D.C.V.).
- **[2h fury/arms]** Single-target priority: Mortal Strike/Whirlwind on cooldown, Overpower when it procs, fill with Slam — only start a new Slam after the current auto-attack lands (don't clip your own swing); Whirlwind is used even on pure single target for one of the top parsing warriors — [[d:warrior#1477507600320041145]] [[d:warrior#1478767553613529244]] (Grettt).
- **[fury dw]** Overpower is worth using in DW Fury specifically when fully raid-buffed with Windfury and rage is a non-issue (i.e., Kara40+ gear level) — it's a free crit GCD with a better hit chance than Master Strike for "fishing" a Windfury proc; conditions: only worry about BT/WW being off cooldown, ignore rage state — [[d:warrior#1477322746936688723]] (Theodore).
- **[fury]** Heroic Strike is never chosen "instead of" a skill — it's queued on next-swing alongside whatever GCD you're using, purely as a rage dump when capping — [[d:warrior#1477507950644957367]] [[d:warrior#1477508131671113769]] (Lipibidy).
- **[fury]** Bloodthirst still out-hits Slam in early-raid (Ambershire MC-tier) gear; MC only has three bosses where Sweeping Strikes gets meaningful use — [[d:warrior#1477052337758408714]] (evella).
- **[prot slam]** Slam's effect on avoidance is directly disputed within the chunk: Lipibidy states Slam does **not** reduce avoidance and has no pushback ("more of a lockout timer... than a castbar"), and builds a whole "Thunderfall Slam" tanking rotation on that premise — [[d:warrior#1478965004169642005]] [[d:warrior#1478965129017168012]] [[d:warrior#1478914373417308301]] [[d:warrior#1478915810360823809]] [[d:warrior#1478917216866009309]] — but days later a different poster states plainly "It is a cast and you can't dodge or parry while slamming" — [[d:warrior#1480513635477622906]] (Valerii), and Jizzus's question that prompted it went otherwise unanswered. **[contested, not resolved in this chunk]**. With a ~2.8-speed weapon you can comfortably fit a 2.5s Slam into a slam>SS>filler loop; even with a standard DefTac build and a slow weapon, working a 2.5s Slam into the rotation is described as "an easy win" for single-target dps/tps if the avoidance-immune premise holds.

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[tank aoe]** Warrior AoE tanking is broadly agreed to be the weakest of any tank kit: Thunderclap is capped at 4 targets, costs 14-20 rage, has a cooldown, and (per community testing) has no AP ratio worth mentioning; base tooltip damage lands around 50, and even with Improved Thunderclap + 5pc T2.5 set bonus it only reaches ~250 tooltip / ~150 actual on unsundered targets, and can be resisted/can't crit because it's coded as a spell rather than a melee attack — [[d:warrior#1476085458072244306]] [[d:warrior#1476086545273454633]] [[d:warrior#1476086839499558922]] [[d:warrior#1476086951684735037]] [[d:warrior#1476087286356643913]] [[d:warrior#1476088366767280199]] (Lipibidy, L0s7).
- **[tank aoe]** Practical dungeon AoE-tank loop: Demo Shout → tab-Sunder each mob → Thunderclap only if rage allows → Battle Shout between pulls; Whirlwind "won't hold threat on anything" reliably and mostly hits already-offtanked adds, so it's situational at best — [[d:warrior#1476325827296887007]] [[d:warrior#1479063827319361617]] (Virasan, Velanir).
- **[tank aoe]** Force Reactive Disk (engineering) + Dream's Herald trinket is a popular budget AoE-threat/self-sustain combo for lower-gear tanks, but explicitly falls off and "would suck" once raid gear reaches Naxx level — [[d:warrior#1476326877579710537]] [[d:warrior#1476327133214413011]] (Virasan; Madcrep).
- **[threat math]** Sunder Armor: 260 base threat at max rank, 390 in Defensive Stance with Defiance. Revenge: flat 355 bonus threat plus its damage, and *both* components are affected by the stance threat multiplier (Defensive Stance + Defiance multiplier = 1.495, pre-buff); there is no separate ability-damage-to-threat multiplier on Revenge, which is why damage modifiers to it are weak. Shield Slam does 1.5x its damage as bonus threat pre-1.18.1. Exact community-derived Shield Slam formula: `damage = 303 + blockvalue + 0.2*AP`, `threat = damage * 1.75 * threatmod`; **pre-1.18.1** the multiplier is `1.5` and the AP ratio is `0.15` instead of `0.20`/`1.75` — i.e. 1.18.1 buffs both Shield Slam's AP-scaling ratio and its threat multiplier simultaneously — [[d:warrior#1475525307053379686]] [[d:warrior#1475526245960650892]] [[d:warrior#1475526355272728730]] [[d:warrior#1475526475221565451]] [[d:warrior#1475526525259354133]] [[d:warrior#1475526570725605556]] [[d:warrior#1475526800473063585]] [[d:warrior#1476086545273454633]] [[d:warrior#1479647366880563201]] [[d:warrior#1479649909555728447]] (Velanir, Lipibidy, "30").

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general]** Weapon skill: each point reduces glancing-blow damage by 2%; auto attacks against a boss have a flat 40% chance to glance, and glancing blows cannot crit. Weapon-skill sources: Fray Island quest chain (~level 60, +5 skill per weapon type, 250g each), a weapon-skill book (+5), and racial bonus (+3 for Human) — human sword total without extra items: 3(racial)+6(quest)+5(book) = 314, with Tempest's Rage adding more toward 315 — [[d:warrior#1477340735866142801]] [[d:warrior#1477341008998957086]] [[d:warrior#1477341125051154513]] [[d:warrior#1477343082084303061]] [[d:warrior#1477343209842544833]] [[d:warrior#1477343296807506104]] [[d:warrior#1477691843981606973]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, Woogz). Edgemasters gloves are "not great, not bad" for weapon skill — a good pair of stat gloves usually beats them — [[d:warrior#1477343800686022690]] [[d:warrior#1477344588338434139]].
- **[general]** Crit cap: threshold is higher than the naive 33.5% because of the offhand-hit talent; crit cap differs for MH vs OH vs "yellow" (special-ability) hits, and OH crit cap is basically unreachable. There is no single magic hit/crit breakpoint to chase — hit remains useful even above the "cap," and crit becomes the better stat once past the hit (dodge) cap. Rule of thumb for dual wield: roughly 3 hit rating ≈ 1 crit rating in value — [[d:warrior#1477369623878828246]] [[d:warrior#1477369690694095091]] [[d:warrior#1477370116042653716]] [[d:warrior#1477371032435167322]] [[d:warrior#1477371091902267473]] [[d:warrior#1477371240514715849]] [[d:warrior#1477371729599795404]] (Squid, 30).
- **[general]** Rage generation formula (Turtle-specific, from a datamine/reverse-engineering post): `R = max(3, floor(D×K + S))`, with `S' = S + (D×K − floor(D×K+S))` if R≥3, or `S' = S + (D×K − 3)` if R=3, where D=damage dealt, K=0.0833 (1/12) for damage taken / unarmed, K=0.0417 (1/24) for weapon damage dealt, S=carried decimal remainder — [[d:warrior#1478313693530951783]] (MoneyCow(ruin my own fun)).
- **[tank]** Defense: ~370-400 is called a good survivability range for an MT — going much higher tanks offensive stats and tanks (pun) your tps too hard. Defense doesn't stop crits by itself; instead, Shield Slam's buff (Improved Shield Slam) and Shield Block grant the *same* buff, so you must use Shield Slam, let a hit consume that buff, then use Shield Block, to sequentially push crushing/critical hits off the table (this replaced the old "2 charges of Shield Block" design, seen as a mitigation nerf) — [[d:warrior#1476092913783017605]] [[d:warrior#1476093163650416716]] [[d:warrior#1476093703821983804]] [[d:warrior#1476093980205777048]] [[d:warrior#1476094089798619176]] [[d:warrior#1476095028836175893]] (L0s7, Lipibidy, "30").
- **[general]** Armor Penetration (ArP) is widely called "grotesquely overvalued" in current item budgets relative to its real value — [[d:warrior#1478059703375499266]] (Lipibidy).
- **[tank]** Sunder Armor should not be applied below 50% target HP (not enough time left to recoup the investment) nor beyond 5 stacks except to refresh before it falls off — [[d:warrior#1476322978114568294]] [[d:warrior#1476323089968005130]] (Virasan).
- **[general]** TWoW's weapon-skill hit/glancing breakpoints are shifted from vanilla's: the hit-cap breakpoint is now 310 (was 308) and the glancing-blow-reduction cap is now 315 (was also 308 in vanilla — vanilla's actual hit-cap and glancing-cap breakpoints were both effectively covered by 315/308, and TWoW's rescale is seen as a dev oversight that increased the glancing-penalty requirement without justification). Kara40 BiS dual-wield weapon skill target is 311. Dual-wield white-hit cap is 27% (26.4% if stuck at only 303 weapon skill); at exactly 315 weapon skill you only need 5% hit from gear to be capped — [[d:warrior#1479636542090641489]] [[d:warrior#1479632094257745971]] [[d:warrior#1479637893571022929]] [[d:warrior#1480481558287880333]] [[d:warrior#1480482079492804608]] [[d:warrior#1480144096411779073]] (Lipibidy, "30", Cannondorf, Squid, Painfool).
- **[general]** Stat-weight guidance from the community "Warrior Raiding on Turtle WoW" guide (`https://rentry.co/e3i565dk`): for head/leg enchants, +8 Str is the default pick; only in late-Naxx/Kara40 gear does +1% haste in those slots become comparable, and even then the difference is called "very minor." Community consensus on why: haste only clearly wins if it nets you a genuinely extra auto-attack before a target dies (situational and gear/fight dependent); otherwise flat Str is the more consistent value, and Str's relative value also increases the more crit you already have. In Naxx-tier gear Str/haste are roughly comparable; in Kara-tier gear haste pulls ahead for single-target specifically, while Arms gets zero haste value on Whirlwind/Sweeping Strikes damage — [[d:warrior#1479841078248079372]] [[d:warrior#1479847565838258391]] [[d:warrior#1479849262740148395]] [[d:warrior#1479849344122359920]] [[d:warrior#1479852699406041250]] (leNerd, worlds #1 ppm expert, Sylvaira).

## Tanking specifics
- **[tank]** Death Wish Tactics (DWT): a DefTac variant leaning on Death Wish's crit multiplier for burst threat, discussed and simmed extensively (with screenshots) in early March as scoring the best threat output among tested DefTac variants; talent shape favors 2/2 Overpower, Impale, 3/3 Defensive Tactics, Death Wish, and (again) zero points in Revenge — [[d:warrior#1478448581265330247]] [[d:warrior#1478450329812209911]] [[d:warrior#1478712046215364619]] (Velanir, Jee, Lans).
- **[tank]** FuryProt is explicitly conditional: "if your raid can't sustain furyProt it shouldn't be used" — as soon as it puts a shield on (dropping Death Wish dual-wield), it becomes "the worst tank" in the group; only makes sense while actively dual-wielding with Death Wish active — [[d:warrior#1478473227272454178]] [[d:warrior#1478473279219044557]] [[d:warrior#1478473535503732746]] (Velanir).
- **[tank]** Fresh/undergeared 60s should tank in Deep Prot rather than FuryProt — the extra stuns/interrupts help manage mistakes, and FuryProt needs hit-cap plus ~15% crit and a fast MH to be worthwhile; FuryProt is "a raid thing" for once you're better geared — [[d:warrior#1476618675212652594]] (Ascuz, corroborated by Ascuz again at [[d:warrior#1479497901460820029]]).
- **[tank]** FuryProt is considered the ideal offtank spec because its dps stays acceptable even when not actively tanking — [[d:warrior#1475982910027862108]] (Virasan).
- **[tank]** Parry-Haste caution on Sapphiron: warrior/melee auto-attacking too fast against the hasted boss can be accidentally triggered and is audible; DPS are told to watch for it — [[d:warrior#1478472064280301783]] [[d:warrior#1478473585289855036]] [[d:warrior#1478473701900030123]] (Kyrsie, Lans, Nost).
- **[tank]** Kruul's enrage phase cannot be Shield Walled through solo; would need 2-3 warriors trading the job — [[d:warrior#1478472676270932079]] [[d:warrior#1478472975467680108]] (Velanir, "30").
- **[tank, era]** Consolidated list (from a longtime warrior tank/PvP voice) of cumulative changes blamed for warrior tanking's weak state, spanning multiple patches: no world buffs allowed; the old Shieldwall-into-dual-wield-swap combo was removed; Defiance was moved to the 4th talent row, denying simultaneous access to Improved Heroic Strike; effectively no AP gain from Naxx-tier to Kara40-tier gear; a general lack of stat scaling; the "shadow nerf" changing 1h Weapon Specialization from all-damage to swing-only damage (see Bugs & quirks); and Revenge being flat threat instead of carrying a large multiplier — [[d:warrior#1480287271830818919]] (Velanir).
- **[tank, era]** Asked whether FuryProt tanking existed in original 2004-2007 vanilla or is a private-server invention: based on old vanilla-era videos, the historical meta was full-mitigation Deep Prot, not FuryProt — implying FuryProt is a private-server/TWoW-era development — [[d:warrior#1480948305058861169]] [[d:warrior#1480948865736769547]] (PBnJames, Velanir).
- **[tank]** FuryProt's lack of Shield Wall access (since it depends on staying dual-wielded rather than sword-and-board) is called out as a real, unaddressed gap for "a solid 4 fights in the game" — [[d:warrior#1480289467846754446]] (Hayden).
- **[tank]** Kara40 Incantagos is specifically named as the one fight where warrior tanking is stronger than other tank classes, because the warrior can generate threat by spamming Sunder while sitting at 100% mitigation — [[d:warrior#1480289819648069712]] [[d:warrior#1480289883460468919]] (Velanir).
- **[tank]** A veteran warrior tank reports historically pulling Patchwerk by opening with Shield Wall while dual-wielded — [[d:warrior#1480289611300208671]] (Velanir).
- **[tank, disputed]** Whether DW or 2H is better for a warrior offtank is disputed: 2H (Sunder-focused) OTs are argued to out-damage DW OTs on trash/AoE because DW OT often lacks the "brain" to use the dual-wield kit well, but single-target boss DPS is agreed to favor DW ("not on trash sure but ST DW outperforms"); a server-record-setting pure-DW warrior (Lashara) reignited the "DW warriors are broken once played correctly" claim — [[d:warrior#1480311419315683508]] [[d:warrior#1480311935647092756]] [[d:warrior#1480312166232887317]] [[d:warrior#1480313864565424218]] [[d:warrior#1480314391063695361]] [[d:warrior#1480314644970078419]] [[d:warrior#1480314874889244965]] (Coxy, mark vasko, Lans) — **[disputed]**.

## PvP
- **[pvp/fury]** Best current warrior PvP build is DW Fury stacking Vampirism talent (lifesteal) with any proc-capable item, because Blood Drinker heals off both melee hits **and** abilities/procs (confirmed by direct testing) — meaning Thunderfury's proc, trinket procs (e.g. a Molten/Sapper-style effect), and ring procs all trigger Blood Drinker healing; nicknamed the "MSA TF" (Mind Shard/Thunderfury) build and called the new PvP BiS — [[d:warrior#1475791924790300674]] [[d:warrior#1475823376387018784]] [[d:warrior#1475798121153040395]] [[d:warrior#1475798173439103006]] (Thirtyseven, Velanir). Observed healing: ~155-160 HPS with TF + a proc ring vs ~130-140 HPS without — [[d:warrior#1475796673711046777]] (Thirtyseven).
- **[pvp/fury]** Thunderfury is called "the only viable mainhand for dual wield PvP" specifically because its proc also triggers Blood Drinker — [[d:warrior#1475769775270527071]] [[d:warrior#1475786297141362729]] (Velanir).
- **[pvp/arms]** Master Strike is considered a poor, niche PvP talent: its per-weapon-type effects are Dagger=silence-like, Axe=snare-like, Mace=disorient (with a side-effect bug of un-queuing Heroic Strike/Cleave); its disorient/silence/disarm durations (2-3s) are seen as too short to matter, and it costs a full 30 rage. It is also mandatorily linked to Master of Arms, a core PvE Arms talent, which the community calls "horrible design" since it forces a PvP-only talent tax on PvE Arms builds — [[d:warrior#1475775102858821693]] [[d:warrior#1475775688140132403]] [[d:warrior#1475775726731919451]] [[d:warrior#1475777519784562749]] [[d:warrior#1475777789897736306]] [[d:warrior#1475775903601528957]] (Lipibidy, Donka; broad agreement) [[d:warrior#1476189529168941236]] [[d:warrior#1478884440603103343]].
- **[pvp]** Polearm's Master Strike variant only increases the wielder's own hitbox, not actual attack range — "anything you can hit from further away can also hit you" — considered poorly designed — [[d:warrior#1475537251877257236]] [[d:warrior#1475537528311382046]] (Lipibidy, Njordd).
