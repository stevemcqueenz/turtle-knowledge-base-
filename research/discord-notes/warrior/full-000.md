## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[general]** Warrior 1.18 patch notes summary (pinned): updated talents, added 2H Fury (haste-stacking variant not included), minor gear updates, updated buffs to include an "Improved" toggle — [[d:warrior#1423745716035260477]] (Ash, 2025-10-03).
- **[general]** Turtle-specific mechanic changes vs classic: Heroic Strike queueing no longer converts offhand swings to yellow hits, but dual wield spec got extra offhand hit to compensate; Enrage is proccable via Bloodrage (counted as a self-crit); Bloodthirst got increased base damage but reduced scaling, plus a movement-speed-on-use effect; weapon skill system overhauled to be more linear/less punishing for "meme races"; 2H got reworked so Slam (not just autos) is the bread-and-butter for both 2H Fury and Arms — Slam pauses the swing timer during its cast but the timer keeps counting, and you can move while casting; shaman totems are now auras instead of weapon enchants, meaning weaponstones can be used on mainhand again — [[d:warrior#1423736226187317358]], [[d:warrior#1423736293342580838]], [[d:warrior#1423736504869589095]], [[d:warrior#1423736589791789209]] (Velanir, 2025-10-03).
- **[general]** Zerker Stance no longer gives +3% crit; it now only gives +10% damage taken (compared to og classic) — [[d:warrior#1424931597110738944]] (somedeadguy, 2025-10-07).
- **[general]** Execute talent reverted back to 1.12.1 behavior; Execute previously had (bugged?) no cooldown, now has a rage-reduction/CD component again per patch — [[d:warrior#1426290102832988312]], [[d:warrior#1426290456009900125]] (Chuyen/30, 2025-10-10).
- **[arms]** Arms' execute-scaling talent was nerfed (what made Arms "top 1 spec"), and in the same patch 2H Fury became the new top warrior spec — [[d:warrior#1426296978291298456]], [[d:warrior#1426297061187260416]] (30, 2025-10-10).
- **[general]** Weaponskill overhaul: ideal range 310–315; hitting only 311 doesn't hurt DPS much; a free 308 skill item is "serviceable" for 2H; a weaponskill quest gives +5 — [[d:warrior#1425545754847477780]], [[d:warrior#1425545850826002454]], [[d:warrior#1425917652651737089]], [[d:warrior#1425545518813286400]] (Velanir/30, 2025-10-08/09).
- **[deep wounds]** Deep Wounds is bugged/underperforming — doing 20%/40%/60% of 60% weapon dmg per point instead of intended value; community upvote thread linked for a fix; confirmed still broken as of 10-09 — [[d:warrior#1424278316902252574]], [[d:warrior#1424278588877963345]], [[d:warrior#1425883132787425330]], [[d:warrior#1425883925716271106]] (Mahel/30/Gulolio, 2025-10-05/09; disputed/unresolved bug).
- **[pvp]** Melee damage was reduced ~20% across the board in PvP at some point (implied recent) — [[d:warrior#1427713998731612201]] (evella, 2025-10-14; single source, unconfirmed date).
- **[general]** T1 DPS 3-set bonus ("gain 10 rage on ability miss") widely mocked as near-useless since players stay hit-capped for abilities and it excludes dodge/parry and white swings — confirmed by testing (Black Hat video) that it works ONLY on ability miss, not dodge/parry — [[d:warrior#1423505226844868668]], [[d:warrior#1424038738689789982]], [[d:warrior#1424038738689789982]] (somedeadguy/Black Hat/Dodging, 2025-10-03/04, +reactions).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[dps]** 2H Fury is currently considered the strongest raiding DPS spec, followed by DW Fury and then Arms (viable but behind at BiS gear); Arms is stronger in low/blue gear and until roughly AQ40-tier gear, then 2H Fury pulls ahead — [[d:warrior#1423644490052141178]], [[d:warrior#1425166963893604475]], [[d:warrior#1427604427451269132]], [[d:warrior#1427604594040504370]] (El Jefe/Minces/Julian, 2025-10-03/07/14; some disagreement, see below).
- **[dps]** Phase-1 (Ambershire fresh server) meta gravitated heavily to DW Fury; logs show DW fury warriors breaking 1500 dps on short fights (Shaz-kill, reck window); disputed whether this generalizes since it's a very short buffed fight — [[d:warrior#1425175765175107624]], [[d:warrior#1425176172492488945]], [[d:warrior#1425176595727126618]] (Dodging, 2025-10-07; disputed via "must be cap"/short-fight caveat by Julian/Dodging).
- **[dps]** 2H Fury needs ~30%+ unbuffed crit in zerker stance for good flurry uptime to outperform Arms; below that, Arms is recommended — [[d:warrior#1424838785396637838]], [[d:warrior#1424840445992767560]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-07).
- **[dps]** 2H Fury struggles with rage generation on trash/AoE because cleave/WW give 0 rage without windfury procs; "if 2H fury could cleave every swing like DW fury it would mog every spec in the game on trash" — [[d:warrior#1425533563415171233]], [[d:warrior#1425534076655374410]], [[d:warrior#1425534172436627528]] (30, 2025-10-08).
- **[tanking]** Warrior tank is considered weak for dungeon/AoE tanking relative to other classes on Turtle ("Warrior tank is just hot trash on dungeons... twow gave all other tanks so much more"); Improved Thunderclap AoE threat falls off drastically after ~level 50 — [[d:warrior#1425581964055937157]], [[d:warrior#1425582170763956355]] (Lans, 2025-10-09, +2 reactions); countered by others noting warriors can tank any dungeon at level with correct play (see Leveling).
- **[raid tank]** For raid tanking, Deep Prot (deftac) is described as competitive on threat, more susceptible to bad openers than FuryProt but not a bad spec; FuryProt is for "going fast with good gear," needs crit for flurry to shine, and depends on a decent chunk of AP for Bloodthirst threat — see Tanking specifics section for the fuller FuryProt-vs-Deftac debate — [[d:warrior#1426794473140125809]], [[d:warrior#1423744484058992722]], [[d:warrior#1423744644616814814]], [[d:warrior#1423744721976557598]] (Sylvaira/Velanir, 2025-10-03/12).
- **[pvp]** Community PvP tier list (single poster, +reactions on adjacent messages): Z-tier (best): Shaman, Rogue, Mage, Shadow Priest, Paladin; S-tier: Balance Druid, Smite Priest, Warlock, Melee Hunter; A-tier: Ret Paladin, Ranged Hunter; F-tier: Warrior — [[d:warrior#1425956900968595606]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-10; single source but detailed, echoed by others calling warrior pvp "horrible," "you either get oneshot or kited," and class balance "fucked" — [[d:warrior#1427709931292983335]], [[d:warrior#1427710267097219183]]).
- **[pve dps ranking]** Warriors considered near/at top PvE DPS along with Rogues; "we go toe to toe with [mages/rogues]... ## Leveling

- **[leveling]** Improved Thunderclap (Arms tree) is recommended for leveling: usable in Defensive Stance on Turtle, gives very solid AoE threat for tanking dungeons while leveling — spam it against 3+ mobs to hold aggro — [[d:warrior#1423418951949484082]], [[d:warrior#1423419033231032330]], [[d:warrior#1423421499133526016]], [[d:warrior#1423645418956263425]] (worlds #1 ppm expert/Vlad, 2025-10-03).
- **[fury leveling]** DW Fury with Blood Drinker (talent) is praised repeatedly for leveling sustain — "basically 0 downtime" once online (~level 40); one user farmed 30 raptors in STV with only one stop to heal; Blood Craze (talent) is also a strong passive heal that scales with health — [[d:warrior#1424919178325917716]], [[d:warrior#1425078654144348242]], [[d:warrior#1424560877633405009]] (Vazdeverence/⚔, 2025-10-06/07).
- **[leveling]** Level 10-20 talent choices barely matter for the leveling experience; taking Cruelty first then swapping to Arms costs only "sweeping strikes and MOA 5 levels later," not worth 1g respec — [[d:warrior#1424744769082363957]], [[d:warrior#1424744119808426016]] (Dodging, 2025-10-06).
- **[leveling]** Warrior is a "role-flex" class by design — can level and dungeon-tank without full respec into Prot; many players tank as Arms without ever going Prot until 60. - **[leveling]** Weapon choice while leveling: slow mainhand + fast offhand for DW (slow MH benefits Cleave/WW weapon-damage abilities; fast OH for rage-gen consistency); 2H prefers slow weapon too — [[d:warrior#1423654607942451432]], [[d:warrior#1423654685683875951]], [[d:warrior#1423654722870575155]], [[d:warrior#1427127878461427783]] (Velanir/Dodging, 2025-10-03/13).

## Rotation: single target
- **[2h fury]** Core single-target loop: after an auto-attack (or mid-swing), cast Slam to clip the swing timer for extra damage/rage; "Swing -> Slam -> Execute -> Swing" in execute phase if it fits; if no instant is ready, "swing -> slam, slam -> swing"; Slam itself generates no rage but the follow-up auto-attack (empowered by the slam cast) generates a lot — [[d:warrior#1425540311706374174]], [[d:warrior#1425540968978972862]], [[d:warrior#1425277968317550622]], [[d:warrior#1425278056737669241]] (Velanir/Thirtyseven/Dodging, 2025-10-08).
- **[2h fury]** Priority order: WW > Cleave > BT > Slam (fill with slam when nothing else available); with Flurry up, Slam cast can be as fast as 1.5-1.6s — [[d:warrior#1425534341282533557]], [[d:warrior#1425540490606149652]] (Thirtyseven/Chuyen, 2025-10-08).
- **[2h fury]** Slam timing macro logic: cast Slam only if `nextSlam > nextSwing` doesn't hold, i.e., skip BT/other GCD abilities if using them would push the slam later than the next swing; example macro: `/run if not PlayerFrame.inCombat then AttackTarget() elseif st_timer>UnitAttackSpeed("player")*0.9 then CastSpellByName("Slam") end` — adjust the 0.9 multiplier to tune — [[d:warrior#1423963170938159205]], [[d:warrior#1424809241008541767]], [[d:warrior#1424809585495113879]] (Agamemnoth/mirr/30, 2025-10-04/06).
- **[dw fury]** DW fury has no dedicated "damage spell" — Sunder Armor stacks up potential damage (less armor = more damage/rage per hit); Bloodthirst, Whirlwind, and Heroic Strike (hits with both weapons) are the frontloaded damage/filler abilities; Slam is not used with a 1H mainhand — [[d:warrior#1425274987727683685]], [[d:warrior#1425275061501296680]], [[d:warrior#1425276264842920091]], [[d:warrior#1425277654374023189]] (Wyrda/Setto, 2025-10-08).
- **[dw fury]** Windfury PVE griefer/filler macro when overflowing rage: `/cast Master Strike /cast Pummel /cast Hamstring /cast Sunder Armor` — used to fish for Windfury/Flurry procs when core abilities are on CD — [[d:warrior#1427383820298096640]] (30, 2025-10-13).
- **[arms]** Sunder priority: 1st-GCD sunder on pull is good; then refresh roughly every 15s or when under 5 stacks on a new target; don't over-invest into Sunder at the cost of Slam/WW damage — [[d:warrior#1424833601484230778]], [[d:warrior#1424834348472995890]], [[d:warrior#1424834988591026237]] (Virasan/Thirtyseven, 2025-10-06).
- **[general]** "Clipping an auto" = casting Slam so its cast completes right as (or during) the auto-attack timer ends, syncing extra damage into that swing — [[d:warrior#1425248139933122610]], [[d:warrior#1425248228089004133]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-08).
- **[general]** Nampower spell-queue setting recommended around 1700ms in the "spell queue window" to allow instant follow-up casts (e.g., BT/WW right after a Slam cast); setting too high (e.g., leaving default ~500ms, tuned for casters) can cause misfires for melee; also allows 1-button stance-swap+ability — [[d:warrior#1427066605485162678]], [[d:warrior#1427062265110204537]], [[d:warrior#1427066470910922882]], [[d:warrior#1427066821172789411]] (Thirtyseven/30/Velanir, 2025-10-13).
- **[execute]** Execute remains worth using in 2H Fury if the Slam can still fit; consensus: use Execute over Bloodthirst below 20% target HP whenever gear/AP is not extremely high; pinned guide has a fuller explanation — [[d:warrior#1427574282497822781]], [[d:warrior#1427576349215821834]] (Zove/Friekshow, 2025-10-14).
- **[hs macro]** Standard Heroic Strike macro pattern uses a "no-queue" cast to avoid double-queueing HS behind other abilities: `/run CastSpellByNameNoQueue("Heroic Strike")` combined with `/startattack` — [[d:warrior#1427073028663021630]] (mark vasko, 2025-10-13).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[leveling tank]** Low-level (20-30) HC tanking rotation: Defensive Stance + shield, Battle/Demo Shout, tab-Sunder targets, Revenge — confirmed pattern by community — [[d:warrior#1424176637217603685]] (Anjuna, 2025-10-05; question, general agreement implied elsewhere).
- **[arms/leveling tank]** AoE tanking approach: charge into pack, Thunderclap, then tab-sunder; mark targets so everyone focuses the same enemy; watch nameplate color (green = you have threat) — [[d:warrior#1423939823659520061]], [[d:warrior#1423940060612526202]], [[d:warrior#1423937718278295623]] (Vlad, 2025-10-04).
- **[2h fury]** 2H Fury AoE priority: Whirlwind first, then Cleave, then BT/Slam filler — 2H Fury has good AoE specifically because of how the WW/Cleave damage formulas work (plus Slam for single-target burst) — [[d:warrior#1426297528571265036]], [[d:warrior#1425534264157671424]] (30, 2025-10-08/10).
- **[tank]** MC "double golems" (Golemagg-adjacent trash?) strat: cannot stack-and-cleave the two golems together — keep them separated, facing away from ranged/healers, because attacking the "debuffed" golem causes it to nuke the raid; treat similar to Twin Emperors (run back and forth) — [[d:warrior#1424774691759067147]], [[d:warrior#1424775608055107654]], [[d:warrior#1424775776842289356]], [[d:warrior#1424776946944053298]] (evella/Dodging/Breaddy, 2025-10-06).
- **[general]** Sword Specialization extra-attack proc is not reliable in raid for Arms since it "finishes your current swing timer" (uniform distribution across the swing, effectively less than "5% additional swings") — recommend Mace/Axe for Arms unless niche reasons — [[d:warrior#1424855707395559556]], [[d:warrior#1424856271676506207]], [[d:warrior#1424857675572842637]] (Kyrsie/Virasan, 2025-10-07).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general]** Hit table (vanilla-style, confirmed for Turtle) works as a single roll filling a "dartboard": dodge/block/parry overwrite "hits" (not crits/crushes) in a predefined order, UNLESS avoidance is high enough to have overwritten all hits, at which point it starts overwriting crits/crushes; you cannot dodge/parry/etc. an enemy's crit specifically — reference links: https://vanilla-wow-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Attack_table and https://sbuggay.github.io/attack-table/ — [[d:warrior#1423743154494111875]], [[d:warrior#1423743257145245861]], [[d:warrior#1423743296240357447]], [[d:warrior#1423743394542391346]] (Velanir/30, 2025-10-03).
- **[tank]** Shield Block/Shield Slam give +75% block chance; any avoidance beyond 102.4% total is worth 0 — [[d:warrior#1423742078801678448]] (30, 2025-10-03).
- **[tank]** Defense: 400 defense needed for daze-immunity vs a level 60 mob (20% base + 0.2 per skill diff), 415 defense vs a level 63 mob; "Daze cap" at 420 def referenced elsewhere — [[d:warrior#1426208364383436831]], [[d:warrior#1426208539575320656]] (Virasan/oliverwz, 2025-10-10).
- **[tank]** 1 Defense ≈ 0.14% avoidance (dodge+parry+miss combined, each ~0.04%) plus ~0.04% reduced chance to be crit; conversion estimate: roughly 3 agi = 1 def in terms of mitigation value (1 agi ≈ 0.05% dodge) — contested/refined through discussion, not a hard official number — [[d:warrior#1427230825237188670]], [[d:warrior#1427232468733267971]], [[d:warrior#1427296088016293909]] (Nost, 2025-10-13).
- **[dps]** AP equivalency rule of thumb floated (unconfirmed/disputed as oversimplified): 1 str = 2 AP, 1 agi = 1.5 AP, 1% crit = 30 AP, 1% hit (before cap) = 40 AP — offered as an in-game quick heuristic, not from sim — [[d:warrior#1423964331896213584]] (Faturday, 2025-10-04; single source, no confirmation found).
- **[dps]** Armor penetration value scales with target armor: "100 arp when target has 216 armor is worth a lot more than 100 arp when target has 3000 armor" — [[d:warrior#1423985397674938430]] (30, 2025-10-04).
- **[dps]** Weapon damage is only "partly" normalized: for Whirlwind, a slower weapon does more damage than a faster weapon at equal DPS, because base weapon damage (not just the AP-derived portion) isn't normalized — [[d:warrior#1426720597747957801]], [[d:warrior#1426724752072114308]], [[d:warrior#1426724897383776298]] (30, 2025-10-12).
- **[dps]** Damage formula referenced: weapon base damage + ((AttackPower / 14) * weapon speed); slower weapons have higher base damage, benefiting Heroic Strike/Mortal Strike/Bloodthirst/WW which scale off base weapon damage — [[d:warrior#1427595184513744906]], [[d:warrior#1427594234042646631]] (Ruzzy/Avenged, 2025-10-14).
- **[2h]** For 2H specs, auto-attack is NOT the biggest damage source (unlike other specs/gearing assumptions) — 7% weapon-skill-derived damage increase estimated as only ~0.06% overall dps increase for 2H Fury by log analysis — [[d:warrior#1425918137970327582]], [[d:warrior#1425918862938869885]] (30, 2025-10-09).
- **[general]** Sword Spec extra attacks: not equal to a flat "5% more swings," since it fires off a uniform random point within the current swing timer — [[d:warrior#1424857675572842637]] (Virasan, 2025-10-07).
- **[deftac tank]** Weapon speed for tanks: MH speed matters (needs to be usable to dump rage via HS), OH speed doesn't matter much for tanks; Heroic Strike is a huge portion of later-game tank threat — [[d:warrior#1427083899036766208]], [[d:warrior#1427083962509164656]], [[d:warrior#1427084538202427543]] (mark vasko, 2025-10-13).
- **[tank]** SBV (shield block value) vs crit tradeoff: str-to-SBV conversion is "dogshit," barely worth considering; 5 SBV roughly equals the benefit of 1% crit; agility is favored over strength for defensive-tactics/deftac tanks by several posters because it "double dips" mitigation and threat, though disputed by others (dewbie) who argue str/stam is better for Deftac specifically since HS/SS threat is largely flat and autos are a large chunk of damage — [[d:warrior#1427326657806471269]], [[d:warrior#1427326737057972234]], [[d:warrior#1427326628534419497]], [[d:warrior#1427325898495098970]] (Nost/Black Hat/dewbie, 2025-10-13; disputed).
- **[tank threat]** On Shield Slam, 1% crit ≈ 18 strength worth of threat at ~1000 AP (from a poster's own calc); at 0 AP, 1% crit only provides ~12 strength worth of threat — value is multiplicative (more crit → more value from str, and vice versa) — [[d:warrior#1427327779795636295]], [[d:warrior#1427331247125954773]], [[d:warrior#1427330886818598993]] (Nost, 2025-10-13; self-derived, not officially confirmed).
- **[deftac threat]** Detailed derivation (Shield Slam: base 350 dmg at 0 AP, 0.15 AP ratio, 1.5x threat modifier, Impale gives ×1.2): 1% crit = 6.4 threat at 0 AP, ~9 threat at 1000 AP; 1 Block Value = 1.73 threat (with Toughness talent ×1.15), so ~5.2 BV = 9 threat; 1 strength = ~0.45-0.537 threat (2 AP × 0.15 ratio × 1.5, plus minor BV from str), so ~18.8 str = 9 threat. Conclusion: at ~1000 AP, 1% crit ≈ 18.8 str ≈ 5.2 BV of threat on Shield Slam alone; the more AP you have the more valuable crit becomes, and vice versa — [[d:warrior#1428796660321882112]] (Nost, 2025-10-17; self-derived, "ends up around the same" independently corroborated by Black Hat's own ballpark calc).
- **[deftac threat]** Proposed Deftac threat stat priority: agi/crit > str > AP > SBV > haste — haste placed last because so much Deftac threat comes from Shield Slam/Concussion Blow, neither of which benefit from haste; weapon skill's value is highly situational (depends on boss damage/rage income) — [[d:warrior#1428801642077229220]], [[d:warrior#1428801750411903106]], [[d:warrior#1428801853742907606]], [[d:warrior#1428802478576763111]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-10-17; several posters note "stat priority" is a poor framework vs. per-item equivalency values, since some items break the ordering).
- **[deftac]** SBV vs Agi: "SBV is good but I'd take 20 agi over 20 SBV every single time" — SBV is rarely a real choice since it's mostly only on T3/Glyph items; the Glyph of the Unworthy(?)/"Glyph" item is described as making SBV largely obsolete once obtained, favoring agi thereafter since agi "double dips" mitigation and threat — [[d:warrior#1428780028379791563]], [[d:warrior#1428780158579380386]], [[d:warrior#1428805720002007121]], [[d:warrior#1428808039477284865]] (Virasan/Black Hat/Nost, 2025-10-17).
- **[tank]** Kiss of the Spider is favored by top tanks over Slayer's Crest / Heart of Dreams for opening burst: extra hastened autos on pull compress the early threat lead and give more rolls to avoid a bad dodge/parry streak (e.g. "1.6 swing timer with Thunderfury vs 1.9 on pull is a big deal"); Heart of Dreams (passive) should essentially never be used over the two active trinkets — [[d:warrior#1428808219555266610]], [[d:warrior#1428808306146676958]], [[d:warrior#1428812852713619689]], [[d:warrior#1428813396039565452]], [[d:warrior#1428883973924323399]] (30, 2025-10-17/18).
- **[dps]** Haste enchant value is roughly comparable to 1% crit or ~30 AP in BiS Naxx-tier gear per the pinned guide (questioned but not refuted); in late-Naxx/Kara40 gear, prefer haste enchants on head/legs over flat strength — [[d:warrior#1428903844686860318]], [[d:warrior#1428903936189796382]] (Don't ping me/30, 2025-10-18). Armor Penetration's value swings heavily with boss armor and whether Badge of the Swarmguard is also being used; a lot of ARP comes passively from Kara40 set pieces already — [[d:warrior#1428904250339102790]], [[d:warrior#1428904250431246467]] (Don't ping me/Breaddy, 2025-10-18).

## Tanking specifics
- **[deftac/deep prot]** FuryProt vs. Deep Prot/Deftac is a long-running, unresolved debate: FuryProt gives more damage/dps but is riskier (misses can be punishing, needs good gear and a competent raid) and doesn't clearly out-threat Deftac since Deftac has more built-in threat multipliers; several experienced players (30, Velanir) run FuryProt as MT with some of the fastest kill times/highest DPS on their server and call "FuryProt is a meme" factually wrong; others (Lipibidy) argue FuryProt on Turtle specifically requires much more gear/team competence than in vanilla, so it shouldn't be the default recommendation for a new tank — putting a 3rd+ tank in FuryProt to learn is suggested as a safe way to try it — [[d:warrior#1428076942371848224]], [[d:warrior#1428077138673926175]], [[d:warrior#1428077290033774622]], [[d:warrior#1428078309811552407]], [[d:warrior#1428078417751838761]] (30/Lipibidy/Velanir/worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-10-15).
- **[deep prot]** Deep Prot (Deftac in Battle Stance) is criticized as "a fish for shield slam crits simulator" — its damage/threat is spiky and crit-dependent, unlike FuryProt's more consistent output, but Deep Prot remains the standard choice when not maintank/2nd tank, since a non-actively-tanking FuryProt does "literally nothing" — [[d:warrior#1428076210122002683]], [[d:warrior#1428076257307787304]], [[d:warrior#1428078698149580971]] (Velanir, 2025-10-15).
- **[tank]** Resistance gear is generally unneeded for melee tanks in AQ40 except specific fights: Ragnaros needs fire resist; Baron Geddon, Firemaw, and Grand Widow Mograine/other BWL/MC drake fights are cited as exceptions where a tank benefits from resist; tanks in AQ40 generally prefer threat/stamina gear over Nature Resist since they get consistent heals — DPS taking nature damage benefit more from NR than tanks do — [[d:warrior#1426848565267005520]], [[d:warrior#1426866397140746303]], [[d:warrior#1426866781691318292]], [[d:warrior#1426868434892882033]] (mark vasko/Virasan, 2025-10-12).
- **[tank]** Twin Emperors (AQ40) is called out as the one fight in the game where a specific defense number (400 def, the daze-cap) actually matters — everywhere else stat allocation is more flexible/vibes-based; going from daze-capped to not is reported as feeling noticeably worse even outside Twins — [[d:warrior#1425674099702890588]], [[d:warrior#1427323896046157916]], [[d:warrior#1426189162943352942]], [[d:warrior#1426208364383436831]] (Dodging/Sylvaira/Virasan, 2025-10-09/10/13).
- **[tank]** Maexxna's web-spray effectively stuns, meaning dodge/parry are fully unavailable while affected ("if you're stunned you cannot avoid attacks period"), though Defense still reduces crit chance while stunned — [[d:warrior#1426970726023434467]], [[d:warrior#1426970873306415205]], [[d:warrior#1426971693523533914]] (Ryiana/30, 2025-10-12).
- **[tank]** Taunt is not affected by melee hit cap; taunt has a separate resist chance discussed as roughly 1-in-6/1-in-7 (community estimate, not authoritative), meaning even at hit-cap a tank can whiff a taunt — recommended mitigation is having 2 people ready to taunt and other abilities (Shield Slam/Concussion Blow-style threat) as backup — [[d:warrior#1426796173733134407]], [[d:warrior#1426796501518127124]], [[d:warrior#1426796793345347787]] (Lean/Sylvaira/Dodging, 2025-10-12).
- **[tank]** General threat troubleshooting framework offered to a struggling player: check logs for whether the healer dropped you for 3+ seconds (healer's issue), died to crit/crit/crush under 1.5s (RNG/partial your fault), or failed to use Last Stand/healthstone/Tea when low (your fault, ~5000 effective healing available from those combined) — [[d:warrior#1425927829346058380]], [[d:warrior#1425927874833289350]], [[d:warrior#1425927941262413915]], [[d:warrior#1425928073638969394]], [[d:warrior#1425928912546037800]] (30/Virasan, 2025-10-09).
- **[tank]** Warrior threat generation is broadly agreed to be weaker than Paladin and especially Druid tanks in current raid balance ("the one from the druid [is] insane to a point you could call it a bug"); Deep Prot is described as the more raid-viable warrior tank threat spec vs. FuryProt precisely because of the multiplier stacking — [[d:warrior#1428596080781693060]] (JohnGood, 2025-10-17).
- **[deep prot pull]** Recommended pull sequence: Berserker Rage, Concussion Blow (has armor pierce, use first), Shield Slam, Sunder Armor or Heroic Strike depending on rage, then keep everything on CD; popping a DPS trinket before the first Concussion Blow is also suggested for opening threat — [[d:warrior#1428770837451903039]], [[d:warrior#1428771062824308771]] (JohnGood, 2025-10-17).
- **[general complaint]** Multiple posters (Sena, JohnGood, Lean) describe warrior tanking as feeling like "racing against DPS" for threat compared to Paladin/Druid tanks, citing specific wipes to fast-DPS classes (rogues, survival hunters) pulling threat off an at-hitcap warrior tank — recorded as a recurring community pain point rather than a confirmed numeric imbalance — [[d:warrior#1428769655438315550]], [[d:warrior#1428772329806237818]], [[d:warrior#1425636433229516922]] (Sena/betcover, 2025-10-09/17).

- **[AQ40]** Huhuran enrage-phase dart mechanic hits the closest ~15 targets; using Peasant Caller raid-wide is the standard cheese to reduce execute-phase pressure — [[d:warrior#1427058635963367546]], [[d:warrior#1427058714514559177]] (Velanir, 2025-10-13).

- **[naxx]** Noth: adds have roughly a 0.5s window where nothing can be done on spawn; add-phase kills reportedly don't count toward personal DPS ranking on Noth, and most top parses are "1-phased" (killed before adds matter) — [[d:warrior#1428908691020710006]], [[d:warrior#1428908781294715060]] (30, 2025-10-18).

- **[general]** Sweeping Strikes currently has a bug where it deals no damage if the primary hit is fully absorbed, and only the leftover (post-absorb) damage if partially absorbed — community bug-report links posted for upvoting: turtle-wow.org/bug-report?id=17867 and id=17868 — [[d:warrior#1428320655203504148]] (Donka, 2025-10-16; unresolved).

## PvP
- **[general]** Community PvP tier-list snapshot (single detailed poster, not officially confirmed): Z-tier: Shaman, Rogue, Mage, Shadow Priest, Paladin; S-tier: Balance Druid, Smite Priest, Warlock, Melee Hunter; A-tier: Ret Paladin, Ranged Hunter; F-tier: Warrior — [[d:warrior#1425956900968595606]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-10-10).
- **[general]** Widespread sentiment that Warrior is currently the weakest PvP class on Turtle ("warrior is probably the weakest class in pvp right now"; "you either get oneshot or kited"); countered by at least one poster (Spiff) that this is "a bit exaggerated," calling Arms specifically "not the best, but more than other classes are cracked by comparison" — [[d:warrior#1427787103302975538]], [[d:warrior#1427709931292983335]], [[d:warrior#1428166435384852594]] (Don't ping me/Thirtyseven/Spiff, 2025-10-15/16).
- **[general]** Master Strike (Mace) had a bug allowing PvP warriors to disorient targets for its full duration regardless of whether damage was dealt; this was hotfixed/removed mid-chunk (~10-15), generating strong community backlash including a sarcastic mock "warrior discord shutdown" post; post-fix, Master Strike is widely called "dead"/"useless" for PvP — [[d:warrior#1427842884425744435]] (Black Hat, +9 reactions, 2025-10-15), [[d:warrior#1427814951078334484]], [[d:warrior#1427815366427410522]] (DREAD, 2025-10-15).
- **[general]** Concussion Blow's PvP behavior discussed as contentious: CD raised to 20s and stun duration reduced specifically to keep Prot from being strong in PvP, per community framing (not an official statement) — [[d:warrior#1428135236558000272]] (Velanir, sarcastic tone, 2025-10-16; single-source characterization).
- **[fury]** Fury PvP has better sustain and an extra immobilize-remover; Arms PvP has built-in healing reduction via Mortal Strike — [[d:warrior#1427786798825865226]] (Don't ping me, 2025-10-15).
- **[race]** For PvP, Gnome is called the best race for Warriors (double root break value); Troll considered the worst Horde-side PvP race for Warrior due to nerfed racial synergy on Turtle; Night Elf also cited as a strong PvP race — [[d:warrior#1428445750630678619]], [[d:warrior#1428445817957646500]], [[d:warrior#1428446648530370722]] (Kyrsie, 2025-10-16).
- **[general]** Melee/warrior PvP is reported hampered by desync/latency issues inherent to the emulator, which some argue makes true PvP "outplay potential" limited regardless of balance changes — disputed by others who report no such issues personally — [[d:warrior#1428179204016701441]], [[d:warrior#1428180269537693848]] (ralleballe/Kyrsie, 2025-10-16; contested, no resolution).
- **[prot]** Prot Warrior PvP is described as essentially non-viable without Thunderfury ("its TF or nothing"); Rend was buffed for PvP prot per community commentary but Hamstring retains a cooldown that PvP-focused players want reverted — [[d:warrior#1425874497743093833]], [[d:warrior#1428135047533432832]], [[d:warrior#1428132073897463891]] (Thirtyseven/sailorVEVO/sailorVEVO, 2025-10-09/16).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[general]** RoidMacros (https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/Roid-Macros) is the standard macro addon; CleveRoidMacros is a popular alternative with more features but reported by some (Velanir) as slower/buggier (e.g., a shield macro randomly breaking) — [[d:warrior#1423741386032681182]], [[d:warrior#1423741464332075109]], [[d:warrior#1423741594078679060]], [[d:warrior#1423741674018050160]] (30/Velanir, 2025-10-03).
- **[general]** SuperWoW + a "SuperWoW compatibility addon" are required for several conditional macros (range checks, castsequence); confirm both are installed if `[nomybuff]`/`[nodebuff]`-style macro conditionals aren't working (e.g., Battle Shout refresh-only macros) — [[d:warrior#1424088204390301827]], [[d:warrior#1424088575015915520]], [[d:warrior#1424088976687632554]], [[d:warrior#1424093173315276811]] (veso/Kyrsie, 2025-10-04).
- **[tank]** ADF (armor/debuff-frame addon) shows boss armor values (useful for trinket/ARP management) and debuff tracking; 30 maintains a lighter custom version; positioned via SavedVariables (`WTF/Account/.../SavedVariables/adf.lua`, e.g. `aDF_x = -278`, `aDF_y = 110`) since the in-game move handles were stripped from the lightweight build — [[d:warrior#1424132205357170698]], [[d:warrior#1424132940178264084]], [[d:warrior#1424133858131054604]], [[d:warrior#1424228329497825440]] (30/Breaddy, 2025-10-05).
- **[general]** BetterCharacterStats addon recommended to visualize hit/miss chances directly on the character sheet — [[d:warrior#1424763236498018474]], [[d:warrior#1424763310330609738]] (Dodging, 2025-10-06).
- **[general]** ActionBarProfiles addon swaps action bars automatically per spec, though items placed on bars won't swap into a profile unless they're actively in inventory — [[d:warrior#1426695888893382847]], [[d:warrior#1426696661694021643]] (Dodging, 2025-10-12).
- **[2h fury]** SP_SwingTimer addon (https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/SP_SwingTimer, requires SuperWoW) tracks swing timing for Slam-clipping; note that only Slam itself interacts with/pauses the swing timer — no other ability resets it except a Windfury proc, which fully resets it — [[d:warrior#1427780654577352734]], [[d:warrior#1427783595698688030]], [[d:warrior#1427786497523716118]] (30, 2025-10-15).
- **[general]** SP_Overpower addon recommended for tracking Overpower procs while in Berserker Stance — [[d:warrior#1427360774308892736]] (Kyrsie, 2025-10-13).
- **[general]** Nampower's default spell-queue window (500ms) is tuned for casters, not melee; melee players should raise it substantially (~1700ms cited) to allow instant-cast follow-ups after Slam without losing casts; too high a value can cause self-inflicted misfires ("griefing yourself") — [[d:warrior#1427066605485162678]], [[d:warrior#1427066821172789411]], [[d:warrior#1427062265110204537]] (Thirtyseven/Velanir/30, 2025-10-13).
- **[general]** PFUI has been anecdotally linked to FPS drops (especially when mousing over gear with active set bonuses) for some users, but not universally reproduced; in at least one case the real culprit was a chat-frame configuration, not PFUI itself — troubleshoot other addons before assuming PFUI is at fault — [[d:warrior#1424419278928740423]], [[d:warrior#1424421085977514084]], [[d:warrior#1424426113068699820]], [[d:warrior#1424438934955491369]], [[d:warrior#1424439039439933482]] (Vlad/Dodging/Black Hat, 2025-10-04/06).
- **[general]** Combat-log-to-turtlogs addon logs automatically start when entering a raid instance; the log file must be manually cleared/pruned between raid nights to avoid merging multiple raids' data on upload; it will chop by zone (anything after a `ZONE_INFO` for a raid instance like Molten Core gets uploaded) — [[d:warrior#1428413605900189736]], [[d:warrior#1428413641623076956]], [[d:warrior#1428414689477394563]], [[d:warrior#1428416123568914432]] (Breaddy, 2025-10-16).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[t1 set]** T1 DPS 3-piece ("gain 10 rage on ability miss") confirmed via testing to trigger ONLY on ability miss, not on dodge/parry or white-swing misses, making it far weaker than a naive reading suggests — [[d:warrior#1424038738689789982]] (Black Hat's video, cited by Dodging, 2025-10-04).
- **[deep wounds]** Deep Wounds was bugged, applying a DoT based on 20/40/60% of 60% weapon damage per point instead of the presumably-intended 20/40/60% of 100%; a mid-chunk patch changed its behavior again to a flat "3%" formulation, community reaction mixed on whether this constitutes a real fix — [[d:warrior#1424278713473699842]], [[d:warrior#1427776062514925619]], [[d:warrior#1427776133025501326]], [[d:warrior#1427776611834396755]], [[d:warrior#1427804910778318858]] (30/Mahel, 2025-10-05/15).
- **[whirlwind]** Whirlwind was fixed (~10-15/16 patch) to properly proc on-hit weapon effects (Crusader, Windfury, Thunderfury, etc.) per swing hit rather than not at all; however, Impale still does NOT apply to Whirlwind damage as of 10-16, a known separate and still-unresolved bug — [[d:warrior#1427956738790789203]], [[d:warrior#1427957405928656908]], [[d:warrior#1428271995124056175]], [[d:warrior#1428278684728430622]], [[d:warrior#1428280121239933008]], [[d:warrior#1428281063267897384]] (Painfool/MoneyCow/Ryiana, 2025-10-15/16).
- **[sweeping strikes]** Sweeping Strikes bug: deals no damage to the secondary target if the primary hit is fully absorbed, and only leftover (post-absorb) damage if partially absorbed — bug reports posted for upvote (id=17867, id=17868) — [[d:warrior#1428320655203504148]] (Donka, 2025-10-16; unresolved).
- **[master strike]** Master Strike (Mace variant) had a multi-month bug letting PvP warriors disorient for the full duration regardless of damage dealt; hotfixed (~10-15) with strong community backlash calling the rework/nerf a "complete failure," leaving the ability considered "dead" for both PvE and PvP by several posters — [[d:warrior#1427814951078334484]], [[d:warrior#1427815366427410522]], [[d:warrior#1427842884425744435]] (DREAD/Black Hat, 2025-10-15).
- **[execute]** Execute's damage correctly copies to a Sweeping Strikes secondary target ONLY if that secondary target is also below 20% HP — otherwise it deals base weapon-swing damage to it; this is a real mechanic, not a bug, confirmed twice independently — [[d:warrior#1423663138582167614]], [[d:warrior#1428909002011447358]], [[d:warrior#1428910614914535575]] (Kyrsie/30/Don't ping me, 2025-10-03/18).
- **[execute cd]** Execute briefly had no cooldown at all (bug) before being reverted to intended 1.12.1 behavior with a proper CD/rage-refund mechanic — [[d:warrior#1426286710328786995]], [[d:warrior#1426288898723811542]], [[d:warrior#1426290456009900125]] (Chuyen/30, 2025-10-10).
- **[display]** Displayed crit% can appear to differ depending on the ORDER gear pieces were equipped in (visual bug, confirmed by several posters reproducing it with T3 vs. Ouro Gloves vs. THC combinations); likely tied to weapon-skill-on-non-weapon-items interaction, but root cause unconfirmed — [[d:warrior#1428612673297186842]], [[d:warrior#1428613140836388905]], [[d:warrior#1428614939991347311]], [[d:warrior#1428618894964293709]], [[d:warrior#1428621286296391711]] (Rheinwg/30/Squid, 2025-10-17).
- **[hunter]** Sunder Armor debuff icons intermittently vanish from nameplates/raid frames once several debuffs are stacked — this was traced to individual PFUI/addon display settings, not a core game bug; using ADF instead of PFUI's debuff display was the suggested fix — [[d:warrior#1424130563723362586]], [[d:warrior#1424131218491969626]], [[d:warrior#1424132896045924494]] (Vlad/Thirtyseven/30, 2025-10-05).

- **[deftac]** Whether Strength or Agility is the correct default prioritization for Defensive Tactics tanks remains unresolved after extensive back-and-forth (10-13 and 10-17 threads); some (dewbie) argue Str/Stam is better since Deftac's threat leans heavily on auto-attacks and flat-scaling abilities, while most others (Nost, Black Hat, Virasan, worlds #1 ppm expert) favor Agi/Crit for its dual mitigation+threat value — no authoritative numeric resolution was reached in-chunk — [[d:warrior#1427325898495098970]], [[d:warrior#1427326628534419497]], [[d:warrior#1428779654151667923]].
- **[tank]** Whether FuryProt is a legitimate baseline recommendation or a gear/skill-gated "meme" on Turtle specifically (vs. vanilla where it was closer to mandatory) was heavily disputed on 2025-10-15 with no resolution; both sides agree it requires substantially better gear/team execution than Deep Prot to pay off — [[d:warrior#1428076942371848224]], [[d:warrior#1428077138673926175]], [[d:warrior#1428078417751838761]].
- **[glancing blow]** The exact glancing-blow damage penalty vs. a level-63 boss (30% vs 35% vs other) has no confirmed official source in-chunk; community tests (small sample size) roughly matched a spreadsheet based on the underlying vMangos formula (github.com/vmangos/core), but posters explicitly note the sample sizes were too small to fully trust and Turtle's own modifications aren't public — [[d:warrior#1428579229716582483]], [[d:warrior#1428585681386606623]], [[d:warrior#1428586172942389351]], [[d:warrior#1428593683984285747]] (Rheinwg/JohnGood, 2025-10-17; question effectively unanswered).
- **[deep wounds]** Whether the ~10-15 "fix" (flat 3%) actually corrects Deep Wounds to its intended value, or is itself another bug/incomplete fix, was still being tested/questioned as of 2025-10-16 — [[d:warrior#1428293938233475163]] (Mahel, 2025-10-16; unanswered in-chunk).
- **[prot devastate]** Community wishlist (Concussion Blow un-avoidable, a Devastate-style ability, Heroic Throw, Sunder AP-scaling) for Prot has been raised repeatedly across patches with no developer commitment; one dev (Lhian) acknowledged Warrior PvP underperformance and "2H Fury needs refinement" / "Arms needs execute buffs" but gave no timeline — [[d:warrior#1427700942563704994]], [[d:warrior#1427705839996899511]], [[d:warrior#1428141134605189130]] (Artifyce/Lhian, 2025-10-14/16; open).
