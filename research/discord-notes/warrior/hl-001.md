## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[2H]** Slam now only *pauses* the swing timer instead of resetting it from the beginning as in classic/vanilla; if the swing was at 1s remaining when Slam is cast, it finishes that 0.5s rather than restarting — better than vanilla, but you can still be caught mid-swing in PvP — [[d:warrior#1404648714382872667]] (IzNebula, 2025-08-12).
- **[Arms]** Precision Cut's rage refund removed (kept the 20/40/60% dmg-per-extra-rage scaling); proposal (+11 reactions) to swap its tree position with Boundless Anger so Arms isn't pushed to poach Fury's Enrage niche — [[d:warrior#1403876059283197992]] (30, 2025-08-10); follow-up tuning wishlist (nerf Slam to 85%, revert Precision Cut to 1.17.2 + swap w/ Boundless Anger, Improved Slam +10% dmg, restore old HS-queue behavior) — [[d:warrior#1406181139772870779]] (30, +5, 2025-08-16).
- **[Arms]** Shifting damage weight from Execute to Slam/Overpower seen as bad for gear progression — makes the single slow 2H weapon matter even more and other gear upgrades matter less — [[d:warrior#1402911506462412920]] (Piemelbrie/siswet, 2025-08-07).
- **[2H]** Slam/Mortal Strike rank-damage history of buffs: Slam R2(lvl24) 60%→65%, R3(34) 70%→80%, R4(44) 80%→90%, R5(54) 90%→100% weapon dmg; Mortal Strike R1(40) 105%→115%, R2(48) 110%→120%, R3(54) 115%→125%, R4(60) 120%→130% — [[d:warrior#1418043820523782335]] (30, 2025-09-18).
- **[Arms]** With Precision Cut's refund gone, Execute is only worth pressing over Slam if its damage (870 + 15 dmg per rage spent above 15) exceeds a Slam's damage — with a slow weapon two Slams fit in the time Execute would take — [[d:warrior#1405656912984408199]] (Kirchhoff, 2025-08-15).
- **[general]** Rage-generation formula reworked to add a weapon-speed term: old `rage += (dmg/rageconversion)*7.5*ragemod`; new non-crit `rage += (((dmg/rageconversion)*7.5)/1.075) + (mh.speed*3.5)/2.25`, crit `... + (mh.speed*7.5)/2.25` — [[d:warrior#1403891986532008027]] (30, 2025-08-10); worked example (1000 non-crit dmg: old 32.52 rage vs new ~36.17 with a 3.8-speed MH) — [[d:warrior#1421233303880470530]] (30, 2025-09-27).
- **[2H]** Whirlwind favors slower weapons because half its damage derives from weapon base damage; slower weapons also net more Heroic Strike/Cleave swings per rage spent, and Windfury Totem's 1.5s internal CD rewards attacking less often — [[d:warrior#1403906550850130041]] (30, 2025-08-10).
- **[PvP]** Universal BG/Arena buff/debuff planned for Patch 1.18.0 (rolling out across 1.18.0 and 1.18.1): reduces incoming physical dmg%, magical dmg%, healing%, and shields% (values were placeholders at time of posting) — [[d:warrior#1404991746445803551]], [[d:warrior#1404993065323724951]] (Zamera, 2025-08-13).
- **[Tank/dev]** Dev (Lhian) confirms bear tank threat is overtuned and will be addressed, and that Revenge (a defining Deep Prot ability) needs help — [[d:warrior#1407345918990221464]], [[d:warrior#1407347230406611104]] (2025-08-19).
- **[bug]** Deep Wounds doing only ~34% damage at max rank instead of the intended 60% — open bug report (turtle-wow.org/bug-report?id=16021) — [[d:warrior#1411063974329319587]] (Cromius, +4, 2025-08-29; question unanswered in-chunk).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[general/PvE]** Endgame PvE DPS specs: DW Fury, 2H Fury, and Deep Arms all viable; PvP: Arms w/ Mortal Strike, Fury w/ Blood Drinker, or an "abomination rat prot flag build"; Tanks: Furyprot = high skill floor/high gear dependency/very squishy, Deep Prot (reprisal-based) = low barrier of entry/low gear dependency, Defensive Tactics = similar to Furyprot but with a shield, high gear dependency — [[d:warrior#1413098055711850626]] (Dodging, 2025-09-04).
- **[2H/DW]** Spec-flavor summary: 2H Fury (Bloodthirst) = strongest single-target/overall dmg, lowest AoE, high gear dependency; 2H Fury (Sweeping Strikes) = strongest AoE, ~Arms-level single-target, very simple "spam Slam" rotation; Arms = big Mortal Strikes + Sweeping Strikes AoE, no cooldowns, consistent but capped below top Rogues; DW Fury = most flexible (can tank, decent ST/AoE via fast Cleaves, strong cooldowns, no casts) but "isn't best at anything" — [[d:warrior#1417859058349637694]] (Guts, +3, 2025-09-17).
- **[Tank]** Warriors seen as weak AoE tanks: "warrior doesn't really do aoe tanking" — Furyprot leans on high rage from low avoidance + dual-wield to push Cleaves and tab-Sunder (e.g. in Kara10); others use Arms w/ Sweeping Strikes; Engineering called massive for warrior tanking/PvE — [[d:warrior#1404189407627706520]] (Dodging, 2025-08-10).
- **[Tank]** South Seas server report: deep prot threat "not as great as people say," shamans out-threat warrior tanks, Furyprot shines with DW daggers/swords; DPS: Fury dominated early with mages, then top-weapon wielders led once geared, Arms tops trash damage with gear, Fury stays consistent — [[d:warrior#1402330767568277636]] (Jerso, 2025-08-05).
- **[Tank]** Warrior tanks judged inferior to Bear/Paladin in single-target threat: Bear = vastly superior threat, nearly as tanky, solid dps; Paladin = vastly superior AoE/leveling but lacks defensive CDs and struggles vs mana burn; Shaman was worst tank pre-buff; Warrior is "good enough" but struggles severely with AoE threat, loses single-target threat races to Bear, and must gear like a DPS since more mitigation would threat-cap its own damage — [[d:warrior#1414938332055470102]] (Virasan, 2025-09-09).
- **[Tank]** Recurring cross-channel complaint: "you go into pala channel and they think they're losing threat to warriors, you go to druid channel and they're complaining every other tank takes half the dmg they do, you go into warr channel and they think every other tank does 2x better than them" — [[d:warrior#1407063522088714413]] (30, +7, 2025-08-18).

## Leveling
- **[leveling]** Traditional order — Fury until level ~40, then switch to Arms for 40-60 — still generally holds, though post-Slam-changes leveling in any spec is viable; Fury-all-the-way may now be slightly more efficient (unconfirmed "vibes") — [[d:warrior#1406088476583268353]] (Spatz, 2025-08-16).
- **[leveling]** Alternate breakdown: 1-43 Arms felt stronger than Fury; 43+ switch to Fury DW with self-heal talents (Blood Drinker) — solo levelers get more uptime and end up ahead of Arms despite slightly less raw dmg; if a friend offheals, stay Arms; a Prot+heal duo also works well, abusing tents for instant dungeon queues — [[d:warrior#1404890696581779597]] (Deleted User, 2025-08-12).
- **[leveling]** Weapon-skill efficiency: Arms only needs one good 2-hander (usually an axe), so less weapon-skill grinding; Fury/DW swaps weapons more often, costing weapon-skill time — [[d:warrior#1404890944548896859]] (Deleted User, 2025-08-12).
- **[leveling]** Defensive stats undervalued while leveling Arms: Deflection (parry) both reduces incoming damage and speeds up your next swing via parry haste, improving uptime; swap to a farmed spirit set out of combat for regen; hamstring-kite to trade hits 1:1 — all reduce downtime — [[d:warrior#1404891636223180801]] (Deleted User, 2025-08-12); same source notes Arms is stronger than Fury for Warmode/enemy-faction PvP while leveling.
- **[leveling]** "Optimal" leveling path: 1-30 Fury w/ 2H, 30-40 Arms w/ 2H (Sweeping Strikes + Whirlwind strong with 2H), 40-60 dual-wield Fury (do the Scarlet Monastery and Razorfen Downs quests for strong swords), carries until ~50 when Thrash Blade becomes available — [[d:warrior#1416208145528914021]] (Hilldric, 2025-09-13).
- **[leveling/Prot]** Leveling Protection before 30 makes little sense since Shield Slam doesn't unlock until level 30 — recommend Arms or Fury until then — [[d:warrior#1410893290328555611]] (Seraaz, 2025-08-29), confirmed [[d:warrior#1418278694090440734]] (Xeraz, 2025-09-17) and [[d:warrior#1420451035801059419]] (Xeraz, 2025-09-24).
- **[leveling/tank]** Arms 1H+shield can tank all early dungeons fine on Hardcore; at low level only Cruelty and Tactical Mastery talents matter; 1H+shield gives Revenge/Shield Block/Shield Bash-interrupt threat; 2H+Whirlwind+Sweeping Strikes (from ~level 36) gives the best initial AoE aggro per pull but drains survivability/threat over a longer fight (mitigate with a post-pull macro weapon-swap to 1H+shield); switch toward FuryProt near level 60 once some hit gear is acquired — [[d:warrior#1406228381384572998]] (Nabuhodonozo, 2025-08-16).
- **[leveling]** Racial choice is a minor factor: Trolls get faster attack speed (Fury synergy) + Beast Slaying + Regeneration; Orcs get a flat damage bonus (helps every spec, lowers healing received) + stun resistance (reduced stun duration) + weapon skill — either is fine — [[d:warrior#1409888090218299482]] (Pumpkinbot, 2025-08-26).
- **[leveling]** Shadowmeld (Night Elf) useful while leveling: eat/drink safely after a rough pull, AFK safely, create distance mid-fight; net positive over 60 levels despite the small movement-speed bonus — [[d:warrior#1401220683307946125]] (Pumpkinbot, 2025-08-02).
- **[leveling gear]** Good early Horde weapon drops: Goblin starting-zone quest gives a strong 2H mace (possibly goblin-only); Tauren zone's Venture Co. supervisor kill gives a 2H mace usable only by Tauren/Goblin; Valley of Trials named mob can drop the "Bloody Pick" 1H axe; a wandering goblin vendor between Ratchet/Crossroads randomly stocks good weapons — [[d:warrior#1407716976377466950]] (Pumpkinbot, 2025-08-20).
- **[leveling]** Warrior leveling described as unusually tough: fast leveling via tents outpaces gear upgrades while classic "murder debuffs" hit hard on a gear-dependent class; said to improve substantially at level 60 — [[d:warrior#1410500712802160742]] (rud3l, 2025-08-28).

## Rotation: single target
- **[2H]** Core loop: time Slam right after an auto-attack lands; since Slam only pauses (not resets) the swing timer, it resolves before the delayed auto-attack, effectively giving free extra damage before the next auto triggers rage — [[d:warrior#1410622558163701922]] (Raphael, 2025-08-28); corroborated: "HS > Slam if you have a really fast weapon or care about threat; with a slow weapon (recommended), Slam is better" (+2) — [[d:warrior#1404235744515592282]] (Pumpkinbot, 2025-08-11).
- **[2H]** Heroic Strike is the lowest-priority filler — use only when Mortal Strike/Bloodthirst/Whirlwind are all on cooldown and rage ≥60, since (unlike Slam) it prevents the next auto-attack from generating rage; Slam is the main 2H-rotation button for both Arms and Fury, cast right after an auto — [[d:warrior#1410859179341643884]] (Draigomir, 2025-08-29).
- **[Arms]** Execute-vs-Slam math (post Precision-Cut nerf): Execute must beat a Slam's damage to be worth using; Execute damage = 870 + 15 per rage spent above 15, and a slow weapon fits two Slam-usable GCDs between auto-attacks — [[d:warrior#1405656912984408199]] (Kirchhoff, 2025-08-15).
- **[2H]** A ~3.30-speed weapon is claimed to line up so Slam fits cleanly between autos with the next auto following almost immediately after; anything faster clips the auto-attack — [[d:warrior#1414659888000536718]] (sappari, 2025-09-08; single source, self-described as possibly wrong).
- **[general]** Bloodrage: as a solo player, use it mainly after a string of misses when low on rage; as a tank, spam it often (also triggers Enrage) — [[d:warrior#1410859179341643884]] (Draigomir, 2025-08-29).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Prot]** Impale value scales with crit: value ≈ crit_rate × 0.2 per point (10% crit ≈ 1% threat/dmg per point, roughly even with 2 Cruelty points and half as good as 1H Spec; 30% raid-buffed crit ≈ 3% per point, beating both) — [[d:warrior#1404604588207898674]] (Puny, 2025-08-12); independently reconfirmed with the same heuristic, concluding Impale only beats full Cruelty above ~25% crit rating (Cruelty = 5 points for 5%, Impale = 7 points for its full bonus) — [[d:warrior#1422359835885699112]] (Not Normal), [[d:warrior#1422382493419573250]] (Nost, 2025-09-30).
- **[Prot]** Impale only applies to yellow (ability) crit damage, buffing the crit multiplier from 200%→220% for 7 talent points; not worth it over maxing Cruelty at low crit, and requires skipping other talents (Improved Heroic Strike, Tactical Mastery, Parry, Improved Thunderclap, or Improved Overpower) — [[d:warrior#1414940419401187358]] (oliverwz, 2025-09-09).
- **[Tank]** 1 Defense = 0.04% dodge + 0.04% parry + 0.04% block + 0.04% chance to be missed; low overall value for warriors since avoidance-cap is already reached via Shield Block/Shield Slam uptime; Furyprot specifically dislikes Defense since it also lowers crit-taken chance, hurting Enrage uptime — [[d:warrior#1409492245597392987]] (Velanir, 2025-08-25).
- **[Prot]** Shield Specialization (5/5) valued over Anticipation (3/3) for Deep Prot (shield worn full-time): 1% block chance beats 0.72% avoidance + 0.24% crit reduction from the equivalent 6 Defense, plus +1 rage on block — [[d:warrior#1417513900697718925]] (JohnGood, 2025-09-16).
- **[Prot]** Parry (1% avoidance per talent point) valued above Anticipation (2.4% avoidance for 3 points) — [[d:warrior#1414289151318032495]] (oliverwz, 2025-09-07).
- **[general]** Crit-cap mechanic: once past the crit cap, every swing is a miss, dodge, glancing blow, or crit — never a "regular" hit — because glancing blows can't crit — [[d:warrior#1412759056879325296]] (Nost), [[d:warrior#1412763381777301534]] (oliverwz, 2025-09-03).
- **[general]** Hit target: 8% hit (from the character stats page) is enough for PvE so your own attacks don't miss; offhand swings get a separate +10% hit from talents; hit beyond that point is valued like any other secondary stat and should be checked via a sim (linked in channel pins) — [[d:warrior#1410149574948945962]] (Guts, 2025-08-27).
- **[Prot]** Deep Prot (Impale build) SBV-vs-AGI threat debate unresolved: one tank's practical testing found T3.5 Shield-Block-Value-heavy gear (4/5 pieces) outperformed pure str/agi/sta loose items, but couldn't fully quantify why — [[d:warrior#1420888571241299998]] (JohnGood question), [[d:warrior#1421064059842854914]], [[d:warrior#1421410156712824864]] (Doite, 2025-09-26/27); broader community consensus leans AGI over SBV since SBV "puts all your eggs in a basket" with "no solid info about actual number comparison" — [[d:warrior#1421477093434195999]] (Black Hat, 2025-09-27) — contested, see Open questions.

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Prot]** Stance-threat multiplier math (Defensive Tactics tree): baseline Defensive Stance ≈ +30% threat, +15% more w/ Defiance; Defensive Tactics adds 180% increased threat from the Def-Stance bonus; net result computed as Battle/Berserker w/ Defiance = 151.3% threat multiplier vs Def Stance w/ Defiance = 149.5% — i.e. Battle/Berserker is very slightly higher threat than Def Stance once Defiance is taken, on top of avoiding the stance's 10% damage penalty — [[d:warrior#1404171365233201225]] (Uzemark), [[d:warrior#1414940419401187358]] (oliverwz, 2025-08-10 & 2025-09-09); a separate self-calculation claims ~45% more threat in Def Stance vs ~54% in other stances under Defensive Tactics — [[d:warrior#1419283903646142584]] (Lÿenn, 2025-09-21; unconfirmed, poster asked for correction).
- **[Prot]** Revenge flagged as underwhelming/non-scaling by both a dev and players, central to Deep Prot/Defensive Tactics' threat problems — dev acknowledgment it needs help — [[d:warrior#1407345918990221464]], [[d:warrior#1407347230406611104]] (Lhian, 2025-08-19); "Revenge does not scale" — [[d:warrior#1417519483605618809]] (JohnGood, 2025-09-16); real-world TPS reports of 350-600 despite full rage and "perfect rotation" (Slam/Revenge/Sunder) vs 400-500 TPS from geared DPS in the same group — [[d:warrior#1414106002604691577]] (Gigglenaut, 2025-09-07).
- **[general]** Overpower cannot be dodged, parried, or blocked (miss is still possible) — [[d:warrior#1410859179341643884]] (Draigomir, 2025-08-29); priority nuance: Overpower shouldn't take priority over Concussion Blow or Shield Slam (both do more dmg/threat) individually, but can take priority over pressing *both* together if doing so would let the Overpower proc window expire unused — [[d:warrior#1422383361279791134]] (Black Hat, 2025-09-30).
- **[general]** Sunder Armor: keep 5 stacks on any mob lasting >4s in raids, including off-tank targets — 5 stacks ≈ 40% more flat damage taken by the target vs 0 stacks, compounding into more rage, more abilities, more raid dps, and more tank threat — [[d:warrior#1400970224232304810]] (Marinatall Ironside, 2025-08-02).
- **[general]** AP overtakes Crit (STR > AGI) in value once crit reaches roughly the "mid-30%" range fully raid-buffed; below that they're roughly equal — [[d:warrior#1400972827796176907]] (Marinatall Ironside, 2025-08-02).
- **[Tank]** Charge/Intercept/Intervene cost rage and function as one-off PvE openers/gap-closers, not sustained mobility; Charge specifically criticized as a rage-costly opener that penalizes prot tanks post-pull, unlike Thunderclap which received a Defensive-Stance usability fix — [[d:warrior#1410996997506338957]] (Lipibidy, 2025-08-29; open grievance, unresolved).
- **[Prot]** Improved Berserker Rage (added in 1.18) can dispel many raid-relevant slows/roots/curses at a 30s CD: Curse of the Rift/King's Curse in Kara40, Noth's Cripple and Gothik's horse-stomp in Naxx, Mind Flay on Scientist packs in Naxx, and roots on Anub'Rekhan or the ZG spider boss — quoted from an external guide — [[d:warrior#1421142174874996736]] (Neurospicy, 2025-09-27).
- **[Prot]** Shield Block and Shield Slam's "guaranteed block" buffs cancel each other instead of stacking, undermining the intended Shield Block→Revenge play pattern; a proposed fix was a new low-cost "Feint" ability to proc Overpower from Battle Stance instead — [[d:warrior#1414251056862597213]] (D.C.V., +4, 2025-09-07), [[d:warrior#1416871878462804134]] (Black Hat, 2025-09-15).
- **[general]** Crushing Blow mechanic: Shield Block raises block chance +75% and removes crushing blows/crits from the attack-result table while active; it has 2 charges, so a mob attacking at ≥2.0 speed can still crush a warrior who fails to avoid 3 attacks in a row — quoted from vanilla-wow-archive wiki — [[d:warrior#1417517165644353657]] (JohnGood, 2025-09-16).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Tank]** Furyprot vs Defensive Tactics vs Deep Prot tradeoffs: Furyprot sacrifices shield mitigation for more overall threat from more frequent hits and no strong opener, relying on damage/threat modifiers; Defensive Tactics can open with Concussion Blow then spam Sunder to 5 for an early lead but can't escape the Def-Stance threat modifier while carrying a shield (strictly a tank spec); Furyprot alone can be talented for some DPS via Improved Execute — [[d:warrior#1401953920166793298]] (Don't ping me, 2025-08-04).
- **[Tank]** "Fury Tanking" history: pure Def-Stance tanking was standard early Vanilla for mitigation+threat, but in higher tiers (AQ40/Naxx) threat, not mitigation, became the bottleneck, so warriors traded mitigation for Fury-stance threat via more frequent hits and threat-from-being-hit — [[d:warrior#1401577825911439507]] (Pumpkinbot, 2025-08-03); disputed as fundamentally backwards design vs. Paladin/Bear tanks, which stay tanky AND generate threat without sacrificing mitigation — [[d:warrior#1401582850695888999]] (Virasan, 2025-08-03).
- **[Tank]** Weapon-speed choice for Defensive Tactics/Impale tanks is disputed: one line of reasoning favors slower weapons since Deep Wounds/Overpower/Cleave/Whirlwind all scale with slow weapons, and DT tanks use Heroic Strike less than Furyprot — [[d:warrior#1401608220585361420]] (Puny), [[d:warrior#1401580907608932383]] (Sylvaira, 2025-08-03) — vs. a report from actual Kara40 tank setups that "it's all fast weapons or TF MH if def tactics" for more swing attempts/procs, since only the first 30 seconds of threat on a pull really matters — [[d:warrior#1420507353048023216]] (dewbie, 2025-09-25); not reconciled in-chunk.
- **[Tank/leveling]** Early dungeon-tank rotation (Prot/Arms hybrid): Mark Skull > Charge > Demo Shout > Thunder Clap > Concussion Blow > Shield Slam > Sunders, Revenge on proc; stays mostly Battle Stance on trash, swaps to Defensive Stance on bosses after the initial Charge to give the healer a breather and use Revenge procs — [[d:warrior#1410572688505831454]] (Raphael, 2025-08-28); also opens with Charge + Intervene back to a healer/caster as a style/utility pull method — [[d:warrior#1410575297413120010]] (Raphael, 2025-08-28).
- **[Tank/leveling]** Low-level Prot tanking: only Cruelty and Tactical Mastery talents are really needed early; 1H+Shield preferred for Revenge/Shield Block/Shield Bash-interrupt threat; 2H+Whirlwind+Sweeping Strikes (from ~level 36) gives the best initial AoE aggro but loses survivability/threat over a long fight, mitigated with a post-pull macro weapon-swap; switch to FuryProt near level 60 once some hit gear is acquired — [[d:warrior#1406228381384572998]] (Nabuhodonozo, 2025-08-16).
- **[Tank stat priority]** External guide cited for full mitigation/defense priority: Shield Block Value > Defence > Block > Crit > Strength > Haste/AP — but no community consensus was reached on exact Defence/Block/Dodge-to-Armor conversion rates — [[d:warrior#1409332627651170457]] (Ciung, 2025-08-25; open question, partially addressed by the Defense-value math bullet under Stats).
- **[Tank]** Switching between a full-time Tactical Mastery build and Furyprot described as "genuinely miserable" once accustomed to always having Tactical Mastery — [[d:warrior#1410270865207197961]] (Dekabre, +3, 2025-08-27).
- **[Tank]** Warrior-vs-Paladin tank comparison: Warrior Shield Wall = 75% dmg reduction for 10-12s every 20-30 min, Last Stand = +30% HP for 20s every 10 min, vs Paladin Divine Shield = full immunity 12s every 5 min, Lay on Hands = full-health heal once/hour, BoP = physical immunity 10s every 3-5 min; Warrior's defensive kit judged weaker/less frequent, but it retains Intercept/Intervene mobility that Paladins lack — [[d:warrior#1406636324030644397]], [[d:warrior#1406636383166267524]] (Ironraven, 2025-08-17).
- **[Tank]** "All fights are furyprot fights with good enough healers" (+3) — [[d:warrior#1413863977729130526]] (Weirdthing, 2025-09-06).
- **[Tank]** Naxx-style threat/targeting logic (from a mob/boss ability script): iterates the threat table up to the 4th-highest player, skips players not in the instance, out of melee range, or immune, selects the highest-HP non-maintank as the "hateful" target, and defaults to the maintank if no valid target is found — [[d:warrior#1414212865501565048]] (Velanir, 2025-09-07; instance/boss name not stated in the message).
