## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Fury/2h]** 2h Fury is considered slightly better than DW Fury overall/lategame, and specifically better in Kara40, though the two specs are close through most tiers; sim doesn't model Slam correctly so can't be trusted for 2h — [[d:warrior#1440413498202128395]] (30, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440413427079057519]] (30, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1441922048279449694]] (30, 2025-11-23), [[d:warrior#1441374012633780395]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-21, "might change with upcoming balance patch").
- **[Fury/DW]** DW Fury is called "probably a top 5 spec right now for overall dmg in raids"; raiding DW fury talent build linked (points=dQIAIYAQ-AoFAoAAoZCVAB-) — [[d:warrior#1441921471822692493]], [[d:warrior#1441921379682357381]] (30, 2025-11-23).
- **[Arms]** Debated: some call Arms a "toilet spec" worse than 2h Fury for nearly the same playstyle; others say Arms is "perfectly viable" with good gear — [[d:warrior#1440494753937621135]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1440500709635133480]] (axelfrog, disputed, 2025-11-19). Consensus lean: 2h Fury > Arms currently, "might change with the upcoming balance patch" — [[d:warrior#1441374012633780395]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-21).
- **[Tank/FuryProt vs DeepProt(DefTac)]** FuryProt and DefTac/Furyprot-adjacent builds both "sacrifice shield block to take more damage for threat" and are seen as largely redundant/superfluous with each other by some — [[d:warrior#1440111692263460901]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-18). Consensus: DT(Impale)/DefTac is "good enough for 95% of raids," versatile; FuryProt needed only in the top ~5% where healers can handle the extra damage — [[d:warrior#1441380034169278612]] (Weirdthing, +3, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441380100556849173]] (Weirdthing, 2025-11-21). Lipibidy argues deep prot tanking is neglected by devs and "not playing furyprot when geared enough is almost griefing" (disputed as extreme) — [[d:warrior#1441290715207569526]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-21), disputed by MoneyCow [[d:warrior#1441290749978349659]].
- **[Tank/DW tanking]** DW tanking viable, weapon-skill/glance considerations debated (see Stats section) — [[d:warrior#1440376290912894986]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-18).
- **[Tank/2h tanking]** 2H tanking "definitely can" work, situational — good while leveling/dungeons via Sweeping Strikes+Whirlwind but "falls off" mid-40s then comes back once overgeared; if you severely outgear a raid you can tank it with 2h fury + lots of stamina — [[d:warrior#1440837053947445353]] (Dodging, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1441916755252478004]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-22: "2H tanking falls off in the 40sish then comes back"), [[d:warrior#1440842845404659773]] (Kyrsie, 2025-11-19).
- **[PvP]** General consensus: warrior PvP is currently weak/"homeless"; Fury feels bad in PvP; Prot warrior in PvP is called "incredibly weak," "miserable," "tragic" (tree meant for PvP but unplayable there) except niche CC/body-blocking utility — [[d:warrior#1441069357634683031]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1441629747208650943]] (Zestitcha, +1, 2025-11-22), [[d:warrior#1441630071667425461]] (Zestitcha, +2, 2025-11-22), [[d:warrior#1441770272146460864]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441586046373724160]] (Aubreykun on Prot PvP utility role: jump on casters/FC, body-block for team, 2025-11-22).
- **[Deep Prot tanking below 60]** FuryProt is not a leveling spec by definition — key talents (Defiance) require level 60; below 60 "furyprot" is just gimped fury with less threat/damage — [[d:warrior#1441913746598527047]] (30, 2025-11-23), [[d:warrior#1441917699608543252]] (Dodging, 2025-11-23).
- **[AoE tanking]** Recurring complaint (asked "every month and a half"): Warrior AoE threat in dungeons is much weaker than Druid/Paladin; players told to "just accept" it, use marks, let non-mark-targeting DPS die, and rely on TF proc — [[d:warrior#1441912754003906680]] (Kyrsie, 2025-11-23), [[d:warrior#1441908913963270185]] (VeganCourage, 2025-11-23), [[d:warrior#1441913518063620138]] (VeganCourage, +3, 2025-11-23, "warrior was the only tank in classic, here they forgot about it").

## Leveling
- **[Leveling spec choice]** Recommend leveling as Arms (not one true spec until 40 anyway): lets you abuse Improved Overpower early, and Arms is useful if you need to tank a leveling dungeon; Arms plays "almost the same" as 2h Fury but 2h Fury is just better once available — [[d:warrior#1441200557141262437]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441200669490024571]], [[d:warrior#1441201192125337671]].
- **[Talent levels]** Revenge unlocks at level 14; Sunder Armor comes with the Defensive Stance quest — "don't skip that quest" — [[d:warrior#1441201843677167788]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-21).
- **[Talent order arms]** Take Improved Thunderclap, Tactical Mastery, Improved Charge early (rage generation/retention); forced to take Improved Rend for Deep Wounds/Impale, and Master Strike for Master of Arms — [[d:warrior#1441201533638414458]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441197162645160190]], [[d:warrior#1441197331633410290]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-21).
- **[Leveling rotation, arms 2h]** Weapon carries ~90% of damage; slam after every swing unless able to Whirlwind; don't spam HS/Cleave or you'll be rage starved; prioritize Mortal Strike for Sweeping Strikes charges (hits harder than Slam); rotation guide: Slam > MS > slam > WW > slam > slam if weapon is 3.4+ speed (avoids clipping) — [[d:warrior#1441193290761506880]], [[d:warrior#1441193963636654190]], [[d:warrior#1441194094897397884]], [[d:warrior#1441194431033245780]], [[d:warrior#1441194764769689771]] (axelfrog/Lipibidy, 2025-11-21).
- **[Sunder while leveling]** Only sunder to about 70% of mob's health (rule of thumb; can be lower level or higher depending on mob); at ~level 20, Thunderclap (tclap) still does good work; not worth using Sunder in open world outside dungeons — [[d:warrior#1441100856849072138]], [[d:warrior#1441101266167267348]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-20), [[d:warrior#1441196740668686386]] (axelfrog, 2025-11-21).
- **[Slam while leveling]** "Slam sucks while leveling" per Dodging — Slam locks your swing (delays auto if ready while casting), and casting loses avoidance the whole time; Slam only worth using if its cast time is less than your weapon's swing speed — [[d:warrior#1441056869585125399]] (Dodging, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441056501895794689]] (Jules, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441056765859856457]] (Dodging, 2025-11-21). (Note: this contradicts earlier statement that Slam no longer pauses swing entirely, see Bugs & quirks.)
- **[Levelling casters]** Caster mobs: dps race, slam then weave hamstring/overpower/MS between slam and swing if weapon slow enough; toggle Heroic Strike if overcapping rage; LoS multiple casters around terrain to bunch them, keep aggro off healer — [[d:warrior#1441103070921490552]], [[d:warrior#1441103624662155316]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-20).
- **[Weapon choice/race]** Weapon skill "means virtually nothing" at low level per one player but disputed by another who feels the 1% hit difference (170 vs 176 skill) while grinding 500-600 mobs/level — [[d:warrior#1440457778438082651]] (Dodging, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1440458101793882235]] (Kitty_TW, disputed, 2025-11-19). WW-axe still recommended pick at level 36 as human for a WW-build leveler; 2H hammer alternative available from Cenarion Grove quest — [[d:warrior#1440457518387171339]] (Kitty_TW, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1440457677057560678]] (Aubreykun: WW sword is a unique transmog/funny, hammer is a "funny pvp bonker", 2025-11-19).
- **[Leveling tank build reasoning]** For a level-53 tank without Fury Prot access: prefer some Fury crit/rage talents over Anticipation/Blood Craze while leveling; furyprot itself isn't accessible until 60 (Defiance-gated) — [[d:warrior#1441910102843195522]] (VeganCourage, 2025-11-23), [[d:warrior#1441909287193542838]] (Dodging, 2025-11-23).
- **[Weapon skill fix]** Weapon skill can be raised later via the Ratchet NPC — [[d:warrior#1440458163769053296]] (Blackjack, single source, 2025-11-19).
- **[Disarm while leveling]** Disarm is a regularly-encountered mechanic while leveling as a weapon-wielding warrior; Blackfathom Depths is specifically called out as "notoriously annoying" for disarm mobs — [[d:warrior#1442460522825125959]] (Vladizlav, +1, 2025-11-24). DW warriors can get disarm immunity from a specific talent/item if built correctly ("DW warrs also have disarm immune if they're not stupid") — [[d:warrior#1442456870764679240]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-11-24).
- **[Staff Master Strike leveling build]** A leveling build using a melee staff + the Master Strike (polearm/staff) talent line plus a Defense-skill/Parry talent can reach "a little over 35% parry" per fight, which both raises DPS (parries shorten your swing timer) and improves survivability while leveling and while 2h-Arms-tanking dungeons; the hard part is finding good leveling staves — the only one mentioned is the Crescent Staff, available around level 18 — [[d:warrior#1442410985062268969]] (Beep, 2025-11-23), [[d:warrior#1442416623834107995]], [[d:warrior#1442416682193915905]], [[d:warrior#1442416863316410470]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-23).

## Rotation: single target
- **[FuryProt]** BT on cooldown, never clip it; Sunder Armor to fill/dump rage between BTs (also can proc Windfury Totem); Heroic Strike queued only when rage is high enough to not miss the next BT (recommend manually judging fight-by-fight rather than a fixed macro threshold, though some use ~45 rage as floor, others recommend 55-60); Revenge on cooldown when available — [[d:warrior#1441782734312706048]], [[d:warrior#1441789617040003344]], [[d:warrior#1441790715477627012]] (Lipibidy/Nost consensus, 2025-11-22), [[d:warrior#1441685585138946200]] (Ryiana: prefers 55-60 rage floor to preserve WF/TF proc refresh windows, 2025-11-22).
- **[FuryProt macro example]** `#showtooltip / /cast [nocooldown] Bloodthirst; [reactive, nocooldown] Revenge; Sunder Armor / /cast [mypower:>=45] heroic strike / /startattack` — used successfully by Lÿenn, though some (Lipibidy) recommend NOT macro-automating HS/Sunder priority for tanks since it can't judge fight state — [[d:warrior#1441798149961289778]] (Lÿenn, 2025-11-22), [[d:warrior#1441787518298099733]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-22).
- **[2h Fury]** Weave Slam between swings only if weapon speed allows without clipping; sim mis-implements Slam so is misleading for 2h fury; execute-phase questions raised (BT if 2k+ AP and not about to rage-cap, otherwise Execute-spam, HS if about to rage-cap) — [[d:warrior#1440413427079057519]] (30, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440355237578936443]] (Fypoolday question, 2025-11-18, unanswered directly in this range).
- **[2h Fury rotation, explicit]** "Flurry Slam > BT > WW > Heroic Strike on top of Flurry to dump rage"; only queue Heroic Strike at ~70 rage or you'll be rage-starved; Whirlwind only cleaves the 2 targets adjacent to your target unless talented into Sweeping Strikes — [[d:warrior#1442845647295217745]] (Dodging, 2025-11-25), [[d:warrior#1442845986752827473]] (Blackjack, 2025-11-25), [[d:warrior#1443974359042625648]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-28).
- **[Slam vs slow 2h weapons]** With a Windfury Totem active, it's usually better to skip Slam entirely on a slow 2h weapon and fill GCDs with Hamstring/Master Strike instead, since Slam's 2.5s cast is long and Flurry/WF procs already fill the swing-timer gap; without WF Totem, Slam may still be worth using — [[d:warrior#1442900910069256253]] (Vladizlav, 2025-11-25). At high Flurry uptime a 3.6-speed weapon can reach ~2.71s effective swing speed, at which point Slam remains usable but flurry-uptime-dependent (crit-gear-gated) — [[d:warrior#1442901949157609638]] (Blackjack, 2025-11-25), [[d:warrior#1442902081852936405]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-25, confirms Flurry currently also reduces Slam's cast time).
- **[Arms 2h execute]** Complaint that Execute (100 rage) feels weaker than Mortal Strike, considered a bug/balance issue by several — [[d:warrior#1441397675223420989]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441397726443995260]], [[d:warrior#1441400186952880149]] (Blackjack confirms, 2025-11-21).
- **[Execute in AoE trash]** Whether to execute every mob below 20% in trash pulls "completely depends" on pull speed/raid speed: the slower you pull, the more worth it to execute (rage decays anyway); benefit of NOT executing is saving rage for next pack — [[d:warrior#1441905823889494056]] and [[d:warrior#1441906117465866261]], [[d:warrior#1441906156040622121]], [[d:warrior#1441906226874290207]] (30, 2025-11-23).
- **[Swing timers]** Slam no longer pauses your swing timer entirely — it only delays the swing if it's ready while you're still casting Slam (changed behavior vs earlier patch) — [[d:warrior#1440179762696949821]], [[d:warrior#1440179796335398952]] (Kyrsie, 2025-11-18, corrected from somedeadguy's initial claim it pauses). Swing timer addon recommended for DW to catch facing/range misses (visually easier to spot red flash than subtle icon color change) — [[d:warrior#1440216256014712842]], [[d:warrior#1440216828063514694]] (30, 2025-11-18).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Arms/2h in dungeons]** Sunder Armor recommended for dungeon trash (not open world); rule of thumb apply sunders until ~70% mob HP — [[d:warrior#1441196740668686386]] (axelfrog, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441101266167267348]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-20).
- **[Prot AoE threat]** Warrior AoE threat tools are weak vs. Druid Swipe / Paladin Consecration; suggested rotation for AoE tanking at low level: Battle Stance, Charge (improved) + Demoralizing Shout, then Defensive Stance + Concussion Blow, tab-Sunder while using Shield Slam + Revenge on CD — [[d:warrior#1441907648734957650]] (Jorrick/Drath describing own attempt, 2025-11-23); response: accept warriors won't out-AoE-threat druid/paladin, mark targets, let non-compliant DPS die, big packs should be tanked by a mage/paladin with more AoE control until warrior is overgeared enough for 2H SS+WW tanking — [[d:warrior#1441916755252478004]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-23).
- **[Battle Shout AoE threat]** Spamming Battle Shout can provide decent AoE threat, works until close to level-60 dungeons where DPS start wearing raid gear — [[d:warrior#1441945286657441793]] (Vibes, single source, 2025-11-23).
- **[Sweeping Strikes]** Sweeping Strikes + Shield Slam ("SS+WW") for 2H tanking cleave once overgeared; Sweeping Strikes + Shield Slam combo generally seen as "fun for dungeons... but just a meme" for endgame raid tanking since Concussion Blow's AP scaling + 10 rage return makes plain Shield Slam/Sweeping Strikes comparatively worse — [[d:warrior#1440382396964012245]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-11-18).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit/glance cap]** Recurring complaint that the glance cap (currently apparently ~315-318 range) is too high on Turtle WoW and should be lowered to 310 to reduce the need for "weaponskill races" via race/gear/quests (e.g. Ouro gloves); disputed by others who don't find it necessary — [[d:warrior#1440372288104173640]] (Lipibidy, +4, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440373819440365629]] (Thirtyseven, disputed: "you dont need to pick a race for the weapon skill", 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440373558650998864]] (Squid, disputed, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440455418735497307]] (Lipibidy repeats ask, 2025-11-19).
- **[Weapon skill]** IF (Ironfoe) + Mordenai/Mord set can only reach 314 weapon skill with Ouro gloves on a human (need Spineshatter instead to reach higher); K40 spear + Ouro gloves + weaponskill quest puts a human at 316 for polearms specifically — [[d:warrior#1440454695947604190]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1440376290912894986]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-18).
- **[Block value / Str]** Str→Block Value ratio described as "terrible"; Agility/Crit prioritized over Str/AP for threat generation, then Shield Block Value (SBV) as tertiary if you need more mitigation; gearing priority: "if you need more agro, Agi/crit >> Str/AP > SBV; if you need more mitigation, Stam/armor do the heavy lifting" — [[d:warrior#1440125579163734137]], [[d:warrior#1440125776984014929]], [[d:warrior#1440130599955468308]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-18). Defense stat is considered "trash" for threat-focused tank gearing by multiple (30, Black Hat) — [[d:warrior#1440128008353615962]] (30, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440132295339020362]] (Nost, 2025-11-18, references extensive prior channel discussion on defense being bad).
- **[FuryProt stat priority]** Stam > Agi > Str, hit cap preferred, weaponskill preferred, crit always welcome (Lipibidy's general prio); Agi valued over Str for FuryProt because it double-dips defensively; AP argued MORE valuable to FuryProt than to pure Fury because FuryProt lacks Impale talent — [[d:warrior#1440374163897319454]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440348551228952619]], [[d:warrior#1440349536890519673]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-18). Crit prioritized somewhat for FuryProt because earlier Flurry uptime matters — [[d:warrior#1440382202406764656]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-11-18).
- **[Str vs Agi benchmark, 2h Fury]** For a geared BWL 2h-fury warrior, Str sims at ~0.77 dps/point vs Agi ~0.59 dps/point (server-specific sim output, "for my warrior rn") — [[d:warrior#1440335670378893323]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-11-18).
- **[AP vs Agi/hit threat question]** Debated at length whether 30 AP is better than 12 Agi + 1 hit (overcap) for FuryProt threat; resolved as: if under hit cap, 12 Agi+1 hit is better; if hit-capped already, 30 AP wins consistently — [[d:warrior#1440381914375651429]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-18).
- **[DPS = Threat for FuryProt]** Multiple confirm: for FuryProt, DPS effectively equals threat (no separate high-threat-low-dps buttons beyond Sunder/Revenge which are "skill rotation," separate from gear choice) — [[d:warrior#1440380579383349400]], [[d:warrior#1440387963019202691]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-18).
- **[Auto-attack scaling with raid size]** Confirmed: on Turtle WoW, auto-attack damage scales up with raid size (20-man vs 40-man), but ability damage does not scale — a fresh player might feel much weaker in 40-mans coming from 20-mans — [[d:warrior#1440399806118756372]] (Squid, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440402027682463805]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, confirms "that damage definitely scales on turtle wow (auto attacks only)", 2025-11-18).
- **[Windfury proc mechanics]** Extra attacks cannot proc further extra attacks, but normal (non-extra) autos can proc any number of extra-attack effects simultaneously (e.g. get both Windfury and Hand of Justice off one normal auto); off-hand extra-attack procs (like Thrash Blade) are much rarer because instants only proc mainhand effects alongside mainhand autos, while offhand effects only proc from offhand autos specifically ("windfury ghost story...laid to rest many times") — [[d:warrior#1440284400150384640]] (Don't ping me, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440284716770263191]] (Don't ping me, +1, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440297090914975875]] (Dodging, 2025-11-18). Windfury Totem has an ICD; can proc off Sunder Armor too — [[d:warrior#1440550066489397379]] (30, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1441790506991358112]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-22).
- **[Armor mitigation formula example]** Worked example: 1000 AP → BT 350 base + 200 = 550 raw damage; against 4204 armor target, mitigation ≈43.3%, so 550 becomes ~312 actual damage (formula: tmpvalue = 0.1*armor/(8.5*level+40); mitigation = tmpvalue/(1+tmpvalue)) — [[d:warrior#1440818495700074667]], [[d:warrior#1440818655314317364]] (30, 2025-11-20).
- **[Armor cap for tanks]** With Stoneshield + Shield + Ancestral Fortitude/Inspiration, a tank is already over the "soft armor cap," making raw armor stacking largely irrelevant beyond that — [[d:warrior#1441252533916930098]] (30, 2025-11-21, re: Patchwerk/hateful-strike soak gearing).
- **[Weightstones/Slam interaction]** Open question: do weightstones (haste enchant) affect Slam cast time the same way other haste sources do? — unanswered — [[d:warrior#1441115846142328872]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-21, question unanswered).
- **[Hit cap vs bosses, precise numbers]** Natural weapon-skill cap is 300; bosses/skull enemies have 315 defense skill. At 300 weapon skill you need 8% hit to never miss a boss (you can still be dodged, and parried only when attacking from the front); every 5 weapon skill above 300 lowers the needed hit by 1%, to a floor of 5% hit at 315 weapon skill. Anything below boss level (less than 3 levels above you) needs less hit to never miss — this is why players keep separate trash and boss gear sets — [[d:warrior#1442566552254943312]], [[d:warrior#1442566640855421169]], [[d:warrior#1442566918815875133]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-24). Rule of thumb given to a fresh 60: aim for ~8% hit and get "as close to 315 [weapon skill] as possible," without fretting over exactly capping it — anything above ~310 is enough per Black Hat — [[d:warrior#1442563212850499655]] (Agamemnoth, 2025-11-24), [[d:warrior#1442580029837152276]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-24).
- **[Weapon skill's real value = glancing blow reduction]** The most important thing weapon skill does is reduce the glancing-blow penalty, which scales linearly to 315 — considered as important as hit cap for any melee spec since white damage is a top-2/top-3 damage source — [[d:warrior#1442567096159699005]], [[d:warrior#1442567194910261408]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-24). Precise glance numbers: glancing blow chance vs a level-63 (boss) target is 40% and does NOT change with weapon skill; each point of weapon skill instead gives +2% glancing damage dealt, capped at 95% damage (i.e. glances start at 65% damage dealt at 300 skill and rise to 95% at 315) — [[d:warrior#1443319985345921047]], [[d:warrior#1443320129080529017]] (30, 2025-11-27). Broken down by target level: vs a level 60 target, 10% glance chance for a 5% damage penalty; vs 61, 20% chance/5% penalty; vs 62, 30% chance/10% penalty; vs a boss (63), 40% chance/35% penalty — [[d:warrior#1442997907706875928]], [[d:warrior#1442998042121998457]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-26).
- **[Weapon skill cost/benefit debate]** Some argue weapon skill is overrated: e.g. going from 300→315 weapon skill (a common single-item swing) was measured at only ~30 dps in one player's sim settings, and going from 310→315 was estimated at only ~1.2-1.5% extra dps by another — enough that a plain stat upgrade item can out-dps a "capping" item picked only to patch 2-3 missing weapon skill — [[d:warrior#1443344341627179009]] (Dodging, 2025-11-27), [[d:warrior#1442587232841240669]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-24). Countered: weapon skill affects glancing damage on 40-60% of overall damage (DW especially, since it's most of the rage generation), so even a small per-point value compounds; 30 calls it "one of the only permanent damage buffs" available and still worth getting before committing to a specific weapon type — [[d:warrior#1443340095141707920]], [[d:warrior#1443341601937035447]] (30/worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-11-27). General advice: don't go far over the 315 cap, and don't buy costly weapon-skill gear if you don't expect to keep that weapon type — [[d:warrior#1442587777727332472]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-24), [[d:warrior#1443345862100258857]] (Dodging, 2025-11-27).
- **[Weaponskill itemization critique]** Weaponskill on TWoW is concentrated in large chunks (+5) on a small number of items per weapon type, which "locks" players into using specific gear (e.g. Edgemaster's Handguards) until better weaponskill items appear; Swords/2H-Swords have the most weaponskill support (many options, e.g. AQ20 gloves), Axes have the least, Daggers have always had strong support; recommended fix (disputed): lower the glance cap to 310 or spread smaller weaponskill amounts across many more items — [[d:warrior#1442622350116913252]], [[d:warrior#1442623757867942135]], [[d:warrior#1442625998742097951]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-25). Edgemaster's Handguards lose value once Ouro (AQ40) drops weaponskill gloves and other custom TWoW items — [[d:warrior#1442620634705170492]] (Virasan, 2025-11-25).
- **[Uncrushable / Anticipation & defense mechanics]** On TWoW you need 322 defense skill plus 2% Shield Block (or Parry) chance to be "uncrushable" while shield-blocking, a higher threshold than the classic +7 defense/+2% block baseline — [[d:warrior#1444036406358966396]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-29). With the Anticipation talent (reworked from 5pts/10def to fewer points giving more defense per point): 3/3 Anticipation only needs 2 more defense to reach that threshold, 2/3 needs 8, 1/3 needs 15 — [[d:warrior#1444088323000762509]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-29). Most tank gear already provides enough defense/avoidance that Anticipation is largely unnecessary except when very undergeared or pushing a DPS-leaning tank build — [[d:warrior#1444055306135928922]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-29), [[d:warrior#1444066988493242457]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-29). Parry is called the best defensive stat for tanking because it also increases DPS (faster next swing) — [[d:warrior#1444090153403420796]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-29).
- **[Defensive Tactics threat math]** With Defiance, Defensive Tactics gives 49.5% extra threat while in Defensive Stance and 51.28% extra threat while in Battle/Berserker stance; separately, Battle/Berserker stance deal ~11% more damage than Defensive Stance — [[d:warrior#1443249408622723155]], [[d:warrior#1443249470988095498]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-27). Without Defiance, base threat modifiers are Battle/Berserker ≈0.8x and Defensive ≈1.3x (Defiance scales the Def Tactics bonus on top of this) — [[d:warrior#1443250141661495296]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-27).
- **[Armor pen threshold]** Armor Penetration is viewed as "all in or basically not at all" — most raid mobs sit near ~800 armor left after Sunder/other debuffs, and ArP needs to clear that remaining threshold to matter, so small amounts of ArP on gear/enchants have little impact — [[d:warrior#1443136619644457111]] (Lokimanor, 2025-11-26, single source/uncertain).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Threat = DPS for FuryProt]** Repeatedly emphasized: for FuryProt, "DPS = threat," except for the dedicated threat tools Sunder/Revenge which is separately "skill rotation, not gear" — see Stats section citations.
- **[Opener importance]** Long, detailed debate (Lipibidy vs Nost vs Not Normal vs 30) converges on: securing a strong opener (first 10-30s) matters far more than average TPS later, because tanks with equal/better gear than DPS should always maintain higher average TPS once ahead; if a tank's avg TPS is genuinely below any DPS's, "something has gone seriously wrong" — [[d:warrior#1441278946753904641]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441279543309762722]] (Not Normal, +1, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441247522742997062]] (30, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441275396485025915]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-21).
- **[Threat and stalling]** Convention: DPS should hold back ~2-3 GCDs on pull to allow tank threat lead in case of parry/dodge RNG on pull; late-fight threat caps are still possible if a DPS pops all CDs at once against a low-avg-TPS tank — [[d:warrior#1441269697512472678]] (Nost, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441270042519146648]] (Nost, 2025-11-21).
- **[Opener burst example]** A tank reported a "quadruple crit" pull opener (Shield Slam + Concussion Blow + Heroic Strike + a Windfury proc all critting) yielding roughly 7-8k threat in one opener — [[d:warrior#1443250783763169481]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-27).
- **[Kiting/soak gearing]** Discussion of "Patchwerk soaker" gearing: for pure hateful-strike soaking, avoidance doesn't help (can't be crit/crushed by hatefuls, and if hateful is avoided you get zero threat from it) — so gear priority is 1H hit cap + as much stamina as possible; armor is largely irrelevant since already past soft armor cap with Stoneshield+shield+buffs; can Sapper on pull for near-guaranteed threat — [[d:warrior#1441252207855927477]], [[d:warrior#1441252441004703905]], [[d:warrior#1441252533916930098]], [[d:warrior#1441252781921927248]] (30, 2025-11-21).
- **[Revenge design]** Revenge deemed weak/low-damage by design intent — it exists for efficient threat per rage cost, not raw damage; can't be used with Defensive Tactics (mutually exclusive threat-boost choice in prot tree); without Def Tactics, Overpower is the "backup" threat tool when Shield Slam gets dodged — [[d:warrior#1440729735926648834]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1440736750862336062]], [[d:warrior#1440737406700752896]] (Sylvaira/Lipibidy, 2025-11-19).
- **[Macro examples for rage-conditional casts]** SuperCleveRoids/RoidMacros syntax examples: `/cast [mypower:>30] SpellName`; conditional emote: `/cast [nocooldown:"SpellName" mypower:>30] {"/roar"}` — [[d:warrior#1441447659402428537]] (Viertakt, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441453971645399192]] (Viertakt, 2025-11-21).
- **[Auto-attack toggle macro]** For auto-attack-always-on macros, users report needing specific action-bar slot placement; general community view: heavy 1-button "IWinEnhanced"-style all-in-one rotation macros/addons are seen negatively ("actively make people worse at the game") though tolerated for accessibility — [[d:warrior#1441801891339309066]], [[d:warrior#1441803246472532109]] (Dodging, 2025-11-22).
- **[Heroic Strike queue change, major]** A patch change now makes a queued Heroic Strike (or other queued next-melee ability) unqueue itself the instant your rage drops below its cost, rather than persisting until the swing lands — quoted patch text: "Queued next-melee spells and abilities...will be interrupted if the player has insufficient power...This can occur if power is depleted by other means after the spell has been queued" — [[d:warrior#1443033417167016138]] (eric4225, 2025-11-25). Community reaction was strongly negative: it forces far more repeated Heroic-Strike keypresses/mousewheel-macro spam (since a later rage gain, e.g. from an offhand hit or Bloodrage, will no longer let a stale HS queue go off), disproportionately hurts higher-ping players, and even the dev (Lhian) didn't know why it was implemented and was asked to reconsider — [[d:warrior#1443038716217000113]], [[d:warrior#1443039202118733967]], [[d:warrior#1443040131207528471]] (30, 2025-11-25), [[d:warrior#1443040559508881520]] (Lhian, +6, "i dont know why it happened to be fair", 2025-11-25). Workaround: bind a mousewheel-up/down macro to Heroic Strike so it can be re-queued instantly and often, since MH/OH swings can land simultaneously — [[d:warrior#1443366954655154322]], [[d:warrior#1443367631448047847]] (30, 2025-11-27). The change disproportionately hurts 2h Fury (which loses HS-queue damage before Slam) more than DW — [[d:warrior#1443255136607801569]] (evella, 2025-11-27).

## Tanking specifics
(See also Spec viability, Stats, Rotation:AoE sections for tank-specific stat/AoE content.)
- **[Tank stat philosophy]** "Tank stats are an engineering issue" — aim to barely survive within acceptable damage-intake tolerance while using minimum high-threat/low-DPS abilities needed to hold threat; anything beyond that is inefficiency that could go toward more damage; disputed as too idealized/divorced from RNG reality of actual tanking — [[d:warrior#1440379685665243137]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440385971815317667]] (Nost, disputed, 2025-11-18).
- **[HP targets]** Aim for ~6.5k HP buffed as a 4-Horsemen (4HM) tank minimum, more if on Patchwerk-style fights with high incoming damage; one player reports 8k+ HP / 9k armor on DefTac gear "well past" that — [[d:warrior#1440845928193069119]] (Kyrsie, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1440847149033197649]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-19).
- **[Tank roster sizing]** Naxx 4-Horsemen encounter needs ~4 dedicated "beefcake" tanks; some guilds run 7 relatively tanky people plus a rogue tank on 4H; guilds without enough dedicated tanks give priority on T3 tank tokens (taunt-resist 4pc) to warriors/druids and roll DPS into occasional tanking, requiring everyone to farm "reels" (resist gear) — [[d:warrior#1440843099168313426]], [[d:warrior#1440843797452820652]], [[d:warrior#1440844725400895508]], [[d:warrior#1440845064212578365]] (Dodging/Piemelbrie, 2025-11-19).
- **[DW tanking mitigation]** DW tanking is more threat-focused; if DW, generally shouldn't be focusing heavily on Defense stat ("if you're dual-wielding in the first place shouldn't really be focusing on defense") — [[d:warrior#1440824933918834799]] (Breaddy, 2025-11-20). Only one boss in the game (Twins in AQ40) is called out as worth specifically caring about your Defense stat for — [[d:warrior#1440825359313666188]] (Breaddy, 2025-11-20).
- **[Enrage mechanic]** FuryProt on many fights actually benefits from taking spiky damage occasionally (if healers can handle it) because the resulting enrage effect boosts threat significantly — [[d:warrior#1440830365366223081]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-20).
- **[3rd-tank role]** A "3rd tank" role in a raid is effectively "a DPS that sometimes tanks, not a tank that sometimes DPSes" per one guild member's framing — [[d:warrior#1440842772365050037]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-19).
- **[Tank mercing/tank-for-hire]** Tank mercenary service (charging gold to tank dungeons you don't need loot from) is explicitly permitted by Turtle WoW devs' rules, though socially controversial in this community — [[d:warrior#1440440673403801642]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1440435429320036362]] (Dodging, calls it "scummy," 2025-11-19).
- **[Anticipation/Parry talent value for Def Tactics]** Def Tactics tanks are advised to actively avoid taking Anticipation/extra Defense — less Defense means more incoming damage, which means more rage, which means more threat/damage; if you need more avoidance on a specific fight, just swap to Defensive Stance and weave Shield Slam/Shield Block instead of baking Defense into your talents — [[d:warrior#1442863484931932212]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-25), [[d:warrior#1442870763899326567]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-11-25). Exception: Improved Taunt/Parry-adjacent talent is "actually really good" for a handful of specific fights like Sartura or Golemagg, worth keeping "in the back pocket" for those pulls — [[d:warrior#1442838321968713749]] (Dodging, 2025-11-25).
- **[Thunderclap value at endgame]** Thunderclap is "not useful unless you have the [T2.5] set bonus, and even then it's not a game changer" for raid AoE threat; recommended usage split is Improved Thunderclap for dungeon leveling/tanking, switching to Improved Heroic Strike once raiding — [[d:warrior#1442842805587284040]] (Blackjack, 2025-11-25), [[d:warrior#1442842647596372061]] (Dodging, 2025-11-25).

- **[Hateforge Quarry]** Warrior tank at level 54 reports major AoE-threat difficulty vs Druid Swipe/Paladin Consecration/other classes' AoE — general answer: accept the limitation, mark targets, prioritize keeping aggro off healer, let big packs be off-tanked by better-AoE classes — [[d:warrior#1441907648734957650]], [[d:warrior#1441916755252478004]] (2025-11-23).

- **[Molten Core item reworks]** Turtle devs reworked several "dogshit" MC items recently (new prog items for paladins/shamans specifically mentioned); similar stat-shift updates expected in the BWL patch — [[d:warrior#1440543516194508831]], [[d:warrior#1440544297957982208]], [[d:warrior#1440544442518995016]] (Dodging, 2025-11-19).
- **[Scholomance]** Weapon-skill quest requires making Marduk (normally neutral) hostile in Scholo — can be done by attacking him/mobs directly but risks aggroing the whole room; historically possible to isolate him in a room corner, unclear if still possible — [[d:warrior#1440823562398208180]] (Dramafever, 2025-11-20), [[d:warrior#1440823863385653328]], [[d:warrior#1440824070840258610]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-20).
- **[BRD]** Orc 2h-spec warriors can rush BRD early (before being "at level") using high weapon skill (e.g. 318 WS as Orc with racial+item) to land hits reliably on higher-level BRD content for the Sleeper recipe (BoP, from a questline vendor outside Scholo) — [[d:warrior#1440459100982087751]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1440459604957073428]] (Dodging, 2025-11-19).
- **[World boss: Thaurissan/Ironfoe farm]** Multiboxing Ironfoe farm (hunter+warrior) without a healer deemed unlikely to work — Thaurissan has very high armor and hits hard during his enrage/"stoneform-enrage" tanky phase — [[d:warrior#1440451033187745974]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-19), [[d:warrior#1440451645430300793]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-19).

For melee, Coldrage Daggers are the premium option with an 8% frost-proc rate vs roughly 2% for most other frost-proc weapons; budget/alternate options: one Coldrage Dagger + Frostbound Slasher, or a weapon that grants extra hits (Ironfoe, Bonechopper) paired with a Frost Oil imbue; Bonechopper MH + Frostbound Slasher OH called a good budget combo (Bonechopper has no frost proc itself but its extra swings can trigger the oil) — [[d:warrior#1442342150854410291]], [[d:warrior#1442342260158234724]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-24), [[d:warrior#1442374413549965384]], [[d:warrior#1442374707293982810]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-24), [[d:warrior#1442385175668457474]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-24). Some argue it's not worth it for melee DPS overall on Viscidus outside organized guilds, since melee take heavy damage for comparatively little effective frost contribution vs. a mage's frostbolt — [[d:warrior#1442366315997429874]], [[d:warrior#1442366469198319647]], [[d:warrior#1442368938544726233]] (MoneyCow, 2025-11-24, disputed by Dodging who says his guild gets good value from cold-rage daggers regardless — [[d:warrior#1442368061507113111]]).

## PvP
- **[General state]** Warrior PvP broadly viewed as weak/homeless currently; neither Arms nor Fury is clearly "better," they "do different things" — Fury feels bad in PvP specifically — [[d:warrior#1441815858312642560]] (Dodging, 2025-11-22), [[d:warrior#1441069357634683031]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-11-19).
- **[Deep Prot PvP]** Deep Prot PvP damage is weak (occasional 2.5k Shield Slam crit on a clothie cited as a highlight, not the norm); considered better than Arms specifically "when you don't have two healers" (i.e., in unhealed comps its utility/tankiness has relative value) — [[d:warrior#1441560026035654879]], [[d:warrior#1441560349731061872]] (Kyrsie, +2, 2025-11-22). Prot's main PvP value is CC/body-blocking/disruption utility rather than damage — [[d:warrior#1441586046373724160]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-22).
- **[Master of Arms polearm bonus]** The polearm branch of Master of Arms only increases hitbox size (not attack range as players expect), widely mocked as poorly designed for PvP; suspected deliberately weak to avoid making the K40 spear (BiS 2H) overpowered when combined with polearm-specific talent bonuses — [[d:warrior#1441669351383105638]] (Dodging, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441669454953185310]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-21), [[d:warrior#1441679009199951932]] (Aubreykun's theory, disputed by Lipibidy/Blackjack as bad design regardless, 2025-11-22).
- **[Crit damage reduction / anti-crit gear]** A PvP set bonus that used to grant "reduced chance to be crit" (e.g. a belt buckle with 1% anti-crit) was changed/nerfed because players used PvP gear for PvE min-maxing; anti-crit itemization is generally viewed as a patchwork fix originally aimed at Paladins/Ferals — [[d:warrior#1440828469306200065]], [[d:warrior#1440828516902899713]], [[d:warrior#1440828545101205504]], [[d:warrior#1440828943149043763]] (Breaddy/Sylvaira, 2025-11-20).
- **[PvP weapon skill]** At low level, weapon skill differences (e.g. 170 vs 176) matter noticeably against PvP/leveling targets — see Leveling section.
- **[Weapon skill and PvP hit]** Weapon skill's glance-reduction benefit doesn't apply in PvP (glancing blows aren't a factor against players), so once at 315 weapon skill for PvE you can trade some Hit rating for other stats in a dedicated PvP set — [[d:warrior#1442161692598337691]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-24).
- **[Shield Slam / Concussion Blow PvP scaling]** Shield Slam scales 1:1 with Block Value and 0.15 with Attack Power — [[d:warrior#1444174153442918472]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-29). Concussion Blow currently does modest damage (~100 damage at ~1k AP, reported by one player as up to ~300) but generates rage and provides a 3-second stun on a (reported) ~20s cooldown, making it a "quite good" opener/disruption tool per some, while others call its damage output embarrassingly low for a nominal damage ability — disputed whether Concussion Blow is meant to be Prot's PvP damage tool at all, or purely CC/dispel-adjacent utility ("Shield Slam is Ghetto Purge") — [[d:warrior#1444171931220971623]] (Dodging, 2025-11-29), [[d:warrior#1444172550018961408]] (Zestitcha, historical note: 2004 vanilla Concussion Blow cost 15 rage with a 45s CD and no bonus damage, 2025-11-29), [[d:warrior#1444173257971335228]] (Thirtyseven, disputed, 2025-11-29), [[d:warrior#1444174162456346724]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-29).
- **[Deep Prot PvP viability, extended]** Deep Prot in PvP is widely agreed to lack a reliable damage/kill tool ("any rogue presses any buttons and ignores 100% of your armor and you die"); its function is framed as disruption (stuns, silences, dispelling buffs via Shield Slam) rather than kills, but participants note it "sucks at it due to dying so fast and being so kiteable" — [[d:warrior#1444173656606511154]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-29), [[d:warrior#1444173707957371103]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-29), [[d:warrior#1444173780279623884]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-29). Glyph of Deflection + a glyphed Shield Slam can hit surprisingly hard on unprepared squishies (example: a glyphed Shield Slam for ~2500 on a clothie) but this is opportunistic, not sustainable damage — [[d:warrior#1444173482630840472]] (Dodging, 2025-11-29), [[d:warrior#1444173536876036158]] (Kyrsie, 2025-11-29).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Slam swing-timer change]** Slam previously fully paused the swing timer; changed so it now only delays the swing if the swing is ready while still casting Slam, rather than pausing outright — [[d:warrior#1440178363162431619]] (somedeadguy, original/outdated claim, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440179762696949821]], [[d:warrior#1440179796335398952]] (Kyrsie, correction, 2025-11-18).
- **[Roids vs Giants macro stacking]** Confirmed via community testing that "Roids" and "Giants" (presumably CleveRoids vs a Giants-branded macro variant, or a similarly named buff) DO stack, contrary to initial assumption — [[d:warrior#1441590047827820694]] (DAB, +6, 2025-11-22), [[d:warrior#1441593338628407389]] (30, +1, 2025-11-22), [[d:warrior#1441618048883884122]] (DAB confirms after testing, 2025-11-22).
- **[Combat log example: extra attacks]** Real combat log snippet showing Windfury Totem extra attack + "Timeless Strike" extra attack both triggering off one swing, crits landing, Flametongue proc resisted — used to discuss whether a specific cloak effect (aura shared with Hand of Justice, no ICD) can proc off Windfury Totem — [[d:warrior#1440604257194872883]] (MoneyCow, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440604480352817212]] (MoneyCow, 2025-11-18), [[d:warrior#1440663702398439435]] (Dodging: "Cloak is the same aura as HoJ", 2025-11-18).
- **[Defensive Stance quest loss]** A player who abandoned the Defensive Stance quest by accident could not reacquire it from any trainer; workaround found — an NPC named Uzzek in the Barrens (near a tree by the bridge into Durotar, ~61.4, 21.1) still offered the quest despite it vanishing from trainers — [[d:warrior#1441154001142812794]] (Deleted User, 2025-11-20/21), [[d:warrior#1441163370496790528]] (Deleted User, +2, "<SOLVED>", 2025-11-21).
- **[Master of Arms polearm effect]** Bugged/poorly implemented — instead of increasing attack range as intended, it only increases the warrior's hitbox size, so melee range against a stationary target doesn't actually improve — [[d:warrior#1441669248639307848]] (coveredinbees, 2025-11-22), [[d:warrior#1441669351383105638]] (Dodging, confirms it's a hitbox change not a range change, 2025-11-22).

- **[Priest Power Word: Shield myth debunked]** Common TWoW myth that a warrior stops generating rage from hitting a Power-Word-Shielded target is false: a shielded player has always generated rage normally from their OWN hits; what doesn't generate rage is being hit while shielded (no damage taken = no rage from damage), which has always been the case — [[d:warrior#1442815465461121087]], [[d:warrior#1442815645862330472]] (30, 2025-11-24, "this is a common myth on twow and idk where it came from"). Compared to another dubious community workaround: removing Shadow Resistance gear before casting in MC to avoid a mind-control "heartbeat" reset bug on Razuvious's add, which some guilds still do based on a GM suggestion even though the real causes are usually range/tremor totem/misclicks — [[d:warrior#1442816049757163635]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-24), [[d:warrior#1442817604078010420]] (Gulolio, 2025-11-24), [[d:warrior#1442818902416691262]] (30, listing the real causes, 2025-11-24).
