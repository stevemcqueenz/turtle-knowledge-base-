## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- Execute rage cost lowered from 15 to 10: a net damage buff, since the freed 5 rage converts directly into extra Execute damage instead of being absorbed into the base cost — [[d:warrior#1467491606910472306]] (2026-01-31)
- Sweeping Strikes on Turtle no longer crits, and previously scaled its off-target hits off the main target's armor debuffs (more dmg vs. heavier-debuffed main targets); this was changed/"gimped" — disputed whether the old behavior was a real vanilla mechanic or an exploited quirk — [[d:warrior#1467378430092906634]] [[d:warrior#1467427650229371106]] [[d:warrior#1467523547063783670]] (2026-01-31; Donka: "ruined the cornerstone of arms warrior gameplay"; Zhafu: possibly just a quirk)
- Confirmed patch change: Cleave's rage cost was reduced — [[d:warrior#1467702960128790722]] (2026-02-01)
- A dynamic PvP damage-dampening system is planned for 1.18.1; some speculate (unconfirmed) warriors will get comparatively less dampening than other classes — [[d:warrior#1467428881597333524]] (single source, speculative, 2026-01-31)
- Conc Blow changed to grant rage — this made the "no-Conc-Blow chinaprot" build (enrage/enrage+DW prot skipping Conc Blow) "super questionable" since it no longer forfeits real value by skipping it — [[d:warrior#1466110801080356966]] [[d:warrior#1466111454200729891]] (2026-01-28)
- Shield Slam scaling/multiplier is being reworked/buffed next patch; projected to make Deathwish+Enrage prot openers (see Spec viability) roughly 4x normal Shield Slam threat — [[d:warrior#1465972940230365460]] [[d:warrior#1465973593899929732]] [[d:warrior#1465974297922113609]] (2026-01-27)
- Anomalus (Kara40) Arcane Resist wipe-check requirement was "GIGA lowered" by devs over time: originally ~225 AR minimum at release, now "at most 110" (raid target recommended ~240 buffed); see Dungeons & raids for full detail and the disputed RNG factor — [[d:warrior#1466626780340293682]] [[d:warrior#1466709085008433254]] (2026-01-29)

## Leveling
- Simple leveling path when unsure: play Fury — Cruelty, Improved Demoralizing Shout (use on every melee mob unless zero downtime), Improved Battle Shout; full-Arms alternative = Improved Rend, Parry, Tactical Mastery ("pretty locked into 2h") — [[d:warrior#1465491789824659659]] (Tole, +1, 2026-01-27)
- Leveling as Deep Prot tank (duo/HC-style): recommended over FuryProt while leveling, since FuryProt "requires good gear to work." Free Revenge (procs off Shield Slam's block buff) is decent while leveling; 3/3 Tactical [Mastery-line talent] good for leveling dungeon-tanking but falls off later. Stay in Defensive Stance, taunt + Revenge; skip Defensive Tactics until better geared; Impale has no value without decent crit. Suggested spread: 3/3 Tactical talent, 5/5 crit talent, rest into Prot tree — [[d:warrior#1465918528266834022]] [[d:warrior#1465918762124574893]] [[d:warrior#1465922876418035757]] [[d:warrior#1465923119633006684]] [[d:warrior#1465923719569608855]] (Lans, 2026-01-28)
- Survivability while leveling comes from gear — stack as much stamina as possible — [[d:warrior#1465926604671684831]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-28)
- Detailed leveling spec-order recommendation (Lipibidy): pure Prot leveling is slow until ~level 40 when Shield Slam unlocks (could arguably stay Arms until 45-50 anyway); Prot questing/tanking at early levels is rough. Go Arms early rather than Fury — easier to land hits at low crit chance, whereas Fury scales with gear you don't have yet while leveling. Once Blood Drinker unlocks (~level 44), Fury gets very strong sustain. Arms also gets Tactical Mastery early plus Improved Overpower, and "Overpower is your only weaponskill button for a long time" while leveling — [[d:warrior#1468413710107410647]] [[d:warrior#1468413796790960159]] [[d:warrior#1468413883755528326]] [[d:warrior#1468414054027497522]] [[d:warrior#1468414186953379942]] [[d:warrior#1468414245707186207]] [[d:warrior#1468414346446114920]] [[d:warrior#1468414511064285255]] (2026-02-04)
- 2H tanking is viable while leveling even though Prot warrior threat generation is weak at low level too — simply wearing full mail/plate gear provides enough mitigation to tank dungeons while leveling as Fury/Arms with a shield swapped in; reiterated general advice: "level as Fury" — [[d:warrior#1468415378303615282]] [[d:warrior#1468415475334779191]] [[d:warrior#1468420189229027460]] (Kyrsie, 2026-02-04)
- Leveling build recipe: Fury talents + 2H weapon to start, switch to Arms at level 40; ASAP run Razorfen Downs (RFD) for 2x Blood-etched Fetish trinkets + cheap vampirism weapon enchants — [[d:warrior#1466450703311569070]] (Must4che, 2026-01-29)

- 2H Fury leveling was fine even before the Flurry-affects-Slam changes ("2h fury through and through") — [[d:warrior#1466261393060663408]] (Billyscrote, 2026-01-29); DW Arms "Overpower fisher" build is decent for leveling until level 40 — [[d:warrior#1466273190283317259]] (Lipibidy, +1, 2026-01-29)

## Rotation: single target / AoE (misc)
- Sunder Armor is worth using on trash as a DPS filler specifically with the T3.5 set bonus equipped — [[d:warrior#1466768249340428373]] (Guts, 2026-01-29)

## Stats, caps, weights
- Hit cap scales with weapon skill: needed hit ≈ +8 at 300 weapon skill, decreasing by 1 hit needed per +5 weapon skill above 300; extra hit beyond that still helps white/auto attacks — [[d:warrior#1466216791234576467]] (Ascuz, 2026-01-29)
- DW hit nuance: the soft hit cap only prevents ability misses, not white-attack misses (white misses stay notably higher); DW's true "no white miss" threshold is much higher (+20 hit or more, exact figure disputed/uncertain among sources); extra hit is most valuable once already crit-capped — [[d:warrior#1466168272519954647]] (Ascuz, 2026-01-29)
- DW Fury hit (restated with numbers): soft hit cap for abilities is weapon-skill dependent (~5% hit at 315 sword skill); above that, hit still matters for the off-hand's white-attack miss chance, but crit/AP take priority — [[d:warrior#1467041416835239956]] [[d:warrior#1467041557839478906]] [[d:warrior#1467042894790787198]] (Captain Caboose/Jakeends, 2026-01-30)

- Itemization rule of thumb: raw AP/Str items usually beat pure-crit items even at a worse nominal ratio, e.g. "~92 AP + 2% crit beats 33 AP + ~3.7% crit most of the time"; crit is roughly worth 30-40 AP depending on current gear — [[d:warrior#1467045366561116303]] [[d:warrior#1467046048987090944]] [[d:warrior#1467046773691383980]] (30, 2026-01-30)

- Gloves enchant for 2H Fury/Arms is 9 Strength rather than 15 Agility (per pinned class BiS sheet): past a crit threshold, 1 Str > 1 Agi in value, but pre-Naxx crit is usually low so Agility can be better there; for Fury, crit value drops sharply after 34% crit (Flurry cap); for Arms, Agility stays good even above 34% crit — [[d:warrior#1467622383731867730]] (VeganCourage, 2026-02-02)

- Extra-attack-on-hit procs (e.g. Timelord's Cape) only reset the current swing timer to 0, so a "1%" proc chance is worth about half an extra attack on average, not a full extra attack — [[d:warrior#1466042637432786975]] (Virasan, 2026-01-28)

- Impale Prot can MT Kara40 with a 3.5-set + off-gear (Kiss of the Spider/Saph trinket) for pull openers, but its trash-phase performance is weak since you can't always set up and must go Defensive Stance to taunt — [[d:warrior#1466111594416308458]] (Zacka, 2026-01-28)
- Taunt has a 9s cooldown and roughly a 1/6 resist chance, and must be cast from Defensive Stance — [[d:warrior#1466112021677478022]] (Virasan, 2026-01-28)
- 2H Fury tanking (off-meta): Heroic Strike crits with a 2H weapon generate very high threat; used to tank Stormwind Vault (SWV); but the 2H swing timer is "completely fucked" for sustained raid tanking — [[d:warrior#1466110482757714113]] [[d:warrior#1466110635854135529]] (Virasan, 2026-01-29)
- In battlegrounds, weapon skill has NO effect on hit — it's purely a PvE melee-dps-adjacent stat — so tank gear carrying Defense skill can cause noticeably more misses in PvP than expected — [[d:warrior#1465822947771154716]] [[d:warrior#1465823121994027082]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-28)
- Def Tactics threat output is raid-dependent: "Def Tactics is decent if the threat output is fine for the raid you are in" — [[d:warrior#1466770553909022733]] (Velanir, 2026-01-29)
- Warrior tanks lack meaningful AoE threat as a class strength (see Spec viability); a suggested unimplemented dev fix is Thunderclap hitting +2-3 more targets with AP scaling — [[d:warrior#1467542930599182377]] [[d:warrior#1467543168424739092]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-31)
- Shield Block value is a small flat damage reduction, not a big mitigation source against large hits — "blocking does f-all except take some flat dmg off"; realistically only ~200-300 damage is blocked off a ~6k boss hit — [[d:warrior#1468315790397669506]] [[d:warrior#1468315916906532874]] (30/Virasan, 2026-02-03)
- Design critique: Def Tactics' only real threat lever is the Shield Slam→Revenge loop; if someone else pulls threat away, a Def Tactics warrior's threat generation "falls off a cliff" with no secondary threat button — a Conc Blow buff was argued to be more valuable than the Shield Slam buff for exactly this reason — [[d:warrior#1468800737302675539]] (L0s7, 2026-02-05)
- Defiance (threat talent) also strengthens plain Battle-stance ("furyprot-style") tanking, not just Defensive-stance play; the core complaint is that base Defensive-stance TPS is low, compounded when a Def Tactics tank has to react to someone else's pull rather than opening themselves — [[d:warrior#1468798768987242672]] (L0s7, 2026-02-05)
- Kara40 boss Sanv (Tas'dal's Rift Feedback add-phase mechanic): a player was one-shot for ~12,936 Arcane damage from Rift Feedback while 10 other raid members took 0 damage from the identical attack; explanation given: Berserker Rage strips the protective debuff Sanv applies that normally shields you from Rift Feedback, so using Berserker Rage during Sanv's add phase can remove that protection and get you one-shot — [[d:warrior#1468022689401929761]] [[d:warrior#1468023039093506110]] (JohnGood/Akanamu, 2026-02-03)
- Fire resistance for tanking Ragnaros (MC): aim for ~315 FR total (with buffs) for a "painless" experience; falling a bit short isn't fatal (occasional knockback, not the end of the world); gear alone can realistically reach ~200-240 FR unbuffed, with Dark Iron Helm + Dark Iron Leggings (FR-enchanted) doing most of the work, then a Fire Resistance buff (Paladin aura, Priest's Fire Resistance/Champion buff, or Shaman totem) plus a Fire Resistance Juju closes the gap to ~315 — [[d:warrior#1468912422654967889]] [[d:warrior#1468913491305037998]] [[d:warrior#1468913597638774898]] [[d:warrior#1468918989144068127]] [[d:warrior#1468919240948973715]] (Spiff/casey, 2026-02-05)

- FuryProt on Mephistroth (Kara40): needs ~440 Defense to reliably Dual-Wield-Deathwish-tank the fight, called "the most consistent" approach; Meph is specifically the one Kara40 boss that "hits decently hard" (elsewhere warriors can tank with little mitigation because content doesn't hit hard); FuryProt does not use Shield Block at all — [[d:warrior#1470040212594757734]] [[d:warrior#1470042091181576264]] [[d:warrior#1470036519740772477]] [[d:warrior#1470036443337588840]] [[d:warrior#1470036324219224106]] (Velanir, 2026-02-08)

- Design tension: if Deep Prot ever matched FuryProt's threat consistency, it would fully replace FuryProt on safety grounds — both specs can't be simultaneously "good" without one making the other pointless, given as the reason devs are cautious about threat-buffing Deep Prot — [[d:warrior#1470042743144189983]] [[d:warrior#1470042961604513915]] [[d:warrior#1470043182451523588]] (Lipibidy/Velanir, 2026-02-08)

- Deathwish Prot ("chinaprot") has the worst consistency of any warrior tank spec, yet sim-tested it shows the best average TPS/threshold-meeting chance — high variance, threat concentrated almost entirely into Shield Slam ("pump the shit out of it and hope nothing gets parried") — [[d:warrior#1470043502502088975]] [[d:warrior#1470043778009141290]] [[d:warrior#1470043842240843957]] (Velanir/Weirdthing/Lipibidy, 2026-02-08)

- Impale Prot big-pull opener: with full setup at pull, can generate "15k threat in 2-3 GCDs," after which — once past an early mechanic phase — threat becomes largely a non-issue and the tank can safely swap to Defensive Stance; risk: if Shield Slam gets parried/mitigated during the big pull, the strategy's threat lead collapses — [[d:warrior#1470040547514384414]] [[d:warrior#1470040699645722654]] [[d:warrior#1470040813294850048]] (Zacka/Velanir, 2026-02-08)

## PvP
- Dampening (core PvP mechanic): in battlegrounds, physical/magic damage and healing/absorbs are debuffed by a scaling percentage — used by devs as a lever to buff/nerf classes for PvP without touching PvE balance — [[d:warrior#1465498625084751984]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-27)
- Sustained multi-day consensus (many voices, many reactions): warrior PvP is the weakest in the game this patch. Cited causes: rage generation dampened on autoattacks, Sweeping Strikes lost/nerfed, Hamstring changes reverted/re-changed repeatedly, Charge and Intercept sharing a stun diminishing-returns category, Intimidating Shout breaking on Deep Wounds ticks, no armor-piercing tool vs. casters who don't pay a "hybrid tax" — [[d:warrior#1465651823804420099]] [[d:warrior#1465796531096518920]] [[d:warrior#1465700917813711109]] [[d:warrior#1465800191541252148]] [[d:warrior#1466545884174094614]] [[d:warrior#1467429117405429924]] (Lipibidy/Blackjack/Piemelbrie/Donka, 2026-01-27 through 02-01)
- In battlegrounds, weapon skill has NO effect on hit chance (it's a PvE melee-dps-adjacent stat only), so tank gear carrying Defense skill can cause noticeably more misses in PvP than expected — [[d:warrior#1465822947771154716]] [[d:warrior#1465823121994027082]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-28)
- Turtle-specific: a mace's on-hit stun effect stops the target's autoattack, similar to a Gouge — [[d:warrior#1465474578569756876]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-27)
- Confirmed: Hamstring's cooldown was removed again (long-requested); Improved Hamstring hasn't been correspondingly adjusted — [[d:warrior#1468790433181208636]] (Kyrsie, 2026-02-05)
- Armor's physical damage reduction reportedly has no hard cap, with diminishing returns starting only after ~14,000 armor — a threshold rarely reached in BGs without full consumables; exact relationship to mob/player level unclear — [[d:warrior#1467429229988937974]] [[d:warrior#1467429678628474881]] [[d:warrior#1467430098041835661]] (Donka/Zhafu, 2026-01-31)
- A dynamic PvP damage-dampening system is planned for 1.18.1; some speculate (unconfirmed) warriors will get comparatively less dampening than other classes in it — [[d:warrior#1467428881597333524]] (Zhafu, single source, 2026-01-31)
- Consolidated tank-PvP wishlist (repeated verbatim by multiple people, signaling broad agreement): Shield Wall is still a 30-minute cooldown and shares its cooldown with Recklessness/Retaliation; tank warriors have 4 PvP talents that go largely unused; Revenge and Thunderclap are considered "dead buttons" in practice — [[d:warrior#1470055597754945557]] [[d:warrior#1470056347763609855]] [[d:warrior#1470056408245604415]] (Lans/casey/Kyrsie, 2026-02-08) — disputed in part: Revenge isn't truly dead, "worth pressing in defensive" [[d:warrior#1470056507700674663]] (Virasan)
- Contested self-perception vs. outside perception of warrior mobility: warriors themselves report struggling to stick to fleeing targets even with Medallion + Improved Berserker Rage, while other classes perceive Hamstring's cooldown removal as making warriors "too sticky" ("perma slow") — [[d:warrior#1469791452409368661]] [[d:warrior#1469791814579126499]] [[d:warrior#1469792933417976111]] [[d:warrior#1469793304877994160]] (casey/Kyrsie/Setto, 2026-02-07/08)

## Bugs & quirks
- Deep Wounds appears to refresh Flurry stacks: on a dummy, an auto crit gives 3 Flurry stacks + applies Deep Wounds; the next (non-crit) auto spends a stack; the Deep Wounds tick then refills Flurry back to 3, yielding 3-4 non-crit autos off a single Flurry proc instead of the expected 3 — flagged as possibly unintended — [[d:warrior#1466338615163355269]] (Tabea <Black Rose>, 2026-01-29)
- Charge/Intercept can be cast with no cooldown via `/cast Intercept` then `/script SpellStopCasting()` — works for any Charge-type ability (plain Charge only fails standalone because it requires being out of combat); flagged as likely to get hotfixed/punished if abused — [[d:warrior#1466325945110757511]] [[d:warrior#1466326162195349577]] (Panthers, 2026-01-29)
- Weapon skill 315→316 "crit cliff": at exactly 315 weapon skill you hold the maximum crit bonus (~+0.6%), but going one point over to 316 removes that entire crit bonus outright rather than continuing to scale — distinct from an older, separately-fixed "crit suppression" bug that used to grant bonus crit exactly at 316 — [[d:warrior#1468346768025452728]] [[d:warrior#1468347062784622699]] [[d:warrior#1468347064772727065]] [[d:warrior#1468347457527353404]] (30/Juliahnna, 2026-02-04)
- Trinket/weapon/shield swapping is blocked while in combat, and swapping right before entering combat frequently still fails to preserve the "use" buff — an "extreme nerf" against pre-popping on-use trinkets, seen as disproportionately hurting classes without a vanish/stealth-swap option — [[d:warrior#1468288061048754379]] [[d:warrior#1468288479271063623]] [[d:warrior#1468288777532477622]] (RuitX, 2026-02-03)
- Sweeping Strikes on Turtle no longer crits, and used to scale its off-target hits off the main target's armor debuffs — changed/nerfed; disputed whether the pre-change behavior was an intended vanilla mechanic or an exploited quirk — [[d:warrior#1467378430092906634]] [[d:warrior#1467523547063783670]] (Zhafu/Piemelbrie, 2026-01-31)
- Self-healing flask/trinket effects were nerfed on Turtle (healing scaling reduced to "0.1") because there is no combat-log damage snapshot to abuse — [[d:warrior#1467873957469356241]] [[d:warrior#1467874038419558421]] (Kyrsie, 2026-02-01)
- Precise figure for the Diamond Flask nerf: healing scaling is 0.1 on Turtle vs. 1.0 in classic (i.e. ~1/10th effective self-healing) — [[d:warrior#1470154082437173411]] [[d:warrior#1470125974346666103]] (Kyrsie/nobus, 2026-02-09)
- Thunderclap Mechanic finding: it is coded as a physical-damage SPELL rather than a melee ability, so it needs spell hit/spell crit to land or crit (largely never crits for warriors), doesn't scale with any stat (flat base damage only), can't benefit from Death Wish/Enrage, and is still mitigated by target armor — this fully explains why it underperforms as an AoE tool — [[d:warrior#1469785382722732073]] [[d:warrior#1469793882718998729]] [[d:warrior#1469793885281587298]] [[d:warrior#1469793787021885555]] (Lipibidy/casey/Kyrsie/Blackjack, 2026-02-07/08)
- Concrete damage test: Thunderclap with talents invested plus T2.5 set equipped, tested against a zero-armor training dummy, dealt only 243 total damage — confirms the ability is weak even under best-case (no-mitigation) conditions — [[d:warrior#1470412400963489814]] [[d:warrior#1470431018786881568]] [[d:warrior#1470413515066970205]] (Kyrsie/Agge the Egg/Lans, 2026-02-09)
- Thunderclap origin/mechanic note: reportedly it was originally meant to also apply a movement-speed slow (like the Warcraft 3 spell) in addition to the attack-speed slow; NPC/mob-cast Thunderclap still carries the movement slow, but the player version does not — [[d:warrior#1470441332206997707]] (goreblaster, 2026-02-09)
