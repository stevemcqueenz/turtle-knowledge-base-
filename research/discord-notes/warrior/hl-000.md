## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
(Chunk predates 1.18.1's preliminary notes (2025-10-03); the material below documents the "Class Update 2" (CC2, patch ~1.17.2, Nov 2024) warrior rework and subsequent hotfixes that shaped the class going into 1.18.1. Treat as historical/era context, not confirmed 1.18.1 content.)
- **[tank/upcoming]** Latest and most concrete forward-looking signal in this chunk (2025-06-08, closest to 1.18.1's preliminary-notes window): a poster who has previously relayed accurate dev/changelog information states plainly that the *new* Concussion Blow "generates rage instead of costing rage" — a fundamental rework of the ability from a rage-cost finisher to a rage-generator, addressing the years of complaints in this chunk about Concussion Blow's rage cost/threat/cooldown being poor value for a tank capstone — [[d:warrior#1391922713123360898]] (Lhian, +9 reactions; not yet confirmed by an official patch note in-chunk).
- **[confirmed/1.18]** The single most concrete, dated confirmation of a 1.18 warrior change in this entire chunk (posted 2025-08-02, the chunk's last day): "you can opt to spam Execute in 1.18 as Furyprot, regardless of which talent build you go with. This is thanks to Execute returning to the GCD like before, and the talent being reverted to its previous version" — i.e. the Execute-on-a-cooldown change from CC2 (see above) is being reverted in patch 1.18, restoring Execute to a plain-GCD ability; the same poster cautions this is a pure-damage play (not a threat play), and should only be used by a tank confident in their threat lead and healer support — [[d:warrior#1400953009688809522]] (Marinatall Ironside).
- **[confirmed dev quote]** A directly-quoted TWoW developer comment (relayed in-channel, exact wording preserved): *"When we introduced a cooldown on Execute in Class Changes 2, our goal was to further distinguish Fury and Arms execute phases. However, the change fell flat in practice and forced most Arms players to spec into the Fury tree simply to avoid the restriction."* — this is the dev-side rationale for the above 1.18 Execute-cooldown reversal — [[d:warrior#1398137181406498906]] (camilongus, relaying dev comment).
- **[tank/definitive]** The most authoritative, worked-through resolution in this chunk to the long-running "does Defensive Tactics actually do anything" confusion (posted 2025-08-01, pinned, +8): with baseline threat multipliers Defensive Stance = 1.3 and Battle/Berserker Stance = 0.8, and 5/5 Defiance = ×1.2: Defensive Stance with Defiance = 1.3×1.2 = 1.56. Defensive Tactics' own multiplier (not the final value) is `(baseline − 1) × 1.8 + 1`, giving 1.54 without Defiance or 2.008 when applied to the already-Defiance-boosted Defensive-Stance baseline (1.56). The final Battle/Berserker-Stance multiplier is then baseline(0.8) × that Defensive-Tactics multiplier: 1.232 without Defiance, or 1.6064 with 5/5 Defiance. Conclusion: Defiance's bonus *does* apply even while tanking in Battle/Berserker Stance via Defensive Tactics, and the two talents stack — Battle/Berserker + Defiance + Defensive Tactics (1.6064) modestly out-threats Defensive Stance + Defiance alone (1.56) — [[d:warrior#1400823950228848670]] (Velanir, pinned, +8 reactions).
- **[stats/tank]** A real threat-value breakdown pulled from a 34-second Maexxna parse (one of very few concrete, sourced threat numbers in this entire chunk): Heroic Strike ≈25,106 threat, Auto-attacks ≈10,499, Sunder Armor (across applications) ≈5,850, Bloodthirst ≈4,482, Thunderfury proc damage ≈2,624, Thunderfury's own bonus threat ≈2,449 — totaling roughly 51,000 threat, matching the poster's recorded VOD — [[d:warrior#1398386096206905435]] (30). This directly answers (for this one DW/Fury-Prot-style sample) the open question elsewhere in this chunk about which abilities actually carry a tank's threat: Heroic Strike dominates by a wide margin over Sunder Armor.
- **[general]** Precise Execute-nerf numbers from the CC2-era rework: Execute damage at 100 rage went from 2,640 pre-nerf to 2,220 post-nerf, and the rage-return portion of Execute was also cut by ~30%, compounding to roughly a 50% total damage nerf on the ability; a full raid-log breakdown from a Kara40 kill showed Execute's contribution dropping from 825,000 to 412,500 (that fight's -50%), partially offset by Slam/Mortal Strike gains from their own reworks (1,014,000 → 1,111,777, +97,777), for a net loss of 314,723 damage — about a 7.8% total damage reduction for that specific parse — [[d:warrior#1398220110761693225]] (Guts).
- **[general]** CC2 warrior rework summary: Rend damage increased, Hamstring given a 6s cooldown (nerf to PvP stickiness), Mortal Strike kept at 120% weapon damage (now framed more as anti-heal than damage), Sweeping Strikes "bug-fixed" back to 1.10-style multi-application from Whirlwind, Improved Slam reduces Slam cast time by up to 0.5s and extends Rend duration by 1.5/3/4.5/6/7.5s (also affects Decisive Strike), Boundless Anger grants 3/6/10 rage when slowed/snared — [[d:warrior#1302062631661731842]] (Drunken Robot, 2024-11-01; +16 reactions).
- **[fury]** Bloodthirst was changed to also deal self-damage (a % of damage dealt) as the enrage-proc mechanic; widely called "overtuned" at launch. Bug-fix changelog: Blood Craze now correctly procs off Bloodthirst/Bloodrage self-damage; Improved Bloodrage no longer increases Bloodrage self-damage; Blood Fury also increases ranged AP; Bloodthirst self-damage reduced to 10% of damage dealt to the enemy, unaffected by armor/modifiers — [[d:warrior#1302506545980112917]] (Punn, 2024-11-08; +5), confirmed dev-side by Turt Reynolds asking players for self-harm damage data to retune the "Half" self-damage value down (potentially to 10% or lower, and to stop applying the target's-armor-style mitigation) — [[d:warrior#1302331370923888640]] (dev, +8).
- **[fury]** By late Dec 2024, self-damage was said to be reverted closer to pre-CC2 levels ("BT is back to pre-cc2") but Fury lost the ability to proc Enrage off itself without a second proc source, seen as a net nerf to fury sustain — [[d:warrior#1320003986069258251]] (beastie, single source).
- **[general]** Execute was reworked: buffed base rage-scaling initially, then contested/adjusted. Two conflicting official-looking value posts: Rank1(lvl24) 125dmg+0.3/rage ... Rank5(lvl56) 600dmg+1.5/rage — [[d:warrior#1320196134517346376]] (30) — vs. a later correction Rank1 125+3/rage ... Rank5 600+15/rage — [[d:warrior#1320219590218874963]] (30); community read this as a ~25-30% Execute damage nerf plus removal of the auto+DS+execute-in-one-GCD combo from precision-cutting talent, seen as a "double nerf" — [[d:warrior#1319869085646983253]] (Sylvaira), [[d:warrior#1319927401978920993]] (Golli, disputed as too harsh by others).
- **[stats]** Weapon skill / hit-cap overhaul: fighting mobs 3 levels above you no longer applies the old 1% hit suppression, so the effective hit cap vs. such targets moved from 9% to 8%; the old "soft cap" of 305 skill (which used to offset that suppression) no longer exists; each point of skill from 300→304 and 305→308 now gives a smooth, equal 0.2% hit chance + 2% reduced glancing-damage penalty per point; weapon skill granted by racials was reduced from +5 to +3 — [[d:warrior#1321641077215723520]] (30).
- **[stats]** Concussion Blow stun duration cut from 5s to 2s in a hotfix; since it shares diminishing returns with Charge's stun, community noted the effective duration "was always 2s anyway" in practice — [[d:warrior#1319539576347426858]] (Tyler).
- **[tank]** Defensive Tactics (prot talent, 180% def-stance threat bonus while in Battle/Zerker stance) buffed; community math shows the buff is small in absolute terms (~6% threat-equivalent, or ~12.4% higher damage-based threat non-def-stance vs. def-stance with talent, "a wash" on base threat) — [[d:warrior#1319532809093779538]] (desespere), detailed calc + bug-report link https://turtle-wow.org/bug-report?id=9455 — [[d:warrior#1321476363470901278]] (chucktar).
- **[tank]** Improved Shield Slam threat buff (intended since ~Oct 22) was "forgotten and ignored for months," only implemented alongside a Revenge nerf and the Defensive Tactics buff — [[d:warrior#1319533706586619955]] (Senpuu, disputed/critical).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[dps]** Fury generally does more raw parse DPS than Arms even for equally-skilled players, but many players still prefer Arms for feel and Execute-phase damage; top players (e.g. "Arki") perform as well or better on Fury than Arms despite disliking it — [[d:warrior#1315728627953827861]] (Nyph, single source).
- **[tank]** "Fury Prot" (Fury talents used for tanking via Enrage uptime) is repeatedly said to remain the practical raid-tank meta over "Deep Prot," because Deep Prot's Defensive Tactics rotation is extremely stance-dance-heavy and still generates less threat than Paladin/Druid tanks for far more effort — [[d:warrior#1306277786318540883]], [[d:warrior#1308808106020438087]] (desespere), corroborated — [[d:warrior#1306871814303125545]] (oldmandan), [[d:warrior#1310285053498097715]] (PrestigeV, considering rerolling over it).
- **[tank]** Contested history: some argue Deep Prot was Blizzard/Turtle's originally *intended* tank spec, and Fury Prot only emerged from gear/world-buff min-maxing; others say Fury/Fury-Prot are "iconic" and shouldn't have been touched — [[d:warrior#1314750049510490165]] (desespere) vs. [[d:warrior#1314755814711365652]] (crushnasty).
- **[general]** A N T I's own working tier read post-CC2: for DPS, Fury > Arms; for tank, Deep Prot > Fury Prot only if you're the assigned main tank; warrior overall very gear-dependent — [[d:warrior#1313502648397332500]] (single source, framed as a question, not confirmed by others in-chunk).
- **[general]** Very late-chunk (end of July 2025) strong negative summary opinion: Warrior is called "a complete letdown" on TWoW — the only spec change (Arms) is "nearly unplayable" in solo PvP due to the Hamstring change plus other classes' buffs, the DPS tier-set design from T2 onward is "toddler design" (Spirit on gear, weak tier bonuses, few stat gains), and Prot Warriors compared to other tank classes are "a meme" — calling Warrior "the least loved class by TWoW devs" — [[d:warrior#1398829222469832744]] (Piemelbrie, single strong opinion, contested by many other more positive posts throughout this chunk).
- **[general]** Countervailing hype for upcoming changes at the very end of the chunk: "taking steps towards increasing overall mobility while not nerfing dmg at all" for Fury, plus "slight buff coming from imp[roved] execute" — [[d:warrior#1398824249300357283]] (Overman, anticipatory/unconfirmed).
- **[general]** Community sentiment (heavily disputed/meme'd): large faction says CC2 gutted warrior's "feel" even where raw numbers were fine, citing hamstring CD, execute changes, and forced Enrage self-harm RNG as core complaints — [[d:warrior#1302295176638627901]] (Peggyo, +3), [[d:warrior#1302294614191116348]] (Mumm-Ra, +10); opposing faction says warrior remains extremely strong/OP overall and complaints are overblown — [[d:warrior#1302283269223612437]] (Mareth, +94), [[d:warrior#1309561977906921502]] (Frumie, +6).

## Leveling
- **[leveling]** Pinned, explicitly "UPDATED FOR 1.18," Arms leveling talent order designed to require *no respecs* the whole way to 60 (useful for players who don't want to spend gold respeccing): Cruelty 5/5 → Improved Rend 2/2 → Tactical Mastery 3/5 → Improved Thunderclap 1/3 → Improved Charge 2/2 → Tactical Mastery 5/5 → Improved Overpower 2/2 → Deep Wounds 3/3 → Two-Handed Weapon Specialization 3/3 → Impale 2/2 → Sweeping Strikes 1/1 → Mortal Strike-tree filler ("Master Strike") 1/1 → Master of Arms 5/5 → Improved Slam 2/2 → Improved Thunderclap 2/3 → Mortal Strike 1/1 → Unbridled Wrath 5/5 → Improved Shouts 5/5 → Enrage 5/5. Author's caveat: respeccing at 30 for earlier Sweeping Strikes or at 40 for earlier Mortal Strike is probably stronger play, but this order is deliberately respec-free; Thunderclap is weak in a level-60 raid setting but still useful AoE/utility while leveling or sub-60 dungeon tanking as Arms — [[d:warrior#1364931945624240191]] (Virasan, pinned, +20 reactions).
- **[leveling]** Companion Dual Wield Fury leveling talent order (explicitly adapted from a raid DW Fury build, author's own caveat that they haven't personally leveled DW Fury and it may not be leveling-optimized), also designed around not respeccing: Cruelty 5/5 → Dual Wield Specialization 5/5 → Improved Rend 2/2 → Tactical Mastery 5/5 → Improved Heroic Strike 3/3 → Improved Charge 2/2 → Deep Wounds 3/3 → Impale 2/2 → Improved Shouts 5/5 → Blood Craze 3/3 → Enrage 2/5 → Death Wish 1/1 → Enrage 5/5 → Reckless Execute 1/2 → Flurry 5/5 → Bloodthirst 1/1 → Reckless Execute 2/2 → Improved Whirlwind 2/3. Rationale given: dip into Arms early for Improved Charge since it's useful leveling utility, and assume lower Enrage-proc-rate/Flurry-uptime at mid levels due to lower crit chance then — [[d:warrior#1369250065914269716]] (Virasan).
- **[leveling]** Full 1-60 leveling/respec plan (Arms/Fury hybrid): 1-29: 2/2 Rend, 3/5 Tactical Mastery, 3/3 Improved Thunderclap (dungeon tanking), 5/5 Cruelty, 2/2 Improved Charge, 2/2 Improved Overpower, 3/3 Deep Wounds. 30-39: respec — move Cruelty into 3/3 Two-Handed Weapon Specialization, 3/5→5/5 Tactical Mastery, 1/1 Sweeping Strikes, re-add 5/5 Cruelty, 4/5 Master of Arms. 40-60: respec — move Cruelty into 4/5→5/5 Master of Arms, 2/5 Deflection, 2/2 Impale (axes/swords), 1/1 Mortal Strike, re-add 5/5 Cruelty, 5/5 Unbridled Wrath or Booming Voice (preference), 1/1 Piercing Howl, 1/5 Booming Voice, 3/3 Blood Craze, 5/5 Enrage — [[d:warrior#1301894970411581510]] (Drunken Robot).
- **[leveling]** Alternative philosophy: level as Cruelty/Arms focusing on debuffs (Rend, Demoralizing Shout, Sunder to 30%), auto-attack to kill, pool rage for next mob, only Execute if about to have downtime/travel; "everything pretty much works" if you're half-decent, but min-maxed high-kill-speed specs need more gear investment — [[d:warrior#1301904177722818580]] (Tole).
- **[leveling]** General rule of thumb regardless of spec: take 5/5 Cruelty (crit%) and 3/5+ Tactical Mastery (avoid losing rage on stance swap) while leveling; Warrior has a low floor (rough while leveling solo) and high ceiling (great in groups/with gear); Arms is "classic," Fury is "totally fine," Prot becomes decent for leveling from around level 40 — [[d:warrior#1313552388640083968]] (Frumie).
- **[leveling/tank]** Leveling as full Prot from the start is viable: order is Rend → Tactical Mastery → Improved Thunderclap → (5/5) Cruelty → finish Tactical Mastery → Overpower → Deep Wounds, then move into prot talents; sword-and-board is fine early since weapon type barely matters until Shield Slam; rend+thunderclap on packs, sunder single target, stance-swap for Overpower/Pummel; some argue for full respec to prot once Shield Slam is available, but it's not required — [[d:warrior#1313599097277644920]] (Drekalo).
- **[leveling]** Leveling as Prot is otherwise considered "a bit shit" even post-buffs, and early prot talents don't synergize with early-level abilities the way Arms/Fury do; you get better overall leveling results just running a normal (Arms/Fury) build even if your end goal is tanking — [[d:warrior#1309517743174848603]] (Tole).
- **[leveling/tank]** Improved Thunderclap recommended for tanky leveling ("don't sleep on" it) — [[d:warrior#1304851905658486874]] (cryft).
- **[leveling]** Tactical Mastery lets you Charge and immediately swap to Defensive Stance without losing all rage; use a ranged weapon + LOS pulls to bring ranged mobs in; carry both a 2H (threat) and 1H+shield (when taking too much damage) hotkeyed — [[d:warrior#1315394683228127323]] (Frumie).

- **[leveling]** Historical leveling-meta shift: before the Hamstring cooldown was added, the leveling "meta" was Arms Hamstring-and-kite — swinging a slow (~3.5s) 2H weapon while kiting mobs that hit faster than you, taking much less damage than you dealt; post-Hamstring-CD (frequent misses on the snare now matter), this is far less reliable, so DW and 2H are now considered roughly equal for leveling since ~80% of damage is still plain auto-attack regardless — [[d:warrior#1372662911058841765]] (Nabuhodonozo).
- **[leveling/HC]** A different account disputes that DW is slower to level than 2H: kill speeds felt "pretty much the same" leveling both specs 1-60 with a variety of weapons; Troll+Fury gets noticeably more healing back (from Bloodthirst's self-heal-adjacent mechanics) than a generic %-health-on-hit approach — [[d:warrior#1352394214218137775]] (Campsie/Lanik). A Fury-tank-while-leveling account similarly reports faster/more consistent rage generation gives more room for group-play mistakes (always ~2s from having rage to Sunder), and disputes that DW "misses a lot" — [[d:warrior#1362778491203883048]] (peter_hammer).

## Rotation: single target
- **[arms]** Clean Arms single-target/cleave priority (widely stated, June 2025): Sunder Armor as the first cast on every new mob → Mortal Strike on cooldown → Whirlwind on cooldown → Slam between autos (only if MS/WW won't come off cooldown within the next 1.5s) → Sweeping Strikes above everything else if it'll be used on 2+ targets; Sweeping Strikes can be pre-popped before the pull lands (lasts 20s, but its cooldown only starts on cast, so with good timing you can sometimes get 2 uses per trash pack) — [[d:warrior#1382408445671772303]] (Angrycat/Powershift).
- **[rotation]** Niche filler-priority tech: Pummel is worth pressing even with nothing to interrupt — as a rage-neutral, potentially-critting filler when other instants are on cooldown (and crits can proc on-hit weapon effects like Thunderfury/Tempest's Rage), and specifically because Pummel's damage can crit and trigger the T3 4-piece's Rend-duration-extension (10% chance per crit) — useful when precasting Bloodrage to land a big Rend and then chaining crit-fishing filler to extend it before it falls off — [[d:warrior#1385202602345631804]] (CheddarBob).
- **[general]** Concise combined single-target/AoE rotation description: Battle Stance — Charge, then Sweeping Strikes if Arms — swap to Berserker Stance: Mortal Strike > Whirlwind > Slam > Heroic Strike, weaving in Charge/Sweeping Strikes/Overpower opportunistically if it won't waste rage and the proc is up; the same priority holds for any number of targets except that at 2+ targets the Berserker-Stance priority becomes Whirlwind > Cleave > any filler that won't delay the next Whirlwind/Cleave — [[d:warrior#1372053070426538034]] (victoria).
- **[fury/DW]** DW Fury raid opener/rotation: with Thunderfury MH + a fast weapon OH and the 8pc T3-style bonus (420 AP-equivalent per weapon-speed on hits), stay in Battle Stance to spam Overpower and Rend: Overpower → Bloodthirst → Rend priority, Heroic Strike as filler; pop Bloodrage, Mongoose-proc-reliant trinkets/consumes, and Reck(lessness) together; wants raid buffs Atiesh (feral horns variant), 5%+2% haste/4% crit buffs, Enhancement Bloodlust, Trueshot Aura, and Chastise — [[d:warrior#1331283762755010673]] (Zulakzil).
- **[general]** Community rotation quick-tips for new players: Heroic Strike/Cleave are minor damage *additions* to white swings, not big hits — only use them once rage is ~70%+ so you don't cap rage; Sunder Armor is a big damage increase and should be pressed; Overpower should be used on cooldown; Rend is worth using on this server; Hamstring humanoid mobs so they can't flee; Bloodrage helps preserve built-up rage between pulls by extending your in-combat timer — [[d:warrior#1341072142376239106]] (Catmaid).
- **[arms/2H]** Debate over whether weaving Rend/Overpower is a DPS gain in 2H Arms/Fury: one poster argues Rend (94 dmg/3s unbuffed at 1166 AP, ~1900 dmg/min if the T3 4pc keeps it refreshed) and Overpower cost 1-2 GCDs that could otherwise go to Heroic Strike, and since Rend's T3 4pc refresh is RNG-gated (10% per crit) it's rarely perfectly maintained — net conclusion is it's "probably equivalent to just not using it and using Heroic Strike," not a clear win — [[d:warrior#1326561028917690379]], [[d:warrior#1326562887019401226]], [[d:warrior#1326565639699300382]] (beastie; disputed by another player in the same thread, their counter-numbers not captured in this chunk).
- **[general]** Historical execute-phase note: before the CC2 rework, Dual Wield out-performed 2H in execute phase because DW could fit an Execute every GCD via fast off-hand swings, and the old execute formula favored high-rage-per-hit at *low* rage totals; the CC2 rework gives 2H Arms a rage refund on Execute plus increased damage, closing/flipping that gap — [[d:warrior#1331630602834808933]] (Sebastian_, single source/opinion).
- **[fury]** Execute-optimization math example: at 120 rage, Execute gives back 36 rage; players discussed chaining Slam+Execute vs. just spamming Execute on cooldown as "better on all accounts as an instant, non-normalized ability with high DPR" rather than over-optimizing the refund loop — [[d:warrior#1320547459075674225]] (beastie).
- **[arms]** Weapon-type math for Arms/2H: axe (121.5 avg swing) vs. mace (127 avg swing) — instant attacks (Mortal Strike, Sweeping Strikes, Whirlwind) deal a flat +6.5 dmg each (+13 on crit) regardless of weapon, with axe crit chance ~15% vs. mace ~10% from Axe/Mace specialization talents; you need roughly a 6.15% weapon-damage edge to make up for losing that flat/crit-scaling advantage when switching from axe to a higher-base-damage mace — [[d:warrior#1308854890478571623]] (Drunken Robot).
- **[arms]** Slam mechanics, definitively clarified (post-CC2 rework): base cast time 2.5s, Improved Slam (a late Arms talent) reduces it by up to 0.5s (fastest talented = 2.0s); damage formula changed from the old "100% weapon damage + flat bonus" to up to 90% weapon damage at max talent rank; the swing timer is no longer reset (old Vanilla behavior) nor fully paused (a post-Vanilla behavior) — it keeps ticking during the cast, but casting Slam right after your last swing still nets you roughly one GCD's worth of time for another ability before your next auto lands — [[d:warrior#1359216661105872967]] (Forlorn Shade); an earlier, consistent report: Slam itself doesn't pause the timer, but if cast ~0.1s before an auto attack would land, that auto is delayed until the Slam cast finishes (so timing right after a swing avoids delaying the next auto) — [[d:warrior#1346848121183014998]] (Bunker).
- **[arms]** Slam weaving: with Improved Slam, a 2s cast + the 1.5s GCD ≈ 3.5s, which is close to an average 2H swing timer, so a skilled 2H Arms player can weave in roughly one extra ability per Slam cast — [[d:warrior#1358836265218347099]] (Forlorn Shade).
- **[tank]** Charge has an under-documented restriction: proximity to other in-combat units (friendly or enemy) can silently block a Charge attempt even at range, because being close to combat can add you to a threat table and disqualify the charge — worth knowing before blaming Improved Charge/talent choice for "charge doesn't work" issues — [[d:warrior#1358906543919202536]] (Forlorn Shade).
- **[stats]** Rage-sustainability note: with only ~30% buffed crit, a "full Arms" rotation spending ~40-45 rage every 3 seconds is hard to sustain; consider a hybrid pacing of ~15 rage per 1.5s GCD with Heroic Strike only above a rage threshold if crit/rage generation is lower than ideal — [[d:warrior#1356279630688026815]] (Esi).
- **[arms]** Slam's melee-range mechanic used for kiting: after the cast starts (must begin in melee range), Slam can still land on a target that has since moved to roughly ~8.8 yards away, making it usable as a kiting tool or a last-hit "anti-kite" option if you're rooted/perma-snared — [[d:warrior#1363250262499786782]] (Forlorn Shade).
- **[dps]** 2H Fury vs. Arms comparison (post-Slam-rework): you can now Slam between swings even with Flurry procced, without clipping the swing timer on a slow weapon — called a "huge buff" to 2H Fury. Key differences: (1) Mortal Strike hits slightly harder than Bloodthirst; (2) Improved Slam makes double-Slamming, or MS/Whirlwind-into-Slam-and-clipped-auto, worthwhile specifically for Arms; (3) Fury leans on Death Wish + Flurry uptime while Arms leans on Sweeping Strikes + Improved Execute — [[d:warrior#1362872758144340139]] (bhop).
- **[dps]** In early-tier gear (T1), 2H Fury is reported to outperform Arms single-target outside execute phase because of Flurry uptime; this may flip once rage generation is high enough to always weave a rage-spender into every Slam gap (Slam/Slam, MS/Slam, or WW/Slam), since Bloodthirst doesn't out-damage Slam until higher AP; 2H Fury is specifically praised for early-gear dungeon tanking with an Earthshaker-enchanted weapon, since Sunder + Flurry attack-speed stacking knocks mobs down a lot — [[d:warrior#1365157106675617823]], [[d:warrior#1365890599013711893]] (bhop).
- **[tank]** Tanking rotation feel differs by spec while gearing up: Arms tanks stance-dance Defensive→Battle (for Sweeping Strikes)→Berserker (for Whirlwind) right at pull-start for burst damage/snap threat; Fury (Fury Prot) tanks instead pool rage in Defensive Stance during the previous pull and spend it on Thunderclap/Cleave/Sunder until ~25 rage, then stance-dance once for Whirlwind — described as feeling "more chill" than the Arms pattern — [[d:warrior#1365157106675617823]] (bhop).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[arms/fury]** Sweeping Strikes was "bug-fixed" during CC2 to revert to 1.10-style behavior, applying its cleave to multiple Whirlwind hits again rather than one; combined with Charge, this makes charging into 3+ clustered mobs very high pressure ("Blender Build") — [[d:warrior#1302062631661731842]], [[d:warrior#1303710319356547142]] (Drunken Robot).
- **[general]** Battle Shout vs. Thunderclap for AoE threat, worked example: BS Rank 7 (70 threat/person) hitting a group of 5 = (70×1.3×1.15×5)/4 ≈ 130.8 threat per mob; Thunderclap does 180 threat but costs twice the rage — response from an experienced tank: still use Thunderclap/Battle Shout/Demo Shout as a tank for their other benefits, but don't treat them as "a cracked form of AoE threat"; establish an AoE threat lead instead via Charge + Whirlwind cleave, then swap to individual targets spreading Sunders, sprinkling shouts/claps with excess rage — [[d:warrior#1331206942370172959]] (Walker, question), [[d:warrior#1331210971875643392]] (Virasan, answer).
- **[leveling]** Prot/AoE leveling tanking with Rend+Thunderclap works like "a pally tank with 0 mana issues" — a Troll leveling with a regen-focused build could tank 3-4 same-level mobs indefinitely at level 25 this way — [[d:warrior#1325254502466457673]] (gribandos).
- **[dps]** Dungeon AoE comparison: Arms deals slightly less single-target damage than Fury but is more stable; with Sweeping Strikes + a full rage bar, Arms kills 2-mob packs very quickly and has more overall AoE damage; single-target damage between specs is considered similar in dungeons — [[d:warrior#1325459556528295977]] (Минихумик).
- **[arms]** Sweeping Strikes charge-consumption rule: each of its (limited) charges copies the *damage dealt* by your next attacks to a nearby enemy — avoid triggering it with low-damage abilities like Hamstring or Pummel while charges remain, since that "wastes" a charge on a near-zero cleave hit; min-max technique: pop Sweeping Strikes, then disable Auto Attack or face away (a Rogue-PvP-opener-style trick) so you can fire Whirlwind (hits multiple targets) plus your strongest single hit (e.g. Execute) to spend the remaining charges efficiently — [[d:warrior#1358900982875947129]], [[d:warrior#1358901630426152991]] (Forlorn Shade).
- **[arms]** Sweeping Strikes/Whirlwind cost-efficiency note: Whirlwind is a 10s-CD, 30-rage ability hitting up to 4 targets for weapon damage — no reason to use it on a single mob; Cleave (20 rage) is argued to be roughly as good as Sweeping Strikes+Whirlwind per rage spent unless you have 60+ rage banked, in which case opening with Sweeping Strikes then Mortal Strike (while Cleave fills the gap on cooldown) was suggested as the efficient order for a 2+ mob pull — [[d:warrior#1346426217712062525]] (Ionnatra, theorycraft/unconfirmed).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[stats]** Weapon skill is called the single best stat on melee gear: above 300 skill, every 5 points give 1% hit, 10% reduced glancing-blow damage penalty, and a small extra chance to avoid being dodged/parried; at 308 (pre a later hotfix) this was worth ~0.4% reduced dodge/parry chance from a boss, i.e. ~0.8% effective extra "hit" for a tank (who eats dodges/parries) and ~0.4% for a DPS standing behind the boss (who doesn't) — [[d:warrior#1309517089702285322]] (Tole).
- **[stats]** CC2-era hit-cap/racial rework: old off-races had a 9% hit cap and, even at cap, only generated 91.4% of the rage of main races (6% hit cap) due to glancing-blow mechanics; the rework moved off-races to 8% hit cap generating 97.3% of new main-race (7.4% hit cap) rage; absolute white-hit damage of off-races only gained the extra 1% hit, while main races' white-hit damage/rage generation dropped ~6% from the old main-race baseline — [[d:warrior#1303371582227484753]] (Drunken Robot).
- **[stats]** Later hotfix (~Dec 2024) further changed weapon-skill/hit-cap math: fighting mobs 3+ levels above you no longer applies the old 1% hit-suppression penalty, so the effective hit cap vs. such targets moved from 9% to 8%; the old "soft cap" of 305 skill (which used to offset that suppression) no longer exists; each skill point from 300→304 and 305→308 now gives an equal, smoothed 0.2% hit chance + 2% reduced glancing penalty; weapon skill granted by racial passives was reduced from +5 to +3 — [[d:warrior#1321641077215723520]] (30).
- **[stats]** Weapon-skill quest chain lets any race reach close to the same effective skill without itemization: for Dual Wield you can hit 305/315 from the quest alone, or 308/315 with a racial bonus (compare the old gap of 300/308 vs. 305/308); for 2H, talents add +3 skill so a favored race is 308 vs. 311 for e.g. human/orc/goblin; combined with itemization (e.g. 2H Axe skill on Flame Guard Gauntlets), race now matters much less for weapon skill than pre-CC2 — [[d:warrior#1310154349154013245]] (chain).
- **[stats]** Glancing blows always have a flat 40% chance on white hits; glance damage against a level-63 boss ranges 65%-95% of average weapon damage depending on weapon skill; each point of skill from 300-315 (the new cap) grants 2% more glance damage, and skill above 315 is useless — [[d:warrior#1325891998921527338]] (chain).
- **[stats]** Rage-generation formula redesign (dev-quoted changelog line): "a new rage generation formula that's calculated 90% depending on your gear and 10% of it is predetermined based on your weapon speed" — intended to make rage gen less dependent purely on weapon speed than before — [[d:warrior#1327963076447830128]] (beastie, quoting official wording; some players initially misread this as "10% gear / 90% speed," corrected by a dev — [[d:warrior#1301953139859259522]] (Eisenhart, confusion), [[d:warrior#1303506019518779465]] (Turt Reynolds, unrelated +10 rage/attack bug fix)).
- **[stats/tank]** Shield Slam Str scaling: every 20 Str gives ~7 Shield Slam damage (20 Str = 40 AP, 40×0.15 = 6, plus the ~1 block value 20 Str grants); look for shields with the highest inherent block value + Str rather than worrying about stamina/defense/block chance if gearing for damage; Shield Slam can be used in Arms/Zerker stance too for more damage, and Crit/Agi help once Impale is taken — [[d:warrior#1313155951041445970]] (Aubreykun).
- **[stats/tank]** Concussion Blow damage formula (community-tested): base damage 235; One-Handed Weapon Specialization (5 points, ~110% dmg in Battle Stance / ~99% in Protection Stance) does *not* scale the base-235 portion in Battle Stance but *does* interact with it in Protection Stance in a way the poster couldn't fully reconcile; in Battle Stance the formula worked out to roughly `235 + (AP/14 × 1.9)`; the AP-scaling-per-point-of-AP was inconsistent between test data points, left as an open question — [[d:warrior#1324236371564429384]] (LR, detailed math, unresolved).
- **[tank/stats]** Non-weapon "+skill" items available at endgame are scarce: two AQ40-boss gloves giving +6 Two-Hand / +5 One-Hand respectively, a +5 Mace glove from a world boss, +6 Two-Hand Mace on Emerald Sanctum boss legs, +7 Two-Hand Axe on MC-boss gloves, +2 Sword (1H+2H) on AQ20-boss legs, and Edgemaster's Handguards at +7 for one-handers — mostly considered marginal once Naxx-geared — [[d:warrior#1331107615014391920]] (Virasan).
- **[stats/gear]** More complete weapon-skill (+skill) item list by weapon type, endgame: Fist Weapons — Ashskin Belt and Remnants of an Old God (+5 each), plus a weak ZG fist weapon at +5; One-Hand Swords — Legplates of Qiraji Command (+2/3, mainly worthwhile for Human), Maladath (+5), Thunderfury (+6), Crystal Sword of the Blossom (Emerald Sanctum hardmode, +3), Anasterian's Legacy (Moroes, Kara10, +4), Tempest's Rage (+4); Two-Hand Swords — Claymore of Unholy Might and Obsidian Edged Blade (+6/7 each); One-Hand Axes — realistically only Doom's Edge (+4); Two-Hand Axes — a BWL axe (~+6) and Flameguard Gauntlets (+7); One-Hand Maces — Fists of the Maker and Anubisath Warhammer (+5 each, the latter poorly itemized otherwise); Two-Hand Maces — realistically only an Emerald Sanctum legs item (+8); Daggers — Ashjre'thul Crossblade/ACLG, a Vaelastrasz dagger, and Death's Sting (+5 each; a C'Thun dagger option is only +3); Hybrid/all-weapon-type items — Carapace Handguards (+6 to all Two-Hand skills), Burrower gloves (+6 to all One-Hand skills), and Edgemaster's Handguards (+7 Sword/Axe/Dagger) — [[d:warrior#1374994039828647988]] (Minces).
- **[stats/tank]** Zebouski-style DPS sim's Hit weighting was reported as badly overtuned even without accounting for the new weapon-skill quest (e.g. rating Truestrike shoulders or Shadowflame boots as BiS, or Accuria ring ~30 dps ahead of a Loatheb ring) — flagged as a sim-accuracy caution, unanswered/unresolved in-chunk — [[d:warrior#1303300943055687681]] (Deleted User, single source).
- **[stats/tool]** The long-running community warrior sim (previously maintained by "Zaas") was reported abandoned as of ~June 2025 (Zaas stepping back from TWoW); a fork/continuation is now maintained by "Thrunk" at `https://thrunk112.github.io/WarriorSim-TurtleWoW/turtle.html`, with recent fixes including gear corrections, Shieldrender trinket support, multi-use potions on long fights, a corrected crit-display sidebar, and the improved rage-generation formula; a Slam rework, Sweeping Strikes support, and a possible tank-stats tab were said to be coming — [[d:warrior#1382140931150708788]], [[d:warrior#1382140952218697728]] (Thrunk). - **[bug]** Intercept is reported to behave like a "blink" rather than working as in original Vanilla: a warrior using Intercept while the *target* is stunned still ends up teleported next to them instead of stopping partway, and likewise Intercept ignores the target being slowed and still closes the gap at full effective speed instead of being interrupted/shortened — [[d:warrior#1381458722517291168]], [[d:warrior#1381459645666689055]] (dindunuffinz, unconfirmed by a dev in-chunk).
- **[open question]** No agreed source for exact TWoW warrior ability threat values/multipliers; poster compares TWoW forum, a vanilla threat-mechanics wiki, and a Reddit guide and finds them inconsistent, asking specifically whether TWoW abilities have their own threat multipliers on the damage portion — unanswered in-chunk — [[d:warrior#1335189453672808469]] (Rollo.Tomasi; question unanswered).

- **[stats]** Haste value theorycraft: Haste is almost always a single-target-only gain (you're stuck attacking one enemy the whole time it's relevant); it becomes a relative DPS *loss* compared to flat stats if you're forced to swap targets often (punishes target-switching harder than raw stats do); on longer fights Haste's value rises well above flat stats since you accumulate more total hits; on short fights and trash, Haste is largely irrelevant since targets don't live long enough to benefit from the extra attack(s) it would produce — [[d:warrior#1400487427830972477]] (Minces).
- **[open question]** Why does TWoW put flat weapon-skill bonuses on some items (e.g. Toughened Silithid Hide Gloves, Blooddrenched Grips) instead of the generic +hit% seen in normal Classic itemization? - **[stats]** 1% Crit is estimated at roughly ~37 AP-equivalent on average (varies with current crit rate, and between Arms/DW Fury/2H Fury); when forced to choose between Crit and AP, Crit is favored most of the time — [[d:warrior#1358366251474223317]] (Forlorn Shade, generally corroborating Tole's hit/weapon-skill priority claims above).

- **[tank/leveling]** Concrete rules for tanking 2H all the way to 60 (as an alternative to sword-and-board): (1) try to be 2-3 levels above the enemy/boss, since being under-leveled gives roughly a 5%-per-level-difference chance to eat a Crushing Blow (150% extra damage); (2) mark priority targets and open with your full rotation on the primary target for early threat — note Whirlwind (Berserker Stance) and Overpower (Battle Stance) both only carry the 0.8 threat multiplier, so Defensive Stance loses access to Whirlwind's AoE utility but Improved Thunderclap covers AoE threat well enough without it; (3) using Whirlwind means taking 10% more damage (no Defensive Stance bonus) while also unshielded, so coordinate with your healer since TWoW dungeon mobs hit harder than in Classic; (4) always carry a shield with a swap macro for when you need extra mitigation — a good shield alone can be worth ~10% damage reduction plus another 10-30% from block — [[d:warrior#1399758949082726482]] (Lukass). Fury Prot is "still pretty damn strong," but other tanks now do specific things better — Prot Paladins are called the "GOATs of AoE tanking" and can instantly remove undispellable debuffs; Bear Druids generate a lot of threat while main-tanking but lean on huge armor/HP to eat damage; Shamans bring spell-damage reduction in their kit. Fury Prot's edge is a large cooldown count and very high threat generation while those cooldowns are active — [[d:warrior#1371407433125335070]] (Marinatall Ironside).
- **[tank]** Deep Prot gearing philosophy debate: many players stack Block Value for Shield Slam scaling, but one experienced tank argues full DPS-plate + a shield works fine for most fights since the shield alone handles a lot of mitigation, and the crit difference (~20% vs ~30%) matters a lot for threat; recommends wearing any T3 piece with Block Value as a baseline, swapping other slots to DPS-plate equivalents, and keeping a full-mitigation set on hand specifically for fights with heavy raid-wide/tank damage (Maexxna's web spray, Thaddius, Patchwerk, Four Horsemen) — [[d:warrior#1375709061353308180]] (Don't ping me).
- **[tank]** HC leveling as "Fury Prot" from level 40+ is called overcomplicated by most people — in practice it's just having Bloodthirst + Flurry as a passive DW kit; one experienced HC leveler uses a Sword of Omen + Sword of Serenity pairing as a strong early-DW power spike and reports Deep Prot, Fury Prot, and Arms are all viable tanking approaches while leveling, with real gear-dependence only kicking in once bosses are 3+ levels above you and hit much harder — [[d:warrior#1376389819806257172]] (Catmaid).
- **[tank]** Real-world AoE-threat difficulty report: a player found warrior AoE tanking in 5-mans "sucks hard" using deep prot/standard approaches and settled for 2H Arms + Sweeping Strikes as the only reliable method (not usable for raid tanking or high-incoming-damage content); by contrast, a T3+Thunderfury Deep Prot warrior in the same run handled 4 of 6 add-pull mobs fine (taunting the 5th), while the reporting player's ~0.5-T1-geared Arms needed a BiS-geared DPS specifically holding back to avoid pulling threat off them — [[d:warrior#1376773600992038942]] (Nabuhodonozo).

- **[tank/contested]** A rare empirical data point favoring Deep Prot over Fury Prot: dummy-testing 5 Sunders + 90 rage over a 30s window, Deep Prot (with some Block Value + threat gear) consistently produced 5-8k more threat than Fury Prot (Dual Wield, mostly T2.5 gear); Fury Prot only matched the *low* end of Deep Prot's range when popping Death Wish — poster concludes there's no clear reason to run Fury Prot unless it scales disproportionately better with raid buffs, contradicting the "Fury Prot is still the way" consensus seen throughout most of this chunk — [[d:warrior#1383778390166868070]] (Weirdthing, single test, unconfirmed by others in-chunk).
- **[tank]** Deep Prot stat-priority summary (comprehensive, ~June 2025): Block Value only affects Shield Slam — bad when Shield Slam doesn't connect, fine otherwise; Crit is very good for threat (bigger hits = more threat and more rage), so Agility/Crit is high value; Haste is "meh" since it doesn't help snap/opener threat unless you have a large haste cooldown; Stamina is good for survival; Defense is "meh" unless you specifically can't afford to get crit; always stay hit-capped; use a fast weapon — [[d:warrior#1382777058391752878]] (Velanir). - **[tank/zg]** Zul'Gurub, the troll packs before Jin'do the Hexxer: apply a stacking curse that increases physical damage taken by roughly 400-500%, meaning melee tanking them normally will "absolutely murder you"; they also stun (effectively lethal while cursed); recommended approach for a solo tank is to kite them through a Hunter trap, a Shaman totem, or your own Piercing Howl to stay out of their melee range as much as possible rather than face-tanking the pack — [[d:warrior#1399585291693461574]] (oldmandan).
- **[raid/mc]** Molten Core stun-boss consumable tip: use a Free Action Potion instead of protection potions specifically on Gehennas, Sulfuron Harbinger, and Razorgore the Untamed's Phase 3, since these fights stun you and you can't otherwise defend yourself while stunned; Greater Stoneshield Potions should be popped on cooldown after other protection pots pre-pull for a Fury Prot MT/OT — [[d:warrior#1369058081744490558]] (Marinatall Ironside).
