- **[Arms/Fury 2H]** The Flurry/Slam interaction is being "fixed," described by the community as a massive nerf to 2H Fury's slam-spam playstyle ("flurry slam") — [[d:warrior#1483426032076984353]] (nudel12, 2026-03-17), [[d:warrior#1483428051504206006]] (Julian: "Its a massive nerf").

- **[Talents]** Tactical Mastery quietly (shadow) nerfed to only preserve rage across stance swaps caused by weapon attacks/swings ("1h spec"), not universally — [[d:warrior#1483554050560626901]] (Velanir, 2026-03-17), disputed reaction [[d:warrior#1483554101500575845]] (Madcrep).

### Patch 1.18.1 went live 2026-03-20 — confirmed effects from live testing
- **[Prot]** Live-tested final Defiance value confirmed as 4/8/12/15/20% (the last point going from 15%→20%) — [[d:warrior#1485376135058555063]] (nUKe13, quoting his own build, 2026-03-23). Live Shield Slam math reported by Velanir pre-patch as 20% AP + 1:1 Shield Block Value scaling, with mark vasko stating it becomes 25% AP after the patch — [[d:warrior#1484330404520526106]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1484331952864760050]] (mark vasko); this is a slightly different framing than the 15%→20% figure quoted from patch notes pre-release (see above), so treat exact before/after numbers as only approximately confirmed.
- **[Fury 2H]** On patch day, a bug caused Flurry stacks to stop being consumed entirely (effectively permanent 3-stack Flurry), acknowledged as a known bug by a dev/mod and hotfixed on the next server restart — [[d:warrior#1484623736580870146]] (Lhian), [[d:warrior#1484623813454073856]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1484635426726609007]] (Lhian, "known, will be fixed on restart"). After the hotfix, Flurry-hastened Slam cast time settled at roughly 1.71-1.77s, nearly identical to the Arms Improved Slam talent's ~1.77s — meaning the fix removed most, but not quite all, of 2H Fury's Slam-speed advantage — [[d:warrior#1484690483874758748]] (Painfool), [[d:warrior#1484690802365038693]] (Thirtyseven).
- **[Fury DW]** The only real change for Dual Wield Fury this patch was Improved Whirlwind gaining a Cleave rage-cost reduction (community estimate ~3 less rage per Cleave) — confirmed by multiple as "nothing else changed" for DW — [[d:warrior#1484569034300198952]] (Yorb), [[d:warrior#1484604481055363195]] (Gwendalyn), [[d:warrior#1484898632233783356]] (Dodging, 2026-03-21: "Nothing really changed").
- **[World buff]** The Champion buff was removed from the game this patch, which several tanks report made their threat feel lower even though their spec was objectively buffed (Defiance increase) — [[d:warrior#1485146573917917264]] (30), [[d:warrior#1484654008445440132]] (Virasan, "glad they removed champ buff"), [[d:warrior#1485300408393138258]] (Dodging: "if you were getting champ before, you are now not getting it... probably doing slightly less threat").
- **[Stat]** Fortune confirmed multiplicative, not additive: a 5% base proc chance plus a 5%-Fortune item yields 5.25%, not 10% — [[d:warrior#1484638990551355504]] (Dodging), [[d:warrior#1484639318097264641]] (Dodging). Consensus after testing: Fortune is a weak "bait stat" not worth chasing at current values — [[d:warrior#1484543792043397232]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:warrior#1484544427107160246]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:warrior#1484516929698795663]] (Lipibidy: "for when devs think it's more interesting to make a junk stat than to balance proc items individually").
- **[Execute]** Confirmed (resolving an earlier dispute) that Execute damage scales off Attack Power, Armor Penetration, and Rage spent — not off weapon damage or weapon speed — [[d:warrior#1484660616991080541]] (Gwendalyn), [[d:warrior#1484660284470591618]] (Yagsick), [[d:warrior#1485080360978092143]] (Kyrsie, restated 2026-03-22).
- **[Tank threat mechanic]** Because Defensive Tactics (3/3) copies the Defiance threat multiplier to Battle/Berserker Stance, and Battle Stance deals more raw damage (no -10% damage penalty), a DefTac tank with 5/5 Defiance + 3/3 DefTac can generate equal-or-greater overall threat tanking in Battle Stance than in Defensive Stance — confirmed via community math after the patch — [[d:warrior#1484538309416845352]] (Dodging), [[d:warrior#1484541709554815167]] (Black Hat), [[d:warrior#1484544158382428334]] (Black Hat), [[d:warrior#1484916553530085428]] (Lipibidy, restated 2026-03-21: "3/3 copies your dstance multiplier to your other stances... Bstance and zerker have a .8 multiplier to lower threat [on damage, not on the copied bonus]").
- **[PvP]** No class-specific "dampening" (a rumored damage-reduction mechanic) was actually added for warriors despite expectations going into the patch — [[d:warrior#1485387938866008235]] (limoseng/Haken/Khang, 2026-03-23: "Dampening was a lye, there is no reduction in magical dmg taken for warrior"); separately, "rage is fixed" for PvP is claimed by a couple of posters without further detail — [[d:warrior#1485327823987019826]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:warrior#1485327953192816792]] (casey). No PvP dampening system tied to the new rank/honor currency was added either — [[d:warrior#1485326960132493414]] (casey: "they didnt add class specific dampening) pvp is the same").

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[PvE DPS]** Current-patch consensus: 2H Fury (with Sweeping Strikes swapped in for Bloodthirst on some builds) is the top overall-damage spec; DW Fury needs heavy gear investment to compete but scales extremely well once geared (server-record parses cited) — [[d:warrior#1482754932187598888]] (Velanir: "2h fury, 2h fury with sweeping strikes instead of BT, and dual wield fury are the meta dps specs"; +confirmed by [[d:warrior#1481664668073201705]] Lashara DW record discussion, multiple reposts/agreement).
- **[PvE DPS]** Arms is considered the weakest of the three DPS trees currently; its playstyle ("lawnmower"/slam-spam) has effectively been absorbed by 2H Fury via Flurry acting like Improved Slam + 30% attack speed, making Arms mostly redundant — extensive single-source argument, well-received — [[d:warrior#1481465328939831501]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1481466232564748419]] (Lipibidy), agreement from 30 [[d:warrior#1482122504792309794]].
- **[PvE DPS]** Some pushback that Arms is "reasonable" again after an execute buff — [[d:warrior#1481504913195405433]] (Kyrsie), though others maintain "2h fury and dw fury gonna be the best with arms being way behind" — [[d:warrior#1482120027602812969]] (Zhafu).
- **[Tank]** Current live-patch ranking per Velanir: FuryProt > ProtPala > deep Prot (DefTac) — [[d:warrior#1482701538563592346]]. FuryProt uses Flurry for extra TPS; DefTac (deep prot) trades TPS for mitigation.
- **[Tank]** Warrior is regarded as the weakest main tank class overall but "more than viable" for all current content; main weakness is AoE threat versus e.g. paladin — [[d:warrior#1482694837080821865]] (Black Hat), corroborated [[d:warrior#1482695904921387184]].
- **[Tank]** After the incoming Shield Slam/Defiance buffs, DeathTactics (deep-Fury-into-Prot hybrid using Enrage+Death Wish+Shield Slam) is predicted by several to become the best warrior MT spec, with sims showing it has a stronger opener than FuryProt and lower standard deviation than ImpaleProt — [[d:warrior#1483553585416638535]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1483550098976669789]] (Velanir sim claim), [[d:warrior#1483552117363838996]] (Velanir).
- **[Tank]** DefTac/DeepProt "feels limp" without Death Wish uptime currently; opinions split on whether post-patch Shield Slam alone carries it outside cooldowns — [[d:warrior#1483614716797653083]] (Jakeends), [[d:warrior#1483559216722481373]] (Dodging).
- **[DPS]** Rogues considered overtuned/top DPS in the current patch — [[d:warrior#1481641725951873175]] (Black Hat).
- **[Post-patch, Arms]** Immediately after 1.18.1, a "Deathcute" build emerged (Death Wish + Execute-focus, dropping Mortal Strike and Master of Arms for Enrage/Precision Cut) and briefly looked like a new meta due to huge Execute crits (up to ~18k reported), but same-day testing found it "feels very slow" — the main issue being dead time with 0 rage right after Execute until the next auto-attack — and its huge Execute numbers are viewed as inflated/"fake" damage against near-dead targets rather than real sustained output — [[d:warrior#1484634311767490690]] (Thirtyseven, naming it), [[d:warrior#1484682261591883897]] (Painfool: "feels very slow"), [[d:warrior#1484683514300338176]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:warrior#1484683789547475084]] (Thirtyseven: "lawnmower is doing real dmg, all deathcute is doing is pumping fake dmg with overkill executes").
- **[Post-patch, Arms vs 2H Fury]** Within a day of the patch, consensus was that 2H Fury ("lawnmower") and DW Fury remained ahead of any Arms/Deathcute build for real raid damage, since "none of the core issues with arms were fixed" — [[d:warrior#1484688431153020948]] (jiginator), [[d:warrior#1484690162494603314]] (Painfool: "Lawnmower and 2h fury are still better than arms"), [[d:warrior#1485299170024620223]] (Kyrsie, after trying Arms: "played like shit... probably just going back to the bloodthirst 2h fury spec").
- **[Post-patch, Tank]** DefTac (deep-Fury-into-Prot with Shield Slam) is confirmed post-patch by multiple first-hand raid reports to be dramatically stronger and now the generally recommended MT choice: "deftac threat is insane now... Im at 3k threat on 1 SS" [[d:warrior#1484651876229320755]] (Peer); "SS buff + def tacts + defiance is really strong now tbh, i doubted it A LOT but i'm surprised" [[d:warrior#1484652509061578956]] (yipeigne); "Personally on bwl as deep prot I was number 1 on threat at basically all times after the update" [[d:warrior#1485306450720063720]] (darthpenance); "tanked naxx tonight and everything felt good" [[d:warrior#1485145112974721147]] (Dodging); "the only reason to go fprot now is if you really need that extra ~150 tps... going dtac is better in 99.9% of cases instead of 95% it was before" [[d:warrior#1485222514119671829]], [[d:warrior#1485222628016001024]] (Weirdthing).
- **[Post-patch, Tank — dissent]** DefTac's core weakness persists post-patch: it still hinges entirely on Shield Slam connecting, with no reliable backup (Concussion Blow/Overpower are Arms/Fury-side, not always taken) — "if you miss 2 shield slams in a row on opener youre doomed... not having the safety net of conc blow and OP is bad" — [[d:warrior#1485223080249917522]], [[d:warrior#1485223313272737894]] (Weirdthing); "the optimal solution to be gambling all your snap threat on an 80 percent isnt very appealing" — [[d:warrior#1485339507912998953]] (Hayden). Some (e.g. Hayden) conclude Warrior "mostly shouldnt be main tanking" and should accept an off-tank/FuryProt role instead — [[d:warrior#1485339851120443402]] (Hayden), [[d:warrior#1485340158751408138]] (Hayden).
- **[Post-patch, Tank hierarchy]** Even after the buffs, Feral Druid and Paladin are judged clearly ahead of any Warrior tank spec by Lipibidy in an extensive, largely unchallenged argument: Warrior tanking tools are split across three mutually-exclusive sub-specs (FuryProt / DefTac / "Revenge Prot") that all still end up leaning on Death Wish just to be viable, whereas Bear and Paladin each have one coherent, TBC-tier tank kit — "it's a bear server right now" [[d:warrior#1484745552615964902]]; "Dtac directly clashes with spec'ing revenge prot, and both are trying to grab deathwish to be like furyprot... Prot feeling like it needs deathwish to tank is a bad spot in general" [[d:warrior#1485348072145031233]], [[d:warrior#1485349063892271194]] (Lipibidy, 2026-03-21).
- **[Tank, dungeon-only build]** "Revenge Prot" (Death Wish + Revenge-focused talents, explicitly skipping DefTac/Shield-Slam investment) is used by some as a lower-commitment dungeon tanking build, at the cost of losing Concussion Blow reliability on trash — [[d:warrior#1485337755646165135]] (Madcrep), [[d:warrior#1485338583689596990]] (Dodging).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Fury (dual wield, with Blood Drinker) is argued to be the only leveling/solo tree that "works" well on Turtle for 2H, DW, or self-found tanking, due to buffed self-healing (Blood Craze, Blood Drinker) — [[d:warrior#1481446311432552528]] (Lipibidy, strong single-source claim, no pushback recorded).
- **[Leveling rotation]** Start with the 5%-crit early talent, then invest into Arms while leveling (can also tank most early dungeons 2H with a sword+shield swap macro for hard hitters) — [[d:warrior#1481439460146286602]] (Crummy), [[d:warrior#1481439662890553474]] (Crummy).
- **[Leveling]** Shield-tanking early causes rage starvation unless talented for rage-on-block; Defensive Stance gives roughly a 1.10x threat multiplier vs. other stances per one player's recollection (not an exact verified number) — [[d:warrior#1481441238308622396]] (Crummy), [[d:warrior#1481441675833376841]] (Crummy), [[d:warrior#1481441835388764231]] (Crummy, "~1.10x").
- **[Leveling]** Heroic Strike generates more threat than damage and has a poor rage-to-damage ratio while leveling; best saved for when rage is capped; Sunder Armor + target-tabbing is the core leveling filler — [[d:warrior#1481442159596142762]] (Peremin), [[d:warrior#1481442258120081644]] (Crummy), [[d:warrior#1481442530435530936]] (Crummy).
- **[HC leveling]** Fury favored for hardcore leveling because nerfed-CD Piercing Howl + self-healing lets a warrior survive without a dedicated healer; the offhand +10% hit talent is called out as great value — [[d:warrior#1481440240483958795]] (Peremin), [[d:warrior#1481440577747681360]] (Peremin).
- **[Leveling]** For leveling generally: go Fury for Blood Drinker; DW leveling is viable "because the new dual wield spec is actually good"; once Blood Drinker is online you basically stop needing to drink water — [[d:warrior#1482755253173616691]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1482755383465476096]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1482755486960062480]] (Velanir).
- **[Leveling]** New-player recommendation: go dual wield with Blood Drinker ASAP, quest mobs at or below your level, worry about gear/enchants only at 60 — [[d:warrior#1484007150962475030]] (casey).
- **[Leveling rotation, level ~40+]** Demo Shout → Heroic Strike; use Sunder-to-5-stacks only if a mob is tanky, otherwise straight to Heroic Strike; once Bloodthirst (36) and Whirlwind (40) unlock, prioritize Bloodthirst > Whirlwind, same rules otherwise — [[d:warrior#1484031184601350286]] (casey).
- **[Leveling rotation]** Battle Shout uptime → Sunder → auto/Bloodthirst → auto/auto → bandage, repeat; use a swing-timer addon; only 1 Sunder is usually worth it on a normal quest mob, more only when soloing an elite; Rend is weak for DW (better for sword & board) and scales too poorly to use past roughly level 30 — [[d:warrior#1484030990027456573]] (Dodging), [[d:warrior#1484032312151769159]] (Dodging), [[d:warrior#1484033053100609636]] (Dodging), [[d:warrior#1484032803472412833]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Farming build]** For solo farming, Fury DW with 5/5 Flurry + Blood Drinker outperforms a shield+spike vengeance-style build (Prot deals no damage, so vamp effects have nothing to scale off); kill-time-reduction (via DPS) beats raw sustain for farming efficiency — [[d:warrior#1481806649273483344]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:warrior#1481809130225144030]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1481809366465122355]] (Dodging).
- **[Gold farming]** Blood Drinker Fury solo-farming RFD for shards (with disenchanting) nets ~70g/hr in pre-raid gear; adding herbalism (Grave Moss) pushes 80-90g/hr; with MC-tier gear, ZF gives similar/better consistency — [[d:warrior#1482212021603799181]] (Hayden), [[d:warrior#1482212076037472367]] (Hayden), [[d:warrior#1482212230379470882]] (Hayden).

- **[Fury]** Deathwish (and other burst cooldowns) are generally best popped in the last ~30 seconds of a fight / execute phase for consistency, rather than trying to line up with RNG procs (dual Crusader, Unrelenting Strikes, Rage of the Mountain); Bloodrage and Juju Flurry are easier to line up with procs since they're short-duration, 1-min-CD — [[d:warrior#1482492282388091114]] (Cannondorf), [[d:warrior#1482492544838533221]] (Cannondorf), [[d:warrior#1482492707048783913]] (Cannondorf), [[d:warrior#1482509910209204345]] (Piemelbrie / siswet, agrees with nuance for mechanics/adds).

- **[Fury/2H]** Slam has a "fake" cast bar: dodge/parry avoidance is not fully disabled while casting it (unlike a real spell cast), but community testing in this chunk found avoidance chance is still dramatically reduced while casting vs. standing still (e.g., ~20% dodge at rest dropping to roughly 1-in-10 casts) — [[d:warrior#1483657137929457804]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1483665755441729596]] (Dodging test result), [[d:warrior#1483668711327010988]] (MoneyCow, confirms some avoidance remains), [[d:warrior#1483675750124097689]] (Lipibidy, "casting dramatically reduces dodging").

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target

- **[Fury farming/AoE]** Typical open-world AoE approach: pull 2-4 mobs, dump excess rage into Cleave while hitting Bloodthirst/Whirlwind on cooldown (works well once Blood Drinker is online for sustain) — [[d:warrior#1484033434824216647]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1484033590059864205]] (Magik).
- **[2H Fury, post-patch]** After the incoming changes, 2H Fury is expected to shred trash packs hard — an extra ~5-10k damage per mob in packs, more if Sweeping Strikes duplicates it, and with T1 3-piece active Sweeping Strikes becomes "rage positive most of the time" — [[d:warrior#1483861037097816196]] (Cannondorf), [[d:warrior#1483861393542479952]] (evella).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit]** Target roughly 8% hit for Fury, minus about 1% for every 5 points of weapon skill above 300 (helps DPS and rage generation) — [[d:warrior#1483968578414973019]] (Friekshow).
- **[Weapon skill]** 315 skill is the effective cap vs. level-63 raid content; going above it only grants a small amount of extra hit and reduced chance to be dodged, and isn't worth chasing on trash or bosses — [[d:warrior#1483240311093330031]] (30), [[d:warrior#1483240385173131274]] (30), [[d:warrior#1483239437885243542]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Weapon skill]** A human can reach 315 mace skill via: +5 book, +5 from Taut Dragonhide/Spineshatter-type enchant, +3 racial, +2 from a ZG mace — [[d:warrior#1484199024377598063]] (Velanir).
- **[Stats, 2H]** Contested: Str vs. Haste priority for 2H Fury gear. "Str is better, don't let hastechuds fool you," str especially favored for trash — [[d:warrior#1482884212791971952]] (axelfrog), [[d:warrior#1483101022565171272]] (axelfrog). Countered: "Haste has a double benefit for 2h so I'd always go haste for 2h," especially relevant for a 30/21 deathwish-execute-oriented build — [[d:warrior#1483102293048955073]] (evella), [[d:warrior#1483103326286647427]] (evella).
- **[Armor Pen]** Armor Penetration is viewed as a largely worthless stat currently — small in magnitude compared to Sunder Armor, though it's fine as a bonus on an otherwise-good item; could matter more on bosses that retain armor after debuffs are applied — [[d:warrior#1483123321141723207]] (Nori), [[d:warrior#1483123694854344854]] (Cannondorf), [[d:warrior#1481300143931392114]] (dewbie, re: raw ARP value on bosses w/ residual armor).
- **[DefTac/1H+shield]** Above the hit cap, additional hit gives literally zero value for a 1H+shield DefTac tank since the spec's threat doesn't lean on white-hit uptime the way DPS builds do — [[d:warrior#1483151599101542460]] (Nori, question), [[d:warrior#1483151759164833832]] (Velanir, confirms: "every point above hit cap does exactly nothing for 1h+shield").
- **[Block value]** Shield Block Value is a weak stat for threat: a typical BV item carries only ~20 SBV, so stacking it yields little extra damage; Glyph of Deflection is called out as the only BV-granting item worth using for damage purposes — prioritize actual DPS stats (Str/crit/hit) instead — [[d:warrior#1482190010483605616]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1482190279309005043]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1482190391049719959]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Sim methodology]** The community warrior sim needs very high iteration counts (100,000+) before per-stat weight differences (e.g., 1 Agi) become statistically meaningful; low iteration counts produce confidence intervals larger than the stat values themselves — [[d:warrior#1482428703718772958]] (30), [[d:warrior#1482791448284893224]] (30), [[d:warrior#1482791532372168725]] (30).
- **[Armor Penetration, detailed]** ArP's value scales inversely with the target's remaining armor relative to its level: against raid bosses that still have some (debuffed-down-but-nonzero) armor, ArP is at its most valuable; outside a raid setting with full armor debuffs applied, ArP is "trash" — [[d:warrior#1484881788668088430]] (Tole, detailed explanation), [[d:warrior#1484880469450297519]] (Madcrep). Rough community rule-of-thumb equivalence: ~75 ArP ≈ 70 AP in value on one specific item comparison (Demonic Deception) — [[d:warrior#1484906414945341440]] (Blackjack). Lipibidy separately argues ArP is overvalued in TWoW's stat budget because enemy armor is such a large swinging variable, making it hard to optimize around, unlike in TBC where bosses were rarely pushed to 0 armor — [[d:warrior#1484906014150098995]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1484906362348507176]] (Lipibidy). The Iron Belt Buckle (with 25 ArP) is called out as an outlier/overtuned item for how much ArP it carries on a T2-level piece — [[d:warrior#1485021430323220531]] (Hayden), [[d:warrior#1485021507469050088]] (Hayden).
- **[Fortune, confirmed math]** See "Patch 1.18.1 went live" note above: Fortune is confirmed multiplicative (5%+5% Fortune item = 5.25%, not 10%) and considered a weak stat at observed values — [[d:warrior#1484638990551355504]] (Dodging).
- **[Weapon skill design critique]** Turtle's custom weapon-skill gear bonuses are criticized as "clunky" itemization: bonuses typically come in chunks of 5-6 points, split per weapon type, on only a handful of item slots — meaning some race/weapon combinations can't reach exactly 315 (e.g., certain gloves grant +6 instead of +7 for some races) even when a player is willing to itemize for it — [[d:warrior#1484221439232970752]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1484232487503659028]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1484233671396556994]] (Lipibidy). Countered by others as a normal stat with normal opportunity costs, no different from stacking any other stat, and that under/over-capping by 1-2 points has negligible real impact (~0.2% hit/dodge) — [[d:warrior#1484234467701821630]] (Jakeends, +4), [[d:warrior#1484236461241929758]] (Jakeends), [[d:warrior#1484233423056011316]] (30), [[d:warrior#1484233754070224946]] (30).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Rage mechanic]** Priest Power Word: Shield reduces/negates rage generation for a warrior because rage is generated from damage taken and the shield absorbs it — [[d:warrior#1484196932808216756]] (dishsoap, question), [[d:warrior#1484197146742755494]] (Hayden), [[d:warrior#1484197162282778674]] (Jhinzuo).
- **[Rage/Enrage mechanic]** Bloodrage always registers as a "crit" mechanically, which is why crit-triggered Enrage effects proc off it; the same mechanic caused the (likely unintended) Enrage proc from the Claw of the Befouler trinket's self-damage tick — [[d:warrior#1481147305347256525]] (Ionnatra), [[d:warrior#1481147594125082805]] (Akanamu).
- **[Tanking utility]** Tactical Mastery (2-3 points) is valued to avoid losing rage when swapping stances to taunt or react, though it was shadow-nerfed (see 1.18.1 section) to only cover weapon-swing-driven rage — [[d:warrior#1483142951285817394]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1483143108257517798]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1483554050560626901]] (Velanir).
- **[Macro mechanics]** Weapon-swap ("equip") macros are notoriously unreliable mid-combat on the Turtle client ("the client hates weapon swapping in combat"); this is a long-standing, not-fully-fixed client quirk — [[d:warrior#1481726614898213019]] (30); see Bugs section.
- **[Revenge scaling, detailed]** Revenge deals a flat 528 damage every 4.5s baseline; only the Revenge talent row and the T3 tank set bonus can increase this (up to roughly 1000 threat per Revenge crit with full investment), and the damage portion does not scale with gear or with Death Wish at all — meaning "the threat per revenge in a full set of grey gear and in full threat bis is the same number," so it falls off in relative value as a tank gears up — [[d:warrior#1485318120104132900]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1485318417849516042]] (Dodging), [[d:warrior#1485318488934453349]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1485318931710476419]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1485319031870455849]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1485319478190280879]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Rend, confirmed weak]** A full fight's worth of Rend damage is roughly half the damage of a single Sapper Charge; Rend is also mechanically almost identical to Deep Wounds, making a numeric buff to it feel redundant rather than a fix — [[d:warrior#1485310564220801217]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1485311735253700688]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Thunderclap, confirmed weak]** Thunderclap is capped at 4 targets, has no damage scaling, and is "next to rend in terms of use" — usefulness falls off almost entirely once a tank has Thunderfury, since TF's own Nature Resist debuff supersedes the reason to press Thunderclap for its debuff — [[d:warrior#1485307139093168128]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1485308906132734153]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1485309497755963642]] (Lipibidy). Thunderclap's damage is confirmed to be Physical, not spell/magic damage, despite looking like a "spell" — [[d:warrior#1485297452713246872]] (Dodging), [[d:warrior#1485298215178862728]] (Kyrsie).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Threat basics]** Opening rotation for new tank players: Demoralizing Shout + Thunderclap on pull for AoE threat, then single-target Sunder Armor / Shield Bash / Concussion Blow / Revenge; threat is tracked separately per target, so splash/off-target aggro needs individual attention — [[d:warrior#1481600118825422920]] (Ronnin79, question), [[d:warrior#1481621221878661172]] (Black Hat).
- **[Rage mechanic]** Blocking with a shield does not generate rage unless specifically talented for it — [[d:warrior#1481441238308622396]] (Crummy), [[d:warrior#1481441375370215475]] (Crummy).
- **[FuryProt vs DefTac]** FuryProt is currently the stronger single-target TPS spec (leans on Flurry); DefTac/DeepProt trades some TPS for mitigation. Community expects the gap to shrink significantly once the 1.18.1 Shield Slam/Defiance buffs land — [[d:warrior#1483138050102919270]] (Bonkers), [[d:warrior#1483138195917897819]] (Squishlish), [[d:warrior#1483138181804200090]] (Jakeends, "gonna be much closer come Friday").
- **[DefTac gearing]** Crit and Strength remain more valuable than Shield Block Value even in DefTac; SBV is only "okay" — [[d:warrior#1483133849948131479]] (Jakeends).
- **[DefTac subspec]** Impale (via Arms) requires reliable Battle Shout/Demo Shout uptime from a trusted second warrior to be worth it; without that, the "shouts" (Fury-side) subspec is preferred; Impale is not strictly required even at a high level — [[d:warrior#1483134510714458183]] (Cannondorf), [[d:warrior#1483135309393952839]] (Cannondorf).
- **[DefTac itemization]** T2 tank set is described as mitigation BiS "forever," worth acquiring even if not worn every fight — [[d:warrior#1483125112822562818]] (Cannondorf).
- **[Stance usage]** Recommended to actually use all three stances while tanking: Battle/Defensive for single-target threat+mitigation+taunting, switching briefly to Berserker Stance for Whirlwind since warrior AoE threat is otherwise weak ("potato") as a tank; remember to keep Mocking Blow bound — [[d:warrior#1483137620698726574]] (Bonkers), [[d:warrior#1483137699962552451]] (Cannondorf), [[d:warrior#1483137845337264230]] (Cannondorf).
- **[Off-hand proc]** Riftcarver is close to Thunderfury in raw damage but roughly 100 TPS behind for DefTac tanking purposes — [[d:warrior#1483887485183594516]] (Jakeends), [[d:warrior#1483888404952387855]] (Jakeends), [[d:warrior#1483888675606495356]] (30, "for deftac no").
- **[Opener, current patch]** In the live (pre-1.18.1) patch, opening with Concussion Blow can out-threat opening with Shield Slam for many guilds' realistic Sunder-application speed (5+ seconds to land 5 sunders); simmed on a 5-sunder/63-level dummy with no other debuffs — [[d:warrior#1483559681908543694]] (30), [[d:warrior#1483559824888434901]] (30), [[d:warrior#1483559886838038568]] (Dodging, confirms sim result). Post-1.18.1 Shield Slam buffs, sim testing (with more realistic 3s armor-debuff timing) shows opening with Shield Slam edges out Concussion Blow by a small, within-variance margin — [[d:warrior#1483588317709598801]] (Jakeends).
- **[Post-patch, DefTac gearing]** For DefTac tanking post-patch, the recommendation is to "ape out on threat stats and wear a shield with a fast weapon" — [[d:warrior#1484717088315670750]] (Dodging). Heroic Strike is roughly equal to Shield Slam as a raid-environment threat tool, so a fast weapon matters for landing more Heroic Strikes; don't tank as DefTac with anything slower than Thunderfury, and avoid Berserker-Stance cleave-tanking as DefTac without Thunderfury since the cleave threat is otherwise too low/risky — [[d:warrior#1484648702718251058]] (Virasan), [[d:warrior#1484648367270395994]] (Virasan), [[d:warrior#1484647921545641984]] (Virasan). A DefTac tank's Heroic Strike swings, being guaranteed on-hit rather than normal white-hit rolls, cannot glance — meaning a shield tank's own damage output is much less sensitive to weapon-skill/glancing than a DPS build's, and running with zero extra weapon-skill gear is fine — [[d:warrior#1485315738053902458]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1485315848385331321]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1485316487324504246]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Post-patch, DefTac vs FuryProt]** After the patch, DefTac's TPS gap with FuryProt is gone or nearly gone, so "there's not much reason to use furyprot now" except a small consistency edge; if using DefTac, don't also take Revenge talents (and vice versa) since they pull in different directions — [[d:warrior#1484900996613279845]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1484903007748362241]] (Lipibidy). For general 5-man dungeon tanking, a DPS spec (2H or Fury) is usually still better than any Prot spec, since multi-target tanking tools are "pretty much non existent in a prot spec" and "Warrior lacks tools for tanking anything but a boss" — [[d:warrior#1484902476934025238]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1484969394714185761]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1484969488079388853]] (Lipibidy), [[d:warrior#1484969866283712562]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Champion-buff removal]** Some tanks measured noticeably lower raid threat right after the patch and traced it to the Champion buff being removed game-wide, not to any nerf of their spec — [[d:warrior#1485146573917917264]] (30), [[d:warrior#1485144231663370333]] (Nylock), [[d:warrior#1485148035263500421]] (Nylock).

## PvP
- **[General sentiment]** Widespread feeling that warrior is weak/unrewarding in PvP relative to other classes, contrasted against strong PvE performance — [[d:warrior#1482752863011606538]] (Velanir, sarcastic post, well-received).
- **[PvP set]** New T3 PvP chest+gloves 2-set is viewed as "so much better than anything else a tank could reasonably wear" for PvE tanking too; armor is not locked purely to R14 (other pieces obtainable at lower rank/effort) — [[d:warrior#1481251862488420562]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1481251941475684463]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1481307363490398434]] (Thirtyseven).
- **[PvP grind]** Estimated T3 PvP grind is roughly 5+ weeks of honor farming per item under the new rank+honor currency system; T2 PvP items cost ~22k honor with T3 assumed to cost at least double, though this figure is explicitly an assumption, not confirmed — [[d:warrior#1481404781942804480]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:warrior#1481404963459698739]] (Thirtyseven), disputed as inaccurate framing by Kyrsie: once you hit R14 you're capped and free to farm honor at your own pace rather than "constant grinding" — [[d:warrior#1481405027083092100]] (Kyrsie).
- **[PvP damage]** Plate/warrior PvP damage is heavily mitigated: reports of ~300-400 Mortal Strike crits from epic 2H weapons (Might of Menethil / BRE) on other plate/leather targets, with roughly 80% damage reduction plus pushback resistance noted for warriors in duels — [[d:warrior#1482762554164908174]] (Painfool), [[d:warrior#1482763274322706562]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:warrior#1482763602053304432]] (Thirtyseven).
- **[PvP build]** Fury PvP struggles to fit Impale + Blood Drinker + Improved Berserker Rage + 5/5 Flurry simultaneously in one build (points too spread out); suggestion (unimplemented) that Blood Drinker and Blood Craze should each be flat 5/5 single talents instead of split across rows — [[d:warrior#1483521577193570387]] (Ryiana), [[d:warrior#1483526444041769082]] (Ryiana).
- **[Post-patch PvP]** Warrior PvP is widely reported as essentially unchanged/still weak after the patch: no class-specific "dampening" (rumored magic-damage reduction) was actually implemented despite pre-patch expectations, and the new PvP rank/honor system added no class-specific dampening either — [[d:warrior#1485387938866008235]] (limoseng/Haken/Khang), [[d:warrior#1485326960132493414]] (casey). "Rage is fixed" for PvP is claimed by a couple of posters without further mechanical detail given — [[d:warrior#1485327823987019826]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:warrior#1485327953192816792]] (casey).
- **[PvP, Sweeping Strikes]** Sweeping Strikes was made unable to crit specifically because critical Sweeping Strikes broke an unspecified raid mechanic — a workaround criticized by some as fixing the class instead of the mechanic — [[d:warrior#1485394033395105993]] (limoseng/Haken/Khang), [[d:warrior#1485393429956657283]] (Julian, calling it "a gimmick").
- **[PvP burst example]** With Hamstring's cooldown removed and the buffed Execute, a fully-buffed 2H warrior can reportedly build 130 rage in ~20 seconds and land an Execute crit around 10k on a low-HP player — cited half-sarcastically as evidence 2H Fury/Deathwish burst is still a relevant PvP tool despite the class's overall weak PvP standing — [[d:warrior#1485389360802037840]] (Czasku).
- **[PvP, DW Fury]** DW Fury is playable/fun in PvP once fully geared, but requires disproportionately more gear investment than other classes need for similar PvP performance — [[d:warrior#1485391057750593728]] (limoseng/Haken/Khang).

- **[UI/WeakAuras equivalent]** DoiteAuras ("A Vanilla WeakAuras Light") by Doite is the main aura-tracking addon; supports CD/buff tracking, pet-aura tracking, fade/transparency visual effects, item-based show/hide conditions; shared UI import strings for most classes posted on r/DoiteAuras "Share your UI"; a power-bar module is planned/in-progress (community member Jeremy volunteering a PR) but author has considered dropping it due to burnout from constant feature requests — [[d:warrior#1482821296717828438]] (Doite v1.6.3), [[d:warrior#1483402604536139796]] (Doite, reddit link), [[d:warrior#1483829163608772729]] (Doite v1.7.0), [[d:warrior#1483830306812334161]] (Doite, on burnout), [[d:warrior#1484111555141435503]] (Jeremy volunteering).

- **[Script/automation]** LazyScript and IWinEnhanced (`github.com/Profiler781/IWinEnhanced`) are Lua-based rotation-automation addons; SuperCleveRoidMacros is better for quick hot-editing mid-fight, IWin for deeper automation (requires a UI reload to apply changes) — [[d:warrior#1483451927952887968]] (Agamemnoth), [[d:warrior#1483483423992709130]] (Xiaobai), [[d:warrior#1483528929611481108]] (Agamemnoth). ## Bugs & quirks
- **[Client]** Weapon-swap/equip macros intermittently fail mid-combat; described as the client fundamentally disliking weapon swaps during combat — long-standing, unresolved — [[d:warrior#1481726614898213019]] (30).
- **[Macro bug]** Ability macro buttons (e.g. a simple start-attack+Sunder macro) occasionally silently fail to cast mid-fight for no apparent reason — unresolved, reported once — [[d:warrior#1481703736991682572]] (Poggums Johnson).
- **[Deep Wounds/Flurry interaction]** If a Deep Wounds DoT tick lands while Slam is being cast, it consumes a stack of Flurry — confirmed via direct testing; has reportedly been "wonky for months" — [[d:warrior#1483173958638506176]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1483174591248728255]] (Velanir, "go ahead tested"), [[d:warrior#1483184845889863771]] (Painfool, "for months").
- **[Slam avoidance]** Casting Slam does not fully disable dodge/parry the way a real spell cast bar would, contrary to the common assumption that "casting = 0 avoidance" — confirmed via in-game testing during this chunk (see Rotation: single target) — [[d:warrior#1483657137929457804]] onward.
- **[Item proc stacking]** On-hit AP/Str/SP procs from custom Turtle items (e.g. Untamed Blade, Blood Fury racial) are a known long-standing issue for not stacking with equivalent vanilla/VMaNGOS procs (Juju Power, Crusader enchant, Strength of Earth Totem); previously fixed for Blood Fury vs. Molten Emberstone/Totem of the Stonebreaker — [[d:warrior#1481321194216231064]] (Fat Pibble with Air Pods), [[d:warrior#1481311664975515668]] (Zhafu), [[d:warrior#1481326228345716806]] (Zhafu, re: prior fix).
- **[Testing artifact]** Level-1 training dummies give abnormally high crit chance above 315 weapon skill, which skews naive DPS comparisons (e.g., axes appearing to crit far more than expected) — not representative of real raid-target behavior — [[d:warrior#1483193445529948341]] (Piemelbrie / siswet), [[d:warrior#1483194280850886888]] (30).
- **[Patch-day bug, Flurry]** For a period right after the 2026-03-20 patch, Flurry stacks stopped being consumed at all (effectively a permanent 3-stack buff); acknowledged by a mod/dev as a known bug, fixed on the next server restart — [[d:warrior#1484623736580870146]] (Lhian), [[d:warrior#1484623813454073856]] (Velanir), [[d:warrior#1484635426726609007]] (Lhian).
- **[Patch-day bug, Slam]** Multiple players reported temporarily losing the Slam ability from their action bars/spellbook right after the patch; fix involved checking the General tab of the spellbook (Slam had moved there), deleting the WDB cache folder, or a full client reinstall — [[d:warrior#1484588756039237642]] (Nopenogain), [[d:warrior#1484589138316365914]] (Jerso), [[d:warrior#1484589358609731725]] (Jerso, found it in General tab/inventory), [[d:warrior#1484927890687787149]] (limoseng/Haken/Khang, recurrence days later).
- **[Patch-day bug, Slam GCD]** Slam's cast/cooldown time was reported as not properly changing when Improved Slam was talented right after the patch — reported once, resolution unclear — [[d:warrior#1484647562144251954]] (karolinka boża).
- **[Item transmog quirk, persists]** Community continued to want the sheathing-position transmog change (see 1.18.1 changes section) reverted so weapons tmog'd to Thunderfury's appearance always sheathe on the back, rather than following the new "appearance dictates sheath position" rule — [[d:warrior#1485202867332579378]] (Azeroth).
