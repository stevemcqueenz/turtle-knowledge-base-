## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[general]** Sweeping Strikes was hotfixed mid-patch: official note quoted "Fixed an issue where Sweeping Strikes benefited twice from damage modifiers." Mechanism explained: "% modifiers like enrage/dw. The base swing has a damage bonus, which is copied to the sweeping strike. Then the ss itself gets the damage mod again" — existed "since the server started," patched abruptly — [[d:warrior#1463496080086929565]], [[d:warrior#1463685877497593906]] (Donka/goreblaster, 2026-01-21; confirmed by 30 [[d:warrior#1463319710522413108]]). New tooltip after fix, quoted verbatim: "Your next 5 abilities and main-hand melee attacks might strike an additional nearby opponent for 20% of the damage" — [[d:warrior#1463776864144199833]] (casey). Post-fix, observed SS hits rarely exceed ~300 (down from multi-thousand crits) — [[d:warrior#1463778406092111942]] (casey). Disputed: Donka argues this was never a "bug" but core vanilla SS math (main target's post-modifier number is recalculated for the swept target) and the nerf is a pure PvE-driven PvP nerf (SS still doesn't proc off absorbed swings) — [[d:warrior#1463540142781239570]] (Donka, disputed by Guts/30/Lhian who say warrior has more on-demand %-modifiers than vanilla, making the interaction disproportionate — [[d:warrior#1463541314737012757]]).
- **[general]** Slam/Flurry haste double-dip formula fixed: wrong formula was `2.5 x 0.7`, correct is `2.5 / 1.3`; non-bugged haste ≈30%, bugged ≈43% (Flurry only); Slam should be ~1.92s cast with Flurry and no other haste (later estimated 1.8-1.9s after the fix). Same underlying bug also affects other classes (Everlasting Affliction, Accelerated Arcana, Rapid Deterioration), fix planned "across the board" — [[d:warrior#1462040333981323327]], [[d:warrior#1462030581717008558]] (Guts/Lhian, 2026-01-17). Landed 2026-01-23: Slam became ~0.165s slower with Flurry in one player's haste gear, but Slam's damage itself was untouched — "still the same thing, you just benefit way less. So if ur bad u get punished less" (Guts); 2h Fury remained strong/competitive, not gutted — [[d:warrior#1464355611406762224]], [[d:warrior#1464396328221020387]] (roboclam/Guts, 2026-01-23). Consequence: the 2% haste shoulder enchant is no longer clearly BiS for 2h fury vs. the "Saph" enchant now that haste double-dips less on Slam — [[d:warrior#1464391776650399951]] (Sylvaira).
- **[general]** Stance-swapping no longer procs Windfury Totem (separate bugfix, same window) — [[d:warrior#1463316562495799450]] (Jakeends, single source).
- **[general]** Hamstring's cooldown (a TWoW-only addition, unpopular since introduction) and Improved Hamstring talent were both removed/reverted, restoring vanilla no-CD Hamstring — "it's back to its original form now," "universally hated and complained about by warriors since it happened" — [[d:warrior#1464451015595262104]], [[d:warrior#1464447976700117204]] (Yorb/Virasan, 2026-01-24).
- **[general]** Weapon-specialization racial/skill bonus reduced from +5 to +3 weapon skill; endgame items to compensate with extra weapon skill, plus the existing 250g weapon-skill quest — [[d:warrior#1464584547197784095]] (Foreseen, 2026-01-24; single source).
- **[arms/fury]** Confirmed 2026-01-23 patch note, quoted verbatim: "Arms/Fury Warrior Tier 3 (4 Set): Effect changed to the following: 'Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your abilities by 4%.'" — [[d:warrior#1464347845950963873]] (Don't ping me). Same patch: Cleave rage cost reduced and Whirlwind cooldown reduced — [[d:warrior#1464348413503209629]] (evella). Made T3 4-piece worth using again in Naxx (roboclam measured "~40-50k more damage per Naxx run" from it) — [[d:warrior#1464422501651185787]].
- **[prot]** Confirmed 2026-01-23/24 tank buff: Defiance's max threat bonus raised from 15% to 20% (per point 3%→4%), and Shield Slam got a threat-modifier and ~33% AP-scaling buff. Exact dev-quoted math: "5/5 Defiance: Defensive stance has a 130% * 120% = 156.0% threat modifier. 180% of the Defensive stance bonus (56.0%) is 100.8%. 200.8% * the 80% threat modifier of Battle or Berserker stance = the final ratio of damage to threat done of 160.64%" — [[d:warrior#1464421792730054788]] (30, confirmed accurate by dev Lhian [[d:warrior#1464422567145115699]]). Estimates of overall Deftac TPS gain range from "5-10%" (Virasan, cautious) to "20-25%" (L0s7, detailed math: Shield Slam threat 912→1197, +31%) to disputed by Lans ("Deep prot isn't getting 20% increase") — [[d:warrior#1464351457259163678]], [[d:warrior#1464972755840667709]], [[d:warrior#1464969273226301595]]. Same-patch: Champion buff items (Proclaim/Empower/Champion's Grace/Bond/Revive Champion) were removed entirely — [[d:warrior#1464368423885869251]] (Thirtyseven). Druid nerfed same patch, quoted: "Reduced the threat modifier of Maul by 15%. Reduced the threat modifier of Savage Bite by 12%." — [[d:warrior#1464423897574277325]] (30). Community verdict after testing: Deftac now sims very close to FuryProt with the buffs (30: "close to the same as furyprot with the same gear" [[d:warrior#1464389847509897236]]; Weirdthing: Deftac sims with 4k more armor, no Deathwish needed, ~600 more HP than fprot for similar threat [[d:warrior#1464384904706920448]]), but Deftac's off-tank damage/threat remains weak ("deftac will still have the problem of low dmg when OT" — 30 [[d:warrior#1464424229934993654]]). Widely called a "light touch"/small numbers-only pass; a larger Prot rework is expected later, Arms rework comes first — [[d:warrior#1464375838320361635]], [[d:warrior#1464852161497399412]] (Lipibidy).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[tank]** Consensus: FuryProt is the strongest warrior tanking spec for Naxx/Kara, doing ~30% more threat than Defensive Tactics for similar gear requirements (Black Hat) pre-1.18.1 buffs — [[d:warrior#1462214145469452539]], [[d:warrior#1462755499429724263]] (30/Black Hat, 2026-01-16/19). Post-1.18.1 buffs, Deftac closes much of that gap (see changes section) and some predict ImpaleProt (deep prot w/ Impale) could become "genuinely decent," though this is speculative/unconfirmed as of patch notes alone — [[d:warrior#1464814144992841821]] (Velanir, 2026-01-24).
- **[tank]** Deftac is viable for "the majority of raid groups"/"99% of raids" if competent, and is noob-friendlier — [[d:warrior#1462213928762212458]], [[d:warrior#1462756550329372682]] (30/Black Hat). Disputed by Kyrsie, who calls prot the "worst tank spec to generate threat with in TWOW" and argues against using it in a top raid — [[d:warrior#1462212212637565029]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-16). A very geared Deftac tank ("30"'s raid, top rogues/warriors/mages) still reports struggling to hold threat, attributed partly to Shield Slam's miss/dodge/parry causing threat gaps — [[d:warrior#1465269638806896856]], [[d:warrior#1465270044132118588]] (30, 2026-01-26).
- **[tank]** Revenge/Reprisal deep prot considered worse than Dtactics in a proper raid environment; low-gear exception noted — [[d:warrior#1462248661537783880]], [[d:warrior#1462277341748138137]] (Virasan, 2026-01-16).
- **[tank]** Don't go FuryProt too early (before T2.5/AQ40 gear) — extra incoming damage can wreck raid progress; stay Deep Prot with DPS/threat gear swapped in as threat becomes the bottleneck; swap once "juicy" T2.5 gear is available — [[d:warrior#1463067390098018540]], [[d:warrior#1463068222734733345]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-20).
- **[tank]** Warrior tanking overall: cost/benefit model where threat and mitigation trade off directly (unlike other tanks); bears considered the most overtuned tank as of this chunk (nerfed same patch, see above) — [[d:warrior#1462496488700645376]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-19).
- **[2h fury]** 2h Fury's patch-defining strength was largely from the (now-fixed) SS double-dip bug plus the Flurry/haste double-dip on Slam; post-fix it remains strong/BiS-tier for PvE, "still going to mog," DW only "maybe slightly better" — [[d:warrior#1464381162456617086]] (axelfrog, 2026-01-23). On Nordanaar (progressed realm) 2h Fury > DW; on Ambershire (newer server) 2h Fury lacks a great weapon option currently — [[d:warrior#1463834872073294007]], [[d:warrior#1463834803794215028]] (Guts, 2026-01-22).
- **[dw fury]** "DW fury is great and is only gonna get better" per the sim author — [[d:warrior#1465015568775254233]] (Don't ping me, 2026-01-25). "Ambershire fury warriors having the time of their lives" this phase in both PvE and PvP — [[d:warrior#1462132135128465613]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-17).
- **[arms]** Arms was overpowered a previous patch via a pure-Execute-spam build (Execute doesn't scale with gear so low-ilvl players competed); nerfed since. Post-1.18.1 Arms gets +4% ability crit damage (T3 4pc) but community is split on whether this is enough — several (Painfool, Sylvaira) feel it doesn't overcome Flurry/DW's dps and 2h still requires "looking at a castbar" — [[d:warrior#1464515903084564483]], [[d:warrior#1464395155095293973]] (Painfool, 2026-01-23/24). Lipibidy is more optimistic: "Arms looks fun again... it could possibly [compete] already" — [[d:warrior#1464509974032945225]].
- **[pvp]** Warrior PvP generally viewed as weak/behind other classes: needs top-tier gear to be competitive at all — [[d:warrior#1464388111755772007]] (Thirtyseven, 2026-01-24). Deep prot in particular does negligible PvP damage/CC — [[d:warrior#1462216541381726321]] (Kyrsie). "Warrior tank fall-off is real" — several (Velanir, Lans) maintank on Druid instead because results are meaningfully worse even near-BiS — [[d:warrior#1463320592768962580]], [[d:warrior#1463320960764739709]] (Velanir, 2026-01-21).
- **[speedrun]** Guts (2h fury/arms) and Yurek empirically tested and claim: without the Champion raid buff, a warrior (Yurek) still beat a rogue with double-shaman support AND the Champion buff active; conclusion offered: "Warrior is the BEST class for overall damage in speedruns, still" — caveated by 30 that this holds specifically for that speedrun context (only one rogue run; a second rogue for IEA support would flip it) — [[d:warrior#1465427998395076648]], [[d:warrior#1465428592807772303]], [[d:warrior#1465431096643551404]] (Guts/30, 2026-01-26).

## Leveling
- **[general]** Turtle WoW makes DW leveling notably easier than in vanilla; sword+shield leveling is much slower than either DW or 2h — [[d:warrior#1463914915021590733]], [[d:warrior#1463914966510735616]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-22).
- **[general]** "Hamstring kiting": fight one mob at a time, prioritize Stamina/Spirit gear early for less downtime, keep food stocked, level First Aid; when mob #1 is low, kite toward mob #2 if healthy enough — "Warrior is weak early, one miss/dodge/parry can be your death" — [[d:warrior#1464992683176230932]] (Shortmo, 2026-01-24; video referenced for a Blizzard server but "similar logic applies here"). Aim for green mobs while leveling if struggling, and don't fight 1 mob at a time from ~30-60 — pull 2+ and level on greens for faster overall XP — [[d:warrior#1464993090824700059]], [[d:warrior#1465267691349868606]] (Thirtyseven/VeganCourage, 2026-01-24/26).
- **[tank]** On a pull: Battle Shout up, Demoralizing Shout on first GCD once mobs are on you, then Sunder to 5 stacks if mob HP is above 50% — [[d:warrior#1461189867944808560]], [[d:warrior#1464993090296217805]] (Lokimanor/Virasan). Thunder Clap ("clap") is useful while leveling as prot to hold AoE aggro in dungeons — [[d:warrior#1463227094623584409]] (notfrog, 2026-01-20). Leveling Prot in dungeons at 30-40: simple sunder/shield-bash-anyone-not-on-you works; "best method is to play Arms" for early tanking aggro if not full prot yet — [[d:warrior#1463003846216519878]], [[d:warrior#1463003880622391408]] (my legs are okay/styr). Leveling Prot is "viable from 30 on, kinda slower obviously" — [[d:warrior#1465128550725324994]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-25).
- **[general]** Progressive rotation as abilities unlock: before Sunder, spam Heroic Strike; once you have Sunder, stack it and auto-attack with HS dumps; once you get Slam, cast it with Overpower on procs; once you get WW/Cleave/Sweeping Strikes, pull multiple mobs and "ball" them up — [[d:warrior#1465267121289166911]] (VeganCourage, 2026-01-26; single source but detailed).
- **[general]** Tactical Mastery (retain rage across stance swaps) is called near-mandatory from ~level 25-30: "Warrior as a class can barely function without it" for stance-swapping (Charge/Intercept/Rend/Overpower access) while leveling and questing — [[d:warrior#1464778584555130900]], [[d:warrior#1464778676829688011]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-24). Improved Rend is "a talent point tax for Deep Wounds and Impale," not good standalone — [[d:warrior#1464777605327880406]].
- **[general]** Slam pauses (does not reset) the white-swing timer: swing timer and Slam's cast count down together; the queued auto-attack fires the tick after Slam finishes — [[d:warrior#1462671024561586249]], [[d:warrior#1462671492255846506]] (axelfrog, 2026-01-19).
- **[leveling]** Charge-fall-break (avoiding fall damage by Charging near landing) is much harder on Turtle's client than vanilla/other pservers, requiring precise timing; wait until near jump-height or near max Intercept range for reduced knockback — [[d:warrior#1461939554826195171]], [[d:warrior#1461954785283538995]] (Kyrsie/Lipibidy, 2026-01-17; casey confirms other pservers don't have this issue).
- **[open question]** Which 1h/2h weapon type is best for leveling given Arms-tree weapon specs (axe for crit, mace for armor pen, sword for extra attacks)? — unanswered — [[d:warrior#1465168870439391253]] (ysocereal, question unanswered).

## Rotation: single target
- **[2h fury]** Empirical dummy-parse test (VeganCourage, slow 3.8-speed 2h weapon, 5-min tests) ranked rotations by total damage: (1) 176k — Slam>Auto>Slam>Auto>Slam>Auto>WW>BT>skip Slam>Auto; (2) 156k — Slam>Auto>Slam>Auto>Slam>Auto>BT>Slam>Auto (risks clipping the last auto); (3) 153k — Slam>Auto repeated (no WW/BT at all), "surprisingly not far behind." Conclusion: replace the would-be 4th Slam with WW+BT — despite both being "9 hits," rotation (1) beat (2) by ~20k damage over 5 min, likely because it's foolproof against auto-clipping — [[d:warrior#1465391439323201680]], [[d:warrior#1465391753866641458]] (VeganCourage, 2026-01-26).
- **[2h fury]** Contested swing-timer discipline: one heuristic from sim testing suggests firing Slam only when >~40% of the swing timer remains, to avoid clipping the auto — [[d:warrior#1463235335243960391]] (Not Normal, single source, from sim experimentation). Counter-view: Kyrsie argues you may lose little dps by delaying an auto and "wouldn't be surprised if you do more dps by spamming Slam and not caring about swing timer progress" — [[d:warrior#1465369289187135642]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-26). Cristian's practical tip to a struggling 2h fury player: "ignore swing timer when using Slam if you're not low on threat/rage — just spam Slam more (single target), and don't use the [swing-timer-gated] Slam macro" — [[d:warrior#1464602650959151225]] (Cristian, 2026-01-23). "Slam and Bloodthirst do equal damage, so single-target damage ends up looking similar" between 2h and DW builds — [[d:warrior#1464592631756619858]] (Yurek).
- **[dw fury]** DW is "the best way to execute": two auto-attacks bring rage to full already, so an Execute reliably consumes the full pool; 2h players must carefully time the GCD with the swing timer to dump full rage into Execute — [[d:warrior#1464451477706899528]], [[d:warrior#1464452003965964321]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-24).
- **[general]** Execute damage scaling clarified: Execute doesn't scale with anything but rage and Armor Penetration; armor debuffs (IEA/CoR/FF) increase the flat damage dealt (target takes more overall) but don't change Execute's rage-conversion scaling; only Boundless Anger (talent) and % damage modifiers increase that scaling; more Str/Agi → more rage from autos → more rage converted into Execute damage — [[d:warrior#1465257447110217819]], [[d:warrior#1465258953825845414]], [[d:warrior#1465260166067785901]] (axelfrog/30, 2026-01-26).
- **[2h fury, unconfirmed]** Proposed rotation without Bloodthirst (i.e. 2h without DW's finisher): ST "WW > auto > Slam > auto" — untested/unconfirmed community suggestion — [[d:warrior#1462928828090290229]], [[d:warrior#1464669442960130375]] (Mario, question repeated twice unanswered).
- **[trash management]** For 2h fury on boss ST vs. trash: don't over-sunder the same target across a long fight (200+ sunders as 2h fury on one boss is wasteful); sunder different mobs to enable more cleave; on single-mob-left, Execute — [[d:warrior#1464601577242234881]], [[d:warrior#1464601052153249818]], [[d:warrior#1464602627231711303]] (Thirtyseven/Doranbolt, 2026-01-23).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general]** Hit cap: base 8%, reduced by 1% per 5 weapon skill above the baseline — [[d:warrior#1462450619959742474]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-18).
- **[general]** Glancing-blow math (vs a level-62 target), from detailed sim-author breakdown: at 300 weapon skill, glance occurs 30% of the time, lowroll 0.80x, highroll 0.90x, average glance penalty 0.85x; at 305 skill (the default after the weapon-skill quest), lowroll rises to 0.91x, highroll 0.99x — meaning weapon skill beyond the quest-default barely helps glance damage vs. level 62 anymore. No player character can land a crushing blow (mobs only). ## Tanking specifics
- **[general]** Lipibidy's thesis (well-received, no pushback): a tank spec's viability is fundamentally about threat; warrior split "the characteristics of a good tank spec" across 3 different specs (Deftac = mitigation, ImpaleProt = crit-threat, FuryProt = max threat), which is why "warrior tanking feels dysfunctional overall" — trying to build variety purely via mitigation-for-threat trades is "ultimately flawed" — [[d:warrior#1464776789690679369]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-24).
- **[general]** Stat priority for a Naxx hateful/general tank (from an actively-tanking source): Stamina > Armor > Parry > Dodge/Defense > Block — [[d:warrior#1462838277068296448]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-19).
- **[general]** T1's 3-piece set bonus is excellent for Shield Block Value (relevant to Dtactics); beyond that, prioritize DPS/threat pieces even while tanking — [[d:warrior#1465387191839756410]] (Amy, 2026-01-25).
- **[general]** When mixing mitigation/threat gear, start swapping toward threat with Bracers, then Helm/Legs (especially with a "ZG enchant"); a couple of thick-stam pieces (e.g. T3 tank boots/helm) can be kept for survivability without sacrificing much threat; T3 tank pants have high enough Block Value to be worth using even in an otherwise threat-oriented set — [[d:warrior#1463636360014463081]], [[d:warrior#1463635393395163298]], [[d:warrior#1463636814287081604]] (Sylvaira/Jakeends, 2026-01-21).
- **[deftac]** Dtactics scales Shield Slam via threat stats mostly, not mitigation — wearing full mitigation/block gear wastes the spec's design — [[d:warrior#1463047309993443371]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-20).
- **[ot]** When off-tanking, run a DPS (Fury) spec with some mitigation gear rather than deep prot, since Deftac's off-tank threat/damage remains weak even post-buff — [[d:warrior#1464424376442159313]], [[d:warrior#1464424229934993654]] (JohnGood/30, 2026-01-23/26).
- **[fury prot]** Backup/off-weapon choices when avoiding an unwanted Thunderfury proc-pull (e.g. KT lightning adds, AQ40 Twins scarabs): anything 60+ dps and fast is fine — ES Hammer, Crystal Blossom, Widow's Remorse, Iblis, Hungering Cold all work; if you also have Hungering Cold alongside Thunderfury, consider fully committing to FuryProot — [[d:warrior#1463048796408905824]], [[d:warrior#1463050826787459093]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-20).
- **[naxx]** Razuvious pull technique (detailed, from an experienced raider): have a Shadow Priest (best range) Mind Control an add from max range while Razuvious is far from the raid; stand near a wall/corner; when MC'd, bring the add back toward the caster; a hunter Rank-1 Arcane Shots Razuvious to redirect aggro onto the MC'd add; avoid Tremor Totem/dispels breaking the MC; the priest must click the pet-bar ability (not a keybind) to avoid breaking control via movement — [[d:warrior#1462620733166325760]] through [[d:warrior#1462621245249028168]] (30, 2026-01-19).
- **[naxx]** Heigan positioning: DPS should hold off attacking while he's casting, since aggro gets dicey mid-platform-dance; a bad pull can wipe dps that stayed in range — [[d:warrior#1462060310541369589]], [[d:warrior#1462062701219020831]] (Velanir, 2026-01-17).
- **[k10]** Kara10 (Lower Karazhan) is tankable in pre-raid BiS; the boss to watch is Clawlord Howlfang, who cannot be stat-checked in pre-raid gear (must be handled mechanically); nothing else hits hard except Ghost Knights and Moroes; a missing mage/druid decurser will cause heavy damage from stacking curses — [[d:warrior#1463944367894036656]], [[d:warrior#1463944462890700976]], [[d:warrior#1463944675101774018]] (Kyrsie/Virasan, 2026-01-21).

Manual stance-swap macros are recommended over "auto stance-swap" addons, since several skills are usable in multiple stances and an automatic swap can waste rage — put `/cast Battle Stance` (or the desired stance) at the end of your ability macros instead — [[d:warrior#1465308094228598857]], [[d:warrior#1465308754340876500]] (VeganCourage, 2026-01-26).
- **[macros]** Fury-Prot weapon+stance swap in one macro: `/startattack` / `/equip "MH name"` / `/equip "OH name"` / `/cast Battle Stance` — [[d:warrior#1465021470567825641]] (Shortmo, 2026-01-25). Stance toggle (Battle<->Defensive) macro: `/run texture,name,isActive,isCastable = GetShapeshiftFormInfo(1); if isActive then CastSpellByName("Defensive Stance"); else CastSpellByName("Battle Stance"); end` — [[d:warrior#1464979093467566110]] (Voldrine, 2026-01-24). A proposed (untested) swing-timer-gated Slam macro was also shared: `/run -- CastSpellByName("Slam")` / `/startattack` / `/run if st_timer>UnitAttackSpeed"player"*0.5 then CastSpellByName("Slam") end` — [[d:warrior#1465370160612511905]] (Jerso, 2026-01-26; unverified syntax).
- **[general]** DistanceDisplay addon (github.com/uc9089/DistanceDisplay) for on-screen range-to-target — [[d:warrior#1465123770124730562]] (D.C.V., 2026-01-25). "Better Character Stats" addon only partially reads real game data — armor/AP are 100% accurate (read directly from a game function) but hit/miss % with DW spec is guessed/half-estimated and can be wrong — [[d:warrior#1463448312459034635]], [[d:warrior#1463448358424281282]] (30, 2026-01-16).
- **[sim tools]** Two community warrior sims exist: Thrunk's (thrunk112.github.io/WarriorSim-TurtleWoW) — good for DW, historically unreliable for 2h/Slam — and JRC's fork (jrc13245.github.io/WarriorSim-TurtleWoW), which added 2h/Slam support and is kept in sync with patch changes (a `turtle181.html` preliminary 1.18.1 version was posted with the T3 4pc bonus and slam/flurry change modeled) — [[d:warrior#1461469391798403287]], [[d:warrior#1461785119994937416]], [[d:warrior#1464408634699284531]] (30/Don't ping me, throughout). By late in the chunk the two sims' Slam calculations were reported to be "now identical" — [[d:warrior#1463294625933562030]] (Don't ping me, 2026-01-20).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[fixed]** Sweeping Strikes double-dipped % damage modifiers (Enrage/Deathwish/Champion/2h-spec, etc.) since server launch; hotfixed 2026-01-21 (see 1.18.1 changes for full detail and the community dispute over whether it was ever really a "bug").
- **[fixed]** Flurry/Slam haste formula was wrong (`2.5 x 0.7` instead of `2.5 / 1.3`), inflating Slam's speed-up under Flurry (43% effective haste instead of ~30%); fixed 2026-01-23, Slam became ~0.165s slower but its damage was untouched.
- **[fixed]** Stance-swapping used to proc Windfury Totem; fixed alongside the SS patch, same window.
- **[fixed/reverted]** Hamstring's cooldown (a TWoW-only, unpopular addition) and Improved Hamstring were removed 2026-01-23/24, restoring the original no-CD vanilla Hamstring.
- **[open bug]** One-Handed Weapon Spec talent still doesn't apply its damage bonus to all damage while wielding a 1h weapon — only affects the weapon's own hits, unlike the paladin equivalent it's modeled on; reportedly "used to work correctly" — [[d:warrior#1462717953182138433]], [[d:warrior#1465398472516698186]] (Lipibidy, repeated single source).
- **[bug, unresolved]** Deep Wounds is separately reported to have its own damage double-dip bug (distinct from the SS one), unresolved as of this chunk — [[d:warrior#1462622343481262316]] (MoneyCow, 2026-01-19; single source, no further detail given).
- **[quirk]** Thunder Clap is coded as a spell (uses spell crit, no spell-power coefficient) despite dealing physical damage, and cannot proc Deep Wounds even on a crit — see Rotation: AoE section.
- **[quirk]** Diamond Flask's healing scaling appears heavily reduced/miscalibrated vs. its stated healing-power coefficient on Turtle (player math, unconfirmed) — see Consumables section.
- **[quirk]** Shieldrender trinket's proc source is disputed: most say autos-only, "30" says it also procs from Cleave — unresolved in this chunk.
- **[quirk/design]** T2.5/T3+ warrior "DPS" plate gear is itemized with Spirit + AP rather than the traditional Str/Agi/Stam/Hit/Crit split used on T1/T2, widely criticized as "dead stats" that also keep the DPS sets low-stam (discouraging their use for tanking); Helm of Endless Rage was restatted (lost str/agi/stam for hit) to become an endgame-relevant item, also controversial — [[d:warrior#1463969552500264961]], [[d:warrior#1463972833183928423]], [[d:warrior#1463972519487738001]] (Lipibidy/30/Sebastian_, 2026-01-22). One (unconfirmed, secondhand) explanation offered: warrior was intentionally denied strong DPS-tier itemization from T2.5 onward because it was already the best raid DPS when those tiers were designed — [[d:warrior#1463970198007578634]] (Sebastian_, single source, unconfirmed insider claim).
- **[quirk]** Warrior's T3 8-piece set bonus is tied to the same 30-minute shared Recklessness/Shield Wall/Retaliation cooldown, making it usable far less often than other classes' 5-piece bonuses which are constantly active — widely cited as one of the worst-designed set bonuses in the game — [[d:warrior#1463986476986531905]], [[d:warrior#1463989604284760276]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-22/23).
