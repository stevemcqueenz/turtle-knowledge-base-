## Leveling
- **[general]** Arms is regarded as the best low-gear/leveling tree; Fury is "perfectly viable" too — [[d:warrior#1471020023043850405]] [[d:warrior#1471020551593267332]] [[d:warrior#1471644296850440202]] [[d:warrior#1471644437032472688]] (Lipibidy/axelfrog, 2026-02-11/13).
- **[dps]** Don't use Slam much while leveling (too slow); use Mortal Strike and Whirlwind, and only follow up with Slam if rage allows — [[d:warrior#1471020023043850405]] [[d:warrior#1471020058716147782]] (Lipibidy, 2026-02-11). Counterpoint for pure 2h leveling: Slam is worth using once available since it is a separate attack that also generates rage (HS only spends rage) — [[d:warrior#1472569602193227776]] [[d:warrior#1472569908666564802]] (VeganCourage, 2026-02-16; slower weapon = better Slam value).
- **[general]** Fury with Blood Drinker (from ~level 35) plus Deathwish/Bloodrage on cooldown removes almost all downtime between mobs — [[d:warrior#1471991775483920386]] [[d:warrior#1471991804433010689]] [[d:warrior#1473012087352459386]] [[d:warrior#1473012894856773829]] (Kyrsie/Velanir, 2026-02-14/16).

- **[general]** Racials for leveling/general play: Night Elf edges out Human — weapon skill is easy to get, 1% crit "mini-cd," and the 1% haste always helps (esp. faster Slam windows once you have haste gear); dodge helps tanks but doesn't generate rage when it procs; racial impact is small overall in PvE — [[d:warrior#1471125474695708928]] (limoseng/Haken/Khang, 2026-02-11).
- **[professions]** Engineering is generally preferred over Blacksmithing while leveling because abundant custom mission/quest reward gear covers most upgrades already; BS becomes more clearly worthwhile at dungeon/raid level; can level both — [[d:warrior#1472629783195029636]] [[d:warrior#1472634898572509315]] (RuitX, 2026-02-16).
- **[general]** First Aid + mining for bandage/gold funding, and keeping weapon up to date, recommended early — [[d:warrior#1470543760495476888]] (Rebelled, 2026-02-10).
- **[tank]** As a fresh-60 leveling/dungeon tank, any spec can tank dungeons; you don't need Goblin Brainwasher/dual-spec unless swapping specs often — you can just carry sword+board on a dps spec — [[d:warrior#1470985310065397920]] [[d:warrior#1471287715449405584]] (worlds #1 ppm expert / कट्टर शत्रु, 2026-02-11).
- **[tank]** Prot leveling gets you Shield Slam earlier (relatively) than dps specs get their main damage tool, cited as part of why some genuinely prefer leveling Prot — [[d:warrior#1474710738357456916]] [[d:warrior#1474710802593480764]] (Velanir, 2026-02-21).
- **[dps 2h]** Once you have Slam, timing it right after a swing lands both the Slam and the white/auto hit, giving damage roughly comparable to Heroic Strike while still generating rage from the auto (unlike HS, which only spends rage) — makes Slam the better choice over HS in this situation; priority-wise, Slam beats Rend but Sunder still comes first — [[d:warrior#1474975775479562512]] [[d:warrior#1474976006413746348]] [[d:warrior#1474976259514568857]] [[d:warrior#1474976895522181200]] (Fyzix/Thirtyseven, 2026-02-22). - **[general]** Battleground grinding (queueing AB/WSG) is a much faster alternative to questing for xp, at the cost of gold income: roughly 4% xp per capture-tick in Arathi Basin, and each flag-cap in Warsong Gulch is worth about a same-level quest turn-in — [[d:warrior#1474789541033410672]] [[d:warrior#1474790113673216010]] [[d:warrior#1474790361703387248]] [[d:warrior#1474790923782328340]] (Faithroll, 2026-02-21).

- **[dps 2h]** Don't macro Slam into your swing — just spam it manually; only worth clipping the auto-attack slightly with Slam if you are NOT rage-starved (a small dps gain) — [[d:warrior#1471332098681082002]] [[d:warrior#1471332489501868104]] (Akanamu, 2026-02-11).
- **[general]** Purpose of Improved Slam: with a slow weapon (e.g. 3.5s) and a 2.5s Slam cast, there's enough time to weave Slam and still land Mortal Strike/Whirlwind on the same swing without clipping — [[d:warrior#1470552118653096110]] [[d:warrior#1470552789590741045]] (Arzon/30, 2026-02-10).
- **[dps]** Example smart-Slam macro (swing-timer based, needs SuperWoW for accuracy): `#showtooltip Slam` / `/startattack` / `/run if not PlayerFrame.inCombat then AttackTarget() end` / `/run if st_timer and (st_timer>UnitAttackSpeed"player"*0.45) then CastSpellByName("Slam") end` — [[d:warrior#1470880971057266819]] (Delf, +1, 2026-02-11); without SuperWoW the swing timer is only guessed — [[d:warrior#1471327770087915562]] (30, 2026-02-11).
- **[dps, open]** Dual-wield weapon-order anomaly: a player's sim showed +20 dps from swapping which weapon (CTS vs Maladath) was mainhand vs offhand, suspected to relate to Windfury's internal cooldown or slower-offhand-for-Flurry interactions — unresolved — [[d:warrior#1471458705290563789]] [[d:warrior#1471460501962621041]] (Captain Caboose/Piemelbrie, 2026-02-12; question unanswered).
- **[dps 2h fury]** Naxx trash/Sweeping Strikes opener technique: open with Sweeping Strikes then sit in Berserker stance; re-press SS if trash is still alive ~30s later; ideally trigger the pull's first SS off a Charge-auto rather than a standing cast (saves rage), or use Bloodrage to fund it from 0 rage if you can't Charge — [[d:warrior#1473966467384086528]] [[d:warrior#1473966515840618506]] [[d:warrior#1473972317620015106]] [[d:warrior#1473971189872595025]] (Lipibidy/VeganCourage, 2026-02-19). A more elaborate version: auto-attack once off the Charge (to not waste the first SS charge on it), activate SS, swap to Berserker, then macro Bloodrage+Whirlwind and queue Cleave if rage allows — this maximizes SS damage but risks double-dipping SS with Execute or losing Cleave/Execute value, so use with that trade-off in mind — [[d:warrior#1473974546813161474]] [[d:warrior#1473990057097564210]] [[d:warrior#1473990127142572043]] [[d:warrior#1473990245165826145]] (Painfool/Guts, 2026-02-19). Lower-risk alternative: swap to Berserker stance during the Charge itself (Charge → SS → Berserker → auto), which gets one weaker auto-hit that doesn't benefit from Impale — [[d:warrior#1473997570970882068]] [[d:warrior#1473997655884562463]] (Guts, 2026-02-19).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[tank]** Hit cap: 8% baseline melee hit, reduced by 0.2% for every point of weapon skill above 300 (no breakpoints besides the 315 weapon-skill cap) — confirmed twice — [[d:warrior#1471550699132293294]] [[d:warrior#1473075129209716820]] (Ascuz, 2026-02-12; Velanir, 2026-02-17).
- **[tank]** Dual-wield FuryProt tanking target: ~13% hit and 316 weapon skill — [[d:warrior#1471541881564299264]] (ScoobertDoobert, +5, 2026-02-12).
- **[general]** Weapon skill has two effects: (1) lowers glancing-blow damage penalty on white/auto damage only — yellow/skill damage (incl. Heroic Strike) can never glance, so this matters little for a deep-prot tank who mostly presses skills/HS; (2) every 5 weapon-skill points lowers your hit-cap requirement by 1% for skill-damage attacks — [[d:warrior#1470893473254084852]] (Lipibidy, 2026-02-11).
- **[dps]** Undead racial stone gives 108 AP (vs. standard 100 AP on the elemental/enchant stone); crit needs to be valued above ~54 AP to prefer the crit-stone over the AP-stone for 2h fury — [[d:warrior#1472261024861389034]] [[d:warrior#1472261069237129229]] (Velanir, 2026-02-12).
- **[general]** PPM (procs per minute) mechanic on TWoW/vanilla: the underlying proc chance is fixed to the weapon's base (unhasted) speed, but haste increases total swings per minute and therefore total procs — this differs from the WotLK+ "self-activating internal-cooldown" proc model; extensively confirmed by multiple posters against one dissenter — [[d:warrior#1472975230485004500]] [[d:warrior#1472975730114695312]] [[d:warrior#1472973271749361734]] [[d:warrior#1472963858887999571]] (worlds #1 ppm expert/Akanamu/Lipibidy/Virasan, 2026-02-16).
- **[tank, disputed]** Windfury Totem's internal cooldown reported by some as ~2s rather than the documented 1.5s, with reports of no consecutive procs on sub-2s swings — unconfirmed — [[d:warrior#1471464562099359899]] [[d:warrior#1471464701169897587]] [[d:warrior#1471507705171542036]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-12).
- **[general]** Flat armor-penetration (ArP) itemization is considered weak/ineffective at current budget levels outside of Shieldrender Talisman; community wants ArP converted to a percentage-based mechanic — [[d:warrior#1471991218396201176]] [[d:warrior#1473059196823011408]] [[d:warrior#1473060049256579195]] (Kyrsie/Blackjack/Donka, 2026-02-12/17).
- **[tank]** Fortune-stat threat value (sim, FuryProt): ~30 Fortune ≈ +115 AP worth of TPS (roughly 1 Fortune ≈ 4 AP); +50 Fortune ≈ +95 TPS specifically from Thunderfury procs — [[d:warrior#1473083250019926288]] [[d:warrior#1473083773456355420]] [[d:warrior#1473085795240775710]] (Velanir/30, 2026-02-17; single-source, early testing).
- **[general]** Bloodthirst's damage formula: 0.35 × Attack Power + 200 at max rank — [[d:warrior#1474097123711651903]] (30, +1, 2026-02-19).
- **[tank]** Heroic Strike's threat formula: threat = (damage + flat threat) × stance threat modifier — e.g. that base value is multiplied by ~1.495 for Defensive Stance with Defiance talented — [[d:warrior#1474055840628604999]] [[d:warrior#1474056274932142325]] (Velanir, 2026-02-19).
- **[general]** Weapon skill is normalized to (target level × 5) for PvP purposes, so its PvE-relevant bonuses/penalties don't meaningfully apply against same-or-lower-level PvP targets — weapon skill "swings between being your best stat and being useless" depending entirely on whether the target is a boss (high level) or a player — [[d:warrior#1474129063000936571]] [[d:warrior#1474129980542812331]] [[d:warrior#1474130799807828191]] (30/Lipibidy, 2026-02-19).
- **[general]** Weapon skill does not reduce glancing-blow damage penalties below 62 skill — its glancing-related benefit only kicks in near/at the higher (tank-relevant) skill ranges — [[d:warrior#1474136812678152339]] (30, 2026-02-19).
- **[general]** Mace-specific "per level" armor-penetration effects scale directly with character level (e.g. 6 ArP/level × 60 = 360 ArP at level 60), worth roughly a 5% damage increase depending on the target's remaining armor — [[d:warrior#1474320024629022840]] [[d:warrior#1474335727851540520]] [[d:warrior#1474337982923145249]] (gastro/casey/Weirdthing, 2026-02-20).
- **[tank]** FuryProt vs. DTac survivability breakdown: FuryProt takes the same +10% physical damage as DTac but 10% LESS spell damage than DTac; however FuryProt loses the Shield Slam block-chance buff and all block entirely, making its incoming damage spikier — Shield Slam's block-chance buff is estimated to prevent roughly 50% of crits when active — [[d:warrior#1473523095468769491]] [[d:warrior#1473523681878605990]] [[d:warrior#1473524008313028669]] (Nost/Lipibidy, 2026-02-17).
- **[tank]** Avoidance (dodge/parry) should not be conflated with mitigation for survivability purposes — it raises damage-intake variance (a healer sees dodge-dodge-crush) rather than lowering average damage taken, so Stamina is generally preferred over small Agility/avoidance gains for a tank — [[d:warrior#1474892000930300048]] (Lipibidy, 2026-02-22).
