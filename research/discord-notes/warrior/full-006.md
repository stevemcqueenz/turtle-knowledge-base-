- **[prot tank]** Tanking as warrior is considered strictly worse (not more complex) than other tank classes: less mitigation and same-or-worse TPS than other two tank specs, main issue is AoE threat — [[d:warrior#1456795924406992989]] (hehe haha, 2026-01-03), [[d:warrior#1456796432333013249]] (hehe haha, 2026-01-03).

Counter-argument: multiple viable tank specs is not inherently contradictory; specs could be differentiated by AoE/utility/survivability rather than forced into one "best" build — [[d:warrior#1458899108134654072]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-08), [[d:warrior#1458900733998137406]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-08), [[d:warrior#1459944449739063509]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-11), [[d:warrior#1459953969949311101]] (Virasan, 2026-01-11), [[d:warrior#1459954399014027503]] (Virasan, 2026-01-11), [[d:warrior#1459964454618796042]] (Sylvaira, 2026-01-11), [[d:warrior#1459949405888184525]] (Jakeends, 2026-01-11).
- **[tanking, practical consensus]** Despite the theory debate, the practical, repeatedly-given advice is: tank in Deep Prot (Revenge/Reprisal build) at low gear (T1/T2) where threat is "fine," then switch to Fury Prot (Defensive Tactics + Impale) once raid DPS (esp. ## Leveling
- **[general]** Recommended leveling path: go Arms, take (Improved) Thunder Clap; low-level dungeons can be tanked in 2H by DPSing for threat, keep a shield-swap macro ready for bosses / when mobs hit hard; author tanked leveling dungeons as Arms until 50+ before respeccing — [[d:warrior#1455880018730156075]] (eric4225, 2025-12-31).
- **[general]** Best way to hold threat in leveling dungeons: mark the kill target with Skull and let DPS "pay the consequences" for not focusing it — [[d:warrior#1455880290772713523]] (eric4225, 2025-12-31).
- **[general]** Talent guidance for the Arms leveling build: avoid Improved Heroic Strike; avoid maxing Tactical Mastery early (put ~2 points into Precision Cut instead, don't max until later); Deflection is good if tanking often (doubles Parry chance, avoids all damage from that attack and hastens next melee); can drop Improved Deflection/Improved Heroic Strike points into Thunder Clap — [[d:warrior#1455894257549443083]] (Arle, 2025-12-31), [[d:warrior#1455894546725736469]] (Arle, 2025-12-31), [[d:warrior#1455916222045491286]] (Arle, 2025-12-31), [[d:warrior#1455887003366195253]] (eric4225, 2025-12-31; +2 reactions).

## Rotation: single target
- **[fury 2h]** Suggested 1-button rotation priority discussion for 2H fury: Slam > Bloodthirst (without slam-clipping) > Whirlwind (without slam-clipping) > Heroic Strike (over 50 rage, just before Slam); Execute usage and cooldown/trinket timing for boss death also asked about — [[d:warrior#1456917651572920504]] (Ugge, 2026-01-03; question, not conclusively answered in-chunk — see Contested).
- **[prot tank]** Single-target threat rotation (60, gear-dependent): Sunder Armor, Concussion Blow, Shield Slam, Revenge as opener/base kit; refined: Concussion Blow > Shield Slam as opener (with Heroic Strike if rage allows), then post-opener priority Shield Slam > Concussion Blow > Heroic Strike — [[d:warrior#1457122046319525999]] (Helorf, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457123304996470917]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457123435695050763]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04).
- **[prot tank]** At low gear, Revenge/Reprisal is better threat than Death Tactics(?)/"dtactics" build; Dtactics (Defensive Tactics-based threat) scales better with threat/DPS stats but trails Revenge threat at low gear; the only useful Dtactics-Improved Thunderclap application is "already questionable value overall" — [[d:warrior#1456108969553690785]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-01), [[d:warrior#1456109210353012909]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-01).
- **[prot tank]** Revenge/Reprisal keeps pace with dtactics in mitigation-heavy (tank) gear sets; getting the most from dtactics requires DPS gear, at which point Fury Prot outperforms both (better consistency/scaling) — [[d:warrior#1456111397816635537]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-01), [[d:warrior#1456111478733017110]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-01; disputed by Nost who "struggles to believe it" [[d:warrior#1456112248580735029]]).
- **[prot tank]** Shield Slam scales with + Shield Block Value: it's a flat bonus to block amount and a flat damage increase to Shield Slam itself — [[d:warrior#1455971985141141757]] (Dodging, 2025-12-31).
- **[prot tank]** Shield Block Value ("SBV") is "good" for threat/dps but gets outscaled by crit/agility fairly hard on other gear slots; shields don't have the same crit tradeoff problem and are usually the only source of SBV, so SBV-heavy shields (e.g., BiS shield "Glyph of Deflection") remain valuable — [[d:warrior#1456025196707184775]] (Dodging, 2026-01-01), [[d:warrior#1456025479030247576]] (Dodging, 2026-01-01), [[d:warrior#1456032406757707837]] (Dodging, 2026-01-01; +1 reaction).
- **[fury dw]** Overpower usage debate: some players fish Windfury Totem procs / Flurry crits with Overpower by macro-swapping to Battle Stance; must return to Berserker immediately and only use with ≤30 rage, never clipping Bloodthirst/Whirlwind, to avoid a net rage/DPS loss; many top DW players skip Overpower entirely as not worth the complexity — [[d:warrior#1457443865886982316]] (JohnGood, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457445065373777971]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04: "it fishes windfury and occasionally crits for flurry"), [[d:warrior#1457446288000946530]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04).
- **[fury general]** Master Strike is considered too expensive (30 rage) for its damage; regarded as a "forced PvP skill" mainly useful to fish Windfury procs via a Hamstring/Sunder/Pummel-style rage dump, and otherwise "ignore it until buffed" — [[d:warrior#1457445419121381540]] (JohnGood, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457445578865901589]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457446247567724666]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-04; +2 reactions).
- **[dw fury]** DW and 2H Fury deal identical Bloodthirst damage for the same AP; because DW gets more AP overall from itemization, DW is "overperforming" relative to 2H — [[d:warrior#1456851807199428831]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-03).
- **[fury 2h]** Concrete single-target 2H Fury rotation posted (Jerso): 1st global Sunder Armor > auto-attack > Bloodthirst > (if rage allows) Whirlwind > auto > Slam (if BT/WW on CD) > auto, repeat; pop Death Wish and stack 30/20/15-second buffs relative to estimated time-to-kill; use Bloodrage on pull if the fight will last over 1 minute (it's back up in 59s); use Execute once in range if AP allows a BT>Execute finish — [[d:warrior#1459198336731975701]] (Jerso, 2026-01-09) through [[d:warrior#1459199872401019001]] (Jerso, 2026-01-09). A close variant (evella): Sunder > auto (if it crits) > Slam > BT > auto > Slam > WW > auto; if the opening auto didn't crit, fish a Bloodthirst crit first before Slamming — [[d:warrior#1459212200026833017]] (evella, 2026-01-09).
- **[fury 2h]** Slam mechanics on Turtle differ fundamentally from vanilla: auto-attack continues to build in the background while Slam's cast bar runs (it only pauses if the swing timer completes before the cast finishes), and you can move freely while casting Slam — unlike vanilla where casting Slam stopped the auto-attack timer entirely — [[d:warrior#1459683480064622714]] (Gs, 2026-01-11; question), [[d:warrior#1459683959792206076]] (Virasan, 2026-01-11).
- **[fury 2h]** Rule of thumb: don't cast Slam if less than ~1.4-1.5 seconds remain on your swing timer, to avoid delaying your next auto-attack — [[d:warrior#1461011637342572668]] (Delf, 2026-01-14).
- **[fury 2h]** A claim that Slam's cast time (via Flurry + gear haste) can drop low enough to fit two Slams inside one auto-attack window on a 3.7-3.8 speed weapon was explicitly debunked: with Flurry, Slam cast can drop to ~1.7s while the auto-attack interval becomes ~2.8s — not the claimed "two slams per auto" — [[d:warrior#1461007370011283739]] (Bansku, 2026-01-14; false claim), [[d:warrior#1461011320861364415]] (Delf, 2026-01-14; correction).
- **[fury dw]** Overpower for a low-gear DW Fury: use it when rage is under 25 AND Bloodthirst/Whirlwind won't be back up within 2.5 seconds, mainly to maintain Flurry uptime; less useful once gear/rage income improves, though it gets renewed value from the T2 3-piece DPS set bonus (Overpower proc grants a haste buff) especially relevant for BWL-era Ambershire gear — [[d:warrior#1460344354655965470]] (evella, 2026-01-12), [[d:warrior#1460344512025989253]] (evella, 2026-01-12).
- **[weapon skill]** Community view solidifying around "hit is overrated" for DPS gearing: missing 1% of attacks/abilities = ~1% less DPS, but critting 1% more = roughly the same or slightly more DPS once Impale/Deep Wounds bonuses are counted — the community "dogma" that hit must be capped first is a simplified new-player heuristic that gets overapplied, e.g. some players take 1% hit over 1% crit on trinkets purely "because hit is this mythical stat you need to cap right now," or waste a full hit% point covering only 0.2-0.4% of miss chance from fractional weapon skill — [[d:warrior#1458604131227795597]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-08) through [[d:warrior#1458607669400178782]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-08).
- **[weapon skill]** Recommended weapon skill target: 310-315; going above 315 is usually a stat sacrifice not worth making — [[d:warrior#1458890331666911403]] (Virasan, 2026-01-07; +2 reactions). One log comparison (321 skill/4% hit vs. 316 skill/5% hit) showed *more* misses with higher weapon skill/lower hit, contradicting a naive assumption that skill fully substitutes for hit past a point — unresolved, recorded as a data point rather than settled fact — [[d:warrior#1459999878003560626]] (Maturi, 2026-01-11).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general]** Hit cap formula: base 8% hit chance to avoid missing yellow (special) abilities, reduced by 0.2% per point of relevant weapon skill above 300; e.g. skill 305 → 7% needed, 310 → 6%, 315 → 5%; this applies identically whether Dual Wield, 2H, or Sword-and-Shield — [[d:warrior#1457400704606929118]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457402757693833248]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457402842074714285]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-04).
- **[dw fury]** Chasing hit-cap for white/auto-attack swings on Dual Wield (as opposed to just the 8%-minus-skill yellow cap) is generally considered sub-optimal ("Trying to get enough hit to not miss whites as DW is sub optimal"), but DW does get partial benefit from hit above the yellow cap (unlike 2H/shield where it's wasted) — [[d:warrior#1457400938598760528]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457403054197313606]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-04).
- **[general]** Strength gives 2 AP per point; Agility gives crit (not AP) — [[d:warrior#1456802956958765136]] (owebsterdesign, 2026-01-03; question), [[d:warrior#1456803602239721514]] (Yorb, 2026-01-03).
- **[general]** Haste is flagged as badly itemized/broken at high stacking levels: "Haste breaks vanilla if there's enough to stack... higher tier items with haste are going to be too strong," attributed to exponential-style scaling with Bloodthirst/Heroic Strike/Cleave; Armor Penetration (ArP) values on gear are said to be too small and boss armor values vary fight-to-fight making ArP optimization awkward — [[d:warrior#1456722084532322626]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-02), [[d:warrior#1456727093160317045]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-02), [[d:warrior#1456729458839916848]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-02: suggests enemy dodge reduction as a stat with "legs").
- **[general]** No fixed haste "cap" like 8% hit; advice is to stack the strongest gear and haste both due to exponential scaling, though there's a "bottom limit" of crit/AP/haste breakpoints below which haste isn't worth stacking (exact breakpoint unknown) — [[d:warrior#1456730916616667332]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-02; +1), [[d:warrior#1456734302917365801]] (Dodging, 2026-01-02).
- **[fury 2h vs dw]** For weapon-enchant choice on a T3 legs-type item, "8 Str vs 1% haste": Kyrsie recommends Strength if Dual Wielding because 2H benefits more from haste due to the Slam double-dip bug; sim reportedly shows haste scoring higher even for some DW players — [[d:warrior#1457417969897439333]] (Yorb, 2026-01-04; question), [[d:warrior#1457419285386494158]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457419404336959613]] (Yorb, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457426087238963382]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04: "haste sims higher").
- **[fury/gear]** Naxxramas DPS gear (Carnage Ribcage, Mentor's Vengeance, Unnatural, Seething items, T3 rings) controversially carries Spirit, seen as wasted stat budget that should have gone to Stamina; full T3 tank set is described as very low-Stamina / a "zero threat set," and T3 DPS set is likewise very low-Stamina (possibly to discourage tanking in it) — [[d:warrior#1457436734223810732]] (Madcrep, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457438337186136147]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457438505306427561]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457439575646666783]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457441141678018701]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04: naxx dps gear has ~230 Stamina from items alone, disputing that it's "too much spirit, not enough stam").
- **[prot tank/sim]** Sim discussion: dropping too much hit for crit/other stats can overshoot — items like Accuria and QSR were simming unexpectedly high after dropping hit too far; community stresses "sim your own gear, don't gear by vibes" — [[d:warrior#1457451642806079639]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04) through [[d:warrior#1457453507429793863]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457452038152785930]] (30, 2026-01-04; +1 reaction).
- **[weapon skill]** TWoW halved vanilla's glancing-blow-damage scaling from weapon skill and added the 250g/type, +5-skill quests as a partial offset (stacks with racial weapon-skill bonuses of +3); Turtle's earlier vanilla-based hit-cap scaling (skill's first 5 points obtusely boosting hit cap due to vanilla's extra baseline miss chance) was also "fixed" — Lipibidy is unsure the net result is actually better than the old 308-skill cap system — [[d:warrior#1458890526186012742]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-07), [[d:warrior#1458890805610807430]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-07).
- **[weapon skill]** Recommended weapon skill target for DW: 310-315; going meaningfully above 315 is "usually a stat sacrifice that's not worth it" — [[d:warrior#1458890331666911403]] (Virasan, 2026-01-07; +2). For DW specifically, aim for roughly 12% hit at mid+ gear per some players, though the hard requirement is capping the MH (yellow-ability) hit chance — [[d:warrior#1458889843898843269]] (Virasan, 2026-01-07).
- **[general]** T1 warrior 3-piece DPS set bonus text confirmed verbatim: "Whenever you miss with an ability, you gain 15 Rage" — noted as a fairly weak/niche bonus (only triggers on your own misses, not dodge/parry) — [[d:warrior#1459582630109515797]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-10), [[d:warrior#1459582673671422088]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-10), [[d:warrior#1458607109678825581]] (30, 2026-01-08; +4 reactions, quoting the exact tooltip).
- **[general]** "Hit is overrated" pushback against community dogma (Black Hat, extensive): missing 1% of attacks ≈ 1% less DPS; critting 1% more ≈ roughly the same or more DPS (plus Impale/Deep Wounds synergy), so the common advice to hard-cap hit before anything else is an oversimplified heuristic that gets "blown out of proportion"; hit matters far less while leveling since gear rarely offers meaningful hit/crit itemization anyway — [[d:warrior#1458603440874983537]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-08) through [[d:warrior#1458607669400178782]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-08).

## Tanking specifics
- **[prot tank]** Confirmed: tanking as warrior has less mitigation than the other two tank classes/specs while having the same-or-worse threat-per-minute; the main practical issue is holding AoE threat — [[d:warrior#1456796432333013249]] (hehe haha, 2026-01-03).

- **[prot tank]** A geared tank should trade some "T1 defense" gear pieces for the DPS-set ("Armor of Might") alternative to cut a large miss chance (example: cutting 4.4% miss) and gain crit/AP for more threat, without sacrificing too much stamina — [[d:warrior#1457122743802794045]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457124820432064512]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457125716960477196]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04).
- **[prot tank]** Many progression raids run with dual-purpose DPS warriors who "put a shield on" for certain trash pulls or specific fights (e.g., brainwash-swap for Four Horsemen) rather than dedicated tank specs for every pull; using a Rogue as an offtank at Mograine is mentioned as a possibly-viable prog strategy — [[d:warrior#1457443153702551572]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457443281448472678]] (Dodging, 2026-01-04).
- **[prot tank/deftac]** "Deftac" (Defensive Tactics tank build) is a niche spec used mainly for specific fights that force it (e.g., Patchwerk-style hateful strike fights, Twin Emperors); one poster says "I know exactly 1 player that uses deftac outside of the fight that forces them" — [[d:warrior#1457401786959659163]] (styr, 2026-01-04), [[d:warrior#1457403804600504403]] (Artifyce, 2026-01-04: still uses deftac for Patchwerk hatefuls and Twin Emperors).
- **[prot tank]** If Deep Prot ("deftac") 3-piece is used with T2, it's competitive with T3 and beats a "2.5" (tier 2.5?) set for that build — [[d:warrior#1457102657650032640]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-04).
- **[prot tank, core structural debate]** Extensively argued position from Lipibidy: Furyprot exists *only because* Deep Prot's threat is weak/non-scaling; if Deep Prot threat were made competitive, Furyprot would become obsolete/niche — "Furyprot's schtick is threat. If you insist on letting furyprot alone be the threat spec, it makes any other tank spec useless in comparison." Recommends: make Revenge scale with defensive stats (dodge/parry/block/armor) and convert its threat into a multiplier, and/or add a Deep Prot talent letting Revenge be used without a proc so Deep Prot can OT — [[d:warrior#1459941223186956466]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-11), [[d:warrior#1459944449739063509]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-11), [[d:warrior#1459951938346160178]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-11), [[d:warrior#1459961193002307721]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-11).
- **[prot tank, counter-argument]** Virasan and others push back hard: multiple viable tank specs (with different tradeoffs — damage vs. survivability vs. AoE vs. utility) is not contradictory, and "if you can tank in a spec that has any survivability why would you tank in fury prot" is not automatically true once other factors (utility, AoE, damage ceiling for speedruns) are weighed; cites that speedrun guilds run Fury Prot specifically for threat ceiling on progression pulls, not "because tank personal damage matters" — [[d:warrior#1459953969949311101]] (Virasan, 2026-01-11), [[d:warrior#1459954106012667966]] (Virasan, 2026-01-11), [[d:warrior#1459954399014027503]] (Virasan, 2026-01-11), [[d:warrior#1459964608994607377]] (Black Hat, 2026-01-11: "I'd rather Fprot being the resource for high end pushers and deftac rated E[asy mode]").
- **[prot tank, practical guidance]** Despite the theory dispute, near-universal practical advice given to new tank askers: tank Deep Prot (Revenge/Reprisal build) at low gear where it's "fine," and switch to Fury Prot (Defensive Tactics + Impale) once raid DPS ("giga pumpers," 2000+ DPS rogues/cats) outpaces what Deep Prot can hold; Fury Prot is called "the only real option" to MT high-end progression content without Thunderfury carrying Deep Prot's AoE threat — [[d:warrior#1458891363574616065]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-08), [[d:warrior#1458892702560030720]] (Lans, 2026-01-08: "you're stuck with furyprot if you want to MT, or just get TF"), [[d:warrior#1458893164596429099]] (Jakeends, 2026-01-08), [[d:warrior#1461052061285220477]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-11).
- **[prot tank]** Deep Prot without Thunderfury is described as "suffering" — the item is treated as close to mandatory to make Deep Prot's AoE threat viable at all — [[d:warrior#1461051959879532604]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-13).
- **[prot tank]** New-tank guidance flagged as internally contradictory by some (Diabase): Furyprot is often the first spec recommended to new tanks in this channel, but it "rarely can work on a tank that is not T3 geared already," which a brand-new tank usually isn't — meaning the simple advice doesn't actually fit their situation, and the honest answer ("go Deep Prot at low gear, but you can't hold threat against anyone who outgears or matches your gear") is harder to communicate — [[d:warrior#1461051631230648363]] (Diabase, 2026-01-13), [[d:warrior#1461051688797606059]] (Diabase, 2026-01-13), [[d:warrior#1461051907421376513]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-13).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[fury dw]** HSQ ("Heroic Strike Queue") bug from Blizzard classic (100% off-hand hit while HS is queued) no longer exists on Turtle, contributing to DW fury feeling weaker post-1.17 — [[d:warrior#1457240036662575336]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-04).
- **[fury 2h]** Slam "double-dips" into haste's cast-time reduction benefit (applying it twice), which is treated as an unintended bug that nonetheless makes haste itemization strong for 2H fury — [[d:warrior#1457419389145190511]] (Kyrsie, 2026-01-04).
- **[macro/UI]** Execute ability macro icon bug: no dedicated icon appears when creating an Execute macro via the default UI, reported by multiple guild members, workaround (`#showtooltip Execute`) inconsistent in effect across players — [[d:warrior#1456590326687993867]] (Rahakesh|Maik, 2026-01-02) through [[d:warrior#1456661939286573152]] (Rahakesh|Maik, 2026-01-02).
- **[bug]** SuperMacro + RoidMacros combined causes macros on the action bar to randomly overwrite each other's icon/command (all macros collapse to showing the same ability); resolved by using only one macro addon — [[d:warrior#1456358252676776149]] (Rahakesh|Maik, 2026-01-01), [[d:warrior#1456359405124714586]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-01), [[d:warrior#1456589857722859613]] (Rahakesh|Maik, 2026-01-02; +1: confirms fix worked).
- **[weapon proc interaction]** Dream's Herald's attack-speed-slow debuff does not stack with Thunderfury's, though both DoTs (bleed/damage-over-time components) still apply independently — [[d:warrior#1456748460094128128]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-03).
- **[prot]** Shield Block previously granted 2 charges; changed so Shield Slam instead grants 1 overwriting stack, specifically to prevent Furyprot from stacking block chance to "avoidance-cap" — creates an unintended, clunky "minigame" never explained by devs — [[d:warrior#1460410895854342196]] (30, 2026-01-13), [[d:warrior#1460410963693142078]] (30, 2026-01-13), [[d:warrior#1460414002952802407]] (30, 2026-01-13).
- **[fury 2h]** Slam's cast time benefiting from Flurry-granted haste is confirmed "as intended" by devs but agreed by multiple regulars to be overtuned/bad design, since it makes Improved Slam (an Arms talent) largely redundant — [[d:warrior#1458723905345683517]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-08), [[d:warrior#1458723943677427804]] (Lipibidy, 2026-01-08).
- **[weapon]** OBD (Ornate Bloodstone Dagger) has a "funny interaction": its proc deals self-damage that generates Rage, letting a player stand on a dummy and spam Heroic Strike every swing (1s) with full rage — an emergent/discovered interaction, not an officially advertised feature — [[d:warrior#1458068904080310452]] (Wedii, 2026-01-07; +10 reactions), [[d:warrior#1458059629337645242]]/[[d:warrior#1458080061453238393]] (Velanir, 2026-01-07: OBD = Ornate Bloodstone Dagger).
- **[weapon]** Bonereaver's Edge's Whirlwind-proc interaction was previously bugged (didn't proc on Whirlwind) but is confirmed fixed as of this chunk — [[d:warrior#1458813085358751755]] (Wilhelm, 2026-01-08; question), [[d:warrior#1458817145235443712]] (Virasan, 2026-01-08; confirms fixed).
- **[not a real change]** A satirical fake "Developer Statement" claiming Fury would be retired and replaced by a "Bard" specialization was posted as an in-channel joke on 2026-01-15; explicitly not a real Turtle WoW announcement — [[d:warrior#1461118596322431241]] (Guts, 2026-01-15).
