## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[tanking]** Fury/Prot ("furyprot") is a real, commonly used spec: (A) a high-scaling main-tank build for guilds wanting maximum tank threat, and (B) a good off-tank build for less-coordinated groups to add damage when not on a boss assignment — [[d:warrior#1445929935079211088]] (Dodging, 2025-12-03). It is used weekly in all current content per 30 — [[d:warrior#1445868575867801621]].
- **[tanking]** Deep Prot ("deftac"/Defensive Tactics) is considered the strongest single-target tank spec and beats Defense-stance tanking on ST, at the cost of being squishier and losing Revenge — [[d:warrior#1445954046770024570]] (casey, 2025-12-03). One tank calls deftac "the absolute highlight of turtle wow," finally tanking with a shield on — [[d:warrior#1445893912920330290]] (Kilkarik, 2025-12-04).
- **[tanking, disputed]** Lipibidy: Fury Prot's popularity is inversely related to Deep Prot's power — when deep prot is strong, furyprot use drops; the current prevalence of furyprot (and "lawnmower" builds) for tank assignments is itself evidence deep prot is mediocre. Lans agrees prot needs work and would rather furyprot be removed as a tank option; 30 and Aubreykun disagree, saying furyprot works well every week and killing a working playstyle "for no reason" is bad — [[d:warrior#1446042420063244439]], [[d:warrior#1445857879428038697]], [[d:warrior#1445867536599220285]] (Lipibidy/Lans/30, 2025-12-03/04).
- **[tanking]** Furyprot's real enabler is good healer play/gear; when the server's healer pool was weak (~1.5 years ago per Aubreykun) many players fell back to bad "shield prot" instead — [[d:warrior#1445937510910791791]], [[d:warrior#1445937798728253530]] (Aubreykun, 2025-12-04).
- **[tanking]** 2H tanking of dungeon trash is viable only if heavily overgeared; it doesn't work in raids outside niche cases because single-target threat is effectively nonexistent — [[d:warrior#1445938912072564847]], [[d:warrior#1445939629067866162]] (30/Kyrsie, 2025-12-04).
- **[tanking, disputed]** Bear (druid) tanks are seen as clearly outclassing warrior tanks on Turtle — a preraid bear reportedly had ~12k armor, "no trade off at all" — [[d:warrior#1445951402672263260]], [[d:warrior#1445952451239739462]] (casey/Nost, 2025-12-03; opinion/complaint, no rebuttal seen).
- **[dps]** 2H Fury ("lawnmower" build, heavy Slam-spam) vs BT-centric Arms/2H rotations debated with real logs: Thirtyseven reports higher personal boss/overall damage with lawnmower vs a same-raid Arms comparison, but MoneyCow argues the BT/MS damage lost (~10-12% per Blackjack) isn't fully offset and calls lawnmower "mediocre" next to Arms/BT 2h fury — [[d:warrior#1446169021824368660]], [[d:warrior#1446169138757111990]], [[d:warrior#1446177605580488815]] (Thirtyseven/MoneyCow/Blackjack, 2025-12-04; disputed, gear/skill-level caveats acknowledged by both sides).
- **[PvP]** Warrior is broadly agreed to currently be the worst class for PvP on Turtle: "Anything with 2k+ defense you can forget about killing," DW fury needed just to survive >20s, even a full K40-BiS warrior needs an armor-penetrating trinket proc to kill anyone — [[d:warrior#1445164854586511432]], [[d:warrior#1445165069569626252]], [[d:warrior#1445165361266954414]], [[d:warrior#1445925489205051584]] (Thirtyseven/Kyrsie, 2025-12-01/02; broad agreement, no dissent seen).
- **[PvP]** Prot Warrior PvP is considered close to useless: easily kited/ignored, dies fast to magic damage; its niche utility (Purge, silence "Bitch Slap," stuns, disarm) is real but insufficient — [[d:warrior#1444176817593520229]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-29; +context from Lipibidy agreeing prot needs a PvP identity, disputed on *how much* to buff it).
- **[PvP]** "Least bad" warrior PvP spec is DW Fury / Blood Drinker — [[d:warrior#1445899843347415120]] (Dodging, 2025-12-04). 2H Fury is bad in PvP because its main filler (Slam) has a cast time and gets outranged — [[d:warrior#1445162609933418638]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-12-02). DW Fury sustain is stronger than 2H in PvP — [[d:warrior#1445161012507705610]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-02).

## Leveling
- **[general]** Xenomorphic asked about current leveling spec; answer: the Deathwish/Sweeping Strikes build "is still gud but what you do changes based on level — 'ideally' you respec at key points as different stuff opens up" — [[d:warrior#1444466859780083853]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-30).
- **[professions]** Recommended leveling profession picks for warrior (Aubreykun, long answer): Alchemy is easy utility even if dropped at 60 (stat food/elixirs/scrolls speed up kills, some items like Frost Oil sell on AH); Herbalism/Mining are simple gold; JC/Engineering are expensive to level pre-60 unless self-funded; Blacksmithing/Enchanting can net early gold via buckles (Bsmithing) but must be sold manually — [[d:warrior#1444330106830061751]], [[d:warrior#1444331459207757864]], [[d:warrior#1444332326292029571]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-29). At endgame: Engineering ("the sweat profession," sappers add real DPS), Blacksmithing (Tempered Runeblade, "free" elemental sharpening stones, Dreamsteel), Alchemy (Mojo Madness, flasks, own MRP) — [[d:warrior#1444333624458743818]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-29). Nost: at endgame get Herbalism first if your guild has an alchemist to craft for you (saves buying consumes), then a 2nd profession funded by herb gold — [[d:warrior#1444347771556724928]] (Nost, 2025-11-29; disputed lightly by Dodging: "farming gold is faster than collecting herbs," [[d:warrior#1444359895024668793]]).

## Rotation: single target
- **[2H, all specs]** Slam does not reset the swing timer anymore (Turtle change), which is why Slam can be weaved into 2H rotations without a dps loss the way it historically did; 2H Slam replaces Heroic Strike as the rage dump when below the rage threshold, HS/Cleave get used above ~70 rage instead since HS won't block MS/WW at that point — [[d:warrior#1444723454212575303]], [[d:warrior#1444724011811733554]], [[d:warrior#1444724324736045181]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-29).
- **[general]** Priority for arms/2h at execute-range: normal rotation (BT/MS) still beats spamming Execute once you have enough AP — the crossover is roughly >2000 AP, below which Execute remains correct; confirmed independently by an Arms player and a 2H player — [[d:warrior#1445711804830449664]], [[d:warrior#1445711870668574782]], [[d:warrior#1445734902380236822]], [[d:warrior#1445740502149370069]] (Sylvaira/axelfrog/30, 2025-12-02). Execute itself was heavily nerfed on Turtle relative to a few months prior — [[d:warrior#1445710166530985994]] (30, 2025-12-02).
- **[DW fury]** As DW there's little reason to swap stance mid-fight except Charging in at pull for faster combat entry; disarm is situationally used on some Naxx packs — [[d:warrior#1445737011775541300]], [[d:warrior#1445737184802897944]] (axelfrog, 2025-12-02).
- **[DW fury]** Bloodrage counts as a crit on Turtle, so it also procs Enrage — described as the biggest DW-fury rotation difference from vanilla, making Bloodrage "a short but potent damage cooldown" — [[d:warrior#1445462507266244680]] (Sylvaira, 2025-12-02).
- **[DW fury]** There is no Heroic Strike queue bug on Turtle (unlike vanilla) — [[d:warrior#1445482653968109629]] (Dodging, 2025-12-02).
- **[DW fury talent]** A newer Dual Wield Specialization talent effect lowers offhand hit-cap toward the mainhand skill hit-cap (adds +10% OH hit), approximating vanilla's old HS-queue-driven OH hit-cap behavior and making Fury easier to play; math dispute over exact residual OH miss chance (30 calculates ~9% OH miss remains even with the talent; Lipibidy argues OH/skill hitcaps now line up under some assumptions) — [[d:warrior#1445488684173365369]], [[d:warrior#1445496810448752753]], [[d:warrior#1445497054364172398]] (Lipibidy/30, 2025-12-02; unresolved, both sides technical).
- **[general]** Whirlwind does not reset your swing timer — it completes/finishes the current swing instead, correcting a common misconception — [[d:warrior#1446095479749480498]] (Dodging, 2025-12-04).
- **[general]** Jumping extends the range of shouts (and reportedly Whirlwind's range) — old classic quirk confirmed to still exist on Turtle — [[d:warrior#1446133321829449870]], [[d:warrior#1446133557918564453]] (Dodging, 2025-12-04; single source, "pretty sure").

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[open world/farm, DW fury Blood Drinker]** For big open-world pulls, use a fast 1H and spam Cleave to keep up with incoming damage; for small packs (4-6 mobs), swap to the biggest/slowest 2H available and use Sweeping Strikes to "melt" it — [[d:warrior#1445079323299151943]], [[d:warrior#1445079442337693870]] (Blackjack, 2025-12-01). Fast 1H is still preferred if actively farming with Blood Drinker even for bigger pulls — [[d:warrior#1445081116821295235]] (Dodging, 2025-12-01).
- **[weapon swap for AoE]** Earthshaker used as a situational weapon swap for certain trash packs (imps/servants) while dungeon-delving or in Kara10 — [[d:warrior#1444386749609672805]] (Blackjack, 2025-11-29).
- **[BRD trash]** Bonereaver's Edge (BRE) is strong for BRD trash specifically because of the zone's generally high mob armor, especially near Emperor — [[d:warrior#1445107738035359744]] (Aubreykun, 2025-12-01).
- **[DW cleave]** For DW cleave-fighting against trash, a balance between weapon speed and per-hit damage is better than pure damage, because faster MH avoids "clipping" autos into cleave windows even though per-swing cleave/WW numbers look lower — [[d:warrior#1445108624224424017]] (Aubreykun, 2025-12-01). Empyrean Demolisher (from world boss Kazzak) is "pretty much BiS for DW cleave fighting without Naxx" — [[d:warrior#1445108915485282496]] (Aubreykun, 2025-12-01); its extra-attack proc is a Flurry-type effect, not a separate cleave-hitting bolt — [[d:warrior#1445109442692517968]] (Aubreykun, 2025-12-01).
- **[2H fury K40 "SS build" vs "BT build"]** Two viable K40 (Kara40) rotation approaches: a Sweeping-Strikes-heavy build swapped in for trash-heavy segments/bosses, vs a Bloodthirst-centric build for the rest. One raider's boss-by-boss swap pattern: SS on trash → Gnarl(moon) → trash → Incantagos → trash, swap to BT for Anomalus → trash → Medivh, back to SS for trash → Sanv(Tas'dal) → Rupturan, BT for Kruul and Meph(istroth) — [[d:warrior#1447524170836611122]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-12-08). A veteran's variant: SS until Anomalus, respec BT before Chess, respec back to SS up to Rupturan, BT for Kruul/Meph — [[d:warrior#1447524574349754490]] (Guts, 2025-12-08). SS is felt to be a little better on trash but "not by much," favored more when trash-pacing is slower (more time to build/use charges); as raids get faster, SS often only fires once per back-to-back trash chain — [[d:warrior#1447522520969511016]], [[d:warrior#1447524288973504557]], [[d:warrior#1447524457739456693]] (Guts/Thirtyseven, 2025-12-08; both admit after ~1 raid tier of testing they're still not fully certain which is better — "learn both, understand strengths/weaknesses in your own environment," disputed/unresolved).
- **[filler priority, all specs]** General ST/AoE filler order when core rotation abilities are on CD: Hamstring and Pummel first as cheap "fish for Windfury proc" filler (small direct damage, so prioritized over Sunder), then Sunder Armor (10 rage baseline cost, "plenty available"; always used as the opening GCD, and as filler any time it won't delay Bloodthirst/Whirlwind) — [[d:warrior#1449265080347918481]], [[d:warrior#1449265186807746722]], [[d:warrior#1449265319364530227]], [[d:warrior#1449265426654560266]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-09).
- **[tanking/dungeon AoE]** Concrete leveling/dungeon-tank AoE priority (fast weapon is fine — don't chase a slow MH for "threat"): Whirlwind > Thunder Clap > Concussion Blow > Shield Slam, filling with Heroic Strike on excess rage and swapping targets/using grenades as needed — [[d:warrior#1449599120640704512]] (casey, 2025-12-09).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[weapon skill, major TWoW change]** Weapon skill's per-point scaling was cut in half compared to vanilla, and the Human/Orc weapon-skill racials were reduced from +5 to +3 — together these racials are "~30% as valuable as in vanilla," so race is no longer a mandatory pick for weapon skill reasons; the calculation change is widely cited as why humans/orcs are no longer a "must play" — [[d:warrior#1448053919497457826]], [[d:warrior#1448037934103269386]] (Lipibidy/casey, 2025-12-10). "There's no BiS race in Turtle once you understand how weapon skill is different than classic," and race is said to matter mainly for PvP now, not PvE — [[d:warrior#1449352408567386112]], [[d:warrior#1449427148749279506]] (Dodging/Kyrsie, 2025-12-13; opinion, some still argue Orc/Gnome/Night Elf are best, disputed/joking).
- **[weapon skill scaling]** Weapon skill's revised hit-scaling needs +15 skill (not +5/+10) to reach the 6% boss hit-cap benefit, linear from +0 to +15 — [[d:warrior#1446306529962889388]], [[d:warrior#1446306654483382345]], [[d:warrior#1446306837044658319]] (Campsie/Lanik/30, 2025-12-06). Weapon skill's main feature is reducing the glancing-blow penalty and crit suppression, not raw hit — "hit is the least important part of weapon skill" — [[d:warrior#1446306743666606320]], [[d:warrior#1448055389235974326]], [[d:warrior#1448056305502785567]] (30/Lipibidy, 2025-12-06/10). It barely matters below level 60 (you scale it 5/level automatically); exceeding 300 isn't significant until a mob is above level 60 with >300 defense — [[d:warrior#1448056046605303808]], [[d:warrior#1448056466371248229]], [[d:warrior#1448056671703269477]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-10). Human weapon-skill cap from items is effectively +12 (vs +15 for non-humans) due to the racial, so a human can "overcap" and waste itemization by a couple of points — [[d:warrior#1447332779678499010]], [[d:warrior#1447332942979403857]], [[d:warrior#1447333075079004337]] (Aubreykun/Dodging, 2025-12-08). Past 315 weapon skill gives only "small hit and crit," described as overkill by multiple people — [[d:warrior#1450127944969093206]], [[d:warrior#1450128345319608382]], [[d:warrior#1449791979033727108]] (Jorrick-Drath/Deleted User/Dodging, 2025-12-11/15).
- **[DW hit/miss]** DW white-hit (auto-attack) miss chance is hard to bring to 0; a naked/base DW white hit-cap is quoted as needing 27% hit total, but "you don't have to cover all of that" since the DW talent and weapon skill already reduce it passively — [[d:warrior#1449429951832129681]], [[d:warrior#1450188728286056468]], [[d:warrior#1450189565934571601]], [[d:warrior#1450189758675288165]] (Dodging/30/silverthornn, 2025-12-10/13). For ability hit (Bloodthirst/MS/etc.) the boss MH hit-cap is 8% miss, and 5 points of weapon skill is worth 1% hit — [[d:warrior#1447390439387955341]], [[d:warrior#1449429549795250289]] (Campsie-Lanik/Kyrsie, 2025-12-08/09). A newer Dual Wield Specialization talent effect gives the offhand +10% hit, narrowing (not eliminating) the OH-vs-skill-hitcap gap; math dispute over exact residual OH miss (see Rotation: single target) — [[d:warrior#1445488684173365369]].
- **[stat priority, DW fury]** Str > Agi > AP/Crit, with +hit and +weapon skill also important (a limited, always-priority resource); Str/AP directly boosts Bloodthirst, Agi/Crit boosts Flurry uptime and Impale's benefit — [[d:warrior#1447322641882153113]], [[d:warrior#1447322850162638938]], [[d:warrior#1447323018237050941]] (Aubreykun, 2025-12-08). Disputed ordering: "Hit cap then everything else" — [[d:warrior#1447323157898858586]] (Blackjack, 2025-12-08; Aubreykun agrees fury's hit-cap math is "weird" vs Arms). Once hit-capped, "more crit is always better," and hit itself is "extremely easy to find in TWoW" so it's not a real bottleneck — [[d:warrior#1447396584055898216]], [[d:warrior#1447396689303572562]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-08).
- **[haste]** For DW Fury, haste "scales well as you get better gear" and is a real consideration for enchants/trinkets since not all BiS gear rolls haste; for Furyprot it's a low priority outside of King of the Sands (KoTS)-type big haste buffs, since it's not available on enough gear to matter like crit does — [[d:warrior#1447410958459863080]], [[d:warrior#1447411181395644436]], [[d:warrior#1447411442528550962]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-08). Haste sources stack multiplicatively, not additively (order: Flurry, then KoTS, then gear haste): `new_swing = old_swing / (haste1*haste2*...*hasteN)`; 100% total haste = half swing timer — [[d:warrior#1447411553728200797]], [[d:warrior#1447412449975336960]], [[d:warrior#1447413061811175424]] (Lipibidy/30, 2025-12-08). Haste "scales exponentially provided you have a lot of other stats like crit and AP" — stacking haste while ignoring crit/AP is "shooting yourself in the foot" — [[d:warrior#1447412406081945752]], [[d:warrior#1447412481336021013]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-08).
- **[Slam + haste interaction, notable/possibly unintended]** Multiple independent reports that gear haste currently affects Slam's cast time twice as much as expected ("double dips"), which is why more gear haste widens the gap between the swing timer and the Slam cast bar; one poster speculates this "might get patched" — [[d:warrior#1446966426026901526]], [[d:warrior#1447672990258172118]], [[d:warrior#1448031185371398287]] (Kyrsie/Lipibidy, 2025-12-07/10). Separately, 30 states that in BiS gear, average Slam damage now exceeds average Bloodthirst damage for 2H fury, calling it "a bit too powerful... a bit crazy" and noting he proposed a fix (a stronger, Arms-exclusive Slam) when the current patch launched — [[d:warrior#1446248852897403010]], [[d:warrior#1446249622543798386]] (30, 2025-12-05).
- **[flat armor penetration, disputed]** Called "a joke stat" by some — PvE bosses "got no armor anyway" (or so much PvP armor it doesn't matter), making ArP "bloat in the item budget" with little point taking ArP enchants — [[d:warrior#1448021669468700704]], [[d:warrior#1448022722335740055]], [[d:warrior#1446267660726702150]], [[d:warrior#1446268041150206123]] (casey/Blackjack, 2025-12-09/10). Disputed: many raid bosses do carry meaningful armor (~300 more than vanilla-equivalent per 30), so ArP is "a decent stat on most" — [[d:warrior#1448025671690616862]], [[d:warrior#1448026054928371712]] (Stunfisk/30, 2025-12-09). For a specific enchant choice (shoulder, HC leveler), Haste was recommended over ArP as "more consistently useful" since ArP "might not be doing anything on some fights" — [[d:warrior#1448030587251331266]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-09).
- **[misc mechanics]** Block and Crit are mutually exclusive results of the same attack-table roll — you cannot block a crit — [[d:warrior#1446191880067874866]] (Black Hat, 2025-12-04). Berserker Rage's extra rage from damage taken is only a 30% increase to the normal rage-from-damage formula (Rage ≈ Damage/230.6×2.5, unconfirmed exact source but reportedly cross-checked against logs/vods); in practice this is small (~7 rage from 2k damage) and "never worth [it to] skip a Slam" unless taking ~5.5k damage in one window — [[d:warrior#1448234430819860482]], [[d:warrior#1448235339666690211]], [[d:warrior#1448236411751759902]], [[d:warrior#1448237213900083355]], [[d:warrior#1448237535536087202]] (Squid/Guts, 2025-12-10). T1's 3pc "gain 15 rage on ability miss" bonus is "completely useless" once you're ability-hit-capped — [[d:warrior#1449431028446789814]], [[d:warrior#1449431108046426164]] (Wilhelm/Dodging, 2025-12-09). A recent hotfix (independent of the 1.18.1 patch discussed above) made creature-type Attack Power bonuses (e.g. Mark of the Champion) apply to auto-attacks, where they previously didn't — confirmed by live testing — [[d:warrior#1448105876668940299]], [[d:warrior#1448128265532477510]] (Yorb/30, 2025-12-10).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[glove enchant, extensively debated]** For (fury)prot tanks, 15 Agility is favored over 2% Threat on gloves by the majority of a long, technical debate: Agi provides crit + dodge + armor (3 useful stats) vs Str's AP + a small amount of block value (2 useful stats); 2% threat has a value ceiling reached early in a fight (it does nothing once you already have a safe lead), while Agi keeps providing crit/dps/armor for the whole fight — [[d:warrior#1444558028354617465]], [[d:warrior#1444763674538672199]], [[d:warrior#1444768044663701696]], [[d:warrior#1444770824891404338]] (Lipibidy, 2025-11-29/30). Some counterpoint that at very low personal-threat margins 2% is "obviously" better for a dedicated deftac tank — [[d:warrior#1444757833878081700]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-30) — largely conceded to be splitting hairs; nobody has actually needed the extra 2% threat as a "turning point." Agi-to-dodge conversion: 100 agi ≈ +20 agi worth from racial/other sources ≈ +1% dodge; 200 agi makes Str/Agi armor-equal in one poster's math — [[d:warrior#1444813604539863211]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-29).
- **[element sharpening stones]** Question of whether Elemental Sharpening Stones stack cleanly to +4% when applied to both weapons for DW was raised but not answered in this chunk — see Contested/open questions.
- **[shoulder/leg enchants]** Fury 2H (Slam-heavy) players debate Haste vs Armor Pen on shoulder/leg enchants precisely because gear-haste "double dips" on reducing Slam's cast time (see Stats); several people (Lipibidy, Nost, 30) treat Haste as the safer, more consistently useful pick, with ArP being fight-dependent — [[d:warrior#1447672272646045777]], [[d:warrior#1447672990258172118]], [[d:warrior#1448030587251331266]] (otep1616/Lipibidy, 2025-12-08/09).
- **[imbues/procs]** Sunder Armor reportedly no longer procs on-hit trinket/weapon effects on Turtle (e.g. Windfury) — a single, secondhand report ("I heard a guy ranting"), unconfirmed by a dev or tester — [[d:warrior#1448373275926331492]] (Dodging, 2025-12-10; single source). By contrast, Master Strike, Hamstring, Pummel, and even a stance-swap Overpower cast can proc Windfury, making them useful "fishing" fillers in rage-rich fights — [[d:warrior#1448970788089630802]], [[d:warrior#1448375105653838005]] (Bitmap/evella, 2025-12-10/11).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[general filler/threat priority]** See Rotation: single target for the Hamstring/Pummel-then-Sunder filler order, and Rotation: AoE for the tanking AoE priority list.
- **[Overpower usage, DW vs 2H]** With decent gear, DW fury easily rage-caps in Battle Stance, so it "can afford to toss away rage" to press Overpower whenever other cooldowns are down; 2H builds risk a costly glancing blow if they swap stance into Overpower and it doesn't land, making 2H Overpower riskier than for DW — [[d:warrior#1448368173849121034]], [[d:warrior#1448368369135911026]], [[d:warrior#1448368437591146679]]. Overpower's "ready" state can trigger off a dodge on any target hit by Cleave/Whirlwind (not just your primary target), but casting it still requires being in Battle Stance and (if the dodge was on a secondary target) tabbing to that target — [[d:warrior#1447280604600205312]], [[d:warrior#1447280651387539649]] (Aubreykun, 2025-12-08).
- **[Improved Slam]** Its main value is *not* about avoiding cast-time-vs-swing-timer clipping on a fast weapon as one might assume; instead, cutting the cast time widens the gap between the cast finishing and the next swing, giving more room to press other buttons (HS/Cleave, MS, Overpower, Master Strike) and more leeway if a GCD clips into the next swing — [[d:warrior#1447281100643762228]], [[d:warrior#1447281265769451563]], [[d:warrior#1447281394664472688]], [[d:warrior#1447281565477371995]] (Aubreykun, 2025-12-08); it is described elsewhere as an "unskippable talent" — [[d:warrior#1446568794129567815]] (Dodging, 2025-12-05).
- **[Master Strike]** Low-priority/optional filler: deals little damage ("hits like a 1h auto," "a waste of rage" on its own) and is mainly taken/used to fish for Windfury procs or refresh Flurry when other buttons are on CD; described as "only if you're a pumper" — [[d:warrior#1446179277841109063]], [[d:warrior#1446179438818627765]], [[d:warrior#1446181398888317168]], [[d:warrior#1449790386334859274]] (Thirtyseven/Blackjack/Kyrsie/Dodging, 2025-12-04/09).
- **[Whirlwind]** Does not reset the swing timer — it completes/finishes the current swing (correcting a common misconception) — [[d:warrior#1446095479749480498]] (Dodging, 2025-12-04). Jumping is reported to extend the range of shouts (and possibly Whirlwind) — [[d:warrior#1446133321829449870]], [[d:warrior#1446133557918564453]] (Dodging, 2025-12-04; single source, "pretty sure").
- **[Slam weaving]** Ideal single-target 2H timing: cast Slam right as the swing timer resets → auto lands → Slam again ("instacast auto slam instacast auto, rinse repeat"); queuing Heroic Strike then pressing Slam lets the HS fire together with it; small auto-clipping happens sometimes but "isn't that detrimental" — [[d:warrior#1447728319843008533]], [[d:warrior#1447728413434581156]], [[d:warrior#1447728670482632714]], [[d:warrior#1447729246876340284]], [[d:warrior#1447729645653856316]] (gastro/evella/Dodging/Kyrsie, 2025-12-08). Slam's cast bar clipping your swing (mistimed) interrupts your swing timer, so timing matters — [[d:warrior#1447727930900742206]] (Dodging, 2025-12-08).
- **[Execute weaving, good gear]** At higher gear, Bloodthirst and even Slam can out-damage a low-rage Execute, so the recommendation shifts to popping Execute only right before a natural weapon swing (to avoid delaying it), or skipping it in favor of BT/Slam once threat is safe: priority becomes "Slam > Execute > auto" — [[d:warrior#1449156068734406789]], [[d:warrior#1449157589425324185]], [[d:warrior#1449423349225881855]], [[d:warrior#1449415850292678779]] (Kyrsie/Thirtyseven/Dodging, 2025-12-07/12/13). You can still use Heroic Strike/Cleave as 2H instead of pure Execute-spam during execute phase — [[d:warrior#1449034805714092133]] (Kyrsie, 2025-12-08).
- **[Berserker Rage rage-gen]** See Stats — a 30% bonus to the normal damage-taken rage formula, small in practice.
- **[dig-rat/rage tricks]** See Consumables.

## Tanking specifics
- **[One-Handed Weapon Specialization bug, prot]** Widely believed (from vanilla/paladin-equivalent expectations) to grant +10% to *all* damage while a 1H is equipped, but on TWoW it only affects damage directly tied to the 1H weapon itself (autos and Heroic Strike) — confirmed not to affect Shield Slam, Revenge, or Concussion Blow after live testing; the warrior talent's wording ("damage you deal with 1h melee weapons") differs from the Paladin version's wording ("all damage you deal while using a 1h weapon"), which may explain the divergence — [[d:warrior#1448688979410161704]], [[d:warrior#1448690595462643904]], [[d:warrior#1448690762941333514]], [[d:warrior#1448780995732967607]], [[d:warrior#1448781177656971304]], [[d:warrior#1448791551915462696]], [[d:warrior#1448791805318402141]], [[d:warrior#1448793697276919878]] (Album/Black Hat/Lipibidy/Dodging, 2025-12-11/12; well-corroborated, treat as confirmed bug/quirk).
- **[Deep Prot / Defensive Tactics ("deftac")]** Regarded as the current best single-target tank spec, beating Defense-Stance tanking on ST too, at the cost of being squishier and losing Revenge — see Spec viability. Naxx-geared tanks in this spec reportedly take surprisingly low sustained damage (one MT under 1000 dps taken on Heigan; a raid cleared Maexxna with ~3 healers in 31s); a single Holy Paladin's Flash of Light rank 1 (1.5s cast) can out-heal a tank's average incoming damage even worst-case — [[d:warrior#1445942131179913257]], [[d:warrior#1445945808582344795]], [[d:warrior#1445946252008226888]], [[d:warrior#1445946430781919322]] (30, 2025-12-04). Loatheb's debuff is cited as why raid-wide healing needs for tanks stay low in Naxx overall (30) — [[d:warrior#1445938473973579960]].
- **[Fury/Prot ("furyprot")]** Real risk is taking more crits (no Shield Block/Shield Slam mitigation); big HP pools compensate, and being in Battle Stance's flat +10% damage taken roughly offsets not having a shield — [[d:warrior#1445946541478117427]], [[d:warrior#1445952338345988096]] (30/Nost, 2025-12-04). Performance is very group/gear-dependent — reports of furyprot doing markedly worse than deftac in undergeared groups, and performance differing a lot by server/realm — [[d:warrior#1445950289650647060]], [[d:warrior#1445948904494600262]] (casey/Dodging, 2025-12-04). One tank confirms successfully MTing MC, ZG, K10, and early BWL as furyprot — [[d:warrior#1449469293141168378]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-12-13). BWL/AQ-era assessment: deftac gains from T2.5-tier gear are "mostly in the mitigation department" and "nothing huge," not comparable to what Paladins get from equivalent gear, while furyprot gets a clearer benefit (can wear less mitigation); AQ is specifically viewed as a tight spot for deftac since it has little dedicated tank loot while some DPS classes spike hard — [[d:warrior#1448461038168309903]], [[d:warrior#1448447872663158928]], [[d:warrior#1448517979959722105]] (Black Hat, 2025-12-11).
- **[T2 dps/tank set mixing]** See Gear/BiS.
- **[2H tanking]** Viable for dungeon trash only when heavily overgeared; doesn't work for raid bosses outside niche cases since single-target threat from 2H is effectively nonexistent in a raid — [[d:warrior#1445938912072564847]], [[d:warrior#1445939629067866162]] (30/Kyrsie, 2025-12-04).
- **[Sapphiron bug]** Guilds report running *no* Frost Resistance and just fighting Sapphiron normally "because his aura is bugged on here" — confirmed independently ("you don't need frost res") by another progressed raider — [[d:warrior#1449484192105631824]], [[d:warrior#1449484906081161307]] (Kyrsie/30, 2025-12-13; some FR may still help non-MT/non-healers to take healer pressure off during progression, per Dodging — [[d:warrior#1449481147103842404]], [[d:warrior#1449481482908336341]]).
- **[Naxx roadblocks]** The commonly cited hard-gate bosses in Naxxramas are Patchwerk, Heigan, Four Horsemen, and Kel'Thuzad; multiple raiders emphasize that avoidable mistakes (threat, mechanics) are what makes Naxx hard, not gear — Patchwerk has reportedly been tanked in full T2 by both a warrior and a bear — [[d:warrior#1449489984410226738]], [[d:warrior#1449489991628488754]], [[d:warrior#1449490160642162983]], [[d:warrior#1449490256666689568]] (Virasan/Minces, 2025-12-13).
- **[Kel'Thuzad]** Was reportedly nerfed/fixed regarding his casts at some point ("pre-fix KT is the biggest random bullshit go fight ever"); low graphics "spell effects" settings make his fissures hard to see — [[d:warrior#1449491100426571887]], [[d:warrior#1449491245486444708]] (Minces/Virasan, 2025-12-14).
- **[lowbie/dungeon prot]** Prot tree is "clunkier than shit for dungeons until the mid 50s" and stays mediocre relative to gear investment; the effective lowbie tanking kit is just Tactical Mastery + Improved Thunder Clap (Imp Tclap spam on packs + tab-Sunder), with Defensive Tactics itself deep in the tree and only marginally useful this early (~15% extra threat outside Def Stance, and threat generally isn't the limiting factor while leveling since you can't out-threat someone 3+ levels above you) — [[d:warrior#1447028050100224212]], [[d:warrior#1447049190558662787]], [[d:warrior#1447027741453975743]], [[d:warrior#1447046949420273855]] (Lipibidy/eric4225, 2025-12-07). Counterpoint: 2H Arms tanking with Sweeping Strikes+Whirlwind works fine for leveling dungeon packs even without Deep Prot, and "the more gear you get, the more mediocre [prot] becomes" is disputed as too pessimistic by at least one practical tank — [[d:warrior#1447043703876227142]], [[d:warrior#1447051586416279612]] (eric4225, 2025-12-07).
- **[AoE dungeon tanking, undergeared]** A workable pull sequence: Charge → mid-charge swap to Defensive Stance before landing → Demoralizing Shout → Thunder Clap → tab-Sunder; the tank's actual job is to keep the healer alive, not necessarily hold every add ("let him tank whatever he pulls off you" if a DPS overaggros) — [[d:warrior#1446128654332596224]], [[d:warrior#1446128745810493533]], [[d:warrior#1446108438084980789]], [[d:warrior#1446109775535280181]] (Aubreykun/Dodging, 2025-12-04). A hybrid 21-0-30-ish "furyprot-lite" (Sweeping Strikes instead of Concussion Blow, or picking up Death Wish/Shield Slam/Improved Whirlwind) is "kinda fun" for dungeon AoE but AoE threat "still sucks," and pairs better with a slower weapon for WW/SS threat (at the cost of slow-weapon tanking's usual downsides) — [[d:warrior#1449764314541195395]], [[d:warrior#1449769389132873882]], [[d:warrior#1449769474734555177]], [[d:warrior#1449777909278572596]] (Bassilthorne-Achuak/Kyrsie/Dodging, 2025-12-14).
- **[bears outclass warrior tanks, disputed/consensus-leaning]** Repeated complaints that Druid bear tanks clearly outclass warrior tanks — a preraid bear reportedly had ~12k armor with "no trade off at all" — [[d:warrior#1445951402672263260]], [[d:warrior#1445952451239739462]] (casey/Nost, 2025-12-03). Lipibidy's summary: "Bears and prot paladins are 70% of their TBC versions while warrior is still 70% of its vanilla version. That's why the gap is as bad as it is, and why bears are so far ahead in damage/threat." — [[d:warrior#1450203696683159744]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-15; widely agreed, occasional pushback that it's an overused complaint rather than disputing the substance).

## PvP
- **[overall state]** Broad agreement warrior is currently the worst class for PvP on Turtle: "warrs are the worst class to PvP with rn"; "anything with 2k+ defense you can forget about killing"; must play DW Fury just to survive fights past 20 seconds; even a full-K40-BiS warrior needs an armor-penetrating trinket proc to kill anyone, otherwise landing ~200-damage crits for 30s — [[d:warrior#1445164854586511432]], [[d:warrior#1445165069569626252]], [[d:warrior#1445165361266954414]], [[d:warrior#1445925489205051584]] (Thirtyseven/Kyrsie, 2025-12-01/02). Warrior is described (Lipibidy) as "everything is stronger in TWoW... PvE is all about fury right now, PvP is rough" — [[d:warrior#1447794525341749299]], [[d:warrior#1447794599132135484]] (2025-12-08). The devs have "heavily hinted" the next ("PvP") patch will bring only minor, PvE-focused changes for warrior — see 1.18.1 changes.
- **[spec tier]** "Least bad" PvP spec is DW Fury/Blood Drinker — [[d:warrior#1445899843347415120]] (Dodging, 2025-12-04). 2H Fury is weak in PvP because its filler (Slam) has a cast time and gets outranged — [[d:warrior#1445162609933418638]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-12-02); DW Fury's sustain in PvP is stronger than 2H's — [[d:warrior#1445161012507705610]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-02). One highly-active PvPer rated warrior overall "D," "B in a good player's hands" — [[d:warrior#1445924851326914651]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-12-01).
- **[Prot PvP]** Prot Warrior PvP is largely useless — easily kited/ignored and dies fast to magic damage; its utility kit (a Purge-like effect, a silence, stuns, Disarm) is real but insufficient — [[d:warrior#1444176817593520229]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-29).
- **[hamstring cooldown]** Confirmed: Hamstring has a cooldown on Turtle (reported as ~6s), a notable nerf vs vanilla ("worse cuz at least you had hamstring without cd and improved hamstring") — [[d:warrior#1445165614678413322]], [[d:warrior#1445088631214575757]] (Thirtyseven/Valerii, 2025-12-01/02). A missed Hamstring in HC leveling can mean death given the cooldown — [[d:warrior#1449754103067443273]], [[d:warrior#1449754343053066271]] (Foreseen, 2025-12-14; Piercing Howl suggested as an alternative kite tool).
- **[shaman matchup]** Enhancement Shaman ("melee specialist") is a particularly bad matchup: high armor/damage, no ICD on their Windfury weapon; recommended counterplay is to not facetank — use Disarm, stamina gear, Fear+bandage, a boomerang/net trinket, and kiting rather than trading — [[d:warrior#1449212320315084953]] (dindunuffinz, 2025-12-13). PvP resilience was hotfixed from ~2.7% to the intended 3% damage reduction per point around this time — [[d:warrior#1446190032556658801]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-12-04).
- **[weapon/gear for PvP]** Big slow one-handers (Castigator, Thunderfall) suit PvP fury; a specific Naxx polearm ("nicknamed 'shart' by the community") is valued in PvP for 41 stam + a 250-armor-pen proc, plus an AoE proc that instantly kills totems/summons — [[d:warrior#1445162890939076629]], [[d:warrior#1446190359452450869]], [[d:warrior#1446190473139196219]], [[d:warrior#1446190526859575326]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-12-02/04). Might of Menethil remains a popular Arms PvP weapon choice — [[d:warrior#1446190534170378371]] (Kyrsie, 2025-12-04). 2H Arms PvP trinket options beyond the PvP Medallion/Lifegiving Gem include Scarab Brooch and various "reflector" resistance trinkets from the database; two different-school reflector trinkets can be worn/procced simultaneously since they aren't unique against each other — [[d:warrior#1447447592358121514]], [[d:warrior#1447448267989450802]], [[d:warrior#1449388038709842061]] (Dodging/dindunuffinz, 2025-12-08/13).
- **[disarm]** Disarm always targets the mainhand weapon; a weapon-chain (disarm-immunity) enchant on either MH or OH grants immunity regardless of which hand it's on — [[d:warrior#1446923546918916287]] (Kyrsie, 2025-12-05).
- **[T2.5/T3.5 for PvP]** T2.5/Kara40-tier gear is regarded as containing some strong PvP pieces, e.g. helm/shoulders/chest slots for a fury PvP set — [[d:warrior#1449439737587957840]], [[d:warrior#1446262229929296034]] (Piemelbrie/Kyrsie, 2025-12-02/09).
- **[charge/intercept mechanics]** Community pushback item: Charge and Intercept currently share a PvP diminishing-returns category, which some want fixed/removed; historically (per correction), the "Juggernaut" talent let Charge be used while already in combat on a longer CD — it did not remove movement slows from Charge, which was reportedly never slowed to begin with — [[d:warrior#1446264636247900373]], [[d:warrior#1446265269633941617]], [[d:warrior#1446265387405803683]] (Blackjack/Lipibidy/Kyrsie, 2025-12-04; the DR complaint is a live wishlist item, not confirmed changed).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[stance/weapon-swap macros]** Swapping a single OH item (weapon↔shield) costs 1 GCD each way; swapping both MH+OH takes 2 GCDs total, so the macro must be pressed twice — [[d:warrior#1447701697060143296]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-09/10). GCD reference given while debugging a macro: Battle Stance and Defensive Stance are oGCD, out-of-combat weapon swaps don't trigger a GCD, Charge is a GCD, and equipping a 1H or a shield each costs a GCD — [[d:warrior#1448001402780778546]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-10).
- **[/equip macro pitfall]** `/equip` fails on items with long random-enchant suffixes like "of the Bear" — use just the base item name (e.g. "Templar Shield," not "Templar Shield of the Bear"); "of X"/random-enchant suffixes are never part of the name the macro needs — [[d:warrior#1447847336247034001]], [[d:warrior#1447848130166128650]], [[d:warrior#1447848204036214816]], [[d:warrior#1447705645704020121]] (12inchesfolded/Weirdthing/Don't ping me, 2025-12-08).
- **[HS/Cleave macro-ing]** Advice against macroing Heroic Strike into every ability button (e.g. cast-on-60-rage tied to BT/WW/Pummel) since it can "blindly" eat rage meant for WW/BT; recommended alternative is a dedicated HS/Cleave keybind on mousewheel-up/down or an extra mouse button so it can be pressed independently while still weaving core abilities — [[d:warrior#1446258485561000130]], [[d:warrior#1446259068116144250]], [[d:warrior#1446259180825739456]], [[d:warrior#1446259223292805242]] (Lipibidy/30, 2025-12-05). A simple HS-above-60-rage macro: `/cast [mypower:>60] Heroic Strike` — [[d:warrior#1446191925664415764]] (Kyrsie, 2025-12-04).
- **[filler-fishing macro]** A "fillerslop" macro used by some fury players stacks several low-priority filler spells in one button for auto-fallback casting: `/cast Master Strike` / `/cast Hamstring` / `/cast Pummel` / `/cast Sunder Armor` — [[d:warrior#1449457518412566559]] (Kyrsie, 2025-12-09).
- **[buff-cap tracking]** No dedicated buff-cap-resistant Battle Shout tracker exists; the workaround is a RoidMacros macro that only casts Battle Shout below a buff-count threshold (e.g. <31) and cancels other buffs (Rejuvenation/Renew/Daybreak/etc.) to make room — [[d:warrior#1448545125423775755]], [[d:warrior#1450127678299574495]] (30/Fypoolday, 2025-12-05/15). Battle Shout can silently fail to apply/display (falling off without warning) in large raids, a client-side issue several raiders hit independently, sometimes fixed by a `/reload` — [[d:warrior#1445417502501896343]], [[d:warrior#1446009212881866854]], [[d:warrior#1446009232238317660]] (KespadA/30, 2025-11-30/12-02).
- **[addon recommendations]** ModifiedPowerAuras is the Turtle-era WeakAuras equivalent — [[d:warrior#1449566983979798752]] (Kyrsie, 2025-12-13). "Attack Power Display" and Warrior Tweaks both provide an AP-tracking HUD, with Warrior Tweaks additionally flagging whether to press BT or Execute — [[d:warrior#1449160022792601772]], [[d:warrior#1449162382814216372]] (Neurospicy/Black Hat, 2025-12-13). Mik's Scrolling Battle Text (Miks) can track Sword Specialization procs — [[d:warrior#1447719315611586560]] (casey, 2025-12-09). https://github.com/pepopo978/SimpleActionSets can help swap action bars on a (Goblin Brainwashing Device) spec change — [[d:warrior#1449803099593248959]] (Squid, 2025-12-14). SP_SwingTimer can't reliably distinguish MH/OH hits and effectively guesses, causing desync for some users, and its Slam macro reportedly throws an error for at least two separate users in this chunk (root cause not resolved in-chunk) — [[d:warrior#1446241590992441437]], [[d:warrior#1449271834557288539]], [[d:warrior#1449446964646379801]] (30/Ikamuzu, 2025-12-03/09/15).
- **[performance]** Large-pack AoE deaths (e.g. AQ40 spider-split packs, K40 trash) cause FPS stutters/freezes for many players regardless of hardware, widely attributed to core client limitations rather than any one addon; commonly suggested mitigations are disabling MSBT, pfQuest, nameplate debuff/buff display, and unused PFUI modules, though results are inconsistent between players — [[d:warrior#1448642066279174275]], [[d:warrior#1448651452221358171]], [[d:warrior#1448673575329464330]], [[d:warrior#1448675863083356212]] (Dodging/Vlad/Kyrsie/Lans, 2025-12-11).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[race choice]** Weapon-skill racial changes (see Stats) mean race is "no BiS" for PvE; race is said to matter mainly for PvP now — [[d:warrior#1449352408567386112]], [[d:warrior#1449427148749279506]] (Dodging/Kyrsie, 2025-12-13).
- **[leveling professions]** Alchemy gives easy, durable value even if dropped at 60 (self-supplied stat food/elixirs/scrolls speed up leveling kills; some outputs like Frost Oil sell on the AH); Herbalism/Mining are simple, low-effort gold; JC/Engineering are expensive to level pre-60 unless self-funded; Blacksmithing/Enchanting can generate early gold via buckles, sold manually — [[d:warrior#1444330106830061751]], [[d:warrior#1444331459207757864]], [[d:warrior#1444332326292029571]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-29). At endgame: Engineering ("the sweat profession" — sappers add real DPS), Blacksmithing (Tempered Runeblade, "free" elemental sharpening stones, Dreamsteel gear), Alchemy (Mojo Madness, flasks, self-supplied MRP) are the standout picks — [[d:warrior#1444333624458743818]] (Aubreykun, 2025-11-29). Recommended endgame order: Herbalism first if your guild has an alchemist to craft for you (saves buying consumes), then a second profession funded by herb gold — [[d:warrior#1444347771556724928]] (Nost, 2025-11-29; mildly disputed — "farming gold is faster than collecting herbs," [[d:warrior#1444359895024668793]] Dodging). "Is there any good PvE prof other than Engi?" was raised without a strong alternative offered besides Alchemy (for self-sufficiency) — [[d:warrior#1445159836688978163]] (Lipibidy, 2025-12-02).
- **[Blacksmithing value, disputed]** Called "dogshit a lot of the time" / "severely overpriced" for low-level output relative to mats cost (e.g. 10 Arcanite Bars for an entry-dungeon-level blue) — [[d:warrior#1445797300621283461]], [[d:warrior#1445794745896144966]] (Sylvaira/Madcrep, 2025-12-01). Countered: it "has some good bits now," mostly preraid/early, letting you skip some preraid gearing by crafting (Rune Etched line, Tempered Runeblade) — [[d:warrior#1445158997660536987]], [[d:warrior#1445159094184054927]] (Aubreykun, 2025-12-02); Dreamsteel (armorsmithing) is "kinda meh" after a nerf and with alt tier sets available — [[d:warrior#1445159308408127650]] (Sylvaira, 2025-12-02).
- **[Strat farming as a warrior]** A described farm: pull from the Dead side through to the Service Gate (kills Unforgiven and a potential Skul spawn), needs the trash/repair-robot loot if pulling that far, and averages ~7-10 minutes solo without consumes once practiced (8-11 min including some consumes/help); requires an expensive Engineering BoP shield plus a "donor item" and specific enchants to reliably break even, so profit scales with speed/skill; recommended mainly if you only have a warrior (no gathering alts) — watch shield durability, Unforgiven's health, 2/2 Berserker Rage availability, Intercept, and the skeleton caster's mana — [[d:warrior#1450084604504576142]], [[d:warrior#1450093185354432584]], [[d:warrior#1450095301938384917]], [[d:warrior#1450106681311887441]], [[d:warrior#1450112056958259306]], [[d:warrior#1450112523998334978]] (Sweezy, 2025-12-15); disputed by at least one warrior who found the repair costs exceeded loot value on a different pull pattern — [[d:warrior#1450102963472175255]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-12-15).
- **[Earthstrike (trinket) farm]** Obtained via the "Silithus Field Duty" quest chain, best done in a small group of 1-3 (ideally with other classes wanting one) to speed up parallel quest turn-ins; solo as a warrior it's "a long boring time," roughly 2 days fast or up to a week solo/inexperienced — [[d:warrior#1447879428725280869]], [[d:warrior#1447931172985241690]], [[d:warrior#1447931228190937150]] (Sweezy/Virasan, 2025-12-08/09).
- **[fresh-60 onboarding]** Run dungeons for loot plus Onyxia/MC/Emerald-Sanctum attunements; Kara10 and ZG can be PUGged from the start as DPS; BWL+ generally needs a stable guild — [[d:warrior#1448776735113089153]] (Bassilthorne/Achuak, 2025-12-11). Stat priority to chase first: hit%, crit%, str, AP — [[d:warrior#1448776258497544313]] (Dodging, 2025-12-11).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Prot] One-Handed Weapon Specialization does not buff Shield Slam/Revenge/Concussion Blow** — only affects autos and Heroic Strike, unlike the (unchanged) Paladin equivalent; confirmed via live in-game testing after a report that it "stopped working" — full details and citations under Tanking specifics.
- **[Sapphiron's Frost aura is bugged/weak on Turtle** — raids reportedly run zero Frost Resistance and still handle the fight fine; see Tanking specifics / Dungeons & raids for citations.
- **[Slam + gear haste "double dip"]** Widely repeated observation that gear haste reduces Slam's cast time more than expected/intended, and that in BiS gear Slam now out-damages Bloodthirst on average for 2H fury — see Stats for citations; flagged by community members as possibly unintended and "might get patched."
- **[Master Strike macro issues]** Multiple independent reports of `/cast Master Strike` silently failing in macros (with SuperWoW+RoidMacros/Cleveroid) while other spells work fine, and of Master Strike pausing/resetting the MH swing timer when used with maces — root cause not resolved in this chunk — [[d:warrior#1446705851170820188]], [[d:warrior#1446744902502453290]] (somedeadguy/Toxicshock, 2025-12-06); mace-specific swing reset — [[d:warrior#1444661277518462976]], [[d:warrior#1444661361056546816]] (somedeadguy, 2025-11-30).
- **[Windfury as an aura, not a weapon enchant]** Windfury (from Shaman) is now implemented as an aura buff rather than a temporary weapon enchant on Turtle, so warriors can still sharpen their own mainhand independently — [[d:warrior#1444956037647175884]] (Breaddy, 2025-12-01).
- **[Tempest's Rage nerf]** Its proc rate (PPM) was reduced from 0.6-0.8 to 0.4 PPM at some point, making its observed uptime (~14-20% in one raider's testing) lower than expected and causing it to sometimes proc outside cooldown windows, losing value — [[d:warrior#1444640009280946227]], [[d:warrior#1444640274507763753]], [[d:warrior#1444640461318000724]] (30, 2025-11-29).
- **[HoJ/Windfury "myth"]** A rumor that Hand of Justice interacts badly with (or is somehow nullified by) Windfury is called "false information that became public knowledge about a year ago" — HoJ remains a legitimately good trinket, especially on Ambershire — [[d:warrior#1448598692767596545]], [[d:warrior#1448628767349276723]] (Lÿenn/Dodging, 2025-12-11).
- **[Sunder Armor proc interaction, unconfirmed]** Secondhand report that Sunder Armor no longer procs on-hit effects (e.g. Windfury) on Turtle — single source, not independently verified in this chunk — [[d:warrior#1448373275926331492]] (Dodging, 2025-12-10).
- **[dodge-proc cloak nerf]** A cloak that used to give a flat 1% dodge at all times was changed to instead occasionally grant a 20% dodge buff that's consumed/lost the instant you dodge anything — [[d:warrior#1445629903348432958]] (Sylvaira, 2025-12-03).
- **[turtlogs.com ranking display]** At least one raider saw the website's boss-damage ranking display a different (higher) DPS number than the same fight's raw combat log (1400 vs 1014 shown) — unresolved, cause unclear — [[d:warrior#1446175511683268812]] (Thirtyseven, 2025-12-01).
