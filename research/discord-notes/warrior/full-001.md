## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Turtle changed Enrage to be unlinked from Blood Craze, called a big DPS buff for 2H fury — [[d:warrior#1428912888428625981]] (Breaddy, 2025-10-18).
- **[General]** Imp Hamstring was removed from the game in a past patch; Hamstring's cooldown (vs other classes' tools) is cited as a PvP nerf source — [[d:warrior#1429234875562721342]], [[d:warrior#1429234816263389184]] (Lipibidy, 2025-10-19).
- **[General]** Slam was normalized (cast no longer disrupts swing timer the way it used to); Decisive Strike reportedly was not normalized — [[d:warrior#1428915610447904778]], [[d:warrior#1428915766261973123]], [[d:warrior#1428915809337475082]] (30/mark vasko, 2025-10-18).
- **[Execute]** Execute was reworked from an old "massive execute" flat/scaling model to a rage-refund model: each rage point spent above the 15 base converts to extra damage (roughly ~3% of AP per point per one estimate, or ~9 flat damage per another), which is why it visually drains all rage; players consider this rework a net nerf ("execute feels so ass rn", "arms execute buffs can't come soon enough") — [[d:warrior#1429823508879511663]], [[d:warrior#1429823761384738988]], [[d:warrior#1432020445883797536]], [[d:warrior#1432019564362727568]] (worlds #1 ppm expert/Xeraz, 2025-10-19; Sylvaira/axelfrog, 2025-10-26).
- **[Tank]** Dragonclaw Gauntlets (DCG) were changed to add haste and spirit, shifting them from a tank BiS item toward DPS BiS, upsetting tanks who wanted them buffed for tanking — [[d:warrior#1430531392776831179]], [[d:warrior#1430531137746243625]] (Kyrsie, 2025-10-22).
- **[Tank]** T3 tier set bonuses were "moved around" in a rework: good for DPS (a raid-wide party buff on 8pc that's worth wearing even off-pieces) but "dogshit for tanks" (2/4/6pc largely irrelevant, 5pc tied to a 30-min CD called weak) — [[d:warrior#1432220919132192882]], [[d:warrior#1432220982948397126]], [[d:warrior#1432220413231894538]], [[d:warrior#1432305773383254058]] (30/Lipibidy/mark vasko, 2025-10-22/27).
- **[Tank]** Reworked warrior class changes are blamed for messing up tank gearing generally ("recent class changes really messed up tank gearing") and for a "new/dogshit weapon skill formula" and hit-cap rework that changed pre-raid gearing assumptions — [[d:warrior#1430018980516462653]], [[d:warrior#1432060634345767104]] (mark vasko/wagehart, 2025-10-21/26).
- **[Tank]** Since a rogue DPS buff, some Deep Prot tanks report specific low-incoming-damage fights (e.g. Naxx Anub'Rekhan) becoming harder to hold threat on because rage generation is too low to recover from Shield Slam parries — [[d:warrior#1433041880400265321]], [[d:warrior#1433041808346054687]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-29; single source).
- **[PvP]** A Deep Wounds bug fix was implemented and is credited with meaningfully raising warrior PvP damage output (screenshot of hours of WSG/AB damage cited as proof) — [[d:warrior#1431914592052711465]] (Spiff, 2025-10-26; +reactions, well received).
- **[Rumor]** Feral druid threat is rumored/expected to be adjusted (nerfed) in 1.18.1; devs reportedly said not to expect a significant warrior class-change patch soon — [[d:warrior#1430538813134082068]], [[d:warrior#1430522403280257036]], [[d:warrior#1430518444205412452]] (Dodging/Breaddy, 2025-10-22; rumor, unconfirmed).
- **[Rumor]** 2H Fury was reportedly on a list being considered for a slight nerf ("i believe 2h fury was possibly on the list for slight nerfs") — [[d:warrior#1432024915921604649]] (Sylvaira, 2025-10-26; single source, unconfirmed, "slight" met with skepticism by others).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[2H Fury]** 2H Fury with a top weapon (e.g. BRE) is considered top MC-phase DPS by many, especially on fresh (Ambershire) servers where BRE and Windfury make it dominant on trash and competitive on bosses; DW Fury with Viskag+Tempest Rage is close but generally trails in Kara40-style content because of heavy snap-AoE demands — [[d:warrior#1430307135836782652]], [[d:warrior#1430326909778133103]], [[d:warrior#1429865745176596611]], [[d:warrior#1432012695082766408]] (Overman/ralleballe/axelfrog, 2025-10-18/22/26).
- **[Arms vs Fury]** Consensus shifted through the chunk: early on, "Arms is worse than 2h fury now" once gear/rage income improves ([[d:warrior#1429209428367769650]], Sebastian_, 2025-10-19); later (2025-10-26) a long debate concluded Arms is strong in progression/lower gear (no Flurry-crit reliance, no "800 AP wet noodle BT") and can top trash/compete on bosses with BRE+WF, while DW/2H Fury pulls far ahead once crit gear is available — disputed with screenshot evidence of Arms warriors "mogging" top Fury parses on Ambershire, and other reports that fury heavily outperforms arms there — [[d:warrior#1431901213770780722]], [[d:warrior#1432012539444596920]], [[d:warrior#1432017512597422243]], [[d:warrior#1432013482005499984]] (axelfrog vs Dodging/evella, 2025-10-26; genuinely contested, both sides bring log evidence).
- **[Execute]** "Really sucks" for 2H Arms; execute rework (rage-refund) is broadly seen as a nerf to Arms specifically since Arms leaned on big executes — [[d:warrior#1429205791625314324]], [[d:warrior#1432019564362727568]] (Sebastian_/Sylvaira, 2025-10-19/26).
- **[DW Fury]** Called a "meme spec" by some vs strictly-better 2H, but top players still post competitive logs (30: #5 overall Naxx DPS as DW fury with no Champion buff; 3.1k DPS on Anub using Badge/Slayer's without even using Kiss) — [[d:warrior#1432071292357709935]], [[d:warrior#1432071606343172267]], [[d:warrior#1432070035861536868]] (Sebastian_/30, 2025-10-26; disputed/single-source counter-evidence).
- **[Tank]** Fury Prot is the dominant warrior MT/tanking spec on Turtle; guilds reportedly use warrior tanks widely for MC/BWL/ZG/AQ20/dungeons, but feral druids and paladins dominate at higher content (K40/Naxx); "Weird Vibes" cited as one of very few guilds still running a warrior MT — [[d:warrior#1430498927286685738]], [[d:warrior#1430499487465345035]], [[d:warrior#1430501588828688485]], [[d:warrior#1430506152663449672]] (Dodging/ralleballe/angels1m, 2025-10-22).
- **[Tank]** Deep Prot ("defensive tactics") widely called weak/boring/a "noob trap": lacks threat scaling, no strong capstone, forces leaving Defensive Stance to keep threat via Defensive Tactics/Impale, "Revenge is a clown show ability." Several devs quoted (secondhand) as saying warrior tanking is by design meant to be an off-tank role — [[d:warrior#1430484431931707442]], [[d:warrior#1430493432350244948]], [[d:warrior#1430525172829388850]], [[d:warrior#1430490807508668426]] (Guts/Velanir, 2025-10-22; long thread, broad agreement Deep Prot underperforms, some disagreement on how bad). Later in the chunk (2025-10-29), tankers converge on a more nuanced take: Deep Prot ("DT") is actually preferred for progression/MT#1 because it's a safer/more consistent threat floor, while Fury Prot is better for OT/farm once gear and healer sustain allow it — [[d:warrior#1433066687787303146]], [[d:warrior#1433040494107168900]] (Black Hat/worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-10-29).
- **[Tank]** Feral (bear) druid does ~2x the TPS of Fury Prot in Naxx/Kara40 gear per one log comparison shared by 30/mark vasko ("bear generates almost 2x threat of furyprot, both in bis") — [[d:warrior#1430538669164466290]]; disputed by rel/Kyrsie who argue warrior is still "the best tank" — [[d:warrior#1429577810615472249]], [[d:warrior#1429578050294780004]] (contested, 2025-10-20/22).
- **[Tank]** Black Hat's top-10-clear-time guild runs 2 Def Tactics (deep prot) tanks successfully (MC 1:20, BWL 1:15 best logs) as counter-example that Deep Prot can work with good dps discipline — [[d:warrior#1430532047759212544]], [[d:warrior#1430533289395097680]] (single guild report, disputed as not generalizable by Guts).
- **[PvP]** Warrior widely considered weak in PvP on Turtle; countered mainly by shamans (hard counter on Alliance) and rogues; Blood Drinker talent called "just THAT GOOD" and DW Fury + Blood Drinker considered the best warrior solo PvP spec currently — [[d:warrior#1429234682654097438]], [[d:warrior#1429235367306858558]], [[d:warrior#1429239229472444446]], [[d:warrior#1429648087416049704]] (Lipibidy/Dodging, 2025-10-19/20).

- **[Dungeon tanking]** Prot recommended over Arms/Fury for leveling dungeon tanking past ~level 40 because mob damage in Twow dungeons is high and healers struggle; Prot unlocks Shield Slam at 30, opening up the spec — [[d:warrior#1429822325510897794]] (Xeraz, 2025-10-20). Countered later: Arms/Fury/DW with a shield in the bag can tank every leveling dungeon fine and generate more threat than a leveling Prot warrior, respeccing Prot is not required — [[d:warrior#1432864256499388647]], [[d:warrior#1432864298937483405]] (Kyrsie, 2025-10-29; disputed/contested).

## Leveling
- **[Fury DW]** Rotation advice for a lvl-47 DW fury doing low DPS: don't spam Heroic Strike unless massive rage surplus; stockpile rage so you can always push Bloodthirst on CD; keep Bloodrage and Death Wish on CD but time them so they land when you actually have rage; try to land 2 BT per enrage window plus WW/cleave; use cheap Agility elixirs/scrolls; prefer slower main-hand weapons — [[d:warrior#1429030610944659488]], [[d:warrior#1429031026226626694]] (Friekshow, 2025-10-18; well-received, no dissent).
- **[Leveling]** Arms is the commonly recommended leveling spec; DW warriors reach Mortal Strike around level 40 by putting points into Arms starting earlier; Blood Frenzy (later Arms talent) "reduces downtime A LOT" — [[d:warrior#1429375776452640849]], [[d:warrior#1432864418168836240]], [[d:warrior#1431368475909034054]] (Jae/Kyrsie/Zeedeo, 2025-10-19/24/29).
- **[Leveling]** Any of Arms/Fury/DW can tank every leveling dungeon and generate more threat than a leveling Prot warrior; keep a shield in the bag (not equipped) for occasional hard-hitting bosses like the moss elemental in Wailing Caverns, without needing to respec — [[d:warrior#1432864139881222295]], [[d:warrior#1432864206025134151]] (Kyrsie, 2025-10-29).
- **[Leveling]** Heroic Strike is not recommended much while leveling, especially with a 2H, due to poor rage efficiency at low gear — [[d:warrior#1432937946989854780]] (30, 2025-10-29).
- **[Leveling]** Weapon recommendation: an SS(Sweeping Strikes)+WW(Whirlwind) Axe is recommended as a leveling weapon "until it starts to lag behind"; other named leveling weapon waypoints mentioned: Staff of Westfall (early), Outlaw Sabre (BFD quest), Bonebiter Axe (post-WW Axe) — [[d:warrior#1431565239698063361]], [[d:warrior#1432391230427500685]], [[d:warrior#1432396223263932456]], [[d:warrior#1432355377449664692]] (ralleballe/Julian/Tole/Flare, 2025-10-25/29).
- **[Leveling]** General weapon-buying advice while leveling: DPS is the most important stat, weapon speed doesn't matter much (though slow is slightly better), 2H usually beats 1H in raw damage, but prefer 1H if stats are equal so you can carry a shield; don't grind dungeons repeatedly for weapon upgrades — buy a cheap blue off the AH instead since 0 XP grinding is counterproductive to leveling speed — [[d:warrior#1432394705580195890]], [[d:warrior#1432394998380236880]], [[d:warrior#1432395222012399616]] (Tole/Dodging, 2025-10-27).
- **[Leveling]** Fiery Weapon enchant/proc called strong ("op") for cheap twinked-out leveling gear, since players can afford to overinvest enchants into low-level items — [[d:warrior#1432894256057548913]], [[d:warrior#1432894726444552406]] (ralleballe/axelfrog, 2025-10-29).

## Rotation: single target
- **[DW Fury]** Priority is Bloodthirst > Whirlwind > Slam, or filler between BT/WW cooldowns should be Master Strike > Pummel > Hamstring > Sunder (not just Heroic Strike) to keep proccing Windfury/Crusader; spamming only BT/WW/HS "gimps" DW fury DPS — [[d:warrior#1428919947429412885]], [[d:warrior#1428920063942983761]], [[d:warrior#1429836696479989844]] (Vlad/mark vasko/Don't ping me, 2025-10-18/20).
- **[2H Fury]** BT does higher damage than Slam per swing; open debate on BT>Slam>WW vs Slam>BT>WW ordering, said to depend on weapon/gear (BT scales off AP, Slam off weapon damage) — [[d:warrior#1428914800074690670]], [[d:warrior#1428920063942983761]], [[d:warrior#1428920334060617870]] (mark vasko, 2025-10-18).

- **[General]** Heroic Strike is a static-damage bonus layered on top of a normal swing (turns it into a yellow hit); Bloodrage should be popped right before Bloodthirst to fit another BT inside the resulting Enrage window — [[d:warrior#1430101657315967098]], [[d:warrior#1430100987120713768]] (Velanir, 2025-10-21).
- **[Execute]** Execute's listed 15-rage cost is a minimum; extra rage above that converts into extra damage (visually consumes all rage); using Heroic Strike during Execute phase is a DPS loss on Turtle and should be avoided ("you shouldn't heroic strike during execute phase on turtle") — [[d:warrior#1429823508879511663]], [[d:warrior#1429823761384738988]], [[d:warrior#1431724717789679626]] (worlds #1 ppm expert/Xeraz, 2025-10-19; mark vasko, 2025-10-25).
- **[2H Fury]** There is a minimum AP threshold above which it's better to use Bloodthirst than Execute inside execute phase; this threshold used to be lower before Improved Execute reduced Execute's rage cost — [[d:warrior#1432093301963493378]], [[d:warrior#1432093844798963923]] (Vlad/30, 2025-10-27; exact number not given, single source).
- **[DW Fury]** For OT/DPS in raids, swapping the main-hand to a 2H weapon (e.g. Unstoppable Force) specifically for trash pulls, then swapping back for bosses, is a known technique ("khoni guide" swap) — [[d:warrior#1432023396169810000]], [[d:warrior#1432023579133874197]] (evella, 2025-10-26).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Tank AoE]** Current warrior AoE-tanking toolkit consensus: go Arms, use Sweeping Strikes + Whirlwind + Cleave — [[d:warrior#1430507109866278922]] (angels1m, 2025-10-22).
- **[Dungeon tank]** For AoE trash: mark skull, spam Thunder Clap on CD, use Cleave when rage allows or Shield Slam/Sunder across targets while holding main-target threat; if a DPS overaggros a non-lethal add, let them tank it 2-3s then Taunt it back — [[d:warrior#1429914054545117269]], [[d:warrior#1429914298666193058]] (Junak'Salsa, 2025-10-20).
- **[Dungeon tank]** Detailed pull sequence: mark skull, open with Demoralizing Shout then Thunder Clap, then start tabbing targets; Battle Shout is bad for AoE threat because it splits threat evenly across the whole pack instead of building it on you; Improved Thunder Clap is great for dungeon packs; don't panic if DPS pull aggro off-mark — let them tank it briefly, mobs will re-target; Gag Order is a good Charge alternative since most dungeon pulls aren't charge-pulls — [[d:warrior#1431974078104993982]], [[d:warrior#1431975527245283359]], [[d:warrior#1431975766190456924]] (Black Hat, 2025-10-25).
- **[Tank]** Recommended deep-prot rotation priority for both single/multi-target: 1. Shield Slam, 2. Revenge/Overpower, 3. Concussion Blow, 4. Heroic Strike (rage ≥37), 5. Sunder Armor (rage ≥47) — [[d:warrior#1430873262748860497]] (Twospooky, 2025-10-22). Alternate pull sequence: Concussion Blow > Shield Slam on pull, then Shield Slam > Concussion Blow > Overpower > Heroic Strike to dump rage; fill in Sunders if rage generation is low — [[d:warrior#1430730458462814341]], [[d:warrior#1430730854350589952]] (Dodging, 2025-10-21).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit/Weapon skill]** Hit cap vs a boss is 8% at 300 weapon skill; every 5 weapon skill points above that reduces the needed hit by 1% — [[d:warrior#1432106446987858031]], [[d:warrior#1432023348405211266]], [[d:warrior#1432056588532977877]] (Dodging/HeadshotHank/worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-10-26/27; consistent across multiple confirmations).
- **[Hit]** Weapon skill also gives roughly -0.05% dodge chance (against boss) per point; boss dodge formula given as roughly 6.5% baseline minus 0.1% per weapon-skill point (i.e. ~5% floor around 315 WS), though posters flagged this as unconfirmed ("i think?") — [[d:warrior#1428916107389042870]], [[d:warrior#1430568105322746048]], [[d:warrior#1430568358390005791]] (30, 2025-10-18; Black Hat, 2025-10-22).
- **[Glancing blows]** Weapon skill only reduces the damage penalty of glancing blows, not how often they occur — glance frequency cannot otherwise be reduced — [[d:warrior#1431364492884971613]], [[d:warrior#1431364604814164120]] (Dodging/Black Hat, 2025-10-24).
- **[Hit/Crit mechanics]** "30"'s attack-table explainer: think of it as ~100 slots vs a boss — roughly 40 glance, 5 dodge, 20 crit, 8 hit(non-crit), 27 miss. More hit converts miss-slots to hit; more crit converts hit-slots to crit; the crit cap is reached once you run out of hit-slots to convert. Crit cap is only meaningfully attainable on white/auto swings, not on abilities, because glances (~40% of the table) don't apply to yellow (ability) hits; on bosses the main-hand is mostly occupied by Heroic Strike/Cleave rather than plain autos, so the character-sheet MH crit number is misleading for actual ability output — [[d:warrior#1432077296428257454]], [[d:warrior#1432079220284063897]], [[d:warrior#1432079323719794892]], [[d:warrior#1432079571423068160]] (30, 2025-10-26).
- **[DW Fury]** Threat/DPS priority for stats when hit-capped: excess hit rating should be converted to Agility or Crit, since MH hit-cap (accounting for dodge/parry) already guarantees ~100% MH landing with Heroic Strike; off-hand hit is lower priority for tanks than for DPS since DPS relies on OH hits for rage generation — [[d:warrior#1430006879731322963]], [[d:warrior#1430007050578165761]], [[d:warrior#1430007342480490506]] (30, 2025-10-21). Going slightly over the yellow-hit cap isn't bad for DW fury — [[d:warrior#1432023243174445218]] (Dodging, 2025-10-26).
- **[Tank threat stat priority]** General stat weight for tank threat given as: Agility/Crit > Strength > Attack Power > Shield Block Value (SBV) > Haste, with the caveat that SBV might rank slightly higher but almost never appears alongside agi/crit gear, making it hard to itemize for — [[d:warrior#1433037675350200380]], [[d:warrior#1433038184882765845]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-10-29).
- **[Tank SBV vs Crit]** Rough conversion (pre-Glyph): 1% crit ≈ 5.2 Shield Block Value for Shield Slam threat specifically at 1000 AP, but ≈ 17.3 SBV when considering overall damage output — meaning crit is far more valuable than SBV once total output (not just SS) is counted. Once a tank gets the Shield Slam Glyph, SBV's value drops sharply further because the abilities scale off each other and crit becomes king — [[d:warrior#1433065224558219526]], [[d:warrior#1433072433220550758]], [[d:warrior#1433069126510837873]] (Black Hat, 2025-10-29). On "good" RNG, ~300 SBV + 15% crit performs similarly to ~150 SBV + 30% crit, but on bad RNG the low-SBV/high-crit build is much swingier/worse — [[d:warrior#1433065959400144906]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-29).
- **[Shield Slam]** Shield Slam's AP scaling coefficient is 0.15 (not 0.35 as one poster assumed), and the whole ability carries roughly a 1.5x threat multiplier on its damage — [[d:warrior#1433037297477226496]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, correcting Weirdthing, 2025-10-29).
- **[Threat formulas]** Community-derived (partially disputed) threat coefficients discussed: BT Threat ≈ AP × 0.45 × 1.49 = 0.67×AP; Shield Slam threat ≈ (0.15×AP + SBV) × 1.49 × 1.5 = 0.34×AP + 2.24×SBV; corrections offered: Defensive Tactics threat modifier may be 1.51 rather than 1.49, Shield Slam users benefit from Impale's 1.2x crit multiplier that Fury Prot lacks, Battle Stance gives an extra 10% damage, and Toughness affects SBV +15% — [[d:warrior#1430240552221147220]], [[d:warrior#1430310785107755079]] (Artifyce/Nost, 2025-10-22; contested/refined, not fully resolved — separately, Bloodthirst's raw damage formula was also stated elsewhere in the chunk as "200 + 0.35 AP" [[d:warrior#1430316752037875796]], Julian, which conflicts with the 0.45 figure above — unresolved discrepancy).
- **[Haste]** Haste is repeatedly called the strongest/most broken stat to stack when available: "why is 2h fury outperforming arms? Because flurry. Why is KoTS so busted? Because 20% haste" — [[d:warrior#1429931648450957492]] (Lipibidy, 2025-10-21); disputed in practice for small increments: a 2% shoulder haste enchant has a very low chance to grant an extra attack, and haste interacts poorly with Windfury procs (a WF instant mid-swing wastes the haste-shortened swing timer) — [[d:warrior#1429932083647611051]], [[d:warrior#1429932646854824047]] (30, 2025-10-21).
- **[Armor Pen vs Haste]** For a 2H Fury shoulder enchant: Armor Penetration is worth roughly 2 ARP = 1 AP in the best case (near-0 armor target), so 40 ARP ≈ 20 AP best case, and 2% haste "definitely clears" that bar; ARP value is highly raid/fight-dependent (worthless once a boss/mob is already debuffed to near-0 armor) — [[d:warrior#1431711149308641410]], [[d:warrior#1431710806239609033]] (Overman/Dodging, 2025-10-25).
- **[DW weapon choice]** Weapon speed for DW is not a hard "always slow/always fast" rule: use whichever weapons are strongest available and upgrade to same-or-higher-ilvl slow weapons when they appear; slow OH favored mainly for steadier rage, but higher weapon DPS matters more; MH weapon is what's checked for weapon-skill procs — [[d:warrior#1429934395388727397]], [[d:warrior#1429934184633208842]], [[d:warrior#1429934606710214666]], [[d:warrior#1429934950026580070]] (Lipibidy, 2025-10-21).
- **[Deep Wounds]** Deep Wounds procs off either weapon's crit (MH crits more often since MH does the weapon-skill/ability hits); tick damage = 60% of that weapon's *average* damage (average of the low/high roll), which is much lower for fast weapons — so a slow, high-average-damage weapon produces bigger Deep Wounds ticks even at equal DPS — [[d:warrior#1432538486359199895]], [[d:warrior#1432538765733134438]], [[d:warrior#1432538657826275419]], [[d:warrior#1432545800612745409]] (Lipibidy/30/Lokimanor, 2025-10-28).
- **[Race]** Racial passive DPS differences are small: sim (50-65s fight) gave Undead racial 1829 DPS, Berserking (troll) 1818 DPS, Blood Fury (orc) 1814 DPS — effectively a wash, "gameplay determines dps by a factor of 100" more than race — [[d:warrior#1429287230886772807]], [[d:warrior#1429287519261818910]] (30, 2025-10-18; +reactions, agreed).
- **[Race]** Weapon-skill racial (e.g. human's) is "not really that big of a deal" — the difference between 312 and 315 weapon skill is worth less than 40 AP, 1% crit, and 1% hit — [[d:warrior#1430712144659353631]], [[d:warrior#1432578585461260390]] (ralleballe/Dodging, 2025-10-22/28).
- **[Tank]** Tanks reportedly don't need Nature Resist gear normally for most fights; can pop Shield Wall during frenzy-type mechanics instead (unconfirmed, "haven't tested it") — [[d:warrior#1430567824753168426]] (Nost, 2025-10-22).
- **[Consumables/AP]** Winterfall Firewater, Juju Power, and R.O.I.D.S. all stack additively (contrary to one player's confusion that R.O.I.D.S. wasn't adding to their AP display, which turned out to be a stale addon). Example numbers from one character: 1202 AP unbuffed → 1262 AP with Juju Power (+60) → 1297 AP with Juju Power + Firewater (+95 total, so Firewater ≈ +35) → 1347 AP with all three plus R.O.I.D.S. (+150 total, R.O.I.D.S. ≈ +50) — [[d:warrior#1431713868559814667]] (30, 2025-10-25).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Threat]** Taunt on Turtle behaves differently from classic: it only copies the target's current aggro level at the moment the debuff is applied (rather than continuously tracking the max aggro during the whole debuff), and it can fail to work if the target has lost its current target or is crowd-controlled — don't taunt a stunned/CC'd target — [[d:warrior#1430903401973285066]], [[d:warrior#1430903507375882241]], [[d:warrior#1430903627610062879]], [[d:warrior#1430905290030583808]] (MoneyCow, 2025-10-24).
- **[Threat]** Sunder Armor is still an MT-threat priority even if a rogue is doing Improved Expose Armor — "half the reason for it is to make the MT generate more threat," and it also helps raid DPS via the armor debuff — [[d:warrior#1432732529512808458]], [[d:warrior#1432733509046374510]], [[d:warrior#1432744898775482398]] (Black Hat/mark vasko/Jimbo, 2025-10-28).
- **[Threat]** Sunder Armor is meh solo but important in dungeons/raids — [[d:warrior#1432895408979443878]] (axelfrog, 2025-10-29).
- **[Trinkets]** Earthstrike and (Molten) Emberstone trinkets share the "in-use" effect cooldown but not the recharge cooldown — using one locks out the other for the duration of its effect (~20s), so an addon like TrinketMenu can be used to pre-swap ~30s before the recharge finishes to always have one ready for trash — [[d:warrior#1432854794875306046]] (JohnGood, 2025-10-29).
- **[Cooldowns]** Death Wish can be used more than once in a single boss fight on longer encounters (e.g. Chromaggus) — [[d:warrior#1431726255010480278]] (mark vasko, 2025-10-25). Recklessness has "almost no wrong time" to use it and is especially good popped on pull for DPS-race-style bosses, giving a temporary burst comparable to a bear's threat/DPS burst for ~15s — [[d:warrior#1431726048051069100]], [[d:warrior#1433043705350328412]], [[d:warrior#1433043406870810624]] (Squid/worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-10-24/29).
- **[Tank]** Reprisal is called "pretty sleeper" (underrated) for 5-man dungeon tanking — [[d:warrior#1431523184880123955]] (Dodging, 2025-10-25).
- **[Tank]** Grim Batol rumor: joked that everything in the dungeon might be nature-immune to punish warriors further — this is speculative dev-banter, not a confirmed mechanic (recorded under Contested).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Deep Prot vs Fury Prot]** Extensive, running debate throughout the chunk (see Spec viability): Deep Prot ("defensive tactics") is widely seen as clunky/weak at low gear (crit too low for Impale, Revenge investment low-value, forced out of Defensive Stance for threat) but valued for progression/MT#1 because it provides a safer, more consistent threat floor that's less reliant on crit RNG; Fury Prot scales much better with gear (dual-wield/Flurry/Bloodthirst, Death Wish+Bloodrage burst) and is preferred for OT roles and once healers can sustain more incoming damage — [[d:warrior#1433066687787303146]], [[d:warrior#1433040494107168900]], [[d:warrior#1430236420190244864]] (Black Hat/worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-10-29; Velanir/Nost, 2025-10-22).
- **[Stance]** For a Defensive Tactics build, Battle Stance is generally preferred over Defensive Stance itself: Defensive Tactics grants most of Defensive Stance's threat modifier anyway (confirmed "almost identical" by 30) while Battle Stance keeps access to Overpower (which scales better than Revenge) and a fuller spell kit; opening in Battle Stance then swapping to Defensive once threat is secure is common; Berserker Stance is generally too risky (extra damage taken for only 3% crit + Whirlwind) — [[d:warrior#1430848306501779527]], [[d:warrior#1430849314011222116]], [[d:warrior#1433155046756847689]], [[d:warrior#1433155155259298015]] (Sylvaira, 2025-10-22; Mortistrike/30, 2025-10-29).
- **[Gearing]** Most tanks maintain three gear loadouts: "full threat," "beefy threat," and "full mitigation" — [[d:warrior#1430018559806935070]] (30, 2025-10-21).
- **[Rage generation]** Fury Prot's rage generation is more consistent than Deep Prot's because dual-wielding constantly generates rage from the off-hand, whereas Deep Prot loses momentum whenever Shield Slam is parried/dodged — [[d:warrior#1430230686794776746]] (Dodging, 2025-10-22). On low-incoming-damage fights (e.g. Naxx Anub'Rekhan, especially after a rogue DPS buff), a rage-starved Deep Prot tank can lose threat badly from just a couple of Shield Slam parries — [[d:warrior#1433041880400265321]] (Weirdthing, 2025-10-29).
- **[Tank weapon choice]** For a fast/slow off-hand weapon debate while tanking (e.g. Blackjack at 2.8s), a faster weapon helps land a second Heroic Strike sooner during the opening few seconds of a pull, though it matters less once Deftac isn't spamming Heroic Strike every swing on bosses — [[d:warrior#1431300026629619723]], [[d:warrior#1431300437004386375]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-10-24). For general tank weapon selection: DPS number is the top priority, then stats, and speed only as a tiebreaker — [[d:warrior#1432331134343188540]] (Black Hat, 2025-10-27).
- **[Threat gear]** Weapon skill above ~309–315 matters more for tanks primarily to reduce boss dodge chance and glancing penalty; a common attainable number is 309 WS (5 from weapon-skill quest + 4 from various items) — [[d:warrior#1432581448053035029]], [[d:warrior#1432581823824662650]] (Diabase, 2025-10-28).
- **[Dungeon-tank gear bootstrapping]** A dps-geared warrior transitioning to tank does not need dedicated "tank gear" — DPS gear is threat gear; just wear plate with high stamina pieces and it will hold aggro fine in dungeons while learning — [[d:warrior#1431018662185144463]], [[d:warrior#1431019487708057642]], [[d:warrior#1431019605656342638]] (Dodging/Nost, 2025-10-24). Stormwind Vault farm recommended for a full "Elite Guard" tank set, though the instance itself is considered bad to learn tanking in (cramped, easy to overpull) — [[d:warrior#1431020503186931762]], [[d:warrior#1431020574653812938]], [[d:warrior#1431024107922915428]] (Nost, 2025-10-24).
- **[Transitioning DPS→Tank]** For a DPS player transitioning to Fury Prot MT, the easiest path is dual-wielding in DPS gear while in Defensive Stance with Salvation turned off, and a quick-swap shield macro for emergencies — [[d:warrior#1432039578155352075]], [[d:warrior#1432040577494548513]] (Dodging, 2025-10-27).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[MC, Thaurissan]** As MT you should position Thaurissan out of the electrical-field circle and stand in the circle yourself to spread the parry load off the raid (but note: this shifts more parries onto the tank) — [[d:warrior#1429479733795623062]], [[d:warrior#1429479943334662275]], [[d:warrior#1429479848341930004]] (mark vasko/Dodging, 2025-10-19).
- **[BWL, Vael]** Sunder discipline matters even if a Paladin is MT'ing Vael — a warrior watched on VOD stood idle at the start doing nothing (stunned) and only reached 4 stacks of Sunder after 44 seconds, 5th stack at 11% boss HP — critique was to sunder as first GCD on pull regardless of role, and not delay Bloodthirst usage waiting on Sunders — [[d:warrior#1431721323088056360]], [[d:warrior#1431722024899973191]], [[d:warrior#1431722872363024435]], [[d:warrior#1431722991615606815]] (mark vasko/Dodging, 2025-10-25). Don't use Heroic Strike during Execute phase on Lashslayer/Vael-style execute windows — [[d:warrior#1431724688475820074]], [[d:warrior#1431724717789679626]] (mark vasko, 2025-10-25). Tank threat comparison: a Thunderfury-wielding warrior tank should out-threat a fast-dagger (1.5 speed) warrior tank on Vael, all else equal — [[d:warrior#1431421758526849024]] (Black Hat, 2025-10-25; single-guild comparison).
- **[BWL, Firemaw]** Fire Resistance gear used to resist a stacking fire debuff; typically one FR-geared tank takes over once the main tank has generated enough threat and needs to shed stacks (except during Wing Buffet); less progression-focused guilds may just run both tanks in full FR the whole fight — [[d:warrior#1431619923934842920]], [[d:warrior#1431620100208984246]], [[d:warrior#1431620799495929938]] (Velanir/Kyrsie/Tole, 2025-10-25).
- **[BWL, Nefarian]** The Ony/Fallen-God-style fire-resist cloak is specifically important on Nefarian; melee should wear it there (and on the three drakes) even if they don't normally gear-swap for trash/other bosses — [[d:warrior#1432580991200919656]], [[d:warrior#1432582059502604378]] (Dodging/Diabase, 2025-10-28).
- **[BWL, Chromaggus]** Death Wish can be popped twice in this longer fight — [[d:warrior#1431726255010480278]] (mark vasko, 2025-10-25).
- **[AQ40, Viscidus]** Melee should use frost-proc weapon oils (Coldrage Dagger effect, Hammer of the Northern Wind) or a frost wand; more frost damage sources across the raid speeds the kill and reduces healer strain from running out of mana — [[d:warrior#1430573201766879313]], [[d:warrior#1430575038922363016]], [[d:warrior#1430575289335021578]] (Black Hat/Velanir, 2025-10-22). Sappers are called BiS for this fight — [[d:warrior#1430634477323026604]] (Virasan, 2025-10-22).
- **[AQ40, Chess event]** Badge trinket is good here specifically on Rook and Knight pieces if the kill is slow — [[d:warrior#1432069431412129883]] (30, 2025-10-27).
- **[AQ40 (implied), Sartura/Trio/Ouro]** Sartura and the Trio require a lot of add "wrangling"; Ouro requires chasing threat and repositioning on your turn — [[d:warrior#1431446441515286559]], [[d:warrior#1431446484980863097]] (Squid, 2025-10-25).
- **[Naxx, Gluth]** Piercing Howl (slow effect) is useful for kiting zombies in some raids, though other guilds instead just have a resto druid kite them without a howl warrior — [[d:warrior#1429660519433768960]], [[d:warrior#1430016848631697538]], [[d:warrior#1429660776347467917]] (mark vasko/Dodging, 2025-10-21).
- **[Naxx, Anub'Rekhan]** Deep Prot MT threat can suffer here because the boss "hits like a noodle" (low tank damage → low rage) so a couple of Shield Slam parries can lose the fight's threat lead; some warriors switch to Fury Prot when raiding with high-DPS rogues to compensate — [[d:warrior#1433041880400265321]], [[d:warrior#1433043053450629150]] (Weirdthing/worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-10-29).
- **[Naxx, spider wing]** Challenging Shout rotations called out by the raid leader are the recommended way to handle spider adds rather than relying on a single tank's AoE taunt — [[d:warrior#1431121542279991370]] (Dodging, 2025-10-24).
- **[Naxx, Four Horsemen (4HM)]** Two Shield Wall-triggering thresholds exist at 50% and 20% boss HP; each shield wall window lasts 20s, but if the 20% trigger is reached within 30s of the 50% trigger's activation, that shield wall can be skipped; there's roughly 10s of "leeway" after a shield wall window closes where you can still get credit before the next one; tanks should push burn windows (e.g. Death Wish) to align right with a Shield Wall proc for max value — [[d:warrior#1430031704332173382]], [[d:warrior#1430032331799924818]], [[d:warrior#1430070491204288655]], [[d:warrior#1430071296653262948]], [[d:warrior#1430071762657476731]] (Ryiana/30/Velanir, 2025-10-21).
- **[K40 scepter questline, Winterspring]** Killing Bovar'kez as part of the Kara40 scepter questline reportedly needs ~20-30 people, a decurse for a debilitating Cripple debuff (worse combined with Rain of Fire), and tank-swapping due to a stacking debuff on the tank — [[d:warrior#1429941194359832607]], [[d:warrior#1429941234121572354]], [[d:warrior#1429941734560890890]], [[d:warrior#1429941780769669273]] (Virasan/30, 2025-10-21).
- **[Dungeon leveling tanking]** For AoE dungeon tanking as leveling Prot/Arms: mark skull, open with Demoralizing Shout + Thunder Clap, then tab-target; Battle Shout is bad here (splits threat); Sweeping Strikes+Whirlwind+Cleave for Arms is the go-to AoE toolkit — [[d:warrior#1429914054545117269]], [[d:warrior#1431974078104993982]] (Junak'Salsa/Black Hat, 2025-10-20/25).

- **[Race]** Human is the most commonly recommended race (sword racial + straightforward DW gearing path via Kara10/AQ20 weapon-skill items); Orc trades easier early weapon skill (axe racial) for weak late-game axe options; race-to-race DPS/racial differences are said to be minimal overall — [[d:warrior#1429273880291184641]], [[d:warrior#1429277012014661695]], [[d:warrior#1429278026637639750]] (JohnGood/ralleballe, 2025-10-19). Weapon-skill racial specifically is "not a big deal" (worth <40 AP, 1% crit, 1% hit) — [[d:warrior#1430712144659353631]] (ralleballe, 2025-10-22).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Crit/Weapon skill]** Spellbook-displayed crit percentage is inaccurate: any weapon-skill "overage" past your base skill (e.g. from THC/Ouro gloves stacking) gets mistakenly added into the displayed crit at a rate of 0.04% crit per point of overage, inflating the number shown vs actual performance — [[d:warrior#1428918579587321939]], [[d:warrior#1428918677629173890]], [[d:warrior#1428918844822392892]] (30, 2025-10-18).
- **[Weapon skill]** Weapon skill was tested to *not* actually grant crit directly, contrary to some players' assumption — [[d:warrior#1428919077480435723]], [[d:warrior#1428919167746048050]] (mark vasko/30, 2025-10-18).

- **[Taunt]** On Turtle, Taunt copies threat only at the moment the debuff is applied (not continuously across its duration like classic), and can silently fail against a CC'd or already-untargeted mob — [[d:warrior#1430903401973285066]], [[d:warrior#1430903507375882241]], [[d:warrior#1430903627610062879]] (MoneyCow, 2025-10-24).
