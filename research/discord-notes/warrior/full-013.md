## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Racial]** Orc Hardiness + Iron Will (stun resist) nerfed; previously ~25% additional stun resist + 5% baseline + 15% from talents (~45% total per Ionnatra's math), now replaced with a flat 15% stun-duration reduction, seen as much weaker — [[d:warrior#1488767313665724580]], [[d:warrior#1488782865893298207]], [[d:warrior#1488787215147470998]] (Ionnatra/Donka, 2026-04-01; disputed reasoning by limoseng who says orc racial was overtuned).
- **[Arms]** Big arms rework swapped Precision Cut and Endless Rage's tree position, intended to push players to actually invest in the rage-per-point talent (Endless Rage) that people were skipping — [[d:warrior#1490386062617477261]], [[d:warrior#1490386323742130207]] (Lipibidy/casey, single source, plausible dev-intent explanation).
- **[Enrage]** Enrage changed to a 1-minute shared CD "for all warriors" (baseline, not talent-gated the way it used to feel) — [[d:warrior#1490461908397195464]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Master Strike]** Stance requirement removed from Master Strike at some point (Kyrsie says he doesn't think it ever had one); cost currently 30 rage and seen as too expensive/low-impact for PvE — [[d:warrior#1489256692678918190]], [[d:warrior#1489256789160755301]], [[d:warrior#1490462764093931611]], [[d:warrior#1490462831835873280]] (Madcrep/Hayden/Lipibidy/Kyrsie).
- **[Dampening]** New PvP dampening pass (early April 2026): white-hit/autoattack damage described as barely dampened (~5%) while many other classes (esp. rogue Ambush) hit noticeably less; warrior overall pvp damage "pretty much didn't change" per parses — [[d:warrior#1490357948289912975]], [[d:warrior#1489694645486096587]], [[d:warrior#1489694684619214870]] (Kyrsie/Donka, disputed: Ionnatra feels dampening hurt his rogue and is unimpressed generally).
- **[Bug]** Flametongue Totem incorrectly inherited the newer Flametongue Weapon spellpower-scaling formula (should not scale with SP this strongly) — confirmed as a bug by 30, expected fix in ~1-2 weeks; extra 200 SP ≈ +80 dmg per FTT proc, making SP-stacking temporarily strong for melee near a shaman — [[d:warrior#1491125669457957016]], [[d:warrior#1491124746476191840]], [[d:warrior#1490991580616851456]] (30/Don't ping me, confirmed bug).
- **[Bug]** Sweeping Strikes doesn't proc off swings that get fully absorbed (e.g. by shields/wards) — open bug report, community upvote requested — [[d:warrior#1489606466443677837]], [[d:warrior#1490588560707944558]] (Donka, bug-report id 17867, unresolved).
- **[Bug]** Defensive Tactics reported not applying its damage bonus correctly in Defensive Stance vs Battle Stance (numbers tested with screenshots); separate bug-report id 21542 filed — [[d:warrior#1490612375710335078]], [[d:warrior#1491114383257108774]] (Herbs open Hearts/Gentleshield; contested — casey dismisses as "skill issue", status unclear/unconfirmed fix).
- **[Weapon skill]** A talent (2H Weapon Spec / similar) gives +3 weapon skill to 2H weapons passively, easy to overlook as a source of the "extra" weapon skill points players see on their character sheet — [[d:warrior#1490941203925962822]] (Ionnatra), confirmed again later as Two-Handed Weapon Spec = +3 skill — [[d:warrior#1493323470170488984]] (Shardphoenix).
- **[Slam]** Slam was "gigabuffed" and Flurry now increases Slam's cast speed, which is what makes 2H Fury a viable, competitive spec again (previously 2H lacked a good filler) — [[d:warrior#1493290053672370227]] (Shardphoenix); not recommended for a fresh/undergeared 60 since Flurry needs solid crit for uptime — [[d:warrior#1493290685703651450]] (Shardphoenix).
- **[Slam hotfix]** By 2026-04-17, Slam no longer delays the swing timer at all when used correctly (must land the hit, not just start the cast, before the next auto) — a change from earlier in the patch where Slam still delayed a swing if its cast time exceeded remaining swing time — [[d:warrior#1494432213700313169]], [[d:warrior#1494432280960045076]] (Piemelbrie/siswet), contrast with the earlier-patch behavior described at [[d:warrior#1492954226882773253]] (Shardphoenix).
- **[Heroic Strike]** Heroic Strike still guarantees the main-hand attack can't miss/glance, but this "unable to miss/glance" guarantee no longer extends to the offhand swing (changed from prior behavior) — [[d:warrior#1494454222442463332]] (30).
- **[Hamstring]** Hamstring's cooldown was removed ("no CD change"); community view is this is a modest QoL buff, not a power spike, since players were already filling the same gap with Pummel/Master Strike GCDs — [[d:warrior#1492529486363627603]], [[d:warrior#1492529722628771850]] (Yagsick/Kyrsie).
- **[Shieldrender]** Shieldrender Talisman's proc was changed to a flat ~1.5 PPM that can also trigger off special abilities (not just white hits); criticized as favoring 2H over DW/feral because it's "1.5-2x uptime for 2h compared to dw" — a 2H's rarer, bigger hits let a single proc's 4 charges last much longer before DW's fast attacks "consume the 4 stacks instantly" — [[d:warrior#1493762711618588782]], [[d:warrior#1493774154221092866]], [[d:warrior#1493774269463924930]] (30/Kyrsie).

- **[Tanking hierarchy]** Consolidated view: Bear > Paladin ≈ Warrior for raid tanking; a well-geared bear leaves "not much argument for warrior tank other than 'I like warrior'"; among warrior tank builds, FProt/dtac is the strongest all-around ("your best warrior tanking build"), but Turtle nerfed FProt (removed the 2nd Shield Block charge) without sufficiently buffing the rest of the prot tree, leaving warrior "viable to tank all fights, but just an overall worse option than a different class" — [[d:warrior#1491603680649150564]], [[d:warrior#1492034395639386143]], [[d:warrior#1492034653119582350]] (Lipibidy).

- **[Tank build comparison]** Current top MT builds: "Death Wish Prot" (drop Concussion Blow, take Imp Shield Slam then continue into Fury for Death Wish, stays uncrittable) is Tole's favored MT build post-buffs; "CB Prot" (keeps two big prot buttons) sacrifices Death Wish for less reliable follow-through; regular FProt is run for all non-MT tank slots since it does much better DPS and only one true mitigation tank is needed per pull on most fights — [[d:warrior#1492027113321271296]], [[d:warrior#1494358776797462558]], [[d:warrior#1494360183076556820]] (Lipibidy/Tole).
- **[Snap vs sustained threat]** Paladin has the best pull/snap threat since its abilities are largely unaffected by dodge/parry/block; Bear pulls ahead over time; Warrior sits in between unless it lands a big crit Shield Slam — [[d:warrior#1494357940268830941]], [[d:warrior#1494358179512061982]] (Shardphoenix/casey).

- **[Slam macro]** SuperWoW/SP_SwingTimer/SuperCleveRoidMacros-based "no-clip" Slam macro shared: `#showtooltip Slam` / `/startattack` / `/cast [noslamclip] Slam`; an older manual version compares swing timer to `CAST_TIME / weapon speed` (example: 2.45s Slam cast / 3.45s weapon speed = 0.71 threshold) — [[d:warrior#1489011734017736704]] (Nabuhodonozo).
- **[Fury 2H]** Execute does not scale with Strength; with enough crit, weaving Slam between Execute casts (via macro) increases total damage during execute phase — [[d:warrior#1488971841572704426]], [[d:warrior#1488972211250397265]] (Yosh/Julian).
- **[Thunderfury]** Thunderfury's attack-speed-slow debuff is valued as "twice as strong" as the closest alternative and "stronger than 5/5 T2.5," functioning as a near-passive threat/DPS tool that's hard to replace — [[d:warrior#1490376920431460444]] (Lipibidy).
- **[2H priority]** Concrete priority given for 2H Fury (post-Slam-buff/Flurry patch): "Slam bt auto slam ww auto" — i.e. weave Slam whenever the swing timer allows, Bloodthirst/Whirlwind on cooldown, and prefer a slow-swinging weapon since Slam's damage is not normalized (doesn't get penalized for weapon speed) — [[d:warrior#1493965821293101086]], [[d:warrior#1494053824065704156]], [[d:warrior#1494053998099955822]] (evella/30). Landing Slam and BT/WW on the exact same swing is "almost never possible"; if Slam can't be started so it lands before the next auto, weaving in Heroic Strike is better than clipping the auto — [[d:warrior#1493900774072258581]] (Fury).
- **[Weapon speed choice]** For DPS, avoid a fast main-hand: it rage-starves you faster (can't dump into HS/Cleave) and produces a smaller Whirlwind hit — go slow MH "unless the dps diff is gigantic" — [[d:warrior#1494053824065704156]], [[d:warrior#1494053998099955822]], [[d:warrior#1494054152685359145]] (30). A slow-hitting weapon is also specifically wanted to maximize non-normalized Slam damage — [[d:warrior#1493292716031742164]] (Shardphoenix).
- **[Windfury/rage fishing]** Hamstring does not reset the warrior's own swing timer by itself (only a Windfury proc does); it's used purely to fish for Windfury procs (or as filler when Flurry isn't up), and only for dual wield — [[d:warrior#1492956763689844756]], [[d:warrior#1492958845503410216]] (Bitmap).
- **[Slam vs Whirlwind clipping]** Whirlwind is "literally impossible" to clip your swing timer with, so if your next auto is under ~1 second away, use WW instead of Slam; DW Fury should press WW even at single-target since it never costs a clipped swing, whereas 2H Fury can mostly just spam Slam and only press WW at 2+ targets — [[d:warrior#1491955711876399265]], [[d:warrior#1491955789735268483]], [[d:warrior#1491954378041589870]], [[d:warrior#1491954418986385518]] (30/Kyrsie). Post the Slam rework, Slam still costs a small DPR loss vs WW from clipping in isolation, but Slam's rage/DPR efficiency lets you Heroic Strike more, offsetting the loss — [[d:warrior#1491965511129628684]], [[d:warrior#1491965568818089994]], [[d:warrior#1491965642205827152]] (Akanamu, Conqueror of Desolation).
- **[Rage generation formula]** Exact rage formula shared: hit = `(dmg*7.5/230.6)/1.075 + (base_swing_timer*3.5)/2.25`; crit = `(dmg*7.5/230.6)/1.075 + (base_swing_timer*7.5)/2.25` — rage generation depends on both damage dealt and the weapon's base swing timer, not attack speed alone, so a slow weapon can still be worth using for tanking if it's otherwise a stat upgrade — [[d:warrior#1494491071366037607]], [[d:warrior#1494489867311124681]], [[d:warrior#1494490071510945952]] (30/Kyrsie).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Weapon skill]** Weapon skill cap referenced at 315 for endgame content; prioritize capping weapon skill for your actual weapon type via gear/talents/consumables before other stats, since bosses have higher effective defense — [[d:warrior#1489544547804647625]], [[d:warrior#1489546059133882378]] (Mini). Missing weapon skill is repeatedly called a huge, disproportionate DPS/threat loss on white hits — [[d:warrior#1490392557874708641]] (30).
- **[Armor pen]** ArP widely criticized as a weak, flat (not %) stat that's hard to itemize around; even a maximized ArP setup only reaches ~653 ArP, "still not enough to ignore all the armor from the chunky bosses" — [[d:warrior#1489654546484559963]], [[d:warrior#1489650351333441670]] (Blackjack).
- **[Tanking]** For effective-health/survivability, prefer Stamina enchants over Agility when in doubt; if threat (not survival) is the concern, Agility/other DPS stats can be better — [[d:warrior#1489426217940680807]], [[d:warrior#1489426443313086546]], [[d:warrior#1489426506332508181]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Shield Block Value]** SBV said not to matter much unless popping the Shield Slam-damage glyph; a stat comparison between two Chromaggus-relevant items works out to "effectively a gain of 4 SBV instead of 300+ armor and 23 stamina" — [[d:warrior#1489433711106850907]], [[d:warrior#1489434104628908052]] (Lipibidy; disputed lightly by eric4225 who prefers bigger Shield Slams).
- **[Hit]** No explicit hit-cap number given in this chunk beyond general "get hit/crit" trinket advice; a question about hit % needed at 315 weapon skill vs level 60/61/62 mobs went unanswered — [[d:warrior#1489396762601984151]] (Nonono; Contested/unanswered).
- **[Fury stat priority]** "AP, crit, and attack speed" for Fury; 1 Strength = 2 AP (2.2 at endgame with Paladin blessing); 20 Agility = 1% crit; at endgame aim for at least 8% hit and 315 weapon skill with your chosen weapon type — [[d:warrior#1492014533726703677]], [[d:warrior#1492015033591402516]] (Shardphoenix). 8% hit is "still good" specifically at 315 weapon skill for DW — [[d:warrior#1492509082991591535]] (Istrefi).
- **[Crit cap]** The 2H crit cap (where excess crit is wasted, e.g. via table capping) is "over 52%," with the Better Character Stats addon showing ~55% for 2H at 315 weapon skill — a player at 38% crit unbuffed is well under this — [[d:warrior#1492227782426628268]], [[d:warrior#1492227910885445714]] (Shardphoenix).
- **[Over-315 weapon skill]** Pushing weapon skill above the 315 cap is "generally not worth the effort" — [[d:warrior#1492871831726587954]] (Shardphoenix); one report claims going 1 point over (316) costs about 0.6% crit "due to a bug," though whether this is a meaningful threat/DPS loss was left unanswered — [[d:warrior#1493627835510820905]], [[d:warrior#1493628526346371215]] (Jhinzuo/Hayden; Contested/unanswered). Separately, some players report weapon skill itemization gaps mean you "either have to stay under or go over" 315 exactly, with no way to land precisely on the cap — [[d:warrior#1492469050230571231]] (Lillbabs; Contested/unanswered).
- **[Defense rating]** Lipibidy: defense rating on tank gear "really never matters" at high gear levels — avoidance (dodge/parry/block from gear) actually costs rage generation since rage comes from being hit, and Shield Block covers the low-gear crit/crush problem better than stacking def rating; Virasan disputes this as an absolute, giving fight-specific counterexamples (Patchwork/Mephistroth/Knight on Chess) where tanks strip all mitigation gear for pure threat — consensus lands on "in general" avoidance isn't very valuable for warrior tanks, with situational exceptions — [[d:warrior#1492251922600558612]], [[d:warrior#1492252231058198599]], [[d:warrior#1492253022955245740]], [[d:warrior#1492256461617893477]] (Lipibidy vs Virasan, disputed).
- **[Power Word: Shield on tank]** PW:S on a warrior tank is bad because it blocks incoming-damage rage generation, "same as def stacking or avoidance" — [[d:warrior#1492254327455088712]] (Ionnatra).

- **[Trinket choice by fight]** Slayer's Crest is preferred over Kiss of the Spider on fights where frontloading matters (e.g. Medivh) since its AP value is higher outside execute phase; Kiss is stronger specifically in execute phase since it "skyrockets" attack speed/rage generation for finishing — [[d:warrior#1492246963108708543]] (Don't ping me). For a Medivh-style early-CD fight you don't want Slayer's/Kiss already on a 30s on-use CD from an earlier rage pop-and-swap, but for a late-CD fight (e.g. Kruul) that's fine — [[d:warrior#1492234696707407903]] (Lipibidy).
- **[T3.5 3pc]** The Tier 3.5 3-piece (rage-cost reduction on Heroic Strike, implied) matters "way more for dps than it does for tanks," since tanks are rarely both being hit and rage-starved at the same time — [[d:warrior#1492207924456722563]], [[d:warrior#1492208057248383016]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Bear/Paladin tank rage gen]** Bears "never starve" for rage: they run a shapeshift-helm enchant for ~20 rage on every shift, and can open pulls with ~40 rage via powershift+enrage; warriors, by contrast, usually can't even Charge on most boss pulls (no room), leaving only ~10 rage from Bloodrage to start with — [[d:warrior#1492030095756628039]], [[d:warrior#1492030461428629516]], [[d:warrior#1492030671370326046]], [[d:warrior#1492030726403653695]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Threat frontloading]** Prot's threat is heavily frontloaded onto Shield Slam landing/critting; when Shield Slam misses, the tank "twiddles thumbs for 4.5s and hopes Heroic Strike can carry" — this inconsistency (vs. Bear's more evenly-distributed Bite/Maul/Swipe) is cited as the reason FuryProt (dtac) is more popular than pure Deep Prot — [[d:warrior#1492028919652552704]], [[d:warrior#1492029069351456805]], [[d:warrior#1492028820776026213]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Sunder/Revenge threat scaling]** Sunder Armor and Revenge generate flat (non-scaling) threat amounts, so their relative value drops as gear improves; Shield Slam has been buffed so heavily that "even as revenge prot, you could press only shield slam and heroic strike and do the majority of your threat" — [[d:warrior#1492031344929280102]], [[d:warrior#1492031644574683297]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Reprisal, further confirmation]** A player who tried Reprisal as MT/2nd/OT concluded it's not worth it: even under heavy incoming damage the "refund isn't worth it," and Revenge's damage is minimal enough that the boost is "completely inconsequential" — corroborates the broader Reprisal criticism above — [[d:warrior#1494538095046037525]] (nUKe13).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Deftac design]** Complaint that Defensive Tactics is built around requiring a shield to function at all; suggestion it "could be interesting if it didn't require a shield," and as a stopgap it should just remove Defensive Stance's damage penalty — [[d:warrior#1492206781076603032]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Taunt range]** Warrior taunt has no ranged option (base 5 yards, "that feels tough"), unlike WotLK's 30-yard taunt; TWoW also lacks WotLK's Vigilance and Warbringer (charge in any stance/in combat) — cited as the main QoL gaps vs later expansions, though Shield Block being an active button rather than a WotLK-style cooldown is called out as a plus — [[d:warrior#1492018303684579398]], [[d:warrior#1492020269622231201]] (Fiky).
- **[Shield Wall/AoE taunt]** Shield Wall and (implicitly) an AoE taunt effect were said to share a cooldown, though the poster wasn't fully sure since he doesn't tank much — [[d:warrior#1492023603263439020]] (Shardphoenix; Contested/uncertain).
- **[Threat addon accuracy]** TwThreat reads actual server-reported threat, while KTM (Kettletank Threat Meter-style addons) only guesses/estimates threat — recommended to verify tank ability behavior (e.g. Defensive Tactics bug testing) with TwThreat, not KTM — [[d:warrior#1491114833934811187]] (Kyrsie).
- **[Chess/Knight tanking]** For the Kara40 Chess event, it's "pretty much" recommended everyone tanking a piece equip the PvP trinket to break potential Mind Control, even though nobody in the channel recalls ever actually seeing a Knight-tanker get mind-controlled — [[d:warrior#1489254299082162336]], [[d:warrior#1489254379608346644]], [[d:warrior#1489273105359704095]] (Kyrsie/Dodging).
- **[Hardcore vs softcore tanking]** Tanking feels meaningfully different in Hardcore vs Ambershire/softcore since HC gear comes only from quests/drops (no AH), making twinked, always-315-weapon-skill tanking builds unrealistic — [[d:warrior#1494451954682822868]] (Minkotoko); weapon-skill racials matter less for HC specifically because "you aren't fighting orange/red level mobs as hc" the way raid tanks fight boss-level (always-orange/red) targets — [[d:warrior#1494453565861658704]] (30).
- **[Loot prio note]** A guildless PUG DPS/tank player notes their guild gives plate/tank-relevant items priority to warriors first for DPS loot too, which "Ionnatra" contrasts with a rogue-first policy elsewhere — reflects that loot council norms for warrior gear vary a lot by guild — [[d:warrior#1490590284407377990]] (Ionnatra; low-signal, guild-specific).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[BWL, general]** BWL is described as "literally built around warrior tanks": Recklessness on the Vael pull for best threat, multiple backup taunts needed on the three drakes (a missed taunt "can fuck up an entire pull"), Shield Wall and anti-fear tools valued on Chromaggus and Nefarian — [[d:warrior#1490529592883023903]] (eric4225).
- **[BWL, Vael]** Warrior considered the best Vael tank; the execute-phase interaction with Vael is called out as especially fun/strong for warrior tanks specifically — [[d:warrior#1489291318084767774]], [[d:warrior#1489291426532556983]], [[d:warrior#1489292200868057229]], [[d:warrior#1489292390693994807]] (casey/Tole/Dodging).
- **[BWL, Chromaggus]** Enrages at 20% HP — without Shield Wall (or heavy healing) the tank can die fast; Improved Shield Slam and Shield Block buffs occupy the same buff slot and don't stack, so tanks must alternate/time them to block one attack each; needs Zanza's/Titans' Grip-style consumes and dedicated tank healing (4 healers overhealing is normal) rather than raid healing — [[d:warrior#1489427165937402028]], [[d:warrior#1489427344761421975]], [[d:warrior#1489428762159480892]], [[d:warrior#1489429065449472083]] (Lipibidy/eric4225).
- **[BWL, general threat]** For rooms with lots of imps/dogs (MC) and the BWL Suppression Room, Paladins are considered the better AoE/Consecration tank than warriors, since without Consecration those pulls are "gonna be difficult" — [[d:warrior#1490391630102925584]] (casey); Hayden pushes back that warriors are still fine there and "people falsely equate being easy to being better" — [[d:warrior#1489591327745245304]] (Hayden, disputed).
- **[Emerald Sanctum, Ursol]** Tank Ursol away from the "poo zones"; adds must be swapped to instantly (but not by whoever they're currently targeting) — [[d:warrior#1489298988741169345]] (30); pop cooldowns soon after pull to push him to 30% quickly — [[d:warrior#1489299128964878518]] (30); a Survival Hunter's Frost Trap on adds helps slow them and decursers should prioritize healers during "Rinse" — [[d:warrior#1489300468877492456]], [[d:warrior#1489300206213267548]] (30). Ursol's tank-armor-reduction mechanic (asked whether it lasts the whole fight) went unanswered — [[d:warrior#1489299459677356102]] (Dodging; Contested/unanswered).
- **[Emerald Sanctum, Incantagos]** No need to use a "Chinese LoS strategy"; tank the boss in the middle and move the melee stack left/right instead of relocating the boss; keep ranged off healers; a bomb that kills someone with a bomb debuff will chain and detonate the next bomb — [[d:warrior#1494570110835363932]], [[d:warrior#1494570296580112537]], [[d:warrior#1494570584728932473]], [[d:warrior#1494570678136082533]] (30).
- **[Kara40 (Tower of Karazhan), Chess]** Tanking the Knight piece repeatedly for a while without ever being Mind Controlled or forced to bow more than twice is reported as normal; see PvP-trinket note under Tanking specifics — [[d:warrior#1489238596715155557]] (Tole).
- **[Timbermaw Hold (TMH)]** Recklessness is often saved for hitting the final boss's push to 30% rather than used on cooldown early, since a full clear ("30 minutes to make it to each boss") means CDs are available on every pull — [[d:warrior#1489297503760420914]], [[d:warrior#1489297377356415197]] (30/Kyrsie).
- **[Naxxramas]** Four Horsemen's enrage timer cited as roughly 20 minutes (in the context of trinket-uptime discussion for Kiss of the Spider vs full-time-equipped alternatives) — [[d:warrior#1493773901518344202]] (Kyrsie).
- **[Kruul]** Attempting to use full-DPS Fury warriors as off-tank threat generators on Kruul (instead of FProt) was tried and called "disastrous"; by contrast Rupturan, Sanv, and Medivh can be tanked fine by regular Fury for the 2nd-4th tank slots — [[d:warrior#1494362473690697748]], [[d:warrior#1494362782508908555]] (Tole).

## PvP
- **[Racial nerf]** Orc's Hardiness/Iron Will stun-resist package was nerfed (see 1.18.1 changes) — one of several factors in warrior's currently weak PvP standing.
- **[Weakness diagnosis]** Warrior's PvP weakness is repeatedly attributed to lack of CC/burst relative to other classes; against a Rogue specifically, "rogue fundamentally bypasses warrior's strengths at a base level... it's hardly winnable if the warrior chooses the fight" — [[d:warrior#1491063097153290320]], [[d:warrior#1491063286567932014]] (Hayden). ArP is called out as a nearly useless PvP stat since even a maxed setup (~653 ArP) can't offset plate/high-armor targets — see Stats section.
- **[Sunder in PvP]** Sunder-stacking in PvP is heavily criticized as unrealistic — opponents kill you mid-stack in "later game phase," and warriors "have no choice other than to frontload as much as possible"; repeated calls for a ranged/instant way to apply 5 sunder stacks, none implemented — [[d:warrior#1489647195371077812]], [[d:warrior#1489647378637258913]], [[d:warrior#1489649670153048115]], [[d:warrior#1489649971618644188]] (Kyrsie/Dodging/Lipibidy).
- **[Target priority]** Recommended PvP target priority: ignore bears/plate targets and RIP low-armor classes before getting CC'd, extending to ignoring druids, priests, mages, and most warlocks; prioritize hunting rogues and pressuring healers with kamikaze pushes while the rest of the team capitalizes — [[d:warrior#1489648607278661744]] (Ionnatra), [[d:warrior#1489649475264974908]], [[d:warrior#1489649742718697623]] (Dodging).
- **[Disarm prevention]** No new Turtle-specific anti-disarm tools exist beyond vanilla: gloves (that prevent disarm) and the Weapon Chain enchant are the only options — [[d:warrior#1492885863145930802]] (Piemelbrie/siswet).
- **[Twink brackets]** The 20s/30s/40s twink brackets are considered "completely dead" (no population) on at least some realms; 29 is workable for DW play using textures/positioning, and one-shotting low-HP targets via Bloodrage+Death Wish+Execute is the main twink kill pattern — [[d:warrior#1490673927326929067]], [[d:warrior#1490661671931215972]], [[d:warrior#1490662215882375268]] (Lans/Ionnatra).
- **[PvP talent choices]** For 2H Fury PvP, Booming Voice (buffed Battle Shout range, useful mainly for Piercing Howl/Demo Shout range so you can "spot rogues a thousand miles around") is preferred over Unbridled Wrath, which is "a pure PvE talent" worth only ~1000 bonus rage over a 2-hour raid — [[d:warrior#1492881927785680998]], [[d:warrior#1492882669443481762]], [[d:warrior#1492883002253119549]], [[d:warrior#1493871249603035256]] (Yurek/Kyrsie/Thirtyseven).
- **[Arms vs Fury PvP]** Arms/Mortal Strike is only clearly better "if you have an organized team" (esp. dual Holy Paladin premades) — [[d:warrior#1492893464516362280]] (Kyrsie); solo/unorganized play favors DW Fury with Blood Drinker, which "gobbles a lot of free 1v1s" on the back of its self-heal — [[d:warrior#1490164939531489300]] (Hayden) — or 2H Fury, valued by some for root-breaking via its mobility — [[d:warrior#1492893663582490655]] (Kyrsie).
- **[Dampening effect on warrior PvP]** After the dampening pass, warriors are "definitely a little better now since everyone else does less damage overall," but it's early and the balance may still be adjusted — [[d:warrior#1491463047418609684]], [[d:warrior#1491462967005413397]] (Kyrsie).
- **[PvP gear, T2/T3]** See Gear section for full PvP-set honor costs (T1 24k / T2 144k / T3 520k+21k conquest, 8+ months minimum) and the PvP cloak's stamina/resilience value; the full PvP set's ~6% crit-damage-reduction plus large stam/str/crit makes it attractive even as off-tank gear.

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[DoiteAuras]** Actively-developed "Vanilla WeakAuras Light" addon by Doite; notable 1.18.1-era features: proc sounds for Overpower/Revenge/Surprise Attack/Riposte/Arcane Surge, a shield-swap notification for FuryProt, a DW-swap notification for FullProt Shield Slam users, and (v1.8.3) tracking of buffs/debuffs beyond the vanilla client's visible cap (debuff soft-cap 16 before rolling into the buff pool, buff hard-cap 32, 48 total slots) — [[d:warrior#1488977098553950441]], [[d:warrior#1490789820618768536]], [[d:warrior#1492473652661325894]], [[d:warrior#1492501552718680175]] (Doite). Criticized for limited choice of visual indicators and a ~5-second update interval that's too slow for fast-changing durations like Slice and Dice — [[d:warrior#1493382334723129344]] (Shardphoenix).
- **[Overpower/proc tracking]** ProcDoc gives a visual Overpower-ready indicator but doesn't show it while in Berserker Stance (see Cooldowns section); DoiteAuras is reported to fix this, showing the Overpower icon in any stance — [[d:warrior#1492088368950022277]], [[d:warrior#1492088489305575535]] (Istrefi).
- **[Debuff tracking]** The vanilla client can't natively track more than ~16 debuffs at once, which is a problem in raids; Cursive is the other addon (besides DoiteAuras) known to have a workaround for this limit — [[d:warrior#1493278731953770707]] (Shardphoenix).
- **[Sunder/armor tracking]** ADF (Armor/Debuff-tracking addon) shows current armor value and debuffs in a HUD; DoiteAuras can be configured to show both stack count and remaining duration of Sunder Armor — [[d:warrior#1493278940351955055]], [[d:warrior#1493513191269990550]] (evella/Fury).
- **[Threat addons]** TwThreat reads real server-side threat; KTM only estimates/guesses threat — use TwThreat when testing ability behavior on threat (e.g. verifying the Defensive Tactics bug) — [[d:warrior#1491114833934811187]] (Kyrsie). Tankalyze/main-tank-mode addons can call out avoidance (dodge/parry) and defensive-CD usage via yells, though this is reported as excessively noisy (spam-yelling while stunned, etc.) — [[d:warrior#1489651567509377045]], [[d:warrior#1489654269601648871]] (Dodging/Albu).
- **[Slam/swing-timer macros]** Reliable "no-clip" Slam play requires SuperWoW + SP_SwingTimer + (Super)CleveRoidMacros; example macros for stance-swap-and-charge, Berserker Rage without eating a GCD (`/cast [nostance:3] Berserker Stance; Berserker Rage`), and combined Death Wish+Perception macros are shared throughout the chunk — see Rotation and Consumables sections for specifics — [[d:warrior#1489011734017736704]], [[d:warrior#1491310014680272968]], [[d:warrior#1491318264230318141]] (Nabuhodonozo/Purple Haze/Azeroth).
- **[Weapon-swap macros]** For swapping 1H+shield ↔ 2H (or MH/OH combos) with a single button using SuperCleveRoidMacros: chain `/equipoh`, `/equip`, `/equipmh` conditioned on `[equipped:...]` checks; base-game Outfitter/ItemRack can also toggle loadouts but need one button per set unless combined with roid-macro conditionals — [[d:warrior#1493643429408932031]], [[d:warrior#1493634835841810683]], [[d:warrior#1493653620196114665]] (Fury/Nandieb/Ionnatra).
- **[Rotation helper]** `https://github.com/Zedorff/LazyAssRotation` is a friend-made addon for suggesting the next optimal ability; see Gear/Sims note — [[d:warrior#1493292206147113121]] (Shardphoenix).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Orc]** Hardiness/Iron Will (stun resist) nerf discussed at length — see 1.18.1 changes; disputed as either an overdue nerf (limoseng: an orc racial "beeing that strong is retardet") or an overcorrection (Ionnatra) — [[d:warrior#1488786006130954331]] (limoseng, disputed).
- **[Race comparison, DPS/PvE]** At endgame parse level, "all races are within 1% of each other" for warriors — [[d:warrior#1494450078235099227]] (Kyrsie); pick whichever race/animations you like rather than min-maxing — [[d:warrior#1494450078235099227]], [[d:warrior#1492621742114607307]] (Screams: "it's more about what you like looking at... if you enjoy looking at a Nelf, that is an excellent choice").
- **[Race comparison, tanking]** Troll's Berserking (10% haste for 10s, up to 15% below 40% HP) is called "very good for tank"; Tauren's War Stomp/racial is "just HP, not mitigation"; the actual fastest Naxx/K40-clearing guild's MT race-changes for fun among Human/Night Elf/High Elf/Tauren/Orc, showing race is not a hard requirement at the top end — [[d:warrior#1494450325128614058]], [[d:warrior#1494450598626459758]], [[d:warrior#1494455585238679652]] (Kyrsie/30).
- **[Weapon-skill racials devalued]** Human's sword-skill racial (and other weapon-skill racials) were "was good in classic for sword skill, not the case on turtle" — old system gave +3% hit/+20% glance dmg from racial weapon skill, current system gives ~+0.6% hit/+6% glance dmg, alongside new gear-based sources of weapon skill that reduce the racial's relative importance; "berserking is always gonna be better threat than 3 weapon skill" — [[d:warrior#1494449883585712269]], [[d:warrior#1494455850377678940]], [[d:warrior#1494454979073933452]] (Fury/30).
- **[Blood Fury vs Berserking]** Orc's Blood Fury currently reduces healing received by -25% while active (reduced from a historical 50% some players still expect) — [[d:warrior#1494455148951375992]] (30); this is separate from Troll's Berserking, which gives +10% haste for 10s (up to 15% below 40% HP) with no healing downside — [[d:warrior#1494455199442669599]] (30).
- **[Professions]** Weaponsmith specializations are exclusive per character (Axesmith/Swordsmith/etc.); the upgraded Elementium Reaper (axe) needs Axesmith and Elementium Champion (sword) needs Swordsmith to wear the BWL-drop upgrade, while plate-armor upgrades just need Armorsmith (any character in the guild) — [[d:warrior#1490122531674390548]], [[d:warrior#1490124384684478474]], [[d:warrior#1490124503861428294]] (darthpenance). One Blacksmith reports having made significant gold from the profession, suggesting it can offset the "bricked" opportunity cost of a locked weaponsmith specialization — [[d:warrior#1491669802803269703]] (Fury, single source, vague).
- **[Gold farming]** Suggested warrior-friendly gold routes: a rogue alt farming ZG (~150g/h); Blasted Lands ore/mat farming or soloing the highest-level dungeon your gear allows; RFD/SM/Gilneas City soloable with a Blood Drinker + vampirism-gear setup (see Gear section); Magic Dust farming (~100g/h); buying low-value raw materials and reselling as crafted consumables via Alchemy — [[d:warrior#1490713267658555585]], [[d:warrior#1490716883790463097]], [[d:warrior#1490715029513965648]], [[d:warrior#1490715082332835841]] (Purge/Bassilthorne/Donka/Black Hat).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Sweeping Strikes]** Doesn't proc from swings that get fully absorbed — see 1.18.1 changes (bug-report 17867, unresolved).
- **[Defensive Tactics]** Reported to not apply its damage bonus correctly comparing Battle Stance vs Defensive Stance — see 1.18.1 changes (bug-report 21542, status unclear).
- **[Flametongue Totem]** Incorrectly scales with spellpower like the newer Flametongue Weapon enchant — see 1.18.1 changes (confirmed bug, fix expected).
- **[Bloodrage]** Using Bloodrage triggers a self-inflicted Daze and also triggers Gift of Arthas on the caster — [[d:warrior#1494051862159167540]] (30; unclear if intended).
- **[Thunderfury/Dream's Herald]** Thunderfury's slow debuff and Dream's Herald's poison DoT appear to overwrite each other on the same target rather than coexisting — reported as a possible waste of ~300g spent on Dream's Herald if you already carry Thunderfury; unresolved whether this is intended or a bug, and how it behaves on off-targets that only get the poison — [[d:warrior#1492276891074302106]], [[d:warrior#1492277053448523866]], [[d:warrior#1492277436396601514]], [[d:warrior#1492277596900298806]] (Hellwraith; Contested/unanswered).
- **[Weapon skill precision]** Some players report it's impossible to land exactly on 315 weapon skill with available itemization — you either sit under or go over — [[d:warrior#1492469050230571231]] (Lillbabs; Contested/unanswered).
- **[Over-cap crit loss]** Going 1 point over 315 weapon skill (e.g. 316) reportedly costs ~0.6% crit "due to a bug" — magnitude/significance unconfirmed — [[d:warrior#1493627835510820905]] (Jhinzuo; Contested/unanswered).
- **[Low-level itemization]** Turtle WoW is noted for odd "vanilla-style" itemization quirks at low level, e.g. ranged AP appearing on a level-24 mail shoulder item even though Hunters can't wear mail until level 40 — [[d:warrior#1489214231571075212]], [[d:warrior#1489214355513016370]] (pero/Junak'Salsa).
- **[Master Strike]** No stance restriction on Master Strike (Kyrsie doesn't believe it ever had one, despite some players recalling a Zerker-Stance restriction from Classic); its rage cost was possibly reduced from 30 to a lower value "sometime in the past few months," unconfirmed — [[d:warrior#1489260512645480488]], [[d:warrior#1490463220534739034]] (Kyrsie/Lipibidy; Contested).
- **[Heroic Strike]** No longer guarantees the offhand swing can't miss/glance (only the main hand retains that guarantee) — a change from earlier behavior — [[d:warrior#1494454222442463332]] (30).
