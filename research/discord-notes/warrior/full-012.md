## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[fury]** Flurry's Slam cast-time reduction was bugged (over-reduced); corrected so 5/5 Flurry gives 1.92s Slam cast (2.5/1.3) instead of the previous 1.75s — dev comment quoted verbatim — [[d:warrior#1485731806379639005]] (Czasku, 2026-03-24), confirmed formula by 30 [[d:warrior#1488572106357604544]].
- **[prot]** Shield Block bug fixed this patch: block classes had been taking their full block value as damage instead of blocking it (e.g. 200 BV vs 210 hit = took 200, blocked 10) — [[d:warrior#1486406992577822871]] (evella), [[d:warrior#1486408921655672962]] (Junak'Salsa detail).
- **[prot]** Shield Slam threat modifier increased (talent ranks going 3/6/9/12/15% → the top rank moved from 15% to 20%), giving Prot noticeably better single-target/opener threat this patch — [[d:warrior#1485969041309827152]], [[d:warrior#1485969230187724922]]; widely called "the shield slam buff" but described as "a bandaid" over deeper prot-tree issues by Lipibidy [[d:warrior#1485783789572456498]].
- **[arms]** Boundless Anger and Precision Cut swapped tree positions in the recent Arms rework; DW/Fury tree unaffected — [[d:warrior#1487614924757799136]] (Nonono).
- **[fury]** Whirlwind talent got a slight buff; no other change to DW fury specs — [[d:warrior#1487615014671224987]] (Nonono).
- **[all specs]** Bloodrage's self-inflicted damage can now crit and that crit can proc Enrage, making Enrage nearly a "baseline" cooldown available every fight for any spec that can Bloodrage — [[d:warrior#1487841821928198335]] (Kyrsie), confirmed/expanded by Lipibidy: "at some point it needs to be addressed that grabbing enrage and/or deathwish is taking a large priority for all specs" [[d:warrior#1488713874688704564]], [[d:warrior#1488715288546640042]].
- **[all]** Following 1.18.1, players also reference a further "1.18.2" patch affecting "shield slam and defiance changes" (asked whether sims were updated for it) — [[d:warrior#1488741258804461732]] (Gigglenaut; question unanswered in chunk).
- **[tanking]** Weapon skill confirmed (via 100k+ test autos at 315/325/330 skill) to have zero effect on chance to be parried or to parry — a long-standing assumption finally tested — [[d:warrior#1486147911069728898]], [[d:warrior#1486148825486852276]] (Dodging & 30).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[dps]** Lipibidy's list of the realistically distinct raid DPS builds: Dual Wield Fury, 2H Fury (Bloodthirst), MS Arms (skip Deathwish for MS), Deathwish Arms ("deathcute", skip MS for Deathwish), Lawnmower (2H Fury, skip Bloodthirst for Sweeping Strikes) — [[d:warrior#1487833646160281820]], [[d:warrior#1487834407795691582]]; 2H Fury still ahead of DW Fury overall, "Arms changes are still fresh" [[d:warrior#1486580928548438066]].
- **[arms]** "Deathcute" (Deathwish/Execute-focused Arms) widely tried post-patch and found underwhelming/slow in practice ("feelsbadman... waiting for an auto sitting at 0 rage feels so ass") despite huge execute numbers on meters — [[d:warrior#1486834175318425761]], [[d:warrior#1486834452662456451]] (Thirtyseven); large share of that execute damage is disputed as "overkill"/not real damage, sparking an extended logs-accuracy debate between Guts and Cannondorf — [[d:warrior#1486631637902032968]] through [[d:warrior#1486639147069345902]] (unresolved/contested).
- **[arms]** MS-Arms considered strong ("Mortal hits like a truck on ST") but Arms as a whole still "missing something outside of cleave," with Lawnmower still winning cleave/trash — [[d:warrior#1486337612930093129]], [[d:warrior#1486336808806649927]]; direct math comparison at 1k AP with trinkets shows MS/Bloodthirst/Shield Slam landing in a similar ~600-660 damage range, but Fury's self-heal + root-break makes Fury better overall right now (Czasku) — [[d:warrior#1485891459445030962]], [[d:warrior#1485892002225852499]], [[d:warrior#1485893615937916949]].
- **[tanking]** No consensus on best raid tank build: FuryProt / DeathTactics(Deftac) / ImpaleProt "all viable, shine in different situations"; ImpaleProt called easiest to learn and "sufficient for the vast majority of raiding teams"; Reprisal (revenge subspec) agreed to be weak — [[d:warrior#1485630356903301130]], [[d:warrior#1485630473261547552]], [[d:warrior#1485630915832057876]], [[d:warrior#1485630552467050526]].
- **[tanking]** FuryProt described as "less a tank and more a fury warrior being healed through a boss" — viable on virtually all bosses given good healers, but very healer-demanding — [[d:warrior#1487889534120104038]], [[d:warrior#1486458223853830256]]. ImpaleProt suffers far less threat loss from wearing mitigation gear than FuryProt does — [[d:warrior#1488265744369324236]], [[d:warrior#1488265832856424458]].
- **[tanking]** A "Chinese Prot" (DW without Deathtactics) build tested live by Cardrin in a 59-min Naxx MT clear felt comparable in threat to FuryProt; idea originated from Tole's brainstorming back at the 1.17 release — [[d:warrior#1486005287344406731]], [[d:warrior#1486010600931987477]] (single test, not broadly repeated — treat as single source).
- **[tanking]** Broad consensus (Lipibidy/Rokas/Tole/Black Hat) that Prot Warrior, while improved this patch on single-target snap threat, remains the weakest raid tank overall vs Paladin/Bear: lacks a real AoE/multi-target threat tool, itemization forces avoidance/hit stats instead of clean threat stats, and devs have said in-channel they're revisiting Prot — [[d:warrior#1487460226851668118]], [[d:warrior#1487461673668317204]], [[d:warrior#1487462415942549646]].
- **[general]** "Realistically there's 5 dps builds" restated by Dodging matches Lipibidy's list above — [[d:warrior#1487831038725193870]] (confirmed by X, single-source consensus).

## Leveling
- **[general]** Dual Wield unlocks at level 10 on Turtle and is commonly recommended even on "classic mindset" leveling; several posters say leveling Fury (not Arms) is correct even on vanilla classic — [[d:warrior#1485632066077851709]], [[d:warrior#1485632509738877050]], [[d:warrior#1485632262631460916]] (Hayden), confirmed by Kyrsie/Czasku in the same thread.
- **[leveling]** For dungeon-heavy leveling as DPS, 2H Arms is recommended over DW ("if ur doing dungeons then id say 2h arms" — [[d:warrior#1485847747801256157]], casey); pure open-world leveling favors DW Fury with Blood Drinker once unlocked.
- **[leveling]** Blood Drinker (deep Fury, heals % of damage dealt) is popular once accessible via Dual Wield for solo leveling/farming sustain — [[d:warrior#1486761907389071360]], [[d:warrior#1486762117179904091]]. Countered by Black Hat: Improved (Berserker) Shouts' 40% enemy-AP-reduction is worth more for leveling survivability than Blood Drinker's healing — [[d:warrior#1486923645128282245]] (disputed/single-source).
- **[leveling/tank]** Don't spec Protection before getting Shield Slam (~level 30) — nothing useful in the tree before it; once you have it, Prot "surprisingly solid" for leveling — [[d:warrior#1485778015593037934]], [[d:warrior#1485778228231798926]], [[d:warrior#1485778395433406624]] (Jakeends).
- **[leveling/tank]** Full-Prot leveling to 60 is doable but inefficient: no survival benefit over Fury/Arms in practice (lower dps → longer fights → similar total damage taken); Tactical Mastery is "mandatory" for solo 2H-hamstring-kiting leveling since threat/rage retention matters more than mitigation at low level — [[d:warrior#1485775913747742740]], [[d:warrior#1485776732488470789]], [[d:warrior#1485777713934631014]] (Nabuhodonozo).
- **[leveling/stats]** DPS stat priority while leveling with 2H: Stamina > Strength > Agility; Hit is valuable if available but less critical due to scarcity. Stamina is "extremely underrated" because surviving longer indirectly increases dps uptime, and lets a leveling DPS warrior comfortably pull threat off tank-less groups — [[d:warrior#1486362597975654420]], [[d:warrior#1486363409879535616]], [[d:warrior#1486363498127691927]], [[d:warrior#1486363984750710986]] (Dodging).
- **[leveling/DW]** When Dual Wielding, don't sacrifice a weapon's overall dps just to chase attack speed — only specifically prioritize fast weapons when building around Blood Drinker's per-hit proc — [[d:warrior#1487094970018959520]], [[d:warrior#1487095771605110965]], [[d:warrior#1487096247868461176]] (Dodging).
- **[leveling/DW]** Downside of early DW leveling: heavy miss penalty on white hits before hit gear is available, making kills slow ("doing 36 dmg on hits... takes forever to kill a yellow mob"); 2H leveling by contrast can remove 1/3 of a mob's health with one crit — [[d:warrior#1487090299212660788]] (otep1616).
- **[leveling/weapon skill]** Weapon-skill quest chains are available on Fray Island (same place Berserker Stance is learned); the sword chain is noted as harder than the others — [[d:warrior#1487598353213558904]] (Neurospicy).

## Rotation: single target
- **[2H Fury]** Baseline pattern: Slam whenever the cast completes without clipping the next auto, weave Bloodthirst/Whirlwind on cooldown around it; a simplified "training wheels" rotation from Guts — always cast Slam, then BT, Slam, WW, Slam, BT, Slam x3, repeat — claimed to put a player in the top 10% by itself — [[d:warrior#1486668639200608256]], [[d:warrior#1486668804619763712]], [[d:warrior#1486668937939779595]].
- **[2H Fury]** Only use Slam when Flurry is active; otherwise use other instants as filler. Use Bloodthirst/Whirlwind on cooldown without delaying them for auto-attack timing — [[d:warrior#1485723095900618782]] (Bassilthorne/Achuak).
- **[Arms MS]** Rotation is Auto → Slam → filler (e.g. Hamstring), repeating; Whirlwind is rarely worth swapping into for 2H Arms since ~80-90% of the time it costs 50+ rage in stance-swap overhead — [[d:warrior#1485948346420953218]] (Guts), [[d:warrior#1486211683738456225]] (casey), [[d:warrior#1486203918412288081]], [[d:warrior#1486210632662515874]] (Lipibidy: WW "extremely niche" for 2H).
- **[all melee]** Slam cast time with 5/5 Flurry = 2.5/1.3 = 1.92s (bugfixed this patch, see Changes section); keeping Slam cast around ~1.5s via extra haste reportedly gives better rotation results — [[d:warrior#1488572106357604544]] (30), [[d:warrior#1487147811987394772]] (Lipibidy, single source).
- **[hit/avoidance]** Need ~8% total hit for special abilities to stop missing; weapon skill contributes some of that indirectly (5% from 315 skill against a same-level target); weapon skill effectively caps around 315 for yellow-damage purposes; Dual Wield takes an additional miss penalty on white-hit swings not present on 2H — [[d:warrior#1485503397527420979]] (casey), [[d:warrior#1485500558671478836]], [[d:warrior#1485500872829177918]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Shield Slam]** Combined miss+dodge+parry chance against a boss at 315 weapon skill is ~19%, considered too high given how threat-loaded Shield Slam is for Prot — [[d:warrior#1487115607617962087]] (Jakeends), [[d:warrior#1487490429157900510]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[2H Fury "Lawnmower"]** Skip Bloodthirst for Sweeping Strikes; the go-to AoE/cleave/trash spec, wins cleave and trash-clearing scenarios over execute-focused Arms builds — [[d:warrior#1486335184155578400]] (Thirtyseven), [[d:warrior#1487834407795691582]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Arms MS]** Also strong on trash/cleave via Sweeping Strikes cleave window roughly every 3.8s (2.4s with Flurry uptime per live testing) — [[d:warrior#1486384393625927822]], [[d:warrior#1486386053546770486]] (Kyrsie).
- **[tanking/AoE]** Thunderfury is described as "a cheatcode for small aoe threat" — its debuff/cleave-like proc let a warrior beat a Prot Paladin's AoE threat on a 3-pack pull in testing — [[d:warrior#1486781276953251931]], [[d:warrior#1486782267786268834]] (Dodging/Kyrsie).
- **[dps/AoE]** On multi-target, it's fine to slot in a Slam between cleave casts if you have time and rage to spare — [[d:warrior#1487839511470932108]] (Lipibidy).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general]** Weapon skill functionally caps around 315 for hit/damage purposes and has zero measured effect on parry/being-parried chance (100k+ test autos) — see Changes section, [[d:warrior#1486147911069728898]], [[d:warrior#1486148825486852276]].
- **[armor mitigation formula]** `x = 0.1*armor / (8.5*60 + 40); y = x/(1+x); mitigation% = 100*y` — [[d:warrior#1487258627919188120]] (30). Typical raid boss armor is ~4211, but many TWoW custom bosses are atypical and can throw off ARP itemization assumptions — [[d:warrior#1488199457177862174]] (Shardphoenix).
- **[armor pen]** ARP is a weak stat to itemize for on Turtle: it's a flat value (not %), and most bosses reach near-0 armor from stacked debuffs (Sunder/Faerie Fire/Expose) during a normal raid anyway, so ARP mostly only matters against trash — [[d:warrior#1488167716261990542]] (Guts), [[d:warrior#1488197833382170635]] (Shardphoenix: "only good against low armor, but not too low").
- **[crit vs haste]** Crit is favored over haste for warriors because Impale multiplies crit damage bonus, crit scales with essentially all damage (except Execute) while haste only touches part of it, and classic's percentage-based (not rating-based) crit system rewards early crit stacking — [[d:warrior#1488167716261990542]] (Guts, detailed explanation).
- **[Shield Slam]** Damage formula stated on Turtle: base weapon damage + Shield Block Value + 20% Attack Power; 1 Block Value = 1 damage on Block — [[d:warrior#1485734380650631400]], [[d:warrior#1485734519079440405]], [[d:warrior#1485734603460313088]] (Czasku).
- **[threat]** Thunderfury threat formula: `threat = damage*threatmod + 234*threatmod`, where 234 comes from the debuff's attack-speed-reduction + resistance-reduction components; affected by damage% modifiers (e.g. Nightfall) but not stance threat multipliers — [[d:warrior#1486451604449263779]], [[d:warrior#1486456801498239196]], [[d:warrior#1486451927071064114]] (30/Velanir).
- **[Revenge]** Base average damage ~90, T3 2-piece adds ~75 more, Reprisal talent +50% → roughly 247 before armor; on a 4.5s CD, potential Shield Slam crit threat (9-10k) dwarfs potential Revenge crit threat (700-800) — [[d:warrior#1487926254266089562]], [[d:warrior#1487928770303099021]] (Lipibidy).
- **[block value]** Strength-to-Block-Value conversion assumed unchanged from vanilla's 20:1 — [[d:warrior#1488151639717056542]] (Lipibidy, "I'd assume").
- **[dual wield]** Soft crit cap tracking (relevant for avoiding overcapping crit vs. white/yellow damage) mainly matters for Dual Wield; single-MH/2H users have a much higher effective crit cap per addon behavior — [[d:warrior#1486739526066901003]], [[d:warrior#1486739626512089159]] (Junak'Salsa, "not sure if correct").

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Death Wish / Enrage]** Long, heavily contested thread on whether Deathwish is "broken": consensus among the more careful posters (Lipibidy) is that it's not the ability itself but the combined Enrage+Deathwish opener-burst stacking across nearly every spec that's the real design problem, since almost every build now wants both — [[d:warrior#1485966474358296667]], [[d:warrior#1485967128220667914]], countered/escalated by Julian [[d:warrior#1485957521788244060]], [[d:warrior#1485958991879340173]] (disputed both ways, extended thread).
- **[Master Strike]** Generally considered a weak/low-damage weapon-proc talent; the Mace variant's disorient can break on the target's own Deep Wounds tick crit (considered a design flaw); the Staff variant's parry buff fades immediately on weapon swap — [[d:warrior#1485975944089964664]] (limoseng/Blackjack), [[d:warrior#1485990359908618331]] (Blackjack, staff parry buff), question that prompted it [[d:warrior#1485985422269087804]].
- **[Thunder Clap]** Cannot benefit from Impale because it cannot critically strike in its current implementation — [[d:warrior#1488502543997731007]] (Dodging), [[d:warrior#1488502809597972490]] (casey).
- **[Sunder Armor]** Once 5 stacks are applied, Rogue's Improved Expose Armor overwrites/replaces Sunder Armor's debuff (long, heavily contested complaint thread about warriors "losing" their own debuff to another class) — [[d:warrior#1486417646508441846]] through [[d:warrior#1486425146289033216]] (Voodoo/30/Kyrsie/Dodging). Sunder Armor does not proc Thunderfury — tested directly — [[d:warrior#1486545162371862548]] (Junak'Salsa).
- **[Concussion Blow]** Widely seen as a mediocre capstone for Prot's rage generation/threat kit; extended community brainstorm on buffing it (e.g. tying bonus effects to 5 Sunder stacks) went nowhere concrete — [[d:warrior#1486343464978354208]] through [[d:warrior#1486348016704032779]] (Voodoo/Dodging/Julian/Guts).
- **[threat item mods]** Flat "+2% threat" gear stats (e.g. weapon-skill gloves) are a straight multiplier unaffected by other stats, valuable when overall threat is genuinely the bottleneck, but Agility/AP items are usually preferred for snap threat + avoidance/armor otherwise — [[d:warrior#1487078754235846666]], [[d:warrior#1487078857713647637]], [[d:warrior#1487082581429194752]] (Black Hat/Dodging).
- **[Bloodrage quirks]** Bloodrage's self-damage crit also applies the Gift of Arthas debuff to the warrior and can self-daze the caster — both confirmed live oddities — [[d:warrior#1487112916929876028]], [[d:warrior#1487113788061782229]], [[d:warrior#1487117113494732982]] (Stonehands/Dodging/Lipibidy).
- **[Taunt]** Can be resisted, confirmed via combat log against Heigan the Unclean — [[d:warrior#1487489449251049632]] (Black Hat).
- **[Overkill/logs]** Extended, unresolved debate about a proposed change to deduct ~50% damage from killing blows on TurtleLogs to correct for Execute overkill inflating meters; Guts argues this introduces its own inaccuracy and is being pushed for social/"gotcha" reasons rather than pure accuracy, Cannondorf argues it's still net more accurate than the status quo — [[d:warrior#1486631637902032968]] through [[d:warrior#1486639147069345902]] (contested / unresolved).

## Tanking specifics
- **[stances/threat]** Battle and Berserker stance carry a baseline 0.8x threat multiplier vs Defensive Stance; the Defensive Tactics talent brings non-Defensive-stance threat back up to roughly match Defensive Stance while forgoing Defensive Stance's 10% damage-taken reduction — [[d:warrior#1488518308587245610]], [[d:warrior#1488519092053872800]], confirmed [[d:warrior#1488520208522936491]] (Lipibidy/Dodging). Many raiders now tank in Battle Stance for most of a pull (better threat/output) and only swap to Defensive once a safe threat lead is built, to mitigate/block-dance on dangerous hits — [[d:warrior#1488520429432864768]], [[d:warrior#1488520746740224050]] (Dodging).
- **[taunt]** Warriors must stance-dance into Defensive Stance to Taunt at all, unlike Paladins who no longer need Seal of Justice to taunt — called out as an inconsistency; creates a "catch-22" of staying in Battle Stance for threat vs. entering Defensive to taunt and losing output — [[d:warrior#1488261505257574571]], [[d:warrior#1488261606453547039]] (Tole), [[d:warrior#1488262016102568116]] (Jakeends).
- **[AoE tanking]** Prot Warrior has no real multi-target threat tool and is explicitly "the single-target tank class"; players are advised to rely on other tank classes for aggressive AoE pulls — [[d:warrior#1488666852841357532]] (garras), [[d:warrior#1487461673668317204]] (Lipibidy).
- **[Defensive Tactics / Shield Block]** Post-patch, Shield Block's crit/crush-immunity buff and Improved Shield Slam's buff are two separate, mutually-overwriting buffs instead of stacking as 2 charges like vanilla — a real reduction in guaranteed crit/crush-immunity uptime, argued at length as a Turtle-specific nerf vs. vanilla mechanics (Lipibidy), disputed by Dodging as "not really, it just creates an interesting rotation" — [[d:warrior#1486459142867521668]] through [[d:warrior#1486465944883822775]] (contested).
- **[stat priority/tanking]** Stamina, Agility, Crit, and AP are the primary FuryProt threat stats due to double-dipping on offense+mitigation; no fixed "magic number" stat budget works across all raid contexts since threat/survival tradeoffs vary heavily by group composition and healer skill — [[d:warrior#1487804045115785357]], [[d:warrior#1487804545257050313]] (Dodging).
- **[leveling/early tank]** Don't spec Protection before Shield Slam (~level 30); a shield can still be carried situationally while playing 2H Arms/Fury tank in dungeons under level 50 — [[d:warrior#1485778228231798926]], [[d:warrior#1486382758027726968]] (Guts), [[d:warrior#1486382907420573696]] (Dodging: "just make a shield macro").
- **[general standing]** Consensus that Prot Warrior, while much improved this patch, still trails Bear and Paladin as a tank overall, especially in a "full toolkit" sense (dungeons/trash/AoE) even though single-target snap threat is now solid — extended discussion [[d:warrior#1487460226851668118]], [[d:warrior#1487461174126706728]] (Dodging pushing back that non-speedrunning guilds don't feel these gaps), [[d:warrior#1487462415942549646]] (devs reportedly looking at Prot again, in-channel statement referenced).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[auto-attack macros]** Turtle doesn't natively auto-attack on ability cast without an addon like SuperCleveRoid/CleveroidMacros; standard macro pattern: `#showtooltip <ability> / /startattack / /cast <ability>` — [[d:warrior#1485601498682363927]] (Dodging), addon named [[d:warrior#1485605895386828850]] (Dodging: "Supercleveroid").
- **[Intercept "bug"]** `/cancelcast` immediately before `/cast Intercept` prevents it from going on cooldown, though it also then consumes rage without stunning or moving you; discussed as possibly bannable but "30" says definitely not — [[d:warrior#1485756716909072564]] (casey), [[d:warrior#1485756908752212108]] (30).
- **[swing timer addons]** SP_SwingTimer and PfUI's built-in swing timer both reported inaccurate around Slam/Flurry proc timing (PfUI desyncs once Slam is used; SP_SwingTimer's associated macro is ~0.5s off on Flurry procs) — [[d:warrior#1485541811165466694]], [[d:warrior#1485772497948246117]] (Nabuhodonozo).
- **[charge/hamstring macro]** A single button `/cast Charge` + `/cast Hamstring` works cleanly because Charge requires being out of melee range and Hamstring requires being in it, so the client picks the correct one automatically — [[d:warrior#1486116942623281343]], [[d:warrior#1486117142477672620]] (Hayden).
- **[utility macro]** Shared "griefer"/utility macro from 30: `/cast Master Strike` / `/cast [nozone:...] Pummel` / `/cast Hamstring` / `/cast Sunder Armor` — [[d:warrior#1486117681081090100]].
- **[sims]** Two community warrior DPS sims exist: thrunk's (`thrunk112.github.io/WarriorSim-TurtleWoW`) and JRC's/"Don't ping me"'s (`jrc13245.github.io/WarriorSim-TurtleWoW`); JRC's includes extra features (combat log, non-Patchwerk fight styles, more items) and updates more often per its own author — [[d:warrior#1488000602050138112]], [[d:warrior#1488000791729143909]]. Known sim limitations: assumes perfect rotation uptime unlike real play, and doesn't model NA latency — [[d:warrior#1486027526525554728]], [[d:warrior#1486031423080104008]] (Blackjack).
- **[talent calculator]** Domain moved from turtle-wow.org to turtlecraft.gg (old pinned guide links broken); a newer alternative tool `talent-builder.dev/collections/1.18.1/warrior` is also in active use — [[d:warrior#1487937492446613695]] (Squid).
