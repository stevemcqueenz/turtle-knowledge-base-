## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Deep Wounds was recently "fixed" (bugfix, not buff): previously gave 20/40/60% of 60% of damage as bleed (effectively too little), now correct 20/40/60% — big DPS increase noted post-fix — [[d:warrior#1433325227118432268]], [[d:warrior#1436742555017678989]] (Blackjack/MoneyCow/Black Hat, 2025-10-31/11-07; disputed detail on exact numbers but bugfix confirmed by multiple).
- **[General]** Weapon skill system reworked vs classic: no awkward breakpoints like 308; it's linear, and 315 is the point of diminished returns because that's where glancing-blow reduction caps — [[d:warrior#1433831031819206656]], [[d:warrior#1433831210412671037]] (Dodging, 2025-10-31).
- **[General]** Weapon skill no longer gives 0.04% crit per point (past bug w/ crit suppression aura was fixed) — [[d:warrior#1434901687474192394]], [[d:warrior#1434901657543508061]] (mark vasko/Kyrsie, 2025-10-31).
- **[Tank]** Turtle reworked "One-Handed Weapon Specialization": unlike the paladin version (all damage), it only boosts 1h weapon strikes and explicitly does NOT affect Shield Slam (dummy-tested: no damage change with 0/5 vs 5/5) — [[d:warrior#1434661273827541033]], [[d:warrior#1434667027196547254]], [[d:warrior#1434663629726158980]] (Dodging/Lipibidy, 2025-11-03; contradicts earlier claim it did buff Shield Slam at cc2 release — [[d:warrior#1434665603574140928]]).
- **[Tank]** Toughness talent's shield-block-absorption bonus also raises Shield Block Value (SBV) by 15%, which does increase Shield Slam damage — [[d:warrior#1433167957072019649]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-10-29).
- **[PvP]** Master Strike (Mace) disorient was patched to cancel on damage "as intended" on Oct 15, but some believe the fix didn't fully work — [[d:warrior#1435065659183595561]], [[d:warrior#1435065724648558714]] (Zestitcha/Kyrsie, 2025-11-04).
- **[General]** Turtle client is based on 1.12.1: Slam/yellow damage does NOT scale with weapon speed the way pre-1.8 classic did; only weapon damage matters for Slam, contrary to old belief that slower weapons hit harder with Slam — [[d:warrior#1435885898624667670]], [[d:warrior#1435890651655835699]] (Letitonatebe correcting Danatious, 2025-11-06).
- **[PvP]** Rogue "Dissolvent Poison" (custom Turtle ability) is considered massively overtuned for PvP damage, community consensus it needs nerfing/rebalance — [[d:warrior#1435067526366367957]], [[d:warrior#1435068143214268577]] (Lipibidy/Zestitcha, 2025-11-04; disputed rationale but overtuned-ness broadly agreed).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[DPS]** Arms is considered one of the best single-target DPS specs currently, possibly the strongest single-target it's ever been in any WoW iteration per some, but was recently nerfed (Precision Cut nerfed hard; MS/Slam buffed instead) — [[d:warrior#1433374026696359977]], [[d:warrior#1433382613263057028]], [[d:warrior#1434706921604255875]] (axelfrog/ralleballe, 2025-10-30/11-03).
- **[DPS]** 2H Fury (Bloodthirst/Slam build) can beat Arms in single-target with good gear (near-BiS): "beat our rogues on a good pull... Arms cant do that unless crit rng is near perfect," but Arms is more consistent on trash/AoE via Sweeping Strikes — [[d:warrior#1435698707105321092]], [[d:warrior#1435698914023051304]] (Jonchkong, 2025-11-06).
- **[DPS]** DW Fury: "in a great place" per Virasan; top DW/2h fury warriors are #1/#2/#4 parsers who get champion buff every raid — [[d:warrior#1435755179327623168]], [[d:warrior#1434795278619115581]] (Virasan/30).
- **[Tank]** Protection (both Deep Prot and Fury-Prot) is broadly agreed to be the weakest warrior spec and the weakest tank overall relative to Paladin/Druid on Turtle, mainly due to threat generation — [[d:warrior#1433384509121761451]], [[d:warrior#1433384558702624870]] (ralleballe, +agreement 2025-10-30); however others say it's "not horrible enough to not do the job" and fully competitive for most guilds outside min-max HC — [[d:warrior#1433543544869617807]], [[d:warrior#1433544495399440476]], [[d:warrior#1435066551844999350]] (Squid/Dodging/Virasan).
- **[Tank]** Best guild on Turtle Anniversary (TA) uses a Warrior MT; other top guilds say "all tanks are viable for even Kara" and rotate tanks — [[d:warrior#1433564522425352383]] (Nost, 2025-10-31). Server "DT" (top guild) uses a Feral druid MT instead — [[d:warrior#1433544978872930475]] (30).
- **[Tank]** On TA (a different ruleset/lower damage server), Deep Prot is more common among top guilds than Fury-Prot because less raid damage (no shaman/paladin) means threat generation from base talents (deep prot) scales relatively better; Fury-Prot ("fprot") reportedly underperforms threat there — [[d:warrior#1436536853283995691]], [[d:warrior#1436538827429908510]], [[d:warrior#1436539408722559118]] (Nost/Velanir, 2025-11-08).

- **[Tank]** Proposed dev-adjacent community fixes for Prot threat discussed but unresolved: buff Defiance to fix scaling/OT issues; make Revenge cleave; move Improved Thunderclap into Prot tree with more damage; general Prot threat increase — [[d:warrior#1434629402263556348]], [[d:warrior#1434633076381843506]], [[d:warrior#1436380297045475349]] (Lipibidy/ralleballe/Lans; contested/open question — no dev commitment).
- **[PvP]** Warrior PvP considered weak/underwhelming compared to Rogue/Shaman/Mage due to lack of utility (no expertise; Hamstring nerfed, Improved Hamstring removed; only stun options share DR) — [[d:warrior#1435075604767838259]], [[d:warrior#1435075743502696602]] (Lipibidy/Blackjack, 2025-11-04); Fury received PvP buffs and does better vs casters, while Arms does better vs Rogues via Overpower — [[d:warrior#1435064922391187466]], [[d:warrior#1435065004071194836]] (Kyrsie).

## Rotation: single target
- **[DPS/2H Fury]** Detailed priority for 2H Fury Slam rotation: BT > WW if BT has ≥1.5s left > Slam if BT AND WW both have ≥1.75s left AND Flurry is up AND there's enough time before your auto attack to not clip it — [[d:warrior#1438595959302000741]] (Don't ping me, 2025-11-13).
- **[DPS/2H Fury]** Debate on pure Slam-spam vs weaving Bloodthirst: with later-tier weapons (higher top-end damage) pure Slam spam can approach max DPS, but in lower/Ambershire-tier gear (e.g. OEB) BT still hits harder and should be prioritized — [[d:warrior#1436173033357443256]], [[d:warrior#1436175989955756103]], [[d:warrior#1436180880644374568]] (Overman/VeganCourage/Dodging, 2025-11-07; contested/gear-dependent). Simple fallback described as "slam, slam, slam," with Cleave weaved into the slam/auto-attack window if rage and a slow weapon allow — [[d:warrior#1436163695872049172]], [[d:warrior#1436163736963387464]] (Crane|Korault).
- **[DPS/Arms]** Raid rotation priority (ralleballe): Sunder > BT > WW > Master Strike > Pummel > Hamstring > Sunder as filler order; in execute phase, use Execute over BT only below ~2000 AP (though Khoni's classic guide cites the real breakpoint as 1785 AP) — [[d:warrior#1435764345626955916]], [[d:warrior#1435764386345123940]], [[d:warrior#1435775874992701543]], [[d:warrior#1435777561887244431]] (ralleballe/evella, 2025-11-06).
- **[DPS/Slam mechanics]** Slam should be paired with a weapon slower than its cast time so the cast doesn't clip your swing timer — [[d:warrior#1435967082079453216]] (BrozTito, 2025-11-06). Slam is a cast that doesn't stop your swing timer while channeling (unlike old pre-1.8 behavior), but it always resets your OFF-HAND swing timer specifically — a deliberate design choice "to prevent DW from using it because everyone agreed the rotation would be dogshit" — [[d:warrior#1438884319153360991]], [[d:warrior#1438884464934654003]] (Sylvaira, 2025-11-13); later restated/patched as an explicit behavior: "Slam will now reset the swing timer of your off-hand weapon" — [[d:warrior#1439270217116287118]] (Ryiana, 2025-11-15).
- **[DPS]** Whirlwind deals weapon damage only and can crit, but has a bug where WW crits don't get the Impale talent's bonus crit damage — [[d:warrior#1437097063426035763]], [[d:warrior#1437098071405236255]] (Kyrsie, 2025-11-08; still reported unresolved as of 2025-11-13, [[d:warrior#1438162972504494090]], [[d:warrior#1438197504297537807]]). Cleave deals weapon damage + a flat bonus, does NOT generate rage, and is comparatively rage-expensive — [[d:warrior#1437106014779474092]] (Dodging).
- **[DPS]** Whirlwind is prioritized over Heroic Strike on single target because it's an instant that doesn't consume/delay the next auto attack and can proc on-hit effects — [[d:warrior#1437115104582959224]] (Dodging).
- **[DPS/Overpower]** Weaving Overpower into DW Fury rotation when BT/WW are on CD was tested/discussed following an "Anniversary"-server video; sims reportedly show a small DPS gain, but the majority view is skeptical for Turtle: less crit than classic reduces OP's value, Turtle has no HS-queue bug to exploit, and accidentally windfury-proccing while in Battle Stance for OP risks massive rage overflow on cleave-heavy K40 fights — largely NOT recommended except carefully on pure single-target fights — [[d:warrior#1436118961770205304]], [[d:warrior#1436119353954406450]], [[d:warrior#1436120934498046054]], [[d:warrior#1436121018509824202]], [[d:warrior#1436124346585317508]], [[d:warrior#1436128571537096725]] (Sweezy vs mark vasko, 2025-11-07; contested).
- **[DPS]** When rage-capped with BT/WW on cooldown, Heroic Strike combined with Master Strike/Pummel/Hamstring/Sunder can be used to "fish" for Windfury procs rather than waste rage — [[d:warrior#1439392155893370882]] (30, 2025-11-16).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[DPS/Arms]** Sweeping Strikes value depends on trash-clear pace: if the guild waits a long time between pulls, a Sweeping Strikes build gives better overall DPS; if trash dies extremely fast ("zug zug"), plain single-target/BT play is fine, or a middle ground of only taking Sweeping Strikes for specific packs (e.g. Anub'Rekhan & Faerlina trash in Naxx) — [[d:warrior#1436702449925750824]], [[d:warrior#1436705435271827477]], [[d:warrior#1436705561986076852]] (mark vasko, 2025-11-08).
- **[Tank]** "Reprisal + Improved Thunderclap" ("reprisal tclap") is recommended as a strong dungeon AoE-tanking build; players report using Thunderclap + Cleave on cooldown as enough to "tank anything and everything" in dungeons — [[d:warrior#1436833984851214518]], [[d:warrior#1436836136432439296]], [[d:warrior#1435954469274779779]], [[d:warrior#1435955837792419842]] (Dodging/Squishlish, 2025-11-07/08).
- **[Tank]** Battle Shout debated as re-castable for threat: Dodging claims spamming Demo Shout does keep generating rage/threat when tested in-game, while Ryiana disputes that it worked that way in classic and argues Battle Shout gives more threat; separately, whether reapplying Battle Shout while it's still active grants additional threat is disputed (Virasan: "doesn't give extra threat for reapplying" vs worlds #1 ppm expert: "you can spam it for threat on everything you hit") — [[d:warrior#1436842700988088340]], [[d:warrior#1436843201309708482]], [[d:warrior#1435036801206321303]], [[d:warrior#1435038925759582490]] (contested/open question).
- **[Tank]** Dungeon pull opener: run in, drop a Sapper Charge (engineering), then spam Demo Shout while tab-targeting — good for holding aggro on adds that aren't being actively focused, not amazing against active DPS focus — [[d:warrior#1434957836592484542]] (Dodging, on Garr-style pulls).
- **[Tank]** Recommended dungeon pull sequence: Charge, then Demo Shout (to use the dead time while mobs run in), then Thunderclap (already in Battle Stance), then Whirlwind third — Thunderclap should hold the pack for 1 GCD until WW lands — [[d:warrior#1437624833616777346]] (Mortistrike, 2025-11-11).
- **[Tank]** General multi-mob threat-holding advice: keep pack pulls to a max of 4 mobs, auto-mark targets so DPS don't scatter their damage, and use Thunderclap+WW+Demo Shout to maintain "bare minimum" threat on off-targets while focusing the marked mob — [[d:warrior#1437615238919884842]], [[d:warrior#1437615340526899260]], [[d:warrior#1437615549646508104]] (Nost, 2025-11-11).
- **[Tank]** Sweeping Strikes is criticized as anti-synergistic for rage-limited dungeon tanking: "SS just baits you into spamming abilities that dungeons don't feed you the rage for" — [[d:warrior#1436828537129664544]] (Dodging, 2025-11-08). More broadly, AoE threat and rage generation are called out as Warrior tanking's core weaknesses — cleave/Sweeping openers eat rage that could otherwise go into Concussion Blow/Thunderclap/Whirlwind — [[d:warrior#1438849069421428817]] (Dodging, 2025-11-14).
- **[Tank]** Sunder Armor still grants threat even if a rogue's Improved Expose Armor is also applied — on Turtle, Sunder does NOT get blocked by a "more powerful spell active" restriction the way some expect from vanilla — [[d:warrior#1438544286802247680]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-13). Multiple warriors sundering the same target share/stack the debuff correctly up to 5 stacks (e.g. 3 warriors sundering = a 3-stack debuff, not wasted) — [[d:warrior#1438491675805745223]] (Velanir).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[DPS/Tank]** Weapon Skill: base miss chance is 8%; every 5 weapon skill = 1% additional hit chance (0.2% per point) — [[d:warrior#1437112520484720832]] (Dodging). 315 weapon skill is the point where the glancing-blow damage-reduction bonus caps out; going beyond 315 has little further value since most of weapon skill's benefit past soft-hit-cap is the glancing reduction — [[d:warrior#1437405222900072610]] (30, 2025-11-09). Turtle flattened/changed the classic weapon-skill coefficient, removing awkward breakpoints (see also 1.18.1 changes).
- **[DPS]** Hit-cap thresholds cited: DW Fury needs ~6% hit at 310 weapon skill, or ~5% hit at 315 weapon skill — [[d:warrior#1435572967039045732]] (somedeadguy/consensus). Special abilities ("yellow"/specials) need lower hit than white-swing softcap; cited figures of 5-6% for specials — [[d:warrior#1433462007293546547]], [[d:warrior#1438568266095136813]] (ScoobertDoobert/Friekshow; two similar-but-not-identical numbers given). 2H (single-wielded) weapon users need ~8% hit at 300 weapon skill for a full hitcap — [[d:warrior#1437492695139352688]] (Kyrsie).
- **[DPS]** Crit-vs-hit tradeoff is a recurring, disputed topic: Rustam/Lipibidy argue hit and weapon-skill softcap should always come first ("nobody should be sacrificing hit or WS below cap for crit"), citing that hit reduces miss chance which raises your effective/soft crit cap (hit does not directly raise crit%) — [[d:warrior#1434075848797065327]], [[d:warrior#1434425539569123389]], [[d:warrior#1438649406399320155]]. Others (Black Hat, 30) argue trading small amounts of excess hit (0.2-0.4%) for crit is a net DPS gain once already near soft-cap, citing Impale/Deep Wounds/Flurry all scaling off crit — [[d:warrior#1438281830003380428]], [[d:warrior#1438282970359136299]], [[d:warrior#1438647019408330836]] (30's rule of thumb: at 8% total hit softcap, "1 crit ≈ 0.4 hit" in DPS value) — contested, no full resolution in-chunk.
- **[DPS]** Weapon on-hit proc rates (PPM as % chance-to-proc, compiled by 30): Shar'tateth 9.5%, Nightfall 10.5%, Sunderer 2.5%, Annihilator 5.67%, Ashbringer 6.0%, Untamed Blade 11.3%, Bonereaver's Edge 11.3%, Sulfuras 18.5%, Thunderfury 22.8%, Tempest's Rage 1.13%, Badge of the Swarmguard: weapon speed×10/60% (e.g. 3.8 speed = 63% chance) — [[d:warrior#1437145334026469627]] (30, 2025-11-09).
- **[DPS]** Proc mechanics: PPM-style procs are normalized to base weapon speed only — haste, extra attacks, and windfury procs do NOT dynamically recalculate a weapon's per-swing proc chance; more attacks (via haste/extra-attacks) simply means more uptime, not a penalized/adjusted rate — [[d:warrior#1437068254630641795]], [[d:warrior#1437068932807659541]] (Stunfisk, worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-11-08). Windfury Totem's proc has an internal cooldown, so a single Cleave/Whirlwind hitting multiple targets cannot trigger multiple WF procs at once — [[d:warrior#1436750396935897198]] (Squid).
- **[Tank]** Shield Block Value scaling from Strength: 20 Strength = 1 Block Value — [[d:warrior#1439665432331161631]] (Sylvaira).
- **[DPS/2H Fury]** A rough unbuffed-crit target of "over 35%" is cited for good Windfury/Flurry uptime on 2H Fury — [[d:warrior#1436899627759702077]] (Thirtyseven).
- **[DPS]** Str vs Haste enchant value on head/legs is very close for 2H Fury ("8 str or 1% haste," difference described as negligible); general lean is toward Haste for boss fights and Str for trash once in Naxx/Kara40-tier gear — [[d:warrior#1435387579305300028]], [[d:warrior#1439368810200301659]] (sofasecurity/30).
- **[DPS]** At lower gear, prioritizing Agility/Crit over Str/AP can be correct point-for-point for 2H Fury, but the crit/AP crossover shifts as gear improves; a concrete example debated: 36% crit/1180 AP judged clearly better than 41% crit/980 AP once crit-capped with buffs — [[d:warrior#1433973503849136241]], [[d:warrior#1434156778240737441]], [[d:warrior#1434157161306521691]] (Virasan/Doranbolt/Kyrsie).

- **[DPS]** Tier 2.5 shoulders are BiS for warrior DPS (both 2H and DW) until Kara40-tier alternatives appear; tank-spec shoulders are fine to wear as DPS since the DPS-spec shoulders only differ by ~1% hit, but tank-spec legs ARE a real DPS downgrade ("the only item that's truly bad for dps") — [[d:warrior#1434336667065385121]], [[d:warrior#1434337227911200860]]. Deathbringer is not a unique-equip item, so DW Fury (esp. Orc) should use double Deathbringer if obtainable — [[d:warrior#1434684588881350788]] (Lipibidy).
- **[DPS]** Tier 3 (Dreadslayer) set for DW Fury: boots/ring/helmet are called "very good," and the 2-piece bonus (reduces BT/MS rage cost by 10) is valued enough to hold onto even while replacing other T3 pieces, since it lets you use Sweeping Strikes + Whirlwind out of combat/without hitting anyone first — [[d:warrior#1439778264636850287]], [[d:warrior#1439778676219576512]], [[d:warrior#1439780163897262166]], [[d:warrior#1439780685064966224]], [[d:warrior#1439871640409210890]] (Kyrsie/Vlad/30/MoneyCow, 2025-11-17).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[DPS]** Bloodrage usage timing: use on pull always, but for sub-1-minute fights do NOT also use it again during Execute phase; for fights ≥1:15, use it both on pull and again heading into Execute, since the resulting Enrage's +20% damage stacks huge with Deathwish during Execute — [[d:warrior#1433265496458789045]], [[d:warrior#1433265542931808389]], [[d:warrior#1433265828664578251]], [[d:warrior#1433266387962560572]] (30, 2025-10-29). Deathwish must be actively toggled on and can accidentally get un-toggled — [[d:warrior#1433261835431317615]], [[d:warrior#1433262001190211664]] (30/Vlad).
- **[Utility]** Berserker Rage breaks any root and almost any slow effect, including curse-based slows like the Chess event's Sanv/Sleep-type curse — [[d:warrior#1434860202125168763]], [[d:warrior#1434861097915121665]] (30/Dodging, 2025-11-03). Improved Berserker Rage is called "goated, useful in every raid" for its free-rage + root-break, and it also cleanses Thunderclap-type slow debuffs on the warrior — [[d:warrior#1433202134224080937]], [[d:warrior#1433202962934661292]], [[d:warrior#1433203798435823707]] (axelfrog/Squid/Dodging, 2025-10-29).
- **[Utility]** Charge has low practical raid uptime since it's rarely available mid-encounter; some argue it's not worth planning around compared to simply hugging the tank/pre-popping Berserker Rage before pull — [[d:warrior#1434863025759977605]] (ralleballe, 2025-11-03).
- **[Tank]** Shield Slam threat formula: threat = damage × 1.5 (Shield Slam's own threat modifier) × any other applicable threat modifiers — meaning being in Battle/Berserker (10% more damage dealt than Defensive Stance) yields slightly MORE than 10% extra threat specifically on Shield Slam — [[d:warrior#1433436861413724191]], [[d:warrior#1440101384006406224]] (worlds #1 ppm expert, restated 2025-10-29 and again 2025-11-18 with full derivation: Defiance-only Defensive Stance = 1.3×1.15 = 1.495 threat modifier; Defensive Tactics retains 180% of the "bonus" 0.495 in other stances = 1.891, but Battle/Berserker apply a flat 0.8× threat penalty, netting 1.891×0.8 = 1.5128 final modifier — about 1% higher than plain Defensive-Stance Defiance).
- **[Tank]** Concussion Blow as pull opener is risky since the target won't yet have 5 stacks of Sunder Armor if it misses/is dodged/parried; some tanks prefer to skip it for Sweeping Strikes in dungeon tanking, valuing SS's AoE-cleave threat over Conc Blow's single-target stun/damage for that use case — [[d:warrior#1438302263343841351]], [[d:warrior#1438692766807883877]] (Lans/Overman, 2025-11-13/14).
- **[Contested]** Community wishlist item: a "Reel"-style trinket or baseline mechanic that temporarily reduces chance to be dodged/parried/blocked (pseudo-Expertise), to counter the ~14% chance for tank pulls to be parried on opener with zero way to itemize against it — proposed but not implemented, and some argue it "isn't good design" to patch around a core vanilla mechanic this way — [[d:warrior#1434595533376454856]], [[d:warrior#1434597747054481448]], [[d:warrior#1434599104373067786]], [[d:warrior#1434613734231249098]] (Squid/Dodging vs Kyrsie, 2025-11-02; open/unresolved).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Tank]** Fresh-60/low-gear tanks benefit from 5/5 Improved Revenge on blue-tier gear; players transition away from Revenge investment toward Defensive Tactics/Fury-Prot builds once better geared — [[d:warrior#1433160947727601704]], [[d:warrior#1433161008322580520]], [[d:warrior#1433161377396035766]] (Mortistrike/Black Hat, 2025-10-29).
- **[Tank]** Defense soft-cap: roughly 360-380 Defense from gear makes most trash unable to crit you; specific late-game bosses (Chromaggus, Twin Emperors, and one other pre-Kara40 boss) can still crit/apply "on fire" effects below ~400 Defense — [[d:warrior#1433173779508494527]] (Arkaris, 2025-10-29). Being in Defensive Stance itself prevents crits regardless of your Defense value — [[d:warrior#1433175532748537867]] (Kyrsie).
- **[Tank]** Hit is called "very important for prot," contrary to a belief that pure Stam-stacking suffices — a hit-starved Shield Slam getting parried/dodged causes "eternal pain" on threat — [[d:warrior#1433199900555870260]] (Dodging, 2025-10-30).
- **[Tank]** Shield-on guidance for MC as a DPS-spec tank/OT: besides Ragnaros, only Domo and (if healers are struggling) Golemagg really warrant putting a shield on; most other MC bosses don't require it — [[d:warrior#1433470964288589844]], [[d:warrior#1433472642513502210]] (ScoobertDoobert/Dodging, 2025-11-03).
- **[Tank]** Root cause of Prot's threat weakness (cross-ref Spec viability section): no Expertise stat means ~14% baseline chance to be parried on pull that cannot be itemized around, producing frequent scuffed openers — [[d:warrior#1434582579251515412]], [[d:warrior#1434582846055649533]] (Squid, 2025-11-02).
- **[Tank]** Fury-Prot "lives and dies by Defiance" for its threat — a pure DPS-spec build without any Prot investment is only acceptable if you're a lower-priority (3rd/4th) offtank with limited tank assignments; otherwise you'll be "useless on every fight you're not tanking" — [[d:warrior#1439317971116757092]], [[d:warrior#1440064639407755425]], [[d:warrior#1440064700531343410]] (Dodging/worlds #1 ppm expert, 2025-11-14/18).
- **[Tank]** Golemagg's dog-add mechanic periodically resets aggro on the dogs with no way to prevent it; standard practice is to taunt-steal the dog's aggro for rage generation, then dump that rage onto Golemagg itself — damage dealt to the dogs themselves does not help kill Golemagg and is "pure parse padding" — [[d:warrior#1437503598740242473]], [[d:warrior#1437503959152721971]], [[d:warrior#1437512696600727633]] (evella/worlds #1 ppm expert/Black Hat, 2025-11-10).
- **[Tank]** Taunting certain casting bosses at the wrong instant can bug the fight: taunting Blaumeux (4 Horsemen, Naxx) during her ~1-second Void Zone cast window causes her to bug out and not properly switch targets even though the taunt itself registers as landing (not shown as a resist) — [[d:warrior#1434755616588562462]], [[d:warrior#1434756172597956780]], [[d:warrior#1434756645153411103]] (30, 2025-11-03).
- **[Tank]** Maexxna: some tanks Fury-tank up to the point of Web Spray, then equip a shield right before the spray, since stuns strip all avoidance (armor/DR still applies, but you can't dodge/parry/block while stunned) — [[d:warrior#1439289801898721360]], [[d:warrior#1439292534642966558]], [[d:warrior#1439293468408156221]] (Ryiana/Julian/Dodging, 2025-11-15). Dual-wield tanking Thane (Naxx) has also been done successfully — [[d:warrior#1439308522096558100]] (Dodging).

- **[PvP]** Warrior's Battle-Stance swing timer in PvP is cited at ~3.8 seconds, meaning a Shaman can land two Frostbolt/Molten-Blast-equivalent casts before a warrior can even generate rage to Pummel — [[d:warrior#1435076829164867604]] (Kyrsie, 2025-11-04).
- **[PvP]** Example PvP Fury/Arms hybrid talent tradeoffs discussed: dropping Impale removes significant burst potential; a common PvP compromise spec takes Master Strike + 2/2 Improved Overpower + 1/2 Impale rather than deeper investment in any one of the three — [[d:warrior#1437892127274303509]], [[d:warrior#1437893309124182016]] (Kyrsie/Dodging, 2025-11-11/12).
- **[PvP]** A dev acknowledged a specific bug (unspecified) that may explain some abnormally large/one-shot-feeling PvP damage instances and said it would likely be patched soon — [[d:warrior#1437683541063241810]] (Squid relaying word from "Haaxor," 2025-11-11).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Macro]** SuperCleveRoidMacros requires SuperWoW and Nampower client mods for some conditionals to function correctly — [[d:warrior#1433223342521057341]], [[d:warrior#1433518186925523045]] (Don't ping me). A recurring weapon-swap macro bug (OH not re-equipping, MH/OH swapping unpredictably, especially worse with sharpening/weightstones applied) was extensively debugged in-chunk; using the `[noequipped]` conditional (e.g. `/equipoh [noequipped]58233`) helps but doesn't fully resolve it, and disabling other addons (PFUI's macrotweak module, action-bar "detect spells in macros") was also recommended — [[d:warrior#1433222638439759933]], [[d:warrior#1433222976802918481]], [[d:warrior#1433224792710058055]] (Don't ping me/Lÿenn, 2025-10-29/30; ultimately not fully solved for all users in-chunk).
- **[Addon]** Overpower-proc tracking addons: sp_overpower (paired with a swing timer), MSBT's info scroll (shows OP procs as text), and ProcDoc (shows a visual effect around the character on Enrage/OP procs) are all mentioned as options — [[d:warrior#1434269988717334569]], [[d:warrior#1434274578963628043]], [[d:warrior#1434270198881452164]], [[d:warrior#1434278689721548810]] (Kyrsie/Dodging/Black Hat/Lans, 2025-11-01/02).
- **[Addon]** Threatplates-style addon: TankPlates recommended, but requires SuperWoW — [[d:warrior#1437535125356286133]] (Cierra/Mommy, 2025-11-11). sp_swingtimer recommended over generic AttackBar addons for warriors — [[d:warrior#1436519369784950957]], [[d:warrior#1437902164994297936]] (Kyrsie).
- **[Macro]** A slam-timing "safety" macro that blocks casting Slam within the last 50% of your swing timer (to avoid clipping autos) was shared and widely adopted: `#showtooltip Slam` / `/startattack` / a swing-timer check before `CastSpellByName("Slam")` — [[d:warrior#1436478047674175520]], [[d:warrior#1436516441242341387]] (Fypoolday/30, 2025-11-08).
- **[Macro]** Stance-conditional ability macros using roid/cleveroid syntax, e.g. `/cast [stance:1/2] Thunder Clap; [stance:3] Whirlwind` for casting the right AoE tool regardless of current stance — [[d:warrior#1436344975620309137]] (Kyrsie, 2025-11-06). Mouseover taunt macro: `/cast [@mouseover]Taunt;Taunt` — [[d:warrior#1436136743966605323]] (Don't ping me, 2025-11-06).
- **[Addon]** TrinketMenu addon (standalone) recommended over Itemrack/Outfitter for trinket-swapping, with keybindable trinket slots — [[d:warrior#1437181094754652273]], [[d:warrior#1437181505553305721]] (axelfrog/Squid, 2025-11-10). ActionBarProfiles recommended for managing separate action-bar layouts across specs — [[d:warrior#1438667092629917797]] (Dodging, 2025-11-14).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Bug]** Weapon-swap macros for stance/gear switching (SuperCleveRoidMacros) intermittently break — OH not re-equipping, MH/OH swapping unpredictably — worse when sharpening/weightstones are applied; root cause not conclusively found in-chunk despite extensive back-and-forth debugging — [[d:warrior#1433224792710058055]] (Lÿenn, 2025-10-30; see Macros section).
- **[Bug]** One-Handed Weapon Specialization had a bug shortly after "CC2" release that let it multiply damage to ~1 million on a single hit; since fixed — [[d:warrior#1434667524527755397]] (30, 2025-11-01).
- **[Bug/Fixed]** Deep Wounds bug: the bleed was applying 20/40/60% of 60% of your damage (i.e., significantly undertuned) instead of the intended flat 20/40/60% — now fixed, resulting in a large perceived DPS increase from the talent — [[d:warrior#1436742555017678989]] (Black Hat, 2025-11-08).
- **[Bug/Fixed]** A separate "weapon damage scaling" fix to Whirlwind was implemented around 2025-11-12/13, causing a large 2H Fury DPS boost (WW previously "critted for like half the damage of Slam" per some); community expects 2H Fury to potentially get nerfed next patch as a result — [[d:warrior#1436732197448061070]], [[d:warrior#1437968277203583007]], [[d:warrior#1437968749520228352]] (Dodging/axelfrog/mark vasko, 2025-11-08 & 12).
- **[Bug]** Troll Racial "Regeneration" and even baseline out-of-combat HP regen were reported broken at low levels: tested formula `Spirit*0.5+6` isn't being applied correctly, and the Troll 20% regen bonus isn't being applied to the baseline 6 HP tick either — [[d:warrior#1436020701042839603]], [[d:warrior#1436051733360021584]], [[d:warrior#1436052773757124720]] (Nick Duggets, 2025-11-06; unresolved in-chunk).
- **[Bug]** A specific level-40 dagger was found to display a bugged tooltip subtext showing 0-80 weapon damage that inflated its calculated DPS from 31 to 63 — flagged as a legitimate item bug rather than intended design, sparked "delete this before it gets fixed"/bug-abuse-ethics banter — [[d:warrior#1438616973620674611]], [[d:warrior#1438604950480621629]] (Dodging/dewbie, 2025-11-13).
- **[Bug]** Sapphiron's periodic frost damage aura ticks irregularly (every 2-4 seconds instead of a clean 2s) on Turtle, and can even hit players during the flight phase — a known Turtle-specific Sapphiron bug that changes standard FR-gearing strategy (see Consumables section) — [[d:warrior#1439502357443379220]], [[d:warrior#1439502431099555924]], [[d:warrior#1439503124833505280]] (MoneyCow, 2025-11-16).
- **[Quirk]** Taunting a boss during a specific cast window can cause it to visually/functionally bug out rather than switch targets even though the taunt itself is not shown as resisted (see Blaumeux example in Tanking specifics) — [[d:warrior#1434756172597956780]] (30).
