## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Confirmed 1.18.1 druid changes summarized by Habu: Owl/moonkin BoAT rework (slower Starfire, higher crit/bonus, seen as net nerf by community); cat loses value from Shieldrender's Talisman trinket rework (only affects K40 raiders with the trinket); bear loses "a little threat" but damage intact; resto T3.5 5/6 set bonus (Blooming Buds) nerfed — [[d:druid#1474303149903970336]], confirmed by Fat Pibble with Air Pods [[d:druid#1476844144235118734]].
- **[Balance]** Eclipse proc chance raised from 30%/50% (Wrath/Starfire) to 40%/60%, confirmed unofficially by devs via Habu/Hotverdomme-Nord — [[d:druid#1477980034995064853]], [[d:druid#1477980189047652443]], [[d:druid#1478098732330516672]]; napkin-math single-target impact ≈ +14 avg dps for Hotverdomme-Nord's gear, described as "gameplay boost more than dmg boost" — [[d:druid#1478003461621547112]], [[d:druid#1479114427390234779]].
- **[Balance]** Moonfury and Wrath both buffed ~10% dmg/scaling "because there is no nature curse" (from Fat Pibble with Air Pods) — [[d:druid#1479729856664047636]], [[d:druid#1477981026230734888]].
- **[Balance]** Balance of All Things (BoAT) talent reworked from RNG-flavored bonus to a flat passive, cut to 3 points; community split — Darklotor/Avereth prefer removing RNG ("you can have whole fights without eclipse, it's not fun" [[d:druid#1481627231838277714]]) vs ingo505 who liked the old proc-based version being "fun" [[d:druid#1481625328500736010]].
- **[Feral]** Cat's Shieldrender's Talisman (K40 trinket) reworked/nerfed due to new "Fortune" stat itemization pass; only affects players who already have the trinket — [[d:druid#1474140094234628361]], [[d:druid#1474303149903970336]].
- **[Bear]** Threat nerf: Maul threat multiplier 175%→~159% (~9% real cut) and Savage Bite 225%→213% (~5.6% cut); disputed math from GolgothaTheGreen (claimed 63% total shift) corrected by Tephus/Hotverdomme-Nord to a much smaller real-world effect — [[d:druid#1481530798686470226]], [[d:druid#1482094328070406297]], [[d:druid#1482094399813849241]]. Bears remain "vastly superior" ST tanks even post-nerf per Lyrazia — [[d:druid#1482094717360406629]].
- **[Resto]** Tree of Life aura healing bonus changed from permanent snapshot to periodic update based on caster's current Spirit, closing a long-standing exploit ("bug that comes back every couple of months") — [[d:druid#1476011073952940083]], [[d:druid#1476124658804854948]]. Preservation talent still doesn't self-update its snapshot — [[d:druid#1476113058660093982]].
- **[Resto]** Blooming Buds (T3.5 5/6-set) duration cut, first reported 15s→12s then to a flat 20s timer; seen as a needed but clumsy nerf to abuse via pre-buffing whole raid — [[d:druid#1473752706530283766]], [[d:druid#1476250050567868568]], [[d:druid#1481657878652915765]].
- **[General]** "Swapping Librams, Totems and Idols in combat now properly incurs a GCD," closing the timed no-GCD idol-swap exploit — [[d:druid#1477688732374138960]], [[d:druid#1477732116841238771]].
- **[General]** New bear + moonkin idols teased for 1.18.1 (contested — some say feral/resto instead), likely from the new raid tier — [[d:druid#1479114886603608084]], [[d:druid#1479129027758526687]], [[d:druid#1482078359331541044]] (disputed by Mr. Berold, single source).
- **[Bear]** Staves gain a haste-granting weightstone/enchant option next patch, offsetting some of the threat nerf — [[d:druid#1479513928869609522]], [[d:druid#1481531503652372530]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Balance]** Consensus: Boomkin is the weakest or tied-weakest DPS spec (with Shadow Priest), ~40% behind top DPS in K40; in a top K40 speedrun the best mage did ~1027 dps vs best boomkin ~647 dps (59% more mage dmg) — [[d:druid#1474211605829914717]], [[d:druid#1476961858781974670]] (+8 reactions), confirmed by multiple ([[d:druid#1474319494683037821]], [[d:druid#1476197786675581081]]).
- **[Feral]** Cat DPS is A/A- tier on bleedable single-target bosses (only rogues beat them, and rogues are seen as overbuffed), B-tier on non-bleedable, and worst-in-game on trash/AoE without the balance set bonus — [[d:druid#1479171231205822576]], [[d:druid#1479172135137050788]], [[d:druid#1479177224748797993]]. Full-Naxx logs place a druid ~9th DPS overall — [[d:druid#1479177467791933543]].
- **[Bear]** Best single-target tank threat in the game, "crazy op," expected to stay so post-threat-nerf — [[d:druid#1475094572374692050]], [[d:druid#1476230181969723533]], [[d:druid#1482094717360406629]]. Downside: very few buttons (Maul/Swipe/Savage Bite), called boring by several including Hotverdomme-Nord — [[d:druid#1481662949423583345]], [[d:druid#1476235838487662633]].
- **[Resto]** Strong healer, competitive with Holy Paladins in T3.5, but healing-meter dominance is heavily skewed by the 30-min-CD Tranquility (can heal up to ~300k in one cast with T3.5+gear+Anomalous Bomb); without Tranquility resto is judged merely "ok," and overhealing is a structural issue in multi-healer raids — [[d:druid#1476235838487662633]], [[d:druid#1476245899612131513]], [[d:druid#1475542395482542081]], [[d:druid#1475542712680845548]].
- **[General]** "On turtlewow all specs are equal. Some just are more equal than others" — Habu, summarizing that all specs can clear content but power gaps are real — [[d:druid#1478092949857370257]].

## Leveling
- **[General]** Feral is the consensus best/fastest leveling spec; Balance is a viable, "more practical than vanilla" alternative if you want to heal dungeons while questing solo; full Resto while leveling is "a very large commitment" and not recommended except for dedicated dungeon-healer duty — [[d:druid#1480938316764549181]], [[d:druid#1480938404735750386]], [[d:druid#1475768331045703711]].
- **[General]** Omen of Clarity has an outsized proc rate on Turtle (~10%, described by Tephus as "pretty nuts" vs. an assumed ~4ppm/15s ICD baseline in vanilla) making it strong for Feral, Resto, and Boomkin alike, even while leveling — [[d:druid#1478393279950225538]], [[d:druid#1478393331095572623]], [[d:druid#1480959547077300234]] (+ reactions, "so good").
- **[Feral]** Dungeon-heal hybrid leveling: gear for int/spirit and heal dungeons in Feral/Balance talents up to ~lvl 50–52; going full Resto for early dungeon healing is unnecessary — [[d:druid#1475768331045703711]].
- **[Feral]** Sylvan Blessing (self-heal on landing hostile spells/last hits) is good while leveling, especially for faster overall mana/health regen between pulls; some respec into Omen instead as a "trap" alternative — [[d:druid#1474010475728736267]], [[d:druid#1474011121861263460]] (Omen still favored over Sylvan Blessing by most — "nothing comes close to the value a single point [Omen] does" [[d:druid#1474010884513992879]]).
- **[Feral leveling gear]** Ice Barbed Spear is called "incredible for level 51" — [[d:druid#1478400719328645293]]; Gnomeregan Cyclone Prod ("MCP")'s old on-use haste proc was moved to an enchant (haste value reduced to 2/5, must apply pre-shift) and the dungeon farm is no longer required, though the item stats (16 str/5 agi) are still decent — [[d:druid#1478394239711973591]], [[d:druid#1478395823149682728]], [[d:druid#1478397274051711006]].
- **[General]** Weapon trainers exist only in the 3 Horde/Alliance capitals, not starting zones (IF = gnome/dwarf, Orgrimmar = troll/orc, Thunderbluff = tauren only) — [[d:druid#1478756773002477708]], [[d:druid#1478762017551679671]].
- **[Feral tank]** Talent Furor + Omen focus for leveling tanks; can tank low-level dungeons even without a dedicated tank spec, though a fully-leveled DPS partner will often out-threat a leveling tank — [[d:druid#1477368724922306642]], [[d:druid#1477371576415686838]].
- **[Solo]** Bear can solo several notable content pieces while leveling/at 60: troll ST bosses (shoulders), Hateforge Quarry boss (trinket), some LBRS bosses (with Engineering), Crescent Grove first boss (2H mace), nature-resist-gear bosses — [[d:druid#1478392073538506842]], [[d:druid#1478392342237941780]], [[d:druid#1478392645725454488]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Feral, bleedable]** Keep Faerie Fire up on target; keep Tiger's Fury refreshed for its buff; Rake if debuff absent; Claw unless an Omen of Clarity proc is up (then Shred if positioned behind); Rip at first 4+ combo points; Ferocious Bite at 5+ combo points after Rip is applied (Carnage talent refreshes Rake/Rip on Bite); Reshift (from lvl 58/60 talent) power-shifts without leaving Cat Form — [[d:druid#1476991316775931974]], corroborated [[d:druid#1477004416195494053]], [[d:druid#1477009155301572629]].
- **[Feral, bleed-immune]** Remove Rake and Rip from rotation, keep the rest (Shred/Claw/Bite loop), typically power-shift-shred + Ferocious Bite since Tiger's Fury gets dropped by powershifting anyway — [[d:druid#1476991316775931974]], [[d:druid#1479160794028441601]], [[d:druid#1479162404381265952]].
- **[Feral]** Claw vs Shred is situational, not fixed: Claw does slightly better damage-per-energy with 2 bleeds up if you have enough energy [[d:druid#1474459431604453564]]; on bleed-immune or no-backstab-access fights (Kruul, Thaddius, Gothik-type) Shred/power-shift is the answer since bleeds can't be maintained — [[d:druid#1478062582110883931]], [[d:druid#1478078461225730048]] (+1) confirms "both: Claw/Bleed on non-immune, Shred for immunes"; disputed clarity though — one player says "mixed answers" persist despite the pins — [[d:druid#1478703193818140822]].
- **[Balance]** Core single-target loop: Moonfire, Insect Swarm, then rotate Wrath/Starfire depending on current Eclipse buff — [[d:druid#1475973766969426088]].
- **[Feral]** Reshift decision rule (community consensus): reshift if TF has <3 sec remaining, energy is below ~30, and no energy tick is about to land, since it fully refreshes Furor's energy/rage bonus (~60 extra energy w/ Furor) — [[d:druid#1480881704733511861]], [[d:druid#1480881470171254826]], [[d:druid#1480919745426362490]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Balance]** Hurricane is the core AoE tool; with a Nature Eclipse proc + T3.5 3-set (+50% AoE dmg) it can do 500–600 dmg per tick per target, described as OK-but-not-OP without that set bonus, and "the worst DPS of every class" even with it, per Micro Jackson's counterpoint — [[d:druid#1478015116103651428]], [[d:druid#1482031207637192887]], [[d:druid#1482032078051741839]] (+1, disputed). Guideline: cast Hurricane over dotting once 3+ mobs can be hit for at least half a cast — [[d:druid#1478014814180606044]], [[d:druid#1478014903280341156]].
- **[Bear]** Swipe is capped at 3 targets (repeatedly requested to be raised, denied so far); Demoralizing Roar helps group mobs but provides little sustained threat; AQ20 set bonus lets Swipe hit 1 additional target but is rarely used — [[d:druid#1479708465545875456]], [[d:druid#1479712614409048095]], [[d:druid#1479712728137465856]]. Windfury and Flametongue totems significantly help bear AoE/threat — [[d:druid#1479712614409048095]], [[d:druid#1476684742576308315]].
- **[Feral]** Cat is "worst AoE in the game" without a balance gear set bonus, and is capped at hitting only 3 mobs (vs. unlimited for Paladins), so it's a poor AoE-farming/leveling choice for players wanting mass-pull play — [[d:druid#1479171313447862323]], [[d:druid#1478380463457439798]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Bear]** 1 STR = 2 Attack Power, confirmed — [[d:druid#1480074298579488909]]/consensus.
- **[Bear]** Crit softcap for white/auto attacks ≈ boss's 100% minus its dodge chance minus glancing-blow chance if hit-capped; yellow-ability crit is effectively uncapped — [[d:druid#1475614666687512878]]. One geared bear reports ~52.5% white-crit cap at 8% hit, 53.5% at 9% hit; going higher generally needs double-shaman-group support or Raka'shishi over Atiesh — [[d:druid#1474765599858229310]].
- **[Bear]** Agility vs Haste/Str debate: Ash argues Agi is "bait" — 3 Haste beats 25 Agi for threat gain, and Agility is only pinned in the community bear-gear list because Atiesh (BiS caster staff) can't take a Counterweight enchant — [[d:druid#1473795292779516119]], [[d:druid#1473795569595060244]]; Blesisi counters that Agi is superior "if you are not crit capped and plan to do kara" — [[d:druid#1474744918055587900]].
- **[Feral]** Hit rating has equal value per point up to the 5% cap (talents add another 3%), then drops to zero value; getting to 5% hit is easy, staying under it (avoiding waste) is the harder problem — [[d:druid#1474083543985684691]], [[d:druid#1474084533266677853]].
- **[Feral]** For cat, Str ≈ 2 AP, Agi ≈ 1 AP + some crit; for bear, Agi grants crit/dodge/armor while Str gives 2 AP — leveling gear suffix guidance: "of the Monkey" (str+agi) generally beats "of the Tiger" (glass-cannon agi/str) except for much-higher-ilvl Tiger items — [[d:druid#1480178791358791823]], [[d:druid#1480171193503055946]], [[d:druid#1480184723455348736]], [[d:druid#1480184943878602812]].
- **[Resto]** Healing Power is the top stat; Intellect ≈ 0.5 Healing Power equivalent; Spirit's value is either ~0.2 (self spirit-aura only) or up to ~1 if it buffs all 5 healers in group via the aura — [[d:druid#1480623593703342272]], [[d:druid#1480623914840359156]].
- **[New stat]** Fortune (1.18.1): "increases your chance to trigger chance-based item effects," amplifying PPM/percent-chance procs rather than working standalone — [[d:druid#1474308189745975337]]. It is explicitly useless for Feral because feral cannot proc "chance on hit" effects from their own attacks (only from being hit, and only "Equip:" effects work in form) — [[d:druid#1474308205592051722]], [[d:druid#1474308606412329023]], [[d:druid#1474308654650884188]].
- **[General]** "Equip:" stat/proc effects work while shapeshifted; "Chance on Hit:" weapon procs (Crusader, etc.) do NOT work in cat/bear form — confirmed repeatedly — [[d:druid#1474055342886227968]], [[d:druid#1479089759891620031]], [[d:druid#1478268941716422788]].

- **[Feral tank]** Community-shared consumable list: Movement=Swiftness of Zanza; Strength=Juju Power > Elixir of Giants; Agility=Elixir of the Mongoose; AP=Juju Might > Winterfall Firewater; Food=Power Mushroom = Smoked Desert Dumplings; Temp weapon enchant=Consecrated Sharpening Stone (vs Undead) > Elemental Sharpening Stone; Blasted Lands debuff=Ground Scorpok Assay > R.O.I.D.S; Demons=Elixir of Demonslaying; Potion=Mighty Rage Potion > Quickness Potion; Haste=Juju Flurry — [[d:druid#1475819828953743372]]; Jujus (Might/Power/Flurry) do not persist through death — [[d:druid#1475832792372740218]].
- **[Bear threat]** Danonzo's 2% haste food buff rated higher (53.2) than Power Mushroom's 20 Str (44) on one player's stat-weight spreadsheet for threat — [[d:druid#1475836397528289542]].
- **[Bear]** Mighty Rage Potion vs Quickness Potion for pull threat: Rage Pot's snap burst ≈132 AP-equivalent for 20s vs Quickness's ~133 AP-equivalent (5% haste) but only Quickness lasts 30s, giving it the edge on sustained fights — [[d:druid#1482102222077825224]], [[d:druid#1482102898094768321]].
- **[Raid]** Scrolls of Agility/Strength reportedly do NOT stack with other raid consumables; only Scroll of Protection IV does — [[d:druid#1482103170816807023]] (per "30").
- **[Boomkin]** Recommended Arcane Resistance for the Anomalus-type fight in K40: guild-dependent minimum ~180–220, progression guilds aim for 280–315+ (negates ~80% of the fight's damage); Scarlet Medics (lvl 54, mind-controllable NPC) can give the raid an +84 Arcane Resistance buff — [[d:druid#1482008086557491230]], [[d:druid#1482008763216171179]], [[d:druid#1482015057482219605]], [[d:druid#1482015630285865001]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Bear]** Core threat rotation reported by a tank and lightly corrected by community: Tiger's Fury (cat, pre-pull) → bear-shift → pull with Feral Faerie Fire → queue Maul always → Savage Bite at >35 rage or on Clearcasting → Swipe on multi-target/rage dump → self-Regrowth/Rejuv pre-pull to give healers margin — [[d:druid#1474473590043836720]], [[d:druid#1477369787599421491]], [[d:druid#1477369043173507183]].
- **[Bear]** Rage/threat coordination: only one MT should build initial threat on a boss; other tanks should let the MT establish threat before engaging or use Mighty Rage Potion for snap threat; Salvation should go to the top threat-generating non-tank, not automatically to a healer/DPS by default — [[d:druid#1474500952965906515]], [[d:druid#1474495679660294380]], [[d:druid#1474483202373714137]].
- **[Bear]** Enrage gives strong threat (especially talented for attack speed) but increases damage taken — avoid using it when the healer is already struggling — [[d:druid#1477368214512996543]], [[d:druid#1477368258221838427]], [[d:druid#1477368423267696690]].
- **[Feral]** Reshift (Cat Form power-shift talent, ~lvl 58–60) refreshes energy/Furor bonus without dropping Cat Form, a major QoL upgrade — [[d:druid#1477056404576665650]], [[d:druid#1480881704733511861]].
- **[Resto]** Swiftmend always consumes whichever HoT (Regrowth or Rejuvenation) has the fewest ticks remaining, regardless of rank — not a bug, but disliked because it can eat a low-rank Rejuv from another druid instead of your own big Regrowth; community wants Regrowth-priority or fixed-value Swiftmend instead — [[d:druid#1476571037243019437]], [[d:druid#1476570670057132123]], [[d:druid#1481349169678651412]].
- **[Resto]** Preservation talent only procs if Rejuvenation is applied to a target before Regrowth (not the reverse order) — [[d:druid#1479916881719590912]], [[d:druid#1478848267449008302]], [[d:druid#1478848391252414476]].
- **[Resto]** Tree of Life aura healing-bonus snapshot: buffs (e.g., +10/+15% from DMF/ZG "Sunflower") applied before entering Tree Form are locked in ("snapshotted") even after the buff expires outside of combat; re-entering via zoning re-triggers the snapshot; example numbers: non-snapshot tree Rejuv ≈455, snapshot tree Rejuv ≈475 (~4% more) — [[d:druid#1475495360280530964]], [[d:druid#1475497612001149001]], [[d:druid#1475498268472643844]], [[d:druid#1475504090397020374]] — this exploit is exactly what 1.18.1 patches out (see Changes section).
- **[Utility]** Rebirth and Tranquility cannot be cast while shapeshifted (Moonkin/Tree), forcing a costly un-shift; long-running community request to allow it — [[d:druid#1481927835563331584]], [[d:druid#1481941899916083413]], [[d:druid#1481953235034963988]]; Hotverdomme-Nord pushes back that healing should stay an active out-of-form choice for design/PvP reasons — [[d:druid#1481943856772681790]].
- **[General]** As of 1.18.1, swapping idols/librams/totems mid-combat now always incurs a GCD, closing a previously known timing exploit — [[d:druid#1477688732374138960]], [[d:druid#1477726168651399240]], [[d:druid#1477732116841238771]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[Bear]** Best in class for single-target threat; simplified community view: "druids have the best ST threat, pallies best AoE, warriors best mitigation" — [[d:druid#1475094572374692050]].
- **[Bear]** Threat nerf breakdown (1.18.1): Maul multiplier from 175%→~159% (~9% real threat loss on that ability) and Savage Bite from 225%→213% (~5.6% loss); overall raid-relevant threat drop assessed as minor, offset partly by the new haste-on-staff option — [[d:druid#1481530798686470226]], [[d:druid#1481531503652372530]].
- **[Bear]** Swipe target cap fixed at 3, a recurring and unaddressed community ask to raise it — [[d:druid#1479708465545875456]], [[d:druid#1479180581999870142]].
- **[Bear]** Windfury Totem preferred strongly over an Agility totem for bear threat/dps — [[d:druid#1476684633050447993]], [[d:druid#1476684742576308315]].
- **[Bear]** Feral Charge (from cat, then shift) used for interrupting casters, closing distance to next pack, or as an escape — [[d:druid#1478421765855383685]], [[d:druid#1478421787569033346]].
- **[Bear]** Bandage / First Aid can be used while in Bear Form on Turtle (does not drop the form), unlike some other servers where it cancels shapeshift — confirmed by in-game testing — [[d:druid#1476736618466381834]], [[d:druid#1476800320523993138]].

## Healing specifics
- **[Resto]** Pinned community Resto guide covers Tree-of-Life (Regrowth+Rejuv) build as primary, plus an NG (Nature's Grace)/Regrowth build without Tree Form (viable with T3 4-set + Innervate/Tea/pots) and a Healing Touch build recommended only once 3+ resto druids are in the raid — [[d:druid#1475224646591971493]], [[d:druid#1475442752664633477]], [[d:druid#1475543213350584482]].
- **[Resto]** Recommended spell ranks (from the pinned guide, relayed by Habu): Regrowth Rank 9 for tanks/melee, Rank 4 for casters/other healers/pets; Rejuvenation almost always max rank (R11), except keep R5 on the bar to proc the T3 2/8-set bonus on Rogues/Feral Druids — [[d:druid#1480084094653169664]].
- **[Resto]** Tranquility can heal up to ~300k total in one cast with T3.5 talents, ~2400 healing power, and Anomalous Bomb (+100% healing) — but only usable once or twice per raid (30-min CD), which heavily inflates parse/meter rankings without reflecting sustained performance — [[d:druid#1476245899612131513]], [[d:druid#1476244834619494561]], [[d:druid#1476243579436531904]].
- **[Resto]** Blooming Buds set bonus (T3.5) was being pre-applied to the whole raid before pulls to farm healing meters; nerfed to a flat duration in 1.18.1 partly because of this — [[d:druid#1476250224564506625]], [[d:druid#1476250050567868568]].
- **[Resto]** Down-ranking guidance: Regrowth can be down-ranked (R3/R4) on low-health casters if mana-constrained; Rejuvenation should stay max-rank except the R5-for-T3-proc case above — [[d:druid#1480083552564416685]].
- **[Resto]** Visual HoT-tracking bug: on heavily-buffed targets (e.g., a Patchwerk tank at the visual buff cap) your HoTs may still be applied and healing but simply won't display — need a spell-cast-tracking addon (not buff-status reading) to verify, e.g., addons discussed under Macros/Addons — [[d:druid#1481256963919843330]], [[d:druid#1481258330071765174]], [[d:druid#1481258380642353162]].

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Kara40]** Mephisto-type boss ring (32 agi + 1 haste) worth prioritizing over the patch (rep) ring for feral due to the haste — [[d:druid#1474084071444451600]].
- **[Kara40 — Kruul]** Phase-2 bleed-immune; the only fight where feral cat can consistently top meters ("A- on bleedable bosses... 5th at best on Kruul which is the only boss that fits" once rogues are accounted for) — [[d:druid#1479176569997099079]], [[d:druid#1479177224748797993]], [[d:druid#1479177306621612183]]; Boomkin swap timing to catch eclipse right as Scythe procs is an active concern here — [[d:druid#1478117479652917400]].
- **[Kara40 — Loatheb]** Only fight where boomkin is said to consistently top the meters — [[d:druid#1474319874485653514]].
- **[Kara40 — Thaddius]** Use Speed Boots or another speed-granting item to make the platform jump without losing Tree Form (avoids re-triggering the snapshot loss) — [[d:druid#1475505763278393526]].
- **[Emerald Sanctum]** Sleep-heavy trash and heavy CC frustrate Hurricane's cast/8s-CD interaction (getting slept+dispelled mid-cast wastes the CD entirely) — a top boomkin complaint (+5, +8 reactions) — [[d:druid#1481829573137141878]], [[d:druid#1481837016881692806]], [[d:druid#1481840898860912824]]. Rupturan-type adds apply a 5s stun followed by an extra ~2s move-lock — [[d:druid#1476534754906996786]].
- **[Rethevus (custom Turtle boss/quest fight, Hyjal/emerald-related)]** Solo-able by feral; a second player can help by keeping max-range during the pull so the add never sees them, and can Sleep the large add or provide Tree of Life/Emerald Blessing auras and shaman totems (poison cleanse, mana spring, strength of earth, flametongue, windfury) without breaking/resetting the encounter — [[d:druid#1480366126184534190]], [[d:druid#1480442986176446576]], [[d:druid#1480443105340690542]].
- **[White Stag event, Hyjal-area]** Suggested group size 8–12+ (can be cheesed with 1–2 people and ~15 minutes); drag the boss uphill away from cliff edges, bring Shaman Tremor Totem to counter fear, watch for knockback throwing people off cliffs — [[d:druid#1474033564722003968]], [[d:druid#1479520322834403339]], [[d:druid#1479521428821905470]], [[d:druid#1479525332959629506]].
- **[Strat UD — Magistrate farm for the Bear idol]** He is untargetable/flees outside the gate area; cheese route: enter main gate, open the right-side gate, kill mobs while hugging the wall, sneak up to the boss (bring disease-dispel or Jungle Remedy to avoid the True-Sight pack detecting you), and he won't flee if approached this way; he resets after ~30 minutes — [[d:druid#1478685056133234778]], [[d:druid#1478691433966997564]], [[d:druid#1478692236697927732]], [[d:druid#1478694735546945598]].
- **[Naxx]** Widely regarded as "irrelevant for rankings" since players' gear now outpaces its intended tuning; T3.5 3-set Hurricane bonus makes boomkin AoE competitive on Naxx trash — [[d:druid#1479204027991326762]], [[d:druid#1479204792327995492]].
- **[ZG — Hakkar]** A community-known "hardmode" exists: skip the priest-boss adds entirely (poor reward for time spent, especially the spider boss with annoying poison); Hakkar's own stun is manageable with a decent tank/dps comp — [[d:druid#1479568454234144798]], [[d:druid#1479569283418947847]], [[d:druid#1479569406622437438]].

- **[Addon]** DoiteAuras (github.com/Player-Doite/DoiteAuras) — actively developed WeakAuras-lite for vanilla; supports custom conditional Lua tracking (e.g., a "Maul queued" icon script), sound-on-cooldown alerts, and shared import strings for Kara40 debuffs and melee/caster consumes — [[d:druid#1475487556333338664]], [[d:druid#1477396726162919626]], [[d:druid#1478842337592479876]], [[d:druid#1480546155740794890]].

- **[Feral]** Accurate bleed (Rip/Rake) refresh timers require SuperWoW; the "Cursive" addon is the go-to for accurate bleed tracking — [[d:druid#1478148415102910465]], [[d:druid#1478149397459238922]], [[d:druid#1478152611139162297]].
- **[Macro]** Nature's Swiftness + Healing Touch cannot be combined into a single-press macro (the client can't queue two casts at once); sample: `#showtooltip / /cast Nature's Swiftness / /cast [@mouseover] Healing Touch(rank x)` still requires two presses — [[d:druid#1474352799402688555]], [[d:druid#1474354456631050323]].
- **[Macro]** `/unshift` is the correct command to cancel shapeshift form (not `/cancelform`), and works with CleverRoidMacros — [[d:druid#1482145012647989371]].
- **[Addon]** pfUI has built-in click-casting (bind spell+rank per mouse button in the addon settings) and an in-form mana bar option; DruidManaBar (github.com/gashole/DruidManaBar) is an alternative mana-bar addon that pairs with pfUI — [[d:druid#1479812609531318383]], [[d:druid#1479821427078074498]], [[d:druid#1474409501347020851]].
- **[Addon]** Puppeteer recommended for raid-frame click-healing; Luna Unitframes recommended for HoT tracking on party/raid frames without Clique-style addons — [[d:druid#1477857449456435332]], [[d:druid#1476703964144730213]].

- **[Addon]** BetterCharacterStats does not reflect all stat sources correctly (e.g., Tree snapshot bonuses, some trinket procs) — check actual combat log/healing numbers instead of the character pane — [[d:druid#1475481796220162069]], [[d:druid#1476966041061036254]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Ride of the Wild / Swift Travel Form]** To be mounted as a passenger you must join the druid's party THEN add the druid to your Ignore list (a known, longstanding "dumb programming error," per Micro Jackson) — [[d:druid#1478841208192106529]], [[d:druid#1478841425289543862]], [[d:druid#1479540321393184958]], [[d:druid#1481101126085513306]] (+ multiple confirmations). Passenger takes 100% fall damage; forced dismounts (e.g., entering an instance/Blackrock Mountain) can visually merge the passenger's model with the druid's; DPS meters can misattribute damage between rider and mount — [[d:druid#1475423483113635861]].
- **[Tree Form move-lock]** Being hit by certain knockback/charge effects while in Tree Form (bear-type Charge, "Magma Surgers" in MC, Kara40 Rapturan adds) causes an unusual 1–2 second movement lock during which casting still works; thought related to Tree Form's innate slow interacting with the knockback CC duration — [[d:druid#1476521586923409581]], [[d:druid#1476533792716881980]], [[d:druid#1476534754906996786]].
- **[Tooltip]** Improved Mark of the Wild's buff tooltip still displays as plain Mark of the Wild even though the improved effect is being applied — a general engine quirk (buff tooltips don't refresh for talent-modified spells), not druid-specific — [[d:druid#1480672200007614524]], [[d:druid#1480672764644819098]].
- **[Database quirk]** Stormrage set's item text listing a "+2000" Rejuvenation-duration bonus is actually milliseconds (~40 min), not seconds, matching a broader pattern of ms/seconds mislabeling in the Turtle item database — [[d:druid#1475588849227923537]], [[d:druid#1475611820034228274]], [[d:druid#1475611949055344741]].
- **[Feral]** Carnage talent's Rake/Rip refresh doesn't visually show on training dummies (bleed effect is still applied under the hood — a display-only bug) — [[d:druid#1479856112659333153]], [[d:druid#1479856225264078858]].
- **[Item]** Cla'kora legs' tooltip claims a proc that scales with Spell Power, but the actual proc rate is far too low to matter for PvE and the claimed "stacks to 3" behavior has never been observed by owners — item is generally called "crap" for that reason — [[d:druid#1474002497889894514]], [[d:druid#1474006949527027891]], [[d:druid#1474007952498098236]].
- **[Feral]** Bandages/First Aid work while in Bear Form on Turtle, unlike some other servers where using one drops shapeshift — confirmed by community testing — [[d:druid#1476736618466381834]], [[d:druid#1476800320523993138]].
- **[Weapon proc bug]** A specific claw bonus-damage talent + weapon-name matching interaction allowed stacking bleed bonus damage from multiple differently-named Rend-type weapon procs at once; flagged as a bug and expected to be removed in the next patch — [[d:druid#1478043114420830321]], [[d:druid#1478044318404186234]] (dev "interesting find"), [[d:druid#1478044420673765508]].
