## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Balance]** Pre-patch announced change: Wrath's Eclipse proc chance 30%→40%, Starfire's 50%→60% — [[d:druid#1482671112696500265]] (Habu, 2026-03-15), confirmed live [[d:druid#1484368183593799711]] (Feenik, 2026-03-19).
- **[Balance]** Balance of All Things (BoAT) talent points reduced from 5 to 3, freeing 2 talent points for personal choice (commonly spent in Reflection 2/3 + Guidance of the Dream 3/3, or 1 Reflection/1 Owlkin Frenzy) — [[d:druid#1484602329511755786]] (Tyrona, 2026-03-20), [[d:druid#1484513369988923472]] (Hotverdomme-Nord), [[d:druid#1484657820899999774]] (Eepyman, 2026-03-21).
- **[Balance]** BoAT's Starfire-crit/mana buff previously persisted as a standalone buff even if dots fell off; post-patch it requires Moonfire/Insect Swarm to be actively up to get the bonus — changes rotation to prioritize refreshing dots inside eclipse — [[d:druid#1484587743546708020]] (Jesh, 2026-03-19).
- **[Balance]** Starfire cast time was increased substantially by the changes; net community consensus after live testing was that pure Wrath-spam now outdamages a Wrath+Starfire rotation, even during Arcane Eclipse — widely reported as likely unintended/bugged — [[d:druid#1484673452152721519]] (Cowmoohflage/Arrowgance, detailed test, 2026-03-21), confirmed by [[d:druid#1484740403105234954]] (Shardphoenix: "It's better to spam wrath even during arcane eclipse"), [[d:druid#1484749662228320407]] (Lythany: "wrath wins out. because 3 sec starfires get pushback even w/ the 70%"), [[d:druid#1484729469976449194]] (Shardphoenix: "Starfire is kill").
- **[Balance]** Starfire crit-chance-from-Moonfire proc rate felt far below the stated increase in live raids (e.g. 1 crit in 18 Starfires) — reported as possibly bugged, unresolved by end of chunk — [[d:druid#1484660180250656818]] (Hotverdomme-Nord), [[d:druid#1484795453462548574]].
- **[Balance]** T3.5 3-set bonus to Hurricane only applies to Hurricane's base damage, not total (~40% of expected value); community filed a bug report (#FIXHURRICANE, bug id 20672, reached 10 upvotes) — [[d:druid#1484634906054230256]] (Endxx🧯, 2026-03-20), [[d:druid#1484643647004872886]] (bug link), [[d:druid#1484677381967839282]]. Devs later ruled this **working as intended**, though Endxx🧯 argues the tooltip is misleading (states it as a "total" damage bonus) — [[d:druid#1486337529870286989]] (Hotverdomme-Nord, 2026-03-25: "Hurricane has been investigated and is working as intended, but they are looking at a Gale of Winds change for the future"), [[d:druid#1485415446172663929]] (Endxx🧯 disputing).
- **[Bear tank]** Threat modifiers on core abilities (Maul, Savage Bite) reduced ("threat nerf") because bear was considered overtuned pre-patch; Habu predicted this before patch — [[d:druid#1483016079209926688]] (2026-03-16). Post-patch community measured ~10-20% overall threat reduction — [[d:druid#1484585026158067752]] (Holydruid: "its in practice around 10-20% total on threat nerf, not even kidding"), confirmed by direct comparison testing where a similarly-buffed warrior tank nearly matched a BiS bear tank's early threat — [[d:druid#1484733781331021875]] [[d:druid#1484734693550067904]] (Valarya, 2026-03-21). Paladins now viewed as competitive/better single-target threat tanks — [[d:druid#1483063038134452305]] (Hotverdomme-Nord), [[d:druid#1484784622805061673]] (Eemo).
- **[Cat]** No talent/spell changes for Feral cat in 1.18.1 — widely noted as a disappointment given large changes to other classes — [[d:druid#1482818697327546489]] [[d:druid#1482819017776562299]] (Habu, 2026-03-15), [[d:druid#1484392621823623329]] (Eemo, 2026-03-19), [[d:druid#1484730718767812648]] (Wawds: "devs forgot cat exists this patch").
- **[Item]** Iron Counterweight enchant can now be applied to staves (previously excluded) — [[d:druid#1482787871978033256]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-17), confirmed live — [[d:druid#1484615359334973663]] (2026-03-20, "You can put counterweight on staves now.... thats basically it").
- **[Community sentiment]** Overall summary of the patch for druid, widely agreed: "Bear = threat nerf, Cat = no change, Boomies = look like buff but it is a nerf" — [[d:druid#1484692349174022437]] (Mitou, 2026-03-21; agreed by many).
- **[New idols]** Two new druid idols added, from new BWL and new Ony bosses respectively: "Acidity" (BWL boss; procs roughly once/min, has a nature-resist-debuff component; simmed weakest, +9.6 avg dmg) and "Equilibrium" (Ony boss; Wrath 8% chance to trigger an extra Insect Swarm tick, Starfire 15% chance to trigger an extra Moonfire tick; scales with IS/MF tick damage, i.e. better with more gear) — [[d:druid#1484609254613848137]] [[d:druid#1484614282422260020]] [[d:druid#1484617181630300242]] (Csqueezay, 2026-03-20), [[d:druid#1484631711861178479]] (Fat Pibble with Air Pods). Krokat's sim (avg dmg gain, single fight): Ebb & Flow +45, Idol of the Moon +16, Equilibrium +13, Acidity +9.6 — [[d:druid#1484624819277529098]] (2026-03-20).
- **[New idols]** Idol of Ebb and Flow (existing/older idol) remains the best-in-slot boomkin idol over both new idols in most raid scenarios per sim math (e.g. on Patchwerk, Ebb & Flow ≈4.2s of extra casting saved vs. ≈1800 extra damage from a new idol, so Ebb & Flow wins) — [[d:druid#1484624255646826677]] (Micro Jackson), disputed by [[d:druid#1484623719946125363]] (Endxx🧯: "Idol of the Moon>idol of ebb and flow in overall situations trust me"), rebutted with numbers by [[d:druid#1484624973657018499]] (Breaddy: "Pretty sure ebb and flow is still king until an idol from Timbermaw dethrones it"). Confirmed still BiS as of 2026-03-23 — [[d:druid#1485545251094659112]] (Hotverdomme-Nord). The BWL-boss idol's overall raid-wide value simmed at only ~2.6k total damage across a K40 clear, called "too lame" — [[d:druid#1485520557062488187]] (Cowmoohflage/Arrowgance, 2026-03-23).
- **[Balance]** A significant, easy-to-miss side effect of the new BoAT: the Moonfire/Insect Swarm dot must be active **on the exact target** currently being cast at with Wrath/Starfire to get the crit/mana bonus — previously the bonus could be "banked" by dotting any convenient mob and spent on whichever target you nuked next. This makes the talent nearly worthless on trash/farm mobs that die before a dot is worth casting — [[d:druid#1485023364883022046]] [[d:druid#1485068626527522827]] (rutherbird, 2026-03-22), acknowledged by [[d:druid#1485399901532852275]] (Fat Pibble/class designer: the new design's upside is not needing 2 buff slots).
- **[Balance]** Old BoAT (pre-patch) used 2 separate buff slots (one per eclipse school) and had extreme RNG swings ("sometimes you did 100% more damage for no reason," up to ~300% bandwidth between lucky/unlucky); this was the dev motivation for the rework, and multiple respected voices (Micro Jackson) argue the new version is a net positive for raid mana/consistency even though rutherbird and others argue it's a net single-target and (especially) trash/farm DPS loss — [[d:druid#1485403484101808270]] [[d:druid#1485404277496217883]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-22), [[d:druid#1485405962385752124]] (rutherbird disputing net DPS claim).
- **[Balance]** Devs (Fat Pibble/class designer) confirmed Moonfury is already an outlier: "usually talents like these are in row 6 and increase dmg by 10%, so Moonfury is already way out of the norm" (already above the normal 10% ceiling for such talents), which they cited as a reason to be cautious about pushing it much further — [[d:druid#1485406966871359570]] (2026-03-22).
- **[Balance]** Concrete post-patch DPS gap estimates from Hotverdomme-Nord (who is in close contact with the class designer): pre-patch boomkin was ~40.5-41% below the server's top DPS; the day-one/patch-week gap was reported similarly (300 dps behind top on straightforward fights); by 2026-03-25 the estimate had narrowed to "roughly 150-350 dps lower than mages, warlocks, warriors, rogues," with boomkins with T3.5 "basically in the same spot we were before [the patch]" and boomkins without T3.5 "quite rough" — [[d:druid#1485068889556652153]] (2026-03-22), [[d:druid#1486337529870286989]] (2026-03-25).
- **[Balance]** Community "buff boomkin" pressure campaign continued all week: reports of raid parties measuring 80-100 overall dps lost per boomkin week-over-week (one player adopted the display name "Koalafurieux - BUFF BOOMIES"), and a player (Fyriel) posted formal written feedback to the official forum (Eclipse gameplay/RNG/proc inconsistency) — [[d:druid#1485406611043254444]] (Koalafurieux, 2026-03-22), [[d:druid#1485753495498784971]] (Fyriel, 2026-03-24, https://forum.turtlecraft.gg/viewtopic.php?f=42&t=24455), [[d:druid#1486194047256100934]] (Comentor summarizing sentiment, 2026-03-25: "devs are like 'We have changed Oomkin, pray we do not change it further'").
- **[Balance, bug]** Wrath of Cenarius trinket/effect no longer scales with number of targets hit (regressed to a flat ~5% chance per cast); reported to the class designer by Micro Jackson, unresolved — [[d:druid#1484940537478774855]] (2026-03-21). The class designer separately confirmed this same trinket was "designed with hybrids in mind" but is performing only mediocre — [[d:druid#1484941183988797573]].
- **[Resto]** Priests received a large healing buff this patch (Renew initially healing ~60% more than Rejuvenation, hotfixed down to ~40% more after complaints by reducing its duration from 21s to 18s); community view is that Resto Druid ("the HOT class") now has objectively worse HoTs than Priest, on top of Priest's AoE heals (Lightwell), shields, flash heals, and big cooldowns (Ascendance) — [[d:druid#1486045725258416419]] [[d:druid#1486046284728238142]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-25).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Boomkin, pre-patch/at-eclipse]** Standard: keep Moonfire + Insect Swarm dots up, cast into Eclipse states; if no Eclipse buff, cast Insect Swarm on up-to-4 mobs with the Insect Swarm idol then Hurricane before Solar/Arcane window ends for burst — [[d:druid#1483578568716849332]] (Voodoo, 2026-03-18).
- **[Boomkin, post-patch — contested]** After 1.18.1, several players found spamming Wrath outperforms working Starfire into the rotation even in Arcane Eclipse, due to Starfire's longer cast time and pushback issues. Example rotation reported as best: open Moonfire→Insect Swarm→Starfire once or twice (to proc Nature Eclipse)→spam Wrath while keeping dots up, recast Starfire only to refresh Nature Eclipse when the debuff wears off — [[d:druid#1484673452152721519]] (Cowmoohflage/Arrowgance, detailed, 2026-03-21), corroborated [[d:druid#1484760064895090698]] (Lythany: opens Moonfire>IS>SF 1-2x>IS>Wrath till nature proc), [[d:druid#1484740403105234954]] (Shardphoenix). Widely believed to be unintended/bugged behavior rather than working as designed.
- **[Feral cat]** Standard bleed rotation: keep bleeds up (Rake, Rip) and spam Claw (buffed by Open Wounds talent), fueled by "basically infinite free energy" from Ancient Brutality talent; this beats powershift+Shred rotations UNLESS the target is bleed-immune (e.g. roughly half of Molten Core bosses), in which case switch to a powershift+Shred spec — [[d:druid#1483241636598386778]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-17). On bleedable targets, in-raid A/B test on Kruul found powershifting vs not-powershifting gave "very similar dps (within error margin)" for two similarly-geared cats — [[d:druid#1483070411171172404]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-16), disputed generally by Fat Pibble ("powershifting will always perform better" as a tool) — [[d:druid#1483068920368857218]].
- **[Feral cat leveling]** Simple rotation: Tiger's Fury → bleeds (Pounce, Rake, Rip) → Faerie Fire → Claw — [[d:druid#1482758584579391551]] (Whatshername, 2026-03-15). Use Rip immediately rather than waiting for 5 combo points on short fights; wait for 5cp Rip only if the fight is expected to run 30+ seconds — [[d:druid#1482760647409143829]] [[d:druid#1482762940250718291]] (Whatshername/Thalamier). Rip refunds energy via the Ancient Brutality talent — [[d:druid#1482770665193865389]] (TowelDruid).
- **[Feral cat, energy breakpoints]** Reshift (powershift) below 28 energy to dump the last GCD before running out; 28 energy = 1 tick away from another Shred (use it), 27 = 2 ticks away — this is the standard breakpoint used in macros — [[d:druid#1482840003154546970]] [[d:druid#1482840263985725440]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-16).
- **[Feral cat, buff maintenance]** Reshifting (powershifting for energy) drops the Tiger's Fury buff (must be reapplied after every reshift) but Blood Frenzy's attack-speed bonus persists through reshift — [[d:druid#1482843960857722960]] [[d:druid#1482844249295945854]] (Řezáč, 2026-03-16; confirmed by poisonouscabbage advice on when to reapply, [[d:druid#1482845022519820418]]).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Boomkin]** Hurricane is the main/only real AoE tool; widely reported as clunky due to its 10-second CD after a channel is interrupted or cancelled early (moving, sleep/CC, silence, even after dispel) — [[d:druid#1482501170680565812]] [[d:druid#1482532141077106709]] (Voodoo), [[d:druid#1482975539810533446]] (Voodoo, Emerald Sanctum example). Community wishlist repeatedly asked for CD removal or per-tick mana cost instead of full-cost-per-cast — see Contested section; Habu (respected voice) argued removing the CD wouldn't fix much and that the high mana cost already self-limits spam — [[d:druid#1482972247600791674]] (2026-03-16).
- **[Feral cat]** No AoE/cleave tool exists for cat; considered a known, longstanding weakness — [[d:druid#1482819317643870320]] (Habu), [[d:druid#1484730718767812648]].
- **[Feral cat AoE, open-world]** Trick: swap gear/idol combos are irrelevant for cat AoE since it has none; players lean on bear Swipe or reroll spec for AoE farming content.
- **[Bear tank AoE]** Toolkit limited to Swipe, Demoralizing Roar (with Thorns talent dealing dmg), and tab-targeting; considered mediocre for large trash packs, especially against mages spamming AoE without an AoE taunt rotation — [[d:druid#1483622481011933184]] (Mot, 2026-03-15).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Spell hit]** Cap is 16% for PvE raids (vs level-63 bosses), 3% for PvP; base miss rate at level 60 is 4% vs same-level, rising to 17% vs bosses (+3 levels); each point above 16% is wasted since there is always a hard 1% miss floor — [[d:druid#1482736105634791637]] [[d:druid#1482736517960040582]] (Habu, 2026-03-15). Emerald Blessing world buff gives +1% spell hit — [[d:druid#1482736224853823622]]. Natural Weapons talent gives 5% hit needed from gear (reducing the gear requirement to reach cap) — [[d:druid#1482512620949864630]] (Fat Pibble with Air Pods).
- **[Boomkin, hit vs crit/sp tradeoff — contested]** Sim tools sometimes suggest dropping ~3% hit for crit/sp is a DPS gain (e.g. +35 dps in one case), but community pushback (Hotverdomme-Nord) says the difference is often small enough that keeping hit for consistent gameplay ("not missing Starfire") is reasonable, especially if not min-maxing for records — [[d:druid#1482879879065112707]] (Lythany) [[d:druid#1482886520363815117]] [[d:druid#1482891219737772245]] (Hotverdomme-Nord, 2026-03-16). Historical note: during AQ40-Naxx era, many boomkins ran a 12-14% hit "BiS" setup rather than capping at 16% — [[d:druid#1482886520363815117]].
- **[Feral cat, hit]** 9% hit is commonly cited threshold combined with Natural Weapons talent (5% from gear covers the remainder to reach cap without over-investing) — [[d:druid#1482511416559796476]] (Powerofvibes, 2026-03-15), [[d:druid#1482512620949864630]] (Fat Pibble with Air Pods).
- **[Feral cat, haste]** Haste multiplies rather than adds: e.g. Tiger's Fury (10%) and a haste set bonus (20%) combine multiplicatively, giving 1/(1.1×1.2) ≈ 0.7575 relative swing timer, not simple addition — [[d:druid#1483140434137190520]] (Blackfox correcting Micro Jackson's math, 2026-03-16). Haste is considered NOT very valuable for cat DPS because cat's damage profile is heavily skill (bleed/finisher)-weighted rather than white-swing-weighted — [[d:druid#1484713476835377243]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-21).
- **[Bear tank]** Defense stat gives very little value to bear tanks; most prioritize Stamina/Agility (avoidance+more hits=more rage) — [[d:druid#1484719584270352385]] (Shardphoenix), [[d:druid#1484719745063325737]] (Ilillill).
- **[Bear tank]** 2% haste on helm (Wolfsheart trinket alternative debate) vs +5 rage: haste generally preferred for versatility across cat/bear unless specifically threat-starved — [[d:druid#1483608887025205388]] (Vic, 2026-03-17).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Boomkin utility]** Boomkin form gives a 3% spell crit buff to the party/group; with 6/8 Naxx set bonus, Moonfire/Insect Swarm ticks debuff the target to take more nature/arcane damage; Insect Swarm also lowers the target's chance to hit, and boomkins apply Faerie Fire's spell-hit debuff more reliably than feral/resto thanks to high spell hit — [[d:druid#1484260797235462236]] [[d:druid#1484261077796524213]] [[d:druid#1484261538767568966]] (Eepyman, 2026-03-19).
- **[Boomkin, dev feedback]** The class designer told Voodoo they do not plan to add any Eclipse-debuff-clearing cooldown, and intend to keep Owlkin Frenzy as a defensively-oriented talent rather than a haste/damage cooldown — [[d:druid#1485305218379022418]] (Voodoo relaying Moriena's feedback, 2026-03-22).
- **[Bear tank]** Toolkit is limited to essentially 2 aggro-generating abilities (Maul, queued for the next swing; Savage Bite, 6s CD) plus Swipe (mostly an excess-rage dump with weak per-target threat on single targets); community request to remove the CD on Savage Bite to allow full rage dumping — [[d:druid#1484741482169499689]] (Valarya, 2026-03-21), [[d:druid#1484741725451845772]] (Comentor).
- **[Feral cat]** Reshifting (powershifting) for energy drops Tiger's Fury but not Blood Frenzy's attack-speed bonus — see Rotation section; Tiger's Fury's attack-speed component is described as already persisting through reshift by one bear/cat player, contradicting the general claim — [[d:druid#1485272907814015056]] (Holydruid, 2026-03-21; contested with [[d:druid#1482843960857722960]]).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Threat, general]** Pre-patch, bear was considered the best/most overtuned raid tank and slated for a threat nerf to core-ability threat modifiers — [[d:druid#1483016079209926688]] (Habu, 2026-03-16). Classic pull-tanking tips for leveling (deadmines-era): mark a skull kill target, pre-cast Regrowth/Rejuv before a pull if starting without rage, LOS pull when possible, ask healers not to shield/HoT the tank pre-pull, use Dragonbreath Chili from level 35, use the best agility (and strength) elixirs available, focus elites over non-elites — [[d:druid#1483462987678351480]] (Dinousek, 2026-03-14).
- **[Threat, post-patch — heavily contested]** Reports diverge sharply by server/gear. Multiple detailed raid tests found threat down ~10-20% overall, with the worst impact in the first ~10 seconds of a pull ("snap threat"), where paladins now clearly win the race even against a similarly-buffed bear — [[d:druid#1484585026158067752]] (Holydruid), [[d:druid#1484733781331021875]] [[d:druid#1484734693550067904]] (Valarya, 2026-03-21), [[d:druid#1485730370954592399]] (Sha: "the first 10 seconds are a nightmare now"), [[d:druid#1485731889728721006]] (Valarya, 2026-03-24), [[d:druid#1485571635330289694]] (TBR: "I now feel like I absolutely have to use all three of our abilities to keep the boss on me, especially in the early stages"). Over a longer fight, sustained threat still favors bear over warrior once the early lead is established — [[d:druid#1485732568736337981]] [[d:druid#1485733638514409613]] (Holydruid/Valarya). Counter-report: on Ambershire, one bear tank felt the threat nerf "didn't feel too impactful" even while tanking a first BWL clear — [[d:druid#1485296567123710134]] (Fikey, 2026-03-23) — and other Ambershire voices maintain druid tanks are "fine... will still get groups and raids without issue" — [[d:druid#1486190103528345680]] (Comentor, 2026-03-25). Dungeon tanking (vs. raid tanking) is generally considered unaffected/fine — [[d:druid#1486190492780728481]] (Mr. Berold: "the threat problem is more of a raid issue").
- **[Threat gearing]** With hit valued at ~0 for tanks and Savage Bite/Maul frequently dodged/parried at pull-start (RNG, not new to this patch), recommended threat gear swap in raids: Kiss of the Spider (near-essential) and Atiesh give a big boost; DFT (Discerning...Talisman) is now considered overrated for threat since hit is worthless post-cap — [[d:druid#1485732399189987398]] (Valarya), [[d:druid#1485584198881251379]] (poisonouscabbage: dodge/parry threat loss at pull start "even before the patch it was like this"). Alternate Ambershire threat/mitigation swap: drop DFT for Whip or Horn of threat, and swap T2 boots for Boots of the Shadowflame (+2% hit, 44 AP, more mitigation) to compensate the hit loss — [[d:druid#1486322494662119545]] [[d:druid#1486324919456370799]] [[d:druid#1486326018565476443]] (poisonouscabbage, 2026-03-25).
- **[Mechanics]** Thick Hide talent's armor bonus does scale multiplicatively with equipped armor in bear form (unlike vanilla, where it reportedly did not): equipping then unequipping a 100-armor item raises bear-form armor by 506 (100 base armor × the ~360% Dire Bear Form armor multiplier × the 10% from Thick Hide ≈ 100×4.6×1.1) — [[d:druid#1486116482122518578]] [[d:druid#1486121629649207456]] [[d:druid#1486121788604940519]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-25).
- **[Wishlist]** Player suggestion for a cooldown that prevents the next few abilities from being dodged/parried/blocked, to solve pull-start snap-threat RNG — [[d:druid#1486168174096416910]] (Atriana, 2026-03-25; unimplemented).

## Healing specifics
- **[Trinkets]** Breath of Solnius is strong for Regrowth-spam resto builds — used over Rejuvenation Gem at fights like Kruul, Anomalus, Chess, Sapphiron, Thaddius, and Gothik; with Eye of the Dead (EotD) up, players lean back to spamming Rejuvenation instead — [[d:druid#1483107829190496400]] [[d:druid#1483108161987543281]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-16). It's the only trinket that lets a Regrowth-spam resto compete with paladin/priest healing throughput, and is usually paired with Warmth of Forgiveness — [[d:druid#1483117422712979548]] (Bapi).
- **[Mana efficiency]** Max-rank Healing Touch is better value per mana than HoTs at typical gear levels; HoTs scale better with healing power per mana and receive a -30% mana cost reduction talent at endgame, at which point they become the better choice — [[d:druid#1484189596945223801]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-18).
- **[Omen]** Getting Omen of Clarity procs is the core resto mana-management tool, enabling free-cast Healing Touch spam — [[d:druid#1483994803665502450]] (Albu).
- **[Healing Touch spec]** Whether a dedicated Nature's Grace + Healing Touch-rank-spam healing style is viable is debated: workable for dungeon/MC-level content but not competitive from BWL onward even with supporting gear — [[d:druid#1484563224048173096]] (Valarya, 2026-03-18); requires specialized gear and "heals somewhat okay" per another assessment — [[d:druid#1483807645243805877]] (Micro Jackson).
- **[1.18.1 nerf]** "Blooming Bud" (a resto item/enchant effect) had its duration nerfed with no compensation buff elsewhere; expected to become a sub-1%-healing-value bonus, making raw healing-power gear (Bishop's Helm, Meph's shoulders) better choices instead — [[d:druid#1484580568040607816]] [[d:druid#1484581881180848279]] (akitoe/Micro Jackson, 2026-03-20).
- **[Raid comp]** Consensus that raids want at most 2 resto druids; a 3rd is "never ever [good]" due to buff-cap/HoT-overlap; the T3.5 5-set healing bonus is good for Molten-Core-level content but "extreme weak" from BWL onward — [[d:druid#1484987888885174362]] (Valarya, 2026-03-21).
- **[1.18.1 comparison]** Priests received a large mana/healing buff same patch; resto druids (nominally "the HoT class") now have objectively worse HoTs than priest's Renew, plus priests get AoE heals (Lightwell), shields, flash heals, and stronger cooldowns (Ascendance vs. the 30-min Tranquility) — [[d:druid#1486046284728238142]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-25), [[d:druid#1486085637793906848]] (Moonshroud: "Tranq so op it needs 30 min cd, but priests can PoH spam for same output every 5 mins with Ascendence... renew > rejuv").
- **[Addons]** Puppeteer raid-frame addon strongly recommended for druid healers — [[d:druid#1484687284187037736]] (Holydruid, 2026-03-19); it broke Swiftmend functionality for some players right after the patch, fixed for at least one user by fully closing the client and updating all addons — [[d:druid#1486074041965215924]] [[d:druid#1486077245108912302]] [[d:druid#1486088867739336724]] (Oaks/Comentor, 2026-03-24/25). Lunaframes recommended as an alternative raid-frame addon — [[d:druid#1485011354527072276]] (Eepyman, 2026-03-22).
- **[Macro]** A Nature's Swiftness + max-rank Healing Touch macro is one of the few macros resto actually benefits from — [[d:druid#1484265840147824702]] (Tarane, 2026-03-19).

- **[PvP, armor meta]** General complaint that Bear Form's armor value is diminished in the current PvP meta because so much non-physical/armor-penetrating damage exists now (rogue poisons with high armor penetration, extra shaman attacks), so bear's armor mainly still matters vs. warriors — [[d:druid#1485775320182296587]] (Miaupow, 2026-03-24), acknowledged/disputed as more of a rogue-class-design issue than a druid one — [[d:druid#1485790619371573441]] (Tim).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Reshift]** Reshifting (powershifting) drops the Tiger's Fury buff, requiring reapplication after every reshift; Blood Frenzy's attack-speed bonus persists through reshift — [[d:druid#1482843960857722960]] [[d:druid#1482844249295945854]] (Řezáč, 2026-03-16).
- **[Ride of the Wild]** The pickup mechanic is reversed from its intended design: the person you want to carry must put YOU on their ignore list (not the other way around) before you can pick them up as a mount — confirmed as an unintentional bug by devs, and reportedly took ~2 weeks to fix — [[d:druid#1482646024085442671]] [[d:druid#1483112695895949453]] [[d:druid#1483112817803526309]] (Astrasa/Eepyman).
- **[Swift Travel Form]** Visual-only bug where, after a ride ends, the passenger still appears (to the rider only, not to the passenger or others) to be riding the druid; a separate long-standing bug lets a travel-form druid appear as and move at mount speed as another player model — [[d:druid#1483952443489521797]] [[d:druid#1483963265213267968]] (Comentor/Eepyman, 2026-03-19).
- **[Mounts]** If a passenger being carried in Swift Travel Form takes fatal fall damage (e.g. jumping off a cliff), the passenger dies but the druid mount survives; the dead passenger's body slides across the terrain until it hits an obstacle (priests using their angel-form avoid this) — [[d:druid#1483873228278595604]] (Comentor), [[d:druid#1483875068072296529]] [[d:druid#1483875112246837310]] (Eepyman, 2026-03-18).
- **[Glyph of the Moon/Stars]** Cosmetic bug throughout the entire chunk (2026-03-20 onward): equipping the glyph while in Moonkin Form randomly turns the character into an unrelated race/gender model (commonly an underwear-clad opposite-faction Night Elf) instead of the intended cosmetic effect; persisted through multiple attempted hotfixes, still broken as of 2026-03-25 with reports describing it as "a 50/50 gamble" each server restart; a temporary per-shift workaround is sitting in a barber chair and cancelling while in Moonkin Form (only lasts until the next unshift) — [[d:druid#1484610059773153430]] [[d:druid#1484642221683904614]] [[d:druid#1484978312861450301]] [[d:druid#1486109840852910131]] (Tyrona/Floatin/Umii/Julia), [[d:druid#1485693297585291415]] (Yoshi🌵, barber chair workaround), [[d:druid#1486161323028119644]] (Magumba, 2026-03-25: still a "50/50 gamble" every restart).
- **[Balance, ruled bug]** Wrath of Cenarius (a trinket/talent effect) no longer scales its proc chance with the number of enemies hit, reduced to a flat ~5%-per-cast chance, contrary to its intended classic behavior; reported to the class designer, unresolved by end of chunk — [[d:druid#1484940537478774855]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-21).
- **[Balance, ruled NOT a bug]** Starfire's underwhelming post-patch damage was confirmed by the class designer to be mathematically correct (BoAT, Moonfury, and Eclipse % calculations all verified) — the underperformance is attributed to the increased cast time rather than a calculation error — [[d:druid#1484928327889326150]] (Fat Pibble/class designer, 2026-03-21).
- **[Balance, ruled WAI]** T3.5 Hurricane 3-set bonus only boosting Hurricane's base damage (not total, ~40% of the value many expected from the tooltip) was investigated and ruled working as intended, though the tooltip wording is disputed as misleading — [[d:druid#1486337529870286989]] [[d:druid#1485415446172663929]] (see 1.18.1 changes section).
- **[Item, possible bug]** One player reported Iron Counterweight applied to a staff gave no attack-speed increase in Bear Form, despite the enchant otherwise being confirmed to work while shapeshifted — [[d:druid#1485034438235521216]] (Duck, 2026-03-22; unresolved, single report).
- **[Visual bugs]** Swipe appeared to reset the melee swing timer for one bear tank per their swing-timer addon (likely a display/addon bug rather than a real mechanic) — [[d:druid#1485557207272788080]] [[d:druid#1485566214418993233]] (TBR/poisonouscabbage, 2026-03-23). Tiger's Fury sometimes shows as active in a tracking macro but is not visibly buffed on the buff bar, unrelated to the buff-cap — possibly a PFUI-specific issue — [[d:druid#1486130624094933022]] [[d:druid#1486143428185161892]] (Erasu/Tephus, 2026-03-25).
- **[Class call, BWL]** Nefarian's class-call mechanic forces druids into Cat Form regardless of whether Cat Form was ever trained; a tree-form resto druid discovered they could shift back to Tree Form during the call — [[d:druid#1486034233624367295]] [[d:druid#1485995298831597688]] (see Dungeons & raids section).
