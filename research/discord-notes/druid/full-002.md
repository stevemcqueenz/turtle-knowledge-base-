- **[Cat]** Feral has essentially zero cleave/AoE, a major recurring complaint from top parsers: "Feral remains as one of two specs in the game with no aoe" — [[d:druid#1452806971668303963]], [[d:druid#1452796866768011397]] (Thalamier, self-described 2nd-best server feral [[d:druid#1452805120684331068]]).
- **[Tank]** Feral bear is "the best MT in the game currently," Paladin close behind as OT/AoE, Warrior good but a step below, Shaman viable high-DPS OT — [[d:druid#1445900337192898801]] (Thalamier), corroborated re: shaman tanks [[d:druid#1452855834571837523]].
- **[Balance]** Boomkin damage is weak overall: ranked ~328th for first moonkin on Naxx logs; called "top 5 worst spec" by one player, though best geared boomies can reach ~2k dps and it's "decent once you get good gear in later phases" — [[d:druid#1453509360780312781]], [[d:druid#1453509360780312781]], [[d:druid#1454497597657186397]], [[d:druid#1455122469324329025]] (disputed: some claim it's "overpowered" [[d:druid#1453496265881948304]]).
- **[Balance]** Boss-by-boss Naxx/Kara40 comparison (~23 bosses): boomkin clearly better on Loatheb, Rupturan, 4HM, Gothik; competitive on Kruul, Echo, KT; worse on the other 17 (mostly bleedable bosses favor cat) — [[d:druid#1446118474593341470]].
- **[Healer]** Resto druid is in "a pretty good spot" but healing meters are dominated by an overtuned Paladin T3.5 set this patch — [[d:druid#1451306437446996029]], [[d:druid#1451331585449263225]].
- **[General]** Druid role summary from a detailed community writeup: Bear = beefiest tank; Cat = highest-effort, high-DPS, physical-crit party buff; Boomkin = "big damn chickens," mediocre DPS but strong utility (chicken aura); Resto = strong raid HoT/burst healing, tree aura is a mandatory-feeling buff — [[d:druid#1452775722996203692]].

## Leveling
- **[General]** Cat form learned at level 20 (trainer); Bear form ~level 10; Travel Form level 30; Moonkin Form level 30 (recently lowered from a higher level per multiple mentions); Swift Travel Form level 60 via quest chain — [[d:druid#1448039669471248639]], [[d:druid#1452477... see below]], [[d:druid#1458250478864433345]] ("Its level 30 now").
- **[General]** Standard advice: level as Feral (bear tank + cat dps share nearly all talents/gear), respec into Omen of Clarity around level 30-37 after core feral talents; Boomkin leveling is viable but has more downtime and worse gear availability pre-60 — [[d:druid#1455323463995752572]], [[d:druid#1453621744240951429]], [[d:druid#1452423158656401471]].
- **[General]** Weapon guide for feral leveling: Smites Mighty Hammer (lvl 18), Manual Crowd Pummeler (lvl 28), Thornroot Maul (lvl 32), Grimlock's Charge from Uldaman (lvl 42), Ice Barbed Spear (lvl 51), then follow the BiS sheet at 60 — [[d:druid#1448738353980637185]].
- **[General]** Grifter's crafted leather set (str/agi/stam, some spirit) recommended while leveling; Dragonmaw set 4-piece good, 6-piece considered bad — [[d:druid#1449379028992786575]], [[d:druid#1450985278226305247]].
- **[Balance]** Sylvan Blessing (mana-on-crit-heal-ish leveling talent) is generally disliked/considered weak for balance, but multiple players say it's decent for feral leveling sustain (heal self, then kill for mana regen) and for dungeon "sniping" last hits like a Spirit Tap — [[d:druid#1448377629701968006]], [[d:druid#1448379992382771420]], [[d:druid#1456857137497112607]].
- **[Stats]** Leveling stat conversion: 1 Strength = 2 AP (bear), 1 Strength = 2.4 AP and 1 Agility = 1 AP (cat, full feral talents); Agility and Strength are worth about the same effective value while leveling (~2.6x AP per point) — [[d:druid#1451073471265116160]], [[d:druid#1447703890131947681]], [[d:druid#1451388536128409833]].
- **[Tank]** Feral leveling-tank threat rotation macro (SuperCleverRoids syntax): Tiger's Fury if no Blood Frenzy, Rip at 5CP if not up, Ferocious Bite at 5CP, Rake if not up, Claw/Shred depending on positional — [[d:druid#1448986229734375424]] (poisonouscabbage).
- **[General]** Fastest-leveling method uses player-craftable survival tents (from the Survival "profession" learned in STV) for constant rested XP near Stormwind gates — [[d:druid#1457170124162207807]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Cat]** Bleedable-target priority: FF on CD (procs Omen), Rake, Claw/Shred to 5 combo points, keep Rip up, Ferocious Bite at 5CP (never bite below 5CP — "20% chance to remove your bleeds this way" at 4CP with Open Wounds math) — [[d:druid#1445714923911909477]], [[d:druid#1450153022339547290]], [[d:druid#1450153362892001310]].
- **[Cat]** "Bleed + Claw > Bleed + Shred > Shred" for damage per energy, per a spreadsheet analysis; but Idol of Laceration makes Shred a big single-target option, and top parses on bleedable bosses are often Shred, not pure Claw — [[d:druid#1445551483088142373]], [[d:druid#1451312455350227044]], [[d:druid#1451328756525301910]].
- **[Cat]** For non-bleedable targets: Claw is bad without bleeds; use Shred + Ferocious Bite at 4CP, and use Idol of Ferocity (does nothing on non-bleedable) vs Idol of Laceration (shred buff, good bleed-immune option) — [[d:druid#1445714923911909477]], [[d:druid#1445715561324482571]], [[d:druid#1453677955770486886]], [[d:druid#1453678172548759619]].
- **[Cat]** Idol swap trick: use Idol of Savagery on the opening Rip, then swap to Idol of Ferocity for the rest of the fight (snapshotted); only the 3 middle relic slots can swap in combat — [[d:druid#1449865381530566807]], [[d:druid#1449867930891321456]].
- **[Cat]** Tiger's Fury: worthless without the talent, "like bloodlust without cooldown" when talented; Reshift (powershift) gives ~30 energy net after TF uptime accounted for, used at low energy/under 17-20 energy — [[d:druid#1454154979450552415]], [[d:druid#1453546931832754248]], [[d:druid#1453547473309007882]].
- **[Balance]** Boomkin single-target: Moonfire+Insect Swarm up, then Wrath-spam into Arcane Eclipse, Starfire-spam into Nature Eclipse; if a cast will finish AFTER the opposing Eclipse could proc, let it finish rather than clip it (it just gets excluded from the current Eclipse and counted toward Eclipse cooldown) — [[d:druid#1445972819505578177]], [[d:druid#1448661226346385581]], [[d:druid#1448664156336164885]].
- **[Balance]** Reapply DoTs only in the last GCD before the current Eclipse expires (snapshot the buff), never clip a Starfire cast to refresh Moonfire early — [[d:druid#1455291442250387527]], [[d:druid#1455291522957316137]], [[d:druid#1455291802281312327]].
- **[Balance]** Suggested macro to auto-pick Wrath/Starfire by current Eclipse/Solstice debuff (requires addon support) — [[d:druid#1452765526282731641]].
- **[Tank]** Bear rage-pooling/threat rotation: Maul is priority (best rage/threat), Savage Bite as rage dump/snap threat on Clearcasting procs; taunt can be used strategically (instant threat = highest current threat holder, useful to redirect/conserve rage) — [[d:druid#1455603526519619807]], [[d:druid#1455920926842556429]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Balance]** Hurricane is the primary boomkin AoE; worth using at roughly 3+ targets (below that, just DoT both targets and cast normally); once committed to AoE, skip dotting and get Hurricane's cooldown ticking ASAP — [[d:druid#1456764745037385898]], [[d:druid#1456765115331383531]], [[d:druid#1456765270025834648]], [[d:druid#1456765385050165341]].
- **[Balance]** Hurricane sometimes shows fewer than 10 ticks on meters due to normal miss/resist chance (even a level-60 vs level-1 target has ~1% base miss), not a bug per se, though on higher-level/boss dummies more misses were observed than on the level-1 dummy — [[d:druid#1448081756388458527]], [[d:druid#1447995418603421747]].
- **[Cat]** Feral has no real AoE; best "AoE" is Swipe (3-target cap) for bear tanking, cat cleave output is essentially zero — [[d:druid#1452802613794967563]], [[d:druid#1448797627171868773]].
- **[Tank]** Bear AoE tanking: tab-target + Swipe (3-target cap), Demoralizing Roar for initial thorns-based threat on a pull (buffed w/ T1/T2 3-piece thorns bonuses), Idol of Evergrowth adds thorns-damage-as-AoE on Roar but is niche (only beats Swipe around ~15 mobs) — [[d:druid#1448813040450670654]], [[d:druid#1448822098804998225]], [[d:druid#1448823173322768597]], [[d:druid#1448343863814000691]] (Roar itself ~80 base threat, ~124-125 with double-buffed thorns).
- **[Contested]** Bear AoE threat viability in raid trash disputed: some say bear AoE is "great," others say it's genuinely weak outside dungeons/ZG-style large single pulls, and that taunt+roar+Lip is often faster on packs than trying to hold everything — [[d:druid#1452023497533685851]] vs [[d:druid#1452023564394823832]], [[d:druid#1452023678156935168]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Balance]** Kangaraxxus stat-weight simulation example (relative to Spell Power=1025 baseline of 1.0 per point): Nature Power 0.47, Arcane Power 0.53, Spell Hit% 21.45, Spell Crit% 17.52, Haste% 12.81, Spell Penetration 1.56 — screenshot [[d:druid#1448306290878316658]].
- **[Balance]** Boomkin hit cap: 16% total needed; talents give 3%, so 13% must come from gear; sim/priority is generally Hit (to cap) > Spell Crit > Spell Power > Int > Spirit, though exact weights depend on gear via the sim — [[d:druid#1449561510392365239]], [[d:druid#1449556886541176954]], [[d:druid#1449556078718095531]].
- **[Cat]** Feral hit cap: 8% before talents needed vs level-63 raid bosses; only 5% needed for most trash (61-62 level mobs need 4-5%) — [[d:druid#1447726944626938069]], [[d:druid#1447574158455079123]].
- **[Cat]** Example cat stat weights (per VanillaRatingBuster addon output, agi/str/ap/crit%/hit%): 2.76 / 2.64 / 1 / 30.13 / 31.85 — [[d:druid#1449398547404423239]].
- **[Healer]** Druid spirit-to-mana regen: MP2 = (spirit/5) + 15 for druids (vs (spirit/4)+13 for priests); MP5 = MP2 × 2.5; with T3.5 set bonus, regen-in-combat can reach 40% of out-of-combat regen — [[d:druid#1450410384627601429]], [[d:druid#1448426912837009498]], [[d:druid#1449511348751827154]].
- **[Tank]** Effective HP from armor scales linearly up to the 75% damage-reduction cap (~17.5k armor); beyond that there is a further diminishing-returns layer on top of the normal formula (community testing ongoing, no confirmed public formula) — [[d:druid#1449681837554405416]], [[d:druid#1449702544262565971]], [[d:druid#1457013521903780107]].
- **[Tank]** Bear defense/crit-immunity: getting to 440 defense (crit immune) requires close to full T2/T3 + specific trinkets/enchants and is considered a trap/low priority by several experienced tanks since bears can't block/parry — mitigation should instead prioritize armor+HP; disputed by others who value crit immunity for burst-damage fights (Twins, dragons) — [[d:druid#1454812937444393186]], [[d:druid#1454816413024780288]], vs [[d:druid#1454831110914703410]].
- **[Tank]** Dodge is largely an "anti-stat" for bears: can't dodge crits/crushes (the actual killer), baits healers into complacency, and doesn't meaningfully help threat; better to itemize for health/stamina instead — [[d:druid#1452942318167654521]], [[d:druid#1453012934564511864]] (disputed by [[d:druid#1452018630631096330]] who values it for big trash pulls).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[General]** No official Turtle-specific pre-raid BiS list existed for balance/resto for much of this window; standard advice was to build a gear-planner list and prioritize Spell Damage > Spell Hit (to cap) > Spell Crit > Intellect > Spirit/MP5 > Stamina — [[d:druid#1446317623544381602]].
- **[Balance]** Scythe of the Unseen Path (legendary) confirmed BIS trinket, "not even close, like 200% better than the second trinket"; acquisition is extremely RNG-heavy (2% drop from a boss kill every ~5 days after farming materials + a rare-recipe drop from ES); widely criticized as the biggest itemization "fumble" on the server — [[d:druid#1454619304614953111]], [[d:druid#1447641968682209373]], [[d:druid#1447620265898152028]].
- **[Balance]** Hatereaver Cog (Hateforge Quarry) is BIS mitigation-ish trinket combo with Araxna's Husk for bears; ~5-6 min stealth-duo farm runs — [[d:druid#1451939426291679365]], [[d:druid#1456774328053006359]] area context [[d:druid#1456774450057052336]].
- **[Balance]** Ring/trinket progression example: Nightmare's Shard + Neltharion's Tear is the best trinket pairing, KT trinket good for trash; Seal of the Damned + T3 ring a strong ring duo if SRs allow — [[d:druid#1456773152796442725]], [[d:druid#1456768135905542146]].
- **[Tank]** Threat set vs mitigation set: bears want two separate sets and swap based on fight ("mit vs threat" tradeoff is described as inherent to bear BiS questions); ~1% haste BIS enchant for threat sets, +100HP or ZG enchant BIS for mitigation sets, Wolfshead helm enchant BIS whenever you might cat-form on a fight — [[d:druid#1446615259614937234]], [[d:druid#1452926334010527857]].
- **[Cat]** T2.5 3-set benefits Claw rotation more; T2.5/T3 5-set makes Shred better when it procs; Idol of Laceration is a "huge Shred buff" that can push Shred ahead even on bleedable fights for well-geared cats — [[d:druid#1451314956363632730]], [[d:druid#1451312455350227044]].
- **[Feral]** Weapon math example (Finkle's Lava Dredger vs Rod of the Churning Hourglass): after converting hourglass's agi/str/haste (accounting for Heart of the Wild +20% str and Kings), Hourglass (~168 effective AP-equivalent + ~0.8% crit + 2% haste) is generally considered better DPS than Finkle's flat 210 AP — [[d:druid#1458621037469241488]], [[d:druid#1458647262313058440]], [[d:druid#1458647421436432487]].
- **[Tank]** Armor Pen mechanics: worth ~1% effective-HP reduction per 50 armor (65 for level 63 targets/bosses) stripped only near a target's last points of armor; scales from worthless (0 armor) up through amazing (mid armor) back down to mediocre against very high-armor targets — [[d:druid#1447903971770630199]].
- **[Tank]** Twins/AQ40 tanking gear reference point: ~13-14k unbuffed armor with early AQ/BWL pieces was enough to offtank Twins on a horde pvp server without pally buffs; general guidance is ~17-18k armor with Inspiration for a progression bear at that stage — [[d:druid#1451947754187133146]], [[d:druid#1451941627110359153]].
- **[General]** Resto pre-raid gearing path (for a fresh healer): crafted healer belt (+8 heal enchant), crafted healer rings (+12 heal enchant), craftable cloak upgrade good until Naxx, Hide of the Wild instead of default cloak once available — [[d:druid#1456477875749392404]], [[d:druid#1456478102682341539]], [[d:druid#1456478368479318099]], [[d:druid#1456479972272111921]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Cat]** Reshift (powershift) mechanics: clears Tiger's Fury buff but not its haste portion; used when the alternative is doing nothing for 1.5s; effectively nets ~30 energy given high TF uptime — [[d:druid#1450189844444614747]], [[d:druid#1453546931832754248]].
- **[Tank]** Base bear-form threat modifier: 130% baseline, 145% with Feral Instinct talent — [[d:druid#1455708290997686473]].
- **[Tank]** Savage Bite has a 225% threat modifier; Maul scales better for raw threat/dps but Savage Bite is a good rage-dump/snap-threat tool especially on Clearcasting procs — [[d:druid#1448753933416923287]], [[d:druid#1455603526519619807]], [[d:druid#1455603607918612632]].
- **[General]** Extra-attack effects (HoJ, Windfury) work in feral forms (chance-on-hit does NOT work in forms; chance-on-equip DOES) — [[d:druid#1451273688388468918]], [[d:druid#1451273877496926248]].
- **[Cat]** HoJ math: resets the swing timer rather than granting a pure extra swing, making it roughly half as valuable to a cat/bear as a flat extra-attack effect like Whip of Encouragement in practice — [[d:druid#1447922912051855370]], [[d:druid#1447923445575974993]], detailed white/yellow-hit breakdown [[d:druid#1447906722785005618]].
- **[Balance]** Innervate for boomkin is already "tuned when used on others" via T3 2-piece set bonus — [[d:druid#1455354717159948338]].
- **[General]** Nature's Reach (+range talent) has limited PvE value; some raiders skip it entirely to put points into damage — [[d:druid#1452604821419855953]], [[d:druid#1452605223301546014]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[Tank]** Bear tanking has no block/parry, so mitigation comes purely from Armor + Stamina; Strength/Agility both convert to AP (bear gets AP only from Strength, not Agility) which drives threat since bear threat scales with damage output — [[d:druid#1451073471265116160]], [[d:druid#1451658073872793810]], [[d:druid#1451658611997802496]].
- **[Tank]** Recommended pull sequence for dungeon/leveling tanking: pre-pull Regrowth/Rejuv on self for early threat gen, Demoralizing Roar for thorns threat, Maul main target, tab+Maul around, Swipe/Savage Bite as rage allows — [[d:druid#1455466125675466864]], [[d:druid#1455467667497422879]].
- **[Tank]** Pre-pull Tiger's Fury (as cat) carries its 18s haste buff into Bear Form; when it falls off, Enrage/Berserk gives another 18s haste window — [[d:druid#1455468117370081304]].
- **[Tank]** Crit immunity (440 defense) explicitly not required or recommended for most bear tanking; instead stack armor/HP (see Stats section for the disputed nuance on when crit immunity does matter, e.g. Twins/BWL dragons).
- **[Tank]** Vek'nilash (AQ40 Twins) tanking notes: he has Uppercut (big knockback), Unbalancing Strike (lowers defense skill by 100, causing crushing blows), and Mutate Bug (an add/DPS problem); the neutral scorpion adds in the room can "thrash" (armor-shred) — don't Demo Roar or Swipe the neutral mobs on pull; low armor + the armor-shred debuff + an unlucky Blizzard tick caused a near-death example (~10.4k damage in 600ms) — [[d:druid#1451947426381435022]], [[d:druid#1451947793861054507]], [[d:druid#1451950951005945990]].
- **[Tank]** Bear survives the dragon-mount escort/riding-quest fight only via ranged kiting now: stay ranged, sleep the add, root the boss, LOS the faerie dragon, kill it close (grants HP/MP regen), dispel poison/curse, attack with 6-7 Wraths while boss is stunned — "melee-bear cheese no longer works" — [[d:druid#1451670530674397306]], [[d:druid#1451670924880515214]].

## Healing specifics
- **[Healer]** Two main resto builds: a raid build (Tree of Life + Rejuvenation/Regrowth spam) and a dungeon build (no Tree Form, Healing Touch-based); the dungeon HT build is still viable in every 5-man, and using Tree-build inside dungeons isn't bad either — [[d:druid#1455276890657915069]], [[d:druid#1455277051883028721]].
- **[Healer]** Healing Touch-focused (non-tree, non-NG) resto is considered weak even with full gear investment (idol, T2.5 set, Rejuvenation ring) — "just the worst flash spammer in the game with crappy HOTs" — [[d:druid#1445709644684329062]].
- **[Healer]** NG/HT spec with the right idol/set can hit ~8.5k healing crits every ~1.7s but burns mana extremely fast; low-rank Regrowth + low-rank Healing Touch spam is comparatively mana efficient — [[d:druid#1446195847502237840]], [[d:druid#1446196246682271754]].
- **[Healer]** Downranking: Regrowth downranking is recommended; Rejuvenation usually doesn't need downranking if using consumables/pots sensibly; rank 3 Rejuvenation (53 mana, ~1200 heal) used for raid-blanket-HoTing at low healing-power gear levels — [[d:druid#1457053837448712316]], [[d:druid#1457086698340090037]] (though Micro Jackson notes ranks below level 20 take a healing-power penalty and rank 4+ is usually better — [[d:druid#1457650046412787816]]).
- **[Healer]** Swiftmend / HoT tracking is a known pain point; recommended addons Puppeteer (shows HoT durations on party frames) or Cursive (with SuperWoW) — [[d:druid#1453408795534295252]], [[d:druid#1451884695242543208]].
- **[Healer]** Spirit-snapshot gear question ("does resto spirit snapshot still work?") answer: no, it does not snapshot per se, but keep spirit gear on hand for mana regen between packs and for Innervate — [[d:druid#1447563668530270319]], [[d:druid#1447565851887931453]].

## PvP
- **[General]** PvP rank does not decay below your current achieved rank on this server (progress within a rank can regress, but you never drop a full rank) — [[d:druid#1450602824592523398]].
- **[General]** Common PvP builds seen are "full boom, boomheal, full feral, or full heal" — hybrid tri-spec PvP builds (e.g., 4/5 Moonfire crit + bear charge + Nature's Swiftness) are discussed as theoretical but rarely played — [[d:druid#1453786865223077991]], [[d:druid#1453784079798370374]].
- **[Balance]** For Arena, Nature's Swiftness is considered a "no-brainer" pick over Eclipse; in BGs, Eclipse gets more value since you hardcast more — [[d:druid#1453642742054916116]], [[d:druid#1453642548752027723]].
- **[Feral]** Windfury (from Enhancement totems) is valued by feral over pure agility for the extra bear-form attack/aggro it grants (works in forms since it's an equip-style proc) — [[d:druid#1458252085136195698]], [[d:druid#1458252295472152597]], [[d:druid#1458296550164922538]].

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[General]** Required addon stack for competitive feral/cat macro rotations: SuperWoW (client mod) + Nampower + UnitXP3 + SuperCleverRoidMacros (SCRM); functionality hierarchy is RoidMacros < CleveRoidMacros < SuperCleveRoidMacros, with some features needing SuperWoW specifically — [[d:druid#1448987079962005555]], [[d:druid#1452770238356656241]].
- **[Cat]** Example full "1-button" bleed rotation macro (SCRM syntax): `#showtooltip / /cast [nomybuff:"Blood Frenzy"] Tiger's Fury; [nodebuff:"Rip",noimmune:bleed,combo:#>4] Rip; [combo:#5] Ferocious Bite; [nodebuff:"Rake",noimmune:bleed] Rake; [nobehind] Claw; [behind] Shred /cast [mypower:<=20] Reshift` — [[d:druid#1448986229734375424]]; caution: `nodebuff` conditionals break if another druid's dots are on the same target — use Cursive integration instead (`[noimmune:bleed] {"/cursive curse Rake|target"}`) — [[d:druid#1451206423689297962]], [[d:druid#1451564831986679861]].
- **[General]** Bleed/DoT tracking is a recurring pain point on this client (buff/debuff caps, unreliable third-party tracking); recommended addons: Cursive (needs SuperWoW), DoTimer, Chronometer, DoiteAuras (a Weakauras-equivalent) — [[d:druid#1449529561611112520]], [[d:druid#1457137043321786379]] (link: github.com/Player-Doite/DoiteAuras).
- **[Healer]** Puppeteer addon recommended for mouseover-cast healing and HoT-duration party frames; PFUI can auto-cancel-form on cast for some spells — [[d:druid#1447009853653651487]], [[d:druid#1453504235198222429]].
- **[Balance]** Eclipse/proc tracker addon: github.com/mdmnky/druid-eclipse-monitor — [[d:druid#1452765140226146415]].
- **[General]** Spammable-without-leaving-form macros for Cat Form/Prowl/Bear Form require CleveRoid-style `nomybuff` conditionals; base classic macro syntax lacks the needed `!` prefix (only SuperCleveRoidMacros supports it reliably) — [[d:druid#1446111485808873533]], [[d:druid#1446111767443804173]], example working macro [[d:druid#1450502116341842115]].
- **[General]** Client limitation: you cannot cast two different spells from one macro click in 1.12 (e.g., Nature's Swiftness + Healing Touch requires two separate button presses) — [[d:druid#1451522054921781351]], [[d:druid#1453506097825447947]].
- **[Tank]** Resistance-checking script: `/script local b,e,p,n=UnitResistance("player",2); DEFAULT_CHAT_FRAME:AddMessage("Fire Resistance: "..e);` (numbers 0-6 map to armor/holy/fire/nature/frost/shadow/arcane) — [[d:druid#1446082289422569624]].

## Races, professions, gold
- **[General]** Engineering + a gathering profession is considered the safe universal choice; most other professions (besides Engineering) are considered low-value "alt professions" for gold at this stage of the server — [[d:druid#1453179590804504667]], [[d:druid#1453178556161982485]].
- **[General]** Herbalism is low-value at low levels ("crazy cheap") but stays relevant because raid flasks need Black Lotus; Mining considered comparatively more profitable early — [[d:druid#1446669772224860221]], [[d:druid#1446671350386262180]].
- **[Feral]** Solo gold farming: Druid is "not a good DME/dungeon farmer" compared to Paladin/Mage but can lasher-farm from level 56+ (2 packs at once with the right gear) or run stealth-solo dungeon-boss loops with a rogue/druid duo — [[d:druid#1446164034079690783]], [[d:druid#1447781704273231973]], [[d:druid#1447782014106337310]] (Thorns is useless while bear farming, since lashers reflect-immune to nature; bear can't block/parry so takes extra damage farming without gear).
- **[General]** Faction matters slightly for druids: race choice affects access to a 1% spell-hit ring for casters — [[d:druid#1445869015393239235]].
- **[General]** Leatherworking's crafted gear is always "about 15 levels below you," making it poor for leveling gear progression, though the Grifter/Dragonmaw sets remain popular exceptions crafted by others — [[d:druid#1456849610969710622]], [[d:druid#1456849500889944279]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Balance]** Hurricane sometimes shows fewer ticks than expected (7-9 instead of 10) especially against higher-level/boss-level targets vs a level-1 dummy; investigated as likely explained by normal miss/resist chance rather than a hard bug, though not fully confirmed — [[d:druid#1448069108020150433]], [[d:druid#1447995418603421747]], [[d:druid#1447993128110915624]].
- **[Tank]** Idol/relic in-combat swap now sometimes incurs an extra hidden GCD depending on swap timing relative to the current GCD window; behavior described as "janky" and possibly unintentional — [[d:druid#1451233726569910383]], [[d:druid#1451245162624712857]], [[d:druid#1451257657305469240]].
- **[Healer]** "Primal's Blessing" buff (shares the Regrowth icon) is actually a Shaman Totem of Flowing Waters-line effect or a belt-proc buff, unrelated to druid Regrowth — causes healing-UI confusion for druids tracking their own Regrowth — [[d:druid#1448009213849768056]], [[d:druid#1448013879106994287]], [[d:druid#1448014061660012554]].
- **[Cat]** Open Wounds only buffs Claw, not Rake, despite some players expecting it to buff both — [[d:druid#1453687273186001008]].
- **[Balance]** Aviana's Torrent-style "extra Hurricane damage on top" only ticks while the target is actually still standing inside the Hurricane radius, does not persist as a separate DoT once they leave — [[d:druid#1452171700031717458]].
