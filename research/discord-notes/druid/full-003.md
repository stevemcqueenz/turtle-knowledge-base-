## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[bear]** Maul threat modifier reduced 15%, Savage Bite threat modifier reduced 12% in preliminary 1.18.1 notes — [[d:druid#1464375699866386593]] (Threat formula quoted: Threat ≈ (Damage × 2.275) + 320), [[d:druid#1464376020655411391]]. Community math (Tephus/Micro Jackson/Shared) argues the real total-threat impact is likely much smaller than the headline "15%/12%" (a modifier reduction, e.g. 175%→~160%, is only ~7-9% of total threat, not 15%) — [[d:druid#1465647929585827955]], [[d:druid#1465651809103384617]], [[d:druid#1465652762007310459]] (unconfirmed by devs; see Contested).
- **[balance]** Balance of All Things (BOAT) reworked from a cross-school RNG-stacking buff (old: chance-based -0.5s Starfire cast + mana on procs) to a same-school design: "Starfire has a 6% higher chance to crit against targets affected by Moonfire" — dev-stated goal is to reduce randomness — [[d:druid#1464392450255749182]], [[d:druid#1464541190186602538]] (dev comment), [[d:druid#1464546621101572239]], [[d:druid#1464547430497259606]].
- **[balance]** Krokat's sim measured the 1.18.1 BOAT rework as a DPS loss: peak DPS dropped from 1549.2 (1.18) to 1454.0 (1.18.1); average ~30 DPS / ~5.5% loss with his gear — [[d:druid#1464358568248737985]], [[d:druid#1464380870935969944]]; some (Endxx🧯) argued real loss is 7-10%+ once idol-swap-GCD change is included — [[d:druid#1464379088058847435]] (disputed by Krokat [[d:druid#1464379267868655666]]).
- **[balance]** After community pushback, devs posted a revised BOAT design later in this chunk: reduced from 5 to 3 points, "Wrath vs a target with Insect Swarm returns 10/20/30% of its mana cost; Starfire has a 0.2/0.4/0.6s reduced cast time against targets with Moonfire" (reverting toward the old cast-time-reduction model) — [[d:druid#1465349903348924598]], dev acknowledgement of the change process [[d:druid#1465350881586319492]].
- **[cat]** Idol/Totem/Libram swapping in combat now properly incurs a GCD, ending pre-patch "idol dancing" (e.g. swap to Idol of Laceration right before a finisher) — [[d:druid#1464357986809024593]], [[d:druid#1465090540801491074]].
- **[all]** Devs stated plainly there was "no time to look at Cats" this patch and "not much time to change Eclipse" either — cat received no buffs beyond incidental effects of the idol-swap nerf — [[d:druid#1465407973789663324]], [[d:druid#1465408046602649763]].
- **[paladin, context for resto]** Holy Paladin T3.5 5-set nerfed hard: "No longer adds additional affected targets to Holy Strike," cutting the set's healing bonus from +265% to +45% (~83% reduction) — relevant because it repositions Resto Druid as a top healer again — [[d:druid#1465682266171768934]].
- **[pvp-server impact]** Horde-only Tel'Abim guilds say the bear threat nerf hits them far harder since Horde lacks Paladin Salvation; multiple bear tanks (Holydruid, Wawds, Viertakt) argue the change should be faction-specific — [[d:druid#1464439603879477311]], [[d:druid#1465370852399775947]], [[d:druid#1465379303742247014]], [[d:druid#1465379400358035540]].
- **[general sentiment]** Split reaction to the whole patch: some (Nost, Tephus) say bear genuinely needed a threat nerf since it was the best-threat/near-best-tankiness tank with little downside; others (MARO, Holydruid, FANGOdruid, Micro Jackson) call it an unwarranted nerf with zero compensation, especially combined with Warrior/Paladin threat buffs in the same patch — [[d:druid#1464357440966623344]], [[d:druid#1464998052837457982]], [[d:druid#1464372085760721048]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[feral]** Cat DPS: strong, consistent single-target damage but zero AoE ability; described as the community's biggest complaint, making Cat a hard sell for speed-focused raid comps versus Rogue/Warrior — [[d:druid#1464369103073710151]], [[d:druid#1464373007177879717]].
- **[balance]** Boomkin consistently rated "middle of the pack" among caster DPS; pre-1.18.1 estimated ~8th of 9 caster specs; Habu's actual K40 log data shows top Balance Druid parses ranking far behind top Mage/Lock/Shadow Priest performers on the same fights — [[d:druid#1464381007183614238]], [[d:druid#1464519110636601521]], [[d:druid#1464519469752782949]], [[d:druid#1464519810833584148]].
- **[bear]** Best single-target threat and among the tankiest tanks (huge HP + armor), easiest tank to gear/play, flexes cleanly to Cat DPS — but worst AoE threat and has essentially no reactive defensive "buttons" compared to Warrior/Paladin — [[d:druid#1460101834881634607]], [[d:druid#1461011436540264468]], [[d:druid#1464998052837457982]].
- **[resto]** High-skill-ceiling healer, "we're #1" claims common (with caveats about effective-healing vs raw numbers); with the Paladin Holy Strike set nerf, seen as regaining top-healer status — [[d:druid#1464410537327722711]], [[d:druid#1464410732266258605]], [[d:druid#1465111743070994594]].
- **[general]** "Every spec is viable" for leveling/dungeons per community consensus — [[d:druid#1462049691331199049]].

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[feral]** "Normal" raid cat build per Micro Jackson (screenshot): **11 / 35 / 5** (Balance / Feral / Resto) at level 60, with a single 5/5 Resto talent maxed and heavy Feral Combat investment (multiple 5/5 and 3/3 capstone-adjacent talents); noted that in raids Wisdom on the target means mana isn't a concern — [[d:druid#1460992604417032421]] (image), [[d:druid#1460992734335602801]].
- **[feral]** Raid talent choice: Ferocity 4/5 + Feral Aggression 5/5 preferred over maxing Ferocity, since raiding is mostly Shred (Claw rare) and Ferocious Bite has very high usage — Feral Aggression's FB damage boost beats Ferocity's Claw energy discount — [[d:druid#1458706720804307136]].
- **[feral]** Open Wounds treated as "mostly a leveling talent" for raid Cat; occasional forced Claw usage in raid is an acceptable, small DPS loss — [[d:druid#1460028294341722304]].
- **[feral]** Rake's short duration (9s / 3 ticks) criticized as too tight to reliably reach 5 combo points before refresh; suggested buff (longer duration) never implemented — [[d:druid#1460032227537719336]].
- **[feral]** Post-1.18.1 threat nerf, Feral Instinct becomes a "must have" talent for Bear (previously often skipped) to offset AoE aggro loss — [[d:druid#1464363900014170358]].
- **[balance]** Suggested Moonkin raid split ~35/0/13 (Balance/-/Feral, i.e. deep Balance with some Feral) referenced in passing — [[d:druid#1462064236820824206]].
- **[resto]** Three distinct raid archetypes in active use, no consensus "best": (1) Tree of Life build — stay shifted, spam down-ranked Rejuv/Regrowth (cannot cast Healing Touch while in Tree); (2) NG/Regrowth build — does not use Tree, leans on Nature's Grace + fast Regrowth casts; (3) Healing Touch (HT) build — also skips Tree, built around Aessinas Bloom talents for fast Healing Touch — [[d:druid#1463955073251020801]], [[d:druid#1464323673933742112]], [[d:druid#1461869876892405975]].
- **[resto leveling]** Recommended leveling route: spec into Balance first for Omen of Clarity + Moonglow + Nature's Reach, then build out Resto — full Resto from the start only recommended for dedicated dungeon-spam leveling — [[d:druid#1459591610831605995]], [[d:druid#1463955244978409604]], [[d:druid#1463955553108627725]].

## Leveling
- **[feral]** Feral is the fastest/most recommended leveling spec — near-zero downtime, good damage, movement speed — [[d:druid#1460978795996647568]], [[d:druid#1465324871235014707]].
- **[feral]** Standard early order debated: Feral Swiftness first for movement, then respec to Omen of Clarity once Travel Form (level 30) is available; others argue Omen's DPS/utility gain outweighs losing early speed and should be taken as soon as possible (level ~20-24) — [[d:druid#1459253630195601529]], [[d:druid#1459271993215025203]] vs [[d:druid#1459275139970765011]], [[d:druid#1460962482121216180]], [[d:druid#1460989025258180702]] ("omen is very good on this server").
- **[feral]** Furor rated lowest-leveling priority / pure QoL (energy on shift) — only really shines with Wolfshead Helm; recommended to grab last — [[d:druid#1459271720753037356]], [[d:druid#1459272445130313900]], [[d:druid#1462987799157276828]].
- **[feral]** Furor + Wolfshead Helm + Reshift returns 60 energy per shift — [[d:druid#1459798058324725914]].
- **[healer leveling]** For a duo with a non-druid, level Feral until 30 (Travel Form) to keep pace movement-wise; no healing talents needed to heal low-level dungeons, just Int/Spirit gear — switch spec after 30 — [[d:druid#1460604881491722324]].
- **[balance leveling]** Mana generally not a big issue while leveling as Balance; playable start-to-60 without constant OOM (slower than Feral but workable with Int/Spirit gear) — [[d:druid#1459690285956333746]], [[d:druid#1466032954370166844]].
- **[balance leveling]** Sylvan Blessing talent (verified via screenshot tooltip, rank 2/2): "Gives you a 100% chance after killing a target that yields experience or honor to allow your Mana to regenerate at 100% rate while casting. Lasts 15 sec" (rank 1 = 50%) — useful while questing/soloing for landing killing blows with Moonfire, less useful for a dedicated healer since it requires the killing blow itself — [[d:druid#1466032796601417749]] (image), [[d:druid#1463960947730288808]], [[d:druid#1465410475616370838]].
- **[feral leveling]** Weapon progression per Micro Jackson: Smites Mighty Hammer (lvl18) → Manual Crowd Pummeler (lvl28, since nerfed/"useless," see Bugs) → Thornroot Maul (lvl32) → Grimlock's Charge (lvl42) → Ice Barbed Spear (lvl51) → follow the BiS sheet at 60 — [[d:druid#1465070333357850765]]. First boss mace in Crescent Grove (Ashenvale) also called out as an early upgrade — [[d:druid#1462516907558047929]].
- **[bear leveling]** Simple toolkit while leveling: Maul + auto-attack on single target, Swipe + tab-target on packs, Faerie Fire (Feral) and Savage Bite added as talents come online; stat priority flexible (any of STR/AGI/STA/AP/haste/crit/defense/dodge "fine" while leveling) — [[d:druid#1462785881978179832]], [[d:druid#1462790992695656545]], [[d:druid#1462791895179853836]].
- **[dungeon leveling]** Black Morass (BM) called "semi-heroic" — fresh-60 tanks/healers will struggle; Hibernate the extra adds in the 3-/4-mob packs (they apply -50% armor / bleed / stun if left alive), pre-pull Barkskin before the bridge, and Hibernate/kite the final boss's adds (final boss has the most armor of any dungeon/raid NPC in the game and is "stupidly overtuned") — [[d:druid#1460287082483421340]], [[d:druid#1460295750545314016]], [[d:druid#1460296478202400810]], [[d:druid#1460298386128502868]], [[d:druid#1460298635903238276]]. Deadmines healable from level 10 fully-enchanted per Micro Jackson, or level 1 per Dreamhaze — [[d:druid#1459162799421784136]], [[d:druid#1459230343793217597]].
- **[duo leveling]** For two druids leveling together: split into one Feral (melee/tank) + one Balance (ranged DPS/healer) to reduce gear overlap and cover more roles; both can also just share the pinned Feral leveling build if preferred — [[d:druid#1460253594824474872]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[feral]** Canonical raid cat DPS macro (SuperCleveRoidMacros): `#showtooltip` / `/cast [nomybuff:"Blood Frenzy"] Tiger's Fury; [nodebuff:"Rip", noimmune:bleed, combo:=5] Rip; [combo:=5] Ferocious Bite; [nodebuff:"Rake", noimmune:bleed] Rake; [behind, mybuff:"Clearcasting"] Shred; Claw` then `/cast [mypower:<32] Reshift` — [[d:druid#1462552241461268501]].
- **[feral]** With 2+ bleeds active, Claw is close to or slightly ahead of Shred (lower energy cost, no behind-target requirement); Shred wins decisively only on bleed-immune targets — [[d:druid#1459359728965648425]], [[d:druid#1459490801263444175]], [[d:druid#1462699726464876852]] (see also Contested — not fully settled).
- **[feral]** Keep both bleeds (Rake + Rip) up for the energy return from ticks; a 5-combo-point Rip is a decent DPS gain over other 5-CP spends — [[d:druid#1459511971023163486]], [[d:druid#1459536446687023217]].
- **[feral]** Pre-1.18.1 idol-swap rotation example (Medvedev): swap to Idol of Laceration before the finisher, Idol of Ferocity while bleeding, Idol of Savagery on bleed-immune targets — [[d:druid#1463105430304718945]].
- **[feral]** Manual powershifting now gives only a marginal DPS gain given Tiger's Fury/bleed energy return; optimal usage is mainly to Reshift right before Tiger's Fury expires (net ~+30 energy from the refresh) rather than constant powershifting — [[d:druid#1463511815764971621]], [[d:druid#1463514119281049640]].
- **[bear]** ST rotation: Maul near-100% uptime (main threat), Savage Bite kept on cooldown as the rage dump (not "feeding" Maul), Swipe spends leftover rage without sacrificing the other two — [[d:druid#1460717569534329089]], [[d:druid#1460718321795334165]], [[d:druid#1460718841431588915]].
- **[balance]** Wrath-spam waiting for Arcane Eclipse (pre-1.18.1: 30% proc/cast), Starfire-spam during Nature Eclipse (50% proc/cast); reapply Insect Swarm near the end of Nature Eclipse if remaining duration < Wrath cast time, so the refreshed dot benefits from the Eclipse damage bonus — [[d:druid#1463303696409559175]], [[d:druid#1463306819836448871]].
- **[resto]** Rank-tuning is common: e.g. rank-1 Rejuv used purely as a personal mana-battery/filler, upranking only as incoming damage increases, to avoid overhealing/wasted mana — [[d:druid#1464292988674117723]], [[d:druid#1464297969422700689]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[feral]** Cat has no AoE ability at all in Turtle WoW; the workaround is to shift into Bear and Swipe — called out repeatedly as the spec's single biggest weakness (no compensating change in 1.18.1) — [[d:druid#1460831352915103879]], [[d:druid#1460865829380952271]], [[d:druid#1464369103073710151]], [[d:druid#1465407973789663324]].
- **[balance]** Hurricane is the primary AoE tool for packs that survive long enough; for fast-dying trash, dots/Hurricane aren't worth casting — just Moonfire/Wrath the pack — [[d:druid#1465406009479659731]], [[d:druid#1465406077695688737]].
- **[bear]** AoE toolkit is Swipe (hits only 3 targets) + tab-targeting + Demoralizing Roar/Thorns idol on pull; widely called weak next to Paladin/Warrior AoE threat; repeated community request for a bigger Cat/Bear AoE tool was explicitly declined this patch due to time constraints — [[d:druid#1464438062347190293]], [[d:druid#1465407973789663324]].

- **[tank general]** Splitting targets 2-3 per tank recommended for multi-tank AoE threat management in 5-/10-mans — [[d:druid#1465725918617145405]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[feral]** Cat conversions (Krokat, per pinned sheet): 1 Str = 2 AP, 1 Agi = 1 AP, 20 Agi = 1% crit — [[d:druid#1463132050713477163]]. Heart of the Wild makes 1 Str effectively ~2.4 AP — [[d:druid#1463134241478021202]]. Rough combined value ~2.6-2.8 per Str/Agi point, Agi weighted slightly higher for the crit gain — [[d:druid#1460344988163379271]], [[d:druid#1460346284228739166]].
- **[feral]** Cat stat priority per SourisFeroce: "8% hit > Agi > Haste > Str > AP" — [[d:druid#1463133012932956366]]; multiple posters (Tephus, Micro Jackson, Erasu) argue classic stat sheets undervalue haste on Turtle because of near-100% Tiger's Fury uptime and a buffed Omen of Clarity proc rate (more autos → more OOC procs); no Cat sim existed yet at the time of this discussion — [[d:druid#1458747043295002767]], [[d:druid#1458852678867357898]], [[d:druid#1458839164660355115]].
- **[feral]** Omen of Clarity proc rate on Turtle WoW is community-perceived as noticeably higher than vanilla's ~2-3.5 PPM ("3x more clearcasts than vanilla" — Blackfox); exact rate never officially confirmed in this chunk — [[d:druid#1459799341358256252]], [[d:druid#1459799978963898491]], [[d:druid#1459820121701748923]].
- **[bear]** Taunt uses spell hit (16% base miss without any spell hit); Turtle bear has many more hit sources than vanilla, so the practical struggle is dropping excess hit rather than reaching the cap — [[d:druid#1463111448652677150]], [[d:druid#1464013456167669915]].
- **[balance]** "BiS" boomkins run ~12-14% hit, not the full cap; hit stops being a real constraint once geared past AQ40 — [[d:druid#1459094412389908603]], [[d:druid#1459096546678411264]], [[d:druid#1465312739978379499]].
- **[general]** No armor cap on Turtle WoW; damage reduction converges toward ~79-80% well past the vanilla "75% cap," so stacking armor to ~22-23k is still worthwhile for bear since scaling isn't linear below 75%. Example: 22k armor vs a level-63 boss ≈ 78% reduction, about 12% more reduction than the old 75% cap (a 3k hit becomes ~2640) — [[d:druid#1461646095770718240]], [[d:druid#1463165077136998551]], [[d:druid#1463168091608449078]], [[d:druid#1463168783484059688]].
- **[balance]** Glove enchant debate (+9 all-spell-power vs +20 Arcane SP vs +1% haste): no consensus, situational; haste valued at roughly 12 SP-equivalent (Breaddy); after the 1.18.1 BOAT rework favoring Arcane, sentiment shifted toward +20 Arcane SP over haste — [[d:druid#1463430145443627184]], [[d:druid#1463492817958600867]], [[d:druid#1464356948051890398]], [[d:druid#1464362802281054250]].
- **[balance]** Crit is unusually valuable for Boomkin pre-1.18.1 because Eclipse's damage bonus scales with crit% ("crit is way more SP for boomies, cause of the Eclipse changes" — Heisenberg); Hurricane cannot crit, so raw SP benefits it more directly — [[d:druid#1463640416372129947]], [[d:druid#1463641025959428200]], [[d:druid#1463641423113879705]].

- **[feral/general]** Reshift (Turtle-specific spell): 1-click shift out and back into the current form; used to trigger Furor's energy return, escape movement-impairing effects, or avoid getting stuck in caster form — [[d:druid#1459797275575587023]], [[d:druid#1459797417116569682]], [[d:druid#1459797519952248884]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[bear]** Core rotation: Maul near-100% uptime as main threat, Savage Bite kept on cooldown as the rage dump, Swipe spends leftover rage; Faerie Fire (Feral) for opener threat, Demoralizing Roar for AoE assist with a Thorns idol — [[d:druid#1460717569534329089]], [[d:druid#1460718321795334165]], [[d:druid#1460718841431588915]], [[d:druid#1461891199056220181]], [[d:druid#1461897391606333636]].
- **[bear]** Mid-fight reshifting (Bear→Cat→Bear) for a "free" Swipe/Maul when rage-starved is accepted practice among experienced tanks, timed around the swing timer/GCD; risky if pushed too hard since it costs a GCD of Maul uptime — [[d:druid#1462331229876654110]], [[d:druid#1462367445959577820]], [[d:druid#1462441542953210112]], [[d:druid#1462508026916241440]].
- **[bear]** Black Morass tactics: Hibernate the extra adds in 3-/4-mob packs (they apply -50% armor / bleed / stun if left alive), Barkskin pre-pull before the bridge, dungeon is "semi-heroic" for fresh 60s, and the final boss carries more armor than any other dungeon/raid NPC in the game — [[d:druid#1460287082483421340]], [[d:druid#1460295750545314016]], [[d:druid#1460296478202400810]], [[d:druid#1460297644495737067]], [[d:druid#1460298386128502868]], [[d:druid#1460298635903238276]].
- **[bear]** Incantagos pull (custom encounter): pre-HoT, Faerie Fire/Demo Roar on pull, Mighty Rage Potion, spam Demo Roar until the first two "leyguy" adds die; don't let Warriors Bloodrage/Demo Shout before the tank has aggro; taunt after AoE taunt if the healer pulls threat — [[d:druid#1462004193492467736]], [[d:druid#1462017835705307148]], [[d:druid#1462017946288263239]], [[d:druid#1462018034129567886]].
- **[bear]** Defensive CD comparison: "Barkskin every minute, feral barkskin every 10 minutes" — regarded as a weak defensive kit relative to other tanks — [[d:druid#1465653427081187378]].
- **[tank]** 4 Horsemen (Naxx): tanks need spell hit for reliable Taunt (16% base miss without any spell hit); Nat Pagle's Broken Reel is recommended specifically for this fight — [[d:druid#1464013456167669915]], [[d:druid#1464013524178309190]].
- **[bear]** Bear preferred over Warrior/Paladin as pure single-target tank ("I'd rather have a bear tank on Patchwerk then warrior and especially pallies") — [[d:druid#1461013775359672434]].
- **[bear, Tel'Abim]** A Horde/Tel'Abim maintank reports needing to gear purely for threat and "griefing my healers" on fights like Sapphiron/Twin Emperors just to keep DPS from pulling aggro, due to lacking Salvation — [[d:druid#1464569669573808219]].
