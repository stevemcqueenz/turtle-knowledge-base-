## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** MotW, Gift of the Wild, and Rebirth are now castable in Moonkin Form as of the "Tower of Karazhan" patch; dev says preserving this is because Rebirth is tied to the AQ balance set — [[d:druid#1349118346225782805]], [[d:druid#1350057620215431168]] (+19), [[d:druid#1360291200334041228]] (dev-adjacent, confirms MotW change required a fight to get in — [[d:druid#1360340268108349641]]).
- **[General]** A form-lock/immunity effect ("This effect cannot be resisted anymore") changes coming with Tower of Karazhan — [[d:druid#1350501800494501990]].
- **[Feral]** Shapeshifting rework: forms will be castable while already shapeshifted (removing the old 2-press cancel-then-cast requirement) — dev quote: "we're enabling the function to change forms while already being shapeshifted... more or less possible through macro play but we want to level the playing field" — [[d:druid#1356063468469489856]]. As of message date this was still WIP/being investigated — [[d:druid#1356076697929257072]] ("we are still looking into this").
- **[Feral cat]** Major Ancient Brutality/Open Wounds buff patch (~2025-03-31/04-01): bleeds go from 20 to 40 energy per tick, making the bleed ("Open Wounds"/"clown cat") build no longer require powershifting and pushing it to outperform Shred — reactions were mixed-to-negative from powershift fans — [[d:druid#1356308606785421495]], [[d:druid#1356070892639359068]]. Disputed/contested: many players (Uglefar, Jamx, Midoko) argued this makes bleeds *mandatory* rather than an alternative to powershifting as originally promised, and that bleed tracking is "awful" in this client — [[d:druid#1357133531486097663]], [[d:druid#1357243921553690674]], [[d:druid#1357261610359459923]]. Counter-argument from Fat Pibble/devs: both bleed and powershift builds have inherent tradeoffs, neither is objectively "harder" — [[d:druid#1357223834998145104]].
- **[Feral]** New Cursive addon update (post-patch) fixes rake/rip tracking to verify actual connection with target so bleeds display correctly — [[d:druid#1357396662712012931]] (github.com/pepopo978/Cursive).
- **[Balance]** T3.5 (Kara40) 5-piece Balance set bonus discussion: reduces Starfire cast time further (stacking with Improved Starfire -0.5s, Balance of All Things -0.8s, Ebb and Flow idol -0.2s, Nature's Grace proc -0.5s); some report diminishing/weird interactions when haste stacks too high with an already-short cast — unconfirmed, no hard data given — [[d:druid#1356628898707800205]].
- **[Feral]** T3.5 (Kara40) gear widely criticized by cat theorycrafters: helm "OK", shoulders only good if you need hit, chest is a sidegrade/downgrade from 2 raids prior, pants called "an abomination, a crime against humanity", boots small upgrade, amulet moderate upgrade — [[d:druid#1364565349546197032]] (Shardphoenix).
- **[General]** Resilience stat reworked but didn't meaningfully change PvP viability of PvE gear vs PvP gear due to large base stat gap — single source, unconfirmed — [[d:druid#1348216804203298846]].
- **[Feral]** "Reshift" ability/mechanic added, making powershifting much easier/faster to execute; described as removing "any excuse to discard shred" — [[d:druid#1406912477232631898]] (Ivina).
- **[Feral]** Post-patch (~1.18) shift-into-form baseline effect grants 20 energy/shift for Cat and 5 rage/shift for Bear; combined with Furor 5/5 (40 cat / 10 bear) this totals 60 energy (cat) / 15 rage (bear) per shift — [[d:druid#1407849899789127772]] (Holidae).
- **[Feral]** Tiger's Fury reworked in 1.18 to also regenerate energy over time when talented (~10 energy every 3 seconds), making it energy-positive — [[d:druid#1403075475584061550]] (orcandpalz).
- **[Feral]** Rip reworked: duration now scales with combo points spent (instead of a flat 12s), dealing "up to 50% more damage per cast"; damage/AP-per-tick unchanged; also confirmed to refresh on Ferocious Bite and to grant ~5 energy/tick — [[d:druid#1398203775621267479]] (dev changelog quote), [[d:druid#1398213390547026010]] (Micro Jackson).
- **[Balance]** Eclipse fully reworked (dev changelog, quoted verbatim): "Damage from Wrath has a 30% chance to grant Arcane Eclipse... Damage from Starfire has a 50% chance to grant Natural Eclipse... The damage bonus granted is 10% plus an additional 60% of your critical spell strike chance. Each effect lasts 15 sec and has its own 30 sec cooldown." This replaces the old crit-triggered eclipse — [[d:druid#1403828822347878630]] (BalCrix, quoting patch preview). Reaction split: Treehide/Verrik calculates this as a net buff (~34%+ eclipse uptime with good crit) though feels the Astral/Nature Boon nerf (0.75→0.5 multiplier) is too harsh — [[d:druid#1399142910485594194]]; Arnoldgurke and others call the low flat Wrath proc chance (30%) a "casino" feeling with "no scaling for the procchance" — [[d:druid#1398160746197815347]], countered by dev Fat Pibble explaining Wrath's shorter cast time justifies the lower base chance — [[d:druid#1398162500947546264]].
- **[Resto]** Rejuvenation and Regrowth heal-tick timing changed (dev quote): "we've reduced the time needed for them to heal, making them heal less but more frequently" to help multiple healers overlap without wasted ticks — [[d:druid#1398203775621267479]]. Hidden accompanying changes per Micro Jackson: the +10% healing talent now scales with healing power, and a bug where Tree of Life form granted ~+100 flat healing power on top of the Spirit conversion was fixed (net buff or nerf depending on the druid's healing power) — [[d:druid#1407247748352442461]].
- **[Resto]** Aessina's Bloom (currently a 60% chance for Healing Touch to trigger bonus healing) is confirmed changing in 1.18 to "be less casino," moving toward reduced Healing Touch cast time + mana refund instead of a random proc — [[d:druid#1395317541424861314]] (dev), discussed further as disappointing by TesseN who felt it removed the only interesting part of the HT talent line — [[d:druid#1401958555581546631]].
- **[Feral]** Weapon Skill formula overhauled server-wide (affects feral threat/hit): extra weapon skill now scales linearly (no more awkward breakpoints); new quest-granted skill books (+5 to specific weapon types) added; fighting mobs 3+ levels higher no longer applies the old 1% hit suppression, so the effective hit cap vs. such targets moved from 9% to 8% and the old "soft cap" of 305 skill no longer exists; skill 300-304/305-308 smoothed to a flat 0.2% hit / 2% reduced glancing penalty per point; racial weapon-skill bonus reduced from +5 to +3 — [[d:druid#1419789864802582648]] (dak_yo, quoting patch notes).
- **[General]** Same-Form-Shifting (e.g. Cat→Cat) confirmed by dev to become possible by default "on next patch"; until then, using mods/macros that fake it is discouraged to keep a level playing field — [[d:druid#1378737675791503360]] (📌PINNED, Fat Pibble with Air Pods).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Feral tank]** Bear is considered very strong / "amazing" and in high demand for groups; feral cat can do good/fun DPS; Balance is popular; Resto is "okay" — [[d:druid#1357843205277749348]].
- **[Feral]** For dungeons, "any kind of druid is king"; for raid, cat DPS is "awfully average" — power-shifting still outDPSes bleed builds (bleeds ~70% of powershift damage for ~4x the tracking effort) — [[d:druid#1349862750687330326]].
- **[Feral tank]** In top speedrunning guilds (Nordanaar, sub-1hr Naxx clears) feral tank, resto druid, and boomkin are all viable/strong; cat "struggles a bit" but can be accepted if parsing well; in the vast majority of guilds any druid spec can raid Kara40 fine — [[d:druid#1368721989501587509]].
- **[Feral tank]** Bear reportedly takes ~2x more damage than other tanks in progression raids, making it weaker for progression but strong (best threat) for farm content — single source — [[d:druid#1369960952954490912]].
- **[Balance]** Widely repeated view that early/fresh-60 Moonkin struggles with mana, improves with gear; comparable OOM risk to a Mage at high end; T3 Moonkin 2-piece gives half an Innervate effect when Innervating someone else — [[d:druid#1361624960111476798]].
- **[Resto]** Regrowth/Natures Grace (NG)/Regrowth build strongly preferred over Moonglow/Tree or pure Healing Touch (HT)/Moonglow for raid healing by an experienced healer who ran both for 6 months / 1.5 years each — Chain Heal/Flash of Light from Shaman/Paladins outheal Rejuvenation ticks so fast that only movement-heavy fights (4 Horsemen, Sapphiron) benefit from HoTs — [[d:druid#1354259733149913238]], [[d:druid#1354262243021164626]], [[d:druid#1359688814599082105]].
- **[Resto]** Pure HT/Moonglow found "underwhelming for anything other than tank healing"; niche builds combining HT/Aessina's Bloom with Regrowth/NG are considered more useful with multiple resto druids in raid — [[d:druid#1354879437551833351]], [[d:druid#1359627357652259006]].
- **[Cat/general]** "generally you can do all content just fine as any druid spec... You are unlikely to be a #1 dps parser with boomie or cat, if 'viable' means 'potential top dps'" — [[d:druid#1351569050945327236]].
- **[General, dev]** Official dev summary of 1.18 design direction per class area: "Resto is still under discussion... Feral we're looking into more itemization benefits and better synergy with our alternative build path for cat, that being bleed focused. Balance I'm personally kind of happy with, there's a few smaller tweaks but overall the design is solid." — [[d:druid#1389246956563333120]] (Jamey/dev).
- **[General]** Per the pinned FAQ: all 4 specs are viable at endgame and get brought to raids; Feral leveling is fastest, Balance leveling is viable, "Leveling as resto sucks" — [[d:druid#1412860092692299806]] (📌PINNED).
- **[Feral]** With good gear, Boomkin/Cat DPS is reported competitive (a guild's Boomkin placed 4th in Kara40 behind 2 warriors and a mage) — [[d:druid#1396801162325524601]] (Micro Jackson); by contrast, Paladins are called "50x better" than druids at open-world mob farming (single source, hyperbolic) — same citation.
- **[Resto]** One critical minority opinion: Druid is "the hardest healer to play" and "doesn't perform well enough to warrant the step in difficulty," calling druids "the worst tank healers" — contested by more positive assessments elsewhere in the chunk (Micro Jackson rates resto highly for proactive HoTs and Tranquility wipe-saves) — [[d:druid#1380877549902495785]] (DrakeReinz) vs [[d:druid#1387712343982608404]] (Micro Jackson).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Recommended leveling path (FishyFish, detailed): 1-20 Balance; 20-40 Balance or Resto (can heal fine pre-40 using Healing Touch r4 + Moonglow + Rejuv without needing dedicated healing gear); 40+ pick a main spec, gear it, keep an offspec — talent links: 1-20 Balance (entangle to solo elites): https://talents.turtle-wow.org/druid?points=BgAaAIAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA= ; 20-40 Feral (front-shred cat leveling, or bear for fast dungeons): https://talents.turtle-wow.org/druid?points=BgAaAIAAAAAAAAFAYYBYDCAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA= ; after 40 get 5pt Furor (resto tree), then Open Wounds/Blood Frenzy/Improved Shred/Feral Adrenaline/Savage Fury/Primal Fury, close with Heart of the Wild > Leader of the Pack, then Ancient Brutality — [[d:druid#1367313040151679067]].
- **[Feral]** Fastest leveling build order debated: some say Feral Swiftness first then respec for Travel Form; alternative: Omen (balance) first, then feral talents "whatever sounds cool", then Furor (resto) — [[d:druid#1368541413419122849]] (Lyrazia).
- **[Feral]** Alternative leveling talent order: Faerie Fire capstone first vs. grabbing Omen of Clarity earlier — recommendation is Omen earlier for throughput/fun, early FS makes level 20-30 faster with a defensive option; suggested order: Feral → FS → Omen → rest of Feral, with Furor worked in mid-tree — [[d:druid#1361501922619822191]] (Chilela).
- **[Feral tank]** Leveling weapon progression for threat: Smite's Mighty Hammer > Crowd Pummeler > Grimlok's Charge (Crowd Pummeler only a small +6 attribute increase; Grimlok's Charge a bigger jump, 15→21→40 attributes from ~level 20→30→40); no good level ~50 replacement found by poster — [[d:druid#1361669915102285925]] (Micro Jackson). By 2025-04-28, "MCP (Manual Crowd Pummeler) is dead on Twow" per another poster, though as of 04-28 some still ask about it — [[d:druid#1366457310972612729]] (Inovatu), [[d:druid#1366146952965918790]] (contested/dated info — question implies some still use it).
- **[Balance]** Leveling as Boomkin is viable with careful mana management (mana pots liberally, "thwack in melee between spells" with Natural Weapons + Omen of Clarity to recover mana from Spirit while in combat, cast/melee/cast/melee rotation using Starfire's long cast time) — [[d:druid#1361762103790407981]] (Lyrazia quoting a guide), [[d:druid#1361726612512571492]] (Hotverdomme-Nord: heal "naked with no talents" using mana pots 1-60; feral is faster but Boomkin viable and fun; forms give int/spellcost benefit from level 40).
- **[Feral]** Feral leveling notes: bear tanking is strong even while leveling — armor/HP from cat/bear leather gear alone can exceed a "classic tank" at similar level (example: lvl 29 druid at 2100 armor / 1900 HP in bear form using agi/str cat gear from lvl 20-29) with no threat problems in dungeons — single source — [[d:druid#1371536857732481185]] (Sandrea, lvl 30 feral).
- **[Resto]** Leveling as pure Resto is discouraged for solo play/questing ("rather painful... if you ever need to solo"); recommended to level Balance or Feral instead and carry a healing set for dungeons — [[d:druid#1360279143190761603]] (Dreamwalker).
- **[Resto]** Early leveling Resto (~level 24) feels weak on HoTs; player found themselves relying on Healing Touch more than expected, with more mana issues than leveling as a Shaman healer — [[d:druid#1372352930115489832]] (ShoddyBox; question unanswered in this range whether that's normal or gear-dependent).
- **[Feral]** Milestone leveling info: Bear Form unlocks at level 10, Cat Form at level 20, Moonkin Form earliest at level 30 — [[d:druid#1393828253470298165]] (def).
- **[Balance]** Leveling comparison (Barwat, detailed): Feral gear is easy to find and leveling is fast/tanky (stamina baked into feral leather); Boomkin gear during leveling comes mainly from crafting/dungeon drops and has little stamina (squishier), but Boomkin can burst down mobs fast once geared (100+ spellpower by level 35 in one account) — the main limiting factor is mana/drinking downtime since low-level mages/water are scarce — [[d:druid#1405191151467626497]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Balance/Boomkin]** Turtle Eclipse rotation (repeated/confirmed by multiple posters): keep Insect Swarm/Faerie Fire/Moonfire up; if Arcane Eclipse active, spam Starfire; if Nature Eclipse active, spam Wrath; on Eclipse ending (Solstice debuff/lockout), keep spamming the same filler you were already using rather than switching — [[d:druid#1349331032729456691]] (Arcomage, detailed), [[d:druid#1352245581405225022]] (Arcomage, confirms), [[d:druid#1363953088377127036]] (MARO, question form later broadly confirmed).
- **[Balance/Boomkin]** DoTs (Moonfire/Insect Swarm) should be refreshed on the last global of an eclipse, then the other dot, then fish for next eclipse; DoTs snapshot buffs — even reapplying at 14.5s of a 15s damage-buff window still gives the full duration of the buff — [[d:druid#1369281047245750374]] (Treehide/Verrik). Trinket procs should be popped on the first crit of a burn window — [[d:druid#1369235779762655325]] (Micro Jackson).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Feral]** Core energy mechanic explained: energy ticks 20 every 2 seconds; Furor (talent) plus a head enchant together used to grant 60 energy when shifting into Cat Form for the cost of 1 GCD, so the optimal play was to hit 0 energy and shift out/back in just before a natural tick to gain 80 energy (60 from the shift + 20 from the tick) rather than only 20 from waiting — [[d:druid#1375132558815264858]] (Lyrazia). Post-1.18 this shift-bonus became a baseline effect (20 energy/shift cat, 5 rage/shift bear) stacking with Furor (see 1.18 changes section) for a 60/15 total.
- **[Feral]** Innervate + weapon-swap technique: effects/heals calculate their stats at the moment of cast and then persist for the full duration, so swapping to a higher-healing-power weapon *before* self-casting Innervate (and back afterward) locks in better numbers; requires two separate macros since a single click can't apply the weapon swap and read the new stat in the same action (you must click one macro to swap+cast, then click again) — [[d:druid#1369554487861051413]], [[d:druid#1369556827938881597]] (Meow).
- **[General]** Druids have no normal battle-resurrection; devs call adding one "very, very, very unlikely" — repeated complaint/request throughout the chunk with no change — [[d:druid#1360291200334041228]] (dev), [[d:druid#1382005489076011178]], [[d:druid#1408056847058866246]] (dev joking about it).
- **[Feral tank]** No threat-drop utility (e.g. a Warrior's Vanish-Charge-style tool) exists for cat DPS; a player requests something like BWL's "Wing Buffet" threat-reduction so cat DPS doesn't have to hold back — not implemented in this chunk — [[d:druid#1408177387224567978]] (Alfras).
- **[Feral tank]** Bear threat is described as scaling in an "exponential" way unlike any other tank (huge % modifiers stacking on Maul from AP, crit, and haste simultaneously), which several posters argue is the actual root cause of the bear-vs-other-tanks threat gap, rather than any single overpowered stat — [[d:druid#1398541174725279815]] (Tephus).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Feral tank]** Two distinct feral-tank talent philosophies described in detail by Midoko: (1) a "main tank" build takes Thick Hide + Brutal Impact instead of Furor (Furor isn't needed for tanking since consumables can now be used without leaving form, and rage can be pooled at the end of a fight for the next pull), while still keeping Open Wounds + Tiger's Fury + Ancient Brutality to cat-DPS on non-bleed-immune trash; (2) an "off-tank / frequent bleed-immune DPS" build instead takes Improved Shred + finishes Natural Shapeshifter, running the classic Shred/Reshift rotation, and needs the Wolfshead enchant instead of the 1% haste head enchant to do the bleed-immune rotation properly — [[d:druid#1411187673644929034]].
- **[Feral tank]** Recommended threat/priority macro set (Sagesse, needs SuperWoW + CleveRoidMacros + ideally Nampower): Maul macro casts Savage Bite instead if rage ≥25 (off cooldown), else Maul rank 7; Swipe macro similarly prefers Savage Bite at ≥25 rage else Swipe rank 5 (with auto-retarget); a mouseover/target Growl macro; a Faerie Fire (Feral)+Demoralizing Roar combo macro (roar only while in Bear Form and not already applied); a Bear-form Tiger's Fury macro that also shifts into Dire Bear Form if not already in it; a form-aware one-button Barkskin macro — [[d:druid#1411769744175923270]], [[d:druid#1411769798215467161]] (also reposted later by Kekkonen at [[d:druid#1418266855226998914]]).
- **[Feral tank]** Furor 5/5 + a 5-rage-per-shift head enchant is called "game changer" for bear tank threat generation even outside of cat DPS — [[d:druid#1411970643209093182]] (Dziadmroz).
- **[Feral tank]** Bear dodge/parry RNG can cause serious threat swings: strings of 5-7 parries in a row when opening on a boss leave a bear "at flat 0" threat while DPS is already "used to zugzugging hard" behind bear's normal threat lead, unlike Paladins (Consecration nearly guaranteed) or Warriors (faster white hits) — [[d:druid#1386681538032439417]] (Midoko).
- **[Feral tank]** "Warrior does like 0 threat compared to bear" — single-source, likely hyperbolic claim reflecting a broader community sentiment that bear threat is currently far ahead of Warrior tanks — [[d:druid#1405302306341257367]] (Shardphoenix).
- **[Feral tank]** Design-critique proposal list for bringing bear more in line (Tephus, "focus on giving weaknesses rather than nerfing strengths"): rework the 8pc T3 set bonus off of a diminishing-returns model (feels excessive without an armor cap), remove Defense from tank gear (and possibly some Dodge) to keep total damage taken high relative to other tanks, and reconsider Barkskin's power level now that the armor cap is gone — [[d:druid#1411977120590200842]].
- **[Feral tank]** Counter-argument on mitigation/armor-cap removal: bear only reaches the mitigation cap in full best-in-slot mitigation gear or under specific buffs ("Inspiration"); the real problem is Protection Warrior's threat generation being too low relative to DPS output, not bear's mitigation being too high — [[d:druid#1411991197186002965]], [[d:druid#1411992653628706846]] (Ivina).
- **[Feral tank]** Feral Adrenaline talent widely considered weak/not worth talent points ("never put talentpoints in feral adrenaline") — [[d:druid#1374610899066945557]] (Inovatu).
- **[Feral tank]** Bears are said to take the *least* net raid damage over a fight despite squishier per-hit numbers, because superior threat lets the raid DPS harder and kill the boss faster, shortening total damage-taken exposure — [[d:druid#1394962514139418685]] (Frood), and are called the best single-target threat machines with an easy path to the armor cap and a large HP pool that makes big crits easy to heal through — [[d:druid#1394962489136910446]] (Dziadmroz).

## Healing specifics
- **[Resto]** Multiple-Resto/"Tree stacking" debate: proponent argument (FlameBRS, detailed) is that Efflorescence gives a healing bonus when HoTs are overwritten, so "wasted" HoTs and overhealing from stacking Trees aren't actually a real downside since each druid does the same thing regardless of stacking — the one point of broad agreement is that running different healer classes/specs together is still generally better than stacking same-spec druids, just not because of any HoT-cannibalization penalty — [[d:druid#1415945584640131152]], discussion continued at [[d:druid#1415967846793482262]] (Blackfox) — unresolved/contested.
- **[Resto]** Three largely separate resto playstyles (Tree/Rejuv spam, NG/Regrowth, HT/Aessina's) each require a different talent build and different gear, with no easy way to switch mid-raid based on the fight — cited as a core design pain point — [[d:druid#1389146046432542752]] (Micro Jackson), [[d:druid#1387712343982608404]] (same author elsewhere calling this "a mess").
- **[Resto]** Push-back protection imbalance: Druids need 5 points (healing pushback) + 3 points (damage pushback) = 8 talent points total to be pushback-immune casting and healing, versus Priests/Shamans/Paladins needing only 2 points to cover both — cited as a design complaint with a suggested fix of combining/moving the talents — [[d:druid#1400055493631672410]] (Micro Jackson).
- **[Resto]** Healing Touch rank-selection guide by situation (Micro Jackson): Rank 3 gives the best mana-per-HP when nothing is critical; Rank 7 is a good middle ground; Rank 11 gives the most HPS but worst heal-per-mana, used with Rejuvenation + Innervate + potions when "lots is going to shit" — [[d:druid#1380928607307956255]].
- **[Resto]** Choice between Resto Druid and Holy Paladin for a new raid healer (detailed comparison, Midoko): both struggle with mana in early raid tiers (druids from lack of Int, paladins from lack of Crit); Paladins are generally more sought-after for raid Blessings and don't create the stacking-penalty concerns multiple Resto Druids can; Resto brings raid-wide Tranquility plus Nature's Swiftness + max-rank Healing Touch for a big instant heal; recommendation ultimately comes down to preferred playstyle (raid-wide HoTs vs. tank healing with more "oh-shit" buttons) — [[d:druid#1404197550243971082]].
- **[Resto]** Player buff-cap (32 visible buff slots) makes HoTs on heavily-buffed raid targets (esp. tanks) invisible to the caster once they're above slot 32, forcing healers to "heal blind" and making Swiftmend unreliable since you can't confirm a HoT is present; partial mitigations discussed include pfui, Chronometer, and a HealersMate fork with SuperWoW-based *predictive* buff tracking (based on detected casts rather than server state, and loses tracking on relog/UI reload) — no full fix exists as of this chunk — [[d:druid#1371523504566173807]] (Suka/Akordea), [[d:druid#1402276976739487837]]/[[d:druid#1402285217636683887]] (Livin), [[d:druid#1415317925564383314]] (Livin again, later).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Balance/Resto hybrid]** Detailed "Rethevus" world-boss/challenge strategy (likely the Swift Travel Form "Dreamer's Challenge" boss, MARO): don't use DoTs or Thorns — they break CC and make the fight "impossible as Feral," so a hybrid Balance-Resto build is recommended; open with Starfire from a LoS position, immediately Hibernate the Emerald Retainer add, then Entangling Roots the boss and retreat to your LoS spot; keep CC up at all costs (refresh Roots then re-Hibernate); Moonfire the periodic "darter" adds for a 30% damage buff if it won't cost you CC/position; only damage Rethevus during his ~10-second Sleep Phase (about 5 Wraths + a Roots cast); recommended consumables: Brilliant Mana Oil, Dreamshard Elixir, Mageblood Potions, a pre-potted Greater Nature Resistance Potion, and a Flask of the Titans if affordable — [[d:druid#1406570869806993478]].
- **[Balance/Resto hybrid]** Fuller step-by-step version of the same fight (Midoko): start left of the spawn point, Barkskin+Regrowth yourself while it spawns, sleep the Retainer and root the dragon (sleep is priority since the Retainer makes the dragon CC-immune), retreat to stone rubble south, Abolish Poison immediately (drains mana fast) and Remove Curse before it stuns, stand on top of the "Darter" whelps as they die for an AoE health/mana regen buff, re-sleep/re-root after each darter, wait ~6-8 seconds into the boss's periodic Enrage (it takes 30% increased damage during Enrage, then falls asleep), pre-cast Starfire to land as it falls asleep (fishing for a 50% Nature Eclipse chance) then spam ~4 Wraths, and re-root/re-sleep before it wakes — never DoT the boss directly, as damage-over-time can break the root — [[d:druid#1406589702651379805]], expanded further at [[d:druid#1412024751932833962]].
- **[Feral tank]** Alternate/tankier execution of the same encounter using a Bear tank (Livin): stack ~10% lifesteal ("vampirism"), take the Carnage talent, and stockpile ~60 Elixir of Poison Resistance to spam on the poison debuff (which "drastically reduces armor"); tank at the furthest tree from spawn so the Retainer/darters never reach you; heal up and return to Bear when the boss sleeps; cleanse the poison with Abolish (or more elixirs) when it's safe — [[d:druid#1407500471509319833]], [[d:druid#1407503196867530773]].
- **[Balance/Resto hybrid]** Confirmed successful clear using a custom 24/0/27 hybrid spec (Guidance of the Dream + Swiftmend as key talents) with ~420 spellpower/400 bonus-healing/3500 HP; fight took about 10 minutes using the standard sleep/root/wrath-during-sleep strategy above — [[d:druid#1415319581186330736]] (Koalafurieux).
- **[Balance]** Kara40 raid-wide-damage assessment used to argue for Boomkin's raid value (Treehide/Verrik): Gnarlmoon is good for Tree/HoTs; Incantagos has heavy raid-wide damage from blizzards; Anomalus fight is described as "giga" (favorable for raid-wide healing/damage kits); depending on Medivh strategy there's room for a well-timed Rejuvenation spike; the Chess event has shadow bolts and other raid-wide sources; Rupturan Heart has many simultaneous damage-taking targets; only "Sanv" and Mephistroth are called less raid-wide-damage-favored fights — [[d:druid#1379865239633395823]].
- **[Feral tank]** Bear tanks commonly fill off-tank (OT) roles in Naxxramas (e.g. Four Horsemen, which needs at least 7 total tanks) because feral gear is comparatively easy to acquire and a druid can flex between tank/DPS gear sets — [[d:druid#1404825931893313568]] (JJPasak).
- **[Boomkin]** Kara40's Anomalus fight has a hard mechanic requiring ~225 Arcane Resistance (implied wipe-level requirement), contrasted with AQ40's ~200 Nature Resistance melee requirement, which is a "soft" requirement that just eases healer load (2x Greater Nature Protection Potions can substitute) rather than being mandatory — [[d:druid#1396794024718766162]] (Micro Jackson).
- **[Feral tank]** Kara40 boss-mechanic complaint: a mind-control-afflicted target released at very low HP can then be hit by Volley or a Fragment+Charge combo and instakilled; suggested fix is to make MC-debuffed targets invalid Charge targets — [[d:druid#1371960415600513096]] (Hotverdomme-Nord).

## PvP
- **[Feral]** Detailed (single-source, framed as a question — "is this true?") account of Feral PvP decline since CC2: pre-CC2, Feral was one of the strongest PvP specs (good Cat damage, ok Bear damage while tanky, and a strong Frenzied Regeneration survivability tool); post-changes, Frenzied Regeneration's healing and cooldown were both nerfed "into the ground," Bear damage was nerfed for tanking-balance reasons (hurting PvP as a side effect), and most other classes (especially casters) got large damage buffs while Cat only got "another bleed playstyle which isn't reliable"; Cat is now very vulnerable to stun-into-burst (can die in seconds if stunned and can't reach Bear Form) and struggles hard against Paladins/Shamans; a "flash bomb" (10-sec fear, 1-min CD, doesn't break on damage, usable by "anyone") is called a near-guaranteed kill if the druid's PvP trinket is down; Bear in PvP is now "just a punching bag that does no damage" — no confirming or denying reply was captured in-chunk — [[d:druid#1402317105650733229]] (ricardo, quoting an outside source).
- **[Feral]** Druid PvP identity ("war of attrition" — outlasting opponents with HoTs/DoTs/Innervate/kiting) is said to no longer function well because other specs' power creep (especially Shaman/Paladin hybrids) has outpaced Druid, and Druid received "hybrid tax" PvP nerfs to Frenzied Regeneration and Savage Bite damage "with nothing else to make up for it," while upcoming battleground damage-dampening changes are expected to favor the already-strong hybrids further — [[d:druid#1404693155030368276]], [[d:druid#1404693608614858847]] (Midoko).
- **[Feral]** Druids currently lack Cyclone; a player-run petition/forum post calls for adding it specifically to enable Rated Arena viability — not confirmed added within this chunk — [[d:druid#1385386874322817169]] (Pixiestixie).
- **[Feral]** Guessed (self-flagged as uncertain) Turtle PvP opener based on "regular Classic" Feral PvP builds (Furor-Omen with points into Brutal Impact): Pounce → Rake → Rip, then Bear Form → Bash, staying in Bear if the target trinkets the stun, or swapping to Cat → Berserk to finish the kill depending on matchup — asker explicitly notes they don't know if there's Turtle-specific PvP tech they're missing — [[d:druid#1413608701654339707]] (AlloyMorph, unconfirmed by replies).
