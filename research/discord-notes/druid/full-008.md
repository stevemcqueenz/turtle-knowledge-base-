- **[general]** Alternate: stay feral until level 30 then respec, because the 30% travel speed from Feral Swiftness (available ~lvl 20) is "too good to skip" while leveling — [[d:druid#1491403473856757811]] (poisonouscabbage). Confirmed as "common strat": feral to Feral Swiftness at 20, then respec at 30 for Omen of Clarity once travel form is available — [[d:druid#1491426472177565918]] (Hassle, 2026-04-08).
- **[general]** Hotverdomme-Nord's detailed order: spec **Improved Wrath** first (used at that level), then straight to **Omen of Clarity** — especially if doing dungeons — reaching this by ~level 20. If not dungeoning, easier to start with Swiftness instead. An early **Furor** point is also good for threat/dmg/less downtime if you enjoy that playstyle — [[d:druid#1491894828227887204]] (2026-04-09).
- **[general]** "Go furor after getting omen and swiftness" reduces downtime between pulls — [[d:druid#1491891724254056720]] (Hassle, 2026-04-09/10); separately, taking Furor before finishing Feral is liked because it removes energy-starvation downtime — [[d:druid#1491229947518390422]] (Hassle, 2026-04-08).

## Rotation: single target
- **[bear tank]** "Press Maul always, press Swipe if more than 2 mobs or full rage, and press Bite above 50 rage" — [[d:druid#1491404394812407858]] (Micro Jackson, +3, 2026-04-08).
- **[feral cat]** Described as a "melee caster" — autoattacking is secondary; the playstyle is to spam abilities faster than any other spec — [[d:druid#1492012241422254160]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-04-09).
- **[feral cat]** Claw vs. Shred: if the boss is **not** bleed-immune, Claw always out-DPS's Shred when played well; a proc idol that fully energizes after 2 Shred crits isn't considered good except specifically vs. bleed-immune bosses; **Idol of Ferocity remains BiS**. All top 1.18.1 parses use Claw; older logs showing Shred-spam ahead were relying on cheese/bugs — [[d:druid#1492276296808661174]] (Ash, 2026-04-11). Open sub-debate: Europe players reportedly still favor Shred even on non-bleed-immune fights like Patchwerk-type encounters — possibly attributable to a specific idol that rewards two Shred crits before Bite — question raised but not conclusively answered — [[d:druid#1492236789857911026]], [[d:druid#1492244796209631485]] (Maturi, 2026-04-10; unresolved/contested). Simplified community heuristic given later: "if it is nice [i.e., will finish the target] then Bite, else Claw" — [[d:druid#1508522976016859156]] (Moonchase, 2026-05-25; single source, informal).
- **[boomkin]** Detailed single-target rotation (Floccumate, for a guildie): **Insect Swarm > Moonfire > Wrath** until Nature eclipse procs, then **Starfire** until eclipse ends, then continue Starfire until the next eclipse, then back to Wrath; keep both DoTs up throughout, refreshing a DoT while its matching eclipse is active for the eclipse damage bonus — [[d:druid#1493622633378484437]] (2026-04-14). Mechanic explanation: Moonfire active on the target boosts Starfire crit chance; Insect Swarm active gives Wrath a mana refund; during an eclipse, having the matching DoT up is a straight damage% increase, so it's usually best to refresh that DoT near the very end of the eclipse window rather than mid-window — [[d:druid#1493631596773769358]], [[d:druid#1493629348941402173]], [[d:druid#1493629589816082634]], [[d:druid#1493629695499960444]], [[d:druid#1493629877566439554]] (Floccumate/Yoshi, 2026-04-14).
- **[boomkin]** Detailed answer on refreshing DoTs across an eclipse boundary (Habu): during **Arcane eclipse**, keep Moonfire up as much as possible — only skip reapplying it if you're near the end of the eclipse and doing so would cost you a Starfire cast; if there's less time left in eclipse than a Starfire cast takes, you can safely reapply Moonfire for its full duration. During **Nature eclipse**, skip reapplying Insect Swarm unless you're at the very end where you wouldn't get another Wrath off anyway; wait to reapply Moonfire until Nature eclipse wears off if it's about to expire mid-eclipse — [[d:druid#1493886936643928205]], [[d:druid#1493888537190334486]] (2026-04-14/15).
- **[boomkin]** Precasting Starfire on the marked/skull target (at pull start, before eclipse is active) is recommended — "well worth it" for the chance at an occasional Nature eclipse proc — [[d:druid#1493635312360886434]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-04-14).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Emerald Sanctum, "Ride of the Wild" quest fight]** Multi-mechanic control encounter (~9 things to track at once). Killing the nearby "faerie"/darter restores HP and mana — stand directly on top of it before killing, one Moonfire should suffice. The poison or curse debuff on the boss is a mana burn and must be cleansed instantly, prioritized over everything else. The boss sleeps after its second breath; roots break within the first ~3 seconds regardless of DoT damage, so re-root up to twice if you're dotting; any DoTs must be applied instantly on sleep. Positioning: use balance's extended spell range to stay outside breath range while remaining in root/nuke range, and stay near a line-of-sight object for the faerie mechanic. A shaman dropping totems or a paladin's auras can help sustain you through it (considered "a bit cheese") — [[d:druid#1491786904578818158]]–[[d:druid#1491795066316914860]] (Micro Jackson/Eepyman/Modwolf, 2026-04-09).

## PvP
- **[feral]** Not a bursty class like rogue or enhancement shaman — better suited to "rotting players down over time" using forms and spells; recommended watching feral PvP videos to get a feel for the intended playstyle — [[d:druid#1491929130952954148]] (Rustam, +3, 2026-04-09).
- **[feral]** Hybrid PvP spec: at 5 combo points on a low-health target, use Ferocious Bite; for a hybrid PvP build you can skip the bleed talents entirely and focus on Shred pressure instead — [[d:druid#1491810030511460517]], [[d:druid#1491810776619421848]] (#1 Shaman Hater/Reynolds, 2026-04-09).
- **[feral]** A rank-8-geared feral in Southsea PvP struggling badly against r13/full-Naxx geared shamans and rogues: general consensus that rogues are currently "busted," shamans have "always" been busted in PvP, and paladin is the only class with a real chance against shamans right now — [[d:druid#1491911624343425247]]–[[d:druid#1491916435977666621]] (Tøxïn/#1 Shaman Hater, 2026-04-09).
- **[boomkin]** PvP is punishing: Starfire's 2.6-2.9s cast time is nearly impossible to land against anyone trying to stop it; boomkin has no dispel protection so priests remove all DoTs in a single GCD (paladins take longer); roots break within the first 3 seconds of taking damage regardless of DoT presence — [[d:druid#1492151406159859824]] (Hotverdomme-Nord, 2026-04-10). Desired fix: an "ebb and flow"-style Starfire buff plus a chance at an instant-cast Starfire (compared to a WotLK warlock talent) to enable entering Nature eclipse in PvP — [[d:druid#1492151427194425354]] (Hotverdomme-Nord).
- **[boomkin]** 1v1 vs. warlock: druids have few tools — Nature's Grasp is only ~35% reliable, Starfire's stun proc is ~15%, and Bash requires melee range which boomkin generally can't get except against a mage after a blink or against another resto druid. Expect a slow fight involving kiting, healing through damage, trying to kill the warlock's pet, and avoiding the deathcoil-fear-silence lock-down cycle — [[d:druid#1494232632307749096]], [[d:druid#1494235468852887553]] (Hotverdomme-Nord, 2026-04-16). Anecdotally, a warlock reportedly could never kill a (presumably feral/hybrid) druid in repeated PvP encounters and complained to GMs about it — [[d:druid#1494248293503340604]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-04-16).
- **[boomkin]** Extended critique of the reworked **Balance of all Things (BoaT)** talent, mostly a PvE/PvP design complaint rather than a numeric tuning one (**contested**, both sides recorded): rutherbird argues the new version (requiring the matching DoT to be up on the exact target being nuked) is worse than the old version in almost every real scenario — farming (rarely worth dotting non-elites before nuking), trash pulls (main kill target usually dies too fast for DoT value), awkward eclipse/DoT timing overlaps, targets about to die, etc. — and calls it worse DPS even in the best-case long boss fight, despite the change being intended partly to reduce buff-slot usage from "boons" — [[d:druid#1493140336657305724]], [[d:druid#1493140439572811856]] (+7 each, 2026-04-14). He suggests reverting BoaT to its pre-patch form (possibly at 3 points instead of 5) while keeping the updated Moonfury/Eclipse proc-rate changes — [[d:druid#1493147645315780639]]. Counter-argument (Bobob): the change was intended to remove an RNG pain point (lining up BoaT procs with the correct eclipse), and buff-slot pressure from consumables wasn't really a practical issue once addons came out — [[d:druid#1493248835806429355]], [[d:druid#1493249035480334527]] (2026-04-13). Hotverdomme-Nord's own detailed breakdown: in PvE, trash-DoT usage is basically unaffected either way; single-target boss usage is "mostly a feel thing" unless heavy movement/fast damage is needed; multi-dottable-target fights lose meaningfully vs. the old system. In PvP, the new Starfire/BoaT is "incredibly garbage" — roots break without needing damage, and most opponents have tools to avoid a slow cast entirely, with no dispel protection to compensate — [[d:druid#1493694239442206781]], [[d:druid#1493695899954839552]] (2026-04-14).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[boomkin]** Moonkin Form has two versions (180% vs. 360% armor); after a respec both can remain on the bars and a simple macro can end up casting the wrong (180%) one. Fix approaches suggested: a `[nomybuff]`/nocastbuff-style Moonkin Form macro to avoid accidentally shifting out and to bias toward the correct form; the Cleverroid addon; nampower's `CastSpellFromName`/`CastSpellFromID` overloads; or `/pfcast Moonkin Form` / "supercleveroid" style macros, which take the highest rank by default — [[d:druid#1491493737711276162]], [[d:druid#1491742529723437187]], [[d:druid#1491758283973853334]], [[d:druid#1491763399338623049]] (Jizzus/Micro Jackson, 2026-04-08/09).
- **[general]** ItemRack addon used to bind full gear-set swaps to a single key (e.g., the Thorns-buffing swap) — [[d:druid#1492057749712801922]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-04-09).
- **[resto]** Cursive addon can recast/track DoTs; raised in response to a request for an addon to track buffs/DoTs (Clearcasting procs, Rake/Rip duration) — [[d:druid#1492229040889794800]], [[d:druid#1492230313458401432]] (Pacyn/Shardphoenix, 2026-04-10). Confirmed Cursive also works for tracking cat bleeds (Rake/Rip) — [[d:druid#1493178717734961172]], [[d:druid#1493179401700118608]] (Eskay Rogue/Micro Jackson, 2026-04-13), though one player reported it not showing up for them and needing to re-enable it — [[d:druid#1493179605019000973]].
- **[resto/HoT tracking]** For showing Rejuvenation/Regrowth remaining time on raid frames: in PfUI, enable it under Unit Frames → Raid → Buff/Debuff Indicators → "Show Time Left" checkbox; getting it to show on **other** players (not just yourself) may additionally require SuperWoW — [[d:druid#1492828325024895038]]–[[d:druid#1492834230927884419]] (Vepter/Puffin/Micro Jackson, 2026-04-12). Alternative: the Puppeteer addon (with supporting addons) also shows Regrowth/Rejuvenation on frames — [[d:druid#1493074616527229089]] (Maat, 2026-04-13).
- **[general]** DoiteAuras (a "Vanilla WeakAuras Light" addon, https://github.com/Player-Doite/DoiteAuras) was released mid-chunk, adding buff/debuff tracking beyond the game's normal visible icon cap, for both player and target — [[d:druid#1492473696462573649]] (Doite, 2026-04-11).
