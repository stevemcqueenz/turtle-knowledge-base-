## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Balance]** Post-patch Kara40 Oomkin (Moonkin) DPS dropped ~19% raid-wide (top 25: 1240→1000 DPS) and ~14% on Medivh specifically (1754→1501 DPS), per parse comparison — [[d:druid#1486638522327633982]] (Krokat, 2026-03-26; disputed as possibly incomplete data by Krokat himself, [[d:druid#1486640477020098591]]).
- **[Balance]** Boat (stacking Balance of All Things proc) mechanic change makes it much harder to build stacks: now can take 4 Wraths to get a Lunar proc, causing 8-second Moonfire timing conflicts with Eclipse windows — [[d:druid#1486646119898284043]] (Jizzus, +7 reactions, 2026-03-26).
- **[Balance]** Balance of All Things (BoAT) talent point cost reduced 5→3, freeing 2 extra talent points — [[d:druid#1487485734779752468]] (Habu, 2026-03-28); [[d:druid#1489736037612912680]] (Nick1192 asking where the 2 freed points go, 2026-03-29).
- **[Bear]** Bear tank got nerfed: lost its main advantage (high snap threat), while downsides (poor defensive CDs, ~2x more damage taken than other tanks) remain — [[d:druid#1486520160226381896]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-26). Paladins got buffed consecration snap threat + a new libram, making Holy Strike+Judgement+Consecration guarantee good pull threat; Bear only takes over after ~10+ sec — [[d:druid#1486521085968257054]], [[d:druid#1486521547043770399]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-26). Paladin snap threat reported ~10k in BiS — [[d:druid#1486532291550253256]] (𝓜𝓲𝓭𝓸𝓴𝓸, 2026-03-26).
- **[Feral/Moonfang idol]** Idol of the Moonfang (crab idol precursor) was nerfed intentionally "so people use more different idols" — result: went from one clearly-BiS idol to a different, weaker BiS idol — [[d:druid#1487109337536139375]], [[d:druid#1487109494180679830]] (Holydruid, 2026-03-27).
- **[Resto]** T3.5 5-set (Blooming Bud / faster Swiftmend) nerfed — Blooming Bud linger reduced to 20s, mostly only benefits tanks now (~240 healing per proc), considered close to worthless — [[d:druid#1486837376969085079]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-27), detailed writeup with rework suggestions by akitoe [[d:druid#1487502017315344455]] (2026-03-28).
- **[Balance]** Idol of Acidity scaling confirmed via testing: total damage = 300 + ~5.5% of total SP, no ICD; still worse than Idol of the Moon — [[d:druid#1487152970276798574]], [[d:druid#1487155459503820951]] (Krokat/Micro Jackson, 2026-03-27).
- **[Balance]** New T3.5 (Timbermaw Hold) set bonus for Hurricane damage bug: tooltip claims 50% total damage bonus but measured formula suggests ~40% (half-strength Hurricane cast, no talents factored); official bug report filed — [[d:druid#1487478176723632271]] (Krokat, 2026-03-28), formula: `Damage = (134+(9.6%*(SP+NP)))*(1+Moonfury[0.12]+Genesis[0.15])*EclipseMulti`; bugged version omits Moonfury/Genesis — [[d:druid#1487511320126427146]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-28); bug link [[d:druid#1487502399475290262]].
- **[General]** Two new Balance idols added this patch are considered underwhelming, weaker than existing Ebb & Flow / Idol of the Moon — [[d:druid#1486662739354189874]] (Krokat), [[d:druid#1487013351082164355]] (Spynello: only ~60 boomkins raided BWL on Ambershire per logs check, 2026-03-28).
- **[Bugs]** Glyph of the Moon (moonkin form cosmetic) bugged post-1.18.1: random race/gender combos (male nelf/female tauren/male tauren body with female nelf skin/underwear) roll every server restart; temp fix via barber chair lost on next shapeshift — [[d:druid#1486560561817714760]], [[d:druid#1486572456461205634]], [[d:druid#1486572780194369606]], [[d:druid#1486573126941933578]] (Yoshi/Tempted, 2026-03-26).
- **[Balance]** New Timbermaw Hold (TMH) Balance idols (2 added) confirmed underwhelming by devs' own scaling data: Krokat's tested/updated per-idol average bonus damage on non-immune targets: Ebb & Flow +45, Idol of the Moon +16, Idol of Equilibrium +13, Idol of Acidity +9.6 — Ebb & Flow remains far ahead of the two new idols — [[d:druid#1488374219006939207]] (Hotverdomme-Nord relaying Krokat's numbers, 2026-03-31); confirmed via Krokat's own updated sim table (AQ40/Naxx/Kara40 BiS gearsets, 120s fight, fully raid-buffed): No Idol baseline 1158/1300/1440 avg DPS; Idol of Equilibrium +1.1%/+1.1%/+0.9%; Idol of Acidity +0.9%/+0.7%/+1.0%; Idol of the Moon +1.3%/+1.3%/+1.2%; Idol of Ebb and Flow +2.9%/+3.4%/+3.3% (best in every tier) — [[d:druid#1488415746592604160]] (Krokat, image, 2026-03-31).
- **[Balance]** Idol swapping mid-combat via macro/script now triggers an internal ~30s cooldown; manual mouse-click swaps have no special ICD but still cost a normal GCD — a real (if small) Boomkin rotation cost that doesn't matter for Cat, which is energy-capped rather than GCD-capped — [[d:druid#1488420223047696424]], [[d:druid#1488420358183715026]], [[d:druid#1488420793691017227]], [[d:druid#1488424928037306472]] (Krokat/Micro Jackson, 2026-03-31).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Bear]** After patch, bear remains best sustained/prolonged single-target boss threat, but no longer best snap/pull threat (now behind Paladin, and Warrior favored for survivability by casual guilds) — [[d:druid#1486520900428894218]], [[d:druid#1486522142169370845]] (Holydruid/Shardphoenix, 2026-03-26). Only "horde tryhards" get value from bear as pull-threat niche — [[d:druid#1486522142169370845]].
- **[Cat/Feral DPS]** Feral cat is "OK dps until AQ40, then falls off HARD" — little new gear in Naxx, Kara40 gear designed poorly for cats (barely better than AQ40, worse than what other classes get); cat is dead-last DPS in Kara40 per Shardphoenix — [[d:druid#1486520160226381896]] (2026-03-26); disputed by Mot (own guild topping meters as cat in Naxx) [[d:druid#1486539188689047633]], and by Tephus (fine for single-target, weak AoE, A-tier claim) [[d:druid#1486705858393018548]]; Ivina: cat has a "sweet spot" during AQ/Naxx progression with T2.5, dies off after, T3.5 "barely better than T2.5" — [[d:druid#1486713655675584542]] (+3).
- **[Cat]** Cat has 52 points of mandatory DPS talents (limited build flexibility) — [[d:druid#1486707721947447397]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-26).
- **[Cat/Feral]** Feral is the only DPS spec that doesn't want to replace Atiesh even in Kara40 — [[d:druid#1486729477026349248]] (Shardphoenix); Atiesh confirmed BiS weapon for all specs — [[d:druid#1487245474904670319]], [[d:druid#1487249365067370508]] (Shardphoenix/FlameBRS, 2026-03-27/28).
- **[Balance]** Boomkin widely called worst/near-worst DPS spec in the game this patch, citing top-100 Kara40 logs where boomkins struggle to break top 100-128 even with Scythe+Atiesh; best boomkin log examples: Rupturan #36 at 1368 dps (margin 200 dps from #20), Krull ~#65, Owlboom broke 1600 dps on Krull but still far from top — [[d:druid#1487505087441141941]] (Koalafurieux, 2026-03-28), caster-only comparison capped at #128, "will be a lot worse Monday night" — [[d:druid#1487500775239454960]].
- **[Balance]** Leveling tier consensus: Feral/Balance leveling is A-tier, "right behind hunter and warlock"; considered by some the "most insane class for leveling" — [[d:druid#1487164344348184731]] (Kiffy, +5), [[d:druid#1487165021715697816]] (Shardphoenix: Leveling A-tier).
- **[PvP tier]** PvP overall: B-tier (C on Alliance since Shamans "eat druids for breakfast"); Raiding: bottom DPS in current endgame raid, bear tank no longer worth taking over Paladin/Warrior; Healer: "you want ONE per raid" — [[d:druid#1487165021715697816]], [[d:druid#1487165065902821507]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-28).
- **[Raid gear scaling]** Every other DPS spec gets a big Naxx upgrade then smaller Kara40 upgrade; Cat gets no big Naxx upgrade, and Kara40 gear is only a small upgrade over AQ40 — [[d:druid#1487166215897088161]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-28). MC/BWL/AQ40 fine for feral, Naxx is where it starts falling off, Kara40 "falls off a cliff" — [[d:druid#1487168231113232497]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-28).
- **[Resto]** Resto is "good all end game" but no longer BiS healer — Priest took that spot; Resto Tranquility still does ~100k healing per use — [[d:druid#1487184318668345544]] (Valarya, 2026-03-28). 4 Resto druids in a raid is considered "griefing" (competing for same HoT-refresh windows) — [[d:druid#1487184546595209217]] (Valarya).
- **[Tank]** Druid tank "good all endgame, not as good as pala"; with Atiesh + Kiss of the Spider + T3 8-piece still a "formidable tank," but Warrior with threat-fury and stacked block value is considered better — [[d:druid#1487166993496015000]], [[d:druid#1487167522406006855]], [[d:druid#1487167652215525687]] (Valarya/Shardphoenix, 2026-03-28).

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[Balance]** Suggested 1.18.1 raid build core: 3/3 Guidance of the Dream, 2/3 Reflection is a common baseline; at least 1 point in Owlkin Frenzy recommended for fights with heavy unavoidable raidwide/pushback damage, since Starfire's cast is slow — full example link with 5 leftover points: `https://www.talent-builder.dev/collections/1.18.1/druid?points=oACYYKFYBLDABA--FAAoAYA` — [[d:druid#1490234290938843236]], [[d:druid#1490234651329953874]] (Yoshi, 2026-04-05). In practice, guilds vary Frenzy investment by fight: Hotverdomme-Nord runs 3/3 Guidance + 2/3 Reflection with no Frenzy for most content, but adds 1 point in Frenzy specifically for Kara40's first boss, Anomalus, and the Chess event (heavy pushback fights) — [[d:druid#1489737036477890580]], [[d:druid#1489737307811741857]], [[d:druid#1489738078540136519]] (2026-03-29).
- **[Bear tank]** Furor is preferred over Feral Aggression in nearly every published tank build: gives rage on Cat→Bear pre-pull shift, enables Reshift, and keeps Feral Charge available; Demoralizing Roar is usually skipped even when specced because any Warrior's shout (specced or not) outperforms the Druid's — [[d:druid#1489571705772048576]], [[d:druid#1489572016884416562]] (Whatshername/TBR, 2026-04-03). Standard pull sequence: Cat form → Tiger's Fury → shift to Bear (Furor rage) → Mighty Rage Potion → Berserk once Tiger's Fury's Blood Frenzy ends — [[d:druid#1488823880549273600]] (Leido, 2026-04-01).
- **[Feral leveling]** Recommended point order: Omen of Clarity first (via early Balance investment), then Furor, then finish the Feral tree; Omen is called "the single best talent for feral" — [[d:druid#1491161697686261821]], [[d:druid#1491169517785845952]] (Hassle/Dinousek, 2026-04-08). Example leveling build (Sylvan Blessing for downtime healing, no early Shred investment): `https://www.talent-builder.dev/collections/1.18.1/druid?points=BSAaAIA-FAYDRaDQQIFQBA-AoA` — [[d:druid#1491170592823709807]] (Hassle, 2026-04-08).
- **[PvP]** Hybrid Balance/Feral PvP builds: 30/0/21 (keeps Nature's Swiftness) or 29/11/11 (keeps Feral Charge) — [[d:druid#1488484356124180692]] (Ecly, 2026-04-01). For a 3v3 comp with Ret Paladin + Arms Warrior: Resto is the safe recommendation; a hybrid Resto/Boomkin only makes sense if all players are skilled; the hybrid build should keep the 2 instant heals + Nature's Swiftness and dip into Feral only for Feral Charge, skipping the rest of the Feral tree — [[d:druid#1487885220026912919]], [[d:druid#1487885405922787509]], [[d:druid#1487886330200457307]], [[d:druid#1487886890156818512]] (Hotverdomme-Nord/Koalafurieux, 2026-04-01).
- **[PvP]** For a bleed-focused Feral PvP offspec's last 3 points, put them in Open Wounds since competent opponents won't expose their back for Shred — [[d:druid#1489995327892230154]], [[d:druid#1489996394122055802]], [[d:druid#1489997559924658187]] (Litchy/Micro Jackson, 2026-04-01).
- **[Resto]** Standard PvE build remains 18/0/33; common confusion noted: Tranquil Spirit (Resto tree) reduces Tranquility's mana cost, not Rejuvenation's — Moonglow (reached via the 33-point Balance investment) is what reduces Rejuvenation's mana cost — [[d:druid#1487561396156760224]], [[d:druid#1489735019915251742]], [[d:druid#1489735161674469550]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-29/04-04). For a brand-new resto healer with no Balance investment, 0/0/51 pure Resto is suggested over a half-hearted 18/0/33 that doesn't reach Moonglow — [[d:druid#1487561396156760224]] (Micro Jackson, 2026-03-29).

## Leveling
- **[Feral leveling]** Natural Weapons talent takes only 3 points (not 5) and also gives 3% melee hit; leveling talent point allocation is "pretty inconsequential" — [[d:druid#1486576416634961930]] (Yoshi, 2026-03-26).
- **[Feral leveling]** A talent point deep in the Feral tree refunds energy on bleed ticks (Ancient Brutality-type effect), reducing the need for powershifting — [[d:druid#1486576416634961930]], confirmed/named "Ancient Brutality" by danimaroo [[d:druid#1486578769077473405]] (2026-03-26).
- **[Feral leveling]** Wolfshead Helm reworked: its energy/rage-on-shift effect moved to a head enchant obtainable via quest, not available/stackable until level 55; can have someone else enchant it onto your helm — [[d:druid#1486576680322728036]], [[d:druid#1486578051826585722]] (Yoshi/dak_yo, 2026-03-26). Because Wolfshead can go on any hat, Furor's relative value is reduced — [[d:druid#1486578769077473405]] (danimaroo).
- **[Feral leveling]** Omen of Clarity procs "way more often" in Turtle WoW than in classic — recommended to take despite conventional vanilla wisdom of avoiding early Balance investment — [[d:druid#1486615623902691358]] (syl, 2026-03-26).
- **[Feral leveling]** For a new lvl 15 druid: Feline Swiftness + 3% hit talents are "arguably the strongest 4 points" to invest for either Boomkin or Feral — [[d:druid#1487004365800603770]] (Mot, 2026-03-27).
- **[Feral leveling]** Gold farming: Furbolgs in Winterspring via a dedicated leveling/farming Balance-side talent spec, ~60g/hour selling Jujus and Firewater on AH and vendoring greys (excludes green D/E value); talent link: `https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/druid?points=oCaAYCFYJ--FAFAAb` — [[d:druid#1487355446288449566]] (Yoshi, 2026-03-28). No competition since nobody can out-tag Moonfire; 5/8 Tier 1 + Idol of the Moonfang help but aren't required; near-infinite mana in this loop — [[d:druid#1487355532514955355]], [[d:druid#1487356111391817768]].
- **[Leveling utility]** Feral gets movement speed early, more at 30 (Travel Form), and cheap Epic riding at 60 via Swift Travel Form quest — [[d:druid#1487164764433027103]] (Eepyman, 2026-03-28).
- **[Leveling]** Swift Travel Form quest is obtained via an NPC on the east side of Moonglade, followed by a solo tank test fight — [[d:druid#1486436692310360248]], [[d:druid#1486436784794501190]] (Eepyman, 2026-03-25).
- **[Leveling]** Powershifting + front-loading Shred after level 26 (once Improved Shred 2/2 available) is more efficient than normal feral rotation for leveling, per question — [[d:druid#1486829305077039344]] (Kiffy, 2026-03-27) — question, no confirmed answer (Contested).

- **[Balance]** Alternate proposed pattern: Starfire → Moonfire/Wrath → check eclipse, then repeat previous pattern; risky since tanks may not have built threat before the first Starfire crit — [[d:druid#1487036606136451082]] (Koalafurieux, 2026-03-28).
- **[Balance]** Hit-cap not strictly required by some published Kara40 BiS lists but generally recommended to get "as close as possible" to hit cap (16% pre-buff, 15% with raid hit buff feasible per some) — [[d:druid#1487159858175283251]], [[d:druid#1487159939389587456]], [[d:druid#1487528146969432178]] (Eepyman/Haunt, 2026-03-27/29).

A resisted spell cannot crit and cannot proc Eclipse — [[d:druid#1487529447895732316]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Balance]** Dotting threshold before switching to Hurricane: not worth dotting more than ~2-3 mobs individually before just pressing Hurricane, since GCDs spent dotting are wasted if mobs die (as they often do, in 5-10s) before the DoT finishes ticking — [[d:druid#1490102392148983931]], [[d:druid#1490102607778287646]] (Nick1192, 2026-04-05); practical approach: dot up the target expected to live, then use Eclipse procs on additional mobs expected to survive their DoT's full duration — [[d:druid#1490103667850874970]] (Hotverdomme-Nord, 2026-04-05). ## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Balance]** Haste breakpoints from Krokat's sim (raid-buffed baseline ~1052 SP / 8% haste): optimal config found at +2% from Medley, +1% Hands, +1% Legs, +1% Head enchants = 13% total haste (drops SP to ~1006), yielding ~10 DPS gain on average, considered a very marginal min-max gain not worth gear-swapping in practice — [[d:druid#1487038801082843198]], [[d:druid#1487040014654115883]], [[d:druid#1487043324874457228]], [[d:druid#1487045127015891014]] (Krokat, 2026-03-27).
- **[Balance]** Rule of thumb: 6 haste ≈ 46-75 SP in value (varies by source: Stunfisk says "46 SP for 6 haste", Krokat's sim calc says "6 haste = 75 SP") — [[d:druid#1487040298994372718]], [[d:druid#1487040775912165491]] (2026-03-27). 1% haste ≈ 11 spell power roughly, but only valid at breakpoints (5/8/16%) — [[d:druid#1487040847919710208]], [[d:druid#1486872576503320646]] (Koalafurieux, 2026-03-27).
- **[Balance]** To hit the 2.5s Starfire cast breakpoint (from 2.6s base with talents), need 8% haste from gear/consumes (e.g., Juju Flurry + Quickness potion ≈ 5-8% combined); 2.51s is called optimal, anything between base and the breakpoint is wasted — [[d:druid#1486871668566790354]], [[d:druid#1486872217001394266]] (Tyrona/Koalafurieux, 2026-03-27).
- **[Balance]** Hurricane (AoE channel) does not scale as hard with SP, so trading SP for haste may cost more on Hurricane-heavy trash/AoE phases than the sim (single-target only) suggests — [[d:druid#1487041023778623600]], [[d:druid#1487040919378071622]] (Stunfisk/Koalafurieux, 2026-03-27).
- **[Balance]** For min/max optimal play, recommended to gear-swap: SP+Crit gear for trash, Haste/Hit/Crit gear for boss fights — [[d:druid#1487054186582642720]] (Krokat, 2026-03-27).
- **[Feral cat]** "20 agi unless crit capped" suggested as current cat food priority (unanswered/unconfirmed in thread) — [[d:druid#1486492989659549737]] (Komo, 2026-03-26; Contested/open question, no reply given).
- **[Balance]** At level 63, boss-type mobs have a hidden baseline 24 resistance to all magic schools that cannot be reduced by any spell penetration; if a boss has additional resistance on top (e.g. 50 total), the no-partial-resist chance drops sharply (~62% at 50 resist) — [[d:druid#1489591376587919382]], [[d:druid#1489593165328421076]] (Endxx🧯, 2026-03-30).
- **[Bear/Feral]** Weapon skill is irrelevant for Bear — all of Bear's abilities are yellow-hit (Maul/Swipe/Bite), and weapon skill only affects white-hit miss/dodge; it only matters for Cat's white-hit component; Feral has no weapon-skill-increasing talents/items at all, unlike some other tank classes — [[d:druid#1489194675750305862]], [[d:druid#1490590831554461717]] (Micro Jackson/Tephus, 2026-04-02/06). Weapon skill also does not change Parry chance — [[d:druid#1489194005798584575]] (Dodging, 2026-04-02). Even at full weapon-skill cap on other tank classes, it only reduces Dodge by ~1.5%; Parry stays flat at ~14%, so ~19% avoidance is unavoidable regardless — [[d:druid#1489686320652746900]] (Jakeends, 2026-04-02).
- **[Feral]** Weapon damage/DPS stat is irrelevant for Feral — Cat/Bear damage scales off the "Attack Power in [form]" tooltip stat, not the weapon's actual swing damage; it is a pure stat-stick — [[d:druid#1488004688514125966]], [[d:druid#1488021351418695681]], [[d:druid#1488021565386919997]] (Bazookajoe/Shardphoenix, 2026-03-30), confirmed again [[d:druid#1489593215555469447]], [[d:druid#1489596058878087179]] (Liroh/akitoe, 2026-03-29).
- **[Feral]** Best weapon enchant: Agility for Cat DPS; Counterweight (attack speed) for Bear threat — [[d:druid#1488003016345784381]] (𝓜𝓲𝓭𝓸𝓴𝓸, 2026-03-30).
- **[Balance]** Mana-regen-while-casting effects (Meditation-type) cap at 100% total and cannot be stacked above that — this is why Innervate is implemented as a separate, more powerful 100%-regen + 400%-intensity effect rather than more Meditation — [[d:druid#1489708401993974001]], [[d:druid#1489714380949160180]], [[d:druid#1489714475559817368]] (Tyrona/Micro Jackson, 2026-04-03, confirmed by asking a dev).
- **[Feral]** Idol sim data: Idol of Savagery (BM drop) simmed ~23 DPS higher than the Claw-energy-cost idol and the ES idol (which sim identically to each other); Idol of Savagery's tradeoff is reduced Rake uptime value — [[d:druid#1488887858931437709]], [[d:druid#1488886963581747373]] (Leido/Tephus, 2026-03-30). - **[Balance]** New "driplet" trinket (new raid, assumed ~10s ICD) sim comparison at raid-buffed 16% hit: similar to Tear (±2 DPS), better than Reos (+10 DPS), similar to Overwhelming Power (-3 DPS); Scythe remains BiS; spell power proc judged "crazy good" with ~50% sim uptime — [[d:druid#1489579562126934113]], [[d:druid#1489579657341702224]], [[d:druid#1489586315153641594]], [[d:druid#1489586405817454764]] (Krokat, 2026-03-29).

- **[Feral]** Anomalus (Kara40) is arcane-immune; general advice elsewhere in server lore is Mages go Fire for it (referenced generally as a known fight mechanic, not detailed further in this chunk) — [[d:druid#1487496463310258256]] (Habu, filter mention, 2026-03-28).

- **[Balance]** BWL: Summoner-type adds are spell-immune; Drakonid adds have very high magic resistance to all schools except one (which varies) — Boomkin trash DPS in BWL is "pure RNG" depending on which vulnerability shows up; getting the right vulnerability on the big Drakonids can let a Boomkin top overall raid damage for that fight — [[d:druid#1489765347732226209]], [[d:druid#1489765971886477482]], [[d:druid#1489767942773145712]], [[d:druid#1489768002168819763]] (Breaddy/Eepyman, 2026-04-04).

- **[Resto/utility]** Timbermaw Hold "portal boss" satyr mechanic: Druids are immune to the stacking Portal debuff, so guilds put a Druid inside to handle it; alternatively a Warlock can Enslave Demon one satyr and use Overwhelming Power to dump damage on it while Banishing the other, or both satyrs can be brought low and killed by redirecting the boss onto them without triggering the wipe condition — [[d:druid#1490024687260926023]], [[d:druid#1490031625529069600]] (Dziadmroz/Midoko, 2026-04-03).

## PvP
- **[General]** PvP tier consensus: B-tier overall (C on Alliance vs. Shaman); Boomkin and Resto are more viable/fun in PvP than their PvE performance suggests, while Feral Cat is considered weak in PvP; Resto's self-healing lets it "tank a lot," feeling strong in solo BG queue — [[d:druid#1487165021715697816]], [[d:druid#1487165331213389907]], [[d:druid#1487165593672089741]] (Shardphoenix/Moonshow, 2026-03-28).
- **[Feral PvP]** Against a well-played Survival Hunter, Feral struggles badly: sleeping/rooting the pet doesn't stop full ranged pressure — [[d:druid#1486394986055467189]], [[d:druid#1486395299932016791]] (Mot, 2026-03-25). Healing kit (self-heals/HoTs) is mainly useful during brief lulls out of direct combat, not mid-fight — [[d:druid#1486429431126819027]] (Miaupow, 2026-03-25).
- **[PvP builds]** Classic-era hybrid PvP build referenced from memory: Nature's Swiftness + Feral Charge (only interrupt/stun-break) + rest Balance or deeper Resto; Nature's Grasp for gap-closing; stack Int+Stam+armor-on-staff while in Bear form for near-unkillable solo play (author notes Druid itemization "sucks" until TBC) — [[d:druid#1490411952797777990]], [[d:druid#1490422451803521104]] (SpicyBanana, 2026-04-05).
- **[PvP builds]** Modern hybrid Balance/Feral PvP builds: 30/0/21 (keeps Nature's Swiftness) or 29/11/11 (keeps Feral Charge) — [[d:druid#1488484356124180692]] (Ecly, 2026-04-01).
- **[PvP]** For a 3v3 comp alongside a Ret Paladin + Arms Warrior, Resto is the safe pick; a hybrid Resto/Boomkin only works if all 3 players are skilled; the hybrid build should keep the 2 instant heals + Nature's Swiftness and dip into Feral only for Feral Charge, skipping the rest of that tree — [[d:druid#1487885220026912919]], [[d:druid#1487885405922787509]], [[d:druid#1487886330200457307]], [[d:druid#1487886890156818512]] (Hotverdomme-Nord/Koalafurieux, 2026-04-01).
- **[PvP]** For a bleed-focused Feral PvP offspec, put the last 3 points into Open Wounds since competent opponents won't expose their back for Shred — [[d:druid#1489995327892230154]], [[d:druid#1489996394122055802]], [[d:druid#1489997559924658187]] (Litchy/Micro Jackson, 2026-04-01).
- **[Balance PvP]** Boomkin PvP damage was noticeably reduced by a class-specific "dampening" balance pass around 2026-04-03/04; players reported it felt less fun and did lower damage afterward compared to a pre-dampening test game — [[d:druid#1489732660891877628]], [[d:druid#1489732913736978453]], [[d:druid#1489733135447883969]] (Eepyman/Hotverdomme-Nord, 2026-04-04).
- **[General PvP]** Barkskin's PvP downside (increased cast-time penalty, castable only from Caster form before shifting) is cited repeatedly as a top pain point for Resto and Boomkin PvP; suggested fix is removing the pushback-protection's cast-time penalty — [[d:druid#1489734670512951386]] (Hotverdomme-Nord, 2026-04-04).
- **[PvP gear]** Full honor PvP set costs ~30k honor per tier (must own the prior tier first, ~9 weeks farm per tier, Rank 13-14 likely needed for tier 3) — [[d:druid#1487252633189355702]], [[d:druid#1487252819852656820]], [[d:druid#1487252940992413856]], [[d:druid#1487258030692237393]] (Aecheur, 2026-03-27). "Veteran" (honor-upgraded) PvP weapons sit slightly below Naxx-level power (more stamina, less damage) after a 2-3 week honor-grind upgrade — [[d:druid#1487408630406975539]], [[d:druid#1487408820220334241]], [[d:druid#1487409239386357761]] (Shardphoenix, 2026-03-28).
- **[PvP gear]** A new spellpower dagger available on Nordanaar for a feral/rogue-adjacent build is "on par with" or slightly better-stat than Wraithblade depending on hit vs. stat preference — [[d:druid#1487409194893312071]] (Stimx, 2026-03-27).
