## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
(Recorded as "CC2"/"Christmas patch"/weapon-skill-patch changes discussed in-channel.)
- **[General]** Weapon Skill formula overhauled: no more breakpoints (linear scaling), new quest books grant +5 skill to a weapon type, 3-levels-higher hit suppression removed (moves the effective hit cap from 9% to 8%), the old 305-skill "soft cap" removed, 300–308 skill smoothed to a flat 0.2% hit / 2% reduced glancing penalty per point, and racial weapon-skill bonuses cut from +5 to +3 — [[d:druid#1329517261354045491]] (Aeos).
- **[Feral]** Despite the 9%→8% hit-suppression fix, feral special ("yellow") attacks reportedly still needed 9% hit to be capped; open bug report #9916 (related to older #9256 on mounted-form daze) — [[d:druid#1309107767579115582]], [[d:druid#1314548848747876383]] — disputed by an earlier report claiming 8% capped cleanly (500 swipes/200 claws, no misses) [[d:druid#1303561869717667851]]; unresolved, asked again unanswered 2025-01-27 [[d:druid#1333193043058364496]].
- **[Feral]** Manual Crowd Pummeler (MCP) no longer functions while shapeshifted as of ~Nov 2024; it's now just a leveling stat-stick around level 30. Compensation: high-ilvl 2H weapons grant +14 feral AP per ilvl above 60 — [[d:druid#1324678534874005555]], confirmed again [[d:druid#1329429655408607304]], [[d:druid#1346277171613925477]].
- **[Balance]** Eclipse/Balance of All Things mechanic: Insect Swarm triggers "Natural Boon," Moonfire triggers "Astral Boon," each stacking to 3 for 60s or until consumed by a matching-school cast; a Wrath crit triggers 15s Astral Eclipse + 30s Natural Solstice (blocks re-trigger), a Starfire crit triggers the reverse — [[d:druid#1329605660827979836]] (Khanaar).
- **[Balance]** Wrath's GCD drops to 0.5s with the relevant talent, which is why haste has any value for Balance despite the spec being crit-gated — [[d:druid#1322657023958974586]].
- **[Feral]** Savage Bite tuning rollercoaster: buffed hard pre-patch, judged overpowered, dev considered a 6s→9s/10s cooldown increase, then reverted to 6s CD with a damage penalty instead — [[d:druid#1320001927940935740]], [[d:druid#1319648633422872628]]. Threat formulas given: Savage Bite = `(auto+61)*2.25`, Maul = `(auto+128)*1.75` — [[d:druid#1320025910015037440]] (Elesion).
- **[Feral]** Swipe damage nerfed alongside Savage Bite in the "Christmas patch" (~2024-12-20/22) specifically to cut bear over-threat; community felt only threat multipliers (not damage) should have been touched — [[d:druid#1320297346109800530]], [[d:druid#1320347554893922326]].
- **[Feral]** Frenzied Regeneration heavily nerfed for PvP reasons, gutting its PvE value too (one report: healed only ~40% of a full-rage cast in a Naxx boss fight); Feral got Barkskin as a new defensive CD around the same time — [[d:druid#1308006939296403456]], [[d:druid#1311064096346476605]].
- **[Feral]** Feral Adrenaline and Ancient Brutality both reworked multiple times through the patch window; final state late in the chunk: Feral Adrenaline ≈60% guaranteed dodge on the attack after taking a crit, ~30s internal CD, still seen as weak because value shrinks as gear/def rises — [[d:druid#1319642404902404116]], [[d:druid#1334216640644251751]]. Ancient Brutality tied to bleed-uptime-based rage/energy return as a non-powershifting incentive — [[d:druid#1319673863201751072]].
- **[Feral]** Open Wounds (bleed/claw alt-cat build) added as a stated alternative to powershifting; community consensus all patch: it still requires powershifting to be optimal, underperforms or barely matches Shred/FB in raid due to widespread bleed immunity (MC, ZG) and unreliable client-side debuff tracking — [[d:druid#1340054766063063071]] (detailed critique), one late test showed it roughly matching a BiS shredder's DPS with much worse gear [[d:druid#1344591258865106984]].
- **[Feral]** Announced upcoming changes (dev-confirmed, not yet detailed): Cat T2.5 3-piece bonus will add Shred to its proc list, and Savage Fury + Predatory Strikes talents will be merged — [[d:druid#1334201544840712226]], [[d:druid#1334201687019360366]].
- **[Balance]** A fix for "bugged hit suppression" was applied with the balance-focused patch — [[d:druid#1318615192694689793]] (dev).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Bear]** Viable MT for all raid content throughout the chunk. Early in the patch (Nov 2024) was arguably the best tank; the Dec 2024 Savage Bite/Swipe nerfs left it "back to clearly the worst of the 3 tanks" relative to Paladin/Warrior — [[d:druid#1322775085840601208]] (Thalamier, detailed comparison). Root cause: bear threat scales as `damage × multiplier` (crit-dependent, spiky) vs Warrior's `damage + flat threat` (smoother, less RNG) — [[d:druid#1338807401687552074]] (Elesion).
- **[Cat]** Middle-of-the-pack melee DPS; strong burst in the first ~30s of a fight, falls off in long fights; purposefully has no AoE — [[d:druid#1322777413934911625]] (Thalamier). Competitive during early Naxx progression but falls to the bottom of the melee pack once KT weapons drop for other classes — [[d:druid#1335975931491254344]] (Elesion).
- **[Feral general]** One of the best leveling specs in the game — constant movement speed, minimal downtime — [[d:druid#1333959974111547402]]; roughly ~10% faster than Balance leveling by one estimate [[d:druid#1335918595435266110]].
- **[Balance/Moonkin]** Mid/lower-pack raid DPS, gated by crit-driven Eclipse RNG, but still brings 4% crit aura, Emerald Blessing, battle rez and Innervate, so it's accepted in endgame raids despite modest parses — [[d:druid#1332393777473654837]], [[d:druid#1333409547594891355]].
- **[Resto]** Considered the strongest raid-healing spec by several early in the patch (Nov 2024) — [[d:druid#1301823324141125673]]. Multiple builds coexist and are all viable; no Resto nerfs landed in the Dec 22 2024 balance patch — [[d:druid#1320441955129495623]].
- **[All specs]** Raid-slot access is generally easy for druids of any spec/role on this server; signups, not class quotas, decide slots — [[d:druid#1327227040163692564]], [[d:druid#1327228197229690890]] (Arcomage, Elesion).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Balance]** Confirmed multi-source core loop: open with Wrath, fishing for a crit to trigger Arcane Eclipse; on Arcane Eclipse, apply Moonfire then switch to Starfire; refresh Moonfire just before Arcane Eclipse ends to snapshot it; keep casting Starfire trying to crit into Nature Eclipse; on Nature Eclipse, apply Insect Swarm and return to Wrath; if ~8 Starfires pass with no Nature Eclipse crit, go back to Wrath rather than keep fishing — [[d:druid#1314190204000534529]], [[d:druid#1317051577416290355]] (Arcomage, both citations consistent).
- **[Balance]** Detailed priority list (Dexter Syphex): precast Wrath ~1.3s before pull to dodge early aggro and start fishing Arcane Eclipse fast (avoid opening with Starfire — it can instantly pull threat); cast 2 Wraths then follow single-target priority; keep Moonfire/Insect Swarm up at all times; don't let Balance of All Things Starfire stacks cap at 3 — cast Starfire once it hits 3 even mid-Nature-Eclipse; otherwise Wrath during Nature Eclipse / Starfire during Arcane Eclipse; outside Eclipse, avoid casting whichever spell's icon/debuff is currently shown on your bar — [[d:druid#1341104557455052900]], [[d:druid#1341104775428837397]].
- **[Balance]** Haste shortens DoT duration as well as cast time (dev-confirmed) — factor this in before stacking haste for Moonfire/Insect Swarm uptime — [[d:druid#1341070230515879996]].
- **[Feral cat]** Standard optimal rotation is powershift + Shred + Ferocious Bite. Powershift mechanics: shifting out (dumping remaining energy) and back into Cat Form grants +20 energy from a helm enchant, +40 from Furor, +20 from the natural energy tick, for 80 total on shift; Shred costs 48 energy, Ferocious Bite 35 — [[d:druid#1325223947188310028]] (Tyrona).
- **[Feral cat]** Full macro-driven powershift rotation (BeefShift): Tiger's Fury if Blood Frenzy missing → Rake if no bleed on target → Rip if no "Ghoul Frenzy"-named buff → Faerie Fire (Feral) if not applied → Ferocious Bite at 4+ combo points else Shred/Claw, plus auto-item-use lines for Rune of Metamorphosis, Nordanaar Herbal Tea, Major Healthstone/Potion at low resource/HP thresholds — [[d:druid#1320403676170289172]], [[d:druid#1320413664960053409]], [[d:druid#1320447018220523591]].
- **[Feral cat, bleed/claw]** Open Wounds rotation criticized as fragile: needs 2 bleeds (Rake+Rip) up before Claw does good damage, losing tempo re-establishing them, and many trash/bosses are bleed-immune (MC, ZG) — [[d:druid#1340054766063063071]], [[d:druid#1340067071433768961]] (aranimas).
- **[Bear]** Threat rotation is Maul on cooldown (main rage dump), Savage Bite on its 6s CD, Swipe as filler; a parried/dodged opening Maul or Savage Bite stalls your whole threat curve since bear threat is crit-multiplied rather than flat like Warrior's — [[d:druid#1338807401687552074]].
- **[Resto]** Tank-healing pattern: Regrowth at ~70% HP then top off with Healing Touch; small chip damage topped with Rejuvenation — [[d:druid#1312463615420272690]] (Mersault). HT/Moonglow + T2.5 5-set variant: spam rank-4 Healing Touch while keeping Regrowth up on the tank (Aessina's Bloom procs an instant bonus heal when the HT target has Regrowth/Rejuvenation) — [[d:druid#1306529156661645353]] (Uglefar).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Balance]** Multi-target priority (Dexter Syphex): strong tank → open Moonfire on all targets; weak tank → Insect Swarm first, then Moonfire; mobs dying in <12s → Moonfire only; mobs living >16s → both DoTs; for 5+ targets, channel Hurricane ~3s into the pull after DoTs are spread, once tanks have threat — [[d:druid#1341104557455052900]].
- **[Balance]** Simpler heuristic given earlier in the patch: if the opening Wrath(es) crit into Arcane Eclipse, spread Moonfire to all targets; otherwise just Hurricane — [[d:druid#1307395333210247261]] (Dexter Syphex).
- **[Balance]** AoE overall is mediocre — "nothing to write home about," but non-zero (unlike Cat) — [[d:druid#1305849240974790736]].
- **[Bear]** Post-buff, bear Swipe (hits up to 3 targets) became a legitimate AoE-damage tool for 3+ mob trash packs, encouraging flexing cat DPS → bear AoE mid-pull; this was later partly rolled back by the Christmas-patch Swipe nerf — [[d:druid#1320297346109800530]] (Uglefar).
- **[Cat]** No AoE by design; this is stated as an intentional, accepted design constraint, not a bug — [[d:druid#1322777413934911625]] (Thalamier).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Bear]** Stat priority: Armor/Stamina (to the armor cap) > Agility (dodge without much rage-starvation risk) > Haste (extra swings = extra threat rolls) > Defense (largely dead stat) — [[d:druid#1315968675530145793]] (Midoko).
- **[Bear]** Armor cap reached around AQ40 gear level (~17–18.5k armor with raid buffs, tooltip shows ~77% reduction at 18.5k); armor beyond the cap is wasted — [[d:druid#1315968675530145793]], [[d:druid#1316398059659202630]]. Exact cap number asked directly and left unanswered in-chunk — [[d:druid#1342651585125417031]].
- **[Bear]** Defense: only the crit-reduction component (0.04%/point) is useful since bears can't block/parry; dodge/miss from defense risks rage-starvation. To zero out a level-63 boss's crit chance from base 300 def you need 440 total defense (+140), which incidentally grants ~11.2% avoidance — full formula given by Elesion — [[d:druid#1326859741959422058]]. Consensus: defense is overpriced for bears, not worth chasing beyond what gear incidentally gives — [[d:druid#1334265120045666365]].
- **[Bear]** Str-vs-Agi threat math: 1 Str ≈ 2.2 AP-equivalent (2 AP/Str + Blessing of Kings), 1 Agi ≈ 1.42 AP-equivalent (via crit conversion + BoK); Agi has a slight edge from non-scaling armor, but Str gives a smoother (non-RNG) threat curve — no clear winner, flagged as needing a proper sim rather than static weights — [[d:druid#1336417269000966166]] (Elesion).
- **[Bear]** Haste's threat value is debated: "statistically 3% haste is BiS" for sustained infinite-length threat, but real fights are finite and early threat matters disproportionately more — community re-evaluating over-weighting haste — [[d:druid#1339301268342177863]], [[d:druid#1339303171272609793]].
- **[Balance]** Stat priority converges on Crit > Hit > Haste (with Spell Power baseline) — [[d:druid#1335435809532153951]]. Community complaint: too much of Balance's itemized budget is forced into Hit (only ~3% comes from talents) at the expense of Crit, which is what actually drives Eclipse — [[d:druid#1345970271588323328]], suggested fix of adding hit to Natural Weapons instead — [[d:druid#1336284549876940860]].
- **[Balance]** Haste's real value is muted: reduces Wrath's GCD to 0.5s with the relevant talent, but itemization-coefficient math on the T3.5 boomkin necklace implied it was effectively itemized ~15 ilvls below equivalent healer-priest/paladin necks because of how expensive haste is treated — [[d:druid#1313083593014116372]] (Elesion).
- **[Balance]** Resist gear is rarely needed (baseline 15 resist covers most fights); exceptions are Solnius (Emerald Sanctum, 50 Nature resist) and Moam (AQ20, 155 resist across the board — best use case for a resist trinket) — [[d:druid#1311112461067489431]] (Arcomage).
- **[Feral]** A Turtle-custom "Feral Combat Skill" stat (distinct from normal weapon skill) affects cat/bear glancing blows and hit table; a recurring community complaint that it holds feral damage/threat back and should get more attention (e.g., via Natural Weapons) — [[d:druid#1313910806093496452]] (Dexter Syphex).

- **[Weapon enchant]** Crusader: ~1 proc/min, +100 Str + self-heal ~75–126 (avg. quoted ~138 elsewhere in the same message, inconsistent) — [[d:druid#1330008486129569883]].
- **[Weapon enchant]** Fiery Weapon: ~6 procs/min, +40 Fire damage — [[d:druid#1330008486129569883]].
- **[Bear]** Cloak/back enchant choice: 70 Armor or 1% Dodge for a mitigation set vs. 3 Agility for a threat set — [[d:druid#1322923552177000508]] (Inovatu, full slot-by-slot threat vs. mitigation enchant/gem table for bear).
- **[Feral cat]** Helm enchant debate: Wolfshead (flexibility to swap into cat DPS, used ~90% of the time by one BiS bear) vs. 1% haste (only when certain you'll stay in one form all fight) vs. 2% dodge vs. the ZG 10-stam enchant — [[d:druid#1327206637248970793]] (Uglefar).
- **[Feral cat]** +elemental-damage gear (e.g. Incendosaur pieces) interacts with Beastslaying/Elementalslaying weapon procs: elemental damage adds roughly 1:1 to cat DPS (attack speed ~1), can be partially/fully resisted based on spell hit, and Beastslaying's flat bonus stacks on top of each elemental-damage instance without being able to crit — exact DPS-equivalence was an open question in the thread — [[d:druid#1322695020628676670]] (Uglefar).
- **[Feral]** Gift of Ferocity weapon enchant is slated to be reworked to grant rage only (per dev comment amid the "second look at Feral" changes) — [[d:druid#1323818648531374205]].

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[Balance]** Loatheb mana plan: pop a major mana potion as early as possible so its cooldown lines up with the first Greater Stoneshield/GSPP repot cycle; follow with a tea/rune; near 50–60% mana, self-Innervate (ideally while running to grab a spore) — [[d:druid#1317490212661760002]] (Moonshlne).
- **[Balance]** Boomkin remains mana-constrained ("Oomkin") on long fights and mana-drain encounters (Kel'Thuzad, Solnius) even after CC2 buffs — [[d:druid#1317117705614262373]].
- **[Balance]** Consumables are described as worth "more than one tier level" of gear for Boomkin; always use them when raid-funded — [[d:druid#1346361963160272957]] (Arnoldgurke).
- **[Bear]** A rage potion is effectively required for a clean opener: landing Maul + Savage Bite + Swipe inside the first 1–1.5s needs more rage than Furor alone provides (~10 rage), and Enrage can't be used without overwriting the Blood Frenzy proc — [[d:druid#1338862206674010185]] (Elesion).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Bear]** Threat model: Maul (~2.2–2.5s swing timer) is the main rage dump, Savage Bite is the 6s-CD snap-threat button, Swipe is the (low-multiplier) filler that also hits up to 3 targets; a parried/dodged opener stalls the whole threat curve for 2.5s — [[d:druid#1338807401687552074]] (Elesion, full comparison vs. Warrior threat mechanics).
- **[Bear]** Bears generate threat via `damage × multiplier` (so crit-dependent and spiky) while Warriors generate via `damage + flat threat` (smoother, less RNG-dependent) — same source.
- **[Bear]** Only one fear-break with a long cooldown and no fear immunity — a specific weakness on encounters like Onyxia that require outside (priest) support — [[d:druid#1338495727189626902]].
- **[Balance]** Tranquility can be cast from Moonkin Form by shifting out; useful in emergencies but costs significant mana/uptime; became "actually worth it" after a buff — [[d:druid#1302281904153825362]], [[d:druid#1304433525986033665]].
- **[Feral]** Feral Charge (bear-only unlock) valued mainly for its interrupt while tanking — [[d:druid#1311534428929134695]].
- **[Balance]** Cower is largely useless for shedding threat as an off-healer: its 5-yard range means the mob is already at melee range by the time it can be used, and in most scenarios you'd need to shed too much threat in one Cower to actually redirect the mob — [[d:druid#1323718783382327407]] (Elesion, full math).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Bear]** Gearing splits into a Threat set (haste/str/agi-leaning) and a Mitigation set (armor/stam/dodge-leaning); full slot-by-slot comparison for both given by Inovatu (head, neck/rings, shoulders, cloak, chest, wrists, gloves, belt, legs, boots, weapon) — [[d:druid#1322923552177000508]].
- **[Bear]** Target ~10–12k armor plus as much stamina as possible through BWL-level content; don't skimp on tanking consumables — [[d:druid#1347104311716614155]] (Midoko).
- **[Bear]** Armor cap is reached around AQ40 gear level; removing/raising it was the community's single most-requested fix throughout the chunk, never confirmed or denied by devs here — [[d:druid#1316401054241259543]], [[d:druid#1326908823264432240]], [[d:druid#1334217860762832927]].
- **[Bear]** T3 8-piece set bonus (Savage Bite stacks a damage-reduction buff): clarified as 2% mitigation per stack, several stacks needed and slow to build (one estimate: 30–54s to max stacks) — weak bonus outside very long fights — [[d:druid#1325582834999496725]], [[d:druid#1325597689466847263]].
- **[Bear]** Jewelry stat convention question: 3-stat pieces preferred over pure 6-stamina for feral tanks — raised but not conclusively answered in-chunk — [[d:druid#1337345523178536962]].

## Healing specifics
- **[Resto]** Four coexisting build archetypes (Elesion): 0/0/51 "Tree" (most flexible — Rejuv blanket, Regrowth, Swiftmend, pushback resist); Moonglow/Healing Touch (very mana-efficient but easily "sniped" for raid healing, fallen out of favor); Moonglow/Tree (efficient Rejuv blanket or Regrowth spam); Nature's Grace/Improved Regrowth ("the new hot spec" after Regrowth's base cost was cut) — [[d:druid#1337160262804832286]].
- **[Resto]** Tank-healing technique: Regrowth at ~70% HP, top off with Healing Touch; small damage topped with Rejuvenation; Swiftmend + reapply the consumed HoT when the tank dips; Nature's Swiftness for an instant emergency Regrowth/HT — [[d:druid#1312463615420272690]], [[d:druid#1312821486083375145]].
- **[Resto]** HT/Moonglow + T2.5 5-set (Aessina's Bloom) is very strong specifically for tank healing (spam rank-4 HT while Regrowth is up on the tank), but weak for raid healing since raid targets rarely carry Regrowth and HT gets "sniped" by faster healers — [[d:druid#1306529156661645353]], [[d:druid#1338424502102528064]].
- **[Resto]** Efflorescence (Tree Form): if your own HoT on a target gets overridden by ANOTHER tree druid's Rejuvenation/Regrowth, it procs an instant mid-size heal — makes running 2 tree druids synergistic; does not trigger from overriding your own HoT — [[d:druid#1341770985745154058]] (Arcomage).
- **[Resto]** Regrowth: 2-sec cast, part direct heal / part HoT; became notably cheaper (~30% base-cost cut) mid-patch, pushing NG/Regrowth to the top and homogenizing what used to be 3 distinct Resto playstyles (community complaint) — [[d:druid#1312463615420272690]], [[d:druid#1321188468193362014]].
- **[Resto]** HoTs from different druids do not stack on the same target (later application overwrites) — mitigated for Tree Form by Efflorescence — [[d:druid#1315991754373333032]], [[d:druid#1344974251907616798]].
- **[Resto]** Downranking guidance: HT Rank 4 sustains indefinitely on normal regen for steady low damage; switch to max-rank Rejuvenation/Regrowth once real healing is needed — [[d:druid#1334875425196867588]] (Arcomage).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Naxx, Loatheb]** Boomkin mana plan: major mana pot early, tea/rune, self-Innervate near 50–60% mana while running for a spore — [[d:druid#1317490212661760002]].
- **[Naxx, Maexxna]** Web Wrap forces bears back into Bear Form + Barkskin during enrage; multiple druids near the visible-buff-display cap can silently fail to receive new HoTs/Abolish Poison — [[d:druid#1312130353821122560]] (context), [[d:druid#1316027928500244583]] (bug detail).
- **[ES/Naxx]** Kel'Thuzad and Solnius (Emerald Sanctum) are notable mana-drain fights for Boomkin — [[d:druid#1317117705614262373]].
- **[AQ40]** Twin Emperors is the first fight in progression where healer mana sustain really starts to matter — [[d:druid#1337778703463088139]].
- **[Naxx, Thaddius/KT/C'Thun]** Fights where you frequently can't stay behind the boss, making the Open Wounds front-attack bleed build situationally useful for otherwise-Shred cats — [[d:druid#1320503485002940518]], [[d:druid#1340287450794299506]].
- **[Naxx, Patchwerk]** The ideal scenario for feral cat DPS (single, stationary target); feral DPS falls further behind on any fight with adds or movement since it lacks Cleave/Sweeping Strikes/Blade Flurry-equivalent tools — [[d:druid#1335975931491254344]] (Elesion).
- **[Emerald Sanctum]** A BWL-difficulty small raid; source of Solnius (50 Nature resist boss) and Head of Solnius / Heart of Dreams trinkets; also a required attunement step for the Swift Travel Form questline — [[d:druid#1311112461067489431]], [[d:druid#1336462815904534630]].
- **[Swift Travel Form questline]** Full step list (dev-confirmed, Fat Pibble): 1) "Mount Hyjal In Turmoil" — Loganaar in Moonglade → travel to Nordanaar; 2) "Shadowed Spectre" — investigate west of Darkhollow Pass, Hyjal; 3) "The Son of Cenarius" — 20 Bright Dream Shards to Keeper Remulos, western Moonglade; 4) "Tethered Memories" — Bough of Cenarius (Dreamstalker, Bough Shadow/Ashenvale), Crystalline Ray of Moonlight (Venethas, cave north of Everlook), Jade Scale of the Dreamer (Solnius, Emerald Sanctum raid); 5) "The White Stag" — defeat the Spectre (now raid-strength) west of Darkhollow Pass, return to Remulos; 6) "Woven Dreams" — 10 Viridian Mushrooms (Jademir Lake, Feralas) + 10 Dreamhide + 6 Dreamthread + 20 Greater Dreamless Sleep Potions; 7) "The Eternal Sleeper" — Seradane, Hinterlands, commune with the spirit of Vithekus; 8) "Under the Vibrant Moonlight" — Twilight Grove, Duskwood — [[d:druid#1325097793362071574]].
- **[Swift Travel Form questline, difficulty notes]** "The Son of Cenarius" is easy; the Dreamstalker/Venethas gathers are ~5-man difficulty, Solnius is BWL-esque and needs a small raid; "The White Stag" fight needs ~10 people and fears constantly (players can be feared off the mountain); "Woven Dreams" is the expensive step (~300–600g of mats); "The Eternal Sleeper" hits very hard — kite with a friend rather than tank it (nearly 1-shot a geared bear); "Under the Vibrant Moonlight" also nearly 1-shot a bear — root and nuke from range with a ~5-man instead — [[d:druid#1336462815904534630]], [[d:druid#1336462886272368722]] (Ginbear/Orangered).
- **[White Stag boss mechanics]** ~150k HP, ~3000 unmitigated melee hits; a Shadow Bolt Volley hits everyone in a range larger than hunter/mage max range for 1000–1500 shadow damage; a knockback melee attack (fall-off-cliff risk near the edge); a point-blank AoE Fear; each ability used roughly every 3s without repeating until all three have fired. Counter: tank + a shaman for Tremor Totem soak the fear, the highest DPS stays at max range so the boss pings between tank and that DPS without reaching squishier ranged players, 1–2 druids root-spam to control its movement, and the group kites it back to the starting spot after each reposition — [[d:druid#1308406716991541249]] (Arcomage), [[d:druid#1308406108196438139]] (Moonshlne).
- **[Swift Travel Form, overall]** Costs ~300–600g in materials on top of already-owned Epic Riding; widely considered "flavor only" since the form doesn't benefit from any movement-speed gear (spurs, glove enchant, trinkets) and shares a mount's 3s cast + no-combat/no-indoor/no-water restrictions — [[d:druid#1316880811840114738]], [[d:druid#1318886432076726333]].
- **[Hyjal breadcrumb]** Druid trainer in Moonglade → Hyjal Town (NPC at the TOP of the inn tower, not "base of the tree") → Ghost Deer at (36, 65) → back to Hyjal Town → Remulos with 20 Bright Dream Shards — [[d:druid#1302127169602060358]] (olivine, refined across several follow-up messages).

## PvP
- **[Feral cat]** Damage feels fine in PvP but cat form has no interrupt; shifting to Bear to Bash costs 2 GCDs, then 2–3 more to shift back to Cat and resume burst — the class lacks a Maim-like stun/kick in cat form — [[d:druid#1333510143786352701]] (Meow).
- **[Feral]** Survivability post-CC2 felt worse in PvP (Frenzied Regeneration nerf, general burst-meta power creep); realistic ceiling described as "1v1 a few classes for 1–2 minutes" at best — [[d:druid#1328423943127961763]] (Midoko).
- **[Balance/Resto hybrid PvP]** Kite, DoT, use Nature's Swiftness for clutch heals, pop Wrath opportunistically; feral cat damage isn't bad but lacks control so time is split kiting/tanking in bear form — [[d:druid#1308975414366175232]].
- **[Feral]** Frenzied Regeneration's PvP-driven nerf (flag-carriers popping it on cooldown) had a large PvE collateral cost (see Changes section) — [[d:druid#1321053899758637096]].
- **[Swift Travel Form]** PvP niches: can't be sapped/sheeped while riding into base in WSG/AB, can carry the flag in that form (like Cat/Bear can), and gives a ~3s head start on cap-and-move — [[d:druid#1328801354222796870]].
