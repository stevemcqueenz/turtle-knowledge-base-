## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Bear]** Idol of Evergrowth was reworked (tested live in this chunk): it no longer snapshots a gear-swap AOE thorns buff; instead it makes Demoralizing Roar deal Thorns-buffed AOE nature damage (18/22/36/44 dmg per target hit, depending on T1/T2 tier stacking) to everyone hit by the Roar, on top of Roar's own ~75-76 threat, for ~120+ AOE threat per cast uncapped by target count — [[d:druid#1440456904559300621]], [[d:druid#1440624182085029968]], [[d:druid#1440977172117585951]]. Confirmed to proc Omen of Clarity and Scythe of Elune, but NOT Sigil of Ancient Accord or "BoED" — [[d:druid#1440665661385736235]], [[d:druid#1440973682678824980]], [[d:druid#1440950893708054660]], [[d:druid#1440870571855581264]]. Old snapshot/buff-others behavior was removed in an earlier patch with "0 explanation" and is missed by some players — [[d:druid#1437721039596421130]], [[d:druid#1437758479858401421]].
- **[Balance]** Sylvan Blessing was reworked from a "kill a target -> 50-100% mana regen while casting" proc into a stacking proc off Wrath/Starfire/Maul/Swipe/Claw/Rake (5 stacks -> mana regen while casting for 15s, 15s CD) — [[d:druid#1436725837352275978]], [[d:druid#1443640878311670001]]. Community still finds the mechanic weak/unreliable in group content; multiple redesign wishlists posted (tie to Owlkin Frenzy, proc on kill-credit not personal kill, etc.), none implemented in-chunk — [[d:druid#1435364939153019051]], [[d:druid#1435426720407621752]].
- **[Bear/general]** Armor formula changed: old hard cap of 17265 armor is now a soft cap with (believed logarithmic) diminishing returns; practical cap is now around 23k armor, described elsewhere as ~20% more marginal DR available past the old cap — [[d:druid#1438986407376060446]], [[d:druid#1444004574741528749]].
- **[Bear]** A +10 STR belt buckle enchant was added; older BiS lists recommending the +6 Agi buckle for cat are now outdated (STR > AGI per point for bear, and considered comparable for cat too) — [[d:druid#1443536751275544586]], [[d:druid#1443537204717551616]].
- **[General]** A native druid mana bar while shapeshifted was added by default in a recent client/UI update, no addon required — [[d:druid#1442984585792323624]], [[d:druid#1442986402231877754]].
- **[Feral]** A new instant-reshift ability/fix was added, resolving the old 1-GCD-per-reshift client limitation that made power-shifting clunky — [[d:druid#1444606528626888859]], [[d:druid#1444606565968773193]].
- **[Balance]** Moonkin Form is granted at level 30 on Turtle, not level 40 as in vanilla — [[d:druid#1444986068192592045]], [[d:druid#1444986843350433802]].
- **[Feral/general]** A "vampirism"/lifesteal stat was added, allowing much more sustainable solo dungeon/elite farming (trades normal DPS stats for lifesteal, doesn't trivialize tanking but eases healer load) — [[d:druid#1444604051143790672]], [[d:druid#1444604644889198634]].
- **[Balance]** Wrath's damage was buffed at some point specifically to counterbalance Starfire benefiting more from Curse of Shadow/Scythe uptime — [[d:druid#1442232341991784630]].
- **[General]** No release date for 1.18.1 was known as of late Nov 2025; community hopes for boomkin RNG fixes and feral bleed-immunity changes, unconfirmed — [[d:druid#1441936802133966979]], [[d:druid#1441937950618292245]], [[d:druid#1444757480704966707]] (question unanswered).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** "Viable" is subjective on Turtle — most specs see raid use, and the server's balance/community is looser than Classic/Retail; feral, boomkin, and resto all confirmed usable start-to-finish — [[d:druid#1435207213273841735]], [[d:druid#1435985919105699974]], [[d:druid#1435986016916738220]].
- **[Feral tank]** "Best single-target tank and 'okay' cat DPS single-target, but worst AOE in the game of any DPS spec, and tank AOE also worst (even vs shaman tanks): Swipe hits only 3 targets" — [[d:druid#1437361025983840317]], [[d:druid#1437367294249664552]]. Reaffirmed later: "Feral DPS is quite solid on Turtle, and it's quite easy to offtank in feral gear; feral DPS in tank threat gear is pretty acceptable" — [[d:druid#1440644351939051552]], [[d:druid#1440644501528907848]].
- **[Feral]** Concrete benchmark: ~800 DPS on a 0-mechanic single-target boss at 2100 AP/39% crit — [[d:druid#1441014731078111264]]. Best-case Kara40 cat DPS ceiling estimated ~1.6-2k average with full BiS, comparable to rogue in the best case, but bleed-immune bosses drag this down heavily — [[d:druid#1441955068835266580]], [[d:druid#1441957898078130277]], [[d:druid#1441958282716647465]].
- **[Feral]** Wishlist poll "what would you change about feral" -> top answer "bleed immune [targets]" — [[d:druid#1441950922430353538]]; general complaint that cat has ~0 real AOE options besides Hurricane, listing every other class's superior AOE kit — [[d:druid#1436019741176102972]].
- **[Balance]** Boomkin is heavily RNG-dependent (Eclipse proc + "BOAT" proc), widely compared unfavorably to low-effort/high-reward specs like Fire Shaman and Arcane Mage; a detailed Reddit post by Scatty triggered extended community debate — [[d:druid#1437478055089274950]], [[d:druid#1437480151171596460]], [[d:druid#1439128761831391334]] (top Echo parse boomkin logged 1,929 DPS vs a Fire Shaman's 2,345 DPS spamming one spell). "Moonkin is a slot machine simulator"; "spamming 10+ wraths without an eclipse is the worst feeling" — [[d:druid#1442255156631568575]], [[d:druid#1442256277345403140]]. Top raider (Hotverdomme-Nord) confirms: "raiding feels horrible... super RNG with high risk of doing no damage" — [[d:druid#1444757480704966707]], [[d:druid#1444767706091094078]]. Concrete numeric illustration: in 31s you can get 30+ Nature's Graces/30+ crits/3 eclipses/~24 BOAT procs, or none of it — a >200% dps swing from zero player input — [[d:druid#1445090764551094434]], [[d:druid#1445091139148578949]].
- **[Balance]** Despite the RNG, boomkin is considered strong/needed in top guilds: "our guild wants 2 boomkins in K40, the highest-end raid" and "great boomkin can sometimes top the DPS meters" — [[d:druid#1435207902867755083]], [[d:druid#1435208412710568050]]. One raider reports being consistently top-14 overall and top-4/top-8 on specific bosses without even having Scythe of Elune — [[d:druid#1444716912318615725]], [[d:druid#1444718438319919316]].
- **[Resto]** "Good resto druids are very good, mediocre resto druids are bad — high skill ceiling and skill floor. For a guaranteed raid spot, shaman is safer since totems give value even ungeared" — [[d:druid#1441028506317492254]]. MC has too little sustained raid damage for tree druids to shine vs Paladins' faster heals; gets progressively better relative to Paladins from BWL onward — [[d:druid#1438997763336700108]].
- **[Feral tank, Tel'Abim/PvP server]** Bear tanks are specifically valued on the PvP server Horde side for high threat output relative to their tankiness (vs Warriors who mitigate more but generate less threat); community fears an unconfirmed rumored bear threat nerf would be catastrophic there since balance is "done primarily with Nordanaar [PvE server] in mind" — [[d:druid#1445018393634799617]], [[d:druid#1445017906411999352]], [[d:druid#1445017818746716231]] (unconfirmed rumor, contested).

## Leveling
- **[Feral, canonical weapon progression]** Smite's Mighty Hammer (18) -> Manual Crowd Pummeler (28, not usable shapeshifted, ~30 min CD, can pre-cast out of form then shift in) -> Thornroot Maul (32, or Ruthless Shiv as budget alt) -> Grimlock's Charge (42) -> Ice Barbed Spear (51) -> follow the sheet at 60 — [[d:druid#1442431828870037605]], [[d:druid#1444602833872617584]], [[d:druid#1435147273721090199]].
- **[Feral]** Consensus: rush Omen of Clarity in the Balance tree ASAP, even before finishing Feral talents — "100% worth delaying the feral talents just to get omen"; procs on all resource types (energy/rage/mana), discounts healing spell mana cost, and Faerie Fire can also proc it — [[d:druid#1437249871949205514]], [[d:druid#1437262431377166427]], [[d:druid#1437266255425048617]], [[d:druid#1439913144163106906]]. Turtle-specific: Omen procs far more often here than in vanilla, "almost mandatory" — [[d:druid#1441278912335450282]], [[d:druid#1439932588394283149]]; generic vanilla-based advice saying it's "not worth it while leveling" is explicitly wrong for Turtle — [[d:druid#1441203667486380052]].
- **[Feral]** Respec sequence: take Feral Swiftness (movement speed, "king of leveling stats") first, level as cat 20-30, respec into Omen at 30 when Travel Form is obtained, then finish the Feral tree skipping Feral Swiftness — [[d:druid#1436097669138485348]], [[d:druid#1438162236073050252]], [[d:druid#1438162385356460145]]. Some keep Feral Swiftness post-30 anyway for QoL (avoids shift-out-of-travel-form delay) — [[d:druid#1438178274244034620]].
- **[Feral]** Bleed-based Claw/Rip/Rake leveling ("Pounce/Rip/Rake makes Claw deal +90% damage for most of a leveling mob's life") is preferred over front-shredding, which most testers found not worth the tapping effort — [[d:druid#1436277019599175780]], [[d:druid#1436322397270573146]]. Bleeds work on ~98% of leveling mobs but bleed-immune mobs do exist even while leveling — [[d:druid#1436272634273992714]], [[d:druid#1444605938198909102]].
- **[Feral]** Cat leveling rotation on bleedable targets: open Pounce (cannot be refreshed), apply Rake+Rip, pop cooldowns, Claw until Pounce falls off, then Shred+powershift; Tiger's Fury regens 10 energy/3s and lasts 18s with the relevant talent — [[d:druid#1444605330041602069]], [[d:druid#1444606175969808395]], [[d:druid#1444606705055957142]], [[d:druid#1444605527299592222]].
- **[Feral]** Pinned leveling advice: skip Feral Aggression, take Feral Charge ("kind of really really good"), and prioritize Omen of Clarity in Balance before finishing Feral — [[d:druid#1443600669230502029]]. 2/3 Feral Instincts + 3/3 Open Wounds is enough while leveling/hybrid; max Feral Instincts + Thick Hide only needed if tanking exclusively by 60 — [[d:druid#1437459193883004928]], [[d:druid#1437462259684216853]] (disputed: some say tanking is fine even without Thick Hide/Feral Instincts while leveling — [[d:druid#1439932710578819167]] — vs "you need Thick Hide and Feral Instincts if you want to tank" — [[d:druid#1439908174802190336]]).
- **[Balance]** Leveling as balance is now "not unreasonable" (vs "feral is still a lot better") per era changes; balance is playable from level 1, no need to level feral first — [[d:druid#1439638208026771609]], [[d:druid#1444984334288425070]]. Important balance leveling talents: knockback protection + Sylvan's Blessing + at least 1pt Owlkin Frenzy (100% knockback immunity, mana from blessing/frenzy) — [[d:druid#1435213516654252054]], [[d:druid#1435214272379617402]].
- **[Feral]** Recognized leveling weakness: no reliable slow for fleeing mobs (Bash/Charge/kill-fast are the only tools; Entangling Roots needs outdoors + has a cast time) — [[d:druid#1442171109985357835]], [[d:druid#1442180979728056472]].
- **[General]** Sylvan Blessing (old and new versions) only procs off your own killing blow, not group kill credit — confirmed repeatedly — [[d:druid#1438184540517433385]], [[d:druid#1444034342216011796]]. Healers can snipe procs by finishing off near-dead mobs with a ranged spell ("wannabe execute") — [[d:druid#1444037598510055424]]; in Shadowfang Keep, not dispelling the Haunted debuff off yourself lets you trigger it almost constantly — [[d:druid#1436218881344667758]].
- **[General]** Feral leveling companion picks: Rogue pairs well (can tag along with stealth/CC, druid solos most content anyway so Priest's survivability add-on is wasted); Shaman also pairs exceptionally well (both can tank/heal/melee/caster-dps, Ghost Wolf matches druid's speed) — [[d:druid#1435632716526784674]], [[d:druid#1435632793186209925]], [[d:druid#1435641709777387684]].
- **[General]** Professions while leveling: gathering professions recommended first for guaranteed gold; Enchanting is a strong pure-gold pick since you can disenchant any gear regardless of skill — [[d:druid#1442170075745620008]], [[d:druid#1442170706799497227]]. Leatherworking offers little for caster (int/spirit) gear until ~level 32 (Guardian set); Herb/Alchemy recommended instead for Balance/Resto levelers — [[d:druid#1441124577198739679]], [[d:druid#1441119518935093360]].
- **[General]** Dragonmaw Retreat drops a strong, easily-attainable BOE bear tank set (2pc/4pc bonuses) around the mid-20s-30s, though the dungeon is long — `https://database.turtle-wow.org/?itemset=699` — [[d:druid#1442894531673587763]].
- **[Bear]** Bear tanking Wailing Caverns before level 20 (pre-Swipe) is rough; recommended to wait until 20 for Swipe — [[d:druid#1442259854059245718]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Feral cat]** Canonical ST rotation: keep max-CP Rip up, keep Rake up, keep Tiger's Fury up, Claw/Shred to 5 combo points, Ferocious Bite — [[d:druid#1445132742516801747]]. Rip: use at 5 CP for long fights (bigger DPT), 3 CP is fine on short fights — [[d:druid#1437413011580256336]].
- **[Feral cat]** Contested: Claw+bleed builds (Ferocity idol/talent line) vs Shred+Laceration builds — Shred/Laceration has a higher DPS ceiling but relies on clearcast/crit RNG and punishes hard when it whiffs; Claw/bleed achieves ~90-95% of a "good" Shred's output more consistently (~3/4 of the time) — [[d:druid#1440032016967008398]], [[d:druid#1440295799870193755]], [[d:druid#1442317213439758417]] (opinion, not settled — Micro Jackson calls the "anyone else is propaganda" framing bad logic — [[d:druid#1440363997567254650]]).
- **[Bear tank]** Rotation: Maul always queued; Swipe when several mobs present; Savage Bite on Omen procs or when rage is plentiful — [[d:druid#1445247274170056725]], [[d:druid#1436429113836703784]]. Maul/Swipe/Savage Bite do not share a GCD and can all be used back-to-back (screenshot proof) — [[d:druid#1436445990809899018]], [[d:druid#1436446840798187602]].
- **[Bear tank]** Threat opener sequence: Cat Form -> Tiger's Fury -> swap to Bear right before pull (keeps the Tiger's Fury Blood Frenzy buff, which persists through the form change) -> Faerie Fire(Feral)/pull -> Mighty Rage Potion -> Enrage once Blood Frenzy fades, staggering two separate 36s buff windows — [[d:druid#1444967001121357986]], [[d:druid#1444967677478178927]], [[d:druid#1445267106819080395]], [[d:druid#1445014486493954188]]. Early on, avoid opening with Maul (cripples rage income); use autos + Swipe after the initial Roar instead — Maul-opener matters less in raids where damage taken keeps rage plentiful — [[d:druid#1438127035804291083]], [[d:druid#1438127177471229992]].
- **[Balance]** Practical leveling/AoE-pack rotation example: Wrath/Moonfire spam while tab-targeting, executing near-dead mobs with Moonfire for instant damage/proc sniping — [[d:druid#1438903873573228736]], [[d:druid#1440382172480536699]]. Boss opener: SF -> MF -> Wrath -> IS -> Wrath, skip re-applying IS near a kill, finish with MF at ~1% for the instant hit — [[d:druid#1440382172480536699]], [[d:druid#1440382414370373714]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Feral]** Cat AOE is essentially "0 options other than Hurricane"; every other class listed has multiple AOE tools — [[d:druid#1436019741176102972]].
- **[Bear tank]** Swipe (3-target cap) is the only real AOE tool pre-rework; "one of the worse AOE threat options in the game" — you must focus one mob at a time unless the tank is Shaman/Paladin, letting Maul's target + Thorns/Swipe build some aggro on the rest before going all-in — [[d:druid#1443042513668542507]], [[d:druid#1443041572668047462]]. Big-Paws idol: "your Swipe has a 10% chance to trigger a second Swipe and generates double the threat" — [[d:druid#1443036285265514687]].
- **[Bear tank]** Idol of Evergrowth rework (see 1.18.1 section) gives Demoralizing Roar ~120+ uncapped-target AOE threat; useful in dungeons for overgeared bear-tank pulling, less relevant in raids where a single tank handles nearly everything anyway; doesn't compete with real AOE classes and won't make bears "the new paladins" — [[d:druid#1440983703974183032]], [[d:druid#1440984930027966560]], [[d:druid#1440982843286224938]].
- **[Balance]** AOE combo: cast Starfire to land Solar Eclipse, then channel Hurricane; "if you get lucky you can outdps mages on AOE" — [[d:druid#1441132564495732949]], [[d:druid#1441135492686151761]]. Hurricane's cast/tick speed is unaffected by haste, and Barkskin doesn't change its duration either (people pop Barkskin before it purely for survivability) — [[d:druid#1441149766040813670]], [[d:druid#1441151366604128326]], [[d:druid#1441150267222659123]]. T3.5 3pc "Aviana's Torment" makes Hurricane very strong (3pc + Nature eclipses can outdps mages on AOE) and contributes 5-8% of top boomkins' total raid damage — [[d:druid#1444720489019867177]], [[d:druid#1444720864053559336]]. The T3.5 4pc Hurricane-extension bonus keeps dealing its +50% damage for the spell's full 10s duration even if the caster stops channeling immediately after the first tick (tested live, confirmed by full-set owner; devs reportedly intended it to require continuous channeling but implementation differs) — [[d:druid#1443219706885439538]], [[d:druid#1443222398861312061]], [[d:druid#1443286163728240851]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Feral]** Weapon speed, weapon damage, and "chance on hit" effects do NOT apply to feral druids in form — only raw stats and "Equip:" effects work — stated as a flat rule, unchallenged — [[d:druid#1442954320487125054]], [[d:druid#1442954612528255146]]; confirmed again: "if an item says 'on hit' it doesn't [work shapeshifted]; if it says 'Equip', it will" — [[d:druid#1444040448451678338]].
- **[Feral]** Weapon-based stat gear (e.g. Iron Counterweight's 3% haste) still applies to cat/bear since it's a stat effect not a proc — Iron Counterweight is considered BiS bear weapon enchant; roughly valued at 30-40 Str equivalent per old classic bear math (uncertain precision, but "3% haste is huge for bear") — [[d:druid#1445112454542786571]], [[d:druid#1445128492344807556]], [[d:druid#1445129612253331496]], [[d:druid#1445133436603072602]].
- **[Feral]** Bleed scaling with AP is weak: Rake ticks scale 2% AP, Rip ticks scale 4% AP — 120 extra AP only adds ~2-3 dmg/tick to Rake, ~4-5 to Rip. Snapshotting bleeds with Mighty Rage Potion or an idol-swap is largely pointless; "bleed scaling could be increased 200% and still wouldn't be OP in PvE" — [[d:druid#1442527798425292871]], [[d:druid#1443622674617204798]]. Direct testing found chasing haste/OOC procs beats trying to snapshot a "super bleed," except idol-twisting for the very first Rip application — [[d:druid#1443621486735786066]], [[d:druid#1443621594751565904]].
- **[Feral]** Existing STR/AGI stat-weight sheets are explicitly flagged as outdated/wrong for Turtle: they use vanilla assumptions and don't account for bleed AP-scaling, always-up Tiger's Fury, baseline OOC, or Ferocious Bite bleed-refresh — "no one has done the correct calculations for TWOW yet" — [[d:druid#1443940954225770580]] (contested/open question).
- **[Feral]** Haste is more valuable on Turtle than the "vanilla sim" suggests, due to baseline Tiger's Fury uptime + universal high-uptime OOC; one experienced player personally values 1% haste at ~26 AP-equivalent (roughly double a naive vanilla estimate, and higher than 8 STR/AGI's ~21-22 AP) and enchants haste on gear as a result — [[d:druid#1443944690142412931]], [[d:druid#1443964936190558330]]; many players remain "anti-haste" for cat, citing the outdated vanilla sim — [[d:druid#1443947202119733308]] (contested; see also the earlier extended Nov 3-4 haste-value debate — [[d:druid#1435240448464523397]], [[d:druid#1435255197994717265]]).
- **[Bear]** Dodge value debated across the whole chunk: originally described as "0 value, maybe even negative" on the community sheet (can't push crits/crushes off the table without parry/block, and dodging rage-starves a bear who needs normal hits for rage/threat) — [[d:druid#1438158632800751637]]. Later softened: no longer negative "because of the dodge talent" (Ancient Brutality returns rage on dodge), but still "very low value" — [[d:druid#1445422497188155503]]; Tephus still argues it's marginally negative for bosses on pure rage-per-event math — [[d:druid#1445440606481551470]]; for a geared MT who never drops below ~50 rage on K40 bosses, the whole rage argument becomes moot and threat/other stats dominate — [[d:druid#1445442851000291538]], [[d:druid#1445443265116639456]]. Counterpoint from a different poster: 4% dodge from Feral Swiftness is worth ~6-7% less incoming damage at ~50% avoidance, "which is huge," for deep tank builds — [[d:druid#1438845983743017032]] (contested).
- **[Bear]** Str >> Agi for bear: Agi only gives crit/dodge + negligible non-scaling armor; on the pinned bear sheet Agi is valued ~45% less than Str — [[d:druid#1441025747773947915]]. For threat gearing, "our best threat gear has both ap/crit and str/agi anyway"; Agi/crit favored for speedrun/burst-threat guilds, Str/haste favored for reliable long-fight threat in casual guilds — [[d:druid#1442798368765120676]], [[d:druid#1442787603135987812]].
- **[Bear]** Defense is a weak stat for bears: can't realistically get enough from normal gear to meaningfully reduce crit chance, and can't cap parry/block at all; "the armor and stam (and str and agi) are good for bear... armor on rings/trinkets/neck/weapon are massive value" since those slots don't normally carry armor — [[d:druid#1444316021778546729]], [[d:druid#1444316992877367408]], [[d:druid#1444316186962821141]]. Armor gets multiplied ~3.6x (some say 2.8x for the Dire Bear Form multiplier alone) by shapeshifting; enchant/gem armor bonuses are NOT multiplied — [[d:druid#1444350531312029988]], [[d:druid#1445031534078853233]], [[d:druid#1441396542073344053]], [[d:druid#1441396568166109297]]. Armor soft-caps around ~23k with diminishing returns past the old 17265 hard cap — [[d:druid#1438986407376060446]].
- **[Bear]** Thick Hide's armor bonus DOES get multiplied by Dire Bear Form on Turtle (armor cap having been removed) — comparable to a permanent Stoneshield Potion at T1-T2 gear, scaling to Greater Stoneshield Potion-equivalent at T3+ — [[d:druid#1444864663656333475]], [[d:druid#1445047155525025915]].
- **[Feral]** Hit cap: "8%" cited directly for feral cat per the pins — [[d:druid#1440283651089764424]]; a separate breakdown for total raid hit gives 16% total (12% from gear, 3% from Natural Weapons talent, 1% from Emerald Blessing raid buff) — [[d:druid#1439595419591250082]] (contexts may differ; both cited as given).
- **[Balance]** Hit cap: level 62 mobs need 5% (covered by baseline 3% talent hit + a little gear); level 63/raid bosses need 17% minus the unavoidable 1% miss = effectively 16%, dropping to 15% once the Emerald Blessing 1% spell-hit buff is available — [[d:druid#1445536068236611839]], [[d:druid#1445536225845973142]], [[d:druid#1445199535231074506]]. Mages get 10% hit from talents vs druids' 3%, explaining why boomkins need much more from gear comparatively — [[d:druid#1445536328505622752]]. Post-Eclipse-rework, hit's value has risen (a miss forfeits both Eclipse-proc and Nature's Grace chances) while crit's relative value dropped since crit no longer directly triggers Eclipse — [[d:druid#1445538911156961391]], [[d:druid#1445537862857789633]], [[d:druid#1445538912226381825]] (contested — Micro Jackson still argues crit should be worth more than the community sim gives it — [[d:druid#1445537355501928548]]).
- **[Healing]** Only direct heals (Healing Touch) can crit; HOTs (Rejuvenation, Regrowth's HoT portion) and DoTs never crit, and haste never speeds up HOT tick rate absent a specific talent — stated unambiguously — [[d:druid#1442857322341863544]], [[d:druid#1442857719412428902]], [[d:druid#1442858022631243879]].
- **[Resto]** Stat priority: Healing Power is king; Spirit better for Tree builds, MP5 better for NG builds (crit/haste also good for NG); Int is the weakest healing stat overall — [[d:druid#1437429730700820692]].
- **[General]** Turtle's haste formula is multiplicative across all sources: `new_time = old_time/(haste1*haste2*...*hasteN)`; no hard cap, but any cast time reduced below the 1.5s GCD floor is "haste wasted" — [[d:druid#1442989641518022667]], [[d:druid#1442989938051383457]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Bear]** Demoralizing Roar is regularly overwritten by warrior shouts in groups, making its debuff largely useless in raids; the Evergrowth idol rework (see AOE section) gives Roar a new reason to be pressed for AOE threat instead of the debuff — [[d:druid#1440582940491780261]], [[d:druid#1440611040021839882]].
- **[Bear]** Blood Frenzy (granted by Tiger's Fury) persists through a cat-to-bear form change, letting bears "steal" a cat cooldown's buff for their opener — [[d:druid#1444967001121357986]], [[d:druid#1445010125013123133]].
- **[General]** Sylvan Blessing/Omen of Clarity both require your own melee/spell cast trigger; Omen can be procced by Faerie Fire even for a pure healer build — [[d:druid#1439913144163106906]].
- **[Healing]** Healing aggro = half the healing done, divided across the mobs on the aggro table — pre-hotting before threat is established is usually safe; Power Word: Shield generates no threat and is good for pre-pull round-up pulls — [[d:druid#1438115714962559038]], [[d:druid#1438115530287480994]].
- **[Bear]** Pulling toolkit beyond Roar/Swipe: self-hotting, thrown explosive consumables, LOS pulls, pre-nuking with Starfire/Wrath/Moonfire/Insect Swarm, marking a non-elite for casters/DPS to burn while the tank focuses hard-hitters — [[d:druid#1438190940601716857]], [[d:druid#1438191280109912175]].
- **[Feral]** A tank can finish off a nearly-dead single target by shifting to cat and Shredding once threat is secure — "the best DPS a tank can offer" — [[d:druid#1444972918290383024]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[Bear]** Fresh-60 bear tanking Black Morass is a legitimate gear-check: "if you can survive pulls in there, you're good to tank raids"; the 4 dragonkin packs can delete an undergeared bear — [[d:druid#1441400547516219402]], [[d:druid#1441393670854152352]].
- **[Bear]** Black Morass's final boss (unnamed "end dragon") is "ridiculously overscaled" — highest armor of any enemy in the game including raid/world bosses, hits hard, stuns (defeating dodge), and enrages; not realistically soloable/tankable at fresh 60 gear — treat the run as a rep farm and CC the trash instead — [[d:druid#1441367430122311740]].
- **[Bear]** Kara10 boss Clawlord Howlfang strips 100% of the tank's armor via a stacking debuff, so only health and (much weaker) dodge matter — Warriors solo him more easily since parry/block still function; bear alternative: let a DPS soak the corner-hugged early stacks (debuff decays fast) and have the bear take over around 10 stacks, or bring a dedicated Warrior OT for just this fight — [[d:druid#1441006493179838486]], [[d:druid#1441008134192627785]], [[d:druid#1441009126829457408]]. Tephus has solo-tanked him ("not fun/comfortable" but possible with strong DPS/healers) — [[d:druid#1441011969254621265]].
- **[Balance]** AQ40 Twin Emperors: to prevent the melee twin from one/two-shotting the caster-tank when it teleports, the healer/tank/off-tank standing on the melee twin should stand closer than the caster tank to steal ~3000 threat before the swap — [[d:druid#1442439018695495790]], [[d:druid#1442440169331625984]].
- **[Bear]** Emerald Sanctum's "Dreamer's Challenge": the second add must be slept (not just the boss rooted) or it channels anti-root protection onto the main boss — [[d:druid#1435724781713490090]].
- **[Bear]** Tel'Abim (PvP server) Horde bear tanks face chronic threat-cap issues (no Paladin Blessing of Salvation available faction-side, and Shaman Tranquil Air competes with Windfury/Grace of Air on the same totem slot); guilds cope via Disc priest Chastise on tanks, prioritizing Atiesh to the MT to raise the threat cap, and putting a Sub rogue in the tank group — [[d:druid#1445011039786631291]], [[d:druid#1445012419029110994]]. Feral/bear tanks are the preferred MT choice there specifically for their higher threat output vs Warriors — [[d:druid#1445018393634799617]].
- **[Bear]** Gear-swapping by fight: full 8pc T3 (mostly DPS/crit-itemized) for most fights, swapping in extra-stamina/mitigation off-pieces (e.g. Four Horsemen legs, C'Thun belt) for the hardest-hitting fights; 8pc bonus gives 8% damage reduction, valuable mainly on high-raid-damage fights like Patchwerk/Loatheb, with 4-6pc sufficient elsewhere — [[d:druid#1445014335733633076]], [[d:druid#1445017422582124554]], [[d:druid#1445014560443727984]], [[d:druid#1445014687208181963]].

## Healing specifics
- **[Resto]** Regrowth is one of the best-scaling heals in the game (~104.6% scaling on the HOT portion, ~130.3% on the crit-able instant portion with talents); using the T2 5pc "10% faster cast" bonus isn't worth the healing-power loss from lower-power gear — [[d:druid#1438467782063947910]].
- **[Resto]** Rank usage in raids scales with gear: early on 3/6/9, later (better meditation/MP5) 4/7/9; rank 3 is close to the most mana-efficient heal-per-mana point — [[d:druid#1438993924428398737]], [[d:druid#1438994301458841723]].
- **[Resto]** Keep Rejuv+Regrowth up on tank/offtank in MC and heal freely — the encounter doesn't put out enough sustained damage to punish inefficiency; gets progressively tighter (and more rewarding for druids relative to Paladins) from BWL onward — [[d:druid#1438997233759682580]], [[d:druid#1438997763336700108]].
- **[Resto]** Stacking multiple resto trees: 1 tree gives a very valuable spirit-based healing aura (~500-700 healing power spread over 5 people); a 2nd tree gives no extra aura value and causes HOT-overlap waste unless coordinated (split rejuv-only/regrowth-only duty); a 3rd makes it worse — but one guild reports 2 trees still topping healing charts in MC — [[d:druid#1435988835820376064]], [[d:druid#1435992271068594188]], [[d:druid#1435989087952699477]] (contested).
- **[Resto]** Pairing two trees with a Nature's Grace/Healing Touch resto for "Aessina's Bloom" (a Turtle mechanic granting +20% healing/-10% mana/-0.3s cast on a follow-up Regrowth) requires precise coordination and is rarely practical outside single-target dungeon healing — one experienced healer found it needs a dedicated gearset/idol and still ends up costing more mana than a comparable Flash Heal — [[d:druid#1436010459303248004]], [[d:druid#1436015435912056932]], [[d:druid#1436015711649665024]] (contested; another poster reports success with it — [[d:druid#1436010459303248004]]).
- **[Resto]** Practical leveling-heal approach (1-30 dungeons): max-rank Rejuv on the tank by default, escalate to second-highest Regrowth if damage increases, then rank-1/2 Healing Touch top-offs, rarely full-rank HT (mana conservation) — [[d:druid#1440918500775563335]].
- **[Resto]** Common early mistake diagnosed: relying on downranked Healing Touch spam doesn't give druids the mana-regen synergies Paladins/Shamans get from their own heal-spam kits, making HT-focused healing mana-inefficient; if Rejuv/Regrowth alone can keep a tank up you're likely already in a strong Tree/Life build — [[d:druid#1441162050767159307]], [[d:druid#1441162282066251859]].
- **[Resto]** Abolish Poison is superior to Cure Poison for the same mana cost (keeps re-attempting a dispel every 2s); Cure Poison's only edge (not using a buff slot) is largely theoretical — [[d:druid#1440953305391173723]], [[d:druid#1440966806960476241]].
- **[Healing addon]** Puppeteer unit frames show aggro-state highlighting and per-HOT icon/remaining-duration countdowns by default, useful for both pre-hotting timing and rejuv/regrowth tracking — [[d:druid#1439057744069136515]], [[d:druid#1444786298547343400]].

- **[Idol-swap macros]** Nampower's `QueueScript` + SuperCleveRoid Macros allow equipping an idol and casting a spell in the same GCD (e.g. swap to Idol of the Moon then cast Moonfire), snapshotting per-cast idol bonuses like the Moonfire idol's +17% DoT — [[d:druid#1440708803007086592]], [[d:druid#1440709007252656280]], [[d:druid#1440709534636314644]]. Reliability is ping-dependent: works well for low-ping (EU) players on instant casts and often on Regrowth, but is unreliable on casted spells like Starfire/Starfall for some players regardless of ping tuning — [[d:druid#1443366602232954981]], [[d:druid#1443367242996645888]], [[d:druid#1443367741074706455]]. A similar Evergrowth-idol-swap-into-Roar macro exists: `#showtooltip Demoralizing Roar / /startattack / /equip [noequipped] Idol of Evergrowth / /cast Demoralizing Roar` — [[d:druid#1440807845615632615]].
- **[Addon]** Cursive (needs SuperWoW) is recommended for tracking Rip/Rake bleed timers, and is the only addon that correctly accounts for Ferocious Bite's bleed-refresh interaction — [[d:druid#1440573427277631562]], [[d:druid#1443613886128459878]].
- **[Addon]** ProcDoc tracks Omen of Clarity and Tiger's Fury procs on-screen — [[d:druid#1440451604900745296]].
- **[Addon]** Lazy Pig has an automatic Salvation-blocker for bear form — [[d:druid#1440252395442671706]].
- **[Addon]** MikScrollingBattleText (`Vanilla_MikScrollingBattleText`) recommended for damage-in/out tracking with a cleaner UI — [[d:druid#1436475414099791982]].
- **[UI bug]** pfUI + druid shapeshifting: main action bar can fail to recognize shifting out of a form, causing it to try casting the wrong-form's spell (e.g. Maul instead of a heal); no clean fix found, some switched to Shagu Tweaks instead — [[d:druid#1443362518662779041]], [[d:druid#1443464783251640351]], [[d:druid#1443464923450445996]].
- **[Addon]** A native druid mana bar in form now works with pfUI+DruidManaBar or Blizzard default frames without SuperWoW for some users (others still needed SuperWoW for accurate live values rather than guessed ones) — [[d:druid#1438858498845708420]], [[d:druid#1438877454625411227]].
- **[UI]** LibramSwap addon lets you pre-swap idols before casting without losing a GCD, for boomkin trash/boss idol switching — [[d:druid#1439160359972765799]].
- **[UI]** FPS in 40-mans tanks badly with certain nameplate/unit-frame debuff/buff icon settings turned on; turning off target buff/debuff icons is the most reliable fix found — [[d:druid#1437933011038375947]], [[d:druid#1437935343356743691]].

## Races, professions, gold
- **[General]** Devilsaur Leather (bear-form farmed from "Tyrants") is an easy early money-maker, but the market gets crashed quickly by oversupply; flipping the raw leather can be more consistently profitable than crafting it — [[d:druid#1434884266600431676]], [[d:druid#1434884478261792831]], [[d:druid#1434884861717516348]].
- **[General]** Only Engineering brings something raid-unique that other professions can't replicate; Herb/Alchemy is more convenient for raiding, Leatherworking better for leveling — [[d:druid#1435986342378213439]]. Contrary to a claim that ferals "can't use engineering," most engineering items do work in and out of form except bombs/sappers, which require leaving form — [[d:druid#1444227823874347118]].
- **[General]** Enchanting is a strong steady gold profession while leveling via disenchanting outgrown gear regardless of skill level — [[d:druid#1442170706799497227]].
- **[General]** Making money passively while low-level: opening the Dire Maul library door for tips, or helping quest/dungeon runs for tips — [[d:druid#1434894772971241616]], [[d:druid#1434891118931214337]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Bear]** Idol of Evergrowth no longer supports the old snapshot-gear-swap trick and cannot be "snapshotted" for others; equipping it is required for the new Roar-AOE effect to apply (see 1.18.1 section) — [[d:druid#1440624378789232680]].
- **[Feral]** Sapper Charges intermittently fail to work in cat form for some players despite normally shifting you out on first cast — unresolved, inconsistent across setups/launchers — [[d:druid#1441930824596324454]], [[d:druid#1441934111882346647]].
- **[General]** Racial/item stealth bonuses ("improves stealth by 1") don't appear to work correctly in PvE on Turtle — [[d:druid#1438490430810619955]], [[d:druid#1438500816414572634]].
- **[General]** Hibernate (and similar CC) can end early via a "heartbeat resist" mechanic — periodic resistance checks that can prematurely end a landed CC effect; doesn't apply to stuns since they aren't a magic school — [[d:druid#1440738870768701521]], [[d:druid#1440745671501287434]].
- **[Feral]** Green cat-form color is a cosmetic reward from a Hyjal farm (Glyph of the Emerald Panther), not tied to hair color — [[d:druid#1440317210466848829]], [[d:druid#1439808663585034330]].
- **[General]** Idol of the Emerald Rot and the T1 8pc set bonus mutually disable each other when both are equipped — reported as likely a bug — [[d:druid#1439705306803470557]], [[d:druid#1439718157081379049]].
- **[General]** A questgiven "Sylvan Blessing" idol/regrowth tooltip inconsistency and other 1.18.1-era idol interactions (Sigil of Ancient Accord not proccing off Hurricane or the Evergrowth-roar effect) were still being actively tested community-wide in this chunk — [[d:druid#1443321431717974146]], [[d:druid#1443322828417142856]].
- **[Feral]** The old client-side reshift limitation (only 1 reshift per GCD, making powershifting clunky) was fixed by a new instant reshift ability — [[d:druid#1444606528626888859]], [[d:druid#1444606565968773193]].
