## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Bear]** CONFIRMED patch note (quoted verbatim): "Reduced the threat modifier of Maul by 15%. Reduced the threat modifier of Savage Bite by 12%. Developer Comment: Feral Bears have been overly prominent in their threat output..." — [[d:druid#1470128951165718528]]. Micro Jackson clarifies the reduction is an absolute percentage-point subtraction, not relative: Savage Bite 225%→213% (~5.6% relative loss), Maul 175%→160% (~9% relative loss) — much smaller than first feared — [[d:druid#1472870879418646640]] (confirmed by Micro Jackson, +community agreement [[d:druid#1472881033757069313]]).
- **[Bear]** Consensus this nerf will be "fine"/negligible for Kara40 tanks; bear expected to remain top ST threat tank; matters mainly if you fall behind at pull due to unlucky dodge/parry — [[d:druid#1472868742336745544]], [[d:druid#1472868867091988630]], [[d:druid#1473358057051525244]] (negligible at leveling levels too).
- **[Feral Cat]** New patch note: Shieldrender Talisman changed to "Your physical attacks have a chance to make your next 4 physical attacks ignore armor. Lasts 10 sec." — [[d:druid#1472658472666661025]], [[d:druid#1472725278446059712]]. Widely seen as a stray nerf to Cat: with 0 weapon skill, druids' extra "attacks" are low-value autos that would burn the 4 charges; community asked devs to make it proc-from-autos/apply-to-abilities instead — [[d:druid#1472699412005523538]], [[d:druid#1472713196627562590]]. Dev did not confirm a change to this in the chunk — [[d:druid#1473646664911950009]] (unanswered).
- **[Balance]** BOAT (Blessing/Boons of Astral Tempo) rework: removes stacking flat cast-time reduction on Starfire (previously up to -0.75s from 3 Astral Boons), raises base Eclipse crit bonus 6%→9% (T3.5 gives +50% more, ~13.5%), adds +2% dmg to all spells; separately Kara40 5pc set bonus reworded to "Wrath vs Insect Swarm target returns 10/20/30% mana; Starfire +3/6/9% crit vs Moonfire target" (down from a flat 50% bonus) — [[d:druid#1467930541188714528]], [[d:druid#1469037279774380043]], [[d:druid#1471143844178301001]] (later note: "+2% damage on Moonfury and 3% more crit on Starfire"). Devs (via Micro Jackson relaying "Moriena") frame it as reducing RNG/improving smoothness, not an intended DPS nerf, but community sims show a real loss — [[d:druid#1470291373780500705]].
- **[Balance]** Estimated DPS loss: casual/non-min-max players ~20-26 dps; min-maxers with idol-swap tricks ~40-50 dps — [[d:druid#1468219371481600040]], [[d:druid#1469589607434944533]] (multiple consistent citations, high signal, single sim author but repeated/refined estimate).
- **[Balance]** Post-patch Ebb and Flow idol (flat cast-time reduction) becomes relatively less valuable as base cast time rises; Haste % becomes comparatively more valuable — [[d:druid#1469592308637761546]], [[d:druid#1469592659743215678]].
- **[Resto]** T3.5 5pc "Blooming Bud" HoT nerfed to a 20-second timer, gutting its value on anyone but a tank; estimated to fall from ~6% of total healing to ~1.5% — [[d:druid#1473498333854044355]], [[d:druid#1473591955811209279]]. Widely criticized as an unnecessary extra nerf to an already weak T3.5 resto bonus — [[d:druid#1473586768480567458]], [[d:druid#1473587890976985200]].
- **[General]** Broad community sentiment: every druid spec is getting nerfed or left untouched this patch (Bear threat, Cat Shieldrender, Balance BOAT, Resto Blooming Bud) while other classes (Prot Paladin, Priest) get large buffs — [[d:druid#1473584428637749384]], [[d:druid#1472675739982758009]], [[d:druid#1472676287343628330]]. No druid tier-set changes this patch; general armor increases across T1-T2.5 items benefit Bear — [[d:druid#1472661108275019952]], [[d:druid#1472661416056983717]].
- **[Priest, context]** Priest Renew getting a large duration/healing buff (+40%+) next patch, which the resto community fears will push Priest ahead of Druid as best HoT healer — [[d:druid#1469448831459524813]], [[d:druid#1471589692544909427]], [[d:druid#1473687831959441592]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Bear]** "Best single-target threat tank at the moment," strongest ST threat, decent AoE (worst AoE threat/initial threat of the tank classes though), good aura w/ Atiesh; Warrior best mitigation, Shaman/Paladin better AoE threat — [[d:druid#1472433943029747803]], [[d:druid#1472437056306417664]], [[d:druid#1470130906286260472]].
- **[Feral Cat]** Strong/competitive ST DPS on bleedable targets, but weak on bleed-immune fights (no AoE, low trash contribution); "middle of the pack" overall in Kara40 because most non-bleedable bosses cuck the spec — [[d:druid#1469512077864337469]], [[d:druid#1472777429050327040]]. Best cat parse on bleed-immune Medivh ranked only ~85th among all melee (war/rogue/surv/cat/ret/enh) — [[d:druid#1469615706503909468]]. Confirmed bleed-immune Kara40/Naxx bosses (per class designer): Anomalus, Medivh, Chess event, Rupturan — [[d:druid#1469613082983530559]] (dev-confirmed).
- **[Feral Cat, dev design intent]** Class designer confirms: "the envisioned design for Feral Cat is to be focused on single target damage" — no AoE/cleave planned by design — [[d:druid#1472862240754372713]], [[d:druid#1472863310939684936]].
- **[Balance]** Currently "literally the worst used spec in Kara40" — ~40% DPS behind top specs (vanilla baseline was 68.5% behind, so a net improvement but still bottom of Turtle's own spread); every class now has at least one spec that out-DPSes best Boomkin — [[d:druid#1473022210397569024]], [[d:druid#1473575760143847447]], [[d:druid#1472868595850674199]]. Contested: Moonshroud calls Boomkin "mid-range dps" rather than worst — [[d:druid#1473680443529232406]] (disputed by [[d:druid#1473575760143847447]]).
- **[Balance]** Raids typically run 1-2 Moonkins now, mainly for utility (crit aura, decurse, Kara40 nature-damage mechanic, CDs) rather than damage; pre-1.18 having 3 moonkin slots was viable — [[d:druid#1467488518191448271]], [[d:druid#1470291373780500705]].
- **[Resto]** Two viable playstyles: Tree of Life/HoT (most popular & most effective) and Healing Touch caster (less popular, weaker throughput, but avoids HoT-overwrite issue with a 2nd resto in raid) — [[d:druid#1473377651677855945]]. Only 1 optimal HoT/Tree resto per raid since HoTs overwrite each other; many guilds bring more anyway for non-tryhard content — [[d:druid#1472633347300659200]].
- **[Resto vs Holy Paladin]** Resto cannot out-heal a well-geared T3.5 Holy Paladin in high-end progression (K40); can be #1 healer vs non-T3.5 paladins or in lower-tier/casual content — [[d:druid#1473684521051230228]], [[d:druid#1473681272323969247]] (disputes Voodoo's "resto is OP rn" claim [[d:druid#1473681053288763488]]).
- **[Overview quotes]** Tephus: "end game boomkin is kind of a gambling sim, end game cat is schizo, end game bear is great but simple, end game resto has lots of variety" — [[d:druid#1473680065425576088]]. Moonshroud's parallel summary: Bear = best ST tank (nerf incoming); Cat = competitive ST DPS but no AoE / bleed-immune issues; Boomkin = mid/low DPS hurt by eclipse RNG; Resto = decent in tree form but raids only want 1-2 — [[d:druid#1473680443529232406]].
- **[Leveling]** Feral is consistently called the best/smoothest leveling spec; Balance is a legitimate but slower alternative, especially post-buffs — [[d:druid#1472995245145849877]], [[d:druid#1472996044123148462]], [[d:druid#1473011588394127401]].

## Leveling
- **[General]** Standard path: start Feral (level to ~30), then respec Omen of Clarity + Feral around 30-37 (contested: some say level 37 makes more sense after taking Blood Frenzy first) — [[d:druid#1466434462937124874]], [[d:druid#1466539697953312920]], [[d:druid#1466540517600002100]]. Take Feral Swiftness pre-40 for movement — [[d:druid#1466539643012124723]].
- **[Balance leveling]** Balance is viable from ~level 30 onward, with Moonkin Form moved earlier in the tree for early armor; start Sylvan's Blessing (Turtle-specific mana-regen-on-kill talent) first — [[d:druid#1470696402005786655]], [[d:druid#1471346820306305095]], [[d:druid#1472995245145849877]]. Level 30 gives Moonkin Form + Hurricane, described as a big leveling power spike — [[d:druid#1473015992664850472]]; AoE-leveling utility limited by mana cost and fast trash death though (contested: [[d:druid#1473013999711813885]] vs [[d:druid#1473013796485201930]]).
- **[Race]** For Balance-then-Feral undecided leveling, choose Tauren for tanking, Night Elf for anything else — [[d:druid#1470138865183363198]]. For Hardcore leveling, Tauren recommended over Night Elf regardless of spec (War Stomp > 1% cast speed while leveling) — [[d:druid#1469077605557997689]], [[d:druid#1469077856754729139]].
- **[Hybrid heal-while-leveling]** Can heal fine to level 40-50 with just Int gear off the AH and zero Resto talents (low-rank spells scale poorly with Healing Power anyway); save Brainwasher gold for a later Resto respec — [[d:druid#1471345148494483569]], [[d:druid#1471345742655525002]].
- **[Feral leveling rotation]** poisonouscabbage: trash = Shred or 1CP Rip + FB at 3-4CP; non-bleedable = Shred + FB at 5CP — [[d:druid#1467979995988361497]], [[d:druid#1467980155644412087]]. `'Betkins: at low level target dies before 5CP is reachable — ignore CP, just Rake+Claw; Ferocious Bite is too energy-costly to be worth it while leveling — [[d:druid#1467987780318789643]], [[d:druid#1467987874593898496]].
- **[Bear form class quest]** Hits hard for its level on this server; strategy = root the mob then Wrath/ranged damage from outside melee range, repeat — avoids nearly all damage — [[d:druid#1468260854071034128]], [[d:druid#1468262593427669085]].
- **[Riding]** The Druid class quest "Tethered Memories" grants (Expert or higher) riding skill for free — [[d:druid#1471514677862469742]].
- **[Swift/Epic Travel Form]** Requires completing the Emerald Sanctum (ES) raid quest chain ("The White Stag"); not completable on Ambershire until ES unlocks there; it's an extra form, does not replace the normal instant 30% MS Travel Form; it is slower than a mount because mount-speed items/effects don't apply to it — [[d:druid#1471205093016604739]], [[d:druid#1471207430401818706]], [[d:druid#1472831919191359578]]. Community guide doc: `https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fEa4FuGraFnK-vL87iuGLH1VJrfPdUpnVcYzXKi4vo4` — [[d:druid#1473044653849710822]].
- **[Professions while leveling]** Mining suggested over Leatherworking/Skinning for gold while leveling (buy BoE gear off AH instead); herb/skinning nodes are low value except AH-flip potential — [[d:druid#1470145542146363598]], [[d:druid#1470145583808512292]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Cat]** General TL;DR: keep bleeds up if target isn't immune and Claw/Shred from behind; if bleed-immune, Shred + Ferocious Bite spam — [[d:druid#1467706022654120222]].
- **[Cat]** Definitive priority per extensive theorycraft/testing: Claw is worse than Shred without bleeds, but Claw+2 bleeds and Claw+3 bleeds beat Shred; optimal = Claw to 5CP with Idol of Ferocity, finish with Idol of Laceration + 1 Shred, repeat — [[d:druid#1466750120862158889]]. Pounce counts as the 3rd bleed alongside Rake/Rip — [[d:druid#1466749628677492837]]. Bleed damage bonus stacks additively (e.g. 120 base → +20%→144 → +60%→231) — [[d:druid#1466779511277490176]].
- **[Cat, contested]** In-development sim shows a pure Shred+Laceration-idol rotation sometimes beating Claw (esp. with bleeds up too), but this isn't confirmed as generally true, still being vetted — [[d:druid#1466801316989898975]], [[d:druid#1466804723905728657]], [[d:druid#1466810974358081657]].
- **[Cat]** Clean HolyShiftMiio addon rotation description: keep Rake up, 5CP Rip opener, 5CP Ferocious Bite (refreshes Rip via a talent), Shred when behind+high energy else Claw, powershift at low energy, maintain Faerie Fire + Tiger's Fury — [[d:druid#1472246978137035022]].
- **[Cat]** Tiger's Fury: 18s of increased attack speed, +50 dmg, and 10 energy every 3s while up — 100% uptime is a "no-brainer" once the level-appropriate talent (attack-speed component) is unlocked — [[d:druid#1468957031791591573]], [[d:druid#1468959997365977144]].
- **[Bear]** Definitive ability priority: Maul = always; Swipe = 3+ enemies or excess rage (also single-target dump after Bite is on CD); Savage Bite = dump excess rage, ideally on Clearcasting/Omen procs — [[d:druid#1470820120833163377]], [[d:druid#1470820576531710084]]. At level 60 with typical gear you'll have enough rage to spam Maul+Swipe even in solo-target raid situations — [[d:druid#1470893111189180466]], [[d:druid#1470893187517382707]]. Simple summary for new players: Maul 100% of the time, Swipe 90% of the time to level 60, then Savage Bite every ~6s once rage allows — [[d:druid#1473353730542796983]].
- **[Bear opener]** Shift to Cat and pop Tiger's Fury pre-pull, then Bear + Enrage, open Maul+Savage Bite, then spam Maul/Bite on CD; save Growl for pulls where you eat double dodge/parry — [[d:druid#1473036752146006037]]. Demoralizing Roar: good for the first second of a pull (keeps mobs on you briefly, low threat cost); on bosses, cast after initial threat is set since it builds almost no threat itself but refreshes its debuff — [[d:druid#1471060381416820809]].
- **[Bear]** Trick: shift Cat→Tiger's Fury→Bear before a pull (or during a lull) to carry the haste/damage buff into Bear tanking; loses pooled rage on the shift so best used pre-pull or during raid-lead explanations, not mid-fight — [[d:druid#1470825685592572141]], [[d:druid#1470826632678871101]], [[d:druid#1470830030056587474]].
- **[Boomkin]** 5-button ST kit: Insect Swarm, Moonfire, Starfire, Wrath, Hurricane (AoE) — [[d:druid#1466846213491130370]]. Best practice: open with Wrath spam fishing for early Arcane Eclipse rather than alternating Wrath/Starfire (alternating = DPS loss per sims, except on trash where Starfire may land before Wrath's travel finishes) — [[d:druid#1473383072685625394]], [[d:druid#1473383117405552713]], [[d:druid#1473383556012183603]]. Boss opener: IS for 3/3 Astral Boons, Moonfire to save mana while wrathing, spam Wrath praying for Arcane Eclipse — [[d:druid#1473383759091859497]]. Addons: EclipseMonitor or DoiteAuras recommended for tracking eclipse state; no reliable one-click auto-swap macro exists on this client — [[d:druid#1473381946087444671]], [[d:druid#1473382288946499827]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Bear]** Standard method: "Tab and Swipe" (cycle targets, Swipe each) plus Demoralizing Roar and Thorns; healer generally won't out-aggro this — [[d:druid#1473127606185623666]], [[d:druid#1473129879196405952]]. Engineering explosives (Sapper Charge, EZ-Throw Dynamite) pull you OUT of Bear Form the instant you use them, confirmed by testing — largely useless for bear AoE tanking — [[d:druid#1473126693303615643]], [[d:druid#1473127780828057741]]. Holy Water no longer generates threat (patched) — [[d:druid#1473129504711901427]]. Most dungeon pulls are ≤5 mobs, so AoE bear threat is described as "meh" but workable; a mob slipping loose can usually be re-picked-up — [[d:druid#1473130460392591380]].
- **[Bear AoE opener]** Pre-hot with Regrowth>Rejuv, shift Bear at pull start; Dragonbreath Chili food is good for AoE threat; can shift briefly to cast Hurricane on the front-most mob so the pack runs through it for initial threat — [[d:druid#1473069183431610428]].
- **[Bear AoE, niche tech]** Stack T1+T2 Thorns bonus for 44 reflect dmg / 25 min duration, spam Demoralizing Roar (Shout) with a Thorns idol + Oil of Immolation weapon oil for passive AoE reflect threat — good specifically vs Skitterers (Naxx) and whelp packs (Emerald Sanctum); Thorns AoE reflect can crit off the shout — [[d:druid#1473218279764922368]], [[d:druid#1473239750042452161]] — but still judged overall weak/"trash" as a core strategy — [[d:druid#1473236665005768860]].
- **[Cat]** No dedicated AoE tool by design (see Spec viability section); shifting to caster form to cast Hurricane is situational — worth it depending on target count and whether you have a spell-power set; having Atiesh makes it more reasonable — [[d:druid#1470722630184206538]], [[d:druid#1470733797904617472]].
- **[Boomkin AoE]** Hurricane is the core AoE tool; heavily boosted only by the T3.5 5pc set bonus (Kara40-locked) — "pretty average AoE in every gear tier but K40" — [[d:druid#1471163021366657239]], [[d:druid#1471163107270463558]]. For fast-dying trash, spam Moonfire only (hard casts won't land in time); for 3+ mobs use Hurricane, ideally after fishing a Nature Eclipse via a precast Starfire — [[d:druid#1473384082850189332]], [[d:druid#1473384167294238810]]. Applying IS/Moonfire to both targets on 2-mob fights is worth it to keep triggering BOAT and maintain the debuff — [[d:druid#1469276520953548840]].
- **[Balance FF utility]** Typically only 1 Balance druid maintains Faerie Fire in a raid (needs spell hit); on short-lived trash it's "not worth losing your global," but worth it on longer-lived trash (IS then FF) — [[d:druid#1472571835219574825]], [[d:druid#1472572320718786694]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Bear stat weights]** Ash's approximate feral bear stat weights: AP 1, Agility 1.5, Strength 2.2, Hit 36, Crit 25, Haste 26, Armor Pen 0.5 — Haste is the best threat stat outside of Hit-to-cap — [[d:druid#1467897618876334284]].
- **[Bear stat priority]** Raid tank priority (post hit-cap): Stamina > Armor > Defense > Haste; with reliable healers, Haste can outweigh Defense — [[d:druid#1467187088469524490]], [[d:druid#1467188479396942040]]. Armor cap doesn't exist on Turtle (differs from Classic); Dodge is somewhat better than classic; Haste is worth a lot and stackable fairly high; "Haste is Bear's best stat once a certain level of armor/stam is reached" — [[d:druid#1467186179618373941]], [[d:druid#1467248978813779989]], [[d:druid#1467248738698268814]]. Enchant priority (fresh 60): haste > strength > armor > stamina — [[d:druid#1470888760542498856]].
- **[Bear stat basics]** Agility → Dodge+Crit+AP; Strength → AP; Spirit/Int irrelevant to bear; Stamina = HP. Bear form gives a flat +1240 HP baseline plus a form multiplier on Stamina-derived HP (~12 HP/point in bear vs the base 10); a late feral talent gives +20% Stamina in bear — [[d:druid#1471170329031217349]], [[d:druid#1473432702509256966]], [[d:druid#1473432622930857984]], [[d:druid#1473432794108661992]].
- **[Bear]** Feral (bear) soft armor cap ≈16,625 armor for ~75% value; useful up to ~23,000, drops off sharply after — [[d:druid#1470162624392986675]].
- **[Bear]** Armor from consumable/food buffs is NOT multiplied by the shapeshift armor multiplier — only gear-sourced armor is — [[d:druid#1473392118826537100]], [[d:druid#1473404091601387631]].
- **[Cat hit/crit caps]** Total hit needed = 8% (raid-boss level; some 5-man bosses are also level 63 and need the same 8%); Natural Weapons (Balance tree) gives 3% baseline, so 5% must come from gear; Turtle has no hit-suppression mechanic (unlike Classic/vanilla) — [[d:druid#1472208440901374072]], [[d:druid#1470828203424616724]]. Crit caps assuming hit-capped: soft cap 53.5%, hard cap 93.5% — [[d:druid#1472205479097794595]].
- **[Cat]** Some dual-wield/cat builds can hit the crit cap at BiS; the T2.5 5pc effect is the main thing that can overshoot it — [[d:druid#1470123554979709053]], [[d:druid#1470123686903156847]].
- **[Cat scaling problem]** Tephus's analysis: cats are hitting crit cap, have a low-but-hard-to-itemize-away hit requirement, no weapon skill, low-value haste, and only ONE scaling stat (AP, with low bleed coefficients) vs other classes' 3+ scaling stats — mathematically worse total scaling (e.g. 3×20% stats = ~73% total vs 60% via one path) — [[d:druid#1473623513289199667]]. Physical classes (War/Rogue/Hunter/Pal) get bonus damage directly from crit, some also get Haste from crit, Rogues also get bonus combo points — Druids (cat) ONLY get bonus combo points from crit — [[d:druid#1473643015599034469]].
- **[Feral weapon skill]** CONFIRMED: weapon skill does not affect ferals at all — Cat/Bear Paw "weapons" have no real weapon skill (effectively fixed, cited as capped at 300, vs the 315 that eliminates glancing blows for other classes), so ferals eat more glancing blows than any other melee; every community request to change this has been denied — devs want ferals mechanically different from other melee — [[d:druid#1470809454944714928]], [[d:druid#1470810086275420367]], [[d:druid#1473643507142099007]].
- **[Feral weapons]** Weapon base damage/DPS numbers do not matter for Cat/Bear damage — only Feral Attack Power (fed by AP/Str/Agi/Stam) matters; "Chance on hit" weapon procs never trigger for ferals, but static "Equip:" effects do work in any form — [[d:druid#1470427093278920734]], [[d:druid#1470427226942996685]], [[d:druid#1473459721934606478]].
- **[New "Fortune" stat, 1.18.1]** New RPG-style luck stat confirmed by the class designer: works multiplicatively on item procs only (not on talents/abilities like Omen of Clarity), e.g. 5% Fortune on a 10% proc raises it to 10.5%; confirmed to work on PPM-based procs (multiplies the underlying %) and on Hand of Justice/Wing of the Timelord (10% Fortune → 1.1% proc from 1%); currently "quite weak," not much scope for abuse — [[d:druid#1472758768050770075]], [[d:druid#1472970656617594903]], [[d:druid#1472972792411590757]], [[d:druid#1472962352155594935]]. Ferals CAN use it (dev pushes back on assumption they can't), though it has limited use for them since they can't leverage weapon procs well — [[d:druid#1472952426352083035]].
- **[General]** Buff/debuff caps confirmed: 32 buffs visible, 48 total buff slots, 16 debuff slots (64 total); buffs can occasionally be "bugged" present-but-invisible below the visible cap too — [[d:druid#1471781046063206541]], [[d:druid#1471777089563918381]].
- **[Resto]** Regrowth has the highest healing-power scaling in the game (barring Tranquility's multi-target total): ~20% initial heal + ~112% on the HoT (with Preservation/Genesis) = ~132% total; Rejuvenation ≈101.2%; Healing Touch ≈110% — [[d:druid#1469614655952388199]] (message id: use citation below), precise numbers at [[d:druid#1469610624022483035]]. Example: 1600 healing power → Regrowth initial heals ~1600, HoT portion ~3600 — [[d:druid#1469610624022483035]]. If you only spam Rejuvenation, Crit/Haste are worthless — more Healing Power is straightforwardly better in that playstyle — [[d:druid#1471588934378066002]].

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[Weapons]** Herald of Woe: 272 AP (bear) / 289 AP (cat), 0.85% crit, is a mace so can take Iron Counterweight for threat. Nature's Gift: 224 AP (bear) / 246 AP (cat), 1.1% crit — [[d:druid#1469260607684350132]]. Best 2H enchant for Bear is Iron Counterweight (3% haste), but only available starting 1.18.1, not yet accessible — [[d:druid#1473037325641453612]], [[d:druid#1473037410156810312]].
- **[Bear trinkets]** BiS mitigation pair: Hateforge Quarry trinket + Husk (Lower Karazhan/Kara10); Blackhand's Breath (BB) recommended over Mark of Tyranny, also useful carried into Cat; Emerald Sanctum has a BiS threat trinket — [[d:druid#1470935086819180615]], [[d:druid#1471023096768106739]], [[d:druid#1471028432539226122]].
- **[Bear rings]** Prefer armor-stat rings for bear threat purposes over low-Str alternatives; Cat prefers the Agility ring, though the hit-from-Natural-Weapons argument for it is now "lackluster" — [[d:druid#1471611361061638328]], [[d:druid#1471616500530941983]]. The "Brood Ring" is swappable for a different one at Blackrock/Ratchet — [[d:druid#1471612570262569152]].
- **[Bear gear]** Underrated picks: jewelry with Armor, and leather with elevated (green-text) armor value — easy to overlook, "giga-valuable" especially early — [[d:druid#1470825500875292966]]. T2 chest/belt are "meh" for feral tank — get T2.5 chest and C'Thun belt, with Dragonhide belt as a stepping stone — [[d:druid#1472364814096662731]].
- **[Boomkin trinkets]** Eye of Diminution (threat-safe, use vs sensitive threat/poor tanks) vs Sapphiron's trinket (pure damage, needs longer fights to pay off since it's on-use); Undead/Demon trinket almost always best vs those mob types; need Neltharion's Tear for hit unless you have Scythe — [[d:druid#1470083472247881952]], [[d:druid#1470109163978887271]], [[d:druid#1470086019675062347]]. Naxx BiS spreadsheet lists Sapphiron's Trinket, though some prefer the Crit trinket — sim-dependent — [[d:druid#1470095813848862741]].
- **[Boomkin gear]** Prioritize +healing/spellpower over Int on gear generally; solve mana via downranking rather than stacking Int/mp5 — [[d:druid#1472451410561073319]], [[d:druid#1468251682872754359]]. Look for "Feral Attack Power" stat as the giveaway for cat/bear itemized gear (AtlasLoot addon useful for browsing) — [[d:druid#1470467322710982656]], [[d:druid#1470467973398401317]].
- **[Scythe of Elune]** BiS legendary trinket/weapon for Boomkin: 4% stats + 40 SP baseline, damage proc + vulnerability debuff (~5-8% dmg), plus a summon — ~300%+ better than 2nd-best option; can take a player from ~12th to ~4th DPS in a raid; costs ~3k gold to build, biggest bottleneck is the Father Lycan world-boss kill requiring 80-100+ people for one item — [[d:druid#1467953501370978476]], [[d:druid#1467953612117381304]], [[d:druid#1469095000045523024]]. Widely criticized as bad design (unlike Sulfuras/Thunderfury/Atiesh, it's near-mandatory for the spec to compete) — [[d:druid#1468228242426040433]].
- **[T3/T3.5 feral sets]** T3 6pc feral bonus is a flat, non-scaling +25% to a small HP amount — weak, not worth holding over better pieces — [[d:druid#1471344095631900837]]. Cat/Bear tier sets cannot be split — same tier armor benefits either role each raid tier ("lottery"), unlike Priest's two Disc set options; expected to keep alternating — [[d:druid#1472660835376824491]], [[d:druid#1472661241855217917]].
- **[Resto items]** Loop of Infused Renewal ring (~10% proc): applies before the HT heal lands so it can trigger Aessina's Bloom; stacks with own Rejuvenation; doesn't show on Luna unit frames; considered weak/underwhelming by some, "fine" by others (+2% healing on top of okay stats) — [[d:druid#1468649772331044896]], [[d:druid#1468451833847615682]], [[d:druid#1468509770846830673]]. Sheldralar staff's healing proc only triggers off direct heals, not HoT ticks — confirmed bug, has a filed bug report — [[d:druid#1469260707819425924]] (see also Bugs section).
- **[Leveling/budget gear]** Primalist's Set (leather, ~lvl40, cheap to craft) is decent budget off-spec Resto gear but not worth investing Cured Rugged Hide in unless Resto is your main; better options exist by 60 (e.g. Battlemaster's exalted chest, reachable in one afternoon of grinding) — [[d:druid#1473321204353073359]], [[d:druid#1473322168170582208]].

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Weapon oils/enchants]** Elemental Sharpening Stone (haste variant) does NOT apply to staves ("balanced weapons"); the crit variant works regardless — [[d:druid#1467888050737778901]], [[d:druid#1467888338571886758]]. 3% attack-speed weapon enchants only affect auto-attack, barely scaling with special abilities — for Cat, 25 Agility is a better choice than 3% attack speed — [[d:druid#1469277144948539600]], [[d:druid#1469280847407349878]].
- **[Weapon enchant, form-specific]** Iron Counterweight: use the Weighted version in Bear, the Agility version in Cat — [[d:druid#1470888313622888533]].
- **[AoE tank tech]** Oil of Immolation weapon oil + Thorns idol + stacked T1/T2 Thorns bonus, see AoE rotation section — [[d:druid#1473218279764922368]].
- **[Head enchant, tank]** Gift of Ferocity is a judgment call — makes dps-ing viable but has near-zero value if you never dps; the 1% Haste head enchant is the other solid pick — [[d:druid#1470849491795181648]], [[d:druid#1470851955541934287]].

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[Food]** Hardened Mushroom + Gurubashi Gumbo food buffs stack — [[d:druid#1467924402665558118]]. Nordanaar Herbal Tea and Dreamtonic (from turning in Hyjal Shards at an ES-tree NPC) give mana/health tea and raw SP elixirs respectively — [[d:druid#1467142356087341213]], [[d:druid#1467143732628291878]], [[d:druid#1467145699249357019]].
- **[Bear pre-pull]** Sequence example: Regrowth, Rejuvenation, Cat form, Tiger's Fury, Bear form, starting ~10s before pull — [[d:druid#1472398513760637101]]. Boss-pull consumable combo: Enrage, Potion of Mighty Rage, Kiss of the Spider, Juju Flurry (plus pre-cast Regrowth/Rejuv) — [[d:druid#1472409131196616948]]. Juju Flurry on Turtle gives only +3% attack/cast speed (no retail-style melee-damage penalty) — [[d:druid#1473368572381102200]].
- **[Rage tech]** Furor talent + a head enchant that grants rage on form-shift together give up to ~15 rage per shift — recommended for tank druids to ease pre-pull rage generation — [[d:druid#1472410728739770508]].
- **[Resto mana]** Items granting "% of Spirit regen while casting" bypass the normal 5-second-rule mana-regen freeze partially — good for rejuv-heavy specs worried about being locked out of mp5/spirit gear — [[d:druid#1472451410561073319]].
- **[Resto min-max]** Top-end technique: stack every available world buff + spirit buff to reach ~900 Spirit and snapshot it entering the raid portal — [[d:druid#1473685728889147436]].
- **[Mana regen mechanic]** No mana regen occurs while shapeshifted (forms don't break the 5-second rule's freeze on their own), but feral abilities themselves don't trigger the 5-second rule; Sylvan Blessing talent gives 15s of normal regen after a killing blow, which DOES apply even while shapeshifted for that window — [[d:druid#1468667082152349911]], [[d:druid#1468667178894102528]], [[d:druid#1468665948134310054]], [[d:druid#1468668127830085714]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Berserk]** 6-minute CD, widely seen as too long vs other classes' offensive CDs (Adrenaline Rush ~3min, Death Wish ~3-5min); community request for a shorter CD has been "flat out" denied by devs — [[d:druid#1469512077864337469]], [[d:druid#1469539039886446779]].
- **[Battle Rez / Rebirth]** Cannot be cast while in Moonkin Form; devs will not add a non-combat "normal" resurrection spell as it's "not blizzlike" — [[d:druid#1473443440191864896]].
- **[Sappers/engineering in form]** Sapper Charges and other explosives can only be used out of Cat/Bear form (they force-cancel the shapeshift) — annoying for Bear, less of an issue for Cat since it's easier to justify shifting out; the Battle/Iron Chicken engineering pet is usable while shapeshifted — [[d:druid#1472554972938895546]], [[d:druid#1472570405758832642]].
- **[Racials in form]** War Stomp (Tauren) is not usable while shapeshifted (same as Classic), a frequent community complaint/wish — [[d:druid#1473355926059880630]], [[d:druid#1473364798971314256]].
- **[Omen of Clarity]** On Turtle it is NOT a castable buff like in Classic — it is always passively active — [[d:druid#1470390148909764641]].
- **[Combo points]** A 2-point talent gives a chance for any crit while building combo points to grant an extra CP — [[d:druid#1471028199185059967]].
- **[Feral utility]** 1 feral per melee group is common for the 3% crit aura (Leader of the Pack), Innervate, Combat Rez, and off-tank flexibility — [[d:druid#1472320012265066739]]. Atiesh is still BiS for Feral specifically (haste proc) — one of the only classes where it remains BiS — [[d:druid#1472320701171110093]].
- **[Resto utility]** Tranquility, Battle/Combat Res, and "Emerald Blessing" (druid-specific raid buff) are the main raid-utility pillars — [[d:druid#1473377932238913537]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[Rotation/priority]** See "Rotation: single target" and "AoE" sections above for the Maul/Swipe/Bite priority and opener sequence.
- **[Vamp]** Bear Form has an inherent 10% lifesteal/vampirism baked in — [[d:druid#1470133081708167262]].
- **[Sustain]** No shield-block/holy-shield equivalent — survivability is pure armor+HP; community jokes it needs very high Defense/Armor to avoid crits, but no confirmed hard number given beyond the earlier stat-weight table — [[d:druid#1468363742449504450]].
- **[Bear threat math]** Maul and Savage Bite each carry large flat threat modifiers (225% pre-nerf on Savage Bite, 175% on Maul) — see 1.18.1 section for the actual nerf math.
- **[Terminology]** Turtle's tank spec is called "Feral Tank," not "Guardian" — [[d:druid#1472911063032008756]].
- **[Professions for tanks]** Engineering weak for feral tank (can't use most consumables in form); Leatherworking okay to start, becomes a low-value craft later; Alchemy stays relevant/valuable to end-game — [[d:druid#1472200959798022301]].

## Healing specifics
- **[Playstyle split]** See "Spec viability" — Tree/HoT vs Healing Touch caster. Hybrid approach recommended by top healers: use Rejuvenation for quick/reactive coverage, Regrowth when incoming damage is spiky; don't commit to only one — [[d:druid#1471593197053546772]].
- **[Downranking]** Regrowth "sweet spot" rank for reducing overheal varies by gear — some use Rank 6, others experimenting with 7-9; overhealing itself is only a problem if it causes mana issues, mana-efficiency (heal/mana ratio) matters more — [[d:druid#1468299742621597760]], [[d:druid#1468301149890281573]], [[d:druid#1468301189916393616]].
- **[HT build practical tips]** Use low-rank HT (e.g. Rank 4) for mana-efficient spam in dungeons, save a higher rank for use with Nature's Swiftness; "Rank 4 HT my beloved" is a common refrain — [[d:druid#1470475428966699039]], [[d:druid#1470481490113593364]]. Rejuvenation Rank 3 in Tree form heals ~1200 for 53 mana and also triggers 2pc T3 — very efficient filler — [[d:druid#1468475459300757669]], [[d:druid#1468475575466328222]].
- **[Efflorescence]** Currently locked to Tree of Life form only — a frequent community complaint (Nature's Grace/Regrowth-focused resto druids can't use it) — [[d:druid#1467257280880971900]]. Dev states self-Efflorescence proccing (from your own heals) is "very unlikely to happen" — [[d:druid#1470447471728660552]].
- **[Bug]** Regrowth + Efflorescence exploit: if a 2nd tree druid casts a lower-rank Regrowth on a target already carrying a higher-rank Regrowth from another tree, the game can't overwrite it and Efflorescence stacks exponentially (observed healing spikes to ~250k); confirmed exploit, dev says "it should already be fixed" — [[d:druid#1471581516873207891]], [[d:druid#1471581817256546345]], [[d:druid#1471601761432113333]].
- **[Entangling Roots / Nature's Grasp]** Roots reportedly cannot be tested/cast on the target dummy because it's not flagged "outside" (outdoor-only restriction); Nature's Grasp still procs/works fine indoors during dungeon healing — [[d:druid#1466585763259879486]] context at [[d:druid#1467264430126534961]], [[d:druid#1467263574295445505]]. Nature's Grasp must be pre-cast before shifting into Bear form (not castable while already in Bear) — [[d:druid#1470006873754439814]].

## PvP
- **[Feral, contested]** Some claim feral is currently weak/uncompetitive in PvP on Nordanaar except for a single standout player, and that "anyone else played well beats it" — [[d:druid#1473019005051863050]], [[d:druid#1473019191312253009]], [[d:druid#1473020208221520056]]. Countered in detail: feral (half cat/half bear geared) is strong when played right — feared in AB, mobile/tanky WSG flag runner, could "turn the tides" in AV; tactic = stun in Cat, load bleeds to bring target past half HP before stun breaks, shift Bear to finish, chain another stun back into Shred; biggest threats are geared Warlocks/Rogues — [[d:druid#1473021122361692334]], [[d:druid#1473021901327827187]], [[d:druid#1473021326535950337]]. General 1v1 druid PvP is "a battle to outlast," group PvP value is providing utility — [[d:druid#1467843965523988716]].
- **[Bear form PvP]** Good mobility + armor scaling makes it a durable, hard-to-one-shot state for flag carrying; Boomkin has similar (if less complete, no armor talent) survivability option — [[d:druid#1473023928166514922]], [[d:druid#1473024256492175431]].
- **[Tree of Life]** Widely regarded as very weak in PvP due to its movement penalty; community repeatedly asks devs to remove/reduce it, framed as a balance risk devs are wary of — [[d:druid#1467260763193475113]], [[d:druid#1467261349024497870]].
- **[Balance leveling PvP]** Balance PvP viability depends heavily on who opens (stealth); Feral is more reliable in PvP overall, but Boomkin with proper talents/form can do good burst; leveling boomkins often lack Nature's Grasp since it's a 5-point investment — [[d:druid#1467837016594186402]], [[d:druid#1467837293216923809]].

- **[Professions, leveling gold]** Mining suggested over Leatherworking/Skinning while leveling for raw gold, buying gear off AH instead of crafting it — [[d:druid#1470145542146363598]], [[d:druid#1470145583808512292]].
- **[Gold, general]** Fishing gives solid steady gold; running multiple alts with every crafting profession plus dedicated "CD alts" (cooldown-material farmers) is described as the best endgame income strategy — [[d:druid#1471926560390119557]], [[d:druid#1471920280158081159]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Weapon skill display bug]** BetterCharacterStats addon apparently misreports weapon skill effects for druids in forms (a player claimed +5 weapon skill reduced his glancing rate; confirmed false/RNG — ferals get no benefit from weapon skill at all, addon creator should special-case druids) — [[d:druid#1470809303941255300]], [[d:druid#1470811173896323308]].
- **[Sheldralar staff]** Its healing proc only triggers off direct heals, not HoT ticks — a filed bug, not intended behavior — [[d:druid#1469260607684350132]] region, confirmed at [[d:druid#1469260707819425924]].
- **[Regrowth/Efflorescence exploit]** See Healing specifics — massive overheal exploit from lower-rank Regrowth overwrite interactions; dev says it should already be fixed — [[d:druid#1471581817256546345]], [[d:druid#1471601761432113333]].
- **[Sound bug]** Shifting into Bear Form plays an extra "drum" sound (like the BG-queue-pop noise) tied to the action bar re-rendering on stance change; reproducible even with all addons disabled — [[d:druid#1471183723134451713]], [[d:druid#1471196297485877493]].
- **[Buff visibility]** Buffs/elixirs can sometimes be active but not visually shown even under the 32-buff visible cap — [[d:druid#1471777089563918381]] (see [[d:druid#1471777089563918381]]).
- **[BoED / Ornate Bloodstone Dagger]** Blade of Eternal Darkness (Equip: Spellstrike) works in all forms and, combined with a Hateforge Quarry spellstrike set piece, can proc twice off a single multi-target Swipe — contested (Stunfisk said this "hasn't worked in ~3 years," Viertakt provided screenshot evidence it still does) — [[d:druid#1471547823026602200]], [[d:druid#1471555790081884402]], [[d:druid#1471557121240858695]]. Separately, "Ornate Bloodstone Dagger" (a different item from Mara princess) got nerfed to no longer trigger from non-physical-damage effects without a cast — [[d:druid#1472681592911560927]].
- **[Engineering + Bear Form]** Sapper Charge / EZ-Throw Dynamite instantly cancel Bear Form on use — [[d:druid#1473126693303615643]], [[d:druid#1473127780828057741]].
- **[Bleed-immune inconsistencies]** Some bosses/mobs bleed inconsistently for lore/legacy reasons inherited from original Blizzard flags (e.g. Garr can bleed, Rupturan cannot); devs deliberately expanded immunities to some previously-bleedable bosses "to make it consistent with other creature types," and once tried making everything bleedable, which caused "enormous" community backlash and was reverted — [[d:druid#1472953343767740507]], [[d:druid#1472954353202630862]], [[d:druid#1472959387235520522]].
