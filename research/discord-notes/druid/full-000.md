## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Reshift is a new single ability that instantly shifts out and back into form (replaces old manual powershift dance); wished it were available earlier than level 60 — [[d:druid#1424532148173537281]], [[d:druid#1427361652831031378]].
- **[Feral]** Omen of Clarity (OoC) reworked into a passive buff (no self-cast needed), flat ~10% proc chance per hit, no ICD, procs on physical attacks only (not spells/healing) — [[d:druid#1425437731009597611]], [[d:druid#1428648263128453141]], [[d:druid#1430493573836443668]], [[d:druid#1425153617685381242]] (18/0/33 build procs it on Faerie Fire opener).
- **[Feral]** "Feral Faerie Fire" is no longer a talent (baseline ability) — [[d:druid#1425999189984411740]].
- **[Feral]** New talent **Open Wounds**: Claw deals bonus dmg with bleeds active (30% w/ 2 bleeds per some discussion), praised as "biggest update for feral dps" — [[d:druid#1424410042136002712]], [[d:druid#1424402530409906288]].
- **[Feral]** New talent **Carnage** (2/2): Ferocious Bite refreshes Rip and Rake durations on 5 CP — [[d:druid#1425289376962838642]] (bug report it wasn't refreshing, later confirmed to work with Cursive+SuperWoW), [[d:druid#1431128574735290399]].
- **[Feral]** Rake: all ranks scale 12% of initial damage + 3% of bleed with AP (Turtle-specific change); Claw deals 100% weapon damage in addition to base; Claw rank 5, Shred rank 6, Rake rank 4 base/DoT damage increased 5% — [[d:druid#1428888454623789109]] (Lyrazia summary of official changelog).
- **[Feral]** Wolfshead Helm effect is now a craftable/questable **enchant**, not a helm item — [[d:druid#1426547464323993703]], [[d:druid#1426366899809157252]].
- **[Feral]** Bear/Cat can now use engineering consumables (grenades, bombs) per patch notes, but players tested and **grenades did NOT work in bear form** in practice — [[d:druid#1431618759629733889]], [[d:druid#1431545903520419923]].
- **[Balance]** New talent **Sylvan Blessing** (mana-on-killing-blow) is widely criticized as weak/not proccing in group content since only the tagger/killer gets credit; community petition to rework it to proc on any tagged-mob kill or % damage contribution — [[d:druid#1432401202963939472]], [[d:druid#1432495619280211979]] (+6 upvotes), [[d:druid#1432793344764936252]].
- **[Balance]** New talent **Balance of All Things (BotF)**: buff stacks up to 3x, 60s duration, carries between mobs, procs well when double-DoTing — [[d:druid#1426118883617669121]], [[d:druid#1426119337248292915]].
- **[Balance]** **Eclipse** talent added — 1 point, described as "a lot of extra damage" and makes rotation more engaging — [[d:druid#1426118883617669121]].
- **[Balance]** **Aessina's Bloom** talent (via T2.5) considered strong/worth pathing into, made Nature's Grace "feel obsolete" — [[d:druid#1425112268881924136]], [[d:druid#1425126085741248573]].
- **[Resto]** Swiftmend added on the path to Natural Swiftness (unlike vanilla) — cited as extra reason to go that route for PvP — [[d:druid#1430586019010580553]].
- **[Druid general]** Swift Travel Form (100% speed epic-mount-equivalent) added via long/expensive level-60 questline; separate from normal Travel Form; grants a glyph to change regular Travel Form appearance to a stag; cannot be used in combat/indoors, 3s cast — [[d:druid#1430978346891739247]], [[d:druid#1430979926915547146]], [[d:druid#1430981583191670946]]. Requires Emerald Sanctum to be released/attuned on a server (not yet on Ambershire as of thread) — [[d:druid#1431000296967901225]] area, [[d:druid#1434329117653729431]].
- **[Bug]** "Blooming Bud" (resto idol?) healing fixed to no longer consume resources/trigger other effects — [[d:druid#1432843964460830940]] (dev bug-fix confirmed via forum link).
- **[Bug, open]** Grandstaff of the Shendralar doesn't proc off HoTs currently (bug report filed) — [[d:druid#1429547508765556756]].
- **[Bug, open]** Resto T3.5 set bonus interaction with "Eye of the Dead" reported buggy (bug report filed by Micro Jackson) — [[d:druid#1429559389249081428]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Feral cat]** Considered "above medium, not top dps"; estimated ~2.2-2.5k dps on Patchwerk-style fights per some, disputed by others as "check turtlogs yourself" — [[d:druid#1425492829303476295]], [[d:druid#1425500236281020567]], [[d:druid#1425504972371398827]] (turtlogs.com/pve/ranking link). Long argument (MoneyCow vs SilverWF) that sample size on Turtle is small, catdps is "mediocre" in Kara40 because half its bosses are bleed-immune, offtank-cat > pure-cat there — [[d:druid#1425528745061519400]], [[d:druid#1425528992491901059]].
- **[Feral cat]** AoE is essentially nonexistent for cat; Hurricane (balance) is the only real AoE tool for druid, ~190 dps/target achievable with balance gear+feral talents — [[d:druid#1430894701052952576]], [[d:druid#1430895644436074506]]. Community wants Swipe target cap raised from 3 — [[d:druid#1432495193332125726]], [[d:druid#1428069484421189723]].
- **[Boomkin]** Considered fun, competitive but not top-tier; can hit ~2K DPS with back-to-back eclipses/good crits, "comfortably top 10 in a good run," but extremely RNG-dependent (eclipse procs), can't beat mages/warlocks/rogue/feral at their best — [[d:druid#1427651624855146598]], [[d:druid#1427651833798594600]], [[d:druid#1427372403125780552]].
- **[Overall dps ranking, single source]** rogue > warrior, shaman, mage, lock > druid, priest, hunter (per one player's opinion) — [[d:druid#1427618465803665419]], disputed implicitly by others citing druid competitiveness.
- **[Resto]** Considered strong relative to vanilla thanks to Tree of Life form + Regrowth changes; raids want max ~2 (HoT non-stacking limits value of more); a lone tree in a raid is highly valued for the aura even if not top of healing meters — [[d:druid#1425559898732888074]], [[d:druid#1425561284971073667]]/[[d:druid#1426583496297807882]], [[d:druid#1426578177014366351]].
- **[Resto vs Paladin]** With T3.5 5-piece, Holy Paladins' AoE heal outclasses druid trees ("if they have T3.5, you will never reach their healing"); without it, druid should outheal — [[d:druid#1429569391607021709]], [[d:druid#1429570320439840809]], [[d:druid#1429570471464271944]] ("could clear Naxx/K40 with 4 healers" once all pallies have 5/5 T3.5).
- **[Druid general]** Subject to "hybrid tax" — flexible (can switch feral/tank/heal on the fly while leveling) but not best-in-class at any one role, by design — [[d:druid#1426583496297807882]], [[d:druid#1426587244159963257]] (detailed complaint: Balance lacks mana sustain leveling, Cat is squishy/lower dmg than Rogue, Bear is meaty but loses all dmg, out-of-form healing suffers huge pushback).
- **[Leveling]** Feral is agreed to be the best/fastest leveling spec (near-zero downtime); Balance is viable/fun but requires drinking downtime, is "never faster than feral"; comparisons: "like Enhance vs Balance shaman" — [[d:druid#1427651224240652298]], [[d:druid#1433192801616662620]], [[d:druid#1426592225923432510]] (counterpoint: Balance solos elites well at 40+, roots are OP).
- **[Tank]** Druid vs Paladin vs Warrior tanking debated; some feel Bear is "lazy"-adjacent to less-interesting Paladin tanking is "very lazy," Bear/Warrior require more adaptation — [[d:druid#1425254783442354197]]. Some say Warrior/Enh Shaman are simply better tanks than Bear — [[d:druid#1426573933301465148]] (disputed, e.g. [[d:druid#1426580460963762217]] "My guild's MT is a bear and he is the best tank I know").

## Leveling
- **[General]** Druid is "dreadful" 1-10 (before Bear Form quest at 10); recommend rushing to 10 then Bear form quest — [[d:druid#1424254260530643015]].
- **[Stats <10]** Use Wrath almost exclusively pre-10, Moonfire when learned, melee only to conserve mana; at level 7 gear stats barely matter, all gear "garbage" — [[d:druid#1424253556558401556]], [[d:druid#1424254991979380818]].
- **[Feral leveling stat/gear note]** Weapon "chance on hit," speed, and DPS are irrelevant in Bear/Cat form — only stats (Str/Agi/Sta) on a weapon matter, since forms replace weapon damage with claws — [[d:druid#1424255168232423565]], [[d:druid#1424255371697979487]].
- **[10-20]** Recommend going Bear from 10-20 rather than staying caster, due to lack of reliable mana regen and going OOM after 2-3 mobs — [[d:druid#1426222119271923864]], [[d:druid#1426225340736475206]].
- **[20-30]** Take Feral Swiftness at 20 for movement speed; at 30, unlock Travel Form and respec into Balance tree to grab Omen of Clarity (via 3/3 Natural Weapons), then respec back into Feral tree, skipping Feral Swiftness since Travel Form replaced its need — this is the consensus "pinned" leveling path — [[d:druid#1433117020874080408]], [[d:druid#1433144886546137149]], [[d:druid#1425986722948448267]].
- **[30+]** After Omen, follow standard Feral tree; Furor (in Resto tree) is "the absolute last thing you get" — [[d:druid#1433117705468252252]].
- **[Balance leveling alt path]** Level Feral until Moonkin Form is available (~level 30ish via respec), use roots/DoTs, open with Starfire, avoid spamming Moonfire except when running down fleeing targets; Sylvan Blessing + 1pt Owlkin Frenzy helps mana at 40+ — [[d:druid#1431038877224927232]], [[d:druid#1431039120959996014]].
- **[Weapon progression, feral leveling]** Smites Mighty Hammer @18 → Manual Crowd Pummeler @28 → Thornroot/Thornwood Maul @32 (usable to ~60) → Grimlock's Charge @42 (still competitive vs some lvl-60 spears) → Ice Barbed Spear @51 → follow sheet at 60 — [[d:druid#1424760083111350312]], [[d:druid#1431171332678225993]].
- **[Feral hybrid leveling gear stat priority]** Agility > Strength for leveling per one answer; also grab hit gear when possible — [[d:druid#1427205807715385415]]. Others note Str/Agi roughly equal value for cat dps ("pick whatever gives more") — [[d:druid#1431067602729570405]].
- **[Feral tank leveling]** Can go full tank spec from level 10 but it's not necessary/loses talents that help solo; recommended to delay tank talents until you have the Goblin Brainwashing Device (respec token) so you can swap between a leveling build and tank build — [[d:druid#1427224203584864417]], [[d:druid#1427226985826091091]].
- **[Hardcore leveling]** For HC, take the raid tanking build directly since you sacrifice leveling speed but gain safety — [[d:druid#1429398197163397252]]. Swift Taxi discouraged on HC, Swift Travel Form is fine — [[d:druid#1430941623755472968]].
- **[Resto leveling]** Consensus: do NOT go full Resto while leveling — all specs can dungeon-heal fine with an int-gear set in bags; going deep Resto talents while leveling is much slower for no benefit — [[d:druid#1433843338326442117]], [[d:druid#1433843537648160860]], [[d:druid#1433391120058028072]]/[[d:druid#1433391120058028072]].
- **[Race/zone tips]** Alliance Night Elf: Darnassus/Teldrassil area quests (Barrow Den, Ursan Heights/"the Avengers") are notoriously rough for new low-level druids due to healing/fast-respawning mobs — [[d:druid#1424256788399783951]], [[d:druid#1424257421416861896]], [[d:druid#1424257599624450143]].
- **[Dungeon spam leveling]** Turtle gives somewhat less dungeon XP than vanilla expectation but rested-XP uptime via dungeon spam is viable; going back to do low-level zone (grey) quests for rested-XP efficiency is a common but debated meta — [[d:druid#1426234466199797834]], [[d:druid#1426234737873129573]], [[d:druid#1426254885531156581]] (Dixa notes this differs from streamer "dungeon cleave" leveling meta known from vanilla classic).
- **[Twink/leveling gear]** Kodo Hide set from Crossroads vendor (Barrens) is very strong low-level leather set, limited vendor supply — [[d:druid#1431715944962723850]], [[d:druid#1431716730979356863]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Cat, post-1.18 consensus]** Keep Tiger's Fury (TF) up; on bleedable targets keep Rake+Rip up, use Claw as filler, Ferocious Bite (FB) at 5 CP refreshes bleeds via Carnage so re-application is rare; on bleed-immune targets, Shred to 5CP then FB — [[d:druid#1434049016488005653]], [[d:druid#1434049222940033114]], [[d:druid#1434049314430259220]].
- **[Cat CC (Clearcasting) usage]** Always dump Clearcasting procs into Shred rather than Claw or FB, even at 5CP (unless Rip about to expire) — energy efficiency > CP efficiency — [[d:druid#1427967004635037858]].
- **[Cat claw vs shred, gear-dependent]** Claw > Shred with 3 active bleeds (esp. if you can Pounce); Shred slightly better with only 2 bleeds; well-geared/high-AP players increasingly favor Shred (scales better, esp. with Idol of Laceration) — [[d:druid#1426703993593266379]], [[d:druid#1431238981877760042]], [[d:druid#1431238439356993568]], [[d:druid#1432689695149523056]] (Medvedev: experiment with Shred+Laceration on well-geared feral). Disputed at high gear by Thalamier: "the difference is marginal at best and if you're properly geared, non-existent" — [[d:druid#1428072878938521760]], countered by Medvedev's math [[d:druid#1428072642723971198]].
- **[Cat: reshift after FB]** Reshift after FB is worth it as long as not Berserking — worst case lose 20 energy from a bleed tick but gain 60-30 net from TF recast; should refresh TF before it drops rather than let it fall — [[d:druid#1432667143790661632]], [[d:druid#1431395851460149371]], [[d:druid#1431630116252942337]] (detailed: don't PS every GCD, only every ~16-18s when TF about to expire, since rake/rip energy regen offsets need).
- **[Cat opener]** TF > Pounce (from stealth) > Rake > Rip > Claw to 5CP > FB, keep TF up; alternate approach with Idol swap: Pounce > Rake > Claw to 5CP, switch to bleed idol, Rip > Rake > Claw spam — discussed as unresolved which opener order is best — [[d:druid#1426703699757105283]], [[d:druid#1432668364186194020]].
- **[Idol swapping]** Idol swaps trigger a GCD when done mid-combat (patch-dependent; some say swapping during an existing GCD doesn't cost an extra one) — advanced players build macros to auto-swap Idol of Laceration/Ferocity around FB — [[d:druid#1431108759396024360]], [[d:druid#1431110249942614128]], [[d:druid#1432693402431913984]], [[d:druid#1432713919381635123]] (full macro example), [[d:druid#1432691241891397713]].
- **[Boomkin]** Alternate Wrath/Starfire depending on Eclipse (Nature Eclipse→Wrath, Arcane Eclipse→Starfire); casting resets swing timer (no melee weaving possible for casters), Insect Swarm is the exception (doesn't reset swing timer, good for pulling multiple mobs while landing melee hits) — [[d:druid#1425063049378664458]], [[d:druid#1425024420514238465]], [[d:druid#1425052560732979310]].
- **[Boomkin DoTs]** Both Moonfire and Insect Swarm should be kept up in raid; Moonfire itself doesn't add much direct dps increase from its proc but the DoT damage is worth it — [[d:druid#1428654878686711839]].
- **[Resto]** Prehot tanks with HoTs before pull; use max-rank Rejuv (rank 7 spammable indefinitely with good mana) as default, upgrade to Regrowth for burst/tank-swap moments; putting HoTs on undamaged targets is wasted since instant-cast healers (paladins/priests) out-heal the tick — [[d:druid#1428335061941358592]].
- **[Resto downranking]** Healing Touch ranks 1-3 suffer heavy healing-power scaling penalty; only rank 4+ is viable ("scaling is prob like old wow"); Rejuvenation scales ~100% with healing power if talented, ~70% if not — [[d:druid#1425481221730992281]].
- **[Resto mana efficiency trash vs boss]** Use lower ranks (4/7/9) on trash to manage mana, higher ranks fine on bosses with good gear/T3.5 meditation gear — [[d:druid#1430089372677443626]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Cat]** No real AoE tool; best options are ST rotation on fast-dying trash or Swiping as bear if forced — [[d:druid#1430895644436074506]], [[d:druid#1430893229758484520]].
- **[Boomkin]** Hurricane is the only AoE without a target limit for druid; ~190 dps/target achievable with good balance gear+feral talents; snapshots on cast, applies a "Hurricane(1)" debuff whose damage is calculated per application (confirmed via combat log by Micro Jackson) rather than recalculating each tick — [[d:druid#1432020562548494336]], [[d:druid#1432014900065992874]].
- **[Bear AoE threat]** Swipe caps at 3 targets (widely wished to be raised to 4-5); best AoE-threat method is: Maul the primary target, tab-target and Swipe others, pre-fight Regrowth/Rejuv self so healing ticks generate threat on everything you're in combat with, Demoralizing Roar for minor extra AoE threat — [[d:druid#1432288944313077811]], [[d:druid#1428999300129755218]] (early-game Swipe > Maul threat since rage-starved), [[d:druid#1427894205031645234]].
- **[Bear AoE, difficulty]** Bear AoE threat "sucks balls" per multiple posters; Swipe "barely does anything" compared to Consecration (Pally) or Stoneclaw Totem (Shaman); mark targets, ask DPS to wait for at least one Maul before attacking, or "let them die" — [[d:druid#1431667615029399755]], [[d:druid#1427894205031645234]], [[d:druid#1428999300129755218]].

- **[Melee/feral hit cap]** 8% (confirmed by multiple; Turtle reduced from vanilla's melee hit ~9%) — [[d:druid#1426765593901666355]] ("even in original vanilla it was only 9%"), disputed by one tester (Velois) claiming they still miss at 8% but not at 10%, unresolved — [[d:druid#1426763696335622287]], [[d:druid#1426765977047142572]].
- **[Spell hit cap]** ~16-17% total needed in vanilla; Turtle reduced each (melee & spell) by 1%; base spell miss chance always ≥1% — [[d:druid#1425489640114098277]]. Boomkin gets 3% spell hit from talent "Natural Weapons" (some confusion in chat about which talent), needing ~12-13% from gear to hit cap since boomkin has no additional +hit talents beyond that — [[d:druid#1426399064013934683]], [[d:druid#1425459631945289764]]/[[d:druid#1425459631945289764]], [[d:druid#1425426107603222548]] (boomkin BiS hit not achievable until Kara40 gear — Treehide/Verrik).
- **[Cat hit]** Once at 5% hit from gear + 3% from talents = 8% cap, additional hit has zero value; getting to 7-8% is described as easy via a few Blackrock Mountain (BM) runs — [[d:druid#1429826595929198672]], [[d:druid#1429828133191680031]].
- **[Cat crit soft cap]** Described as very high, "like 50%" or specifically ~58%, not reachable with then-current gear — [[d:druid#1429879954321047553]], [[d:druid#1431422295825711237]].
- **[Str vs Agi, cat]** Bleeds (Rake/Rip/claw-bleed) scale with Attack Power, and STR gives more AP than AGI in Turtle, so STR ≈ AGI in value for cat (unlike vanilla where AGI was ~20% better per point); AGI still gives crit/dodge — [[d:druid#1431061079546527754]], [[d:druid#1431064541642493993]], [[d:druid#1430924910527512618]] ("classic: STR 2.64, AGI 2.76 AP/pt, but here bleeds scale with AP so STR at least equal if target bleeds").
- **[Cat AP conversion]** Strength weapon gave 433 AP vs Agility weapon 419 AP in one player's test at same enchant tier, confirming AP-from-Str is present in cat form (contrary to old vanilla assumption that only Agi→AP mattered) — [[d:druid#1426995758699122779]], [[d:druid#1427003246442184794]] ("You do, and you are").
- **[Cat haste]** Haste is one of cat's worst stats pre-T3.5 (white hits are the worst-scaling damage source due to low weapon skill); becomes good once you have T3.5 5-set (haste synergy) — [[d:druid#1427245844725370981]], [[d:druid#1432681941672656966]].
- **[Bear stats]** HP and Armor are most important; Dodge is a negative/zero-value stat for bear (reduces rage generation, no parry/block available, dodge doesn't push crits off the table) — [[d:druid#1428922745852399647]], [[d:druid#1428930563212841081]], [[d:druid#1432306133560725606]] (Micro Jackson's detailed writeup: bear can only Dodge among avoidance stats, unlike Warrior/Paladin/Shaman who dodge+parry+block+generate rage from hits/parries).
- **[Bear Defense]** Defense gives "70-80% less value" for bear than Warrior/Paladin/Shaman since bear can't push crits off the table without huge investment (theoretical max defense stacking discussed reaching ~139-143, still a few points short of "uncrittable") — [[d:druid#1428087601268789288]], [[d:druid#1432323048639299697]], [[d:druid#1432326637130747997]] (itemized list of max-defense pieces).
- **[Bear Dodge/Defense disputed]** Ivina argues dodge/defense still valuable (reduces spiky damage/crit chance, "never rage-starved with ~40% dodge + rage-on-dodge talent"); Lyrazia/Viertakt/Micro Jackson counter that dodge creates inconsistent "reactive" healing patterns and is a net negative; both sides preserved — [[d:druid#1432302031011581995]], [[d:druid#1432305356910104666]], [[d:druid#1432313574847414323]].
- **[Bear haste]** ~1% haste ≈ value of 12 STR; haste is a "really big stat" for single-target bear tanking — [[d:druid#1432273778221518868]].
- **[Boomkin]** ~15 spell power ≈ 1% crit per gear-planner (Kadeejah/Micro Jackson) — [[d:druid#1426693372034289664]].
- **[Weapon damage formula, cat/bear]** final_damage = weapon_damage + (weapon_speed × AttackPower / 14); haste is NOT part of this formula (uses base weapon speed only) — [[d:druid#1431298975591563344]], [[d:druid#1431297744689041418]], [[d:druid#1431300696354984140]].
- **[Multiplicative vs additive buffs]** Community-tested conclusion: feral bleed/Claw damage multipliers (Natural Weapons 10%, Predatory Strikes 20%, Open Wounds bleed bonus) stack multiplicatively, not additively — [[d:druid#1432020562548494336]]/[[d:druid#1431299228214366268]], [[d:druid#1431301549400789012]] (most talent mods described as additive but claw bleed mods specifically multiplicative), [[d:druid#1431303013103829064]] (Medvedev's Kruul log data aligns with multiplicative).
- **[Weapon skill]** Doesn't matter for druid forms — a fixed formula (5× level) applies to Bear/Cat "Paws," independent of trained weapon skill; equip-effects work in form, but "chance on hit" procs and weapon type/dmg/speed do not — [[d:druid#1427019374606159965]]/[[d:druid#1427022374062260254]], [[d:druid#1428828946916577391]], [[d:druid#1431203448166285452]].
- **[Armor scaling]** Armor scales linearly with effective HP up to ~17.2k (some say up to 23k still "somewhat worth it"), diminishing after; exact post-nerf DR formula was undocumented as of this chunk, devs said they'd share it "soon" — [[d:druid#1432306094843236352]], [[d:druid#1432308111083049022]] (Tephus math link), [[d:druid#1434798291589009419]].

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Weapon oils]** Feral can use "elemental" and "consecrated" weapon oils/sharpening stones (these are the options usable while shapeshifting into forms, since equip-effects persist) — [[d:druid#1430848679421411388]].
- **[Enchants don't stack in form]** Enchants (armor kit stamina/armor bonuses etc.) do NOT get multiplied by Bear Form's armor/stat multiplier — only base gear stats on the item get multiplied; enchant bonuses apply flat — [[d:druid#1425973110548463647]] confirmed [[d:druid#1426185475009282120]].
- **[Head enchant, tank]** Druid head-enchant questline (Gift of Ferocity / Rapidity, etc.) is easy to complete once, then costs 3 Bright Dream Shards (~6g) per additional enchant on other head pieces — [[d:druid#1430578410635395295]], [[d:druid#1430578940480589876]].
- **[Boomkin head enchant]** 12 Spell Power still considered the best head enchant for boomkin, with haste "close" as an alternative — [[d:druid#1431175185440051284]], [[d:druid#1431177417762013264]].
- **[Cat leg enchant]** Debate: pinned sheet says Agility, other resource says Haste; consensus haste is BiS specifically for Kara40 5-set haste synergy, otherwise mostly pointless for cat pre-T3.5 due to low weapon skill white-hit penalty — [[d:druid#1427244731032666197]], [[d:druid#1427245844725370981]].
- **[Cat belt enchant]** +6 Agi vs +6 Str for feral cat — considered roughly equal/no simulator exists to settle it definitively — [[d:druid#1432086376807141437]], [[d:druid#1432117976705925162]].
- **[Bear armor/mitigation shopping list]** 4 specific items (Cracked/various database items linked, IDs 61059/60559/18531/18413) said to together add ~2624 bear-form armor when combined with a crafted armor cloak — [[d:druid#1430553366580302006]], [[d:druid#1430551590502400103]].
- **[Lifesteal/on-hit]** Weapon "chance on hit" and "Lifesteal" enchants do NOT proc in Bear/Cat form; only "Equip:" (passive/always-on) effects work — [[d:druid#1428828946916577391]], [[d:druid#1431203448166285452]], [[d:druid#1427022374062260254]] (weapon skill also irrelevant to feral forms).
- **[Vampirism/lifesteal talent, feral]** 7-17% Vampiric-style life-steal from feral gear tested as insufficient to solo Hateforge Quarry bosses solo; considered strong mainly for Paladins with mass-AoE (Consecrate) rather than druids — [[d:druid#1430870199661494353]], [[d:druid#1430875987943624724]], [[d:druid#1430880432685318216]].

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[Elixirs stack check]** Elixir of Greater Nature Power, Dreamshard Elixir, Greater Arcane Elixir, Elixir of Greater Arcane Power, Dreamtonic all confirmed to stack together for boomkin — [[d:druid#1426348084497092618]], [[d:druid#1426348385257918494]]; but later disputed that Dreamtonic/Dreamshard Elixirs specifically DO stack while some others only visually stack (see below) — [[d:druid#1433828439353196618]].
- **[Scrolls do NOT stack]** Scroll of Agility/Strength/Protection do NOT stack with their equivalent buffs (Elixir of Mongoose/Giants, Elixir of Fortitude/food) — they "visually stack" on the buff bar but the higher-value effect (Juju/Elixir) takes precedent and the stat doesn't actually increase further; confirmed via testing by Micro Jackson and Stunfisk — [[d:druid#1433887910083428544]], [[d:druid#1433828476867317990]], [[d:druid#1433843687099728032]].
- **[Buff-slot / "buffcap" problem]** Feral in particular runs into the 64-buff-slot limit due to stacking cat-form buffs (Tiger's Fury, Clearcasting, Shieldrender, etc.) plus a huge raid consumable list; community request (from Thalamier/Medvedev) to reduce total consumable count and make fewer/stronger "super consumables"; devs say it's essentially unfixable on the 1.12 client — [[d:druid#1433734940641464331]], [[d:druid#1433735085768839210]], [[d:druid#1433822085897130026]] (full example consumable list with ~17 items), [[d:druid#1433831131886911488]].
- **[Resto trash mana]** Rank 4/7/9 Rejuv used on trash for mana management, higher ranks fine on bosses when well-geared with meditation gear — [[d:druid#1430089372677443626]].
- **[Nature Resistance]** Tanks generally do NOT need Nature Resistance gear for AQ40 — Druid tanks especially have little use for it; the one fight requiring high physical/nature focus is Viscidus, and druids are considered poor Viscidus tanks (missing magic mitigation, low static threat) — Warrior + poison-resist elixirs recommended instead — [[d:druid#1433813167682748427]], [[d:druid#1433813665479397539]], [[d:druid#1433814464242647062]].
- **[Bleeds immune bosses in MC]** Only Golemagg (giant), Ragnaros, and Thaurissan are immune to bleeds in MC per one answer — [[d:druid#1434682035460636862]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Reshift]** Single-button ability that shifts out then back into current form (used for power-shifting or escaping roots); requested to be a lower-level spell instead of level 60 — [[d:druid#1424532148173537281]], [[d:druid#1427361652831031378]].
- **[Barkskin trick]** Bear tanks can cast the human-form Barkskin every minute; the buff persists into Bear Form and reduces incoming damage at the cost of 20% attack speed — [[d:druid#1432725711852146830]].
- **[Threat basics, bear]** Maul is the core single-target threat generator (toggles over autoattack); a threat meter addon strongly recommended; keep rage semi-full between pulls; healing self pre-pull (Regrowth/Rejuv) generates AoE threat on everything you're in combat with; Demoralizing Roar for minor extra threat/mitigation, often overwritten by warrior shouts and considered low value — [[d:druid#1427893249762132031]], [[d:druid#1428885929678077982]], [[d:druid#1427894740451590248]].
- **[Growl]** Puts you top of a target's threat list — useless if cast on a target with no threat list yet (i.e., don't open a pull with Growl); save for stealing back a mob that a DPS pulled aggro on — [[d:druid#1432722952813543505]], [[d:druid#1432724568094806016]].
- **[Taunt]** Off-GCD; useful to "leapfrog" a caster's early burst threat lead (ask a shaman/priest to open with Earth Shock/Mind Blast, then taunt) — [[d:druid#1432367846968655993]]/[[d:druid#1432367846968655993]], [[d:druid#1432383268447326410]].
- **[Furor]** 5/5 Furor (Resto tree) gives guaranteed rage on shapeshift, letting you pull out of form to heal/pull with a spell then shift back into combat-ready rage — [[d:druid#1427952852009222195]], [[d:druid#1430577105573249137]] (comfort talent for edge cases).
- **[Feral Charge]** Good as an interrupt if you can strafe away from a caster then charge back in; does NOT generate threat, so don't use it to open a pull — [[d:druid#1433148107368960173]], [[d:druid#1433148306586075292]].
- **[Mighty Rage Potion / Enrage]** Recommended to dump rage bar full on pull for immediate Maul + Savage Bite; also stack cooldown trinkets (e.g., Kiss of the Spider from Naxx) for burst threat — [[d:druid#1432381051661062394]].
- **[Vampirism (life-steal)]** Considered weak for druid tanks/solo farming compared to Paladins, since Paladins can AoE infinitely with Consecrate to leverage it — [[d:druid#1430875987943624724]].
- **[Mana as resto]** Resto with T3.5 40% Meditation amulet can spam max-rank Rejuv/Regrowth through an entire fight and self-Innervate; mana becomes more of an issue on trash (chain-pulling) than on bosses — [[d:druid#1430089372677443626]].
- **[Mana, feral tank]** Judgement of Wisdom (Paladin debuff) returns mana on hit, effectively removing OOM concerns for feral/tank without needing to reshift-manage mana; without a paladin, consumables (mana pots) are needed — [[d:druid#1431309045888979098]], [[d:druid#1431309182279094453]].
- **[Nordanaar consumable buff-slot workaround]** Suggested devs could consolidate the ~17-item raid consumable list into 3 "super consumables" (short/medium/long) to free up buff slots — [[d:druid#1433822085897130026]], [[d:druid#1434660717474222171]].

## Tanking specifics
- **[Dodge/Defense debate, both sides preserved]** Camp A (Micro Jackson, Stunfisk, Lyrazia, Viertakt): Dodge and Defense are near-worthless for bear — no Parry/Block, dodge reduces rage generation, defense's "no-crit" threshold is unreachable without absurd investment; stack Stamina/Armor/Strength/Haste/Crit instead, only take Dodge/Defense when it comes attached to otherwise-good gear — [[d:druid#1432306133560725606]], [[d:druid#1432309295026016287]], [[d:druid#1432313574847414323]] (feral only has 3 of 6 tank survivability stats: HP, Armor, Dodge — no Parry/Block). Camp B (Ivina): with ~40% dodge + rage-on-dodge talent, never rage-starved on bosses; crit avoidance genuinely reduces spike damage/healing burden; values Defense past Armor around the 13000 armor mark — [[d:druid#1432302031011581995]], [[d:druid#1432305960134770740]]. No resolution reached; both viewpoints repeated with supporting math from each side.
- **[Threat gear vs mitigation gear]** Advice to actively separate "threat" gear sets from "mitigation" gear sets and swap based on encounter (trash = threat gear, hard mitigation fights = armor/stam gear); Lion Horn of Stormwind is more of a raid-melee buff than personal threat tool — [[d:druid#1429727469958856726]], [[d:druid#1431438751875469414]].
- **[Bear AoE aggro struggles]** Frequently reported problem: DPS pulling before tank establishes threat; general guidance is it's a pacing issue, not purely a threat issue — wait for tank to Maul before attacking, and let overzealous DPS die if they refuse — [[d:druid#1429180534604435726]]/[[d:druid#1429180534604435726]], [[d:druid#1432297651004772523]], [[d:druid#1432401091458371666]].
- **[Weapon choice for tank]] Stats (Str/Sta/Agi + Equip effects) are all that matter; weapon skill, base weapon damage, and speed do not translate through Bear/Cat's fixed claw damage; even a "gray" 1000-damage weapon loses to a stat-stick with useful stats — [[d:druid#1429823295477383198]], [[d:druid#1429823718754095217]].
- **[Boss-specific bear tank tips]** Black Morass last boss hits extremely hard "within 2 seconds of pull" without proper armor trinkets — get armor trinkets (Hatereaver Cog, DM Tribute armor weapon, HFQ armor trinket) before attempting — [[d:druid#1430547261997322330]], [[d:druid#1430547813992890471]], [[d:druid#1430548339803295845]]. Golem boss (BRD, "Lord Roccor") can be soloed at level 60 with an Idol drop chance (25%) from Kara Crypts first boss for the Idol of Laceration equivalent — [[d:druid#1434057256021921934]], [[d:druid#1434057744398291085]].
- **[Nefarian/Chromaggus bear tanking]** Nefarian doesn't hit hard physically (main burst is unreduced breath damage) — "only dealing with a slightly smaller HP pool" than a warrior; Chromaggus considered scarier for bear at comparable gear — [[d:druid#1432763222804402259]], [[d:druid#1432763325883879514]], [[d:druid#1432763468901122049]].
- **[Pull technique]** Preheal self with Regrowth, pull with ranged Moonfire/Insect Swarm/Faerie Fire out of form (Insect Swarm reduces mob-to-hit-you by 2% and costs less mana), then shift to Bear and Swipe+Maul+Demo Shout — [[d:druid#1427910474661367868]] area / [[d:druid#1427911420602876027]], [[d:druid#1427914229494710423]].

## Healing specifics
- **[Tree Form vs Human Form choice]** Tree Form = more efficient/throughput for sustained multi-target HoT healing; Human/caster form better when heavy single-target throughput (Healing Touch) is needed — [[d:druid#1428314263495376967]].
- **[Regrowth mechanics]** Initial heal portion scales weakly (~20%) with healing power but crits often; the HoT portion scales strongly (~95% with talents); Healing Touch scales ~110% with talents; capped on healing one target with only 2 HoTs available in Tree form — [[d:druid#1428316548724166717]].
- **[Rejuv + Regrowth interaction]** Rejuvenation improves Regrowth's healing via the "Preservation" talent — full Rejuv-only spam (no Regrowth) is considered suboptimal by top healers — [[d:druid#1428339171646242906]], [[d:druid#1428337935811346493]].
- **[Swiftmend]** Consumes whichever of Rejuv/Regrowth on the target has the LEAST remaining duration, not chosen at random — [[d:druid#1431102498898186371]], [[d:druid#1431102535753793649]].
- **[Prehotting]** Top healers (per Micro Jackson) apply HoTs to tanks before they pull, rather than reactively; putting a HoT on someone not taking damage yet is often pointless since instant-cast healers will out-heal the tick anyway — [[d:druid#1428335061941358592]].
- **[Set bonus interplay]** Full T2 8/8 set bonus trades away too much raw healing power/stats for 17% more Rejuv ticks; considered not worth it by top players; better to mix T3/T3.5 pieces for the 2/8 and 5/5 bonuses — [[d:druid#1427389460844380331]].
- **[Spirit vs +healing]** For a raid healer with 5+ healers present, ~1 point of Spirit ≈ 1 point of effective healing power via Spirit gearset + Tree of Life aura snapshot mechanic (aura snapshots on entering Tree form, so gearing high Spirit right before pull then swapping to healing gear via ItemRack maximizes team-wide healing-power buff) — [[d:druid#1430111867057209365]], [[d:druid#1430112597725806732]], [[d:druid#1430113096361443429]]. NOTE: later corrected — Tree of Life aura actually "periodically updates itself," so it CANNOT be snapshotted this way — [[d:druid#1430143310647001181]] (Fat Pibble with Air Pods correction).
- **[Balance/off-spec healing]** A geared Balance-spec druid CAN heal dungeons effectively using downranked Healing Touch (mana-efficient) plus higher-rank Regrowth for emergencies — [[d:druid#1432453215970005083]], [[d:druid#1432453472296501391]].
- **[Feral off-heal leveling]** Feral players commonly carry an Int gear set to swap into for dungeon healing while leveling; considered the smoothest leveling experience, viable for healing up through BRD-level content — [[d:druid#1433108224315490324]], [[d:druid#1433108498027249787]].
- **[T3.5 healer competition]** With T3.5 5-set, Holy Paladins can out-heal Resto Druids significantly via a busted AoE-heal set bonus (roughly +59% to their AoE heal per one estimate) — [[d:druid#1428335061941358592]].

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Moonglade "Dreamer's Challenge" solo dragon quest]** Solo quest (confirmed not group content); strategy: Sleep the helper add, Root the dragon, use Line-of-Sight (LoS) behind a pillar/rock to avoid Faerie/poison damage, kill the "faerie darter" adds close-up for HP/MP regen, only attack with Wrath/Starfire (never DoTs) during the dragon's periodic self-stun after it enrages (roughly every 2 breaths); dispel poisons/curses instantly (curse otherwise stuns you ~8s) — [[d:druid#1428350646100426803]], [[d:druid#1428359052173377617]], [[d:druid#1432508789659009196]] (Rethevus/enrage kite reset variant — note dragon can re-engage instantly on enrage, requiring the sleep/root/LoS loop rather than kiting).
- **[Maraudon Princess]** Bossmechanics on Turtle differ from classic guides — she is NOT immune to Nature damage here and periodically "farts" (an AoE) even from range, forcing LoS breaks; strategy: melee/dot when possible, disengage and heal/DoT when she starts "farting," Feral Swiftness works in that boss room; soloable easily at 60 even pre-BiS — [[d:druid#1425149570144272434]], [[d:druid#1425152504869687316]].
- **[Hateforge Quarry, Hatereaver Annihilator]** Soloable with half-resto/half-feral gear specced Resto; watch for OOM/stun (not in Bear form) as main death risks; ~8 minute fight, bring 2 Major Mana Potions, Dragonslayer buff helpful but not required, Scroll of Spirit + mana oil recommended — [[d:druid#1426274369683984487]].
- **[Black Morass last boss]** Very dangerous without proper armor trinkets (can 2-shot fresh-60 tanks); recommend getting armor trinkets first (Hatereaver Cog, HFQ, DM Tribute) before attempting — [[d:druid#1430547261997322330]] through [[d:druid#1430553461606580435]].
- **[AQ40 nature resistance]** Only fight where druids/tanks meaningfully need NR is Viscidus (100% nature dmg); recommend using a non-druid (warrior) tank there with poison-resist elixirs instead of stacking NR gear — [[d:druid#1433813167682748427]] through [[d:druid#1433814865452990508]].
- **[Kara40 ("Tower of Karazhan")]** Considered a technically demanding raid where cat DPS offers less value than an offtank-capable cat, since roughly half the bosses there are bleed-immune — [[d:druid#1425528745061519400]], [[d:druid#1425527381564850237]].
- **[BWL]** All bosses believed bleedable ("bwl bosses should bleed yea") — one report of Rake failing to apply properly on a boss while Rip refreshed fine, suspected debuff-limit or Cursive tracking bug rather than true immunity — [[d:druid#1425592735750033458]], [[d:druid#1425593089988235317]].
- **[Rethevus/dragon boss kite]** When the dragon enrages and resets/rushes the player instantly (kiting no longer works), switch to the sleep-helper/root/LoS-faerie/stun-window Wrath strategy described above rather than continuing to kite — [[d:druid#1432508789659009196]].

## PvP
- **[Feral cat PvP]** Bear Form strongly recommended in PvP vs melee classes ("you will be melted by any melee class" otherwise) — [[d:druid#1428742043164872824]].
- **[Bleed/Open Wounds in PvP]** Bleeds ignore armor, making the bleed build reasonably attractive in PvP even though Shred/Claw discussions mostly focus on PvE — [[d:druid#1428755592671137895]].
- **[Boomkin PvP]** Standing still to hardcast Starfire/Wrath (the Turtle "power boost" playstyle) doesn't translate well to PvP due to the speed of incoming damage; better to drop the Feral tree for full Moonkin form for damage + range, prioritize Mana Efficiency over Gift of Nature since the latter's extra healing isn't affected by gear and is further reduced by PvP dampening — [[d:druid#1433561904009183338]]. Note: later correction that Gift of Nature "no longer only affects base values" (buffed at some point) — [[d:druid#1433569091695673426]].
- **[Resto PvP build]** THICCNICK's ad hoc PvP resto build shared; mainly Regrowth/Rejuv + running, not much Healing Touch use; some players use Balance-tree burst spells (Wrath/Starfire) alongside heals for hybrid damage/healing play — [[d:druid#1427338675254984957]], [[d:druid#1427343668632616960]].
- **[Battle-healer/PvP hybrid build]** "Add chaos" build leverages Feral Charge as an escape/interrupt-stun tool, Wolfshead on head for 15 rage to enable charge+stun combo, focuses on DoTs/HoTs while kiting rather than committing — [[d:druid#1427353429797244938]], [[d:druid#1427354330461372417]].
- **[Nature's Swiftness path]** Considered near-mandatory PvP path (Nature's Grasp / Feral Charge / Natural Swiftness), enhanced further on Turtle since Swiftmend now sits on that path too — [[d:druid#1430586019010580553]].
- **[PvP hit cap query]** Question about PvP hit cap for cat asked but left unanswered in this chunk (contested/open) — [[d:druid#1431333626154455193]].
- **[Race/PvP]** Shadowmeld (Night Elf) has both an active stealth component and a PASSIVE stealth-detection-reduction effect while already stealthed/prowling — meaning Nelf cat druids are inherently harder to detect than other races even without actively using Shadowmeld — [[d:druid#1430666833014423733]], [[d:druid#1430669659405025362]], [[d:druid#1430670081406402612]].
- **[Sap immunity]** Moonkin form is NOT immune to Sap (rogues can't sap "animal" forms like Bear/Cat, but Moonkin is still classified as Humanoid so it can be sapped) — [[d:druid#1427685519596458005]].

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Essential addon stack]** SuperWoW + Cursive strongly recommended for feral cat specifically, to accurately track bleed durations/refreshes via Ferocious Bite (client UI otherwise can't reflect Carnage refreshes correctly) — [[d:druid#1426704550135337000]], [[d:druid#1434176225407926302]].
- **[SuperCleveRoidMacros]** GitHub addon (`github.com/jrc13245/SuperCleveRoidMacros`) recommended repeatedly for conditional macros (creature-type checks for bleed immunity, "swimming" conditional for Aquatic Form, etc.); recommend uninstalling/disabling plain "Roid Macros" to avoid conflicts — [[d:druid#1424529587941146655]], [[d:druid#1424529870334988400]], [[d:druid#1432081329075388596]].
- **[Mana bar in form]** PFUI's "show druid mana bar" toggle requires SuperWoW DLL hookup to function; alternative standalone addon "DruidManaBar" also works — [[d:druid#1427273217185415233]], [[d:druid#1427268058958925904]], [[d:druid#1430857004926107740]].
- **[Combo point/energy tracking]** "Cursive" is the only addon confirmed to accurately track Rake/Rip/combo points including post-FB refresh (though it can fail if debuff-slot overflow occurs on heavily-buffed targets) — [[d:druid#1430280170614423625]], [[d:druid#1433009609681010782]].
- **[Threat/nameplates]** "Threatplates"/"Tankplates" (via SuperWoW) recommended for bear tanks — colors nameplates green/red based on personal aggro status — [[d:druid#1432722863256899674]], [[d:druid#1431983526819139715]] (can't do this without SuperWoW).
- **[Buff/debuff tracking on raid frames]** "Puppeteer" and "RABuffs" recommended for tracking who has MotW/HoTs and dispellable debuffs — [[d:druid#1429863494748078190]], [[d:druid#1428069484421189723]]/[[d:druid#1432041228094144744]].
- **[Trinket swap addon]** "TrinketMenu" is the go-to for managing/swapping trinkets, notifies on CD, distinguishes threat vs mitigation trinkets — [[d:druid#1426995804043874404]], [[d:druid#1426995758699122779]].
- **[Item swapping]** "ItemRack" used for pre-set gear swaps (spirit set for Tree-form aura snapshot attempt, mount-speed set, threat vs mitigation tank sets, etc.) — [[d:druid#1430089372677443626]] area / [[d:druid#1430896049605709834]].
- **[Eclipse tracking]** "Eclipse Monitor" no longer requires SuperWoW but SuperWoW still recommended for Cursive/other features — [[d:druid#1424931183644508202]].
- **[Combo-point sound addon]** "NCB" (name partially garbled in chat) used with sound cues scaled by combo point count; known to sometimes require manual scale/showempty commands to display — [[d:druid#1431239211373166733]], [[d:druid#1431243624816840825]].
- **[Rotation macros]** Extensive CleveRoid/SuperCleveRoid conditional macro examples shared throughout for cat rotation (bleed vs no-bleed Claw macros, Prowl→Ravage macros, idol-swap macros, Reshift+shred macros) — see e.g. [[d:druid#1424526109432873031]], [[d:druid#1424528416996200598]], [[d:druid#1433521844685770993]] (full idol-swapping automated rotation macro).
- **[Auto-decline "auto DPS macro" sentiment]** Multiple experienced players (Micro Jackson) express distaste for fully automated "press 1 button" rotation macros, noting they personally prefer manual play even if technically suboptimal — [[d:druid#1432680562555883601]], [[d:druid#1433521139904282754]].
- **[Cancel-buff automation]] "VCB" addon has an auto-cancel-buff feature (e.g., to strip unwanted buffs like Salvation automatically) — [[d:druid#1433745834125692938]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Grandstaff of the Shendralar]** Does not proc off Heal-over-Time (HoT) effects, only direct casts, at the time of this chunk (bug reported, upvotes requested) — [[d:druid#1429547508765556756]].
- **[T3.5 resto + Eye of the Dead]** Reported buggy interaction (bug report filed, upvotes requested) — [[d:druid#1429559389249081428]].
- **[Boomkin T3.5 3-piece "DoT" bonus]** Tooltip implies an independent damage-over-time debuff from Hurricane's set bonus, but testing showed it's just +50% damage applied inside Hurricane's radius (a second overlapping "Hurricane" application), NOT a real independent DoT — confirmed by Fat Pibble with Air Pods; considered misleading tooltip, no dev response found in-thread — [[d:druid#1433997624075096127]], [[d:druid#1434137344935723050]], [[d:druid#1434144886453702697]].
- **[Engineering pets do minimal damage]** Mechanical Dragonling and Gnomish Battle Chicken tested in-game and found to deal almost no damage under normal conditions (Dragonling ~2 dmg/hit until "enraged" ~70s in, jumping to 85 dmg) — considered essentially bugged/non-functional as advertised — [[d:druid#1431556843544514651]], [[d:druid#1431572201085341759]].
- **[Grenades/engineering in form]** Patch notes claimed Bear/Cat could use consumable engineering items, but live testing showed grenades do NOT actually work while shapeshifted — [[d:druid#1431618759629733889]], [[d:druid#1431545903520419923]].
- **[Idol/trinket swap GCD]** Idol swapping mid-combat now triggers a GCD (changed from patch 1.17 behavior), making rapid idol-swap rotations harder than before, though swapping DURING an already-active GCD reportedly avoids adding a new one — [[d:druid#1432681267580637275]], [[d:druid#1432711611763654678]], [[d:druid#1432693793878048864]].
- **[Weapon skill display]** Un-trained 2H weapon skill causes displayed melee attack value to show as "1" — purely a UI display bug, does not affect actual outgoing damage (irrelevant in Bear/Cat form anyway since weapon skill doesn't apply there) — [[d:druid#1432033249529692161]], [[d:druid#1432040187722207235]].
- **[Root break RNG]** Disputed whether DoTs (Insect Swarm, Moonfire) can break Entangling Roots early — some claim only initial-hit spells break roots (DoTs never do), others report DoT ticks and even the root's own damage occasionally breaking it early; unresolved/anecdotal — [[d:druid#1426807649717977128]], [[d:druid#1426844855073505363]], [[d:druid#1426852724225478696]], [[d:druid#1426948005839376526]].
- **[AI answers unreliable]** A Google AI Overview screenshot about druid spell coefficients was posted and immediately debunked by multiple experienced players as confidently wrong — cautionary note against trusting AI-generated WoW mechanics answers, especially for Turtle-specific changes — [[d:druid#1434207379091619981]], [[d:druid#1434208995878371443]], [[d:druid#1434208082627530794]].
- **[Stealth level mechanic]** "Stealth level" (the mechanic making higher-level stealthed units harder to detect) reportedly does not function at all on Turtle — [[d:druid#1428094304336871444]], [[d:druid#1428099986809421924]].
