## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Holy]** Considered the strongest/most necessary priest spec by role, and the least controversial ("Holy is still the strongest priest spec considering its role") — [[d:priest#1344010059990175795]] (2025-02-25, +confirmed by DemonBoyJr), but also criticized as having received the fewest QoL updates of any healer class relative to Paladin/Shaman/Druid — [[d:priest#1356348264835846335]], [[d:priest#1408843579773816842]] (2025-08-23, Paladins now out-cleanse priests).
- **[Disc/Smite]** Mid-pack raid DPS with chronic mana issues; considered gear-hungry because it competes for every generic caster offset piece and (until 1.18) lacked a dedicated DPS tier set beyond T2.5 — [[d:priest#1397823662085902349]] (2025-07-24), [[d:priest#1408244157788717218]] (2025-08-22, devs promised a Smite tier set "next patch"). Consensus: strong for leveling, 5-mans, and PvP; weak scaling into T3.5/Kara40 — [[d:priest#1315819900903882762]] (2024-12-10), [[d:priest#1342946726729879582]] (2025-02-22, math on Searing Light nerf math), [[d:priest#1390297867519787160]] (2025-07-03, "you grief your guild when not playing Shadow" in Kara40 gearing terms).
- **[Shadow]** Considered mid-pack single-target DPS but "king" for progression/casual raids because of VE healing/mana-battery for the group and the +15% shadow-damage buff to warlocks; has the smoothest gearing path (old high-SP items + dedicated T1/T2/T3/T3.5 shadow-flavored sets) — [[d:priest#1382593433323049031]] (2025-06-12), [[d:priest#1406008664870883509]] (2025-08-16), [[d:priest#1388039868801749074]] (2025-06-27). PvP shadow considered heavily gimped due to VE cast time (pre-1.18), Blackout nerf, and Shadowform-exit GCD — extensive repeated complaint thread, e.g. [[d:priest#1364948820844351498]] (2025-04-24), [[d:priest#1373996404883718217]] (+8, 2025-05-19, "Shadow priest play is literally classic minus").
- **[General]** "All specs are viable" on Turtle's non-elitist raiding scene, but Disc/Holy contribute less unique raid value than a Shadow priest or than other classes' healers — [[d:priest#1344010059990175795]] (2025-02-25), disputed strongly by [[d:priest#1405898633295167539]] (2025-08-15, "no reason to bring disc/Holy rather than 1-3 shadow priests"). Repeated root-cause claim: no Turtle dev mains Priest, unlike other classes, causing slower fixes — [[d:priest#1368738880278368327]] (2025-05-05), [[d:priest#1403627705001443469]] (2025-08-09), [[d:priest#1413067440274800680]] (2025-09-04).
- **[Race]** Human/High Elf best for PvE mana sustain and generally race-agnostic PvE per multiple posters — [[d:priest#1353576109496598558]], [[d:priest#1412041784460316813]] (2025-09-01). Troll best PvP Shadow (Shadowguard procs Blackout stun + cheap spammable 20% healing-reduction curse, harder to dispel than Undead's disease) — [[d:priest#1346479817842888767]], [[d:priest#1380637958687686687]], [[d:priest#1406777193786249216]]. Undead best PvE Shadow DPS (Devouring Plague) but weak vs Paladin-heavy PvP comps since it's a dispellable disease — [[d:priest#1353576109496598558]], [[d:priest#1355575839491751996]].

## Leveling
- **[All specs]** Universal opener: 2/2 Wand Specialization + 5/5 Spirit Tap first, "regardless of your build" — repeated by nearly every leveling-advice poster: [[d:priest#1349074139369046039]], [[d:priest#1354927343189364785]], [[d:priest#1360047653580177420]], [[d:priest#1417169831743983636]] (2025-09-15). Wand damage/dps outpaces early spells until ~Smite Rank 3 — [[d:priest#1349154106849366117]] (2025-03-12, Manxy).
- **[Smite/heal leveling]** "Disc leveling" from scratch is a noob trap because early DPS talents are deep in Disc while core cast-time-reduction/pushback-protection talents for Smite live in Holy; correct order is 2/2 Wand + 5/5 Spirit Tap → Holy tree to 5/5 Spiritual Guidance (~lvl 36-40, "same path for Smite and Healer") → branch into Disc (Searing Light, Purifying Flames) for damage or deeper Holy (Spiritual Healing +30%) for healing — [[d:priest#1331378617153753258]] (2025-01-22, Greendam), confirmed by [[d:priest#1346332822574469120]], [[d:priest#1349074139369046039]], [[d:priest#1361395436165922988]], [[d:priest#1403596492039524493]] (2025-08-09).
- **[Shadow leveling]** Shadow doesn't come online until ~lvl 40-44 (Shadowform + Mind Flay Rank 4 + access to cheap Shadow SP BoEs); respec at 40 (loses 2/2 Wand Spec) or wait to 44 to keep it — [[d:priest#1357498906262372362]] (2025-04-04), [[d:priest#1332161439959613440]] (2025-01-24), [[d:priest#1418229976964137121]] (2025-09-18, "good at endgame, not good at leveling until lvl44").
- **[Spirit Tap caveat]** In a duo/group, Spirit Tap only procs on the killing blow, so it may be worth skipping if a melee partner tags kills — [[d:priest#1352935354180571138]] (2025-03-22, DemonBoyJr); questioned generally in groups with a mage (infinite water) — [[d:priest#1317599493151133716]] (2024-12-15).
- **[Stats while leveling]** Spirit is king for leveling due to Spirit Tap's 100% bonus Spirit; stack Spirit gear, chain-pull mobs to frontload spellcasts, then wand/melee weave to regen — [[d:priest#1331388964594126850]] (2025-01-22, Odi), [[d:priest#1373116828645326848]] (2025-05-17, DemonBoyJr). Healing power/downranking matters less below ~lvl40 (can't stack enough, no room to downrank further) — [[d:priest#1402778925096243240]] (2025-08-07, Greendam).
- **[Bow leveling, Night Elf]** "Superior star shards bow leveling build" mentioned as a meme/niche option [[d:priest#1338142748578025503]]; extensive debate that Nelf bow leveling is weak (no SP scaling on autoshot, 10s CD Searing Shot, deadzone, bag-slot cost) — long thread [[d:priest#1366072242039164969]] (2025-04-27, Autumn) vs [[d:priest#1366208451021373480]] (Misandria), unresolved (see Contested).
- **[Solo play]** Priest solos well vs multiple/higher-level mobs via Shield+self-heal+wand+Fear, but should not use mana-expensive burst since "priest is easiest (maybe second) to level" when played patiently — [[d:priest#1406995835094438100]] (2025-08-18), [[d:priest#1394090458178650152]] (2025-07-14).
- **[Solo elite-killing]** Priest solo-elite rotation: Buff → PW:Shield → Holy Fire → SW:Pain → wand ×2 → if shield breaks: Fear (or Mind Control) then re-shield/heal — [[d:priest#1320479806118170675]] (2024-12-22, Ionnatra).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Smite]** Holy Fire clipping/refresh rule of thumb, tested across 3 rotation variants with little difference: "Cast Holy Fire immediately after an instant Smite (Searing Light proc), otherwise spam Smite" — keeps Purifying Flames buff up almost continuously with 30%+ crit — [[d:priest#1316200984245964871]] (2024-12-11, Phobicus). Macro version: instant Smite if Searing Light is up, otherwise Holy Fire — [[d:priest#1332034430507815052]] (2025-01-23, Iosifa).
- **[Smite]** Math: 7×Smite (14s) ≈1000 dps vs Holy Fire+5×Smite (13s) ≈1015 dps at ~2000 avg dmg/cast — near break-even unless pre-casting HF on groups — [[d:priest#1301765751522459738]] (2024-11-01, phyx), disputed as oversimplified since Holy Fire does 35-40% more than a single Smite while costing ~50% more cast time, so you want to fill Purifying Flames with Smite and only HF when the buff would otherwise fall off or no Searing Light proc pending — [[d:priest#1301775651237531703]] (2024-11-01, Darg).
- **[Shadow]** Standard single-target rotation: keep SW:Pain up, Mind Blast on cooldown, Mind Flay as filler, Pain Spike only as a kill-finisher (does poor sustained dps if opened with) — [[d:priest#1332141183258071161]] (2025-01-24, Fairweather), [[d:priest#1317263316015513640]] (2024-12-14, "pain spike does zero dps, if you open with it you're just wasting mana/gcd"), confirmed [[d:priest#1380266980992483529]] (2025-06-05, DemonBoyJr: "MB into 2x Mind Flay into MB, keep SWP up").
- **[Shadow]** VE/SWP application priority on trash: only DOT mobs you'll get most of the SWP duration on (e.g. 3rd/4th mob of a 4-pack), open damage on the 2nd mob rather than returning to a near-dead 1st mob — [[d:priest#1333294296627023943]], [[d:priest#1333297005467930707]] (2025-01-27, phyx).
- **[Shadow, boss opener]** Spend first 2 GCDs on SW:Pain then VE on bosses; on trash, use a Decursive-style addon to tab-SWP multiple targets since tank will have built threat lead by then — [[d:priest#1378268215163224158]] (2025-05-31, phyx).
- **[Shadow, mana]** Mid-40s DPS test (self-buff only, 2.8k mana pool, spirit tap 5/5): Smite-only 171.1 dps; MB/MF 115.7 dps; Shadowform+MB/MF 151.9 dps; Shadowform+MB/MF/SWP 169.2 dps — Smiter is static one-button dps with stable threat; Shadow is threat-harder-to-control and mana-heavier — [[d:priest#1393291134188650589]] (2025-07-11 / reposted [[d:priest#1400424000689279077]], haunt).
- **[Disc PvP]** Chastise priority target list (highest value first): Affliction Warlocks & Arcane Mages > 2H melee (Arms Warr, Melee Hunters, AP Rets, Enh Shamans) > Dual-wield melee (Fury/Rogue) > everything else; target must be above 80% HP to Chastise — [[d:priest#1383364969432875028]] (2025-06-14, HolyScheiss).

- **[Smite AoE / Lasher farm]** Rank-1 Holy Nova spam is the AoE tool of choice; a full Lasher pack needs ~9 Rank-1 Novas (~6k mana); Disc gets 10% more mana from Mental Strength and 20% PW:S buff from Force of Will pre-pull — build: `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/priest?points=DoACRDaAZAAoAACoFCABAoBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA=` — [[d:priest#1354596333456986244]] (2025-03-27, DemonBoyJr).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Spell hit]** Spell hit table (chance to miss = base 1% inherent + level-diff): same-level mob 4% (cap 3% needed from gear), +1 level 5%(cap 4%), +2 6%(cap 5%), +3 or more 17% ("whopping", cap 16% needed) — [[d:priest#1382724302079524936]] (2025-06-12, HolyScheiss). For leveling gear, only 1 point in the 3/3 hit talent is needed since 16% cap is a raid-only concern — same message.
- **[Shadow stat priority]** WgizmO's stated raid priority: ≥16% Spell Hit Rating > Spell Damage > MP5 > Intellect > Spirit ("Spirit is at the bottom... most gear a Shadow Priest obtains has generally low Spirit") — [[d:priest#1411063959145939136]] (2025-08-29).
- **[Agility/RAP]** Agility gives Priests only +1 Ranged Attack Power (vs the +2 RAP standard other ranged classes get), making bow/wand-weaving for damage "wholly unnecessary"; haste does not apply to auto shot, and Smite/Holy Fire/Mind Blast specifically reset the auto-shot timer — [[d:priest#1302877346168311838]] (2024-11-04, Darg), confirmed [[d:priest#1310109970079023174]] (2024-11-24, Darg: "every class capable of using a ranged weapon gets +2 RAP from agi. Priest is now the odd one out").
- **[Smite spell efficiency]** Mana-per-damage-point math for Smite ranks: Rank7 148mana/313-351dmg=0.47 mana/dmg; Rank8 189mana/403-450=0.46; Rank9 216mana/423-476=0.53 (Rank9 is inefficient, "totally a joke" vs a comparable mage Fireball rank) — [[d:priest#1389476710071664690]] (2025-07-01, HolyScheiss), disputed value further by [[d:priest#1378100818376392906]] (2025-05-31, Greendam).
- **[PW:Shield vs Spiritual Guidance math]** Resurgent Shield gives +heal bonus = 10% of shield absorbed × spell coefficient (e.g. 1600 absorbed → +160 before coefficient); Spiritual Guidance/talented Spiritual Healing (+6% total healing always) outperforms it whenever a single heal exceeds ~2667 raw healing, which is common when geared — [[d:priest#1328076244880982086]] (2025-01-12, soug).
- **[Crit formula]** Priest base spell crit formula (from vmangos core, shared as reference): `2.97 + intellect / (10.03 + .82 * playerLevel)` — [[d:priest#1342982211418194041]] (2025-02-23, "30").

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Enlighten]** Mechanic: 10% chance per Holy/Disc spell cast to trigger; self-only gives +15% spell damage/healing for 8s; on another player, both get +10% for 8s — [[d:priest#1364813230991478894]] (2025-04-24, DemonBoyJr). Enlighten build widely preferred over Champion for raid healing (mana return from Champion is negligible) — [[d:priest#1389606372689051809]] (+5, 2025-07-01, "the current answer is never play champion").
- **[Champion]** Proclaim Champion gives 10% healing bonus to self on the champion target and mana on damage taken by the champion; Revive Champion is a 2.5k-mana, 5s cast, 1-hour CD battle-rez usable only on the champion; considered worth it mainly in progression raids / dungeons where mana is the bottleneck and a MT battle-rez has high value, weak otherwise — [[d:priest#1347891910706204714]], [[d:priest#1347905844842463323]] (2025-03-08, HolyScheiss), [[d:priest#1369230498827735172]] (2025-05-06), [[d:priest#1399969333509357669]] (2025-07-30, Lirya: "Patchwerk progression champ is genuinely good, mana return basically an extra Innervate").
- **[Threat math, Shadow]** Detailed Mind Blast crit threat calculation with -25% Shadow threat talent and -25% Salvation: 3.5k MB crit (200%)=7k threat -50%=3500; VE healing threat (150% of 3.5k dmg)=5250 -25% salv=3937; VE mana-gen threat 350 -25%=262.5; total ~7699 threat in a single MB crit — repeated by Zitronenhaze across multiple dates as an ongoing balance complaint — [[d:priest#1381443790979469312]] (2025-06-09), [[d:priest#1396854313028161566]] (2025-07-21), later patch reduced MB crit multiplier 200%→170% for ~7199 threat — [[d:priest#1398133903843393566]] (2025-07-25).
- **[Fade]** T3.5 (3) set bonus: Fade grants +15% shadow damage for 10s — mathematically equal to +100% for 1.5s, i.e. it mostly offsets the DPS lost from casting Fade itself rather than being a net gain; best used with on-use trinkets, during movement, or fight starts, not on cooldown for "extra" damage — [[d:priest#1357206800369455124]] (2025-04-03, McPewPew). Fade is pressed on pull for extra burst damage or during boss-immune phases/movement windows, not routinely — [[d:priest#1390933590107357214]] (2025-07-05, Eymix).
- **[PW:Shield]** Do not shield Warrior/Druid tanks routinely — it prevents them generating rage/energy from hits (only useful pre-pull or on a DPS at risk, or paladin/shaman tanks) — [[d:priest#1350125463573495819]] (2025-03-14, Shemp), disputed by [[d:priest#1371017279352999936]] (2025-05-11, Shemp: comparison to rogue stun argument, claims warrior tanks with blues generate enough rage anyway).
- **[Fear Ward]** Baseline for all priest races now (Dwarf previously had it as racial, replaced by Avatar); reduces value of Undead's Will of the Forsaken since Horde priests can just self-Fear-Ward — [[d:priest#1344803282211573873]] (2025-02-28), [[d:priest#1346479817842888767]] (2025-03-04).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Priest-as-tank theory]** Shadow priest tanks are "Withstand hit" style tanks like Warrior/Bear/Paladin/Shaman; Discipline priest tanks lean "Cooperative protection" via Shield, which is unique among tank classes since it prevents spell pushback on the priest's own damage dealers while tanking — [[d:priest#1329886315780767765]], [[d:priest#1329887475065425992]] (2025-01-17, RUTHINAS). Priest has soloed/tanked every instance from Deadmines to Hateforge Quarry per this poster — [[d:priest#1308554019052195880]] (2024-11-20).
- **[Reflective Shield, era note]** Pre-nerf Reflective Shield let priests measure damage mitigated (absorb + damage taken ≈ 60-80% of group's total damage taken, comparable to a "regular" tank's share); after its removal this calculation is no longer possible — [[d:priest#1325871691070570568]] (2025-01-06), [[d:priest#1329578031747960852]] (2025-01-17, RUTHINAS).
- **[Mind Control tanking]** MC'd mobs usually can't tank properly, so AoE-ing while one is up causes more party damage than it prevents; best used narrowly, not as a real tank substitute — [[d:priest#1329580271489515600]] (2025-01-17, RUTHINAS).

## Healing specifics
- **[Downranking]** Never use Rank 1 Heal (low-level penalty) or Rank 4 Heal / Rank 3 Flash Heal / Rank 5 Renew (values bugged by a global healing-spell nerf above level 30 on Turtle) — [[d:priest#1346965636810805339]] (2025-03-06, Greendam).
- **[Renew]** Renew is the most mana-efficient heal if all ticks land; place on tank pre-emptively, use while moving between packs, and combo with a big heal (finish a heal sequence with Renew to use the post-cast 5-second-rule downtime productively) — [[d:priest#1354430510805549208]] (2025-03-26, Bunker), [[d:priest#1359144337857843372]] (2025-04-07, Bunker), [[d:priest#1411672451196256317]] (2025-08-31, L-star). 60% of one poster's total healing came from Renew in a raid setting — [[d:priest#1420845548981719080]] (2025-09-25, Daimonoz).
- **[Spec choice]** Two main endgame healing builds: (1) 21 Disc / 30 Holy for Enlighten, (2) deep Holy (20/31) for Proclaim Champion — Enlighten build considered technically stronger, Champion retained by some for the battle-rez and small mana return especially pre-BiS/5-mans — [[d:priest#1384233746848419842]] (2025-06-16, IllegallyParkedFrog), [[d:priest#1399969333509357669]] (2025-07-30, Lirya).
- **[Rank selection, raid healing]** Suggested rank spread: Heal Rank 3 / Greater Heal Rank 1 for raid members vs tanks (more HP); Flash Heal Rank 4 & 7 to compete with sniping Paladins/Shamans; Greater Heal Rank 4 only if assigned to a tank anticipating burst damage; Renew/Prayer of Healing at max rank, situational — [[d:priest#1415664757167358044]] (2025-09-11, Kedaze).
- **[Discipline "healing"]** Disc is not really a heal spec on Turtle; its shields hinder rage generation (works best on Paladin/Shaman tanks) — better to go Proclaim Champion (Holy) for mana regen or full 21/30/0 Holy for real healing throughput — [[d:priest#1422162698208149545]] (2025-09-29, Daimonoz), [[d:priest#1355093191204016208]] (2025-03-28, Bunker: "better off using PW:S on a non-tank... continue DPSing").
- **[Overhealing / triage]** Don't heal anyone above ~70-90% HP (passive regen/between-pack recovery covers it); batch heals — engage mana once, heal everything that needs it, then let the 5-second rule regen kick in — [[d:priest#1378653877729099889]] (2025-06-01, Duciel), [[d:priest#1420845548981719080]] (2025-09-25).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Naxx, Razuvious]** MC'd adds mid-ramp are supposed to auto-aggro the other 3 adds + Razuvious when reaching the circle center; some report this doesn't trigger reliably on Turtle and recommend positioning slightly to the side — [[d:priest#1371570175526043718]] (2025-05-12, PBE(Erzsbet), question, unanswered in-chunk).
- **[Naxx, VE usage by boss]** Gluth: don't move as caster; Thaddius: refresh VE 1-2× max during the fight; Faerlina: dies within one VE duration, cast once and forget; Razuvious/Grobbulus: no need for VE; 4 Horsemen: by the time you're in 36yd SWP range you're often already in Mind Flay range, so it barely matters where you cast VE; Blaumeux void-zone movement / Zeliek chain-range positioning noted as marginal cases; Sapphiron blizzard-phase and KT void zones are the clearer VE-refresh cases but happen rarely enough not to matter much overall — [[d:priest#1367418638159712256]] (2025-05-01, Duciel).
- **[AQ20, Vek'lor/Vek'nilash]** Vek'lor is physical-immune, Vek'nilash is magic-immune; use the Holy capstone (Mind Control-transfer style spell) to shift Shadow Bolt damage to a large-HP target, or bring an Affliction/Demonology Warlock with Soul Link + Shadow absorbs + high-threat Searing Pain; alternatively Mind Blast + Smite from a priest can fill the "transfer" role — [[d:priest#1359078422080389252]] (2025-04-08, Ionnatra).
- **[Kara10/UBRS]** Considered easy/entry progression content where spec choice matters little ("Optimal vs viable" not "Optimal vs trash") — [[d:priest#1331014250402938972]] (2025-01-21, IGG).
- **[Lasher farming (Emerald Sanctum-adjacent dungeon farm)]** One of the best gold farms for a priest without a farming alt; Disc build with 1 point in Holy Nova, ~9 Rank-1 Novas to kill a pack (~6k mana); Herbalism adds ~20% extra income; aegis-style CD nerfs (Aegis of Preservation, dagger proc-per-cast limit) made the historical BWL-trinket version of this farm much weaker than before — [[d:priest#1420896033189396512]] (2025-09-26, Greendam), [[d:priest#1415329787970584767]] (2025-09-10, Longpig, era note on nerfs).
- **[MC (Molten Core), Chromaggus/AQ40 crit gear]** Only meaningful priest crit item mentioned pre-T3.5 was Empowered Leggings (Chromaggus) — relevant for an Inspiration/crit-oriented healing build — [[d:priest#1347459498373283863]] (2025-03-07, Bunker).
- **[Sunken Temple]** Full points in Shadow at low level are still sufficient to heal all dungeons up through Sunken Temple provided Spirit gear is prioritized — [[d:priest#1350125463573495819]] (2025-03-14, Shemp).

## PvP
- **[Shadow]** Considered "not the worst but not the best" PvP spec: one on-demand CC (Psychic Scream), unreliable Blackout, decent anti-caster tools (Silence, Mana Burn); recommended to farm BG PvP gear, stick to groups, use VE from range for sustain — [[d:priest#1386357721149739090]], [[d:priest#1386358252157140992]] (2025-06-22, Jonny Gabagool). VE cast time (pre-1.18) was called the single biggest PvP nerf to the spec because it forces standing still to cast a spell that does nothing on cast and can be instantly dispelled — [[d:priest#1384569369656426548]] (2025-06-17, DemonBoyJr), [[d:priest#1379907940336664696]] (2025-06-04, Tzeip).
- **[Race for PvP]** Troll best for Shadow PvP (Shadowguard: stuns via Blackout proc, adds Shadoweave stacks, decent SP-scaled damage on 3 charges; spammable -20% healing curse hard to dispel); Undead edges out for Disc/Holy PvP (Devouring Plague good in 1v1/world PvP despite easy Paladin dispel) — [[d:priest#1399158076417769513]], [[d:priest#1399243620271390761]] (2025-07-28, DemonBoyJr).
- **[Disc PvP hit]** Only ~3% hit needed for same-level PvP targets (vs 16% PvE raid cap) — [[d:priest#1400568275561087046]] (2025-07-31, DemonBoyJr).
- **[Nelf bow in PvP]** Searing Shot is off-GCD-adjacent, usable while moving, and can proc Searing Light for an instant Smite — useful for maintaining pressure while kiting/chasing, one of the few genuinely good PvP applications of the Nelf bow racial — [[d:priest#1412816842149597355]] (2025-09-03, DemonBoyJr).
- **[Trinket/CC changes]** Trinket changes made fear easier to escape/reduced Wotf's relative value now that all priests have Fear Ward; general complaint that horde has more anti-fear tools than alliance has counters — [[d:priest#1399361005800390676]] (2025-07-27), [[d:priest#1400256326206099669]] (2025-07-31).
- **[PvP burst context]** Priests seen as squishy/low-mobility relative to burst classes (Mage, Destro Lock, Shaman) — recurring complaint that priest defensive toolkit (VE, Fear) is comparatively weak against one-shot-capable classes — [[d:priest#1352606322595467265]] (2025-03-21, Eemo, +variants).

- **[Searing Light rework]** Long-running unresolved debate on how to fix Searing Light: proposals include removing the ICD and reverting mana reduction to 40-80% (many posters), vs a full redesign to a stackable crit-damage/mana-reduction buff (Greendam's proposal) vs simply granting +100% crit damage like other classes' capstones (rejected as "boring") — [[d:priest#1378078375352205403]] (2025-05-30, Greendam), [[d:priest#1378095681020891216]] (2025-05-30, HolyScheiss), [[d:priest#1397978135185719437]] (2025-07-24, Martinez) — no confirmed resolution within this chunk's date range.
- **[Resurgent Shield]** Widely called a bad/PvP-only talent in its current form (mana refund good, but competes for points with Force of Will/Spiritual Guidance and its SP-buff-on-proc math doesn't justify the investment for PvE DPS); multiple full-rework proposals (Greendam's "individual spell, no Weakened Soul", Autumn's "regenerating shield" rework) — [[d:priest#1325977450588540928]] (2025-01-12, Fairweather), [[d:priest#1342948198167089255]] (2025-02-22, Greendam), [[d:priest#1365286898423304323]] (2025-04-25, Autumn) — unresolved.
