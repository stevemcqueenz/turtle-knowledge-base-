## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Priest healing spells (Lesser Heal, Renew, Heal, Flash Heal, Prayer of Healing, Greater Heal, Desperate Prayer, Lightwell) had base healing above level 30 AND spell-power scaling for all ranks cut 15%, explicitly to push healers into deep Holy (5/5 Spiritual Healing restores parity) — [[d:priest#1425815068720758784]], [[d:priest#1428434194568057033]] (30, 2025-10-17; "if you go 5/5 you're at the same healing as before" [[d:priest#1425818939153322024]]).
- **[General]** Full list of custom spell-coefficient/cost changes vs vanilla (dug from patch notes): Mind Flay SP coeff 45%→75%; Mind Blast 42.85%→60%; Pain Spike 42.85%→25% and CD 30s→40s; Holy Fire initial-cast coeff →100% (was 60% in the first pass) +10% base dmg; Smite +10% base dmg and mana cost cut on ranks 3-9 (e.g. 300→240 mana at rank 9); PW:Shield SP coeff 10%→35% — [[d:priest#1427076111866790032]] (Alaserus, 2025-10-13, +1).
- **[General]** Fear Ward and Lightwell are now baseline for all priests (race no longer matters for these); Disc was reworked into a genuine DPS spec built around Smite; Shadow buffed vs. Classic — [[d:priest#1435646400791838852]] (IllegallyParkedFrog, 2025-11-05).
- **[Holy]** Champion buff (Proclaim/Empower Champion) duration was extended from 5 min to 10 min after community pressure — [[d:priest#1425999123554766992]], [[d:priest#1426011721708867624]] (L-star, 2025-10-10).
- **[Shadow]** Vampiric Embrace had a cast time added, then reverted ~9 months later — [[d:priest#1438563859630919825]], [[d:priest#1440344733745545258]] (Izmo, 2025-11-18).
- **[Shadow]** Shadowform now grants a passive 15% in-combat mana regen, making shadow far less mana-starved than Classic (still tight until Naxx gear) — [[d:priest#1436363609696370829]], [[d:priest#1436363730287071364]] (Zitronenhaze, 2025-11-07).
- **[Shadow]** T3 6-piece set-bonus proc was changed to Shadow-school damage "last patch," causing it to be resisted/immune on high shadow-resist targets and ~3 of 9 Kara40 bosses — [[d:priest#1438548838540644414]] (2025-11-13); bug report on the proc filed at https://turtle-wow.org/bug-report?id=15128 [[d:priest#1438815581221421056]].
- **[Holy — leaked/upcoming]** New talent screenshot circulated, "Book of Prayers": *"Whenever you cast a healing spell, you regain [15/30]% of its mana cost if it is not a repeat of a previous healing spell"* — [[d:priest#1440791138440319081]] (Jamey, 2025-11-19, +6 reactions; image transcribed). Reception mixed: seen as reinforcing the existing Renew/Flash-Heal rotation meta rather than fixing Holy's core issues (no better AoE heal, PW:S/Improved Renew still base-value only) — [[d:priest#1440809774899007620]] (Greendam), [[d:priest#1440995480510402580]] (Zeraya), [[d:priest#1441094329543295077]] (Hadjo, calls it "that leaked talent").
- **[General]** Devs are seen as slow/absent on priest balance ("priest is the only class without an attached dev"); Blackout, Devouring Plague CD, and Shadow AoE requests have gone unanswered for a long time — [[d:priest#1425570671286550538]] (Xerq), [[d:priest#1440159057343156267]] (30, 2025-11-18, +12).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Holy]** Consensus that Holy is currently the weakest of the three healing classes ("pala > druid/sham > priest") yet still functions as raid-viable given other healers were also nerfed — [[d:priest#1440957032432795709]] (30, +8), [[d:priest#1427635167798886514]] (Koys, +25 reactions, "priest is still the top 1 healer... to compensate the nerf of other healing classes").
- **[Disc]** Disc is a legitimate DPS spec on Turtle (unlike vanilla), competitive with casters when gear/talents line up, but contributes far less raid utility than Shadow (no group heal/mana) and is called "least helpful of the 3" by some — [[d:priest#1435364550370398372]] (Exhale), countered by [[d:priest#1435533510298304583]] (notallowed, keeps pace with arcane mages/aff locks) and [[d:priest#1435393684274155672]] (Qurv, a full-Naxx-BiS disc beat their shadows in early Kara40).
- **[Shadow]** Widely described as "support DPS" rather than a top parser: provides group mana return + Vampiric Embrace healing + a shadow-damage-taken debuff for casters, at DPS below most pure classes — [[d:priest#1434690003878805524]] (L-star), [[d:priest#1427744000206700755]] (Vellasta, "top support spec but bottom dps"). Individual results vary hugely by fight/gear: some report top-3-8 DPS + top HPS in guild raids [[d:priest#1427967040102207538]] (Daimonoz), a shadow priest logged 2nd-overall raid DPS (1,689.7/s) on Upper Karazhan per a shared parse screenshot [[d:priest#1434795697265315870]] (肌肉Jiroushaman, 2025-11-04, +6; MF(dot) 46.3%, MB 15.6%, SW:P(dot) 14.1% of damage) — while others report "0 spriests in top100" on MC-era Naxx logs [[d:priest#1438992922447183915]] (Zitronenhaze).
- **[Shadow]** Haste gives zero DPS value to Shadow (only shortens Mind Blast's cast, not the GCD) — [[d:priest#1436098795644387559]] (Greendam); Mind Flay would need ~13% haste for a 4th tick per client-side testing, "not viable" — [[d:priest#1438517763936227338]] (irotos).
- **[General]** Undead 2%-vs-Humanoid/Undead racial is called the single biggest racial DPS edge for Shadow, especially relevant in Naxx — [[d:priest#1429513089807548497]], [[d:priest#1430166784677515455]] (Zitronenhaze).

## Leveling
- **[All specs]** Standard order regardless of eventual endgame spec: 2/2 Wand Specialization → 5/5 Spirit Tap (Shadow tree) → pour points down the Holy tree to 5/5 Spiritual Guidance (turns Spirit into Spell Power) → then diverge deeper into Holy (more healing, for dungeon-heavy players) or Disc (more damage, for solo questers) — [[d:priest#1424820220966604822]], [[d:priest#1431788257900892231]] (Greendam), confirmed repeatedly by [[d:priest#1425178435629875402]] (Snackz) and dozens of similar answers throughout the chunk.
- **[Smite/Disc]** Smite priest is called the best/fastest leveling spec 1-60, "giga buffed" here versus vanilla where you'd go Smite only until 40 before swapping to Shadow — [[d:priest#1426107866238156840]] (Greendam, 2025-10-11).
- **[Shadow]** Shadow doesn't "come online" until 40 (Shadowform) and really 44 (Mind Flay rank 4); leveling in Shadow before level 42 only meaningfully uses the 2 Wand Spec points, and deep-Shadow kill speed is roughly half of Smite's after 30, only partially catching up post-40 — [[d:priest#1427441712506077356]], [[d:priest#1427444376367530038]] (Greendam, 2025-10-14). Summary: "wands very potent early through mid-30s; smiter very strong 1-60; shadow gets good after 40, great after 44, excellent in dungeons/party play" — [[d:priest#1435209728266338354]] (haunt, 2025-11-03).
- **[Stats]** Spirit is BiS while leveling (mp5 + Spirit Tap synergy + Spiritual Guidance spell-power conversion); "stack as many as you can" — [[d:priest#1428272774643978312]] (Greendam), [[d:priest#1424846576496545912]] (spirit tap + spiritual guidance "insane synergy"); Spirit Tap is not strictly mandatory if healing-focused with good Spirit gear and no downtime needed, per a counter-example duo — [[d:priest#1427045389617598544]] (Dreamhaze).
- **[Gear]** Melee-weave with a slow 2H staff between wand shots — wand shots don't reset the melee swing timer, adding up to ~30% extra damage at low levels — [[d:priest#1430606679807889511]] (Greendam, 2025-10-22). Wand upgrade path: Greater Magic Wand (AH) early → Cookie's Stirring Rod (Deadmines boss) or Gravestone Scepter (BFD quest, carries to ~30, no RNG) — [[d:priest#1436968060119421018]] (Valaen), [[d:priest#1436055370169712821]] (Greendam).
- **[Racial/leveling]** Undead: train Devouring Plague to max rank if racing to undead; UD/Troll best for PvE/PvP shadow respectively; Alliance racials weak for Shadow except Nelf (Starshards+Shadowmeld) for PvP — [[d:priest#1423671122561339485]] (DemonBoyJr, +1).
- **[HC]** For Hardcore leveling, Wand Spec 2/2 + Spirit Tap 5/5 are treated as mandatory; going Shadow before Shadowform is discouraged since MF has no pushback resistance and burns mana fast once shield drops — [[d:priest#1436917703242944552]] (irotos, 2025-11-09).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Smite/Disc]** Core loop: Holy Fire → Smite ×4, resetting the sequence if 10s pass without completing it (macro: `/castsequence reset=10 Holy Fire, Smite, Smite, Smite, Smite`) — [[d:priest#1430589489633431643]], [[d:priest#1430602666861396172]] (Greendam, 2025-10-22). Leveling opener: prepop PW:Shield, open Holy Fire, Smite to ~50%, apply SW:Pain, wand the remainder — [[d:priest#1428392666965475338]] (Baldrick).
- **[Shadow]** Open with SW:Pain + Vampiric Embrace to give the tank threat headroom, then cycle Mind Blast → 2×Mind Flay → repeat, prioritizing MB on cooldown — [[d:priest#1430465373366648873]], [[d:priest#1433119108936761416]] (Izmo), [[d:priest#1438856319212847178]] (Daimonoz). At 4/5 Improved Mind Blast the CD (6s) lines up exactly with 2 Mind Flays.
- **[Shadow]** Fade timed to resnapshot SW:Pain with 1pt Improved Fade covers a full SWP + 1 MB + 2 MF window at +15% damage; after initial skepticism ("always worse than a GCD of damage"), live testing across raids/dummy confirmed a net DPS gain when paired with a snapshot-friendly trinket (Eye of Dormant Corruption) or the T3.5 3-set — [[d:priest#1441591696784756817]] (Zitronenhaze, initial doubt), [[d:priest#1441865417495810159]], [[d:priest#1444088076384338121]] (Zitronenhaze retraction, 2025-11-29), [[d:priest#1441870850482503710]] (Mendeleev).
- **[Holy]** Raid healing with assignments: tank healer keeps Renew up + precasts rank-1 Greater Heal, escalates to max-rank Flash/Greater Heal for spikes; without assignments, "meta" play is spamming Flash Heal to snipe whoever needs it — [[d:priest#1428292872272089098]] (SlekkeLekke, 2025-10-16).
- **[Holy]** Keep raid members at 80-90% HP rather than topping to 100% to conserve mana; the missing 10-20% "almost never" causes dungeon deaths — [[d:priest#1428691896514908271]] (Daimonoz).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General]** Spell hit cap for PvE is 16% (6% from gear + 10% from the Shadow talent line), reduced to 6% needed from gear if a druid's Emerald Blessing/Wisdom buff is up — [[d:priest#1429309438174564412]] (AȥυɾҽSƙყ), [[d:priest#1436825901559054576]] (Zitronenhaze).
- **[Shadow]** Priority is Hit-cap > Spell Power > Crit; Haste does "literally nothing" for Shadow DPS — [[d:priest#1436098795644387559]] (Greendam). Crit-to-SP value estimated ~1% crit ≈ 1sp baseline (like Classic), rising to ~2-4sp once you have the T3 6-set (more Mind Blast casts = more crit rolls) — [[d:priest#1436133338896400424]] (Zitronenhaze), [[d:priest#1433513589582598165]] (Greendam, "1 crit = 3sp... shouldn't be a huge different here since both mind blast and mind flay got coef buff"), [[d:priest#1440808869797560330]] (Greendam, "crit ~=~3sp, 4spirit ~=1sp"). 1% crit ≈ 59.24 Int per calculated conversion — [[d:priest#1436900142006669342]] (Mendeleev).
- **[Shadow — Spell Pen]** Only matters against a few high shadow-resist bosses (Mephistroth ~200 base SR, Kel'Thuzad ~100; most other bosses 10-40); Mephistroth needs ~75 SR removed from gear after Curse of Shadow (-125) — worth roughly 25 spell power's worth of DPS per 1 point of penetration against him — [[d:priest#1436138912954454148]], [[d:priest#1436470618920063036]] (Zitronenhaze). Mind Flay is a "binary" resist spell (each tick fully lands or fully resists), so under-penetrating causes noticeably more full-resisted ticks/lost GCDs than Mind Blast — [[d:priest#1436260006545719376]] (Greendam), log analysis by [[d:priest#1436255342270091335]] (Mendeleev, lost ~23% of Mind Blast damage at only 20 pen vs. Meph).
- **[Holy]** Renew's untalented healing coefficient is actually ~80%, not the 100% shown by the wiki/TheoryCraft addon; with 5/5 Spiritual Healing + other Holy talents it reaches ~112% (Heal rank2 ~96%, Flash Heal 37%→47.8%) — measured live by testing before/after healing power — [[d:priest#1427140530994806844]], [[d:priest#1427162017315164160]], [[d:priest#1427172253866524703]] (L-star, 2025-10-13). Greendam separately warns the community TheoryCraft addon has incorrect priest coefficients for Turtle — [[d:priest#1428434743111848048]].
- **[Holy]** PW:Shield's SP scaling coefficient was buffed 10%→35%, but the Improved PW:Shield and Force of Will talents only increase the spell's BASE value, not its SP scaling — full formula `942 * (1 + 0.15 IPWS + 0.2 FoW) + healing_power*0.35`, meaning all 8 points across both talents only add ~330 flat shield (measured: 1293 untalented → 1622 with both talents at ~1000 healing power) — [[d:priest#1427125476564140102]], [[d:priest#1427126126756630529]], [[d:priest#1427129205245739041]] (L-star, 2025-10-13). Improved Renew is likewise base-value-only and near worthless unless spamming max-rank Renew for parsing — [[d:priest#1432849290006499498]] (Greendam).
- **[Holy]** Holy Nova's coefficient is only ~7.14%, so spell crit is prioritized over spell power for a dedicated AoE/Nova set — [[d:priest#1446692096135401512]] (Vuln, 2025-12-06).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[General]** Fade is a flat, temporary threat reduction — the reduced threat returns once it expires; smart to use pre-emptively on pull so mobs missed by the tank don't latch onto minor healing threat — [[d:priest#1423720492048842943]] (Greendam).
- **[Holy]** Proclaim Champion (base) is tankier/mana-returning for its target, but the priest personally only gets mana back from the target's damage taken if the priest is specced Shadow ("spec heal, put champ on tank, respec shadow, profit") — [[d:priest#1425959707767734292]] (Duciel). Empower Champion's flat +5% damage is better placed on the top DPS than the tank since tank threat isn't purely damage-based — [[d:priest#1425957576612511835]] (DemonBoyJr).
- **[Bug/exploit]** Casting Champion while healer-specced, then respeccing to Shadow (or vice-versa) retains the buff's 5% mitigation/mana-return for the raid duration — an acknowledged, unpatched exploit as of Nov 2025 — [[d:priest#1429434368925110464]], [[d:priest#1429435846225952840]] (Greendam, 2025-10-19); the buff disappears only if either the tank or priest relogs — [[d:priest#1429436171293036644]].
- **[General]** The Champion buff overrides other class buffs (Shadow Protection aura, totems, Mark of the Wild, Magic Protection potion) — they can't stack — [[d:priest#1427615694249529365]] (Duciel).
- **[General]** Buff cap: 32 visible slots, 64 total (invisible) slots — tanks/ret palas/enh shamans commonly hit visual cap; a togglable buff (mount, Blade Flurry, etc.) pushed into an invisible slot can get stuck on until relog, so avoid buffing melee/tanks who don't need Renew/Shield/resist buffs — [[d:priest#1435577623198826516]], [[d:priest#1435581154953662527]] (30/Greendam, 2025-11-05).
- **[Shadow]** Mind Vision rank 2 has a ~300yd range vs. rank 1's ~100yd; can't scout across separate world "shards" — [[d:priest#1428455114288664702]], [[d:priest#1428461271782785146]] (Greendam).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Shadow]** Priests (usually Shadow, sometimes Disc) can tank Twin Emperors in AQ40/Kara40: need ~200-315 shadow resistance and to drop threat-reduction talents and Salvation; Disc-tanking is more viable but produces spikier damage and needs much more hit for Smite — [[d:priest#1441148898981707970]], [[d:priest#1441149606133108917]] (Misandria). A full Excel-planned gear set reaching 315-345 total shadow resistance was shared (goal: 200 minimum): Preceptor's Hat, Eidolon Talisman, Shoulderpads of Prophecy, Juno's Shadow, Garb of Royal Ascension, Burrower Bracers, Handguards of Transcendence, Runed Stygian Belt/Leggings/Boots, Ring of the Elder Warden, Peridot Circle of Shadow Resistance, Demon's Blood trinket, Anathema, Serpentine Skuller wand, plus Prayer of Shadow Protection (+60) and Gift of Arthas (+10) — [[d:priest#1441244848643375295]] (IllegallyParkedFrog, 2025-11-21; image transcribed).
- **[Disc]** The final boss of Hateforge Quarry can be soloed by a Disc priest, but it's "a hard gear check" — kill the two 10k-HP adds one at a time as they spawn using basic elixirs (no flask needed), viable "after CC2" class changes — [[d:priest#1424296554872963163]], [[d:priest#1424297461920567296]] (Greendam, 2025-10-08).
- **[Shadow]** Shadow priests can also tank the Chess event in Kara40 — [[d:priest#1428028181935292546]] (Mendeleev).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Assignment-based tank healing: keep Renew up + precast rank-1 Greater Heal, escalate to Flash/max Greater Heal for damage spikes; group healing uses pre-emptive Renews on ticking damage, shields to prevent caster pushback, and rank-1 Prayer of Healing for AoE damage — [[d:priest#1428292872272089098]] (SlekkeLekke, 2025-10-16).
- **[Holy]** Downranking rule: don't go below Heal rank 2 without ~200+ healing power, since lower ranks below that threshold get a mana-efficiency penalty — [[d:priest#1430698914775830539]] (Xerq).
- **[Holy]** Keep the raid at 80-90% instead of 100% to save mana; missing 10-20% health rarely causes deaths in dungeons — [[d:priest#1428691896514908271]] (Daimonoz).
- **[Holy]** Pre-shielding is fine while walking to a pull at full mana, but bad against Warrior/Feral Druid tanks since PW:Shield blocks rage generation — roughly 12 rage lost per 1k absorbed against a level-60 mob's opening hit — [[d:priest#1426238485303394435]] (Duciel), [[d:priest#1426239198679273472]] (avoid shielding warrior/druid tanks except pre-pull or emergencies).
- **[Holy]** Spiritual Healing (1pt, unconditional +6% healing, doesn't affect Renew) is strictly better than Swift Recovery (5pt, conditional +6% Renew-only) — the latter is "really really bad"; Improved Renew and Improved PW:Shield both only affect the spell's BASE value (near-worthless except for max-rank parsing) — [[d:priest#1432849290006499498]] (Greendam, 2025-10-29).
- **[Holy]** Enlighten: casting it grants both caster and target a small holy-damage proc (10% chance) plus a temporary +spell-damage% buff to both; the buff is lost on zone change and reportedly when swimming, and cancels Water Walking even under a shield — [[d:priest#1443112481026478171]] (30), [[d:priest#1443125271300669501]] (Xerq).
- **[Holy]** Champion-buffed tank healing example numbers: max-rank Renew ≈1500 on the tank, Heal rank4 ≈1700 (no crit) at ~400-500 healing power with Improved Healing on Champion — [[d:priest#1428690829610057779]] (Daimonoz).
- **[PvP]** In battlegrounds without threat mechanics, Holy priests should prioritize self-preservation > protecting teammates > DPS, since enemies will focus healers immediately once identified — [[d:priest#1426927342332280923]] (DrSlump).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Kara40] Faerlina — Mind Control:** MC the first add on pull and silence immediately (5yd range) to stop Poison Volley and remove the need to tank that add; time follow-up MC either right after a Rain of Fire cast (so you don't get roasted mid-channel) or ~20s before enrage if raid DPS is lower — [[d:priest#1432402678176157847]] (Greendam, 2025-10-27).
- **[Kara40] Mephistroth:** ~200 base shadow resist, 125 after Curse of Shadow, needs ~75-110 more penetration from gear/enchants to land reliably; Mind Flay's binary-resist nature makes it noticeably weaker than Mind Blast when under-penetrated; Eye of Moam can be popped twice — on pull and to "zerg" phase 2 — [[d:priest#1436138912954454148]], [[d:priest#1436149292875382814]] (Greendam/Zitronenhaze).
- **[Kara40] Mephistroth adds:** the summoned crystals have no shadow resist, so Shadow/Holy priests can still use Holy Nova effectively against imp spawns — [[d:priest#1436149274801864804]] (Shadurloc).
- **[Kara40/general]:** T3 6-set's shadow-school proc and the T3.5 5-set "disease" debuff are both fully immune on ~1/3 of Kara40 content (bosses like Anomalus/Rapturan/Chess and related trash) — [[d:priest#1438552505175773284]].
- **[Kara40] Anomalus/Gnarlmoon/Kruul:** Shadow priest DPS "almost trivializes" these encounters — [[d:priest#1428028181935292546]] (Mendeleev).
- **[Hateforge Quarry] Final boss:** solo-able by Disc priest — see Tanking specifics above.
- **[DM:E] Lashers/Hydrospawn:** the signature priest gold/leveling farm from ~level 50-54+ using Shield + Holy Nova on packs; the Elemental boss Hydrospawn in the same area is also soloable once geared — [[d:priest#1432727974754521100]] (YN), [[d:priest#1432728962638811288]].
- **[Maraudon]:** the offhand+dagger combo from Maraudon Purple gives +21 Int, +10 Spirit/Stam and is recommended to hold until level 60; Princess is soloable by a geared smite priest similarly to Hydrospawn — [[d:priest#1430936443072675951]] (Mat), [[d:priest#1446229592120758332]] (Snackz).
- **[Karazhan Crypt/Kara10]:** drops the Scythe (notable shadow weapon) and a strong low-level trinket from a specific quest — [[d:priest#1425163744375345263]] (Greendam).
- **[Naxx]:** Sapphiron's aura reportedly deals no damage on Turtle (bugged/changed), making Renew-only spam ideal on that fight since Renew doesn't consume some absorb-shield trinket procs — [[d:priest#1435675506749210674]] (Greendam).

## PvP

- **[Shadow]** Troll is considered the best race for shadow priest PvP overall; Nelf is the best Alliance PvP race for priest (Shadowmeld + buffed Starshards, bow is secondary) — [[d:priest#1440395622929924177]] (Xerq), [[d:priest#1427506275792719942]] (Greendam).
- **[Shadow]** Blackout (shadow-damage chance-to-stun) is explicitly PvP-only and, at only a 2s stun, is called "almost useless" and needing a buff/revert to a longer stun — [[d:priest#1424674352330444830]] (Tzeip), [[d:priest#1438552505175773284]] (Greendam wishlist item: revert Blackout to 3s stun).
- **[General]** Current PvP trinkets remove ALL effects on use (not just class-specific ones like in vanilla), which changed CC-timing play broadly — [[d:priest#1440676406890594434]] (Zuley). Will of the Forsaken (Undead) has a built-in immunity window that blocks pre-casting Mind Control on a feared target using it — [[d:priest#1440386666157310054]] (L-star).
- **[General]** Rogues are considered extremely oppressive vs. priests currently ("broken," "pop lip [invisibility] or you stand no chance") — [[d:priest#1426334545799806986]] (Greendam), corroborated by multiple later messages about rogue silences chain-locking spriests.
- **[Racial]** Desperate Prayer (Human/Dwarf) needs a GCD and can't be used while silenced/locked out of the Holy school, unlike a healing potion, making it weaker than it looks on paper; Devouring Plague (Undead) gives an instant ~1200 self-heal + damage-over-time with no mana cost but a long CD and can be dispelled — [[d:priest#1425357263790407743]] (L-star), [[d:priest#1425347819715166279]] (Zent).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Mouseover]** pfUI has built-in click-cast/mouseover functionality via `/pfcast`, though it can be unreliable (not true retail-style mouseover in 1.12); SuperWoW + SuperCleveRoidMacros is recommended for reliable `[@mouseover]` syntax macros — [[d:priest#1423944465361535087]] (Greendam), [[d:priest#1424790424287187004]] (Vascher).
- **[Healing]** Puppeteer is widely recommended for beginners (heal prediction, HP-loss tracking, click-cast frames, targetless rez) — [[d:priest#1430301336456396943]] (IllegallyParkedFrog). "NotGrid" (a Grid fork) is the common raid-frame addon — [[d:priest#1425157370094551101]] (Snackz).
- **[Dispel]** Rinse is recommended for dispel tracking, but caution: Mind Control has appeared as a dispellable "buff" on some fights (e.g., Nefarian), so blind Rinse spam can accidentally strip a rogue's Salvation/other buffs — [[d:priest#1429080998120915015]] (Duciel), [[d:priest#1429146212774187250]] (L-star).
- **[DoT tracking]** Cursive is called the most accurate SW:Pain/dot-tracking addon available — [[d:priest#1444627632527245413]] (Duciel), [[d:priest#1444703254171619410]] (irotos).
- **[Rez]** LazyRes (with a pfUI-specific fork) allows mass-rezzing dead players without targeting each one — [[d:priest#1441854469217193985]] (Greendam).
- **[Numbers]** Mik's Scrolling Battle Text shows who healed you, Renew ticks, and overheal amounts as floating text — [[d:priest#1425856033875689545]] (t0uchm3).
- **[Meters]** Built-in "Shagu" meter is lightweight (no FPS hit) but its default view resets per-boss; DPSMate (needs SuperWoW) shows full-raid data but can tank FPS — [[d:priest#1433730377222193172]] (Faithroll).
- **[Boss timers]** BigWigs is the DBM-equivalent used for boss mechanic warnings (e.g., Mephistroth's silences) — [[d:priest#1439508606700752966]] (Faithroll).
- **[Theorycraft]** The community "TheoryCraft" addon has outdated/incorrect priest spell coefficients for Turtle; use with caution — [[d:priest#1428434743111848048]] (Greendam).
- **[Rotation macros]** Nampower enables "spell queueing" (e.g., queueing a Mind Flay recast); extensive debate on whether double-tap-to-cancel-cast beats manually timing the Mind Blast/Mind Flay weave — Classic-era testing cited (~30 DPS lost per early clip on a WotLK dataset, no-channel macros costing ~200ms vs. ~50-70ms manual) concluded manual timing is superior for min-maxers despite being harder — [[d:priest#1438543729278324899]], [[d:priest#1438541866097184859]] (Zitronenhaze, 2025-11-13).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Racials]** Undead: Devouring Plague (instant ~1200 self-heal + DoT, ~900 mana/3min CD, dispellable) + Touch of Weakness, plus a +2% damage buff vs. Humanoid/Undead that's the single biggest racial DPS edge for Shadow — [[d:priest#1429512976401961153]] (Greendam). Troll: Shadowguard (reflect shield, 3 charges) + Hex of Weakness, +5% damage vs. Beasts, best race for Shadow PvP — [[d:priest#1444421127790268438]] (Xerq). Dwarf: Desperate Prayer + Stoneform. Human: Desperate Prayer + Feedback (mana-burn-style reflect); called "the worst" priest race for its weak racial value — [[d:priest#1425357263790407743]] (L-star). Night Elf: Starshards (buffed shadow-school ranged DoT, ~2k damage over 6s at 60) + Shadowmeld, plus the ability to equip a Bow — the bow has a hunter-style deadzone and is lower DPS than a wand at the same level until high-tier bows, mostly "flavor"/PvP utility — [[d:priest#1427363848641576991]] (Greendam). High Elf: Grace of the Sunwell ("mini Power Infusion") + cheaper Smites + mana restore.
- **[Gold]** DM:E Lasher farming and the Hydrospawn elemental boss are the priest's signature solo farms from ~level 50-54+ (see Dungeons section); T0.5 farming is widely considered a poor time investment versus simply running ZG/K10/AQ20 pugs for equal or better gear — [[d:priest#1425148199504580668]] (Snackz).
- **[Professions]** Tailoring: the Shadoweave set is worthwhile while leveling a Shadow priest; otherwise skip ahead to the Dreamweave set (~level 40-45, balanced Spirit/Int/Spell dmg-healing) which is praised as genuinely strong — [[d:priest#1442771827331043338]] (Greendam), [[d:priest#1442771717788401785]] (Lirya). Older sets like Bloodvine/Mooncloth are considered power-crept and low-value on the live economy (Mooncloth <20g on Ambershire) — [[d:priest#1433062802511233155]] (Alfadir).
