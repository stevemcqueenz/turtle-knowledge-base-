## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[all]** Prior to this chunk's leak, healing was cut: "base healing values for ranks above level 30 and Spell Power scaling for all ranks have been reduced by 15%" for Lesser Heal, Renew, Heal, Flash Heal, Prayer of Healing, Greater Heal, Desperate Prayer, Lightwell; confirmed intended by a dev — [[d:priest#1452408401312350359]] (Silkroad quotes patch note), [[d:priest#1454039265225736272]] (Fat Pibble: "intended... will be addressed" re related bug report), [[d:priest#1465273660993699841]] (30 re-quotes and derives Flash Heal coefficient).
- **[all]** Fear Ward is now baseline for all priests (no longer a racial) — [[d:priest#1447464110588428309]], [[d:priest#1453151986462494873]].
- **[shadow]** Mind Blast threat multiplier reduced from 200% to 170% in an earlier patch — called a half-fix that didn't address the real (VE-driven) threat source — [[d:priest#1451618600485130362]], [[d:priest#1459707517813788682]].
- **[shadow]** Holy Nova mana cost was already cut from 750→600 in an earlier patch, but base damage and SP scaling were nerfed at the same time, so net dps/mana ratio barely improved — [[d:priest#1459708011449684001]].
- **[preliminary 1.18.1 patch notes, discussed at length 2026-01-23 to 01-29]:**
  - **[holy]** Proclaim Champion and its connected spells (Empower/Champion's Grace/Champion's Bond/Revive Champion) **removed** — [[d:priest#1464351944230436874]].
  - **[holy]** New capstone **Ascendance**: "your healing spells cost 33% less mana, are cast 20% quicker and grant their targets the Apotheosis effect, increasing their healing taken by 15% for 10 seconds. Lasts for 30 seconds" (280 mana, 5 min CD); usable under CC and clears CC on cast — [[d:priest#1464351944230436874]]. Does not reduce GCD, by design ("to encourage use of slower spells") — [[d:priest#1464513806959902842]].
  - **[holy]** Renew duration increased by ~6 seconds (to ~21s) — [[d:priest#1464526195851788329]], [[d:priest#1464515005922938995]].
  - **[holy]** New talent refunds ~30% mana on a heal cast if it's not a repeat of your last-cast spell (encourages spell variety, e.g. Renew→PoH) — [[d:priest#1464364265942155335]], [[d:priest#1464380456018514096]].
  - **[holy]** Lightwell fully reworked: single rank; no longer a placeable object requiring clicks. Instead, casting Lesser Heal/Flash Heal/Heal/Greater Heal on a friendly target has Lightwell heal another injured target within 30yd for 20% of that heal's value (40% with "Reservoir of Light" talent) via "Splendor of Light," once per 5s per target; lasts 1 min or 10 charges. Confirmed it does **not** proc off Prayer of Healing — [[d:priest#1464386661973622835]], [[d:priest#1464513512071233628]], [[d:priest#1464512234863460493]].
  - **[shadow]** Shadow Weaving reworked into a self-buff: casting shadow spells stacks a buff on yourself (up to 5 over ~10s), then the next new target you cast a shadow spell on receives the full debuff instantly; whether the 10s self-buff window can refresh mid-duration was disputed between players and the dev in-thread (dev said assumption of "no refresh" is wrong) — [[d:priest#1464388502857974038]], [[d:priest#1464397539288678460]], [[d:priest#1464398607368327476]].
  - **[shadow]** Improved Fade reworked from a cooldown-reduction talent to "Reduces the threat returned when Fade ends by 25/50%," moved from Row 3 to Row 4; removing the CDR breaks the Shadow T3.5 3-piece rotation that depended on frequent Fade+SW:P realignment — widely called a stealth dps nerf — [[d:priest#1465056435242270985]], [[d:priest#1464388353569853814]], [[d:priest#1464397799138529402]].
  - **[shadow]** Shadow Reach capped at 0/2 (down from 0/3) — [[d:priest#1464412475947487262]].
  - **[shadow]** Spirit Tap now also triggers on Mind Blast critical strikes (not just killing blows) — [[d:priest#1465391740797194504]], [[d:priest#1465434396805955739]].
  - **[shadow]** Holy Nova mana cost reduced ~15% for all ranks — [[d:priest#1465391740797194504]], [[d:priest#1465407440026599487]].
  - **[shadow/pvp]** Blackout and Improved Mind Blast swapped talent rows so players aren't forced to take both; per L-star this was about giving a choice, not PvP balance — [[d:priest#1465391740797194504]], [[d:priest#1465480805882269839]].
  - **[disc/holy pvp]** Improved Mana Burn moved deeper (Row 4) since Mana Burn itself is learned at level 24; community argued for moving it back to Disc tree or baseline since it's now unreachable for the specs (PvP Holy/Disc) that actually want it — [[d:priest#1465620222806593557]], [[d:priest#1465757894938071285]].
  - **[disc/smite]** Instant Smite proc chance raised from 50%→60% and its mana cost reduced (~100→~80), confirmed by a dev — [[d:priest#1464632561321115774]], [[d:priest#1464658822667698273]].
  - **[disc]** Disc gets proper tier sets across T1/T2/T3/T3.5 (previously only had T2.5), confirmed by dev — [[d:priest#1464382969543266535]], [[d:priest#1464383050837397565]], [[d:priest#1466466496308838400]].
  - **[all]** Alternate Tier and T3.5 sets will no longer be "unique-equipped," ending the pre-Mephistroth spellpen-enchant/re-enchant cycle — [[d:priest#1465835566917746900]], [[d:priest#1465835657392951427]].
  - **[dev-confirmed]** Existing T2/T3/AQ20 set bonuses (incl. T3 Mind Control %-hit bonus) carry over unchanged — [[d:priest#1464382348195008690]].
  - **Net community read:** Holy gets major, well-received buffs (Ascendance, +Renew duration, reworked Lightwell, mana-refund talent); Shadow gets only minor QoL (reworked Shadow Weaving/Fade, Spirit Tap+MB-crit, small SP scaling) with no real dps increase, and *loses* a passive mana source (the "Champion cheese": buffing MT with Empower Champion pre-fight then respeccing shadow) — [[d:priest#1464382616013639933]], [[d:priest#1459677026373079112]] (Shadow hard-capped near 1600-1700 dps vs 1900+ for other classes at same tier), [[d:priest#1464400122736803852]].

- **[shadow]** Consistently rated the server's worst (or near-worst) dps spec; best BiS Shadow Priests were ranked ~#405 (Kara) / ~#305 (Naxx) overall server dps rankings at the time — [[d:priest#1464610844536078347]] (single detailed source, +reactions), [[d:priest#1457184647372079196]] (independent corroboration: "shadow priest is the worst dps by far right now"), [[d:priest#1459677026373079112]].

- **Shadow raid build:** 5/5 Spirit Tap, 2/2 Wand Spec, then ~20 points up through Spiritual Guidance in Holy, back into Shadow for Shadow Focus/hit talents; drop Range talent to 0/2 (or 0/3 pre-patch) and Anti-Threat talent points as gear improves and hit is capped via gear — [[d:priest#1461998624664850516]], [[d:priest#1465017996824019132]], [[d:priest#1465018910716989738]]. Endgame smite/Disc raid PvE build targets 10% haste (3% talent + 2% food + 5% gear/enchant) for guaranteed 4-Smite Purifying Flames windows — see Stats section.

## Leveling
- **Early rotation (Holy/Smite build):** Holy Fire → Shadow Word: Pain → Smite spam, wanding to finish; drop SW:Pain after ~level 30 because mobs die too fast for it to be worth the mana/GCD (want ≥3 ticks) — [[d:priest#1447653406020866089]], [[d:priest#1447630076026945557]], [[d:priest#1450846541362499627]].
- Concrete level snapshots from players: level 20 rotation "Holy Fire → Smite → PW:Shield rank2 → wand auto" — [[d:priest#1448670109785915402]]; level 34 disc "Holy Fire → Shield → Smite x3 → wand," advised to weigh Renew/Inner Fire refresh cost vs Shield cost by mana rather than auto-shielding — [[d:priest#1448661437873389680]], [[d:priest#1448687675803566190]].
- Spirit Tap is strong even leveling as Holy/Smite (not just Shadow): snipe kills with Smite to proc it; with decent gear it can regen mana nearly as fast as you spend it — [[d:priest#1449304837027135619]], [[d:priest#1451685637819339005]], [[d:priest#1458096208248574013]].
- Discipline is viable for leveling/dungeon-healing but for pure leveling speed go Holy first since the leveling dps talents (Spiritual Guidance, Spirit Tap) sit in the Holy tree — [[d:priest#1448799161590743142]].
- Stat priority while leveling: Int/Stamina below level 40 (most players can't reliably land killing blows in dungeons for Spirit Tap); Spirit becomes valuable once deep in Spirit Tap/Spiritual Guidance synergy or for solo play — [[d:priest#1458073332997161022]], [[d:priest#1458075051667624060]], [[d:priest#1458127838963175712]].
- By ~level 33 with correct build, expect to be a "smite machine gun that never needs to drink" — [[d:priest#1461691841031307295]].
- Shadow leveling: not worth it until ~level 44 (some say never until 44); a shadow-from-1 build is possible (5/5 Spirit Tap → 2/2 Wand Spec in Disc → back to Shadow for hit% and improved pain duration) but most just smite/wand level to 30+ then swap — [[d:priest#1460341857102987354]], [[d:priest#1464447451854540911]].
- Race/spirit tap synergy and dungeon healing notes: Human minor mp5/spirit edge for healers; wand+bow are "roleplay only" (no spellpower bow items, dead-zone issue) — [[d:priest#1459558980815949968]], [[d:priest#1464646745421119622]].

## Rotation: single target
- **Shadow raid rotation:** SW:Pain, Mind Blast, Mind Flay x2, Mind Blast, Mind Flay x2, repeating; use 2-tick Mind Flay clips to reduce Mind Blast downtime at the cost of overall damage, or 3-tick flays for more mana efficiency — [[d:priest#1451277154942718034]], [[d:priest#1463542235487408269]]. Only bother casting SW:Pain if the target will live long enough for ≥3 ticks — [[d:priest#1450846541362499627]], [[d:priest#1447630238434721973]].
- **Smite/Disc raid rotation (Snackz, 10% haste build):** Inner Focus → Holy Fire → 4x Smite → Holy Fire, repeating; only cast SW:Pain while moving; use Chastise on a friendly Arcane Mage/Affliction Lock on cooldown (sacrificing a Smite) rather than on self due to the GCD tax — [[d:priest#1465500759394353345]].
- Precast (max range) Holy Fire → Mind Blast → SW:Pain into Smite/wand for solo target if it'll live for dots; cut SW:Pain if the mob won't — [[d:priest#1447630076026945557]].
- Threat management is core to the single-target rotation for Shadow: avoid Vampiric Embrace unless its mana/heal is actually needed, since a single Mind Blast crit generates roughly 7-7.7k threat once VE-heal-threat and mana-return-threat are added on top of the (170-200%) MB threat multiplier — [[d:priest#1459704209179152626]], [[d:priest#1459705039181320296]], [[d:priest#1451617807597965393]].

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- Holy Nova is only damage-efficient at ~5+ targets; mana is always the limiting factor, not damage — [[d:priest#1463297546909909187]], [[d:priest#1463297721220862117]].
- Shadow has effectively no real AoE: only option is talenting into Holy Nova usable while in Shadowform (holy damage, does not scale with shadow gear/talents), and even in full Kara BiS you go OOM after ~9-10 GCDs of Nova; requires swapping to a dedicated crit/mp5 "nova set" of gear before using it — [[d:priest#1459283418805436467]], [[d:priest#1465011522345373922]], [[d:priest#1465015798513795154]]. Community repeatedly asked for Shadow-damage Holy Nova or Mind Sear instead — [[d:priest#1459724303716319400]], [[d:priest#1464409847712973033]].
- Blade of Eternal Darkness / similarly-worded "on spell hit" procs (e.g. Ornate Bloodstone Dagger) proc once per target hit by an AoE spell, making them strong for Holy Nova farming/AoE (Lashers, even Kara trash) — [[d:priest#1463593375658676314]], [[d:priest#1463596161444085831]].
- "New meta" for multi-add tank/AoE fights (e.g. Garr, Rupturan): AoE all adds evenly and swap to a fire-immune trinket right before they die — [[d:priest#1454603179659362417]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- Spell hit cap is 16% for all specs. Smite gets 6% from talents (Piercing Light) and needs 10% from gear; Shadow gets 10% from talents (Shadow Focus) and needs only 6% from gear — confirmed twice independently — [[d:priest#1447424446972297398]], [[d:priest#1448831999078039743]], [[d:priest#1461819164548403306]], [[d:priest#1461821566764712006]].
- Holy spells can only be fully hit or fully resisted/missed (no partial resist); a "resisted" combat-log entry for a holy spell is actually a miss — [[d:priest#1447459514969227435]], [[d:priest#1447459640316264568]].
- Crit value: for Smite ~12.6 spellpower per 1% crit — [[d:priest#1460092939824009236]]; for Shadow, historically nearly worthless (~2-3sp per 1%, "never more") since only Mind Blast can crit, but post-1.18.1 (MB crit → Spirit Tap) estimated to rise to ~4-5sp equivalent — [[d:priest#1450962931717116145]], [[d:priest#1465439838126870864]]. Shadow full Kara BiS crit is ~15%, ~20% with raid buffs — [[d:priest#1465453726868308133]], [[d:priest#1465453975477293066]].
- Haste: Shadow benefits from **no** spells at all (confirmed repeatedly; even Mind Flay's channel isn't affected) — [[d:priest#1450143690969317406]], [[d:priest#1462847478679212257]], [[d:priest#1462847606664466595]]. Smite/Disc target **10% haste** to remove RNG from Searing Light procs: at 10% haste one Searing Light proc reliably fits 4 Smites inside the 10s Purifying Flames window; below 10% you need 2 procs (bad luck risk). Recipe: 3% from talent + 2% from food + 5% from gear/enchant — [[d:priest#1462462678000730297]], [[d:priest#1464653364577964032]], [[d:priest#1464653767331938409]].
- PW:Shield spell coefficient is 35% — [[d:priest#1454242484266860770]].
- Healing spell coefficients (post 15%-nerf era) reported as: Renew 0.857, Lesser Heal 0.433, Heal 0.857, Greater Heal 1.0, Flash Heal 0.433, Prayer of Healing 0.433, Holy Nova 0.114, PW:Shield 0.35 — [[d:priest#1465272044705939650]]; worked example for 5/5 Spiritual Healing Flash Heal: 0.85×1.3×1.5/3.5 ≈ 0.473 — [[d:priest#1465273975394533387]].
- Mana regen: base out-of-combat regen/2s = Spirit/4; in Kara40 BiS (~360 Spirit fully buffed) that's ~100 mp2 out of combat, ~45% of that while casting via Meditation-type talents — [[d:priest#1457886891159588916]], [[d:priest#1457890490908147762]]. Spirit Tap formula while active: (13+Spirit/4)×0.50 per tick at 50% uptime — [[d:priest#1465459795648843930]].
- Human racial (5% spirit + 5% mana regen while casting): totals roughly +15-17 mp5 and +5 spellpower/healing in endgame gear — [[d:priest#1464028828274921644]], [[d:priest#1464029730738143317]]; stacks with Meditation for up to ~20% total regen while casting — [[d:priest#1450694463998525523]].
- Improved Inner Fire gives 73 base spellpower, plus 30% more via the talent (a common point of confusion, e.g. mistaken for 94) — [[d:priest#1450724908547051552]], [[d:priest#1450726232269389945]].

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- Tier set overview: T1 has mediocre bonuses but a few good stat pieces; T2 shadow bonuses are near-worthless (3pc disabled, 5pc "broken forever," fix promised 1.18.1); T2 holy 3pc (15% mana regen while casting) and 8pc (guaranteed extra Renew on Greater Heal cast) are strong; T3/T3.5 generally strongest, though T3.5 5pc applies via a disease effect that 3 of 9 Kara40 bosses (and roughly half the Tower trash) are immune to — [[d:priest#1459630004785905778]], [[d:priest#1450831022051823646]], [[d:priest#1451914500771938304]], [[d:priest#1459727503198257255]].
- Holy T2 8pc vs T3/T3.5 flash-set is contested and situational: irotos/Eironea say T3 set bonuses are largely irrelevant so go individual stat pieces + keep T2 3pc; others say go full T3 since its pieces beat T2 in raw spellpower and 6pc alone is enough — [[d:priest#1450831022051823646]], [[d:priest#1450706246222086204]]. Later consensus (30/Greendam/t0uchm3): keep 8/8 T2 on raid-wide-damage fights (Anomalus, Kruul) and progression/slow raids generally; switch to T3/T3.5 flash+renew set as your raid clears faster and GH stops landing — [[d:priest#1454630161562730507]], [[d:priest#1458845249651736587]], [[d:priest#1466390383834693653]].
- T2 8pc Renew tested to **not** reliably proc Breath of Solnius above the normal ~5% base rate (300 Greater Heal casts → 17 procs) — [[d:priest#1465464280941072579]].
- Anomalus full-T2 Arcane Resist is achievable without a Warchief's/WPL buff: T2 pieces + extra enchanted head/legs (+25 AR each) + two 20-AR rings (each +8 AR enchant) + 20-AR cloak (+15 AR enchant) + 9-AR wand + 15-AR neck (+8 AR enchant) = 183 AR, enough with a Druid buff — [[d:priest#1459262973766795264]].
- Shadow T3 6pc gives a windfury-style proc on Mind Flay that *can* crit (unlike base Mind Flay) — worth taking despite crit otherwise being low value for Shadow — [[d:priest#1462007751432736835]], [[d:priest#1462007991887728807]].
- Nordanaar-server BiS callouts: T3.5 neck > Cthun's neck; T3 ring + Mephistroth's ring > Ring of the Martyr (holy) — [[d:priest#1447644842153869382]]. Mana regen items: Loatheb ring (Naxx, 10%), Rapid Evolution gloves (BWL, 5%), Maexxna neck, Gnarlmoon cloak, Hyjal redeemable helm, Emerald Sanctum staff+neck (head quest), BoE Sandals of Inner Peace — [[d:priest#1449672362759950477]].

- Shadow ZG BiS: the ZG wand beats Naxx wands, Cloak of Consumption is "super big," and a 40sp head piece (Hexxer's Cover) is very strong and long-lasting — [[d:priest#1456718038282801152]], [[d:priest#1456751545713561672]]. - Disc raid BiS notes: run 8/10% haste breakpoint gear; Kumisha's item and Medivh pair-ring are BiS; tailoring gloves (~3% haste) are a reasonable interim item even in K40 gear — [[d:priest#1464654071150412071]], [[d:priest#1464654168663785725]].

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- No shadow- or holy-specific glove spellpower enchant exists (mage equivalent gives 18sp+1% hit) and no shadow/holy-exclusive JC gems exist, forcing Shadow/Smite priests to choose between spellpower items and needed Shadow Resist (for Mephistroth) on the same slots; flagged to the dev, who said they'd ask the item team — [[d:priest#1459687027019874426]], [[d:priest#1459687759265402942]].
- Pre-1.18.1, keeping BiS at Mephistroth required re-enchanting head/legs with spellpenetration (75 spellpen total: base + 2×25 from head/leg enchants) before the pull and back to spellpower afterward — a tedious cycle removed once tier gear stops being unique-equipped in 1.18.1 — [[d:priest#1451915027950080000]], [[d:priest#1465835657392951427]].

## Consumables & world buffs
- Fruitcake (a % mana-regen food, like the mage-crafted "orange") is the top pre-raid mana-regen food; obtainable seasonally (Christmas) or year-round from a Booty Bay goblin for 100g access + 2g/5 — [[d:priest#1448094613586972794]], [[d:priest#1448145373540651119]].
- Empower Champion buff (pre-1.18.1) lasts 1 hour once applied even after leaving the party, but is lost on logout — used as a pre-raid mana-cheese by respeccing to Shadow right after buffing the tank (removed with Proclaim Champion line in 1.18.1) — [[d:priest#1448433905580380381]], [[d:priest#1464683006085173542]], [[d:priest#1464683794375377020]].
- Active trinket sequencing for Shadow (e.g. Remains/ZG "Essence" trinket + Scythe): pop the passive-CD trinket (Remains) ~30s before pull, then Essence on pull, then Scythe — using Essence before Scythe only puts Scythe on a 20s internal CD, while using Scythe first locks Essence for 1 minute — [[d:priest#1464777512717648175]], [[d:priest#1464778576552525937]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- Vampiric Embrace generates outsized threat: a single 3.5k Mind Blast crit generates roughly 3500 (MB, post-nerf 170%) + 3937 (VE heal @150%, minus 25% Salvation) + 262 (mana-gen threat) ≈ 7199 total threat from one GCD — the community's central complaint about Shadow — [[d:priest#1459705039181320296]]. VE healing is estimated 90%+ overheal at current Shadow damage levels — [[d:priest#1459716663501914345]].
- Fade (Shadow): pre-1.18.1 Improved Fade gave a CD reduction (30s→24s) that let it align with SW:Pain refresh for the T3.5 3pc bonus; 1.18.1 removes the CDR in favor of a 25/50% "threat-on-expiry" reduction, breaking that combo — see 1.18.1 section.
- Mind Control (MC) loot/xp rules: you get credit only if your MC'd mob lands the killing blow on another mob (or you land a hit on your MC'd mob before/after control) — killing the controlled mob with someone else's damage gives you nothing — [[d:priest#1448450776459644948]], [[d:priest#1448451422986436619]]. MC requires the caster to keep the controlled unit within cast range at all times (movement outside range cancels it) — [[d:priest#1463721736867680368]].
- The "shadow resist breaks MC" myth was extensively tested and largely debunked: self-buffing with Shadow Protection/gear resist has no measurable effect, but applying an actual Shadow Resistance **aura/buff to the MC'd mob** (e.g. via a Paladin's aura in party) does cause measurably more early breaks; most reported "breaks" are simply the mob wandering out of MC range or a Shaman Tremor Totem — [[d:priest#1448739340724338789]], [[d:priest#1448833571262431303]], [[d:priest#1448887514625736814]].
- Mind Vision rank 2 can be re-cast continent-wide by chaining it (cast on someone, then on whoever they walk near) but cannot directly target a unit frame outside your current vision range — [[d:priest#1454881418357379337]], [[d:priest#1454886013875916911]].

## Tanking specifics
- Smite Priest can tank Twins (AQ40) with SR (survival/spell-reflect) gear: open with melee-range Mind Blast when they teleport (instant threat, avoids the slow Holy Fire cast), keep threat via a heavy-hitting rotation, and use self Flash Heal + Shield + Shadow Protection potion as an "oh-fuck" button when low; PW:Shield breaking 100% of the time gives a large spellpower boost from Resurgent Shield-type talents — [[d:priest#1461105005976358954]], [[d:priest#1461309845457342610]], [[d:priest#1461310065742188544]].
- Shadow priests can shadow-tank in AQ40, and it "works pretty well," but is considered weaker than a Warlock voidwalker/felguard tank or Bear tank overall — [[d:priest#1460291304553713825]], [[d:priest#1460311331415785705]], [[d:priest#1460972306774888611]].
- Instructor Razuvious MC (Naxx) requires a minimum of 16% spell hit (T3 2pc bonus hit counts toward it); some players report success with lower hit (3-5%) as Holy using shadow T3 pieces — [[d:priest#1461398705235300475]], [[d:priest#1461405054635479239]].

## Healing specifics
- Current meta favors Flash Heal + Renew spam over Greater Heal on most fights, since Paladins/Shamans out-snipe slower priest heals — [[d:priest#1454629825896644825]], [[d:priest#1461071153669079040]]. Exceptions: 8/8 T2 Greater Heal spam remains strong specifically on fights with steady/frequent raid-wide damage (named: Anomalus, Kruul; T3.5 flash/renew is preferred on Gnarlmoon, Chess, Sanctum, Rupturan, Mephistroth) — [[d:priest#1454630161562730507]], [[d:priest#1454630442161405973]].
- Discipline healing is weaker than Holy in raids mainly due to the cumulative healing nerf plus high mana costs on heal spells; only really viable if significantly overgeared — [[d:priest#1456362257096376363]], [[d:priest#1456362625167523952]].
- PW:Shield is priest's strongest instant heal (low CD, can act as a temporary extra health pool) but combat logs/parsers don't count absorbs, understating priest healing output — [[d:priest#1464390988888801549]].
- Healing addons commonly used: Puppeteer (raid frames/healing), HealComm, Rinse (dispel-relevant debuff tracking), Cursive (dot/debuff tracking, mainly Shadow), MageHud (tracks PW:Shield, though inaccurately for HP-scaling shields), NotGrid (usable but limited vs. classic Grid2) — [[d:priest#1449507929471586436]], [[d:priest#1449532264995356692]], [[d:priest#1461042812299776233]], [[d:priest#1454639371205607653]].
- Priest healing overall is considered "sniped" by faster/smarter Paladin (instant/auto-reactive heals), Shaman (Chain Heal), and Druid (fast-ticking HoTs) kits which are said to be at a higher (TBC/Wrath) power level than Priest's vanilla-level toolkit — [[d:priest#1451929582616842483]], [[d:priest#1451933889458208965]], [[d:priest#1451934208363724911]].

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **Kara40 boss order** (per community shorthand): Gnarlmoon → Anomalus → Chess → Sanctum ("Sanv") → Rupturan → Kruul → Mephistroth — [[d:priest#1454630161562730507]].
- **Anomalus**: Arcane damage/burst fight; full T2 8pc (Greater Heal) is strong here specifically, but requires enough Arcane Resistance (183 AR recipe above) since 8/8 T2 alone can't hit the AR floor without help — [[d:priest#1459262973766795264]], [[d:priest#1459229174375125186]]. Shadow priests use a dedicated full-SP+AR gear set on this fight — [[d:priest#1451914785242484851]].
- **Chess / Sanctum**: two of the three Kara bosses immune to the T3.5 5pc disease proc — [[d:priest#1451914500771938304]], [[d:priest#1459727503198257255]].
- **Rupturan**: only PvE fight where Mana Burn actually sees some use (vs. the boss); Shadow uses 6pc T3 here for the extra spellpower value from the encounter's long duration; multi-dot fight where the "different spell" mana-refund talent (1.18.1) has actual relevance — [[d:priest#1463494991207862447]], [[d:priest#1451914785242484851]], [[d:priest#1464396953902121215]].
- **Garr / Rupturan**: good "big rock" AoE farming for Shadow — new meta is to AoE all adds evenly and swap to a fire-immune trinket right before they die — [[d:priest#1454601699468509286]], [[d:priest#1454603179659362417]].
- **Naxx**: Sapphiron and general boss list used by top Shadow parsers for per-boss dps benchmarking (concrete numbers: Anub'Rekhan 2030dps #1, Maexxna 1918dps #1, Patchwerk 1692dps #2, Gluth 1527dps #1, Noth 1905dps #1, Loatheb 1704dps #3, Faerlina 1626dps #2) — [[d:priest#1460389926838468770]].
- **Heigan**: the one fight where Shadow's short Mind Flay range (20yd base) is actually a liability, since staying at range to avoid mechanics can put you outside Mana Burn/mechanic range without the range talent — [[d:priest#1454802940958347295]], [[d:priest#1454803006255267881]], [[d:priest#1459627960708370617]].
- **Instructor Razuvious (Naxx)**: MC requires 16% hit minimum (see Tanking); the persistent "shadow resist breaks MC" myth traced to Razuvious specifically — [[d:priest#1448739340724338789]].
- **Dire Maul: East**: commonly farmed by priests for Lashers; can skip several packs and even fight the entrance mini-boss solo by exploiting mob aggro-range/reset spots — [[d:priest#1463784145590157502]], [[d:priest#1463872425392607295]].
- **RFK (Razorfen Kraul)**: solo-able at 60 as Shadow using Mind Soothe to skip packs; Smite OOMs too fast to be practical for the same solo farm — [[d:priest#1448220702929846283]], [[d:priest#1448244065224102011]].

## PvP
- Race: Troll considered "100% PvP BiS" for Shadow due to the Shadowguard racial (proc-based reflect/stun synergy with Blackout); Undead valued for 2% extra damage vs Undead/Humanoid, Devouring Plague (Undead-only spell), and the racial's use to break the "MC drops Shadowform" bug instantly — [[d:priest#1447169859694628974]], [[d:priest#1453176348586672240]], [[d:priest#1451617193245802691]], [[d:priest#1466152737333973126]].
- Turtle PvP is a "burst meta": you die in 2 hits with or without PvP gear, so Kara-level PvE gear for max damage output is often preferred over dedicated PvP gear — [[d:priest#1451623968057331732]], [[d:priest#1451624095136616588]].
- Smite/Disc PvP rotation: Chastise (self, for disorient) → Holy Fire → Smite, fishing for instant-Smite procs off Searing Light; instant Smites have a 1.5s ICD so you must weave another spell in between to chain them; Holy Nova+instant-Smite is strong vs melee at range; kiting is weak vs. Rogues/Warriors without a trinket — [[d:priest#1452042331783172290]], [[d:priest#1452013492961021983]], [[d:priest#1462905759712018432]], [[d:priest#1458930070176665793]].
- Mana Burn is largely not worth it in PvE (niche use only on AQ20's boss or Rupturan) and situational in PvP — best used vs. mana-hungry Shaman/Resto healers who lack efficient heals, but not effective against Paladins (mana-cheap heals) — [[d:priest#1463494991207862447]], [[d:priest#1465782188216942645]], [[d:priest#1465785204026114217]].
- NE Bow "Searing Shot": instant-cast version is "not horrible" for PvP fishing procs, but memey/weak for PvE; no spellpower bows exist so it's a damage sacrifice — [[d:priest#1457541567446519899]], [[d:priest#1456972848752099451]], [[d:priest#1456980780575228140]].
- Nordanaar PvP-purchasable BiS-tier items (bypass early raiding): Ornate Bloodstone Dagger, Spellwoven Nobility Drape (11sp, 1% hit, proc 150 int), assorted "...of Shadow Wrath" items — enough to skip straight to Naxx for T3 — [[d:priest#1451628084242550825]], [[d:priest#1451628390548242645]], [[d:priest#1451628915985617040]].

## Races, professions, gold
- For healing (Holy/Disc), race barely matters; Human gives only a slight spirit/regen edge — [[d:priest#1453940036289691658]], [[d:priest#1455241017715327151]]. For Shadow PvE: Undead (2% dmg vs Undead/Humanoid, Devouring Plague, MC-bug racial escape); for Shadow PvP: Troll (Shadowguard) — [[d:priest#1453176348586672240]], [[d:priest#1466152737333973126]].
- Professions: for a first/leveling character, double-gathering (Herbalism+Mining or Skinning) recommended since all crafting professions are gold sinks that don't pay off until level 60 with rare recipes — [[d:priest#1464014152988233738]], [[d:priest#1464013348910792837]]. Engineering is the only profession that adds direct damage (sappers) but isn't a "must" for casual/healer players since healers are rarely in sapper range — [[d:priest#1464011410257346610]]. Tailoring considered the weakest endgame profession (cooldown item hard to sell); Enchanting's cooldown item (enchanted armor kit) sells better — [[d:priest#1462906906518622279]], [[d:priest#1462914769873932395]], [[d:priest#1462915005862117548]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **MC drops Shadowform bug (long-standing, years-old, multiple bug reports unaddressed):** when a Shadow Priest is Mind Controlled by a boss, the MC'd unit's cast of Vampiric Embrace (a shadow spell) somehow forces Shadowform off, but the client still thinks the priest is in Shadowform (breaking the visual/action bar), and Shadowform can't be recast until fixed. Workarounds: be Undead (racial breaks MC instantly before it casts), get CC'd/disabled by someone else, walk through a mage portal, or relog — [[d:priest#1447894110194368622]], [[d:priest#1447894327211851938]], [[d:priest#1458239869145972816]], [[d:priest#1462934576153366548]].
- Shadow T3 6pc Mind Flay proc was mislabeled in combat logs (shown as physical/ambiguous "mind flay" text for both hits and misses); confirmed to actually be shadow damage — a logging/naming issue, being clarified for 1.18.1 — [[d:priest#1459808464325836800]], [[d:priest#1459841226948870409]], [[d:priest#1459842550142730413]].
- Set bonus bugs: Shadow T2 3pc disabled, 5pc "broken forever" (fix promised 1.18.1); Smite/Disc T2.5's first effect confirmed not working — [[d:priest#1459630004785905778]], [[d:priest#1459630197635547326]], [[d:priest#1459744175959441538]].
- Turtlogs (log parsing tool) sometimes shows Shadow Priest/Warlock casts as "cast Mind Blast then just waited for it to come off cooldown" — attributed to a logger issue, not a real gameplay bug — [[d:priest#1460318965115912447]], [[d:priest#1460353494312816723]].
- Chastise sometimes fully heals a (specifically flying) mob when it tags them — reported as possibly mob-specific, unresolved — [[d:priest#1465369788292530359]], [[d:priest#1465371025154769233]].
- Mind Vision rank 2 doesn't behave as documented (should be continent-wide, but can't directly target unit frames outside current vision range) — [[d:priest#1454877555436818443]], [[d:priest#1454886013875916911]].
