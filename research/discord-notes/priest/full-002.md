## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[General]** Priest is broadly regarded as the weakest class DPS-wise on the server pre-1.18.1, offset partly by healing/mana utility; multiple posters call it "the only class playing vanilla while everyone else plays vanilla+" — [[d:priest#1466844805601693847]] (Tzychotic, 2026-01-30), [[d:priest#1474136572340207646]] (Xerq, 2026-02-19).
- **[Disc]** Discipline is the DPS spec on Turtle WoW ("Smite priest"), not a healing spec; Holy is the dedicated healing tree — [[d:priest#1473059446874968095]] (haunt, 2026-02-17), [[d:priest#1472628228869980210]] (2KILL, 2026-02-16). Pure disc-healing (deep shield build) is considered non-viable at 60 endgame: mana runs out fast and heals are weak vs. deep-Holy — [[d:priest#1480925749274742794]] (t0uchm3, 2026-02-26).
- **[Shadow vs Smite]** At full BiS, Shadow and Smite do roughly the same DPS, but Shadow gets raid slots over Smite/Disc because of the shadow-weaving debuff + mana battery (VE); both perform well in PvP — [[d:priest#1471920785001152666]] (Greendam, 2026-02-13).
- **[Holy]** Community debate on whether Holy or Disc is the "worst" spec pre-patch: one view is Holy is worst, countered that Disc is worse — [[d:priest#1475772503115825193]] (Easter Bunny, 2026-02-24), [[d:priest#1475772603833516164]] (Stunfisk, 2026-02-24; disputed).
- **[DPS benchmark]** Concrete comparison from raid logs: best average Shadow Priest DPS on Patchwerk-style fights (post-1.18) ≈ 1600 (Zitronenhaze), vs best Enhancement Shaman ≈ 1550, vs best Retribution Paladin ≈ 2000+ — used to argue Shadow is closer to "average" than "bottom of the barrel" — [[d:priest#1474125218435043358]] (Snackz, 2026-02-19; disputed by Koys/Zitronenhaze who argue guild-wide/average parses tell a worse story).
- **[Hybrid build]** The common "hybrid healer" build for casual raiding is 21/30/0: Enlighten (Disc) + Spiritual Healing (Holy), using heal-stat gear with DPS-oriented talents since Priest gets a lot of "free" spellpower from talents relative to healing scaling — [[d:priest#1481016128506560614]] (Illithris, 2026-02-26; L-star also recommends this approach, 2026-02-17 [[d:priest#1473059628836458578]]).
- **[Shadow]** Niche raid value: exactly one Shadow Priest is typically brought per raid for the shadow-weaving debuff; contested whether the mana battery from VE is still valuable in later raids — [[d:priest#1470614434740175023]] (Saedar, 2026-02-10), disputed [[d:priest#1470614660393992334]] (Afraido, 2026-02-10: "mana battery is not even that good in later raids").
- **[Tanking]** Shadow Priest can off-tank AQ40 Twin Emperors / Bishop of Twilight fights using shadow-resist gear (empowered Shadow Bolt only hits within ~20 yards, no empowered Volley) — [[d:priest#1477507796521058465]] (Mendeleev, 2026-03-01), [[d:priest#1477435494802391101]] (Illithris, 2026-03-01), practical example with 315+ SR "easiest tank to heal" [[d:priest#1477508513457635440]] (Mendeleev, 2026-03-01). Smite priest can also tank Twins with decent base threat + MB spam, though shadow-resist gear is harder to get without the tier helm's SR — [[d:priest#1476578557147877427]] (Snackz, 2026-02-27), [[d:priest#1476588281528455371]] (Snackz, 2026-02-27).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Recommended leveling talent order: 2/2 Wand Specialization (Disc) → 5/5 Spirit Tap (Shadow) → then invest in Holy tree (Divinity, Divine Fury — the latter buffs both offensive Holy spells and healing) — [[d:priest#1468388067369943335]] (irotos, 2026-02-04), confirmed as "the leveling spec pinned" [[d:priest#1468394752398594120]] (Lirya, 2026-02-04). Alternate ordering suggestion: 2/2 wands → 5/5 spirit tap → 5/5 Divinity → 5/5 Divine Fury for both solo dps and healing — [[d:priest#1473663128427434099]] (haunt, 2026-02-17).
- **[General]** Stat priority while leveling: Spirit > Intellect > Stamina; spellpower gear only starts mattering meaningfully from ~level 30-35+ (spells before that are tuned around low spellpower) — [[d:priest#1468260440646619156]] (Lynathel, 2026-02-03), [[d:priest#1474712461763416276]] (Lynathel, 2026-02-20), [[d:priest#1467965917081112811]] (Lynathel, 2026-02-01).
- **[General]** Wand Specialization + a good wand trivializes early leveling: at level 13, Greater Magic Wand + 2/2 Wand Specialization out-damages actual spells; wanding "carries you into the 30s with ease" — [[d:priest#1468158386821660827]] (Philes, 2026-02-03), [[d:priest#1468158831044591782]] (Stunfisk, 2026-02-03).
- **[General]** Talent respec cost is capped at 5 gold on Turtle WoW (first reset costs 1g), so it's viable to take Proclaim Champion at 40 in a 5-man leveling group, then respec into Spirit Tap at 41-45 — [[d:priest#1473663196677148788]] (haunt, 2026-02-18), [[d:priest#1473663398540738735]] (haunt, 2026-02-18).
- **[Holy/Disc leveling]** In 5-man dungeon leveling, Wand Spec → Spirit Tap → Holy lets a priest be top DPS if there's another healer, or a competent backup healer if needed; solo grinding from ~level 30 on is Holy Fire → Smite → Smite → Smite chaining off Spirit Tap procs without going oom — [[d:priest#1468394752398594120]] (Lirya, 2026-02-04, extended explanation).
- **[Shadow leveling]** Shadow is rough before level 40 (no Shadowform), acceptable after 40/44 (Mind Flay usable), and only becomes mana-steady with the level-60 Improved Shadowform; recommendation is to level Disc/Smite and respec to Shadow at 60, or at earliest 40 — [[d:priest#1469050938994987018]] (Stunfisk, 2026-02-05: "passable at around lvl 40 or 44"), [[d:priest#1474903366231658617]] (haunt, 2026-02-22: "shadow = bad before 40, ok after 40, good after 60"), [[d:priest#1478066132786352139]] (Saedar, 2026-03-02).
- **[Gear]** The Stormreaver tailoring 4-piece set (only gloves craftable via rare SFK pattern drop, rest drop in dungeon as green BoE/blue BoP) is called out as very strong for leveling — [[d:priest#1468366946050506783]] (Xerq, 2026-02-04).
- **[Gear]** Night Elf priest can use bows from level 20 (via the racial Searing Shot spell); mostly a leveling/PvP flavor tool since wands out-scale it once white-damage weapon mana-return (Wand Spec) matters more at endgame — [[d:priest#1473400271664058389]] (Valaen, 2026-02-17), [[d:priest#1474452374935310460]] (Darkride, 2026-02-20: useless at endgame, wand is better).
- **[Talent]** Improved Renew vs Divinity choice while leveling holy debated; recommendation is Renew if you don't have Inspiration yet, otherwise Divinity — [[d:priest#1469989484966973520]] (Sada, 2026-02-08), [[d:priest#1469994073669500991]] (Aladar, 2026-02-08).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Smite]** Core rotation is Holy Fire → 4x Smite inside the 10-second Searing Light/Purifying Flames damage-buff window from Holy Fire's proc; requires ~10% haste to reliably fit exactly 4 Smites + next Holy Fire without relying on 2 Searing Light procs (unreliable even at ~45% crit BiS) — [[d:priest#1467601161828827177]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-01), [[d:priest#1476796345179181107]] (Koys, 2026-02-27), [[d:priest#1476798169538101248]] (Snackz, 2026-02-27), [[d:priest#1481690213138038855]] (Snackz, 2026-03-12: without 10% haste you need 2 Searing Light procs, which is unreliable even near BiS crit).
- **[Smite]** At high ping (~250ms), the 10% haste breakpoint may not be enough and needs to be raised; test in-game with min buffer/channel-latency-reduction settings tuned and watch for "spell not ready" errors — [[d:priest#1470968763804287141]] (Xerq, 2026-02-10), [[d:priest#1470972310453485631]] (Greendam, 2026-02-10).
- **[Shadow]** Standard single-target rotation: SW:P → Mind Blast → Mind Flay x2 (repeat), prioritizing keeping dots refreshed and using Mind Blast on cooldown; VE cast first if used at all — [[d:priest#1471593092183232739]] (Shangchi, 2026-02-12, confirmed by Koys).
- **[Shadow]** Mind Flay "clip" mechanic: casting 3-tick Mind Flay twice is more mana-efficient than three 2-tick Mind Flays for filling Mind Blast's 6-second cooldown window, but you must manually cancel (clip) the channel right after the last wanted tick rather than letting nampower auto-continue; a small "clip window" appears near the end of each tick, made visible by watching the castbar's latency band — [[d:priest#1471312489563033670]] (via Bluefear/Oldmana downranking thread context, 2026-02-12), [[d:priest#1481904819215138839]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-03-13), [[d:priest#1481904983258435708]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-03-13), [[d:priest#1481909198819426459]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-03-13: red latency band marks where you can safely cancel-cast into Mind Blast without losing the tick).
- **[Shadow]** Early clipping of dots (before they finish ticking) is "super bad" on Turtle WoW compared to retail conventions — [[d:priest#1468633694993649820]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-04).
- **[Disc PvP]** Simple rotation macro request pattern: Inner Focus on CD → Holy Fire on CD → Smite filler — [[d:priest#1476190065645453505]] (Svargth, 2026-02-26; question, standard disc/smite loop).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Holy Nova]** Scales very poorly with spellpower (only ~15% SP coefficient), costs roughly 50% more mana than Arcane Explosion for less damage at equal scaling; best used procced off Blade of Eternal Darkness (BoED) or Sigil of Ancient Accord rather than up-ranked as a real AoE nuke — [[d:priest#1470850932731281480]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-10), [[d:priest#1470859828321718446]] (Stunfisk, 2026-02-10), [[d:priest#1475610081788956755]] (Snackz, 2026-02-24: swap weapon to BoED, trinket to Sigil of Ancient Accord, pants to Magispark Leggings for a dedicated Nova set).
- **[Shadow AoE]** Shadow has essentially no real AoE tool; the only "AoE" is multi-dotting SW:P across a pack to carry 15% shadow-weaving into the next pull, which costs 4+ globals per pack and rarely gets more than 1 SW:P tick off before packs die in a fast-clearing guild — [[d:priest#1472910100401360992]] (haunt, 2026-02-16), [[d:priest#1472913815174647820]] (irotos, 2026-02-16), [[d:priest#1472914109048819826]] (irotos, 2026-02-16).
- **[Smite/AoE]** Shadow priests wearing offspec Smite/Nova gear at trash for a bit of dmg + heal via Holy Nova is a common but acknowledged-weak "hybrid" trash pattern — [[d:priest#1470847342704988357]] (Stunfisk, 2026-02-10), [[d:priest#1478224818523607091]] (Lynathel, 2026-02-26: alternates Holy Nova + instant Smite procs from Searing Light on 6+ target packs, otherwise prefers single-target).
- **[T3 Smite]** With the new T3 4pc bonus, a Holy Fire opener naturally stacks Blessed Wildfire to 10 charges before the next Holy Fire, so alternating into Holy Nova spam on trash consumes those stacks at roughly +4% dmg per Nova as the buff ticks down — [[d:priest#1481657406965682349]] (Snackz, 2026-03-12), [[d:priest#1481657661740286065]] (Snackz, 2026-03-12).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Smite/Disc raid]** Stat priority: Haste to the 10% breakpoint > Hit to 16% > Spellpower/Crit > Mana — [[d:priest#1476777966410272788]] (Snackz, 2026-02-27), [[d:priest#1481676227906633780]] (Snackz, 2026-03-12), [[d:priest#1474440884903870625]] (Snackz, 2026-02-20: "You should be at 16% for bosses"). Note: 3% haste from talents and 2% from food do not show on the character sheet — [[d:priest#1476778037130301645]] (Snackz, 2026-02-27).
- **[General]** Caster spell-hit cap (all casters) confirmed at 16% — [[d:priest#1473350667056709844]] (Snackz, 2026-02-18).
- **[Shadow]** Shadow-specific hit cap is 6% (with the Shadow Focus talent) — [[d:priest#1475213116433240154]] (ShadowPriestOnlyPriest, 2026-02-22).
- **[Shadow]** Crit is worth roughly 2.5-3 spellpower for shadow priest (low value since only Mind Blast/Pain Spike can crit, and Mind Flay/SW:P — ~80% of the rotation — cannot); disputed higher/lower in a long side-thread but converges near this figure — [[d:priest#1475213371380076676]] (Shadurloc, 2026-02-22), [[d:priest#1475220498450350210]] (Shadurloc, 2026-02-22). Pre-patch, 2% crit is worth less than 7 spellpower for a shadow priest, but this is expected to shift once Spirit Tap procs off Mind Blast crit — [[d:priest#1473425959771439214]] (Greendam, 2026-02-18).
- **[Smite/Disc]** Roughly 1 crit ≈ 12 spellpower for smite/disc (dynamic, sheet-dependent); haste before its cap and hit before its cap both outweigh crit and spellpower — [[d:priest#1474554280303132742]] (Greendam, 2026-02-20).
- **[Leveling]** Spirit > Intellect > Stamina while leveling (regen + Spiritual Guidance SP conversion); base stats matter more than chasing pure-SP items at low level — [[d:priest#1474554827982766111]] (Greendam, 2026-02-20), [[d:priest#1474555528624472166]] (Greendam, 2026-02-20).
- **[PvP]** Suggested PvP stat priority: Stamina > Intellect > Spirit (big health/mana pool to keep fighting), gearing toward stam/int with instant-spell disc talents (PW:S, Renew, mana cost reduction) — [[d:priest#1472313528693035219]] (Tzychotic, 2026-02-15), [[d:priest#1472314500333174997]] (Xerq, 2026-02-15).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Shadow]** Detailed threat math for Mind Blast: base 170% threat multiplier, plus 30% of damage healed to each party member via VE (150% of damage as extra threat on top for a 5-man party context), plus 2% mana regen per person contributing more threat; a 3.5k Mind Blast crit generates ~7199 threat once VE healing/mana threat and the -25% shadow-threat talent / -25% Salvation are factored in, and Zitronenhaze reports 5k Mind Blast crits in Kara BiS gear — [[d:priest#1475974263558111403]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-25), [[d:priest#1475984733321167092]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-25), [[d:priest#1475984870336626890]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-25: at 200% MB threat, 7600 threat total). Shadow priests do not have enough talent points to take full anti-threat talents without sacrificing key damage/mana talents — [[d:priest#1475974383846559745]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-25).
- **[Shadow]** Fade is described as "currently still useless" pre-patch since it only removes threat ~1-in-10 uses in practice and tanks need 110% threat in melee to reclaim aggro — [[d:priest#1475974456240504945]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-25), [[d:priest#1475980104315179053]] (Lynathel, 2026-02-25).
- **[Shadow]** The shadow anti-threat talent (Shadow Affinity, -25% shadow-spell threat) does not reduce VE's threat contribution — [[d:priest#1475981605284614224]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-25).
- **[General]** Fear Ward is baseline for all priests (not race-locked); this is occasionally a mana/GCD burden since raid members request it off-cooldown — [[d:priest#1476021580273553511]] (Potatolord, 2026-02-25).
- **[Smite/Holy]** Pain Spike: mostly a 0-DPS filler outside of execute windows, but since most raid bosses carry a heal-reduction debuff, its self-damage-derived healback rarely fully negates the damage done, making it "better than 0 dps" filler — [[d:priest#1474775352864931881]] (Eemo, 2026-02-21), [[d:priest#1474782366689136730]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-21: in a full Kara run did 60k dmg via Pain Spike, healed back only 13k).
- **[Item]** Loop of Infused Renewal (ring): Greater Heal has a chance to proc a free Renew; with the T2 8pc set bonus already rolling a Renew, this can stack 3 renews on a target, functionally a free max-rank Flash Heal every ~3 seconds; measured as roughly 20% better than a T3 ring in sustained GH-spam fights (Gnarlmoon/Mediv/Kruul) in one priest's testing, though disputed as "0.4% of effective healing" by another — [[d:priest#1476197973456060689]] (Yoland, 2026-02-25), [[d:priest#1476209340376547358]] (t0uchm3, 2026-02-25), [[d:priest#1476248084550778973]] (L-star, 2026-02-25, disputes the value).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Shadow]** Shadow Priest can off-tank AQ40's Bishop of Twilight fight: Bishop casts Empowered Shadow Bolt (2x normal damage) only on targets within ~20 yards, no Empowered Volley exists, so any Shadow-Resist tank works and Shadow Priests with 315+ SR describe it as "the easiest tank to heal" for that fight, sustained largely via self Renew+VE — [[d:priest#1477419985264447578]] (Illithris, 2026-03-01), [[d:priest#1477507796521058465]] (Mendeleev, 2026-03-01), [[d:priest#1477508513457635440]] (Mendeleev, 2026-03-01).

## Healing specifics
- **[Holy]** Downranking guidance: don't downrank in leveling dungeons (use max available rank of Lesser Heal/Heal/Greater Heal, follow with Renew to bridge the 5-second-rule gap); Flash Heal reserved for non-tanks/emergencies; Prayer of Healing only worth it if 3+ people benefit — [[d:priest#1471253529334382634]] (Bluefear, 2026-02-11). At 60, main is Heal R4 with max-rank Renew rolling on tank at all times, R5 Renew for incidental raid damage, Inner Focus + Prayer of Healing, max-rank GH when timing allows — [[d:priest#1471253832221589577]] (Bluefear, 2026-02-11).
- **[Holy]** There is a level-30 "global nerf wall" on healing spells: the rank learned right after level 30 is inefficient (e.g., Heal R4 costs 50 more mana than R3 for only ~40 more healing, vs R1→R2 or R2→R3 which each give ~150 more healing for the same +50 mana); recommendation is to skip that specific rank (Heal R4, Renew R5, Flash Heal R3) entirely when downranking until the nerf is addressed — [[d:priest#1471312489563033670]] (Greendam, 2026-02-12), [[d:priest#1471314520969576534]] (Greendam, 2026-02-12), [[d:priest#1471315568798863655]] (Greendam, 2026-02-12), context/history [[d:priest#1471320712961724528]] (Bluefear, 2026-02-12: those ranks were nerfed specifically for being too efficient in classic/anniversary).
- **[Holy]** "ABCs of priest healing: Always Be cRenewing" — Renew uptime philosophy repeatedly endorsed — [[d:priest#1471865143410430062]] (Tzychotic, 2026-02-13).
- **[Holy]** Renew's healing coefficient with Spiritual Healing talent maxed calculated at 1.12x of healing power — [[d:priest#1476694995363823717]] (L-star, 2026-02-27).
- **[Holy]** PWS's spell-power scaling coefficient increased from 10% to 35% (does not affect pre-level-20 ranks) — [[d:priest#1468757047750295754]] (Lirya, 2026-02-05).
- **[Holy]** Client/combat-log limitation: shielding (PWS) doesn't register on the healing meter/combat log at all pre-1.18.1, so priests focusing on shields will look artificially weak on parses; this wasn't fixed on retail until Cataclysm either — [[d:priest#1477416417417756724]] (Illithris, 2026-03-01).
- **[Holy]** Haste math clarified: talent/consumable haste (e.g., Breath of Solnius's 20%) divides cast time (cast/1.2), it does not subtract a flat percentage; the ZG trinket's "40% reduced GH cast time" is a separate flat-percentage-of-base reduction, not treated as haste, so it stacks multiplicatively and is worth more than its % suggests — [[d:priest#1477523346437705738]] (30, 2026-03-01), [[d:priest#1476372804797468672]] (30, 2026-02-26), [[d:priest#1476373214296018974]] (30, 2026-02-26), [[d:priest#1476373295271116850]] (30, 2026-02-26: haste modifiers multiply together). Practical takeaway: any cast pushed below the 1.5s GCD floor from haste enchants is wasted — [[d:priest#1476375804719136890]] (JohnGood, 2026-02-26), [[d:priest#1476377172339064873]] (30, 2026-02-26).
- **[Holy]** Bugged healing report: respeccing into Holy (especially via "goblin device"/dual-spec item) can cause Improved Renew / Spiritual Healing scaling to silently apply incorrectly (renew ticking ~350 instead of expected ~420+, confirmed by tank taking the lower value, not just an addon display bug); only fix found was deleting the WDB cache folder — [[d:priest#1476685485064585367]] (🐹 hamster 🐹, 2026-02-26), [[d:priest#1476695118038831226]] (🐹 hamster 🐹, 2026-02-26), corroborated independently after a Naxx run with unexplained weak Greater Heals post-respec [[d:priest#1476694041679757517]] (L-star, 2026-02-26).
- **[Disc]** Disc-focused shield/flash healer builds are viable "for fun" (e.g., taking Enlighten + Resurgent Shield + Mental Strength) but sacrifice ~18-24% total healing throughput from Spiritual Healing, and are explicitly called out by multiple posters as sub-optimal from a raid-throughput perspective even though enjoyable — [[d:priest#1470462761900183592]] (Pyronus, 2026-02-09), [[d:priest#1478094654460264570]] (Greendam, 2026-03-02: ~24% less healing after accounting for Spiritual Healing being a full-healing modifier not a base-only one, plus losing the strong Divine Fury/T2 capstone tradeoffs).

- **[Shadow]** Darkness talent (5/5) applies incorrectly to Mind Blast and Pain Spike — testing showed Pain Spike's damage range didn't scale with 5/5 Darkness at all, while Mind Flay and SW:P scaled correctly; confirmed to be a real bug by dev-adjacent poster and slated for a 1.18.1 fix — [[d:priest#1472019702606991605]] (McPewPew, 2026-02-14, initial test), [[d:priest#1472147167996280914]] (McPewPew, 2026-02-14, refined test showing Pain Spike range mismatch), [[d:priest#1472197137713463369]] (Fat Pibble with Air Pods, 2026-02-14: "Darkness is total and not base values"), [[d:priest#1472223649951125575]] (Fat Pibble with Air Pods, 2026-02-14: fix confirmed for 18.1).
- **[Holy]** Respeccing into Holy (particularly via the dual-spec "goblin device") can cause Improved Renew/Spiritual Healing healing values to silently under-apply (e.g., ticking ~350 instead of the correct ~420+, confirmed by the target's own health-bar movement, not just an addon misread); only known workaround is deleting the local WDB cache folder — [[d:priest#1476685485064585367]] (🐹 hamster 🐹, 2026-02-26), [[d:priest#1476694041679757517]] (L-star, 2026-02-26, independent corroboration).
- **[Shadow]** Mind Control breaking prematurely (e.g., on Razuvious/Faerlina) is most often caused by: standing at the edge of MC range rather than a few steps further in, someone dropping Tremor Totem, another player dispelling the MC'd unit, or accidentally fat-fingering another spell mid-channel — not usually a client bug — [[d:priest#1474928240593342515]] (30, 2026-02-22), [[d:priest#1474972369452793886]] (Mendeleev, 2026-02-22), [[d:priest#1480338698091364373]] (Greendam, 2026-03-09: dispelling opposite-faction targets during Lucifron MC prioritizes stripping their buffs over removing the MC due to cross-faction dispel-priority behavior, so use Restorative Potions or dispel same-faction only).
- **[Shadow]** Mind Control channel breaking spontaneously on stationary targets (e.g., Naxx Instructor/trash "understudies") is most often just the ~1% per 2-3 second heartbeat-resist chance compounding (~27% chance to break over a typical channel), not a deeper bug, assuming range/LoS/no-cast conditions are respected — [[d:priest#1472102462134423593]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-14), [[d:priest#1472301980151054498]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-02-14), disputed as possibly dispel-related by Greendam [[d:priest#1472294487546921126]] (2026-02-14).
- **[Item]** Shard of Nightmare's sleep effect not functioning is confirmed a bug (not intentional design), tracked on the bug tracker — [[d:priest#1480784577621524611]] (IllegallyParkedFrog, 2026-03-10), [[d:priest#1480786806977986570]] (🐹 hamster 🐹, 2026-03-10, links bug report #20408).
- **[Item]** Ornate Bloodstone Dagger (spellstrike-proc weapon) was changed/nerfed, apparently to close a spellstrike-rogue interaction; specifics of the change were unclear to posters — [[d:priest#1472693950858133607]] (Ydr, 2026-02-16), [[d:priest#1472694486306918652]] (Greendam, 2026-02-16).
