## Leveling
- **[Smite/Discipline]** Recommended talent order for a Smite-focused leveling build: Wand Specialization → Spirit Tap → then into Spiritual Guidance, before taking further Discipline talents — [[d:priest#1498605725612638208]] (Stunfisk, 2026-04-28).
- **[Discipline]** Spirit Tap's value for leveling: it lets you regen mana passively after every kill "without having to drink," used to answer whether it can be skipped in an early Disc leveling spec — [[d:priest#1509941377499402371]] (Shadurloc, 2026-05-29) responding to a screenshotted 2 Discipline / 10 Holy / 0 Shadow leveling spec (icon-only talent-calc image, exact talent names not legible) — [[d:priest#1509870043951923251]] (Marwat, 2026-05-29; question of whether Spirit Tap is mandatory left unanswered).
- **[General]** Downranking heals while leveling/dungeoning: not worth it early (no healing power to make it matter) or while leveling-healing dungeons generally (fights too short); downranking becomes common in late leveling and early level 60 due to gear/mana shortfalls; at endgame raid healing, downranking is largely abandoned again for better parses/meter position — [[d:priest#1494886816871092336]] (elle, 2026-04-18), [[d:priest#1494888479052533902]] (Greendam, 2026-04-18).
- **[Leveling][gear]** Alliance-side melee-focused priest leveling: most +Strength cloth quest reward items are Horde-only, making a "melee priest" (no offensive spells/wand) harder to gear on Alliance — [[d:priest#1504317535510597743]] (McPewPew, 2026-05-14; +3).
- **[Leveling][enchant]** For a melee-leveling priest, Fiery Weapon enchant was chosen over Crusader on the reasoning that Fiery has a fixed proc-per-minute so weapon speed doesn't matter (Crusader argued as more thematically fitting for melee, "Fiery is best on 1H stuff"); weapon DPS called the most important weapon stat while leveling — [[d:priest#1504472575764332655]] (Lynathel, 2026-05-14), [[d:priest#1504487007718609117]] (McPewPew, 2026-05-14; +1).
- **[Leveling]** Shadow vs Smite for leveling: Smite recommended as "way more custom changes than shadow" and thus more distinct an experience — [[d:priest#1498575111458914334]] (LuanaZoa, 2026-04-28; +1).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Discipline/Smite][T3 set]** 8-set-bonus opener: Chastise → Holy Fire → Power Word: Shield → 2x Smite → Holy Fire, then pump Chastise/shield stacks toward Blessed Wildfire as desired; described as "more loose and fun" rather than a strict rotation, hitting 5-6k+ crits on Holy Fire with full buffs/consumes — [[d:priest#1504764793372803122]] (HolyScheiss, 2026-05-15).
- **[Discipline/Smite]** Smite's instant-cast crit chain has an ICD that is order-dependent: if Smite crits before Holy Fire and Holy Fire also crits, the player loses a chance at back-to-back instant Smites; if Smite crits, Holy Fire doesn't, and the resulting instant Smite does crit, it grants another instant Smite. Considered intentional (removing the ICD would be "game breaking") but also called punishing — [[d:priest#1504813044545618073]] (Stunfisk, 2026-05-15), [[d:priest#1504813192776515785]] (Stunfisk, 2026-05-15), [[d:priest#1504813325635031150]] (Stunfisk, 2026-05-15), [[d:priest#1504768457709981850]] (HolyScheiss, 2026-05-15).
- **[Shadow][Mind Flay clip]** Clipping Mind Flay with the "nampower" addon's spell-queue feature adds roughly a 125ms delay between the last Mind Flay tick and the next spell's cast start (trainable down to ~50ms); clipping after 2 ticks happens on about 50% of casts; nampower's queuing is fine for Mages (much longer channel, clip less often) but costly for Priests who must clip Mind Flay frequently — [[d:priest#1494659622181797990]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-04-17), [[d:priest#1494659723784491118]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-04-17), [[d:priest#1494659832127553597]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-04-17), [[d:priest#1494660310336929883]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-04-17), [[d:priest#1494660401265115146]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-04-17).
- **[Shadow][Mind Flay clip — disputed]** Doomsvilla could not reproduce the 125ms delay when clipping after tick 2 with a current nampower build (fires instantly), speculating nampower had since been patched; disputed by Zitronenhaze's original testing — [[d:priest#1494666651105493052]] (Doomsvilla, 2026-04-17), [[d:priest#1494666864318746765]] (Doomsvilla, 2026-04-17).
- **[Shadow]** Stopping Mind Flay by moving is "REALLY bad" (extra delay vs. clipping via a fresh cast); many rotation situations require casting MF-rank2-into-MF-rank2 or MF-rank2-into-MF-rank3 back to back to avoid losing DoT uptime or missing a Mind Blast cooldown window (referenced from Zitronenhaze's pinned guide "gameplay" tab) — [[d:priest#1494662933056327902]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-04-17; +1), [[d:priest#1494663901382578277]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-04-17), [[d:priest#1494663968915062845]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-04-17).
- **[Shadow][macro]** Practical clip technique: keep channel-queuing ON for normal end-of-channel casts (queues the next Mind Flay to fire ASAP, ~75% of current latency before channel end, without clipping the last tick); for intentional clips, bind a separate button that does move-right-then-cast with 0ms delay between the two actions — [[d:priest#1494764743972491385]] (Doomsvilla, 2026-04-17).
- **[Discipline]** Pain Spike used by a Smite Priest as a trash finisher when a mob has under ~2 seconds left to live and a full spell cast isn't worth starting — [[d:priest#1504912888022831298]] (HolyScheiss, 2026-05-15).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Discipline/Smite]** Holy Nova spam build (using set-bonus stacks: 5 shield stacks, 10 Chastise stacks toward Blessed Wildfire) is viable and "adds flavor," but a dedicated AoE gearset is preferred over the T3 set for pure AoE: epic chest from Sunken Temple, "Bindings of Magic" bracers (Kara40), Kara40 legs, and other spell-hit-effect items (BoED, WoC) — [[d:priest#1504764793372803122]] (HolyScheiss, 2026-05-15).
- **[Shadow]** Shadow Priest AoE is limited to Holy Nova (which is Holy-school, not Shadow), since "Spriest was never intended to do AoE damage"; some players kept a separate Holy-damage AoE set, others skipped AoE entirely; wished-for fix (never implemented): make Holy Nova become Shadow Nova while in Shadowform, with adjusted spell power scaling / mana cost — [[d:priest#1504912888022831298]] (HolyScheiss, 2026-05-15), [[d:priest#1504891925373653002]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-05-15) — this was the single most common piece of feedback in a 13-Spriest community survey (see Contested/open questions).
- **[Discipline/Smite][BiS-conditional]** Windhorn Caverns 6-piece set: the Holy Nova AoE damage-proc rate felt low or capped to few targets from a pack (suspected proc-amount cap similar to BoED); the healing-effect side (15% movement speed for 8s) was assessed as strong and usable purely as a mobility tool for running dungeons/raids after a wipe — [[d:priest#1504772174337146920]] (HolyScheiss, 2026-05-15).

## Healing specifics
- **[Downranking]** No downranking needed at low levels (insufficient healing power to make it matter) or while leveling-healing dungeons (fights too short to matter); downranking becomes common in late leveling / early level 60 due to gear and mana shortfalls; at raid endgame, downranking is largely dropped again in favor of max-rank casts for better parses — [[d:priest#1494886816871092336]] (elle, 2026-04-18), [[d:priest#1494888479052533902]] (Greendam, 2026-04-18).
- **[Addons]** Recommended raid-frame/healing addons: Puppeteer ("most powerful," bigger on-screen frame, click-to-heal-with-any-button flexibility), pfUI raid frames (least clutter) and Luna also viable; Rinse addon also recommended as a companion tool — [[d:priest#1494954650951094394]] (Greendam, 2026-04-18), [[d:priest#1494976451882258603]] (Istrefi, 2026-04-18), [[d:priest#1494976772150788126]] (Istrefi, 2026-04-18).
- **[Shadow — never implemented]** Community-suggested redesign for Shadow Mend (Shadow Priest's spell-based self/party heal, considered ineffective): apply a "borrowed time"-style debuff/penalty to whoever receives the heal, with a scaling buff/bonus to the Shadow Priest based on how often they "heal" someone this way, on the reasoning that "shadow priests were never intended to heal with spells directly," being closer to a Vampiric-Embrace-leech identity — [[d:priest#1504916496789540966]] (HolyScheiss, 2026-05-15; +2; suggestion only, not implemented).
- **[Bug/interaction]** 6% spell-hit talent does not apply to set-bonus-triggered effects: Burning Zeal's guaranteed Shadow Word: Pain removal can still be prevented by a resist, forcing a full re-cast of SW:P + Holy Fire to reapply — [[d:priest#1494697448340193431]] (Misandria, 2026-04-17; +1), [[d:priest#1494700489034698842]] (Misandria, 2026-04-17).
- **[Bug/interaction]** Casting Holy Fire on a target that already has Shadow Word: Pain applied triggers two separate resist checks instead of one, believed to be unintended — [[d:priest#1494701319129141380]] (Zvyrhol, 2026-04-17).
- **[Suggestion — never implemented]** Player-proposed redesign so Holy Fire/set-bonus debuffs (e.g. Burning Zeal, Chastise) apply regardless of resist result: e.g. Holy Fire ticks have a chance to proc the 5-set DoT instead of a guaranteed application, or Chastise applies Burning Zeal to all targets hit by a Holy Fire — [[d:priest#1494706064128606432]] (Misandria, 2026-04-17; +2).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Kara40 — Anomalus]** The Eastern Plaguelands Arcane Resistance buff obtainable via the (now-removed) Champion/Proclaim Champion ability could be used to bypass or reduce the intended arcane-resistance gold-sink requirement for the Anomalus encounter in Kara40 (Tower of Karazhan); this interaction was known to the devs and intentionally left in ("if people wanna go out of their ways power to them"), the same design pattern as LBRS's fire-resistance buff obtainable via Mind Control — [[d:priest#1504884269825396807]] (Mithreon, 2026-05-15), [[d:priest#1504887971655057469]] (Dragunovi, 2026-05-15), [[d:priest#1504888050856235058]] (Dragunovi, 2026-05-15).
- **[Kara40 — progression]** Using the Champion MC-buff strategy for Anomalus required raid competency: the buff lasted only 1 hour and was lost on death, so a raid "couldn't wipe on the first 2 bosses" to make use of it before reaching Anomalus — [[d:priest#1504898252896600257]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-05-15), [[d:priest#1504899526379442287]] (Zitronenhaze, 2026-05-15).
- **[Kara40 — loot]** Ring of Holy Light drops from Kara40 trash and was used as a BiS Discipline/Smite trinket until server closure; Bindings of Magic (bracers) and Kara40 legs are cited as part of a dedicated AoE gearset for priests — [[d:priest#1504764793372803122]] (HolyScheiss, 2026-05-15).
