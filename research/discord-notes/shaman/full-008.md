## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Tank]** Rockbiter (Elemental Weapons talent) reworked from a flat % damage-reduction to an absorb shield: "Your physical damage builds up an earthen bulwark for 20% of the damage dealt, tripled while wearing a shield. The bulwark absorbs 5/10/15% of incoming damage for 8s or until it mitigates enough damage. Bulwark durability capped at 20% of max health." — [[d:shaman#1480077263381860416]] (Ciftar, quoting patch notes). Reduction is applied to damage after armor (per Frost) — [[d:shaman#1480477941975744534]], [[d:shaman#1480479209393623201]]. Change was explicitly PvP-motivated (shaman tank DR was too strong/PvP-abusable), not intended as a PvE nerf, but community was split on whether it's a net PvE buff or nerf; Zeruel/Nandieb argued it could be a single-target buff since shield caps at 15% almost always up, but a nerf to AoE/spike survivability — [[d:shaman#1480079493669326869]], [[d:shaman#1480476370881744926]], [[d:shaman#1480478975179624561]] (disputed, unresolved before patch).
- **[Tank]** Community consensus after patch-note review: shaman tank threat nerfed via Tier 2.5 5-piece set bonus change; T3/T3.5 sets have little/no defense/EHP upgrade, so tanks are pushed to raw stats and later tiers to "come online" — [[d:shaman#1482109583492120658]], [[d:shaman#1482110917570465972]], [[d:shaman#1481640818258477207]] (+multiple agreeing).
- **[General]** Relic swapping is being changed to a real GCD instead of the old "instant swap on every ability" workaround; players expect this to hurt totem-relic-swap-based ele/enh gameplay significantly — [[d:shaman#1478918768145993962]], [[d:shaman#1478924107138732063]], [[d:shaman#1480346220366135346]] (multiple agreeing it kills current relic-macro tech).
- **[Ele]** Flametongue Weapon change (root cause of the "fire ele"/Molten Blast-spam meta) is being reverted/nerfed next patch — expect Molten Blast spam ("fire ele") to be non-viable after 1.18.1: "it will be gone by next patch", "fire ele in all likelihood is gone" — [[d:shaman#1479988743493390479]], [[d:shaman#1479993267339788370]], [[d:shaman#1482088141891436595]] (+multiple confirmations e.g. [[d:shaman#1481670009473339446]], [[d:shaman#1481721938589257891]]). Cause: an unintended interaction ("bugged"/overtuned interaction with Earthquake hitting extra times was also separately being fixed) — [[d:shaman#1481602940111163422]].
- **[Ele]** Earth Shield / Lightning Shield: mana cost of Lightning Shield reduced in the patch; a player suggested Earth Shield rank 1 also get reduced cost to help leveling elemental shamans, paired with a new 70% cast-interrupt-avoidance passive — [[d:shaman#1479226040848220240]], [[d:shaman#1479226337821589618]] (suggestion, not confirmed as implemented).
- **[Enh]** Windfury proc chance is being raised (~+5%, described as changed to 25% total) — [[d:shaman#1481607595830673449]], [[d:shaman#1482106637560053762]] — but cannot be procced by Stormstrike, so real-world benefit is limited since players already get a lot of haste — [[d:shaman#1482106694816235601]], [[d:shaman#1482107589558014002]].
- **[Enh]** Windfury Totem changed from a temporary weapon imbue application to an aura — it does not work on any character that already has an active weapon imbue — [[d:shaman#1482404861407662253]], [[d:shaman#1482405907043324020]].
- **[Class-wide]** Devs previewed upcoming buffs: Enhancement getting buffed, Nature Elemental buffed, Fire Elemental effectively removed; changes summarized by one player as small ("antipushback talent" for ele, +5% WF proc + water shield buff for enh) — [[d:shaman#1481607595830673449]], [[d:shaman#1482088141891436595]].
- **[Resto]** Community expects Chain Heal to be touched again (nerfed) and/or Resto reworked in a later patch; devs reportedly stated resto shaman is getting "a look over" post-1.18.1, but 1.18.1 itself makes no resto changes besides removing the old T1 set bonus — [[d:shaman#1481360979307794433]], [[d:shaman#1481084158158835863]], [[d:shaman#1481604887312728176]], [[d:shaman#1481605022851665921]].
- **[PvP/general]** New "dynamic damage dampening" system introduced in 1.18.1, spec/talent-point based; exact mechanics/values not published for classes at time of writing (contested/open question) — [[d:shaman#1480081322822340679]], [[d:shaman#1480650225961009263]], [[d:shaman#1480486156146835580]].
- **[PvP]** New PvP armor set reward system replaces old 2-tier sets with 3 tiers (Rare ilvl ~71, Epic ilvl ~77, Epic ilvl ~92); top tier gated behind a new Conquest Points system tied to high-rated arena/wPvP — [[d:shaman#1481374586938327223]], [[d:shaman#1481402377016836228]].

- **[Enh]** Confirmed post-patch: Windfury Weapon proc chance raised from 20% to 25% per patch notes text — [[d:shaman#1484671660475940896]]. Stormstrike's old "double-proc" interaction with Windfury was fixed as a bug, and the WF proc-chance buff was explicitly intended to compensate for that fix — [[d:shaman#1484672127876595926]], [[d:shaman#1484672811631906826]].
- **[Enh]** New Elemental Weapons effect: Windfury imbue now also grants a stacking haste buff (tooltip said 2% per stack, intended to stack to 6/8/10% or similar across ranks) — heavily bugged at launch: many players saw it capped at 2 stacks / falling off before restacking, not gaining stacks from Stormstrike or Lightning Strike (only autos), and not stacking additively with Flurry; Not Normal confirmed via testing that it does stack to 6% total despite the tooltip, but the interaction with Flurry remained bugged for days — [[d:shaman#1484571771612172550]], [[d:shaman#1484572381904109799]], [[d:shaman#1484582435948855336]], [[d:shaman#1484607703354441898]], [[d:shaman#1484808044729864284]], [[d:shaman#1484808175034302484]].

- **[Ele]** Flametongue/Frostbrand Weapon spell-power scaling was patch-noted to now vary by weapon base attack speed instead of being flat, but at launch this coefficient change had NOT actually been implemented in-game (confirmed via testing) — [[d:shaman#1484375513194561568]], [[d:shaman#1484566631240700105]], [[d:shaman#1484606087784173730]], [[d:shaman#1484612212902330378]] (still unfixed after multiple server restarts).

Community math (Ordinary Joe) suggested that under normal MT conditions the shield should stay near-permanently full even with only auto-attacks feeding it (~900 DPS at 40% physical → ~216 absorb/sec vs. ~150 damage/sec taken at 15% on a 1000 DTPS fight), breaking down mainly on stuns, downtime, or very high burst damage — [[d:shaman#1484239802969886924]], [[d:shaman#1484239840408240199]]. In practice on live testing, many tanks found the shield "slow to charge," fell off from parries, and was generally harder to maintain for AoE/multi-target tanking than single-target — [[d:shaman#1484566233155375278]], [[d:shaman#1484569287556468736]], [[d:shaman#1484571343033729095]], [[d:shaman#1484809155410792458]] (unresolved/disputed at chunk end whether this is a bug or intended; see Contested).

- **[General]** Community members with visibility into dev discussions (Zhafu, sailorVEVO) confirm the Rockbiter/Earthen Bulwark rework was pushed largely unilaterally by the lead dev (Haaxor1689) and was not substantively vetted by the shaman Discord community beforehand, contrary to some players' assumption that the "shaman cabal" drives these decisions — [[d:shaman#1484812425772662847]], [[d:shaman#1484812848084553878]], [[d:shaman#1484813173579055245]], [[d:shaman#1484814283417190460]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Resto]** "Resto, always has been always will be" the best/default shaman spec for raid utility — [[d:shaman#1479464864308003021]] (+2 reactions).
- **[Enh]** Enhancement functions as both the tank spec (via talents for threat/survivability) and a viable DPS spec depending on talent choices; can even run tank talents with a 2H for DPS, missing only the threat-reduction talent — [[d:shaman#1479477357457702972]], [[d:shaman#1479478293181894839]], [[d:shaman#1479478619490226318]].
- **[Ele]** Community view: Ele DPS is fun but crit-dependent — mana sustain and damage scale with crit, so it's weak early and strong late; hit-capping matters less than raw crit once ~12-13% hit/500 SP is reached — [[d:shaman#1480346861108727838]], [[d:shaman#1480349166218510400]], [[d:shaman#1480349315602972856]].
- **[Ele]** Nature-resist/immune mobs are a major issue for Elemental in MC/BWL (decent nature resistance, not immune) and especially AQ20 (heavy nature immunity); AQ40 has less nature resistance than expected overall except specific bosses — [[d:shaman#1480348171811950653]], [[d:shaman#1480349616464728226]], [[d:shaman#1480348390444236820]].
- **[Ele]** "Fire ele" (Molten Blast spam build via Flametongue interaction) called good "if you want to only press one button and perform well" but explicitly temporary/pre-nerf — [[d:shaman#1479988743493390479]]; alternative "Nature ele" mixes Flame Shock/Molten Blast in with Lightning Bolt/Chain Lightning — [[d:shaman#1479908235858284786]], [[d:shaman#1479908328413855796]].
- **[Enh - Spellhance]** Spellhance (2H Flametongue-imbued caster-hybrid enh, ~22/28 spec) is real but niche: never beats traditional physical-Enhancement in single-target; its niche is AoE, where it beats physical-Enhancement in trash-heavy zones like Kara/ES; does not work in BWL because BWL dragons are fire-immune — [[d:shaman#1481702238333440081]], [[d:shaman#1481697850760106164]], [[d:shaman#1481698074928877749]], [[d:shaman#1482286791753994342]] ("Spellhance is the most fun but dogshit bad in mc/bwl").
- **[Tank]** Shaman tank widely called viable/"legit" for endgame — "Poor at start, better with talents, need to be ahead of the curve to tank dungeons early. End game they are extremely gear dependent but outperform other tanks in threat and damage by far if you build them properly" — [[d:shaman#1479393840405151774]] (+1), confirmed multiple times e.g. [[d:shaman#1482188429465882664]], [[d:shaman#1481776158172381380]] (viable in all 5-mans/UBRS/10-mans even in dungeon blues). Considered "offmeta" by devs deliberately, gated behind later gear tiers to be competitive — [[d:shaman#1481562694363648101]].
- **[Tank]** Shaman tank has the best single-target and AoE snap threat of any tank class in current content (Ambershire MC/ES) at the cost of being the squishiest tank (no defensive CD, lowest EHP); this asymmetry is why the threat nerf is controversial — [[d:shaman#1481296231560118402]], [[d:shaman#1481751828151668826]] (single-source high estimate), [[d:shaman#1482110917570465972]], [[d:shaman#1481758276562718941]].
- **[Tank]** Disputed: whether shaman tank survivability (EHP/crit avoidance) is worse than bear tanks — Zamka claims shaman takes less overall damage than a druid tank in comparable logs; Diving Instructor Knife disputes using specific boss logs showing shaman dying to back-to-back crits that a bear wouldn't — unresolved argument, treat as contested/anecdotal — [[d:shaman#1481765594167640217]]..[[d:shaman#1481770886691557548]].
- **[General]** No shaman tank items exist in AQ40 loot pool; players feel shaman tank gear support (mail tanking pieces) is generally thin compared to plate/leather tank itemization — [[d:shaman#1481301059405217934]], [[d:shaman#1480079807000608788]], [[d:shaman#1480079928295821412]].
- **[Enh]** Enhancement DPS/Tank talent overlap is very high (~90%) — a bloodlust-build tank enh in DPS gear can perform "almost as much dps as the dedicated dps shamans" mid-fight, missing effectively only Enhancing Totems — [[d:shaman#1481298646464069643]], [[d:shaman#1481304103836586024]].

- **[Ele]** Recurring community complaint: Elemental has no unique caster-group utility/totem, so in practice it is nearly always assigned to a melee group (for Windfury/Strength of Earth) rather than a caster group, unlike Enhancement which fits melee groups by design — "without a caster totem, you are simply a worse enhance shaman... you don't even receive their buffs (feral crit, battle shout, trueshot)" — [[d:shaman#1483918066323558481]], [[d:shaman#1483933073153327117]], [[d:shaman#1483935718089949385]]. Countered by others noting Ele already brings Threat Totem + Mana Totem and ~10% of a fight's damage from Searing Totem, and that the deeper issue is guilds wanting 2 shamans per melee group (oversupply relative to demand for a caster-group niche) rather than Ele itself being weak — [[d:shaman#1483934209788612839]], [[d:shaman#1483934820835917947]], [[d:shaman#1483935450975572079]].
- **[Raid comp]** Community-cited optimal 5-man melee raid group composition: Enhancement Shaman / Enhancement Shaman / 2H Fury Warrior / Combat Rogue / Combat Rogue — "objectively the highest rdps melee group right now," with Combat Rogues considered current top-tier raid DPS/buff providers, ahead of Feral Druid or MM Hunter for a group slot — [[d:shaman#1483970042243580015]], [[d:shaman#1483970236595306770]], [[d:shaman#1483967634750832793]].

## Leveling
- **[Enh]** Easiest/standard leveling route: Enhancement with a 2H weapon or 1H+shield — [[d:shaman#1479831446721007842]] (unanswered directly but treated as baseline assumption throughout thread), reinforced at [[d:shaman#1481395218761646344]] ("follow the pinned guide and level enh while being able to fill tank/dps/heal spots in dungeons").
- **[Leveling/stats]** For a leveling Enhancement 2H build, stat priority is Agility > Strength for leveling specifically (differs from raid priority of Str > Agi) — [[d:shaman#1482417811039981791]] ("Agi>Str for leveling. Every stat is useful for shaman, but those 2 the most").
- **[Ele]** Elemental leveling is comparatively rough: can't cast reliably while being hit (no pushback resistance pre-patch), and mana issues; better after 1.18.1 pushback-avoidance changes — [[d:shaman#1479226337821589618]], [[d:shaman#1481788271867597021]] (Ele leveling "biggest issues... just not being able to cast when being hit by mobs + mana").
- **[Leveling]** Tank-spec shaman is called the best power-leveling/dungeon-farming setup: "Tank shaman hands down best plvler. Drop stoneclaw, have your alt tag the mobs, kill mobs" — [[d:shaman#1480608160799653979]] (+1).
- **[Leveling/racials]** Orc racial "Hex" / spirit wolves (Feral Spirit-like ability from level 40) scales poorly at higher levels per one player's understanding (unconfirmed, "don't take my word for it") — [[d:shaman#1479149190088102111]].
- **[Race]** Racial choice for 2H weapon: Orcs get Axe Specialization; Taurens get bonus health — "whichever is stronger is what you take" — [[d:shaman#1479520261370810450]].
- **[Weapon imbues while leveling]** All imbues override Windfury Totem/Flametongue Totem; if no imbue is active, WFT and FTT stack with each other (but using a personal imbue is still generally better) — [[d:shaman#1479014654599495820]], confirmed "fun fact: firetongue totem and wf totem stack" [[d:shaman#1479062211136720898]] (+2 reactions).

- **[Leveling]** Enhancement is the clear consensus leveling spec over Resto: "there's no real reason to spec resto, the talents don't provide a meaningful contribution to your ability to heal until much later in the game," so speccing resto while leveling just slows kill speed for no benefit; you can still heal adequately in dungeons without resto talents — [[d:shaman#1482832014532153497]], [[d:shaman#1482832089924767908]], [[d:shaman#1482588031813947482]], [[d:shaman#1482588090991251467]].
- **[Leveling/imbues]** Detailed weapon-imbue-by-level-bracket progression for a leveling Enhancement shaman (Not Normal): levels 10-20, Flametongue and Frostbrand perform about evenly regardless of weapon speed (FT does flat damage independent of speed); levels 20-30, use Rockbiter for its interaction with Lightning Strike; levels 30-45ish, choice between Windfury or Rockbiter; levels 45-60, use Frostbrand once both Elemental Weapons (Enh) and Clearcasting (Ele) are trained; at 60, swap back to Windfury — [[d:shaman#1483998607655964712]].
- **[Leveling]** Standard leveling talent path: get Stormstrike + Elemental Weapons in Enhancement first, then dip into Elemental for Clearcasting, running Frostbrand imbue — the Frostbrand-triggered auto-crits combined with Clearcasting give strong mana sustain; this "Frostbrand + Clearcasting" playstyle is leveling-only and not used at endgame. - **[Leveling/stats]** For a leveling tank-spec Enhancement build, Stam > Int > Str is reasonable if committed to dungeon tanking (Agi may be better than Str for a leveling tank); however for general overworld solo leveling, prioritizing kill speed over survivability is recommended unless playing Hardcore — [[d:shaman#1482845235691262107]], [[d:shaman#1482849936709259308]].

## Rotation: single target
- **[Ele]** Basic single-target rotation: open with Flame Shock, then Lightning Bolt spam, weaving Chain Lightning on cooldown and Molten Blast to refresh Flame Shock — [[d:shaman#1479908235858284786]].
- **[Ele]** Frost Shock + Frostbrand Weapon interaction (with Elemental Weapons talent): Frostbrand melee procs auto-crit on targets carrying the Frost Shock debuff; this only matters vs. world mobs since the debuff doesn't land on level-60 bosses or training dummies, so in raids Windfury imbue outperforms Frostbrand — [[d:shaman#1481149720092217394]], [[d:shaman#1481151305593393273]], [[d:shaman#1481227004442443927]] (+1), [[d:shaman#1481256109980385281]], [[d:shaman#1481256649199976609]].
- **[Enh]** Weapon-imbue-by-situation cheat sheet: Frostbrand for solo, Flametongue for dungeon trash, Windfury for bosses (with a good weapon), Rockbiter "when you're ready to be a man" — [[d:shaman#1481259106588168303]].
- **[Ele - stats]** Shock spell scaling: Frost Shock and Earth Shock = 38.5% SP coefficient + 10% AP coefficient; Flame Shock = 21.4% SP / 8.5% AP on direct hit, plus 10% SP / 1.5% AP per DoT tick — [[d:shaman#1480665051777204328]] (Not Normal). Earlier/rougher estimate: shocks scale ~10% of AP at max rank — [[d:shaman#1479397225481375849]] (+1). Note shock AP-scaling talents only apply at max (or near-max) spell ranks, so shocks feel weak while leveling below rank cap — [[d:shaman#1480776712634761449]], [[d:shaman#1480776899079704576]], [[d:shaman#1480777327695757332]].
- **[Ele - Molten Blast mechanic]** Molten Blast's "release stored Flame Shock DoT ticks" effect can whiff/do no damage if you spam it before a Flame Shock tick has actually fired — this is a known mechanic, not purely a bug, though it changed behavior after the patch nerf (Molten Blast can no longer crit and procs its bonus effect only ~60% of the time) — [[d:shaman#1482323357469769728]], [[d:shaman#1482323429783765033]], [[d:shaman#1482325708901453846]], [[d:shaman#1482330319154581627]] (contested exact mechanic vs. pre-nerf behavior, unresolved by chunk end).
- **[Enh - tank]** MT rotation logic: Stormstrike + Shock are GCD-chain-locked when their cooldowns don't align, so extra crit/mitigation talent points matter less than raw threat/Bloodlust support — see Talent builds section, [[d:shaman#1480695712084922418]].

- **[Ele]** Raid single-target rotation confirmed by Not Normal: open with Flame Shock, refresh via Molten Blast roughly every 10-12s, Earthquake on cooldown, Chain Lightning on cooldown, weaving Lightning Bolt x3 between Chain Lightning casts; omit Flame Shock/Molten Blast entirely on fire-immune targets; if not well-geared for Clearcasting uptime, hold Chain Lightning until Clearcasting is up to manage mana — [[d:shaman#1482850420484210790]].
- **[Enh - Spellhance]** Detailed Spellhance (2H Flametongue) attack-window rotation math (Dwi): Flame Shock right after the first buffed Flametongue attack, then Stormstrike, then 2 Molten Blasts within Stormstrike's 3.5s buff window (with ~4% haste and 3-piece T3), then a Stormstrike-buffed Earth Shock + weapon attack at end of GCD, then 3 more Molten Blasts within the next window, repeating Stormstrike > 2 Molten Blasts > Earth Shock > 3 Molten Blasts > Earth Shock > 3 Molten Blasts > restart. Roughly 10% haste is needed to reliably fit 3 Molten Blasts into a 5s window; a ~1.90 speed weapon was calculated as ideal to land just under 1.5s effective speed with Flurry + 4% haste — [[d:shaman#1483497475074691204]], [[d:shaman#1483497760690012160]], [[d:shaman#1483498383011348592]]. Hand of Edward the Odd's instant-cast proc does not reset the auto-attack swing timer when used to instant-cast Molten Blast or Chain Lightning, unlike some other instants — [[d:shaman#1483519084049203223]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Ele]** Recommended stat-weight ratios relative to Spell Power for gear-planner tools: Hit ≈ 15 SP, Crit ≈ 10 SP, Haste ≈ 8 SP — [[d:shaman#1481698281204482068]] (Not Normal).
- **[Ele]** Rule of thumb: 1% spell hit ≈ roughly 1.5% spell crit in value for Elemental — [[d:shaman#1480349135218544810]] (Not Normal); don't over-invest to hit cap at the expense of overall gear quality — [[d:shaman#1480348860923777256]] (+3), [[d:shaman#1480349166218510400]] (crit becomes more valuable than hit past ~12-13% hit and ~500 SP).
- **[Ele]** Even hit-capped, there is always a baseline ~1% resist chance against boss-level targets — [[d:shaman#1480349412567027742]].
- **[Ele/tank]** Spell Penetration (esp. 15 SP-pen "cap" for the baseline 15 Nature Resist on raid bosses) is one of Elemental's highest-value DPS stats per itemization budget since the entire Ele kit is Nature damage — [[d:shaman#1480474342381977661]], [[d:shaman#1480474479237791825]]; not worth it for shaman tanks since tank threat/damage is mostly physical, not nature — [[d:shaman#1480474192129429545]], [[d:shaman#1480474558937956495]].
- **[Enh]** Hit-cap target for raiding Enhancement: 8% total hit needed (8% baseline miss chance vs. bosses), broken down as 3% from talents + 1% from 5 extra weapon skill (buyable) + 4% needed from gear — [[d:shaman#1482104891295141980]].
- **[Enh]** Str vs Agi on gear for raid DPS: 1 Str is always more valuable than 1 Agi ("crit doesn't mean shit if you don't hit for shit because you have shit attack power") — [[d:shaman#1482188189362946128]].
- **[Resto]** With sufficient mana-sustain (pots/tea), stacking pure Healing Power over Haste is the standard guidance; response depends on current healing power and main spell used (chain heal users should stack healing) — [[d:shaman#1480208781454348479]], [[d:shaman#1480209509971394571]].
- **[Tank]** ~250 unbuffed Fire Resistance is described as enough to comfortably tank Ragnaros (with occasional knockback from Rag) and handle Baron Geddon; ~315 FR gives a "decent chance" to fully resist Ragnaros's knockback and threat wipe — [[d:shaman#1480121471106945105]], [[d:shaman#1480181995899453493]], [[d:shaman#1480209394993201293]].
- **[Tank]** +100 HP vs +4-stat itemization tradeoff on tank chest debated inconclusively — both have valid arguments ("100 HP" vs "+4 stats"), with +4 items generally being far more expensive/luxury upgrades; +4 stat chest can double as a DPS item in some cases (SGC) — [[d:shaman#1478854051520315415]], [[d:shaman#1478857462194114560]], [[d:shaman#1478860344775671818]].

- **[Enh]** Precise hit/weapon-skill math confirmed by multiple sources: spell/melee hit cap for Enhancement is 8% total (3% from talents + 0.2% hit chance per weapon skill point above 300, e.g. 305 axe skill = +1% hit; +5 skill from the weapon-skill quest = +1% hit typically cited). An Orc's +3 racial axe skill is worth +0.6% hit (equivalent), needing 3.4% from gear instead of the "4% from gear" baseline for non-Orcs; with 313 weapon skill (2.6% from skill) only ~2.4% hit is needed from gear, meaning the last ~1% hit from gear alone is about 40% less "efficient" to chase since most is covered by weapon skill — [[d:shaman#1483167267842818272]], [[d:shaman#1483167322897383577]], [[d:shaman#1483191112150679705]], [[d:shaman#1483298677157265499]], [[d:shaman#1483300877241421975]], [[d:shaman#1483301086852022413]]. Separately, block is confirmed useless for shaman tank mitigation value (200 block value vs. 2000+ hits taken; no block-chance-boosting abilities exist for shaman unlike Paladin Holy Shield/Warrior Shield Block) and the 5/5 "Shield Specialization" (block chance) tank talent is considered a trap — take the row-1 stat talent instead — [[d:shaman#1482863699676102810]], [[d:shaman#1482868550723960872]], [[d:shaman#1482868883303170100]], [[d:shaman#1482870643258036274]].
- **[Ele]** Spell hit resolution mechanic (not a simple single-roll table like melee): spell hit/miss uses separate rolls for miss, then partial-resist, then crit; a level-63 (raid boss) target has an 83% base hit chance for spells, with level-based hit-chance penalties for lower-level targets calculated as 3/4/5/16% miss chance vs. level 60/61/62/63 mobs respectively in PvE; spell hit is capped at 99% even fully stacked in 1.12-based clients — [[d:shaman#1483033758096560158]] citing https://royalgiraffe.github.io/resist-guide, [[d:shaman#1483040690312052816]]. - **[Resto]** Haste-vs-healing-power tradeoff for Chain Heal builds: with Solnius trinket proc active, going from ~9% to ~11% haste can push Chain Heal cast time from just above to right at/under the 1.5s GCD floor; the marginal healing-power cost of chasing that last bit of haste via enchants (helm/shoulder/pants haste enchants) is estimated at under ~100-150 effective healing power given typical gear at this stage, making haste worth prioritizing up to the ~11% "soft cap" alongside Solnius, beyond which more Healing Power is preferred — [[d:shaman#1483802114420510912]], [[d:shaman#1483804395878486166]], [[d:shaman#1483807384605556736]], [[d:shaman#1483813752364208238]], [[d:shaman#1483819627997040691]], [[d:shaman#1483825522151784488]], [[d:shaman#1483826132884131973]]. ## Tanking specifics
- **[General/weapon]** Standard tank weapon-speed guidance: prefer a slower 1H weapon over a faster one at similar stats/DPS — [[d:shaman#1481776158172381380]] ("typically slower is better, if you're looking at two weapons with near even stats/dps").
- **[General]** Shaman tank viable from fresh 60 in dungeon blues through 5-mans/UBRS/10-mans; main risk at low gear is multi-pack AoE pulls before gear catches up — [[d:shaman#1481776153437016076]], [[d:shaman#1481776938514120915]].
- **[Weapon swap]** "Good shaman tanks have 4 gear sets": swap from 1H+Shield to 2H after establishing threat since threat multipliers are multiplicative and initial-pull threat matters most, but this sacrifices some ongoing threat/adds risk — [[d:shaman#1480713877640712294]], [[d:shaman#1480712645936943187]], [[d:shaman#1481296575639720069]] (can swap 2H mid-fight and be near-full DPS, keeping only Enhancing Totems as the missing talent).
- **[Tank/DR mechanics]** Post-rework Rockbiter absorb-shield applies after armor mitigation and is generally expected to be "always full" on single-target fights unless a boss hits for >15% of 20% max HP in one swing, which is rare at current gearing — [[d:shaman#1480477941975744534]], [[d:shaman#1480478194707599470]] (contested/theorycrafted pre-patch, not yet tested live).
- **[Tank]** No planned defensive cooldown for shaman tank (unlike Shamanistic Rage in later expansions) — devs reportedly avoid adding one due to PvP implications; community sentiment is mixed on whether it's needed given healthstone/tea-based recovery already covers most cases — [[d:shaman#1480080749754450022]], [[d:shaman#1480589474638462988]], [[d:shaman#1480081322822340679]] (dynamic dampening system might open room for one in the future).
- **[Tank]** NR (Nature Resist) gear debate for AQ40 volley/silence phases: one shaman argued Elemental Warding's 10% magic damage reduction talent makes a dedicated NR set less necessary for shaman than for other melee; alternative strategy is summoning "vampirism" pets (Barov Peasant Caller, Yeti trinket from Winterspring quest, Cinder Elemental from Fire Festival) instead of stacking NR — [[d:shaman#1480222354771804326]], [[d:shaman#1480225556107755731]], [[d:shaman#1480226070660911195]], [[d:shaman#1480226476698763344]] (10% DR "doesn't seem like a huge deal"), confirmed by Zeruel at [[d:shaman#1480242376181022770]] ("You're correct").

- **[Tank]** Damage-reduction order of operations confirmed by Ordinary Joe (theorycrafted, consistent with observed behavior): armor mitigation applies first, then Rockbiter/Earthen Bulwark's 15% reduction applies to the post-armor damage, then any further flat % reductions (e.g., old T2.5 5% bonus) apply multiplicatively on top of that, and Block Value is subtracted last from the final post-all-reductions damage value — meaning Block Value is proportionally far more valuable against small hits than against large ones (e.g., 250 block value is a 50% reduction on a 500-damage hit but only ~5% on a 5000-damage hit) — [[d:shaman#1484538733805179114]], [[d:shaman#1484538950738776064]], [[d:shaman#1484538994199888025]], [[d:shaman#1484539068623884298]], [[d:shaman#1484539135703257189]].
- **[Tank/Naxx]** Hateful Strike (Patchwerk) mechanic explained: targets whichever of the 2nd/3rd/4th highest-threat players currently has the most HP among that group, hitting for a large chunk of damage (~30k pre-armor, reduced by armor/DR); because it always targets highest-HP among that band, getting hit once often means you won't be picked again immediately since your HP is now lower — Ordinary Joe notes that Rockbiter's Earthen Bulwark falling off (e.g., if the shield hasn't built up yet early in a pull due to threat-establishment sequencing) can turn a normally-survivable Hateful Strike into a much larger effective hit — [[d:shaman#1484545083171930193]], [[d:shaman#1484545249891192913]], [[d:shaman#1484545285458890855]], [[d:shaman#1484544288892387380]].
- **[Tank]** Shaman tank is widely agreed (including by the devs, per community consensus) to be the squishiest tank in the game — lowest armor, no defensive cooldowns, historically lowest baseline DR, and (with the T2.5 change) reduced threat — with Fury-Prot Warrior as a possible exception since that's an optional/non-standard build; this is repeatedly cited as the core justification for community requests for a shaman-tank defensive cooldown (e.g., a Shamanistic-Rage-style ability), which devs have avoided adding due to PvP balance concerns — [[d:shaman#1484533235005395075]], [[d:shaman#1484598450065768600]], [[d:shaman#1484598513206558720]], [[d:shaman#1484598673370251325]], [[d:shaman#1481563514555338914]], [[d:shaman#1484545997056118835]].
- **[Tank]** Disputed counter-claim (Zamka, single MT's self-reported logs from a top progression guild): shaman tank takes consistently less damage-per-second than a comparable bear tank on most Kara40 bosses even without a defensive CD, attributing this to Rockbiter/Earthen Bulwark DR plus better avoidance stacking; disputed by Ordinary Joe and others as methodologically flawed (doesn't control for gear-priority differences, fight length, or effective-health/burst-survival differences vs. raw DTPS) — treat as contested, single-source claim vs. broad community consensus that shaman tank has the lowest EHP/no burst-survival tools — [[d:shaman#1484650625814757436]], [[d:shaman#1484652222250881226]], [[d:shaman#1484657945290608640]], [[d:shaman#1484659312734240930]], [[d:shaman#1484662831780729084]].
- **[Tank]** Community-proposed (unimplemented) defensive-cooldown design ideas floated in response to the squishiness problem: converting Spirit Armor/Enhancing Totems into an active mitigation cooldown; giving Earthen Bulwark a flat Defense-rating bonus (~60 Defense) while active, framed as PvE-only in effect since Defense has low PvP value; making Earthen Bulwark purgeable in PvP as a tradeoff for a stronger PvE version; or splitting Elemental Weapons into two talent paths (one keeping Bloodlust, one granting a Shamanistic-Rage-like defensive option) — all speculative, none confirmed as planned by devs — [[d:shaman#1484546929399431292]], [[d:shaman#1484547484213838036]], [[d:shaman#1484548705683247134]], [[d:shaman#1484549236560367686]], [[d:shaman#1484546860558323744]].
- **[Tank]** Confirmed: Flametongue's damage-buff-to-fire-totems does NOT count Shock spells as "attacks" for its proc condition — [[d:shaman#1484571215959031948]].
