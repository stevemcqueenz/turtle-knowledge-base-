- **[Tank]** Shield-related tank talent now grants extra threat specifically for using a shield, making 2H-vs-shield tanking threat comparisons "debatable" again — [[d:shaman#1424398236973531258]] (peter_hammer, 2025-10-06).
- **[Elemental/Fire]** Fire Elemental ("Fire Ele") build is a very recent, extremely strong outlier build widely expected to be nerfed once devs notice — [[d:shaman#1423402278500438037]], [[d:shaman#1423770752888344616]], [[d:shaman#1424480173927170058]] (Ash, sailorVEVO, Squid; +reactions on multiple).

- **[Elemental/Fire]** Fire Ele is judged broken/OP single-target DPS ("stupid good," "grossly OP," "brain dead 1-button spam") but is handicapped in MC/Onyxia/BWL because those raids are heavily fire-resistant, so it only shines from AQ onward — [[d:shaman#1423402460466380830]], [[d:shaman#1423485382405652541]], [[d:shaman#1424254279165808742]] (Not Normal, DemonBoyJr, Not Normal; multiple corroborations).
- **[Elemental]** Outside Fire Ele, the traditional 3 specs (Resto/Ele/Enh) are considered well-balanced with no urgent buff/nerf needed — [[d:shaman#1424129821591470372]], [[d:shaman#1423961202270277652]] (Not Normal, Zaaparou).
- **[Elemental vs Enhancement]** Enhancement is consistently recommended over Elemental for both leveling and raid DPS ("It is generally worse than enh in almost all aspects" for leveling; "You will not find it easier to outperform enhance as ele") — [[d:shaman#1424690353125982340]], [[d:shaman#1424816716407505028]], [[d:shaman#1424134297232605203]] (peter_hammer, Squid, Not Normal — leveling specifically "pretty bad", endgame "good, a lot of fun" but sub-Enh).
- **[Elemental]** Ele is "underappreciated" and viable/fun at endgame per Squid, but ele shamans are rare in top guilds — [[d:shaman#1424540735553802332]], [[d:shaman#1424636442021203968]].
- **[Resto]** Multiple players call Shaman the best healer in the game currently (raw sustained-damage healing, best raid utility, benefits from stacking) — [[d:shaman#1423936535975301120]] (+3 reactions), [[d:shaman#1423960261122986129]] (peter_hammer) — but disputed: paladins bring more raid utility until multiple paladins are stacked, and historically (classic) Paladin > Shaman > Druid in raid healing, "seems more even on turtle between those 3" now — [[d:shaman#1423960689428529152]], [[d:shaman#1423942018119503902]] (Diving Instructor Knife, Nandieb).
- **[Tank]** Shaman tank is a fully viable, fun OT (and MT with gear) per an extensive community-endorsed explainer: competitive with other tanks for dungeon tanking, gaps appear at endgame AoE tanking if not investing spellpower (unlike Paladin), low-variance damage intake from high armor + Rockbiter's innate reduction, no dedicated defensive CD, playable 2H (agi/crit/dodge/threat) or sword-and-board (str/parry/block) — [[d:shaman#1424335900141289492]] (peter_hammer, long post, thanked).
- **[Tank]** Community repeatedly praises shaman tank as "actually good and fun to play," easy threat, low incoming damage when paired with a healer, works from low levels through endgame OT — [[d:shaman#1423519492893315214]], [[d:shaman#1423832334180024413]], [[d:shaman#1424951996628340807]] (ThatsTheJoke, Burekslayer).

## Leveling
- **[Enhancement]** Standard/traditional leveling order: get Stormstrike + Elemental Weapons first as pure Enhance, THEN respec to dip into Elemental for Elemental Focus — going Ele-first (Elemental Focus before Stormstrike) is called "a terrible choice" since it delays key enhance tools and crit is too low early to benefit from EF anyway — [[d:shaman#1424480794470256773]], [[d:shaman#1424480661623935078]] (+2 reactions, Not Normal).
- **[Enhancement]** Weapon imbue choice while leveling: before Elemental Focus, Rockbiter (stable damage + 10% damage reduction with 3/3 Elemental Weapons) or Windfury (procs off Stormstrike-buffed Earth Shocks) are both fine, RB is more stable; Frostbrand + Elemental Weapons becomes the leveling powerhouse afterward for its huge mana-cost reduction ("abilities cost 60% less"), crit-fishing, and eventually removes the need for food/drink once Water Shield scales up — [[d:shaman#1423482779928236085]], [[d:shaman#1424094434504609844]] (DemonBoyJr, Tewas).
- **[Enhancement]** Weapon speed: always favor slow weapons for Lightning Strike scaling, though pre-Stormstrike damage is low enough (~90% of damage from empowered Lightning Shield procs) that it barely matters until ~30 — [[d:shaman#1424451243505484029]], [[d:shaman#1424451283552567346]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enhancement]** 1H+shield is favored over 2H while leveling/questing before Stormstrike: shield's ~15% damage reduction outweighs Lightning Strike's small early damage, especially in leather where the relative mitigation gain is high; after Stormstrike, kill speed jumps and pure LS damage matters less than before — [[d:shaman#1424458925595558011]], [[d:shaman#1424459354416877679]], [[d:shaman#1424459727773106186]] (billienomates, Not Normal).
- **[Enhancement]** Notable early custom weapon: Axe of Raging Winds, from a Turtle-custom shaman quest in Stonetalon, obtainable around level 22, lasts a long time (item id 58128) — [[d:shaman#1424445399829057630]] (Greybull-Ambershire).
- **[Enhancement]** Slow 2H weapon at 45+: Uldaman last boss drop; comparable options later: Crescent Grove 2H mace (on par), RFK 2H axe (slightly lower level but still an upgrade) — [[d:shaman#1423700684641144922]], [[d:shaman#1424420779445190677]] (Windshade, Not Normal).
- **[Leveling talent order — leveling tank]** A pinned tank-oriented leveling build/guide exists but is contested by some players as too low-damage; devs of that view (peter_hammer, Not Normal) counter there is "no noticeable DPS benefit from going non-tank enh when leveling" — [[d:shaman#1424419044609622016]], [[d:shaman#1424419722316877917]] (disputed; MindSlug reports frustration with build vs peter_hammer/Not Normal defense).
- **[Leveling]** A "top raider" leveling tip to skip Stormstrike in favor of the Concussion shock talent was corrected as wrong: Concussion is described as an "endgame talent," and the 10%-lower-mana-cost talent is more valuable while leveling — [[d:shaman#1424421393797353513]], [[d:shaman#1424421660735311892]] (peter_hammer; source of the bad tip unnamed/unconfirmed).
- **[Leveling — Resto]** Leveling as Resto is broadly agreed to be "meh"/miserable solo (struggles killing mobs for quests) unless spamming dungeons for a healer role; most players level as Enhance or Tank spec and only pick up Resto talents/gear near level 40-60 when prepping to heal raids — [[d:shaman#1424068759165075567]], [[d:shaman#1424069345964982363]], [[d:shaman#1424940970079813725]], [[d:shaman#1424942914378666076]] (Squid, Zaaparou, DemonBoyJr, billienomates).
- **[Leveling — HC]** Hardcore-specific: shamans can heal dungeons in a non-resto spec as long as they're over-leveled and carrying int gear; true Resto leveling is safer for clutch heals but mediocre solo — many donate for the cash-shop dual-spec toy (~$18) rather than pay the 350g gold respec cost — [[d:shaman#1424034833323262024]], [[d:shaman#1424035020787548332]] (Chalupa, vonsky).
- **[Leveling — Tank stats]** Pinned leveling guide recommends STA > AGI > STR > etc. for a leveling tank build; alternative simplified advice: crit/armor/dodge plus some stamina "so you don't die quickly" — [[d:shaman#1423546954008231938]], [[d:shaman#1423547632491565067]] (Adonnys asking, Hrochy answering) — question of "does everyone level as tank" left open/contested.
- **[Leveling — mana]** Struggling to kill even same-level mobs solo is usually a sign of overusing Shock spells (mana drain) combined with an outdated weapon; get Stormstrike ASAP and check gear before blaming talents — [[d:shaman#1424416195003809922]], [[d:shaman#1424416964092231762]] (Not Normal diagnosing MindSlug's leveling problems).
- **[Enhancement — imbue leveling]** Rockbiter and Flametongue are roughly comparable only at very low levels; Rockbiter is "just better" once Lightning Strike scales, and slow-weapon Rockbiter is "king until 30," after which players move to Windfury (DPS enhance) or Frostbrand — [[d:shaman#1425251478401978480]], [[d:shaman#1425229020961964274]] (billienomates). Frostbrand's base proc rate is 9 PPM (e.g. a 1.7s weapon ≈ 25.5% base chance, rising to 50.5% total with Elemental Weapons' flat +25%) — [[d:shaman#1425523740686549052]] (Not Normal).
- **[Elemental — leveling]** Elemental leveling is only tolerable from ~39-40 once Lightning Strike's cast time drops to 2s; before that there's no meaningful mana regen and Elemental Focus barely procs due to low crit, forcing constant drinking; Elemental Mastery's mere 10s duration is separately criticized as a weak "keystone" talent for a leveling caster — [[d:shaman#1426720089268424836]], [[d:shaman#1426732561639542864]] (Therix; repeatedly corroborated as a long-standing, unaddressed complaint).
- **[Tank — leveling rotation]** Concrete tank leveling loop: Rockbiter on main-hand, drop Stoneclaw Totem every pack; Searing/fire totems are a mana waste for tanking until the Totemic Alignment talent is taken — until then just tab-target and Shock; once Totemic Alignment is up, Stoneclaw and Nova totems become good AoE threat tools; once Lightning Strike unlocks, add it to the tab/shock loop. Early tanking is mana-limited, so avoid AoE totems when simple tab-threat is enough — [[d:shaman#1424961681087271086]], [[d:shaman#1426100807572389968]] (billienomates, DemonBoyJr).
- **[Leveling — stats]** For normal (non-tank) Enhancement leveling, stat priority is Str > Agi > Stam > Int; tank leveling shifts more weight toward Stamina — [[d:shaman#1426594071451013221]] (Not Normal).
- **[Tank — leveling duo]** Priest is called the ideal duo/leveling healer partner for a leveling shaman tank ("you can just send it") — [[d:shaman#1426757911891152896]] (ThatsTheJoke).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Elemental]** Core single-target loop: keep Flame Shock up, spam Lightning Bolt, weave Chain Lightning on cooldown or on Clearcasting procs (with better gear you don't need to wait for Clearcasting), refresh Flame Shock via Molten Blast starting the MB cast ~5s before the DoT falls off, and use Earthquake on cooldown (its aftershocks double-dip on crit damage, so it's worth using even single-target) — [[d:shaman#1424156800252645466]], [[d:shaman#1424159811288698961]], [[d:shaman#1424221501737009177]] (Not Normal, corroborated by Squid).
- **[Elemental]** A suggested priority-string variant: FS → CL → MB → EQ → MB → CL → MB → LB, with a note that alternating LB/MB more often may be even better (untested idea from "Peter") — [[d:shaman#1424159684310339584]], [[d:shaman#1424159332655829002]] (Squid).
- **[Fire Elemental]** Rotation is simply: pop cooldown/damage buffs, apply Flame Shock, spam Molten Blast, and keep Searing Totem up — extremely simple ("brain dead 1-button") but very strong single-target — [[d:shaman#1423402725831610539]], [[d:shaman#1424156829239218218]] (Not Normal).
- **[Spellhance]** Rotation mirrors normal Enhance ability usage, but Stormstrike is de-prioritized outside of maintaining its buff; Shocks get top priority, fit Lightning Strike/Stormstrike in without clipping shock cooldowns; use Fire Nova Totem in downtime then replace Searing Totem afterward — [[d:shaman#1424185064375320676]] (Not Normal).
- **[Tank]** Rank 1 Earth Shock is reserved purely for interrupts/emergency-mana casts — never use it for threat generation, use higher ranks for threat instead — [[d:shaman#1424427249884463326]], [[d:shaman#1424746021623435284]] (peter_hammer, Holidae, corroborated).
- **[Enhancement — contested]** A "sub channel" guide recommending Lightning Strike → Stormstrike → Earth Shock → Lightning Strike was called out as inconsistent, since Stormstrike's 12s buff outlasts Lightning Strike's ~9s cooldown in an incompatible way and Earth Shock uses its own charge system — [[d:shaman#1424125914920255669]], [[d:shaman#1424126166821896324]], [[d:shaman#1424126529222344875]] (braindance flagging it, Squid/Zeruel clarifying mechanics) — treat that specific guide's sequencing as disputed/unreliable.
- **[Tank]** Confirmed single/multi-target tank rotation: Earth Shock → Stormstrike → Lightning Strike loop; for multi-target the same loop, adding a Fire Nova Totem as enemies approach and a Stoneclaw Totem once they arrive — [[d:shaman#1425501957367533568]] (Squid).
- **[Enhancement/Elemental]** Frost Shock has essentially no real use in normal PvE rotations: Earth Shock does more damage, costs more mana at max rank, and benefits from Stormstrike's bonus damage. Frost Shock is only situationally correct when about to overshoot threat with Earth Shock or against Nature-immune targets; it's mainly relevant while leveling with Elemental Weapons for extra proc chances — [[d:shaman#1426778186393583666]] (DemonBoyJr).
- **[Mechanics]** Windfury Weapon (self-imbue) is a flat 20% proc chance with no internal cooldown on Turtle; Frostbrand is PPM-based at 9 PPM baseline — a common source of community confusion/misinformation that experienced players had to repeatedly correct — [[d:shaman#1427006776213110894]], [[d:shaman#1425841011829116969]], [[d:shaman#1425842399808327811]] (Not Normal; Windfury Totem, unlike the self-imbue, does have its own ICD).
- **[Enhancement]** Mechanism for why slow weapons are best for Enhancement: Attack Power's damage bonus scales with a weapon's base speed, so Stormstrike/Lightning Strike (both AP-scaled) hit harder with slower weapons; a weapon's own listed base damage range is only ~1/3 of its total damage output, with the rest coming from AP scaling — this (not just proc-rate math) is why slow weapons dominate despite giving fewer Frostbrand/on-hit procs — [[d:shaman#1426984111301595167]], [[d:shaman#1427006659175125034]] (Not Normal).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Tank]** AoE pulling/threat: place Stoneclaw Totem (buffed by Totemic Alignment talent to funnel pulse threat straight to the tank) on every pull; some players drop Fire Nova Totem + Stoneclaw together for big initial AoE threat, then just maintain Stoneclaw afterward; when not grouped with melee (e.g., pure dungeon groups), place Searing or Magma totem instead for extra burn — [[d:shaman#1423910870509027411]], [[d:shaman#1423911028248547412]], [[d:shaman#1424431771755348079]] (sailorVEVO, billienomates).
- **[Tank]** Improved Fire Totems talent: buffs Fire Nova speed and Searing Totem, but the accompanying nerf to Magma Totem's threat generation is a real tradeoff to weigh for pack-tanking — [[d:shaman#1423909386996158484]] (Ciftar; question, partially answered by the Stoneclaw-focused advice above — treat magma-vs-fire-nova-totems choice as open/situational).
- **[Enhancement]** Nova (Fire Nova) totems used aggressively for burst AoE DPS even in some single-target contexts, at the cost of heavy mana drain, framed as a deliberate mana-for-damage tradeoff unique to Enhance's resource design — [[d:shaman#1424845170339479707]] (Not Normal).
- **[Tank]** Confirmed mechanic: Stoneclaw Totem does NOT taunt — it applies a large amount of aggro to all enemies within its range, and with the 90% Totemic Alignment talent that aggro transfers directly to the tank as a large "free" threat source. Caution: while soloing, Stoneclaw now redirects pulled mobs onto you instead of acting as an escape distraction, unlike its old behavior — [[d:shaman#1425458216980058142]], [[d:shaman#1425459466526265344]] (billienomates).
- **[Tank]** Earth Shock's threat multiplier scales with the damage it deals (not a flat bonus) and is confirmed to be well above the commonly-cited 1.5x once Rockbiter, T2.5 set bonus, a 2% threat talent, and Spirit/armor-style talents are all stacked; max-rank Earth Shock is also the only rank that scales with Attack Power, effectively forcing its use for both damage and threat — [[d:shaman#1426098782017290281]], [[d:shaman#1425888524225482793]] (DemonBoyJr, Squid).
- **[Tank]** Lightning Strike can hit for up to ~5k threat in good gear despite its cooldown, per one tank's testing (Eluzia, single source/opinion that its threat-per-CD ratio should be even higher) — [[d:shaman#1425887237983436981]].
- **[Tank/threat]** Mana returned to the shaman (e.g. via Water Shield procs) also generates threat, so dropping self-buffs over group buffs (like skipping Windfury Totem) is less of a single-target threat loss than it first appears; by the same logic, Mana Spring Totem's mana return can draw aggro if a mob flees/fears toward it — [[d:shaman#1427010841416302622]], [[d:shaman#1427011338579742901]] (billienomates, Not Normal). Shaman tanks are reputed for having very strong AoE snap threat via Stoneclaw+Totemic Alignment ("stealing paladin's job") — [[d:shaman#1427010254062751905]] (Dodging).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[General]** Melee hit cap on Turtle WoW is 8% (down from vanilla's 9%) — [[d:shaman#1423411384552657017]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enhancement]** Weapon skill: 5 skill = 1% hit, and Turtle smoothed the weapon-skill curve to be fully linear (no vanilla-style breakpoints) — [[d:shaman#1424619210562080778]], [[d:shaman#1424618857641021501]], [[d:shaman#1424486091649581127]] (poto, Not Normal). Orcs can cleanly reach 315 weapon skill (axes) without overcapping glancing blows, worth roughly 2% extra effective crit by community stat-weights — called "the strongest case for using a particular race in the game currently" — [[d:shaman#1424619628310691944]], [[d:shaman#1424619940597731381]], [[d:shaman#1424485989211963593]]. Weapon skill also boosts glancing-blow reduction, and ~60% of enhance melee damage is glanceable, making weapon skill exceptionally strong for the spec — [[d:shaman#1424262456880009277]], [[d:shaman#1424262640028352624]] (Not Normal, Squid).
- **[Elemental]** No hard requirement to reach spell hitcap; don't sacrifice other stats chasing it. Crit is valued mainly for mana efficiency via Clearcasting rather than raw damage — [[d:shaman#1424692702359978025]], [[d:shaman#1424694768717402153]] (Frecher, Ciftar). One suggested approach: keep two gear sets, ~13% hit set for bosses, higher crit/spellpower set for trash (better mana/dps on trash) — [[d:shaman#1424695652092477461]] (𝓜𝓲𝓭𝓸𝓴𝓸, single source). Rough value: 1 spell crit ≈ 10-12 spellpower at medium gear level, scaling up with more spellpower — [[d:shaman#1424700586602790942]] (Ciftar).
- **[Restoration]** Stat-value math from Squid: 8 mp5 ≈ 192 extra mana over a 2-minute fight; converting Int to mana at ~15 int per 1 mana gives only ~90 mana from 8 equivalent int — "Int is much worse than mp5 on longer fights," though this doesn't fully weigh crit's mana-efficiency value — [[d:shaman#1423427240573800508]] through [[d:shaman#1423429206171320482]]. Crit is explicitly not favored by this expert for healers ("would rather provide stable consistent heals than spiky ones") — [[d:shaman#1423427240573800508]].
- **[Restoration]** Chain Heal's healing coefficient is disputed but very high: ~61% base with additional ~50%-reduced bounces (61%+30.5%+15.25% ≈ 108% across 3 targets per Squid) vs. an alternate calc assuming an 85.7% base (3s cast/3.5) with -50% per chain giving up to ~150% total SP coefficient (Not Normal) — called possibly the "highest healing coefficient in the game" — [[d:shaman#1423428990408065125]], [[d:shaman#1424794434331087071]], [[d:shaman#1424798823334805576]] (contested exact number, single-thread math).
- **[Restoration]** MP5 is considered devalued on Turtle vs. old vanilla/2019-classic wisdom (where mp5 was "god tier" for resto): crossfaction access to Paladin's Blessing of Wisdom plus Shaman's own Mana Spring Totem, plus newer talents (Tidal Surge, Improved Water Shield, Undertow) reduce mp5's marginal value — [[d:shaman#1424162047028891770]], [[d:shaman#1424163612141948928]], [[d:shaman#1424163810062766180]] (Squall noting the discrepancy, Not Normal explaining). A counter-claim that "mp5 is effectively mp2" (regens twice as fast as tooltip implies) is unconfirmed/disputed — [[d:shaman#1424163612141948928]], [[d:shaman#1424164901940629524]] (Zaaparou pushes back on the exact framing).
- **[General]** Spirit provides zero direct mana-regen benefit to shaman while casting (unlike Druid/Priest, who get "% mana regen while casting" effects); mp5 from spirit is paused during normal mana spending — [[d:shaman#1424252986439503922]], [[d:shaman#1424253217612497028]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enhancement]** Theoretical max haste-stacking (Enh): Kiss + Bloodlust + Berserking + Juju Flurry + Potion of Quickness + T3.5 5-piece bonus can reach up to ~241% effective haste for a 15s window per one spreadsheet estimate, translating to a theoretical 1.37s swing timer with Atiesh/Chastise buffs added — though there's a disputed claim that 2H weapons can't swing faster than a 1.5s floor — [[d:shaman#1424489135636484157]], [[d:shaman#1424489248379375779]], [[d:shaman#1424489875251396692]], [[d:shaman#1424489607478902868]] (Not Normal's numbers, Squid's floor claim; unresolved).
- **[Enhancement]** Troll (Berserking) may edge out Orc for burst-haste-stacking scenarios/fight lengths, since Blood Fury gives AP+SP (not haste) — but general consensus still favors Orc overall for the weapon-skill advantage above — [[d:shaman#1424486502666342563]], [[d:shaman#1424487097057935542]] (billienomates vs. broader poto/Not Normal consensus; contested/situational).
- **[Enhancement]** Vampirism (life-leech) gear stat is confirmed to proc off melee/Windfury strikes, but is judged mostly useful for solo elite-farming rather than raid DPS — [[d:shaman#1423427259720798280]], [[d:shaman#1423427354633699379]] (Squid).
- **[Elemental — Earth Shock]** With consumables and attack power alone (no elemental/spellpower gear), Earth Shock crits up to ~3k are achievable; investing in full elemental gear isn't necessary except for trinket/neck/ring slots where spell crit is worth prioritizing — [[d:shaman#1423548375432958033]] (Eluzia, single source, re: threat/tank-adjacent shock damage).
- **[Enhancement]** Sulfuras's proc alone is valued at the stat-weight equivalent of ~76 Strength on Not Normal's spreadsheet, explaining why it out-values weapons with much better raw stats/speed for both physhance and spellhance — [[d:shaman#1425964923162329218]].
- **[Enhancement]** Deep-endgame Enh stat conversions from Not Normal's spreadsheet: 1% haste ≈ 9 Strength; 1 spell/melee crit ≈ 20.324 Attack Power ≈ 17.39 Agility (with Kings+Ancestral Knowledge) ≈ 8.76 Strength (≈ 1 Str : 2 Agi) — [[d:shaman#1425186503084212254]], [[d:shaman#1425966601450291240]]. For a spellhance build specifically: 1 spellpower ≈ 2 Attack Power, 1 Strength ≈ 2.3 Attack Power — [[d:shaman#1426218252438077530]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enhancement]** At 315 weapon skill you're at the glancing-blow cap; any weapon-skill value beyond that point comes only from continued dodge reduction, not further glance reduction — [[d:shaman#1425196631825059850]], [[d:shaman#1425197081076830248]] (Not Normal, Squid).
- **[Spellhance]** Elemental Devastation triggers off physical (melee) crit, not spell crit, making physical crit unusually valuable for spellhance — roughly a third of crit's total stat-weight value in a spellhance build comes from Elemental Devastation's proc alone; Ele Deva has a flat 10s duration vs. Flurry's stack-based ~6-8s (attack-speed dependent) duration, so Ele Deva uptime should always be ≥ Flurry's uptime for the same crit rate — [[d:shaman#1426218630554583101]], [[d:shaman#1425202105819332721]], [[d:shaman#1425201099132113087]] (Not Normal, Squid, detailed collaborative math).
- **[Elemental]** Partial-resist mechanic explained: each hit independently rolls a 0%/25%/50%/75% resist against the target's relevant resistance stat; every level-63 boss innately has 15 Nature Resistance (causing visible partial resists even without special boss abilities); Curse of Elements reduces resistance by ~75 (contested; initially assumed higher); +spell penetration gear/jewelry enchants (+6 spell pen per gem) help offset it — [[d:shaman#1426456257975422976]], [[d:shaman#1425563428931637409]], [[d:shaman#1426592626596909087]] (Squid, Not Normal, Imperator).
- **[Restoration]** +healing itemization complaint: raw "of Healing" epics with no Int/Stam/mp5 are usually worse picks than lower-headline-number blues/greens that carry stat budget elsewhere — "any healer worth their salt would never" take a 62-healing-power green over a 48-healing-power item with 23 Int/Stam and 8 mp5 — a broad community view even among non-healers — [[d:shaman#1425198074027839570]], [[d:shaman#1425197111401648149]] (Not Normal, Squall/Zeruel pushing back that itemization still feels too flat between raid tiers).
- **[Restoration]** Concrete Chain Heal coefficient math: the healing difference between Rank 1 and Rank 2 base healing (~92.5 avg healing) works out via Chain Heal's coefficient to roughly 149 healing power of equivalent value — [[d:shaman#1425200401577152583]] (Not Normal).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Enhancement]** Weapon imbue progression while leveling/soloing: Rockbiter until Clearcasting + Elemental Weapons are online, then switch to Frostbrand + Elemental Weapons for near-constant Clearcasting uptime and mana efficiency ("95% of the time" the right call per sailorVEVO); a minority (Zaaparou) still prefer Rockbiter with a fast 1H + high crit due to personal testing, calling it close — [[d:shaman#1423587203329556500]], [[d:shaman#1423590255898005657]], [[d:shaman#1423590387980697710]], [[d:shaman#1423588432478867536]] (contested minority view flagged).
- **[Enhancement — raid]** In raids, Windfury (main-hand imbue) is standard for enhance melee DPS; Flametongue can out-damage it specifically against very-high-armor targets — [[d:shaman#1423583754823270462]], [[d:shaman#1423587072572129382]] (kaio, billienomates, Zaaparou).
- **[General]** Windfury Weapon totem and Flametongue Weapon totem stack with each other, but a player's own weapon imbue overrides seeing the totem's version of the same buff type on themselves — party members without that imbue will still benefit from the totem version — [[d:shaman#1424039912524484778]], [[d:shaman#1424035380495388702]], [[d:shaman#1424035462154027099]] (Zaaparou, Squid).
- **[Tank]** Crusader is the standard/only real weapon enchant choice for tank shaman ("There is no alternative to crusader"); guides recommending Unholy Weapon or Icy Chill instead are considered outdated/wrong — [[d:shaman#1423909047710650400]], [[d:shaman#1423910406115561473]], [[d:shaman#1423710213818814484]] (poto, DemonBoyJr).
- **[Enhancement]** Jewelry enchant preference: +3 all stats over +6 Strength for general play (pure min-max DPS still favors +6 Str); best cloak enchant choices at top-tier are only -2% threat or +3 Agility, and even these don't make top-end enhance threat "manageable" — [[d:shaman#1424807431871135848]], [[d:shaman#1424807626805346426]], [[d:shaman#1424807771668353034]], [[d:shaman#1424808081736466583]] (Squid, Tomo).
- **[General]** Weapon-imbue-vs-totem exclusivity confirmed precisely: an active weapon imbue always blocks benefiting from Flametongue Totem or Windfury Totem (any totem that adds an effect to your melee swings won't stack with an imbue), but non-swing-affecting totems (Strength of Earth, Grace of Air, Stoneskin, etc.) still apply normally — [[d:shaman#1426980357160308968]], [[d:shaman#1426980409215942756]], [[d:shaman#1426980459971350639]] (Holidae).
- **[Enhancement — leveling]** Rockbiter is specifically discouraged for a pure-DPS leveling build because its threat-increase component actively works against you if you're not tanking ("hard to deal dps if you're on the ground") — [[d:shaman#1425272571355463732]] (Spicyahi).
- **[Enhancement]** Crusader "blows 30 spellpower out of the water and it's not even close" as a weapon enchant, making it the default choice even for spellhance builds where Str and SP are otherwise close in value — [[d:shaman#1425988072763293828]] (Not Normal). Flametongue's and Frostbrand's spellpower coefficients are both a flat 10% (contrasted with Retribution Paladins' Seal of Righteousness getting 12.5% × weapon speed on every hit) — widely viewed as outdated/needing a numbers update — [[d:shaman#1425316367929839648]] (Not Normal).

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[Raiding]** Baseline T1-phase raid consumables: Fire Protection Potions plus normal class consumes (Mongoose, Wizard Oil); add Greater Fire/Arcane Protection potions for specific fire/arcane-heavy bosses; Juju Embers are worth farming early since they're also needed into BWL — [[d:shaman#1424898432392495145]], [[d:shaman#1424899470562299955]], [[d:shaman#1424899747449143459]] (Tewas, Dwi).
- **[Restoration / mana]** Oranges (mage-food style items, ~3% mana/hp per second) are unlocked from AQ20; Fruitcakes (~5% mana/hp per second) are bought in Booty Bay (100g one-time unlock, then 40s each in stacks of 5/20) and are a big deal for mana-tight healers to drink full during a fight; Teas serve as a cheap, easy substitute for Dark/Demonic Rune-type instant mana on Turtle — [[d:shaman#1424165669565501492]]/[[d:shaman#1424165868056612896]], [[d:shaman#1424166931971309638]], [[d:shaman#1424167055615197306]] (Zaaparou, Squall).
- **[PvP — Resto]** A strong flag-carry combo for Resto in PvP uses debuff food + Rocket Boots + a "fap" item to survive/kite — [[d:shaman#1423638685865480314]] (Zaaparou, single source, terminology unclear).
- **[Consumables]** Budget-conscious Enhance raid consumable stack: Elixir of the Giants, Mongoose, Roids (Blasted Lands), Zanza's potions from ZG rep if available, plus a stack of cheap (~20s each) Nordanaar Herbal Teas — [[d:shaman#1425973555417579562]], [[d:shaman#1425973667539714048]] (Squid). Stacking rule: Merlot/Rumsey Rum and the Zanza potions do not stack with each other (pick one); concoctions (e.g. Mongoose+Dreamshard, Firewater+Dreamtonic, Giants+Greater Arcane Elixir) each occupy one buff slot and do NOT stack with their standalone constituent elixirs — [[d:shaman#1425136654405992469]], [[d:shaman#1425974998694232184]] (Squid).
- **[Consumables]** Flask of Distilled Wisdom (mana) vs. Flask of the Titans (HP) value is realm/progression dependent: on a realm with smaller mana pools (~8k), an extra 2k mana from the wisdom flask barely matters, so Titans wins on cost-efficiency there, while on a less-progressed/newer realm the mana flask can still be worth it — [[d:shaman#1425348172443680789]] (Squid).
- **[Consumables]** A heavily-progressed player reports using ~60 Nordanaar Herbal Teas in a single Naxx raid night, underscoring how central cheap teas are to sustained mana for endgame play — [[d:shaman#1425188939093774358]] (Squid).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Addons/mechanics]** The Nampower addon removes the client's default forced pause between totem drops (normally requires a "spell completed" confirmation before the next cast); with Nampower, players can bind up to 4 totem drops to a single macro button, firing near-instantly — [[d:shaman#1423408732406157382]], [[d:shaman#1423408844704452629]], [[d:shaman#1423416968869970021]], [[d:shaman#1423417184297812122]] (Holidae, Squid). Caveats: Nampower totem-drop macros can randomly fail to fire a totem that has its own individual cooldown when combined with others (e.g., Fire Nova + Earthbind in one macro) — [[d:shaman#1423412150612922378]] (Holidae); Nampower also doesn't reliably support equip-commands or spells with cast times inside macros (e.g., a relic-swap macro for Lightning Bolt) — [[d:shaman#1424853371629863084]], [[d:shaman#1424850767768191016]] (Squid, Fraya).
- **[Addons]** Call of the Elements (CoE) addon is widely used for totem-set macros (`/coe throwset "name"`, `/coe forcethrowset "name"`); it has recurring bugs requiring `/reloadui`, especially with totem timers, and some suspect certain gear/talent combos can trigger it (unconfirmed) — [[d:shaman#1423406805517865000]], [[d:shaman#1423439711321591941]], [[d:shaman#1423449239064805567]], [[d:shaman#1424048817883316224]] (Adonnys, Zeruel, ThatsTheJoke; alternative addons: ArchiTotem `github.com/codeshard/ArchiTotem` and CallOfElements `github.com/MarcelineVQ/CallOfElements`, [[d:shaman#1424053360591507578]] Zaaparou).
- **[Mechanics — bug]** The buff-slot cap appears to have a real hard limit (not just visual) around 32: when buff-capped, reapplying Lightning/Water Shield causes the shield to visually fail to appear and denies Lightning Strike's "empowered shield" proc until another buff expires — reported and reproduced by two players; exact number contested (32-33 visual, possibly 64 hidden total per one guess) — [[d:shaman#1424721215981097042]], [[d:shaman#1424725822761078814]], [[d:shaman#1424726412555714656]], [[d:shaman#1424903617575387176]] (phyx, Zaaparou, Gruks, Greybull-Ambershire).
- **[Tank]** Threat mechanics: Rockbiter Weapon's threat component is now a flat 35% increase rather than an old AP-scaled bonus, invalidating old dagger-recommending tank guides — [[d:shaman#1424460395892048006]] (Squid). Rank 1 Earth Shock should be bound and used exclusively for interrupts, never threat — [[d:shaman#1424427249884463326]], [[d:shaman#1424746021623435284]] (peter_hammer, Holidae).
- **[Elemental]** Molten Blast can refresh Flame Shock's duration even when the DoT is being applied via Searing Totem (fire totem procs count) — reported as tested-and-confirmed by at least one player relaying secondhand testing — [[d:shaman#1424883689728381019]], [[d:shaman#1424903952931094669]] (Not Normal, Spicyahi; single-hop confirmation, flagged as not personally tested).
- **[General bug]** Grandstaff (legendary-tier staff) does not currently proc off HoTs — flagged as a known bug with an open bug-report ticket (#16806) players are encouraged to upvote — [[d:shaman#1424145137243787396]] (Frood).
- **[Mechanics]** Totem of the Flowing Water's water-totem bonus effects (see 1.18.1 changes) only trigger while IN COMBAT — players who tested it out of combat wrongly concluded it was non-functional — [[d:shaman#1425807929390661642]], [[d:shaman#1425808141009817621]] (Unapologetically proud Shammy).
- **[Elemental]** Relic-swap macro technique for Flame Shock: since Molten Blast/Flame Shock refreshes reset the DoT (so you don't want to clip it with Earth Shock while the dot is up), players macro in Flame-Shock-boosting relics like Totem of Rage for a net DPS gain; the "Crackling" relic effect is essentially free to macro in this way, while whether "Broken Earth" or "Stonebreaker" is the better relic to macro onto Earth Shock specifically is unresolved (Stonebreaker alone likely has poor uptime) — [[d:shaman#1426219962862665882]], [[d:shaman#1426220183466278972]] (Not Normal).
- **[General]** Sulfuron Ingots (a Sulfuras crafting material) are described as "the bane" of anyone trying to acquire the weapon due to scarcity/PUG raid-roll competition — recommended to join a guild that pools mats rather than solo-farm — [[d:shaman#1426076298023014400]] (poto).
- **[Enhancement/Spellhance]** Fire Nova Totem does not snapshot Flametongue's damage bonus — Flametongue must remain equipped until the totem's ~4s delayed pulse actually fires for the fire-damage buff to apply, complicating imbue-swap AoE macro tricks — [[d:shaman#1425217914130268160]] (Not Normal).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Tank]** Full playstyle summary (peter_hammer, extensively quoted/thanked): tank shaman lacks a dedicated tanking-ability kit but is competitive with traditional tanks in dungeons and viable as OT (or MT) at endgame; relies on fire totems/talents for AoE threat (unlike unresistable AoE threat tools other tanks get), which creates endgame AoE gaps without spellpower investment (similar tradeoff to Paladin); high armor + Rockbiter's inherent damage reduction give a low-variance damage profile without sacrificing damage/threat output the way other tanks' stance-swaps do; two viable tank styles — 2H (agility/crit/dodge/threat) or 1H+shield (strength/parry/block); heavy talent overlap with Enhancement DPS allows quick spec-flexing — [[d:shaman#1424335900141289492]].
- **[Tank]** Threat comparison: shaman tanks can out-threat many DPS classes in a min-maxed gear/talent setup according to Eluzia (mitigation set / threat set / mana set rotation across 3 gear sets, clearing up to Naxx without drinking except a few AQ20 mobs/1 Kara10 boss) — disputed by Squid who maintains Bears will always have more HP/armor headroom — [[d:shaman#1423533006596870219]] vs [[d:shaman#1423533392296677459]] (Contested).
- **[Tank]** AR fight gearing: leather AR set (melee stats) generally preferred over the healing-only mail AR set for threat, unless the encounter has meaningful physical damage requiring the mail set's higher base armor — [[d:shaman#1423527578341474324]], [[d:shaman#1423565304230973530]] (Jakeends).
- **[Tank]** No dedicated pre-AQ tanking gear exists; players progress successfully as tanks using Enhance sets/off-pieces pre-AQ — [[d:shaman#1423681987155066942]] (peter_hammer).
- **[Tank]** 2H tanking is sometimes used specifically to boost threat output ("Zamka would 2H tank so he could get more threat") — [[d:shaman#1423506014531096616]] (DemonBoyJr), though Shield Spec's threat buff (see 1.18.1 changes) makes the 2H-vs-shield threat comparison debatable again per peter_hammer.
- **[Tank]** Leveling-tank core loop: get Totemic Alignment talent, use Rockbiter, cast Stoneclaw Totem, Earth Shock for threat/profit; pair with a threat meter and only spend extra mana on threat when actually needed — [[d:shaman#1424737310695624776]], [[d:shaman#1424744056956784711]] (Ciftar, Zeruel).
- **[Tank — concrete gearing]** Mr Benedict's tested Phase-1 tank benchmark: tanking everything with only 315 Defense (well below old "defense cap" habits) by instead stacking Stamina/Armor/mainly Dodge — ~7500 Armor, ~4.25k HP unbuffed, 46% total avoidance (~23% Dodge) — "no issues whatsoever"; his practical minimum bar for viable tanking is ~7000 Armor, 20%+ Dodge, ~6% Hit — [[d:shaman#1426183303240876082]], [[d:shaman#1426617958221873305]], [[d:shaman#1426618393905070183]].
- **[Tank]** Not Normal: Defense is "not really a great stat, contrary to old vanilla common wisdom" for a shaman tank — prioritize a good shield, then stack Agi/Stam/Dodge; always keep Grace of Air Totem up for its 4-5% dodge chance — [[d:shaman#1426607169578602557]], [[d:shaman#1426607732659847380]]. Squid counters that avoidance stacking has real limits: even well-geared, a shaman tank is still critted ~5.6% and crushed ~15% of the time, and extra dodge mostly just prevents damage a healer should already cover — [[d:shaman#1426618944029982872]] — disagreement not fully resolved, value framed as diminishing-but-nonzero (Contested).
- **[Tank]** Confirmed: crush-immunity/avoidance cap is not achievable pre-AQ for a shaman tank, unlike Warrior/Paladin who get 20 free Defense from talents; Druids get roughly triple the armor values of other tank classes by base design — [[d:shaman#1425570664915406918]], [[d:shaman#1425571359974494439]] (poto).
- **[Tank — design gap]** Multiple active tanks flag that shaman has no analogue to Shield Wall/Astral Shift-style defensive cooldown; the closest thing (Earth Shield) only reduces heal-spell pushback and "helps very little" as damage mitigation — a real, acknowledged design gap, generally attributed to PvP balance concerns — [[d:shaman#1426799420065714329]], [[d:shaman#1426803841357189140]] (Hopestotem).
- **[Tank — perception]** Because loot databases (Atlasloot) show almost no dedicated shaman tank gear, shaman tanks face an uphill battle being taken seriously by guilds even though their actual threat/mitigation performs fine — a recurring, unresolved social/perception complaint distinct from the numeric gear gap — [[d:shaman#1426799236892200982]] (Zeruel).
- **[Tank — boss reports]** poto (on a separate, less-progressed realm) reports tanking Nefarian weekly with no issue, calling threat great and mitigation "not that bad" — [[d:shaman#1426756594011603099]].
- **[Tank]** Stoneskin Totem's talent bonus is confirmed via in-game character-sheet testing to be +30% Block RATE, not +30% Block Value as the tooltip wording might suggest — a real point of community confusion — [[d:shaman#1425321308484403310]] (Wahisky, billienomates).

## Healing specifics
- **[Resto]** Best-healer debate reasons (pro-shaman): high sustained raid-damage healing, best raid utility of any healer, and benefits strongly from stacking multiple shamans — [[d:shaman#1423960261122986129]] (peter_hammer). Countered: Paladin utility remains stronger until multiple Paladins are stacked — [[d:shaman#1423960689428529152]] (Diving Instructor Knife).
- **[Resto]** Practical dungeon-healing approach recommended: spam Healing Wave rank 3-4 on a tank to signal attentiveness while basically never running OOM — [[d:shaman#1423689120575651962]], [[d:shaman#1423689222509957162]] (Squid).
- **[Resto — mana]** Downranking is rarely needed at high gear levels except in mana-race sections (e.g., portions of Naxx en route to Razuvious) — [[d:shaman#1424847931294613514]] (peter_hammer).
- **[Resto]** Water Shield vs Lightning Shield tradeoff for mana management during a fight: start on Lightning Shield, swap to Water Shield once mana gets low, though this is a real DPS/HPS loss when done mid-fight — [[d:shaman#1424865709695172649]], [[d:shaman#1424851314323488860]] (Spicyahi, Not Normal).
- **[Resto — gear]** New Water Totem reworked buffs are relevant to healer totem choice: Healing Stream now gives +2% healing done/taken to nearby party (see 1.18.1 changes); some healers now use Healing Stream on specific fights (Thaddius, Anomalus, Chess, maybe Gnarlmoon) rather than always defaulting to Mana Spring — [[d:shaman#1423963185177956355]], [[d:shaman#1423962328839491696]] (Zaaparou, peter_hammer).
- **[Resto — set bonus]** A set-bonus effect that triggers Healing over Time procs was recently nerfed from proccing on every Chain Heal bounce to a single proc, because it was too strong for too long — [[d:shaman#1424645688154787851]], [[d:shaman#1424646459441020973]] (Squall, Stunfisk).
- **[Resto — PvP]** PvP healing/damage dampening nets out roughly neutral for resto (both healing done and incoming damage are reduced ~20%, giving more reaction time rather than being a pure nerf); recommended PvP gearing: prioritize Stamina and a shield, avoid cloth/leather, keep good +healing (dampening doesn't devalue it as much as first assumed since damage is dampened equally); cited must-have PvP pieces: Helm of the Lifegiver, Hide of the Wild, Time-Shifting Wheel — [[d:shaman#1427039103991546048]], [[d:shaman#1427041928674803854]], [[d:shaman#1427042234397622402]] (Nandieb, peter_hammer, Not Normal).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Professions]** Community-common pick is Engineering + Herbalism (Engineering for PvE/PvP utility tools, Herbalism because it pairs with Lasher farming, "Shaman's best gold farm") — [[d:shaman#1425698342167515177]] (DemonBoyJr). Not Normal frames professions purely as moneymaking tools rather than shaman-specific bonuses: recommends Herbalism (feeds Lasher farming) or Skinning (feeds Dragonscale farming, since Enhance solos elites well) paired with Enchanting (very low-effort to level via disenchanting drops); Engineering is only "technically BiS" for personal performance via Goblin Sapper Charges, described as "tryhard"; Alchemy is not worth it without Herbalism to feed it — [[d:shaman#1426597598504030290]], [[d:shaman#1426597898979770388]], [[d:shaman#1426604274489622619]], [[d:shaman#1426606882025504908]].
- **[Gold]** Lasher farming (Dire Maul) is repeatedly confirmed the community's default/best shaman gold farm, roughly 100g+/hour depending on realm/herb prices, commonly started around level 52-53; Totem of Tides (30 frost damage totem) is called the biggest speed/QoL upgrade for lasher pulls since lashers are nature-immune (so shaman's usual nature-based reactive damage doesn't work on them) — [[d:shaman#1425738262923972720]], [[d:shaman#1426808984995631187]] (Zamka, poto).
- **[Gold]** Dragonscale farming (soloing red/black dragonkin elites) is a viable alternate gold farm: red scales are roughly on par $/hour with lashers but sell slower; black scales sell faster for less; the rare Dragonscale Belt Buckle recipe (from black dragonkin, Searing Gorge) can sell for 800-1000g when it drops — [[d:shaman#1426246866986602538]], [[d:shaman#1426247033739542569]] (Not Normal).
- **[Gold]** General open-world questing at 60 is considered sufficient for day-to-day gold needs without dedicated farming; higher-level zones (EPL/Winterspring/Burning Steppes/Silithus) pay ~4-5g/quest, lower-level 50s zones ~3g/quest, plus mob drops — [[d:shaman#1425819094678114345]] (Nandieb).
- **[Races]** Racials remain broadly agreed to be low-impact "flavor" that shouldn't drive race choice (Orc's weapon-skill edge for Enhancement being the one real exception — see Stats/Gear); Tauren's Plainsrunning is confirmed genuinely fast (not slower than a mount as some assume) — fast enough that it's restricted in Battlegrounds — [[d:shaman#1426096156382986250]] (Ciftar).
