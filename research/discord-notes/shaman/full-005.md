## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Turtle's patch-note documentation is described as unreliable ("dogshit"); e.g. a forum claim that Windfury weapon imbue ICD is 6s is false/outdated — the WF totem (not the weapon imbue) has a 1.5s ICD — [[d:shaman#1453869282185445436]], [[d:shaman#1453869396048216216]] (Soshane13/Not Normal, 2025-12-26; confirmed).
- **[Ele]** Chain Heal's "smart targeting" AI is currently bugged: instead of jumping to lowest-HP or biggest-missing-HP target, it jumps to any target missing any HP; devs previously said they fixed this but it was reportedly changed back — open bug report cited — [[d:shaman#1453404790829875456]], [[d:shaman#1453410763799527547]], [[d:shaman#1453437514307080332]], [[d:shaman#1453182896637481202]] (Chidi/Bnkyo, 2025-12-24; disputed: Not Normal/Squid say "working as intended", Chidi disputes with bug-tracker history).
- **[Ele/Fire]** Elemental Fury gives 10% damage bonus to Nature and Fire damage only, not Frost — seen as inconsistent since crit-damage component from Elemental Fury does include Frost. Frost Shock does roughly half the damage of Earth Shock (e.g. ~1200 non-crit Earth Shock vs ~800 Frost Shock) — flagged as a known, intentional gap by devs per Not Normal — [[d:shaman#1453177536698908743]], [[d:shaman#1453178449609887921]], [[d:shaman#1453181062481055774]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-24; "working as intended" per devs, disputed as bad design by billienomates/ThatsTheJoke).
- **[Enh]** Stormstrike currently buffs "next 2 Nature damage sources" (includes Lightning Strike); community wants it changed to buff shocks specifically (esp. Frost Shock) for better Frost Shock viability — pure theorycraft/wishlist, no confirmed change — [[d:shaman#1453209598545887415]], [[d:shaman#1453210058002399315]], [[d:shaman#1453215463596101804]] (billienomates/Not Normal, 2025-12-24).
- **[Enh imbue]** Frostbrand imbue is confirmed to be getting buffed "a bit" in an upcoming patch per Ciftar's dev conversations — [[d:shaman#1452567885997281343]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-21; single source, insider claim).
- **[General]** No major spec buffs/nerfs planned beyond making imbues like Frostbrand more usable in raids; Ele and Enh are said to remain roughly equal in power per dev-adjacent info — [[d:shaman#1452662855840170169]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-21).
- **[Resto]** Mana Spring totem confirmed to have always been 1-minute duration in vanilla; complaint that its regen was nerfed while Wisdom (paladin) was left stronger — perceived favoritism toward paladin ("Devs playing Paladin" meme) — [[d:shaman#1453482269225386148]], [[d:shaman#1453482469939482636]], [[d:shaman#1453483740809658449]] (billienomates, 2025-12-25).

Community pushback: if Molten Blast is nerfed without compensation, Fire Ele (simple rotation) will just become "bad" rather than balanced, since the rotation's simplicity isn't the actual problem, the damage output is — [[d:shaman#1458779386995020031]] (Kairion, 2026-01-08).
- **[Bug]** Stormhowl (resto T3.5) 3/6 set bonus was previously bugged to increase (instead of decrease) time between Water Totem ticks; confirmed fixed via official patch note ("now works as intended") — [[d:shaman#1458831863727657030]], [[d:shaman#1458835614731337832]] (Khandis/Zaaparou, 2026-01-08).
- **[Bug]** MC 3-set totem range bonus is currently bugged to 40 yards instead of intended 30 yards; will be fixed in next client update/patch — [[d:shaman#1457869012804567131]], [[d:shaman#1457869613395349580]] (grubberry/Ciftar, 2026-01-06).
- **[Bug]** Chain Heal AI: devs have now acknowledged (per Chidi's screenshot) that Chain Heal jump targeting is "not working properly"; a fix may follow in a few weeks. Nandieb maintains it's an intentional nerf rather than a bug, and that devs wanted to change the jump logic to target lowest current HP — [[d:shaman#1456690077131341854]], [[d:shaman#1456690482448040171]], [[d:shaman#1456690571300176065]] (Chidi/Nandieb, 2026-01-02).
- **[Windfury/imbue mechanics]** Weapon-imbue totems (Windfury Totem, Flametongue Totem) now function as auras, not actual weapon imbues like sharpening stones — they stack with each other for party members, but do NOT stack with a shaman's own personal weapon imbue (using WF weapon + dropping FT totem gives you nothing extra from the totem; only other players benefit from both totems) — [[d:shaman#1454866936591683696]], [[d:shaman#1454432791272882343]], [[d:shaman#1458591364160688330]], [[d:shaman#1458591418170872001]] (Not Normal/tomo/gastro, 2025-12-28/2026-01-07; multiple confirmations, well-established).
- **[Frost Shock/Earth Shock imbalance]** Widely-discussed and disputed design gap: Frost Shock does roughly half Earth Shock's damage due to Elemental Fury (10% dmg) applying only to Nature/Fire, not Frost; community (Not Normal et al.) argues the gap should be narrowed so Frost Shock is a meaningful lower-threat alternative rather than a strict downgrade; devs reportedly consider current behavior "working as intended" — see also 1.18.1 changes section above.
- **[Enh imbue tech]** Whether sharpening stones work alongside Windfury weapon imbue was asked but left unanswered in this chunk — [[d:shaman#1455504623178612817]] (Xaln, 2025-12-25; question unanswered).

## Leveling
- **[General]** Weapon speed doesn't matter much until Stormstrike, and even then matters less than weapon DPS until max level — [[d:shaman#1452746747787874436]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-22).
- **[Ele leveling]** Open with 2-3 Lightning Bolt based on distance, then finish with melee/shocks/totems while leveling Ele — [[d:shaman#1452769060776316998]] (Spicyahi, 2025-12-23). Cast Molten Blast (instant, fire) while closing distance, then transition to melee once mob reaches you — hardcasting while being hit is a DPS loss — [[d:shaman#1452756437959708793]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-23).
- **[Enh leveling]** Levels ~52/54+ can farm/level using Elemental weapon imbue + tank talents in Enh, or Fire Totem talents in Ele; Frost Nova → Magma Totem sufficient for AoE with good gear (extra Nova for green gear) — [[d:shaman#1452333229045452871]] (chain, 2025-12-22).
- **[Enh leveling power spikes]** Levels 20, 30, and ~40-42 (with respec for guaranteed Frostbrand crits) are noted power spikes — [[d:shaman#1452657749367328950]] (CJ, 2025-12-21).
- **[Weapon imbue leveling]** Frostbrand (fast 1h + Frostbrand) recommended low-level for consistent Clearcasting procs regardless of gear ("perma clear casting with high damage irregardless of your gear is just too good") until reaching Elemental weapons around level 60 when Frost Shock/Frostbrand can carry to 60 — [[d:shaman#1453081010056200336]], [[d:shaman#1453081869649186866]] (Porkins, 2025-12-23). Slow 1h still preferred once using Frostbrand if choosing 1h for other reasons; no benefit to faster weapon unless DPS is substantially higher — [[d:shaman#1453105455055372368]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-23). Counter: FB proc is PPM-based, so a 2.7-speed 1h is better than very fast 1h; Tor Ghaz (Grim Reaches drop) is a strong early-mid FB weapon, hard to replace into 50s — [[d:shaman#1453110863392276676]] (billienomates, 2025-12-23).
- **[Mount/movement]** At level 40, riding skill gives 60% mount speed vs Ghost Wolf's 40%; Ghost Wolf costs mana and interrupts 5s out-of-combat mana regen, making it worse for questing once mount is available — [[d:shaman#1452664011265933464]], [[d:shaman#1452667292704964719]] (Kekkonen/Ciftar, 2025-12-21).
- **[Windfury weapon]** Question about level shamans get Windfury Weapon enchant raised but not answered in this range — [[d:shaman#1452774983955648583]] (lipidus, 2025-12-23; question unanswered).
- **[Twinking]** Enh 2h with Corpse Maker or An'she totem + Rockbiter + enchant viable for low-level twinking but considered a weak class/spec choice to twink overall — [[d:shaman#1452281200629776606]], [[d:shaman#1452287636835139666]] (otep1616/Nandieb, 2025-12-21).
- **[Level ~55 switch]** Switching from Enh to Ele around level 55 suggested as reasonable timing to start gathering pre-BiS, though staying Enh to 60 via dungeons is fine too — [[d:shaman#1453613967556608103]], [[d:shaman#1453615600713732169]] (gastro/sailorVEVO, 2025-12-25).
- **[Respec combo]** Around level 42, respec to Frostbrand + Clearcasting combo; slower weapon preferred whether 1h or 2h, both viable with "virtually 0 downtime" — [[d:shaman#1453631522232864768]], [[d:shaman#1453642862809059422]] (CJ/Not Normal, 2025-12-25).
- **[Level-tanking]** Enh viable for level-tanking dungeons but "touchy" until level 30 when proper defensive talents unlock; becomes easy once Elemental Weapons imbue is available — [[d:shaman#1453141327062765619]], [[d:shaman#1453146304699170968]], [[d:shaman#1453146785630650459]] (ChickenSandwhich/billienomates, 2025-12-24).
- **[Race pick for enh]** For leveling as tank-then-raid-DPS enh, Tauren favored: health bonus, AoE stun (War Stomp) helps, Plainsrunning is "irreplaceable," racial single-target attack-speed-reduction debuff also useful; Troll better at 60 for pure DPS, Orc considered top min-max raid DPS pick — [[d:shaman#1453148334968672441]], [[d:shaman#1453148561712877568]], [[d:shaman#1453148737621725325]] (billienomates, 2025-12-24).
- **[Enh leveling imbue progression, detailed]** Rockbiter is main damage source and correct choice until level 30 (buffs Lightning Strike's Lightning Shield hits hard), should be paired with Lightning Shield; never use Rockbiter in dungeons unless tanking — swap to Windfury there — [[d:shaman#1455032665261609123]], [[d:shaman#1455035581167046820]], [[d:shaman#1458191578928648405]], [[d:shaman#1458191670423326791]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-29/2026-01-07). At 30 (Elemental Weapons unlocked), can continue Rockbiter+Lightning Shield or swap to Windfury+Water Shield; Not Normal personally used WF/Water Shield 30-45 for comfort/less drinking; both are viable, "neither option has particularly significant advantages" — [[d:shaman#1455935867641139424]], [[d:shaman#1455936119807152198]], [[d:shaman#1455936858365366302]], [[d:shaman#1453642862809059422]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-25/29). After reaching Elemental Weapons + Clearcasting (~level 40+), Frostbrand + Frost Shock becomes best due to guaranteed crits (Elemental Weapons auto-crits Frostbrand), giving near-permanent Clearcasting uptime, zero downtime, and no need to drink — [[d:shaman#1457272682222583902]], [[d:shaman#1458192186511458405]], [[d:shaman#1458192310842953839]], [[d:shaman#1457723292621345055]] (Ravenhost/Not Normal/Holidae, 2026-01-05/07; strongly and repeatedly confirmed, "You almost forget how to play any other way"). Stormstrike and Elemental Weapons should be prioritized before any Elemental tree points; Clearcasting requires 10 more levels past Elemental Weapons (level 30), both can be had by level 31 via build `https://talents.turtlecraft.gg/shaman?points=FQAYI-FACYAI-` — [[d:shaman#1455971456281219197]], [[d:shaman#1455973127279480883]] (Not Normal/Nandieb, 2025-12-29).

- **[Enh leveling gear]** At endgame Frostbrand is basically obsolete for damage (doesn't scale with physical stats at all; autocrit doesn't work on bosses and many trash elites are immune); Windfury becomes better once gear provides more AP/Str/crit/Agi/phys hit — [[d:shaman#1456932459018059798]], [[d:shaman#1456932651507384436]] (Ciftar, 2026-01-03). Frostbrand's spellpower coefficient is only 10%, poor scaling — [[d:shaman#1456947109239455754]] (Ciftar, 2026-01-03). At raid gear levels Windfury+Water Shield alone provides "virtually endless grind sessions" without needing Frostbrand; FB doesn't work in dungeons/raids since most enemies (esp. bosses) are slow-immune — [[d:shaman#1457138846163534032]] (Zeruel citing personal testing, 2026-01-04).
- **[Enh tank leveling difficulty]** Enh tanking is fragile before level ~25-30 (before defensive talents/Spirit Shields and mail armor); every other tank class is considered better at low levels; becomes fully "online" at level 44 when Clearcasting + mail armor + Elemental Weapons (extra 10% DR) all come together — before that, expect to go OOM a lot; stack Stamina then shield armor then mitigation stats; farm Totem of Tides early (very strong for mana efficiency at low level) — [[d:shaman#1459024370428018728]], [[d:shaman#1459182669022887979]], [[d:shaman#1459182893774409975]], [[d:shaman#1459182980525330505]], [[d:shaman#1455593817569755147]] (Koae/Ordinary Joe/Not Normal, 2025-12-30/2026-01-09).
- **[Enh leveling gear tip]** At level 33, two Obsidian Brooches (+11-12 stamina each, not unique) recommended for tank leveling toughness — [[d:shaman#1459182669022887979]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-01-09).
- **[Leveling water/lightning shield choice]** Use Lightning Shield when high on mana, Water Shield when low, to kill faster and drink less — save looted mana potions for chain-pulling — [[d:shaman#1456015634986041386]] (Spicyahi, 2026-01-01). At endgame: Lightning Shield on bosses (with Judgement of Wisdom for sustain), Water Shield on trash/dungeons (esp. combined with Totem of Tides while leveling, "doing way more work at lower levels") — [[d:shaman#1457507332471783485]], [[d:shaman#1457507386473582908]], [[d:shaman#1457514144566743180]], [[d:shaman#1457514253987610754]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-05).

- **[Enh]** Earth Shock is the dominant threat tool: ~40% of tank TPS from Earth Shock alone, ~20% from combined Stormstrike/Lightning Strike (excluding Lightning Shield component); white/auto-attack threat scales up significantly with better gear/weapon skill, eventually becoming the top threat "ability" at BiS — [[d:shaman#1453500008375521330]], [[d:shaman#1453500513638416487]], [[d:shaman#1453722323411533935]] (Not Normal/Eluzia, 2025-12-25). In "classic deep enh" variant, Earth Shock ~35%, Stormstrike/Lightning Strike ~20%.
- **[Enh]** Frostbrand+Frost Shock rotation breakdown at endgame in tanking gear: ~40% damage from Frostbrand proc, ~20% Frost Shock, ~20% auto-hit — [[d:shaman#1453140224568987758]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-24).
- **[Enh Spellhance]** Vial of Potent Venoms snapshots with Elemental Fury, Stormstrike, and Elemental Mastery active — ticks for 95 with those 3 up (vs presumably lower base), will also snapshot with Natural Alignment Crystal later for "best single-target combo for a very long time"; with all buffs active ticks for 114, "almost double value than other classes" — [[d:shaman#1452579153831268372]], [[d:shaman#1452579381326381100]], [[d:shaman#1452579722646130783]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-21; does not scale with SP).
- **[Enh trinkets]** Rotation for melee dps trinkets: Blackhand's Breath + Molten Emberstone first, switch to HoJ on Emberstone's cooldown — test showed Emberstone+rotation (23k dmg/min on dummy) beat HoJ+rotation (19k dmg/min); HoJ is generally "not good for shamans" since other trinkets scale better with imbues — [[d:shaman#1452643145786327041]], [[d:shaman#1452645296822681753]], [[d:shaman#1453015582189293578]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-21/23). Trinket Menu addon recommended to auto-queue trinket swaps — [[d:shaman#1452647263187894353]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-21).


- **[Ele MC rotation]** For MC/general Ele ST rotation: Chain Lightning, Earthquake, Lightning Bolt spam ("cl eq lb lb cl lb lb lb cl, etc"), no shocks in the rotation ("they take up a GCD and delay your casting" — LB does more damage, higher crit chance, and costs less mana than a shock); exception: use a shock if the mob is about to die before another cast finishes — [[d:shaman#1459496645828935691]], [[d:shaman#1459503774186999850]], [[d:shaman#1459503952096661679]], [[d:shaman#1459504361771372617]] (Spicyahi/gastro, 2026-01-10). Flame Shock only used in later phases, not MC (fire immunities); Earth Shock actually pulls HIGHER threat and deals LESS DPS than LB/CL, hence avoided for a pure DPS rotation — [[d:shaman#1459504021302673534]], [[d:shaman#1459504235296063682]] (gastro/Spicyahi, 2026-01-10).
- **[Ele traditional/nature build detailed rotation]** Flame Shock, Earthquake on cooldown, then Chain Lightning → 3x Lightning Bolt, replacing one LB with Molten Blast roughly every other CL cycle (to refresh Flame Shock); with enough haste, 4x Lightning Bolt per CL cycle is possible (clips CD slightly but still better) if SP ≥ ~600-700 — [[d:shaman#1460690134575091854]], [[d:shaman#1460690300761542862]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13). In MC/BWL specifically, skip Imp Fire Totems, Imp Molten Blast, and Call of Flame, redistribute to 5/5 Ancestral Knowledge or Improved Water Shield — [[d:shaman#1460689747013013594]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13). Nature Ele build requires ~23/28/0 with Elemental Weapons for Flametongue to be worthwhile — [[d:shaman#1460693593281794200]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13). A threat talent is important for Ele since "anyone without a threat talent will cause problems" — Ele isn't top DPS but pulls enough threat to need management, manageable with Blessing of Salvation — [[d:shaman#1460694375985057803]], [[d:shaman#1460663604193001614]], [[d:shaman#1460663856396239121]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13).
- **[Fire Ele "Molten Blast spam" mechanics, detailed]** Core interaction: Improved Molten Blast's refresh ("Rekindle") damage "double dips" on % fire damage increases — Flame Shock's DoT damage is already boosted by fire % modifiers, then the Improved Molten Blast rekindle tick is boosted by those same modifiers a second time. Stacked on top: the MC totem/relic (boosts Flame Shock DoT contribution from 60% to 80%, effectively +33%), the T3 2-piece bonus (gives Molten Blast a free effective 10% haste), and Searing Totem scaling unusually well with spellpower and also getting buffed by the same fire % increases — [[d:shaman#1461010372193423390]], [[d:shaman#1461010456570495007]], [[d:shaman#1461010782518120508]], [[d:shaman#1461010946347634721]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-14; detailed mechanical breakdown, high confidence). Because the refresh damage is fixed regardless of when in the DoT window it's used, there's no meaningful timing skill involved — described as making the rotation less interesting, not more — [[d:shaman#1460723499814490369]], [[d:shaman#1460723636137623644]], [[d:shaman#1460723959728177314]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13).
- **[Enh Frostbrand proc math]** Frostbrand's base proc chance formula: 9 / (60 / base weapon speed). Example: 3.6s weapon → 54% base, 79% with 3/3 Elemental Weapons; 1.8s weapon → 27% base, 54% with 3/3 Elemental Weapons — [[d:shaman#1461006589493252168]], [[d:shaman#1461006790345887948]], [[d:shaman#1461007087546138707]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-14).
- **[Enh weapon speed math, full derivation]** AP is a flat damage addition scaled by weapon speed (AP/14 × speed = added damage per swing). Example: 500 AP + 3.5s weapon → 125 damage added/swing. AP accounts for ~50-60% of total damage at any given time. For pure auto-attacks, total DPS is speed-independent, but Stormstrike/Lightning Strike inherit the same per-hit damage bonus while firing at a fixed rate independent of weapon speed — so slower weapons deal more damage per strike use, making a 3.8s/61.4dps weapon (e.g. Unstoppable Force) outperform a 3.3s/78dps weapon (e.g. Aspect of Seradane) in practice for enh — [[d:shaman#1460367999587324025]], [[d:shaman#1460368788146098422]], [[d:shaman#1460369455774437643]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13). No meaningful case for preferring a faster weapon on shaman outside niche procs, unlike DW classes/hunters/warriors — [[d:shaman#1460369861036343309]], [[d:shaman#1460369895861649652]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13).
- **[Enh Spellhance flametongue weapon speed]** Flametongue technically scales better with faster weapons, but this is offset because Stormstrike/Lightning Strike prefer slower weapons and also proc Flametongue's per-hit damage a fixed number of times, which benefits more from a slower weapon's higher FT base damage — net effect roughly a wash — [[d:shaman#1460664131936849931]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13).
- **[Ele CL vs LB]** Chain Lightning always better than Lightning Bolt even on single target for Ele with the 2.5-set (or generally) — CL's 1.5s cast vs LB's 2s cast means CL wins on cast-time-normalized DPS math (compare LB damage × 0.75 vs CL damage) — [[d:shaman#1461195417214779403]], [[d:shaman#1461268465124905034]] (Not Normal/Spicyahi, 2026-01-15). Exception: in MC and BWL specifically, go full Nature Ele and just use CL/EQ/LB (implying standard rotation applies, Fire is weak there due to immunities) — [[d:shaman#1461268760173215755]] (Spicyahi, 2026-01-15).
- **[Enh Str vs Agi, detailed]** Definitively stated (and "mathematically provable," confirmed via dynamic sim): 1 Strength is ALWAYS worth more DPS than 1 Agility for enhance shaman, roughly 1.5-1.8x more valuable — this holds "every time," regardless of current crit/gear levels; the idea that Agi "catches up" to Str past some crit threshold (e.g. "35% crit") is a myth — Flurry uptime does plateau at some point but the DPS impact of Agi before/after that plateau is "functionally not very different" — [[d:shaman#1461356189739778211]], [[d:shaman#1461356257519599731]], [[d:shaman#1461361487762292877]], [[d:shaman#1461362315290214639]], [[d:shaman#1461386796628971531]], [[d:shaman#1461387911000887511]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-15; strongly and repeatedly confirmed, disputed initially by Stryyke/Dobin citing "logs" as counter-evidence, but Not Normal notes high parses don't prove itemization choice is optimal — "uncontroversial and easily provable with sims"). Caveat: some individual items with more Agi/crit can still be better than an alternative item with more Str, due to other stats on the item — this doesn't mean Agi > Str as a pure stat — [[d:shaman#1461387976067256576]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-15).
- **[Enh crit cap]** A "crit cap" (1 - glance% - dodge% - miss%) exists only for auto-attacks, and is mostly a dual-wield consideration due to DW's higher miss penalty; for 2h shaman (hit-capped), the effective crit cap is roughly 54-55%, essentially irrelevant in practice since normal raid crit is far below that — [[d:shaman#1461360955022901321]], [[d:shaman#1461361055555911772]], [[d:shaman#1461361123327737940]], [[d:shaman#1461361203799523504]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-15).
- **[Haste/GCD mechanics testing]** Not Normal ran live cast-time sampling (48-55 casts per haste level on a 1.5s base-cast spell) and found the shaman/caster global cooldown (GCD) is NOT reduced by haste (unlike TBC's Patch 2.4 Sunwell change, which explicitly excluded melee/ranged abilities from spell-haste GCD reduction) — a small ~0.06-0.07s "phantom" reduction seen going from 0% to ~4% haste is attributed to a server-side latency/rounding quirk (also reported anecdotally by fire mages), not real GCD scaling; a 2s-base-cast spell showed clean linear haste scaling, confirming GCD-capped 1.5s spells behave differently from longer casts — [[d:shaman#1461403895921115169]], [[d:shaman#1461407171542515723]], [[d:shaman#1461407270712774810]], [[d:shaman#1461410194402709525]], [[d:shaman#1461417834625368237]], [[d:shaman#1461418154332000449]], [[d:shaman#1461424858327158990]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-15; empirically tested, high confidence). Instant-cast abilities are unaffected by haste in the way 1.5s-cast spells are — [[d:shaman#1461424858327158990]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-15).
- **[Enh tank stat priority, consolidated]** Defensive stat focus: Stamina and Armor primary (shield armor especially important); Agility good (multipurpose: crit, armor, dodge) but not top priority; Dodge/Parry decent but not a priority (comes naturally at higher gear); Defense "ok but not a priority" — [[d:shaman#1461396095585222756]], [[d:shaman#1461396539829256400]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-15). Weapon/threat stat choice depends on build: in 22/29 (spell-focused threat build) both AP and SP are good; in 14/37 build focus more on physical stats — [[d:shaman#1461397431181774981]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-15). Enh tier gear tends to be well-itemized for tanking (good Stam, Agi, great armor) though some off-pieces beat tier in specific slots, e.g. Boots of the Flamewalker (MC) has better defensive stats than T1 boots though T1 wins on threat; T2 boots likely beat both due to higher armor + comparable stam + better threat. Blackstone Crown (Abyssal High Council) called out as an exceptionally strong tank item — [[d:shaman#1461398255903572207]], [[d:shaman#1461398428713222276]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-15).
- **[Enh weapon skill vs Fist/Spinal Reaper]** Fist of the Forgotten Order is better than Spinal Reaper at roughly equal weapon skill (e.g. 305/305); but if axe skill is 315 vs mace skill only 305, Spinal Reaper pulls ahead — [[d:shaman#1461889506910277754]], [[d:shaman#1461889568625266698]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-16).
- **[Water Shield / mana clarifications]** Water Shield alone does NOT passively regenerate mana; it returns mana only when its charges are consumed (i.e., when the shaman is hit) — a common leveling misconception (see also Leveling section for the WF+Water Shield combo) — [[d:shaman#1461970267554320537]] (cthulhu6989 raising the confusion, 2026-01-16; implicitly corrected elsewhere by mana-return explanations already in Leveling notes).

- **[Ele stat weights]** Uncapped spell hit weight estimated at ~12-18 SP (Not Normal) or ~15 SP (Kred: "spell hit is equal to 15 spell power and 1 crit is equal to 10 spell power") vs crit ~8-12 SP; ratio of hit:crit value roughly 1.1-1.2:1 in some analyses but disputed — Not Normal's own spreadsheet-driven comparisons (dynamic stat-weight sheet) showed value is highly gear/context dependent, e.g. Zanzil's Seal (11sp+1%hit, plus 2-pc set for +1 hit, 23 int) compares closely against Dragonslayer's Signet/Band of Servitude (18sp+1%crit each); at ~700 SP Zanzil combo edges out by ~1-2sp equivalent, but at lower SP the crit rings are better — [[d:shaman#1452397748677513389]], [[d:shaman#1453086446767571216]], [[d:shaman#1452453036957106349]], [[d:shaman#1452485056021926022]], [[d:shaman#1452493272667652147]] (Not Normal/Kred/Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-12-21/22; contested — no firm consensus number, context-dependent). - **[Enh stat weights]** Not Normal's rough guidelines: 1% crit ≈ 20-25 AP; 1% hit ≈ 35-45 AP (until capped); 1% haste ≈ 12-15 AP (more valuable past Naxx); 1 Str ≈ 2.3 AP; 1 Agi ≈ ~1.3 AP equivalent (note: Agi gives NO AP directly to shamans, unlike hunters/rogues — a common point of confusion); 1 weapon skill ≈ ~15 AP if hit-capped, ~22-25 AP if not hit-capped (up to 315 total weapon skill) — [[d:shaman#1453511632058712145]], [[d:shaman#1453042332034404445]], [[d:shaman#1453043306908094655]] (Not Normal/Ravenhost/poqoqu0741, 2025-12-23/25). - **[Enh weapon skill vs T3.5 3pc]** At 313 weapon skill, dropping Ancient Jade Leggings to wear 3x T3.5 pieces (pants required) is endorsed: T3.5 3pc bonus is "giga turbo hyper busted" and outperforms the weapon-skill loss, though weapon skill should eventually be recovered (e.g. via AQ40 gloves) — [[d:shaman#1454114868071502048]], [[d:shaman#1454136686329200684]], [[d:shaman#1454136752615981127]] (Khandis/Not Normal, 2025-12-26).
- **[Resto stat weights]** Int is a secondary stat for resto (healing power is primary); 1% haste ≈ ~15 healing power, 1% crit ≈ ~12 healing power — [[d:shaman#1454137191113687255]], [[d:shaman#1454137300031246456]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-26).
- **[Enh tank weapon]** Weapon speed: slower is always better for tank threat all else equal; weapon skill is the single most impactful weapon stat for any melee (can't be gotten elsewhere); defensive stats on weapons are a bad tradeoff since weapon is typically best threat-stat slot — [[d:shaman#1453494476122099964]], [[d:shaman#1453542318622904361]], [[d:shaman#1453543202505101396]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-25). Example ideal shaman tank weapon: "Remnants of the Old God" (item 60003) — slow, high DPS, some stamina, +5 weapon skill — [[d:shaman#1453543392842743880]], [[d:shaman#1453543468755456051]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-25). Other examples: item 19362 (2.3s speed, +3 axe skill, 12 agi 9 str, some stam) vs item 17068 (still better for threat due to speed alone despite lacking weapon skill) — [[d:shaman#1453543987779469376]], [[d:shaman#1453544533718466793]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-25).
- **[Tier armor bug note]** T2.5 legs have 448 armor / boots 340 (~25% less, normal ratio); T3 legs 484 armor and T3 boots ALSO 484 armor — suspected copy-paste dev error giving T3 boots too much armor — [[d:shaman#1453065368003412120]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-23; unconfirmed bug observation).
- **[Ele stats]** Priority spell hit > spell crit >= haste; roughly 1% spell crit ≈ 9-12 SP, 1% spell hit ≈ 12-15 SP until hit-capped — [[d:shaman#1458549208826122527]], [[d:shaman#1458549320616902818]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-07).
- **[Enh weapon speed mechanics]** Detailed explainer: AP converts to weapon DPS via AP/14 formula; at ~2250 AP (full BiS T3.5 raid buffed) roughly 61.6% of total weapon damage comes from AP, meaning slower weapons hit harder per swing at a fixed AP amount; this same dynamic applies not just to Stormstrike/Lightning Strike ("strikes") but also to Windfury procs triggered by those strikes, since strikes/WF-from-strikes fire at a fixed rate independent of weapon speed (unlike autos and WF-from-autos, which are "normalized" because they scale with attack rate) — [[d:shaman#1456041124362846370]], [[d:shaman#1456078039174877214]], [[d:shaman#1456078297636012093]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-25/2026-01-01). Practical example: Aspect of Seradane (3.3s, 78 DPS) can perform worse than Unstoppable Force (3.8s, 61.4 DPS) despite lower listed DPS, due to slow-weapon-favoring strike math — [[d:shaman#1455935705397202944]], [[d:shaman#1456045346584002713]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-25/2026-01-01).
- **[Enh weapon skill]** Weapon skill more valuable than 1% crit per point; every +1 weapon skill outweighs 1% crit — [[d:shaman#1457768815529038090]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-05). Weapon choice for non-orc enh: Fist of the Forgotten Order + Ancient Jade Leggings is close to Dark Edge of Insanity (DTC) + Flameguard Gauntlets for orcs; for non-orcs Fist+jades generally better, DTC+FGG still close — [[d:shaman#1456087432981381212]], [[d:shaman#1456090185438597296]], [[d:shaman#1456090359418327250]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-01).
- **[Enh — weapon skill vs Spinal Reaper]** Fist of the Forgotten Order (standard +5 weapon skill) beats Spinal Reaper unless you have Flameguard Gauntlets (+7 axe) as orc, in which case Spinal Reaper edges ahead — [[d:shaman#1453834069237305467]], [[d:shaman#1453834265648304272]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-25).
- **[Resto stat weights, detailed]** Full resto priority: Healing Power = Haste > Int > mp5 > Crit; Spirit is a "completely dead stat," does literally nothing for shaman healers — [[d:shaman#1457836516368978143]], [[d:shaman#1457836629275316287]], [[d:shaman#1456144601583845396]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-26/2026-01-06; +reactions, no pushback). Crit is weak for resto because it has no synergistic mechanics (unlike Holy Paladin), only a flat 50% crit-heal bonus, and increases overhealing volatility, getting worse the more spells are downranked; ~1% crit worth ~5-8 healing power (at ~900 healing power / 10-12% crit) — [[d:shaman#1457838505907454105]], [[d:shaman#1457846645872918700]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-06; disputed by Zaaparou citing a BiS crit-stacking resto performing as well as others, but Not Normal maintains the math). Haste scales very well because base cast times are reduced by talents, giving 2-3x the output of crit; smart-heal spells (Chain Heal) benefit further since haste = more casts = less overhealing — [[d:shaman#1457846205878112376]], [[d:shaman#1457842944018219244]] (Not Normal/Zamka, 2026-01-06). Haste soft-caps around 33% (post multiplicative stacking) for Chain Heal, lower if running Breath of Solnius; Healing Wave keeps benefiting from haste beyond that cap — [[d:shaman#1457847126821175551]], [[d:shaman#1457847281586933872]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-06). The relative value of healing power vs. haste/crit decreases as healing power stat total rises (diminishing % returns), explained with worked example: at 100 HP, +100 healing power = +15% output; at 1000 HP, +100 healing power = only +6.87% — meanwhile % stats like haste give flat % increases regardless of current HP total — [[d:shaman#1458113506720350208]], [[d:shaman#1458113887973933368]], [[d:shaman#1458114242552008745]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-06). Recommended resto items: Living Root of the Wildheart-type mp5 items generally skipped in favor of raw healing power; specific gear recs at https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=60501 and https://database.turtlecraft.gg/?item=18471 over crit/sp trinkets — [[d:shaman#1455717175515021312]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-31).
- **[Resto leveling stats]** Recommended stat priority while leveling as healer: Stamina/Str for block chance, Str for melee AP, Int for mana pool (from a newer player perspective) — [[d:shaman#1455683544687837296]] (Nikitunya, 2025-12-31; lower-confidence, from a less established source than Not Normal's later endgame breakdown).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[Enh hit% by weapon skill]** With 2h weapons (no benefit from over-capping hit): required hit% scales down with weapon skill — 5% hit needed at 300 weapon skill, 4% at 305, 3% at 310, etc. — [[d:shaman#1460645366121894071]], [[d:shaman#1460645615460417608]] (tomo, 2026-01-13).
- **[Enh weapon skill quest]** Weapon skill books cost 250g each via a Fray Island questline (south of Ratchet, Barrens); 1h and 2h share the same skill type (e.g. one axe book covers both 1h and 2h axes); can learn multiple types — [[d:shaman#1455404578689515531]], [[d:shaman#1460382999714463949]], [[d:shaman#1460383171383267338]], [[d:shaman#1460426260319572051]] (Squid/Not Normal, 2025-12-30, 2026-01-13). MC gloves give +7 2h axe skill (higher cap than mace's 2pc Towerforge option); recommend doing Emperor (LBRS "EMP") runs for HoJ/Dreadforge Retaliator as good early weapons while saving for the weapon skill quest — [[d:shaman#1460646309412208789]], [[d:shaman#1460646104860328021]], [[d:shaman#1460646930555076809]] (tomo, 2026-01-13). Dreadforge Retaliator (from EMP/LBRS) is "a fantastic starter weapon"; Kara10 mace and Kara10 mail pieces generally excellent for enhance — [[d:shaman#1460646930555076809]], [[d:shaman#1460647269098455174]] (tomo, 2026-01-13).
- **[Enh T3 2pc bug]** Confirmed bug (reported by Not Normal, https://turtlecraft.gg/bug-report?id=19559): Enh T3 2-piece bonus is only boosting the AP-derived portion of Empowered Lightning Shield damage, not its total damage as intended — [[d:shaman#1459948892736061603]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-11).
- **[Enh BWL/ES neck & ring]** For BWL/ES-tier enhance: Eye of Hakkar recommended for neck over Onyxia Tooth Pendant (the latter judged "kinda terrible for enh" outside of a hit-cap emergency; hit should be obtainable elsewhere on better-statted items); for rings, Quick Strike Ring and Master Dragonslayer's Ring preferred — [[d:shaman#1460105295656255488]], [[d:shaman#1460105677530730617]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-12).
- **[Enh Molten Core weapon]** "The Unstoppable Force" (2h mace) vs "Fist of the Forgotten Order" (axe, +5 weapon skill): Fist judged "a little better" even accounting for full stat comparison, but the swap is a minor (~2%) difference at 35%+ raid-buffed crit; weapon-skill quest prioritization should favor whichever weapon type (mace vs axe) the player will use until BWL — [[d:shaman#1457771524680319009]], [[d:shaman#1457772604130726093]], [[d:shaman#1457769513838973008]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-05).

- **[Enh Shart axe / weapon skill combo]** Non-orc BiS weapon-skill combo discussion: Shart axe + Flameguard Gauntlets, or trading FGG for Rupturan pants to hit 6/6 T3.5 vs 5/6 with Earthquake Leggings — resolved elsewhere as "6/6 T3.5 gives up FGG's +7 axe skill" tradeoff, generally in favor of the T3.5 6-piece where attainable — [[d:shaman#1454436849304080568]], [[d:shaman#1454439710603874415]] (Shangchi, 2025-12-28; see also Talent builds section for T3.5 discussion).
- **[Enh general itemization]** Flameguard Gauntlets remain strong regardless of weapon type (even non-axe), likely BiS glove slot until T2 gloves — [[d:shaman#1458132300016648316]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-06).
- **[Race choice, consolidated]** Best race by spec (rough consensus, small overall differences): Orc for Enh DPS and (slightly) Ele DPS; Tauren for tank; Troll for Resto — [[d:shaman#1460373099932422247]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13). Racials generally weak/flavor-only: Orc gets "wolf ghosts" (weak DoT-summon, largely considered useless), Troll gets Hex (short CC, good in PvP), Tauren gets Totemic Slam/War Stomp (AoE stun, useful for tanking) — [[d:shaman#1458505789625991248]], [[d:shaman#1458507267589538067]], [[d:shaman#1458270904005034147]] (otep1616/LLVE, 2026-01-07). Troll's Regeneration racial is weak for tanking, since HP regen from Spirit is extremely low for shaman relative to e.g. Warriors (an order of magnitude higher) — [[d:shaman#1460352228719329340]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13).
- **[Fire resist gear]** No consolidated Fire Resistance gear list found/available for shaman tanks as of this range — [[d:shaman#1460661908343623957]] (Shifuu, 2026-01-13; question unanswered).
- **[Tank stat targets, endgame]** Example endgame tank stat checkpoints discussed: 4480 unbuffed HP / 7869 armor (58.9% DR) / 44% avoidance (19% dodge, 6% parry, 12% block) / 341 defense judged sufficient for entry raiding (fine for Kara MT, doable for MC/Ony OT, fire resistance still important for MC/Ony); another with 5.4k unbuffed HP / 34% avoidance successfully tanked BWL and easy AQ40 bosses — advice is to deprioritize Defense/avoidance chasing (can't reach uncrittable early, many "def" blue items are stat traps, e.g. an 8stam/5def/1%dodge neck is worse than a 25-stam neck) in favor of stacking Stamina — [[d:shaman#1461062231822176409]], [[d:shaman#1461063526490570855]], [[d:shaman#1461064985567301827]], [[d:shaman#1461065056862339177]], [[d:shaman#1461065481552134165]] (Kobeyashi/Snook/Purge, 2026-01-14).
- **[Tank block/avoidance vs crit/crush note]** Clarification: block occupies its own slot on the attack table and cannot itself prevent a crit or crush (those are rolled with higher priority); "uncrittable" avoidance thresholds only become relevant at very high (near-BiS, raid-buffed) avoidance levels — [[d:shaman#1461098329957077154]], [[d:shaman#1461111565733593332]], [[d:shaman#1461117559335030907]] (Zeruel/Squid, 2026-01-15; some disagreement over exact framing but converges on this point).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Weapon imbue progression]** Full leveling→endgame imbue arc consolidated (see Leveling section for detail): Rockbiter until level 30 (pairs with Lightning Shield), then either continue Rockbiter+Lightning Shield or switch to Windfury+Water Shield at 30+, then Frostbrand+Frost Shock once Elemental Weapons+Clearcasting are up (~40+) for near-permanent Clearcasting and zero downtime; Frostbrand falls off hard at endgame (10% SP coefficient, no scaling with physical stats, doesn't work on slow-immune mobs/bosses) and Windfury becomes correct once geared — [[d:shaman#1455032665261609123]], [[d:shaman#1457272682222583902]], [[d:shaman#1456932459018059798]], [[d:shaman#1456947109239455754]].
- **[Weapon imbue math]** Frostbrand base proc chance = 9 / (60 / weapon speed); e.g. 3.6s weapon → 54% base / 79% with 3/3 Elemental Weapons (see Rotation section) — [[d:shaman#1461006589493252168]]. Windfury imbue is 20% chance to hit twice with +300 AP per hit, no ICD; Windfury Totem is only 20% chance to hit once with +0 AP and a 1.5s ICD — personal imbue "substantially" better than the totem version — [[d:shaman#1460661586535514224]], [[d:shaman#1460661705272070195]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-13).
- **[Totem-imbue stacking rule]** Weapon-imbue totems (Windfury Totem, Flametongue Totem) function as auras that stack with each other for OTHER party members, but never stack with a shaman's own personal weapon imbue — a shaman using WF weapon gets nothing extra from also dropping FT totem (see 1.18.1 changes section for full detail and citations).
- **[Flametongue scaling]** Flametongue imbue's damage is boosted by Elemental Weapons, Elemental Fury, and Call of Flame; Flametongue TOTEM (not the imbue) is not boosted by these — [[d:shaman#1461145861072486586]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-14). Flametongue imbue gets +30% fire damage from Elemental Weapons, making Flametongue the default Spellhance/fire-build imbue; scales technically better with faster weapons in isolation, but this is offset by Stormstrike/Lightning Strike favoring slow weapons (see Rotation section).
- **[Sharpening stones]** Whether sharpening stones function alongside a Windfury weapon imbue was asked but left unanswered in this chunk — [[d:shaman#1455504623178612817]] (see Contested section).
- **[Spellstrike/proc items]** "Spellstrike" gear (rings, off-hands, weapons with "Equip: Deal additional X elemental damage on hit") used to proc Wrath of Cenarius, Judgement of Wisdom, and Clearcasting for Spellhance builds; notable items: Embergem Cuffs (bracers), a ring from Incindis (new MC boss), the Incendosaur set (Hateforge Quarry, 2pc good for proc-hunting), a necklace of Kazzak; Flametongue imbue and shocks do NOT count as "true spells" for Paladin-style Seal-of-Righteousness-alike triggers but DO count as spellstrikes for Wrath of Cenarius-type procs — [[d:shaman#1455756268114280500]], [[d:shaman#1455756305682661376]], [[d:shaman#1459603534310801613]], [[d:shaman#1459603371236266046]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-31, 2026-01-11). Wrath of Cenarius uptime example: 2h Spellhance with Flametongue + 1 spellstrike item ≈ 50% uptime; 1 spellstrike + slow 1h ≈ ~40% uptime — [[d:shaman#1463213138773016801]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-21).
- **[Proc weapon comparisons]** Ornate Bloodstone Dagger (OBD, has an ICD) is more single-target-melee-oriented ("for meleeing if u got nice spelldmg"); "BoED"-type fast proc weapons (no ICD) suit mana-return/AoE farming better — [[d:shaman#1463057819275104276]], [[d:shaman#1463057922794848382]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-01-21). "Blade of Eternal Darkness"-type weapon (referenced as "the blade") procs from essence-of-the-flame-type attacks per Ciftar's confirmation, has no ICD, procs from Fiery Blaze enchant hits on multiple mobs simultaneously, and Fiery Blaze itself has a higher PPM than the Fiery Weapon enchant, making it BiS on this weapon — [[d:shaman#1463038519269261455]], [[d:shaman#1463042586271944745]], [[d:shaman#1463044153762910259]], [[d:shaman#1463097667402989693]] (Not Normal/Ciftar, 2026-01-21).
- **[Tank enchants]** Crusader weapon enchant preferred over an Agility weapon enchant for a "normal" (non-Kara-optimized) tank, since sub-1% crit/dodge isn't worth giving up Crusader's proc; belt buckle choice (+5 Defense vs -3% crit damage taken) discussed with no clear consensus, since shaman tanks typically don't chase def cap anyway (Eluzia: pick +10 Str on Nord, else +6 Agi) — see Tanking section for full detail and citations.

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Threat tools]** Earth Shock is the dominant single-target threat tool (~35-40% of tank TPS); Stoneclaw Totem (only with Totemic Alignment) and Fire Nova Totem are the two AoE threat tools, used situationally rather than one replacing the other (see Rotation and Tanking sections for full detail and math).
- **[Mana tools]** Water Shield does not passively regenerate mana — it only returns mana when a charge is consumed (i.e., when hit); Lightning Shield preferred on bosses (with Judgement of Wisdom for sustain), Water Shield preferred on trash/dungeons, especially combined with Totem of Tides. See Leveling and Tanking sections for full water/lightning shield usage guidance and citations.
- **[Resto mana tools]** Water Shield + T1 3-piece bonus are the main mana-saving tools for Spellhance/hybrid and resto builds; a geared paladin's Judgement of Wisdom uptime effectively removes mana as a concern in raids — [[d:shaman#1460593337462554704]] (Ciftar, 2026-01-13).
- **[Utility: totems]** Standard 4-totem raid loadout: Strength of Earth, Windfury (party), Flametongue (party), Mana Spring; swap Strength for Tremor if fear/sleep is present, add Poison/Disease Cleansing Totem as needed, Fire/Frost Resistance totems for elemental damage phases (Nature Resistance rarely worth it over Windfury Totem) — see 1.18.1 changes and Leveling sections for full citations. Totemic Recall (learned at level ~10-20 depending on race, Tauren get it at level 1) removes/recalls totems — see Bugs/Leveling sections.
- **[Utility: Tremor Totem]** Breaks fear immediately on drop, then pulses cleanse every 4 seconds — relevant for boss fear mechanics (e.g. Gurubashi Berserkers' Intimidating Shout in ZG) — [[d:shaman#1459821631760502889]] (piraja, 2026-01-11).
- **[Utility: Totemic Recall animation]** Totemic Recall reportedly still uses the regular totem-placement animation rather than a dedicated recall animation — cosmetic-only complaint — [[d:shaman#1452756897588318342]] (Thaodan, 2025-12-23).
- **[Resource management, Enh]** Rockbiter+Lightning Shield is mana-efficient early; Windfury+Water Shield trades slightly more mana for comfort/less drinking; post-Elemental Weapons+Clearcasting, Frostbrand+Frost Shock+Water Shield removes mana as a concern almost entirely (see Leveling section for full citations).
- **[Cooldown popping, endgame tank]** See Consumables section — haste pot/Bloodlust/kiss trinket/Juju popped together at pull for T3.5 set-bonus-driven threat spike, disputed value at lower gear levels.

## Tanking specifics
- **[Viability]** Enh Tank strong at all levels of content including highest raid tiers; main-tanks Kara40 "with ease"; only real downside is lack of a defensive cooldown — [[d:shaman#1454231138490122433]], [[d:shaman#1454231478362833081]], [[d:shaman#1456285608820478012]], [[d:shaman#1456285801162866892]] (Not Normal/Ciftar, 2025-12-27, 2026-01-01; "People who call this a meme are very new at the game").
- **[Tank ranking]** Bear (Druid) tanks considered the best/OP threat class; among the other 3 tank classes (Warrior, Paladin, Shaman) Enh threat is "incredibly strong." Shamans are "pretty well rounded" with weakness in AoE tanking (mitigated somewhat by Totem of Tides + Sigil of Ancient Accord); best tank for magical damage due to talents + T2.5 set — [[d:shaman#1454231138490122433]], [[d:shaman#1455361229357846686]] (Not Normal/Maleficennt, 2025-12-27, 2025-12-30). Warrior tanks better mitigation but worse threat unless building a threat set at the cost of mitigation — [[d:shaman#1455361229357846686]] (Maleficennt, 2025-12-30).
- **[Weapon choice]** Slow weapons always preferred for tank threat; weapon skill is the single best stat obtainable on a weapon; defensive stats on weapons are a bad trade (see Stats section for full detail and BiS weapon examples like "Remnants of the Old God" item 60003).
- **[Shield]** A shield roughly doubles armor; block chance/value is considered a near-dead stat for shaman tanks — no synergy/ability interacts with block (unlike Paladin's Holy Shield or Warrior's Shield Slam/rage generation); block value applies after armor, and the Stoneskin totem's flat block-value bonus is % based, not flat, per one correction — [[d:shaman#1458436698798031089]], [[d:shaman#1458436826472648734]], [[d:shaman#1458534092797902850]], [[d:shaman#1458143010176106765]] (Ciftar/Holidae/Bassilthorne, 2026-01-07). Block does NOT reduce chance to be crit/crushed — hit table works by priority (crit/crush ranked above hit), so block chance only converts an ordinary hit into a lesser hit unless avoidance is stacked so high crits/crushes get pushed off the table entirely; that avoidance-cap threshold is "not even possible in pre-Naxx gear and not worth it later" — [[d:shaman#1458518665472376904]], [[d:shaman#1456680178909450301]], [[d:shaman#1456680347885506823]] (Zhafu/Nandieb, 2025-12-30, 2026-01-07).
- **[Shield Specialization talent]** Consistently called a wasted/dead talent for both leveling and endgame tanks: "yes, its a complete waste, pretend it doesn't exist"; the only exception is deliberately building toward avoidance cap in Naxx/Kara40 gear, which is itself not generally recommended — [[d:shaman#1457836189464658163]], [[d:shaman#1457836292233625782]], [[d:shaman#1458133076411547680]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-06; multiple confirmations). Countervailing/nuanced view: 5% stat bonus isn't "remotely nothing" even at low levels since value-per-point of stats is higher when stats are scarce; but block itself is still worthless outside avoidance-cap builds — [[d:shaman#1458104791954620518]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-07, correcting an earlier simplification). Community sentiment: devs should remove Shield Spec from the tree entirely and replace with something more impactful — [[d:shaman#1457516993149145170]], [[d:shaman#1457518713526816961]] (Not Normal/Zamka, 2026-01-05).
- **[Wishlist/community ask]** Repeated wish for Shaman to get a defensive cooldown akin to WotLK's "Shamanistic Rage" (30% damage reduction + mana regen chance) since Shield Spec/block investment is considered dead weight — [[d:shaman#1457576596150943834]] (Abuelito, 2026-01-05; wishlist, unconfirmed).
- **[Defense rating]** Views split: "as much as possible" (Nandieb) vs. "whatever, if you have it, it's cool, if you don't, it's not a big deal" (Squid) — [[d:shaman#1457301316467556488]], [[d:shaman#1457303113458516143]] (2026-01-04, disputed). One tank reports getting crit even at 440 Defense on bosses like Mephistroth/Rupturan, questioning if that's a bug or intended — [[d:shaman#1457307981321605142]], [[d:shaman#1457308108853481544]], [[d:shaman#1457347006841360385]] (Zamka, 2026-01-04; unresolved). Shamans lack Defense-boosting talents/abilities and have no Defense from ZG-style enchants that other classes get, making 440 Def very costly in threat sacrifice; best K40 MTs reportedly don't bother reaching def cap — [[d:shaman#1457329408816971878]] (Ciftar, 2026-01-04).
- **[Enchants/gems for tank]** Recommended: mostly health/stamina enchants; +3 stats on rings/neck; keep a DPS-gear "pocket set" with strength enchants if a geared DPS isn't watching threat; best shoulder enchant pre-Naxx is 2% haste (expensive, hold off until decent gear) — [[d:shaman#1456784816153559211]], [[d:shaman#1456785087206002945]] (Zeruel, 2026-01-03). Weapon enchant recommendation: drop weapon Agility enchant, use Crusader instead (less than 1% crit/dodge not worth it over Crusader) for a "normal" (non-Kara-optimized) tank — [[d:shaman#1456722525529833612]], [[d:shaman#1456722809320505557]] (Zeruel, 2026-01-02).
- **[Shocks vs threat]** Earth Shock dominant threat tool (see Rotation section); Frost Shock does half the damage but comparable discussion of trading it in for lower threat is an open ask (see 1.18.1 changes). Devs reportedly told players "not to use ES" as physhance/spellhance despite Frost Shock doing half damage, seen as a poor recommendation by Ciftar — [[d:shaman#1456332397598605383]] (Ciftar, 2026-01-01).
- **[Threat tools note]** DoTs (e.g. Flame Shock) are bugged such that school resistance applies at 1/10th the normal rate, so resistance is a non-issue for shock DoTs — [[d:shaman#1457769121931464854]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-05).
- **[Totems for tanking]** Totem of Tides is the default AoE-tanking totem choice ("there's not really another totem for it"); grants extra threat on Water Shield procs and on Lightning Strike when Water Shield is active — [[d:shaman#1455087584765153322]], [[d:shaman#1455087680865177793]] (billienomates, 2025-12-29). Totem of Tides procs when Water Shield buff is simply active, not tied to charge expenditure — [[d:shaman#1456601234642440193]] (Ciftar, 2026-01-02). Stoneclaw Totem generates threat ONLY if Totemic Alignment talent (2/2) is taken, which transfers totem threat to the shaman; otherwise it does nothing for tank aggro — [[d:shaman#1455604636529463359]], [[d:shaman#1455604717332992041]], [[d:shaman#1458133139397410886]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-30, 2026-01-06). On bosses (non-double-shaman groups) Strength of Earth Totem preferred over Stoneclaw/Stoneskin — [[d:shaman#1458133255537688663]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-06). Stoneskin's damage-reduction is applied before armor, so in practice reduces incoming hits by only ~10 damage rather than the tooltip's ~30, minimal value against 1500+ boss hits — [[d:shaman#1458137175219769549]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-06).
- **[Tank BiS resources]** Shaman tank pre-BiS spreadsheet (Maleficennt/Jinxes), covers gear before AQ40's official tank set: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1CzwEalBFWUzw4ohP7ERIjB6f_O1hDiw79hw9FblFbjo — includes ~4 talent build options (cookie-cutter mitigation, AoE variants, dungeon spec) — [[d:shaman#1455352324238213274]], [[d:shaman#1455362548974096458]] (Maleficennt, 2025-12-30). AV-exalted rep shield judged BiS (better than Vault Defender); Argent Dawn exalted shield trades 1% dodge + 6 agi for ~180 armor and is a strong alternative, easier to farm since Strat/Scholo now give large AD rep — [[d:shaman#1455385516362039549]], [[d:shaman#1455387786583740530]], [[d:shaman#1455405857625080001]], [[d:shaman#1455406017180602450]] (billienomates/Not Normal, 2025-12-30). Skyfury Helm (from level-50 class quest) is one of the better enhance pieces, kept until T2 for its 5-piece bonus; haste+crit stats good — [[d:shaman#1457489635159703706]], [[d:shaman#1457491454892183684]] (Bassilthorne/Hiryu, 2026-01-05).

- **[Spell-focused/hybrid tank build]** Enh tank going spellpower-hybrid stats: viable pre-Naxx/Kara ("makes sense until 5/5 T3.5"), since Ele set bonuses for T3/T3.5 are considered poor ("absolute dogshit") for tanking; once at 5/5 T3.5 the meta shifts to an attack-speed-stacking build due to set procs — [[d:shaman#1453749772098732136]], [[d:shaman#1453749977619759235]], [[d:shaman#1453750062025801738]] (Ciftar/Eluzia, 2025-12-25). At endgame (T3.5), a good MT should pop cooldowns/consumables (haste pot, Bloodlust, kiss trinket, Juju) together at pull for a 10%+ set-bonus-driven threat spike; disputed whether haste pots specifically are worth it over stoneshield/spellpower flask setups at lower gear (Zeruel skeptical, Eluzia defends it as endgame-only tech) — [[d:shaman#1453827460901896244]], [[d:shaman#1453828530755473623]], [[d:shaman#1453828921005969540]], [[d:shaman#1453829313412468816]] (Zeruel/Eluzia, 2025-12-25).
- **[Enh tank AoE threat tools]** Stoneclaw Totem and Fire Nova Totem are the two real AoE threat tools; they work differently rather than one being flatly better: Stoneclaw is cheap, longer cooldown, threat spread over time; Fire Nova is a large burst of threat if timed well (e.g., nova timed to detonate right as a pulled pack arrives) but costs more mana — recommendation is to use both to varying degrees and manage mana accordingly — [[d:shaman#1463217046652326013]], [[d:shaman#1463218165520793783]], [[d:shaman#1463218424695099526]], [[d:shaman#1463218506685223168]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-21).
- **[Enh tank build 22/29 AoE variant]** 22/29/0 tank build variation confirmed to give "crazy AoE threat," observed anecdotally with a well-geared MT pulling with Molten Blast/Flame Shock — [[d:shaman#1463230990905512202]], [[d:shaman#1463231212985258090]] (otep1616/Not Normal, 2026-01-21).
- **[Enh tank mitigation vs threat debate]** Extended, heated community disagreement on whether shaman tanks should take mitigation talents (e.g. the enh armor talents, ~15% armor + 6% dodge) at the cost of threat: one camp (Zamka, joined by "top shaman tanks"/Eluzia per Zamka) argues threat is king and mitigation is unneeded when healers can handle the damage, citing a heuristic "if 15% less damage taken (7% EHP) only buys 2% more threat, that's a bad trade"; opposing camp (Diving Instructor Knife) argues recommending against baseline mitigation talents is "griefing" newer players, especially when logs show the mitigation-light build taking much more damage than peers — dispute unresolved in this chunk, records both sides — [[d:shaman#1462354547279265812]], [[d:shaman#1462355318183821383]], [[d:shaman#1462357029472702531]], [[d:shaman#1462357989465002187]], [[d:shaman#1462358077658370121]] (Zamka/Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-01-18; contested, unresolved). A third voice frames it as guild/DPS-context-dependent: "pumper" groups favor threat-over-mitigation, "chill" raid nights favor mitigation; experienced tanks carry multiple gear/talent sets — [[d:shaman#1462358479871148065]], [[d:shaman#1462359561858973736]] (LordHodenLurch, 2026-01-18).
- **[Enh tank avoidance-cap discussion]** To fully "clear the crit table" against a raid boss requires >100% total avoidance (dodge+parry+block+miss), since the miss-chance portion (~9%) is often omitted from naive avoidance-stacking math; full avoidance-cap tanking is a niche "deep mitigation" set, not a baseline requirement, and trades away significant DPS/threat — [[d:shaman#1462014889080913930]], [[d:shaman#1462024479323586661]], [[d:shaman#1462038870169489489]] (LordHodenLurch/poqoqu0741, 2026-01-17). Avoidance/defense cap (440 Def) is "basically just a deep mitigation set" akin to Prot Warrior/Feral/Prot Paladin's — useful for specific fights relying on taunts rather than threat-pumping, but not a must-have for a shaman tank generally — [[d:shaman#1462306713289097268]], [[d:shaman#1462306966138523876]], [[d:shaman#1462307098883915777]], [[d:shaman#1462307334939217931]] (Zamka, 2026-01-18).
- **[Twin Emperors (AQ40) shaman tank note]** The melee twin in the AQ40 Twin Emperors encounter is magic-immune, so a shaman tank on that boss generates threat purely from physical hits — a genuinely hard matchup requiring raid cooperation ("your raid have to play with u") — [[d:shaman#1462200380053000193]] (LordHodenLurch, 2026-01-17).
- **[Enh tank viability, endgame]** Enh tank confirmed fully viable including K40, arguably "even better than the leveling phase" once set up correctly (armor talents + Elemental Focus + Reverberation + Totemic Alignment for AoE); especially strong on single-target tanking after a new set — [[d:shaman#1462092307838013693]], [[d:shaman#1462116738190807181]], [[d:shaman#1462137004363092194]], [[d:shaman#1462139444277547133]] (Veddos/Ironbob/OmniSalat, 2026-01-17).

## Healing specifics
- **[Chain Heal AI bug]** See 1.18.1 changes section — currently jumps to any target missing any HP rather than intended lowest/biggest-missing-HP logic; devs have acknowledged the issue (per screenshot) — [[d:shaman#1456690077131341854]] (2026-01-02).
- **[Resto stat priority, full]** Healing Power = Haste > Int > mp5 > Crit; Spirit is a completely dead stat (see Stats section for full detail and math).
- **[Resto rotation/spell usage]** Typical spell usage in practice: Rank 4 and 9 Healing Wave, Rank 1 and 3 Chain Heal, and max-rank Lesser Healing Wave depending on situation, managing totems and consumables alongside — [[d:shaman#1454232980594753689]] (Chargearc, 2025-12-27). Lesser Healing Wave is best reserved for emergency/PvP-style burst healing when Ancestral Swiftness isn't up — it's the highest HPS single-target spell at max rank but "an absolute mana demolisher"; downranking regular Healing Wave is generally preferred otherwise — [[d:shaman#1458535088282402817]], [[d:shaman#1458535191810408539]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-07). Lower ranks of healing spells with shorter cast times benefit less from +healing power than the max rank does, so putting +heal on a rank-2 Healing Wave instead of picking up Int is a mistake — [[d:shaman#1456182031146025092]], [[d:shaman#1456182403130327071]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-26).
- **[Resto totems/tools]** Enhancing Totems talent gives ~20 Str from Strength of Earth Totem alone to entire group; Windfury Totem takes priority over Grace of Air unless double-shaman group or no melee present — [[d:shaman#1457772348437692608]], [[d:shaman#1457772430641594595]] (Not Normal, 2026-01-05).

Whip of Encouragement), Kara Crypts, then Black Morass once gear is decent (mobs "hit like a truck"); haste is prioritized very highly ("like crack to restos"), mp5 nice but not priority, crit "mostly underwhelming." Buy Gloves of Unwinding Mystery (haste) and "Frost Shock and You" book (for a trinket, worth ~50g) off AH — [[d:shaman#1455337266615685272]], [[d:shaman#1455337371049525339]], [[d:shaman#1455337777150427327]], [[d:shaman#1455338300935245824]], [[d:shaman#1455339043666530355]], [[d:shaman#1455339348323991574]], [[d:shaman#1455339513411801171]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-30).

## PvP
- **[Fire Ele vs Nature Ele in PvP]** Fire Ele judged clearly stronger in PvP: acts as a "melee hybrid spec" that can 2h + Lightning Shield burst opponents while still having ranged Molten Blast spam as a fallback, or swap to 1h+Rockbiter+Earth Shock; Nature Ele is "terrible in PvP" — can't self-heal effectively, and gets shut down by any interrupt/silence leaving only Frost Shock — [[d:shaman#1462910455143075903]] (Spicyahi, 2026-01-20). A Nature-Ele talent tied to needing a crit to trigger is dismissed as "complete garbage" for PvP since needing to already be critted usually means near-death, and the caster is still interruptible regardless — [[d:shaman#1462910926893219863]] (Spicyahi, 2026-01-20). Additional Fire Ele PvP advantages cited: +8% shock crit from talents, faster Earth Shock cooldown recovery after an interrupt (3s vs 10s if casting a Nature spell when Counterspelled), better melee damage via Flametongue, better mana efficiency (no Chain Lightning spam), Rockbiter option for 10% damage reduction, and can double as a Lasher-farming spec with 2 points in Totemic Alignment — [[d:shaman#1462849621280817295]] (Zaaparou, 2026-01-19). Minority dissenting view: prefers Nature Ele for PvP due to better interrupt-recovery options and avoiding forced melee — [[d:shaman#1462838365090087198]], [[d:shaman#1462838501551767552]], [[d:shaman#1462838603343331484]] (Purge, 2026-01-19; disputed, minority view).
- **[Ele PvP hit cap]** PvP spell hit cap is 3% (much lower than PvE's 16%), freeing up talent points (e.g. into Elemental Warding or Convection) once met — [[d:shaman#1459427503142600918]] (Ciftar, 2026-01-10).
