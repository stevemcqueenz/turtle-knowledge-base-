## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[General]** Windfury Weapon internal cooldown reduced from 6s to 0.5s and proc chance increased from 15% to 20% (dated Nov 1 2024 patch note, still in effect); as of mid-2025 there appears to be no ICD at all besides not proccing off itself (no WF chains) — [[d:shaman#1384083459877900329]].
- **[Elemental/PvP]** "Electrify" talent (post-"CC2" class changes) added ~7 months before 2025-07 chat: 10% bonus damage per stack to Shocks/Lightning-family, meant to curb burst; called a failure by critics since T3 8pc (+20%) and +10% crit to shocks made Ele burst stronger than pre-CC2, while Ele feels weak without the right gear — [[d:shaman#1390399353117479004]] (disputed intent/effectiveness, single strong opinion, no rebuttal in chunk).
- **[Elemental]** T3 8-piece elemental set bonus interaction with Electrify was questioned as possibly not working as tooltip implies (expected 5+5+10+10=30% total but seemed like shocks only generate Electrify stacks without full stacking value) — [[d:shaman#1374450645884735599]] (question, no confirmed answer in chunk).
- **[General]** Devs discussing possible shaman tank defensive CD (Stoneskin Totem rework floated) as of 2025-07-07/08; community split on whether it's needed given already-high avoidance/DR (15% all-damage DR, 12% spell DR from T2.5, 10% Fire/Frost/Nature DR from talents) — [[d:shaman#1391547286362001531]], [[d:shaman#1391990780591210496]] (contested, dev response pending per Luke: "it's planned" [[d:shaman#1388611729688236142]]).
- **[Enhance]** Frostbrand stacking behavior believed by some to be an unintended bug; community expects a "significant buff" to Frostbrand/Spellhance in 1.18 patch, following a pattern of Turtle devs nerfing things right before major patches — [[d:shaman#1397504618157838377]] (single source, unconfirmed).
- **[Ele]** WF Totem does not stack with personal Windfury Imbue (self-overwrite); exception is rank 5 Frostbrand which is called a bug that still works while WF totem is up — [[d:shaman#1377784055701180586]].

- **[1.18 patch, all specs]** Major Windfury Totem rework (per official dev post): effect is now an Aura (not an imbue); only procs off main-hand attacks; totem now has a single rank; no longer grants bonus Attack Power on the extra attacks; internal cooldown increased from 1s to 1.5s; shaman's own weapon imbues take priority over the totem (totem does not affect the shaman while any personal imbue is active), meaning WF Totem "does not compete with temporary weapon enchants" for the caster — [[d:shaman#1417854178696499201]].
- **[1.18 patch, all specs]** Flametongue Totem rework: effect is now an aura; now stacks with Windfury Totem (previously didn't); no longer scales with Spell Power; imbues take priority over the totem for the shaman; the totem's extra damage can no longer trigger on-hit effects — [[d:shaman#1417854178696499201]] (Not Normal questioned why FT was made non-scaling instead of just tuning numbers — [[d:shaman#1399878430337536021]], unanswered).
- **[1.18 patch, Elemental]** New spell Molten Blast added: burns target with Fire damage, refreshes Flame Shock duration, and focuses Searing Totem onto that target; 2s cast, flat mana cost, 36 yd range, 57.14% spellpower coefficient. Ranks: R1 (lvl20) 61-73 dmg/65 mana; R2 (lvl28) 96-119/95; R3 (lvl36) 136-155/120; R4 (lvl44) 185-204/145; R5 (lvl52) 238-268/175; R6 (lvl60) 290-331/210 — [[d:shaman#1403298839271575552]]. Dev comment: designed to let Ele re-target Searing Totem and refresh Flame Shock without needing to be in shock range, and to remain effective vs enemies highly resistant/immune to Nature damage — [[d:shaman#1405938354968727562]].
- **[1.18 patch, Elemental]** New talent Improved Molten Blast (Row 3, 2 points): refreshing Flame Shock with Molten Blast deals bonus damage equal to 30/60% of the *already-ticked* portion of the refreshed duration (not +60% MB damage) — [[d:shaman#1402268257981038633]], clarified — [[d:shaman#1402760887596486776]], dev comment confirms design intent (good both for spam and single-refresh use) — [[d:shaman#1405936269220905113]].

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Enhance]** Considered "mid pack" DPS overall — behind Mages/Warriors/Locks — but strong totem-driven melee-group damage; main problems cited: excessive threat (esp. WF proc chains), mana efficiency, and unclear tank gearing path before AQ40 — [[d:shaman#1390348150858059797]].
- **[Elemental]** Viable single-target: in last 3 months of parses (as of 2025-06-23) Ele had 7 of top 20 Shaman Patchwerk parses vs 13 Enhance — roughly half as many but still "significant," showing both specs viable for ST DPS — [[d:shaman#1386588553680781372]].
- **[Enhance]** Threat complaints widespread and repeated: "unacceptably high," WF procs can force players to stop attacking; blamed on non-normalized weapon damage on Stormstrike/Lightning Strike (rewards slow weapons), Calming Winds threat modifier (0.85) weaker than other classes' (0.8), and shocks not covered by Calming Winds at all — [[d:shaman#1377869564762914948]], [[d:shaman#1384859692391600148]], [[d:shaman#1398123902126915585]] (+9 reactions on a dev-facing complaint thread).
- **[Tank]** As of 2025-07, shaman tank ranked dead last for mitigation among tank classes, but above warrior for threat generation *only* when using a 2H weapon — [[d:shaman#1391630460227944561]].
- **[Tank]** T2.5 (tier 2.5) set is the only tank-purpose gear; comparable in mitigation to warrior's defensive T3, but shaman tanking viability is otherwise still being worked out (no clear consensus on 2H vs shield vs elemental-gear tanking) — [[d:shaman#1383327059123638333]], [[d:shaman#1396026930393907210]].
- **[Elemental]** Community view: Ele scales worse from crit than most casters despite crit being central to its identity — only deep Affliction lock and Shadow Priest scale worse; Ele/Frost/Arcane mage all get 200% (not higher) crit damage norm, while other classes have ignite/imp shadow bolt/nature's grace/searing light bonuses; Ele is pushed toward SP/hit priority instead — [[d:shaman#1397625620288241779]] (detailed, single source, not disputed).
- **[General]** Multiple messages call current Shaman (esp. Enhance) "broken"/"most faceroll class" as of 2025-06-08/06-24 — [[d:shaman#1381033982954831954]], [[d:shaman#1387079599883817050]] (opinions, contested by threat complaints above).

- **[Post-1.18, Elemental Fire vs Nature]** Fire ("Molten Blast spam") Ele build sims ~7.5-9% DPS increase over baseline Lightning Ele on a single-target rotation (per community sim, see Talent builds section), and reportedly tops server DPS parses on Patchwerk-style fights (top Fire Ele parse 1926.7 vs top Lightning Ele parse 1602.9, i.e. Fire ~20% ahead at the very top of the leaderboard) — [[d:shaman#1403405717246836776]], [[d:shaman#1417613687581310976]]. However, rough resistance-based estimate suggests Fire's raw damage lead is largely offset by fire resistance on many raid bosses, meaning Fire Ele likely underperforms Lightning Ele specifically in MC (half the bosses fire-immune or high fire-resist, including Ragnaros) — [[d:shaman#1417602573875281991]], [[d:shaman#1422911619553427477]]. In Kara40, Fire/Lightning Ele parses are close except a few fights (Incantagos being a clear Fire/Nature-favoring exception) — [[d:shaman#1420648921696309279]].
- **[Post-1.18, Elemental]** Fire Ele in melee/WF groups: some Ele players prefer staying in the melee group even post-1.18, citing less reliance on crit/mana-conservation tricks (no need for Vampiric Embrace-equivalent) and that their damage buffs come from Mage/Lock curses/debuffs on the boss rather than caster-group auras, making Fire Ele "fit" a melee raid slot — [[d:shaman#1419000930472300586]], [[d:shaman#1400973390428241951]].
- **[Post-1.18, Elemental utility]** Ele's "tanky spell-DPS" (22/29/0-style, 1H+shield, spell power gear) hybrid playstyle gained traction post-1.18: benefits from all Enhance armor talents plus Rockbiter's flat 10% damage reduction, Stormstrike (+20% Earth Shock damage) and Lightning Strike for melee pressure, while Earth Shock scales with SP not AP — pitched as a safer alternative to 2H Enhance without sacrificing much damage since players often have better Ele gear than Enh gear — [[d:shaman#1418416455099355278]], [[d:shaman#1419270788871098410]], example video referenced twice — [[d:shaman#1414779857136779264]], [[d:shaman#1419270788871098410]].
- **[Post-1.18, Resto]** Strong criticism that Chain Heal still dominates Resto gameplay/output (99% of healing per one player's Kara40 log check) despite patch changes, "invalidating" other Resto talents/spells (Undertow, Tidal Surge, Ancestral Fortitude, Healing Wave-proc-on-CH) that are individually flavorful but rarely matter next to CH spam — [[d:shaman#1399898074272239617]], [[d:shaman#1406281022697508986]], [[d:shaman#1410642291390943242]] (multiple independent complaints; some players (peter_hammer) welcomed the devs' attempt to move away from CH spam and are waiting to see the result — [[d:shaman#1406289165435408454]]).
- **[Post-1.18, Tank]** Consensus among experienced tank mains (DemonBoyJr, Rinzu, peter_hammer) that pre-August/pre-1.17 shaman tank guides (e.g. a well-known forum guide) are outdated and unreliable — "nobody took that guide seriously even a week or two after 1.17 release," criticized for e.g. calling Stoneskin Totem "mandatory" — [[d:shaman#1416151840462082170]] (direct quoted Discord exchange).
- **[Post-1.18, PvP]** General perception that recent PvP-targeted nerfs (Purge CD, Grounding Totem CD +33%, Eye of the Storm change, Ancestral/Nature's Swiftness change) were driven by Paladin-channel pressure ("It's very very hard to not feel like the pala discord/devs have a big sway on how shaman changes land," +7) — recurring, contested sentiment throughout the 1.18 lead-up — [[d:shaman#1405417292153618524]], [[d:shaman#1405447146819555358]], [[d:shaman#1406007562654056499]].

## Leveling
- **[Enhance, general path]** Common consensus leveling order: get Lightning Strike (LS), then Stormstrike (SS) — or swap order — then Elemental Focus (via Ele tree) around level 40; equip a big 2H weapon; use Rockbiter solo. Respec-to-Ele common around level 40 if desired. For tanking, take shield talent until ~level 40 when Ancestral (Guardian?) becomes relevant; totem threat talent optional, not necessary until endgame. For healing pre-50s, no dedicated Resto talents needed — int gear + max-rank spells suffice — [[d:shaman#1374532772730765434]].
- **[Enhance, key combo]** At level ~41-42 (42 with Stormstrike), spec ~11 pts into Elemental tree for "Elemental Focus" and ~21 pts Enhancement for "Elemental Weapons": Elemental Weapons makes Frostbrand Weapon always crit while target has Frost Shock debuff, giving near-permanent Elemental Focus uptime (60% reduced mana cost on Shocks/LS/SS); combined with Water Shield, near-endless mana for leveling — [[d:shaman#1377735610227490956]] (talent link included, level 42 build), reaffirmed repeatedly — [[d:shaman#1379672131331428382]], [[d:shaman#1389471063745757205]], [[d:shaman#1391072308038471835]] (4 citations, strong consensus).
- **[Enhance]** Suggested single-target leveling rotation once LS+SS available: LS → SS → ES → LS (LS has longer CD so get it rolling first); once Frostbrand available, open with Frost Shock so first auto has crit chance — [[d:shaman#1383511723767496835]].
- **[Enhance]** Multi-target leveling: Chain Lightning to pull 3 mobs, then cycle Stormstrike → Lightning Strike → Earth Shock per target; mana-intensive, Water Shield mandatory — [[d:shaman#1393882527638487080]].
- **[Enhance, weapon choice]** Slow weapon preferred once Stormstrike available (level ~42) since LS/SS scale off weapon damage range; before that, faster weapon (e.g. dagger) with Flametongue gives more crit procs for Clearcasting; can weapon-swap 2H for LS/SS casts if latency allows, but resets swing timer so often not worth it — [[d:shaman#1394048913341812817]], [[d:shaman#1394029153870811268]].

Mark targets so party focuses kill order. Ask Paladins for Judgement of Wisdom on skull. ## Rotation: single target
- **[Elemental]** Per-target thresholds (end-game BIS gear): 1 crit point ≈ 12.4 SP, 1 hit point ≈ 18.7 SP for value-equivalence; for early gearing (450 SP, 10% hit, 30% crit): 1 crit ≈ 7.9 SP, 1 hit ≈ 11 SP. Numbers don't factor Clearcasting uptime, and trash rotations (lower hit-cap needs) should weight crit higher — [[d:shaman#1375812612729737236]].
- **[Elemental]** Basic ST rotation: spam Lightning Bolt (LB) and maintain totems; if mana-constrained, stop there. If not too tight, weave Chain Lightning (CL) on Clearcasting procs. Maintain Flame Shock debuff if running with Fire mages. If fight is very short/mana not an issue, spam CL on CD (usually 3x LB into 1x CL, more LB if high haste). On cleave packs, spam CL on CD; use Earth Shock as a finisher on adds that need to die fast — [[d:shaman#1375813451875749929]].
- **[Elemental]** Never stop casting to swap spell types mid-cast for a Clearcasting-CL weave if reaction time/latency won't allow it (e.g., at 36 yards) — better to just keep spamming LB — [[d:shaman#1375814204275298374]].
- **[Elemental]** At low mana/low gear, skip Chain Lightning and Shocks on single target — pure Lightning Bolt spam is sufficient; better to downrank to Rank 4 LB (full SP scaling retained) than unequip Totem of the Storm when low on mana — [[d:shaman#1395883650171801621]].
- **[Elemental]** Haste value depends on rotation (BIS numbers): pure LB rotation, 1% haste = 14.8 SP; 3xLB-into-CL rotation, 1% haste = 12.1 SP; LB+CL+maintain Flame Shock rotation, 1% haste = 11.2 SP. For CL-on-CD rotations, haste becomes useless once LB cast+delay drops under ~2s (CL has 6s CD) — above ~4% haste wasted unless doing 4xLB-into-CL — [[d:shaman#1381099264209584301]], [[d:shaman#1385252925902819421]].
- **[Elemental]** Lightning Bolt overtakes Chain Lightning single-target only at very high haste (~25% haste needed at 1200 SP per one calc); the closer to that threshold, the more CL's mana efficiency wins; 3xLB-into-1xCL becomes suboptimal at far less haste than that (waiting on CL CD) — [[d:shaman#1394815047800193034]].
- **[Elemental]** Flame Shock vs Earth Shock as filler: math (400 SP + 102 nature SP, 30% crit, 15% fire dmg talent, Electrify 2-stack) gives Flame Shock avg 1311.72 vs Earth Shock avg 1032.28 over 10000 iterations — but in practice shocks are mainly used as an instant "finisher" when there's no time to land another LB, so Earth Shock (no missed DoT value) is often chosen over Flame Shock; Flame Shock used mainly while moving — [[d:shaman#1397216591611166852]], [[d:shaman#1397221480965865593]].
- **[Elemental]** Adding Flame Shock (with Scorch debuff present) to rotation calculated as ~23 dps increase; without Scorch (all-Arcane-mage raid) value is much smaller — [[d:shaman#1397229128574566472]].
- **[Elemental]** Spell penetration: use a spell pen enchant/piece for bosses — every boss has 15 penetrable resistance, a larger DPS gain than alternative SP enchants; only skip it if using Thunderfury (raises resist threshold to 25+, rare) — [[d:shaman#1396685406371709090]].
- **[Enhance]** Basic leveling/general DPS rotation: totems → auto attack → Stormstrike → auto/Earth Shock → Lightning Strike — [[d:shaman#1375916876336599050]].
- **[Enhance]** Frostbrand + Elemental Weapons + Frost Shock combo gives ~100% crit chance on Frostbrand procs (while Frost Shock debuff up), driving near-permanent Elemental Focus (-60% mana cost) — core Enhance leveling/hybrid rotation gimmick — [[d:shaman#1378100552306786356]] (see Leveling section for more citations).

- **[Enhance, 1.18 rotation optimization]** Detailed cooldown-timing debate on Stormstrike/Lightning Strike/Earth Shock priority: with SS on a short-ish CD, LS ~4.5s(ish) CD-adjacent, and ES on 5s CD, simply pressing whichever is off cooldown causes cascading delays; math comparison of 3 sequencing options concluded that prioritizing Stormstrike-then-Lightning-Strike (accepting a small Earth Shock delay) loses less overall value than delaying Stormstrike/Lightning Strike to always fire Earth Shock on cooldown, since despite Earth Shock's priority for damage, the CD lost from delaying it is smaller than CD lost delaying SS/LS — net recommendation: SS -> LS -> ES repeating (weave in Flame Shock only if using it) — [[d:shaman#1422243273824800830]], [[d:shaman#1422259194995671270]], [[d:shaman#1422256929970983003]] (extensive math, community converged on SS->LS->ES with occasional double-ES).
- **[Elemental Fire, Molten Blast math]** Detailed community math on whether Molten Blast (MB) is worth using over just refreshing via Flame Shock (FS) recast, or over pure Lightning Bolt (LB): raw unbuffed dummy test at 460 SP showed MB hits ~748, a "rekindled" bonus tick ~906, vs a Lightning Bolt hit at ~920 — meaning even a 3-tick-FS-refresh + MB combo has a higher combined SP coefficient than one Lightning Bolt, when curses/debuffs are excluded — [[d:shaman#1423302667169431663]]. Further analysis: swapping a plain Flame Shock recast for a Molten Blast refresh only nets roughly one bonus MB hit's worth of damage every ~12-15s (a few hundred DPS at most, situationally), and FS's DoT snapshot value degrades quickly since FS only lasts 15s, meaning strict adherence to "always MB right before FS falls off" underperforms simply spamming Lightning Bolt after the first few refresh windows — [[d:shaman#1422980244259999895]], [[d:shaman#1423244896042483763]], [[d:shaman#1423297895473614878]] (contested/nuanced — some maintain MB is still worth using "as late as possible" for a modest but real gain, calling the resulting rotation more complex than raw button-mashing — [[d:shaman#1423339091201753272]]).
- **[Elemental]** Flame Shock snapshots caster stats at the moment of cast (not continuously): if Elemental Mastery is popped *before* casting Flame Shock, the DoT's full duration benefits from EM's crit bonus even after EM itself expires; if EM is popped while an old Flame Shock is already ticking, it does NOT retroactively apply — [[d:shaman#1420830381762154628]].
- **[Elemental, hit gearing]** Actual raid experience: cleared an entire MC/Onyxia phase with only 6-8% spell hit (well under the notional 16% cap) and still outperformed most other DPS — practical takeaway that pre-raid-BiS players shouldn't feel forced to reach full hit cap immediately; personal rule cited: willing to sacrifice up to 20 spell power for a spell hit or spell crit upgrade, since SP is more replaceable via consumables/enchants (150+35+22+30+15 from consumables, +12+12+9+9+30+12 from enchants) while crit/hit are not — [[d:shaman#1418551299586261066]], [[d:shaman#1421875988198854767]].

- **[Enhance, weapon damage mechanic]** Non-normalized ability damage formula: damage = weapon's base damage + ((Attack Power / 14) * weapon speed) — meaning as AP grows, slower weapons gain disproportionately more bonus damage per swing from the AP term, so a slower weapon can out-DPS a nominally-higher-DPS faster weapon once AP is significant. 14 AP = 1 DPS-equivalent; normalized abilities instead use a fixed per-weapon-type multiplier (example values cited: 1H axe = 2.4, 2H sword = 3.3) instead of actual weapon speed — Stormstrike and Lightning Strike are explicitly NOT normalized and use the weapon's actual speed — [[d:shaman#1409510584935776357]], [[d:shaman#1409525745725870120]], [[d:shaman#1411517327739977809]], [[d:shaman#1422125039880241294]].
- **[Enhance]** Frostbrand's PPM-style proc mechanic is not true PPM — it standardizes proc chance based on weapon speed, so slower weapons get more Frostbrand procs (higher %-chance per swing); this does NOT apply to Stormstrike/Lightning Strike, which trigger at the same rate regardless of weapon speed — [[d:shaman#1411831088401092688]].
- **[Tank]** Stat priority rule of thumb: Stamina (enough to keep healers comfortable) > Dodge/Defense/Agility > Strength/Block. Block value from Strength isn't very impactful; block chance mainly matters for pushing Crushing Blows off the attack-table (e.g. a 2% block-chance shield enchant is rated above a stamina enchant for that specific purpose) but isn't worth prioritizing over other mitigation forms — [[d:shaman#1409306944912625707]], [[d:shaman#1420519789637402746]] (Strength of Earth Totem ironically also raises block value since Str affects blocked-damage amount).
- **[Tank]** Avoidance/defense math: 102.4% total avoidance = fully crush-capped; 87.4% avoidance = crit-capped (crits removed from the attack table); alternatively, 440 Defense skill achieves crush/crit immunity by raising Defense rating instead. WoW's attack-roll priority order: Miss > Parry > Dodge > Block > Crushing Blow > Critical Hit > Normal Hit — [[d:shaman#1416233642010742865]], [[d:shaman#1416231348481294380]].
- **[Tank]** At lower/mid gear levels (e.g. Ambershire pre-raid), true crit/crush immunity is unreachable — beyond the ~15 Defense needed to equalize Defense-vs-boss-Attack, additional Defense mostly trades normal hits for dodge/parry/block rather than meaningfully reducing crit/crush chance; some argue this means early tank gearing should favor armor/HP ("bear logic") over chasing Defense past that threshold — [[d:shaman#1413163416499130368]] (single source, not confirmed/disputed in chunk).
- **[Elemental hit]** PvP spell hit cap is only 3% (easily reached with any real gear), unlike PvE's higher hit requirement — reduces the need to slot dedicated hit talents in PvP Ele builds — [[d:shaman#1418862931726041118]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Enhance threat]** Root cause analysis: (1) non-normalized weapon damage on Stormstrike/Lightning Strike rewards slow, high-variance weapons; (2) combined with high-variance Windfury Imbue creates "rollercoaster" threat spikes; (3) Calming Winds threat-reduction modifier (0.85) is weaker than other classes' stance/form modifiers (Cat/Battle/Berserker/Rogue ~0.8); (4) Shocks are entirely uncovered by Calming Winds, dealing full threat — [[d:shaman#1384859692391600148]].
- **[Enhance threat]** Proposed community fix (unofficial, discussed with devs "in talks" per pinned mod comment): rework the Windfury effect within Elemental Weapons talent to specifically reduce threat from Windfury procs; suggestion also floated to give Calming Winds a flat "% threat reduction from all spells/attacks" — [[d:shaman#1384859692391600148]], [[d:shaman#1398125790691852448]] (+9 reactions), [[d:shaman#1398136714115026964]] (mod: "must've gotten lost in the log but calming winds is still under talks," +21 reactions).
- **[Elemental]** Clearcasting is a strong efficiency talent, more valuable at low gear (mana concern) than at high gear where mana is rarely a bottleneck — [[d:shaman#1397625620288241779]].
- **[Totem macros/tools]** Standard way to drop all 4 totems fast: nampower addon (enables instant-cast queuing) + either a `/cast (totem1)` ... `/cast (totem4)` stacked macro, or the CallOfElements (CoE) addon (`/coe throwset x` to throw an active set, `/coe advised` for next advised totem); CoE also offers a contextual cleanse-poison/cleanse-disease button. If CoE has issues (reported incompatible with Healer's Mate addon), use a specific fork (laytya/CallOfElements) using the code, not release version — [[d:shaman#1375992840655736843]] (repeated verbatim multiple times: [[d:shaman#1378344608555204698]], [[d:shaman#1378649970466361455]]).
- **[Totem macro alt]** Alternative: bind a 4-stacked totem-cast macro to mouse scroll wheel, allowing manual control over how many/how fast totems drop by scroll speed — [[d:shaman#1378343932429340682]].
- **[Totems, leveling QoL]** Many totem-related buttons can be unbound/simplified: Cure Disease/Cure Poison can be one decursive button; unbind Mana Spring, Water Walking/Breathing (put on unbound bar); Imbues can go on an unbound bar; can likely unbind Res totems, Nature Resistance (never used in PvE), Calm Elements; use Clique for one-click totems/heals/res — [[d:shaman#1374704635008385135]].

- **[Tank]** Stat/gearing focus while leveling as a shield-tank: Stam/Int/Agi early (Rockbiter adds large AP amount); Str not very valuable besides threat; Int lets you cast more (heals/shocks); big threat sources are Earth Shock and Lightning Strike; Stoneskin preferred over Strength of Earth for personal survivability, switch to Grace of Air once available; Tranquil Air droppable on casters at higher level while tanking away from your own totem — [[d:shaman#1376000989362130974]].
- **[Tank]** Totemic Alignment (threat-transfer talent) widely considered weak/not worth it — see AoE section for detailed contested debate — [[d:shaman#1380202223950106734]] et al.
- **[Tank]** T2.5 set = primary/only dedicated tank set; comparable in mitigation power to Warrior's defensive T3; devs/community still disagree on 2H vs 1H+shield vs elemental-gear approaches for shaman tanking, all viable "personal taste" as of 2025-07 — [[d:shaman#1396026930393907210]].
- **[Tank]** Real numbers: geared 2H tank hitting 900+ TPS on Gluth with 2.05 threat multiplier on shocks and ~1.375 on melee/other; can "one-tank" Gluth's decimate stacks; Loatheb sustained ~700 TPS due to mana throttling — [[d:shaman#1394268514969325589]].
- **[Tank]** Avoidance at endgame with correct gear can reach "avoidance capping," described as unprecedented for a vanilla-based tank class; before critical avoidance mass, defensive CD "would be appreciated" specifically against physical damage — [[d:shaman#1391990780591210496]].
- **[Tank]** Passive mitigation from T2.5 + talents: 15% DR on all damage, 12% DR on spells (from T2.5 set), plus 10% DR each vs Fire/Frost/Nature from talents — [[d:shaman#1391990780591210496]].
- **[Tank]** Community debate ongoing (2025-07) on a proposed defensive cooldown for shaman tanks — options discussed: a Stoneskin Totem rework into a downside-free defensive CD (matching other tank classes' CDs having no drawback), a block-chance-based cooldown (100% avoidance window, doesn't help PvP casting), or a % damage-reduction CD; concern that any version locking out Earth Shock/Taunt during use would hurt enrage-phase threat — [[d:shaman#1391547286362001531]], [[d:shaman#1391659539853611131]], [[d:shaman#1391819825269182504]] (contested, unresolved; dev "planned" per Luke [[d:shaman#1388611729688236142]]).
- **[Tank]** EHP-check fights (e.g. Chromaggus in BWL, hits hard + enrages at 20%) cited as scenarios where pure avoidance can't save a tank — armor/stam stacking still needed — [[d:shaman#1391993201631559791]].
- **[Tank]** Crushing blows: with Kings/Edict-type buffs pushing defense over the crushing-immune threshold, a geared shaman tank reportedly saw a "crit once every ~160 autos" rate and never saw a crit on Loatheb/Sapphiron MTing for months — [[d:shaman#1392395930896040008]].
- **[Tank/enhance mixed]** Ele gear (agility etc.) not favored for tanking — agility doubles as threat+mitigation stat but Ele gear lacks it; stacking spell power on a shaman tank likened to stacking strength on a bear tank: "technically good but other stuff is much better" — [[d:shaman#1375791369528021062]].

- **[Tank, 1.18 design summary]** Widely-copy-pasted community/dev-aligned summary of post-1.18 tank shaman design (posted verbatim many times by peter_hammer and others, suggesting it's treated as an authoritative description): Tank Shaman lacks a dedicated tanking-ability kit but is competitive with traditional tanks for dungeon-tanking, and can serve as a versatile off-tank (with main-tank potential) at endgame; AoE tanking is talent/fire-totem-dependent (unlike other tanks' unresistable/untalented AoE threat sources) and stays competitive in dungeons but shows gaps at endgame unless investing in spell power similarly to a Paladin; despite lacking a dedicated defensive cooldown, Tank Shaman has a low-variance damage-intake profile from high armor + Rockbiter Weapon's innate damage reduction, without sacrificing damage/threat the way other tanks' stance-swaps do; two distinct playstyles exist — a 2H build stacking Agility for dodge/crit-driven threat, and a sword-and-board build focusing Parry/Block/Strength; high talent overlap with Enhancement DPS allows quick spec-flexible swaps — [[d:shaman#1404939100125134919]] (reposted identically at least 6 times across the chunk by multiple users, indicating strong community consensus/canonical status).
- **[Tank, two playstyles]** Confirmed dual playstyle split: (1) 2H weapon build stacking Agility (dodge + crit threat) — favored for max single-target/snap threat and AP scaling on Rockbiter; (2) 1H+shield build focusing Parry/Block/Strength — favored for survivability/EHP and armor value from the shield itself (shield's raw armor often exceeds the rest of the gear combined) — [[d:shaman#1404939100125134919]], [[d:shaman#1386725006649593969]].
- **[Tank, post-1.18 threat sources]** Post-1.18, Earth Shock hits very hard on a short 5s CD without requiring hard-casting, benefiting fully from Spell Power; Stormstrike adds +20% Nature damage to Earth Shock; Lightning Strike adds solid melee-range damage; Rockbiter gives a flat 10% damage reduction from all sources plus a large AP boost that further buffs Earth Shock via AP scaling — this combination lets a "Spell Tank" (Ele-geared, 1H+shield) match Enhance-tank damage/threat while being tankier since it doesn't need to trade the shield for a 2H — [[d:shaman#1418416455099355278]], [[d:shaman#1419270788871098410]], [[d:shaman#1421996329776840757]].
- **[Tank, mana]** At level 35-48 tanking, common mana-spending priority order reported: totems (Stoneskin/Mana Spring/Windfury if useful) > interrupt-rank Earth Shock > snap-threat Earth Shock (rare) > Fire Nova Totem (3+ healthy enemies, mana allowing) > Stormstrike > Lightning Shield (if mana >50%, else Water Shield refreshed on any free GCD) > Lightning Strike; sustainable indefinitely only by running Water Shield ~90% of the time — [[d:shaman#1414581006043975730]].
- **[Tank, AoE threat at 60]** With 2/2 Totemic Alignment (post-buff) + Stoneclaw Totem, AoE threat is "usually enough" without needing Magma/Nova on every pull; Magma favored for sustained AoE threat, Nova for burst — for dungeon pulls where mana isn't a concern, both can be dropped together — [[d:shaman#1418972945577021502]].
- **[Tank, raid role]** Community consensus (2025-09) that a geared Shaman tank can main-tank most fights; exceptions cited: Heigan (mana-burn mechanic) and Maexxna (needs a hard defensive CD) considered basically impossible or highly impractical for Shaman MT, but otherwise "any phase is able to be tanked by a shaman of suitable gear level" — [[d:shaman#1417530017402847366]].

## Healing specifics
- **[Resto]** Chain Heal is the raid workhorse; Healing Wave (esp. high-rank/full T1 bonus) is comparatively mana-inefficient per the math debate above — see Stats/Gear sections — [[d:shaman#1377261425705226350]].
- **[Resto]** Healing philosophy: proactive healing is the correct playstyle in vanilla-style content (damage is spiky/rng, not scripted like modern MMOs); judge performance via total healing + cast count relative to peer healers' gear level, not raw meter position — [[d:shaman#1377421442781548564]].
- **[Resto]** Spirit Link seen as low-value/rarely essential in Naxx and even Kara40 except specific fights (e.g. Maexxna); some consider dropping it for reduced Reincarnation ("ankh") CD instead, since tanks generally shouldn't die regardless — [[d:shaman#1379431123049779222]] (question, largely unanswered/contested in chunk).
- **[Resto]** Suggested Chain Heal / healing rotation priority: Mana Tide → Chain Heal → Ancestral (Fortitude?) → Shield, finishing with crit Healing Wave/Undertow procs, then Spirit Link. Healing Wave cast-time-reduction and Undertow can be picked up earlier than "meta" if the player's groups need it — Spirit Link's value increases with more raid members present (more people to split damage to) — [[d:shaman#1376706168780488776]].
- **[Resto]** Full T1 stats and Healing-Wave-vs-Chain-Heal mana-efficiency math — see Stats section.
- **[Resto healing itemization]** Green Dragonscale armor 3-set (30% mana regen while casting) + Water Shield mana regen stacking to ~48% total mana regen from gear+talent combo called a strong, cheap early healing setup — [[d:shaman#1381567129513623563]], [[d:shaman#1381568329868902501]].
- **[Resto]** Crit-stacking build reported to achieve similar HPS to an HP-stacking build (comparable healer using Sapphiron cloak, KT neck, 2x Meph crit rings) due to Ancestral Fortitude synergy, though considered less optimal than pure HP without full Naxx BiS — [[d:shaman#1389953326711570493]] (single source, informal).
- **[Resto]** Dungeon healing doesn't require Resto talent investment — any spec with Int gear + current max-rank heal spells can heal average groups through the 40s — [[d:shaman#1374532772730765434]], [[d:shaman#1395907912014237889]].

- **[PvP, Tank]** Shaman tanks in 2H builds described as very strong/unbalanced in PvP ("semi trucks rolling over people"), motivating proposals to make Rockbiter reduce damage dealt in exchange for more threat/defense (like Warrior Defensive Stance) so PvP power doesn't scale 1:1 with PvE tank threat needs — [[d:shaman#1391556839598063678]].
- **[PvP]** Faction imbalance complaint (Turtle Alliance/"TA" server, no Paladins): Alliance lacks Blessing of Kings/Salvation-equivalent percentage buffs, gap grows as content progresses; also, Windfury Totem locks the shaman into the melee group so the raid loses Mana Spring Totem's raid-wide value versus Paladin's raid-wide Seal of Wisdom/Blessing of Wisdom — [[d:shaman#1375396887712108567]].

- **[PvP, 1.18 Purge nerf detail]** After the 8s Purge cooldown was added, many mobs/NPCs on Turtle instantly recast a purged buff before the shaman's own cooldown expires, meaning casting Purge is frequently strictly worse than not casting it (wastes mana+GCD for zero benefit and puts Purge on CD for when it might matter) — raised as new-player-experience-breaking and evidence the change wasn't well tested — [[d:shaman#1405509160073953321]] (extensive single narrated example, +6 reactions cumulative across replies).
- **[PvP, itemization]** Enhancement's T3 tier set carries ~6% bonus hit (totaling ~9% with talents, excluding jewelry/trinkets) despite Enhance being a single-weapon (non-dual-wield) spec that doesn't need dual-wield-level hit — repeated dev-facing complaint that this "dead" stat should be converted to crit/haste, noting T2 chest/boots/gloves are already better than T3 equivalents due to T2's haste stats — [[d:shaman#1405565696036962385]], [[d:shaman#1405577262555861034]], [[d:shaman#1411771101851156591]] (compares to Ret Paladins only getting 4% hit from their T3).
- **[PvP]** Hex confirmed useful in PvP even against classes that can still auto-attack through it (deals half damage while silenced); combo Earth Shock -> Hex -> Earth Shock creates an extended lockout window from casting/healing, useful even against melee classes to buy time for another shock or Chain Lightning — [[d:shaman#1418338864245903492]].
- **[PvP, Resto]** Resto Shaman PvP described by some as unrewarding/boring post-Purge-nerf ("half of my value... flushed away... just a worse healer than every other class that can heal") since it lost peel/CC utility without gaining offense — [[d:shaman#1408662662027546790]]. Others still find Resto Shaman viable in BG healing (citing faction imbalance in healer availability) but note gearing is awkward since good mail healing gear is scarce and most mail items roll physical or spell-damage stats rather than a healing variant — [[d:shaman#1420649996801544302]].
- **[PvP]** Grounding Totem's cooldown increased by ~33% as part of the same PvP-nerf wave as the Purge CD — cited alongside Purge as removing key Shaman defensive tools while Paladins retained multiple stuns, an invulnerability, and an instant-cast large heal — [[d:shaman#1406007562654056499]].
