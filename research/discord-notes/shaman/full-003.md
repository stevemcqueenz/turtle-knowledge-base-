## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[tank]** Shaman tanks are fully viable/strong; best-in-game threat, but lack a dedicated defensive cooldown and have an itemization quirk (T2.5 is the only tank-specific tier set and is "never removed once obtained") — [[d:shaman#1437848458961617027]] (Not Normal).
- **[tank]** peter_hammer's detailed take: tank shaman is competitive with traditional tanks in dungeons, can off-tank or main-tank endgame; AoE tanking relies on fire totems/talents (resistible) unlike other tanks' unresistable threat, and starts to show gaps at endgame unless investing in spellpower like a paladin. Low-variance damage intake from high armor + innate DR from Rockbiter Weapon, which (unlike other tanks' stance) does not sacrifice damage/threat. Two tank playstyles: 2H stacking agility (dodge) + crit (threat), or sword-and-board (parry/block/str). High talent overlap with enhance DPS for a quick spec-swap — [[d:shaman#1437849217853952142]].
- **[tank]** Threat comparison vs other tanks: shaman ~equal to or slightly better than paladin on single target, paladin better on AoE (Consecration); shaman never feels "tankier" than other options until near avoidance cap; nowhere close to druid's raw tank damage/threat — [[d:shaman#1439095173077532774]], [[d:shaman#1439096067567255613]], [[d:shaman#1439095869595979876]] (poto).
- **[tank]** Two distinct tank-DPS archetypes discussed: "Bloodlust" (2H enhance-style, strike-based threat) vs "Shock" (spellpower-leaning, AoE-heavy via shocks/totems/relic); ele/shock tank has much more AoE threat at the cost of being squishier and needs to balance ele gear with mitigation — [[d:shaman#1439653466082643989]], [[d:shaman#1439653621745844327]] (peter_hammer).
- **[tank]** Low skill-ceiling critique: without defensive cooldowns, tank shaman gameplay is largely "drop Stoneclaw, shock on CD, tab-target"; survivability swings hugely between good/bad players mainly via shield choice (bad shield = ~1000 less armor / ~700 less health than a DPS in the group) — [[d:shaman#1438949471433130045]], [[d:shaman#1438949480664924321]], [[d:shaman#1438949610176512091]].
- **[elemental]** Ele shaman seen by several raiders as the spec with least unique raid-comp value (no unique buff other than enabling Windfury Totem so Enhance can run Improved Strength of Earth/Agi totems) — [[d:shaman#1438881918321168447]], [[d:shaman#1438892645811486842]] (Frenchie/Squid: "correct reason" to bring Ele). Resto can also provide WFT. Contrast: Nature Ele is "incredibly good" on any AoE-heavy fight — [[d:shaman#1438929134058340484]].
- **[elemental/fire]** Fire Ele controversy (2025-11-12): Fire spec (Elemental Mastery + trinket + Flame Shock + spam Molten Blast) is a "one-button" rotation that matches or beats Nature Ele ST DPS with worse gear (peter_hammer sim, same 12/15 Naxx bosses) — [[d:shaman#1438197573369466981]], [[d:shaman#1438198841055772784]]. Consensus of several strong players: needs a real nerf, not cosmetic (10-15%+), because the issue is decision-density not just damage output ("one presses flame shock 30 times followed by 443 molten blasts" vs deep-ele's active CD/dot management) — [[d:shaman#1438198072177070162]], [[d:shaman#1438194435702788177]], [[d:shaman#1438194724304322581]]. Fire Ele's AoE (Flame Shock spread + Molten Blast cleave) is actually strong; Nature Ele is only better at *uncapped* AoE, which is less valuable — [[d:shaman#1438194699885346897]]. Fire Ele's original design intent was to be a *situational tool* for nature-immune fights inside a normal Ele rotation, not a standalone spec — [[d:shaman#1438190856912769126]], [[d:shaman#1438190928836821082]]. Dissent (Windshade): fire is useless in 3/7 current raids (nature-immune-free ones don't need it either), shouldn't be nerfed for the ~raid-clearing minority, no official nerf source exists — [[d:shaman#1438187474839928984]], [[d:shaman#1438191164678471854]].
- **[elemental]** Deep/pure Elemental is a poor open-world/leveling spec (low sustain without high crit for Clearcasting); a hybrid "frosthance" (enhance-leaning, using Ele gear) is more efficient for open-world farming/questing even at level 60 — [[d:shaman#1438539247253717044]] (peter_hammer), [[d:shaman#1438535217458446376]]. Even in raids, deep Ele mana is "a struggle in early phases" — [[d:shaman#1438544229872828610]] (Ciftar).
- **[pvp]** Resto is mostly weak/unused in PvP outside niche duel value from Water Shield; nobody plays "deep resto" or uses Spirit Link in PvP; arena resto is rare even in 3s/5s — [[d:shaman#1437869746241536040]], [[d:shaman#1437869949799370792]], [[d:shaman#1437870661157650572]] (Zestitcha).
- **[pvp]** Enhance without a "FAP" (weapon proc window?) is not considered strong in PvP because of perma-slow/root uptime; majority of PvP shamans reportedly roll Elemental — [[d:shaman#1437798459611676713]] (Ciftar).
- **[pvp]** Enhance vs Feral Druid: close/toss-up 1v1 depending on gear/skill; a geared feral can beat enhance, but a bear needs a huge gear deficit to be near-one-shot; Enhance vs Resto Shaman for a druid: resto favored to outlast via Water Shield in duels — [[d:shaman#1437867530462040317]], [[d:shaman#1437868344748277953]] (Zaaparou), [[d:shaman#1437866282186838167]] (Zestitcha: resto wins, other 2 specs tough for druid).
- **[pvp]** Nandieb's 1v1 PvP tier list (shaman perspective, hardest→easiest): Warlock, Rogue, Priest, Druid, Mage, Paladin, Warrior, Hunter — warlock/rogue "can feel impossible" at equal gear, priest/druid/mage ~50/50, pala/war/hunter ~80/20 in shaman's favor; shamans considered "too tanky for hunters" on TWoW (contra vanilla assumption) — [[d:shaman#1440035968185798686]], [[d:shaman#1440037618287906999]], [[d:shaman#1440040077043568721]]; Squid would move Druid above Priest in difficulty — [[d:shaman#1440040340471156987]].

## Leveling
- **[enhance]** Canonical leveling progression (Holidae, corroborated by Ciftar/billienomates): level 30 gives Stormstrike (same level as Windfury); level 33 gives 3/3 Elemental Weapons, making Frostbrand procs on a Frost-Shocked target an automatic crit (Lightning Strike and Stormstrike can also proc Frostbrand); level 44 gives Elemental Focus, where each of those crit-Frostbrand procs grants 2 stacks of Clearcasting (60% spell cost reduction) — "literally all your mana issues disappear" — [[d:shaman#1437957602431729715]], [[d:shaman#1437941969765793803]], [[d:shaman#1437942054964560063]].
- **[enhance]** Enhance has real mana problems until the Elemental Weapons/Clearcasting setup online (~33-44); use Rank 1 Frost Shock to save mana until Elemental Focus, since Frostbrand proc damage doesn't scale with the shock's rank — [[d:shaman#1437946685006282873]], [[d:shaman#1438203307020783798]] (Holidae).
- **[leveling]** Two-hander is generally faster while leveling; sword-and-board (1H+shield) is "just as valid," only slightly slower but much tankier, and becomes competitive once you have Totem of the Tides or a Frostbrand build — [[d:shaman#1438203789080264724]], [[d:shaman#1439720484106145904]] (chincer). Rockbiter + Lightning Strike + keeping Lightning Shield up is the efficient early-level rotation; use mana to self-heal between mobs rather than other spells, whose mana efficiency for damage "is pretty poor" — [[d:shaman#1439724223365910679]] (Porkins), confirmed [[d:shaman#1439771465959604254]] (Not Normal).
- **[leveling]** Melee-leveling alternative: put 11 points into Elemental first (for Clearcasting, "actually the best talent for leveling as a melee shaman"), then switch to Enhance with 1H+shield for questing — [[d:shaman#1438526676588953753]] (Ciftar). Pure caster-Ele leveling (elemental focus first at low levels, e.g. `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/shaman?points=FADYI-F-`) is criticized as delaying what makes enhance work if the goal is to level as enhance — [[d:shaman#1437945342887723129]], [[d:shaman#1437946183136837694]] (Holidae).
- **[tank leveling]** As a leveling tank, stay in Enhance until Spirit Shields talent; even then, consider grabbing Elemental Weapons + Stormstrike for the 10% damage reduction — [[d:shaman#1437946515438964996]] (billienomates); it's "rough to not have Spirit Shields by 26" given custom-content mob damage — [[d:shaman#1437948072637366412]].
- **[leveling gear]** Recommended leveling pickups: Isha Awak questline shield (best pre-dungeon shield at low level); a shaman-exclusive weapon quest in Stonetalon; trinkets from Mulgore and Ratchet; blue ring from the Lt. Alverod questline; Helm of Orgrimmar at 25; dungeon drops Leggings of the Fang and Black Defias armor (Deadmines), Kodo Hide set (Crossroads vendor) — [[d:shaman#1439722584261394474]], [[d:shaman#1439724019657080924]], [[d:shaman#1439722476396478497]] (chincer). Efficient early quest hubs: Silverpine (pre-SFK) and Stonetalon (one questline leads into BFD, given by the water elemental in the horde town) — [[d:shaman#1439719424511840306]], [[d:shaman#1439738889421197503]].
- **[leveling gap]** Tank shoulder gear gap: nothing good between "Herod's" shoulders and higher without a dungeon; alternatives are the Abyssal shoulders from a Silithus questline or item from BRD (`https://database.turtle-wow.org/?item=22212`) — [[d:shaman#1439775088970043472]], [[d:shaman#1439778140946694345]], [[d:shaman#1439778973012983920]].
- **[resto leveling]** Resto weapon while leveling: use anything with int/spirit/+healing power; 2H mace is fine at low level — [[d:shaman#1440060029947351061]] (Kekkonen/others).

## Rotation: single target
- **[tank]** Pull with Chain Lightning, then Earth Shock before any DPS should hit the target; prefer Earth Shock over Flame Shock for pull/tank threat until Stormstrike buffs all shocks (currently only Nature damage) — Earth Shock currently gets the extra 25% from that talent — [[d:shaman#1438946994407342223]], [[d:shaman#1438947108383228075]] (billienomates). Molten Blast > Chain Lightning is good opening burst threat — [[d:shaman#1438950665572450448]] (Holidae).
- **[spellhance/tank]** Shock scaling: Earth Shock gains ~43% from Spellpower and ~10% from Attack Power — [[d:shaman#1439691262822191156]] (Ciftar). Recommended spellhance rank usage: max rank Frost Shock and Earth Shock, but only ranks 5-6 of Flame Shock (not max rank) — [[d:shaman#1439694621902114947]] (Squid).
- **[enhance]** Lightning Strike's real damage payoff comes from the "empowered shield" effect (procs while Lightning Shield is up), which "hits VERY hard"; base ranks scale roughly rank1 ~20% weapon dmg, up to rank3 ~60-63% weapon dmg + ~21-25% nature dmg (values approximate per two posters) — [[d:shaman#1439300057945083944]], [[d:shaman#1439301172283445319]], [[d:shaman#1439309918795595970]]. Water Shield also lets Lightning Strike proc Windfury/Frostbrand — [[d:shaman#1439316432893644810]].
- **[enhance]** Weapon skill matters a lot for enhance because auto-attacks and Windfury procs are "white" damage (can glance/miss/dodge/parry) while ability damage (Stormstrike, Earth Shock, etc.) is "yellow" and can't glance; white crit cap ≈ 1 − glance% − miss% − dodge% − parry% (and block if applicable), mainly relevant vs level-63 (raid boss) targets — [[d:shaman#1438947759368573068]], [[d:shaman#1438947443533414491]], [[d:shaman#1438947965623341077]] (Squid/Not Normal).
- **[enhance]** The "cap crit at 33% because of Flurry uptime" rule of thumb is misleading — Flurry uptime doesn't meaningfully devalue additional crit; per Not Normal's spreadsheet, crit is roughly equal-or-better than haste up to the crit cap for enhance — [[d:shaman#1438941061954146385]], [[d:shaman#1438942121821474868]]. Practical flurry breakpoint: ~80% Flurry uptime at 30% crit with diminishing returns beyond; agi is a low-priority stat for enhance once you have decent AP gear (not pre-raid blues) — [[d:shaman#1438927575450779751]], [[d:shaman#1438928242206838804]], [[d:shaman#1438928673649590394]] (Zhafu).
- **[enhance stats]** "For enhance, exactly 0 spellpower value. Crit > all, then AP/str" — [[d:shaman#1438299413129986088]] (Izmo).
- **[weapon speed]** Weapon base damage matters much less than weapon speed for converting Attack Power into damage; a "slow" weapon is effectively just a higher-damage weapon, not inherently better for threat/DPS by itself — [[d:shaman#1438588966705041429]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1438562454778019964]] (Zykes).
- **[frostbrand]** Frostbrand proc math confirmed by in-game testing: base ~9 procs-per-minute (PPM) scaled by weapon speed (not a flat 9/min cap); Elemental Weapons' proc-chance bonus is confirmed additive (flat +25 percentage points), not multiplicative — tested with Frostbrand in MC — [[d:shaman#1438385295858073711]], [[d:shaman#1438399840257183754]] (Ciftar, "100% additive"). Tested proc-with-elemental-weapons chances: Coldrage Dagger 47.5%, Cobalt Crusher 73%, Sulfuras 80.5% — Coldrage called "abysmal, not even worth getting" for Frostbrand — [[d:shaman#1438411455669075988]], [[d:shaman#1438418828584423434]]. Slower/heavier weapons get more Frostbrand value overall since each ability use has a higher per-hit proc chance — [[d:shaman#1438384047616168067]], [[d:shaman#1438385932519739568]] ("30").
- **[gear]** Frost weapon procs (e.g. weapon-innate frost effects) are largely inconsequential compared to Frostbrand's own proc volume; frost oil vs Frostbrand weapon stacking is unresolved/unclear (question raised, not answered) — [[d:shaman#1438378975427825706]], [[d:shaman#1438375729334063254]] (Dwi, open question).

- **[elemental/fire]** Fire Ele's AoE plays via cycling Flame Shock across multiple targets and refreshing with Molten Blast ("tab corruption"-style multi-dotting), giving it strong cleave; Nature Ele is only better for truly uncapped/large-pack AoE — [[d:shaman#1438195226631082028]], [[d:shaman#1438194699885346897]].

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[resto]** Stat priority rule of thumb (no fixed BiS list exists per peter_hammer, since pre-raid BiS "only matters for 2-3 weeks after raids open"): **+healing > crit > haste > int/mp5** — [[d:shaman#1439904903098273792]], [[d:shaman#1439903970117161031]].
- **[elemental]** Ele shock scaling: Earth Shock ≈ 43% SP / 10% AP — [[d:shaman#1439691262822191156]]. Nandieb's tested MC gear: 11% hit, 16% crit (26% effective on LB/CL with talents) called "enough to perform very well" and #1 DPS in a pug MC run, contra advice to under-prioritize hit at this gear phase (choice is limited before more raid tiers open) — [[d:shaman#1439942417624207372]], [[d:shaman#1439943845264490556]].
- **[tank]** Water Shield does not scale with spellpower at all (contra some players' hopes); Lightning Shield ("empowered" Lightning Strike) has roughly a 25% AP coefficient vs ~28% SP coefficient, but AP is much more available on gear, making it effectively AP-favored despite the close nominal coefficients — [[d:shaman#1438361983530438870]], [[d:shaman#1438362203626537051]] (Not Normal). Poto initially thought Lightning Shield leaned SP; corrected by Not Normal after checking values — [[d:shaman#1438291240134381648]] vs [[d:shaman#1438361693586722918]].
- **[tank EHP vs threat]** Weapon math example (AV Axe vs Lava Shard Axe): swapping cost ~0.6-0.7% DPS/TPS to gain ~1.7% EHP — judged a "decent tradeoff" by the tester — [[d:shaman#1438714741206945822]] (Zeruel, via Not Normal's spreadsheet).
- **[resto]** Ancestral Knowledge (5% more int) vs Improved Water Shield (+9 mp5) for the last few points in a 31/0/14 Ele/Resto split: roughly equivalent value over a ~2 minute fight (~225 mana each), amounting to "one extra Lightning Bolt at best" — both called fairly inconsequential for Ele — [[d:shaman#1438694180603039754]], [[d:shaman#1438710796027822151]], [[d:shaman#1438712425586098216]] (Not Normal).
- **[general]** Resource: community-maintained enhance/tank gear-and-DPS planner spreadsheet (Not Normal, data sheets by Squid): `https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1hDvUiFQt1sbxlxu3l5xlhON3NwcTtb0z2R5NzyNW2g8/edit` — [[d:shaman#1438589684434337903]]. General all-class stat-weight spreadsheet also referenced: `https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1gBYi4RsHW6Ddjx06qQEUZPWmXAQRX3UvBH0uwLOugdA/edit` — [[d:shaman#1441419802567774361]] (Dwi).
- **[elemental]** Spell penetration value scales *up* with spellpower (it's a percentage-based effect, not flat): community sim quoted gives ~1.5 SP-equivalent value per point of spell pen at 100 SP, ~1.8 at 250 SP, ~2.3 at 500 SP, ~2.9 at 750 SP, ~3.4 at 1000 SP — [[d:shaman#1442768727509831710]], [[d:shaman#1442768182065500161]]. Independent math check: reducing a boss's 15 Nature Resist to 0 via 15 spell pen was calculated as roughly a 4% damage increase — [[d:shaman#1442745361339912274]]. Rougher in-thread estimates before that sim (Ciftar): ~1.5 SP per 1 spell pen generally, or ~3-4 SP per 1 pen specifically for Nature Ele — [[d:shaman#1442568359060176986]], [[d:shaman#1442571539345051818]].
- **[elemental]** Spell hit: no way to get spell hit from consumables or enchants — only from gear (plus 3% from a talent, and a 1% buff from a Druid available from a later phase); missing a cast wastes 100% of its mana and can't crit or proc Clearcasting, so hit has real mana-efficiency value beyond raw throughput — [[d:shaman#1441101960404275331]] (Dwi). Personal gearing rule of thumb used by one Ele theorycrafter: don't sacrifice more than ~20 spell power on a single item for 1% hit — [[d:shaman#1441101960404275331]]. Target ~16% hit from BWL (phase 4) onward; ran with only 10-11% hit through MC without sacrificing too much — [[d:shaman#1441101960404275331]].
- **[enhance]** Weapon skill (the +5 quest reward from mace/axe weapon-skill quests) is "free threat" and worth getting; a shaman tank running full T2.5+4pc-T3 doesn't need to itemize further for it — [[d:shaman#1442417974555181108]], [[d:shaman#1442418309885857804]], [[d:shaman#1442424447188598804]]. Weapon skill reduces how often *you* get parried by the enemy (not your own parry chance) — [[d:shaman#1442428426895233064]]. Extra weapon skill keeps adding white damage and hit up to 320 skill, letting you drop hit itemization elsewhere once soft-capped — [[d:shaman#1443229206917152840]]. Diminishing returns near the 315 white-hit-cap are small: going from ~312-313 to 315 skill only moves glancing-blow damage from ~91%→95%, not worth sacrificing stats (e.g. low-stat Carapace Hand Guards) for — [[d:shaman#1442938123129458749]], [[d:shaman#1442939885735514376]].
- **[weapon speed / frostbrand]** Slower weapons (1H or 2H) generate more total Frostbrand procs than faster ones because Frostbrand's proc chance is normalized to weapon speed (PPM), and Stormstrike/Lightning Strike get used the same number of times regardless of weapon speed, so their proc chance is simply higher on a slow weapon. Worked example with 3/3 Elemental Weapons: a 3.6s weapon nets ~24.4 total FB procs/min vs ~23.8 for a 2.6s weapon (~2% more) — [[d:shaman#1442929254353338518]], [[d:shaman#1442929912703881276]], [[d:shaman#1442930596056928308]], [[d:shaman#1442932009453223966]] (Not Normal).

## Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses
- **[tank, early]** Pre-Kara10 tank pickups (poto): T1 Enhance set is an easy early upgrade; Molten Core off-tanking is easier than Kara10 if you can avoid assignments like Golemagg; Shoulders of True Flight (MC trash) and Blackstone Crown (Baron Kazum, Silithus) are BiS shoulders/helm until AQ; the belt from Dark Reaver is one of the best tanking belts — [[d:shaman#1437921259022450748]], [[d:shaman#1437921483530961021]], [[d:shaman#1437921547904880821]], [[d:shaman#1437921782630973480]]. Additional tank pickups (Zeruel): a stam+armor+defense neck in Hateforge, and the shield off Ragnaros as best-available shield "for now" — [[d:shaman#1437929669440573633]], [[d:shaman#1437929752165089373]].
- **[tank]** Chest debate: SGC vs the K10 chest are close overall; K10 chest's extra AP + armor pen judged worth more than SGC's extra agility/crit — [[d:shaman#1437860342729543812]], [[d:shaman#1437871231507365888]], [[d:shaman#1437871661650022531]].
- **[weapon]** DTC (a rare 3-chance/week drop) is BiS until Axe of C'thun unless you get Sulfuras; it takes Windfury imbue like any weapon; extremely low drop rate ("2 months, zero drops") and often lost to hunters when nobody else needs it — [[d:shaman#1437916576035639469]], [[d:shaman#1437917107621728327]], [[d:shaman#1437917375361061114]], [[d:shaman#1437917803502899260]].
- **[tank/enh]** Hand of Ragnaros vs the 4-Horsemen mace ("Maul"): Rag's fire proc carries via high white damage in MC/BWL/Ony (~5% of total damage if the boss isn't fire-immune/resistant), but 3 dragons + Nefarian + Vael in BWL are fire-immune, so the general call is "Maul in MC/BWL/Ony, Rag everywhere else" — [[d:shaman#1438288855370305718]], [[d:shaman#1438290209790754869]], [[d:shaman#1438306231260545075]], [[d:shaman#1438308285966061589]]. Sulfuras vs Redeemed Crusader mace math out roughly equal on paper (26 AP vs 1% haste + 40 SP), but Sulfuras's fire proc was judged the stronger overall package outside of fire-immune fights — [[d:shaman#1438301240881709158]], [[d:shaman#1438301324557947086]], [[d:shaman#1438301385467629754]], [[d:shaman#1438301567945150565]].
- **[tank]** Hand of Ed(ward) is repeatedly called the best/cheapest tanking weapon: instant Chain Lightning/Lightning Bolt/Healing Wave give unmatched threat + utility (Ciftar proc rate observed ~4-6/min), and by simulation for spellhance it's only beaten by Hand of Ragnaros and Naxx-tier weapons — [[d:shaman#1439263865232691341]], [[d:shaman#1439268833625116893]], [[d:shaman#1439276059429310495]], [[d:shaman#1442806393009737801]]. It's good for tanking dungeons/10-20-mans, gives big totem burst threat after a Chain Lightning pull to enable chain-pulling, and its Healing Wave can double as an emergency self-heal — [[d:shaman#1442803738925465762]], [[d:shaman#1442804076680056912]], [[d:shaman#1442808808425525372]], [[d:shaman#1442809008347021433]]. Recommended to just own one regardless of gear level, similar to BoED — [[d:shaman#1442805016347021372]], [[d:shaman#1442805168201535551]].
- **[elemental]** Nature Ele MC gear path (Nandieb, self-reported #1 DPS in a pug MC run at 350 SP/11% hit/16% crit): farm the T0.5 set (especially waist/legs/feet for hit, since MC upgrades there are minor); the T0.5 chain unlocks the ring from Valthalak (SP+hit) and the offhand from Jarien & Sothos (hit+crit), both usable well into BWL. Skip the final T0.5 quest step (head/chest aren't needed for raiding) and use Spellweaver's Turban or Crown of Shattering (Kara Crypt) plus BOE Harness of the High Thane / Spellwoven Nobility Drape instead — [[d:shaman#1439942417624207372]], [[d:shaman#1439943097810288750]], [[d:shaman#1439943513633718302]], [[d:shaman#1439944652601167873]], [[d:shaman#1439944908570890351]].
- **[general]** No fixed pre-raid BiS list is actively maintained for most specs; peter_hammer: a fixed list "only matters for 2-3 weeks after raids open," and for resto the durable rule is a stat-priority ordering (+healing > crit > haste > int/mp5), not a fixed item list — [[d:shaman#1439903970117161031]], [[d:shaman#1439904903098273792]]. An Elemental BiS spreadsheet is community-maintained by Dwi (updated through AQ) — [[d:shaman#1440007394850639942]] — but taking its literal #1 pick in every slot for phases 4-5 would total ~16% hit from gear alone, more than needed on top of 3% talent + 1% Druid buff — [[d:shaman#1441032635525890058]], [[d:shaman#1441096624838545459]].
- **[tank]** Trinket progression (Ambershire): Mark of the Chosen (50 AP, 1% crit, 1% dodge, 250 mana, 300 health) has near-permanent uptime through most 5-mans but falls off once you hit MC (not enough hits to sustain it) — [[d:shaman#1441082023791034558]], [[d:shaman#1441082304381321298]], [[d:shaman#1441082397994258512]]. Hatereaver Cog (Hateforge) is far stronger than Tyranny and, alongside Kara10's Araxna's Husk ("spider husk"), forms the two best non-epic tank trinkets going into Kara10 — [[d:shaman#1441106997675036733]], [[d:shaman#1441107116684214456]], [[d:shaman#1441107746027077823]]. Pendant of the Faceless is called the best trinket overall but is gated behind a dungeon's final boss — [[d:shaman#1441107752687632486]], [[d:shaman#1441107942186418300]].
- **[tank]** Endgame tank gearing (Squid, detailed answer to an AP-vs-SP tank question): stick to tier sets — 5pc T2.5 + 4pc Enh T3 (both set bonuses are strong for tanking); Cloak of the Rook-Guard is BiS cloak in either build; Natural Alignment Crystal is the best on-pull threat trinket; with 2.5+T3 your threat is already massive, so fill the rest with stam/mitigation unless you're deliberately trying to out-threat a Feral tank — [[d:shaman#1440747206146523237]], [[d:shaman#1440747347574259803]], [[d:shaman#1440747687619330201]], [[d:shaman#1440747798092841101]]. AP-vs-SP weapon choice is hard to quantify (losing weapon damage and swing speed simultaneously); one comparison found Forgotten Raven's Mallet (SP) slightly ahead of the faster Al'Kazeth (AP) under a specific set bonus — [[d:shaman#1440759341073301587]], [[d:shaman#1440759388871856261]]. billienomates: better to stack SP at *lower* gear levels since the weapon-dps gap to top options is smaller early; bigger Earth Shocks mean bigger threat; true outliers like Deathbringer are "so obscene" they override the comparison entirely — [[d:shaman#1440761385226338415]], [[d:shaman#1440761921107263591]].
- **[tank]** A "Bronze Dragonscale" set exists in the item database, clearly designed for shaman tank and reportedly quite good for early raid tiers, but is not actually obtainable in the live game — [[d:shaman#1442190331800981659]], [[d:shaman#1442190887785463999]].
- **[tank stat priority]** Pre-AQ40, prioritize effective health (stamina, armor) over defense/avoidance enchants — you can't realistically reach meaningful defense/avoidance thresholds before AQ40-tier gear anyway; get the best-DPS one-hander available and don't worry about its defensive stats — [[d:shaman#1442269290349854802]], [[d:shaman#1442269646202863656]], [[d:shaman#1442272596711182626]]. Str is better than Agi for pure threat once established/well-geared, but Agi is "arguably more valuable as a whole" early on since it covers both threat (crit) and mitigation (dodge) simultaneously — [[d:shaman#1442281609360310312]], [[d:shaman#1442282680380297296]], [[d:shaman#1442283953145909289]], [[d:shaman#1442284931152744598]] (billienomates/Not Normal). Concrete example: ~12 extra Strength was judged not worth trading away ~100 armor / 1% dodge / ~90 health (i.e. don't drop a BiS piece like Shoulders of True Flight, which has 0 Strength, for a Strength-heavy alternative) — [[d:shaman#1442284792073687085]]. General tank enchant answer: "stamina at any point" — [[d:shaman#1442223290214125568]].
- **[tank reality check]** A practicing 10/20-man tank (Not Normal) reports that tanking with mostly DPS mail gear + a 3k-armor Naxx trash shield gives great threat but leaves the tank "pretty squishy"; a properly full-mitigation-geared shaman tank should not feel squishy at all in Kara10 — survivability is almost entirely a function of gearing choices rather than the class being inherently fragile — [[d:shaman#1442285448004239370]], [[d:shaman#1442285669815812216]], [[d:shaman#1442285825118306488]], [[d:shaman#1442285942575726703]].
- **[tank]** Defense cap (440) is hard to reach before Naxx/Kara40 gear and is mainly useful for a handful of specific high-threshold fights (the Chess Knight, Loatheb, Sapphiron); avoidance capping is comparatively easier (mostly pre-Naxx gear) but still needs a couple of hard-to-replace pieces (e.g. Cloak of the Rook-Guard, King's Edict) and no good mail bracers exist outside tier gear — [[d:shaman#1442067706265206915]], [[d:shaman#1442068133484560394]], [[d:shaman#1442068380915077171]], [[d:shaman#1442068810516398175]], [[d:shaman#1442077747076661256]]. Once you have T2.5 + 4pc T3 Enh (Earth Shock crit bonus), you're gear-set for Kara40 tanking — [[d:shaman#1442066454840082595]] (Eluzia, a top K40 tank). A 101%-avoidance set is achievable but judged not worth the effort outside a novelty solo-farming set — [[d:shaman#1442065187820666961]].

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[leveling]** Leveling imbue windows per the pinned community guide: Rockbiter levels 1-32, Windfury ~32-43, Frostbrand ~43-60 (once Elemental Focus is up) — [[d:shaman#1441720404099203092]] (Kekkonen). Practical mana management before Elemental Weapons: keep Water Shield up at all times outside dungeons and fish for Windfury procs until Elemental Weapons + Stormstrike are online, then switch to Frostbrand (or keep using WF until Elemental Focus) — [[d:shaman#1441721814265888810]] (billienomates).
- **[leveling]** Rockbiter vs Flametongue for solo leveling: Not Normal recommends Rockbiter almost always, because Lightning Strike causes Lightning Shield to scale off Attack Power, giving RB's AP a bigger payoff than FT's flat spell damage; before Lightning Strike is learned (roughly 10-20) it barely matters which you use — [[d:shaman#1441543132138442762]], [[d:shaman#1441543213524717679]], [[d:shaman#1441543335947927584]], [[d:shaman#1441543530823553178]]. Rockbiter's damage is roughly 4 AP-equivalent per point (~2.4-3.6 dps in the low 20s); Flametongue ignores armor while Rockbiter doesn't, complicating direct comparison — [[d:shaman#1441556921319686245]], [[d:shaman#1441557660729082008]].
- **[endgame]** Windfury vs Rockbiter at endgame: Windfury does more overall damage and scales better with gear; Rockbiter's damage is a flat addition so it's relatively stronger early but is overtaken once geared. Rockbiter is also reported to generate disproportionately high threat relative to its ~35% bonus (tank pulling aggro off good tanks using it on fights like C'thun or Kara40's last-boss crystals) — [[d:shaman#1441709939843268628]], [[d:shaman#1441711428372529193]], [[d:shaman#1441711723852599396]], [[d:shaman#1441723135383572642]].
- **[frostbrand]** See Rotation/Stats sections above for the detailed Frostbrand PPM math, the confirmed +25%-additive Elemental Weapons proc bonus, and slow-weapon-favors-Frostbrand mechanics.
- **[tank]** Weapon imbue on a nature-damage-on-hit proc weapon (e.g. certain Naxx trash maces) "eats" the Stormstrike self-buff, weakening subsequent Earth Shocks — a real threat-rotation trap for tanks; historically Thunderfury and Serpent Sting had the same interaction in vanilla — [[d:shaman#1442481023643422740]], [[d:shaman#1442481255781367978]], [[d:shaman#1442481678714146977]], [[d:shaman#1442481991026217011]].
- **[vampirism]** Lifesteal/Vampirism enchants are weak for shaman overall because a lot of shaman AoE damage comes from totems, which don't proc vamp; its main use is niche AoE-farming builds (paired with the Engineering shield or a "Skullflame"-type item) — [[d:shaman#1442206353388208240]], [[d:shaman#1442207016134246592]], [[d:shaman#1442226949874389053]]. A detailed vamp-tank gear list was theorycrafted (Grail trinket + 2x vamp ring + Gilneas boss neck ≈ 11% vamp, +4% from boots/wrist/shoulder enchants, +3% from K10 cloth gloves/belt/cape) for a for-fun AoE farming build, referencing a known video of a vamp-geared shaman pulling 50 mobs in Stratholme — [[d:shaman#1442466489457184778]], [[d:shaman#1442472926367449140]], [[d:shaman#1442473166592020585]], [[d:shaman#1442475363245494272]].

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[general]** Windfury Totem now functions as an "aura" rather than a classic burst proc, so "totem twisting" is no longer feasible or desired — shamans have their own steady rotation instead — [[d:shaman#1440812785524015145]], [[d:shaman#1440812860883075082]], [[d:shaman#1440812914062790686]] (poto). A shaman using a personal weapon imbue gets *no* benefit from their own Windfury Totem (imbues and totems never stack) — [[d:shaman#1440812607958159360]], [[d:shaman#1440812661045592225]].
- **[5-man tank]** In dungeons, a tank shaman's mana mostly goes to fire AoE totems (Searing/Magma/Fire Nova) + Stoneclaw; it's "not realistic" to also expect a full Windfury+Strength-of-Earth set for the group — typical dungeon totem loadout is AoE totem, Tremor, Grounding, and dispel as needed; Windfury is skipped without a Paladin's Salvation to offset the aggro risk, and Mana Spring isn't worth it on short pulls — [[d:shaman#1440813470428696638]], [[d:shaman#1440814035397382195]], [[d:shaman#1440814144986153093]], [[d:shaman#1440814285579358359]], [[d:shaman#1440814500998545611]]. In full raids, by contrast, AoE totems are almost never dropped — [[d:shaman#1440813704496283648]].
- **[stats]** Grace of Air numbers: 20 Agi = 1% dodge + 1% crit; max-rank Grace of Air totem = 77 Agi (96 with Enhancing Totems talent, ~110 with Ancestral Knowledge + Kings active) → ~5.5% crit and ~5.5% dodge from the totem alone when fully buffed; Agi is also worth roughly 1 armor per point (~110 armor from a maxed totem) — [[d:shaman#1440839262915530843]], [[d:shaman#1440839300622057624]], [[d:shaman#1440840756666302554]].
- **[totems]** Totem of Rage adds a flat +30ish guaranteed damage every 5s, which is also added to every Flame Shock DoT tick and modified by %-damage talents (Stormstrike, Concussion, etc.) — considered "busted for Flame Shock" and BiS for that spell over Totem of the Stonebreaker; if only carrying one totem, use Stonebreaker, otherwise macro-swap relics per spell (Not Normal, Zhafu, Ciftar) — [[d:shaman#1440585988945477672]], [[d:shaman#1440647856359997541]], [[d:shaman#1440648438659285023]], [[d:shaman#1441473167754264576]], [[d:shaman#1441473247853150250]]. Totem of Crackling Thunder was nerfed at some point in the past but is now considered a good starter enhance DPS totem and, alongside Totem of Rage, forms a BiS combo for enhance on Ambershire — [[d:shaman#1440638099213521008]], [[d:shaman#1441455640425005180]], [[d:shaman#1441469099233509548]]. If forced to pick just one totem for physhance, Stormbreaker/Stonebreaker was named the single best option — [[d:shaman#1441473719154376784]].
- **[tank]** Fire Nova + Sapper Charge + Stoneclaw Totem together make a strong snap-AoE-threat combo at pull; untalented Fire Nova needs 4s to place+detonate, while the talented (faster) version is strongly preferred in 5-mans — [[d:shaman#1438960736440156210]], [[d:shaman#1438960929516814406]], [[d:shaman#1438961125449400411]]. Fire Nova + Totemic Recall lets you get a mana refund shortly after the totem's eruption if timed right (janky but useful when mana-limited) — [[d:shaman#1442094408659898428]], [[d:shaman#1442095379847053422]], [[d:shaman#1442095469403705344]], [[d:shaman#1442095594696081515]].
- **[tank threat]** Shaman-tank threat is largely damage=threat; only Earth Shock has a built-in threat modifier decoupled from its raw damage, so all other damage sources (buffed melee via Rockbiter, etc.) should not be neglected for threat generation — [[d:shaman#1442083465012187196]], [[d:shaman#1442083596943757404]], [[d:shaman#1442084075677417514]].
- **[tank/EHP]** Damage-reduction tools are gear-based, not stance-based, unlike Warrior's Defensive Stance: Rockbiter Weapon (the "tank stance") actually *increases* damage rather than reducing it, unlike other tanks' mitigation stances — a repeatedly cited structural feature of the spec (see "Spec viability" above) — [[d:shaman#1437849217853952142]].
- **[tank CD gap]** Shaman tank has no true defensive cooldown; this is a long-running, unresolved community complaint (see Contested section). Spirit Link (see 1.18.1 changes) is now the closest thing to a group-facing "oh shit" tool, giving up to 30% damage reduction spread raid-wide within 30 yards, on roughly a 60s cooldown (so two Resto shamans could keep it up permanently) — [[d:shaman#1443097465292193842]], [[d:shaman#1443097537413382287]], [[d:shaman#1443112634785337405]], [[d:shaman#1443159024572043366]]. Practical use cases: burn phases on enraging adds/bosses when the tank is near death (e.g. Huhuran's last 30%); less useful on fights where allies are expected to stay away from the tank (e.g. Twin Emperors) — [[d:shaman#1443098135030136852]], [[d:shaman#1443098263833284640]], [[d:shaman#1443116541779378226]].
- **[totem utility]** Flametongue Totem's raid contribution is small but real: measured via logs at roughly +15-20 DPS on a Rogue/Warrior and ~+30 DPS on a Paladin (from doubling their Judgement of Wisdom procs), scaling with the number of mana-melee in the group; judged the lowest-value shaman totem but still a real (not "1% and forget it") contribution — [[d:shaman#1438921706726096976]], [[d:shaman#1438921906555326527]], [[d:shaman#1438922289059074220]].
- **[raid utility]** Strength of Earth / Grace of Air totems are highly valued raid buffs: losing Windfury+Strength cost one tested Warrior sim ~200 DPS, Agi (via Grace of Air) was worth ~40 DPS to a Warrior, and Agi is especially valuable to Rogues (~"a second Windfury" in output terms) since Combat spec converts Agi into extra energy — [[d:shaman#1438933886372548689]], [[d:shaman#1438933951153439033]], [[d:shaman#1438936021621276867]], [[d:shaman#1438936744757166192]], [[d:shaman#1438937085775052811]].

## Tanking specifics
(See also Spec viability, Rotation, Stats, Gear, and Cooldowns/threat sections above for extensive tank-specific material: tank archetypes, EHP-vs-threat tradeoffs, weapon choice, totem priority, Hand of Ed, defense/avoidance caps, and the "no defensive CD" gap.)
- **[skill ceiling]** Without a real defensive cooldown, tank-shaman decision-making is mostly "drop Stoneclaw, shock on cooldown, keep tab-targeting" — the biggest swing between a good and bad shaman tank is shield choice (observed gap: nearly 1000 armor and ~700 health between a good shield and a bad one on an otherwise-similar character) — [[d:shaman#1438949471433130045]], [[d:shaman#1438949480664924321]], [[d:shaman#1438949610176512091]].
- **[pull rotation]** Pull with Chain Lightning, then Earth Shock before DPS should hit; Molten Blast > Chain Lightning is a strong opener if purchased — [[d:shaman#1438946994407342223]], [[d:shaman#1438947108383228075]], [[d:shaman#1438950665572450448]].
- **[perception]** Because almost nobody tanks shaman in top-end raids, players wrongly assume shamans can't tank 5-mans either; in practice 5-mans are argued to be where shaman tanking is at its best relative to other classes — [[d:shaman#1442067059826753536]], [[d:shaman#1442067107767652422]].
- **[gear-dependent survivability]** A properly-mitigation-geared shaman tank is not fragile; most "shaman tank is squishy" complaints trace to under-itemized shields/gear rather than a structural class weakness (see Gear section) — [[d:shaman#1442285448004239370]]–[[d:shaman#1442285942575726703]].
- **[talents]** A widely-used "for everything" tank build (Eluzia, a top Kara40 tank): `https://talents.turtle-wow.org/shaman?points=AoCYLBQDAC-FAVYALBQDIAY-`, flexible enough to even play Fire Elemental in it; takes 1 point in Improved Molten Blast (for spamming Molten Blast at range) over 3/3 Elemental Warding — [[d:shaman#1442089163020308551]], [[d:shaman#1442089380297834596]], [[d:shaman#1442089499122470912]]. Logs show little threat/DPS difference between taking Bloodlust vs Elemental Fury in a tank build; Bloodlust also buffs the whole group — [[d:shaman#1442090838308945952]], [[d:shaman#1442091068148551742]]. A more moderate/comfortable-leveling tank preference: skip 31-point Bloodlust investment, take the 10% nature/fire damage + spell crit damage talents instead for more comfortable 5-man AoE threat (crits of ~1400 on Nova, ~150 on Magma cited) — [[d:shaman#1442286285908869120]], [[d:shaman#1442286485675180042]].
- **[fresh 60]** Open/unresolved talent question: 22/29/0 with Elemental Fury (for big Earth Shock crits) vs a more basic 15/36/0 for a fresh-60 tank — no clear consensus captured in this chunk — [[d:shaman#1441044216225730680]].

## Healing specifics
- **[general]** No fixed resto stat list; durable rule of thumb: **+healing > crit > haste > int/mp5** — [[d:shaman#1439904903098273792]].
- **[leveling]** Water Shield is considered clearly weaker than Paladin's Blessing/Seal of Wisdom for mana sustain while leveling as a healer; shamans "lack a regen talent like priests/druids get" while healing throughput isn't as efficient as a paladin's — [[d:shaman#1441131112041550008]], [[d:shaman#1441127862597062746]], [[d:shaman#1441127985280454728]].
- **[endgame build]** peter_hammer endorses a "full AK [Ancestral Knowledge] and 9-charge shields" build as "the only reasonable endgame resto build"; an alternate "buffing totems" support-oriented build (`https://talents.turtle-wow.org/shaman?points=-FAQaYDAQQ-FoDoaAAYAK`) was judged only marginally useful in low-AoE dungeon scenarios, "500x worse" than standard resto for raiding — [[d:shaman#1440271994233815152]], [[d:shaman#1440272674960969839]], [[d:shaman#1440273270669578335]], [[d:shaman#1440273510814715967]], [[d:shaman#1440273711075954698]]. Complaint: Chain Heal's changes (shortened cast time) weren't matched by a scaling adjustment, so it "still scales like a 2.5s-cast spell" despite casting faster — [[d:shaman#1440272821174669363]].
- **[chain heal]** Chain Heal is not raid-group-locked: casting it on a party member prioritizes jumping to other party members first — [[d:shaman#1438669998405976155]]. A live bug report exists claiming Chain Heal's "smart heal" targeting sometimes bounces to players who've only taken ~200 damage instead of the most-injured targets — [[d:shaman#1441537114817958091]] (single source, unresolved).
- **[gear/mechanics]** Consuming a Water Shield charge via Lightning Strike does restore mana — less than a normal-cost Lightning Strike, but more than a Clearcasted one — [[d:shaman#1440433224734998539]].
- **[T1 resto "meta"]** A community claim that full T1 Resto tier is unusually mana-efficient because the set's bonus treats Chain Heal's bounces as individual spell-casts (each proccing Water Shield's mana-return talent), unlike normal Chain Heal — reported anecdotally as strong ("makes we want to go resto," "I lose to T1 with BiS Kara40 gear") but disputed by at least one other healer who reported out-healing full-T1 shamans without even full T3 — treat as contested/unverified — [[d:shaman#1441442429495410689]], [[d:shaman#1441442580532428892]], [[d:shaman#1441445431988191243]], [[d:shaman#1441445633281233118]].
- **[addons]** Puppeteer is called the most polished/standard healer raid-frame addon (click-cast with modifiers); NotGrid is a bare-bones retail-Grid clone; Luna UnitFrames is heavily customizable and shows more info but needs manual setup — [[d:shaman#1438574406921224423]], [[d:shaman#1438574610453889176]], [[d:shaman#1438574773876822117]], [[d:shaman#1438578335566401566]].
- **[healing use-case guidance]** Healing Wave is more viable while leveling/in 5-mans; raiding leans on Chain Heal, though Healing Wave retains legitimate niche uses; totems should be swapped to match group composition needs (interrupts, grounding, etc.) — [[d:shaman#1438574282350530560]].

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[MC]** Threat/mitigation: with a full raid buffed (Improved EA/Faerie Fire/Curse of Recklessness stacked), most MC bosses sit around 516 armor, dropping to 266 with a "shart" (Improved Sunder-type effect); Badge of the Swarmguard is judged not worth using outside high-armor exceptions like Patchwerk/Loatheb/Thaddius/Kruul where it's compared against Earthstrike's value over the fight — [[d:shaman#1437835634164371597]], [[d:shaman#1437835738367398080]].
- **[BWL]** The three dragons, Nefarian, and Vael are fire-immune, capping Hand of Ragnaros/fire-proc value there; recommend swapping to a pure-DPS weapon (e.g. the Maul) for that instance — [[d:shaman#1438306231260545075]], [[d:shaman#1438308285966061589]].
- **[AQ40 — Twin Emperors]** Raiders generally shouldn't stand near the tank on this fight, which limits Spirit Link's usefulness there despite its 30-yard raid-wide range — [[d:shaman#1443116541779378226]], [[d:shaman#1443127940815782019]].
- **[AQ40 — Huhuran]** A good use case for Spirit Link: burning down the tank's incoming damage during the final 30% berserk-adjacent phase if the tank is close to dying — [[d:shaman#1443098135030136852]], [[d:shaman#1443098442112045249]].
- **[Molten Giants egg fight (unnamed boss, likely Naxx-tier)]** Community reports the boss detonates eggs almost immediately after spawning them rather than allowing the intended "kill the eggs" counterplay, making the mechanic feel non-functional; consensus workaround is to just ignore/avoid the eggs and have a dedicated add-tank pull adds away from the raid rather than trying to kill eggs — [[d:shaman#1441160975125577869]], [[d:shaman#1441162097798152325]], [[d:shaman#1441169751425552446]], [[d:shaman#1441170339416641556]].
- **[Kara10]** Reported as a relatively easy tank; only the 3rd boss "trucks," but pre-potting armor resolves it — [[d:shaman#1442300283605618791]].
- **[Ragnaros]** General belief (based on old memory, not directly tested by the speaker) that Ragnaros's damage is mostly physical with a harsh fire DoT that historically needed Fire Resist gear; a modern strategy is reported to just DPS-race him without caring about FR on DPS/healers — [[d:shaman#1441134426124718090]], [[d:shaman#1441134917391089664]], [[d:shaman#1441135065286574202]] (uncertain/secondhand, flagged as such by the poster).
- **[general dungeon quirks]** Turtle custom dungeons (e.g. Stormwrought) are noted for many blind-corner pulls and enemies that can hit players through walls/around corners (e.g. spellcasters' Rain of Fire); unclear if intended or a LoS bug — [[d:shaman#1437949075692589160]], [[d:shaman#1437949199978205367]], [[d:shaman#1437949491218088099]], [[d:shaman#1437949706754986047]].

## PvP
- **[general]** Community 1v1 tier list from a shaman's perspective (hardest→easiest): Warlock, Rogue, Priest, Druid, Mage, Paladin, Warrior, Hunter — warlock/rogue can feel "impossible" at equal gear, priest/druid/mage ~50/50, pala/war/hunter ~80/20 in the shaman's favor; shamans are considered "too tanky for hunters" on Turtle contrary to vanilla assumptions — [[d:shaman#1440035968185798686]], [[d:shaman#1440037618287906999]], [[d:shaman#1440040077043568721]] (Nandieb); Squid would move Druid above Priest in difficulty — [[d:shaman#1440040340471156987]].
- **[enhance]** A good Warlock using Curse of Exhaustion + kiting/LoS is considered "kinda nasty" for enhance; needing to "fap" (burn cooldowns) at the start of a warlock fight if opening in the open, and being smart about starting distance/LoS, is recommended — [[d:shaman#1440023887705411624]], [[d:shaman#1439784444381630594]], [[d:shaman#1439784487620837478]]. Rogues are also very strong currently if they land the opener with cooldowns — [[d:shaman#1440024107071832157]]. Poison Cleanse Totem can no longer remove Blind (see 1.18.1 changes) — [[d:shaman#1440024319504683029]].
- **[racials]** Blood Fury (Orc) reworked to give both 120 AP and 60 SP with a less punishing healing-received penalty, called "the single best racial in the game, not close" for a shaman since it benefits both halves of its kit — [[d:shaman#1439185343772360704]], [[d:shaman#1439198812156465222]]. peter_hammer's overall race guidance: Orc for DPS Enh/Ele and for SP tank builds, Tauren best for tanking/healing, Trolls should be Hunters or DW Warriors — [[d:shaman#1439237420787699874]]. Blood Fury judged stronger than Berserking even for Ele in near-BiS gear per posted calculations — [[d:shaman#1439236767701139529]]. Troll is called best for arena PvP because Hex is genuinely good there; for Ele, Berserking and Elemental Mastery share the same duration/cooldown so they macro together — [[d:shaman#1440249261311787029]] (Nandieb).
- **[Ghost Wolf in PvP]** Split opinion at level 60: some consider Improved Ghost Wolf (2/2) close to essential for mobility (WSG flag running, AB flag defense/movement, general kiting) since you can't always disengage combat to mount — [[d:shaman#1442364622144540752]], [[d:shaman#1442378040276422657]], [[d:shaman#1442378382322044990]]. Others (Zestitcha) call it overrated at 60 — more a leveling tool — recommending the epic mount instead, especially as Elemental with 36-yard range attacks; conceded it's fine if it's part of a build you find fun — [[d:shaman#1442373264100950119]], [[d:shaman#1442373329917837434]], [[d:shaman#1442373575519502347]]. Ancestral Swiftness (Resto talent) makes the 3-second Ghost Wolf cast instant, useful as an emergency escape even in a non-Resto PvP build — [[d:shaman#1442374431279419453]].
- **[pvp gearing]** Arathi Basin's exalted dagger (1% crit, 40 spell power) is called the most important early PvP item, with nothing comparable until the very rare Lok'amir drop — [[d:shaman#1441118920240009447]].
- **[resto pvp]** Resto is largely unplayed competitively in PvP; nobody uses "deep resto" or Spirit Link in PvP, and arena resto is rare even in 3s/5s — [[d:shaman#1437869746241536040]], [[d:shaman#1437869949799370792]], [[d:shaman#1437870661157650572]].
- **[tank talents in pvp]** A common PvP approach for both Ele and Enh is to borrow tank talents from the Enhance tree for survivability rather than a pure DPS build — [[d:shaman#1438243044896931850]] (poto).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[imbue macros]** Fixed imbue-conditional macros allowing a bar button to check current weapon imbue before casting:
```
/cast [mhimbue:Flametongue]Flame Shock
/cast [nomhimbue]Flametongue Weapon
```
```
/cast [mhimbue:Flametongue]Frostbrand Weapon
/cast [mhimbue:Frostbrand]Frost Shock
```
— [[d:shaman#1438631938738094240]] ("Don't ping me").
- **[totems]** Nampower (a client-side .dll mod) enables `/cast totem 1` / `/cast totem 2` style macros to drop multiple totems simultaneously; requires enabling "queue instant spells" in its settings to avoid a 2-second forced-stationary delay — [[d:shaman#1439051885935071423]], [[d:shaman#1439051969963753604]], [[d:shaman#1441000984372445254]], [[d:shaman#1441008807458377738]]. Call of Elements (CoE) addon provides a totem bar and lets you build/drop full totem sets with one click, at the cost of a somewhat clunky "/cod" menu workflow for editing sets (players wish it worked like WotLK's totem bar) — [[d:shaman#1440971897922654281]], [[d:shaman#1441011079298809889]], [[d:shaman#1441011420262039582]]. Correct/maintained CoE fork: `https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/CallOfElements` — [[d:shaman#1442558996270813468]].
- **[macros]** Advanced relic/totem swap-on-cast macros using SuperCleveroids conditionals, so a totem/relic only equips right when you use the matching spell (avoiding a separate pseudo-GCD by piggybacking on the spell's own GCD), e.g. for Lightning Strike: `/run -- CastSpellByName("Lightning Strike")` / `/startattack` / `/use [nocdgcd:Lightning_Strike inrange:Lightning_Strike harm] 61292` / `/cast Lightning Strike` — considered legitimate (replicable manually, not an exploit), though it "may be something they change in the future" — [[d:shaman#1441474509122306200]], [[d:shaman#1441474645026013237]], [[d:shaman#1441475282941902939]], [[d:shaman#1441475460612755557]]. A simpler GCD-check variant: `/cast [cdgcd:"Lightning Bolt"] {"/equip TotemName"}` (swap only during an active GCD window) — [[d:shaman#1441476298777169961]].
- **[buff tracking]** For tracking Water Shield/Lightning Shield/elemental-shield uptime, recommended options are Power Auras (Mpowa) or CallOfElements; no single addon universally works, and some users report version-specific bugs — [[d:shaman#1440048593544286388]], [[d:shaman#1440050280375914581]], [[d:shaman#1440807256340955298]], [[d:shaman#1439843615714316318]], [[d:shaman#1439856540524744757]].
- **[bug/quirk]** A long-known "bug, not exploit" (per community consensus/ruling): certain trinket procs long thought to only trigger from spell-casts also trigger from *placing totems*, letting players macro totem-drops to farm extra procs (e.g. Breath of Solnius) — reportedly known privately by some players for ~6 months before being shared openly — [[d:shaman#1442457607859212478]], [[d:shaman#1442515363660173455]], [[d:shaman#1442515701725266106]], [[d:shaman#1442517114513784882]].
- **[gear swapping]** ItemRack addon is commonly used for weapon-swap macros (e.g. swapping to a 1H/shield or back) and can trigger swaps on events like stance/form changes, though users report the Ghost Wolf shapeshift event specifically doesn't reliably fire this trigger — [[d:shaman#1440546249228030113]], [[d:shaman#1440547116932923392]], [[d:shaman#1443221345344295012]], [[d:shaman#1443226779958378569]].

## Bugs & quirks
- **[addon/client]** Debuff timers are not natively exposed by the 1.12 client; addons like Cursive/Pfui only "guess" durations from combat-log parsing and can get confused by non-standard refresh mechanics — e.g. Pfui fails to recognize that Molten Blast refreshes Flame Shock's duration, causing its displayed timer to simply expire early even though the debuff is still up — [[d:shaman#1440222123925962792]], [[d:shaman#1440222558648664135]], [[d:shaman#1440193220171665571]], [[d:shaman#1440193441312145511]].
- **[totem]** Grounding Totem's tooltip says it doesn't work against AoE effects, but players report it does absorb AoE effects like the frost novas in Stormwrought in practice — [[d:shaman#1438952222108024942]] (single source, unconfirmed mechanically).
- **[trinket proc]** See Macros section: certain totem-placement actions trigger trinket procs (e.g. Breath of Solnius) that were assumed to require spellcasts — ruled a "bug, not an exploit" by the community/devs and openly macro-able — [[d:shaman#1442457607859212478]], [[d:shaman#1442515701725266106]].
- **[item]** Invulnerable Mail's proc was changed on Turtle from classic's full physical-damage immunity to instead 75% reduced physical damage taken, still on a 30-second cooldown for only 3 seconds duration — a deliberate Turtle-specific tuning change to a classic crafted item, though the very short window/long cooldown makes it feel gimmicky for reliable tank use relative to its ~1.2k-gold (~20-30 Arcanite Bar) cost — [[d:shaman#1442905865223934245]], [[d:shaman#1442906607074676806]], [[d:shaman#1442904399411413134]], [[d:shaman#1442907040581288049]].
- **[totem]** Windwall Totem is considered to have essentially zero practical use cases on Turtle and is jokingly suggested it should just be merged into Stoneskin Totem — [[d:shaman#1443053916144144464]], [[d:shaman#1443053996247093388]].
- **[unreleased content]** Several "totem weapon" relic items exist in the item database (e.g. item 83063, level 59, with flavor text) but are confirmed not currently in the game; speculated to be intended for an upcoming "Thousand Needles"-type dungeon or recycled test-totem-weapon art assets uploaded long ago by a dev (Kazgrim) — status unconfirmed — [[d:shaman#1443028409117573120]], [[d:shaman#1443032739006648431]], [[d:shaman#1443037447238975528]], [[d:shaman#1443039056182247485]], [[d:shaman#1443039938873397413]], [[d:shaman#1443227918963703951]].
