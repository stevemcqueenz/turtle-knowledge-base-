# Notes: #shaman full chunk 009 (2026-03-21 .. 2026-03-29)

## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Enh tank]** New Earthen Bulwark (rockbiter-derived tank shield) only builds stacks from white/auto-attack melee hits, NOT from Stormstrike, Lightning Strike, shocks, or totems — makes it near-useless in stunned/CC'd or caster-weapon situations and is widely called broken/bugged — [[d:shaman#1484954736342995116]] (Travoltino, 2026-03-21; +0 but heavily discussed), confirmed bug by [[d:shaman#1484956076356145153]] (Ordinary Joe) and [[d:shaman#1485071543183741008]] (Not Normal: "the new earthen bulwark thing does" take a buff slot).
- **[Enh tank]** T2.5 tank set 5pc reworked: lost its old 10% damage-reduction-from-Rockbiter effect and its AoE threat/spellpower bonus; T2.5 is now positioned as the "mitigation set" (high stamina) while T3/T3.5 are "threat sets" — [[d:shaman#1485029053336653925]] (Zhafu), [[d:shaman#1484851024656072777]] (Wahisky, +8: old resto/rockbiter build gave "9k armor easy with 10% damage reduction from old rock bitter"), [[d:shaman#1485071956373012713]] (Olivaar of Moments: "you lost aoe threat because they removed spell power from your 2.5 as well"), [[d:shaman#1485032859906473996]] (Not Normal: "t2.5 is now the mitigation set, all other tier sets are threat sets").
- **[General]** Relic swapping mid-combat (e.g. swap for shocks vs Lightning Strike vs Stormstrike) removed/no longer possible — [[d:shaman#1484870695224606811]] (phyx, "no more relic swap"), [[d:shaman#1485193543122161874]] (Keldjor), [[d:shaman#1485240959070634158]] (Hiryu).
- **[Enh]** Flurry bugged post-patch: not proccing from auto-attack crits, only from ability crits (Stormstrike/Lightning Strike); also gets consumed/overridden by the Elemental Weapons Windfury 2%-haste-per-stack buff (both are haste effects from the same tree and cancel each other) — [[d:shaman#1485038032280096838]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1484967511811293436]] (Gromthrall, confirmed by BarrooM: "proccing on a crit, just not an auto attack, only with Storm Strike or Lightning Strike crit" — [[d:shaman#1484971389214785676]]), [[d:shaman#1485037350076813433]] (Not Normal, +2: same bug existed in 1.18 and was fixed then, "literally impossible... to have intentionally designed it this way"). Official patch note "Flurry will now properly be consumed when attacking" mocked as making it worse — [[d:shaman#1484937561859166399]] (oi-er).
- **[Enh]** Elemental Weapons Windfury proc consumes Flurry stacks (bug) — [[d:shaman#1485142213636657203]] (tomo), [[d:shaman#1485210755971153992]] (Heathen: "the windfury procs themselves even consume flurry stacks"). Bug tracker: id 20839 (Flurry not proccing on melee crits), id 20841 & 20717 (Flurry/Rushing Winds stacks consuming each other) — [[d:shaman#1485206991948353626]] (Therz, +7).
- **[Enh]** Lightning Strike broken/relocated: rank 1 shows in Enhancement spellbook tab, rank 2 moved to General tab, rank 3 (learned from trainer in "General/racial" area) frequently fails to be learnable at all, and the talent point resets out of the tree on every relogin forcing a respec — [[d:shaman#1484932084643532882]] (Amari), [[d:shaman#1484891093123203182]] (Gumbeaux), [[d:shaman#1485076597974368388]] (Gumbeaux), [[d:shaman#1485215297152745664]] (Lórien), [[d:shaman#1485217087780556851]] (T@B@: trainer won't teach ranks 2/3), [[d:shaman#1484992748594532534]] (Abuelito: "rank 3 is on your racial tab"), [[d:shaman#1485054822150439012]] (Jinzio: devs acknowledged only the visual/location bug, not the flurry issue). Macros must explicitly call rank 3 or they'll default to rank 1 — [[d:shaman#1484940392347734207]] (Holidae).
- **[Ele]** Earthquake (capstone) changed: now hits the main target only twice (down from more) and the AoE portion no longer benefits from Elemental Fury (crit-damage talent); tooltip changed to match — widely believed a stealth nerf mislabeled as a "bug fix," devs said "Fixed multiple issues with Earthquake" — [[d:shaman#1484880940814565536]] (Zhafu), confirmed as likely intentional (not accidental) by [[d:shaman#1485050548070650020]] (Not Normal, +0) and [[d:shaman#1485050713309577367]] (Not Normal: "aoe portion isn't benefiting from elemental fury"); Zhafu says devs "didn't mean to nerf eq apparently" / "just a copy and paste" [[d:shaman#1484880676284137553]], [[d:shaman#1484880785583378472]] — contradicts Not Normal's later read that it's intended; net: contested, unresolved as of chunk end. No bug-report thread found for EQ yet as of 2026-03-21 — [[d:shaman#1485050114031747222]] (Lionhermit).
- **[Fire/Nature Ele]** "Fire shaman" (Molten Blast-spam / Spellhancer/OBD one-button build) gutted this patch: devs reworked it into being "part of nature elemental" — rotation folded into: Flame Shock refresh via Molten Blast, keep Chain Lightning and Earthquake on CD, Lightning Bolt filler — [[d:shaman#1485240088047652894]] (Aest). Consensus (Therz, Dwi, Kokuyo, Zhafu) it was overcorrected: fire was overtuned/one-button but nature is now weaker for it (EQ nerf) rather than fire being fixed — [[d:shaman#1484852644785950720]] (Therz), [[d:shaman#1484930457085804625]] (Dwi), disputed by Aest who argues fire was never meant to be permanent and true elemental ("nature") "is so much more fun" — [[d:shaman#1485238462239080489]].
- **[Resto/PvP]** Water Shield mana return from AP changed: 1 mana per 20 AP → 1 mana per 18 AP (small buff), affects Lightning Strike cast off Water Shield ("uses the shield" and refunds mana at that improved rate rather than the larger Lightning-Shield-based bonus) — [[d:shaman#1485038801050140803]] (Not Normal, +1), [[d:shaman#1485038812643201074]] (Ordinary Joe). One player reports Water Shield giving less mana than before despite "buff" framing — [[d:shaman#1484932680213594303]] (Tribak, unconfirmed/contested).
- **[Resto/PvP]** Rockbiter/Earth Shield-style damage reduction removed from Resto entirely as a PvP balance measure aimed at ranged/caster shamans abusing DR while healing — widely condemned since it also gutted the tank spec's only survivability tool; player request was to just disable it in PvP instead — [[d:shaman#1484816358159028344]] (poto), [[d:shaman#1484873514858188943]] (phyx: "why not just turn off DR in PvP?"), [[d:shaman#1484946314394472601]] (Deleted User) + agreement [[d:shaman#1484951471710797995]] (Therz, +1: "nothing wrong with old Elemental Weapons Rockbiter").
- **[Resto PvP]** Old resto T1 set (with mana-return talents triggering 3x per Chain Heal cast, enabling near-infinite rank-10 Healing Wave/Chain Heal spam) is being removed/nerfed for enabling rank-10 healing-wave spam in PvP; suggestion was an internal cooldown on the mana-return talents instead of removing the set's identity — [[d:shaman#1484962875033522257]] (Dwi), [[d:shaman#1484962905916047492]] (Dwi), [[d:shaman#1484959665854087358]] (Travoltino: "Don't remove shaman T1 resto set that is over 20 years old").
- **[Cross-class context]** Same patch buffed Holy Priest heavily: Lightwell now does mini Chain-Heal-like 10 casts, Prayer of Healing no longer restricted to the priest's own raid group, priests can recover 30% mana per heal-type-varied cast, and Ascendance talent judged to outclass Shaman's Spirit Link — [[d:shaman#1484828080185937971]] (Svargth), [[d:shaman#1484828164067823759]] (Svargth: "Ascendance completely blows Spirit Link out of the water"). ## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Enh, hit cap]** Enhancement hit cap is 8% total (to avoid dodge/parry); comes from 3% talent (e.g. Elemental Precision-type) + 315 weapon skill giving 3% + gear — meaning talent+skill alone (6%) leaves little room, and many players are "way over cap" once gear hit is added, especially post AQ40/Naxx gear which has abundant hit — [[d:shaman#1484820588387242044]] (poto), [[d:shaman#1484821734816350219]] (poto), [[d:shaman#1484821791615750164]] (poto: "shaman just has too much hit on gear after aq40/naxx"). A base minimum dodge/miss chance exists regardless of hit — [[d:shaman#1484822084965236806]] (poto, unconfirmed "if i remember correctly").
- **[Enh]** A geared enhancement shaman (raid leader) still topped meters "consistently right beside mages, rogues, hunters and feral druids" on Patchwerk (SA) pre-full-patch-fix, per a shared log — [[d:shaman#1484823992878501959]] (poto).
- **[Ele]** Post-1.18.1, elemental (nature) raid viability strongly disputed: one player reports going from top-5 damage to bottom of the meter in new BWL doing ~half the damage of top parses (mages/rogue/warriors: 1.7M vs shaman's ~0.85M) with full consumes and K40 gear, concluding "elemental shamans are no longer a viable raid spec for a guild pushing for performance" — [[d:shaman#1485064793009295371]] (mylargeson) — disputed as an overgeneralization from a single data point by [[d:shaman#1485065525901135973]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enh/Spellhance hybrid]** "Spellhancer" (physhance) hybrid build: normal Enhancement talents/rotation but using Flametongue weapon imbue instead of Windfury/Rockbiter, weaving Fire Nova Totem and Searing Totem into single-target rotation, gearing for hybrid AP+SP items (e.g. Ancient Jade Leggings) or SP gear where hybrid isn't available. Pre-1.18.1 it was ~10-15% lower single-target DPS than standard Enhancement but with much better AoE; post-patch (once bugs fixed) estimated closer to 5-8% lower than standard Enhance due to a large Flametongue spell-power-coefficient buff — [[d:shaman#1485045691481985095]] (Not Normal, talent link below), [[d:shaman#1485046277111812196]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485046525804548167]] (Not Normal). Weapon choice: Hand of Ragnaros ("the dream"), or Maul of the Redeemed Crusader as a more attainable option; Might of Menethil / Sharpened Silithid Tooth-type weapons said to be better if attainable — [[d:shaman#1485084222338367518]] (Not Normal).
- **[Resto tank-healing]** Resto Shaman is "pretty solid" at tank healing, not as overpowered as Holy Paladin, but historically overshadowed for raid/tank healing because Chain Heal is so strong there was never a reason to use Resto for pure tank-healing — [[d:shaman#1485057691465547987]] (Not Normal, +1), [[d:shaman#1485057739922079826]] (Not Normal, +1).
- **[Enh tank]** Fast weapons preferred for tanks post-patch because it reduces the window the Earthen Bulwark shield can fall off from a parry mid-combat; slow weapons offer no benefit since bulwark stacks per-hit not per-damage — [[d:shaman#1484926217462480966]] (oi-er), [[d:shaman#1484836113863409786]] (poto: "quick and slow would do nothing for it... dealing a 250 damage hit and dealing two 125 damage hits build up the same amount" — contradicts oi-er's claim about parry-avoidance specifically, both address different mechanics: fast weapons help avoid parry windows, but total build-up rate is damage-based not hit-count-based).
- **[Enh tank]** Consensus that Enhancement tank ("shaman tank") is significantly weaker after the patch versus 4th-tank-class norm: takes more damage while dealing less, threat harder to hold, and only has one true 5th-tier set (T2.5) which is now a "mitigation set" rather than getting a T3/T3.5 tank-itemized set like Warriors/Paladins/Druids get — [[d:shaman#1485165588698501261]] (Twiek96), [[d:shaman#1485166647458136136]] (Diving Instructor Knife), [[d:shaman#1485219505394417844]] (Therz: "why is only shaman, the worst tank class of all 4, getting extra handicap mechanic?"), [[d:shaman#1485167797460799568]] (Therz: with the T2.5 set-bonus rework "I doubt it's even worth to run T2.5 over T3 enhancement set").
- **[Ele]** Single-target rotation and DPS are near-unchanged from pre-patch per parse analysis; only AoE is worse due to the Earthquake bugs — [[d:shaman#1485683698342035487]] (Not Normal). Parse-ranking claim: nature ele is not the worst caster spec — disc priest, destro lock, and frost mage rank clearly below it; arcane and demo lock "gap everyone"; enh is likewise not the worst melee (feral/survival/enh are roughly on par, below warrior/rogue, with ret arguably the actual worst) — [[d:shaman#1486383461534011483]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1486385562997882951]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1486385916103622766]] (Not Normal) — disputed by community sentiment that shaman is "the worst" across the board, e.g. [[d:shaman#1486379828310442056]] (Zli).
- **[Enh]** Flurry fix history across the week: (1) initially only procced from ability crits, not autos; (2) hotfixed so it procs from autos AND abilities (SS/LS/shocks/hardcast crits), confirmed excluding Flametongue/Frostbrand/Spellstrike procs and totem-of-tides procs by design (only "abilities you cast yourself") — [[d:shaman#1485794403133030550]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1486367830508175532]] (Not Normal); (3) Elemental Weapons' Windfury-proc haste buff ("Rushing Winds") continued to cancel/overwrite Flurry stacks as a separate, still-unresolved bug through the end of the chunk — [[d:shaman#1486279382803943434]] (Frost), [[d:shaman#1486184061843280064]] (Orcsnaker, video confirmation). Uptime data: physhance (standard) Enhancement gets ~75-95% Flurry uptime depending on gear; spellhance can approach ~99% if FT/FB procced it (deliberately excluded to avoid this) — [[d:shaman#1486368453249204244]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1486368501240168488]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485795137224179854]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enh tank]** Earthen Bulwark mechanics clarified: shield caps at 20% of max HP; a "durability" stack counter maxes at 100 (each point ≈0.2% max HP of shield); the shield only reduces 15% of incoming damage while up; having a shield equipped gives a +60% buildup-rate modifier — [[d:shaman#1486164601929338900]] (Moovement), [[d:shaman#1486164878652604517]] (Moovement), [[d:shaman#1485280660573655080]] (Hiryu), [[d:shaman#1485640394174894121]] (Not Normal). - **[Leveling/Ele]** Elemental leveling talent order: max Convection (spell-cost-reduction, row 1) first if crit is poor at low level; core rotation for leveling/endgame nature-ele is Flame Shock → Molten Blast refresh → Lightning Bolt filler, Chain Lightning on Clearcasting — [[d:shaman#1485976858523734117]] (peter_hammer), [[d:shaman#1485686440070479892]] (Not Normal: "thats currently the ideal rotation... used to be earthquake was also worth using in single target but its bugged"). ## Leveling
- **[Enh]** General leveling talent order: take Enhancement defensive/damage talents up through Stormstrike, dip into Elemental for Elemental Focus (Clearcasting), then return to Enhancement for Stormstrike and Elemental Weapons; once Elemental Weapons is up, switch to Frostbrand + Frost Shock ("you'll never run out of mana and hit like a truck," since Elemental Weapons makes Frostbrand-imbued Frost Shock auto-crit) — [[d:shaman#1486935597934313613]] (Hassle), [[d:shaman#1486938327276982383]] (Hassle), [[d:shaman#1487164548028039289]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1487164992846565496]] (Ordinary Joe). Elemental Focus/Clearcasting not very valuable until ~level 41+ since leveling crit chance is too low before then — [[d:shaman#1487164881273753600]] (Cariad).
- **[Enh]** Flurry is not worth taking while leveling (crit chance too low, fights too short for uptime to matter) — [[d:shaman#1487165798102470787]] (Ordinary Joe). While tanking/leveling, prioritize the two armor talents and Totemic Alignment; melee crit talents also good — [[d:shaman#1487165848580788377]] (Ordinary Joe). Armor talents in general are "goated" while leveling — [[d:shaman#1487165923549909133]] (poto).
- **[Enh leveling weapon/imbue]** In dungeons specifically, avoid Rockbiter since it "generates extra threat" you don't want as non-tank; Rockbiter is otherwise the strongest leveling choice until Windfury + decent gear are available because it reduces downtime (less damage taken) and synergizes with Water Shield's AP-based mana return — [[d:shaman#1487225880143986879]] (Kirchhoff), [[d:shaman#1487227188397998253]] (Kirchhoff).
- **[Tank leveling weapon]** Weapon-speed rule while leveling as tank: anything slower than ~2.3s speed is fine; small differences (e.g. 2.8 vs 2.9 speed) barely matter — prioritize slow, high-DPS weapon upgrades over speed differences — [[d:shaman#1486941771157209088]] (Capslock), [[d:shaman#1486943390825320618]] (sailorVEVO), [[d:shaman#1486943480516317307]] (sailorVEVO).
- **[Enh tank/dps identity]** Tank spec is essentially an addition bolted onto the (mostly DPS-oriented) Enhancement tree; a DPS-specced Enhancement player can tank in a pinch (squishier, worse AoE threat due to no Totemic Alignment) and a tank-specced player can go DPS gear/rotation reasonably well but risks overthreat without Calming Winds — [[d:shaman#1486943594681073797]] (sailorVEVO), [[d:shaman#1486943724100522116]] (sailorVEVO), [[d:shaman#1486943898298482740]] (sailorVEVO), [[d:shaman#1486944348212957194]] (sailorVEVO). For pure DPS, skip Rockbiter, Spirit/Shield-armor talent, and Totemic Alignment — [[d:shaman#1486944433344741429]] (sailorVEVO).
- **[Enh]** Leveling/farming mana sustain: gearing 3 pieces of Tier 1 (15% cost reduction to nearly everything) helps a lot; world buffs before farming help; use Stormstrike/Lightning Strike on cooldown but hold shocks unless Elemental Focus (Clearcasting) is up; Frostbrand+Frost Shock is also good for mana in the 40-50 range — [[d:shaman#1486631471937753138]] (Cariad). At 3pc T3.5, Water Shield popped at ~20% mana can refill to ~80% in ~30 seconds due to the set bonus — [[d:shaman#1486631908606476409]] (Cariad).
- **[Leveling]** Windfury Totem vs Grace of Air Totem rule of thumb while leveling/dungeons: use WF if 2+ party members benefit from extra-attack procs (e.g. multiple melee, especially warriors/rogues); otherwise use Grace of Air for the agility (dodge/AP/crit) — [[d:shaman#1486724583900839967]] (trallnar), [[d:shaman#1486730676978843719]] (trallnar), [[d:shaman#1486731739085799527]] (Guyye: "Grace is straight up better for combat rogues, 100 extra agi makes the energy ticks go brrrr").
- **[Leveling dungeons]** Stormwrought Keep/Castle (first half, ~level 35-36+) and Stormwrought Descent (second half, final boss level 40) drop Totem of the Tides; can be queued separately via dungeon finder, or the final-boss key can be farmed directly through a "backdoor" entrance — [[d:shaman#1485086381637243142]] (Dwi), [[d:shaman#1485086794029600843]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485097000125862038]] (Nation). Stormsong Village/similar can reportedly be soloed at level 45 in regular tank spec via a specific safespot, skipping most trash except 2 skeletons+1 necromancer and 2 battlemasters — [[d:shaman#1486192894804234342]] (chaospanda).
- **[Leveling item]** Razorgust quest item (drops from an elemental NPC in Stonetalon) starts a level-20s shaman quest chain in a new dungeon rewarding "Raging Wind" axe — [[d:shaman#1486901098613706835]] (Hassle).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Enh, hit]** Enhancement hit cap: 8% total, made up of 3% from talent + 3% from 315 weapon skill + remainder from gear; post-AQ40/Naxx gear tends to push well over cap — [[d:shaman#1484820588387242044]], [[d:shaman#1484821734816350219]] (poto) (see also Spec viability section).
- **[Ele, hit]** Elemental spell-hit target is 12% (3% from Elemental Devastation talent — may not show on some character-stat addons/panels — + up to ~1% from a Druid buff, remainder from gear) — [[d:shaman#1486434725848416427]] (Key), [[d:shaman#1486439926412087380]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1486439783516475604]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enh, stat weights, BWL gear]** Community-generated stat-weight table (screenshot, assumes fully consumed): 1 AP=1.00(ap)/1.88(sp); 1 Str=2.32/4.36; 1 Agi=1.43/2.69; 1 Int=0.16/0.30; 1% phys crit=24.92/46.74; 1% phys hit=0.00/0.00 (already hit-capped assumption); 1% haste=17.54/32.91; 1 weapon skill=16.21/30.40; 1 Armor Pen=0.40/0.76; 1 SP=0.53/1.00; 1 Nature SP=0.36/0.68; 1 Fire SP=0.17/0.32; 1 spell crit=8.16/15.30; 1 spell hit=9.41/17.65; 1 spell pen=2.52/4.73 — [[d:shaman#1486691372466569246]] (Kyle Sussenbach, screenshot; ratio-based weights, gear/consumable-dependent).
- **[Enh tank]** Threat/stat priority for gearing: Stamina and armor (especially shield armor level) first for defense; for threat, Strength > Spell Power > Agility; a couple points of spell hit can help threat/consistency but caster-stat items rarely also carry good defensive stats, so early on defensive stats should be prioritized on main armor slots and caster stats picked up opportunistically on rings/trinkets/neck — [[d:shaman#1486559128083103835]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1486559654745079838]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1486560060925808831]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enh tank]** Weapon skill matters a lot for threat since Stormstrike/Lightning Strike aren't weapon-DPS-normalized; getting to exactly 315 weapon skill (via gear + racial + quest reward) removes dodge/parry losses — [[d:shaman#1486861889248952521]] (Ciftar), [[d:shaman#1487191048840020049]] (Ordinary Joe: "5 wep skill is 1 hit so you need 4 from gear with skill book" — permanent +5 weapon-type skill obtainable via a quest chain).
- **[Enh tank shield comparison]** Numeric shield stat comparison at BWL gear level: Onyxia shield ≈ 4137 armor / 240 HP / 5 def; Jadestone Protector ≈ 4401 armor / 140 HP / 9 def (also ~2% block chance, ~71 block value, ~14 stam per a separate recollection); Earthen Bulwark (the new craftable/drop shield) ≈ 4195 armor / 230 HP / 7 def — judged very close in overall value, with Jadestone edging ahead in one player's specific stat-weight calc against Patchwerk — [[d:shaman#1487057091310129254]] (Thataintmilk), [[d:shaman#1487067496291897384]] (Ordinary Joe).
- **[Enh, avoidance math]** Windfury proc gives ~+20% damage-equivalent value on autoattacks at moderate hit levels vs 5% crit being ~+10%, illustrating why Windfury Totem outperforms Grace of Air/crit-based options for melee-heavy groups — [[d:shaman#1486729926253219970]] (Gurdaz, illustrative, not exact formula).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Ele]** Elemental Mastery (capstone cooldown) judged extremely weak relative to its 3-minute cooldown: only ~10s duration for +15% damage, calculated as roughly a 1.25% total-fight DPS contribution on a 2-minute fight vs. e.g. Warrior's Death Wish (20% damage/30s on a similar cooldown) contributing ~5% — a 4x disparity for the same talent-tree "capstone" position — [[d:shaman#1485731517710860439]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485731559855231157]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485732605365063770]] (Not Normal: still probably good specifically for tanking use). Suggested fixes (not implemented): 20% for 20s, or 15% for 30s, or keep 15%/10s but drop the cooldown to ~1 minute — [[d:shaman#1485704150690828348]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485704185994547200]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enh tank threat]** Calming Winds (Enhancement threat-reduction talent) reported by some as not doing much / needing verification — [[d:shaman#1484925075386798212]] (Keldjor). General community math on T2.5's threat-bonus loss (see 1.18.1 changes) pegs it at roughly a 6% relative threat-multiplier reduction (old ~1.573x vs new ~1.485x from Rockbiter's threat modifier alone) — [[d:shaman#1486121153046118420]] (Ordinary Joe, detailed math), [[d:shaman#1486113628942762116]] (LokiDelphoric, later revised to ~10% loss factoring in the Flurry-related AP interplay).
- **[Enh tank threat]** Earth Shock has a threat multiplier bonus and is the default "spam on cooldown" tank threat tool; Frost Shock deals less damage/threat than Earth Shock by design (a deliberate damage-vs-threat tradeoff talent point), and switching to Frost Shock (or refreshing Flame Shock earlier) is recommended specifically to reduce threat generation when overshooting the tank on a threat ceiling — [[d:shaman#1485350968982765568]] (sqsh), [[d:shaman#1486399922365530282]] (Zeruel), [[d:shaman#1486400009447411906]] (Zeruel), [[d:shaman#1486409423319732357]] (Ordinary Joe, proposing Frost Shock = Earth Shock damage as a fix, opposed on design-tradeoff grounds by Not Normal at [[d:shaman#1486409747975377007]]).
- **[Enh dps threat]** Standard advice for dps enhance pulling too much threat: request Tranquil Air Totem/Salvation from tanks; if problems persist even with Salvation, the likely cause is undergeared or poorly-played tanks rather than the shaman's own threat output (shaman threat is easy to extrapolate from parses since it's roughly proportional to dps) — [[d:shaman#1486395760894414898]] (Diving Instructor Knife), [[d:shaman#1486406025262137467]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1486401541781848166]] (Zeruel), [[d:shaman#1486404424514539723]] (Jinzio).
- **[General]** Extra-attack effects (Windfury, Hand of Justice, etc.) cannot proc further extra attacks off each other on this server — [[d:shaman#1486104341692809237]] (Zhafu, re: whether Windfury can proc off a Hand of Justice extra attack: "no, extra attacks cant proc extra attacks on this server").
- **[Totems]** Recommended totem-management addons: Call of Elements (CoE, widely used but reportedly buggy/outdated, e.g. `/coe throwset` macro command frequently broken for users) and a newer community-made alternative "Sticks and Stones" (SnS, `/sns force` or `/sns smart` macro commands) praised as more intuitive; both require nampower/superwow to drop all totems simultaneously via one keypress — [[d:shaman#1484855698876661890]] (Zli), [[d:shaman#1484857366095855788]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1484867027301826740]] (SuchMonkey), [[d:shaman#1486733022131912856]] (rel, SnS github link), [[d:shaman#1486746434249429023]] (Hjelp: "much more intuitive... 10/10"), disputed reliability of CoE reported widely through the chunk (e.g. [[d:shaman#1486508017880141874]] trallnar, [[d:shaman#1486510441885733035]]).

## Tanking specifics
- (See also 1.18.1 changes & Spec viability sections for Earthen Bulwark mechanics/bugs, and Rotation sections for pull/AoE rotations.)
- **[Enh tank]** Shaman tank is explicitly the least durable of the 4 tank classes but has strong/"killer" threat, making it more viable as an off-tank/adds-tank or a main-tank choice specifically valued for snap-threat and high sustained TPS rather than survivability — [[d:shaman#1486562477322797198]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1486562586102071387]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1486562768629792828]] (Hopestotem: "Meatbag that generates threat"). One MT reports doing roughly double the damage of other tanks in his raid while maintaining threat lead — [[d:shaman#1487177101772849214]] (Ordinary Joe).
- **[Enh tank]** 2H tanking (i.e., dropping the shield for a 2H weapon) is broadly condemned as non-viable at any real gear/difficulty level: a shield gives ~50% of total armor and a 10% bonus-threat modifier, so 2H tanking roughly doubles damage taken for little gain; disputed by one self-described "top-3 guild MT" who claims to successfully 2H-tank in current MC/BWL-phase content with strong healers — [[d:shaman#1487039557395546222]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1487039579356659952]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1487039831027613697]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1487039526483398778]] (Frost, disputing), [[d:shaman#1487040065556451369]] (Ordinary Joe: "even in bwl, tanking any of those bosses with 4k armor is regarded"); consensus caveat that Broodlord and Nefarian specifically require a shield — [[d:shaman#1487040265381347407]] (Chidi \<beta tester\>).
- **[Enh tank]** Shield Specialization (block-chance talent) is judged low-value design: block only meaningfully reduces damage once total avoidance is high enough (~80-85%, depending on defense rating) to start displacing Crush results from the attack table; below that point extra block/block-value is nearly worthless since base shaman block value is very low (~100) — [[d:shaman#1485012544820089056]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485014460627419228]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1485014582463299789]] (Ordinary Joe: "block is like 100 lol"). A theorized "gigablock" build (300 block value via specific gear, ~30% block chance, Glyph of Deflection cooldown pushing to 600 block value for 20s) is calculated as competitively tanky but at a steep AP/threat cost — [[d:shaman#1487169652491817171]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1487169742535135382]] (Ordinary Joe).
- **[Enh tank]** No dedicated defensive cooldown exists for shaman tank (unlike Paladin's Righteous Fury-adjacent options or Warrior stance/Shield Wall); repeated community requests for something like a "Last Stand"-style ability or a Bloodlust-tied mitigation cooldown went unaddressed this patch — [[d:shaman#1485695891204473016]] (Mikoto Rin: "we need a def CD, we are the only class without one"), [[d:shaman#1485695764805193898]] (Zeruel).
- **[Enh tank]** Old (pre-1.18.1) tank-relevant Rockbiter/T2.5-combo effects, cited from memory during design debate: roughly 25% increased health, 10% flat damage reduction, 6% reduced chance to be critically hit, Earth Shock acting as a taunt, and a ~65% total threat-modifier boost, all bundled into essentially one buff — cited as an example of bad/overloaded design regardless of whether it was strong for players — [[d:shaman#1487175534403387424]] (poto), [[d:shaman#1487175624614215943]] (poto), [[d:shaman#1487175767765815387]] (Hopestotem), [[d:shaman#1487176058045464749]] (Not Normal: "having any power injection like that that is just flat and doesn't scale... is objectively bad design").
- **[Enh tank]** Community view (Not Normal, widely engaged with) that developers "don't have a cohesive vision for shaman tank" — evidenced by simultaneously stripping SP from T2.5's tank set while adding new tank gear (Timbermaw) with defense+block+SP, and by not addressing the core tension between physical ("physhance") and spell-power ("spellhance") tank itemization paths — [[d:shaman#1487170982593691763]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1487171119239659550]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1487171319496970321]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1487171745302577274]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enh tank]** Race choice for tanking: Tauren "unambiguously better" primarily due to Plainsrunning (movement-speed racial utility), with Orc/Troll viable for aesthetic/flavor preference only among the community consulted — [[d:shaman#1485717674263711864]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485726982716002416]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1487229910664609874]] (Zeruel: "no reason to pick troll for tanking other than aesthetics").
- **[Tank leveling]** Enhancement tank early-game weakness window is roughly the first ~25 levels until the 2/2 Spirit/Shield-armor talent gives sufficient armor; no strong escape tool is a persistent weakness at all levels — [[d:shaman#1485457421525712999]], [[d:shaman#1485457615470330088]] (sailorVEVO).

## Healing specifics
- **[Resto]** Chain Heal's target-selection ("smart heal"/lowest-HP-jump prioritization) does not and reportedly never has worked as in original vanilla WoW on this server; community requests to "fix" it to vanilla behavior were told this is not a bug to fix but working as intended for Turtle's implementation, sparking prolonged frustration — [[d:shaman#1485252771279143012]] (Lhian, dev: "chain heal has never had most wounded prio on this server"), [[d:shaman#1485253500085469184]] (natty, disputing from vanilla-era memory), [[d:shaman#1485254003129581699]] (Zhafu: "it never worked like that on this server... several turtle wow knowers have come to this conclusion"), [[d:shaman#1485254196264439808]] (Zhafu: "initial 1.18 was a whole different situation because the ability just broke entirely"), [[d:shaman#1485306095978414134]] (Thataintmilk, "working as intended" response repeated as a running community joke).
- **[Resto]** Priest's Lightwell was specifically coded with "smart healing" target selection, used as a comparison point by players asking for Chain Heal parity — [[d:shaman#1485253242484031691]] (natty).
- **[Resto]** Practical rank-healing guidance (single source, contested elsewhere in chunk for reliability): Chain Heal rank 1 is best-in-slot spam as long as its bounces aren't pure overheal; Healing Wave rank 3 (1.7s cast) is the most mana-efficient rank that isn't crippled by being under the global cooldown floor; rank 4 has near-max scaling with still-good mana efficiency; Lesser Healing Wave is essentially never downranked; use higher Healing Wave ranks based on incoming damage-per-hit and available mana — [[d:shaman#1487131827796836525]] (Diving Instructor Knife), [[d:shaman#1487131843328213013]] (Diving Instructor Knife), [[d:shaman#1487132001134838074]] (Diving Instructor Knife), [[d:shaman#1487132538299486218]] (Diving Instructor Knife, caveats this assumes decent +healing as the main stat).
- **[Resto]** Resto's mana-return/utility identity is considered gutted post-patch: its only distinguishing PvP-relevant tool (Rockbiter/Earth-Shield-style DR) was removed, and its historic T1 tier-set identity (enabling rank-10 Healing Wave/Chain Heal spam via triple mana-return procs) is being phased out without a replacement mechanic; suggested replacement is a dedicated "lifegiving"/healing weapon imbue carrying the old Earth Shield 30% pushback-reduction property plus mana-per-5, freeing Earth Shield itself for a new effect — [[d:shaman#1486004180458930238]] (Zamka), [[d:shaman#1486004583825412276]] (Zamka), [[d:shaman#1486004806135976116]] (Zamka), [[d:shaman#1486004848984854668]] (Therz).
- **[Resto raid role]** Multiple experienced resto mains report dropping from top-2-3 healer parses (out of ~9 healers) to 6th-7th, or switching mains to Priest entirely, directly attributing this to the same-patch Holy Priest buffs plus resto's own nerfs (see 1.18.1 changes) — [[d:shaman#1485771848426852413]] (Svargth), [[d:shaman#1485771860736999467]] (Svargth), [[d:shaman#1486000761195462848]] (Svargth: "I dropped resto and I'm healing in raid with a priest").
- **[Resto]** Pushback-reduction talent (Earth Shield line) requirement: most raids have raid-wide-damage bosses causing spell pushback, so casters (including resto healers) commonly take the pushback-resistance talent branch; skipping it is described as "pretty miserable" on such fights — [[d:shaman#1485713565343023214]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485713596942913587]] (Not Normal).
- **[Resto build note]** Suggested resto healing talent starting point (while talent calculator was down): take the elemental-shield talent in Enhancement (pushback reduction) plus Ancestral Knowledge/Stable Shields, remainder into Resto — [[d:shaman#1486204255722274826]] (Netharia), [[d:shaman#1486206423242248192]] (Netharia).

## PvP
- **[General]** Broad community sentiment: Shaman feels weak/mediocre in PvE (squishiest tank, mediocre-to-bad DPS specs, lowest-output healer per some) but strong-to-overperforming in PvP (burst, self-sufficiency), leading to "Schrödinger's shaman: simultaneously too broken to play and too broken to play against" — [[d:shaman#1486002554646167643]] (peter_hammer), [[d:shaman#1485297058947530933]] (Hiryu). Enhancement PvP burst via Windfury crits is described as excellent but vulnerable to slows/kiting/ranged; ele/resto PvP power level is separately debated — [[d:shaman#1486808462783545517]] (Kyle Sussenbach), [[d:shaman#1486821661633282240]] (Kyle Sussenbach: "Ele, Locks, and Rogues are all top-tier" in PvP).
- **[PvP]** The core justification given for nerfing Rockbiter/Earth-Shield DR (see 1.18.1 changes) was PvP abuse by ranged/caster-spec shamans (Elemental/Resto) stacking survivability while healing/casting; PvE tank players broadly argue this should have been solved via a PvP-only interaction (increased cast time, reduced healing/damage while imbued, or simply disabling it in PvP) rather than a global rework — [[d:shaman#1485222091111403592]] (Awe), [[d:shaman#1486000818103783424]] (Ordinary Joe, proposed: Rockbiter reduces incoming heal effectiveness from LHW/HW/CH by 50%, plus a 10s weapon-swap lockout after losing the buff, as a PvP-targeted alternative).
- **[PvP]** New PvP reward tank set for shaman (see Gear section) has essentially no defensive stats itemized (only utility: silence resist, interrupt-duration reduction) yet reportedly outperforms current PvE mitigation sets on raw stamina alone — [[d:shaman#1487313790658809866]] (Gurdaz), [[d:shaman#1487324480169709598]] (ThatsTheJoke).
- **[PvP]** Windfury Totem 2-second internal cooldown (not 1.5s) confirmed via log analysis — relevant PvP/PvE theorycraft impact for any dual-wielder benefiting from WFT (see Enchants/imbues section for full citation).
- **[PvP gear]** Field Medic PvP set requires Rank 14 for the full set — [[d:shaman#1487437782313078866]] (Nesushka).
- **[PvP]** Race choice for PvP debated: Tauren's War Stomp (stun) + extra HP favored by some, though camera/visual-clutter complaints noted; Orc's Hardiness (stun resist) valued; Troll's berserking/regen also mentioned as helpful for maintaining Earthen Bulwark uptime in the current buggy tank meta — [[d:shaman#1485740409610178732]] (Paliote), [[d:shaman#1485717868111990794]] (Hiryu).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Totems]** Call of Elements (CoE) is the most widely used totem-management addon but multiple users report its macro commands (`/coe throwset`) breaking or simply not registering after this patch, and its UI displaying totem timers for only the last-dropped totem when totems are cast via a manual multi-totem macro instead of its own function; requires the button-lock option enabled in-game (General tab) to display totems at all for some users — [[d:shaman#1484855698876661890]] (Zli), [[d:shaman#1484886918201217165]] (Abstractz: "Try to lock the buttons first"), [[d:shaman#1486508017880141874]] (trallnar), [[d:shaman#1486510441885733035]] (trallnar: "/coe throwset... does nothing and seems to not be recognized"), [[d:shaman#1486516337282125895]] (trallnar). Community-made alternative "Sticks and Stones" (SnS) by Abstractz praised as more intuitive with easier totem-set editing — [[d:shaman#1486733022131912856]] (rel, github: `github.com/Abstr4ctz/SticksAndStones`), [[d:shaman#1486746434249429023]] (Hjelp, 10/10).
- **[Totems]** `nampower` (a client-side performance/behavior mod) is required to drop all 4 totems simultaneously via a single macro/keypress with either CoE or SnS — [[d:shaman#1484857366095855788]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1484867027301826740]] (SuchMonkey).
- **[Macros]** SuperCleveRoidMacros addon recommended to fix an auto-attack-toggling macro bug (pressing a `/cast Stormstrike` macro twice was turning off auto-attack); use `/startattack` plus `/cast <ability>` in one macro instead — [[d:shaman#1486527303902167110]] (cinu), [[d:shaman#1486529447484985384]] (cinu), github: `github.com/jrc13245/SuperCleveRoidMacros`.
- **[Totems]** A single-totem-swap macro trick (calling the addon's internal keybind function directly, e.g. `/run App.BindCastElement(3)` for Call of Elements) lets a player drop one totem of a chosen element while switching which specific totem is bound to a single key, avoiding a separate keybind per totem variant — [[d:shaman#1486782873674711151]] (Abstractz), [[d:shaman#1486790015089512740]] (Wolfcola, confirmed working).
- **[Addons]** DoiteAuras recommended for tracking buff/debuff timers (Flame Shock duration through Molten Blast refreshes, Clearcasting, Stormstrike buff, Lightning Strike/totem uptimes, Earthen Bulwark visual indicator since the ability has no default visual effect); ability ID references given: Stormstrike buff = 52412 ([[d:shaman#1485991326947348601]], Fat Pibble with Air Pods), a totem-related buff = 17364 ([[d:shaman#1485994405574742106]], Fat Pibble with Air Pods) — [[d:shaman#1485235144573845616]] (Hiryu), [[d:shaman#1485235260953464952]] (Hiryu), [[d:shaman#1486429706596385000]] (Ordinary Joe).
- **[Addons]** ShaguDPS meter only tracks totem damage as attributable to the shaman if SuperWoW is installed; without SuperWoW, no damage meter can attribute totem DPS to the shaman — [[d:shaman#1485700264278233199]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485700382918447249]] (Not Normal).
- **[Addons]** PerfBoostSettings guide/tool can disable specific ability animations (cited example: Earthquake, also usable for Lightning Shield) for players sensitive to spinning/flashing visual effects — [[d:shaman#1487101535065346079]] (McMulligan), github: `gitea.com/avitasia/PerfBoostSettings`.
- **[Theorycraft tools]** Community-built shaman simulation tools exist: a tank-threat/stat-weight simulator (by Ordinary Joe, "stochastically determined" weights) and a separate spreadsheet (Not Normal's) for DPS/tank stat weights, both allow setting target-resistance values to approximate fire-immune-boss scenarios — [[d:shaman#1486430576046244030]] (Ordinary Joe), [[d:shaman#1486720548741447791]] (Kyle Sussenbach), [[d:shaman#1486873112518590535]] (Ordinary Joe).
- **[UI note]** Turtle WoW's central item database (turtlecraft.gg / atlasloot-style tools) goes offline for several days after major patches, disrupting theorycrafting/gearing lookups; alternative lookups during downtime: `/db Totem of` via pfUI, or the external gear-planner site `pbrigade.gitlab.io/gear-planner` (does not include totems newly added in 1.18.1 until DB comes back) — [[d:shaman#1485634914706526228]] (Dwi), [[d:shaman#1485636435519344720]] (Dwi).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Tanking]** Tauren rated the best tanking race primarily for the Plainsrunning racial (movement speed utility); Orc and Troll are viable but chosen mainly for aesthetics/animations among tanks — [[d:shaman#1485717674263711864]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1487229910664609874]] (Zeruel).
- **[Professions]** Engineering considered top profession choice broadly; Jewelcrafting judged "okay" for gear (some elemental-usable pieces short-term) but its gem/enchant output has ongoing value as new gear becomes available; profession choice otherwise depends on wealth/guild support: poor/solo players lean Herbalism+Alchemy or Mining+Blacksmithing, wealthier/group players can pick whatever the group needs, and Enchanting+Skinning is suggested for players who don't want to pause leveling to farm profession mats — [[d:shaman#1486946342768083027]] (Capslock), [[d:shaman#1486946491015762201]] (sailorVEVO), [[d:shaman#1487467235919397095]] (Juan_seb63), [[d:shaman#1487468618605264947]] (Collin), [[d:shaman#1487468899837415445]] (Collin).
- **[Weapons/itemization complaint]** Shamans (Enhancement specifically) are limited to essentially 2 usable weapon categories (slow maces/axes/fist weapons for 2H, and a very narrow set of usable 1h/offhand spellpower options), unlike most other 2H-capable classes which have roughly double the weapon-type options; long-running community request for shaman access to polearms and more strength/AP-based staves has not been implemented — [[d:shaman#1485788181126189237]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485839095497490542]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485996667395244116]]/[[d:shaman#1485997016692555978]] (community requests), rejected historically per developer commentary reasoning that polearms are thematically hunter/fisherman weapons — [[d:shaman#1485997235488559245]] (Dwi).

- Whether the Earthquake single-target/AoE nerf (only hitting main target twice, AoE portion losing Elemental Fury benefit) was an intentional design change or an accidental "copy-paste" bug remains unresolved as of chunk end; a dev (Lhian) called it "more complex" than a simple bug and said it needed more time, without confirming intent either way — [[d:shaman#1484880676284137553]] (Zhafu), [[d:shaman#1485050548070650020]] (Not Normal), [[d:shaman#1485246404103241748]] (Lhian), [[d:shaman#1485251866576293939]] (Zhafu: root cause was the change simply not being communicated, fueling the panic).
