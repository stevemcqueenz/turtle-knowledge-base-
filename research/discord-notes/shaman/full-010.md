## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[tank]** Earthen Bulwark replaced the old flat damage-reduction system: it now builds up from 0 during combat (from physical damage dealt) and can drop mid-fight; widely reported as bugged/not building from Storm Strike or Lightning Strike, only basic melee — [[d:shaman#1487726640682369034]], [[d:shaman#1489002718675861636]], [[d:shaman#1490007576413470720]] (Therz/T@B@, 2026-03-29/04-05; disputed severity, see Tanking section).
- **[tank]** Rockbiter/Elemental Weapons nerf aimed at resto PvP tankiness reportedly didn't reduce resto survivability much, while new resto PvP gear makes them "more unkillable" — [[d:shaman#1487726640682369034]] (Therz, 2026-03-29; single source, forum complaint).
- **[enh]** Elemental Weapons' Windfury bonus haste is bugged: intended to stack up to 6 times (2 stacks per WF proc, 3/3 talent, 1%/stack) but usually grants only 1 stack per proc — [[d:shaman#1487883029270495503]], [[d:shaman#1490194328176427089]] (Not Normal, 2026-03-29/04-05; confirmed by multiple, tooltip also wrong).
- **[enh]** Flurry vs Elemental Weapons stacking bug reportedly fixed, but Elemental Weapons still gives wrong (lower) total haste — [[d:shaman#1487863744217354400]] (Heathen, 2026-03-29).
- **[enh]** Flametongue/Frostbrand spellpower coefficients were announced in the 1.18.1 patch notes as changing to scale with weapon speed (like Seal of Righteousness) but the change was never actually deployed, weeks later — [[d:shaman#1488705173353136340]], [[d:shaman#1489387157704540220]] (Not Normal/poto, 2026-04-01/03; "listed as fixed... never made it in").
- **[enh]** Windfury talent (enhancement) buffed to 25% proc chance in this patch; called "underwhelming" for 2h/slow-weapon builds since the haste buff is only ~1%/stack and falls off fully — [[d:shaman#1487798512002400286]], [[d:shaman#1488905544696860812]], [[d:shaman#1488911603263865005]] (Failedshaman/Trimur, 2026-03-30; Trimur proposes 1-stack-per-tick falloff + 9s duration instead of full reset).
- **[enh]** Stormstrike double-proccing Windfury was fixed this patch; devs compensated top-end Enh DPS elsewhere, and current top Patchwerk parses are already higher than pre-1.18 — [[d:shaman#1489377174208385105]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-03).
- **[tank]** T3 8-piece tank set bonus resets shock cooldowns; actual proc chance is 50%, not the 25% listed in the tooltip — [[d:shaman#1487659549044248687]], [[d:shaman#1488272279728029819]] (Keldjor/Ordinary Joe, 2026-03-29/30).
- **[all]** New Onyxia-boss totem "Totem of Thundercall" scales 12% of AP as damage over its 4s duration (3%/tick); the new "Totem of Calm Cascades" (Emerald Sanctum) scales 15% of Healing Power per the patch notes but this scaling never actually shipped — [[d:shaman#1489329800480034970]], [[d:shaman#1490930386338054265]] (Not Normal/magusbones, 2026-04-02/06).
- **[resto]** Chain Heal has long not prioritized lowest-HP targets; TWoW staff says this is intended "for class balance" despite bug reports (id 18234) and community pushback; Chain Heal's heal coefficient was also nerfed last year — [[d:shaman#1488865232578150430]] (Healingmoo, 2026-03-31; single source but detailed/linked).
- **[ele]** Earthquake (EQ) totem was quietly reworked/nerfed ("multiple fixes for EQ" patch note, no detail given); currently considered garbage — long cast, low crit multiplier, and a bug where if the main hit doesn't crit, the AoE portion also fails to crit — [[d:shaman#1488087895079256225]], [[d:shaman#1490732254463266898]], [[d:shaman#1490739300546121890]] (Therz/Kokuyo, 2026-03-30/04-06; disputed as "shadow nerf," Ordinary Joe notes tooltip was updated to match the bug).
- **[general]** Buff-cap-related bugs: Stormstrike's buff no longer applies its Earth Shock/Lightning Strike bonus once at buff cap (used to work pre-patch), and Elemental Weapons WF stacking is worse near buff cap — [[d:shaman#1490903959970254918]], [[d:shaman#1491016083774115850]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-04-07).
- **[tank]** T3.5 3-piece set bonus (Stable Shields talent) appears to have gained an internal cooldown (~1 proc/sec) that wasn't announced; possibly a stealth nerf aimed at PvP burst, disputed as bug vs. intentional — [[d:shaman#1489370647816704150]], [[d:shaman#1489375467411734648]] (Mithreon/Not Normal, 2026-04-03; later partially retracted after a log showed two procs 0.1s apart — [[d:shaman#1489383547843383326]]).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[tank]** Shaman tank is viable through Kara40 and (per some) all raid content pre-Naxx/K40 with proper gear; considered the squishiest/most RNG-dependent tank due to poor dedicated itemization and the new Bulwark system — [[d:shaman#1487817545707946108]], [[d:shaman#1490265394454204457]], [[d:shaman#1490266497724055592]] (Krulltak/Mithreon, 2026-03-29/04-05; "our shaman tank... doesn't feel any difference from 1.18" with near-BiS gear).
- **[general]** "All shaman roles are viable" but viability ≠ optimal; enh, ele and resto are all noted as being hit by "systematic nerfs" that reduce their competitiveness even while remaining playable — [[d:shaman#1487818776643633243]], [[d:shaman#1487819861072543814]], [[d:shaman#1487829161379627169]] (Not Normal/Svargth, 2026-03-29; disputed by Not Normal who asks "what systematic nerfs?").
- **[enh]** Enhancement considered the strongest/least-buggy spec currently ("best spec RN because it has the least bugs") — [[d:shaman#1489898819624898600]] (Ciftar, 2026-04-03).
- **[resto]** Resto is "cooked"/most underdeveloped healer: role largely reduces to dropping totems and spamming Chain Heal; priests took over as the dominant AoE healer after a recent Holy Priest update — [[d:shaman#1489717659921813667]], [[d:shaman#1489898819624898600]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-04; "most ppl don't want to spam chainheal and be the worst healer").
- **[ele]** Elemental "needs a lot of gear to do something," feels weak/mana-hungry at moderate gear; enhancement is "stealing" elemental's itemization while already having more available than ele — [[d:shaman#1488197955599995034]], [[d:shaman#1489438383603449948]], [[d:shaman#1489597570102722824]] (Smitejr/Bnkyo/Kokuyo, 2026-03-30/04-04).

## Talent builds (endgame)
- **[resto]** Standard resto build: 5 points Ancestral Knowledge + 3 points Stable Shields in the Enhancement tree, then dump remaining points into Restoration; only Reincarnation and the Resto threat-reduction talent are skipped — [[d:shaman#1488188307899809832]], [[d:shaman#1489683338087235586]] (Not Normal, 2026-03-30/04-03; repeated/confirmed twice).
- **[tank]** Common endgame tank build referenced as "23/28 tank" with Improved Ghost Wolf substituted for Totemic Alignment (PvP variant); leveling/dungeon tank builds take Improved Fire Totems, Totemic Alignment, and skip/limit Shield Spec past level 40 in favor of Ancestral Knowledge — [[d:shaman#1490541764052779138]], [[d:shaman#1488877108905119804]] (oi-er/Thataintmilk, 2026-04-06/03-30; "the math has been done countless times").

## Leveling
- **[general]** Totem unlock levels: Earth from the start, Fire at 10, Water at 20, Air at 30; each element requires completing a class questline (the Air totem quest is called out as unusually long/annoying) — [[d:shaman#1488288472379949328]], [[d:shaman#1488289134689910955]] (Lionhermit/Spicyahi, 2026-03-31; +6 reactions).
- **[general]** With nampower installed, up to 4 totems (one per element) can be dropped via a single macro using 4 `/cast` lines — [[d:shaman#1488297140617875517]], [[d:shaman#1488673189386256545]] (Netharia/Not Normal, 2026-03-31/04-01).
- **[tank]** Level 26: Water Shield + Mana Stream Totem "make a HUGE difference" for dungeon tanking/mana. Level 33: Totem of the Tides (drop, Stormwrought dungeon) procs Water Shield constantly for AoE damage/threat, letting a tank stay on Water Shield instead of swapping to mana-costly Lightning Shield — [[d:shaman#1488873898115203122]], [[d:shaman#1488142383202959371]] (trallnar, 2026-03-30/29).
- **[enh/frost leveling]** Frostbrand + Frost Shock is the standout leveling combo once Elemental Weapons is taken: Frost Shock guarantees a Frostbrand auto-crit, which with Elemental Weapons is a 50% proc chance and grants free Clearcasting → near-zero mana downtime from level ~45; strongly preferred over Windfury for leveling once available — [[d:shaman#1487775668908589147]], [[d:shaman#1490747705037688872]], [[d:shaman#1490752657227518092]], [[d:shaman#1490754492419801138]] (Ordinary Joe/Not Normal/Hassle, 2026-03-29/04-06; multiple confirmations).
- **[leveling]** Proc chance is normalized, so slower weapons give better proc uptime; for leveling this barely matters since mobs die fast — [[d:shaman#1488640148642988154]], [[d:shaman#1488640353706705129]] (Ordinary Joe/Hassle, 2026-04-01).
- **[tank leveling]** Sample tank pull routine for dungeons (e.g., Wailing Caverns): mark kill target with skull, pull with Rank-1 Lightning Bolt + LOS to group ranged mobs, use Fire Nova + Stoneclaw Totem for AoE threat, conserve mana by not spamming Lightning Strike/Earth Shock once threat is set; Magma Totem is called a "huge mana drain" — [[d:shaman#1488885982047768770]] (trallnar, 2026-03-31).
- **[leveling zones]** Horde leveling route shared: Barrens ~12, Thousand Needles ~23, Balor ~29, Grim Reaches ~34 — [[d:shaman#1489666140748910643]] (trallnar, 2026-04-03; single source, imprecise on last zone).
- **[leveling]** Lasher farming (mobs, gold farm) can start around level 52-56 for shaman; if undergeared, spec partly into Resto for pushback resistance and run a shield/Stoneshield-type setup to self-heal through packs; Survival Hunters can start slightly earlier (45) — [[d:shaman#1488144215681663127]], [[d:shaman#1488144925869477908]] (Hassle, 2026-03-29).
- **[leveling]** Weapon choice for leveling tank changes often with new item power jumps between 1h/2h; damage-focused fast leveling favors 2h + windfury classic-style, or fast MH+shield+Elemental Weapons frost build for those who also want to tank — [[d:shaman#1488206084387897416]], [[d:shaman#1488105525206843423]] (trallnar/WobblySlug, 2026-03-30).
- **[leveling]** Int gives no threat/damage/tankiness and doesn't regen mana on its own — it's just mana pool; without mp5/spirit regen it's not very useful while leveling, Water Shield/pacing mana use matters more — [[d:shaman#1488141660893347981]], [[d:shaman#1488142139748782172]] (trallnar/Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-03-29).

## Rotation: single target
- **[enh]** Core enhancement priority: Stormstrike before Earth Shock always; shocks are otherwise a monolithic loop of Flame Shock → Earth Shock → Earth Shock repeat ("in all circumstances"); Flame Shock is used as a filler GCD while running in, not necessarily on cooldown-perfect timing — [[d:shaman#1489380594012918013]], [[d:shaman#1489381819735605390]], [[d:shaman#1489381188496916563]] (poto/Not Normal, 2026-04-03).
- **[enh]** Weighted priority given as "22/29 carrying the weight, ES > SS > LS, weave in fire totems as mana allows" (talent build reference 22/29 split) — [[d:shaman#1488601959559598201]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-03-31).
- **[tank]** Tank rotation: Lightning Strike, Earth Shock, Storm Strike spam with totems, cycling Lightning/Water Shield as mana allows; priority given elsewhere as Earth Shock > Storm Strike > Lightning Strike, refresh shields — [[d:shaman#1488136124617724004]], [[d:shaman#1489000281315475559]] (Boorenka/Mikoto Rin, 2026-03-30/04-01).
- **[ele]** Simmed elemental single-target priority: Flame Shock > Molten Blast (only to refresh Flame Shock) > Earthquake > Chain Lightning > Lightning Bolt (Earthquake is "DPS-neutral whether you press it or not except for AoE") — [[d:shaman#1490703901098250300]], [[d:shaman#1490703968576471180]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-04-06).
- **[enh]** A full-duration Flame Shock (dot) is claimed to out-DPS Earth Shock if timed correctly; ranking given as FS(dot) > ES > SS > LS is disputed/unclear, but general take is Earth Shock's only edge is that DoTs can't crit (irrelevant for physhance's low spell crit) — [[d:shaman#1489379406307791098]], [[d:shaman#1489379424867455168]] (poto/Not Normal, 2026-04-03).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[tank]** AoE threat generation: Stoneclaw Totem (not Stoneskin — corrected multiple times) for burst AoE threat, Fire Nova/Magma Totem for sustained AoE threat; Totemic Alignment talent boosts totem threat — [[d:shaman#1488862048069881857]], [[d:shaman#1488930476977819800]], [[d:shaman#1488930749573894226]] (Ordinary Joe/Dinousek, 2026-03-31).
- **[tank]** Improved Fire Totems (-50% threat on fire totems) is still recommended by most despite the threat penalty, because of the 2-second Fire Nova cooldown reduction and a decent single-target threat bonus from Searing Totem; total AoE threat is technically lower but tanks "don't really struggle with AoE threat anyway" — [[d:shaman#1489583151977074851]], [[d:shaman#1489583199905382490]], [[d:shaman#1489586957632671804]] (Ordinary Joe/Zamka, 2026-04-04; Zamka notes it's worse specifically "at the 9 second mark").
- **[tank]** Totem of the Tides is used mainly for AoE (procs on every hit while Water Shield is up, including on-hit effects like Sigil/Insomnius); it does less overall damage than Lightning Shield/Stonebreaker/Rage/Broken Earth for single target — [[d:shaman#1488155589258383410]], [[d:shaman#1488155669780762804]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-03-30).
- **[tank]** At 2+ targets, Totem of Tides (with Water Shield) beats Lightning Shield's single-target throughput split across targets; math given: Tides ~58 combined dps at 2 targets (25-33 frost dmg per proc, ~1/2s) vs Thundercaller totem ~40 dps split (20 each) — [[d:shaman#1489365192427966534]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-03).
- **[ele]** AoE totems (Magma/Fire Nova) scale poorly with spell power (low SP coefficients); they hit hard mostly from flat talent bonuses, and spell crit helps more than SP for AoE farming — [[d:shaman#1489305677687033907]], [[d:shaman#1489305737153871913]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-03).
- **[general]** Oil of Immolation is a cheap AoE damage cooldown worth using on double/triple pulls (e.g., lasher farming) — [[d:shaman#1489306615118172341]] (Zeruel, 2026-04-03).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[enh]** Rule of thumb: 1 STR ≈ 2 AGI in value for enhancement; AGI's value comes purely from crit (no AP from AGI), so at raid-buffed levels STR gives ~2.3 AP per point (with Kings) vs. AGI giving only crit — [[d:shaman#1487779517429710899]], [[d:shaman#1487779917075582996]], [[d:shaman#1488992585413558515]] (Ordinary Joe/Kyle Sussenbach, 2026-03-29/04-01).
- **[enh]** Weapon skill/hit: 5 weapon skill over 300 = 1% hit; hit cap is 8%. At 315 weapon skill + 3% hit from talents, ~6% hit needed from gear (numbers as stated by posters; note some confusion in-thread about how skill from quests interacts) — [[d:shaman#1488248016212332544]], [[d:shaman#1488253556829196459]], [[d:shaman#1489259512547250328]] (Rag/Hassle/Junak'Salsa, 2026-03-30/04-02; with weapon-skill quest to 305 skill, "you need 7% hit"; a Kargath quest gives 2% hit mail gloves).
- **[enh]** Orc's extra +3 axe skill from racial, plus quest (+5) and MC gloves (+7), gets orcs to weapon-skill cap using fewer gear slots than other races, who need 2 dedicated weapon-skill items (e.g. FGG, EQ Leggings) — both said to be well-statted regardless, so the DPS cost is "negligible" per Not Normal, disputed by Jinzio as a real setup-flexibility loss (e.g. for T3 3pc empowered-shield bonus) — [[d:shaman#1488664043743871188]], [[d:shaman#1488664557902631153]], [[d:shaman#1488665052255883364]] (Jinzio/Not Normal, 2026-04-01).
- **[tank]** Spell hit for tanks: Azure Flight + Insomnius, or Azure Flight + Seal of the Damned (3%, soft-capped), or Neltharion's Tear can reach soft spell-hit cap; multiple gear options exist by ES/BWL phase — [[d:shaman#1487831708878508054]], [[d:shaman#1487831886075003030]], [[d:shaman#1487832023157706922]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-03-29).
- **[tank]** Defense cap is unreachable in practice for shaman tanks; community consensus is to prioritize EHP (stam+armor) over defense/avoidance, though a moderate defense pickup is fine as long as it doesn't cost significant stam/dodge — [[d:shaman#1490404706437304341]], [[d:shaman#1490407432403419336]] (Xorlord/Zeruel, 2026-04-05).
- **[enh]** Haste stat weight: roughly 17-18 AP-equivalent value in pre-Naxx gear per Not Normal's sim; grows substantially with Kiss of the Spider (K40 trinket) + T3.5 5pc's 10% haste buff + heavy haste stacking ("hastemaxxing"), at which point 2% haste can approach or beat the Naxx shoulder enchant (26 AP + 1% crit) — [[d:shaman#1488973642187411602]], [[d:shaman#1489338042727534702]], [[d:shaman#1490438815847026858]] (Not Normal/Kyle Sussenbach, 2026-04-01/04); Slayer's Crest + STR-stacking may edge out haste-maxxing mathematically but the difference is small and haste "feels" better to many players — [[d:shaman#1489339000098525345]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-02).
- **[tank/enh relic]** Stonebreaker relic ≈ 30 raw dps, ~50% proc uptime if shocking on cooldown ≈ worth ~65 AP (comparable to a Crusader proc) — [[d:shaman#1488161234644631622]], [[d:shaman#1488162001049096303]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-03-29). Thundercaller totem (new Onyxia relic) beats Stonebreaker by ~5-7 dps at most gear levels but Stonebreaker edges ahead again at absolute top-end K40 BiS because Thundercaller scales with AP (which plateaus) while Stonebreaker synergizes with other stacked stats — [[d:shaman#1489364533209202749]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-03).
- **[weapon theory]** "Top end"/base weapon damage range is a poor value heuristic; weapon speed matters far more, since roughly 60% of actual melee damage comes from AP converted to damage via speed — at 2000-2500 AP (top gear levels), even 2000 AP alone yields ~143 weapon-dps, dwarfing the strongest weapon's own ~100 weapon-dps; this is why e.g. Fist of the Forgotten Order (slow, lower "top end") beats Aspect of Seradane, and Elementium Reaper is "significantly far ahead of every other option" for physhance on Ambershire (only Sulfuras beats it) — [[d:shaman#1493664161022869535]], [[d:shaman#1493664572870234216]], [[d:shaman#1493665210572079186]], [[d:shaman#1494337592064213102]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-14/16).
- **[enh]** Weapon skill value: each point of weapon skill below the 315 cap is worth roughly 14 AP at current Amber gear levels, so hit gained from over-capping (e.g. via extra weapon-skill gear when already hit-capped by other means) is worth proportionally less than its nominal % — [[d:shaman#1491837172561739907]], [[d:shaman#1491837276249002225]] (Zeruel/Not Normal, 2026-04-09). Ancient Jade Leggings are a more gear-efficient weapon-skill source than the crafted Towerforge mace; getting Towerforge just for 2 extra weapon-skill points is not an efficient use of a gear slot — [[d:shaman#1491836338562007223]], [[d:shaman#1491837060196339722]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-09).

- **[general]** Community-maintained BiS spreadsheets for Amber (tank mitigation/threat + enh dps) repeatedly updated and shared throughout the chunk by Boorenka — [[d:shaman#1489046160906780753]], [[d:shaman#1490512425605271643]], [[d:shaman#1492440547670098021]]. Ordinary Joe's community gear-planner/sim site (ichasarmory.quest) models most set bonuses (all but 6pc T3 as of writing) and includes a talent calculator — [[d:shaman#1491099460963401820]], [[d:shaman#1494381833390002297]] (2026-04-07/16).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[enh]** Weapon imbues always override totem versions of the same effect for the caster and do not stack with them; totems like Windfury Totem/Flametongue Totem are purely for party members' benefit while an imbue is active on the shaman's own weapon — confirmed repeatedly — [[d:shaman#1488975408689844235]], [[d:shaman#1493233532561002776]], [[d:shaman#1494609724447133829]], [[d:shaman#1494624457162096650]] (Ordinary Joe/Diving Instructor Knife/gastro, throughout).
- **[tank]** Not worth using spellpower weapon/gear enchants as a tank shaman: SP contributes to tank threat but "not nearly as good as for paladins"; better to enchant all-stats, agility, or stamina — [[d:shaman#1492143660685459497]], [[d:shaman#1492143795557372016]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-11).
- **[enh]** New/confirmed Flametongue and Frostbrand spellpower coefficients (from Not Normal's own testing, matching what eventually shipped): **FT = 0.16 + 0.03 × weapon speed**; **FB = 0.25 + 0.04 × weapon speed** — i.e., partly flat, partly weapon-speed-scaling, so faster weapons aren't fully "cucked" the way the old flat-10% model implied — [[d:shaman#1491208166766284860]], [[d:shaman#1491846849165004901]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-08/09). Frostbrand SP scaling given concretely as ~30% at 1.3 speed and ~39% at 3.6 speed — [[d:shaman#1491846954660139173]] (Ciftar, 2026-04-09).
- **[enh]** Frostbrand + Frost Shock is also viable outside leveling: decent for overworld farming/solo content at 60 before a full raid kit, and for Spellhance when a boss is fire-immune or frost-vulnerable (e.g. Chromaggus phases) — [[d:shaman#1494757737912074311]], [[d:shaman#1494773366316470394]] (Zeruel/Not Normal, 2026-04-17).
- **[enh]** Str/Agi enchants generally preferred over 2% haste except at near-BiS K40 hastemaxxed gear (consistent with the stat-weight note above); movement-speed boot enchant doesn't stack with Druid's Emerald Blessing buff (only the stronger single effect applies) so it's mainly useful outside 40-man raid contexts — [[d:shaman#1490438815847026858]], [[d:shaman#1490440451474325715]] (Kyle Sussenbach/Not Normal, 2026-04-05).
- **[tank]** Armor pen (from talents/items) is "ok, not great" for enhancement — roughly 1 STR ≈ 5 armor pen in value, not worth chasing out of your way — [[d:shaman#1494082990370721939]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-16).

## Consumables & world buffs
- **[general]** The 3 "Concoction" potions (SP-type) stack with each other, including their SP portions, and also stack with Dreamshard Elixir — [[d:shaman#1488759860949422140]], [[d:shaman#1488781116000436404]] (Xaln/Zappinu, 2026-03-31).

- **[enh]** Limited Invulnerability Potions (LIPs) recommended as an emergency threat-management tool for DPS: they don't fix threat but make you immune to (and untargetable by) physical damage briefly, buying time for the tank to catch up — [[d:shaman#1489322209888698508]], [[d:shaman#1489323335161614478]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-03).
- **[general]** Community-built consumables reference guide/database shared (incomplete but functional) — [[d:shaman#1491113709270208622]] (Jore, 2026-04-07; tinyurl.com/twowconsumes).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[tank]** Recommended AoE-threat hierarchy by cost/need (leveling and raid trash alike): Stoneclaw Totem + Totem of the Tides is baseline/"bread and butter"; add Nova Totem if the party AoEs heavily; Magma after Nova only as a last resort (heavy mana cost); Chain Lightning on pull is strong but costly — treat it as a bonus for ranged pulls, not mandatory — [[d:shaman#1492243908304830534]], [[d:shaman#1492243972607705210]], [[d:shaman#1492244223238476030]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-10; leveling tanks skip Improved Fire Totems since it's not yet available).
- **[tank]** Stoneclaw Totem does ~150 threat per pulse (every 1.5s, pre-modifiers), and with Totemic Alignment talents ~90% of totem threat is credited to the shaman and only ~10% to the totem itself — it "won't look like it's doing anything" without a threat/damage meter — [[d:shaman#1491156093550989312]], [[d:shaman#1491173915328774334]] (Ordinary Joe/trallnar, 2026-04-07/08; corrects an earlier overstated "450 threat/sec" claim).
- **[general]** Windfury (imbue or totem) cannot trigger while Rockbiter is active as your weapon imbue — imbues are mutually exclusive and Rockbiter blocks WF from proccing entirely, a recurring point of confusion — [[d:shaman#1493233112308650017]], [[d:shaman#1493233224825176195]], [[d:shaman#1493233571391864983]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-04-13).
- **[ele]** Elemental Mastery is broadly considered "criminally bad": only 10s duration on a 3-min cooldown for a 15% damage buff, making it barely better than a filler talent; repeated community suggestion to extend duration to ~20s or reduce cooldown to ~60-90s (to synergize with the T3.5 5pc buff window) — [[d:shaman#1491955264189104312]], [[d:shaman#1491956539383349371]], [[d:shaman#1491962050971304040]] (Not Normal/Kokuyo, 2026-04-10).
- **[travel]** Shaman travel-ability levels: Ghost Wolf at 20, Astral Recall at 30 (15-minute cooldown, very useful for returning to quest hubs), mount at 40 — [[d:shaman#1492149412686725230]], [[d:shaman#1492149559739285627]] (trallnar, 2026-04-11).
- **[enh]** Molten Blast refreshes Flame Shock to its full max duration (adds time = max duration − remaining, preserving next-tick timing, no talent required); base full duration is 15s, extended +3s by a Zul'Farrak-sourced totem item and +6s by a set bonus — [[d:shaman#1492512142899937351]], [[d:shaman#1492512356406919269]], [[d:shaman#1492517323838460035]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-04-11).

## Tanking specifics
- **[tank]** Earthen Bulwark mechanics (as currently bugged): the absorb shield starts every combat at 0% and can only be built from basic melee **auto-attack** damage — it should also build from Storm Strike and the physical portion of Lightning Strike per its own tooltip, but currently doesn't; caps at 20% of max HP; loses **all** stacks at once (not gradually) after 8 seconds without a landed autoattack; cannot be built at range or while unable to melee — [[d:shaman#1493187800193957978]], [[d:shaman#1493215880765509722]], [[d:shaman#1493309560428171430]], [[d:shaman#1493310658203091165]] (LLVE/Nandieb/Not Normal, 2026-04-13; confirmed as a genuine bug, not intended, by Not Normal, and left unfixed for 3+ weeks — "inexcusable," per Not Normal — [[d:shaman#1493387191328571602]]).
- **[tank]** Real-world impact of the bug is heavily gear/fight dependent: Zamka reports MTing all of K40 without issue, taking <4k crits from Mephistroth and even solo-tanking "Anomalous," while others (Mikoto Rin, Nandieb, T@B@) report serious trouble holding Bulwark uptime on hard-hitting bosses (Chromaggus, Nefarian, BWL drakonids) even in full mitigation gear because threat is a non-issue but sustained melee autoattack damage isn't — [[d:shaman#1492702400383942928]], [[d:shaman#1493215880765509722]], [[d:shaman#1490241820980940912]] (2026-04-05/12/13; unresolved, gear/fight dependent).
- **[tank]** Because Bulwark currently only cares about autoattack landing, faster weapons (that land more autoattacks) can out-perform traditionally "better" slow tank weapons for keeping Bulwark up; some tanks (Boorenka) deliberately swap toward faster-swing weapons like Spineshatter for this reason, in tension with the general SS/LS-focused advice to use the slowest weapon possible — [[d:shaman#1493257991397572819]], [[d:shaman#1493255869973004348]] (Boorenka/Nandieb, 2026-04-13).
- **[tank]** Sample math on Bulwark's cap: a raid tank with 8k unbuffed HP can stack Bulwark up to ~1.6k; average melee autoattack ≈630 dmg, crit ≈1.2k, so higher per-hit damage (AP/crit stacking) fills the shield faster per hit while attack speed fills it more often — both are valid approaches to keep it topped up — [[d:shaman#1493187800193957978]], [[d:shaman#1493186300541403267]] (LLVE/Nandieb, 2026-04-13).
- **[tank]** The Bulwark/Rockbiter rework was introduced specifically to nerf a dominant pre-1.18.1 PvP build — a 1h+shield "shockaman"/spellhance-shield spec using Rockbiter (which buffs Earth Shock via AP) to spam tanky 2-3k Earth Shocks — rather than to target Enhancement DPS or PvE tanking directly; this is disputed by Nandieb (who says it was in practice largely an Elemental-with-enhancement-talents hybrid, and that the "collateral damage" to real tanks/enhance wasn't necessary) against oi-er/Zeruel (who cite the devs' own stated target as non-enhance specs using Rockbiter) — [[d:shaman#1493377700335980556]], [[d:shaman#1493376776418754611]], [[d:shaman#1493379194720358490]] (2026-04-13/14; unresolved).
- **[tank]** Weapon choice for tanking: prefer weapons slower than 2.0s speed (ideally close to 3.0s) since Storm Strike/Lightning Strike use weapon damage; block is considered much weaker mitigation than dodge/parry for shaman tanks due to poor block-value itemization on mail gear — [[d:shaman#1488258513531830490]], [[d:shaman#1488259059412107304]], [[d:shaman#1494780356904095845]] (Hassle/Zeruel, 2026-03-30/04-17).
- **[tank]** Def cap is unreachable for shaman tanks in practice; EHP (stam + armor) is prioritized over avoidance/defense, though moderate defense is fine if it doesn't cost much stam/dodge — [[d:shaman#1490404706437304341]], [[d:shaman#1490407432403419336]] (Xorlord/Zeruel, 2026-04-05; reiterated throughout).
- **[tank]** Enhancing Totems talent makes Stoneskin Totem increase other tanks' block amount by 30%, making it a viable support totem specifically in double-shaman-tank groups (otherwise Stoneskin is widely considered weak/"blows") — [[d:shaman#1493246348177309846]], [[d:shaman#1493259643546238976]] (Ordinary Joe/Hopestotem, 2026-04-12/13).
- **[tank]** Sample early-leveling tank AoE pull routine (RFC/WC-level dungeons): mark kill target, pull at range, use Stoneclaw+Fire Nova for burst threat, watch mana closely since threat/mana balance is the main challenge before Water Shield (26) and Totem of the Tides (33) — [[d:shaman#1488885982047768770]] (trallnar, 2026-03-31; see also Leveling section).

## Healing specifics
- **[resto]** Resto's raid role largely reduces to dropping totems and spamming Chain Heal (rank 1); low-rank (4-5) Healing Wave used for single-target top-ups, Lesser Healing Wave for fast emergency heals when no cooldown is available — [[d:shaman#1489717659921813667]], [[d:shaman#1490128865564037250]], [[d:shaman#1489450784704823316]] (Not Normal/Hassle, 2026-03-30/04-04).
- **[resto]** Haste is resto's strongest per-point stat for raw healing output, worth roughly 12-20 effective HP per 1% (more valuable the more healing power you already have); stat weights for healing are inherently context-dependent — spiky/melee-heavy content (e.g. AQ40) makes haste feel especially valuable for fast burst response — [[d:shaman#1491509951838031992]], [[d:shaman#1491510178921582713]], [[d:shaman#1491518087168790678]] (Not Normal/Rembo, 2026-04-08).
- **[resto]** T2.5 3-piece set bonus (mp5 scaling per Water Shield charge) is very weak — roughly 2 extra mp5 total from the 3pc's +2 Water Shield charges (4% × ~50mp5 base), compared to paladin's flat +57 Healing 2pc — widely cited as an example of TWoW's inconsistent set-bonus balance for shaman — [[d:shaman#1489728720888529208]], [[d:shaman#1489729839027064965]] (Bezawit/Not Normal, 2026-04-04).
- **[resto]** T0.5 (starter tier) 4-piece proc rate for resto is reportedly low (~3.5%), disputed against an anecdotal claim it has "good uptime" — unconfirmed — [[d:shaman#1494049085466873989]], [[d:shaman#1494050287038759073]] (Nandieb/Not Normal, 2026-04-16).
- **[healing addons]** Recommended healing-QoL addons: Healmate or Decursive for macro/raid-frame healing, Puppeteer for pseudo click-casting (click-to-target without needing a separate cast macro; also supports mouseover and a debuff-tracking config), Luna and pfUI raid frames for native mouseover-cast support — [[d:shaman#1494243224934551642]], [[d:shaman#1494245910224572416]], [[d:shaman#1494246656034603008]] (Ionnatra/Mithreon, 2026-04-17).
- **[general]** Boss-specific healing note: on Solnius (Emerald Sanctum hardmode), dispelling or Tremor-Totem-ing the normal 60% attack/cast-speed debuff instead re-applies a stronger "Emerald Rot" debuff (80%); Tremor is fine (and needed) only against the add-whelps' sleep effect — [[d:shaman#1491585347228926092]], [[d:shaman#1491595081562456084]], [[d:shaman#1491595432965701825]], [[d:shaman#1491708850120822925]] (Bezawit/Ryuuhei/Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-04-09).

- **[tank]** Amber tank logs cited: one Chromaggus MT log showed 91% Bulwark uptime, while another tank (ThatsTheJoke) reported closer to ~80% across two attempts — both still below what would be expected once the SS/LS-building bug is fixed — [[d:shaman#1493389625421594714]], [[d:shaman#1493392747636461749]] (Not Normal/ThatsTheJoke, 2026-04-13).
- **[tank]** Zamka reports solo-tanking "Anomalous" in Kara40 successfully, impressing the raid, and MTing K40 broadly with sub-4k crits from Mephistroth in appropriate EHP gear (10.5k max HP set) — [[d:shaman#1488997508976873473]], [[d:shaman#1492703034747130048]] (2026-04-01/12).
- **[general]** Nightmare Dragons (summonable world bosses) give the best available nature resist gear for AQ, including a guaranteed-drop ring (Malfurion's Signet, from a quest item) and a cloak — [[d:shaman#1492069792633327699]], [[d:shaman#1492069882307547166]] (Frost, 2026-04-11).
- **[tank]** MC/Rag tanking rough entry benchmark cited by one poster: ~8k armor, 5k HP unbuffed, 120 fire resist (with fire totem), ~+30 defense, deemed likely sufficient — [[d:shaman#1489054853899620542]], [[d:shaman#1489072008456310895]] (Hobo/Boorenka, 2026-04-01; single source estimate, "bear-boss test" — if you can tank the last boss of BM you can probably tank Rag).
- **[leveling]** Lasher farming (Dire Maul East, "DME") remains a popular shaman gold-farm from ~level 52-56 onward using Totem of Tides + resto pushback-resistance talents if undergeared; ~5 lockouts done in about 30 minutes, herb nodes inside give incidental extra gold — [[d:shaman#1488144156000911462]], [[d:shaman#1489289875076415550]] (Diving Instructor Knife/trallnar, 2026-03-29/04-01).
- **[leveling]** Stormwrought Keep/Castle (in Balor) is the source of Totem of the Tides, considered a huge power spike for leveling tanks; Maraudon's Princess boss drops a rare (~1%) epic dagger with a mana-refund proc enabling near-infinite mana for lasher-style AoE farming — [[d:shaman#1492245570935324722]], [[d:shaman#1489289875076415550]] (Not Normal/trallnar, 2026-04-03/10-01).

## PvP
- **[pvp/tank]** The Bulwark/Rockbiter rework's origin: it targeted a dominant pre-1.18.1 PvP build — 1h+shield with Rockbiter imbue (which buffs Earth Shock via AP) spamming 2-3k Earth Shocks while tanky, popularized in PvP videos (referred to as "shockaman"). Community dispute over whether this build was really "Enhancement" or an Elemental-hybrid using enhancement talents, and whether nerfing it needed to also collaterally weaken real tank/PvE-Enhance Bulwark generation — [[d:shaman#1493376776418754611]], [[d:shaman#1493377700335980556]], [[d:shaman#1493383526895059097]] (Nandieb/oi-er, 2026-04-13/14).
- **[pvp]** Resilience (new PvP stat) is reported to functionally cap crit damage down close to normal-hit damage ("resil basically turns crits into normal hits"), sharply reducing burst; this is cited as making paladin/shaman healers much harder to kill, with repeated community calls (mainly from Diving Instructor Knife) for an added ~30% PvP healing-dampening specifically on Paladin and Shaman healers, on the view that 1.18.0 felt balanced but 1.18.1's resistance/dampening changes overtuned them relative to Priest/Druid — [[d:shaman#1492727874082570391]], [[d:shaman#1494616303908950157]], [[d:shaman#1494668079047774320]] (Hopestotem/Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-04-11/17; extended, largely unresolved flame-war-style debate with no consensus).
- **[enh pvp]** 2h+Windfury is reported significantly weaker in PvP than in raids because PvP lacks the AP-buff/consumable stack of a 40-man raid (WF is essentially a multiplier on existing AP); many PvP enhancers use Rockbiter instead at lower (Amber/BWL) gear levels for its AP boost, Empowered Lightning Shield synergy, and Earth Shock buff — [[d:shaman#1492226642452549642]], [[d:shaman#1492231612283486312]] (Zeruel/Not Normal, 2026-04-12). Windfury damage is also heavily reduced against high-armor targets unless using an item like Shieldrender's Talisman — [[d:shaman#1492231612283486312]] (Not Normal, 2026-04-12).
- **[enh pvp]** To land big WF crits, stack crit chance aggressively (agility enchants, crit gear) to guarantee at least 2 crits per WF window; one poster reports ~34-38% crit (with GoA) on Naxx-level gear as the target range — [[d:shaman#1492241005557317773]], [[d:shaman#1492241601203011766]], [[d:shaman#1492241976127393842]] (Barnyard, 2026-04-11).
- **[general pvp]** Traditional PvE-style Enhancement play ("a warrior in mail who casts sometimes") is not good in PvP per Not Normal: no mobility, physical damage is easily mitigated/avoided, and enhance has no real CC — Spellhance (spellpower/Flametongue-based) is called a genuinely strong, distinct PvP spec by multiple posters (Ciftar, Spicyahi) — [[d:shaman#1492232263952236777]], [[d:shaman#1493380140057628715]] (Not Normal/Spicyahi, 2026-04-12/14).
- **[general pvp]** Rank/honor economy: Rank 6 needed to enter the "Hall of Legends" vendor; weekly honor cap is 20k (~20 hours of play to fill); the underlying honor-value numbers were rebalanced this patch (a "0" removed from costs/values) — [[d:shaman#1491797398027243662]], [[d:shaman#1491797732728373352]] (Porkins, 2026-04-08).
- **[pvp weapon]** Earthshaker (stun proc mace) is popular for PvP — can near-permanently chain-stun combined with Windfury and a Tauren war-stomp — [[d:shaman#1492265069092802590]], [[d:shaman#1492266580640272445]], [[d:shaman#1492271640388702218]] (Rocked/Boorenka/Zeruel, 2026-04-11). Sulfuras remains strong until Naxx-tier gear for either PvE or PvP.
- **[healing pvp]** Resto in PvP is reported strong specifically due to Earth Shield enabling face-tanking without an interrupt, plus interrupt-duration reduction from set bonuses; but is described as easy to "tunnel" and lock out if focused, with only one hard-cast heal every 3 minutes as a safety valve otherwise — [[d:shaman#1494663845619437609]], [[d:shaman#1494671492556918784]] (Bipst, 2026-04-17).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[tank, major]** Earthen Bulwark builds only from basic melee **autoattacks**, not from Storm Strike or the physical portion of Lightning Strike as its tooltip implies it should — confirmed as a genuine bug (not intended design) by Not Normal, and left unfixed for 3+ weeks of the patch despite heavy community pressure and multiple "fixed in this hotfix" notes that never actually shipped — [[d:shaman#1493309560428171430]], [[d:shaman#1493387191328571602]], [[d:shaman#1490007576413470720]] (throughout April; devs sarcastically described by players as saying "Earthen Bulwark is working as intended" — [[d:shaman#1493513096432586753]]).
- **[general]** Flametongue/Frostbrand spellpower coefficient changes were announced in the original 1.18.1 patch notes but not actually implemented for roughly the first 3 weeks of the patch; Not Normal derived and published the eventual real formulas (FT = 0.16+0.03×speed, FB = 0.25+0.04×speed) from testing before the fix even landed — [[d:shaman#1488705173353136340]], [[d:shaman#1491208166766284860]] (throughout).
- **[bug]** Flametongue Totem (FTT) briefly and erroneously inherited the new FT-weapon spellpower coefficient with no falloff, causing absurd scaling for high-spellpower classes standing near it (e.g. Ret Paladins wielding Sulfuras hitting huge FTT numbers); reported fixed by 2026-04-16 — [[d:shaman#1491212596819529851]], [[d:shaman#1493738414300991518]], [[d:shaman#1494356331543396445]] (Not Normal/Zeruel, 2026-04-08/14/16).
- **[bug]** Elemental Weapons' Windfury bonus haste stacking remained bugged as late as 2026-04-11 (should give 2 stacks per WF proc, mostly gives only 1) — [[d:shaman#1492305342195957842]], [[d:shaman#1492306106947735743]] (Mylan/Not Normal).
- **[bug]** Buff-cap interactions: at buff cap, Stormstrike's hidden Earth Shock/Lightning Strike damage-buff stopped applying (worked correctly pre-patch); Ordinary Joe documented concrete before/after numbers (Earth Shock crits capped ~1948-2000 at buff cap vs. 2.5k+ when under cap) across several K40 boss logs — [[d:shaman#1491016083774115850]], [[d:shaman#1491023935977033728]], [[d:shaman#1491024515046834266]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-04-07; unresolved as of writing, no dev confirmation found in-chunk).
- **[bug]** One tank (rel) reported threat output dropping ~50-66% overnight with unchanged gear/consumes, resolved after a relog; Not Normal found no other corroborating reports or known threat-related patch changes and concluded it was likely a client/session-side fluke — [[d:shaman#1492668110891913458]], [[d:shaman#1492671061987295282]] (2026-04-12; single source, resolved without explanation).
- **[addon bug]** TotemTimers (Turtle WoW) addon suspected (unconfirmed) of causing shaman-specific client crashes for one user — [[d:shaman#1490650458497355779]] (Machusetas, 2026-04-06; single source).
