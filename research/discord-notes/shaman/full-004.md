## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Tank/Enh]** Rockbiter Weapon threat generation reworked: used to give a fixed threat amount per hit (favoring fast weapons), now gives a flat 35% bonus, making slow weapons better for tank/threat — [[d:shaman#1444609987270938768]] (OmniSalat, 2025-11-29).
- **[General]** Ghost Wolf form no longer allows herbing while active ("ghost-herbing"); worked as recently as Aug/Sept 2025, broken/patched since — [[d:shaman#1444080698834292836]] (Keldjor, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444081664350359653]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444133937638015186]] (Keldjor, 2025-11-29).
- **[Resto/utility]** Totem range talent (bonus totem range) was reworked; 30-yard totem range is now baseline for everyone, not something you need to talent — [[d:shaman#1444553826958053416]] (Zhafu, 2025-11-29).
- **[Fire Ele/Spellhance]** Devs have stated Fire Elemental/Molten Blast will be nerfed in the next patch; current Molten-Blast-snapshot flame shock cheese "will not be doing that much damage on next update" — [[d:shaman#1444807013165961277]] (peter_hammer, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444807359296831611]] (peter_hammer, 2025-11-28; original dev post deleted but archived at message 1411765855888478329).
- **[Fire Ele]** Community expects the nerf to target Elemental Weapons' interaction with Molten Blast specifically (e.g., excluding MB from the elemental-weapons bonus, or a short CD on MB) rather than a flat fire damage cut — [[d:shaman#1445291276693016636]] (billienomates, 2025-12-02).
- **[Frost imbue]** Devs (Haaxor1689) have said a buff to Frostbrand is planned; community calc shows it currently needs its damage coefficient raised from 10% to 40%+ to be competitive with Flametongue — [[d:shaman#1445293004842729634]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-02), [[d:shaman#1445308809609478176]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-02; confirmed by Haaxor1689 himself elsewhere in channel).
- **[Healing]** Chain Heal's jump targeting (bouncing to random players/pets instead of prioritizing lowest HP) is believed to be an intentional nerf rather than a bug, despite an open bug report claiming otherwise — [[d:shaman#1445104273833721946]] (Adonnys, 2025-12-01; question), [[d:shaman#1445114215588171826]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-01), open bug thread [[d:shaman#1445135042538897530]] (Adonnys, 2025-12-01).
- **[Enh/Tank]** Windfury Weapon imbue has no internal cooldown; Windfury Totem does have an ICD — [[d:shaman#1445538618981486662]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-03).
- **[Upcoming]** Next patch (Mograine questline) will add Corrupted Doomhammer and a +7 str boots enchant option for shamans — [[d:shaman#1445006340287107224]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-02).
- **[Enh/Fire]** A December 8-ish patch fixed Flametongue Weapon's damage formula (was miscalculated; fix is a near-wash, e.g. ~350 SP went from 229 to ~225 FT proc damage) and fixed the Earthfury (T1) 5pc set bonus, which previously only boosted Flametongue's base damage instead of its total damage after spellpower scaling — now applies to the full post-SP damage, a small net buff for FT-focused (spellhance) gearing — [[d:shaman#1448153121615384577]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-10), [[d:shaman#1448334971293929503]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-10).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Enhance]** Enhance is regarded as one of the best shaman specs currently; shaman DPS overall is considered fine and competitive unless badly undergeared relative to the raid — [[d:shaman#1444521407890587779]] (Squid, +1, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444395532750684312]] (billienomates, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444509882555432971]] (Zhafu, 2025-11-29).
- **[Enhance: classical Str 2h vs Spellhance]** Classical Str/2h Enhance has clearly better single-target DPS; the newer Spellhance build (hybrid-statted gear + spellpower + flametongue weapon, only viable since the latest patch) trades single-target for much stronger AoE. Not Normal estimates ~1500 patchwerk-dps ceiling is achievable for spellhance (roughly marksman-hunter tier), and reports hitting ~1250 dps himself in sub-BiS AQ40/early-Naxx gear — [[d:shaman#1444480213420609698]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444480639800709233]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444490987828150466]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444491421804531935]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-29).
- **[Enhance]** Disputed: Gurdaz argues Str-enhance is at the bottom of raid DPS meters without top-tier gear (Kiss of the Spider, MoM, Sharteth) and posts a turtlogs parse as evidence; Not Normal disputes the conclusion, arguing gear-level/skill differences (not the spec) explain the gap, and that his own guild's enh shamans "compete much more closely" than Gurdaz's log shows — [[d:shaman#1444327582991515699]] (Gurdaz, 2025-11-29, turtlogs link), [[d:shaman#1444351070963765379]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-29), disputed by [[d:shaman#1444356621449826445]] (Gurdaz, 2025-11-29).
- **[Tank]** Tank shaman is a versatile, competitive dungeon/off-tank and can main-tank; strong AoE tanking via fire totems (but needs SP investment, unlike other tanks' unresistable AoE threat) and low-variance damage from high armor + Rockbiter's damage reduction; two viable builds — 2h agi/crit (dodge+threat) and sword-and-board str/parry/block — [[d:shaman#1444299877151412317]] (peter_hammer, 2025-11-28).
- **[Tank]** Shaman tank's niche: arguably best dungeon tank at 60, one of the best AoE tanks, probably best vs caster bosses, best snap/early threat currently on Ambershire, but not best vs hard-hitting physical bosses (lower innate mitigation than bear/warrior/paladin) — [[d:shaman#1444300176104886374]] (Nandieb, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444301267076780102]] (Nandieb, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444301564771827782]] (Nandieb, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444608951974105199]] (Nandieb, 2025-11-29; "best for dungeons/small raids like UBRS/Kara10").
- **[Tank]** "Shaman is the best tank on ambershire right now" per multiple posters, but riskier for undergeared/mixed pug raids since lower mitigation can bite back if healers/DPS are weak — [[d:shaman#1445695870828941364]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1444611905414103162]] (Nandieb, 2025-11-28).
- **[Enhance leveling]** Enhance does not feel weak at any leveling stage per multiple posters, including after level 30 (contra reddit claims) — [[d:shaman#1444283786098638958]] (isleden, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444286342480662569]] (Ciftar, 2025-11-29).
- **[Spellhance]** Two spellhance sub-builds exist: a "2h route" (Not Normal) and a Hand-of-Edward-the-Odd 1h+SP-shield route (Ciftar); both are viable through Naxx and neither falls far behind the other, though 2h scales single-target slightly better while HoE plays more caster-like (instant procs + occasional hardcast) vs 2h's more melee-like feel. Both still trail classical (physhance) enhance in single-target and are best saved for post-BWL/AQ40 due to abundant fire-immune bosses pre-AQ — [[d:shaman#1448361486551613503]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-09), [[d:shaman#1450871639414669465]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-16; "aoe, boss scenarios with aoe"), [[d:shaman#1450876767341379675]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-16). Not Normal cautions against recommending spellhance to anyone not into deep theorycrafting, as of end of chunk it's "not established enough" with no solid knowledgebase yet — [[d:shaman#1452097226003185835]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-21).
- **[Elemental]** "Coming online" for Elemental is disputed: one view ties it to reaching ~30%+ unbuffed-consumed crit on Lightning spells so you stop needing to downrank Lightning Bolt to avoid oom; another reports doing fine (consistently top-5 dps, never downranking) with only 13% hit / 26% lightning crit — Zhafu separately says there's no special elemental gear-spike beyond normal caster scaling. A different framing says Ele mainly needs to clear the mana hurdle via a dungeon set (e.g. T0.5, "just need dg4 or 6/8") rather than needing tons of raid gear — [[d:shaman#1448505626094342165]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-12-11), disputed by [[d:shaman#1448586544528101458]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-11), [[d:shaman#1448484659385335938]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-11), [[d:shaman#1452083125898182780]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-12-21), contra [[d:shaman#1451221941804470455]] (Kokuyo, 2025-12-19; "nature ele shaman is lacking of dmg").
- **[Raid utility]** Enhance is generally always wanted in a raid over Elemental for its group support (Enhancing Totems, Bloodlust) even at similar personal dps; spec choice for pure dps purposes mostly doesn't matter ("whatever pumps") except where a fight favors specific utility — [[d:shaman#1449513815338127542]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-13), [[d:shaman#1449516894909366404]] (Psyker, 2025-12-13). Shaman is reported as the least-populated class on at least one server and correspondingly in high demand for a raid spot regardless of spec — [[d:shaman#1451961967987392613]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-20).

## Leveling
- **[Resto]** Leveling as Resto solo is a poor choice (3s cast Lightning Bolt, no melee skills); only makes sense if leveling exclusively via dungeons since talent choice barely affects leveling healing capability anyway — [[d:shaman#1445171470702481458]] (Dwi, 2025-12-02), [[d:shaman#1445171744309776405]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-02), [[d:shaman#1445171843165196478]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-02).
- **[Resto/Enhance]** Safe to switch to full Resto at level 52+ (once you can safely run Hateforge Quarry/early Blackrock Depths) and level 52-60 by farming pre-raid BiS gear via dungeons + rested XP — [[d:shaman#1444438508100915261]] (Dwi, 2025-11-30).
- **[Enhance]** Can flex into tank spec while leveling enhance to get faster dungeon queues; a bit squishy until ~level 20-25, solid past that — [[d:shaman#1445172830290448434]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-01), [[d:shaman#1445173013552169202]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-01).
- **[Enhance/HC]** For hardcore leveling, no special build needed — go Enhance and just be careful with pulls — [[d:shaman#1444428632293511390]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-11-30).
- **[Leveling gear]** Weapon speed doesn't matter much for a leveling upgrade unless it's a big (~10-level) jump, per dev Haaxor1689 — [[d:shaman#1445396502066499656]] (Haaxor1689, 2025-12-03).
- **[Leveling gear, tank/enh]** While leveling, prioritize stamina and the highest-armor shield available over armor-piece quality; the shield does most of the mitigation work at low level — [[d:shaman#1445870346208153690]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-04), [[d:shaman#1445870536151404674]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-04).
- **[Mana]** Elemental Weapons talent + Clearcasting largely solve early leveling mana problems, described as available "so late" (~level 42) — [[d:shaman#1444093603084505168]] (Kekkonen, 2025-11-29).
- **[Tank leveling]** Tanking is rough before level 26 (before Magma Totem and Water Shield); early tankable dungeons like Wailing Caverns have mobs highly resistant to Earth Shock, so it's recommended to level normally until 26 then start tanking in Shadowfang Keep — [[d:shaman#1448921448990244896]] (Koae, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1448921578992832714]] (Koae, 2025-12-08). Stoneclaw Totem alone can out-AoE-threat Magma Totem at low level, and at low level there's little else needed as long as DPS focuses your primary target — [[d:shaman#1449018291744932002]] (billienomates, 2025-12-08). Dragonmaw Retreat is tankable around level 30 with a decent shield (a Shadowfang Keep or Marbled Buckler shield from RFK helps); shaman tanking's "real weakness" is needing to stay on top of gear more than other tank classes — [[d:shaman#1449019965184413788]] (sailorVEVO, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1449020349814542447]] (billienomates, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1449020532153385052]] (billienomates, 2025-12-08).
- **[Leveling imbue]** Rockbiter is the best solo-leveling weapon choice roughly levels 1-30 (Rockbiter-boosted Lightning Shield hits get +25% of AP as bonus damage); Rockbiter/Windfury are both decent from 30 onward; then around level 32 (1 point in Elemental Weapons + Stormstrike), dip into the Elemental tree until Clearcasting and switch to Frostbrand — with Elemental Weapons, Frostbrand auto-crits vs a Frost-Shocked target, giving guaranteed Clearcasting procs for mana efficiency plus solid damage; this is a leveling-only strategy, switch back to Windfury at 60 for group content — [[d:shaman#1446973252227432749]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-07), [[d:shaman#1446973528061644823]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-07), [[d:shaman#1446973636299985109]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-07), confirmed [[d:shaman#1451938790007636121]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-19; "for leveling frostbrand is much better than wf, it's only on a geared 60 where wf outscales it"). Rockbiter should never be used as a raid/group dps choice at endgame (it doesn't scale like Windfury does) — [[d:shaman#1446984340251607271]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-07).
- **[Leveling imbue math]** A shaman running Rockbiter + Windfury Totem + Flametongue Totem simultaneously (2 totem "auras") still does less damage than simply using a Windfury weapon imbue alone: WF imbue gives +2 extra-attack swings with bonus AP per proc, while WF Totem only grants 1 extra attack with no bonus AP, and Flametongue Totem's flat damage is too small to close the gap — [[d:shaman#1451881425463808100]] (tinosheep, 2025-12-19), [[d:shaman#1451883523861647360]] (Gurdaz, 2025-12-19).
- **[1h+shield leveling]** Once you have Elemental Weapons + tank talents, 1h+shield with Frostbrand can solo almost any quest and only costs "next to no damage" for a real armor/mitigation gain, since Frostbrand doesn't scale with weapon speed the way Windfury's extra attacks do — disputed by a later poster who says Frostbrand actually is proc-per-minute (PPM) based, so slower weapons (2h) get a higher per-swing proc chance and thus more procs overall once Stormstrike/Lightning Strike procs are included — [[d:shaman#1449877412119838750]] (billienomates, 2025-12-13), [[d:shaman#1449877607696044083]] (billienomates, 2025-12-13), disputed by [[d:shaman#1450019263682445332]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-12-13). 1h+shield synergizes well with Rockbiter for reliably tackling tough elites (extra ~10% damage reduction from Elemental Weapons while Rockbiter is active) — [[d:shaman#1450019328169738361]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-12-13).
- **[Leveling races]** Detailed racial breakdown for leveling/endgame: Tauren is BiS for dungeon tanking (War Stomp) and pure mitigation (5% more total health, plus reportedly a stronger attack-speed debuff than Thunderfury); Orc comfortably reaches 315 axe skill (best for Ambershire raid tanking with Edgemaster's Handguards/Expert Goldminer's Helmet + Deathbringer mainhand pre-AQ40), and Orc's threat/healing-reduction downside on its racial was reduced from 50% to 25%, making it a reasonable risk/reward threat cooldown; Troll scales very well with Bloodlust-focused builds (Flurry+Bloodlust+Berserking+Kiss of the Spider haste stacks multiplicatively) and has the highest base agility, giving a slim edge for "uncrushable"-avoidance tank builds — [[d:shaman#1450022654814978079]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-12-13). Orc's Blood Fury now also buffs spellpower (not just AP), pulling it further ahead for PvE — [[d:shaman#1451971357540745408]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-20). For resto specifically, Troll is "technically best" mainly for the haste from Berserking, though its real healing value is debated (a burst-haste racial doesn't map cleanly onto sustained healing efficiency) — [[d:shaman#1451974093414076426]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-20), [[d:shaman#1452004340381384787]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-20; "none of the racials make a huge difference either way, but troll's def the best among them for heals").

## Rotation: single target
- **[Fire Ele/Spellhance]** Fire rotation: Searing Totem → Flame Shock → Molten Blast spam; use as many cooldowns/trinkets as possible at pull to snapshot the Flame Shock DoT (Rekindled Flame damage is a multiplier of the DoT), refresh the DoT via a trinket proc (e.g. Totem of Rage) to keep it up all fight; requires Elemental Weapons talent and Flametongue Weapon imbue (no oils) — [[d:shaman#1444787973378932898]] (Affinity, 2025-12-01), [[d:shaman#1444803730057728011]] (Dwi, 2025-12-01), [[d:shaman#1444807013165961277]] (peter_hammer, 2025-11-28).
- **[Spellhance 2h]** Hard-casting Molten Blast generally isn't worth it in a standard 2h-spellhance rotation; only worthwhile for Hand of Edward/OBD-style gimmick builds, which are considered a separate playstyle entirely — [[d:shaman#1444491226190450860]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-29).
- **[Enhance, classical]** Enhance almost never casts Molten Blast — [[d:shaman#1446548956262695144]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-04).
- **[Tank]** Use Water Shield through the bulk of a fight for mana sustain; Lightning Shield sometimes used briefly at pull for extra threat before swapping to Water Shield; Earth Shield is essentially useless for a tank (150 healing/4s vs 1-2k+ raid boss hits) — [[d:shaman#1444651606842015845]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-01), [[d:shaman#1445533926499024946]] (poto, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445538289376559134]] (poto, 2025-12-03).
- **[Tank]** Hard-casting while being hit is bad — it drops your dodge/block/parry to zero for the cast duration, greatly increasing damage taken; avoid hardcasting under active melee pressure — [[d:shaman#1445658999881207950]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-03).
- **[Enhance]** Single-target priority: Stormstrike > Lightning Strike > Flame Shock > Earth Shock — Lightning Strike does substantially more damage than either shock and can also proc Windfury, so it outranks both shocks — [[d:shaman#1450924897978023976]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-17), [[d:shaman#1450925139993559211]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-17).
- **[Enhance]** Stormstrike's Nature-damage-buff charge is consumed by the next applicable Nature source — Lightning Shield's proc, Lightning Strike's own (very small) Nature tick, or Earth Shock all compete for it; since Lightning Shield/Lightning Strike's own Nature portion is tiny, it's better used on Earth Shock (sequence: Lightning Strike into Earth Shock to spend the charge on the shock, not the shield proc) — [[d:shaman#1451909694539956345]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-19), [[d:shaman#1451909730426687579]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-19), [[d:shaman#1451913975150415996]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-19).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Spellhance/Physhance]** AoE priority: Earthquake and Chain Lightning on cooldown; once both are on cooldown, Fire Nova Totem does more damage than a Lightning Bolt on a decent trash pull — [[d:shaman#1444776794799734784]] (Zhafu, 2025-11-29).
- **[Spellhance]** Fire Nova Totem timing: two nova explosions within 20 seconds (one at ~4s, one at ~19s) with Magma Totem ticking in between — [[d:shaman#1446594733941067776]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-05).
- **[Tank/solo]** A shaman soloed all of Molten Core up to and including the full Emperor throne room encounter (pre-MC/early-MC gear) using Earth Shield and tank gear, prioritizing killing squishier or higher-damage adds first; struggled specifically against the boss's "wife" caster add and had no clean answer beyond a Holy/Shadow protection potion — [[d:shaman#1446585484108365928]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-05), [[d:shaman#1446591432977551581]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-05), open question at [[d:shaman#1446591555862397132]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-05).
- **[Contested]** Whether shaman AoE (fire nova/magma totem) out-damages paladin consecration-style AoE in a straight lasher-farming race was raised but never actually tested/answered — [[d:shaman#1446592855073358004]] (Loafcat, 2025-12-06), [[d:shaman#1446595249513435188]] (Loafcat, 2025-12-06; question unanswered).
- **[Fire Spellhance]** Sample fire-spellhance AoE opener/loop: start in Flametongue imbue > Flame Shock > Fire Nova > Stormstrike > Flame Shock > Magma Totem > Lightning Strike > Flame Shock > swap to Windfury > strikes+Earth Shock; stay in Flametongue while a lot of adds are still alive, cycling Flame Shock into another Fire Nova, and swap back to Windfury once the pack thins (Magma's bonus fire damage stops once you swap off Flametongue) — [[d:shaman#1448374319557251112]] (Phhilu, 2025-12-09), [[d:shaman#1448374823242829874]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-09), [[d:shaman#1448374883443539980]] (Phhilu, 2025-12-09).
- **[Fire Spellhance]** Flametongue Totem's ~30% AoE damage buff only clearly beats Windfury once a pack is large (~10 mobs); on a ~5-mob pack the two options are roughly a wash, so it's not worth swapping for small pulls — and it's easy to forget to swap back to Windfury after the big pack is dead — [[d:shaman#1448355202242449590]] (Phhilu, 2025-12-09), [[d:shaman#1448364461600800820]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-09).
- **[Fire Spellhance]** Weaving Nova Totem into the AoE/ST rotation (timing a new Nova on a free GCD, then dropping Searing in the gap before it detonates) is what makes spellhance's damage higher, but it is a mana-intensive habit that leaves you "on the precipice of oom" — offset partly by spellhance's larger max mana pool and better Judgement-of-Wisdom uptime from melee Flametongue hits; whether to keep weaving Novas late in a fight is a per-fight judgment call — [[d:shaman#1450878251953033358]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-16), [[d:shaman#1450878678320939101]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-16), [[d:shaman#1450878911125917848]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-16).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Elemental]** Spell hit cap for PvE is 16% total: 3% from talents (Elemental Precision-type) plus up to 1% from a Druid's Emerald Blessing buff, remainder from gear — [[d:shaman#1444817481205022832]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444955931753582712]] (Psyker, 2025-12-01).
- **[Elemental]** Getting to the full hit cap from gear is a "fool's errand" before Tier 3 due to opportunity cost vs spellpower/crit; becomes feasible around AQ40 gear (Solnius/Nefarian/C'thun-tier items can get you to 15-16%) — [[d:shaman#1444828227494478025]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444831782473302046]] (poto, 2025-11-28).
- **[Elemental]** Contested: Squid argues don't chase a fixed hit number, take as much as possible without sacrificing SP; Spicyahi argues for hit-capping anyway to avoid Molten Blast resists, plus 30%+ crit to avoid oom/downcasting — [[d:shaman#1444817749715980520]] (Squid, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444827327224873010]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444827440089141349]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-11-28).
- **[Elemental]** Crit is weighted more valuable than hit or cast speed for most gearing decisions ("yes, easily almost always" crit > hit once past the hit floor) — [[d:shaman#1444976992666648656]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-01).
- **[Elemental math]** Because crit rate applies to the full pool of casts (not just casts that land after misses), naive hit-vs-crit comparisons undervalue crit relative to hit — e.g. 20% crit with 10% miss still yields 20/100 crits, not 18% of hits — [[d:shaman#1444849797067444396]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-28).
- **[Enhance]** Strength is a better stat than Agility point-for-point for enhance DPS — [[d:shaman#1445002048142835835]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-02), confirmed [[d:shaman#1444589576399224903]] (Windshade, 2025-11-29). Only ~30% unbuffed crit is needed for good Flurry uptime; stacking crit well beyond that (e.g. 42%) is suboptimal because it costs too much Strength — [[d:shaman#1445003809419034807]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-02), [[d:shaman#1445004255319822428]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-02).
- **[Enhance PvP]** For PvP, raw Agility (not crit specifically) is probably the better focus stat for 2h enhance; on Ambershire currently it's best to itemize full stam/hp for PvP survivability instead — [[d:shaman#1445007410019434568]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-02), [[d:shaman#1445016624771108986]] (Nandieb, +1, 2025-12-02).
- **[Enhance]** No diminishing returns on Agility for dodge or crit — it scales flat; Armor's apparent "diminishing returns" is by design so that EHP scales linearly with armor rather than exponentially (the game increases the armor needed per DR% as armor rises) — [[d:shaman#1445987152021426226]] (poto, 2025-12-02), [[d:shaman#1445993874039505040]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-02), [[d:shaman#1445994509497274479]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-02).
- **[Tank weapon]** Rockbiter Weapon threat is now a flat 35% bonus (post class-changes), so slower weapons generate more threat than before; slower weapons also hit harder with Stormstrike/Lightning Strike for both tank and enhance — [[d:shaman#1444609987270938768]] (OmniSalat, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444824199406289027]] (ThatsTheJoke, 2025-12-01), [[d:shaman#1444825894693900430]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-01). Daggers, once considered for weapon skill, are now regarded as bad (low CD scaling, more parry-haste, less threat, more damage taken) — [[d:shaman#1444612834842509353]] (Zamka, 2025-11-29).
- **[Shock spells]** AP scaling for shocks: ~10% AP-to-spellpower-equivalent for Frost Shock/Earth Shock, ~6% for Flame Shock's initial hit and ~1.5% per DoT tick — [[d:shaman#1445657402165694547]] (Squid, 2025-12-03).
- **[Tank avoidance]** Defense adds 0.04% each to chance to be missed/parried/dodged/blocked and to reduce crit chance per point; crush is lowest priority on the attack table and is the last thing pushed off; extended community math converged on needing roughly 55.8-61.8% avoidance from sources beyond the naked baseline before 1% Block starts pushing crush off, with ~102.4% total avoidance cited as the "crush cap" target — [[d:shaman#1444792013684998276]] (Squid, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444794094915289149]] (Squid, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444797275699155096]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-28).
- **[Tank]** 440 Defense is the crit-cap (point at which crits are removed from the boss's attack table), not a hard "breakpoint" like a haste plateau. Disputed: Zamka argues below 440 "isn't going to matter" and it's not worth chasing before AQ40 because hitting it costs too much stamina; Dinousek counters that defense below cap still reduces crit chance and smooths damage spikes, EHP and defense aren't mutually exclusive as long as you're not sacrificing other stats for it, and shaman's poor mail defense itemization is the real issue — unresolved, both later agree gearing options for it are simply bad in Twow — [[d:shaman#1446253379205468180]] (Zamka, 2025-12-04), [[d:shaman#1446495069912698963]] (Dinousek, 2025-12-05), [[d:shaman#1446510609528000673]] (Dinousek, 2025-12-05), [[d:shaman#1446529507614789876]] (Dinousek, 2025-12-05).
- **[Melee hit]** Physical hit cap against a boss is 8%: 3% from talents + 4% from gear + 1% from 305 weapon skill — [[d:shaman#1450954202103808152]] (Squid, 2025-12-17), correcting a rumored "9%" figure at [[d:shaman#1450968396891033772]]/[[d:shaman#1450962723071328358]] context. PvP hit caps (vs level-60 players) are lower: ~5% physical, ~3% spell — [[d:shaman#1448038818618937436]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-09).
- **[Enhance]** Rough conversion rules of thumb: 1 Strength ≈ 2 Attack Power ≈ 2 Agility (agility may be worth a bit more while leveling when crit is scarce); 1% crit floats between ~20-25 AP in value depending on gear (closer to 20 with high crit/low AP, closer to 25 with high AP/low crit); Armor Penetration is worth roughly 1 AP ≈ 2.5 armor pen, and — because of how armor's DR curve works — armor pen's *relative* dps value stays roughly stable regardless of the target's armor total (never a strong stat in the quantities gear provides it, but very strong via debuffs like Faerie Fire/Sunder/CoR since those stack with your own) — [[d:shaman#1447718815164141608]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1451751754378248346]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-19), [[d:shaman#1447646747177386024]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1447663815909970071]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-08). Attack power adds flat weapon damage proportional to weapon speed (AP ÷ 14 × weapon speed), which is why a much slower weapon can out-hit a faster one with lower listed weapon damage once AP is high — [[d:shaman#1451737025454800978]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-20).
- **[Elemental]** At near-BiS K40 gear (90s-fight assumption, no Blood Fury), Not Normal/Squid's custom dps-sim spreadsheet weights haste noticeably higher than at pre-K40 gear (~40% lower weight pre-K40); a rough pre-T2/T3 ordering floated was crit > str > agi/AP, though both cautioned that crit/haste/hit shouldn't be ranked on the same axis as str/agi/ap since they scale differently — 1% spell hit is valued around 6-8 AP-equivalent (spell damage is ~25% of total output, and spell hit's value is about 25% of what uncapped physical hit would be worth) — [[d:shaman#1450930328700063866]] (Squid, 2025-12-17), [[d:shaman#1450932009177448653]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-17), [[d:shaman#1450934429567549531]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-17). Haste becomes worth more than Strength for enhance once you have haste-scaling sources like Kiss of the Spider and T3.5; before that it's worth "about the same" as Str — [[d:shaman#1452099338363732142]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-21).
- **[Spellhance]** For 2h spellhance, spellpower is roughly equal in value to strength but comes in higher quantities on gear (prioritize it where available); Not Normal's own AP:SP conversion for his 2h build fluctuates ~1.87-2.42 AP per 1 SP depending on food/trinkets/set bonuses (e.g. 2.13 baseline, 2.38 with T1 5pc, 2.42 with Neretzek); for the Hand-of-Edward variant it's roughly 4 AP : 1 SP — [[d:shaman#1449465961169293506]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-14), [[d:shaman#1449498659338191061]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-14), [[d:shaman#1449499143331643494]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-14). Spell Penetration is "Omega BiS" for Fire Spellhance/Fire Ele in MC, but for classical Enhance a flat +8 Str option is preferred over armor-pen/spell-pen on the same slot — [[d:shaman#1449496466551865427]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-14). Spell Penetration head enchant is roughly a 4% DPS increase for Elemental if nobody in the raid has Thunderfury — [[d:shaman#1451725059575120084]] (Dwi, 2025-12-20).
- **[Tank FR]** Fire resistance breakpoints (from a community reddit-derived writeup, applied to Ragnaros): 210 FR guarantees ≥25% partial resist on non-binary (pure-damage) spells; 315 FR is the resist cap (~68.75% average mitigation on non-binary spells); binary spells (debuff-carrying, e.g. Firemaw stacks) are either fully resisted or fully hit, with 315 FR giving 75% full-resist chance. Elemental Warding talent caps at 10% fire dmg reduction, +10% more from Elemental Weapons talent, +5% more from T2.5 5pc — stacking these with 210 FR gives ~40%+ total fire damage reduction — [[d:shaman#1445484138307256400]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-30), [[d:shaman#1445501893370576977]] (Guyye, 2025-11-30), [[d:shaman#1445502121041461371]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-30).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[Enhance]** Cloak enchant options considered weak/underwhelming: +3 agi vs -2% threat — [[d:shaman#1444088166863867935]] (ThatsTheJoke, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444089849559777341]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-29). Chest/cloak enchants generally seen as poor value across the board — [[d:shaman#1444095177533948046]] (poto, 2025-11-29). - **[Enhance]** Ring enchant: Time Lord (haste) preferred over an extra-attack-chance enchant, since extra attacks can't proc each other and haste value is high given current haste totals; Strength > Agility on ring stat choice as well — [[d:shaman#1444554133389709342]] (Squid, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444589576399224903]] (Windshade, 2025-11-29).
- **[Tank]** Avoid the 1% Block ring/neck enchant if any other option is available — block is a weak stat for shaman tanks; prefer raw stats or more HP — [[d:shaman#1446014070804451409]] (sparkletoots, 2025-12-04), [[d:shaman#1446066205428547594]] (Zamka, 2025-12-04).
- **[Boots]** Speed enchant is BiS pre-Emerald Sanctum; no Strength boot enchant exists above ~level 30 currently (the +9 SP enchanted armor kit is the only real alternative); after ES, Druid's Emerald Blessing gives higher movement speed than the boot enchant but doesn't stack with it — [[d:shaman#1445151655371866314]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-01), [[d:shaman#1445170381123227870]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-01), [[d:shaman#1445005285033906287]] (Greybull-Ambershire, 2025-12-02), [[d:shaman#1445005962695016458]] (Greybull-Ambershire, 2025-12-02), [[d:shaman#1445005509458530326]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-02).
- **[Shoulders]** Haste enchant is a reasonable alternative to the ZG AP enchant on shoulders for enhance — [[d:shaman#1445004537554272286]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-02).
- **[Rotation]** Elemental Weapons talent + Flametongue Weapon imbue (not oils) is the recommended fire spell setup — [[d:shaman#1444803730057728011]] (Dwi, 2025-12-01).
- **[Group play]** Windfury Totem and Flametongue Totem stack with each other for allies, but a shaman with an active weapon imbue gets no benefit from either of their own totems; for a full-melee group, Flametongue Totem is a raid DPS gain over the shaman's own Searing Totem (personal DPS) — with 2+ melee, FTT essentially always beats Searing Totem, only a full-caster group or a solo shaman with no other melee justifies Searing — [[d:shaman#1444843541250576514]] (Squid, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1445730679336992848]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445737041504505988]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445814366762176692]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445814610488856576]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-03).
- **[Totems]** Agility Totem gives roughly 3% crit and dodge; worth learning as a solo DPS upgrade, and a strong pickup for a party's rogues in particular once past early raid content (competes with Windfury Totem for the group's totem slot) — [[d:shaman#1446236175634530535]] (billienomates, 2025-12-05), [[d:shaman#1446197264430141706]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-04), [[d:shaman#1446204537882017822]] (Squid, 2025-12-04).
- **[Enhance]** Weapon oils/sharpening stones cannot be applied while any weapon imbue (Windfury, Flametongue, etc.) is active — it's imbue OR oil/stone, never both — [[d:shaman#1450550876228943922]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-16), [[d:shaman#1450551190189375691]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-16).
- **[Enhance]** Pants enchant: Voracity (flat AP) is better with low raw AP; Rapidity (haste) is generally preferred long-term as gear improves and also boosts Bloodlust's value, which matters more given the phase's short fight times; Kara10 also has "Invocation"-named pants enchants worth knowing about — [[d:shaman#1449131321132580939]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-13), [[d:shaman#1449131949351239851]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-13).
- **[Totems]** Totem of Rage vs Totem of Crackling Thunder for enhance: both can be used simultaneously with no opportunity cost (different totem elements). Crackling Thunder procs its +8% attack-speed buff off Lightning Strike (15% chance) and Lightning Bolt (10% chance) — NOT Chain Lightning; by the math (15% × ~6.66 Lightning Strikes/min, 8s buff) that's only ~13% average uptime, i.e. roughly 1 proc per fight. Without a full sim, Totem of Rage (Earth Shock damage buff) was guessed to be somewhat better, though its damage increase is also described as fairly low — [[d:shaman#1450944659072352320]] (Squid, 2025-12-18), [[d:shaman#1449458344053051442]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-13), [[d:shaman#1450949687824613558]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-18), [[d:shaman#1450946302136549568]] (Squid, 2025-12-18), [[d:shaman#1449453544771162374]] (Bogorl, 2025-12-13).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Totems]** Stoneclaw Totem gives cheap AoE threat (140 mana); higher ranks matter for the aura's value/radius (rank 1 aura value 22 vs a higher rank at 150), not just totem HP — [[d:shaman#1445538918345871442]] (poto, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1444997039556395040]] (Link, 2025-11-30, w/ database links to spell 10424 vs 5729).
- **[Totems]** Tremor Totem's range was significantly increased ("insane range... thank god") — [[d:shaman#1444554211110027426]] (Aest, 2025-11-29).
- **[Tank]** Tranquil Air Totem reduces raid threat by 20% and is multiplicative with Salvation, but is useless for a tanking shaman since it also reduces their own threat — [[d:shaman#1444540588916604978]] (Link, 2025-11-29; question), [[d:shaman#1444541380910125177]] (Squid, 2025-11-29), [[d:shaman#1444541559700848765]] (Squid, 2025-11-29).
- **[Threat]** Community wants Purge taken off cooldown again (well-received complaint) — [[d:shaman#1444296843126374564]] (Avns, +4 reactions, 2025-11-28).
- **[Trinkets]** Badge of the Swarmguard is now mostly a trash trinket; on bosses it's only worthwhile against unusually high-armor targets (roughly a 2 armor-pen : 1 AP value), good picks being Loatheb/Patchwerk/Thaddius/Kruul; with 2 Sharteth-wielding 2h DPS in a raid providing near-permanent armor-pen debuff uptime, Badge's relative value drops further — [[d:shaman#1444826049912377384]] (Squid, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444826541925208185]] (Squid, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444827135998169308]] (Squid, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444827248208380058]] (Squid, +1, 2025-11-28). Reconfirmed later: it's still not real BiS even for non-undead K40 bosses — in K40 only Kruul and Mephistroth have notably high armor, and even there Kiss of the Spider/Slayer's Crest are usually better; recommended trinket combos: Kiss+Slayer's Crest for most bosses, Kiss+a KT trinket for undead/demon bosses — [[d:shaman#1450219912969719848]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-16), [[d:shaman#1450260680266612776]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-16), [[d:shaman#1450260717386334342]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-16).
- **[Tank]** Block's real mechanic and Stoneskin Totem's math: Block applies after armor and always reduces damage by the exact block-rating value shown on your character sheet, but Stoneskin Totem's flat reduction (30, or 60 with the Enhancing Totems talent) applies before armor, so with e.g. 37% armor DR a "30" Stoneskin only nets ~19 actual damage reduction (30 × (1-0.37)) — one poster believed this is a Turtle bug/inconsistent with vanilla, Not Normal maintains Stoneskin has always worked this way and recalls the same conclusion being reached in the SoD shaman community — [[d:shaman#1451360526746521673]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-19), [[d:shaman#1451360733718777946]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-19), [[d:shaman#1451420421973999789]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-19), disputed by [[d:shaman#1451362287750221946]] (Xerq, 2025-12-19). The Enhancing Totems talent's block-rating bonus to Stoneskin only applies to the shield's base/inherent block value and the block rating gained from Strength, not to any bonus green-text "+shield block rating" stat on gear — [[d:shaman#1451249810865651714]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-19).
- **[Tank]** Block value itself is considered a nearly useless stat at normal (non-avoidance-capped) gear levels: blocking replaces a hit with a small flat reduction, and does not by itself change crit/crush chance on the attack table — only once total avoidance is extremely high (~80%+, approaching the ~97-102% total-avoidance cap) does adding more avoidance/block meaningfully start displacing crushes/crits — extensively debated and clarified by Zamka/Zeruel/Dodging/Veilith; a real example: one geared shaman tank reported ~79% base avoidance, ~83% with Grace of Air Totem — [[d:shaman#1449348441804243076]] (Zamka, 2025-12-14), [[d:shaman#1449439634781241505]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-14), [[d:shaman#1449453518933983253]] (Dodging, 2025-12-14), [[d:shaman#1449453929233387592]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-14), [[d:shaman#1449445827171975419]] (Poletank, 2025-12-14).
- **[Totem design]** Community critique (Not Normal/billienomates/poto) of underperforming totems: Stoneskin and Windwall Totem are seen as needing full redesigns (Stoneskin's pre-armor flat reduction and non-scaling nature make it weak, as above); Flametongue Totem and Healing Stream Totem are viewed as close to fine and just needing a small buff — FTT can add ~2-3% of a player's total dps even at deep endgame (compared loosely to a hunter's Trueshot Aura contribution) — [[d:shaman#1451096237938839704]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-18), [[d:shaman#1451098091620012094]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-18), [[d:shaman#1451098913984610418]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-18).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Tank]** Two viable tank builds: 2h weapon stacking agility/crit for higher threat output, or sword-and-board stacking strength/parry/block; high talent overlap with enhance DPS allows quick spec-swap between the two roles — [[d:shaman#1444299877151412317]] (peter_hammer, 2025-11-28).
- **[Tank]** AR (fire resistance) gearing for K40: a mix of leather pieces carrying threat stats can outperform mail pieces that lack those stats — [[d:shaman#1445549415854247987]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-03).
- **[Tank]** Slow 1h weapon generally recommended for tanking (bigger Stormstrike/Lightning Strike hits and, post-rework, better Rockbiter threat); a fast weapon gives more Clearcasting procs → more free shocks, a real but smaller tradeoff, especially relevant at low level — [[d:shaman#1445386348050518187]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445406961175629964]] (Psyker, 2025-12-03).
- **[Tank]** Elemental Warding (10% max) + Elemental Weapons talent (+10%) + T2.5 5pc (+5%) can stack for up to ~25% extra fire damage reduction, though the T2.5 set bonus may not fit alongside a dedicated FR set for Ragnaros — [[d:shaman#1445501893370576977]] (Guyye, 2025-11-30).
- **[Tank]** Talents exist that transfer totem threat to the tank, which affects whether upgrading Stoneclaw Totem rank matters for threat generation (open/unclear, but noted as a live question) — [[d:shaman#1444993268537495643]] (Link, 2025-11-30; question).
- **[Tank]** Mana management: run Water Shield through most of a fight for the mana return on melee hits; some run Lightning Shield instead as a flat dps increase and drink down to ~20% before swapping to Water Shield, or swap to Water Shield opportunistically on Clearcasting procs to save mana — [[d:shaman#1449455044914184415]] (Poletank, 2025-12-14), [[d:shaman#1449455367640842251]] (Poletank, 2025-12-14), [[d:shaman#1449455574541533195]] (Poletank, 2025-12-14).
- **[Tank]** Recommended tank mainhand options: Hand of Edward the Odd or Blade of Eternal Darkness (the latter especially for AoE) — [[d:shaman#1451577752967708723]] (Ciftar, 2025-12-19). Rockbiter is the wrong DPS imbue for a tank in a group with a competent tank (too much threat/too little scaling); Windfury, Frostbrand, or Flametongue are all reasonable DPS-imbue choices when someone else is tanking — [[d:shaman#1451603291569131580]] (Bassilthorne/Achuak, 2025-12-19), [[d:shaman#1451594176192970855]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-19).
- **[Tank]** A known (very situational, guild-competitive-only) MC opener trick: have another shaman open with Chain Lightning, then Earth Shock to pull threat off the puller and Earth Shock again ASAP yourself — risky due to the ever-present 1% taunt-failure chance and needing Elemental Devastation active via a crit first; only worth it for a speed-focused guild — [[d:shaman#1449148295262900245]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-12-12), [[d:shaman#1449156036757028864]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-12).
- **[Tank]** Shazzrah (Molten Core) is commonly assigned to a shaman tank because he doesn't hit hard, letting the shaman front-load threat, and the shaman can keep applying threat via shocks even through his teleports — [[d:shaman#1449144466526044191]] (billienomates, 2025-12-13), [[d:shaman#1449144707102937282]] (billienomates, 2025-12-13).
- **[Tank]** Community view on the overall spec, as of this chunk: "least tanky and probably most difficult to gear but it can and does tank most content," with the best threat generation of the tank classes but requiring the most player dedication to pull off well — [[d:shaman#1447904806436999238]] (Dodging, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1447905933614256278]] (Dodging, 2025-12-08). RFC is tankable early with just Rockbiter+Earth Shield despite no AoE threat; once Totemic Alignment (transfers totem threat to you) plus Nova/Magma Totem are available, AoE tanking becomes effective — the main early obstacle is gear, since you're stuck in leather/light mail — [[d:shaman#1447916112821092485]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1447916163240693882]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-12-08).

## Healing specifics
- **[Resto]** Basic raid AoE-healing loop: drop a totem, spam Chain Heal until the pack is dead, recall totems, move to next pack, repeat — [[d:shaman#1446474284166811672]] (peter_hammer, 2025-12-04).
- **[Resto]** Tier 1 8-piece bonus buffs Chain Heal and, via a Turtle-exclusive talent, can return up to ~700 mana per cast; described as playing like old Holy Paladin "infinite mana" via rank 3 Healing Wave spam or occasional big rank 6-10 casts — [[d:shaman#1445177080668229713]] (Illithris, 2025-12-01). A rumored interaction: with 8pc T1, Solnius (haste proc item) may treat a Chain Heal cast as 3 spells, giving near-permanent 20% haste uptime — unconfirmed/contested, poster admits it's a guess — [[d:shaman#1445178623995805849]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-12-01), [[d:shaman#1445178749573140592]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-12-01).
- **[Resto]** Healing Focus talent (2 points, Resto tree) gives 70% pushback reduction on heals; Earth Shield covers the remaining 30% pushback reduction, but only while Earth Shield is up — [[d:shaman#1445718894680735787]] (Dingacik, 2025-12-01).
- **[Resto]** Chain Heal's jump no longer reliably prioritizes the lowest-HP target, bouncing to random players/pets instead; treated by some as an intentional nerf rather than a bug (see 1.18.1 changes) — [[d:shaman#1445104273833721946]] (Adonnys, 2025-12-01).
- **[Resto]** Mana problems can persist even in pre-Naxx BiS gear if still using rank-1 Chain Heal; recommended fix is taking the Enhancing Totems talent and avoiding wasted points in Call of Flame/Improved Molten Blast, which do nothing for resto — [[d:shaman#1446547653793022064]] (Domy, 2025-12-04; question), [[d:shaman#1446548549398433903]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-04).
- **[Resto]** Water Shield return isn't overridden by using Lightning Strike per se, but casting Lightning Strike consumes more mana than it returns while Water Shield is active — raised as a live cost/benefit concern for tank/hybrid mana management — [[d:shaman#1445533539939385455]] (Eftychia, 2025-12-03; question).
- **[Resto]** A proc trinket/item ("chance on landing a damaging/healing spell") tested by Cariad: 7% chance to cast a bonus Lesser Healing Wave specifically off casting (rank) Healing Wave; the proc LHW scales terribly with spellpower (~600 healing at 1200 healing power, worse than even LHW rank 1) and has an internal cooldown (~10s) that pulls its effective proc rate below the stated 10%; it does NOT proc from Chain Heal (only direct single-target heals trigger it per testing); community verdict was that it's "exceptionally bait" — some report ~4-6% effective HPS gain from a related item when paired with the T1 8pc set specifically, others call it "near worthless for resto" — [[d:shaman#1448621972648104028]] (Cariad, 2025-12-11), [[d:shaman#1448622484592001116]] (Cariad, 2025-12-11), [[d:shaman#1448608348705390612]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-12-11), [[d:shaman#1448645358442774659]] (Dodging, 2025-12-11), [[d:shaman#1448668681864351874]] (Zaaparou, 2025-12-11), [[d:shaman#1448783353825329213]] (sailorVEVO, 2025-12-11).
- **[Resto]** No dedicated Turtle-specific pre-raid BiS list exists for resto in this chunk; repeated advice is to use vanilla classic resto pre-bis lists as a base and add Turtle-exclusive pieces — the mp5 totem from Black Morass, an epic Jewelcrafting healing ring buyable on the AH, plus Atlas Loot lookups for the custom late-game dungeons (Hateforge, Stormwind Vault, Karazhan Crypt, Black Morass) and the red dragonscale leatherworking set — [[d:shaman#1446713855467327622]] (Dwi, 2025-12-06), [[d:shaman#1449172973649920120]] (Cariad, 2025-12-13). A guild-made shaman-tank prebis sheet (not resto) also circulated: `https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1CzwEalBFWUzw4ohP7ERIjB6f_O1hDiw79hw9FblFbjo` — [[d:shaman#1447140176894627932]] (Maleficennt/Jinxes, 2025-12-07). A community "Pre Raid / XII Gears" section for Lower Karazhan/MC/Onyxia gear was also posted to the Turtle forums: `https://forum.turtle-wow.org/viewtopic.php?t=16272` — [[d:shaman#1448132875219374301]] (Mimishammini, 2025-12-10).
- **[Resto]** T2 gear with a heal-focused build is described as very strong — capable of taking a resto shaman into pre-Naxx content alongside 10/20-man-tier jewelry/weapons — [[d:shaman#1448825114169839698]] (magusbones, 2025-12-08).
- **[Frost/Fire leveling-to-resto]** For a character planning to respec Resto at 40/60 but still needing to solo/farm beforehand, an SP-gear-supplemented Frostbrand+Frost Shock build (switching to Flametongue+Flame Shock+Searing Totem plus Earthbinding Totem+Frost Shock for kiting tough elites) works well, is less gear-dependent than traditional Str-enhance, and successfully solo'd the entire T0.5 quest chain (vs ogres/naga) — [[d:shaman#1449664895070113884]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-13), [[d:shaman#1449665914898219120]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-13).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Stormwrought Ruins]** Chieftain Stormsong (level-36 elite) is soloable around level 52-53+ with decent gear; his Lightning Strike can hit for a large chunk of damage if unlucky, riskier when undergeared — [[d:shaman#1445505082438320422]] (Kekkonen, 2025-12-03; question), [[d:shaman#1445513951189139587]] (billienomates, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445535840435568731]] (billienomates, 2025-12-01; "at 48, trash skipping might be tight").
- **[Stormwrought Ruins]** The Tide Totem needed for the shaman quest drops from a specific boss guarded by ~4-5 mob packs; if you have the dungeon's back-entrance key you can skip straight to that boss and farm just him — [[d:shaman#1445123184067346584]] (Mharrokk, 2025-12-01; question), [[d:shaman#1445125890173309049]] (Holidae, 2025-12-01).
- **[Black Morass]** Considered by some the hardest dungeon in the game but actually fairly forgiving — mostly a tank gear-check rather than a mechanics check; loot is heavily overstatted (8-to-all-stats plus defense AND spell damage on some items) — [[d:shaman#1445573785754407011]] (poto, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445573762568425524]] (billienomates, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445574359262560287]] (billienomates, 2025-12-03).
- **[Karazhan Crypts]** Notorious fear-chain room (sand/skeletons that fear players into more skeletons that fear again) causes more wipes than Black Morass despite Crypts otherwise being enterable/clearable with weak gear given patience — [[d:shaman#1445574039929356309]] (poto, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445574058518511662]] (billienomates, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445574493203333260]] (poto, 2025-12-03).
- **[Molten Core]** Lucifron (first boss) hits harder than expected partly because of 2 adds assisting him; Ragnaros/Geddon require dedicated FR gear (Molten Helm, Corehound Boots, Lava Belt, etc.) — see FR breakpoints under Stats — [[d:shaman#1445539804170158286]] (billienomates, 2025-12-03), [[d:shaman#1445457359760654377]] (sunflowerdeath, 2025-12-02).
- **[World boss / "Emperor"]** A shaman soloed the entire encounter up to and including the full throne-room fight using Earth Shield plus tank gear, prioritizing squishy/high-damage adds first; open question on how to burn down the boss's "wife" add fast enough without dying — [[d:shaman#1446585484108365928]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-05), [[d:shaman#1446591555862397132]] (Zeruel, 2025-12-05; unanswered).
- **[Kara40 / Tower of Karazhan]** Per-boss gearing suggestion through K40: threat gear for boss 1, some AR for boss 2, AR + stamina for boss 3, full DPS gear for Medivh, T2.5 for the Chess event (rook/knight positions), threat gear again later, T2.5 for the remaining bosses — [[d:shaman#1446540223906320567]] (Eluzia, 2025-12-05).
- **[BWL]** All BWL bosses are fire-immune except Razorgore, Broodlord, and Chromaggus — [[d:shaman#1444777299286425631]] (Zhafu, +1, 2025-11-29).
- **[Molten Core]** Shazzrah is a common shaman-tank assignment: doesn't hit hard, so the shaman can front-load threat, and can keep threat up through his teleports via shocks — [[d:shaman#1449144466526044191]] (billienomates, 2025-12-13).
- **[Naxxramas]** Enhance-specific tactics for mana-burn fights: on Heigan, lean on a paladin's Judgement of Wisdom uptime and avoid shocking right after a mana burn (just melee-strike instead) to conserve mana; on Thaddius, if caster DPS is consistently behind melee DPS, ask the raid lead to put you on the side with more caster-friendly buffs; Major Mana Potions, Tea, Water Shield, and a Mageblood Potion all help both fights — [[d:shaman#1451610016069976064]] (Squid, 2025-12-19), [[d:shaman#1451610153177579671]] (Squid, 2025-12-19), [[d:shaman#1451609061916151930]] (Argent, 2025-12-19), [[d:shaman#1451612602021969980]] (Argent, 2025-12-19).
- **[AQ40/Naxx]** Spellhance-favorable AoE fights (per Not Normal/Zeruel): Maexxna, Noth, possibly Four Horsemen with a stack-and-burn strategy, Bug Trio and Sartura in AQ40 (some cleave room), Loatheb (rewards both phys and spell crit); Faerlina is less good since her early adds scatter rather than clump; Fankriss's aoe value is more "fluff" than impactful — [[d:shaman#1450906428431929394]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-16), [[d:shaman#1450906775879421962]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-16), [[d:shaman#1450906868410089473]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-16).
- **[Leveling tank]** Dragonmaw Retreat is tankable around level 30 with a decent shield; recommend doing RFK first for the Marbled Buckler shield upgrade — [[d:shaman#1449019965184413788]] (sailorVEVO, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1449020532153385052]] (billienomates, 2025-12-08).
- **[Hateful-strike-style raid mechanic]** For "soaker" tanks: stacking Dodge is viable if paired with enough HP/armor from consumables (stone shield potion, hp flask); stacking Dodge with a low HP pool (e.g. ~9k using SP flasks) is riskier since you can still be hit twice in a row for large hits; if your HP is too low you may simply not be chosen as a hateful-strike target, and the raid should be topping soakers off before the next hateful volley — [[d:shaman#1446422523041415208]] (Eluzia, 2025-12-05), [[d:shaman#1446423618178257001]] (Eluzia, 2025-12-05), [[d:shaman#1446433871330672711]] (Zamka, 2025-12-05), [[d:shaman#1446436495266283541]] (chain, 2025-12-05).

## PvP
- **[Enhance]** Frostbrand imbue is claimed by some to hit harder than Flametongue in PvP right now due to a self-buff/crit interaction, but disputed — Ciftar/poto argue the raw scaling comparison is irrelevant because Frost has essentially no synergy with the rest of the shaman kit (no Stormstrike interaction, unlike Flame Shock's Elemental-tree ties) — [[d:shaman#1445311876371058769]] (Gurdaz, 2025-11-30), [[d:shaman#1445312354706259978]] (Gurdaz, 2025-11-30), [[d:shaman#1445313121442070608]] (Gurdaz, 2025-11-30), disputed by [[d:shaman#1445311068867133521]] (poto, 2025-11-30), [[d:shaman#1445311934609227888]] (poto, 2025-11-30).
- **[Melee]** Melee shaman specs are considered weak in 1v1 PvP against mobile targets (no charge/stealth/sprint tools); Ghost Wolf retains a cast time even talented and is easily interrupted by a Nature-school lockout — [[d:shaman#1445313610216902749]] (Ciftar, 2025-11-30), [[d:shaman#1445337371238334564]] (Ciftar, 2025-11-30), [[d:shaman#1445342726928203796]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-11-30).
- **[Enhance PvP]** Consumable trinkets (FAP/LIP-type freedom/immunity potions) are considered a near-necessity for competitive PvP on Turtle and disproportionately help melee specs like enhance escape or avoid snares/stuns, though also viewed as a pay-to-win crutch — [[d:shaman#1445314783615254608]] (billienomates, 2025-11-30), [[d:shaman#1445315487784243322]] (Ciftar, 2025-11-30), [[d:shaman#1445315775991644231]] (billienomates, 2025-11-30).
- **[Enhance PvP]** Enchant/stat focus for PvP: raw Agility over crit specifically for 2h enhance; on Ambershire, stacking stam/hp enchants is currently considered best overall for PvP survivability — [[d:shaman#1445007410019434568]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-02), [[d:shaman#1445016624771108986]] (Nandieb, +1, 2025-12-02).
- **[Elemental PvP]** Kiting a melee attacker up close: use Frost Shock + Earthbinding Totem to slow/root, Fire Nova Totem and Searing Totem for damage, and weave in Stormstrike-buffed Earth Shock — since all of these are instant casts, kiting stays easy — [[d:shaman#1449322892763598910]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-13), [[d:shaman#1449322990163726478]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-13), [[d:shaman#1449323538036293652]] (Nandieb, 2025-12-13).
- **[Elemental PvP]** Elemental Mastery's PvP burst was nerfed (now "just some damage buff" rather than a big instant/burst tool, and no more Electrify-stack ramp mechanic needed), which several PvP-focused players dislike; Earthquake+Chain Lightning+Elemental Mastery is still described as hitting hard by others — [[d:shaman#1447328543645241525]] (Olivertwist, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1447344550493552741]] (Porkins, 2025-12-08; "that was the point"), [[d:shaman#1447330342770835597]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-08). The current PvP meta leans on Lightning Shield burst instead, pushing players toward a spellhance-like setup for PvP damage — [[d:shaman#1451659256402280654]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-12-19).
- **[Racials, PvP]** Troll's Hex racial is solid in PvP but its 5-minute cooldown is a big downside (wished it were shorter/a talent); Tauren's Totemic Slam has low damage but a -25% attack-speed debuff on a 1.5min CD, useful to force healers to trinket/bubble for a clean cast — both are considered mediocre in PvE — [[d:shaman#1450012202533781507]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-12-14), [[d:shaman#1450012478577573930]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim, 2025-12-14), [[d:shaman#1449983374566494218]] (Abuelito, 2025-12-14).

- **[Attack tables]** Two community avoidance/attack-table calculators are in circulation: `https://sbuggay.github.io/attack-table/` (bug: uses level-60 mob baseline avoidance even when computing vs a boss) and `https://tegas.github.io/legacy-sim/#/defense` (uses the correct level-63-boss baseline of 4.4%); the tegas one is more accurate against bosses — [[d:shaman#1444792888318890055]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-28), [[d:shaman#1444793052291010702]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-28).

- **[Talent planning]** Talent point links throughout this chunk use `https://talent-builder.haaxor1689.dev/c/1.18.0/shaman` — the community-standard 1.18 talent calculator. - **[Totems]** Alternative totem-management approach: bind all 4 totems to Alt/Ctrl/Shift + a single key (e.g. alt+1234 default, alt+1X34 for a fire-resist swap) using standard modifier macros — no addon required beyond NamPower for the actual multi-drop; splitting hotbar rows by element helps avoid misclicks — [[d:shaman#1450217146989674701]] (Dwi, 2025-12-16), [[d:shaman#1450217876194594960]] (Dwi, 2025-12-16). A modifier-based single-macro example for individual totem spells: `#showtooltip` / `/cast [mod:alt]Magma Totem; [mod:ctrl]Magma Totem; [mod:shift]Fire Nova Totem; [nomod]Searing Totem` — [[d:shaman#1450624960274895032]] (LillaGummen, 2025-12-15).
- **[Addons]** Cursive (`https://github.com/pepopo978/Cursive`, requires SuperWoW) provides targetable healthbars sortable by mark/HP and accurately tracks DoT (e.g. Flame Shock) durations across multiple targets, unlike the default tooltip timer which is unreliable through refreshes — useful for both single and multi-dotting Flame Shock — [[d:shaman#1447382202911555707]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1448026619263586344]] (Not Normal, 2025-12-09), [[d:shaman#1447395333448536115]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-08).
- **[Macros]** A `/startattack` macro (e.g. `/cast Stormstrike` then `/startattack`) is recommended for melee so auto-attack doesn't lapse between ability uses while waiting on cooldowns — not a native TWoW command, requires a macro/runscript or addon — [[d:shaman#1447377442682568816]] (Zhafu, 2025-12-08), [[d:shaman#1447377748460044421]] (Porkins, 2025-12-08).
