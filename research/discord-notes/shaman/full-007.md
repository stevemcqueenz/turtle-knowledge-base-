## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[enhance]** Stormstrike had a bug causing it to try to proc Windfury Weapon twice; being fixed to proc only once as intended in 1.18.1. To compensate, Windfury Weapon's base proc chance is being raised from 20% to 25% — [[d:shaman#1470268538269995080]], [[d:shaman#1470268980248842451]] (Zhafu, 2026-02-09; some, incl. Zhafu himself, think the compensation buff is unwarranted [[d:shaman#1470269053372203231]]).
- **[general]** Cariad's data point: enhance shaman currently ranks low in kara40 logs, outperformed by rogues/warriors/warlocks/mages/hunters/druids/ele shamans/paladins, ahead only of priests — used as justification that the WF-proc buff makes sense as compensation for the bugfix nerf — [[d:shaman#1470277584271970304]] (2026-02-09; disputed as methodology by Zhafu, who says kara logs are misleading due to trash/consistency/class-balance confounds [[d:shaman#1470282395092648007]], [[d:shaman#1470284471503949865]]).
- **[ele]** Elemental will be forced to use weapon oils instead of Flametongue Weapon imbue next patch (unless playing melee/spellhance) — [[d:shaman#1470506986113077521]] (Phatwater, 2026-02-09; single source, framed as a downgrade).
- **[general]** Relic-swapping macros reportedly getting removed next patch — [[d:shaman#1470207927959949322]] (Zhafu, 2026-02-09; single source/uncertain, phrased as a question).
- **[PvP]** Dissolvent (rogue) poison scaling reportedly getting a 4% nerf — [[d:shaman#1470871490659352648]] (Phatwater, 2026-02-10; Diving Instructor Knife notes "r1 is not r2" implying rank distinction matters [[d:shaman#1470871534129254633]]).
- **[PvP]** Fire (elemental) with Storm/Shock builds is very strong right now but is being nerfed for PvP in 1.18.1 — [[d:shaman#1470815734581039125]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-02-10).
- **[tank]** Rockbiter (shield-focused tank talent line) is being changed/removed; new resto-tank PvP-oriented build reportedly fits the 1.18 design intent as a replacement spec direction — [[d:shaman#1470895032637591582]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-02-11).
- **[resto]** Community-reported "class leak": Stormstrike will buff healing spells in an upcoming change — [[d:shaman#1470914645270335694]] (Zhafu, 2026-02-11; unconfirmed/rumor).
- **[general]** Sentiment (multiple posters, e.g. Misai, Kokuyo, Oronak, poto): elemental shaman brings no unique raid buff/utility versus enhance, and devs are seen as focusing balance attention mostly on enhance while leaving elemental/resto's structural issues (totems useless for casters, resto forced into Chain Heal spam, T1 8-piece combo being reworked) unaddressed — [[d:shaman#1470892097656717504]] (+5 reactions on agreement post [[d:shaman#1470896983681007679]]), [[d:shaman#1470907061318713450]], [[d:shaman#1470913051741130960]] (2026-02-11).

- **[tank, 1.18.1 major rework]** Massive discussion of the 1.18.1 tank itemization change: Rockbiter Weapon's shield-generation mechanic changes from generating a barrier worth 60% of physical damage dealt (with T2.5 5-piece giving +improved Rockbiter/threat/150 AP) to a barrier worth 75% of physical damage dealt but blocking a smaller % of incoming damage (15% cap vs previous ~18-20%, with T2.5 5p bonus removed). Net effect debated at length: losing T2.5 5-set costs ~150 AP and the "+43% threat generated" from Improved Rockbiter, a large single-target threat loss — [[d:shaman#1472700021920108566]], [[d:shaman#1472721627619000341]], [[d:shaman#1472726674348183758]] (Zamka/poto/Zeruel, 2026-02-15/16).
- **[tank]** As a result, community consensus shifts toward tanking in T3/T3.5 gear instead of T2.5, since strength/agi are now higher value than spellpower for threat and the T2.5 defensive niche (barrier/anti-spell 3p bonus) is seen as no longer mandatory except vs specific casters — [[d:shaman#1472699202579595477]], [[d:shaman#1472700021920108566]], [[d:shaman#1472702504948207707]] (poto, 2026-02-15). Also noted: T3 has similar stamina to T2.5, but worse avoidance and slightly worse EHP than T2.5; T3.5 is closer to T2.5 in EHP/avoidance than T3 but still not equal — [[d:shaman#1472965557614673970]] (Zeruel, 2026-02-16).
- **[tank]** 1.18.1 Enhancement tank tier gear also loses spell power entirely in favor of strength (previously hybrid stat tank sets existed; now none do) — seen as bad for the "spellhance tank"/fire-shield playstyle since Flametongue Weapon (scales off SP) and shock damage both suffer — [[d:shaman#1472681420802752725]], [[d:shaman#1472681829629690111]] (Not Normal, 2026-02-15).
- **[tank, PvP driver]** Multiple posters attribute the shaman tank threat/mitigation nerf to a PvP balance concern about Rockbiter-resto (not elemental) being deemed too strong in Battlegrounds by a vocal PvP community — [[d:shaman#1472960871213826213]], [[d:shaman#1472969889990971659]] (peter_hammer, 2026-02-16; disputed by Travoltino who thought it was about elemental [[d:shaman#1472963365671276667]]).
- **[tank, contested]** Zamka argues the changes may knock shaman out of the "main tank" conversation for Kara40 progression since the current MT justification is pure threat output (9k threat before boss reaches him, per his video), and losing that removes the case for MTing over other classes — [[d:shaman#1472720460281282655]], [[d:shaman#1472721175162519572]], [[d:shaman#1472726056665616487]] (2026-02-15). Zeruel disputes the severity, arguing T3.5's higher str/agi + 5p bonus should offset most of the threat loss and that EHP loss is being overstated (crush from an ~4k-damage boss hit isn't existential at 8k+ HP with a healer topping you) — [[d:shaman#1472721430838902838]], [[d:shaman#1472957136735703284]] (2026-02-15/16).
- **[tank, new stat]** New "Fortune" stat announced for 1.18.1: increases chance to trigger chance-based item procs (works on both PPM and flat-chance effects); found only on necklaces, cloaks, trinkets. Community reaction is skeptical it will be balanced (fears of mage/rogue/pala proc-stacking abuse; comparisons to the "dead" Vampirism stat) — [[d:shaman#1472661222834049240]] (Stryyke, 2026-02-15).
- **[tank]** T1 Elemental/Enhancement/Resto Turtle-specific set items are broadly considered weak stat-wise outside their set bonuses (e.g., 8-piece resto combo now being nerfed; T1 enhance 5-set's Flametongue-proc synergy undermined because none of the T1 items carry spell power) — [[d:shaman#1472940203185541160]] (Travoltino, 2026-02-16, detailed breakdown).
- **[resto]** Confirmed patch note (talent renamed): "Eye of the Storm" renamed to "Call of Earth" — Earth Shield gains 1/2 additional charges and grants an additional 35/70% chance to avoid interruption while casting a spell (image of official change text) — [[d:shaman#1476646583251636427]] (Shaggy, 2026-02-26). Community notes it will still be absorbed by pets and near-full-health players (99% HP) since it's not prioritizing lowest-HP targets, which remains unfixed/"working as intended" per devs — [[d:shaman#1476011956103020546]], [[d:shaman#1476012027548926082]], [[d:shaman#1475913015235842148]] (2026-02-24/25).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[enh vs ele]** Both specs are roughly comparable in overall damage; enhance pulls ahead in kara40 gear, while elemental is more dependent on high target counts for big Earthquake/Chain Lightning damage. Enhance is "just consistently good" — [[d:shaman#1470213506157773036]], [[d:shaman#1470213710063603908]] (Zhafu, 2026-02-09).
- **[enh]** clesk's guild comparison of top players: enhance is "below average now, but viable," gap is not large; warrior, feral, rogue, survival hunter all out-damage enhance; ret paladin possibly close; ranged-vs-melee comparisons are muddied by fight mechanics — [[d:shaman#1470372020691796071]] (clesk, 2026-02-09).
- **[general]** Zhafu recommends shaman generally over warrior for a new player who wants strong DPS across "all forms of content besides raiding" — [[d:shaman#1470218047548686602]] (2026-02-09).
- **[tank]** Shaman tanking is viable and versatile compared to Warrior/Druid/Paladin tanks, and TWoW's shaman is "less BiS-or-get-out" than other realizations — general community sentiment in response to a newbie question — [[d:shaman#1470549759893962925]], [[d:shaman#1470550245246111939]] (WobblySlug, 2026-02-10; framed as a question, not a strong "yes" from an expert, but no pushback).
- **[resto/PvP]** Shaman is described as a top-priority kill target in PvP/Bloodring because of strong chain healing, ranged burst, and totem utility — "you're gonna get chain banged by 3 alliance at once" — [[d:shaman#1470853367684141166]] (Zamka, 2026-02-10; agreement from Zamka's own dueling-rank experience [[d:shaman#1470853598903795876]]).
- **[general/PvP]** Multiple posters (Zamka, Zhafu) describe current shaman as being "in a real good spot" / "winning at everything" right before the nerf patch lands, implying a pre-nerf peak — [[d:shaman#1470864467901550663]], [[d:shaman#1470865329763782748]] (2026-02-10).
- **[enh]** Enhance is described as "the easiest class to get a raid spot with," regardless of actual DPS output, because it brings the best melee buffs (Windfury Totem, Grace of Air, Strength of Earth) in the game; raid PUGs reportedly invite self-identified "elemental shaman" applicants instantly even when told they're actually enhancement, since people just want totems — [[d:shaman#1474284705997262973]], [[d:shaman#1474284837190762506]], [[d:shaman#1474400021813264486]], [[d:shaman#1474337055365664830]] (Squid/Holidae, 2026-02-20). Zhafu: shaman "always gets a raid spot because of totems anyway," regardless of spec — [[d:shaman#1474220722376409339]] (2026-02-20).
- **[enh vs ret pal]** Enhancement and Retribution Paladin are considered roughly similar in raid DPS strength, but enhance is seen as bringing "elite tier" support/utility on top (buffs/totems) that ret paladin lacks — [[d:shaman#1477387046405542018]], [[d:shaman#1477387496420544575]] (Arzon/Not Normal, 2026-02-28).
- **[ele, raid utility]** Recurring complaint (see also 1.18.1 section) that Elemental brings nothing unique to a raid besides personal DPS — no raid buff comparable to Boomkin's crit aura is suggested as a fix by multiple posters, unresolved as of this chunk — [[d:shaman#1470892097656717504]] (Misai, 2026-02-11; ongoing sentiment reiterated through the chunk, e.g. Alundo asking whether to play Ele or a Shadowpriest alt since "there's no real caster totems or benefit to bringing an Ele specifically" [[d:shaman#1472167777925337256]], 2026-02-14).
- **[general]** Community assessment (clesk, corroborated by others) recommends new players start as Enhancement rather than Elemental raiding early, since a badly-geared Elemental has low single-target DPS, goes OOM fast, and suffers heavily from pushback/knockback in MC/BWL hard-cast content; suggests waiting to try Elemental until AQ40 gear access — [[d:shaman#1472175286543057079]] (clesk, 2026-02-14).

- **[enh, leveling math]** Frostbrand's proc-per-second follows a U-shaped curve by weapon speed: a 1.3s weapon gets ~4-5% more procs/sec than a slower weapon purely from the imbue's own math, but slower 2H weapons still win overall once accounting for the extra Lightning-Strike/Stormstrike procs and raw hit damage they enable — [[d:shaman#1476713732103999622]], [[d:shaman#1476713960756740096]], [[d:shaman#1476712885345325154]] (Not Normal, 2026-03-01).
- **[tank, leveling milestones]** Leveling as a shaman tank is "viable but not optimal" and squishy/mana-constrained until several milestones are hit: the two armor talents (~level 20-24), unlocking mail armor (level 40), the Water Shield totem relic from the Stormwrought Keep dungeon, and the Clearcasting talent; level 44+ is called the practical "sweet spot" where things start feeling much better — [[d:shaman#1477605832404504731]] (Lord Grim, 2026-03-01). More than 2 mobs pulled in Wailing Caverns before the armor talents risks an OOM healer and a dead tank — [[d:shaman#1477631643857457153]] (Porkins, 2026-03-01).
- **[tank, leveling stat gearing — tested and rejected]** A detailed community test found gearing a leveling tank shaman for Int/Stam/Spirit (over Agi/Str/Stam) "falls apart fast": missing Agi/armor means taking more damage, and threat drops once mobs stop caring about the Lightning Shield proc; feels good very early but by the mid-40s the character is squishy, constantly drinking, and well behind a normal Agi/Stam tank setup. Recommendation if still wanting more Int: use the Revantusk/High Elf reputation gear set at fresh 60, then let tier gear (which natively carries Int) cover mana — [[d:shaman#1474547998150103122]], [[d:shaman#1474617456474460160]] (bahlgrim/SteveNash, 2026-02-20). Water Shield does a good job of keeping mana manageable for a leveling tank; Int (and especially Spirit) otherwise won't do much — [[d:shaman#1474636287272157366]], [[d:shaman#1474636434194300978]] (Not Normal, 2026-02-20).
- **[enh, leveling — Auto-crit/Clearcasting synergy]** The (talent-tree, ~level 30-33 available) auto-crit effect needs the Elemental-tree Clearcasting talent to really shine — without Clearcasting, a crit is just extra damage, "nice but not game-changing"; combined, the two give near-100% Clearcasting uptime while leveling, meaning you almost never have to stop and drink while questing — [[d:shaman#1478383635806687313]], [[d:shaman#1478384643983282289]] (Zeruel/Holidae, 2026-03-03).

## Rotation: single target
- **[ele, raid]** Priority order: Earthquake > Chain Lightning > Lightning Bolt, opening each fight with a Flame Shock — [[d:shaman#1470188065296486635]], [[d:shaman#1470188139623616688]] (Squid, 2026-02-09).
- **[spellhance, OBD/1H]** Rotation with Obsidian Battle Dagger or fast 1H weapons: snap Flame Shock → Stormstrike → Molten Blast → Earth Shock → Molten Blast → Stormstrike/Lightning Strike → Molten Blast → Earth Shock → Molten Blast → Stormstrike → Molten Blast (works because Molten Blast is an instant cast landing within the GCD) — [[d:shaman#1470482583560261818]], [[d:shaman#1470482640678420500]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-02-09).
- **[spellhance, base rotation]** Ciftar's rotation: Totems (Searing Totem or Fire Nova depending on target count) → Flame Shock → Stormstrike (on CD) → Lightning Strike (on CD) → Earth Shock → Earth Shock → Flame Shock — [[d:shaman#1470481764110831647]] (Ciftar, 2026-02-09).
- **[spellhance, 2H vs 1H Molten Blast weaving — contested]** Diving Instructor Knife/Ciftar/Spicyahi argue that with a fast 1H weapon (e.g. Hand of Edward), weaving Molten Blast to refresh Flame Shock instantly without stopping the swing timer is worth it and "the play." With a slow 2H weapon, Not Normal argues Molten Blast weaving is NOT clearly optimal: casting fully resets/loses 1-2 swings' worth of 2H damage, requires 2 points in Improved Molten Blast (hard to get both easily), and raid-damage/movement mishaps compound the loss — net math is unclear without more data — [[d:shaman#1470483654076793045]], [[d:shaman#1470503895062155500]], [[d:shaman#1470504456528592918]] (2026-02-09).
- **[spellhance, 1H nuance]** With 1H, snap Flame Shock and keep it running with repeated Molten Blasts, focusing more spell damage; refreshing Flame Shock via Molten Blast does not "recheck" (re-snapshot) damage on refresh — [[d:shaman#1470482268886798590]], [[d:shaman#1470482925328928910]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-02-09).
- **[spellhance, Hand of Edward]** With Hand of Edward (cheap, ~200-300g), you can refresh Flame Shock via instant Molten Blast without stopping your swing, though it's "not reliable" so you must still be ready to hardcast if Flame Shock is about to fall off. Recommends SuperWoW mod + Cursive addon to accurately track Flame Shock remaining duration (normal UI is inaccurate). Instant Chain Lightning on trash is strong with Stormstrike buff active — [[d:shaman#1470495003641123070]], [[d:shaman#1470495254317633709]] (Ciftar, 2026-02-09; achieved up to 1k DPS in MC with this weapon/style).
- **[enh]** Stormstrike increases the next two sources of Nature damage — it does NOT interact/buff Flame Shock (Flame Shock's instant damage component does not get the Stormstrike bonus, contrary to a player's assumption) — [[d:shaman#1470451742012473396]] (Zhafu, 2026-02-09).
- **[ele]** Extended, math-heavy dispute over Earthquake vs. Lightning Bolt single-target DPS: peter_hammer maintains Earthquake yields roughly 20-50% more DPS per second-of-cast-time on average than Lightning Bolt/Chain Lightning once you factor in the ~11% extra crit chance LB/CL get from talents that EQ does not, and that this holds mathematically "no matter how you interpret" the comparison; Kokuyo disputes based on played experience, arguing a critting Lightning Bolt often outdamages a non-critting Earthquake, and that EQ's long cast time (~2.3s vs ~1.8s for LB) isn't sufficiently rewarding when it doesn't crit — resolved in favor of peter_hammer's math by the thread but Kokuyo remains unconvinced ("your maths guys only hurt the gameplay of the specs") — [[d:shaman#1473612303055458432]], [[d:shaman#1473612338522493041]], [[d:shaman#1473614643845926956]], [[d:shaman#1473619989209546763]] (2026-02-18).
- **[ele/spellhance]** Sulfuras's on-hit proc is confirmed to benefit from both Call of Flame and Elemental Fury talents (including Elemental Fury's 100% crit-damage bonus), despite the tooltip wording being identical to effects that don't get these bonuses elsewhere — described by Ciftar as "arbitrary," i.e., inconsistent interactions are common on Turtle and must be checked case by case rather than assumed from tooltip text — [[d:shaman#1473710148110520542]], [[d:shaman#1473713200527839436]], [[d:shaman#1473729249214070945]], [[d:shaman#1473736948165120284]] (Xaln/Ciftar, 2026-02-18).
- **[ele]** Shock spells (Earth Shock etc.) are reportedly coded internally as melee attacks — flagged as potentially relevant to keeping the new 1.18.1 fire-damage-taken buff/debuff mechanics active more easily for Elemental than expected — [[d:shaman#1477673559085027389]] (Bipst, 2026-03-01; speculative/unconfirmed mechanical detail).
- **[ele, weapon choice]** For a starter Elemental raiding weapon, Ciftar estimates roughly 40 spell "connections" per minute on single target with Blade of Eternal Darkness, equating to ~400 damage-per-minute / ~6.66 DPS from its proc; recommends using it primarily on trash to reduce drinking — [[d:shaman#1476819628108939307]] (2026-02-27; also [[d:shaman#1476818706427412551]]).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[ele]** Earthquake and Chain Lightning scale with target count; elemental's damage output is "dependent on having a high target count for big earthquakes and chain lightning," unlike enhance which is "just consistently good" regardless — [[d:shaman#1470213710063603908]] (Zhafu, 2026-02-09).
- **[spellhance]** Choose Searing Totem for single target vs Fire Nova Totem depending on number of enemies, as part of the opening totem drop — [[d:shaman#1470481764110831647]] (Ciftar, 2026-02-09).
- **[T2 5-piece build]** A Lightning Bolt-spam playstyle built around T2 Elemental 5-piece (Lightning Shield procs) with Totem of Crackling Thunder: relies on spamming Lightning Bolt to proc extra bolts; crit/double-crit does not affect Lightning Shield proc damage at all, so pure spell power (not crit) is favored; uses less mana than standard rotation so Potion of Quickness can replace Major Mana potion — [[d:shaman#1470633831500484743]] (Dwi, 2026-02-10; framed as niche/fun rather than optimal — "who cares if it ends up not being optimal").
- **[open question]** Oronak questions the design of Earthquake as basically a reskinned Flamestrike with a long cast time, suggesting Flame Shock explosion (Fire Nova-like from later expansions) would be better — no resolution, framed as a design critique — [[d:shaman#1470909062144200775]] (2026-02-11).
- **[tank]** For AoE threat, Stoneclaw Totem is repeatedly called the best low-mana AoE threat totem for shaman tanks ("costs low mana, huge threat," "that shit is gas") — it does not taunt but instead generates AoE threat toward the caster on every pulse, confirmed mechanically by Not Normal — [[d:shaman#1476055102123937972]], [[d:shaman#1475719810548760719]], [[d:shaman#1477781427889246340]] (Cleric/Squid/Not Normal, 2026-02-24 to 03-02). Stoneclaw is often used specifically to hold aggro long enough for a delayed Fire Nova Totem pop, not as the main AoE threat source itself — [[d:shaman#1472944537872896103]] (Holidae, 2026-02-20).
- **[tank]** Fire Nova Totem is considered clearly better than Magma Totem for both threat and DPS in AoE; the "Improved Fire Totems" talent that's supposed to buff Magma doesn't meaningfully change its output/threat, a widely-agreed design flaw — a flat +10% Magma damage is suggested as a simple fix — [[d:shaman#1475674214840143913]], [[d:shaman#1475674890379202671]], [[d:shaman#1475675081844723774]], [[d:shaman#1475675327350177988]] (Zhafu, 2026-02-24; disputed by Zamka who says skipping the talent lets Magma "pump" [[d:shaman#1475720192146280478]]). Magma Totem is also unusually mana-expensive (650 mana at max rank, more than "the whole paladin spellbook combined" per one complaint), and the mana-reduction elemental talent reduces its threat instead of its mana cost, which the community feels is backwards — [[d:shaman#1475558771370627207]], [[d:shaman#1475559168394920066]], [[d:shaman#1475559461484626083]], [[d:shaman#1475561834151936134]] (poto/Holidae/Eluzia, 2026-02-23).
- **[tank]** Sigil of Ancient Accord + Totem of Tides is the signature shaman-tank AoE threat/damage combo: Totem of Tides' periodic AoE damage gives repeated chances to proc Sigil of Ancient Accord; Boorenka reports Ancient Accord contributing ~7% of his total tank damage on almost every boss using this combo — [[d:shaman#1473298508504236146]], [[d:shaman#1473301013757956140]], [[d:shaman#1476252716882853949]] (Holidae/Boorenka, 2026-02-18/25). Combined with Force Reactive Disk, this trio (ToT + Sigil + FRD) is cited generally as a strong AoE-threat/tanking kit, e.g. for lasher farming or dungeon AoE tanking — [[d:shaman#1471521279524737202]] (Therz, 2026-02-12).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[tank, EHP math]** Community math (Nandieb): EHP gains from armor are linear — a 5% armor increase yields a 5% EHP increase. At ~8000 armor, Malistar's over Sulfuron Aegis (~400 more armor via Spirit Armor talent scaling) is worth more than the 14 stam (with Blessing of Kings-type buff) from Sulfuron Aegis: 400 armor ≈ 5% armor-boost ≈ 5% EHP gain vs 154 effective HP ≈ only 3% EHP gain at 5k HP — [[d:shaman#1471232551183188031]], [[d:shaman#1471233478266982400]], [[d:shaman#1471233885038841897]] (Nandieb, 2026-02-11).
- **[tank, armor-per-stam off shield]** Zeruel: armor gained "off shield" (i.e., relative value of armor items vs stam) is roughly 25-30 armor per stam-equivalent for most shaman tanks — meaning armor accessories like Dark Iron Ring effectively "waste" 15+ STR/AGI worth of stats for only ~4 stam-equivalent of value — [[d:shaman#1471211292386791506]], [[d:shaman#1471211363836624957]], [[d:shaman#1471211529247658149]] (2026-02-11).
- **[tank, defense/avoidance — contested]** Multiple tanks (Nandieb, Diving Instructor Knife, Phatwater, poto, Zeruel) argue Defense Rating and avoidance (dodge/parry) are low priority relative to stam/armor: defense doesn't stop crushing blows, gear can't reach real avoidance/crush-immune caps on Ambershire's current content, and stacking avoidance only "tricks healers" into thinking less damage is coming rather than truly reducing spike damage. Consensus: never take Defense Rating over stam/armor unless the item is otherwise best-in-slot — [[d:shaman#1471198503509430435]], [[d:shaman#1471199571148279888]], [[d:shaman#1471210369715277916]], [[d:shaman#1471220108297769141]] (2026-02-11; broad agreement, no strong dissent found in this range).
- **[tank, stat-priority TLDR]** 𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫's posted priorities: Threat gear stat prio = hit cap > crit (melee or spell) > str > sp > agi > ap > int. Mitigation gear stat prio = stam > parry > dodge > defense > agi > armor — [[d:shaman#1471165280544882749]] (2026-02-10, +2 reactions).
- **[enh]** riku/Rocked discuss str vs haste tradeoff on Ambershire enh gear: roughly 23 str ≈ 53 AP vs 3% haste; consensus was to favor strength (not agility) for now, but switch priority toward haste after BWL gear becomes available ("haste scales better later on") — [[d:shaman#1470477480484933732]], [[d:shaman#1470478164919717981]], [[d:shaman#1470478237783031861]], [[d:shaman#1470479613816864851]] (2026-02-09).
- **[spellhance]** Ele Devastation talent + Emerald Blessing gives spell hit; combined with 1H weapon skill (gets more crit than 2H, plus a 3%-crit talent for LS/SS and ~1% from weapon-skill book) lets 1H spellhance approach spell-hit cap fairly consistently even with low gear-based spell hit (e.g. 1% from gear still allows ~1k DPS) — [[d:shaman#1470501344832917555]], [[d:shaman#1470501450776842435]], [[d:shaman#1470508995403055335]], [[d:shaman#1470508343738237032]] (Diving Instructor Knife/Not Normal/Ciftar, 2026-02-09).
- **[ele]** Spell Penetration is only worth gearing for on bosses without a Thunderfury user in raid: most bosses have ≤15 Nature Resist (fully covered by Thunderfury's -25 target resistance debuff); bosses with more than 25 total nature resist are rare enough that Spell Pen isn't generally worth chasing. Without a Thunderfury user, up to 15 Spell Pen is an efficient stat choice (e.g. via jewelry enchants); more than that is overkill — better to put 2 of 3 possible +spell dmg jewelry enchants instead since a 3rd would be wasted against most bosses — [[d:shaman#1471247280756752528]], [[d:shaman#1471247507131728075]], [[d:shaman#1471267256192471226]], [[d:shaman#1471267410907500564]] (Not Normal, 2026-02-12).
- **[ele]** Improved Water Shield (mp5 talent) is "pretty meh" for ele — mana is instead managed via Clearcasting procs from crit; only worth taking with a lot of external mp5 support (e.g., no Blessing of Wisdom paladin) or for very long (2min+) fights. With 5 spare talent points, Ancestral Knowledge (max mana + spell crit) usually nets more value than Improved Water Shield's mp5 for typical fight lengths — detailed mana-math comparison by Dwi/Spicyahi (e.g. 300 int base: AK grants ~229 mana + crit vs Improved Water Shield ~216-330+ mana over 2 min depending on mp5 gear) concluded AK is better for short fights, Water Shield closes the gap or wins for 2+ minute fights, and AK scales better as gear/crit improves — [[d:shaman#1471340255390072935]], [[d:shaman#1471345753611046942]], [[d:shaman#1471354168592568533]], [[d:shaman#1471372740626878628]], [[d:shaman#1471373683519127736]] (Not Normal/Dwi/Spicyahi, 2026-02-12).
- **[ele]** Never skip Earthquake talent per Not Normal, contra a claim that it's optional for a fresh 60 — [[d:shaman#1471340255390072935]] (2026-02-12).
- **[ele/mana management]** Diving Instructor Knife: no class should ever go OOM without a mana-burn mechanic; if you expect to OOM at max-rank spam, alter your rotation instead (downrank, use max-rank Lightning Bolt only to fish for Clearcasting procs then uprank). A useful heuristic (Spicyahi, +2 reactions): downrank your spells if your mana % is lower than the boss's HP %. For specific fights, plan mana pacing across phases (e.g. Kel'Thuzad P1 should leave you at 90%+ mana so you can max-rank spam through P2/P3) — [[d:shaman#1471509581535514739]], [[d:shaman#1471509719553150997]], [[d:shaman#1471574271343067246]], [[d:shaman#1471510384967155775]] (2026-02-12).
- **[ele, community report]** Ele shamans reportedly consistently rank top 1-5 DPS in Naxxramas on Tel'Abim server — [[d:shaman#1471507805872717856]] (Temu, +1 reaction, 2026-02-12; contrasts with earlier "ele brings nothing to raids" sentiment).
- **[enh]** Weapon speed for Windfury proc: WF Weapon imbue is 9 PPM base — math given: ~60% proc chance for a 4.0s weapon (unmodified), ~19.5% for a 1.3s weapon; Elemental Weapons talent (3/3) gives a flat +25% chance, making it ~85% for a 4.0s weapon and ~44.5% for a 1.3s weapon — [[d:shaman#1472000154809925722]], [[d:shaman#1472000543671976080]], [[d:shaman#1472000731316883518]], [[d:shaman#1472000796852748475]] (Not Normal, 2026-02-14). Community testing (poto) found slower weapons give slightly better results for Frostbrand, "just by a slight margin" — [[d:shaman#1472006193546399947]] (2026-02-14).
- **[enh]** Best-in-slot orc racial (Blood Fury) beats troll racial (Berserking) for both Ele and Enh DPS per community math, reversing an earlier-patch conclusion — [[d:shaman#1471895235184295960]] (peter_hammer, 2026-02-13; single strong claim, some pushback/agreement mixed).
- **[tank]** Shaman tank white-hit hit cap is 8% (1H+shield, not dual-wield); above 8% only matters for dual-wielders, and shaman cannot dual wield — [[d:shaman#1474648419124641853]], [[d:shaman#1474648717737853049]], [[d:shaman#1474648756384301126]] (poto, 2026-02-21).
- **[tank]** Tank main-hand weapon speed should always be as slow as possible, since Stormstrike/Lightning Strike/Earth Shock damage isn't normalized by weapon speed — a slow weapon means bigger, more consistent per-hit threat/damage — [[d:shaman#1474648056380133448]] (poto, 2026-02-21). Not Normal separately confirms this same logic drives 1H spellhance preferring slower weapons overall despite faster weapons getting more Stormstrike/Lightning Strike procs (see Rotation section).
- **[tank]** Overall tank stat priority (poto): main stats are ARMOR = STAMINA > AGI; Defense Rating is "a nice addition but never a focus." Agi is generally valued over Str for a tank per point since it helps both defense (dodge, armor) and threat, whereas Str mainly helps block value (Str:Block ratio is 20 Str : 1 Block value) and per-point is "better for threat" specifically — [[d:shaman#1474649022043263029]], [[d:shaman#1474643822771310713]], [[d:shaman#1477427996251001034]] (poto/Not Normal, 2026-02-21; Boorenka/Squid, 2026-03-01).
- **[tank]** Crushing Blow chance is a flat 15% per boss swing (unless pushed off the table via total avoidance) and is NOT reduced by Defense Rating — Defense affects crit chance and avoidance, not crush directly, contrary to a common misconception (and contrary to what the "Better Character Stats" addon tooltip on Turtle implies) — [[d:shaman#1478722202269384705]], [[d:shaman#1478722334851600476]], [[d:shaman#1478724070509117500]] (Zhafu, 2026-03-04). Roughly 85-88% total combined avoidance is needed to start pushing crushing blows off the table (slightly higher vs. bosses than character-sheet numbers suggest); shaman tanks generally cannot reach this on mail gear since they lack a Warrior/Paladin-style Shield Block/Holy Shield mechanic to supplement raw dodge/parry — [[d:shaman#1474644185624744067]], [[d:shaman#1474645833302413427]], [[d:shaman#1474646309788192838]] (Not Normal, 2026-02-21). Other tank classes (Fury-prot Warrior, Feral Druid) also take crushes / lack full avoidance-cap access, so shaman is not uniquely disadvantaged here — [[d:shaman#1478769226318614663]], [[d:shaman#1478731331843391598]] (Zeruel/oi-er, 2026-03-04).
- **[ele/spellhance]** Rough community value estimates: 1% spell hit ≈ 16-20 spell power; 1% spell crit ≈ 14 spell power, for the purpose of hit/crit-vs-power tradeoffs pre-Emerald Sanctum/BWL when hit capping is hard — recommended max trade-off of ~15-20 spell power sacrificed for hit was considered reasonable, running at 11-14% hit rather than the 16% cap during MC/ZG/Kara10-only phase — [[d:shaman#1474090140237561856]], [[d:shaman#1474090201293787268]], [[d:shaman#1474091165627322440]] (Chidi/Dwi, 2026-02-19).
- **[ele/spellhance hit-cap nuance]** If playing an Elemental-Devastation-fueled spellhance/hybrid rotation, only about 4% gear-based spell hit is needed (the rest comes from melee-crit-triggered Elemental Devastation); a pure ranged Elemental caster needs the full ~16% from gear since it can't reliably trigger that melee-crit proc — [[d:shaman#1474157598109601974]], [[d:shaman#1474166031395061891]] (Mithreon/Not Normal, 2026-02-20).
- **[general, set-bonus benchmark]** Not Normal's rule of thumb for evaluating whether a set bonus is well-designed: ~30-45 added DPS is a "solid, well-designed" set bonus; ~10-15 DPS is a weak one; a few outliers (unintentional interactions) can hit 150-200+ DPS and are considered broken/unhealthy for balance — [[d:shaman#1474869812126683398]], [[d:shaman#1474870041672679527]] (2026-02-22; see also the T3.5 enh 3pc bug entry in 1.18.1 section).
- **[enh, solo/farming]** Concrete community DPS math (Mimishammini) comparing Windfury Weapon vs. Rockbiter Weapon for solo farming with a 3.80-speed weapon (300 base damage): over 10,000 simulated auto attacks, Rockbiter Weapon totals ~13.17M damage vs. Windfury Weapon's ~18.78M damage (using WF's 20% proc rate pre-1.18.1, 2 extra attacks at +333 AP-equivalent per proc) — concluding Windfury is significantly better DPS than Rockbiter even accounting for its proc uncertainty, though Rockbiter's guaranteed damage can still be preferable in very short fights/farming bursts where luck matters more than average — [[d:shaman#1476931297246052464]] (Mimishammini, 2026-03-01). Diving Instructor Knife notes WF becomes 25% (from Elemental Weapons) on top of base proc rate in 1.18.1, and that one specific attack type could double-proc it under the old bug (see 1.18.1 section) — [[d:shaman#1476933556763627684]] (2026-03-01).
- **[general]** Enchanted Armor Kits (crafted armor consumable) reportedly give +9 Defense Rating — [[d:shaman#1478760421082267811]] (firfir, 2026-03-04).

- **[tank]** Practical counterpoint from experienced tanks (Holidae, Zeruel): the only shaman-tank deaths they've seen in raid were botched pulls or healer inattention, not lack of a defensive CD; ask for Power Word: Shield before a pull, and Stormstrike + first auto generates enough shield to block ~10-15% of an incoming crit almost immediately — [[d:shaman#1472947457523454137]], [[d:shaman#1472958174960029842]] (2026-02-16; contested by Travoltino as understating pre-1.18.1-buff risk once BWL-level damage lands).
- **[tank, off-tank role]** Common shaman-tank raid role described as OT + Fire-Resist-designated tank, switching to a DPS spec when OT isn't needed, in ~25-30 person raids — [[d:shaman#1471208883690410196]] (Boorenka, 2026-02-11).
- **[tank]** For a hard-hitting fight where healers struggle, running more "soakers" (extra tanks/stam-focused players) is the suggested fix rather than gearing more defensively on the primary tank — [[d:shaman#1471602832397303954]] (Zhafu, 2026-02-13).
- **[tank/farming]** Deviate Faerie Dragon-style trash farming (e.g. Maraudon lashers) for shaman tanks: use Fire Totem talents + a shield + Enhancing Totems (Stoneskin, Grace of Air, Healing Stream, Earth Shield) to hold and clear packs; clear speed is "2-3x slower than Paladins," typically 1-3 packs pulled at once, with runs completable in roughly 8-12 minutes with T1/T2 elemental gear — the Windfury/Elemental Weapons nerf (fewer free imbue procs) is expected to slow this further — [[d:shaman#1472999454784229378]], [[d:shaman#1473000007471857684]], [[d:shaman#1473038454576381974]], [[d:shaman#1473001948000293076]] (Phatwater/Dwi, 2026-02-16). Level 54+ lasher farming is viable while leveling too (30-min levels reported), requiring Healing Focus talent and mid-pack Earth Shield self-heal swaps — [[d:shaman#1473038310816354350]], [[d:shaman#1473044636707590318]], [[d:shaman#1473044788126289982]] (Bezawit/Vyking, 2026-02-16). Stoneskin Totem is specifically for killing lots of small enemies (e.g. lasher/trash farming) — it's "not good for tanking at all" in a raid/dungeon boss context — [[d:shaman#1475199417656279181]] (Holidae, 2026-02-12). Boiling down mana cost via Water Shield + heavy Nova/Magma spam is common for AoE farming and self-sustains well with T1/T2/T3.5 elemental gear pieces — [[d:shaman#1474758857183203442]], [[d:shaman#1474761035360637059]], [[d:shaman#1474761098812068033]] (Eluzia/Zamka/Khandis, 2026-02-21).
- **[tank]** Shaman tanks can Main Tank the entirety of Kara40 — [[d:shaman#1475424938616684688]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-23, responding to whether Enhancement-spec'd shamans can tank effectively).
- **[tank, Howlfang (Kara10) strat]** Howlfang applies a stacking debuff to whoever is in melee range of him and enrages around 50% HP (hitting for ~5k on the tank in one report); recommended strats: swap the tank periodically and step away to let stacks fall off, or (if solo-tanking) simply stack enough HP/avoidance and hope healers keep pace — high DPS and good healers are needed to solo-tank him reliably; at enrage, some tanks swap to an Agility totem to boost avoidance, and rely on healer cooldowns (Power Word: Shield/Spirit Link) — swapping tanks around the 15% HP mark is recommended for an "average" group rather than risking the solo tank — [[d:shaman#1474923935286951967]], [[d:shaman#1474925478329716828]], [[d:shaman#1474926708506235000]], [[d:shaman#1474927102837915822]], [[d:shaman#1474931984647327744]], [[d:shaman#1474932299002024173]] (Vyking/Zamka/poto/Eluzia, 2026-02-21). Defense Rating is explicitly called a poor measure of tank readiness for this fight — [[d:shaman#1474925478329716828]] (Zamka, 2026-02-21).
- **[tank, professions]** Universal profession recommendation for shaman (tank, enh, or ele) is Engineering plus any second profession — engineering explosives/gadgets help with pulls, sappers give AoE threat utility while tanking, and it's the only profession broadly agreed to add DPS — [[d:shaman#1475212516761276660]], [[d:shaman#1475214619525124208]], [[d:shaman#1473598065050128439]], [[d:shaman#1477232322800779264]], [[d:shaman#1477232404530860234]] (Zhafu/Mithreon, 2026-02-23, 02-15, 02-28). Alchemy is explicitly debunked as providing any extra shaman-specific buffs (aside from one BoE alchemist trinket that isn't tank-relevant) — [[d:shaman#1471890562008481905]], [[d:shaman#1471890802677514324]] (peter_hammer, 2026-02-13). For tanks specifically choosing a second profession, Blacksmithing is favored for the mail tank/FR set craftable items (Fiery Chain); Leatherworking's late-game armor is mostly DPS-oriented and less useful for a tank's own gear — [[d:shaman#1476041815240216626]] (Méroulkas, 2026-02-25; unresolved/open, no clear consensus answer given in-thread). Goldsmithing has a spell-resist neck usable only by that profession; otherwise almost nothing craftable is worth it outside Engineering except Dreamthread (resto pre-BiS bracer, expensive to learn) — [[d:shaman#1477232594012868708]], [[d:shaman#1477234521366728755]], [[d:shaman#1477234643286622229]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-28). Axesmithing speculated to become relevant in 1.18.1 due to new craftable-item raid-upgrade system — [[d:shaman#1477235094317039618]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-28).

## Healing specifics
- **[resto]** Recommended healing spec "8/43 is the only non-meme healing spec" (peter_hammer): Spiritual Link worth it at least as an "oh shit" button; no weapon imbue (mana oil instead); buy only ranks 1 & 3 of Chain Heal plus ranks 4/5 and 10 of Healing Wave — [[d:shaman#1472590831775912169]] (2026-02-15). A meme ~0/21/30 spec exists that swaps to enh DPS gear on low-healing fights but is bad on mana — [[d:shaman#1472591244382441492]] (peter_hammer, 2026-02-15). Shaman "shits out threat like crazy" and with a halfway-decent healer you won't feel the lack of defensive CDs; AoE threat holding is described as effortless — [[d:shaman#1472591752316719155]], [[d:shaman#1472591995498401892]] (Holidae, 2026-02-15; tank-adjacent but from a healing/support framing).
- **[resto]** Haste value for resto: roughly 1% haste ≈ 20-26 healing power depending on how much healing power you already have (Zamka's estimate); the SWV haste whip trinket is roughly equivalent to 60+ healing power. Community pushes stacking haste in the ~500-700 heal range to push Chain Heal's cast time down toward the 1.5s global cooldown floor and spam Rank 1, since Chain Heal is a "smart heal" that lands first and bounces to injured party members — described as extremely mana-efficient this way. One healer reports getting to a 1.62-1.75s cast with talents/gear/buffs — [[d:shaman#1477569162967646339]], [[d:shaman#1477569482150121647]], [[d:shaman#1477569610671853578]], [[d:shaman#1477574470733664316]], [[d:shaman#1477574605085347840]], [[d:shaman#1477574997248839745]], [[d:shaman#1477575311955857431]], [[d:shaman#1477575791637303347]], [[d:shaman#1477575984495722667]], [[d:shaman#1477576204402954240]] (Zamka/Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-03-01). General advice: figure out Chain Heal's own scaling/haste formula and self-calculate from your own stats rather than trusting a flat rule someone else quotes, since those numbers are usually tied to a specific gear level — [[d:shaman#1477572251443527680]], [[d:shaman#1477572380728623104]], [[d:shaman#1477572489050853497]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-03-01).
- **[resto, PvP design gap]** Multiple posters (Snert, Diving Instructor Knife) lament that "PvP resto doesn't really exist" as a deep-resto-tree playstyle — the best PvP resto specs always put more points outside the Resto tree (into Elemental or Enhancement with Nature's Swiftness) than inside it, since deep Resto offers no meaningful single-target throughput or survivability choice; would like an alternative deep-resto capstone that offers real throughput to make that choice meaningful — [[d:shaman#1477649528969756712]], [[d:shaman#1477662559602737203]], [[d:shaman#1477661493863518378]], [[d:shaman#1477681469374861483]], [[d:shaman#1477681853459595446]], [[d:shaman#1477674518678999201]] (Nesushka/Snert, 2026-03-01).
- **[resto]** Breath of Solnius (healing trinket) is considered especially strong for shaman specifically among healers, because dropping totems can proc it and totems don't consume the global cooldown, giving shamans more proc opportunities than most other healers; still generally BiS for any healer not spamming purely 1.5s-cast spells — [[d:shaman#1478291258127548437]], [[d:shaman#1478293624956387461]], [[d:shaman#1478330395383562240]], [[d:shaman#1478330477025951906]], [[d:shaman#1478330557451469002]] (Zhafu/Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-03-03).
- **[resto]** Earth Shield does scale with healing power (roughly a 20% healing-power coefficient per instance, typical for a HoT), can crit, and — unusually — triggers *before* the damage instance that procs it, making it function like a smart/preemptive heal; this same "before-damage" behavior is noted as potentially problematic when trying to use it defensively on a tank (since it isn't purely reactive) — [[d:shaman#1474505919600656527]], [[d:shaman#1474506024676495592]], [[d:shaman#1474507356451770478]], [[d:shaman#1474507533547864145]] (Not Normal, 2026-02-21).
- **[resto, unclear]** Nobody in the channel could confirm what the (Turtle-specific) resto T2 8-piece set bonus actually does in practice ("I don't think anybody ever notices, and I have never tested it") — flagged as a case where healer set-bonus testing/awareness is weak community-wide — [[d:shaman#1474498908960067812]], [[d:shaman#1474499163071975675]], [[d:shaman#1474499268755591374]] (Dwi/poto, 2026-02-21).
- **[resto]** Sigil of Ancient Accord procs off multiple shaman spells including Lightning Shield, Earth Shock, and Lightning Strike — confirmed in the context of tank AoE builds (see Rotation: AoE section) but also asked generically about which spells trigger it, without a single definitive list being given in-thread — [[d:shaman#1476252716882853949]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-02-25; partially answered elsewhere in the chunk for the tank use-case).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[MC, Majordomo Executus]** Detailed strat posted by Doite for a 20-25 person Majordomo attempt: run with 2 tanks (preferably 1 with FR gear for the Ragnaros follow-up) and roughly 1 healer per group, scaled down from the full-40 recommendation of 3 tanks/2 mages/5 healers/25 people (optionally 1 kiting hunter). Mages can only Polymorph 2 healers max on Turtle — sheep the 2 healers closest to the boss. One Hunter uses Aimed Shot (Salvation off, closest-to-boss target) then kites; a second, ballsy hunter can sometimes pick up a 3rd add depending on multishot positioning. Main tank holds Majordomo exactly where he stands, facing the raid, repositioning to intercept his teleport-run toward healers (who should stand in a line); 1-2 additional tanks handle the warrior adds on each flank while warriors cleave. Kill order: unsheeped healers > broken/resheep-failed healers > warrior adds. Pull timer: set to 20s, hunter starts Aimed Shot at 3s left (lands at 0), mages start sheeping at 1.5s left (lands at 0) — [[d:shaman#1474002920419627039]], [[d:shaman#1474003301736648880]], [[d:shaman#1474005341233610905]], [[d:shaman#1474005699938881650]], [[d:shaman#1474007176682148005]], [[d:shaman#1474007403526754304]], [[d:shaman#1474007555629125654]], [[d:shaman#1474008345697321002]], [[d:shaman#1474013292954386515]], [[d:shaman#1474015698597576917]], [[d:shaman#1474015991221452851]] (Doite/Nyamuu, 2026-02-19).
- **[MC, Ragnaros]** Most groups do not need heavy Fire Resist gear to progress past Ragnaros (can cheese his Elemental/Sons-of-Flame knockback with 3-4 tanks and zero FR gear); however, Firegut/Geddon-style add-heavy phases and Golemagg-adjacent content are cited as wanting real (315) Fire Resist — getting to ~210 FR nets roughly 70% of the mitigation value of 315 FR for about half the gearing investment. High FR (315) is specifically recommended for soloing/cheesing Geddon-related mechanics — [[d:shaman#1473009346924253402]], [[d:shaman#1473009469972676769]], [[d:shaman#1473009892750131383]], [[d:shaman#1473010197072052244]], [[d:shaman#1473010296900681841]] (Zamka/Zeruel/poto, 2026-02-16). Ragnaros is considered one of the easier bosses mechanically but "annoying," especially for a shaman since he can target them with multiple of his abilities — [[d:shaman#1473010197072052244]], [[d:shaman#1473010296900681841]] (poto, 2026-02-16).
- **[Kara10, Howlfang]** See Tanking specifics section for the full enrage/tank-swap strategy.
- **[general, raid content structure]** Ambershire is on a slower/different phase order than Nordanaar (custom Turtle server progression): Ambershire had only MC/Ony/ZG/Kara10 open through most of this chunk, with BWL, then (accidentally/early — see Bugs & quirks) Emerald Sanctum arriving toward the end of the chunk; Timbermaw Hold (a 1.18.1 feature) will not be available on Ambershire until a later phase, and current-tier PvP gear availability is also being reworked/TBD for Ambershire's BWL phase — [[d:shaman#1477241706394030090]], [[d:shaman#1477241770864808108]], [[d:shaman#1477242197513601168]], [[d:shaman#1477242296704700466]] (Mithreon, 2026-02-28).
- **[AQ40]** AQ40 is regarded by veteran raiders as containing some of the best-designed bosses in the base game (better than MC/BWL), completed by geared groups in as little as ~50-60 minutes; the Skeram encounter's Mind Control mechanic combined with large character models causing target-swap/TP confusion is called out as its worst/most frustrating element, and clearing the optional Mindslayer packs for reputation/Shard of the Fallen Star drops (only ~6 total mobs drop it if packs are skipped) was described as a slog during initial progression — [[d:shaman#1473202510201360455]], [[d:shaman#1473202618397622453]], [[d:shaman#1473202746260983809]], [[d:shaman#1473203024657780736]], [[d:shaman#1473203189536002283]], [[d:shaman#1473202286485573642]] (Zamka/poto, 2026-02-17).
- **[MC, initial launch]** The initial revamped-MC launch on this server was considered badly overtuned ("giga overtuned, harder than AQ40"): unnerfed bosses were reportedly destroying realm-first-competing top guilds, and full MC clears took so long some guilds stopped doing them; Golemagg was cited as having health comparable to Chromaggus at one point — [[d:shaman#1473199115985752140]], [[d:shaman#1473199196902395990]], [[d:shaman#1473199312958787585]], [[d:shaman#1473199371737895226]], [[d:shaman#1473199733446152330]], [[d:shaman#1473199866967494749]], [[d:shaman#1473199903428710413]] (poto/Zhafu/Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-02-17).
- **[raid utility, general]** Guide-style checklist for playing Elemental/ranged support in raids without a dedicated guide (Dwi): behave like a ranged DPS but come within Earth Shock range periodically (~every 2 min) to drop totems affecting melee; Earth Shock while walking away from mechanics you can't otherwise act during; Earth Shock to interrupt interruptible enemy casts (heals, Shadow Volley, etc.); use the correct resistance totem when a fight calls for it (obvious fire fights, or when the raid leader calls for Greater Fire Protection Potions); refresh poison/disease-cleansing totems immediately when party members are afflicted; keep Tremor Totem active for any Fear-heavy fight — timing a Tremor cast ~5.5 seconds before a telegraphed Fear (Tremor pulses every 6 seconds) nearly instantly cures fear on cast. Do NOT drop Nature Resistance Totem casually — melee will prefer to eat damage over losing Windfury — [[d:shaman#1475607369789607957]], [[d:shaman#1475608230116720834]] (Dwi, 2026-02-24).

## PvP
- **[general]** Shaman is described as a top-priority kill target in PvP/Bloodring because of strong chain healing, ranged burst, and totem utility — "way too good for enemy melee to let them live," expect to be "chain banged by 3 alliance at once" regardless of spec — [[d:shaman#1470853367684141166]] (Zamka, 2026-02-10; his own dueling-grind experience on 2 characters confirms it as "a festival of fucking shaman as hard and fast as you can" [[d:shaman#1470853598903795876]]).
- **[PvP]** Chain Heal is essentially never used in small-scale/arena-style PvP (no -1 sec cast reduction talent path available there); it IS commonly used in large-scale PvP (WSG/AV) where a shaman can find a safe spot and blast Chain Heal into the group until OOM, since a stacked-up team makes it very efficient — [[d:shaman#1470853509183180850]], [[d:shaman#1470853945797640234]], [[d:shaman#1470854165080178798]], [[d:shaman#1470854282017505365]], [[d:shaman#1470854987251847449]] (Diving Instructor Knife/Zamka, 2026-02-10).
- **[PvP tips]** Always open in Ghost Wolf if expecting Rogues, since it prevents being Sapped; watch for Druid Hibernate; use Tinfoil/Tiny Balm + Noggenfogger to become Tauren-turned-Gnome-sized and hide inside your own totems for concealment; consumables ARE usable in Bloodring — [[d:shaman#1470853297660366872]], [[d:shaman#1470853835948953652]], [[d:shaman#1470854579158515793]], [[d:shaman#1470851970444038345]] (Boorenka/Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-02-10).
- **[PvP]** Water Shield (with the totem) can be used to break a Rogue/Druid out of stealth if they get too close, since it's a self-buff proc that can trigger on their approach — [[d:shaman#1470855625066942617]] (Zamka, 2026-02-10). The single biggest PvP threat cited repeatedly is Rogue "Dissolvent" poison openers — considered nearly unavoidable/hard-countered only by running away — [[d:shaman#1470855730998542520]], [[d:shaman#1470878945133072506]] (Spicyahi/Rocked, 2026-02-10).
- **[PvP utility]** Hex → Earth Shock (interrupt) → Hex, or Hex → Totemic Slam (stomp) → self-heal, cited as a strong CC/burst-denial chain against healers, despite Hex's long (5 min) cooldown and it not fully locking control (target can still auto-attack and interrupt spellcasts via that auto) — [[d:shaman#1471610575976136786]], [[d:shaman#1471610841274253365]], [[d:shaman#1471611308167139483]] (Spicyahi/Diving Instructor Knife/Dwi, 2026-02-13). Hex cannot be cast while moving (contrary to some claims) and can be interrupted like any other cast — [[d:shaman#1478442861753012469]], [[d:shaman#1478452708028579861]], [[d:shaman#1478453341842575582]] (Grinched, 2026-03-03/04). Hex works on almost nothing in raid trash (most mobs aren't Humanoid/Beast), so its value is mostly PvP/a few dungeon pulls — [[d:shaman#1478444194480783612]] (Zeruel, 2026-03-03). In Arenas specifically, Hex's cooldown is said to reset when entering a new arena match, meaning Troll's racial gets a "free" use every game — [[d:shaman#1478469508724297862]] (Nandieb, 2026-03-04).
- **[PvP, tank/resto]** Post-1.18.1, 13/17/21 is predicted to become the best PvP Resto spec (already considered so pre-nerf); the Rockbiter-resto nerf is largely believed by shamans to have been driven by a vocal PvP community complaining about Rockbiter-resto tankiness/damage reduction in Battlegrounds — Diving Instructor Knife argues the nerf barely affects the actual best PvP resto spec since 13/17/21 doesn't rely on the changed mechanics, calling the whole nerf misdirected — [[d:shaman#1473208657322836059]], [[d:shaman#1476645726711713803]], [[d:shaman#1478009666306052167]], [[d:shaman#1476645161176928266]], [[d:shaman#1476645319415566458]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-02-17, 02-26, 03-02). A dampening (diminishing-effectiveness-over-time) system for PvP is being introduced per-class/spec in 1.18.1; the community is skeptical devs can tune spec-specific dampening cleanly and worries it'll be a long-term-buggy "band-aid," while also noting the devs said PvP concerns specifically drove the Rockbiter changes despite claiming dampening would let them decouple PvP and PvE balance — [[d:shaman#1474074626006188083]], [[d:shaman#1474075242195583087]], [[d:shaman#1474075526837829830]] (Snert/Zhafu, 2026-02-19; contested/open — see Contested section).
- **[PvP]** Windfury Totem does not stack with any shaman weapon imbue for the caster (see Enchants section); in solo PvP/farming, Windfury Weapon imbue is favored over Rockbiter Weapon by most veteran players for raw average damage despite proc variance (see Stats section's DPS math) — [[d:shaman#1476931297246052464]] (Mimishammini, 2026-03-01).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[addons]** Call of Elements (CoE) is the standard totem-bar/quick-cast addon; at least three actively-maintained forks exist (MarcelineVQ, sica42, laytya versions) with no clear consensus on which is best — [[d:shaman#1473241444251598908]] (Melo, 2026-02-18). TotemNesia is an alternative totem-tracking addon by TheRealFayz, though one user reported icon-display bugs with it that went unresolved — [[d:shaman#1473253094631673991]], [[d:shaman#1473306222076756081]] (Hiryu/Melo, 2026-02-18). Nampower technically lets you drop all 4 totems on a single keypress natively without CoE, but CoE is still valued for its totem bar/cooldown-timer UI — [[d:shaman#1475307271784370349]] (poto, 2026-02-23).
- **[addons]** DoiteAuras (a "WeakAuras-light for Vanilla," by Doite) is the community-standard proc/cooldown/aura tracker, under active development through this chunk (multiple version releases: 1.5.2 through 1.5.8) — notable features added: no longer requires SuperWoW (works via Nampower alone), tracking buffs beyond the visible 32-buff cap (player-side, then target-side), custom conditional code-block tracking (e.g., for Heroic Strike queuing), and sound alerts on cooldown/aura state changes. Shared community import-string profiles exist for Warrior/Druid/Paladin/Mage/Shaman(Elemental)/Kara40-boss-debuffs/melee & caster consumables, collected via a subreddit — [[d:shaman#1475149925544235019]], [[d:shaman#1475152031168725013]], [[d:shaman#1475387880678359051]], [[d:shaman#1475388161591873586]], [[d:shaman#1475408012263821352]], [[d:shaman#1476890829988827146]], [[d:shaman#1476912125938110585]], [[d:shaman#1476912826256724183]] (Doite, 2026-02-19 through 03-01). For tank-specific DoiteAuras usage, tracking Clearcasting is highlighted as valuable for mana conservation, and elemental shield procs are called out as "the only thing useful to track" via WeakAuras-style addons for a shaman generally (most procs don't affect ability damage) — [[d:shaman#1474555347128811773]], [[d:shaman#1474559147297997073]], [[d:shaman#1474559412843712532]], [[d:shaman#1474559612706361559]], [[d:shaman#1474605011479826665]], [[d:shaman#1474605153284919326]] (Zaaparou/Nyamuu/Porkins, 2026-02-20).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[races]** Orc is generally rated best for pure DPS (Blood Fury racial, see Stats section); Tauren is rated best/BiS for tanking (extra HP racial, War Stomp AoE stun) and strong for PvP generally; Troll gives a haste racial (Berserking, situational) and slightly more tank threat, plus 5% increased healing for 10s every 3 min (called negligible); overall "Troll is the worst race for shamans unless you plan to only ever heal" per one strong opinion — [[d:shaman#1471894476053155871]], [[d:shaman#1471894833563046072]], [[d:shaman#1471895374372274186]], [[d:shaman#1471895698256433183]], [[d:shaman#1471896701525557431]], [[d:shaman#1471897006908903455]] (peter_hammer/Therz, 2026-02-13). Orc's Feral Spirit (wolves) racial is called "a garbage shaman spell," while Tauren's Totemic Slam (War Stomp equivalent) is "at least usable" — [[d:shaman#1471895698256433183]] (Therz, 2026-02-13); wolves mainly matter for a PvP pushback-absorb use case, and Hex (Troll) is a castable-while-moving CC mainly relevant to PvP/dungeons — [[d:shaman#1478420088867782927]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2026-03-01).
- **[races]** Tauren's HP racial is considered so strong that even a hypothetical nerf to 2.5% (from 5%) wouldn't make anyone drop the race; it's described as clearly better than everything Troll gets, especially combined with Plainsrunning (mount speed increase) usable in Battlegrounds — [[d:shaman#1478444318074212402]], [[d:shaman#1471897006908903455]], [[d:shaman#1471897242477527164]] (Zeruel/peter_hammer, 2026-02-13, 03-03). Plainsrunning + Water Walking together is called "the single greatest racial" by one veteran player for open-world QoL — [[d:shaman#1478420203955290194]] (Holidae, 2026-03-01). Tauren's melee-ability range bug (see Bugs & quirks) is not considered a strong reason to avoid the race — most players report never noticing it in practice — [[d:shaman#1476682444563288205]], [[d:shaman#1476682615560863806]], [[d:shaman#1476682724776611982]] (sailorVEVO, 2026-02-27).
- **[professions]** Universal recommendation is Engineering + any second profession for all specs (DPS, tank, healer) — it's the only profession broadly agreed to add personal DPS/utility (explosives, sappers for AoE threat) — [[d:shaman#1475212516761276660]], [[d:shaman#1473598065050128439]], [[d:shaman#1477232322800779264]] (Zhafu/Mithreon, 2026-02-15, 02-23, 02-28). See Tanking specifics section for tank-specific profession notes (Blacksmithing for the Fiery Chain FR set, Goldsmithing's spell-resist neck, Dreamthread for resto).
- **[gold]** Lasher farming (Maraudon) is a primary gold-farming method for shamans of any spec at 54+: mostly vendor-trash gold (roughly 3-4g/run reported), plus BoE drops sellable/disenchantable and herbs/other AH-sellable materials; herbalism is described as only a minor bonus to profitability, not a requirement — [[d:shaman#1472995989852913747]], [[d:shaman#1473082022149750886]], [[d:shaman#1473085958420435189]], [[d:shaman#1473091287032004811]] (Nandieb/Zaaparou, 2026-02-16).
- **[gold]** Server herb economy example: dreamfoil was reported as the most expensive herb on the (comparison) South Seas server due to potion demand (Flask of Titans, Arcane Elixir, Mageblood), and prices are expected to spike again once BWL raises elixir/potion consumption on Ambershire; conversely Gromsblood stays cheap indefinitely absent a new recipe demand — [[d:shaman#1472997601803763723]], [[d:shaman#1473005038589972658]], [[d:shaman#1473006123232788703]], [[d:shaman#1473006264354345020]], [[d:shaman#1473006472534298624]], [[d:shaman#1473006752407621800]] (Dwi/Kolobrnda/Zeruel, 2026-02-16).
- **[gold]** Shaman tanks specifically may need supplemental gold for Mana Potions/engineering explosives when tanking dungeons at very low level/gear ("without spamming mana pots and engineering explosives you're probably gonna have a bad time") — [[d:shaman#1477599035371290694]] (Zamka, 2026-03-01).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[bug]** Tauren-race shamans have a documented range-mismatch bug: their melee auto-attack range is longer than Stormstrike/Lightning Strike's usable range (the reverse of most races, where autos and abilities line up, or abilities slightly exceed auto range) — confirmed by multiple testers as Tauren-specific (a female Tauren and an orc both tested; the orc/troll gap between auto and ability range was much smaller) — reported to the dev theorycrafting channel but received no fix or acknowledgment within this chunk ("it's a feature now") — [[d:shaman#1476428524125421598]], [[d:shaman#1476441076393967838]], [[d:shaman#1476444199271268404]], [[d:shaman#1476451953419747349]], [[d:shaman#1476520679347327170]], [[d:shaman#1476520953247957063]], [[d:shaman#1476521833586233447]], [[d:shaman#1476523689708097650]] (Cariad/Rocked, 2026-02-26; originally reported by Cariad six months prior with no action taken).
- **[bug]** The Enhancing Totems talent correctly increases the underlying Strength stat (confirmed via character sheet, e.g. +76 instead of the base totem's +61) but the buff tooltip itself does not update to reflect the talent bonus — a display-only bug; talents that increase an aura's effect generally don't show correctly on the buff tooltip even though the actual stat gain applies properly — [[d:shaman#1474051825291231334]], [[d:shaman#1474052377702170808]] (Pluffyhoof/Zhafu, 2026-02-20).
- **[bug]** Hybrid "combined" elixirs (e.g. Concoction of Mongoose, Arcane Giant, Dreamwater/Dreamtonic) were reported to not actually grant their spell power/spell crit component correctly on a training dummy test, even though separate un-combined elixirs (Elixir of Mongoose, Firewater, etc.) work as expected — flagged as an ongoing, seemingly dev-dismissed bug report (conflated by devs with an unrelated stat-addon display bug) — [[d:shaman#1478434803043930153]], [[d:shaman#1478435336932692069]], [[d:shaman#1478477667333574858]], [[d:shaman#1478477940042760434]], [[d:shaman#1478478149120557159]] (rel, 2026-03-03/04). Separately, Arcane Giant + Dreamtonic previously did not stack their spell power bonus together (only Arcane Giant did) — reported fixed per a later changelog check — [[d:shaman#1475207084504252516]], [[d:shaman#1475207092003672104]], [[d:shaman#1475207107933634710]], [[d:shaman#1475208640339378529]] (oi-er, 2026-02-22).
- **[bug]** Chain Heal was confirmed (via official 1.18.1 patch notes) to have been incorrectly targeting/healing Totems and Guardians, not just players — now fixed; the separate "doesn't prioritize lowest-health target, just any injured target" behavior remains unfixed and is officially "working as intended" per devs — [[d:shaman#1476010066816536607]], [[d:shaman#1476011956103020546]], [[d:shaman#1475911772778139853]], [[d:shaman#1475906072920461403]] (sailorVEVO/Mithreon/Stunfisk, 2026-02-24/25).
- **[bug/quirk]** Recently-raid-looted items cannot be enchanted/imbued until the character relogs (a safeguard against enchanting an item that might still be traded within the post-loot trade window); relogging removes the trade-eligibility even before the 10-minute trade window expires, letting you safely enchant right away — [[d:shaman#1476724960239030312]], [[d:shaman#1476724798921904283]] (poto/otep1616, 2026-03-01).
- **[bug/quirk]** The Elemental talent intended to reduce Magma Totem's threat generation does not appear to reduce its actual mana cost, and separately the "Improved Fire Totems" investment intended to buff Magma Totem doesn't meaningfully change its output/threat for anyone (DPS or tank) — widely called a design oversight (see AoE Rotation section) — [[d:shaman#1475674214840143913]], [[d:shaman#1475675081844723774]], [[d:shaman#1475559461484626083]] (Zhafu/Holidae, 2026-02-23/24).
- **[quirk]** Elemental Weapons' Windfury proc bonus is a flat +25% added on top of whatever the base 9-PPM-derived proc chance already is for a given weapon speed (not a percentage-of-percentage multiplier) — [[d:shaman#1476706668803920148]] (Not Normal, 2026-03-01).
- **[quirk, resolved mid-chunk]** Lightning Shield's proc/tracking was reportedly broken for a period and was fixed via a DoiteAuras update mid-chunk, confirmed working again by testers — [[d:shaman#1475149925544235019]], [[d:shaman#1475152031168725013]] (Doite/Not Normal, 2026-02-19).
- **[quirk]** A green (level 1-ish) item, "Strangely Glyphed Legplates," was flagged as absurdly overtuned for its level bracket — 21 stam/21 str/10 defense plus effectively 1 crit and 1 dodge, more Int than every Elemental/Balance tier chest including T3.5 — widely mocked as clearly unintended itemization that nonetheless remains obtainable — [[d:shaman#1471378414303711324]], [[d:shaman#1471379388816691354]], [[d:shaman#1471380232408666112]] (Zeruel/Dwi, 2026-02-12).
- **[server incident]** Emerald Sanctum (a 1.18.1-era raid) was accidentally opened early on Ambershire before its intended release — theorized by Boorenka to be caused by a leftover release-failsafe/backup check from before the ZG/BWL launch sequencing existed, which incorrectly triggered when no official release flag was set for a given date. This let a random PUG raid clear it hours before organized "race-to-world-first" guilds could attempt it, causing community frustration; devs' options (rollback vs. let it ride vs. lock items) were all seen as lose-lose — [[d:shaman#1478374415841366146]], [[d:shaman#1478374512985772237]], [[d:shaman#1478374791512588371]], [[d:shaman#1478376196789113004]], [[d:shaman#1478376461944487998]], [[d:shaman#1478377296875225271]] (Holidae/Boorenka, 2026-03-03).

## Contested / open questions
- **[design]** Whether "spellhance" is even an intended playstyle (vs. an emergent byproduct of Flametongue's melee-range requirement) is disputed — no consensus definition exists even among practitioners; some define it as MB-weaving with a fast weapon, others (Not Normal, the self-described highest performer with the style) explicitly exclude Molten Blast weaving and just describe it as enh-with-Flametongue-and-more-fire-totems — [[d:shaman#1473356537341804668]], [[d:shaman#1473362895466401945]] (PlasticSpork/Not Normal, 2026-02-17; unresolved).
- **[design/tank]** Whether Rockbiter's damage shield blocks only physical incoming damage or all damage types is unclear even to tanks actively discussing the 1.18.1 patch notes — no definitive test/confirmation found in this chunk — [[d:shaman#1472630863836610816]], [[d:shaman#1472647419014680640]] (Zeruel, 2026-02-15).
- **[balance]** Whether the Enhancement T3.5 3-piece set bonus (unintended empowered-Lightning-Shield procs, ~150-200 DPS) should be nerfed to its "intended" ~50 DPS version, with the class baseline buffed to compensate, remains an open and heated dispute between theorycrafters (Not Normal) and players who value the fun/power of the current bugged version (Khandis/Kundir, Squid, Zamka) — [[d:shaman#1474871787870359813]], [[d:shaman#1474869812126683398]], [[d:shaman#1474821956300963962]] (2026-02-22; unresolved).
- **[design]** Whether Earthquake actually out-DPSes Lightning Bolt on non-crit casts is disputed between math (peter_hammer: yes, consistently, by 20-50%) and feel/played-experience (Kokuyo: a critting LB often "feels" better than a non-critting EQ) — see Rotation: single target section; unresolved as a community consensus even though the math side is more rigorously argued — [[d:shaman#1473612303055458432]], [[d:shaman#1473614643845926956]], [[d:shaman#1473619989209546763]] (2026-02-18).
- **[design]** Whether shaman tank needs a baseline defensive cooldown (like Warrior's Last Stand or Paladin's bubble) remains actively debated — Eluzia/Zamka/others argue current EHP + healer support is sufficient and a CD "isn't really a weakness" given how good shaman tank threat/mitigation already is, while others (Travoltino, Grinched-adjacent posters) argue the total lack of any damage-reduction CD becomes existential once BWL/AQ40-level burst lands, especially after the 1.18.1 tank nerfs — [[d:shaman#1475719803019722884]], [[d:shaman#1475719852927881238]], [[d:shaman#1476108643693760654]], [[d:shaman#1476111181428035755]], [[d:shaman#1478743640808624168]] (2026-02-24 to 03-04; long-running, unresolved).
- **[design]** Whether the resto/PvP-driven Rockbiter nerf actually targeted Elemental or Resto PvP is itself disputed within the community (peter_hammer says it was specifically about Resto tankiness in PvP; Travoltino initially believed it was about Elemental) — [[d:shaman#1472960871213826213]], [[d:shaman#1472963365671276667]] (2026-02-16; only partially resolved by peter_hammer's later clarification).
- **[open]** No definitive spell list exists in this chunk for everything that can proc Sigil of Ancient Accord — Lightning Shield, Earth Shock, and Lightning Strike are confirmed via separate mentions, but nobody gave a complete authoritative list when directly asked — [[d:shaman#1476252716882853949]] (Ordinary Joe, 2026-02-25).
- **[open]** Whether Blacksmithing or Leatherworking is the better second profession for a shaman tank was asked directly and never received a clear consensus answer in this chunk — [[d:shaman#1476041815240216626]] (Méroulkas, 2026-02-25).
- **[open]** Whether the exact armor-mitigation formula Turtle uses matches the commonly-cited vanilla wiki formula (`Armor / (Armor + 400 + 85*Level)`) or a modified version (with an attacker-level-scaling term) is unresolved — Zeruel found a discrepancy between the two but could not get in-game confirmation of which the server actually uses, leaving the exact "armor per stam" tank value (~25-30, his working estimate) uncertain — [[d:shaman#1471236646081921127]], [[d:shaman#1471238510617825533]], [[d:shaman#1471245024699093094]], [[d:shaman#1471245274738462964]] (Zeruel, 2026-02-12).
