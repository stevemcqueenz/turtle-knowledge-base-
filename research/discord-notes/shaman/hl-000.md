# Notes: #shaman hl chunk 000 (2021-04-18 .. 2025-05-20)

Scope note: almost all substantive content in this chunk concerns the "CC2" shaman class-rework patch (rolled out ~2024-11-01, hotfixed heavily through 2025) and its follow-up balance passes into 2025-05. This predates 1.18.1 "Nightmares of Ursol" (prelim. notes 2025-10-03); treat CC2 facts as "state as of CC2, up to 2025-05", which 1.18.1 may have further changed. Flagged as "(CC2)" where relevant. Kara40 "Tower of Karazhan" launched partway through this chunk (referenced from ~2025-03).

## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
(No content in this chunk names 1.18.1; all patch-change material below is about the "CC2" shaman rework, Nov 2024 onward, kept here as the only patch-change info present.)
- **[General]** CC2 (~2024-11-01) reworked shaman broadly: added Lightning Strike (melee/nature hybrid), Electrify (elemental crit-stacking, replacing flat crit bonus), a shaman tank kit (Rockbiter Weapon threat/DR scaling, Stoneclaw Totem taunt via Earthshaker Slam), and retuned Stormstrike, Flurry, Windfury, Bloodlust, Crackling Thunder Totem — [[d:shaman#1301636855833297007]] (30, 2024-11-01), [[d:shaman#1301961189806571624]] (outsider, T3 enh set bonuses, 2024-11-01).
- **[Enhancement]** Day-1 CC2 nerfs vs. plan: SS/LS mana costs doubled, WF internal cooldown doubled, Flurry cut from 30% to 15%, SS/LS could no longer proc Windfury/Crackling Thunder — a last-minute panic change after devs found WF had no ICD due to a coding error — [[d:shaman#1301965143571304458]] (hiiklaas, 2024-11-01), [[d:shaman#1308124738417332345]] (Mist, confirmed dev-side, 2024-11-18). Later (2025-03) a shaman confirmed via forum info that no ICD was ever actually implemented even on the test server — devs mistakenly believed one existed and "increased" a nonexistent 3s ICD to 6s; only the Flurry and Flametongue nerfs actually shipped — [[d:shaman#1346243470507114639]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-03-04).
- **[Enhancement]** Nov 2024 consensus (dozens of messages): OOM in ~20-30s, threat overwhelming even with reduction talents, DPS far below Warriors/Paladins/Hunters despite BiS — [[d:shaman#1302994816572854293]] (Kordinator, 2024-11-07), [[d:shaman#1303512089486299196]] (Stabi, ZG/BWL/AQ40, +10, 2024-11-06), [[d:shaman#1304315384970870815]] (Keegan, "half that of Warriors", +6).
- **[Enhancement]** Mid-Nov–Dec 2024 hotfixes: reduced SS/LS mana costs, fixed WF proc bug (was ~10-15% vs. intended 20%, bug #9518; retested at ~12% [[d:shaman#1305173853781102633]] qoth 2024-11-10; later statistically confirmed ~22%/close to 20% [[d:shaman#1331592945219928178]] Akil, 2025-01-22), fixed 0-mana Stormstrike bug, LS confirmed proccing WF, Ancestral Knowledge fixed/clarified as +10% (later reduced) total-stats multiplier — [[d:shaman#1305172958049992777]] (Kordinator, +7), [[d:shaman#1308102261494251540]] (Aokana), [[d:shaman#1319657951665062030]] (Grom, 2024-12-20).
- **[Enhancement]** By 2025-01, mana "finally feeling good": Water Shield for self-sustain vs. Lightning Shield for burst with a Paladin/mana support — a real choice — [[d:shaman#1305462811878490152]] (Akil, 2024-11-11).
- **[Enhancement]** Feb 2025: dev team nerfed Enhancement T3 tier-set stats (framed as "improvements") without explanation; large, sustained community backlash citing lack of communication and comparing favorably-treated Paladin tier changes; devs (Dragunovi/Jamey/Fat Pibble) engaged partially but were seen as dismissive — [[d:shaman#1344242517981270059]] (Rinzu, asks why, +10), [[d:shaman#1344310500699406418]] (Kordinator, "0 argument for it", +7), [[d:shaman#1346858124279021599]] (Kordinator, T3 wasn't overpowered, breakdown by slot).
- **[Enhancement]** March–May 2025: recurring, unresolved complaints that Windfury-proc-driven threat spikes (triple-crit WF procs) make Enhancement the highest-threat-per-damage DPS spec, worse than fully-BiS/Titan's-Fury-equipped Warrior/Paladin tanks can handle; devs (Jamey) stated their data didn't show a catastrophic problem but promised to monitor — [[d:shaman#1356065572231839866]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, "Threat is not managed when you get a triple windfury crit", +4), [[d:shaman#1367197743235469384]] (Therix, pulled aggro on KT before MC went out, 2025-04-30), [[d:shaman#1358909018902364171]] (Therix, +9, 2025-04-08).
- **[Elemental]** Electrify was the most contested elemental change: hard to build 5 stacks in PvP, stacks fall off between pulls in PvE, locks ele into Elemental Mastery to ramp — [[d:shaman#1310817616268492841]] (Spaz, +3), [[d:shaman#1302377671543492719]] (Temu, +4). Reduced from 5 stacks to 2 stacks in a hotfix around late Dec 2024/Jan 2025, after which most players (even former critics) called Elemental "totally fine now" / "quite strong in both pvp and pve again" — [[d:shaman#1318635164594733156]] (Rinzu, "Haaxor said electrify will see changes with this reset", 2024-12-17), [[d:shaman#1341313370581893216]] (limoseng, "electrify is totally fine now", +3, 2025-02-18), [[d:shaman#1364135965748232192]] (Papairoh, confirms 2-stack version strong in both PvP/PvE, 2025-04-22).
- **[Elemental]** Clearcasting bugged to consume both charges on a single Lightning Bolt cast instead of one at a time; fixed at a subsequent weekly restart (~2024-12-28) — [[d:shaman#1308490501904666707]] (Saphie), [[d:shaman#1309958153156431952]] (Papairoh, detailed proof), [[d:shaman#1322614328054255696]] (Dragunovi, confirms fix, +4).
- **[Elemental]** Fire Totems (Magma/Fire Nova/Searing) confirmed via bug report #12275 to get 0% hit/crit benefit from the shaman's own stats — an unaddressed CC2 oversight still present through the end of this chunk (2025-05) — [[d:shaman#1354960635103936564]] (Vigrid, "Absolutely fucking embarassing that the CC2 team did not address fire totems whatsoever", 2025-03-28), [[d:shaman#1339135049198604330]] (limoseng, same complaint, 2025-02-12).
- **[Elemental/Tank]** Salvation-related threat-reduction nerf (~5%, ~2025-03-31) triggered a second major "poor dev communication" flare-up, distinct from the T3 nerf drama — [[d:shaman#1356062964956008448]] (Abbe, +9), [[d:shaman#1356186359173480611]] (Kordinator, detailed rebuttal to devs' data claim).
- **[Tank]** New tools (Rockbiter Weapon scaling AP + 10-15% DR via 2.5-tier; Stoneclaw Totem "Earthshaker Slam" taunt) made 5-man and even some raid tanking viable, but with recurring bugs: Totemic Alignment (totem threat transfer) reported non-functional at times — [[d:shaman#1323816738193932421]] (Rejuv, 2025-01-01) — and Calming Winds (threat-reduction talent) reportedly increasing rather than reducing threat (bug #9478) — [[d:shaman#1306358779868352533]] (Stabi, bug roundup: #9518/#8692/#9523/#9481/#9478, 2024-11-14).
- **[Tank]** Buff cap: 13 raid buffs + up to 17 consumable buffs + racial/trinket/totem procs caused shaman tanks to hit the buff cap and be unable to receive shields/pots mid-fight — [[d:shaman#1304185446829391882]] (Kordinator, detailed breakdown, +5, 2024-11-08).
- **[Item bug]** Ghaz'rilla Scale Armor's on-equip "Adds 3 Lightning damage" effect was found to consume a Stormstrike charge for no extra damage (3×1.2 still rounds to 3), gimping the very spec it's itemized for — reported to devs, unresolved in-chunk — [[d:shaman#1351556636598337657]] (Maggat, 2025-03-19).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Enhancement]** Bottom-tier DPS in both PvE/PvP through Nov-Dec 2024; improved markedly by ~2025-01+ once mana/WF bugs were fixed, though threat remained the defining complaint through May 2025 — [[d:shaman#1303385279444549664]] (Gigatexas, ZG/Naxx/AQ40, +4), [[d:shaman#1325410476406083616]] (Akos, Jan 2025 problem list, still OOM-prone but playable).
- **[Enhancement/Elemental]** Turtlogs data point (Naxx Patchwerk, top-10 avg, ~2025-04): Fire Mage/Demo Warlock ~1.9k DPS; Elemental and Enhancement ~1.3k (~25-30% behind the top, but many other classes also sat at 1.3-1.5k, so "pretty average", not uniquely bad) — [[d:shaman#1357460367495663837]] (Yanapaq, 2025-04-04).
- **[Elemental]** Split opinion on whether the CC2 net change was a buff: several experienced players (Papairoh) argued the mana/clearcasting rework was a net positive once the CC bug was fixed; others felt Ele lagged behind other casters overall — [[d:shaman#1308526034219565086]] (Papairoh, "large majority of ele shaman changes have been great", 2024-11-20) vs. [[d:shaman#1310802881296994414]] (Mist, "Ele is lacking a lot compared to other casters", 2024-11-26). By 2025-03, Barnyard rated it "Elemental has been gimped with the worst talent in the game (Electrify), Enhance is still a gambling simulator but less shit, Resto is still the best spec" — [[d:shaman#1337485994806611989]] (Barnyard, 2025-02-07).
- **[Resto]** Considered strong/largely untouched-or-buffed by CC2, centered on Chain Heal; a 2024-12 exploit made 8/8 T1 (Undertow/Primal Surge interaction with Chain Heal bounces re-proccing Water Shield charges) effectively BiS for raw output — [[d:shaman#1305074980530290710]] (Vigrid, detailed logs, 2024-12-10). Deep-resto was already viable at the highest CC1-era raiding level (Mortality guild ran full deep-resto, fastest MC/BWL/AQ40) — [[d:shaman#1304522289349660776]] (Papairoh, 2024-11-08). T1 8pc still called "goated" in Naxx as of 2025-03 — [[d:shaman#1333403941928701993]] (Mayenna).
- **[Tank]** Explicitly a "5-man / early-raid" tank, not a full Warrior/Paladin/Druid replacement; a detailed community tank-guide rated it 7/10 vs. paladin/warrior's 9-10/10, citing no stat-debuff (no Demoralizing-Shout analog), no panic defensive CD, and no +Defense talent — [[d:shaman#1351284411542405120]] (Yanapaq, full guide, 2025-03-18). Enhancement (tank tree) has 48 total talent points available vs. Protection Paladin 50 and Protection Warrior 53 — cited as concrete evidence of a shallower defensive toolkit — [[d:shaman#1348871490166325359]] (Yanapaq, 2025-03-11). Viable through Naxx for OT roles specifically (strong single-target threat, weak AoE) — [[d:shaman#1349076690680414281]] (Rinzu, 2025-03-11), [[d:shaman#1325819109438525451]] (Aokana, 2025-01-06).
- **[PvP]** Enhancement/Resto rated strong support/heals in PvP; Elemental burst strong once electrify fixed, but still an easy focus-target once caught (low defensives) — [[d:shaman#1314297992265863329]] (lmao, +4). By 2025-02/03, several claim shaman (esp. Ele/Enh-Resto hybrid tank-heal builds) had become "way too strong" in PvP, prompting nerf debates independent of PvE balance — [[d:shaman#1338602857980756038]] (Barnyard), [[d:shaman#1358753456139141294]] (Akos, on tankhance/resto PvP builds).

## Leveling
- **[Enhancement]** Levels 1-20ish: 1H weapon + shield with Rockbiter + Shocks; ~20+ switch to a slower weapon with Flametongue Weapon; Windfury Weapon available at 30+ but weak/inconsistent without gear — [[d:shaman#1302013756112638045]] (Aokana, 2024-11-02).
- **[Enhancement, post-CC2]** 40+ reported to feel bad: WF barely procs, Lightning Strike underwhelming without Lightning Shield, Water Shield's return is poor, Shocks drain mana after ~2 mobs — [[d:shaman#1308572403135418368]] (Therix, level 45, 2024-11-20). By 2025, consensus leveling order: Enhance to Stormstrike, then Elemental Focus/Clearcasting in the Elemental tree (with the 3% hit talents along the way), then back into Enhancement — grinding (esp. off caster mobs) faster than questing, Shocks often a mana waste while leveling — [[d:shaman#1343800681689124937]] (Ennvii, HC leveler, 2025-02-25), [[d:shaman#1343802382923989013]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, very detailed level-by-level order, 2025-02-25).
- **[Enhancement leveling]** Frostbrand + Frost Shock combo (guaranteed crit via Elemental Weapons on a frost-shocked target) called strong/near-BiS for leveling once Elemental Weapons + Elemental Focus are both up (near-100% Clearcasting uptime); Rockbiter still generally the most consistent imbue before that point (no split damage, benefits fully from crits) — [[d:shaman#1336328196781768765]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-02-04), [[d:shaman#1371016218990870619]] (DemonBoyJr, "feels op", 2025-05-11), [[d:shaman#1371499015161778237]] (Heathen, sequencing advice, 2025-05-12).
- **[Resto, Hardcore]** Viable for HC leveling: Nature's Swiftness (late-acquired) is a life-saving button; Flametongue + fast dagger + shield keeps kill speed acceptable — [[d:shaman#1313210374870536193]] (JustBe, 2024-12-02). Generally considered painfully slow to level pure Resto solo; a Warrior-duo cleave partner or constant dungeon-queueing recommended if going Resto — [[d:shaman#1352617253824364585]] (Rinzu, 2025-03-21), [[d:shaman#1367520844942802986]] (Papairoh, 2025-05-01).
- **[Tank]** Shield Spec and Improved Ghost Wolf both called "useless for leveling" past ~level 20 — [[d:shaman#1323436395062231172]] (Choogz, +9, 2024-12-31); disputed by Akos/DemonBoyJr who value Shield Spec's flat mitigation early when stats are low — [[d:shaman#1347375943391514714]] (DemonBoyJr, 2025-03-07).
- **[Tank/mixed]** Earthbind Totem doubles as a defensive tool while solo leveling: root and disengage when low on health, letting threat/kill-order finish mobs off — [[d:shaman#1315488884364410881]] (Akos, 2024-12-09). Stoneclaw Totem gives free "extra HP" (its heal-on-cast component) whenever pulling more than 1 mob while leveling — [[d:shaman#1345332727834214483]] (Zaaparou, 2025-03-01).
- **[Weapon choice, leveling]** Slow weapons favored generally for Stormstrike/Lightning Strike scaling; for Frostbrand specifically a fast weapon (dagger) can work well since the crit is guaranteed post-Elemental-Weapons — [[d:shaman#1367778615869177858]] (Lofam, 2025-05-02), [[d:shaman#1373515998325510186]] (DemonBoyJr — Daggers generally weak for Enh outside meme spellhance builds like Blade of Eternal Darkness/Ornate Bloodstone Dagger, 2025-05-18).
- **[Professions]** Mining (hot due to JC-leveling demand), Skinning (early coin), Herbalism (weaker post-lasher-nerf, farm Grave Moss/Black Lotus), Leatherworking (custom "Grifter's" set good for enhancement, mid-40s), Engineering (recommended for PvP or raid sappers) — [[d:shaman#1302499311485194251]] (outsider, 2024-11-03). Engineering reaffirmed as the BiS DPS profession for Enhance; gathering professions (Mining/Herbalism) recommended if gold is the priority — [[d:shaman#1363065820569272431]] (Rinzu, 2025-04-19).
- **[Turtle specific]** Shamans cannot dual-wield (frequent newbie surprise) — [[d:shaman#1318157235016171553]] (solis, +7, 2024-12-16).
- **[Race]** Tauren shamans receive their class/racial spell (Totem Slam) at level 1 baseline; Orc and Troll must complete a level-40 racial quest chain to unlock theirs (Bloodlust-adjacent / Hex) — [[d:shaman#1370277629235691600]] (DemonBoyJr, 2025-05-09).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Enhancement, post-CC2]** Core loop: auto-attack → Bloodlust (on CD) → Shock (Flame/Frost/Earth share one CD, forcing a meaningful per-use choice) → Stormstrike → Lightning Strike, with Lightning Shield up for damage or Water Shield for sustain — [[d:shaman#1346605098591322122]] (Insomniac, 2025-03-05), [[d:shaman#1342135225932709999]] (Windshade, design-praise post, "shocks share CDs so I have to make a meaningful decision", 2025-02-20).
- **[Enhancement]** Slow weapons favored over fast for SS/LS weapon-damage scaling; post-CC2 Rockbiter's threat became a flat modifier to ALL sources (not just autoattacks), so SS/LS benefit doubly from slow weapons under Rockbiter — [[d:shaman#1302610238024978433]] (Zamka, +1, 2024-11-05), [[d:shaman#1339670372659757056]] (Rinzu, explains the rework, 2025-02-13).
- **[Elemental]** Single-target: rank-4 Lightning Bolt for mana efficiency, max rank for Clearcasting procs or short fights; Chain Lightning on CD for trash or only when Clearcasting is up (never blind on long fights); downrank on very long fights (Nefarian/Sapphiron) once mana runs low; Earth Shock reserved for movement/interrupt windows — [[d:shaman#1308506796104749167]] (Papairoh, +2, 2024-11-19), [[d:shaman#1341574365589803151]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, near-identical rotation confirmed, 2025-02-19), [[d:shaman#1303443718803689595]] (Mudbutt, downranked ~50% on Nefarian/Sapphiron, 2024-11-07).
- **[Elemental]** Electrify opener/upkeep: cast Lightning Bolt, then Elemental Mastery (grants a 2nd Electrify stack instantly), then Chain Lightning; if EM is on CD, opening with Chain Lightning (even at 0 stacks) beats casting two rank-1 Lightning Bolts for overall damage — [[d:shaman#1358190619583054005]] (𝓜𝓲𝓭𝓸𝓴𝓸, 2025-04-06).
- **[Elemental]** With 8pc T3, Shocks (esp. Earth Shock, capped at 20yd) join the rotation for the set's damage buff but are mana-inefficient; many preferred 4pc + off-set for mana over full 8pc — [[d:shaman#1307506344311652473]] (Mudbutt, 2024-11-17).
- **[Elemental hit priority]** At AQ40-level gear, ~1 hit rating ≈ 12 SP-equivalent, ~1 crit rating ≈ 10-11 SP-equivalent for output value; overcapping hit (past the 16% boss cap, of which 3% is talents + 1% is the Druid buff) to drop SP/crit is a DPS loss — [[d:shaman#1366078370647117935]] (Papairoh, 2025-04-27), [[d:shaman#1367517284914167821]] (Papairoh, worked example, 2025-05-01).

## Rotation: AoE / multi-target
- **[Tank AoE threat]** Canonical opener: Chain Lightning pull → immediately Fire Nova Totem → Stoneclaw Totem as the pack arrives → Lightning Strike (Water Shield for mana/threat) → tab Earth Shock → tab Stormstrike, keep tab-targeting; on 3-4 mob packs, skip AoE totems entirely (not worth the mana) — [[d:shaman#1311814743362764861]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2024-11-29), [[d:shaman#1331758186423783597]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, expanded playstyle notes — only fire-totem giant packs of 4-6, spread ES/LS/SS across 3 targets without a fire totem for packs of 3, 2025-01-23).
- **[Tank AoE, endgame view]** By 2025-05, the recommended technique explicitly avoids totems/explosives: burst Chain Lightning or an Earth Shock + strike onto each separate mob in a short window IS the AoE threat; relying on totem-threat talents/rotation is called a habit that "promotes bad habits" for new tank players — [[d:shaman#1374145028611506267]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-05-20); disputed by poqoqu0741 who notes this advice assumes overgeared players carrying undergeared groups, and that fresh-60/undergeared tanks genuinely need the totem talent on 5+ mob packs — [[d:shaman#1374187730480791572]] (poqoqu0741, 2025-05-20; disputed).
- **[Tank AoE]** Stoneclaw Totem: no target limit, pulses AoE threat like a "tremor totem" (tooltip calling it a taunt is misleading, it is not a hard taunt); 30s internal CD, not spammable — [[d:shaman#1313272691616252016]] (Akos, 2024-12-03).
- **[Enhancement/Elemental AoE]** Fire totems (Magma/Searing) and Flametongue Totem scale poorly and are a small % of trash damage in logs (~5% Fire Nova, less Magma) vs. Chain Lightning at ~40% of elemental trash damage; widely cited as a weak point of CC2 for both DPS specs, compounded by the fire-totem hit/crit-scaling bug (see Bugs) — [[d:shaman#1319681814687649903]] (Grom, log-based numbers, 2024-12-20).
- **[Elemental]** Flametongue Totem vs. Searing Totem for raid trash: FT averages ~17k total melee damage over a Naxx/Kara40 raid (~1-2% of a 4-melee group's damage); Searing Totem only worth it if not giving FT to melee (i.e., not in a melee group) or when solo — [[d:shaman#1372190476211458098]] (& Enjoyer, worked-out math, 2025-05-14).
- **[Tank]** Multi-target without Stoneclaw: rotate autoattacks/abilities across 3-4 targets, use Taunt (Earthshaker Slam) only as an emergency button; on packs >4, healing/shield-charge threat (water/earth shield procs) also contributes AoE threat across the whole pack — [[d:shaman#1313075786525442090]] (peter_hammer, 2024-12-02), [[d:shaman#1347592475212255293]] (Rinzu, 2025-03-07).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Hit, tank]** Post-CC2 weapon-skill rework removed the old 1% hit "suppression", raising physical hit softcap for tanks from 6% to 8% (5% if not taking Elemental Devastation) — [[d:shaman#1317826835882709023]] (Rinzu, correcting an older guide, 2024-12-15). By 2025-04, community flagged that BiS shaman-tank gear itemizes 5-6% OVER the 8% cap with no easy way to redistribute it, called "bad design" — [[d:shaman#1364042321183768666]] (Zamka, 2025-04-22).
- **[Hit, elemental]** Spell hit cap vs. bosses is 16% (12% needed from gear after 3% talent + 1% Druid buff, or less with T3 4-set's 3% hit); being 1% under cap can be correct if it preserves more SP/crit — [[d:shaman#1366078370647117935]] (Papairoh, 2025-04-27).
- **[Tank threat]** Rockbiter Weapon gives +30% threat on melee-based attacks (autos, Stormstrike, Lightning Strike), further improved by 2.5-tier set; the rework changed it from a flat per-swing bonus to a modifier on ALL threat sources; totems contribute 60% of their "threat value" to the shaman with Totemic Alignment; Earth Shock does 1.5x threat (not 2x) — [[d:shaman#1315493130715271250]] (Akos, detailed numbers, 2024-12-09), [[d:shaman#1339670372659757056]] (Rinzu, rework explanation, 2025-02-13), [[d:shaman#1318352471814439002]] (Akos, "earth shock only does 1,5x threat not 2", 2024-12-17).
- **[Tank]** Rockbiter's threat is now dynamic (scales with weapon/AP) rather than the old fixed TPS; at pre-raid-BiS gear this is initially LOWER passive TPS than the old flat Rockbiter (~114 vs. ~169 est.) but scales up with better weapons/strikes/shields — [[d:shaman#1350989362187337728]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-03-17).
- **[Tank]** Crush/crit immunity (Defense ~140/440 rating range cited) matters less than raw stat gain except very near the cap — [[d:shaman#1306625738526556160]] (qoth, 2024-11-14); shamans realistically cannot reach crush-immune without sacrificing too many threat/EHP stats, should target high EHP + high threat instead, "kinda like a bear" — [[d:shaman#1349076690680414281]] (Rinzu, 2025-03-11), [[d:shaman#1358753456139141294]] (Akos, "we are bears with mana", 2025-04-07).
- **[Elemental]** Spell Penetration useless for PvE (no raid boss found with >baseline 24 Nature Resistance, a floor that can't be reduced); minor PvP value only — [[d:shaman#1322765402341904424]] (Mudbutt, 2024-12-29).
- **[Enhancement DPS stat priority]** Str (most important, boosted further by a 5%-raw-stats talent) > AP from items ≥ similar Str item > Agi (1% crit per ~20 Agi) > Stam ≈ Int (roughly equal: Int for mana, Stam for survival) after hit-cap/weapon-skill (315 skill cap; glancing blows do ~45% damage uncapped vs. ~95% at 315 skill) — [[d:shaman#1344741721480757349]] (Mist, detailed breakdown, 2025-02-27); disputed slightly by Akos who leans Agi > Str for Enh DPS (crit → ES landing, Flurry uptime, Clearcasting mana, Bloodlust) — [[d:shaman#1363100622575898654]] (Akos, 2025-04-19).
- **[Weapon choice, DPS]** Threshold formula for when Windfury out-damages Rank-5-Frostbrand+WF+FT against a ~12.8%-armor-mitigation target: Threshold AP ≈ 1382 − 4×(weapon base damage); e.g. a 150-base-damage weapon needs ~782 AP before WF pulls ahead (formula doesn't account for LS/SS proccing WF) — [[d:shaman#1373079025034989650]] (& Enjoyer, 2025-05-17).
- **[Tank stat priority, non-verified]** Str/Agi/Sta/Int/Spi rough conversions cited from a "WoW Classic Wiki"-style source for Tauren (single source, unverified in-chunk): 9 Str=18 AP+0.45% block w/ shield, 5 Agi=10 Armor+0.25% Crit/Dodge, 9.7 Sta=101.85 HP, 8.5 Int=127.5 MP+0.13% spell crit, 10.2 Spi=19.04 MP5 — [[d:shaman#1302100796652912651]] (Kahno).

- **[All specs, pinned]** Flametongue, Frostbrand, and Windfury can all proc Clearcasting on Turtle (unlike vanilla); Flametongue also procs Elemental Devastation (as of the patch current at the time of posting); tested at level 60 with best weapon buffs; shorter fights favor Windfury less due to its RNG — [[d:shaman#1112676953393217597]] (Ashnagarr, PINNED, 2023-05-29; credits Defcamp & Melderon TV).
- **[Turtle specific]** Frostbrand does not work against raid bosses/most raid trash because Frost Shock (needed to guarantee the crit via Elemental Weapons) doesn't apply its slow to boss-immune targets — cited as a core reason Enhancement struggled in raids — [[d:shaman#1303385279444549664]] (Gigatexas, +4, 2024-11-06).
- **[Enhancement, imbue choice]** Rockbiter: most consistent, no split damage (bigger crits), doesn't rely on procs, always-on 10% DR via Elemental Weapons once talented — recommended default outside of pure DPS windows — [[d:shaman#1362221656520654982]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, "always has been, even pre-cc2, even in vanilla", 2025-04-17), [[d:shaman#1336329116156432428]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, split-damage explanation, 2025-02-04). ## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Enhancement]** Bloodlust nerfed to a 5-minute cooldown and party-only (not raid-wide), widely seen as a weak capstone relative to its opportunity cost — [[d:shaman#1306362808996204555]] (Garth, +3, 2024-11-14), [[d:shaman#1303512089486299196]] (Stabi's list of "wins/losses", 2024-11-06). No baseline defensive cooldown exists for shaman (e.g. no Shamanistic Rage/Astral Shift analog); repeated community suggestions for one (Shamanistic Rage-style 30% damage reduction + mana-on-hit) were never implemented in this chunk — [[d:shaman#1304355359674335252]] (Noce, suggestion, 2024-11-09), [[d:shaman#1311669572025454612]] (Cukorbaro, suggestion, 2024-11-28).
- **[Utility]** Grounding Totem absorbs one targeted spell — useful proactively against casters; reported broken on specific raid-boss spells (AQ40 Twins' Shadow Bolt, ZG Hakkar) via bug report #12671 — [[d:shaman#1360807816633384980]] (phyx, 2025-04-13).
- **[Utility, tank]** Against magic damage, shaman tanks lean on a combination of resistance totems (prevent by school), Grounding Totem (negate targeted casts), and a ~5-6s-CD interrupt (Earth Shock/Wind Shear-equivalent) as a proactive "Lego-piece" defensive kit instead of a reactive CD; against physical damage, high dodge (not crush immunity) is the main tool — [[d:shaman#1354213123988525241]] (Akos, 2025-03-26).
- **[Utility]** Earthbind Totem used for kiting large pulls while still building threat at range with Earth Shock/self-heals — [[d:shaman#1331761194306113557]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-01-23).
- **[Design commentary]** Windfury's raid-wide strength structurally forces Enhancement, Elemental, and Resto shamans alike into melee groups (for WF/Str totems) even when a caster/healer synergy group would suit their spec better — a repeatedly raised, unresolved structural design issue — [[d:shaman#1345521043615383574]] (Akos, 2025-03-02), [[d:shaman#1346211795135627366]] (Akos, "very hard to do anything about it without totally revamping how WF works", 2025-03-04); see also [[d:shaman#1343442270518775890]] (Vigrid, Ele/Resto "chained to melee groups who do basically nothing for them", 2025-02-24).
- **[Bug]** Totemic Alignment (totem-threat-transfer talent) reported completely non-functional at points (0 threat gained from any totem type) — [[d:shaman#1323816738193932421]] (Rejuv, 2025-01-01).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Core kit]** Rockbiter Weapon = shaman tank's "defensive stance" (AP + scaling threat + damage reduction via Elemental Weapons, up to 15% with the 2.5-tier set); Earth Shock generates strong ranged threat/semi-taunt; Stoneclaw Totem's "Earthshaker Slam" is a real taunt added by CC2 — [[d:shaman#1363691702992371763]] (WheezeTheJuice, beginner basics, 2025-04-21), [[d:shaman#1315589854469292122]] (Rinzu, 2024-12-09).
- **[Weapon choice]** Slow 1H+shield or slow 2H both viable; slower weapons benefit Stormstrike/Lightning Strike's weapon-damage scaling and Rockbiter's threat modifier more; 2H tanking is viable ("like CC1 Fury-Prot") but loses ~1/3 of armor value from no shield, can't block, and requires a shield swap to Taunt — [[d:shaman#1316112373223915570]] (Azure, 2024-12-11), [[d:shaman#1325819996684681347]] (Aokana, 2H comparison to Furyprot, 2025-01-06).
- **[Threat, AoE]** No reliable AoE taunt; totems (Stoneclaw/Fire Nova/Magma) are the closest analog but expensive and capped in effectiveness; devs (Haaxor) stated the totems' AoE threat "works as intended" (i.e., intentionally sub-par) when questioned — [[d:shaman#1369540777855680552]] (Rinzu, relays dev statement, 2025-05-07), [[d:shaman#1369539701954056224]] (Vigrid, "Protadin scaling just shits on us in AoE threat", 2025-05-07).
- **[Threat, single-target]** Very strong ST threat via Rockbiter + Earth Shock + Stormstrike/Lightning Strike burst; considered on par with or better than Warrior/Paladin at lower gear, but the *ratio* of threat-to-damage stays poor as gear improves (competing DPS classes' damage scales faster than the shaman's threat-generating kit) — [[d:shaman#1315731513345839215]] (Azure, 2024-12-09), [[d:shaman#1320000363553620012]] (Akos, ele/enh both massively over-threat their own damage output at BM even vs. undergeared party, 2024-12-21).
- **[Mana]** The single most-repeated shaman-tank complaint throughout the chunk: fire/AoE totems and Lightning Shield both drain mana fast; practical fixes cited: use Water Shield as default, only drop AoE totems on genuinely large packs (4-6+), pull with Rank-1 Lightning Bolt or a single Earth Shock instead of Chain Lightning to save mana, don't overspend once comfortably ahead on threat — [[d:shaman#1331758186423783597]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, detailed playstyle-fix, 2025-01-23), [[d:shaman#1348190180623908864]] (community consensus repeated, 2025-03-09).
- **[Defensives]** No panic defensive CD, no stat-reducing ability, no +Defense talent — the three most-cited structural gaps vs. Warrior/Paladin/Druid tanks; compensations: layered "Lego" defensive totems/shields (Earth Shield+LS, Stoneskin, Grace of Air), the Hakkar (ZG) trinket as an optional defensive-CD trinket slot, and defensive consumables — [[d:shaman#1351284411542405120]] (Yanapaq, full guide, 2025-03-18), [[d:shaman#1354212003153051690]] (Akos, 2025-03-26).
- **[Race]** Tauren (+5% HP, Totemic Slam AoE stun) and Troll (self-heal racial, Berserking) are the most commonly recommended tank races; Orc's Hardiness (stun resist) is reported bugged/nerfed (25%→15%, contested) post-changes — [[d:shaman#1349706039339122728]] (limoseng, 2025-03-13), [[d:shaman#1354899288861311029]] (DemonBoyJr, 2025-03-27).
- **[Turtle specific]** Shaman tanks generate meaningful threat via healing/shield-charge procs on top of direct-damage threat, unlike most other tanks — worth accounting for when tab-targeting threat across a pack — [[d:shaman#1316113342154145918]] (Mimishammini, 2024-12-11).

## Healing specifics
- **[Resto]** Chain Heal is central to the spec and (per critics) narrows design space — its bounce mechanic combined with the T1 8-piece bonus (Undertow/Primal Surge triggering extra Water Shield procs off the same cast) was reported as an exploitable, near-BiS healing-throughput loop as of Dec 2024 — [[d:shaman#1305074980530290710]] (Vigrid, detailed Faerlina/Patchwerk/Thaddius-P2 logs, 2024-12-10).
- **[Resto]** Spirit Link's 15-yard cast range (much shorter than 40-yard totems/Chain Heal) makes it awkward to land on a moving tank; no dev response found in-chunk — [[d:shaman#1341918713385058374]] (Cariad, 2025-02-20; question unanswered).
- **[Resto]** In practice, raid healing rarely calls for full-rank Healing Wave; Lesser Healing Wave / Chain Heal cover the large majority of raid healing, with full Healing Wave reserved for Nature's Swiftness emergency casts — [[d:shaman#1343496728803938365]] (Mist, 2025-02-24).
- **[Resto]** Undertow (procs Water Shield off Healing Wave bounces) is only valuable in "spam Healing Wave" phases/fights; in normal Chain-Heal-centric play it's largely dead weight — [[d:shaman#1365147055802290199]] (& Enjoyer, 2025-04-25).
- **[Resto, stat testing]** Haste appears to not reduce Chain Heal's 2s base cast time purely multiplicatively at a specific ~11-14% haste breakpoint — reported as a possible bug/inconsistency, not resolved in-chunk — [[d:shaman#1353536918909878423]] (Stickman, 2025-03-24; unresolved).
- **[Resto]** For gearing, a "max Healpower" set and a haste-leaning set (T3 has some haste baked in) end up roughly equal in HpS at high investment, since haste requires "chugging a lot of mana" to sustain — [[d:shaman#1353777025701122108]] (Stickman, 2025-03-24).
- **[Resto, leveling]** Prio Stam+Int gear and MP5 while leveling as healer for dungeons; not very demanding — [[d:shaman#1323540351109169234]] (Noce, 2024-12-31).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[Black Morass]** In phase 2 of the first big dragon boss, the summoned add ("echo of the lich king") is Hexable — using Hex on it while it's still weak lets it die to the frog-duration alone, removing the mortal-coil/fire-AoE annoyance it causes — [[d:shaman#1318354711631499295]] (Akos, 2024-12-17).
- **[Naxx]** Maexxna: watch for auto-attack Windfury procs while positioning near the boss's large hitbox — one player died when a WF proc landed simultaneously with the tank being parried, exposing them to a hit meant for the tank — [[d:shaman#1356202449425137805]] (Kordinator, 2025-03-30).
- **[General raid]** Enhancement DPS repeatedly reports having to fully hold back (auto-attack only) on several Naxx/AQ40 bosses due to overwhelming threat generation from Windfury procs, even fully consumed/BiS — recurring across many raid nights in this chunk (see Tanking/Threat and Cooldowns sections for the mechanic).
- **[General raid]** For Ele/Enh in a caster-unfriendly (all-melee) raid comp, dropping a Tranquil Air Totem behind the group (out of totem-radius) mimics a partial Salvation for undergeared casters without sacrificing the melee group's Windfury/Str totems — [[d:shaman#1315488366304821248]] (Akil, 2024-12-09), [[d:shaman#1367406017075351603]] (Papairoh, 2025-05-01).
- **[Uldaman]** A slight tank difficulty spike due to hard-hitting Trogg packs; shaman tanks should be ready to kite with Earthbind Totem if overpulling — [[d:shaman#1331803317054996570]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-01-23).
- **[Kara10]** Called a good fit for a shaman off-tank alongside a Paladin/Warrior main tank due to strong AoE-via-Chain-Lightning-pull threat that "cleans up" what the main tank doesn't pick up — [[d:shaman#1311623214434357341]] (Zaaparou, 2024-11-28).

## PvP
- **[Elemental]** Electrify (crit-stack mechanic) initially required 5 stacks (hard to reach in PvP before the target dies/escapes), reduced to 2 stacks in a hotfix (~Dec 2024/Jan 2025), after which Elemental PvP was widely called strong again — see 1.18.1/patch section for citations.
- **[Elemental, burst math]** Stacking PvP nature-damage bonuses that ONLY Earth Shock benefits from simultaneously: Electrify (+10%), a Stormstrike-adjacent +20% nature bonus, T3 8-set (+20% shock damage), Natural Alignment Crystal (+20% spell damage), plus a WSG-specific +30% berserk buff — cited as the core of Ele's PvP burst complaints/nerf requests — [[d:shaman#1338476152506810399]] (51108, 2025-02-10).
- **[Enhancement/Ghost Wolf]** Ghost Wolf form makes the shaman a Beast-type, granting immunity to Sap but exposing it to Beast-targeted CC like Scare Beast/Hibernate from Druids — a double-edged, situational fact — [[d:shaman#1316789024135843871]] (Loki, +6), [[d:shaman#1316789153735905331]] (Loki, +6), [[d:shaman#1316798732892639253]] (Floccumate, confirms surprise, +3).
- **[Enhancement]** Frost Shock used defensively to kite/disengage from melee (Warriors especially, since it resets Diminishing Returns if the fight runs >15s) — [[d:shaman#1316790501432627201]] (Loki, +7).
- **[Enhancement]** Weapon-swap tech from TBC-era wisdom still applies: dual-wield equivalent (2H+WF) to burst squishies, but swap to 1H+shield specifically against Warriors/Rogues (heavy sustained physical damage) — [[d:shaman#1365197906990071882]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-04-25).
- **[Race]** Troll (Hex CC, Berserking) and Tauren (War Stomp AoE stun, +HP) are the top recommended PvP races since shaman otherwise lacks hard CC; Orc suits pure Enhancement DPS slightly better (extra Axe Skill + Blood Fury) — [[d:shaman#1349706039339122728]] (limoseng, 2025-03-13), [[d:shaman#1357475841948520639]] (Yanapaq, 2025-04-04).
- **[Contested]** Whether shaman (esp. Enhance/Ele or Enhance-Resto hybrid tank-heal builds) is currently overtuned in PvP was a live, unresolved dev/community argument through Feb-May 2025, tangled with the T3/Salvation nerf controversies (see patch-change section).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Client mods]** VanillaFixes-dxvk + Nampower (`pepopo978/nampower`) + NampowerSettings addon are the standard combo enabling instant-cast spell queueing, required for one-click multi-totem-drop macros — [[d:shaman#1310640896437391400]] (zbib, +5, 2024-11-25), reposted [[d:shaman#1314072518126206986]] (Dogolate, 2024-12-05).
- **[Macro]** Simple sequential totem-drop macro: `/cast Strength of Earth Totem` / `/cast Mana Spring Totem` / `/cast Sentry Totem` / `/cast Flametongue Totem` (requires Nampower's instant-cast queueing) — same sources as above. A RoidMacros conditional version prevents overwriting an already-active totem buff: `/cast [nobuff:Strength_of_Earth] Strength of Earth Totem` etc.; there's a ~0.5s delay between totem-type casts so the macro needs to be pressed 3+ times to drop all totems — [[d:shaman#1329360988121726977]] (Rangtuk, 2025-01-16).
- **[Addon]** Call of Elements (CoE, `MarcelineVQ/CallOfElements`) gives a dedicated totem-set button plus a contextual cleanse-poison/cleanse-disease button; some users report it needing SuperWoW despite claims otherwise — [[d:shaman#1373907839088857208]] (& Enjoyer, 2025-05-19).
- **[Addon]** ProcDoc (`wsmaxcy/ProcDoc`) — lightweight proc-alert overlay (Clearcasting etc.), auto-detects procs, `/procdoc` to configure — [[d:shaman#1329340656992387155]] (Clempton, 2025-01-16).
- **[Macro]** Shield-swap macro for shock rotations using `/equip <totem/relic>` + `/cast <spell>` pairs to swap relics on cooldown-gated spells; known issue: Lightning Strike's cooldown doesn't display on the action bar when relic-swapped this way (Earth/Flame Shock do) — [[d:shaman#1355387305572958369]] (Gumbo, 2025-03-29).
- **[Macro, buggy]** A shared community macro that checks for the Lightning Shield buff icon and casts it if missing (`/run CastSpellByName...UnitBuff...LightningShield...`) reportedly breaks/doesn't work correctly when adapted for Water Shield — cause not identified in-chunk — [[d:shaman#1358532949695271164]] (Deleted User, 2025-04-07; unresolved), [[d:shaman#1360064319861162065]] (Deleted User, same issue, 2025-04-11; unresolved).
- **[Addon]** mpowa (mouseover power auras) shield-tracking script reads Water Shield/Lightning Shield/Skin of the Earth buff icons via a Lua loop to show one unified icon for all three shields; noted to interact poorly with the (at the time) invisible Stormstrike buff bug — [[d:shaman#1308195057337237566]] (Akil, 2024-11-19).
- **[Addon, general]** Nampower described as required infrastructure for several other addons (Tankplates, AutoMarker, an accurate Luna HealComm, better swing timer, better Decursive) beyond just totem macros — [[d:shaman#1372357081759219825]] (& Enjoyer, 2025-05-15).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Race, tank]** Tauren (+5% HP, Totemic Slam AoE stun/attack-speed debuff, best-known synergy, racial available at level 1 unlike Orc/Troll) is the most recommended tank race; Troll (self-heal racial + Berserking) also strong — [[d:shaman#1349706039339122728]] (limoseng, 2025-03-13), [[d:shaman#1370277629235691600]] (DemonBoyJr, level-1-racial detail, 2025-05-09).
- **[Race, DPS]** Orc: +3 Axe Skill + Bloodlust racial (Blood Fury) modestly improves Enhancement DPS but is minor; Orc's wolf-summon racial (unrelated to shaman spells) was heavily nerfed then partially reverted across this chunk (from ~500 dmg/hit down to negligible, then buffed again) — [[d:shaman#1363065820569272431]] (Rinzu, 2025-04-19), [[d:shaman#1336495446872555571]] (Abuelito, 2025-02-05), [[d:shaman#1357401124986163442]] (Abuelito, 2025-04-03). Orc's Hardiness (stun-resist) reported nerfed/bugged from 25% to 15%, contested and unresolved through the chunk — [[d:shaman#1357472002692222996]] (ganktrain_umberto, 2025-04-04).
- **[Race, caster]** Troll's Berserking racial gives more consistent throughput/Clearcasting-proc value for Elemental than Orc's Command (useless for shaman) — [[d:shaman#1361422773666447370]] (DemonBoyJr, 2025-04-14).
- **[Turtle specific]** BiS weapon for Kara40-era shamans was reported to be an axe, giving Orc an edge via its Axe Skill racial — [[d:shaman#1305856250600357910]] (Kordinator, 2024-11-12).
- **[Professions]** Engineering = BiS DPS profession for Enhancement (repeated multiple times); gathering professions (Mining/Herbalism) recommended for gold; Leatherworking's custom "Grifter's" set useful mid-leveling for Enhancement; Blacksmithing has little unique value beyond the (classic) fear-immunity trinket and raid resistance-gear crafting — [[d:shaman#1363065820569272431]] (Rinzu, 2025-04-19), [[d:shaman#1360456924096892938]] (Mist, 2025-04-11).
- **[Gold]** Weapon-skill training quest/grind costs roughly 500g; consumables for raiding are the main ongoing gold sink — shaman doesn't need much gear investment to farm efficiently (e.g., Lashers) to fund this — [[d:shaman#1374036484163633237]] (& Enjoyer, 2025-05-19).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Windfury]** Proc rate was bugged to ~10-15% instead of the intended 20% (bug #9518), fixed by ~2025-01 (statistically confirmed back to ~20-22%) — [[d:shaman#1305172958049992777]], [[d:shaman#1331592945219928178]].
- **[Windfury]** No ICD ever actually existed on the test server for Windfury; devs mistakenly believed one did and "increased" a nonexistent 3s ICD to 6s — only the (real) Flurry and Flametongue nerfs from that patch cycle actually landed — [[d:shaman#1346243470507114639]] (𝓶𝓮𝓮𝓫𝔂𝓫𝓫, 2025-03-04).
- **[Windfury]** Extra-attack effects (Hand of Justice, wound-proc weapons like Drake Talon Cleaver) do not proc Windfury, confirmed by rigorous player testing — [[d:shaman#1342044965861068860]], [[d:shaman#1331640772021321859]].
- **[Stormstrike]** Bugged to cost 0 mana at one point; fixing this was treated as top priority by devs — [[d:shaman#1308102261494251540]] (Aokana, 2024-11-18). Stormstrike buff was also invisible/undetectable via unit-buff macros for a period (bug #9523) — [[d:shaman#1306358779868352533]] (Stabi, 2024-11-14).
- **[Crackling Thunder Totem]** Lost its proc from Stormstrike and Lightning Strike post-CC2 (bug #8692), a recurring complaint through Nov 2024 — [[d:shaman#1306358779868352533]].
- **[Calming Winds]** Threat-reduction talent reportedly increased threat instead of reducing it (bug #9478) — [[d:shaman#1304797889410895873]] (Maxmojo, +11, 2024-11-09).
- **[Clearcasting]** Consumed both charges on a single Lightning Bolt cast instead of spreading across two casts; fixed at a weekly restart around 2024-12-28 — [[d:shaman#1309958153156431952]], [[d:shaman#1322614328054255696]].
- **[Fire Totems]** Confirmed (bug #12275) to receive 0% hit/crit scaling from the caster's own stats; unaddressed through the end of this chunk (2025-05) — [[d:shaman#1354960635103936564]] (Vigrid, 2025-03-28).
- **[T3 enhance 6/8-set]** 8-set shock-reset had a visual bug not reflecting on the shock's cooldown bar; the 6-set effect's duration only works as intended (6s window) if Reverberation is also talented — [[d:shaman#1355267366870712352]] (AppleK, 2025-03-28). 4-set duration bug (#12413) fixed from 6s to 10s after report — [[d:shaman#1355244796255666309]], [[d:shaman#1355268757593002124]].
- **[Enhancement 2.5]** 3/5 set-bonus fix only applied to the Lightning Shield variant; Earth/Water Shield variants remained bugged (#12852) — [[d:shaman#1362766766421311721]] (Rinzu, 2025-04-18).
- **[Grounding Totem]** Reported non-functional against specific raid-boss spells (AQ40 Twins' Shadow Bolt, ZG Hakkar) — bug #12671 — [[d:shaman#1360807816633384980]] (phyx, 2025-04-13).
- **[Item]** Ghaz'rilla Scale Armor's "+3 Lightning damage" on-equip proc consumes a Stormstrike charge for no net damage gain — [[d:shaman#1351556636598337657]] (Maggat, 2025-03-19; unresolved).
- **[Totemic Alignment]** Reported to grant 0 threat transfer from any totem type at points, despite the talent description — [[d:shaman#1323816738193932421]] (Rejuv, 2025-01-01).
- **[Haste]** Chain Heal's cast-time reduction from Haste appeared inconsistent with pure multiplicative stacking at an ~11-14% breakpoint — flagged, not resolved in-chunk — [[d:shaman#1353536918909878423]] (Stickman, 2025-03-24).
- **[Windfury animation]** Classic "gust of wind" visual reportedly replaced with minimal smoke — unclear if intentional graphics change or a personal client issue, unanswered in-chunk — [[d:shaman#1335193085914910782]] (Yelandreï, 2025-02-01).

## Contested / open questions
- **[Design]** Whether/how to fix Windfury's outsized influence on party-composition/design-space (forces melee-group placement for all 4 shaman specs) without gutting one of vanilla's iconic mechanics — raised repeatedly, no resolution found in-chunk — [[d:shaman#1345521043615383574]] (Akos, 2025-03-02), [[d:shaman#1346211795135627366]] (Akos, 2025-03-04).
- **[Elemental]** Whether Elemental Shaman should get access to Scythe of Elune and/or Atiesh — raised at least 6 separate times across Dec 2024-May 2025 with no dev commitment recorded in-chunk — see Gear section citations.
- **[Tank]** Whether shaman tank should ever be viable as a raid Main Tank vs. strictly OT/5-man — opinions split throughout the chunk between "it's fine as a niche OT/dungeon tank" and "it's a half-finished spec that needed a defensive CD/+Defense talent to be complete" — see Tanking section.
- **[Elemental]** Whether Fire Totems' 0%-hit/crit-scaling bug (and their generally low AoE scaling) will be fixed — reported (#12275) but unaddressed as of chunk end (2025-05-20) — [[d:shaman#1354960635103936564]].
- **[Frostbrand vs. bosses]** Community suggestion to make Frost Shock's debuff visually/mechanically apply (without the slow) to slow-immune raid bosses/trash so Elemental Weapons' guaranteed-crit Frostbrand effect has raid relevance, mirroring how Repentance/rogue poisons were made to "work" on immune targets — proposed twice, no dev response recorded — [[d:shaman#1363659764160270568]] (malfurious, 2025-04-21), [[d:shaman#1365772411731181719]] (malfurious repost, 2025-04-26; question unanswered).
- **[Communication]** A sustained, multi-week community perception (Feb-May 2025) that shaman-specific balance changes (T3 nerf, Salvation nerf) were made without explanation or player consultation, contrasted unfavorably with how the Paladin channel was engaged — relevant context for weighing how final/durable any given CC2-era shaman number is — [[d:shaman#1344242517981270059]] (Rinzu), [[d:shaman#1356186359173480611]] (Kordinator); single-source/perception, not a verifiable game-mechanic fact.
