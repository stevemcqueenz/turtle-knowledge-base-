## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Fire Elemental]** "Rekindled Flame" (Molten Blast refresh-on-Flame-Shock talent) had its fire-damage double-dipping removed (no longer benefits twice from fire-dmg-amp talents) — a ~6-10% overall dps loss, worst case ~30-40% per pessimistic estimates, but Kairion/Tyrchast converge on ~7-9% real loss — [[d:shaman#1463467574439182346]] (Kairion, 2026-01-21, "6-7% overall dmg"), [[d:shaman#1463474539613655060]], [[d:shaman#1463479128845062219]] (Tyrchast: "~10% total dps loss"). Confirmed a bugfix, not an intended nerf, per devs — [[d:shaman#1463604956681474154]].
- **[Fire Elemental]** Separately, Rekindled Flame lost the ability to crit entirely — this was judged the more severe change since crits fed mana sustain (via Omen-style effects); community says this alone can make the Molten Blast build unsustainable in longer fights — [[d:shaman#1463528459295789100]], [[d:shaman#1463532598847340601]], [[d:shaman#1464323364138258473]] (peter_hammer: total loss "about 7-9% of your DPS" once averaged).
- **[Elemental]** peter_hammer's dummy testing (post-fix) found "regular" (non-Molten-Blast-spam) Fire Elemental lost only ~2-3% boss dps; no further Elemental nerfs are planned — [[d:shaman#1463803956772732988]], [[d:shaman#1464234918350422049]]. Ele and Enhancement are stated to be on the same overall tier: Ele better at AoE/trash, Enh better single-target — [[d:shaman#1464234918350422049]].
- **[Elemental]** Fire spec remains favored over Nature spec because Fire benefits from Curse of Elements + Scorch (~25% dmg amp), while Nature spell school has no equivalent amp support on Turtle; long debate on why CoE doesn't include Nature school (unresolved / lore-based justification only) — [[d:shaman#1463817834294677617]], [[d:shaman#1463481634266288138]] (contested, no dev answer).
- **[Tank/Enhancement]** Elemental Weapons' Rockbiter effect reworked: old flat 10% damage reduction (+5% from the now-removed cross-class "Proclaim Champion" buff) replaced with "Earthen Bulwark" — physical damage dealt builds an absorb shield capped at 20% of max HP that then blocks 15% of each incoming hit (i.e. a 15% DR once fully stacked), confirmed via patch note text "The earthen bulwark absorbs 5/10/15% of incoming damage" — [[d:shaman#1464581407077826829]], [[d:shaman#1464582765789057045]]. Dev Haaxor1689: "for actual tanks it shouldn't change much, tiny nerf at most" but "big nerf for spelltanks with novelty, low phys DPS weapons" (spellpower/AoE-tank builds where <40% of damage is physical) — [[d:shaman#1464414658818736291]], [[d:shaman#1464414877098573846]], [[d:shaman#1464422618072617012]] (peter_hammer: <40% of his dmg as spellpower tank is physical).
- **[Tank]** Zeruel's math: physical DPS must exceed ~16.67% of incoming DPS to match the old flat 10% DR, and ~25% of incoming DPS to hit the new 15% cap — [[d:shaman#1464466756444750069]]. Real MC data point (oi-er): needed ~115 physical dps to sustain max shield against a boss doing ~459 incoming dps — [[d:shaman#1464462628561748141]]. Disputed severity: Frost/Travoltino argue the shield is applied after all other mitigation (like an absorb), making it much worse in practice (claims up to "2.67x more damage taken") especially with 0% DR at pull-start and after boss dodge/parry resets — [[d:shaman#1464524894162387085]], [[d:shaman#1464674247950602527]]; Zeruel/Zamka dispute this framing, stating the bulwark applies last (as intended) similarly to old Rockbiter+Proclaim, and can be a net buff for high-avoidance/high-physical-dps tanks — [[d:shaman#1464581494142926994]], [[d:shaman#1464584186672451626]], [[d:shaman#1465013035302260999]] (needs 2 boss crits totaling more than full HP, with zero dps output, to fully deplete a maxed shield).
- **[Tank]** Enhancement Tier 2.5 5-piece set bonus reworked (leaked by dev Haaxor via Zamka): old bonus gave +25% to Rockbiter's effect (scaling both a 35% threat bonus and ~700 AP); new bonus is "Increase the amount of earthen bulwark built up from physical damage for Elemental Weapons' Rockbiter effect by 25% and increases the amount of damage absorbed by the earthen bulwark by an additional 3%" — net loss of ~8.75% threat, ~150 AP, and 5% mitigation, offset only by +3% max DR — [[d:shaman#1464841656544002212]], [[d:shaman#1464841933716197436]], [[d:shaman#1464958672257155125]]. Consensus: shaman tank's signature advantage (best snap AoE threat, e.g. Fire Nova + Stoneclaw combo currently outthreats most paladins) is reduced right as warriors/paladins also get threat buffs, while bears get a comparable threat nerf (reduced Maul/Swipe threat) — [[d:shaman#1465008488899018818]], [[d:shaman#1465317531551141929]], [[d:shaman#1465249265893900288]].
- **[Tank]** Totems, Librams, Totems and Idols can no longer be swapped mid-combat without incurring a GCD (previously free) — kills the haste-relic/AP-relic swap-macro tech used by tank/enhance shamans; dev Haaxor1689 clarifies the goal was not to fully kill swapping — [[d:shaman#1464407673838764104]], [[d:shaman#1464413026479505530]], [[d:shaman#1464396063149395988]] (macro/relic swap tech description).
- **[Enhancement]** Windfury Weapon imbue proc chance increased from 20% to 25% per swing; separately, Stormstrike bug fixed so it can no longer roll and trigger Windfury Weapon's effect twice per activation (previously ~4% double-proc chance on top of ~36% single-proc chance from Stormstrike) — [[d:shaman#1464677997616304293]], [[d:shaman#1464356924102279239]], [[d:shaman#1464397983402561710]].
- **[Enhancement]** Elemental Weapons' "extra attack" (Windfury-proc) effect reworked: "Gaining extra attacks increases your attack speed by 1% for 5 sec, stacks up to 2/4/6 times" — replaces old "extra swing speed on missed extra WF attack" wording; described as a minor QoL buff (+1-6% attack speed) rather than a major change — [[d:shaman#1464433797364977765]], [[d:shaman#1465004130345947268]].
- **[Elemental]** "Eye of the Storm" talent is being reworked next patch into "a real talent" (currently considered bad/not worth taking); recommendation is to park those 2 points in Storm Reach until the rework lands — [[d:shaman#1464756179170496604]], [[d:shaman#1464756796680966164]].
- **[Resto]** Tier 1 8-piece set bonus reworked from (old) "Healing Wave/Lesser Healing Wave jump to 2 additional low-health targets like Chain Heal, synergizing with the 5pc for extra procs" to (new, quoted verbatim) "Your Healing Wave and Lesser Healing Wave have a 25% chance to surround the target with an earth wall, absorbing 250 damage. Lasts 6 sec." — near-universal negative reaction; removes the Healing-Wave-spam build that had been mana-sustain-competitive with Chain Heal spam — [[d:shaman#1464377036415172758]] (verbatim quote), [[d:shaman#1464372895936549048]], [[d:shaman#1464394789003722805]]. Dev-side: peter_hammer reports "Haaxor said resto changes are only pushed back to a later date" (i.e. a fuller resto rework is planned but not in this patch) — [[d:shaman#1464396764609118320]], [[d:shaman#1464636424987873533]].
- **[Resto]** Chain Heal's "smart" targeting (prioritizing lowest-HP nearby targets) is reported broken — heal now jumps to arbitrary targets (even pets/totems) regardless of HP lost; devs have stated in a bug report that this is "intended," triggering major community backlash since Chain Heal is considered core to resto shaman identity — [[d:shaman#1464616611121205390]], [[d:shaman#1464616924683174012]] ("This is intended behaviour"), [[d:shaman#1464367220531658843]], [[d:shaman#1464976318281289779]] (unverified report that CH can target totems).

## Rotation: single target
- **[Fire Elemental]** Rotation is essentially: keep Searing Totem up, maintain Flame Shock, spam Molten Blast to refresh it (triggering Rekindled Flame) — [[d:shaman#1464346129930916065]] (question), consistent with the whole Rekindled Flame discussion above.
- **[Enhancement, 1-button macro]** Verbatim SuperCleveRoidMacros-based macro posted (requires the SuperCleveRoidMacros addon): auto-buffs Water Shield, auto-targets low-HP (<30%) enemies, ranged-pulls with a shock, and uses Stormstrike/Lightning Strike in melee, casting Flame Shock or Earth Shock conditionally on Clearcasting proc:
  `#showtooltip` / `/target [harm,nodead,meleerange,hp:<30]` / `/cast [mybuff:"Water Shield"<#2] Water Shield` / `/cast [distance:>5,hp:<15,behind,inrange:"Earth Shock"] Earth Shock` / `/cast [distance:>15,inrange:"Molten Blast"] Molten Blast` / `/cast [meleerange,mybuff:"Clearcasting"] Stormstrike` / `/cast [meleerange]Lightning Strike` / `/cast [meleerange,mybuff:"Clearcasting",noimmune:fire,nodebuff:"Flame Shock"] Flame Shock; [meleerange,noimmune:nature,mybuff:"Clearcasting"&"Stormstrike"] Earth Shock` — [[d:shaman#1464584369015492701]] (⎛⎝TR0Y⎠⎞).
- **[Lightning Strike mechanics]** Lightning Strike itself does not benefit from increased Nature spell damage; it's a physical attack (benefits from crit chance/hit chance talents, can proc Flurry). However, the Lightning Shield orbs it consumes DO scale with Nature spell damage — [[d:shaman#1463574010972344465]] (Kairion). Detailed breakdown after testing (18 casts): the physical-damage portion of Lightning Strike can crit (physical crit); the Nature-damage portion (non-shield) can crit separately and can be resisted like a spell; the Lightning Shield-orb portion cannot crit at all, whether triggered by being hit or by Lightning Strike. Elemental Fury's crit bonus applies to both the physical and the (non-shield) nature portions of Lightning Strike — [[d:shaman#1468040018328293436]], [[d:shaman#1468041196201840818]], [[d:shaman#1468043074281275638]], [[d:shaman#1468043109723279474]], [[d:shaman#1468043237250830571]], [[d:shaman#1468058427476541522]] (dusty, Cariad, Squid).
- **[Elemental, "regular"/non-MB-spam]** Even after the Rekindled Flame nerf, weaving Flame Shock + Molten Blast to refresh the DoT every ~10-12s remains a dps increase for "regular" Elemental (not full MB-spam); reportedly stops being worth it once you have the T3.5 5-piece set bonus, though that hasn't been mathed out. The full single-target rotation for regular Ele also includes Chain Lightning and even Earthquake, not just Lightning Bolt — [[d:shaman#1468420065203454003]], [[d:shaman#1468442605737218120]], [[d:shaman#1468444316136505479]], [[d:shaman#1468444520097120420]] (gastro, Not Normal, Zhafu).
- **[Enhancement tank]** Fast vs. slow weapon has real tradeoffs even for tanks: a fast weapon gives more Clearcasting/Elemental Devastation procs (mana efficiency, chain-pulling); a slow weapon gives more overall damage/threat per hit, though the difference is muted since Earth Shock is by far the largest threat contributor. Hand of Edward the Odd (see Gear) procs an instant Chain Lightning roughly every 15-20s for ~1500 threat without needing a crit, argued by some to out-threat 3-4 slower strikes, but disputed as unreliable and as "bricking" Stormstrike/auto damage by giving up a proper stat-stick weapon; Stormstrike alone can be up to ~15% of a tank's total damage, so switching to a weak-stats fast weapon can noticeably hurt single-target threat — [[d:shaman#1467598943440474316]], [[d:shaman#1467599912660369675]], [[d:shaman#1467600605613658175]], [[d:shaman#1467600707245969420]], [[d:shaman#1467610007464972361]], [[d:shaman#1467612515247718563]] (Ciftar, poto).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Tank]** Defense: 440 def gives crit-immunity vs bosses (0% remaining crit chance); any other number is "an arbitrary line" only relevant vs low-level trash. Priority debate: several argue armor/health/avoidance matter more than chasing exact def caps since you "can't reasonably get to 0% incoming crits" anyway — [[d:shaman#1463550905663361096]] (Kairion: "440 makes you crit immune... any other number is arbitrary"), [[d:shaman#1463559042944466976]], [[d:shaman#1463617844595134474]] (gastro: armor and stam are the priority, not def).
- **[Tank]** Alternative "unhittable" avoidance-stacking gearing philosophy: >100% avoidance means you only take damage via blocked hits ("technically unhittable"); requires wearing full-avoidance gear in every slot vs the 440-def build which allows mixing in some dps pieces — [[d:shaman#1465166233698697301]], [[d:shaman#1464539537094082785]] (phyx). Unless you can actually reach 440 def, stacking defense just reduces crit frequency, not eliminates it — prioritize stamina/armor first until you have K40 access — [[d:shaman#1465166233698697301]] (Cariad).
- **[Enhancement, weapon]** Windfury Weapon proc chance: 25% per swing after the patch (up from 20%); each proc also grants +1% attack speed for 5s, stacking up to 2/4/6 times depending on talent rank — [[d:shaman#1464677997616304293]], [[d:shaman#1465004130345947268]].
- **[Enhancement]** Badge of the Swarmguard-style trinket proc rate formula: weapon speed × 10 / 60 = % proc chance per swing (e.g. 3.8 speed → 63%, 3.6 speed → 60%) — [[d:shaman#1463999408235479142]], [[d:shaman#1463999553790410815]] (user "30").
- **[Resto]** GCD haste breakpoints: Chain Heal needs ~25% haste to hit the 1.0s GCD cap; Healing Wave rank 4+ needs ~34% haste. With T2.5+ gear + general raid buffs (excluding Atiesh) resto shamans can reach ~16% haste — a hypothetical 5% haste from talents was requested to make CH gearing easier — [[d:shaman#1464922728115998815]], [[d:shaman#1464928439990489256]] (Mithreon).
- **[Tank]** Avoidance/defense attack-table mechanics, clarified after debate: Avoidance (dodge/parry/miss) pushes off attack-table entries in order — normal hits first, then crushing blows, then critical hits last; Defense specifically converts crits into normal hits (not into avoidance), and shields/block converts whichever is the "least threatening" remaining attack type (usually a normal hit) into a (cheaper) blocked hit — block does not directly reduce crit/crush chance except indirectly once you're already avoidance-capped enough that crushes are being pushed off. Reaching 100% total avoidance leaves ~2.4% residual crit chance; full avoidance-cap requires 102.4% total avoidance to push off everything (hit, crush, and crit) — [[d:shaman#1466318340279763096]], [[d:shaman#1466318542394622095]], [[d:shaman#1466319139403464757]], [[d:shaman#1466319368626638908]], [[d:shaman#1467935502719123609]], [[d:shaman#1467936200827474156]], [[d:shaman#1467936701191290891]] (Zeruel, poto, phyx).
- **[Tank]** Defense/avoidance anti-synergy: stacking Defense (which converts crit into a normal hit) actually makes reaching a target avoidance cap *harder*, because those converted hits still have to be pushed off by avoidance before crushes/crits are — i.e. every % of crit converted to a hit via Defense requires that much *more* avoidance to still reach the same cap goal. Once avoidance is capped at 102.4%, Defense's anti-crit function becomes entirely wasted since crit is already fully prevented — [[d:shaman#1467934908495171624]], [[d:shaman#1467935286272196801]], [[d:shaman#1467935660307644576]], [[d:shaman#1467935787063443516]], [[d:shaman#1467961246681796831]] (Zeruel). In practice: reaching full 102.4% avoidance is judged extremely hard/impractical (needs nearly every best avoidance item in the game enchanted for avoidance in every slot), and one detailed manual calculation only reached ~95.12% avoidance even with an aggressive avoidance-only loadout — [[d:shaman#1467934241575797021]], [[d:shaman#1467957727262146906]], [[d:shaman#1467957960050479338]] (Zeruel, Cariad). Defense-cap (440) is itself only barely reachable for shaman tank and requires using the AQ40 shield over Naxx/K40 shields plus other sacrifices — [[d:shaman#1467942903199305877]] (Cariad).
- **[Tank]** Defense point value: 1 point of Defense gives 0.04% each to dodge, parry, miss, block chance, AND crit reduction simultaneously (i.e. 5 defense ≈ 0.2% to each) — making 5 Defense roughly comparable in total value to a standalone 1% Dodge enchant, though Dodge is arguably slightly better (pure avoidance, vs. Defense's block-% component which still takes some damage) or arguably worse (Defense's 0.2% anti-crit could outweigh its 0.2% block) — judged "a hard call, kinda splitting hairs" — [[d:shaman#1468007395530772745]], [[d:shaman#1468012468902301758]], [[d:shaman#1468012539538444465]], [[d:shaman#1468013038400704596]], [[d:shaman#1468013299277893765]], [[d:shaman#1468013317229379791]] (Squid, Zappinu, Zhafu, Zeruel).
- **[Enhancement, weapon speed]** For a given weapon dps, a slower weapon is *always* strictly better than a faster one for standard Enhancement — there is no specific "sweet spot" speed range — [[d:shaman#1469420065966264392]], [[d:shaman#1469420176142241915]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enhancement, physhance]** General crit-to-AP conversion rate: roughly 1% crit ≈ 20-25 AP in value for a typical physical-Enhancement gear setup; exact weights shift with gear, so close item comparisons (e.g. a crit ring vs. an AP+stam ring) require simming to resolve precisely — [[d:shaman#1469706680890822737]], [[d:shaman#1469707008872550644]], [[d:shaman#1469707378399117366]] (Not Normal).
- **[Enhancement, hit/weapon skill]** Hit cap for Enh is 8%; 3% comes from talents, 1% from the "+5 weapon skill book"; an Orc using an axe plus a +7-weapon-skill-to-2h-axes item would only need 2% hit from gear; each point of weapon skill is worth 0.2% reduced hit-chance-needed — [[d:shaman#1467795868198375495]] (Greybull-Ambershire).
- **[Elemental]** ~15 spell penetration is treated as a priority stat for Elemental shaman (rationale assumed known/established, not detailed in-channel); no confirmed spell-pen enchant exists — the only known in-chunk source is a Kara10 quest turn-in ("overcharged ley energy") that grants a 25 spell-pen enchant — [[d:shaman#1467704806201364676]], [[d:shaman#1467715232007852266]], [[d:shaman#1467718352767160513]], [[d:shaman#1467718516680425685]] (gastro, tomo).
- **[General]** Armor Penetration (ArP) valued at roughly 2:1 to AP when the raid has Improved Expose Armor / Curse of Recklessness / Faerie Fire all active (i.e. 50 ArP ≈ 25 AP); value scales up the more armor is already stripped (up to 0), and drops to worthless once a boss is at 0 armor — [[d:shaman#1466993984940540099]], [[d:shaman#1466994143900471368]], [[d:shaman#1466994206890393681]], [[d:shaman#1466994318391906471]], [[d:shaman#1466994320610558025]], [[d:shaman#1466994428261568572]] (Squid).
- **[Resto]** Healing-power scaling depends on a spell rank's cast time, not the level it's learned at: Healing Wave ranks that take 3s to cast get full/max healing-power scaling, while ranks that cast in 2.5s or less take large scaling reductions — correcting the common assumption that scaling only "unlocks" at Rank 5/level 20 — [[d:shaman#1467961839215181854]], [[d:shaman#1467962596962340987]] (Sheyba, Cariad).
- **[Resto]** Enchant tradeoffs at moderate gear: 1% haste vs ~30 healing power on gloves; 1 haste vs ~13 heal+15 int on pants/helm; 2% haste vs ~33 healing power on shoulders (or a Naxx-level enchant, only marginally better than the ZG one). Rule of thumb: compare added HPS from a haste enchant vs a healing-power enchant for your primary spell; if under-geared/chain-heal-spamming, healing power wins because base Chain Heal value is low but its scaling is good — [[d:shaman#1464139707112751117]], [[d:shaman#1464139963405701151]], [[d:shaman#1464141254219534425]] (Diving Instructor Knife).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Tank]** Shaman tank threat in dungeons is described as extremely strong — "you have to actively try to lower your threat to lose aggro"; players downrank Earth Shock to save mana/speed up runs while staying comfortably ahead on threat — [[d:shaman#1465319530887643259]], [[d:shaman#1465319654850560064]] (Holidae).
- **[Tank]** Current raid threat comparison (pre-patch numbers): shaman tank threat is very strong right now — arguably ahead of paladins/warriors on snap/early threat, though bears currently sustain better long-fight threat and paladins may lead on AoE; all of this is expected to shift once warrior/paladin threat buffs and bear/shaman threat nerfs land together in 1.18.1 — [[d:shaman#1465249265893900288]], [[d:shaman#1465250053022023772]] (Cariad, Nandieb).
- **[Racials]** Orc racials (Blood Fury dps CD — reduces healing received while active, so avoid using while tanking; and an Axe weapon-skill racial) are considered better for shaman than Troll (whose racials "got nerfed pretty hard") — [[d:shaman#1463535652950900787]], [[d:shaman#1463537440416338097]] (Ryuuhei).
- **[Racials]** Race-specific shaman abilities: Troll = Hex ("kinda crap"); Orc = Ghost/Spirit Wolves (~40 dmg/hit, situational — can add cast time to certain casts and soak specific raid mechanics, works on most targets but not bosses or those immune to attack-speed effects; Ghost Wolves are Orc-only, "not terribly good outside of causing pushback in PvP"); Tauren = Totemic Slam (5% base mana, 5yd range, instant, 90s CD, deals 10% of AP as damage and reduces target's attack/cast speed 25% for 8s — judged "insanely shit," not worth an action-bar slot since it prevents only about half an auto-attack's worth of damage per 90s) — [[d:shaman#1463536026273447936]], [[d:shaman#1463536890362855597]] (Ryuuhei, Kairion), [[d:shaman#1468375480175693947]] (Cariad, Ghost Wolves Orc-only).
- **[Race tier list]** peter_hammer's summary: Orc racial outperforms Troll in all caster specs; for shaman specifically — Orc is best DPS, Tauren is best mitigation (and PvP), Troll has a slight healing edge (matters mainly when about to wipe) and ramps threat faster as tank; differences are "not big enough to make a decision based on them alone." Weapon skill racials (e.g. Orc's +axe skill) are much less impactful than in vanilla because weapon skill's formula was normalized (reaching 305 used to only give ~70% of the value of 315 due to a penalty vs. targets 3+ levels higher; that penalty is gone) — [[d:shaman#1467151197633904732]], [[d:shaman#1467152418734211093]], [[d:shaman#1467152609403211903]], [[d:shaman#1465411662181040422]] (peter_hammer, Kairion).
- **[PvP race pick]** For PvP: Orc recommended overall for Enhancement (with Tauren second); Troll favored for Elemental, then Orc — differences are real but not decisive; a full race-choosing guide was posted separately (not in this chunk) — [[d:shaman#1467128728185475261]], [[d:shaman#1467134234056724669]], [[d:shaman#1467151871180538011]] (A N T I, Snook, peter_hammer).
- **[Tauren]** Plainsrunning: instant-cast (no cast time), ramps up in 3 speed phases (final phase = your riding-skill speed, e.g. 60% or 100%); taking any damage removes one of the 3 stacks, slowing you down; stacks rebuild fast so a single hit isn't costly; works with water walking and is unaffected by mount-speed-increasing items; costs a short questline at level 40, speed scales with purchased riding skill; does not work in same-faction BGs but does work in cross-faction BGs; useful for short hops, chasing/closing gaps before a target can cast/mount, and staying in non-riding gear via itemrack — [[d:shaman#1467172641147453564]], [[d:shaman#1467174179705978933]], [[d:shaman#1467174630090477719]], [[d:shaman#1467181106754224239]], [[d:shaman#1467182330694209668]], [[d:shaman#1467192098263666709]], [[d:shaman#1467226371838447656]] (Wawds, Zaaparou, Squid, Xaln, peter_hammer, SteveNash). Bonus interaction: water walking currently stacks with Spirit Wolf (+40% speed) and Plainsrunning (+60%), making Tauren shaman the fastest race on water — flagged by a player as a likely-unintended but liked interaction — [[d:shaman#1467658015782994101]] (Pluffyhoof).
- **[Totem mechanic]** Totem of Tides (a Stormwrought item) only procs when a Water Shield charge is consumed by taking damage — if the hit is dodged, no charge is consumed and it can't proc; it's considered "game changing" for leveling/leech survivability and AoE tanking, and combos well with Sigil of the Ancient Accord — [[d:shaman#1466477100428034048]], [[d:shaman#1466478275953033226]], [[d:shaman#1466479162502942941]], [[d:shaman#1466482615274962985]], [[d:shaman#1466482767310360647]] (Frost, poqoqu0741, Kairion). It also counts as "casting/landing a spell" for the purposes of Sigil of Ancient Accord-style spell-triggered procs — [[d:shaman#1467505141715308699]] (Zamka).
- **[Vampirism/leech]** The shaman toolkit itself isn't well-suited to leech/vampirism farming builds; making it work requires specific enabler pieces (Totem of Tides, Band of Eternal Divinity, Force Reactive Disk, Shawl/Force of Will); Totem of Tides plus sufficient leech alone is enough to solo the Lashers in Maraudon — [[d:shaman#1466474724073935044]], [[d:shaman#1466476775243780320]], [[d:shaman#1466477100428034048]], [[d:shaman#1466477209626869881]], [[d:shaman#1466477646891188306]] (Frost).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Design philosophy]** peter_hammer's widely-referenced summary of tank shaman's design intent (quoted in full to a new player's question): tank shaman is competitive with the other traditional tanks for dungeon tanking, is a versatile off-tank at endgame with potential to main-tank, and offers AoE tanking when talented — but that AoE threat relies on fire totems/talents rather than the unresistable threat sources other tanks use, so it starts to show gaps at endgame if not investing in spell-power (similar to a paladin). Tank shaman lacks a dedicated defensive ability, but compensates with a low-variance damage-intake profile from high armor plus Rockbiter's innate DR, achieved *without* sacrificing damage/threat the way Bear Form, Defensive Stance, or Righteous Fury cost other tanks. Two distinct playstyles exist: a 2h build stacking agility (dodge) and crit (threat), or a sword-and-board build focusing on parry/block/strength. High talent overlap with Enhancement DPS allows a quick respec/gear swap between roles — [[d:shaman#1465767302099370066]] (peter_hammer).
- **[1.18.1 tank rework — mechanic]** Elemental Weapons' Rockbiter effect replaces the old flat 10% DR (+5% from the now-removed "Proclaim Champion") with "Earthen Bulwark": physical damage dealt (60% of Stormstrike, autoattack, and a small portion of Lightning Strike with a shield equipped) builds an 8-second-duration absorb shield capped at 20% max HP; the shield then blocks 15% of each incoming hit (a 15% DR once fully built). This is a genuine DR (confirmed by the patch-note wording "absorbs 5/10/15% of incoming damage"), not "15% of the shield consumed per hit" as some players initially misread it — [[d:shaman#1467188756648824974]], [[d:shaman#1464581407077826829]], [[d:shaman#1464582765789057045]], [[d:shaman#1464581494142926994]] (Therz, Zeruel).
- **[1.18.1 tank rework — why]** Multiple community members and a dev (Kokuyo, Therz) state the Rockbiter DR change's real motivation was to stop Resto/PvP shamans from abusing the Elemental Weapons flat-DR talent in PvP, not primarily a PvE tank-balance change — seen as collateral damage to the tank spec ("totally kills spellpower shaman tanks from getting to use rockbiter shield properly") — [[d:shaman#1467188910021935339]], [[d:shaman#1467207383460614340]], [[d:shaman#1467207641456574484]]. Dev Haaxor1689: "for actual [physical] tanks it shouldn't change much, tiny nerf at most" but "big nerf for spelltanks with novelty, low phys DPS weapons" — [[d:shaman#1464414658818736291]], [[d:shaman#1464414877098573846]].
- **[1.18.1 tank rework — no defensive CD]** Shaman tank is confirmed NOT getting a defensive cooldown in 1.18.1; a defensive CD exists in the game's database but was never implemented because devs reportedly "didn't know how to balance it with PvP" — [[d:shaman#1467187717174722612]], [[d:shaman#1467188910021935339]], [[d:shaman#1467189035863904339]], [[d:shaman#1467193501027532872]] (Wawds, Kokuyo, Therz).
- **[1.18.1 T2.5 rework]** Enhancement Tier 2.5 5-piece leaked (via dev Haaxor): old bonus gave the Rockbiter/Elemental Weapons effect +25% (scaling a 35% threat bonus and ~700 AP); new bonus is "Increase the amount of earthen bulwark built up from physical damage... by 25% and increase the amount of damage absorbed... by an additional 3%" — netting roughly -8.75% threat, -150 AP, -5% old-style mitigation, offset only by +3% max DR (15%→18% cap) — [[d:shaman#1464841656544002212]], [[d:shaman#1464841933716197436]], [[d:shaman#1464958672257155125]], [[d:shaman#1466724417282379806]], [[d:shaman#1466724720161329214]]. Comparative stat note: the T2.5 breastplate has 5 less stamina than T3, and 7 less than T3.5 — [[d:shaman#1466673117295415400]] (Squid).
- **[Tank experience — reassurance]** Practicing tank shamans push back on doom narratives with concrete play data: one MT/OT'd Ambershire MC for ~2 months with only decent pre-BiS gear and "never had any memory of total struggle on any boss," even Garr pulls, once geared with a ZG trinket + Stormstrike — [[d:shaman#1465442844914225437]] (Boorenka). Another runs 40% dodge and 9000 armor, "barely even taking hits above 25% of my health already," describing shaman tank as "a chaotic threat tank relying on RNG," not meant to be "a walking fortress" — [[d:shaman#1465006179963895852]] (Catallergies). A third notes spell crits now proccing Flurry (1.18.1 change) is a genuine uptime improvement that may help offset the Rockbiter nerf, and that Fire Nova + Stoneclaw Totem currently out-threats most paladins on the pull — [[d:shaman#1465008488899018818]], [[d:shaman#1465008955767128135]] (Catallergies).
- **[Tank experience — pessimistic]** Other tank mains (Zamka, Frost, Travoltino) argue the cumulative changes (Rockbiter rework + T2.5 rework + relic/totem-swap GCD) leave shaman tank with "mediocre threat and no defensive CDs," no longer warranting a raid slot over other tanks once warrior/paladin threat buffs land — [[d:shaman#1464832484238688379]], [[d:shaman#1464843561907261624]], [[d:shaman#1465006179963895852]] (disputed).
- **[Threat kit]** AoE threat tools: Stoneclaw Totem (burst AoE threat), Fire Nova Totem, Magma Totem, Totemic Alignment (routes 90% of totem threat to the shaman), and Totem of Tides (pulses frost AoE damage off Water Shield procs) — [[d:shaman#1465318727527698504]], [[d:shaman#1465319125374206178]] (Holidae). Shaman dungeon threat is described as extremely strong — "you have to actively try to lower your threat to lose aggro" — with players downranking Earth Shock purely to save mana/speed runs while staying comfortably ahead — [[d:shaman#1465319530887643259]], [[d:shaman#1465319654850560064]].
- **[Weapon choice]** Slow weapons generally favored for tanking after early levels — "we don't use daggers anymore, that's old-ass meta from when we were barely tanks"; a cheap, accessible pre-raid weapon option is Hand of Edward the Odd (Caverns of Time rep, ~200-300g/few hours' farm on Ambershire), though its instant-Chain-Lightning proc (~1500 threat every 15-20s) is disputed as reliable AoE threat vs. simply bricking Stormstrike/auto damage from a weak stat-stick — [[d:shaman#1467596213296566403]], [[d:shaman#1467598441264845115]], [[d:shaman#1467598647158902935]], [[d:shaman#1467599912660369675]], [[d:shaman#1467610007464972361]].
- **[Defense/avoidance strategy]** Kairion's rule of thumb: "either don't focus too much on defense and stack armor/health like a maniac, or go for the full 440 defense cap — no half measures." Rugged armor kits (survive a rare crit better) are preferred over Defense-oriented armor kits unless you can actually reach 440 def while using them, since unpredictable big crits are what actually kill tanks, not steady expected damage — [[d:shaman#1466102519502602346]], [[d:shaman#1466104112411316264]], [[d:shaman#1466115983541538979]] (Kairion). See also the avoidance-cap math under "Stats, caps, weights."
- **[Block]** Block is broadly considered a poor stat for shaman tank absent a dedicated avoidance-capping strategy — "you don't get any benefit from it" beyond reducing the damage of the single least-threatening hit type, unlike warriors who get counterattacks/extra abilities on block; stat points are better spent on stamina or dodge/parry — [[d:shaman#1466299596568399932]], [[d:shaman#1466299758191575234]], [[d:shaman#1466299847098241177]], [[d:shaman#1466299989872607363]]. Shamans have no itemized block-oriented tank gear/tier set support, unlike other block-capable tanks — [[d:shaman#1464446689736917075]] (Hopestotem).
- **[Leveling AoE]** Struggling to hold AoE threat while leveling (reported around level 42) was resolved by recommending Totem of Tides (farm Stormwrought until it drops) — [[d:shaman#1467922217235910961]], [[d:shaman#1467922267903365211]], [[d:shaman#1467922473700823273]] (Rattlesnake, Nyamuu).

## Healing specifics
- **[1.18.1 changes]** See "1.18.1 changes & their effect" above for the Tier 1 8-piece rework and the Chain Heal "smart targeting" controversy — both are core to how Resto plays this patch.
- **[Reality check]** Post-patch, Resto is expected to return to being almost entirely Chain Heal spam once the T1 Healing-Wave build is removed; a typical non-T1 K40 resto rotation is "give or take 1 or 2 abilities" beyond Chain Heal, plus totems, Purge, and the ranged interrupt — [[d:shaman#1470172792484921488]], [[d:shaman#1470179645444854035]], [[d:shaman#1470180033304854663]], [[d:shaman#1470180395835326555]] (Axem, Mithreon).
- **[Rotation guidance]** Practical advice for raid healing: Rank 1 Chain Heal is the main raid heal; use higher-rank Healing Wave (e.g. Rank 6+) as a secondary single-target tool if healing power is good; only use higher ranks of either when raid damage is high enough to justify the mana cost; bind heals to click-cast via a raid-frame addon (e.g. Pfui) for speed. As gear improves, rely more on higher-rank Chain Heal and use Rank 1 mostly for topping off; fish for the "Ancestral Healing" proc with Rank 1 Healing Wave while the tank is running in, for aggressive single-target healing — [[d:shaman#1464784154250969201]], [[d:shaman#1464792806097682655]] (Kroeskop, Bubkez).
- **[Spell-rank usage examples]** Community-reported keybind setups: Healing Wave 1/4/7/10, Lesser Healing Wave 6, Chain Heal 1/3 (Cariad) — [[d:shaman#1467960699597619263]]. Another simpler take for leveling/dungeon healing at 60: just keep max-rank Lesser Healing Wave, max-rank Healing Wave, and max-rank Chain Heal on the bars — "never struggled," even the worst case (a 4-elemental pull in BM) was manageable — [[d:shaman#1470096881924313335]], [[d:shaman#1470097052947185696]] (Holidae). For 5-mans specifically: use Healing Wave far more than Chain Heal; only spam Chain Heal in a dungeon if the whole group is stacked/melee, since spamming it otherwise burns mana for no benefit — [[d:shaman#1470095010350497975]], [[d:shaman#1470095666398363749]], [[d:shaman#1470095703727411342]], [[d:shaman#1470095854676344926]] (Bezawit, Holidae).
- **[Undertow/mana management]** For early-level healing (~level 29), "Undertow + Tidal Surge + Healing Wave on the tank" is usually sufficient; a bad group taking avoidable damage will still burn a lot of your mana regardless of build — [[d:shaman#1466917683474993214]], [[d:shaman#1466917853986029650]] (Mithreon). At endgame, detailed math: Undertow (triggered by Lesser Healing Wave) costs 361 mana per 15s to keep Healing Way active, which needs Healing Wave to be at least Rank 5 to be "net positive" mana-wise — [[d:shaman#1464966827112399102]] (Mithreon).
- **[Haste breakpoints]** Chain Heal needs ~25% haste to hit the 1.0s GCD; Healing Wave Rank 4+ needs ~34% haste; achievable raid haste from T2.5+ gear plus general buffs (excluding Atiesh) is roughly 16% — [[d:shaman#1464922728115998815]], [[d:shaman#1464928439990489256]] (Mithreon). (See also "Stats, caps, weights" for the healing-power-by-cast-time scaling rule.)
- **[Ancestral Healing / Earth Shield]** Earth Shield's heal effect can crit, and a crit from your *own* Earth Shield (proc'd when you take damage, not from Lightning Strike consuming a charge) triggers the Restoration passive "Ancestral Healing," an armor/mitigation self-buff — but the effect only triggers off your own heal, so being healed/shielded by another shaman or a priest doesn't grant it to you in a raid setting; largely irrelevant for group content, but can matter for solo mitigation. A similar Paladin ability/buff is confirmed to stack with it — [[d:shaman#1470025846390788186]], [[d:shaman#1470026459933573245]], [[d:shaman#1470030344253341768]], [[d:shaman#1470030557001027715]], [[d:shaman#1470030724743696567]], [[d:shaman#1470031227913240782]], [[d:shaman#1470032120754737152]] (𝓐𝓼𝓱𝓮𝓻𝓪, Diving Instructor Knife).
- **[Leveling]** You don't need Resto talents to heal 5-man dungeons while leveling — Intellect gear alone is sufficient; going full Resto brings meaningful mana-efficiency benefits (see "Leveling" section above) but "it is very hard to quest as" pure Resto — [[d:shaman#1467542278141640881]], [[d:shaman#1469195633037541417]] (Ciftar, Marnibanks). One player bought the cash-shop "Goblin Brainwashing Device" specifically to make dungeon healing easier while leveling in Enh spec/int gear, though full Resto is still described as much easier for that purpose — [[d:shaman#1467556333963444257]] (SteveNash).
- **[Pre-raid BiS]** See "Gear, BiS, pre-raid, set bonuses" above for the Red Dragonscale set, trinket, and totem recommendations.

- **[Resto PvP]** The Elemental Weapons/Rockbiter flat-10%-DR removal is widely believed to have been done specifically to stop Resto shamans from abusing that talent for extra PvP survivability ("resto shaman having 10% flat mit in a field of dissolvent-poison rogues and paladins is just too good"), at the cost of gutting the PvE spellpower-tank build that relied on the same talent — [[d:shaman#1467242141163061373]], [[d:shaman#1467243765998686318]], [[d:shaman#1467207383460614340]], [[d:shaman#1467207641456574484]] (Spicyahi, Tel'abim; Diving Instructor Knife; Therz). - **[Enhancement PvP]** vs. Affliction Warlock: Curse of Exhaustion out-slows Frost Shock; recommended counterplay is abusing line-of-sight, and — if you have PvP boots — using Ghost Wolf's speed bonus to close the gap under Curse of Elements after landing a Flame Shock first; getting caught in the open by a warlock or mage without an answer is usually a loss — [[d:shaman#1469486519168532512]], [[d:shaman#1469487274608689375]], [[d:shaman#1469487348164202607]], [[d:shaman#1469491569483579427]], [[d:shaman#1469491729123246120]] (Afraido, Zhafu, Zeruel). - **[Enhancement PvP]** Weapon proc comparison: Fist of the Forgotten Order reported as a decent upgrade over The Unstoppable Force in practice, despite lower-than-expected proc rate; The Unstoppable Force's stun proc + 2% crit may still be worth keeping if unbuffed crit is otherwise low (example: 25% unbuffed) — [[d:shaman#1468558608542466120]] (Bogorl; single-source, anecdotal).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Totems]** Dropping all 4 totems with one keybind requires Nampower (available via the Turtle launcher); with Nampower, a simple macro of one `/cast <Totem Name>` line per totem places all 4 — no special addon syntax needed. Without Nampower, only one totem can be placed per click — [[d:shaman#1466411861506265119]], [[d:shaman#1466421661237510279]], [[d:shaman#1468380321287508081]], [[d:shaman#1469082258240700641]] (Holidae, Squid). An alternative Call of Elements (CoE) addon-based one-liner (sourced from a YouTube comment, unofficial): `/run for _,b in pairs({COETotemFire1,COETotemEarth1,COETotemWater1,COETotemAir1})do if b then b:Click() end end` — [[d:shaman#1466481213232386183]] (Somebody).
- **[1.18.1 change]** After the patch, swapping totems/relics/librams/idols mid-combat will incur a GCD (previously free), which will noticeably slow down (but not fully prevent) rapid totem-swap macros — [[d:shaman#1464407673838764104]].
- **[Addon]** Call of Elements (CoE) is the standard totem-management addon; version-compatibility errors with Nampower are common — using the "namreeb" Nampower build together with certain CoE versions causes persistent errors; the fix is to use the MarcelineVQ fork of CoE (https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/CallOfElements) together with a compatible (non-namreeb) Nampower release — [[d:shaman#1465969484660740180]], [[d:shaman#1465973459069964439]], [[d:shaman#1465982641663770655]] (phyx, Paliote). CoE also has a built-in Windfury-weapon-imbue check-and-cast function, avoiding the need for a separate macro — [[d:shaman#1467645485136216097]] (Ciftar).
- **[Addon]** DoiteAuras is used to track Water Shield/Lightning Shield "charges" (not "stacks" — a terminology distinction that initially caused false bug reports to the addon author) for reminders to refresh before running out — [[d:shaman#1469288332147298398]], [[d:shaman#1469375113789968577]], [[d:shaman#1469393120977223914]], [[d:shaman#1470081762355974176]], [[d:shaman#1470082730510389361]] (Doite, Phatwater[NA], Not Normal).
- **[Addon]** Alternatives for shield/proc reminder tracking: MPowa (reported buggy/hard to configure by some), Power Auras (recommended as "clean and easy"), Simple Auras — all generally need SuperWoW; ProcDoc conflicts with Power Auras (throws errors) — [[d:shaman#1468981983219810539]], [[d:shaman#1468988013257293890]], [[d:shaman#1469005974571581575]], [[d:shaman#1469008328125579469]], [[d:shaman#1469021201425891526]] (Sniijz, Hiryu, verd).
- **[DPS meters]** ShaguDPS is common but "oversimplified"; DpsMate is considered "the best" (graphs + proc tracking, more data than Shagus) though no longer supported and somewhat glitchy; combat logs are the closest thing to reliable data but are "kinda scuffed too" on Turtle — [[d:shaman#1466474724073935044]], [[d:shaman#1466475090035347758]], [[d:shaman#1466475329504805028]], [[d:shaman#1466506678097674341]], [[d:shaman#1466506896587624468]] (Frost, Rahnd2137, gastro).
- **[Nameplates]** Nameplate addons like Cursive or Shaguplates poorly estimate DoT/debuff durations and don't account for a debuff being refreshed by a "custom" spell (e.g. Flame Shock refreshed by Molten Blast); this caused a false report that Molten Blast's Flame Shock refresh was broken when it was actually just an addon display issue — [[d:shaman#1468333449109573704]], [[d:shaman#1468339089261592658]], [[d:shaman#1468339332086632599]], [[d:shaman#1468339461120332003]], [[d:shaman#1468339551750717684]] (Zeruel, Timwab, poto).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Fixed]** Molten Blast's damage double-dipping into fire-damage-amplification talents via Rekindled Flame was identified and fixed as a bugfix (not framed as an intentional nerf by devs) — see "1.18.1 changes" above.
- **[Fixed]** Frostbrand imbue previously stacked with Windfury Totem for solo-farm purposes; this interaction has since been fixed and no longer stacks — [[d:shaman#1465587636247855125]] (clesk).
- **[Fixed]** Stormstrike could previously roll and trigger Windfury Weapon's proc twice per activation (~4% double-proc on top of ~36% single-proc); fixed in 1.18.1 to roll only once — see "1.18.1 changes" above.
- **[Historical, fixed]** Shield Specialization (tank talent) was once bugged to grant a flat +10% to all stats instead of the intended "+10% + stats" scaling — since corrected (now roughly 0.5% dodge per point, ~2.5% at 5/5) — [[d:shaman#1467946249075425280]], [[d:shaman#1467946659098132633]], [[d:shaman#1467947176515862551]] (Eluzia, Zeruel).
- **[Unresolved/"intended"]** Flametongue weapon imbue's damage scales with the Elemental Fury talent but not the (identically-worded) Concussion talent — devs say this is not clearly a bug, they're simply undecided on which is intended — [[d:shaman#1467573176233103622]], [[d:shaman#1467586509967462490]], [[d:shaman#1467586635662229576]] (Lokimanor, Not Normal).
- **[Non-bug, misdiagnosed]** Personal Flametongue imbue appearing to "not always proc" was traced to unlogged spell resists (the game doesn't clearly log a resisted Flametongue tick unless specific combat-log display settings are enabled) rather than an actual proc-rate bug — [[d:shaman#1467080703920439353]], [[d:shaman#1467381607114735638]], [[d:shaman#1467407770998542534]] (Lokimanor, Not Normal, Diving Instructor Knife).
- **[Bugged, in-progress]** Calming Winds is currently bugged and does not reduce the mana/threat cost of Flametongue as intended — [[d:shaman#1469031612858499174]] (Not Normal).
- **[Addon quirk, resolved]** A totem-drop macro that worked fine while leveling suddenly only placed 1 of 4 totems for one player after switching RoidMacros-addon versions; traced to a stale cached macro file conflict and resolved by clearing it — [[d:shaman#1465249020204155169]], [[d:shaman#1465259946529652886]] (Heathen). A separate, apparently unresolved report of the same symptom (macro placing only 1 totem at level 60 despite working fine while leveling) appears later in the chunk with no follow-up — [[d:shaman#1466635861176094802]] (Zllig).
- **[Historical trivia]** In old (pre-~1.10) vanilla WoW, Sentry Totem had a bug that froze all of the caster's movement (including fall damage) while active, letting shamans "safe-land" off big jumps; not confirmed whether this applies on Turtle — [[d:shaman#1468640191672750151]] (Skylead; historical/vanilla trivia, not confirmed current).
