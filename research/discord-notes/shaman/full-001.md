## 1.18.1 changes & their effect
- **[Ele/general]** Clearcasting used to only affect Shocks and normal elemental spells; it was changed so Lightning Strike and Stormstrike are also reduced/consume clearcasting charges — considered a "recent" CC change; some feel mana economy was better before the change for less-geared players, but it's likely better for well-geared raiders — [[d:shaman#1427436887991255262]], [[d:shaman#1427437157798252544]], [[d:shaman#1427437737702723705]] (Zykes/Not Normal/Squid, 2025-10-13; disputed impact by gear level).
- **[Enh/Spellhance]** Shocks (earth/frost/flame) gained AP-scaling coefficients "added in 1.17" (Earth/Frost Shock 10% of AP, Flame Shock 6% initial +1.5%/tick), meant to give enh players scaling off any of their normal stats since shocks are mostly an enh thing — [[d:shaman#1427351108413685943]], [[d:shaman#1427351153384886482]], [[d:shaman#1427351194053120162]], [[d:shaman#1427351454166945922]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-13).
- **[Enh]** Empowered Lightning Shield (from Lightning Strike proc) scales AP at 25% vs its own 26.8% SP coefficient — "basically equal" scaling per point, but AP is far easier to stack, so it favors physhance over spellhance; called an unintended/annoying design outcome — [[d:shaman#1427352928355881021]]–[[d:shaman#1427353149500428429]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-13).
- **[Fire/Consumable]** Dragonbreath Chili's spellpower scaling was nerfed recently to 33% SP coefficient (otherwise same as vanilla) — [[d:shaman#1427399747160772729]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-14).
- **[Ele/Elemental Weapons bug]** Flametongue itself and Flametongue Totem do NOT benefit from Call of Flame's %fire damage bonus (devs confirmed "working as intended", marked duplicate reports) — only Elemental Weapons talent and Elemental Fury apply to all fire damage; this "double dip" nerf on Rekindling Flame -20% in PvP is also referenced — [[d:shaman#1427374687981404292]], [[d:shaman#1427374747603173468]], [[d:shaman#1427375622899892265]], [[d:shaman#1428138652143194113]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-13; confirmed by dev responses).
- **[Ele/Molten Blast "Fire Ele"]** The Molten Blast fire-ele build is widely expected to be nerfed soon ("Rip fire ele finally getting axed"); devs reportedly redesigning Elemental Weapons; community debates how to nerf MB without also killing spellhance melee (spelldmg melee weaving Flametongue/Flame Shock/Rekindling) — [[d:shaman#1427269041139224688]], [[d:shaman#1427377194094821426]], [[d:shaman#1428448415708221470]], [[d:shaman#1427376329325678733]]–[[d:shaman#1427379080898150421]] (Gumbo/Not Normal/Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-10-13/14; contested how to fix, unresolved).
- **[General]** Weapon skill breakpoints changed in Turtle so every 5 points of weapon skill = 1% hit (cleaner than vanilla) — [[d:shaman#1428121232133591171]] (Zyith, 2025-10-16).
- **[Tanking]** "1.18.1 nerfed" self-heal/threat-tank viability somewhat, per a shaman who healed geared warriors/ferals tanking dungeons without armor — [[d:shaman#1427359171657072791]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-10-13).

## Spec viability / tier opinions
- **[Enh]** Enhancement is the best/only good leveling spec for solo content; "you can level with no talents and no gear, but enh is the best" — [[d:shaman#1427654966088171600]], [[d:shaman#1427655144907997224]], [[d:shaman#1427972159946358935]] (peter_hammer/Stormtaurus, 2025-10-13/15; unanimous, no disputes).
- **[Ele]** Elemental is widely called the worst leveling spec: constant drinking, no pushback reduction, weak totems solo, no CC — countered by Not Normal noting Earthbind Totem (CC) and Earth Shield (pushback reduction) do exist — [[d:shaman#1428141021333684264]], [[d:shaman#1428141332290863157]], [[d:shaman#1428141637338529853]], [[d:shaman#1429166386348949514]] (Therix vs Not Normal, 2025-10-16; disputed).
- **[Ele endgame]** Nature ele (LB/CL spam) is called "legit overpowered" in K40, and Molten Blast ("Fire Ele") is called strongest single-target spec currently, expected until nerfed; fire ele is fine on Razorgore, Broodlord, Chromaggus but immune/resisted on most MC bosses (only Lucifron and Sulfuron Harbinger usable with Curse of Elements) — Baron Geddon and Ragnaros are fully fire immune — [[d:shaman#1427094629995642991]], [[d:shaman#1427106847072063520]], [[d:shaman#1427097458239868968]], [[d:shaman#1429168634613338233]], [[d:shaman#1429168896975437824]] (poto/Not Normal/mango/Squid, 2025-10-13/19).
- **[Ele]** Elemental is considered least popular spec but "not because it's bad", especially in raids where MB fire spec excels; drawbacks: no curse to strip nature resistance from bosses, missing raid debuffs like Improved Scorch/Shadow Vulnerability equivalents — [[d:shaman#1428448945159536661]] (billienomates), [[d:shaman#1428455917065736234]], [[d:shaman#1428456127666065499]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-17).
- **[Enh vs Tank]** Standard tank build talents give ~95% the dps of a dedicated dps build minus threat-reduction talent (Calming Winds); "Tank build will be a better DPS build than a DPS build is a Tank build" — recommend taking tank talents even when not planning to tank — [[d:shaman#1428990330245746808]], [[d:shaman#1428992161659228181]], [[d:shaman#1428995969407516692]] (DemonBoyJr/Squid/sailorVEVO, 2025-10-18).
- **[Resto]** Resto shaman viewed as class-baseline-good and always wanted, but tends to be outhealed by paladins/druids on raw log numbers because heals are expensive; healer logs called "a fool's errand" to compare directly — [[d:shaman#1427724224251957388]], [[d:shaman#1427724408172052521]], [[d:shaman#1428127466760699975]] (Squid, 2025-10-14/16).
- **[Resto vs Holy Paladin]** Holy Paladin considered ahead of resto shaman due to many instant-cast smart heals (Daybreak, Judgement of Light, Holy Strike); resto players hope for more "interactive" resto changes (totem-based heals hinted) — [[d:shaman#1428128251946995844]], [[d:shaman#1428128399540355146]], [[d:shaman#1428130056990883982]] (Miado/sailorVEVO, 2025-10-16).

## Leveling
- **[Enh]** Enhancement is the best leveling spec; can level with no talents/gear but enh optimal — [[d:shaman#1427654966088171600]] (peter_hammer).
- **[Enh weapon imbue leveling order]** General guidance: use Rockbiter until ~level 20-30 (or until Frostbrand available), then Frostbrand (forces crits via Frost Shock synergy = mana efficiency) until Elemental Weapons + Windfury are strong (around 60, or once good phys crit gear); Flametongue viable for dungeon dps pre-40 but weak without spell damage; skip training new Flametongue ranks once Frostbrand is accessible to save money — [[d:shaman#1428172447546609767]], [[d:shaman#1428172887663050883]], [[d:shaman#1428186023329796116]], [[d:shaman#1428186352171614341]], [[d:shaman#1428410400826851440]], [[d:shaman#1428409507796615292]] (Frenchie/billienomates/Zeruel, 2025-10-15/16; multiple confirmations).
- **[Enh mana/frostbrand vs WF]** Frostbrand more mana-efficient/consistent up to pre-raid gear via guaranteed crits (when Frost Shock landable); Windfury overtakes once phys crit is high, and always outperforms in raids where raid buffs boost WF and Frost Shock auto-crit isn't relevant — [[d:shaman#1427390473798160566]], [[d:shaman#1427390567628935270]], [[d:shaman#1427397446144757841]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-14).
- **[Mana sustain leveling]** Elemental Weapons (1/3, ~mid-40s) + Elemental Focus give Frost Shock spam + Frostbrand synergy (25% increased proc rate, 100% crit) that reliably procs Elemental Focus for reduced mana cost, solving early mana problems — [[d:shaman#1427261833491779615]], [[d:shaman#1427261996994138154]] (Bezawit, 2025-10-13).
- **[Leveling gear]** WC (Wailing Caverns) quest reward staff: 7 int/7 stam/7 spirit, worth grabbing — [[d:shaman#1427403261073424384]] (Dwi, 2025-10-14). Custom quest north of Silverpine (~level 25) rewards a rare spellpower shield — [[d:shaman#1427403868039286977]] (Dwi). Dragonmaw Ruins gear: 4-piece Dragonmaw set (with Grifter mix) carries into the 40s; solo the last WC boss ~28-30 for leggings/world buff, Gnomeregan doable ~30 (last boss lvl 34); Kodo Hide pants from Crossroads vendor good before Leggings of the Fang — [[d:shaman#1429215726262288525]], [[d:shaman#1429216886284877914]], [[d:shaman#1429217678329118740]], [[d:shaman#1429210534275973151]], [[d:shaman#1429230581551599827]] (chincer/Not Normal/Spicyahi, 2025-10-19). Dragonmaw LW-exclusive/BoP gloves are hard to obtain and not great for enh anyway (more rogue-oriented) — [[d:shaman#1429218274737913936]], [[d:shaman#1429218572499947670]].
- **[Tank leveling]** Totems: don't bother dropping Strength of Earth/Searing Totem on small/normal pulls (waste of mana), only for bigger pulls or elites; Stoneclaw Totem situational (distracts a second mob, doesn't tank); Stoneskin Totem best vs many small hits (e.g., lasher farming, BRD AoE, Stratholme rat trap) — [[d:shaman#1428199312290091018]], [[d:shaman#1428200131630731425]], [[d:shaman#1428142257198071978]], [[d:shaman#1427367897256431687]], [[d:shaman#1427368156451705112]] (ThatsTheJoke/billienomates/peter_hammer/Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-10-13/15). Stoneskin Totem alt tech: drop it where you can drag mobs to it one-by-one for significantly reduced damage; with enh talent, halved damage — [[d:shaman#1428247831000518667]] (Lodmat, 2025-10-16).
- **[Tank leveling stats]** Early on, Stamina is the priority stat while tank leveling; Agility gives 0 AP, Rockbiter provides most AP while leveling; gear tends to be mixed Str/Agi/mail at 60 — [[d:shaman#1427807560873939010]], [[d:shaman#1427807788041633822]] (DemonBoyJr, 2025-10-13).
- **[Weapon choice tank]** Prefer a slow 1H weapon (not fast) plus shield for tanking, per DemonBoyJr — [[d:shaman#1427808219778252963]] (2025-10-13).
- **[Totems delayed]** Don't rush Water Totem before Poison Cleansing Totem; Grounding Totem (with Air) worth rushing (interrupts); Stormwrought Ruins has many disease/poison mobs making Water/Poison Cleanse valuable there — [[d:shaman#1427427689391718572]], [[d:shaman#1427427838226858054]], [[d:shaman#1427427956460093542]], [[d:shaman#1427428236362514512]] (billienomates, 2025-10-13).
- **[Elemental leveling]** Widely discouraged; "99% of mobs you can kill" but requires constant drinking, low downtime efficiency, uses Molten Blast (fire dmg) as filler; "nobody has ever leveled as elemental" (joke, but reflects rarity) — [[d:shaman#1427650148993073273]], [[d:shaman#1427650247491850302]], [[d:shaman#1429166386348949514]], [[d:shaman#1429167444349685974]] (Dreamforge/peter_hammer/Zeruel/Therix, 2025-10-14/16).
- **[Healing while leveling]** Chain Heal not much used pre-40 (before it's available)/even in low-level dungeons; Healing Wave carries through most dungeons even at 60 — [[d:shaman#1427723124308185148]] (Squid, 2025-10-14).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[Resto]** Priority: 1) Healing Power, 2) enough Stamina to survive raid damage, 3) enough mana to not go OOM, 4) crit and haste — [[d:shaman#1428075745908883639]] (peter_hammer, 2025-10-15).
- **[Enh dps]** Priority: 1) Hit, 2) AP, 3) Crit and Haste, 4) Stamina and Int — [[d:shaman#1428075943775436841]] (peter_hammer, 2025-10-15). Weapon skill is effectively "hit + extra" and substantially more valuable than raw hit due to glancing-blow reduction; 5 weapon skill ≈ +2.7% total dps per spreadsheet testing; 15 skill = ~46% boost to glancing blow damage (glances are ~40% of attacks) + 3% accuracy — [[d:shaman#1428118777882738739]], [[d:shaman#1428122775951769770]], [[d:shaman#1428123311560331324]] (Not Normal/Zeruel, 2025-10-16). Weapon skill ≈ crit in value per spreadsheets; ~0.04% crit per point of usable weapon skill (unconfirmed exactly); ~3 points of usable weapon skill is often where an item supersedes alternatives — [[d:shaman#1428495519163875338]], [[d:shaman#1428496072489042080]], [[d:shaman#1428496376022700082]] (Squid, 2025-10-17).
- **[Hit cap]** Total hit cap is 8%; talents provide 3% of that (Turtle-specific) — [[d:shaman#1427802019565010955]] (Squid, 2025-10-13).
- **[Enh crit cap]** Crit cap (dual wield-relevant only) is 440 (rating? or crit%) per Squid; going past 55% crit as tank/enh not useful unless 315+ weapon skill; hit doesn't raise crit chance unless already at the crit cap — [[d:shaman#1429305874102358057]], [[d:shaman#1429306341557796967]], [[d:shaman#1429306944778141736]] (Not Normal/Squid, 2025-10-19).
- **[Attack table mechanics]** Vanilla-style single roll table: e.g. hypothetical 100-slot table = 40 glance / 14 parry / 5 dodge / 10 crit / 23 hit / 8 nothing; more hit converts "nothing" to hit; more crit converts a hit-slot to crit; crit and miss are NOT independent rolls, so higher miss chance effectively reduces available crit outcomes too — [[d:shaman#1429307580714455080]], [[d:shaman#1429307696380772474]], [[d:shaman#1429307749476601978]] (30, 2025-10-19).
- **[Defense]** 1 point of Defense = 0.04% each of dodge, parry, miss, block, and reduced chance to be crit; Defense cap referenced as 40 (i.e. defense skill target) for avoiding crushing blows-equivalent; no confirmed exact source, "check Google for twow defense" — [[d:shaman#1429305623614324786]], [[d:shaman#1429308238415007804]] (Spicyahi/Squid, 2025-10-19).
- **[Taunt/Spell hit tank]** Taunt (Stoneclaw totem too) uses spell hit; need 15% spell hit for ~99% taunt reliability; shamans reportedly have the highest taunt-hit chance of tank classes given good crit (Elemental Devastation talent helps close the gap) — [[d:shaman#1429308783573860472]], [[d:shaman#1429308925877944463]], [[d:shaman#1429309128593117345]], [[d:shaman#1429308972069945438]] (Squid/Not Normal, 2025-10-19).
- **[Healing coefficients]** No spell has a healing-power scaling cap ("no spell has sp scaling cap" — confirmed by peter_hammer after a user claimed diminishing returns on Chain Heal Rank 1 around 650-1000 healing power; consensus: user's result attributed to spell damage RNG variance, not a real cap) — [[d:shaman#1427318109940744213]], [[d:shaman#1427324557802344631]], [[d:shaman#1427325009994711060]], [[d:shaman#1428052265704357888]] (Squid/peter_hammer/Zeruel, 2025-10-13/16; disputed then resolved as no cap, later retested and confirmed normal by Squid).
- **[Healing coefficient formula]** General formula for non-DoT/HoT cast-time healing/damage spells: cast time / 3.5 = spellpower coefficient; Chain Heal is an exception at ~61% coefficient for the first hit, halving per bounce — [[d:shaman#1427328334408319227]], [[d:shaman#1427328443363758254]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-13). Desmos calculator to check single-target CH HPS correctness: https://www.desmos.com/calculator/pvadmeckq9 — [[d:shaman#1427345285746524362]] (peter_hammer, 2025-10-13); mana/haste tradeoff calculator: https://www.desmos.com/calculator/s1t7vmvo7o — [[d:shaman#1427751310165278740]] (peter_hammer, 2025-10-15).
- **[Resto haste]** ~2% haste ≈ roughly +40 effective healing in a mid-geared setup per peter_hammer's math; a flat +1500 mana offsets a meaningful mp5 loss; haste especially strong when damage spikes are expected, and generally "superior in general gameplay feel" since more casts = more mana spent per unit time too — [[d:shaman#1427749678031835156]], [[d:shaman#1427750424219484371]], [[d:shaman#1427752050887884800]], [[d:shaman#1427754175630147686]] (peter_hammer, 2025-10-15). Well-geared resto benchmark: ~10k mana buffed is "well geared"; ~7k unbuffed considered good too — [[d:shaman#1427744120457138387]], [[d:shaman#1427744491548446768]] (peter_hammer/Kroeskop, 2025-10-14).
- **[Str vs Spellpower for spellhance tank threat]** For a spellhance shaman tank choosing a weapon enchant, Crusader beats a flat +30 spellpower enchant: 50 Str (with Kings+Ancestral Knowledge ≈ x2.3) → 115 AP → 11.5 dmg added to Earth Shock (10% AP coefficient), versus 30 SP → 11.58 dmg (38.6% SP coefficient) — roughly even on Earth Shock alone, but Str/Crusader adds far more overall damage/threat since AP buffs all physical damage too, plus Crusader gives ~40-60% uptime on 100 Str (avg +40-60 Str) and heal-based threat — [[d:shaman#1427350484632731872]], [[d:shaman#1427351039425646704]], [[d:shaman#1427351615374884884]], [[d:shaman#1427346387217158224]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-13). Weapon-damage (+9 dmg) enchant is far inferior to Crusader for shamans; +9 dmg to a 3.4-speed weapon ≈ only ~37 AP equivalent — [[d:shaman#1427345164430606337]], [[d:shaman#1427345938397007923]], [[d:shaman#1427346061567070338]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-13).
- **[Enh]** Weapon skill above 300 gives 0.2% hit per point; 3% from talents + 3% from gear (weapon skill quest book + one item like Flameguard Gauntlets) ≈ 7.4% total hit, treated as a "soft cap" beyond which more hit is devalued vs crit; ~0.6% hit ≈ value of 1% crit around that point — [[d:shaman#1429465287916523672]], [[d:shaman#1429465734655905882]], [[d:shaman#1429466092459393045]], [[d:shaman#1429466266510430383]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-19). Worked example: raising weapon skill 305→312 shifts glancing-blow damage from 75% to 89% of normal-hit damage, netting roughly a 3% total dps increase in a sampled enh parse (128 glances, 97 crits, 58 hits) — [[d:shaman#1431201743349481573]], [[d:shaman#1431202907885146113]], [[d:shaman#1431205807755624561]], [[d:shaman#1431206076421771305]] (Jaki, 2025-10-24).
- **[Int→crit]** Spell crit conversion from Intellect is ~59.5 Int per 1% spell crit, "around" classic values — [[d:shaman#1430029559323033702]], [[d:shaman#1430034612301598800]] (dusty/Squid, 2025-10-21).
- **[Empowered Lightning Shield]** Confirmed to scale with BOTH spellpower and AP simultaneously when procced via Lightning Strike (native SP scaling of Lightning Shield doesn't disappear) — [[d:shaman#1430382312356581376]], [[d:shaman#1430383804760981626]] (Zeruel/Not Normal, 2025-10-22).
- **[Water Shield mana return]** ~20 AP grants ~1 extra mana returned per Water Shield charge; a tested example at 343 AP returned ~68 mana per charge, tracking the ratio — [[d:shaman#1432756628322779320]], [[d:shaman#1432759066140344400]] (Diving Instructor Knife/Shayerah, 2025-10-28). Lightning Strike used while Water Shield is active only returns the plain tooltip mana amount (no "empowered" multiplier), unlike Lightning Shield which hits for ~2-3x tooltip when empowered by Lightning Strike — called out as inconsistent/possibly unintended by the tester — [[d:shaman#1432742459011498129]], [[d:shaman#1432745174055911465]], [[d:shaman#1432748725985349793]] (Shayerah, 2025-10-28; single-source observation, unresolved).
- **[Threat stacking math]** Multiple % threat-reduction modifiers (e.g., Salvation, Calming Winds/subtlety-type talent) multiply sequentially against base threat rather than summing, and each step rounds down: e.g. 1000 threat × 0.75 (salv) × 0.75 (calming wind) × 0.98 (subtlety-type talent) ≈ 550 threat, not the naively-expected additive reduction — [[d:shaman#1432258885984583700]], [[d:shaman#1432260294851428362]], [[d:shaman#1432249809934487634]] (Squid/Zhafu, 2025-10-27).
- **[Threat table specifics]** Rockbiter Weapon on Turtle increases threat by a *percentage* (not the classic flat per-hit amount), which is why slow weapons are now preferred for tank threat instead of classic's fast-dagger meta — [[d:shaman#1430203667453317254]] (Holidae, 2025-10-19).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[Tank threat]** Reverberation (a shock-related threat talent) is favored over Thundering Strikes/Flurry for tank threat because it's "more consistent" — [[d:shaman#1430277807308669079]], [[d:shaman#1430278212729962648]] (Squid, 2025-10-21). Elemental Focus (mana return on crit) and Elements Grace (crit chance) are called major, high-priority pickups for tanks — losing them "will hurt" — [[d:shaman#1430276365659275315]], [[d:shaman#1430276736892928070]], [[d:shaman#1430276771374174410]] (Not Normal/Squid, 2025-10-21). For threat, prioritize Reverberation and Elemental Focus early over deeper Enhancement filler; Stoneclaw Totem + threat talents (Totemic Alignment redirects Stoneclaw's aggro to the tank) offsets a lot of AoE threat/mana cost — [[d:shaman#1432044052924076042]], [[d:shaman#1430279394592555018]] (Sinister Minister/Miado, 2025-10-21/26).
- **[Stoneclaw ranks]** Higher ranks of Stoneclaw Totem generate proportionally more threat, so upranking is usually worth it despite the mana cost, per Frenchie (single source, not deeply tested) — [[d:shaman#1432046323695095968]] (2025-10-26).
- **[Bug]** Eye of the Storm (spell pushback-immunity-on-crit talent) only procs on spell crits post-1.18, not melee crits — the old "sit to force a crit" melee trick no longer procs it; multiple players confirmed via testing (peter_hammer did the sit trick, got melee crits, no proc), and it's suggested this should be reported as a bug — [[d:shaman#1429906572401971420]], [[d:shaman#1429907169318408192]], [[d:shaman#1429909780977291335]], [[d:shaman#1429910402208235611]] (Squid/peter_hammer/DemonBoyJr, 2025-10-20).
- **[HoJ / Windfury "anti-synergy"]** Long-running, largely unverified community folklore that Windfury Weapon procs have reduced or zero chance to trigger Hand of Justice/other extra-attack-on-hit items; one 8-hour, full-scholomance-run test with HoJ + BM Cloak + WF imbue found zero occurrences of a WF-attack triggering another proc, but the expected proc rate is calculated at ~0.08% per hit (needing ~5000+ hits to statistically confirm/deny), so the test is inconclusive; more likely explanation is that WF and other extra-attacks proc independently/simultaneously rather than off each other — [[d:shaman#1428877484392321176]], [[d:shaman#1428879937817018388]], [[d:shaman#1428881424379023360]], [[d:shaman#1428884749291618404]], [[d:shaman#1428886051941777578]], [[d:shaman#1428882490181025934]] (poto/Dodging/billienomates/Zhafu/Squid, 2025-10-18; disputed/unresolved, "log schizoing" acknowledged by participants).

## Tanking specifics
- **[Stat priority]** Stamina > Dodge > Agility is the standing shaman tank stat priority — [[d:shaman#1431377619760185507]], [[d:shaman#1430204003240640512]] (Holidae, 2025-10-19/25).
- **[Block near-useless]** Consensus across many messages: block chance/value gives shamans very little because (unlike paladins/warriors) they have no talents/abilities that convert blocks into extra mitigation or threat; a 1% chance to block ~60 damage is worth much less than 300 armor per hit or 1% dodge; block only starts to matter once avoidance is high enough to push crushing blows/crit off the attack table (~40-60%+ avoidance, roughly Naxx-level gearing) — [[d:shaman#1430652888060792874]], [[d:shaman#1430653988101034024]], [[d:shaman#1430656532915294278]], [[d:shaman#1430660772890939493]], [[d:shaman#1431396854762963176]], [[d:shaman#1431398219115659377]] (Zeruel/Not Normal, 2025-10-22/25). Shield Specialization talent (block value/chance) is essentially never taken by raid tanks — Ancestral Knowledge's 5% all-stats (which also gives dodge via Agi) is considered clearly better — [[d:shaman#1430211534600147096]], [[d:shaman#1430211862615691345]], [[d:shaman#1430210564931715132]] (DemonBoyJr/Holidae, 2025-10-21). Not Normal pushes back that block's real value may be underrated and wants to quantify it properly (framed as an open question, no resolution reached) — [[d:shaman#1431005607535579398]], [[d:shaman#1431402420277346324]], [[d:shaman#1431405098923458601]] (2025-10-23/25).
- **[Shield Spec exception]** Some tanks (e.g. LordHodenLurch, phyx) still run Shield Spec for 35% block w/ 250 block value, defended as viable at higher gear where block starts pushing off crush/crit; majority view (DemonBoyJr, Holidae, Porkins) says the 5% stats from Ancestral Knowledge is better in essentially all raid-tanking cases — [[d:shaman#1430211432720502884]], [[d:shaman#1430211534600147096]], [[d:shaman#1430211862615691345]], [[d:shaman#1430214810972454922]] (2025-10-21; disputed, minority view preserved).
- **[Leveling→raid transition]** While tank-leveling, take Shield Specialization over Ancestral Knowledge (AK doesn't provide much value until level 60 when stat budgets on gear jump); switch to AK once raiding or once stamina/gear is high enough (~200 stamina suggested as one personal threshold) — [[d:shaman#1430215022503526532]], [[d:shaman#1430938220652400692]] (DemonBoyJr/chincer, 2025-10-21/23).
- **[EHP mechanics]** Armor-to-EHP conversion depends only on the attacker's level and the tank's raw HP, NOT on how much total armor the tank already has — e.g. at 4k HP, +600 armor is worth the same ~463 EHP whether starting from 7k or 12k armor — [[d:shaman#1430651515735113748]], [[d:shaman#1430651702759133234]] (Zeruel, 2025-10-23).
- **[2H vs 1H+Shield]** Two playstyles: 1H+shield (stacks Strength/Parry/Block, more traditional "sword and board") vs 2H (stacks Agility for dodge + crit for threat); the sword-and-board approach is considered the "real tank" default, with 2H reserved for burst-aggro pulls or specific fights — [[d:shaman#1430206328974086214]], [[d:shaman#1430208196831608893]] (CantBelieveMyDecidueye/LordHodenLurch, 2025-10-19). Some raid tanks do use 2H full time on fights like Onyxia/Sulfuron (Solnius) — [[d:shaman#1430144272480473170]], [[d:shaman#1430154637700235309]] (IrnBruBreath/Stormtaurus, 2025-10-20).
- **[No defensive CD]** Shaman tank's main acknowledged weakness is the lack of a dedicated defensive cooldown (unlike Shield Wall/Barkskin/etc.); the ZG hero trinket is cited as a partial substitute defensive-CD-like item for shaman tanks — [[d:shaman#1430208992105468038]], [[d:shaman#1430209995915530300]] (DemonBoyJr/phyx, 2025-10-19).
- **[Viability confirmed]** Multiple independent confirmations that shaman tanks are fully viable through Naxx/Kara40 and can even MT: "Shamans raid tank K40 currently which is much harder than Naxx", guilds have run shaman MTs from MC through Naxx without gear boosts, and a detailed comparison to bear tank was given (shaman lacks a dedicated defensive tool but has a flatter/lower-variance damage-intake profile via Rockbiter's innate reduction, without sacrificing dps/threat like bear/defensive stance do) — [[d:shaman#1430208992105468038]], [[d:shaman#1431326414602502315]], [[d:shaman#1432054781924610158]], [[d:shaman#1432056290108248134]] (DemonBoyJr/Squid/peter_hammer, 2025-10-19/26).
- **[Totems for tanking]** Strength of Earth or Stoneclaw are the two main tank totem choices in dungeons (swap based on need to hold multiple mobs); Stoneskin Totem is very weak (reduces only ~11-27 damage per hit at low level, negligible at raid-boss hit sizes) and mainly useful vs many small hits (lasher farm, BRD AoE farming, elites/large pulls); pull multiple packs back to totems rather than dropping fresh totems per pull to stay mana-positive — [[d:shaman#1432046323695095968]], [[d:shaman#1432047119107096669]], [[d:shaman#1432047213600833627]], [[d:shaman#1427475715036549161]], [[d:shaman#1427279461681729638]] (Frenchie/chincer/peter_hammer, 2025-10-13/26).
- **[Weapon]** Slow 1H weapons preferred for tanking due to Rockbiter's %-based threat (see Threat table note above); Deathbringer is BiS tank weapon through most of AQ40; shamans lack a truly ideal tanking-statted weapon compared to other tank classes (no dedicated defensive 1H like paladins/warriors get) — [[d:shaman#1429607826963173469]] (billienomates, 2025-10-20).
- **[Squishy early]** Shaman tanks have no defensive talents/mitigation until level 26 and are very squishy before then; Eye of the Storm (spell-crit only) is the only early defensive tool — [[d:shaman#1429906433797001379]], [[d:shaman#1429906513421668363]], [[d:shaman#1429906572401971420]] (Holidae/Squid, 2025-10-20).

## Healing specifics
- **[Priority & workflow]** peter_hammer's "Resto 101": stack +healing, learn encounter mechanics and tank/DPS squishiness, position for both totems and heal range, drop totems, spam Chain Heal, reposition and repeat — [[d:shaman#1427735977207533720]] (2025-10-14).
- **[Rank usage]** Suggested rank guidance: Healing Wave R4 if low HP pool, up to R6 depending on consumables/mana; fill downtime with melee (Judgement of Wisdom procs) or R1 Healing Wave spam to proc Water Shield; Chain Heal R1 for general use, R3 only under heavy raid damage (burns mana fast); Lesser Healing Wave is the fastest heal (mana-inefficient) — some downrank it, others prefer R4 HW or Chain Heal instead; Healing Stream Totem is very good for offloading small/incidental damage in dungeons — [[d:shaman#1429871300943282317]], [[d:shaman#1429871523447050396]], [[d:shaman#1429871876066513058]] (Miado, 2025-10-19).
- **[Mana sustain criticism/wishlist]** Resto's mana sustain talents (Tidal Mastery/Tidal Surge/Undertow) don't scale with gear level, unlike other healers, and community wishlist items include: mp5 baseline on Water Shield, crit procs generating mana return, and moving the "consume a Water Shield charge" mechanic earlier in the tree / extending it to more spells (Earth Shock, Lightning Bolt, Lightning Strike) so Elemental/Enhancement benefit too — [[d:shaman#1429873964452417556]], [[d:shaman#1429874045716791478]], [[d:shaman#1429875046461079552]], [[d:shaman#1429881038150045799]] (Not Normal/Miado/Holidae, 2025-10-19). Chain Heal is considered "too good" relative to other resto tools, and Holy Paladins are considered too strong at tank-healing (Daybreak talent specifically called out as overpowered), which together explain why resto doesn't get picked for dedicated tank-healing despite a solid kit since 1.18 — [[d:shaman#1429872827795771452]], [[d:shaman#1429873588428734546]], [[d:shaman#1429873653000175799]] (Not Normal, 2025-10-19).
- **[Resto weapon melee]** "Melee resto" (using Lightning Strike procs for mana return with a two-hander like Finkle's Lava Dredger or Shen'dralar staff) is a viable playstyle to reduce OOM risk — [[d:shaman#1430747070674763826]], [[d:shaman#1430747135187353600]] (billienomates, 2025-10-23).
- **[Healing addons]** Puppeteer/Smartheal-style "click-and-it-decides" healing addons (e.g. Quickheal) are viewed very negatively by top players as "for DPS who want to flex heals but not learn how to heal"; peter_hammer argues 99.9% of shaman healing skill is just correct targeting, not addon automation — [[d:shaman#1427726815740428299]]-[[d:shaman#1427734247472627764]] (Lodestar/Squid/peter_hammer, 2025-10-14; strongly disputed/mocked in-channel).

## Dungeons & raids (tactics by instance / boss)
- **[MC]** Baron Geddon and Ragnaros are the only bosses fully immune to fire damage; the rest can be fire-DPS'd with Curse of Elements active, though heavy-fire-resist bosses (unnamed beyond Garr/Magmadar as "beefy" even after CoE) remain tough; Lucifron and Sulfuron Harbinger are technically fire-usable if CoE is up — [[d:shaman#1427106847072063520]], [[d:shaman#1427107277948719175]], [[d:shaman#1427107414338965654]], [[d:shaman#1427097458239868968]] (Not Normal/poto, 2025-10-13).
- **[BWL/Naxx fire]** Razorgore, Broodlord Lashlayer, and Chromaggus are all fine for fire-spec dps (Chromaggus is a "sorta unique case" — nature ele may be easier to pivot on than fire ele needs to pivot off) — [[d:shaman#1427106847072063520]], [[d:shaman#1427097207525478491]], [[d:shaman#1427097276781957202]] (Not Normal/Squid, 2025-10-13).
- **[AQ40 Huhuran]** Peasant Caller (Nature Resist trinket, PvP drop, farmable solo at Chillwind Camp) recommended for the whole raid to soak her poison volleys, timed for the 30% enrage phase where volleys become frequent; boss doesn't have much HP and can be burst through enrage — [[d:shaman#1427796907266150500]], [[d:shaman#1427798050167849062]], [[d:shaman#1427798318276280321]], [[d:shaman#1427802857146683435]] (poto/Squid/Spicyahi, 2025-10-13).
- **[AQ40 Twin Emperors]** Shaman tanks specifically struggle here because Earth Shock (a big threat tool) can't be used, and the fight is generally "very defensive heavy"/healer-intensive — [[d:shaman#1429610780776468632]], [[d:shaman#1429610531098071122]], [[d:shaman#1429611281459187732]] (Squid/poto, 2025-10-20). Shaman can 3rd-tank Skeram, the bug Trio, Sartura, and Ouro — [[d:shaman#1429612004775170120]] (Squid).
- **[Naxx Loatheb]** Some guilds run Loatheb with zero direct heal casts, relying on HoTs (spriest DoTs/druid HoTs) alone — [[d:shaman#1428129323189272676]], [[d:shaman#1428129395477971084]] (30, 2025-10-16). Maexxna should be killed before her first web cast if possible — [[d:shaman#1429947652145021069]] (Miado, 2025-10-21).
- **[Black Morass duo]** For 2-man DME/BM-style runs with a shaman, mana-burn mechanics on the last boss make a druid tank + shaman heal combo preferred over shaman tank, since the shaman can be disadvantaged by mana burn while tanking; kiting the boss on the ledge is also an option — [[d:shaman#1429745742163218492]], [[d:shaman#1429747984081489921]], [[d:shaman#1429760269650759741]], [[d:shaman#1429769180323905587]] (Jorrick/Cariad, 2025-10-19).
- **[Kara10/Kara40]** Kara Crypts (Kara10) and Black Morass are the top fresh-60 enh gear sources; Kara10 groups always want a shaman for spider-basement poison cleansing, and the Fist of the Forgotten Order (from Moroes) is specifically called out as a target item — [[d:shaman#1429385869264945223]] (peter_hammer, 2025-10-19).
- **[Stormwrought Ruins/Castle]** Contains a Water Shield-related totem upgrade path (Totem of Tides, obtainable ~level 33) that adds AoE damage on Water Shield charge consumption; also a source of poison/disease-heavy trash making Poison Cleansing/Water Totem valuable there — [[d:shaman#1427428236362514512]], [[d:shaman#1432048253100359710]], [[d:shaman#1428999894085013605]] (billienomates/Frenchie/Dinousek, 2025-10-13/17/26).
- **[Lasher farming / AoE farm]** See Rotation: AoE section for the core Fire Nova → Magma → Fire Nova rotation and gear (Earth Shield, Flametongue imbue, Stoneskin/Grace of Air totems). Additional notes: Vampirism items do NOT heal off totem damage (Fire Nova/Magma), which is called a major limiting factor preventing shamans from safely doing very large (8+) pack pulls the way some other classes can — [[d:shaman#1428145842682069032]], [[d:shaman#1428146441007661198]], [[d:shaman#1428146581076705372]] (billienomates/Squid, 2025-10-16). Force Reactive Disk does NOT work on lashers because they are nature-immune and FRD deals nature damage — [[d:shaman#1430997994865557658]], [[d:shaman#1430998620336951337]] (poto, 2025-10-27; disputed slightly — poto later clarifies it does massive damage vs other AoE targets, just not lashers specifically).

## PvP
- **[General]** Shaman PvP strength is explained mostly by every spec being able to cheaply grab enhancement-tree tank/defensive talents (10% damage reduction, armor/dodge bonuses) without sacrificing much, producing tanky-yet-hard-hitting hybrids across all 3 specs; keeping mail armor + decent stamina from tier sets adds to this — [[d:shaman#1431461318296080384]], [[d:shaman#1431461397300252794]], [[d:shaman#1431461600644169758]], [[d:shaman#1431462185535803494]], [[d:shaman#1431462509348393000]] (poto/Squid, 2025-10-25).
- **[Elemental Weapons proc — ninja change]** The Elemental Weapons talent's on-miss/dodge/parry proc was silently changed from a 10% movement speed buff back to a 30% haste buff on the next melee attack — the movement-speed version was "never intended" per devs (unofficial/no patch notes); the change was generally well received ("good ninja change" since the movement speed version was seen as "stupid") — [[d:shaman#1429628134948606032]], [[d:shaman#1429629294551765023]], [[d:shaman#1429638771376128133]] (Abuelito/Squid/billienomates, 2025-10-20).
- **[PvP totem management]** Use Call of Elements totem-set-swapping macros/addon with the "autoswitch sets in PVP" feature to auto-drop counter-totems (e.g., Earthbind vs rogues, Magma vs melee) based on enemy class — [[d:shaman#1427548144488546345]], [[d:shaman#1427562362608812133]] (Scatty, 2025-10-13).
- **[PvP relics]** For a "shocker"/PvP shock build, Totem of Rotten Roots (healing reduction) is recommended over Totem of Rage for the anti-heal utility, since better alternatives exist for pure shock damage (Broken Earth is BiS but far off) — [[d:shaman#1431086315038052352]], [[d:shaman#1431095201866190919]] (DemonBoyJr/Nandieb, 2025-10-24).
- **[PvP builds referenced]** peter_hammer posted example PvP tank/dps splits "34/17/0" (defense-leaning) and "30/0/20" (damage-leaning) as a level-tanking Balor guide context; a "spellhance tank" PvP build of roughly 22/24/0 is mentioned by Diving Instructor Knife, who also notes running mostly resto-tank in PvP; an Elemental PvP build of ~27/24/0 fire-leaning (swapping some pushback-on-crit talents) is also referenced — [[d:shaman#1430518058077913120]], [[d:shaman#1430518289544642560]], [[d:shaman#1432370336023838993]], [[d:shaman#1432369843939840030]] (peter_hammer/Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-10-22/27; all single-source, treat as anecdotal starting points not confirmed cookie-cutter).
- **[Enh PvP]** Historically strong/near-unkillable in earlier patches per multiple nostalgic references to "SoD" enhance shaman tankiness (not Turtle-specific — treat as color, not a Turtle mechanic claim).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[Totem management]** Call of Elements (CoE) + Nampower are the standard combo for one-button multi-totem drops; Nampower must be installed via the official Turtle launcher's "mods" tab (not a normal addon) for CoE's "throwset" to drop all 4 totems in a single press — without Nampower, CoE / other totem addons will only drop totems one at a time — [[d:shaman#1427843757457412217]], [[d:shaman#1427845465826787530]], [[d:shaman#1428836554499031072]] (HippoDoe/Squid/DemonBoyJr, 2025-10-13/18). Two CoE forks exist: the Marceline edition (https://github.com/MarcelineVQ/CallOfElements) is recommended as more Turtle-optimized (supports Nampower/SuperWoW natively); the Laytya fork is newer/more features but not Turtle-optimized and some users couldn't get it working — [[d:shaman#1427910498845724843]], [[d:shaman#1428271369992278138]], [[d:shaman#1428276678437634158]] (Scatty/arrolla/peter_hammer, 2025-10-15/18).
- **[Troubleshooting]** If CoE/Nampower totem-drop isn't working, verify nampower.dll exists in the game directory and is listed in dlls.txt, confirm launching via the official launcher (not a raw client), and as a last resort fully uninstall/reinstall Nampower (fixed at least one user's issue) — [[d:shaman#1427911491801317376]], [[d:shaman#1427913316642062468]], [[d:shaman#1427914184972308561]], [[d:shaman#1427915832637263914]] (Scatty/GnomeGanker, 2025-10-15).
- **[Known bug]** CoE's totem bar randomly disappears mid-session requiring a UI /reload; reported as a bug (tracked at https://turtle-wow.org/bug-report?id=16603) but unresolved as of this chunk, "apparently caused by certain gear/talents" per one report, cause not confirmed — [[d:shaman#1429763003997229076]], [[d:shaman#1431879594624946326]], [[d:shaman#1432704463159951381]] (Chompsky/Seekay/Hiryu, 2025-10-16/26/28).
- **[Macro syntax]** Without CoE, multi-totem-drop macros can use `/run CastSpellByName("Totem Name")` lines (one per totem) via Nampower; with RoidMacros installed, syntax simplifies to `/cast Totem Name` allowing more totems per macro; a totem-bar addon (Call of Elements or ArchiTotem) is needed to see accurate multi-totem cooldown timers — [[d:shaman#1431282899604213865]], [[d:shaman#1431283182757609493]], [[d:shaman#1432361668385701939]] (Porkins/Holidae, 2025-10-24/27).
- **[Dot/debuff tracking]** PFUI mis-displays Flame Shock's duration (shows 12s instead of the actual 15s and doesn't reflect Molten Blast refreshes); switching to ShaguTweaks+Plates fixes the active/inactive display but the numeric timer is still wrong; no fully accurate solution found in-thread besides workarounds — [[d:shaman#1427962680207081513]], [[d:shaman#1427966395198472253]] (Gizeh, 2025-10-15).
- **[Other]** SuperWoW mod causes a visible fake "Berserking" 5-second buff icon on trolls whenever hit — this is actually the game's internal haste-racial tracking becoming visible, not a real bug — [[d:shaman#1427966395198472253]], [[d:shaman#1428015532526927905]] (Gizeh/Squid, 2025-10-15).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[Race]** Orc considered best pure-dps race for shaman (weapon skill/axe racial synergy repeatedly called top-tier) — [[d:shaman#1428516042841522187]] (Abuelito, 2025-10-17). Tauren favored for tanking (bonus HP, Warstomp AoE stun utility, though its raid usefulness is disputed — some call it "useless in raids," others say anyone dismissing Stomp "isn't operating at 100% brain efficiency" since raid leads do call for stomp usage); Troll liked for aesthetics/animations and racial skill kit — [[d:shaman#1428500232366460990]], [[d:shaman#1428517402043355179]], [[d:shaman#1428517677126910012]], [[d:shaman#1428517777139962027]] (Kokuyo/Squid/sailorVEVO, 2025-10-17; disputed on Stomp's raid value).
- **[Professions]** Engineering (Force Reactive Disk shield, Sappers, Dynamite) called "the best profession for basically every spec" mainly due to Sappers and FRD, though several argue it's not strictly necessary since shamans already have strong AoE threat/damage without it; Enchanting does not require a gathering profession to pair with it (unlike Alchemy needing Herbalism) — any profession that produces high-level greens+ to disenchant works — [[d:shaman#1428248936015204413]], [[d:shaman#1428249153879806084]], [[d:shaman#1428251894127525919]], [[d:shaman#1429007269663866880]], [[d:shaman#1429008277915566231]] (billienomates/Squid/Banther, 2025-10-15/18).
- **[Gold]** Herbalism/Alchemy geared toward Lasher farming is a primary shaman gold-making strategy (fast AoE farm via Fire Nova/Magma rotation, see AoE section); some report ~30g/hour running personal dungeon lockouts, or being paid ~100g/run to power dungeons for guildmates — [[d:shaman#1428383599299919972]], [[d:shaman#1428381861528211647]], [[d:shaman#1430935656959447170]] (Tomo/Squid/DemonBoyJr, 2025-10-15/22). Farming lashers en masse with alt accounts + mail-automation addons was questioned as possibly bannable but confirmed NOT a ToS violation, "done by the thousands on Nordanaar" — [[d:shaman#1432599130638782567]], [[d:shaman#1432599354543308930]], [[d:shaman#1432599465365340170]] (Greybull/Squid, 2025-10-28).

## Bugs & quirks
- **[Flametongue/Call of Flame]** Flametongue (both the weapon imbue and the totem) does not benefit from the Call of Flame talent's fire damage bonus; devs marked bug reports about this "working as intended" and a follow-up report "duplicate" — see 1.18.1 changes section for full citations.
- **[Eye of the Storm]** No longer procs off melee crits post-1.18 (only spell crits) — see Cooldowns/threat section for citations; suspected regression from removing the talent's old healing-cast-pushback-protection component.
- **[Elemental Weapons proc]** Silently changed from 10% movement speed to 30% haste-on-next-attack (see PvP section) — undocumented/no patch notes ("ninja change").
- **[Ragnaros T2 legs loot table]** Database-inspection claim (single source, not independently verified) that Ragnaros' loot tables include one copy of every class's T2 leg token per group EXCEPT shaman and paladin, which each only appear once total across both tables, potentially making those two classes' leg tokens rarer than others' from Rag specifically — [[d:shaman#1430616750969323680]], [[d:shaman#1430618430532485190]], [[d:shaman#1430618851120513115]] (Dodging/Zeruel, 2025-10-22; low-confidence, database-inference only, not confirmed by drop-rate data).
- **[Nature-resistance/CoE]** No curse or debuff exists to strip Nature resistance from bosses (unlike Curse of Elements/Shadow for Fire/Shadow), which the community feels is a design oversight — [[d:shaman#1429132507588202638]], [[d:shaman#1429132607735468183]], [[d:shaman#1429136033697628372]], [[d:shaman#1429136069483298939]] (SoyKnight/Not Normal, 2025-10-19).

## Contested / open questions
- **[Fire Ele / Molten Blast nerf]** Widely expected to be nerfed (called "an abomination" by Not Normal, requiring 25 talent points in Enhancement mostly wasted just to access one talent), but no consensus on how to nerf it without also collaterally damaging spellhance melee (which shares the same Elemental Weapons/Flametongue interactions) — unresolved as of this chunk.
- **[HoJ/Windfury anti-synergy]** Never conclusively tested at sufficient sample size; remains folklore. See Cooldowns/threat section.
- **[Block's real value for shaman tanks]** Not Normal argues the common wisdom that block is "lightyears" worse than dodge/parry may be overstated once actual math is done, but no one in the chunk completes that math — open question.
- **[Frost Shock vs Earth Shock for enh in MC]** One observed log showed an enh player using Frost Shock over Earth Shock exclusively in MC; proposed reasons (some MC mobs have frost vulnerability, or Curse of the Elements) were speculative and never confirmed — [[d:shaman#1427921324004868146]], [[d:shaman#1427937638933069834]] (Zykes/peter_hammer, 2025-10-15; unanswered).
- **[StormStrike raid-buff removal net impact]** Disputed whether removing StormStrike's damage buff to nearby shamans was really "ele shamans losing 20% overnight" (billienomates) or whether the actual loss is smaller/more nuanced ("Ele got consistent 10% instead" — peter_hammer; "hardly a 20% nerf... a bit misleading" — 30) — [[d:shaman#1430063518714953739]], [[d:shaman#1430066536898629694]], [[d:shaman#1430064698778189954]] (2025-10-21; unresolved).
- **[Shield Spec vs Ancestral Knowledge, precise value gap]** Zeruel speculates the two talents "could be closer than we thought" once crush-immunity thresholds are properly modeled, but no math was completed in-chunk — [[d:shaman#1431436607956848700]], [[d:shaman#1431437498793590886]] (2025-10-25).
- **[Vamp/lasher self-heal ceiling]** Whether any item lets shamans self-sustain through very large (8+) AoE pulls remains an open complaint — Vampirism gear doesn't proc off totem damage, and no clean workaround was found.
