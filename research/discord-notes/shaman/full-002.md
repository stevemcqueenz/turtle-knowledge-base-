## Rotation: single target
- **[elemental]** Basic single-target rotation described: Flame Shock → Lightning Bolt → Chain Lightning → refresh Flame Shock with Molten Blast → repeat — [[d:shaman#1434425008507064381]] (12inchesfolded, 2025-11-02; question, no correction posted = tentatively confirmed pattern given no pushback, but treat as single source/unanswered directly).

- **[enhancement]** When at risk of exceeding threat cap, use Frost Shock instead of Earth Shock more often ("half the shock CDs could be FS") to reduce damage/threat while still doing something — but Flame Shock is preferred over Frost Shock when available (ordering: Flame > Frost for damage), unless the boss is fire-immune (e.g., Firemaw) in which case Frost Shock is used — [[d:shaman#1434273408560005382]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-02), [[d:shaman#1434277861686247465]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-02), [[d:shaman#1434277992086896650]] (30, 2025-11-02).
- **[enhancement]** Weapon speed: slower weapons preferred for Enhancement scaling — bigger Stormstrikes, bigger Windfury procs, and more threat since Rockbiter Weapon's threat bonus is a percentage increase rather than a flat per-hit amount, so slower/bigger hits benefit more; general community norm is ~2.7 speed or slower — [[d:shaman#1434627838199468092]], [[d:shaman#1434628125731324182]] (Squid, "Yes, slow is best"), [[d:shaman#1434629879831199744]] (billienomates, 2025-11-02).
- **[enhancement]** To guarantee correct extra-attack sequencing, ensure your normal swing lands on the boss before pressing Stormstrike, or you risk losing an auto-attack if Stormstrike itself procs Windfury — [[d:shaman#1434594354118524958]] (Squid, 2025-11-02), confirmed by [[d:shaman#1434624362945843263]] (billienomates, 2025-11-02).
- **[enhancement]** Molten Blast/Elemental Devastation-type melee crit procs scale with attack speed (more swings = more crits = more procs) but such "elemental devastation" proc is reported (via combat log test) to have NO internal cooldown, meaning slow-weapon Enhancement gets comparatively less value from it than fast-swinging classes — [[d:shaman#1434979586826834051]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-03), [[d:shaman#1434984084714557501]] (30, 2025-11-03, log evidence), [[d:shaman#1434984272250405077]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-03).
- **[enhancement]** Weapon DPS math: for a slow weapon, the AP contribution to DPS ≈ AP / 14 × weapon speed; Strikes (Stormstrike/Lightning Strike) and the 2 extra Windfury swings benefit more from a weapon's max/top-end damage than from its "on paper" DPS rating, which is why Sulfuras remains BiS for most shamans until Might of Menethil despite lower-listed-DPS weapons like Dark Edge/Severance existing — [[d:shaman#1435748215210119333]] (poto, 2025-11-05), [[d:shaman#1435747982342619186]], [[d:shaman#1435748183924805723]] (billienomates, 2025-11-05), [[d:shaman#1435759380929843372]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-05: "weapon speed is a multiplier on AP after dividing by 14... slower is better for maxing WF procs, SS and LS"). At very low AP a fast weapon can win on pure white-damage output, but at typical pre-raid+ AP levels the slower weapon wins — [[d:shaman#1435759380929843372]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-05). Yellow (special) strikes are NOT normalized for Lightning Strike/Stormstrike, so slower weapons benefit there too, though white-hit damage is usually the larger share of total damage — [[d:shaman#1437158586328416459]], [[d:shaman#1437158724413427802]] (Stunfisk, 2025-11-09).
- **[enhancement]** HoJ (Hand of Justice) and Windfury Weapon DO proc together at a normal rate; confusion about "HoJ nerfing WF" comes from two factors: (1) Windfury-triggered attacks cannot proc HoJ and vice versa — extra attacks never chain into further procs — and (2) DPS-tracking addons (e.g. DPSmate) count these extra attacks as normal auto-attacks when computing proc rate %, which visually deflates the apparent proc rate even though actual proc behavior is unaffected — [[d:shaman#1437170743803642118]] (Squid, 2025-11-10, detailed testing writeup), confirmed understanding by [[d:shaman#1437172145640833217]] (Gaellan, 2025-11-10).
- **[enhancement]** Stormstrike's role in a tank rotation: use on cooldown — it does direct damage, procs Windfury, and buffs Nature damage (making the following Earth Shock/Lightning Strike/Lightning Shield procs hit harder); recommendation is to use it on CD and then follow with Earth Shock/Lightning Strike for damage and threat — [[d:shaman#1437614164502909059]], [[d:shaman#1437614286355562516]], [[d:shaman#1437614876175368336]] (Sinister Minister/Squid/Not Normal, 2025-11-10). Lining Stormstrike up so the Nature-damage buff hits an Earth Shock rather than being "wasted" on a Lightning Shield proc tick matters more when using a 22/29-style tank spec, but shock cooldown is short enough that this is easy to manage without much effort — [[d:shaman#1437615317911077035]], [[d:shaman#1437616621244907551]], [[d:shaman#1437616784189558865]] (Sinister Minister/Not Normal, 2025-11-10).

- **[elemental fire]** Fire/Molten Blast spec rotation and snapshotting mechanics (detailed): Flame Shock "snapshots" its tick damage at cast time and does NOT recalculate on refresh — so you want to pop damage cooldowns (spell damage trinkets, Elemental Mastery) and wait for external buffs (Improved Scorch/Curse of Elements) to be applied BEFORE casting/snapshotting Flame Shock; that snapshotted Flame Shock then deals increased damage for its whole duration, and Rekindling Flame's proc also checks the (snapshotted) Flame Shock damage. Searing Totem also snapshots, so drop a fresh one when you pop cooldowns. Totem of Rage adds a flat +30 to shock ticks (effectively +51 with your % fire bonus) so using it while a Flame Shock is up (rather than switching totems) is a DPS gain. After the snapshot, gameplay reduces to spamming Molten Blast to refresh Flame Shock — [[d:shaman#1437411481833439323]] through [[d:shaman#1437412523069673522]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-11-09). Minimum recommended spell hit for Fire spec ~13%, though some overshoot to 16%; Spell Crit is valuable because your fire totems (Searing/Magma/Fire Nova) inherit your spell crit rate and contribute meaningfully to fire spec's total damage — [[d:shaman#1437406726327369789]], [[d:shaman#1437407581181186079]] (Eluzia, 2025-11-09).
- **[elemental]** There are two distinct "fire" playstyles: pure Molten Blast spam (2-button, described by one player as capping their own skill as an offspec) and a melee-weaving "Rekindling weaver" that uses Stormstrike for its Chain-Lightning-buffing proc plus melee swings with Flametongue, occasionally finishing with Lightning Strike (procs off Lightning Shield can do 1k+ damage) — considered "more interactive" by proponents but disputed as a meaningful DPS contributor (estimated ~4-6% of total damage from the melee/Stormstrike component) by Squid, who ran logs showing a melee-weave fire shaman still doing less/comparable damage to backing up the claim — [[d:shaman#1437469193284091914]] through [[d:shaman#1437476188384788560]] (AxenPunker vs. Squid, 2025-11-11; contested, disputed).
- **[elemental]** Regular (Nature) Elemental rotation questions and answers: "EQ on cd > CL on cd > LB and repeat" pattern discussed (Earthquake, Chain Lightning, Lightning Bolt filler); community also describes it as Earthquake→Chain Lightning→(Fire Nova Totem if worthwhile)→Lightning Bolt→Earth Shock as a finisher — [[d:shaman#1437160508649377942]] (Kokuyo, 2025-11-09), [[d:shaman#1437471533315653732]] (AxenPunker, 2025-11-09).

## Stats, caps, weights
- **[general]** Spirit and Meditation are "dead stats" for Shaman: Shamans (like Paladins and Warlocks) are Int-based casters who don't get natural mana regen from Spirit the way Mage/Priest/Druid do — [[d:shaman#1435063795960778855]] (peter_hammer, 2025-11-03/04 — actually dated msg id near 1434512577865715814), [[d:shaman#1434512577865715814]], [[d:shaman#1434512675668230289]], [[d:shaman#1434512913221029990]] (peter_hammer, 2025-11-03).
- **[enhancement]** Haste ≈ 1% haste worth ~16 AP per pinned raid-environment stat-weight list (cross-faction raid average); another poster estimated ~30 AP per 1% haste — [[d:shaman#1434498204883357827]] (Hiryu, 2025-11-03, "I think I saw once"), [[d:shaman#1434508306835308677]] (Wolfcola, 2025-11-03, citing the pinned stat-weight post; disputed/differing numbers, treat cautiously).
- **[enhancement]** Haste valued highly by top players: "more swings = more WF procs," some prefer haste over AP as Enhancement — [[d:shaman#1434760604526055524]] (billienomates, 2025-11-03).
- **[elemental]** Spell hit: total needed for raid is ~16% effective (there's an inherent base 1% chance to miss spells, so a nominal "17%" listed hit gets you to 16% effective); 3% of the 16% comes from talents, meaning ~13% needed from gear; no "Improved Faerie Fire"-equivalent debuff exists on TWoW to reduce this requirement — [[d:shaman#1434836791340306432]] (Fjpala, 2025-11-03), [[d:shaman#1434839010072854599]] (Zhafu, 2025-11-03), [[d:shaman#1434839056768041020]] (Zhafu, 2025-11-03). Disputed: Zhafu initially claimed hit-cap isn't the top priority for Elemental ("balance between hit and crit"), which Fjpala disputed citing classic-era theorycrafting favoring hit-to-cap first — [[d:shaman#1434837960259276842]] vs [[d:shaman#1434838701275349082]] (contested, unresolved).
- **[shield mechanics — Empowered]** "Empowered" (talent that scales shield spell values with Attack Power) scaling ratios: Lightning Shield: 4 AP → 1 extra damage per charge; Water Shield: 20 AP → 1 extra mana per charge; Earth Shield: 15 AP → 1 extra health per charge — [[d:shaman#1435220698858782730]] (user "51108", 2025-11-04; single source, detailed/specific, no dispute).
- **[resto]** mp5-to-healing value estimate for resto (theorycrafted, "personal bias"): on Chain Heal rank1→rank3 upranking, gain ≈1.78 more healing per mana spent; dividing by ~2.5 (mana-per-cast-time-per-5sec) gives ≈0.712 healing gained per mana point of mp5 in that context; general personal valuation: rate mp5 as worth 0–2 healing points depending on fight length and if free to rank up/down Chain Heal (very short fight ≈ 1 mp5 = 2 healing; can maxrank whole fight ≈ 1 mp5 = 0 healing) — [[d:shaman#1432785086951985353]], [[d:shaman#1432785712775696616]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-10-28; single source, explicitly caveated as "personal bias nonsense" — disputed by peter_hammer who considers any mp5 beyond current T3 gear useless once not going oom [[d:shaman#1432785964929122345]]).
- **[tank]** 70 defense rating ≈ 2.8% dodge/parry/block (avoidance) — [[d:shaman#1433086857796780113]] (Hiryu, 2025-10-29). More precisely: Defense gives 0.04% chance each to dodge, parry, block, "crit-to-hit" conversion, and to be missed, per point (i.e. ~0.8% total avoidance per 5 Defense) — [[d:shaman#1436769892786241596]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-08).
- **[enhancement]** Crit vs. Haste for Enhancement: stat-weight analysis consistently shows Crit ~30-40% stronger than Haste (one specific data point: crit worth ~35% more than haste over a ~90s fight, ~23% more over a ~46s fight, in phase-2 enhance gear); reasoning given: Haste doesn't affect Stormstrike/Lightning Strike cooldowns (their tooltips are unaffected by haste), doesn't affect yellow-damage the way crit does, and there's essentially never a real gearing choice between the two since most items don't force you to pick — [[d:shaman#1436812446001266759]], [[d:shaman#1436813024123162846]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-09), [[d:shaman#1436838301947658283]] (Greybull-Ambershire, 2025-11-09), [[d:shaman#1437661895392759878]], [[d:shaman#1437662119796412488]] (poto/Xorlord, 2025-11-11). Once you already have enough crit for 100% Flurry uptime, haste (multiplicative) is argued to become strong past ~10% — open/unresolved question in the chunk — [[d:shaman#1437661703310545008]], [[d:shaman#1437661852204007544]] (Xorlord, 2025-11-11).
- **[enhancement]** Rough stat-weight guide (per Squid, based on a since-revised guild theorycrafting sheet): 2 Agility ≈ 1 Strength; Haste and Crit "equal-ish"; Armor Penetration ≈ 2 Armor Pen : 1 AP if your raid brings Improved Expose Armor + Faerie Fire + Curse of Recklessness (missing armor debuffs increase armor pen's value; existing debuffs make haste more valuable due to Windfury) — [[d:shaman#1436810026902622329]], [[d:shaman#1436810089347416186]] (Squid, 2025-11-09), [[d:shaman#1436811130663075993]], [[d:shaman#1436811221562163397]] (poto, 2025-11-09).
- **[enhancement]** Enhancement raid spell-hit cap: 8% total needed for bosses (3% from talents, 1% from a "book" item, remainder from gear/weapon skill); a Flameguard Gauntlets bonus reduces the needed gear-hit to 4%; being an Orc with Flameguard + book reduces it to 2%; overcapping isn't worth chasing unless an item is specifically itemized for it (e.g. Truestrike) — [[d:shaman#1437197315893887107]], [[d:shaman#1437197427978272921]], [[d:shaman#1437197649479733329]], [[d:shaman#1437198036345290762]] (poto/billienomates/Holidae, 2025-11-09/10).
- **[elemental]** Elemental spell-hit stat weight discussion: hit, crit, and haste are separate multipliers whose relative value depends on total current stats/gear/buffs (diminishing/compounding returns argument, disputed clarity in-chat) — [[d:shaman#1437006067652366346]], [[d:shaman#1437006214440685660]] (Ciftar (Edward the Odd), 2025-11-09; not fully resolved, treat as informal heuristic).
- **[tank]** EHP (effective health) math for shaman tanks: DR = Armor / (Armor + 400 + 85×AttackerLevel); EHP = HP / (1 - DR). Practical stam-to-armor conversion ratios worked out collaboratively: roughly 1 Stamina ≈ 19 Armor as a baseline midpoint; if HP is low relative to armor, 1 Stam is worth up to ~25 Armor; if armor is low relative to HP, 1 Stam is worth as little as ~15-16 Armor; Rockbiter Weapon/armor talents bump the effective armor value ~10-15% higher. At a typical early-Phase-1 armor level (~2-2.5k unbuffed, ~6-7k with all buffs before AQ), these ratios hold; the two Armor talents together give a shield ~49.5% (or ~45%, cited slightly differently) more effective armor than its tooltip value — [[d:shaman#1436374761633747074]], [[d:shaman#1436382421544210604]], [[d:shaman#1436384413704388738]], [[d:shaman#1436386686736269473]], [[d:shaman#1436406259342508215]], [[d:shaman#1436659968622202891]] (Zeruel/Greybull-Ambershire, 2025-11-06/08), [[d:shaman#1433581348689412278]], [[d:shaman#1433581397506785301]], [[d:shaman#1433581838835908659]], [[d:shaman#1436583908111286384]] (chincer/Not Normal, 2025-10-31/11-08).
- **[tank]** Avoidance (dodge specifically) is deliberately NOT folded into the EHP formula by several theorycrafters — treated as a separate axis, since EHP (armor+health) determines "how many hits in a row you can survive," while dodge only reduces the chance of any single hit landing and doesn't reduce crit/crush chance; too much avoidance stacking can make incoming damage "spikier" since crushing-blow chance stays constant while normal hits get dodged away first (WoW's attack table resolves Miss/Dodge/Parry → Normal → Crit → Crush in that priority order) — [[d:shaman#1437224926372167700]], [[d:shaman#1437225083868282980]], [[d:shaman#1437225288600911972]] (poto/Squid, 2025-11-09), [[d:shaman#1436773978235601037]], [[d:shaman#1436773773893439558]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-08).
- **[tank]** Getting Defense-capped/crit-immune is not realistically achievable pre-AQ40 without wearing dedicated defense-heavy pre-raid items that would tank your EHP too much; before AQ you generally can't get meaningful dodge (best-case ~+9% dodge from full agility itemization in Phase 1) without sacrificing stam/armor, so the recommendation is to stack stam+armor and simply accept crushes/crits — [[d:shaman#1436064235330207744]], [[d:shaman#1436064402418958486]], [[d:shaman#1436066823056523485]], [[d:shaman#1436068214579658857]], [[d:shaman#1436068263745294490]] (poto, 2025-11-06), [[d:shaman#1437225428208320603]], [[d:shaman#1437225565202681886]], [[d:shaman#1437225626703499345]] (billienomates/poto, 2025-11-09).

## Enchants, imbues, oils, poisons, stones
- **[general]** Weapon imbue (Flametongue/Windfury/Frostbrand cast on your own weapon) and the corresponding party-wide Weapon Totems (WFT/FTT) are different things: the totems are for your party, not yourself, meaning everyone else can benefit from a totem PLUS their own imbue, giving them 2 buffs, while the shaman gets only their own 1 imbue on weapon — considered a known asymmetry/tradeoff, not a bug, "your 1 imbue is better than their 2" — [[d:shaman#1433029288629633144]] through [[d:shaman#1433044114504548352]] (Olivaar/peter_hammer, 2025-10-29).
- **[tank]** Rockbiter Weapon imbue relevant mainly/only for tank shamans (threat) since Rockbiter+Windfury combo would otherwise be a DPS non-choice — [[d:shaman#1433041682345234523]], [[d:shaman#1433043144399917086]] (Dodging/Olivaar, 2025-10-29).
- **[general]** Weapon-proc totems' effects have a 1.5-second ICD, unlike direct weapon-imbue enchants, which have no such shared ICD — [[d:shaman#1434578880269914113]] (peter_hammer, 2025-11-02); TWoW implemented extra-attack-chain prevention differently from vanilla, and extra attacks cannot chain into further procs on TWoW — [[d:shaman#1434578994627612763]], [[d:shaman#1434579765146157189]] (Stunfisk/Squid, 2025-11-02).
- **[enhancement]** WF weapon imbue strongly preferred over Elemental Sharpening Stone + relying on a party's WF totem for your own weapon — the difference between having your own WF imbue vs. only the totem is "an order of magnitude larger" than any benefit from the sharpening stone — [[d:shaman#1434263544605442280]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-02, +2 reactions).
- **[enhancement]** Enchant recommendations at low-mid level (~38-45): Squid recommends +3 all stats on jewelry (extra stam), +8 Strength on head/legs, 1% haste on wrist/gloves (easy to get) or Strength; at raid level, Crusader enchant considered "incredibly overpowered" compared to other options, hard to justify Crit enchant over Crusader without being at high (33%) baseline crit — [[d:shaman#1434747949350649926]] (Squid, 2025-11-03), [[d:shaman#1434760727239069748]], [[d:shaman#1434761660467515544]] (billienomates, 2025-11-03).
- **[tank]** Belt buckles (jewel-slot-equivalent add-ons) did not exist in classic; only added starting Wrath of the Lich King; on Turtle WoW (currently Phase 1), belt buckle options remain identical to vanilla-classic until Emerald Sanctum releases the Dreamsteel Buckle — [[d:shaman#1433520760072306750]] (poto, 2025-10-30), corrected by Holidae re: WotLK origin [[d:shaman#1433521943914741760]].
- **[tank]** Shield spike enchant not worth it for Lasher farming specifically; a regular stamina shield enchant preferred for general (non-Lasher) shield use — [[d:shaman#1433523357013377025]], [[d:shaman#1433523509530857653]], [[d:shaman#1433523635636670554]] (Holidae/Zeruel, 2025-10-30).
- **[general]** Hand of Justice does work with Windfury Weapon enchant (question raised, implied yes since discussed alongside "infinite attacks" jokes about combining HoJ + Ironfoe + WF) — [[d:shaman#1433113270612656231]] (Matyko, 2025-10-29), [[d:shaman#1434563311021592650]] (Holidae, 2025-11-02).

- **[resto]** Consumable stack recommended for resto healing beyond just tea/pots: oils, Mageblood Potion, Dreamless Sleep/Dream Elixir — [[d:shaman#1432782838381547590]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-10-28).

## Cooldowns, resources, threat, utility
- **[general]** Weapon imbue effects last 1 hour (buffed up from the original vanilla 5 minutes, and beyond even Burning Crusade's later 30-minute value); a talent now grants passive effects tied to which imbue is active — [[d:shaman#1436458914328875098]], [[d:shaman#1436459371432251515]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-08; not independently disputed by others, treat as a real balance/QoL note).
- **[tank]** Downranking Earth Shock and Fire Nova Totem to "whatever gives you enough threat to stay in the lead" (rather than always max-rank) is recommended for mana conservation while tanking — [[d:shaman#1436372049571610707]] (Holidae, 2025-11-06).
- **[tank]** Totem of Crackling Thunder and Totem of Stonebreaker (or similar shock-totem/threat totems) can both be used, situationally — [[d:shaman#1436045190556155905]], [[d:shaman#1436045273146065026]] (Keldjor/Squid, 2025-11-06); relic swapping recommendation is to use Totem of Rage while a Flame Shock snapshot is active for the +30 flat shock tick damage, rather than switching totems mid-fight — [[d:shaman#1437411774453514291]] (Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-11-09).
- **[general]** Mana Spring Totem and Blessing of Wisdom stack (do not overwrite each other); resistance totems/auras remain the one category that does not stack with matching paladin auras (see Consumables section for era context) — [[d:shaman#1435354837238681620]] (Squid, 2025-11-04).
- **[enhancement/tank]** Elemental Devastation's mana-cost-reduction on Frost Shock (via 5/5 Convection) is applied as a percentage reduction off the BASE cost, then that flat amount is subtracted from the already-reduced cost — this can look like a miscalculation if you expect a percentage-of-current-cost reduction (observed: Frost Shock rank 3 costs 292 with 5/5 Convection, and Elemental Devastation dropped it by 97 more, which only makes sense against the base cost) — [[d:shaman#1436839579469484033]], [[d:shaman#1436841296336785571]] (Holidae, 2025-11-09).
- **[general]** Confirmed weapon-imbue/proc interaction rules from live combat-log testing: extra attacks from one proc source (e.g. Windfury) cannot trigger a DIFFERENT proc source (e.g. Hand of Justice) and vice versa — they can occur off the same base swing simultaneously but never chain off each other's generated attacks — [[d:shaman#1437170743803642118]] (Squid, 2025-11-10).

## Tanking specifics
- **[tank]** Spirit Armor talent is "literally what makes a shaman tank" and is "probably the biggest source of damage reduction a shaman can have" — [[d:shaman#1436558519733321828]], [[d:shaman#1436558610128961597]] (poto, 2025-11-08). Two separate armor talents stack multiplicatively with a shield: one increases armor contribution from all gear by 15%, another increases armor specifically from an equipped shield by a further 30% — together giving a shield ~45-49.5% more effective armor than its tooltip value ("basically bear form equivalent for shaman") — [[d:shaman#1436371696029401150]] (Holidae, 2025-11-06), [[d:shaman#1433581348689412278]], [[d:shaman#1433581397506785301]], [[d:shaman#1433581422442053742]], [[d:shaman#1433581838835908659]], [[d:shaman#1436583908111286384]] (chincer/Not Normal, 2025-10-31/11-08).
- **[tank]** Shaman tank squishiness is "super squishy until the shield talent" pre-40; once you have level-appropriate gear plus both armor talents (post-40) you can sit at 50-60% damage reduction fairly easily, but the real limiting factor is a lack of crit immunity, so unlucky crit/crush streaks can "ping pong hard" — [[d:shaman#1436463511176872008]], [[d:shaman#1436463901087891466]] (billienomates, 2025-11-08). One player reported 61% damage reduction pre-raid-buffs using a mix of pre-MC/MC gear with only 3 actual tank pieces — [[d:shaman#1436373138085773384]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-06).
- **[tank]** Enhancing Totems talent's block-amount bonus for Stoneskin Totem may or may not be working: damage-reduction portion is confirmed to work; the block-damage-increase portion is reported as possibly still broken since the talent's rework — unresolved in chat, testers disagree — [[d:shaman#1436456842631839754]], [[d:shaman#1436469310460530719]], [[d:shaman#1436469505873285244]] (Hiryu/Diving Instructor Knife, 2025-11-08). Block damage itself is considered low-value for shaman tanks regardless ("isn't worth a damn") — [[d:shaman#1436469401754009621]] (billienomates, 2025-11-08).
- **[tank]** A tanking trinket (relic-based, exact name unlisted in text but shown via screenshot) that procs off Lightning Shield/shock damage/Totem of Tides tidal-wave damage was measured by one tester as contributing roughly 10% of total damage over a full raid night in MC, and separately as high as 19% of total damage during a single fight (Majordomo, while tanking adds) — it does NOT proc from auto-attacks, shield block damage, or single-target Tidal Wave hits reliably, only from AoE-heavy tidal wave procs; also confirmed to proc Insomnius (another on-hit trinket) — [[d:shaman#1437100521994326237]], [[d:shaman#1437101071754461388]], [[d:shaman#1437118450110566501]], [[d:shaman#1437132237249314947]], [[d:shaman#1437134058978283600]], [[d:shaman#1437136271695941816]] (Eluzia, 2025-11-09; expected to get nerfed per other players' comments, single detailed tester).
- **[tank]** Spellhance tanking (spell-power based tank build/gear) uses the exact same overall setup as normal Enhancement tanking, just with different talent priorities and spells emphasized; you cannot fit meaningful dodge into a Phase-1 tank build (~+9% max from best agility items) without sacrificing stam/armor, so stack stam+armor instead pre-AQ — [[d:shaman#1436063231507566702]], [[d:shaman#1436063333202661477]], [[d:shaman#1436063458394243122]], [[d:shaman#1436064235330207744]], [[d:shaman#1436064402418958486]], [[d:shaman#1436064461382357113]] (poto, 2025-11-06).
- **[tank]** A slow single-hand spell-power weapon choice gimps threat significantly because there simply aren't good slow 1H spell-power weapons available; a shaman tank should not focus on pushing threat output early on in gear progression — [[d:shaman#1436067064275144794]], [[d:shaman#1436067236052996116]], [[d:shaman#1436067489405730929]] (Squid/poto, 2025-11-06).
- **[tank]** Str vs Agi for shaman tank: Agility gives more dodge/mitigation, Strength gives more threat (via Attack Power from Rockbiter Weapon's AP-scaling); since shaman tanks already have strong baseline threat generation, prioritize tankiness (Agi/Stam/Armor) over more threat (Str) especially in 5-mans — Strength is considered "lower value" than for other tank classes specifically because RBW already grants AP — [[d:shaman#1435591472551428208]] (Porkins, 2025-11-05), [[d:shaman#1435592518027968562]] (peter_hammer, 2025-11-05), confirmed at level 60 by [[d:shaman#1435675558963843083]] (billienomates, 2025-11-05, noting Str eventually "replaces agi" in value at 60 for full-progression tank gear via other talent interactions).
- **[tank]** +100 HP is worth more raw EHP than +8 Stamina even accounting for Kings and Ancestral Knowledge multipliers, per napkin math — [[d:shaman#1437144339246481549]], [[d:shaman#1437144558214582435]] (IrnBruBreath/Zeruel, 2025-11-09).
- **[tank]** Dodge chasing is considered secondary to EHP for shaman tank philosophy: "we like dodge, don't get me wrong, but we don't chase it" — dodge doesn't stop the guaranteed crushing-blow chance, and Grace of Air Totem (~+3% dodge) is treated as sufficient rather than gearing further into dodge — [[d:shaman#1436374289279357008]], [[d:shaman#1436376685015470251]], [[d:shaman#1435299724885491804]] (Zeruel/Holidae, 2025-11-04/06).
- **[tank]** Shaman tanks can (and do) tank Ragnaros in Molten Core, though mana-based tanks (as opposed to Warrior/Druid-bear which are described as immune to the caster fireball knockback mechanic) are more likely to be knocked back on Rag's fireball mechanic — a fully-defense-capped 315 fire-resist tank still got knocked back, suggesting the knockback isn't purely resisted by FR — [[d:shaman#1434444518480154656]], [[d:shaman#1434444586985717780]], [[d:shaman#1434444651338661999]], [[d:shaman#1434444775104450631]], [[d:shaman#1434444833799405638]], [[d:shaman#1434445047763435522]] (IrnBruBreath/poto, 2025-11-01). Naxx-geared shamans have successfully tanked Rag ("shaman can tank Rag with shitty Vanilla Tank Talents" per one player, though this was said sarcastically in a broader class-balance argument) with corroborating claim that a Naxx-geared enhancement shaman "showed their worth beating up Rag" — [[d:shaman#1437174753730494614]], [[d:shaman#1437178323250712818]] (Zestitcha/billienomates, 2025-11-10).

## Healing specifics
- **[resto]** Chain Heal is considered actively bugged: it is not applying its "smart heal" targeting correctly (missing the highest-damage-taken targets), and its jump/bounce range measured at only ~7.6 yards instead of the intended ~9.8-10 yards — an active, upvote-requested bug report exists — [[d:shaman#1436023188399198389]], [[d:shaman#1436023292006891675]], [[d:shaman#1434598299830194327]], [[d:shaman#1434632518543343737]], [[d:shaman#1434632971616256112]] (AxenPunker/Chidi, 2025-11-02/06), [[d:shaman#1436372724275609612]], [[d:shaman#1436373066413510818]] (Chidi, 2025-11-06, jump-range bug detail).
- **[resto]** No viable "hybrid resto" build exists per community consensus: Chain Heal needs its 25-point talents to not be "complete shit," and Healing Wave's 2.2s base cast time makes a hybrid too slow/exposed (e.g. to being "sniped" by enemy interrupters in PvP) — [[d:shaman#1436116690885808229]] (Miado, 2025-11-06).
- **[resto]** Leveling advice for a resto-intending shaman: don't put a single point into the Resto tree before level 50 if you don't have a dedicated tank (healing while questing solo is inefficient and each mob kill "will take forever" with resto talents); if playing mostly Enhancement while leveling, keep spare +Intellect gear on hand to heal dungeons with existing base healing spells — [[d:shaman#1436881688713236605]], [[d:shaman#1436882401551978667]], [[d:shaman#1436882543965110385]], [[d:shaman#1436882617042735247]], [[d:shaman#1436882684054995168]] (Squid/poto, 2025-11-08). Countered/nuanced: if you DO have a dedicated tank, you can follow the standard endgame Resto build from early on; alternatively even 11 points into Resto (for the cast-speed-reduction talent) plus the rest into other trees gives a more comfortable early-healing experience with minimal loss to leveling speed — [[d:shaman#1435681320175927447]] (peter_hammer, 2025-11-05), [[d:shaman#1435683232375898196]], [[d:shaman#1435687611107905748]] (Miado/billienomates, 2025-11-05).
- **[resto]** Respec-to-resto-for-dungeon-healing timing: not a strict requirement if you're geared/skilled and have a competent group, but damage output in dungeons ramps up significantly by Zul'Farrak, making faster/cheaper heals from Resto talents important there onward; by Sunken Temple (Eranikus) a Resto respec is basically required — [[d:shaman#1437273857420562572]], [[d:shaman#1437274154872344696]] (billienomates, 2025-11-10).
- **[healing mechanics]** Shield healing-power scaling confirmed: Earth Shield scales with healing power, Water Shield does NOT scale with any stat, Lightning Shield scales with spell power; via the "Empowered" talent these further scale off Attack Power (see Stats section for exact ratios) — [[d:shaman#1435024400545091725]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-04), [[d:shaman#1435220698858782730]] (51108, 2025-11-04).
- **[healing mechanics]** Lightning Shield CANNOT crit — an addon-reported "crit list" showing Lightning Shield as capable of critting was confirmed to be an addon (an old BetterCharacterStats) bug/mishap after live testing with a full T2 elemental set — [[d:shaman#1437503994489602128]], [[d:shaman#1437504917454458912]], [[d:shaman#1437511932159590431]] (Diving Instructor Knife/Squid/Dwi, 2025-11-10).
- **[healing mechanics]** Earth Shield is very strong for solo self-sustain: excellent healing-per-mana-spent (especially paired with Lightning Strike triggering it) and requires no active casting to heal you — recommended as an option even for DPS/tank solo play, not just as a healer tool — [[d:shaman#1437517486697484389]], [[d:shaman#1437517720735449149]], [[d:shaman#1437517868840390753]] (Not Normal, 2025-11-10), [[d:shaman#1437527769792778301]] (Zestitcha, 2025-11-10, "Earth Shield is often underrated").

## PvP
- **[elemental]** Elemental Mastery no longer grants 100% crit chance on TWoW (nerfed from an earlier/different implementation); Fire spec is still the dominant PvP elemental choice over Nature spec despite this — [[d:shaman#1435285381774970960]], [[d:shaman#1435293465889210499]] (Evyn/Zaaparou, 2025-11-04).

## Macros, addons, UI
- **[macros]** Working Hearthstone/Astral Recall priority macro: `/cast [nocooldown:Astral Recall] Astral Recall` then `/use Hearthstone` (uses Astral Recall when off cooldown, falls back to Hearthstone) — [[d:shaman#1432907775897501877]] (Squid, 2025-10-29).
- **[macros]** Instant-cast weapon-imbue-conditional macros via SuperCleveRoidMacros: `mhimbue`/`ohimbue` conditionals check for the presence (or, with an argument, a specific name) of a main-hand/off-hand imbue without needing the enchant's numeric rank — e.g. `/cast [nomhimbue:Flametongue]Flametongue Weapon` then `/cast [mhimbue:Flametongue]Flame Shock`; useful for rogues checking poisons too, and lets you use a racial (e.g. Blood Fury) even when not currently running Rockbiter — [[d:shaman#1437616764455358484]], [[d:shaman#1437617558026063924]], [[d:shaman#1437617418749874219]] (Don't ping me/Squid, 2025-11-11).
- **[macros]** Totem-swap macro for Lightning Strike/Earth Shock rotations: `/equip Totem of Crackling Thunder` + `/cast Lightning Strike` (the `/equip` line does the actual relic swap; plain `/cast Lightning Strike` will not swap the totem) — [[d:shaman#1437316804459364516]], [[d:shaman#1437286379750031472]] (Squid, 2025-11-10). Sample SuperCleveRoidMacros templates for Lightning Strike/Earth Shock using nocdgcd conditionals and totem item IDs were shared — [[d:shaman#1436045375353131038]], [[d:shaman#1436045484174344384]] (Hiryu, 2025-11-06).
- **[addons]** CoE (Call of Elements, a totem-bar addon) has been broken/unreliable due to a Blizzard/TWoW API change: pressing its "place all totems" macro does not replace a totem that was destroyed by a mob mid-fight (only replaces on natural expiry or being out of range); it can also fail to recall totems' remaining-time correctly after Totemic Recall is used. See Bugs & quirks section for full citations. ReloadUI is a known partial workaround — [[d:shaman#1435095115361161300]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-04).
- **[addons]** CoE has an option (`/coe`) to disable automatic PvP-set gear switching if it's incorrectly swapping totem/gear presets when targeting different classes — [[d:shaman#1437409684788084828]], [[d:shaman#1437425666927755294]] (Zaaparou/peter_hammer, 2025-11-09).
- **[addons]** ImprovedCharacterStats (a popular character-stat addon) is not built for Turtle WoW's custom stat sources and under-reports things like ring +healing enchants and possibly shaman parry avoidance; BetterCharacterStats is a more accurate alternative that correctly shows parry — [[d:shaman#1433076175898415326]], [[d:shaman#1433080649807433762]], [[d:shaman#1433088795909029938]] (2025-10-29, see Gear section for full citations).
- **[addons]** VCB (Vanilla Consolidate Buff Frames) recommended for repositioning buff/debuff/totem icon displays (e.g. moving them above the health bar instead of mid-screen) — [[d:shaman#1433795013493526548]] (Mythblack, 2025-10-29).
- **[addons]** MonkeySpeed addon (requires SuperWoW) lets you inspect and verify which movement-speed effects are/aren't stacking in real time — [[d:shaman#1434929028040495245]] (Zaaparou, 2025-11-03).
- **[addons]** `/aux tooltip disenchant value on` and `/aux tooltip disenchant distribution on` (Auctioneer/Aux addon commands) show expected disenchant gold value and material breakdown percentages directly in item tooltips — [[d:shaman#1435323731030446231]], [[d:shaman#1435333387664756827]] (Squid, 2025-11-04).

## Races, professions, gold
- **[races]** Tauren vs Orc for shaman: Tauren better while leveling (Warstomp CC + more health outweighs Bloodfury at low level, plus the exclusive Plainsrunning racial ability — full mount-equivalent run speed unlocked via a Bloodhoof Village quest after training riding at 40, allows casting buffs like Water Walking while running); Orc considered better at endgame for min-maxed DPS (Bloodfury racial, Axe Specialization synergy) and is specifically called out as strong for Fire spec — [[d:shaman#1435151752864333954]] (Ciftar, 2025-11-04), [[d:shaman#1435431023776104539]], [[d:shaman#1435431339318055033]], [[d:shaman#1435431582126182564]] (Holidae/Dinousek, 2025-11-04), [[d:shaman#1437534680089100460]], [[d:shaman#1437534776134336512]], [[d:shaman#1437534943830867989]] (billienomates, 2025-11-11). Tauren also gets Plainsrunning + Totemic Slam (reduces target attack speed, cool for enrage-mitigation scenarios, does not stack with Thunderfury) — [[d:shaman#1434487240163332097]], [[d:shaman#1435152086168764599]], [[d:shaman#1435152179236311120]], [[d:shaman#1435188122081169418]] (Diving Instructor Knife/Ciftar/Cariad, 2025-11-01/04, Totemic Slam confirmed not to stack with Kara40's similar totem or Thunderfury).
- **[professions]** Alchemy profit is directly tied to raid frequency/demand — with only MC/Onyxia running, alchemy profit is weak; profits reported as poor on some servers (e.g., ~15g profit selling 10 of 30 crafted potions/elixirs over 6 hours on Ambershire) — [[d:shaman#1433516345114492938]] (peter_hammer, 2025-10-30), [[d:shaman#1435057554798678027]] (Zeruel, 2025-11-04). - **[gold]** Basilisks and Scorpids in Desolace/Tanaris are cited repeatedly as strong "grey item" farming spots for early gold (5-12 silver per grey drop, ~5-7g per full bag), useful while leveling a Shaman — [[d:shaman#1433823335791202344]], [[d:shaman#1433855128921968662]], [[d:shaman#1433855468236701819]], [[d:shaman#1433855817307787325]], [[d:shaman#1433859562892431471]] (Holidae/Stunfisk, 2025-10-30/31). Devilsaur leather farming cited as a solid shaman gold farm on Nord as an alternative to Lasher farming — [[d:shaman#1435056833592164442]] (Holidae, 2025-11-04).
- **[gold]** No single reliable gold farm for shamans on Ambershire in this period besides Lasher farming, which itself crashed in profitability due to herb oversupply/low raid demand; enchanting materials via disenchanting (using an alt/bank toon leveled just enough to disenchant) was reported as a steady if unspectacular income source — [[d:shaman#1435308789216706580]], [[d:shaman#1435322304258375780]], [[d:shaman#1435323038991388722]], [[d:shaman#1435323333238329444]] (Nandieb/Windfury/Holidae, 2025-11-04).
